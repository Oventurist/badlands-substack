## Garbage truck presser and cartel asset seizure proposal (October 2024)

In the final week of the 2024 campaign, Trump turned a Democratic insult into a stunt, speaking to reporters on Wednesday, October 30 from the passenger seat of a campaign-branded garbage truck marked "LOADMASTER" while wearing an orange hi-vis vest.[{n}] "How do you like my garbage truck?" he asked supporters. "This truck is in honor of Kamala [Harris] and Joe Biden."[{n}] The sequence had begun with Trump saying immigration had turned the United States into a "garbage can," continued with a speaker at his Madison Square Garden rally calling Puerto Rico a "floating island of garbage," and culminated in [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] appearing to call Trump's supporters "garbage" — "The only garbage I see floating out there is his supporters," Biden said, calling the "demonization of Latinos" unconscionable and un-American.[{n}] Trump kept the garbageman costume on at a rally in Green Bay, swaying to Lee Greenwood's "God Bless the U.S.A." in the vest over his button-down and tie.[{n}] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the episode as [[trumpamania|TrumpaMania]] at full strength, arguing Trump grasped that troop morale is decisive in a long conflict and had found a way to make his campaign whimsical while humiliating the [[uniparty|Uniparty]].[{n}]

The previous day, Tuesday, October 29, Trump said at Mar-a-Lago that his administration would seize "the assets of criminal gangs and drug cartels" and direct the proceeds to families affected by "migrant crime" if elected the 47th president.[{n}] The funds would create a compensation fund for victims of crimes committed by people in the United States illegally, with Trump adding that "the government will help in the restoration."[{n}] Asset seizure has long been used by the federal government against drug trafficking organizations, and former [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]] officials described it as an effective tool in the pursuit of justice.[{n}] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed the proposal in a chronology alongside Trump's December 2017 executive order blocking the property of persons involved in serious human rights abuse or corruption, his December 2023 declaration of "war on cartels," the [[toronto-dominion-bank|Toronto-Dominion Bank]] fentanyl laundering case, and the [[bank-of-america|Bank of America]] money-laundering enforcement warning.[{n}]
