## Variant tracking and the 2023 booster campaign

At the time of the September 11, 2023 [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]] authorization of updated [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccines]], CDC variant-proportion data showed that XBB.1.5 — the Omicron subvariant the new shots targeted — had already largely been displaced by newer strains including EG.5.[{n}] The mismatch between the targeted subvariant and circulating strains was cited by critics of the authorization.[{n}]
