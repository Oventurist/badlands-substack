## September 2023 commentary

In the September 12, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Burning Bright wrote on NATO's announcement of the [[steadfast-defender-exercise|Steadfast Defender]] drills, recalling that he first entered the information war publicly by examining the macro of the Ukraine-Russia conflict as a proxy for the struggle between the forces of sovereignty and globalism worldwide.[{n}] He argued that the Western media had begun turning on its "fledgling proxy state" while [[nato|NATO]] warmongers prepared war drills as an open threat display against [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], and predicted Putin would not respond kinetically.[{n}] He identified two effects on the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]]: ratcheting fears of World War III, and casting NATO and the collectivist West as the aggressors, closing that "the age of peace is coming" but would require peacemakers on the game board.[{n}]

In the same edition he wrote on the opening of the [[united-states-v-google-antitrust-trial|Google antitrust trial]], calling it potentially one of the most impactful cases in history and highlighting that the investigation was launched under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[justice-department|DOJ]] and continued under the Biden administration.[{n}]
