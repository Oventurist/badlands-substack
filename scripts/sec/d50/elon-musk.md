## Unsealing of X Holdings shareholders (August 2024)

A federal judge ordered on Tuesday, August 20, 2024 that Musk's [[x-corp|X]] unseal the list of shareholders involved with X Holdings Corp., giving the public an official view of the investors who aided his $44 billion purchase of [[twitter|Twitter]] in October 2022.[n] The filing listed nearly 100 entities, though many appeared to represent different funds controlled by the same firm or person, and X had not previously detailed its stakeholders publicly.[n]

Named shareholders included venture firm [[andreessen-horowitz|Andreessen Horowitz]], Saudi Prince [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]], Twitter founder and former chief executive [[jack-dorsey|Jack Dorsey]], [[8vc|8VC]] — the venture firm co-founded by [[joe-lonsdale|Joe Lonsdale]], co-founder of intelligence contractor and data analysis platform [[palantir|Palantir]] — a fund linked to hip-hop mogul [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]], and lesser-known holders such as the Bologna-based Italian financial services company UnipolSai S.P.A.[n]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the disclosure as "totally saturated in 'planned long ago' vibes," arguing that the official revelation on the eve of the final push of the election cycle looked like a deliberate deployment, and noting that Andreessen and Horowitz had recently endorsed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], that Dorsey might be playing a far larger behind-the-scenes role than commonly believed, that Lonsdale is directly affiliated with [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], and that Alwaleed bin Talal had not controlled his own assets since their 2017 seizure by [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]].[n] He concluded that Musk and Trump were executing a "MIL-CIV pincer" on the [[cabal|Cabal]], and that "if the enemy didn't know who was helping them before, they do now."[n]
