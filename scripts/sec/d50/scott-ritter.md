## August 2024 FBI search

In early August 2024 the [[fbi|FBI]] searched Ritter's home as part of a Justice Department investigation into Americans with ties to Russian state television networks, an effort framed by prosecutors as countering potential Kremlin interference in the 2024 presidential election.[n] Newsweek described Ritter as a former [[united-nations|United Nations]] Special Commission weapons inspector, U.S. Marine Corps intelligence officer, and convicted sex offender who regularly contributes to Russian state media and often mirrors Kremlin talking points on the war in Ukraine.[n] In January 2024 Ritter visited the Russian republic of Chechnya, offering its leader [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]] and his fighters "friendship" with the United States during an address in the capital, Grozny.[n] U.S. officials said further searches were likely and did not rule out criminal charges.[n]
