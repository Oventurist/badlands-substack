## Kennedy censorship ruling (2024)

In an order issued Tuesday, August 20, 2024, Doughty found that [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] could proceed with his censorship lawsuit against the Biden administration despite the Supreme Court's June 2024 decision denying standing to state and individual plaintiffs in the related case.[n] Doughty concluded that Kennedy satisfied the standard the Court had set, pointing to "ample evidence" that he had previously been censored at the direction of government actors and a "substantial risk" that such censorship would continue.[n] He summarized the [[murthy-v-missouri|Murthy v. Missouri]] test as requiring "specific findings that a particular defendant pressured a particular platform to censor a particular topic before the platform suppressed a particular plaintiff's speech on that topic."[n]
