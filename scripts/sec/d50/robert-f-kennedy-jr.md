## Kennedy v. Biden censorship suit (August 2024)

A federal judge ruled on Tuesday, August 20, 2024 that Kennedy could continue to pursue his censorship lawsuit against the Biden administration.[n] The [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] had held in June 2024 that state and individual plaintiffs alleging the administration violated their First Amendment rights by pressuring social media companies to suppress speech lacked standing to sue; District Judge [[terry-doughty|Terry Doughty]] found that Kennedy nonetheless met the standard set by the Court, citing "ample evidence" that he had been censored in the past at the direction of government actors and a "substantial risk" that the censorship would continue.[n] Under the Court's [[murthy-v-missouri|Murthy v. Missouri]] ruling, Doughty explained, "a court must make specific findings that a particular defendant pressured a particular platform to censor a particular topic before the platform suppressed a particular plaintiff's speech on that topic."[n]
