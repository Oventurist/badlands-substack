## Partial reinstatement of Arizona proof-of-citizenship law (August 2024)

On Thursday, August 22, 2024 the Court partially granted the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]]'s emergency request to revive an Arizona law strengthening proof-of-citizenship requirements for voting.[n] In a 5–4 vote the justices reinstated the provision requiring officials to reject state voter registration forms when the applicant did not provide proof of citizenship.[n] The three liberal justices — [[sonia-sotomayor|Sonia Sotomayor]], [[elena-kagan|Elena Kagan]] and [[ketanji-brown-jackson|Ketanji Brown Jackson]] — dissented alongside conservative Justice [[amy-coney-barrett|Amy Coney Barrett]]; in voting to deny the RNC's other request, those same four were in the majority, joined by at least one of Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] or Justice [[brett-kavanaugh|Brett Kavanaugh]].[n] RNC Chairman [[michael-whatley|Michael Whatley]] called the order "a seismic win in the fight to stop non-citizens from voting — more to come!"[n]

Earlier the same term, the Court had ruled in June 2024 that state and individual plaintiffs in the [[murthy-v-missouri|Murthy v. Missouri]] censorship case lacked standing, a holding a federal district judge subsequently distinguished in allowing [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]'s parallel suit to proceed.[n]
