## August 2024 FBI search

The [[fbi|FBI]] searched Simes's home in early August 2024 as part of the Justice Department's investigation into Americans with ties to Russian state media in the run-up to the 2024 presidential election.[n] Simes, who is Russian-born, served as an adviser to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first presidential campaign in 2016 and hosts a weekly current affairs program on Russia's state-run Channel One.[n] Officials cited in reporting on the probe said additional searches were likely and that criminal charges had not been ruled out.[n]
