## Refusal of 2024 intelligence briefings

In an exclusive interview with DailyMail.com published in August 2024, Trump said he would not accept the intelligence briefings customarily offered to presidential nominees, for fear of being accused of leaking classified information.[n] U.S. spy agencies offer such briefings to candidates once they secure their party's nomination in order to prepare them for the presidency, but after the [[fbi|FBI]] recovered government documents including classified papers at his [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago]] home in Florida, critics questioned whether Trump could be trusted with sensitive material.[n] Trump said he could get the briefings if he wanted them but sensed a trap: "I don't want them, because, number one, I know what's happening. It's very easy to see what's happening," he said, before criticizing President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[n]

Badlands commentary from [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] endorsed the refusal, arguing that the intelligence agencies had been wrong about nearly every public proclamation of the previous decade, that their propaganda had degraded into fabricated stories and anonymous quotes fed to media surrogates, and that their silence and refusal of transparency following the attempt on Trump's life discredited any claim to his trust.[n]

## August 2024 crime and law-enforcement platform

In a law-enforcement-centered speech on August 20, 2024, flanked by local Michigan sheriffs, Trump attacked Harris's "anti-police" policies while his campaign rolled out a tougher-on-crime platform that included pursuing the death penalty for child rapists and a nationwide return to [[stop-and-frisk|stop-and-frisk]] strategies.[n] He accused Harris of waging an "anti-police crusade" and of backing "extremist, pro-crime, anti-police" positions such as "defund the police," declaring, "We will never even think about defunding the police... The concept is so chilling and so horrible," and vowing, "We'll re-fund the police."[n] Trump also criticized low arrest rates under Biden and Harris, saying the administration "goes after guys like me but they don't go after people that kill people," and blamed Harris for a crime spike since he left office.[n]

Badlands read the platform as a direct threat to the [[deep-state|Deep State]], pairing Trump's promise to execute pedophiles and [[child-trafficking|child traffickers]] with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s speeches accusing Western elites of satanism and of attempting to normalize pedophilia, and framing both as evidence of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] operating on all cylinders.[n]
