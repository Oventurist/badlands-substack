## Application to the RFK Jr. censorship suit (2024)

In August 2024, District Judge [[terry-doughty|Terry Doughty]] applied the Murthy standard to allow [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]'s censorship lawsuit against the Biden administration to proceed, finding "ample evidence" of past censorship at the direction of government actors and a "substantial risk" of continued censorship.[n] Doughty described the ruling as requiring that "a court must make specific findings that a particular defendant pressured a particular platform to censor a particular topic before the platform suppressed a particular plaintiff's speech on that topic."[n] The June 2024 decision itself had held that the state and individual plaintiffs who alleged First Amendment violations from federal pressure on social media companies lacked standing to sue.[n]
