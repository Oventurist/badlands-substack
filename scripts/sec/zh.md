## Coverage of the Youngkin pardon

In September 2023 ZeroHedge reported that Virginia Governor [[glenn-youngkin|Glenn Youngkin]] had pardoned Scott Smith, the Loudoun County father convicted of obstruction of justice and disorderly conduct after confronting school officials over his daughter's rape in a school bathroom and the district's attempted cover-up.[{n}] The report was reproduced as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]]; see [[loudoun-county-school-bathroom-assault-case]].[{n}]
