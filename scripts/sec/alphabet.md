## Role in the 2023 Google antitrust trial

As Google's corporate parent, Alphabet was directly implicated in the antitrust trial that opened in September 2023 over Google's search engine dominance, described as the biggest such U.S. case since the [[microsoft|Microsoft]] litigation of the late 1990s.[{n}] Alphabet chief executive Sundar Pichai, who succeeded [[google|Google]] co-founder [[larry-page|Larry Page]] in 2019, was expected to be among the most prominent witnesses in the ten-week trial before U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta.[{n}] See [[united-states-v-google-antitrust-trial]].[{n}]
