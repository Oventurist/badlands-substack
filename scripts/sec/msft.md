## Precedent for the 2023 Google case

Microsoft's late-1990s antitrust battle over its dominance of personal computer software served as the reference point for the U.S. government's case against [[google|Google]] that went to trial in September 2023, described as the biggest American antitrust trial in roughly a quarter century.[{n}] Reporting noted that the Google case mirrored the Microsoft action in many ways, including the existential threat it posed to a renowned technology giant whose products billions of people rely on.[{n}] See [[united-states-v-google-antitrust-trial]].[{n}]
