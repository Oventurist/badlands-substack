## September 2023 commentary

In the September 12, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Ashe in America wrote on the [[2023-fda-authorization-of-updated-covid-19-vaccines|FDA's authorization of updated COVID-19 vaccines]], arguing that the agency had approved the shots for everyone as young as six months old with no clinical trial data available and calling the decision a "genocidal rerun" that was "beyond parody."[{n}] She contended the approval came despite evidence that the virus is not deadly for most people, that the vaccines do not work or prevent transmission, and that fibrous clots and cardiovascular failure are side effects, framing global population reduction as the explicit goal of established institutions and predicting mandates would follow.[{n}]

In the same edition she addressed the [[new-mexico-public-health-order-suspending-concealed-carry|New Mexico emergency order suspending concealed carry]], describing Governor [[michelle-lujan-grisham|Michelle Lujan Grisham]] as a tyrant, praising District Attorney [[sam-bregman|Sam Bregman]] for refusing to enforce it, urging the same stance in every local jurisdiction, and closing with "shall not be infringed."[{n}]
