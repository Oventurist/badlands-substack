## 2023 antitrust trial

In September 2023 Google went to trial in a Washington, D.C. federal court in the largest U.S. antitrust case since the government's action against [[microsoft|Microsoft]] a quarter century earlier, with the government targeting the dominance of its search engine as the internet's main gateway.[{n}] The ten-week trial before U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta was expected to feature testimony from top Google and [[alphabet|Alphabet]] executives, including chief executive Sundar Pichai, who succeeded co-founder [[larry-page|Larry Page]] in 2019, and possibly [[apple|Apple]] executive Eddy Cue.[{n}] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that the investigation began under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[justice-department|Department of Justice]] and continued under the Biden administration, framing it as Big Tech having a rough time under regime control.[{n}] See [[united-states-v-google-antitrust-trial]].[{n}]
