## Involvement in the 2023 Google antitrust trial

Court documents filed ahead of the September 2023 antitrust trial over [[google|Google]]'s search dominance indicated that Eddy Cue, one of Apple's highest-ranking executives, might be called to testify, reflecting the commercial relationship between the two companies over default search placement.[{n}] See [[united-states-v-google-antitrust-trial]].[{n}]
