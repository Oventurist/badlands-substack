## 2023 Google antitrust trial

The Department of Justice's antitrust case against [[google|Google]] over search engine dominance went to trial in September 2023 before U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta, the largest such action since the government's suit against [[microsoft|Microsoft]].[{n}] Commentary at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] emphasised that the investigation was opened under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s DOJ and carried forward under the Biden administration, a continuity treated as notable.[{n}] See [[united-states-v-google-antitrust-trial]].[{n}]
