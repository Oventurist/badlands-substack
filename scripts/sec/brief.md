## September 12, 2023 edition

The September 12, 2023 edition, subtitled "NATO Warmongers & Google Searches for a Way Out," covered news items from Monday, September 11 and opened with the standard framing that the news cycle is almost impossible to track fully and that the Badlands Media team hand-picks items of interest to the Truth Community, with original commentary from its citizen journalists.[{n}] Main items covered NATO's [[steadfast-defender-exercise|Steadfast Defender]] exercise, the [[2023-fda-authorization-of-updated-covid-19-vaccines|FDA's authorization of updated COVID-19 vaccines]], the opening of the [[united-states-v-google-antitrust-trial|Google antitrust trial]], and the refusal of Bernalillo County officials to enforce the [[new-mexico-public-health-order-suspending-concealed-carry|New Mexico order suspending concealed carry]].[{n}] Commentary was supplied by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].[{n}]

Bonus items reproduced [[caitlin-johnstone|Caitlin Johnstone]]'s report on [[jens-stoltenberg|Jens Stoltenberg]]'s admission regarding NATO expansion and the invasion of Ukraine, and a [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] item on Governor [[glenn-youngkin|Glenn Youngkin]]'s pardon in the [[loudoun-county-school-bathroom-assault-case|Loudoun County school bathroom assault case]].[{n}] The edition closed by inviting readers to comment and reiterating that the series would expand as the information war continues.[{n}]
