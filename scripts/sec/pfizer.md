## 2023 updated formulation

On September 11, 2023 the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] authorized an updated Pfizer COVID-19 vaccine alongside [[moderna|Moderna]]'s, approved for people aged 12 and older with emergency authorization for children from six months to 11 years.[{n}] The reformulated shot targeted the XBB.1.5 Omicron subvariant, already largely displaced by newer strains according to the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]].[{n}] See [[2023-fda-authorization-of-updated-covid-19-vaccines]].[{n}]
