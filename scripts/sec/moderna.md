## 2023 updated formulation

On September 11, 2023 the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] authorized an updated Moderna COVID-19 vaccine alongside [[pfizer|Pfizer]]'s, cleared for people aged 12 and up with emergency authorization extending to children as young as six months.[{n}] The updated shot targeted the XBB.1.5 subvariant of Omicron, which the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] said had already largely been displaced by newer strains including EG.5.[{n}] See [[2023-fda-authorization-of-updated-covid-19-vaccines]].[{n}]
