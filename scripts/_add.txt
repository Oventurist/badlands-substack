## Gaza City offensive (November 2023)

On Tuesday, November 7, 2023, Gallant issued a battlefield update announcing that for the first time Israeli troops were "in the heart" of Gaza City, declaring that "Gaza is the largest terrorist base ever built" and that "We are going to destroy Hamas."[2] He said the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] had [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]], the leader of [[hamas|Hamas]] in Gaza, surrounded in a bunker, and noted that fighting was taking place in residential areas.[2]

Gallant also appeared to reject U.S. pressure to implement a humanitarian "pause," emphasizing that "there will be no humanitarian truce without the return of the hostages."[2] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], speaking from the Kirya military headquarters in Tel Aviv, affirmed the same position, said Gaza City was surrounded, and warned Lebanon's [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] that opening a new full war front would be "the greatest mistake of its life."[2]
