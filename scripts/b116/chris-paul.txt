## Counter-narrative discussions (2023)

In the November 8, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described discussions with Chris Paul about the idea that the "Covid-23 Scariant" was effectively blunted by the mass counter-narrative deployments of the decentralized [[truth-community|Truth Community]] and the alternative media — a dynamic he then applied to the funding of proxy wars and to [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s postponed visit to Israel.[N]
