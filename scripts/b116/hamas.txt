## Gaza City encirclement (November 2023)

By November 7, 2023, Israeli Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] announced that Israeli troops were "in the heart" of Gaza City, describing Gaza as "the largest terrorist base ever built" and stating, "We are going to destroy Hamas."[N] Gallant said the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] had Hamas leader [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]] surrounded in a bunker and that fighting was underway in residential areas.[N] He rejected U.S. pressure for a humanitarian "pause," saying there would be no humanitarian truce without the return of the hostages.[N] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] said the IDF had reached deeper into Gaza than Hamas ever imagined and that pressure on the group was deepening "every hour, every day," ruling out fuel entry or a ceasefire absent the release of those kidnapped.[N]
