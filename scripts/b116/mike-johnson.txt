## November 2023 shutdown deadline

As Speaker of the House, Johnson faced a government funding deadline of November 17, 2023, with House Republicans showing no sign on November 7 of coalescing around a plan to keep federal agencies open.[N] Republican lawmakers, holding a slim 221-212 majority, met behind closed doors to debate how to structure a [[continuing-resolution|continuing resolution]].[N] Johnson described the closed-door debate as "positive," telling a news conference: "We certainly want to avoid a government shutdown. It's a dangerous time around the world right now. We recognize that," and adding, "We'll be revealing what our plan is in short order."[N]

Republicans, who said they feared being forced to back a rival funding measure from the Democratic-led [[us-senate|Senate]], expected Johnson to unveil a stopgap within days and to hold a House vote early the following week; Representative Dusty Johnson predicted the speaker would "make a play call" that the overwhelming majority of the conference would back.[N]
