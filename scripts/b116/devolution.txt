## Afghanistan aid flows cited as proof (November 2023)

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the November 2023 [[special-inspector-general-for-afghanistan-reconstruction|SIGAR]] finding that some $11 billion in U.S. aid had flowed into Afghanistan since August 2021 as evidence for Devolution, arguing that continued American funding reaching the [[taliban|Taliban]]'s orbit implied a continuing arrangement traceable to the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] rather than the improvised collapse presented publicly.[N] He described the finding as "both a Devolution Proof and a Sovereign Alliance-Regions of Responsibility Proof rolled into one."[N]
