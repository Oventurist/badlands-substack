## November 2023 funding impasse

Ten days before a shutdown deadline of November 17, 2023, House Republicans had not coalesced around a plan to keep federal agencies open, meeting behind closed doors to debate the structure of a [[continuing-resolution|continuing resolution]].[N] The conference held a slim 221-212 majority. Representative Brian Babin summarised the state of play: "Everybody's got a little bit different position, different opinions. Right now, we're cogitating about it."[N] Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] called the debate "positive" and promised to reveal a plan "in short order," while members said they feared being forced to back a rival funding measure from the Democratic-led [[us-senate|Senate]].[N] Representative Dusty Johnson predicted the speaker would "make a play call" backed by the overwhelming majority of the conference.[N]
