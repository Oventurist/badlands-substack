## Gaza City operations (November 2023)

On November 7, 2023, Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] announced that IDF troops were for the first time "in the heart" of Gaza City and that the force had [[hamas|Hamas]] leader [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]] surrounded in a bunker, with fighting taking place in residential areas.[N] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] said the IDF had been reaching deeper into Gaza than Hamas ever imagined and that Gaza City was surrounded, adding that the force was "deepening the pressure on Hamas every hour, every day."[N]
