## Rival funding measure (November 2023)

During the November 2023 government funding standoff, House Republicans told reporters they feared being forced to back a rival funding measure originating in the Democratic-led Senate, a concern that shaped their internal debate over how to structure a stopgap [[continuing-resolution|continuing resolution]] before funding expired on November 17.[N] Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] was expected to unveil a House alternative within days to pre-empt the Senate's approach.[N]
