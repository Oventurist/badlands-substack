## Financial system centralization (2023)

In March 2023, Xi created two new Chinese Communist Party committees overseeing finance, a restructuring described as bringing China's financial system under the Party's direct control.[N] Chinese state media revealed in early November 2023 that Vice Premier [[he-lifeng|He Lifeng]] had become head of both bodies, shortly before He travelled to the United States for economic talks with Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]] and ahead of a potential Xi meeting with [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] around the [[asia-pacific-economic-cooperation|APEC]] summit in San Francisco.[N]
