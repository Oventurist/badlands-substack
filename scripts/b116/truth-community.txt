## Pre-empting narrative deployments (November 2023)

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] said he and [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] had discussed the idea that the "Covid-23 Scariant" was effectively blunted by the mass counter-narrative deployments of the decentralized Truth Community and the alternative media.[N] He argued that if "the Hegelian Deployments of the Deep State are preceded, they can be cut off at the knees before they can gain a foothold in the trauma-induced reactivity centers of the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]]."[N] He applied the same logic to the postponed Israel visit of Ukrainian president [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], contending the trip was cancelled not because plans leaked but because of the "Narrative Whiplash" those plans caused in a rapidly awakening American population — a demonstration, in his account, of the community's ability to help stop the funding of one proxy war while globalists attempted to spin up another.[N]
