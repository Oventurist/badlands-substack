## Response to Israeli "nuke Gaza" remark (November 2023)

On Tuesday, November 7, 2023, Zakharova said in a televised interview that remarks by Israeli Minister of Heritage [[amihai-eliyahu|Amihai Eliyahu]] — who described dropping a nuclear bomb on the Gaza Strip as "a possibility" — raised a multitude of questions.[N] "It raised a great number of questions," she said. "Question number one: Does this mean we are hearing an official statement acknowledging [Israel's] possession of nuclear weapons? Accordingly, the next set of questions that everyone has is: Where are the international organizations, including the IAEA [[[international-atomic-energy-agency|International Atomic Energy Agency]]]; where are the inspectors?"[N] Her remarks, carried by TASS and reported by The Cradle, highlighted Israel's status as the world's only unacknowledged atomic power, having never signed the [[nuclear-non-proliferation-treaty|Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty]] nor submitted to inspection by the U.N. nuclear watchdog.[N]
