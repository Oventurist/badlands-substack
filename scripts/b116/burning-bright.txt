## Contributions to News Brief #116 (November 2023)

On [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s postponed Israel trip, he wrote that he and [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] had discussed how the "Covid-23 Scariant" was blunted by counter-narrative deployments from the decentralized [[truth-community|Truth Community]] and alternative media, and concluded that Zelensky cancelled not because his plans leaked but because of the "Narrative Whiplash" they caused in an awakening American population.[N]

On the [[special-inspector-general-for-afghanistan-reconstruction|SIGAR]] finding of $11 billion in post-withdrawal aid to Afghanistan, he credited [[just-human|Just Human]] with linking the Kabul withdrawal to the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] and described the ongoing flows as "both a [[devolution|Devolution]] Proof and a Sovereign Alliance-Regions of Responsibility Proof rolled into one."[N]
