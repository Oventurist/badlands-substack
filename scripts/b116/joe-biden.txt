## Coalition splintering over Israel and the economy (November 2023)

By November 2023, Biden's Democratic coalition was reported to be splintering over Israel and the economy, with two Democratic House members among his most visible problems.[N] Activists circulated a message declaring, "Joe Biden supported the genocide of the Palestinian people. The American people won't forget. Biden, support a ceasefire now or don't count on us in 2024."[N] In response, the pro-Israel group [[democratic-majority-for-israel|Democratic Majority for Israel]] released a six-figure television ad in Detroit attacking Rep. [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]] over her cease-fire calls and criticism of the Israeli government, citing her 2021 vote against replenishing Israel's [[iron-dome|Iron Dome]] and her vote against an October 2023 resolution standing with Israel.[N]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Biden faced a real problem because activist factions on the left were firmly on the side of Palestine while his administration pushed support for Israel, and that his team's history of enabling revolution in the Middle East was in focus.[N] She contrasted this with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated position — "I want people to stop dying" — arguing that diplomacy was possible but not achievable under the current American leadership.[N]
