## Contributions to News Brief #116 (November 2023)

Ashe in America supplied commentary for the November 8, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], "A Splintered Establishment & Tension Tightropes."[N] On the fracturing of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s coalition over Israel, she wrote that activist factions on the left were firmly on the side of Palestine while the administration pushed support for Israel, and that "the emerging fissures in traditional political coalitions in America are too great to ignore."[N] She invoked [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s line, "I want people to stop dying," arguing that diplomacy was possible but not achievable with the current American leadership: "Peace or bust."[N]

On the death of [[paul-kessler|Paul Kessler]] at duelling protests in Thousand Oaks, she called the killing a tragedy, warned that protests turning violent in America was "a red flag — and a sign of things to come," and criticized the media's handling of the case as narrative shaping, urging readers not to let the government gaslight them that the death was an accident.[N]
