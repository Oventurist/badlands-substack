## Address to the nation (November 7, 2023)

Speaking from the Kirya military headquarters in Tel Aviv on November 7, 2023, Netanyahu addressed the nation to update Israelis on the war, saying: "In the south, the war is moving forward with force that [[hamas|Hamas]] has never seen. Gaza City is surrounded. We are operating within it, we are deepening the pressure on Hamas every hour, every day."[N] He said the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] had been reaching deeper into Gaza than Hamas ever imagined, and declared: "There will be no entry of fuel into Gaza or a ceasefire without the return of the kidnapped people."[N] He also warned Lebanon's [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] that opening a new full-on war front would be "the greatest mistake of its life."[N]

The speech affirmed the position set out the same day by Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]], who reported that Israeli troops were "in the heart" of Gaza City and that Hamas leader [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]] was surrounded in a bunker; ZeroHedge framed the pair of statements as contradicting U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken over post-war control of Gaza and humanitarian pauses.[N]
