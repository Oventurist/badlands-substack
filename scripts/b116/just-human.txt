## Kabul withdrawal analysis cited (2023)

In the November 8, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that he began following Just Human in August 2021 not only for his calm, reasoned takes on the Kabul Withdrawal and the ensuing disaster — both for troops on the ground and narratively for the Biden administration — but because of the way he connected the chaos to the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[N] In that reading, the agreement promised cooperation with the [[taliban|Taliban]] to create a de facto Region of Responsibility permitting U.S. troops to withdraw from a war zone crafted under the Bush administration and perfected under the Obama administration.[N]
