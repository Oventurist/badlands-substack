## Post-withdrawal aid as claimed evidence (2023)

Badlands commentary treated the continuing flow of U.S. money into Afghanistan as circumstantial support for the Doha framework's persistence beyond the Kabul withdrawal. A November 2023 report by [[john-sopko|John Sopko]] of [[special-inspector-general-for-afghanistan-reconstruction|SIGAR]] found that roughly $11 billion in U.S. aid had reached Afghanistan since August 2021, in cash shipments of about $80 million every 10 to 14 days.[N] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] credited [[just-human|Just Human]] with connecting the chaos of the Kabul withdrawal to the Doha Agreement signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], which he described as promising cooperation with the [[taliban|Taliban]] to create a de facto Region of Responsibility allowing U.S. troops to leave the "Forever Warzone."[N] He argued that the U.S. military equipment left in Kabul was subsequently used by the Taliban to eradicate most of what remained of ISIS in the country, consistent with the deal's original structure despite its supposed voiding, and read the ongoing funding as both a [[devolution|Devolution]] proof and a "Sovereign Alliance–Regions of Responsibility" proof.[N]
