## Memetic penetration of the Normie Layers (November 2023)

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the November 2023 meeting between Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]] and Chinese Vice Premier [[he-lifeng|He Lifeng]] to describe how stories enter the Collective Mind.[N] Recalling media accounts of Yellen unknowingly consuming psychoactive mushroom soup during an earlier trip, he suggested such episodes are "Ghosts in the Machine" — fake or planted stories that make the Establishment look cartoonishly inept, evil or foolish.[N] He argued that reversing a humiliation ritual on Deep State controllers is secondary to the primary function of such narratives, which is "to force an otherwise-ignored, mundane political or economic story into the awareness of the Collective Mind by transforming it into a meme."[N] The most memetic political deployments, he wrote, tend to mix the strange, the humorous and the oddly threatening, and in the modern world this is "the only way to get the Normie Layers of the Collective Mind to engage with the truth of their would-be leaders."[N]

He applied the same framework to narrative pre-emption, arguing that Deep State deployments can be cut off before gaining "a foothold in the trauma-induced reactivity centers of the Collective Mind."[N]
