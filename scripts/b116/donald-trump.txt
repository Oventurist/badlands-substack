## "I want people to stop dying" (2023)

Amid the Israel–Hamas war in November 2023, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] quoted Trump's statement, "I want people to stop dying," in arguing that diplomacy remained possible but was not achievable under the leadership of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[N] Badlands commentary in the same edition also revisited Trump's [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] with the [[taliban|Taliban]], which [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described as having promised cooperation to establish a de facto Region of Responsibility permitting the withdrawal of U.S. troops from Afghanistan.[N]
