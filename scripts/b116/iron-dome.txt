## 2021 replenishment vote and the 2023 backlash

A 2021 bill providing $1 billion to replenish Israel's Iron Dome missile defense system, passed by the House after a progressive dispute, became a political marker in the following years.[N] In November 2023, [[democratic-majority-for-israel|Democratic Majority for Israel]] cited Rep. [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]]'s vote against that replenishment bill — alongside her vote against an October 2023 resolution standing with Israel — in a six-figure television advertisement attacking her in her Detroit-area district.[N]
