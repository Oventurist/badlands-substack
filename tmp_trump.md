## Trump in the "Mirrored Quadrant" (December 2022)

The Badlands Media essay "Mirror, Mirror" (December 14, 2022) treated [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as one leg of a "Trinity" of sovereign leaders alongside [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], analysed through four shared attributes the author called a Mirrored Quadrant.[N] It dated the Deep State's decision to go "ALL IN" in defence of its system to the moment "a certain Orange Man came riding down a golden escalator."[N]

On populist rise, the essay described Trump as the first American leader — perhaps the first major world leader — in decades to wage a narrative war on the globalist class, breaking from decades of subversive and administrative tradition by extolling independence, strength and idealism in a manner closely mirroring Presidents Reagan and Kennedy.[N] That rise was said to have awoken a sleeping giant in the American populace, one tickled and ultimately tricked by the Bush Jr. administration and demoralized under [[barack-obama|Obama]]; Trump did not create the preconditions of sovereignty in the American zeitgeist but tapped a well already gushing beneath the surface.[N]

On military backing, the piece noted that Trump has no public military background although he attended the New York Military Academy in the 1960s, and that he tapped the same military and nationalist base Bush Jr. had subverted.[N] It argued that Trump recalled Kennedy's cabinet appointments and civilian advisory councils by leaning heavily on his support within the US military, specifically the [[united-states-marine-corps|Marines]], and that his inauguration speech — delivered ringed by Marines — was widely read in this community as a shot across the bow to the collectivists.[N]

On the war against the oligarchy, the essay credited Trump with breaking from the tradition of leaders who wear national pride as a "subversive skin suit" en route to Forever Wars enriching the oligarchic class, instead going to war on a narrative level with the US political class and using the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] to boost his own messaging while putting corporate and political cronies on the defensive.[N] The result claimed for this campaign was the steady and complete exposure of a plurality of the American elite, as Fortune 100 companies and opposition and intra-party officials alike closed ranks against a man and a movement they considered an outsider threat to their systemic illusion.[N] Against the globalists, Trump's contribution was framed as highlighting the inherent weakness and corruption of the globalist-aligned system to a steadily waking American public.[N]
