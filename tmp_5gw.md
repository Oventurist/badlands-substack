## A war across every system ("Mirror, Mirror," December 2022)

The Badlands Media essay "Mirror, Mirror" (December 14, 2022) described "Information War" as only one of many names for a conflict that spans virtually every system and function of global society — economic, financial, kinetic and psychological — and characterised it as a war waged both for and against the individual's mind, bank account, economic and social power, and soul.[N] The author held that the movement is not on the cusp of World War III but already in it, potentially for decades, with the conflict intensifying once Donald Trump entered politics and provoked the [[deep-state|Deep State]] to mount a total defence of a "System of Systems" that is more brittle and vulnerable than commonly believed.[N]

The essay advocated "Macro-level thinking" and "Directional thinking" applied to both Actual and Potential/Narrative Events and Deployments as the method for seeing through surface appearances, and framed the whole conflict as a "War of Stories" layered over a real financial, kinetic and psychological war fought in more theatres than any single analyst can parse.[N] Its author warned that observers who read events as a straightforward ideological, fiscal and sometimes kinetic contest between East and West — taking in the devolution of the US political and economic system, the toothless bureaucracy of the [[european-union|European Union]], engineered social movements, the war in Ukraine and a trade war with China — would miss the forest for the trees.[N] Leaders in this war were described as both real and unreal at once, playing assigned or self-assumed roles and sometimes hemmed in on the game board by better players with longer-term strategies.[N]
