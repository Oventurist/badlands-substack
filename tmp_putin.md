## Putin in the "Mirrored Quadrant" (December 2022)

The Badlands Media essay "Mirror, Mirror" (December 14, 2022) placed [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] within a "Trinity" of sovereign leaders alongside [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], analysed through four mirrored attributes.[N] The author's earlier [[righteous-russia|Righteous Russia]] series was cited for the claim that Putin's populist rise echoed Trump's, the Russian people being no strangers to communist infiltration, Marxist subversion and Soviet subjugation.[N]

The essay noted that Putin possessed a far more extensive military career than Trump — "that we know of" — and made comparable appointments on taking the Russian presidency in what it called a shock rise in the late 1990s, ushering in an era of Russian sovereignty and nationalism not seen in a full century, since the forced ending of Czar Nicholas and his family.[N]

On the oligarchy, the article stressed that Putin's rise did not come directly on the heels of the Soviet collapse but after a decade of faux-capitalist rule in which the means of Soviet and Russian production, money lending and favours were split among what it called perhaps the most infamous oligarchy in the world, leaving ordinary Russians worse off than under communism as they traded Marxist masters for corporate and energy overlords.[N] Putin vowed to end this crony "inside baseball" and, following his installation as president, spent the first decade of the new millennium waging legal and sometimes kinetic war on the oligarchs, steadily transferring their corporations, businesses and assets back to the Russian people, while bureaucrats in Brussels and Washington painted him a threat to "their Democracy."[N]

Against the globalists, the piece described Putin's approach as far less subtle than Trump's, holding that he had effectively neutered the globalist system on the world stage through what it called his shocking, effective and ultimately successful Special Military Operation in the "proxy state" of [[ukraine|Ukraine]], while powers on his borders threatened and sneered but remained rooted in powerless inaction.[N]
