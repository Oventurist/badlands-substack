# 2025 Israeli Strike on Doha

The 2025 Israeli strike on Doha refers to Israeli missile and bomb attacks carried out on September 9, 2025 against the Qatari capital, Doha, targeting figures associated with [[hamas|Hamas]].[1] The attack on the territory of a sovereign Gulf state produced a sharp increase in military-political tensions across the Middle East and drew condemnation from Russia and Gulf partners.[1]

## Russian condemnation

Speaking on September 11, 2025 at a Russia–[[gulf-cooperation-council|Gulf Cooperation Council]] Strategic Dialogue session in Sochi, Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] said the strike demonstrated Israel's unwillingness to end the humanitarian catastrophe in Gaza and warned it would further destabilize the region.[1] He noted that the meeting was taking place "against the backdrop of a sharp increase in military-political tensions in the Middle East due to Israeli missile and bomb attacks on Doha, the capital of [[qatar|Qatar]], on September 9," which Russia had received with deep concern.[1]

Lavrov characterized the attack as "a gross violation of international law, primarily the UN Charter, an encroachment on the sovereignty and territorial integrity of an independent state," and "a step leading to further destabilization of the situation in the Middle East."[1] He emphasized Qatar's role as a key mediator in the indirect negotiations between Israel and Hamas over ceasefire terms in the Gaza Strip, calling it a particular cynicism that a principal mediator had been struck, and said the Israeli action unambiguously showed an intent to undermine international peace efforts.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the episode as Russia beginning to confront Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] over Israeli conduct, and asked what [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and Lavrov would do to help Qatari Emir [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Sheikh Tamim]], Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], and UAE President [[mohammed-bin-zayed|Mohammed bin Zayed]] navigate what he called a quagmire of unbridled hubris.[1]

## Trump-Al-Thani meeting

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] planned to meet Qatari Prime Minister [[mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani|Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al-Thani]] in New York on the Friday following the strike, a White House official said without elaborating on timing or agenda.[2] Israel had attempted to kill the political leaders of [[hamas]] in the Tuesday attack, a strike that risked derailing US-backed efforts to broker a truce in Gaza and end the nearly two-year-old conflict, and that was widely condemned across the Middle East and beyond as an escalation in an already tense region.[2]

Trump said he was unhappy with the strike, describing it as a unilateral action that did not advance US or Israeli interests, while Washington continued to count [[qatar|Qatar]] as a strong Gulf ally and a principal mediator in ceasefire negotiations, hostage releases, and post-conflict planning for Gaza.[2] Al-Thani blamed Israel for trying to sabotage chances for peace but said Qatar would not be deterred from its mediating role.[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Trump had already ordered [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] to draft a defense agreement with Qatar granting it military protection in the event of another Israeli strike, suggesting that was why Rubio had been "summoned to Israel like a dog."[2]
