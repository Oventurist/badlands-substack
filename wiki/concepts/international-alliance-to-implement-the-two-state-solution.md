# International Alliance to Implement the Two-State Solution

The International Alliance to Implement the Two-State Solution is a multinational diplomatic grouping formed in 2024 to press for the establishment of a Palestinian state. It was unveiled in September 2024 on the sidelines of the United Nations General Assembly and brings together nations from the Middle East, Europe and beyond.[1]

## Riyadh inaugural meeting

[[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] hosted the alliance's first meeting in Riyadh on Wednesday, October 30, 2024.[1] Saudi Foreign Minister [[faisal-bin-farhan|Prince Faisal bin Farhan]] said nearly 90 "states and international organizations" were taking part in the two-day gathering.[1]

Speaking at the meeting, Faisal said, "A genocide is happening with the goal of evicting the Palestinian people from their land, which Saudi Arabia rejects," describing the humanitarian situation as "catastrophic" and denouncing the "complete blockade" of northern Gaza.[1] Israel has rejected accusations of genocide, stating that the aim of its ongoing operation is to eliminate [[hamas|Hamas]] and to rescue the 251 hostages kidnapped on October 7, 2023, an assault in which some 1,200 people were killed.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands Media writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the Riyadh meeting as evidence that "the world is coming together in a very literal way to unite against the forces of evil—the globalist hegemony," asking how soon the grouping would be called the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[1] He placed Saudi Arabia at "the tip of the spear" and credited the rise of Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] with averting what he suggested would otherwise have been a far more violent global conflict, while predicting further Middle East escalation culminating with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] back in office.[1] The same commentary suggested Trump had delivered an ultimatum to Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]
