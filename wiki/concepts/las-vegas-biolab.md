# Las Vegas Illegal Biolab

The Las Vegas illegal biolab was an unlicensed biological laboratory discovered in a single-family rental home in east Las Vegas in late January and early February 2026.[1]

## Discovery

The [[fbi|FBI]] and local police searched the property and found lab equipment, test kits, vaccines, influenza samples and other biological materials stored in a locked garage.[1] Hundreds of vials of rare and highly infectious disease specimens were reported to have been stored haphazardly, in some accounts simply shoved into an old refrigerator.[1] Multiple people with access to the property became "deathly ill" after exposure, and the house cleaner — herself among the sick — said the place reeked of "foul stagnant hospital air."[1]

## Principals

The property was managed by [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]], an Israeli citizen on a non-immigrant visa who managed 37 properties in the area and who hired the cleaner and allowed others to live in the house.[1] It was owned through an LLC by Chinese national [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]], who hired Solomon and who was separately convicted over the [[universal-meditech|Universal Meditech]] operation in California.[1] Materials found in the garage may have been connected to Zhu's California work.[1]

## Legal outcomes

Solomon was arrested in February 2026 on a federal charge of illegal firearm possession, having six firearms while on a nonimmigrant visa, but was released pending trial by federal magistrate [[elayna-youchah|Elayna J. Youchah]].[1] In May 2026 federal prosecutors under [[sigal-chattah|Sigal Chattah]] dismissed the complaint without prejudice in the interests of justice, leaving only a pending Clark County charge for improper disposal of hazardous waste with a status hearing set for June 4.[1]

The episode was presented by Badlands as highlighting overlapping problems of improper biological material storage and fraudulent medical-device activity across state lines, and — in [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s commentary — as an example of foreign nationals, particularly Israeli citizens, escaping consequence within the U.S. justice system.[1]
