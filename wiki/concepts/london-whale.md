# London Whale

The "London Whale" was a 2012 trading episode in which [[jp-morgan|JP Morgan]] absorbed roughly $6 billion in losses on a large credit derivatives position.[1]

## Course of events

As recounted by Badlands Media, [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]] of [[saba-capital|Saba Capital]] discovered that JP Morgan was shorting a particular investment class — that is, betting its value would fall.[1] Weinstein accumulated a position in the same instruments and then, at an investment conference hosted by JP Morgan itself, encouraged attending investors to buy them.[1] The resulting demand drove prices upward and produced a short squeeze against the bank, generating a multi-billion-dollar loss for JP Morgan and a large profit for Weinstein.[1]

## Significance

The article uses the episode to illustrate two claims: that mechanically the trade resembled the later [[gamestop|GameStop]] short squeeze, and that it demonstrates how [[deep-state|Deep State]]-aligned financial actors will inflict enormous losses on one another when it is profitable to do so.[1]
