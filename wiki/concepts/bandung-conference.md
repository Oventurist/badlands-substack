# Bandung Conference

The Bandung Conference of 1955 was the Asian–African gathering from which the Five Principles of Peaceful Coexistence emerged.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Legacy

Badlands Media commentary states that the Five Principles of Peaceful Coexistence articulated at Bandung set the tone for the Non-Aligned Movement.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) These principles are grouped with [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]]'s [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]], the Atlantic Charter, the [[united-nations|UN Charter]] and the UN Declaration of Human Rights of 1947 as expressions of the same universal freedoms.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) The author presents this lineage as the model for the cooperative architecture now being advanced by the states gathered at the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|SCO]] summit.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)
