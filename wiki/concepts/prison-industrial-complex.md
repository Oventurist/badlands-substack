# Prison Industrial Complex

The **prison industrial complex** is the term used by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] for the multi-billion-dollar annual industry that bloomed in the United States alongside the [[war-on-drugs|War on Drugs]].[1]

As billions of dollars were funnelled into law enforcement, military operations, and international partnerships aimed at cartels and traffickers, private incarceration grew into a substantial commercial sector; DeLarme directs readers to the published revenue reports of the operators CoreCivic and the GEO Group as evidence of its scale.[1]

In DeLarme's framing the domestic side of the drug war was never about protecting Main Street but about "control, cash, and chaos," with the result at home being "prisons packed, communities gutted, and plenty of funds to facilitate god knows what."[1] He argues that the persistence of drug flows despite vast [[pentagon|Pentagon]] interdiction resources reflects a deeper agenda: maintaining a perpetual war to justify budgets and control.[1]
