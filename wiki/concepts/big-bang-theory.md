# Big Bang Theory

The **Big Bang Theory** is the mainstream cosmological model holding that the observable universe originated from an initial singularity. In the January 24, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Who Says Free Energy Isn't Real?", the [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] author used the theory as the principal target of a critique of mainstream science, arguing that its unexamined assumptions conceal the logical basis for [[free-energy|free energy]].[1]

## Objections raised in the essay

The author posed a series of what he called common-sense questions: how it was possible for all the laws of physics to spring into existence at once via the Big Bang; according to what conditions and laws the initial explosion could have taken place; and how the material and logic of existence could emerge from genuine randomness in which nothing legitimately existed.[1] He argued that it is impossible to explain existence without accounting for how non-extended, non-material "nothing" becomes both the physical universe — which he termed the *matterium* — and the set of laws governing it.[1]

He concluded that mainstream science cannot be entirely correct about the Big Bang and that a more fundamental origin must be sought than the singularity itself, asking where that singularity came from, assuming it exists at all.[1] If the universe truly came from nothing, he argued, the universe as a whole is by definition its own free energy source.[1]

## Framing as suppressed inquiry

The author suspected that the question of the singularity's origin is rarely addressed because answering it would reveal how free energy works, and asserted that dogmatic academic science will never produce a genuine model of the emergence of the universe because its funders do not want humanity empowered with accurate information about the true nature of existence.[1] He placed the Big Bang alongside the Theory of Evolution as fundamental assumptions of mainstream science now being widely questioned by the public, and predicted that further assumptions would receive the same treatment as awareness spreads.[1]
