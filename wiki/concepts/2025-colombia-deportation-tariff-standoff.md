# 2025 Colombia Deportation Tariff Standoff

The 2025 Colombia deportation tariff standoff was a brief confrontation on Sunday, January 26, 2025 between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] over [[colombia|Colombia]]'s refusal to accept deportation flights of its nationals from the United States.[1]

## Course of events

After Petro attempted to go head to head with Trump on the issue, Trump moved to impose "emergency" tariffs of up to 50 percent on Colombian goods and imposed visa sanctions and enhanced inspection requirements on Colombian travelers.[1] Petro continued to protest briefly but capitulated within hours, even offering his own presidential plane to transport deportees.[1]

Commentary in the New York Post noted that the resolution required no U.S. promises to address Colombia's underlying social problems — which range from traditional gangs to narco-terror armies — and no handouts or special treatment from Washington; making clear that refusing repatriations would carry real economic and political consequences, and then allowing those consequences to take effect, produced total capitulation.[1] The episode was presented as exposing a flaw in what the Post called the Biden-Harris theory of illegal immigration, under which America is at fault for border crossings and must bribe origin nations with trade deals and "root causes" spending.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary argued that Trump was not changing anything that could not have been changed in his first term; what differed was the social permission and mandate he now held, illustrating the view that politics is fundamentally a "War of Stories" and that Trump had the winning story.[1]
