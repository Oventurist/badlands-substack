# Executive Order 13899

Executive Order 13899 is an order on combating anti-Semitism issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on 11 December 2019.[1] It became a focus of Badlands Media commentary in 2025 because a subsequent Trump order, issued 29 January 2025, explicitly affirms and strengthens it.[1]

## Content emphasized by Badlands

The Badlands article "On Infiltration and Israel" directs readers to look closely at the text of EO 13899, arguing that the order explicitly reaffirms the primacy of the First Amendment.[1] On that basis the author rejects the widely circulated claim that Trump's anti-Semitism orders strip Americans of free speech rights.[1]

## The 2025 follow-on order

The January 2025 order, described by the author as extremely short, states that it "shall be implemented consistent with applicable law and subject to the availability of appropriations" — a line the article locates in section 4(ii)(b).[1] Having read it, the author says he cannot see how the free-speech-abolition narrative holds water.[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands speculates that the true aim of the 2025 order is to target criminal and terror cells believed to be behind the anti-Israel protests that erupted on college campuses after 7 October 2023.[1] The author frames this as Trump using the cabal's own weapons against it: because opponents made all criticism of Israel into a hate-speech issue, Trump can use the "boogieman" of [[antisemitism|antisemitism]] to pass measures aimed at criminal groups operating within universities.[1] The article further wonders aloud how many right-wing influencers promoting the free-speech alarm are directly or indirectly funded by [[mossad|Mossad]].[1]
