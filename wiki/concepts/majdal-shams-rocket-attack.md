# Majdal Shams Rocket Attack

The Majdal Shams rocket attack was a strike on a soccer field in the [[druze|Druze]] village of Majdal Shams that killed twelve people, all aged between ten and twenty years old.[1] Attributed to [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], it was the deadliest attack on civilians since the October 7 onslaught by [[hamas|Hamas]], and it sparked fears of still greater escalation or even all-out war in northern Israel.[1]

## Effect on Netanyahu's American visit

The strike occurred at the end of Israeli prime minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s July 2024 trip to the United States, which had included [[2024-netanyahu-address-to-congress|his address to a joint session of Congress]], meetings with [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], and a visit to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]].[1] Netanyahu's return home, scheduled for the Saturday evening, was moved forward by a few hours in response to the attack.[1]

## Contested framing

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treated the attack as a claim rather than an established fact, noting that he had not personally witnessed it and that mass-casualty events have been staged before.[1] Writing that it was "not beyond the realm of possibility" that the rocket blast was staged in order to justify the [[assassination-of-ismail-haniyeh|killing of Hamas's leader in Iran]] five days after the Mar-a-Lago meeting, Carlson stopped short of asserting that the attack did not occur, arguing instead that ordinary observers lack the facts necessary to reach a final determination about any of the events in the sequence.[1] The conventional framing — that Israel killed the Hamas leader in retaliation for the deaths of twelve young people on an Israeli soccer field — was, in his account, one of the "narratives" the public is invited to accept without verification.[1]
