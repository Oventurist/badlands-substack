# Q Post 189

**Q post 189** is a message from the [[qanon|Q]] drops in which readers are told to follow the "Y/Owl" symbolism across the world. It is treated in part five of [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series as a riddle that the series' linguistic research may help solve.[1]

## The riddle

The article states that in post 189 Q told readers to follow the "Y/Owl" symbolism, and notes that the owl was one of the [[illuminati|Illuminati]]'s symbols.[1] The author's proposed key is the ancient German term for "Holy Owl," *Wai Ulu*, a discovery he credits to [[frederick-dodson|Frederick Dodson]].[1] He asks whether anyone has yet decoded the significance of the "Y," suggests the *wai* of *wai ulu* as a candidate, and allows the alternative that the "Y" is intended to depict two horns.[1]

## Global scope

The author places particular weight on the instruction's geographic reach: Q's directive to find the Y/Owl all over the world strongly suggests that the phenomenon in question must be global, since there would otherwise be no reason to say "everywhere."[1] Because the [[ancient-german-language-thesis|ancient German connection]] is on his account precisely a worldwide phenomenon, he treats the post as consistent with — and possibly pointing toward — that research.[1] The essay opens by stating that in the end it hopes to have made some progress solving one of Q's riddles, making post 189 the article's stated destination.[1] A fuller treatment of the argument appears at [[y-owl-symbolism|Y/Owl symbolism]].[1]
