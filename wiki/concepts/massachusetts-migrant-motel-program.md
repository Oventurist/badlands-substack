# Massachusetts Migrant Motel Program

The Massachusetts migrant motel program refers to the arrangement under Governor [[maura-healey|Maura Healey]] by which the Commonwealth housed newly arrived migrants in state-funded motels during 2023, an arrangement that drew criticism in July 2023 when reporting indicated that the state's wealthiest communities were excluded from hosting duties.[1]

## Scope and cost

In response to an inquiry from state Rep. [[peter-durant|Peter Durant]], Healey's office said nearly 40 motels were then being used by the state to house new arrivals, but refused to give taxpayers a cost estimate.[1] Durant framed the issue as one of fiscal transparency, asking how many of the roughly 1,300 families involved were in the country illegally.[1]

## Geographic distribution

Boston Herald columnist [[howie-carr|Howie Carr]] reported that among the two dozen towns and cities hosting state-funded migrant motels, nearly none of the Massachusetts "millionaire destinations" — Nantucket, Martha's Vineyard, Cambridge or Newburyport — appeared.[1] Coverage of Carr's column by [[breitbart]] described the pattern as Healey sparing affluent enclaves from absorbing illegal aliens living at taxpayer expense.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the program to illustrate what she called establishment hypocrisy on immigration — a ruling class that demands open borders as a humanitarian posture while expecting small-town and middle America to absorb unvetted arrivals.[1] The commentary invoked the earlier Martha's Vineyard episode, in which the island declared an emergency over a small number of migrant arrivals despite abundant empty vacation homes and rentals, and quoted former Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]]'s remark, "Why are you shipping these immigrants up north? We need them to pick the crops down here."[1]
