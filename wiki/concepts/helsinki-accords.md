# Helsinki Accords

The Helsinki Accords are a 1970s multilateral agreement that eased Cold War tensions in Europe by binding rival blocs to a common framework of security, economic cooperation and human rights commitments.[1]

## Proposed Middle East application

In May 2026, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] floated the idea of a Middle East non-aggression pact with [[iran|Iran]] as part of postwar planning discussions with allies once the US-Israeli conflict with Tehran ends, according to Financial Times reporting.[1] Diplomats said Riyadh pointed to the Helsinki Accords as a possible framework for reducing regional conflict and expanding economic cooperation.[1]

Arab diplomats said most Muslim and Arab nations would likely support the concept, though including [[israel|Israel]] remained a major obstacle as regional anger over Israeli military operations in Gaza, [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and [[syria|Syria]] continued to grow.[1] The proposal was reported alongside Saudi efforts to deepen coordination with [[pakistan|Pakistan]], [[turkey|Turkey]] and Egypt, and a Pakistani proposal to add [[qatar|Qatar]] and Turkey to the [[saudi-pakistan-defense-pact|Saudi-Pakistan defense pact]] to build a broader regional economic and military alliance less dependent on outside powers.[1]
