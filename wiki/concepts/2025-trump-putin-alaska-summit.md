# 2025 Trump–Putin Alaska Summit

The 2025 Trump–Putin Alaska summit was a planned meeting between US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in Alaska, announced in August 2025 to discuss a potential ceasefire in Ukraine.[1]

## Announcement

Trump announced the summit at the [[white-house|White House]] on Friday, August 8, 2025, saying the conflict "could be solved very soon."[1] The announcement fell on the deadline Trump had imposed on Putin to finalize a peace deal or face potential financial penalties.[1] The meeting, scheduled for the following Friday, marked the first time the leaders of the two countries had held talks since 2021.[1]

## Territorial dispute

Prospects for the negotiations were quickly thrown into doubt after Trump suggested that Ukraine would have to cede territory to Russia as part of any peace deal.[1] Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] pointedly rejected any arrangement involving the transfer of Ukrainian territory, saying in a Saturday video statement on Telegram that "The answer to Ukraine's territorial question is already in the constitution of Ukraine" and that "Ukrainians will not gift their land to the occupier."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that the summit touched "the original narrative that launched Burning Bright," and that events might be entering the beginning stages of a final act, asking what was so significant about Trump, Putin and the Bering Strait and suggesting that Trump "might be known as the Peacemaker when all is said and done … but he won't be the only one."[1]

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued on *Devolution Power Hour* that European leaders were meeting "to figure out how to make peace between Trump and Putin impossible," framing the conflict not as Russia versus Ukraine but as "the global regime vs. sovereign powers like the U.S. and Russia," and contending that peace talks conducted without Ukraine prove its borders are not sovereign.[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the summit as a checkpoint for reading South Caucasus politics, suggesting the behavior of [[nikol-pashinyan|Nikol Pashinyan]] and [[ilham-aliyev|Ilham Aliyev]] over the [[zangezur-corridor|Zangezur corridor]] after the meeting would be telling.[1]
