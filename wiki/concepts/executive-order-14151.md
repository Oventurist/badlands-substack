# Executive Order 14151

Executive Order 14151 is a presidential directive signed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 20, 2025, ending federal Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) programs and halting what the order characterises as ideological conditioning within the federal government.[1]

## Provisions

According to the White House, EO 14151 effectively dismantles DEI mandates across federal departments, requiring the termination of all such programs and redirecting training and promotion practices toward individual merit.[1] The order states that federal employment practices, including federal employee performance reviews, "shall reward individual initiative, skills, performance, and hard work and shall not under any circumstances consider DEI or DEIA factors, goals, policies, mandates, or requirements."[1]

## Implementation

The shift prompted concrete administrative actions, including the Department of Education placing employees on leave and revising federal contracts to exclude DEI considerations.[1] Legal challenges subsequently blocked some provisions on a temporary basis.[1]

## Reception

Critics described the order as regressive and warned that its aggressive scope could curtail legitimate DEI efforts, a concern voiced by civil rights organisations including the [[american-civil-liberties-union|ACLU]] and [[human-rights-watch|Human Rights Watch]].[1] Supporters characterised it as a legal scalpel cutting away bureaucratic indoctrination they trace to Marxist ideology.[1]

## Place in the legal architecture

Badlands Media commentary groups EO 14151 with [[executive-order-13818|EO 13818]], [[executive-order-13848|EO 13848]], and [[executive-order-13912|EO 13912]] as the executive instruments that collectively dismantled the legal scaffolding of the entrenched administrative state and returned jurisdiction to constitutional actors.[1] In this account the order is one component of a broader legal counteroffensive against the [[deep-state|Deep State]] that also encompassed the restructuring of the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]].[1]
