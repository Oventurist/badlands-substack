# 2024 Bolivian Coup Attempt

The 2024 Bolivian coup attempt was a brief military action against the government of President [[luis-arce|Luis Arce]] on Wednesday, June 26, 2024, led by disgruntled former army chief [[juan-jose-zuniga|Juan José Zúñiga]].[1]

## Events

Bolivians were first set on edge by armoured vehicles circling the Plaza Murillo in historic downtown La Paz, and the sense of collective confusion and shock reached fever pitch as troops marched on the presidential palace.[1] By 2:30pm a small tank was ramming the gates of the neoclassical Palacio Quemado; troops forced their way inside and Zúñiga faced off against Arce, who ordered him to withdraw his troops.[1] The confrontation was filmed, lasted several minutes, and ended with Zúñiga leaving and speeding away in a bulletproof army vehicle.[1]

## Self-coup allegations

Arce was accused of plotting the coup against himself to boost his popularity.[1] Badlands Media commentary treated the episode as an example of a regime staging an attack on itself in order to psyop the population into sympathizing with it, comparing the tactic to what the [[cia|CIA]] is said to understand better than anyone: the importance of optics.[1]
