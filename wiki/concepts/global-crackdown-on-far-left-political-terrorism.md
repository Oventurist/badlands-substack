# Global Crackdown on Far-Left Political Terrorism (2026)

In July 2026 Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] convened officials from more than 65 countries in Washington to launch a new international initiative focused on combating transnational far-left political terrorism.[1] The Trump administration alleged that violent left-wing extremist networks had become a growing global security threat and announced plans to expand international cooperation, terrorist designations, sanctions and visa restrictions.[1]

White House Deputy Chief of Staff [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]] said the administration was entering an "action phase" pairing diplomatic, law enforcement and financial tools to target organizations that use violence or intimidation to advance political objectives.[1] Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] said the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] would intensify efforts to investigate and disrupt funding networks supporting such groups.[1]

Administration officials said the initiative targets violent transnational organizations rather than lawful political activity.[1] Critics argued that the administration risks conflating extremist violence with broader political movements, while the White House maintained that the focus is on groups engaged in criminal or terrorist conduct.[1]

## Badlands commentary
[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the summit as an appetizer to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Thursday-night address to the nation, and argued that the partisan "far-left terrorism" framing that dominated headlines obscured a deeper reality the administration was simultaneously combating and disclosing in real time.[1] He characterized infiltration as the preferred method of asymmetric warfare directed at a United States that is essentially impervious to defeat on the conventional battlefield, and said such infiltration — though most effective on the left — had become ubiquitous across American government and society, from the proliferation and siloing of non-governmental organizations to anti-American rhetoric and policy within the judicial and legislative branches.[1] He further suggested the country had "very likely suffered under the yoke of communist manchurian candidates" in the highest office for most of the current century, with only two Trump terms as reprieves.[1]

Burning Bright urged readers to follow the signal from Rubio, Bessent and Miller regarding what he called the foundational bedrock of an insurgency: fixing the election system is a must, but rooting out, exposing and very publicly punishing the cultivation of anti-American, anti-Christian and anti-human philosophies is "the true endgame."[1] He concluded that the war on election fraud and foreign interference and the war on the terrestrial Marxist, Communist [[deep-state|Deep State]] are not separate but twin fronts of the same existential conflict, one entering a new phase in the second half of 2026.[1]
