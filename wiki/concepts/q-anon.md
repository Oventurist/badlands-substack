# Q Anon

Q Anon is a far-right conspiracy theory that emerged on the internet in 2017, centered on the claims of an anonymous individual or group known as "Q" who purported to have insider knowledge of a deep state cabal working against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," Q Anon is referenced as a framework for understanding the systematic corruption of the U.S. government.

According to the article, [[q-anon|Q drop #2807]] explicitly identified money as the "keystone" of corruption, stating that money was the common denominator connecting all corruption and treason. The source quotes the drop as saying "Money is the keystone" and urging followers to "follow the money" because "everything is connected to money." The article uses this as its central thesis, arguing that taxpayer theft is the primary mechanism through which the cabal exercises control.

The source claims that Q identified a pattern in which anyone who tries to stop the flow of money from taxpayers to the hidden enemy and their puppets "is deemed a threat," and that when such a person occupies the most powerful position in the world, "extreme measures are used to eliminate that threat." The article specifically links this framework to the death of [[jfk-jr|John F. Kennedy Jr.]], claiming that he was eliminated because he was aware of the enemy's identity and posed a political threat.

According to the article, Q Anon's framework explains why [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is the target of such intense hostility: he is an "outsider" who threatens the cabal's control of taxpayer money. The source claims that Trump "accepted that reality long before he ever announced he was running for president," and that the war against the cabal is real and ongoing. The piece frames itself as part of the Q Anon narrative, using Q's framework to interpret events in Iraq, Libya, Syria, and the United States.
