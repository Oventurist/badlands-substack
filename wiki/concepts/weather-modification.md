# Weather Modification

Weather modification refers to deliberate human intervention in atmospheric processes, most commonly through cloud seeding, in which particles are dispersed into clouds to encourage precipitation.[1] The practice is licensed and regulated in Texas by the state's Department of Licensing and Regulation, which maintains suspension criteria that operators must observe.[1]

## As a subject of public suspicion

Weather modification has become a recurring theme in alternative-media coverage of natural disasters.[1] Following the [[2025-central-texas-floods|2025 Central Texas floods]], theories attributing the disaster to weather modification circulated widely, with the most viral of them centering on the cloud-seeding company [[rainmaker-technology-corporation|Rainmaker Technology Corporation]].[1] The firm's chief executive, [[augustus-doricko|Augustus Doricko]], publicly documented Rainmaker's operational timeline and its suspension of operations under Texas regulatory criteria, arguing that no seeding activity could plausibly have contributed to the flooding.[1]

Badlands commentary observed that once the weather-modification theory was declared debunked, the same commentators pivoted to attributing the disaster to climate change and to alleged cuts in NOAA funding under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — a substitution the Brief characterized as gaslighting offensive to survivors and bereaved families.[1]
