# ODNI COVID-19 Origins Declassification (2026)

The ODNI COVID-19 origins declassification refers to a June 2026 release by the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] of communications and documents concerning government assessments of the origins of COVID-19 and the role of former National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases Director [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]].[1]

## The release

ODNI announced the publication in a press statement issued on a Thursday, describing the materials as not previously made public.[1] Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] said the release was the result of a yearlong declassification review conducted by the agency, a review that included testimony from multiple Intelligence Community whistleblowers and produced documents the agency said shed light on interactions between Fauci and intelligence officials during the pandemic.[1]

## Allegations

ODNI alleged that Fauci influenced Intelligence Community assessments regarding COVID-19 origins and asserted that the newly released correspondence contradicts testimony he gave to Congress in 2024.[1] The press release also stated that the agency had gathered whistleblower accounts alleging retaliation against analysts who challenged prevailing conclusions about the virus's origins, including claims that some analysts who supported the lab-leak hypothesis faced professional repercussions and that dissenting views were suppressed.[1]

Examples from those whistleblower accounts were referred by Gabbard to the [[intelligence-community-inspector-general|Intelligence Community Inspector General]] for review; the press release did not indicate the outcome of any such review.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the release in terms of organizational change management, writing that in a transformational change program one moves fast and breaks things, cannot let the old guard set the pace or the agenda, and breaks rules because the old guard's rules prevent transformation.[1]
