# GART Nashville

GART Nashville is an installment of the Great American Road Trip (GART) event series run by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], which takes the network on the road four times a year for three-day gatherings with its hosts and community.[1] The eleventh event was scheduled for April 9–12, 2026, in Nashville, Tennessee, promoted as a chance to meet like-minded Americans and "be part of something real."[1]

The event featured in the March 30, 2026 Brief both as a promotion and as a reference point in commentary: [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] joked that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s optimism about a possible peace deal in the [[iran-war|Iran war]] was "awfully considerate" timing ahead of GART Nashville.[1]

## GART 11 (April 2026)

Promotion in the April 8, 2026 Brief billed GART 11 as taking place April 9–12, 2026 in Nashville, Tennessee, describing [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s practice of hitting the road four times a year for a three-day "patriot-packed" event with its hosts and community.[2] Readers unable to attend in person were offered a GART Virtual Pass granting access to the official Telegram group.[2]

Promotion in the April 7, 2026 Brief issued a "last call" for GART 11, again dated April 9–12, 2026 in Nashville, Tennessee, and reiterated that [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] hits the road four times a year for a three-day patriot-packed event with its hosts and community, with a GART Virtual Pass available for those unable to attend.[3]

## GART 11, April 2026

A Badlands Brief published on April 1, 2026 promoted GART 11 as one week away, scheduled for April 9-12 in Nashville, Tennessee, describing the three-day event as a patriot-packed gathering with Badlands hosts and community members.[4] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted "MAGA in a Post Trump World" as one of the panel topics, framing it as a needed conversation at a moment when, in her view, the movement is in shambles and the "Gray Champion" is about to exit the scene.[4] She promoted both virtual and in-person tickets and argued that the coming Golden Age must be built and owned by the people rather than handed to them.[4]

## Announcement of GART 11 (March 2026)

A dedicated announcement post published on March 16, 2026 and authored by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invited readers to join their favorite [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] hosts in Nashville, Tennessee for the eleventh stop along the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], billed as the first time the tour had touched down in Nashville and as the fifth GART of what the network called "the new American Golden Age."[5] The post described the events as an opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots and as "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[5]

By the time of the announcement VIP tickets had sold out, while Full Weekend General Admission tickets remained available, granting access to a Friday night Welcome Party and meet-and-greet with Badlands Media personalities along with three days of panels; in-person tickets were sold through a TicketSpice page.[5] Virtual tickets allowed purchasers to stream the entire event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, with long-term DVR access to replays afterward.[5] Virtual ticket holders also received access to an exclusive GART Telegram chat, which the announcement said typically persists as a long-running chat room for the network's most dedicated audience members for months after each event, hosting behind-the-scenes photos and video and "virtual shenanigans galore."[5] The post closed on behalf of the Badlands Media team with the hope of seeing familiar faces and, "as has happened at every GART so far, plenty of new ones," signing off with Burning Bright's customary "Stay Bright!"[5]

## Last Call and Single-Day Ticket Release (March 2026)

A follow-up post titled "Last Call for GART: Nashville!" was published on March 29, 2026, again authored by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], announcing that single-day and virtual tickets were on sale and telling readers that the event was "almost time! Kind of."[6] It described the Nashville gathering as the eleventh stop along the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the fifth GART of the "new American Golden Age," and the network's first time touching down in Nashville.[6] As in the earlier announcement, the post characterized GART events as an opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots and as "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[6]

The post cautioned that VIP tickets might already be sold out by the time the mailer arrived, while Full Weekend General Admission tickets remained available with access to a Friday night Welcome Party and meet-and-greet plus three days of panels.[6] Newly released single-day tickets were aimed at readers living closer to Nashville and granted full access to one day of the buyer's choice, with pricing and package breakdowns hosted on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] TicketSpice pages.[6] Virtual tickets again allowed streaming of the full Friday-through-Sunday panel slate with long-term DVR access to replays and admission to an exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat, whose links were emailed to virtual ticket buyers.[6] The post noted that these Telegram groups become long-lived chat rooms for the network's most hardcore audience members, thriving for months after each event and serving to "document the journey to the latest GART."[6] It closed on behalf of the Badlands Media team with the hope of seeing familiar and new faces alike and Burning Bright's customary "Stay Bright!"[6]
