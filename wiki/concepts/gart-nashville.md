# GART Nashville

GART Nashville is an installment of the Great American Road Trip (GART) event series run by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], which takes the network on the road four times a year for three-day gatherings with its hosts and community.[1] The eleventh event was scheduled for April 9–12, 2026, in Nashville, Tennessee, promoted as a chance to meet like-minded Americans and "be part of something real."[1]

The event featured in the March 30, 2026 Brief both as a promotion and as a reference point in commentary: [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] joked that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s optimism about a possible peace deal in the [[iran-war|Iran war]] was "awfully considerate" timing ahead of GART Nashville.[1]

## GART 11 (April 2026)

Promotion in the April 8, 2026 Brief billed GART 11 as taking place April 9–12, 2026 in Nashville, Tennessee, describing [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s practice of hitting the road four times a year for a three-day "patriot-packed" event with its hosts and community.[2] Readers unable to attend in person were offered a GART Virtual Pass granting access to the official Telegram group.[2]

Promotion in the April 7, 2026 Brief issued a "last call" for GART 11, again dated April 9–12, 2026 in Nashville, Tennessee, and reiterated that [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] hits the road four times a year for a three-day patriot-packed event with its hosts and community, with a GART Virtual Pass available for those unable to attend.[3]

## GART 11, April 2026

A Badlands Brief published on April 1, 2026 promoted GART 11 as one week away, scheduled for April 9-12 in Nashville, Tennessee, describing the three-day event as a patriot-packed gathering with Badlands hosts and community members.[4] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted "MAGA in a Post Trump World" as one of the panel topics, framing it as a needed conversation at a moment when, in her view, the movement is in shambles and the "Gray Champion" is about to exit the scene.[4] She promoted both virtual and in-person tickets and argued that the coming Golden Age must be built and owned by the people rather than handed to them.[4]
