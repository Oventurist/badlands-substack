# Q Clearance

**Q Clearance** is described in Badlands Media's "[[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]]" series as the highest security clearance issued within the United States [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]].[1] The term is significant within [[qanon|Q]] interpretation because the anonymous poster's designation "Q" is generally read as a reference to this clearance level.[1]

## Interpretation in Q drop #48

Chapter 6 of the series takes up [[qanon|Q]] drop #48 of November 2, 2017 and reads it as an assertion that the person supplying information about the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One]] scandal holds Q Clearance.[1] At the same time, the author argues, the drop indicates that this person does not work within the Department of Energy itself, but rather holds a very high level of clearance spanning multiple agencies.[1]

The chapter reasons that only a small number of individuals would hold clearance of that breadth, and offers as examples Admiral [[mike-rogers|Mike Rogers]] and the President of the United States himself.[1] This reading serves the wider argument of the series that the Q operation was run from inside the government by holders of the most sensitive access, rather than by outsiders.[1]

## "Who would have the goods on U1?"

[[qanon|Q]] drop #48 of November 2, 2017 asked what Q Clearance is, what hint it explicitly refers to, and "Who would have the goods on U1?" — while clarifying that using the name "Q" does not indicate the poster works at the [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] but rather that they hold the highest level of security within all departments.[2] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] explains that Q Clearance is the highest classification at the Department of Energy, that the department therefore holds the full evidentiary record on the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]], and that Q is a military intelligence operation rather than a departmental source.[2]
