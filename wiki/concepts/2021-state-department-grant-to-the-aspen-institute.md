# 2021 State Department Grant to the Aspen Institute

On September 1, 2021, the [[state-department|State Department]] — then led by Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] — awarded the [[aspen-institute|Aspen Institute]] a $10.3 million grant under the category "Professional and Cultural Exchange Programs – Citizen Exchanges."[1]

Badlands Media, citing USAspending records, reported that this was the first State Department grant to the institute in over ten years.[1] The only two prior awards were made on September 25, 2010 and November 24, 2010, for $148,500 and $297,000 respectively; the 2021 grant was therefore more than 23 times the combined total of State Department funding the institute had received in the preceding 11 years.[1]

The article emphasized the timing: the award came almost exactly one year after Aspen Digital's [[aspen-institute-hack-and-dump-exercise|hack-and-dump exercise]] and after Blinken, alleged to be the "impetus" of the [[october-2020-intelligence-officials-letter-on-the-hunter-biden-laptop|October 2020 intelligence officials letter]], had been appointed to lead the department.[1]
