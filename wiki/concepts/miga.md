# MIGA (Make Iran Great Again)

**MIGA**, an acronym for "Make Iran Great Again," is a slogan derived from [[make-america-great-again|MAGA]] that circulated in June 2025 following a social-media post by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] referring to "regime change" in Iran. In the June 26, 2025 Badlands Media essay "Signal Collapse," published under the byline of [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], MIGA is interpreted not as conventional political branding but as what the author calls a "memetic activator" — a compressed phrase intended to reawaken a suppressed national memory rather than to advocate a policy.[1]

## Interpretation in the "Signal Collapse" framing

The essay argues that the power of MAGA never resided in the literal words but in what it calls "the frequency of memory," and that MIGA mirrors the same technique applied to Iran.[1] Under this reading, Trump's "regime change" remark is not a forecast of upheaval but a marker that an internal shift inside Iran has already occurred; the author asserts that Trump "speaks in completed tense" and "closes loops" rather than predicting events, citing phrases such as "We caught them all" and "They should have signed the deal" as parallels.[1]

The essay claims that MIGA is dangerous not to Iran but to what it terms the "old machine," because the slogan "doesn't impose — it unseals," allowing a pre-existing cultural memory to resurface.[1] Iran, in this account, is not being liberated by foreign powers but "reclaimed by its own vibration," a process framed as re-inhabitation and correction rather than revolution or coup.[1]

## Family of derived slogans

"Signal Collapse" presents MAGA as an "activation code" applicable to any nation said to retain cultural memory, and lists a family of derived glyphs said to be echoing globally: MIGA (Iran), MCGA (Canada), MEGA (Europe), and MHGA — "Make Humanity Great Again."[1] Each is described as "a carrier signal for mythic restoration" associated with archetypes the essay names as the Sovereign, the Healer, the Warrior, and the Rememberer.[1] The same arc is projected onto Greenland, Canada, France, Germany, Israel, and the United States.[1]

## Assessment

MIGA as described here is an interpretive construct within alternative-media commentary rather than a documented political program. The essay offers no evidentiary basis for its central claims — that Trump's phrasing systematically post-dates covert operations, or that slogans function as measurable "frequency" effects on populations — and Badlands Media notes that its features represent the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
