# Third Eugenics Conference

The Third Eugenics Conference was held in New York in 1932, co-sponsored by JP Morgan banker [[william-draper-jr|William Draper Jr]] — head of General Motors and a leading figure of Dillon Read and Co — and the Harriman family.[1]

The conference brought together leading eugenicists from around the world, who came to study America's successful application of [[eugenics|eugenics]] laws, which had begun in 1907 under the enthusiastic patronage of [[theodore-roosevelt|Theodore Roosevelt]].[1] Hiding behind the veneer of science, Ehret writes, these figures discussed a new age of "directed evolution of man" to be made possible under a global scientific dictatorship.[1]

Speaking at the conference, the British fascist [[fairfield-osborn|Fairfield Osborn]] argued that eugenics "aids and encourages the survival and multiplication of the fittest" while indirectly checking "the multiplication of the unfitted," complaining that in the United States "there are millions of people who are acting as dragnets or sheet anchors on the progress of the ship of state" and that civilization was "encouraging the survival of the un-fittest."[1]
