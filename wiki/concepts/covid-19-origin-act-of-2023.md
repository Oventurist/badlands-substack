# COVID-19 Origin Act of 2023

The COVID-19 Origin Act of 2023 is the legislation signed by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] on Monday, March 20, 2023, requiring the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] to declassify information on any possible links between a laboratory in China and the origins of the Covid-19 pandemic.[1]

## Passage

Both the House and the Senate passed the legislation unanimously earlier that month.[1] The push to make classified origin material public followed the Energy Department's conclusion, reached with "low confidence," that the virus was likely the result of an accidental laboratory leak in China.[1]

## Terms

Under the legislation, Director of National Intelligence [[avril-haines|Avril Haines]] was given 90 days to declassify all information on possible links between the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]] — a major center of coronavirus research — and the origins of Covid.[1] In a signing statement, Biden said his administration would "declassify and share as much of that information as possible, consistent with my constitutional authority to protect against the disclosure of information that would harm national security."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] laid out three readings of the signing.[1] It could represent "real" disclosure, given the unanimous votes in both chambers.[1] Alternatively it could serve as further justification for escalating tensions with China, on the expectation that the public story would blame the Chinese state rather than rogue elements within Wuhan.[1] Either way, he argued, a limited hangout would prove ineffective because "the cat's out of the bag" regarding [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] and the [[national-institutes-of-health|NIH]].[1]

He described the continual shifting of the acceptable position on the Wuhan Institute of Virology as the clearest example yet of [[overtons-goalpost|Overton's Goalpost]], and argued that whatever COVID ultimately was — a virus, a bioweapon or a PsyOp — its net effects were catastrophic and [[deep-state|Deep State]] elements were responsible.[1] Forcing the adversary to release more intelligence than it previously had, he concluded, is a sign that it has lost ground in the Mind War.[1]
