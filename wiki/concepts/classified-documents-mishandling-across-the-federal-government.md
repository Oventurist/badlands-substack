# Classified Documents Mishandling Across the Federal Government

In February 2023, USA Today reported that beyond the high-profile discoveries of classified material in the homes of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]], federal court records portrayed a broadly sloppy system for tracking the country's most important secrets.[1]

## Findings

The reporting described intelligence agency staffers and contractors caught in recent years "squirreling away enormous troves of documents," including one contractor who mailed home computer hard drives filled with secrets from Afghanistan to Texas.[1] Stashes of secret documents had been scattered through homes, sheds and cars, and staffers sometimes copied documents onto compact discs or even handwritten notes.[1] The paper noted that it was not always the documents themselves that exposed the offenders: one path to thousands of pages of classified records "was strewn with marijuana leaves."[1] Despite the sloppy handling, the secrets at stake were among the country's most important.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] noted that Trump had posted the article on Truth Social on the Sunday after publication and called it "a pretty good read."[1] Trump tied the piece to "Mad Dog Psycho" Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], remarking that Smith "at a minimum, should give Biden, Obama, and all of the others the same treatment!"[1] Just Human read the post as "foreshadowing" of a wider reckoning over selective prosecution of document cases.[1]
