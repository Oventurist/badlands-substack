# Ethereum

Ethereum is the second-largest cryptocurrency network by value, sitting behind [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] in both market value and, the Badlands article argued, in trust.[1] It hosts the smart contracts on which most [[decentralized-finance|DeFi]] projects and tokens are deployed, including the Saitama token contract created on the Ethereum network on May 30, 2021.[1]

## The Merge

In September 2022 Ethereum completed its migration from [[proof-of-work]] to [[proof-of-stake]], an upgrade known as "the Merge." The network reportedly reduced direct energy consumption by 99 percent while increasing transaction speed.[1] Critics including [[ryan-matta|Ryan Matta]] argued that the trade-off was greater centralization, since proof-of-stake validators are selected according to how many coins they have staked.[1] Matta, a former robotics engineer, had mined Ethereum when it traded at $2.64.[1]

## Gas fees

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] recounted setting up a wallet and buying a small amount of Ethereum, only to find she could not move it because her holdings were insufficient to cover the transaction fee — her introduction to "gas fees," the network's charge for executing transactions.[1]
