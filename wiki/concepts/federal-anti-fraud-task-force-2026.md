# Federal Anti-Fraud Task Force (2026)

The federal anti-fraud task force is a proposed initiative reported in February 2026 to be under preparation by the second Trump administration.[1]

## Reporting

CBS News, citing "multiple sources briefed on the plan," reported that Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] would lead a new federal anti-fraud task force, to be announced possibly later that month by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a new executive order.[1] According to those sources, the task force would initially focus on fraud in California state programs.[1] The [[white-house|White House]] declined to comment on the report, and California officials disputed the story.[1]
