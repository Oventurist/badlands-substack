# Sound of Freedom

Sound of Freedom is a 2023 American independent film about child sex trafficking, starring [[jim-caviezel|Jim Caviezel]] and directed by [[alejandro-monteverde|Alejandro Monteverde]].[1] The film is based on the life of [[tim-ballard|Tim Ballard]], founder of [[operation-underground-railroad|Operation Underground Railroad]], an organisation dedicated to rescuing children from sex trafficking operations.[1]

## Box office performance

Despite a run of negative coverage from mainstream outlets including Rolling Stone and Bloomberg News, the film grossed $100 million at the box office within several weeks of its debut.[1] According to [[variety|Variety]], it became the first independent film to reach $100 million at the North American box office since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic.[1] Made on a budget reported at $14 million, it ranked as the 16th highest-grossing film of 2023 at the time of the milestone.[1] Audience and critical response were both strongly positive: the film received an A+ CinemaScore and a 100 percent score on Rotten Tomatoes.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed the film's performance against the backdrop of a depressed industry and the concurrent Writers Guild and Screen Actors Guild strikes, which he described as threatening to burn Hollywood down on a permanent basis.[1] That same weekend produced one of the strongest box offices in years on the strength of the counter-programmed hits Barbie and Oppenheimer — the former characterised by Burning Bright as one of the most openly neo-feminist films ever made, the latter as an attempt to imprint the mainstream narrative on nuclear war into the collective mind.[1] Beneath that programming, he wrote, Sound of Freedom continued to take the third spot and to accumulate well over $100 million on what he described as a $15 million budget, making it arguably the most financially successful film of the year on a per-screen basis relative to cost — and, he suggested, not a coincidence.[1]
