# 40,000-Foot View

The **40,000-foot view** (often written "40k ft view") is a term used throughout the Q drops and the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series for the elevated, whole-picture perspective that readers of the drops are urged to adopt rather than reacting to individual news items.[1]

In Chapter 21, [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] describes drop #128 of November 9, 2017 as largely an instruction in how to acquire this vantage point.[1] In that drop Q asks anons to review the compiled graphic every day after big news breaks, on the reasoning that looking back provides answers to what is being seen in the present — the [[future-proves-past|future proves past]] principle.[1] Q also instructs readers to "learn to distinguish between relevant/non-relevant news," which Fleuret glosses as looking past headlines to separate signal from noise, and compares to [[michael-flynn|General Flynn]]'s advice not to chase shiny objects.[1]

Fleuret argues that a collection of crumbs becomes a map, and that the map becomes a guide once it is understood to be depicting corruption and evil running through government.[1] The related question "What is a keystone?" — see [[the-keystone|the keystone]] — is posed in the same passage, and Q opens the following section with "think full picture," which Fleuret reads as a direct instruction to adopt the 40,000-foot view before considering what follows.[1]
