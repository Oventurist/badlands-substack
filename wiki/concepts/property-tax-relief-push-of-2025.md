# Property Tax Relief Push of 2025

The property tax relief push of 2025 refers to a cluster of state-level initiatives, announced in mid-April 2025, aimed at reducing homeowners' property tax burdens amid rising living costs.[1]

## Pennsylvania

On Tuesday, April 15, 2025, Pennsylvania Governor [[josh-shapiro|Josh Shapiro]] unveiled a comprehensive budget proposal addressing several key challenges facing the state, including significantly reducing homeownership costs.[1] For the first time, the budget included a $1 billion property tax relief initiative delivered through the homestead and farmstead exclusion.[1] [[newsweek|Newsweek]] framed the proposal as easing one of the most significant financial burdens on Pennsylvanians while setting a precedent for broader fiscal reform in the state's approach to housing affordability.[1]

## Indiana

The same day, Indiana Governor [[mike-braun|Mike Braun]] signed Senate Bill One into law after lawmakers passed it late the previous night.[1] A priority bill for the governor and Senate Republicans, it was projected by his office to save Hoosiers $1.3 billion in long-term tax reform.[1] Senate Enrolled Act 1 creates new deductions for certain homeowners, reducing the taxable value of their homes, and caps the total property tax that local governments may impose.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that "all the Uniparty governors are trying to get out in front of the collapse of the property tax scam," reasoning that if the collapse were not already locked in, planned, and inevitable, officials would not be publicizing relief as though it were a favor.[1] He added that the property tax system had "legitimately destroyed lives en masse" and that its administrators took pride in it.[1]
