# Morphic Field

The **morphic field** is a concept associated with the biologist [[rupert-sheldrake|Rupert Sheldrake]] and invoked in "Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field," the fifth installment of the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series published by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] through [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on August 9, 2025.[1]

In the article's usage, the morphic field is the mechanism by which private remembering becomes collective: "This is how the morphic field works. It doesn't ask you to believe. It asks you to listen… to feel into it."[1] The author argues that when enough people speak, the field shifts — not because they have agreed on terms, but because something in their signal has aligned.[1] The field is characterised as non-linear, spiralling and echoing rather than spreading in straight lines, and as remembering in patterns; it is said to respond to recognition and to deepen through communion.[1] Sheldrake's assertion that "Memory is inherent in nature" grounds the claim, which the article ties to its broader account of a [[resonance-field|resonance field]] restoring coherence after the conditioning described in earlier installments.[1]
