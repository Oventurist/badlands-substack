# Remain in Mexico

"Remain in Mexico," formally the Migrant Protection Protocols, is a U.S. border policy requiring asylum seekers arriving at the southern border to wait in Mexico while their claims are adjudicated in U.S. immigration courts.[1]

## Reinstatement in 2025

The policy was reinstated during the opening weeks of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term as part of a torrent of executive actions reshaping border and immigration policy.[1] It accompanied the declaration of a national emergency at the southern border, the shutdown of the [[cbp-one|CBP One app]] as a legal pathway for asylum seekers, plans for mass deportations, and moves to end [[birthright-citizenship|birthright citizenship]] for the children of undocumented immigrants.[1] Supporters credited the combined package with drastically changing the threat level at the border, while acknowledging that years of work remained.[1] Enforcement of the broader deportation agenda fell to border czar [[tom-homan|Tom Homan]].[1]
