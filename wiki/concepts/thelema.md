# Thelema

Thelema — from the Greek word for "Will" — is the "revealed doctrine" propagated by [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], which he promoted through the pages of the New York journals published by [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]] during World War I.[1]

As editor of The International, Crowley used the platform to advance both German political propaganda and his Thelemic and occult new-age theories.[1] The doctrine was institutionalized through Crowley's orders, including the [[ordo-templi-orientis|Ordo Templi Orientis]], of which he was made outer head by [[theodor-reuss|Theodor Reuss]] in 1912, and the Argentum Astrum (A∴A∴), into which Major General [[j-f-c-fuller|J.F.C. Fuller]] was initiated in 1907 and of which the German Theosophist Franz Hartmann was a member.[1] Crowley seeded occult cells across the United States between 1914 and 1919, including OTO bodies in New York and California, the latter later led by Jack Parsons and L. Ron Hubbard.[1]

Between the world wars, Viereck continued to promote and practice Crowley's religion of Thelema while interfacing with nascent fascist organizations in the United States, Mexico, Canada and Britain.[1]
