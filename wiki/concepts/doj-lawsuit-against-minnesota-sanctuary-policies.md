# DOJ Lawsuit Against Minnesota Sanctuary Policies

On Monday 29 September 2025 the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] sued the state of [[minnesota|Minnesota]] and both Twin Cities — [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]] and [[saint-paul|St. Paul]] — over so-called [[sanctuary-cities|sanctuary city]] policies.[1]

## Allegations

Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] announced the action, saying that "Minnesota officials are jeopardizing the safety of their own citizens by allowing illegal aliens to circumvent the legal process."[1] In the 34-page lawsuit the department asks a judge to invalidate sections of the state constitution, city codes in St. Paul and Minneapolis, and Hennepin County administrative orders.[1] Federal prosecutors claim that the collective policies preventing local cooperation with Immigration and Customs Enforcement are unconstitutional.[1]

Both Minneapolis and St. Paul maintain city ordinances that prohibit cooperation with federal immigration enforcement.[1]
