# Cardboard Ayatollah

"Cardboard Ayatollah" is an internet nickname applied in March 2026 to [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]], the newly installed supreme leader of [[iran|Iran]], after a video circulated showing a cardboard cutout of him on stage at a pro-government rally.[1]

## Origin and spread

Mojtaba Khamenei was appointed supreme leader on March 9, 2026, following the death of his father, [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]], who was killed in a U.S.- and Israeli-backed airstrike in Tehran on February 28.[1] Since assuming the role he had not appeared publicly or released any verified audio or video statements as of March 21; a March 12 address attributed to him was delivered by a state television anchor rather than by Khamenei himself, and subsequent communications were issued as written statements or broadcast substitutes.[1] The absence extended to the Persian New Year, when he released a written Nowruz message rather than delivering the traditional speech.[1]

The cutout video "launched a thousand ships," in the words of [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], producing a viral meme campaign he described as "as retarded as it was hilarious."[1]

## Related rumors and interpretation

The New York Post reported that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been briefed that the new ayatollah was gay, prompting White House journalists to question Trump about it in the Oval Office; Trump answered, "Well, nobody knows…", appearing to hold back laughter.[1] U.S. intelligence agencies, including the [[cia|CIA]], monitored for indications of Khamenei's activity and assessed that he is likely alive but operating under heightened security constraints.[1]

Badlands commentary treats the entire episode as a psychological operation. GhostofBasedPatrickHenry had long speculated that the elder Khamenei actually died of natural causes — cancer — around the time of the 12 Day War in the previous summer, and argued that when [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]] of the National Supreme Security Council implemented a [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] plan in August, the ayatollah was removed from the chain of command and became an irrelevant figurehead.[1]
