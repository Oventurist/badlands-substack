# Cardboard Ayatollah

"Cardboard Ayatollah" is the nickname the internet attached to [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]], [[iran|Iran]]'s supreme leader from March 9, 2026, after a video circulated showing a cardboard cutout of him on stage at a pro-government rally.[1] The clip, in the words of [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], "launched a thousand ships," seeding a viral meme campaign he described as "as retarded as it was hilarious."[1]

## Origins in the succession vacuum

The meme grew out of a genuine absence. Khamenei was appointed after the death of his father [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] in a U.S.- and Israeli-backed airstrike on Tehran on February 28, 2026, and had not appeared publicly or released verified audio or video statements as of March 21.[1] A March 12 address attributed to him was read by a state television anchor; later communications came as written statements or broadcast substitutes; and he issued a written Nowruz message rather than delivering the traditional Persian New Year speech.[1] U.S. intelligence agencies including the [[cia|CIA]] assessed he was likely alive but under heightened security constraints.[1]

## As an information operation

Badlands treats the phenomenon as a deliberate operation — "the Ayatollah psyop is one of the better ones to spin out of this Persian Adventure."[1] Its companion strand was the New York Post report that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been briefed the new Ayatollah was gay, which drew Oval Office questions and Trump's deadpan reply, "Well, nobody knows…"[1] The commentary pairs this with an older speculation that the elder Khamenei actually died of cancer in mid-2025, and with the claim that after [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]] implemented a [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] plan in August 2025 the Supreme Leader was removed from the chain of command — making the cardboard joke an inadvertently accurate description of a hollow office.[1]
