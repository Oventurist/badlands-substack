# Sino-Russian Treaty of Friendship

The Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation between the People's Republic of China and the Russian Federation — commonly rendered in Badlands coverage as the Sino-Russian Treaty of Friendship — is a bilateral treaty signed in 2001 that forms the legal foundation of the China-Russia strategic partnership.

## The 25th anniversary and the May 2026 Beijing visit

According to the May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, the [[kremlin|Kremlin]] announced that Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s visit to Beijing on May 19–20, 2026 for talks with Chinese leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] coincided with the 25th anniversary of the 2001 treaty. The Kremlin said the visit would focus on bilateral relations, economic cooperation, and international and regional issues. Ahead of the visit, Xi and Putin exchanged congratulatory messages, with Xi quoted as saying China-Russia cooperation had "continuously deepened and solidified." The Brief notes the two countries have expanded political and economic ties in recent years, particularly after Russia's 2022 invasion of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] increased Moscow's dependence on Chinese trade and energy purchases.
