# US-Japan Energy and Nuclear Agreement 2026

The US-Japan energy and nuclear agreement of 2026 is a package of investment commitments announced around the March 2026 White House meeting between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Japanese Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]].[1]

## Nuclear component

The two countries advanced a major nuclear energy initiative including plans for small modular reactor development in the United States, with project estimates around $40 billion.[1] Badlands reporting specified that the small modular reactor plants would be built in Tennessee and Alabama.[1]

## Gas, minerals and infrastructure

[[japan|Japan]] pledged tens of billions of dollars in U.S. energy sector investment as part of a broader economic agreement covering nuclear power, natural gas and infrastructure, plus cooperation on critical minerals and rare earth supply chains aimed at reducing reliance on [[china|China]].[1] A further $33 billion was announced for natural gas generation facilities in Pennsylvania and Texas, with discussion of joint ventures in space exploration, technology development and deep-sea rare earth mineral cultivation.[1] The package built on the 2025 U.S.-Japan Strategic Trade and Investment Agreement, under which Japan announced in February 2026 a $36 billion program including the largest natural gas facility in history in Ohio, a deepwater crude oil export facility in the Gulf of America, and onshoring of 100% of synthetic diamond grit production.[1]

## Strategic context

The leaders discussed energy security in the context of global instability, including oil supply concerns tied to the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] and the broader regional conflict with [[iran|Iran]].[1] Takaichi reiterated Japan's limits on military involvement under its pacifist constitution while affirming continued economic and strategic cooperation.[1] The [[white-house|White House]] fact sheet framed the strengthened alliance as vital to regional stability and beneficial to both nations.[1]
