# Syrian Civil War

The Syrian civil war is the multi-sided conflict in Syria that Badlands Media dates to 2012 and treats as a central theater of what it calls the War of Sovereignty.[1]

## Origins and parallel conflicts

The outlet notes that the war involved intervention from both Russia and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] and ran parallel to the Russian move into Ukraine in 2014, which it characterizes as a response to a CIA-led Maidan coup against the elected Ukrainian government.[1] Russian assistance to the Syrian government is credited with reducing the jihadist group Jabhat al-Nusra — since rebranded [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham|Hayat Tahrir al-Sham]] — to parts of Idlib province in the northwest, alongside the defeat of other rebel militants including the [[islamic-state|Islamic State]].[1]

## Multi-party theater

By late 2024 the parties present in Syria, as enumerated by Badlands Media, included Iran and Hezbollah, Ukraine and ISIS, Russia and [[wagner-group|Wagner]], the United States and [[nato|NATO]], the [[kurds|Kurds]], [[turkey|Turkey]] with its proxy militias, and the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]], with the Chinese navy positioned to the east in the Persian Gulf and the U.S. navy to the west in the Mediterranean.[1] Israel had been striking Iran-linked targets in Syria for years and intensified those raids after the October 7, 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack, including the [[2024-israeli-strikes-on-mazzeh-damascus|November 2024 strikes on Mazzeh]] in Damascus.[1]

The outlet argued the accumulated presence of these forces set the stage for a battle of biblical proportions in a theater biblical in its history, coinciding with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s return to the presidency.[1]
