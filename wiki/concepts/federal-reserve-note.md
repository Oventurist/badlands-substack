# Federal Reserve Note

The Federal Reserve Note is the paper currency issued through [[federal-reserve|the Federal Reserve]] system and, in Badlands Media's legal analysis, the object of the contractual relationship between the United States government and a private credit issuer.[1]

## As a contracted benefit

The article argues that by congressional act the United States made itself liable to pay the Federal Reserve system in order to receive the "benefits" of using Federal Reserve Notes, with the federal government acting as [[agency-law|agent]] for the American people and thereby becoming the Federal Reserve's customer on their behalf.[1] Whether such benefits are desired or warranted is set aside by the author as a discussion for another time.[1]

## Credit and debt

Because the Federal Reserve is described as a corporate system, users of its credit must abide by the terms of its internal corporate code to enjoy that benefit.[1] A creditor is an entity to whom money is owed, and the article states that every time the Federal Reserve issues credit that issuance becomes part of the debt of the United States, in keeping with the ordinary creditor-debtor relation.[1] The overall arrangement is characterized as a corporate contract of indebtedness.[1]

## Jeffersonian framing

The article situates the paper note within [[thomas-jefferson]]'s warning that Crown agents intended to recapture the United States through a *paper system of "money"* that would reunite the destinies of America and Great Britain under a [[bastard-system-of-laws|bastard system of laws]].[1] See also [[jurisdiction]] and [[state-citizenship]].
