# 2025 ICE Arrests of Iranian Nationals

In late June 2025, federal agents arrested 11 Iranian nationals living in the United States, an operation announced by [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] officials on Tuesday, June 24.[1]

## The sweep

Almost all of the arrests were made on the Sunday immediately after U.S. military aircraft bombed key Iranian nuclear sites.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he had ordered the strikes to end the nuclear threat posed by what he called the "world's number one state sponsor of terror."[1] The arrests came amid fears among some Americans of violence inside the United States tied to the conflict with [[iran|Iran]]; mayors from New York City to Los Angeles said they were watching for threats to public safety.[1]

## Those arrested

Officials named several of those detained: Mehran Makari Saheli, 56, convicted of illegal firearm possession and a former member of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps; Ribvar Karimi, a former Iranian army sniper who had failed to adjust his immigration status; and Yousef Mehridehno, labelled by Homeland Security a "suspected terrorist" who lied on a visa application.[1] Federal officials said Karimi was carrying an Islamic Republic of Iran Army identification card when arrested.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that it was good the Trump administration had "essentially closed the border and stopped the criminals and their payloads from entering the nation," arguing that the arrests confirmed there were people in the country who did not belong there — "especially if they're enemy combatants of a hostile foreign nation."[1] The enforcement action was carried out by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]].[1]
