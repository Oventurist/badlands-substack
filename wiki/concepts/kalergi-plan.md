# Kalergi Plan

The "Kalergi Plan" is the name given by its critics to a program attributed to [[richard-coudenhove-kalergi|Count Coudenhove-Kalergi]], described in Badlands Media commentary as involving the deliberate flooding of Europe with non-Europeans in order to produce a single "euro-asiatic, negroid race."[1] The Badlands article "On Infiltration and Israel" presents the phrase as Kalergi's own wording.[1]

## Origins and patronage

The plan is dated by the article to the 1910s and 1920s.[1] Kalergi's works are said to have been championed by the banker [[max-warburg|Max Warburg]], who is described in the same passage as having set up a publishing house for Lenin in 1916 — placing the plan within the financial network the author calls the "ziobanksters" and connecting it to the material documented in [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]]'s [[wall-street-trilogy|Wall Street Trilogy]].[1]

## Alleged modern implementation

Badlands argues that mass migration into Europe a century after Kalergi wrote has been facilitated by Western governments "with such zeal" that many Western leaders must directly or indirectly subscribe to his vision.[1] The article links this to the [[grooming-gangs|rape gangs in the UK]], asserting that such phenomena would not exist absent deliberate infiltration, and that European countries have been flooded with millions of military-aged men.[1] The broader framing overlaps with the [[great-replacement-theory|Great Replacement theory]], with the author contending that European peoples were "groomed and demoralized" into welcoming their own replacement.[1]

## Relationship to the Israel argument

The article states that the same financier class that funded and promoted the Kalergi Plan was also involved in the creation of [[israel|Israel]], citing the [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]]'s addressee, Lord Rothschild, of the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]].[1] The author uses this connection to pose what he treats as the decisive question: how can such financiers be said to look out for ordinary Jews when their actions place Jewish lives in danger by fuelling backlash?[1]
