# Badlands Blitz Episode 17

**Badlands Blitz Episode 17**, subtitled "Big Trump Visits Little China as Election Deniers Seize the Narrative," is the seventeenth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video recap published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] through its Substack newsletter. The announcement post is dated May 16, 2026 and carried 206 likes at the time of the source capture. It opens with the customary "Badlanders!" salutation and restates the program's remit as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."

## Hosts

The episode is hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], the pairing that also carried Episodes 12, 14 and 16. Both are linked in the post to their Substack author profiles.

## Content

The announcement states that the hosts "spend a good chunk of the show discussing the prospective [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]–[[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] alliance." The source's central interpretive claim is that although this potential Sino-American pairing "doesn't get as much attention as potential coordination between Trump and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]," it "might actually be a pairing the globalist enemy fears even more." The subtitle's phrase "Big Trump Visits Little China" frames the discussion around a Trump visit in the week under review.

The second announced theme is what the post calls "the rapidly-shifting American narrative on [[election-denial|election denial]]," which the source describes as "more popular and bipartisan than ever before" — an assertion that skepticism about election outcomes has moved from a partisan liability to a broadly shared position across the American political spectrum. Consistent with the program's house style, the announcement enumerates no individual cases, officials or races; the specifics are left to the video itself.

## Publication and support model

The post repeats the standing editorial notice that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," and that the team can pay itself only through direct reader support, inviting readers to upgrade to a paid subscription in recognition of "the step-up in said work for 2026." It closes with the program's customary sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
