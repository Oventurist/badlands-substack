# The Kick

**The Kick** is an analogy used by [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] in the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series to describe a single event large enough to force a mass psychological awakening in the general public.[1] He sets the theory out at length in Chapter 21, published December 8, 2024.[1]

## The analogy

The term is borrowed from the film *Inception*, in which the character Cobb explains the dream world to Ariadne.[1] Fleuret maps the film's structure onto his own reading of political reality: the dream world corresponds to the narratives the public is force-fed, the constructed dream to the world as presented, and the process of waking within the dream to [[red-pilling|red-pilling]] in ordinary life.[1] In the film, escape from the dream requires a "kick" — something sufficiently traumatic within the dream to jolt the sleeper awake — and Fleuret asks what the equivalent would be for society as a whole.[1]

## Candidate event

Fleuret's own guess draws on what he and others on [[eye-of-the-storm|Eye of the Storm]] have been tracking as "missile comms," a category of signals he says has escalated in frequency without yet resolving into a clear meaning.[1] He connects these to the intensifying nuclear and ICBM rhetoric around Russia and Ukraine, and to [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s repeated statement that he will "end WWIII before he takes office."[1]

On that basis he proposes that little would wake people more effectively than a nuclear launch and the imminent onset of a third world war, halted by Trump before he had even been inaugurated.[1] Anyone still opposed to Trump after such an event, he argues, would have to be counted among the 4–6% Q said would never awaken.[1]

## Context

The theory is presented against Fleuret's sense that the period following the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 election]] had been "too quiet."[1] Holding to the position that patriots are in full control, he treats the quiet not as cause for concern but as reason to watch for the operation that might snap a large portion of the population awake.[1]
