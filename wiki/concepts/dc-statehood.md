# District of Columbia Statehood

District of Columbia statehood is the proposal to admit the federal district as the 51st state of the United States. The debate was reignited in August 2025 after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] declared a crime emergency in the capital and deployed the [[national-guard|National Guard]] to patrol its streets.[1]

## 2025 revival of the debate

Following Trump's Monday announcement, protests emerged under the banner of keeping D.C. "free," driven in part by fears that [[congress|Congress]] could rescind the district's [[dc-home-rule-act|Home Rule Act]], the statute granting the city limited self-government.[1] In response to the federal takeover of the [[metropolitan-police-department|Metropolitan Police Department]] and the Guard deployment, Mayor [[muriel-bowser|Muriel Bowser]] led a renewed call to grant the city statehood.[1]

"It's times like these when America needs to know why your nation's capital, a place where 700,000 taxpaying Americans live should be the 51st state," Bowser said in an appearance on The Breakfast Club.[1]

## Counterargument

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] offered a contrary view, arguing that if the federal city is to continue existing, its police force should at minimum be accountable to the federal government rather than to the elected officials of what he described as an unaccountable local jurisdiction.[1]
