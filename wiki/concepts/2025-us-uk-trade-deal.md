# 2025 US-UK Trade Deal

The 2025 US-UK trade deal was the first trade pact announced by the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] following the sweeping import taxes unveiled in April 2025 under [[liberation-day-tariffs|Liberation Day]].[1] Announced on 8 May 2025, both governments described the agreement as still being worked out in the weeks ahead and released only limited details.[1]

## Terms

Under the outline presented, the 10% import tax Trump had announced on most UK goods remained intact.[1] The bulk of the deal consisted of [[white-house|the White House]] agreeing to walk back some of the import taxes it had imposed on strategic sectors such as cars and steel.[1] In exchange, the White House said it had won unspecified changes expanding opportunities for sales of American beef, ethanol and other agricultural products in the United Kingdom.[1]

BBC analysis concluded that anyone hoping the White House would either significantly roll back its tariffs or extract major concessions abroad appeared bound for disappointment, and treated the pact as a template for the kind of agreements Trump might seek globally.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer Burning Bright said he had expected the first "major deal" to be with India as a means of pressuring China, and found the UK outcome darkly amusing.[1] He argued that the [[european-union|European Union]] and what he called the EU's "twin bitches" — Canada and Australia — would struggle to play hardball once "the former Empire" had been the first to capitulate.[1] The same commentator elsewhere linked the UK deal to Trump's Victory Day proclamation, characterizing the United Kingdom as "the evil empire."[1]
