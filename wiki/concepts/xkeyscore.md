# XKeyscore

**XKeyscore** is a signals-intelligence search and analysis program operated by the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]].[1]

## Reference in the Q drops

Drop #30 of November 1, 2017 is read in Chapter 4 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as Q explaining that vague phrasing and wording were used in part to evade sniffer programs deployed by the [[deep-state|Deep State]], which Q described as "xkeysc on steroids."[1] The chapter identifies "xkeysc" as XKeyscore and links to its public description as an NSA program.[1]

The author's interpretation is that the programs used to sniff out Q, the drops, and the information being disseminated by anons were far more powerful than XKeyscore itself, and that this technical exposure is at least one reason for Q's deliberate vagueness.[1] Another stated reason is the preservation of national-security protocols, along with leaving operational room for those behind the drops and the disbursement of intelligence to move around and keep distance.[1]
