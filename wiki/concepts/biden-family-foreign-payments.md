# Biden Family Foreign Payments

The Biden family foreign payments controversy concerns allegations, presented publicly by House Republicans in May 2023, that members of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s family and their business associates received and attempted to conceal more than $10 million in payments from foreign nationals.[1]

## The May 10, 2023 Oversight presentation

On May 10, 2023, the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] under chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] released a memorandum laying out bank-record evidence of what it described as a vast network of Biden family dealings.[1] Its principal findings as reported were that the family received and tried to hide over $10 million in payments from foreign nationals; that roughly $1 million in previously undisclosed Romanian-linked payments existed; that the family had ties to Romanian "influence peddling"; and that a web of more than 20 limited liability companies with a complicated corporate structure had been created, at least 15 of them formed after Biden became vice president in 2009, several owned or co-owned by [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1] Individual payments into these entities ranged from $5,000 to $3 million.[1]

The memorandum stated that "bank records show the Biden family, their business associates, and their companies received over $10 million from foreign nationals' companies," identifying payments both during and after Joe Biden's vice presidency.[1] Comer argued the transactions "appear to conceal the source of the funds and reduce the conspicuousness of the total amounts," and that "Chinese nationals and companies with significant ties to Chinese intelligence and the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] hid the source of the funds by layering domestic limited liability companies."[1] The committee said it wanted to know what legitimate business the Biden family was in, noting that Joe Biden had said claims about funds from China were "not true."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that although the material was old news within the Truth Community, it was not to the general public, and that Comer had seeded four narratives with substantial evidentiary support: influence peddling by the Biden family and by extension the Obama administration; foreign interference in the executive branch; collusion between big banks, intelligence agencies, and the Bidens; and media suppression.[1] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] framed the disclosures as vindication and urged readers to arm themselves with the details ahead of the coming campaign.[1]

The timing drew particular attention because [[george-santos|George Santos]] was arraigned on money-laundering and fraud charges the same day.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] asked whether the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] could apply to the Biden family the rigor it applied to a single congressman, noting U.S. Attorney [[breon-peace|Breon Peace]]'s language about holding defendants accountable for "brazen misrepresentations."[1]
