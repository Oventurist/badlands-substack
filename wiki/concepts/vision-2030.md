# Vision 2030

Vision 2030 is the long-term economic and social transformation program associated with Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], aimed at diversifying the Kingdom away from hydrocarbon dependence toward tourism, hospitality and high technology through historic levels of development and investment.

The [[badlands-brief-323|Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026]] invokes the plan as evidence against the claim that MBS would pursue a war of regional destruction against [[iran]]. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that the Crown Prince — described as "The Reformer" who expelled the radical clerics and effectively ended Wahhabism as a state institution — has spent eleven years implementing Vision 2030, and asks how any of it could be achieved "if the Middle East is destroyed in a war."

In this reading the plan also explains Saudi diplomacy: realizing the economic vision required partners and a security architecture capable of providing regional stability, which is why MBS assembled a grouping of states that "are not historic allies," their partnership predicated on a common future rather than shared history. The commentary contends that this unifying role — "not a caliphate that is built to destroy, instead a society built to prosper economically" — is precisely why the [[deep-state|Deep State]] targets the Kingdom.
