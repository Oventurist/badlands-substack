# 3I/ATLAS

3I/ATLAS is a newly discovered object travelling through the solar system on a trajectory originating outside it, and only the third interstellar object of its kind ever observed visiting the solar system.[1]

## Nature and trajectory

Most astronomers, including those at NASA, believe the object is a comet.[1] Dr. Laura Driessen, a radio astronomer at the University of Sydney, said that while its origin point is unknown, "it definitely came from outside the solar system," and that it would "cruise through the inner solar system — in between Mars and Earth's orbit — and then it will fly past the sun."[1]

## Alien-artifact hypothesis

Harvard astronomer [[avi-loeb|Avi Loeb]] repeatedly suggested that 3I/ATLAS was sent by an extraterrestrial civilization and might serve as a "Turing Test" for humanity — a test of how intelligent humans are.[1] Loeb argued that the establishment's rapid classification of the object as a comet was premature, citing Hubble Space Telescope images that show a glow ahead of the object without the prominent trailing tail typical of ordinary comets.[1] Badlands contributor Chris Paul rejected the alien framing outright.[1]
