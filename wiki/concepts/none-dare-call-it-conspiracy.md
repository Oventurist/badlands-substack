# None Dare Call It Conspiracy

None Dare Call It Conspiracy is a book by [[gary-allen|Gary Allen]] that, according to Badlands Media, sold more than five million copies while being ignored by the establishment media.[1]

The work argued that both major American political parties had been captured by "Insiders" associated with the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1] Allen's best-known formulation, offered just before the 1972 elections, was that voters faced no meaningful choice — only "CFR world government advocate Nixon and CFR world government advocate Humphrey," with the rhetoric changed to fool the public.[1] He called on Democrats and Republicans alike to break Insider control of their parties, arguing that either the CFR-types and their supporters must be invited to leave or the "Patriots" must.[1]
