# The Ultimate Cancel Act (SB 1248)

"The Ultimate Cancel Act," designated SB 1248, was a bill filed in the Florida state Senate on Tuesday, February 28, 2023 by Republican state Senator [[blaise-ingoglia|Blaise Ingoglia]] of Spring Hill that would have eliminated the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]]'s ballot status in Florida.[1]

## Provisions

The bill would have required the Florida Secretary of State to cancel the filings of, and de-certify, any political party that supported slavery during the Civil War.[1] Because the Democratic Party adopted pro-slavery planks in its antebellum platforms, the measure was understood to target that party specifically.[1] If enacted, voters registered with any "canceled" party would have been reclassified as non-party-affiliated voters.[1]

## Context

The bill was filed after Florida Democrats sustained what was described as tremendous losses in the preceding election cycle.[1] Ingoglia summarized the logic as: "The Democrat party adopted pro-slavery stances in their party platforms and this bill says that if you have done that in the past, then the Secretary of State shall de-certify and get rid of the party."[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued the bill's practical fate was secondary to its rhetorical effect: whether or not it passed, it forced a national awareness that the Democrats had been the party of slavery and obliged Democrats to explain how they had "changed."[1]
