# 2025 UN Two-State Solution Conference

The 2025 United Nations conference on reviving the two-state solution for Israel and the Palestinians was convened at UN headquarters in New York City in late July 2025, co-chaired by France and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[1] The gathering had been organized by Saudi Arabia and France the previous December.[1]

## The declaration

Seventeen countries, plus the [[european-union|European Union]] and the Arab League, endorsed a seven-page text agreed at the conference.[1] Signatories included Qatar, Saudi Arabia and Egypt.[1] The declaration states that "in the context of ending the war in Gaza, [[hamas|Hamas]] must end its rule in Gaza and hand over its weapons to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]], with international engagement and support, in line with the objective of a sovereign and independent Palestinian State."[1] The text also condemns the Hamas attacks against Israel of October 7, 2023, which launched the war.[1] France called the declaration "both historic and unprecedented."[1]

## US absence and controversy

The United States did not attend the conference and did not sign the declaration alongside France and other European countries.[1] Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and State Department spokeswoman [[tammy-bruce|Tammy Bruce]] publicly rejected the conference as a "publicity stunt" designed to help Hamas; Bruce delivered that characterization at a [[state-department|State Department]] press briefing minutes before the declaration was announced, warning it would prolong the war and embolden Hamas.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the US posture as a disgrace, arguing that the demand for Hamas to surrender to the Palestinian Authority and for its leadership to leave Gaza permanently was unprecedented and marked a significant historic change in Arab politics.[1] He suggested the episode may have been a test of how Rubio and Bruce would handle the situation while President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was traveling in Scotland, and predicted that one of Trump's billionaire friends — likely [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]] — would need to mend diplomatic relations with the Saudis.[1]

## Saudi framing and US opposition

Opening the high-level international conference at the [[united-nations|United Nations]] on Monday, July 28, 2025, Saudi Foreign Minister Prince [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] said that implementation of a [[two-state-solution|two-state solution]] was "the key to regional stability," calling the New York conference "a pivotal milestone toward implementing the two-state solution."[2] He said peace must begin with securing the legitimate rights of the Palestinian people and welcomed French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]]'s stated intention to formally recognize a state of Palestine in September.[2]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] had been planning the meeting since at least December 2024 as part of a broader strategy of leveraging Arab financial power to win European support on a bilateral basis, with results visible at the United Nations and the European Union.[2] [[state-department|State Department]] spokeswoman [[tammy-bruce|Tammy Bruce]] issued a statement condemning the meeting and, in his reading, the concept of a two-state solution itself.[2]
