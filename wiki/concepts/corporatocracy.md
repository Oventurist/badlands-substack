# Corporatocracy

**Corporatocracy** denotes rule exercised through corporations rather than through formally accountable government, and is one of the mechanisms [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] asked ChatGPT to build into its map of globalist warfare.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) His prompt paired it with the use of social media monopolies and with the [[satanic-inversion|satanic principle of inversion]] as warfare tactics, and he later asked specifically for the strategic, operational and tactical use of [[esg|ESG scores]] within the corporatocracy as they align with the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

In the model's synthesis, corporate power is the transmission belt for ideological objectives that could not be legislated directly. ESG scoring pressures corporations into aligning with social-justice ideologies including [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and [[critical-race-theory|CRT]]; rewards organisations promoting secular and globalist values; incentivises support for contraception and population control; and pushes firms to back policies prioritising collective safety over individual rights, as with [[covid-mandates|COVID mandates]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Control over major media outlets, acquired gradually, allows the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]] to shape narratives and marginalise critical voices.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
