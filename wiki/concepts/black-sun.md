# Black Sun (Schwarze Sonne)

The Black Sun, or Schwarze Sonne — also rendered as the Central Sun (German: *Zentral Sonne*) — is an esoteric occult symbol commissioned in marble by [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] on the floor of a central tower at [[wewelsburg-castle|Wewelsburg Castle]].[1]

The concept was introduced by [[helena-blavatsky|Helena Blavatsky]] in *The Secret Doctrine*, where it appears as the invisible centre of the universe, the cause and origin of all existence, and what the Gnostics called the "creative light."[1] In Blavatsky's writings the notion of "black light" is attributed exclusively to a secret Aryan teaching brought from the far North, and she associated the cult rites of this central sun with a legendary ancient people said to have lived beyond the Arctic Circle.[1]

The symbol's placement at Wewelsburg tied this Theosophical cosmology directly to the ritual life of the SS [[new-templar-order|New Templar]] initiates.[1]
