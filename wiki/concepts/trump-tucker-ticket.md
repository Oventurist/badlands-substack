# Trump-Tucker Ticket

The **Trump-Tucker ticket**, sometimes abbreviated "T2," was a speculative 2024 presidential pairing of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with broadcaster [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] as running mate, promoted in a November 2023 [[american-thinker|American Thinker]] blog item that circulated in the Badlands News Brief.[1]

## The argument

The piece framed such a ticket as no longer far-fetched and argued it would put the Make America Great Again movement and the America First alliance "on steroids."[1] Rather than a conventional "balanced ticket," the author described it as a deliberate "double down" on an American exceptionalism platform.[1]

The article contended that a T2 ticket would electrify and energize an already mobilized base while putting globalist elites and what it termed "Deep State hooligans" on notice that the movement would not be intimidated and would not apologize.[1] It further suggested the pairing might hold off "the communists/deep state/globalist elites," result in the jailing of BLM and Antifa criminals, secure the First and Second Amendments, and save what it called the American empire "from the dustpan of history."[1]

## Assessment

The item was rhetorical and aspirational rather than reported: no formal steps toward such a ticket were described, and the piece consisted largely of enthusiastic speculation about the electoral and cultural effects of the pairing.[1]
