# California Reparations Task Force

The California reparations task force was a first-in-the-nation commission established under Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]], with appointments announced in May 2021, to study reparations for African Americans in the state.[1]

## Mandate and limits

The commission carried an expansive mandate to examine the effects of racial discrimination and slavery on African American Californians, a scope Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described as unparalleled in its audacity.[1] However, the body lacked the authority to actually implement policies, which in DeLarme's assessment rendered it completely hollow and ineffective.[1]

## Political function

DeLarme argued that the task force's real value to Newsom was as a campaign credential: the governor could run on the precedent, cloaking himself in the mantle of civil rights without doing a single tangible thing to improve them, and most voters would not know any better.[1] He placed the commission alongside the Senate appointment of [[laphonza-butler|Laphonza Butler]] as a leading example of Newsom's mastery of [[identity-politics|identity politics]].[1]
