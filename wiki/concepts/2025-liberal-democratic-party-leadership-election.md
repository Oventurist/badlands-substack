# 2025 Liberal Democratic Party Leadership Election

The 2025 leadership election of Japan's ruling [[liberal-democratic-party|Liberal Democratic Party]] (LDP), held on October 4, 2025, chose hardline conservative [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]] as party leader, putting her on course to become [[japan|Japan]]'s first female prime minister.[1]

## Course of the vote

Five candidates contested the leadership to replace outgoing prime minister [[shigeru-ishiba|Shigeru Ishiba]].[1] None secured a clear majority in the first round, sending Takaichi and Shinjiro Koizumi into a run-off that went swiftly in Takaichi's favor.[1] A parliamentary vote to confirm Ishiba's replacement as prime minister was expected on October 15.[1]

## Takaichi's position

A former economic security and internal affairs minister with an expansionary fiscal agenda, Takaichi faced the task of rebuilding confidence in the LDP while fending off opposition groups promising stimulus spending and clampdowns on migrants.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited Takaichi's win — alongside [[andrej-babis|Andrej Babiš]]'s victory in the [[2025-czech-parliamentary-election|2025 Czech parliamentary election]], Slovakia's attendance at the Beijing Victory Day parade, and Hungary's opposition to escalation with Russia — as evidence that "the Global Hegemon is dead."[1]
