# Polycrisis

"Polycrisis" is a term for a period of simultaneous, compounding global crises, treated in Badlands commentary as a plan announced in advance rather than a forecast.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing about the controversy over mass citizenship verification of voter registrations, recalled that [[mckinsey|McKinsey]] had told the public a polycrisis was coming and that a sustained crisis cascade subsequently overwhelmed the nation — "as if they were revealing a plan more than making a prediction."[1] She argued the responsible actors were not Democrats but "unipartisan globalists," concluded that "it is, indeed, all connected," and said the apparatus was being dismantled and restructured.[1]

## WEF theme and the October 2024 crisis cascade

"Polycrisis" was the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]'s theme of the year for 2024, a fact [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited sardonically: "It's crazy how good those benevolent globalists are at predicting world events. It's almost like they're planning."[2] Writing on October 4, 2024, she catalogued the simultaneous crises then converging — the [[fema|FEMA]] funding shortfall amid [[hurricane-helene|Hurricane Helene]] recovery, border mismanagement, an energy crisis attributed to draining the strategic petroleum reserves and constraining U.S. energy production, the supply-chain crisis surrounding the [[2024-east-and-gulf-coast-dockworker-strike|dockworker strike]], Middle East escalation, and the economy — arguing that they were interconnected products of policy rather than coincidence.[2]

She summarized the underlying logic as: "If you let them break the law in a crisis, they will create a crisis to break the law," citing Cicero's maxim in *De Legibus* that "in times of war, the law is silent," and Machiavelli's endorsement in *The Prince* that "a wise ruler ought never to keep faith when by doing so it would be against his interests."[2] The alternative formulation "crisis cascade" was credited to [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]].[2]
