# Psychological Operations in Guerrilla Warfare

*Psychological Operations in Guerrilla Warfare* is a Contra-era [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] training manual, declassified only in 2017 and available through a MuckRock FOIA release.[1] Badlands Media presents it as the clearest documentary evidence of the tradecraft underlying what is now marketed as "[[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]]."[1]

## Timing and significance

The manual was published less than a year before the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] was founded, after which intelligence work was reorganized to run through plausibly deniable proxy organizations, or "cut-outs."[1] The article treats this sequence as the hinge of the [[plausible-deniability-network|plausible deniability network]].[1]

## Contents

Section headings flagged in the article's index review include "Creating a Martyr for the Cause," "Selective use of Violence for Propagandistic Effects," and "Implicit and Explicit Use of Terror."[1] Tab C is titled "Emphasis on the Christian Nature of the Guerilla Movement," reflecting a strategy of making inroads with local churches and congregations because the CIA's Marxist adversaries were atheists.[1] Tab F concerns the "Use of Agitators—Including the Hiring of Professional Criminals to Manipulate Mass Meetings and Assemblies which can Result in General Violence" — described as the seeding of agent provocateurs into protests with the explicit aim of generating violence.[1]

The article quotes page 66: the psychological war team "must develop in advance a hostile mental attitude among the target groups, so that at the given moment they can turn their anger into violence, demanding their rights taken away by the regime," with preconditioning campaigns aimed at political parties, professional organizations, students, workers, the unemployed masses, ethnic minorities, "or any other vulnerable or recruitable sector of society."[1] The introduction states that guerrilla warfare as political war makes psychological operations decisive, and that "the target, then, are the minds of the population, the entire population: Our troops, the enemies troops, and the civil population."[1]

## Context

The manual arose from operations in Latin America, where the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], the CIA, and the [[state-department|State Department]] are described as having created the modern drug trade — in this case in Nicaragua — to fund opposition to rising left-wing Marxist groups.[1] Those movements grew from the 1950s onward, coinciding with Cuba's communist revolution, and displaced US-friendly leaders who had held the status quo since the Banana Wars of the late 19th and early 20th centuries.[1] The article argues the manual's word "guerilla" can be read as "paramilitary" — an informal assembly with the capacity to use force, which is precisely what a rent-a-riot is: paramilitary street muscle.[1]
