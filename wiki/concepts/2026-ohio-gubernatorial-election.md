# 2026 Ohio Gubernatorial Election

[[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] won the Republican primary for Governor of Ohio in the May 2026 contest and is expected to win the general election.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized Ramaswamy's trajectory as "quite the journey": a presidential run, a brief period hyping DOGE, and then a pivot to the Ohio governor's race.[1] She qualified the expectation of victory with the observation that "elections are still fake," suggesting that with Ramaswamy as the ostensible chief executive of the Buckeye State they might be "even more fake than we thought," and closed with the single-word accelerationist verdict, "Accelerate."[1]
