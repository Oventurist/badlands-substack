# SOGI Curriculum

The Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity (SOGI) curriculum is a school program used in Canadian and American classrooms and treated by Badlands Media as a principal vehicle for introducing [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]] to children.[1]

## Badlands critique

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]] and other organizations distribute flyers and propaganda at Canadian and American schools, and that this material is a natural outcome of the culture the SOGI curriculum creates.[1] In his account, the curriculum instills in children an obsession with [[queer-theory|queer theory]] alongside a preoccupation with gender expression and sexual identity, training them to view the world through a narrow, gender-based, hyper-sexualized lens.[1]

Deschamps argued that this material should infuriate any typical parent, but that [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] dismisses such concerns as lies.[1] He described this dismissal as a deliberate feature of the operation: because Trudeau is not addressing the majority, he can make demonstrably false statements while keeping his radicalized base inside a media echo chamber.[1]

## Parental opposition and the CAHN frame

Deschamps noted that when Muslim communities in Canada opposed gender ideology in schools, the government-aligned response was to portray parents as ignorant victims of hateful propaganda misled by far-right radicals lying about classroom content, and as pawns in the American right's attacks on the LGBTQ2S+ community — a characterization he attributed to the [[canadian-anti-hate-network|Canadian Anti-Hate Network]].[1] He treated this as a textbook instance of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] supporting an [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric]] political operation.[1]

He also observed that both the Liberal and Conservative parties voted against parental rights when they enacted the so-called conversion treatment bill, arguing that the underlying conflict is not left versus right but a matter of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[1]
