# Gold Clause Cases

The Gold Clause Cases are a group of United States Supreme Court decisions concerning contractual clauses requiring payment in gold, cited by Badlands Media as part of the judicial foundation for the federal government's control of the money supply.[1]

## Role in the Badlands argument

Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] groups the Gold Clause Cases with the [[legal-tender-cases|Legal Tender Cases]] as past Supreme Court rulings that granted the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] power and monopoly over American money.[1] He argues these precedents should be overturned in the same way the modern originalist Court has reversed other long-standing rulings.[1]

## Anticipated showdown

As dollar devaluation accelerates, Lange predicts a showdown at the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] to overturn the fiat money monopoly and restore the constitutional standard of real money — gold and silver coin, or notes fully backed by and redeemable in such coin.[1] He expects the Court to rule for the states, with the states then exercising their constitutional rights to keep the country on a [[gold-standard|gold standard]] and prevent future centralization of the money supply.[1]
