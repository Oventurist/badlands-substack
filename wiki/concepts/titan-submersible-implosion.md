# Titan Submersible Implosion

The Titan submersible implosion was a June 2023 deep-sea disaster in which [[oceangate|OceanGate]]'s Titan vessel was destroyed during a dive to the wreck of the Titanic, killing all five people aboard.[1]

## Course of events

The submersible went missing during the dive, prompting an international search.[1] Among the dead was OceanGate chief executive [[stockton-rush|Stockton Rush]].[1]

## Prior warnings

[[david-lochridge|David Lochridge]] had been sacked by OceanGate in 2018 after warning about safety issues with the program.[1] He later told the BBC that he had hoped the outcome would not occur but "just knew if they kept carrying on the way they were going and with that deficient equipment, then there would be an incident."[1]

## Coast Guard findings

A report from the [[us-coast-guard|US Coast Guard]] published in August 2025 found that OceanGate's failures over safety, testing and maintenance were the main cause of the disaster.[1] Lochridge's verdict on the venture was that "people were sold a lie."[1]
