# Massachusetts v. EPA

Massachusetts v. EPA is the U.S. Supreme Court decision that enabled the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] to treat greenhouse gases as pollutants subject to regulation under the [[clean-air-act|Clean Air Act]].[1]

## Significance in the Badlands framing

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the case enabled the 2009 [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]], describing the resulting regime as a "bureaucratic nightmare" that unlocked sweeping carbon mandates across energy, manufacturing, transportation, and logistics.[1] Environmental groups challenging the 2026 rescission cited Supreme Court precedent upholding the finding as a basis for litigation.[1]
