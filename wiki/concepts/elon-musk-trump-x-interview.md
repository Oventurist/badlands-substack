# Elon Musk–Trump X Interview

The **Elon Musk–Trump X interview** was a long-form live conversation between [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] held on Monday, August 12, 2024 at 5 p.m. Pacific time on [[x|X]].[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] covered the event before, during and after its broadcast, streaming immediate reaction and analysis.[1]

## Reception within the Badlands orbit

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the event "awesome," writing that Trump had demonstrated mastery of long-form podcasting and had "put the rest of us on notice," and drawing a contrast with the public performance of Joe Biden and [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] over the prior four years.[1] He questioned whether it was a coincidence that Trump returned to X and made his first post since his January 2021 ban on the same day as the conversation, concluding that "Trump and Elon are working together, and nothing is as it seems."[1]

## Trump's return to X

Trump resumed posting on X ahead of the interview, publishing three campaign videos that emphasized his vision for the country and attacked his opponent, whom he labeled "San Francisco Radical Kamala Harris."[1] The return came amid Harris' rise in election polling.[1] His previous post had been on August 24, 2023, when he circulated his mug shot with the caption "Election interference. Never surrender!"; before that he had not tweeted since January 8, 2021.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the timing as "Coordination = Control," arguing that while the surface explanation was electoral leverage on the world's largest communications platform, the convergence functioned as a proof of "patriot plans" within the [[information-war|Info War]].[1] He noted the claim that Musk had been the largest Department of Defense contractor under the Trump administration and had "taken over" what he described as the Deep State's largest fifth-generation warfare center before publicly endorsing Trump: "First, they divided to conquer. Now, they're coming together to unify."[1]
