# Global Digital Health Certificates

**Global digital health certificates** are a documentation system, proposed across multiple articles and annexes of the draft amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]], that would record an individual's vaccination and health status in digital or paper format.[1] Badlands Media identified the certificate scheme as one of the most consequential elements of the amendment package, arguing that several of the proposed amendments appear "more concerned with public surveillance rather than public health."[1]

## Context within the IHR amendments

The certificate proposal sits alongside amendments that would grant the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] authority to require medical examinations, proof of prophylaxis, proof of vaccine, and to implement contact tracing, quarantine, and treatment under Article 18.[1] Taken together with proposals converting WHO proclamations from advisory recommendations into legally binding instruments, critics argued the certificates would function as a permanent instrument of population control rather than an emergency measure.[1]

The article noted that a total of 307 IHR amendments had been proposed, that they were submitted on 30 September of the prior year, and that no updated negotiated draft had been made public since — leaving the certificate proposal, like the rest of the package, "cloaked in secrecy."[1]
