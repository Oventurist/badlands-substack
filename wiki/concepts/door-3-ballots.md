# Door 3 Ballots

"Door 3" is the name given to the receptacles into which [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] voters deposited ballots that could not be tabulated on site during the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]], after widespread failures of [[ballot-on-demand-printers|ballot-on-demand printers]] and tabulators on November 8, 2022.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] reported that her own polling place used a container labeled "BOX 3" rather than a door, and that she and her husband were told the ballots would be transported to the Maricopa County Tabulation and Election Center accompanied by both a Republican and a Democrat — an assurance she found unpersuasive given the possibility of nominal party members on either side.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Chain-of-custody allegations

The November 19, 2022 letter from Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] treated Door 3 handling as one of three major categories of concern, stating that the county "appears to have failed to adhere to the statutory guidelines in segregating, counting, tabulating, tallying, and transporting the 'Door 3' ballots."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) The letter recorded the county's own admission that at some voting locations non-tabulated Door 3 ballots were commingled with tabulated ballots, and cited a sworn complaint from an election observer alleging that more than 1,700 non-tabulated Door 3 ballots from a single voting location had been placed in black duffle bags intended for tabulated ballots.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) Because these questions bore on the county's ability to lawfully certify results, Wright demanded answers before the statutory [[canvassing|canvass]] deadline of November 28, 2022.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
