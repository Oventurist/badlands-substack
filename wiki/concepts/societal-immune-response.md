# Societal Immune Response

The "societal immune response" is the central metaphor of the Badlands Media article "Q is Our Best Fren", which models the alleged [[cabal]] as a parasitic infection of civilisation and [[anons]], truthers, Patriots and independent citizen journalists as the white blood cells of society's immune system, alongside sound elements within the military and law enforcement.[1]

## The problem statement

The article frames the White hats' dilemma as extracting the parasite without killing the patient — removing the cabal without destroying civilisation itself — which requires orchestrating an immune response among the masses.[1] An immune system cannot fight a pathogen it cannot identify, and identification requires the capacity to sort things into categories according to a set of instructions.[1] If those instructions are inadequate, they must be updated.[1]

The worst case, the article argues, is a pathogen capable of tricking the immune system into attacking itself, and one that alters host behaviour by hijacking internal communication systems — a point the author connects to the concentration of media ownership among a handful of corporations.[1] Under such conditions the immune system lacks proper instructions and cannot communicate freely enough to update them.[1]

## Coded communication as the solution

The article concludes that the only remedy is for one part of the immune system to devise a way of communicating with the rest undetected by the pathogen, which necessitates coded communication.[1] This is presented as the explanation for the encrypted, semi-cryptic character of the Q drops, and thus as evidence that [[qanon|Q]] is working on the public's behalf.[1] Anons are described as the relay mechanism distributing the updated instructions society's immune response requires.[1]

## Historical application

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 victory over [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] is identified as the moment when truther efforts coalesced into a credible threat to the cabal and the mainstream media's reign of influence began to end.[1] The Covid pandemic is then read as the cabal's system-wide pathogenic counterstrike, originally intended as an endgame of total enslavement but repurposed against Trump and the awakening public.[1] The article argues the pandemic exposed the cabal as never before, since supposedly independent governments worldwide acted in lockstep to shut down economies and administer experimental mRNA products.[1] The author speculates that at least one further system-wide attack remains, and that [[the-storm|The Storm]] will come either in response to it or to provoke it.[1]
