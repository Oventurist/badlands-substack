# Dominion v. Fox News Defamation Lawsuit

The $1.6 billion defamation suit brought by [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] against [[fox-news|Fox News]] produced court filings in February 2023 showing that the network's leading hosts privately doubted the voter fraud allegations advanced on air by allies of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Disclosed communications

According to a redacted summary judgment brief filed by Dominion's attorneys and reported by the [[associated-press|Associated Press]], host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] told a producer on November 16, 2020 that "Sidney Powell is lying" about having evidence of election fraud.[1] Carlson also described [[sidney-powell|Powell]] in texts as an "unguided missile" and "dangerous as hell."[1] Fellow host [[laura-ingraham|Laura Ingraham]] told Carlson that Powell is "a complete nut. No one will work with her. Ditto with Rudy," a reference to former New York mayor [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]].[1] [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]] said in a deposition that "that whole narrative that Sidney was pushing, I did not believe it for one second."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Setting the lawsuit itself aside, [[just-human|Just Human]] argued that the off-camera statements illustrate the mindset at Fox News and explain both its eagerness to call Arizona as early as possible on election night and its reluctance to give airtime to fraud claims: the hosts did not value the claims in the first place.[1] Just Human further contended that Fox News "misses the pre-Trump days," when it was much easier to conceal the existence of the [[uniparty|Uniparty]] and when election fraud "was just part of the political game."[1] The disclosures form part of the broader record surrounding [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]].[1]
