# 2025 Chinese Student Visa Opening

In August 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said that 600,000 Chinese students would be allowed into the United States to study at American colleges amid ongoing trade talks with China.[1]

## Announcement

Speaking at the [[white-house|White House]] on Monday, August 25, 2025, Trump signaled a potential thaw in U.S.–China relations following escalating tariffs and restrictions on Chinese students.[1] "I hear so many stories that we're not going to allow their students," Trump told reporters. "We're going to allow their students to come in. It's very important, 600,000 students. It's very important. But we're going to get along with China."[1]

## Reaction

The announcement drew immediate criticism from America First commentators. Fox News host [[laura-ingraham|Laura Ingraham]] grilled Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] on how admitting 600,000 Chinese students could be reconciled with an "America First" agenda, a line of questioning Badlands contributor CannCon endorsed with the comment "She's not wrong."[1]
