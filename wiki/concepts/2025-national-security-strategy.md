# 2025 National Security Strategy

The 2025 National Security Strategy (NSS) is the strategy document unveiled by the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in December 2025, one of whose central pillars is a plan to update the more than 200-year-old foreign policy statement known as the [[monroe-doctrine|Monroe Doctrine]].[1]

## Western Hemisphere pillar

The document states that "after years of neglect, the United States will reassert and enforce the Monroe Doctrine to restore American preeminence in the Western Hemisphere, and to protect our homeland and our access to key geographies throughout the region."[1] It goes on to lay out a "[[trump-corollary-to-the-monroe-doctrine|Trump Corollary]] to the Monroe Doctrine" prioritizing American-led cooperative efforts to combat mass migration, drug trafficking, and "hostile foreign incursion or ownership of key assets."[1] Coverage framed the strategy as the latest evolution of a doctrine that has been held up as either an argument for peace and non-intervention or as justification for American imperialism, depending on how successive U.S. presidents interpreted it.[1]

## Commentary and European dimension

Badlands host Burning Bright argued that combining the new National Security Strategy with Trump's recognition of the anniversary of the Monroe Doctrine positioned what he called the "American Restoration" as a callback to the Revolution, directed at the same "Invisible Enemy" as before — "the one who never left."[1] In a separate item, Burning Bright described the NSS as doctrinal leverage against the [[european-union|European Union]]: the Trump Corollary integrated into the NSS asserts U.S. dominance in the Western Hemisphere and limits European interference in regional affairs, while criticizing Europe's current path as misaligned with U.S. interests and calling for reforms that could amount to de facto regime change in Brussels.[1]

## Middle East applications

The strategy also touched on the Middle East: Trump stated in the National Security Strategy that he wants [[turkey|Turkey]] included in the [[international-stabilization-force|International Stabilization Force]] and present in [[gaza|Gaza]], a position that put Washington at odds with Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]
