# Wizards & Warlocks

**"Wizards & Warlocks"** is a phrase appearing in [[qanon|Q]] drop #80 of 5 November 2017, immediately after Q confirmed the [[alice-and-wonderland|"Alice & Wonderland"]] cipher identifying [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] as the "Bloody Wonderland."[1]

The [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series treats the term as one of the drops' unresolved codewords, with its author stating plainly that he is not sure many readers have figured it out yet.[1] In the chapter's sequence it sits between the resolution of the Alice cipher and Q's reference to Snow White, which the author associates with the [[snow-white-supercomputers|CIA supercomputers]] and, alternatively, with [[operation-snow-white|Operation Snow White]].[1]

## The tarmac tip-off

"[[the-biggest-boomerang-in-history|The Biggest Boomerang In History]]" quotes Q drop 36 of November 2, 2017, in which the wizards and warlocks are said to have tipped off a local reporter about the supposedly unscheduled stop that produced the [[clinton-lynch-tarmac-meeting|tarmac meeting]] between [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]], while the NSA under Admiral Mike Rogers had the meeting miscatalogued under a false identity so that bad actors could not locate it and would believe no logs existed.[2] The drop then asks what really happened when the wizards and warlocks revealed what they had, and whether [[james-comey|Comey]] was forced into the spotlight shortly afterwards, not by choice.[2]

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] glosses the term as the "guardians" of intelligence and credits them with turning a private meeting into a national news story that ultimately broke the [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] protection network.[2] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.

## Guardians of intelligence

In "The Biggest Cover-up in American History," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cites [[qanon|Q]] drop #2624 of December 12, 2018, in which an anonymous poster asked who or what the Wizards and Warlocks are and Q answered "'Guardians' of intelligence."[3] Lange identifies them as military intelligence, waging a long war against a cabal of puppet masters, and notes that Q drop #80 of November 5, 2017 listed "Snow White. Wizards & Warlocks" alongside the "[[alice-and-wonderland|Alice & Wonderland]]" formulation on the day [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]] was arrested — a pairing he reads as Q identifying the real combatants, with [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] merely a pawn.[3]
