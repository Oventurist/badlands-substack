# Wizards & Warlocks

**"Wizards & Warlocks"** is a phrase appearing in [[qanon|Q]] drop #80 of 5 November 2017, immediately after Q confirmed the [[alice-and-wonderland|"Alice & Wonderland"]] cipher identifying [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] as the "Bloody Wonderland."[1]

The [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series treats the term as one of the drops' unresolved codewords, with its author stating plainly that he is not sure many readers have figured it out yet.[1] In the chapter's sequence it sits between the resolution of the Alice cipher and Q's reference to Snow White, which the author associates with the [[snow-white-supercomputers|CIA supercomputers]] and, alternatively, with [[operation-snow-white|Operation Snow White]].[1]
