# Moldovan Billion Dollar Bank Fraud

The Moldovan billion-dollar bank fraud, popularly known as the "theft of the century," was a scheme carried out between 2011 and 2014 in which roughly $1 billion — representing about one-eighth of [[moldova|Moldova]]'s GDP — was stolen from the country.[1] It was orchestrated by the oligarch [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]] working closely with Israeli-Moldovan businessman [[ilan-shor|Ilan Shor]] and fellow oligarchs [[vyacheslav-platon|Vyacheslav Platon]] and [[vlad-filat|Vlad Filat]].[1]

## Mechanism

The model was simple and based on the use of offshore shell companies, which laundered money and received fraudulent loans issued through the Moldovan Central Bank.[1] That bank had itself taken over three major financial institutions — Banca de Economii, Unibank and Banca Sociala — which extended fraudulent loans to businesses controlled by Ilan Shor and others within Plahotniuc's network totalling roughly $1 billion.[1] Some $100 million was used directly by Plahotniuc to purchase banks, businesses, hotels and other luxury real estate around Europe and Moldova.[1]

## Accountability

Although the facts of the fraud could not long be kept secret, Plahotniuc's control over the levers of justice — especially the Constitutional Court, packed with his partisans — ensured that little could be done to bring him to justice.[1] Filat was sentenced in 2016 to three years in prison for bribes and participation in the heist; Shor was charged with laundering and embezzlement in absentia but never saw a jail cell; and Platon was found guilty in April 2017 and given 18 years.[1] Plahotniuc never faced a single charge in Moldova, though Russian courts convicted him of attempted murder in 2017 and of drug trafficking and money laundering in 2019.[1]

The scandal devastated public confidence in a nation ranked lowest on the European Union poverty index, where nearly 50% of the male population lives and works abroad as cheap labour; Plahotniuc's disapproval rating reached 96%, and the [[democratic-party-of-moldova|Democratic Party]] lost power to the Socialists under [[igor-dodon|Igor Dodon]].[1]
