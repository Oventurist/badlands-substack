# December 2024 Dow Losing Streak

The December 2024 Dow losing streak was a ten-session decline in the Dow Jones Industrial Average that culminated on Wednesday, December 18, 2024, when the index fell 1,123.03 points, or 2.58%, to 42,326.87 — its worst losing streak since an eleven-day slide in 1974.[1] The Wednesday decline was the index's worst since August and only the second time that year it lost more than 1,000 points in a single session.[1]

The selloff was driven by a disappointing rate outlook from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1] The central bank reduced its overnight borrowing rate by a quarter point to a target range of 4.25% to 4.5%, as expected, but signaled that it would cut rates only twice in 2025 — fewer than the four cuts indicated in its previous forecast.[1] Fed Chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] said the recent cuts allowed the central bank to "be more cautious as we consider more adjustments to our policy rate."[1]

Traders had hoped the Fed would remain aggressive with rate cuts in 2025 to fuel the bull market further.[1] The S&P 500 lost 2.95% to 5,872.16 and the Nasdaq Composite shed 3.56% to 19,392.69, with losses intensifying into the close.[1] Treasury yields jumped following the cautious outlook, pressuring share prices, and the 10-year Treasury yield crossed above 4.50%.[1]
