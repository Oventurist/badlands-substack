# Nova Music Festival

The Nova music festival was an open-air electronic music event held in southern Israel near the Gaza border and was among the principal targets of the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 attacks]] by [[hamas|Hamas]].[1] It has since become a central reference point in Badlands Media's argument that the massacre was enabled by deliberate Israeli security failures.[1]

## Badlands allegations of a setup

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that just two days before the festival began, the event was relocated to a site in close proximity to the Gaza border with no enhanced [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] security and no quick reaction force on standby.[1] He further asserted that when the IDF finally arrived — seven hours later — its helicopters and tanks "accidentally" killed hundreds of Israeli citizens.[1]

He argued that these circumstances alone made the episode feel like a setup, and that the subsequent revelation that the IDF and Israeli intelligence had advance knowledge of Hamas's plan to raid Israel and kidnap 250 people converted that suspicion into what he described as an unsettling certainty.[1] Drawing an analogy to 9/11, he contended that such operations reflect the ethos of an elite class that treats populations as fodder and uses identity labels to prevent organized peaceful resistance.[1]

## Israeli police friendly-fire admission

An Israeli police investigation into the Hamas attack on the festival, reported by Haaretz on November 18, 2023, found that an Israeli attack helicopter had killed some of the attendees.[2] According to a police source, a combat helicopter that arrived at the scene from the Ramat David base fired at Hamas fighters and other Palestinians who had crossed through the border fence from Gaza into Israel, but also fired on some of the Israelis attending the festival.[2] Police placed the total death toll at the site at 364 people.[2]

The Israeli military and rescue services had previously claimed that 260 Israelis were killed at the festival, all by Hamas and Palestinians in a deliberate massacre, making the police finding the first official acknowledgement that Israeli forces killed some of their own at Nova.[2] Earlier reports in Israeli media had described a comparable episode at Be'eri, a settlement also near the Gaza border, where Israeli forces opened fire — including with tank shells — on homes in which Hamas fighters were holding Israeli captives, killing both captives and fighters.[2]

## Claims of friendly fire

Badlands has argued that the destruction visible in photographs of the festival parking lot is inconsistent with an attack carried out solely by Hamas infantry.[3] [[canncon|CannCon]], a Marine veteran of Iraq, said on [[badlands-daily|Badlands Daily]] in November 2023 that the damage was indicative of hellfire missiles launched from helicopters and could not have been caused by rifles and RPGs.[3] The claim accompanied coverage of Israeli helicopter pilot testimony and investigative reporting cited by Badlands as evidence that military personnel were ordered to fire on Israeli citizens on October 7, killing hundreds.[3]
