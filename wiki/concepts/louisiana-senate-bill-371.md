# Louisiana Senate Bill 371

Louisiana Senate Bill 371 is a 2024 measure that would authorize physical (surgical) castration, as opposed to chemical castration, for persons convicted of certain sex offenses against children.[1]

## Provisions

The bill, sponsored by state Senator [[regina-barrow|Regina Barrow]], a Democrat, would sentence offenders aged 17 and older convicted of the rape of a victim under the age of 13 to be physically castrated.[1] The procedure is contingent upon a determination by a court-appointed medical expert that the defendant is an appropriate candidate for surgery, a determination that must be made within 60 days of the imposition of sentence.[1] Where the offender is sentenced to incarceration or confinement, the procedure must be performed no later than one week prior to release.[1]

The Louisiana Department of Public Safety and Corrections would oversee the procedure, which "will not be performed if not medically appropriate."[1] An offender who fails to appear or refuses to undergo the procedure may be charged with failure to comply with the court order and sentenced to imprisonment for between three and five years, without benefit of parole, probation, or suspension of sentence.[1]

## Passage

The bill passed the Louisiana House 74–24 and the Senate 29–9, and in early June 2024 advanced to the desk of Republican Governor [[jeff-landry|Jeff Landry]] for signature or veto.[1] The measure was reported by [[epoch-times|The Epoch Times]].[1]
