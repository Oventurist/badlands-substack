# Secure America Act

The Secure America Act is the name given in Badlands commentary to the major immigration and border-security package signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in June 2026.

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that its passage and signing left the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] "fully funded," providing tens of billions of dollars in additional resources for DHS, [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], and [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] through the remainder of the president's term — roughly $70 billion in additional immigration-enforcement funding over three years.

The Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] links the signing directly to the department's announcements about the scale of child trafficking, noting that the legislation funds new resources for child-exploitation investigators and supports the surge of ICE and [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] personnel into sanctuary jurisdictions. He characterises the measure as evidence of an administration that has "chosen to fund the hunters rather than the networks."
