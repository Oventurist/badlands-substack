# Secure America Act

The **Secure America Act** is the name given by Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to the major immigration and border-security funding package signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in June 2026.[1]

## Provisions

The legislation provides tens of billions of dollars in additional funding for the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]], [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], and [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] through the remainder of Trump's term, with the administration announcing that DHS was thereby "fully funded."[1] Reporting cited by the Brief put the additional immigration-enforcement allocation at roughly $70 billion over three years.[1]

## Reception

Burning Bright wrote that the signing arrived alongside DHS announcements detailing what he called the true, horrifying scale of [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]] that exploded under previous administrations, and read the two together as a "bright signal": a president treating ICE and Border Patrol personnel as heroes while moving to locate missing children and prosecute the networks responsible.[1] He noted that the package funds new resources for child-exploitation investigators and supports ICE and Homeland Security Investigations surges into sanctuary jurisdictions.[1]

He also called the moment a dark signal, because in his view the enforcement campaign surfaced "inside a sea of engineered noise" — principally the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein Narrative]] — whose function appeared to be distraction from and subversion of precisely that campaign.[1]
