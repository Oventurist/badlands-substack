# Iran General License X

Iran General License X is a temporary 60-day general license issued in June 2026 by the [[office-of-foreign-assets-control|Office of Foreign Assets Control]] (OFAC) of the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|United States Department of the Treasury]], authorizing transactions related to Iranian crude oil, petrochemical products, and petroleum products through August 21, 2026.[1]

## Background

Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] said the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration had reached a framework agreement with [[iran|Iran]] following ongoing talks in Switzerland, including commitments on free transit through the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] and on allowing [[international-atomic-energy-agency|IAEA]] inspections.[1] Bessent announced that Treasury would issue a temporary 60-day general license authorizing the production, delivery, and sale of Iranian oil as part of the arrangement.[1]

## Scope

A Treasury press release confirmed the issuance of the license and described its terms.[1] The license permits activities tied to production, transport, shipping services, and related maritime operations, and allows certain payments to be made in United States dollars.[1] It also includes limited authorizations for imports into the United States when tied to otherwise permitted transactions.[1]
