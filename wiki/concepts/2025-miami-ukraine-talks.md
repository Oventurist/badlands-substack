# 2025 Miami Ukraine Talks

The 2025 Miami Ukraine talks were a weekend of separate United States-hosted meetings in Florida in late December 2025 aimed at ending the [[russia-ukraine-war|war in Ukraine]].[1]

## Course of the talks

White House envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] met his Russian counterpart [[kirill-dmitriev|Kirill Dmitriev]] and, separately, Ukrainian national security adviser [[rustem-umerov|Rustem Umerov]], describing both sets of discussions on Sunday, December 21, 2025 as "productive and constructive."[1] The readouts nonetheless offered no clear breakthrough toward ending the war.[1] Witkoff's statement said Ukraine "remains fully committed to achieving a just and sustainable peace," with the shared priority being to stop the killing, ensure guaranteed security, and create conditions for Ukraine's recovery and long-term prosperity.[1]

The meetings followed the [[trump-ukraine-peace-plan|Trump administration's peace proposal]], an early draft of which Kyiv officials and their European Union backers had called unworkable.[1] Negotiators for Russia, Ukraine, Europe and the United States continued to hash out proposals over the preceding weeks, though the two belligerents never negotiated directly, meeting only separately with US officials.[1] Representatives of both Russia and Ukraine shared Witkoff's remarks on social media but added no details on possible progress.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] and the Russians were aligning with President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s vision for post-war Ukraine, and that Dmitriev and other Russian diplomats had spent the weekend in Miami "hammering out the details" of a peace deal.[1] Judging by the attendees, he suggested the sessions concerned economic cooperation rather than a military armistice — implying the peace deal was already effectively done and that the parties were negotiating economic collaboration meant to smooth over earlier disputes.[1] He tied the timing to [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s planned pre-New Year visit to Trump, which he characterized as an attempt to subvert the peace process.[1]
