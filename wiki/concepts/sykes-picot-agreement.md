# Sykes-Picot Agreement

The Sykes-Picot Agreement is the 1916 secret arrangement that carved up the Middle East into the national borderlines that persist today, drawn in the interests of Britain and France.[1]

## Contemporary invocation

The agreement re-entered Badlands coverage in June 2025 when [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]], the US ambassador to Türkiye and special envoy for Syria, published a post on X referencing it.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the reference — which he likened to the ending of the film *Lawrence of Arabia* — as a signal about the evolving diplomatic architecture of the region, alongside Barrack's statement days earlier that "Israel could be left behind" as Syria policy shifted toward Turkey.[1]
