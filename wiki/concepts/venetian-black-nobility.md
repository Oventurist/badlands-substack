# Venetian Black Nobility

The **Venetian Black Nobility** is a term used in conspiracy literature, and in Ryan DeLarme's Badlands Media series "The Men Behind the Curtain," for a contingent of old European families said to descend from the oligarchy of Venice and to stand behind — and above — the more visible banking dynasties of the modern era.[1]

## Role in the series' thesis

DeLarme reports that while some authors believe the Morgans and the Rockefellers were merely gofers for the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]], others claim the Rothschilds themselves were a front group for even more well-hidden interests operating from Geneva together with families descended from what was once called the Venetian Black Nobility or the "Lombard Banking Dynasty."[1] He stresses repeatedly that "even these seemingly unrivaled Rothschilds were themselves being used by a much older and intensely more powerful order."[1]

The Rothschild practice of funding both sides of a war is likewise described as not purely Rothschild in origin but as harking back to the Lombards of Venice.[1] Agents of Venice and Geneva said to have been working throughout the world in the nineteenth century include [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]], Giuseppe Mazzini, Antonio Schinella Conti and [[albert-pike|Albert Pike]]; the "Enlightenment" itself is described in the article as an era manufactured by agents of the high cabal such as Abbe Conti and [[isaac-newton|Sir Isaac Newton]].[1] DeLarme states these groups will be treated at length in a follow-up [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] series called "Charting the Cabal."[1]
