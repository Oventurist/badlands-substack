# Trump Executive Privilege Claim Over Pence Grand Jury Testimony

The **Trump executive privilege claim over Pence's grand jury testimony** is a sealed legal dispute that became public in March 2023, in which former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] asked a federal court to block former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] from testifying to a grand jury about certain matters covered by executive privilege.[1] The request formed part of the criminal investigation into efforts to overturn Trump's 2020 election loss.[1]

## Filing and procedural posture

The request was submitted secretly in a sealed proceeding on Friday, March 3, 2023, according to sources familiar with the move.[1] It followed earlier indications from Trump's team to special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] — who oversees the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s criminal investigations of Trump — that the former president intended to assert privilege over Pence's testimony, and came after the DOJ had asked a judge to compel Pence's appearance before the grand jury.[1] Observers noted that it was unclear how long the sealed proceedings would take, and that the dispute might be resolved by the district court, appealed to a federal appeals court, or eventually reach the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|US Supreme Court]].[1]

## Pence's separate Speech or Debate Clause argument

Pence advanced his own, distinct objection to testifying, arguing that his role on January 6, 2021 as President of the Senate is protected by the Constitution's Speech or Debate Clause.[1] He had already said he was willing to take that argument all the way to the Supreme Court.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] described the situation as "a very interesting, and unprecedented, court battle," highlighting that both Trump and Pence were working to block the testimony on different legal theories, and said he would not be surprised if the matter reached the Supreme Court.[1]
