# 2023 Russia-Africa Summit

The 2023 Russia-Africa summit was a diplomatic gathering held in St. Petersburg and hosted by Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in late July 2023.[1]

## Prigozhin's appearance

[[wagner-group|Wagner]] chief [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]] was photographed at the summit, appearing on the sidelines of the event just one month after launching a failed mutiny against the Russian military leadership.[1] Two pictures circulated on social media on Thursday, July 27, showed Prigozhin in casual jeans and a white shirt, smiling as he greeted visiting officials.[1]

In the first picture, posted to Facebook by an Africa-based aide, Prigozhin shook hands with a suited African delegate identified by the watchdog All Eyes on Wagner as the chief of protocol to [[faustin-touadera|Faustin Touadéra]], president of the [[central-african-republic|Central African Republic]].[1] In the second picture, Prigozhin, in the same outfit, shook hands with the "director of Afrique media" according to a Wagner-branded Telegram channel.[1]

## Niger coup claim

In a subsequent post, the Orchestra Wagner Telegram channel claimed credit for the [[2023-niger-coup|coup in Niger]] that took place on the eve of the St. Petersburg summit, although the claim could not be independently verified.[1]
