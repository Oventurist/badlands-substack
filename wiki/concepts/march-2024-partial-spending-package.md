# March 2024 Partial Spending Package

The March 2024 partial spending package was an appropriations bill signed by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] on Saturday, March 23, 2024, funding several federal agencies including border and immigration enforcement.[1]

## Contents

The package funded [[customs-and-border-protection|US Border Patrol]] and [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], bumping their budgets up by a combined $4 billion.[1] The [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] received a $1 billion increase; Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described HHS as the entity that contracts with NGOs and moves what she called criminal invaders and their trafficking victims throughout the interior of the country.[1] The [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] was also funded in the package, including a 5.2 percent pay increase for military personnel.[1]

## Relationship to the supplemental packages

Ashe in America noted that the President was still asking Congress to pass separate supplemental spending packages on national security and the border despite these increases.[1] She argued that "you'd be forgiven for believing these supplementals are just money laundering endeavors," and credited Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] with preventing their passage to that point.[1]

She predicted that the regime would use incidents such as the El Paso border surge and the arrest of MS-13 leader [[freddy-ivan-jandres-parada|Freddy Ivan Jandres-Parada]] to push for the border supplemental despite the recent $4 billion and $1 billion increases, calling it an easy prediction "because it's never been about securing the border."[1] Her conclusion was that officials were "looting the treasury while enabling invasion," that the pattern had become obvious, and that "cornered animals are notoriously dangerous."[1]
