# Forced Conscription in Ukraine

Forced conscription in Ukraine refers to the practice, widely documented in video footage circulating during 2023, of Ukrainian military recruitment officers seizing young men off the street and pressing them into military service.[1]

## Background

As [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s frontline manpower began to dwindle, military leaders grew increasingly desperate to locate new recruits to propel the counteroffensive against Russia forward, while the number of young people volunteering for such service plummeted.[1] Recent videos of young Ukrainian men being conscripted across the country circulated in popular encrypted messaging applications inside Ukraine, and those fearful of being sent to the front actively engaged in evasive and, in some instances, illegal tactics to avoid that fate — with many young men reportedly afraid to leave their homes at all.[1]

## Documented incidents

The Hungarian newspaper Magyar Nemzet reported that brutal mobilization by Ukrainian recruitment officers had been occurring for a year and a half by mid-2023.[1] According to that reporting, "many conscripted men are taken straight off the street by uniformed men," and in Subcarpathia a surveillance camera recorded authorities abducting a man from his bicycle in broad daylight as he attempted to go to a store.[1] The seizure took place on a street in a small village in the Municipal District of Munkács, carried out by police and conscription officers, with the victim's bicycle left lying in the road.[1] The account was published by [[remix-news|Remix News]].[1]
