# FIM-92 Stinger

The FIM-92 Stinger is an American man-portable air-defense system (MANPADS) that became the iconic weapon of the [[soviet-afghan-war|Soviet-Afghan War]].[1]

During the expansion of [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]] under the [[ronald-reagan|Reagan]] administration, the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] increased both funding and the volume of equipment delivered to the Afghan rebels, and among the weapons provided was the then state-of-the-art Stinger, with at least 2,300 units shipped into [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1] The system gave the [[mujahideen]] the ability to threaten Soviet air power and is treated in the Badlands account as emblematic of how thoroughly the United States armed the factions that would later form the [[taliban]].[1]
