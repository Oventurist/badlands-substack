# Follow the Money

"Follow the money" and its companion maxim "Follow the family" are the investigative methods "A Family Affair" adopts from Q.[1] Corruption can be revealed by tracing money, the author writes, but equally by tracing family: family connections show who is loyal to the country and who are traitors, and expose corruption hidden beyond the mainstream media narrative — an example being Q drop #3381.[1]

The article applies the method comparatively. The media has been forced to reveal Biden family corruption, whose crimes appear rooted in greed; [[bush-family|Bush family]] corruption is said to be far more evil, involving far more money, an indifference to human life, decades above the law, and treason. The author concedes the truth will be hard to take for those who once supported and defended them, but holds that it will also set them free.[1]

Its recurring moral frame is that "the love of money can truly be the root of all kinds of evil" — invoked to explain why [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] would falsify evidence to shield his own brother's killer.[1] The method's payoff in this article is a chain running from a Mexican murder to a Houston bank account to [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Offshore]] and the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion|Bay of Pigs]].[1]
