# Crimean Bridge Attack (July 2023)

The Crimean Bridge, also known as the Kerch Strait Bridge, connects the Crimean peninsula to Russia's Krasnodar region and serves as an important supply artery for Russian operations in Ukraine.[1] In July 2023 the bridge was struck in an attack that suspended traffic and, according to Russian accounts, killed two civilians.[1]

## Attribution

A Ukrainian official, speaking on condition of anonymity because of the sensitivity of military operations, told The Washington Post on Monday, July 17, 2023 that Ukraine's military and security services were responsible for the attack, and that Ukraine had used sea surface drones to strike the bridge.[1] Russia denounced the incident as a "terrorist attack."[1]

## Link to the grain corridor

The attack coincided with Russia's termination of the [[black-sea-grain-initiative|Black Sea Grain Initiative]], prompting speculation that the strike was the final trigger for Moscow's withdrawal.[1] The Kremlin downplayed the connection, with spokesman Dmitry Peskov noting that Russia's intention to exit the grain arrangement had been communicated before the bridge was hit.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the bridge attack and other recent explosions as evidence that Ukraine and its leadership were escalating both rhetoric and operations, including against Russian civilians, even as the [[2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive|counteroffensive]] stalled.[1] He cautioned that in a conflict waged as heavily in "Fifth-Gen PsyWar style" over the internet as on the ground, it is "virtually impossible to suss out where the truth lies."[1]

## Hersh's allegation of U.S. involvement

In late July 2023 investigative journalist [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]] published a Substack report alleging that U.S. intelligence had helped Ukraine destroy the Kerch (Crimean) Bridge earlier that month, timing that corresponded with President [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]'s refusal to renew the [[black-sea-grain-initiative|Black Sea Grain Initiative]].[2] Hersh's sources also described U.S. assistance in the larger initial Kerch Bridge explosion of October 2022 that temporarily disabled the crossing, and he wrote that "the Biden administration's role in both attacks was vital."[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the bridge strikes as part of a chain of suspected false-flag operations extending from the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream sabotage]] to the destruction of the [[kakhovka-dam-destruction|Kakhovka Dam]], noting that while airtight reporting was not available for every event, "the usual suspects seem to be involved every time," and identifying the [[cia|CIA]] as the clandestine kinetic arm behind them.[2]
