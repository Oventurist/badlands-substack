# Q Post 2807

Q drop #2807 is the [[qanon|Q]] post that Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats as the thesis statement of his "Illusions of Democracy" article, and the origin of the phrase itself.[1]

## The taxpayer theft section

The first portion quoted in the article reads: "**[TOPIC: TAXPAYER THEFT]** INSIDER vs OUTSIDER RETAIN CONTROL vs. LOSE CONTROL Kennedy was an outsider **[assassinated]** Reagan was an outsider **[assassination attempt]** POTUS is an outsider **[CLAS HIGH]**."[1] Lange reads this as establishing that American politics has always been a contest between insiders and outsiders, with the insiders stealing taxpayer money and protected by a [[two-tier-justice-system|two-tier justice system]] set up by a [[shadow-government|shadow government]] operating for many generations.[1] [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], on this reading, is an outsider working to transform the system of government and restore the constitution — and the fate of [[john-f-kennedy|Kennedy]] and Reagan is offered as evidence of what happens to outsiders.[1] The theme of [[taxpayer-theft|taxpayer theft]] runs through the whole drop.[1]

## The funds questions

The second portion quoted asks a chain of questions about money: "Who controls the distribution of funds? Who accounts for the funds? Who has access to the funds? Who 'really' has access to the funds? Do funds return in the form of political donations? Do funds return in the form of massive donations to 'FOUNDATIONS' and/or 'INSTITUTES'? Do funds return in the form of offshore bank accounts for future payments/payoffs?"[1] It continues: "Replicate: Environment ('Green'), AID assist, etc. NO HONEST CONTROL IN PLACE. NO CHECKS & BALANCES IN PLACE. NO SAFEGUARDS IN PLACE. Threat of an OUTSIDER becoming PRESIDENT? LOSS OF CONTROL?"[1]

Lange singles out two of these — who accounts for the funds, and who has access to the funds — as the questions that motivate his call for a new, open and honest banking system in which the people's personal savings are protected.[1]

## "Illusion of democracy"

The closing section quoted reads: "'Trillions of dollars at stake.' 'Controlled by group of special interests.' 'Illusion of democracy.' **[Welcome to the Real World]** WE CANNOT AFFORD TO LOSE THIS RARE OPPORTUNITY TO RIGHT THE WRONGS. WE, THE PEOPLE, MUST RECLAIM WHAT IS RIGHTFULLY OURS. WE MUST RISE. WE MUST FIGHT. GOD BLESS THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA. Q"[1]

From this Lange derives both his title and his subtitle: the "real world" today is an [[illusion-of-democracy|illusion of democracy]], and the task is "reclaiming what is rightfully ours."[1] He contrasts that illusion with the founding design — the United States as a constitutional republic in which the people hold all the power and are supposed to be in control.[1] The drop's repeated insistence that there are no checks, balances or **safeguards** in place is the hinge on which the rest of his argument turns, leading him to the [[declaration-of-independence|Declaration of Independence]]'s call to "provide new Guards for their future security" and to the Costa Rican safeguards of [[jose-figueres-ferrer|José "Pepe" Figueres Ferrer]].[1]
