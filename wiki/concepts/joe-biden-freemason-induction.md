# Joe Biden Freemason Induction

The Joe Biden Freemason induction refers to the conferral of Master Mason membership on former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] on January 19, 2025 — one day before he left office — by the Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Free and Accepted Masons of the State of South Carolina.[1] Because Biden is Catholic, the step was reported as an offense potentially punishable by excommunication under the canon law of the Catholic Church.[1]

## The conferral

According to an announcement by the Conference of Grand Masters, Biden was conferred with "Master Mason membership" and "full honors."[1] The resolution read: "Herefore, be it resolved, that I, 27th Most Worshipful Grand Master, Victor C. Major, on behalf of the members of the Most Worshipful Prince Hall Grand Lodge of Free and Accepted Masons of the State of South Carolina, hereby confer membership upon President Joseph R. Biden, Jr. in recognition of his outstanding service to the United States of America."[1] Joining the Freemasons as a Catholic forbids an individual from receiving communion and carries the potential for excommunication.[1]

## Historical background

Coverage cited by Badlands noted that although the origins of [[freemasonry|Freemasonry]] stretch back to the fifteenth century, the modern form developed in Britain in the early 1700s and grew into a Europe- and US-wide fraternity for philosophers, religious dissidents, occultists, and the business and political elite.[1] Prince Hall Masonry is a historically black branch of Freemasonry founded in 1775 by a freed slave in Boston.[1] Pope Clement XII forbade Catholics from practicing Freemasonry in 1738, and that papal ban remained in force until 1983, when the Vatican issued a new order banning membership in organizations that "plot against the Church"; although the new order did not name Freemasonry explicitly, it retained excommunication as a potential penalty.[1] Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger, later Pope, declared at the time that "the faithful who enroll in Masonic associations are in a state of grave sin and may not receive Holy Communion."[1]
