# Liberation Theology

Liberation Theology is a doctrine described by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as an innovation of networks concentrated in Ibero-America that emerged out of the same currents that shaped the [[second-vatican-council|Second Vatican Council]].[1]

Its logic held that Marxism was the purest expression of Christ's message and that all true Christians were obliged to take up "La Revolutione" against capitalism around the world during the dark days of the Cold War.[1] Ehret traces the impulse back to [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]], who, when asked what should be done about the stagnant Catholic Church, called for a revolutionary Marxist merger by saying that "a good dip into Marxism might start things moving again."[1]

Ehret writes that while Popes John Paul I and John Paul II attempted to push back against this deconstruction of Christianity, "a touch of poison and a couple of assassin's bullets" brought the Holy See back into line, setting the ground for a full Jesuit takeover of the Church and the integration of Christianity into a new, [[eugenics|eugenics]]-driven religion.[1]
