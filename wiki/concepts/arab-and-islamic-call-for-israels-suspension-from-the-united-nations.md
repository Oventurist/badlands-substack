# Arab and Islamic Call for Israel's Suspension from the United Nations

In September 2025, Arab and Islamic leaders called for [[israel|Israel]] to be suspended from the [[united-nations|United Nations]] over alleged violations of the organization's charter.[1] The demand came amid Israel's military campaign in [[gaza|Gaza]] and in the immediate aftermath of an Israeli airstrike on Doha that killed six people, including a Qatari security officer.[1]

## The Doha summit

The leaders of the League of Arab States — the [[arab-league|Arab League]] — and the [[organization-of-islamic-cooperation|Organization of Islamic Cooperation]] convened an emergency summit in Doha.[1] In their final statement, the group called on member nations to "consider the compatibility of Israel's membership in the UN with its Charter" and to coordinate efforts to suspend Israel from the organization.[1]

Israel maintained that its strike on [[qatar|Qatar]] had targeted [[hamas|Hamas]] officials, but Arab and Islamic leaders branded the attack a "dangerous escalation that exposes the extremist hostility of the Israeli government."[1] They further accused Israel of undermining international mediation and peacemaking, noting that the Qatari capital had served as a key venue for peace talks between West Jerusalem and Hamas.[1] The statement urged all states to review their diplomatic and economic relations with Israel and to take "legal and effective measures" to halt Israeli actions, including sanctions and the suspension of arms and dual-use exports.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that rather than drawing swords and declaring jihad as some American cable coverage would have predicted, the assembled leaders were pursuing a more strategic path: leveraging newly formed bilateral relationships to isolate Israel geopolitically and to strip it of UN membership.[1] He noted the irony that it was the UN itself that created Israel in 1947 through the [[un-partition-plan-for-palestine|Partition Plan of Palestine]].[1] He further predicted that should Israel actually be suspended, the Republican-led Congress would move to withdraw the United States from the organization, an outcome he expected would render the UN financially insolvent.[1]
