# 2023 Cannon Building Fire Alarm Incident

The 2023 Cannon Building fire alarm incident refers to Representative [[jamaal-bowman|Jamaal Bowman]]'s pulling of a fire alarm in the Cannon House Office Building on September 30, 2023, while House Republicans were moving a stopgap spending bill intended to avert a government shutdown.[1] Critics characterized the act as an attempt to delay the vote, and the episode became a focal point in arguments about selective prosecution of obstruction offenses.[1]

## The incident

Bowman was caught on video pulling the alarm as the House worked to keep the government open.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized the charge plainly: he "interfered with and obstructed an official proceeding — and he was caught on video doing it."[1]

Bowman's explanation, relayed by Axios Capitol Hill reporter Juliegrace Brufke, was that he "thought the alarm would open the door," and he said Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] (D-NY) "understood that it was a mistake."[1] Bowman confirmed the account in a written statement issued the following Saturday night.[1]

## Reaction

The event went viral almost immediately. Representative George Santos (R-NY) filmed a mock public service announcement about fire alarms, and users on X recirculated Bowman's own January 2021 posts lamenting the seriousness of obstructing an official proceeding.[1]

Representative [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]] (R-GA) called on the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] to prosecute Bowman under the same obstruction statute used against January 6 defendants, writing that he "interrupted the official proceedings of the House as Republicans worked to keep the government open."[1] Greene added that Democrats "will do anything to shut OUR government down because billions aren't going to their favorite country: Ukraine."[1] Representative Nicole Malliotakis (R-NY) said she would introduce legislation to expel Bowman.[1]

On Sunday, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] called Bowman's actions egregious, and by that evening Bowman was reported to be under investigation.[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

Ashe in America placed the incident directly after the [[biden-impeachment-inquiry|Biden impeachment inquiry]] hearing as a second demonstration of Democratic hypocrisy in a single week, concluding that "the hypocrisy chaser is a strong one for democrats."[1]
