# DOJ Subpoenas of Pediatric Gender Clinics

In July 2025 the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced that it had issued more than twenty subpoenas to doctors and clinics suspected of performing transgender medical procedures on minors, as part of a broader investigation into what the department described as deceptive and potentially criminal practices in the medical industry.[1]

## Scope and legal theories

The department said it was investigating possible violations including healthcare fraud and false statements, but its brief press release did not name the clinics or physicians targeted.[1] Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] said in a statement confirming the subpoenas that "medical professionals and organizations that mutilated children in the service of a warped ideology will be held accountable by this Department of Justice."[1]

## Parallel executive and legislative action

The announcement came the same day the [[federal-trade-commission|Federal Trade Commission]] held a Washington, D.C. workshop titled "Dangers of 'Gender-Affirming Care' For Minors."[1] DOJ chief of staff [[chad-mizelle|Chad Mizelle]] confirmed that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration was preparing draft legislation and "working with Congress" on existing criminal laws relating to female genital mutilation, with the aim of more robustly protecting children from what he called chemical and surgical mutilation.[1]
