# Executive Order 13848

Executive Order 13848 is a United States executive order declaring a national emergency with respect to foreign interference in, or the undermining of public confidence in, United States elections.[1]

## Annual extension

In September 2025, the national emergency declared by the order was extended for another year under the formal title "Continuation of the National Emergency With Respect to Foreign Interference in or Undermining Public Confidence in United States Elections."[1] Badlands Media host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] highlighted the extension alongside coverage of the dismissal of the [[michigan-fake-electors-case|Michigan fake electors case]], treating the order's continued life as significant to the [[devolution|devolution]] thesis that election-integrity authorities remain in force.[1]
