# Section 117 of the Higher Education Act

Section 117 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 requires U.S. colleges and universities to report foreign gifts and contracts totaling $250,000 or more in a calendar year.[1]

In February 2026 the [[state-department|State Department]] announced an interagency agreement with the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]] to strengthen enforcement of these disclosure requirements, citing President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s [[executive-order-14282|Executive Order 14282]] on transparency regarding foreign influence in higher education.[1] Under the agreement, the State Department's Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs supports administration of Section 117, with officials saying the partnership is designed to improve the accuracy and transparency of reporting while allowing national security experts to more effectively assess potential foreign influence or threats.[1]

The arrangement expands the State Department's role in managing and reviewing data from the Section 117 reporting portal. Leveraging its expertise in national security and foreign-national academic admissions, State helps review compliance, shares information with federal stakeholders, and works to ensure the data can be used proactively to safeguard U.S. research and students.[1]
