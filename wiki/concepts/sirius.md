# Sirius

**Sirius** is a star which [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] raises at the close of the [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] essay "On Languages and Cover-ups" as a possible thread for future research.[1]

## Role in the series

The essay observes that a few stars appear prominently in mythologies from all over the world; the [[pleiades|Pleiades]] are named as a major one, and Sirius as another that is often discussed.[1] The author then poses the question of why Sirius is called the **Dog Star**, and asks whether any connection is to be found between it and werewolves.[1] He does not answer, writing instead that this is something for Q and [[donald-trump|Trump]] to know and for readers to find out.[1]

The reference sits within the author's broader argument that the series' context must now broaden beyond ancient history and beyond the solar system, using etymology, symbolism and "mythology" — which he suggests may simply be history — as the available tools.[1] He concedes that a great deal cannot be stated definitively, while maintaining that what little can be said is nonetheless jaw-dropping.[1]
