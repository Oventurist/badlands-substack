# Truth Search AI

Truth Search AI is an artificial intelligence search feature built into the [[truth-social|Truth Social]] platform by [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media and Technology Group]].[1] The company announced in August 2025 that it had begun public beta testing of the feature.[1]

## Technology and partnership

Truth Search AI is powered by [[perplexity-ai|Perplexity]], a software and AI company focused on delivering direct, contextually accurate answers with transparent citations.[1] Trump Media said the tool was intended to enhance Truth Social and exponentially increase the amount of information available to its users.[1] Chief executive and chairman [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] said the company was proud to partner with Perplexity and that the launch would make Truth Social "an even more vital element in the Patriot Economy."[1]

Trump Media, which trades on Nasdaq and NYSE Texas under the ticker DJT, also operates the streaming platform Truth+ and the FinTech brand Truth.Fi.[1]

## Commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] linked the partnership to earlier reporting that Perplexity might merge with TikTok, restating his speculation that TikTok would ultimately land under Trump Media, with [[jeffrey-yass|Jeff Yass]] positioned as a financial common denominator in a structure resembling the Twitter sale.[1]
