# MAGA Minute

**MAGA Minute** is a short-form video communications product launched by the [[white-house|White House]] in February 2025 and promoted through the administration's official account on X.[1] The format packages a rapid summary of the administration's claimed accomplishments into a brief, social-media-native segment rather than a traditional briefing or press release.[1]

## Format and delivery

Based on remarks by the White House Press Secretary at the time of the launch, the MAGA Minute was expected to be delivered on a weekly cadence, with the Press Secretary summarizing administration accomplishments as they accumulated.[1] Commentators described the product as "White House comms in a TikTok form," an explicit adaptation of executive-branch messaging to the vertical-video conventions that dominate contemporary social platforms.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the launch as "a smart play," arguing that social media has rapidly reshaped how the public consumes information and that the [[white-house|White House]] was "grabbing mindshare by meeting people where they are."[1] She acknowledged the countervailing concern — that short-form content reinforces low attention spans and that long-form material better promotes critical thinking — but concluded that "the realities of the American attention span are what they are."[1] She added that the weekly format was "a gift, really, because the accomplishments are coming so fast it's hard to keep up."[1]
