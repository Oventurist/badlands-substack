# 2023 OpenAI Leadership Crisis

The 2023 OpenAI leadership crisis was a five-day governance upheaval at [[openai|OpenAI]] that ended early on Wednesday, 22 November 2023, when the company announced that [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] would return as chief executive.[1]

## Resolution

OpenAI posted on X at around 0100 ET: "We have reached an agreement in principle for Sam Altman to return to OpenAI as CEO," alongside a board shakeup naming "a new initial board of Bret Taylor (Chair), [[larry-summers|Larry Summers]], and Adam D'Angelo."[1]

Altman wrote that everything he had done over the preceding days had been "in service of keeping this team and its mission together," that joining [[microsoft|Microsoft]] on the Sunday evening had appeared to be the best path at the time, and that with the new board and Satya Nadella's support he looked forward to returning and building on the partnership with Microsoft.[1]

## Employee pressure and Microsoft's role

The decision to reinstate Altman and reshuffle the board was expected to appease the more than 700 employees who had signed a letter threatening to quit and join Microsoft — OpenAI's largest shareholder — unless all directors resigned and Altman was restored.[1] Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said he was "encouraged by the changes to the OpenAI board," calling it "a first essential step on a path to more stable, well-informed, and effective governance."[1]
