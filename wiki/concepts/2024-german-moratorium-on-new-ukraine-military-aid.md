# 2024 German Moratorium on New Ukraine Military Aid

In August 2024, the government of [[germany|Germany]] moved to halt new military aid to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] as part of its ruling coalition's spending-reduction plan, a decision reported by the Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung and covered by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] under the headline "The Beginning Of The End?"[1]

## The reported decision

According to a Saturday report in the Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung, the German government would stop new military assistance to Ukraine as part of the coalition's effort to reduce spending.[1] The report, which cited non-public documents and emails as well as discussions with unnamed sources, noted that the moratorium was already in effect and would affect new requests for funding rather than previously approved aid.[1]

## Relationship to the Nord Stream narrative shift

ZeroHedge tied the announcement to a Wall Street Journal report published three days earlier that sought to shift the narrative over the Nord Stream sabotage.[1] Rather than the [[cia|CIA]] being blamed for the destruction of the critical gas pipeline running from Russia to Europe, unnamed intelligence sources advanced an account in which a top Ukrainian general — operating initially on instructions from [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] but then going rogue after Zelensky got cold feet — coordinated the sabotage using a handful of amateurs who reached the bottom of the Baltic Sea and carried out an unprecedented military operation.[1] ZeroHedge had predicted at the time that relations between Germany and Ukraine were "about to turn ugly" and asked why the story was surfacing at that moment.[1] Its answer was that the narrative U-turn — which on its face implied [[nato|NATO]] should turn its military power against Ukraine, the party that had attacked German assets — was meant to soften the blow of Germany's decision to cut off what it termed Zelensky's unprecedented grift.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the development through what he called "Overton's Goalpost," a five-stage progression describing the European Union's shifting stance on Ukraine.[1] The stages ran from DENY (Russia is invading a sovereign nation in an unprovoked act of war challenging NATO peacekeeping and threatening the global west); to DIMINISH (Russia's claims of "denazifying" the Donbas while responding to NATO expansion are propaganda consistent with its 2016 efforts supporting [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]); to QUALIFY (while technically true that NATO violated long-standing treaties and that a genocide of ethnic Russians followed the 2014 Maidan coup, this is cover for [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]'s true aims); to DISTRACT (Russia is deepening ties with China and North Korea and moving nuclear warheads as a new Axis forms on the eve of World War III); and finally to ADMIT (Ukraine is a globalist criminal state being systematically purged of regime control, whose association can no longer be sustained).[1]
