Devolution, in the context of [[badlands-media]], refers to a body of theories concerning "Devolution and Continuity of Government" that the network uses as an analytical lens for current events.[1] The concept is central to the network's flagship program, the Devolution Power Hour, "the show that started it all on the Badlands Media Network," hosted by founder [[jon-herold]] with co-host [[burning-bright]] on Wednesday nights.[1] The Devolution Power Hour applies Devolution and Continuity of Government frameworks to analyze recent political and social developments.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

## Afghanistan aid flows cited as proof (November 2023)

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the November 2023 [[special-inspector-general-for-afghanistan-reconstruction|SIGAR]] finding that some $11 billion in U.S. aid had flowed into Afghanistan since August 2021 as evidence for Devolution, arguing that continued American funding reaching the [[taliban|Taliban]]'s orbit implied a continuing arrangement traceable to the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] rather than the improvised collapse presented publicly.[3] He described the finding as "both a Devolution Proof and a Sovereign Alliance-Regions of Responsibility Proof rolled into one."[3]

## Netanyahu and the January 2021 rationale

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued in August 2024 that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was one of the main reasons implementing Devolution was necessary for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in January 2021.[4] He pointed to the January 15, 2021 reversal of sanctions on Israeli oligarch [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]], obtained by ambassador [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]] through contacts inside the [[state-department|State Department]], as evidence that the Deep State and Israel retained considerable control over the federal bureaucracy — enough that a civil war could easily have broken out.[4] He also cited Netanyahu's phone call congratulating [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], the first from any foreign leader, which triggered a cascade of media and international support and suggested that a domestic conflict would have invited foreign intervention on US soil.[4] On this account Trump executed a strategic retreat, allowing his enemies to occupy a position he had already booby-trapped.[4]

## Afghanistan funding cited as a devolution proof

In August 2023 [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described continued US funding of [[taliban|Taliban]]-ruled [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] — $2.35 billion over two years according to [[special-inspector-general-for-afghanistan-reconstruction|SIGAR]] — as "a good one" among devolution proofs.[5] The argument holds that the Kabul withdrawal consummated [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]], positioning the Taliban as rulers of Kabul while funding and arming them as a bulwark against ISIS, and that this plan proceeded to completion even with Trump "supposedly out of office" — the signature pattern devolution theorists look for.[5]

## Inauguration Theme Echo (January 2025)

Badlands writer Jon Herold observed in January 2025 that the announced theme of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second inauguration invoked "the Founder's commitment to future generations of Americans to preserve the continuity and stability of our democratic system of government" — wording he described as very similar to the Federal Mission Resilience strategy released in conjunction with Executive Order 13961, the order at the center of the devolution thesis and sometimes called the "Devo EO."[6] He read the echo as a signal that the "Devo term is almost coming to an end."[6]

## The Gertler sanctions episode as a proof

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the handling of sanctions against Israeli oligarch [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] as a Devolution proof.[7] In that account, [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]] reversed the [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]] sanctions inside the [[state-department|State Department]] on January 15, 2021, only for Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]] to reissue them in March 2021 and the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] to strip Gertler of his African mining rights in 2022 — rights subsequently acquired by members of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[7] The sequence, he argued, functions simultaneously as a Devolution proof and a Sovereign Alliance proof.[7]

## Applied to Biden energy policy (2023)

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the September 2023 [[arctic-national-wildlife-refuge|ANWR]] lease cancellations to illustrate the interpretive difference Devolution makes.[8] He noted that [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], while a capable aggregator, carries a doomer's tinge and assumes that an openly corrupt Biden administration must therefore be Deep State controlled.[8] Under the Devolution and Continuity of Government framing, the same events read instead as [[joe-biden|Biden]] being used to execute a controlled demolition of the U.S. Deep State in the public mind — with the caveat that it remains necessary to watch whether these narratives collapse into the Actual.[8]

## Putin's March 2024 remarks as claimed evidence

Badlands writer Burning Bright argued that Vladimir Putin's March 13, 2024 comments on Russian readiness for nuclear war may have constituted his most Devolution-related public communication to date, coming two days before Russia's presidential election.[9] Burning Bright highlighted a translation in which Putin said, "Apart from (US President Joe) Biden, there are enough other experts in the sphere of Russian-American relations and strategic restraint. So I don't think that everything is going to go head-on here, but we are ready for it."[9]

He tied the remark to a long-standing argument that one key purpose of the Devolution operation researched by Jon Herold has been its implications for the globalist regime, contending that with Donald Trump out of public power the international game board has shifted away from collectivism and toward sovereignty and the multipolar concept advanced by Putin, Xi Jinping, Mohammed bin Salman and Trump.[9] He summarized the position with the formulations "The Scare Event is a Story" and "The Sovereign Alliance is real."[9]

## Referenced in June 2024 cyber coverage

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted in June 2024 that [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] had recently answered a Continuity of Government/Devolution question at an open panel by turning it toward the Biden administration and asking what it might attempt along that track to interfere with the 2024 election, which he read as a hint that the regime was beta testing a refusal to cede power under the guise of foreign interference.[10]

## Application to the Gaza veto (February 2024)

Analysing the February 20, 2024 US veto of a [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] ceasefire resolution, Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] suggested breaking the event into an Actual-Narrative structure and considering it in light of Devolution theory, asking which globalist-aligned proxies were so threatened by the kinetic events in Gaza that the entire EU bloc would call for a cessation of violence there while demanding escalation in Ukraine.[11]
