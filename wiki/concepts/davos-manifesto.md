# Davos Manifesto

The Davos Manifesto was crafted at the 1973 event of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], outlining [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s notions of [[stakeholder-capitalism|stakeholder capitalism]].[1]

Badlands Media reporting emphasizes that the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]]'s 1968 neo-Malthusian computer models — used to justify supposedly fixed [[limits-to-growth|limits to humanity's growth]] — were incorporated into the World Economic Forum during that same 1973 event.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] therefore treats the Davos Manifesto as the point at which the closed-system economics descended from [[john-stuart-mill|J. S. Mill]] and [[david-ricardo|David Ricardo]] was institutionalized within the body that would later host the [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] doctrines of [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]] and the [[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]] agenda.[1]
