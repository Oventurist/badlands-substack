# Las Vegas Tourism Decline

The Las Vegas tourism decline of 2025 was a sustained year-over-year contraction in visitor traffic to the Nevada resort city, evidenced most clearly in passenger counts at Harry Reid International Airport (LAS).[1]

## Air traffic data

According to airport data for November 2025, traffic was down 9.6 percent year over year, falling to 4.3 million passengers — a worse month than October, which had recorded an 8.2 percent decline.[1] For the two preceding years, November had been one of the busiest months of the year in Las Vegas owing to the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix held the week before Thanksgiving.[1] In 2025, however, the Grand Prix fell in the same month as the longest government shutdown in United States history, during which air traffic was cut back at dozens of airports, including LAS, as air traffic controllers went unpaid for weeks.[1]

## Canadian boycott

The steepest declines came from Canadian airlines.[1] In 2024, 28 percent of foreign tourists to the United States were Canadian; in 2025 that figure fell by 23 percent, amounting to a roughly $4 billion hit to the US economy.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariffs on Canadian products triggered a countrywide boycott of travel to the United States, an effect felt particularly hard in Las Vegas, where Air Canada traffic dropped 40 percent from the prior year.[1]
