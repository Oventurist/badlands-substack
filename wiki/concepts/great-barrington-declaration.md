# Great Barrington Declaration

The Great Barrington Declaration is an October 2020 open letter opposing COVID-19 lockdown policies, authored by [[jay-bhattacharya|Jay Bhattacharya]] of Stanford University, Martin Kulldorff of Harvard University, and Sunetra Gupta of Oxford University.[1]

The document called for an end to the COVID-19 lockdowns that had been in effect for most of 2020 and garnered hundreds of thousands of signatures.[1] Its authors wrote: "As infectious disease epidemiologists and public health scientists we have grave concerns about the damaging physical and mental health impacts of the prevailing COVID-19 policies."[1]

The declaration became a defining credential for its signatories within the dissident public-health community, and Bhattacharya's authorship was cited in November 2024 reporting on his candidacy to lead the [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]] in the incoming [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]
