# 2023 House Resolution Condemning College Presidents' Response to Antisemitism

In December 2023 a bipartisan group of four high-profile House lawmakers introduced a resolution condemning antisemitism on university campuses and the viral congressional testimony of three university presidents delivered the week before.[1]

## Background

Exactly one week before the resolution's introduction, the presidents of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]], the [[massachusetts-institute-of-technology|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]] and the [[university-of-pennsylvania|University of Pennsylvania]] came under sustained criticism after appearing at a House hearing, where they dodged questions from Representative [[elise-stefanik|Elise Stefanik]] about whether students calling for the genocide of Jews violated their schools' codes of conduct and should be punished.[1]

## The resolution

The measure, first obtained by [[nbc-news|NBC News]], was authored by House GOP Conference Chair Stefanik of New York.[1] Its three other lead sponsors were Majority Leader [[steve-scalise|Steve Scalise]] of Louisiana and two Jewish American Democrats: Problem Solvers Caucus co-chair [[josh-gottheimer|Josh Gottheimer]] of New Jersey and Representative [[jared-moskowitz|Jared Moskowitz]] of Florida.[1] Stefanik's office said the resolution was expected to receive a floor vote that same week.[1]
