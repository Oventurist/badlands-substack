# 2025 Tarasin Landslide

The 2025 Tarasin landslide was a natural disaster in Tarasin village, in the Marrah Mountains of Central Darfur, western [[sudan|Sudan]], which reportedly killed an estimated 1,000 people.[1] It is described as one of the country's deadliest recent natural disasters.[1]

## Event

The landslide occurred on Sunday, August 31, 2025, following days of heavy rainfall in late August.[1] It was reported by the [[sudan-liberation-movement-army|Sudan Liberation Movement-Army]], the rebel group that controls the area.[1] The group stated that initial information indicated the death of all village residents, with only one survivor, and that the village had been "completely levelled."[1]

## Humanitarian response

The Sudan Liberation Movement-Army appealed to the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and international aid groups for assistance in recovering bodies.[1] Relief efforts were complicated by Sudan's ongoing civil war, which has rendered much of the Darfur region — including the Marrah Mountains — largely inaccessible to the UN and aid organisations.[1] The Badlands News Brief carried the story as a bonus item in its September 2, 2025 edition.[1]
