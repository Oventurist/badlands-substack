# Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Agreement (Russia–North Korea)

The Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Agreement is a bilateral treaty between Russia and [[north-korea|North Korea]] that came into force in December 2024 and provided the legal basis for the deployment of North Korean troops on Russian soil during the Kursk campaign.[1]

## Provisions

The treaty provides for mutual military assistance in the event of an attack on either party, pledging immediate support "by all means available" under Article 51 of the UN Charter, the provision recognizing the inherent right of individual or collective self-defense.[1]

## Application in Kursk

North Korean units were deployed to Russia under the agreement during the [[kursk-incursion|Kursk]] campaign.[1] Leader [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong-un]] personally ordered his military to fight alongside Russian forces "to annihilate Ukrainian neo-Nazi occupiers and liberate the Kursk region," according to state media, and Pyongyang officially confirmed the deployment in April 2025 after Chief of the General Staff [[valery-gerasimov|Valery Gerasimov]] praised the contribution in a report to President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1]

Badlands commentary noted that because the North Korean forces appeared to be deployed only within Russian territory in Kursk, the arrangement could be read as collective defense of an ally's sovereignty rather than participation in an invasion of Ukraine.[1] The assistance allowed Russia to keep its own forces concentrated on the main eastern front, blunting the strategic purpose of the Ukrainian Kursk offensive.[1]
