# 2026 National Defense Strategy

The 2026 National Defense Strategy is the unclassified defense-planning document released by the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] that places defense of the U.S. homeland at the top of the American military priority list while pressing allies for greater burden-sharing as Washington reassesses its global military footprint.[1]

## Priorities and force posture

The document ranks homeland defense above the Indo-Pacific and signals potential reductions to U.S. force posture in Europe and South Korea.[1] It emphasizes allied contributions and expanded investment in the U.S. defense industrial base.[1] The Pentagon's stated priorities are defending the U.S. homeland, deterring [[china|China]] in the Indo-Pacific "through strength, not confrontation," increasing burden-sharing with allies, and "supercharging" the U.S. defense industrial base.[1]

## Relationship to the National Security Strategy

The strategy follows the [[white-house|White House]]'s National Security Strategy released in December 2025 and reflects notable political shifts from the 2022 edition, referencing [[donald-trump|President Trump]] far more frequently despite being a shorter document.[1] It highlights Trump's [[golden-dome|Golden Dome]] missile defense initiative, a renewed emphasis on counter-drone systems, a modernized nuclear deterrent, and an updated interpretation of the [[monroe-doctrine|Monroe Doctrine]].[1]
