# Watts Riots (1965)

The Watts riots were race riots that occurred in 1965 in the Watts neighborhood of Los Angeles.[1]

## Role in the alleged thirty-year cycle

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites Watts as one of four data points in an argued pattern of American race riots recurring roughly every thirty years, together with the [[harlem-riots-1935|Harlem riots of 1935]], the [[la-riots-1992|1992 Los Angeles riots]] that followed the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] verdict, and the 2020 unrest surrounding [[george-floyd|George Floyd]].[1] He argues the spacing reflects an understanding, whether correct or not, that racism could begin to disappear if America went more than thirty years without a racially divisive incident, and that riots are therefore engineered to fan the flames.[1] Carlson attributes the design of this cycle to the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]].[1]
