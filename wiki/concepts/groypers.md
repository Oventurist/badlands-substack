# Groypers

The Groypers are an online right-wing movement associated with commentator [[nick-fuentes|Nick Fuentes]], known for aggressive criticism of establishment conservatism and of U.S. support for [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Role in 2026 controversies

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s appointment of [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] as ambassador to Israel "triggered Nick Fuentes and the Groypers into the stratosphere," and argued the appointment was a deliberate signal.[1] He further described the "extra thirsty Groyper brigade" as a target audience for media attacks on [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], architect of the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]].[1]

The movement's prominence grew after [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] interviewed Fuentes, which prompted backlash and calls for Carlson's censorship.[1]

## Characterization as a controlled counter-movement

The May 2025 Badlands essay "On Infiltration and Israel" named the Groypers as an example of what it called a false counter-movement: a "veritable galaxy" of fake pro-Hitler and pro-White movements said to have been deployed by the dark cabal in response to growing global awareness of Israeli conduct.[2] The author argued that the most popular voices ostensibly speaking on behalf of White people are "obviously hellbent" on giving White people the worst possible optics, thereby validating the notion that anyone who notices demographic change is a Nazi.[2] He added that glaring holes in the orthodox holocaust narrative inadvertently bolster the credibility of such groups, and framed the whole dynamic as part of the [[5g-war|5G war]].[2]
