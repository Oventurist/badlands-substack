# You will own nothing and be happy

"You will own nothing and be happy" is a slogan that Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] attributes to the "psycho elites who run the global financial system," and which he treats as a statement of intent rather than a prediction.[1]

## Carlson's asset-transfer reading

Carlson connects the slogan directly to [[blackrock|BlackRock]]'s residential property purchases. Two years before his April 2023 essay, he wrote, BlackRock offered more for houses than owners even thought they were worth, prompting them to sell; homeowners thereby exchanged "real" assets for the cabal elite's dollars, which had since lost roughly 15% of their value and were continuing to fall.[1]

He argues this apparent violation of the "buy low, sell high" rule only makes sense if the buyer expects money to become worthless, in which case converting currency into real assets before that happens is rational.[1] It follows, in his view, that those making the purchases were either planning to crash the financial market or had foreknowledge that it would be crashed — a general heuristic he states as: when the corrupt, satanic cabal does something that makes no logical sense with their money, they are probably working a scam.[1]

Carlson links the strategy to the predictive capability of [[aladdin|Aladdin]] and to a broader thesis that the pace and shape of the coming financial reset — whether it feels like "driving over a speed bump or driving into a brick wall" — has itself been modelled in advance.[1] See also [[great-reset|the Great Reset]].[1]
