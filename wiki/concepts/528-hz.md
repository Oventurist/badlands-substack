# 528 Hz

**528 Hz** is a sound frequency often called the "Love Frequency," celebrated in alternative-healing literature for a claimed ability to repair DNA, promote emotional healing, and harmonize the body's natural rhythms.[1] In the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series it is described as resonating deeply within cellular structure to promote coherence and balance — likened to tuning an instrument to perfect pitch and aligning every fiber of the body with the vibrational symphony of the universe.[1]

## Role in the frequency argument

The series treats 528 Hz, together with 432 Hz, as a harmonious counterweight to the standardized [[440hz-tuning-standard|440 Hz concert pitch]], which it characterizes as fostering agitation and emotional imbalance.[1] Where discordant frequencies are said to fragment thought and energy, 528 Hz is presented as restoring inner harmony and resisting external manipulation.[1]

## Application to brainwave states

Exposure to harmonious frequencies such as 528 Hz is recommended alongside meditation and breathwork as a means of recalibrating the brain out of the reactive [[brainwave-manipulation|beta wave state]] and into alpha and theta ranges associated with calm, problem-solving, intuition, and flow.[1] Binaural beats and isochronic tones available through meditation applications are named as practical delivery methods.[1] The stated aim is to make individuals less susceptible to manipulation and more aligned with their purpose.[1]

The series frames this within its broader claim that frequency is "more than a metaphor; it is a mechanism," directly tuning what it calls the mind-body-soul complex.[1]
