# State of the Union

The State of the Union (SOTU) is the annual address delivered by the US president to a joint session of [[congress|Congress]]. The February 24, 2026 Badlands Brief covers the 2026 address by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], scheduled for 9 PM ET that evening, describing it as taking place amid ongoing political and policy challenges including tariff disputes and international tensions.

## The 2026 address

Multiple House and Senate Democrats planned to skip the speech, attending alternate events or protests instead. Those named include [[ed-markey|Ed Markey]], [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]], [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]], [[tina-smith|Tina Smith]], and [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]]; more than a dozen Democratic congresswomen planned to wear white in the chamber as a protest. The US women's Olympic ice hockey team, gold medalists in Milan, declined an invitation to attend, citing academic and professional commitments following the Games while expressing gratitude for the recognition. The men's team, also gold medalists, appeared in a viral video to accept, though Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] cautioned that "there's no way to have special guests on the floor because it's a literal session of Congress," adding that leadership would "work and do what we can to accommodate."

## "First SOTU of the Golden Age"

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] frames the 2026 speech as "the first SOTU of the Golden Age," noting that media outlets had insisted the previous year's joint address was technically not a State of the Union. She anticipated that Trump would honor the 2026 gold medal hockey team as he had honored the 1980 Team USA squad in December, the two victories separated by exactly 46 years. The occasion functions in Badlands coverage as a recurring civic ritual through which the movement measures the progress of the [[global-golden-age|Golden Age]] narrative.
