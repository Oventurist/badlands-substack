# State of the Union Address

The State of the Union (SOTU) address is the annual message delivered by the President of the United States to a joint session of [[congress|Congress]], reporting on national conditions and setting out a legislative agenda. In Badlands coverage the 2026 edition is framed as the "first State of the Union of the Golden Age," on the reasoning that corporate media had insisted the President's 2025 joint address technically was not a State of the Union.[1]

## The 2026 address

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was scheduled to deliver the 2026 State of the Union to a joint session of Congress at 9:00 PM ET on the evening of February 24, 2026.[1] The speech came amid ongoing political and policy challenges, including disputes over tariff policy and international tensions.[1]

### Democratic boycott and protest

Multiple House and Senate Democrats planned to skip the address, opting for alternate events or protests. Lawmakers reported as not attending included Ed Markey, Chris Van Hollen, Ruben Gallego, Tina Smith, and Jeff Merkley.[1] More than a dozen Democratic congresswomen planned to wear white during the address as a form of political protest.[1]

### Olympic hockey invitations

Fresh from a gold-medal victory in Milan, the US women's Olympic ice hockey team declined Trump's invitation to attend, citing scheduling and travel commitments following the Games. In a statement released Monday, the team said it was "sincerely grateful for the invitation" but that "due to the timing and previously scheduled academic and professional commitments following the Games, the athletes are unable to participate."[1]

Trump also invited the US men's Olympic hockey team after their own gold-medal win; a viral video appeared to show the team accepting, though no official confirmation was released.[1] Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] told reporters that Capitol leadership was working to accommodate a hockey team despite the size of the group and the chamber's space limits, noting, "There's no way to have special guests on the floor because it's a literal session of Congress. But we're going to work and do what we can to accommodate."[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that Trump had honored the 1980 Team USA men's hockey squad in December and was expected to honor their successors — gold medalists exactly 46 years later, in overtime — during the address.[1] She used the occasion to argue for broader youth access to hockey as a sport that instills honor, humility, patriotism, discipline and merit, noting that boys and girls play together at youth levels.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] planned full live coverage of the address, with [[canncon|CannCon]] and Ashe reviewing the high points on the following day's *Badlands Daily*.[1]

## February 2026 address

In the February 2026 address, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced a federal "[[war-on-fraud|war on fraud]]" to be led by Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], an initiative whose first action — the suspension of roughly $259 million in [[medicaid|Medicaid]] payments to [[minnesota|Minnesota]] — followed the next day.[2] In the same speech Trump discussed his strong partnership with Venezuelan President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]], remarks that [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected to the limited [[venezuela-amnesty-law|amnesty bill]] then advancing under Delcy's brother [[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]].[2]
