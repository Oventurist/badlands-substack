# Deepfake

A deepfake is a synthetic audio, image, or video artifact generated by artificial intelligence to depict a real person saying or doing something they did not. In the Badlands Media corpus the technique is presented as one of the concrete mechanisms driving [[reality-collapse|reality collapse]].

The July 2023 article "Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason" states the thresholds bluntly: "Three seconds of audio is all it takes for AI to simulate your voice and create a deep fake of you saying anything it wants. Just three seconds. A still photo or brief video clip is all that is required to deep fake your face saying those words."

The article's framing of this capability is consistent with its wider thesis that the danger of AI is not machine sentience but human misuse — "the machine is a tool in the hands of men." Deepfakery is grouped with AI-mediated summarization and fMRI-based dream mapping as capabilities that dissolve the evidentiary basis of shared reality, and is invoked alongside George Orwell's *1984* passage on the continuous falsification of the record.
