# Justice Department Indictment over Iranian Hack of the Trump Campaign

In late September 2024 the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] charged three men with carrying out [[iran|Iran]]'s alleged hack-and-leak attack against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s presidential campaign, describing a far-reaching effort to steal data from current and former government officials and to sow distrust in the presidential election.[1]

## The charges

The indictment charged Iranian nationals Masoud Jalili, Seyyed Ali Aghamiri, and Yasar Balaghi with having "prepared for and engaged in a wide-ranging hacking campaign" targeting current and former U.S. officials, political campaigns, members of the media, and others.[1] Reporting had previously indicated that the Justice Department was preparing to bring charges in the case, as part of a broader effort by U.S. officials to act quickly when foreign governments seek to tamper with American politics.[1]

## Garland's statement

Speaking at Justice Department headquarters on Friday, September 27, 2024, Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] warned the public that Iran was just one of several foreign countries attempting to wreak havoc in a presidential election year, calling out Iran, Russia, and China for repeatedly trying to interfere with U.S. politics and elections through hacking, disinformation, and surreptitious influence campaigns.[1] "These authoritarian regimes, which violate the human rights of their own citizens, do not get a say in our country's democratic process," Garland said. "The American people, and the American people alone, will decide the outcome of our country's elections."[1]
