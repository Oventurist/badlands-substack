# K Street

K Street is the shorthand for Washington's lobbying and government-affairs industry, which by late September 2025 was reported to be operating in fear of a retribution campaign by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## The Monaco episode

Trump's attempt to jawbone Microsoft into firing former Biden Justice Department official [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]] as a top D.C. operative put K Street on high alert about whom it hires.[1] Politico reported that much of the private sector was paralyzed by Trump's broader efforts to leverage the might of government to bend companies to his whims, and that this pressure was magnified in government-affairs shops across Washington, where corporations were desperate to curry Trump's favor and avoid his wrath.[1]

## Hiring dynamics

Jeff Forbes, a founding partner at the lobbying firm Forbes Tate Partners, described the new calculus: "Anything that comes across my desk now is like, 'What Republican can we hire? And is the Republican MAGA?'"[1] He added that the job market had become so precarious that even moderate Republicans struggled to land a major influence job in Washington, let alone Democrats.[1] While it is not unusual for corporate lobbyists to cycle out of prominent roles when their party loses power, one veteran Democratic consultant said that "to have a target on your back because of past political affiliation in what has traditionally been a very nonpartisan area is a totally new dynamic."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called the report "quite the admission," noting that lobbyists facing accountability for a career of selling out the nation was being described as a novel dynamic.[1] She wrote that K Street lobbyists being both toxic to the powerful and fearful of accountability for their America-last portfolios was precisely what she had voted for, closing with the Badlands refrain: "Accelerate."[1]
