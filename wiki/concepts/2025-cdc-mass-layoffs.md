# 2025 CDC Mass Layoffs

The 2025 CDC mass layoffs were a reduction in force at the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] announced late on Friday, October 10, 2025, as part of a wider staffing cut across the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]].[1] Dozens of CDC employees were notified of their termination, and according to a court filing HHS began laying off between 1,100 and 1,200 employees that day, with staff across multiple divisions receiving reduction-in-force notices.[1] An HHS spokesperson declined to specify which divisions were affected.[1]

## Offices affected

The layoffs shuttered the CDC's Washington office and affected staff in the Global Health Center and the National Center for Immunization and Respiratory Diseases, according to a current CDC official and a former administration official.[1] One official said that "entire offices are being eliminated, including people in senior leadership roles."[1] Among those dismissed were officers in the agency's Epidemic Intelligence Service — known as the "disease detectives" — fellows in the Laboratory Leadership Service, and leaders within the Public Health Infrastructure Center and the Office of Science.[1]
