# The Squad

"The squad" is the informal name for a bloc of progressive members of the United States Congress, including [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]], noted for positions at odds with Democratic leadership.[1]

## Role in the 2023 Israel debate

Badlands Media observed that while elected officials appeared outwardly to be in near-complete unison in their support of Israel following the October 2023 outbreak of the [[israel-hamas-war|Israel–Hamas war]], there were obvious outliers such as Tlaib and the squad.[1]

The publication nonetheless argued that the squad's apparently adversarial position ultimately fuels the same goal as the pro-Israel consensus: the generation of public support for another war.[1] This follows the article's core thesis that the deep political establishment does not care which side a citizen picks, only that they pick one, and that manufactured binaries produce mobilised constituencies on both flanks that serve the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]].[1]
