# No-Fly List

The **no-fly list** is a United States watchlist that physically prevents a listed person from traveling by airplane into or within the country.[1]

## Post-9/11 tightening

Chapter 8 of the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series notes that after the September 11, 2001 attacks many new protocols were imposed on air travel and safety, chief among them the tightening of the no-fly list.[1] The series raises the list in the course of unpacking Q Drop #67's questions about how politically connected figures move in and out of the country, contrasting the tightly controlled list with the far looser screening applied to private aviation.[1]

## Relevance to the drops

Because the [[transportation-security-administration|TSA]] administers such lists and holds the associated flight data, the series treats then-administrator [[david-pekoske|David Pekoske]] as potentially well placed to observe [[deep-state|Deep State]] movements.[1] The list's existence is also implicitly contrasted with the [[2001-saudi-evacuation-flights|2001 Saudi evacuation flights]], in which travelers left the United States despite a nationwide grounding.[1]
