# Disinformation Governance Board

The Disinformation Governance Board was a short-lived body established under the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] and headed by [[nina-jankowicz|Nina Jankowicz]].[1] The board never held a meeting, but it became a durable symbol in American debates over government involvement in the regulation of speech.[1]

## Purpose

The stated purpose of the board was to coordinate disinformation efforts at DHS across topics ranging from migration to addressing plots attributed to Russia and Iran.[1]

## Criticism

The board was heavily criticized by the GOP and regularly covered by [[fox-news|Fox News]].[1] The [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] dubbed it the "Ministry of Truth," invoking George Orwell's novel *1984*, and couched the board's definition of disinformation as "any speech we don't like."[1] Fox News mentioned Jankowicz and her role leading the board more than 300 times in its coverage, with hosts and commentators asserting that she planned to censor speech.[1]

## Legacy

The board's collapse did not end the controversy: Jankowicz subsequently filed a defamation suit against Fox News over its coverage, a case commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] framed as a potential historic precedent for [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] protections, since a government official was seeking redress for criticism of her official role.[1] The episode is frequently cited in the broader critique of the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship industrial complex]].[1]
