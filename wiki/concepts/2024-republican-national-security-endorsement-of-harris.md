# 2024 Republican National Security Endorsement of Kamala Harris

On September 18, 2024, more than 100 Republican former national security figures, along with former members of Congress, signed an open letter endorsing Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] for president and citing former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s "demonstrated chaotic and unethical behavior."[1]

## Content of the letter

The letter set out a litany of rationales for endorsing Harris and asserted that "she possesses the essential qualities to serve as President and Donald Trump does not."[1] It described Trump's "susceptibility to flattery and manipulation by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and Xi Jinping, unusual affinity for other authoritarian leaders, contempt for the norms of decent, ethical and lawful behavior, and chaotic national security decision-making" as dangerous qualities, concluding that "he is unfit to serve again as President, or indeed in any office of public trust."[1] The signatories endorsed Harris on the grounds that she had "consistently championed the rule of law, democracy, and our constitutional principles," had agreed to sign a bipartisan border security bill, and had expressed support for U.S. allies while condemning adversaries.[1]

## Signatories

Signatories included former Senator [[william-cohen|William Cohen]] of Maine, who served as Defense secretary under President Clinton; former [[cia|CIA]] Director [[michael-hayden|Michael Hayden]]; former Director of National Intelligence [[john-negroponte|John Negroponte]]; former FBI and CIA Director [[william-webster|William Webster]]; and former U.S. Trade Representative [[robert-zoellick|Robert Zoellick]].[1] Former Republican lawmakers who signed included former Representatives Charles Boustany of Louisiana, [[barbara-comstock|Barbara Comstock]] of Virginia, Mickey Edwards of Oklahoma, [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]] of Illinois and Christopher Shays of Connecticut.[1] The letter was reported by [[the-hill|The Hill]].[1]
