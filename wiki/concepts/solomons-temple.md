# Solomon's Temple

Solomon's Temple is the biblical sanctuary in Jerusalem that occupies a foundational place in Freemasonic and occult symbolism. In [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account, the temple's mythic site was excavated by Sir [[charles-warren|Charles Warren]] on behalf of Edward VII, and Warren presumed the rock at its center to be the location on which the blood of all mass sacrifice victims of the ancient pagan syncretic cult system of Israel had flowed.[1]

Warren's excavations beneath the [[temple-mount|Temple Mount]] uncovered caverns and tunnels which he hypothesized were conduits for mass human sacrifice, and he believed the temple embodied Kabbalistic proportions built around the Vesica Pisces.[1] Ehret writes that Warren and other Rosicrucians regarded the temple as a source of power for channeling demonic forces.[1]

Ehret stresses that Warren's obsession had little to do with what ordinary Jews or Christians read in the Old Testament, and much more to do with the occultist belief that Solomon was not the monotheist king of scripture but a sorcerer king and overseer of occult sacrifices, with a special devotion to the Sidonian deity Ashtoreth and to [[molech|Molech]] — the Canaanite deities of child sacrifice.[1]

The contemporary Freemason [[kevin-gest|Kevin Gest]], author of *The Secrets of Solomon's Temple*, offers a solar-lunar etymology for the name: "Because of its special features, the temple became known as the 'Temple of Sol and Amon'. Sol meaning Sun, Amon meaning Moon. After his death Jedidiah was referred to as the man who built the Sol and Amon Temple, which became corrupted over time to Sol-amon and then to Solomon."[1]

Warren's Grand Mastership of the [[quatuor-coronati-lodge|Quatuor Coronati lodge]] is described as having set the stage for reviving the temple.[1]
