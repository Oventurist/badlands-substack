# Agenda 2030

Agenda 2030 is the United Nations sustainable development framework, treated in the Badlands corpus as the operational timetable of the globalist project.

## Treatment in the corpus

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] argues that transatlantic rupture would spell the end of the agenda. Commenting on reports that [[european-union|EU]] officials are preparing to confront President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over [[greenland|Greenland]], [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] writes that such a confrontation "could easily lead to a diplomatic divorce between the two continents, which would mean the official end of the ambitions set forth in Agenda 2030."

The same commentary pairs Agenda 2030 with the [[great-reset|Great Reset]], which the author describes as the mechanism intended to supply enforcement power to "international law." He argues that with the world "moving in the opposite [direction], away from the consolidation of power and toward the decentralization of authority," both projects are losing their footing — a reading consistent with the corpus's broader account of the collapse of the [[rules-based-international-order|rules-based international order]].
