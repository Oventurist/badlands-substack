# Agenda 2030

Agenda 2030 is, in Badlands Media commentary, the globalist program of restructuring national economies and societies by the end of the decade, treated as the operational timetable of the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and the transition to a [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1]

## Framing in Badlands commentary

Writing after [[fitch-ratings|Fitch]] downgraded the United States credit rating in August 2023, contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the deliberate failure of American economic management served this agenda, since "American sovereignty, let alone exceptionalism, cannot be allowed if Agenda 2030 is to succeed."[1] In that reading, the [[2023-united-states-credit-rating-downgrade|downgrade]] was not a technical judgment but a marker in a longer campaign in which "America must be brought to her knees" for the global regime to complete its project.[1]
