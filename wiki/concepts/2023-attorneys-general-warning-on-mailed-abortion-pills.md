# 2023 Attorneys General Warning on Mailed Abortion Pills

The 2023 attorneys general warning on mailed abortion pills was a joint action taken on Wednesday, February 1, 2023, in which twenty Republican state attorneys general notified the pharmacy chains [[cvs-health|CVS]] and [[walgreens|Walgreens]] that plans to distribute abortion pills through the mail would violate state and federal law.[1]

## The letter

The effort was led by Missouri Attorney General [[andrew-bailey|Andrew Bailey]].[1] In the letter sent to the chain drug stores, Bailey wrote that many people are not aware that federal law expressly prohibits using the mail to send or receive any drug that will be used or applied for producing an abortion.[1] "As Attorney General, it is my responsibility to enforce the laws as written, and that includes enforcing the very laws that protect Missouri's women and unborn children," Bailey said.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the episode to argue that the enactment of a law does not automatically produce enforcement, because district attorneys and law-enforcement agencies possess "incredible discretionary power" that can be exercised to inhibit justice and knowingly allow criminal conduct to continue.[1] He asked why large corporations such as CVS and Walgreens could use the mail to distribute abortion drugs without any major effort to stop it, contrasting that with the prosecution an ordinary citizen would face for openly mailing illicit drugs as a matter of routine business.[1] Deschamps concluded that until citizens "occupy our sacred office" as the single greatest check on government corruption, the status quo of two-tiered justice would continue — what he called "color of law, not the true rule of law."[1]
