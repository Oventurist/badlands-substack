# Ideological Subversion

**Ideological subversion** is the multigenerational model of communist warfare described by the defector [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]], in which the transformation of a society's beliefs occurs gradually over numerous generations rather than through sudden revolution.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] instructed ChatGPT to tie this concept into the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]]'s plan, mapping psychological warfare that proceeds subtly and gradually across generations so that the strategic objective of installing a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] can be pursued slowly over time.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The resulting synthesis is organised around that time-horizon. [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-generation warfare]] tactics — decentralised networks, psychological operations and misinformation campaigns — are described as spanning decades and adapting to changing societal norms and expectations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Long-term research into AI-driven information warfare develops advanced algorithms over generations to shape public opinion and infiltrate education systems.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Key institutions in academia, media and politics are infiltrated over years, while laws and international agreements are influenced to shift power gradually toward global governance.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Cultural warfare works by slowly changing societal norms until traditional values are replaced with globalist ideals.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

At the tactical level the same patience appears: control of major media outlets is acquired over generations and critical voices are marginalised over time; psychological operations influence the values and beliefs of entire generations by integrating [[critical-race-theory|CRT]], [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and [[climate-change-narrative|climate narratives]] into education; and activist movements are funded and guided across multiple generations so that activists are subtly conditioned to promote the Consortium's ideologies.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
