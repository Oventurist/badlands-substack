# Ideological Subversion

**Ideological subversion** is the multigenerational model of communist warfare described by the defector [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]], in which the transformation of a society's beliefs occurs gradually over numerous generations rather than through sudden revolution.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] instructed ChatGPT to tie this concept into the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]]'s plan, mapping psychological warfare that proceeds subtly and gradually across generations so that the strategic objective of installing a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] can be pursued slowly over time.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The resulting synthesis is organised around that time-horizon. [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-generation warfare]] tactics — decentralised networks, psychological operations and misinformation campaigns — are described as spanning decades and adapting to changing societal norms and expectations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Long-term research into AI-driven information warfare develops advanced algorithms over generations to shape public opinion and infiltrate education systems.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Key institutions in academia, media and politics are infiltrated over years, while laws and international agreements are influenced to shift power gradually toward global governance.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Cultural warfare works by slowly changing societal norms until traditional values are replaced with globalist ideals.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

At the tactical level the same patience appears: control of major media outlets is acquired over generations and critical voices are marginalised over time; psychological operations influence the values and beliefs of entire generations by integrating [[critical-race-theory|CRT]], [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and [[climate-change-narrative|climate narratives]] into education; and activist movements are funded and guided across multiple generations so that activists are subtly conditioned to promote the Consortium's ideologies.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## As a modern form of mind control

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] has argued that ideological subversion should be understood as a contemporary form of mind control, and that the popular conception of mind control — drawn from the robotic, distinct programming described by [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] survivor [[cathy-obrien|Cathy O'Brien]] — is too narrow.[2] Subversion of this kind requires neither sexual trauma rituals nor advanced neurological technology; its foundations were set by the rules of communist ideological subversion, namely that the work be done slowly across generations.[2] Once established to a sufficient degree, Esler argues, it operates in much the same way as overt programming.[2]

He cites as evidence a video from the [[2024-pro-palestinian-campus-protests|2024 pro-Palestinian campus protests]] in which a student admitted having no idea why she was demonstrating, saying only that propaganda had told her to come to the university, support local students and chant the correct slogans, and knowing nothing of what [[new-york-university|New York University]] had allegedly done wrong.[2] The inability of such participants to justify their own behaviour logically is presented as the signature of a centralised system of control.[2]

## Escape and the woke case

Esler applies the framework to what he calls the [[woke-mind-virus|Woke Mind Virus]] in post-secondary education, including the sanctioning of [[leigh-rivers|Leigh Rivers]] at the [[university-of-toronto|University of Toronto]] and the staged propaganda described by students in [[the-coddling-of-the-american-mind|The Coddling of the American Mind]].[2] He argues that [[detransitioners|detransitioners]] and recovered students together disprove [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]]'s contention that the subverted cannot recover, and that their empirical accounts of deconstructing the ideology constitute [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation]] weaponry usable against the process itself.[2]

## Detransitioners as a counter-example

In a March 2023 feature, [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] quoted [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]]'s 1984 definition of ideological subversion — changing the perception of reality so that, despite an abundance of information, no one can reach sensible conclusions in defence of themselves, their families, their community or their country — and his description of demoralised individuals who cannot be reached even by authentic information.[3] Esler argued that [[detransitioners|detransitioners]] represent an exception arising within the current theatre of war: contaminated to the fullest extent during their years as "trans youth," they were nonetheless awakened by the trauma of their mistakes, and their testimony amounts to an account of waking out of mind control.[3]
