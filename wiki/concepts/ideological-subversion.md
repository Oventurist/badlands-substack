# Ideological Subversion

Ideological subversion is the doctrine, associated in the Badlands Media corpus with Soviet defector [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]], that a target society is conquered not by force but by the slow corruption of its beliefs, institutions and moral confidence over the course of several generations.

The essay [[ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo|AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO]] invokes the concept in its final refinement prompt, where [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] asked [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to incorporate "the multigenerational concept of communist warfare as described by communist defector Yuri Bezmenov" and to map the resulting psychological warfare as subtle and gradual, unfolding across generations so that a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] could be installed slowly over time.

The AI's synthesis renders this as a decades-long programme: [[5th-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] conducted through decentralised networks and misinformation campaigns that adapt to changing societal norms; the long-term infiltration of academia, media and politics; the gradual alteration of laws and international agreements; the funding and conditioning of activist movements across multiple generations; and the patient integration of [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]], [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and the [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]] into education systems. The article's closing argument — that using AI to assemble these dispersed elements yields "collective situational awareness" — is a direct response to the covert, generational character of subversion so defined.
