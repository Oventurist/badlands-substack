# Truncated mRNA

Truncated mRNA refers to incomplete messenger-RNA fragments present in mRNA-based pharmaceutical products, and became a focus of criticism of [[pfizer|Pfizer]]'s COVID-19 vaccine in reporting published by [[epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] in February 2023 and summarized in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Regulatory objection

According to the summary of key facts reproduced in the brief, Pfizer's COVID-19 vaccine contains truncated mRNA, which the [[european-medicines-agency|European Medicines Agency]] flagged as a reason for a "major objection" — a designation indicating a preclusion of approval.[1] Pfizer was reported not to have investigated the detrimental outcomes of truncated mRNA in its vaccines.[1]

## Data integrity allegations

The reporting further alleged that Pfizer submitted Western blot figures to the [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] and the European Medicines Agency that were digitally generated rather than derived from actual experiments, and that there had been an alarming lack of action taken by health authorities on the issue.[1]

## Claimed health consequences

Truncated mRNA was said to potentially contribute to multiple vaccine-related injuries, including misfolded spike protein-induced fibrous blood clots, autoimmune disorders and cancer.[1] The reporting argued that such problems could have produced drastic product-quality variation from batch to batch, which in turn could explain differences in adverse events experienced by vaccine recipients.[1] The root cause of the conduct was characterized as a lack of ethics on the part of pharmaceutical companies and health authorities.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] summarized the matter as "They knew and they did it anyway," arguing that the situation with the Pfizer product was not an isolated incident but reflected research methods used across physics, biotech, psychology and social statistics.[1] He contended that nothing would change because there is no negative incentive to change it, with fines and resignations amounting to a cost of doing business.[1] As a remedy he proposed citizen-led peer review organizations capable of scrutinizing study methods to identify scientific frauds, describing such careful assessment as an essential part of the societal healing required to retain freedoms recovered during the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]].[1]
