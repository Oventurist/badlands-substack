# Old Guard

"Old Guard" is the term used across Badlands Media analysis for the entrenched transnational establishment — legacy political leaders, intelligence services, financial institutions and allied media — held to be in structural conflict with the emerging [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] of nationally oriented leaders.[1]

## Usage in Badlands analysis

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued in June 2024 that the Sovereign Alliance was "firing on all cylinders," habitually out-foxing the Old Guard elite in diplomacy, economics, on the battlefield, and increasingly in narrative warfare and public messaging.[1] He added that there is "absolutely nothing endearing or appealing about the Establishment, and what it claims to offer."[1]

The category is applied to individual leaders as well as institutions. He placed Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] within the Old Guard, contrasting the subtle hostility he perceived in Netanyahu's exchanges with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] against the visible warmth of Trump's relationships with figures such as [[jair-bolsonaro|Jair Bolsonaro]], [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]], [[shinzo-abe|Shinzo Abe]], [[nigel-farage|Nigel Farage]] and even [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]].[1] In this framing Trump represented a threat to Netanyahu's status quo just as he did to every other status quo across what the writer called the Old Guard universe.[1]

Related Badlands commentary treats the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] as an offensive diplomatic instrument designed to catalyze Old Guard bad actors into accelerating plans that predated Trump.[1]

## The removal of Joe Biden

Writing after [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s withdrawal from the 2024 race, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] offered a compact formulation of the concept: "The Old Guard tried and failed to remove Donald Trump. The Old Guard succeeded in removing Joe Biden. The Old Guard is on full display."[2] He added that this "wasn't their original plan," and concluded with the observation that "control comes in many forms."[2] In the same piece he argued that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had used [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] as his first cypher to expose the [[deep-state|Deep State]], spent his first term absorbing establishment attacks, and that the Biden administration had then drawn a clear line of demarcation between the establishment and the public — a sequence he described as replacing "Trauma-Paralysis programming" with "Trauma-Catharsis programming."[2]
