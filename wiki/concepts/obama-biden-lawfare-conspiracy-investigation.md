# Obama-Biden Lawfare Conspiracy Investigation

The Obama-Biden lawfare conspiracy investigation is a Justice Department effort, disclosed in late December 2025 by Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], to examine the Obama-Biden era of [[lawfare]] as an ongoing election-meddling criminal conspiracy.[1]

## Disclosure

Bondi told [[just-the-news|Just the News]] that she had asked prosecutors to investigate the period as a continuing conspiracy that protected Democrats from criminal investigation while infringing the civil rights of Republicans, including President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his supporters.[1] Recovering from eye surgery at the time, Bondi made the revelation in written answers submitted Sunday, December 28, 2025 to questions from the outlet.[1]

In doing so she signaled agreement with [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], who earlier in 2025 had authored a memo predicating an investigation into the weaponization of intelligence and law-enforcement powers dating back to the Russia collusion case, treating it as an ongoing conspiracy.[1]

## Legal theory

The ongoing-conspiracy framing is significant because it allows prosecutors to charge defendants with alleged crimes that would otherwise fall outside the statute of limitations, on the theory that those acts were connected to a conspiracy that continued into the chargeable period.[1] The approach mirrors racketeering-style prosecutions historically brought against the mafia and drug cartels.[1]

"At my direction, our U.S. Attorneys and federal agents are actively investigating instances of government weaponization nationwide," Bondi said, describing the matter as "a ten-year stain on the country committed by high-ranking officials against the American people."[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, argued that the Obama-Biden era lawfare extended well beyond plaintiffs and prosecutors to encompass judges, litigation strategists, academics who designed the schemes and then testified as expert witnesses, and a media that uncritically adopted the resulting narratives.[1] She wrote that there is "no question" the era constituted an ongoing criminal conspiracy, and pointed in particular to the weaponization of the FBI's Washington Field Office against Trump supporters and advocates for conservative causes ranging from Right to Life to parents' rights and election integrity.[1] Describing the issue as personal, she said those who weaponized the investigatory apparatus and the courts must be held accountable, and that a record had been built in the belief that justice would one day be possible.[1]
