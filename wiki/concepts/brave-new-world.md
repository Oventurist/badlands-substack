# Brave New World

*Brave New World* is [[aldous-huxley|Aldous Huxley]]'s dystopian novel, treated by Badlands Media not as speculative fiction but as a transparent statement of the oligarchical program safely hidden behind the veneer of fiction.[1]

In the novel the transhuman control system standing above the test-tube alphas, betas and deltas is represented by the figure of Mustapha Mond.[1] The article quotes at length Mond's suppression of a paper titled "A New Theory of Biology," which he judges "heretical and, so far as the present social order is concerned, dangerous and potentially subversive. Not to be published."[1] Mond reflects that admitting explanations in terms of purpose might decondition the more unsettled minds among the higher castes, making them lose faith in happiness as the Sovereign Good and believe instead that the purpose of life was "some intensification and refining of consciousness, some enlargement of knowledge" — which the Controller concedes is quite possibly true, but not admissible.[1]

Badlands Media reads this passage as the kernel of the problem faced by scientific managers who wish to govern society on "scientific principles," and as the blind spot of [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]] and his eco-anarchist imitators: the idea of science and technology to which the social engineers are committed is a chimera with no bearing on the nature of the systems they wish to govern like gods.[1]

The novel's drug 'soma', its 'feelies' and its loveless sex are described as the imperative cultural norms of a hedonist world of sensualism — a template that Huxley pursued in reality through the LSD and psilocybin cultural matrix and his recruitment of [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]], which in conjunction with the new high priests of [[cybernetics|cybernetics]] emerging out of [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] would supply the foundations for a new era of feudalism and willful depopulation.[1] The article also invokes the Shakespearean "womb-born" outsider John Savage as the archetype of the person who responds to that order by burning it down.[1]
