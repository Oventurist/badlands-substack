# South Fork Dairy Explosion

The South Fork Dairy explosion was a large blast and fire at a dairy operation in Dimmitt, Texas, on the night of Monday 10 April 2023.[1]

## Incident

The explosion engulfed multiple structures and produced smoke visible from as much as 80 miles away, according to reporting by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]].[1] Texas authorities said more than 18,000 cattle died, with the incident described in some accounts as approaching 20,000 animals lost.[1] Castro County Sheriff Sal Rivera said, "There's some that survived, there's some that are probably injured to the point where they'll have to be destroyed."[1] A mass casualty incident was declared and the cause was reported as unknown.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the Badlands News Brief, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] questioned the "machinery malfunction" explanation, comparing it sarcastically to the equipment failures reported at Maricopa County election centers in November 2022 and arguing that no ordinary factory system should be capable of a blast of that magnitude.[1] DeLarme suggested the event resembled a bomb detonation and situated it within what he described as roughly 300 comparable "accidents" affecting American food infrastructure since [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] took office.[1] He linked the pattern to concerns about farmland ownership by [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] and Chinese interests and to a projected future food supply of insects, antibiotic-heavy poultry, and produce treated with glyphosate and heavy metals.[1]
