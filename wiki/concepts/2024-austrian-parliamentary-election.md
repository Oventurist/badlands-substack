# 2024 Austrian Parliamentary Election

The 2024 Austrian parliamentary election was held on Sunday, September 29, 2024, and produced the first national victory for a far-right party in Austria since the Second World War.[1]

## Result

Preliminary results showed the [[freedom-party-of-austria|Freedom Party (FPÖ)]] finishing first, prompting its leader [[herbert-kickl]] to hail a "new era."[1] Commentators placed the outcome within a wider European drift toward anti-immigration, populist parties, comparable to gains by the far right in eastern German state elections earlier in 2024.[1]

## Coalition deadlock

Austrian law requires a coalition government, and every other party had ruled out governing with the FPÖ, making it unlikely that the election winner would take power.[1] The one nominal exception was the outgoing conservative People's Party (ÖVP) under Chancellor [[karl-nehammer]], who nonetheless declared he would not work with Kickl because of the FPÖ leader's conspiracy beliefs.[1]
