# Glenn Amendment

The Glenn Amendment is a United States statutory provision restricting foreign assistance to states that acquire or possess nuclear weapons outside the nonproliferation framework.[1]

## Relevance to Israel

In Badlands commentary on the November 2024 [[handala|Handala]] leak of photographs said to depict Israeli nuclear facilities, commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the amendment is a central reason Israel has never signed the [[nuclear-non-proliferation-treaty|Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty]]: signing would require Israel to disclose weapons and facilities it is widely believed to possess, and a formal admission would, under the Glenn Amendment, render it immediately ineligible for further U.S. aid.[1] He dated the provision to 1961 and associated its passage with President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], though the amendment is more commonly dated to later nonproliferation legislation.[1]
