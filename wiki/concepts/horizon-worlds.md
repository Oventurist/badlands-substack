# Horizon Worlds

Horizon Worlds is [[meta-platforms|Meta]]'s social virtual-world platform, the consumer-facing centerpiece of the company's metaverse strategy and a product of its [[reality-labs|Reality Labs]] division.

## 2026 shutdown reversal

According to the Badlands Brief edition "Golgotha & The Metaverse" (March 20, 2026), Meta reversed an earlier announcement that Horizon Worlds would cease to be accessible in virtual reality after June 15. The company instead pivoted its metaverse experiences toward mobile platforms and away from fully immersive virtual worlds toward mixed reality, while committing to continue funding Reality Labs for the foreseeable future.

Meta technology chief Andrew Bosworth said in a social media post: "We will keep Horizon Worlds working in VR for existing games, to support the fans who've reached out. For people who already have games they like that they're using in Horizon Worlds, [they] will be able to download the Horizon Worlds app and use it in VR for the foreseeable future."

Bosworth also disputed coverage of the original announcement, saying, "We announced, 'hey, we're moving away from Horizon Worlds in VR,' and the headline is that Horizon is dead. It's not. And likewise, VR is not dead. We're continuing to invest tremendously."

## Reception

Badlands contributor [[matthew-trump|Matthew Trump]] treated the episode as emblematic of the metaverse's failure, describing [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s effort to "stake his entire company's future on a virtual reality avatar world that almost no one wanted to explore" as among the most mock-worthy ventures of the social media era. He suggested the reprieve may have been motivated partly by spite toward critics who celebrated the shutdown, and predicted the underlying concept would eventually return "in a new less repulsive form," with the original remembered as a pioneering experiment that failed because it was ahead of its time.
