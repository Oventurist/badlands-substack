# MIL-CIV Alliance

The "MIL-CIV alliance" is a term used in Q-adjacent commentary for a claimed partnership between white-hat factions of the military and civilian citizen journalists, in which each side performs a task the other cannot.[1]

## Formulation

In the January 28, 2025 Badlands Media article "On Golden Bridges and the Art of War," [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] described the alliance in functional terms: military tribunals are required for the crimes attributed to the [[cabal|cabal]], whereas citizen journalism is required to expose those crimes sufficiently before the world.[1] On that reading, "the White hat factions of military need us, and we need them."[1]

The author connected the arrangement to the claim, attributed to [[qanon|Q]], that the [[white-hats|White Hats]] never telegraph their moves to their enemies.[1] Because [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and allied figures cannot raise "far out conspiracy" topics at rallies or in television appearances, the burden of raising them falls to ordinary [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]]; once enough of the public is aware of a topic, the argument runs, public officials become free to address it directly.[1] The stated real goal is therefore "to get the masses to participate in the exposure of the cabal," and the more knowledgeable the public becomes, the more freedom white-hat operators have to advance their operations.[1]

The author predicted that the process would culminate in one final "scare event" before a final revealing, and expressed the belief that [[the-storm|the Storm]] and the revealing would occur in the third or fourth quarter of 2025.[1]
