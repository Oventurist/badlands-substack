# 2024 Trump Vice Presidential Selection

The 2024 Trump vice presidential selection was the contest to become [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s running mate on the Republican presidential ticket. By January 2024 the backstage fight over the pick — and particularly over who would *not* be chosen — was described as fiercer than the visible primary campaign in Iowa and New Hampshire.[1]

## The "Stop Nikki" effort

As [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] emerged as Trump's most formidable remaining primary opponent and toughened her rhetoric heading into the coming states, her Trumpworld adversaries intensified efforts to block her from what they viewed as the consolation prize of the vice presidency.[1]

Haley's critics privately warned Trump that making her first in line to the presidency would effectively set him up for an intra-party coup, arguing that GOP senators would use any legal or political pretext to remove him from office and elevate the more old-guard-aligned Haley.[1]

Rep. [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] (R-Fla.) said that "Nikki Haley as VP would be an establishment neocon fantasy and a MAGA nightmare," adding that "on Day One she would convert the Naval Observatory into an anti-Trump, resistance headquarters, undermining him at every step." The forcefulness of the Stop Nikki effort reflected that Trump's decision would go to the heart of the party's debate over its own identity.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued there were only two possibilities regarding the VP pick and all subsequent administrative decisions: either they had already been made and the public was watching a slow drip of that information, or the team was beta-testing potential choices in the MAGA and [[america-first-movement|America First]] ether and adjusting internal logistics accordingly. Either way, he wrote, the campaign was watching public interaction with these deployments and measuring sentiment, typically starting with its most targeted base before traversing what he calls the Normie Layers of the Collective Mind.[1]
