# 2026 Los Angeles Mayoral Election

The 2026 Los Angeles mayoral election is the contest for the mayoralty of [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]], in which incumbent [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] faces a field that includes former reality-television figure [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]]. As covered in the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," the race has been reshaped by Pratt's unconventional, low-budget campaign.

## Pratt's insurgent campaign

Pratt's campaign runs on homelessness, street disorder, anger over the city's fire response, and anti-bureaucracy messaging. His most publicised tactic has been the use of power washers and stencils to blast clean slogans into filthy city sidewalks — "IMAGINE IF THE STREETS WERE THIS CLEAN" and "SPENCER PRATT FOR MAYOR" — a stunt [[modernity-news|Modernity News]] framed as a guerrilla campaign advertisement whose force increases with the dirtiness of the street.

## Polling and fundraising

Reporting cited in the Brief indicates that polling and fundraising momentum have placed Pratt in serious contention for a runoff position against Bass. Newsweek reported over the weekend of the Brief's publication that Pratt was dominating in fundraising, and the most recent polling cited by the New York Times placed him within three points of Bass. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cautioned that this poll came from a private research firm with no sponsor cited, describing such work as "inbred polling."

## Reaction from Los Angeles establishment

Local elite reaction has been hostile. [[drew-carey|Drew Carey]] publicly called Pratt a "serial scammer," while other critics have attacked his residency, hotel spending and MAGA-adjacent support. The Brief argued that such attacks reinforce rather than undercut Pratt's central visual argument: "if the city were clean, the ad wouldn't work."
