# Badlands Blitz Episode 25

**Badlands Blitz Episode 25**, subtitled "It's Treason, Then …", is an installment of the weekly recap program produced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and published to the Badlands Substack on July 18, 2026. At the time of archiving, the post had recorded 223 likes. Like its predecessors, the episode is framed as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]," the outlet's weekday newsletter.

## Format and hosts

The episode is hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], the pairing that fronts most editions of the Blitz. Departing from the program's usual survey of several stories, this installment devotes its entire runtime — described in the announcement as "the whole hour (and change)" — to a single subject.

## Subject matter

The announcement states that the hosts discuss what it calls Donald Trump's "seismic (and yes, it actually was seismic) election fraud speech" delivered on the Thursday preceding publication. The parenthetical aside is presented as an insistence that the adjective is literal rather than rhetorical inflation, a framing consistent with the outlet's habit of arguing that events dismissed as routine by mainstream coverage carry structural significance.

Three lines of implication are flagged. First, the consequences of the speech for what the source terms the "[[deep-state|Deep State]] cabal." Second, its bearing on the "Shadow War," the covert dimension of conflict that Badlands writers place beneath visible politics. Third, its bearing on the [[information-war|Information War]]. The announcement argues that the speech "represents the beginnings of a true phase shift in both the Shadow War and the Information War at once" — that is, a simultaneous change of state in the covert and narrative theatres rather than a development confined to either. The episode title, "It's Treason, Then …", signals the legal register in which the hosts frame election fraud allegations.

## Publishing model

The post repeats the outlet's standard closing appeal. It affirms that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," while noting that the team is compensated only through voluntary paid subscriptions, and invites readers to upgrade to a paid subscription, "cancel at any time." Reference is made to "the step-up in said work for 2026," language echoing earlier episodes and indicating an expanded editorial output. The article closes with the recurring sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
