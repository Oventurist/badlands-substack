# Designating English as the Official Language of the United States

**Designating English as the Official Language of the United States** is a presidential action issued by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in March 2025 that declared English the official language of the United States for the first time in the nation's history.[1]

## Content

The order observed that English has been used as the national language since the founding of the Republic, and that the country's historic governing documents — including the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution — were written in English.[1] It argued that a nationally designated language is "at the core of a unified and cohesive society," and that the United States is strengthened by a citizenry able to freely exchange ideas in one shared language.[1]

Framing the measure in terms of assimilation, the order stated that encouraging new Americans to learn and adopt the national language would make the United States a shared home and empower new citizens to achieve the American dream, noting that speaking English opens economic doors and helps newcomers engage in their communities and national traditions.[1] It also explicitly recognized the tradition of multilingual American citizens who learned English and passed it to their children.[1] The stated rationale for designating "one — and only one" official language was to promote unity, cultivate a shared American culture, ensure consistency in government operations, streamline communication, and create a pathway to civic engagement.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] joked in response that Trump should sign a further executive order renaming the "English" language as the "American" language.[1]
