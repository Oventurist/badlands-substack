# Rational Hope

**Rational hope** is a term used by the pseudonymous [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] for hope grounded in logic and evidence rather than in blind belief.[1] In Part 1 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]], published 22 October 2024, the author writes that [[q-operation|Q]] "has offered us something I call *rational hope*, which is not the same thing as wishful thinking," and holds that because it rests on reasoning rather than assertion it is "significantly more powerful than the alternative."[1]

## Distinction from blind belief

The author is careful to separate rational hope from faith understood as blind belief, remarking in passing that the common equation of the two is not justifiable, though he defers the argument to another occasion.[1] The practical difference he draws is that rational hope, unlike blind belief, "can alter reality itself for the better" — a claim he says becomes clear once the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe]] is fully comprehended, since on that model observer participancy allows cognition to help shape the contents of reality.[1]

## Function within the Great Awakening

Rational hope is presented as the psychological resource that makes the [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldier]] role sustainable during the end phases of what the author calls the [[5g-war|5G war]].[1] Without "certain friends in high places," Q among them, he argues there would be "no hope whatsoever for the future of humanity," and the puzzle-form delivery of Q's material is treated as the means by which that hope was made rational — participants assemble the picture themselves rather than accepting a promise.[1] The concept underpins the wider white-pill project of the series, which the author frames as a permanent antidote to blackpilling.[1]
