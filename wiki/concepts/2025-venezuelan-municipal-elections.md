# 2025 Venezuelan Municipal Elections

The 2025 Venezuelan municipal elections were held on July 27, 2025, and were presented by the Venezuelan government and allied media as a demonstration of peace, mass participation and democratic resilience.[1]

## Official framing

[[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]] hailed the vote as a historic triumph of peace and participation.[1] State-aligned outlet TeleSur reported that Venezuela's political landscape had been reaffirmed "not through conflict, but through mass civic engagement, peace, and youth protagonism," describing the elections as more than a contest for mayoralties — "a national affirmation of peace, unity, and the enduring strength of participatory democracy."[1]

The most transformative element, in that account, was the unprecedented role of youth, institutionalized through the National Youth Popular Consultation: a parallel vote allowing young Venezuelans to select community projects they had themselves designed across 5,338 communal circuits nationwide.[1]

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] wrote that Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] wanted to make it "absolutely clear to the world" that the administration did not approve of the exercise.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] offered a second reading, remarking that there is "nothing new under the sun" and quoting Mao Zedong's November 17, 1957 address to Chinese students and trainees in Moscow: "The world is yours, as well as ours, but in the last analysis, it is yours. You young people, full of vigour and vitality, are in the bloom of life, like the sun at eight or nine in the morning. Our hope is placed on you.… The world belongs to you. China's future belongs to you."[1] She offered "thoughts and prayers for [[venezuela|Venezuela]] and their impending state-sanctioned violence."[1]
