# Zuckerberg Hawaii Bunker

The Zuckerberg Hawaii bunker is a roughly 4,500-square-foot underground structure on the 1,500-acre Kauai ranch owned by Meta chief executive [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]], widely described in the press as a "doomsday bunker."[1]

## Denial

In an interview with Bloomberg's Emily Chang at his Lake Tahoe estate, Zuckerberg was asked, "You do have a bunker there; is there something you know that we don't?"[1] He denied the structure was a doomsday bunker, calling it a "little shelter ... basement," and said his whole ranch "got blown out of proportion as if it was some kind of doomsday bunker, which is just not true."[1]

## Reporting and context

In late 2023, Wired reported that the bunker measured around 4,500 square feet and was equipped with a blast-resistant door and enough food for Zuckerberg and companions to survive an apocalypse.[1] Coverage of the denial paired it with speculation about what Zuckerberg might know regarding coming world events, referencing commentary asking whether a third world war is already underway.[1] The same report noted that non-billionaires have cheaper options, citing Zillow listings for a bunker in an old missile silo in Missouri with EMP shielding and another in Kansas priced under a million dollars.[1]
