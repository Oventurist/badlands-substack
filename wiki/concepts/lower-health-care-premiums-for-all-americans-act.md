# Lower Health Care Premiums for All Americans Act

The Lower Health Care Premiums for All Americans Act is a partisan package of health care policies passed by House Republicans on Wednesday, December 17, 2025, designed as a conservative alternative to extending the expiring ObamaCare subsidies.[1]

## Content and passage

The bill includes policies widely popular among Republicans, such as funds to pay for "cost-sharing reductions," reforms to the pharmacy benefit manager industry, and an expansion of association health plans.[1] It passed the [[us-house-of-representatives|House]] without difficulty by a vote of 216–211, with Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] (R-Ky.) joining all Democrats in voting against it.[1]

## Limitations

The legislation has little chance of passing the [[us-senate|Senate]], and it does not address the cost cliff expected to hit 22 million Americans when the enhanced [[affordable-care-act|Affordable Care Act]] tax credits sunset on December 31 — a dynamic that infuriated centrist Republicans who had clamored for months to extend the benefits.[1] Even those frustrated Republicans supported the conservative policies in the package.[1]
