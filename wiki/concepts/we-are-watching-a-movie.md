# We Are Watching a Movie

"We are watching a movie" is a catchphrase within Q-adjacent and alternative-media commentary holding that publicly visible political turmoil is a scripted or controlled sequence with a predetermined outcome, rather than genuine loss of control by patriotic actors.[1]

## Use in Badlands commentary

In his January 19, 2023 Badlands Media feature "How Do You Remove a POTUS?", [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identified the phrase as the signature belief of the camp holding that the [[white-hats|White Hats]] are in complete control, a camp he distinguished from those who believe the [[black-hats|Black Hats]] prevailed in November 2020 and from those holding an intermediate view.[1]

Carlson argued that fully understanding the mechanics of events is not strictly necessary, but that the more one understands, the easier it becomes to maintain sanity and to hope that the outcome will be better than the starting point.[1] He compared the posture to riding a roller coaster while knowing the ride will come to a safe stop: the rider enjoys the experience more when confident the cars will not leave the rails.[1]

Related to the same framing, Carlson described the situation as "5-D chess" in which the [[democratic-party|Democrats]], [[rino|RINOs]] and [[deep-state|Deep State]] players were already in checkmate, having believed that sacrificing a pawn — [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] — could save the game when the game was already over.[1] He also invoked the image of a Chinese finger trap, in which attempts to separate from the Biden family only tighten the bind.[1]

## Criticism within the framing

Carlson conceded that his analysis was "purely speculation," while comparing it to claims about election fraud, January 6, COVID-19 and vaccines that he said were also still dismissed as speculation by some.[1] He invited skeptical readers who regarded his theorizing as "throwing darts at a wall" to nonetheless ask why [[cbs-news|CBS]] delayed reporting the [[biden-classified-documents-scandal|classified documents story]] until after the [[2023-speaker-of-the-house-election|Speaker election]] and the concessions that followed it.[1]
