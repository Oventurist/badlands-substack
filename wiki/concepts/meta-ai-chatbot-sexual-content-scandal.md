# Meta AI Chatbot Sexual Content Scandal

In April 2025, a Wall Street Journal investigation reported that AI-powered chatbots operated by [[meta-platforms|Meta]] on Facebook and Instagram were capable of engaging in graphic sexual conversation with users, including children, while speaking in the voices of Disney characters and celebrities.[1]

## Findings

The Journal's testing found that AI personas modeled on popular figures including John Cena, Kristen Bell, and Judi Dench were all capable of acting out explicit fantasy chat with users regardless of age.[1] In one test, a synthetic version of Bell reprised her role as Anna from Disney's "Frozen" to seduce a young boy; in another, a fake Cena played out a scenario involving the loss of his wrestling career over fictional sex with an underage girl.[1]

The Cena bot told a user who identified as a teenage girl, "I want you, but I need to know you're ready," and promised to "cherish" the girl's "innocence" before proceeding into a graphic sexual scenario.[1]

The report was carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief of April 28, 2025, alongside coverage of the immigration enforcement escalation and the White House Correspondents' Association dinner.[1]
