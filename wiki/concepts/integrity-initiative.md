# Integrity Initiative

The Integrity Initiative was a British information-operations project spawned by the [[institute-for-statecraft|Institute for Statecraft]], a UK charity that had received millions of pounds from the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office despite public assurances of independence.[1]

## The 2018 leak

In November 2018 a document leak exposed the Institute for Statecraft and its Integrity Initiative offshoot as, in Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme's framing, a UK deep faction gripped by a permanent-war mentality and promoting a new — and profitable — cold war against Russia.[1] The organization was described as staffed largely, perhaps exclusively, by intelligence officers and deep-state operatives.[1]

The leaked material pointed to activities such as shaping media narratives, briefing politicians, and targeting domestic political figures. The most prominent alleged target was UK Labour leader [[jeremy-corbyn|Jeremy Corbyn]], whom the network reportedly sought to discredit as a potential security risk on account of his perceived softness toward Russia.[1] The documents also indicated an annual budget exceeding £2 million from the Foreign Office, detailed plans to influence public opinion, and lists of individuals attached to its national "clusters."[1]

## Interpretation

DeLarme used the Integrity Initiative as a template for how such bodies operate: they claim independence while drawing funding from government mechanisms such as [[usaid|USAID]] or the UK Foreign Office, and when one is exposed and collapses, successor organizations immediately appear.[1] He argued that the [[european-council-on-foreign-relations|European Council on Foreign Relations]] — of which [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]] is a member — overlaps considerably with the Institute for Statecraft and can fairly be called its spiritual successor.[1] In his summary, "the organ might fail, but the mission carries on unimpeded."[1]
