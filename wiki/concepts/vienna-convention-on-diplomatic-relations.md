# Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations

The Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations is the international treaty framework governing the treatment and protection of diplomatic missions and their personnel by host states.[1]

## Invocation over the West Bank attack

Russia invoked the convention in August 2025 after a Russian diplomatic vehicle was attacked by Israeli settlers near the settlement of Giv'at Asaf in the occupied West Bank on July 30.[1] [[russian-foreign-ministry|Russian Foreign Ministry]] spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] said Moscow considered the incident a violation of the convention, citing both the damage to the vehicle and the threats shouted at the diplomats.[1] She further criticized the failure of [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] soldiers to intervene, which in Russia's framing compounded the host state's failure to discharge its protective obligations.[1]

The occupants of the vehicle belonged to Russia's diplomatic mission to the Palestinian Authority and were also accredited with the [[israeli-ministry-of-foreign-affairs|Israeli Foreign Ministry]], placing them within the protective scope Moscow asserted.[1] See [[givat-asaf-settler-attack-on-russian-diplomats|the Givat Asaf incident]] for details.[1]
