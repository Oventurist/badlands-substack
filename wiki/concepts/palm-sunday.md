# Palm Sunday

Palm Sunday is the Christian feast commemorating Jesus' triumphal entry into Jerusalem, marking the start of [[holy-week|Holy Week]].[1] In Jerusalem it is observed with processions and Masses centered on [[church-of-the-holy-sepulchre|the Church of the Holy Sepulchre]].[1]

## 2026 Jerusalem incident

On Palm Sunday, March 29, 2026, [[israeli-police|Israeli police]] prevented senior Catholic leaders, including Latin Patriarch [[pierbattista-pizzaballa|Pierbattista Pizzaballa]], from entering the Holy Sepulchre to celebrate a private Mass limited to a handful of clergy.[1] Church authorities said it was the first time in centuries that top leaders had been unable to conduct Palm Sunday services at the site.[1] Italian leader [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] said barring the Patriarch and the Custos of the Holy Land on "a solemnity central to the faith such as Palm Sunday" was an offence against believers and against every community recognizing religious freedom.[1] U.S. ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] likewise called the exclusion difficult to justify.[1]
