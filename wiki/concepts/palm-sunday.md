# Palm Sunday

Palm Sunday is the Christian feast marking Jesus Christ's triumphal entry into Jerusalem, celebrated on the Sunday preceding Easter and opening Holy Week, the most solemn period of the liturgical year. Congregations traditionally carry palm branches in procession, recalling the crowds who greeted Jesus with cries of "Hosanna!" Because the events it commemorates occurred in Jerusalem itself, the observance carries particular weight at the city's Christian shrines, above all the [[church-of-the-holy-sepulchre|Church of the Holy Sepulchre]].

## 2026 Jerusalem controversy

Palm Sunday 2026, falling on March 29, became the occasion of an international dispute over religious freedom when Israeli police blocked Cardinal [[pierbattista-pizzaballa|Pierbattista Pizzaballa]] and other senior Catholic leaders from entering the Holy Sepulchre to celebrate a small private Mass. The [[latin-patriarchate-of-jerusalem|Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem]] called the action "manifestly unreasonable and grossly disproportionate," and church authorities described it as the first time in centuries that the leadership of the church had been unable to conduct Palm Sunday services at the site.

Israeli authorities cited missile threats from the war with [[iran|Iran]], limited shelter availability and restricted emergency access in the Old City. Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] later ordered access permitted, and said Israel had "temporarily asked worshippers from all faiths not to worship at the holy sites in Jerusalem's Old City to protect them." Criticism came from Italian Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]], who called the exclusion "an offence not only against believers but against every community that recognises religious freedom," and from U.S. Ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]].

## Badlands framing

In the Badlands Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] compiled a year-by-year chronology of Palm Sunday restrictions at the Holy Sepulchre — full closure in 2020 under [[covid-19-pandemic|COVID-19]] health rules, limited reopening in 2021, police entry limits in 2022, barriers and controlled Old City entry in 2023, and war-related reductions in 2024 — arguing that 2026 marked a qualitative break because clergy themselves were denied entry rather than merely crowds being managed. She characterized the progression as a "guided" sequence beginning with what she described as the transformational events of 2020, and closed her commentary with "Hosanna!"
