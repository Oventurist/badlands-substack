# Electoral Confidence Preservation Act

The "Electoral Confidence Preservation Act" is a fictitious law invented by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] for the Badlands Jury experience, a live mock-trial format debuted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[jonathan-drake|Jonathan Drake]] during the Deadwood [[gart|GART]] to what the Brief described as smash reviews.[1]

The premise of the fictitious case was that Badlands had been indicted for violating the Act, a made-up statute that criminalized doubting the delivery mechanism of democracy.[1]

## Relevance to the SHIELD statement

Ashe in America argued that the language of the July 2026 [[shield-of-the-americas|Shield of the Americas]] joint statement on [[colombia|Colombia]] was reminiscent of the invented law, in particular its declaration that the sovereign will of citizens formalized by competent electoral authorities is the sole foundation of the legitimacy of public power, and its rejection of any effort to discredit electoral authorities.[1] The satirical statute thus functions in Badlands commentary as a shorthand for real-world diplomatic and legal norms that treat questioning election administration as itself illegitimate.[1]
