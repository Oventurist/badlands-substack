# Megachile lucifer

Megachile (Hackeriapis) lucifer is a native Australian bee species, newly described in November 2025, distinguished by tiny devil-like "horns" on the face of the female.[1] It was discovered in the Goldfields region of Western Australia, and its description was presented as evidence of how much remains unknown about Australia's native pollinators.[1]

## Discovery

The bee was found during surveys of a critically endangered wildflower, Marianthus aquilonarius, which grows only in the Bremer Range region between the towns of Norseman and Hyden.[1] The lead author of the description, Curtin Adjunct Research Fellow Dr Kit Prendergast of the Curtin School of Molecular and Life Sciences, said the female bee's unusual horned face inspired the epithet lucifer — Latin for "light-bringer," and also a playful nod to the devilish appearance.[1]
