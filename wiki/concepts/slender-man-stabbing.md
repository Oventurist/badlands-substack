# Slender Man Stabbing

The Slender Man stabbing refers to an attack in Wisconsin in which two 12-year-old girls stabbed a friend 19 times, saying they had been told to do so by "Slender Man," a fictional internet horror meme.[1] The victim, Payton Leutner, who was also 12 at the time, survived the attack.[1]

One of the two attackers, [[morgan-geyser|Morgan Geyser]], was subsequently confined and later placed in a group home under the authority of the Wisconsin Department of Corrections, wearing an ankle monitor.[1] In November 2025, at age 23, Geyser removed the monitor and fled the group home, prompting a nationwide manhunt that ended with her arrest in Posen, Illinois.[1] The case returned to national attention at that point and prompted commentary about the handling of violent offenders with severe mental illness, including calls to reconsider institutional psychiatric care.[1]
