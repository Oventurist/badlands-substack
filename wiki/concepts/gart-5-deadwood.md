# GART 5: Deadwood

**GART 5: Deadwood** was the fifth stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live conference series organised by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood, South Dakota]] in August 2024.[1] The event was promoted in an August 11, 2024 Badlands Brief post titled "Last Call for GART: Deadwood," written by network host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who announced that he would be joining his fellow Badlands hosts in Deadwood the following week.[1]

## Framing

In the promotional post, Burning Bright described the GART events as having "presented a great opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots, and create an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place" — a formulation the network reused across subsequent tour stops.[1]

## Ticketing

By the time of the final call, VIP tickets for the Deadwood event had sold out.[1] A limited number of single-day tickets remained available for attendees local to the area who wished to attend part of the Friday or Saturday festivities, sold through the network's TicketSpice storefront.[1]

For those unable to attend in person, Badlands sold a virtual ticket that streamed the entirety of the event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.[1] Virtual purchases included long-term DVR access to panel replays following the weekend, so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss the programming.[1] The virtual ticket also granted access to an exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat, described as a space to interact with hundreds of fellow Badlanders and Badlands hosts, view behind-the-scenes photos and videos, and take part in "virtual shenanigans galore."[1]

## Programme

Badlands advertised a full published schedule of speakers for the weekend.[1] Burning Bright previewed the range of subject matter as spanning "everything from coming Full Circle on the Q Drops to Futurism and Freedom Cities," linking the network's foundational [[qanon|Q]]-adjacent research interests to forward-looking technological and civic themes.[1] He closed the announcement by saying he hoped to see familiar faces and, "as has happened at every GART so far, plenty of new ones," signing off with his customary "Stay Bright!"[1]
