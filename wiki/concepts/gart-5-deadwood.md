# GART 5: Deadwood

**GART 5: Deadwood** was the fifth stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live conference series organised by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood, South Dakota]] in August 2024.[1] The event was promoted in an August 11, 2024 Badlands Brief post titled "Last Call for GART: Deadwood," written by network host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who announced that he would be joining his fellow Badlands hosts in Deadwood the following week.[1]

## Framing

In the promotional post, Burning Bright described the GART events as having "presented a great opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots, and create an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place" — a formulation the network reused across subsequent tour stops.[1]

## Ticketing

By the time of the final call, VIP tickets for the Deadwood event had sold out.[1] A limited number of single-day tickets remained available for attendees local to the area who wished to attend part of the Friday or Saturday festivities, sold through the network's TicketSpice storefront.[1]

For those unable to attend in person, Badlands sold a virtual ticket that streamed the entirety of the event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.[1] Virtual purchases included long-term DVR access to panel replays following the weekend, so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss the programming.[1] The virtual ticket also granted access to an exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat, described as a space to interact with hundreds of fellow Badlanders and Badlands hosts, view behind-the-scenes photos and videos, and take part in "virtual shenanigans galore."[1]

## Announcement and format

Tickets for GART 5 went on sale in a July 6, 2024 Badlands Brief post titled "The Great American Restoration Tour Continues!", which invited readers to join the network for the fifth stop of the tour.[2] The post confirmed the event would run from August 15 to 18, 2024 in [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood, South Dakota]], with the conference itself held at the Holiday Inn Resort Deadwood Mountain Grand.[2] Because of the limited venue options in the area, the hosting hotel was already sold out at announcement, and ticket buyers were told they would be responsible for sourcing their own accommodation and travel at nearby hotels or short-term rentals.[2]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] described the event as "a live, ticketed event, with in-person speakers and meet-and-greets all weekend long," offering live in-person versions of the network's shows, live Q&A sessions with Badlands panels, and the chance to meet show hosts face to face.[2] The announcement joked that attendees might "trap speakers into unofficial lobby or bar socials that last until 3am … if you've got whisky on hand," and stressed that the event's chief value was forming lifelong connections with fellow "Badlanders."[2] The network framed the tour within its slogan "We are the news, now," a saying it said had come to define a growing movement within the Truth and America First communities, and credited audience support with turning Badlands into one of the most-watched independent networks online, carrying dozens of citizen journalists, podcasters and personalities.[2]

In-person tickets included a Welcome Reception with Badlands Media on the Friday night and breakfast and lunch across the weekend, with the stated aim of helping attendees "level up in the [[information-war|Information War]] together."[2] The Thursday-night VIP dinner was already sold out at the time of the July announcement, and readers were directed to watch for GART 6 tickets later in the year.[2] Tickets were sold through the network's TicketSpice storefront.[2]

### Expected speakers

The announcement listed an expected speaker roster of [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]], [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]], [[brian-lupo|Brian Lupo]], [[ghost-of-bph|Ghost of BPH]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[stormy-patriot-joe|Stormy Patriot Joe]], AbsoluteTruth, [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], Alpha Warrior, G-Money, Patriots in Progress, Abbey Getz and Matt Trump.[2]

### Virtual tickets

For those unable to attend in person, virtual tickets were offered giving live-stream access to all three days of panels along with months of post-event access to the replays.[2] Virtual buyers also received a direct invite to an exclusive "GART: Deadwood" [[telegram|Telegram]] chat where in-person and virtual attendees could interact with speakers before and after the event; the chat was to go live closer to the event dates, with invites and stream links sent to the email address used at purchase.[2]

## Programme

Badlands advertised a full published schedule of speakers for the weekend.[1] Burning Bright previewed the range of subject matter as spanning "everything from coming Full Circle on the Q Drops to Futurism and Freedom Cities," linking the network's foundational [[qanon|Q]]-adjacent research interests to forward-looking technological and civic themes.[1] He closed the announcement by saying he hoped to see familiar faces and, "as has happened at every GART so far, plenty of new ones," signing off with his customary "Stay Bright!"[1]
