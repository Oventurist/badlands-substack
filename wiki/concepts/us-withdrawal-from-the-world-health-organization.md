# US Withdrawal from the World Health Organization

On his first day of his second term in January 2025, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced the intent of the United States to withdraw from the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1]

## WHO response

In a January 21, 2025 statement, the WHO said it regretted the announcement, asserting that the organization "plays a crucial role in protecting the health and security of the world's people, including Americans, by addressing the root causes of disease, building stronger health systems, and detecting, preventing and responding to health emergencies, including disease outbreaks, often in dangerous places where others cannot go."[1] The statement noted that the United States was a founding member of the WHO in 1948 and had participated in shaping and governing the organization's work ever since, alongside 193 other member states, including through active participation in the World Health Assembly and Executive Board.[1] The WHO expressed hope that the United States would reconsider and said it looked forward to constructive dialogue to maintain the partnership.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] paired the WHO withdrawal with the simultaneous day-one withdrawal from the Paris Climate Accord, observing that "globalists" were "big mad," and noting that Trump was scheduled to speak virtually to the World Economic Forum on the Thursday of that week.[1]
