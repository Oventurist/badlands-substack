# Twelve Day War

The Twelve Day War is the name used in Badlands commentary for the June 2025 round of hostilities involving [[iran|Iran]] and [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Islamic-world response

During the conflict, Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] told Iranian President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] that "Saudi Arabia stands with its brothers in Iran and will spare no effort to support them," adding that "the entire Islamic world is united in backing Iran."[1] The [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] joined the collective condemnation of what commentary described as Israel's unprovoked aggression, while Pezeshkian and Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] expressed solidarity with the Muslim world and referred to their longtime rival [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] as "brothers."[1]

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] concluded that Israel had succeeded in uniting the entire Islamic world under a single banner of opposition to Israeli aggression — context he used to argue that later drone attacks on Gulf states were false flags intended to disrupt Saudi–Iranian rapprochement.[1]

## Course of the war and ceasefire

On the twelfth day of the war, Iranian state television announced that a ceasefire had begun, even as Israel warned the public of a new missile barrage launched from Iran, making the competing messages from the two governments impossible to immediately reconcile.[3] Iranian state television displayed the ceasefire announcement in an on-screen graphic without acknowledging the new barrage, which came after the deadline set by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in his earlier ceasefire announcement.[3] A series of Iranian barrages before the deadline killed three people and injured at least eight others, according to Israel's Magen David Adom rescue services, with one person believed trapped in rubble and a residential building in Israel's south sustaining heavy damage.[3]

Trump's announcement that Israel and Iran had agreed to a "complete and total ceasefire" came soon after Iran launched a limited missile attack on the American [[al-udeid-air-base|Al Udeid Air Base]] in Qatar, retaliating for the American bombing of its nuclear sites; Israel did not acknowledge the Trump ceasefire announcement.[3] Speaking by phone on Monday night, Trump said he expected the ceasefire to end all military hostilities between the two countries: "I think the ceasefire is unlimited. It's going to go forever."[3] Asked whether the war was completely over, he answered, "Yes. I don't believe they will ever be shooting at each other again."[3] Trump announced on Truth Social that the "12 Days War" was over, the label from which the conflict's Badlands name derives.[3]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands commentators read the conflict as primarily narrative rather than kinetic. [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] described it as "6th generation warfare," in which both sides conduct fourth-generation warfare by publicly admitting to privately giving each other advance notice of their third-generation bombings, which are carried out only to appease the warmongers in their respective countries who are conducting fifth-generation warfare on the public.[3] [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] rejected the earlier claim that Trump and Benjamin Netanyahu had spent two months deceiving Iran along with Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the UAE, calling it obviously false at the time and criticizing those who deferred to "Authoritative Sources."[3] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that one does not coordinate a kinetic war with an enemy but does coordinate a narrative war, contending that "Iran was never the target" and that the episode was a narrative translation of a shadow war "largely in the bag."[3] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the three days of direct American involvement were dramatic and rejected the post-ceasefire claim that Trump supporters had been wrong to question him, insisting the movement "has never been about blind loyalty."[3] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] summarized the outcome as "WW3 is cancelled. NeoCons RUGGED."[3]

## Aftermath and renewed Israeli planning

By December 2025, Israel was reported to be increasingly concerned that Iran was rebuilding and even expanding its ballistic missile production in the wake of the June war, and that Tehran was reconstituting a nuclear enrichment program heavily damaged by Israeli and American strikes during the conflict.[2] Those concerns underpinned reporting that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] would present fresh Iran attack plans to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Mar-a-Lago at the end of December 2025, with officials describing the missiles rather than the nuclear program as the more pressing worry.[2]
