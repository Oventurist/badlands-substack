# Twelve Day War

The Twelve Day War is the name used in Badlands commentary for the June 2025 round of hostilities involving [[iran|Iran]] and [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Islamic-world response

During the conflict, Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] told Iranian President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] that "Saudi Arabia stands with its brothers in Iran and will spare no effort to support them," adding that "the entire Islamic world is united in backing Iran."[1] The [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] joined the collective condemnation of what commentary described as Israel's unprovoked aggression, while Pezeshkian and Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] expressed solidarity with the Muslim world and referred to their longtime rival [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] as "brothers."[1]

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] concluded that Israel had succeeded in uniting the entire Islamic world under a single banner of opposition to Israeli aggression — context he used to argue that later drone attacks on Gulf states were false flags intended to disrupt Saudi–Iranian rapprochement.[1]
