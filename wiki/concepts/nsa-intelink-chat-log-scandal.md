# NSA Intelink Chat Log Scandal

The NSA Intelink chat log scandal was the February 2025 disclosure of chat logs showing intelligence community employees discussing sexual fetishes, polyamory, and transgender surgery on official government messaging systems during work hours.[1]

## The chat logs

Chat logs obtained by *City Journal* showed [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]], [[cia|CIA]], and [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] employees discussing polyamory, transgender surgeries, and explicit sexual topics on government time using the NSA's Intelink messaging platform.[1] Sources claimed the discussions had been legitimized as part of the agency's diversity, equity and inclusion initiatives within LGBTQ+ employee resource groups.[1] A review of the logs found employees discussing "male-to-female transgender surgery" and "hair removal, estrogen injections, and the experience of sexual pleasure post-castration."[1]

The employee who supplied the logs said he had "witnessed hundreds of sexually provocative discussions" taking place "mostly on taxpayer time."[1]

## Investigation and response

The NSA said it was investigating government employees discussing fetishes and transgenderism on official chat systems, and confirmed the authenticity of the chats, stating: "We're taking action against any and all employees who abused this system."[1] Reporting framed the episode as underscoring the importance of the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s efforts to root out misuse of taxpayer resources.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry joked that the story would supply the [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]] with content for the next eighteen to twenty-four months, and noted the irony that agencies accused of monitoring Americans' communications for decades had now had their own private and humiliating communications exposed to the world.[1] He urged readers to laugh at the agencies whenever they attempt to exert themselves against the American people, closing with the single word "Accelerate."[1]
