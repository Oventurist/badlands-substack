# Censorship Industrial Complex

The **Censorship Industrial Complex** (CIC) is the term used in the Badlands Media corpus for what [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] describes as "an entire industry whose sole purpose is to cripple independent media and snuff out all dissent."

## Origin of the concept in the corpus

In A Swan Song for the MSM (February 2024), DeLarme argues that the Western establishment fears nothing more than the success of a capable and honest decentralized media, because "a self-organizing industry of citizen journalists operating on principle would render the mainstream obsolete." The CIC is presented as the institutional response to that threat. The author flags it as a subject meriting its own dedicated essay rather than treating it exhaustively.

## Interpretation

The article reads the emergence of the CIC as a symptom of weakness rather than strength: "You could see the emergence of this CIC as an indicator of panic, a sign that the Western establishment isn't quite as invincible as previously believed." It concludes that the establishment is afraid not only of the decentralized media but of its audience, "because at the end of the day it's the audience that will decide who has won this war."

## Related mechanisms

The corpus links the CIC to concrete pressures on [[citizen-journalism|citizen journalists]]: demonetization, deplatforming, accusations of spreading foreign state disinformation, restrictions by large technology platforms, and the withdrawal of payment and fundraising services — the article cites GoFundMe's freezing of donations to [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]]. It also situates the CIC downstream of the [[countering-foreign-propaganda-and-disinformation-act-of-2016|Countering Foreign Propaganda and Disinformation Act of 2016]] and the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]], which the author regards as institutionalizing the "Russian disinformation" label as a tool against inconvenient reporting.
