# Cabal Symbolism

**Cabal symbolism** is the Q-movement doctrine that the alleged Satanic Deep State Cabal deliberately embeds its identity and intentions in symbols placed openly in corporate logos, entertainment, numerology and ritual, and that recognising those symbols will ultimately bring about its downfall.[1]

## The doctrine

As set out in [[q-post-4826|Q Post 4826]] and elaborated by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the Q Team is said to be aware that the Cabal hides symbolism in plain sight.[1] Carlson wrote that the motive is a mystery: the Cabal may simply be showing off — telling the public who they are, what they are and whom they worship, while implying nothing can be done to stop them — or they may be attempting to lessen their Karmic Retribution by announcing their actions in advance.[1] Either way, he argued, Q either searches for the symbolism directly or learns of it by monitoring Deep State communications.[1]

## Recurring symbols

Carlson catalogued a set of recurring motifs. The spiral or "pedo swirl" is identified by the [[fbi|FBI]] as denoting "little boy lover," and appears painted on the victim's back in season one of [[true-detective|*True Detective*]], alongside antlers echoing the well-known photograph of a Rothschild party at which a guest wore a deer's head.[1] The numeral 666 appears in the [[joe-30330|Joe 30330]] arithmetic and, Carlson said, in the logos of [[disney|Disney]] and [[google|Google]].[1] He read the [[nvidia|Nvidia]] logo as a pedo swirl in the shape of the all-seeing eye, and noted that *invidia* in Latin means envy, jealousy, spite, ill will, hatred, dislike and the evil eye.[1]

## Objections and rebuttals

Carlson anticipated the charge that he was seeing things that are not there, conceding the possibility while asserting that "the more I learn, the more I realize there really aren't a lot of pure coincidences, and that most coincidences are actually connections."[1] Against the argument that a controlling Cabal would require too many participants — an objection also raised about rigged elections, 9/11 and the Covid response — he replied that total control requires few people, with blackmail and pressure keeping the rest in line, offering as illustration the observation that narrative control over climate science requires only declining to fund dissenting scientists.[1] He summarised the point with the joke that "95% of scientists agree with the people who are funding them."[1]

He also argued that the persistence of Nvidia's logo in a culture that renames sports teams to avoid offence, in which Supreme Court justices decline to define a woman and psychologists no longer classify being transgender as a mental disorder, suggests the symbol is retained deliberately.[1]

## Call to action

Carlson closed with an exhortation to recognise the symbols and the evil around us, to be cautious but courageous, prepared but ready to celebrate.[1] He held that the Cabal's reign will not end until the people know the truth, that the media will not tell them, and that ordinary citizens have been commissioned to break the spell — concluding that "justice will be served."[1] See also [[decoding-symbolism|Decoding Symbolism]] and [[music-industry-initiation-rituals|music industry initiation rituals]].[1]
