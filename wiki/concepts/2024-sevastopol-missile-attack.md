# 2024 Sevastopol Missile Attack

The 2024 Sevastopol missile attack was a strike on the Crimean city of Sevastopol on June 23, 2024, which the [[russian-foreign-ministry|Russian Foreign Ministry]] attributed jointly to Ukraine and the United States.[1]

## The Russian account

Moscow said it had no doubt that Washington was involved, declaring that "the US' involvement in this horrible crime is beyond any doubt" and that "a response to this crime will certainly follow."[1] The ministry's statement said that "on June 23, the Kiev regime, with the support of the United States and its satellites, committed another heinous terrorist crime against the civilian population of Russia," and asserted that one of the most important Orthodox holidays — the day of the Holy Trinity — had been intentionally chosen for the attack.[1]

Four people were reported killed, including a two-year-old playing on the city beach and a nine-year-old girl.[1] Some 151 people sought medical help and 82 were hospitalized — 55 adults and 27 children — with many in serious condition.[1] According to the [[russian-ministry-of-defence|Russian Defense Ministry]], American ATACMS tactical missiles equipped with cluster warheads were used to inflict maximum damage, all flight paths were entered by US specialists using their own satellite reconnaissance data, and an American Global Hawk reconnaissance drone was on duty in the skies near Crimea.[1]

Russia's Investigative Committee opened a probe, and the Foreign Ministry said all those responsible for this and other attacks on Russian territory would be brought to justice, accusing Washington and its satellites of having created and fostered a neo-Nazi regime in Kiev and encouraged acts of international terrorism against Russian civilians.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry treated the strike as a threshold event, writing that it was not the first act of brutal terrorism committed by the U.S. [[state-department|State Department]] but the first to occur in clear view of the entire world.[1] He argued that the perception of American exceptionalism is dead overseas and has been replaced by contempt and rational fear, and that American audiences have been conditioned to accept the bombing of foreign populations as a form of moral victory — a doctrine from which he said [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s presidency was a brief reprieve.[1]

The commentary emphasized the religious dimension of the target, recalling the account [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] gave [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] that the Baptism of Russia by Vladimir the Great in 988 AD took place on a beach in Sevastopol, and framed the killing of Christian civilians there as an occasion for national self-examination.[1] It also referenced a contemporaneous terrorist attack in Dagestan that the author attributed to the [[cia|CIA]], while arguing that Putin bore a moral obligation to protect his people.[1]
