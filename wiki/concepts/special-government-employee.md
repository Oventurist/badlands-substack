# Special Government Employee Status

"Special government employee" (SGE) is a federal designation permitting an individual to serve in government while retaining outside employment. In "America for Sale — Part 2" the designation is presented as the legal device that enabled systematic conflicts of interest inside [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s [[state-department|State Department]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Huma Abedin

[[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] held SGE status, which allowed her to draw a $15,000-a-month consulting fee from [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]] over a seven-month period while simultaneously being paid to advise the Secretary of State.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The same status let her take on consulting work for the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], where she assessed the charity's programs to pave the way for Clinton's return after she left government in early 2013.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The author argues this was an obvious conflict of interest because Abedin was not an ordinary employee needing a second job but an aide privy to secret information at the highest levels.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Declan Kelly

[[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] received the same designation as Special Envoy to Northern Ireland, allowing him to retain roughly $2.4 million in consulting income from Dow Chemical.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) He took no State Department salary and hired his own staff — none of whom, the department later confirmed, appeared in its employment records, meaning their work escaped the oversight normally applied to State employees and their disclosure obligations were unclear.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Significance

The article treats SGE status as the connective tissue of the alleged pay-for-play system: it permitted the same individuals to serve simultaneously as government officials, private consultants and foundation staff, blurring the boundary between diplomacy and commerce.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)
