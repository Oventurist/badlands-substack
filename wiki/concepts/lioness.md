# Lioness

*Lioness* is a Paramount+ television series about a group of [[cia|CIA]] operatives infiltrating the Mexican drug cartel to rescue a kidnapped American politician.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) In "A New Hollywood," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats the show's second season — released less than three months before he wrote — as a marker of an ideological shift in [[hollywood|Hollywood]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

## The anti-woke scene

Carlson describes a scene in the third episode in which a father, a doctor, cooks breakfast while his daughters discuss how one girl's friend identifies. The daughters call their father transphobic and backwards for questioning the identification; he replies that a phobia is an irrational fear, and that disagreeing with someone or feeling one lacks enough information to simply accept a statement is not fear.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) His wife adds that everyone must have the right to disagree and challenge another's point of view, and says that as a CIA agent she fights to protect that right.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) Carlson writes that he "almost fell off the couch," calling it the most anti-woke thing he had seen come out of Hollywood in years, if ever — and prompting him to ask who owns [[paramount-global|Paramount]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

## Cartel themes

The second season's plot involves rescuing an American politician from a Mexican drug kingpin along with trafficked children. Carlson notes that the series depicts the ruthlessness of the cartel and its use of the carrot or the stick: politicians and government officials are offered large sums to protect the cartel, and if they refuse, their whole family will be killed — showing that corruption is "not as simple as corrupt people taking money to do corrupt things."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) He reads the series as preparation for his expectation that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] will take down the Mexican drug cartel.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)
