# Israeli Influencer Tour Program

The Israeli influencer tour program is a public-diplomacy initiative reported in July 2025 in which the [[israeli-ministry-of-foreign-affairs|Israeli Foreign Ministry]] funded a tour of Israel for U.S. social media influencers associated with the MAGA and America First conservative movements.[1]

## Design and objectives

The stated objective was to improve Israel's image among young American conservatives by encouraging influencers to share messaging aligned with Israeli government policy.[1] The tour was to bring 16 U.S.-based influencers under the age of 30, each with audiences ranging from hundreds of thousands to millions of followers. Officials framed the effort as a response to declining support: while older conservatives remain pro-Israel, "positive perspectives towards Israel are falling across all younger age groups."[1]

## Badlands assessment

Badlands commentator Chris Paul situated the program in a longer history of Israeli outreach to American cultural figures, noting that Israel previously ran comparable trips for Hollywood actors and that comedian Bobby Lee has discussed his own such trip publicly.[1] Paul characterized the initiative as giving propagandists "the star treatment in hopes they will further propagandize their followings," predicting participants would take money to act in pro-Zionist commercials "as themselves."[1]
