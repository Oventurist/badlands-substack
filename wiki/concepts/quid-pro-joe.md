# Quid Pro Joe

"Quid Pro Joe" is a phrase used in the Truth Community to describe allegations that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], as vice president, leveraged US aid to Ukraine for the benefit of his family's business interests.[1] It is also the title of a Badlands Media deep dive by [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] examined in the May 23, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## The 2015 Kyiv speech

The piece centers on a speech Biden delivered to the Ukrainian Rada during his December 9, 2015 visit to Ukraine, in which he said: "I said, we will not recognize any nation having a sphere of influence. Sovereign states have the right to make their own decisions and choose their own alliances. Period."[1] Badlands Media described the address as "one of the most monumental speeches of all time, because never has such hypocrisy been on display so brazenly."[1]

It was during that same visit that Biden threatened to withhold $1 billion in loan guarantees to Ukraine unless the government fired the prosecutor investigating [[burisma|Burisma]], the energy company then paying [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] large sums to serve as a "board member."[1]

## The Jake Sullivan connection

The article extends the story with a reported connection between Hunter Biden and [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]], noting reporting that Hunter Biden served on a national security advisory board with Sullivan for two years, raising further questions about the overlap of family business and national security policymaking.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for Badlands Media, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] called the piece "a great piece by Jon Herold that I believe is worthy of BREAKING designation in this community," noting that while the infamous clip of the "Pretender in Chief" bragging about the prosecutor's firing was widely known, "some new connections have been made from some old Emails. Damning ones."[1] He added that the "[[deep-state|Deep State]] always leaves a (digital) paper trail."[1]
