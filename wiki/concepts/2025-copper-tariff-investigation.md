# 2025 Copper Tariff Investigation

The 2025 copper tariff investigation was an inquiry President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] directed his Commerce secretary to conduct on Tuesday, February 25, 2025, into the need for possible tariffs on copper — the latest industry to be targeted under his sweeping trade agenda.[1]

## The directive

Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] said in a statement that "like our steel and aluminum industries, our great American copper industry has been decimated by global actors attacking our domestic production," and that "to build back our copper industry, I will investigate the imposition of possible tariffs."[1] He added that tariffs can help rebuild the American copper industry if necessary and strengthen national defense.[1]

## Rationale

[[white-house|White House]] officials argued that copper and copper alloys are critical for military hardware and national security preparedness.[1] They cited concerns about foreign dumping of copper to oversaturate the market and about reliance on foreign copper production as the reasons Trump directed Lutnick to conduct the investigation that could precede tariffs, with the stated goal of increasing domestic copper production.[1]
