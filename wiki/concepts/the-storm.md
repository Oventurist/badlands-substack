# The Storm

"The Storm" is a term of art within the [[qanon|Q]] and broader [[truth-community|Truth Community]] milieu referring to an anticipated mass exposure and takedown of the [[deep-state|Deep State]], culminating in the restoration of power to the American people.[1] Followers of the Q material hold that the word "storm" carries several layered meanings tied to this larger operation, rather than a single literal event.[1]

## Expected announcement

A widely held expectation among adherents is that the operation's public commencement would be marked by a broadcast statement — most often imagined as a post reading "My fellow Americans. The Storm is upon us."[1] Many assumed that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would be the one to publish it on Twitter, although the Q material itself is never specific about who would make the statement or when.[1] The associated slogan "nothing can stop what is coming" (NCSWC) is invoked to describe the perceived inevitability of the takedown operation.[1]

## The Pence remark

In May 2023, former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] told [[fox-news|Fox News]] anchor Bill Hemmer that "a storm is coming one week from today at the Southern Border," a warning about an anticipated surge of illegal border crossings as [[title-42|Title 42]] expired.[1] Badlands commentator [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the remark as an occasion to ask whether Pence — one of the most reviled of Trump's former close associates, owing to a widespread sense of betrayal over his inaction in 2020 — might have been alluding to the Q concept rather than merely to migration.[1] Deschamps noted the competing theory that Pence had been playing a scripted part in a larger operation, allowing the alleged 2020 fraud to proceed so that the "sleeping giant" of the American public would awaken, and judged that the awakening was working but incomplete, with the public still in the "you've got to get mad as hell" phase.[1]

## Timing expectations (2025)

Writing in June 2025, the [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] author judged that the Storm appeared to be almost here, citing the confrontation between the military and illegal entrants, the threat of a third world war, and the imminent collapse of what he called the debt-based circus comprising the economy.[2] He stated that he had always maintained the Storm would occur between the end of that summer and 4 July 2026, while cautioning that it could come sooner or later, because defeating an ancient enemy requires misdirection that also throws off those without direct access to the plan.[2]

He argued that many people will never accept [[q-operation|Q]]'s legitimacy until the Storm hits, and that this is acceptable because the goal is not to reach everyone but to reach the reachable.[2] He also urged that there is never a point at which learning the truth is a bad use of one's time, whether the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] arrives next week or next decade.[2]

## Preparedness framing (2024)

Writing in July 2024, the same author stated that he was certain the Storm would happen "one way or another," and argued that helping friends, family and acquaintances understand Q beforehand "could spell the difference between life and death when the storm comes."[3] His reasoning was psychological rather than logistical: when it appears that everything is over and there is no coming back, people tend to lose composure entirely and become liable to destructive acts against themselves and others.[3] He said he personally expected to feel no fear when the Storm hit, because he knew what it was and had been anticipating it, and that if he could he would transmit that understanding to everyone in order to spare them the existential horror of believing the world was ending.[3] He acknowledged that some readers would consider this an extreme position but declined to soften it.[3] The framing formed part of the rationale for his [[in-defense-of-q-series|In Defense of Q]] series and his conception of the [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldier]] role.[3]

## The anticipated "storm is upon us" message

Chapter 6 of Badlands' [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series argues that [[qanon|Q]] drop #44 of November 2, 2017, which pointed to an important message [[donald-trump|President Trump]] would deliver on a Friday before departing, is best read as a "[[future-proves-past|future proves past]]" drop whose referent had not yet occurred.[4] The author states his personal belief that the message in question will be the anticipated "My Fellow Americans, the storm is upon us" address.[4] He rejects each of the five Trump tweets from the following day as candidates, including the tweet about his account being disabled for eleven minutes and two tweets on stolen elections.[4]

## Anticipated trigger and timing (March 2025)

In "Q is Our Best Fren" (March 25, 2025), [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] speculated that the [[cabal]] retains at least one further system-wide attack of the kind he attributes to the Covid pandemic, and that the fabled Storm will occur either in response to such an attack or as a means of goading the cabal into launching it.[5] He presented this explicitly as speculation set aside from the essay's main argument.[5]

The essay assigns [[anons]] a preparatory role, describing their accumulated research as work that would one day prove crucial in helping mankind identify the parasites in its midst and weather the Storm.[5] It closes with the author's statement that he believes the Storm will arrive very soon, and with an appeal for readers to keep their nerves in check and become as "un-triggerable" as possible while the battle continues.[5]
