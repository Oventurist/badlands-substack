# 2025 Ukrainian Anti-Corruption Law Protests

The **2025 Ukrainian anti-corruption law protests** were nationwide demonstrations that erupted across [[ukraine|Ukraine]] on the night of Tuesday, July 22, 2025, after lawmakers voted to scuttle the independence of the country's two national anti-corruption bodies.[1]

## Background and the vote

Ukraine's parliament passed legislation stripping the independence of the two principal anti-corruption institutions — the [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine]] and the [[specialized-anti-corruption-prosecutors-office|Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office]] — bodies that had been created as conditions of Western financial and political support.[1]

## The protests

Hundreds of people demonstrated in cities including Kyiv, Lviv, Dnipro and Odesa, in what [[politico|Politico]] described as the largest protests to hit the country since Russia launched its full-scale invasion in February 2022.[1] Any kind of protest in Ukraine is curtailed under martial law and limited by the nighttime curfew, yet hundreds of Ukrainian civilians, war veterans and soldiers, as well as corruption watchdogs, gathered in central Kyiv and chanted "Veto! Veto! Veto!" facing the office of President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1]

A 21-year-old biology student identified only as Anastasia told Politico: "This is fucking outrageous. War has brought so many tragedies to us, but it was also a signal that our country is fighting for better. We can't let it backslide. We want no corruption and we want honest government. And I want to live in Ukraine."[1]

## Badlands commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that anti-corruption agencies are themselves "part of the Regime's [[color-revolution|Color Revolution]] playbook," inverting the conventional reading of the protests: rather than a grassroots defense of clean government, he framed the watchdog bodies as instruments of external control and Zelensky's move against them as an assertion of independence — "Our Boy Z is getting it done."[1] This reading gave the brief its headline theme, "The Corruption of Anti-Corruption."[1]
