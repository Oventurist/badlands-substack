# Trump Wuhan Origins Op-Ed (2023)

The **Trump Wuhan origins op-ed** is an opinion piece published in the Daily Mail in early March 2023 under the headline "DONALD J TRUMP: The world has finally woken to the truth about the Wuhan virus. Now it's time to hold China to account."[1]

## Content

In the piece, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] wrote that three years earlier he had declared that COVID-19 almost certainly came from the Chinese Wuhan lab, and that the world was finally admitting the truth.[1] He called the cover-up of the virus's origins "one of the greatest scandals in the history of the world," noted that millions of people around the planet had died from what he termed the China Virus, and said the cost of the outbreak and of the lying about its origins was incalculable — by some estimates in excess of $50 trillion.[1] He argued that it was time to hold China, and "the corrupt forces who have facilitated this colossal suppression of facts," accountable for the damage inflicted on humanity.[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] welcomed the op-ed, observing that it echoed much of what Trump had said about China and COVID-19 in his CPAC speech.[1] His favourite lines were: "There must now be a reckoning. The sinister censorship regimes in the United States and throughout the West must be dismantled and destroyed. This scandal is the best possible reminder of why we must have free speech."[1]

The op-ed appeared in the same news cycle as congressional findings from the [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]] alleging that [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] had prompted the drafting of the [[proximal-origin-paper|"Proximal Origin"]] paper to rule out the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]].[1]
