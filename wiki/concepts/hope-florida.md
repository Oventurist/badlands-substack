# Hope Florida

Hope Florida is a Florida state assistance program associated with First Lady [[casey-desantis|Casey DeSantis]], designed to connect residents in need with services and charitable resources.[1] In spring 2025 it became the center of a funding scandal that cast a shadow over the legacy of Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] and complicated his wife's prospective run to succeed him.[1]

## The $10 million routing

Florida lawmakers spent the spring of 2025 investigating why $10 million from a state Medicaid settlement was routed to a charity connected to Hope Florida.[1] That charity then transferred the same amount to two groups that financially backed a DeSantis-led campaign opposing the legalization of recreational marijuana.[1] Key lawmakers publicly suggested that the flow of money appeared illegal.[1]

## Defense and political fallout

The DeSantises fiercely defended the program and denied wrongdoing.[1] The governor characterized the criticism as "all political," and Casey DeSantis described Hope Florida as "a philosophy" that "shows we can help people in need."[1]

The most politically significant feature of the episode was the identity of the investigators: the inquiry was conducted by Republicans, not Democrats, which observers read as a striking indicator of DeSantis's diminished clout in Tallahassee following the collapse of his presidential ambitions.[1] The scandal also intersected with the succession fight, since a Casey DeSantis candidacy would run against [[byron-donalds|Byron Donalds]], the choice of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
