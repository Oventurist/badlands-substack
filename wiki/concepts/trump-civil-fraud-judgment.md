# Trump Civil Fraud Judgment

The Trump civil fraud judgment is the New York state civil penalty imposed on former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], his company, and top executives following a case brought by Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] and decided by Justice [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]].[1]

## The bond dispute

On Monday, March 18, 2024, Trump's lawyers told a New York appellate court that posting a bond covering the full $454 million judgment while he appealed was impossible, writing that "obtaining an appeal bond in the full amount" of the judgment "is not possible under the circumstances presented."[1] With interest, Trump owed $456.8 million; together with co-defendants including his company and senior executives, the total came to $467.3 million, and obtaining a bond would have required posting collateral worth $557 million.[1] The defendants said they had approached 30 surety companies through four separate brokers without success.[1] A state appeals court judge had ruled the previous month that the Republican presidential candidate must post a bond covering the full amount to pause enforcement of the judgment, with enforcement due to begin on March 25.[1]
