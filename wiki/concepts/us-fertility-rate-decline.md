# US Fertility Rate Decline

The US fertility rate decline refers to the long-running fall in American birth rates, which reached a record low in federal data released on April 25, 2024. The total fertility rate fell to 1.62 births per woman in 2023, a 2% decline from the prior year and the lowest rate recorded since the government began tracking the measure in the 1930s.[1]

## Reported causes

Reporting attributed the decline to a confluence of factors: American women having fewer children and having them later in life, expanded careers and greater access to contraception, and rising uncertainty among young people about their futures.[1] Young Americans were also spending more of their income on homeownership, student debt, and child care.[1] Women who delay childbearing may end up with fewer children than they otherwise would have, in part because of declining fertility with age.[1] Karen Benjamin Guzzo, director of the Carolina Population Center at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, said people are making "rather reasoned decisions about whether or not to have a child at all," and that more often than not the decision is "Yes, I'd like to have children, but not yet."[1]

## Badlands commentary: beyond the depopulation frame

Badlands Media writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argued that the standard dissident framing — sinister globalists ushering in a [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] dystopia for purposes of population control — is real but insufficient preparation for a harsher possibility.[1] He accepted that the adversary's vision of transhumanism is rooted in manifesting the ultimate security state, while warning that awareness of that agenda does nothing to answer how society would cope if population plummets in tandem with a genuine deterioration in the ability to produce offspring, noting that unplanned childlessness is already on the rise.[1]

In that scenario, Esler contended, winning the culture war becomes more important than ever, because the family unit as a symbol of generating and sustaining legacies of wisdom and achievement grows more precious as family formation becomes exceptional.[1] He warned that conditions would be far worse if [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxism]] rules the day and the cultural ideal of family is shattered just as having a family becomes difficult, framing this as the element of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s pronatalist ambitions worth considering apart from any [[new-world-order|New World Order]] agenda.[1] Rather than fearing engineering into a dystopia of grown babies and micromanaged conception, he posed the inverse question: what if humanity ends up needing such technology simply to produce enough people?[1] His conclusion was that loving, honouring, and understanding the power of the natural family must be locked into collective identity now, so that any future reliance on reproductive technology is anchored to that culture — otherwise, in a desperate need to imitate nature to survive, society will build technology born of tyranny rather than solutions inspired by the divine nature of family.[1]
