# Helium-3

Helium-3 is a light isotope of helium, abundant in the lunar regolith and prized as a potential fuel for fusion power. In Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s essay "Time to Revive the Spirit of JFK," the bountiful Helium-3 of the far side of the Moon is identified as a major game-changing goal of Chinese strategy, tied directly to China's drive for commercially viable fusion power — a subject Ehret says "merits an entire essay unto itself."[1]

The resource dimension of lunar development connects several threads of the article: the [[chang-e-6|Chang'e-6]] far-side sample return of June 2024, the Russia–China agreement ratified on June 16, 2024 to build a permanent lunar base with full industrialization and mining operations by 2040, and the eighth of the ten [[artemis-accords|Artemis Accords]] principles, which concerns the development of space resources within the lunar regolith.[1]
