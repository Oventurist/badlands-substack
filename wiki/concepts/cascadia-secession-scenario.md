# Cascadia Secession Scenario

The "Cascadia" secession scenario was one of the outcomes war-gamed by the [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]] (TIP) in its June 2020 election exercises, in which a defeated Biden campaign encourages the western states of California, Oregon and Washington to collectively become known as "Cascadia" and secede from the Union.[1]

## Associated proposals

In the same set of exercises, TIP participants forecast that the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico would gain statehood, that California would be split into five separate states to gain greater control of the Senate, and that the [[electoral-college|Electoral College]] would be eliminated — a goal the group's founders were described as adamant about.[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues that these scenarios, nominally predictions about the opposing side's behaviour, were in fact a preview of what TIP's own political allies intended to do had [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] won the 2020 election or refused to permit it to be stolen — alongside the parallel scenario of a nationwide corporate strike to force the counting of every mail-in ballot.[1]
