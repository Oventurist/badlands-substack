# DOGEQUEST

DOGEQUEST is a website that published the personal details of Tesla owners across the United States as part of what reporting described as a terror campaign against the company and its owner, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], the frontman of the Department of Government Efficiency.[1] The site was reportedly linked to far-left anarchists and listed home addresses, email addresses, and phone numbers for Tesla customers.[1]

## Mechanics of the campaign

The site offered to remove a Tesla customer's information, but only on the condition that the individual provide proof of having sold their Tesla vehicle — an explicit coercion mechanism converting the doxx into pressure to divest from the brand.[1] Beyond vehicle owners, the group behind the site also published the personal information of DOGE employees, and the address of Federal Bureau of Investigation Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] appeared on the site as well.[1]

## Context

The campaign coincided with a wave of hostility directed at [[tesla-inc|Tesla]] and at Musk personally following his prominent role in the second Trump administration's government-efficiency effort, in which vehicle ownership was treated by activists as a proxy for political affiliation.[1] The Badlands News Brief carried the item among its bonus stories on March 20, 2025.[1]

## Las Vegas arson and the emergence of the site

Dogequest surfaced publicly alongside a violent incident in Las Vegas in March 2025, in which several Tesla vehicles were deliberately set on fire at a service center in an attack involving Molotov cocktails and gunfire.[2] Las Vegas police described the episode as a "targeted attack" involving political graffiti.[2] Coverage at the time described Dogequest as a newly emerged website doxxing Tesla owners across the United States and encouraging them either to sell their cars or to remain on a public list.[2]

Authorities investigated the incidents, which prompted widespread social media discussion of possible motives, generally understood as political sentiment directed against Tesla's chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[2]
