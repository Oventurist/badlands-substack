# 2023 CSA poll on French immigration

A survey conducted by the CSA Institute for the French broadcaster CNEWS and published on Tuesday, December 12, 2023 found that an overwhelming majority of French citizens opposed further immigration and that two-thirds backed holding a referendum on the issue.[1]

## Findings

Eighty percent of respondents said France should ban further immigration, against just 19 percent in favor of new arrivals.[1] Opposition to mass immigration was the majority view in every age group: the most welcoming demographic was younger people, of whom 68 percent were nonetheless opposed compared with 31 percent in favor, while the age bracket most opposed was 50–64, at 88 percent — five percentage points above those aged 65 and over.[1]

Opposition was near-unanimous among right-leaning voters, with 100 percent of supporters of [[eric-zemmour|Éric Zemmour]]'s Reconquête and 98 percent of [[national-rally|National Rally]] voters against further immigration.[1] A total of 56 percent of left-wing voters also opposed more immigration, with slim majorities in favor found only among supporters of the Radical Party of the Left (55 percent), La France Insoumise (55 percent) and the Greens (52 percent).[1]

## Context

The survey was carried out on December 11–12, 2023, while the French government's immigration bill was being rejected by parliament.[1] Left-wing parliamentary factions argued the bill infringed on human rights, while right-wing politicians including [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]] characterized it as "pro-immigration."[1] The polling was reported by [[remix-news|ReMix News]] and carried in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief.[1]
