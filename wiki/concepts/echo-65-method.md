# Echo 65 Method

The **Echo 65 Method** is a proposed [[hand-counting|hand-counting]] procedure for tabulating paper ballots, published by TBTR Strategies on Substack.[1]

Election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] referred to it in February 2025 as "a game changing hand count method," while cautioning that its existence does not resolve the underlying timing problem: with the next national election twenty-one months out, the United States was in his view running desperately low on time to change the way it runs national elections.[1] The reference appeared in his argument that machine replacement, however desirable, is less achievable in the near term than exposing corrupted [[voter-registration|voter registration]].[1]
