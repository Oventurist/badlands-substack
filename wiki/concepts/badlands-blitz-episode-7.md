# Badlands Blitz Episode 7

**Badlands Blitz Episode 7** is the seventh instalment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly recap programme published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on its Substack. Dated February 21, 2026, the accompanying post carried 252 likes at the time of capture. Its subtitle is "Donald Trump, Deep State Burn Cards & the Return of Honor Culture."

## Format and purpose

The post restates the standing premise of the series: The Blitz is "intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." The episode is structured, as in previous weeks, around the programme's two recurring analytical categories — the week's "Narrative" deployments and its "Actual" deployments, a distinction discussed at length in [[narrative-and-actual-deployments|Narrative and Actual Deployments]].

The episode is co-hosted, with [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] joining the regular host as guest for the discussion.

## Segments

The post lists three principal segments.

The first concerns what the source calls "the Epstein Web's expansion and subversion," which the hosts say led to "the Prince Andrew inflection point." The framing treats the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein Narrative]] not merely as an expanding disclosure but as something simultaneously being subverted, with the fall of [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] presented as the point at which the story turned.

The second segment addresses [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s "Fake, but kind of Real Alien Disclosure Op," treated on this wiki at [[alien-disclosure-op|Alien Disclosure Op]]. The phrasing signals the programme's characteristic double reading: an operation staged for narrative effect that nonetheless carries genuine content.

The third segment covers what the hosts describe as "the major convergence in the offing when it comes to the US vs. [[iran|Iran]]," which they assess as "a whole lot of smoke, mirrors and a few juicy Actuals that signal a major realignment along Sovereign Alliance terms." The reference is to the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework used throughout the corpus, under which apparent great-power confrontations are read as cover for a realignment of state actors. The hosts explicitly mark the reading as their own estimation rather than established fact.

The subtitle's second element, "[[deep-state-burn-cards|Deep State Burn Cards]]," is not given its own segment in the written post but bears on the Epstein material. The subtitle's third element, "the Return of Honor Culture," is not elaborated in the written post and is developed in the video segment.

## Publication model

The post repeats the outlet's standing appeal, stating that all Badlands content — including The Blitz — will continue to be free, and that paid subscriptions are the only means by which the team can pay contributors for their time. It refers to a "step-up in said work for 2026" and notes that subscriptions may be cancelled at any time. It closes with the series' customary sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
