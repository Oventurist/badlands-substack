# Pride Night

**Pride Night** is a promotional event staged by professional sports franchises in which teams — and often individual players, through themed warmup jerseys — publicly display support for the LGBTQ community.[1] Badlands Media coverage described such events as the leading vehicle by which what it termed "the LGBTQ agenda has crept into every vector of professional sports," with the sporting establishment using Pride Nights and similar promotions to press players into involvement with the LGBTQ community.[1]

## Coercion critique

The central objection raised in Badlands coverage is that participating players are frequently given no genuine choice: in most cases they are said to comply out of fear of becoming a target of [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]].[1] The practice was placed alongside BLM advocacy, masking and vaccination campaigns as agendas heavily pushed through sports, with declining attendance and television ratings cited as evidence that American audiences were nearing the point of "cancelling the cancelers."[1]

## 2023 Flyers Pride Night

The best-known flashpoint came on the evening of January 17, 2023, before the [[philadelphia-flyers|Philadelphia Flyers]] hosted the Anaheim Ducks, when both teams were to wear jerseys supporting the LGBTQ community during pregame warmups.[1] Flyers defenseman [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]] declined to take part, citing his Russian Orthodox faith.[1] The Flyers organization issued a statement affirming its commitment to inclusivity and its pride in hosting the annual Pride Night, without mentioning Provorov's faith — an omission Badlands read as a refusal to defend a player's right to religious freedom and as an illustration of God and Christianity being driven out of public institutions.[1]
