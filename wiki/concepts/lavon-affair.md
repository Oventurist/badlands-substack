# Lavon Affair

The Lavon Affair was a botched Israeli false flag operation that took place in October 1954, in which a group of Egyptian Jews were recruited by Israeli military intelligence to plant bombs inside Egyptian, American, and British-owned targets in Alexandria and Cairo.[1] The cell was caught red-handed before the operation could be carried out.[1]

## Purpose

According to Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], the purpose of the operation was to instigate conflict between Egypt and the West while also motivating Britain to maintain a military presence in the Suez Canal.[1]

## Denial and eventual acknowledgment

Despite what DeLarme describes as indisputable evidence, [[israel|Israel]] denied its involvement for over 50 years, until 2005, when then-Israeli President Moshe Katsav honored the three surviving members with certificates of appreciation.[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

DeLarme cites the Lavon Affair — alongside the [[uss-liberty-incident|USS Liberty incident]], a false flag that killed 34 and injured 171 American soldiers — as evidence that Israel has historically had no qualms about committing deadly and subversive operations, and that when [[mossad|Mossad]], [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]], or the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] carry out such acts, they simply deny them while Western governments allow them to pass.[1] He invokes this pattern as a frame for the [[assassination-of-ismail-haniyeh|assassination of Ismail Haniyeh]] in Tehran.[1]

## Attribution to Isser Harel

Badlands Media identifies [[isser-harel|Isser Harel]] — founding director of [[mossad]] and [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]] and "Father of Israeli Intelligence" — as the man behind the Lavon Affair, describing it as a Mossad false flag operation in which Egyptian-Israeli agents placed bombs inside British, American and Egyptian targets in the hope that Muslim groups would be blamed.2 Israel finally admitted to the attack 51 years later, in 2005.2 The episode is cited as a precedent in the article's discussion of alleged Israeli foreknowledge of [[september-11-attacks|9/11]].2
