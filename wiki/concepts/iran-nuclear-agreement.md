# Iran Nuclear Agreement

The Iran Nuclear Agreement is the multilateral accord negotiated under the Obama administration to constrain Iran's nuclear programme, and a recurring fault line inside the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Role in the Tillerson dismissal

Erik Carlson's December 27, 2023 article "The Sabotage of Trump's Administration" identifies support for the agreement as the decisive cause of Secretary of State [[rex-tillerson|Rex Tillerson]]'s firing in April 2018.[1] Tillerson, a former ExxonMobil executive with close ties to Russia and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] through his energy background, backed the deal, while Trump considered it a horrible arrangement — a view shared by [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]], whom Trump regarded as loyal and moved from the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] to the [[state-department|State Department]] to replace him.[1]

Carlson uses the episode to argue that loyalty, rather than an unblemished record, was the trait Trump required of appointees: a president taking office with too few "beyond reproach" figures available in Washington had to choose whom he could work with, and could look past past conduct so long as an official pushed his agenda rather than working against him behind his back.[1]
