# Mass Arrests

"Mass arrests" is the expectation, widespread in Q-adjacent commentary, that a large number of corrupt politicians, executives, and entertainers will be taken into custody in a coordinated action once conditions permit.[1]

## Framing in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 Badlands Media essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that the arrest of [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]] was "the priming of the pump" — tying prominent figures to corruption while simultaneously "warming the general public up to what is to come: mass arrests."[1] Carlson maintained that the Combs case reaches ordinary Americans in a way the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] prosecutions never did, noting that 99% of Americans did not know who Epstein and Maxwell were when they were arrested while high-school students treat Combs as a meme.[1]

## Preconditions

The essay held that "maximum exposure and maximum damage" to the [[deep-state|Deep State]] Cabal was necessary both for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to win the 2024 election and to secure public consent — not a bare 52% or 60% majority, but "an overwhelming majority of Americans."[1] Carlson answered the common objection that no one on the Epstein list was ever arrested by arguing that it makes more sense to take everyone down "in one fell swoop, at a time when Trump is in complete control," adding that, as in finance, past performance does not guarantee future returns.[1]

## Venue and early signals

Carlson wrote that he believes the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]] is "where a lot of the Cabal will meet their demise," pointing to the recent arrest of New York City mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] and predicting many more.[1] He left open whether Adams's arrest showed that patriots now control the DOJ or whether Adams was arrested for turning on the Deep State.[1]

## Institutional countermeasures

The essay interpreted a bipartisan congressional push for a "continuity amendment" — nominally a response to the risk of a mass-casualty attack on [[congress|Congress]] — as an attempt to guarantee that arrested Deep State legislators could be swiftly replaced by like-minded appointees who would continue obstructing Trump.[1] See [[continuity-amendment|continuity amendment]].[1]
