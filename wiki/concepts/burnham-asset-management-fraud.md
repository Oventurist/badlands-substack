# Burnham Asset Management Fraud

The Burnham Asset Management fraud was a securities fraud scheme involving business partners of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] at the firm Burnham Asset Management, in which a fraudulent bond transaction of roughly $60 million was carried out.[1] When the case wrapped up two of Hunter Biden's business partners, he himself escaped scrutiny.[1]

## Newly reported ties

Bank records and drafts of corporate documents later showed that one of the shared bank accounts of the future first son had been used in the fraudulent bond transaction, indicating deeper ties to the scheme than previously known.[1] His name and signature also appeared in the investigation record.[1]

After his partners were arrested and charged, Biden moved immediately to distance himself from the firm, and he later told lawmakers during the impeachment inquiry into his father, former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], that the proposed work with Burnham never came to fruition.[1] Any personal liability for his association with the company at the center of the fraud scheme was subsequently wiped out when Joe Biden delivered a sweeping pardon for his son covering the period from 2014 to the present day.[1] See [[hunter-biden-pardon|the Hunter Biden pardon]] and [[biden-impeachment-inquiry|the Biden impeachment inquiry]].[1]
