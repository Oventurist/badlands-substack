# Tigers and Flies

"Tigers and Flies" is the popular name for the anti-corruption campaign launched by [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] after taking power in China, targeting both "tigers" — high-level oligarchic predators within the Party, state, and military — and "flies," the swarming petty corruptors beneath them.

## Treatment in the Badlands corpus

The January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief presents the campaign as the interpretive key to the purge of [[central-military-commission|Central Military Commission]] Vice Chairman [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]] and General [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]]. Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] describes the decade-spanning crusade as having "proceeded with unflinching methodical precision, mistaken by Western observers for stagnation when it was merely the patient accumulation of leverage" — leverage he argues was finally deployed against the [[peoples-liberation-army|PLA]]'s most senior and previously untouchable figures.

In this reading, Western coverage of the purge as evidence of instability inverts the actual meaning: the alarm expressed by outlets Burning Bright groups under the [[media-protectorate|Globalist media protectorate]] is itself the [[sovereign-signal|sovereign signal]] that what is occurring is consolidation rather than fragility, and the expulsion of [[deep-state|Deep State]] penetration from Chinese institutions consistent with the corpus's [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framing. The claims are partisan interpretation of contested reporting and should be treated accordingly.
