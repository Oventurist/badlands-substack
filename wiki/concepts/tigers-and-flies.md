"Tigers and flies" is the slogan associated with the anti-corruption campaign of Chinese leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], and the title of the second entry in the Badlands Media China series, which precedes "The Two Chinas," "Empire's Enigma" and "Mirror, Mirror."[1] In Badlands Media's usage the phrase denotes the paired targets of Xi's purges: the "tigers," meaning senior and entrenched figures of the Chinese oligarchy of legacy families and bloodlines, and the "flies," meaning the lower-level administrative and government insiders who sustain the same system of corruption.[1]

The December 2022 essay "Mirror, Mirror" treated the slogan as evidence of a populist rise mirroring those of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], arguing that Xi built his campaign for power on a promise to go to war with the tigers and flies of an entrenched, embedded enemy that would otherwise control the country from on high.[1] The same essay characterised the resulting campaign as going further than the comparable efforts of Trump or Putin: rather than confining himself to narrative and legal pressure, Xi was said to have rooted out, arrested and imprisoned members of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] oligarchy, sometimes through military deployments and mass-scale actions, with millions officially punished for corruption alongside thousands of accused government and administrative insiders.[1]

Badlands Media conceded that it is difficult or impossible to verify whether these arrests were justified, but argued that the net effect of the campaign was the purging of legacy families from a Party that had previously served the Chinese people up to the American oligarchy represented by [[apple|Apple]], [[google|Google]] and [[amazon|Amazon]], effectively farming out a slave class to the collectivist West.[1] Within the wider [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework, the tigers-and-flies campaign is read as the Chinese instance of a shared "war on the oligarchy" quadrant common to the leaders the network identifies as sovereigns.[1]

## Place in the Badlands China series

"The Two Chinas" (November 10, 2022) identifies itself as the third entry in the ongoing [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] China series, which began with "Taiwan Tipping" and continued with "Tigers and Flies," and states that it is best read following those features.[2] The subsequent installment in the sequence is "Empire's Enigma," billed as a deeper dive into the record of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s first two terms and the stated intentions of his third.[2]
