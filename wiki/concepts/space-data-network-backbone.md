# Space Data Network Backbone

The Space Data Network Backbone is a US military satellite communications program announced in May 2026, when the [[united-states-space-force|US Space Force]] awarded [[spacex|SpaceX]] a $2.29 billion contract to build it.[1] The system is intended to securely move military data around the world at high speeds, forming a critical "backbone" for future US military operations.[1]

## Architecture

According to Space Force, the program will use a constellation of low-Earth orbit satellites linked together with optical, or laser-based, communications technology.[1] The design is intended to allow US military forces to rapidly and securely share targeting information, sensor data, battlefield communications, and other intelligence across the globe, even in contested environments where conventional links may be degraded or denied.[1]

## Schedule and related programs

The contract requires SpaceX to deliver a fully operational prototype by the end of 2027.[1] The effort is to work alongside the [[space-development-agency|Space Development Agency]]'s Transport Layer program, another military satellite network already under development, with both systems expected to operate together as part of a larger War Department communications architecture.[1] Space Force officials characterized the project as part of a broader push to accelerate military space acquisitions through rapid prototyping and commercial partnerships, while expanding the industrial base supporting US national security space programs.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] presented the award as the latest confirmation of an alliance between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] visible primarily in contracts and integrations rather than rhetoric, arguing that backbone infrastructure of this kind ties Musk's decentralization and autonomy themes to the reorientation of American defense priorities under Trump's [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] doctrine.[1]
