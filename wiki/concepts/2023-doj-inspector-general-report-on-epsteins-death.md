# 2023 Justice Department Inspector General Report on Jeffrey Epstein's Death

The 2023 Justice Department Inspector General report on the death of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] was released on Tuesday, June 27, 2023, and documented the institutional failures at the [[metropolitan-correctional-center|Metropolitan Correctional Center]] in the month preceding Epstein's death.[1] The report, produced by the [[department-of-justice-office-of-inspector-general|Justice Department Office of the Inspector General]], concluded that Epstein died by suicide while in the custody of the [[federal-bureau-of-prisons|Bureau of Prisons]] and stated that foul play "was not possible."[1]

## Findings

Epstein died by hanging on August 10, 2019, at the Manhattan jail while awaiting trial on federal sex trafficking charges.[1] According to the report, the noose was fashioned from "a sheet or a shirt," and a subsequent search of the cell recovered extra bedsheets, another mattress and a noose.[1] Officer Michael Thomas, who was charged criminally in connection with the case, told investigators he said "Breathe, Epstein, Breathe" upon finding Epstein unresponsive, and on seeing him dangling from the bed remarked, "We're going to be in a lot of trouble."[1] The report was covered by [[abc-news|ABC News]], which emphasised the Inspector General's conclusion that the death was in fact a suicide.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the report's certainty as its least credible feature, calling the assertion that foul play was "not possible" stunning.[1] She listed the failures the report itself acknowledged — non-functioning cameras known to be broken, the removal of Epstein's cellmate, guards skipping required checks, the movement of the body, and an abundance of linens in the cell of an inmate on suicide watch — as evidence of incompetence so extreme it defied belief.[1] That investigators could spend months on the case without determining whether the ligature was a shirt or a sheet, she argued, rendered the report insulting to the public, and she summarised the reaction with the slogan that Epstein did not kill himself even as authorities went to great lengths to convince the public he did.[1]
