# Badlands Blitz Episode 13

**Badlands Blitz Episode 13**, subtitled "Is the Real Fake War Ending ... Or Only Just Beginning?", is the thirteenth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video recap published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on its Substack newsletter. The announcement post is dated April 18, 2026 and carried 238 likes at the time of the source capture. As in previous installments, the post opens by addressing readers as "Badlanders" and restates the program's purpose as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."

## Hosts and format

The episode is co-hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], the same pairing that presented Episode 11. The announcement states that the two hosts "spend the entire hour dishing on" a single subject rather than working through a series of micro topics, continuing the show's drift, visible since Episode 10, away from strict week-in-review segmentation toward extended single-theme discussion.

## Subject matter

The stated focus of the episode is what the post calls President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s "Friday morning blitzkrieg of the Info War battlespace." The announcement argues that this intervention "remains relevant today, even if the global mindscape and all players have yet to accept the fact that the ending to the 'war' with [[iran|Iran]] was decided before it was fought."

That formulation extends a line of argument the program had been developing across the preceding episodes: that the Iran conflict is best understood as an engagement in the [[information-war|Info War]] whose outcome was prearranged, and that public perception of it lags behind the settled reality. The subtitle's question — whether the "real fake war" is ending or only beginning — frames the episode's ambivalence about whether the resolution the hosts describe has actually taken hold in the wider [[narrative-warfare|narrative]] environment.

## Publication model

The post repeats the standing Badlands funding notice: all Badlands content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free, and the team is able to pay itself only through readers who choose to support the newsletter directly. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription, cancellable at any time, in support of what the post calls the "step-up in said work for 2026." The announcement closes with the network's customary sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
