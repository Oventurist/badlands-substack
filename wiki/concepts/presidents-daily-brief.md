# President's Daily Brief

The President's Daily Brief (PDB) is the top-secret intelligence product prepared for the President of the United States and, by custom, made available to former presidents.[1]

## Revocation of Biden's access (March 2025)

In March 2025, Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] posted that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had officially revoked former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s access to the President's Daily Brief.[1] Biden — who had barred Trump from receiving classified briefings after the January 6, 2021 attack on the U.S. Capitol by a pro-Trump mob — would no longer receive the top-secret intelligence, under a directive Trump first ordered begun in February.[1]

ABC News framed the move as the latest in Trump's retribution campaign against what he claims was "weaponization" of the justice and intelligence segments of the federal government under Biden and Democrats.[1]

## Related clearance revocations

Trump also ordered revoked the security clearances of former Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]], former national security adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]], former Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]], New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]], Manhattan District Attorney Alvin Bragg, and attorneys Mark Zaid, Norman Eisen and Andrew Weissman.[1]
