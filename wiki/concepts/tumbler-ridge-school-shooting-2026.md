# Tumbler Ridge School Shooting (2026)

The Tumbler Ridge school shooting was a mass shooting in Canada on Wednesday, February 11, 2026, in which eight people were killed and dozens injured.[1]

## The attack

According to reporting aggregated by Badlands Media, an 18-year-old male-to-female transgender individual identified as Jesse Strang killed eight people, including five students between the ages of 12 and 13.[1] The shooter also killed an 11-year-old stepbrother.[1] A 12-year-old girl was left in critical condition and fighting for her life in hospital.[1]

Reports varied as to the suspect's age, given as either 17 or 18.[1] CNN reported that the shooter had transitioned about six years earlier, placing the age of transition at roughly 12.[1] The suspect was known to Canadian police, who according to CNN had visited the residence several times over mental health concerns.[1] Authorities said the investigation was ongoing.[1]

## Coverage framing

Badlands Media presented the incident under the headline "Another Trans Shooter Kills 8, Injures Dozens in Canada," situating it within a recurring pattern the outlet identifies between transgender-identifying assailants and mass shootings — a framing contested in mainstream coverage.[1] The item was carried as an appended segment following a bonus item on Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]]'s congressional testimony.[1]
