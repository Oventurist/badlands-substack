# WPATH Standards of Care

The Standards of Care are the clinical guidelines issued by the [[wpath|World Professional Association for Transgender Health]] governing the treatment of gender dysphoria, including in adolescents.[1]

## Composition of the drafting committee

Reporting by [[reduxx|Reduxx]] in March 2024, summarized in the Badlands News Brief, found that [[laura-a-jacobs|Laura A. Jacobs]] — a New York psychotherapist and trans-identified male who has publicly described himself as a "kinky, BDSM sexworker" and who authored an anthology chapter promoting "age play" and "genital torture" — served as an influential member of the committee that established the most recent edition of the Standards of Care.[1] Jacobs also influenced the development of the guidelines' treatment of gender dysphoria in adolescents.[1]

## Reception

The disclosure fed broader criticism, articulated by Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], that gender medicine standards are being written by figures embedded in ideological currents he identified with [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxism]] and [[transhumanism|transhumanism]], rather than by strictly clinical criteria.[1]
