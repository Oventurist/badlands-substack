# Remigration

**Remigration** refers to the return of migrant populations to their countries of origin, whether voluntarily or through state policy. The term is used in nationalist and populist political discourse to describe a deliberate reversal of prior immigration flows, and appears in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as a marker of domestic political realignment.

## Use in Badlands commentary

In Badlands Blitz Episode 26, published July 25, 2026, hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] are described as taking "a circuitous route from American remigration to peace in the Middle East" in the course of discussing the [[global-golden-age|Global Golden Age]]. The source thereby treats American remigration as one starting point among several for a broader argument about global transition, linking immigration reversal at home to conflict resolution abroad.

The announcement gives no policy detail, figures or named officials, so the specific measures the hosts have in mind are not recoverable from the archived text. The concept is politically contested, and its use here is interpretive.
