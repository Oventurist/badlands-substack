# Conscious Creation

**Conscious creation** is the fourth stage set out in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], subtitled "Manifesting in the Quantum Field."[1]

## Mechanics claimed

The article argues that while manifestation is widely discussed, few understand its mechanics, and proposes that reality bends to a person's energetic state.[1] It invokes [[rupert-sheldrake|Rupert Sheldrake]]'s [[morphic-resonance|morphic resonance]] for the proposition that thoughts and emotions do not remain in the head but ripple into a collective field, likening the effect to dropping a stone in a pond, where one person's coherence strengthens a pattern that others can unconsciously tune into.[1] The essay characterises this as "physics meeting consciousness" rather than wishful thinking, and states that the universe responds not to wishes but to one's energetic state.[1]

A vignette describes sitting in sunlight and holding a felt vision of slow mornings, purposeful work and an unburdened heart for five minutes, letting it "root in your bones" — creation framed as a seed planted in the present rather than a distant dream.[1]

## Practices

The essay recommends shifting from problem-solving to vision-building (asking "What does a life of ease look like?" instead of "I need less stress"); training the nervous system for expansion by spending five minutes daily feeling the emotions of the desired reality as if already present; practising deep presence, such as focusing solely on taste and texture while eating; and engaging in "future self" embodiment by dressing, walking or speaking as the version of oneself already living the truth.[1]

The stated key takeaway is that creation is the bridge and that reality mirrors the state one embodies.[1] The theme is developed further in the essay's closing section on stepping into the quantum field, where [[joe-dispenza|Joe Dispenza]] is quoted from *Becoming Supernatural* saying that "when we break free from conditioned reality, we step into the quantum field where all possibilities exist."[1]
