# 2023 Xi Jinping State Visit to Russia

In March 2023, Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] made a three-day state visit to Russia, a trip described by experts and former U.S. officials as offering a symbolic shot in the arm to his increasingly isolated counterpart [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and as highlighting Xi's determination to push back against American power in the world.[1]

## The Moscow talks

Calling each other "dear friends," the two leaders held informal talks in Moscow for almost four and a half hours on the Monday of the visit, according to the Russian state news agency Tass, with talks between their full delegations following on Tuesday.[1] In televised remarks after greeting Xi inside the Kremlin, Putin said he had "carefully studied" China's recent proposal for ending the [[russia-ukraine-war|war in Ukraine]], a conflict in which Beijing had sought to portray itself as a potential peacemaker.[1]

After the Tuesday meeting there appeared to be no sign of a diplomatic breakthrough over Ukraine.[1] Putin praised Xi's peace plan and blamed Kyiv and the West for rejecting it, while Xi barely mentioned the conflict, saying only that China held an "impartial position" on it.[1] The visit underscored how Xi, unlike his predecessors, wished to position himself as a world-shaping leader and his country as a counterweight to America's long-standing global dominance.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] criticized "either-or" rhetoric within a community that prides itself on heterodox thought, arguing that theories often treated as incongruent are in fact complementary.[1] He held that the "peacemaker" moves by Xi and Putin on the world stage lent more credence to the [[devolution|Devolution]]/Continuity of Government theory, writing that [[donald-trump|Trump]] "never left" and had been "silent-running while necessary objectives are forwarded" — objectives he cautioned should not be assumed to be exclusively domestic.[1]
