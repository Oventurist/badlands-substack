# Storm Shadow Missile

The Storm Shadow is a British-made long-range air-launched cruise missile that became a focal point of Western escalation debates during the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]].[1]

## First use against Russian territory

In November 2024, [[ukraine|Ukraine]] launched British-made Storm Shadow missiles at military targets inside [[russia|Russia]] for the first time, according to four people familiar with the matter cited by the Financial Times. A Western official briefed on the strike said at least one Russian military target had been fired at by multiple missiles.[1]

The Storm Shadow attack followed Ukraine's first use of U.S. long-range ATACMS missiles on Russian soil the previous day, after authorization from President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]. Kyiv had pleaded for months with Western allies to be permitted to use their long-range weapons against Russian territory as Ukrainian troops struggled to hold ground they had occupied in the [[kursk-incursion|Kursk region]].[1]

A Russian pro-war military blog on Telegram posted photographs of what it said were fragments of a Storm Shadow missile, including engravings identifying it as such, which it said had landed near Marino, a village in the Kursk region. Alexei Smirnov, the local governor, said Russian air defences had shot down two Ukrainian missiles.[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands Media commentary characterized the escalation as a "Ukrainian Missile Crisis" analogous to the Cuban Missile Crisis — a second attempt by entrenched national-security interests to provoke war with Russia during the tenure of a sovereign-minded U.S. president — and argued that the narrative escalation should be read as story rather than inevitability.[1]
