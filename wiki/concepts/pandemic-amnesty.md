# Pandemic Amnesty

"Pandemic Amnesty" is the title of an October 2022 *[[the-atlantic|Atlantic]]* opinion essay calling for forgiveness of the policy failures of the COVID-19 pandemic response. In the Badlands corpus it is invoked as evidence of the magazine's complicity in what commentators describe as pandemic-era crimes.

## Invocation in the measles fiction controversy (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief quotes Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] recalling the essay during the controversy over *The Atlantic*'s fictional measles story: "Remember when *The Atlantic* wanted a 'Pandemic Amnesty' for their crimes against humanity during Covid? Hilarious." The essay is also referenced elsewhere in the corpus as a "desperate plea for amnesty" by one of the pandemic era's "central narrative deployers," alongside the magazine's later coverage of the prosecutions of [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|NIAID]] scientists.
