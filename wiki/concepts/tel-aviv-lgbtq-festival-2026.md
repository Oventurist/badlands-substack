# Tel Aviv LGBTQ Festival (2026)

In April 2026 [[israel|Israel]] promoted a major LGBTQ festival in Tel Aviv, expected to draw large international crowds and reinforce the city's reputation as a global hub for LGBTQ culture and tourism.[1]

## Controversy

The event came amid ongoing war and international scrutiny of Israel.[1] Critics said the festival projects a positive image during conflict, while supporters argued it reflects normal cultural life continuing despite geopolitical tensions.[1] Badlands carried the item under the heading "The Holy Land Bets on Gay Tourism," among its bonus items for the day.[1]
