# Snow's Island

Snow's Island was the swamp stronghold used as a base of operations by [[francis-marion|Francis Marion]] during the southern campaign of the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]] — a three-mile stretch of dry land buried deep in the marshes at the confluence of the Pee Dee River and various creeks.[1]

## Why the swamps were empty

Conventional wisdom of the period held that malaria and other diseases were spawned by the festering morasses of lowland swamps such as those dominating much of eastern South Carolina.[1] For that reason the marshlands were uninhabited, which made them the perfect base for a vigilante force.[1]

## The stronghold

The article casts Snow's Island as Marion's Sherwood Forest, with bulwarks of fallen trees, ramparts of moss-covered rock and a moat of endless muck, guarded by legends of sleepless rifles and cunning swamp men who moved like the Cherokee of Appalachia — a fortress as impregnable as any castle in Europe.[1] Marion maintained other similar refuges scattered across the region and was constantly on the move, keeping his hideouts mythic and the true strength of his numbers a mystery; for all his band's other deficiencies, its operational security is described as tight as a drum.[1] A related refuge was the swamp at Jack's Creek, considered impenetrable, into which Marion withdrew his men overnight to escape [[banastre-tarleton|Banastre Tarleton]]'s pursuit.[1]
