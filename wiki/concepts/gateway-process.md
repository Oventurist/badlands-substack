# Gateway Process

The **Gateway Process** is cited in Badlands Media's *Structures of Control* series as an example of classified research into expanded states of consciousness that was, in the author's telling, "buried under layers of classified secrecy" rather than shared with the public.[1]

## Role in the Structures of Control argument

Part 3 of the series lists the Gateway Process alongside [[mk-ultra|MKUltra]] under the heading "Suppression of Technology," arguing that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] co-opts and silences leading scientists and innovators so that advances in energy, healthcare and quantum technologies are either delayed or weaponized.[1] Where MKUltra is described as having exploited neuroscience for mind control rather than human liberation, the Gateway Process is presented as the mirror case: genuine inquiry into expanded awareness that was classified precisely because, in the author's framing, it demonstrated capacities the control system had an interest in concealing.[1]

The essay places this suppression within a broader mechanism it calls technological suppression and weaponization, in which breakthroughs in energy, medicine and cognitive enhancement are systematically withheld or repurposed to maintain centralized control — the same logic the series applies to [[nikola-tesla|Nikola Tesla]]'s wireless energy work and to the [[project-stargate|Stargate Project]]'s remote viewing research.[1]

## Interpretation

For the series, the significance of the Gateway Process is less documentary than symbolic: it functions as evidence that the state has already investigated, and quietly validated, the proposition that consciousness is an active shaper of reality rather than a passive observer.[1] That proposition underpins the essay's argument that the [[white-hats|White Hats]]' strategy of graduated disclosure is itself a form of [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth Generation Warfare]], since each declassified document is treated as a "quantum event" shifting the collective trajectory.[1]
