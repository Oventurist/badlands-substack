# One Big Beautiful Bill Act

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act is the omnibus legislative package enacted during President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term that, among many other provisions, delivered a large multi-year funding increase for federal immigration enforcement.

In the January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief the Act appears as the baseline against which a subsequent appropriations fight was measured: Senate Democrats moved to block a six-bill spending package over [[department-of-homeland-security|DHS]] provisions adding a further $10 billion for [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] "on top of prior allocations from last year's One Big Beautiful Bill Act." That dispute produced the [[january-2026-government-shutdown-standoff|January 2026 government shutdown standoff]], with affected agencies including DHS, the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], Labor, Transportation, and HUD.
