# How to Blow Up a Pipeline

***How to Blow Up a Pipeline*** is a 2021 book by Swedish Lund University professor [[andreas-malm|Andreas Malm]] and the 2022 Hollywood film adapted from it.[1]

In the film, several young, anxiety-ridden eco-activists conspire to blow up pipelines in Texas in order to "save civilization," set against ignorant and tyrannical government authorities; the narrative closes by depicting their example inspiring further acts of eco-terror.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues that the film is not entertainment but part of a larger recruitment drive designed to radicalize young nihilistic products of what he calls a morally bankrupt educational system into becoming eco-terrorists.[1] The intended effect, in his reading, is the creation of "an armada of useful idiots" charged with hatred of humanity and willing to kill and be killed in defense of Gaia, whose fragile balance industrial civilization and the sovereign nation state are said to have upset.[1]

Ehret notes that the underlying book is mandatory reading in hundreds of universities across Europe and North America and therefore cannot be dismissed as fringe thinking, and that it belongs to a body of literature he considers a precondition for tearing down civilization from both "bottom up" and "top down" directions.[1] The work has been praised by [[naomi-klein|Naomi Klein]] and its thesis was echoed publicly by [[david-suzuki|David Suzuki]].[1]
