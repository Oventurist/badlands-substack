# 2025 Trump Regime Change Remarks on Iran

The 2025 Trump regime change remarks on Iran were a social media post by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Sunday, 22 June 2025, raising the prospect of regime change in Iran following US strikes on key Iranian sites over the weekend.[1]

## The post

"It's not politically correct to use the term, 'Regime Change,'" Trump wrote on his own social media platform, "but if the current Iranian Regime is unable to MAKE IRAN GREAT AGAIN, why wouldn't there be a Regime change??? MIGA!!!"[1] The post came after senior officials in his administration, including Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and Defence Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], had stressed that the United States was not working to overthrow Iran's government; Hegseth told reporters at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] that "this mission was not and has not been about regime change," calling it "a precision operation" aimed at Iran's nuclear programme.[1] At the same time, administration officials warned Tehran against retaliation.[1]

## Commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] objected that no one was asking whether the Iranian people themselves wanted regime change, as though it were an American decision, and mocked the assumption that ordinary viewers could know what life inside Iran is like when "most people don't know what life is like for the people next door."[1] He argued that "America First and 'regime change' are polar opposite concepts," and criticised online triumphalism as the mere enjoyment of televised spectacle.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] offered a contrary reading, arguing that between the "baited story beats" Trump was signalling that there would be no war, that the deals were already done, and that the dramatic public narrative was not representative of the endgame but another step toward it, as Trump provoked a sifting that pushes the collective mind toward peace.[1] He attributed the method to three mechanisms: timeline manipulation, sovereign (alliance) signal, and done deals.[1] The strikes themselves are covered at [[operation-midnight-hammer|Operation Midnight Hammer]].[1]
