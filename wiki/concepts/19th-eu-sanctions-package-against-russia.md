# 19th EU Sanctions Package Against Russia

The 19th sanctions package is the nineteenth set of restrictive measures imposed by the [[european-union|European Union]] on [[russia|Russia]] since the start of its full-scale invasion of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] more than three years earlier.[1]

## Adoption

A weeks-long stalemate holding up the package ended on the night of Wednesday, October 22, 2025, when [[slovakia|Slovakia]] lifted its veto, a step confirmed by the Danish presidency of the Council of the EU.[1]

## Contents

The bulk of the package focuses on sapping the Kremlin's war chest by imposing restrictions on energy traders and financial institutions, many of them located in third countries.[1] Companies assisting the Russian war effort are targeted, and the measures add 117 new tankers assessed to be part of the shadow fleet that ships Russian fossil fuels in violation of the oil price cap.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the Slovak and Hungarian obstruction that preceded adoption — associated with prime ministers [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] and [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]] — as evidence that those two states are "splintering the Globalist Hegemon from within," with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] helping them do it.[1] In that framing the fractures running through the EU and NATO blocs are described as seismic rifts rather than incidental friction, and the on-again, off-again character of Ukrainian peace talks is presented as deliberate rather than chaotic.[1]
