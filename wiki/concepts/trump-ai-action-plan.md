# Trump AI Action Plan

The AI Action Plan is a policy framework issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], accompanied by related executive orders most of which date from 2025, that frames American artificial-intelligence development in national-security terms while simultaneously advancing the nuclear and permitting reforms needed to make AI-scale power generation possible.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the plan as the American half of a matched pair with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s parallel national push in [[china|China]], characterizing both as "Warp Speed buildouts" in which frontier models serve as the justification layer for a much larger energy and computing mesh.[1] In his reading, the plan is a component of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] program for an age of abundance built on a [[nuclear-renaissance|nuclear renaissance]], gargantuan data centers and the on-shoring of critical manufacturing.[1]

## Rollout and America First framing (July 2025)

On Wednesday, July 23, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] unveiled the [[white-house|White House]]'s plan to help the United States lead the global race to develop artificial intelligence, and signed three executive orders facilitating exports of U.S. technologies and boosting the build-out of data centers.[2] The measures advanced the agenda of executives and investors seeking to profit from an AI investment boom, and the plan was delivered at an event co-hosted by the Hill and Valley Forum — an influential interest group founded by tech leaders — and the Silicon Valley podcast "All-In," co-hosted by White House AI and crypto czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]].[2] Coverage described the rollout as the moment Silicon Valley's risky bet on Trump began paying dividends.[2]

Trump paired the announcement with a demand that technology companies put America first: "For too long much of our tech industry pursued a radical globalism that left millions of Americans feeling distrustful and betrayed… Many of our largest tech companies have reaped the blessings of American freedom while building factories in China, hiring workers in India, and stashing profits in Ireland, all the while dismissing and even censoring their fellow citizens right here at home. Under President Trump, those days are over. We need US technology companies to be all-in for America."[2]
