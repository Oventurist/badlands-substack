# Psychopath Proof

***Psychopath Proof*** is a book by the pseudonymous author [[rogue-hypnotist|Rogue Hypnotist]], recommended in the opening installment of the Badlands Media series [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] for readers seeking a thorough treatment of psychopathy.[1] The author of that essay describes it as "the single most insightful and useful book I've ever read on the subject."[1]

## Context of the recommendation

The recommendation appears in a section arguing that learning to profile psychopaths and psychopathic behaviors is among the most important things ordinary people could do to defend themselves, and that the masses cannot defend against psychopathy they are unable to recognise.[1] The essay states that it has only enough space to detail a few essential facts on the subject and directs interested readers to book-length treatments, of which *Psychopath Proof* is presented as the foremost example alongside [[anthony-napoleon|Anthony Napoleon]]'s [[shadow-men|*Shadow Men*]] and [[andrew-lobaczewski|Andrew Lobaczewski]]'s [[political-ponerology|*Political Ponerology*]].[1]

The book is framed within the series' larger thesis that accurate knowledge about the nature of psychopathy is a master key both to defeating the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and to preventing its re-emergence, and that such knowledge constitutes ammunition in [[information-warfare|information warfare]].[1] It is also connected to the wiki's treatment of [[psychopathy-and-parasitism|psychopathy and parasitism]].[1]
