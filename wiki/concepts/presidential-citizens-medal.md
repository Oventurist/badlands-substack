# Presidential Citizens Medal

The Presidential Citizens Medal is a US civilian honor awarded by the president for "exemplary deeds of service for their country or fellow Americans."[1]

## January 2025 awards

In early January 2025, President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] announced he would award the medal to former Rep. [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] (R-Wyo.) and nineteen others, as reported by the Washington Post.[1] The award came while Cheney — vice chair and a key member of the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]] — braced for a potential [[fbi|FBI]] probe after House Republicans demanded the Bureau investigate her for allegedly tampering with "at least one" witness before the panel.[1]

President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nominee for FBI director, [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], had not signaled whether he was open to investigating the former congresswoman.[1] Cheney maintained that she should not go to jail for alleged wrongdoing during her tenure on the committee, while a Rasmussen Reports poll found that a majority of likely voters — 57 percent — supported an FBI investigation of her.[1]
