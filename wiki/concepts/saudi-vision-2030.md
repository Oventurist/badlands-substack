# Saudi Vision 2030

**Saudi Vision 2030** is the ambitious economic and infrastructure modernization program associated with Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], intended to diversify the kingdom away from oil dependence.[1]

## Connection to Syrian infrastructure plans

Badlands commentary on the [[fall-of-the-assad-regime|fall of the Assad regime]] noted that Mohammed bin Salman and the Chinese had approached [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] over the preceding years about resurrecting his [[five-seas-vision|Five Seas Vision]] rail proposal, implementing it as part of China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]] and tying it into MBS's Vision 2030 plans.[1] In this reading, the destruction of the Syrian state served to foreclose a regional prosperity corridor that would have linked Gulf, Levantine and Eurasian economies outside Western control — one of the motives advanced for why the regime was toppled.[1]

## US Partnership (May 2025)

At the [[saudi-us-investment-forum|Saudi-US Investment Forum]] in Riyadh on May 13, 2025, Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] said the United States was among the largest partners of the Vision 2030 reform agenda, and described joint investment as one of the most important pillars of the economic relationship between the two countries.[2] He confirmed that Saudi Arabia had signed deals with the United States worth more than $300 billion, said the Kingdom was examining $600 billion in investment opportunities with hopes of reaching $1 trillion, and noted that the United States accounted for roughly 40 percent of the global investments of the [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]].[2]
