# Coeur d'Alene Firefighter Ambush

The **Coeur d'Alene firefighter ambush** was an attack on Sunday, June 29, 2025, in which a sniper shot and killed two firefighters and wounded a third while they were battling a wildfire near Coeur d'Alene, Idaho.[1]

## Events

A SWAT team located a deceased man and a nearby firearm on Canfield Mountain hours after the shooting, the Kootenai County Sheriff's Office confirmed late Sunday.[1] A shelter-in-place order was lifted, though the wildfire remained active, and roughly 300 law enforcement officers responded to the scene.[1]

The International Association of Fire Fighters confirmed that the two victims killed were firefighters, stating that "while responding to a fire earlier today in Coeur d'Alene, Idaho, IAFF members were ambushed in a heinous act of violence. Two of our brothers were killed by a sniper, and a third brother remains in surgery," and asking for prayers for the victims, their families, and law enforcement.[1]
