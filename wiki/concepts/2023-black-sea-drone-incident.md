# 2023 Black Sea Drone Incident

The 2023 Black Sea drone incident was a mid-air encounter on Tuesday, March 14, 2023, in which a United States [[mq-9-reaper|MQ-9 Reaper]] drone crashed into the Black Sea following an intercept involving Russian aircraft, prompting the first direct call between the American and Russian defense chiefs in months.[1]

## Deconfliction call

In an afternoon press briefing the following day, Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] confirmed that he had spoken by telephone with his Russian counterpart, Defense Minister [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]], about the incident — the first such call since October.[1] "I just got off the phone with my Russian counterpart, Minister Shoigu," Austin said, framing the exchange as an act of deconfliction and adding that "it's important that great powers be models of transparency and communication, and the United States will continue to fly and to operate wherever international law allows."[1] Both sides sought to underscore that they were not looking for a fight.[1]

## Wreckage recovery and airspace restrictions

Both Russia and the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] said they were seeking to recover the drone's wreckage, but it reportedly went down in a very deep part of the sea, making recovery unlikely.[1] The [[kremlin|Kremlin]] said it had restricted additional airspace over the Black Sea while conducting its 'special operation' in Ukraine, setting the stage for the possibility of further dangerous intercept encounters.[1]

## Commentary

Austin also repeated the Biden administration's position that President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] alone is ultimately making the decisions on the execution of the war to push Russian forces out, a line ZeroHedge noted was being repeated with increasing frequency.[1] Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the narratives generated by such events matter more than the underlying facts, since the media, military and political record is nearly impossible to parse, and concluded that regardless of fault the episode represented "yet more escalation between the US and Russia" for ordinary observers.[1]
