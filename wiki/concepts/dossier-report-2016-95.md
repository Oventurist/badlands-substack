# Dossier Report 2016/95

Report 2016/95, also called Report 95, is one of the individual [[steele-dossier|Steele or "Company" reports]] produced by [[orbis-business-intelligence|Orbis Business Intelligence]] and was entered as Government Exhibit 109 at the 2022 trial of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]].[1]

## Significance at trial

The report attributes information to "Source E," who is identified as [[sergei-millian|Sergei Millian]], making it the documentary hinge of the four counts concerning Danchenko's statements about Millian.[1] Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] raised it on the opening day as one of the unresolved motions in limine, telling the court there was some consensus but continuing disagreement between the parties over how much of the report should be admitted.[1]

The heart of the dispute was whether Danchenko had attributed all of the information in the report to Source E or only a portion of it.[1] The defence argued that the interviewing agents had not been clear enough in their questioning — a theme it had also advanced in its motion to dismiss, contending that Danchenko gave truthful answers to ambiguous questions.[1] [[anthony-trenga|Judge Trenga]] said he would have to rule on the report within the context of the testimony of Danchenko's handling agent.[1]
