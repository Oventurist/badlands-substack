# AUKUS Undersea Drone Program

The AUKUS undersea drone program is a joint United States, United Kingdom and Australia effort to develop new uncrewed undersea vehicles under the [[aukus|AUKUS]] defense pact, announced in June 2026 by War Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] in Singapore.[1]

## Announcement and scope

Hegseth said the project was the first "Pillar Two" initiative focused on uncrewed undersea vehicles, describing it as a move to deliver "highly adaptable multi-mission UUV payloads" in order to maintain maritime advantage.[1] AUKUS, formed in 2021, strengthens Indo-Pacific defense cooperation, covering nuclear-powered submarine development under Pillar One and advanced technologies including artificial intelligence, cyber and quantum systems under Pillar Two.[1]

## Context

The announcement followed earlier concerns over a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] review of the pact, which were later eased when President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] reaffirmed US support.[1] Hegseth said the United States remained committed to AUKUS and was moving quickly to expand submarine capabilities in the Pacific.[1]
