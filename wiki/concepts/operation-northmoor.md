# Operation Northmoor

**Operation Northmoor** was the British Ministry of Defence investigation, running from 2014 to 2019, into allegations of unlawful killings by UK forces — principally the [[special-air-service|Special Air Service]] — during operations in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]]. It was the main military probe covering the 2010–2013 period in which night raids by special forces units allegedly produced repeated killings of unarmed men and detainees, with weapons planted on bodies afterward.

The investigation was closed without prosecutions. A later public inquiry concluded that it had been shut down "inappropriately and prematurely" despite serious unresolved concerns, and heard testimony that the director of UK Special Forces had made a "conscious decision" to conceal potential wrongdoing. Those findings, together with a 2022 [[bbc|BBC]] *Panorama* investigation identifying 54 suspicious deaths caused by one SAS unit in a single six-month Helmand tour, revived the matter and fed into the human rights investigation that by April 2026 was pursuing 242 special forces personnel and triggering mass resignations from 22 SAS.
