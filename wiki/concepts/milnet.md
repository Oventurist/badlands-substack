# MILNET

MILNET is a government-owned, contractor-operated satellite communications constellation in low Earth orbit being developed for the [[united-states-space-force|U.S. Space Force]] in partnership with [[spacex|SpaceX]].[1]

## Architecture and operations

The constellation was described in June 2025 by Delta 8 Commander Col. Jeff Weisler as consisting of more than 480 satellites operated by SpaceX, transitioning into Space Force operations via Space Systems Command and Delta 8.[1] A Delta 8 mission director is to oversee operations so that they align with wartime tempo.[1] The satellites use SpaceX's [[starshield|Starshield]] terminals, which feature enhanced encryption and interoperability with the commercial Starlink network.[1]

MILNET is intended to support the military's emerging "hybrid mesh network," which integrates commercial and Defense Department satellites into a single communications fabric.[1] Delta 8, based at Schriever Space Force Base in Colorado, manages satellite communications for senior U.S. leadership and for global military forces.[1]
