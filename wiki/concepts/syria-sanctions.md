# Syria Sanctions

**Syria sanctions** refer to the unilateral and international economic restrictions imposed on [[syria|Syria]], which became a central diplomatic question following the change of leadership in Damascus in late 2024.[1]

## Riyadh meeting, January 2025

On Sunday, January 12, 2025, Saudi Foreign Minister Prince [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] called for the lifting of sanctions on Syria, saying the curbs hindered the country's rebuilding and development.[1] He made the call at a press briefing following high-level meetings on Syria held in Riyadh.[1]

"We stressed the importance of lifting unilateral and international sanctions imposed on Syria, as their continuation hinders the aspirations of the Syrian people to achieve development and reconstruction," Prince Faisal said.[1] The Riyadh meeting was attended by representatives from Syria, Jordan, Lebanon and Türkiye, as well as the United Kingdom and the United States, with officials from the Gulf Cooperation Council, the European Union and the United Nations also taking part.[1]

The Saudi foreign minister said the meeting sought to provide support and advice to the new leadership in a way that respects their independence and recognizes that the country's future belongs to the Syrian people.[1] He also stressed the importance of continuing humanitarian and economic support, achieving stability and reconstruction, and preparing an appropriate environment for the return of Syrian refugees, adding that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] was keen to continue coordinating with and standing by Syria and its people.[1]
