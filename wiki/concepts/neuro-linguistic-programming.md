# Neuro-Linguistic Programming

Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) is a set of techniques involving semantic rules and pattern-recognition analysis, promoted by some commentators as a method for evaluating arguments without domain expertise. It is the first of four "epistemological blindspots" identified by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] in his November 15, 2025 essay "Don't Be Symbol-Minded."[1]

Ehret summarized the thesis advanced by [[peter-duke|Peter Duke]] on [[hrvoje-moric|Hrvoje Moric]]'s program: that NLP techniques are a substitute for needing to understand any particular topic debated within the information war, because NLP allows the mind to appreciate structures of logical argumentation and therefore to see holes or fallacies without carrying out serious study of the subject.[1] Ehret rejected this, treating NLP-managed thinking — like AI-managed thinking — as "incommensurably inferior" to human creative reason.[1]

For critical analysis of NLP's origins, Ehret pointed readers to [[cynthia-chung|Cynthia Chung]]'s essay "Huxley's Ultimate Revolution: The Battle for Your Mind and the Relativity of Madness," describing NLP as having been developed as a substitute for human thinking in the "bowels of the Mithraic hub known as the [[esalen-institute|Esalen Institute]]" in Big Sur, California.[1]

Ehret linked the acceptance of NLP to a wider epistemological failure: the habit of evaluating reality solely through pattern recognition or pure bottom-up empiricism, without appreciation for the higher realm of causality discussed by Plato, Cicero, St. Augustine, Leibniz and [[lyndon-larouche|Lyndon LaRouche]].[1] That habit, he argued, makes the conclusion that everything is controlled and wired to enslave the people seem natural.[1]

## NLP in fifth-generation warfare (February 2025)

A February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]] presented Neuro-Linguistic Programming in a positive light, describing it as a technique woven into [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] strategies to activate what the author called the mind-body-soul complex.[2] NLP was defined there as a framework exploring the connection between neurological processes, language and behavior, said to help individuals reframe limiting beliefs, enhance self-awareness and build resilience against manipulation.[2]

Techniques named include anchoring positive states, modeling empowering behaviors and refining internal dialogue, which the essay argued foster coherence between thought, emotion and intuition and thereby shield individuals from external manipulation.[2] Combined with energetic practices such as guided meditation, sound healing and community-based engagement, these methods were said to elevate collective vibrational states and counter the fragmentation tactics of [[fourth-generation-warfare|Fourth-Generation Warfare]].[2]
