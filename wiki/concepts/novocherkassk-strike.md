# Novocherkassk Strike

The Novocherkassk strike was a Ukrainian air attack on 26 December 2023 that damaged a Russian landing ship moored in the Black Sea off Crimea.[1] Both Kyiv and Moscow confirmed the attack, which came at a moment of low Ukrainian morale following battlefield disappointments and mounting doubts about the future of Western aid.[1]

## The attack

Russia's Defense Ministry said Ukrainian warplanes fired guided missiles at the landing ship *Novocherkassk*, which was moored at a base in the Crimean city of Feodosia.[1] Video circulating on Russian and Ukrainian social media showed an explosion in the port.[1] Ukrainian authorities claimed the ship had been destroyed outright and said it was likely carrying ammunition and possibly drones.[1] The 360-foot vessel was rated to carry ten tanks and 225 sailors.[1]

Ukrainian air force spokesman Yurii Ihnat told the US-backed Radio Liberty that "we saw how powerful the blast and detonation were. It's extremely difficult for a ship to survive something like that."[1] Ukrainian air force commander Mykola Oleschuk posted a mocking message on Telegram declaring that "the Russian fleet has become smaller," and urged Russians to leave Crimea "while it's not too late."[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the strike as a rare and isolated success rather than a turning point, comparing it to a struggling child's lone goal in an otherwise winless soccer season: "Ukraine scored a goal, but they're far from winning a game, let alone a season."[1]
