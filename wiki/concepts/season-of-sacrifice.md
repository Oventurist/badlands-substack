# Season of Sacrifice

The Season of Sacrifice is the name given by occult researcher [[mark-passio|Mark Passio]] to a forty-day window running from approximately March 19th to May 1st, during which, he argues, a disproportionate number of mass-casualty events and suspected [[false-flag-operation|false flags]] have historically occurred.[1] The concept supplies the organizing thesis of the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]].[1]

## Astrotheological derivation

The season is derived from the twelve signs of the zodiac.[1] The window opens when the sign of Pisces transitions to Aries at approximately the time of the spring equinox, and closes at the midpoint of the sign of Taurus on May 1st, marked by the pagan holiday of Walpurgisnacht.[1] In [[astrotheology|astrotheological]] terms, this combines a point on the Minor Zodiacal Cross — formed by the four seasonal beginnings of March 21st, June 21st, September 21st and December 21st — with a point on the Grand or Galactic Cross, formed by the midpoints of the seasons: May 1st (Walpurgisnacht), August 1st (Lammas), October 31st (Samhain) and February 2nd (Candlemas).[1] Each of these midpoints was traditionally marked by a Sabbat, a time for performing sacred rites; Walpurgisnacht, also called May Day or Beltane, is associated with fertility, the emergence of life, and an offering of sacrifice to ensure a bountiful harvest.[1]

## Catalogued events

The article offers a non-exhaustive list of events falling within the window: the Battles of Lexington and Concord (4/19/1775); the Baltimore Riots of 4/19/1861, in which thirteen died; the sinking of the Titanic on 4/14/1912, with 1,512 dead; the assassination of Martin Luther King Jr. on 4/4/1968; the Waco massacre of 4/19/1993; the [[oklahoma-city-bombing|Oklahoma City bombing]] of 4/19/1995; the Port Arthur massacre in Australia on 4/28/1996; the Heaven's Gate cult "suicides" of 3/26/1997; the Westside Middle School massacre in Arkansas on 3/24/1998; the Columbine High School shootings of 4/20/1999; the "Shock and Awe" opening of the Iraq War on 3/20/2003; the Virginia Tech massacre of 4/16/2007; the Moscow subway bombings of 4/29/2010; the BP oil rig disaster of 4/19/2010; the beginning of the U.S. invasion of Libya on 4/19/2011; and the Boston bombings of 4/15/2013.[1]

## Caveats

The article is explicit that false flags can occur throughout the year and that not every such event must fall on a day of occult significance.[1] It also cautions that the forty-day cycle may or may not be filled with events on days of significance in any given year, and that operations important to what it calls the cabal may not appear significant on the surface — false flags, by contrast, "act like candle lights, standing out as more recognizable."[1]
