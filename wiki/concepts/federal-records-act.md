# Federal Records Act

The Federal Records Act is the United States statute requiring federal officials to preserve communications related to government business.[1]

Agencies typically implement the law with respect to encrypted messaging platforms by mandating that messages be forwarded to official systems or otherwise archived.[1]

## 2025 Signal litigation

The Act was the central legal hook in the March 2025 lawsuit brought by [[american-oversight|American Oversight]] against Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] and other Trump administration officials over their use of [[signal|Signal]] to discuss strikes on Houthi targets in Yemen.[1] The watchdog argued that messages set to auto-delete on the commercial app amounted to unlawful destruction of federal records, and asked the court to recover deleted messages and prevent further destruction.[1] [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] was named in the suit in his capacity as acting archivist, the office with custodial responsibility for federal records, as well as secretary of state.[1]
