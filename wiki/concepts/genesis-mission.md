# Genesis Mission

The Genesis Mission is a U.S. [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] initiative with which the department's [[fusion-science-and-technology-roadmap|Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap]] is explicitly aligned.[1] The roadmap, implemented through the department's newly created [[office-of-fusion|Office of Fusion]], situates fusion commercialization within the Genesis Mission's broader framing of accelerated national science and technology development, including high-performance computing and [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]].[1]

## Executive order launch (November 2025)

The Genesis Mission was launched by a presidential action signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on November 24, 2025, titled "Launching the Genesis Mission."[2] The order framed the initiative as a dedicated, coordinated national effort to unleash "a new age of AI-accelerated innovation and discovery," explicitly comparing its urgency and ambition to the Manhattan Project, which the order noted was instrumental to victory in World War II and a critical basis for the founding of the [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] and its national laboratories.[2]

Under the order, the Genesis Mission is to build an integrated AI platform harnessing Federal scientific datasets — described as the world's largest such collection, developed over decades of Federal investment — in order to train scientific foundation models and create AI agents that test hypotheses, automate research workflows, and accelerate breakthroughs.[2] The order situated the effort within a broader administration push for global technology dominance, including prior executive orders and America's AI Action Plan.[2]
