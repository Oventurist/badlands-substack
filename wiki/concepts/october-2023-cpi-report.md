# October 2023 CPI Report

The **October 2023 Consumer Price Index report**, released in mid-November 2023, showed inflation missing expectations across the board, with core inflation falling to its lowest level in more than two years.[1]

## Background

The release followed two consecutive months of hotter-than-expected prints, which had been driven by surging energy prices and by methodology changes in the healthcare component of the index.[1] Consensus expected the October headline figure to slow materially from the prior month — from 3.7% to 3.3% — while core inflation was expected to remain unchanged at 4.1%.[1]

## Results

Headline CPI came in at 3.2% year over year, below the 3.3% expected.[1] On a month-over-month basis headline CPI printed unchanged at 0.0%, below the 0.1% consensus and sharply below the prior month's 0.4% increase.[1] Core CPI followed a similar pattern: the October month-over-month print was 0.2%, below the 0.3% consensus and down from September's 0.3% increase, while the annual rate fell from 4.1% to 4.0%, missing expectations for an unchanged reading and marking the lowest core print since September 2021.[1]

The report was covered by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and circulated in the Badlands News Brief as a bonus item.[1]
