# Digital Asset Market Clarity Act

The Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, commonly called the CLARITY Act, is broad United States legislation intended to establish comprehensive rules for the cryptocurrency sector.[1]

## House passage

The [[us-house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] approved the bill by a vote of 294–134 in July 2025, as part of a wider legislative push that also sent the [[genius-act|GENIUS Act]] to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for signature and advanced anti-CBDC legislation.[1] The CLARITY Act still awaited action in the Senate, where lawmakers were drafting their own version.[1]

Senate Banking Committee Chairman [[tim-scott|Tim Scott]] said he hoped to complete the Senate process by September 30 and described the House bill as a "strong template" for final legislation.[1] The votes were reported as a turning point for a crypto industry that had struggled for years with unclear rules and hostile regulatory oversight, and reflected growing bipartisan support, with a large bloc of Democrats joining Republicans.[1]
