# North American Union

The North American Union is a proposed continental integration scheme calling for the merging of the United States, Canada, and Mexico under shared governance structures. Badlands Media contributor Matthew Ehret traces the modern formulation to the 2005 Task Force report co-drafted by Privy Councillors John Manley and Thomas Axworthy, who also co-founded the Ottawa think tank [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]].[1]

That report called for the adoption of a single currency modelled on the Euro, a harmonized regulatory regime across the continent, and an unelected class of managers ruling across borders.[1] Ehret argues these objectives were never abandoned but merely delayed, and that they are resurfacing with new force in the era of [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]'s premiership.[1]

## Contemporary revival

The same figures who speak of climate resiliency also champion militarized continental defence, according to Ehret.[1] The so-called [[golden-dome|"Golden Dome"]] missile shield, supported by both Carney and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], envisions integrated military control across North America.[1] Ehret pairs this with the revival of the "North American Technate" concept — long dismissed as fringe — as it is re-branded for the 21st century, and with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s "X" project aiming to replace traditional banking with a global digital currency platform tied to behavior rather than gold or productive output.[1]

Ehret frames the deeper danger not as Canada becoming a republic or joining the USA, but as the possibility of the USA itself falling back into the British Empire, noting that Trump has expressed openness to [[king-charles-iii|King Charles III]]' offer to rejoin the British Commonwealth as an associate member.[1]
