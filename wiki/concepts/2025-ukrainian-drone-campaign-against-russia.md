# 2025 Ukrainian Drone Campaign Against Russia

In late May 2025, [[ukraine|Ukraine]] sharply escalated long-range drone attacks inside [[russia|Russia]], an offensive that coincided with a visit by President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] to the Kursk Region and with renewed peace negotiations in Istanbul.[1]

## Kursk drone swarm and Putin's helicopter

Yury Dashkin, a commander of a Russian air defense division, told the channel Russia 1 in an interview aired Sunday that the helicopter carrying Putin had found itself "in the epicenter of an operation to repel a massive drone attack by the enemy" in Kursk Region.[1] Putin had traveled there on Tuesday for the first time since the region was fully cleared of Ukrainian forces in April, meeting Governor [[aleksandr-khinshtein|Aleksandr Khinshtein]], heads of local municipalities, and volunteers assisting those affected by the repulsed incursion.[1] Dashkin said Kiev launched an "unprecedented" UAV assault while the president was present, with Russian air defenses destroying 46 incoming fixed-wing drones.[1]

## Scale of the raids

According to the Russian military, 776 drones and 12 missiles were intercepted over Russian territory between Tuesday and Friday morning, while 12 drones struck their targets; the Russian Defense Ministry reported a further 104 UAVs intercepted overnight into Saturday.[1] A parallel account put the Tuesday-to-Friday interception figure at at least 764 drones, with hundreds more destroyed over the weekend.[1] Russia responded with a major strike on a drone and missile production plant in Kiev on Saturday and additional reported strikes on Ukrainian military facilities on Sunday.[1]

## Lavrov's accusation

Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]], during a Q&A session in Moscow, attributed the drone escalation to "some European nations led by the UK, France, Germany, and the EU leadership," accusing them of backing "Ukrainian Nazis" and declaring: "We are convinced that they bear their share of responsibility for these crimes," adding that Moscow would seek to "put an end to this policy."[1] He said the goal of Kiev's backers was to disrupt the peace talks renewed in Istanbul after Putin offered to resume negotiations without preconditions.[1]

## Trump's reaction and commentary

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] claimed that Moscow had targeted Ukraine "for no reason whatsoever" after the Russian retaliatory missile strikes, a characterization reported alongside the record of the preceding week of Ukrainian drone raids.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the Kursk episode as a masked assassination attempt on Putin encouraged by European elites, argued that it constituted an act of war that Putin would downplay to avoid an expanded conflict with Europe, and urged the United States to walk away from the war.[1]
