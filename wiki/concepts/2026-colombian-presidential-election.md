# 2026 Colombian Presidential Election

The 2026 Colombian presidential election was held on June 21, 2026. According to the July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief, it was won by [[abelardo-de-la-espriella|Abelardo de la Espriella]]; incumbent President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] challenged the outcome, claiming the real winner was [[ivan-cepeda|Iván Cepeda]] and alleging the result was manipulated using technology from California.

The [[shield-of-the-americas|Shield of the Americas]] coalition — the United States and twelve regional partners — issued a July 10, 2026 joint statement declaring that the sovereign will of citizens expressed at the ballot box is the sole basis of legitimate government, rejecting any effort to delegitimize the mandate, discredit electoral authorities, or obstruct Colombia's institutional transition process, and urging respect for the certified results.

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked the election in arguing that discrediting electoral authorities "with basis" requires public hearings, and connected the dispute to her broader claims about the "public-private elections industry" enabling fake elections.
