# Nuclear Fusion

Nuclear fusion is the energy-generating process pursued as a source of clean, effectively limitless power. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as a component of a broader technological and geopolitical transition rather than as a purely scientific subject.

In the April 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-07c|Badlands Brief]], the contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argues that the concurrent, full-throttle push by the United States and [[china|China]] into fusion at scale "isn't coincidence, but competitive convergence," with both powers racing toward net-energy breakthroughs that would "render the old fossil fuel and intermittent green paradigms obsolete overnight." He links the race directly to the energy demands of rapidly scaling [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] systems, which he describes as devouring the power they need "to scale into something that rewrites human potential," and suggests that this potential is why both fusion and AI are "being demonized by all the usual suspects."

The commentary situates fusion within the same framework as the emerging [[war-economy|war economy]]: an accelerated, state-backed technological transition made politically possible by geopolitical competition and executive action.
