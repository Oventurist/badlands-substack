# NGO Migrant Transport Network

The **NGO migrant transport network** refers to what ZeroHedge described as a shadowy network of taxpayer-funded non-governmental organizations bussing illegal immigrants from the U.S. southern border deeper into the interior of the United States.[1]

## Reporting

Ahead of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s February 29, 2024 visit to the southern border — with former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] visiting simultaneously to slam the radicals in the [[white-house|White House]] for sparking what he called the worst migrant invasion the nation has ever seen — a new graphic released by Bloomberg showed the locations where illegal immigrants had ended up after being bussed through the nation via this network.[1] The latest figure from [[customs-and-border-protection|US Customs and Border Protection]] at that time showed 7.3 million illegal immigrants had entered the country under Biden's first term.[1]

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] posted on X on the Tuesday night before the border visits: "I am ringing the alarm bell, because the flood of illegals is crushing the country!"[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argued that the reveal of a shadowy NGO network driving the invasion deeper into America amounted to open warfare in the context of the [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth generation battlefield]], noting that 5GW is normally qualified by the fact that it cannot easily be identified as warfare — leading him to wonder how much of the increasing overtness was helping normies see the plan to destroy America.[1]

Esler linked the reporting to [[toronto-protocols|The Toronto Protocols]], a document he described as a map of the warfare strategy for implementing a New World Order, depicted in two meetings in 1967 and 1985 by a group calling themselves the 6.6.6.[1] In his opinion, [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] exposing the shadowy network of NGOs transporting immigrants deeper into the US, alongside Bloomberg mapping the depth of the invasion, created a massive red pill for the affair being run as a warfare operation.[1]

He quoted the document's passage calling for agents infiltrating the Ministries of Intergovernmental Affairs and Immigration of nation-states to change their statutes so as to "open the doors of immigration to Western countries with a large mass of immigrants entering across their frontiers (immigration that we have indeed caused by having taken care to break out here and there, new localised conflicts)."[1] The passage continues that through "well-orchestrated press campaigns targeting public opinion in the Nation-States, we provoke them to accept a large influx of refugees which will have the effect of destabilizing the domestic economy, and increasing racial tensions," ensuring that "groups of foreign extremists are part of the influx of immigrants," facilitating political, economic and social destabilization.[1] Esler paired the quotation with the question of how close the general public was to understanding precisely what the [[deep-state|Deep State]] is attempting to use mass immigration to do.[1]
