# Lolita Express

"Lolita Express" is the popular nickname for the private aircraft used by [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] to transport himself and his guests, whose flight logs have become a central piece of documentary evidence in public scrutiny of his network.[1]

In Badlands Media commentary by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the flight logs function as the baseline record against which alternative travel routes are measured. Carlson argues that a public figure who owned property on [[water-island|Water Island]] and vacationed in the [[us-virgin-islands|U.S. Virgin Islands]] could reach the vicinity of [[little-st-james|Little St. James]] without appearing on the logs at all, and that even if listed, could claim to have been "bumming a ride" to his own property.[1] Travel aboard Air Force One or Air Force Two while serving as vice president would, he contends, have a similar effect.[1]

Carlson pairs the flight-log question with speculation about other transport modes — a helicopter flight of 15 to 20 minutes from St. Croix, or a submarine trip from the base near [[st-thomas|St. Thomas]] — that would leave no comparable paper trail.[1]
