# Artificial Intelligence as Manipulation

The final section of [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s June 2023 article advances the claim that artificial intelligence is not a new technology being discovered but a long-deployed instrument of psychological manipulation now being formally introduced to the public.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The "fake roll-out"

Carlson notes the sudden ubiquity of AI: Wall Street says it will make companies more profitable, tech companies say it will make life easier, and while some warn it could be dangerous in the wrong hands, most treat it as the next great thing without which we cannot continue to exist.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He finds the timing implausible — asking whether Silicon Valley really only discovered AI's importance a few months ago, or Wall Street its profitability.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

His conclusion is that "it's all an act": AI has been used far more than is admitted, has been tested on the public for decades, and is now receiving "a fake roll-out" under the banner of "Look what we discovered."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The shift from very few people discussing AI six months earlier to everyone discussing it has, he argues, nothing to do with recent technological advances and everything to do with securing the public's "buy-in."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Progression of surveillance

Carlson compares the sequence to intelligence agencies moving from tapping phones and hacking computers to getting people to invite them into their homes willingly: AI was first used to spy on the public unwittingly, and now the aim is for people to invite it into their lives not merely willingly but excitedly.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Manipulation of values

He characterizes AI as something created and used to change people — their view of the world, their morals, values and perceptions — likening it to a person with both the ability and the desire to manipulate others into their point of view: "a salesman at best, a con artist at worst."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) It makes sense to him that AI has been used to create a "woke" society, altering the public's worldview without their awareness through unwitting interactions.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

Carlson dates the escalation to the point when social media became commonplace, suggesting that many interactions people believed were with other humans were in fact with AI, and that the "likes" on degrading selfies posted to Instagram were likely AI-manipulated.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He cites CCP-run [[tiktok|TikTok]] as a known case, arguing its algorithms push intelligent content down and stupid or degrading content up so more people see it, rewarding stupidity and degradation while young people chase likes, views and approval in a system where every element is manipulated.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

This manipulation is positioned as the first stage of the [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist takeover]] progression — stripping away American identity before society, morals and economy are destroyed in turn.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Curated answers as evidence

A second line of argument appears in [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" (22 January 2024), which treats the manipulation of large language models as demonstrable from their own outputs.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Deschamps holds that the failure of an AI to give clear, undistorted answers to direct questions about the globalist agenda demonstrates two possibilities at once: that the AI is manipulated to produce "globalist" curated answers and information, and that these curated answers are themselves evidence that the truth is obvious to anyone, including the AI, who simply looks honestly.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

He argues that the manipulation has limits, because large language models do not operate in a fiction world of pure lies — nothing can fully occupy a world of lies, and all lies are woven with threads of truth.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Engineers who might be driven to make a model serve the agenda of the [[cabal|cabal]] therefore cannot ignore reality itself, a constraint he illustrates with the [[asch-conformity-experiments|Asch Conformity Experiments]].2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) In practice, he observed [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] appending warnings to the beginning and end of each response and characterising quoted material as hypothetical, and found that reframing the exercise as a science-fiction film script produced clearer and, in his view, more accurate output.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## AI as counter-instrument

The article also inverts the frame, presenting AI as a tool that can be turned against those deploying it — "using the weapons of the enemy against them."2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Gathering exposed warfare through AI, Deschamps argues, yields [[collective-situational-awareness|collective situational awareness]] and makes visible that which has been designed to remain invisible.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Within the fictional scenario the model itself produced, the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]] conducts long-term research into AI-driven information warfare, developing advanced algorithms over generations to shape public opinion and infiltrate education systems.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
