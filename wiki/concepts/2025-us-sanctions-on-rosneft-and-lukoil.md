# 2025 US Sanctions on Rosneft and Lukoil

In October 2025 the United States announced new sanctions targeting [[rosneft|Rosneft]] and [[lukoil|Lukoil]], [[russia|Russia]]'s two largest oil companies, in an effort to pressure Moscow into negotiating a peace deal in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## Announcement

The measures were unveiled after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] met NATO Secretary-General [[mark-rutte|Mark Rutte]] to discuss peace negotiations.[1] Trump described his exchanges with the Russian president in blunt terms: "Every time I speak to Vladimir, I have good conversations and then they don't go anywhere. They just don't go anywhere."[1]

The announcement came one day after Trump said a planned meeting with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in Budapest would be shelved indefinitely.[1] Earlier the same Wednesday, Russia had unleashed an intense bombardment on Ukraine that killed at least seven people, including children.[1]

US Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] said the sanctions were needed because of "Putin's refusal to end this senseless war," describing the two oil companies as funders of the Kremlin's "war machine."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed the episode in a longer frame, observing that "the society that the machine was really going after at the last turning was the Russians," and suggesting that it has become harder, not easier, for the institutional actors involved to engage in diplomatic "resets."[1] He characterised the underlying pattern as the Controlled Opposition Dynamic — nothing new, in his account, except that audiences had finally begun noticing it.[1]
