# Election Variant

"Election variant" is a derisive term adopted by American critics of pandemic restrictions in August 2023 to describe the reported uptick in COVID-19 infections and the accompanying revival of institutional mask mandates, which skeptics argued was timed to justify emergency voting procedures ahead of the 2024 elections.[1] The phrase was popularized in an August 23, 2023 post on X by author George Papadopoulos, who wrote, "And just like that, the election variant emerges."[1]

## Renewed mandates

Several institutions reinstated masking requirements in the summer of 2023.[1] On August 20, Morris Brown College announced on Instagram that it had reinstated its COVID-19 mask mandate "effective immediately" following reports of infections among students in the Atlanta University Center, requiring all students and employees to wear face masks for a fourteen-day period and directing students to observe physical distancing.[1] The Hollywood studio [[lionsgate|Lionsgate]] circulated a memo asking employees to mask after some workers were infected; the memo, written by Sommer McElroy, specified that "employees must wear a medical grade face covering (surgical mask, KN95 or N95) when indoors except when alone in an office with the door closed, actively eating, actively drinking at their desk or workstation, or if they are the only individual present in a large open workspace."[1] The primary care provider [[kaiser-permanente|Kaiser Permanente]] began requiring staff, patients and visitors at its Santa Rosa facilities to wear face masks, and Upstate Medical's two hospitals in New York reimposed mandatory masking after reporting an increase in infections.[1]

## Public pushback

Many Americans publicly resisted the reimposed mandates and questioned the timing of the reported case increase with the 2024 elections approaching.[1] In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary elsewhere in the same brief, the renewed COVID messaging was characterized as a "Scariant COVID-23 Scamdemic" being spun up in an attempt to enable the same election irregularities alleged in 2020.[1]
