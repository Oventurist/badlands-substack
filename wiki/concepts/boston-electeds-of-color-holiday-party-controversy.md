# Boston Electeds of Color holiday party controversy

The Boston "Electeds of Color" holiday party controversy was a December 2023 dispute over a city hall holiday event planned exclusively for non-white elected officials, which drew national criticism after the invitation was mistakenly circulated to the entire Boston City Council.[1]

## Events

Mayor [[michelle-wu|Michelle Wu]]'s invitation to a December 13, 2023 "Electeds of Color Holiday Party" was sent in error to all city councilors by her aide, Denise DosSantos.[1] Fifteen minutes later DosSantos followed up with an apology: "I wanted to apologize for my previous email regarding a Holiday Party for tomorrow. I did send that to everyone by accident, and I apologize if my email may have offended or came across as so. Sorry for any confusion this may have caused."[1] The message contained no apology for the plan to host a racially segregated event; the invitation had been intended only for the council's six councilors of color, with its seven white members excluded.[1]

## Reaction

Outgoing City Councilor Frank Baker told the Boston Herald the episode was "unfortunate and divisive."[1] The story was reported by the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]] and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief of December 15, 2023.[1]
