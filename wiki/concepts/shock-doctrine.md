# The Shock Doctrine

**The Shock Doctrine** is the book by [[naomi-klein|Naomi Klein]] cited in Part 4 of the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series as documentation of how disaster functions as a delivery mechanism for centralized policy.[1]

## Use in the series

The essay summarizes the doctrine as follows: disaster is the favored trigger, and the event — pandemic, war, or economic collapse — produces disorientation.[1] Into the resulting vacuum a prepared script is deployed, consisting of centralization, surveillance, and submission presented to the public as safety.[1]

The article treats this pattern as the operational core of what it labels the fourth dimension of control, where the objective is no longer immediate obedience but the generation of reality itself through generational entrainment.[1] It pairs Klein's account with the claim that [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]] whitepapers describe crisis as an opportunity to install behavioral norms, concluding that in this layer the future is scripted rather than forecast.[1]

## Related claims in the article

Public health is named as following "the same playbook," alongside the educational reforms attributed to the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] and scaled globally through [[unesco|UNESCO]], and the later "future skills" agendas attributed to the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and [[world-bank|World Bank]].[1] The essay argues that such scripted timelines depend on energetic coherence that begins to unravel when suppressed records — it names the JFK, MLK, RFK, and Hunter Biden files, mRNA injection data, and [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud]] claims — are exposed.[1]
