# 2025 Alien Registration Requirement

The 2025 alien registration requirement is an immigration enforcement measure of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration mandating that non-citizens present in the United States register with the federal government.[1]

## Litigation

Judge [[trevor-mcfadden|Trevor Neil McFadden]], a Trump appointee, ruled in favor of the administration in April 2025, accepting its argument that the mandate was not a new policy but rather enforcement of existing federal law applying to all non-citizens.[1] McFadden did not weigh in on the merits of the policy itself, instead ruling that the organizations challenging it lacked legal standing to bring the case.[1]

## Terms of the rule

Following the decision, the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] reminded the public that individuals in the United States for more than 30 days were required to register by that Friday, when the enforcement measure took effect, and that the requirement would be fully enforced going forward.[1] Registration is mandatory for all individuals aged 14 and older present in the country without legal status; registrants must submit fingerprints and provide a residential address, and parents or guardians are responsible for registering minors under 14.[1] The requirement also applies to Canadian nationals who remain in the United States for more than 30 days.[1]

According to administration estimates, between 2.2 million and 3.2 million people could be affected by the new enforcement.[1]
