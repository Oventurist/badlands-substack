# Terrorism

Badlands Media treats "terrorism" less as a stable category than as a term whose meaning was deliberately reengineered during the late Cold War.[1]

For more than 150 years after it was coined, "terrorism" described violence inflicted *by the state on its people* for political aims.[1] That changed in 1986 — during [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]] — when then-UN Ambassador [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] repopularized and, in the article's framing, forever altered the definition upon the release of his book *Terrorism: How the West Can Win*.[1] Aided by [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]]'s repetition of the term ad nauseam after [[september-11-attacks|9/11]], the word became specifically synonymous with Islamic extremism directed against Western hegemony.[1]

The redefinition is central to the article's larger argument: that the same decades in which the United States, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[israel]] were funding and radicalizing the [[mujahideen]] also produced the vocabulary that would justify the [[war-on-terror|War on Terror]].[1]
