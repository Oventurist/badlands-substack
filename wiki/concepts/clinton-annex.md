# Clinton Annex

The "Clinton annex" is a long-classified appendix to the Justice Department Office of Inspector General's 2018 report on the FBI's investigation of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s use of a private email server. It was declassified in July 2025 at the request of Senate Judiciary Committee Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]], and its contents were characterized as showing major investigative failures by the [[fbi|FBI]] during that probe and during its review of Clinton's mishandling of highly classified information.[1]

## Declassification

The annex was released by Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] and FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]. Grassley had pressed for the document's release since 2018, including requests directed to former Attorneys General Bill Barr and to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and he reiterated the request earlier in 2025 before crediting Bondi and Patel for honoring it.[1]

## Thumb drives and unexamined evidence

The central revelation reported from the declassified watchdog material is that the FBI, during the Obama administration, failed to fully examine several thumb drives containing hacked [[state-department|State Department]] data, including messages linked to [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], during its 2016 Clinton email investigation.[1] The drives were supplied by a confidential source and were deemed too sensitive to review fully at the time because of concerns about privileged victim data they might contain. A draft internal FBI memo recommended analyzing the material in order to "assess the national security risks" tied to Clinton's private server — a step the bureau ultimately declined to take.[1]

Grassley told the *Washington Examiner* that the document "shows an extreme lack of effort and due diligence in the FBI's investigation of former Secretary Clinton's email usage and mishandling of highly classified information."[1]

## Related intercepted intelligence

Material discussed alongside the annex included intercepted Russian intelligence describing purported communications between Rep. [[debbie-wasserman-schultz|Debbie Wasserman Schultz]] and two individuals who worked for the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], in which Wasserman Schultz allegedly stated that the FBI had no hard evidence against Clinton because "data was removed from the mail servers just in time."[1] Badlands commentator Jon Herold argued this would imply Wasserman Schultz knew hard evidence had existed and was aware of a cover-up. The same intelligence reportedly stated that Obama used Attorney General [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] to pressure FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] into burying the email scandal.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands writer Burning Bright placed the annex within a broader narrative of convergence, arguing that the original scandals weaponized against Trump are "boomeranging" onto Clinton and Obama, and pairing the annex with [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s release of Russiagate files as synchronized disclosures accelerating exposure of what the outlet terms the Deep State.[1]
