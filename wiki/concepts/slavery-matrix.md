# Slavery Matrix

The **slavery matrix** is the term used by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] in the June 21, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "The Zombie Apocalypse vs. The Great Awakening" for the system of legal, economic and cultural arrangements he argues the world's populations presently inhabit, and which he holds operates in direct conflict with any reasonable conception of God's Will.[1]

## Control for its own sake

The essay defines the aim of evil with a single word — *control* — and specifies that what is meant is not control for an agreed-upon beneficial purpose but control for its own sake, a formulation the author borrows from the film The Matrix.[1] In the absence of genuine authority, control for its own sake is presented as completely synonymous with slavery.[1]

From this the essay derives a theological test: it asks whether it would be logical or sensible for a truly omnipotent, omniscient and omnipresent being to engage in the practice of slavery, and answers that any deity endorsing slavery cannot be the same deity who gave mankind free will and dominion over the Earth.[1] The author treats this as a fundamental contradiction in terms and concludes with what he calls total certainty that any group or society practising slavery cannot simultaneously be serving God or following anything resembling real Law.[1]

## Relation to law and teleology

The argument is linked back to the earlier Badlands essay "God and the Declaration of Independence," which held that the [[rule-of-law|rule of law]] cannot be sustained in the absence of God, since without God what mankind calls law devolves into a matter of mere preference.[1] The essay grants that most people agree theft, slavery and murder are bad, but argues that if nothing in creation has intrinsic meaning then such atrocities carry no significance beyond their effect on those directly harmed, lacking any valid *telos* to impart transcendent significance.[1] If it is God's Will that mankind be free, then thwarting human freedom works against what the author calls the self-selected telos of God, which makes injustice a matter of concern not only to victims but to God and creation at large.[1] The elimination of [[teleology|teleology]] from public thought is accordingly described as a precondition for spiritually disarming the masses.[1]

## Symptoms and self-concealment

The author holds that the legal systems and economies of the world today largely appear designed to wantonly usurp rights and inflict harm for its own sake, citing medical coverups, endless wars, endless media misdirection and the use of mass hypnosis to induce mass psychosis as evidence that insanity and sadism are built into the system as it currently operates.[1]

He argues that one of the most salient features of the slavery matrix is the lengths to which it goes to obfuscate its own nature and existence, which he takes to imply that it cannot afford for humanity to recognise it for what it is.[1] The corollary drawn is that humanity is more powerful than whatever force is attempting to enslave it: the cost of keeping everyone enslaved and in the dark would not be borne unless it were a necessity.[1] For the same reason the matrix is said to have been installed by *infiltration* rather than *invasion*, the invasion option being infeasible — and the infiltration is described as reaching past institutions down into culture and language itself.[1]
