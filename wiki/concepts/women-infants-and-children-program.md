# Women, Infants, and Children Program (WIC)

The **Women, Infants, and Children program**, commonly abbreviated **WIC**, is a federal nutrition-assistance welfare program administered by the [[usda|U.S. Department of Agriculture]] that provides food benefits and support to low-income mothers and young children.[1] Because enrollment rises and falls with household economic circumstances, participation figures are frequently used as a proxy indicator for the financial condition of lower-income families.[1]

## 2017 participation decline

USDA data showed that participation in WIC fell to a 17-year low in 2017.[1] Badlands Media's compilation of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s economic accomplishments cited the drop as further proof that the administration's domestic policies were aimed at helping the middle class and lower-income families, interpreting reduced enrollment as evidence that households were moving off assistance and into work rather than as a reduction in program access.[1] The item appears in the article alongside record-low Black unemployment, record middle-class income, and the [[opportunity-zones|Opportunity Zones]] initiative as part of a cluster of claims about gains among lower-income Americans.[1]
