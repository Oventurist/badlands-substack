# Retail Theft Wave

The "retail theft wave" refers to a pattern of large-scale, organized shoplifting in liberal American cities that the Badlands Media corpus interprets as a deliberate Communist plot to destroy downtown commerce and create racial division.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## The Lululemon and Ulta Cases

The June 2023 article "[[A Communist Plot to Take Over America]]" cites two specific incidents: [[Lululemon]] firing two employees who called 911 during an organized in-store theft, and [[Ulta Beauty]] projecting continued profit declines due to mass merchandise theft without reversing the post-2020 store policies that enabled it. Author [[Erik Carlson]] argues that corporations are tolerating theft not due to corruption alone but because they have "puppet masters" who want to destroy America.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

Carlson suggests the young people committing these thefts may be being coerced to steal in order to generate racial animus and social chaos. He interprets the public firing of the Lululemon employees as a deliberate deterrent message: anyone who interferes with the destruction of the country will be punished.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]
