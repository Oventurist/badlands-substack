# Retail Theft Wave

The retail theft wave is the pattern of organized, unpunished merchandise theft from corporate stores that [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] presents as one of the strongest pieces of evidence for a deliberate [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist plot]] against America.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The pattern described

Carlson writes that the vast majority of this theft occurs in liberal cities and is carried out by groups of young people who are "fully aware they can steal whatever they want, whenever they want from these corporate stores, and no one will do anything to stop them," and equally aware there are no repercussions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He cites [[lululemon|Lululemon]]'s firing of two employees who called 911 during a theft, and [[ulta-beauty|Ulta Beauty]]'s expectation of depressed profits all year due to continued massive theft without any return to pre-2020 store policies.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He dates the policy shift to "the original BLM looting three years ago."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Carlson's inference

Carlson argues that publicity around the Lululemon firings will make thieves still more comfortable, and speculates that the young people involved may themselves be "coerced to steal to create more racism and division in our country."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

His central argument is one of elimination: if the companies were merely corrupt, how would tolerating so much theft benefit them, when it takes directly from their profits?[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) Noting that the downtown areas of liberal cities are becoming ghost towns due to the violence, he concludes that "the only explanation that makes sense is these companies have puppet masters who are trying to destroy America by bringing in a Communist state."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

The [[subway-chokehold-prosecution|prosecution of the ex-Marine]] who intervened on a New York subway is offered in the same passage as the enforcement side of the same phenomenon — a message that anyone who gets in the way will be punished.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The financial coercion of the same corporations is treated under [[woke-capital|woke capital]] and [[deep-state|Deep State]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)
