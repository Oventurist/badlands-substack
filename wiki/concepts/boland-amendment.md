# Boland Amendment

The **Boland Amendment** was the congressional measure that cut off official United States funding to the [[nicaraguan-contras|Nicaraguan Contras]] during the 1980s.[1] In the account of Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], the amendment is the pivot on which the Contra drug scandal turns: with [[congress|Congress]] having closed the legal funding channel, the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and its allies allegedly found an alternative in drug money.[1]

The consequences documented by the 1989 [[kerry-committee-report|Kerry Committee report]] — Contra suppliers and pilots entangled in cocaine trafficking, and [[state-department|State Department]] payments to firms owned by known traffickers — followed directly from the funding gap the amendment created.[1] The same gap underlies the [[oliver-north|Oliver North]] resupply network and the events that drew [[mena-arkansas|Mena, Arkansas]] into the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] scandal.[1]
