# Ukraine as a Western Weapons Laboratory

"Ukraine as a weapons lab" refers to the characterization — reported by [[cnn|CNN]] in January 2023 and amplified by ZeroHedge — of the [[russia-ukraine-war|war in Ukraine]] as an opportunity for the United States and its allies to observe how their weapons systems perform in a conflict against a major military power.[1]

## The CNN report

CNN reported on Monday, January 16, 2023 that Ukraine had turned into a "lab" for Western arms.[1] A source familiar with Western intelligence on the war told the network that Ukraine is "absolutely a weapons lab in every sense because none of this equipment has ever actually been used in a war between two industrially developed nations," describing the conflict as "real-world battle testing."[1] The item was carried by ZeroHedge under a report by Dave DeCamp.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that while CNN's spin framed Ukraine as fending off an unprecedented act of aggression by a corrupt establishment in Russia bent on world domination, the American public was growing restless at the apparent collusion between the media and the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] to keep the machine running.[1] He argued that the Ukraine saga was beginning to remind ordinary Americans of the Forever War in the Middle East — one, in his framing, that it took [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to bring to an end.[1]
