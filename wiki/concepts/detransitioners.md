# Detransitioners

"Detransitioners" are people who underwent gender transition and subsequently reversed course, and whose testimony occupies a particular place in critiques of ideological capture in Western institutions.[1]

## Significance in ideological subversion theory

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] has argued that detransitioners matter because they represent an exception to a claim made by [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]] in his 1984 interview on [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]]: that people subjected to multi-generational ideological conditioning would become unable to see that "black is black and white is white," making the condition cognitively inescapable.[1] Detransitioners, in this account, were completely ideologically captured — to the point of sterilising and maiming themselves — and nonetheless escaped, disproving the inevitability thesis.[1]

Because of this, their accounts of how they recognised, deconstructed and exited the ideology are treated as empirical intelligence on the cognitive battlefield and as [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation]] weaponry, rather than merely as personal narratives.[1] Esler reports encountering the same dynamic directly while shooting his film [[canadas-red-shadow|Canada's Red Shadow]], where the belief in an ongoing "trans genocide" functioned as a propaganda endpoint comparable to the "autism genocide" claim described by a student in [[the-coddling-of-the-american-mind|The Coddling of the American Mind]].[1]

## Parallel with recovered students

The students profiled in *The Coddling of the American Mind* are presented as occupying the same category at a lower level of severity: they escaped the [[woke-mind-virus|Woke Mind Virus]] without the irreversible physical harm suffered by detransitioners.[1] Esler argues that the minds of formerly captured students are more valuable than those of students who saw through the ideology from the outset, because only the former can describe the mechanism of escape — and because their visible joy and purpose after recovery is what actually attracts other young people.[1]
