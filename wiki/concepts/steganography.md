# Steganography

**Steganography** is the practice of hiding secret data within an ordinary, non-secret file or message in order to avoid detection.[1]

## Role in the COVFEFE Dig

In "The COVFEFE Dig," [[gmoney|GMONEY]] introduces steganography as the answer to a problem posed by his own thesis: if a plan existed to end the central bankers' and Rothschild-controlled [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] system, its participants required a communications channel that could be neither hacked nor intercepted.[1] The article argues that [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is not only a monetary system but such a communications system, and suggests that hidden payloads could be carried inside images — "perhaps in an image of a PEPE."[1]

The author cites [[qanon|Q]] drop 2712, which pairs a [[pepe-the-frog|Pepe]] image with discussion of secure communications, as evidence for the mechanism, and connects the theme to the [[covfefe-act-of-2017|COVFEFE Act of 2017]], a bill about electronic communications named after the Tweet.[1]
