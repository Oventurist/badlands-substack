# Government Gangsters

*Government Gangsters* is a book by [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] arguing that entrenched officials within the U.S. federal bureaucracy — what Patel and allied commentators call the [[deep-state|Deep State]] — operate as a self-protecting network hostile to elected leadership, and proposing reforms to dismantle it.[1]

## Argument on purging disloyal officials

In the reform sections closing Part II, Patel argues for the removal of officials disloyal to the [[us-constitution|United States Constitution]] from positions of power.[1] In Chapter 9 he writes: "The Deep State has a lot of power and hatred, but they don't have a lot of imagination. The strategy of every ginned up Deep State fantasy is the same: make something up, get the fake news mafia to carry your water, and leak classified intel to falsely attack Trump."[1]

## Badlands coverage

The book was the subject of episode 5 of the [[badlands-book-club|Badlands Book Club]], where [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[canncon|CannCon]] covered the end of Part II.[1] Ashe in America applied Patel's framework to the April 2025 suspensions of [[dan-caldwell|Dan Caldwell]] and [[darin-selnick|Darin Selnick]] in the [[2025-pentagon-leak-investigation|Pentagon leak probe]], arguing that the leak strategy Patel describes depends on sympathetic personnel occupying key posts with access to intelligence.[1]
