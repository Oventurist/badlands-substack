# Third Temple

The Third Temple refers to the proposed rebuilding of the Jewish Temple in Jerusalem, a project with deep eschatological significance for parts of both Jewish and Christian religious communities and a recurring subject of Badlands commentary on the religious dimensions of the [[iran-war|Iran war]].[1]

## Role in the 2026 Iran war narrative

Commentator [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] claimed that the Hasidic movement [[chabad-lubavitch|Chabad-Lubavitch]] was a "hidden hand" behind the war involving Iran and suggested the movement was pushing for a broader religious war connected to rebuilding the Temple in Jerusalem.[1] Critics described the claim as a conspiracy theory, and Jewish security organizations warned that such rhetoric could contribute to an increase in antisemitic narratives online.[1]

## Badlands theological critique

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] said he had been writing and speaking about the effort to build the Third Temple for nearly three years and welcomed the topic finally reaching an audience of millions.[1] He argued that it was Jesus who destroyed the Second Temple when He condemned Jerusalem and the Sanhedrin for rejecting Him as Messiah, calling them the Synagogue of Satan and vowing that every stone would be torn down — a razing carried out by the Romans in 70 AD.[1]

From that Christian standpoint, he wrote, there is no need to rebuild the Temple a third time, because Jesus Christ is Himself the Third Temple and the fulfillment of all prophecy.[1] He criticized the contingent of [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionists]] eager to see the Temple rebuilt, some of whom believe cooperation with the project will lead Jews in Jerusalem to convert, and cited a sermon by Rabbi [[yosef-mizrachi|Yosef Mizrachi]] mocking such Christians with the epithet "JC Penny" for Jesus.[1] He contended that the Temple in the eyes of those Jewish practitioners is intended for the Mashiach — described as a mortal man who will rule the world and fill it with God's knowledge — an entity he likened to [[the-antichrist|the Antichrist]] rather than Jesus.[1]

## Red heifer ritual at Al-Aqsa (August 2024)

In August 2024 a group of religious Israelis were pictured practising the ritual of the [[red-heifer|red heifer]], which is meant to herald the building of a new Jewish temple on the site of the [[al-aqsa-mosque|Al-Aqsa Mosque]].[2] According to Jewish tradition, the ashes of a perfectly red heifer are needed for the ritual purification that would allow a third temple to be built in Jerusalem, and radical Jewish groups hold that the temple must be constructed on the raised plateau in Jerusalem's Old City known as the Temple Mount, where Al-Aqsa and the Dome of the Rock stand today.[2] Some believe this will herald the arrival of the messiah and possibly the end of the world.[2]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] tied the effort to [[kabbalah|Practical Kabbalah]], arguing that the 16th-century Rabbi Isaac Luria had held that pursuing such practices without a rebuilt Third Temple or the ashes of burned red heifers risked the unwitting summoning of demons, and that the [[temple-institute|Temple Institute]] activists driving the project may be attempting to summon not the messiah but the Antichrist.[2]
