# Fox News Lawsuit Against Los Angeles County

The Fox News lawsuit against Los Angeles County is a public records action filed in late January 2025 by [[fox-news|Fox News]] seeking documents about the county's relationship with the voting software and hardware company [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] Corp.[1]

## Background and filing

Attorneys filed the suit days after a New York-based court of appeals declined to dismiss Smartmatic's $2.7 billion defamation lawsuit against the network over false "conspiracy theories" about rigging of the 2020 election allegedly reported on air.[1] The complaint alleges evidence of corruption between Los Angeles County and Smartmatic employees at some point during the bidding process for the provision of voting technology.[1]

The lawsuit states: "Fox News brings this action to obtain public records that relate to the relationship between L.A. County and election systems company Smartmatic — two entities with recent and extensive allegations of corruption in government contracts."[1] It adds that "Fox News has already uncovered evidence showing personal benefits, including some undisclosed, flowing from Smartmatic to one County official."[1]
