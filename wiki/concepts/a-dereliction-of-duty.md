# A Dereliction of Duty

"A Dereliction of Duty" is a Badlands Media article by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], published November 16, 2024. It serves as a follow-up to an earlier piece on the 2012 Benghazi attack and advances a conspiratorial framework in which the Obama administration deliberately failed to rescue U.S. personnel under attack in order to protect a covert weapons-smuggling operation and cover up the administration's role in arming and funding terrorist organizations.

The article opens by asserting that after [[muammar-gaddafi|Muammar Gaddafi]] was overthrown, [[benghazi|Benghazi]] became a "safe haven" for [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] training camps and the focal point for funding and supplying jihadists fighting in Iraq and Afghanistan. It claims that Libya descended into chaos and the newly formed government was controlled by Al Qaeda elements, such as [[abdelhakim-belhaj|Abdelhakim Belhaj]]. According to the source, removing Gaddafi did not bring peace and freedom but instead gave Al Qaeda free rein in Libya.

## The Stevens Liaison Role

The article claims that Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] served as the liaison between weapons dealers and Al Qaeda in Libya, and that he was helping to coordinate weapons transfers from Libya to Syria in order to arm [[isis|ISIS]] in its attempt to overthrow [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]]. It describes this as a "secret operation" by the [[state-department|State Department]] under [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the [[cia|CIA]] under [[david-petraeus|David Petraeus]], conducted with [[barack-obama|President Obama's]] full approval.

According to the article, Obama "protected and nurtured ISIS" in Syria and Iraq, purposely allowing its expansion as the administration enriched itself by arming the group with heavy weapons including surface-to-air missiles. The source claims that as Secretary of State, Clinton was in charge of authorizing all private U.S. weapons sales around the world, and that she took advantage of that power by funneling weapons to U.S. enemies along with money into the Clinton Foundation.

The article asserts that Obama micromanaged the wars by requiring the military to request permission from the [[white-house|White House]] for every action on the battlefield. It claims this was essentially a [[stand-down-order|stand down order]] protecting ISIS and Al Qaeda, and that the longer the wars lasted, the more money could be stolen through weapons sales and money laundering.

## Osama Bin Laden as CIA Asset

The article claims that Osama Bin Laden had been working with the [[cia|CIA]] since the time he was fighting in Afghanistan against the Soviets in the 1980s, and that he was made the face of the "never-ending war on terror" after the 9/11 attacks. Citing a Daily Mail report, the source claims that former CIA agents — among them former Islamabad station chief [[bob-grenier|Bob Grenier]] — said they were prevented from killing [[osama-bin-laden|Bin Laden]] before 9/11 because of a [[memorandum-of-notification|Memorandum of Notification]] signed by President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] in August 1998 that forbade lethal action against him. The article describes this as a [[stand-down-order|stand down order]] and claims that Clinton was afraid the memorandum would be revealed by the 9/11 commission. It links this fear to the October 2003 episode in which national security adviser [[sandy-berger|Sandy Berger]] removed and destroyed classified documents taken from the [[national-archives|National Archives]].

According to the article, Bin Laden was a "CIA asset" who was "untouchable" for years, with people within the U.S. government sharing intelligence with those in contact with Bin Laden to protect him. The source claims that Bin Laden became "expendable" only when Obama's re-election prospects looked poor due to ISIS expansion and the wars in Iraq and Afghanistan going badly. It asserts that Obama removed Bin Laden's protection for political expediency but was still protecting ISIS because the weapons deals were too lucrative.

## John Brennan and the Benghazi Cover-Up

The article focuses heavily on [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], claiming he played a larger role in Benghazi than most people realize. According to the source, Brennan was serving as Obama's top counter-terrorism advisor during the Benghazi attack, and had previously been nominated to be head of the [[cia|CIA]] before withdrawing due to controversy over his involvement in CIA rendition and torture programs. The article claims Brennan was "certainly in the loop on everything happening in Benghazi" and that he facilitated the growth of terrorism in the Middle East.

According to an Investors Business Daily report cited in the article, Brennan was "nowhere to be found" in the media 20 days after the Benghazi attack, while other officials were speaking about it. The source claims this was because Brennan was preoccupied with covering up his role. It further asserts that Brennan became CIA director only months after the Benghazi attack as a reward for his part in the cover-up, and that he forced CIA operatives involved in the rescue efforts to sign new non-disclosure agreements at a memorial service for the fallen.

The article quotes former Special Forces Officer [[kris-paronto|Kris Paronto]] as saying they were told to "stand down" during the attack — a warning issued after [[john-tiegen|John "Tig" Tiegen]] told the base chief the team was "losing the initiative" — and claims that a team at Aviano Air Base in Italy was ready to deploy but never received the order. It notes that [[africom|AFRICOM]] learned of the attack within 30 minutes, and that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] was already "spinning up" forces to respond, citing Department of Defense Chief of Staff [[jeremy-bash|Jeremy Bash]]'s message to the State Department, but that the order never came. Citing Investors Business Daily, the article attributes the consulate assault to [[al-qaeda-in-the-islamic-maghreb|Al-Qaeda in the Islamic Maghreb]], armed with heavy weapons seized from the Libyan army.

## The Dereliction of Duty

The article claims that Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] obstructed the direct order of the Commander in Chief to rescue Americans under attack. Citing The Hill, the source notes that Clinton was more concerned with the diplomatic impact a rescue mission might have with the Libyan government than with saving American lives, and that she required approval from the Libyan government for any deployment of U.S. forces into Libya.

According to the article, a deputies meeting was convened at the [[white-house|White House]] at 7:30 pm on the night of the attack, led by Clinton while Obama and Secretary of Defense [[leon-panetta|Leon Panetta]] were absent. The source claims that despite Panetta supposedly issuing an order to deploy assets, the order was never carried out because Clinton purposely held back the rescue effort.

The article asserts that Ambassador Stevens was "sacrificed" and "purposely placed in a consulate that had no security." It claims that Stevens had become "expendable" because he knew too much about the covert weapons operation, and that the administration needed to shut down the operation before Congress learned the whole truth. The source notes that Stevens was meeting with [[turkey|Turkish]] Consul General [[ali-sait-akin|Ali Sait Akin]] at the consulate one hour before the assault began, which it describes as suspicious.

## Weapons Smuggling

The article details a massive weapons-smuggling operation in which Libyan weapons were shipped to ISIS in Syria. Citing The Times, it claims that ships carrying surface-to-air missiles from Libya were anchoring off the Lebanese coast while weapons were ferried ashore under cover of darkness and passed to Syrian rebel fighters. It notes that an arms dealer claimed Libyan leader Mustafa Abdul Jalil wanted to weaken Libyan militias by sending weapons to Syria.

According to the article, the first Libyan ship full of weapons was discovered in April 2012, and at least seven other ships carrying weapons from Libya were intercepted since then. It claims that only 5,000 of the 25,000 shoulder-fired missiles in Libya were accounted for, and that the rest were deliberately diverted to terrorist groups. The source asserts that Human Rights Watch and western intelligence agencies were worried these missiles would fall into Al Qaeda's hands, but that the new Libyan government was already controlled by Al Qaeda and was intentionally diverting them.

The article claims that CIA agents were deployed to [[turkey|Turkey]] to organize the arming of Syrian rebels, relying on a shadowy network of intermediaries including the [[muslim-brotherhood|Syrian Muslim Brotherhood]], paid for by [[qatar|Qatar]], [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], and Turkey. It also quotes State Department spokeswoman [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] insisting that "we are not in the business of arming in Syria." It notes that a New York Times report in June 2012 exposed this operation, and that the Obama administration was fearful of the exposure.

## The Taliban Connection

The article claims that the Obama administration was also giving weapons to the [[taliban|Taliban]]. Citing a 2014 New York Post report, it states that Taliban fighters in Kunar province successfully targeted a U.S. Army CH-47 helicopter with a Stinger missile on July 25, 2012. The source claims the missile traced back to a lot that had been signed out by the CIA recently, and that it was part of the same lot the CIA turned over to Qataris in early 2011—weapons that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton's]] State Department intended for anti-Gaddafi forces in Libya.

According to the article, Qataris delivered between 50 and 60 of those Stingers to the Taliban in early 2012, along with an additional 200 SA-24 Igla-S surface-to-air missiles. The source claims that Qatar was expected to hold five Taliban commanders released from Guantanamo for a year before allowing them to go to Afghanistan. It asserts that the U.S. government was literally arming its enemies in Libya, Syria, Iraq, and Afghanistan, and that they were using those weapons to kill U.S. troops—all for money.

## Conclusion

The article concludes by asserting that Christopher Stevens was a major player in the weapons operation who thought he was part of a "protected class" and "untouchable." The source claims that Stevens was sacrificed on September 11, 2012 because it was time to shut down the operation before Congress learned the whole truth. It claims that Obama and Hillary Clinton coordinated their narrative after the attack, and that Obama defended Hillary because they were "both puppets of Prince Alwaleed."

The piece asserts that the entire operation was designed to enrich government officials through weapons sales and money laundering while using taxpayer money to fund terrorist organizations. It claims that the Obama administration's "dereliction of duty" was not incompetence but a deliberate choice, and that the stand-down orders protecting Bin Laden were essentially the same as the stand-down orders that protected ISIS and Al Qaeda in Benghazi.
