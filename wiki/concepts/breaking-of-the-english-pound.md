# Breaking of the English Pound

The "breaking of the English pound" refers to the 1992 currency episode in which [[george-soros|George Soros]] made around $2 billion by shorting the British pound, earning the nickname "the man who broke the Bank of England."[1]

## The event

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identifies the trade as the principal source of the lore surrounding Soros as an investment genius, and notes that it falls in the early 1990s — the same period in which he says the [[british-cabal|British Cabal]] launched a wave of hedge funds and wealth management firms.[1]

## Carlson's objection

The problem with the standard narrative, Carlson argues, is that others made far more from the same short — including [[jp-morgan|J.P. Morgan]] — but kept it secret, while Soros was paraded around for the achievement.[1] He notes claims that J.P. Morgan is a front for British investors in America, which he suggests may explain why the firm could not brag about what it had done.[1] In this reading the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]] episode functions less as proof of Soros's genius than as a piece of manufactured reputation.[1]
