# Treasury Bond

A **Treasury bond** is a debt instrument issued by the United States government, described by Badlands Media as the starting point of the modern money-creation cycle.[1]

In the outlet's worked example, the government decides it needs $10 billion — for infrastructure, say, or foreign aid to a war-torn country — and requests the sum from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], which in turn requests $10 billion in government bonds.[1] The government issues Treasury bonds equal to the dollars requested, the Fed issues [[federal-reserve-note|Federal Reserve Notes]], and the two are exchanged.[1] When the government deposits those notes into a bank account they become legal tender and the money supply increases by $10 billion.[1]

Because the currency thus created exists only as the counterpart of a debt obligation, Badlands Media uses the exchange to support its central claim that money *is* debt.[1] In practice the bonds, the notes and the subsequent loans are all created electronically rather than physically.[1]
