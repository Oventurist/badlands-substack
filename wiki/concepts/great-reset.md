# Great Reset

The **Great Reset** is treated in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as the flagship program of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and of the oligarchical class contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] identifies with it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

In "On Ted Kaczynski and False Prophets" (25 October 2025), Ehret places the Great Reset alongside the World Economic Forum as the contemporary vehicle of a worldview whose axioms he argues were shared by [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) In this reading the Great Reset belongs to the same family of anti-industrial projects as [[transhumanism|transhumanism]] and [[eco-terrorism|eco-terrorism]] — movements Ehret describes as sculpted to draw as many people as possible into the willful self-destruction of industrial civilization.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## As the alternative to multipolar cooperation

In "A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation" (3 September 2025), Ehret frames the Great Reset as one of only two available futures following the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit in Tianjin.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Under this option, Russia, China, India and the other states of the multipolar alliance would abandon both their sovereignty and their pursuit of a win–win system, and would submit to what he calls a nest of technocratic unipolar priests managing a new world government from above the structures of elected regimes.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Ehret argues this would require erasing or rewriting the [[united-nations|UN Charter]], which premises international law on national sovereignty, non-interference and mutual cooperation, and would oblige states to accept draconian population-reduction measures under the rubric of "decarbonization" — sold as a new order of peace, degrowth, sustainability and a long-awaited utopian "end of history."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Judging by the profiles, words and performance of multipolar leaders over the previous decade, he concludes that they will not do this.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) He contrasts the anti-humanist Great Reset and transhumanist New World Order with a different kind of reset: an emergency summit convened before nuclear war, organised around the objective problems named in Franklin Roosevelt's [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## As an input to the AI warfare map

In "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" (22 January 2024), [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treats the Great Reset as a discrete plan that a globalist cabal can pick up and use, instructing [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to add [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s Great Reset as an additional plan for the fictional [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]] to utilise.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) It joins the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and the [[esg|ESG]] system as the institutional apparatus through which the model was asked to trace strategies, operations and tactics toward a [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Competing resets

Joe Lange's article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?" frames the Great Reset as inevitable but contested.[4] He writes that the world is indebted by hundreds of trillions of dollars and has reached the point where countries can no longer service the interest, so a reset was always coming; the real question is whose reset succeeds — Trump's plan or the [[cabal|cabal's]], financial freedom for the people or complete cabal control.[4]

In his telling the dismantling of the [[debt-slavery|global debt slavery system]] is already under way, with [[brics|BRICS]] building a parallel architecture, the world returning to a [[gold-standard|gold standard]], and [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDCs]] serving as the transition mechanism.[4] He rejects the assumption embedded in most Great Reset commentary that the enemy controls events, arguing instead that [[donald-trump|Trump]] and the military are forcing the moves.[4]
