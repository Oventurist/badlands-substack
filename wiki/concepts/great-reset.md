# Great Reset

The Great Reset is described in Badlands Media commentary as an anti-humanist, technocratic program for reorganizing global governance, contrasted against a cooperative reset grounded in national sovereignty.[1]

## Content as described

Under the scenario set out by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the states of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]] would have to abandon their sovereignty and their desire for a win-win system, and agree to a Great Reset conducted on the terms of "a nest of technocratic unipolar priests managing a new world government from above the structures of elected regimes."[1] This would require erasing or rewriting the [[united-nations|UN Charter]], which currently premises international law on national sovereignty, non-interference and mutual cooperation.[1] States would also have to accept "new systems of draconian population reduction under the rubric of 'decarbonization,'" in return for a promised new world order of peace, degrowth and sustainability, and a long-awaited utopian "end of history."[1]

## Assessment

Based on the profiles, words and performance of multipolar leaders over the past decade, the article concludes they will not accept these terms.[1] Ehret proposes instead a different kind of reset: an emergency summit held before nuclear war, shaped on Franklin Roosevelt's [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]] and the original spirit of the [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods system]], rather than a transhumanist new world order.[1]
