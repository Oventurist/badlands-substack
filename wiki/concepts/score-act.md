# SCORE Act

The Student Compensation and Opportunity Through Rights and Endorsements (SCORE) Act is federal legislation that regulates the compensation college athletes receive based on their name, image and likeness.[1]

## December 2025 floor delay

On Tuesday, December 2, 2025, a handful of members of the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]] delayed action in the House in protest of the bill.[1] Republican Representatives [[byron-donalds|Byron Donalds]] of Florida, [[scott-perry|Scott Perry]] of Pennsylvania and [[chip-roy|Chip Roy]] of Texas voted with all Democrats against the procedural rule legislation that would have teed up votes on the SCORE Act and other measures, stalling the vote at 207-209.[1] House Majority Leader [[steve-scalise|Steve Scalise]] huddled with Roy, Freedom Caucus Chair [[andy-harris|Andy Harris]], Representative [[andrew-clyde|Andrew Clyde]] — who had not yet voted — and a few other members and staff.[1] Harris and Clyde eventually cast their votes in favour of the measure, and leaders waited for an absent Republican to arrive in order to pass the rule.[1]

The episode was reported alongside the public feud between Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] and Representative [[elise-stefanik|Elise Stefanik]] as evidence of a House Republican Conference operating on a razor's edge.[1]
