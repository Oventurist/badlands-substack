# Tigers and Flies Campaign

The Tigers and Flies campaign is the long-running anti-corruption drive led by Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], targeting both high-level oligarchic figures — the "tigers" — and the mass of petty corrupt officials beneath them — the "flies."[1]

## Character and duration

The campaign has spanned more than a decade and has proceeded, in the description of Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], with unflinching methodical precision, accumulating leverage over time in a manner that Western observers mistook for stagnation.[1] Within the military, related anti-corruption efforts had ousted dozens of high-ranking officers since 2023.[1]

## January 2026 military purge

The investigations announced in late January 2026 into [[central-military-commission|Central Military Commission]] Vice Chairman Gen. [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]] and Gen. [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]] for "serious violations of discipline and law" were presented as the campaign's most daring application to date, reaching officers previously regarded as untouchable.[1] In the Badlands reading, the escalation marked the point at which accumulated leverage was finally deployed, and represented consolidation of authority rather than the fracturing of it — the expulsion of what the author characterized as Globalist Deep State tendrils embedded in China's institutional framework.[1]

## Role in the Sovereign Alliance framework

In April 2025 Badlands commentary, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] presented the Tigers and Flies campaign as evidence that [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] has been waging war on the same oligarchic class that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] oppose in their respective nations.[2] He dated the campaign to 2013 and stated that it has resulted in the arrest of over 10,000 CCP members, including generals, politicians, judges and magistrates, many of whom he said ushered in and perpetuated the communist-globalist system the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] is taking apart.[2] On this reading, the US–China trade confrontation and the campaign are complementary instruments of the same de-oligarchization process, with the apparent tensions between the three leaders described as manufactured.[2]
