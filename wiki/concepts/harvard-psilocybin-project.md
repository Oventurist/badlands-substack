# Harvard Psilocybin Project

The Harvard Psilocybin Project was a program of drug experimentation conducted at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] from 1960 to 1962 by [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]], with the blessing of Department of Social Relations chairman [[henry-a-murray|Henry A. Murray]].[1] Badlands Media reports that it experimented on large numbers of student subjects and was overseen directly by [[sidney-gottlieb|Sidney Gottlieb]] of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] program.[1]

The project's dates are treated as significant because they coincide precisely with the 1959–1962 window in which Murray's own experiment subjected twenty-two undergraduates — including [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]], code-named "Lawful" — to what the article calls a drug-assisted transformation.[1] A June 2000 London *Guardian* piece noted that by the late 1950s Murray had become interested in hallucinogens including LSD and psilocybin, and that Leary's return to Harvard followed soon after Murray's experiments were under way.[1]

In Badlands Media's framing, the project was one node in a wider effort — advanced by [[aldous-huxley|Aldous Huxley]], [[gregory-bateson|Gregory Bateson]] and [[margaret-mead|Margaret Mead]] — to found a drug-based "scientific paganism" capable of dissolving Judeo-Christian civilization and preparing populations for [[cybernetics|cybernetic]] management.[1]
