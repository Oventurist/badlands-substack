# Smart Cities

"Smart city" refers to an urban model in which dense sensor networks, digital identity systems and real-time data platforms are used to manage infrastructure, movement and resources.[1] Critics in the Badlands Media orbit treat the concept as a euphemism for pervasive surveillance and centralized population control.[1]

## Gaza as a proving ground

The essay "Our 'Greatest Ally'" argues that pre-war [[gaza|Gaza]], while not visually resembling Dubai, was technologically sophisticated in ways that did not benefit Gazans, and was probably one of the most highly surveilled places in the world.[1] Reporting quoted in the article describes the digital network monitoring the territory and its residents as "a variation on the data-intensive 'smart city' concept," and holds that the intimacy of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict turned the region into a proving ground for purpose-built surveillance technology that could be plugged into a future smart city.[1] The [[gaza-reconstruction-mechanism|Gaza Reconstruction Mechanism]], which logs every unit of building material entering the strip, is presented as one component of that architecture.[1]

The author speculates that the razed strip "would be a prime location for a new Smart City," linking the destruction to a subsequent reconstruction process from which institutional actors — including the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and finance houses modelled on Ukraine's "reconstruction bank" — stand to profit.[1]
