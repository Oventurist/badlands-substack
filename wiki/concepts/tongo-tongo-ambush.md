# Tongo Tongo Ambush

The Tongo Tongo Ambush was an incident in [[niger|Niger]] in which four U.S. soldiers were killed by [[islamic-state|Islamic State]] fighters.[1]

## Badlands analysis

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describes the ambush as a scandal in which the American soldiers were clearly set up by bad actors within the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and the [[cia|CIA]].[1] Analysis of the ambush also established, in his account, that U.S. Special Forces were operating in the same area of [[nigeria|Nigeria]] where 110 schoolgirls kidnapped by [[boko-haram|Boko Haram]] were rescued in March 2018.[1] He covered the episode on The Book of Trump with The AlphaWarrior Show.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the October 2017 ambush as one of a series of scandals showing Africa to be a battlefield between sovereign and globalist forces.[2] In his account, a Green Beret unit was sent into a remote village in [[niger|Niger]] to attend a tribal meeting and was ambushed by ISIS forces who killed four American soldiers along with several Nigerien troops accompanying them.[2] The episode became a scandal when it emerged that the American operators had expressed concern about the mission but were ordered to proceed without air support, intelligence, or mission-specific training, and after local leaders alleged the meeting was a set-up in which the village was complicit.[2]

He further argued the affair was "cooked up" by the [[cia|CIA]] and designed to be [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s Benghazi, noting that CNN quickly blamed Trump and the Green Berets while covering for [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and [[state-department|State Department]] leadership, and that Defense Department investigators faulted the operators for the mission's failure.[2] Other Green Beret units in the area reportedly volunteered for a rescue and were denied, with one unit defying the order, taking off in a helicopter, and being diverted and grounded.[2]
