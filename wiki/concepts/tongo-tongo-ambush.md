# Tongo Tongo Ambush

The Tongo Tongo Ambush refers to the October 2017 attack in Niger in which four U.S. soldiers were killed by [[isis|ISIS]]-affiliated fighters, an event that Badlands commentary treats as evidence that U.S. Special Forces were operating in the Lake Chad Basin region and as a scandal in which the soldiers "were clearly set up by bad actors in the Pentagon and CIA."

## Place in the Sahel narrative

According to the May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] states that "we know for a fact that US Special Forces were operating in that exact area from our analysis of the Tongo Tongo Ambush, where four US soldiers were killed by ISIS in a scandal where they were clearly set up by bad actors in the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and [[cia|CIA]]." The commentary uses this to support its account of the 2014 kidnapping of hundreds of schoolgirls in [[nigeria|Nigeria]] by [[boko-haram|Boko Haram]], 110 of whom were rescued in Nigeria in March 2018, and of the long-running U.S. military presence in the region that culminated in the May 2026 joint operation that killed [[abu-bilal-al-manuki|Abu-Bilal Al-Manuki]].
