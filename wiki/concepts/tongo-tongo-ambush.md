# Tongo Tongo Ambush

The Tongo Tongo Ambush was an incident in [[niger|Niger]] in which four U.S. soldiers were killed by [[islamic-state|Islamic State]] fighters.[1]

## Badlands analysis

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describes the ambush as a scandal in which the American soldiers were clearly set up by bad actors within the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and the [[cia|CIA]].[1] Analysis of the ambush also established, in his account, that U.S. Special Forces were operating in the same area of [[nigeria|Nigeria]] where 110 schoolgirls kidnapped by [[boko-haram|Boko Haram]] were rescued in March 2018.[1] He covered the episode on The Book of Trump with The AlphaWarrior Show.[1]
