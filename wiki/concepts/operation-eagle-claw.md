# Operation Eagle Claw

Operation Eagle Claw was the failed United States military effort in 1980 to rescue the American hostages held at the US Embassy in Tehran, [[iran]].[1]

## Involvement of the USS Nimitz

Helicopters launched from the aircraft carrier [[uss-nimitz]] were part of the rescue attempt.[1] The Nimitz's participation in Eagle Claw was invoked in June 2025 reporting as historical context for the carrier's accelerated redeployment to the Middle East during the Israel–Iran exchange of strikes, with commentary noting that the carrier had been present at both bookends of a long confrontation.[1]

## Legacy framing

Coverage of the 2025 deployment characterized the operation's failure as the opening of a decades-long "shadow war" between the United States and Iran that has continued ever since.[1]
