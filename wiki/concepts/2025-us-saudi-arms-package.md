# 2025 US–Saudi Arms Package

The **2025 US–Saudi arms package** was an agreement announced on Tuesday, May 13, 2025, under which the United States agreed to sell Saudi Arabia weapons and defense services worth nearly $142 billion. A White House fact sheet described the deal as "the largest defense cooperation agreement" Washington had ever concluded.[1]

## Scope

The agreement was signed during US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s visit to the Saudi capital, Riyadh, and covered arrangements with more than a dozen US defense companies. Named areas of cooperation included air and missile defense, air force and space advancement, maritime security, and communications.[1] The fact sheet called the package "a clear demonstration of our commitment to strengthening our partnership."[1] Reuters had reported the previous month that the package would be worth well over $100 billion, a figure the final announcement substantially exceeded.[1]

## Context and commentary

The arms package was one component of a broader Gulf diplomatic tour that also produced defense and trade agreements with [[qatar|Qatar]] and the lifting of US sanctions on Syria.[1] Badlands host Jon Herold questioned the timing, noting the contrast between the scale of the weapons deals and earlier official messaging that the United States was running short of weapons and munitions for its own stocks.[1]

Badlands commentators read the Riyadh leg of the trip through the lens of Trump's 2017 visit to Saudi Arabia, treating both the ceremonial [[sword-dance|Ardah]] and the arms agreements as public signals in what they describe as the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]]'s conflict with entrenched globalist power.[1] Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] was characterized in that commentary as a partner who, backed by Trump, had engaged in prolonged asymmetric conflict against those interests between 2017 and 2021.[1]
