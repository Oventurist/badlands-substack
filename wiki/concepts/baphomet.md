# Baphomet

Baphomet is an occult figure commonly depicted as a winged, goat-headed man with female breasts, and widely associated in popular culture with Satanism.[1] The name recurs in Badlands Media commentary as a symbolic shorthand for what its contributors describe as an androgynous, inversion-oriented current in Western culture.[1]

## Templar association

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that, beginning in 1307 and continuing through the 14th century, members of the [[knights-templar|Knights Templar]] were routinely arrested amid accusations of conducting occult rituals in which Baphomet was worshipped.[1] The commentary linked the Templars and other Christian military orders founded for the Crusades — such as the Knights of Malta — to occult mysticism, and asserted an affinity between those orders and the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]], citing a photograph of Nathaniel Philip Rothschild's $87 million yacht and the flag flown on its stern, supplied by *Breaking History* co-host [[matt-ehret|Matt Ehret]].[1]

## Use in the gender-transition debate

The Baphomet motif appeared in Badlands' response to [[patriarch-kirill|Patriarch Kirill]]'s January 2025 statement that Western acceptance of gender reassignment surgery "smells of apocalypse," with the contributor arguing that transgender ideology is a manifestation of Satanism that has spread into secular institutions and churches.[1]
