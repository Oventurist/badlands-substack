# Psychogenic Triggers

Psychogenic triggers are programmed responses to specific stimuli, installed in victims of [[ritual-abuse|ritual abuse]] and [[mind-control-programming|trauma-based mind control]] by their trainers or handlers within a cult or perpetrator group.[1] The stimulus may be verbal — a keyword or phrase — or visual, such as a geometric or occult design.[1]

## Clinical observations

Dr. [[james-randall-noblitt|James Randall Noblitt]] noticed that dissociative patients frequently scribbled geometric shapes, and that in group art therapy some patients fell into an apparent trance after looking at another patient's artwork, an effect that was especially pronounced with stereotypical occult geometric designs.[1] Testing this with his patient "Sharon," he asked her to free-associate to drawings of a circle, triangle, square and star; her responses were unremarkable until the star, to which she answered "Sex."[1] She eventually disclosed that her father drew a star in blood on her abdomen before raping her, and that "my daddy said never to tell."[1]

The most striking verbal instance Noblitt recorded occurred in a hospital group for patients with multiple personality disorder. When a patient recalled a biofeedback therapist repeating "deeper and deeper," another group member, "Karen," angrily demanded that the words not be spoken — and was herself in a deep trance moments later.[1] Looking around the room, Noblitt found all four group members glassy-eyed and staring at the floor, something he had never seen in eleven years of running groups.[1]

## Function

Clinicians later recognized such episodes as programmed responses rather than spontaneous psychopathology.[1] While in the trance state, victims of cult programming are susceptible to prompts from anyone who knows the keywords or possesses the required external stimuli, allowing specific [[alter-personalities|alters]] and behaviors to be elicited on command.[1] The 1991 report of the [[los-angeles-county-commission-for-women-ritual-abuse-task-force|Los Angeles County Commission for Women's Ritual Abuse Task Force]] described the surrounding system as one of brainwashing, programming, indoctrination, hypnosis and mind-altering drugs whose purpose was to keep victims silent and obedient while remaining undetected in wider society.[1]
