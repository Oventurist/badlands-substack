# Sharia Law

Sharia law is the Islamic legal and religious code that the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] seeks to establish under a global caliphate.[1] Badlands Media characterizes it as inherently oppressive, particularly toward women, adherents of other religions, and free speech.[1]

## Role in the Arab Spring

According to Badlands Media, the common thread linking the Arab leaders toppled or targeted during the [[arab-spring|Arab Spring]] — in Tunisia, Libya, Egypt, Yemen, Syria, and Bahrain — is that all of them were suppressing the Muslim Brotherhood in their own countries and preventing it from enforcing strict Sharia law.[1] On this reading, the uprisings removed the principal obstacles to the Brotherhood's project rather than merely deposing "oppressive" rulers as the Obama administration claimed.[1]

In Egypt, [[mohamed-morsi|Mohamed Morsi]] won the post-[[hosni-mubarak|Mubarak]] election, installed Muslim Brotherhood allies in powerful government positions, and began implementing Sharia law, including efforts to rewrite the Egyptian constitution.[1] Millions of Egyptians took to the streets in protest, and the military removed the Morsi government in an action backed by the population; the Brotherhood was again outlawed and its leaders arrested.[1]

## Introduction into the United States

Badlands Media argues that the promotion of Sharia norms inside the United States proceeded through legal and rhetorical channels rather than force. The Obama administration signed [[un-resolution-16-18|UN Resolution 16/18]], which criminalizes speech deemed to incite imminent violence on the basis of religion, then pushed an [[innocence-of-muslims|anti-Islam video]] as the cause of the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]] and jailed the video's maker, [[nakoula-basseley-nakoula|Nakoula Basseley Nakoula]] — described by author Kenneth Timmerman as "the first victim of Islamic Sharia blasphemy laws in the United States."[1] This gradual insinuation of Sharia into Western societies is what the Brotherhood terms [[civilization-stealth-jihad|civilization jihad]].[1]
