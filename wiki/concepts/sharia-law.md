# Sharia Law

Sharia is the body of Islamic religious law. In the Badlands corpus it appears chiefly as the stated objective of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s alleged campaign of institutional infiltration in the West.

## Treatment in the corpus

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]] states that the Brotherhood's goal is "to create a global sharia law caliphate," pursued through the twin tracks of charity-funded militancy abroad and [[civilization-stealth-jihad|"civilization/stealth jihad"]] within non-Muslim states. The article argues that the Brotherhood "is against assimilation, and instead wants to replace the constitution with sharia law," and that articles from the *[[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]]* have been used in universities worldwide "to teach the promotion of sharia law and the supremacy of the Muslim religion."

Quoting PJMedia, the article describes the ultimate aim as incrementally infiltrating "sharia principles in our law, our institutions, and our public policy," and creating enclaves — physical, academic, and governmental — "in which sharia is honored as basic law and Muslims are given safe haven to refrain from pressures to assimilate." The author extends this claim to the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration, which he describes as the culmination of decades of infiltration, and to [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], whose alleged "sixteen year plan" he characterizes as an effort to destroy the Constitution "while forcing Sharia law on the people."
