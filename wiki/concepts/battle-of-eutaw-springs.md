# Battle of Eutaw Springs

The Battle of Eutaw Springs, fought 8 September 1781, was one of the bloodiest battles of the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]] and the last major field engagement in the South.[1]

## Marion's militia at Eutaw Springs

[[francis-marion|Francis Marion]] commanded the front line of militia at Eutaw Springs, a body that distinguished itself by firing seventeen volleys per man — an extraordinary performance given that militia were never expected to fire more than a single volley before retreating.[1] The battle is depicted in F. C. Yohn's 1903 painting reproduced in the article.[1]
