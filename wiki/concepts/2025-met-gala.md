# 2025 Met Gala

The 2025 Met Gala was the annual fundraising event held at the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City, staged in early May 2025 with the theme "Superfine: Tailoring Black Style."[1]

## Theme

The theme paid homage to the history and cultural influence of Black style, with particular emphasis on the 18th-century dandy.[1] Dandyism was described as a fashion movement originating in the 1700s and rooted in a history of resistance and a celebration of Black individuality.[1]

## Participants

The event's co-chairs were actor Colman Domingo, racing driver Lewis Hamilton, rapper A$AP Rocky, producer Pharrell Williams, and American Vogue editor-in-chief Anna Wintour.[1] LeBron James served as honorary chair.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary criticized the theme as hypocritical, arguing that the same cultural institutions and figures who spent years condemning "cultural appropriation" had built the year's gala around dressing in a style coded to Black identity.[1]
