# A Look At Recent Features (August 15, 2025)

"A Look At Recent Features," subtitled "Catch Up on the Latest from Badlands Contributors!", is an edition of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] published on August 15, 2025 by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on its Substack. The post recorded 51 likes at the time of archiving. Like other roundup editions of the brief, it contains no original reporting; instead it collects and re-presents recent long-form feature articles by Badlands contributors while the editorial team was away at GART. A separate roundup carrying the same title was published on November 7, 2025 and is catalogued at [[a-look-at-recent-features-84d|A Look At Recent Features (November 7, 2025)]].

## Format and framing

The edition opens by explaining that "with the team at GART this week, today's Badlands News Brief is a look back at our recent feature articles from Badlands contributors." It carries a sponsored placement for All Good, a product line by Jessica Storm, described as crafted "with care, free from any junk ingredients," using "only the best quality organic oils and 100% premium grass fed tallow," with "no fillers and nothing extra"; readers are directed to use the promo code "Badlands." The brief tells readers that regular publication will resume the following week and that it is "not too late" to obtain GART virtual tickets.

Each entry in the roundup lists the feature's title, author, publication date, cover image and a short excerpt, followed by a "Read full story" link to the full piece on the Badlands Substack.

## Featured articles

The edition highlights seven features published between July 19 and August 12, 2025.

"Trump, Putin, Alaska and the Future of Multipolarity" by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] (August 12, 2025) previews the summit between [[donald-trump|President Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]: "On August 15, an extremely important meeting will take place between President Trump and Vladimir Putin in the state of Alaska. What will be discussed between these two men and what arrangements have been decided are shrouded in mystery." The piece is framed around the trajectory of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar order]].

"Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] (August 9, 2025) is introduced as "the fifth article in a series on the Hijacking of the Human Operating System, and what's being done about it," with readers advised to "start with Part 1."

"The Birth of America's Deep State" by Matthew Ehret (August 6, 2025) is described as "the third entry in the new Badlands Media 'Founders & Foundations' series." The excerpt states that "Part 1 focused on Alexander Hamilton, Part 2 focused on Abraham Lincoln and Part 3 covered Benjamin Franklin," and the piece addresses the origins of the American [[deep-state|deep state]].

"Hijacked Consciousness - The Silent War" by Observing Consciousness (August 2, 2025) is presented as "the fourth article" in the same Hijacking of the Human Operating System series.

"A Hypnotist Rants about How to Wake Up the Masses" by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] (July 26, 2025) is excerpted only by its salutation, "Greetings Frens and Patriots."

"And Iran, Iran So Far Away" by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] (July 24, 2025) is described as "a direct continuation of The Deep Politics of the '12-Day War.'"

"A Hypnotist Rants about Q, Epstein and the Nature of Reality Itself" by American Hypnotist (July 19, 2025) is excerpted with the salutation "Greetings Frens," and takes up [[q-anon|Q]], Jeffrey Epstein and the nature of reality.

## Editorial notes

The brief closes with the outlet's standard disclaimer that "the opinions expressed in the Badlands News Brief are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the attitudes or positions of Badlands Media," an appeal to "share far and wide" and to comment, and a solicitation for paid subscriptions to "help our collective of citizen journalists take back the narrative." It ends with the outlet's signature slogan, "We are the news now."
