# Albuquerque GOP Headquarters Arson

The **Albuquerque GOP headquarters arson** was an alleged arson and vandalism attack on the Republican Party of New Mexico's headquarters in Albuquerque in late March 2025.[1]

## The incident

The entrance to the building was destroyed in what the organization described as an alleged arson attack combined with a vandalism attempt, after the party shared images showing damage to the entryway.[1] The incident occurred early on a Sunday morning.[1]

The Albuquerque Fire Department confirmed the attack, saying crews were dispatched just before 6 a.m. and brought the fire under control within five minutes of arrival.[1] The structure suffered damage to the front entryway and smoke damage throughout the building; no injuries to civilians or firefighters were reported.[1] The fire department and the Albuquerque Police Department said the matter was an active and ongoing joint [[fbi|FBI]]/[[bureau-of-alcohol-tobacco-firearms-and-explosives|ATF]] investigation.[1]

Republican Party of New Mexico Chairwoman Amy Barela shared images of the damage on X, calling the incident "a deliberate act of arson which also included spray-painting the words 'ICE=KKK.'"[1] The party characterized the attack as a "direct assault on our values."[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued there were "good odds that we have another 'summer of love' coming," contending that domestic radicals had been restrained during the previous administration and were now "itching to get physical and set things on fire."[1] She linked the arson to an incident in Colorado in which activists allegedly threw themselves onto cars and claimed Republicans had struck them, and to protests outside a Steve Bannon speech in Colorado.[1] Her conclusion was that "these radicals are going to get worse before they get better."[1]
