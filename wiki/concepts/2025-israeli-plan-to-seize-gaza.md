# 2025 Israeli Plan to Seize Gaza

In May 2025 the Israeli Cabinet approved a plan to seize the [[gaza|Gaza Strip]] and to remain in the Palestinian territory for an unspecified period of time.[1]

## Approval and content

Two Israeli officials confirmed that the plan was approved in an early morning vote by Cabinet ministers on May 5, 2025.[1] If implemented, it would vastly expand Israel's operations in the territory and was expected to draw fierce international opposition.[1] The plan also called for hundreds of thousands of Palestinians to relocate to Gaza's south, a step that would likely amount to forcible displacement and worsen an already dire humanitarian crisis.[1]

## Israeli political and military reaction

Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] instructed Israeli citizens to "embrace Gaza occupation," saying nothing would stop the war — not even the release of the hostages.[1] [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] Chief of Staff Lt. Gen. [[eyal-zamir|Eyal Zamir]] warned that launching the operation could mean losing the remaining hostages held in Gaza.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that conquering Gaza had been the underlying reason [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] facilitated the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]], and that announcing a hostile takeover made sense as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] prepared to visit multiple Arab countries — but not Israel — while promising a new nuclear deal with [[iran|Iran]].[1] The commentary concluded that Americans would eventually have to confront the consequences of what it described as blind support for the government of [[israel|Israel]].[1]
