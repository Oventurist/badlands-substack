# 2023 Ukrainian Defense Ministry Purge

The 2023 Ukrainian defense ministry purge was a sweeping dismissal of senior Ukrainian defense officials ordered by President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] in September 2023, immediately before his trip to Washington to lobby [[congress|Congress]] for a new multi-billion-dollar aid package for Kiev.[1]

## The firings

Taras Melnychuk, permanent representative of the Ukrainian Cabinet of Ministers, announced on Telegram that six deputy defense ministers had been dismissed from their posts.[1] The dismissals came two weeks after Defense Minister [[oleksii-reznikov|Oleksii Reznikov]] was sacked over corruption.[1] Neither Melnychuk nor the newly appointed defense minister [[rustem-umerov|Rustem Umerov]] gave a reason for the terminations.[1]

## Interpretation

The New York Times reported that Zelensky made the move to demonstrate to American officials and other Western leaders that his government was not squandering, through graft or mismanagement, the tens of billions of dollars in aid already sent to Ukraine.[1] The paper judged the practical impact to be long-term rather than immediate: battlefield decisions run directly from Zelensky to the uniformed general staff, largely bypassing the civilians at the defense ministry, whose role is primarily logistics — procurement, salaries and benefits — rather than tactics.[1]

Ukraine was widely described as the most corrupt country in Europe before the Russian invasion; since then Washington has sent Kiev over $100 billion in weapons and direct financial assistance.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], analyzed the purge as an instance of what he calls [[trojan-horse-methodology|Trojan Horse methodology]] — the practice of baiting populations into an illusion of globalist benevolence by conspicuously punishing a bad actor, thereby restoring faith in the trustworthiness of the system as a whole.[1] He argued the purge was undertaken at the behest of Zelensky's Washington counterparts, who needed to sell the American political class on the moral necessity of the war, and expressed doubt that Zelensky was a genuinely reformed figure.[1]
