# Astana Process

The Astana Process is the long-running negotiating track on Syria named for Astana, the capital of [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], where its talks were hosted.[1]

In Badlands commentary, GhostofBasedPatrickHenry described the Astana Process as "the long-running plan to save Syria" and argued it was in fact a Sovereign Alliance plan to exfiltrate Bashar al-Assad from Syria and begin a countermove against the cabal.[1] He noted that the process began in January 2017 — the same month [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took office — and that it culminated days before Assad was "overthrown" in December, with key meetings occurring immediately before and after Trump's re-election.[1]

The same commentary situated Astana within a wider reading of Kazakhstan's strategic position: a 2022 coup that he interpreted as [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s move to take benevolent control of the country, and Kazakhstan's role in Eurasian rail corridors linked to the [[kazakhstan-united-states-locomotive-deal|September 2025 US locomotive deal]].[1] See also [[astana-accords|Astana Accords]].
