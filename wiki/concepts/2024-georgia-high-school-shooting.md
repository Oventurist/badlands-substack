# 2024 Georgia High School Shooting

The 2024 Georgia high school shooting is invoked in Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as a contemporary illustration of a pattern the Q drops allege: that mass-casualty events surface whenever politically damaging news breaks for the Democratic Party.[1]

## Framing in Chapter 19

Chapter 19 presents a graphic showing that less than a week after [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] and [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] — both former Democrats — endorsed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a shooting occurred at a high school in Georgia.[1] The author describes the timing as "like clockwork" and advises readers to watch for [[false-flag-operation|false flags]] whenever bad news breaks for the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]

## Alleged motives

Drop #120 asks why a terrorist attack or mass shooting seems to follow every piece of bad news for Democrats. The Badlands author offers two motives: first, to pull the news cycle away from whatever damaging story is circulating; and second, to reinforce a narrative that guns are dangerous, supporting a longer-term push to disarm Americans.[1] The chapter argues that the effect has been the opposite of what its alleged planners intend, with each such event convincing more Americans that firearms are necessary and that the [[second-amendment|Second Amendment]] is essential to national sovereignty.[1]

These claims are the interpretive assertions of the Badlands author and are not established findings about the incident.
