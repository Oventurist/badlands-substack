# Learned Helplessness

**Learned helplessness** is a psychological phenomenon, associated with the research of [[martin-seligman|Martin Seligman]], in which repeated exposure to uncontrollable events creates a state where individuals cease to resist or even attempt change.[1] It is cited as one of the established foundations for the concept of [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]] set out in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay.[1]

The essay argues that populations conditioned to feel powerless are easier to control, and that passive resignation becomes deeply ingrained until a societal mindset takes hold in which external authority appears to be the only viable solution.[1] GDS is said to exploit this through a calculated engineering of crises — economic downturns, health emergencies, and geopolitical conflicts — that are framed as too complex for individual or local resolution.[1] By perpetuating this state, the [[deep-state|Deep State]] is said to ensure that populations internalize dependence on centralized power as a necessary safeguard, entrenching generational cycles of conformity and submission.[1]
