# Presidential Walk of Fame

The **Presidential Walk of Fame** is a display installed near the Oval Office by the [[white-house|White House]] during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term, consisting of framed portraits of past United States presidents.[1] It was unveiled in September 2025 and drew immediate attention for a single conspicuous substitution.[1]

## The Biden autopen portrait

Notably absent from the installation was a conventional portrait of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]; in its place the White House hung an image of an autopen signing Biden's name.[1] The substitution referenced Trump's repeated claim that Biden had used an autopen to execute important documents, including pardons, rather than signing them personally — the core allegation of the [[autopen-scandal|autopen scandal]].[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

Badlands editor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] responded to the display with a single line — "Love it" — and highlighted the accompanying White House framing as an example of what he called "the Devo term."[1] Contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the post as among the most significant the White House or Trump had ever published, reading it through a three-term schema in which Trump's first term was "Seeding," the second "Germination," and a prospective third "Harvest," and crediting the outcome to "Our Boy Blue."[1]
