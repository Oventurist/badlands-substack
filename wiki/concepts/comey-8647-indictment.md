# Comey 8647 Indictment

The "8647" indictment is the federal criminal case against former FBI director [[james-comey|James Comey]] arising from an Instagram post displaying the numerals 8647, read by prosecutors as a threat against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Blanche's Meet the Press remarks

Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] appeared with [[kristen-welker|Kristen Welker]] on NBC's Meet the Press in early May 2026 and said the case extends beyond the social-media post itself, while declining to give further detail.[1] "Rest assured that it's not just the Instagram post that leads somebody to get indicted," he said, adding that intent is proven "with witnesses… with documents, with materials."[1] Blanche promised a public trial at which "everybody in this country will know exactly what evidence the government has against Mr Comey."[1]

## Reported second case

Bloomberg Law, citing anonymous sources, reported that the Justice Department was pursuing a separate indictment of Comey for leaking classified documents, referencing information Comey passed to Daniel Richman that appeared in a 2017 New York Times article.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected that thread to the Badlands thesis that Comey's 2017 conduct was the cover-up of [[barack-obama|Obama]]-era actions in 2016, asking whether the second matter constitutes a distinct trial or additional overt acts in a treasonous conspiracy spanning nearly a decade.[1]

## Context in DOJ's week

The [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] listed the Comey indictment among its weekly highlights on X, alongside the indictment of a former [[anthony-fauci|Fauci]] advisor for hiding COVID-19 origin data from FOIA, unsealed charges against three individuals for allegedly assaulting a [[turning-point-usa|TPUSA]] journalist, a report from the Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias and the Weaponization Working Group on the Biden administration's treatment of Christians, the sentencing of opioid manufacturer Purdue Pharma for fraud and kickback conspiracies, and ATF regulatory reforms for gun owners and businesses.[1]

## Trial delay

In May 2026, US District Judge Louise Wood Flanagan granted Comey's request to delay his criminal trial in the case, setting a new trial date of October 21 with arraignment scheduled for September 30; prosecutors did not oppose the delay.[2] Comey had been charged over an Instagram post showing seashells arranged on a beach in the pattern "86 47," which prosecutors and Trump allies allege amounted to a veiled threat against the 47th president.[2] At a press briefing announcing the charges, Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] said the post crossed a legal line and was not protected by the First Amendment, arguing that threats against the president are explicitly prohibited under federal law.[2]
