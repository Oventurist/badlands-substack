# Garden of Heroes

The Garden of Heroes is a proposal associated with [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] for a national park in which monuments dedicated to American heroes — and the stories attached to them — would be showcased.[1]

## Background

[[donald-trump|Trump]] has spoken many times about ambitious plans to restore patriotism, national pride and a sense of purpose to American culture, and the Garden of Heroes was one component of that program.[1] Writing in June 2023, [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]] noted that the details of the concept remained relatively vague, but described it as posited as some kind of national park displaying monuments to American heroes and their stories.[1]

## Relationship to the 1776 Commission and The Patriot Chronicles

The Garden of Heroes was identified as one of the two aspects of Trump's [[1776-commission|1776 Commission]] that most excited [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]], the other being the restoration of true Classical Architecture to federal buildings — the author's professional background.[1] Because he had been composing short heroic histories for friends and family by email and social media as far back as 2015, the concept resonated with him personally, and he explicitly framed [[patriot-chronicles|The Patriot Chronicles]] as "a digital kickstart for President Trump's vision."[1]
