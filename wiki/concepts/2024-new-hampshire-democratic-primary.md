# 2024 New Hampshire Democratic Primary

The 2024 New Hampshire Democratic presidential primary became a flashpoint between the state Democratic Party and the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] after President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] declined to file for the ballot.[1]

## Biden's decision not to file

In October 2023 Biden's reelection campaign announced that he would not file to have his name appear on the state's 2024 Democratic primary ballot, opting to skip a contest that New Hampshire planned to hold in defiance of a revamped primary calendar the White House had championed.[1] Campaign manager [[julie-chavez-rodriguez|Julie Chavez Rodriguez]] wrote to New Hampshire Democratic Party chair Raymond Buckley that "while the president wishes to participate in the primary, he is obligated to comply" with party rules.[1] Rodriguez added that the president looked forward to appearing on New Hampshire's general election ballot as the party's nominee after securing the nomination at the 2024 Democratic National Convention, where he would "tirelessly campaign to earn every single vote in the Granite State."[1] Write-in votes nonetheless remained an option for New Hampshire Democrats.[1]

## Calendar dispute

Biden had urged the DNC in 2022 to reorder the 2024 nominating calendar, replacing Iowa's leadoff caucus with the South Carolina primary in order to better empower Black and other minority voters seen as crucial to the party's base.[1] In February 2023 the DNC approved a new calendar beginning with South Carolina on February 3, followed three days later by New Hampshire and Nevada.[1]

New Hampshire balked at the plan, arguing that it had traditionally hosted the nation's opening primary — a rule Iowa had only circumvented by holding caucuses rather than a primary.[1] Top New Hampshire Democrats maintained that state law mandates hosting the first primary in the nation, and officials vowed to hold their contest before South Carolina's regardless of the DNC's decision.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the ballot news to California governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]]'s concurrent meeting with Chinese leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] in Beijing, a trip billed as a summit on climate change and the fentanyl crisis.[1] She predicted roughly a month until Biden was out and "Greasy Gavin" became the Democratic nominee, warning that with the 2024 election one year and twelve days away, "it's gonna get weird."[1]
