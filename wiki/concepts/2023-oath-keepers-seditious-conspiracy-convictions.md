# 2023 Oath Keepers Seditious Conspiracy Convictions

In January 2023, four members of the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]] were convicted of seditious conspiracy in Washington, D.C., in connection with the events of January 6, 2021.[1] Conservative outlet [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] reported the convictions under the framing that the four had been found guilty by a "DC Kangaroo Court" for standing outside the U.S. Capitol without weapons, marching up the Capitol steps in a stack and singing the national anthem.[1]

The four were described as the latest Trump supporters to be charged over attendance at the January 6 protests in Washington, D.C., on what the outlet characterised as fraudulent charges.[1] The proceedings were overseen by Obama-appointed judge [[amit-mehta|Amit Mehta]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]] contrasted the convictions with contemporaneous violent unrest in Atlanta by radical leftists, arguing that peaceful Trump supporters were being convicted of seditious conspiracy for singing the national anthem outside the Capitol.[1] He described the disparity as more than a double standard — a "two-tiered system of justice" designed to have a "chilling effect" on anyone considering protesting stolen elections in Arizona and elsewhere.[1]
