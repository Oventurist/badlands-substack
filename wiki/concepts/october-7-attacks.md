# October 7 Attacks

The October 7 attacks were the assault on southern [[israel|Israel]] launched from [[gaza|Gaza]] by [[hamas|Hamas]] in 2023, the event that precipitated the subsequent Gaza war and the diplomacy that produced the October 2025 U.S.-brokered ceasefire.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief treats the attacks primarily through a foreknowledge allegation. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites a report newly released by the Israeli government which, he writes, claims that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] knew about Hamas's plan to invade southern Israel as far back as early 2018. Because Netanyahu subsequently arranged for Hamas to receive tens of millions of dollars per month after the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] cut it off financially — money solicited from [[qatar|Qatar]] on the argument that Gaza faced a humanitarian crisis, then walked into Gaza and given to prominent families with known ties to Hamas — the commentary concludes that "if all of this is true, then that means that Netanyahu knowingly funded October 7th."

The Brief presents this as the author's interpretation rather than an established finding, framing it within his broader argument that Netanyahu has repeatedly sought pretexts for regional escalation, including war with [[iran|Iran]].
