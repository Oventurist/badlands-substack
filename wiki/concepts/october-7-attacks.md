# October 7th Attacks

The October 7, 2023 Hamas attacks on Israel serve, in Badlands Media's account, as the pivot point at which large parts of the American right abandoned their previously stated free-speech commitments.[1]

## Aftermath

The article describes the wake of the attacks as producing a surge of traumatic and hysterical social media posts followed by atrocity-focused content designed to build support for the bombing campaign in Gaza that everyone anticipated.[1] That campaign is placed in a sequence of prior Israeli operations: Summer Rains (2006), Autumn Clouds (2006), Cast Lead (2008–2009), Pillar of Defense (2012), Protective Edge (2014), Guardian of the Walls (2021) and Breaking Dawn (2022).[1]

## Political exploitation

As after September 11th, politicians moved to capitalize on public fear and anger to push legislation.[1] Conservative politicians began advocating censorship using arguments that mirrored those of liberal pundits during Trump's first term — that certain ideas must be suppressed because they could incite violence or endanger minority groups.[1] [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] forced the deactivation of a [[students-for-justice-in-palestine|Students for Justice in Palestine]] chapter within days, and [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] competed with him over pro-Israel positioning.[1] The [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]] reported some 1,200 antisemitic incidents on U.S. campuses between October 7, 2023 and September 24, 2024, mostly harassment and [[hate-speech|hate speech]] classifications.[1] The legislative products of this period include the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]] and [[executive-order-14188|Executive Order 14188]].[1]
