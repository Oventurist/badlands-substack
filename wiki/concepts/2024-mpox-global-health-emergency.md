# 2024 Mpox Global Health Emergency

The 2024 mpox global health emergency was the declaration issued on Wednesday, August 14, 2024, when the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] designated the ongoing mpox outbreak in Africa a public health emergency of international concern — the highest level of alarm available under international health law.[1]

## Background and declaration

The WHO convened its emergency committee amid concerns that a deadlier strain of the virus, clade Ib, had reached four previously unaffected countries in Africa, having been previously contained to the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of Congo]].[1] The committee's independent experts met virtually to advise Director-General [[tedros-adhanom-ghebreyesus|Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus]] on the severity of the outbreak, after which Tedros announced that he had accepted their advice and declared the emergency.[1]

Tedros described the situation as "very worrying," citing "the detection and rapid spread of a new clade of mpox in eastern DRC, its detection in neighboring countries that had not previously reported mpox and the potential for further spread within Africa and beyond."[1] He said that "it's clear that a coordinated international response is essential to stop these outbreaks and save lives," and the WHO's African contagion tracking reported more than 17,000 mpox cases in Africa since the beginning of the year.[1] Tedros framed the outbreak in terms of two clades — broad groups of viruses that have evolved over decades — one of which he characterized as highly transmissible and productive of severe disease.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the declaration as a rebranded revival of the earlier monkeypox scare rather than a genuine emergency. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that "MonkeyPox is back, with a new name and amped up fear porn," dismissed the WHO's case count as coming from "not-at-all-credible global medical authorities," and characterized Tedros as "the guy that was wrong about everything (but highly effective at spreading tyranny)" during [[covid-19-narrative|COVID]].[1] Her stated response to the call for a coordinated international response was twofold: refusal, and a demand to know what was planned "to the people in Africa (again)."[1] She predicted a U.S. mpox reboot would arrive not before Election Day but the following June.[1]

The declaration was published in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] edition covering the news of Wednesday, August 14, 2024, and was linked by Badlands writers to a broader [[information-war|information war]] pattern in which recurring health emergencies function as narrative pressure ahead of elections.[1]

## Related developments

The mpox narrative was invoked almost immediately in domestic U.S. politics, with some officials calling for the [[2024-democratic-national-convention|2024 Democratic National Convention]] to be delayed or cancelled for the betterment of public health.[1]
