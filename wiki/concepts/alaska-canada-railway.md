# Alaska-Canada Railway

The **Alaska-Canada Railway** is a 2,570 km rail project intended to connect Alaska for the first time with Canada and the lower 48 states. [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced federal support for the long-overdue project on September 26, 2020, announcing it in a tweet the same day.[1]

## Significance

Ehret describes the potential re-emergence of the railway, driven under a pro-Pacific and pro-Arctic development model, as the first genuine display of that paradigm in North America in decades.[1] If it survives the oncoming Environmental Impact Assessments and the Federal Government of Canada — which he characterizes as run by anti-development Rhodes Scholars and technocrats committed to depopulation and world government — it would open bountiful resources locked in the inaccessible Arctic, create tens of thousands of direct jobs and millions indirectly, and vector North America's economic destiny toward the growing Asian markets led by China.[1] Most importantly, in his framing, it would break the west free of the twofold trap of anti-development environmentalism on one side and pro-militarization right-wing views on the other, moving toward win-win cooperation with Eurasian partners.[1]

## Implementation

The Alaska-Alberta Railway Development Corporation was tasked by Trump with building and managing the $17 billion project, and features programs to tie North America into the Asian market and to integrate a North American transport system whose once-proud rail network has fallen derelict since WWII and the age of "highways and cars."[1] Whether such programs can proceed now that globalist arrangements such as NAFTA and the TPP have been jettisoned — giving nation states authority to exercise a dirigistic role in long-term economic planning — remains to be seen.[1] The railway is conceptually linked to the [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait Tunnel]] and the [[polar-silk-road|Polar Silk Road]].[1]
