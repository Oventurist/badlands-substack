# Secular Humanism

**Secular humanism** is described by Badlands Media as the name for atheistic morality.[1]

## Account given

In this system, objective truth rather than God is held to be the source of rights and values, expressed through formulations such as universally preferable behavior or mutually agreeable rights and values.[1] The article argues that the most brilliant atheists in history were never able to devise a genuinely alternative theory of the origin of rights and values, and ironically arrived at a conclusion similar to that of theists: in substance, whether one says rights come from God or from the truth, the two are conceptually the same.[1]

## Relation to the broader argument

This claim supports the article's wider thesis that [[objective-morality|objective morality]] — not religion as such — is the real safeguard against [[mass-psychosis]], and that the drift toward [[moral-relativism|moral relativism]] leaves populations vulnerable to [[menticide]].[1] At the same time the article treats the secularization of Western society, meaning the removal of active philosophy and theism, as well advanced and as a precondition for cultural degradation.[1]
