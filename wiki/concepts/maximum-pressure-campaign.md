# Maximum Pressure Campaign

The **maximum pressure campaign** is the sanctions policy [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] adopted toward [[iran|Iran]] after withdrawing the United States from the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|JCPOA]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

When Trump pulled out of the deal he threatened every country on Earth under the campaign, claiming there would be "enormous consequences" for evading sanctions on Iran.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] situates this within a wider reorientation: on taking office Trump reversed the working foreign policy consensus from maximum pressure on Russia to maximum pressure on Iran and [[communist-china|China]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) It was this maneuver, DeLarme writes, "perhaps more than anything else, that put the crosshairs on Trump's back."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

[[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s policy was the opposite: within two months of taking office he allowed China to sign a $400 billion agreement with Iran.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)


## Kellogg’s 2025 Revival Call

In January 2025, [[keith-kellogg|Keith Kellogg]], the incoming special envoy on the Ukraine conflict for President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], called for reviving the maximum pressure campaign against [[iran|Iran]] while speaking at a [[national-council-of-resistance-of-iran|NCRI]] event in Paris.[2] "These pressures are not just kinetic, not just military force, but they must be economic and diplomatic as well," he said, urging action "to change Iran for the better" without delay and arguing that the West "must exploit the weakness we now see."[2]
