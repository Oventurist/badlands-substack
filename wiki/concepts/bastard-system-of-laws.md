# Bastard System of Laws

"Bastard system of laws" is a phrase attributed to [[thomas-jefferson]] and used by Badlands Media as the organizing concept for its analysis of how a private credit issuer came to function as America's central bank.[1]

## Jefferson's warning

According to the article, Jefferson's writings warned that agents of the Crown plotted to recapture the United States after the Revolutionary War by means of a paper system of "money," the effect of which would "unite the destinies" of the United States and Great Britain once again under a "bastard system of laws."[1] Jefferson specifically warned that Crown agents had infiltrated the federalists and would use federalism to assimilate the governments of the United States and England.[1]

## Evidence cited

The article contends that this plan largely — though not completely — succeeded, pointing to two pieces of evidence.[1] Four of the first five [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] justices in the newly federalized Union were trained at [[middle-temple|the Middle Temple]] in London.[1] In addition, the state of Delaware still practices equity law in the same manner as it was practiced in England in the eighteenth century.[1]

## Meaning of the phrase

The author argues Jefferson chose the phrase deliberately: a "bastard system" suggests a *blending* of previously separate systems of law.[1] The article's remaining sections are devoted to explaining how two systems of law can operate in the same place at the same time — the answer being that different systems attach to different classes of *person* and different [[jurisdiction|jurisdictions]].[1]

Among the many types of law the article lists as diverging, recombining and encapsulating one another are Natural Law, the Law of Nations, Common Law, Civil Law, Admiralty Law, Maritime Law, Criminal Law, Constitutional Law, Ecclesiastical Law, Corporate Law and Trust Law.[1] The specific blend at issue is the coexistence of [[natural-law|Natural Law]], under which the people are sovereign, with Corporate Law, under which they are treated as customers and debtors of [[federal-reserve|the Federal Reserve]].[1] The bridge between the two, the article argues, is the dual citizenship created by [[fourteenth-amendment|the 14th Amendment]].[1] See [[state-citizenship]].
