# Maryland Three Airports

The **"Maryland Three"** are three small general-aviation airports located just outside Washington, D.C.: College Park Airport, Potomac Airfield, and Hyde Executive Field.[1]

## Role in the "Q for Dummies" reading

Chapter 8 of the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series argues that when Q Drop #67 draws attention to airports near the capital, it is unlikely to mean the two major D.C.-area fields — Dulles and Reagan — because both carry far too many eyes and both have restricted access and restricted airspace, making undetected movement difficult for bad actors.[1] The series therefore concludes that the Maryland Three are the likely airports Q was pointing to, since smaller private fields suit travelers who prefer relaxed screening.[1]

The argument rests on the same contrast the chapter draws between commercial and private security: private travel spares figures such as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] or [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] questions about their destinations, origins, or contacts, in a way the [[no-fly-list|no-fly list]] and standard [[transportation-security-administration|TSA]] screening do not.[1]
