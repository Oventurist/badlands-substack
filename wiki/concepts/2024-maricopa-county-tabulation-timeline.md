# 2024 Maricopa County Tabulation Timeline

The **2024 Maricopa County tabulation timeline** refers to the pre-election warning by officials in Arizona's most populous county that counting the November 5, 2024 general election could take nearly two weeks.[1]

## The announcement

At an October 22, 2024 press conference, [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] Deputy Elections Director Jennifer Liewer said officials expected tabulation of all incoming ballots to take between 10 and 13 days, and asked "for the community's patience."[1] She framed the delay as a function of competing goals: "We want to make sure that this is a secure process, but we also want to make sure that it is an accurate process."[1]

Assistant Maricopa County Manager Zach Schira told the same press conference that longer ballots and higher interest in the 2024 general election would create longer lines on Election Day, "and that's okay."[1] He advised voters wishing to avoid long lines to vote by mail or early in person.[1]

Badlands Media carried the item as a bonus entry in its October 28, 2024 News Brief, without extended commentary, amid its broader coverage of the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 general election]].[1]
