# Taylor Swift Pentagon Psyop Claims

The Taylor Swift Pentagon psyop claims refer to a January 2024 media controversy over suggestions that singer [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]] might be a "front for a covert political agenda" on behalf of the Biden administration.[1]

## Origin

The claims were aired on Tuesday, January 9, 2024 by Fox News host [[jesse-watters|Jesse Watters]] on his program Jesse Watters Primetime.[1] Watters questioned how Swift had soared in popularity over the years, noting that her sold-out Eras Tour had an economic impact greater than the GDP of fifty countries.[1] He said that "around four years ago, the Pentagon's psychological operations unit floated turning Taylor Swift into an asset during a NATO meeting," describing the asset in question as "a psyop for combating online misinformation."[1] He then aired a clip from a 2019 conference panel titled "Information Warfare: defining and analyzing" at the 11th International Conference on Cyber Conflict in Tallinn, Estonia.[1]

## Pentagon response

The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] responded on Wednesday, January 10, 2024 through spokesperson [[sabrina-singh|Sabrina Singh]], who denied the claims in a tongue-in-cheek statement to [[politico|Politico]] laden with references to Swift's catalogue.[1] "As for this conspiracy theory, we are going to shake it off," she said, referencing the song "Shake It Off," adding: "But that does highlight that we still need [[us-congress|Congress]] to approve our supplemental budget request as Swift-ly as possible so we can be out of the woods with potential fiscal concerns."[1] The exchange was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief of January 12, 2024.[1]
