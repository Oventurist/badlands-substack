# Jerusalem Day Flag March

The Jerusalem Day Flag March is an annual procession through Jerusalem's Old City marking the city's "reunification" in the wake of Israel's victory in the 1967 Six-Day War.[1]

## May 2025 march

On Monday 26 May 2025, tens of thousands of religious Zionist revelers thronged the alleyways of the Old City, chanting anti-Arab refrains as they paraded toward the Western Wall. Many celebrants were heard chanting "death to Arabs" and singing "may your village burn."[1] Before the march formally began in the afternoon, young marchers were already roving the Old City and skirmishing with Palestinian shopkeepers and police officers.[1]

It was the second time the march — a contentious event in its own right — had taken place amid the war in Gaza that began with Hamas's attack of 7 October 2023. Extremist Jewish youths attending the Flag March have been known to harass and beat Palestinians during the procession, particularly as it enters the Old City through the Muslim Quarter's Damascus Gate.[1]
