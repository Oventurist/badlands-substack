# Biden Valley Forge Speech (January 2024)

The Valley Forge speech was President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s first major campaign event of 2024, delivered near Valley Forge, Pennsylvania on Friday, January 5, on the eve of the third anniversary of the January 6, 2021 attack on the U.S. Capitol.[1]

## Content

Biden argued that democracy and fundamental freedoms were under threat if former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] returned to the [[white-house|White House]], calling Trump and far-right extremists a threat and a danger to the freedoms on which the country was founded and echoing themes from his 2020 campaign, which he had called "a battle for the soul of the nation."[1] In remarks that seemed especially personal, he opened by saying, "The topic of my speech today is deadly serious. And I think it needs to be made at the outset of this campaign."[1]

He continued: "Today we are here to answer the most important of questions: Is democracy still America's 'sacred cause'?" — repeating a phrase used by George Washington — and added, "This is not rhetorical, academic, or hypothetical. Whether democracy's still America's 'sacred cause' is the most urgent question of our time. And it's what the 2024 election is all about."[1]

## Preparation

Aides told [[abc-news|ABC News]] chief White House correspondent Mary Bruce that the president was closely involved in writing the speech, which followed a meeting he held that week with historians and scholars at the White House.[1] The item was carried as a bonus entry in the [[badlands-media|Badlands News Brief]] of January 8, 2024, whose headline framed the address as Biden "barking at MAGA hats."[1]
