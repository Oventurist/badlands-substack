# Save America (book)

*Save America* is a book by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] reported in late August 2024 as due for publication the following week.[1] It drew attention in the September 2, 2024 Badlands News Brief for a passage warning that anyone who illegally interferes in the 2024 election will be jailed for life.[1]

## Contents reported

Modernity News reported that in the book Trump singles out Facebook founder [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]], saying he will "spend the rest of his life in prison" if meddling is proven.[1] Trump wrote that he is "watching him closely" ahead of the November election.[1] Recounting his time in office, Trump said Zuckerberg would visit him and "bring his very nice wife to dinners, be as nice as anyone could be," while secretly mounting a "plot against the President," adding: "He told me there was nobody like Trump on Facebook. But at the same time, and for whatever reason, steered it against me."[1] The passage concludes: "We are watching him closely, and if he does anything illegal this time he will spend the rest of his life in prison – as will others who cheat in the 2024 presidential election."[1]

## Context and commentary

The reporting tied the warning to [[facebook|Facebook]]'s role in censoring the Hunter Biden laptop story before the 2020 election on the basis of claims that it was a Russian disinformation operation, claims later shown to be false; some observers argued the suppression of that story could have changed the outcome of the vote.[1]

The Badlands contributor Ashe in America welcomed the book, comparing it favorably to *Art of the Deal* and arguing that making the "criminal cartel" fear getting caught is a sound strategy in an information war fought over the mind.[1] The commentary noted that Zuckerberg had apologized the previous week for caving to government censorship requests, producing what the writer called an interesting set of plot points timed alongside the book's release.[1]
