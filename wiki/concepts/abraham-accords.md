# Abraham Accords

The Abraham Accords are a series of normalisation agreements between [[israel|Israel]] and several Muslim-majority states, originally brokered during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first presidency. As described in the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," Trump listed the existing membership as the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], [[bahrain|Bahrain]], [[morocco|Morocco]], [[sudan|Sudan]] and [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]].

## Proposed expansion, May 2026

In a [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement reproduced in the Brief, Trump argued that the Accords should be expanded as part of a wider settlement with [[iran|Iran]]. Following talks with regional leaders, he said that it "should be mandatory that all of these Countries, at a minimum, simultaneously, sign onto the Abraham Accords," naming [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], the United Arab Emirates (already a member), [[qatar|Qatar]], [[pakistan|Pakistan]], [[turkey|Türkiye]], [[egypt|Egypt]], [[jordan|Jordan]] and Bahrain (already a member). He allowed that "one or two" might have reason not to sign, and that this would be accepted, but held that most should be prepared to make the settlement with Iran "a far more Historic Event than it would, otherwise, be."

Trump characterised the Accords' record for existing members as "a Financial, Economic, and Social BOOM, even during this time of Conflict and War," noting that no member had suggested leaving or pausing, and predicted that a broadened agreement would "bring true Power, Strength, and Peace to the Middle East for the first time in 5,000 years."

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the signalling around the expanded Accords as evidence that a comprehensive Middle East deal has already been agreed among the relevant parties and is nearing public disclosure. In his framing, Trump is calling for a "Macro Region of Responsibility" in the Middle East that includes Iran itself, an outcome he presents as the resolution of the "Forever War Gordian Knot" of the region.
