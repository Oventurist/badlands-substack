# 2023 OPEC+ Production Cut

The 2023 OPEC+ production cut was a surprise decision announced at the start of April 2023 by [[opec|OPEC+]] producers to remove roughly one million barrels per day of crude from the market, including a voluntary cut of 500,000 barrels per day by [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] beginning in May.[1] Oil prices surged in early trading after the announcement.[1]

## White House response

A spokesperson for the [[national-security-council|National Security Council]] at the [[white-house|White House]] responded that "output cuts aren't advisable right now given market uncertainty," adding that the administration "will continue to work with all producers and consumers to ensure energy markets support economic growth and lower prices for American consumers."[1]

## Analysis

[[helima-croft|Helima Croft]], head of commodity strategy at RBC Capital Markets, described the cut as evidence that Saudi Arabia was pursuing an economic strategy independent of the United States following the deterioration of relations between Riyadh and Washington during the [[joe-biden|Biden administration]].[1] She called it "a Saudi-first policy," noting that the Kingdom was "making new friends, as we saw with China," in reference to the Beijing-brokered rapprochement between Saudi Arabia and [[iran|Iran]], and said the Kingdom was signalling that "it's no longer a unipolar world."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the cut through the lens of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] theory, under which sovereign factions abroad advance financial and economic objectives that leave the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] hemmed in and globalist trade systems "teetering on the brink of extinction."[1] He characterised the Kingdom's move as a public and humiliating rebuke of the Biden administration, concluding that "power is power" and that Saudi Arabia was reminding the West of that fact "one barrel—or lack thereof—at a time."[1] The episode is treated within Badlands writing as a marker of the emerging [[multipolar-world|multipolar world]].[1]
