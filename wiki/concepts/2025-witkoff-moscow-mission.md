# 2025 Witkoff Moscow Mission

The 2025 Witkoff Moscow mission was a diplomatic trip by US special envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] to the Russian capital in early August 2025, undertaken as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s deadline for imposing new sanctions over the Ukraine conflict approached.[1]

## Purpose and context

Witkoff travelled to Moscow to meet Russian leadership as Washington attempted to persuade President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] to sign a ceasefire agreement with [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] Trump had grown significantly frustrated with Putin in the preceding weeks and gave him until that Friday to make progress toward peace or face tougher sanctions.[1] Reporting indicated Trump was considering new sanctions on Russia's "shadow fleet" of oil tankers if no ceasefire was agreed by the deadline.[1]

## Arrival

Witkoff arrived in Moscow on Wednesday, August 6, 2025, according to state media, and was greeted by Russia's investment envoy [[kirill-dmitriev|Kirill Dmitriev]], a source told Reuters.[1] The Kremlin said it did not rule out a meeting between Witkoff and Putin.[1] Trump had said earlier that Witkoff might travel to Moscow that week in yet another diplomatic effort to end the hostilities.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Chris Paul argued that the emerging settlement amounted to the status quo ante — that all the disputed regions had been parts of Russia for years and the outcome matched what had been agreed before what he termed "Putin's Very Brutal Invasion."[1] He contended that such an outcome "shouldn't be possible if a huge war occurred."[1]
