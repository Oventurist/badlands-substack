# The Mukaab

The Mukaab is a $50 billion cube-shaped megastructure under construction in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, part of the New Murabba development and one of a wave of futuristic building projects across the kingdom.[1] Construction officially broke ground in October 2024, with the building billed as the "world's largest building."[1]

## Design and scale

The Mukaab is planned at roughly 1,300 feet high and 1,200 feet wide, giving it sufficient internal volume to contain twenty Empire State Buildings.[1] It is to offer fine dining, retail, offices and restaurants across roughly 2 million square feet of floor space, functioning as a self-contained "downtown-in-a-box."[1] Descriptions of the concept portray a tower set inside a giant domed interior whose walls act as an immense projection surface rendering hyper-realistic landscapes.[1]

## Rationale and commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the enclosed-volume concept, while superficially absurd, is intelligible given the site: unlike Jeddah, Dubai or Abu Dhabi, Riyadh sits inland in the Arabian Desert without sea views or coastal breezes, so an interior capable of simulating an outdoor experience has practical appeal.[1]

The project is presented as part of [[mohammed-bin-salman|Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman]]'s broader effort to diversify Saudi Arabia beyond oil alongside [[neom|Neom]] and the [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]].[1] Badlands commentary noted reports that Saudi construction could absorb 20 percent of the world's steel supply over the following decade, with the kingdom — not a major steel producer — sourcing heavily from China and India, and framed this as a warning about the state of American industry.[1]
