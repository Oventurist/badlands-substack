# Frederick Douglas Trafficking Prevention Act

The Frederick Douglas Trafficking Prevention Act is the name given in 2018 to anti-trafficking legislation originally proposed in 2017 by Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] as a new version of the [[trafficking-victims-protection-act|Trafficking Victims Protection Act]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

In a March 13, 2025 article, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] noted that numerous subsequent acts had been passed since 2000 that refine or add to the original TVPA, and singled out Grassley's 2017 proposal, which was renamed the Frederick Douglas Trafficking Prevention Act the following year — a renaming the author said he could not explain.[1] He connected the sponsorship to the [[qanon|Q]] instruction to "trust Grassley," presenting the senator's authorship of the successor legislation as consistent with that guidance.[1] The author acknowledged that he had not yet examined the later acts in detail, and directed readers to the [[us-department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s key legislation page for further material on the TVPA and its successors.[1]
