# 2023 Disney Streaming Content Write-Off

The 2023 Disney streaming content write-off was a $1.5 billion impairment charge recorded by [[walt-disney-company|The Walt Disney Company]] in its June 2023 fiscal quarter after the company removed dozens of shows and films from Disney+ and Hulu.[1]

## Details

In an SEC filing made public on Friday, June 2, 2023, Disney stated that on May 26, 2023 it had removed "certain produced content" from its direct-to-consumer streaming services, and would consequently record a $1.5 billion impairment charge in its fiscal third quarter to adjust the carrying value of those content assets to fair value.[1] The company said it was continuing to review content across its platforms and anticipated that additional produced content would be removed, largely during the remainder of the third fiscal quarter, potentially producing further impairment charges of up to about $400 million.[1]

Chief financial officer [[christine-mccarthy|Christine McCarthy]] had signalled the move on Disney's prior earnings call, projecting a write-down of $1.5 billion to $1.8 billion.[1] Writing down the assets allowed Disney to remove them from its balance sheet and reduce its tax bill.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the removals as a purge of "some of the WOKEST of the woke content" from Disney+ intended to generate tax write-offs offsetting two years of losses in streaming and theatrical release, and noted the company also faced pending litigation with the state of Florida that analysts suggested could cost tens of billions of dollars.[1] The commentary grouped Disney with [[bud-light|Bud Light]] and [[target|Target]] as brands suffering consumer backlash, predicting that "the whiplash is only beginning."[1]
