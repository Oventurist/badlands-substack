# White House Underground Complex

The White House underground complex is a military construction project that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said in late March 2026 the US military is building beneath the new [[white-house-ballroom|White House ballroom]].[1]

## Disclosure

Speaking to reporters aboard Air Force One on a Sunday night, Trump described a "massive complex" under construction and said the details had become public after a "stupid lawsuit" forced elements of the project into the open.[1] He said construction was already underway and that the military was "doing very well" with the work.[1]

## Features

Trump said the structure includes enhanced security features such as bulletproof glass and drone-resistant roofing and ceilings, saying the upgrades reflect the realities of modern threats.[1] He added that the national security component of the project was originally intended to remain classified, that the military "wanted it more than anybody," and that it became public due to what he called "unpatriotic" disclosures.[1]
