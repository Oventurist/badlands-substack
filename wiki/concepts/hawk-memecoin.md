# HAWK Memecoin

HAWK ($HAWK) is a cryptocurrency memecoin tied to internet celebrity [[haliey-welch|Haliey "Hawk Tuah Girl" Welch]] that launched in December 2024, crashed within hours, and became the subject of an investor lawsuit alleging violations of federal securities law.[1]

## Launch and collapse

HAWK gained popularity almost entirely through its association with Welch's viral persona.[1] Its price collapsed within hours of launch, wiping out many retail buyers and triggering insider-trading allegations.[1]

## Litigation

A lawsuit filed in a District Court of New York in December 2024 by twelve plaintiffs who lost a combined $151,000 alleged that HAWK acted as an unregistered security under established legal precedent and caused substantial financial damage to cryptocurrency investors.[1] The complaint asserted that the token drew in first-time crypto investors, that a common enterprise arose from the pooling of funds, and that the token's value depended on the efforts of Welch and her team, satisfying the criteria for a security.[1] It also alleged that a wallet controlled by the Tuah Foundation, an entity associated with the project, collected $3 million in transaction fees.[1] The plaintiffs sought a jury trial.[1]

## Regulatory implications

Badlands commentary by Ashe in America described HAWK as emblematic of the "back alley slot machine" of unregulated memecoin gambling, and predicted the case would provide authorities — particularly the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]] — the pretext to impose new legislation and regulation on crypto markets.[1]
