# Rio Earth Summit (1992)

The 1992 Rio Summit, overseen by [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]], is described in Badlands Media commentary as having "established a new era in the consolidation of NGOs and corporations under the 'green' agenda."[1]

## Outcomes

The doctrine advanced at Rio was formalized in Agenda 21 — later renamed [[agenda-2030|Agenda 2030]] — and in the [[earth-charter|Earth Charter]], co-authored between 1996 and 2000 by former Soviet Premier [[mikhail-gorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]] and Strong, with the international drafting committee chaired by [[steven-rockefeller|Steven Rockefeller]].[1]

## Aftermath for Strong

After the Rio conference, Strong took up the leadership of Canada's nuclear power sector as head of Ontario Hydro from 1992 to 1995, where the article says he "went to war with the entire Canadian nuclear industry," using every available means to cancel refurbishments, new reactor designs and research and development funding in favour of "sustainable" energy pathways.[1] Some of that damage was later reversed, but the source argues Canada's nuclear industry has continued to decay and its fusion energy programme was destroyed outright.[1]

## Interpretation

Rio is treated as the institutional hinge between the 1970s [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] takeover of western policy and the later [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] agendas, all of which the source characterizes as expressions of [[malthusianism|Malthusian]] limits-to-growth ideology.[1]
