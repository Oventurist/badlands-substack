# Enemy of the State

Enemy of the State is a 1998 American thriller film starring [[gene-hackman|Gene Hackman]] as an intelligence-community "white hat" and Will Smith as a labor lawyer.[1] Following Hackman's death in February 2025, Badlands contributor Ashe in America described it as her favorite Hackman film and argued that its themes had become newly relevant.[1]

## Plot and themes

In the film, a congressman is assassinated and a birdwatching whistleblower captures the killing on video; the assassination turns out to have been carried out by the government, which subsequently targets the birdwatcher and anyone else who learns of its secrets.[1] Before his own death, the birdwatcher slips the footage to an unsuspecting labor lawyer who is already entangled in a dispute with Italian organized crime.[1] The lawyer's life is then dismantled using [[national-security-agency|NSA]] capabilities that officially were not supposed to exist — the very capabilities at issue in the legislation whose opponent was assassinated.[1] The Badlands commentary characterized the resulting story as an "off books Operation Gladio adventure" that showcases the methods of the surveillance state.[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

The film was released three years before the Patriot Act, a chronology Badlands cited in arguing that its civil-liberties questions were prescient.[1] Ashe in America encouraged readers to watch it, writing that the questions it raises "are more important now than ever because, at this moment, we may actually have the power to do something about it."[1] The film was also invoked in Badlands commentary on the dismissal of more than 100 intelligence-community employees over explicit chatrooms, where the writer paired it with the disclosures of Edward Snowden as reference points for NSA surveillance capabilities.[1]
