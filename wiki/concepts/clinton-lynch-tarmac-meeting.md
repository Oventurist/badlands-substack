# Clinton–Lynch Tarmac Meeting

The Clinton–Lynch tarmac meeting refers to the private encounter between former president [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and then-Attorney General [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]], an episode singled out in Q drop #36 of November 2, 2017 and discussed at length in the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" treatment of drops #31–40.[1]

## Q drop treatment

Drop #36 mentions Clinton and Lynch and their meeting directly. The Badlands commentary reports the "scuttlebutt" that Lynch "was offered a SCOTUS position in order to stick to the Russiagate lie and play her part" — that is, that a Supreme Court appointment was dangled in exchange for her cooperation in shielding [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[1]

The drop is further read as indicating that the [[national-security-agency|NSA]], under the direction of Admiral [[mike-rogers|Mike Rogers]], "had all the info they needed on the tarmac meeting, and more." The commentary notes as an aside that Rogers was the 17th Director of the NSA, a numerological point of interest to Q readers.[1]

## Related sequence

In the same drop, Q is read as indicating that [[james-comey|James Comey]] "was pushed to do what he did at the time he did by the good guys," with the commentary arguing that Comey's reopening of the email investigation materially affected Hillary Clinton's standing in the 2016 election, and that Comey may therefore have been operating on behalf of the [[q-operation|Q operation]].[1]

## Comey's response and the leak hunt

"Setting the Stage" adds a detail about the bureau's internal handling of the episode.[2] Joe Lange cites Newsweek reporting that as a senior official [[david-bowdich|David Bowdich]] was involved in conversations following press reports of the tarmac meeting between then-Attorney General [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] and former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] while the FBI's probe of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was ongoing, and that documents show Bowdich and [[james-comey|James Comey]] discussed those reports by email.[2]

Lange reads the emails as evidence that Comey was in cover-up mode and wanted to identify who had leaked the story, and asserts that the leak came from the military intelligence "[[wizards-and-warlocks|Wizards and Warlocks]]."[2] Because Bowdich retains that correspondence, Lange treats him as holding documentary leverage over Comey.[2]

## The tarmac meeting in "The Biggest Boomerang In History"

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats the tarmac meeting as the leverage point that broke [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] away from the [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] camp.[3] He quotes Q drop 36 of November 2, 2017, which asks what was negotiated on the tarmac between [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and Lynch at a time when Clinton's victory was expected, suggests the [[wizards-and-warlocks|wizards and warlocks]] tipped off a local reporter to the supposedly unscheduled stop, and states that the NSA under Admiral Mike Rogers had the meeting miscatalogued under a false identity so bad actors could not locate it while being assured no logs existed.[3] The drop further asks whether [[james-comey|Comey]] was thereby forced into the spotlight, not by choice, just before the election.[3]

Lange writes that the Inspector General report proved the meeting had been arranged in advance, and that [[donald-trump|Trump]] knew the Clintons were offering Lynch a Supreme Court seat in exchange for dropping the Clinton investigation — a reading he supports with Q drop 1147 of April 15, 2018, which pairs a "Supreme Court/Loretta Lynch deal drop" with a Trump tweet and the tarmac.[3] Because the story broke in June 2016, Comey was pushed into publicly exonerating Clinton the following month while Lynch escaped scrutiny, which Lange explains by asserting that Lynch flipped and has since given evidence to grand juries.[3] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
