# Kostensuchus atrox

Kostensuchus atrox is an extinct crocodile-relative species identified by scientists in Argentina and reported in August 2025.[1]

## Discovery and description

The fossil was discovered in Argentina's Chorrillo Formation and dates back approximately 70 million years, to the late Cretaceous period.[1] Researchers classified the animal as a "hypercarnivore" and an apex predator, estimating it at around 3.5 metres long and weighing 250 kilograms.[1]

Its powerful jaw and large teeth enabled it to prey on medium-sized dinosaurs, making it the second-largest predator known from the site.[1] Despite the association with dinosaur fauna, Kostensuchus atrox was not a dinosaur but an extinct peirosaurid crocodyliform related to modern crocodiles.[1]

The find was carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief, sourced to The Independent.[1]
