# 2025 Trump–Putin Budapest Summit Announcement

The **2025 Trump–Putin Budapest summit announcement** followed a two-hour telephone call on October 16, 2025 between U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], their first publicly reported extended conversation in the two months since their summit in Anchorage, Alaska.[1] Although the full content of the call was not disclosed, the two leaders agreed to meet again in person.[1]

## Announced terms

The leaders planned to meet in Budapest, [[hungary|Hungary]], with no fixed date announced; Trump said the meeting would take place "within two weeks or so, pretty quick."[1] He added that "high-level" advisers from both sides would meet the following week.[1] Writing on Truth Social, Trump described the aim of the meeting as bringing the "inglorious" war between [[russia|Russia]] and [[ukraine|Ukraine]] to an end.[1] Hungary's Prime Minister, [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]], said preparations for the summit were already underway, and Putin's special envoy characterized the call as "positive and productive."[1]

The call came on the eve of Trump's White House meeting with Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], and Trump indicated the two would discuss his conversation with Putin.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] focused on Trump's choice of the word "inglorious," noting that the film *Inglourious Basterds* concerns the hunting of Nazis while Putin frames the Ukraine war as a de-Nazification effort, and observing that the exchange coincided with Putin and [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] discussing the plight of children.[1]
