# BBC January 6 Footage Editing Scandal

The **BBC January 6 footage editing scandal** is a 2025 controversy in which the British public broadcaster was accused of splicing footage of a speech by U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] delivered on January 6, 2021, so as to create the impression that he was inciting supporters to riot at the Capitol.[1]

## Fallout

The accusations forced the resignation of the [[bbc|BBC]]'s director general and its head of news.[1] Trump reportedly threatened to sue the broadcaster for $1 billion.[1]

## Lavrov's intervention

Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] told Russian journalists on Tuesday, November 11, 2025, that attempts to deflect from the BBC's responsibility for the scandal were "a disgrace."[1] Framing the episode as one of several instances of "unprofessional and harmful coverage of certain events" by international — and primarily British — media, Lavrov said: "What is happening with the BBC now is well known. The fact that some 'figures' are trying to justify what happened and are talking about some kind of staged campaign is a disgrace."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry characterized the episode as a "tag-team pincer move" by Trump and the Russians against legacy media, arguing that the mainstream press was being overwhelmed by what he termed the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] after Trump had "collected a scalp."[1] He singled out outgoing BBC director general [[tim-davie|Tim Davie]] for particular scorn, saying Davie should never again work in media journalism.[1]
