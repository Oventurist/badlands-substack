# Shoulder-Fired Missiles

Shoulder-fired surface-to-air missiles (SAMs, or MANPADS) are the physical thread running through the article's account of the Libya-to-Syria weapons pipeline.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Under the Gaddafi regime the Libyan armed forces acquired sophisticated Russian-made Grinch SA-24 missiles, capable of downing a warplane at up to 11,000 feet.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The Times reported that ships carrying such missiles from Libya anchored off the Lebanese coast, with weapons ferried ashore under cover of darkness and passed to Syrian rebel fighters, according to an arms dealer known as Abu Ahmad.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) A western official said that of an estimated pre-revolution stock of 25,000 SAMs, fewer than 5,000 units had been accounted for: "Access is easy."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Human Rights Watch reported widespread looting of thousands of SA-24 and SA-7 missiles, raising concern in western intelligence agencies that they would reach Al Qaeda.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The article treats the missing 20,000 as purposeful, arguing [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[john-mccain|John McCain]] smuggled them out to arm [[isis|ISIS]] against Bashar al-Assad.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Former [[cia|CIA]] officers warned the [[white-house|White House]] for months that thousands of shoulder-fired missiles were reaching Al Qaeda, to no effect.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Related weapons reached other theaters: a new-generation Stinger fired at a U.S. CH-47 in Kunar province on July 25, 2012 was traced to a CIA-signed lot, and sources believed [[qatar|Qatar]] delivered 50–60 such Stingers and 200 SA-24 Igla-S missiles to the Taliban in early 2012.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
