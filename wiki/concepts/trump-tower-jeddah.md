# Trump Tower Jeddah

**Trump Tower Jeddah** is a planned luxury development in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, announced on July 1, 2024 as the second collaboration between [[dar-global|Dar Global]] and [[trump-organization|The Trump Organization]].[1] The project marks Dar Global's debut in Jeddah and is aimed at the luxury Saudi market and international investors, advancing the developer's strategy of expanding across key cities in the Kingdom.[1]

## Announcement

Dar Global chief executive [[ziad-el-chaar|Ziad El Chaar]] said the tower would build on the "tremendous success" of [[trump-international-oman|Trump International Oman]] and would leverage the strengths of both organizations to attract international investors and vacationers.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the Jeddah announcement as the headline development, noting that Jeddah is Saudi Arabia's Red Sea port city with an upscale beach-town component, and linking the project to [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s stated ambition to turn the Red Sea coast into an iconic tourist destination comparable to the French Riviera.[1] He argued that bringing the Trump brand to the waterfront was a major step toward that goal, and paired the news with reports of Emirati and Saudi developers investing hundreds of millions of dollars in U.S. construction for the first time.[1] The same commentary noted, as a caveat to the optimistic framing, that "forever wars" were the principal threat to the trend.[1]
