# Leftism

**Leftism** is analyzed in Badlands Media commentary by the contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] not merely as a political position but as a psychological configuration and, in his strongest formulation, as "a MASS GROOMING operation designed primarily to work on a particular type of mind."[1]

## The "Predators and Prey" Thesis

The author has repeatedly argued that the political left comprises entirely of *predators* and *prey*.[1] He acknowledges the claim sounds uncharitable or oversimplified, and clarifies its limits: he is not claiming that all leftists are evil — some are, but not all — and he notes that predatory and cult-like dynamics also exist on the right, naming (without identifying them) a couple of active "cults of personality" on the right that he regards as morally equivalent to the worst of the left.[1] He speaks, he says, as someone who was himself groomed into extremist leftism and subsequently woke up from it.[1] The analogy he offers is alcoholism: the problem cannot be dealt with until it is correctly identified.[1]

## Relationship to Liberalism

The article distinguishes leftism from [[liberalism]] while insisting the two are closely linked: liberalism can easily give way to leftism if fully divorced from certain indispensable values found, in the author's view, nowhere other than in right-wing [[conservatism]].[1] The environment created by liberalism is described as necessary for leftism to emerge; leftism is characterized as "the out of control, spoiled child of liberalism."[1]

## Psychological Account

Leftism is said to deny people genuine contact with reality and thereby to rob them of the opportunity to know themselves, which in turn drives leftists into ideological extremity in pursuit of a self-identity that validates them.[1] The author reports that by age eight he believed the world was ending because of global warming and specifically because of people like him — described in his schooling as evil, greedy, polluting, warmongering White males — and that before reaching double digits he carried the guilt of genocide, slavery, and ecocide as though he had personally caused them.[1] Such a worldview, he argues, constitutes an extreme break from reality that can only stabilize with the help of nonstop hypnotic, abusive messaging.[1]

He states as an "iron law" of human development that one can either teach a child self-love and self-respect — enabling them to navigate the world and set boundaries — or teach them self-hatred and thereby prime them to be exploited by predators, but not both simultaneously.[1] He connects this directly to [[grooming-gangs|the rape gangs operating in the UK]], which he says often begin grooming young British girls by playing on their "White guilt" and then relieving that guilt through feigned affection and gifts.[1] He argues those girls likely received an education comparable to his own American schooling.[1]

## Mental Health and In-Group Bias

Liberals and leftists are said to have significantly higher rates of diagnosed mental illness and correspondingly higher pharmaceutical consumption, a disparity the author attributes to a missing ingredient: conservatism's grounding in love of one's own people and country.[1] Conservatives are described as having higher in-group bias — being inherently proud of who they are in a way liberals on average are not — which he says explains why liberals and leftists are far easier to persuade that the total transformation of their society by mass migration is desirable.[1]

The author qualifies that leftist collectivism is real but is loyalty to ideologies that provide a sense of safety or dopamine reward, rather than genuine kinship loyalty.[1]

## r/K Framing

Leftism is framed within [[r-k-selection-theory|r/K selection theory]] as an r-selected survival and replication strategy that emerges in conditions of material abundance, which is offered as the explanation for the long-observed leftward drift of prosperous societies.[1] The mind susceptible to leftist grooming is described as the "r-selected" mind.[1]

## Conclusion of the Argument

The author concludes that the left's psychology makes it highly susceptible to forms of programming — which he equates with hypnosis — that can render adherents extremely destructive to social health and stability, and that anyone supporting the invasion of their own country, even unwittingly, is "willing prey."[1] He expresses hope that when the mind-control apparatus of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] is dismantled, most leftists will snap out of it, and that [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]] can help.[1] Describing leftism as "a psycho-social prison that runs on broken amygdalas, malfunctioning dopamine circuits, and existential-grade self-hatred," he says overcoming his own leftist programming felt like escaping hell, and that every leftist who genuinely wants to do the right thing deserves the chance to escape it.[1]
