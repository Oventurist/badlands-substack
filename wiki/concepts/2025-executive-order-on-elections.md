# 2025 Executive Order on Elections

In the spring of 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed an executive order addressing the conduct of United States elections.[1] In remarks accompanying the signing, Trump characterized American elections as "fake," a framing that election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] cited while staking out a more qualified position of his own.[1]

## Reception

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on April 10, 2025, Keshel said he was "not so far down the rabbit hole" as to believe Trump had won an entirely fake election, given the years of lawfare and even assassination attempts the president had survived before returning to office, and argued that campaigning, vote-getting and fortune still played a real — if declining — role in the 2024 race.[1] He positioned the order within a broader argument that structural features such as [[automatic-voter-registration|Automatic Voter Registration]], universal [[mail-in-voting|mail-in voting]] and [[ballot-harvesting|ballot harvesting]] give incumbent-aligned interests a systematic edge, and that only full transparency, uniform federal rules and meaningful consequences for violators would end what he called "quasi-elections."[1] A comprehensive breakdown of the order was published on Keshel's own Substack, Captain K's Corner.[1]
