# Gold Standard

The **gold standard** is the monetary regime under which currency is redeemable in gold. In the United States it spanned from 1873 to 1933.[1]

## Badlands Media account

The article holds that the only way the [[fractional-reserve-lending|fractional reserve lending]] scam could be stopped was if the people demanded their gold back en masse; getting off the gold standard was therefore a priority of the bankers.[1] Living in a goldless monetary system, citizens have less power: they can no longer demand their gold back from bankers, which gives banks the ability to manipulate society at will.[1]

The gold standard was inaugurated by the [[coinage-act-of-1873|Coinage Act of 1873]], which ended silver coinage and produced deflation that increased the buying power of the currency.[1] The article associates it with the Founding Fathers' [[hard-money|hard money]] tradition of tradable coins.[1]

## Modern advocacy

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] nominated [[judy-shelton|Judy Shelton]] to the [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve]] because she was an advocate of bringing back the gold standard.[1]
