# Intuition

**Intuition**, in the usage of Part 4 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]] (15 February 2025), is the name given to the human subconscious mind's capacity to perceive truth directly, without the aid of the physical senses.[1] The essay by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] treats this faculty as the central vulnerability that the cabal's system of mass indoctrination is designed to neutralise, and as the faculty that the [[q-operation|Q operation]] is designed to vindicate and equip.[1]

## Logical grounding in the CTMU

The article's argument is that intuition need no longer be defended as a matter of belief, because the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe]] supplies a logical framework establishing beyond a shadow of a doubt that non-local information transfer from any part of the universe to a given human mind is possible.[1] Since, on [[chris-langan|Chris Langan]]'s account, each person is a "secondary image" of the living language that constitutes reality, every human both receives and transmits signals to and from the universe itself.[1] This is offered as the reason it matters that ordinary people at least know of the CTMU's existence, even without mastering its technical content.[1]

## Layered mind and translation

The essay describes the human mind as layered, so that what is picked up intuitively at the subconscious level must be *translated* into a form readable by the conscious mind.[1] For that reason a certain degree of intellectual rigour and mental stillness is held to be necessary to unlock what is buried in the subconscious.[1] The author concedes that one is not always reading the signals accurately, but insists that the signals are being received either way.[1] He appeals to common experience for corroboration — a bad feeling about a person that is later vindicated, or thinking of someone unseen for years and hearing from them the same day — and reports that in more than a decade of asking, everyone he has questioned recalls such experiences.[1]

## Suppression and discreditation

The article holds that intuition itself cannot be switched off, since "the subconscious mind doesn't come with an 'off' button," but that access to it can be thwarted by filling a mind with false beliefs.[1] Cabal propaganda is described as installing a shield estranging the mind from its intuitive capacities and as prompting the propagandized to install the same shield in others, a process the author calls [[zombification|zombification]].[1]

Because the intuitively gifted are said to have always posed the greatest threat to the cabal — being most likely to discover the truth despite the firewall of propaganda — the essay argues that simple trickery is insufficient against them and that special programs are required to discredit intuition itself and intuitive people broadly.[1] Much of the "new age" movement is presented as serving this function, the test offered being whether the phrase "intuitively gifted" calls to mind a charlatan selling Tarot readings or online spiritual coaching.[1] [[hollywood|Hollywood]] is described as the other half of this strategy, discrediting researchers by making fact and fiction indistinguishable.[1]

## The hundredth-monkey threshold

A further reason given for the cabal's concern is a threshold effect: if too many intuitively gifted people awaken to the truth, the essay claims, "the 100th monkey effect starts to kick in, and their awareness of the truth starts to 'seep through' to everyone else."[1] The [[q-proofs|Q proofs]] are cast as the instrument that lets such people move beyond hunches into logical and mathematical proof, accelerating the process enough to thwart the new world order's endgame.[1]
