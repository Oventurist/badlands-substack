# Project Cedar

Project Cedar is a wholesale digital-currency pilot program sponsored by the [[federal-reserve-bank-of-new-york|New York Federal Reserve]] that, according to reporting summarized by [[breitbart|Breitbart]], would convert regular U.S. dollars into a "brand-new" type of dollar with what the report described as massive implications for all American citizens.[1]

## Origins

On November 15, 2022, several of the world's biggest banks agreed to the plan, which the reporting characterized as one that could lead to a complete overthrow of the U.S. dollar as it is currently known.[1] Participants named in the coverage include banking giants such as Wells Fargo, Citigroup, HSBC and Mastercard, among others.[1]

## Commentary and interpretation

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that, if macroeconomist and CIA advisor [[jim-rickards|Jim Rickards]] is to be believed, Project Cedar — in addition to [[executive-order-14067|Executive Order 14067]] — could be one of several avenues leading to the creation of a new currency.[1] DeLarme framed the pilot within a broader thesis that globalists at Davos are eager to roll out what he called an "unholy union between technocracy and transhumanism," and that overthrowing the U.S. dollar in favor of a trackable [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]] is a critical part of that plan.[1] He noted that the Breitbart article contained a link to a video presentation by Rickards for readers interested in the topic.[1]
