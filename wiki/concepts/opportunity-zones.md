# Opportunity Zones

**Opportunity Zones** are a federal tax-incentive program created under the 2017 [[tax-cuts-and-jobs-act|Tax Cuts and Jobs Act]] to stimulate economic development and job creation by encouraging long-term private investment in designated low-income neighborhoods.[1] Investors who place capital gains into qualified funds targeting these census tracts receive preferential tax treatment on those investments, with the benefit scaling according to how long the capital remains committed.[1]

## Role in the Trump economic record

Badlands Media's April 2023 compilation of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s economic accomplishments treated Opportunity Zones as one of the signature structural achievements of his first term, and as concrete follow-through on his stated intention to drive economic and employment opportunity into inner cities and distressed areas.[1] The article linked the program directly to labor-market outcomes, arguing that the incentives worked and that minority unemployment fell across the board as a result — pairing the initiative with the record-low Black unemployment rate reported during the same period.[1]

The program is presented in that framing as part of a broader domestic policy orientation aimed at the middle class and lower-income households rather than at established capital centers, alongside falling welfare participation and rising middle-class incomes.[1]
