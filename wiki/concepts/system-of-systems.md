# System of Systems

"System of Systems" is a framing used by Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to describe the [[deep-state|Deep State]] not as a single conspiratorial organization but as an interlocking architecture of institutions, incentives, and ideologies.[1]

Burning Bright has noted that while the framing seems apparent to Anons and the Truth Community, it would likely confuse the vast majority of "normies" across the sociopolitical spectrum — a state of affairs he does not expect to be permanent.[1] In his account, the process of awakening is not merely about waking people to the existence of a Deep State criminal cabal or the enemies of the MAGA movement, but about "exposing the core tenets of centralization, collectivism and globalism itself, and all the dark pillars that hold it together," which he traces in part to the Prussian empire he argues has been largely memory-holed over the past century.[1]

Although explaining the depth and scope of the System of Systems can appear a daunting or impossible task, Burning Bright argues that the [[narrative-warfare|War of Stories]] can take a single inflection point — such as the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination of Donald Trump]] and the surrounding controversy over the [[austin-private-wealth|Austin Private Wealth]] put option — and turn it into a "Shatterpoint of multi-directional awakening."[1]

## The Paradoxical Machine

In the July 28, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that he had been framing the [[deep-state|Deep State]] as the "System of Systems" for long enough that the term carried more immediate meaning to him than the alternative — not because it was cleverer or catchier, nor because the original term was lacking, but because it hinted at "the core contradiction that is not a contradiction" at the heart of the Deep State itself.[2]

He described the System of Systems as a "Paradoxical Machine": a machine running on perpetual motion, that motion being the corruption and shared leverage of those wittingly or unwittingly caught in its "greasy, sometimes-bloody gears."[2] The core paradox, in his account, is that the machine only appears impossibly complex from outside, while internally it consists of nothing more than several mutually supporting pillars — the Narrative (news and entertainment media), the Financial, and the Clandestine — bracing one another against sovereign attempts at discovery and reformation.[2]

Burning Bright argued that nothing summed up the concept better than the rampant online censorship experienced firsthand by many over the preceding years, citing testimony by [[emma-jo-morris|Emma-Jo Morris]] on the suppression of the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]] story.[2] He concluded that the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] could not be stopped.[2]

## Application to the 2025 NBA gambling case

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked the System of Systems in October 2025 when discussing the federal illegal-gambling indictment that swept up [[national-basketball-association|NBA]] figures [[chauncey-billups|Chauncey Billups]] and [[terry-rozier|Terry Rozier]] alongside [[la-cosa-nostra|La Cosa Nostra]] crime families.[3] He argued the convergence implied by the sting should not be underestimated and that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's [[fbi|FBI]] had reasons for signal-boosting a case tying professional sports to organized crime, concluding: "The System of Systems is all around us. And sports are fake."[3]
