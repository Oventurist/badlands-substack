# Defense Contractor Buyback Restrictions

In January 2026 President [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]] signed an Executive Order intended to "prioritize the warfighter" by curbing defense contractors' stock buybacks and excessive corporate payouts where such spending comes at the expense of production, innovation and the on-time delivery of military equipment.[1]

## Provisions

The order directs the Secretary of War to identify underperforming contractors and to require remediation plans or invoke enforcement options available under existing law.[1] It also seeks to change future contracts so as to limit stock buybacks and to tie executive compensation to performance metrics such as delivery timeliness and increases in production.[1] The administration presented the measure, alongside the simultaneous [[us-withdrawal-from-66-international-organizations|withdrawal from 66 international organizations]], as reflecting a focus on American sovereignty, fiscal restraint and national security priorities.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the tightening of Department of Defense contracts as the second-order component of a broader institutional transformation, arguing that if such a program were genuinely under way this is precisely what one would expect to observe.[1] She noted that the reforms should be judged by results — "Let's SEE what happens" — while comparing the dynamic to the visible effects of earlier [[usaid|USAID]] funding cuts on dependent organizations.[1]
