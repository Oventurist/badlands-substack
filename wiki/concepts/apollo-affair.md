# Apollo Affair

The "Apollo Affair" is the name given to the alleged diversion of weapons-grade nuclear material from a facility in Apollo, Pennsylvania to Israel.[1] In the September 11, 2024 *Badlands News Brief*, the affair is described as an operation in which the Israeli intelligence officer [[rafi-eitan|Rafi Eitan]] was "effectively caught stealing materials to construct a nuclear weapon," including hundreds of kilograms of weapons-grade uranium, from the Pennsylvania plant.[1]

## The Milchan connection

The Brief links the affair directly to [[arnon-milchan|Arnon Milchan]], the Hollywood producer and former Israeli intelligence officer, stating that Eitan was working with Milchan to ship the materials back to Israel.[1] Badlands treats Milchan's procurement role for what it calls "the illegal Israel nuclear weapons program" as the key to reading his later film work — in particular his production of Oliver Stone's *JFK*, a film that Badlands notes blames the [[cia|CIA]], and only the CIA, for the assassination of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1] In this reading, Milchan had a conflict of interest, or a motive, in steering the assassination narrative away from other actors.[1]

## Eisenhower's warning

Badlands situates the affair inside a longer proliferation history, citing declassified JFK files showing that outgoing President [[dwight-eisenhower|Dwight Eisenhower]], meeting Kennedy the day before his inauguration, warned that Kennedy's biggest challenge would be stopping Israel from obtaining a nuclear weapon.[1] The Brief uses this to argue that contemporary alarm over Iran's nuclear program is "pure projection."[1]

## Butler, Pennsylvania

The Brief closes its treatment of the affair with a geographic observation it offers as narrative synchronicity rather than evidence: the stage in Butler, Pennsylvania where [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was shot in the head is less than thirty miles from the facility where the Apollo Affair occurred.[1]
