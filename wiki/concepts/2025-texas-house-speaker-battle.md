# 2025 Texas House Speaker Battle

The 2025 Texas House Speaker battle was a leadership fight within the Texas Republican Party that brought long-simmering internal tensions into the open in January 2025.[1]

The contest pitted state Representative [[dustin-burrows|Dustin Burrows]], a top ally of outgoing Speaker [[dade-phelan|Dade Phelan]], against state Representative [[david-cook|David Cook]], who had been voted the state GOP's Speaker nominee the previous month. The fight underscored long-standing friction between the remnant of what was once the Texas party's establishment and the rising far-right faction that by then controlled most of the state's government.[1]

Reporting held that if Cook won, it would be the first time in more than a decade that a candidate from the right wing of the Texas GOP claimed the speakership — described as a political earthquake that belied a slower, decades-long process of intrapartisan grinding below the surface.[1]
