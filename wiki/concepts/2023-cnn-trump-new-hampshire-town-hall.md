# 2023 CNN Trump New Hampshire Town Hall

The 2023 CNN Trump New Hampshire town hall was a live televised forum announced in early May 2023, at which former president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] agreed to appear before viewers of [[cnn|CNN]] — a network he had frequently derided as "fake news" while in office.[1]

## Format and setting

The event was scheduled for May 10 at St. Anselm's College in Goffstown, New Hampshire, and was to be televised at 9 p.m. Eastern with [[kaitlan-collins|Kaitlan Collins]] moderating.[1] Trump was to take questions from Republicans and undeclared voters planning to participate in the 2024 GOP primary.[1]

Although Trump had taken audience questions at some rallies and had been interviewed by Sean Hannity and Tucker Carlson on Fox News in the preceding weeks, the CNN forum was his first town-hall-style event of the 2024 campaign.[1] He had never given an interview to a CNN journalist during his presidency.[1]

## Political significance

The booking was described as a clear attempt by Trump to draw a contrast with Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], an expected opponent in the Republican presidential primary.[1] It was also characterized as a significant moment for a slumping CNN as the network worked to move beyond the era of its former leader Jeff Zucker.[1] A Trump adviser, not authorized to speak publicly, said that stepping outside the traditional comfort zone for Republicans had been key to Trump's success in 2016, and that CNN executives had made a compelling pitch.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] framed the announcement in terms of speculation within the Truth Community that "Trump and the patriots are in control of CNN now," while withholding judgment on whether that reading was correct.[1] He argued that if anyone could use "verbal fencing to win a battle for truth and freedom" it was Trump, and anticipated the forum as an ill-fated event for the Deep State.[1]
