# 2025 Burkina Faso Coup Plot

The 2025 Burkina Faso coup plot was an alleged conspiracy to overthrow junta leader Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]], which the country's military government said it had foiled in April 2025.[1]

Security Minister Mahamadou Sana announced on state television that the attempt was led by current and former soldiers working with "terrorist leaders," and that the plotters had intended to attack the presidential palace the previous week.[1] The government alleged the conspirators were based in neighbouring [[ivory-coast|Ivory Coast]].[1] According to Sana, the aim of the plan was to "sow total chaos, and place the country under the supervision of an international organisation."[1]

The announcement was the latest of several claimed attempts to remove Traoré, who seized power in 2022 amid escalating militant violence.[1] Like its Sahel neighbours, [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] has been fighting armed jihadist groups, with an estimated 40 percent of its territory under their control.[1]
