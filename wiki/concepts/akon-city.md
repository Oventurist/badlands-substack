# Akon City

Akon City was a proposed $6 billion, Wakanda-inspired "city of tomorrow" planned by the recording artist [[akon|Akon]] on 136 acres granted by the Senegalese government just outside Dakar, the capital of [[senegal|Senegal]].[1]

## Plan

As announced, the futuristic metropolis was to include state-of-the-art condominiums, amusement parks, a seaside resort, hospitals and a university, all powered by solar energy and transacted in Akon's Akoin cryptocurrency.[1]

## Collapse

After several missed payments to the government and a dramatic devaluation of Akoin, Akon was given an ultimatum in 2024 by Sapco-Senegal, the state-owned company focused on developing the country and driving tourism, which demanded he begin work within two weeks.[1] Bloomberg later reported that the head of Sapco-Senegal told the Senegalese press the project was officially dead.[1] The government reclaimed the land, and Sapco proceeded with a $1.2 billion plan to develop the area as a tourist destination with hotels, apartments, a marina and a promenade.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]] used the collapse as a contrast with American construction practice, arguing that the pictured structure would take six months at most in New York City concrete — most of it prep and foundation — and that the entire city could have been finished in five years.[1] He pointed to the Ridge Hill project, built from a flattened mountain between 2008 and 2011, as evidence, and attributed American capacity to work ethic and efficiency rather than privilege.[1]
