# 2023 Prigozhin Plane Crash

The 2023 Prigozhin plane crash was the downing of a private Embraer jet northwest of Moscow on August 23, 2023, carrying ten people including [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] founder [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]].[1] Contemporary reporting indicated that the entire top command of the Russian mercenary outfit had been aboard, and that an anti-air missile had struck the aircraft.[1]

## Confirmation and reaction

Wagner confirmed Prigozhin's death, with the Wagner-affiliated Telegram channel *Grey Zone* calling the event a "murder."[1] The channel's statement read: "The murder/assassination of Prigozhin will have catastrophic consequences. The people who gave the order do not understand the mood in the army and morale at all. Let this be a lesson to all. You always have to go to the end."[1] Russian media reported that the bodies of Prigozhin and his second-in-command [[dmitry-utkin|Dmitry Utkin]] had been identified, and the state news agency [[tass|TASS]] made it official, reporting that the Federal Air Transport Agency had confirmed both men were on board the crashed Embraer.[1] The account was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]

## Badlands framing

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed the presumed death at the end of a chain of Russia–Ukraine narrative developments: Prigozhin's appearance at an Africa peace summit with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], which in his reading revealed the earlier "Russian coup" to be a narrative operation; a NATO security chief's warning that the [[2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive|Ukrainian counteroffensive]] had "failed"; Belarusian President [[alexander-lukashenko|Alexander Lukashenko]]'s claim that "most leaders acknowledge" Putin had accomplished his goals in Ukraine; Wagner and Putin's support for the nationalist [[2023-niger-coup|coup in Niger]] and Niger's rejection of a visit by the U.S. [[state-department|State Department]]; the opening of the [[2023-brics-johannesburg-summit|BRICS summit]] in South Africa, which Putin did not attend due to arrest threats from Western-aligned "groups"; [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and Putin signalling financial war on the G-7 and calling devaluation of the U.S. [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] "inevitable"; [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]]'s claim that intelligence sources confirmed the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream 2]], [[kakhovka-dam-destruction|Kakhovka Dam]] and [[crimean-bridge-attack|Crimean Bridge]] attacks were the work of, or coordinated by, the [[cia|CIA]]; and statements by [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] and [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] that peace in Ukraine was not an option and escalation would continue "to the end."[1]

From that sequence, Burning Bright argued sarcastically that it was hard to see why anyone would think the world was approaching a precipice requiring "a certain Western leader" to steer it away from World War III.[1] He cautioned that "where it concerns Prigozhin, nothing is usually as it seems," urging readers to "focus on the Story being told, and who's telling it," and predicted that "the Story of the Peacemakers marches on" toward a happy or at least cathartic ending.[1]
