# House Oversight Investigation into Tim Walz's CCP Ties

In August 2024, the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|House Committee on Oversight and Accountability]] opened an investigation into Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]], the vice-presidential running mate of [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], over reports of his long-standing connections to [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] entities and officials.[1]

## The inquiry

Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] of Kentucky announced the investigation and wrote to [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] requesting information, documents and communications related to Walz's engagements with CCP-linked entities and officials, as well as any warnings or advice the Bureau may have given the governor regarding CCP influence operations targeting U.S. political figures.[1]

"The CCP has sought to destroy the United States through coordinated influence and infiltration campaigns that target every aspect of American life, including our own elected officials," Comer said. "Americans should be deeply concerned that Governor Walz, Kamala Harris's vice-presidential running mate, has a long-standing and cozy relationship with China."[1] He noted that Walz had visited China dozens of times, served as a fellow at a Chinese institution devoted to the CCP, and spoken alongside the president of a Chinese organization that the [[state-department|State Department]] had exposed as a CCP effort to influence and co-opt local leaders, concluding that the American people deserved to understand how deep the relationship went.[1]
