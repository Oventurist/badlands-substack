# Collective Situational Awareness

**Collective situational awareness** is the objective [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] offers as the payoff of using AI to map globalist warfare.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

His argument is that identifying the ways people are assaulted by [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] "by an ancient death cult" is inherently complex, because attacks on families and on the ability to think independently are ever-covert and incorporated into daily life.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Using AI to gather all the warfare that has been exposed therefore confers a distinct advantage: "We can make visible that which has been designed to remain invisible."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The concept is explicitly action-oriented rather than merely analytical. It is from this place of collective situational awareness, Deschamps writes, that people can take steps together toward collective action truly inspired by everything the enemy is trying to hide.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The framing mirrors his opening rationale — that truth is coherent, that the mind is a truth-discovering machine, and that the [[cabal|cabal]]'s power lies in preventing people from looking or from valuing what they see, as demonstrated in the [[asch-conformity-experiments|Asch Conformity Experiments]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
