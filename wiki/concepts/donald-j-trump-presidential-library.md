# Donald J. Trump Presidential Library

The Donald J. Trump Presidential Library is a proposed 50-story waterfront building in Miami, Florida, intended to house the presidential records and commemorative exhibits of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. Plans were unveiled on March 31, 2026.[1]

## Design and announcement

The project was announced by [[eric-trump|Eric Trump]], who described the building as "a masterpiece, the likes of which have never been seen in Florida or really anywhere" and a "lasting testament" to his father.[1] As presented, the tower would be clad in gold and feature a giant statue of the former president, an Air Force One plane displayed in the lobby, an amphitheater, and replicas of the Oval Office and a White House ballroom.[1] It is to be built on prime land transferred to the [[trump-library-foundation|Trump Library Foundation]] by [[miami-dade-college|Miami-Dade College]].[1] No construction timeline had been released as of the announcement.[1]

The unveiling coincided with Florida's renaming of Palm Beach International Airport after Trump.[1]

## Reaction

The plans triggered immediate and widespread ridicule for their ostentation.[1] The office of California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] compared the golden Trump statue to effigies of authoritarian leaders, and historian [[marvin-dunn|Marvin Dunn]] criticized the underlying land transfer as a "land giveaway."[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the library announcement less as spectacle than as a marker of political time, writing that it made her sad because "it means the era of this President is coming to a close" and that the "Gray Champion" is about to exit the scene.[1] She tied the moment to a scheduled [[gart-nashville|GART Nashville]] panel topic, "MAGA in a Post Trump World," arguing that the movement is in disarray and that the coming Golden Age "isn't something that is going to be built for us to inhabit" but must be built and owned by the people themselves.[1]
