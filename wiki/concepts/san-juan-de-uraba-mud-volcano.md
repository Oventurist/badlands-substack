# San Juan de Urabá Mud Volcano Eruption

In February 2026 a mud volcano erupted in San Juan de Urabá in northern Colombia, sending mud and debris across nearby land and infrastructure.[1]

## Impact

Local reports indicated that animals were killed as a result of the eruption.[1] The event also damaged roads and surrounding infrastructure, disrupting transportation in the area.[1] Videos circulating on social media showed mud flowing across rural terrain near San Juan de Urabá, and authorities were assessing the extent of the damage.[1] The item was carried as a bonus entry in the Badlands Brief.[1]
