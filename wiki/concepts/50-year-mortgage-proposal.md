# 50-Year Mortgage Proposal

The 50-year mortgage proposal is a Trump administration plan, confirmed in November 2025, to introduce 50-year mortgage terms for home buyers as an affordability measure.[1]

## Announcement

[[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]] Director [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] said on a Saturday in November 2025 that the administration was "working on" the plan, writing on X: "Thanks to President Trump, we are indeed working on The 50 year Mortgage – a complete game changer."[1]

Pulte's statement followed a Truth Social post earlier that day by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who shared a graphic juxtaposing an image of himself with one of former President [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin D. Roosevelt]].[1] The Roosevelt administration, which oversaw the New Deal, established the 30-year mortgage as the American standard to help citizens recover from the Great Depression.[1]

## Political context

Trump campaigned on creating affordability for the younger generation, but faced headwinds on the subject as prices rose during his term.[1] The proposal appeared alongside other affordability-focused initiatives that month, including the [[trump-tariff-dividend-proposal|$2,000 tariff dividend]] and a plan to redirect [[affordable-care-act|Obamacare]] insurer payments directly to Americans, in what ZeroHedge described as an emerging 2026 midterm cycle centered on affordability.[1]
