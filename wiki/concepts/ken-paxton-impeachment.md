# Impeachment of Ken Paxton

The **impeachment of Ken Paxton** was the mid-2023 proceeding against the Attorney General of Texas, [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]], in which he was impeached and his salary unilaterally suspended on the prosecution's assertion that the crimes under investigation were of sufficient seriousness.[1]

## As a case study in weaponized finance

The episode is presented in the first installment of the [[money-wars|Money Wars]] series by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] as a second example — alongside the [[freedom-convoy|Freedom Convoy]] account freezes — of the financial system being used as a weapon against freedom, trust, and the rule of law.[1] Paxton discussed the matter in an interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] on X.[1]

According to Deschamps, the prosecution used taxpayer money both to fund a secret investigation and later to hire attorneys to prosecute Paxton, while denying Paxton access to public funds for his own defense — a posture he contrasts with the entitlement of even common criminals to a state-paid defense.[1] The impeachment was quietly pushed through over Memorial Day weekend, and legal process was used to freeze Paxton's pay and impose a gag order.[1] The effect, in this reading, was that money was used to prevent a man later acquitted of all charges from defending himself.[1]
