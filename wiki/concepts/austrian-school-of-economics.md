# Austrian School of Economics

The **Austrian School of Economics** is a heterodox economic tradition cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a defining intellectual commitment of one faction within the [[america-first-movement|America First]] coalition.[1]

## Role in the MAGA coalition

In his November 2025 essay [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], DeLarme describes the "reluctantly right-leaning libertarians" as the people who voted for Ron Paul and [[pat-buchanan|Pat Buchanan]] and who are typically proponents of the [[mises-institute|Mises Institute]] and the Austrian School.[1] He characterizes them as a distinct subset of the broader libertarian movement that emphasizes classical liberal principles with a conservative tilt, focusing on reducing government intervention while prioritizing property rights, free markets and traditional values in some areas.[1]

The faction is one of roughly nine that DeLarme identifies inside the coalition that elected [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2024 — a coalition he argues had fallen "in tatters" by late 2025, principally over foreign policy.[1]
