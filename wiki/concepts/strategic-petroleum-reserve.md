# Strategic Petroleum Reserve

The Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) is the United States' emergency stockpile of crude oil, maintained as a buffer against supply disruption and as an instrument of energy security.[1]

## Drawdowns under the Biden administration

Critics of the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration have pointed to the draining of the SPR to what they characterize as dangerously low levels as one element in a broader pattern of policies weakening American energy independence.[1] Reporting cited in the Badlands corpus noted that some of the released barrels — roughly six million — were sold to a Chinese energy giant linked to [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1]

The SPR drawdown was grouped with the cancellation of the [[keystone-pipeline|Keystone XL pipeline]] project, pledges of "no more oil drilling" on US soil, and the cancellation of seven oil and gas leases in the [[arctic-national-wildlife-refuge|Arctic National Wildlife Refuge]] as evidence supporting the question posed by critics: "Who is Biden working for?"[1] The contrast drawn was with geopolitical adversaries — two of whom, in this telling, had paid the Biden family handsomely — building up their own energy stockpiles, with China heavily boosting crude stockpiling using discounted Russian oil.[1]

## Badlands framing

Contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cautioned against reading such episodes only through the doomer lens common in alternative media, noting that outlets like [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] assume the administration's open corruption proves Deep State control, whereas the same facts can be read as a controlled demolition of the Deep State in the public mind — a framing associated with [[devolution|Devolution]] and Continuity of Government.[1]

## 2024 drawdowns and the Northeast gasoline stockpile

By 2024, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had drained roughly half of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, a step [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] attributed to avoiding Democratic losses in the 2022 midterm elections.[2] In May 2024 the administration went further, announcing the sale of the nearly one million barrels in the separate [[northeast-gasoline-supply-reserve|Northeast Gasoline Supply Reserve]] and effectively closing it.[2] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had topped the reserves up while in office, and framed the drawdowns as trading long-term energy and national-security stability for a publicized few cents per gallon.[2]
