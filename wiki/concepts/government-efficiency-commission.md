# Government Efficiency Commission

The **government efficiency commission** was a body proposed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the 2024 presidential campaign, to be led by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], tasked with conducting a complete financial and performance audit of the entire federal government and recommending drastic reforms.[1] It was the direct antecedent of what became [[doge|DOGE]] after Trump's election.[1]

## Announcement

Trump unveiled the proposal on Thursday, September 5, 2024, as part of a slate of new economic plans presented in remarks at the Economic Club of New York.[1] "At the suggestion of Elon Musk, who has given me his complete and total endorsement … I will create a government efficiency commission tasked with conducting a complete financial and performance audit of the entire federal government and making recommendations for drastic reforms," he said, adding, "We need to do it. Can't go on the way we are now."[1]

The idea followed a recent conversation Trump had held with Musk on X, during which the Tesla chief executive suggested the former president create such a group to tackle inflation and appoint him to it; Trump responded at the time, "I'd love it."[1]

## Accompanying economic proposals

In the same speech Trump outlined proposals to further reduce the corporate tax rate and to tackle government regulations, including those aimed at energy production.[1] He vowed to rescind all unspent funds from one of the key laws passed under the Biden administration, embraced cryptocurrency, and reaffirmed his plan to place sweeping tariffs on imports.[1] The Wall Street Journal first reported the economic package, which came days before Trump and Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] were scheduled to meet in Pennsylvania for their presidential debate.[1] Harris had recently released her own economic proposals, including measures aimed at aiding small businesses and making housing, groceries, child-rearing and health care more affordable for the middle class.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] wrote that Trump and Musk had announced a commission that would address systemic corruption "while helping to build a bridge to an American Future few thought possible," presented under the guise of an organic, sudden alliance emerging from the sociopolitical zeitgeist.[1] He argued that Musk's references to DOGE — which he glossed as "Director of Government Efficiency" — together with Musk's position as the largest [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] contractor under Trump, his control of the world's most powerful decentralized communications infrastructure, and his early capture of the techno-optimist left, proved rather than merely suggested "the convergence of something that was planned long ago."[1] He tied the reading to the War of Stories framing, in which the main players are "cognitive cyphers," and closed with the line, "The future is already here. It's just not evenly distributed yet."[1]
