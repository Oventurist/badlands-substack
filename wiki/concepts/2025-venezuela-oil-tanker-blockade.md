# 2025 Venezuela Oil Tanker Blockade

The 2025 Venezuela oil tanker blockade was a US measure announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in December 2025 barring all sanctioned oil tankers from entering or leaving [[venezuela|Venezuela]], part of an escalating pressure campaign against Venezuelan president [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

## Effects on shipping

Following the announcement, tanker loading at Venezuelan ports slowed, with most ships moving oil cargoes only between domestic ports, according to the Reuters news agency.[1] Reports described slowing activity at Venezuelan ports generally.[1]

## Trump's statements

Trump again called for Maduro to leave power and reiterated that the United States would keep or sell the oil it had seized off the coast of Venezuela in recent weeks.[1] Asked whether the goal was to force Maduro from power, Trump told reporters: "I think it'd be smart for him to do that," adding, "if he wants to do something, if he plays tough, it'll be the last time he's ever able to play tough."[1]

## International response

[[china|China]] and [[russia|Russia]] expressed support for Venezuela as it confronted the US blockade of sanctioned oil tankers.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] connected the episode to his concept of "Sovereign Alliance Maneuver Defense," recalling that he had said of an earlier event, "At the time, I had said I think this is the first obvious example of Sovereign Alliance Maneuver Defense."[1] He argued that in December 2024 the US and Russian militaries had executed a pincer movement on ISIS in Syria, and asked whether December 2025 was about to see the same pattern repeat in Venezuela.[1]
