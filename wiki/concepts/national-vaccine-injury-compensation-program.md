# National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program

The National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program (NVICP) is the United States federal system through which claims of vaccination-related injury and death are adjudicated and compensated, in operation since 1988.[1]

## Caseload and payouts

As of statistics published in November 2023, 26,789 petitions for vaccination-related fatalities and injuries had been submitted to the program since its inception, of which only a small portion resulted in compensation.[1] Nevertheless, families of those injured or killed following vaccination had received payouts totaling more than $5 billion, an amount that continues to grow.[1]

## Role in reform advocacy

The program's figures are frequently cited by vaccine-safety advocates as evidence of systemic harm and of the liability shield enjoyed by manufacturers. [[childrens-health-defense|Children's Health Defense]] invoked NVICP statistics when launching its [[reform-pharma|Reform Pharma]] initiative in November 2023, an effort whose objectives include restoring vaccine manufacturer liability.[1] The data was relayed by [[vigilant-news|Vigilant News]] and reproduced in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
