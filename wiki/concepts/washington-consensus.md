# Washington Consensus (foreign policy establishment)

In Badlands Media usage, the "Washington consensus" denotes the bipartisan foreign-policy orthodoxy that routinely endorses every conflict and perpetual war favored by the large foreign-policy think tanks, and whose primary purpose is preserving the status quo rather than delivering the changes voters demand.[1]

## Diagnostic function

The publication treats unified establishment hostility as a signal: when the establishment factions of both political parties unite against an individual, there is good reason to believe that person threatens the consensus.[1] The February 2025 confirmation hearing of [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] for [[director-of-national-intelligence|Director of National Intelligence]] was offered as a demonstration, with [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] attacking from MSNBC while the neoconservative [[national-review|National Review]] attacked from the right in the same weekend.[1]

## The script

During the hearing, senators of both parties were described as demanding that Gabbard recite the consensus script — that [[edward-snowden|Edward Snowden]] is a traitor, that [[section-702|Section 702]] deserves support, and that Vladimir Putin is simply a mad dog.[1] Gabbard's refusal to bend to that orthodoxy over a full day of questioning was, in the author's opinion, precisely what made her uniquely qualified for the post.[1]

Underlying the consensus, the article argued, is a [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] that thrives on conflict and funds the campaigns of career senators who never seem worried about losing their seats.[1] The 2024 election was read as evidence of radical public dissatisfaction with how Washington operates, with the permanent political class actively working to counteract its own constituents' wishes.[1]
