# Executive Order 14188

Executive Order 14188, "Additional Measures to Combat Anti-Semitism," was signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 29, 2025.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) The order expands measures addressing antisemitism, including the monitoring and potential deportation of non-citizens — such as international students — who engage in speech deemed antisemitic.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

## Reliance on the IHRA definition

Determinations under the order are frequently based on the [[ihra-definition-of-antisemitism|IHRA definition of antisemitism]], which Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argues encompasses many legitimate criticisms of Israel.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

## Assessment

DeLarme presents the order alongside the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]] as evidence that the erosion of the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] is not driven by the "deep state" alone but is being advanced through Republican channels as well.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) Both instruments, in his framing, are censorship frameworks that threaten to criminalize dissent under vague definitions of [[hate-speech|hate speech]], serving the interagency [[deep-state|blob]]'s longstanding objective of [[government-sponsored-censorship|government-sponsored censorship]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)
