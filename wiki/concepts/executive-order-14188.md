# Executive Order 14188

Executive Order 14188, titled "Additional Measures to Combat Anti-Semitism," was signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 29, 2025.[1] The order expands federal measures against antisemitism, including the monitoring and potential deportation of non-citizens — such as international students — who engage in speech deemed antisemitic.[1]

## Reliance on the IHRA definition

Enforcement under the order frequently rests on the [[international-holocaust-remembrance-alliance|IHRA]] working definition of antisemitism, which Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argues sweeps in many legitimate criticisms of the state of Israel.[1] Paired with the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]], the order is characterized as one of the two censorship frameworks by which the interagency blob advances [[government-sponsored-censorship|government-sponsored censorship]] under vague definitions of [[hate-speech|hate speech]].[1]

## Context

DeLarme situates the order within a broader argument that conservative officeholders, having previously denounced [[safe-spaces|safe spaces]] and [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]], adopted the same speech-restrictive logic after the [[october-7-attacks|October 7th attacks]], demonstrating that commitments to the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] on the right were conditional rather than principled.[1]
