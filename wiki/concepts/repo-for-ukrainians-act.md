# REPO for Ukrainians Act

The REPO for Ukrainians Act is proposed U.S. legislation, sponsored by Rep. [[michael-mccaul|Michael McCaul]] of Texas, that would authorize the confiscation of Russian sovereign assets under American jurisdiction, liquidate them, and deposit the proceeds into a fund supporting Ukraine.[1]

## Role in the 2024 Ukraine aid fight

In March 2024, House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] told Republican senators that the House intended to send over Ukraine legislation that would "look substantially different" from the $95 billion foreign aid package the Senate had passed the previous month.[1] Johnson floated structuring the assistance as a loan or lend-lease program — an idea [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had raised in February — and suggested incorporating a mechanism similar to the REPO for Ukrainians Act.[1] Senators present said the discussion contained no mention of accompanying U.S. border reforms such as "Remain in Mexico" language.[1] Separately, Johnson told the New York Post that Ukraine funding would take a back seat to funding the federal government for the remainder of the fiscal year, a divergence critics read as telling different audiences different things.[1]
