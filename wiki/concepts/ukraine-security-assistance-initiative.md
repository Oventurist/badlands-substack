# Ukraine Security Assistance Initiative

The Ukraine Security Assistance Initiative (USAI) is the United States funding mechanism through which long-term military aid to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] is procured, as distinct from the drawdown of existing [[pentagon|Pentagon]] stocks used for rapid deliveries of equipment, weapons and ammunition.[1] Money provided under the initiative is spent over the coming months or even years, on the premise of ensuring Ukraine's future security needs rather than meeting immediate battlefield demand.[1]

## May 2023 $1.2 billion package

U.S. officials said on Monday, May 8, 2023 that the United States would provide an additional $1.2 billion in long-term military aid to Ukraine, to bolster its air defenses as [[russia|Russia]] continued to strike Ukrainian targets with drones, rockets and surface-to-air missiles.[1] The package, expected to be announced the following day, was to be funded through the initiative.[1] It was slated to pay for HAWK air-defense systems, air-defense munitions and drones for air defense, and additionally to buy artillery, rockets and satellite imagery assistance, along with funding for ongoing maintenance and spare parts for a variety of systems.[1] The officials described the package on condition of anonymity because it had not yet been formally announced.[1]
