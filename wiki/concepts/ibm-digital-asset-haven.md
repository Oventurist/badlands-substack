# IBM Digital Asset Haven

IBM Digital Asset Haven is a digital-asset management platform unveiled by [[ibm|IBM]] in a press release on Monday, October 27, 2025, targeted at banks, governments, and large corporations seeking to hold and move digital assets securely across multiple blockchains.[1]

## Capabilities

The platform provides custody, transaction routing, and settlement spanning more than 40 public and private chains.[1] It also bundles compliance functionality — including identity verification and anti-money-laundering screening — integrated through APIs and developer tooling.[1]

## Rollout

A software-as-a-service edition was slated to go live in the fourth quarter of 2025, with broader support planned for the second quarter of 2026.[1]

## Context

The launch followed rising demand among financial institutions and businesses for [[stablecoin|stablecoins]] and tokenized real-world assets, adopted to manage and move money across borders more cheaply, faster, and with less friction than legacy banking rails.[1] Badlands commentary read the development as evidence that the anticipated financial transition is already under way.[1]
