# Summit of the Future

The Summit of the Future is a [[united-nations|United Nations]] gathering scheduled for September 2024 — described in reporting as taking place "less than two months before the next U.S. presidential election" — at which member nations were expected to adopt the [[pact-for-the-future|Pact for the Future]].[1] The summit was framed as the moment at which numerous policy reforms advanced by the U.N. over the preceding two years under its "Our Common Agenda" platform would be consolidated into a single agreement.[1]

## The emergency platform proposal

Among the proposals slated for adoption, the most consequential according to The Federalist — whose reporting was relayed by The Gateway Pundit — was a plan for a new "emergency platform," characterized as "a stunning proposal to give the U.N. significant powers in the event of future 'global shocks,' such as another worldwide pandemic."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the summit as the culmination of a coordinated push for supranational emergency authority, placing it alongside the [[pandemic-treaty|Pandemic Treaty]] and proposed amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]] as a third front in the same campaign.[1] Contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that if such powers were granted — with the support of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s administration — it "could be seen as a stepping stone toward establishing a permanent world-governing body."[1]
