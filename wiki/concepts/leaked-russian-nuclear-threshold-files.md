# Leaked Russian Nuclear Threshold Files

The **leaked Russian nuclear threshold files** were a cache of classified Russian military documents reported by the Financial Times in late February 2024 that purported to describe criteria for the use of tactical nuclear weapons.[1]

## Contents

According to the reporting, forces under [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had rehearsed using tactical nuclear weapons at an early stage of a conflict with a major world power, with training scenarios that included an invasion by China.[1] The classified papers, seen by the Financial Times, described a threshold for using tactical nuclear weapons lower than [[russia|Russia]] has ever publicly admitted, according to experts who reviewed and verified the documents.[1]

The cache consisted of 29 secret Russian military files drawn up between 2008 and 2014, including war-gaming scenarios and presentations for naval officers that discussed operating principles for the use of nuclear weapons.[1] Criteria for a potential nuclear response ranged from an enemy incursion on Russian territory to more specific triggers, such as the destruction of 20 percent of Russia's strategic ballistic missile submarines.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] grouped the leak with two other stories published within five hours — Putin's state of the nation address warning of nuclear war and Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]]'s congressional testimony that [[nato|NATO]] would fight Russia if Ukraine fell — describing the trio as a "triple-decker in Hegelian propaganda."[1] He characterized the third headline as sourced via "leaked" documents alleging Russia's threshold for defending itself with nuclear weaponry was "lower" than previously thought, and summarized the intended effect as the mainstream media telling normies that Putin's tolerance meter was almost exhausted — "And Normies believe them."[1]
