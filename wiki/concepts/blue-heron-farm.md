# Blue Heron Farm

Blue Heron Farm is a 28.5-acre estate in Chilmark on Martha's Vineyard, Massachusetts, best known as the summer retreat the Obamas rented during their time in the White House.[1]

## 2025 sale to a Wexner trust

In August 2025 the property quietly changed hands for $37 million, some $2 million below its May asking price.[1] The buyer was a trust tied to billionaire retail magnate [[les-wexner|Les Wexner]], the longtime head of L Brands and founder of Victoria's Secret, according to the Real Deal.[1]

The estate had been off the market for more than a decade following an extensive transformation carried out by its previous owners, British architect Norman Foster and his wife, Elena Ochoa Foster.[1]
