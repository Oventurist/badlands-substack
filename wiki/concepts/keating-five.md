# Keating Five

The Keating Five were the five members of [[congress|Congress]] who took campaign money from [[charles-keating|Charles Keating]], one of the worst perpetrators of the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan scandal]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats the affair less as a scandal than as a distraction. He argues the narrative of the entire S&L crisis was purposely focused on the Keating Five by the [[shadow-government|shadow government]]-controlled media in order to draw attention away from what was really happening.[1] While the country spent several years debating corrupt campaign financing, it remained blind to the massive money laundering occurring across the country — another "red herring" argument, after which nothing happened to the five members of Congress.[1] Lange sets the episode aside in favour of figures nobody discusses, such as [[herman-k-beebe|Herman K. Beebe]].[1]
