# Executive Order 14067

Executive Order 14067 is the U.S. executive order on digital assets cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as a policy vehicle that, alongside the [[project-cedar|Project Cedar]] pilot, could lead toward the creation of a new form of U.S. currency.[1]

## Commentary

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that, per macroeconomist and CIA advisor [[jim-rickards|Jim Rickards]], the order is one of several avenues that could culminate in a trackable [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]], which he framed as a critical component of a globalist agenda joining technocracy and transhumanism.[1]
