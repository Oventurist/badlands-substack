# Operation Warp Speed

**Operation Warp Speed** was the United States government program that facilitated the creation and rollout of a COVID-19 vaccine in a historically short timeframe during President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term.[1]

## Trump's 2025 remarks

Speaking at length to the press during a cabinet meeting on Tuesday, August 26, 2025, Trump praised his administration's handling of the COVID-19 pandemic while extolling his first-term economic record.[1] "I had the greatest economy in the history of our country, my first four years despite covid, which was a very unfortunate situation for the whole world," the president said. "We did a great job with it, never got the credit for the job we did."[1] He went on to laud the government operation behind the accelerated formulation, production, and distribution of vaccines to millions of Americans: "Operation Warp Speed, people say, is one of the greatest achievements ever in politics or in the military because it was almost a military procedure," adding that "everybody, including [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], said that Operation Warp Speed, what you did with that, nobody can believe it and we did a great job."[1]

Mediaite noted that Trump had spoken less frequently about the program in recent years despite the praise it garnered from many of his critics, as sections of his base became increasingly opposed to vaccines — especially COVID-19 shots — with coalitions such as the Make America Healthy Again (MAHA) movement pushing strongly for the elimination of vaccine mandates.[1]

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] responded that "normies still think Operation Warp Speed was about a vaccine," arguing that they remain deluded because they consume [[con-inc|ConInc]] information operations as a guide to navigating the world.[1] The brief paired this with an excerpt from *Devolution — Part 15 — Vaccine Bewilderment*, the section of [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s Devolution series dealing with Operation Warp Speed, presented as an alternative reading of the program as a military rather than pharmaceutical undertaking.[1]
