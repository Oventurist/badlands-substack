# Trump Accounts

Trump Accounts are a savings program established under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term intended to secure "the financial futures of millions of American newborns."[1]

The program was cited in the [[white-house|White House]] statement issued after the [[2026-state-of-the-union|2026 State of the Union Address]], which listed Trump Accounts among the accomplishments congressional Democrats declined to applaud during the speech.[1]

## Enactment in the 2025 budget bill

The sweeping budget bill that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed into law on Friday, July 4, 2025 included a provision creating investment accounts that parents can open on behalf of children under 18, with the federal government seeding the accounts with an initial $1,000 contribution for U.S. citizens born from 2025 through 2028.[2] Republican lawmakers nicknamed the vehicles "Trump accounts," though the underlying idea of so-called baby bonds long predates them: [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] raised the concept during her 2007 campaign, and Democratic Senator [[cory-booker|Cory Booker]] proposed legislation in 2021 offering up to $50,000 for newborns.[2] Financial commentators described the seed money as a "no-brainer" while cautioning that other savings vehicles may serve families better.[2]
