# Good to Great

*Good to Great: Why Some Companies Make the Leap … and Others Don't* is a management book by Jim Collins, quoted at length in Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]]'s December 9, 2022 essay "We Are 'The Plan'."[1]

## The flywheel passage

The passage Gillespie reproduces asks the reader to imagine a five-thousand-pound, thirty-foot metal flywheel mounted horizontally on an axle, and the task of getting it to rotate as fast and as long as possible. Early effort yields almost imperceptible movement; after two or three hours of pushing the wheel completes one full turn. Continued, consistently directed effort produces a second turn, then more, until the wheel builds speed and reaches a breakthrough at which its own weight carries it forward with "almost unstoppable momentum."[1]

Collins then poses the rhetorical question of which single push caused the wheel to spin so fast, and answers that the question is nonsensical: it was "all of them added together in an overall accumulation of effort applied in a consistent direction," with each turn compounding the work done earlier.[1]

## Political adaptation

Gillespie describes the passage as permanently burned into her memory and repurposes it as the [[flywheel-of-freedom|Flywheel of Freedom]] — an argument that small, consistent acts of civic engagement by ordinary citizens compound into political breakthrough, and that no participant's contribution is too small to matter.[1]
