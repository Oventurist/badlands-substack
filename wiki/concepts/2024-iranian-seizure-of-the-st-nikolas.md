# 2024 Iranian Seizure of the St Nikolas

On Thursday, January 11, 2024, [[iran|Iran]]'s navy captured an oil tanker in the Gulf of Oman that only months earlier had seen its cargo of Iranian oil seized by the United States over sanctions linked to Tehran's nuclear program, further escalating the tensions gripping the Middle East's waterways.[1]

The vessel had previously been known as the Suez Rajan when it was at the center of a yearlong dispute beginning in 2021 that ultimately saw the U.S. Justice Department take the one million barrels of Iranian crude oil aboard it.[1]

The seizure came after weeks of attacks by Yemen's Iranian-backed [[houthis|Houthi]] rebels on shipping in the Red Sea, including their largest barrage of drones and missiles late on Tuesday, January 9, and immediately preceded the [[january-2024-us-uk-strikes-on-houthi-targets|U.S.-led retaliatory strikes]] launched early Friday.[1] The episode was reported by [[abc-news|ABC News]] and carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief of January 12, 2024.[1]
