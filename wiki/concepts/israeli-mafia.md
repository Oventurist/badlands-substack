# Israeli Mafia

The "Israeli Mafia" is the term used in Badlands commentary for organized crime syndicates said to operate within Israel, and in particular for networks alleged to have migrated there from Ukraine and the former Soviet Union.[1]

## Origins claimed in Badlands commentary

A July 2024 Badlands News Brief asserted that a Jewish-Ukrainian mafia migrated to Israel in 1989, naming Semion Mogilevich as its most famous figure.[1] The commentary presented this migration as the substance behind long-running speculation about criminal influence in Israeli politics, citing a 2017 *[[times-of-israel|Times of Israel]]* article titled "Is Israel becoming a mafia state?" which reported that a large share of [[knesset|Knesset]] members were implicated in questionable dealings.[1]

## Alleged infiltration of Likud

The same commentary cited [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]]' 2010 publication of US diplomatic communications in which the US Embassy in Israel expressed "grave concern" over the election of Inbal Gavrieli to the Knesset as a member of the [[likud|Likud]] party.[1] Gavrieli is described as the niece of the head of the Gavrieli crime family, making her uncle one of the most powerful crime bosses in Israel; the cables were said to reveal serious concern that organized crime syndicates had infiltrated and effectively controlled Likud's central committee.[1] The Badlands writer speculated that the disclosure of these communications may have contributed to the persecution of [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]].[1]

## Connection to Ron Dermer

The mafia thread was introduced in the context of a profile of [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]], whose early campaign work for [[natan-sharansky|Natan Sharansky]]'s [[yisrael-baaliyah|Yisrael BaAliyah]] party — representing Soviet Jewish immigrants originating in the [[pale-of-settlement|Pale of Settlement]] — was said to tie him to these Ukraine-Israel networks.[1]
