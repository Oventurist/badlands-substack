# Narrative and Actual Deployments

**Narrative Deployments** and **Actual Deployments** are the paired analytical categories used to organise each weekly instalment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the recap programme published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].

## The distinction

The scheme sorts the week's events into two classes. A *Narrative Deployment* is an event released primarily for its effect on public perception — a story placed, amplified or timed so as to shape what audiences believe is happening. An *Actual Deployment* is a real movement of power, capital, personnel or force, whatever narrative may be wrapped around it. The programme's method consists in separating the two, on the premise that the visible story is frequently cover for, or a distraction from, the substantive action occurring alongside it.

In [[badlands-blitz-episode-7|Badlands Blitz Episode 7]], published February 21, 2026, the hosts state that they will "hit the major Narrative and Actual Deployments of the week," using the terms as established shorthand requiring no definition for the audience. The same episode supplies a compact illustration of the method in its treatment of the United States and [[iran|Iran]]: the hosts describe the confrontation as "a whole lot of smoke, mirrors and a few juicy Actuals" — that is, a predominantly narrative construction within which a small number of genuine deployments can be identified, and from which they infer a realignment along [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] lines.

The categories are interpretive rather than empirical; assignment of an event to one class or the other is the analytical claim being made, not a matter of record.
