# Menopause Care Equity Act

The **Menopause Care Equity Act**, designated AB 432, is a California bill concerning coverage of menopause-related medical care.[1] Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] vetoed the measure in two consecutive years, most recently in 2025.[1]

The repeat veto became a public flashpoint when actress [[halle-berry|Halle Berry]] — founder of the menopausal care company Respin and a personal backer of the bill — denounced it from the stage of The New York Times' DealBook Summit, calling the veto an insult to women's health and saying that a governor who "has overlooked women, half the population, by devaluing us" should not become the next president.[1]

## Debate over the bill's substance

Commentary in the Badlands News Brief framed the dispute as one about payment rather than access. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued she would have vetoed the bill as well, contending that Berry's complaint was not that menopause treatments are unaffordable in themselves but that insurers are not compelled to cover them, and that the campaign therefore reinforced institutional medicine and momentum toward single-payer health care instead of pursuing affordability, accessibility, or patient education.[1]
