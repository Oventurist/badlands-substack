# Milwaukee School Lead Paint Crisis

The Milwaukee school lead paint crisis is a public health emergency that emerged in late 2024 and continued through 2025, in which thousands of children in Milwaukee, Wisconsin are feared to have been exposed to lead — a toxin linked to cancer and autism — because of deteriorating school buildings.[1]

## Exposure and closures

At least eight schools were found to have chipping lead-based paint producing toxic dust that, when inhaled, can cause a range of health problems.[1] Several schools were temporarily closed for remediation after high levels of the toxin were detected in students' blood.[1] Kat Cisar, a mother of six-year-old twins, learned in February 2025 that her children had been exposed; their school was among those forced to shut as the crisis emerged.[1]

## Remediation timeline

Health officials planned to use the summer of 2025 to inspect half of the district's roughly 100 schools built before 1978, the year lead paint was banned, so that they would be ready for the return of classes in the fall, with the remaining schools inspected before the end of the year.[1] Parents objected that this timeline left young children potentially exposed to a metal known to damage the developing brain.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Chris Paul used the story to jab at official explanations of rising autism rates, writing sarcastically that "it's not the childhood vaccine schedule. It's stuff like lead paint and asbestos."[1]
