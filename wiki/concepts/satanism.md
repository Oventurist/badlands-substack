# Satanism

In Badlands Media commentary, Satanism is identified as the shared creed binding together the figures at the top of the [[british-cabal|British Cabal]], and is offered as the explanation for cohesion among actors who otherwise share no nationality, race or religion.[1]

## As the common bond of the elite

In "The British Cabal" (August 7, 2023), [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] described the British Cabal as "these Satanic elites" who coordinate many operations that appear unconnected on the surface, with the ultimate purpose of securing control over money, resources and people.[1] He stressed that "the British Cabal are Satanists and Satanists come from all walks of life," a formulation intended to detach the claim from any single ethnic or national group.[1]

Summarising his overall thesis, Carlson wrote that he was not arguing the British are the only participants in the conspiracies that plague the world, but rather that they sit at the top of the pyramid — and that "the commonality the puppet masters at the top share is Satanism."[1]

## Relation to the colonial argument

Carlson connected the theme to his revisionist reading of the [[american-revolutionary-war|American Revolutionary War]], suggesting that the colonists may have regarded their British controllers as a globalist, Satanic cult rather than merely as tax collectors — a framing he used to argue that the tea-tax account of the revolution is a sanitised substitute for a far darker grievance.[1]
