# Boulder Terror Attack

The Boulder terror attack was an antisemitic assault in Boulder, Colorado on Sunday, June 1, 2025, in which twelve people were hurt, including a Holocaust survivor.[1]

## The attack and suspect

The victims were rallying for the release of hostages held by Hamas in Gaza, taken during the group's attack on Israel on October 7, 2023.[1] The suspect, 45-year-old Mohamed Sabry Soliman, used incendiary devices to burn people at the rally and faced federal hate crime charges along with numerous state-level attempted murder charges.[1] According to authorities, Soliman was in the country illegally after overstaying his visa and work authorization.[1]

## Political fallout

Colorado Democrats drew criticism for holding a fundraiser with Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] less than an hour after the attack.[1] The "private reception" took place in nearby Denver roughly thirty minutes later, with many other Democrats listed as hosts and ticket prices ranging from $500 to $7,000.[1] Omar, a member of the progressive group of lawmakers in [[congress|Congress]] known as the "Squad," has been a major critic of Israel, and critics called the timing "reprehensible."[1]

Colorado Governor [[jared-polis|Jared Polis]] called the assault an act of terror, and officials across the Colorado state government denounced it in the same terms.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who attended school in Boulder, wrote that the near-universal condemnation was "the weirdest bit," given what she described as years of Colorado progressives courting this kind of political violence, and argued that the authorities' expressed surprise was "quite unbelievable."[2]

## Initial reporting

Early accounts on June 1–2, 2025, reported six people injured after a man began "setting people on fire" at an outdoor pedestrian mall on Pearl Street in Boulder.[2] The [[fbi|FBI]] described the incident as a "suspected act of terrorism" carried out during a peaceful weekly event held in support of Israeli hostages.[2] [[mohamed-sabry-soliman|Mohamed Sabry Soliman]], 45, an Egyptian national whose visa had expired in 2023, shouted "free Palestine" as he threw Molotov cocktails and used a makeshift flamethrower against demonstrators before being taken into custody.[2] Badlands noted that U.S. News and World Report had, the previous week, ranked Colorado the second most dangerous state in the nation.[2]
