# Music Industry Initiation Rituals

**Music industry initiation rituals** is the term used in Badlands Media commentary for alleged rites of degradation said to be required for entry into the highest levels of popular music.[1]

## The allegation

Writing for Badlands Media, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] reported that, according to some accounts, reaching the top tier of hip hop or pop music requires visiting a back room at music executives' parties to defile oneself or permit oneself to be violated, after which success is unlimited; refusal ends a career or imposes a glass ceiling.[1] He said the mechanism explains the abundance of one-hit wonders, and that performers who comply enjoy careers far exceeding their talent, often ending as judges on network talent shows.[1]

Carlson added that some are alleged to sacrifice a loved one to obtain this success, while others transition their children as a substitute, citing [[megan-fox|Megan Fox]]'s three transgender children.[1] He argued that the cases of [[sean-combs|P. Diddy]] and, he expected, other musicians to come, show that advancement can turn not on talent but on what a person is willing to do, or to do to others.[1]

## Related practices in film

Carlson described a parallel practice of public shaming in [[hollywood|Hollywood]], in which leading men dress as women — sometimes framed innocuously as comedy, undercover roles or sketch work — before their careers are advanced, and pointed to [[john-cena|John Cena]]'s naked Oscars appearance as a recent instance.[1] The treatment of [[britney-spears|Britney Spears]] served as his central illustration of the human cost.[1]
