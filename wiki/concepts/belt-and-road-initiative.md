# Belt and Road Initiative

The Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) is China's large-scale international infrastructure and development program, presented in Badlands Media commentary as the productive core of an emerging multipolar financial architecture.[1]

## Scale

The development programs unveiled by the productive financial architecture of China and its growing array of allies have already unleashed over $3 trillion in large-scale infrastructure investment around the growing Belt and Road Initiative.[1] The BRI is increasingly tied to the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]], which the article says is quickly preparing the groundwork for a fully integrated new financial architecture founded on real-world values as outlined on numerous occasions by Sergey Glazyev.[1]

## Security dimension

The article notes that Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan, both members of the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] that host Russian bases and Chinese BRI-connected vital infrastructure, saw fighting break out around the time of the 2025 Tianjin summit — timing Ehret associates with suspected Anglo-intelligence operations.[1]

## Outlook

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues there is no shortage of cooperative projects to build together across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and the Americas, which could create real employment, rebuild decayed factories and infrastructure, and establish trust among nations set at each other's throats for too long.[1]
