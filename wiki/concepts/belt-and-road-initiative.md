# Belt and Road Initiative

The Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) is China's global infrastructure and development-finance program. In the Badlands Media article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] states that the development programs unveiled by the productive financial architecture of China and its growing array of allies have already unleashed over $3 trillion in large-scale infrastructure investment around the growing Belt and Road Initiative.

## Relationship to other institutions

The article describes the BRI as tied increasingly to the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]], which it says is preparing the groundwork for a fully integrated financial architecture founded on real-world values as outlined by [[sergey-glazyev|Sergey Glazyev]]. Together with the SCO Development Bank announced in the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]], the BRI is presented as the financial and physical-economic backbone of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]], and as a modern echo of the original [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods]] intention to emit long-term credit for large-scale development projects.

The article also notes that Central Asian states such as Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan host BRI-connected vital infrastructure, and treats the outbreak of fighting between them during the Tianjin summit period as suspiciously timed.
