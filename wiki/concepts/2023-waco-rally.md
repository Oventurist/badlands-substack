# 2023 Waco Rally

The 2023 Waco rally was the first major rally of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2024 presidential campaign, held in Waco, Texas, on Saturday, March 25, 2023.[1]

## Content of the speech

At the rally Trump attacked Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], criticized the investigations into his business and political dealings, and claimed to have spoken with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] about Ukraine.[1] He said he would "have the disastrous war between Russia and Ukraine settled" before reaching the Oval Office if elected again in 2024, adding: "I used to talk to Putin, I get along well with Putin. I used to talk to Putin about it."[1] Describing the war's origins, Trump said: "They started putting soldiers on the border but even he didn't want to do it. He wanted to get a piece. Now it looks like he'll end up probably getting the whole thing."[1]

Trump called Putin and Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] "very smart people" following their meeting in Moscow that week.[1] He also stated, "I'm proud to be the only president in decades who did not start a new war," and argued, "It's no coincidence that the deep state is coming after me even harder, since I pledged to swiftly end the war in Ukraine."[1] He claimed he would broker a Russia–Ukraine settlement "within 24 hours," asserted that he "will prevent World War III," and alleged that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was "courting nuclear holocaust."[1]

## Aftermath and the Manhattan case

Following the rally, speaking to reporters on his plane, Trump said of the [[manhattan-district-attorneys-office|Manhattan District Attorney's]] investigation: "I think they've already dropped the case … they have absolutely nothing," and called it "a fake case."[1] Earlier that day he had written that "The Manhattan D.A. Witch Hunt against me is DEAD, no evidence at all, & it has been conclusively proven that I did nothing wrong!"[1]

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Trump "playing Peacemaker" would carry more weight in the collective mind if done as a civilian, contending that such a move would display his strength and standing on the world stage regardless of title, present an inverted mirror of Biden, supply a public "Solution" to the problem–reaction phases initiated by what he called the globalist uniparty, and help solidify the multipolar world order being ushered in by the [[brics|BRICS]] nations with an implicit stamp of U.S. approval.[1]
