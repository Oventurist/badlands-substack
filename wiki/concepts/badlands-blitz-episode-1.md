# Badlands Blitz Episode 1

"Badlands Blitz Episode 1," subtitled "Dueling Color Revolutions and Narrative Convergence," is the debut installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], a weekly video program published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack on January 10, 2026. The post announcing the episode records 393 likes. It functions both as a programming announcement — explaining the format and intent of the new show — and as the first of the show's weekly reviews of the preceding week's news cycle.

## Purpose and format

The article describes The Blitz as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]," positioning the new show as a companion to the network's daily weekday output rather than a replacement for it. Hosting duties are to alternate among three Badlands contributors: the post's author, [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]. According to the source, episodes are expected to run 60 to 70 minutes on average and to drop on Saturdays, although the debut ran longer than that target because of the introductory explainers about the show's purpose.

A recurring segment involves reading from the Substack comments section, with the hosts dipping into reader responses accumulated over the week — a practice the article frames as part of an ongoing dialogue with what it calls the audience of "beautiful savages."

## Subject matter of the debut

After the format explainers, the hosts turn to what the article calls "the Info War chaos of the previous week," summarized in the source as ranging "from Mad-lad Maduro to the Minnesotamali Madness" — shorthand references to news developments concerning Venezuelan president Nicolás Maduro and to a controversy involving the Somali community in Minnesota. The episode's subtitle, "Dueling Color Revolutions and Narrative Convergence," signals the framing applied to these stories, connecting them to the broader theme of [[color-revolution|color revolutions]] and to the convergence of competing media narratives.

## Editorial and business context

The article closes with an appeal for reader support. It states that all Badlands content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free, but that paid subscriptions are the only mechanism by which the organization can compensate its contributors for their time. The author describes an intention to "serve the Badlands Substack audience with exclusive content this year," characterizing Substack subscribers as among the network's most loyal and engaged, and frames 2026 as a year of stepped-up output. Readers are encouraged to share the show and to comment. The post ends with the author's customary sign-off, "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright."
