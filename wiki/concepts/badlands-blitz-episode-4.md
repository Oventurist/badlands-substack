# Badlands Blitz Episode 4

**Badlands Blitz Episode 4**, subtitled "Color Revolutions Come Home to Roost," is the fourth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video program published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack. It was posted on January 31, 2026, and had attracted 279 likes at the time the text was archived.

## Format and stated purpose

The accompanying post opens with the customary address to readers — "Badlanders!" — and restates the show's remit: The Blitz is "intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." The host is joined for this episode by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], continuing the program's rotating co-host arrangement.

As in previous episodes, the hosts close by dipping "into the comments section to see what you beautiful savages have been saying all week," the recurring subscriber-interaction segment of the show.

## Topics covered

The episode is described as covering "a week that was absolutely full of [[system-sway|System Sway]]," with the host arguing that virtually every subject treated in the week's briefs converges on a single explanatory frame. The three examples named are the expansion and acceleration of the [[minnesota-color-revolution|Minnesota Color Revolution]], the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County Election Raid]], and what the author calls the ongoing pattern of [[canadian-capitulation|Canadian Capitulation]]. According to the source, these disparate stories "are converging to illuminate the same Macro System, and its inevitable dissolution" — the show's framing of a [[color-revolution|color revolution]] methodology now visible inside the United States rather than only abroad.

## Runtime and subscription appeal

The host repeats a running production note: "As I've been saying from the jump, the Blitz is intended to get CLOSE to being a 60-minute show, and our last two nearly hit the mark!" The post then makes the standard appeal to the newsletter's audience, stating that Badlands wants "to serve the Badlands Substack audience with exclusive content this year," that all Badlands content — The Blitz included — will remain free, and that paid subscriptions are the only means by which the outlet can compensate its contributors for their time. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription, cancellable at any time.

The post signs off with the program's habitual valediction: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
