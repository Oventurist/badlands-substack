# Great American Restoration Tour

The Great American Restoration Tour (GART) is a live event series staged by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], bringing the network's show hosts and Substack contributors together with viewers, readers and supporters.[1]

As promoted in the April 19, 2023 *[[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]]*, GART was about to arrive in Chandler, Arizona for "a weekend of fun, conversation and inspiration," described as a convergence in the desert around what the brief called a "humble, growing hub for citizen journalism."[1] VIP tickets had sold out by then, though a final batch of in-person tickets remained available along with single-day passes for local attendees.[1] The entire event — all live panels, Q&As and related programming — was additionally offered through a special pay-per-view live stream via the Badlands Media events page.[1]

## Cocoa Beach (GART 10)

In November 2025, Badlands announced that the tenth stop of the tour would take place that week in Cocoa Beach, Florida, the network's second event "in the land of rockets both real and fake," and the fourth Great American Restoration Tour of what contributors call the new American Golden Age.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that the events offer an opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots and create "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place," adding that the team hoped to see familiar faces and, as at every prior GART, plenty of new ones.[2] A schedule and ticket link were published with the announcement.[2] An earlier stop, GART 9, was held on August 15, 2025 in Deadwood, South Dakota, where [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] delivered remarks arguing that elections are fake.[2]

## GART 9 (Deadwood, August 2025)

In July 2025, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] announced that in-person and virtual tickets were available for the ninth stop of the tour, returning to Deadwood, South Dakota in August.[4] The promotion, carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], joked that "no matter what Jordan says, the karaoke isn't terrible… at least not entirely," and noted that all sessions would be recorded and available on demand for virtual ticket holders.[4]

## GART 6 (Myrtle Beach, November 2024)

In November 2024 the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] promoted the tour's Myrtle Beach, South Carolina stop, running Thursday through Sunday of that week, and encouraged readers unable to attend in person to join "hundreds (maybe thousands)" of fellow Badlands savages with a virtual ticket.[5] Virtual tickets allowed holders to stream the entire event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and included long-term DVR access to panel replays after the weekend so that viewers who could not watch live would not miss out.[5] The ticket also granted access to an exclusive GART Telegram chat where attendees could interact with Badlands hosts, view behind-the-scenes photos and videos, and take part in "virtual shenanigans galore."[5]

## Tenth tour and virtual tickets (2025)

Ahead of the tenth Great American Restoration Tour in Cocoa Beach, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] announced in October 2025 that virtual tickets were on sale for those unable to attend in person.[6] The announcement described GART as a four-day "patriot-packed" event held four times a year, where attendees meet like-minded Americans, hear content from Badlands hosts, and take part in the wider Badlands community.[6]

## GART 7 — Las Vegas (March 2025)

The seventh Great American Restoration Tour event was scheduled to take place from 6 through 9 March 2025 in Las Vegas, Nevada, and was promoted as the first GART event of 2025, with both in-person and virtual tickets offered.[7]

## Tenth stop: Cocoa Beach (November 2025)

In early November 2025, Badlands Media released the schedule for the tenth stop of the Great American Restoration Tour, held in Cocoa Beach, Florida — described as the organization's second event "in the land of rockets both real and fake" and the fourth GART of what hosts called the new American Golden Age.[8] Announcing the event, host Burning Bright wrote that the tour stops had presented an opportunity for attendees to meet and network with fellow patriots and to create "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[8]

## Las Vegas stop (March 2025)

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted a Las Vegas edition of the tour running March 6–9, 2025, telling readers "Badlands is descending on Sin City."[9] Virtual tickets were advertised as including access to an exclusive GART Telegram chat, ticket-holder livestreams, and behind-the-scenes interviews with attendees.[9]

## GART 7: Las Vegas (March 2025)

The seventh stop on the tour took place in Las Vegas, Nevada, beginning Thursday, March 6, 2025, and was billed as the first GART of the "new American Golden Age."[10] Badlands promoted the event as an opportunity for readers to meet and network with fellow patriots and to create "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[10] Those unable to attend in person could purchase a virtual ticket to stream the entirety of the event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, with long-term DVR access to panel replays afterward and access to an exclusive GART Telegram chat featuring Badlands hosts and behind-the-scenes material.[10] Because the Badlands team was travelling to Las Vegas, no [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] was published that Friday.[10]

## Cocoa Beach (September 2023)

An earlier Cocoa Beach stop opened on Friday, September 8, 2023, with the first live panel scheduled for 9 a.m. ET.[11] In-person tickets, including single-day options, remained available to late-comers, while a Virtual Stream was also on sale granting access to all three days of GART panels, entry to an exclusive Telegram chat with fellow viewers and attendees that continued beyond the event, and permanent access to video replays of all Cocoa Beach panels.[11] Badlands described the format as letting subscribers "experience everything you love about Badlands in the form of a live, ticketed event."[11]

## Deadwood, South Dakota (GART 5)

In August 2024 Badlands Media announced the fifth stop on the tour, held in Deadwood, South Dakota, and issued a "last call" for attendance in that week's [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[12] The outlet described the events as an opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots and as "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[12]

Those unable to attend in person could purchase a virtual ticket to stream the entire event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, with long-term DVR access to panel replays following the weekend.[12] Virtual tickets also granted access to an exclusive GART Telegram chat with hundreds of other attendees and Badlands hosts, behind-the-scenes photos and videos.[12] Programming for the Deadwood stop was billed as ranging from coming "Full Circle on the Q Drops" to futurism and Freedom Cities.[12]

### GART 7, Las Vegas
The Badlands News Brief of February 20, 2025 announced that The Great American Restoration Tour 7 would take place from March 6 through 9, 2025 in Las Vegas, Nevada, billed as the first GART event of 2025 with both in-person and virtual tickets available.[13]

### GART 9 ticket announcement (July 4, 2025)
The Independence Day 2025 edition of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] advertised that in-person and virtual tickets to GART 9 were available immediately, with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] "returning to Deadwood, SD in August, for the 9th stop on the Great American Restoration Tour."[14] The promotion invited readers to attend and joked that "no matter what Jordan says, the karaoke isn't terrible… at least not entirely."[14] It reiterated that virtual tickets were on sale as well and that all sessions would be recorded and available on demand, closing with an invitation to "get your tickets and come hang out."[14]

## Chandler, Arizona launch (April 2023)

The tour's inaugural gathering took place in Chandler, Arizona, over three days beginning in mid-April 2023, after months of advance discussion by the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] team.[15] Many of the outlet's show hosts and Substack writers held group panels, Q&A sessions and informal meet-and-greets across the weekend.[15] VIP tickets sold out ahead of the event, and only a handful of single-day and full-weekend in-person tickets remained from the final batch released the week prior.[15] All panels were streamed live for holders of a virtual event ticket, with replays made available for six months afterward.[15]

## Tenth stop: Cocoa Beach, Florida

In November 2025 Badlands convened in Cocoa Beach, Florida for the tenth stop along the Great American Restoration Tour, billed as the fourth of the new American Golden Age, with panels beginning at 10 a.m. ET on Thursday, November 6.[16] Attendees used the Space Coast setting to watch rocket launches from the beach, including a [[spacex|SpaceX]] launch and the scrubbed [[united-launch-alliance|United Launch Alliance]] Atlas 5 flight carrying the ViaSat-3 F2 satellite, with [[viasat|Viasat]] personnel staying at the same GART hotel.[16]

## GART Cocoa Beach (September 2023)

In September 2023, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted a Cocoa Beach stop of the tour, telling readers they could "experience everything you love about Badlands in the form of a live, ticketed event."[17] In-person tickets, including single-day options, remained available for late-comers, and virtual stream tickets were also on sale.[17] The virtual pass granted access to all three days of GART panels, entry to an exclusive Telegram chat with fellow viewers and attendees that continued beyond the event, and permanent access to the video replay of all GART Cocoa Beach panels.[17]

The Cocoa Beach promotion was repeated in the September 7, 2023 brief, which noted that the tour "hits Cocoa Beach this week" and that virtual stream tickets were available then.[18]

## GART 9 Deadwood

Tickets for the ninth Great American Restoration Tour went on sale in June 2025, with the event scheduled for August 14–17, 2025 in historic Deadwood, South Dakota, and promoted by Badlands Media as expected to be the biggest GART yet.[19]
