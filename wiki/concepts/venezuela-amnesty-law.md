# Venezuela Amnesty Law (2026)

The Venezuela amnesty law of February 2026 is a limited amnesty measure approved by the [[national-assembly-of-venezuela|National Assembly]] as part of broader legal and political shifts under [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s transitional government.[1]

## Content and passage

The bill was approved after a second debate headed by [[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]], brother of President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]].[1] It is reported to provide for the release of hundreds of political protestors held in Venezuelan prisons, while omitting homicide and other violent crimes from its scope.[1]

## Context and criticism

The amnesty advanced in the same period that Attorney General [[tarek-william-saab|Tarek William Saab]] resigned and was named acting Ombudsman, a move human rights organizations condemned as a setback for justice reform.[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted the paradox that Delcy and Jorge Rodríguez — figures alleged in Ralph Pezzullo's book *Stolen Elections: The Takedown of Democracies Worldwide* to be central to election theft — were now releasing political prisoners their own prior regime had jailed, asking "when you overthrow a regime, do you usually follow their leadership succession plan and employ the next-in-line regime members to run the new government?"[1] She linked this to Badlands Book Club discussions and to the question of whether a public "rewrite" of the Venezuela election fraud story was intended to get ahead of, and possibly undermine, the restoration of Venezuela, while asking pointedly where opposition figure [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] had gone.[1] She also noted that during Tuesday's [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union]] address, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] discussed his strong partnership with President Delcy Rodríguez.[1]
