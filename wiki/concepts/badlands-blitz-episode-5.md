# Badlands Blitz Episode 5

**Badlands Blitz Episode 5**, subtitled "The END of the START …," is the fifth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video program published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack. It was posted on February 7, 2026, and had attracted 290 likes at the time the text was archived.

## Format and stated purpose

The accompanying post opens with the program's customary address to readers — "Badlanders!" — and restates the show's remit: The Blitz is "intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." For this episode the host is joined by [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], continuing the show's rotating co-host arrangement, and the post promises that together they "wade through the noise and find [[sovereign-signal|Sovereign Signal]] galore" — adding parenthetically, "(As if you're surprised.)"

## Topics covered

The post previews the episode's range as running "From Iranian Nuclear Rugs to the END of the [[new-start-treaty|New START]] treaty," and poses the framing question that gives the installment its analytical spine: "when is escalation actually de-escalation?" The formulation is characteristic of the program's interpretive method, in which apparently alarming geopolitical developments are read as controlled moves within a larger de-escalatory process rather than as drift toward conflict. The reference to "Iranian Nuclear Rugs" alludes to the week's coverage of Iran's nuclear file in the daily briefs.

The second question raised for the episode concerns media dynamics rather than geopolitics: the host asks whether observers "are taking the right lessons from the proliferation and amplification of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein Narrative]]," treating the story's saturation of public attention as itself a phenomenon requiring analysis.

## Subscription appeal

As in previous installments, the post makes the outlet's standing appeal to its audience. It states that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," but that "the only way we're able to pay our people for their time is due to your generous support, should you choose to provide it." Readers who value "the work this Substack team does on a daily basis and the step-up in said work for 2026" are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription, cancellable at any time.

The post signs off with the program's habitual valediction: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
