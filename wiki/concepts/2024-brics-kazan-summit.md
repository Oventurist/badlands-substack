# 2024 BRICS Kazan Summit

The 2024 BRICS summit, held in Kazan, Russia in October 2024, was a three-day gathering at which [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] — ostracised by the West and labelled a possible war criminal by the International Criminal Court — hosted representatives from 36 countries, including China, India and [[iran|Iran]].[1] Russia held the rotating chair of the [[brics|BRICS]] group that year, and Moscow described the meeting as the largest international gathering Putin had hosted since ordering the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022.[1]

## Agenda and de-dollarization

One of the summit's main aims was to accelerate ways to reduce the number of dollar transactions and so mitigate the ability of the United States to use the threat of sanctions to impose its political will.[1] Badlands commentary framed this as the practical program of a sovereign bloc flourishing while the hegemonic West flounders, and speculated that Saudi Arabia's deliberate delay in formally accepting its invitation to join BRICS was a strategic hold — perhaps waiting for the right moment to help end the fiat U.S. dollar.[1]

## Attendance

Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] greeted Putin in Kazan as his "dear friend," praising the "profound" friendship between the two countries and observing that "The world is undergoing profound changes unseen in a century, and the international situation is chaotic and intertwined."[1] Badlands enumerated a guest list including Xi, Narendra Modi, Nicolás Maduro, Cyril Ramaphosa, Ibrahim Traoré, Mahmoud Abbas, Abdel Fattah el-Sisi and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, and noted that Julian Assange's father was also in attendance.[1]

The attendance of UN Secretary-General [[antonio-guterres|António Guterres]] — who had also attended the previous year's summit — infuriated many in the West, including the Ukrainian foreign ministry, given that the ICC issued warrants for Putin's arrest in March 2023 over the abduction of children.[1] The UN said Guterres would repeat his view that the invasion of Ukraine breaches the UN Charter.[1] Badlands read his presence as a major signal that the geopolitical tides had shifted toward a multipolar world order.[1]

Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] was notably absent, hosting the Hashemite King of Jordan instead — an encounter Badlands presented as a redemption arc tracing back to 1902 and the Saudi campaign that drove the Hashemites from Mecca.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

A second Badlands take, from [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], argued the summit was "dripping with signal" and represented rapid narrative progression en route to "Trump the Peacemaker."[1] In this reading Putin, Xi and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] are the principal market-movers both in the realm of the real and in the psychological theater of the Info War; by calling the Western world a "hegemony" and poking at "New World Order" framing, the "New Axis" of Russia and China lets Info Warriors treat them either as Alpha Chads of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] or as necessary villains for the current arc.[1] "Two legs of the tripod are in place," the commentary concluded, awaiting the third.[1]

## India-China thaw on the sidelines

Shortly before [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] were due to arrive in Kazan for the summit hosted by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], India announced an agreement with China on patrolling arrangements along the Line of Actual Control, easing the [[india-china-border-dispute|India-China border dispute]] four years after deadly clashes.[2] Indian Foreign Secretary [[vikram-misri|Vikram Misri]] said weeks of diplomatic and military contacts had produced "disengagement and a resolution of the issues that had arisen in these areas in 2020," while Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesman [[lin-jian|Lin Jian]] confirmed the two sides had "reached a solution."[2] Speculation had mounted that the two leaders would use the Kazan sidelines to discuss normalizing relations.[2]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] presented the timing as evidence that the [[brics|BRICS]]-centered "Sovereign Alliance" was establishing itself as the champion of peace and prosperity while the West declined, expressing envy at not being in Kazan planning "The Future."[2]
