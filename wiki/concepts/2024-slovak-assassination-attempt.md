# 2024 Slovak Assassination Attempt

The 2024 Slovak assassination attempt was the shooting of Slovak Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] on Wednesday, May 15, 2024, in which the populist leader sustained several gunshot wounds and was rushed into surgery.[1] By the following day Fico was reported to be improving, with Deputy Prime Minister Tomas Taraba telling the BBC that the prime minister "is not in a life-threatening situation at this moment" and adding that "the operation went well – and I guess in the end he will survive."[1]

## Official characterization

Deputy Prime Minister Robert Kalinak told reporters that "there is no doubt" the attack was a politically motivated assassination attempt, framing it as the product of hostility toward Fico from political opponents: "The inability to accept the will of some part of the public, which some group does not like, is the result that they have worked towards today."[1] A widely circulated video of the detained suspect's interrogation showed the man, identified as [[juraj-cintula|Juraj Cintula]], stating that he "disagreed" with his government's policies.[1]

## Western media response

Coverage of the shooting in Western outlets drew criticism for appearing to rationalize the attack. Journalist [[glenn-greenwald|Glenn Greenwald]] singled out a Sky News segment, writing that the report not only came "close to justifying it because he opposes aid to Ukraine," but also "casually imply[ied] that he's being paid by the Kremlin," a habit he described as "so prevalent in the West, and toxic."[1] Fico, alongside Hungarian Prime Minister [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]], had been a prominent dissenter from the [[nato|NATO]] consensus on Ukraine.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the shooting through the lens of [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]], the post-World War II "stay-behind" networks in Europe used, in this reading, for assassinations, color revolutions, and psychological operations on behalf of the CIA.[1] He noted that Fico had voted against Ukraine's accession to NATO, opposed the World Health Organization pandemic treaty, and two days before the shooting had [[slovakia|Slovakia]] abstain from the European Union's New Pact on Migration and Asylum, which would govern how all 27 member states absorb future migration waves.[1] In his framing, Fico "habitually opposed the hegemony," and Slovakia's position on Ukraine's western border made that opposition strategically significant.[1] Fellow contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] situated the attempt on Fico — described as a close friend of Orbán and a known critic of NATO's anti-Russia sanctions and anti-China rhetoric — within a broader week of setbacks for globalist actors.[1]

## Widening of the investigation

Slovak police announced that they were investigating the possible involvement of more people than a single 'lone-wolf' shooter in the attempt on Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]]'s life.[2] Government officials had initially said a single perpetrator with a political motive was responsible for the 15 May attack; days later authorities said cooperation with domestic and foreign intelligence services had broadened the probe to include a version in which an unidentified group may have been linked to the crime.[2] Interior Minister [[matus-sutaj-estok|Matuš Šutaj Eštok]] cited the deletion of the assailant's social media communications by another person roughly two hours after the shooting as support for the broader-plot theory.[2] Coverage stressed that this was the first such attack on a European leader in more than two decades and that it had highlighted inflammatory politics prevalent in [[slovakia|Slovakia]] since the pandemic and turbocharged by Russia's war on Ukraine.[2]
