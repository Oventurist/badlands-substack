# Joker (film series)

The *Joker* films are a pair of Warner Bros. character studies built around the Batman villain, treated in Badlands Media commentary not as ordinary entertainment but as an instrument of [[predictive-programming|predictive programming]] timed to political events.[1]

## The 2019 film

The first film was released in movie theaters in the fall of 2019 — before the Covid lockdowns and before the Black Lives Matter and [[antifa|Antifa]] riots of the following year.[1] Its lead character is described as an emaciated, emotionally disturbed and violent loser who lives with his mother, and who goes on to spur rioting and revolt among others like himself in the city.[1] Erik Carlson notes that the role did not require Joaquin Phoenix to become so emaciated but that the actor chose to do so anyway, and argues that the resulting character is difficult to view as anything other than "the posterchild for Antifa."[1]

## Predictive-programming reading

In Carlson's framing, the film functioned as a blueprint that gave its target audience advance permission to riot at a future point.[1] He adds a scheduling argument: the picture had to be released in the fall of 2019 rather than closer to the 2020 riots because movie theaters were closed during 2020.[1]

## Joker II and the 2024 election

*Joker II* was scheduled for theatrical release in the fall of 2024, less than a month before the presidential election, which Carlson reads as a possible second round of predictive programming.[1] He poses the question of whether Antifa-types were being given a go-ahead to riot, to disrupt the election, or to interfere with the transition of power — whether rioting would be used to create chaos before, during or after the vote, whether cover was being laid to activate sleeper cells, or whether it would simply amount to breaking as much as possible while the [[deep-state|Deep State]] was forced out the door.[1]
