# R/FIMI — Counter Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference Hub

R/FIMI, the Counter Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference hub, was an office housed at the U.S. [[state-department|State Department]] and created in the final days of the Biden administration as a successor vehicle for the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]] (GEC).[1]

## Origins

In November 2024, shortly after the presidential election, a continuing resolution for the federal budget became the subject of intense dispute, in part because it attempted to reauthorize the censorship capacity of the GEC.[1] The dispute escalated into a public scandal; [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] threatened to fund primary challengers against any Republican who voted for the resolution.[1] The effort succeeded — the budget was killed to prevent the GEC from being funded, and without reauthorization the center was set to expire that day.[1]

As one of its final acts, the outgoing Biden administration created R/FIMI and lodged it within the State Department, retaining the same staff and continuing the GEC's functions under a new acronym.[1]

## Function

Badlands Media described R/FIMI as a web of private and governmental operatives tasked with policing speech in ways that served the deep political establishment.[1] It formed one node in the broader [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship-industrial complex]], a network rather than a single institution, spanning CISA, the CIA, USAID, NATO, the UN, the EU, universities, the State Department, the GEC and various civil society organizations.[1]

## Termination

In March 2025, Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and Acting Under Secretary for Public Diplomacy [[darren-beattie|Darren Beattie]] terminated more than 100 contractors associated with the R/FIMI hub, ending the GEC's covert continuation.[1] Rubio subsequently discussed the shake-up with former State Department cybersecurity official [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]], and authored an op-ed in The Federalist arguing that the government's censorship efforts had been specifically designed to bankrupt and destroy outlets that countered official narratives.[1] The article noted the particular indignity that the apparatus was financed with the tax dollars of the very people it was built to silence.[1]
