# Lagevrio

Lagevrio is the COVID-19 antiviral pill developed by [[merck|Merck & Co.]] and designed to treat infection by altering the virus's genetic code.[1] In early 2023 the drug drew scrutiny after a preprint study linked its use to new, viable and spreading mutations of the virus.[1]

## Mutation findings

Researchers in the United States and at the Francis Crick Institute, Imperial College London and other UK institutions identified Lagevrio-linked mutations in viral samples from dozens of patients.[1] [[bloomberg|Bloomberg]]'s account of the study noted researcher concern that the drug might give rise to more contagious or more health-threatening variations of COVID-19.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized the result as the drug "actually causing the virus to mutate in patients, thereby making the virus far more communicable and deadly," and questioned whether the effect was accidental or intentional.[1] The item ran as the lead entry in the February 3, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
