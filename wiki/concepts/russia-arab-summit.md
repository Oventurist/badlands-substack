# Russia-Arab Summit

The Russia-Arab summit is a first-of-its-kind meeting between Russia and the member states of the [[arab-league|Arab League]], announced in May 2025 and scheduled for October 15 of that year in Moscow.[1]

## The invitation

Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] invited the heads of state and government of Arab League countries, along with the League's Secretary-General, to attend; the Kremlin published the invitation on its official website on a Saturday while Arab leaders were gathered in Baghdad to discuss the war in Gaza.[1] "We intend to further actively develop constructive dialogue with the League of Arab States, as well as friendly relations with all its members," Putin said.[1] He acknowledged that the meeting would take place "in a difficult international situation," pointing to the "sharp escalation of the Palestinian-Israeli confrontation" and "numerous civilian casualties," and described the Arab League as "an effective mechanism for multilateral dialogue and joint response to the challenges and threats faced by the peoples of the Middle East and North Africa."[1]

## Context

At the 34th Arab League Summit held the same Saturday, regional leaders collectively condemned Israel's military actions in Gaza, called for an immediate ceasefire, and pledged financial support for the reconstruction of the enclave.[1]

## Badlands commentary

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry judged the timing deliberate, writing that Putin "saw the same clips that we all saw last week and had some serious FOMO" following [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s Gulf tour, and that the natural response was "to call up the lads and invite them all over for a scrum."[1] He found Putin's decision to formally include the League's leadership notable, comparing it to Syria's 2023 readmission to the organization after an eleven-year absence — an arrangement he attributed to groundwork laid by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] as Secretary of State in 2012 — and asked whether Putin would use the League to displace the new Damascus leadership and restore Bashar al-Assad now that Western sanctions had been lifted.[1]
