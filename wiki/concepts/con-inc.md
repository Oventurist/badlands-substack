# Con Inc.

"Con Inc." is a pejorative shorthand used within the [[america-first|America First]] and [[maga|MAGA]] online community for what its users describe as the professionalized conservative movement — a network of pundits, media outlets, think tanks and consultants alleged to profit from conservative grievance while functioning, wittingly or not, as controlled opposition. The label plays on both "conservative incorporated" and the word "con."

## Treatment in the corpus

The Badlands Media essay [[all-assets-deployed|All Assets Deployed]] makes Con Inc. a central subject. Its author, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], argues that in the weeks surrounding the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]] a "baited and then actualized 'turn'" occurred, in which Con Inc. and a portion of the alternative right pivoted against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the heart of the America First movement. The author draws particular attention to the fact that the figures who turned were, in his account, precisely those who had "retained immense influence and engagement on the most notorious Globalist censorious platforms" during the height of Big Tech's crackdown on dissenting speech — a fact he treats as diagnostic.

Rather than lamenting the split, the essay welcomes it, describing Trump's Mar-a-Lago announcement as an occasion to separate "the MAGA and America First wheat from the Con Inc., controlled opposition chaff." The author warns that Con Inc. and the "DeSantis over Trump" crowd, aligned with [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], would "smell blood in the water from a somewhat demoralized and depressed MAGA base" and attempt to turn hairline fractures into chasms, fragmenting the movement into "an archipelago of intra-faction wars." In the framework of the [[information-war|information war]], he concludes, "knowing who is whom and what is what IS the war itself."
