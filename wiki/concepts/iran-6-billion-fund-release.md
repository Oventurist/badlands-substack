# Iran $6 Billion Fund Release

The Iran $6 billion fund release refers to the unfreezing of approximately $6 billion in Iranian assets by the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration in September 2023, shortly before the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]] on Israel.[1]

## Controversy

Democrats came out swinging against allegations that the released funds could be spent on anything other than humanitarian purposes.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], rejected that defense on two grounds: money is fungible, and the U.S. government has no reliable mechanism to monitor or influence how [[iran|Iran]] allocates its funds.[1]

She noted that [[politico|Politico]] had been getting ahead of the narrative three weeks earlier, and that Iran had reportedly claimed it would use its unfrozen funds in whatever way it wished.[1] On the Saturday of the Hamas assault, Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] issued a statement on X saying, "God willing, the cancer of the usurper Zionist regime will be eradicated at the hands of the Palestinian people and the Resistance forces throughout the region" — a post X ruled violated platform terms but allowed to remain.[1]

Ashe linked the release to a wider pattern of establishment war-drumming: billions in new funding to Israel following the $6 billion release, alongside Biden's September 20, 2023 promise of full and permanent U.S. government support to Palestine.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the escalation as playing out against the backdrop of the Biden administration's recent and past (Obama-era) attempts to fund the Iranian regime, in deliberate contrast with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]].[1]

## Refreezing agreement (October 2023)

On October 12, 2023, the United States and [[qatar|Qatar]] reached an agreement to prevent [[iran|Iran]] from accessing the $6 billion unfrozen as part of the prisoner swap.[2] Deputy Treasury Secretary [[wally-adeyemo|Wally Adeyemo]] told House Democrats in a closed-door briefing that the money "isn't going anywhere anytime soon."[2] The agreement followed calls from lawmakers of both parties to re-freeze the funds in the wake of the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]], as Washington scrutinized Iran's role in backing [[hamas|Hamas]].[2] The Biden administration said there was no evidence Iran had played a direct role in the assault, while the [[white-house|White House]] maintained Tehran was complicit given its decades of support for the group.[2]

## Carlson: the announcement as a de-escalation device (October 2023)

In "Was the Israel-Hamas Conflict De-escalated?" (October 18, 2023), [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treated the $6 billion release as the pivot of the entire October 2023 escalation.[3] He noted that on September 11, 2023 it was reported that the [[biden-administration|Biden Administration]] was freeing up $6 billion to [[iran|Iran]] in a five-for-five prisoner exchange, and argued that no administration could be "that tone deaf" as to unfreeze funds for a terror-supporting government on the anniversary of the largest terrorist attack on American soil by accident.[3]

After the [[hamas|Hamas]] assault on Israeli civilians, Carlson wrote, observers across the political spectrum immediately assumed the attack had been financed with the newly freed funds, while the [[white-house|White House]] insisted every cent remained in the Qatari bank.[3] Carlson said that although he could not verify the claim, he believed it was true — in his framing because "the Patriots" retained enough control to prevent the money reaching Tehran.[3]

His central theory was that Trump and allied insiders learned that [[joe-biden|Biden]], and probably [[barack-obama|Obama]], intended to send Iran a large sum secretly, and forced the administration to acknowledge the transfer publicly; having to attach itself to the money in public made the funds unusable, because any spending could be traced straight back.[3] He likened the manoeuvre to "pull[ing] a fire alarm to avoid a fire from being set," and cited the October 12, 2023 ABC News headline "US, Qatar agree to prevent Iran from tapping previously frozen $6 billion fund" as the visible result.[3] On this reading, Iran was substantially removed from the equation and a wider regional war was defused.[3]
