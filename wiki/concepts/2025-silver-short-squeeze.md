# 2025 Silver Short Squeeze

The 2025 silver short squeeze was a dislocation in the London silver market in October 2025, during which the spot price of silver pushed above $51 per troy ounce on Friday, October 10, and rose further the following Monday, driven by a short squeeze and a shortage of metal in London vaults.[1] Market observers described the resulting lack of liquidity as comparable to — or worse than — conditions in the early 1980s, when the Hunt brothers attempted to corner the silver market in an episode that ended with a crash in prices.[1]

## Concurrent market turmoil

The squeeze coincided with severe stress in cryptocurrency markets, where the data tracker Coinglass characterized the events of the same Friday as the "largest liquidation in history," producing heavy declines in cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin.[1] Attention also briefly turned to political instability in Japan before shifting back to the United States.[1]

## Trade escalation with China

On the same Friday, [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] said he saw "no reason" to meet President [[xi-jinping|Xi]] of [[china|China]] in October, threatened restrictions on certain US software exports, and warned that he would impose tariffs of 100% on Chinese goods beginning November 1.[1] The reported driver of this response — which followed several months of constructive US–China talks and apparent efforts by the administration to stabilize the relationship — was China's renewed tightening of export controls on critical raw materials, particularly rare earth elements.[1]
