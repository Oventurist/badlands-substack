# JD Vance 2028 Presidential Planning

JD Vance 2028 presidential planning refers to the preparatory political strategy attributed to Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] in late December 2025, reported by [[axios|Axios]], for an expected run for the presidency in 2028.[1]

## The reported strategy

According to the reporting, Vance planned to "fly above" the rising civil war within the MAGA movement — campaigning coast to coast in the midterm elections while sticking close to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The core tension identified was that Vance must prepare for a national campaign without appearing too eager, since Trump is not inclined to share the spotlight.[1]

Vance's aides insisted publicly that he was focused on the November 2026 midterms rather than 2028.[1] Speaking with outside Vance allies and others familiar with his thinking, Axios assembled a five-pronged plan for making 2026 pay off later: be a loyal vice president; stay out of MAGA's civil war; raise big money; play big in the 2026 cycle; and lean on an "influencer army."[1] A statement provided to the outlet said that "In the new year, Vice President Vance will continue delivering on those promises while campaigning aggressively with Republicans across the country to win the midterms."[1]

## Commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], writing for the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, characterized the coverage as a coordinated messaging operation from "both sides of the Uniparty" promoting Vance as the next ruler, and dismissed the rollout with the phrase "Grovel time!"[1]
