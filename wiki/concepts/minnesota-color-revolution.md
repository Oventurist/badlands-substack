# Minnesota Color Revolution

The **Minnesota Color Revolution** is the label used in Badlands Media coverage for a set of events in the state of Minnesota that the outlet interprets through the [[color-revolution|color revolution]] framework — that is, as an internally driven regime-change operation rather than ordinary domestic politics.[1]

## Coverage

The subject was a leading topic of the fourth episode of [[badlands-blitz|The Blitz]], published January 31, 2026 under the title "Color Revolutions Come Home to Roost," in which host and guest [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] discussed what they described as the expansion and acceleration of the Minnesota Color Revolution.[1] The episode presented it not as an isolated state-level story but as one of several converging threads — alongside the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County Election Raid]] and the pattern of [[canadian-capitulation|Canadian Capitulation]] — said to illuminate a single [[macro-system|Macro System]] and its inevitable dissolution.[1]

The recap post did not enumerate the specific Minnesota incidents involved, treating the topic instead as an ongoing storyline familiar to readers of the daily Badlands Brief published Monday through Friday.[1]
