# Minnesota Color Revolution

The **Minnesota Color Revolution** is the name given by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators to unrest and political conflict in the state of Minnesota which they interpret as an instance of [[color-revolution|color revolution]] methodology applied inside the United States.

The term appears in Badlands Blitz Episode 4, published January 31, 2026 and subtitled "Color Revolutions Come Home to Roost," where the host cites "the expansion and acceleration of the Minnesota Color Revolution" as one of the week's principal stories. In the episode's framing it is grouped with the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County Election Raid]] and [[canadian-capitulation|Canadian Capitulation]] as evidence of [[system-sway|System Sway]] — separate events said to illuminate a single "Macro System" approaching dissolution.

The source article does not supply further detail on the specific incidents in Minnesota, discussing them only by reference; the substantive treatment was given in the video episode and in the week's Badlands Brief editions. The characterisation is interpretive and contested.
