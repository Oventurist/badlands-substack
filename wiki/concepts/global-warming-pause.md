# Global Warming Pause

The "global warming pause" refers to the period between 1998 and 2012 in which, according to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s June 5, 2025 Badlands Media essay, warming trends tapered off to a near standstill despite continuously increasing [[carbon-dioxide|carbon dioxide]] emissions.[1] Ehret describes the hiatus as an embarrassment for modelers whose scare-mongering predictions "fell to pieces," to the point that they can only pretend the pause does not exist.[1]

The pause is presented as one of a set of anomalies in the temperature record. Another is the absence of any warming during the period of intensive industrial growth from 1940 to 1977 — an interval in which many climate scientists were instead ringing the alarm over an impending ice age.[1] A third is the [[medieval-warm-period|medieval warming period]].[1] Taken together, Ehret argues, these anomalies pose the question of why they would appear at all if CO2 drove temperature.[1]
