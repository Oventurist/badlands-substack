# Clinton Body Count

The "Clinton body count" is a long-standing claim that former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and former Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] are responsible for the deaths of a number of individuals who could be regarded as political opponents.[1]

## Trump's May 2025 amplification

Among a litany of social media posts shared on Saturday, May 17, 2025, sitting President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] resurfaced a 2016 video overlaid with the caption "THE VIDEO HILLARY CLINTON DOES NOT WANT YOU TO SEE," which rehearsed the claims.[1] The Washington Post reported the material as false, situating it among what it described as thousands of false or misleading statements Trump has made about political opponents across his two terms, and noted Democratic warnings that such rhetoric could lead to violence and Trump's repeated vows to use federal power against his foes.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer Burning Bright treated the episode as significant precisely because of its source, observing that "Donald Trump, the sitting POTUS just boosted the most direct Clinton Body Count proof he could, including [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]]"[1] He described the move as "indicative of the timeline war we're engaged in, and the fact that said timelines are converging."[1]
