# Mass Psychosis

Mass Psychosis is the term used by Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] for the engineered condition of collective belief that he argues has been deliberately cultivated in Western populations over decades in order to guide public reaction to touchstone events in the American political, economic and social theaters.[1] In his usage the phenomenon is not spontaneous social contagion but a *directed* program — the product of sustained narrative management by the globalist element variously called the [[deep-state|Deep State]] or the [[cabal|Cabal]].[1]

## The programmed collective

Burning Bright frames Mass Psychosis as the mechanism by which the enemy attempts to build a "programmed, mindless and zombified collective," in explicit contrast to the "decentralized, self-affirming and self-actualizing WE" that he sees emerging in the [[information-war|Information War]].[1] Individuals held inside the psychosis are, in this reading, usable assets: their reactions can be predicted and harvested to advance the controllers' paradigm during manufactured crises.[1] He argues that the controllers seek to control the public for precisely the same reason they fear it — because ordinary people can choose to join a growing, decentralized collective that increases in influence, complexity and sovereignty by the day.[1]

## Breaking the psychosis

A distinctive feature of Burning Bright's treatment is his claim that deprogramming does not require conversion. One need not have an audience adopt one's own paradigm or worldview, he writes, in order to gain a marked advantage or make significant progress toward freeing them from "the matrix."[1] It is sufficient to provoke a person to examine, and potentially reject, the paradigm they were either born into or programmed into: the result is "a trapped mind" unchained, one that may never fully adopt the alternative view but that can no longer be used to advance the controllers' agenda.[1]

He applied this standard to the coverage of the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia-Ukraine war]], arguing that reachable minds need not engage with complex topics such as [[false-flag-operation|false flags]] or [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] to begin sensing that [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and its leadership are not what the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] first portrayed, and that the supposed invaders are not the mustache-twirling villains presented in early 2022 news chyrons and social media reactions.[1]

## Relationship to other Badlands concepts

Mass Psychosis functions as the negative counterpart to the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] in Badlands writing: the same mass cognitive substrate, either colonized by directed programming or liberated into decentralized, self-directed inquiry.[1] Burning Bright treats the rapid public dismantling of the November 2022 attempt to blame [[russia|Russia]] for a missile strike on [[poland|Polish]] territory as evidence that the psychosis is weakening, since the narrative collapsed within roughly twelve hours under distributed scrutiny.[1] He likewise warns readers to recognize engineered tension — such as a pre-planned MAGA "Split" — as an artifact of the same programming rather than an organic dispute.[1]
