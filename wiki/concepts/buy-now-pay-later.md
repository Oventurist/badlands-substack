# Buy Now, Pay Later

**Buy now, pay later** (BNPL) is a form of short-term consumer financing offered at checkout by online retailers, allowing shoppers to receive goods immediately and pay for them in installments.[1] Its rapid spread into everyday spending categories, particularly groceries, became a marker of household financial stress in the United States in 2023.[1]

## Growth in grocery

According to Adobe Analytics data released in March 2023, the share of online BNPL transactions involving groceries grew 40% during the first two months of 2023 — the fastest pace among the retail categories Adobe studied.[1] Over the same period, the home furnishings sector's share of BNPL orders rose 38% and the apparel category's share advanced 8%, while the electronics sector's share of the BNPL market declined at a 14% clip.[1] Trade coverage attributed the surge to online grocery shoppers turning to retailer BNPL programs "at a blistering pace" as they coped with turbulent economic conditions.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]] argued that Americans — especially millennials — had burned through their savings and maxed out their credit cards, and now had to delay payment simply to buy food.[1] He described the trend as "a historic level of systemic vulnerability," warned that civil unrest becomes more likely when people cannot buy food, and urged readers to secure their assets and exit the traditional financial system.[1]
