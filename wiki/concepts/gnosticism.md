# Gnosticism

Gnosticism is one of the religio-philosophical currents with which [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] is commonly held to have emerged in parallel during Late Antiquity, the 3rd to 7th centuries CE.[1] The association is noted in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of the Hermetic tradition.[1]

On the popular account DeLarme summarises, Hermeticism arose alongside early Christianity, Gnosticism and [[neoplatonism|Neoplatonism]], with believed overlap also with the Chaldaean Oracles and late Orphic and Pythagorean literature.[1] The [[corpus-hermeticum|Corpus Hermeticum]], dated by modern scholars to the second century CE or earlier, belongs to this same milieu, and its concerns — the oneness and goodness of God, purification of the soul, and the relationship between mind and spirit — overlap with Gnostic preoccupations.[1]
