# Russia–Venezuela Strategic Partnership Law

On Monday, October 27, 2025, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] signed legislation formalizing a strategic partnership with Venezuela, strengthening cooperation across energy, mining, finance, and defense.[1]

## Origins

The law enshrines an agreement first signed on May 7 during a Moscow meeting between Putin and Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1] It underscores the growing alignment of Moscow and Caracas as both face US sanctions, and signals Russia's broader push to promote a multipolar global order.[1]

## Provisions

Under the pact, the two nations will expand collaboration in the economy, energy, mining, disarmament, and non-proliferation.[1] The agreement also commits them to countering neo-colonial narratives, condemning racism and genocide, and opposing the "distortion of colonial history."[1] Key provisions reject unilateral coercive measures, including extraterritorial sanctions, as violations of international law and the UN Charter.[1] Both sides pledged to develop a joint financial infrastructure insulating bilateral trade from Western influence, and to advance cooperation on arms control and equal security for all nations.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands hosts welcomed the move, framing Putin as stepping up to defend Maduro and warning that any effort to remove him would now have to contend with Russia.[1]
