# Flooding of Hamas Tunnels in Gaza

The flooding of Hamas tunnels in Gaza refers to an Israeli military tactic, reported publicly in December 2023, of pumping seawater from the Mediterranean into the underground tunnel network used by [[hamas|Hamas]] beneath [[gaza|Gaza]].[1]

## Reporting

The Wall Street Journal reported the operation, citing United States officials briefed on the matter, and said the flooding had begun sometime in the previous month.[1] Two U.S. officials confirmed the account to ABC News but cautioned that only some of the tunnels were being flooded, with the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] seeking to compare the tactic's effectiveness against other approaches previously used against the network, including airstrikes, explosives, drones and robots.[1]

IDF chief of staff [[herzi-halevi|Herzi Halevi]] said the previous week that flooding the tunnels was "a good idea, but I won't comment on its specifics," according to The Times of Israel.[1] A spokesperson for the Israeli defense minister declined to comment to the Journal, saying tunnel operations were classified.[1]

The item was carried in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of December 13, 2023, as a bonus item drawn from The Daily Beast's account of the reporting.[1]
