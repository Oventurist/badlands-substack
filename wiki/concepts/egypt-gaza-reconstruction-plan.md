# Egypt's Gaza Reconstruction Plan

Egypt's Gaza reconstruction plan is a multi-year proposal for rebuilding the Gaza Strip that, according to a Palestinian source cited by Sky News Arabia in March 2025, received initial approval from the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The approval was reported to be contingent on two conditions: the removal of [[hamas|Hamas]] from power in Gaza, and the disarmament of the strip.[1]

## Scope of the plan

The plan is projected to span five years and exceed $50 billion in cost.[1] Its initial phase focuses on clearing debris and constructing temporary housing for the Gazan population, with rubble removal proceeding simultaneously.[1] More advanced phases contemplate the construction of an artificial island, public institutions, a seaport, an airport, and additional infrastructure projects.[1]

## Governance and diplomacy

According to the reported source, the Trump administration inquired with [[egypt|Egypt]] about its ability to facilitate Hamas's ouster from Gaza, and Washington was pressuring Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to accept a proposal under which a Palestinian committee — backed by Arab nations and the international community — would govern the territory.[1] Egypt and the United States were expected to hold discussions later that month on operational mechanisms and timelines for the reconstruction initiative.[1]
