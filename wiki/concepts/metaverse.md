# Metaverse

The metaverse is a term for a virtual world, or a collection of virtual worlds, that exist in a common digital space and that users can access over the internet.[1] As defined in market research outlooks, metaverses include applications of virtual reality, augmented reality and other immersive technologies.[1]

## Market projections

[[statista|Statista]]'s Advertising & Media Markets Insights estimated that worldwide metaverse revenue would reach $490 billion in 2030.[1] Statista analyst Katharina Buchholz characterised that figure as a comparatively conservative forecast, noting that other analyst firms assumed a market volume of between roughly $750 billion and $1.7 trillion.[1] The projections were relayed by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] under the framing of "how the metaverse is making money."[1]

## Critical commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described the use of gaming as "a more recent innovation for the powers that should not be," and "a brilliant weapon to use on the unwitting masses."[1] His argument was that games increasingly supply people with experiences they should be obtaining from real life but cannot, because of what he described as an intentionally toxic contemporary culture.[1] The result, in this account, is that millions of men who would otherwise pursue mastery of their lives, social circles and civic contribution are instead confused, frustrated and improving at "unreal fantasies."[1]

Deschamps allowed that games are enjoyable and arguably confer some benefit outside the game, but held that the majority of long-term effects are deleterious, and that the damage is difficult to measure because the cultural baseline of expected male development has itself shifted and because an undeveloped individual's forgone potential is never visible.[1] He framed the metaverse projections and the mass adoption of online gaming as a major concern, warning that once the bulk of society adopts virtual reality solutions for daily life a positive feedback loop would push humanity toward "degrees of self imposed slavery that would make regaining freedom almost impossible."[1] He concluded on an optimistic note that people were not buying it, and that a growing counter-culture focused on restoring traditional ways of living continued to gain momentum.[1]
