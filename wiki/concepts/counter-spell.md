# Counter-Spell

**Counter-spell** is the term used in the Badlands Media essay "The Extraordinary Power of Digital Soldiers" (July 31, 2023) for the effect that truthful public speech is said to have upon occult concealment.[1] The concept follows from the essay's premise that ritual spellcasting works by *spelling words* — that local symbolic acts can influence nonlocal events — so that speech aimed at truth necessarily works in the opposite direction.[1]

## Mechanism

On the essay's account, when [[anons]] focus collectively upon the truth they generate a "field effect" that makes it significantly harder for dark occultists to shield themselves from detection by the general public.[1] The evidence offered is lexical: it took only a short time for "Illuminati" to enter mainstream usage, and the "deep state," an obscure notion to most people less than a decade earlier, has become part of the popular lexicon with nothing the occultists can do to reverse it.[1] That, the author writes, "is what we call a **counter-spell**."[1]

## Consequences claimed

The essay argues that this accumulating effect is building toward a moment at which a rapid, worldwide removal of the dark occultists from power becomes feasible, and that were it not for anons speaking the truth and rendering verdicts online no such event might be possible at all.[1] Its closing exhortation restates the principle in practical terms: with every lie exposed and every spirit uplifted, another successful counter-spell is cast.[1] See also [[authority-as-authorship|authority as authorship]] and [[law-of-attraction|the law of attraction]].[1]
