# Panic of 1893

The Panic of 1893 was a stock market crash and ensuing economic depression in the United States, cited by Badlands writers as a historical precedent for lame-duck presidential sabotage.[1]

In the account given by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], outgoing president [[benjamin-harrison|Benjamin Harrison]] deliberately torpedoed the economy to harm his successor, using friendly media to publish doomsday editorials that scared off foreign investment and directing the U.S. Treasury to spend the surplus.[1] The market crashed with only eight days left in Harrison's term, and [[grover-cleveland|Grover Cleveland]] was inaugurated into the wreckage.[1] The resulting depression continued through 1897, and Cleveland absorbed the blame.[1]

The episode was invoked in November 2024 as an analogue to what the authors characterized as [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s lame-duck escalation against [[russia|Russia]] — "harming Americans because you lost an election, while holding the most powerful office in the world," which the commentary labelled treason.[1]
