# Saudi Purge

The "Saudi Purge" refers to the November 2017 anti-corruption sweep in Saudi Arabia, in which [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] detained scores of princes, ministers and businessmen. Within Badlands commentary it is treated as a foundational event — described by contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] as "the biggest Q proof of all time" and as heralding the Golden Age to come.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

In this reading, the purge was never mere theater for Western applause or "performative anti-corruption cosplay," but rather a proof-of-concept and "delayed-fuse prototype" for what a sovereign leader could achieve once the Deep State's puppets were cut loose and national wealth was redirected away from propping up Treasury bonds for a decaying unipolar empire and toward independence and abundance creation.[1]

Q drops are said to have aligned with the purge in real time, signaling that the Kingdom's "great drainage" would mirror — and in fact precede — America's own, described as a temporal pincer movement in which the Saudis moved first while [[donald-trump|Trump]], constrained by the theatrical necessities of the 2020 election and subsequent chaos, prepared the American battlefield.[1] In this framing the delays were features rather than bugs: the purge bought cycles and "reclaimed bricks for the golden path" while the American collective mind endured its own engineered fracture.[1]

## Closing of the circuit

Badlands commentary holds that the circuit closed during Trump's second term, days after the November 2025 Washington summit, when MBS arrived not as supplicant or junior partner but as benefactor and co-architect of abundance.[1] The Kingdom's previously promised $600 billion in US investments was upgraded, with capital directed toward advanced manufacturing in the American heartland, gas turbines in Greenville, South Carolina, critical-minerals joint ventures intended to break China's rare-earth stranglehold, and civilian nuclear partnerships restarting American reactors and building small modular systems.[1]

Trump's dismissal of the [[jamal-khashoggi|Khashoggi]] question — "A lot of people didn't like that gentleman" — is presented as sealing an inversion in which the journalist is recast as a Brotherhood-adjacent operative while the Crown Prince emerges elevated, his [[vision-2030|Vision 2030]] platform reframed from desert fantasy to working blueprint.[1] Saudi Arabia is thereby described as "the single greatest accelerator of the Golden Age transition we have yet identified."[1]
