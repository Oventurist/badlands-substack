# Israeli Art Students Affair

The "Israeli art students" affair refers to reports, circulating around the time of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], concerning groups of young Israelis who presented themselves as art students while operating in and around US government facilities and residential complexes in the United States.[1]

Journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] invoked the affair in September 2025 while discussing his forthcoming documentary series on 9/11, stating that public knowledge about the episode has been very limited and that reporting on it ultimately vanished.[1] He raised the matter alongside his broader claim that Israeli intelligence had foreknowledge of the attacks, arguing that questions of this kind are legitimate and distinct from the blanket assertions he said are used to discredit real inquiry.[1]
