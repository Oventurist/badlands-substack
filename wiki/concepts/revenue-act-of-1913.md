# Revenue Act of 1913

The Revenue Act of 1913 was the law that dramatically lowered tariffs and implemented a federal income tax, following the ratification of the [[sixteenth-amendment|Sixteenth Amendment]] earlier that year.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

Democratic leaders agreed to seek its passage after ratification.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) Representative [[oscar-underwood|Oscar Underwood]] quickly moved the bill through the House of Representatives, and it won approval in the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]] after extensive lobbying by the Wilson administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) President [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] signed the Act into law on October 3, allowing for the establishment of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

Together with the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act]] signed that December, the article treats the Revenue Act as the second half of the mechanism by which every American was bound to the [[fiat-currency|fiat]] monetary system and the income tax.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
