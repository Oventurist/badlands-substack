# Revenue Act of 1913

The **Revenue Act of 1913** dramatically lowered tariffs and implemented a federal income tax, following ratification of the [[sixteenth-amendment|Sixteenth Amendment]].[1]

Representative [[oscar-underwood|Oscar Underwood]] quickly moved the bill through the [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]], and it won approval in the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]] after extensive lobbying by the Wilson administration. President [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] signed the Act into law on October 3, 1913, allowing for the establishment of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]].[1]

Badlands Media treats the Act, alongside the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act]], as one half of the 1913 settlement that bound Americans to permanent taxation and a fiat currency.[1]
