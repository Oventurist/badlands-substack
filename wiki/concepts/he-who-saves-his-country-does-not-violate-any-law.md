# "He Who Saves His Country Does Not Violate Any Law"

"He who saves his Country does not violate any Law" is a quotation posted by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to Truth Social and X on Saturday, February 15, 2025, widely read as a claim that the president is incapable of breaking the law.[1] The official [[white-house|White House]] account on X also shared the message.[1]

## Context

The post came after a blitz of executive actions and threats to federal agencies under [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] were challenged in courts across the country, raising alarms among critics that the administration was preparing to disregard court orders and ignite a constitutional crisis.[1] Trump's efforts to gut federal funding, fire thousands of aid workers and unilaterally redefine the Fourteenth Amendment had been blocked in federal courts in the preceding days.[1] The line is often attributed to Napoleon Bonaparte — reaching Trump by way of Rod Steiger's screen portrayal — and Napoleon used it to justify his despotic regime as the will of the people of France.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] responded that a president exercising wartime powers likewise does not violate any law, pointing to the decision in Campbell v. Clinton (2000, Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit): "Importantly, the Court made clear that it would not dispute the President on measures necessary to repel foreign aggression. The President alone must determine what degree of force the crisis demands."[1]
