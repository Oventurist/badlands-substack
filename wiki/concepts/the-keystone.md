# The Keystone

**The Keystone** is a term introduced in Q drop #128 of November 9, 2017, in which Q asks readers directly, "What is a keystone?"[1] In Chapter 21 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series, [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] explains the term by way of its architectural sense: the keystone is the wedge-shaped stone at the crown of an arch that holds the whole structure together, and without which the arch — and any bridge it supports — would collapse.[1]

Fleuret notes that the question is posed in the same section of the drop in which Q is teaching readers to acquire what the series calls a "40,000-foot view," the elevated vantage point from which the accumulated crumbs form a map of corruption running through government.[1] In that framing, identifying the keystone is presented as the step that converts a map into a guide.[1]

## Interpretations

Many competing theories about the keystone's identity have circulated within the [[truth-community|truth community]], and Fleuret records that as of December 2024 no solid consensus had emerged.[1] His own guess is that the keystone is more than likely a person — a single figure whose exposure or cooperation would be sufficient to bring the entire structure down.[1]

The keystone question sits alongside Q's repeated assurance in the same drop that "future proves past," the idea that later events will retroactively decode earlier drops and that the full picture will only emerge over time.[1]
