# Everything App

The "everything app" — also described as a "super app" — is a software concept in which a single application consolidates messaging, social media, commerce, payments, banking and other daily services so that a user never needs to leave the platform.[1] The model is popular in China, where applications such as WeChat are used for text and video calls and also provide facilities to pay bills, book cabs, reserve restaurants and conduct other financial activities in-app.[1]

## Musk's adoption of the model

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] has repeatedly described the everything app as the strategic motive behind the [[twitter-rebrand-to-x|rebranding of Twitter to X]], which he framed as a step toward creating a platform with multiple integrated functionalities.[1] In April 2023 the platform introduced a feature allowing users to access crypto markets and other financial market services without leaving the app.[1] Musk stated that if executed correctly, X could become a leading venue for finance, banking, payments and data, and over time might come to constitute "half of the global financial system."[1]

## Criticism

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that an everything app is a single point of failure from a technology standpoint, and questioned how the consolidation of behavioral, psychological and financial data in one platform would interact with an anticipated [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]].[1] She summarised the structural objection as "centralization requires complexity, and complexity breeds and hides corruption," and said she had no interest in such an application.[1]
