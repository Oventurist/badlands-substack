# Cultural Marxism

Cultural Marxism is the term used in [[patriot-chronicles|The Patriot Chronicles]] for what its author describes as a coordinated program to erase Western historical memory and cultural identity as a means of political control.[1]

## Characterization in The Patriot Chronicles

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]] wrote that PhDs in modern mainstream academia are among the most pernicious liars in society — whether opportunistic grifters or delusional fanatics — and that they have proven their loyalty is pledged to their financiers rather than to the pursuit of fact-based knowledge.[1] Those backers, he argued, seek the eradication of Western Civilization through Cultural Marxism: the enslavement of mankind through the destruction of knowledge and memory.[1]

He extended the critique to citation practice, noting that academic journals increasingly cite only secondary rather than primary sources, so that successive generations of scholars can build layered misunderstandings of past events even under the best of intentions — a reason, he said, to keep history records decentralized.[1]

## The Culture War

The essay described a highly coordinated, sophisticated and well-funded effort to strip readers of their heritage and of the memory of common struggles, so that they might be more easily controlled, divided and manipulated into hating one another.[1] Asking how the Cultural Marxists could be defeated and the American Spirit revived, the author answered with the proverb about eating an elephant one bite at a time.[1] He tied the fight to [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s writing on the [[war-of-stories|War of Stories]] and to General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]]'s treatment of [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth Generation Warfare]], arguing that the same obfuscation occurring within academia is also occurring within culture, and that the loss of shared history would dissolve the bonds of fellowship needed to unite and fight back.[1] He contrasted the positive perception of American culture he hoped to promote with "the trashy degeneracy being pumped out by Hollyweird and mainstream Marxist academia."[1]
