# Demolition of the Hamas Parliament Building

The **demolition of the Hamas parliament building** was an Israeli military operation in Gaza City in mid-November 2023 in which the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] destroyed the structure serving as the legislative seat of [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

## Events

At the start of the week, IDF troops photographed themselves inside the building and the room that serves as the "Hamas parliament."[1] On Wednesday, November 15, 2023, Israeli media confirmed that the entire building had been demolished, in what appeared to be a symbolic act by which Israel signalled to the world that the terror group was "finished" in the Gaza Strip.[1]

"After troops photographed themselves inside Hamas's parliament building in Gaza City several days ago, the military has demolished the site," the [[times-of-israel|Times of Israel]] reported, adding that the building had been captured by the 7th Armored Brigade and the Golani Infantry Brigade.[1]

## Continuing fighting

Despite the symbolism of the demolition, fierce fighting continued in the Strip, and the IDF reported at least 51 Israeli troops killed since the start of the ground invasion.[1] The item was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] alongside coverage of flooding fears from heavy rains adding to Gazan misery as infectious diseases surged.[1]
