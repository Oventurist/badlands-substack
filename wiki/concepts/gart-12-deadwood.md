# GART 12 Deadwood

**GART 12** was the twelfth stop on the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live event series staged by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], scheduled to begin on June 25 in [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood, South Dakota]].[1] It was announced on April 26, 2026 in a Badlands post written by network host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], roughly two months before the event.[1]

## Framing and significance

Badlands billed GART 12 as the sixth GART "of the new American Golden Age" and as the network's third time touching down in Deadwood, a town the announcement joked "might as well be official Badlands HQ at this point."[1] The organisers described the tour as presenting "a great opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots," creating "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[1]

## Ticketing

At the time of the announcement VIP tickets were nearly sold out, with only a few remaining, while Full Weekend General Admission tickets were still available.[1] The General Admission package granted access to a Friday-night welcome party and meet-and-greet with Badlands Media personalities, along with three days of panels.[1] In-person tickets were sold through the network's TicketSpice page, which set out the pricing breakdown and the contents of each package.[1]

## Virtual ticket package

Attendees who had never been to a GART event in person could purchase a virtual ticket to stream the entirety of the event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.[1] Virtual purchases included long-term DVR access to panel replays after the weekend, so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss out.[1] Badlands ran an Early Bird Virtual Special through May 15 that priced the virtual package at $25 rather than the standard $35.[1]

The virtual ticket also carried access to an exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat, with links distributed by email to virtual ticket holders during the month of the event.[1] Badlands described the chat as a venue where hundreds of Badlanders and Badlands hosts could interact, view behind-the-scenes photographs and video, and "engage in virtual shenanigans galore."[1] According to the announcement, these Telegram groups typically became long-lived chat rooms for the network's most committed audience members, continuing to thrive for months after each event and serving as a place to enjoy the GART action, pass the time between events in "entertaining and memetic fashion," and document the journey to the next GART.[1]

## Reception and framing

The announcement closed on behalf of the whole Badlands Media team with the hope of seeing familiar faces and, "as has happened at every GART so far, plenty of new ones," ending with Burning Bright's customary sign-off, "stay Bright!"[1] The post recorded 21 likes.[1]

## Final call (June 25, 2026)

A closing "Last Call for GART: Deadwood!" post was published on June 25, 2026, again written by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], telling readers that "this week" many of them would join their favourite Badlands hosts in Deadwood for the twelfth stop of the tour.[2] The post reiterated that the gathering was the sixth GART of the new American Golden Age and the network's third time in the town, where Badlands had "developed a habit of taking the town over."[2] It carried the full Deadwood event schedule as an image linked to the [[badlandsmedia-tv|BadlandsMedia.tv]] event page, and recorded 42 likes.[2]

At the time of the final call some in-person tickets were still available, and virtual tickets remained on sale via TicketSpice for those who had never attended a GART physically, covering the whole event including the Friday, Saturday and Sunday panels with long-term DVR replay access.[2] The virtual ticket again included the exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat, with links emailed to purchasers, which the post again characterised as a durable community space thriving for months after each event.[2] The notice closed on behalf of the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] team with the hope of seeing familiar faces and "plenty of new ones," signing off "Stay Bright!"[2]
