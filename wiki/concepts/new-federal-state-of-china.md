# New Federal State of China

The New Federal State of China is a government-in-exile project founded by [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] together with the exiled Chinese businessman [[miles-guo|Miles Guo]], devoted entirely to the overthrow of the Chinese government.[1] Matthew Ehret describes it as arriving with "a shiny new flag, constitution and cheesy anthem."[1]

When the project was unveiled, Bannon and Guo stood shoulder to shoulder on Guo's $28 million yacht in New York harbor with the Statue of Liberty in the background and planes overhead carrying flags announcing the new state.[1] Ehret suggests the design may have been inspired by Count [[richard-coudenhove-kalergi|Coudenhove-Kalergi]]'s century-earlier blueprint for the European Union, itself complete with a prescribed flag and anthem.[1]

The venture fits Bannon's declared civilizational posture, expressed in his statement to The Economist that he wanted the world to look back in 100 years and say "their mercantilist, Confucian system lost. The Judeo-Christian liberal West won."[1]
