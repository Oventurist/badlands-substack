# Starobelsk Dormitory Attack

The Starobelsk dormitory attack was a Ukrainian drone strike on a college dormitory in Starobelsk, in Russia's Lugansk People's Republic, carried out late at night on May 22, 2026, while students were asleep.[1] The attack reportedly killed 21 people, mostly teenage girls, and injured 70 others.[1]

## Russian response

[[russia|Russia]] answered with a large-scale overnight missile and drone campaign using precision weapons, including hypersonic missiles, against Ukrainian military airfields, defense industry facilities, fuel and transport infrastructure, and a drone production plant linked to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], striking targets across Kyiv, Zaporozhye, Kherson, Dnipropetrovsk, Poltava, Khmelnytskyi, and Sumy.[1]

Russian Defense Ministry officials said all designated targets were hit and described the operation as a response to "terrorist attacks" by Kiev.[1] President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] called the episode a new chapter in Ukraine's "crime spree" that would bring "well-deserved and inevitable punishment."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted reporting that a drone film-scouting company linked to Zelensky's associates had become Ukraine's "miracle" factory producing its long-range drones, questioning how many other Ukrainian film production companies had been repurposed for the war effort.[1] He grouped the dormitory strike with the drone attack on the [[zaporozhye-nuclear-power-plant|Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant]] and a drone incident in Romania as evidence of escalating and increasingly reckless conduct by Ukrainian leadership.[1]
