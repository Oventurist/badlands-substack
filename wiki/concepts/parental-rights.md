# Parental Rights

Parental rights, in the context of the gender debate, refers to the authority of parents to direct the upbringing, education, and medical care of their children — an authority critics say has been progressively displaced by state and institutional actors advancing [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]].[1]

## The Canadian case

A February 2024 Badlands Media essay by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argues that in [[canada|Canada]] lawfare and gradual cultural conditioning play a central role in what he calls the war on parental rights and on children's minds and bodies.[1] Parents who object to their children being told they may be "trapped in the wrong body," or who oppose [[drag-queen-story-hour|drag queen story hour]] programming, find their position increasingly criminalized as hate.[1] The essay insists such objections are not bigotry, and that the "anti-2SLGBTQ+" label is a rhetorical device rather than a description.[1]

## Medical displacement

In medicine, the [[gender-affirming-care|gender affirmative]] model is described as displacing parental consent entirely in some settings. The article points to [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]] in the United States, which it says allows young girls who self-diagnose [[gender-dysphoria|gender dysphoria]] same-day access to testosterone without a prescription or parental permission.[1] By contrast, the [[nhs|NHS]] in England drafted guidelines in October 2022 requiring specialist tertiary paediatric units, mental-health partnerships, and robust safeguarding — and calling for local authorities to be alerted in some cases where young people obtained puberty blockers or hormones privately.[1]

## Mobilization

The nationwide [[1-million-march-for-children|1 Million March for Children]] is cited as evidence that Canadian parents have awakened to the issue, and as an event that mainstream media met with aggressive propaganda.[1] Both Canada and the United States are described as adhering to [[wpath|WPATH]]'s de-psychopathologization agenda in ways that endanger children and take away parental rights.[1]
