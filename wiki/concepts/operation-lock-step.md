# Operation Lock Step

"Lock Step" is the name of a scenario contained in a [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] report on future technology and international development, dated to May 2010 and commonly referenced as a 2011 publication.[1]

The scenario is cited as one of the key pandemic exercises — alongside [[operation-dark-winter|Operation Dark Winter]] of 2000 and [[event-201|Event 201]] — that made planning for authoritarian pandemic response a normalized feature of western geopolitical life well before 2020.[1] In [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret's]] treatment, the accumulation of dozens of such war games between 2000 and 2019 makes the hypothesis of conscious deployment, rather than accidental laboratory leak, a serious possibility, and points attention toward the transatlantic institutions that authored them rather than toward China.[1]

Lock Step therefore appears in the essay's fifth "fact": that the international response template for COVID-19 originated in western scenario planning, not in Beijing.[1]
