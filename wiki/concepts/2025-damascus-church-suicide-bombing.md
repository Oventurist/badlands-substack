# 2025 Damascus Church Suicide Bombing

The 2025 Damascus church suicide bombing was an attack on the Greek Orthodox Church of the Prophet Elias in the Dweila neighbourhood of Damascus, [[syria|Syria]], in which at least 25 people were killed and 63 wounded, according to Syria's health ministry.[1]

## The attack

According to the Syrian interior ministry, a man opened fire with a weapon inside the church during an evening service on a Sunday before detonating an explosive vest.[1] The ministry said the attacker was affiliated with the jihadist group [[islamic-state|Islamic State]], though there was no immediate claim of responsibility from the group itself.[1] Photographs and video from inside the church showed a heavily damaged altar, pews covered in broken glass, and blood spattered across the walls.[1]
