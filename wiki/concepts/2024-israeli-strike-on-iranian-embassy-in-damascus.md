# 2024 Israeli Strike on the Iranian Embassy in Damascus

On Monday, April 1, 2024, suspected Israeli warplanes bombed [[iran|Iran]]'s embassy compound in [[syria|Syria]], in a strike Iran said killed seven of its military advisers, including three senior commanders, marking a major escalation in [[israel|Israel]]'s war with its regional adversaries.[1]

## The strike

Reuters reporters at the site in the Mezzeh district of Damascus saw emergency workers clambering atop the rubble of a destroyed building inside the diplomatic compound, adjacent to the main Iranian embassy building, with emergency vehicles parked outside and an Iranian flag hanging from a pole by the debris.[1] Iran's ambassador to Syria said the strike hit a consular building within the embassy compound and that his residence occupied the top two floors.[1]

## Casualties

The [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] said in a statement that seven Iranian military advisers died in the strike, including [[mohammad-reza-zahedi|Mohammad Reza Zahedi]], a senior commander in its [[quds-force|Quds Force]], the elite foreign espionage and paramilitary arm.[1]

## Reaction and significance

Syrian Foreign Minister [[faisal-mekdad|Faisal Mekdad]], seen at the site along with Syria's interior minister, said: "We strongly condemn this atrocious terrorist attack that targeted the Iranian consulate building in Damascus and killed a number of innocents."[1] Israel had long targeted Iran's military installations in Syria and those of its proxies, but the April 1 attack was the first time it hit the vast embassy compound itself.[1] The strike was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of April 2, 2024.[1]

## Aftermath and Security Council fallout

The April 1, 2024 strike killed seven Iranian military officers, including two senior commanders, at Tehran's consulate in Damascus; later reporting identified them as members of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] [[quds-force|Quds Force]], among them two high-ranking generals.[2] The attack directly precipitated the [[april-2024-iranian-attack-on-israel|Iranian drone and missile barrage against Israel]] of April 13, which [[iran|Iran]] framed as a response to the "Zionist regime's numerous crimes, including the attack on the consular section of Iran's Embassy in Damascus."[2]

At the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]], Russian ambassador [[vassily-nebenzia|Vassily Nebenzia]] argued that Western members had blocked any rebuke of [[israel|Israel]] over the strike despite its violation of international law on the inviolability of diplomatic installations, and that this "disgraceful inaction" had produced the subsequent escalation.[2]

## United States assessments and escalation risk

Current and former United States officials told The New York Times on Tuesday, April 2, 2024 that the alleged Israeli strike killing Iran's top [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] commander in Syria was a strategic blow to Tehran that risked escalating regional tensions.[3] Although Iran and its allies blamed Israel, Israel did not officially comment on the attack on the building in Damascus, which killed Gen. [[mohammad-reza-zahedi|Mohammad Reza Zahedi]], the IRGC's most senior official in Syria, along with his deputy Gen. Mohammad Hadi Hajriahimi, five other IRGC officers and at least one member of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[3]

In the wake of the strike, United States troops based in southeastern Syria intercepted an attack drone — the first such incident in two months, a Defense Department official told the Times on condition of anonymity.[3] Iran-backed militias had refrained from attacking American bases in an apparent attempt at de-escalation after three United States troops were killed in a drone strike in Jordan in January.[3]

Badlands Media contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted the widespread reading in the truth community that a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] warning to Israel about an imminent Iranian attack presaged a CIA operation against Israel to be blamed on Iran, but chose instead to focus on military analysis.[3] Citing retired four-star general [[frank-mckenzie|Kenneth F. McKenzie Jr.]], he noted the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] had attributed the attack to Israel while Iran blamed both Israel and the United States, and that Iran's cumulative loss of leadership since the 2020 assassination of [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] likely left it unable to mount a rapid, precise response.[3] He added that clandestine forces appeared to be moving public narrative toward World War III, a situation he said he had predicted the previous June.[3]
