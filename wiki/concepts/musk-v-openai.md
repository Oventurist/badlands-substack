# Musk v. OpenAI

Musk v. OpenAI was a civil lawsuit filed in 2024 by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] against [[openai|OpenAI]] and its chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]], alleging that they abandoned the company's founding nonprofit mission.[1] Musk co-founded OpenAI in 2015 and left its board in 2018.[1]

## Claims and relief sought

The suit alleged breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment arising from OpenAI's shift away from its nonprofit origins and its later corporate restructuring.[1] [[microsoft|Microsoft]], an early investor beginning in 2019, was also named, with Musk alleging that it aided and abetted a breach of charitable trust.[1] Musk sought as much as $134 billion in ill-gotten gains, the removal of Altman and OpenAI president [[greg-brockman|Greg Brockman]], and reversal of OpenAI's 2025 restructuring, arguing that any recovered funds should go to "the OpenAI charity."[1]

## Verdict

In May 2026 a federal jury rejected Musk's claims after less than two hours of deliberation, siding with the defense.[1] U.S. District Judge [[yvonne-gonzalez-rogers|Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers]] adopted the advisory jury's finding that Altman and OpenAI were not liable, and separately dismissed the breach-of-charitable-trust and unjust-enrichment claims as untimely; the claims against Microsoft were also dismissed.[1]
