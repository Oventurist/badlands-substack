# Seditious Conspiracy

Seditious conspiracy is a federal crime codified at 18 U.S.C. § 2384, covering, in the language quoted in the Badlands Media feature "Transparency and Prosecution," "whoever knowingly or willfully advocates, abets, advises, or teaches the duty, necessity, desirability, or propriety of overthrowing or destroying the government of the United States."[1]

## The "knowingly" element

The article places heavy emphasis on the statutory term "knowingly," a legal term of art meaning that the accused intentionally and deliberately promoted or supported the overthrow or destruction of the government, rather than acting out of ignorance or mistake.[1] Author [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] notes that Q posts emphasized the word "knowingly" and tied it directly to treason, arguing that this was a signal about the specific charge that would eventually be brought.[1]

## Application in the article's argument

The statute is invoked as the legal frame for the accusations levelled against Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and Representative [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]], both of whom the article says travelled to Syria under the guidance of [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]] and thereby aided and funded [[isis|ISIS]].[1] The article asserts that McCain "knowingly" committed both treason and sedition by arming and financing enemy forces, and separately by working to frame President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for Russian collusion through the Steele dossier.[1]

The concept underpins the article's central promise of a coming "justice phase," in which exposure of evidence precedes prosecution — summarized in the Q formulation "Transparency and Prosecution. There is simply no other way."[1] The author further connects the topic to the reinstatement of the federal death penalty under Trump and to Q's references to gallows and firing squads.[1]
