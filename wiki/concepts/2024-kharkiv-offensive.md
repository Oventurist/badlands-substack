# 2024 Kharkiv Offensive

The 2024 Kharkiv offensive was a Russian military advance into northern Ukraine launched on Monday, May 13, 2024, with attacks on towns and villages in the Kharkiv region.[1]

## Missing fortifications

The offensive struck precisely the sector where Ukrainian defensive fortifications had been budgeted but, according to anti-corruption reporting, never built. [[martyna-bohuslavets|Martyna Bohuslavets]], head of the Mezha Anti-Corruption Center, reported in Ukrainian Pravda that millions of dollars intended for fortification construction had been "transferred to Kharkiv OVA to front companies of avatars," with the Kharkiv Regional Military Administration paying fictitious firms out of a total of 7 billion hryvnias spent in the region.[1]

Ukrainian commander [[denys-yaroslavskyi|Denys Yaroslavskyi]], head of the Ukrainian Special Reconnaissance Unit in the area, told the BBC that "there was no first line of defence" and that "the Russians just walked in… without any mined fields," concluding: "It wasn't a failure. It was a betrayal."[1]

## Oversight context

The episode coincided with continued US funding of the war and with a March 2024 [[government-accountability-office|Government Accountability Office]] finding that the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] lacked quality data to track delivery of defense articles to Ukraine.[1] Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued the combination pointed to something other than routine incompetence in the aid pipeline, raising [[continuity-of-government|continuity of government]] speculation and proposing the topic for discussion with [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on the Power Hour.[1]
