# Protecting Our Democracy Act

The **Protecting Our Democracy Act** was a bill introduced in the 117th Congress as House Resolution 5314, described in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as legislation that "sought to federalize elections" and was pushed hard by progressive Democrats.[1]

## Treatment in Badlands commentary

In his January 1, 2024 essay, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the bill's title as a textbook instance of what he called "subtle, linguistic hypnotism" — the same rhetorical move he identified in the phrase "[[danger-to-our-democracy|protect our democracy]]" itself.[1] In his framing, naming a bill after the protection of democracy forecloses argument about its contents, because opposition can be recast as opposition to democracy as such.[1]

He grouped the Act with corporate and nonprofit initiatives deploying the same vocabulary, including [[microsoft|Microsoft]]'s [[democracy-forward|Democracy Forward]] project and the funder-backed group [[defending-digital-campaigns|Defending Digital Campaigns]], arguing that in each case the word "our" designates not the general public but a governing class that reserves to itself the authority to decide which institutions are fundamental to a healthy democracy.[1]
