# FDA Blocked Vaccine Safety Studies

In May 2026 it was reported that the [[food-and-drug-administration|U.S. Food and Drug Administration]] had blocked the publication of several studies that supported the safety of widely used COVID-19 and shingles vaccines.[1]

## The studies

The research concerned [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccines]] made by [[pfizer|Pfizer]] and [[moderna|Moderna]], as well as [[gsk|GSK]]'s Shingrix shingles vaccine.[1] The FDA withdrew the research before publication, and no specific details of the studies' findings were disclosed in the reporting, which was first published by The New York Times.[1]

## Official explanation

[[department-of-health-and-human-services|HHS]] spokesman [[andrew-nixon|Andrew Nixon]] stated that the studies were withdrawn because the authors had drawn broad conclusions not supported by the underlying data, and said the FDA acted "to protect the integrity of its scientific process and ensure that any work associated with the agency meets its high standards."[1] The action came amid broader policy shifts under Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], a longtime vaccine critic.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that mainstream coverage obscured the story, reading it instead as confirmation that the FDA had suppressed publications asserting vaccine safety precisely because the underlying data showed the opposite.[1] On that reading, the agency knowingly dispensed a vaccine that was not safe and "published fake science to support their grift," conduct that — given the coercive character of the COVID vaccine rollout — should be treated as a crime against humanity warranting imprisonment.[1]
