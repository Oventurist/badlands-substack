# Alchemy

Alchemy is the occulted science most closely associated with the Hermetic tradition; it often went by the name "the Hermetic art" or "the Hermetic philosophy," a usage that supports the open-ended sense of the term [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] as covering any system drawing on subject matter associated with Hermes.[1] Its Hermetic pedigree was outlined by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] in his February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey.[1]

## Hermetic foundations

[[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]] is counted among the founders of alchemy along with science, religion, mathematics, geometry, philosophy, medicine and magic.[1] Alchemy is also one of the three parts of the wisdom of the whole universe — with astrology and theurgy — that the [[emerald-tablet|Emerald Tablet]] says Hermes knows, an account used to explain his epithet "thrice great."[1]

The Emerald Tablet was highly regarded by Islamic and European alchemists as the foundation of their art, and its fourteen stanzas are described as the very foundation of European alchemy.[1] History's greatest alchemists believed the tablet harboured the secret of the *prima materia* and the [[philosophers-stone|Philosopher's Stone]].[1] [[isaac-newton|Sir Isaac Newton]] devoted the later portions of his life to studying the text.[1]
