# Zircon Hypersonic Missile

The **Zircon** is a Russian hypersonic cruise missile described in Badlands Media coverage as capable of reaching speeds of Mach 9 and of using its kinetic velocity, rather than a large warhead, to destroy its target.[1] This design yields a smaller but more concentrated blast radius, making the weapon well suited to precision strikes against high-value targets such as aircraft carriers while retaining sufficient speed to penetrate existing missile defense systems.[1]

## Deployment near Cuba, June 2024

The Zircon entered Badlands commentary in June 2024 when the Russian nuclear-powered cruise missile submarine *Kazan* — named for the city of [[kazan|Kazan]] — arrived off the Cuban coast alongside three other Russian vessels for a five-day official visit preceding a large simultaneous air and maritime exercise in the Caribbean.[1] The deployment also included the frigate *Admiral Gorshkov*, itself armed with Zircon missiles, which reporting characterized as an additional challenge for Western militaries.[1] United States officials said they were monitoring the vessels and did not anticipate imminent danger in the region, though US and [[nato|NATO]] officials had long expressed anxiety over the capabilities of the *Kazan* and similar Yasen-class submarines.[1]
