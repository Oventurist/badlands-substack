# The Blitz

The Blitz is a weekly video program launched by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on its Substack newsletter in January 2026. Its stated purpose, given in the announcement accompanying [[badlands-blitz-episode-1|its debut episode]] on January 10, 2026, is to serve as "a weekly recap and dissection" of the work published Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].

## Format

According to the source, the show rotates its hosting duties among three Badlands contributors — the author of the debut announcement, [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] — rather than assigning a fixed presenter. Episodes are planned to run an average of 60 to 70 minutes and to be released on Saturdays. The debut episode ran longer than that target because of introductory explanations of the show's intent.

A regular feature of the program is a review of the newsletter's comments section, in which the hosts read and respond to subscriber remarks from the preceding week.

## Editorial positioning

The Blitz was introduced as part of an effort described in the source to give the Badlands Substack audience exclusive content in 2026. The announcement emphasizes that the show, like all Badlands content, remains free to view, while noting that paid subscriptions are what allow the organization to compensate contributors. Its subject matter is drawn from what the network terms the "Info War" — the contested media and narrative environment covered daily in the Brief.

## Later episodes

By [[badlands-blitz-episode-10|Episode 10]], published March 14, 2026, the program had begun departing from its strict recap format: that installment set "micro topics" aside so that [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] could devote the whole show to divisions within the [[truth-community|Truth Community]]. The free-to-view, subscription-supported model described at launch remained unchanged.

[[badlands-blitz-episode-11|Episode 11]], "Iran, NATO and the Invisible Enemy," followed on March 21, 2026 with Burning Bright and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as hosts, returning the show to geopolitical analysis by way of the Iran War, the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], and the [[invisible-enemy|Invisible Enemy]]. That post again described The Blitz as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the Badlands Brief" and pointed to a "step-up" in the team's output for 2026.

[[badlands-blitz-episode-12|Episode 12]], "The Fake and Gay, but Also Real and Serious War," appeared on March 28, 2026 with Burning Bright and Ashe in America hosting. It opened on the irreconcilable claims then circulating about [[ali-khamenei|Ayatollah Ali Khamenei]] before turning to [[narrative-warfare|Narrative Warfare]], [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]] and [[first-principles|First Principles]] — a pattern, consistent across the later episodes, of using a topical controversy as an entry point to the program's standing analytical themes.
