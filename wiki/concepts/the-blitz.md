# The Blitz

**The Blitz** is a weekly video program launched by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on its Substack newsletter, debuting on 10 January 2026 with "Badlands Blitz Episode 1: Dueling Color Revolutions and Narrative Convergence."[1] The show is intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work the Badlands team produces Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands Brief]].[1]

## Format and hosting

Hosting duties rotate among three Badlands contributors, with the show alternating between [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], and the episode's unnamed co-host and author of the debut post.[1] Episodes are expected to run 60–70 minutes on average and drop on Saturdays, although the debut ran somewhat longer than that target because of the explanatory segments placed at the beginning outlining the intention behind the new show.[1]

A recurring segment sees the hosts dip into the newsletter's comments section to review what readers — addressed throughout as "Badlanders" and "beautiful savages" — have been saying over the course of the week.[1]

## Debut episode content

After the opening explainers, the debut episode turned to what the hosts characterised as the [[information-war|Info War]] chaos of the previous week, ranging from "Mad-lad Maduro" — a reference to Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro — to what the post terms the "Minnesotamali Madness."[1] The episode was framed under the theme of dueling [[color-revolution|color revolutions]] and narrative convergence.[1] The post recorded 393 likes.[1]

## Editorial and business model

The Blitz is presented as part of a 2026 push to serve the Badlands Substack audience with exclusive content, on the grounds that Substack subscribers are among the outlet's most loyal and engaged.[1] All Badlands content, including The Blitz, is to remain free; the accompanying appeal states that the only way the outlet can pay its people for their time is through voluntary reader support, and invites readers who value the daily output and its 2026 step-up to upgrade to a paid subscription, cancellable at any time.[1] Readers are asked to share the show widely and to comment.[1] The post closes with the Badlands sign-off exhorting readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"[1]

The episode 12 post repeats the standing appeal: all Badlands content, including The Blitz, remains free, and the team can only pay itself for the time through readers who choose to support it directly, with paid subscriptions cancellable at any time.[2] It closes with the customary sign-off, "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"[2]
