# Automated Clearing House (ACH)

The **Automated Clearing House (ACH)** network is the electronic funds-transfer system that handles most routine payments between U.S. bank accounts. In the Badlands Media article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?", [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] uses ACH as the foil against which the advantages of blockchain-based digital money are measured.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

Lange draws a sharp distinction between *electronic* currency moving over ACH and *digital* currency built on [[blockchain|blockchain]] technology.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) ACH payments, he writes, do not settle in minutes but can take several days before a transfer becomes official, and that delay is controlled by a third party he identifies with the [[cabal|cabal]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) The intermediary window allows transactions to be cancelled before they are final, and the same actors already hold the power to freeze accounts and credit cards.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

By contrast, digital currency is peer-to-peer and settles almost instantly, eliminating third-party control of transactions and, in Lange's framing, delivering more freedom to individuals.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Cited Forbes material likewise contrasts digital settlement with ACH and wire transfers, which can take days for financial institutions to confirm.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) The [[fednow|FedNow]] service is presented as a partial remedy within the existing dollar system.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)
