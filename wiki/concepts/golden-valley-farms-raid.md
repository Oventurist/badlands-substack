# Golden Valley Farms Raid

The Golden Valley Farms raid was the June 2023 inspection, condemnation and seizure action taken by the Virginia Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (VDACS) against a 100-acre Amish family farm in Virginia operated by Samuel B. Fisher, reported by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and summarized in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of August 29, 2023.[1]

## Events

The Fisher family farm supplied what its customers regarded as healthy food to local consumers, serving roughly 500 people through a farm membership program.[1] The ordeal began on June 14, when a VDACS inspector made an unannounced visit to the farm; Fisher said he had "no idea" what prompted the inspection and told Townhall that the officials "came with a search warrant."[1]

The following day officials returned with a sheriff's deputy to conduct a thorough search of the property.[1] Fisher's meat was tagged under "administrative detention," leaving him unable to sell or even consume his own produce.[1] The state condemned and seized his livestock and meat-processing facility, and Fisher estimated that about $10,000 worth of products were seized and dumped.[1] The underlying legal dispute concerned his practice of processing meat onsite rather than at a facility inspected by the [[usda|USDA]].[1]

## Consequences and framing

Beyond the loss of income, the brief described the action as producing a crisis of trust in a community that prides itself on independence and sustainable farming.[1] Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the case to introduce [[conditional-acceptance|conditional acceptance]], a contract-law technique he argues citizens can deploy against health inspectors and other regulators to force the state to prove the factual basis of its demands.[1]
