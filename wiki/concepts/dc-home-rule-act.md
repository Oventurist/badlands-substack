# District of Columbia Home Rule Act

The Home Rule Act is the federal statute that grants the District of Columbia limited autonomy over its own affairs, including an elected mayor and council.[1]

## 2025 controversy

In August 2025, after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] declared a crime emergency in the capital and federalized the [[metropolitan-police-department|Metropolitan Police Department]], protests emerged in the district under the slogan "keep D.C. free," motivated by fears that [[congress|Congress]] could rescind the Home Rule Act altogether.[1] Because the district's autonomy exists only by congressional grant rather than by constitutional right, the episode fed directly into renewed advocacy for [[dc-statehood|D.C. statehood]] led by Mayor [[muriel-bowser|Muriel Bowser]].[1]
