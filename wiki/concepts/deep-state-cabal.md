# Deep State Cabal

The "Deep State Cabal" is the term used in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay for the transnational network he argues sits above nominal governments, intelligence services, and criminal syndicates.[1]

## Described structure

Carlson describes the Cabal as a strict hierarchy in which visible actors are middle management. In his account, the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] answers to the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]], which answers to the [[cia|CIA]]; the CIA and [[mossad|Mossad]] in turn answer to the British [[mi6|MI6]]; and Tel Aviv and Washington, D.C. answer to the [[city-of-london|City of London]], which he places at the top of the structure.[1] He argues the City of London controls the United States, Israel, Iran, and China alike.[1]

## Composition and motive

Carlson writes that the Cabal consists of Jews and non-Jews alike, and that it deliberately uses Jews and Israel as a scapegoat so that its actual membership can operate unobserved.[1] He characterizes its members as Jews who masquerade as Jews, fake Christians, fake Muslims, and atheists, asserting that the single binding element among them is the worship of Satan.[1]

## Alleged methods

The Cabal's domestic playbook, in Carlson's telling, includes manufacturing racial division through engineered riots roughly every thirty years, trafficking guns and narcotics to promote foreign wars and inner-city disorder, and filling privatized prisons for profit.[1] Its geopolitical methods include unleashing communism on Russia through the [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik Revolution]] and, after the Soviet collapse, pivoting its control to Communist China while Japan entered a thirty-year economic stagnation.[1]

Carlson argues that the genuine conflict of the present era is not the war in Ukraine, the conflict in Israel, or the threat of China and Iran, but a silent war between [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] and the Cabal seated in the City of London.[1] He contends that many within MAGA demand the punishment of recognizable middle management while Trump targets their superiors, comparing the strategy to laying poison for an ant colony rather than stepping on a single ant.[1]
