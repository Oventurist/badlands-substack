# Deep State Cabal

The "Deep State Cabal" is the term Badlands Media commentary uses for the network of families, financiers and institutional actors said to control Western politics through the money supply rather than through elected office.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) In [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s formulation the arrangement rests on concealment: "For the Cabal to exist, they need people to believe that they don't exist. For the system to exist, they need us to believe there isn't actually a system. For the Cabal to control the system, they need to control the money."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Financial basis

Carlson locates the Cabal's power in the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and the private banking system, arguing that without private bankers controlling America's money supply the Cabal "stands no chance of surviving."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) On this reading the [[federal-income-tax|federal income tax]] is not a funding mechanism at all but an illusion — a device to make Americans believe they pay for their government and that the government therefore works for them — while the real flow of money runs from the Fed to banks, corporations, governments and politicians.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) [[inflation|Inflation]] is described as the largest of these scams, a hidden transfer whose cost is passed to the public through higher prices and larger loans, with Carlson writing that "Bernie Madoff is a lightweight compared to these Ponzi schemers."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

The same logic is extended internationally through the [[petrodollar|Petrodollar]] arrangement, which Carlson says allowed the cost of American money creation to be exported to the rest of the world, and which he identifies as the reason the [[brics|BRICS]] nations now seek an alternative settlement currency.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Panic of 2024

Carlson's central claim is that the events of July 2024 — the abandonment of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] by the media after the presidential debate, his withdrawal from the race, and the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination of Donald Trump]] — reflect "panic like we have never seen before," since the Cabal could have replaced Biden or moved against Trump at any point in the preceding three years.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) He proposes that the trigger was [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s subtle June 10, 2024 pledge to make tips tax-free, read by the Cabal as an opening move toward abolishing income tax and then dismantling the money supply system itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Internal power struggle

Carlson argues the Cabal is not unified, noting that some players stuck by Biden while others abandoned him, and asks whether the disarray stems from a power struggle between the Cabal families.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) He cites the announced engagement of [[alex-soros|Alex Soros]] and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] as an intermarriage of the kind royal families use to consolidate power and keep peace, and asks whether it was intended to calm the mayhem or resulted from a power grab already underway.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) Looking ahead, he predicts the American Deep State will shift toward Europe and outside Trump's reach, with Cabal families in England, France and Germany contesting a larger share of power — offering the collapse of [[boeing|Boeing]] stock after Biden's inauguration, alongside the absence of bad press for France-based [[airbus|Airbus]], as a hint of that migration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Assessment

Carlson concludes that the Cabal has already lost the war and that the coming period will be one of continued exposure, a set-up for American economic recovery in 2025, and eventually the dismantling of the system and the return of power to the people.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) He urges readers to stop calibrating their expectations of the future against a past shaped by the Cabal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Trump's July 2026 election fraud speech

Badlands commentary has treated overt presidential statements about election integrity as direct blows to the Cabal. Episode 25 of The Blitz, published 18 July 2026 and subtitled "It's Treason, Then ...", was given over entirely to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election fraud speech of the preceding Thursday night and to what hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described as its implications for the Deep State cabal.[2] The hosts characterised the speech as seismic and argued it represented the beginnings of a true phase shift in both the [[shadow-war|Shadow War]] and the [[information-war|Information War]] at once.[2]
