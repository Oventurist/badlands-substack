# 2026 St. Petersburg International Economic Forum

[[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] served as the official guest country at the 2026 St. Petersburg International Economic Forum (SPIEF), sending a high-level delegation that included Energy Minister Prince [[abdulaziz-bin-salman|Abdulaziz bin Salman Al Saud]] along with officials from sovereign wealth funds and major state enterprises.[1]

## Agreements

On the sidelines of the event, the two sides signed approximately 30 agreements covering energy, investment, education, tourism and humanitarian cooperation.[1] Russian companies are exploring opportunities in petrochemicals, hydrogen, digital technologies and nuclear energy, while Saudi officials expressed interest in Russian expertise in IT, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and agriculture.[1]

The deepening ties reflect shared priorities on sovereignty and a multipolar international order, alongside Saudi Arabia's pragmatic engagement with [[brics|BRICS]] structures while maintaining flexibility on regional diplomacy.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recommended an RT op-ed on the forum, noting that [[russia|Russia]] and Saudi Arabia had worked together to stabilize world energy markets in 2020 during what he called the COVID scam, and that Russia was the first nation to recognize the newly formed Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1926 — exactly one hundred years earlier.[1] He argued that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s choice of Saudi Arabia as special guest was no coincidence, and reiterated his long-held view of the Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] as one of the generational figures of the age.[1] Should the Middle East achieve [[vision-2030|his Vision]] and become a first-world society, he wrote, people would flock to the region rather than flee it, forever wars would cease to fester, and the radicalization of vulnerable populations would diminish.[1]
