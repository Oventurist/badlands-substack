# Transgender Movement

The Transgender Movement, referred to in Badlands Media commentary as the "Trans push" or "Trans Movement," is described as the most damaging of the movements attributed to the [[deep-state|Deep State Cabal]].[1]

## Alleged origin and design

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that the movement originated from the [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock Institute]] in London, England, and that it preys on people with psychological disorders.[1] He argued that "the Trans push is designed to encourage and amplify mental disorders" rather than to relieve them.[1]

## Alleged consequences

Carlson wrote that, unlike other narratives that can be abandoned with little permanent damage, the effects of the Trans Movement on some of its participants are irreversible: people "physically altered, and permanently," some who "will never function normally again," unable to have children, with genitals destroyed and hormones that will never return to normal, left as "outcasts in a two-gender society."[1] He characterized the end result as the Cabal creating chaos, division, hate and confusion "while keeping a not insignificant number of people from reproducing ever again," tying the movement to an elite agenda of population control.[1] He further asserted that, as seen in school shootings, the same dynamics have "created domestic terrorists out of mentally unstable people," and that clinically unwell people are now being told they will be rounded up into internment camps while the media adds fuel to that hysteria rather than easing it.[1]

Alongside abortion, climate change, [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]] and [[black-lives-matter|BLM]], the trans agenda is listed among the causes defining the [[democratic-party|Democrat Party]] in Carlson's account.[1]
