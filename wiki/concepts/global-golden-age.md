# Global Golden Age

The **Global Golden Age** is a framing used by the Badlands Substack team to describe what it presents as an emerging worldwide period of renewal, discussed at length on Episode 26 of [[badlands-blitz|The Blitz]], published July 25, 2026.[1]

## Treatment on The Blitz

Episode 26, subtitled "On Colombian Hobbits, MAGA Ents and the Global Golden Age," was hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who said they would discuss the advent of the Global Golden Age "from some unlikely angles."[1] The hosts described their treatment as a circuitous route running from American remigration at one end to peace in the Middle East at the other, presenting these apparently unrelated threads as facets of the same emerging order.[1]

## Tolkien framing

A distinguishing feature of the episode's presentation was its use of Tolkien imagery: the hosts flagged in advance that they really would talk about "Colombian Hobbits and MAGA Ents," a metaphorical vocabulary they used to characterize the actors and slow-moving forces they see driving the transition.[1] The imagery supplied the episode's title.[1]

## Context

The discussion sat within The Blitz's standing role as a weekly recap and dissection of the weekday reporting published in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].[1] The episode recorded 124 likes and closed with the team's customary sign-off, "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"[1]

## Iran as accelerant (2026)
[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued in June 2026 that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] treats the Iran conflict — the very confrontation said to be eroding his public mandate — as "the very accelerant into the Golden Age he built his public platform on a decade ago," a plan aimed not only at the United States but at "a reimagined world."[2]

## Golden Age for the Middle East (April 2026)

In April 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] declared a Golden Age for the Middle East, a move [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read as advancing "the collective mind forward, toward a normalization with [[iran|Iran]]."[3] He argued Trump was attempting at least two things: breaking the public free of its conditioning to view Iran as a malignant adversary, much as with [[venezuela|Venezuela]], and diffusing any attempt by [[israel|Israel]] to subvert the peace process by accelerating the zeitgeist to a Golden Age discussion.[3] If Trump and the Iranians were seen earnestly discussing prosperous trade and development deals while Israeli media and political proxies kept "frothing at the mouth for war," he wrote, their status as warmonger and regional provocateur would be cemented and "there will be no way for the Zionists to maintain their eternal victimhood status."[3]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] added that the emerging "deal" was less a deal than the preview of one already done long ago, with the public watching "the translations and codifications of them on a necessary delay."[3] In his framing the disentanglement operation doubles as a pincer move — economic, financial, kinetic and mass psychological — steering toward a "reformed" Iran stripped of destabilizing cartel elements and integrated into the multipolar fold, its energy leverage redirected away from the old Hegemon's choke points.[3] He argued the deal itself "isn't the important thing," being merely the final translation layer and the end of the chaos chapter; what mattered was what the world learned in the interim, as the Globalist Hegemon reeled and the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] liked what it saw.[3]


## June 2026 framings

The release of the Department of Energy's [[fusion-science-and-technology-roadmap|Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap]] prompted [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] to declare that "the Golden Age is upon us," arguing that the long-teased technological revolution is real and arriving and that its release will draw out the "vipers, varmints, and jackals" who suppressed these developments and stifled the creative power of mankind.[4] He framed the moment as the end of living in fear of such forces and a call for good men to become defiant against aspiring tyranny, holding that only when those who demand the suppression of speech, the desecration of the Bill of Rights and the tempering of Christian teaching are chased back into the shadows will the Golden Age become possible.[4]

In the same edition, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the apparent U.S.–China AI arms race is in fact the build-out of "the infrastructure of the Golden Age itself," with frontier models serving as the investable justification layer for an energy and computing mesh built at sovereign scale.[4]
