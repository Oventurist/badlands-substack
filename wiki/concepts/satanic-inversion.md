# Satanic Principle of Inversion

The **satanic principle of inversion** is the rhetorical and psychological technique [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] asked ChatGPT to treat as a warfare tactic throughout its mapping of the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]]'s campaign.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Deschamps centred his prompts on inversion partly because the group described in the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]] identified itself as the "6.6.6."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

In substance, inversion means presenting a thing as its opposite: what is protective is recast as oppressive, and what is destructive is recast as liberating. In the model's synthesis, the Consortium uses inversion to portray traditional family structures as oppressive and regressive, while [[critical-race-theory|CRT]] and [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] are deployed to challenge binary gender roles and family norms and to sow seeds of doubt within society.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The same logic underwrites the framing of traditional moral values and religious teachings as obstacles to progress and unity, and the presentation of measures that curtail individual rights as necessary for the greater good.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Deschamps repeatedly instructed the model to interweave inversion throughout the plan wherever relevant, including alongside the [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]], [[esg|ESG scores]] and the treatment of CRT and Queer Theory as [[neo-marxism|neo-Marxist]] warfare, and asked for an explicit iteration in which inversion was named as a mechanism at all levels of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
