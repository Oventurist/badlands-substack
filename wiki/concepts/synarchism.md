# Synarchism

Synarchism is the term [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] uses, alongside "full-spectrum dominance," for the end goal pursued by the loose network of clandestine groups commonly lumped together under the label "Illuminati."[1]

DeLarme argues that the term [[bavarian-illuminati|Illuminati]] is thrown around inaccurately as a catch-all for any and all groups operating in a clandestine fashion, and that it carries more baggage than more precise terms like "[[deep-state|Deep State]]" or "Cabal."[1] In our ignorance, he writes, we tend to see the Deep State as a single secretive institution, when in reality it is more accurate to think of it as a loose-knit amalgamation of groups reluctantly working hand in glove with one another to achieve full-spectrum dominance of humanity — that is, synarchism.[1]
