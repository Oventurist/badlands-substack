# Hands Off! Protests

The "Hands Off!" protests were a nationwide wave of demonstrations held across the United States on Saturday, 5 April 2025, in opposition to the second administration of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Scale and grievances

Organizers said more than 1,300 rallies of varying sizes took place that day, making the mobilization the most widespread of Trump's second term to that point.[1] Demonstrators were animated by differing issues but converged on the single message "Hands off!"[1] Grievances cited in reporting included the mass firing of federal workers, immigration raids, and the involvement of billionaire [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in the federal government through [[doge|the Department of Government Efficiency]].[1]

At a rally in Washington, D.C., attendee Kelley Laird of Rockville, Maryland, told NPR: "There are so many issues... They're coming after education, coming after health care, coming after the arts, coming after the press."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] framed the protests as evidence that "the election fraud system is still in place," and attributed responsibility to what he called the ConInc GOPe controlled opposition that much of the MAGA base continues to support out of desperation.[1] In separate commentary on a Border Patrol detention death, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the same weekend's ralliers had marched in part for "hands off healthcare."[1]
