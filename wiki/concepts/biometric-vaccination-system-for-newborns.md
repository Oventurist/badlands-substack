# Biometric Vaccination System for Newborns

The biometric vaccination system for newborns is a component of Kenya's [[maisha-namba|Maisha Namba]] digital identity scheme. It is designed to replace conventional birth certificates with a biometric registration, while also tracking children from birth to five years of age in order to ensure that all children receive their scheduled vaccines.[1]

## Context

The system is part of the government-backed digital ID program announced in Kenya following a series of "closed-door meetings" between [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] and President [[william-ruto|William Ruto]]. Badlands Media points to Gates's track record in Africa — which it characterizes as marked by failures and human rights violations — as the reason his involvement in such programs draws suspicion about hidden agendas behind ostensibly philanthropic efforts.[1]

## Criticism

The article situates newborn biometric enrollment within a broader trajectory toward a [[technocracy|technocratic]] digital control system, arguing that such systems allow ruling classes to monitor and manipulate the population, regulate information flow, restrict access to resources and enforce societal norms that perpetuate existing power dynamics. Enrolling citizens at birth removes even the notional element of consent, since a newborn cannot opt in or out of a lifelong identity credential.[1] Human rights groups have separately warned that Kenya's digital ID architecture risks discrimination and the erosion of privacy.[1]
