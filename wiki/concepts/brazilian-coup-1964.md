# 1964 Brazilian Coup d'État

The 1964 Brazilian coup removed President [[joao-goulart|João Goulart]] from power.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) According to the article, alumni of the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]]'s [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]] who had been placed in the metalworkers' union provided intelligence to the [[cia|CIA]] and the [[pentagon|Department of Defense]], material that served to justify the military intervention.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

The episode is cited as one of the clearest illustrations of the article's thesis that U.S. labor institutes functioned as intelligence conduits, with the same model applied in Guyana in 1962–63 and in [[chilean-coup-1973|Chile]] a decade later.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
