# Assassination of Robert F. Kennedy

The assassination of Senator [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]] took place on June 5, 1968, at the Ambassador Hotel in Los Angeles, one day after his victory in the California Democratic presidential primary.[1] Badlands Media treats the killing as the decisive event that closed off a possible American future of peace, development and racial reconciliation, coming two months after the murder of [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]] and less than five years after the death of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1]

## The official story and its holes

[[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]], a young Palestinian man, was convicted as the lone gunman.[1] Badlands argues that mountains of evidence accumulated since point to a much darker story, beginning with the findings of Kennedy's coroner, who established that the killing bullet entered at close range from the back of the neck rather than from the front where Sirhan stood.[1] Medical examiner Thomas Noguchi placed the muzzle one to one and a half inches from the skin behind Kennedy's right ear, while witnesses put Sirhan at least eighteen inches away, prompting the suggestion that one of the security guards fired the fatal round.[1]

Sirhan confessed but said he had no memory of the shooting, and declassified material shows he had joined a California Rosicrucian temple in 1966.[1] His attorneys William Pepper and Laurie Dusek argued in a 2011 appeal filing that the neck bullet did not come from Sirhan's weapon and that the evidence bullet had been switched.[1]

## Conspiracy analysis

In 2021 Jeremy Kuzmarov of Covert Action Magazine published "New Evidence Implicates CIA, LAPD, FBI and Mafia as Plotters in Elaborate 'Hit' Plan to Prevent RFK From Ever Reaching White House," which Badlands regards as the clearest reconstruction to date of the plot, the cover-up and the motive.[1] The motive advanced is that a Kennedy presidency threatened to end the Vietnam war and the Cold War system of Mutually Assured Destruction, dissolving the control structure of the international financier oligarchy and the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]].[1]

## Aftermath

Kennedy's death, following King's, removed the last figures capable of holding together a nonviolent civil rights coalition.[1] Badlands argues that covert intelligence operations then replaced King's movement with the "New Left," including bomb-building organisations such as the [[weather-underground|Weather Underground]], while the [[apollo-program|Apollo]]-era space vision atrophied and the war in Vietnam consumed what remained of young Americans' patriotism.[1]
