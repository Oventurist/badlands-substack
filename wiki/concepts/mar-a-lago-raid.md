# Mar-a-Lago Raid

The **Mar-a-Lago raid** was the [[fbi|FBI]]'s August 8, 2022 search of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] residence in Palm Beach, Florida, conducted in connection with the handling of presidential records. It was personally approved by then-Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]].

## Internal Justice Department doubts

The Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 reports on an August 10, 2022 email — two days after the search — in which senior [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] official [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]] wrote to National Security Division attorney [[sophia-brill|Sophia Brill]] questioning whether Trump had declassified the seized documents. Stemler observed that although standard declassification procedures exist, "the President as Commander in Chief" is not necessarily bound by them, drawing an analogy to the pardon power. The email was obtained by [[just-the-news|Just the News]] and, according to the article, was discovered during the Justice Department's own review of federal law enforcement actions.

## Prior declassification claims

The search followed assertions from Trump's legal team — including a May 2022 letter from attorney [[evan-corcoran|Evan Corcoran]] — that Trump possessed broad declassification authority and had issued a standing order treating documents removed to his residence as declassified.

## Interpretation in the corpus

Badlands commentary treats the Stemler email as evidence that the raid's legal footing was doubted internally even as it was defended publicly, and as one data point in a wider argument that the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of federal law enforcement]] under the Biden administration was deliberate policy rather than the improvisation of rogue officials. That characterization is the source's interpretation rather than an established finding.
