# Tigris

Tigris was the code name of an artificial-intelligence chip venture that [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] was working to fund in the weeks before his abrupt removal as chief executive of [[openai|OpenAI]] in November 2023.[1]

## Fundraising

According to people familiar with the matter, Altman was actively working to raise billions of dollars from some of the world's largest investors for the venture, travelling to the Middle East to solicit backing.[1] The plan was to spin up an AI-focused chip company capable of producing semiconductors that would compete with those of [[nvidia|Nvidia Corp.]], which at the time dominated the market for artificial intelligence workloads.[1] The venture had not yet been formed and talks with investors were in early stages, the people said, speaking anonymously because the discussions were private.[1]

Altman was simultaneously raising money for an AI-focused hardware device developed in tandem with former [[apple|Apple Inc.]] design chief [[jony-ive|Jony Ive]], and had discussed both ventures with [[softbank|SoftBank Group Corp.]], Saudi Arabia's [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], [[mubadala-investment-company|Mubadala Investment Company]] and others, seeking tens of billions of dollars in total.[1] Many details of the scale and focus of Altman's chip ambitions, including the Tigris code name itself, had not previously been reported.[1] The disclosures emerged in the immediate aftermath of the [[2023-openai-leadership-crisis|2023 OpenAI leadership crisis]].[1]
