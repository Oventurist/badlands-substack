# Badlands Blitz Episode 3

**Badlands Blitz Episode 3**, subtitled "Donald Trump's Global Victory Tour," is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] published to the Badlands Substack on January 24, 2026. The post accompanying the episode had accrued 348 likes at the time of archiving. Like the rest of the series, the episode is presented by the host writing under the pen name [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] as a weekly recap and dissection of the material produced Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].

## Format and guest

The episode features [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as co-host. The introduction describes the pair diving into a week "absolutely full of Sovereign Alliance proofs, Trumpian narrative deployments and Central Banker capitulation" — a framing that ties the week's news cycle to the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] thesis that recurs throughout Badlands commentary, to messaging attributed to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and to what the hosts characterise as concessions by central banking authorities.

As is standard for the program, the show also includes a segment drawn from the Substack comments section, in which the hosts review reader responses posted over the preceding week. The author notes that the Blitz is intended to run close to sixty minutes and that this episode "nearly hit the mark," an aside consistent with the series' ongoing effort to standardise its runtime.

## Editorial notes

The post restates the outlet's 2026 editorial posture: [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] intends to serve the Substack audience with exclusive content, describing those subscribers as among its most loyal and engaged. The author reiterates that all Badlands content, including The Blitz, will remain free, and that paid subscriptions exist solely to compensate contributors for their time. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription, with the note that they may cancel at any time. The post closes with the series' customary sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright."
