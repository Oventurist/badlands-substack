# Tumbler Ridge School Shooting

The Tumbler Ridge school shooting was a mass shooting reported to have taken place in Canada on Wednesday, February 11, 2026, and covered as a bonus item in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief under the headline "Another Trans Shooter Kills 8, Injures Dozens in Canada."

## Reported facts

According to the Brief, citing *[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]]*, an eighteen-year-old male-to-female transgender person identified as [[jesse-strang|Jesse Strang]] killed eight people, including five students between the ages of twelve and thirteen and an eleven-year-old stepbrother. A twelve-year-old girl was reported in critical condition and fighting for her life in hospital. The Brief cites [[cnn|CNN]] live coverage for the report that the shooter had transitioned roughly six years earlier — around age twelve — and notes that accounts differed on whether the suspect was seventeen or eighteen. CNN further reported that the suspect was known to Canadian police, who had attended the residence on several occasions because of mental health concerns. Authorities stated that the investigation was ongoing.

## Framing

The Brief presents the incident without extended contributor commentary, but its headline places the shooting within a recurring narrative theme in the corpus concerning the gender identity of mass shooting suspects and the way legacy outlets report it. As with other rapidly developing incidents, the details summarized derive from early reporting and remained subject to revision at the time of publication.
