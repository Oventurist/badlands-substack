# Spike Ferritin Nanoparticle Vaccine

The **Spike Ferritin Nanoparticle** vaccine, or **SpFN**, is a pan-coronavirus vaccine candidate developed by the [[walter-reed-army-institute-of-research|Walter Reed Army Institute of Research]] and manufactured at the institute's Pilot Bioproduction Facility, the Department of Defense's small-scale vaccine and biologics plant.[1] In Badlands Media commentary it is referred to as the "supervax."[1]

## Design and development

The Army laboratory received its first DNA sequencing of the COVID-19 virus in early 2020 and elected from the outset to pursue a vaccine effective against all potential variants rather than only the circulating strain.[1] SpFN uses a soccer-ball-shaped protein with 24 faces, permitting scientists to attach spike proteins from multiple coronavirus strains to different faces of the same particle — a design distinct from the existing mRNA and viral-vector products.[1] Animal trials completed in 2021 with positive results, and Phase 1 human trials concluded in December 2021, also positive and undergoing final review; Phase 2 and Phase 3 trials remained outstanding.[1]

## Defense One report

A Defense One article of December 21, 2021 titled "US Army Creates Single Vaccine Against All COVID & SARS Variants, Researchers Say" reported that WRAIR scientists expected within weeks to announce a vaccine effective against COVID-19 and all its variants, including Omicron, as well as earlier SARS-origin viruses.[1] Dr. Kayvon Modjarrad, director of Walter Reed's infectious diseases branch, gave the outlet an exclusive interview describing the results.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]], writing on December 29, 2022, treats the Defense One report as a decisive and under-discussed fact, emphasising that the Army stated it *had created* — not was working on — such a vaccine, and that the article remained published.[1] She speculates that SpFN may have been distributed covertly, possibly to U.S. service members subject to vaccine mandates and possibly in vials nominally labelled as other manufacturers' products, and links this to her wider thesis that [[donald-trump|Trump]] and [[woody-johnson|Woody Johnson]] arranged for millions of doses to contain saline or the Army formulation instead of the commercial [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccines]].[1] She frames the outcome as the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s [[depopulation-agenda|depopulation]] plan being foiled.[1] These claims are speculative and are offered by the author as such.[1]

Gillespie also directs readers interested in the "soccer-shaped protein" to her own article "Soccer Balls and High Fashion" at Kitty's Newsletter.[1]
