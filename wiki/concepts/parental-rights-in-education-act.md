# Parental Rights in Education Act

The Parental Rights in Education Act is a Florida law signed by Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] that restricts classroom instruction on gender identity, dubbed the "Don't Say Gay" bill by its detractors.[1] As originally enacted the measure applied through the third grade; it was later extended to cover instruction through the twelfth grade.[1]

## Political consequences

The law became the flashpoint of DeSantis's confrontation with [[disney]] after the company's leadership publicly opposed the bill.[1] That dispute led directly to the February 2023 legislation abolishing the [[reedy-creek-improvement-district|Reedy Creek Improvement District]] and creating the [[central-florida-tourism-oversight-district|Central Florida Tourism Oversight District]], whose board DeSantis appointed.[1] The new district in turn eliminated all diversity, equity, and inclusion programs within its jurisdiction in August 2023.[1]

The act sits alongside the [[stop-woke-act|Stop WOKE Act]] as a centerpiece of DeSantis's cultural agenda, which he carried into his 2024 presidential campaign with the pledge that "the woke agenda ends up in the dustbin of history."[1]
