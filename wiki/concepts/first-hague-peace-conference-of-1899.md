The First Hague Peace Conference of 1899 was an international diplomatic conference held in The Hague, Netherlands, that produced foundational treaties and conventions of modern international humanitarian law.[1] A May 2023 Badlands Media article cited international law professor [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]] as having studied treaties going back to this 1899 conference in the course of evaluating the proposed pandemic treaty.[1]

Boyle was cited arguing that the pandemic treaty's provision to "automatically come into effect immediately upon its signature" was without precedent among the treaties he had studied, including those dating to the First Hague Peace Conference — a point the article used to underscore the novelty and danger of the WHO instrument.[1]
