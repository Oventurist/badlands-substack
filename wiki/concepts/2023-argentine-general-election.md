# 2023 Argentine General Election

The 2023 Argentine general election culminated in a presidential run-off on 19 November 2023 that was won by the libertarian outsider [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]], wrenching the country sharply to the right after a bombastic anti-establishment campaign.[1] Coverage of the result drew explicit comparisons between Milei's insurgent style and that of former U.S. president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and framed the vote as a referendum on an economy suffering one of the world's highest inflation rates.[1]

## Result

Milei's rival [[sergio-massa|Sergio Massa]] conceded on the Sunday evening of the run-off in a brief speech given before official results were announced, telling supporters that "Milei is the president elected for the next 4 years" and confirming that he had already called to congratulate him.[1] Provisional results showed Milei with over 55 percent of the vote — 13,781,154 ballots — with more than 94 percent counted, per Argentina's National Electoral Chamber, which had not at that point declared an official winner.[1]

## Significance

The victory marked an extraordinary rise for a former television pundit who entered the race as a political outsider promising to "break up with the status quo" exemplified by Massa.[1] Milei's campaign pledge to dollarize Argentina, if enacted, was expected to thrust the country into new territory, as no country of Argentina's size had previously turned over the reins of its own monetary policy to decisionmakers in Washington.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Milei had stunned the world in August 2023 by taking the largest share of votes in the primary as a third-party candidate, and that the run-off confirmed that surge.[1] She predicted that his connections with Russia would be "immediately investigated" and anticipated fabricated scandals designed to derail his agenda, describing his published plan for government as "a Libertarian fever dream and a Hail Mary to save his nation."[1] Posing the question of whether the world would discover that the "Argentine Paradox was a feature, not a bug," she closed with "Congratulations and Godspeed, President Milei."[1]
