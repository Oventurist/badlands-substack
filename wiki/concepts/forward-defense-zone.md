# Forward Defense Zone

**Forward Defense Zone** is the term used by the Israeli military, as reported in the Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026, for the new deployment line it has established in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]] — an Israeli-controlled zone extending approximately 5 to 10 kilometers from the border that places dozens of villages inside Israeli-held territory.

## The deployment

According to Reuters reporting cited in the Brief, the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] published a map of the zone showing five Israeli divisions, backed by naval forces, operating within it to dismantle [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] infrastructure and address threats to northern Israel. The zone is described as effectively an occupation belt carved out of Lebanese territory under the cover of the ceasefire terms, which the Brief notes Israel has simultaneously said it will enforce with "full force."

## Territorial logic

Badlands commentary interprets the zone as the coastal complement to the capture of [[mount-hermon|Mount Hermon]] in the east: by moving the effective Israeli border north toward Tyre, Israel creates the factual basis for shifting the maritime boundary and annexing the [[qana-gas-field|Qana Gas Field]] — territory it had previously sought only through lawfare. The zone is thus presented as the mechanism by which the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel project]] converts military occupation into a new border, with the gas field as the economic prize.
