# Deep State Archipelago

The "Deep State Archipelago" is an analytical framework developed by the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] holding that the American permanent bureaucracy and its allied media apparatus are far more fragmented and siloed than they publicly appear.[1] Rather than a unified command structure, the model describes scattered islands of institutional power that have been cut off from their former information channels, leaving officials "genuinely grasping in the dark when presented with reasonable, direct questions for people who should ostensibly have the answers ... were they in control."[1]

The concept is presented as running alongside [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s [[devolution|Devolution]] research, which posits a covert continuity-of-government arrangement displacing the visible administration.[1]

## Application

Burning Bright applied the framework to the August 8, 2024 [[pentagon|Pentagon]] press briefing in which spokeswoman [[sabrina-singh|Sabrina Singh]] refused to rule out Ukrainian strikes on Moscow during the [[kursk-incursion|Kursk incursion]], arguing that "to be nefarious, there has to be some level of competence on display" and that the exchange showed none.[1] A characteristic feature of the model is that official statements can be read "bicamerally" — as either threatening or confused and mystifying — and that either reading produces the same effect on what the writer calls the Collective Mind: unease, anxiety and fear.[1] The commentary concluded with the framing that the regime is the problem and [[donald-trump|Trump]], "and by extension, we," are the solution.[1]
