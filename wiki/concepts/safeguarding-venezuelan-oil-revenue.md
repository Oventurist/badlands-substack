# Safeguarding Venezuelan Oil Revenue (Executive Order)

"Safeguarding Venezuelan Oil Revenue for the Good of the American and Venezuelan People" is an executive order signed by [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] on January 9, 2026, six days after the United States military operation that captured Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]. It is reported in the January 19, 2026 Badlands Brief.

## Provisions and stated purpose

The order is described as aimed at shielding Venezuelan oil revenues held in United States Treasury accounts from seizure or judicial attachment. The [[white-house|White House]] said the action was meant to protect the funds so that they could support American foreign policy goals and help stabilise Venezuela's economy.

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterises the order as protecting Venezuelan oil revenue "from bankers and creditors by placing it in a Qatari bank account and prohibiting any liens or sanctions to be placed on it." He pairs it with the lifting of Venezuelan sanctions by Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] and argues that together the two actions constitute a deliberate "move against the global banking system," timed to coincide with Maduro's exit.

In his account, the significance lies in what follows: with sanctions lifted, a civil action brought by Maduro against executives of banks, commodity trading houses, and oil companies — dismissed without prejudice in 2019 because of national-security concerns tied to Trump's sanctions — can be resumed, potentially opening discovery into money laundering connected to [[pdvsa|PDVSA]], the [[matthias-krull|Matthias Krull]] case, and institutions such as [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].
