# Potential Events

**Potential Events** is an analytical term coined by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] in his writing on China, Russia and the [[information-war|Information War]].[1]

## Definition

Potential Events do not refer to "almost" events or "false" events, but to events that take place in the realm of the Narrative.[1] Burning Bright argues that virtually everything the Media Industrial Complex deploys on behalf of its [[deep-state|Deep State]] and systemic masters is a Narrative or Potential Event, and that counters from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and other political figures and thought leaders tracked in the Information War fall along the same lines.[1]

He rejects the intuition that "potential" or "narrative" events are less important than Actual Events, holding that nothing could be further from the truth.[1] Potential Events originate within the realm of the mind and are deployed either in service of or in opposition to the Collective Mind of American and world society — the ultimate prize sought both by globalists in the [[system-of-systems|System of Systems]] and by patriots within the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] opposing them.[1]

## Relation to Fifth-Generation Warfare

The framework is situated within [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation War]] for the psyche of humanity, fought on many fronts simultaneously and at different layers of reality.[1] Burning Bright insists there is nothing magical or pseudo-scientific about the concept: narrative deployments and media blitzes are as much weapons of war as kinetic deployments, and are how globalist actors infiltrated institutions and administrations in world governments to seize levers of power, corrupt financial systems and subvert sovereign culture in the west.[1]

## Contrast with "Actuals"

By contrast, Burning Bright describes the Chinese theater of the Shadow War as unusually rich in Actual Events — real-world data points such as the [[chips-and-science-act|CHIPS Act]], [[apple|Apple]]'s shift of production to Arizona and the growth of [[brics|BRICS]] — which he reads as evidence of a genuine decoupling of the American and Chinese economies.[1] That decoupling, painful in the interim and beginning with the COVID-era lockdowns, he expects to become a powerful mechanism for positive change in international trade, sovereignty and freedom.[1]

## Actual and Potential fused (November 2022)

In "Death Blossom" (November 23, 2022), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] refined the distinction by noting that although he usually describes Potential Events as existing purely in the realm of the Narrative, some events fall into the realm of the Actual as well.[2] He cited the November 2022 missile strike on Polish territory — which he characterized as an attempt by [[ukraine|Ukraine]] to frame [[russia|Russia]] for the kick-off of World War III — as an instance in which a real-world kinetic event and a narrative deployment were fused.[2] Some of the most effective and damaging events in human history, he argued, represent exactly this "morbid genius fusion of the Actual and the Potential."[2]

By his account the Actual Event succeeded in provoking the reactions it was designed to provoke, but the Potential Event layered on top of it — the framing of Russia as responsible — collapsed within roughly twelve hours, owing to the ruthless logic, attention to detail and decentralized hivemind coordination of the Badlands-adjacent research community.[2] Had the twin Actual-Potential deployment held, he wrote, the world might have changed "on a dime," spilling into a pre-planned cascade of dominoes en route to a forced global reset under cover of world war.[2] He also applied the framework to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], arguing that Trump's deployments are "almost always Potential in nature" — rarely kinetic even when he was public Commander-in-Chief, and rarely Actual even in the political realm, with his best and most frequent moves existing in the realm of the Narrative.[2]

## Potential damage made Actual (December 2022)

In "The War of Stories" (December 30, 2022), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] restated the distinction while cautioning against reading "Potential" as harmless: although he often refers to Narratives as Potential Events, "there is nothing potential about the very real 'Actual' damage they can inflict" once absorbed, regurgitated and ultimately actioned by industry complexes far more literal in their deployments than the media, such as the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]].[3] He also observed that much of the national and international news cycle, from Potential and Narrative Events through to Actual Events, is obfuscated by all sides on the [[game-theory-game-board|Game Theory Game Board]].[3]
