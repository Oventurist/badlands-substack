# North-South Transportation Corridor

The North-South Transportation Corridor (NSTC) is a multimodal trade route linking South Asia and the Indian Ocean through [[iran|Iran]] to Russia and northern Europe, intended as an alternative to maritime routes running through the Suez Canal.

In the March 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites the corridor to explain why [[russia|Russia]] and [[china|China]] appear to be encouraging Iran to continue fighting rather than seeking stabilization of the Persian Gulf. He notes that the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]] has invested billions in the NSTC and that Iran is "the critical central node in connecting Asia to Moscow," then asks whether the corridor can continue to operate effectively if Iranian infrastructure is destroyed in the war.

The corridor is thus presented in the corpus as a material interest underlying the diplomatic posture of the powers Badlands describes as the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]]: a strategic defeat for Iran would not only damage the credibility of that alliance's power projection but would sever a physical trade artery in which its members have already sunk substantial capital.
