# Peregrine Mission One

Peregrine Mission One was a commercial American lunar lander mission that failed shortly after launch in January 2024 and was deliberately destroyed by re-entry into Earth's atmosphere on Thursday, January 18, 2024, burning up somewhere over the South Pacific.[1]

## Mission and failure

The lander, built by Pittsburgh-based [[astrobotic|Astrobotic]], cost approximately $108 million and launched from Florida aboard a Vulcan rocket. Shortly after launch it suffered a critical propellant leak. Engineers were able to stabilize the spacecraft, but the loss of oxidizer meant a safe touchdown on the lunar surface could never be attempted.[1]

Experts worked with [[nasa|NASA]] and other space companies to identify the safest and most responsible way of ending the mission. Astrobotic ultimately decided to dispose of the craft through controlled re-entry rather than allow it to wander through space as a collision hazard.[1]

## Payload

The lander was reported to be carrying a sample of President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]]'s DNA along with the cremated ashes of dozens of people — reportedly up to seventy — all of which were lost in the re-entry. British outlet Metro carried a more alarmist framing of the same detail, reporting that "JFK's DNA 'could be lost forever in space' after moon launch hitch."[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the top-line story — a $108 million lander failing and being burned up — concealed more significant details. She noted that NASA was mentioned repeatedly in coverage while the [[united-states-space-force|Space Force]] appeared only in a photo caption identifying the launch site as Space Launch Complex 41 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.[1]

Ashe contended that placing Kennedy's DNA on the Moon served no scientific purpose; at best it was nostalgia, and given the price tag more likely ceremonial or religious in character. She linked the episode to a broader argument that "The Science" functions as a religion requiring theatrical sacrifices and ongoing censorship, citing the film *Leave the World Behind* — produced by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s production company — in which two minors wear shirts reading "obey" and "nasa" side by side, and which depicts a shadowy cabal running the world. She concluded that space, like climate, nuclear, and virology, "presents as a religious endeavor that cannot be questioned."[1]
