# SAVE Program

The Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) program is a [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] system used to verify immigration and citizenship status, which in 2025–2026 was applied at scale to state voter registration files.[1]

## Use in voter-roll checks

The Associated Press reported in May 2026 that the Trump administration had run at least 67 million voter registrations through SAVE, in eligibility checks that critics said could lead to valid voters being removed from the rolls before the November elections.[1] [[uscis|USCIS]] said 60 million checks identified about 24,000 potential noncitizens, and AP reported that at least 25 states had used SAVE for voter-roll checks since April 2025.[1]

## Related legislation and polling

The program's expansion accompanied congressional action on the [[save-act|SAVE Act]], H.R. 22, which would require documentary proof of U.S. citizenship to register to vote in federal elections.[1] [[rasmussen-reports|Rasmussen Reports]] found in a 8–10 March 2026 survey of 1,098 likely voters that 63% supported the SAVE Act and 32% opposed it.[1]

Commenting on the controversy, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the citizenship-verification fight as one element of a broader engineered crisis cascade, writing that the driving force was not Democrats but "unipartisan globalists" and that the structure was being dismantled and restructured.[1]
