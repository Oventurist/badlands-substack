# Coutts 4

The Coutts 4 are four men who led a border-crossing protest at Coutts, Alberta during the 2022 [[freedom-convoy|Freedom Convoy]] and were subsequently imprisoned for two years without trial.[1]

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] described the case as one of the most important coming out of the convoy, noting that he had interviewed the only journalist in Canada covering it.[1] Two of the four were released in early 2024, and — most significantly — the charges alleging a plot to kill an RCMP officer were dropped.[1]

Esler argued the dropped charges were consequential on two fronts: they had been the stated justification for holding the men in remand, or pre-trial imprisonment, for two years, and they had also been one of the narratives promoted in Canadian legacy media to justify the invocation of the [[emergencies-act|Emergencies Act]].[1] The case forms part of what Esler characterized as a broader Canadian [[lawfare|lawfare]] front in which rulings against the public are repeatedly exposed as corrupt government operations.[1]
