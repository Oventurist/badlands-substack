# Wokeism

Wokeism, in the account given by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in the February 5, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Control Through Language," is a foreign-engineered ideological program deployed against the United States to divide the country, produce psychologically unwell people and damage the American economy.[1]

## Claimed origin

Carlson attributes the creation of wokeism to Europe, and specifically to think tanks run by the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[1] He states that its antecedent, "political correctness," was created as a weapon of control in pre-Nazi Germany and later introduced to US university campuses in the early 1990s, and that the British [[cabal|Cabal]] created and controlled both communism and the Nazis.[1]

## Rise and claimed collapse

Carlson argues wokeism peaked roughly twelve to eighteen months before he wrote, a period in which even rolling one's eyes at it invited being labelled a threat to America or a Nazi, and that by early 2024 it had entered free-fall — still present but fading fast.[1] He illustrates the shift with his own family: at a high school parents' night sixteen months earlier he heard about classrooms as [[safe-spaces|safe spaces]] and an LGBTQ+ club with no Christian club permitted, amid BLM and rainbow flags, whereas his son later reported that the material had largely disappeared from the school, though many teachers still criticise [[donald-trump|Trump]] openly.[1] He cites [[qanon|Q]]'s claim that 4–6% of American minds are permanently broken by mind control, and suggests the figure is at least double that among public school teachers.[1]

Carlson speculates that wokeism may have been built in order to be knocked down — a test of how far the public would go in accepting ideas he characterises as mentally unwell before refusing, and a means of establishing parameters for other matters.[1] He contrasts truth, which he likens to a lion needing no protection, with lies, which he likens to an infant requiring constant care.[1] He also recalls [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] setting aside hundreds of millions of taxpayer dollars on leaving office to fund thousands of "woke journalists" writing propaganda for two years, after which their media employers underwent mass layoffs.[1]

## Economic weapon

The essay's central geopolitical claim is that wokeism functions as economic warfare. Carlson notes that [[boeing|Boeing]]'s chief competitor [[airbus|Airbus]] produces mostly in Europe while Boeing produces in the United States, and that the same European interests that spread wokeism through America pick up the business American firms lose.[1] He argues consumers boycotting American brands are therefore unwittingly serving the [[cabal|Cabal]]'s aims, citing the [[2023-bud-light-boycott|Bud Light boycott]], in which the people most hurt were factory workers, warehouse staff and delivery drivers rather than the DEI and [[esg|ESG]] operatives who engineered the campaign and were well paid for it.[1] He maintains the trans advertising campaign was intentional rather than a blunder, and that destroying an American brand Americans identify with — and the American economy with it — was the goal.[1]

He extends the analysis to [[boeing|Boeing]] after a door detached from an aircraft in mid-flight, noting that the emerging narrative blamed excessive diversity hiring and LGBTQ+ initiatives over aircraft safety.[1] While agreeing Boeing should hire the most qualified people, he asks why the media is suddenly permitted to attack wokeism at all.[1]
