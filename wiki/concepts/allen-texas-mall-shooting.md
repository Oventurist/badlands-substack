# Allen Texas Mall Shooting

The Allen, Texas mall shooting was a mass shooting at a Dallas-area shopping mall in early May 2023 that left eight people dead and several others injured.[1]

Law-enforcement sources identified the suspected gunman as 33-year-old Mauricio Garcia.[1] Anonymous law-enforcement sources claimed Garcia may have been motivated by white-supremacist ideology, citing extremist social-media posts and a patch worn on his chest bearing the letters "RWDS."[1]

## Media reaction

The attribution of white-supremacist motive to a Hispanic suspect became the focus of national commentary. On [[the-view|The View]], co-host [[ana-navarro|Ana Navarro]] argued that being Hispanic or Black does not make a person immune from racism, radicalization, or white supremacy, citing [[proud-boys|Proud Boys]] chairman [[enrique-tarrio|Enrique Tarrio]].[1] Similar arguments had been advanced previously by commentators including Jemele Hill and Bree Newsome Bass regarding Black police officers.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] criticized this framing as narrative-driven opportunism, writing that the left was "more concerned about spinning these events to fit a narrative" than with the people who had just been killed.[1]
