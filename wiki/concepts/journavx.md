# Journavx

**Journavx** is a non-opioid pain medication developed by Vertex Pharmaceuticals and approved by the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] on 30 January 2025.[1] The drug was approved for short-term pain of the kind that often follows surgery or injury, and was designed to eliminate the risks of addiction and overdose associated with opioid medications such as Vicodin and OxyContin.[1]

Journavx was described as the first new pharmaceutical approach to treating pain in more than twenty years, offering an alternative to both opioids and over-the-counter medications such as ibuprofen and acetaminophen.[1] Reporting on the approval noted that the medication's modest effectiveness and lengthy development process underscored the difficulty of finding new ways to manage pain.[1]

Kennedy Jr.]] at the Department of Health and Human Services.[1]
