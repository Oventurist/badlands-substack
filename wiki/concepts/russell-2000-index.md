# Russell 2000 Index

The Russell 2000 Index is used by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in the February 5, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Control Through Language" as a broader alternative to the [[sp-500-index|S&P 500]] for judging the health of American equities.[1] Carlson describes it as covering the top 2,000 US companies rather than the top 500, and states that its constituents were down 2% in performance over the preceding three years — essentially since [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] took office.[1]

He compares this to showing up to work for three years not only without pay but at a net cost, such as paying for parking in order to work for free.[1] Factoring in what he calls "real" [[inflation|inflation]] estimates of around 33%, he concludes the market has lost a third of its purchasing power, so that the stock market is in reality down roughly 33% over two to three years depending on the index used — a framing under which talk of "all-time highs" loses its force.[1] Carlson further argues that the 99% of American companies not represented in any stock index are doing worse still, and that many are being cannibalised by [[cabal|Cabal]]-controlled mega-corporations.[1]
