# Student Loan Wage Garnishment (2026)

Student loan wage garnishment refers to the federal government's administrative seizure of a portion of a defaulted borrower's paycheck to satisfy outstanding federal student debt.[1]

## Resumption in January 2026

The [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] announced that it would begin garnishing the wages of student loan borrowers in default in early January 2026, a spokesperson for the [[department-of-education|U.S. Department of Education]] confirmed to [[cnbc|CNBC]] on December 23, 2025.[1] It marked the first time a portion of borrowers' paychecks had been at risk since the beginning of the Covid pandemic, when collection activity was halted.[1]

Starting the week of January 7, the Education Department expected roughly 1,000 defaulted borrowers to receive notices of administrative wage garnishment, with the number of notified borrowers increasing thereafter.[1]

## Collection powers

The US government holds extraordinary collection powers on federal debts, and can seize borrowers' federal tax refunds, wages, and Social Security retirement and disability benefits.[1] The Education Department can seize up to 15% of a student loan holder's after-tax income and apply it toward the debt.[1]
