# LIBOR

LIBOR, the London Interbank Offered Rate, was the London-set benchmark interest rate used to price loans and derivatives worldwide. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]], LIBOR is treated as the financial mechanism through which the [[city-of-london|City of London]] exercised control over the American banking system.

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that since early in his first presidency, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "has been fighting a silent war against the City of London," and that "he has replaced the LIBOR system with the [[sofr|SOFR]] system, taking control of the American banking system away from the City of London and giving it to the American [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]." The transition is presented in the article as one of the concrete, verifiable moves in a conflict the author considers largely invisible to the public, alongside the onshoring of manufacturing and the use of tariffs against globalist power.
