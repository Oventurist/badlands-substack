# Tower of Babel

The **Tower of Babel** is the biblical account of a time when mankind spoke a single language, invoked in [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] essay "On Languages and Cover-ups" as mythological corroboration for the [[ancient-german-language-thesis|ancient German language thesis]].[1]

## Use in the argument

The essay, which refers to the story as the Tower of Babylon, notes that many religious mythologies — the Babel account being the most famous — claim that mankind once all spoke the same language, and argues that readers should therefore not be surprised to find linguistic evidence of such a tongue.[1] The claimed common language is identified as ancient German or something almost identical to it, a proposal the author says makes sense because *germ*, like *seed*, often refers to the first of something.[1]

The invocation fits the series' wider position that a great many stories which purport to be myths or legends may in fact be accurate accounts of history, while official history may itself be largely mythological.[1] The author offers the mainstream account of Christopher Columbus's discovery of America as an example of official history he considers impossible.[1]
