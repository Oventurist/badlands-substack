# 2023 Central American Migrant Caravan

The 2023 Central American migrant caravan was a large group of migrants, drawn heavily from Central America and Venezuela, that set off from southern Mexico toward the United States on Monday, October 30, 2023.[1]

## Composition and route

Officials in the southern Mexican state of Chiapas put the group at some 3,500 people departing on foot from Tapachula, near the Guatemalan border, while organizer [[irineo-mujica|Irineo Mujica]] estimated around 5,000 participants.[1] According to Mujica, most were from Cuba, El Salvador, Guatemala, Haiti, Honduras and Venezuela.[1] Escorted by civil protection officials and ambulances, the migrants walked a coastal highway around midday and planned to spend the night in Huehuetan, roughly 16 miles (25 km) north of their starting point.[1]

## Political context

The caravan's departure added to renewed pressure on the southern border as President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], seeking reelection in 2024, faced criticism over illegal crossings.[1] Separately, Republican senators including [[john-barrasso|John Barrasso]], [[john-cornyn|John Cornyn]], [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]] and [[pete-ricketts|Pete Ricketts]] said after an October 31 border visit that agents had intercepted crossers carrying explosive devices Barrasso called "tailor-made for terrorism," and cited more than eight million illegal crossings under Biden.[1]

Commentary from [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] brief characterized the situation as "the purposeful destruction of a country," invoking [[saul-alinsky|Saul Alinsky]]'s *Rules For Radicals* and arguing that open borders were intended to flood the United States with fighting-age males and stress its systems to collapse.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the border as a "third front" alongside Ukraine and Israel in what he framed as a scheme enriching the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]], while maintaining that [[devolution|Devolution]] planning would protect National Essential Functions.[1]
