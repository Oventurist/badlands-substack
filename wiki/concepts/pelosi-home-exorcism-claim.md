# Pelosi Home Exorcism Claim

The Pelosi home exorcism claim was a January 2023 controversy arising from statements by [[alexandra-pelosi|Alexandra Pelosi]], daughter of former House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], that her mother had priests attempt an exorcism of the family's San Francisco home following the [[paul-pelosi-attack|attack on Paul Pelosi]].[1]

In an interview published by The New York Times on January 21, 2023, Alexandra Pelosi said: "I think that weighed really heavy on her soul. I think she felt really guilty. I think that really broke her. Over Thanksgiving, she had priests coming, trying to have an exorcism of the house and having prayer services."[1]

[[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] sought confirmation from the [[archdiocese-of-san-francisco|Archdiocese of San Francisco]], and on Monday, January 23, 2023, Peter Marlow, the archdiocese's Executive Director of Communications and Media Relations, replied: "The Archdiocese is unaware of the exorcism question you raised."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that it was obvious from the start that the episode was "some sort of publicity stunt or flat-out lie," joking that the best supporting evidence was that Nancy Pelosi still lived in the house and "hasn't yet burst into flames."[1] With the archdiocese's denial on record, he posed the remaining question of whether the story was "merely the secondhand ramblings of the withered former Speaker, or was this meant to be some sort of communication letting others know that the house was now clean," concluding that one could only speculate.[1]
