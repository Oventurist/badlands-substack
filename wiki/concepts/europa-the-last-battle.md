# Europa: The Last Battle

*Europa: The Last Battle* is a ten-part documentary series described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as neo-Nazi propaganda circulating in the contemporary alternative media space.[1]

Ehret cites it as a leading example of the pro-Nazi material now emerging onto the scene, alongside the film *Occupied* produced by [[stew-peters|Stew Peters]].[1] He argues that such productions rely on [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]] as "proof" of the thesis that evil Jews running the world are the cause of all evil, and that the series promotes the narrative of "Jewish bankers' conspiracies undermining the noble honest royal families of Europe" previously advanced by occultists such as [[nesta-webster|Nesta Webster]], [[gerard-encausse|Papus]], [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], and [[alice-bailey|Alice Bailey]].[1]
