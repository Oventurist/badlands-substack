# Cairo Geniza

The Cairo Geniza is a cache of roughly 250,000 manuscript fragments discovered at the end of the 19th century.[1]

Matthew Ehret cites the Geniza as a critical evidentiary source for the history of [[khazaria|Khazaria]], noting that physical evidence of the Khazar Kingdom was nearly all destroyed or suppressed, leaving modern scholars little empirical material and ample room for what he calls speculative gossip led by figures such as Arthur Koestler and David Icke.[1] Dozens of Christian and Muslim scholars of the 8th–12th centuries nonetheless wrote extensively of the kingdom's existence, and some of the Geniza fragments are finally being made public, bringing direct evidence to light for the first time in millennia.[1]
