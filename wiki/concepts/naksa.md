# Naksa

The **Naksa** — Arabic for "setback" — is the name given to the displacement of Palestinians during and after the 1967 [[six-day-war|Six Day War]].[1] Badlands Media describes it as the most egregious example of Israel's practice of instructing Palestinian civilians to "temporarily" evacuate their homes so that counter-terrorism operations can be conducted.[1]

## Account

According to the Badlands account, the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] warned residents of the [[west-bank|West Bank]] that it intended to conduct massive bombing campaigns and that all villages near the Israeli border needed to empty out.[1] Palestinians left expecting to return within days; instead the IDF erected fences and declared the villages part of Israel, and the promised bombings never occurred.[1] More than 300,000 Palestinians were displaced by this deception and never returned to their homes, while Israel was praised by Western oligarchs for expanding its borders with limited bloodshed.[1]

## Significance

Badlands notes that when Saudi officials demand that the borders under the [[two-state-solution|Two-State Solution]] be returned to pre-1967 conditions, the Naksa displacement is what they are referring to.[1] The episode is cited as a direct precedent for Foreign Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]]'s August 2024 call for a "Gaza-style temporary evacuation" of Palestinians in the northern West Bank.[1]
