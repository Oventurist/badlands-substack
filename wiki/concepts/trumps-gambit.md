# Trump's Gambit

"Trump's Gambit" is an analytical framing used by Badlands Media commentators to describe a set of converging geopolitical pressures on Israeli prime minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] in the closing weeks of the 2024 US election cycle.[1]

## The pincer

As described by the Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, the gambit operates as a "pincer move." On one flank, the Arab states Netanyahu cites as prospective normalization partners were said to be demanding a Palestinian state — a fully ratified [[two-state-solution|two-state solution]] — before any further normalization under the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] would resume.[1] On the other flank, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was described as demanding that Netanyahu end his war before Trump returned to office.[1]

A third pressure point was added when reporting suggested [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] might be brought in as a broker of an Israel–[[hezbollah]] ceasefire, a role that would give Moscow influence over the arrangement at a time when roughly a fifth of Israel's Jewish population is of Russian or Ukrainian origin.[1] Commentary noted that the draft ceasefire was being backed by US presidential advisers [[amos-hochstein|Amos Hochstein]] and [[brett-mcgurk|Brett McGurk]], and treated Hochstein's involvement as an irony given accusations that he had been advancing an escalatory agenda.[1]

## Netanyahu's constraint

The framing holds that Netanyahu is caught in a vice between the religious factions in his coalition — on whom his political survival depends and who are described as unwilling to accept any settlement that does not include annexation of claimed biblical lands — and the external actors positioning themselves in a multipolar order.[1] Netanyahu's statement to the Knesset that Israel would pursue peace agreements with Arab countries once its military objectives in Gaza and Lebanon were achieved was read in this light as evidence of pressure rather than intent, possibly "hollow rhetoric" or a feint.[1]
