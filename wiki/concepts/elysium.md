# Elysium

*Elysium* is the 2013 science fiction film starring Matt Damon, discussed at length by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in his January 24, 2023 Badlands Media essay "The World is a Vampire" as an example of [[predictive-programming|predictive programming]].[1] Carlson reports having rewatched the film the week before writing, and found it not only full of predictive programming but also unintentionally comic in that a bald Matt Damon closely resembles [[sam-brinton|Sam Brinton]], the luggage-theft figure whom [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had placed in charge of nuclear waste disposal.[1]

## Plot as Carlson describes it

Carlson summarises the film as set in a future in which the Earth is an environmental disaster destroyed by human over-population, marked by crime and squalor, with the population policed by AI military combat robots.[1] The elite have escaped Earth to a space station built in the shape of a five-pointed star, where the rich enjoy luxuries including healing machines.[1]

## The reading

Carlson notes that the inhabitants of the space station are 99% White while the Earth depicted is predominantly Hispanic.[1] The protagonists must reach the station in order to heal a dying Hispanic girl, and the film refers to these rebel figures as "illegals," while the authorities charged with keeping them off the station stand in for the Department of Homeland Security.[1] He observes that the film was released roughly four years before what he calls the border fiasco, and asks rhetorically whether the messaging could have been made any more obvious.[1]
