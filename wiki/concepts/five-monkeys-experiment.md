# Five Monkeys Experiment

The **Five Monkeys Experiment** is a well-known allegory on conditioned behavior, used in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay to illustrate the mechanics of [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]].[1]

In the version recounted by the essay, a group of monkeys is placed in a cage with a banana hanging from the ceiling; when one climbs the ladder to reach it, all the monkeys are sprayed with ice-cold water. Over time they stop trying — not because the banana is unattainable, but because of the punishment that follows.[1] The monkeys are then replaced one by one. Each newcomer, unaware of the original punishment, instinctively tries to climb the ladder and is attacked by the others. Eventually none of the monkeys has ever been sprayed, yet all enforce the rule, and no one questions why.[1]

The essay presents this as an exact analogue of how [[intergenerational-trauma|generational trauma]] operates: fear, limitations, and outdated beliefs are passed down not because they remain relevant but because "that's how it's always been," whether expressed as poverty mindsets, emotional repression, or cultural suppression, with the cycle repeating until someone dares to climb the ladder.[1]
