# Mystery Schools

Mystery schools are the initiatory religious institutions of the ancient Mediterranean world, invoked in the February 24, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "The Steering Wheel of Civilization" as one of the mechanisms by which [[roman-empire|Rome]] maintained its collective identity.[1]

The essay states that for the Romans, mystery schools and the metropolitan lifestyle of a well-functioning civilization together helped foster a Roman identity, linking to the Metropolitan Museum of Art's reference material on the subject.[1] This identity is presented as the cultural reinforcement mechanism accompanying Rome's use of conquest, violence and slavery, ensuring that the different layers of Roman society each played their part.[1] Within the essay's broader argument, such institutions are examples of how a socio-religious foundation supplies spiritual justification for the methods a tyranny-leaning civilization uses to maintain [[common-purpose|common purpose]].[1]
