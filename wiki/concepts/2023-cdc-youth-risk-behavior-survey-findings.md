# 2023 CDC Youth Risk Behavior Survey Findings

In February 2023 the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] released survey data showing sharply deteriorating mental health among American high school students, and in particular among girls.[1]

## Findings

A staggering 30 percent of high school girls in the United States surveyed by the CDC said they had "seriously considered attempting suicide" in 2021, up from a still-shocking 19 percent in 2011.[1] Almost 60 percent of high school girls surveyed reported persistent feelings of sadness or hopelessness in 2021 — reported by the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] as 57 percent, compared with 36 percent in 2011.[1] The CDC said that while both girls and boys reported mental-health challenges, girls reported record high levels of violence, sadness and suicide risk.[1] Among high school boys, 29 percent reported persistent feelings of sadness and hopelessness in 2021 against 21 percent in 2011, while 14 percent reported seriously considering a suicide attempt, up from 13 percent in 2011.[1]
