# The Post-American World

*The Post-American World* is a book by [[fareed-zakaria|Fareed Zakaria]] that features in Q Drop #117 as the answer to Q's question about what book [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] was "caught reading."[1]

## Significance in the Q drops

Badlands Media's "Q for Dummies" series describes the book as being about what many consider to be the fall of America.[1] The series treats the photograph of Obama holding the book as a deliberate signal rather than an incidental image, asking why an American president would be reading a work about American decline "much less putting that fact on full display."[1]

The book is discussed in the same passage of Drop #117 that covers Obama's mentors and formative influences — [[saul-alinsky|Saul Alinsky]], [[jeremiah-wright|Jeremiah Wright]], and [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]] — figures the series characterizes as unsympathetic to America and its way of life, and alongside the claim that [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]] financed Obama's Harvard education.[1] Zakaria's own career associations with major mainstream outlets are cited as further context.[1]
