# Jingle Bells Racism Controversy

The Jingle Bells racism controversy refers to a December 2025 social media dispute over claims that the Christmas song "Jingle Bells" originated in blackface minstrelsy.[1]

## The viral video

Former MSNBC host [[joy-reid|Joy Reid]] shared a viral video on Instagram claiming that the song is rooted in racism and was written "as a mockery of Black people."[1] In the video, a man expressed disapproval of a plaque in Medford, Massachusetts, honoring the site where James Lord Pierpont is believed to have written the song in 1850; the caption read, "This is where a racist Confederate soldier wrote 'Jingle Bells' to make fun of Black people," and asserted that the plaque "ignores its origins in blackface minstrelsy."[1]

## Claims made

The video argued that Pierpont's original title, "The One Horse Open Sleigh," was written with the intended purpose of being used in racist performances in which white actors donned blackface to mock Black people attempting to engage in winter activities.[1] It further claimed that the "laughing all the way" theme of the original lyrics likely referenced a racist comedic routine known as the "Laughing Darkie."[1] The item was carried in the Badlands News Brief as a bonus story sourced to One America News.[1]
