# Bad Actors

"Bad Actors," subtitled "What Makes a Good Movie?", is an article published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on June 29, 2024, written by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]. It is a continuation of the author's "we are watching a movie" series and argues that a network of what the author calls "bad actors" — politicians installed and financed by hidden "puppet masters" — carried out a long-term plan to destroy the United States, and that the plan collapsed when [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] won the 2016 election.

## The movie framing and the "sixteen year plan"

The article opens with the observation that no matter how good a movie script is, bad actors will ruin the film. According to the source, the party most disappointed by the current state of affairs is "the cabal of elite puppet masters," who are described as being in disbelief at how quickly they are losing control. The script they were working from is identified as the [[sixteen-year-plan|sixteen year plan]], a scheme said to have been designed to destroy America and the [[united-states-constitution|Constitution]] across the presidencies of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and an anticipated [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] administration.

A central component of that plan, the article argues, was the initiation of a Third World War between the United States and [[russia|Russia]], intended to cull a large portion of the world population and to eliminate what the author identifies as the two greatest threats to elite control: Russia and the U.S. Constitution.

## War, debt, and the Great Reset

The source connects the war plan to the financial system, asserting that the worldwide debt system based on [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Notes and [[fiat-currency|fiat money]] is going bankrupt and that the cabal requires a new "[[great-reset|Great Reset]]" to replace it. War, in this reading, functions both as a distraction for the public and as the pretext for a new global monetary settlement, a pattern the article claims was followed in both world wars. It argues that this time the war will not come: Trump is described as purposely bankrupting the existing system while the [[brics|BRICS+]] nations build a decentralised replacement that protects national sovereignty and is backed by gold or other commodities.

## 2016, the military, and the 2020 election

The article states that the cabal's plans were destroyed when Trump won in 2016 and took away their control of "the most powerful position in the world." Citing Q drop #199 of November 22, 2017, and Q drop #36 of November 2, 2017 (see [[q|Q]]), it claims Hillary Clinton was never supposed to lose, that the election had already been rigged, and that the fraud was insufficient because the public's dislike of Clinton and support for Trump were underestimated — which the author offers as the explanation for Clinton never delivering a concession speech.

The source further claims that Obama intended to prevent Trump from taking office and that the military stopped him, having prepared "the UNTHINKABLE" — a planned military coup to remove the Obama administration and corrupt politicians — a plan the article says was deliberately leaked to Obama and his officials as a warning, causing them to back down.

The author then raises the question of why the same military did not intervene in 2020, and answers that the theft was permitted deliberately: [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would be installed as "a millstone around the democrats' neck" and used to expose corruption across every system, including elections and justice. The article references the author's earlier piece "The Chosen One," which argued that Trump helped select Biden as the Democratic nominee, and says the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|stolen election]] ignited a public awakening, part of the broader Great Awakening. It insists that Biden's Russia rhetoric over Ukraine is "a narrative war" rather than the sixteen year plan's intended war, because a war over Ukraine cannot be sold to the American people in the way a bombing blamed on Russia could have been.

## The Clinton Foundation and the $221 million payment

Turning to the question of who controlled the sixteen year plan, the article points to the largest contributor to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], invoking Q drop #10 of October 29, 2017. It links both Clinton and Obama to the [[uranium-one|Uranium One]] deal and the Iran nuclear deal, describing both as puppets of the same person.

The article's central documentary claim concerns a last-minute foreign payment. Citing The Hill and the Associated Press, it notes that the Obama administration sent $221 million to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] on the Friday as Obama prepared to leave the White House, with [[congress|Congress]] formally notified just hours before Trump's inauguration. Quoting Newsweek, the article describes the Palestinian Authority's "pay to slay" program — see [[pay-to-slay]] — under which a 2004 law guaranteed salaries for convicted terrorists and their families, 2010 resolutions stipulated monthly payments to Palestinian inmates convicted of terrorism-related offenses in Israeli prisons, and a 2013 amendment by then-prime minister [[rami-hamdallah|Rami Hamdallah]] added benefits on release, including guaranteed positions in the PA for men imprisoned ten or more years and women imprisoned five.

Citing The Independent, the article reports that the Trump administration froze the package, that Hamdallah was told not to expect the $220 million, and that Obama had overruled Congress to make the payment, which was officially intended for good governance and humanitarian aid. The same report records that the House was informed by Secretary of State [[john-kerry|John Kerry]] hours before the inauguration and that acting [[state-department|State Department]] spokesperson Mark Toner said Kerry had instructed [[usaid|USAID]] to release $220.3 million for Gaza recovery programs.

## USAID, the State Department, and the CIA

The author asserts that USAID "is just a CIA front group and always has been," a taxpayer slush fund for the [[cia|CIA]] since its inception, a claim developed in his earlier article "The Difference Between Friend And Foe." He notes that Obama's final budget proposal in 2016 designated almost $33 billion to the State Department and USAID together, and argues that because both are controlled by the CIA, the agency received by far the most foreign aid money — one of the mechanisms by which, in his account, the United States funds its enemies.

## Alwaleed bin Talal and Khalid al-Mansour

The article identifies [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] as the puppet master in question, asserting that when Trump became president Alwaleed controlled the CIA and was therefore Trump's biggest threat. Q drop #199 is read as connecting Obama's Harvard education to Clinton Foundation contributions as obligations to the same person, and Q drop #1948 of August 28, 2018 is quoted as asking: "Who paid _Hussein's Harvard Law Degree? Who financed _Hussein's political life? Who is Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal? Why are Hussein's school records under seal?"

To develop the connection the article recounts the 2008 Politico story in which former Manhattan Borough President [[percy-sutton|Percy Sutton]] told NY1's Dominic Carter that he had been asked to write a Harvard Law School recommendation letter for Obama by [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]] of Texas, described as "the principle adviser to one of the world's richest men" and as raising money for Obama. Obama spokesman Ben LaBolt flatly denied the account, saying Obama "did not know and does not know Khalid al-Mansour," that no such letter was written to their knowledge, and that Obama was in Chicago when he applied. Politico also noted Obama attended Harvard with student loans, reporting $42,753 in debt, and quoted al-Mansour saying "I've never met him."

The article treats these denials as "well-scripted LIES," argues there are no records of Obama taking student loans, and presents the sealing of Obama's records as evidence of concealment. It suggests the Politico piece was written to help bury the connection because exposure could have ended Obama's presidential run and derailed the sixteen year plan.

Drawing on two Newsmax articles, the article traces al-Mansour's rise from a 1977 introduction to the Saudi king in Riyadh, arranged by a "Saudi friend" who was a student in Newport, California; it identifies that friend as Alwaleed, who was studying at Menlo College in the late 1970s and took a business administration degree there in 1979, and whose California court case was handled by al-Mansour's law partner. Newsmax described Obama as closely associated by age 25 with "a key adviser to a Saudi billionaire who had mentored the founding members of the Black Panthers," and quoted Sutton, founder of Inner City Broadcasting, describing al-Mansour's request that he write in support of "a young man that has applied to Harvard."

The article emphasises that Sutton did not say why al-Mansour was helping Obama or on whose behalf he was raising money, and answers that it was obviously Alwaleed. It portrays Obama as groomed: a Columbia University graduate and Chicago community organizer who, per a New York Times description quoted by Newsmax, arrived at Harvard an unknown and left with national media exposure, a book contract, and the editorship of the Harvard Law Review, graduating magna cum laude. The author speculates that al-Mansour — also known as Donald Warden and the author of numerous books the article characterises as anti-American and racially divisive — may have written Obama's book and helped run the Law Review on his behalf.

## Competing factions and the "infiltration" thesis

Quoting a further Q drop, the article presents rival "competing factions [each with a horse in the race] seeking direct WH control [+USMIL][+Trillions _overseas transfer of US taxpayer FUNDS]," with [[george-soros|George Soros]] backing Clinton as early as 2008 while Alwaleed groomed Obama, and asserts Alwaleed was the more powerful of the two because he controlled the CIA. The drop's phrase "[infiltration not invasion][VJ _HA]" is read as identifying [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] as the handlers of Obama and Clinton respectively, with their connections to Alwaleed promised for a later article. The author summarises the thesis as an insurgency infiltration within the U.S. government rather than an invasion, in which White House control equals control of the U.S. military plus trillions in overseas transfers of taxpayer funds under the guise of aid, military assistance, and climate programs.

## Iraq, the missing billions, and the Lebanon bunker

The final section extends the argument to [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]]. Quoting The Boston Globe, the article describes how after the 2003 defeat of Saddam Hussein's government, trucks delivered pallets of shrink-wrapped $100 bills to Andrews Air Force Base for airlift aboard C-17s to Baghdad; over eighteen months $12–14 billion went by airlift and a further $5 billion by electronic transfer. The author asks why cash was used at all, answering that cash is harder to trace.

[[stuart-w-bowen-jr|Stuart W. Bowen Jr.]], a Texas friend of Bush appointed in 2004 as special inspector general for Iraq reconstruction, is credited with pursuing the money seriously despite, in the author's view, having been chosen so the story would go away amid congressional investigations of waste and fraud. Bowen's investigators eventually determined that $1.2–1.6 billion had been stolen and moved to a bunker in rural [[lebanon|Lebanon]] for safekeeping; Bowen said "I don't know how the money got to Lebanon." The article stresses that Lebanon is the home base of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] and the place where Alwaleed was raised as a young child, and asks whether these are coincidences.

Bowen's investigation was code-named [[brick-tracker|Brick Tracker]] and kept secret. He told the Globe that "billions of dollars have been taken out of Iraq over the last 10 years illegally" and that the case could not be closed "for reasons beyond our control." He expressed frustration that neither the Bush administration nor, after the bunker was found, the Obama administration pursued the lead, despite briefings to the CIA and the [[fbi|FBI]]; he believed one reason officials declined was "because it was Iraqi money stolen by Iraqis." The article treats that formulation as a deliberately manufactured narrative and argues that either American taxpayers or the Iraqi people, or both, were robbed.

Citing ShadowProof.com's account of James Risen's reporting, the article notes that American proconsul [[paul-bremer|Paul Bremer]], "on his own," ordered the airlift, with some estimates of the total as high as $20 billion, and quotes the head of the [[treasury-department|Treasury Department]] team on Iraq's financial reconstruction saying "We did not know that Bremer was flying in all that cash… I can't see a reason for it." ShadowProof also reported that the Iraqi government never tried to retrieve the money and kept the Lebanese bunker secret; that Bowen met resistance from the U.S. embassy in Beirut, was not allowed to travel to Lebanon, and that two investigators who did travel were denied permission to see the bunker; and that Lebanon's prosecutor general initially agreed to cooperate before reversing. By 2011 the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and the Iraqi government claimed to have accounted for all but $6 billion. House Government Reform Committee investigators charged in 2005 that U.S. officials "used virtually no financial controls to account for these enormous cash withdrawals."

## Conclusion and forthcoming articles

The article concludes that by controlling presidents Alwaleed controlled the government, the military, and the worldwide financial debt system, and that Trump "took out Alwaleed first," referencing a Q drop posted four days before Alwaleed's arrest in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] alongside hundreds of allies. Obama, Clinton, and Bush are named as three of the biggest bad actors, and a fourth is introduced: [[john-mccain|John McCain]], whom Q designated "No-name" and whom the article calls one of the biggest traitors in the war, alleging he made a secret trip to a foreign country as part of the sixteen year plan. The author promises follow-up articles on [[libya|Libya]], "[[the-sum-of-all-fears|The Sum of All Fears]]," and the Clinton Foundation, closing with the assertion that "the flow of information is vital in a narrative war."

The piece carries the standard Badlands Media disclaimer that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.
