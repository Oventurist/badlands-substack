# Badlands Brief

The Badlands Brief is the daily weekday output of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on Substack. It is described in the January 10, 2026 announcement of [[badlands-blitz-episode-1|the debut episode]] of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] as "the work we do Monday through Friday," and it supplies the raw material that The Blitz recaps and dissects each Saturday.

## Role within Badlands Media

The Brief functions as the network's running coverage of the news cycle, tracking developments the organization frames as part of an ongoing information war. Its weekday cadence is the reference point against which The Blitz was designed: the weekly show exists to condense and analyze five days of Brief coverage in a single 60- to 70-minute installment. The source states that all Badlands content, the Brief included, remains free to readers, with paid subscriptions serving to compensate contributors for their time.

The Brief's comment section is also treated as part of the product, with subscriber responses collected over the week and read on air during The Blitz.

The Brief's Monday-through-Friday output continued to supply the raw material for the weekly show through 2026; the announcement for [[badlands-blitz-episode-10|Episode 10]] repeats the same description of the Blitz as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the Badlands Brief."

## Continuity into 2026

The March 21, 2026 announcement for [[badlands-blitz-episode-11|Badlands Blitz Episode 11]] restated the Brief's weekday cadence — "the work we do Monday through Friday in the Badlands Brief" — and referred to a "step-up in said work for 2026," indicating an expansion of the daily output that the Blitz recaps.
