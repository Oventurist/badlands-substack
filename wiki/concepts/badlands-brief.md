# Badlands Brief

The **Badlands Brief** is the daily newsletter published by the Badlands Substack team, appearing Monday through Friday.[1] Its weekday output forms the raw material for [[badlands-blitz|The Blitz]], the outlet's weekly recap and dissection program.[1]

## Editorial output

The Brief is produced on a five-day-a-week schedule by a team of contributors, and its readers are addressed collectively as "Badlanders".[1] Recurring subject matter referenced in the Blitz recaps includes the continuing Epstein saga and the [[election-reform-campaign|Election Reform Campaign]].[1] The publication described a deliberate "step-up" in the scale of its work for 2026.[1]

## Access and support

All Badlands content, including the Brief and The Blitz, is free to read.[1] Paid subscriptions are offered as a purely voluntary form of support, and the team states that reader contributions are the only mechanism by which it is able to compensate its people for their time.[1] Subscriptions may be cancelled at any time.[1]

## June 8, 2026 edition

The June 8, 2026 Brief, titled "The Bibi Break-Up Begins, As Does The IC Purge...", covered the open rupture between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] over the [[iran|Iran]] strikes, Trump's instruction to acting DNI [[william-pulte|Bill Pulte]] to cut staff at the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|ODNI]], [[ed-martin|Ed Martin]]'s prediction of the bar associations' collapse, the reported [[defense-intelligence-agency|DIA]] assessment raising Israeli espionage to "critical," and Trump's walk-out on [[kristen-welker|Kristen Welker]] over fake elections.[2] Contributors to the edition were [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].[2]

The Brief describes itself as "your daily drop of Badlands' takes on the narratives dominating the info war," inviting readers into the comments section, and carries a standing disclaimer that the opinions expressed are those of the tagged authors and do not necessarily reflect the positions of Badlands Media.[2] Editions are free, supported by paid subscriptions and sponsor placements, and typically close with a bonus item — in this case [[badlands-blitz|Badlands Blitz]] Episode 20.[2]

The April 7, 2026 edition, titled "American Discombobulation," followed the standard structure: summarized news items on the [[2026-fraud-investigations|fraud investigation wave]], the [[iran-war|US-Iran war]], and the Supreme Court's action in the [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] contempt case, each followed by commentary from a tagged Badlands author, plus a bonus item on the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]]'s Brazil censorship report and a short piece on the [[national-capital-planning-commission|National Capital Planning Commission]]'s approval of the [[white-house-ballroom|White House ballroom]].[3] The edition carried sponsored placements and promotion for the [[gart-nashville|GART]] event alongside the standing disclaimer and subscription appeal.[3]


## June 10, 2026 edition

The June 10, 2026 edition, "Peaceful Retaliations, Futuristic Fusions and Based Puritans," covered [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s claim that Middle East negotiations were in their "final throes," China's [[china-ai-data-center-buildout|AI data center buildout]], the Department of Energy's [[fusion-science-and-technology-roadmap|Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap]], the [[red-mirage|Red Mirage]] framing of California's mail-ballot counting, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]'s role at the [[2026-st-petersburg-international-economic-forum|St. Petersburg International Economic Forum]], and a [[gallup|Gallup]] poll showing declining moral acceptance of several personal-liberty issues.[4] Commentary came from [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].[4]

## May 4, 2026 edition

The May 4, 2026 edition, "Cartel & Comey Indictments Plus War Ripples & Election Fraud," opened with a survey of federal and state election cases across New Jersey, Ohio, New Mexico, Pennsylvania, Washington, Georgia, Michigan, Wyoming and California, followed by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]'s argument that only the [[election-fraud-rico|election fraud RICO]] ultimately matters.[5] Subsequent segments covered the [[suicide-dolphins-narrative|"suicide dolphins" media narrative]], the [[iran-war|Iran war]]'s second-order pressure on [[germany|Germany]] and on U.S. fuel prices, the resignations of Mexican officials amid the [[indictment-of-mexican-officials-in-the-sinaloa-cartel-case|Sinaloa cartel indictments]] and the deaths of two [[cia|CIA]] officers in Chihuahua, and Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]]'s comments on the [[comey-8647-indictment|Comey 8647 indictment]].[5] Commentary came from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]].[5] Bonus items included [[the-blitz|Badlands Blitz]] Episode 15, the [[federal-organ-donation-review-2026|federal organ donation review]], and a Weather Channel clip of iridescent clouds in Indonesia, alongside sponsored placements for MeWe and the Great American Restoration Tour in Deadwood.[5]
