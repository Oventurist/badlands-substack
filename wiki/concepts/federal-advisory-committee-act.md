# Federal Advisory Committee Act

The Federal Advisory Committee Act (FACA) is the United States statute governing the establishment, operation, and termination of advisory committees within the executive branch. It imposes structural and procedural requirements intended to ensure that outside advice to the federal government is provided transparently, is balanced in point of view, and results in formal, documented recommendations rather than informal or hidden influence.

FACA is cited in the March 26, 2026 Badlands Brief as the framework under which the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]] operates, meaning the council's advisory work on artificial intelligence, national security technology, and research priorities is channeled into formal recommendations to the president.
