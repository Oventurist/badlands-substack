# Standing and Jurisdiction

Standing and jurisdiction are the threshold requirements a case must satisfy before a court will hear evidence or testimony.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) Badlands Media treats these doctrines as central to understanding why litigation over the 2020 election never reached the merits.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in)

## Cause and elements

Every court case begins with a cause — the illegal or unlawful act alleged to have occurred.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) What matters is whether the prosecutor or plaintiff can present the proper court with a sufficient amount and type of admissible evidence to prove that cause.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) A cause must also satisfy its elements: in a defamation action, for instance, the plaintiff must show an intentional lie, malice, and resulting damages.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) A false statement made in sincere belief is not defamation.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in)

## The three jurisdictions

Jurisdiction divides into three components — territorial, personal, and subject matter.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) A court must confirm that the acts occurred within its territory (a dispute between Ohio neighbors is not heard in Tennessee), that the actor is an entity over whom the court has jurisdiction under the doctrine of legal personality, and that the acts are cognizable by that court (one does not bring a trillion-dollar suit to small claims).1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in)

## Consequence for the election cases

If these elements are absent, a case never reaches trial or hearing; without standing, a court cannot rule on the evidence.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) Fifty of the 55 cases filed over the 2020 election were dismissed on standing, meaning a large body of [[2020-election-fraud-claims|alleged evidence]] was never heard.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) By contrast, the prosecutions of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] and [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] are underway, placing Trump in a position to introduce that evidence as a defendant.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in)
