# Trump Train

The **Trump Train** is a slogan and meme used by supporters of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the 2016 presidential campaign onward, popularized in social media by campaign aide [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]], who repeatedly posted "CHOO CHOO" style messages tied to rallies and vehicle convoys.[1] In Badlands Media commentary the phrase is treated as far more than a rally chant: the article "Pepe, the Trump Train, and Hillary's Automobile," written by Patriots in Progress for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], frames the Trump Train as a navigational device — a set of dated posts whose timestamps allegedly unlock connections among [[qanon|Q]], [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], [[covfefe|Covfefe]], and [[pepe-the-frog|Pepe the Frog]].[1]

## Framing device

The article is written as an in-character tour of a passenger train, addressing the reader as a VIP boarding a cabin stocked with popcorn, blankets, "hot Covfefe," and oranges, and closing with the Kekistani salutation "Shadilay."[1] Readers are pointed to a list of "historical downloads" comprising earlier Badlands digs, including the COVFEFE Dig, the COVFEFE 2.0 Dig, "Celebrating 15 Years of Indictments and Justice!," "Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space," and "Kekistani Code Talkers."[1]

## Scavino posts as data points

The author states that a survey of Scavino's account for mentions of the Trump Train yields several dozen data points.[1] Among those highlighted:

- A Truth Social post dated 3/15/24 that references a Tweet from eight years earlier on 3/15/16, said to connect the Trump Train to Covfefe.[1]
- One of the earliest Trump Train Tweets, dated 3/21/16, declaring "BTC for the Trump Train! CHOO CHOO!" — read by the author as an early Bitcoin signal, with the 21 tied to Bitcoin's 21 million supply cap.[1]
- An early Tweet of 3/11/16 referencing Periscope, the Twitter-based live-video service. The author quotes a dictionary definition of a periscope as an optical instrument for viewing objects above the level of direct sight, and asks whether Scavino was indicating that the Trump Train could be used to gain a "high altitude, big picture view (RARE)" from within the fog of [[fifth-generation-warfare|5th Generation Warfare]], or was pointing toward another cryptographic key like Covfefe.[1] The 3/11 date is further read as 11.3, tied to Q [[qanon|drop]] #519 ("SEC TEST") and the 1/13/18 minting of the TEST token on [[counterparty|Counterparty]].[1]
- A post of 10/31/20, dated eleven years after the release of the Bitcoin whitepaper by [[satoshi-nakamoto|Satoshi Nakamoto]] on 10/31/2009 — described as "a great 11-year delta."[1]
- A 2020 post in which Scavino marveled at the length of the Trump Train as it passed through Laredo, Texas, where the convoy reportedly stretched more than fifteen miles along IH-35 with roughly 7,000 participants, per [[breitbart|Breitbart]].[1] The author notes that Laredo is nicknamed "The Gateway City" and likens riding the Trump Train to "entering a portal to a new reality."[1]
- A post of 5/26/24 referencing a Tweet from eight years earlier, matched by timestamp to Q drop #3521, which links a Devin Nunes Tweet and states "farms produce corn" — connected to Bitcoin's nickname "corn" — and ends with "5:5."[1]

## Oranges and the memetic freight

Scavino's posts of 5/13/24 carrying orange, rocket-ship, and freedom symbols are presented as the article's opening evidence, with the 5/13 date read against the Covfefe signal of digits 1, 3, and 5 and orange read as a stand-in for Bitcoin.[1] The author notes the posts appeared on X at 10:37 PM and on Truth Social at 10:38 PM, and maps those times onto Q drops #1037 and #1038, observing that Q re-posted #1037 within #1038 and that #1037 states "BOOMs en route."[1] BOOMs, the article says, map back to both 3/15 and [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]].[1] A further Scavino post dated 3/15/23 at 12:53 AM highlights a speech Trump delivered at Jackson's Hermitage in Tennessee on 3/15/17, marking Jackson's 3/15/1767 birthday, in which at 4:40 Trump says "BOOM" while referring to the Jackson portrait he hung in the White House.[1]

## Connection to Pepe

The Pepe link is made through a Scavino Tweet of 10/9/16 that mentions the Trump Train, quotes Trump's response to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s [[basket-of-deplorables|"basket of deplorables"]] remark, and declares "WE LOVE OUR DEPLORABLES!!!"[1] The author supports this with a timeline running from Trump's 10/13/15 Tweet depicting himself as Pepe, through the 9/9/16 deplorables remark and the same-day minting of the [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]] token, [[donald-trump-jr|Don Jr]]'s 9/10/16 "The Deplorables" meme, Clinton's apparent fainting episode on 9/11/16, and her campaign website's 9/12/16 explainer attacking Pepe.[1] A closing "dasting dig" notes that RAREPEPE was minted exactly thirty days before the 10/9/16 Scavino Tweet, and compares "[30 days]" against Q drop #36 of 11/2/17 — read as 11 11, and thus as a Covfefe connection because 11/11 is the 315th day of a non-leap year.[1]

## Interpretive claim

The article concludes that the Trump Train "can be used to unlock important details about the root cause and solution to our current tyranny," having in its telling connected nearly all the major elements — Q, Bitcoin, Covfefe, and Pepe.[1] It ends with a series of open questions about whether Scavino was pointing toward COIN, "Digital 1776," and [[softwar|Softwar]]; why Q never mentioned Bitcoin or the color orange; and whether Trump called Bitcoin a scam in order to protect a winning strategy.[1] The final speculation asks whether the military acted on 1/3/09 in the domain of cyberspace by introducing a decentralized network, with the rest of the world only slowly catching up.[1] A PEPETRAIN token on Counterparty is offered as the last data point, its series and supply numbers said to contain the Covfefe signal and its card number, #47, read as pointing to 11/5/24.[1]
