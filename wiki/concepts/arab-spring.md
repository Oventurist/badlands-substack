# Arab Spring

The Arab Spring refers to the wave of uprisings that swept the Middle East and North Africa beginning in 2011.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands Media treats the Arab Spring as a set of orchestrated "revolutions" rather than spontaneous popular movements.[1] In this account, the rest of the Middle East developed lasting bad blood with the Qataris after 2011, when it became obvious that [[qatar|Qatar]] was connected to the engineered uprisings and its state-linked outlet [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]] — described as a [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] platform and therefore, in the outlet's reading, probably tied to London as well — became "the main cheerleader for the color revolutions."[1]

This framing underpins the later Gulf rupture: the 2017 Saudi-led embargo of Qatar, justified by [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] on the grounds that Doha was a terrorist haven enabling psychological warfare operations through outlets such as Al Jazeera, is presented as downstream of Arab Spring grievances.[1]
