# US-Australia Critical Minerals Agreement

The US–Australia critical minerals agreement is a bilateral accord signed at the White House on October 20, 2025 by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Australian Prime Minister [[anthony-albanese|Anthony Albanese]], aimed at countering China's dominance of critical mineral supply chains.[1]

Trump spoke to reporters at the start of the meeting, where he also pledged to accelerate deliveries of nuclear submarines to Australia.[1] Asked whether U.S. actions in Indo-Pacific waters were a sufficient deterrent to keep Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] from invading [[taiwan|Taiwan]], Trump replied, "China doesn't want to do that," before praising the size and strength of the U.S. military.[1]

The agreement was signed days before a planned Trump–Xi meeting on the sidelines of an economic conference in South Korea, at which Trump said he expected to reach a fair trade deal even as his top trade negotiator accused Beijing of "economic coercion."[1]
