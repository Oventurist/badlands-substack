# Arab Alternative to Trump's Gaza Plan

The Arab alternative to Trump's Gaza plan refers to a February 2025 diplomatic effort, spearheaded by [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], to develop an Arab-authored plan for Gaza's future as a counter to [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s ambition for a "Middle East Riviera" cleared of its Palestinian inhabitants.[1]

## The initiative

According to ten sources cited by Reuters, draft ideas were to be discussed at a meeting in Riyadh that month involving Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Jordan and the United Arab Emirates.[1] Five of those sources said proposals may involve a Gulf-led reconstruction fund and a deal to sideline [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

Saudi Arabia and its Arab allies were reported to be aghast at Trump's plan to "clean out" Palestinians from Gaza and resettle most of them in Jordan and Egypt — an idea immediately rejected by Cairo and Amman and seen in most of the region as deeply destabilising.[1] Saudi dismay was aggravated because the plan would nullify the kingdom's demand for a clear path to Palestinian statehood as a condition for normalising ties with Israel, which in turn would have paved the way for an ambitious military pact between Riyadh and Washington shoring up the kingdom's defences against Iran.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who discussed the topic on [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s show The Daily Herold, argued that Trump had used narrative warfare to bring the plan of [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]] to the surface and to provide the Arab states with an opportunity to assert their collective strength and demonstrate that they are capable of managing the affairs of the Middle East without western intervention.[1] He framed this as the [[multipolar-world|multipolar world]] emerging courtesy of the Sovereign Alliance, and as "the Death of Unipolar Globalism."[1]
