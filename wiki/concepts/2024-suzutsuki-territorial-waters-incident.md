# 2024 Suzutsuki Territorial Waters Incident

The Suzutsuki incident was a July 2024 encounter in which Chinese naval vessels fired at least two warning shots at a [[japan|Japanese]] Maritime Self-Defense Force destroyer after it entered [[china|Chinese]] territorial waters off Zhejiang Province in East China.[1]

## Disclosure

The event was revealed for the first time roughly a year later, when Japan's Kyodo news agency cited high-level diplomatic sources over the weekend of August 9–10, 2025.[1]

## Circumstances

The destroyer Suzutsuki was described as monitoring Chinese military exercises in international waters when the incident occurred.[1] Japanese government sources appeared to concede fault on the part of the Japanese vessel, explaining to Kyodo that the ship's electronic navigation system failed to display territorial boundaries.[1] The destroyer thus unknowingly crossed into Chinese territorial waters, coming within 12 nautical miles (about 22 kilometers) of China's coast for approximately 20 minutes on July 4.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the belated disclosure as a possible retroactive revelation of a 2024 false-flag attempt — quipping "Gulf of Taiwonkin?" — and argued that the episode went unreported at the time because it failed to produce the intended escalation.[1]
