# Turkish F-35 Sale Dispute

The **Turkish F-35 sale dispute** concerns American fifth-generation fighter aircraft that [[turkey|Turkey]] has paid for but has not received.[1] Turkish pilots and maintenance crews were trained for the aircraft, and the purchase price was paid, yet the planes have been withheld by Washington.[1]

## Grounds for the withholding

Badlands commentary attributes the hold to President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]]'s relationship with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and to concern that Russia would acquire access to the aircraft's classified technology.[1] Contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] disputed the priority of that concern, arguing that the United States should be more worried about [[israel|Israel]] stealing and reselling American classified technology, which he said Israel has an actual history of doing.[1]

## Trump's 2026 comments

Asked in the Oval Office in June 2026 whether he would follow through on his promise to sell the F-35s to Erdoğan — put to him in the context of the Turkish leader's attacks on Israel — President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] praised Erdoğan as a strong and effective negotiator and emphasized the importance of maintaining U.S.–Turkish relations.[1] Badlands analysis noted that Trump made clear he stands with Erdoğan and is not worried that Turkey would start a war with Israel, but did not address whether he was concerned that Israel might start a war with Turkey.[1] The exchange highlighted a contrast between Washington's posture and the increasingly hostile public exchanges between Ankara and Jerusalem.[1]
