# United States v. Wong Kim Ark

*United States v. Wong Kim Ark* is the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] decision holding that children born in the United States to non-citizen parents are citizens under the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1] It is the key precedent underpinning American [[birthright-citizenship|birthright citizenship]].[1]

## Relevance in 2026

The case became central to [[trump-v-barbara|Trump v. Barbara]], the challenge to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order narrowing birthright citizenship.[1] Legal analysts cited in reporting indicated that overturning or narrowing *Wong Kim Ark* would require the Court to reinterpret more than a century of constitutional law.[1] Lower federal courts relied on the precedent, together with the constitutional text, in blocking the executive order.[1]
