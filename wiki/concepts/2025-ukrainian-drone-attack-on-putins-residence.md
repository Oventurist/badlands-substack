# 2025 Ukrainian Drone Attack on Putin's Residence

The 2025 Ukrainian drone attack on the residence of Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] was an attempted long-range strike reported by Moscow on Monday, December 29, 2025, against a presidential residence located in Russia's Novgorod Region.[1] Russian authorities said the residence came under a concentrated overnight attack involving a total of 91 kamikaze drones, all of which they claimed were intercepted.[1]

## Russian response

Top Russian officials condemned the attempted strike as a terrorist attack and vowed retaliation.[1] Moscow further signaled that the incident was bound to affect Russia's position in the ongoing negotiations to settle the [[ukraine|Ukraine]] conflict, framing the raid as an escalation timed to disrupt diplomacy.[1] Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergei Lavrov]] publicly commented on the attack.[1]

## American reaction

Speaking alongside Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] outside the [[white-house|White House]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] confirmed that he had learned of the attack directly from Putin during a telephone conversation earlier that day.[1] Trump said the incident made him "very angry," particularly because it came during what he called "a delicate period of time."[1] He added: "Early in the morning [Putin] said he was attacked. It's not good. Don't forget, you know, the Tomahawks. I stopped the Tomahawks. I didn't want that," referring to the US-made cruise missiles repeatedly requested by Kiev and withheld by his administration.[1]

The exchange between the two presidents was separately confirmed by Kremlin foreign policy aide Yury Ushakov, who said Trump was "shocked" by the attack on Putin's residence and remarked, "Thank God, we did not give the Tomahawks" to Kiev.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that, assuming the reported narrative was accurate, the episode marked the point at which Putin would "let loose the dogs of war" on the Kiev government and possibly its foreign backers, calling it "pathetic" that the war had been allowed to continue as long as it had.[1] He added that the world despises the American political class nearly as much as Americans do, and lamented that too many citizens remained "caught up in the fake television drama that is the US [[congress|Congress]]."[1]
