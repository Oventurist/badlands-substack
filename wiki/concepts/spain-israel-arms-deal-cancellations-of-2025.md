# Spain–Israel Arms Deal Cancellations of 2025

In September 2025, [[spain|Spain]] scrapped nearly €1 billion (about $1.18 billion) in defense contracts with Israeli firms, the strongest measure yet taken under Prime Minister [[pedro-sanchez|Pedro Sánchez]]'s newly announced package of steps against [[israel|Israel]] over its offensive in [[gaza|Gaza]].[1] The cancellations were reported by media citing government sources and marked the first major move by a [[nato|NATO]] member state to sever defense procurement ties with Israel over the war.[1]

## The contracts

The Spanish Defense Ministry reportedly canceled a €700 million ($826 million) deal for twelve SILAM rocket launchers and a €287.5 million ($339 million) contract for 168 Spike L.R. anti-tank missiles.[1] The SILAM systems, based on [[elbit-systems|Elbit]]'s PULS platform, were to have been built by a Spanish consortium, according to the EFE news agency.[1] The cancellations were expected to be finalized the following week, with Madrid reportedly preparing to disengage militarily and technologically from Israel while seeking alternative suppliers.[1]

## Political context

The step followed Sánchez's pledge the previous week to enshrine in law a ban on arms sales to and purchases from Israel over the Gaza offensive.[1] It came as Israel pressed an assault on Gaza City, which it described as [[hamas|Hamas]]' last major stronghold; the Israeli security cabinet had approved the plan the previous month, seeking to bring the city — one of the few areas not under IDF control — under full occupation.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that while some [[european-union|European Union]] members were calling for sanctions against Israel, others were cancelling arms deals the IDF was counting on to sustain its war machine.[1] He argued that it is very difficult to survive without friends, particularly when a state appears determined to pick fights with all of its neighbors, and predicted that Israel's self-destruction would be studied by scholars indefinitely.[1] The cancellations paralleled Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s own warning that Israel would need to prepare for isolation and adapt to an economy with autarkic features because its defense industries might be blocked.[1]
