# Thousand Talents Program

The Thousand Talents Program is a Chinese state talent-recruitment initiative that compensates foreign-based scientists for transferring expertise and research capacity to Chinese institutions.[1]

## The Lieber prosecution

The program featured centrally in the federal prosecution of [[charles-lieber|Charles Lieber]], the former Chair of Harvard's Chemistry and Chemical Biology Department. The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] stated in December 2021 that "there is now no question that Charles Lieber lied to federal investigators and to Harvard in an attempt to hide his participation in the Chinese Thousand Talents Program... He exploited the openness of our academic system."[1]

Through the arrangement Lieber received roughly $50,000 per month from the Wuhan University of Technology and over $1.5 million to establish a Chinese laboratory, while concurrently holding more than $15 million in U.S. federal research grants.[1] Badlands Media used the case to argue that the program functions as a mechanism of technology transfer in dual-use fields such as nanowire batteries and biosensors, with direct warfare and surveillance applications.[1]
