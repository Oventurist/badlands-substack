# Crisis Cascade

**Crisis Cascade** is an analytical term used by the Badlands Media contributor writing as [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to describe a period in which multiple engineered or narrative-driven crises converge and reinforce one another over a short span of time.[1] The writer applied the term to the political and economic environment surrounding the [[2022-midterm-elections|2022 U.S. midterm elections]], describing that cycle as "one of the most important election cycles in recent American history."[1]

## Components

In the October 2022 essay "Taiwan Tipping," the cascade was described as comprising raging consumer inflation, an engineered economic crash, and the escalating hot-and-cold energy war in the east between Russia and the proxy forces of NATO and the Western powers.[1] The re-emerging tension between [[china|China]] and [[taiwan|Taiwan]] was presented as an additional implied crisis resurfacing within the same window.[1]

## Interpretation

The author framed the cascade in dramatic terms, describing world events as a stage production in its "Second Act," a phase that features rising narrative tension en route to a series of escalating climaxes.[1] Unlike commentators inclined toward "fear-mongering, blackpilling and general dooming," the author argued that the cascade, though unpleasant for freedom-minded citizens to weather, would ultimately prove beneficial.[1] Two benefits were claimed: the opportunity to shake the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] and ordinary citizens out of surface-level acceptance of official programming, and the opportunity for newly installed state and national leaders to rebuild sovereign financial and international systems out of the ashes of the old.[1]

The concept is closely tied to the author's broader [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] framework, in which exposure to contradictions within an existing paradigm is treated as the mechanism that causes audiences to disengage from it.[1]
