# Cuauhtemoc Brooklyn Bridge Collision

On Saturday night, May 17, 2025, the Mexican Navy's 147-foot tall ship Cuauhtemoc struck the Brooklyn Bridge in New York City, killing two crew members and injuring at least 17 other people.[1]

## The incident

Footage captured the moment of impact, which left terrified crew members dangling and clinging to the rigging.[1] Sources said the 43-year-old vessel was en route to the Brooklyn waterfront to refuel before setting sail for Iceland and had plenty of diesel aboard, but appeared to have lost its steering ability because of a mechanical malfunction and was quickly carried into the bridge by powerful East River currents.[1] Retired U.S. Navy Captain Alfred McLaren told the New York Post that the scene aboard must have been chaotic: "There must have been a lot of frantic, 'Look out!' There had to be some pandemonium."[1]

## Comparison

Coverage described the crash as eerily reminiscent of the previous year's Baltimore bridge disaster.[1]
