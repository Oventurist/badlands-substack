# Cultural Hegemony

**Cultural hegemony** is the theory, associated with [[antonio-gramsci|Antonio Gramsci]], that dominant groups manipulate societal values in order to normalize their authority rather than relying on overt force.[1] It is one of three established frameworks — alongside [[intergenerational-trauma|intergenerational trauma]] and [[learned-helplessness|learned helplessness]] — that a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay draws upon to construct its concept of [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]].[1]

In that essay's reading, cultural hegemony explains how compliance becomes self-enforcing: narratives, educational structures, and workplace hierarchies frame restrictions on freedom as essential protections against chaos, so that obedience is experienced as reasonable and moral rather than coerced.[1] The related Gramscian idea of a "culture of consent" is used to account for the passivity and erosion of critical faculties the author attributes to populations conditioned across generations.[1] The essay also cites Gramsci's premise that every system of control begins with fragmentation, linking hegemony to the deliberate erosion of trust in local institutions under [[fourth-generation-warfare|Fourth-Generation Warfare]].[1]
