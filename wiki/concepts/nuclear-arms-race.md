# Nuclear Arms Race

The nuclear arms race is the Cold War competition in atomic weaponry that followed the [[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]], and the subject Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer* revisits.[1]

## The film's counterfactual

*Oppenheimer* embeds an alternate history in which Hiroshima and Nagasaki might have been spared and the arms race and subsequent Cold War prevented, had American authorities listened to the [[communism|communists]], shared information with the Soviets rather than keeping national secrets, and paused development so the ethics of the bomb could be debated.[1]

## As metaphor for AI

Badlands argues the primary metaphor the regime uses to drive [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] hysteria and push [[global-governance|global governance]] is the nuclear arms race, with slogans such as "AI is an arms race" and "nukes can't create more nukes, but AIs can create more AIs."[1] The threat of nuclear winter no longer carries the weight it did in the mid-1900s and is unlikely on its own to make Americans yield their remaining liberty for security; the film, in this reading, reduces public apathy about nuclear weapons chiefly to strengthen the metaphor's use.[1] The underlying message is summarized as: fear us and submit, for we have the power to destroy you.[1]
