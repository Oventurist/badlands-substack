# West Bank

The West Bank is the Palestinian territory occupied by [[israel|Israel]] since 1967 and administered in part by the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] under the framework of the [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]].

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the Israeli Security Cabinet approved a package of measures moving toward greater Israeli control of the territory. These included shifting building authority in Hebron, facilitating settler land purchases, repealing restrictions on non-Muslim real estate transactions, and expanding Israeli enforcement into Palestinian-controlled areas. [[likud|Likud]] Energy Minister [[eli-cohen|Eli Cohen]] praised the measures as establishing "de facto sovereignty" that rules out a Palestinian state. The article states that the actions violate the Oslo Accords and drew U.S. concern over regional stability.

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly opposed the moves. In an interview with Barak Ravid he said, "I am against annexation," adding, "We have enough things to think about now. We don't need to be dealing with the West Bank."

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] notes that the measures were implemented through executive power rather than legislation, and explains their mechanism: Israelis may purchase tracts of land in the West Bank and register those properties with the Israeli government rather than the Palestinian Authority, placing the land under Israeli jurisdiction in the eyes of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s Security Cabinet. He reports that Muslim leadership across the Middle East is enraged, that the move "is designed to be a provocation," and that [[hamas|Hamas]] is calling for kinetic escalation against Israel in response — which, he argues, is precisely what Netanyahu wants.
