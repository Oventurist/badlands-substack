# Saving Seoul

*Saving Seoul* is the debut novel published by [[burning-bright-publishing|Burning Bright Publishing]], an independent American publisher affiliated with the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] ecosystem.[1] It is the first book in the *Sword Punk Trilogy*.[1]

## Premise

The book is described as a martial arts epic centered on Akio Prince, a fighter battling a crime syndicate in a near-future Far East.[1]

## Crowdfunding campaign

The novel's release was promoted through a Kickstarter campaign hosted at BadlandsMedia.tv/SwordPunk, which ran through August 31, 2025.[1] Backers were offered a signed, leatherbound collector's edition of the book.[1] Promotional copy framed the campaign in explicitly cultural-political terms — "Politics is culture, and culture starts with stories" — and invited readers to "Join the War of Stories," reflecting the broader Badlands thesis that narrative production is a front in the [[information-war|information war]].[1] The item appeared in the Badlands News Brief as sponsored content.[1]
