# 2023 Kim Jong Un–Putin Vladivostok Summit

The 2023 Kim Jong Un–Putin summit was a meeting between North Korean leader [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] held on Wednesday, September 13, 2023, in the Russian Far Eastern city of Vladivostok.[1]

At an official dinner, Kim raised a toast praising a "heroic" Russian army that he said Putin would lead to a "great victory" over its enemies, stating: "We are confident that the Russian army and people will win a great victory in the just fight to punish evil groups who pursue hegemony, expansion, and ambition."[1] Kim added, "I will always be standing with Russia," in footage broadcast on Russian television, while Putin responded that "An old friend is better than two new ones" and hailed "the future strengthening of cooperation and friendship between our countries."[1]

The two leaders held roughly two hours of one-on-one talks before the dinner, which observers widely speculated may have focused on Moscow purchasing North Korean ammunition for use in Ukraine.[1] Putin also toured Russia's Far East spaceport, the Vostochny Cosmodrome, with Kim, remarking that "the leader of the DPRK shows great interest in rocket technology, and they are trying to develop (their presence in) space."[1]

Just as the two leaders — described in Western coverage as "pariahs" — were about to meet, Pyongyang fired two ballistic missiles into the sea in what was read as a further warning to South Korea and its principal military backer, the United States.[1]
