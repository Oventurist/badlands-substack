# Kobayashi Maru

The "Kobayashi Maru" is a fictional training exercise from *Star Trek* designed as an unwinnable no-win scenario, used in political analysis as shorthand for a situation offering no acceptable exit.

In the March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], the term is applied to the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's position in the war with [[iran|Iran]]. As the Brief summarizes the alternative-media consensus, retreating from the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] campaign risks a Suez Crisis-style geopolitical defeat, while continuing it threatens prolonged energy market chaos and higher global inflation — a classic Kobayashi Maru for Washington.

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] disputes this reading while claiming authorship of the framing. He writes that the alternative media is "even going so far as to mimic my exact framing of the Kobayashi Maru I have employed stretching back to 2023," but argues that they have inverted the roles: "the Iranian Knot is not Donald Trump's Kobayashi Maru. It is the no-win scenario he has deliberately engineered for the Globalist Enemy that has long disguised itself as our so-called Allies." In his account, Trump has placed "the rules-based international order, the neoconservative war machine, the energy cartels and the scarcity engineers into a pincer movement from which there is no clean or face-saving exit," a strategic inversion he says he has been mapping since 2023 and which rests on "the complete inversion of the Axis and Allies paradigm."
