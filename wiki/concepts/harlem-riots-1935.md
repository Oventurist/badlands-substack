# Harlem Riots (1935)

The Harlem riots of 1935 were race riots in the Harlem neighborhood of New York City.[1]

## Role in the alleged thirty-year cycle

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identifies the 1935 Harlem riots as the earliest example in his proposed roughly thirty-year cadence of American racial unrest, followed by the [[watts-riots-1965|Watts riots of 1965]], the [[la-riots-1992|Los Angeles riots of 1992]] arising from the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] verdict, and the 2020 [[george-floyd|George Floyd]] protests.[1] Carlson presents the sequence as evidence of a deliberate playbook operated by the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] to keep Americans divided by race.[1]
