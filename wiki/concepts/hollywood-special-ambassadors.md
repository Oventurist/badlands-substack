# Hollywood Special Ambassadors

The Hollywood Special Ambassadors were three actors — [[mel-gibson|Mel Gibson]], [[sylvester-stallone|Sylvester Stallone]] and [[jon-voight|Jon Voight]] — named by President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a social media post on January 16, 2025, to serve as his "eyes and ears" in the entertainment capital.[1]

## Announcement and rationale

Trump said the goal was "bringing Hollywood, which has lost much business over the last four years to Foreign Countries, BACK — BIGGER, BETTER, AND STRONGER THAN EVER BEFORE!"[1] The New York Times reported that the motivation was unclear but that Trump was probably referring to runaway production: because of costs, major movies are increasingly shot in other countries, with Britain, Hungary, Australia and Canada offering studios generous tax incentives.[1]

The initiative came as a surprise even to one of the participants. In a statement distributed hours later by his publicist, Gibson said: "I got the tweet at the same time as all of you and was just as surprised. Nevertheless, I heed the call." He added, "Any chance the position comes with an Ambassador's residence?" — a reference to the loss of his Malibu home in the recent California wildfires.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Burning Bright treated the appointments with characteristic irony, framing the announcement as Trump dispatching special ambassadors to Hollywood "pretty much because it's too fake and gay these days," and advising readers that "it's okay to relax and enjoy the show."[1]
