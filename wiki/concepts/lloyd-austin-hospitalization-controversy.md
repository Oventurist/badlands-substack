# Lloyd Austin Hospitalization Controversy

The Lloyd Austin hospitalization controversy refers to the January 2024 episode in which U.S. Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] was hospitalized in secret, leaving much of Washington — including President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] — unaware for days of who was actually running the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1] The Wall Street Journal account was excerpted in the Badlands News Brief of January 12, 2024.[1]

## Sequence of events

Austin was secretly rushed to Walter Reed Military Medical Center on New Year's Day suffering from nausea and severe pain, and it would be eight days before the president learned why.[1] For four days in January, most of Washington did not know who was running the Pentagon.[1] Austin — sixth in the line of presidential succession and second in the line of military command after the president — was hospitalized while his deputy was required to step in from a beach in Puerto Rico where she was on vacation, days before Biden was informed.[1]

Only a small cadre of aides knew of the hospitalization. Most others in the Pentagon, including Deputy Defense Secretary [[kathleen-hicks|Kathleen Hicks]], who was carrying out Austin's duties, were kept in the dark according to the Pentagon's own account of events.[1] Sasha Baker, the Defense Department's top policy official and the most senior official physically present at the Pentagon during that period, attended a White House meeting on January 3 unaware that Austin was hospitalized.[1] Observers described the sequence as without modern precedent.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the episode as a stress test for the credibility of what he called the Central Narrative, arguing that it was becoming increasingly untenable for a sane mind to absorb official messaging without mounting cognitive dissonance.[1] He noted the simultaneity of the claims being pressed on the public: that the world was on the brink of a third world war, that U.S. and U.K. carrier groups were actively engaged with Iran-backed terrorists in the Red Sea, and that China was growing more bullish about reunification with Taiwan — all while the man presented as Secretary of Defense was missing from his own administration for four days and the officer reportedly running the department was doing so from a beach.[1] His conclusion was framed as a binary: either much of the above is at various levels of fake, or it signals that these people do not currently wield the power they claim to.[1]
