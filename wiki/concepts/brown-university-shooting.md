# Brown University Shooting

The **Brown University shooting** was a mass shooting at Brown University in Providence, Rhode Island, on Saturday, December 13, 2025, in which a gunman entered a lecture hall and opened fire with a rifle.[1]

## Casualties and investigation

At least two students were killed and another nine wounded, one of them in critical condition at a hospital.[1] Nearly thirty hours after the shooting, Rhode Island authorities said on Sunday night that they were still searching for the gunman.[1]

A person of interest had been taken into custody on Sunday morning, but the investigation — which also involved the FBI — took a turn when top Rhode Island officials announced at 11 p.m. that the detained man would be released.[1]

## Manhunt (December 16, 2025)

With the shooter still on the loose on Tuesday, December 16, 2025, Providence police released enhanced videos and a timeline of a person of interest's movements as investigators continued to pursue evidence in the case.[3] Police released multiple videos of the man suspected in Saturday's attack in an engineering building classroom, where two students were killed and nine others were wounded; none of the videos showed the man's face, which was either turned away or masked, though clearer footage was emerging on Tuesday.[3] The [[fbi|FBI]] described the suspect as stocky and about 5 feet, 8 inches tall.[3] A church on the university campus planned to host a "Community Service of Lament, Healing and Hope" on Tuesday night.[3] Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] responded that he was "patiently waiting for the gait analysis from The Blaze."[3]

## Resolution

On the night of Thursday, December 18, 2025, authorities announced that the suspect had been found dead in a storage unit in New Hampshire.[2] The suspect was identified as [[claudio-manuel-neves-valente|Claudio Manuel Neves Valente]], 48, a former Brown student and Portuguese national last known to live in Miami, according to Providence Police Chief Col. Oscar Perez.[2] His body was discovered Thursday evening with firearms next to him in a storage facility in Salem, New Hampshire, and officials said he had taken his own life.[2] [[leah-foley|Leah Foley]], the US Attorney for the District of Massachusetts, said the same man was believed to have fatally shot [[massachusetts-institute-of-technology|MIT]] physics professor [[nuno-loureiro|Nuno F. G. Loureiro]] at his home in Brookline, Massachusetts, on the preceding Monday.[2] The final toll at Brown was two students killed and nine injured.[2]
