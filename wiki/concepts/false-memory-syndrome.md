# False Memory Syndrome

**False Memory Syndrome** is a term used to describe purportedly implanted or fabricated recollections of abuse. Badlands Media treats it as a manufactured construct deployed to discredit victims of ritual abuse and trauma-based mind control.[1]

## Sinason's critique

[[valerie-sinason|Dr. Valerie Sinason]] confronts the concept in her book *Memory in Dispute*, arguing that the term itself and the myriad organizations that sprang up around the alleged diagnosis have no foundation.[1] In her account the term serves only to protect abusers and to give mainstream media ammunition to discredit victims of ritual abuse and trauma-based mind control.[1]

## Absence of clinical standing

The article notes that mainstream publications continue to apply the label to this day even though it has no agreed-upon definition and has never been included in the DSM as an actual medical diagnosis.[1] The more one examines the concept, the article argues, the more it reads as a psychological operation strategically positioned to deny elite and government use of torture, abuse and the occult as common weaponry.[1]

## Intelligence linkage

Whether the founders of False Memory Syndrome organizations can be definitively tied to [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] or to the intelligence agencies generally is described as a moot point.[1] Because the mainstream media is characterized as an arm of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], the article reasons that either these foundations originated in intelligence operations or they were created by people friendly to the mind-control cover-up whose theories were then uplifted and propagated by the controlled press.[1] Either way, extensive propaganda has been built on the term and used to construct narratives whose sole purpose is denying the science of trauma-based mind control.[1]
