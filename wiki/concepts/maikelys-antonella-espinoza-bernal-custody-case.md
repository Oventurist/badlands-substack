# Maikelys Antonella Espinoza Bernal Custody Case

The **Maikelys Antonella Espinoza Bernal case** was an international custody dispute arising in 2025 after a 2-year-old Venezuelan girl was left in the United States when both of her parents were removed from the country.[1]

## Events

The child's mother was deported to Venezuela without her, while her father was sent to a megaprison in El Salvador.[1] The separation drew condemnation from immigrant rights groups and raised concern about the potential for further family separations as the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration sought to expedite removals.[1]

On Wednesday, May 14, 2025, the child was returned to Venezuela, according to her family and the Venezuelan government.[1] Venezuelan state television showed footage of the country's first lady, [[cilia-flores|Cilia Flores]], holding Maikelys in her arms at the airport, and the child's grandmother said the family was rushing to reunite with her. Venezuela's leaders publicly celebrated her arrival.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host Ashe in America placed the case among a set of immigration stories she described as appeals to emotion designed to induce doubt about the speed and necessity of removals, arguing that the legal authority to remove unlawful criminal entrants was unaffected by individual sympathetic narratives.[1]
