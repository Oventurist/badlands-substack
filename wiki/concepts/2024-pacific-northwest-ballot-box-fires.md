# 2024 Pacific Northwest Ballot Box Fires

The 2024 Pacific Northwest ballot box fires were a series of arson incidents targeting ballot drop boxes in Washington state and Oregon in the weeks before the November 2024 general election, in which hundreds of ballots were damaged or destroyed.[1]

## Incidents

At least three ballot drop boxes were set on fire.[1] Almost all of the ballots burned on the Monday before the election were in a drop box in Vancouver, Washington, while most ballots in a drop box in Portland, Oregon, survived a fire set the same day, according to election officials.[1] Both incidents were believed to be connected to a third fire on October 8, also in Vancouver.[1] Portland police released a physical description of a suspect but said they had not identified him.[1]

## Response

The fires prompted Washington governor [[jay-inslee|Jay Inslee]] to place members of the state [[national-guard|National Guard]] on standby, citing "specific instances of election-related unrest" in the southwest region of the state.[1] Oregon governor [[tina-kotek|Tina Kotek]] similarly said the Guard stood ready while her office coordinated with local, state and federal agencies.[1] The incidents fed into broader warnings from the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] that threats to election infrastructure remained high going into the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 general election]].[1]
