# 2025 Impeachment Vote Over the Iran Strikes

On Tuesday, June 24, 2025, the United States House of Representatives voted to table an article of impeachment against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over his decision to strike Iranian nuclear sites without congressional authorization.[1]

## The resolution

Rep. [[al-green|Al Green]] (D-Texas) forced a vote on a five-page impeachment resolution accusing Trump of "Disregarding the Separation of Powers" and "Devolving American Democracy into Authoritarianism by Unconstitutionally Usurping Congress's Power to Declare War."[1] The resolution focused on the fact that Trump had not sought congressional authorization for the strikes and had not provided evidence demonstrating an "imminent threat" to U.S. forces.[1]

## The vote

The House voted 344 to 79 on a Republican motion to table — that is, kill — Green's article of impeachment.[1] Among Democrats, 128 voted with Republicans to defeat the measure while 79 voted to bring it to the floor.[1] Axios characterized the outcome as the clearest signal to date of the distaste many Democrats held for even attempting to impeach Trump, an effort that would likely have failed in either chamber of [[congress|Congress]].[1]

## Context

The vote followed a wave of Democratic objections to the strikes. Rep. [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] (D-N.Y.) had suggested Trump could be impeached, and Rep. [[jasmine-crockett|Jasmine Crockett]] (D-Texas) argued publicly that "Congress must authorize military force before any president—including Trump—launches a strike."[1]
