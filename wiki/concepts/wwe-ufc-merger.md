# WWE–UFC Merger

The WWE–UFC merger was the April 2023 agreement to combine [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] with the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]] into a single publicly traded combat sports and entertainment company controlled by [[endeavor|Endeavor Group]].[1]

## Terms

The companies announced the transaction on Monday, April 3, 2023.[1] Endeavor would hold a 51 percent stake in the new company, while WWE shareholders retained the remaining 49 percent.[1] The agreement valued WWE at $9.3 billion and UFC, already owned by Endeavor, at $12.1 billion.[1] The transaction was expected to close in the second half of 2023.[1] Markets reacted negatively on the day of the announcement: WWE shares fell more than 2 percent and Endeavor shares declined over 5 percent.[1]

## Leadership

[[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]] was named chief executive of both Endeavor and the new company, and described the combination in a CNBC "Squawk on the Street" interview as "UFC 2.0."[1] [[vince-mcmahon|Vince McMahon]], WWE's longtime controlling figure, was to serve as executive chairman.[1] Endeavor president and chief operating officer [[mark-shapiro|Mark Shapiro]] would hold the same roles at the new company, [[dana-white|Dana White]] would remain president of UFC, and WWE chief executive [[nick-khan|Nick Khan]] would stay on as president of the wrestling business.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the merger as a consolidation of independent media-adjacent properties under Hollywood ownership.[1] He argued that UFC and WWE audiences skew heavily MAGA and countercultural, that Endeavor's earlier acquisition of UFC had already led to stagnation and homogenization of the product even though the promotion "still can't quite control the athletes," and that WWE was more vulnerable because its programming is scripted, leaving storylines and interviews entirely to writers acting on corporate direction.[1]
