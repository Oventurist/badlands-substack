# Public Opinion (book)

*Public Opinion* is the book by [[walter-lippmann|Walter Lippmann]], described in the Badlands Media essay "God and the Declaration of Independence" as having been written a century ago, and cited there for its account of how democratic majorities actually function.[1]

## Argument as cited

According to the essay, Lippmann showed in *Public Opinion* that the will of the majority is largely a mirage, and that the only true power in a democratic society comes from the ability to sway the masses — an ability the author adds is extremely easy to exercise for those who own the media.[1]

The essay deploys this claim against secular political philosophy, which it says can appeal to nothing more objective than majority preference and consequently makes "democracy" its highest ideal.[1] If majority will is itself a manufactured artifact, the essay reasons, then a purely majoritarian foundation for rights is no foundation at all, and the transcendent source of rights asserted in the [[declaration-of-independence|Declaration of Independence]] becomes the only stable alternative.[1]
