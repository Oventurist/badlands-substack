# Austrian School

The **Austrian School** is a tradition of economic thought founded on methodological individualism — the concept that social phenomena result exclusively from the motivations and actions of individuals.[1]

Badlands Media cites the school as the intellectual home of the sharpest critics of [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]], naming [[jesus-huerta-de-soto|Jesús Huerta de Soto]] and [[murray-rothbard|Murray Rothbard]], both of whom called for the practice to be outlawed and criminalized.[1] Their case rests partly on the Austrian Business Cycle Theory, which holds that bank money creation generates macroeconomic instability, and partly on the moral claim that fractional reserve lending is embezzlement or fraud legalized through banker influence over governments.[1]
