# Trickle-Down Economics

**Trickle-down economics** is the theory that tax breaks and benefits granted to corporations and the wealthy will filter down to everyone else.[1] It argues for income and capital gains tax breaks, and other financial advantages for large businesses, investors and entrepreneurs, on the grounds that these stimulate economic growth.[1]

Badlands Media argues that, viewed through the lens of [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]], the theory describes the opposite of what actually occurs: the wealth of the general population is funneled perpetually upward into the coffers of a ruling class through the mechanism of debt plus interest.[1] Because the money supply contains only the principal created by lending and never the interest owed on it, the outlet contends that the resulting permanent shortfall drives money toward the top 10% and continually widens the gap between the wealthiest and everyone else.[1]
