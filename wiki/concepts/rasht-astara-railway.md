# Rasht-Astara Railway

The Rasht-Astara railway is a 114-mile rail link in northern [[iran|Iran]] connecting Rasht, the capital of Gilan province, with Astara on the Iran–[[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]] border, a city half of which lies in each country.[1]

Three years before 2026, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] signed a deal with Iran to finance and build the line.[1] In February 2026 [[russia|Russia]] and Iran signed the final portion of the agreement, and construction reportedly began in April 2026.[1]

Badlands analysis framed Putin's investment in so short a stretch of track as strategically decisive: the missing link is the final piece needed to complete the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]]'s [[north-south-transport-corridor|North-South Transport Corridor]], which expects to compete with the Suez Canal for traffic and freight moving between Asia and Europe.[1] The corridor's completion is discussed alongside other regional developments, including the restoration of the Baku–Tbilisi passenger train route between Azerbaijan and Georgia, suspended during COVID and never reopened until 2026, and a newly launched container rail corridor linking [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], Uzbekistan, [[china|China]], and Afghanistan.[1]
