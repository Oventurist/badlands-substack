# CERAWeek

CERAWeek is the annual energy industry conference held in Houston, Texas, widely regarded as the principal gathering of global oil, gas, and power executives, energy ministers, and financiers. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as the venue where US energy policy and industry sentiment are read for signals.

## The 2026 conference and the Iran conflict

The March 24, 2026 Badlands Brief, Alternative Annexations & New(ish) Regime Change, reports that Energy Secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]] appeared at CERAWeek in Houston urging oil and gas leaders to increase domestic production immediately, while the [[white-house|White House]] held direct meetings with executives and diplomats to coordinate higher output amid tensions with Iran. [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treats the conference as a diagnostic moment: he argues that the "unusual stress among American oil executives" visible in their Houston comments is "perhaps the clearest indicator that something larger is underway," since these are men who "should be celebrating $100+ oil and expanding margins" yet instead appeared cautious and focused on longer-term volatility. He interprets that caution as recognition that the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] crisis is forcing a permanent sovereign disentanglement of energy flows and a multipolar realignment in which oil conglomerate margins could eventually be squeezed.
