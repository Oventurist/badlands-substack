# Anti-CBDC Act

The Anti-CBDC Act is legislation aimed at barring the creation of a United States central bank digital currency, passed by the [[us-house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] in July 2025 as part of a three-bill crypto legislative package.[1]

The measure moved alongside the [[genius-act|GENIUS Act]], which establishes regulatory oversight of stablecoin issuers and was sent to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for signature after a 308–122 House vote, and the [[digital-asset-market-clarity-act|Digital Asset Market Clarity Act]], which passed 294–134 and awaited Senate action.[1] Taken together, the package was described as the first major crypto legislation poised to become law and a turning point for an industry long hampered by unclear rules.[1] Opposition to a central bank digital currency is closely tied in this milieu to broader criticism of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1]
