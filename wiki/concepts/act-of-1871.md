# Act of 1871

The **Act of 1871** is a piece of United States legislation concerning the government of the [[district-of-columbia|District of Columbia]] that features prominently in alternative constitutional theories. In the April 3, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Is Jerome Powell a Patriot?", [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites it as the legal instrument by which the American republic was allegedly converted into a corporation.[1]

## The claim

Carlson writes that some posit that the Act of 1871, passed by Congress, created a separate corporation known as THE UNITED STATES along with a corporate government for the District of Columbia, and that D.C. therefore acts as a corporation through the Act.[1] He presents the claim in reported form — "some posit" — rather than as established fact.[1]

## Context in Carlson's argument

The Act appears in a passage arguing that Washington, D.C. is "not part of the USA," possessing its own flag, its own independent constitution and ten square miles of territory, and that it is one of three corporate city-states beyond national authority alongside the [[city-of-london|City of London]] and [[vatican|Vatican City]].[1] Carlson notes that one of multiple theories about the three red stars on the District's flag holds that they denote D.C., Vatican City and the City of London.[1]

The Act thus functions in the essay as legal underpinning for the broader thesis that the United States has never truly controlled its own decisions and has been governed by foreign bankers since the founding — the condition Carlson believes [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] are working to reverse.[1]
