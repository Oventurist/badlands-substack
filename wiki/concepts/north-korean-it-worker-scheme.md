# North Korean IT Worker Scheme

The North Korean IT worker scheme is a sanctions-evasion and identity-fraud operation in which North Korean state actors placed workers in remote jobs at American companies and diverted their salaries to Pyongyang, ultimately helping fund the weapons programs of [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]].[1]

## DOJ indictment

On June 30, 2025, the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] announced that it had seized hundreds of computers and accused 13 people of tricking U.S. companies into paying salaries to North Korea.[1] A new indictment filed in Massachusetts federal court alleged that conspirators fooled more than 100 American firms across Washington, D.C. and 27 states.[1]

## Targeted companies

The affected firms were not named, but authorities said they included Fortune 500 companies and a defense contractor in California with access to sensitive military technology files.[1]

## Method

Investigators detailed an elaborate scheme in which at least two U.S. citizens worked with North Korean state actors from 2021 to 2024 to steal identities, use them to get people hired in American industries, and then siphon their salaries to Pyongyang.[1] The operation relied on so-called laptop farms maintained inside the United States to disguise the true location of the workers.[1]
