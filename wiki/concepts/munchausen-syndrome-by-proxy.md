# Munchausen Syndrome by Proxy

Munchausen Syndrome by Proxy (MSBP) is a recognized mental illness, also known as medical child abuse or factitious disorder imposed on others, in which a caregiver seeks attention by exaggerating or fabricating an illness in a child or another person in their care.[1] According to reporting cited by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]], the condition is exhibited mostly by women.[1]

## Relationship to "Transhausen by proxy"

The condition became a reference point in debates over the rise in children identifying as transgender after psychiatrists coined the informal term "[[transhausen-by-proxy|transhausen by proxy]]" — a play on MSBP — to describe narcissistic parents alleged to push so-called "gender transitioning" onto their children.[1] Unlike MSBP, transhausen by proxy is not an officially recognized psychological condition, but experts told The Epoch Times that the dynamic it describes has real effects on society.[1]

Commentators pointed to celebrity culture as an amplifier, citing a May 2023 Pride.com headline promoting "15 Celebs Who Are Out & Proud of Their Trans & Nonbinary Kids," which praised figures such as Cher, Sade, Jennifer Lopez, and Charlize Theron for supporting children who reject their biological sex.[1]

## "Munchausen Moms" and pediatric gender medicine

In December 2025, following publication of a [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] review of pediatric gender dysphoria treatment, Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked the disorder in describing parents who pursue medical transition for their children, asking rhetorically what would become of "the Munchausen Moms with their fad children."[2] She rejected the notion that such parents could credibly claim to have been hoodwinked, arguing that no scientific finding should be required to deter a parent from "the sexualization, sterilization, mutilation, and psychological torture" of their own children because that restraint is "biologically wired into parents. Unless those parents are psychos."[2] See also [[pediatric-gender-transition-treatment|pediatric gender transition treatment]].[2]
