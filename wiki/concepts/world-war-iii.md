# World War III

World War III (WWIII) appears in the Badlands corpus chiefly as a war that the authors expect to be attempted and to fail — a mobilization that cannot achieve escape velocity because the propaganda required to launch it is no longer functioning.[1]

## The "car that won't start" thesis

[[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] predicted in October 2023 that WWIII "will be like a car that won't start — or at least has a lot of trouble getting started — prompting people to look under the hood," adding that no amount of "rage-fuel" can repair a broken engine.[1] The reasoning rests on the claimed breakdown of [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] conditioning: social engineers can still generate fear and rage, but can no longer reliably direct where the public discharges it, which strips the weapon of most of its utility.[1] The [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel-Palestine conflict]] is identified as the likely final straw in that breakdown.[1]

The predicted consequence is exposure rather than escalation: people looking under the hood will see what Badlands readers have long seen, including [[smedley-butler|Smedley Butler]]'s verdict that war is a racket in which the puppeteers play both sides against each other, and will find in the [[q|Q]] framework a place to go when their prior narratives collapse.[1]
