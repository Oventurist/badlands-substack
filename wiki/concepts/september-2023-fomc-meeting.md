# September 2023 FOMC Meeting

The September 2023 meeting of the [[federal-open-market-committee|Federal Open Market Committee]] concluded on Wednesday, September 20, 2023, with the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] leaving its benchmark interest rate unchanged while issuing projections that were more hawkish than markets had expected.[1]

## Decision and projections

The committee held the federal funds target range at 5.25%–5.50%, a 22-year high, while simultaneously signaling a "soft landing" through upgraded growth forecasts.[1] The committee's "dot plot" of rate projections showed policymakers still anticipating one more hike before the end of 2023, while the median projections for both 2024 and 2025 rose by a half percentage point — a signal that the Fed expected rates to remain higher for longer.[1]

Twelve of the nineteen policymakers on the committee expected one additional rate hike in 2023 to be appropriate; the remaining seven favored holding rates steady.[1] The median projection for economic growth in 2023 jumped to 2.1% from 1% in the June forecast round, and officials significantly reduced their unemployment forecasts, now expecting the jobless rate to peak at 4.1% rather than 4.5%.[1]

The post-meeting statement repeated prior language saying officials were considering "the extent of additional policy firming that may be appropriate," and acknowledged that job gains had "slowed" while insisting they "remain strong."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] connected the rate environment to the bank failures earlier in 2023, arguing that the hiking cycle had caused those collapses.[1] He characterized the policy stance as a deliberate "controlled implosion of the economy for political needs" tied to the 2024 elections and serving as a preamble to the [[great-reset|Great Reset]].[1] He suggested that investors who understood how to position for a falling economy and acquire assets on the other side would weather the downturn, while cautioning that if the Great Reset materialized, no one would be unaffected.[1]
