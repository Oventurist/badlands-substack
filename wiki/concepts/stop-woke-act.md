# Stop WOKE Act

The Stop WOKE Act is a Florida law enacted under Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] that bars schools and businesses in the state from teaching material that could cause a person to "feel guilt, anguish or any form of psychological distress" on the basis of race, national origin, or sex.[1]

The statute is one of the principal legislative vehicles of DeSantis's campaign against what he calls the woke agenda, alongside the [[parental-rights-in-education-act|Parental Rights in Education Act]].[1] On the 2024 presidential campaign trail he pledged that "we're going to make sure that the woke agenda ends up in the dustbin of history," pairing that promise with a vow to begin "slitting throats" of [[deep-state|deep state]] personnel on his first day in office.[1]

The law's logic — that race-conscious instruction and hiring are themselves discriminatory — also underpinned the August 2023 decision by the [[central-florida-tourism-oversight-district|Central Florida Tourism Oversight District]] to abolish its diversity, equity, and inclusion programs.[1]
