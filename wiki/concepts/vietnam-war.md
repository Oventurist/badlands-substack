# Vietnam War

The Vietnam War was one of two gruesome post-World War II conflicts — alongside the Korean War — that, in the framing of the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] corpus, "forever scarred the baby boomer generation" and served to make [[vietnam|Vietnam]] and [[north-korea|North Korea]] critical proxy states for the [[state-department|State Department]] and the [[cia|CIA]].[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands commentary treats Vietnam as a different kind of proxy state from North Korea.[1] Where North Korea was propped up as a puppet boogeyman with the Kim family installed as figurehead, Vietnam is described as a narcotics theater.[1] Under French colonial rule the country hosted a large-scale opium operation, part of the same commercial-imperial system that Britain and France had used against China during the nineteenth-century Opium Wars.[1]

When France was driven out of Vietnam by Soviet and Chinese-backed forces in the years after the Korean War, the [[gulf-of-tonkin-incident|Gulf of Tonkin incident]] was staged to bring the United States into the conflict.[1] The CIA moved its opium operation into Laos, while American soldiers fought the Vietcong in a campaign that, on this reading, functioned to prevent the complete shutdown of the drug trade.[1] The commentary asserts that the Empire's main heroin customer base during the war consisted of American GIs serving in Vietnam, which it advances as the explanation for the addiction and mental-health crises among returning veterans.[1]

## Relevance in 2024

The war's history was invoked in June 2024 to explain the symbolic weight of [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s visit to Hanoi and his meeting with National Assembly Chairman [[tran-thanh-man|Tran Thanh Man]], which Badlands writers read as a deliberate mirror of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2017 foreign tour and as a step in assembling a worldwide network of sovereign-minded allies.[1]
