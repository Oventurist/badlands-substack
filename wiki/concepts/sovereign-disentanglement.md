# Sovereign Disentanglement

**Sovereign Disentanglement** is a concept discussed by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators concerning the withdrawal of a nation's affairs from entangling foreign, financial and institutional commitments in order to restore independent self-governance.[1] It was named as one of the three principal themes of the twelfth episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], "The Fake and Gay, but Also Real and Serious War," released 28 March 2026.[1]

## Treatment in The Blitz Episode 12

Hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] paired sovereign disentanglement with [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] and [[first-principles|first principles]] as the substantive content of an episode whose ostensible hook was the dueling narratives about the Iranian Ayatollah's status.[1] The framing presented these three ideas as having been largely forgotten both in the wider world and within the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] itself.[1]

## Application to Venezuela and Iran (2026)

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote in April 2026 that the Sovereign Disentanglement framework he had laid out in January had been "stress-tested in real time," with [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s version of regime change in Caracas delivering results the Globalists never wanted: continuity beneath the optics, sovereign control preserved, and oil flowing on terms serving the multipolar reordering rather than the old scarcity cartel.[2] He argued the Iranian operation was headed for the same codification, producing in each case "a cascading series of Prussian Proxy Dominos" in which a regime that looks suspiciously like the old one emerges once globalist interests have been surgically disentangled, paving the way toward the "political reconciliation" the U.S. [[state-department|State Department]] referenced after re-opening the U.S. Embassy in Caracas.[2]

Under this reading Trump claims the win, the Collective Mind gets its narrative catharsis, and sovereign realignment proceeds without the blood and treasure the Hegemon demanded — a pathway toward true [[multipolar-world|Multipolarity]] and the antithesis of what the Collectorate wanted.[2] Burning Bright framed the resulting trap as falling not on Trump nor on cooperative elements within the Iranian regime, but on the [[invisible-enemy|Invisible Enemy]] — the faceless collectivist architecture that has "worn nations like skin suits for generations."[2]

## Iran as template (April 2026)

Writing after the collapse of the [[islamabad-agreement|Islamabad]] peace talks, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the fragile off-ramp from the Iran theater had detonated "a cascade of second-order exposures" laying bare the acceleration of Sovereign Disentanglement and the patient architecture of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] inverting the unipolar globalist hegemonic paradigm in real time.[3] What is presented as de-escalation, he wrote, is neither retreat nor stalemate but a "public translation layer" revealing pre-coordinated realignments in global energy leverage and currency rails that force the [[collectorate|Collectorate]] to confront its dependencies.[3]

He pointed to [[iran|Iran]]'s assertion of tiered control over the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] as the template projected long before the crisis arc began: a sovereign actor leveraging its geography and resources for its own people and the emerging multipolar framework rather than for distant overlords.[3] He tied this explicitly to the [[venezuela|Venezuela]] Model, whose continuity of stewardship over energy abundance inspired his naming of the Sovereign Disentanglement pattern in the first place.[3] The globalist hegemon, he concluded, never feared any particular regime but rather this naturalistic template — nations drawing strength inward first, then radiating it outward untethered from the interdependencies propping up the collectivist order.[3]

## Recirculation in the June 2026 Iran commentary

In the June 2, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] quoted his own January 11 "Sovereign Disentanglement" essay, in which he had written that the Info War was picking up on a template being set for [[iran|Iran]] — "another hotbed of regime ops that is currently in the midst of its own color revolution" — and asked whether [[donald-trump|Trump]] would "spirit away Khamenei, only to leave the rest of his ruling party in control while rug-pulling the global regime's planned version of the change event."[4] He presented those questions as the ones keeping Western hegemonic propagandists awake and as the basis of his belief in the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] and the [[multipolar-war|Multipolar War]].[4]

## Codification in the Iranian theater (March 2026)

Responding to [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s statement that the United States was negotiating with a "new, more reasonable regime" in [[iran|Iran]] and to [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]]'s escalate-to-de-escalate remarks, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that the pair were further proof of the codification of the Sovereign Disentanglement process he first laid out in January during Operation Absolute Resolve in [[venezuela|Venezuela]], now unfolding in the Iranian theater of [[operation-epic-fury|Operation Epic Fury]].[5] As in Syria and Venezuela, he argued, Trump and his compatriots in the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] are executing a deliberate inversion that turns the enemy's own [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian]] trap against them.[5]

Burning Bright described Trump's dialectic toward the Iranian regime as "bicameral by design," with narrative pressure building public demand for resolution even as the actual path forward was baked in from the start, keeping the geopolitical and mass-psychological terrain in a state of "chaotic stasis."[5] Under the template, he predicted, the "New Regime" would bear more than a passing resemblance to the old one, as in Venezuela and, more cartoonishly, Syria — producing "functional continuity under reformed stewardship" with destabilizing elements extracted.[5] The result, he wrote, would be a reformed Iran that deprives the Globalist Machine of the perpetual never-endstate it craved: a region fractured, warring, and forever bleeding energy and stability into a unipolar black hole.[5]
