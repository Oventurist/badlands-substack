# Russia Day

**Russia Day** is the Russian Federation's national holiday, observed annually on **12 June**, commemorating the 1990 Declaration of State Sovereignty of the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic. The declaration, adopted a year and a half before the formal dissolution of the Soviet Union, asserted the primacy of Russian law over all-Union law and is treated in official Russian memory as the founding moment of the post-Soviet Russian state.

## In the Badlands corpus

Russia Day entered Badlands coverage through the 12 June 2026 remarks of [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], who used a Russia Day meeting to declare that Russia was standing against the so-called [[collective-west|Collective West]] "single-handedly" in the Ukraine conflict, that no external force could impose a strategic defeat on Moscow, and that the war had become a highly technological contest in which all NATO members were uniformly intensifying their efforts against Russia. Putin reiterated the Russian position that Moscow did not start the war — arguing that Western powers had orchestrated a coup in Ukraine, obliging Russia to protect the population of Crimea, and that Ukrainian forces had then begun large-scale hostilities by bombing Donetsk from the air. He framed the confrontation as the latest in a sequence of united European coalitions against Russia, invoking Napoleon and Hitler.

The Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the choice of date as significant in itself, reading 12 June as a deliberate signal: the anniversary of the sovereignty declaration marks the beginning of the decade that produced the Russian oligarchy, and, in his telling, the eventual rise of the leader who broke that oligarchy through an anti-corruption purge later mirrored by Xi Jinping in China and by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the West. Burning Bright further noted that Putin's use of the phrase "Collective West" matched the vocabulary of his own *Righteous Russia* series, and argued that Russia functions as a core animating engine of the theorized [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] rather than an isolated holdout.
