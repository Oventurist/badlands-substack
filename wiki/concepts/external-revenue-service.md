# External Revenue Service

The External Revenue Service is a proposed United States federal body, pledged by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], that would oversee the collection of tariffs and other potential foreign revenue.[1] It is framed as the counterpart — and eventual replacement — for the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]], shifting the federal government's revenue base from domestic income taxation to levies on foreign trade.[1]

## Origins and rationale

The concept builds on Trump's broader plan of "tariffing and taxing foreign nations to enrich our citizens," and accompanies his floated idea of abolishing federal income taxes altogether.[1] On February 19, 2025, Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] said on Fox News that Trump's goal was to "abolish" the IRS, remarks that came as the tax agency was reportedly poised to lay off thousands of workers.[1]

Lutnick set out the sequencing on Jesse Watters Primetime: [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], as a senior White House adviser leading [[doge|DOGE]], was "going to cut" $1 trillion, "and then we're going to get rid of all these tax scams that hammer against America, and we're going to raise a trillion dollars of revenue."[1] Asked by [[jesse-watters|Jesse Watters]] whether DOGE savings would be returned to the American people, Lutnick replied: "Think about it, Donald Trump announces the External Revenue Service, and his goal is very simple ... his goal is to abolish the Internal Revenue Service and let all the outsiders pay."[1]

## Reception

Badlands commentary treated the proposal as part of a package of rapid and radical fiscal change, alongside Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]]'s reopening of the Department of the Treasury and the [[doge-dividend|DOGE dividend]] concept.[1] One contributor cautioned against simply exchanging one system of trust for another, arguing that "what the system is valued on is secondary to the ledger that tracks it" and asking what a system whose value *is* its ledger might look like.[1]
