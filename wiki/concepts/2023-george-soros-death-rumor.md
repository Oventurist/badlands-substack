# 2023 George Soros Death Rumor

On Monday, May 15, 2023, a viral social media rumor claimed that the 92-year-old billionaire financier and Democratic mega-donor [[george-soros|George Soros]] had died of a heart attack.[1]

## Origin and debunking

The claim spread from a tweet by the account "Politics For All Ireland," which went viral on Monday morning before being deleted.[1] A representative of Soros said the rumor was not true, and Soros himself posted a tweet debunking it, writing: "Rumors that I had a heart attack are completely false. I am alive and healthy."[1] The episode was covered by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], which described Soros as a billionaire Democrat mega-donor who had financed political upheaval domestically and abroad.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted the story in its news brief, with commentator [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] offering only the one-line reaction, "Oh. That's too bad."[1]

## Circulation within the truther movement

In "Gaslighting From All Sides" (May 22, 2023), [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounted reading the death claim on Mother's Day in a [[truth-social|Truth Social]] post by a prominent figure in the "truther" movement, who later acknowledged having fallen for a hoax.[2] Carlson pointed out that both of the notable fake news items he encountered that week — this one and a claim that [[joe-biden|Biden]] had extended [[title-42|Title 42]] at the last minute — appeared on Truth Social rather than on Twitter, MSNBC or CNN, which he took as evidence of a motivated effort to mislead the movement on its own platforms by telling it what it wanted to hear.[2]

Carlson also used the episode as a case study in [[black-pilling|black-pilling]].[2] Reviewing the comments on the post, he found that most respondents who believed Soros had died replied "who cares, his son is worse than him anyway" or that someone equally bad would simply replace him, a level of hopelessness he found shocking, though he conceded an unknown share of the accounts may have been bots.[2]
