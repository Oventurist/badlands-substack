# Grievance Studies Affair

The Grievance Studies affair — also known as "Sokal Squared" — was an academic hoax in which the writer Helen Pluckrose, mathematician [[james-lindsay|James Lindsay]] and philosopher Peter Boghossian submitted deliberately absurd papers to peer-reviewed journals in cultural, queer, gender, race and fat studies, and succeeded in having a number of them published.[1]

## The submitted papers

The trio authored peer-reviewed and published academic papers advancing intentionally ridiculous or immoral theses, including a case for men using dildos to self-penetrate as a means of reducing transphobia, a study of the men who patronize so-called "breastaurants" such as Hooters, and a study of the prevalence of rape culture among dogs in Portland dog parks.[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] described the project as "a phenomenal experiment successfully exposing woke academia as moronic and low grade nonsense upheld by no meaningful standards at all."[1] He invoked the affair in February 2024 when the journal Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology retracted a paper containing AI-generated nonsense imagery, arguing that both episodes revealed the emptiness of credentialed authority.[1]

Esler extended the argument beyond academia, contending that populations had "suffered generations of operant conditioning" teaching them that peer-reviewed studies could and should replace their own inner authority and the wisdom of direct empirical experience.[1] Operations exposing these "false authorities," he wrote — whether conducted by dissident academics or by alleged foreign paper mills — served the same corrective function.[1]
