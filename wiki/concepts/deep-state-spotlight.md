# Deep State Spotlight

Deep State Spotlight is an expanding series of profile essays published on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] that examines individual political figures the outlet associates with the [[deep-state|deep state]] and the permanent political establishment.[1]

## Scope

By December 2023 the series had chronicled [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] and [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], with further installments promised.[1] The December 8, 2023 entry, written by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], profiled California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] under the subtitle "The Master of Identity Politics Aims for the White House," and drew on the earlier Harris piece for its account of San Francisco mayor [[willie-brown|Willie Brown]]'s patronage network.[1]

## Method

Entries combine biographical excavation — family connections, patronage relationships, and financing — with analysis of a subject's public political style, typically arguing that culture-war and [[identity-politics|identity politics]] positioning masks continuity with establishment donor interests.[1] The Newsom installment, for example, traced his ascent through the [[getty-family|Getty family]] orbit and his father [[william-newsom|William Newsom, Jr.]]'s service to the Getty heirs before turning to his [[culture-war|culture war]] record in office.[1] Badlands notes that its articles and features represent the opinions of contributing authors rather than the views of Badlands Media itself.[1]
