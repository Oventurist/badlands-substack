# Palisades Fire

The Palisades Fire was a wildfire affecting the Los Angeles area whose aftermath became a significant issue in the city's subsequent municipal politics.

In Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, the fire is cited as the backdrop to the 2026 [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]] mayoral race: former reality television personality [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] entered the contest as a critic of city leadership following the fire, and his campaign's national profile derived in part from that criticism. Public dissatisfaction with the city government's handling of the disaster, alongside homelessness and crime, formed the core of his message against incumbent Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]].
