# Mark of the Beast

The **Mark of the Beast**, described in Revelation 13 as a mark stamped or imprinted on the forehead or hand without which no one may buy or sell, is treated in the Badlands Media essay "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?" as the defining action of [[the-beast|the Beast]].[1]

## What makes the mark unique

The author finds the physical aspect the least interesting part, since technologies capable of marking a forehead or hand — tattoos, for example — already existed when the prophecies were written.[1] Two distinctions matter instead: the Beast requires the mark from everyone, and the mark functions as a discriminator governing a person's ability to participate in the economy.[1] The analytical question then becomes how the Beast could leverage that discriminator, promote fear, and use coercion to cause people to receive it.[1]

## Candidate: vaccine passports

Measured against those criteria, the author judges that the COVID-19 vaccine campaign "wanted to win" — it was global, forced, fear- and coercion-driven, and carried an economic discriminator in the form of vaccine passports — but that its intended targets, universal vaccination and passport-based control, were not reached.[1] He calls it a close shave.[1]

## Candidate: CBDCs and digital identification

The second candidate arises from the highly digital, interconnected, and global nature of modern society: with the internet, computers, smartphones, satellites, and artificial intelligence, it is now possible to surveil, centralise, and control entire populations in ways impossible even decades earlier.[1] The article quotes a source predicting that "not too far in the future, all 'global citizens' are required to possess proper 'digital identification' or else they will not be permitted to access the new global digital financial system," with [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]] rolled out worldwide and usable only by proving identity through that new global digital ID system.[1]

Restated, the author argues there are actions underway to implement a system of total control and enslavement through CBDCs, and notes that General Michael Flynn has highlighted data on CBDCs and has referred to them as the Mark of the Beast.[1]

## Stakes

The essay's closing argument holds that the [[millennial-reign|Millennial Reign]], whenever and however established, is reserved for those who do not worship the Beast, do not worship his image — the god-kings aligned with Satan — and do not receive the mark.[1] Enduring [[the-tribulation|the Tribulation]] and resisting the mark is, in the author's revised reading, what [[the-rapture|the rapture]] actually consists of.[1]
