# Mark of the Beast

The Mark of the Beast is the mark described in Revelation 13 as being imposed on the forehead or hand and required for participation in commerce. In the Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]] it is treated as the defining action of [[the-beast|the Beast]] and is examined for candidate real-world fulfilments.

## What makes it unique

Patriots in Progress argues that the physical aspect of the Mark — being stamped or imprinted on forehead or hand — is the least interesting part, since technologies capable of that, such as tattoos, already existed when the prophecy was written. The distinguishing features, he writes, are that the Beast requires the Mark from *everyone*, and that it functions as a discriminator on a person's ability to participate in the economy, to buy or sell. The relevant question therefore becomes how the Beast could leverage that discriminator, promote fear and use coercion to "cause" people to receive it.

## Candidate fulfilments

The first candidate considered is the [[covid-19-vaccines|Covid vaccine]] campaign, which the author says "wanted to win": it was global, forced, driven by fear and coercion, and tied to vaccine passports as an economic discriminator. It failed, in his account, because the intended targets — universal vaccination with control via passports — were not reached. "I'd say it was a close shave!"

The second and stronger candidate is [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]]. Citing a source describing a near future in which "all 'global citizens' are required to possess proper 'digital identification' or else they will not be permitted to access the new global digital financial system," the article frames CBDCs plus global digital ID as a system of total control and enslavement. It notes that General Michael Flynn has highlighted data on CBDCs and has referred to them explicitly as the Mark of the Beast, and concludes: "Ready or not, they are here!" The prerequisite conditions, the author observes, are made newly possible by a highly digital, interconnected and global society equipped with the internet, computers, smartphones, satellites and AI.
