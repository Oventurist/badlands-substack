# Global Cartel

**Global Cartel** is the term used in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary for what its writers describe as a single transnational structure joining narcotics and human-trafficking organisations, financial intermediaries and energy interests across multiple continents. In this usage the word "cartel" is not restricted to the Latin American drug organisations from which it is borrowed; it denotes the whole apparatus through which illicit revenue is generated, laundered and converted into political influence.

## Use in Badlands analysis

The concept receives its most explicit statement in [[badlands-blitz-episode-15|Badlands Blitz Episode 15]], published May 2, 2026 and subtitled "Dissolution of the Global Cartel." The announcement for that episode describes the hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] tying "the Actual and Narrative threads together between the Middle East, South America and the Global Cartel we believe is being dismantled in plain sight on kinetic, financial and energy grounds."

Three features of that formulation are characteristic. First, the cartel is treated as geographically integrated: events in the Middle East, including the confrontation with [[iran|Iran]], and events in South America are presented as fronts in one campaign rather than unrelated regional crises. Second, its dismantling is said to proceed along three simultaneous axes — kinetic (military and law-enforcement action), financial (sanctions, seizures and pressure on payment channels) and energy (control of oil and gas flows and the revenues they carry). Third, the process is described as occurring "in plain sight," meaning that the public record is held to contain the evidence of it even where official narration does not acknowledge it.

## Relationship to other Badlands concepts

The Global Cartel functions in the corpus as a counterpart to related umbrella terms for a hostile transnational power structure, and its treatment relies on the standard Badlands separation between an "Actual" sequence of events and the "Narrative" layer constructed around them. Coverage of the theme appears alongside the program's arguments about the war-powers dispute between the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[congress|United States Congress]], with the implication that formal debates over whether a war is underway obscure a broader campaign already in progress.
