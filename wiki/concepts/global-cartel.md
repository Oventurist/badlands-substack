# Global Cartel

The **Global Cartel** is the term used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators for what they describe as a transnational criminal-financial network operating across the Middle East and South America, which they believe is being dismantled in plain sight on kinetic, financial and energy grounds.[1]

## Usage in The Blitz

The concept supplied the subtitle of the fifteenth episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], "Dissolution of the Global Cartel," published on 2 May 2026.[1] Hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented the dismantling of the cartel as the "real meat" of the episode, arguing that it is best understood by tying together the "Actual" and "Narrative" threads running between the Middle East and South America.[1]

## Three grounds of dismantlement

In the Badlands framing, the cartel is being taken apart along three simultaneous lines of effort: kinetic operations, financial pressure, and energy leverage.[1] The hosts treated the concurrent dispute between the [[trump-administration|Trump Administration]] and [[us-congress|Congress]] over the [[iran|Iran]] War as an entry point into the question of when a war is and is not happening at the same time — a framing consistent with their view that visible conflict and covert dismantlement can proceed together.[1] The episode concluded that "there is a plan, all right, and it seems to be coming together quite splendidly."[1]
