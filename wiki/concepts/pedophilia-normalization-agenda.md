# Pedophilia Normalization Agenda

The **pedophilia normalization agenda** is a term used within independent and populist-right media to describe an alleged coordinated effort by international institutions, academic bodies and education systems to reframe adult sexual contact with minors as a matter of rights and consent rather than criminal abuse.[1]

## The 2023 UN report claim

The concept received renewed attention in April 2023, when attorney [[elizabeth-yore|Elizabeth Yore]] told [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s [[war-room|War Room]] that the [[united-nations|United Nations]] was promoting a report recommending the normalization and decriminalization of pedophilia.[1] Yore stated that the UN was "declaring that pedophilia is a human right" that "should not be criminalized or prosecuted," and that the document implied minors are capable of consenting to sex with adults.[1] She described the approach as "softening the target" through the grooming of children and teachers in schools, and called the report unprecedented.[1] The claim was circulated by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], which framed it as grounds for the United States to withdraw from and defund the UN.[1]

## Commentary and asserted linkages

In accompanying commentary for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that child sexual abuse is far more prevalent than acknowledged, citing expert estimates that only about 10 percent of cases are ever reported.[1] He contended that the transgender youth movement functions as a component of the same normalization project, reasoning that if children are deemed competent to consent to irreversible medical interventions, the argument that they can consent to sex with adults follows.[1] He also pointed to sexual education curricula delivered to children as young as five or six as evidence of the same trajectory.[1]

Deschamps expressed skepticism that the effort would ultimately succeed, given widespread public opposition to what he termed the trans grooming issue, but warned that the existing situation was already dire and could deteriorate further if left unopposed.[1]
