# Golden Dome

Golden Dome is the proposed American missile-defense system associated with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], criticized by Russia and China in the May 2026 Multipolar World Declaration signed in Beijing.

## International criticism

The joint declaration signed by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] condemned US missile defense initiatives, including Trump's proposed Golden Dome system, and objected to the expiration of key nuclear arms limitations between Washington and Moscow. The criticism framed US missile defense as a destabilizing element of American foreign and nuclear policy.

## Context

The Golden Dome proposal appears in the Badlands corpus against the backdrop of the Iran war, during which the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] assessed it had expended more than 200 THAAD interceptors — roughly half the American inventory — along with large numbers of SM-3 and SM-6 naval missiles defending against Iranian attacks, a conflict that critics said exposed the limits of Western missile-defense production capacity.
