# Golden Dome

Golden Dome is a proposed United States missile-defense initiative advanced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] In their May 2026 Beijing summit declaration, [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] specifically criticized US missile-defense initiatives including the Golden Dome system, alongside objections to the expiration of key nuclear arms limitations between Washington and Moscow.[1]

The criticism formed part of a broader joint statement condemning "hegemonism" and calling for a [[multipolar-world|multipolar world]] less dominated by the United States and Western alliances.[1]

## Proposed expansion into Greenland (January 2026)

RT reported in January 2026 that the framework deal between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[nato|NATO]] Secretary General [[mark-rutte|Mark Rutte]] over [[greenland|Greenland]] includes language expanding the Golden Dome missile defense system into the island.[2] The same proposal reportedly allows the United States to mine rare-earth minerals in parts of Greenland without seeking permits and updates the 1951 [[greenland-defense-agreement|Greenland Defense Agreement]] with [[denmark|Denmark]].[2] Trump characterized the arrangement as one that "gets everything we wanted, including especially real national security and international security."[2]

## Greenland as a Golden Dome outpost (January 2026)

In January 2026, ahead of a White House meeting with Danish and Greenlandic officials, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] advanced a new rationale for acquiring [[greenland|Greenland]]: that the island would serve as a strategic outpost for the Golden Dome missile defense array.[3] Badlands analyst [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] questioned whether missile defense was a realistic priority but saw value in deploying the argument to hawkish factions in Washington.[3]

## Priority status during shutdown planning

The Golden Dome initiative was named among the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s highest priorities in the Department of War's September 2025 [[pentagon-shutdown-contingency-guidance-2025|shutdown contingency guidance]], alongside securing the US southern border, supporting Middle East operations, and maintaining depot work, shipbuilding, and critical munitions.[4]
