# Shark Fin Template

The "Shark Fin Template" is an interpretive framework used within [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary to describe how certain foundational subjects in the [[information-war|Information War]] behave in public consciousness. The template posits that some of the most important such subjects hover just below the surface of conscious thought within the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] — visible only as a fin above the waterline — even though the independent-media community has ventured down those rabbit holes for years and with frequency.[1]

## Application

The framework was articulated by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] in October 2023 in connection with the [[2023-russian-nationwide-nuclear-drill|first-ever Russian nationwide nuclear drill]].[1] In that commentary, two examples were given of subjects fitting the template: the "crimes against children" truths said to nest at the heart of the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]], and the [[nuclear-scare-event|Scare Event]], which many anons believe will be fulfilled on a narrative level through the threat of a nuclear strike amid escalating global tensions.[1]

A corollary of the template is that narrative seeding for such subjects is international rather than merely Western, because the engineered fear underlying a scare event must be near ubiquitous for the resolving story — in this case the emergence of [[donald-trump|Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] as peacemakers — to imprint on the largest possible audience.[1]

## Application to the Diddy investigation

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked the template in March 2024 to describe the federal raids on [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]], writing that "the Shark Fin Template strikes again where it concerns the Darkest Path in the Info War" — the continued exposure of coordinated, systemic sex trafficking by elites.[2] He argued that although the rollout can frustrate truthers, the darkest path never fully disappears from the Mind War and returns "with a vengeance," sweeping normies into the drama with unusual intensity.[2]
