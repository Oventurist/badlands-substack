# Egypt–Israel Peace Treaty

The Egypt–Israel peace treaty of 1979 is the agreement that has maintained peace between [[egypt|Egypt]] and [[israel|Israel]] since its signing and that governs, among other things, the permitted military postures of both states along their shared frontier.[1]

In February 2024, with Israel on the verge of invading Gaza's southernmost city, Egypt warned that such a move could trigger a suspension of the treaty.[1] The warning centered on the [[philadelphi-corridor|Philadelphi Corridor]], where the accompanying diplomatic accords cap troop numbers in delineated zones and do not permit large Israeli armored deployments.[1]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the episode as part of a wider pattern in which Israel's growing isolation — condemnation from the US State Department, the EU, Germany and the UK, plus Saudi and Egyptian anger — placed long-standing regional treaties under threat.[1]
