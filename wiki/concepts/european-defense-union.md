# European Defense Union

The European Defense Union is the proposed transformation of the [[european-union|European Union]] into an integrated defense bloc, set out by [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]] in the political program she published through the [[european-commission|European Commission]] in July 2024 as she sought re-election as Commission president.[1]

## The proposal

The program stated that "our work in the next five years will be focused on building a true European Defense Union."[1] Von der Leyen added that EU member states "will always retain responsibility for their own troops, from doctrine to deployment, but there is a lot Europe can do to support and coordinate efforts to strengthen the defense industrial base, innovation and the Single Market."[1] She also announced plans to appoint a European commissioner for defense issues, writing that "to help coordinate this work at the European level, I will appoint a Commissioner for Defense, who will work closely with the next High Representative/Vice-President in accordance with the Treaty."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued the move made sense given his contention that the founding documents of the European Union — created in 1993 — were "basically photocopies of the Third Reich's plans for post-war Europe, had the Nazis won WW2," adding that he was not convinced the Nazis had genuinely lost.[1] He described a continental army for Europe as a long-speculated but "despicable and draconian" concept: a transnational military force empowered to impose itself on individual nation-states.[1]

Characterizing the initiative as "one small step for Europe, one giant step for globalism," he linked it to reporting that at the preceding [[nato|NATO]] summit, [[pentagon|Pentagon]] officials had promised weapons manufacturers that NATO would ramp up orders for bombs and weapons for many years to come.[1] He named [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orban]], [[nigel-farage|Nigel Farage]], and [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] among the European leaders resisting the trend, concluding that "the War of Sovereignty must be won."[1]
