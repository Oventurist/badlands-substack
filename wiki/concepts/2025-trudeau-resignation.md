# 2025 Trudeau Resignation

On Monday, January 6, 2025, Canadian Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] announced his resignation as leader of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party]] in the face of rising discontent over his leadership and following the abrupt departure of his finance minister, which had signaled growing turmoil within his government.[1]

## The announcement

Trudeau said that "internal battles" meant he "cannot be the best option" in the next election, and planned to stay on as prime minister until a new Liberal Party leader was chosen. An official familiar with the matter said Parliament, which had been due to resume on January 27, would be suspended until March 24, allowing time for a Liberal leadership race. All three main opposition parties said they planned to topple the Liberal Party in a no-confidence vote when Parliament resumed, making a spring election to pick a permanent replacement almost assured.[1]
