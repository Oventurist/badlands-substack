# British Israelism

British Israelism is the belief, held by many of the "apostolic" cult creators described in the article, that the ten lost Tribes of Israel actually settled in Britain and that the British Royal Family is directly descended from the House of David — specifically from the supposed "secret children" of Jesus and Mary Magdalene.[1]

## Popular culture

The article argues these were genuine beliefs of the oligarchy that were converted into popular mythology through works such as Martin Scorsese's film *The Last Temptation of Christ* and the book *Holy Blood Holy Grail*.[1] It notes that Michael Baigent, co-author of *Holy Blood Holy Grail* — which informed Dan Brown's *The Da Vinci Code* — was also a member of the [[quatuor-coronati-lodge]].[1]

## Royal symbolism

The article contends that most people watching [[king-charles-iii]] being sprinkled with water from the River Jordan at his coronation had no idea of the symbolism involved: in the mind of Charles and the oligarchy he represents, the ritual marks him as blood heir to the throne of Christ.[1] It also points to his carrying of a metallic globe and cross, symbolizing a divine right to rule the entire globe as *prima inter pares* — a symbol of the Holy Roman Emperor.[1] The article further asks why [[pope-francis]] gave shards of the cross on which Jesus is said to have died as a coronation gift to a British Israelite who "probably believes himself to be a blood heir to Jesus himself."[1]

[[henry-drummond]] is identified as a British Israelite who in 1834 lamented that most of the religious world disbelieved that the Jews were to be restored to their own land and that Christ would return to reign in person on earth.[1]
