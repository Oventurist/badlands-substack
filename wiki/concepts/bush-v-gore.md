# Bush v. Gore

Bush v. Gore is the disputed 2000 United States presidential election contest cited by the [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]] as historical precedent for its 2020 election war-gaming.[1]

## Use by TIP

TIP stated that its assessments — that the 2020 election would be marked by a chaotic legal and political landscape and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would likely contest the results by legal and extra-legal means to hold onto power — were bolstered by the historical experience of Bush v. Gore in 2000 and other electoral dysfunctions.[1]

## Criticism of the precedent

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identifies this reliance as a contradiction in TIP's reasoning: the group dismissed Trump's concerns about election fraud as preposterous on the grounds that fraud is virtually non-existent, while simultaneously grounding its own predictions in an election that it treats as having been stolen, with Bush and his associates acting illegally to obtain the presidency.[1] Carlson concludes that arguing fraud is non-existent out of one side of the mouth while basing forecasts on past fraud out of the other makes no sense.[1]
