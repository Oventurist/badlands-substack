# GART 9 Deadwood

**GART 9 Deadwood** was the ninth installment of the Global Awakening Roundtable (GART) conference series hosted by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in Deadwood, South Dakota in 2025.[1]

## Virtual pass and livestreams

Badlands offered a virtual ticket giving remote attendees access to the GART conference, pre-livestreams, a Telegram chat with behind-the-scenes access, and an early screening of [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s documentary [[codex-911|Codex 9/11]].[1] An exclusive livestream for GART ticket holders was announced for Thursday, July 24, 2025, at 2 p.m. ET, billed as the first GART 9 ticket-holder livestream.[1]

## Codex 9/11 premiere

The documentary CODEX 911 premiered at GART Deadwood.[1] In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 23, 2025, Badlands Media urged readers to read Zerbo's personal feature "September 11th 2001," describing it as the filmmaker's back story and the source of his personal inspiration for the film.[1]

## Promotion in the Badlands News Brief

Badlands Media promoted GART 9 in its August 13, 2025 news brief, announcing that the organization was headed to Deadwood for the event and that [[codex-911|CODEX 9/11]] would premiere that Saturday.[2] The promotion advertised a movie premiere, exclusive streams, a GART Telegram chat, and panel and behind-the-scenes content from Deadwood, and directed readers to purchase a virtual ticket.[2]

## Announcement and ticketing

GART 9 was announced on July 7, 2025 by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who noted that the event fell in the month following the announcement and coincided with his own birthday.[3] It was billed as the ninth stop on the Great American Restoration Tour and the third GART "of the new American Golden Age," as well as the network's second event held close to the actual Badlands region.[3] Badlands' earlier 2024 stop in [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood]] had been described as a favourite among both hosts and attendees, and the organisers said they expected the 2025 return to be no different.[3]

The programme was expected to revert to the panel format used at the previous Deadwood event, after other formats had been trialled at intervening stops, with three-to-four-host panels on macro topics.[3] VIP tickets had sold out by the time of the announcement, while full-weekend General Admission tickets — including the Friday-night welcome reception and three days of panels — remained on sale through the network's TicketSpice page.[3]

## Virtual ticket package

Virtual tickets allowed purchasers to stream the entirety of the event, including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and included long-term DVR access to panel replays after the weekend so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss out.[3] The virtual package also carried access to an exclusive GART [[telegram]] chat, with links distributed in the month of the event, where holders could interact with other Badlanders and with Badlands hosts and view behind-the-scenes photographs and video.[3] Badlands said these Telegram groups typically became long-lived chat rooms for its most committed audience members, continuing to thrive for months after each event and serving as a place to follow the GART action and to "document the journey" to the next one.[3]

## Final ticket push (August 2025)

On August 13, 2025, days before the event, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] published a "last call" notice announcing that single-day and virtual tickets were on sale.[4] The post described the gathering as the ninth stop along the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the third GART "of the new American Golden Age," and the network's second event held not far from the Badlands region that gave the company its name.[4] Burning Bright characterised the events as an opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots and as "an awakening pressure cooker" in which a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building took place.[4]

VIP and full-weekend General Admission tickets remained available at that point, and a limited number of single-day tickets were released for attendees near the area who wished to attend on one or two of Friday, Saturday or Sunday.[4] In-person tickets were sold through the network's TicketSpice page, with a separate page for virtual tickets.[4] The post reiterated that virtual buyers received streaming access to all three days of panels, long-term DVR access to replays, and entry to the exclusive GART Telegram chat with hundreds of fellow Badlanders and Badlands hosts, behind-the-scenes photos and video.[4] It also emphasised that GART 9 was particularly unique because the virtual ticket included access to the Saturday premiere of [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s feature-length documentary [[codex-911|Codex 9/11]].[4] A full schedule graphic accompanied the announcement, which closed by saying the Badlands Media team hoped to see familiar faces and, as at every prior GART, plenty of new ones.[4]
