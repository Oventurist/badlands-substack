# GART 9 Deadwood

**GART 9 Deadwood** was the ninth installment of the Global Awakening Roundtable (GART) conference series hosted by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in Deadwood, South Dakota in 2025.[1]

## Virtual pass and livestreams

Badlands offered a virtual ticket giving remote attendees access to the GART conference, pre-livestreams, a Telegram chat with behind-the-scenes access, and an early screening of [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s documentary [[codex-911|Codex 9/11]].[1] An exclusive livestream for GART ticket holders was announced for Thursday, July 24, 2025, at 2 p.m. ET, billed as the first GART 9 ticket-holder livestream.[1]

## Codex 9/11 premiere

The documentary CODEX 911 premiered at GART Deadwood.[1] In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 23, 2025, Badlands Media urged readers to read Zerbo's personal feature "September 11th 2001," describing it as the filmmaker's back story and the source of his personal inspiration for the film.[1]

## Promotion in the Badlands News Brief

Badlands Media promoted GART 9 in its August 13, 2025 news brief, announcing that the organization was headed to Deadwood for the event and that [[codex-911|CODEX 9/11]] would premiere that Saturday.[2] The promotion advertised a movie premiere, exclusive streams, a GART Telegram chat, and panel and behind-the-scenes content from Deadwood, and directed readers to purchase a virtual ticket.[2]
