# Louisiana Territory

The Louisiana Territory was the vast landmass sold by Napoleon to the United States in 1804, an event that [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes as a surprise to all parties — including the Americans — which threw a large wrench into British plans to take control of the land and again suffocate the USA as a closed system locked onto the Atlantic coast, as had been attempted earlier with the 1774 Quebec Act.[1]

The territory immediately became the object of the western phase of the [[burr-conspiracy|Burr conspiracy]].[1] [[aaron-burr|Aaron Burr]] persuaded President [[thomas-jefferson|Thomas Jefferson]] to place his co-conspirators in powerful positions there, with Col. [[james-wilkinson|James Wilkinson]] appointed Governor.[1] The plan called for British soldiers and American mercenaries under Burr's employ to take full control of Louisiana and New Orleans, expand the territory by declaring war on Spain, and then depose the sitting president so that Burr could be established as monarch of a new British American confederation.[1]
