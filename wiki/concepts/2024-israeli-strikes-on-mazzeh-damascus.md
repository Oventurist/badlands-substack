# 2024 Israeli Strikes on Mazzeh, Damascus

In November 2024, Israel carried out a series of air attacks on the Mazzeh suburb of Damascus, according to the Syrian state news agency SANA.[1]

## The strikes

SANA reported on a Friday that "Israeli aggression targets Mazzeh area in Damascus," giving no further detail, after explosions were heard earlier that day in the vicinity of the capital.[1] The attack came a day after a wave of deadly strikes on what Israel described as militant targets in the Syrian capital; fifteen people were killed on the Thursday in strikes on residential buildings in the Mazzeh and Qudsaya suburbs, according to state media.[1] Israel said those attacks targeted military sites and the headquarters of [[palestinian-islamic-jihad|Islamic Jihad]], and offered no immediate comment on the Friday strikes.[1]

## Mazzeh as a target

Commanders of Lebanon's [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] and of Iran's Revolutionary Guards based in Syria have been known to reside in Mazzeh, according to residents who fled after earlier strikes killed key figures in those groups.[1] The suburb's high-rise blocks have also been used by Syrian authorities to house leaders of Palestinian factions including [[hamas|Hamas]] and Islamic Jihad.[1]

Israel has struck Iran-linked targets in Syria for years but escalated such raids after the October 7, 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack on Israel that touched off the Gaza war.[1]
