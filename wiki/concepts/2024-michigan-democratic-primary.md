# 2024 Michigan Democratic Primary

The 2024 Michigan Democratic presidential primary, held February 27, 2024, became a focal point of intra-party dissent over President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of the Israel–Gaza war when more than 100,000 Democrats cast ballots for "uncommitted."[1]

## Results

With more than 95% of votes counted, "uncommitted" accounted for 13.3% of the statewide total, against 81.1% for Biden.[1] [[marianne-williamson|Marianne Williamson]], who had suspended her campaign three weeks earlier, edged the still-campaigning Representative [[dean-phillips|Dean Phillips]] 3.0% to 2.7%.[1] In Wayne County — home to Dearborn and its large Muslim population — 21% voted uncommitted with 75% of the count completed.[1]

## Significance

The uncommitted total was more than nine times [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 10,704-vote margin over Hillary Clinton in Michigan in 2016, in a swing state worth 15 electoral votes in the November general election.[1] Observers noted that the 100,000-voter coalition turned out for a primary whose outcome was a foregone conclusion, suggesting many more shared the sentiment without bothering to vote.[1]

Michigan has one of the largest Muslim populations in the United States, and community leaders angered by Biden's lopsided support for Israel and his failure to restrain an Israel Defense Forces campaign that had caused tens of thousands of civilian casualties promoted a nationwide [[abandon-biden|Abandon Biden]] campaign.[1] An allied effort under the banner of [[listen-to-michigan|Listen to Michigan]] was championed by Michigan Representative [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]].[1] The result is generally treated as the signature event of the broader [[uncommitted-movement|uncommitted movement]].[1]
