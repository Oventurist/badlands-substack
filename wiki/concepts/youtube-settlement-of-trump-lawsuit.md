# YouTube Settlement of Trump Lawsuit

In September 2025 [[youtube|YouTube]] joined the list of technology and media companies to settle a lawsuit brought by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after the start of his second term.[1]

## Terms

The Alphabet-owned company agreed to pay $24.5 million to resolve litigation over the suspension of Trump's account following the attack on the US Capitol by a mob of his supporters in 2021, according to court papers filed on Monday 29 September 2025.[1] Under the deal, $22 million is directed to a trust for the National Mall, the entity handling construction of a $200 million [[white-house-ballroom|ballroom at the White House]].[1] The remainder is earmarked for other plaintiffs in the case, including the American Conservative Union, Andrew Baggiani, Austen Fletcher, Maryse Veronica Jean-Louis, Frank Valentine, Kelly Victory, and Naomi Wolf.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] responded to the settlement with a single line — "Cool. When do the rest of us get paid?" — registering the view that the rank-and-file users censored during the same period received no comparable remedy.[1]
