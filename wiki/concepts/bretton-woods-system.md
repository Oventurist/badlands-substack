# Bretton Woods System

The Bretton Woods System is the postwar international monetary framework created in 1944 and brought online alongside the [[united-nations|United Nations]] after the Second World War.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Badlands Media commentary presents its original design as a model for the kind of emergency financial reorganisation the author believes is required today.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Original design

At Bretton Woods a new financial system was created in two weeks, founded upon principles of banking that demanded fixed exchange rates in order to block unbounded speculation — a practice the author describes as having been used as economic warfare against poor nations for centuries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) It was premised originally on the emission of long-term credit for large-scale projects, effectively internationalizing the New Deal in an age envisioned as one of multipolar cooperation and win-win development.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Capture and revival

The article states that the Bretton Woods institutions such as the IMF and World Bank — and the US government itself — were hijacked by deep state operatives representing the worst oligarchical inclinations of humanity in the post-World War II years.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Nevertheless, the author argues, the noble origins of these institutions should not be ignored, noting that [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] and, to a lesser but relevant extent, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attempted to revive them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Contemporary echo

The productive financial architecture of China and its allies is described as having already unleashed over $3 trillion in large-scale infrastructure investment through the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], tied increasingly to the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]] and a new financial architecture founded on real-world values as outlined by [[sergey-glazyev|Sergey Glazyev]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)
