# Bretton Woods System

The Bretton Woods system is the post-Second World War international financial order created alongside the [[united-nations|United Nations]] as a framework intended to prevent renewed war and economic collapse.[1]

## Original design

At Bretton Woods a new financial system was created in two weeks, founded on principles of banking that demanded fixed exchange rates in order to block unbounded speculation, which the article describes as having been used as economic warfare against poor nations for centuries.[1] It was premised originally on emitting long-term credit for large-scale projects, so as to internationalize the New Deal in what was envisioned as an era of multipolar cooperation and win-win development.[1] Its spirit is linked to the Atlantic Charter, the UN Charter and Franklin Roosevelt's [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]].[1]

## Capture and revival

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues that the Bretton Woods institutions — the IMF and World Bank, as well as the US government itself — were hijacked in the post-war years by deep state operatives representing the worst oligarchical inclinations of humanity.[1] Nevertheless, he writes, the noble origins of these institutions, which John F. Kennedy and, to a lesser but relevant extent, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attempted to revive, should not be ignored.[1]

The article asks why a similar conference bringing together diverse national representatives could not be arranged today, and answers that the discussion has already begun among leaders representing the majority of the world's population, as seen at the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|SCO]] summit in Tianjin.[1]
