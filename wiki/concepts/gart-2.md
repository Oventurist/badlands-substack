# GART 2

GART 2 was the second installment of the Great Awakening Round Table live event series hosted by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in Cocoa Beach, Florida, and streamed in its entirety.[1]

## The event

Badlands described GART 2 as an occasion where "Badlanders" from across the United States and beyond gathered for a weekend of discernment, hope and (mostly) intentional entertainment.[1] Following the event, replays of the full stream were made available through the Badlands events page.[1]

## Announcement and promotion

Badlands Media issued a "last call" for GART 2 on August 28, 2023, confirming that the event would run from September 7 to 10, 2023 in Cocoa Beach, Florida, with the conference itself hosted at the [[hilton-cocoa-beach-oceanfront|Hilton Cocoa Beach Oceanfront]].[2] The promotion framed the tour as an extension of the slogan "We are the news, now," a saying the network said had come to define a growing movement within the Truth and America First communities.[2] Badlands credited reader support with its growth into what it called one of the most-watched independent networks online, carrying dozens of citizen journalists, podcasters and personalities across its shows.[2]

The event was billed as a live, ticketed version of the network's output, featuring in-person editions of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] shows, live Q&A sessions with Badlands panels, and meet-and-greets with show hosts throughout the weekend.[2] General Admission tickets included a Friday night Welcome Reception with Badlands Media, breakfast and lunch across the weekend, and open socializing with hosts and other "Badlanders" as attendees sought to "level up in the [[information-war|Information War]] together."[2] By the time of the last-call post the VIP package had sold out, though single-day tickets had been released for those wanting to attend a single day of panels, food and socializing.[2] Speakers were listed on the event poster rather than in the announcement text.[2]

## Virtual attendance

Alongside in-person admission, Badlands sold Virtual Tickets allowing purchasers to stream the entirety of GART 2 from home.[2] The network noted that the first tour stop had been "a great success on the digital front" and suggested that watching the stream alongside fellow Badlanders was a way to feel part of the action and to gauge whether in-person attendance was worthwhile in future.[2] Every purchase also carried an invitation to a live [[telegram|Telegram]] chat with other GART 2 attendees and virtual stream viewers, which organizers said would continue long after the event concluded.[2] Tickets were sold through the Badlands Media events page and a TicketSpice checkout.[2]

## Sequel

The announcement of GART 2 replays in September 2023 coincided with the opening of ticket sales for [[gart-3|GART 3]], scheduled for Irvine, California.[1] Badlands framed both events as milestones in the organization's continued evolution and thanked supporters for enabling in-person gatherings of the [[truth-community|Truth Community]].[1]
