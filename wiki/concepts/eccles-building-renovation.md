# Eccles Building Renovation

The Eccles Building renovation is the $2.5 billion overhaul of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]'s headquarters in Washington, D.C., which became the focus of a White House oversight fight in July 2025.[1]

## OMB criticism

In a letter obtained by the [[new-york-post|New York Post]], [[office-of-management-and-budget|Office of Management and Budget]] Director [[russ-vought|Russell T. Vought]] attacked Fed Chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] over the project, which had already run $700 million over budget according to public disclosures, and warned that construction could be halted.[1]

## Background

In April 2025 the Post had exclusively reported that the Fed was pushing ahead with the overhaul despite mounting losses at the central bank, prompting criticism that likened its top officials to French royalty seeking to build a "Palace of Versailles."[1]
