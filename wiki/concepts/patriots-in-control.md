# Patriots in Control

"Patriots in control" is a framework, recurrent in Badlands Media commentary, holding that despite outward appearances of [[deep-state|Deep State]] dominance, a countervailing faction aligned with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has retained decisive control of events.[1]

## Core claims

Writing in September 2024, Erik Carlson describes himself as "a believer in patriots in control."[1] In his account, Trump handpicked the opponents he faced in 2020 — [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] — and allowed the election to be stolen because there were not yet enough awakened Americans to complete the necessary work across consecutive presidential terms, and because much of the heavy lifting Trump wanted done was better carried out on someone else's watch.[1] Much of what occurred during Biden's presidency, on this reading, functioned to shift public opinion and set the table for a subsequent Trump term.[1]

The framework extends to personnel: Carlson argues Harris is Vice President because Trump wants her to be, is running against Trump because he wants her to be, and Biden remains President because Trump wants him to remain.[1] He speculates that Trump may have known of disqualifying facts about Harris — citizenship, drugs and alcohol, or something else — that would prevent her from succeeding to the presidency, thereby forcing the Deep State to keep Biden in place for the full term even as public confidence in the Democratic Party eroded.[1] Under that logic, if Harris replaced Biden and could not legally serve, Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] would become acting President, placing Republicans in control of the White House during the election and the January transition — a worst-case scenario for the Cabal.[1] Carlson further asks whether [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] was installed as Speaker to give the Deep State a false sense of security, with Johnson's replacement of him a year before the election serving as a signal of who was really in charge.[1]

## Function in the argument

The framework is used to bound expected damage. Carlson expresses hope that patriot control will keep the Deep State from doing too much damage on its way out, and that as with the preceding three and a half years most of the damage will prove reversible — designed to create chaos and fear as psychological warfare rather than to inflict permanent harm.[1] He envisions a temporary stock market crash, another fake pandemic, a media blackout, or rioting in blue cities, while maintaining that none of it would be real or long lasting.[1] The stance is explicitly anti-blackpill: he asks whether those trying to blackpill readers have ever been right about anything, and notes that his hope has repeatedly been vindicated by later facts.[1]
