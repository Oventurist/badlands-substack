# Israeli Deep State

The "Israeli Deep State" is a framing used in Badlands Media commentary holding that [[israel|Israel]], like the United States, contains both an entrenched unaccountable faction — a [[deep-state|Deep State]] — and a countervailing body of patriots, rather than functioning as a single unified actor.[1]

## The argument

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that the world is not as black and white as commentators would like it to be: while it is obvious to his readership that the United States has both a Deep State and patriots, he held that the same is true of Russia, China, Israel and [[iran|Iran]], each of which contains "a French Resistance, if you will."[1] On this reading the Deep States of ostensibly hostile nations are controlled by the same puppet masters, making apparent enmity between them a performance rather than a genuine rivalry.[1]

Carlson extended the argument to [[qanon|Q]] drops stating that after North Korea had its [[cia|CIA]] strings cut, Iran would be next, and that Israel was "being saved for last."[1] The common reading of the latter — that Israel's control over the United States would be severed — was, he suggested, possibly backwards: it may instead mean that the same people who controlled [[north-korea|North Korea]] and still control Iran will be removed from Israel, freeing the Israeli people.[1]

## Evidence from Netanyahu's 2024 visit

Carlson treated the reception of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] during his July 2024 trip to Washington as evidence against the simpler claim that Israel controls the entire U.S. government.[1] Roughly 100 members of the House and 28 senators skipped [[2024-netanyahu-address-to-congress|his address to Congress]]; [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] arranged to be out of town and afterwards pressed him over the humanitarian crisis in Gaza; and [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] called the address "by far the worst presentation of any foreign dignitary" ever invited to address Congress.[1] Carlson read the split — 44 standing ovations from those present, mass absence from the rest — as proof only that [[congress|Congress]] and the [[us-senate|Senate]] are not in lockstep, and not a party-line divide but "something more."[1] He posed the open question of whether the ovation-givers or the absentees are the politicians controlled by Israel's Deep State, and whether Netanyahu himself works for or against it — allowing the possibility that he once served it and no longer does.[1]

## Forced collision thesis

Carlson speculated that if Israel's Deep State were forced into a war against Iran's Deep State, the two would be compelled either to destroy each other or to reveal, by refusing, that they are actors on a shared stage and answer to the same controllers.[1] Framing this as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] calling the Deep State's bluff, he concluded that in neither branch can they win: "in one scenario, they destroy each other; in the other, the whole system is exposed."[1]
