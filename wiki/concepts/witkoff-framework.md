# Witkoff Framework

The "Witkoff framework" is the ceasefire-and-hostage proposal associated with U.S. envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], under which the war in the Gaza Strip would be paused for roughly 60 days in exchange for the release of about half of the hostages held by [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

## Trump's rejection of a temporary pause

During a tense 40-minute phone call with Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] on Monday, June 9, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] reportedly told the premier that the Witkoff framework "will not suffice" and that he must permanently end the war in Gaza, according to Israeli television reporting relayed by *The Times of Israel*.[1] Trump also told Netanyahu that attacking Iran was off limits for the time being.[1]

Netanyahu had to that point refused to negotiate a truce-hostage deal that would permanently end the fighting — a red line for him and his far-right coalition partners.[1] Trump reportedly argued that ending the war would assist both the administration's nuclear negotiations with Iran and normalization talks with Saudi Arabia.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributors framed the call as a "rugpull" on Netanyahu and on the [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionist]] constituency that had backed the Gaza campaign, predicting that some figures would defy Trump and continue agitating for war with Iran, and linking such advocacy to [[mossad|Mossad]] influence operations.[1]
