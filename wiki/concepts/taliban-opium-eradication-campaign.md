# Taliban Opium Eradication Campaign

The **Taliban opium eradication campaign** is the prohibition of poppy farming imposed by the [[taliban|Taliban]] government of [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]], which drastically reduced opium cultivation across a country that until recently produced roughly 90% of the world's heroin.[1] Western sources have estimated reductions of up to 99% in some provinces, an outcome that has been called "the most successful counter-narcotics effort in human history."[1]

## The ban and its implementation

The ruling Taliban government announced the prohibition of poppy farming in April of the preceding year, citing both its strong religious beliefs and the severe social costs that heroin and other poppy-derived opioids had inflicted across Afghanistan.[1] Enforcement has been conducted by teams of counter-narcotics brigades who travel the country cutting down poppy fields armed with little more than sticks.[1]

## Measured results

Research from the geospatial data company Alcis indicates that poppy production fell by around 80% within a year of the ban.[1] Satellite imagery of Helmand Province — the region that produces more than half of the national crop — showed a 99% drop, with Alcis estimating fewer than 1,000 hectares of poppy remaining in an area where poppy fields had been dominant only twelve months earlier.[1] Farmers have shifted to planting wheat, which has helped stave off the worst of a famine that U.S. sanctions helped create.[1] Afghanistan nonetheless remains in a perilous state, with the United Nations warning that six million people are close to starvation.[1]

## Implications

The speed and scale of the Taliban's success raised serious questions about the seriousness of U.S. drug eradication efforts in Afghanistan over the preceding twenty years.[1] Experts also warned MintPress News that as global heroin supplies dry up, the vacuum could accelerate use of [[fentanyl-crisis|fentanyl]] — a drug dozens of times stronger than heroin that already kills more than 100,000 Americans annually.[1] The item was carried as a bonus entry in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief.[1]
