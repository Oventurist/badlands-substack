# Communist Takeover of America

The "Communist takeover of America" is the organizing thesis of [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s June 5, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] article, which argues that a sequence of cultural, economic and technological developments constitutes a single deliberate progression toward a communist state.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The stated progression

Carlson sets out the sequence explicitly: first strip Americans of what makes them American — their American identity; then destroy American society, values and morals; then destroy the American economy; at which point "the country is ripe to be taken over by the Communists."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

He asserts that "Globalism leads to Communism," and defines Communism as a two-class society in which the elites hold total control: the elite eat meat while the working class eat bugs, the elite travel by private jet while the working class ride city buses, the elite live on estates while the working class rent apartments in smart cities.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The removal of the middle class is presented as the objective.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Mechanisms alleged

Several distinct phenomena are gathered under the thesis. Corporate "wokeness" imposed through investment-portfolio pressure and financing is one; the resulting consumer boycotts of [[americana-brands|Americana brands]], which [[andy-frisella|Andy Frisella]] argues are themselves coerced, are another.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) Tolerated mass [[retail-theft-wave|retail theft]] in liberal cities is offered as an instance no ordinary corporate motive can explain, leading Carlson to conclude the companies must have puppet masters seeking to destroy America.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The prosecution of the ex-Marine who restrained a violent man on a New York subway is read as a deterrent message: anyone who tries to stop the destruction will be punished.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) Finally, [[artificial-intelligence-manipulation|artificial intelligence]] is cast as the instrument that changed the public's morals, values and perceptions in the first place.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Call to action

Carlson concludes that Americans "have been manipulated," "fooled into believing what is important and good in life," and "convinced good is bad and bad is good," such that they are "willingly assisting in [their] own demise."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He tells those who are awake that they have been chosen to see and bear responsibility for explaining what is happening to others, argues that America cannot exist long term if divided and that conservatives, moderates and liberals are all needed, and urges forgiveness of those who ridiculed and rejected them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He closes with the warning that "if America falls, so does the world" and that a loss in this fight admits no second chance.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Communism as a virus

In his later article *A Virus, or a Metaphor?* (July 17, 2023), Carlson advances the thesis that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has placed a double meaning on the word "virus," the hidden meaning being Communism.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Communism, on this reading, has been unleashed on the United States to destroy it from within; it is not organic but a contrived plan, and just as Trump said Covid was intentionally created in a lab and released on the world, the narratives and agendas Americans now experience were essentially created in a lab and dropped on them with the intention of destroying America and the world.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

Carlson connects this to Q's repeated use of [[the-hunt-for-red-october|Red October]], which he takes as a signal that America is undergoing a Communist takeover in the manner of the far-left Bolsheviks of 1917 — taken down by narratives and agendas that divide, discourage and demoralize.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Trump's repeated and much-mocked reference to the [[spanish-flu|Spanish Flu]] "of 1917" rather than 1918 is read as pointing at the same date and the same [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik uprising]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

Under Communism, he restates, there is a ruling elite and a working-class poor with nothing in between — one is either served or server, slave or master — and anyone not classed as an "essential" worker during the [[covid-19-lockdowns|lockdowns]] may have been told, in effect, that the ruling elite has no place for them; when the Soviet Union had no place for people, they faced a firing squad.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Communism as a tool

Carlson qualifies the thesis by insisting Communism is only a tool used by the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]] to control, "like an axe that is being swung by an axe-murderer."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) The real enemy is the person swinging the axe — the Cabal, whom he identifies as modern-day Nazis — and by fighting Communism, he argues, Americans are keeping the Cabal from getting their hands on the axe and using the tool to destroy them.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Historical lineage of the plot

In a June 2023 essay Carlson traces the tool's ancestry: the Bavarian Illuminati led to [[karl-marx|Karl Marx]], Marx led to the Bolsheviks, and the Bolsheviks led to Communism, which he calls a Cabal instrument created and used to control the world and implemented slowly.[3] He argues that neither the Russians of 1917 nor the Germans who received Nazism genuinely wanted these systems; both were propagandised and then coerced, with early demonstrations staged and fake and citizens rightly afraid to resist.[3] He states that the Bolsheviks had attempted an earlier takeover of Russia that was squashed, after which many fled to Germany, where their reception progressively degraded German culture, leading to Weimar and then to the Nazis.[3] Communism, on this account, begins by eliminating God and morals from a culture.[3]

## Wokism as the American propaganda stage

America, Carlson writes, is far along in the propaganda stage of Communism, but it is being imposed under the label of "wokism" rather than its own name.[3] Americans are told that government is the only solution to their problems and that more government control is the answer; many young people embrace this, some believing every American home should be government-monitored.[3] Political correctness, which he identifies as a tried and true Communist tactic designed to take down a society from the inside through division, entered universities in the 1990s, and "wokeness" is political correctness on steroids.[3]

He argues that the transgender, gay, liberal, immigration, corruption and godlessness controversies are individually real but are pieces of a single puzzle whose picture is Communism, pushed to usher in a new totalitarian state.[3] Notably, these agendas do not benefit Wall Street: marketing to a tiny demographic cannot plausibly raise sales more than a fraction of a percent, so the actors involved are not pursuing money but "following orders … to destroy their companies," which is why the Deep State Cabal placed them in key corporate positions.[3]

## The 30-year cycles

Carlson proposes that a 30-year plan to destroy America through Communism was implemented in the 1960s, with the mid-1940s through the 1950s used to put assets in place.[3] The 1960s divided the country — black and white, men and women, young and old, conservative and liberal, war versus peace — and injected Satanism into American culture, moving it from secret societies into films, music and even board games.[3] The 1970s tore down the economy through high gas prices, inflation, interest rates and unemployment, and took the United States fully off the gold standard.[3] The 1980s were to deliver the death blow, but [[ronald-reagan|Reagan]] — "an imperfect man from California" — became a two-term president, survived an assassination attempt in his first year, made concessions to stay in office and alive, and restored the economy and national confidence, forcing the Cabal to let the exhausted [[soviet-union|Soviet Union]] fall and begin again.[3]

The 1990s repeated the 1960s pattern of division and Satanic revival, lowered moral standards from the highest office downward, squashed groups that opposed the government, and embedded political correctness in universities.[3] The 2000s brought war, control and fear — 9/11, Iraq, Afghanistan and the 2008 financial crisis — weakening the economy while increasing control over citizens.[3] The 2010s were to bring the new death blow until "another imperfect man, this time from New York," [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], ruined the plan; the Cabal must now quadruple its efforts and compress the timetable, because too many people are waking up for another 30-year cycle to work.[3]

## Post-Soviet realignment

Carlson questions why America never embraced Russia after the Soviet collapse as it embraced East Germany and the Eastern Bloc, suggesting Russia was retained as "a yin to America's yang" and held in reserve as a future villain while Muslim terrorists filled the role in the interim, with Hollywood likewise swapping the Italian Mob for the Russian Mob.[3] He notes he has heard it claimed the CIA wrote the Scorpions' "Wind of Change," which he cannot confirm.[3] Simultaneously, in the early 1990s America became a major trading and manufacturing partner of [[communist-china|Communist China]], with the Clintons of Arkansas and [[walmart|Walmart]] developing strong partnerships there, Walmart displacing small private businesses across the country, and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] later found to be selling classified information to China.[3]

Carlson attributes Trump's reticence about naming Communism to conditioning: Americans have been trained to dismiss anyone who raises Communist infiltration as a crazy conspiracy theorist or a fascist, and have been conditioned for 75 years to react to any mention of Russia with fear and paranoia.[3] He observes Trump beginning to use "Marxist" and "fascist" for the DOJ and FBI in place of "the Deep State," "the Swamp" and "Chyna," and reads Trump's nickname shift from "Sleepy Joe" to "Crooked Joe" for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] as a deliberate redirection from Biden's mental decline to his corruption.[3] Carlson concludes that the Communist plan will implode before reaching its pinnacle.[3]
