# Communist Takeover of America

The "Communist takeover of America" is the organizing thesis of [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s June 2023 Badlands essay, which holds that a sequenced program is underway to convert the United States into a two-class communist state, and that many ostensibly unrelated phenomena — corporate wokeness, tolerated retail theft, the sudden public arrival of artificial intelligence — are stages of that single program.[1]

## The stated sequence

Carlson set out the progression explicitly: first strip Americans of what makes them American, their American identity; then destroy American society, values and morals; then destroy the American economy.[1] Once all three are accomplished, the country is "ripe to be taken over by the Communists."[1]

## Globalism as the vector

Globalism, in this account, leads to communism, and communism is defined as a two-class society in which the elites hold total control.[1] Carlson illustrated the resulting stratification in concrete terms: the elite eat meat while the working class eat bugs, the elite travel by private jet while the working class ride city buses, and the elite live on estates while the working class rent apartments in smart cities.[1]

## Mechanisms

Three mechanisms are named. The [[wokeness|wokeness]] imposed on corporations is said to be a means of destroying [[americana|Americana]] brands, with consumer boycotts unwittingly completing the work — an argument Carlson credited to [[andy-frisella|Andy Frisella]].[1] The [[retail-theft-crisis|tolerated retail theft]] in liberal cities is offered as otherwise inexplicable, since it destroys corporate profits, leading Carlson to conclude the companies must have "puppet masters" pursuing a communist state.[1] And the [[artificial-intelligence-rollout|rollout of artificial intelligence]] is presented as a long-running manipulation campaign now being openly normalized.[1]

Carlson also read the punishment of resistance into the pattern, citing the ex-Marine facing possible jail time for subduing a violent man who had threatened passengers on a New York subway, which he interpreted as a clear message that anyone obstructing the destruction of the country would be punished.[1]

## Political dimension

Carlson connected the cultural program to the political one, arguing that figures including [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] and [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] — whatever their intentions — were exposing the RINOs, Neocons, the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and the Communist plot.[1]

## Call to action

The essay closes by arguing that Americans "have been convinced good is bad and bad is good" and are "willingly assisting in our own demise."[1] Carlson called this "a game we can't afford to lose," urged those who are awake to explain events to others, and argued that reconciliation across conservatives, moderates and liberals is required because a divided America cannot survive.[1] If America falls, he wrote, so does the world, with no second chance.[1]
