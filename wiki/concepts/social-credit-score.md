# Social Credit Score

A **social credit score** is a system, associated principally with China, in which a citizen's access to services is conditioned on a rating derived from their behavior.[1] In Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]], the concept supplies the enforcement mechanism for a future in which all household energy arrives as electricity.[1]

Carlson imagines "a Black Mirror or Chinese credit score situation" in which electricity is priced according to a person's social credit rating.[1] He offers concrete illustrations: a controversial social media post raising one's electricity rates the way a speeding ticket or DUI raises car insurance premiums; attendance at an "anti-government control" rally resulting in a month-long prohibition on charging one's electric vehicle; and an excessive carbon footprint triggering a cap limiting an EV battery to a 50 percent charge in order to restrict driving.[1] He concludes simply that "anything is possible."[1]

The scenario depends on the consolidation thesis: such penalties are enforceable only if energy arrives through a single metered channel, because households with gas, oil, or other alternatives retain a way around the switch.[1] Carlson links this to broader plans he attributes to the [[deep-state|Deep State]], including proposals for smart cities and [[15-minute-cities|15-minute cities]] in which residents remain within a short radius of home.[1]
