# Mountain Valley Pipeline

The Mountain Valley Pipeline (MVP) is a major natural gas pipeline project in the Mid-Atlantic region, planned beginning in the Obama administration to carry gas sourced from fracking operations in West Virginia.[1] Along with its sister project, the [[atlantic-coast-pipeline|Atlantic Coast Pipeline]], it faced sustained opposition that Badlands commentary attributed to the "Uniparty" and to non-governmental organizations such as the [[sierra-club|Sierra Club]] working alongside what the writer described as Soros-funded state attorneys who filed injunctions against the project.[1]

## Timeline and obstruction

On December 22, 2017 — the day after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] issued Executive Order 13818 — the Army Corps of Engineers issued verification that the Mountain Valley Pipeline met all criteria, and formally reinstated that verification on July 3, 2018; these verifications were later documented in court cases between the pipeline's owners and the Sierra Club.[1]

The pipeline was nevertheless delayed for years and ran billions of dollars over budget.[1] Construction was completed only in June 2024, with operations beginning shortly afterward, but the project does not benefit most of Virginia because it terminates near Roanoke.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the pipelines represented significant advances in affordable energy for Appalachia and North Carolina — regions later devastated by hurricane flooding and, in his account, subsequent federal malfeasance — and that obstruction of the projects amounted to officials embedded in the executive branch collaborating with NGO actors to prevent infrastructure that would have lowered the cost of living on the East Coast.[1]
