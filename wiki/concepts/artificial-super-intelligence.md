# Artificial Super Intelligence

"Artificial Super Intelligence" is the term used by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] for a machine intelligence exceeding human capability across all domains — the same object that, in the Badlands Media article "Dark Equation," researchers in the independent media had long described as the "A.I. God" that the "cabal" wished to build.[1] Author [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that what "seemed like a wackadoo, far-out, hyper-imaginative concept just a few years ago is now all but verified by Elon Musk's account and the public statements of [[larry-page|Larry Page]] and others."[1]

## Origins of the claim

The claim traces to Musk's account, given in an interview with Tucker Carlson, of his falling-out with Page over the direction of AI development.[1] At the time of the split [[google|Google]] had just acquired [[deepmind|DeepMind]], and Musk said Google's approach to AI held "great potential for good, but there's also potential for bad."[1] Musk went on to co-found [[openai|OpenAI]] in 2015 with LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman, Y Combinator founding partner Jessica Livingston, PayPal co-founder [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], and machine-learning researcher Ilya Sutskever.[1]

DeLarme appended an author's note cautioning that whether these figures are "part of a more organized and clandestine rollout of A.I." or "simply individuals playing out their lives is for the reader to decide," adding that he was reserving judgment on Musk.[1]

## Significance in the article

The concept functions in "Dark Equation" as the framing device for everything that follows: the 2023 [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] meeting's focus on artificial intelligence, [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]]'s influence over US AI policy, and [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]]'s [[worldcoin|Worldcoin]] biometric identity project are all read as steps toward centralized machine governance rather than as unrelated commercial developments.[1]
