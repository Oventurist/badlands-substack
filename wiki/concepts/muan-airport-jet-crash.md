# Muan Airport Jet Crash

The Muan Airport jet crash was an aviation disaster in southwestern South Korea on Sunday, December 29, 2024, in which a passenger jet crash-landed at an airport, careening down the runway on its belly before bursting into flames.[1]

Of the 181 people on board, just two survived; the deaths of 179 people were confirmed by the local fire department, and the two survivors were crew members pulled alive from the crash site.[1] It was the deadliest aviation disaster to strike South Korea since 1997, when a Korean Airlines Boeing 747 crashed in the Guam jungle with the loss of 228 lives.[1]

The crash was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of December 30, 2024, reproduced from CNN reporting without editorial commentary.[1]
