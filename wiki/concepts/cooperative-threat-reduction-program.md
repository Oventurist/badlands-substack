# Cooperative Threat Reduction Program

The Cooperative Threat Reduction Program is a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] funding vehicle through which U.S. taxpayer money has for years supported biological laboratories abroad to study pathogens and improve biosecurity.[1]

## Role in the overseas biolab review

The program featured prominently in a review led by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] into more than 120 U.S.-funded biological laboratories located in over 30 countries, including more than 40 in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] that may be at risk amid the ongoing war.[1] The review follows President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s executive order banning federal support for gain-of-function research in high-risk nations, and aims to identify exact laboratory locations, the pathogens studied, and any dangerous experiments conducted.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the existence of such labs — which he characterized as secret bioweapons facilities explicitly denied by [[anthony-fauci|Tony Fauci]] and his staff — implied deliberate illegality, and speculated that Russian President Putin's awareness of the programs factored into the decision to invade Ukraine.[1]
