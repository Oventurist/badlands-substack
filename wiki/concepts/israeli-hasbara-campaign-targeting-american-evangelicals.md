# Israeli Hasbara Campaign Targeting American Evangelicals

In late 2025, the government of [[israel|Israel]] signed contracts worth millions of dollars with American firms to rehabilitate Israel's standing in US public opinion, both online and offline, amid a sharp drop in support from the conservative right.[1] Reporting by [[haaretz|Haaretz]] described the effort as a new phase in Israel's post-war public diplomacy strategy.[1]

## Scope of the campaign

Beyond conventional hasbara — public diplomacy — campaigns, the contracts covered outreach to millions of Christian churchgoers, bot networks to amplify pro-Israel messaging online, and efforts to influence both search engine results and the responses generated by popular AI services such as ChatGPT.[1] Among the experts recruited was a former campaign manager for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and many of the other firms were linked to the Republican Party or to Evangelical communities, indicating that Israel was directing substantial resources at constituencies once considered automatically pro-Israel.[1] Fighting antisemitism, which had risen alongside the decline in support for Israel, was among the campaigns' stated goals, and the effort marked a shift in how Israel deploys agents — both AI and human influencers — for hasbara abroad.[1]

## Legal framework

American firms representing foreign governments must register with the US Department of Justice under the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]].[1] Filings submitted over the two months preceding the report showed that the Government of Israel — acting through the [[israeli-ministry-of-foreign-affairs|Foreign Ministry]], the Tourism Ministry, and the Government Advertising Agency known as LAPAM — had signed multiple contracts in the United States to promote Israeli interests.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that if Israel had lost both the Democratic and the Republican base, this amounted to an admission that Israel maintains a strong and influential relationship with the Uniparty establishment on both sides, because the establishments of both parties are globalists.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] added that the levels of "delusions of grandeur" on display help explain why Zionists are, in his framing, dead weight to the America First movement.[1]
