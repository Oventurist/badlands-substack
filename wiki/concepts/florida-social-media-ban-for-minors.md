# Florida Social Media Ban for Minors

The Florida social media ban for minors is a state law signed on March 25, 2024 by Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], giving Florida one of the most restrictive regimes in the United States governing children's access to social media platforms.[1]

## Provisions

Once in effect, the law bans social media accounts for children under 14 and requires parental permission for 14- and 15-year-olds.[1] It takes effect on January 1.[1]

## Legislative history

The bill was the top legislative priority of Republican state house speaker [[paul-renner|Paul Renner]].[1] The enacted text was slightly watered down from an earlier proposal that DeSantis had vetoed earlier in March, one week before the annual legislative session ended.[1]

## Outlook

Reporting on the signing noted that the law must still withstand expected legal challenges.[1] The measure was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of March 27, 2024.[1]
