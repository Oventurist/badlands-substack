# 2025 Washington D.C. National Guard Shooting

The 2025 Washington, D.C. National Guard shooting was an attack on November 26, 2025 in which a gunman opened fire on two [[national-guard|National Guard]] members patrolling a street a short distance from the White House.[1]

## The attack

At approximately 2:15 p.m., the assailant fired at close range on two West Virginia National Guard members on patrol, critically wounding both.[1] U.S. Army Specialist [[sarah-beckstrom|Sarah Beckstrom]] died of her injuries the next day, while U.S. Air Force Staff Sergeant Andrew Wolfe remained in critical condition.[1]

## Suspect

The suspect was identified by four senior law enforcement sources as [[rahmanullah-lakanwal|Rahmanullah Lakanwal]], a 29-year-old Afghan national.[1] Officials and a relative said he had served alongside U.S. troops in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1] Reporting established that he had worked with the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] during the U.S. occupation.[1] [[john-ratcliffe|CIA Director John Ratcliffe]] said the suspect was admitted to the United States in September 2021 "due to his prior work with the US Government, including the CIA, as a member of a partner force in Kandahar, which ended shortly following the chaotic evacuation."[1] He had entered under a special program created to evacuate vulnerable Afghans, including those who had worked with Western troops, after the Taliban's 2021 return to power.[1]

## Reaction and commentary

The shooting became a defining story in independent media because of the suspect's intelligence background. [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] recalled that [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] had predicted two weeks earlier that elements of the CIA would activate Islamic sleeper cells in the United States.[1] [[canncon|CannCon]] framed the incident as an attempted [[color-revolution|color revolution]] — arguing that with private-sector funding vehicles such as [[usaid|USAID]] and the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] curtailed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], hostile factions had turned to politicizing the military and inserting themselves into a chain of command they did not belong to.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that exposing and dismantling the CIA would be an early step in any genuine national recovery.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected the shooting to a set of adjacent data points: a years-old post by former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] invoking "common sense, experience, and wisdom"; [[elissa-slotkin|Elissa Slotkin]]'s forecast of violence involving the National Guard less than a month earlier; and the role of Representative [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]], one of the [[seditious-six|Seditious Six]], as a leading advocate of the 2021 Afghan resettlement effort.[1]

## Policy aftermath

In a Thanksgiving statement, Trump announced that he would permanently pause migration from all Third World countries, terminate Biden-era admissions including those authorized by autopen, end federal benefits and subsidies to noncitizens, denaturalize migrants who undermine domestic tranquility, and deport foreign nationals deemed public charges or security risks.[1]
