# Price Floors Industrial Policy (2025)

The price floors industrial policy is a [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] initiative, announced by Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] in October 2025, to set minimum prices across a range of industries in order to combat market manipulation by [[china|China]].[1]

## Rationale

Bessent told CNBC in an exclusive interview on Wednesday, October 15, 2025 that China had driven foreign competitors in the rare earth industry out of business over the previous two decades by using its global dominance in refining and processing to slash prices.[1]

Speaking to Sara Eisen at CNBC's Invest in America Forum in Washington, D.C., Bessent framed the measure as a necessary departure from laissez-faire practice: "When you are facing a nonmarket economy like China, then you have to exercise industrial policy." He continued, "So we're going to set price floors and the forward buying to make sure that this doesn't happen again and we're going to do it across a range of industries," declining to name specific sectors beyond rare earths.[1]

## Strategic mineral reserve

Bessent said the United States also needs to establish a strategic mineral reserve, and stated that [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]] is interested in working with the administration to create one.[1]

Rare earths are used to produce magnets that are crucial inputs in U.S. weapons systems such as the F-35 warplane and Tomahawk cruise missiles, and are also essential for civilian commercial applications including electric vehicles.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright argued that [[scott-bessent|Bessent]] holds "the most interesting role in the 2025 Shadow War," having telegraphed the policy in 2024, and that Chinese conduct is now supplying the narrative cover needed to execute it. In this reading, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "isn't waging a war with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]" so much as "copying his playbook" — adopting state-directed industrial policy of the kind long practiced by Beijing.[1]
