# Pence Classified Documents Discovery

The Pence classified documents discovery was the January 2023 disclosure that former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] had found documents bearing classified markings from his vice presidency at his private residence.[1]

## Discovery and disclosure

Pence informed [[congress|Congress]] on Tuesday, January 24, 2023 that he had discovered documents with classified markings from his time as vice president at his home in Carmel, Indiana, on January 16.[1] The searches that turned up the material were prompted by the earlier revelations that classified documents from President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s tenure as vice president had been found at the [[penn-biden-center|Penn Biden Center]] think tank and at Biden's Wilmington, Delaware property; in response, Pence's team searched both his Indiana home and the office of his political advocacy group, [[advancing-american-freedom|Advancing American Freedom]].[1] The discovery was reported by [[fox-news|FOX News]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]] said it was hard to tell whether Pence was a secret ally of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] or whether his public distance from Trump was genuine, but judged that the initial optics of the discovery served only to help Biden and hurt Trump; he allowed that the documents Pence turned in might eventually reveal something important.[1] [[just-human|Just Human]] offered a shorter assessment: "Pence is a patriot."[1]
