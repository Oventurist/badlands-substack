# Truth.Fi

Truth.Fi is a financial services brand launched by [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media and Technology Group]], the operator of the [[truth-social|Truth Social]] platform, announced on January 29, 2025 and pitched as a way to protect "American patriots" from Big Tech censorship and debanking.[1]

## Launch and products

Trump Media said it would deploy its cash to establish bitcoin, cryptocurrency, and crypto-related securities holdings, along with customized separately managed accounts (SMAs) and customized exchange-traded funds, all under the Truth.Fi name.[1] The company's stock, traded under the ticker DJT, rose more than 6% on the day of the announcement.[1]

## Political context

The launch followed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s complaint to Bank of America chief executive Brian Moynihan that banks "don't take conservative business," and his broader argument that Big Tech companies have suppressed conservative voices — a claim followed in the preceding weeks by Meta Platforms' decision to adjust its fact-checking approach on Facebook.[1]
