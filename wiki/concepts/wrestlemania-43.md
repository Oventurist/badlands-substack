# WrestleMania 43

WrestleMania 43 is the 2027 edition of [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]]'s flagship annual event, announced in September 2025 to be held in Riyadh, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]. It will be the first time WrestleMania — described in the announcement as a cultural phenomenon and "the biggest date on the wrestling calendar" — is staged outside North America.[1]

## Announcement

The event was unveiled at a press announcement at the Fontainebleau Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas by [[turki-alalshikh|Turki Alalshikh]], chairman of the [[general-entertainment-authority|General Entertainment Authority]] and president of the Saudi Arabian Boxing Federation, together with WWE Chief Content Officer [[paul-levesque|Paul Levesque]], better known by his ring name Triple H.[1] Several of professional wrestling's biggest stars joined them on stage.[1] Alalshikh said the deal delivered on a promise he had made in 2018 to "change the future by doing big projects together" in Saudi Arabia, and called the announcement "a significant moment in our partnership with WWE."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] responded to the news with the quip that Saudi Arabia would be hosting "Trump-a-Mania 43," a play on the intertwining of the Kingdom's entertainment ambitions with American political spectacle.[1] In a longer analysis in the same brief, contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Gulf sovereign funds pouring into American sports — from LIV Golf's disruption of the PGA to stakes in the [[ufc|UFC]] and emerging media ventures — constitute a Trojan Horse aimed not merely at profit but at reconciliation between populations long conditioned toward mutual enmity, with sport and entertainment serving as the medium through which the East is rebranded in Western eyes and the West in Eastern hearts.[1]
