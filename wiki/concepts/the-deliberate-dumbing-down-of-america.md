# The Deliberate Dumbing Down of America

**The Deliberate Dumbing Down of America** is a book by [[charlotte-iserbyt|Charlotte Iserbyte]] that the Badlands Media contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] recommends to readers in his article on the left-right paradigm.[1]

According to his summary, the book includes hundreds of references establishing, among other things, that hardcore Soviet methods of brainwashing were in use in American schools by the 1990s — including, he says, at the public school he personally attended.[1] He adds that conditions have only worsened since.[1]

The recommendation appears in the context of the author's account of his own childhood indoctrination: by age eight he had been taught that the world was ending because of global warming and that people like him — described in his schooling as evil, greedy, polluting, warmongering White males — were responsible.[1] He presents the book as documentary support for his claim that such schooling constitutes a deliberate program rather than an accident, and links it to his broader argument that [[leftism]] operates as a mass grooming operation.[1]
