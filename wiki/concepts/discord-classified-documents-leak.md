# Discord Classified Documents Leak

The Discord classified documents leak was the April 2023 appearance online of more than 100 classified US documents, including maps and current intelligence, which had circulated via a Discord server before reaching wider distribution.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] used the leak as the reference point for assessing mainstream coverage of classified material.[1] He observed that the leak of actual classified maps and documents coincided with a [[new-york-times|New York Times]] story about investigators questioning witnesses over a map allegedly shown by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after leaving office.[1] In his reading, media outlets that would otherwise have spent days explaining the sensitivity of presidential maps and the reach of the Espionage Act were placed in the position of either reporting the severity of the real leak or ignoring it.[1]

Just Human suggested the New York Times piece had been intended to run two or three weeks earlier and framed the collision of the two stories as either coincidence or a mistimed narrative synchronization.[1] The Badlands News Brief edition carrying the analysis was titled "Real Leaks & Fake News," contrasting the Discord disclosures with the [[police-service-of-northern-ireland|PSNI]] document found in a Belfast street and with contested media claims elsewhere in the issue.[1]
