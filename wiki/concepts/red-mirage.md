# Red Mirage

"Red Mirage" is the predictive term used in 2020 to describe a pattern in which Republicans appear to be winning on election night, only for Democrats to prevail once all ballots are counted in the days or weeks that follow.[1] The phrase has re-entered circulation in 2026 as media outlets invoke it to explain public distrust in elections and to pre-frame expected result reversals at the [[2026-midterm-elections|midterms]].[1]

## California case

In the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|Los Angeles mayoral race]], early returns on election night showed City Councilmember [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]] trailing challenger [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] by roughly eight points; as additional mail ballots were counted over subsequent days the margin reversed, positioning Raman to potentially advance to a runoff against incumbent Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]].[1] Registered Democrats outnumber Republicans nearly three-to-one in Los Angeles County, and late mail ballots have consistently shifted results toward Democratic candidates in recent California elections.[1]

California permanently adopted a system of automatically mailing ballots to all active registered voters in 2021 after expanding the practice during the COVID pandemic.[1] Ballots postmarked by Election Day remain eligible if received within seven days, producing a slow, multi-day or weeks-long count that frequently generates large election-night leads which narrow or disappear once remaining mail ballots are tallied.[1]

## Political response

Trump administration officials and Republican allies have pointed to the delayed counting and late swings in California — including the Los Angeles contest — as signs of potential fraud.[1] The state's top federal prosecutor in Los Angeles has said his office has multiple election-fraud investigations underway and has accused California of blocking a federal audit of its voter rolls.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and congressional Republicans used the episode to renew calls for the [[save-act|SAVE Act]] and other measures requiring proof of citizenship and stricter voter identification.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that "election reruns are playing on the big screen," arguing the Red Mirage pattern visible in the California primaries would be run again at "The Midterms."[1] She linked it to a Glenn Beck / Billy Bush media operation that she characterized as a rerun of the film *Rigged*, in which the establishment conceded the 2020 election was stolen "legally" and directed supporters toward ballot chasing, get-out-the-vote efforts and changing the law.[1] Establishment Republicans in office, she noted, never bothered to change the law, because in her view the argument has never been about securing elections but about defending the legality of an illegitimate election system and ultimately blaming voters — recycled narratives she called an insult to voters' intelligence.[1]
