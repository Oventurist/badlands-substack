# 2026 Venezuela Earthquakes

The 2026 Venezuela earthquakes were a series of deadly seismic events in [[venezuela|Venezuela]] that triggered a humanitarian crisis and a US-led relief response in mid-2026.[1]

## US response

The [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration stated that its immediate priority following the quakes was earthquake response, including ongoing US search-and-rescue operations and humanitarian assistance.[1]

## Machado controversy

The administration criticised Venezuelan opposition leader [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]]'s efforts to return to Venezuela in the aftermath of the earthquakes, with a senior [[white-house|White House]] official describing the move as "grotesque political opportunism."[1] According to some reports, US officials support Machado's eventual return but believe doing so during the humanitarian crisis could undermine relief operations and destabilise the country.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] welcomed the administration's language, arguing that it is "always such a relief when the administration breaks character and starts speaking honestly about a topic" because doing so provides social permission for others to follow, shifting the [[overton-window|Overton Window]].[1] The commentary asserted that Machado "was always a bad guy," describing her as "literally a creation of [[usaid|USAID]]," originally dispatched by the Bush administration to topple [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]], and criticised those who promoted her because [[con-inc|Con Inc]] did so.[1]
