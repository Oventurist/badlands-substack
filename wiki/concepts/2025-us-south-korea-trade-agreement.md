# 2025 US–South Korea Trade Agreement

The 2025 US–South Korea trade agreement is a bilateral arrangement announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Wednesday, July 30, 2025, setting a 15% tariff on imports from Seoul while exempting American goods from reciprocal tariffs.[1]

## Terms

Under the deal as announced, [[south-korea|South Korea]] pledged $350 billion in US-controlled investments and $100 billion in purchases of American energy products.[1] Trump described it on Truth Social as a "Full and Complete Trade Deal" designed to "stabilize economic relations" with a key Asian ally, emphasizing that US goods would face no reciprocal tariffs.[1]

South Korean President [[lee-jae-myung|Lee Jae-myung]] characterized the outcome as the product of careful preparation, writing in a Facebook post that Seoul "gathered diverse opinions and refined our strategies" before reaching the agreement.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recalled that when Lee was elected, establishment-aligned influencers and mainstream media — he singled out [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] — had accused him of being a left-wing communist, whereas in his view Lee has pursued peace and de-escalation with North Korea while promoting populist nationalism, earning the nickname "the Trump of South Korea."[1]

He observed that the bullet points closely mirror the [[2025-us-eu-trade-agreement|deal announced days earlier with the European Union]]: a 15% tariff on incoming goods, 0% on US exports, plus large investment and energy-purchase pledges, differing only in scale.[1] Noting that the French Prime Minister had described the EU agreement as "submission," he argued a global pattern was emerging.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] added a skeptical note, asking when a deal is not a deal and what the true nature of American power is.[1]
