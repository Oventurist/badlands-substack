# Philosopher's Stone

The Philosopher's Stone is the substance that, in [[alchemy|alchemical]] tradition, supposedly transmutes base metals such as lead into gold.[1] It is discussed in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]].[1]

History's greatest alchemists believed the [[emerald-tablet|Emerald Tablet]], attributed to [[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]], harboured the secret of the *prima materia* and of the Philosopher's Stone.[1] The Tablet's concise text — short enough to read in minutes yet studied for lifetimes, including by [[isaac-newton|Sir Isaac Newton]] — is presented as the foundation of the European alchemical search for the Stone.[1]
