# Gleiwitz Incident

The Gleiwitz incident is cited in the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" as the second of two illustrative examples drawn from a list of forty-two government-admitted [[false-flag-operation|false flags]].[1]

A major with the Nazi SS admitted at the Nuremberg trials that, under orders from the chief of the Gestapo, he and other Nazi operatives faked attacks on their own people and resources and blamed them on the Poles, in order to justify the invasion of Poland.[1]

Together with the [[mukden-incident|Mukden Incident]], the episode is used in the article to establish that staged provocations attributed to an intended victim are a documented instrument of statecraft rather than a purely speculative category.[1]
