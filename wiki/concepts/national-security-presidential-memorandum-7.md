# National Security Presidential Memorandum-7 (NSPM-7)

National Security Presidential Memorandum-7, commonly abbreviated NSPM-7, is a directive issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] addressing purported "domestic" terrorists.[1]

## Use in the 2025 antifascist designation

In November 2025, Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] designated a European antifascist group — the group that attacked a neo-Nazi rally in Budapest in 2023 — as a terrorist organization.[1] Although the designation authority is aimed at foreign groups, Rubio said he was acting in accordance with NSPM-7.[1] Reporting by The Intercept framed the move as exposing supporters of anti-Nazi militants to potential U.S. material-support terrorism charges.[1]

The Intercept situated the designation in a longstanding European controversy: Hungarian Prime Minister [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]]'s government launched a continent-wide manhunt after the 2023 attack, while showing no comparable appetite for cracking down on the annual fascist rally itself, which in February 2025 drew attendees sporting SS patches, swastikas, and the "Totenkopf" death's-head symbol under the watch of Hungarian police.[1] That asymmetry — aggressive response to antifascists, kid-gloves treatment of neo-Nazi demonstrators — has roiled European politics for years.[1]
