# 2024 Trump–Putin Phone Call

The 2024 Trump–Putin phone call was a telephone conversation between US President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] reported by the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] on November 10, 2024, and summarized in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] edition "The Media Tucks Tail as Peacemakers Take the Field."[1] It was described as the first conversation between the two men since Trump won the November 2024 presidential election.[1]

## The call

According to several people familiar with the matter who spoke to the Washington Post on condition of anonymity, Trump took the call from his resort in Florida and advised Putin not to escalate the war in Ukraine, reminding the Russian president of Washington's sizable military presence in Europe.[1] The two men discussed the goal of peace on the European continent, and Trump expressed interest in follow-up conversations aimed at "the resolution of Ukraine's war soon."[1]

During his presidential campaign Trump had said he would bring an immediate end to the war in Ukraine without offering details on how he intended to do so.[1] Reporting indicated he had signaled privately that he would support a settlement in which Russia retained some captured territory, and that he briefly raised the issue of land during the call.[1]

## Badlands commentary

The Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the call as vindication of his "Righteous Russia" essay series, published roughly two and a half years earlier before what he characterized as the Russian Federation's "denazification" effort in the Donbas.[1] He said the series resonated because he approached the subject as a storyteller engaging with the story of Russia as it had been told to the American mind for a century or more.[1] Quoting Part 4 of the series, he recalled predicting that upon returning to office Trump would "demand" peace with Putin: "Trump is not a man who will sue for peace. He does not plead. He does not beg. Trump will not request peace. He will have it, and Vladimir Putin will share in it."[1] The brief's editors grouped the episode with parallel Middle East developments under the heading "Peacemakers on Deck."[1]
