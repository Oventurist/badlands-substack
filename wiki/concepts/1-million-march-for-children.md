# 1 Million March for Children

The 1 Million March for Children was a nationwide Canadian protest movement organized by parents opposed to [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]] in schools and to the erosion of [[parental-rights|parental rights]].[1] A February 2024 Badlands Media essay by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], assisted by [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], cited the marches as evidence that, even though [[canada|Canada]] appears to be the most radical country on these questions, parents had awakened to what he describes as a decades-long psyop.[1]

## Significance

The essay argues the marches showed that the use of [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxist]] ideology in the legal, healthcare, and education systems is no longer as subversive or invisible as it once was.[1] It notes that mainstream media in Canada and the United States both obscured the dissent emerging from other nations and targeted successful events like the 1 Million March for Children with aggressive propaganda, including framing counter-protest coverage around anti-LGBTQ claims.[1]

## Cross-border dimension

Deschamps concludes that the pattern reveals a reluctance to allow dialogue between parents across borders: propaganda is a localized phenomenon that must be tailored to each population, so global coherence among parents falls apart the information-warfare effort as soon as citizens compare the victories and losses of neighbouring countries.[1] He presents this as a strategic weakness of what he calls an international globalist coup and its [[uniparty|Uniparty]] minions — "know your enemy as they are manifest elsewhere in the world, and you just may find their weakness here at home."[1]
