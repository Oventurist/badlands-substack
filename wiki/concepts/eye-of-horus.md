# Eye of Horus

The **Eye of Horus** is an ancient Egyptian symbol associated with the sky god Horus. In the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series it is presented as the true meaning concealed behind modern uses of the [[eye-of-providence|Eye of Providence]], and is emphasized for its association with sacrifice.[1]

## Claimed substitution for the Eye of Providence

Commenting on Q Drop #133, the author writes that while the original meaning of the Eye of Providence was benign, "[they] have absolutely perverted it" and now use it as cover for what it really represents, the Eye of Horus.[1] The prevalence of left-eye symbolism among public figures is offered as corroborating evidence, and the author underlines that the Eye of Horus represents sacrifice — a point treated as significant given the drop's subsequent questions about cults, Satan worship, and the abuse of children.[1]

## Color symbolism

Horus is described in the article as represented by the colors blue and gold.[1] This is used to interpret Q's question about the significance of colors: blue for fertility, white for the purity taken from children, and yellow or gold for eternal life.[1] The author applies that reading to the temple on [[little-saint-james|Epstein's island]], arguing that its color scheme is explained by the Horus association, and then raises the same question about the palettes used by [[disney|Disney]] Cruise Lines and the set of [[ellen-degeneres|Ellen DeGeneres]]'s show.[1]

## Sacrifice and the pyramid

Within the chapter's framing, the sacrifice motif attached to the Eye of Horus is the connective tissue between symbolism and alleged conduct: the author argues that the people described in the drop attempt to make themselves into gods, and that one of the means by which they seek that power is through sacrifice and the defilement of children.[1] Epstein's temple is said to sit atop a mountain because the trade in question occupies the summit of the group's pyramid of power.[1]
