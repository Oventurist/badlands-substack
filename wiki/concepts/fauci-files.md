# Fauci Files

The **Fauci Files** is the informal name given to more than 1,100 pages of private diary entries by Dr. [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], spanning late 2019 to 2022, released by Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] in late July 2026 as part of his investigation into the origins of COVID-19 and related research.[1]

## Contents

The journals allegedly show Fauci privately noting as early as January 2020 that the Wuhan wet market was an "amplifier" rather than the source of the virus, and recording early scientific discussions in which multiple experts viewed features such as the furin cleavage site as potentially non-natural or "constructed."[1] These private reflections contrast with his public emphasis on natural animal-to-human spillover.[1] The diaries also capture Fauci's awareness of his rising media profile, which he described as flattering.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who applied a self-imposed 72-hour rule before commenting on the details, summarized the highlights as showing that Fauci was enriching himself and employing lawyers to find loopholes enabling further enrichment; that he was "straight up lying about case and mortality rates"; that he attempted to hide potential vaccine harms to pregnant women while mandating that they be vaccinated; and that he concealed his own vaccine injury.[1] She remarked that some of the material is "so criminal that it's hard to believe Rand Paul and his legislative friends have known this and Fauci still walks free."[1]

## Redaction controversy

Rand Paul subsequently redacted the entry concerning Fauci's own vaccine injury, a change Ashe found strange and speculated might be a privacy measure — which she called darkly humorous given that "no one had privacy respected in the age of Covid."[1] She credited the Substack writer Kanekoa with tracking these changes and with surfacing material indicating that Fauci stopped the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] from reporting that the vaccines were not effective.[1]

## Related allegations and reactions

Separately, HHS Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] accused Fauci of concealing a personal COVID vaccine injury — a pulmonary infarction five months after receiving the vaccine in January 2021 — while continuing to promote the shots and dismiss risks as he received elite medical care; Kennedy further charged that Fauci's policies produced the highest COVID mortality rate among major countries.[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] reacted to the diary release by saying he "grew tired of" Fauci, who "wanted to close everything down," while emphasizing that he left lockdown decisions to governors.[1]

## Testimony

Fauci was scheduled to testify before the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee, chaired by Paul, who has long alleged that Fauci misled [[congress|Congress]] on gain-of-function research and has referred the matter for potential prosecution.[1] Paul indicated that if Fauci lies in testimony, his pardon — which Badlands writers characterize as an illegitimate [[autopen-scandal|autopen]] pardon — would not protect him.[1] Badlands Media aired special coverage of the hearing on [[rumble|Rumble]], streamed by Jessica Storm.[1]
