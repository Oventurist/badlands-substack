# TurkStream

TurkStream is a natural gas pipeline carrying Russian fuel under the Black Sea to Türkiye and onward into southeastern Europe. Launched in 2010, the pipeline has an annual capacity of 31.5 billion cubic meters of natural gas and supplies Hungary, Serbia, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Greece in addition to Türkiye.[1]

## January 2025 drone attack

In January 2025, Moscow said that Ukrainian forces had launched nine explosives-laden drones over the preceding weekend against a section of TurkStream in southern Russia, an attack Russian officials described as thwarted.[1] The [[european-commission|European Commission]] expressed concern at the reports, given that the pipeline supplies several European Union member states.[1] At a Monday press briefing the commission's spokesperson for energy, climate and environment, Anna-Kaisa Itkonen, said that "obviously, any attack on energy infrastructure is something that is worrying," adding that the commission had not had particular contacts with the Ukrainian side about the reporting but continued to monitor security-of-supply questions in Ukraine, Moldova, and member states.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the strike as part of a pattern of provocations against Türkiye whose logic pointed at [[article-5|Article Five]] of the North Atlantic Treaty: an attack by Ukraine on Turkey, he argued, would oblige Europe to join Russia's side against Ukraine, while an attack by Israel on Turkey would oblige Europe to join Iran's side against Israel.[1] He said he had not discerned what the "Deep State's play" was and expected ceasefire negotiations in both Ukraine and the Middle East to be subverted so as to keep the respective conflicts going.[1]
