# Red Masonry

"Red Masonry," also called *Fratres Lucis*, is the term used by the Masonic researcher [[william-stuart|William Stuart]] for what he describes as the fusion of the old Pagan Celtic religion with the [[eleusinian-mysteries|Eleusinian Mystery School]] of ancient Greece, as reported in the Badlands Media feature "Legacy of the Bavarian Illuminati – Part 2."[1]

In the article's framing, Red Masonry constitutes the Rosicrucian stratum of [[freemasonry|Freemasonry]] under which hundreds of clandestine orders were founded across several centuries — degrees that [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] suggests could be understood as "the 'Cabal' degrees, as not everyone in Freemasonry is part of the Deep State—some are actually God-Fearing patriots."[1] Stuart has compiled a list of these societies with their founding dates, in which the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]] appears as a single order among many.[1]

DeLarme argues from this list that "there have been various, interwoven societies created to carry out specific agendas in different parts of the globe for many generations," working "hand in glove to achieve countless goals through subversive means over the last few centuries," and that they therefore cannot be reduced to the singular term "Illuminati."[1] Later incarnations of the same agenda are said to include the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg Group]] and the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]].[1]
