# Trump Universal Tariff Plan

The universal tariff plan was President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s proposal for broad across-the-board import tariffs, and the subject of a market-moving media dispute in early January 2025.[1]

On January 6, 2025, the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] reported, citing anonymous sources, that Trump aides were considering dialing back the universal tariff plan. Trump responded on Truth Social by blasting the paper for "quoting so-called anonymous sources, which don't exist," and incorrectly stating that his tariff policy would be pared back: "That is wrong. The Washington Post knows it's wrong. It's just another example of Fake News."[1]

The report sent the Bloomberg Spot Dollar Index tumbling by more than 1% — one of the biggest intraday drops since 2023 — before the dollar reversed its losses following Trump's denial.[1]
