# Operation Gideon

Operation Gideon was the name given to a failed paramilitary operation to overthrow the government of Venezuela, for which a group of former US Green Berets was arrested in Venezuela in May 2020.[1] The retired special forces soldiers were acting as mercenaries in the operation.[1]

Several of the mercenaries were released in 2023 as part of a prisoner exchange between Washington and Caracas.[1]

## Later prosecution

In October 2025, a federal prosecutor in Florida sought to have the leader of the mercenaries jailed, accusing him of being a flight risk.[1] The veteran had already been arraigned on charges related to the failed coup, with a trial scheduled for February.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] suggested that the February trial would likely surface "some juicy details regarding the true nature of these regime-change operations."[1]

## Context in Badlands analysis

The operation was invoked in the context of reporting that [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] had secretly been negotiating economic concessions with the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] for months, and of speculation that US military activity off the Venezuelan coast might be aimed less at Maduro than at cartels and at [[color-revolution|color revolution]] military operators alleged to be aiding [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] in overthrowing the Venezuelan government.[1]
