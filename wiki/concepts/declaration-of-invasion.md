# Declaration of Invasion

A "declaration of invasion" is the constitutional theory, advanced by border-state Republicans during the 2022 election cycle, that a U.S. state may unilaterally act to defend its border when the federal government fails to do so.[1] It was the signature policy pledge of [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] during her 2022 campaign for governor of Arizona, who said throughout the campaign that declaring an invasion at Arizona's border with Mexico would be her first act after being sworn in.[1]

## Constitutional argument

Speaking on CBS's *Face the Nation* in early October 2022, Lake framed the theory around two clauses of the Constitution.[1] She argued that Article 4, Section 4 obliges the federal government to protect the states from invasion, and that under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s "lack of leadership" it was failing to do so.[1] In that circumstance, she said, Arizona would invoke its Article 1, Section 10 authority "to take care of our own border and protect our own border," describing the text as "right there in black and white in the Constitution."[1]

Lake said the state met all three criteria she considered necessary: that an invasion was under way, that the people of Arizona were in imminent danger, and that time was of the essence with no room for delay.[1] She characterised the situation on the ground as one in which the cartels — "these narco-terrorist groups" — held operational control of the border and were using Arizona to smuggle people, to traffic children, and to traffic [[fentanyl-crisis|fentanyl]], which she called "the most dangerous drug we've ever seen."[1] She also said a couple of other governors had already indicated they were willing to help.[1]

## As a Day One priority

Lake repeated the pledge in a November 9, 2022 interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], telling him that on Day One, "I'm going to take my hand off the Bible, and we're issuing a declaration of invasion at the border," immediately followed by convening state legislators in special session on election reform.[1] Commentator [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] treated the declaration as settled — "Yes, Kari will declare an invasion into Arizona from Mexico" — while arguing that Lake's more consequential, unspoken priority would be reopening the question of the 2020 election.[1]
