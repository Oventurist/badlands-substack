# Fourth Republican Presidential Debate (2023)

The fourth Republican presidential primary debate of the 2024 cycle was held at the University of Alabama in Tuscaloosa in early December 2023, with the Iowa Caucuses 39 days away.[1] The final four Republican aspirants vying for second place behind the absent yet dominant former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took the stage for the fourth time that year.[1]

## Participants and dynamics

The four participants were Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], former South Carolina Governor [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], tech entrepreneur [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]], and former New Jersey Governor [[chris-christie|Chris Christie]].[1] The wide-ranging and often combative exchanges may not have changed many settled minds, but revealed that Haley's polling surge had rivals — particularly DeSantis and Ramaswamy — zeroing in on her.[1]

By the end of the two-hour debate it was Ramaswamy who was in the bullseye, with Haley drawing an ovation for refusing to respond to his baiting and Christie calling him "the most obnoxious blowhard in America."[1] Ramaswamy at one point told Christie "to get the hell out of the race" because he had no chance to win and was merely taking up space.[1]

Topics included electability, foreign affairs, COVID-19 vaccines, border security and immigration, as well as China, Ukraine, Israel, and Trump.[1]

## Badlands assessment

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the event as "the fourth installment of the race for relevance" — combative and animated but "largely just a show for neocons to continue deluding the electoral franchise that the inevitable — Trump 2024 — isn't actually inevitable."[1] In her account Ramaswamy attacked all the candidates effectively, Haley attempted to explain away her corruption, Christie spent nearly the whole evening leaning on a podium while projecting strength, and DeSantis was stronger than in prior performances against a low bar.[1] Her verdict: "Trump won, and Ramaswamy came in second."[1]
