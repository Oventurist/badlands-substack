# German Child Pornography Decriminalization Bill (2024)

In May 2024 the German parliament ([[bundestag|Bundestag]]) voted to forward a bill reclassifying possession and distribution of child pornography from a felony to a misdemeanor, greatly reducing the penalties available.[1]

## Official justification and criticism

German officials said the change was intended to address inconsistencies in previous child pornography statutes, which had sometimes been applied to people who "receive images or videos through email or social media without their permission," and to cases where two minors exchanged images and were charged with creation or possession.[1]

Critics argued that the government could easily have carved out those specific exceptions to prevent wrongful imprisonment of innocent people, and that instead it was instituting sweeping changes reclassifying the crime and extending greater legal protection to child predators, since the new law makes no exception excluding adult offenders.[1]
