# Wokeness

"Wokeness" in the Badlands corpus refers to the set of progressive social narratives and policies that American corporations are said to adopt not from conviction but under financial coercion.[1]

## The coercion thesis

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] first set out the argument in an earlier Badlands article titled "Get Woke, Go Broke," holding that American corporations face a dilemma: adopt "ridiculous 'woke' narratives and policies" and lose customers, or refuse and have their stock pulled from large investment portfolios.[1] He maintained this view in June 2023, adding that the coercion does not make the companies innocent, only that they are forced into wokeness whether they want it or not.[1] [[andy-frisella|Andy Frisella]] independently stated the same mechanism, noting that non-compliant firms lose portfolio status, funding and financing.[1]

## The boycott problem

Carlson had held that boycotts work, citing Budweiser and Target as examples of companies losing money.[1] Frisella's counter-argument — that consumers are being coerced into destroying the companies that represent [[americana|Americana]] as part of a [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist plot]] to remove American culture and eliminate the middle class — forced a reassessment.[1] Carlson did not recommend abandoning boycotts of firms pushing wokeness, Satanism or the grooming of children, but argued the possibility of serving the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s aims must be weighed.[1]

## Origins in AI manipulation

Carlson proposed that the woke society itself was manufactured, arguing it "makes sense to me that AI has been used to create a 'woke' society," with people's view of the world altered through unwitting interaction with [[artificial-intelligence-rollout|artificial intelligence]] on social media platforms.[1]
