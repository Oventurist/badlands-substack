# Deepfake Jeffries Sombrero Video

The deepfake Jeffries sombrero video is an AI-generated clip posted by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to his social media platform in late September 2025 depicting House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] with a fake mustache and wearing a sombrero.[1] The video also dubbed Senate Minority Leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] saying disparaging things about his own party.[1]

## Timing

Trump posted the video on Monday, 29 September 2025, shortly after Jeffries and Schumer had met with Trump, Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and Republican congressional leaders in an attempt to reach a deal to avoid a government shutdown.[1] The shutdown began at midnight on 1 October.[1]

## Vance's response

At the White House daily briefing on Wednesday, 1 October 2025, Vance laughed off criticism of the post, claiming the deepfake was just a joke.[1] "Oh, I think it's funny," Vance said. "The President's joking and we're having a good time. You can negotiate in good faith while also poking a little bit of fun at some of the absurdities of the Democrats' positions and even, you know, poking some fun at the absurdity of the Democrats themselves."[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that accusations of racism had lost their coercive power, pointing to the ABC headline "Vance laughs off…" as evidence that Democrats no longer knew how to respond.[1] She wrote that the sombrero memes were funny and widely regarded as such, and defended them as accurate rather than racist on the grounds that Jeffries was advocating for migrants, calling the White House's mockery "delightful."[1] She noted that a similar treatment had been given to Senator Chris Van Hollen, and dismissed Jeffries as a powerless minority leader.[1]
