# Argentine Austerity Protests (December 2023)

In late December 2023, socialist activists and workers' unions converged on Buenos Aires to protest the impending "austerity" cuts of newly inaugurated President [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]].[1]

## The protests

Demonstrators carried images of Che Guevara and Eva Perón as they marched in the capital.[1] The cuts formed part of Milei's sweeping economic measures, which would erase or rewrite over 300 rules regulating and restricting private enterprise within [[argentina|Argentina]].[1] Protesters argued that deregulation would pave the way for big business interests while reducing welfare programmes and public protections.[1]

Commentators noted that the protests rested on a number of assumptions and were reactionary at best, given that Milei had been in office for a mere two weeks.[1] Speaking from the presidential palace, Milei said the goal was "to start along the path to rebuilding the country... and start to undo the huge number of regulations that have held back and prevented economic growth."[1]

## Framing

The source coverage treated the backlash from leftist elements as an illustration of the pitfalls of allowing socialism to take root in a country for any length of time, and dismissed the argument that Argentina "was never truly socialist" as a classic far-left deflection deployed whenever a socialist government or economy fails.[1]
