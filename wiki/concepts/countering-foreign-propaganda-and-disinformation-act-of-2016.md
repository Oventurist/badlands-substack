# Countering Foreign Propaganda and Disinformation Act of 2016

The Countering Foreign Propaganda and Disinformation Act of 2016 was U.S. legislation, introduced in the 114th Congress as S.3274, signed into law by President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]. It authorized a coordinated federal effort against foreign state and non-state propaganda and established the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]] within the [[state-department|State Department]].

## Statutory purpose

As quoted in [[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]], the mandate was "to direct, lead, synchronize, integrate, and coordinate U.S. Federal Government efforts to recognize, understand, expose, and counter foreign state and non-state propaganda and disinformation efforts aimed at undermining or influencing the policies, security, or stability of the United States, its allies, and partner nations."

## Badlands interpretation

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] presents the Act as evidence that the decline of the [[mainstream-media|mainstream media]] was already apparent to the outgoing Obama administration before the rise of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]: the government, on this reading, moved to shore up narrative control precisely because the centralized press was losing its grip. The article argues that the resulting apparatus made "Russian disinformation" a reusable label for "any incriminating evidence of establishment corruption," a strategy the author says "still persists to this day." In the corpus the Act is treated as a legal foundation of the [[censorship-industrial-complex|Censorship Industrial Complex]].
