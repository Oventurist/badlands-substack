# Countering Foreign Propaganda and Disinformation Act of 2016

The Countering Foreign Propaganda and Disinformation Act of 2016 was legislation signed by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], originating as S.3274 in the 114th Congress, which authorized a coordinated federal effort against foreign state and non-state propaganda.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

The Act is associated with the establishment of the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]] at the [[state-department|State Department]], tasked with directing, leading, synchronizing, integrating and coordinating U.S. government efforts to recognize, understand, expose and counter propaganda and disinformation aimed at undermining the policies, security or stability of the United States and its allies.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

Badlands Media reads the law as evidence that the establishment knew the [[centralized-media|mainstream media]] was already dying before the Trump era, and as the legal seed of the [[censorship-industrial-complex|Censorship Industrial Complex]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm) In the outlet's account, the framework it created enabled the practice of dismissing incriminating evidence of establishment corruption as "Russian disinformation," an approach described as an effective go-to strategy for years afterward.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)
