# Detroit Ballot Demand (2026)

The Detroit ballot demand refers to the U.S. [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s April 2026 request for access to roughly 865,000 Detroit ballots as part of a federal review of election procedures, with a warning that legal action could follow if local officials do not comply.[1]

## Legal basis

The demand is tied to federal record-retention and election oversight laws, reportedly including Civil Rights Act provisions governing election records retention and access, which require jurisdictions to preserve election materials.[1]

## Local response

Detroit officials raised concerns about the logistical challenges and implications of turning over such a large volume of materials, including storage, chain-of-custody, and administrative burden.[1]

## Context

Badlands grouped the ballot demand with two other election-integrity developments the same day: the DOJ's selection of [[joseph-digenova|Joseph DiGenova]] to lead the [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] criminal probe, and a new [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] report asserting that [[actblue|ActBlue]] accepts illegal foreign donations en masse and misled Congress to cover it up.[1]
