# Glenn Valley Foods ICE Raid

The Glenn Valley Foods ICE raid was a June 10, 2025 immigration enforcement operation at the Glenn Valley Foods meat processing plant in Omaha, Nebraska, in which agents of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] arrested up to 80 illegal immigrants said to be working under fake identification documents.[1]

## Confrontation outside the plant

Footage that emerged the same day showed three bystanders who had gathered outside the manufacturing plant blocking the path of a tinted black ICE van loaded with detainees.[1] According to the account, the agent behind the wheel began driving toward them and the trio refused to move, clutching their phones to their chests as they filmed the van striking them.[1] As onlookers shouted and pleaded, the three jumped onto the bonnet of the van, which then abruptly halted, throwing them off the hood. One of the men told the agent, "You f***ing ran me over."[1] The video was captured by the Flatwater Free Press and reported by the Daily Mail.[1]

## Context

The raid was carried out during a period of intensified workplace immigration enforcement, and the confrontation became one of several viral incidents in which bystanders physically attempted to obstruct ICE transport vehicles.[1] Badlands published the item as a bonus entry in its June 12, 2025 news brief.[1]
