# Starship Flight 11

Starship Flight 11 was the eleventh test flight of a full-scale Starship vehicle, launched by [[spacex|SpaceX]] on Monday, October 13, 2025, from the southern tip of Texas.[1] The mission carried the rocket roughly halfway around the world and released mock satellites, repeating the profile of the preceding flight.[1]

## Flight profile

The Super Heavy booster peeled away and made a controlled entry into the Gulf of Mexico as planned, while the spacecraft skimmed space before descending into the Indian Ocean.[1] Nothing was recovered from the flight.[1] SpaceX's Dan Huot marked the vehicle's return on the company broadcast with the words "Hey, welcome back to Earth, Starship. What a day," as employees cheered.[1]

## Significance

Starship is described as the biggest and most powerful rocket ever built, and SpaceX founder and chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] intends to use it to send people to Mars.[1] The nearer-term requirement belongs to [[nasa|NASA]], which cannot land astronauts on the Moon by the end of the decade without the 403-foot (123-meter) Starship, the reusable vehicle intended to carry crews from lunar orbit down to the surface and back up.[1]
