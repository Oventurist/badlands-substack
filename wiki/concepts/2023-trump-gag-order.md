# 2023 Trump Gag Order

On Monday, October 16, 2023, U.S. District Judge [[tanya-chutkan|Tanya S. Chutkan]] imposed a limited gag order on former President [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]], restricting him from making public statements attacking witnesses and specific prosecutors or court staff members involved in the federal case concerning his efforts to overturn the 2020 election.[1]

## The order

Chutkan held that Trump's free speech rights do not permit him "to launch a pretrial smear campaign" against those people, adding: "No other defendant would be allowed to do so, and I'm not going to allow it in this case."[1]

The order was narrowly tailored. It explicitly left Trump free, as he pursued his presidential campaign, to continue disparaging the Justice Department and President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], and even allowed him to assert that he believed his criminal prosecution was politically motivated.[1] The judge apparently also left Trump leeway to attack her personally.[1]

Chutkan additionally addressed the thorny question of former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]], who was both a witness in the case and one of Trump's rivals for the 2024 Republican nomination. She ruled that Trump could criticize Pence so long as the attacks did not touch on Pence's role in the criminal prosecution.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] rejected the premise that Trump "does not have the right to do and say as he pleases," arguing that the First Amendment guarantees that right.[1] He framed the order as continuous with efforts to interfere with Trump's election campaigns dating back to Russiagate in 2016, describing it as "a feckless Deep State throwing the mother of all temper tantrums" in a futile attempt to slow "Trump 2024," and predicting the move would further galvanize Trump's support heading into the election.[1]
