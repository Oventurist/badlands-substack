# Spatial Disorientation

Spatial disorientation is an aviation phenomenon that [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] uses as the central analogy for how deception operates in the [[information-war|info war]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) Quoting the FAA's Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge, the article defines it as "a condition in which a pilot's perception of direction, altitude, or speed is misleading or incorrect, usually due to conflicting sensory information," occurring "when a pilot cannot accurately interpret their aircraft's position relative to the Earth."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)

## In aviation training

Flight instructors deliberately induce the condition in simulation so that pilots learn to trust their instruments over their senses and instincts; with the horizon obscured, a student must fly against instincts that would otherwise kill him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) Retired general [[steve-kwast|Kwast]] related this training experience to the group at the fifth [[gart|Great American Restoration Tour]] gathering.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) The article notes that the fatal plane crashes of [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]] and of Kobe Bryant were both attributed to spatial disorientation.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)

## Mechanism and the Cosmos parallel

Without a horizon, a person cannot reliably judge the slope of a surface for lack of a dependable reference point.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) At the [[cosmos-mystery-area|Cosmos Mystery Area]] the reference points are made unreliable on purpose: the optical illusions there are described as the product of an obstructed horizon combined with a deceptively designed physical environment and intentional [[disinformation|disinformation]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) A 2003 study in which scientists recreated cosmos-style areas concluded that "antigravity-hill effects follow from a misperception of the eye level relative to gravity, caused by the presence of either contextual inclines or a false horizon line."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)

The physiological chain matters to the argument: the eyes report a false vertical, the brain accepts it, and the body is forced to cooperate with the false reality, producing real discomfort.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi) By extension, the article argues, people can be caught in involuntary psychological reactions to deceptively designed stimuli — often with physiological effects — and so "lose sight of the horizon" in public life.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi)
