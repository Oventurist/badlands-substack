# Tether Investment in Rumble

The Tether investment in Rumble was a $775 million strategic transaction disclosed in December 2024, in which the digital assets company [[tether|Tether]] took a large minority stake in the video platform [[rumble|Rumble]].[1] Following the announcement, Rumble's stock rose 76% to trade at $12.66, which reporting attributed to investor excitement over an alliance framed as supporting decentralization.[1]

## Terms

Under the deal, Tether agreed to purchase 103.3 million shares of Rumble Class A common stock at $7.50 each.[1] Rumble intended to deploy $250 million of the proceeds to support its balance sheet and drive growth objectives, with the remainder financing a self-tender offer for up to 70 million Class A shares in order to provide liquidity to existing holders.[1] [[chris-pavlovski|Chris Pavlovski]] would retain his majority stake after the sale, while Tether would hold a minority position without board participation.[1] The agreement was scheduled to close in the first quarter of 2025, subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory clearances.[1]

## Statements

Pavlovski stressed the connection between Rumble and Tether with respect to decentralization and free expression, saying the alliance positioned Rumble for its next stage of expansion given Tether's dedication to similar ideals of independence and openness.[1] Tether CEO [[paolo-ardoino|Paolo Ardoino]] described the partnership as a means of encouraging decentralization and free speech and pointed to potential joint work in cloud services, crypto payment solutions and advertising.[1]
