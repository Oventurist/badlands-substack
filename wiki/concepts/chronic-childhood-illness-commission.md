# Chronic Childhood Illness Commission

The Chronic Childhood Illness Commission is a proposed special presidential commission announced by former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a June 2023 [[agenda-47|Agenda 47]] campaign video, to investigate the causes of the decades-long rise in chronic illness in the United States, with particular attention to children.[1]

## The proposal

Trump promised to address the sharp rise in the nation's chronic illnesses and health problems, especially among children, as part of his plans for a return to the Oval Office.[1] He said he would establish a commission "of independent minds who are not bought and paid for by [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]]," and would "charge them with investigating what is causing the decades-long increase in chronic illnesses."[1] "I understand Big Pharma, I believe better than anyone else," he added; "I know where they're coming from."[1] The commission would then be asked "to publish recommendations for how every American child can have a safe and healthy childhood."[1] The announcement was made in a video released on Rumble and covered by [[newsmax|NewsMax]].[1]
