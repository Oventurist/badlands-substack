# Semiconductors

**Semiconductors**, colloquially "chips," are the microelectronic components underpinning virtually all modern technology. In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s coverage they function as the central material stake in the US–China economic decoupling.[1]

## Entry into public consciousness

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote in December 2022 that semiconductors did not enter the public consciousness in a significant way until the lockdowns of 2020, when international restriction policies severely contracted Chinese trade and rendered western — and global — dependence on slave labor and "too good to be true" production methods stark, sobering and horrifying.[1]

## Trade significance

Researching the [[chips-and-science-act|CHIPS Act]], Burning Bright reported being shocked to learn that semiconductors are the most-traded commodity in the world: more than oil, more than steel, more than any single staple.[1] He described chips as making everything run, from personal computers to household thermostats to vehicles and the backbone of every piece of technology sustaining modern society.[1]

## Policy treatment

On that basis he argued that semiconductor supply should be treated as a red-line national security directive, and characterized the CHIPS Act as severely limiting the fiscal benefit of U.S. companies doing business in China while restricting foreign production and Chinese interference in the industry.[1] He pointed to [[apple|Apple]]'s transition of production from China to Arizona as an early result, and to the broader public mandate to reduce dependence on cheap foreign production, onshore jobs and infrastructure, and produce national energy at home.[1]
