# 2024 Pennsylvania Mail Ballot Returns

The 2024 Pennsylvania mail ballot returns refer to the early pattern of [[mail-in-voting|mail voting]] in the state ahead of the November 2024 general election, which analysts read as evidence that early ballots were being cast overwhelmingly by highly engaged habitual voters.[1]

By late October 2024, just shy of 14 million Americans had already cast ballots nationwide.[1] In Pennsylvania, 787,900 voters had successfully returned ballots as of Friday, October 18, according to the most recent data update from the Pennsylvania Department of State.[1] Democrats accounted for just over 500,000 of those ballots, while Republicans had sent in roughly 210,000.[1]

A [[politico|Politico]] analysis of the state's mail ballot data found that for both parties the ballots cast were largely from voters who had participated in recent elections: roughly 84 percent of those who had voted so far also voted in the state in 2020, a share relatively similar across Republicans and Democrats.[1] The true percentage was described as even higher, because of people moving and re-registering.[1] Looking across all elections in the previous four years, at least 93 percent of Pennsylvanians who had cast ballots had voted at some point in that window, including 95 percent of Democrats and 93 percent of Republicans.[1]

Pennsylvania's importance in the cycle was underscored by contemporaneous forecasting: of the seven swing states seen as decisive, Pennsylvania was alone in still favoring [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] in its polling average even as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took the lead in the Decision Desk HQ/The Hill model.[1]
