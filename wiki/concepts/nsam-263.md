# NSAM 263

National Security Action Memorandum 263 was the directive introduced by President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] in October 1963 to begin a full American withdrawal from Southeast Asia.[1] Kennedy was assassinated one month after issuing it.[1]

The memorandum formalized a policy that Kennedy had been coordinating since as early as 1962 with President [[ngo-dinh-diem|Ngo Dinh Diem]] of South Vietnam, who was himself murdered on November 2, 1963.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cited NSAM 263, together with Kennedy's June 10, 1963 speech "What Kind of Peace Do We Seek?", as evidence that John and [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert Kennedy]] fought the American deep state against a new forever war — directly contradicting the claim, attributed to [[marilyn-monroe|Marilyn Monroe]]'s unseen diary, that JFK intended to increase troop levels in Vietnam.[1]
