# October 7

October 7 refers to the October 7, 2023 attack on [[israel|Israel]] by Hamas that opened the current war, and in the Badlands corpus functions as the chronological and political pivot of the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]]'s manpower crisis.

## Role in the IDF manpower crisis

In the March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief, commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] uses October 7 as the baseline for measuring the strain on the Israeli military. Since the attack, the IDF has reportedly been short anywhere from 10,000 to 15,000 enlisted soldiers, and the over-reliance on reserve troops has forced reservists to serve as many as six or seven combat tours. The total number of reservists mobilized since October 7 is reportedly 375,000 — before the [[knesset|Knesset]] approved the mobilization of an additional 400,000.

The commentary argues that because reservists are Israeli citizens who constitute the entire civilian economy, their repeated recruitment into a conflict lasting over 2.5 years creates severe economic pressure: small businesses close or cannot find labor, supply chains break down, and citizens flee the war zone. It concludes that the IDF, a relatively small fighting force never built for protracted war, risks collapse or mutiny.

## Political consequences

October 7 also marks the point at which [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s predecessor as prime minister was politically consumed: the commentary notes that IDF Chief of Staff [[eyal-zamir|Eyal Zamir]] was appointed by Netanyahu after his predecessor "became the scapegoat for October 7."
