# One-World Government

"One-world government" refers to the prospect of a single, globally unified governing authority. In the Truth Community it is treated as the end-state of the globalist project, closely associated with the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and supranational bodies that centralize decision-making away from nations and localities.[1]

## Musk's warning at the World Government Summit

Speaking by video on Wednesday, February 15, 2023 to the Dubai-based [[world-government-summit|World Government Summit]] 2023, held under the theme "Shaping Future Governments," [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] argued against the concept.[1] "I know this is called the World Government Summit, but I think we should be maybe a little bit concerned about actually becoming too much of a single world government," he said, adding that "we want to avoid creating a civilizational risk by having, frankly — this may sound a little odd — too much cooperation between governments."[1] Musk suggested the world needs "some amount of civilizational diversity" and warned that "if we are too much of a single civilization, then the whole thing may collapse."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] joked that the headline should read "Based Sigma Lectures Globalists on the Dangers of Globalism," while cautioning that he did not know how "based" Musk truly is.[1] DeLarme argued that too much power concentrated in one place attracts only those wishing to abuse it, invoked [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]]'s line that "people keep on looking to government for the answer, and government is the problem," and cited the European Union as proof that centralizing power does not benefit ordinary people.[1] He tied the cascade of contemporary crises — supply line failures, attacks on the power grid, mass culling of chickens, food shortages and rampant energy prices — to the argument that "you can't have a Great Reset without getting rid of the existing civilization," and urged patriots to pursue self-sufficiency and strong communities as resistance to the globalist agenda.[1]

## Constant conflict as the method (October 2023)

In "Women, Children ... and Terror," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] restated the thesis of his previous essay on the [[marshall-plan|New Marshall Plan]]: that the desired end result of [[world-war-ii|World War II]] was a unified Europe, a continent controlled by corporations and unelected, semi-secret societies, and that the war was created to achieve it with both sides controlled and manipulated.[2] The ultimate goal, he wrote, is a one world government that rules the world — but reaching it takes steps, and constant conflict is required.[2] Following World War II, creating a state of permanent conflict in the Middle East became a major goal of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] Cabal.[2]

Carlson added a structural rule that follows from the thesis: "You need to control both sides of a conflict to create a world war," which is why, he argued, the Ukraine war could not be escalated — Russia not being under Cabal control — and why the Middle East was reopened instead.[2]
