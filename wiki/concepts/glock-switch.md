# Glock Switch

A "Glock switch" is a device that converts a semiautomatic [[glock|Glock]] handgun into a fully automatic weapon, and which became the focus of municipal litigation and multistate legal pressure in 2024.[1]

## Legal controversy

In March 2024 the city of Chicago sued Glock, claiming the manufacturer is liable for the ease with which a Glock switch can be used to convert its semiautomatic pistols to full-auto fire.[1] The suit identified China as one of the sources of "auto sears" — the central component of a Glock switch — but did not explain the degree to which China is allegedly committed to saturating the United States with such devices.[1]

On March 26, 2024, thirteen Democrat state attorneys general wrote to Glock citing the Chicago lawsuit and expressing concern over "machine-gun Glocks."[1] The signatories represented Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, New Jersey, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Vermont, Washington, and Washington, D.C.[1] Their letter demanded that the company preserve documents concerning the conversion of its handguns into automatic weapons, the design and development of its pistols' semiautomatic function, its knowledge of state and federal law relating to switches and converted machine guns, financial and profit information tied to Glock pistols, and its public-facing marketing and advertising.[1]

The episode was reported by [[breitbart-news|Breitbart News]] and carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
