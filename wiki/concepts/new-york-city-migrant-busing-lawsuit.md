# New York City Migrant Busing Lawsuit

The New York City migrant busing lawsuit is litigation filed in January 2024 by the administration of Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] against 17 charter bus companies that transported migrants from the Texas border to New York City.[1]

## Claims

The suit alleges that the carriers collectively hauled about 33,600 individuals from Texas to the self-proclaimed "sanctuary city," and seeks to hold them liable for the $708 million the city has spent caring for those passengers.[1] The complaint asserts that the operators earned "millions of dollars" while acting in "bad faith" to carry out Texas Governor [[greg-abbott|Greg Abbott]]'s agenda.[1]

## Developments

In March 2024, [[roadrunner-charters|Roadrunner Charters]] — one of the 17 defendants — agreed to halt transporting illegal immigrants to New York City while the case proceeds.[1] Abbott's office declined to comment on the litigation, though the governor has called the suit "baseless" and said it "deserves to be sanctioned."[1]

## Analysis

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] contended the suit was misdirected: the bus companies were performing a lawful contract with a state government, making their conduct arguably state action, so relief would properly be sought against Texas itself.[1] Suing Texas, however, would force litigation over New York's [[sanctuary-cities|sanctuary city]] declaration and could end the sanctuary city model more broadly — which, in her reading, explains why Adams instead sued private carriers "for not denying a government order."[1]
