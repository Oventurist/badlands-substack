# 2028 Los Angeles Olympics

The 2028 Summer Olympics are scheduled to be held in Los Angeles, and by March 2025 had become a subject of political prediction in American media and business circles.[1]

## Shapiro's "Donald Trump's Olympics" prediction

Speaking at a Morgan Stanley media conference in March 2025, [[endeavor|Endeavor]] and TKO executive Mark Shapiro predicted that the Games would be "out-of-control good" despite the setback of that year's Los Angeles wildfires, and that credit for their success would be taken by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Shapiro argued that the "L.A. and Back" narrative would drive strong interest in corporate hospitality packages offered by TKO's On Location subsidiary.[1] Asked by Morgan Stanley analyst Ben Swinburne whether he was bullish that the Games would go on as planned despite the fires, Shapiro replied: "They've had time to rebuild, and it's going to be a coming-out party."[1]

Turning political without prompting, Shapiro said: "President Trump is going to walk out of office with the trophy that is the L.A. Olympics, that 'I did it, I brought it back, I was behind it, I put the resources, the support.' He'll be sitting front and center. I'm a true believer that this will be his Olympics as much as anything else."[1]

## Parallel with the 1984 games

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] observed in February 2025 that Los Angeles will host the Olympics in 2028 during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s presidency, just as it hosted the [[1984-los-angeles-olympics|1984 games]] during [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]]'s, an event he says brought a resurgence of American pride throughout the nation.[2] Asking "what are the odds?", Carlson counted the repetition among the many Reagan–Trump correspondences he regards as non-coincidental.[2]
