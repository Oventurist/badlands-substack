# US Energy Cartel

"US Energy Cartel" is the term used by Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to describe the American oil and energy industry, argued to function as a price-setting cartel comparable to [[opec|OPEC]].[1]

## Trump's warnings

In late June 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] issued direct public warnings to US oil companies for failing to cut prices at the pump quickly enough as geopolitical risk eased.[1] Burning Bright argued these warnings deserved to be "filed away for safe keeping," on the premise that Trump had not destabilized one cartel only to prop up another.[1]

## The pricing asymmetry argument

Energy analysts in financial media defended the delay by citing the mechanistic lag between chokepoint easing and retail pricing.[1] Burning Bright conceded the technical point but argued it missed Trump's target: pump prices surged on the news of Trump's campaign in Iran and continued climbing with every hint of bad news, pausing only temporarily on the opposite, demonstrating that domestic energy and manufacturing companies price in consumer pain based on futures markets whose actuals have not materialized, while failing to pass through relief when the same futures markets turn dovish.[1]

## Venezuela and international disentanglement

The analysis further held that Trump had effectively removed the US oil cartel from Venezuela and was nationalizing that foreign market by proxy for the benefit of a population that American corporations had been "robbing blind for decades."[1] Trump's disentanglement of the international oil market, on this reading, is mediated through Islamic regimes and narco-dictators, but the underlying plans were drafted in Big Oil boardrooms long before being handed to the US intelligence apparatus.[1]

The conclusion offered was that the price at the pump is not a financial metric but a mass psychological one, and that Trump understands why "the last remaining cartel" is raising it ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].[1]
