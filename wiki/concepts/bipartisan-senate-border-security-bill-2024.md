# Bipartisan Senate Border Security Bill (2024)

The **bipartisan Senate border security bill** of early 2024 was a negotiated national security package pairing border reforms with funding for Israel and Ukraine, which House Republicans refused to pass on the grounds that it was deeply flawed.[1]

## White House pressure campaign

The [[white-house|White House]] responded by playing hardball, with the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration warning of open border chaos if House Republicans did not agree to the Senate-negotiated deal.[1] Press Secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] told reporters aboard Air Force One that "because congressional Republicans are choosing partisan politics over our national security and refusing to pass the bipartisan national security agreement that includes significant border reforms and funding, over the coming weeks, ICE will be forced to reduce operations because of budget shortfalls."[1] She added that "we have asked Congress for additional funding and resources, and every time Congress has provided less than we asked for, or most recently, completely ignored our supplemental request."[1]

Critics characterized the posture as the administration threatening to leave the border open unless [[us-congress|Congress]] capitulated, effectively making reduced [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] operations the consequence of legislative refusal.[1]
