# US–Houthi Ceasefire Announcement (2025)

In May 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that the United States would stop bombing the [[houthis|Houthis]] in Yemen, saying the Iran-aligned group had agreed to stop interrupting important shipping lanes in the Middle East.[1]

## Announcement

The comments came during an Oval Office meeting with Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]], when Trump announced that the Houthis had said they no longer wanted to fight, without elaborating on the message.[1] "They said please don't bomb us anymore and we're not going to attack your ships," Trump said. There was no immediate response from the Houthis.[1]

## Israeli reaction

Israel was not informed in advance about the announcement, an informed source told *The Jerusalem Post*.[1] Asked about the conflict between the Houthis and Israel, Trump appeared to treat it as a separate and independent situation, saying: "I don't know anything about that, but they want nothing to do with us."[1] Israeli journalist [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]] offered a similar read, reporting on his X account that Trump did not seem fazed by the prospect that Israel and the Houthis might continue fighting one another.[1]

## Commentary

*Badlands News Brief* contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that during the Carney meeting Trump, discussing his upcoming trip to the Middle East, said he had a major announcement forthcoming, and speculated that it could be Iran joining the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] — something Trump teased in an interview with Hugh Hewitt on October 7 — along with U.S. moves toward normalizing relations with Tehran.[1] Given Israel's recent announcement that it intended to restart a major offensive campaign in Gaza, the commentary argued it would be a striking reversal for Trump to make peace with all of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s enemies, leaving the Israeli prime minister isolated.[1]
