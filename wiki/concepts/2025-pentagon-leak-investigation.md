# 2025 Pentagon Leak Investigation

The 2025 Pentagon leak investigation was an internal [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] probe into unauthorized disclosures of sensitive information that resulted in the suspension of two senior political appointees in April 2025.[1]

## Suspensions

On Tuesday, April 15, 2025, the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] placed two top political appointees on administrative leave following the probe, according to three defense officials cited by [[politico|Politico]].[1] [[dan-caldwell|Dan Caldwell]], a senior adviser to Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], was escorted out of the building by security officers and had his access suspended pending further investigation.[1] [[darin-selnick|Darin Selnick]], the Pentagon's deputy chief of staff, was suspended and escorted out as part of the same probe.[1]

## Material under investigation

The leaks under review reportedly included military operational plans for the [[panama-canal|Panama Canal]]; the movement of a second aircraft carrier to the Red Sea; [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s controversial visit to the Pentagon; and the pausing of intelligence collection shared with [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected the episode to the [[badlands-book-club|Badlands Book Club]] discussion of [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s *[[government-gangsters|Government Gangsters]]*, in which Patel argues for removing officials disloyal to the [[us-constitution|Constitution]] from positions of power.[1] She quoted Patel's contention that "the Deep State has a lot of power and hatred, but they don't have a lot of imagination," and that its recurring strategy is to fabricate a claim, have friendly media amplify it, and leak classified intelligence to attack [[donald-trump|Trump]].[1] Observing that the leaked subjects spanned Panama, the Red Sea, Ukraine, and the Pentagon, she suggested the disclosures were selected for maximum headline damage to the administration and argued that dismissal should be followed by prosecution under racketeering theories.[1]
