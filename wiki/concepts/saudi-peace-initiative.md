# Saudi Peace Initiative

The Saudi Peace Initiative of 2002 is the Arab diplomatic framework offering normalisation of relations with [[israel|Israel]] in exchange for a settlement of the Palestinian question, and it required a two-state solution as a precondition.[1]

## Contrast with the Abraham Accords

In the April 3, 2026 Badlands Brief, Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] is cited explaining how the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] have been used to subvert the Saudi Peace Initiative — that is, to obtain Arab normalisation with Israel while stripping out the two-state requirement that the 2002 initiative had made central.[1]

Badlands presents Lavrov's argument as one strand in a broader Russian rhetorical offensive against Israel during the [[iran-war|Iran War]], alongside [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]]'s demand that Israel halt its attacks on [[iran|Iran]] and [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]]'s warning over strikes on the [[bushehr-nuclear-power-plant|Bushehr nuclear power plant]].[1] The initiative's continued invocation by [[russia|Russia]] and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] is read as evidence that the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] is scheming against Israel specifically, rather than against the conflict's other participants.[1]
