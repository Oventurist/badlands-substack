# 2025 Miami Mayoral Election

The 2025 Miami mayoral election, a technically nonpartisan contest, resulted in Democrat [[eileen-higgins|Eileen Higgins]] defeating Republican Emilio Gonzalez and flipping the office for the first time in nearly three decades, per Decision Desk HQ.[1] Higgins became the first Democrat to serve as mayor of Miami since 1997.[1]

Gonzalez, a former Miami city manager, had served on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Homeland Security Department transition team.[1] The winner succeeded incumbent Mayor Francis Suarez, a Republican.[1]

The result was framed as part of a broader Democratic surge following better-than-expected November elections and a strong performance in a special House election in Tennessee, with the party hoping an energized base and a focus on affordability would let it flip the House and possibly the Senate in the coming midterms.[1]
