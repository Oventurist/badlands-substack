# Russian March

The Russian March is an annual demonstration in Russia that draws ultranationalists, including participants who adopt swastika-like symbols and chant slogans such as "Russia for ethnic Russians."[1] It is described in Western reporting as a rally associated with ultra-nationalist far-right groups.[1]

## Association with Alexei Navalny

The march returned to public attention in February 2024 after the death of [[alexei-navalny|Alexei Navalny]], when commentators revisited what a Yahoo News report called the more uncomfortable aspects of his career.[1] Navalny took part in the Russian March and never apologised for that decision or for his earliest xenophobic videos.[1] Shortly before releasing videos in which he compared Muslim immigrants to "cockroaches" and migrants in Moscow to tooth cavities requiring "full sanitisation," he was expelled from the liberal Yabloko party over his "nationalist activities," having participated in the march.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] used this record to argue that the Navalny being eulogised by Western liberal pundits would have been cancelled in the United States were he not serving as the Western establishment's designated answer to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1]
