**GART 10** was the tenth stop on the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live event series produced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in Cocoa Beach, Florida.[1] It was announced to subscribers on October 6, 2025 in a post authored by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who addressed readers as "Badlanders!" and told them the event would take place "next month," bringing audiences together with "your favorite Badlands hosts."[1]

## Framing and significance

The announcement described the tour's events as presenting "a great opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots," and as creating "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[1] GART 10 was billed as the fourth GART "of the new American Golden Age" and as Badlands Media's second event touching down in Florida.[1] The organizers noted that the previous Cocoa Beach stop had been "absolutely fantastic" and was considered a favorite among both hosts and attendees, and said they expected the tenth stop to be no different.[1]

Because the Cocoa Beach venue was smaller than the one used for the preceding Deadwood event (see [[gart-9-deadwood|GART 9: Deadwood]]), the announcement warned that GART 10 could sell out and that single-day tickets could not be guaranteed.[1]

## Ticketing

Three ticket tiers were described.[1] VIP admission granted access to a Thursday night dinner and meet-and-greet with Badlands Media personalities.[1] General Admission granted access to the Friday night welcome reception and party along with three days of panels.[1] In-person tickets were sold through a TicketSpice page dedicated to the Cocoa Beach event, with a pricing breakdown of what each package included.[1]

A virtual ticket allowed purchasers to stream the entire event, including the Friday, Saturday and Sunday panels, and included long-term DVR access to panel replays after the weekend so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss the programming.[1] The announcement stated that virtual GART prices had been "slashed significantly," with a further discount for orders placed before October 15, 2025.[1]

## GART Telegram chat

Virtual tickets also conferred access to an exclusive GART Telegram chat, with invitation links to be distributed during the month of the event.[1] The post described the group as a place where hundreds of Badlanders and Badlands hosts congregate, share behind-the-scenes photos and videos, and "engage in virtual shenanigans galore."[1] According to the announcement, these Telegram groups typically become long-running chat rooms for the network's most dedicated audience members, thriving for months after each event and serving as a venue to follow the GART action, pass the time between events "in entertaining and memetic fashion," and document the journey to the next tour stop.[1] Burning Bright added: "You should join it, is what I'm saying."[1]

The post closed on behalf of the whole Badlands Media team, saying they hoped to see familiar faces and, "as has happened at every GART so far, plenty of new ones," and ended with Burning Bright's customary sign-off, "Stay Bright!"[1]

## Final ticket call (November 2025)

On November 2, 2025, days before the event, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] published a follow-up post titled "Last Call for GART: Cocoa Beach!", subtitled "Single Day AND Virtual Tickets on Sale Now!"[2] It told readers that "this week, many of you will be joining your favorite Badlands hosts in Cocoa Beach, Florida" for the tenth stop of the tour, which it characterised as the network's "second event in the land of rockets both real and fake" — a reference to Florida's Space Coast — and reaffirmed its status as the fourth GART of "the new American Golden Age."[2]

The post reported that a handful of VIP and full-weekend General Admission tickets were still available, and that a limited number of single-day tickets had been released for those near the area who wanted to attend on one or two of Friday, Saturday or Sunday.[2] In-person sales continued through the event's TicketSpice page, with a separate page for virtual passes.[2] The virtual ticket again included streaming of the entire event with panels on all three days and long-term DVR access to replays, plus entry to the exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat, which the post said was "already bumpin'" with hundreds of fellow Badlanders and hosts sharing behind-the-scenes photos and videos.[2]

The announcement also published the full schedule for GART: Cocoa Beach as a graphic linked to the [[badlandsmedia-tv|BadlandsMedia.tv]] event page, and closed on behalf of the whole [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] team with the hope of seeing "some familiar faces and, as has happened at every GART so far, plenty of new ones," ending with the customary "Stay Bright!"[2]
