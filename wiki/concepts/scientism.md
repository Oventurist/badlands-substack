# Scientism

Scientism, in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] usage, refers to what its writers describe as the globally acceptable state religion: a faith-based belief system presented in the language of science and enforced through public institutions.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that scientism "usually presents as 'climate change' or 'gender ideology' — weather communism or rainbow communism," and that both domains are "entirely unscientific, faith-based belief systems."[1] On this account, only state-approved religious messaging is allowed in public spaces, a dynamic she illustrated with the [[football-association|Football Association's]] treatment of [[marc-guehi|Marc Guehi]]'s "I love Jesus" armband and the [[ontario-human-rights-commission|Ontario Human Rights Commission's]] penalties against Emo mayor [[harold-mcquaker|Harold McQuaker]].[1]

She contended that the state — "whether it's England, Canada or the UN" — does not get to pick acceptable religions, claim them as science, elevate their acceptance, and demand compliance, but has done exactly this "in every nation and institution," succeeding "only because the emotional blackmail of tolerance created the apathy that allowed it."[1] Her formulation: "Weaponized empathy results in apathy, impotence, or both."[1] The proposed remedy was global adoption of the American [[first-amendment|First Amendment]], which she called "one of the most beautiful sentences ever written."[1]
