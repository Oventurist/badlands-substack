# Rashidun Caliphate

The Rashidun ("Rightly Guided") Caliphate was the state governed for thirty years after the death of the Prophet [[muhammad|Muhammad]] by four of his trusted companions, each sequentially elected by their peers.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

Muhammad left no dictated line of succession; most of his followers supported [[abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], the first caliph.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Under Abu Bakr the Quran was written.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Over the caliphate's span the empire grew from modern-day Libya to India, becoming the most powerful military, cultural, and economic force in West Asia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The fourth of these caliphs was [[ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], whose contested status as Muhammad's intended heir gave rise to the [[shia-islam|Shia]]–[[sunni-islam|Sunni]] division.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

Historically, most caliphs of Islam have been either sharifs (descendants of Muhammad) or members of the Banu Hashim — unlike the modern rulers of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
