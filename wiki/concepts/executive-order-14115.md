# Executive Order 14115

Executive Order 14115 was an order issued by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] imposing sanctions on Israeli settlers in the West Bank who engaged in violence against Palestinians. It was among the many Biden-era orders revoked by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 20, 2025, his first day back in office.[1]

Badlands Media commentary offered a two-sided reading of the revocation. On the surface, rescinding the settler sanctions appeared to be a concession to the radical elements of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s cabinet at a moment when violence by the [[israeli-settler-movement|Settler Movement]] against Palestinians was escalating in the West Bank. But the commentary argued that the order's real significance was domestic: it empowered the federal government to control the transfer of property between US citizens on the basis of mere suspicion of specific conduct, apparently without conviction or due process.[1]

The Badlands position was that better remedies existed than expanding federal power — chiefly conditioning American aid and trade to Israel on behavior, a form of leverage that Middle East envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] was said to have raised explicitly in his own remarks around the inauguration.[1]
