# UFC 314

UFC 314 was a mixed martial arts event held on Saturday 12 April 2025 at the UFC Apex in Las Vegas, Nevada, and broadcast on ESPN+ pay-per-view.[1]

Among the card's highlights was a light heavyweight bout in which [[dominick-reyes|Dominick Reyes]] knocked out Nikita Krylov with a counter left hand, inflicting the first true knockout loss of Krylov's long career.[1] Reyes saluted President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who attended cageside.[1]

Badlands commentary focused on Trump's reception by the crowd, contrasting an earlier appearance where he was received as "a gladiator, and the people's champion" with this one, where the writer said he was greeted as "a conquering hero," and closing with "Rubicon. Crossed."[1]
