# Madleen Flotilla Interception

The Madleen flotilla interception was the seizure by Israeli naval forces of the Gaza-bound civilian aid boat Madleen in international waters in early June 2025.[1] The vessel, operated by the [[freedom-flotilla-coalition|Freedom Flotilla Coalition]], was attempting to deliver humanitarian aid to Gaza and carried climate activist [[greta-thunberg|Greta Thunberg]], French MEP Rima Hassan and Irish actor Liam Cunningham among its crew.[1]

## The interception

Israeli forces intercepted the boat early on a Monday and towed it to the Israeli port of Ashdod.[1] Israel's Foreign Ministry confirmed the detention and said the group was "expected to return to their home countries," posting on X that "All the passengers of the 'selfie yacht' are safe and unharmed. They were provided with sandwiches and water. The show is over."[1] Tel Aviv had dubbed the vessel a "selfie yacht."[1] Before the interception, Thunberg reported that the boat had been sprayed with a mysterious white substance by an IDF aircraft, prompting speculation that it was either a bioweapon or a marking substance intended to increase the vessel's visibility to other Israeli aircraft and ships at night.[1]

He further noted that the event fell on the 58th anniversary of the USS Liberty incident, in which the Israeli Defense Force attacked and nearly sank a U.S. Navy ship off the coast of Israel and Gaza.[1] The potential net effect, he argued, was that a privileged Swedish celebrity and her European compatriots might become Islamic martyrs for the Third World if harmed in IDF custody — "the inversion of woke."[1] He concluded that whether or not the affair was a manufactured psyop, it represented "some of the best writing we've seen out of Hollywood in years," and that reality needed to become absurd enough to break the matrix, adding that the IDF ultimately fed the detainees halal bread.[1]
