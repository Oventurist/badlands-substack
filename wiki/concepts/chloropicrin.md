# Chloropicrin

Chloropicrin is a choking agent banned under the [[chemical-weapons-convention|Chemical Weapons Convention]].[1]

## Role in Russian allegations against Ukraine

In early July 2025, [[russia|Russia]] announced the discovery of a makeshift laboratory allegedly used by Ukrainian troops to store chloropicrin, and claimed that Ukrainian forces had dropped toxic substances from drones.[1] Moscow subsequently asked the [[organization-for-the-prohibition-of-chemical-weapons|Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons]] to investigate, with envoy [[vladimir-tarabrin|Vladimir Tarabrin]] telling the OPCW Executive Council in The Hague that Russia was documenting both individual uses of toxic chemicals and military-grade poison agents and evidence of a widespread system for their mass production in Ukraine.[1]
