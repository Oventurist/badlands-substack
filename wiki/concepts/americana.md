# Americana

"Americana" is used in the Badlands corpus to describe the commercial brands and cultural markers held to embody American identity, and which are said to be targets in the [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist takeover of America]].[1]

## The brand list

Asked by [[andy-frisella|Andy Frisella]]'s argument to identify which brands most represent American culture, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] named, in no particular order, Coca-Cola, Disney, Levi's, Hershey's, Budweiser and McDonald's, allowing that the list may differ from person to person.[1]

## The destruction hypothesis

Carlson observed that other than perhaps McDonald's, all of these companies had gone out of their way to upset their customer base through [[wokeness|wokeness]].[1] He asked whether Communists were forcing the companies woke specifically in order to destroy them and create a new, less American culture, and whether consumers boycotting them were unwittingly completing the operation.[1] McDonald's exemption from the pattern prompted a separate speculation: that it is permitted to prosper "woke-free" because it serves to make Americans sick and unhealthy.[1]

## Identity as the first target

The destruction of Americana occupies the first position in Carlson's stated sequence for the takeover — strip Americans of their American identity, then destroy American society, values and morals, then destroy the economy.[1]
