# 2025 Minnesota Lawmaker Shootings

The 2025 Minnesota lawmaker shootings were a pair of targeted attacks carried out in the early hours of Saturday, June 14, 2025, in suburbs of Minneapolis, Minnesota, that killed Minnesota House Speaker [[melissa-hortman|Melissa Hortman]] and her husband Mark at their home in Brooklyn Park and wounded Democratic State Senator [[john-hoffman|John Hoffman]] and his wife Yvette at their home in Champlin.[1]

## Events

Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] described the shootings as "politically motivated" in a news briefing on the day of the attacks.[1] Investigators said the shooter was dressed as a police officer, and authorities identified 57-year-old [[vance-boelter|Vance Luther Boelter]] as the suspect.[1] Boelter's car was located on Sunday, and the Minnesota State Patrol reported that a stack of "No Kings" flyers was found inside it — literature referencing the anti-Trump [[no-kings-protests|"No Kings" protests]] staged in cities nationwide that same Saturday.[1] Statewide "No Kings" events planned for Minnesota were canceled after the shootings.[1] Boelter was arrested in Sibley County on the evening of Sunday, June 15, 2025, a Minnesota police source confirmed to Fox News.[1]

Records showed Boelter had served on a state board with Hoffman for more than six years, and investigators said they were examining whether he knew either lawmaker personally.[1]

## Reaction and commentary

The shootings occurred on the same weekend as the [[2025-army-250th-birthday-military-parade|Army 250th birthday military parade]] in Washington, D.C., and were widely cited in press coverage as evidence of political violence shadowing the celebration.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] called the killings tragic and criticized the speed with which figures in both major parties and online commentators sought to attribute the assassin to an opposing party, arguing that "political parties are fake" and that such conflict served to distract from what the outlet characterized as the bipartisan expansion of globalism.[1] Commentators also circulated speculation about Boelter's unusual biography and possible institutional ties.[1]

## Arrest and charges

After a 43-hour manhunt and intense search, authorities arrested [[vance-boelter]], 57, a Minnesota man accused of shooting two state Democratic lawmakers and their spouses; he faced both federal and state charges in connection with the killings of [[melissa-hortman|Melissa Hortman]] and her husband, Mark.[2] He was also accused of shooting Democratic state Senator [[john-hoffman|John Hoffman]] and his wife, Yvette, both of whom survived the attack, and of going to the homes of two other unnamed state lawmakers that morning "with the intent to kill them," according to authorities.[2] Boelter was apprehended Sunday night in a wooded area near where he lives, Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] said at a news conference, after authorities deployed hundreds of detectives and 20 SWAT teams to assist with the arrest and capture.[2]
