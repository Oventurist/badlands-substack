# New York City Fiscal Year 2027 Preliminary Budget

The New York City fiscal year 2027 preliminary budget is a $127 billion spending plan released on Tuesday, February 17, 2026, by Mayor [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]].[1]

## Property tax proposal

The plan includes a proposed 9.5 percent hike in property tax rates, the first such increase in more than two decades.[1] Mamdani described the move as a reluctant "last resort" to address a $5.4 billion deficit he said he inherited from the previous administration, which he blamed on years of fiscal mismanagement.[1] The hike would generate roughly $3.7 billion.[1]

## Two paths

Mamdani laid out two paths for closing the gap: pressing Governor [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]] and state lawmakers in Albany to raise taxes on high earners and profitable corporations, or relying on the limited tools available to the city — meaning the broad-based property tax increase on millions of homes and commercial properties, along with withdrawals from reserve funds.[1] City Comptroller [[mark-levine|Mark Levine]] called the property tax option "a pretty extreme option."[1]
