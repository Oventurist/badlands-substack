# Discernment

**Discernment** is presented as the navigational faculty of awakening in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], a follow-up to the earlier essay "The Bridge."[1] The article calls discernment "your compass."[1]

## Definition in the article

The essay defines discernment as more than the identification of deception: it is the recognition of what serves a person's growth and what drains it.[1] Rather than reacting emotionally to surrounding distortions, the practitioner learns to observe, question, and choose where to direct their energy.[1]

Discernment is introduced as the answer to what the article calls the first challenge of awakening — realising that the world operates on multiple frequencies, some tuned to fear, scarcity and control, others to coherence, connection and possibility.[1] The resulting dissonance can leave two people physically present at the same table while perceiving entirely different worlds; the article frames this fracturing not as chaos but as a sign of expansion.[1] [[david-bohm|David Bohm]] is invoked for the claim that reality is an "unbroken wholeness" beneath its fragmented surface, and [[ervin-laszlo|Ervin Laszlo]] for the description of the universe as "a symphony of vibrations."[1]

[[ken-wilber|Ken Wilber]]'s AQAL framework is offered as a lens in which awakening integrates the inner self (consciousness), the body (biology and energy systems), relationships (interpersonal reality), and societal structures (governing narratives); the article holds that true discernment bridges all four, rippling personal shifts into larger systems.[1]

## Grounding practices

The article lists practices for grounding awareness: limiting exposure to fear-based narratives by swapping an hour of newsfeed scrolling for a walk in silence; trusting the felt sense of the body and asking whether tension after a conversation is one's own or absorbed from another's frequency; holding space for others who cling to old beliefs out of fear; and developing a ritual of integration, such as writing each evening three things noticed shifting in oneself, building over time a map of transformation.[1]

The section's stated key takeaway is that discernment is the compass and that patience is required while rewiring how one engages with reality — "the bridge steadies as you learn to listen."[1]
