# Venture Capital

Venture capital is the private financing industry that funds early-stage technology companies in exchange for equity, and in Badlands commentary it is treated as inseparable from the [[big-tech|Big Tech]] sector it built.[1]

## Political turn in 2024

In an August 2024 essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] listed venture capitalists and crypto figures — [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], [[david-sacks|David Sacks]], [[marc-andreessen|Marc Andreessen]] and [[ben-horowitz|Ben Horowitz]] — among those who had recently and publicly climbed aboard the Trump Train.[1] He argued that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s choice of [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] as running mate made sense precisely because Vance is connected to the venture capital world, has relationships within it, and understands how it works.[1] Vance, in Carlson's framing, is not seen by that world as another clueless politician who calls only when he wants money.[1]

Carlson wrote that Trump and these financiers were seeking a genuine working relationship rather than a transactional one, and speculated that cryptocurrency may be the single biggest reason for the alignment.[1]

## Relationship to state capital

The essay also situates venture capital downstream of government money, claiming that many of the largest technology firms were created, funded and grown by [[darpa|DARPA]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] before private investors compounded those advantages.[1] Carlson's argument for the 2024 defection is that these investors have concluded the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] now intends to destroy America and its economy, and that if they stick with it everything they built will be destroyed with the country.[1]
