# Badlands Blitz Episode 18

**Badlands Blitz Episode 18**, subtitled "Tina Peters Coming Out; Speech Laws Coming In," is the eighteenth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video recap published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] through its Substack newsletter. The announcement post is dated May 23, 2026 and carried 206 likes at the time of the source capture. It opens with the customary "Badlanders!" salutation and restates the program's standing remit as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." Unusually, the post begins with an apology for the episode's late release, which the team attributes to "a miscommunication."

## Hosts

The episode is hosted by [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], both linked in the post to their Substack author profiles. The pairing continues the program's practice of rotating hosting duties among Badlands contributors rather than assigning a fixed presenter.

## Content

Two subjects are announced. The first is the commutation of the sentence of [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]], the former Colorado county clerk whose prosecution over election-equipment access has been a recurring cause among election-integrity advocates; the subtitle's phrase "Tina Peters Coming Out" refers to her release. The second is what the post calls "the coming ban on antisemitism and other speech laws," a framing that treats pending or anticipated legislation against antisemitism as part of a broader movement toward statutory restrictions on speech. The announcement does not enumerate specific bills, jurisdictions or sponsors; consistent with the program's house style, the detail is left to the video itself.

The post adds that the hosts "also hit on geopolitics and expectations for the golden age," the last phrase being a recurring term in Badlands commentary for an anticipated period of national renewal.

## Publication and support model

The post repeats the standing editorial notice that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," and that direct reader support is the only means by which the team is able to pay itself for its time. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription in recognition of "the step-up in said work for 2026," with cancellation available at any time. It closes with the program's customary sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
