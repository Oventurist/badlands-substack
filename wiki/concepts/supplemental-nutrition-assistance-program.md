# Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program

The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), also known as food stamps, is the principal United States federal food assistance program, administered through the [[usda|US Department of Agriculture]] in partnership with the states.[1] In late 2025 SNAP became the focus of litigation over the Trump administration's effort to restrict eligibility for certain immigrants.[1]

## 2025 eligibility restrictions

On October 31, 2025, the USDA issued guidance that would bar SNAP eligibility for certain lawful permanent residents, including refugees and asylum seekers.[1] The guidance stems from changes to SNAP made under the [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|One Big Beautiful Bill Act]], signed into law by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on July 4; provisions in that law significantly reduced the number of immigrants who can qualify for supplemental food assistance.[1]

## Litigation and delay

A coalition of 20 state attorneys general filed suit on November 26, 2025, arguing that the administration had failed to provide a legally required 120-day compliance period.[1] On Monday, December 15, 2025, a federal judge ruled that the USDA must allow states additional time to bring themselves into compliance with the new guidance.[1] Judge Kasubhai wrote that "the inability to provide compliance in the time period in which they were forced to by virtue of the guidance contributed to an erosion of trust," and extended the grace period to April 9, 2026.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarised the ruling as "if you like your food stamps, you can keep your food stamps – at least until April 2026," and remarked that it was "cute that the judge thinks there is still public trust."[1] She suggested that while a challenge to the ruling could usefully clarify presidential authorities, spending the people's resources contesting a four-month compliance grace period was a low priority given that "there's still a treasonous conspiracy to prosecute."[1]
