# Memeplex

**Memeplex** is the term used in Part 4 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]] (15 February 2025) for a coherent set of ideas, narratives and beliefs that must be held by a population for a given power structure to remain viable.[1] The article's subtitle frames current events as "Q, Trump, Anons and a Battle of Memeplexes," and its concluding section presents the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] as the outcome of a contest between two such systems.[1]

## The Clown World memeplex

[[clown-world|Clown World]] is defined in the essay as a memeplex — glossed parenthetically as "the set of ideas, narratives, and beliefs that the masses must believe in order for Clown world to remain viable."[1] Clown World and its agents are described as attempting to steer mankind in one direction, with dark occultism characterised as the manipulation of reality through the manipulation of human minds.[1] Its supporting apparatus is said to include a shadow budget of untold trillions of dollars, mass censorship, and media assets who pose as Q followers in order to make the movement look ridiculous.[1]

## The counter memeplex

Against this the Q team is described as acting in direct opposition by mimetically seeding certain ideas into the collective, using [[anons|anons]] and "autists" as a kind of signal repeater.[1] What those researchers work out is held to become in itself another thought-form capable of influencing the minds of the masses with hardly anyone noticing, until a critical threshold is passed at which almost everyone begins to notice.[1] The author states that autists "picked up the puzzle and collectively formulated a *counter memeplex* of astounding power, and culture is shifting because of it."[1]

## Assessing the outcome

The essay proposes four diagnostic questions for assessing the operation's success over the preceding decade: whether awareness of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] has increased or decreased, whether awareness of elite child trafficking has increased or decreased, whether trust in mainstream news has increased or decreased, and whether awareness of the contrived nature of current events has increased or decreased.[1] Weighing these against the censorship faced by the movement and the resources arrayed against it, the author concludes that it is "beyond obvious that Q and [[donald-trump|Trump]] have defeated the 'memeplex' that is Clown World," citing Trump's 2024 victory as the answer to the narrative war.[1] He allows that most people do not recognise the hand Q played in the shift and treats that as unimportant to the result.[1]
