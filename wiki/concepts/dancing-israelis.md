# Dancing Israelis

"The Dancing Israelis" is the popular name for an incident on September 11, 2001, in which eyewitnesses reported seeing "Middle Eastern" men "dancing and celebrating" across the river in New Jersey as the Twin Towers burned.[1] Officials were alerted, and the men were questioned and subsequently arrested.[1]

According to Badlands Media, explosive residue, box-cutters, and Arab clothing were found in the moving van the men were using, which was registered under [[urban-moving-systems|Urban Moving Systems]] — later described as a [[mossad]] front company managed by the Israeli national Dominic Suter.[1] The arrested men — Yaron Shmuel, Omer Marmari, Sivan and Paul Kurzberg, and Oded Ellner — are identified in the account as Mossad agents.[1]

The episode is presented as one of several strands of alleged Israeli foreknowledge of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], alongside the [[odigo]] warning messages and the abrupt departure of the shipping company [[zim-integrated-shipping-services|ZIM]] from the North Tower a week before the attacks.[1] The article's author explicitly disclaims any suggestion of a "vast Jewish conspiracy," arguing only that elements of Israel's government acting without oversight are as susceptible to corruption as any other power center, and notes that many of these points are contested.[1]
