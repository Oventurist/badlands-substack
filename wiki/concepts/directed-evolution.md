# Directed Evolution

"Directed evolution" is the term that entered political controversy in January 2023 after an undercover [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]] video captured a figure identified as [[pfizer|Pfizer]] executive [[jordan-trishton-walker|Jordan Trishton Walker]] describing a company plan to mutate SARS-CoV-2 in order to anticipate — and profit from — future variants, keeping COVID-19 a "cash cow."[1]

## Relationship to gain-of-function research

Critics treated the phrase as a rebranding exercise. [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that recasting [[gain-of-function-research|gain-of-function research]] as "directed evolution" was a deliberate softening of terminology that was not meant to be exposed publicly at that point, and that the muted press response reflected an interrupted narrative-management operation rather than an absence of newsworthiness.[1] In this reading, control of terminology precedes control of opinion: the framing must be established through friendly talking heads and influencers before the underlying activity becomes public.[1]

## Suppression of the story

The controversy was amplified less by the underlying scientific claim than by the response to it. Coverage described intermittent Big Tech throttling, a partial media blackout, and the removal of an article on the sting by a prominent tabloid, the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]], even as members of Congress expressed interest in the footage.[1] Commentators linked this pattern to [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]]-style coordination, the [[asch-conformity-experiments|Asch conformity experiments]] on social conformity, and the search-ranking disclosures of Google whistleblower [[zack-vorhies|Zack Vorhies]].[1]
