# 1MDB Scandal

The **1MDB scandal** is an international corruption and money-laundering affair arising from the looting of 1Malaysia Development Berhad, a Malaysian sovereign wealth and strategic development fund. Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] described it as "a massive embezzlement operation to steal $5 Billion from the Malaysian sovereign wealth fund 1Malaysia Development Berhad (1MDB)," an enterprise that "implicates bankers, Hollywood, celebrities, and government officials in ten different nations across five continents."[1]

## Central figures

The scheme is most closely associated with the Malaysian financier [[jho-low|Low Taek Jho, known as Jho Low]], who directed undisclosed lobbying campaigns aimed at persuading the United States government to abandon its investigation of the fund.[1] Others named in connection with the associated influence operation include [[elliott-broidy|Elliott Broidy]], Nickie Lum Davis, George Higginbotham and the entertainer [[pras-michel|Prakazrel "Pras" Michel]].[1]

Just Human noted that the scandal even reaches [[turki-bin-abdullah-al-saud|Turki bin Abdullah Al Saud]], one of the eleven Saudi princes detained by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in 2017.[1]

## United States prosecutions

On 26 April 2023 a federal jury convicted Pras Michel of orchestrating an unregistered back-channel campaign, begun in or about 2017, to influence the then-administration of the President of the United States and the [[us-department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] to drop the investigation of Jho Low and others for embezzlement and related offences connected to 1MDB, and to secure the return of a Chinese national to China.[1] The same prosecution covered conspiring to make and conceal foreign and conduit campaign contributions during the 2012 U.S. presidential election.[1] Michel was convicted on ten counts, including conspiracy, concealment, false records, witness tampering and violations of the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]].[1]

## International spread

On the same day as the Michel verdict, Swiss authorities announced the indictment of two further 1MDB conspirators, executives at [[petrosaudi|PetroSaudi]], a company co-founded by Turki bin Abdullah Al Saud.[1] Badlands commentary concluded that the "massive kleptocratic scandal ain't over yet."[1]
