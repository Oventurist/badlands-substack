# Cuba Regime Change Planning 2026

Cuba regime change planning refers to reporting in March 2026 that the United States was weighing legal and strategic measures to escalate pressure on the leadership of [[cuba|Cuba]], with anonymous officials describing regime change on the island as "lined up."[1]

## Naval pressure and the Anatoly Kolodkin

A Russian oil tanker, the *Anatoly Kolodkin*, was approaching the Caribbean carrying crude oil to Cuba at a moment when the US Navy was increasing efforts to restrict fuel shipments to the island. The convergence framed the episode as an energy-interdiction contest between Washington and Moscow in the Western Hemisphere.[1]

## Economic collapse and migration

Cuba faced a deepening economic crisis marked by widespread blackouts, shortages and out-migration, while its government acknowledged ongoing discussions with the United States over potential reforms. About 10 percent of Cuba's population has left the country since COVID-19.[1]

## Reported contingency planning

According to [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]], officials familiar with internal planning said the United States was considering legal and strategic measures that could escalate pressure on Cuban leadership, including possible indictments and broader contingency plans. Anonymous officials told the outlet that regime change in Cuba is "lined up."[1]
