# 2024 St. Petersburg International Economic Forum

The **2024 St. Petersburg International Economic Forum (SPIEF)** was held in [[russia|Russia]] from June 5 to 8, 2024, under the theme "The Formation of New Areas of Growth as the Cornerstone of a Multipolar World."[1] The forum is organized by the Roscongress Foundation, with [[tass|TASS]] serving as its information partner.[1]

## Programme

Scheduled events included meetings for small and medium-sized businesses and creative industries, a SPIEF Youth Day, and the Drug Security, SPIEF Academy and SPIEF Junior forums.[1]

## Taliban participation

A delegation of the [[taliban|Taliban]] — a movement then still outlawed in Russia — attended the 2024 forum, led by Minister of Labor and Social Affairs Abdul Manan Omari and including the head of the Afghan Chamber of Commerce and Industry.[1] Russian special presidential envoy Zamir Kabulov said the foreign and justice ministries had reported to President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] that the movement could be removed from the list of banned organizations, an initiative Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] said reflected objective reality.[1] Taliban representatives said they had come seeking economic investment in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the delegation's presence in St. Petersburg as confirmation that Western portrayals of the group had been propagandistic, and as consistent with the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] framework in which the Taliban act against [[isis|ISIS]] and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] networks.[1]

## Additional coverage

The 2024 forum was held on June 5–8 under the theme "The Formation of New Areas of Growth as the Cornerstone of a Multipolar World," organized by the Roscongress Foundation with [[tass|TASS]] as information partner.[2] On its sidelines [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] told the heads of major international news agencies that claims Russia intended to attack [[nato|NATO]] were "bulls**t" designed to sustain Western hegemony through fear.[2] Zimbabwean President [[emmerson-mnangagwa|Emmerson Mnangagwa]] used the same venue to thank Russia for grain and fertilizer supplies, predict an "incredible harvest" for [[zimbabwe|Zimbabwe]], and declare that "Africa will soon roar like a lion."[2]
