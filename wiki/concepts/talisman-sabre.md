# Talisman Sabre

**Talisman Sabre** is a recurring joint military exercise conducted by the United States and Australia. The 2023 iteration ran from July 22 to August 4 and was billed as the largest-ever version of the drill, reflecting American focus on preparing for a potential future conflict in the Asia-Pacific.[1]

## 2023 stockpile in Australia

Following the 2023 exercise, the United States left behind a new stockpile of military equipment in Australia as preparation for a possible war with [[china|China]] over [[taiwan|Taiwan]].[1] According to Reuters reporting relayed by [[antiwar-com|AntiWar.com]], the equipment included 330 vehicles and trailers along with 130 containers stored in a warehouse in Bandiana, a suburb of Wodonga in southeastern Australia.[1] The supplies were described as sufficient to sustain three logistics companies whose role would be delivering equipment to US troops fighting elsewhere in the region.[1]

General Charles Flynn, the top US Army commander in the Pacific, said of the practice, "We're looking to do this more and more," adding that agreements already existed with a number of other countries in the region.[1] The United States was reported to be working to establish comparable stockpiles in the Philippines, Japan and elsewhere, while [[us-congress|Congress]] was separately examining a weapons stockpile inside Taiwan itself — a step that might be kept unpublicized because of the risk of provoking China.[1]
