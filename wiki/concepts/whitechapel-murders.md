# Whitechapel Murders

The Whitechapel Murders of 1888 — the killings attributed to "Jack the Ripper" — are treated by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] not as an ordinary criminal case but as an operation with occult and political dimensions.[1]

Ehret notes that the geometric configuration of the Whitechapel murder victims corresponded to the Vesica Pisces, the same Kabbalistic proportion that Sir [[charles-warren|Charles Warren]] believed underlay [[solomons-temple|Solomon's Temple]].[1] Warren, who had excavated beneath the [[temple-mount|Temple Mount]] and led the [[palestine-exploration-fund|Palestine Exploration Fund]], was Chief of London's Metropolitan Police during the murders.[1]

According to Ehret, the killings were accompanied by a serious anti-Jewish propaganda campaign designed to terrorize the Jews of England sufficiently to drive them out of Europe and toward the desert — an aim he treats as closely connected to the shaping of modern [[zionism|Zionism]].[1]
