# 2024 Meta global outage

On March 5, 2024 — the same day as [[2024-super-tuesday|Super Tuesday]] — [[meta-platforms|Meta]]-owned [[facebook|Facebook]] and [[instagram|Instagram]] suffered a global outage lasting more than two hours and affecting hundreds of thousands of users.[1]

The disruptions began around 10 a.m. Eastern time (1500 GMT), with many users reporting on the rival platform X that they had been booted out of Facebook and Instagram and could not log back in.[1] At the peak of the outage, the tracking site Downdetector.com registered more than 550,000 reports of disruption for Facebook and about 92,000 for Instagram.[1]

The White House National Security Council monitored the incident, and a spokesperson said it was not aware of any specific malicious cyber activity at the time.[1] Meta spokesman Andy Stone wrote on X: "Earlier today, a technical issue caused people to have difficulty accessing some of our services. We resolved the issue ... for everyone who was impacted," without elaborating on the cause.[1] Both services were back up by later that Tuesday.[1]
