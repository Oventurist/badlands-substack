# United States as a Federal Corporation

The claim that the **United States is a federal corporation** is a recurring theme in constitutionalist and sovereignty-oriented commentary. It was argued at length in a May 16, 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], which used the claim to question whether the public knows "the true form of our own government."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

## The statutory citation

The essay's central evidentiary point is Title 28 of the United States Code, section 3002(15)(A), which for purposes of the Federal Debt Collection Procedures chapter defines "United States" to include "a Federal corporation."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) The author observes that, in his reading, no other passage in the Code defines the *nature* of the United States; other provisions define it geographically, which he treats as immaterial to the question.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) Americans are variously told they live in a democracy and in a republic, but the essay asks for a citation proving either, and finds the corporate definition standing without counter-citation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

## Chain of authority

The article stresses that the corporate definition does not, by itself, dissolve the underlying constitutional order. Its governing premise is that no governmental body, agency, or commercial entity can exceed the authority of whatever created it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) On this view a corporate "United States" was at some point installed, but the pre-existing chain of authority remains intact above it: federal authority is sourced from the states, the states derive their authority from the people, and — per the [[declaration-of-independence|Declaration of Independence]] — ultimately from God.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) Corporations, the essay notes, cannot exist prior to nations or body politics.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

To substantiate the chain, the author quotes the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] decision in [[chisholm-v-georgia|Chisholm, Ex'r v. Georgia]] (cited in the article as February 1794), which held that a state does not owe its origin to the federal government, existed before it, and derives its authority from "the voluntary and deliberate choice of the people."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

## Alleged maladministration and "shell games"

Because, in the author's account, the corporate United States was installed without the knowledge or consent of the people, he characterises the situation as "an extraordinary act of maladministration" that "has been covered up almost completely," while conceding that "we do not yet know the full details of how all this works" and that the research remains ongoing.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

The essay attributes the confusion to what it calls *linguistic shell games* — the surreptitious redefinition of familiar words so that a speaker's ordinary meaning diverges from the meaning the system presumes, producing an effectively dual language.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) Multiple entities, it asserts, call themselves "the United States": a corporate one and a federal one, all of which nevertheless ultimately derive authority from the people and from God.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) Remove that foundation, the author argues, and none of the superstructure can stand.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

## Prescription

The essay frames public education on these matters as the "ground level" complement to reform "upstairs," suggesting that while [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[qanon|Q]] team address the problem from above, ordinary citizens can assist by informing themselves and stepping into their role as the de jure civilian authority — with an explicit disclaimer that no specific action is being recommended.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) The author closes by suggesting that simply sharing the Title 28 citation can be enough to prompt a reappraisal in others, noting that these facts were what shifted his own politics in his late twenties.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)
