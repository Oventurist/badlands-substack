# 2025 French Music Festival Syringe Attacks

In June 2025, 145 people across France reported having been pricked with syringes during the country's nationwide Fête de la Musique festival, prompting a criminal investigation and multiple arrests.[1]

## Incidents

The alleged stabbings occurred during Fête de la Musique, an annual music festival held across France every June 21.[1] According to reporting published Monday, June 23, twelve suspects had been arrested following the 145 claims.[1] The New York Times reported that 13 of the cases came out of Paris.[1] A representative for France's Interior Ministry told CNN that "toxicology tests are being done" to determine whether any of the victims had been injected with drugs or other substances.[1]
