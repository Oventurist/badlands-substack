# X Corp. v. Media Matters

**X Corp. v. Media Matters** is the defamation and business-disparagement lawsuit filed on Monday, November 20, 2023 by [[x-corp|X]] against the left-leaning non-profit [[media-matters|Media Matters]] and "all those who colluded" with it, over a report that X's owner said "completely misrepresent[ed]" the real user experience on the platform.[1] The suit was filed in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas, Fort Worth Division, and alleges that the organization's tactics were manipulative and deceptive.[1]

## Background

Early on Saturday, November 18, 2023, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] posted that X would be "filing a thermonuclear lawsuit" against Media Matters and its collaborators.[1] Musk stated the filing would come on Monday, a promise that generated widespread commentary on the platform over the intervening days, with pro-censorship commentators publicly speculating that he would not follow through — one CNBC reporter went so far as to claim that "Musk may have been lying, like he has done before."[1] The suit was in fact filed as promised.[1]

## Parallel state investigation

On the same day, Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] opened an investigation into Media Matters for potential fraudulent activity, stating that under the Texas Business Organizations Code and the Deceptive Trade Practices Act the Office of the Attorney General "will vigorously enforce against nonprofits who commit fraudulent acts in or affecting the state of Texas."[1] Paxton said he was "extremely troubled by the allegations that Media Matters, a radical anti-free speech organization, fraudulently manipulated data on X.com (formerly known as Twitter)," and that his office was "examining the issue closely to ensure that the public has not been deceived by the schemes of radical left-wing organizations who would like nothing more than to limit freedom by reducing participation in the public square."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the filing as part of "something of a lawsuit cascade developing in the current stage of the [[information-war|Info War]]," arguing that what stood out was less the target than the timing and the figures involved.[1] He described Musk as "an enigmatic figure among Anons and the public at large" who is nonetheless "standing in the breach against the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s Narrative power projection arm," while leaving open whether he does so in direct coordination with patriots.[1] Burning Bright predicted the suits were "just the beginning of a template-setting campaign meant to go scorched earth on those who've assaulted our constitutional right to free speech," fought simultaneously in court and in the court of public opinion ahead of what he called the most consequential election in US history.[1]

## Filing and advertiser exodus

The suit was filed on Monday, 20 November 2023, alleging that [[media-matters|Media Matters]] manipulated the X feed to produce screenshots of major advertisements beside Nazi imagery.[2] X's complaint stated that "the end result was a feed precision-designed by Media Matters for a single purpose: to produce side-by-side ad/content placements that it could screenshot in an effort to alienate advertisers," and that all but one of the companies featured in the Media Matters report pulled all advertising from the platform, including Apple, Comcast, NBCUniversal and IBM.[2] Reporting characterized the campaign as a successful effort to brand [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and X as antisemitic following Musk's agreement with a controversial post about liberal Jews.[2] In the days after the filing, early-2000s blog posts by Media Matters president [[angelo-carusone|Angelo Carusone]] resurfaced, adding a charge of hypocrisy to the dispute.[2]
