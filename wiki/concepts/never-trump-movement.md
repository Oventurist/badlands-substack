# Never Trump movement

The **Never Trump movement** is the loose coalition of Republican and conservative figures organized in opposition to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the MAGA political project.[1] Following Trump's victory in the November 2024 presidential election, Politico reported that the already fractured movement had splintered "into shards," leaving its most prominent figures scrambling for relevance in a reordered Republican Party.[1]

## Post-2024 collapse

In the weeks after the election, former U.N. Ambassador [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] criticized two of the president-elect's top appointees, [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], on her radio show; former Virginia Representative Barbara Comstock described Trump's nominees as a Cabinet of "Putinists and pedophiles"; and former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] attempted to rally anti-abortion conservatives against Kennedy's nomination as Health and Human Services secretary.[1] Politico judged that these critics were "screaming their words of caution from the sidelines" while Trump remade Washington with the backing of congressional Republicans who had little political incentive to listen.[1] Veteran GOP strategist Scott Reed, leader of the pro-Pence Committed to America PAC, said "the Never Trumpers and Lincoln Project folks just need to climb back under their rocks for a few years."[1]

## Joe Walsh's assessment

Former Illinois congressman [[joe-walsh|Joe Walsh]] told Politico the movement faced two options: "Productively throw rocks at the administration — kind of be like a group in exile and from a distance do what we can to damage MAGA, knowing we can never go back — or become Democrats."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked Politico for bolstering the relevance of Walsh, Haley and Pence by publishing an article about their irrelevance, arguing that hating Trump was no longer fashionable and that the figures involved were struggling with how little regard the American people had for them.[1] She read Walsh's remark as an admission that the real fight was not between Republicans and Democrats but between the people and the establishment, and predicted future generations would mistakenly say "the parties switched" in 2024 when in reality "the people pushing the two party psyop just shape-shifted again."[1]
