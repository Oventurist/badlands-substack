# CIA Headquarters Armed Man Incident (2025)

On the morning of Wednesday, March 19, 2025, a suspected gunman was taken into custody outside the headquarters of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] in Langley, Virginia, after he was seen making threats with a weapon near the agency's base.[1] The incident occurred days after the release of a large tranche of records related to the assassination of President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1]

## The incident

The armed man was described as disheveled, wearing camouflage and sporting long hair and a beard.[1] His presence sent the Langley headquarters into lockdown as police raced to the scene.[1] Reports indicated the suspect, who was not identified, had experienced a "mental health crisis," was seen sitting on a bench outside the property, and pointed a handgun at the agency headquarters.[1]

Police, SWAT teams and bomb squads responded, and a passerby described the law enforcement presence as "massive."[1] Early reports falsely indicated the man had fired his weapon into the air outside the headquarters; a law enforcement source told WJLA that no shots were fired.[1] There were no reports of injuries or deaths, though the area around the agency was closed down.[1]
