# Badlands Blitz Episode 15

**Badlands Blitz Episode 15**, subtitled "Dissolution of the Global Cartel," is the fifteenth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video recap published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] through its Substack newsletter. The announcement post is dated May 2, 2026 and carried 236 likes at the time of the source capture. It opens with the customary address to "Badlanders!" and restates the program's remit as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."

## Hosts

The episode pairs [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] with [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], a combination that had previously carried Episodes 11 and 13. Both are linked in the post to their Substack author profiles.

## War powers and the definition of war

The announcement says the two hosts use "the current debate between the Trump Administration and Congress regarding the Iran War to try to define when a war is and is not happening at the same time." The framing places the dispute between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s administration and the [[congress|United States Congress]] over authorisation for hostilities against [[iran|Iran]] at the centre of the episode, and treats the question of whether a war is legally or factually underway as itself indeterminate — a recurring theme in the program's treatment of the Iran conflict as an engagement whose outcome and even existence are contested in the information space as much as on the battlefield.

## The Global Cartel thesis

The post states that "the real meat of the discussion" lies in "tying the Actual and Narrative threads together between the Middle East, South America and the [[global-cartel|Global Cartel]] we believe is being dismantled in plain sight on kinetic, financial and energy grounds." This distinction between an "Actual" layer of events and a "Narrative" layer of public presentation is standard in Badlands analysis. The claim advanced here is that operations in the Middle East and South America are not separate theatres but coordinated fronts against a single transnational criminal-financial structure, and that the dismantling is proceeding simultaneously through military action, financial pressure and control of energy flows. The announcement concludes on that theme: "There is a plan, all right, and it seems to be coming together quite splendidly."

## Publication model

The post repeats the network's standing funding notice: all Badlands content, including The Blitz, remains free, and the team is able to pay itself only through readers who support the newsletter directly. Readers are invited to take a paid subscription, cancellable at any time, in support of the "step-up in said work for 2026." It closes with the customary sign-off, "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
