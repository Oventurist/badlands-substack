# Big Pharma

"Big Pharma" is the colloquial term for the concentrated global pharmaceutical industry and the regulatory, media and academic institutions that critics argue operate in its interest. Within [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary the phrase carries a specific analytical meaning: [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described Big Pharma as "one of the key cogs in the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s System of Systems."[1]

## The vaccine data reckoning

The framing gained force as independent analysts began publishing findings drawn from official data. [[phinance-technologies|Phinance Technologies]], founded by former [[blackrock|BlackRock]] portfolio manager [[ed-dowd|Ed Dowd]] with partners Carlos Alegria and Yuri Nunes, reported that the rate of Serious Adverse Events in the mRNA COVID-19 vaccine clinical trials closely tracked a spike in disabilities recorded after the vaccine rollout.[1] For eighteen months prior, skeptics of the mRNA products and those pointing to high rates of adverse reactions had been subject to ostracism, deplatforming and contested fact-checks intended to suppress analysis conflicting with official narratives.[1]

Burning Bright, writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], was explicit that he did not claim to know where the truth lay on vaccine adverse effects, though he had declined the vaccine himself and vehemently opposed mandates and lockdowns.[1] His longer-standing argument was that whatever the truth turned out to be would not reflect kindly on the pharmaceutical industry, and that its exposure could produce a paradigm shift in Western — perhaps global — culture regarding the trust society has placed in "the 'men in white coats'" and comparable authority figures for generations.[1] He anticipated a slow groundswell of simmering anger in the American public as publicly available data converged on what the community had argued for years.[1]

Related institutional controversy touched [[pfizer|Pfizer]] directly: [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] attributed his removal as chief executive of [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]] to the organisation having broken a major exposé on the company.[1]

## Trump's proposed commission (June 2023)

In June 2023, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] promised in an [[agenda-47|Agenda 47]] campaign video to establish a [[chronic-childhood-illness-commission|special presidential commission]] "of independent minds who are not bought and paid for by Big Pharma" to investigate the decades-long rise in chronic illness, particularly among children.[2] "I understand Big Pharma, I believe better than anyone else," he said; "I know where they're coming from."[2] Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] framed the pharmaceutical industry as a globalist instrument that hijacks health by weaponizing food and environmental exposures, banning non-pharmaceutical treatments, capturing medical education and research, and shaping media and legal systems so that only its agenda reaches the public mind.[2]
