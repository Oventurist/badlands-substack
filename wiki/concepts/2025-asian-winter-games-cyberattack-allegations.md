# 2025 Asian Winter Games Cyberattack Allegations

In April 2025, Chinese police in the northeastern city of Harbin publicly accused the United States [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] of launching "advanced" cyberattacks during the Asian Winter Games held there in February 2025, alleging that essential industries were targeted.[1]

## Allegations

According to a report by the state news agency [[xinhua-news-agency|Xinhua]], Harbin police added three alleged NSA agents — Katheryn A. Wilson, Robert J. Snelling, and Stephen W. Johnson — to a wanted list and offered a reward.[1] The three were said to have repeatedly carried out cyberattacks on China's critical information infrastructure and to have participated in attacks on [[huawei|Huawei]] and other enterprises.[1] Chinese authorities additionally accused the University of California and Virginia Tech of involvement, without specifying how the two American universities were said to have participated.[1]

## Context and commentary

The Asian Winter Games took place in Harbin in February 2025, just weeks after [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] returned to the White House.[1] Badlands commentary noted that no public record could be found of the named individuals working for the NSA, that the Trump administration had neither confirmed nor denied the allegations, and that no official statements existed beyond Xinhua's account.[1] Because the United States and China frequently accuse one another of cyberattacks and espionage, the episode was assessed as possible saber-rattling, though the alternative possibility was raised that it reflected a foiled operation carried out by rogue actors amid the chaos of the presidential transition.[1]
