# Executive Order 13884

Executive Order 13884 is a United States sanctions order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2019 targeting the Government of Venezuela and property in which it holds an interest.[1]

## Use in the 2024 aircraft seizure

US authorities cited a specific violation of Executive Order 13884 when they seized an aircraft used by Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] in the Dominican Republic in September 2024.[1] The plane — a Dassault Falcon 900-EX valued at $13 million and described by officials as Maduro's version of Air Force One — had been purchased through a straw company in violation of sanctions law and export controls, and was flown by [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] to Fort Lauderdale, Florida, on the morning of 2 September 2024.[1]

The seizure resulted from a joint investigation by HSI and the [[department-of-commerce|US Department of Commerce]].[1] Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] said the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] "seized an aircraft we allege was illegally purchased for $13 million through a shell company and smuggled out of the United States for use by Nicolás Maduro and his cronies," pledging continued pursuit of sanctions and export-control violators.[1]
