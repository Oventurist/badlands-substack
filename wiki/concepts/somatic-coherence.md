# Somatic Coherence

**Somatic coherence** is the state that "Hijacked Consciousness - The Silent War" identifies as the casualty of the [[frequency-war|frequency war]] described in the fourth installment of the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1] In the author's definition, coherence "doesn't just mean mental clarity"; it means somatic integrity — "the inner rhythm between breath, pulse, gut, voice," described as "the vibration of selfhood that aligns with truth without apology."[1]

## Loss of coherence

The essay argues that the human nervous system is designed to attune and seeks coherence, but will entrain to whatever signal is available when coherence is absent, including toxic or traumatizing ones.[1] Under fragmented school rhythms — bells, breaks and constant rotations with no space for deep work, wandering or imagination — the nervous system is said never to settle, remaining in low-grade vigilance and adapting to "being seen but never known."[1] What is lost is coherence and what is gained is performance, until "the body's truth starts to feel... unsafe," which the author states is the point.[1]

Stripped of coherence, the author contends, the result is not merely confused children but "programmable ones… drones trapped in rote," since the real aim of frequency warfare is described as control through artificial order — "a manufactured harmony designed to suppress" in which predictable behaviour arises from a muted signal and compliance is repackaged as success.[1] Talk of optimization is characterised as concealing flattening: "calibration over creativity."[1]

## Splitting and the hidden self

Children who survive the system are said to do so by splitting: one self "built for the system, shaped to perform, to comply," and another "tucked away, quiet and unseen, singing only in dreams."[1] The author holds that the sacred persists in that hidden self, because the system can dull resonance but cannot erase it — the blueprint remaining "stored in fascia, at the bottom of each breath, and in memory that cannot be erased," reactivated by a song, a silence, or a glance from someone who remembers.[1] On this reading the war on frequency was never aimed at total erasure but at delay: postponing awakening and "exhausting the nervous system before the soul can rise," described as "a preemptive energetic strike on the soul."[1]

## Recovery

Recovery is presented as possible because "the human system isn't just adaptive, it's regenerative": the signal can be recovered, coherence restored and the nervous system healed.[1] A musical note, a spoken word, or a well-placed moment of silence is said to be capable of cutting through the noise so that coherence returns even briefly — long enough for the body to remember "that you were never broken, you were just vibrating to someone else's frequency."[1] The author insists this will not come through policy or reform but through "re-enchantment… resonance, presence, and remembering," and warns that the remembering itself must be protected because the system is relentless and always present.[1] The related model of collective coherence is developed further in the following installment on the [[resonance-field|resonance field]].[1]
