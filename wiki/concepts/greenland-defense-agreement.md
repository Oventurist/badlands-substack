# Greenland Defense Agreement

The Greenland Defense Agreement is the 1951 treaty between the United States and [[denmark|Denmark]] that permitted the United States to build military bases on [[greenland|Greenland]] and to establish "defense areas" on the island if [[nato|NATO]] believed it necessary.[1]

## Proposed 2026 update

According to an article by Israeli journalist [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]], the framework agreed between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and NATO Secretary General [[mark-rutte|Mark Rutte]] in January 2026 includes updating the 1951 agreement.[1] Reporting by RT indicated the proposal contains language expanding the [[golden-dome|Golden Dome]] missile defense system into Greenland and allowing the United States to mine rare-earth minerals in parts of the island without having to seek permits, with bases on the island treated as US territory in the Arctic region.[1] Trump described it as "a deal that people jumped at, really fantastic for the USA, gets everything we wanted, including especially real national security and international security."[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued the updated arrangement delivers the desired sovereign basing and mineral rights without the burden of absorbing roughly 60,000 Greenlandic citizens or opening a statehood debate, calling it "*The Art of The Deal* all along."[1]
