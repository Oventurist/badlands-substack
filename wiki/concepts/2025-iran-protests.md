# 2025 Iran Protests

The 2025 Iran protests were a wave of demonstrations that erupted throughout [[iran|Iran]] in late December 2025, driven substantially by deteriorating economic conditions and joined by bazaar merchants.[1] The unrest coincided with heightened US and Israeli military pressure on the Iranian state following coordinated strikes earlier in the year.[1]

## Pahlavi's intervention

On Monday, December 29, 2025, exiled Iranian Crown Prince [[reza-pahlavi|Reza Pahlavi]] publicly backed the protests in a video message to his social media followers.[1] "I send my greetings to you, the bazaar merchants, and the people who have taken the streets into their own hands," he said, adding that "As long as this regime remains in power, the country's economic situation will continue to deteriorate."[1] He continued: "Today is a time for greater solidarity…I call on all segments of society to join your fellow citizens in the streets and raise your voices demanding the downfall of this system."[1]

## Commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], writing for the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, characterized Pahlavi's reappearance as that of "the Maria Corina Machado of Iran, supporting the Color Revolutionists," invoking the [[color-revolution]] framework.[1] He questioned why so many self-described "America First conservatives" were supporting such operations, calling that the central question raised by the episode.[1]

Separately, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the events as a color revolution launched against the government of Iran alongside threats to kill as many Iranians as necessary to conquer the country, arguing that this was "the behavior of villains."[1]
