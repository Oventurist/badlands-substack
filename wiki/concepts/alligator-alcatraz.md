# Alligator Alcatraz

"Alligator Alcatraz" is the popular name for a Florida detention facility whose surrounding wetlands and resident alligators were publicly promoted as a natural barrier against escape. The nickname entered international policy discussion when Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]] cited it as the inspiration for a proposal to surround Israeli prisons holding Palestinian security detainees with Nile crocodiles.

## Israeli adaptation

As reported in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], Israel took a legal step enabling consideration of the concept when Environmental Protection Minister [[idit-silman|Idit Silman]] reclassified the Nile crocodile into a category permitting security agencies to keep the animals under specified conditions. The [[israel-prison-service|Israel Prison Service]] had previously sent officials to a crocodile farm to examine practical aspects of the proposal. The reclassification proceeded despite objections from the [[israel-nature-and-parks-authority|Israel Nature and Parks Authority]] and the Environmental Protection Ministry's legal adviser, who argued the plan lacked sufficient legal and professional justification. Reporting indicated the change enabled consideration of a pilot program but did not mean crocodiles had been deployed at any facility.
