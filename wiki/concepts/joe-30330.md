# Joe 30330

**Joe 30330** refers to a phrase used by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] during a debate in the run-up to the 2020 election, when he told viewers, "If you agree with me, go to 'Joe 30330' and help me in this fight."[1] The remark was widely read as a garbled reference to his campaign's texting platform, which was engaged by texting "JOE" to the shortcode 30330, as advertised on his Twitter account.[1]

## Numerological claim

Q-adjacent researchers noted that dividing 2020 by 30330 produces 0.0666, and treated the appearance of "666" as deliberate rather than coincidental.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] posed the question rhetorically: what are the odds that a campaign team would choose a texting number that yields 666 when the election year is divided by it, and what are the odds that the Q Team would think to run that division?[1]

The episode is cited in [[q-post-4826|Q Post 4826]] as an example of the alleged practice of hiding symbolism in plain sight.[1]
