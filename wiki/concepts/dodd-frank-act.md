# Dodd-Frank Act

The Dodd-Frank Act was the 2010 US financial regulation law passed under President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] which, together with the Volcker Rule, was presented as an "FDR revival" but which [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues did not actually regulate speculation.[1]

## Criticism

Ehret writes that no productive credit was created to grow the real economy under a national mission, as had been the case in 1933–1938 under [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]].[1] Banks were not broken up, and [[derivatives|derivatives]] grew by 40% after 2010, with the new bubble concentrated in the corporate and household debt sector.[1] During this period nation-states continued to be stripped as austerity was imposed.[1] For Ehret, the only adequate remedy for the West is a return to [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall]] bank separation.[1]
