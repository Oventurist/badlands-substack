# CODEX 9/11

CODEX 9/11 is a documentary film produced by a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor and premiered on the network's Rumble channel on Sunday, August 24, 2025 at 6 p.m. Eastern.[1]

## Premiere and promotion

The film was promoted in the Badlands News Brief in the context of Russian allegations that [[ukraine|Ukraine]] was arming and training militants in Africa's Sahel region.[1] Writing about that story, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the moment as opportune for "a major 9/11 revelation ... re: 'radical Islamic terrorism,'" and said Badlands had exactly such a film — one that could be launched into the zeitgeist and "explode like a narrative weapon of mass destruction."[1]

Having watched the film, he wrote that it "has the potential to fundamentally disrupt the entire information battlespace," encouraged readers to tune in to the free premiere, and urged them to be prepared to share the link widely.[1] The film was also discussed on the previous night's episode of SitRep featuring General Kwast.[1]

## Deadwood premiere and the 9/11 justice debate

In mid-July 2025 the film was again promoted in connection with the appellate ruling that permitted the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] to withdraw from plea agreements with three Guantanamo prisoners accused of plotting the September 11 attacks, including [[khalid-sheikh-mohammed|Khalid Sheikh Mohammed]].[3] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the premiere of [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s film at GART Deadwood — a few weeks before the 24th anniversary of the attacks — came at "a perfect time," given that more people were open to inside-job theories than at any prior point.[3] She argued that public support is a requirement for change and possibly for justice itself, and framed the film as a contribution to national healing, adding that a virtual ticket was justified by the premiere alone.[3]

## Filmmaker's backstory and Deadwood premiere

In July 2025 Badlands Media highlighted a feature by filmmaker Brad Zerbo recounting his personal experience of September 11, 2001 — being woken with the news that "a bomb just went off in one of the Towers," watching the first tower burn, and then seeing United Flight 175 strike Tower 2 as he walked into the kitchen.[2] Zerbo described the moment Flight 11 "became no accident, and crisis became King," and identified the attacks as the event that shaped the rest of his life.[2] Badlands Media presented the essay as the filmmaker's back story and the source of his personal inspiration for CODEX 911, which was set to premiere at GART Deadwood, with virtual tickets available to viewers unable to attend in person.[2]

## Promotion in the July 28, 2025 News Brief

The July 28, 2025 Badlands News Brief promoted the GART Deadwood premiere of "CODEX 911," described as an original film by [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]], and listed it high among the reasons to obtain tickets to the event.[4] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that readers could take part in the premiere with a virtual ticket available through the network's events page.[4]

## Zerbo's origin essay (July 2025)

In July 2025 the Badlands News Brief highlighted a personal essay by filmmaker [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]] titled "September 11th 2001," recounting the morning of the attacks — being woken by his friend Sal with news that "a bomb just went off in one of the Towers," watching the burning north tower on television, and then seeing United Flight 175 explode into tower 2 as they walked into the kitchen.[5] Zerbo wrote that "everything changed," that the impact of Flight 175 was the moment Flight 11 became no accident, and that "crisis became King."[5]

Badlands Media urged readers to read the feature, describing it as the filmmaker's back story and the source of his personal inspiration for CODEX 911, which was premiering at GART Deadwood with virtual tickets available.[5]

## Streaming release (August 2025)

Badlands Media announced that *CODEX 9/11* was streaming free to all audiences on Rumble following its August 24, 2025 premiere, describing it as "a groundbreaking documentary that smashes the official narrative of September 11th into a thousand pieces and scatters it to the winds."[6] The film was written, researched and narrated by [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]], and the promotional item stated it "will never have you look at that fateful day the same again."[6]

## Comparison with Tucker Carlson's 9/11 series (September 2025)

In September 2025, after [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] told [[piers-morgan|Piers Morgan]] that Israeli intelligence had advance knowledge of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] and previewed a forthcoming documentary series on the subject, Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] suggested Carlson's project could become "a great companion documentary" to CODEX 9/11.[7] He wrote that Zerbo's film definitively demonstrates that 9/11 was an inside job and advances the most compelling theory he had encountered about what happened with the planes — that they were remote-piloted drones — adding that the zeitgeist had shifted and the American people appeared finally prepared to confront the hard truths surrounding the attacks.[7]

## Premiere at GART Deadwood

CODEX 911 premiered at [[gart-9-deadwood|GART Deadwood]], with an early screening made available to holders of the GART 9 virtual pass alongside access to the conference, pre-livestreams and a behind-the-scenes Telegram chat.[8] In July 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted a personal Substack feature by director [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]] titled "September 11th 2001," describing it as the filmmaker's back story and the source of his personal inspiration for the documentary.[8] In the piece Zerbo recounts being woken on the morning of the attacks, watching United Flight 175 strike the second tower, and concluding that this was the moment "Flight 11 became no accident, and crisis became King."[8]

## September 2025 screenings

Around the 24th anniversary of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], Badlands promoted a live screening of CODEX 9/11 at the Paradigm Theatres in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, with additional live screenings in Marietta, Georgia and Yucca Valley, California.[9] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] urged readers to watch the film that day, writing that "we honor the legacy of the Americans sacrificed on September 11th with the truth."[9]

Lt. Gen. [[steve-kwast|Steve Kwast]] (USAF, Ret.) saw CODEX 9/11 in August 2025 at a Badlands event in Deadwood, South Dakota, and called it "a nuclear weapon in the information war"; two weeks later he joined the Badlands podcast to discuss it, as relayed by [[canncon|CannCon]].[9]
