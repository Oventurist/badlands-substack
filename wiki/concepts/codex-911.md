# CODEX 911

**CODEX 911** is an original film by [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]], produced under the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] banner.[1] The film was announced as premiering at the GART Deadwood event, promoted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025.[1] Badlands promoted GART tickets in part on the strength of the premiere, and offered a virtual ticket option allowing remote audiences to take part in the screening.[1]

## Deadwood premiere and trailer (August 2025)

A new trailer for CODEX 9/11 was released by [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]] in early August 2025, ahead of the film's premiere the following Saturday in Deadwood, South Dakota.[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted the premiere in tandem with its GART gathering, offering virtual tickets alongside exclusive streams, panel content and behind-the-scenes material.[2]

## Premiere and inspiration

In July 2025 Badlands Media promoted the premiere of CODEX 911 at GART Deadwood, offering virtual tickets through its events page.[3] The network published filmmaker [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s personal essay "September 11th 2001" as the back story and personal inspiration for the film, in which Zerbo recounted watching the towers burn and seeing United Flight 175 strike the second tower, writing that "everything changed" and that it was the moment "Flight 11 became no accident, and crisis became King."[3]
