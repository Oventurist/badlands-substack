# Sound Money Initiative

The **Sound Money initiative** is a fictional monetary reform attributed to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the Badlands Media speculative feature "Trump Did It" by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], written as a retrospective from the year 2050.[1]

## Narrative background

In the article's timeline, Trump takes office as the 47th President in 2024, and in 2025 the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], "in partnership with the globalist bankers," collapses the global economy.[1] Trump responds by declaring that "the people who know, understand, and embrace the true rule of law can solve any problem," and that "we can manage our own money supply, just like the founding fathers did," refusing to sell the country "to the same snake bankers that got us into this mess in the first place."[1]

## Debt cancellation and the clean slate

Within days of the initiative's launch, the article says, "trillions in bogus and fraudulent debts were canceled," and the public was "shocked to discover that the vast majority of the world's debt was fraudulent."[1] Because the pre-existing economy is described as "a massive pyramid scheme," no restitution could make everyone whole; Trump therefore proposes that the new financial system — one ending "banker debt, usury, and inflation" — begin from "a clean slate."[1] In a quoted address he argues that people cannot "fight each other over pennies and fiat currency that were from a ri[g]ged system in the first place," and that sound money "means everyone gets a fair and fresh start, not just these sick globalists with their [[great-reset|Great Reset]]."[1]

## Claimed results

The article reports initial distrust, followed by rapid improvement by 2026 as the removal of "mountains of unnecessary debt" lowers the cost of living, increases free time, and improves quality of life.[1] Business formation becomes easy, jobs plentiful, and "revivals, parties, parades, and gatherings became a regular feature of life," as if "the whole world had woken from a collective nightmare."[1] By the time Trump leaves office in 2028, the article states, "the world was irreversibly changed for the better."[1]

## Resistance

A minority described as "recalcitrant holdovers" refuse to accept the new order even after "the deceivers they believed in switched sides," and the article credits them with nearly triggering a third world war.[1] Their resistance is nonetheless framed as instructive, helping others "crawl out from under the false narrative of their globalist controllers."[1]

## Status

The Sound Money initiative exists only within the article's acknowledged fictional projection; the author's note describes the piece as a dramatization of "one likely scenario" rather than reportage.[1] It is best read as an expression of the [[globalism|anti-globalist]] and hard-money themes recurring in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary.[1]
