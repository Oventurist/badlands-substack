# Proba-3

Proba-3 is a [[european-space-agency|European Space Agency]] mission consisting of two spacecraft — the Coronagraph and the Occulter — that fly in precision formation to create artificial total solar eclipses in orbit.[1]

## First artificial solar eclipse

In March 2025 the mission achieved what the agency said no other mission had accomplished before: its two spacecraft flew 150 metres apart in perfect formation for several hours without any control from the ground.[1] While aligned, the pair maintained their relative position to within a single millimetre, a feat enabled by a set of innovative navigation and positioning technologies.[1]

To demonstrate this precision, the spacecraft used their formation-flying time to align with the Sun so that the 1.4-metre disc carried by the Occulter covered the bright solar disc as seen by the Coronagraph, casting a shadow roughly 8 centimetres across onto its optical instrument, ASPIICS.[1] The resulting images captured the solar corona with full occultation.[1]
