# 2026 California Gubernatorial Election

The 2026 California gubernatorial election drew an early high-profile Democratic entrant when Representative [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]], a leading critic of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, announced on Thursday, November 20, 2025 that he would run for governor.[1]

## Swalwell's announcement

Swalwell made the announcement on his official campaign website, which went live Thursday night ahead of a scheduled appearance on "Jimmy Kimmel Live!"[1] He represents California's 14th Congressional District, covering a large swath of the East Bay, has held office in the U.S. House since 2013, and was previously a prosecutor for Alameda County; his political career includes a 2020 presidential run centered on gun control and a call for a complete ban on assault rifles.[1]

While running for governor, Swalwell may also have to contend with a potential federal investigation: [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]] Director [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] referred him to the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] over alleged tax and mortgage fraud, and Swalwell has denied any wrongdoing.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] used the announcement to challenge readers who believe U.S. elections are real to identify which part of the election process is certainly legitimate, lawful and effective.[1] He directed the question at "truthers" still monetizing election fakery, at people discussing the midterms, and at those speculating about the 2028 primaries, predicting they will have no answer because they know the elections are illegitimate and are merely statists and normies who believe themselves awake.[1]
