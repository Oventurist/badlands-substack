# February 2023 Drone Attacks Inside Russia

On Tuesday, February 28, 2023, Russia came under attack by multiple drones, with one reportedly causing a fire at an oil depot in the south of the country and another crashing outside Moscow, triggering air raid sirens and widespread disruption.[1]

## Tuapse oil depot fire

The attack on the oil facility occurred in Tuapse, roughly 150 miles southeast of the Crimean peninsula.[1] Reuters, citing local media and the RIA news agency, reported that emergency services put out a fire at the depot overnight after a drone was spotted flying overhead.[1] The fires began at about 2:30 a.m. and were extinguished after spreading across an area of some 200 square meters; a local official, Sergei Boyko, said "The oil tanks were not affected. There was no spill of oil products. No injuries."[1] Tuapse lies about 500 kilometers from the nearest Ukrainian-held territory, a distance that would demonstrate significant reach if the unmanned aerial vehicle was launched by Ukrainian forces.[1]

## Moscow region crash

Another drone crashed in the Moscow region the same day. Governor Andrei Vorobyov said the flight was likely an operation intended to target civilian infrastructure.[1] The events were reported by ZeroHedge sourcing Reuters and other outlets.[1]
