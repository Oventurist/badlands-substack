# Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena

"Unidentified anomalous phenomena" (UAP) is the official United States government term that replaced "unidentified flying object" (UFO) in describing objects and sightings that resist conventional identification.[1] The renaming accompanied the broadening of official inquiry to include objects operating in domains other than the air, notably underwater, under the [[all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office|All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office]].[1]

Official investigation was organized first through the Navy-launched [[unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force|Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force]] under the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security, and later through AARO.[1] Public attention to the topic intensified after the 2017 release of Navy UAP videos associated with the USS Nimitz and USS Theodore Roosevelt, which commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] cited as confirmation that the military encounters objects operating beyond the known technological capacity of aboveboard forces.[1]

## Disclosure debate

The June 2023 [[david-grusch|David Grusch]] whistleblower account, published in [[the-debrief|The Debrief]], alleged the existence of covert programs holding retrieved craft of non-human origin.[1] Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that [[unacknowledged-special-access-programs|unacknowledged special access programs]] give the intelligence community near-limitless capacity to conceal both terrestrial and extraterrestrial UAP information, while cautioning that the disclosure sequence may itself be preparation for an [[alien-disclosure-op|alien disclosure operation]] or "cosmic false flag."[1]
