# Reedley Illegal Biolab

The Reedley illegal biolab was an unlicensed, large-scale medical laboratory discovered inside an ordinary warehouse in the city of Reedley, in Fresno County, California, and publicly reported in late July 2023.[1]

## Discovery

The facility was uncovered in April 2023 after a local code enforcement officer noticed a garden hose illegally attached and protruding from a wall at the back of the building, prompting an investigation.[1] The warehouse, located at "I" and Ninth Streets, had been recorded as an empty structure used only for storage.[1] Investigators instead found a black-market laboratory testing facility containing bioengineered mice, infectious agents, nearly thirty refrigerators and freezers — some of them non-operational — incubators and other equipment.[1] Local, state and federal agencies all became involved in the investigation.[1]

Officials described the discovery as without precedent in their careers. Reedley City Manager Nicole Zieba said, "This is an unusual situation. I've been in government for 25 years. I've never seen anything like this."[1] Joe Prado, Assistant Director of the Fresno County Department of Public Health, said, "I've never seen this in my 26-year career with the County of Fresno."[1] The story was reported by YourCentralValley.com and carried by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], which characterized the facility as a mysterious Chinese bio lab discovered in a remote California city.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the episode as the discovery of a Chinese bio lab holding deadly pathogens operating inside a California neighbourhood at the very moment U.S. legislators were denouncing bio labs in China and Ukraine.[1] She argued it would be naive to assume it was the only illegal Chinese biolab on U.S. soil and hoped the bust would yield intelligence about a larger operation, linking it to the earlier 2023 discovery of illegal Chinese police stations in New York and asking what the end game of such operations was.[1] She attributed the vulnerability to an open border with no preventative security measures and no means of knowing who is in the country, and expressed no expectation that the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration would confront China in defence of American interests.[1]

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the discovery to raise the broader question of how easily disease events can be manufactured and what benefit accrues from introducing pathogens, toxins and other disease vectors into society.[1] Citing historical precedents of [[historical-biological-warfare|biological warfare]], he argued the concept is well established, and suggested the California lab might represent a glimpse into a state- or globalist-sponsored disease machine in which food, technology and biological pathogens are staples.[1] If so, he wrote, such cells are likely everywhere and the past two hundred years of epidemiological history could be rendered highly suspect, leaving open the question of which epidemics were real and which were not.[1]
