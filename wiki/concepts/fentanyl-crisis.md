# Fentanyl Crisis

The fentanyl crisis is the mass-casualty synthetic-opioid overdose epidemic in the United States, described in July 2024 reporting as killing roughly 80,000 to 100,000 Americans each year — a toll characterized as "about one Vietnam every six months."[1] Commentators have referred to the crisis as a "Reverse Opium War," inverting the nineteenth-century narcotics campaigns waged against China.[1]

## Political framing

Coverage aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] attributed the crisis's escalation to the open southern border under [[joe-biden|President Joe Biden]], and reported that lawmakers on Capitol Hill were planning "serious action" in response.[1] The emerging strategy was described as comprehensive, targeting Chinese chemical suppliers, money launderers, Mexican cartels, and the banks that facilitate the trade.[1]

## Proposed plan of attack

Former [[drug-enforcement-administration|DEA]] official [[ray-donovan-dea|Ray Donovan]], credited with helping bring down Joaquín "El Chapo" Guzmán, laid out a multi-faceted approach with a former Trump official that leveraged legal, financial, military, and media tools to target the foundational support structures of drug trafficking organizations.[1] The plan contemplated prosecuting domestic and foreign banks and pairing terrorist designations with RICO charges.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] connected the plan to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated intention to deploy Tier 1 "kill teams" against cartels south of the border if returned to office, arguing that the convergence would let the public tie the border crisis to the wider [[deep-state|Deep State]] cabal.[1]
