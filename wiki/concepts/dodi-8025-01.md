# DoDI 8025.01 (DoD Internet Content Blocking)

DoDI 8025.01 is a Department of Defense instruction titled "DoD Internet Content Blocking," issued by the [[department-of-war|Department of War]] on November 18, 2025.[1] The substantive details of the instruction were not publicly available at the time of reporting.[1]

## Timing and interpretation

The instruction was issued on the same day that six Democratic lawmakers released the [[seditious-six-video|"Seditious Six" video]] urging service members to refuse illegal orders.[1] Badlands writer Jon Herold noted the coincidence of dates and speculated that the new content-blocking instruction was connected to the video, though he acknowledged the underlying document's contents were unknown.[1] The department is led by Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], who publicly denounced the video as a politically-motivated influence operation.[1]
