# Strongyloides stercoralis

*Strongyloides stercoralis* is a parasitic nematode invoked as an extended metaphor in the Badlands Media article "Q is Our Best Fren".[1] The article notes that the organism can evade the host immune system for decades, colonising the body thoroughly while producing no symptoms at all, and that deadly complications can follow the moment the immune system finally recognises the threat and mounts a response.[1]

## Use as a political metaphor

The author argues that the alleged [[cabal]]'s mode of operation can be specifically likened to this parasite: undetected infiltration of nearly all the "organs" of society, followed by the capacity to mount a system-wide response whenever a local branch of the criminal network is threatened.[1] This framing underpins the article's broader model of a [[societal-immune-response|societal immune response]] in which [[anons]] serve as white blood cells and the Covid pandemic represents the system-wide flare that made the infection visible.[1] It also connects to the article's wider treatment of [[psychopathy-and-parasitism|psychopathy as advanced parasitism]].[1]
