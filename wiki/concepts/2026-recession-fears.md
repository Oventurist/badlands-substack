# 2026 Recession Fears

Recession fears intensified in 2026 as shares of several major U.S. consumer-facing companies plunged following weak earnings and lowered outlooks.[1]

## Corporate warnings

Whirlpool, Shake Shack, and Planet Fitness each posted sharp declines after citing weaker consumer spending, rising costs, and slowing demand.[1] Whirlpool's decline was tied in reporting to profit damage from the Iran war shock, while Shake Shack fell alongside a McDonald's CEO warning about a faltering consumer, and Planet Fitness dropped on a membership slump that hit its outlook.[1] Broader recession concerns continued to pressure markets.[1]

The downturn coincides with the 2026 [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] crisis and the associated energy-route disruption, which has raised costs across consumer-facing sectors.[1]
