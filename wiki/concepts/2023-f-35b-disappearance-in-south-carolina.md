# 2023 F-35B Disappearance in South Carolina

In September 2023 a United States Marine Corps [[f-35|F-35B Lightning II]] stealth fighter went missing over South Carolina after its pilot ejected during what the military termed a "mishap," prompting a public search and, on Monday, September 18, the discovery of a "debris field."[1]

## Search and debris field

The military said it had located a debris field in Williamsburg County, South Carolina, roughly two hours northeast of [[joint-base-charleston|Joint Base Charleston]], that might be linked to the missing jet, though it did not confirm the connection.[1] Joint Base Charleston told nearby community members to avoid the area "as the recovery team secures the debris field," and said the [[united-states-marine-corps|U.S. Marine Corps]] would begin a "recovery process."[1] A Marine Corps spokesperson said search-and-recovery efforts for the aircraft had been ongoing and that the mishap was under investigation.[1]

## Background

The Marine Corps had previously asked for the public's help locating the aircraft.[1] Joint Base Charleston said on Sunday that its personnel were working with Marine Corps Air Station Beaufort to track down the fighter, which is worth upwards of $80 million.[1] "Based on the jet's last-known position and in coordination with the FAA, we are focusing our attention north of JB Charleston, around Lake Moultrie and Lake Marion," the air base said.[1] The pilot ejected safely and was in stable condition after being taken to a local medical center.[1] It remained unclear what the "mishap" was or how, exactly, the stealth fighter went missing.[1]
