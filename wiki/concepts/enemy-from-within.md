# Enemy From Within

"Enemy from within" is a phrase used by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in October 2024 to describe domestic political opponents whom he characterized as a greater threat to the United States on Election Day than foreign infiltrators or "outside agitators."[1] The remark, delivered in an interview that aired on Fox News's *Sunday Morning Futures* with host [[maria-bartiromo|Maria Bartiromo]], became one of the defining rhetorical flashpoints of the final weeks of the 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Origin of the phrase

Asked by Bartiromo whether he expected "chaos" on Election Day, Trump first replied, "Not from the side that votes for Trump."[1] When Bartiromo raised the prospect of outside agitators, including individuals on the terrorist watch list, Trump redirected the answer toward his domestic political opponents: "I think the bigger problem is the enemy from within, not even the people that have come in and [are] destroying our country."[1] He added, "I think the bigger problem are the people from within. We have some very bad people. We have some sick people."[1]

Trump suggested that such disorder "should be very easily handled by, if necessary, by [[national-guard|National Guard]], or if really necessary, by the military, because they can't let that happen," a statement widely reported as a proposal to deploy troops domestically in response to what he called "radical left lunatics."[1]

## Naming Adam Schiff

Later in the same interview, Trump named Representative [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]], the California Democrat then running for the U.S. Senate, as an example of an "enemy from within," saying, "The thing that's tougher to handle are these lunatics that we have inside, like Adam Schiff."[1] Schiff had served as a House manager during Trump's first impeachment and later sat on the select committee investigating the January 6, 2021 attack on the Capitol.[1]

A second Badlands take, written by Burning Bright, argued that the continued elevation of "the most clownish Deep State stooges" to positions of proxy power is itself a proof of patriot control, and described that control as "bicameral": either the alliance has direct control over all public actors and is pantomiming the storm en route to a mass awakening, or it has hamstrung the real power players and placed stooges into a fifth-generation-warfare maze producing the same result.[1] The take concluded that the 2024 contest was "not an election campaign" but "a victory march."[1]
