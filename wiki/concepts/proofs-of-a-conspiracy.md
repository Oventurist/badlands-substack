# Proofs of a Conspiracy

Proofs of a Conspiracy is a book by [[john-robison|John Robison]] cited in Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" as an early and influential warning about clandestine power.[1]

The article recounts that [[george-washington|George Washington]], shortly before his death, read the book and opined: "It was not my intention to doubt that the doctrines of the Illuminati and principles of Jacobinism had not spread in the United States. On the contrary, no one is more truly satisfied of this fact than I am."[1]

Robison, along with many contemporary and later researchers, was convinced that the machinations of a hidden group of [[freemasonry|Freemasons]] known as the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]] had infiltrated the new world.[1] The episode opens DeLarme's argument that warnings about [[secret-society|secret societies]] have come from far more than "fringe conspiracy theorists."[1]
