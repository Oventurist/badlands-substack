# Blue Stream Pipeline

Blue Stream is a natural gas pipeline that transports Russian gas to Türkiye across the Black Sea. It is described in the Badlands Media corpus as one of [[russia|Russia]]'s key gas export routes to [[turkey|Türkiye]].

## July 2026 drone strike

According to the July 10, 2026 Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles", [[gazprom|Gazprom]] reported that Ukrainian drones targeted the Krasnodarskaya compressor station serving Blue Stream. Gazprom said the attack caused minor damage to a building at the facility but did not interrupt deliveries, as repairs were completed promptly and backup measures maintained supply. [[ukraine|Ukraine]] had not publicly commented at the time of reporting.

Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] condemned the strike as a threat to critical international energy infrastructure and said Russia expected countries affected by the pipeline, Türkiye among them, to respond.

## Strategic reading in the Badlands corpus

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats the strike as an indirect attack on Türkiye rather than on Russia alone, arguing that it followed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s praise of President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] at the [[nato|NATO]] summit in Ankara and was carried out on behalf of [[israel|Israel]]. He identifies the parallel [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] route across the Black Sea, the island of [[cyprus|Cyprus]], and Türkiye's Aegean coast as further potential targets in the scenario he outlines.
