# Sean Combs Federal Trial

The federal criminal trial of music mogul [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] began with jury selection on Monday, May 5, 2025, in Manhattan.[1]

## Charges

Combs faces charges of sex trafficking, racketeering, and transportation to engage in prostitution.[1] He was indicted in the Southern District of New York in September and pleaded not guilty.[1] Prosecutors allege that Combs organized so-called "freak offs," which they describe as "elaborate and produced sex performances."[1]

## Jury selection

Day two of jury selection, on Tuesday, May 6, produced a widely reported exchange in which a prospective juror was excused after disclosing his marijuana habits.[1] "I smoke marijuana personally for recreational use," the prospective juror told the court; while he assured the court it would not make him biased, he admitted it could affect his judgment.[1] Asked whether he could stop smoking during the trial, expected to last eight weeks, he said yes, but revealed he smoked marijuana every night, including the previous night, and that quitting would be difficult. He was dismissed.[1]

## Commentary

*Badlands News Brief* contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] found the exchange humorous, remarking that the juror had given everyone an excuse to escape jury duty, and noted that because the proceeding is in federal court the trial will not be televised — a rule she argued should be changed.[1]
