# The Family

The Family is a 2019 Netflix documentary series which, according to [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], exposes the existence of a neoconservative Christian movement that has been active within American politics at the highest levels for over 80 years.[1]

Deschamps cites the series while advancing his own view — formed, he says, from studying occult groups and the dark ideological outlook of the so-called [[cabal|Cabal]] — that a faction of crypto-Sabbateans or Frankists was embedded within the United States over 200 years ago and has become increasingly active since the end of the Second World War.[1] The reference appears within his broader historical argument connecting [[neoplatonism|Neoplatonism]], [[sabbateanism|Sabbateanism]], [[jacob-frank|Jacob Frank]] and modern [[zionism|Zionism]] to the politics of the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]].[1]
