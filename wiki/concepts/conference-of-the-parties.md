# Conference of the Parties (WHO CA+)

The **Conference of the Parties**, abbreviated **C.O.P.**, is the governing body established under Article 20 of the draft [[who-pandemic-accord|WHO CA+]] instrument, as revealed in "The Bureau's Text of the WHO CA+" released on 22 May 2023.[1] Badlands Media described it as a new bureaucracy operating beyond Congressional or Parliamentary oversight and control.[1]

## Composition

Per the Bureau's Text, the body would include "representatives of the United Nations and its specialized agencies" as well as "representatives of any body or organization, whether national or international, governmental or nongovernmental, private sector or public sector, which is qualified in matters covered by the WHO CA+."[1] The inclusion of private-sector and nongovernmental representatives is central to critics' objection that unelected and unaccountable actors would be seated alongside states.[1]

## Powers

Under Article 34 of the draft, members of the Conference of the Parties would be empowered to add protocols to the agreement without the consent of the public.[1] The article characterizes this transfer of authority from "We the People" to the unaccountable, unelected, and mostly invisible members of the C.O.P. as a veiled but direct attack on the sovereignty of nations.[1] Related proposals in the draft would establish a WHO-overseen international supply network, require member states to dedicate at least 5% of national health budgets to health emergencies, and implement the [[one-health|One Health]] agenda.[1]
