# Argentine Nuclear Plan

The Argentine Nuclear Plan is an energy programme unveiled by Argentine President [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]] in late December 2024, intended to transform [[argentina|Argentina]] into what he described as a global energy powerhouse by making nuclear power a core component of the nation's future.[1]

## Content

The plan centres on the construction of [[small-modular-reactor|Small Modular Reactors]] (SMRs) — compact nuclear units designed to supply power to commercial sectors and other large-scale operations.[1] Announcing the programme, Milei declared: "After years of stagnation, nuclear energy is making a powerful comeback, and we are determined to lead, not follow."[1] He emphasised Argentina's abundant natural resources and skilled workforce, and singled out Patagonia's cold climate as ideal for housing energy-intensive technologies such as artificial intelligence.[1] Milei also argued that "nuclear energy is the only source that is sufficiently efficient, abundant and rapidly scalable to cope with the development of our civilization."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the plan as part of a broader "timeline war" over energy, arguing that SMR technology represents the "REAL Green New Deal" that Americans could embrace, in contrast to what he called the globalist inversion of a wind-and-solar future exemplified by the 2021 Texas grid failure.[1] He connected the Argentine push to a long-running argument that the "Nuclear American Dream" was taken from the United States after the Eisenhower administration and the assassination of John F. Kennedy, and noted that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was filling his incoming cabinet with figures from the re-emerging nuclear industry, including [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]].[1] He also observed that Milei's record of shrinking government while increasing GDP and reducing the deficit mirrors promises Trump made to American voters.[1]
