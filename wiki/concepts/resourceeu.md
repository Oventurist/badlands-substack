# REsourceEU

REsourceEU is a [[european-commission|European Commission]] action plan, released in early December 2025, intended to secure the supply of critical raw materials used in electric cars, wind turbines and semiconductors.[1]

The plan includes investing 3 billion euros ($3.49 billion) over the following year to fast-track projects capable of cutting European reliance on supplies from any single country — principally China, which dominates global [[rare-earth-elements|rare earths]] processing.[1]

[[bernd-schaefer|Bernd Schaefer]], chief executive of the EU-funded agency [[eit-rawmaterials|EIT RawMaterials]], welcomed the plan as "a real step change from ambition to delivery" while saying it was "still missing a couple of things," and warned that Europe must guard not only against Chinese dominance but also against being overwhelmed by the United States in the sector.[1]
