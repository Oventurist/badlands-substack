# Political Ponerology

***Political Ponerology: the Science of Evil*** is a book by the psychologist [[andrew-lobaczewski|Andrew Lobaczewski]], cited in the opening installment of the Badlands Media series [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] as one of the key works for understanding the psychology of those who rule.[1]

## Argument as summarised

The essay presents Lobaczewski as an escapee from communist Poland who had many close encounters with psychopathic personality types, and draws from his work the finding that psychopaths feel oppressed by the common morality of the people because they see no reason for it.[1] Such individuals are described as frequently regarding themselves as a "more evolved" form of humanity, unburdened by moral considerations they consider silly, and as wishing to take revenge on the populace whose morality impedes the satisfaction of their appetites.[1]

## Use in the series

*Political Ponerology* is paired in the article with [[anthony-napoleon|Anthony Napoleon]]'s [[shadow-men|*Shadow Men*]] and with [[rogue-hypnotist|Rogue Hypnotist]]'s [[psychopath-proof|*Psychopath Proof*]] as recommended reading for citizens attempting to profile psychopathy, a skill the author treats as essential equipment in [[information-warfare|information warfare]].[1] Together these sources underpin the essay's claim that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] amounts to organized psychopathy and its wider treatment of [[psychopathy-and-parasitism|psychopathy as parasitism]].[1]
