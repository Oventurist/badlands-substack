# Social Engineering

**Social engineering** appears in "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" as one of the three operational-level activities ChatGPT identified when decomposing the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]] passage into levels of warfare, alongside psychological manipulation and legal and political maneuvering.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The model defined it as implementing policies and initiatives aimed at breaking down traditional family structures and reducing economic disparities.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Psychological manipulation, its companion operation, was described as influencing public opinion and perceptions to make the intended changes appear popularly supported and democratic — matching the Protocols' own requirement that the programme carry "the appearance of 'Democracy'."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

At the tactical layer these operations are executed through propaganda that shapes public opinion via media and information dissemination, legislation that advances the objectives even where it does not align with traditional democratic principles, and economic policies that redistribute wealth or alter economic structures to reduce social classes.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The model closed by noting that such strategies raise concerns about authoritarianism, social engineering and the erosion of individual freedoms and cultural values.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Read within [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s broader framework, social engineering is the mid-level connective tissue between the strategic objective of a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] and the everyday tactics of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
