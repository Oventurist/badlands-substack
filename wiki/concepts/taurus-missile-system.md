# Taurus Missile System

The Taurus is a German-built air-launched cruise missile whose potential transfer to Ukraine became one of the central escalation questions of the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]] in 2025.[1] The weapon has a range of roughly 300 miles (482 km), far enough to allow Ukrainian strikes to reach deep into Russian territory.[1]

## Political controversy

German Chancellor [[friedrich-merz|Friedrich Merz]] indicated in May 2025 that Berlin would be open to supplying Kiev with the Taurus system, reversing the caution of his predecessors.[1] [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s special envoy [[keith-kellogg|Keith Kellogg]] cited Merz's comments as evidence that "escalatory issues are still there" even as he predicted the peace process would ultimately succeed, quoting the chancellor's position directly: "well, I'm going to give the Ukrainians the Taurus missile system."[1]

## Russian position

Kellogg said Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had made his position unambiguous: if such long-range weapons are supplied to Ukraine, Russia will regard the suppliers themselves as parties to the conflict.[1] That framing fed directly into Kellogg's broader concession that Putin was justified in treating the war as a [[nato|NATO]] proxy war against Russia.[1]
