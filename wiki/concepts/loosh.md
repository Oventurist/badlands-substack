# Loosh

Loosh is an esoteric concept describing the purported harvest of vital essence siphoned from human emotional states — fear, anguish, rage and despair — by non-human or extradimensional entities.[1] The term entered contemporary alternative discourse through the out-of-body explorations of Robert Monroe, whose book *Far Journeys* described encounters with entities said to cultivate human emotion as a form of energetic sustenance, "much like farmers tending crops for yield," perpetuating cycles of trauma to maximize the yield of the substance.[1]

## Badlands treatment

In an October 2025 commentary, Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked loosh while discussing coverage of the [[no-kings-protests|'No Kings' rallies]] and Fox News host [[jesse-watters|Jesse Watters]]' admission that his own mother had marched.[1] The essay framed loosh as steeped in satanic undertones and analogous to the parasitic dynamics of "archonic forces" in Gnostic thought — predators of the soul that thrive on the inversion of divine energy, transforming human experience into fodder for control matrices in which engineered discord, "from wars on the macro to personal and private strife on the micro," serves as the mechanism of extraction.[1]

Burning Bright argued that whether or not loosh is literally true "in a corporeal sense," it names something perceptible in the culture: an insatiable hunger at the core of institutional evil.[1]

## The warning against reverse harvesting

A central move in the essay is a caution addressed to the truth community itself. In the present "battlespace," the writer argued, there is an ironic temptation to wield the same weapons, institutions and narrative tools against the adversary — a "reverse vampirism" in which the emotional turmoil of the enemy's downfall becomes loosh collected by their opponents.[1] Indulging in "base schadenfreude" at the spectacle of agencies hemorrhaging, mandates dissolving and the once-mighty pleading in court filings risks entrenching the very cycle the movement seeks to shatter, descending to the vibrational level of lesser men rather than ascending beyond predatory paradigms.[1]

The commentary extended the framework to what it called the Mind War, the Info War and the Shadow War operating simultaneously, including inside the truth community, where "Enemies often come gilded in false unity while Allies come bearing division and discord" — the latter being healthy insofar as it reflects commitment to finding truth rather than branding it.[1]
