# NESARA/GESARA

**NESARA/GESARA** (National/Global Economic Security and Reformation Act) is a recurring theme in populist and anon-adjacent commentary describing a purported forthcoming global financial reset: a worldwide jubilee of debt forgiveness paired with a redistribution of wealth intended to lift underdeveloped countries and peoples out of poverty so that "all the world's population will experience abundance."[1]

## Treatment in Badlands Media commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in December 2022, [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] addressed NESARA/GESARA with explicit epistemic caution, stating that "it could be bunk. It might not be. Or it might be somewhere in between. There's no way to know what's true and what's not. Not until it happens."[1] She nonetheless described the promise of a global debt jubilee as something she continued "to dream of and pray for."[1]

Gillespie linked the concept to the expansion of the [[brics|BRICS]] bloc, arguing that the long list of economically distressed nations seeking membership was "laying the groundwork for a new global currency" that might carry the world toward the outcome NESARA/GESARA describes.[1] The framework is presented alongside the broader argument that [[fiat-currency|fiat currency]] is dying, that [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]] is a trap, and that an asset-backed currency held in [[digital-wallets|digital wallets]] is the intended replacement.[1]
