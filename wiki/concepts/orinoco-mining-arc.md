# Orinoco Mining Arc

The **Orinoco Mining Arc**, also referred to as the Orinoco Belt, is the Venezuelan region containing both the country's heavy crude deposits and extensive gold mining operations.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] describes the Arc as a linchpin of the survival economy that kept [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] in power despite U.S. sanctions.[1] By 2020, Chinese firms including CNPC were operating joint ventures in the Orinoco Belt, extracting oil that doubled as repayment on [[china|China]]'s restructured loans.[1] Moscow, for its part, dispatched Wagner-linked technicians to guard gold mines in the Belt, and from 2018 Russian mercenaries secured illegal gold mines in the Arc on behalf of Maduro's inner circle, including the [[cartel-of-the-suns|Cartel of the Suns]].[1]

The resulting triad — oil to China for debt servicing, gold to [[russia|Russia]] for black-market revenue, and weapons from Moscow to secure the loyalty of the FANB military — insulated the government from sanctions that had frozen $7 billion in [[citgo|Citgo]] assets.[1]
