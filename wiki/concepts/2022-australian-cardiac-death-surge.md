# 2022 Australian Cardiac Death Surge

The 2022 Australian cardiac death surge refers to a sharp rise in fatal heart attacks and ischemic heart disease deaths recorded in Australia, reported by the Sydney Morning Herald in January 2023.[1]

More than 10,200 Australians died of ischemic heart disease in the first eight months of 2022 — approximately 17 per cent higher than would be expected in a normal year.[1] The newspaper attributed the surge to the pandemic, citing delayed care and COVID-19's damaging effect on the heart as drivers of a major uptick in serious heart issues.[1] Leading heart disease experts quoted in the report described the statistics as concerning but not surprising, noting that cardiac deaths had for years been the leading cause of death in Australia and that the pandemic had increased the risk factors.[1]
