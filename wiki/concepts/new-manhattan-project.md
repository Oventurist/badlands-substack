# New Manhattan Project

The "New Manhattan Project" is a phrase used by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and interpreted in Badlands commentary as shorthand for a national mobilization around nuclear energy to power the artificial intelligence era.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Trump's Davos confirmation of an American nuclear pivot vindicated a theory he had developed over several years: that the "Nuclear Nightmare" was an engineered [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian]] narrative designed to move the United States off the future both Eisenhower and Kennedy envisioned.[1] On this reading, Trump's habit of calling nuclear "the N word" reflects his understanding that the original Manhattan Project was a narrative deployment rather than an actual one — hence the need for what Trump describes as a New Manhattan Project.[1]

Burning Bright held that nuclear power has the literal capacity to accelerate the transition to the [[global-golden-age|Golden Age]], which is why establishment institutions pivoted from demonizing nuclear energy to demonizing the [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] it will power — a shift he saw in [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]]'s Davos warning on behalf of [[blackrock|BlackRock]].[1] Anyone who has accepted the "AI is going to destroy us all" narrative, he wrote, "has been played by the exact same deployment as your grandparents."[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] concurred, noting the parallel fear structures of the nuclear arms race and AI risk discourse.[1]
