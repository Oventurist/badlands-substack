# Monty Python's Life of Brian

The Life of Brian is the Monty Python comedy film that became, in May 2023, the subject of a public dispute over whether one of its scenes should be cut in response to activist objections.[1]

## The Loretta scene

The contested sequence involves the character Stan, who announces that he wants to be a woman named Loretta and to have babies, declaring, "I want to be a woman… It's my right as a man. I want to have babies… It's every man's right to have babies if he wants them," and, when challenged by [[john-cleese|John Cleese]]'s character, snapping, "Don't you oppress me!"[1] Activists argued the scene made fun of transgender people and demanded that it be removed from the film.[1]

## Reception of the controversy

[[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] noted the selectivity of the objection: the film's many jokes about Italians, Christians and Jews drew no comparable campaign, only the gender-identity scene did.[1] He placed the episode within a longer pattern in which comedians report that campuses are "dead as venues," hate-speech laws constrain performance, and roughly six out of ten students view offensive jokes as hate speech.[1] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that the film had, four decades in advance, captured the logic of the contemporary gender debate.[1]
