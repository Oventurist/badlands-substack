# ISIS Weapons Cache Operation in Southern Syria

The ISIS weapons cache operation in southern Syria was a joint counterterrorism operation conducted from 24 to 27 November 2025 by forces of United States Central Command ([[centcom|CENTCOM]]) and the Syrian Ministry of Interior, which located and destroyed more than 15 sites containing [[isis|ISIS]] weapons caches.[1]

## Conduct of the operation

U.S. military personnel from Combined Joint Task Force – Operation Inherent Resolve (CJTF-OIR) worked with Syrian forces to identify and eliminate ISIS weapons storage facilities across the Rif Dimashq province, using multiple airstrikes and ground detonations.[1] The combined operation destroyed over 130 mortars and rockets, multiple assault rifles, machine guns, anti-tank mines, and materials for building improvised explosive devices.[1] Forces also discovered and destroyed illicit drugs at the sites.[1]

Admiral Brad Cooper, the CENTCOM commander, said the operation "ensures gains made against ISIS are lasting and the group is not able to regenerate or export terrorist attacks to the U.S. homeland and around the world."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary highlighted the irony of the arrangement, noting that the Syrian government now cooperating with Washington against ISIS is led by [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed al-Sharaa]], formerly known as [[abu-mohammad-al-jolani|Abu Mohammad al-Jolani]], a onetime Al Qaeda and ISIS-aligned commander.[1] The writer suggested this partnership helped explain why Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and allied figures had turned sharply against the Syrian president.[1]
