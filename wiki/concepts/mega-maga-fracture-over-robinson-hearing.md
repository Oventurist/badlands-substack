# Mega MAGA Fracture Over Robinson Hearing

The "Mega MAGA fracture" refers to the July 2026 public rupture among prominent American conservative figures over the preliminary hearing of [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]], the man accused of murdering [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1]

## The dispute

Prominent conservatives condemned [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]], claiming evidence from Robinson's preliminary hearing undercut her assertions of a conspiracy in Kirk's murder.[1] Owens had argued that Robinson did not act alone, implicating [[israel|Israel]], Egypt, the US military, [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] staff, and Kirk's widow [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]].[1]

[[dinesh-dsouza|Dinesh D'Souza]] called Owens a "malignant narcissist," while [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]], [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]], Catturd and Lauren Southern also condemned her.[1] [[politico|Politico]] reported that TPUSA staff received death threats and increased security after Owens' broadcasts named them.[1] Owens fired back on [[x-platform|X]], alleging a coordinated "Zionist PR" campaign against her, while her spokesperson accused TPUSA figures of targeting her for months.[1] [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] and [[megyn-kelly|Megyn Kelly]] faced backlash for continuing to stand by Owens amid the growing rift.[1]

## Badlands analysis

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that despite a crowded news cycle, the Kirk assassination still dominated online conversation, and discussed the case with [[jonathan-drake|Jonathan Drake]] on [[culture-of-change|Culture of Change]] alongside broader questions of trials, juries, illegitimate international courts and the Colombian election.[1]

Over the preceding weekend, [[canncon|CannCon]] discovered that the purportedly secret "once you've seen what we've seen, you will agree this trial is over" video was in fact the same footage captured in the Court TV stream, which Owens then played on her show.[1] Ashe described the 4K-video claim as dramatically overblown and originating from the same crew behind the Epstein binder episode.[1]

The most generous reading, she wrote, is highly effective engagement farming, but she assessed the objective as broader: influencing the jury pool, as with what she has previously called the Derek Chauvin op, and beyond that, enforcing a narrative that it was a lone gunman with no conspiracy — closing debate before the trial and casting doubters as conspiracy theorists whose skepticism supposedly hurts Erika Kirk and marks them as bad Christians.[1] She emphasized that the proceeding was a probable cause hearing and that the accused deserves his day in court under the presumption of innocence, warning that a hyper-religious drama born from a legitimate mass trauma event may be intended to influence a trial, incite a mob, sell a narrative and possibly cover up a broader portfolio of crime.[1]
