# Multipolar War

The "Multipolar War" is [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s framing for the ongoing global contest between the unipolar Western hegemonic order and an emerging bloc of sovereign states, of which the 2026 Iran confrontation is treated as a central theater.[1]

## Framing

Burning Bright argues that the correct way to read [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s handling of the Iran conflict is to invert the assumed endgame: rather than a war eroding his mandate, the conflict is the accelerant into the [[global-golden-age|Golden Age]] he has campaigned on for a decade, and part of a plan "not just for the United States, but for a reimagined world."[1] The questions raised in his January 2026 essay "Sovereign Disentanglement" — including whether Trump would remove Khamenei while leaving the ruling party in place and rug-pull the global regime's own planned change event — are described as the ones that "cemented my belief in the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] and the Multipolar War."[1]

In this reading the process is templated and therefore mappable in detail: [[iran|Iran]] follows the [[venezuela-model|Venezuela Model]], "just bigger and more complex," and the resolution is expected to produce a new order in geopolitics, global governance, finance and trade.[1] The [[collectorate|Collectorate]] is cast as the losing party, panicking as the damage to its system becomes irreversible.[1]
