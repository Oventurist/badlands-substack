# 2023 US Veto of UN Security Council Gaza Resolution

On Wednesday, October 18, 2023, the United States vetoed a [[united-nations-security-council|United Nations Security Council]] resolution that would have called for humanitarian pauses in the conflict between [[israel|Israel]] and [[hamas|Hamas]] militants to allow humanitarian aid access to the [[gaza|Gaza Strip]].[1]

## The vote

The vote on the Brazilian-drafted text had been delayed twice over the preceding couple of days while the United States attempted to broker aid access to Gaza through other channels.[1] Twelve of the council's fifteen members voted in favor of the draft text, while Russia and Britain abstained and the United States exercised its veto.[1]

US Ambassador to the United Nations [[linda-thomas-greenfield|Linda Thomas-Greenfield]] told the council after the vote, "We are on the ground doing the hard work of diplomacy. We believe we need to let that diplomacy play out."[1] She added that while "resolutions are important" and "this council must speak out," the actions taken "must be informed by the facts on the ground and support direct diplomacy efforts," saying "the council needs to get this right."[1] Reuters noted that Washington traditionally shields its ally Israel from any Security Council action.[1]
