# ESG

ESG — Environmental, Social and Governance scoring — is a framework for rating corporations and institutions against non-financial criteria. In the Badlands Media corpus it is treated less as an investment methodology than as a coercive instrument by which a globalist [[cabal|cabal]] disciplines the corporate world into ideological compliance.

The essay [[ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo|AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO]] records [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] repeatedly prompting [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to incorporate "the strategic operational and tactical use of E.S.G. scores within the corporatocracy," and specifically to explain how environmental scores interlock with the climate change narrative while social and governance scores interlock with [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]].

In the resulting synthesis, ESG functions across nearly every objective attributed to the fictional Global Harmony Consortium. Scores reward organisations that promote secular and globalist values, reinforcing moral relativism against traditional religious teaching; they reward firms promoting contraception and population control, linked rhetorically to overpopulation and climate; they pressure corporations to elevate collective rights and group identity over individual freedoms; they reward women's organisations that deconstruct traditional family roles and incentivise career-focused individualism; and they push corporations to back policies prioritising collective safety over individual rights, such as COVID mandates. The output describes ESG scoring outright as "a powerful tool in the Consortium's multigenerational plan" and as a warfare instrument in its own right.
