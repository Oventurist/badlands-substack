# Precinct Strategy

The Precinct Strategy is a grassroots organizing approach that urges rank-and-file Republicans to fill the large number of vacant precinct committeeman positions inside their state and county parties, on the theory that those positions ultimately control candidate selection.[1] It is associated with [[dan-schultz|Dan Schultz]], Steve Stern, and the team at PrecinctStrategy.com, who have worked to educate party members about the role of precinct committeemen and about what advocates describe as a deliberate establishment strategy of keeping those positions empty.[1]

## Endorsement and rationale

The effort has been publicly endorsed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who described it as "a great way to restore our Republic by transforming our Party from currently at less than half-strength at the precinct level into a full-strength Get Out The Vote powerhouse - as it should be."[1] Advocates argue that because precinct committeemen are the voting members who select delegates and, through assemblies and conventions, decide which candidates reach the primary ballot, control of those seats is more consequential than any individual campaign.[1]

## Application in Colorado

Longtime [[america-first-movement|America First]] Republicans in Colorado — described by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as the "America First elders" of the state party — held coffee-shop meetups in early 2021 to educate newcomers and fill vacant positions, and succeeded in taking over the party organization in several counties.[1] The most significant of these efforts was in [[el-paso-county|El Paso County]], the most populous county in Colorado and the only county in the United States home to five military commands.[1] By the [[2022-colorado-republican-state-assembly|2022 state assembly and convention]], America First grassroots had filled enough voting positions to affect outcomes.[1]

## Advocacy for 2024

Writing in June 2023, Ashe in America argued that the precinct strategy should be the single highest priority for Republican voters heading into the 2024 candidate-selection cycle, warning that the party had kept the roles empty because it served the establishment's interests and would fill them with neoconservatives if grassroots activists did not take them first.[1] She paired that recommendation with advice to learn each state's specific candidate-selection process in advance, to build a coalition and plan for hand-marked paper ballots before the convention rather than at it, and to sign up to work elections at the precinct level regardless of party affiliation.[1]

## Promotion in the Badlands corpus

Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] directed readers concerned about election integrity to get involved and trained for the 2024 election through PrecinctStrategy.com, presenting it as one of the concrete individual actions available to ordinary citizens.[2] Her December 2022 essay quoted Trump's February 27, 2022 statement in full, attributing the strategy's explanation to Dan Schultz and describing it as a way to restore the Republic by transforming the party from less than half-strength at the precinct level into a full-strength Get Out The Vote powerhouse.[2] Gillespie treated such participation as one of the accumulating pushes on what she called the [[flywheel-of-freedom|Flywheel of Freedom]].[2]
