# Executive Order 14251

Executive Order 14251 is an order issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that excludes certain federal agencies from collective bargaining requirements under federal labor law.[1] The order directs that agencies whose primary functions relate to intelligence, counterintelligence, investigative, or national security work may be excluded from collective bargaining obligations under the Federal Service Labor-Management Relations Statute.[1]

## Litigation

Federal employee unions challenged the order, and a lower court issued a preliminary injunction blocking its implementation.[1] In late February 2026 a three-judge panel of the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-ninth-circuit|US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit]] vacated that injunction, holding that the unions were unlikely to succeed on the merits of their claim that the order constituted retaliation violating the First Amendment, and that the injunction had therefore been wrongly imposed.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized the ruling by writing that "collective bargaining in national security agencies is dead," counting it among attacks on the president's agenda that had been thwarted that week.[1]
