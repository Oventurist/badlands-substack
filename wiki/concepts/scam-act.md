# SCAM Act

The Stop Citizenship Abuse and Misrepresentation Act, known by the acronym SCAM Act, is a bill introduced in January 2026 by Senator [[eric-schmitt|Eric Schmitt]] (R-Mo.) and backed by the [[white-house|White House]]. It was introduced as a direct legislative response to the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal]].

According to the Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026, the bill would expand federal denaturalization powers by creating a ten-year window after naturalization — potentially reducible to five years — during which citizenship could be revoked. Grounds for revocation would include defrauding the government of $10,000 or more, aggravated felonies, espionage, and terrorist affiliations, with such acts retroactively disqualifying the "good moral character" requirement underlying naturalization.

Deputy Chief of Staff [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]] praised the bill and characterized the Minnesota scandal as "one of the greatest financial scandals in American history," pledging immediate denaturalization and deportation of implicated immigrants. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] endorsed the measure, summarizing her position as "deport the Somalis; prosecute the Americans," arguing that participants in a "massive criminal enterprise" should not retain citizenship and that enablers within American institutions should be prosecuted as well. She also praised the bill's name as "delightful."
