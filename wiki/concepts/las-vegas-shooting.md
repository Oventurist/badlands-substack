# Las Vegas Shooting

The Las Vegas shooting of October 2017 occupies a pivotal place in the article's narrative, both as a national moment of somber reflection and as the immediate precursor to the appearance of the anonymous poster known as [[qanon|Q]].[1]

The author, then searching inward for answers a few months after his brother's death, describes the rest of the world joining him in that reflection when the shooting occurred.[1] Just weeks later an Anon appeared on 4chan offering cryptic musings in a style not entirely unique to the board.[1]

Speculation across the internet held that the young Crown Prince — [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] — was somehow connected to the tragedy, perhaps as the intended target.[1] The author also refers to "inexplicable datapoints surrounding the Vegas Shooting that were being wiped from the internet," which together with the [[sword-dance|Sword Dance]] drew him toward research on Saudi Arabia.[1]
