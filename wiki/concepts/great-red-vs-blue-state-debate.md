# The Great Red vs. Blue State Debate

"The Great Red vs. Blue State Debate" was a 90-minute televised debate broadcast by [[fox-news|Fox News]] on the evening of Thursday, November 30, 2023, from Alpharetta, Georgia, pitting Florida governor and Republican presidential candidate [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] against California's Democratic governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]], who was not a candidate for president.[1] It was moderated by Fox News host [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]].[1] Contemporary coverage described it as possibly the most unorthodox match-up of the 2024 presidential primary campaign to date.[1]

## Format and content

Newsom said he was taking part in order to "tell the truth about the [[joe-biden|Biden]]-[[kamala-harris|Harris]] record and also compare and contrast Ron DeSantis' record."[1] Asked what grade they would assign President Biden, the two governors gave predictable answers: an "A" from Newsom, a Biden campaign surrogate, and a "fail" from DeSantis, who charged that Newsom was "joined at the hip with Biden and Harris."[1] The exchange was reported by [[cbs-news|CBS News]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the most reasoned participant in the event appeared to be the moderator, Hannity — "and that tells you everything you need to know."[1] She wrote that she was puzzled throughout as to what the program was meant to be: Newsom's role as a Biden surrogate was obvious, but DeSantis's purpose was not.[1] In her assessment DeSantis was "completely detached from the conservative base, independents, and thinking people," delivering headline talking points against California without substance, while the most salient points on all topics came from Hannity.[1]

She singled out DeSantis's handling of Newsom's attack over his consideration of pardons for January 6 defendants: rather than answer, DeSantis reverted to his California messaging, passing up an opportunity to align with the growing share of Americans who, in her view correctly, regard J6 as a government set-up staged to cover for a stolen election.[1] When DeSantis produced a book on gender during the debate, she concluded that he believed the GOP base was the Daily Wire audience.[1]

Newsom, whom she called "a toolbox," was judged to have performed poorly as well — at one point correcting DeSantis's pronunciation of Kamala Harris's name and declaring "It's Madame Vice President, to you!" — but she considered him largely irrelevant until he formally announces a candidacy.[1] Her structural criticism was that the format forced DeSantis to attack California, the state with the most electoral votes in the nation, while running for president, a trap Newsom capitalized on and which she believed DeSantis recognized on stage.[1] She characterized the decision to appear as on brand for a governor who ignores sound counsel and follows "the terrible advice of the right wing of the [[uniparty|uniparty]]."[1]

At the debate's end, Newsom proposed extending another half hour and both DeSantis and Hannity agreed; after the commercial break Hannity announced that both men had prior engagements and closed the program.[1]
