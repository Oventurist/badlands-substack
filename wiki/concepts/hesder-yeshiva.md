# Hesder Yeshiva

A Hesder yeshiva is an Israeli religious academy operating under an arrangement that combines Torah study with military service in the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]]. The programme is associated with the Religious Zionist community and produces a disproportionate share of Israeli combat soldiers and officers.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports that the leaders of twelve Hesder yeshivas threatened to stop sending students to Armored Corps units in response to an IDF pilot programme integrating female soldiers into tank crews. The IDF subsequently stated that men and women would not serve inside the same tank. The Brief notes that the threat carried genuine force precisely because Hesder graduates are heavily represented in combat units and the officer corps, so a boycott of armored service would have measurable recruitment consequences. Badlands commentary frames the episode as one facet of a broader and worsening conflict between Israel's religious communities and the military, aggravated by an April ruling of Israel's High Court of Justice requiring the IDF to proceed with integration.
