# Prison Privatization

Prison privatization — the transfer of American prison operations to private companies running them for profit — is the economic engine at the centre of Erik Carlson's account of the 1990s.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## The mechanism described

Under President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], Carlson writes, the prison system was becoming privatized. The difficulty was that crime was starting to drop significantly, so the parties who benefited from full prisons needed the trend reversed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

Three levers are identified. First, legislation: the [[crime-bill-1994|Crime Bill of 1994]], authored by Clinton and Senator [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], lengthened sentences for non-violent drug offenders.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) Second, culture: the FBI and [[cia|CIA]] allegedly engineered the shift to [[gangster-rap|gangster rap]] to influence young black men toward a life of crime.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) Third, supply: the CIA is said to have smuggled drugs from South and Central America for sale on the streets of South Central Los Angeles, where [[rick-ross|Rick Ross]] grew wealthy distributing them.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

Carlson summarizes the arrangement as a racket in which guns were traded in Central America for drugs, the drugs were sold on American city streets, and the resulting drug trade filled the private prisons — with kickbacks to the politicians involved.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
