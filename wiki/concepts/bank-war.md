# Bank War

The **Bank War** was the political struggle that developed over the rechartering of the [[second-bank-of-the-united-states|Second Bank of the United States]] during the presidency of [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]].[1] The conflict ended in the shutdown of the Bank and its replacement by state banks.[1]

## Use in Badlands commentary

In "The COVFEFE Dig," [[gmoney|GMONEY]] invokes the Bank War to explain why [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] hung a portrait of Jackson as the first artwork installed after moving into the White House.[1] Jackson is characterized as the president known for destroying the central bankers and for coining the phrase "Drain the Swamp," making him the historical template for the dig's reading of Trump's monetary intentions.[1] Jackson's birth date of 3/15 supplies the numerological anchor of the article, since the letters C-O-V decode under a simple A=1 substitution to 3-15-22.[1]
