# Sexualization of Children

The sexualization of children is a recurring theme in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, framed not merely as a moral or cultural problem but as a deliberate instrument of social engineering. The theme was developed at length by contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] in May 2023, responding to Australian senator Malcolm Roberts's article asking whether the [[united-nations|United Nations]] is "trying to groom our kids."[1]

## Deschamps's argument

Deschamps argued that the aim of sexualizing children is to normalize adult sexual contact with minors as "just another 'queer' sexual identity," and that this is pursued not only for perverted reasons but because it would radically change culture, the human psyche and therefore society itself.[1] Sexualizing children, in his framing, is a weapon in service of a much more insidious design.[1]

His psychological account holds that sexuality is meant to come online at puberty, holistically activating experiences accumulated in childhood, and that the constituent elements of healthy sexual function build up slowly during pre-puberty — a period in which the child should be developing an appreciation for people, character, aesthetics and the social impacts of pair-bonding rather than sexualizing anything.[1] Pre-relationship skills needed in adulthood are learned in childhood by observing parents and others in their relationships.[1]

Where sexual thoughts, behaviors and perspectives are pushed on children too early — he cites Disney and modern overly sexualized media — the resulting adult relationship with sex tends to be distorted, hedonistic and unsatisfying, focused on the self and on somatic pleasure rather than on the romantic pair-bonding and life-giving meaning of sexual expression.[1] The problem is magnified when actual grooming occurs under the formula "we show love through sex," which he describes as a standard methodology of pedophile groomers; the result, he argues, would be generations unable to pair-bond properly and therefore unable to raise children within a stable parental and communal landscape.[1]

## The r/K framing

Deschamps situated the agenda in a wider claim that globalists seek to "destroy the human family 2.0," the first such campaign having already succeeded to the point that the world has been "post-healthy family" for over a century.[1] In his account, the state and globalist institutions then present themselves as managers of the problem, offering solutions that shift human mating away from K-selected strategies toward r-selected ones, so that sex functions "more like a biological drug than an act of deep spiritual union."[1]

He described r-selected strategies as characteristic of large-scale herd animals, in which each infant receives only a small share of total parental attention, and claimed that in humans this produces low-IQ, high-neuroticism, high-ACE adults with central nervous system problems, mental issues, group-think tendencies and reliance on extrinsic authorities to manage their lives.[1] The end goal, he concluded, is not the sexualization of children as such but "the creation of a totally controlled mentally, emotionally, and sexually deranged population, the perfect pawn for the technocratic slave plantation" — which is why, in his view, real family, good parenting, healthy marriages and true community face a relentless assault.[1]
