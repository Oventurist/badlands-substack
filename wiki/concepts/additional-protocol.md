# Additional Protocol

The Additional Protocol is an enhanced safeguards framework of the [[international-atomic-energy-agency|International Atomic Energy Agency]] intended to strengthen international nuclear inspections beyond the baseline safeguards agreements attached to the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty. Adoption of the protocol gives inspectors broader access and information rights, and its presence or absence in a bilateral civil nuclear agreement is treated as a key indicator of the agreement's nonproliferation rigor.

## Relevance to the U.S.–Saudi nuclear agreement

The Badlands Brief reported, citing CNN, that the draft civilian nuclear cooperation agreement tentatively agreed between the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] would not require the kingdom to adopt the Additional Protocol. Democratic lawmakers and nonproliferation advocates criticized the framework on that basis, and Reuters previously reported that critics fault it for omitting both the Additional Protocol and the longstanding American "gold standard" commitment under which partner countries forgo domestic uranium enrichment and spent-fuel reprocessing. The Trump administration responded that the agreement would strengthen strategic and economic ties while complying with applicable legal safety requirements. The unsigned agreement had not been submitted to [[congress|Congress]] for the review required after presidential signature.
