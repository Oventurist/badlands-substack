# RICO Grande

**RICO Grande** (also referred to as the "Grand RICO") is the term used in the Badlands corpus for a federal racketeering prosecution, announced by investigative journalist [[john-solomon|John Solomon]] in February 2026, that its proponents expect to hold the institutions of the Russiagate era accountable for the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of government]].

## The claim

According to the Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026, Solomon has said the Grand RICO includes the 2020 election fraud allegations, and he sounds to commentators like [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as though he is deploying a phased communications plan. Ashe, who first wrote about the prospect in the Brief on July 17, 2025, describes the RICO as "a vehicle for Truth and Reconciliation" that provides accountability "we haven't had before now" — while acknowledging it "could be a rug" (a false promise or diversion). He states that he wants the July 4, 2026 part of the plan "to be underwritten by real joy in victory."

## Timeline and stakes

Solomon is reported to have bet [[roger-stone|Roger Stone]] a steak dinner on indictments landing by June 20, 2026 — the end of spring — a wager Ashe frames with the Deadwood-derived slogan "Indictments by Deadwood? Let's go!" The Brief ties the RICO narrative to the February 2026 [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] investigation based in Fort Pierce, Florida, into former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] and Senate records, and reads the [[the-atlantic|Atlantic]]'s April 2026 report on FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] as a smear intended to blunt the coming "justice phase."
