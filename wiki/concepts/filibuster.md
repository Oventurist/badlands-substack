# Filibuster

The filibuster is the [[senate|United States Senate]] procedure by which a minority can prolong debate and effectively require a sixty-vote threshold to advance most legislation. In the Badlands Media corpus it is the central procedural obstacle cited in disputes over the pace of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2026 legislative agenda.

## Trump's call for termination

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief quotes Truth Social posts in which Trump declares that "anybody who doesn't want to Terminate the Filibuster is a FOOL." He argues that Democrats would end the filibuster within minutes of taking office and then add two states, four senators, additional House members, and expand the Supreme Court to twenty-one justices, producing an insurmountable Electoral College advantage — after which, he says, "the Republican Party will never win another Election" and he would be the last Republican president. He directs Senate Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] and the Republican Senate to terminate the filibuster and immediately approve the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]]. In a companion post he warns that Utah is moving to an all-mail-ballot format like Colorado's and urges either termination of the filibuster or passage of the Act to stop it.

## The Senate counter-argument

Per Politico reporting summarized in the Brief, Thune has emphasized working through regular legislative process, while Senators Thom Tillis, John Cornyn, and Bill Cassidy have resisted the expectation that senators approve proposals without amendment or extended debate, citing Senate procedure and narrow majorities. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reads this resistance through change-management theory: in a transformational change program one moves fast and breaks rules precisely because the old guard's rules prevent transformation, and the resisters are "deliberately destabilized" — discombobulated — which is exactly where a change agent wants them.
