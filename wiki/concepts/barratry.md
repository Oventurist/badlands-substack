# Barratry

Barratry is an old common-law offense dating back to medieval England. At its core it means habitually inciting, promoting or fomenting lawsuits and legal disputes in which the instigator has no legitimate interest.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked the term while arguing that "abusing the legal process for narrative gain should be a crime," in the context of the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s superseding indictment of the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]].[1] She observed that the offense is not much in vogue in 2026, and proposed that the country should "make barratry great again, beginning with the SPLC and their collaborators."[1] The remark followed her account of parties who, she says, fabricated claims under the [[ku-klux-klan-act|Ku Klux Klan Act]] against Badlands-affiliated figures in 2022.[1]
