# Barratry

Barratry is an old common-law offense dating back to medieval England. As defined in the June 4, 2026 Badlands Brief, "at its core, it means habitually inciting, promoting, or fomenting lawsuits and legal disputes in which the instigator has no legitimate interest."

## Invocation in Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] introduces the term while considering whether "abusing the legal process for narrative gain" is a crime, in the context of the superseding indictment against the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] and of claims she says were fabricated against her and her colleagues under the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]] in 2022. Observing that the offense is "apparently not really in vogue in 2026," she argues that "we should make barratry great again, beginning with the SPLC and their collaborators," adding that she has a list of further targets. The concept functions in the corpus as a proposed legal framework for prosecuting what its authors describe as coordinated lawfare by advocacy organizations.
