# From Dictatorship to Democracy

*From Dictatorship to Democracy* is [[gene-sharp|Gene Sharp]]'s how-to pamphlet on nonviolent revolution, described by Badlands Media as the practical field guide of the [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]] doctrine.[1]

The pamphlet has been translated into more than thirty languages and is cited by protest movements around the world.[1] American activist training programs and scholars of nonviolence promoted its ideas extensively, and Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] states they have been implemented by nearly every significant protest movement of the past four decades.[1]

In Serbia, [[freedom-house|Freedom House]] funded the translation, production and distribution of 5,000 copies of the pamphlet, material eventually disseminated to 70,000 activists and operatives during the campaign that produced [[otpor|Otpor!]] and the fall of [[slobodan-milosevic|Slobodan Milosevic]].[1] Sharp's larger work *The Politics of Nonviolent Action* was separately translated and compiled as the "Otpor! User Manual."[1]
