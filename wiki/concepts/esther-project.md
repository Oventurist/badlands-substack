# Esther Project

The "Esther Project" is the code name of an influencer-recruitment campaign run on behalf of the government of [[israel|Israel]] in the United States, disclosed in filings made with the U.S. Department of Justice and reported by [[jerusalem-post|The Jerusalem Post]] on 1 October 2025.[1]

## Disclosure

Newly filed records with the Department of Justice showed that Israel's government had quietly launched a two-track influence operation in the United States, blending big-budget political advertising with grassroots-style influencer campaigns.[1] The filings revealed that a firm called Bridges Partners LLC had been hired to manage an influencer network under the project code-named the "Esther Project."[1] The disclosure was required under the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]].[1]

In its disclosure, Bridges said its work was intended to "assist with promoting cultural interchange between the United States and Israel," and specified that the engagement runs through a German division of the global public relations firm Havas.[1] Attached documents showed that the firm — owned by Israeli consultants Uri Steinberg and Yair Levi — was formed in June 2025 in Delaware and soon afterwards received nearly $200,000 to recruit and coordinate U.S.-based social media influencers.[1]

## Relationship to Project Esther

It is unclear whether the "Esther Project" has any link to [[project-esther|Project Esther]], a plan to combat antisemitism published by the American right-wing think tank the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]].[1]

## Commentary

[[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] framed the disclosure in explicitly military terms, arguing that "the warfare going on is at the narrative level," that "you have a foreign country paying American citizens to influence Americans' opinions," and that such conduct constitutes "an act of war in information warfare" — concluding that "by definition that makes these influencers enemy combatants."[1]
