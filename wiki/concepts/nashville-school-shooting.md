# Nashville School Shooting

The Nashville school shooting was a mass shooting in Nashville, Tennessee in late March 2023 in which three children and three adults were killed.[1] The attack took place on Monday, March 27, 2023 at a Christian school in Nashville and was carried out by [[audrey-hale|Audrey Hale]], a 28-year-old female-to-male transgender person who had reportedly attended the school as a child.[2]

The [[metro-nashville-police-department|Metro Nashville Police Department]] moved quickly in its investigation and released video footage showing Hale shooting a way into the building and then methodically moving through it.[2] At a press conference, police said Hale had left behind a "manifesto" along with maps annotating security camera locations, doorways and other building details.[2] Police Chief [[john-drake|John Drake]] said investigators possessed writings pertaining to the date of the attack and "a map drawn out of how all this was gonna take place," and indicated that a theory of motive existed but had not been confirmed.[2] Police confirmed to reporters that the attack was targeted and acknowledged "some theory" that the shooter's gender identity bore on the choice of target.[2]

The shooting triggered immediate political conflict in the Tennessee legislature. Democratic state Representatives [[justin-jones|Justin Jones]], [[justin-pearson|Justin Pearson]] and [[gloria-johnson|Gloria Johnson]] joined protesters in the state House chamber and chanted for gun-control legislation, prompting a GOP-led move to expel the three Democrats from the House.[1] Jones was later voted back into office, and on April 17, 2023 carried a child-size coffin into the State Capitol during a gun-violence protest led by Bishop [[william-barber-ii|William Barber II]], where the sergeant-at-arms blocked the prop from the House floor.[1]
