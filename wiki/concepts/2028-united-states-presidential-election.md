# 2028 United States Presidential Election

The 2028 United States presidential election is set to be the first race since 2016 without a sitting president vying for the nomination on either side.[1]

## Early polling (December 2025)

A [[cnn|CNN]] poll conducted by SSRS and reported on December 21, 2025 found that about half of Americans had already given the 2028 election at least some thought, with nearly three years to go.[1] About two-thirds of Americans had not yet settled on any possible candidate, while 33% said they did have someone in mind.[1] Those respondents named 65 different people they would like to see run, with little consolidation around any single figure: most potential candidates were mentioned by 1% of respondents or fewer.[1]

Among Republicans and Republican-leaning independents, 22% named Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], 4% named Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and 2% named Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]].[1] Another 3% said they would like President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to make another run even though he is barred by the two-term limit prescribed by the 22nd Amendment of the Constitution.[1]

If it were a show designed to make people stop paying attention, they couldn't be doing a better job."[1]
