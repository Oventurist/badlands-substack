# Watts Riots

The Watts riots were the 1965 civil disturbances in the Watts neighborhood of Los Angeles. They appear in the Badlands Media essay A Journey of Ever changing Truths as one data point in the article's proposed thirty-year cycle of engineered racial unrest.

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] lists them tersely — "In 1965, there were race riots in the Watts neighborhood of Los Angeles" — between the [[harlem-riot-of-1935|Harlem race riots of 1935]] and the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] beating of 1991 with the [[los-angeles-riots-1992|Los Angeles riots]] that followed, and ahead of the [[george-floyd|George Floyd]] events of 2020. The pattern, in the source's reading, reflects an operating assumption by the [[cabal|Cabal]] that "if America went more than 30 years without an incident that creates racial division, racism could possibly start to disappear," and that a riot must therefore appear roughly every three decades "to fan the flames of racism."
