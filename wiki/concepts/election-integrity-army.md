# Election Integrity Army

The "Election Integrity Army" is the name President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] gave to the Republican election-monitoring operation he announced for the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]], presented as a revival and expansion of the force he said Republicans fielded in every state during the [[2024-united-states-presidential-election|2024 election]].

## Trump's announcement (May 2026)

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief leads with a [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement in which Trump declared "This Election Will Be Fair" and announced the army. He said that during the 2024 election — which he described as a historic win in which he took every swing state and won both the Electoral and Popular votes — "the Republicans had an Election Integrity Army in every single State to preserve the sanctity of each legal vote," and that the 2026 operation "will be much bigger and stronger."

Trump framed the announcement as a response to what he called a Democrat-led "Election Integrity Group" being assembled by Senate Democratic leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]], which he said would include [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]] — "famous for handing guns to Mexican cartels under the Barack Hussein Obama administration" — and the election lawyer [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], whom Trump blamed for the "fake Russia dossier from a foreign nation" in 2016. "The Democrats are totally unhinged," Trump wrote, "and we will not allow them to threaten the integrity of our Elections." He closed: "All Americans should have their voices be heard by casting a vote. Be assured this Election will be fair!"

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded that "a 'fair' election using the same rigged systems is not a real election," arguing that "we cannot have real elections until we are honest about how fake they've been." She wrote that the 2026 midterms might "reveal the systemic corruption in US elections and deliver that honesty – the reckoning of fake elections," and that the elections are so fake "they're part of a treasonous grand conspiracy," urging prosecution of the alleged co-conspirators. Trump's declaration, in her reading, was insufficient without systemic reform: "There must be honesty about US elections after years of gaslighting by those responsible for public trust."
