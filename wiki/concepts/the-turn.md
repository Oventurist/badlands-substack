# The Turn

**The Turn** is a narrative concept developed by Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] on his Substack, describing the moment at which a long-play strategy finally pays off and the faith an audience has placed in a leader — faith often predicated on speculative presumption — becomes material, paying dividends against the odds.[1]

## Application to the Gaza war

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] applied the concept in June 2024 to Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], arguing that something significant had occurred that he had been waiting for since before October 7.[1] In his reading, MBS had absorbed criticism from many directions for his apparently inert posture toward Israel over the preceding nine months while playing the most important role in the Gaza conflict: acting as "the rock in the storm."[1]

After the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] invaded Gaza, MBS convened an emergency summit of the Islamic world, where the writer presumes he asked the elders for trust in his leadership. The Muslim world then had to do "the one thing that nobody in the world expected them to do: nothing." MBS had to hold back nearly two billion citizens while serving as the whipping post for their vitriol and accusations of betraying Palestine; failure to keep the peace — to prevent the Ummah from turning into a Caliphate — would, in this account, have meant a third world war.[1]

The prediction attached to the framework was that MBS would eventually turn on [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], described as the logical progression of a counterinsurgency. For the Turn to be genuine and not merely trigger calls for revenge, he had to bide his time and let Netanyahu "show the world who he is."[1]
