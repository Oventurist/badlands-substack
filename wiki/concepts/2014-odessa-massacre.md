# 2014 Odessa Massacre

The **2014 Odessa massacre** refers to violent clashes in the Ukrainian port city of Odessa on May 2, 2014, during the early days of the Ukraine crisis, in which street battles between pro-Ukraine and anti-Maidan activists culminated in a fire at the city's Trade Unions House that claimed dozens of lives.[1] Moscow has consistently described the incident as a massacre carried out by Ukrainian nationalist groups.[1]

## Lavrov's Third Reich comparison

Speaking on Sunday, June 29, 2025, after talks with Kyrgyz Foreign Minister Jeenbek Kulubaev, Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] drew a direct parallel between the Third Reich and modern [[ukraine|Ukraine]] in their treatment of populations they seek to subjugate.[1] "The Nazis burned Jews simply because they were Jews, and the Ukrainian Nazis burned Russian people in Odessa on May 2, 2014 simply because they were Russian," he said.[1]
