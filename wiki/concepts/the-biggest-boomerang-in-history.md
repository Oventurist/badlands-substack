# The Biggest Boomerang In History

"The Biggest Boomerang In History" is the name [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] gives, in a Badlands Media article of May 17, 2025, to his thesis that the trap set for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by the outgoing [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration was inverted into the instrument of that administration's own exposure.[1] The article is subtitled "How Do You Trap a Very Dangerous Animal?" and forms part of a serialised narrative that includes "Moves And Countermoves" and "Nothing Is As It Appears."[1]

## The three-stage framing

Lange organises the story into three escalating labels drawn from Q drop 4012 of April 30, 2020, which called the affair the "BIGGEST POLITICAL SCANDAL IN AMERICAN HISTORY" and predicted a "MUCH BIGGER" graphic would eventually be needed.[1] [[spygate|SPYGATE]] denotes the original mass spying operation by the Obama administration intended to influence the 2016 election and secure a [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] victory — the biggest scandal in American history.[1] [[fisagate|FISAGATE]] denotes what followed her defeat: a conspiracy to frame and remove a duly elected president, and therefore the biggest cover-up in American history.[1] The BOOMERANG is Lange's term for Trump's conversion of both into a mechanism that destroys their authors.[1]

The conspiracy, in this account, ran through the Intelligence Community under [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] and [[james-clapper|James Clapper]], the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] and [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]], and the [[fbi|FBI]] under [[james-comey|James Comey]] and [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]], with many underlings.[1] The enemy believed it had regained control of Trump's DOJ when [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] recused himself from the Russia investigation.[1]

## Firing Comey as the first move

Lange identifies Comey as the weak link: he sat at the centre of everything, remained FBI director as an Obama holdover, and had initiated the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] investigation designed to trap Trump.[1] Firing him cost the enemy control of the bureau, allowed Trump to clean house, and forced them to secure a Special Counsel investigation immediately, before a new FBI director could be appointed.[1] Trump is said to have forced [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] to write the memo enumerating grounds for the dismissal, focused on Comey's mishandling of the Clinton server investigation.[1]

## The Flynn trap and its reversal

Comey's FBI had entrapped [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]], altered documents to obtain a [[foreign-intelligence-surveillance-act|FISA]] warrant on [[carter-page|Carter Page]] and concealed exculpatory evidence from the FISA court.[1] Lange stresses that FISAGATE was a cover for spying already underway through [[five-eyes|FVEY]] partners in the United Kingdom and Australia long before any FISA application.[1] Flynn resigned on February 13, 2017 and pleaded guilty on December 1, 2017; four days later Q declared him "safe."[1] Sentencing before Judge [[emmet-sullivan|Emmet Sullivan]] was repeatedly deferred on cooperation grounds, and Q drop 2548 pointed to a separate criminal investigation outside [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]]'s purview to which Flynn was providing significant assistance.[1]

The Boston Herald reporting quoted in the article describes released Justice Department documents showing an exchange between [[lisa-page|Lisa Page]] and [[peter-strzok|Peter Strzok]] about reminding Flynn that lying to the FBI is a crime — Page recommending Strzok "slip it in" — and agents uncertain whether the object was to catch Flynn in a lie or elicit a [[logan-act|Logan Act]] violation, possibly to create conditions for his firing.[1] Lange calls these emails evidence of a conspiracy to commit, and notes that Comey could only have known Flynn was lying if the administration was already reading transcripts of his calls.[1]

## DECLAS as the weapon

The inversion, in Lange's telling, rested on the presidential power to [[declas|declassify]].[1] Q drop 3784 of January 23, 2020 set out the chain of command — POTUS to [[william-barr|Barr]], Barr to [[john-durham|Durham]] — with foreign classified intelligence provided as needed and the question of whether Durham wanted to freeze public declassification because of grand jury material, closing with "Slow drip > Flood."[1] Fox News reported that Trump issued a memo giving Barr authority to declassify any documents related to 2016 surveillance of the Trump campaign and directed the intelligence community to cooperate fully.[1] Lange argues Barr coordinated DECLAS across multiple [[racketeer-influenced-and-corrupt-organizations-act|RICO]] investigations, used the declassified emails to drop the Flynn prosecution, and fed documents to select members of Congress and to journalists including Sara Carter and [[john-solomon|John Solomon]]; Trump's public criticism of Barr, like his earlier criticism of Sessions, was deception.[1]

The declassified Strzok–Page text messages, Lange writes, were narrative-war material that introduced the public to the pair and shifted coverage from Trump–Russia collusion to FBI bias, while the emails Barr released were the real bombshells.[1] They in turn led to [[gmail-draft-communications|Gmail draft communications]], which Congress began investigating as a means of evading the [[freedom-of-information-act|Freedom of Information Act]].[1]

## Obama, the paper trail and the library raid

Lange contends Obama personally ordered the spying — through Brennan's [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] using FVEY partners, and through Comey's FBI via a counterintelligence rather than criminal investigation, so that it could be kept classified — and that a paper trail led straight to him because nobody believed Clinton would lose.[1] White House visitor logs for the FISA court judge who approved the Page warrants were among the material Obama designated classified and moved to his presidential library, following what Lange calls the routine way past presidents hid their crimes.[1] He asserts Trump ordered a raid on the Obama Presidential Library office early in his administration, reported only by Q, and that the silence around it reflects Obama's exposure: his own 2009 Executive Order forbade classifying information to conceal violations of law.[1]

## The Clinton strand

The article devotes its second half to the [[hillary-clinton-private-email-server|Clinton private email server]], Comey's conflicts of interest through his brother [[peter-comey|Peter Comey]] and [[dla-piper|DLA Piper]], his compensation from [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]] and board seat at [[hsbc|HSBC Holdings]], and the [[clinton-lynch-tarmac-meeting|tarmac meeting]] that Lange says the [[wizards-and-warlocks|Wizards and Warlocks]] exposed by tipping off a local reporter.[1] He concludes that Lynch flipped rather than go down with Clinton, that the Weiner laptop emails forced the reopening of the case on October 26, 2016 — the biggest [[october-surprise|October Surprise]] in history — and that Trump used Clinton's own instrument against her.[1] Lange predicts Comey will be the first big unsealed indictment and promises a further article on another figure central to the [[two-tier-justice-system|two tier justice system]].[1]

The article carries Badlands Media's standard disclaimer that contributions represent the opinions of their authors; its assertions are Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
