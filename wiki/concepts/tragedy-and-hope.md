# Tragedy and Hope

*Tragedy and Hope* is the historical work by [[carroll-quigley|Carroll Quigley]] cited in Badlands Media's May 2025 essay "Wilted Ivy" as documentary support for the claim that elite academic and financial institutions were deliberately organized to serve a centralized system of control.[1]

## The quoted passage

"Wilted Ivy" describes Quigley as "a historian with privileged access" and quotes him writing that "the powers of financial capitalism had another far-reaching aim... nothing less than to create a world system of financial control in private hands."[1] The article treats this passage as an insider admission rather than speculation, and uses it as the interpretive key for its account of the [[ivy-league|Ivy League]].[1]

## Use in the article's argument

On the strength of the Quigley quotation, the essay argues that [[harvard-university|Harvard]] "acted less as a neutral educator and more as a strategic hub," reinforcing an orthodoxy of narratives, of institutions, and of credentialed "experts" upholding the prevailing system.[1] Graduating from an Ivy League school, in this framing, was "less about critical thinking, and more about credentialing for access into the upper tiers of an increasingly globalized control system."[1] The article contends that this arrangement was cultivated rather than spontaneous, pointing to alumni patterns spanning Wall Street, media, pharmaceutical leadership and intelligence work, and to [[yale-university|Yale]]'s [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society.[1]
