# Warrior Dividends

"Warrior Dividends" is a payment program for U.S. military personnel promoted by the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and described as being for "our great military heroes."[1]

The program appeared on the list of accomplishments in the [[white-house|White House]] statement issued after the [[2026-state-of-the-union|2026 State of the Union Address]], among the items the administration said congressional Democrats refused to applaud.[1]
