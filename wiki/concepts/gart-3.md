# GART 3 (Irvine)

GART 3 was the third installment of the Great Awakening Round Table live event series produced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held January 11–14, 2024 in Irvine, California.[1]

## Format

The event was promoted as a live, ticketed gathering offering in-person speakers and meet-and-greets across the weekend, along with food and social programming.[1] Attendees could watch live, in-person versions of Badlands Media shows, participate in live question-and-answer sessions with Badlands panels, and meet show hosts in person.[1]

For those unable to attend in person, virtual tickets to stream the entirety of GART 3 were made available; these also included a direct link to a private Telegram chat where purchasers could interact with speakers and with hundreds of other in-person and virtual attendees.[1] All virtual ticket purchases included access to recorded replays of every GART 3 panel during the event and for at least six months afterward.[1]

The event was promoted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands News Brief]] of January 11, 2024 as a "last call" for ticket sales.[1]

## Announcement

Ticket sales for GART 3 in Irvine, California opened in September 2023, announced in the same Badlands News Brief that made replays of [[gart-2|GART 2]] — held in Cocoa Beach — available.[2] Badlands framed both events as part of the organization's continued evolution and expressed hope of seeing supporters in person.[2]

## Final promotion

A "Last Call for GART 3: Irvine!" notice ran in the [[badlands-media|Badlands News Brief]] of January 8, 2024, confirming the January 11–14, 2024 dates and naming the Marriott Irvine Spectrum as the conference venue.[3] The notice again invited readers to buy in-person or virtual tickets, with all virtual purchases including access to recorded replays of every GART 3 panel during and following the event.[3]

## Opening weekend

A further "Last Call for GART 3: Irvine!" item ran on January 12, 2024, describing the event as a live, ticketed weekend combining in-person speakers, meet-and-greets, food and community for those who follow Badlands online.[4] Virtual tickets remained available for those unable to attend in person, streaming the entirety of GART 3 and including a direct link to a private Telegram chat where holders could interact with speakers and with hundreds of other in-person and virtual attendees.[4] All virtual purchases carried access to recorded replays of every panel during the event and for at least six months afterward.[4] The brief also pointed readers to a live stream of the GART 3 kickoff party hosted by [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on Rumble as a preview of the event's atmosphere.[4]

## Pre-conference call-in stream

On December 6, 2023, Badlands Media announced that several GART 3: Irvine speakers would host an exclusive live stream on Thursday, December 7 at 12pm ET.[5] The stream was described as a fun, informal hang-out session between ticket-buyers and speakers, and included a call-in element from the Irvine audience.[5] Access was restricted to purchasers of either an in-person or digital live stream ticket, who would receive the direct link by email on Thursday morning.[5] Badlands said it planned to run a couple more such streams leading up to the January conference, with all ticket-buyers receiving links to those as well.[5] Ticket-buyers unable to watch live could use the emailed link to view an exclusive replay at their convenience, and were urged to enter a regularly used email address when purchasing.[5]

## Ticket tiers and venue

In-person tickets included a Welcome Reception with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on Friday night, breakfast and lunch all weekend, and the opportunity to socialize with other attendees and show hosts throughout the weekend as the community framed the gathering as an effort to "level up in the [[information-war|Information War]]" together.[5] A VIP package was also offered, adding a separate Thursday night dinner and meet-and-greet with Badlands show hosts and personalities to open the GART weekend.[5] The conference was held at the Marriott Irvine Spectrum, with a group room-booking link provided.[5] Promotional copy joked that attendees might "trap" [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], [[just-human|Just Human]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[jaytriot|Jaytriot]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost]] into unofficial lobby or bar socials lasting until 3am, given sufficient whisky.[5]

## Expected speakers

The December 2023 promotion listed the expected speaker roster as [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], Kate Buckley, [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[just-human|Just Human]], [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], [[gmoney|GMoney]], [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]], [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of BPH]] and [[jaytriot|Jaytriot]].[5]
