# GART 3 (Irvine)

GART 3 was the third installment of the Great Awakening Round Table live event series produced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held January 11–14, 2024 in Irvine, California.[1]

## Format

The event was promoted as a live, ticketed gathering offering in-person speakers and meet-and-greets across the weekend, along with food and social programming.[1] Attendees could watch live, in-person versions of Badlands Media shows, participate in live question-and-answer sessions with Badlands panels, and meet show hosts in person.[1]

For those unable to attend in person, virtual tickets to stream the entirety of GART 3 were made available; these also included a direct link to a private Telegram chat where purchasers could interact with speakers and with hundreds of other in-person and virtual attendees.[1] All virtual ticket purchases included access to recorded replays of every GART 3 panel during the event and for at least six months afterward.[1]

The event was promoted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands News Brief]] of January 11, 2024 as a "last call" for ticket sales.[1]

## Announcement

Ticket sales for GART 3 in Irvine, California opened in September 2023, announced in the same Badlands News Brief that made replays of [[gart-2|GART 2]] — held in Cocoa Beach — available.[2] Badlands framed both events as part of the organization's continued evolution and expressed hope of seeing supporters in person.[2]

## Final promotion

A "Last Call for GART 3: Irvine!" notice ran in the [[badlands-media|Badlands News Brief]] of January 8, 2024, confirming the January 11–14, 2024 dates and naming the Marriott Irvine Spectrum as the conference venue.[3] The notice again invited readers to buy in-person or virtual tickets, with all virtual purchases including access to recorded replays of every GART 3 panel during and following the event.[3]
