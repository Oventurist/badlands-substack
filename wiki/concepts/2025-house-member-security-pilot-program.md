# 2025 House Member Security Pilot Program

The **2025 House member security pilot program** was an increase in security measures for members of the [[us-house-of-representatives|U.S. House of Representatives]], announced on Tuesday, July 22, 2025, to run as a pilot over the following two months.[1]

## Background and terms

The boost came as political violence and threats left lawmakers concerned while preparing to leave Washington for the August recess.[1] Among the changes was a doubling of the amount of funds congressmen may use to install security equipment at their personal residences, from $10,000 to $20,000.[1]

House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] said that in a few months leaders would "evaluate all the data points [of the pilot program], see how effective it was, how it was utilized, and then make decisions going forward."[1] The program was reported by The Hill and relayed by [[newsmax|Newsmax]].[1]
