# Trump Firing Squad Execution Proposal

The Trump firing squad execution proposal refers to reporting published in February 2023 by [[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]] claiming that former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had, in the run-up to the 2024 presidential campaign, repeatedly asked close associates what they thought of firing squads.[1] Three people said to be familiar with the situation described the question as more than idle curiosity.[1]

## Reported content

According to the sources cited in the report, Trump remained committed, if re-elected, to expanding the use of the federal death penalty and to bringing back execution methods that had been banned.[1] He was said to have discussed reviving death by firing squad and by hanging and, according to two of the sources, possibly even by guillotine, as well as group executions.[1] One source recounted that Trump had mused about televising footage of executions, including showing condemned prisoners in their final moments.[1] The report placed these discussions in the context of planned campaign rhetoric and policy desires, and of Trump's stated disdain for President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s approach to crime.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], contributor [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] summarised the story in a single word — "Panic."[1] The commentary argued that as opposing narratives collapsed, those responsible were beginning to grasp that talk by Trump and other leaders of crimes against humanity signalled the end of their position, and that with a Trump return to office in 2024 appearing inevitable they were panicking over what justice a second Trump presidency would bring.[1]
