# RINO

"RINO" — an acronym for "Republican In Name Only" — is a pejorative used within the American conservative movement to describe elected Republicans regarded as nominally partisan but substantively aligned with the bipartisan establishment.[1] In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary the label operates as a near-synonym for members of the [[uniparty|Uniparty]], the faction held to govern regardless of party branding.[1]

## Use in the 2023 speakership fight

The term featured heavily in Truth Community analysis of the [[2023-speaker-of-the-house-election|2023 Speaker of the House election]].[1] Assessing the proposal that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] be installed as Speaker, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that "the RINOs have the voting power to block it," and that unless the conflict had been "sheer kayfabe all along" it was difficult to imagine members who hate Trump's guts and were laughing at his prosecution voting to hand him the gavel.[1] He added a silver lining: such a vote would identify exactly who the real patriots in [[congress|Congress]] were, though the same information could be obtained simply by examining voting records.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the term similarly in dismissing the 2024 Republican primary field, describing Trump's rivals as "nothing but Uniparty shills & RINO cast-offs" whose challenge was beneath his notice.[1]
