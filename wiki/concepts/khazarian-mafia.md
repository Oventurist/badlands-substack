# Khazarian Mafia

**"Khazarian mafia"** is a term used in alternative-media literature to describe an alleged criminal power network descended from the ancient kingdom of [[khazaria|Khazaria]].

## Introduction of the term

The Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — Agents from Russia" states that publications such as [[veterans-today|Veterans Today]] and the writer [[benjamin-fulford|Ben Fulford]] have spotlighted the "Khazarian mafia" since at least 2015, and credits them with introducing the phrase into common conspiracy parlance.[1]

## The author's assessment

The author explicitly declines to vouch for either source, writing that he does not personally trust Veterans Today or Fulford, while maintaining that the term's diffusion is itself significant.[1] He suggests it is "almost as if a great deal of 'conspiracy' literature has been disseminated to guide the public's awakening" about the significance of that part of the world, and adds the caveat that "all professional-grade disinfo contains some truth."[1]

## Relation to the wolf cult thesis

Within the essay's framework, Khazaria and the [[koryos|wolf cult]] are held to be deeply connected, both originating in the region where the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]] is being fought.[1] The essay associates the same network with the entity [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] called a "ball of vampires," and with the labels Illuminati, NWO, Satanists, Luciferians and the Babylonian Brotherhood used in popular culture.[1]
