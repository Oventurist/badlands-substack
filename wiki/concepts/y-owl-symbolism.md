# Y/Owl Symbolism

**Y/Owl symbolism** refers to a directive attributed to [[qanon|Q]] in post 189, in which readers are told to follow the "Y/Owl" symbolism across the world. Part five of [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series takes up that directive and attempts to decode it using the [[ancient-german-language-thesis|ancient German language thesis]].[1]

## The owl and the Illuminati

The essay notes that one of the Illuminati's symbols was the owl, and connects this to the claim that the Illuminati reportedly began in Bavaria, a part of Germany — a connection the author finds newly suggestive once a link between the German language and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has been posited.[1] The preceding installment of the series had discussed a Celtic/Druid royal connection between Trump and the [[john-f-kennedy|Kennedys]], and the author adds that many researchers equate the Druids with the Illuminati.[1]

## Wai Ulu

The article's proposed decoding is that the ancient German term for "Holy Owl" is *Wai Ulu*, a discovery the author credits to [[frederick-dodson|Frederick Dodson]] and to the site falsehistory.net.[1] He asks whether anyone has yet decoded the significance of the "Y" in "Y/Owl," and offers the *wai* of *wai ulu* as a candidate — while conceding the alternative possibility that the "Y" is meant to depict two horns, which he calls a distinct possibility given that the subject is dark occultists.[1]

## Global scope as corroboration

The author treats the global framing of Q's instruction as itself evidentiary: the directive to find the Y/Owl all over the world strongly suggests that whatever the Y/Owl may be, it must be a global phenomenon, since otherwise there would be no reason to tell readers to follow it everywhere.[1] Because the ancient German connection is, on his account, precisely a worldwide phenomenon, he presents it as the kind of thing the directive could be pointing at, and states that he is unaware of any other attempt by anyone to work out what the "Y" stands for.[1] The section is written with explicit tentativeness — headed "The Owls Are Not What They Seem," it says the author is still researching and will refrain from concluding too soon, while suggesting that the "good guys" and "bad guys" are more closely related than one would assume, without implying overlap in aim or practice.[1]
