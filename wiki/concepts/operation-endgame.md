# Operation Endgame

Operation Endgame is the name given, in Badlands commentary, to an alleged
[[cia|CIA]]/[[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]/[[qatar|Qatari]]
partnership whose documents were leaked to the press in January 2024.[1]

## Alleged target and timeline

Badlands contributor
[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that
[[youssef-al-otaiba|Yousef Al Otaiba]], the
[[united-arab-emirates|UAE]] Ambassador to the United States, was the intended
target of the operation.[1] He placed its implementation in late 2017 or 2018
and treated it as part of the psychological operation surrounding the murder of
[[jamal-khashoggi|Jamal Khashoggi]], with the Khashoggi affair aimed at Saudi
Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and Operation Endgame aimed at Al Otaiba.[1]

The framing situates both operations within a broader contest in which
[[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], the UAE and the
[[trump-administration|Trump administration]] took on Qatar and the Muslim
Brotherhood — and, by extension, the CIA.[1]
