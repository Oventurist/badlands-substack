# 2024 Il-76 Shootdown Investigation

On January 24, 2024, a Russian Ilyushin Il-76M military transport aircraft carrying Ukrainian prisoners of war to a planned exchange was shot down, killing everyone aboard: 65 POWs, three Russian guards and six crew members.[1]

On June 25, 2024, the [[russian-investigative-committee|Russian Investigative Committee]] announced that senior military officials in Kiev were responsible, saying the aircraft had been brought down by a US-made Patriot interceptor missile.[1] The committee said it had gathered sufficient evidence to accuse Ukrainian military commanders and the leadership of the country's military intelligence directorate, the GUR, asserting that they "knew for certain" a prisoner swap was scheduled and "ordered the destruction of the plane" nonetheless.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the finding as another instance of what he described as US government war crimes against Russia, writing that Russian authorities had recovered fragments of both the aircraft and two missiles and determined through forensic analysis that the projectiles were MIM-104 [[patriot-missile-system|Patriot]] munitions of American manufacture.[1] He asked rhetorically whether the [[state-department|State Department]] intended to claim the Ukrainian military had shot down a prisoner plane carrying its own troops, and called the department "America's greatest liability" for giving the world's powers reason to go to war with the United States.[1]
