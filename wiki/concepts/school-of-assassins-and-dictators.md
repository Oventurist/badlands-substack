# School of Assassins and Dictators

"School of Assassins and Dictators" is an earlier article in Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s series on the [[shadow-government|shadow government]], referenced as background in his later piece "Illusions of Democracy."[1]

## Content as summarized

In that article, Lange described how [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] installed dictators throughout Central and South America, training those countries' militaries at the [[school-of-the-americas|School of the Americas]] to terrorize and murder their own people so that control of the governments could be maintained.[1]

## The Costa Rica thread

The article also singled out one country that did not fit the pattern. Unlike the other Central American states under CIA influence, [[costa-rica|Costa Rica]] was not ruled by a dictator or brutalized by its own government, and is still considered by its citizens one of the happiest places on earth — a republic similar in form to the United States.[1] Lange picks up that thread in "Illusions of Democracy," attributing the difference to the safeguards installed by [[jose-figueres-ferrer|José "Pepe" Figueres Ferrer]] after the [[costa-rican-civil-war|Costa Rican Civil War]].[1]

## Place in the series

The piece belongs to a broader sequence in which Lange argues a shadow government established by George H.W. Bush captured the executive branch, used "free trade" as cover to expand drug trafficking across the southern border, and used banking "deregulation" to loot the treasury and launder money through the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|Savings and Loan crisis]].[1]
