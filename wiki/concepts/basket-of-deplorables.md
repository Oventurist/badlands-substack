# Basket of Deplorables

The **"basket of deplorables"** was a phrase used by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] on 9 September 2016 during her presidential campaign against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] In the quoted remarks she said: "You know, to just be grossly generalistic, you could put half of Trump's supporters into what I call the basket of deplorables. Right? The racist, sexist, homophobic, xenophobic, Islamaphobic—you name it. And unfortunately there are people like that. And he has lifted them up."[1]

## Reappropriation by Trump supporters

Rather than functioning as an attack that stuck, the label was rapidly adopted as a badge of identity by Trump's base.[1] The day after the remark, [[donald-trump-jr|Don Jr]] posted "The Deplorables" meme, which featured Trump rendered as [[pepe-the-frog|Pepe the Frog]] among the campaign's figures.[1] On 10 October 2016 campaign aide [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] tweeted a message mentioning the [[trump-train|Trump Train]], quoting Trump's response to the deplorables remark and declaring "WE LOVE OUR DEPLORABLES!!!"[1]

In Badlands Media commentary the episode is read as a pivotal moment in the meme war of 2016: the Badlands Brief article "Pepe, the Trump Train, and Hillary's Automobile" is subtitled "The Deplorables Guide to the Orangest of Oranges" and treats the deplorables sequence, the minting of the [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]] token on the same date as Clinton's remark, and the Clinton campaign's subsequent website attack on Pepe as a single interlinked chain of events.[1]
