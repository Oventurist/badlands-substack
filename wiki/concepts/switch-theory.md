# Switch Theory

Switch theory is a Badlands Media interpretive framework, discussed for years in parallel with [[devolution]], holding that the policy machinery built under the Biden administration was in substance a continuation of programs initiated under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], set up so that Trump could later "flip the switch" and claim the completed transition.[1]

## Formulation

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] gave a compressed statement of the theory: the "Fake Biden Admin" did not stop the 'Good' Reset, it accelerated it, so that Trump gets to flip the switch to the Golden Age while the outgoing regime takes responsibility for the transition.[1]

## Application to the CHIPS program

The theory was invoked in September 2025 when the [[department-of-commerce|Commerce Department]] under [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] reclaimed the $7.4 billion semiconductor initiative run by [[natcast]], which Lutnick called a "semiconductor slush fund."[1] [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] argued that once one understands switch theory — and that the chips initiative was really Trump's to begin with — headlines about the administration seizing "Biden's" program become comical.[1]

## Relationship to shutdown framing

The same interpretive posture informs Badlands commentary on the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|October 2025 government shutdown]], in which Burning Bright argued that because the deep state and weaponized agencies are the declared target, shutdowns are not Damoclesian threats to the awakened but promises.[1]
