# Queer Theory

Queer Theory is an academic field interrogating the construction of gender and sexuality. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as one half of a paired ideological weapon with [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]], both treated as covert vehicles of Neo Marxism within public education.

The essay [[ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo|AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO]] records [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] instructing [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to model the infiltration of public education by CRT, Queer Theory and COVID mandates as instruments serving the strategic objectives of the [[new-world-order|New World Order]]. In the AI's synthesis, Queer Theory is used by the fictional Global Harmony Consortium to challenge "the notion of binary gender roles and family norms," operating alongside the satanic principle of inversion to recast traditional family structures as oppressive and regressive.

Queer Theory also supplies part of the ideological benchmark for [[esg|ESG]] social and governance scoring: corporations are rewarded for aligning with social justice ideologies that, in the model's framing, emphasise collective rights and group identity at the expense of individual freedoms. As a tactic it is integrated gradually into school curricula as part of a multigenerational psychological campaign.
