# Nuclear Scare Event

The "nuclear scare event" is a phrase drawn from [[qanon|Q]] postings, foretelling a nuclear-related crisis or near-miss as a marker of the late stage of an anticipated political conflict.[1]

In February 2026, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked the phrase in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] while analyzing Russian claims that Western states were weighing nuclear transfers to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and that a Ukrainian-linked plot to sabotage Black Sea gas pipelines had been uncovered. He argued that while President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] wages a narrative war against [[european-union|EU]] elites, President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] wages a kinetic one — and that because Putin is winning, EU elites are growing desperate enough to resort to terrorism or even a mass-casualty nuclear event.[1]

He characterized such an attack as a last resort that would cost EU elites their claimed moral high ground, and framed Western accusations against Putin as projection, comparing them to religious extremists accusing Iran's leadership of religious extremism. He concluded by anticipating the moment when Trump and Putin "join forces in the narrative."[1]
