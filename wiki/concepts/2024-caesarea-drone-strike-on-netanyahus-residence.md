# 2024 Caesarea Drone Strike on Netanyahu's Residence

On the morning of Saturday, October 19, 2024, a drone strike was launched toward the private residence of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] in the town of Caesarea, north of Tel Aviv, according to the prime minister's office.[1]

Netanyahu and his wife were not in the building at the time and no casualties were reported.[1] The [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] said the drone had crossed from Lebanon and "hit a structure in the area of Caesarea," roughly 50 miles from the northern border, adding that two additional drones were intercepted and that the incident was under review.[1]

In a post on X, Netanyahu blamed "Iran's proxy [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]]" and described the attack as an assassination attempt, warning that anyone who tries to harm Israeli citizens "will pay a heavy price."[1] He said, "The attempt by Iran's proxy Hezbollah to assassinate me and my wife today was a grave mistake," and added, "This will not deter me or the State of Israel from continuing our just war against our enemies in order to secure our future."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media's analysis argued that the alleged targeting of Netanyahu's home supplied "the gasoline Bibi has been looking for to pour on the bonfire of war," noting sardonically how such openings tend to work out in Netanyahu's favor.[1] The commentary contended that Netanyahu had opened the situation by assassinating the leaders of his declared enemies, and that with Vladimir Putin having drawn a red line, Netanyahu now believed he had justification to cross it.[1] The item was published alongside coverage of the [[2024-israel-iran-intelligence-leak|leak of US intelligence documents on Israel's attack preparations against Iran]].[1]
