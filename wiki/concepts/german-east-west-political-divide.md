# German East-West Political Divide

The **German east–west political divide** describes the finding, illustrated by a February 2024 map from the German poll aggregator Wahlkreisprognose, that party preference in Germany still tracks the boundary of the former Iron Curtain.[1]

## The Wahlkreisprognose map

The map colored Germany's Bundestag constituencies according to which party led in each.[1] In the territory of the former German Democratic Republic, [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative für Deutschland]] held a clear lead across almost the entire region, while the western part of the country was overwhelmingly dominated by the conservative Christian Democratic Union and its Bavarian sister party, the CSU.[1] The result was a country sharply divided along the old West Germany–East Germany line.[1]

In the map's color scheme, black represented the center-right CDU, blue the AfD, red the [[social-democratic-party-of-germany|Social Democratic Party of Germany]], and green the Greens, with fainter shades indicating a narrower lead in a constituency and darker shades a more dominant party position.[1]
