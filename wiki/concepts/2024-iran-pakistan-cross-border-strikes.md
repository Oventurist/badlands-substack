# 2024 Iran–Pakistan Cross-Border Strikes

The 2024 Iran–Pakistan cross-border strikes were an exchange of military attacks in mid-January 2024 in which Iran struck a Sunni militant group inside Pakistan and [[pakistan|Pakistan]] retaliated with drone and rocket attacks on alleged Iranian militant hideouts, killing at least nine people.[1]

## Sequence of events

Iran conducted a cross-border ballistic missile attack against a Sunni militant group it believed responsible for the Kerman city suicide bombings. Pakistan's leadership reacted with fury to what it called Iran's "unprovoked violation of its airspace" on Tuesday.[1]

In the early hours of Thursday, January 18, 2024, Pakistan's air force launched fresh retaliatory strikes against alleged Iranian militant hideouts. The attack killed at least nine people according to the Associated Press.[1]

## Pakistani framing

Pakistan's foreign ministry described the operation as "a series of highly coordinated and specifically targeted precision military strikes," adding that "this morning's action was taken in light of credible intelligence of impending large scale terrorist activities" and that it was "a manifestation of Pakistan's unflinching resolve to protect and defend its national security against all threats."[1]

Pakistan's military deployed "killer drones, rockets, loitering munitions and standoff weapons" — systems fired from a distance, indicating that Pakistani aircraft likely did not cross into Iranian airspace during the operation.[1]
