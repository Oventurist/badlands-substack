# Ten Points to True Election Integrity

The **Ten Points to True Election Integrity** is a reform platform that election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] has advanced for roughly three years as his desired end state for American election administration.[1]

In his February 13, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] guest essay applying [[smart-goal-setting|SMART goal setting]] to election reform, Keshel presented the Ten Points as the full, long-horizon objective while distinguishing it from what is immediately achievable.[1] He noted that he had also identified three key pillars for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to pursue in the near term, but that the complete system implied by the Ten Points "takes much more work to install."[1]

The distinction matters within his framework: under the "achievable" criterion of SMART, restraint is critical, and Keshel argued that attempting to install the entire program before the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]] would exceed the available time.[1] He presented the Ten Points in graphical form in the essay rather than enumerating them in prose.[1]
