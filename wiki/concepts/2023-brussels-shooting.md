# 2023 Brussels Shooting

The 2023 Brussels shooting was a mass shooting in central Brussels, Belgium, on the evening of Monday, October 16, 2023, in which two people were shot dead and others injured, with the perpetrator initially remaining at large, according to Belgian police.[1]

## Attack

Authorities confirmed that the shooting took place on Ieperlaan at around 7:15 p.m. local time, and that the two people killed were of Swedish nationality.[1] The victims were understood to be supporters of the Swedish national football team who were visiting the Belgian capital to attend a European Championship qualifying fixture between Belgium and Sweden at the Heysel Stadium that evening.[1]

Amateur footage circulating online showed the perpetrator dressed in a fluorescent orange jacket and wearing a white helmet, equipped with an assault rifle and rampaging through the city.[1] The suspect arrived at the scene on a scooter before opening fire in the street, causing pedestrians to flee into a nearby apartment building.[1] The attacker was reported to have claimed the killings were carried out to "avenge Muslims."[1]
