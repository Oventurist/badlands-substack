# Global NATO

"Global NATO" is the term used in the article to describe the transformation of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization from a Cold War defensive alliance into an aspiring global offensive structure.[1] The refusal of neoliberal politicians in positions of authority to abandon the policy of Global NATO and international unipolarism is identified as a principal reason why leading figures of the "free and democratic" west have become blind even to their own strategic interests, to the point of risking thermonuclear war.[1]

## Expansion

Even basic security guarantees given to Moscow at the end of the Cold War were abandoned: the promise by then-Secretary of State [[james-baker|James Baker]] not to move [[nato|NATO]] "one inch beyond its 1992 configuration" was increasingly disregarded as the alliance absorbed as many former Soviet nations as it could acquire.[1] [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]], as US Ambassador to NATO, oversaw the bloc's expansion from 16 to 24 nations by 2008, and under her lead Georgia's and Ukraine's aspirations to join were officially welcomed.[1]

## Consequences

President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] set firm red lines regarding NATO's continued encroachment on Russia in his 2007 Munich Security Conference speech.[1] The article notes that Putin had earlier made known his intention for Russia itself to participate in NATO, on the assumption that as long as figures who thought differently exercised power in western nations, the organization's destructive orientation could be neutralized.[1]

By the mid-2020s a comparable structure was taking shape in the Pacific — a "NATO of the Pacific" that some have dubbed the Quad — with Taiwan positioned as a potential flashpoint following the 2014 Sunflower Revolution, just as post-color-revolution Ukraine had been used as a flashpoint against Russia.[1] The article concludes that the insane policies of Global NATO can lead only toward thermonuclear war unless an engaged "other America" intervenes.[1]
