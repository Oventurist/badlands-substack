# Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias

The Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias is a federal body announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Thursday, February 6, 2025 and led by Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], created to investigate the alleged targeting of Christians by the United States government.[1]

## Announcement

Trump announced the task force while speaking at a pair of events in Washington surrounding the [[national-prayer-breakfast|National Prayer Breakfast]].[1] He said the body would be directed to "immediately halt all forms of anti-Christian targeting and discrimination within the federal government," naming the Department of Justice, the IRS and the FBI among the agencies involved.[1] Trump added that Bondi would also work to "fully prosecute anti-Christian violence and vandalism in our society and to move heaven and earth to defend the rights of Christians and religious believers nationwide."[1]

Hours after the two events, Trump signed an executive order directing the new task force to identify unlawful policies, practices or conduct across all executive departments and agencies and to recommend further presidential or legislative action.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that "a lot of the action against Christians over the past four years came through the FBI," and connected the task force to the stalled confirmation of [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] as FBI director, noting that the Senate Judiciary Committee had punted its vote to the following Wednesday.[1] She also flagged the confirmation of [[russ-vought|Russell Vought]] as OMB Director in the same news cycle.[1]
