# Hand Counting

**Hand counting** is the manual tabulation of paper ballots, advanced by election-integrity advocates as an alternative to electronic voting and tabulation systems.[1]

Election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] regards hand counting as desirable but logistically distant. Writing in February 2025, he noted that while some counties were working toward a paper-ballot system, key counties in key states — "the Maricopas and Fultons of the world," referring to [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] and [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] — remained far from doing so.[1] Converting them would require replacing existing systems root and branch with a tested and proven hand-counting system operated by efficient and precise personnel.[1]

Keshel pointed to the [[echo-65-method|Echo 65 Method]] as what he called a game-changing hand-count method, but argued that with the next national election only twenty-one months away, there was not enough time to change how national elections are run.[1] This time constraint is the core of his argument for prioritizing [[voter-registration|voter registration]] reform over machine replacement.[1]
