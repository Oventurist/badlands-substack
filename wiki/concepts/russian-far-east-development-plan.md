# Russian Far East Development Plan

The Far East development plan is President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s designation of Russia's Far East as a "21st century national priority."[1] It is presented in Badlands Media commentary as a leading practical example of [[open-systems-vs-closed-systems|open-system]] economics.[1]

## Scope

The program covers the development of new cities, mining, transport corridors and the oil and natural gas resources of Russia's Far East, described as one of the greatest opportunities for economic investment of the coming century.[1] It already features an array of partners from China, Japan, South Korea, India and other APEC nations.[1]

## Invitation to the United States

Putin's 2018 proposal that the USA join the project as an exercise in [[win-win-cooperation|win-win cooperation]] is characterized as important not only for building trust and creating business opportunities but for re-establishing the lost art of long-term thinking, and for linking Western business into the Asia-Pacific development process being shaped by China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]].[1]

## India and the BRI schism

Although tensions have been inflamed to schism China and India from cooperating directly on the BRI, India's embrace of Russian Far East development investments is described as creating a non-linear flank capable of bringing the two Asian giants into harmony.[1] The plan is treated as part and parcel of the wider Arctic development dynamic including the [[polar-silk-road|Polar Silk Road]], the [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait crossing]] and the merger of the BRI with the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]].[1]
