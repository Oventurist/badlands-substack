# Vault 7

**Vault 7** is the codename for the series of documents that [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] began publishing in March 2017, described as the largest leak of [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] documents in history.[1]

## Contents

The material detailed the agency's hacking capabilities and the software tools it used to break into smartphones, computers and other internet-connected devices.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands reading

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats Vault 7 as the culminating item in a sequence of releases — following the [[dnc-email-leak|DNC emails]] and the [[podesta-emails|Podesta emails]] — that [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] was able to publish from inside the [[ecuadorian-embassy-in-london|Ecuadorian Embassy in London]] while his location was known to all parties.[1] Carlson finds it implausible that an agency documented by Vault 7 itself as able to compromise virtually any connected device would have been powerless to prevent those publications, and uses the point to argue that Assange's role in the wider information war has not been satisfactorily explained.[1]
