# Drag Queen Story Hour

Drag Queen Story Hour refers to public library and school events at which drag performers read to children. In [[canada|Canada]] such events became a flashpoint in the conflict over [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]] and [[parental-rights|parental rights]], and were the subject of proposed protest-restriction legislation in [[ontario|Ontario]].[1]

## The Ontario NDP proposal

On Tuesday, April 4, 2023, the [[ontario-new-democratic-party|Ontario NDP]] introduced a law that would make demonstrations during drag queen story hour illegal, framed as creating public safety zones for drag performances.[1] A February 2024 Badlands Media essay by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued the announcement's language carefully dehumanized every protester while ignoring the actual concern of parents — the exposure of children to acts and content inappropriate for their developmental stage.[1]

## Parental objections

The essay states that some parents object to instilling gender ideology in their children, including telling them they might be "trapped in the wrong body" or that watching a man in a dress will help them sort it out.[1] It also notes that some parents are aware drag has a history steeped in adult sexual entertainment, and object to performers teaching children that being sexual in front of adults can win attention and sometimes money.[1] Deschamps rejects the characterization of such objections as hatred or bigotry, arguing that opposition to the "2SLGBTQ+" framing is unrelated to prejudice but that decades of [[social-engineering|social engineering]] have made many Canadians accept the accusation.[1]

## Political framing

The article treats the measure as political theatre giving the impression that the NDP was pushing back against conservatives, and rejects any framing of the dispute as NDP versus Conservative or Conservative versus Liberal, since all three parties contributed to Canada's gender-identity laws — making it, in the author's terms, a [[uniparty|Uniparty]] problem.[1]
