# Compliance Ladder

The **compliance ladder** is a term from marketing and persuasion, adopted in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, for the incremental process by which people keep changing themselves to conform to shifting social norms.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Mechanism

[[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] observes that people generally do not change unless they are in pain in some sense, and that the pain of social ostracization alone is often sufficient to drive continual self-modification — a process described as very hypnotic to many people, though not to all.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Failure condition

The essay argues that the spell breaks when the social environment degrades past the point where it can deliver coherent instructions to its members about how to act and think: a ladder must be functioning to be climbed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The bar for coherence is said to be very low for many people, but some minimum is necessary, because one literally cannot walk forwards and backwards at the same time — an instruction that cannot be executed is not a program.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) When a program ceases to be executable it "short circuits," and for the person undergoing that breakdown the experience can be primordially terrifying.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) This is offered as the mechanism by which incoherence in coverage of the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]] is dissolving [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] conditioning across both political camps.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)
