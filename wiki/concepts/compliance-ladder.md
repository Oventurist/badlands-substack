# Compliance Ladder

The "compliance ladder" is a term from marketing and persuasion for the process by which people are led to change themselves incrementally in order to conform to shifting social norms.[1]

## Mechanism

[[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] observes that people generally do not change unless they are in pain of some kind, and that the pain of social ostracization alone will often cause a person to keep modifying themselves as the norms move — a process described as very hypnotic to a lot of people, though not to all.[1]

The ladder requires a functioning social environment to operate. The spell breaks, on this account, when the environment breaks down past the point where it can deliver coherent instructions to its members about how to act and think.[1] The bar for coherence is very low for many people, but a minimum is necessary, because one literally cannot walk forwards and backwards at the same time; as long as a program is *executable*, that is what counts.[1] When a program ceases to be executable it short-circuits, and for the person undergoing the breakdown the experience can be primordially terrifying — a condition the essay links to the "pain incoming" warnings associated with [[q|Q]] and to the collapse of [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] conditioning under the pressure of the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel-Palestine conflict]].[1]
