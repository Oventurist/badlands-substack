# Zangezur Corridor

The Zangezur corridor is a proposed transport route linking mainland [[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]] to its exclave of Nakhichevan through a narrow strip of land in southern [[armenia|Armenia]] along the border with [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## Washington agreement (August 2025)

On Friday, August 8, 2025, Armenian Prime Minister [[nikol-pashinyan|Nikol Pashinyan]], Azerbaijani President [[ilham-aliyev|Ilham Aliyev]], and US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed an agreement in Washington to open the corridor.[1] Under the agreement, the United States would manage the corridor under Armenian sovereignty via a 99-year land lease, subletting it to a consortium for construction and operations.[1]

## Iranian objections

[[iran|Iran]] condemned the plan, warning that it posed a threat to regional stability and would undermine Tehran's security interests.[1] Iranian officials said they welcomed peace agreements between Yerevan and Baku but "vehemently" opposed placing the corridor under US control.[1]

## Badlands analysis

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the peace deal likely had "something to do with severing the geographical connection between Russia and Iran."[1] He noted that Pashinyan and Aliyev had both worked to entrap [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in a quagmire in 2020, when Russia deployed peacekeeping troops to the same corridor to end violence between the two countries, and argued that NATO had spent years trying to pull the Caucasus into Europe's orbit via Turkey for the same reason it has pressed on Ukrainian soil — because the region sits on Russia's doorstep.[1] The corridor, he added, is also Iran's doorstep.[1]

The commentary held that the optics of US contractors on the Iranian–Azerbaijani frontier had fueled a narrative that the corridor would become a gateway for offensive action against Iran by Israel and NATO, though it observed that Azerbaijan had allegedly already been used by the IDF to launch drone strikes on Tehran, making the Armenia scenario feel redundant.[1] The contributor said the behavior of Pashinyan and Aliyev after the [[2025-trump-putin-alaska-summit|Trump–Putin Alaska summit]] would be revealing.[1]
