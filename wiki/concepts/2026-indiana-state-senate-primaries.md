# 2026 Indiana State Senate Primaries

In Indiana's primary elections held Tuesday, May 5, 2026, candidates endorsed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] defeated at least five incumbent Republican state senators, according to results reported by multiple outlets.[1]

## Redistricting origins

The primaries followed a dispute inside the Indiana Republican Party over a Trump-backed proposal to redraw the state's congressional districts during the 2025 legislative session.[1] Several Republican state senators opposed the mid-decade redistricting effort, and Trump subsequently endorsed challengers against many of those lawmakers.[1] The contests therefore functioned as a direct referendum on the failed redistricting push and on Trump's continuing hold over Republican primary voters ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].[1]

## Results

The Wall Street Journal reported that Trump-backed challengers Blake Fiechter, Tracey Powell, Michelle Davis, Brian Schmutzler and Trevor De Vries won their Republican primaries, while Trump-endorsed candidate Jeff Ellington won an open-seat race.[1] Several reports described the Indiana results as a test — and a demonstration — of the strength of Trump's support within the GOP, and connected them to the broader [[2026-redistricting-battle|2026 redistricting battle]].[1]
