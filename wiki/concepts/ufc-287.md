# UFC 287

UFC 287 was a mixed martial arts event held by the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]] at the Kaseya Center in Miami, Florida, on April 8, 2023, described in coverage as "the hottest ticket in Miami."[1]

The card's co-main event featured Cuban-American welterweight [[jorge-masvidal|Jorge Masvidal]] against Gilbert Burns.[1] Former president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attended the event with one of the best seats in the house, and a photo was tweeted showing him ringside next to UFC president [[dana-white|Dana White]].[1]

## Political significance

Commentary from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] emphasized the reception Trump received: he "got a deafening pop on the PPV broadcast" in front of the largely Hispanic audience assembled to watch Masvidal, while ESPN "wouldn't touch him during the prelims."[1] Burning Bright argued that such appearances in the "wild" of the American public demonstrate the continued, growing and inevitable power of the [[america-first-movement|America First movement]].[1]
