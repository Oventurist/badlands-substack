# Narrative Whiplash

Narrative whiplash is a term used in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] analysis to describe the disorientation produced when the dominant media storyline reverses or is abruptly replaced, forcing audiences to adopt positions incompatible with the ones they held only weeks earlier.[1]

## Formulation

The concept was elaborated in the November 7, 2023 Badlands News Brief by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who argued that the tension gripping the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] was "a feature of 'The Plan,' not a bug."[1] In that framing, the War of Stories was breaking through walls of cognitive dissonance that had stood for years, and the mechanism doing the breaking was the visible collision of incompatible narratives.[1]

Three concurrent reversals were offered as illustration. American conservatives were being reminded how quickly and easily they had been drawn into the original Forever War in the Middle East by emotional neoconservative appeals.[1] American liberals were being labeled antisemites by their own mainstream media and establishment institutions for taking the Palestinian side of the world's most contentious sociopolitical and religious divide.[1] And both camps were watching a [[narrative-convergence|Narrative Convergence]] in which the Ukrainian proxy war ran out of steam while the [[uniparty|Uniparty]] attempted to substitute a new conflict in its place.[1]

## Relation to the peacemaker frame

Burning Bright concluded that the net effect of this whiplash was to position [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] simultaneously as the truth-teller and as "the Peacemaker in waiting," a reading consistent with the broader [[peacemaker-theory|peacemaker theory]] advanced in Badlands commentary.[1] In the same Brief the author applied a related lens to Russia coverage, arguing that [[overtons-goalpost|Overton's Goalpost]] was reaching its endpoint in Western globalist media as [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] pressed the point that Western nations were "changing their tune" on defeating Russia.[1] There the whiplash was described as escalating from a "Crisis Cascade" into a "Crisis Convergence," through which the public was said to be learning about both the corruption and the inherent weakness of the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
