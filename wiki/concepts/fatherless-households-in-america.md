# Fatherless Households in America

The prevalence of **fatherless households in America** is invoked in Badlands Media commentary as a structural vulnerability that makes young Americans unusually susceptible to political and cultural propaganda.[1]

## Statistics cited

In a November 2023 article, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cited figures indicating that 3% of Chinese families and 4% of Indian families lack a father in the home, against 23% of American families — six to seven times the rate of either comparison country.[1] Carlson presented the disparity without a named source, describing it as a statistic he had recently encountered.[1]

## The argument

Carlson's use of the statistic was to support his claim that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] served as a surrogate father figure for a generation of young Americans who lacked one at home.[1] In his framing, Obama was not physically present in their homes but appeared on television every night, and unlike an absent or abusive father would never get drunk, ignore them, miss a school event, or leave — presenting, in the eyes of many, as the perfect dad: calm, cool and caring.[1]

The argument extends into his broader [[obama-youth|Obama Youth]] thesis: that children lacking proper role models and parental instruction can be made to believe almost anything, from being born in the wrong body to believing climate change will render the world uninhabitable within a decade, and that even children with good parents are still affected by constant propaganda and indoctrination.[1] Carlson compared this dynamic to the [[hitler-youth|Hitler Youth]], whose purpose he described as stealing a generation away from its parents and grandparents.[1]
