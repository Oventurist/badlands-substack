# Depopulation Agenda

The "depopulation agenda" is the term used by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] for what he presents as a continuous elite program to reduce human population and industrial capacity under ecological pretexts, running from the [[malthusianism|Malthusian]] revival of 1968–1972 through the [[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]] of the present day.[1]

## Ideological architecture

In Ehret's account the agenda required both a governing doctrine and a religion. The doctrine was "The End of History," premised on the writings of [[francis-fukuyama|Francis Fukuyama]] and anticipated by [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]], under which the nation state ceased to be the principal creative force and international banks and multi-national corporations planned in terms far in advance of it.[1] The religion was the [[gaia-cult|Gaia cult]], a revival of ancient Earth Mother worship given a scientific veneer through terms like "reducing carbon footprints," "sustainable development," "adapting to carrying capacity" and "stopping global warming."[1]

## Institutional vehicles

Ehret names the UN's Millennial Development Goals, [[agenda-21|Agenda 21]] (later renamed [[agenda-2030|Agenda 2030]]) and the [[earth-charter|Earth Charter]] — overseen by [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]], [[steven-rockefeller|Steven Rockefeller]] and [[mikhail-gorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]] — as the instruments that converted fringe Gaia concepts into international legal doctrine.[1] Nation states were increasingly expected to act only in conformity with un-elected and often anonymous boards of [[ipcc|IPCC]] experts demanding drastic cuts to living standards.[1] Eco-warrior central bankers such as [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] and [[mario-draghi|Mario Draghi]] are said to have begun rewiring the world economy around total depopulation through Green New Deals and decarbonization, ignoring that CO2 is plant food and that the carbon molecule is the driving force behind life on earth.[1]

## Historical origins

The agenda's origins are traced to the vacuum left by the elimination of pro-development leaders such as [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], Enrico Mattei, [[charles-de-gaulle|Charles de Gaulle]], [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Bobby Kennedy]], after which establishment figures ushered in a paradigm shift toward "conservationism" and rejected the industrial growth ethic of western civilization.[1] This answered the challenge posed by [[eugenics|Eugenics]] Society president and [[unesco|UNESCO]] founder [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]], who wrote in 1946 that political unification in some sort of world government would be required and that UNESCO should ensure the eugenic problem was examined so that "much that now is unthinkable may at least become thinkable."[1] Institutionally the program was carried by the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]], the [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Nature Trust]] and the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]], and codified in [[limits-to-growth|*Limits to Growth*]].[1]

## Opposition

Ehret argues the agenda cracked in 2013, when China and Russia asserted sovereign power against the financier oligarchy of the City of London and Wall Street and launched the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], integrated with the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]] and [[polar-silk-road|Polar Silk Road]], alongside the [[brics|BRICS]], the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]] and the [[g20|G20]].[1] Within the United States the MAGA movement is presented as a parallel rejection of eco-genocide, while [[lyndon-larouche|Lyndon LaRouche]]'s 1982 *There Are No Limits to Growth* supplied the earlier theoretical refutation.[1]

## Depopulation and COVID-19 origins

In "The Wuhan Lab Leak Hoax," [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] makes depopulation the first of five facts weighing against the [[lab-leak-theory|Wuhan lab leak hypothesis]], describing it as a driving factor behind unipolar policy today just as it was during the Second World War era, when the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]], the Macy Foundation, the City of London and Wall Street backed both the rise of fascism as an economic solution to the Depression and [[eugenics|eugenics]] as the governing religion of a new scientific priesthood.[2]

He argues the agenda now masquerades behind a [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] vocabulary of "[[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]]," "decarbonized economies" and "[[great-reset|Great Resets]]," with two primary targets: the institution of the sovereign nation state, and the world's "overpopulated zones," a category that has included both China and the USA for decades.[2] The doctrinal pivot is located in [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger's]] 1974 [[nssm-200|NSSM-200]] report, which conditioned development, food and financial assistance on recipient states' fertility-reduction measures and contemplated mandatory programmes.[2]

On this reading China is an implausible author of a depopulation-aligned pandemic, since it has reversed course — raising the [[one-child-policy|one-child]] limit to two in 2015, with Bank of China economists urging total elimination of birth limits — while extending long-term investment and advanced technology at home and abroad through the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]].[2]
