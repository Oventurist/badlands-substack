# Walmart DEI Rollback

The Walmart DEI rollback was the November 2024 decision by [[walmart|Walmart]], the world's largest retailer, to phase out several diversity, equity and inclusion initiatives in its workplaces amid escalating attacks on such programs from conservatives.[1]

## Measures

Among several sweeping changes, the company said it would no longer consider race and gender as a means to improve diversity when making offers to suppliers.[1] It would stop offering DEI trainings through the Racial Equity Institute, a consultancy specializing in improving corporate diversity and equity, and would not renew a racial equity center it had established through a five-year commitment to "address the root causes of gaps in outcomes experienced by Black and African American people in education, health, finance and criminal justice systems."[1] The retailer would also remove LGBTQ+ merchandise from its stores.[1]

## Company statement and origins

Walmart told The Guardian that its purpose, "to help people save money and live better, has been at our core since our founding 62 years ago," adding that it can deliver on that purpose "because we are willing to change alongside our associates and customers who represent all of America," and that it wants "to be a Walmart for everyone."[1]

The decision was first announced by the conservative pundit [[robby-starbuck|Robby Starbuck]], who posted on X that "Walmart is ending their woke policies" and continued to attack DEI efforts as part of an ongoing campaign.[1]
