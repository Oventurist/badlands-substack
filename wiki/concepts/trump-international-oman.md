# Trump International Oman

**Trump International Oman** is a $500 million luxury mixed-use development located within the AIDA project near Muscat, Oman, built by [[dar-global|Dar Global]] under a licensing and management agreement with [[trump-organization|The Trump Organization]].[1] AIDA is described as one of the largest premium mixed-use real estate projects in the world.[1] The development's projected opening date is December 2028.[1]

## Origins and terms

The project stems from a licensing and management deal signed between the Trump Organization and [[dar-al-arkan|Dar Al Arkan]] in 2022 and announced in November 2022.[1] Under the arrangement, the Trump Organization and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] receive significant fees for use of the Trump brand name plus additional fees to manage the hotel, golf course, and luxury villas to be developed and launched over the following decade; exact terms have not been made public.[1] Trump's 2023 financial disclosure covering 2022 showed $5.35 million in licensing fees earned from the Oman golf project.[1] The Trump Organization team working on the project is led by [[eric-trump|Eric Trump]].[1]

## Scope and expansion

In March 2024, Eric Trump and Dar Global released renderings of the villas planned for Phase One, citing a project cost of $200 million; the July 2024 launch announcement carried a $500 million figure, suggesting the project had roughly doubled in size.[1] Around the same time, [[donald-trump-jr|Donald Trump Jr.]] drew media scrutiny for a Middle East trip and social-media posts announcing the 2028 opening of the villa community and golf course.[1]

## Commentary

Critics quoted in the Badlands brief argued that the Trump Organization continued to profit from foreign companies while Trump mixed business and political interests during a presidential campaign.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] instead framed the Oman project and the simultaneous [[trump-tower-jeddah|Trump Tower Jeddah]] announcement as evidence of deepening Gulf–American commercial integration, joking that Trump would be called "Trump of Arabia."[1]
