# FACE Act

The Freedom of Access to Clinic Entrances Act, commonly known as the FACE Act, is a federal statute that criminalizes the use of force, threats or physical obstruction to interfere with people seeking or providing reproductive health services and, in a parallel provision, with people exercising the right of religious freedom at a place of worship. The latter provision — protecting houses of worship from interference — is the aspect invoked in the Badlands corpus.

According to the Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026, Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]] identified the FACE Act, alongside the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]], as a statute potentially violated when left-wing activists stormed [[cities-church|Cities Church]] in St. Paul, Minnesota, during a Sunday service. Dhillon stated that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] would take a zero-tolerance approach, that disrupting religious services is not protected speech, and that the Civil Rights Division and the [[fbi|FBI]] were investigating. Former CNN host [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] was placed "on notice" for possible federal charges arising from the incident.
