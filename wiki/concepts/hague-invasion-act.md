# Hague Invasion Act

The "Hague Invasion Act" is the popular name for the U.S. legislation, enacted in 2002, authorizing measures — including the use of force — to free American or allied personnel held by the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] in The Hague.[1] It is the standard shorthand for the American posture of non-submission to ICC jurisdiction.

## Invocation in 2024

The statute was invoked rhetorically in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of May 23, 2024, which asked whether Israel would "eventually come up with its own Hague Invasion Act" after the ICC Prosecutor sought arrest warrants against Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] alongside [[hamas|Hamas]] leaders.[1]

The question was sharpened by [[european-union|EU]] foreign policy chief [[josep-borrell|Josep Borrell]]'s insistence that all states which have ratified the [[rome-statute|Rome Statute]] are legally bound to execute the court's decisions, meaning Netanyahu or Gallant would face arrest if they traveled to a European country.[1] Netanyahu subsequently issued a warning to U.S. leaders over the warrants, telling them "you're next."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the episode as an instance of globalist institutions being weaponized against their own architects, arguing that oligarchs would now be forced to advocate for the delegitimization of institutions such as the ICC rather than risk sharing Netanyahu's status as a global pariah.[1]
