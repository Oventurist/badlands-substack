# Cognition Cognos Support

**Cognition Cognos Support** is the parent contract under which the [[red-dawn-iii|Red Dawn III]] delivery order to [[icr-inc|ICR, INC.]] was issued.[1] Parent/child contract structures of this kind are commonly used to break large programs of work into manageable projects and subprojects, which is what appears to be occurring here.[1]

## Classification

The parent contract carries the same North American Industry Classification System coding as its child — 54 (Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services), 5415 (Computer Systems Design and Related Services) and 541511 (Custom Computer Programming Services).[1] Its Product or Service Code differs: AC6, research and development on defense system electronics and communication equipment, at the AC61 basic research level, whereas Red Dawn III is coded AC3/AC33 for advanced development on shipboard defense systems.[1]

## Interpretation

The article argues that the program name refers to [[ibm-cognos-analytics|Cognos]], the business-intelligence and analytics product line sold by [[ibm|IBM]].[1] IBM markets Cognos Analytics as an AI-powered platform whose natural-language assistant builds data visualisations on request and which can forecast outcomes, predict results and explain why they may occur.[1] From this, the article concludes that Red Dawn III sits inside a larger business-intelligence effort in which the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] applies IBM artificial-intelligence tooling to research on electronics and communication equipment for defense systems, with the child project delivering custom programming for ships' defense systems.[1]

Executives listed on the Cognition Cognos Support contract differ from those on Red Dawn III, with the exception of Robert T. Dunn, whose compensation figures are not identical between the two awards.[1]
