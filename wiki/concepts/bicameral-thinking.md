# Bicameral Thinking

**Bicameral Thinking** is an analytical method described by the Badlands Media and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] contributor of the same name, used to interpret narratives whose true intent is deliberately obscured in what the author calls the "Strange War."[1] The approach involves holding two readings of an event simultaneously: the surface reading advanced by the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]], and the alternative reading held by dissident analysts, then following each to its logical conclusions.[1]

## Application to China and Taiwan

Applying the method to the [[china|China]]–[[taiwan|Taiwan]] question in October 2022, the author first traced the establishment reading to its end point: tensions going "hot" around [[taiwan-strait|the Taiwan Strait]] would provide a convenient excuse for the United States to enter another proxy war, distracting Americans from domestic controversies and crises while propping up a [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]] said to be bleeding profits after [[doha-agreement|the Doha Agreement]] was fulfilled by the [[biden-administration|Biden administration]] in the wake of the Kabul withdrawal.[1]

The author then set that reading against a second one, arguing that patriots in the west along with allied sovereigns in the east had engineered a "Reverse Hegelian Dialectic" on the controllers, in which the narratives those controllers seeded ultimately undermine their own ends and their own paradigm.[1] Bicameral Thinking is thus presented not as a choice between the two readings but as a discipline of reasoning through both, a companion technique to the author's treatment of [[narrative-whiplash|Narrative Whiplash]] and the [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian dialectic]].[1]
