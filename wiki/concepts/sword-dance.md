# Sword Dance

The "Sword Dance" refers to the ceremonial ardah performed during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s May 2017 visit to [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], in which the American president joined Saudi royals in the traditional dance.[1]

## Significance in the movement

The article calls it "infamous" and describes it as an event that became iconic within the movement, analyzed ad nauseam by its most brilliant minds and spotlighted by "the most notorious Anon of them all" — a reference to [[qanon|Q]].[1] The mainstream media reaction at the time was apoplectic, which the author recalls reading with satisfaction from a beach in the Dominican Republic in the days afterward, a week before his wedding.[1] For him the event confirmed his instinct to support Trump from the moment he descended the escalator, and it symbolized a sense that America was back after the Obama and Bush years.[1]

Months later, when an anonymous poster appeared on 4chan following the Las Vegas shooting, it was the cryptic recounting of the Sword Dance in Saudi Arabia — rather than prophecies of arrests of political villains — that captured the author's attention and drew him into following the drops, which soon focused heavily on "SA."[1] The Sword Dance thus functions in the article as the emblem of the convergence between Trump and [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], two figures the author describes as warriors on separate quests to slay the same dragon.[1]
