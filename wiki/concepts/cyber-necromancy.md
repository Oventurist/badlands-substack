# Cyber-Necromancy

**Cyber-necromancy** is a term coined by the pseudonymous [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] in Part 8 of [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] series, published August 20, 2024, to describe what the author presents as an occult practice of controlling living beings through a combination of "ghost energy" and malicious bots.[1]

## Definition

Classical necromancy is characterized in the article as the practice of controlling the living by controlling the dead.[1] Cyber-necromancy is offered as an extension of that theme: it is defined not merely as the control of the living via the dead, but as "the thwarting of all that is *telic* (living) by any and all *anti-telic* ('dead') means."[1] Its purpose, on the author's account, is the reduction of unique souls into replaceable, mindless automatons — the creation of [[zombification|zombies]] and, in the essay's phrasing, the triumph of death over life.[1]

## Mechanism

The essay argues that a false impression of current events is engineered into mass consciousness through the simultaneous manipulation of spiritual energy and of cyberspace, so that what people see on a screen confirms what the author says is whispered to them by negative spiritual beings.[1] Both the malware bots of the [[dead-internet-theory|dead internet]] and what the author calls "disgruntled ghosts" are described as running nonstop, highly stereotyped "attack scripts."[1] The theoretical underpinning is drawn from [[voodoo|voodoo]] and dark occultism, in which words and symbols are treated as conduits of power; on that view it matters occultly that the phrase "dead internet" has caught on in popular usage, because a *dead* internet is held to influence *living* beings.[1]

## Relation to the Werewolf series

The author places cyber-necromancy within the wider argument of the Werewolf series that werewolf, vampire and zombie symbolism all point to a single cult said to run the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1] Part 3 of the series had argued that the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult and similar lineages are intentionally trying to manifest hell on Earth; Part 8 proposes that one of the key methods for doing so is the manufacture of zombies through rituals, robots and ghosts used in combination — that is, cyber-necromancy.[1] The article states that the author observed a major uptick in mass zombification efforts following the assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], describing it as a possible last-ditch effort by the cabal to retain control over the minds of the masses.[1]

## Countermeasures

The essay closes by arguing that mass zombification is not inevitable, and that thwarting it requires people to stop agreeing, in word or in deed, that they are replaceable cogs in someone else's machine, and instead to discover and develop what makes them unique.[1] It contends that [[qanon|Q]]'s insistence that followers grow into their highest potential as independent researchers stands as a direct impediment to the zombification process, and that the outcome the occultists least want is people linking up to build new societies founded on the principles of the [[declaration-of-independence|Declaration of Independence]].[1] The article also warns of the atrophy of natural faculties through technological dependence, citing loss of navigational ability among GPS users as an analogue for what it predicts will happen to the creative faculties of heavy AI users, and cautions that "almost all of mankind's would-be enslavers have presented themselves as 'liberators' and 'helpers' to the masses."[1]
