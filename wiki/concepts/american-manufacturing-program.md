# American Manufacturing Program

The American Manufacturing Program (AMP) is an initiative announced by [[apple|Apple]] in August 2025 dedicated to bringing more of the company's supply chain and advanced manufacturing into the United States.[1] Through AMP, Apple said it would increase its investment across America and incentivize global companies to manufacture more critical components domestically.[1]

## $600 billion commitment

AMP was unveiled alongside a new $100 billion commitment to America, an acceleration that brought Apple's total planned US investment to $600 billion over four years.[1] Chief executive [[tim-cook|Tim Cook]] said the program included new and expanded work with ten companies across America that produce components used in Apple products sold worldwide, and thanked the President for his support.[1]

## Coverage and commentary

Badlands Media covered the announcement live, and contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that legacy outlets deliberately led with the $100 billion figure rather than the $600 billion total in order to minimize the impact of Apple's expanded commitment.[1] She characterized this as part of a routine pattern of media deception intended to slow public recognition of the shift underway.[1]
