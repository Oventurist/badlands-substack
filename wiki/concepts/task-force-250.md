# Task Force 250

Task Force 250 is a body created by executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 29, 2025, to organize the celebration of the 250th anniversary of American independence, which falls on July 4, 2026.[1]

## Mandate

Under the order, the commission is charged with creating a "grand celebration of the semiquincentennial of the ratification of the Declaration of Independence," with the stated aim of providing "a grand celebration worthy of the momentous occasion of the 250th anniversary of American independence."[1] According to Fox News Digital, the commission is modeled on the bicentennial celebration of 1976.[1] The Daily Signal framed the anniversary as a symbolic occasion to declare that the "1619 Project" era of reframing American history as a long tale of oppression was over, and argued that Trump's move ensured anti-American activists would not have the last word.[1]

## Accompanying provisions

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] summarized the executive order as doing three things: creating the task force to organize a large 250th birthday celebration; reinstating the National Garden of American Heroes executive orders from 2019 and 2020; and reinstating Executive Order 13933, "Protecting American Monuments, Memorials, and Statues and Combatting Recent Criminal Violence."[1] He noted that the party-planning apparatus would be housed in and funded by the [[pentagon|Department of Defense]].[1]
