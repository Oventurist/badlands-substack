# Palm Springs Fertility Clinic Bombing

The Palm Springs fertility clinic bombing was an explosion at American Reproductive Centers, an IVF clinic in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday, May 17, 2025.[1]

## The attack

FBI investigators believe Guy Edward Bartkus, 25, packed his 2010 silver Ford Fusion with explosives before making the hour-long drive from Twentynine Palms to Palm Springs, where he intentionally parked outside the clinic.[1] The bombing triggered a massive investigation that sprawled across Southern California, with activity continuing at the suspect's Twentynine Palms home.[1] Bartkus is believed to have died in the incident.[1]

## Suspect and motive

Investigators said they were still working through a series of Bartkus's online posts and writings, which appear to indicate anti-natalist beliefs — the view that people should not continue to have children.[1]

Bartkus's father, Richard Bartkus, spoke to CBS News Los Angeles, saying he had not spoken to his son in more than a decade and that he was not the person he remembered: "He tried to help people. After Twentynine Palms he just changed."[1] The father said he had been unaware that his son was a suspect and unaware that he was believed to be dead, adding, "It didn't say anything about him dying and I read later that he died."[1]
