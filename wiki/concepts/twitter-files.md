# Twitter Files

The Twitter Files are a series of disclosures of internal Twitter documents released after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s acquisition of the platform, detailing how the company moderated, throttled and removed content and how it interacted with government agencies. In the Badlands Media corpus the releases are treated as documentary vindication of long-standing claims about coordinated narrative management.

## Treatment in the corpus

[[j-b-white|J.B. White]] opens [[anthony-fauci|"Anthony Fauci: Soldier for the Communist Cause"]] by describing the Twitter Files as an "ongoing process of revelation" that continues to expose "remarkable wrongdoing by a RICO-worthy collection of fascistic rascals unworthy of American public service." He reads the disclosures as evidence of the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s "massive efforts to prevent competition for their preferred narratives and efforts and protocols," a formulation drawn from [[j-e-dyer|J. E. Dyer]]'s analysis of censorship as a precondition for gaslighting.

White states that his column was written to go hand-in-hand with whatever Twitter Files instalment might eventually address [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], and he links the anticipated release to Musk's tweet declaring his pronouns to be "Prosecute/Fauci." As a concrete instance of the conduct he expects the files to document, he cites the disappearance of the September 2020 tweet and account of user Daniel Levitt, which had summarised eight dissenting claims about COVID-19 and had drawn 2,053 retweets and 4,085 likes before vanishing — a fate White describes as "the normal result for many on the conservative side of the aisle."
