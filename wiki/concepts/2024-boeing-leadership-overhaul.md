# 2024 Boeing Leadership Overhaul

The 2024 Boeing leadership overhaul was the sweeping management shake-up announced by [[boeing|Boeing]] on Monday, March 25, 2024, amid the aircraft maker's most significant safety crisis in years.[1]

## Announcement

Boeing said abruptly that it was overhauling its leadership, with chief executive [[dave-calhoun|Dave Calhoun]] to depart at the end of the year.[1] [[stan-deal|Stan Deal]], head of the division that makes planes for airlines and other commercial customers, retired immediately and was replaced by chief operating officer [[stephanie-pope|Stephanie Pope]].[1]

## Background

The company had been under mounting pressure from regulators, airlines and passengers as it struggled to respond to the fallout from an incident in early January 2024 in which a panel blew off a Boeing 737 Max 9 plane midair during an Alaska Airlines flight.[1] The incident roiled a company considered by many to be a prized American institution and renewed concerns about its commitment to safety and quality, five years after two crashes of 737 Max 8 aircraft killed a total of nearly 350 people.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argued that the episode advanced a narrative of diversity, equity and inclusion programs damaging the aviation industry, describing such failures of scale as clarifying moments that make the practical dangers of what he called the "woke mind virus" obvious.[1] He suggested the case had become memetic enough to serve as a symbolic reference point, and asked whether Boeing would in turn become a symbol of an American corporate giant recovering through a return to reason, safety and profitability under new management.[1]
