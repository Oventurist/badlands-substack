# Inflation

Inflation is the general rise in prices and corresponding loss of purchasing power of a currency. In the Badlands Media corpus it is treated not as a natural economic phenomenon but as a deliberately engineered transfer of wealth — in the words of the essay [[a-week-to-remember|A Week to Remember]], "the biggest financial scam of them all."

## The mechanism as described

The article walks through a housing example to illustrate the claim. The [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] prints money and gives it to banks to lend; this "dilutes the purchasing power of existing money … causing the cost of houses to go up." The bank then lends that money to a home buyer at interest, profiting from money it received at no cost. "The politicians are paid by the banks, the banks are given free money to loan out, and the home buyer is paying for both through the inflation, manifesting itself in a higher home price and the interest on the loan." The essay adds that banks can extend larger loans precisely because the Fed's money creation has inflated prices.

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describes inflation as the largest single tax Americans pay, estimating that between conventional taxation and inflation the average American retains "around 30 to 40 cents" of each earned dollar: "In our minds, we think we pay 15%, but in reality, it's more like 60-70%."

## Political management of inflation

The article argues that much of the inflation experienced after 2021 originated in the money printing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ordered during the COVID-19 lockdowns of early 2020 — a step the author defends as "the lesser of two evils" and as "fighting the [[cabal|Cabal]] with their own weapon, the Federal Reserve," given a plan the essay attributes to the Cabal to destroy the American economy and keep the country locked down for years.

Crucially, the source observes that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and its media allies did *not* blame Trump for the resulting inflation. "To accuse Trump is to draw attention to the Ponzi Scheme," the article reasons. "The Cabal wants the world to believe inflation is a naturally-occurring event, and that it's not purposely created by the printing of money backed by nothing." Instead the media downplayed inflation and "told the public that they weren't really experiencing what they were experiencing" — because, in the author's framing, blaming Trump "would bring down the system."
