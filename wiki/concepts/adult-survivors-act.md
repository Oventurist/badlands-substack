# Adult Survivors Act

The Adult Survivors Act is a New York state law that allows victims of alleged sexual assault to sue for civil damages beyond the ordinary statute of limitations.[1] In Badlands coverage the statute is significant chiefly as the mechanism that enabled the civil litigation against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over conduct alleged to have occurred decades earlier.[1]

## Role in the Carroll Litigation

[[e-jean-carroll|E. Jean Carroll]] accused Trump of sexually assaulting her in the mid-1990s in a dressing room at the Bergdorf Goodman department store in Manhattan.[1] Although the alleged attack occurred decades before, Carroll sued under the Adult Survivors Act, which permitted the claim to proceed notwithstanding the elapsed time.[1] In late January 2024 a jury in her second civil defamation trial ordered Trump to pay $83.3 million in damages; Trump denounced the outcome on Truth Social as "absolutely ridiculous" and characterized the legal system as "out of control."[1]

## Badlands Framing

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the verdict as one more entry in a sequence of legal and narrative deployments against Trump, arguing that Trump is "the President of Precedent" and that the machinery being built against him would ultimately be aimed at the wider public: "They're not after him. They're after you. He's just in the way."[1]
