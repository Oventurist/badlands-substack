# MacArthur Park Immigration Operation

The MacArthur Park immigration operation was a federal enforcement action carried out on Monday, July 7, 2025, in Los Angeles, in which dozens of federal immigration agents in tactical gear, accompanied by members of the California National Guard, were deployed to a mostly empty MacArthur Park on Monday morning.[1]

It was unclear whether anyone was taken into custody during the operation, which sparked outrage among local residents and which Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] called "outrageous" and "an attempt to spread fear." An [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] spokesperson told CNN that the agency does not comment on ongoing operations.[1]

## Mayoral confrontation

Bass said the same day that she had personally confronted federal agents at the park amid tensions between her city and the federal government over immigration. "When I got to MacArthur Park, by the way, and my purpose was one, to witness what was going on, but then when I got there, to do what I could to stop it," she said at a press conference.[1]

She described asking who was in charge among the Customs and Border Protection personnel present and being put on the phone with that individual: "I said that this needed to end, what was the purpose in them being here, but it absolutely needed to end. He said that they would be leaving within minutes, and I believe a few minutes later they left."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary by [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that Bass was effectively asserting a right to violate federal law in order to keep California's illegal alien policy intact, contending that if California lost its illegal alien population and were forced to represent only its "real" population, the state's outsized political power would be substantially diminished. The commentary questioned how much power Bass believes she holds, framing Los Angeles as treated like a protected regime asset owned by one or many of its corporations, and posed the central question of whether city officials should be allowed to keep federal authorities out of their jurisdictions.[1]
