# 2023 Maui Wildfires

The 2023 Maui wildfires were a series of devastating fires that swept the Hawaiian island of Maui in August 2023. Contemporary reporting put the death toll at almost 100 people, with approximately 1,000 still unaccounted for, and described residents jumping into the ocean to escape the flames as the historic town of Lahaina was destroyed.[1]

## Response and leadership controversy

The response was led by Maui police chief [[john-pelletier|John Pelletier]], who had previously served as incident commander during the [[las-vegas-shooting|2017 Las Vegas massacre]].[1] [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] reported that conservatives were alarmed by the overlap, arguing that it would not give Americans confidence that the true cause of the fires would ever be established and that a cover-up was the likelier outcome.[1]

## Open questions raised by Badlands

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] catalogued questions raised by local accounts emerging from the island: whether the true death toll was 90 or 900, why the emergency sirens never sounded, and whether residents had faced pre-fire pressure to sell their property to globalist interests.[1] She characterised these as valid questions grounded in testimony from locals, dismissed by narrative makers as conspiracies, and wrote that as residents navigated the wreckage and grieved their losses the rest of the public was left uncertain whether the truth would ever be revealed.[1]
