# Administrative Detention

Administrative detention is a controversial Israeli legal practice under which suspects can be held indefinitely without charge and without due process.[1]

## Application

The tool is employed against several thousand Palestinians and a number of Israeli Arabs.[1] Defense Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] ordered an end to its application against Israeli Jews earlier in 2025.[1]

## The Mohammed Ibrahim case

The practice drew international attention through the case of [[mohammed-ibrahim|Mohammed Ibrahim]], a Palestinian-American teenager and Florida resident detained in February 2025 over allegations — denied by his family — that he threw stones at Israeli troops in the West Bank.[1] Ibrahim was never charged, turned 16 in prison, lost more than 20 pounds and contracted scabies, outcomes that Israel's own Public Defender's Office describes as common for Palestinian prisoners.[1] He was released after more than nine months when [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] intervened with senior Israeli officials; Washington objected that a U.S. citizen was being held without charge by an allied country.[1]
