# A Communist Plot to Take Over America

"A Communist Plot to Take Over America" is a June 5, 2023 article by Erik Carlson published on Badlands Media that advances a comprehensive conspiracy theory framing contemporary American events as coordinated steps in a deliberate communist takeover of the United States. The author presents the theory as an evolution of his prior thinking, acknowledging confusion about day-to-day details while insisting the "bigger picture" of a cabal-driven destruction of American identity is clear. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Overview and Epistemological Stance

Carlson opens the article by describing a state of personal confusion that he contrasts with his earlier certainty while "asleep" to what he now considers mainstream narratives. He states that he no longer accepts those narratives and is attempting to understand the "real" mechanisms of power. While he claims not to be confused about the "bigger picture"—asserting that "God will never abandon us" and that [[Donald Trump]] and "the Patriots" retain some control—he expresses uncertainty about the precise day-to-day operations and details of the process. The author emphasizes that hard conclusions are foolish and that every person or situation must be evaluated from every possible angle, including whether individuals are being used "willingly or unwillingly" and whether multiple things are happening simultaneously. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Kevin McCarthy and the 2023 Speakership

The article devotes considerable attention to [[Kevin McCarthy]]'s 2023 campaign for Speaker of the House as a case study in the broader conspiracy framework. Carlson initially viewed McCarthy as "just another weak Republican, maybe even a RINO," and stood with the Congressmen opposing his speakership. He acknowledged that [[Donald Trump]] endorsed McCarthy but also endorsed the opposing Congressmen, leading him to conclude that Trump retained control over McCarthy regardless of the outcome. After McCarthy's confirmation and the subsequent appointment of opposing Congressmen to key committee positions, Carlson believed the arrangement served hidden purposes: McCarthy needed to appear to be working with Neocons and Democrats to maintain their trust, while secretly advancing a different agenda. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

Carlson identifies the subsequent escalation in events—classified document discoveries, train derailments, Chinese spy balloons, and the uncovering of Nord Stream pipeline sabotage—as evidence that the deep state had entered "full-on panic mode" in response to McCarthy's speakership. However, the subsequent debt limit agreement caused him to reconsider this interpretation. He poses several alternative hypotheses: that an increased debt limit with virtually no spending cuts was part of Trump's plan to expose a "Uniparty," that economic deterioration was necessary before Trump could retake power, or that the agreement was designed to destroy the "Cabal banking system." A further possibility he raises is that Trump intentionally endorsed McCarthy knowing he would be quickly ousted, and that McCarthy's concessions to Democrats on the debt ceiling were designed to expose RINOs and Neocons. He notes that "America First Congressmen" had pushed for measures making it easier to impeach the Speaker of the House, and finds it notable that Trump had not mentioned the debt ceiling deal at the time of writing, focusing instead on [[Ron DeSantis]]. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Ron DeSantis and the 2024 Presidential Race

Carlson treats [[Ron DeSantis]]'s 2024 presidential campaign as another data point in the broader framework. He poses a series of questions about DeSantis's true allegiance: whether he is a Neocon who has sold out to the deep state, whether he was always a Deep State player commissioned to appear conservative in the manner of past figures such as John McCain, Mitt Romney, and the Bush family, or whether he is working with [[Donald Trump]] "willingly or unwillingly." Regardless of the answer, Carlson argues that DeSantis is "in fact exposing the RINOs, Neocons, the Deep State and the Communist plot." ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

If Trump and DeSantis are working together, Carlson speculates that their shared purpose is to expose people who claim conservatism and profess support for Trump and America while acting otherwise. He predicts that when DeSantis drops out of the race, he will endorse Trump and bring Neocon campaign funding with him—money that Neocons would never have sent to Trump directly. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Corporate "Wokeness" and the Boycott Dilemma

Carlson connects the article to an earlier piece he wrote titled "Get Woke, Go Broke," in which he argued that American corporations face a choice between upsetting customers with "woke" narratives and losing their place in large investment portfolios. After viewing a video by entrepreneur Andy Frisella, he revisits this thesis and raises a more disturbing possibility: that boycotting "woke" corporations may be exactly what the deep state Cabal wants. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

Frisella suggests that Americans are being coerced into destroying companies that represent American culture, which Carlson frames as part of a communist plot to remove the middle class and usher in a communist state. The author lists what he considers the most "American" brands—Coca-Cola, Disney, Levi Strauss & Co., Hershey's, Budweiser, and McDonald's—and notes that all of them except McDonald's have upset their customer bases through "wokeness." He questions whether this is deliberate destruction of American culture, whether McDonald's is being allowed to prosper because it makes Americans "sick and unhealthy," and whether boycotts are unwittingly assisting the Communists' goal of removing "Americana" from America. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Retail Crime and Urban Decline

The article cites two specific incidents to illustrate what Carlson characterizes as deliberate economic destruction. At a Lululemon store, two employees were fired for calling 911 when a group entered and began stealing merchandise; the company's policy prohibits employees from stopping theft. At Ulta Beauty, the company expected declining profits throughout the year due to continued massive merchandise theft, primarily occurring in liberal cities where young people are fully aware they can steal without repercussions. Carlson argues that these young people may be "coerced to steal to create more racism and division," and concludes that the only explanation is a Communist plot: if the companies were merely corrupt, allowing theft would not benefit their profits or the downtown areas becoming "ghost towns" due to violence. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

Carlson also references the case of an ex-Marine who intervened against a violent criminal on a New York subway and now faces possible jail time, framing this as a message that anyone who tries to stop the destruction of the country will be punished. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Artificial Intelligence and Social Media Manipulation

Carlson advances a theory that the mainstream rollout of artificial intelligence in 2023 was a staged "fake roll-out" designed to secure public buy-in, rather than a genuine recent breakthrough. He argues that AI has been used for decades to manipulate people, and that the public was first subjected to AI surveillance without consent—analogous to the FBI and CIA moving from wiretapping to being "willingly invited into your home." The author claims AI has been used to create a "woke" society by manipulating people's views of the world, morals, and values without their knowledge. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

He identifies the rise of social media as the inflection point for AI manipulation, arguing that many interactions users thought were with other humans were actually with AI. The author cites TikTok, which he states is operated by the Chinese Communist Party (CCP), as an example of algorithmic manipulation that rewards stupidity and degradation to maximize engagement, particularly among young people seeking likes and views. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## The Stages of Communist Takeover

Carlson outlines what he presents as the sequential stages of the Communist takeover of America:

1. Stripping away American identity
2. Destroying American society, values, and morals
3. Destroying the American economy

Once these stages are complete, he argues, the country will be ripe for communist takeover. He describes this final state as a two-class society in which elites enjoy private jets, meat, and estates while the working class rides city buses, eats insects, and rents apartments in "smart cities." He claims Americans have been manipulated into believing what is important and good in life, convinced that good is bad and bad is good, and are now "willingly assisting in our own demise." ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]
