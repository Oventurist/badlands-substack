# Ghouta Chemical Attack

The Ghouta chemical attack was a 2013 incident in which chemical weapons were deployed against civilians in Syria, and which Western media and the intelligence apparatus attributed to Syrian President [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

## Contested attribution

Years after the attack, author [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]] published an article asserting, with supporting evidence, that the attack was more likely conducted by ISIS forces supplied with chemical agents by the [[cia|CIA]], which had trafficked them from Libya where they had been taken from the private armory of the late [[muammar-gaddafi|Muammar Gaddafi]].[1]

A second chemical attack occurred in Syria in 2018, and a chorus of online voices pointed to the same elements of the Western intelligence community.[1] Meanwhile, what [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] termed "the Blob" called Assad a war criminal and demanded accountability, with Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] at the head of that effort.[1]

## Invoked as precedent in 2024

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] raised the Ghouta precedent in October 2024 while discussing reports that Israeli tanks breached a [[unifil|UNIFIL]] peacekeeping base in southern Lebanon and that shells released smoke which sickened U.N. personnel.[1] He noted that regardless of who conducted the Syrian gas attacks, they were universally categorized as war crimes by the international rules-based order, and asked whether Netanyahu and the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] would be given the same treatment as Assad or whether Israel would continue to enjoy privileges of impunity granted to no other country.[1] He predicted that such incidents would destroy the globalists' New World Order.[1]
