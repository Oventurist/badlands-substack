# 2023 Riyadh Ukraine Peace Plan Meeting

The 2023 Riyadh Ukraine peace plan meeting was a secret gathering held on December 16, 2023 in Saudi Arabia between Ukraine, its Group of Seven allies and a small group of Global South countries, convened to rally support for Kyiv's conditions for holding peace talks with Russia.[1]

## Purpose and format

The previously undisclosed meeting brought together national security advisers and followed larger, publicized gatherings aimed at countering Moscow's attempts to divide Ukraine's coalition and to portray Kyiv and its allies as unwilling to negotiate an end to the war.[1] According to people familiar with the matter, the secrecy was intended in part to make participant countries more comfortable about attending, and the smaller format allowed a freer and more frank discussion of Ukraine's so-called peace formula, plans for advancing that process, and principles for potentially engaging with Russia in future.[1]

The meeting was reported as part of the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]] diplomacy track and was carried by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as a bonus item in its January 10, 2024 news brief.[1]
