# 2024 Trump Bronx Rally

The **2024 Trump Bronx rally** was a campaign event held by former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Crotona Park in the Bronx, New York City, on Thursday, May 23, 2024. It was covered as a lead item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] published the following day under the headline "Trump Hits the Bronx as the Establishment Panics."[1]

## Background

The Bronx is one of the most heavily Democratic areas of New York City, and reporting on the event framed Trump's decision to campaign there as a breach of political norms, since the borough has historically not been receptive to Republican candidates.[1]

## The event

Trump's campaign estimated the crowd at roughly 25,000 supporters, far exceeding the 3,500 attendees initially projected. The estimate included people lined up outside the venue, many of whom had waited for hours for a chance to get inside even after the program had begun.[1]

From the stage, Trump declared: "I'm here tonight to declare we are going to turn New York City around, and we are going to turn it around very, very quickly," pledging to restore public safety and improve the city's schools, and adding that he would "make New York bigger, better and greater than ever before."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that the Badlands crew habitually scans Trump rally statements for "Devo comms" and Q confirmations while marveling at the crowd sizes Trump draws in nominally hostile territory and at the reaction of what the outlet calls the Media Industrial Complex.[1] He quoted the commentator [[thestormhasarrived|TheStormHasArrived]] approvingly and argued that the rally's historic significance lay not in its size, its location in a blue state, or the fact that it took place in New York, but in something "intrinsic, American and yes, racial" that he claimed the Deep State fears more than anything else.[1]

Burning Bright tied the event to the broader Great Awakening thesis, writing that the awakening had yet to reach "a fever pitch" domestically but that the movement was "beginning to transition from the 'very slowly' phase to the 'all at once' coda."[1]
