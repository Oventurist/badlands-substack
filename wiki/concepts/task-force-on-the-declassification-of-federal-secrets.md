# Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets

The Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets was announced on Tuesday, February 11, 2025 by [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] (R-Ky.).[1] The panel was intended to press for the release of long-withheld federal records on subjects such as the assassinations of John F. Kennedy and Martin Luther King Jr. and the September 11 attacks.[1]

## Reception

Salon treated the task force's creation as evidence that House Republicans had surrendered their core constitutional role — the power of the purse — to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]], leaving them to fill their time with what the outlet called "thuddingly stupid" symbolic work.[1] Salon characterized the arrangement as allowing a private citizen to conduct what "looks very much like a coup," while Senate Republicans confined themselves to rubber-stamping [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Cabinet appointees.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] commentary was skeptical from the opposite direction. Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Burning Bright told readers to "stop being thirsty" and argued that declassification of files on JFK, MLK, and 9/11 would not arrive by way of a congressional task force, but from the president, who already holds the unilateral authority to declassify.[1]
