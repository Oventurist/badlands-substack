# Executive Order 14196

**Executive Order 14196**, titled "A Plan for Establishing a United States Sovereign Wealth Fund," was signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on February 3, 2025.[1]

The directive tasks the Secretaries of the Treasury and Commerce, in coordination with the Assistant to the President for Economic Policy, with developing a comprehensive plan for creating a U.S. sovereign wealth fund.[1]

## Role in the Ivy Dominion argument

The Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" invokes the order as the legal foundation for a proposed "sovereign reclamation" of assets shed by collapsing elite institutions.[1] The author argues that rather than allowing courts to dismantle endowment holdings piecemeal — or letting other private equity giants absorb them — a [[united-states-sovereign-wealth-fund|United States Sovereign Wealth Fund]] could intercept and redeploy distressed endowment properties, real estate holdings, research portfolios, patent libraries, vacant Ivy League satellite campuses, federally subsidized intellectual property and data infrastructure tied to behavioral research programs.[1]

The article pairs EO 14196 with Executive Orders [[executive-order-13818|13818]] and [[executive-order-13848|13848]], which it says give the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] power to freeze and seize assets linked to corruption and election interference — arguing that institutions such as [[harvard-university|Harvard]] or the [[university-of-pennsylvania|University of Pennsylvania]] are legally exposed if they concealed foreign gifts or funneled capital into ideological enforcement mechanisms.[1] The proposed fund is envisioned as a transparent, citizen-governed "citizen dividend trust," contrasted with the sovereign funds of Norway, Singapore and the UAE.[1]
