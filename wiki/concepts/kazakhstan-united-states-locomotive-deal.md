# Kazakhstan–United States $4 Billion Locomotive Deal

The Kazakhstan–United States locomotive deal is a $4 billion agreement announced on Monday, September 22, 2025, under which [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]] committed to purchase American locomotives and rail equipment.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] unveiled the agreement following a phone call with Kazakh President [[kassym-jomart-tokayev|Kassym-Jomart Tokayev]], calling it "the largest railroad equipment purchase in history."[1]

Writing on Truth Social, Trump said: "They have signed the largest Railroad Equipment Purchase in History, $4 Billion Dollars Worth of United States Locomotives and Rail Equipment. We need to support our U.S. Rail Industry, which has been attacked by Fake Environmentalists for years," and congratulated Tokayev on the agreement.[1] Earlier the same day Tokayev met in New York with US Presidential Special Envoy for South and Central Asia [[sergio-gore|Sergio Gore]] and with a [[meta-platforms|Meta]] vice president, reaffirming Kazakhstan's role as Washington's main economic partner in Central Asia.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry read the deal through the geopolitics of the Eurasian rail corridor, citing a diagram whose three nodes are the capitals of Russia, China, and Iran — the triumvirate that [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] warned in his 1998 book *The Grand Chessboard* was the only combination capable of threatening the emerging New World Order.[1] He argued that Kazakhstan's geography makes it uniquely important: strategically located, essentially a flat plain ideal for large infrastructure projects, and the world's largest producer of uranium.[1] He connected the story to reporting from a French outlet warning that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] was assembling a grand railroad master plan as a potential silver bullet against Western hegemony, a network implicating Azerbaijan, India, Pakistan, Iran, the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation, China, Russia, and former Iranian President [[hassan-rouhani|Hassan Rouhani]], who cut the ribbon on the railroad's official opening in March 2019.[1] He speculated about an eventual rail link running from Alaska through Kazakhstan to the Middle East and Africa.[1]
