# McCain Syria Trips

The McCain Syria trips were two visits to [[syria|Syria]] by Arizona senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] — the first in 2013 under President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], the second a "secret trip" in February 2017 shortly after the inauguration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — which Badlands commentary treats as the key to allegations of treason against him.[1]

## The 2017 trip

ABC News reported that McCain made a secret trip to northern Syria to meet with U.S. troops and Kurdish fighters amid their battle to defeat [[isis|ISIS]].[1] A McCain spokesperson said the senator "traveled to northern Syria last week to visit U.S. forces deployed there and to discuss the counter-ISIL campaign and ongoing operations to retake Raqqa," ISIS's Syrian capital, and called the visit "a valuable opportunity to assess dynamic conditions on the ground in Syria and Iraq."[1] The report appeared on February 17, 2017, while the U.S.-led anti-ISIS coalition pushed further into Mosul and the U.S. military and its allies prepared a campaign to retake Raqqa.[1] ABC noted that members of Congress rarely travel to Syria, as the country does not have diplomatic ties to the United States.[1]

## The 2013 trip

McCain, then chairman of the [[senate-armed-services-committee|Senate Armed Services Committee]], had also traveled to Syria in 2013 to meet with Syrian rebel leaders fighting the government of President [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1] The Badlands account emphasizes that this earlier visit was not to U.S. troops but to rebel commanders, and asks who those leaders were.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

The article argues that both trips are connected and that the 2017 visit was driven by panic: McCain feared Trump would discover the real reason for his 2013 meeting, and sought a face-to-face encounter because "sometimes the enemy needs a face to face meeting in order to hide their treason."[1] It contends this fear of evidence of treason, rather than health, explains the timing of his cancer diagnosis and surgery immediately before the [[skinny-repeal-vote|skinny repeal vote]].[1]

[[qanon|Q]] addressed the 2013 trip in a string of drops on April 8, 2018, including drops 1087 and 1090, the latter containing a photograph whose building background Q invited readers to examine, and used the term "spider web" — described in the article as both a reference to a wide web of corruption connecting top government figures to one major conspiracy and as trolling to signal that the evidence had been collected.[1] The author states that Trump and the military know the exact location of the photograph, why McCain went to Syria, and the identities of everyone pictured, and promised to reveal them and connect the matter to Libya and Benghazi in a subsequent article.[1] The article compares McCain's secret travel to [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]]'s secret trip to North Korea.[1]
