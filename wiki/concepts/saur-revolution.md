# Saur Revolution

The Saur revolution of April 1978 was the Afghan coup that deposed King Daoud and brought the country's communist-aligned party to power.[1]

In the account given by [[paul-fitzgerald|Paul Fitzgerald]] and [[elizabeth-gould|Elizabeth Gould]] in [[valediction-three-nights-of-desmond|Valediction: Three Nights of Desmond]], the revolution served the objective of [[hafizullah-amin|Hafizullah Amin]] of undermining the nationalistic forces associated with King Daoud within the PRPD.[1] That aim was bound up with the ambitions of the Ghilzai tribe, to which both Amin and [[gulbuddin-hekmatyar|Gulbuddin Hekmatyar]] belonged and which had long sought to assert dominance over Kabul.[1]

The revolution set the stage for the assassination of U.S. Ambassador [[adolph-dubs|Adolph Dubs]] in February 1979, the Soviet military entry of December 1979, and the launch of [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]].[1]
