# Apollo-Soyuz Test Project

The Apollo–Soyuz mission of 1975 was the first joint crewed spaceflight between the American and Russian space agencies, and was commemorated on Russian postage stamps as a symbol of space cooperation.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] lists Apollo–Soyuz alongside the 1967 [[outer-space-treaty|Outer Space Treaty]] and the 1969 Moon landing as milestone achievements demonstrating that "the call of a new age of cooperation continued to resonate" despite the intrigue and evil dominating geopolitics in those years.[1] He then poses it as an unresolved anomaly: why did the Apollo–Soyuz program fail to serve as the foundation for a new era of space diplomacy?[1] His answer is the rise of [[neo-malthusianism|neo-Malthusian]] statecraft across Western governments following the assassinations of pro-development leaders such as [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1]
