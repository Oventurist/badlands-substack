# 2024 French Dissolution of Extremist Groups

In late June 2024 the French government ordered the dissolution of four far-right groups and one Islamic association, days before the first round of the snap legislative election called by President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]].[1]

## The banned organizations

The groups dissolved included GUD, an organization known for violence and antisemitism, along with the Lyon-based "Les Remparts," La Traboule and Top Sport Rhône.[1] The Islamist association Jonas Paris was also dissolved.[1] Interior Minister Gérald Darmanin wrote on X that "Hatred of extremes must be fought by the Republic."[1]

## Political context

Macron had called the snap election after far-right parties made gains in several countries in the European elections, results an expert told Newsweek signaled a pronounced rightward shift across the continent.[1] At the time of the dissolutions, [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]]'s [[national-rally|National Rally]] was leading all polls while Macron's centrist alliance lagged, though the outcome remained highly uncertain because of the two-stage voting system and the possibility of alliances.[1] Newsweek noted it would be the first time since the Nazi occupation that France could elect a far-right government.[1]
