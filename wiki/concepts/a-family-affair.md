# A Family Affair

"A Family Affair" is a November 22, 2023 article by Joe Lange published on badlands media that examines the historical record of [[bush-family]] political and financial connections, linking the Bush family to drug trafficking, CIA operations, money laundering, and corrupt foreign leaders. The article argues that family networks reveal corruption beyond mainstream media narratives, framing the [[bush-family]] as "traitors" who have operated above the law through CIA front companies, international banking relationships, and covert political influence in Mexico and Venezuela.

## Bush family and the CIA

The article traces the Bush family's relationship with the [[cia]] back to the early 1950s, when [[george-hw-bush]] and J. Hugh Liedtke formed [[zapata-offshore]] (originally Zapata Petroleum Corporation) in Houston. The source argues that [[zapata-offshore]] was a CIA front company from its inception, financed in part by [[texas-national-bank]], which had been created in 1953—the same year as Zapata Petroleum. According to the [[wayne-madsen-report]], Texas National Bank "played a significant role in helping finance the early development of Zapata Off-Shore Company."

The article further claims that three ships owned by Zapata Offshore and financed by Texas National Bank—the *Houston*, *Barbara J*, and *Zapata*—were loaned to the CIA for the 1961 [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]], which was code-named [[operation-zapata]] by the agency. The source notes that George H.W. Bush consistently denied CIA membership before becoming director in 1976, arguing instead that he had been a leader of clandestine operations since the early 1950s.

## Texas Commerce Bank and CIA operations

[[texas-commerce-bank]] emerged from a 1964 merger between National Bank of Commerce and Texas National Bank. The article describes it as a CIA bank from its beginning, noting its board members included Lady Bird Johnson and recently defeated President Gerald Ford after Ford had chosen Bush to lead the CIA in 1976. In the 1980s, [[enron]] founder Kenneth Lay—described in the source as having CIA-connected business ties—became a board member, and Howard Hughes's summa corporation (noted as CIA-linked) used Texas Commerce Bank to purchase Las Vegas Strip properties. The bank was acquired by chemical bank in 1987 and later became part of jpmorgan chase.

## Jeb Bush as CIA operative

A central claim of the article is that [[jeb-bush]] was placed as a CIA "non-official cover" (NOC) officer inside [[texas-commerce-bank]]'s international division. According to the [[wayne-madsen-report]], George H.W. Bush arranged the appointment through his close friend [[james-baker]], whose family founded the bank. A letter from Jeb Bush to CIA operative Robert Gambino, uncovered by the Wayne Madsen Report, is cited as evidence that Jeb received training in "overseas banking."

In 1977, shortly after his father left the CIA directorship, Jeb Bush—described as fluent in Spanish from an exchange student stint in Guadalajara—was sent to Caracas, Venezuela, at age 24 to serve as branch manager and vice president of Texas Commerce Bank's Venezuelan operation, alongside his wife columba bush. The source claims he was unofficially the CIA's main financial liaison to the Venezuelan oil industry and Colombian drug cartels.

## NAFTA and Mexican drug trafficking

The article connects the Bush family to Mexican corruption through [[nafta]], the North American Free Trade Agreement, arguing that the trade deal was designed to facilitate drug trafficking across the southern border and increase CIA profits. The salinas family—specifically [[carlos-salinas]], president of Mexico from 1988 to 1994, and his brother [[raul-salinas]]—is identified as the key Mexican partner in this arrangement. [[raul-salinas]] was convicted as the intellectual author of the September 1994 murder of José Francisco Ruiz Massieu, secretary general of the [[institutional-revolutionary-party]] (PRI). The victim had previously been married to Adriana Salinas, sister of Raul and Carlos.

## Gulf Cartel and money laundering

The article links [[raul-salinas]] to the [[gulf-cartel]], led by [[juan-garcia-abrego]]. According to the source, García Abrego transformed the Gulf Cartel from smugglers into cocaine suppliers by renegotiating with the [[cali-cartel]], securing 50 percent of every Colombian shipment as payment for delivery. The salinas family provided "green light protection" for Gulf Cartel drugs moving through Mexico, and NAFTA made smuggling easier by reducing border scrutiny.

## Mario Ruiz Massieu and the cover-up

After José Francisco Ruiz Massieu's murder, his brother [[mario-ruiz-massieu]] was appointed special prosecutor by President Carlos Salinas. Mario resigned claiming party leaders were blocking the investigation, but later evidence suggested he had altered witness statements to remove references to Raul Salinas. According to *The Guardian* and U.S. grand jury testimony, Mario Ruiz Massieu deposited nearly $10 million in cash at [[texas-commerce-bank]] in Houston between December 1993 and February 1995, in 26 cash deposits ranging from $98,000 to $800,000. His "bagman," [[jorge-stergios]], reportedly carried the money in cardboard boxes and suitcases on commercial flights. U.S. prosecutors alleged the funds were bribes paid by drug traffickers for immunity from prosecution. Mario was arrested in Newark in March 1994 while attempting to board a flight to Spain with $46,000 in undeclared cash. In 1997, a Houston federal jury confiscated $9 million from his Texas bank account.

## Venezuelan connections and Carlos Andrés Pérez

During Jeb Bush's tenure in Caracas, [[carlos-andres-perez]] served as president of Venezuela (1989–1993). The article notes that Pérez had been elected in 1988 with U.S. advertising and political consulting assistance, suggesting possible CIA involvement in his campaign. Pérez's presidency was marred by corruption allegations, including embezzlement from a secret presidential discretionary fund and secret joint bank accounts held with his mistress cecilia matos in New York. He was sentenced to 28 months in prison in 1996, prosecuted again in 1998, and eventually fled to miami, where he became a vocal opponent of [[hugo-chavez]].

The article draws a parallel between Pérez and Carlos Salinas, both described as corrupt leaders installed or supported by CIA-connected networks who later fled to Miami. Jeb Bush also established his base in Miami after Venezuela, creating connections with Cuban exile networks.

## Miguel Recarey and Medicare fraud

[[miguel-recarey]] is identified as a Cuban exile who built the Miami-based HMO International Medical Centers into what became the largest Medicare fraud case in U.S. history. From 1980 to 1987, Recarey received nearly $1 billion from Medicare while contributing to both Democratic and Republican politicians and hiring well-connected lobbyists. Among his supporters was [[jeb-bush]], who not only did business with Recarey but also intervened on his behalf when federal regulators sought to enforce a rule limiting Medicare enrollment. Jeb's phone call secured an exemption that allowed Recarey to exceed the 50 percent Medicare enrollment cap. After the HMO collapsed amid allegations of forged enrollments, phony billings, and bribery, Recarey fled to Venezuela, later moved to Spain, and was ultimately freed from a Madrid jail rather than extradited. Recarey paid Bush's real estate company $75,000, which the source describes as payment for Jeb's intervention.

## Real estate and corporate dealings

While serving as Florida's commerce secretary, [[jeb-bush]] gave state contracts to former CIA officer [[richard-lawless]], who later paid Bush a $213,000 commission on a 1993 real estate deal. Through his partnership with Coral Gables real estate developer [[armando-codina]], Jeb Bush acquired a 40 percent stake in Codina's company without contributing capital. In 1984, Bush invested $1,000 in the Museum Tower office building and sold it for approximately $346,000 by 1990.

A larger scandal involved the 1997 sale of IBM's Boca Raton office park, assessed at $100 million. The property sold for $46.1 million to Blue Lake Ltd., a group including Republican fundraiser [[mark-guzzetta]], who was best man at Bush's wedding and finance co-chair of his gubernatorial campaign. The Codina Bush firm refused to disclose its commission on the deal.

## Lehman Brothers and the 2008 financial crisis

The article concludes by connecting [[jeb-bush]] to the [[lehman-brothers]] collapse. According to *Fox Business*, Bush earned more than $14 million over approximately seven years as an adviser to Lehman Brothers and, after its 2008 bankruptcy, to [[barclays]], which acquired the firm. In 2008, Bush led "Project Verde," an effort to convince Mexican billionaire [[carlos-slim]] to make a potentially life-saving investment in Lehman. Slim declined interest in a joint venture or stock purchase on July 5, 2008. The article notes that Slim and the salinas family were close associates, and that Slim had been a client of Lehman Brothers who had already lost money as the bank's stock collapsed.

The source ends by previewing its next installment, which will focus on another financial crisis centered on the Bush family and [[donald-trump]]'s supposed plan to return banking power to the people.
