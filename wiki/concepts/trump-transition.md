# Trump Transition

The "Trump Transition" is a framing used by Badlands analyst [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] for the period in which, in his reading, the [[iran|Iran]] War and its resolution produce a wholesale reordering of geopolitics, global governance, finance and trade under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The term is the title of the seventh installment of his Iran War series and was invoked again in the June 2, 2026 Badlands Brief.[1]

## Argument

Burning Bright asked why Trump was treating the geopolitical conflict said to be eroding his public mandate as though it were "the very accelerant into the Golden Age he built his public platform on a decade ago," and answered that understanding this requires inverting the assumed endgame of Trump's plans for the United States and for "a reimagined world."[1] He cited Trump's repeated invocation of Venezuela — "Just like the results in Venezuela, which the media doesn't like talking about, the results in Iran will be amazing," and "This is being perfectly executed, on the scale of Venezuela, just a bigger, more complex operation. The result will be the same" — as evidence that the "Iranian Knot" is following the [[venezuela-model|Venezuela Model]], only bigger and more complex.[1]

He argued that "The Plan" is not merely something to be cheered from the sidelines or glimpsed at the macro level in Q Drops but can be mapped in detail once the templates are understood, pointing to his own January 11, 2026 essay [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]] as an early statement of the Iran template.[1] The longer the process plays out, he wrote, the harder it becomes for the adversary to undo the damage to their system — which is why the [[collectorate|Collectorate]] appears to be panicking even as commentators insist the Iran War narrative is destroying the Trump movement's mandate.[1] In his account, the opposing power structure knows Trump's true endgame and knows it is living through the Trump Transition, a conviction he ties to the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] and the [[multipolar-war|Multipolar War]].[1]

## 2024 transition team leadership

In August 2024, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] appointed two friends and financial backers, [[linda-mcmahon|Linda E. McMahon]] and [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]], as chairs of a transition team preparing for his potential return to power, a move the New York Times characterized as well behind schedule.[2] The announcement came as polls showed his once-commanding lead had evaporated after President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] dropped out of the race and Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] became the Democratic nominee.[2] The two chairs were tasked with identifying and vetting potential political appointees and drafting executive orders and other plans to implement Trump's policy proposals, including a sweeping crackdown on immigration and raising tariffs on imported goods.[2]

The selection was notable both for its timing — coming months after a typical presidential campaign begins contingency planning — and because neither McMahon nor Lutnick had been associated with [[project-2025|Project 2025]], the consortium effort to develop personnel and policy plans for the next Republican president, from which Trump had recently sought to distance himself as Democrats seized on its more radical proposals.[2] "I have absolute confidence the Trump-Vance administration will be ready to govern effectively on Day 1," Trump said in a statement.[2]
