# Robovan

The Robovan is one of the autonomous-vehicle products unveiled by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] at a [[tesla|Tesla]] showcase of technologies the company and Musk were developing, presented alongside the [[cybercab|cybercab]] and Tesla's AI robots.[1]

## Market reaction

Following the showcase, Wall Street drove Tesla's stock price down 8%, with the reaction summarized as a claim that what was presented to investors did not impress.[1] Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treated the verdict as absurd, asking whether cabs that drive themselves and robots that can perform mundane tasks are genuinely unimpressive at a moment when the public is told that artificial intelligence is the future.[1]

## Reception in the "truth community"

Carlson wrote that the showcase of Musk's futuristic, AI-driven products caused many people within the truth community to turn on Musk, including many who had only recently accepted that he was on the "good guy's side," because they saw in the products a dangerous future he was trying to create.[1] Carlson found the distrust mind-boggling and argued that Musk was not describing a future he hoped to develop but demonstrating technology that already exists and has existed for some time — with the public AI push of the preceding two years serving to bring people up to speed with capabilities already in covert use for decades.[1] He offered [[blackrock|BlackRock]]'s [[aladdin|Aladdin]] system as his supporting example and concluded that Musk "isn't creating a robot army; he is working with [[donald-trump|Trump]] to take down the system."[1]
