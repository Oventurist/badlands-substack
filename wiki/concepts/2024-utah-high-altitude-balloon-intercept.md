# 2024 Utah High-Altitude Balloon Intercept

On Friday, February 23, 2024, U.S. fighter aircraft intercepted a high-altitude balloon flying over Utah.[1] The [[norad|North American Aerospace Defense Command]] said the following day that the object was a "likely hobby balloon" and that it had since left United States airspace.[1]

## Official account

NORAD described the object as a "small balloon" intercepted on Friday morning at an altitude of 43,000 to 45,000 feet, and said it was allowed to continue flying above the United States because it was determined not to pose a national security threat.[1] "After yesterday's fighter intercepts, and in coordination with the [[federal-aviation-administration|Federal Aviation Administration]], the North American Aerospace Defense Command monitored the likely hobby balloon via ground radars until it left US airspace overnight," the command said in a Saturday statement.[1] NORAD said it had no additional information on the balloon; the episode was reported by [[abc-news|ABC News]].[1]
