# Tesla Takedown

**Tesla Takedown** (styled online as #TeslaTakedown) is a coordinated protest and divestment campaign directed against [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], his role in the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], and his leadership of [[tesla-inc|Tesla Inc.]][1]

## Scope and tactics

More than 200 coordinated protests erupted across the United States on a single Saturday in late March 2025 as part of what organizers described as a global movement opposing Musk's political role.[1] The campaign urged supporters to divest from Tesla stock and to reject Musk's influence over federal government policy exercised through his leadership of the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]].[1]

Demonstrations were held at Tesla locations in major cities including Washington, D.C., Chicago, Boston, and Rockville, Maryland, with organizers reporting thousands of participants nationwide.[1] Protesters voiced opposition to the sweeping budget cuts and federal workforce reductions spearheaded by DOGE under Musk's direction.[1]

## Stated grievances

Participants raised concerns about the campaign's impact on public health, scientific research, and foreign aid, citing DOGE's attempts to dismantle agencies such as the [[united-states-institute-of-peace|United States Institute of Peace]] and to cut staffing at the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] by 20%.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor Burning Bright argued that the "brilliance of the Tesla Takedown storyline can't be overstated from a narrative seeding perspective," describing it as memetic because it combined the strange, the frightening, and the humorous — in his view "the perfect combo for mass awareness."[1] He further contended that the campaign was leading to the exposure of what he called a "Marxist dark money matrix," implying that the protests were funded and coordinated rather than spontaneous.[1]
