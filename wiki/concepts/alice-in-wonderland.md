# Alice In Wonderland (Q drop motif)

**"Alice In Wonderland"** is a recurring sign-off phrase in the [[q-operation|Q]] drops, appearing several times in the November 2, 2017 posts covered by Chapter 7 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]].[1]

## Interpretations

The Chapter 7 author states that the phrase carries more than one meaning across the drops.[1] In some instances he reads it as a reference to [[cia|C.I.A.]] supercomputers; in others, as a statement about the depth of the "rabbit hole" being pointed at.[1] For the drops in this chapter — notably drops #52, #57 and #59 — he judges the latter reading to be the operative one, taking Q's use of the phrase as a signal that the corruption under examination runs far deeper than readers realise.[1]

## Association with the Saudi Arabia drops

Because the phrase closes drop #59, the drop that opens the [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] sequence, the author flags in advance that "Alice In Wonderland" also plays a specific role in the Saudi drops that follow, and advises readers to keep the phrase in mind as that material unfolds.[1]
