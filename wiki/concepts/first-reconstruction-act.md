# First Reconstruction Act

The **First Reconstruction Act** was the 1867 federal statute enacted following the American Civil War that divided the former Confederate states into military districts subject to the military authority of the United States.[1]

## Text and structure

The Act provided "that said rebel States shall be divided into military districts and made subject to the military authority of the United States as hereinafter prescribed," assigning Virginia as the first district; North Carolina and South Carolina as the second; Georgia, Alabama, and Florida as the third; Mississippi and Arkansas as the fourth; and Louisiana and Texas as the fifth.[1]

## Invocation in 2026

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the anniversary of the Act in March 2026 as a "cautionary tale" while discussing state-level resistance to federal immigration enforcement in Minnesota and Colorado, writing that "it's important to remember the precedents" and that "Minnesota is playing with fire. So is Colorado."[1] The reference was made in the context of the Hennepin County investigation led by [[mary-moriarty|Mary Moriarty]] into federal agents including [[gregory-bovino|Gregory Bovino]].[1]
