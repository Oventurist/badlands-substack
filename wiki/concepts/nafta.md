# NAFTA

The North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) is treated in the Badlands series not as a trade liberalization measure but as an instrument of narcotics logistics. The preceding article in the series connected the [[bush-family|Bush]] and [[salinas-family|Salinas]] families to a massive increase in drug trafficking across the Mexican border "under the guise of free trade," concluding that NAFTA "was all about increasing drug trafficking across the southern border in order to increase the CIA's profits."[1]

"A Family Affair" supplies the mechanism. Because [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]] had renegotiated the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]]'s arrangement with the [[cali-cartel|Cali Cartel]] to take 50 percent of every Colombian shipment in exchange for guaranteeing its delivery, reliable transit became commercially essential; NAFTA made smuggling across the southern border substantially easier and so underwrote that guarantee.[1] A third family — the Ruiz Massieus — is identified as also entangled in the NAFTA scandal and drug trafficking, the thread that leads back to [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] in Houston.[1]
