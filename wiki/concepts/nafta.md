# NAFTA

The North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) is presented in the Badlands Media series not as a trade policy but as an instrument of narcotics logistics. The author states that in the preceding article he connected the Bush and [[salinas-family|Salinas]] families to a massive increase in drug trafficking across the Mexican border under the guise of free trade, and that NAFTA "was all about increasing drug trafficking across the southern border in order to increase the [[cia|CIA]]'s profits."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

The mechanism offered is the delivery guarantee that [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]] accepted from the [[cali-cartel|Cali Cartel]] in exchange for 50 percent of every Colombian shipment: because the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] now had to guarantee arrival, easier border crossings became commercially essential.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) A third family — the Ruiz Massieus — is introduced in this article as also connected to the NAFTA scandal and drug trafficking.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

## Role in the unipolar order

In [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account, NAFTA belongs to the institutional architecture of the unipolar world order that followed the collapse of the Soviet system in the early 1990s, alongside the European Union, the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]] and the 1999 destruction of [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Together these converted the trans-Atlantic into a cage of "post-sovereign nations" that no longer had actual control of their own powers of credit generation.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Ehret adds that NAFTA has since been seriously challenged by a restoration of protectionism in the United States, one of several developments he reads as marking the end of the unipolar script.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
