# Baby Boomers

The Baby Boomer generation is the American cohort that preceded [[generation-x|Generation X]] and came of age during the 1960s. In a December 2024 Badlands Media essay, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that "the whole decade of the 1960s was a psyop on the Baby Boomers," encompassing the hippie counterculture, drug culture, the [[womens-movement|Women's Movement]], and the [[civil-rights-movement|Civil Rights Movement]], all set against the Vietnam War.[1]

Carlson contends these movements were funded and orchestrated by the [[cia|CIA]] to instill discord and confusion in the Boomer generation, leaving it "confused about how to attain happiness and fulfillment" and guilt-ridden when it came close to achieving them.[1] He summarizes the generational arc as "hippies transitioned to yuppies, eventually becoming Karens."[1]

The essay also notes that the December 1980 murder of [[john-lennon|John Lennon]] in Manhattan affected Baby Boomers most acutely, since Lennon had been a childhood idol to many of them.[1] On sexual identity, Carlson reports that 4% of Baby Boomers identify as LGBTQ+ and 0.2% as transgender, the lowest figures of any living generation in his comparison.[1] In the 2024 election, 49% of voters aged 65 and over — largely Boomers — backed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], short of a majority.[1]

Carlson observes an irony in the 2024 transition: Trump, himself a Baby Boomer, is removing a [[deep-state|Deep State]] cast composed mostly of Boomers and members of the Silent Generation — the Obamas, the Clintons, the Bidens, Pelosi, Schumer, and McConnell — and replacing them with appointees drawn predominantly from Generation X.[1]
