# Gmail draft communications

"Gmail drafts" refers to a covert communication technique in which two or more parties share a single email account and exchange messages by writing them into the drafts folder rather than sending them, so that no email ever transits between accounts and no traceable trail is created.[1] The method occupies a central place in the Badlands Media article "The Biggest Boomerang In History," where [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] presents it as the hidden channel used by senior [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration officials to coordinate what he characterises as treason.[1]

## Origin in the Petraeus affair

The article traces public awareness of the technique to a 2012 Politico report that former [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] director [[david-petraeus|David Petraeus]] and his biographer Paula Broadwell discussed their extramarital relationship using an email method favoured by Al Qaeda: rather than sending messages, both wrote into the drafts folder of a shared account, with the other party logging in, reading the draft, deleting it and composing a reply as a new draft.[1] Politico noted that the method avoids creating a traceable email trail and that PBS's "Frontline" had reported in 2005 that the tactic was often used by Al Qaeda and other terrorist groups.[1] Lange argues it is no coincidence that a CIA director knew the same technique, suggesting the agency most likely taught it to Al Qaeda.[1]

## Alleged use by the Obama administration

Q drop 1504 of June 15, 2018, quoted in the article, raises "GMAIL DRAFTS" alongside the assertion that the Inspector General report revealed [[james-comey|James Comey]]'s Gmail account, that "they all had them," and that convicting [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] over private emails would mean convicting themselves; the drop names Google chief executive [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]] as "KEY."[1] Lange writes that Comey's private Gmail account surfaced in the Inspector General's report into the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] investigation and that most major players in the Obama administration likewise used private accounts.[1]

In his account, the officials used private emails, secret servers and Gmail drafts because they knew they were committing crimes and wished to avoid the [[freedom-of-information-act|Freedom of Information Act]].[1] Congress, alerted by declassified material referenced in Q drop 1282 ("Lawmakers learned gmail draft comms yesterday? Lawmakers go hunting?"), is described as beginning to investigate the practice.[1]

## Relationship to DECLAS

Lange argues that the surviving drafts are why the enemy feared [[declas|DECLAS]]: alongside the declassified email communications that Attorney General [[william-barr|William Barr]] released, the government also possesses the Gmail drafts, on the principle that "nothing is ever TRULY DELETED."[1] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
