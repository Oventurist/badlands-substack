# The AI Dilemma

*The AI Dilemma* is a presentation released in March 2023 by the [[center-for-humane-technology|Center for Humane Technology]] and delivered by [[tristan-harris|Tristan Harris]] and [[aza-raskin|Aza Raskin]]. It is the point of departure for the July 2023 Badlands Media article "Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason."

## Content and setting

The published description, quoted in full by the Badlands article, states that Harris and Raskin "discuss how existing A.I. capabilities already pose catastrophic risks to a functional society, how A.I. companies are caught in a race to deploy as quickly as possible without adequate safety measures, and what it would mean to upgrade our institutions to a post-A.I. world." The talk was given at a private gathering in San Francisco on March 9, 2023, before an audience of leading technologists and decision-makers, and prior to the launch of GPT-4. Viewers were encouraged to call their political representatives to advocate for hearings on AI risk and for adequate guardrails.

By July 2023 the recording had been viewed more than 2.7 million times.

## Reception in the corpus

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] identifies two apparent objectives: raising the collective understanding of AI researchers and the viewing public, and framing the public conversation constructively. She accepts the technical substance — including the presenters' claims about fMRI-based dream mapping and the broader phenomenon of [[reality-collapse|reality collapse]] — but rejects the closing prescription that researchers voluntarily pause and slow public release. That solution, she writes, "requires a complete rewrite of human nature," and she cites the Covid-19 pandemic as demonstrating that "given the opportunity, powerful people will fabricate and exploit fear among the population to further centralize their power."

The presentation's argument was echoed shortly afterward in the [[pause-giant-ai-experiments-open-letter|March 22, 2023 open letter]], which Harris and Raskin both signed.
