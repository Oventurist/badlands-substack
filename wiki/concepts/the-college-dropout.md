# The College Dropout

***The College Dropout*** is the debut studio album of [[kanye-west|Kanye West]], released in February 2004 after years in which West worked as a producer for major mainstream artists — particularly [[jay-z|Jay-Z]] — while struggling to establish himself as a lyricist and rapper.[1]

## "Jesus Walks" and its themes

One of the five singles West selected to promote the album was "Jesus Walks," a song in which, according to Badlands Media commentary, he explicitly decried the fact that the music industry is controlled by satanists who hate Jesus and promote degeneracy over Christian values.[1] The commentary treats the record as an early and costly act of defiance, noting pointedly, "We all saw what happened to Kanye in the years that followed."[1]

## Context in Badlands coverage

The album is discussed in a December 2024 Badlands News Brief item on the civil lawsuit against Jay-Z, where [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recounts West's backstory: years spent as a relatively unknown producer whose musical genius made him a go-to for stars seeking hit songs, and whose ambitions as a performer were allegedly stifled by Jay-Z's clout over the industry.[1] The same account traces a through-line from "Jesus Walks" to West's later gospel album [[jesus-is-king|Jesus Is King]] and his stated desire to make Christianity appealing to young people again.[1]
