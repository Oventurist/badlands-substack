# The Hunt for Red October

*The Hunt for Red October* is the Tom Clancy novel, later adapted as a film, about a Soviet submarine captain who defects with his crew to the United States. Within the Badlands Media corpus it is discussed chiefly as a recurring reference in the [[q-anon|Q]] drops, and as a case study in decoding symbolic communication.

## The conventional reading

In A Virus, or a Metaphor?, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] notes that anyone who has followed Q has encountered the phrase, and that the Q team "has posted many references to this story." His own earlier interpretation, held for years, was that the plot represents [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his defection from a corrupt system — an attempt to bring truth to the public while under attack from all sides. The article allows that this may still be one valid meaning.

## The title as the message

The essay's revised conclusion is that the larger meaning lies not in the plot but in the title. "Red October" refers to the October 1917 [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik uprising]], which overthrew and later murdered the Russian monarch and ushered in [[communism|Communism]] and the Soviet Union. The author classifies that event as a [[color-revolution|color revolution]], "where a small group takes over the control of a country by creating propaganda and chaos, through funding violence in the streets, buying off the media, and rigging elections."

On this reading, the persistence of Red October references was Q signaling that "we were experiencing a Communist takeover" — that America, like Russia in 1917, is being brought down by narratives and agendas that divide, discourage and demoralize the country. As the article puts it: "it's not the movie plot as much as it is the title."
