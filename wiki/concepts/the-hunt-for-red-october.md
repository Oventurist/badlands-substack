# The Hunt for Red October

*The Hunt for Red October* is the Tom Clancy novel, later a film, about a Soviet submarine captain who defects with his crew to the United States.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Anyone who has followed [[qanon|Q]] has heard of it, because the Q team has posted many references to the story.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Carlson's reading

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that for years he could not understand the significance of the repeated references.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Fairly recently he concluded that the story represents [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his defection from a corrupt system — Trump trying to bring the truth to the public while being attacked from all sides.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He allows that this may still be one meaning, but now believes the true and bigger meaning of "Red October" refers to Communism itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

On this reading the phrase points not to the plot but to the title: Red October refers to the 1917 [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik uprising]] that overthrew and later murdered the Russian monarch and ushered in a state of Communism and the Soviet Union.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Carlson has elsewhere described that event as a [[color-revolution|color revolution]], in which a small group takes over control of a country by creating propaganda and chaos, funding violence in the streets, buying off the media and rigging elections.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Implication

Q, in Carlson's interpretation, was telling its readers that America is experiencing a Communist takeover — that like the far-left Bolsheviks of 1917, America is being taken down by narratives and agendas that divide, discourage and demoralize the country, something he says anyone can now see all around them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)
