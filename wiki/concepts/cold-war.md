# Cold War

The Cold War is conventionally understood as the decades-long geopolitical confrontation between the United States and the [[soviet-union|Soviet Union]]. Badlands Media treats the Cold War primarily as a narrative — an official story used to justify covert operations rather than an accurate description of what was happening.[1]

## The official narrative

The article summarizes the version pushed on the American public: the United States was locked in a Cold War against an evil communist empire that was always looking to expand communism around the world, and [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] was simply its latest conquest.[1]

## The Badlands counter-reading

Badlands Media argues the opposite: it was not the Soviet Union that was expanding worldwide but the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and the [[shadow-government|shadow government]], and CIA expansion into the Middle East was a direct threat to the Soviet Union that triggered the intervention in Afghanistan.[1] In this reading, the CIA had been overthrowing governments and installing dictators to protect worldwide drug trafficking, using the Cold War with the Soviet Union as the stated reason for its actions, and the Cold War framing served as the excuse for proxy wars all over the world and for the diversion of taxpayers' money.[1]

## Transition to the war on terror

The [[soviet-afghan-war|Soviet invasion of Afghanistan]] is described as a pivotal point in world history: it marked the end of the Cold War narrative and the start of the "never-ending [[war-on-terror|war on terror]]." The cabal, the article argues, knew the Cold War was coming to an end and needed a new never-ending war, which became the war against terrorism, deliberately centered on the Middle East as a permanent war zone generating fear and expenditure.[1]

The CIA's heroin operation in Afghanistan, in this account, was disguised as just another battle in the Cold War and as support for freedom fighters.[1]
