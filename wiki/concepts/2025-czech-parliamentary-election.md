# 2025 Czech Parliamentary Election

The 2025 Czech parliamentary election was held on October 3 and 4, 2025, and was won by the populist ANO party of [[andrej-babis|Andrej Babiš]], described by The Economist as "the Czech Trump."[1]

## Campaign

The election in the [[czech-republic|Czech Republic]] followed what The Economist called an unusually bitter campaign.[1] In September, Babiš — a populist billionaire and the race's frontrunner — was struck on the head with a crutch by a pensioner and briefly hospitalized.[1] Babiš and the incumbent prime minister, [[petr-fiala|Petr Fiala]], traded petty insults over subjects ranging from foreign policy to the price of butter, with the challenger at one point calling Fiala a "fool" whose brain was clouded by "anti-Babisism."[1]

## Results

ANO — an acronym that also spells the Czech word for "yes" — finished first with 35% of the vote.[1] SPOLU ("Together"), an alliance of three parties led by Fiala, came a distant second with 23%, and STAN, a liberal-conservative alliance, was third with 11%.[1] The liberal Pirate Party outperformed forecasts with 9%, the hard-right SPD underperformed at 8%, and the populist Motorists for Themselves auto-owners' party took around 7%.[1] Stačilo ("enough"), a pro-Russian hard-left alliance, failed to clear the 5% threshold for parliamentary representation.[1] Turnout was high at nearly 70%.[1]

## Government formation

With so many parties in play, the shape of the next coalition was unclear immediately after the vote.[1] The government was expected almost certainly to be led by ANO, but Babiš would need one or two partners, and SPOLU had ruled out joining him.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] grouped the Czech result with Slovakia's attendance at the Beijing Victory Day parade, Hungary's public opposition to escalation with Russia, and the elevation of a hardline conservative in Japan, concluding: "The Global Hegemon is dead."[1]
