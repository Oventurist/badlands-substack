# Yalta European Strategy Conference

The Yalta European Strategy (YES) Conference is an annual gathering of Western and Ukrainian political figures focused on Ukraine's relationship with Europe.[1] Badlands described the September 2024 edition as an event "where globalists gathered to indulge their collective hatred of [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]."[1]

## 2024 remarks by Boris Johnson

At the conference, former British Prime Minister [[boris-johnson|Boris Johnson]] argued that if the United States withdrew its military from Europe he could envision [[ukraine|Ukraine]] serving as the future defender of the continent, given the heroism Ukrainian forces had shown on the battlefield, and asserted that "only Ukraine" had demonstrated the capability of defeating the Russians.[1] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] dismissed the remarks in blunt terms as those of a "dangerous imbecile."[1]

Economist [[jeffrey-sachs|Jeffrey Sachs]] responded to Johnson's statement in an interview with [[andrew-napolitano|Judge Andrew Napolitano]], noting that former Secretary of State [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]] was seated next to Johnson at the event—an observation Badlands echoed with the hope that Pompeo would "end up on the right side of things," while noting that he "keeps some strange company."[1]
