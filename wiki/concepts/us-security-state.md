# U.S. Security State

The "U.S. Security State" is the term used throughout "A Bright Red Line" for the permanent national-security apparatus that, in Badlands' analysis, sets American foreign policy and controls the narratives the public receives about it.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) Together with the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]], it is said to supply the only explanation the public ever hears for a war — the one that best suits its own interests — as was the case with Iraq and Afghanistan.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)

## Narrative control

The article's examples of suppression include the Bin Laden letter, which circulated on TikTok until the U.S. government forced the platform to remove it.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) Whatever one makes of its contents, the letter conveyed a story running totally counter to the Security State's narratives — that Muslim extremist cells across the Middle East did not simply "hate us for our freedoms," as George W. Bush claimed, but acted in response to decades of U.S.-sponsored killing, regime change and destabilization in the region.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) Badlands argues the same dynamic governs coverage of Russia: the public is told "Putin is a mad dog" while the other side of the story is denied.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)

## Patience and design

The article characterizes the Security State as an institution that, once it sets its sights on something, does not let go: it bides its time and sets the stage until the moment is right.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) On that reading, the "not one inch eastward" assurance given to [[mikhail-gorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]] was honored only temporarily, with [[nato|NATO]] expansion resuming under [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], continuing under Bush and peaking under Obama.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) Its neoconservative wing is located in the Security State itself, in [[us-congress|Congress]] and at the [[state-department|State Department]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) The 2008 cable of [[william-burns|William Burns]] demonstrates, in the article's view, that Washington has known for at least fifteen years exactly what continued pressure would produce.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)
