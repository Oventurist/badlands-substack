# 2025 Nobel Peace Prize

The 2025 Nobel Peace Prize was awarded to Venezuelan pro-democracy activist [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] for her "tireless work promoting democratic rights for the people of Venezuela," announced by the Norwegian Nobel Committee in October 2025.[1] The decision drew an immediate rebuke from the [[white-house|White House]], which accused the Nobel Committee of placing "politics over peace" for choosing Machado over US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Trump had been outspoken about his desire for the award, taking credit for ending several global conflicts and raising the subject regularly, including during his address to the UN General Assembly in September 2025.[1] Despite the White House's public criticism, a senior White House official told CBS that Trump called Machado to congratulate her and to say that she deserved the award.[1]
