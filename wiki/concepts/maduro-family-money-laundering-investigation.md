# Maduro Family Money Laundering Investigation

The Maduro family money laundering investigation is an [[fbi|FBI]] case, opened in 2019 by the bureau's Miami field office, into an international scheme to move illicit funds into the United States on behalf of the children of Venezuelan president [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and their associates.[1]

## The scheme

Documents obtained by Fox News Digital indicate that close allies of the chavista regime opened bank accounts inside the United States to move illicit money in defiance of existing sanctions on the Venezuelan government.[1] Businessman Arick Komarczyk is alleged to have orchestrated the management of transfers and accounts held in the names of Maduro's children and their associates, with suspicious activity reports showing that he received funds from [[venezuela|Venezuela]] for introduction into the American financial system.[1]

In 2022, an undercover operation caught Komarczyk and his partner, Irazmar Carbajal, agreeing to transfer $100,000 said to have come from chavista officials; of that amount, $25,000 successfully entered the United States.[1] Investigators were struck less by the transaction than by Komarczyk's reaction to it — rather than showing alarm, he called the maneuver a "sexy matter," which reporting treated as reflecting the casual attitude of chavista operatives toward such activity.[1]

## Charges

On September 25, 2025, a federal court in Florida formally charged Komarczyk with money laundering and conspiracy to conduct unlicensed money transfers, while Carbajal was charged with conspiracy to conduct unauthorized transactions.[1] Carbajal, originally from Uruguay, had recently traveled to the Dominican Republic and was deported on October 2; the FBI took him into custody during a layover in the United States.[1] Komarczyk remained in Venezuela.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] used the case to raise a broader question about the campaign against Maduro, asking why figures including [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], [[michael-flynn|General Flynn]], [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], and [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]] — as well as global NGOs acting on behalf of banks and oil companies — were all working to remove Maduro from power.[1]
