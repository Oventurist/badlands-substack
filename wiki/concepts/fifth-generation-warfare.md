Fifth-Generation Warfare (5GW) is a conceptual framework used by several [[badlands-media]] programs to interpret current events as a form of conflict waged through narrative, perception, and information rather than conventional force.[1] The network's Game Theory show (Thursday, 12pm ET), hosted by Sean Morgan and [[burning-bright]], applies "Game Theory and Fifth-Generation Warfare" to find "the truth beneath the surface" of national and international events.[1] The NCSWIC Podcast (Sunday, 7:30pm ET), with Tru Reporting and Absolute1776, analyzes "Fifth-Generation Warfare" and [[information-war]] topics.[1] The Defected show (Sunday, 9pm ET), with [[burning-bright]] and Just Human, examines events through "Narrative Warfare, Game Theory and Fifth-Generation Strategy."[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

## AI-driven domestic information operations

By January 2023 fifth-generation warfare had become the frame Badlands contributors used for the adaptation of military-grade artificial intelligence - originally deployed to silence [[isis|ISIS]] - to identify and censor American dissent on vaccine safety and election integrity.[3] Reporting indicated that the [[national-science-foundation|National Science Foundation]] had granted several million dollars to universities and private firms to build tools resembling DARPA's 2011 [[social-media-in-strategic-communication|Social Media in Strategic Communication]] program.[3] Sean Morgan argued that "the weapons that were once pointed at foreign enemies like ISIS are being swiveled around at our own citizens," and called for rapid engagement in the AI arms race to establish safeguards protecting citizens from being targeted.[3]

## Applications cited in January 2023

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributors applied the fifth-generation warfare frame to several January 2023 news items.[4] [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]]'s fifth-generation warfare manual as describing a playbook in which subgroups are curated within the general population, neighbour is pitted against neighbour, and the rhetoric of any group threatening the social order is labelled "hate speech" — a pattern he saw in [[vera-jourova|Věra Jourová]]'s Davos remarks.[4] DeLarme separately described [[antifa|Antifa]] as the civilian proxy force that fourth- and fifth-generation warfare require to make the manoeuvres of an invisible enemy appear organic.[4] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the term to describe "soft eugenics": subtle cultural methods, including platform policies promoting transgender ideology, deployed to reach depopulation and control objectives that overt culling could never achieve politically.[4]

## Ambiguity and randomness

Writing on the March 2024 [[francis-scott-key-bridge-collapse|Francis Scott Key Bridge collapse]], [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] quoted The Citizen's Guide to Fifth Generation Warfare on ambiguity and randomness: "All actions are precipitated by thoughts, attitudes, and decisions, incorrect or otherwise. Mass manipulation requires the use of physical situations which are framed into psychologically focused messages that are repeated over and over from various sources, told in their own way to affect people's attitudes for the purpose of influencing their behavior. Ambiguity and randomness are tenants of 5GW, and that's the reason people can't see the manipulation occurring."[5]

Esler applied the framework to kinetic-network attacks on critical infrastructure, arguing that the chaotic and contradictory information following such an event is itself a fog of war typical of 5G conflict, that such incidents set the stage for subsequent "perception" warfare, and that the appropriate counter is to expose coordinated efforts and shift toward decentralized systems less vulnerable to network warfare.[5]

## Application to the Macron visit and Disney dispute (2023)

In April 2023, Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked Fifth-Generation Warfare in analysing the [[2023-macron-state-visit-to-china|2023 Macron state visit to China]], writing that he did not know whether [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] was acting "via direct control, leverage or the machinations of Fifth-Gen Warfare and Game Theory," but that the outcome showed "what control looks like in the war between the forces of Globalism and Sovereignty" — where "friends become enemies, enemies become friends," and enemies are sometimes forced to work against their own self-interest.[6] Commenting separately on the [[disney-v-desantis|DeSantis–Disney dispute]], he described the conflict as a "War of Stories," a framing he said many in the community had adopted as their understanding of 5GW evolved over time.[6]

## Trump's Iran narrative as layered traps (June 2025)

Commenting on the preliminary U.S. report that the June 2025 strikes had set [[iran|Iran]]'s nuclear program back by only a few months, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that "[[donald-trump|Trump]]'s narrative deployments are layered traps in a fifth-generation war meant to cultivate the mass public mandate for peace," and that achieving this required exposing those who wanted the opposite.[7] [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] made the complementary claim that "the fake kinetic war is the fully-visible cover story for the very real information war."[7]

## Perception as the outer defence (2025)

In "Countering an Ocean of Deceit," [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] wrote that 5G war is waged partly by subduing the target's ability to perceive that they are under attack at all, and likened that inability to the Death Star's outer shell — a nearly impenetrable layer requiring a precision strike to bypass.[8] Quoting [[martin-geddes|Martin Geddes]], he held that the main goal of "our side" of the 5G war is to help the masses understand that a 5G war is under way in the first place.[8] The method he proposed is to make people aware of incontrovertible facts that, once registered, cannot be contradicted — with the legitimacy of the [[q-operation|Q operation]], evidenced by the [[q-proofs|Q proofs]], serving as the paradigm case because its irrefutability, not merely its truth, is what makes it effective.[8]

## Application to the Pizzagate cover-up

In March 2024, [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] used the framework of *The Citizen's Guide to Fifth-Generation Warfare* by [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] and [[boone-cutler|Boone Cutler]] to analyze the suppression of [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]].[9] He quoted the book's definition of hybrid warfare as "the combination of cyberwarfare in social media being influenced by bots sharing contrived news stories that focus on reporting selective information," creating emotionality that polarizes an audience while the opposition is censored and a smaller supportive audience is promoted.[9] Anyone who tried to post about Pizzagate when it broke, he wrote, was either censored or hit by [[politifact|PolitiFact]] or another weaponized fact checker in exactly the manner described.[9]

Esler argued that a video by [[ian-carroll|Ian Carroll]], retweeted by Flynn on January 3, 2024, exposed a further tactic — an information feedback loop — and validated another dimension of the doctrine: the involvement of a shadow government, described in the book as using "an unseen hand to manipulate political, societal, and economic change directly through the masses by censoring the messages and influencers they don't want and giving greater access to the messages and influencers that support their agenda."[9] He cited the [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]]'s $382,997 grant at PolitiFact's 2015 founding as a tangible microcosm of how such an operation is run, while noting the full Pizzagate cover-up was far larger.[9]

## The cognitive battlefield and childhood

Esler extended the doctrine to argue that war has evolved away from the destruction of human life toward dominance over human cognition, so that modern normalization of [[child-sacrifice|child sacrifice]] would take the form of sophisticated mind control operations designed to engineer a culture blind to childhood innocence.[9] Within this framing, [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]]-style trauma programming and the promotion of [[false-memory-syndrome|"False Memory Syndrome"]] are treated as complementary weapons on the same battlefield.[9]

## The "Finite" side and the Death Blossom (November 2022)

In "Death Blossom" (November 23, 2022), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] set out one of the clearest Badlands Media statements of why the globalist side is described as the "Finite" or losing player in the Fifth-Generation War: it is expending valuable psychological, narrative and even kinetic ammunition at an unsustainable rate and to increasingly lackluster or self-defeating results.[10] He explicitly rejected the "Doomer's Refrain" and the cynical, closed-pathway emotional thinking that reads current events as defeat, arguing instead that it is the collective enemy — whether called the [[deep-state|Deep State]], the [[cabal|Cabal]] or any other name for the globalist element — that is behaving desperately.[10]

The essay distinguishes Potential deployments, which exist purely in the realm of the Narrative, from Actual Events in the kinetic or political world, and argues that the most damaging operations in history fuse the two.[10] It also situates the conflict as a [[shadow-war|Shadow War]] fought across Political, Narrative, Cultural and Social domains simultaneously, which Burning Bright collectively labelled [[the-beast|the Beast]], a "System of Systems."[10]

Burning Bright further argued that the decentralized public constitutes a genuine combatant in this form of warfare: patriots inside the military and political structures "cannot be everywhere at once and they cannot fight every battle for us," so the mass investigation, dissection and dissemination of enemy stories before their "First Act" is the community's decisive capability.[10] He closed by framing the conflict as "a War for Peace" and predicting that the World War the enemy seeks would never be actualized.[10]

## AI-driven psychological programming (April 2023)

An April 2023 Badlands essay treated AI-generated media as a uniquely hazardous input to fifth-generation warfare, arguing that AI could be used in psychological operations to generate desired behaviours or effects in a target audience at a scale no human operation could match.[11] It quoted General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]]'s comparison of AI to nuclear warfare: "Foreign-born AI-driven psychological programming has emerged as a formidable weapon in the arsenal of fifth-generation warfare. It wreaks havoc on the social, political and psychological fabric of our nation," causing lasting damage to a nation's core even though its methods of attack differ from conventional ones.[11] The author's proposed countermeasure was to impose "severe physical costs" on AI-generated data through [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] micro-transactions, filtering the noise of machine-produced content out of the information environment.[11]

## Faith as an anchor in 5GW (May 2023)

In "Gaslighting From All Sides" (May 22, 2023), [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that the single asset allowing him to traverse the Fifth-Generation Warfare in which he believed Americans currently exist was his religious faith, grounded in the conviction that God would never abandon him, his family or his country.[12] He compared the experience to a rock climber ascending El Capitan in Yosemite National Park, anchoring safety lines as they climb: the closer together the anchors, the shorter the fall when one slips, and his faith constituted those anchors in the rock face.[12] When events became overwhelming, confusing or seemingly hopeless, he fell back on that anchor and on the corollary that what is being experienced must occur.[12]

Carlson also situated everyday information hygiene within this conflict, noting that he had himself been fooled by fake news and had withheld an unverified claim about Title 42 from a prior Badlands post after failing to corroborate it.[12] He argued that a motivated effort exists to seed false stories specifically on friendly platforms such as [[truth-social|Truth Social]] in order to fool the movement into believing what it wants to hear, and recommended waiting a day or two before commenting on unverified information.[12] He characterized [[gaslighting|gaslighting]] and [[black-pilling|black-pilling]] as the principal weapons in this environment, deployed both by the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and, in his reading, by [[donald-trump|Trump]] and the Patriots for opposite ends.[12]
