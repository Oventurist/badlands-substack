# Trump Defamation Lawsuit Against The New York Times

In September 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that he had filed a $15 billion defamation and libel lawsuit against [[new-york-times|The New York Times]], accusing the paper of serving as a "mouthpiece" for Democrats and of engaging in decades of false reporting.[1]

## Claims

Trump called the suit the "Great Honor" of holding the outlet accountable, and claimed that the Times' endorsement of Kamala Harris amounted to the largest illegal campaign contribution in history, noting that the endorsement had been given front-page placement, "something heretofore unheard of."[1]

In his statement, Trump accused the paper of a longstanding pattern of lying about him, his family, his business, and the America First movement.[1] He likened the case to past legal battles against media outlets, citing prior settlements with networks such as ABC, Disney, and [[cbs|CBS]] over allegations of fabricated and altered material.[1] He argued that the Times' conduct amounted to intentional and malicious defamation, saying the paper had been permitted to "freely lie, smear, and defame me for far too long."[1]

## Filing and framing

The suit was filed in Florida, where Trump vowed the effort would put an end to what he called unacceptable and illegal media abuse.[1] He closed his announcement with his customary sign-off: "Thank you for your attention to this matter. MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!"[1] The case was reported by Badlands Media as a bonus item in its September 2025 news brief.[1]
