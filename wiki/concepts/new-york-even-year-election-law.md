# New York Even Year Election Law

The Even Year Election Law is a 2023 New York State statute that would move most town and county elections to even-numbered years, aligning them with presidential and gubernatorial contests.[1] Proponents have argued that consolidating local races with higher-turnout statewide and federal elections increases voter participation, while critics — especially Republicans — have argued that the law is intended to boost Democratic candidates by folding local contests into partisan federal turnout waves.[1]

## Litigation

In October 2024 state Supreme Court Justice Gerard Neri, sitting in Onondaga County, declared the even-year law unconstitutional.[1] On May 7, 2025, a five-member panel of the Appellate Division, Fourth Department unanimously upheld the law's constitutionality, reversing Neri's decision and clearing the way for implementation beginning in 2026.[1]
