# Iranian Revolution

The Iranian Revolution of 1979 overthrew [[mohammad-reza-pahlavi|Mohammad Reza Shah]] and created the radical Islamic state that governs Iran today.[1]

## Background

The Consortium Agreement of 1954, which had restored British ownership of Iran's oil fields after the [[1953-iranian-coup|1953 coup]], was set to expire in 1979. The intervening decade saw the 1973 Yom Kippur War, the resulting oil crisis in the West, and the 1974 settlement negotiated by Secretary of State [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] with Saudi King Faisal that created the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]], placing Aramco atop the industry while Iran remained subservient to Britain.[1]

## The Rex Theater and the revolution

According to [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], when it became clear the disillusioned Shah intended to refuse renewal of the Consortium Agreement, the [[cia|CIA]] declassified its own documents in 1978, revealing to the Iranian people that their government was a puppet state created and managed by the Anglosphere. Operatives dressed as the Shah's secret police then chained shut the doors of the 500-person Rex Theater during a show and burned the occupants alive with jet fuel — the catalyst that ignited the revolution.[1]

## Aftermath

The revolution produced a radical Islamic state, which in turn led to an ideological arms race with Saudi Arabia over which Islamic sect, Sunni or Shia, could become the more orthodox society. That contest was fueled by madrassas built throughout the Middle East by [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]]'s National Security Advisor [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] as a gesture of goodwill toward his operative [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]], who was spearheading the mujahedeen movement against the Soviets under [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]].[1]

Blinken later attributed the failure of American regime-change efforts in Iran to the absence of diplomatic relations since April 1980, when President Carter severed ties following the 1979 hostage crisis.[1]
