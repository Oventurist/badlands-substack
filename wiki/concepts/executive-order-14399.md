# Executive Order 14399

Executive Order 14399 is an order of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] concerning election integrity, cited as the legal basis for proposed federal rulemaking on the handling of election mail.

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]] proposed new regulations governing mail-in and absentee ballots for federal elections expressly citing the order. Under the proposal, ballots for federal general, special, and runoff elections would be required to use USPS-approved envelope designs bearing the official Election Mail logo and unique Intelligent Mail barcodes on both outbound and return envelopes. Election officials would be required to submit ballot-mailing and participation data through a new Federal Ballot Mail Portal, including information used to associate mailed ballots with official election records and to support reconciliation of ballot mailings. USPS also proposed a verification process before accepting outbound ballot mailings from election officials, with non-compliant mailings returned for correction.

The agency stated that the changes were intended to create a standardised process for tracking and verifying election mail, to provide greater visibility into ballots moving through the postal system, and to support the objectives outlined in the executive order.
