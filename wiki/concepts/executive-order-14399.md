# Executive Order 14399

**Executive Order 14399** is a presidential executive order on election integrity issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], cited by the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]] as the authority behind its June 2026 proposed rulemaking on election mail.[1]

## USPS implementation

USPS proposed new regulations governing the handling of mail-in and absentee ballots for federal elections, stating that the changes were intended to support the objectives outlined in Executive Order 14399 while improving the handling and monitoring of election mail.[1] Under the proposed rule, ballots for federal general, special, and runoff elections would be required to use USPS-approved envelope designs featuring the official Election Mail logo and unique Intelligent Mail barcodes on both outbound and return envelopes.[1]

Election officials would also be required to submit ballot-mailing and participation data through a new Federal Ballot Mail Portal, including information used to associate mailed ballots with official election records and to support reconciliation of ballot mailings.[1] The proposal would establish a verification process before USPS accepts outbound ballot mailings from election officials, with non-compliant mailings returned for correction.[1] USPS said the framework would create a standardized process for tracking and verifying election mail and provide greater visibility into ballots moving through the postal system ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]].[1]
