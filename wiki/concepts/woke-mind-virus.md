# Woke Mind Virus

The "Woke Mind Virus" is a term used by critics to describe the spread of what they characterise as Marxist or neo-Marxist ideologies through Western institutions, particularly education, framed deliberately in the language of contagion and infection.[1]

## Framing

In this account, a silent war is being waged in academia for the minds of the young, conducted through a web of Marxist ideas rather than through conventional politics or activism.[1] The virus metaphor is extended to the response: the academic collective is described as an immune system identifying a sickness and developing the capacity to defend against it, with organisations such as the [[heterodox-academy|Heterodox Academy]] representing a crystallisation of that process.[1]

## Manifestations in education

Faculty accounts gathered at a Toronto screening of [[the-coddling-of-the-american-mind|The Coddling of the American Mind]] identified a surge in students seeking special accommodations, armed with letters demanding preferential treatment and extended deadlines.[1] [[leigh-rivers|Leigh Rivers]] of the [[university-of-toronto|University of Toronto]] raised the phenomenon first; a former instructor at the [[ontario-college-of-art-and-design|Ontario College of Art and Design]] reported that it appeared earlier at art schools.[1] Rivers' sanctioning over a [[spectrum-street-epistemology|Spectrum Street epistemology]] exercise is cited as evidence that even the physical sciences are no longer insulated, particularly since gender theory's entry into human biology.[1]

The influence is described as extending downward into kindergarten-through-grade-12 education, the subject of a master's thesis by an attendee at the same screening.[1] Commentators argue that having the academic class itself study and document this penetration carries distinct tactical and strategic value against those deploying it.[1]

## Escape and recovery

The virus framing carries an implied cure. Students profiled in *The Coddling of the American Mind* are presented as having recovered, describing themselves as having been extremely depressed and unhappy under the ideology's influence and as happier, with joy and purpose, afterward.[1] Their descriptions of how they deconstructed the ideology are treated as empirical [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] weaponry, alongside the more extreme testimony of [[detransitioners|detransitioners]].[1] One student's account of being taught that curing autism amounts to an "autism genocide" is used to show the staged mechanics of the propaganda.[1]

Critics link the phenomenon to [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]] as described by [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]], arguing that it constitutes a subtler form of mind control than programmes such as [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] — requiring no trauma rituals or neurological technology, only generational patience.[1] The [[2024-pro-palestinian-campus-protests|2024 pro-Palestinian campus protests]] are cited as its most visible current expression.[1]
