# Syria–Turkey Counterterrorism Cooperation (2025)

In October 2025, the Syrian Arab Republic and Turkiye agreed to collaborate on counterterrorism along their shared border in order to enhance military cooperation between the two countries.[1] The arrangement was reported by the SANA news agency following a high-level Syrian delegation's visit to Ankara.[1]

## The Ankara talks

On a Sunday in mid-October 2025, Syrian Foreign Minister Assaad Hassan Al-Shaibani discussed the issues with Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan, Defense Minister Yasar Guler, and General Intelligence Chief Ibrahim Kalın.[1] Syria's Defense Minister Maj. Gen. Murhaf Abu Qasra and General Intelligence Chief Hussein Al-Salama also joined the delegation that traveled to the Turkish capital.[1] The talks focused on common issues in counterterrorism, border control, enhancing regional stability, and bilateral cooperation, and the two sides discussed joint training to improve coordination.[1]
