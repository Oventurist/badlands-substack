# Wireless Festival

The Wireless Festival is a London music festival at which [[kanye-west|Kanye West]] was scheduled to headline all three nights in July 2026 as part of a European tour.[1] The booking drew criticism over West's past antisemitic statements.[1] Pepsi and Diageo withdrew sponsorship from the festival following the backlash, and British Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] said the decision to book West was "deeply concerning."[1]
