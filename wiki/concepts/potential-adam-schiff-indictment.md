# Potential Adam Schiff Indictment

The potential indictment of Senator [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] of California refers to reporting in November 2025 that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Justice Department was preparing charges against the Democratic senator, a prospect that alarmed his colleagues in the [[us-senate|Senate]].[1]

## Context

After securing indictments of former [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] and New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]], Trump set his sights on Schiff, who as a member of the House had managed the president's first impeachment trial.[1] Politico reported that Trump's retribution campaign against his political adversaries could soon reach the Senate, and that lawmakers were bracing for impact.[1]

If Schiff were indicted on allegations of mortgage fraud — a charge he has vehemently denied — or on any other claim, it would mark an unprecedented escalation in targeting an outspoken political adversary who is also a federal elected official.[1]

## Reaction

Schiff solicited money for a legal defense fund and built an expansive political operation prepared to do damage control around any potential charges.[1] His Democratic colleagues in [[congress|Congress]] grew increasingly anxious about their own vulnerability and frustrated at the unwillingness of Republican senators to speak out on Schiff's behalf.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Democrats could not believe Republicans were declining to defend Schiff against what she framed as Trump bringing him to justice, and argued that anyone standing up for Schiff after his conduct during Trump's first administration should be considered complicit, investigated and prosecuted accordingly.[1] She contended that Schiff and his co-conspirators, including Republican ones, knew they would be caught and put the country through it anyway, closing with "No deals."[1]
