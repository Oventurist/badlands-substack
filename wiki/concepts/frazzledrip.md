# Frazzledrip

**Frazzledrip** is the name given in online circles to an alleged video said to have been stored in a folder labeled "insurance" on the laptop of Anthony Weiner.[1] The claim has circulated for years alongside the related [[pizzagate|PizzaGate]] allegations and has never been substantiated publicly.[1]

## 2026 congressional hearing

The term entered mainstream congressional proceedings in March 2026, when [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] testified before a House committee as part of inquiries tied to the late financier [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] According to Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], as Clinton was about to be asked about the existence of the video, her attorneys objected forcefully before the question could be completed.[1]

Ashe described the response as "outsized for the stimulus" and "not definitive, but eyebrow raising," while noting that the very fact Clinton was asked the question directly was itself astounding.[1] The questions on PizzaGate and Frazzledrip were posed by Representative [[lauren-boebert|Lauren Boebert]], whom Ashe credited with courage for raising them.[1]
