# 2023 Ukrainian Corruption Scandal

The 2023 Ukrainian corruption scandal was a mass wave of resignations and dismissals among senior Ukrainian government officials, confirmed by the Ukrainian government on Tuesday, January 24, 2023, and described at the time as the biggest mass resignation and graft scandal since the beginning of the Russian invasion.[1]

## Scope of the shake-up

Roughly a dozen officials quit their posts amid a political shake-up driven by allegations and active probes spanning bribery, mismanagement of aid funds earmarked for food purchases, and embezzlement, as well as reputational complaints such as officials driving expensive cars while ordinary Ukrainians suffered under wartime conditions.[1] Among those forced out were a top presidential adviser, four deputy ministers — two of them defense officials — and five regional governors.[1] The departing governors included officials overseeing regions of intense fighting, among them the Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions, where Russian forces had recently reported gains.[1] The scandal was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] said the misuse of funds for personal gain by top Ukrainian officials surprised no one, characterizing the region as a longstanding hotbed of corrupt activity.[1] He argued that globalist actors used the area for staple illicit enterprises — human trafficking, sex trafficking, drug trafficking, weapons trafficking and blackmail — which he framed as instruments for degrading a culture's moral fabric in service of what he called the cabal machine.[1] Given that, in his account, the Ukrainian deep state had worked with Biden-era globalists for years, Deschamps questioned the timing of the sudden purge and asked whether a cleanup was under way, or whether [[joe-biden|Biden]] and the scandals surrounding him had brought unwanted attention to the region such that scapegoats were being produced to create the illusion of anti-corruption reform.[1] He favored the latter reading, noting that Biden and other supporters of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]] had proposed billions more in aid to what he termed a vassal state of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1]
