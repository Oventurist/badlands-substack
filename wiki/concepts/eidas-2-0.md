# eIDAS 2.0

eIDAS 2.0 is the revised European Union regulatory framework for electronic identification and trust services, under which the EU mandates digital identity across its member states.[1]

## Provisions

As reported by Forbes and summarized by Badlands Media, eIDAS 2.0 was to go into effect in September 2023 and would ensure that all EU Member States offer a digital identity wallet (DIW) to citizens and businesses.[1] The European Commission set an accompanying target that at least 80% of citizens should be able to use a digital ID solution to access key public services by 2030.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands account

Badlands treats eIDAS 2.0 as the first leg of a "trifecta" of globalist institutions imposing [[digital-identification|digital identification]], alongside the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1] The EU's digital COVID-19 certification system, developed in the same regulatory environment, was subsequently adopted by the WHO as the basis for the [[global-digital-health-certification-network|Global Digital Health Certification Network]], making the European framework the de facto template for a worldwide system.[1]
