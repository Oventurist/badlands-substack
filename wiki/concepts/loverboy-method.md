# Loverboy Method

The "Loverboy Method" is a term for a pattern of pimping and coercion in which a trafficker feigns a romantic relationship with a target in order to secure emotional dependence before exploiting her.[1] In commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], the term is used to describe the technique by which the United Kingdom's [[grooming-gangs|rape and grooming gangs]] procure underage children.[1]

## Description in Badlands commentary

The January 28, 2025 article "On Golden Bridges and the Art of War," by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], identified the Loverboy Method as the characteristic recruitment technique of the UK grooming gangs, describing it as a method "of pimping and coercion" used to procure underage children.[1]

## Alleged use by a Western influencer

The article stated that a "VERY famous so-called 'right wing' influencer" stood accused of using the Loverboy Method to groom underage girls and of having sold courses teaching other men to do the same; the author noted that this man came from a part of England where the grooming gangs had notoriously operated for decades, but declined to name him out of stated respect for Badlands Media, reserving such identifications for the author's personal Substack.[1] The author observed that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] appeared to support and promote this figure, and argued that rather than indicating complicity this was an instance of the [[golden-bridge-strategy|golden bridge]] tactic: the more Musk promoted him, the more famous the Loverboy Method would become, and the better the public would be able to recognize trafficking and grooming where it had previously gone unnoticed.[1]

The article further suggested that the influencer was connected to a broader network of popular "right wing" figures, and that this network was being targeted for demolition by way of making its members and their tactics famous.[1] The author asserted that many people on the political right were implicated in activities of the same kind as those of the UK gangs.[1]
