# 2025 India–Pakistan Conflict

The 2025 India–Pakistan conflict was a short but intense military escalation between the two nuclear-armed South Asian states, triggered by the [[pahalgam-terror-attack|Pahalgam terror attack]] in Kashmir and ended in part through American diplomatic intervention.[1]

## Outbreak

The crisis began after a terrorist attack on April 22, 2025, in Kashmir, which killed 26 people, mostly Hindu tourists.[1] In response, India launched strikes against terror camps in Pakistan and Pakistan-occupied Kashmir.[1] Pakistan retaliated, and the conflict soon involved drone incursions and air force dogfights.[1]

## US involvement

When tensions escalated, the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] at first showed little interest in stepping in.[1] Rising concern over the possibility of a nuclear exchange between the two neighbors changed that calculus, and top US officials were, by this account, forced to intervene as the threat of full-scale war loomed.[1] By the weekend, Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] intervened, fearing a nuclear confrontation, according to reporting by The New York Times relayed by India Today.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] urged readers to note the framing of the coverage: an India Today piece citing the New York Times that painted [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s decision-making as so chaotic that Vance and Rubio had to step in to prevent nuclear war — a characterization she called absurd on its face.[1] She argued that nuclear war is the favored threat of the neoconservative war machine, and that the story cast Rubio and Vance as the serious adults in the room in opposition to the president.[1] In her reading the piece was a narrative deployment, because peace is a devastating prospect for the powers profiting from war, and the narrative of war must therefore continue.[1] She added a side note that she would be surprised if Rubio lasted through the end of the year, while Vance had a much longer character arc whose direction remained unsettled.[1]

## Pakistani "credible intelligence" warning (April 30, 2025)

Roughly a week after the Pahalgam massacre and before the exchange of strikes, Pakistan's Information Minister [[attaullah-tarar|Attaullah Tarar]] claimed early on Wednesday, April 30, 2025 that Islamabad had "credible intelligence that India intends carrying out military action against Pakistan in the next 24-36 hours," posting the warning on X in the middle of the night without elaborating on the underlying evidence.[2] The claim came as both the United States and China urged restraint.[2] India had accused Pakistan of involvement in the attack, which killed 26 tourists in the mountainous town of Pahalgam in Indian-administered Kashmir; Islamabad denied the accusation and offered a neutral investigation.[2] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] commented that "the Regime will take more war wherever they can get it."[2]
