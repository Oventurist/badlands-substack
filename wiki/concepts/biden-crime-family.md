# Biden Crime Family

"Biden Crime Family" is the term used across the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] to describe the alleged network of influence-peddling and foreign-money arrangements surrounding [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and his relatives, most prominently [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1]

## Framing in Badlands commentary

Writing in the July 28, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that while media attention focused on each new indictment of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a "very REAL [[racketeer-influenced-and-corrupt-organizations-act|RICO]] case" appeared to be unfolding — or being disclosed — before the American public in the form of the Biden Crime Family.[1] In this reading, Trump had baited the Bidens into seeking the office that many in the community believe he willingly abdicated as part of the [[devolution|Devolution]] operation.[1]

## Related disclosures

The censorship of the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]] story before the 2020 election is treated within this framing as evidence of institutional protection for the family, a case detailed in testimony by former [[new-york-post|New York Post]] editor [[emma-jo-morris|Emma-Jo Morris]] before the [[house-weaponization-committee|House Weaponization Committee]].[1]

## Bobulinski testimony (February 2024)

On February 13, 2024, former Biden business associate [[tony-bobulinski|Tony Bobulinski]] testified behind closed doors before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight]] and [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary]] Committees that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] sat at the center of the family's overseas dealings.[2] Bobulinski said Biden was not merely aware of the influence-peddling operation run by [[hunter-biden|Hunter]] and [[james-biden|James Biden]] during his vice presidency but actively enabled it, describing him as "the Brand" the family sold.[2] The operation, he said, ran "from China to Ukraine and elsewhere" and sold access to Biden and the U.S. government to foreign actors, with the president buffered by "a complex scheme to maintain plausible deniability."[2] His account echoed the July 2023 testimony of [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]], a former [[burisma|Burisma Holdings]] board member alongside Hunter Biden.[2]

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argued that Biden's role in these arrangements required a functioning and intelligent operator, and that the contrast with his later incapacity — documented in the [[robert-hur|Hur]] report — showed how the networks he served discard their own once useful roles change.[2]

## The Shokin ouster and the 2023 European Commission report

A central pillar of the Biden crime family narrative is the claim that then-Vice President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] withheld U.S. aid from Ukraine until Kyiv fired Prosecutor-General [[viktor-shokin|Viktor Shokin]], who was investigating [[burisma]] — the company paying [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] roughly $83,000 per month.[3] In September 2023 the New York Post published a December 2015 [[european-commission|European Commission]] progress report praising Shokin's anti-corruption work, dated nine days after Biden demanded his removal, which Badlands treated as refuting the official-foreign-policy defense.[3] Combined with [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]]'s testimony that Burisma pressured the Bidens and [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]]'s on-the-record praise of Shokin, the argument advanced by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] was that in any other reality the Bidens would already have been arrested.[3]

## Oversight subpoena plans, August 2023

In August 2023, [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] said he intended to subpoena members of the Biden family, telling Fox Business that "this is always going to end with the Bidens coming in front of the committee" and that his panel was "putting together a case" in expectation that the subpoenas would be litigated in court.[4] Reporting at the time placed Hunter Biden–linked foreign proceeds at roughly $20 million, including transfers connected to Russia, Ukraine and Kazakhstan.[4]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the Biden corruption storyline and the Justice Department's pursuit of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as parallel narratives in what he called the War of Stories: one delivering near-daily disclosures of Biden corruption with no visible justice, the other exposing a two-tier justice system.[4] He predicted the first narrative would eventually "collapse into the realm of the Real" while the hunt against Trump would fall apart before the nation's eyes, both serving to prepare the collective mind for what he termed the real "Witch Hunt."[4]
