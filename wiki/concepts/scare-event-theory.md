# Scare Event Theory

The "Scare Event" theory is an analytical framework advanced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] describing the characteristics a mass psychological event would need to possess in order to function as an inflection point in the public awakening he and other Badlands writers anticipate.[1]

## The four criteria

In his "Scare Event" feature, Burning Bright laid out four objectives such an event must hit to qualify: it must be frightening; it must be all-consuming; it must be immediate; and it must be unifying.[1]

## Preparation of the "Collective Mind"

He argued that a long series of narrative shocks — from COVID and Big Pharma to globalism, energy, collusion, election fraud, precedent, and the weaponization of intelligence agencies — had already worn grooves and planted seeds within the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] the movement seeks to wake.[1] On that basis he proposed reframing the concept under the term "Unifying Event," describing the expected sequence as trauma, catharsis, awakening, unification and, finally, justice.[1]

## Application to Gaza

Assessing the Israel–Gaza war and specifically the [[jabaliya-refugee-camp-strike|Israeli strike on the Jabaliya refugee camp]] of October 31, 2023, Burning Bright judged that the situation did not yet satisfy the four criteria, but wrote that it was "bringing us closer to an event that does."[1]
