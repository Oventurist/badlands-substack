# Colorado Capitol Trump Portrait Dispute

The Colorado Capitol Trump portrait dispute was a March 2025 controversy in which President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly demanded that the state of Colorado remove a painting of him hanging in the State Capitol, which he described as "purposefully distorted."[1]

## Trump's statement

In a Sunday post on Truth Social, Trump wrote that "nobody likes a bad picture or painting of themselves, but the one in Colorado, in the State Capitol put up by the Governor, along with all other Presidents, was purposefully distorted to a level that even I, perhaps, have never seen before."[1] He added that the same artist had also painted President Barack Obama, who "looks wonderful," while the portrait of himself was "truly the worst," and remarked that the artist "must have lost her talent as she got older."[1] Trump said he would prefer having no picture at all to keeping the one on display, and stated that many Coloradans had contacted the state seeking to have it changed or removed.[1]
