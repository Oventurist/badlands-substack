# Operation Underworld

**Operation Underworld** was the Second World War arrangement by which the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]], the wartime predecessor of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], cultivated working relationships with Italian and Jewish organised crime networks.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterises the bargain in blunt terms: the criminal syndicates would assist the American war effort, and in exchange the government would look the other way while they trafficked heroin.[1]

DeLarme describes the operation as "little-known but supremely important," and points readers to [[whitney-webb|Whitney Webb]]'s *One Nation Under Blackmail* for a thorough treatment of the merger, noting that aspects of it were dramatised in HBO's *Boardwalk Empire*.[1]

## Significance

In DeLarme's personal assessment, Operation Underworld marks the moment at which the Western intelligence community permanently fused with international organised crime, producing the arrangement later described as the [[deep-state|deep state]].[1] The pattern established during the war — tolerating narcotics trafficking by useful allies — recurs throughout the history DeLarme traces in the [[war-on-drugs|War on Drugs]], from the [[french-connection|French Connection]] in Marseille to the [[golden-triangle|Golden Triangle]] and beyond.[1]
