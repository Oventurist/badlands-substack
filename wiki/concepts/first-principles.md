# First Principles

**First Principles** refers to the practice of reasoning from foundational, independently verifiable premises rather than from inherited conclusions, consensus or the framing supplied by others. In classical usage the term denotes the irreducible starting points of an argument; in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] corpus it functions as a methodological standard held up against the habits of both mainstream and alternative commentary.

## Use in the Badlands corpus

[[badlands-blitz-episode-12|Badlands Blitz Episode 12]] names First Principles as the third of three subjects, alongside [[narrative-warfare|Narrative Warfare]] and [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]], that hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argue "a world—and a [[truth-community|Truth Community]]—that has quite forgotten them" needs to recover. The criticism is directed inward as much as outward: the episode uses the contradictory claims about the Iranian Supreme Leader, [[ali-khamenei|Ayatollah Ali Khamenei]], as evidence that audiences within the alternative-media space adopt narratives on the basis of who is telling them rather than on the basis of what can actually be established. Reasoning from first principles is offered as the corrective — a discipline that would allow an observer to hold that a story may be staged in its presentation while the underlying conflict remains real, without collapsing into either credulity or reflexive dismissal.
