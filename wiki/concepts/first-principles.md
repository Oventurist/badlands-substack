# First Principles

**First Principles** refers to reasoning from foundational, independently verified premises rather than from inherited conclusions or prevailing narratives.[1] The term is used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators as a methodological standard for analysis, and it was one of three stated themes of the twelfth episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], published 28 March 2026.[1]

## Treatment in The Blitz Episode 12

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that first principles, along with [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] and [[sovereign-disentanglement|sovereign disentanglement]], have been "quite forgotten" both in the wider world and inside the [[truth-community|Truth Community]].[1] The episode used the mutually contradictory reports about the Iranian Ayatollah — treated as simultaneously a fabricated story and a real and serious matter — to illustrate why analysts must return to foundational reasoning rather than adopting whichever narrative arrives first.[1]
