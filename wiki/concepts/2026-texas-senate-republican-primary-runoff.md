# 2026 Texas Republican Senate Primary Runoff

The **2026 Texas Republican Senate primary runoff** was the deciding contest for the Republican nomination to [[texas|Texas]]'s U.S. Senate seat, in which state Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] defeated four-term incumbent Senator [[john-cornyn|John Cornyn]].

## Result

According to the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026, Paxton won with roughly 63% of the vote to Cornyn's 37%. The runoff was necessitated by a competitive March primary in which neither candidate secured a majority. The outcome is described in the source as one of the biggest primary upsets in modern Texas political history.

## Campaign

Paxton, characterized as a hard-charging conservative and strong ally of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] known for legal battles against the Biden administration, campaigned as a fighter against the Washington establishment. Cornyn, a longtime Senate insider and former Majority Whip, was portrayed by Paxton as out of touch. The race featured heavy spending on attack ads, and Trump's endorsement of Paxton days before early voting is credited in the article with energizing the MAGA base and proving decisive.

In his victory remarks Paxton said, "Texas has spoken loud and clear. We're done with business as usual." The source concludes that the result signals a further rightward shift within the Texas Republican Party, with voters favoring Trump-aligned candidates over establishment figures.
