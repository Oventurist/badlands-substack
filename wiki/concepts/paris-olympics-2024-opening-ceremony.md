# Paris Olympics 2024 Opening Ceremony

The **opening ceremony of the 2024 Paris Olympics**, staged in late July 2024, became an immediate global controversy over a tableau widely read as a parody of the Last Supper. In Badlands Media commentary the ceremony is treated less as a cultural misjudgment than as a deliberately engineered provocation.

## The controversy

The ceremony offended many people, in the view of [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] rightfully so — but he argued that offense was the point, and that anger was exactly the reaction its designers wanted from the world.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) Objection was described as effectively universal across religious and non-religious lines: Jews, Christians, Muslims, Hindus, Buddhists, and agnostics alike were said to be offended.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

Particular attention fell on what Carlson called the blasphemous Transgender Last Supper and on the figure seated at the head of the table, whom he described in terms of stacked demographic categories, arguing that the composition appeared calculated to maximize hate and disgust — that one could scarcely check off any more boxes.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

## As distraction

The ceremony is placed within a broader [[distraction-operations|distraction]] framework. The questions posed are what the spectacle distracts from and whom the resulting division serves: a divided nation is a weak nation, a divided world is a dangerous world, and divided, distracted people are more easily controlled.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) The concrete answer offered is that while the Olympic controversy saturated social media, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was speaking at the [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] Conference about making Bitcoin an important part of the American economy and about the US Government stockpiling it.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

Carlson described his own experience as a worked example: he was as offended as everyone else on first seeing posts about the opening-ceremony debauchery, but by the next day, when every other post in his feed was about the Friday-evening blasphemy, he concluded he was being manipulated by the [[deep-state|Deep State]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

## Transgender participation argument

The article pairs the ceremony with a claim about Olympic eligibility rules, arguing that the [[international-olympic-committee|Olympic Committee]] had essentially barred transgender athletes who did not fully transition by the age of twelve on the grounds of unfair advantage — and that this exposes the ceremony's inclusivity as insincere.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) If the committee truly cared about transgender people and their rights, the argument runs, it would let them compete; the conclusion drawn is that they do not care, and that transgender people are being used by the [[cabal|Cabal]] to upset and distract the world without understanding that they are being used, while spectators do not understand that they are being purposely manipulated by the show.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)
