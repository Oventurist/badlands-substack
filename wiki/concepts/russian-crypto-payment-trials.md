# Russian crypto payment trials

The Russian crypto payment trials were experiments begun on 1 September 2024 in which [[russia|Russia]] tested cryptocurrency exchanges and the use of digital tokens for cross-border transactions, intended to ease payment difficulties for companies hit by international sanctions.[1]

## Framework

Bloomberg reported that Russia would use the National Payment Card System for swapping between rubles and cryptocurrencies when testing payments and the exchange platform.[1] Russia's parliament had in July 2024 passed bills legalising crypto mining and creating a framework to allow the testing of digital tokens for cross-border payments under supervision by the central bank, and President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] signed the bills into law on 8 August.[1]

The experiment came as Russian businesses faced growing obstacles in paying overseas suppliers and in getting paid for exported commodities, challenges stemming from a U.S. move in June 2024 that widened the parameters for determining whether to impose sanctions on foreign banks working with Russia.[1] As recently as January 2022, weeks before Russia's full-scale invasion of [[ukraine|Ukraine]], the central bank had proposed a blanket ban on the use and creation of cryptocurrencies.[1]
