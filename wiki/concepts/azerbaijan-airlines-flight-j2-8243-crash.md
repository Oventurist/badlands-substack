# Azerbaijan Airlines Flight J2-8243 Crash

The crash of Azerbaijan Airlines Flight J2-8243 occurred on a Wednesday in late December 2024 near the city of Aktau in Kazakhstan, killing dozens of people aboard an aircraft that had flown hundreds of miles off its scheduled route.[1]

## The crash

The Embraer 190 passenger jet was flying from [[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]] to Russia with 62 passengers and five crew on board, according to Kazakh authorities.[1] As of that Wednesday afternoon those authorities reported 38 dead and 29 injured survivors.[1] The flight had crossed the Caspian Sea to crash on the opposite shore from its intended route, and officials did not immediately explain why.[1]

## Attribution

The crash came shortly after drone strikes hit southern Russia, and drone activity had previously shut airports in the area; the nearest Russian airport on the plane's flight path was closed on the morning of the crash.[1] Russia's aviation watchdog said the event was an emergency that may have been caused by a bird strike.[1] Coverage of the incident nonetheless attributed blame to Russia.[1]
