# 2025 Shenzhou Space Station Stranding

The 2025 Shenzhou space station stranding refers to the situation in which three Chinese astronauts were left aboard the [[tiangong-space-station|Tiangong space station]] without a dedicated return vehicle after a spacecraft was damaged by suspected space debris.[1]

## Sequence of events

The astronauts were part of the Shenzhou-21 mission, a six-month rotation aboard China's space station, and were left with no means of returning to Earth after the previous mission's Shenzhou-20 capsule was damaged.[1] The Shenzhou-20 spacecraft was struck by suspected space debris, delaying its crew's return by over a week.[1] Rather than fly home in the damaged vehicle, the Shenzhou-20 crew returned aboard the Shenzhou-21 capsule, which had just delivered their replacements to the station — a decision that left the incoming crew without a dedicated return craft.[1]

## Official response

The [[china-manned-space-agency|China Manned Space Agency]] stated that the Shenzhou-20 spacecraft "does not meet the requirements for the astronauts' safe return and will remain in orbit to continue relevant experiments."[1] A new Shenzhou-22 spacecraft was expected to be launched to retrieve the stranded crew, but no specific launch date had been announced at the time of reporting.[1]
