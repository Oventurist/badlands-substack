# Ten Days of Darkness

"Ten days. Darkness." is among the most widely circulated phrases from the [[qanon|Q]] drops, appearing in drop #88 of November 5, 2017 and discussed at length in Chapter 11 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series.[1]

## Interpretation

The Chapter 11 author treats the phrase as forward-looking rather than descriptive of anything that had already occurred, noting that Q had stated one drop earlier that much of what he wrote operated on a [[future-proves-past|future proves past]] basis.[1] On that reading, "ten days of darkness" anticipates a future interval in which the internet, communications, and possibly power grids are taken down, during which the adversary described in the drops as "[they]" would saturate the public with fear.[1] The author expects this to occur only when the deep state is so cornered that it cannot maneuver, and expresses the view that such a moment is rapidly approaching.[1]

## Context within drop #88

The remainder of drop #88 dealt with the November 2017 Saudi events. Q stated that attention was deliberately kept on the United States as a red herring while the Saudi purge proceeded, a tactic the author describes as keeping the deep state off balance — a priority under a wartime footing.[1] The drop also addressed the brief takedown of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Twitter account on November 3, which the author reads not as an accident but as an attempt to stifle communication between Trump and the Saudis.[1] Trump's final tweet before the takedown expressed U.S. support for [[saudi-aramco|Aramco]] and the Saudis; the author and others believe that tweet functioned as a "go code" to [[mohammed-bin-salman|Bin Salman]] to begin the takedown of the Saudi royal family.[1] Q also indicated that POTUS was insulated and safe at the time, and that military operations were ongoing inside the United States — operations the author says remain unidentified.[1]
