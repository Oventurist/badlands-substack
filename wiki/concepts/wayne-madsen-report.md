# Wayne Madsen Report

The Wayne Madsen Report is an investigative journalism and analysis outlet founded by Wayne Madsen, a former NSA officer and journalist known for publishing claims about CIA covert operations and political corruption. In the Badlands Media article [[a-family-affair]], the Wayne Madsen Report is cited as the source for key claims about [[jeb-bush]]'s CIA non-official cover status at [[texas-commerce-bank]], his letter to CIA operative Robert Gambino, and the history of Texas Commerce Bank's relationship with the Bush family and the CIA.

## Key citations

According to the article, the Wayne Madsen Report uncovered a letter from Jeb Bush to CIA operative Robert Gambino while Jeb was employed at Texas Commerce Bank, which the outlet interpreted as evidence that Jeb was being trained as a CIA "overseas banking" operative. The report also provided the history of Texas Commerce Bank's "cozy relationship" with [[george-hw-bush]] and [[zapata-offshore]], including details of Jeb's hiring as a CIA NOC through [[james-baker]]'s family.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

The Wayne Madsen Report is cited multiple times in the Badlands Media corpus as a source for claims about CIA covert operations, the Bush family's network, and the intersection of intelligence agencies with international banking. Its claims are presented as investigative journalism that mainstream media has failed to report.
