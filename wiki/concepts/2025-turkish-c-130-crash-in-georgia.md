# 2025 Turkish C-130 Crash in Georgia

On Tuesday, November 11, 2025, a **Turkish Air Force C-130 military transport plane crashed in Georgia**, according to the Turkish Defense Ministry, which later confirmed that all 20 personnel on board, including the crew, were killed.[1]

## Circumstances

The Georgian Interior Ministry said the aircraft had taken off from Azerbaijan and was flying back to Türkiye when it went down in Sighnaghi Municipality, roughly five kilometers from the Azerbaijani-Georgian border.[1] Video from the scene showed the large aircraft spiraling into the ground.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry called the incident "very strange," situating it against weeks of discussion that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] appeared intent on drawing the Turkish military into the security architecture around Israel in order to deter the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] from attacking Muslim security forces — the logic being that because [[turkey|Turkey]] is a [[nato|NATO]] member, [[article-5|Article 5]] would be in play against the Netanyahu government.[1] He observed that the aircraft appeared to come apart in midair with its propellers still spinning, with no apparent explosion, and that the forward and rear fuselage sections separated along clean straight lines and fell without burning, asking how the crash was accomplished and who was behind it.[1]
