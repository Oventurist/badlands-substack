# 2025 DIA Assessment of the Iran Strikes

The 2025 Defense Intelligence Agency assessment of the Iran strikes was a preliminary classified U.S. report, reported publicly on June 24, 2025, concluding that the American bombing of three nuclear sites in [[iran|Iran]] had set back the country's nuclear program by only a few months.[1]

## Findings

According to officials familiar with the findings, the strikes sealed off the entrances to two of the facilities but did not collapse their underground buildings.[1] Before the attack, U.S. intelligence agencies had assessed that if Iran attempted to rush to a bomb it would take about three months; after the U.S. bombing run and days of attacks by the Israeli Air Force, the [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] estimated the program had been delayed by less than six months.[1] The report also said much of Iran's stockpile of enriched uranium had been moved before the strikes, which destroyed little of the nuclear material, and that Iran may have relocated some of it to secret locations.[1]
