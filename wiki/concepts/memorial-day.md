# Memorial Day

Memorial Day is the American federal holiday of remembrance for those who died in military service, described in a 2025 presidential proclamation as "a sacred day of remembrance, reverence, and gratitude for the brave patriots who have laid down their lives in service to our great Nation."[1]

## Prayer for Peace, Memorial Day, 2025

In the "Prayer For Peace, Memorial Day, 2025" presidential action, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] wrote that throughout American history brave men and women have been called to defend the cause of liberty on foreign shores, their noble sacrifices "marked by flag-draped coffins and the silent sorrows of those left behind."[1] The proclamation singled out America's Gold Star Families — "whose sons, daughters, wives, and husbands are among the honored" — as enduring unfathomable heartache, pledging them unwavering support, deepest gratitude, and highest respect, and stated that lives lost in war are a solemn reminder of why the nation must pursue peace through strength.[1]
