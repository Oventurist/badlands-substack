# Kidflix

Kidflix was a darknet child sexual abuse material platform shut down in an international operation announced by [[europol|Europol]] on April 2, 2025.[1] Created in 2021, it is described as having been one of the largest platforms of its kind in the world and the largest to date to have existed in Europe.[1]

## Scale

Europol estimated the platform had roughly 1.8 million users worldwide, with investigative activity coordinated across 35 countries and the network itself spanning more than 30.[1] Before the shutdown the site hosted over 91,000 child pornography videos, with an average of about three and a half videos uploaded every hour.[1] Users paid a fee for the ability to stream and upload videos of child sexual abuse.[1]

## Enforcement

The takedown was directed by the Bavarian State Criminal Police Office in Germany and coordinated through Europol.[1] At the time of the announcement, 79 people had been arrested and nearly 1,400 additional suspects identified, more than one hundred of whom were located in Germany.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributors treated the arrest figures as evidence of selective or truncated enforcement. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that 79 arrests out of a documented user base of nearly two million represented approximately 0.004 percent of known offenders, despite the existence of system log evidence inherent to a technology platform, and questioned why authorities publicized the operation instead of quietly pursuing a larger share of suspects.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted the coincidence of the announcement falling on the same day as the [[liberation-day-tariffs|"Liberation Day"]] tariff announcement.[1]
