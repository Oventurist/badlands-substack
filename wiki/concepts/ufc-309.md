# UFC 309

UFC 309 was a mixed martial arts event held at Madison Square Garden in New York City on Saturday, November 16, 2024, notable politically for the entourage that accompanied President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to the arena roughly two months before his inauguration.[1]

## Trump's entourage

Trump entered the arena alongside [[ufc|UFC]] chief executive [[dana-white|Dana White]], receiving what press accounts described as a hero's welcome from a raucous crowd.[1] Walking in behind him were House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]], billionaire [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], his eldest son [[donald-trump-jr|Donald Trump Jr.]], [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], and former Hawaii Representative [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]], among others.[1] The New York Post characterized the group as offering clues to the president-elect's inner circle as he assembled his administration, noting that nearly all of them sit outside the traditional Washington power structures.[1] During the event, UFC champion Jon Jones gave Trump his heavyweight belt and thanked him in a victory speech.[1]

Bulwark reporter [[marc-caputo|Marc Caputo]] told CNN that the scene "really looks like ancient Rome," describing "a conquering, Republican Caesar, who is going into the Colosseum" with "his political gladiators with him," and argued the appearance was a message to the [[us-senate|Senate]] about Trump's willingness to fight for his nominees.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands Media presented the appearance as evidence that Trump 2.0 functions as a narrative translation layer of the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] and the transition to what it calls the Golden Age, staged as a series of apparently organic convergences.[1] The outlet dubbed the group a "de facto MAGAvengers" walking out into a modern-day colosseum and read the moment as the culmination of a public narrative arc in which several mass-psychological pincers had closed around the central narrative.[1] It added that while the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] was among the most encouraging announcements in modern American history, its memetic origins and the bombast of its leadership had "story" written all over it.[1]
