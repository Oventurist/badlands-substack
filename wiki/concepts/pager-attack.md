# Pager Attack

The pager attack was an Israeli intelligence operation against [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in which pagers distributed to the organization were converted into incendiary devices and detonated — an operation commonly described as the "exploding pagers."[1]

## Invocation by Greenblatt

The operation re-entered US political debate through [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]] of the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]]. A viral video clip shows Greenblatt referencing the "genius" behind [[israel|Israel]]'s pager-based intelligence operation against Hezbollah and saying that similar ingenuity is needed to confront [[antisemitism|antisemitism]].[1]

## Commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] situated the remark within a pattern of Greenblatt statements made while addressing the Israeli Knesset, including an argument that it was important for Israel to purchase [[tiktok|TikTok]] and control that information battlespace — a platform he notes was subsequently purchased by Israeli entities — and a later appearance in which Greenblatt described developing an AI designed to detect antisemitic attitudes online and report them to relevant authorities.[1] He argued that this combination suggests a larger scheme to justify restrictions against the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]], and concluded that Americans may face a choice between maintaining Israel as an ally and keeping the First Amendment.[1]

## Aftermath and strategic consequences

Badlands coverage in September 2024 reported that thousands of pagers and radios belonging to Hezbollah were detonated in a devastating operation widely attributed to Israeli secret services, prompting Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] to declare "a new phase" of the war centered on Lebanon rather than Gaza.[2] Hezbollah said it held "the Israeli enemy fully responsible" for the attacks and promised a "difficult reckoning" in response.[2] Badlands writers argued that the perceived narrative "success" of the operation emboldened the Netanyahu government and initiated a fresh escalation phase for the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]], even as [[centcom|CENTCOM]] commander [[michael-kurilla|Michael Kurilla]] warned Israel it would be on its own in any ground war.[2]
