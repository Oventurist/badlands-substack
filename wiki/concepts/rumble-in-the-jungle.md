# Rumble in the Jungle

The "Rumble in the Jungle" was the heavyweight championship bout between Muhammad Ali and George Foreman held on October 30, 1974, in Kinshasa, in what is now the Democratic Republic of the Congo.[1] It is considered one of the most iconic boxing matches in history, with Ali stunning the world by knocking out Foreman in the eighth round.[1]

## 50th anniversary commemorations

Fifty years later, the Congolese government marked the anniversary with a program of events in Kinshasa.[1] The venue was renamed the Ali-Foreman Stadium, having formerly been known as Stade Tata Raphael.[1] American boxing legend [[mike-tyson|Mike Tyson]] traveled to Kinshasa for the closing events, met with young boxers, and held a press conference at the renamed stadium, where he said, "I would love to be in the same room that Ali and Foreman were in. Yeah, I would like to do it, that'd be amazing."[1] At the close of the commemorations Tyson met Congolese President [[felix-tshisekedi|Felix Tshisekedi]], describing the meeting as a "beautiful experience" and acknowledging his own Congolese heritage.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands commentary treated the anniversary trip less as a sports story than as a possible instance of celebrity-mediated diplomacy, drawing a parallel to the Dennis Rodman–North Korea episode and highlighting the role of agent [[darren-prince|Darren Prince]], who has represented both Tyson and Ali.[1]
