# Federal Organ Donation Review 2026

A federal review reported in May 2026 raised concerns about United States organ donation procedures after investigators found cases in which patients showed signs of life during the organ procurement process, prompting some procedures to be halted.[1]

## Congressional response

Lawmakers including Senator [[ron-wyden|Ron Wyden]] sought answers about oversight and the safeguards meant to ensure that donors are properly declared dead before organ recovery begins.[1] Health officials ordered reforms in response, including stricter safety protocols and increased oversight of organ procurement organizations.[1]

Officials emphasized that organ donation remains a regulated, life-saving system, while acknowledging that the findings demonstrated the need for stronger safeguards.[1] The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] carried the review as a bonus item in its May 4, 2026 edition.[1]
