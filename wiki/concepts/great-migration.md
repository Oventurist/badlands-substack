# Great Migration

The "Great Migration" is a term used in Badlands commentary to describe what contributor Ashe in America characterized as a transformational change program pushed by the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and its partners over the past two decades to reshape the demographic composition of Western nations.[1]

The framing was invoked in May 2026 in connection with the "[[unite-the-kingdom-rally|Unite the Kingdom]]" rally and reports of neighborhood patrols in the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]] and France, with the argument that both the problem statement and proposed solutions in mainstream coverage are the work product of that program, and that opposing it constitutes good sense rather than bigotry.[1]
