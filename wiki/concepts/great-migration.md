# Great Migration

The "Great Migration" is a term used in Badlands commentary to describe what contributor Ashe in America characterized as a transformational change program pushed by the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and its partners over the past two decades to reshape the demographic composition of Western nations.[1]

The framing was invoked in May 2026 in connection with the "[[unite-the-kingdom-rally|Unite the Kingdom]]" rally and reports of neighborhood patrols in the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]] and France, with the argument that both the problem statement and proposed solutions in mainstream coverage are the work product of that program, and that opposing it constitutes good sense rather than bigotry.[1]

## Ireland and the assimilation question (November 2023)

Following the [[parnell-square-stabbing|Dublin stabbing of children]] and the [[2023-dublin-riots|riots]] that followed, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that public debate was fixating on the perpetrator's status as "an immigrant" while missing the larger point raised by [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] and [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]], who described Ireland as "a powder keg" and warned the crisis was "coming here, at scale."[2] She characterised the great migration as a coordinated change effort carried from strategy through execution by globalists over the preceding two decades.[2] Noting that the Dublin attacker had lived in Ireland for 23 years and remained, in her framing, incompatible with Irish or Western culture, she argued that the failure to assimilate was a data point requiring critical examination because the phenomenon was "coming for all of our communities."[2] Setting aside public cost, strain on local infrastructure and the sheer volume of arrivals, she posed the central question of whether assimilation — long treated as a long game — remains likely or even possible.[2]
