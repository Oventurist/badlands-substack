# Draft Wayne Gretzky Movement

The "Draft Wayne Gretzky" movement is a proposal floated by President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on social media in December 2024, urging Canadians to recruit hockey great [[wayne-gretzky|Wayne Gretzky]] to replace Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] amid political upheaval in [[canada|Canada]].[1]

## Trump's post

Writing on Christmas Day 2024, Trump said: "I just left Wayne Gretzky, 'The Great One' as he is known in Ice Hockey circles. I said, 'Wayne, why don't you run for Prime Minister of Canada, soon to be known as the Governor of Canada – You would win easily, you wouldn't even have to campaign.'"[1] Trump added that Gretzky "had no interest, but I think the people of Canada should start a DRAFT WAYNE GRETZKY Movement. It would be so much fun to watch!"[1] The remark came alongside Trump's recurring suggestion that Canada become a US state governed rather than led by a prime minister.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that while many would dismiss the post as trolling, it was a potentially sound political move for Canada.[1] She noted Gretzky turned professional at seventeen, captained four separate professional teams, and holds several of hockey's most durable records, including most career points (2,857) and most career goals (894) — many set with a wooden stick and before modern safety protocols or equipment advances.[1] Since retiring in 1999 he has coached, owned teams and pursued business ventures including a Niagara-area winery, maintaining name recognition and personal brand that she described as a golden political asset.[1] She concluded that a Trump–Gretzky alliance could make Canada a geopolitical contender in the coming global shakeup, while expressing reservations about Canada becoming a full US state.[1]
