# Dark Enlightenment

The "Dark Enlightenment" is a term used to describe a tech-oriented, anti-liberal ideological current that Russian philosopher [[alexander-dugin|Alexander Dugin]] identified in April 2025 as the animating force behind a deeper layer of the American [[deep-state|deep state]].[1]

## Dugin's argument

Dugin argued that the rise of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] represented not merely populism but a deeper, technology-driven ideological shift aimed at accelerating humanity toward the technological singularity by dismantling liberal globalism through an alliance between populist and tech-right forces.[1] In his framing, Trumpists had declared and waged outright war against the deep state and already scored significant victories, such as the closure of [[usaid|USAID]].[1]

According to Dugin, Trumpists define the deep state as a ruling elite adhering to liberal-democratic ideology — both left-wing and neoconservative — deeply entrenched in the U.S. government and supported by financial, military, and high-tech oligarchies, with networks permeating the intelligence agencies.[1] That elite, he wrote, tied the fate of America and the West to globalism, unipolarity, and the planetary spread of woke ideology, including the legalization of perversions, forced ethnic mixing through promoted mass migration, and the weakening of sovereign nation-states.[1]

Against this, Dugin described the MAGA ideology as resting on diametrically opposite principles: traditional values, a binary understanding of gender, protection of peoples from mass and especially illegal migration, strengthened sovereignty, preservation of nation-states, and recognition of a multipolar world that Trump terms the "Great Power Order."[1] He characterized the result as an ideological and geopolitical revolution with far-reaching consequences for international politics.[1]

## Interpretation of Trump's victory

Dugin contended that if his interpretation is correct, it clarifies which force allowed Trump to win — noting that elections can be prevented, as he argued occurred in the 2020 U.S. election and in contemporary European politics — and why deep-state resistance proved relatively easy to overcome: one segment of the deep state, the high-tech sector along with certain factions inside the security and intelligence communities, had already been ideologically reformed along Dark Enlightenment lines.[1] It also explains, in his account, why Trump acts so decisively, describing it not as temperament but as a global plan to accelerate the progression of time itself — a matter of philosophy, strategy, and even metaphysics.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] wrote that Dugin recognizes the existence of a deeper layer of the deep state and the ongoing infiltration of the "Regime" into all sides of the conflict.[1]
