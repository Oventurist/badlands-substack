# The Bibi Files

*The Bibi Files* is a documentary directed by [[alexis-bloom|Alexis Bloom]] and produced by [[alex-gibney|Alex Gibney]] that presents leaked police interrogation videos of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to a public audience for the first time.[1] Its world premiere took place at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 9–10, 2024, after Israeli courts rejected Netanyahu's request to block the screening.[1]

## Premiere and reception

The screening drew a tense and vocal audience, many carrying signs reading "Bring Them Home" and "Deal Now," referring to hostages held in Gaza.[1] Coverage by [[the-guardian|The Guardian]] described the film as "urgent and incendiary" and said it builds a rigorous and damning case around an argument close observers may already know: that Netanyahu is prolonging the war in Gaza — which had amassed more than 40,000 casualties — to avoid possible prison time stemming from corruption charges, making a humanitarian crisis that flouts international law a matter of his self-preservation.[1]

## Contents

The interrogation videos in the film were recorded by police between 2016 and 2018, before charges of corruption were formally brought against Netanyahu.[1] The footage includes Netanyahu addressing allegations that he and his wife [[sara-netanyahu|Sara Netanyahu]] accepted expensive champagne, Cuban cigars and jewelry from the Hollywood producer [[arnon-milchan|Arnon Milchan]]; Netanyahu minimizes the champagne and cigars as gifts from a friend while denying knowledge of the jewelry.[1] Several witnesses who worked for Milchan and the Netanyahus also appear speaking to police, painting a picture of regular gifts expected in exchange for favours — one such favour being a marginal tax break extension that benefited Milchan.[1] Netanyahu argues his unusual interference over the tax break served the good of the state rather than Milchan; Milchan himself corroborated much of the witness testimony, though in one excerpt he gently asks police not to use the word "bribery" because it would make him look bad.[1]

Talking heads in the film include the Israeli journalist [[raviv-drucker|Raviv Drucker]], former Shin Bet head [[ami-ayalon|Ami Ayalon]], and a childhood friend of Netanyahu, who together trace what the film calls a long-running pattern of Netanyahu serving his own interests while clinging to power — from deliberate ploys to sabotage an alliance between the West Bank and Gaza by enabling Hamas, to his alliance with the violent far right and his attempted overhaul of the supreme court.[1]

## Legal context

According to the documentary, Netanyahu's lawyer filed a motion to delay the corruption trial then scheduled for December 2024, citing the ongoing war as the reason.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In the September 11, 2024 *Badlands News Brief*, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the film's findings "sound familiar" because Badlands had been covering the same material for months, and used Milchan's appearance in the story as a pivot into Milchan's intelligence background, the [[apollo-affair|Apollo Affair]], and his production of Oliver Stone's *JFK*.[1]
