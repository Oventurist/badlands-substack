# 2024 Lebanon Pager and Walkie-Talkie Attacks

The 2024 Lebanon pager and walkie-talkie attacks were a two-day covert operation in which thousands of personal communications devices used by [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] members in [[lebanon|Lebanon]] were detonated remotely, an operation attributed to [[israel|Israeli]] intelligence services.[1]

## First wave: pagers

On Tuesday, September 17, 2024, Israeli intelligence services blew up thousands of pager devices used by members of Hezbollah's military units and institutions.[1] At least nine people were killed, including a child, and more than 2,800 were wounded.[1]

## Second wave: two-way radios

On Wednesday, September 18, 2024, a second wave detonated thousands of two-way personal radios used by Hezbollah members, according to two sources with knowledge of the operation who spoke to [[axios|Axios]].[1] Lebanon's health ministry said 14 people were killed and 450 wounded in the Wednesday attacks.[1] The second wave occurred during the afternoon, local time, while funerals were being held for some of the Hezbollah members killed in the first attack.[1]

The walkie-talkies had been booby-trapped in advance by Israeli intelligence services and then delivered to Hezbollah as part of the militia's emergency communications system, which was intended for use during a war with Israel.[1] Analysts characterized the operation as a serious security breach within Hezbollah's ranks that further damaged the group's military command-and-control system and increased pressure on the organization.[1]

## Commentary and criticism

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the operators could not have known who was physically holding each device at the moment of detonation, and were instead acting on a presumption—perhaps informed by intelligence—that many devices would be in Hezbollah hands.[1] He compared the indiscriminate character of the attacks to the mail-bombing campaign of [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]], noting that Kaczynski killed three people and injured 23 across seventeen years, a far smaller toll than the casualties produced in a single 24-hour period in Lebanon.[1]

Reports circulated that the devices had been rigged with explosive materials possibly linked to supply chains in Taiwan or Hungary.[1] Badlands linked the operation to a broader "shift in military strategy" toward "all out war," drawing a parallel to remarks by [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] describing a strategy of targeting Russian civilians to undermine domestic support for [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s war effort—conduct Badlands characterized as terrorism, echoing the description of the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] offered by Russian Foreign Ministry spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]].[1] The commentary framed the [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] government as a regime "descending into madness" whose indifference to child casualties forfeited any claim to benevolence.[1]
