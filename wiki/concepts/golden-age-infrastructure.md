# Golden Age Infrastructure

"Golden age infrastructure" is a term used within Badlands commentary — coined in the outlet's back-channel discussion by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and relayed by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] — to describe US-based artificial intelligence data centers as the physical substrate of a coming American renaissance.[1]

## The Texas data-center fight

A May 2026 report described activist groups connected to the broader funding network associated with [[george-soros|George Soros]] as supporting campaigns opposing major AI and data-center expansion projects in Texas.[1] [[indivisible|Indivisible]] chapters and allied organizations were reported to be involved in local resistance efforts centered on energy use, water consumption, land acquisition and grid strain.[1] Critics of the campaigns argued they could slow Texas' push to become a national AI infrastructure hub, while organizers described their work as focused on community and environmental concerns.[1]

## Ethical framing

Ashe in America described herself as both an early adopter of technology and "a bit of a Luddite," expressing mixed feelings about a centrally planned technological transformation and warning that ignoring ethics for the promise of progress is historically how atrocities happen.[1] At the same time she characterized the emerging tools as a potential miracle capable of breaking humanity's chains and enabling Americans to define and seize new destinies, framing the outcome as a matter of decision rights: what the lines are, and what the golden age actually is.[1]

She also noted that opposition from Indivisible instinctively inclined her to support the projects, while cautioning — following [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]]'s advice — that withholding belief is critical when the public is told if and how it may make change.[1]
