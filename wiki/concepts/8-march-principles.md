# The 8 March Principles

"The 8 March Principles for a Human Rights-Based Approach to Criminal Law Proscribing Conduct Associated with Sex Reproduction, Drug Use, HIV, Homelessness and Poverty" is a report issued by the [[united-nations|United Nations]] in March 2023.[1] Reported by The Gateway Pundit and amplified by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in April 2023, the document drew sharp criticism from conservative and independent media on the grounds that it encouraged member states to decriminalize sexual conduct between minors and adults.[1]

## Contested language

On page 22 the report states that "With respect to the enforcement of criminal law, any prescribed minimum age of consent to sex must be applied in a non-discriminatory manner," and that "Enforcement may not be linked to the sex/gender of participants or age of consent to marriage."[1] It goes on to say that "sexual conduct involving persons below the domestically prescribed minimum age of consent to sex may be consensual in fact, if not in law."[1] Critics read the latter clause as an invitation to soften enforcement of statutory age-of-consent laws.[1]

## Reaction

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that the report demonstrated the UN was "a Godless institution working to reshape the world in the image of wokeness and sexual predation towards children," arguing that its aim was to normalize pedophilia and that a first step had already been taken through promotion of the term "minor attracted person," or MAP, in place of "pedophile" or "child molester."[1] He observed that neither Joe Biden nor the British Royal Family appeared troubled by the document, and predicted escalation toward hiring quotas and celebratory observances.[1]

DeLarme used the episode to raise a broader question about American participation in international institutions, asking why the United States remains involved in the UN at all and arguing that spending on the UN and [[nato|NATO]] is unreasonable relative to the results.[1] He closed by calling the nation's moral trajectory a swift descent into "a modern Sodom and Gomorrah" and urging an end to US participation in globalist institutions.[1]
