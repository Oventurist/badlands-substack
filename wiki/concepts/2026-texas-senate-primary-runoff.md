# 2026 Texas Republican Senate Primary Runoff

The 2026 Texas Republican primary runoff for the state's U.S. Senate seat produced a major upset when Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] defeated four-term Senator [[john-cornyn|John Cornyn]].[1]

## Result

Paxton won with roughly 63% of the vote to Cornyn's 37%.[1] The runoff was forced by a competitive March primary in which neither candidate secured a majority.[1] The result is being described as one of the biggest primary upsets in modern Texas political history.[1]

## Campaign

Paxton, a hard-charging conservative and strong [[donald-trump|Trump]] ally known for legal battles against the Biden administration, campaigned as a fighter against the Washington establishment, while Cornyn — a longtime Senate insider and former Majority Whip — was portrayed as out of touch.[1] The race featured heavy spending on attack ads, and Trump's endorsement of Paxton days before early voting helped energize the MAGA base and proved decisive.[1]

In his victory remarks Paxton said, "Texas has spoken loud and clear. We're done with business as usual."[1] Badlands framed the outcome as signaling a further rightward shift within the Texas Republican Party, with voters favoring Trump-aligned candidates over establishment figures.[1]
