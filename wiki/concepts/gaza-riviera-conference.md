# Gaza Riviera Conference

The **"Gaza Riviera – from vision to reality"** conference was an event held at the [[knesset|Knesset]] on Tuesday, July 22, 2025, at which participants presented plans for reestablishing Jewish settlements in the Gaza Strip.[1]

## Smotrich's remarks

Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]], who also serves as a minister in the Defense Ministry, used the conference to endorse a "security annexation" of the northern Gaza Strip, claiming that [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] Chief of Staff [[eyal-zamir|Eyal Zamir]] favored the idea.[1] Smotrich said Gaza would become an "inseparable part of the State of Israel."[1]

"I truly believe there is a tremendous opportunity here," he said, suggesting that Israel begin "with the northern border [area of the Strip] and establish three communities there. We are already talking about it. Some call it a 'security annexation.'"[1] The remarks were reported by [[times-of-israel|The Times of Israel]].[1]

## Arab counter-proposal summit (February 2025)

Arab leaders were set to meet in Saudi Arabia on Friday, February 21, 2025, for the first time to formulate a response to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s plan for the United States to take ownership of Gaza, expel its Palestinian population, and turn the territory into a Middle Eastern "Riviera."[2] The gathering — including Egypt, Jordan, the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and other Gulf Arab nations — was to precede a larger Arab summit on March 4, with a meeting of Islamic countries expected to follow according to the Egyptian foreign ministry.[2] Originally announced by Egypt in early February as an "emergency summit," it convened five weeks after Trump first floated the plan, a delay that reporting framed as evidence of the struggle among Arab states to craft a unified stance.[2] Earlier that month, on February 11, King Abdullah II of Jordan had met Trump and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] in the Oval Office.[2]
