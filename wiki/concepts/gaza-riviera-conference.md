# Gaza Riviera Conference

The **"Gaza Riviera – from vision to reality"** conference was an event held at the [[knesset|Knesset]] on Tuesday, July 22, 2025, at which participants presented plans for reestablishing Jewish settlements in the Gaza Strip.[1]

## Smotrich's remarks

Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]], who also serves as a minister in the Defense Ministry, used the conference to endorse a "security annexation" of the northern Gaza Strip, claiming that [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] Chief of Staff [[eyal-zamir|Eyal Zamir]] favored the idea.[1] Smotrich said Gaza would become an "inseparable part of the State of Israel."[1]

"I truly believe there is a tremendous opportunity here," he said, suggesting that Israel begin "with the northern border [area of the Strip] and establish three communities there. We are already talking about it. Some call it a 'security annexation.'"[1] The remarks were reported by [[times-of-israel|The Times of Israel]].[1]
