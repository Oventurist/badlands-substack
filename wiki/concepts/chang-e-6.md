# Chang'e-6

Chang'e-6 was a Chinese lunar sample-return mission whose capsule landed in Mongolia on June 24, 2024 after collecting soil samples from the far side of the Moon, setting another world record for China.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents the mission as part of a sustained Chinese advance in space science that followed China's exclusion from cooperation with [[nasa|NASA]] under the 2011 [[wolf-act|Wolf Act]].[1] China had earlier become the first nation to land on the far side of the Moon on January 3, 2019, and landed the first robot on the lunar surface in forty years in 2013.[1]

Ehret emphasizes the strategic role of the far side of the Moon and its bountiful [[helium-3|Helium-3]], describing it as a major game-changing goal for China that ties into the country's drive for commercially viable fusion power.[1] These lunar ambitions are linked to the June 16, 2024 ratification by Russia and China of treaties for a joint permanent scientific base on the Moon, aimed at full industrialization and mining operations by 2040.[1]
