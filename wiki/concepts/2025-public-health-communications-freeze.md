# 2025 Public Health Communications Freeze

The 2025 public health communications freeze was a pause on external communications imposed across the United States federal health bureaucracy during the first days of the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in January 2025.[1] The freeze affected the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]], the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] and the [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]], described in contemporaneous coverage as an explosion hitting "the whole of public health bureaucracies."[1]

Trump personally blamed those bureaucracies in part for the meltdown of the final year of his previous term, and the pause in operations was described as designed to figure out exactly what was going on inside the agencies.[1]

## Reaction and commentary

The [[brownstone-institute|Brownstone Institute]] rejected the claim, advanced by [[paul-krugman|Paul Krugman]] on his Substack, that Trump wanted people to die, noting that Krugman had supported lockdowns and vaccine mandates and had welcomed what he called the great reset while much of the world was confined.[1] Brownstone argued that what actually appeared to be "dying the death" was the public health bureaucracy itself.[1]

The freeze formed part of a broader wave of first-week executive actions reshaping the federal workforce, alongside a hiring freeze, the end of remote work, the removal of job protections and the elimination of diversity programs.[1] See [[2025-federal-workforce-executive-orders|the 2025 federal workforce executive orders]].[1]
