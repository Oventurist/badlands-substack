# Elizabeth Warren DNA Test Episode

The **Elizabeth Warren DNA test episode** refers to the sequence in which [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly goaded Senator [[elizabeth-warren|Elizabeth Warren]] into taking a genetic ancestry test, and the political damage that followed when the result undercut her long-standing claim of Native American heritage.[1]

## Background

Trump had already attached the nickname "Pocahontas" to Warren over her claim of partial Native American ancestry, which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary described as having been asserted in order to secure a position at Harvard Law School at a time when the school was seeking to hire more minority faculty.[1]

## The challenge and the test

At a rally in Montana, Trump challenged Warren to take a DNA test to prove she was what she claimed to be.[1] By the next morning the challenge led coverage on the news channels and became the dominant narrative of the cycle, pressure that in this account encouraged Warren to "take the bait."[1] She took the test, and the result indicated she was only 1/1024th Native American, producing nationwide mockery.[1]

## Aftermath

The Republican Party followed up with political advertising, a talking-points memo, and a talk-show circuit push built around the line that Warren was "1/1024th Native American, 100% Liar."[1]

Contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cited the episode as an early demonstration of Trump's ability to set the media agenda and inflict lasting damage on a rival, and as a partial explanation for the persistent doubt among Democratic voters in the [[2020-democratic-presidential-primary|2020 Democratic presidential primary]] that Warren could defeat Trump — doubt that, despite Warren's momentum in polling and small-dollar fundraising, coexisted with Biden's own decline.[1]
