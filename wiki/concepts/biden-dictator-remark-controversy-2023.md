# Biden Dictator Remark Controversy (2023)

The Biden dictator remark controversy was a June 2023 diplomatic flashpoint in which President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] referred to Chinese leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] as a dictator, prompting a sharp rebuke from Beijing.[1]

## The remark

Speaking on the evening of Tuesday, June 20, 2023, at a campaign fundraising reception at a private residence in Kentfield, California, Biden said Xi had been upset when the United States shot down a Chinese spy balloon in February.[1] According to the official [[white-house|White House]] transcript, Biden said: "China is real — has real economic difficulties. And the reason why Xi Jinping got very upset in terms of when I shot that balloon down with two boxcars full of spy equipment in it is he didn't know it was there... That's what's a great embarrassment for dictators, when they didn't know what happened."[1] He added that the balloon "wasn't supposed to be going where it was," that it "was blown off course up through Alaska and then down through the United States," and that Xi denied it was even there.[1]

## Chinese response

On Wednesday, June 21, [[china|China]]'s Foreign Ministry spokesperson [[mao-ning|Mao Ning]] called the comments "extremely absurd and irresponsible" at a daily press briefing in Beijing.[1] "It is a blatant political provocation. China expresses strong dissatisfaction and opposition," she said.[1] The clash came one day after Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] concluded his visit to Beijing, where he met with Xi amid mounting tensions between the two nations.[1]

## Truth Community commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that in context the remark read less as a deliberate insult than as "a dementia moment," suggesting Biden may have meant it as a term of endearment.[1] She described the broader speech as a rambling, muddled mess in which Biden romanticized his relationship with Xi, citing his claim of 82 hours of recorded one-on-one time over twelve years and an exchange on the Tibetan Plateau in which Xi asked him to define America and Biden answered "possibilities."[1] She noted that Biden also addressed the [[ukraine|Ukraine]] imperative, repeated the Charlottesville "very fine people" claim in altered form, and asserted that 63% of Americans worry about their civil liberties being violated while apparently not believing they blame him.[1] Her bottom line was that tension between Biden and Xi is a fabricated cover for Biden being a CCP puppet.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the episode as part of a broader escalatory posture by the Biden administration that was finally reaching the mainstream, listing Blinken's Beijing meeting being followed by Xi's warning that Washington must choose between "cooperation and conflict"; China pursuing a military-infrastructure strategy in [[cuba|Cuba]] mirroring the US posture in [[taiwan|Taiwan]]; the administration declining to recognize Taiwan as an independent state; and the administration courting [[iran|Iran]] — designated a State Sponsor of Terrorism by its own [[state-department|State Department]] — while straining relations with the [[saudi-arabia|Saudis]].[1] He added that US-supplied missiles were being used in Ukraine's failing counteroffensive, prompting increased Kremlin rhetoric, and that [[russia|Russia]] was calling the United States a state sponsor of Nazism.[1]

## Recurrence at the November 2023 San Francisco summit

Biden repeated the characterization in November 2023, when a wrap-up press conference following his meeting with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] in San Francisco ended on a deeply negative note after he again called Xi a "dictator" in response to a reporter's question.[2] Days later Xi publicly hailed "firm" ties between China and [[russia|Russia]], saying that positive Russia ties would "inject more stability into the world" and that Beijing stood ready to work with Moscow "resolutely" on bilateral relations and a permanent friendship.[2] [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] simultaneously sent Xi a congratulatory letter marking the tenth meeting of the dialogue mechanism between the ruling parties of the two countries, describing their comprehensive strategic partnership of coordination as at the highest level in history.[2]
