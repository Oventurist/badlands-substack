# Taxpayer Theft

"Taxpayer theft" is the organising concept of the article: the claim that the systematic diversion of American public money to hostile actors is the true motive behind war, foreign policy and political violence.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## Money as the keystone

The article asserts that what the enemy values above all else is money, and that control is merely a by-product of it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) Money is described as the *keystone* — the common denominator in all the corruption and treason — which is why the instruction was to follow the money and why "everything is connected to money."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) It follows that anyone who tries to stop the flow of money from taxpayers to the hidden enemy and their puppets is deemed a threat, and that when such a person holds the presidency, extreme measures are used against them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The struggle is framed as one between insiders and outsiders, with [[donald-trump|Trump]] as the outsiders' champion.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## War as cover

The first mechanism examined is war. The article recounts that some $40 billion in cash was flown from the [[federal-reserve-bank-of-new-york|New York Federal Reserve]] to Baghdad between 2003 and 2008, with a meticulous chain of custody on the American side and no paper trail whatsoever after arrival — an omission it argues was purposeful.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) More than half the money is said to have been stolen, with over $1.5 billion later found in a bunker in Lebanon intended to fund Hezbollah, and [[george-w-bush|President Bush]] shutting down the resulting investigation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The article's formulation is that "a 'war zone' has always been the perfect COVER for TAXPAYER THEFT," and that the longer a war runs, the more money can be stolen — hence a twenty-year war in Afghanistan and the question of how many trillions disappeared.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) Libya is described as an even larger money-laundering operation than Iraq.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## Lobbying as cover

The second mechanism is [[lobbying|lobbying]]: groups such as the [[libyan-emergency-task-force|Libyan Emergency Task Force]] and the [[syrian-emergency-task-force|Syrian Emergency Task Force]], both run by [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]], are described as vehicles set up by the enemy so that [[congress|Congress]] would appropriate taxpayer money directly to [[al-qaeda|Al-Qaeda]] and [[isis|ISIS]] affiliates.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The article's conclusion is that politics is one big scam, that both parties are stealing the same money, and that the American taxpayer is "one giant piggy bank."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)
