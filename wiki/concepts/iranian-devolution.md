# Iranian Devolution

"Iranian Devolution" is the term used in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] for a Continuity of Government (COG) protocol said to have been implemented by [[iran|Iran]] in August 2025.[1] It is an application of the broader [[devolution|devolution]] framework developed in Badlands commentary.[1]

## Rationale and mechanics

According to [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], the protocol was adopted because Iranian authorities had determined that [[mossad|Mossad]] and the [[cia|CIA]] had likely infiltrated the highest levels of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] and the intelligence community.[1] The COG measures effectively abolished the control hierarchy, removed the Ayatollah from the chain of command, and devolved political and military powers to regional governors and lower-level commanders.[1]

## Timing and the Twelve Day War

The Brief argues Iran only had the opportunity to implement the protocol because President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] provided it with time, capping off the "Twelve Day War" with Operation Midnight Hammer and thereby giving Tehran room to regroup and prepare for an anticipated coup attempt.[1]

One of the main stated reasons for initiating COG was the concern that the West would use currency manipulation to crash the Iranian financial system, stoking unrest and provoking a political revolution.[1] The Brief holds that this attempt occurred in December and January and was defeated only because the operatives were forced to use Starlink, provided by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], to communicate, after which [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] and [[russia|Russia]] helped the Iranians "hack" Starlink and geo-locate all of the operatives' positions.[1]

## Significance of the Ahmadinejad reports

The reported exposure of former President [[mahmoud-ahmadinejad|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad]] as an alleged Mossad asset is treated in this framework as validating the theory, with Iran described as being purged of infiltrators and Western collaborators exposed for their malfeasance in the process.[1] The Brief adds the caveat that this is never a binary — one black-hat actor does not make his political opponents white hats.[1] It closes by suggesting that [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]]'s departure creates an opportunity for an actual reset of relations with Iran, if Americans have the courage to make peace with a culture they have been conditioned to hate.[1]
