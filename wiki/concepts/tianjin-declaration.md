# Tianjin Declaration

The Tianjin Declaration is the concluding document of the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit held in Tianjin, China, on August 31 and September 1, 2025. In the Badlands Media article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents the declaration as the statement of "immutable principles" underpinning a new financial and security architecture.

## Principles as summarised in the source

The article enumerates five commitments. First, total respect for the sovereign equality of nations, which Ehret contrasts with what he calls the "completely arbitrary" [[rules-based-international-order|Rules Based International Order]]. Second, the rejection of unilateral coercive measures — that is, sanctions imposed outside multilateral authorisation. Third, the creation of a new SCO Development Bank to provide productive credit for all members. Fourth, resistance to what the declaration frames as the rehabilitation of Nazism spreading throughout the West. Fifth, the position that a just settlement of the Palestinian issue is the only way to ensure peace and stability in the Middle East.

## Interpretation

Ehret treats these principles as continuous with the [[un-charter|UN Charter]]'s premises of national sovereignty, non-interference and mutual cooperation, and as evidence that the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]] is building institutions oriented toward the common good rather than toward a technocratic world government. The declaration is thus positioned in the article as a contemporary counterpart to the founding documents of earlier post-war settlements such as the Atlantic Charter and the [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods]] agreements.
