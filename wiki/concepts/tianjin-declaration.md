# Tianjin Declaration

The Tianjin Declaration is the founding document issued at the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit held in Tianjin, China on August 31 – September 1, 2025.[1] It set out the principles on which member states proposed to build a new financial and security architecture.[1]

## Principles

As summarized by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the declaration rested on five immutable principles:[1]

1. Total respect for the sovereign equality of nations, contrasted against what the article calls the completely arbitrary [[rules-based-international-order|rules-based international order]].[1]
2. Rejection of unilateral coercive measures.[1]
3. Creation of a new SCO Development Bank to provide productive credit for all members.[1]
4. Resistance to what the declaration characterizes as the rehabilitation of Nazism growing throughout the West.[1]
5. Affirmation that a just settlement of the Palestinian issue is the only way to ensure peace and stability in the Middle East.[1]

## Significance

The article treats the declaration as evidence that the foundations of an alternative security architecture — with a vital financial component — are being laid, and links its adoption to the broader agenda of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]] and to the sovereignty-based framework of the [[united-nations|UN Charter]].[1]
