# Tianjin Declaration

The Tianjin Declaration is the concluding document of the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit held in Tianjin, China on August 31 – September 1, 2025.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) It is presented in Badlands Media commentary as the statement of "immutable principles" underpinning a new financial and security architecture backed by states representing nearly half the world's population and about 20% of global GDP.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Core principles

The declaration is summarised in five points: total respect for the sovereign equality of nations, contrasted against what the author calls the completely arbitrary [[rules-based-international-order|Rules Based International Order]]; the rejection of unilateral coercive measures; the creation of a new SCO Development Bank to provide productive credit for all members; resistance to the rehabilitation of Nazism said to be growing throughout the West; and the assertion that a just settlement of the Palestinian issue is the only way to ensure peace and stability in the Middle East.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Significance

The author treats the declaration as evidence that the foundations of an alternative security architecture — including a vital financial component — are coming online.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Its emphasis on sovereignty and non-interference is aligned by the author with the original [[united-nations|UN Charter]] rather than with the unipolar order.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)
