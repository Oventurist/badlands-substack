# Shadow Government

"Shadow government" is the term Badlands Media uses for an unaccountable governing structure said to have grown out of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s capture of the machinery of American oversight.[1]

## Infiltration of Congress

Drawing on QAnon drop 4164, Badlands writer [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] read the reference to "the Farm" as a reference to the CIA, which was working to elect agency members to Congress in order to take control of oversight and budget authority — thereby neutralising Congress's oversight powers through infiltration and creating a shadow government accountable to no one.[1] Lange noted that [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] had himself been a congressman, and suggested that this helped him persuade Congress to respond to the 1976 investigations with CIA reforms rather than criminal charges — described as Washington's customary way of sweeping matters under the rug.[1]

## Capture of the President's Daily Brief

The second mechanism identified is control of the [[presidents-daily-brief|President's Daily Brief]].[1] During his eleven months as Director of Central Intelligence, Bush secured direct access to President [[gerald-ford|Gerald Ford]] and CIA control over the brief, which elevated the agency within the executive branch and gave it influence over every administration since.[1] Control over the information reaching the president, Lange argued, accelerated the formation of the shadow government, which expanded rapidly while Bush served as [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]]'s vice president.[1]

## Protection of criminal enterprises

Badlands extends the concept to the protection of global criminal networks, asking how [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] could operate what it calls the largest drug trafficking operation in the world for decades without public knowledge, and answering: "They were protected by a shadow government."[1] The same framework covers the CIA's alleged expansion of global drug trafficking through [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Offshore]] operating locations and the [[secret-war-in-laos|secret war in Laos]].[1]

## Reversal

In the Badlands telling, the shadow government's own instrument was eventually turned against it: the President's Daily Brief that Bush had made "a cabal tool of control" over the presidency was used to expose treason, described as "the perfect boomerang."[1]
