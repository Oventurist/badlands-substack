# Asclepius (Hermetic treatise)

The *Asclepius* is a Hermetic treatise dating from roughly the same period as the [[corpus-hermeticum|Corpus Hermeticum]] — c. 100 to c. 300 CE — surviving mainly in a Latin translation.[1] It is identified in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] as one of the two most popular works of the "Religio-philosophical Hermetica," the subgroup of writings attributed to [[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]] that gives the term "Hermeticism" its narrow sense.[1]

Together with the Corpus Hermeticum, the *Asclepius* dwells on the oneness and goodness of God, urges purification of the soul, and expands on the relationship between mind and spirit.[1]
