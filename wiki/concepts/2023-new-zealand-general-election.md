# 2023 New Zealand general election

The 2023 New Zealand general election was held on Saturday, 14 October 2023, and ousted the Labour Party once led by [[jacinda-ardern|Jacinda Ardern]], installing what coverage described as the country's most conservative government in decades.[1]

## Result

Conservative [[christopher-luxon|Christopher Luxon]] was elected New Zealand's next prime minister.[1] His centre-right National Party was indicated to win around 50 seats in the 121-seat parliament and was expected to form a coalition with one or two minor parties; the most likely partner, the ACT party with 11 seats, would bring the bloc to 61 seats and a slim majority.[1] Goldman noted the results were largely consistent with pre-election polling, and the incumbent Labour Party was on track to lose its outright majority for the first time since 2017.[1]

Luxon told supporters in Auckland, alongside his wife Amanda and their children, "You have reached for hope and you have voted for change."[1] Outgoing Prime Minister [[chris-hipkins|Chris Hipkins]], in office for nine months after Ardern's abrupt resignation, conceded by phone late on election night.[1]

## Interpretation

Coverage aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] presented the result as a backlash against pandemic authoritarianism, citing the outgoing government's mandating of vaccines, its decree that state news constituted the only "truth," and the arrest of peaceful protesters who opposed COVID-era mandates.[1]
