# AB 957

AB 957 was a California bill considered in 2023 that would have amended the California Family Code so that a child's "gender affirmation" was listed alongside the child's need for "health, safety, and welfare" in custody determinations. Critics argued the change would expose parents who decline to affirm a child's gender transition to child abuse allegations.[1]

## Legislative history and controversy

A Democratic senator amended the bill on June 5, 2023 to insert the gender affirmation language into the Family Code.[1] At the June 13 hearing of the California Senate Judiciary Committee, Republican senator [[scott-wilk|Scott Wilk]] — one of two Republicans on the committee — was reported as the lone voice warning against the amended language, and told parents in the hearing room to leave the state.[1] Democratic members of the committee maintained that the bill would only "give the judge more information," a characterization Wilk disputed.[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that the bill represented an intentional line-crossing meant to gauge how far activists could push and whether the public would push back, and to polarize anti-trans and pro-trans constituencies into open conflict that would generate favorable media framing.[1] He argued the backlash was instead surfacing deeper questions about bodily autonomy, competence, age of consent, and medical practice, and that public rejection of the measure showed people were newly aware of a problem many had not known existed.[1]
