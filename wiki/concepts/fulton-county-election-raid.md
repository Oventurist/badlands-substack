# Fulton County Election Raid

The **Fulton County Election Raid** is the term used in Badlands Media reporting for an enforcement action concerning election materials or election administration in Fulton County, Georgia.[1]

## Coverage

The raid was among the principal topics discussed on the fourth episode of [[badlands-blitz|The Blitz]], published January 31, 2026 and subtitled "Color Revolutions Come Home to Roost," with guest [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].[1] The episode grouped the Fulton County raid with the [[minnesota-color-revolution|Minnesota Color Revolution]] and the pattern of [[canadian-capitulation|Canadian Capitulation]], presenting all three as manifestations of what the hosts called "System Sway" and as evidence of a single [[macro-system|Macro System]] moving toward dissolution.[1]

The recap post itself gave no procedural detail about the raid — who conducted it, what was seized, or under what legal authority — treating it instead as a running story covered in the daily Badlands Brief across the preceding week.[1]

## Gabbard's participation and seizure details

Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] personally participated in the [[fbi|FBI]] raid on the Fulton County election facility, in which agents seized nearly 700 boxes containing 2020 physical ballots, tabulator tapes, ballot images, and voter rolls, citing alleged violations related to record preservation and false ballots.[2] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] congratulated the agents by phone during the operation.[2] The raid formed part of a broader set of Trump administration probes into the 2020 election, including an intelligence community review of voting machine vulnerabilities and foreign interference running parallel to a [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] criminal investigation.[2]

## County motion to recover records (February 2026)

On February 4, 2026, [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] filed a motion in the Northern District of Georgia challenging the [[fbi|FBI]] search warrant and seeking the return or narrowing of materials seized from the county's Election Hub, including 2020 election records such as ballots and voter rolls.[3] "This morning, February 4, 2026, Fulton County has filed a motion in federal court, in the Northern District of Georgia, seeking the return of all files from the 2020 Election that were confiscated by the FBI," a county spokesperson said, adding that "The motion also seeks the unsealing of the affidavit filed in support of the search warrant."[3]

At a press conference that week, county official [[robb-pitts|Robb Pitts]] said he had been told to be prepared for arrest warrants against himself and other state officials, declaring that "Our Constitution itself is at stake in this fight" and that "The Constitution is not a suggestion."[3]
