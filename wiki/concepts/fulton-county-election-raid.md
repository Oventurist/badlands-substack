# Fulton County Election Raid

The **Fulton County Election Raid** is the term used in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary for a law-enforcement action concerning election records or election infrastructure in Fulton County, Georgia, treated by the outlet as a significant development in the long-running dispute over the county's role in American elections.

The event is named in [[badlands-blitz-episode-4|Badlands Blitz Episode 4]] of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], published January 31, 2026, where it is listed alongside the [[minnesota-color-revolution|Minnesota Color Revolution]] and the pattern of [[canadian-capitulation|Canadian Capitulation]] among the week's topics. In the host's framing all three "are converging to illuminate the same Macro System, and its inevitable dissolution," an instance of what the program calls [[system-sway|System Sway]].

The archived post refers to the raid only in passing and provides no dates, agencies, or particulars; the substantive discussion took place in the episode itself and in the corresponding [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] coverage.
