# Four Pillars of Government Control

"Four Pillars of Government Control" is a framework advanced by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in a December 14, 2024 essay, which holds that Americans live under a structure supported by four systems: the health care system, the mainstream media, the financial system and the election system.[1]

## The framework

Carlson argues that confinement within this structure means no one is truly free — that awake or asleep, wherever a person may be in the process, everyone is controlled to some extent by these systems.[1] One pillar, or even two, may affect one person less than another, but there is no way of completely escaping all four short of building a cabin deep in the forest and living entirely off the land and off the grid, a life very few actually lead.[1]

So long as the four pillars stand firm, the roof Americans live under retains structural integrity. Should the pillars begin to crack — with visible and even audible signs of cracking — Carlson predicts the public will stop trusting the structure it believes protects it and will run from it in anticipation of collapse.[1] In late 2024, he writes, the structure is not only cracking but beginning to shift.[1]

## Chronology of construction

Carlson assigns each pillar a defining turning point, while cautioning that the four systems were not captured independently of one another:[1]

- Healthcare: the founding of the [[rockefeller-institute-of-medical-research|Rockefeller Institute of Medical Research]] in 1901.[1]
- Finance: 1913, when the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], the [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] and the income tax were implemented.[1]
- Elections: 1960, when in his view the U.S. presidential election was stolen.[1]
- Media: the 1960s, when the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] began [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]], funding media members to promote agency talking points.[1]

He allows that media control might be dated instead to 1911 and the building of the first Hollywood studio, and says he has no idea how far back election tampering goes — "probably back to the very beginning."[1] To anyone who believes there was ever a time when propaganda was not used on Americans, he offers [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]], who owned multiple newspapers and used aliases and pseudonyms to advance opposing talking points, in some cases arguing back and forth with himself.[1]

## The healthcare pillar

The Rockefellers are described as having spent more than a century building a healthcare system used to control Americans, by funding medical schools and research, shifting treatment from natural remedies to chemical-based drugs, and creating the [[american-medical-association|American Medical Association]] to control medical licensure.[1] The result is a system that profits from keeping Americans sick as rates of cancer, obesity and diabetes climb.[1] Carlson cites distrust of doctors and hospitals rising from 28.5% early in the COVID-19 pandemic to 59.9% less than four years later, before [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] had even entered the Trump administration, and predicts the pillar has no chance of surviving 2025.[1]

## The financial pillar

The 1913 creation of the Federal Reserve is described as giving Wall Street bankers control over the U.S. money supply — interest rates, the assets moving on and off the Fed balance sheet, the creation of money and therefore the rate of inflation — allowing manipulation of financial markets that effectively transfers wealth from the poor and middle class to elites.[1] Income tax and inflation are cast as instruments of debt slavery, with the IRS created to keep Americans from wandering "too far off of the debt plantation"; Americans are allowed to keep enough of a paycheck to survive but not to thrive.[1] Carlson invites doubters to stop paying their mortgage or property taxes and see how long they are allowed to remain in their homes.[1]

He links the July 2024 assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to Trump's hint a month earlier that he intended to take down the financial system, reading the "no tax on tips" proposal as a shot across the bow signalling that income tax and excessive interest rates would soon end.[1]

## The election pillar

Carlson recounts the belief that the Chicago Mob cut a deal with [[joseph-p-kennedy|Joseph Kennedy]] in 1960 to throw the election to [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] in exchange for lenient DOJ treatment of the Mafia — a bargain the Kennedys allegedly broke, which he offers as the motive for Mafia involvement in the JFK assassination three years later and that of Robert F. Kennedy five years after.[1] He notes the Mafia's asserted influence over the Teamsters Union and its working relationship with the FBI and CIA since World War II.[1]

Statistically, ten states in 1960 were decided by fewer than 10,000 votes; Kennedy carried Illinois by under 9,000 while running up a 450,000-vote advantage in Cook County, home of the Chicago Mob, and won the national popular vote by 113,000 — so the Cook County margin delivered both Illinois and the popular vote.[1] Carlson concludes the ease of that theft persuaded the [[deep-state|Deep State]] never to allow a fair election again, while maintaining the appearance of fairness through perpetually close results and alternating eight-year party turns, both parties controlled by the same people until Trump.[1] He believes 2016 was rigged for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] but not by enough stolen votes, that 2020 was blatantly stolen, and that even 2024 was manipulated to shrink Trump's margin and take down-ballot seats.[1] Without election integrity, he writes, the United States is merely a banana republic wearing the appearance of a first-world democratic republic.[1]

## The media pillar

The media section traces CIA control of the press from [[richard-nixon|Nixon]]'s blocked request to see Bay of Pigs and JFK assassination files, through the [[church-committee|Church Committee]]'s 1975 exposure of [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]], domestic surveillance and Operation Mockingbird, to [[william-colby|William Colby]]'s removal and death.[1] Carlson notes the irony that [[carl-bernstein|Carl Bernstein]], who exposed the CIA-press relationship in a 1977 [[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]] article, worked for the [[washington-post|Washington Post]], itself considered by some a CIA-controlled outlet — exposure after the fact, he argues, lending the media borrowed credibility.[1]

He holds that CIA control of the media persists and has expanded, citing [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s 2012 repeal of the [[smith-mundt-act|Smith-Mundt Act]] as licensing the government to propagandize its own citizens without repercussion.[1] Aggressive anti-Trump propaganda, however, has driven trust in media down to 31%, though many of the 69% who profess distrust still unknowingly repeat media talking points.[1] He cites [[abc-news|ABC News]] calling RFK Jr.'s June 2024 Mockingbird allegations "bonkers" despite the acting CIA director having made the same claims under oath in 1975.[1]

## Outlook

Carlson concludes that the Deep State can likely lose only one of the four pillars and still maintain control, and that it is very unlikely any will be left standing in two years.[1] The healthcare, financial, media and election systems will always be needed, he writes, but must be torn down and rebuilt with safeguards preventing the takeovers of the past century — rebuilt to serve Americans rather than control them.[1]
