# Florida 6th Congressional District Special Election

The 2025 special election in Florida's 6th Congressional District was held to fill the seat vacated by [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]], who resigned from the House after being appointed national security adviser to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Candidates

Democrat [[joshua-weil|Joshua Weil]], a public school educator in Osceola County, and Republican [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]], then serving in the Florida Senate's 19th District based in Brevard County, won their respective primaries and advanced to the general special election.[1] Andrew Parrott ran as a Libertarian, Randall Terry ran non-party affiliated, and Chuck Sheridan ran as a write-in candidate.[1]

## Geography and turnout

Several parts of Northeast Florida fall inside the 6th District, including all of Putnam County, all of Flagler County, and portions of St. Johns County — Butler Beach, Crescent Beach, Flagler Estates, Hastings, St. Augustine Shores and St. Augustine South.[1]
