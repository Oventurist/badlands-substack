# 2009 Copenhagen Climate Summit

The December 2009 United Nations climate conference in Copenhagen — referred to in the source as COP14 — was intended to produce legally binding emissions targets to guide the decarbonization of much of global society.[1] It failed, and Badlands Media commentary describes that failure as a deliberate act of sabotage by China and India.[1]

## Collapse of the talks

Leaders including Nicolas Sarkozy, Angela Merkel and Barack Obama had championed the summit as the opening of a new era of green global governance backed by a binding decarbonization treaty.[1] The London *Guardian* reported at the time that "Copenhagen was a disaster… The truth is this: China wrecked the talks, intentionally humiliated Barack Obama, and insisted on an awful 'deal' so western leaders would walk away carrying the blame."[1]

According to the same account, it was China's representative who insisted that industrialized-country targets — a previously agreed 80% cut by 2050 — be struck from the deal, prompting Merkel to demand "Why can't we even mention our own targets?" and Australian prime minister Kevin Rudd to bang his microphone in frustration.[1] China, backed at times by India, then removed the remaining numerical commitments, including a 2020 global emissions peaking year, replacing it with language suggesting emissions should peak "as soon as possible."[1]

## Interpretation

The source argues that China and India — along with African governments such as Sudan — refused to sacrifice their industry and national sovereignty to climate models produced by technocrats who had been publicly exposed as frauds weeks earlier in the Climategate affair at East Anglia University.[1] It frames the sabotage, and the later failures of binding treaties in 2021 and 2022, as a defense of three principles: the right of all nations to develop without culling their populations, the right of sovereign nations to supersede the edicts of globalist institutions, and the rejection of the New World Order framework outlined by George H. W. Bush, [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] and Joe Biden in 1992.[1]

The episode is treated as foundational evidence against the claim that the [[brics|BRICS]] and multipolar bloc are controlled opposition aligned with the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and the [[great-reset|Great Reset]].[1]
