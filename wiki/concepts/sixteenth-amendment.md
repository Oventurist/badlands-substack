# Sixteenth Amendment

The **Sixteenth Amendment** to the United States Constitution, ratified in 1913, authorized the federal income tax.[1]

In the Badlands Media account, the drive for a federal income tax grew out of the late nineteenth century, when various groups including the [[populist-party|Populist Party]] favored its establishment. The article argues that the [[long-depression|Long Depression]] was damaging the rich and powerful, who therefore needed a mechanism to permanently suppress citizens.[1]

Following ratification, Democratic leaders agreed to seek passage of the [[revenue-act-of-1913|Revenue Act of 1913]], which lowered tariffs and implemented the income tax; President [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] signed it on October 3, 1913.[1]
