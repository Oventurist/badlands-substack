# Shadow Men

"Shadow men" is the term drawn from [[anthony-napoleon|Anthony Napoleon]]'s book *Shadow Men: An Encyclopedia of Mind Control* and adopted in the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" for a hereditary class of covert rulers said to have operated behind formal power structures throughout history.[1]

## Characteristics

Napoleon argues that a consistent and reliable modus operandi has been used all throughout history by these figures, who tend to be intelligent psychopaths.[1] Their foundational technique was learning to track the motion of the heavens and to assign socio-religious significance to the movement of celestial bodies, eventually predicting celestial events with staggering accuracy and pairing those predictions with seeded narratives presenting them as proof of communion with the gods.[1] This established their authority over a less intelligent and superstitious citizenry and links [[astrotheology|astrotheology]] to occult power.[1]

## Historical trajectory

In the deep past, small tribes centered on shamanistic practices are said to have developed human sacrifice and psycho-drama, or ritual magic, as standard features of shadow-men control.[1] As societies grew more complex, the shaman or witch doctor could no longer indoctrinate everyone directly, and with the emergence of monarchy that function passed to much larger religious institutions.[1] From these primordial roots, the article argues, more sophisticated dark elite groups emerged to form the civilizations of Sumer, Babylon, Egypt and Phoenicia and onward through history, with their rites modernizing over time into forms such as the [[false-flag-operation|false flag]] ritual.[1]

The [[eleusinian-mysteries|Eleusinian Mystery Schools]] are cited as a key institutional vehicle, later used to recruit and cement members of the general population into a secret power cult that itself served the shadow men operating behind the scenes.[1] After the fall of the Roman Empire the shadow men are said to have "crypto-ized," clothing themselves in other religions such as Judaism, Islam and Christianity, and later in secularism; the article asserts that today secular systems of power overshadow the older exoteric socio-religious systems while the underlying astrotheological basis for shaping world events remains alive.[1]
