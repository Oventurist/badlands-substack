# Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park

In January 2018, [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] signed legislation enabling the establishment of a national park honoring [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]][1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands Media presented the signing as evidence against the frequent characterization of Trump as a racist, noting that prior to entering politics he had been repeatedly praised by leaders of the black community for his work with minorities.[1] The article cited his receipt of the Ellis Island Award — given to New York City community leaders who do the most to advance minorities in the city — in the early 1990s as further support for that argument.[1]
