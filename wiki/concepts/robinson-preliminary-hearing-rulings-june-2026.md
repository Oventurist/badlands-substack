# Robinson Preliminary Hearing Rulings (June 2026)

On June 1, 2026, a Utah judge handed [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] — the man accused of assassinating [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] — two significant pretrial defeats governing the openness of his forthcoming preliminary hearing.[1]

## The rulings

Judge [[tony-graf|Tony Graf]] ruled that Robinson's July preliminary hearing would remain open to the public and the media, rejecting defense efforts to close portions of the proceedings.[1] Graf also declined to halt the case while Robinson appealed a separate ruling allowing cameras in the courtroom.[1]

## Expected evidence

The July 6–10 hearing was expected to be the most substantial public presentation of evidence in the case to that point.[1] Prosecutors indicated they planned to introduce forensic analyses, surveillance footage, witness statements, autopsy findings, and alleged messages in which Robinson admitted responsibility for the September 2025 killing of Kirk at Utah Valley University.[1] Authorities also cited DNA evidence allegedly linking Robinson to the rifle used in the shooting.[1]

Robinson, 23, faced aggravated murder charges with prosecutors seeking the death penalty.[1] A separate hearing was scheduled for June 12 on defense claims that prosecutors had improperly influenced public opinion through media comments about the case.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the hearing would fall just after the nation's birthday and would present the most extensive, ostensibly conclusive evidence against Robinson, adding "nothing can stop what is coming."[1] She stated flatly that she was not sure anyone could convince her Robinson killed Kirk, arguing the story never made sense and that the institutional establishment had shamed those asking obvious questions — a pattern she treats as the signature of "a tightly controlled lie."[1] She said she would probably still watch, but that it would take "a hell of a presentation of evidence" to persuade her.[1]
