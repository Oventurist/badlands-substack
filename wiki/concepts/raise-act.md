# RAISE Act

The RAISE Act was an immigration reform bill considered by [[us-congress|Congress]] in 2017 and supported by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], described in conservative commentary cited by Badlands as a "true conservative immigration reform bill."[1]

## Contrast with the Gang of Eight approach

The bill is presented in the Badlands account as the alternative to the revived [[gang-of-eight-immigration-bill|Gang of Eight]] amnesty framework that Sen. [[john-mccain|John McCain]] sought to bring back in August 2017.[1] While Congress considered the RAISE Act, McCain was reported to be "pleading for amnesty," a posture the article characterizes as making him a thorn in the side of conservatives and, in its own framing, an enemy of the people.[1] Signing a Gang of Eight-style bill would have broken several of Trump's key promises to the American people, including the creation of a physical barrier on the southern border to reduce illegal immigration.[1]
