# Hong Kong National Security Law (2024)

Hong Kong lawmakers passed a new national security law on Tuesday, March 19, 2024, which critics say grants the government broader power to quash dissent.[1]

## Provisions

The law imposes stringent penalties across a wide range of offenses, including "treason" and "insurrection," which are punishable by life imprisonment.[1]

## US reaction and Chinese response

Following the law's passage, the United States [[state-department|State Department]] described the threats outlined in the statute as "poorly defined and incredibly vague."[1] Spokesperson Vedant Patel said: "We believe that these kinds of actions have the potential to accelerate the closing of Hong Kong's once open society."[1]

China's embassy in the United States hit back on Thursday, March 21, saying the US should "respect China's sovereignty" and that other countries should not interfere in China's affairs, framing the criticism as "slandering" the city — summarized in the position that "Hong Kong is China's Hong Kong."[1] The exchange was reported by [[cnbc|CNBC]].[1]
