# 2025 Chicago Midway Runway Near Miss

The 2025 Chicago Midway runway near miss was an aborted landing by a Southwest Airlines flight at Chicago's Midway Airport on the morning of Tuesday, February 25, 2025, after another aircraft crossed the runway ahead of it.[1]

## The incident

Video showed the airliner, Flight 2504, nearly touching down before revving back up and climbing away.[1] Ahead of it, a FlexJet business jet — described by NBC Chicago as "unauthorized" — was crossing the runway.[1]

Southwest said the flight "safely landed 'after the crew performed a precautionary go-around to avoid a possible conflict with another aircraft that entered the runway,'" adding that "the Crew followed safety procedures and the flight landed without incident" and that "nothing is more important to Southwest than the Safety of our Customers and Employees."[1]
