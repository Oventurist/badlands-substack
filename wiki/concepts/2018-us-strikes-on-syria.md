# 2018 US Strikes on Syria

The April 2018 missile strikes on [[syria|Syria]] were ordered by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in retaliation for a suspected chemical weapons attack. Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues the stated rationale was a deliberate "cover story" for the destruction of a clandestine Iranian nuclear facility.[1]

## The strikes as reported

According to NBC News, quoted in the article, Trump on a Friday ordered the United States military — in conjunction with France and the United Kingdom — to launch strikes on Syria in retaliation for a suspected chemical weapons attack by the regime of President [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] on a Damascus suburb the previous week.[1] The president said the United States would aim to hit sites "associated with the chemical weapons capabilities" of Assad's regime.[1] NBC also reported that on the following Saturday [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] reaffirmed Russia's view that the purported chemical attack was a fake, criticising the United States and its allies for launching the strike without waiting for inspectors from the international chemical weapons watchdog to visit the area.[1] Trump publicly praised the strike.[1]

## The "cover" interpretation

Lange fastens on the end of [[qanon|Q]] drop #1306 of April 30, 2018: "Why did we strike Syria? Why did we really strike Syria? Define cover. Patriots in control."[1] Reading the strikes against that drop, he suggests the operation actually destroyed the satellite Iranian nuclear facility located in Syria, and says he "would bet on it," calling the chemical-weapons justification a perfect cover story for taking out a secret nuclear facility controlled by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]].[1] In his framing this is how Trump prevented the planned nuclear war that the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] and the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran nuclear deal]] had been designed to enable — a war for which, he complains, Trump gets no credit.[1]

He contrasts the enforced strike with [[obamas-red-line-in-syria|Obama's unenforced red line]], and argues more broadly that most of what the controlled media has told the public about the Syrian civil war has been a lie.[1] As evidence he cites *The Times of Israel*, which reported that US and Russian troops exchanged friendly greetings and posed for photographs together on a northeast Syrian highway, their patrol routes intersecting at an oil field near the town of Qahtaniyah in Hassakeh province, with several troops even exchanging uniform patches as mementos — behaviour Lange finds irreconcilable with the official narrative of implacable enmity being used to justify spending on the war in Ukraine.[1] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
