# Q Drop Stringers

"Stringers" are a recurring format within the [[qanon|Q]] drop corpus: blocks of seemingly disjointed characters, code words, coordinates and abbreviations posted without explanatory prose. In the interpretation advanced by Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies]] series, stringers are generally not addressed to the public reading the board at all; they are described as instructions or status messages intended for operators executing an operation against the [[deep-state|Deep State]], with the anon audience acting only as an incidental witness.[1]

## Function within the drop corpus

The series treats stringers as evidence that the Q operation was an active military communications channel rather than a purely rhetorical exercise. Because the messages are addressed to field personnel, their content is expected to be opaque to outside readers; the reader's role is to record them so that later events can be matched against them under the [[future-proves-past]] framework.[1]

## Drops #101–#102 and the "Obama extraction" stringer

Drop #101, posted November 5, 2017, opens by confirming the accuracy of the then-current Q-Graphic before presenting a stringer that the series reads as pointing to an "extraction" of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]. The chapter stresses that "extraction" need not mean physical seizure, and argues the stringer's real purpose was to demonstrate that white hats were tracking Obama's movements — knowing that he was in the Philippines, and knowing when he departed — while he travelled through Asia ahead of [[donald-trump|President Trump]] in what the author characterizes as an attempt to undermine Trump's forthcoming regional talks.[1]

Drop #102 continues with a second stringer beginning with a set of geographic coordinates that resolve to a location in Manila, near a hotel identified as the Sun Star Grand. Beneath the coordinates appears the word "green," which the series interprets either as a go order issued to operators in the field, or as a deliberate signal designed to intimidate Obama by showing that his movements were known.[1]

## Interpretation as psychological pressure

The chapter situates the stringers within a cluster of contemporaneous pressures on the Deep State: the [[saudi-purge|Saudi round-up]] a day earlier, Jeff Sessions' moves against leakers, the initiation of John Durham's work, Q's recent arrival, and Trump's earlier actions. Against that backdrop, the series argues, the stringers functioned as a message to Obama that "Q-Team" was tracking his every step.[1]

## OpSec objection and drop #108

Drop #108 records an anon asserting that publishing stringers amounted to a breach of operational security. Q's reply — that "nothing is as it seems" — is read by the series as confirming the opposite: that the stringers were deliberate, and that their intended effect, the flushing out of Obama, had already been achieved.[1]
