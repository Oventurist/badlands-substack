# Dead Sea Scrolls

The Dead Sea Scrolls are a corpus of ancient Jewish manuscripts whose dating has long depended on a combination of radiocarbon analysis and paleography, the expert study of handwriting styles.[1]

## AI redating with "Enoch"

In June 2025 researchers reported an artificial intelligence system called "Enoch" that combines radiocarbon dating with automated handwriting analysis in order to date ancient manuscripts more accurately than traditional methods.[1] Tested against 135 Dead Sea Scroll fragments, the system consistently predicted earlier dates than those assigned by expert paleographers, implying that some of the religious texts may be decades older than previously believed.[1]

## Significance

The findings, if sustained, would reshape the chronology of early Judaism, indicating that sophisticated religious communities and their written output emerged in ancient Judaea earlier than scholars had assumed.[1] The item was carried as a bonus story in the Badlands News Brief of June 6, 2025.[1]
