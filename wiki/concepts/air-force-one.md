# Air Force One

Air Force One is the official air traffic control call sign for any United States Air Force aircraft carrying the President of the United States, and by extension the name given to the presidential aircraft.

## Qatari donation and the media subpoenas (July 2026)

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s new Air Force One was donated by [[qatar|Qatar]], and that the administration's subpoenas to several [[new-york-times|New York Times]] journalists followed the outlet's report on the aircraft, compelling the reporters to testify before a federal grand jury in Manhattan. The subpoenas came days after the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and [[pentagon|Pentagon]] established a joint task force on media leaks.

## The February 2026 aliens press gaggle

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that during a press gaggle on Air Force One on Thursday, February 19, 2026, [[peter-doocy|Peter Doocy]] of [[fox-news|Fox News]] asked President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] about [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s viral comments on extraterrestrial life. Trump responded that Obama "gave classified information. He's not supposed to be doing that," and when Doocy noted that the President can declassify anything, Trump replied "Maybe I'll get him out of trouble. I may get him out of trouble by declassifying." Hours later Trump posted on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] directing the Secretary of War to begin releasing government files on alien life, UAPs and UFOs. Trump also used the gaggle to describe [[cuba|Cuba]] as "a failed nation" without jet fuel for its airplanes.
