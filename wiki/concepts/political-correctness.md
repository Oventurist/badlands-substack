# Political Correctness

Political correctness is described in Badlands Media commentary as a weapon of cultural subversion rather than a code of mutual respect.[1]

## Alleged origin and purpose

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that political correctness and [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]] "were not unleashed on America 30+ years ago to nurture respect for one another," and that both concepts were created in pre-Nazi Germany at the [[frankfurt-school|Frankfurt School]].[1] Their purpose, in his account, was "to infiltrate and destroy an enemy state from within" and "to weaken and defeat an enemy without a shot being fired."[1]

The concept sits within the article's larger claim that woke policies are engineered to produce the opposite of their stated aims — a society that is "less inclusive, less united, less connected, less understanding, less forgiving."[1]
