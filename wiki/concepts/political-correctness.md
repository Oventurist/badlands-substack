# Political Correctness

Political correctness is described in Badlands Media commentary as a weapon of cultural subversion rather than a code of mutual respect.[1]

## Alleged origin and purpose

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that political correctness and [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]] "were not unleashed on America 30+ years ago to nurture respect for one another," and that both concepts were created in pre-Nazi Germany at the [[frankfurt-school|Frankfurt School]].[1] Their purpose, in his account, was "to infiltrate and destroy an enemy state from within" and "to weaken and defeat an enemy without a shot being fired."[1]

The concept sits within the article's larger claim that woke policies are engineered to produce the opposite of their stated aims — a society that is "less inclusive, less united, less connected, less understanding, less forgiving."[1]

## As an enabler of atrocity (2025)

In "On Golden Bridges and the Art of War" (January 28, 2025), [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] described political correctness as a form of mass hypnosis that "enables atrocities to be committed against people cast, rightly or wrongly, in the role of 'oppressor.'"[2] Applying the claim to the United Kingdom's [[grooming-gangs|grooming gangs]], he asserted that potentially millions of European children had been abused because a politically correct society had deemed the victims the "bad ones" purely on the basis of skin colour.[2]

The same essay argued that Western civilisation has been intentionally hamstrung by political correctness on immigration, and that the concept of nationalism had been reframed as backwards and inherently violent when, in the author's view, denying the existence and rights of nations is itself a form of political and spiritual violence.[2] He predicted that the "politically correct" defenders of the cover-up were being exposed and would reach a point where they "won't be able to walk down the street," noting that such matters could not have been discussed five to ten years earlier without loss of employment or social media access.[2]
