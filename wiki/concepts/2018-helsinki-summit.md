# 2018 Helsinki Summit

The 2018 Helsinki summit was the meeting between U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in Helsinki, Finland, best remembered in Badlands Media commentary for the joint press conference at which Putin handed Trump a soccer ball and remarked that the ball was now in Trump's court.[1]

## Press conference remarks

At the press conference Trump acknowledged that "the disagreements between our two countries are well-known" and said the two leaders had discussed them at length, but argued that solving global problems would require finding "ways to cooperate in pursuit of shared interests."[1] He warned of the consequences "when diplomacy is left on the table" and cited the benefits of cooperation, noting that the United States and Russia had fought alongside one another in the Second World War and maintained a strong dialogue even during the tensions of the Cold War.[1]

## Context and the "competitor" framing

The summit took place while the American press was, in Badlands Media's characterization, conducting a full-court press on Russian collusion allegations and pressing Trump to confront Putin over election interference.[1] Shortly before, on the sidelines of the NATO summit in Brussels, Trump had declined to call Putin an enemy, describing him instead as a competitor who might become a friend in time and adding: "he's representing Russia and I'm representing the United States."[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] reads the Helsinki language as a deliberate signal that a coalition was forming around a common project of financial independence from central banking, and builds his concept of [[shared-interests|Shared Interests]] directly on Trump's phrasing there.[1]

## "The ball is in your court" (Lange, 2023)

Badlands Media's "The Master Builder" (February 2, 2023) treats the Helsinki summit of June 2018 as a symbolic turning point in what it describes as a coordinated campaign against the global central-bank debt system.[2] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] notes that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] met privately as well as publicly at Helsinki and appeared to get along well during the press conference, and singles out the moment when Putin handed Trump a World Cup soccer ball and announced that the ball was now in Trump's court.[2] In Lange's reading, that gesture marked the point at which the ball began to roll on the takedown of the central-bank system: Putin was willing to play his part later, but needed Trump to act first.[2]

The article also notes that a Daily Caller commentary by retired Lt. Col. [[tony-shaffer|Tony Shaffer]], defending Trump's pressure on [[nato|NATO]] allies to meet their defense commitments, appeared the day after the summit.[2]
