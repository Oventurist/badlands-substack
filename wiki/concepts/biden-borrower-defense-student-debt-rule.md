# Biden Borrower Defense Student Debt Rule

The **Biden borrower defense student debt rule** was a regulation issued by the administration of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] intended to make it easier for student borrowers to have their debts relieved if they were misled or defrauded by a university.[1] The rules took effect in July 2023 and aimed to expand the range of circumstances that justify loan relief.[1]

## Fifth Circuit injunction

In August 2023, a federal appeals court granted an injunction blocking the new rules.[1] A three-judge panel of the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-fifth-circuit|US Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit]] granted an injunction request by Career Colleges & Schools of Texas, a group representing more than seventy for-profit higher education institutions in Texas, while the underlying ruling remained on appeal.[1]

The two-page order did not explain the reasoning behind the decision, but noted that the case would be heard that fall.[1] It stated: "It is ordered that appellant's opposed emergency motion for injunction pending appeal of the borrower-defense and closed-school provisions of a 'rule' governing student loan discharges is GRANTED."[1]

The group's lawsuit stemmed from an April 2023 filing against the [[department-of-education|Education Department]].[1] The item appeared as a bonus entry in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], sourced to [[washington-examiner|The Washington Examiner]], without additional editorial commentary.[1]
