# Operation Midas

**Operation Midas** is the name of a sweeping anti-corruption investigation in Ukraine conducted by Western-backed anti-corruption agencies, centered on an alleged $100 million money-laundering scheme.[1]

The probe reached the highest level of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]]'s government when investigators raided the residence of [[andriy-yermak|Andrey Yermak]], head of the Office of the President since 2020 and the Ukrainian leader's most powerful aide.[1] Yermak formally left his post in late 2025 over alleged links to the scheme, denying any wrongdoing.[1]

Yermak's departure did not settle questions about his residual power. His former colleague [[yuliya-mendel|Yuliya Mendel]], once Zelensky's spokeswoman, argued that Yermak retained a network of loyal officials and would work to shape policy from behind the scenes or return to office outright.[1]
