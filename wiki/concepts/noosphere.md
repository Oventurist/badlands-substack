# Noosphere

The Noosphere is the term used by the Jesuit [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]] for the third phase of his four-stage cosmology — the emergence of cognition, or homogenesis — preceding the final spiritual convergence of humanity at the [[omega-point|Omega Point]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] stresses that Chardin's Noosphere was a very different beast from the Noosphere developed by the Russian academician [[vladimir-vernadsky|Vladimir Vernadsky]] (1863–1945), who was locked into his own parallel fight against the mechanists attempting to crush the soul of science in Russia.[1]

In Chardin's usage the concept dovetailed with the rise of [[cybernetics|cybernetics]] after 1945, and he explicitly framed radio, television and "astonishing electronic computers" as the material scaffolding of a collective consciousness — "a kind of super-Brain, capable of attaining mastery over some super-sphere in the universe."[1]
