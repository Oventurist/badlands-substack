# 2025 Fronton Border Patrol Shooting Exchange

On Monday, January 27, 2025, suspected Mexican cartel members fired on U.S. Border Patrol agents near Fronton, Texas, prompting a heated exchange of gunfire.[1] No Border Patrol agents were injured.[1]

## Circumstances

The shooting occurred as a group of illegal immigrants was noticed attempting to cross the Rio Grande.[1] Fox News correspondent Bill Melugin reported on X, citing multiple law enforcement sources, that agents near Fronton were fired upon from Mexico by suspected cartel gunmen as migrants were being brought across the river, that [[us-border-patrol|Border Patrol]] returned fire, that nobody was hit on either side, and that the migrants did not complete the crossing.[1]

## Response

Texas Governor [[greg-abbott|Greg Abbott]] responded that the Texas Tactical Border Force had deployed to the Rio Grande Valley to collaborate with U.S. Border Patrol agents, adding that "Texas is working closely with the Trump Administration to secure the border and make America safe again."[1]
