# Georgia Guidestones

The Georgia Guidestones were a granite monument inscribed with a set of guidelines for humanity, best remembered in dissident media for the depopulation language of its opening precept: "Maintain humanity under 500,000,000 in perpetual balance with nature."[1] That first guideline is routinely invoked as shorthand for an elite intention to reduce global population, and the monument functions in alternative-media commentary as an interpretive key for stories touching fertility, medicine, and public health.[1]

## Invocation in Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the Guidestones directly when reacting to research linking [[tylenol|Tylenol]] exposure in pregnancy to impaired sexual development in male fetuses, writing that stories of that kind "almost immediately" bring the monument to mind.[1] The reference framed the fertility findings not as an isolated pharmacological result but as one data point in a longer-running argument about deliberate population management, sitting alongside earlier claims connecting the same drug to autism.[1]

## Invoked as evidence of the plan

In an October 2022 Badlands Media essay, contributor [[pepe-lives-matter|Pepe Lives Matter]] pointed to the destruction of the Georgia Guidestones as a sign that the tide had turned, writing that the monument "highlighted their ultimate goal of world depopulation and slavery" and that "those with eyes to see" recognised the significance of its fall.[2] He grouped the toppling with the overturning of [[roe-v-wade|Roe v. Wade]] as wins the [[cabal|cabal]] would never have permitted, and urged readers to treat both as foundational groundwork to recall whenever a perceived loss came into view.[2]
