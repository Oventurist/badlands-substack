# CIA REMVE Assessment on Women

The CIA REMVE assessment on women is a Biden-era [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] intelligence product, dated October 2021 and titled "Women Advancing White Racially and Ethnically Motivated Violent Extremist Radicalization and Recruitment," which was declassified and publicized in 2026.[1] The document explicitly identified "motherhood" and "homemaking" as potential indicators of "white racially and ethnically motivated violent extremism" (REMVE).[1]

## Contents

The assessment claimed that women who promoted traditional family roles, pro-life activism, or skepticism toward multiculturalism and globalism could serve as gateways to domestic terrorism, even if they did not themselves advocate violence.[1] It included fictional "radicalization" scenarios featuring suburban women as threats.[1]

One character, "Ann," was depicted as a middle-aged pro-life mother who became more devout after her own mother's death and was flagged for simply asking about biblical views on "violence in defense of life"; the memo instructed her pastor to intervene, speak with her husband, and monitor her church group.[1] Another figure, "Courtney," a divorced mother in her mid-30s, was labeled a "budding conspiracy theorist" for believing the government was involved in child trafficking — claims later corroborated, the article notes, by CBP and DHS data showing hundreds of thousands of missing unaccompanied minors during the Biden years.[1]

## Disclosure and retraction

[[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] obtained and publicized the documents, describing them as evidence of top-to-bottom ideological bias inside the Biden-era CIA.[1] The Trump administration formally retracted the assessment earlier in 2026.[1] The documents were also said to reveal broader Biden administration efforts to frame everyday conservative women as domestic-terror risks, and [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s retraction was hailed by critics of the assessment as a reversal of a politicized weaponization of federal intelligence against conservative women and families.[1]
