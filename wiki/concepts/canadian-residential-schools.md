# Canadian Residential Schools

The **Canadian Residential Schooling system** was a network of institutions in which First Nations children were placed, and within which, as Badlands Media acknowledges, indigenous people were genuinely tortured and murdered.[1] The system became the focus of a contested media episode in the summer of 2022 that Badlands Media characterizes as a [[limited-hangout|limited hangout]].[1]

## The mass grave narrative

In the summer of 2022 the Canadian government and media began what the article calls a massive propaganda operation claiming to have uncovered "mass graves" of indigenous children, allegedly resulting from torture and sacrifice within the residential school system.[1] Alternative Canadian media, including *The Counter Signal*, quickly debunked the entire narrative, according to the article, proving there were no mass graves.[1]

## The limited hangout hypothesis

Since the underlying abuses were real, the article asks why the Canadian government would perform a limited hangout around the abuse and torture of native children.[1] In his book *Murder by Decree*, author [[kevin-annett|Kevin Annett]] describes an anonymous phone call from a man claiming to be a retired member of the Canadian civil service.[1] The caller alleged that the torture of indigenous people was tied up in Department of National Defense projects, with the [[royal-canadian-mounted-police|RCMP]] delivering tortured victims into mind control programs, and pointed to the program's German origins with direction coming from the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

## Subsequent corroboration claimed

The article holds that this theory was validated a year later when [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] posted new documents revealing that [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] had targeted indigenous and black children in medical torture experiments.[1] The inference offered is that the well-coordinated mass-grave story may have served as cover for MK Ultra programs.[1]
