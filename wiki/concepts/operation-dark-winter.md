# Operation Dark Winter

Operation Dark Winter was a pandemic war-game exercise conducted in 2000, described as the earliest of the modern series of simulated biological-attack scenarios that normalized pandemic planning within western geopolitical life.[1]

Dark Winter is grouped with the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation's]] Lock Step scenario of 2010–2011 and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]/Gates Foundation/[[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] exercise [[event-201|Event 201]] as the principal precedents that, in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret's]] argument, laid the groundwork for the international response to COVID-19.[1] Because dozens of such exercises had been staged in the two decades before 2020, the possibility that a release was consciously deployed rather than accidental was treated as a serious one from the outset.[1]

Ehret presents the war-game record as his fifth "fact" indicating that China was not the causal nexus of the pandemic response: the institutions that rehearsed the lockdown template were transatlantic, not Chinese.[1]
