# Uranium One Deal

The "Uranium One deal" is the name given to the sequence of transactions, spanning 2005 to 2013, by which Kazakh and American uranium assets passed from Canadian financiers to Russian state ownership — a sequence the article presents as a planned money operation organized by the Clintons and executed by the Obama administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Sequence of events

The article dates the beginning of the deal not to Russia's acquisition but to September 2005, when [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] flew aboard [[frank-giustra|Frank Giustra]]'s private jet to Almaty, [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], dined with authoritarian president Nursultan A. Nazarbayev, and publicly backed Nazarbayev's bid to head an international elections monitoring group — undercutting American foreign policy and criticism of Kazakhstan's human rights record, including criticism from his wife, then a senator.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Within days, Giustra's [[urasia-energy|UrAsia Energy]] signed a preliminary deal for stakes in three uranium mines controlled by [[kazatomprom|Kazatomprom]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Several months later Giustra donated $31.3 million to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], which the article labels quid pro quo.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

In 2007 UrAsia merged with the South African company [[uranium-one|Uranium One]] in a $3.5 billion transaction, and [[ian-telfer|Ian Telfer]] became chairman.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Between 2009 and 2013 Russians acquired control in three transactions, with [[rosatom|Rosatom]] subsidiary ARMZ approved in 2010 to take ownership of Uranium One and its American assets — roughly one-fifth of U.S. uranium production capacity.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Because uranium is a strategic asset, the deal required approval from a committee of U.S. agencies including the [[state-department|State Department]], then headed by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], and, the article adds, from most of the Obama cabinet.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Money flows

Canadian records showed $2.35 million in undisclosed donations from Uranium One's chairman's family foundation, plus $500,000 paid to Bill Clinton for a Moscow speech by a Kremlin-linked Russian investment bank promoting Uranium One stock.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Telfer's Fernwood Foundation gave $1 million reported in 2009 — the year his company appealed to the American Embassy to help keep its Kazakh mines — $250,000 in 2010 as the Russians sought majority control, $600,000 in 2011 and $500,000 in 2012.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article observes that concealing these donations amounts to tax evasion, the same crime for which [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]] was indicted and pardoned.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Russian investor [[sergei-kurzin|Sergei Kurzin]], a former Rich employee involved in both the Kazakh and Uranium One transactions, donated $1 million to the Clinton Foundation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## The Russian Reset as enabler

The article argues the deal required a public narrative in which Russia was no longer an enemy, supplied by the 2009 [[russian-reset|Russian Reset]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) It cites [[qanon|Q]] drop #48 (Nov 02, 2017), which asks what the Russia reset really provided and answers "Clearance/pathway to complete the U1 deal?", and Q drop #46, which asks about export approval to Canada and the EU and whether the Obama administration made false statements that U1 uranium would never be exported.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Q drop #1345 (May 12, 2018) states the operation was "organized/planned by BC/HRC," carried out by "Hussein," motivated by money, and connected to the $1.7 billion in-cash transfer to Iran.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Concealment from Congress

Assurances by the [[nuclear-regulatory-commission|Nuclear Regulatory Commission]] that no uranium could be exported were undercut when the NRC amended an existing export license held by trucking firm RSB Logistics Services rather than issuing Rosatom a direct license, thereby moving yellowcake from Wyoming to Canada and onward to Europe between 2012 and 2014 without congressional notice.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Invocation in Twitter Files coverage

In April 2023 The Gateway Pundit invoked Uranium One while covering the twenty-first installment of the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]], arguing that the claim of Russian accounts materially impacting U.S. elections was a lie manufactured by the [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[barack-obama|Obama]] camps before the 2016 election out of fear that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would investigate their crimes.[2] The outlet asserted that the Obama administration, with Clinton as Secretary of State, had allowed the sale of a significant amount of U.S. uranium to the Russians and that the Clintons received millions in return, with the extent of any payments to other administration officials unknown.[2] It placed the transaction within a wider alleged crime spree that it said also encompassed setting up the Trump administration, payments to Iran, an opened border, sanctuary cities, support for Soros-backed activities, and the trafficking of minors.[2]

## Leverage over Mueller and Rosenstein

In "Disinformation is Necessary," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats Uranium One as the source of leverage that allowed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to flip the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller special counsel investigation]].[3] Q drop 3 (October 29, 2017) declared that "Rod Rosenstein/Bob Mueller were key players in the Uranium scandal" and asked why Trump would not tweet about removal given the clear conflict.[3] Q drop 2512 (November 30, 2018) stated that "MUELLER will face charges re: Uranium1" and that "he's working to save himself," which Lange reads as evidence that declassification exposed [[robert-mueller|Mueller]]'s participation in covering up treasonous crimes committed during the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration.[3]

Lange links the scandal to the [[anthony-weiner|Weiner]]–[[huma-abedin|Abedin]] laptop, arguing that the newly discovered trove of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] emails dating from her first months as Secretary of State would necessarily contain secret correspondence about the Uranium One deal, exposing Mueller's role as FBI director.[3] On this account [[james-comey|James Comey]] was protecting his friend Mueller as well as Clinton when he buried the material, and [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] shared the same exposure — the "common denominator" of Q drop 1704.[3] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.

## Invoked in the rogue-state proliferation thesis

Badlands contributor Joe Lange invokes the Uranium One investigation at the climax of a May 2024 feature on rogue nuclear states, citing Q drop 1187 of April 19, 2018 and its "classified level highest" notation and concluding: "We were arming our enemies."[4] In his framing the significance of the Uranium One deal becomes clear only once one accepts that American political figures were profiting from helping sanctioned states acquire nuclear weapons with taxpayer money — the same charge he levels regarding [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]]'s undisclosed trips to [[north-korea|North Korea]] and [[armenia|Armenia]].[4]

## The 37 withheld pages

In "[[irregular-warfare|Irregular Warfare]]," Joe Lange revisits journalist [[john-solomon|John Solomon]]'s 2018 reporting in The Hill that, eight years after an FBI informant uncovered criminal wrongdoing inside Russia's nuclear industry, the bureau had identified 37 pages of documents that might reveal what agents told the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration, then-Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and others about the deal — but claimed it must keep the memos secret.[5] The FBI's declaration and list of withheld records, entitled simply "Uranium One Transaction," was posted in its [[freedom-of-information-act|FOIA]] vault, and the handful of documents actually released consisted of already-public letters from members of Congress.[5] Solomon had earlier been the first to report that the FBI, under [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]], ran an undercover investigation into Uranium One before the Obama administration approved the sale to Rosatom.[5]

Lange's explanation for the withholding is that the material remains evidence in an ongoing RICO investigation launched in 2017 by [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] and pursued by [[john-huber|John Huber]] and [[michael-horowitz|Michael Horowitz]], whose limitations period restarts with each connected cover-up crime.[5]

## Rosenstein, Mueller and the cover-up

The Hill's reporting quoted in the article notes that the evidence accumulated while Clinton courted Russia, while former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] collected a $500,000 speech fee in Moscow, and while the administration approved the sale of Uranium One to Rosatom, turning over a large share of America's untapped uranium deposits.[5] The Russian official Mikerin was charged and convicted along with some American officials, but only years later, in a case brought by [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]].[5] Lange argues Rosenstein delivered sweetheart plea deals on minimal charges and thereby participated in the cover-up, and that the Mueller/Comey FBI worked with the Eric Holder/Rosenstein [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] to sweep the matter out of public view.[5] [[qanon|Q]] drop 1318's formula — if Rosenstein is dirty Mueller must be dirty, and vice versa — identifies Uranium One as the "common denominator."[5]

In this account the [[russiagate-hoax|Russian collusion]] investigation was itself the cover-up for Uranium One, serving both to remove [[donald-trump|Trump]] from office and to gather and shield the underlying evidence from disclosure.[5] Q drop 3473 (July 24, 2019) links "[MUELLER]" directly to "U1."[5]
