# September 11th Victim Compensation Fund

The September 11th Victim Compensation Fund provides financial support to first responders, recovery workers, and volunteers who suffered illness or injury as a result of the September 11, 2001 attacks and the subsequent cleanup at the World Trade Center site.[1]

## Trump-era extension

Badlands Media reported that [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] signed a bill extending the victims' compensation fund into law, describing it as part of a broader record of support for the victims of 9/11.[1] The same article asserted that on the day of the attacks Trump, then a private developer, provided thousands of contractors to assist with cleanup and rescue operations and supplied food to those working on scene — a claim the author said most readers were unaware of.[1]
