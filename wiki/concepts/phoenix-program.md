# Phoenix Program

The Phoenix Program was a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operation conducted during the [[vietnam-war|Vietnam War]] which Badlands Media describes as "a CIA death squad program targeting civilians."[1]

## Badlands framing

The program is invoked in the August 2023 Badlands article "Clowns in America" as one of the covert CIA operations under way in Southeast Asia at the moment [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] travelled to Vietnam in late 1967 with his [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Offshore]] business partner [[thomas-j-devine|Thomas J. Devine]], a CIA officer operating under commercial cover.[1] Writer [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] paired it with the [[secret-war-in-laos|secret CIA war in Laos]] to protect the opium trade and argued that the timing of the Bush–Devine trip was not coincidental.[1]

The program is presented as part of a pattern of agency conduct — assassination plots, secret wars, antidemocratic coups and mind-control experiments — that the [[church-committee|Church Committee]] investigations of the 1970s exposed but never stopped.[1]
