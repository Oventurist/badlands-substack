# Disclosure Defense Theory

The **Disclosure Defense theory** is an interpretive framework circulated within [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and the wider [[truth-community|Truth Community]] holding that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is deliberately using the criminal cases brought against him as a vehicle for exposure and disclosure rather than merely defending against them.[1]

## Content of the theory

Under the theory, the indictments and [[lawfare|lawfare]] deployments directed at Trump create courtroom venues in which evidence — particularly evidence concerning the 2020 election — can be introduced legally and placed on the public record, a mechanism its proponents argue maps onto well-known Q drops.[1] A second component holds that the legal campaigns function as the core of Trump's 2024 presidential campaign itself, converting prosecution into political mobilization.[1] The framework is generally invoked alongside [[devolution|Devolution]] as a lens for reading otherwise discouraging headlines emerging from what Badlands writers call the Trump Witch Hunt.[1]

## Application to the Georgia RICO case

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] applied the framework to the August 2023 [[state-of-georgia-v-trump|Georgia RICO indictment]], writing that similar or mirrored takes would recur across the Badlands team — especially from [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] and [[just-human|Just Human]] during Power Hour sessions — on the confluence of indictments and lawfare against Trump.[1] Commenting on [[mark-meadows|Mark Meadows]]'s petition to move his Georgia charges to federal court, he wrote that whether Meadows succeeds remains to be seen, but that the fact he wants to is "a tantalizing hint as to where all roads in the Trump RICO case could be heading down the line," closing with a pointed aside about what the Supreme Court is up to these days.[1]
