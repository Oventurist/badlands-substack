# Calm Before the Storm

"The calm before the storm" is a phrase used by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that Q followers treat as the originating public signal of [[q-operation|the Q operation]]. The phrase is invoked in Q drop #38 of November 2, 2017, and is discussed in the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" chapter covering drops #31–40.[1]

## Significance

The Badlands commentary calls the phrase "self explanatory" and stresses its timing: "President Trump made that statement three weeks before the Q Operation began." Asked whether that is coincidence, the author answers, "I highly doubt it."[1] The remark is thus read as a pre-announcement of [[the-storm|the Storm]] — the anticipated public phase of mass arrests and disclosure.

## Context within drop #38

Drop #38 is read by the commentary as a [[future-proves-past|"future proves past"]] passage warning that when the most serious offenders are apprehended, they will attempt to flee and hostile nation-states may exploit the moment, which is offered as the reason four carrier fleets would be positioned in the Pacific.[1] The drop closes by instructing followers to be prepared to reassure and comfort the public when events unfold — to act as "lighthouses" rather than deliver "I told you so's."[1]

## As deliberate persuasion

The April 25, 2023 essay "The Significance of White Squall" by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] treats the remark as a deliberate act by a master persuader rather than an offhand comment: speaking cryptically of the "calm before the storm" at a signing ceremony weeks before the first Q drop, Trump knew the mainstream media would go into a frenzy trying to paint him as crazy over it.[2] The essay likens the tactic to the martial art of aikido, in which an opponent's force is used against them — Trump counting on the press to boost the signal he wanted to send, that a storm was gathering.[2]

On this reading the media's predictability is itself the mechanism: had it been able to rein in its hubris and simply ignore him, it might have stood a chance, but instead it helped give him the power he wields in the culture war.[2] The essay holds it is no coincidence that the remark came immediately before [[qanon|Q]] appeared, confirming that a storm was coming.[2]
