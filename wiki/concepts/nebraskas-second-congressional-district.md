# Nebraska's 2nd Congressional District

Nebraska's 2nd Congressional District is a three-county area centered on Omaha that carries outsized national significance because Nebraska is one of only two states that split their Electoral College votes by congressional district.[1] That arrangement allows either party to seize a single extra electoral vote, which can prove decisive in a close presidential contest.[1]

## 2024 contest

Ahead of the 2024 election, election officials, local party leaders and voters in the district agreed that the outcome would turn purely on turnout.[1] Democrats worked to peel off the district's electoral vote while Republicans sought to keep the most populous part of the conservative Cornhusker State in the GOP column, with voter registration data and local history indicating a narrow margin between the district going blue or staying red.[1]

Without significant outside spending, local Republicans concentrated on grassroots organizing through [[trump-force-47|Trump Force 47]], the Republican National Committee's joint venture with the [[donald-trump|Trump]] campaign, which targeted low-propensity Republican voters through door-to-door canvassing in Douglas County and elsewhere.[1]
