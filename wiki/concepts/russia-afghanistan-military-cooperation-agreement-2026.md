# Russia–Afghanistan Military Cooperation Agreement (2026)

The Russia–Afghanistan military cooperation agreement of 2026 is a military-technical cooperation accord signed May 27, 2026 at an international security forum in the Moscow region, as reported by [[interfax|Interfax]].[1] The signing followed a meeting between Russian Security Council Secretary [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]] and Afghan Defense Minister [[mohammad-yaqub|Mohammad Yaqub]].[1]

## Terms and statements

Yaqub said ties with [[russia|Russia]] were important and that the two countries had expanded bilateral relations.[1] Shoigu used the occasion to argue that Western countries should unfreeze Afghan assets and take responsibility for post-conflict reconstruction in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1] Interfax had earlier reported Shoigu saying, on May 14, that Russia had built a dialogue with the [[taliban|Taliban]] and was developing a full partnership with Kabul.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the agreement as a callback to August 2024, when President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] invited the Taliban to the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum, where the delegation drew attention by touring the event with shopping bags and newly purchased dress shoes.[1] He noted that the same week, reporting indicated the Biden administration's [[state-department|State Department]] had "mistakenly" sent the Taliban $239 million.[1] He also recalled the 2020 [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], which he said the Taliban honored by hunting down and killing the ISIS-K terrorists responsible for the Abbey Gate bombing that killed 13 U.S. soldiers during the 2021 Afghanistan withdrawal.[1]

In this reading, Putin's formalization of security ties with the Taliban — paired with Russia's public demand that the West lift all sanctions on Afghanistan — signals a move toward reconstruction and full partnership rather than mere tactical alignment.[1]
