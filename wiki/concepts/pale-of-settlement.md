# Pale of Settlement

The Pale of Settlement was the western region of the Russian Empire within whose boundaries Jewish residency was permitted, encompassing much of present-day Ukraine, Belarus, Lithuania, Poland and Moldova.[1]

## Role in Badlands commentary

The Pale of Settlement is a recurring reference point in Badlands writing and livestreams, invoked as the geographic origin of populations whose later migrations the commentary treats as historically consequential.[1] In a July 2024 Badlands News Brief, commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] traced the "Refusenik" Soviet Jewish community — the constituency of [[natan-sharansky|Natan Sharansky]]'s [[yisrael-baaliyah|Yisrael BaAliyah]] party, whose 1996 campaign was run by [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]] — back to the Pale, and used that lineage to argue for a chain connecting Dermer to Ukraine, Russia and Israel.[1]

The same commentary linked this history to claims about organized crime, asserting that a Jewish-Ukrainian mafia migrated to Israel in 1989 and citing a 2017 *[[times-of-israel|Times of Israel]]* article headlined "Is Israel becoming a mafia state?" as corroboration.[1]
