# M10 Booker

The M10 Booker is the light tank developed for U.S. Army infantry forces under a contract the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] awarded to [[general-dynamics|General Dynamics]] in June 2022 — the first new American tank program in more than forty years.[1]

## Significance

The Booker program's timing became a point of commentary after the United States shipped 31 [[m1-abrams|M1A1 Abrams]] tanks to Ukraine in January 2023 and Ukrainian crews subsequently reported that the vehicles were inadequately armored against drone-era threats.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the existence of the Booker contract, awarded before the transfer, suggested the United States was clearing obsolete surplus rather than furnishing cutting-edge capability.[1]

The program was regarded as promising enough that in September 2023 the Pentagon abandoned its plan to upgrade the aging Abrams fleet, choosing instead to develop an entirely new Abrams prototype alongside the Booker.[1]
