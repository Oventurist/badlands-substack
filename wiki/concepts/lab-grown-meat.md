# Lab-Grown Meat

Lab-grown or cultivated meat is animal protein produced by growing harvested animal cells in industrial bioreactors rather than by raising and slaughtering livestock.[1] The technology reached the American consumer market after the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] cleared two cultivated chicken products for human consumption.[1]

## Production method

Producers harvest cells from live animals and from animal tissue, then use those cells to grow meat in stainless-steel tanks known as bioreactors.[1] [[upside-foods|Upside Foods]] and [[good-meat|GOOD Meat]], both based in California, were the first two firms to receive FDA clearance for such chicken products; GOOD Meat also maintains a Singapore office.[1]

## Regulatory posture

The FDA announced in 2022 that Upside Foods' laboratory-derived chicken was "safe to eat," and subsequently stated it had "no further questions" about the product's safety, with Commissioner [[robert-califf|Robert M. Califf]] declaring it fit for human consumption.[1]

## Criticism and the food supply debate

[[natural-news|Natural News]] characterized the products as chicken "grown from CANCER CELLS" and asked whether consumers were ready to eat "tumor nuggets," describing the FDA as a corrupt regulator.[1] Badlands contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] argued that real food and imitation food are nowhere close to the same product, that the food supply is tainted to an extraordinary degree such that avoiding a laundry list of toxins is nearly impossible in many places, and that readers should build direct relationships with farmers.[1]

He further contended that rumors of mRNA technology being engineered into lettuce and other produce were likely true, and that a reasonable person should assume nothing sold in a big chain grocery store or restaurant can be fully trusted.[1] At the same time he urged optimism that human bodies may be far more resilient to the onslaught of toxins than presently understood, invoking the unexpectedly rapid return of vegetation around Chernobyl as evidence that life finds a way, and closing that "your body is not a machine—it's a miracle."[1]
