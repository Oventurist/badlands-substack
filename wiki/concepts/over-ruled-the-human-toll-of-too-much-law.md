# Over Ruled: The Human Toll of Too Much Law

*Over Ruled: The Human Toll of Too Much Law* is a book by Supreme Court Justice [[neil-gorsuch|Neil Gorsuch]] arguing that the sheer volume of American law has grown beyond what anyone can meaningfully know.[1]

## Use in Badlands commentary

Writing about the list of federal statutes potentially violated by [[actblue|ActBlue]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked Gorsuch's thesis in a "more philosophical sense," noting that according to the Justice no one actually knows how much law exists.[1] She announced the book as the next selection for the Badlands Book Club, following [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s *[[government-gangsters|Government Gangsters]]*, which the club still had a few weeks to finish.[1]
