# 2025 South Carolina $30 Million Money Laundering Case

The 2025 South Carolina money laundering case is a federal prosecution announced in April 2025 against three men accused of running a $30 million laundering operation using drug proceeds collected across several South Carolina counties.[1]

## Indictment

Interim U.S. Attorney Brook Andrews announced the indictments on Thursday, April 24, 2025, describing the scheme as "one of the largest professional money laundering operations" in South Carolina.[1] Sumter residents Nasir Ullah, 28, and Naim Ullah, 32, together with Georgia resident Puquan Huang, 49, were charged with conspiracy to commit money laundering.[1]

According to the indictment, the three collected money from drug sales in York, Sumter, Richland and Charleston counties and other parts of the Southeast.[1] The proceeds were then used to purchase electronic devices that were shipped to China, the Middle East and elsewhere for resale.[1] Prosecutors said the suspects used encrypted communications platforms such as WeChat and WhatsApp to coordinate the operation.[1]
