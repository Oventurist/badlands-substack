# Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act

The Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act is a New York State law enacted in 2019 that substantially expanded rent regulation and tenant protections across the state.[1]

Housing organizer [[cea-weaver|Cea Weaver]], then coordinator of the coalition "Housing Justice for All," was described as a key figure in the law's passage.[1] That record was cited when Mayor [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]] appointed her in January 2026 to direct the [[mayors-office-to-protect-tenants|Mayor's Office to Protect Tenants]].[1]
