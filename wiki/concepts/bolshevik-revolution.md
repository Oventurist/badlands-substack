# Bolshevik Revolution

The Bolshevik Revolution is treated in the Badlands Media essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]] as an externally engineered operation rather than a domestic Russian upheaval.

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues that Russia earned permanent enemy status because it "stood up to the [[city-of-london|City of London]] for hundreds of years," citing Russia's refusal of England's demand for an alliance against the Americans during the American Revolutionary War and the blocking of American ports by Russian ships during the American Civil War, which he says prevented British intervention on behalf of the South.

"This is why the City of London unleashed communism on Russia," the article states, "to gain control of this rebellious nation." Overthrowing Russia through the Bolshevik Revolution, in this account, "gave the City of London control of this centuries-old thorn in their side, which they controlled until the early 1990s."

The source adds a wartime casualty comparison as evidence of the Cabal's indifference to Russian life: during the Second World War 450,000 English people died while the Soviet Union lost 24 million, "50 times more." When Soviet control lapsed in the early 1990s, the article contends, the City of London "pivoted from controlling the Soviet Union to Communist China" — a shift it links to Japan's simultaneous thirty-year economic stagnation and China's emergence as an economic juggernaut.


## Red October as a decoding key (A Virus, or a Metaphor?)

The July 2023 essay [[a-virus-or-a-metaphor|A Virus, or a Metaphor?]] treats the October 1917 uprising as the hidden referent behind two recurring [[q-anon|Q]] motifs. [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] summarizes the event as the far-left Bolsheviks overthrowing and later murdering the Russian monarch and his family, ushering in [[communism|Communism]] and the formation of the Soviet Union "largely accomplished through the use of propaganda and chaos" and by dividing the country between the "whites" and the "reds" to foment civil war. He classifies it as a [[color-revolution|color revolution]].

On this basis the article argues that Q's repeated references to [[the-hunt-for-red-october|Red October]] signalled a Communist takeover of the United States by the same method, and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s insistence on dating the [[spanish-flu|Spanish Flu]] to 1917 rather than 1918 — maintained even after public ridicule — was a deliberate pointer to the Bolshevik uprising.
