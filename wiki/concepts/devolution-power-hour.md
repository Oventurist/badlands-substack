# Devolution Power Hour

The **Devolution Power Hour** is the flagship program of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and, by the network's own account, "the show that started it all on the Badlands Media Network."[1]

Hosted by Badlands founder [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] — known publicly as Patel Patriot — the program analyzes recent events through the lens of [[devolution|Devolution]] and [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] theories, the research thesis that first built Herold's audience.[1]

As of the December 2022 schedule the show aired twice weekly at 10:30pm ET: Wednesday nights with co-host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], and Saturday nights with co-host [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]].[1] Like all Badlands programming, it aired live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived in library format on the Badlands Media Network channel.[1]
