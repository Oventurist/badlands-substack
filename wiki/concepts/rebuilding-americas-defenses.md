# Rebuilding America's Defenses

"Rebuilding America's Defenses" is the flagship policy document of the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] (PNAC), the neoconservative think tank co-founded by [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]] and [[william-kristol|William Kristol]].[1] Badlands Media's "Deep State Spotlight" profile of [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] describes the paper as openly advocating total global military domination.[1]

The document is presented in that profile as the written blueprint of the [[neoconservatives|neoconservative]] program that PNAC developed in the mid-1990s and that, in contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s account, has haunted the globe in the decades since.[1] He notes that PNAC pressured then-President Bill Clinton relentlessly to adopt a more aggressive posture toward Iraq, and that its members planted the seeds of war with Iraq and Iran well before the September 11 attacks supplied the pretext they wanted; many of those members subsequently held the highest-level positions in the [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] administration.[1] The profile ties the document's ambitions to the invasion of Iraq that [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] and his adviser Nuland advocated, and to the use of [[nato|NATO]] as a vehicle for bypassing the [[united-nations|UN]] Security Council.[1]

DeLarme further argues that the outcomes France and Germany warned of — regional instability, a strengthened Iran, and a power vacuum that produced ISIS — were precisely what the authors of the PNAC agenda were hoping for, and that Kagan and Kristol therefore bear as much blood on their hands as Nuland does.[1]
