# Secure Enclave (Program)

Secure Enclave is a secretive U.S. federal program intended to establish domestic production of advanced semiconductors with military and intelligence applications.[1] It became publicly prominent in September 2024 when [[intel|Intel Corp.]] officially qualified for as much as $3.5 billion in federal grants to manufacture chips for the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] under the program, following a binding agreement with U.S. officials.[1]

## Scope and structure

The program spans multiple states, including a manufacturing facility in Arizona.[1] Although Intel had always been the frontrunner for the award, the process drew pushback from rival chipmakers, concern in Washington about the wisdom of relying on a single firm for such sensitive production, and a funding fight across multiple agencies and Capitol Hill that threatened to cut into Intel's total award.[1] Reporting by [[bloomberg|Bloomberg]] indicated the funding could be announced within a week of the story.[1]

## Relationship to the CHIPS Act

The Secure Enclave money would come on top of a possible $8.5 billion in grants and $11 billion in loans awarded to Intel in March 2024 under the [[chips-and-science-act|Chips and Science Act]], the law signed by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in 2022 to revitalize U.S. semiconductor manufacturing and reduce reliance on Asia.[1]
