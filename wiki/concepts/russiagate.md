# Russiagate

**Russiagate** is the informal name for the sustained set of investigations, media reporting, and congressional inquiries beginning in 2016 that alleged coordination between Donald Trump's presidential campaign and the Russian government. In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary the episode is referred to almost exclusively as the "Russiagate Hoax," a term signalling the network's position that the allegations were not a good-faith counterintelligence concern but a manufactured operation.

## Badlands interpretation

The Badlands treatment holds that Russiagate was constructed by elements of the United States intelligence and law-enforcement bureaucracy in concert with legacy media outlets, and that it functioned as an [[information-war|information-war]] operation intended to delegitimize and constrain the [[donald-trump|Trump]] presidency rather than to establish any factual finding. On this reading the affair is a case study in how investigative and journalistic institutions can be used as instruments of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]].

## Unraveling

Badlands Blitz Episode 14, published April 25, 2026, devotes a substantial segment to what its announcement calls "the unraveling of the Russiagate Hoax," presenting it as one of several developments in a week of "white pills" and as evidence of a "dawning age of American accountability." The episode links the unraveling directly to the indictment reported against the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]], with hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treating the two stories as parts of a single accountability sequence reaching institutions long thought immune from consequence.


## April 2026: the enforcement phase

The Brief of April 20, 2026 reports the revived [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] investigation into Russiagate's origins: a request for transcripts and records from two [[senate|Senate]] committees involving former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] with a February 23 deadline, the Senate's failure to transmit the materials, and the scheduled start of [[joseph-digenova|Joseph diGenova]] as lead prosecutor on Russia-collusion matters — an inquiry based in Fort Pierce, Florida. The Brief also frames the surrounding politics: [[john-solomon|John Solomon]]'s [[rico-grande|RICO Grande]] (including 2020 election fraud) with a steak-dinner bet with [[roger-stone|Roger Stone]] on indictments by June 20, 2026, Rasmussen polling showing large majorities believing the 2020 election was affected by cheating and the FBI weaponized, and the [[the-atlantic|Atlantic]]'s report on FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] read as a smear to stop the justice phase.


## Monaco's alleged role (May 2026)

In the May 22, 2026 Badlands Brief, contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described former Biden Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]] as, in her opinion, "one of the central architects of the coordinated campaign against President Trump during his first administration," speculated to be a central player in [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]], [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]], and other key RussiaGate plotlines "ostensibly at the direction of Obama and his intelligence community.
