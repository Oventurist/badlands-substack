# Russiagate

**Russiagate** is the informal name for the sustained set of investigations, media reporting, and congressional inquiries beginning in 2016 that alleged coordination between Donald Trump's presidential campaign and the Russian government. In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary the episode is referred to almost exclusively as the "Russiagate Hoax," a term signalling the network's position that the allegations were not a good-faith counterintelligence concern but a manufactured operation.

## Badlands interpretation

The Badlands treatment holds that Russiagate was constructed by elements of the United States intelligence and law-enforcement bureaucracy in concert with legacy media outlets, and that it functioned as an [[information-war|information-war]] operation intended to delegitimize and constrain the [[donald-trump|Trump]] presidency rather than to establish any factual finding. On this reading the affair is a case study in how investigative and journalistic institutions can be used as instruments of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]].

## Unraveling

[[badlands-blitz-episode-14|Badlands Blitz Episode 14]], published April 25, 2026, devotes a substantial segment to what its announcement calls "the unraveling of the Russiagate Hoax," presenting it as one of several developments in a week of "white pills" and as evidence of a "dawning age of American accountability." The episode links the unraveling directly to the indictment reported against the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]], with hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treating the two stories as parts of a single accountability sequence reaching institutions long thought immune from consequence.
