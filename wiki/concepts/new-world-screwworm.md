# New World Screwworm

The New World Screwworm is a flesh-eating parasite associated with outbreaks in Central America that produced the first confirmed U.S. human case in years during the summer of 2025.[1]

## 2025 Maryland case

Federal officials confirmed the first human case of the parasite in the United States connected to ongoing Central American outbreaks.[1] The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention worked with the Maryland Department of Health to confirm the case on August 4, 2025, according to a Department of Health and Human Services spokesperson.[1] HHS spokesperson Andrew Nixon said that "currently, the risk to public health in the United States from this introduction is very low."[1] The parasite was found on a person after they returned from travel to El Salvador, which was at the time experiencing an outbreak.[1]

The case featured in Badlands coverage alongside immigration-enforcement and border stories, with the brief's title referencing "Salvadoran Screwworms."[1]
