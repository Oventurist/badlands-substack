# 2023 Cable News Firings

The 2023 cable news firings refers to the cluster of senior departures from American television news organizations over a single weekend and Monday in April 2023, which independent media treated as a coordinated event rather than a coincidence.[1]

## Sequence of events

On Sunday, April 23, 2023, [[nbcuniversal|NBCUniversal]] announced that chief executive [[jeff-shell|Jeff Shell]] had left his role effective immediately over an "inappropriate relationship with a woman in the company."[1] On Monday morning, April 24, [[fox-news|Fox News]] announced that it and prime-time host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] had severed ties, giving no explanation beyond thanking him for his service and noting that his last show had aired Friday, April 21.[1] The decision had been made on the evening of April 21 by Fox Corporation chief executive [[lachlan-murdoch|Lachlan Murdoch]] and Fox News chief executive [[suzanne-scott|Suzanne Scott]], and Carlson was informed Monday morning.[1] Within an hour of that announcement, [[cnn|CNN]] fired anchor [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] over sexist on-air comments and reports of mistreatment of female colleagues across his seventeen years at the network; Lemon said his agent told him and that he was "stunned."[1]

## Interpretation

CNN's coverage described Carlson as "the right-wing extremist who used his prime time perch at the talk network to exert a firm grip over the Republican Party," a framing [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] quoted deliberately to illustrate the pincer move he believed the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] was caught in.[1] He submitted the "twin & congruent firings" as direct evidence against the existence of coincidence in the [[information-war|Information War]], and listed three anticipated net effects: conflating Fox and CNN as equivalent corporate media in the collective mind despite the vast gap in the two hosts' standing within the America First movement; outing Fox to "normie" MAGA viewers who had remained loyal despite the network's call of Arizona for Joe Biden on November 3, 2020 and its $800 million settlement with [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]]; and continuing the shared ratings-collapse cascade of legacy media while further decentralizing information dissemination.[1]

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], speaking on [[newsmax|Newsmax]] the night of April 24, said he was "shocked" by Carlson's exit, praised him as talented and "terrific to me," and questioned whether it had been a firing at all.[1] Burning Bright read those remarks as implying a timeline for Carlson's turn toward the America First agenda and as a deliberate dig at Fox.[1]
