# 2025 WEF Investigation into Klaus Schwab

In April 2025 the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] confirmed that it had opened an independent investigation into allegations against its founder and former chairman, [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]].[1]

## The investigation

Confirming a Wall Street Journal report, the WEF said its board of trustees "unanimously supported the Audit and Risk Committee's decision to initiate an independent investigation following a whistleblower letter containing allegations against former Chairman Klaus Schwab."[1] Schwab, then 87 and for decades the host of presidents and tycoons at the forum's annual meeting in Davos, said there was "not a shadow of proof" for the accusations and told the Swiss newspaper Blick that he had begun defamation proceedings.[1]

Schwab had resigned abruptly on the Monday preceding the confirmation, "with immediate effect"; in a statement at the time the WEF board praised his "outstanding achievements" across 55 years in charge.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] compared the situation to the prosecution of Al Capone for tax evasion, arguing that "sometimes, the accounting is the only route to justice."[1] She called that an unsatisfying outcome but defended it on the grounds that the American system prioritizes the rights of the accused while organized criminals design their crimes to skirt justice, and that the presumption of innocence and limits on the government's ability to weaponize justice should not be discarded.[1] She read the alleged financial crimes of Schwab's 55-year tenure as "signal," noting that President [[donald-trump|Trump]] had long been invoking the Capone precedent.[1]
