# Iraqgate

**Iraqgate** refers to the hidden aid provided by the United States and its military to [[saddam-hussein|Saddam Hussein]]'s Iraq during its war with Iran in the 1980s.[1] The [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] material quoted in Badlands Media commentary describes [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]]'s role in the affair, both as vice president and as president, as "much clearer" than his contested role in the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra affair]].[1]

The United States is known to have provided the Iraqi regime with biological cultures that could have been used for weapons, with nuclear know-how, and with conventional weapons.[1] As ABC broadcaster Ted Koppel put it in a June 1992 *Nightline* program following the [[gulf-war|1991 Persian Gulf War]], "It is becoming increasingly clear that George [H.W.] Bush, operating largely behind the scenes through the 1980s, initiated and supported much of the financing, intelligence and military help that built Saddam's Iraq into the aggressive power that the United States ultimately had to destroy."[1]

## Badlands interpretation

The article argues that Bush used the [[bank-of-credit-and-commerce-international|BCCI]] bank to fund [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operations during both Iran-Contra and Iraqgate, and that he was shielded by subordinates from blame because he was regarded as "untouchable."[1] It nonetheless notes that special prosecutor Lawrence E. Walsh and some members of the press understood Bush to be heavily involved and reported accordingly.[1] The article maintains that the BCCI scandal was more dangerous to Bush than either Iraqgate or Iran-Contra, because BCCI implicated the whole [[bush-family|Bush family]].[1]

## Assessment

The historical claims are drawn from quoted journalism; the surrounding conspiratorial framework is the author's. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
