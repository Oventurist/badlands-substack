# 2025 Trump Gulf Tour

The **2025 Trump Gulf tour** was US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first major foreign trip of his second term, taking him to Saudi Arabia and Qatar in mid-May 2025 and producing a series of defense, trade, and diplomatic agreements.[1]

## Riyadh

In Riyadh, Trump signed the [[2025-us-saudi-arms-package|US–Saudi arms package]] worth nearly $142 billion, described by the White House as the largest defense cooperation agreement in US history.[1] He also addressed a summit of the United States and the [[gulf-cooperation-council|Gulf Cooperation Council]], reaffirming the US strategic commitment and partnership with the region.[1] On the sidelines of the summit Trump met his Syrian counterpart, [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed al-Sharaa]], a day after announcing the lifting of US sanctions on Syria.[1]

## Doha

Trump then travelled to Doha for the first state visit by a US president to Qatar.[1] Qatari Emir Sheikh [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani]] said the two countries had taken their relationship to "another level" after signing agreements to boost defense and trade ties.[1] The ceremony included record-breaking [[boeing|Boeing]] purchases and new defense contracts, which Trump hailed as the largest aircraft order in Boeing's history.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] produced special live coverage of the Doha visit.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands hosts framed the tour as a mirror of Trump's first foreign trip in May 2017, which was defined by a Saudi [[sword-dance|Ardah]] and the widely circulated photograph of Trump with a glowing orb. In their reading, 2017 was "a declaration of war" and 2025 "a declaration of victory," with the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] no longer concealing itself.[1] One host argued that where the 2017 Ardah was Trump's open defiance of the Deep State alongside Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], the 2025 Qatari Ardah represented Qatar — long an enigma given its ties to the Five Eyes intelligence community and the military-industrial complex — publicly pledging allegiance to Trump, an outcome described as an enormous milestone in Arab diplomacy.[1]
