# Blair Doctrine

The Blair doctrine was unveiled by the end of the 1990s as a new phase of the de-nationalization process that followed the collapse of the Soviet system, explicitly calling for a "post-Westphalia" world order.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the doctrine unleashed a wave of regime change wars in the Arab world beginning with 9/11, with a long-term intention to target Libya, Syria, Iran and Lebanon while expanding [[nato|NATO]]'s hegemony against the potential re-emergence of Russia and China.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) It followed the institutional consolidation of the unipolar order through the European Union, [[nafta|NAFTA]], the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]] and the 1999 destruction of [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall]], which together converted the trans-Atlantic into a cage of post-sovereign nations.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
