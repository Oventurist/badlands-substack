# New START Treaty

**New START** (Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty) is the bilateral United States–Russia nuclear arms control agreement that caps the two states' deployed strategic warheads and delivery systems and provides for mutual verification. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as a subject of [[badlands-blitz-episode-5|Badlands Blitz Episode 5]], published February 7, 2026, whose subtitle — "The END of the START …" — announces the treaty's expiry or termination as the week's principal geopolitical story.

## Treatment in the corpus

The source does not narrate the treaty's provisions; it treats its ending as a test case for the program's recurring analytical question, "when is escalation actually de-escalation?" In that framing, the lapse of the last major arms-control instrument between Washington and Moscow — an event conventionally read as a dangerous unravelling of nuclear restraint — is examined instead as a possible symptom of an underlying realignment, consistent with the [[sovereign-signal|Sovereign Signal]] reading applied across [[the-blitz|The Blitz]]. The episode pairs the treaty's end with coverage of Iran's nuclear file, presenting the two as parts of a single nuclear-order story running through that week's [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].
