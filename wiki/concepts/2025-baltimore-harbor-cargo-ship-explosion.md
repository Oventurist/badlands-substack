# 2025 Baltimore Harbor Cargo Ship Explosion

On Monday, August 18, 2025, an apparent explosion occurred aboard a cargo ship in Baltimore Harbor on the Chesapeake Bay in Maryland, an incident captured in video footage that circulated online.[1]

The vessel was passing near the remains of the Francis Scott Key Bridge at the time of the blast, a location already associated with the earlier collapse of that span.[1] Baltimore City fire officials told WBAL-TV that the fire was fully contained, and fire department spokesperson John Marsh confirmed that no injuries had been reported aboard the ship.[1] The incident was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief for that week.[1]
