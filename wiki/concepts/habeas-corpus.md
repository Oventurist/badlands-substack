# Habeas Corpus

**Habeas corpus** is the legal writ by which a detained person may compel the state to justify their detention before a court. Its suspension is treated in the [[qanon|Q]] literature as one of the formal preconditions for the deployment of the United States military against domestic populations.[1]

## Role in Q drop analysis

Chapter 3 of the Badlands Media series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]], analysing drop #22 of November 1, 2017, addresses Q's question of whether conditions must be met before the [[national-guard|National Guard]] and the [[united-states-marine-corps|Marines]] can operate alongside one another on American soil.[1] The author answers affirmatively, stating that [[posse-comitatus-act|Posse Comitatus]] must be declared and habeas corpus suspended in order for the United States military to activate and serve among its own population.[1]

He points to the presidency of [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]] as the historical precedent in which both conditions were satisfied, describing Lincoln's [[martial-law|congressionally-approved martial law status]] and his use of the military to suppress spies, dissenters and others said to be seeking the destruction of the Republic during the Civil War.[1]

The author reads the drop's closing assurances — that there will be no civil war and no war on American soil, and that any such action would be "clean and swift" — as an indication that any domestic uprising would be quickly suppressed rather than as a promise of prolonged emergency rule.[1]
