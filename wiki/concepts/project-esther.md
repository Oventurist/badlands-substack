# Project Esther

Project Esther is the name given, in Badlands Media's account, to a section of the [[project-2025|Project 2025]] policy manual that outlines a plan to make state-level antisemitism "hate speech" measures into federal policy.[1]

## Context

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] situated Project Esther within a broader campaign in which roughly 40 U.S. states — most of them controlled by the [[republican-party|Republican Party]] — adopted measures classifying criticism of the government of [[israel|Israel]] as hate speech, using the [[international-holocaust-remembrance-alliance|International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance]]'s working definition as the cited authority.[1] Virginia Governor [[glenn-youngkin|Glenn Youngkin]] was described as the first to enact such a measure, in 2022; South Dakota Governor [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] titled her own edict "Ensuring the Security of God's Chosen People."[1]

## Badlands commentary

In this framing, Project Esther represents the federalization of the state-level template and therefore, in the contributor's view, the gravest current threat to the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1] The argument holds that the [[israel-lobby|Israel lobby]] is conflating a political entity — a government — with a religion as a survival tactic, because it does not believe support for Israel can be maintained without penalizing criticism of Israeli policy.[1] The contributor further warned that because the [[ihra-definition-of-antisemitism|IHRA definition]] is explicitly described as fluid and subject to future change, the precedent could later be extended to shield domestic political movements or American governments from criticism.[1]
