# SOFR

SOFR is the benchmark rate system that, in Erik Carlson's account, replaced [[libor|LIBOR]] and shifted control of American banking from London to Washington.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Role in the article

Carlson credits President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with replacing the LIBOR system with the SOFR system, thereby taking control of the American banking system away from the [[city-of-london|City of London]] and giving it to the American [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) He frames the change as part of a silent war Trump has waged against the City of London since early in his first presidency.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
