# SOFR

SOFR, the Secured Overnight Financing Rate, is the American benchmark interest rate that replaced [[libor|LIBOR]]. The Badlands Media essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]] presents the changeover as a decisive act in what it calls a silent war between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[city-of-london|City of London]].

According to [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], Trump "has replaced the LIBOR system with the SOFR system, taking control of the American banking system away from the City of London and giving it to the American [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]." In the article's framing this is not a technical reform of a discredited benchmark but a transfer of sovereignty over dollar-denominated finance from London to Washington — one component of a campaign that also includes onshoring manufacturing out of China and using tariffs to dismantle globalist power.
