# White House Digital Asset Summit

The White House Digital Asset Summit was the first event of its kind, held in March 2025 at the [[white-house|White House]] under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Announcement

Opening the gathering, Trump said: "Welcome to the first-ever White House Digital Asset Summit. Last year, I promised to make America the Bitcoin superpower of the world, AND WE'RE TAKING HISTORIC ACTION TO DELIVER ON THAT PROMISE."[1] The remarks were posted from the official POTUS account on X, and the summit was carried in the Badlands News Brief as one of its bonus items alongside other administration actions of that week.[1]
