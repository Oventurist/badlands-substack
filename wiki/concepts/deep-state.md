# Deep State

The **Deep State** is the term Badlands Media uses for the permanent establishment interests — intelligence services, legacy media conglomerates, aligned governments and non-governmental "watchdog" organisations — that the article holds responsible for the recurring censorship offensives of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## A small playbook

The article's core observation is that the Deep State playbook is actually quite small: the same handful of strategies is rehashed endlessly against successive targets.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) Chief among them is the deployment of sexual-misconduct allegations against effective communicators, a tactic traced through [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]], Matt Gaetz, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and, most recently, [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) The tactic works because accusations alone, absent proof, reliably trigger anger and aggression in men and bonding sympathy with the accuser in women.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Selective protection

The same pundits and media conglomerates that amplify allegations against dissidents are described as having protected Prince Andrew, Bill Clinton, Joe Biden and other accused individuals who remain loyal to establishment dogma — evidence, in the article's reading, that the censorship regime pursues defectors rather than offenders.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Instruments

Beyond reputational attack, the Deep State's instruments in this account include legislation such as the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]] and the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]], regulators such as [[ofcom]], media coalitions such as the [[trusted-news-initiative|Trusted News Initiative]], advertiser-pressure campaigns of the sort run by the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]], and the freezing of publications' assets by financial platforms.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Dependence on public ignorance

A May 2023 essay by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] framed legal opacity as a Deep State asset, asserting that "the Deep State requires our collective ignorance to operate unopposed."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) On this reading the impenetrability of the modern legal code is not incidental but functional, and the countermeasure proposed is a collectively assembled public database of the legal system's most blatant absurdities — knowledge itself operating as the safeguard.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the) The same piece situates the [[united-states-federal-corporation|corporate "United States"]] as an act of maladministration "covered up almost completely," and casts an informed population as the ground-level complement to reform pursued from above by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[qanon|Q]] team.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-startling-contradiction-at-the)

## The indictments dilemma

Badlands commentary holds that the last thing the Deep State wants is for viable [[2020-election-fraud-claims|evidence of election fraud]] to reach a court record — and that the 2023 indictments of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] and [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] make exactly that outcome likely.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) Had no charges been filed, the argument runs, media mouthpieces could simply have continued discrediting Trump indefinitely.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in)

The author offers competing explanations: that the Deep State cannot help itself and loathes the idea of Trump remaining free, or that the prosecutions are part of a plan designed to serve Trump on both the legal and optics fronts simultaneously.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in) He also suggests mass arrests may have been withheld to avoid sparking a civil war, since a sufficient threshold of popular support is needed before Trump and the military could act.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in)

## Distraction and division as method

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] characterized the Deep State's principal 2024 tactic as the manufacture of [[distraction-operations|distraction]], anger, and division, following the ancient logic of [[bread-and-circus|bread and circus]]: content and distracted people tend not to notice or resist government oppression.[4] He argued that political opinion is deliberately herded toward the middle because lukewarm citizens complain but take no real action, leaving policy change to the committed minorities at the edges.[4]

On this account the cascade of mid-2024 events — the debate, the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempt on Trump's life]], [[joe-biden|Biden]] contracting Covid and stepping down, [[kamala-harris|Harris]] taking over, and the [[paris-olympics-2024-opening-ceremony|Olympic opening ceremony]] — was designed to distract and divide rather than to unfold organically.[4] Carlson said that upon recognizing manipulation he turns off his anger and asks what he is being steered away from; his answer in this case was the systematic destruction of the old-guard financial system signalled by [[tax-free-tips|tax-free tips]] and by [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] Conference remarks.[4] He closed with the assurance that the patriots are in control, are not being distracted or manipulated, and that the dismantling of the old guard cannot be stopped.[4]

## Historical roots

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] locates the American Deep State far earlier than the twentieth century, describing an anglophile parasite class that ran both Wall Street in the north and the southern slave power and that repeatedly subverted the anti-slavery statesmen [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]], John Jay, [[john-quincy-adams|John Quincy Adams]], [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]], Charles Sumner, William Seward and [[william-gilpin|William Gilpin]].[5] He calls it a "multiheaded hydra lurking within the heart of the USA" with its own perverse idea of [[manifest-destiny|Manifest Destiny]] standing in diametric opposition to the ambitions of those statesmen.[5]

In Ehret's account the Deep State's economic base was the City of London–Wall Street–New Orleans triangle: between 1801 and 1840 southern cotton exports exploded from 100,000 to 1 million bales a year with 80% going to Britain, New Orleans held over 12% of all U.S. banking capital, and the southern slave states grew into the world's fourth biggest economy with British financial and logistical support.[5] During the Civil War the British supplied weapons, warships, logistical support, intelligence hubs in Canada and funding to the rebels.[5] Ehret adds that constant Deep State sabotage, and an embarrassingly large and independent military-intelligence industrial complex that did not weaken after Kennedy's assassination, obstructed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s attempt to restore a sane security doctrine.[5]

## The 2026 phase shift

In the twenty-fifth episode of [[badlands-blitz|The Blitz]], published 18 July 2026 under the title "It's Treason, Then ...", hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] devoted a full hour to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election fraud speech of the preceding Thursday night and its implications for what they called the Deep State cabal.[6] The hosts treated the speech as seismic, arguing that a sitting president publicly airing election fraud allegations at that scale placed the cabal on defensive footing in a way prior disclosures had not.[6] They framed the moment as the beginning of a true phase shift in both the [[shadow-war|Shadow War]] and the [[information-war|Information War]] simultaneously — a convergence in which covert operational conflict and public narrative conflict were said to be moving in step for the first time.[6]


## Inauguration Diplomacy and the NSC Purge (January 2025)

Writing ahead of [[second-inauguration-of-donald-trump|Donald Trump's second inauguration]] in January 2025, Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the Deep State was by then widely recognized as real by the majority of the American public and deeply despised both domestically and by foreign leaders fighting for the sovereignty of their nations against what he called the feudalism of the US State Department.[7] In the same period, incoming National Security Adviser [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]] announced that all intelligence detailees at the [[national-security-council|National Security Council]] would be removed at 12:01 p.m. on Inauguration Day, a move framed as cleaning out Deep Staters.[7]

## Power projection through limited hangouts

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] describes the [[limited-hangout|limited hangout]] as a signature tactic of the Deep State's power projection apparatus, carried out specifically through the CIA-controlled [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird Media]] to "disguise lies with cleverly-gilded bits of Truth."[8] He cites CIA-inserted JFK assassination narratives as the classic case and applied the framework to the January 2024 [[epstein-court-document-unsealing|Epstein document unsealing]], warning that heavy media amplification suggested victims and targets would be smeared alongside the guilty.[8]

## July 2024 commentary

In the wake of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s withdrawal from the 2024 race, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that a stolen election could serve to trigger a public mandate for election integrity and retribution against the Deep State, while [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had used [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] as "his first cypher to expose the Deep State to the American people" before spending his first term absorbing establishment attacks.[9] Burning Bright also described [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s specialties as including "losing Deep State money & weapons," and characterized 2024 cyber doom-mongering as the Deep State "planting the seeds for an election steal via a false flag cyber attack."[9]
