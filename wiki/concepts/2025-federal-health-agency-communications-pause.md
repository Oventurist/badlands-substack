# 2025 Federal Health Agency Communications Pause

The 2025 federal health agency communications pause was a directive issued in the first days of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second administration instructing federal health agencies to halt all external communications pending review.[1]

## The directive

According to nearly a dozen current and former officials and others familiar with the matter, the administration instructed agencies to pause external communications including health advisories, weekly scientific reports, website updates and social media posts.[1] The instructions were delivered on Tuesday, January 21, 2025 — one day after the new administration took office — to staff at agencies within the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]], among them the [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]], the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] and the [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]].[1] Sources spoke on condition of anonymity to describe private conversations.[1]

Some officials acknowledged that they expected some level of review during a presidential transition, but said they were confused by the scope of the pause and its indeterminate length.[1]

## Effect on pandemic reporting

The CDC had been scheduled to publish several Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report items that week, including three concerning the [[h5n1-avian-influenza|H5N1 avian influenza]] outbreak, according to a federal health official who spoke anonymously.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the narrative for a next pandemic — this time bird flu — had been seeded for months, and that official communication from the health agencies is a central instrument in selling a pandemic.[1] She read the pause as frustrating pandemic narrative control while also signaling that health communications were being routed through a centralized review, concluding: "This is the way. You can't tell the people, you have to show them."[1]
