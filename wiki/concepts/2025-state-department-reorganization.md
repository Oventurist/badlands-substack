# 2025 State Department Reorganization

The 2025 State Department reorganization is an overhaul of the United States [[state-department|State Department]] overseen by Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], described by officials as the biggest shake-up at the department "in decades."[1]

## Scope

Reporting by The Free Press, based on the administration's actual plans, indicated the reorganization would shutter more than 130 offices, including those dealing with human rights, countering extremism, and the prevention of war crimes.[1]

## Rubio's characterization

Rubio said the effort was "not a cost-cutting exercise, although it certainly will provide savings to the American taxpayer," framing it instead as "a policy exercise."[1] On soft power, he argued for realism: "We need to be grown-ups about how we talk about this. Promoting democracy and human rights in our relations, for example, with some country in the Middle East, is going to look different than it would with some country in Central America or South America. That's just the geopolitical reality."[1] On Iran he stated simply, "We do not want a war."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] described Rubio as "making the case to the commies at The Free Press," suggesting the framing was pitched so that what he called Standard-Issue Villagers of the Uniparty Left could understand it.[1]
