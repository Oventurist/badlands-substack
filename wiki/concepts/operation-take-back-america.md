# Operation Take Back America

Operation Take Back America is a nationwide initiative of the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] that, in the Department's own description, marshals the full resources of the DOJ to repel the invasion of illegal immigration, achieve the total elimination of cartels and transnational criminal organizations, and protect communities from the perpetrators of violent crime.[1]

## Application

Federal prosecutors have brought cases under the Operation Take Back America banner that extend beyond immigration enforcement into politically salient violent-crime and arson matters. The indictment of Fort Collins resident [[cooper-jo-frederick|Cooper Jo Frederick]] over a fire at a [[tesla|Tesla]] service center in Loveland, Colorado — charged as malicious destruction of property by fire and possession of an unregistered destructive device — was announced as part of the initiative.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described criticism of the initiative as claims of "apocalyptic overreach" from people on the wrong side of the awakening, arguing instead that the Trump DOJ was pursuing domestic terrorists with abandon and that the individuals charged had likely been under federal observation for years.[1]
