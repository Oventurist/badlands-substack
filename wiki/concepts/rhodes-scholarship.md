# Rhodes Scholarship

The Rhodes Scholarship is a postgraduate award to the University of Oxford established under the will of [[cecil-rhodes|Cecil Rhodes]] and first granted in 1902. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], the program is presented not as an academic honour but as the recruitment mechanism of an imperial cadre.

## The source's characterization

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that the program was "begun in 1902 to advance a re-organized British Empire," has worked alongside the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] for over a century, and has produced more than 7,000 scholars who have permeated across all fields of society — media, education, government, military and corporate. He grounds this in Rhodes's 1877 will, which envisioned members tested for endurance and eloquence, "bound by oath to serve for the rest of his life," and "sent to that part of the Empire where it was felt he was needed."

The article characterizes what "every good Rhodes Scholar is conditioned to do upon their completion of their indoctrination at Oxford" in the case of [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]]: facilitate the transition of the pre-collapse world economy into a green post-collapse world economy. Other Rhodes Scholars named in the source include [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]], who managed Freeland's entry into Canadian politics, and Strobe Talbott, a participant in the post-Soviet liberalization of Russia. A footnote adds that five Rhodes Scholars founded the [[league-of-social-reconstruction|League for Social Reconstruction]] in Canada in 1932.
