# Operation Golden Dynamite

**Operation Golden Dynamite** was the codename for the December 2025 mission that extracted Venezuelan opposition leader [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] from Venezuela so she could attend the Nobel Peace Prize ceremony in Oslo.[1] The name was a nod to Alfred Nobel, founder of the Peace Prize and inventor of dynamite.[1]

## Planning and execution

The operation was led by U.S. Special Forces veteran [[bryan-stern|Bryan Stern]], founder of the [[grey-bull-rescue-foundation|Grey Bull Rescue Foundation]], whose team of U.S. military veterans had only one week to plan the escape.[1] Stern said the mission was made especially difficult by Machado's fame, describing her as "the second most popular person in the Western Hemisphere after [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]]."[1]

## Context

Machado had spent more than a year in hiding after her opposition movement defeated President Nicolás Maduro in the 2024 election by a wide margin according to voting records validated by international observers. Maduro refused to leave office and ordered a massive crackdown on the opposition.[1]
