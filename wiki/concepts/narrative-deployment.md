# Narrative Deployment

A **Narrative Deployment** is a term of art in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] lexicon, used most extensively by the contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], for the deliberate release of a story, framing or messaging event by a faction on what he calls the Game Theory Game Board.[1] The term treats stories as munitions: things that are launched, timed and aimed rather than merely reported.[1]

## Usage

In his January 12, 2023 essay "Right the Ship," Burning Bright described the opening dozen days of 2023 as "a blitzkrieg of Narrative Deployments from each and every side, faction and figure in the American and Global zeitgeist," accompanied by the continued and escalating weaponization of what he termed the Globalist Media Industrial Complexes.[1] Immediate analysis of these converging, conflicting and cascading deployments, he wrote, produced instances of [[narrative-whiplash|Narrative Whiplash]] as readily as cognitive dissonance, making the start of the year spectacularly confusing.[1]

## Seeding versus reacting

Burning Bright offers Narrative Deployments as the basis for a heuristic about who is winning the [[information-war|Information War]]. Because the true extent of the game board is unknowable — though vast, and encompassing virtually every political, military and media faction in the world — he proposes instead observing which sides, factions and public players appear to be *reacting* to Narrative Deployments and which appear to be *seeding* them.[1] Under [[game-theory|Game Theory]], he notes, a combatant need not win every political, administrative, rhetorical or kinetic engagement to win the war, only control more of the board than the adversary.[1]

## Actual and Potential

Closely tied to the concept is the distinction between Actual and Potential Events. Burning Bright argues that Potential Events — those that might have been relegated to the hypothetical, rhetorical or "media" realms — can provoke Actual Events on the game board, and retain real power of their own in the way they shape the directional thinking, and therefore the directional momentum, of the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]].[1] He treats [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 2023 statement describing the [[2023-speaker-of-the-house-election|Speaker of the House contest]] as an "event," in quotation marks, as an example of a deployment signalling control over the play being watched.[1]

He regards patriots' own use of narrative framing as "a necessary evil" — the weapons of the enemy turned against them in furtherance of an [[america-first-movement|America First]] agenda built on exposure, disclosure and eventually justice — while warning that the truth community can itself be swept up in the Kabuki Theater it seeks to expose.[1] His advice to readers confronted with a baffling deployment from either side is to read the confusion not as failure but as a sign that the Systemic paradigm of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] "is beginning to wobble and quake."[1]

## Narratives as vehicles for regimes (December 2022)

In "The War of Stories" (December 30, 2022), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the [[patriot-pendulum|pendulum swing]] practice as being carried out "largely through the deployment of Narratives, which regimes ride into the public levers of power."[2] Although he customarily refers to Narratives as [[potential-events|Potential Events]], he stressed that there is nothing potential about the "Actual" damage they inflict once absorbed, regurgitated and actioned by industry complexes more literal than the media — chief among them the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]].[2]

He also argued that Narrative Deployments are delivered through public cyphers, larger-than-life personalities functioning as vehicles for paradigm-shifting stories, and that recognising this renders the [[game-theory-game-board|Game Theory Game Board]] more legible.[2] His closing appeal to readers was to judge such figures by the net effects of their deployments — not only on the sovereign agenda but on the opposing one — and to observe the counter-deployments of the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] and the shrinking cultural presence of the cyphers it amplifies.[2]
