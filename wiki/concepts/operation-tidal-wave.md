# Operation Tidal Wave

Operation Tidal Wave was a multiday immigration enforcement operation conducted in Florida in April 2025 by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] in partnership with Florida state and local law enforcement agencies.[1] ICE announced nearly 800 arrests in the first four days and described the effort as a "highly successful operation."[1]

## Structure and scale

ICE characterized the operation as "a first-of-its-kind partnership between state and federal partners," stating that "ICE Miami and Florida law enforcement arrested nearly 800 illegal aliens this week during the first four days of #OperationTidalWave — a massive, multi-agency, immigration enforcement crackdown."[1] The agency accompanied the announcement with photographs posted to its X account.[1]

The operation relied on ICE's 287(g) program, which allows the agency to deputize local law enforcement agencies to assist in enforcing federal immigration law — the legal mechanism that made the scale of state participation possible.[1]

## Political framing

Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] praised the joint effort on social media, calling it "an example of FL and [the Department of Homeland Security] partnering to deliver big results on immigration enforcement and deportations," and adding that "Florida is leading the nation in active cooperation with the Trump administration for immigration enforcement and deportation operations!"[1]

Badlands commentary contrasted Operation Tidal Wave favorably with a contemporaneous nightclub raid in Colorado Springs conducted by federal agencies including the [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]], noting that the Florida operation produced roughly eight times as many arrests.[1]
