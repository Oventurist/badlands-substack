# 2025 Israeli Transfer of Lebanon-Captured Weapons to Ukraine

In January 2025, Israel was reported to be considering sending Soviet- and Russian-made weapons captured in Lebanon to Ukraine, with indications that transfers may already have been under way.[1]

## Reported transfers

Israel reportedly met with Ukrainian diplomats on Tuesday, January 21, 2025 to discuss a weapons transfer.[1] In the days that followed, US military cargo planes were tracked flying from Israel to an airbase in eastern Poland.[1]

"There are signs that Israel has begun supplying Ukraine with Soviet and Russian-made weapons," Two Majors, a pro-Russia military blog on the Telegram messaging platform, told its 1.2 million subscribers on Sunday, January 26.[1] The channel posted photographs of dozens of shoulder-mounted missiles laid out on hard-baked ground, along with two screengrabs of a US military plane flying from Ramstein airbase in Germany to Hatzerim airbase in Israel and then on to Rzeszów in Poland, near the Ukrainian border.[1]
