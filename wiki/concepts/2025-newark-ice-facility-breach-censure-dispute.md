# 2025 Newark ICE Facility Breach Censure Dispute

The **2025 Newark ICE facility breach censure dispute** concerned potential House discipline of three Democratic representatives who breached an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] detention center in New Jersey in May 2025.[1]

## Speaker's response

On Wednesday, May 14, 2025, House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] said the three members might face disciplinary action from the chamber.[1] He outlined three possible measures: censure, which he said "probably does seem appropriate here"; removal from committees, which he characterized as "a new tradition begotten by the Democrats in recent years"; and expulsion from Congress.[1] Johnson noted that expulsion requires a two-thirds majority vote, which he described as "not likely" given the current House margins, adding that leadership was "looking at what is appropriate."[1]
