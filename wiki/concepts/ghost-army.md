# Ghost Army

The Ghost Army was a United States Army deception unit of the Second World War, cited in Badlands Media's "Q For Dummies" series as a historical illustration of strategic deception and of [[game-theory-strategy|game theory]] applied to warfare.[1]

## Role in Q decoding commentary

In Chapter 13 of the series, the author invoked the Ghost Army alongside the modern 4th Psychological Operations Group as examples of methods that function as game theory in practice: techniques that generate additional apparent moves against an opponent and thereby force that opponent into a permanent posture of countermoves.[1] The argument was that a side which is constantly reacting is by definition off balance, an idea the author compared to an athlete forced onto his back foot who ends up "playing not to lose" and consequently losing.[1]

This framing sits inside the chapter's larger claim that open warfare against the [[deep-state|deep state]] would be unwinnable, and that the alternative is the use of knowledge, misdirection, and the opponent's own ego to lead adversaries into successive traps while they believe they hold the advantage.[1] The author linked this doctrine to [[sun-tzu|Sun Tzu]] and The Art of War, quoting "Let your plans be dark and impenetrable as night, and when you move — fall like a thunderbolt" and "Appear weak when strong," and noted that both [[donald-trump|President Trump]] and [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] are described as proponents of the text.[1]
