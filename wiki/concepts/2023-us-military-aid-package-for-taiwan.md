# 2023 U.S. Military Aid Package for Taiwan

In late July 2023 the United States announced $345 million in military aid for [[taiwan|Taiwan]], described by the Associated Press as the Biden administration's first major package drawing on America's own weapons stockpiles to help Taiwan counter [[china|China]].[1]

## Contents of the package

The [[white-house|White House]] said on Friday that the package would include defense, education and training for the Taiwanese.[1] According to two U.S. officials who spoke on condition of anonymity because of the sensitivity of the matter, Washington would send man-portable air defense systems (MANPADS), intelligence and surveillance capabilities, firearms and missiles.[1]

## Political context

U.S. lawmakers had been pressuring the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and the White House to speed weapons deliveries to Taiwan, with the stated goals of helping the island counter China and deterring an attack by making the price of invasion prohibitively high.[1] Chinese diplomats protested the drawdown, while Taiwan's representative office in the United States said the decision to pull arms and materiel from existing stores provided "an important tool to support Taiwan's self-defense," pledging in a statement to work with Washington to maintain "peace, stability and the status quo across the Taiwan Strait."[1]
