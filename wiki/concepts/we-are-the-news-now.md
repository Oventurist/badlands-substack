# We Are the News, Now

**"We are the news, now"** is a slogan used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and, in the network's account, by the wider independent-media current from which it emerged.[1] In its February 25, 2023 announcement of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], Badlands described the phrase as "a saying that has come to define a growing movement within the Truth and [[america-first-movement|America First]] communities," adding that the outlet had "been doing our best to make it a reality."[1]

## Meaning and use

The slogan expresses the claim that audiences and independent citizen journalists — rather than legacy outlets — now perform the function of the press.[1] Badlands invoked it as a statement of purpose immediately before crediting its readers' support for the network's growth "into one of the most-watched independent networks online, with dozens of citizen journalists, podcasters and personalities across our shows."[1]

The phrase also frames the network's event programming. The Great American Restoration Tour was pitched as a way for supporters to gather in person and "level up in the [[information-war|Information War]] together," extending the slogan's participatory logic from online viewership to live conferences with panels, Q&A sessions and meet-and-greets.[1]
