# Zombie Apocalypse (Symbolism)

The **zombie apocalypse**, in the June 21, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "The Zombie Apocalypse vs. The Great Awakening" by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], is treated not as a horror-fiction trope but as an occult symbol for the condition in which the minds of the masses have been fully captured.[1]

## Definition

The essay describes the goal of what it calls the dark "elite" cults as the hollowing out of the minds and bodies of all peoples, turning them into zombies.[1] It states that the elites have invoked the zombie-apocalypse symbol for at least a couple of centuries to describe the point of total mental capture, and that because these cults have had a long time to chip away at language and culture their mindset and ideology are embedded in society at a level few suspect.[1]

The operative definition of a zombie is drawn from [[rogue-hypnotist|Rogue Hypnotist]], whose trilogy on vampire-, werewolf- and zombie-themed cults surviving from antiquity to the present is cited: a zombie is a human being who does not possess a will of his or her own.[1] Zombies are described as neither truly dead nor alive, possessing rudimentary motor function and wandering the Earth perpetually hungry — which the essay says is how the world's death cults view humanity.[1]

## "Consumers" and "useless eaters"

The essay reads the terms "consumers" and "[[useless-eaters|useless eaters]]" as variations on the zombie theme, carrying the implication that ordinary people serve no function other than consumption.[1] This is tied back to the secularist-materialist viewpoint that denies transcendent significance to the human spirit or human experience: like a petty conman, the death cults are said to want people to believe their souls do not exist or do not matter, because a person so persuaded is more likely to give the soul up.[1] The author dismisses the whole apparatus as smoke, mirrors and linguistic or hypnotic trickery, and states that the cults want to foment the zombie apocalypse because they want to control this realm.[1]

## Cancellation and the Great Awakening

The essay's central antithesis is that the planned zombie apocalypse can be *cancelled* and replaced by a [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] the moment a sufficient number of people wake up to two truths: that they are not merely physical beings produced by an accident of chemistry and evolution, and that the "powers that be" have no valid claim to authority.[1] The author adds that some people, himself included, believe the war has already been won.[1]
