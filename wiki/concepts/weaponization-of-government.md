# Weaponization of Government

**Weaponization of government** is the term used across the Badlands Media corpus for the alleged deployment of federal law enforcement, regulatory and intelligence power against domestic political opponents. In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 the phrase refers both to the alleged conduct itself and to the ongoing [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] review of federal law enforcement actions being conducted under the second Trump administration.

## The DOJ review

The article reports that the August 10, 2022 email from [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]] to [[sophia-brill|Sophia Brill]] questioning the legal basis of the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago raid]] was itself discovered during that review. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] writes that the email "was reportedly discovered during the Justice Department's investigation into weaponization of federal law enforcement," and cautions that a proper investigation of the subject "would not be a quick undertaking."

## The corpus argument

The central contested claim advanced in the source is that weaponization "during the Biden years was not the work of rogue officials, it was official policy — as evidenced by the resource allocation and the consistency in tactics in field offices across the nation." Periodic news reminders that an investigation remains active are described as "signal in the midst of an all out assault of noise," proof that "what they did to us has not been forgotten." The commentary expresses hope that following the investigation where it leads will show that treatment of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was only "the tip of the iceberg," and concludes that "there must be a justice phase before we can have a golden age."

Related items in the same edition extend the concept in the opposite direction — describing federal subpoenas against left-aligned activists such as [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]] and [[medea-benjamin|Medea Benjamin]] as accountability rather than weaponization.
