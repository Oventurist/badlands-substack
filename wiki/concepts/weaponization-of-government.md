# Weaponization of Government

"Weaponization of government" refers, in Badlands usage, to the deliberate use of federal law enforcement and regulatory power against domestic political opponents, and to the ongoing federal investigation into that conduct during the Biden years.[1]

## The ongoing federal investigation

The [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s review of federal law enforcement actions has become a recurring source of disclosures. It was during that review that an August 10, 2022 email from [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]] to [[sophia-brill|Sophia Brill]] — questioning whether [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had declassified the documents seized in the [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] raid — came to light.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that a proper investigation into weaponization would necessarily be a slow undertaking, and that weaponization during the Biden years "was not the work of rogue officials, it was official policy — as evidenced by the resource allocation and the consistency in tactics in field offices across the nation."[1] Periodic reminders that the investigation remains active are described as "signal in the midst of an all out assault of noise," indicating that the abuses have not been forgotten and are, ostensibly, to be remedied.[1]

Although most such stories concern weaponization directed at Trump, Ashe expressed hope that following the investigation where it leads would show that "45 is the tip of the iceberg," and framed a justice phase as a precondition for any "golden age."[1]

## Adjacent pressure campaigns

Badlands writers link the weaponization file to parallel actions against activist networks — including the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury]] subpoenas of [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]] and [[medea-benjamin|Medea Benjamin]] over the [[nuestra-america-convoy|Nuestra America Convoy]], and moves against the [[southern-poverty-law-center|SPLC]] model of narrative NGOs — as components of a single unravelling of institutional proxies.[1]

## June 2026 cluster of cases

In mid-June 2026 several developments were grouped by Badlands under the weaponization heading in a single week.[2] A federal judge overseeing the criminal case against former [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] barred amicus briefs; the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] declined to revive [[carter-page|Carter Page]]'s FISA surveillance lawsuit against the FBI; California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] announced a [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] investigation he called politically motivated; and newly released DOJ documents revealed internal warnings about the [[doj-school-board-memo-2021|2021 school board memo]].[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that every one of these cases involves weaponized government, but from opposing directions: Comey claims weaponization against him because the government is investigating his own weaponization against [[donald-trump|Trump]] and Trump supporters, while Page was denied a further chance to argue that the government was weaponized against him during Comey's tenure — bringing FISA abuse back into public discussion as [[congress|Congress]] struggled to pass a FISA extension.[2] The recurrence of "internal warnings were raised and ignored" stories, also seen with the Mar-a-Lago raid, was read as evidence that the public is being prepared to recognize weaponized government as a sweeping and long-standing conspiracy.[2]
