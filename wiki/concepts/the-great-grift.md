# The Great Grift

"The Great Grift" is the title of an [[associated-press|Associated Press]] investigation published in June 2023 that found roughly $400 billion in federal Covid-19 relief funds had been stolen or wasted.[1] The findings were amplified by [[the-federalist|The Federalist]], which presented them as evidence that shutting down the economy and replacing economic activity with government transfer payments was a costly strategic error.[1]

## Findings

The AP analysis determined that the federal government appropriated nearly $5.2 trillion on Covid-related efforts during the pandemic, including roughly $3.2 trillion spent under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the $1.9 trillion stimulus package Democrats passed after President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] took office.[1] The Federalist tied the scale of that spending, and the fraud within it, to the nearly $32 trillion federal debt, noting the investigation followed earlier AP work showing significant relief spending on projects with little connection to the coronavirus.[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

Contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] argued that $400 billion was "only a drop in the bucket" and framed the pandemic response as a "scamdemic" designed to seize control, throw the country into disarray, and enable election rigging.[1] He contrasted the absence of oversight over relief fraud with the aggressive prosecution of January 6 attendees and the harassment of parents at school board meetings, writing that the I.R.S. and [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] prioritized domestic terrorism while pandemic loan fraud went unchecked.[1] He also pointed to small businesses lost permanently, families ruined, and social division over vaccine status as the human cost of the programs.[1]
