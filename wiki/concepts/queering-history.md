# Queering History

"Queering history" is the term used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] to describe what he characterizes as a deliberate strategic objective of [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxist]] cultural warfare: the retroactive recasting of prominent historical figures as homosexual or transgender in order to establish a new historical narrative.[1]

## Esler's framework

Writing in February 2024 about the [[netflix|Netflix]] series depicting [[alexander-the-great|Alexander the Great]] as homosexual, Esler argued that commentators should move beyond generic references to communism and Marxism and describe events explicitly as communist or Neo-Marxist warfare, so that the lens of warfare — strategic objectives, operations to achieve them, and the tactics composing those operations — could be applied.[1] In that framework, "queer history" is a primary strategic objective and the Netflix series is an operation serving it.[1]

Esler described Neo-Marxist warfare as focused on establishing "counter-hegemonies," which target a cultural power structure perceived to have a white, heteronormative, cisnormative patriarchal figure at its top and then invert that structure; he characterized [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]] and [[esg|ESG]] scores as massive counter-hegemony operations.[1] Tactically, he wrote, "queer history" operations target a single cultural figure and use them to build a new historical narrative, citing earlier claims that Joan of Arc was transgender as a precedent.[1]

He urged critics to name the phenomenon as warfare rather than mere political difference, arguing that those who do not understand they are facilitating it have been ideologically subverted into participating, and calling for bringing "the war itself into the Overton window."[1]
