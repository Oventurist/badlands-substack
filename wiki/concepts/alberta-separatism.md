# Alberta Separatism

Alberta separatism refers to the movement seeking greater sovereignty for the Canadian province of Alberta, up to and including independence from the Canadian federation. In 2026 the movement reached an institutional milestone when Premier [[danielle-smith|Danielle Smith]] announced an October referendum asking Albertans whether the province should begin the legal process toward a future binding independence vote.[1]

## Federal response

Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] warned Alberta separatists not to repeat what he described as the "dangerous bluff" of [[brexit|Brexit]], invoking his firsthand experience as governor of the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]] during the United Kingdom's 2016 vote to leave the European Union.[1] Carney argued that Alberta voters should be wary of the claim that separation is merely a negotiating tactic with Ottawa, contending that many Britons were told they could vote for a "soft" exit and negotiate the terms later, only to face years of political and economic fallout.[1]

## The Brexit analogy and "Project Fear"

Critics immediately noted the irony of Carney's intervention: he had been one of the most prominent establishment voices warning Britain against Brexit in 2016, and pro-Brexit conservatives accused him at the time of running "Project Fear."[1] Alberta conservatives now make the same argument — that federal elites are trying to scare voters away from asserting provincial leverage and sovereignty.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described Canadian independence from the Crown as an effort she could support, arguing that her principal objection to Canada is that it swears allegiance to Britain and therefore functions as a "global regime proxy" on America's northern border.[1] She characterized Carney as "the crown's man" white-knuckling the status quo, and linked her support for the Alberta separatists to earlier support for the Canadian trucker convoy and for a broader "Canexit," noting that a Canadian declaration of independence in America's 250th year would carry symbolic weight — while conceding she is "not optimistic it will happen."[1]
