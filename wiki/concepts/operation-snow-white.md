# Operation Snow White

**Operation Snow White** was a covert program conducted by [[scientology|Scientology]] that revolved around the infiltration of the United States government.[1] It is cited in the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as one of two candidate readings for the "Snow White" references that appear in the [[qanon|Q]] drops.[1]

## Relevance to the Q drops

Drop #80 of 5 November 2017 closes with a mention of Snow White following Q's confirmation of the [[alice-and-wonderland|"Alice & Wonderland"]] cipher.[1] The Q for Dummies author states that the reference could point to several different things, and that the most popular reading — and the one he aligns with — is that Q was directing readers toward the [[snow-white-supercomputers|CIA supercomputers]] named after the characters in *Snow White*.[1] He offers Operation Snow White as the alternative possibility, linking to an account of the Scientology infiltration story.[1]
