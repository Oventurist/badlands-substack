# Common Core State Standards Initiative

The **Common Core State Standards Initiative** is a set of standardized academic benchmarks for American public education. In the Badlands Media "Structures of Control" series it is presented as a central instrument in what the article calls the transformation of American education, a case study in [[4d-timeline-control|4D timeline control]].[1]

## Claims made in the series

The article states that the Common Core was spearheaded by the [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]], introducing a standardized approach to education that it says deprioritizes creativity and exploration in favor of test-based performance metrics.[1] Common Core mathematics is singled out for criticism on the grounds that it focuses more on abstract processes than on practical problem-solving, leaving students and parents confused and frustrated.[1]

The initiative is discussed alongside "global citizenship" curricula attributed to the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], which the article says emphasize interconnectedness and compliance over critical thinking.[1] Taken together, these programs are characterized not as isolated policy changes but as deliberate tools to erode sovereignty and critical thinking while fostering compliance with a centralized, globalist order.[1] The frustration of parents watching children struggle with what the article calls nonsensical Common Core methods is offered as evidence validating the broader thesis.[1]

## Claimed outcomes

According to the article, students conditioned by these frameworks graduate prioritizing international governance structures such as the United Nations [[sustainable-development-goals|Sustainable Development Goals]] over national sovereignty or local accountability, and come to treat centralized authorities such as the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] as the ultimate arbiter of truth and justice.[1] The claimed result is a citizenry adept at following rules but ill-equipped for independent thought, in which compliance is a virtue and skepticism is stigmatized — a population the article says is primed to accept digital IDs and [[central-bank-digital-currency|centralized digital currencies]].[1]
