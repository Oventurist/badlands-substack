# Trump Order to End the Penny

In February 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] called for the U.S. Treasury to discontinue production of new pennies, announcing the decision through a post on social media.[1]

## Rationale

Trump highlighted the financial inefficiency of penny production as the primary motivation.[1] The cost of manufacturing a single penny exceeds two cents, and a U.S. Mint report from the prior year indicated that the full process of making and distributing each penny amounts to more than three and a half cents.[1]

## Context

The directive was reported alongside a broader wave of cost-cutting and institutional restructuring in the early weeks of Trump's second term, including the wind-down of [[usaid|USAID]] and scrutiny of [[fema|FEMA]] spending driven by the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]].[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] filed the item among the brief's "bonus items," pairing the theme of international dealmaking with what it wryly termed "collectible pennies."[1]
