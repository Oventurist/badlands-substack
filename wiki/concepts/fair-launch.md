# Fair Launch

A "fair launch" refers to a cryptocurrency project that gives everyone an equal chance of acquiring tokens regardless of status — no insider, founder, or early backer is privileged with an allocation above any other participant.[1] For an investor attempting to be informed and responsible in a market populated by scammers, whether a project is fair launch is a key factor in the decision to invest.[1]

## Misuse as a marketing claim

Badlands Media documented the claim's abuse in the case of [[saitama-token|Saitama Token]], whose website advertised a fair launch while 5.76 quadrillion tokens — roughly 5.8 percent of the initial supply — were quietly reserved as undisclosed "team tokens" for a group of about eight people.[1] The article characterized this as, in the traditional sense, a textbook case of misleading investors, and argued it would constitute a violation of [[securities-exchange-act-of-1934|Section 10(b) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934]] where those reserved tokens were subsequently used to manipulate the market and defraud investors.[1]
