# Trump-Kennedy Connection

The **Trump-Kennedy connection** is the claim, developed in [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series and drawing on [[frederick-dodson|Frederick Dodson]]'s article "The Hidden Trump-Kennedy Connection," that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] and [[kennedy-family|Kennedy]] families are linked by ancestry, association, and shared opposition to the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]

## Personal and social links

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]] were famous friends.[1] The series notes that Trump possesses [[john-f-kennedy|JFK]]'s rocking chair, which the author reads as highly suggestive of a "throne" passed from one "king" to the next.[1] The Trump children were known to be friends with the children of former Supreme Court justice [[anthony-kennedy|Anthony MacLeod Kennedy]], a coincidence the author underscores.[1] JFK Jr. is reported to have told Trump to run for office, and once in office Trump briefly placed [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|RFK Jr.]] in charge of vaccine policy for his administration.[1] The families are also reported by *Forbes* to co-invest with one another to the present day.[1]

## Genealogical claims

The author reports rumors that the Kennedys were the original royalty of Britain prior to being ousted by the Windsors, saying he has found nothing convincingly substantiating this but considers it a distinct possibility.[1] He argues that the name "Kennedy" harkens back to Scotland and suggests a connection to ancient Druidic bloodlines once considered royalty, pointing readers to [[michael-tsarion|Michael Tsarion]]'s *The Irish Origin of Civilization*.[1]

In the 15th century, the series says, a man named Ulric Kennedy set up a home base on the Isle of Skye, while Trump's mother [[mary-anne-macleod-trump|Mary Ann Macleod]] was born on the nearby Isle of Lewis; the Macleod clan's Dunvegan Castle also stands on Skye.[1] The author concedes these connections do not yet prove anything but argues they strongly suggest the families have known each other for centuries and warrant further investigation.[1]

## Significance in the series

Combined with the claim that Trump's ancestral surname *Drumpf* is German — and with [[erhard-landmann|Erhard Landmann]]'s thesis about the global reach of ancient German — the Druidic royalty connection is used to argue that both families descend from a culture of massive worldwide influence, forming a counter-elite to the [[koryos|Koryos]]-descended cult.[1]
