# September 2023 Ukraine Supplemental Spending Request

The September 2023 Ukraine supplemental spending request was a $40 billion supplemental funding package sought by the Biden administration, of which roughly $24 billion was designated for Ukraine, that triggered an internal revolt among House Republicans against Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]].[1]

## Congressional fight

Members of the House Freedom Caucus demanded that the Ukraine money be separated from the remainder of the bill rather than passed as a single package.[1] ZeroHedge framed the confrontation as the opening of another round of "shutdown theatre," in which Republicans posture before capitulating to Democrats at the eleventh hour.[1]

Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] simultaneously warned McCarthy that he was expected to open an impeachment inquiry into President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] over his family's foreign business dealings or risk losing the speakership.[1] Speaking to radio host Todd Starnes, Gaetz said, "I worked very hard in January to develop a toolkit for House Republicans to use in a productive and positive way. I don't believe we've used those tools as effectively as we should have," adding: "That means forcing votes on impeachment. And if Speaker McCarthy stands in our way, he may not have the job long."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that McCarthy occupied the speakership only because [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had directly encouraged [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]], Gaetz and other Freedom Caucus members to acquiesce during the January 2023 speakership fight.[1] In this reading, the Gaetz–McCarthy feud represented an accelerating destruction of the [[uniparty|Uniparty]] establishment in public consciousness, elevating the Freedom Caucus and Trump as the genuine political future of the United States, with the exposure of what he described as a money-laundering arrangement between the U.S. deep state and Ukraine as an added benefit.[1]
