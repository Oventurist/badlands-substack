# International Security Force (Gaza)

The International Security Force (ISF) is a proposed multinational force for [[gaza|Gaza]] set out in a draft United Nations resolution circulated by the United States in November 2025.[1]

## Draft resolution

On Monday, November 3, 2025, the United States sent several members of the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] a draft resolution establishing an international force in Gaza for a duration of at least two years.[1] The draft, designated "SENSITIVE BUT UNCLASSIFIED," would give the United States and other participating countries a broad mandate to govern Gaza and provide security through the end of 2027, with the possibility of extensions thereafter.[1] The draft was to form the basis for negotiations among Security Council members, with the goal of a vote within weeks and the deployment of first troops to Gaza by January.[1] A U.S. official stressed that the ISF would be an "enforcement force and not a peacekeeping force."[1]

The force is intended to provide security during a transition period in which Israel gradually withdraws from additional parts of Gaza and the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] conducts reforms enabling it to take over Gaza in the longer term.[1]

## Commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the two biggest implications of the resolution were that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] expects Turkish soldiers to form part of the ISF and that he intends for [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] and the Palestinian Authority to govern Gaza long-term — both conditions that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] has vowed would never occur.[1] He contended that Turkish participation is a strategic imperative because [[turkey|Turkey]] is a [[nato|NATO]] state, which would effectively bring Gaza under the protection of [[article-5|Article 5]], meaning an IDF attack on the ISF would place Israel at war with NATO.[1] He further argued that Trump gave his word to Abbas on May 3, 2017 that he would help liberate Palestine from Israeli occupation, and that Trump treats such a promise as a sacred bond.[1]
