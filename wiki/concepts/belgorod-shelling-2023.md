# Belgorod Shelling (December 2023)

The Belgorod shelling was a strike on the centre of the Russian border city of Belgorod on Saturday, 30 December 2023, which killed 21 people, including three children, according to local officials.[1]

## The attack

A further 110 people were wounded, said regional governor Vyacheslav Gladkov, making it one of the deadliest attacks on Russian soil since the start of Moscow's invasion of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] 22 months earlier.[1] [[russia|Russian]] authorities accused Kyiv of carrying out the strike, which came the day after an 18-hour aerial bombardment across Ukraine killed at least 41 civilians.[1]

Images circulating on social media showed burning cars and plumes of black smoke among damaged buildings as air raid sirens sounded.[1] One strike hit close to a public ice rink in the heart of the city, which lies 25 miles north of the Ukrainian border and 415 miles south of Moscow; previous attacks on Belgorod had rarely occurred in daylight or claimed as many lives.[1]

The [[russian-ministry-of-defence|Russian Defence Ministry]] said it had identified the ammunition used as Czech-made Vampire rockets and Olkha missiles fitted with cluster-munition warheads, providing no additional information that could be independently verified.[1] "This crime will not go unpunished," the ministry said in a statement on social media.[1]
