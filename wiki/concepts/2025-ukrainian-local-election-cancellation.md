# 2025 Ukrainian Local Election Cancellation

In October 2025 Ukrainian lawmakers voted to delay all local elections and extend the powers of regional councils and officials until martial law is lifted in the country.[1] Local elections in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] had been scheduled to take place at the end of October 2025.[1]

## Parliamentary vote

MP Yaroslav Zheleznyak announced on Wednesday, October 8, 2025 that parliament had passed a resolution officially recognizing that it is impossible to hold elections during wartime.[1] The decision was adopted with 308 deputies voting in favor, none against, and one abstention.[1] "To put it simply, there will be no local elections in October 2025," Zheleznyak wrote on Telegram, citing the inability to guarantee democratic standards and the safety of voters.[1]

The resolution blames the inability to hold elections on Russia and states that local councils and mayors will remain in office until martial law is lifted.[1] It affirms that a decision on new local elections will be made in accordance with the constitution, the electoral code and the laws of Ukraine once the conflict ends.[1] The move followed repeated delays of presidential and parliamentary elections by [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]].[1]
