# Public Service Loan Forgiveness

Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) is a federal student-loan relief program under which borrowers employed in public service — including teachers, nurses, law enforcement officials, and first responders — have their remaining loan balance eliminated after making 120 qualifying monthly payments.[1]

## July 2024 cancellation

On Thursday, 18 July 2024, the [[education-department|Education Department]] announced that the Biden administration was cancelling an additional $1.2 billion in student loans for roughly 35,000 public-service borrowers through changes made to the existing PSLF program.[1] [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] said in a statement that the approved borrowers "are public service workers — teachers, nurses, law enforcement officials, and first responders who have dedicated their lives to strengthening their communities, and because of the fixes we made to Public Service Loan Forgiveness, they will now have more breathing room to support themselves and their families."[1]

## Contrast with the SAVE plan

The announcement came the same day that the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-eighth-circuit|8th Circuit Court of Appeals]] blocked the administration's broader [[save-plan-litigation|SAVE repayment plan]] in full.[1] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the juxtaposition to argue for a long-term view of "emotion-stoking narratives," noting that while the initial announcement of mass loan forgiveness provoked "much gnashing of teeth and white-knuckled takes about the communist, dictatorial nature of such a decree," what actually materialized fell far short of what Biden's most radical supporters expected, and even those provisions were being placed on hold in many states.[1] The lesson he drew was to observe the effects of a "Narrative Deployment" before reacting to it.[1]
