# Donroe Doctrine

The "Donroe Doctrine" is a term used in Badlands commentary — a portmanteau of Donald and [[monroe-doctrine|Monroe]] — for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s assertion of unapologetic American primacy in its hemisphere and Arctic approaches, enforced through tariffs, deployments and open ultimatums rather than through multilateral institutions.[1]

## Davos 2026 as the doctrine "in full bloom"

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that Trump returned to Davos in January 2026 "not to ask permission, but to remind them of the new rules — the Donroe Doctrine in full bloom," resting on what he called the mass sifting of 2025 and the mass accelerant of 2026.[1] He enumerated its instruments: tariff deployments that bent supply chains to American will; a [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]] in Venezuela that intercepted the globalist [[color-revolution|color revolution]] playbook and turned it into "a rug-pull for the ages"; and unapologetic posturing over [[greenland|Greenland]], framed as a non-negotiable national security imperative under Trump's 2025 National Security Strategy.[1] The message across every vector, he wrote, is that "Power is Power."[1]

## European reaction

Burning Bright characterized the [[collective-west|Collective West]]'s demeanor as having shifted "from smug dismissal to a brittle cocktail of denial and capitulation."[1] The [[european-parliament|EU Parliament]]'s trade committee froze approval of a US trade deal indefinitely citing coercion and territorial integrity; [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]] said the bloc was "prepared to act"; and [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] floated the [[anti-coercion-instrument|Anti-Coercion Instrument]] against the United States for the first time.[1] Markets, panicked earlier in the week, "shrugged in response to the response," and Slovak Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] said openly that the EU is "not taken completely seriously" by world leaders.[1] In this reading, the cards have been laid on the table and Europe does not hold them.[1]
