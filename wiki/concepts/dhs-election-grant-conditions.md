# DHS Election Grant Conditions

DHS election grant conditions refers to a reported [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration policy under preparation in June 2026 that would condition certain [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] grants on states adopting specified election-related measures.[1]

## Reported terms

According to CNN reporting, the guidance under preparation could require states to adopt hand-marked paper ballots, manual audits, and additional citizenship verification procedures in order to remain eligible for the funding.[1] More than $1 billion in grant funding could be affected, though the policy had not been formally announced as of the Sunday preceding the report.[1]

## Legal and political reaction

Any effort to link homeland security funding to election administration would likely face legal challenges, with critics arguing that the federal government cannot compel states to administer elections in a particular manner, while supporters contend the changes would strengthen election security.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the initiative would allow President Trump to say he did everything he lawfully could to secure elections, while those opposing him would be on record defending the existing system, and urged readers to remember who protected that system once "the truth about that system has the social permission to be believed."[1] She also noted that [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] had said on "The Daily Herold" that election disclosure was expected that Wednesday.[1]

## Related disputes

The reported policy emerged amid continuing election administration tensions in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona, where County Recorder [[justin-heap|Justin Heap]] and the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Board of Supervisors]] were locked in a struggle over ballot drop-box locations and chain-of-custody procedures.[1]
