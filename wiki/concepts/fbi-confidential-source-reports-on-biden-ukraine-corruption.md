# FBI Confidential Source Reports on Biden Ukraine Corruption

The FBI confidential source reports on Biden Ukraine corruption are a set of bureau records — known as FD-1023s — documenting allegations from three separate confidential human sources that the family of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] engaged in corruption in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## Disclosure

[[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] said there is no record that the bureau sought to thoroughly investigate the claims.[1] Senate Judiciary Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] of Iowa and Senator [[ron-johnson|Ron Johnson]] of Wisconsin released two new FD-1023s — records of reporting from the bureau's confidential human sources — focused on allegations of Biden family corruption.[1]

## Relationship to the 2023 memo

The newly released records match closely a previous memo Grassley released in 2023 containing similar claims.[1] Grassley said he wants to determine why the FBI apparently failed to fully investigate those allegations, telling Patel at a September hearing that "to date, the FBI has never answered Congress whether they investigated the text messages, audio files and financial records referenced in that 1023."[1]
