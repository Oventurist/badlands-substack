# Gaia Cult

The "Gaia cult" is the name given by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] to what he describes as the state religion of the New World Order: a generalized worship of Gaia, the same Earth Mother worship that shaped the cult systems of much of the ancient world.[1]

## Rationale

Ehret argues that since ancient times it has been understood that no political structure can function through simply utilitarian force, and that the social engineers setting the stage of the New World Order therefore knew a new religious system would be needed to keep populations obedient and controllable during the "End of History" age.[1] The only difference between this new cult and the earlier Earth Mother cults of ancient Rome, Persia and Babylon, he writes, is that the 21st century variant carries a more "scientific" veneer and is expressed in terms such as "reducing carbon footprints," "sustainable development," "adapting to carrying capacity" and "stopping global warming."[1]

## Legalization

The UN's Millennial Development Goals, [[agenda-21|Agenda 21]] (later renamed [[agenda-2030|Agenda 2030]]) and the [[earth-charter|Earth Charter]] — all overseen by [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]], [[steven-rockefeller|Steven Rockefeller]] and even [[mikhail-gorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]] — did much to legitimize fringe pseudo-scientific Gaia concepts as international legal doctrine.[1] Strong was himself a devoted follower of the Gaia religion during his lifetime and called for creating a "World Gaia Day" as an international holiday marking the inauguration of the UN's Conference on Sustainable Development in 1992, over which he also presided.[1]

## Consequences and resistance

Over the ensuing years vast pressure was placed on nation states to castrate themselves on the altar of the war against global warming, soon renamed climate change, and to defer to un-elected boards of [[ipcc|IPCC]] experts.[1] Ehret describes modern Gaia worshippers as a religious-like movement that will not easily abandon its obsession with saving nature from humanity, and treats the healing of several generations of brainwashing as a slow undertaking.[1] He connects the cult directly to the [[depopulation-agenda|depopulation agenda]] and the [[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]], arguing that a world covered by solar panels would in fact increase desertification through heating.[1]
