# Powell Renovation Criminal Investigation

The Powell renovation criminal investigation is the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] probe opened in early 2026 into [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] over claims that he misled [[congress|Congress]] about a $2.5 billion renovation of the Fed's headquarters.[1]

## Grand jury subpoenas

The Justice Department issued grand jury subpoenas in connection with the allegations, escalating what had previously been a political dispute over the cost and scope of the headquarters project into a criminal matter.[1] Powell characterized the probe as politically motivated pressure on monetary policy and vowed to resist it.[1]

## Context

Badlands presented the investigation alongside Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]]'s probes into dark-money NGOs and the wider anti-cartel campaign as elements of a single convergent enforcement posture by the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] against entrenched institutional actors.[1] Bessent, in the same period, said 5–10% of the federal budget is lost annually to fraud and improper payments and framed his own waste-elimination work — including a new whistleblower program — as continuing the mission of [[doge|DOGE]].[1]
