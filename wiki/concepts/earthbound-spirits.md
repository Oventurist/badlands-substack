# Earthbound Spirits

**Earthbound spirits** are, in the framing used by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] in the [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series, demonic or human-derived beings that remain "stuck" on Earth rather than passing on after death.[1] The theme is described as recurring persistently in the author's research into the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult, and as appearing in variant forms across spiritual and religious systems worldwide, with a general consensus that many ghosts were people who died in a state of trauma or confusion that prevented them from "going back into the light."[1]

## Ritual manufacture

The article's central and most contested claim is that dark occultists perform sacrifice rituals in part to *create* such spirits deliberately, producing ghosts that remain controllable by their sacrificers after death.[1] For this to work, the child must die believing that its soul is under the eternal control of those killing it, a belief said to thwart passage into the light.[1] The author states that the most potent ghost-energy is held to come from children with kind hearts, and lists the necessary conditions as belief in the sacrificer's legitimate authority, belief in that authority's power to grant or deny Heaven, and conviction of one's own irredeemable badness — the impressionability of children being what predisposes them to become loyal ghosts.[1] He presents the claim as testimony received from a person born into an elite family and independently confirmed by an acquaintance working with [[satanic-ritual-abuse|satanic ritual abuse]] survivors, rather than as documented fact.[1]

## Voluntary possession

The Koryos are described as intentionally permitting such spirits to possess their bodies in the belief that this conferred supernatural abilities, including superhuman strength and ferocity.[1] Combining this with the account of ritually tortured children, the author proposes that an ancient science of mind control existed in which children were groomed into ferocious spirits who then entered the bodies of other cult members to imbue them with the same demonic ferocity.[1]

## Hypnosis across the veil

Because the article treats hypnotic conditioning as affecting both the living and the dead, it argues that [[post-hypnotic-triggers|post-hypnotic triggers]] can remain active "across both sides of the veil," which the author takes as evidence that hypnosis operates directly on mind rather than merely on brain.[1] The related account of spirit hierarchies given by Dr. [[jerry-marzinsky|Jerry Marzinsky]] supplies the counterpoint: earthbound spirits can be reasoned with, shown that their handlers lied to them, and helped into the light.[1]
