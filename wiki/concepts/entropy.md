# Entropy

Entropy — the second law of thermodynamics — is discussed in Badlands Media reporting as the organizing dogma of the [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] and closed-system worldview.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues that a mind operating within this paradigm is "forced to presume that the 2nd law of thermodynamics is the only dominant law shaping all change in every closed system they try to understand, from a biosphere, to a brain, to an economy and to the entire universe, while ignoring all evidence of creative change, design and purpose built into the entire fabric of space time."[1]

He connects this to zero-sum Hobbesian geopolitics, in which all "wholes" are thought of merely as the sum of their parts, and any material system existing at a given moment is all that can ever exist because creative change and universal principles are denied existence.[1] The counter-tradition of [[henry-c-carey|Henry C. Carey]] and [[lyndon-larouche|Lyndon LaRouche]] instead treats increased human mentation as raising the power to support life over time, refuting fixed [[limits-to-growth|limits to growth]].[1]
