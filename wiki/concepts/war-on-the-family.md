# War on the Family

The **war on the family** is the long-standing focus of [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s work and the entry point for his AI experiment in "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Because of that concentration, his initial prompts to ChatGPT were passages from the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]] emphasising the 6.6.6.'s fixation on the family's annihilation as a strategic requirement for the [[new-world-order|New World Order]]'s implementation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The quoted Protocols passage states that to construct a World Government in which all individuals are subject to the "World State" of the "New Order," it is necessary first to remove the family — which will in turn lead to the loss of ancestral religious teachings — and second to level all individuals by removing the social classes, particularly the middle classes.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

ChatGPT's reading of that passage placed the eradication of the family structure among the secondary strategic objectives, interpreting it as a means to weaken social bonds and allegiances not tied to the World Government and to make individuals more dependent on the central authority.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

In the fuller synthesis, the weakening of traditional family structures is pursued through the [[satanic-inversion|principle of inversion]], which portrays those structures as oppressive and regressive, and through [[critical-race-theory|CRT]] and [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] challenging binary gender roles and family norms.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Supporting measures include the use of the [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]] to frame overpopulation as a threat linked to traditional family values; [[esg|ESG]] rewards for organisations advocating the deconstruction of traditional family roles; and public education curricula that frame the choice between career and traditional family life as a path to empowerment.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Cultural mechanism in "Parenting For Freedom"

In "Parenting For Freedom" (May 12, 2023), [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described the practical, day-to-day form of this campaign as a broken chain between the [[nuclear-family|nuclear family]], the local community, and mass media, in which parents say and do one thing while community and culture contradict them and mass media propagandizes further.[2] He argued that parents today, through no direct fault of their own, largely act as handlers for globalist tyrants and technocrats, having been recruited into an insidious campaign designed to deflate the innocence and joy of childhood and replace it with endless homework, toxic school environments, and social isolation.[2] Children are then offered as respite overly stimulating and mentally distorting media — video games, children's programming, and sexualized music — while social media displaces real, healthy social interaction.[2] He cited the transgender craze harming young girls as an illustration of community and culture confusing rather than guiding children.[2] His proposed counter was the recovery of the [[role-model|role model]], attention to the [[zone-of-proximal-development|zone of proximal development]], and the practice of [[courage-and-radical-acceptance|courage and radical acceptance of the truth]], which he claimed could raise children immune to propaganda, deception, and corruption.[2]
