# The Sum of All Fears

*The Sum of All Fears* is another Tom Clancy novel, later a film, that [[qanon|Q]] referenced frequently and that [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats as a coded warning.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Premise as summarized

In Carlson's summary, a group of [[modern-day-nazis|modern-day Nazis]] play Russia against the United States: the Nazis unleash a bioweapon on [[ukraine|Ukraine]], attack a US Naval ship, and detonate a nuclear bomb in Baltimore, all in the hope of getting the US and Russia to destroy each other.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Claimed message

Q, Carlson argues, is using the story to say that the real modern-day Nazis are trying to create a war between Russia and the United States, and that readers should not fall for it.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He adds that the two people the Nazis have never been able to control are [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Carlson signals that he intends to explain who the Nazis really are in a subsequent post, saying he hopes to blow some minds.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Elsewhere in the same article he identifies the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]] as modern-day Nazis, with Communism serving merely as their tool.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## The film and Q's use of it in "We Are Watching a Movie"

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] gives the film's particulars from a Brave search engine overview: *The Sum of All Fears* is a 2002 American action-thriller directed by Phil Alden Robinson, based on the 1991 [[tom-clancy|Tom Clancy]] novel of the same name, and a reboot of the Jack Ryan series with Ben Affleck as a young [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] analyst.[2] The plot, set in 2002, has a group of rogue Russian nationalists led by a former KGB agent attempting to start a war between the United States and Russia by detonating a nuclear bomb at a football game in Baltimore, with Ryan uncovering and thwarting the conspiracy.[2]

Lange treats the film as deliberate propaganda on two axes: it demonises nationalism in order to glorify globalism, and it demonises Russia — a purposeful narrative he says [[hollywood|Hollywood]] repeats because the cabal hates [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] for refusing to submit.[2]

## As a Q keyword for the Uranium One plot

In Lange's reading, Q's repeated capitalised invocation of "THE SUM OF ALL FEARS" is a pointer to a real operation rather than merely a film.[2] The relevant drop traces the route "Uranium 1 - Canada - Europe - Asia - Iran/North Korea" and asks what other bad actors were involved, whether the US knew, where the cash payments went, how many planes delivered and whether all planes landed in the same location — before concluding "The world cannot handle the truth. This pill cannot be swallowed by most. Risk in painting this picture. THE SUM OF ALL FEARS."[2] Q drop #1306 of April 30, 2018 asks where the U1 material ended up, whether it is traceable, what would happen if it ended up in [[syria|Syria]], and to "define cover."[2]

Lange's synthesis is that the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] and the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran nuclear deal]] existed to place traceable weapons-grade uranium in a Syrian facility, financed by cash flown to [[iran|Iran]] at night, so that a dirty bomb detonated on American soil could be blamed on Russia and trigger nuclear war — the plot of the movie enacted for real.[2] This, he argues, is why the [[2018-us-strikes-on-syria|April 2018 strikes on Syria]] mattered. These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
