# The Sum of All Fears

*The Sum of All Fears* is a Tom Clancy novel, later adapted as a film, that the Badlands Media corpus identifies as one of the works most frequently referenced in the [[q-anon|Q]] drops. In A Virus, or a Metaphor?, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] summarizes the premise and offers a geopolitical reading of why Q would cite it repeatedly.

## Premise as summarized

As described in the article, the story follows "a group of modern-day Nazis" who play Russia against the United States. The Nazis unleash a bioweapon on Ukraine, attack a U.S. Naval ship, and detonate a nuclear bomb in Baltimore — all in the hope of getting the United States and Russia to destroy each other.

## The decoded warning

The essay reads this as a direct message: "Q is telling us the real modern-day Nazis are trying to create a war between Russia and the US. Don't fall for it." The article further asserts that "the two people the Nazis have never been able to control are Trump and Putin," naming [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] as the obstacles to that plan. The bioweapon element of the plot is connected elsewhere in the essay to its claims about biolabs in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].

The author identifies the Nazis of the plot with the [[cabal|Cabal]], the entity he describes as swinging [[communism|Communism]] like an axe, and promises a subsequent post explaining "who the Nazis really are."
