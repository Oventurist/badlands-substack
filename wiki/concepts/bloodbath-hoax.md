# Bloodbath Hoax

The "bloodbath hoax" is the term used in Badlands commentary for the March 2024 media campaign in which corporate outlets and Democrat-aligned accounts recast a passage of a [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] speech as a threat of political violence.[1]

## The campaign

Corporate media and allied social media operators, including the Biden/Harris campaign team, launched what ZeroHedge characterized as a disinformation and misinformation campaign against Trump by purposely misconstruing his remarks at a Saturday event.[1] The framing was propagated over roughly fifteen hours by leftist newspapers, figures within the Biden administration, and Democrat lawmakers.[1] Legacy outlets including NBC News, [[abc-news|ABC News]] and [[politico|Politico]] were named among those that took the speech entirely out of context.[1]

## Exposure and walkbacks

The counter-reaction on X was rapid, producing what the coverage described as "an awakening ... of just how bad Democrats are at propaganda."[1] [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] commented on the episode with the line "Legacy media lies," and MSNBC's [[joe-scarborough|Joe Scarborough]] deleted his own "bloodbath" post after being called out by Musk — the first of a series of walkbacks.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] opened his commentary with the truism "when someone shows you who they are, believe them," arguing that narrative coordination by the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] surprises no anon, citizen journalist or direct-path reasoner.[1] He drew two "white pills" from the affair: that the time between the deployment of a media operation against Trump, the MAGA base or the [[truth-community|truth community]] and its complete exposure by that same decentralized collective had shrunk exponentially from years to months to minutes; and that the enemy are not the only ones coordinating their messaging, since anyone doubting that Trump and Musk were "in cahoots" was, in his words, watching a different movie.[1]
