# US and Israeli Targeted Killings in Syria and Lebanon (July 2025)

On Friday, July 25, 2025, the United States and [[israel|Israel]] announced that their respective forces had killed terror leaders from [[isis|ISIS]] and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in separate counterterrorism operations.[1]

## The operations

[[centcom|CENTCOM]] troops carried out a raid in al-Bab, Aleppo Governorate, [[syria|Syria]], killing senior ISIS leader Dhiya' Zawba Muslih al-Hardani and his two adult sons, who according to CENTCOM also had ties to the organization.[1] CENTCOM stated that three women and three children who "were also on the target" were unharmed in the raid, and that "these ISIS individuals posed a threat to U.S. and Coalition Forces, as well as the new Syrian Government."[1] In a separate operation, the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] targeted Hezbollah.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that there was "something duplicitous" about the framing of the Fox News headline, which he said appeared designed to imply that the US and IDF had worked together on both operations, effectively merging Hezbollah and ISIS in readers' minds.[1] As the article itself explained, the US military struck ISIS while the IDF conducted a distinct operation against Hezbollah.[1]

He noted that Hezbollah was one of the few groups to first oppose and fight ISIS in 2011, when it came to the aid of Syrian President [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]], and has therefore been fighting ISIS as long as anyone.[1] In his account, Hezbollah and the IDF are fighting because Israel is occupying southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and southern Syria, and because Hezbollah has pledged to fight the IDF until it leaves [[gaza|Gaza]].[1] He concluded that [[fox-news|Fox News]] continues to obfuscate information in ways that benefit the regime and its pro-war agenda.[1]
