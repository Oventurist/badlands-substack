# Charles University Shooting

The Charles University shooting was a mass shooting that took place on Thursday, December 21, 2023, at Charles University in Prague, Czech Republic, leaving at least 15 people dead.[1]

## Events

Fourteen people were killed at the university and the perpetrator also killed his own father, bringing the death toll to at least 15; twenty-four other people were wounded at the university.[1] The gunman, a 24-year-old student of world history at Charles University, killed himself after the shooting spree in central Prague.[1] He had first killed his father at their family home in the town of Kladno, outside Prague, according to Radek Jiroudek, a police officer with Interpol Prague.[1]

## Investigation

Police partly identified the assailant as David K., following the European practice of releasing only a first name and last initial for privacy reasons.[1] At a news conference in Prague, the chief of the national police force, Martin Vondraska, said the assailant "got inspired by a similar terrible event abroad," without specifying where.[1] Authorities said they did not believe the gunman's actions were connected to international terrorism.[1]

The incident was reported by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
