# Executive Order 14282

Executive Order 14282 is an order issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] concerning transparency regarding foreign influence in American higher education.[1]

The order was cited in February 2026 as the basis for a new interagency agreement between the [[state-department|State Department]] and the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]] to strengthen enforcement of foreign funding disclosure requirements at U.S. colleges and universities under [[section-117-higher-education-act|Section 117 of the Higher Education Act]].[1]
