# Premierato

The Premierato is a proposed set of Italian constitutional reforms championed by Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] that would modify Italy's traditional parliamentary system so that the prime minister is elected directly by the people to a five-year term.[1]

## Purpose

Under the existing system, the prime minister is appointed by the president on the basis of coalition support within Parliament — support that can be withdrawn at any time, triggering new elections. Badlands analysis attributes to this volatility the fact that Italian governments do not typically survive longer than a year or two before collapsing.[1] The Premierato is intended to deliver greater political stability, described as a hallmark of Meloni's administration, which took office on October 22, 2022 and became the second longest-serving government in post-WWII Italian republican history in May 2026.[1]

## Context

The Premierato is pursued in parallel with the [[nordio-reforms|Nordio Reforms]] on judicial career separation, which failed at referendum in March 2026.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public taunting of Meloni ahead of the [[2026-ankara-nato-summit|Ankara NATO summit]] effectively made her a martyr, boosting her support inside Italy and across Europe and strengthening the mandate she could apply to constitutional reform.[1]
