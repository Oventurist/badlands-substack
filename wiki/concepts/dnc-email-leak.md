# DNC Email Leak

The **DNC email leak** refers to the cache of Democratic National Committee emails and documents published by [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] in July 2016.[1]

## Contents and effect

The material showed how the committee had railroaded Bernie Sanders in favour of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] during the 2016 Democratic presidential primary.[1] It was released by [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] while he was living inside the [[ecuadorian-embassy-in-london|Ecuadorian Embassy in London]], and was followed months later by the [[podesta-emails|John Podesta emails]] and, in March 2017, by [[vault-7|Vault 7]].[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] notes that this release, along with the Podesta material, is the principal reason many in the truth community regarded Assange as a "white hat."[1] Carlson argues that damaging Clinton and [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] is not by itself proof of patriotism, pointing out that neither figure was ever imprisoned as a result, and raising the possibility that the two had been selected by the [[cabal|Cabal]] as scapegoats whose exposure Assange merely executed.[1] Notably, the Ecuadorian government did not cut Assange's internet access after the DNC release — that step came only after the Podesta emails, and lasted six weeks.[1]
