# Accelerationism

Accelerationism, in the usage adopted by the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and by Badlands Media commentators, refers to the deliberate hastening of a hostile system's collapse rather than its direct destruction — allowing internal contradictions and economic pressure to bring about a transition in stages.[1]

## Official adoption in Cuba policy

In May 2026 reports described the administration's escalating economic pressure campaign against [[cuba|Cuba]]'s regime as, in the words of one senior administration official, "the best way to describe it is 'accelerationism'" — explicitly glossed as the philosophy of hastening societal collapse.[1] Senior officials framed the goal as methodically constricting Cuba's economy and creating conditions for a peaceful transition away from the current regime without immediate regime elimination, with limited humanitarian assistance routed through non-government channels such as the Catholic Church and charities to demonstrate alternatives to the existing system.[1]

## Badlands usage

"Accelerate" is a recurring closing refrain in the Badlands Brief, used to signal approval when establishment structures are being pushed toward visible failure.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] responded to the official's quote by asking whether the Trump administration had "been reading the Badlands News Brief," offering himself as a consultant on the subject.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] deployed the same refrain after the federal court ruling on voter citizenship checks, arguing that establishment legal obstruction is failing and that "the weeping and gnashing of teeth will continue."[1]

The concept overlaps with the Brief's wider reading of narrative conflict: that prolonging a story cycle — as in the Iran coverage generated by the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] — can itself accelerate the exhaustion and self-exposure of the opposing side.[1]

## The runaway train metaphor (2024)

In April 2024, Badlands contributor
[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described accelerationism
as the strategy he and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] had championed as the
most logical way to protect a runaway train targeted by bandits for ambush: the
train cannot be stopped or slowed, because doing so would only make it easier
to attack — hence, in his framing, Operation Warp Speed.[2] He applied the
concept to France's converging crises alongside Burning Bright's "Crisis
Cascade" and his own [[horseshoe-theory|Horseshoe Theory]], closing with the
exhortation "Accelerate, anons."[2]
