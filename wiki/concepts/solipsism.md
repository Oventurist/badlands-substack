# Solipsism

**Solipsism** is the philosophical position that "only the Self exists."[1] In the Badlands Media essay "The Extraordinary Power of Digital Soldiers" (July 31, 2023), the author writing as [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] treats solipsism as the hidden content of mainstream [[law-of-attraction|law of attraction]] teaching and as a nihilistic doctrine deliberately propagated to the public.[1]

## The moral objection

The essay's principal argument against solipsism is moral rather than epistemological: morality itself ceases to have any basis in a solipsistic universe where only one mind and its thoughts exist, because morality requires a universe containing two or more beings.[1] Popular LOA teaching is said to inherit this defect wholesale by insisting that every fortune and misfortune is self-created and that the individual is at fault for everything that happens to him.[1]

## Civilizational framing

Solipsism is described as antithetical to both Christianity and science, and therefore incompatible with Western civilization.[1] Its mainstreaming after the 1960s is characterized as a watershed moment for the ruling class, likened to a culture-wide lobotomy, and attributed to occult fraternities pursuing a centuries-long project to displace Christian ethics and Greco-Roman empiricism.[1] The author contrasts solipsism with the position he defends, in which human consciousness is embedded within a wider reality by way of a shared universal language — a move he argues rescues the intelligible portion of the LOA while discarding its solipsistic form.[1]
