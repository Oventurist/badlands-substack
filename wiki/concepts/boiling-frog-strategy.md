# Boiling Frog Strategy

The "boiling frog" theory is an apologue used to describe a strategy in asymmetrical warfare in which pressure is escalated so gradually that the adversary fails to recognize the danger until it is too late.[1] In April 2024 The Cradle applied the metaphor to [[iran|Iran]] and its regional allies in West Asia.[1]

## The metaphor

As the legend goes, a frog placed in a shallow pot of water heating on a stove will remain happily in the pot as the temperature climbs, and will not jump out even as the water slowly reaches boiling point and kills it, because the change of one degree at a time is too gradual to notice.[1] Although the story is a fable rather than a biological fact, it is frequently invoked by militaries and geopoliticians to describe the "long game" of reaching strategic objectives.[1]

## Application to Iran and West Asia

The Cradle argued that it is Iran and its regional allies who are using a measured approach to increase temperatures in West Asia until the water boils the US and Israeli "frogs" to death, and that strategy, discipline and rare patience — the antithesis of Western short-termism — will bring Iran victory.[1] The article quoted the Taliban aphorism "Americans have watches, but we have the time," and stated that time is now on the side of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] and its regional allies, which it said were calibrating temperatures like scientists in a laboratory.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the article encapsulated what he believed had been happening in the Middle East for more than six months.[1] He argued that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s rapid ascent to the office of Prime Minister in 1995, on the heels of Yitzhak Rabin's assassination, was an insurgency catalyzed by the 1993 signing of the [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]], which had promised the end of the IDF's occupation of Palestine and a two-state solution — accords Netanyahu has boasted he intentionally sabotaged upon entering office.[1]

In his framing, everything since [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] became President and [[mohammed-bin-salman|MBS]] became Crown Prince has been a counterinsurgency against Netanyahu, culminating in the 2020 signing of the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]].[1] Both [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and Palestine were absent from the original Accords, but once they were signed MBS dispatched his most capable diplomat, [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]], to facilitate a Saudi Peace Initiative that would bring both parties in and deliver on Trump's promise of Middle East peace.[1]

That mission, described as on the cusp of completion, was disrupted by the October 7 attack, after which Israel engaged in conventional kinetic war seemingly convinced its provocations would draw Iran and others into the conflict and thwart the Accords entirely.[1] Iran instead showed restraint, opting for what the commentator called 5th Generation irregular warfare, while Israel continued to shed blood and destroy its own credibility in the eyes of the world — a dynamic he summarized with the maxim "never interfere with an enemy when he's in the process of destroying himself."[1]
