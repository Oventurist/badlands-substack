# Strait of Hormuz

The Strait of Hormuz is the narrow waterway connecting the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman and the open sea, through which a large share of the world's seaborne crude oil passes. Its control has long made it a strategic pressure point in confrontations involving [[iran|Iran]].

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports continued clashes in and around the strait during the United States–Iran confrontation of mid-2026. American forces carried out strikes on Iranian targets and enforced a naval blockade on Iranian shipping, while Iran launched retaliatory missile and drone attacks and threatened broader disruption of regional energy flows, with each side accusing the other of violating ceasefire arrangements.

Reopening the strait "without tolls" was reported by Axios to be a central provision of the draft [[islamabad-agreement|Islamabad agreement]] between Washington and Tehran, paired with sanctions relief tied to Iranian compliance and a 60-day extension of the ceasefire.


## The coalition refusal and the oil war (March 2026)

The March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-1cd|Badlands Brief]] reports that [[nato|NATO]] allies refused President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s call for an international coalition to secure the strait, with France, Germany, Spain, Italy and Greece all declining. Analysts quoted in the Brief framed the resulting dilemma as a "[[kobayashi-maru|Kobayashi Maru]]": retreat risks a Suez Crisis-style defeat, while continuing threatens prolonged energy market chaos and higher global inflation.

In the same edition, following the reported killing of [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]] and Basij commander [[gholamreza-soleimani|Gholamreza Soleimani]], [[iran|Iran]] escalated into what it called a "new phase of oil war," launching drone swarms against energy infrastructure including the Shah field in the UAE, Iraq's Majnoon field and targets in Saudi Arabia, while Iranian officials continued to assert control over the strait and warned that its security situation had permanently changed.
