# Department of Education Organization Act

The **Department of Education Organization Act** is the 1979 federal statute that split the Department of Health, Education, and Welfare into the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]] and the Department of Health and Human Services.[1] President [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]] signed the act into law on October 17, 1979, and the new cabinet-level department began operating on May 4, 1980.[1]

## Opposition

Upgrading education to cabinet-level status in 1979 was opposed by many in the Republican Party, who regarded the department as unconstitutional on the grounds that the Constitution does not mention education, and who deemed it an unnecessary and illegal federal bureaucratic intrusion into local affairs.[1] During the 1980 presidential campaign Governor [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] called for the department's total elimination, and the 1980 Republican platform called for undoing the act's central creation; a Democratic House of Representatives prevented Reagan from doing so.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In a January 2025 Badlands feature, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] characterized Carter's signing of the act on his way out of office as the founding act of what he calls a federal "re-education" apparatus, arguing that once the department survived Reagan's defunding campaign it became the mechanism by which the [[deep-state|Deep State]] could dictate not only where federal education money went but what was taught in American schools.[1]
