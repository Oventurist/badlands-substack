# Disinformation

**Disinformation**, as defined in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], is the art of blending truth and falsehood into a dizzying web designed to stir the passions of the masses and lead them toward specific actions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The definition emphasizes admixture rather than outright fabrication: the difficulty is not knowing that the media lies but sifting which parts are true.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Mechanism

Disinformation is analyzed as an emotional rather than purely informational operation. Because heightened emotion makes a subject suggestible, the countermeasure proposed is to remain as emotion-neutral as possible amid chaos.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) A key feature of divisive propaganda is that it must both activate the amygdala and provoke a *pre-programmed* response; fear and rage alone are insufficient if the resulting energy is not discharged in a direction chosen by the social engineers.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Media environment

The essay notes that image manipulation tools have advanced from Photoshop to deep fakes, such that "anybody can make anything look like anything," warranting near-categorical skepticism of what one finds online and off.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Society nonetheless demands that people pick sides before they have any reasonable opportunity to work things out for themselves.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author argues that being legitimately "awake" means recognizing a world of fake news designed to hijack cognition by first hijacking emotions and needs.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Related techniques include the misuse of [[nominalization|nominalizations]] and hypnotic repetition.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## "Disinformation is Necessary"

A contrasting treatment appeared in [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s feature "Disinformation is Necessary," published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on 28 October 2025 and later highlighted in the 7 November 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] round-up of recent features.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) The piece frames disinformation not simply as an attack on the public but as a deliberate instrument, posing as its organizing question: what would be the purpose of creating confusion?[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

In that feature Lange grounds the argument in Sun Tzu's maxim that "the whole secret lies in confusing the enemy, so that he cannot fathom our real intent," and in Q drop 1318, which asked directly, "What would be the purpose of creating CONFUSION?"[3] His thesis is that disinformation is necessary in war and deception is required, because the purpose of both is to hide one's plans from the enemy.[3] The main battlefield, he writes, is for the hearts and minds of the people: the conflict is mostly an information war, and control of the narrative is contested by both sides.[3]

Lange applies the principle to the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller special counsel investigation]], arguing that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] fed the false narrative of being a victim of a witch hunt as purposeful deception and cover for what was actually happening, using himself as bait to trap the enemy.[3] He cites Q drop 4373, which declared, "YOU ARE WITNESSING THE GREATEST [COORDINATED] DISINFORMATION CAMPAIGN TO EVER BE LAUNCHED AGAINST THE AMERICAN PEOPLE. INFORMATION WARFARE. INFILTRATION V INVASION INSURGENCY. IRREGULAR WARFARE."[3] The [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] counterintelligence investigation and its handoff to the special counsel are characterized as a disinformation campaign designed to convince Americans that Trump stole the 2016 election and was a puppet of Russia.[3]

A further example offered is the judiciary: the narrative that the entire judicial branch is corrupted and fighting Trump is described as itself disinformation, since in Lange's reading the resistance comes from a small number of district judges found through "judge shopping," while the Supreme Court repeatedly overturns their rulings.[3]

## "Disinformation is necessary" in Drop #97

The Badlands Media "Q For Dummies" series drew attention to the line at the foot of Drop #97 stating that disinformation is necessary.[4] The author endorsed the proposition, cautioning readers that not everything [[qanon|Q]] says is directed at them and not everything Q says is true, because red herrings must sometimes be thrown out to keep an adversary off track.[4] He connected the practice to the drops' explicit acknowledgement that both friends and enemies monitor the boards, and to his own inference that some crumbs function as instructions to operators rather than as messages to anons.[4] Within the chapter the concept is treated as an element of [[game-theory-strategy|game theory]] rather than as deception of followers.[4]

## "Disinformation is necessary" in the Q drops

Chapter 10 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series treats [[qanon|Q]] drop #72 of November 4, 2017 as the clearest statement of the operation's use of disinformation, in which Q told readers that both disinformation and distractions are real and necessary.[5] The commentary explains this on the grounds that hostile actors read the drops alongside anons, so that mixing falsehood with truth kept those actors guessing: if researchers could not determine exactly what was happening, neither could the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[5] The cited example is the set of earlier drops implying imminent arrests of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[john-podesta|John Podesta]], characterised as red herrings and markers for future events while the actual target was the [[house-of-saud|Saudi Royal Family]].[5] Drop #74 adds that everything has meaning, including disinformation and typos.[5]
