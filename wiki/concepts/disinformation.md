# Disinformation

**Disinformation**, as defined in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], is the art of blending truth and falsehood into a dizzying web designed to stir the passions of the masses and lead them toward specific actions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The definition emphasizes admixture rather than outright fabrication: the difficulty is not knowing that the media lies but sifting which parts are true.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Mechanism

Disinformation is analyzed as an emotional rather than purely informational operation. Because heightened emotion makes a subject suggestible, the countermeasure proposed is to remain as emotion-neutral as possible amid chaos.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) A key feature of divisive propaganda is that it must both activate the amygdala and provoke a *pre-programmed* response; fear and rage alone are insufficient if the resulting energy is not discharged in a direction chosen by the social engineers.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Media environment

The essay notes that image manipulation tools have advanced from Photoshop to deep fakes, such that "anybody can make anything look like anything," warranting near-categorical skepticism of what one finds online and off.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Society nonetheless demands that people pick sides before they have any reasonable opportunity to work things out for themselves.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author argues that being legitimately "awake" means recognizing a world of fake news designed to hijack cognition by first hijacking emotions and needs.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Related techniques include the misuse of [[nominalization|nominalizations]] and hypnotic repetition.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## "Disinformation is Necessary"

A contrasting treatment appeared in [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s feature "Disinformation is Necessary," published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on 28 October 2025 and later highlighted in the 7 November 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] round-up of recent features.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) The piece frames disinformation not simply as an attack on the public but as a deliberate instrument, posing as its organizing question: what would be the purpose of creating confusion?[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)
