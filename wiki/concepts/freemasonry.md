# Freemasonry

Freemasonry is the fraternal tradition named in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] as one of the two bodies whose practice of Hermetic teachings has caused the tradition to be viewed with suspicion, the other being the [[hermetic-order-of-the-golden-dawn|Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn]].[1]

DeLarme writes that although Hermeticism "often gets a bad rap" for having been practiced by the Freemasons and the Golden Dawn, the teachings themselves are relatively neutral, and it is the way in which they are applied that determines whether they are for good or ill.[1] On that basis he proceeds to survey the [[corpus-hermeticum|Corpus Hermeticum]], the [[emerald-tablet|Emerald Tablet]], the [[divine-pymander|Divine Pymander]] and the seven principles of [[the-kybalion|the Kybalion]] on their own terms.[1]
