# Operation Choke Point

**Operation Choke Point** is the name applied, in commentary circulating on Twitter after the March 2023 [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] collapse, to an alleged coordinated effort to strangle disfavored parts of the financial system in order to clear the way for a central bank digital currency.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[jb-white|J.B. White]] summarized the speculation in "The Schedule F Imperative - Part 2" (March 13, 2023).[1]

## The four-step plan as described

As reproduced by White, the alleged plan runs: Step 1, Operation Choke Point; Step 2, devastate regional banks, venture capital, stablecoins and crypto without any chance of bailouts; Step 3, chastise those entities for being too risky for retail investors and make examples of them; and Step 4, introduce [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDCs]] as the "much needed, risk-free" alternative.[1]

## White's reading

White wrote that he was predisposed to favor that kind of speculation, that it filtered through his "healthy skepticism" and rang true, but asked whether the theory was incomplete.[1] He noted that Silicon Valley Bank was unlike most large American banks in that almost all of its business involved technology startups and venture capital speculators, and asserted that close inspection revealed "curious connections" between figures in Silicon Valley and the Chinese Communist Party, and Russians, running through the bank.[1] Citing reports that at least 90% and perhaps upwards of 97% of SVB deposits exceeded the FDIC insured limit of $250,000, he argued the institution was "most definitely not a 'regular' bank" but a unique tool within the banking system, and wondered whether it had been a knowing tool of the "Invisible Enemy" he called the Corporatist Industrial Complex.[1]

Within his broader thesis, White treated the collapse as a possible instance of the true [[operation-warp-speed|Operation Warp Speed]] turning the adversary's own plan against it, asking whether that battle plan includes a "controlled demolition of certain corporate sectors" and whether it takes advantage of Operation Choke Point in another "Backatcha, bitches!" fashion.[1] He closed the section by asking readers whether imminent storms were approaching on the corporate horizon, especially within the financial services industry he described as favored by "the criminal syndicate of criminal syndicates."[1]
