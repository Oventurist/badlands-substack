# Badlands Blitz Episode 19

**Badlands Blitz Episode 19**, subtitled "The Media Guesses the Game ... Too Late," is the nineteenth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video recap published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] through its Substack newsletter. The announcement post is dated May 30, 2026 and carried 201 likes at the time of the source capture. It opens with the customary "Badlanders!" salutation and restates the program's standing remit as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."

## Hosts

The episode is hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], both linked in the post to their Substack author profiles. The pairing is a recurring one in the series and continues the program's practice of rotating hosting duties among Badlands contributors rather than assigning a fixed presenter.

## Content

Three subjects are announced. The first is what the post describes as "the ongoing flirtation between the Trump Administration and the Real, but also Fake Iranian Regime" — a formulation that reflects the Badlands house view that the government of [[iran|Iran]] is simultaneously an authentic negotiating counterparty and a managed or manufactured adversary, and that dealings between it and the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] should be read on both levels at once.

The second subject is the claim that "select segments of the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] are starting to guess the game (too late,)" — the argument being that portions of the establishment press have belatedly begun to perceive the strategic logic that Badlands commentators say they have been describing in real time, but only after the relevant events have already moved past the point where such recognition would matter.

The third is a broader structural question: "what it takes to keep a Multipolar World stable over the long run." The announcement does not summarise the hosts' conclusions; consistent with the program's house style, the detail is left to the video itself.

## Publication and support model

The post repeats the standing editorial notice that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," and that direct reader support is the only means by which the team is able to pay itself for its time. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription in recognition of "the step-up in said work for 2026," with cancellation available at any time. It closes with the program's customary sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
