# Manufactured Race Riots (Thirty-Year Cycle)

The "thirty-year cycle" of manufactured race riots is Erik Carlson's claim that the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] playbook for keeping Americans divided by race requires man-made, manipulated race riots to occur roughly every thirty years.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## The alleged pattern

Carlson lists a sequence of incidents spaced at approximately thirty-year intervals:[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

- 1935 — the Harlem race riots in New York City.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
- 1965 — race riots in the Watts neighborhood of Los Angeles.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
- 1991–1992 — the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] beating and the resulting [[la-riots-1992|Los Angeles riots]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
- 2020 — the [[george-floyd|George Floyd]] incident and the ensuing BLM protests, violence, destruction and looting.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Rationale claimed

The underlying premise, in Carlson's framing, is an understanding — whether correct or not — that if America went more than thirty years without an incident creating racial division, racism could begin to disappear; a riot therefore appears on roughly that schedule to fan the flames.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) He anticipates the objection "where's your proof, you conspiracy nutjob?" and answers with the pattern itself, noting that because 2020 is recent and many people now question what they see, a substantial number believe the Floyd killing was staged or at least that something about it is not right.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
