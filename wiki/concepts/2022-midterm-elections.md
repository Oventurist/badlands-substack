# 2022 Midterm Elections

The **2022 United States midterm elections**, held in November 2022, were a focal point of Badlands Media commentary both before and after the vote. Many within the MAGA and America First community believed they had witnessed another highly public and, in their view, obvious voter fraud campaign that deprived the American people of some of their most favored candidates — candidates thought capable of stemming the political and administrative damage attributed to the occupation of the [[white-house|White House]] by the Biden administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## The Red Wave that did not arrive

The widely anticipated "Red Wave" failed to materialize at the scale expected, producing a somewhat demoralized and depressed mood across the base.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed) Badlands writers had, however, argued beforehand — unpopularly at the time — that an actualized Red Wave might have undone the exposure operation they believed was underway, by placing the movement in front of a runaway train and casting it as a miscast Judas that would shoulder the blame for systemic failure.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## Outcomes

The article records real wins amid what its author regarded as fraud: the projected reclaiming of the House and a potentially interesting deadlock in the [[united-states-senate|Senate]], where, in the author's characterization, a few key Democrat votes never seem to go quite the way the powers that would be intend.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed) These were nonetheless described as far short of the actualized wins many in the movement had hoped for.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## Aftermath and fracture

The post-election period saw what Badlands described as the activation of "assets" — an opportune moment for a subversive enemy to strike at a MAGA base perceived as fracturing.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed) The author's counter-argument was that the movement fractures only if it allows itself to, and that the emergence of hostile actors is itself useful, on the principle that sunlight is the best disinfectant and that exposure and disclosure must precede justice.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)
