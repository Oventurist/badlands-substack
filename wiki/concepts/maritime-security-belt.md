# Maritime Security Belt

"Maritime Security Belt" is the name of a recurring trilateral naval exercise series conducted by [[russia|Russia]], [[iran|Iran]] and [[china|China]], held annually since 2019.[1]

## February 2026 iteration

As tensions escalated over reported US preparations for military strikes on Iran, the three countries launched joint naval drills in the Gulf of Oman and near the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1] The exercises involved warships and aircraft from all three nations operating off Iran's southern coast.[1] The Badlands Brief, citing RT, characterized the drills as underscoring expanded military coordination among the three countries in a strategically sensitive maritime corridor.[1]

## Interpretation in the corpus

The Brief's commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] placed the drills within an argument that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was being pushed toward war with Iran by external forces — [[israel|Israel]], the military-industrial complex and elements of American politics — and that Russian involvement, personified by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], represented the principal counterweight capable of deterring escalation.[1]
