# Lifeline Program

The Lifeline program is a federal subsidy scheme administered through the [[federal-communications-commission|Federal Communications Commission]] that provides discounted phone service to low-income Americans.[1] It is popularly known as the "Obama phone" program, and the U.S. Department of Justice has described it as "a celebrated federal program providing discounted phone service to people in need."[1]

## Q Link Wireless fraud (2013–2019)

The program was the target of a large fraud prosecuted by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of Florida.[1] [[q-link-wireless|Q Link Wireless LLC]] and its CEO [[issa-asad|Issa Asad]] were sentenced in July 2025 for a years-long conspiracy to steal over $100 million from the program.[1]

According to Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]], citing the Federal Register, Q Link Wireless collected $618,736,494 from the Lifeline program between 2013 and 2019.[1] Of that, more than $109,000,000 was stolen through fraudulent billing, after which the company fabricated records to cover up the scheme once Asad realized he was under investigation.[1]

The resolution required a forfeiture money judgment of over $109 million and restitution of $109,637,057 to the FCC, part of total penalties and restitution exceeding $128 million.[1] Badlands framed the case as evidence of massive fraud embedded in a signature social program of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] era.[1]
