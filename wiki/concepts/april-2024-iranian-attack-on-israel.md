# April 2024 Iranian Attack on Israel

The April 2024 Iranian attack on Israel was a retaliatory drone and missile barrage launched by [[iran|Iran]] on Saturday, April 13, 2024, in response to a suspected Israeli strike on Iran's embassy compound in Damascus, Syria. [[iran|Iran]] launched hundreds of drones and missiles at [[israel|Israel]], most of which were downed before reaching Israeli territory; a young girl was critically injured and there were widespread concerns of further regional escalation.[1]

## Advance warning dispute

A central controversy surrounding the attack concerned whether [[iran|Iran]] provided advance notice. Turkish, Jordanian and Iraqi officials said on Sunday that [[iran|Iran]] had given wide notice days before the strike, and Iranian Foreign Minister [[hossein-amirabdollahian|Hossein Amirabdollahian]] stated that [[iran|Iran]] gave neighbouring countries and Israel's ally the United States 72 hours' notice that it would launch the strikes.[1]

[[turkey|Turkey]]'s Foreign Ministry said it had spoken to both Washington and Tehran before the attack, conveying messages as an intermediary to ensure that reactions remained proportionate. A Turkish diplomatic source said that [[iran|Iran]] had communicated that its reaction would be a response to Israel's attack on its embassy in Damascus and "would not go beyond this," adding that "the developments were not a surprise."[1]

United States officials disputed this account. One senior official in the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] denied Amirabdollahian's statement, saying Washington had contact with [[iran|Iran]] through Swiss intermediaries but did not receive notice 72 hours in advance, and asserting that Tehran was aiming to cause significant damage.[1]

## Legal framing

Commentators associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] emphasized that [[iran|Iran]] framed its response under [[article-51-of-the-un-charter|Article 51 of the UN Charter]], which permits member states to defend themselves with reciprocal action provided they notify the Security Council in advance and keep the response proportionate to the initial offense. [[iran|Iran]] publicly announced that its kinetic actions against [[israel|Israel]] were concluded, a posture described as notable given the historical rhetoric between the two states.[1]
