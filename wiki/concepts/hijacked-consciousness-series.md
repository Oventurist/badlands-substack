# Hijacked Consciousness (series)

"Hijacked Consciousness" is a serialized run of feature articles published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack by the contributor writing as [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) Badlands describes the series as concerning "the Hijacking of the Human Operating System, and what's being done about it," and each installment carries an editorial note instructing readers to start with Part 1.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Installments

The August 15, 2025 Badlands News Brief roundup identified two recent entries in the series. Part 4, "Hijacked Consciousness - The Silent War," was published on August 2, 2025.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) Part 5, "Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field," followed on August 9, 2025.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) The titles indicate a progression from framing the subject as covert conflict toward discussion of a "resonance field" model of consciousness.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Framing

By treating human cognition as an "operating system" subject to hijacking, the series aligns with Badlands' broader editorial focus on narrative control and non-kinetic conflict, a territory the outlet elsewhere discusses under headings such as [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)
