# Hijacked Consciousness (series)

"Hijacked Consciousness" is a serialized run of feature articles published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack by the contributor writing as [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) Badlands describes the series as concerning "the Hijacking of the Human Operating System, and what's being done about it," and each installment carries an editorial note instructing readers to start with Part 1.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Installments

The August 15, 2025 Badlands News Brief roundup identified two recent entries in the series. Part 4, "Hijacked Consciousness - The Silent War," was published on August 2, 2025.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) Part 5, "Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field," followed on August 9, 2025.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) The titles indicate a progression from framing the subject as covert conflict toward discussion of a "resonance field" model of consciousness.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Framing

By treating human cognition as an "operating system" subject to hijacking, the series aligns with Badlands' broader editorial focus on narrative control and non-kinetic conflict, a territory the outlet elsewhere discusses under headings such as [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Part 6: Field Builders

The sixth installment, "Hijacked Consciousness - Field Builders," was published on August 28, 2025 with the subtitle "How Education Was Weaponized to Sever the Soul, Fracture Memory, and Re-write Reality," and recorded 79 likes on the platform.[2] Its editorial note repeats the series' standing instruction to begin with Part 1, "Hijacked Consciousness - Sacred Origins," and it opens by recapping the preceding installment on the resonance field, in which the series argued that coherence returns through presence and embodiment rather than explanation.[2]

The article moves outward from the individual to the collective, arguing that once "the Signal" is restored it begins to reorganize the world around the person carrying it.[2] It proceeds through five sections: an anatomy of the [[resonant-human|resonant human]], the building of [[microfields|microfields]] as tuning chambers, the return of [[sacred-education|sacred education]], an "emergent architecture" of signal clusters, and a closing passage on the [[grid-carriers|grid carriers]] who hold the field.[2] A quotation from [[bruce-lipton|Bruce Lipton]] on perception rewriting the chemistry of the body is used as a hinge between the first two sections.[2]

The piece explicitly declines to be a conclusion, describing itself instead as "the stabilization of the signal" and promising a final installment on "the frequency holders… the resonance weavers… the ones who remember."[2]

## Part 1: "Sacred Origins"

The opening installment, "Hijacked Consciousness - Sacred Origins," was published on July 8, 2025 and drew 170 likes; it was introduced as "the first in a new series on the Hijacking of the Human Operating System, and what's being done about it."[3] Its subtitle — "How Education Was Weaponized to Sever the Soul, Fracture Memory, and Re-write Reality" — became the standing subtitle for later entries.[3] The author framed the series as being "not just about education" but about "memory, control, and the war for the human soul," with each part walking through a different layer of the hijack, "from sacred beginnings, to psychological inversion, to the return of coherence," and told readers they would not be told what to believe but might "start to remember what you've always known."[3]

Part 1 is organised in three movements. "Remembering the Spiral" surveys [[ancient-sacred-learning-traditions|ancient sacred learning traditions]] from Sumer, Egypt, Vedic India, Mesoamerica, Greece under [[pythagoras|Pythagoras]], the Celtic bards and the early European monasteries.[3] "Fracturing the Child" describes the [[industrial-model-of-schooling|industrial model of schooling]], the "Ivy obedience grid" of [[harvard-university|Harvard]] and [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]], and [[pharmaceutical-compliance-in-schools|pharmaceutical compliance in schools]].[3] "The Silence Beneath the Spell" argues that the operation was never about information but "control of consciousness," on the principle that "to truly hijack a species, you don't imprison the body. You colonize the mind."[3]

The installment closes with the questions that set up Part 2: what kind of world must be constructed to keep a soul from remembering its original frequency, and who would need to engineer such a world.[3] It explicitly links forward to Part 2, "The Hijacking."[3]
