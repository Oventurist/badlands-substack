# Treaty of Versailles

The **Treaty of Versailles** of 1919 concluded the First World War and gave rise to the [[league-of-nations|League of Nations]], whose opening conference met on January 10, 1920.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that the world was told the cause of the war had been German imperial ambition and the nation state system itself, and that discussion of the truth was not deemed appropriate amid what he calls a frenzy of looting.[1] Everything Germany possessed — vital agriculture, mines, rail, industry and colonies — went up for grabs, while debts were imposed on the beaten state and North Silesia, the Ruhr and Alsace-Lorraine were confiscated along with the means of paying reparations.[1]

The article details that after the 1918 Armistice dismantled Germany's army and navy, the country was forced to pay 132 billion gold marks and to surrender territories holding 10% of its population, 15% of its arable land, 12% of its livestock, 74% of its iron ore, 63% of its zinc production and 26% of its coal, as well as 8,000 locomotives, 225,000 railcars and all of its colonies — described as "a field day of modern pillage."[1]

Kaiser [[wilhelm-ii|Wilhelm II]]'s Germany thus emerged from a war that the article attributes to the encirclement strategy of the [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]] and [[triple-entente|Triple Entente]].[1]
