# Trump Transition Afghanistan Withdrawal Courts-Martial Review

In November 2024, reporting indicated that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] transition team was compiling a list of senior current and former U.S. military officers directly involved in the 2021 American withdrawal from Afghanistan and exploring whether they could be court-martialed for their roles.[1]

## Scope

Officials working on the transition were considering creating a commission to investigate the 2021 withdrawal, including gathering information on who was directly involved in the military's decision-making, how the operation was carried out, and whether military leaders could face charges as serious as treason, according to a U.S. official and a person familiar with the plan.[1] The person with knowledge of the plan said the effort was being taken "very seriously."[1] The transition team did not immediately respond to a request for comment.[1]

Matt Flynn, a former deputy assistant secretary of defense for counternarcotics and global threats, was reported to be helping lead the effort, which was framed as a broader review of how the United States entered the Afghan war and how it eventually left.[1] The item was carried in Badlands Media's brief as a bonus entry without accompanying editorial commentary.[1]
