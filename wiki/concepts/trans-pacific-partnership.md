# Trans-Pacific Partnership

The Trans-Pacific Partnership (TPP) was a multinational trade agreement whose fast-track negotiating authority became a defining fault line in American populist politics during 2015.[1] In Badlands commentary it functions as a litmus test separating genuine economic nationalists from what writers describe as the [[uniparty|Uniparty]].[1]

## The H.R.2146 maneuver

According to an account published by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], in April 2015 [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] and allies on the Senate Finance Committee quietly introduced S.995, the "Bipartisan Congressional Trade Priorities and Accountability Act of 2015."[1] That Senate bill never received a floor vote; it was read twice and then folded into H.R.2146, a measure originally titled the "Defending Public Safety Employees' Retirement Act" and written to protect the pensions of first responders from the Internal Revenue Service.[1]

S.995 overhauled the procedure for concluding trade agreements with foreign nations, and the language McConnell inserted into H.R.2146 before the June 17, 2015 vote was framed as being "in support of the Trans-Pacific Partnership."[1]

## DeSantis and the vote

[[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], then a member of Congress, voted "YES" on H.R.2146 on June 17, 2015 — the day after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced his presidential candidacy.[1] GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that in doing so DeSantis "conspired with the Uniparty in Washington to destroy the remaining manufacturing base in America and ship it off to China," and concluded that this record, more than any later betrayal of Trump personally, permanently disqualifies him from the presidency.[1]
