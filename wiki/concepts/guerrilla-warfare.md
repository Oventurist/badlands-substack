# Guerrilla Warfare

Guerrilla warfare — irregular, mobile, ambush-based combat conducted by small partisan bands behind enemy lines — is the central subject of the Patriot Chronicles installment on [[francis-marion|Francis Marion]], who is presented as the father of the modern form.[1]

## Marion as progenitor

Marion is described as a classic guerrilla warrior, constantly on the move to keep his hideouts mythic and the strength of his numbers a mystery, operating from swamp refuges such as [[snows-island|Snow's Island]].[1] For frustrating the movement of British supplies and communications across an entire occupied colony, he is recognized in the article not only as the father of modern guerrilla warfare but also as the progenitor of U.S. Army Special Forces, specifically the 75th Ranger Regiment and the Green Berets.[1]

## Elite hostility to the method

General [[horatio-gates|Horatio Gates]], English-born and academy-trained in open-field set-piece warfare, regarded guerrilla warfare as both an inferior form of combat and evidence of inferior intelligence — "the blunt blade of undisciplined savages."[1] Marion's recruits, by contrast, were largely Scotch-Irish veterans of the French and Indian War who had fought the Cherokee on the frontier and suffered the art of irregular warfare firsthand, together with half-breed Catawba Indians, freemen, highborn Scotsmen, lowborn Englishmen, Irishmen, Huguenots and Poles.[1]

Marion trained them to ride and shoot from horseback and to fight with the saber, since most carried only a few rounds of ammunition and the lightweight Kentucky long rifle could not take a bayonet.[1] Sabers being scarce, he foraged discarded mill-saws and recruited blacksmiths to reforge them in the forest, while his men foraged constantly for clothing, many of them barefoot.[1]

## Modern application

The article urges readers to "recalibrate your minds for the 5GW battlefield" and become partisan guerrillas in the swamps of cyberspace — harassing narrative deployments, sabotaging messaging, flooding the airwaves with counter-narratives and coordinating an effective [[fifth-generation-warfare|5GW]] counterinsurgency, with ridicule and humor as weapons.[1]
