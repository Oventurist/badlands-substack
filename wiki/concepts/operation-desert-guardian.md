# Operation Desert Guardian

Operation Desert Guardian is a joint state task force created by Arizona Governor [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] by executive order in February 2025 to combat transnational criminal organizations operating across the state's border with Mexico.[1]

## Structure and mandate

The executive order, announced in a news release on Tuesday, February 25, 2025, requires the task force to be established by the Arizona Department of Public Safety, the Arizona Department of Homeland Security, and the Department of Emergency and Military Affairs, focused on "the identification, deterrence, interdiction, and dismantling of" transnational criminal organizations.[1] The Arizona Department of Homeland Security is separately required to convene the task force and ensure a coordinated, collaborative strategy with state agencies as well as county sheriffs, local law enforcement agencies, and federal law enforcement agencies that join it.[1]

Hobbs said she was "proud to launch Operation Desert Guardian to combat the cartels, stop drug smuggling and human trafficking, and secure Arizona's border," and stated that her administration had been in contact with the federal government and local sheriffs about the operation, its objectives, and a shared commitment to keeping criminals and drugs out of Arizona communities.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Chris Paul dismissed the initiative in a single line — "Fake governors don't have choices" — casting the order as a forced accommodation to the political pressure of the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s border agenda rather than a genuine policy shift.[1]
