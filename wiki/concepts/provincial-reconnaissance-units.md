# Provincial Reconnaissance Units

The Provincial Reconnaissance Units (PRUs) were CIA-sponsored units that worked for the [[phoenix-program]] during the Vietnam War.[1] In 1971 [[felix-rodriguez|Felix Rodriguez]] trained PRUs, which Joe Lange describes plainly as the death squads of the Phoenix Program.[1]

Rodriguez was supervised in this period by [[donald-gregg|Donald Gregg]], as recorded in Chapter 29 of the Walsh Report, while [[ted-shackley|Ted Shackley]] oversaw the Phoenix Program as a whole.[1] Lange presents this chain of command — Shackley over Gregg over Rodriguez, with all three later tied to Vice President [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] — as one of the structural continuities linking Vietnam-era covert operations to the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] network.[1]
