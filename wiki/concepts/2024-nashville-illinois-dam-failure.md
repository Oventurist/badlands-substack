# 2024 Nashville, Illinois Dam Failure

On the morning of Tuesday, July 16, 2024, a dam and then a secondary dam failed in Nashville, Illinois, prompting an evacuation warning for city residents.[1] The secondary failure was reported around 10 a.m., more than an hour after the first dam gave way, according to the Washington County Emergency Management Agency.[1]

## Cause and impact

The failures followed more than six inches of rain in the area. The Washington County EMA stated that the Nashville Reservoir was overtopped after extensive rainfall beginning the night of July 15 and continuing through July 16.[1] Some residents experienced waist-deep flooding, and one woman had to be rescued from her home.[1] Flooding closed many roads and restricted travel on all major roads throughout Washington County.[1] An estimated 200 homes were considered endangered after the reservoir was overtopped; the evacuation was subsequently completed, and no deaths or significant injuries were reported.[1]
