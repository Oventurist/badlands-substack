# Biden Water Heater Efficiency Rule

The Biden water heater efficiency rule is a Department of Energy regulation issued in the final days of the Biden administration that effectively bans certain natural gas water heaters from the US market as part of its climate change agenda.[1]

## Provisions and criticism

The rule takes non-condensing, natural gas-fired water heaters off the shelves by 2029 in a bid to reduce carbon dioxide emissions, which climate advocates and [[joe-biden|President Biden]] say cause global warming.[1] New tankless gas water heaters will be required to use about 13 percent less energy than today's least efficient tankless models.[1] Although the rules nominally apply to both non-condensing and condensing gas water heaters, they raise efficiency requirements to a threshold only condensing models can meet, effectively banning the cheaper but less efficient non-condensing units, according to reporting by the Washington Free Beacon.[1]

Critics quoted in the coverage argued the move will jack up energy costs for low-income and senior households.[1] The Brief paired the item with Biden's move to permanently bar new offshore oil and gas development, presenting both as late-term regulatory actions designed to constrain the incoming administration's energy agenda.[1]
