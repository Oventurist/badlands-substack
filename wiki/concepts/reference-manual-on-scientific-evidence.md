# Reference Manual on Scientific Evidence

The **Reference Manual on Scientific Evidence** is a resource used by federal judges to evaluate scientific testimony.[1] In 2026 it became the subject of a presidential review over the climate-related guidance it had contained.[1]

## 2026 Trump review

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ordered a federal review into the development of the climate-related guidance included in the manual, arguing the material contained politically biased and "discredited" climate science and calling for a review of those involved in its preparation.[1] The climate chapter had been removed earlier in the year after objections from a coalition of Republican state attorneys general.[1] The review focuses on how the guidance was developed and any potential federal contracting or funding issues, rather than on judicial decisions themselves.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the episode in terms of the broader [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]], writing that "the climate cult" had been responsible for much of the social change of the previous two decades, and that stories about "the carbon monster" led to a portfolio of unfortunate questions and answers.[1] In her account, climate change was always about centralizing decision rights globally in the hands of a few, and the "sustainable development goals" — originally pitched as a climate response when "the science was settled" — evolved quickly into a framework defining acceptable binaries in every domain of human existence.[1] She endorsed investigating, prosecuting and holding accountable those behind climate "science," calling it especially necessary on the cusp of a national Golden Age.[1]
