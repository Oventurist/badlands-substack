# Intervision

Intervision is a song contest organized by Moscow and portrayed as an alternative to Eurovision, from which Russia has been excluded amid tensions with the European Union over the Ukraine conflict.[1]

## 2025 edition

Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] highlighted the contest at the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]] summit in Tianjin, noting that it would be held in Moscow on September 20, 2025, and that it was expected to feature performers from Latin America, Africa, and Asia.[1] Putin described the "large-scale project" as aimed at promoting universal values, arguing that "traditional values are already fading into the background" and that "it is time to bring them back to the international agenda."[1]

At the same summit Putin invited delegations to the St. Petersburg International United Cultures Forum and the "Russia – Country of Sports" forum in Samara in November.[1] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry contrasted Putin's promotion of national sovereignty and traditional values with what he characterized as the decadence of European elites.[1]
