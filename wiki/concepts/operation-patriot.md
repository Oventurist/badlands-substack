# Operation Patriot

**Operation Patriot** was an immigration enforcement surge conducted by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] (ICE) throughout May 2025 in Massachusetts, described in reporting as the largest ICE operation ever conducted.[1]

## Scope and results

The operation concluded on Saturday, May 31, 2025, after netting 1,461 illegal aliens across the Greater Boston area and the state of Massachusetts, a jurisdiction containing numerous sanctuary cities.[1] Sources at ICE told Fox News that 790 of those arrested had criminal convictions or pending charges, and 277 had final removal or deportation orders.[1] Those sources stated that all of the targeted criminals — including murderers, rapists, drug traffickers and child sex predators — had been at liberty in Massachusetts cities before being apprehended.[1]

The operation drew on ICE teams from other states in the Northeast and was supported by the FBI, DEA and ATF.[1]
