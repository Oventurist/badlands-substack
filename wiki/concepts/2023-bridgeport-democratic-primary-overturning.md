# 2023 Bridgeport Democratic Primary Overturning

The 2023 Bridgeport Democratic primary overturning was a Connecticut court decision voiding the Democratic mayoral primary in Bridgeport after video evidence of absentee-ballot fraud was presented to the court.[1]

## The ruling

On Wednesday, November 1, 2023, Judge [[william-clark-connecticut|William Clark]] issued a 37-page ruling ordering a new Democratic primary.[1] The decision rested on 180 pieces of evidence assembled by counsel for mayoral candidate [[john-gomes|John Gomes]], including footage Gomes posted to Facebook on September 16 that appeared to show Bridgeport Democratic Town Committee vice chair [[wanda-geter-pataky|Wanda Geter-Pataky]] inserting stacks of ballots into an absentee drop box.[1] Geter-Pataky invoked the Fifth Amendment rather than testify about the video.[1] Clark wrote that "the videos are shocking to the court and should be shocking to all the parties," and noted that incumbent Mayor [[joe-ganim|Joe Ganim]] was "correct to be 'shocked'" when shown the clips on the stand.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, argued that election fraud "only works when the people don't know about it," and that public vigilance had forced fraudsters to improvise and thereby get caught.[1] She observed that overturned cases have largely involved Democratic primaries, citing the 2022 DeKalb County, Georgia contest in which candidate Michele Longspears placed last until a hand recount showed she had actually finished first, reversing the result.[1] Ashe urged the America First movement to treat restoration of public trust through free and fair elections — rather than backing particular candidates — as the primary objective heading into 2024.[1] The case is frequently cited in discussion of [[ballot-harvesting|ballot harvesting]].[1]
