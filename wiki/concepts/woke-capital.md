# Woke Capital

"Woke capital" describes the mechanism by which, in Badlands Media commentary, American corporations are compelled to adopt progressive social narratives and policies through financial rather than consumer pressure.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The original argument

In an earlier article titled "Get Woke, Go Broke," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that American corporations are placed in a bind: either they upset and lose customers through what he calls ridiculous "woke" narratives and policies, or they have their stock pulled from large investment portfolios for failing to enact them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) Carlson maintains this analysis, while stressing that it does not make the companies innocent of anything — only that they are forced to be woke whether they want to be or not.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

[[andy-frisella|Andy Frisella]] is described as stating essentially the same thing, adding that non-compliant companies "even lose their funding and financing."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The revision

What Carlson had not considered, and adopts from Frisella, is that the resulting consumer backlash may be the point. On this reading the public is being coerced into boycotting and destroying [[americana-brands|Americana brands]] — a step in the [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist plot]] to remove American culture and eliminate the middle class.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) Carlson had previously argued boycotts work, citing [[budweiser|Budweiser]] and [[target|Target]] as obvious examples of companies losing money, but now asks whether that outcome is what the [[deep-state|Deep State]] Cabal wanted all along.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)
