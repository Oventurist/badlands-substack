# Grok 4

Grok 4 is the artificial intelligence chatbot released by [[xai|xAI]] and demonstrated by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in July 2025.[1]

## Launch claims

During the demonstration Musk argued that Grok 4 was the smartest AI in the world, better than almost all graduate students across all disciplines simultaneously.[1] At the same event he said the notion that humans once managed an economy would seem quaint in the future, comparable in retrospect to "cavemen throwing sticks into a fire."[1]

The remarks were framed by Fortune as the latest instance of a Silicon Valley figure declining to affirm the value of human continuity: two weeks earlier [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] had struggled to answer whether he would prefer the human race to endure, and it was now Musk's turn to opine on whether technology might remove the need for mankind's existence.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] responded dryly that [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] probably won't destroy humanity, "but if it does, at least we'll get to witness it."[1]
