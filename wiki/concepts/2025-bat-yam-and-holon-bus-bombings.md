# 2025 Bat Yam and Holon Bus Bombings

The Bat Yam and Holon bus bombings were a series of explosions on the night of Thursday, February 20, 2025, in parking lots in the Tel Aviv suburbs of Bat Yam and Holon, which Israeli police described as a suspected strategically planned terror attack.[1]

## The attack

Three empty buses exploded in quick succession, causing no reported injuries.[1] Police said they neutralized two additional unexploded devices on nearby buses, which Hebrew media reported were discovered in Holon.[1] Each device was said to contain five kilograms of explosives.[1] Police combed the scenes searching for suspects while bomb sappers hunted for further suspicious items in the vicinity.[1]

## Israeli government response

Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s office said the premier was receiving constant updates and held a security assessment late that night, after which it announced that he had instructed the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] to embark on a massive operation in the West Bank against terrorist hubs.[1]
