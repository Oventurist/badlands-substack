# Unite the Right Rally

The "Unite the Right" rally was a 2017 gathering in Charlottesville, Virginia that became a recurring reference point in litigation and commentary about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and political violence.[1]

## Role in the Colorado disqualification trial

During the October 2023 Colorado trial seeking to bar Trump from the 2024 ballot under Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]], petitioners' expert witness [[pete-simi|Pete Simi]] presented the rally as a predecessor to January 6, 2021 and as an illustration of how Trump endorses the behavior of "his followers" — a pattern he argued should be extrapolated to the events at the Capitol.[1]

The dispute in court centered on Trump's remarks about the rally. Petitioners played the "very fine people" clip without the surrounding condemnation of neo-Nazis and white nationalists, while Trump's attorney played the full clip in response.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the selective editing as emblematic of the petitioners' case, which she described as a laughable but dangerous attack on activity protected by the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1]
