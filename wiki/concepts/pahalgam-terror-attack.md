# Pahalgam Terror Attack

The **Pahalgam terror attack** was an assault in Indian-administered Kashmir in April 2025 that prompted the central government of [[india|India]] to order short-term visa holders from [[pakistan|Pakistan]] to leave the country.[1]

## Expulsion of Pakistani short-term visa holders

In the wake of the attack, the Indian central government issued a directive requiring Pakistani nationals on short-term visas to depart.[1] In [[maharashtra|Maharashtra]], chief minister [[devendra-fadnavis|Devendra Fadnavis]] said on Monday, 28 April 2025 that there were no Pakistanis unaccounted for on the list of those ordered to leave, a statement issued a day after deputy chief minister [[eknath-shinde|Eknath Shinde]] said 107 were unaccounted for.[1]

Fadnavis's home department sought to clear up the confusion, reporting that all but 14 of the 55 Pakistanis in the state on short-term visas had exited in accordance with the central directive, and that the state government had tracked down the remaining 14, some of whom were present on medical visas.[1] "The citizens living on short term visas were asked to leave the state by Sunday. The ones on medical visas will have to exit by Tuesday," an official from the home department stated.[1]

## Broader visa data

Departmental data clarified that 5,023 Pakistanis were living in various parts of Maharashtra excluding Mumbai, with 2,740 on long-term visas and 2,088 on extensions to long-term visas.[1] Another 34 had overstayed long-term visas and were living in the state illegally, with three of them in prison at the time.[1]
