# 2025 Trump Apple Tariff Threat

In May 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] threatened tariffs on [[apple|Apple]] unless the iPhone maker moved production fully to the United States, prompting California's attorney general to raise the prospect of litigation.[1]

California Attorney General [[rob-bonta|Rob Bonta]] said on Friday, May 23, that he was prepared to sue if appropriate to defend state companies such as Apple: "We definitely will assess our potential role... We will be monitoring what he does, if he does anything, and we'll make sure that if the law is broken and we have standing to sue, we'll take appropriate action."[1] Bonta was speaking at a San Francisco press conference following a roundtable with local business leaders about harms from tariffs, and was reacting to a Truth Social post Trump had shared that morning.[1] Bonta has emerged as a leader of the blue state's resistance to the administration, launching or joining numerous lawsuits against White House policies, including the first state challenge to the president's trade measures.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that by threatening Apple with the same levies used against nation states, Trump was not making a mistake but signaling that globalist corporations "have been acting as de facto nation states all along."[1]
