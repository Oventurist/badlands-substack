# Wonsan Kalma

Wonsan Kalma is a beach resort on the east coast of [[north-korea|North Korea]], opened as the centerpiece of leader [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]]'s bet on tourism as a source of revenue for the sanctioned state.[1]

## Opening and description

The resort was scheduled to open to domestic tourists on July 1, 2025, six years after its original completion date; it was unclear when it would admit foreigners.[1] State media agency [[korean-central-news-agency|KCNA]] claimed the resort could accommodate up to 20,000 visitors across a four-kilometre (2.5 mile) stretch of beach, with hotels, restaurants, shopping malls, and a water park — none of which could be independently verified.[1]

## Context

Kim grew up in luxury in Wonsan, where many of the country's elite maintain private villas, and has sought to transform the town, which once hosted a missile testing site.[1] North Korea, heavily sanctioned for decades over its nuclear weapons programme, is among the world's poorest countries and directs most of its resources toward its military and toward monuments and landmarks — often in Pyongyang — that embellish the cult of the Kim family, which has ruled since 1948.[1]

Badlands commentary used the resort's opening to argue that mainstream media coverage had misrepresented Kim Jong Un.[1]
