# 2024 SCO Summit in Islamabad

The 2024 heads of government meeting of the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] was held in Islamabad, Pakistan, on October 17, 2024, attended by India, Pakistan, China, Russia and six other member countries.[1] Indian External Affairs Minister [[s-jaishankar|S. Jaishankar]] travelled to Islamabad for the meeting, the first such Indian visit in nine years.[1] The SCO's member countries are India, China, Pakistan, Russia, Iran, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan and Belarus.[1]

## Outcomes

The meeting produced cautious movement on India–Pakistan ties, while India remained the only SCO member not to endorse China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]].[1] Jaishankar also flagged Indian concerns about China and Pakistan in veiled references.[1] He and his Pakistani counterpart, Deputy Prime Minister [[muhammad-ishaq-dar|Muhammad Ishaq Dar]], spoke twice in under 24 hours, with indications that the talks explored resuming some form of cricketing ties between the two countries.[1] Sources described the conversations as preliminary, with a possible first step being the Champions Trophy hosted by Pakistan the following February.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] had flagged the Islamabad summit in advance, noting that India and Pakistan were not scheduled to hold bilateral discussions on the sidelines, and watching for any pivot toward dialogue.[1] He described the India–Pakistan border dispute as potentially one of the oldest running conflicts in human history, divided by culture as much as religion and likely predating Islam, though exacerbated by it.[1] He argued that [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] is a man of peace and that Russia's public backing of Pakistan's bid to join [[brics|BRICS]] — of which India is a founding member — made reconciliation strategically necessary if the two were to join forces against the globalist cabal.[1]

On the cricket question, he argued that sports create bonds outside economics and politics, comparing it to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s insistence on getting North Korea to participate in the 2018 Winter Olympics hosted by South Korea, and concluding that a face-to-face meeting producing a mutually beneficial decision requiring future cooperation is itself diplomacy.[1] He added that he had previously speculated on Devolution Power Hour that the relevant rapprochement was with China rather than Pakistan, given the ancient India–China feud that [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and others had suggested could turn kinetic along the border.[1] The next event on his watchlist was the BRICS summit opening in Kazan, Russia.[1]
