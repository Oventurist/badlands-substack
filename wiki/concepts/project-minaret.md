# Project MINARET

Project MINARET was a [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] surveillance program uncovered by the 1975 [[church-committee|Church Committee]], which identified it among the programs that had never before been known to the American public.[1]

## Description

MINARET, alongside [[project-shamrock|Project SHAMROCK]], monitored wire communications to and from the United States and shared portions of the resulting data with other intelligence agencies.[1] The disclosure of the two programs sat at the center of the committee's finding that the intelligence community had undermined the constitutional rights of citizens, and stood in sharp contrast to the assertion by the NSA's general counsel, relayed to Chief Counsel [[frederick-schwarz|Frederick Schwarz]], that "the constitution does not apply to the National Security Agency."[1]
