# 60/40 Rule

The **60/40 rule** is a heuristic articulated by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describing what he regards as the tipping point at which a suppressed truth becomes irreversible in public consciousness.[1] In Carlson's formulation, once public acceptance of a hidden truth reaches 60 percent of the population, "it's game over" — the figure quickly climbs toward 95 percent, with a residual 5 percent he describes as permanently broken and unwilling to accept reality.[1]

## Application to healthcare

Carlson applied the rule to the collapse of American confidence in medicine following the [[covid-19-narrative|Covid narrative]].[1] Citing a 50-state survey of U.S. adults led by [[northeastern-university|Northeastern University]] professor David Lazer, he noted that trust in physicians and hospitals fell from 71.5 percent in April 2020 to 40.1 percent in January 2024, leaving 59.9 percent of Americans distrustful — a strong majority and, in his view, past the threshold.[1] He concluded that the healthcare system would therefore implode, particularly with [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy, Jr.]] "ripping it apart at its seams," and predicted that by the end of 2025 few Americans would support the industry in its existing form.[1]

## Application to elections

Carlson used the same threshold to explain the behaviour of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] in the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 presidential election]].[1] He estimated that roughly 60 percent of Americans are now "awake enough" to the lies of the media and government — mirroring the 59.9 percent who distrust healthcare — against roughly 30 percent when the Covid narrative was first deployed, which he matched to the 28.5 percent who distrusted doctors and hospitals at that time.[1] He also estimated that about 60 percent of voters actually supported [[donald-trump|Trump]] in 2024.[1]

On this reasoning, an outright steal of the 2024 election would have been catastrophic for the fraudulent election system itself, because at a 60/40 split the apparatus "loses all control."[1] Carlson argued that the reported outcome — Trump near 50 percent and [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] at 48–49 percent — was engineered to look like an evenly divided country rather than the roughly 90-million-to-60-million result he assumed had actually occurred out of some 150 million votes cast.[1]
