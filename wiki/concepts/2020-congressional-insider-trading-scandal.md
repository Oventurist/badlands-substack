# 2020 Congressional Insider Trading Scandal

The 2020 congressional insider trading scandal is cited by Badlands Media as a concrete illustration of the [[two-tier-justice-system|two-tiered justice system]] exposed by the pandemic.[1] According to [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], before and after the declaration of a pandemic in January 2020, insider trading schemes allowed members of Congress and their allies in the business world to gain unfair advantages from the [[covid-19-lockdowns|lockdowns]].[1]

He characterized the episode as a shady partnership between corrupt elected officials in Washington, D.C., globalist business moguls, and co-conspirators in the medical industrial complex and the media, through which those in the know amassed considerable wealth and power within less than a year at the public's expense.[1]

## Context in the article

The scandal is presented as the fulfillment of [[first-inaugural-address-of-donald-trump|Trump's 2017 inaugural warning]] that "the establishment protected itself, but not the citizens of our country," a line Deschamps repeated as proof that [[donald-trump|Trump]] knew what was coming.[1] It is grouped with related pandemic-era asymmetries: politicians and experts who refused to follow their own restrictions, mothers barred from sick children, families losing income, and children kept from school, while fifteen companies — Netflix, Zoom and Facebook among them — added over a trillion dollars in market capitalization by March 2021.[1]
