# Thrombosis with Thrombocytopenia Syndrome

Thrombosis with thrombocytopenia syndrome, abbreviated TTS, is a medical condition in which a person suffers blood clots along with a low platelet count.[1] Platelets typically help the blood to clot, making the combination of clotting and platelet depletion clinically unusual.[1]

TTS became the central medical question in litigation over COVID-19 vaccination after [[astrazeneca|AstraZeneca]] admitted in a February 2024 filing with the High Court that its COVID-19 vaccine "can, in very rare cases, cause TTS" — the first such admission by the company.[1] The reaction is described as exceedingly rare, but it sits at the heart of a multi-million-pound class action brought by dozens of families who allege that they or their relatives were maimed or killed by the vaccine.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the admission as strategically limited, arguing that conceding a small, well-defined injury such as TTS can serve to deflect attention from broader excess-mortality trends in countries that mandated the injections.[1]
