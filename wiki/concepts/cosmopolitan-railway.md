# Cosmopolitan Railway

The *Cosmopolitan Railway* was the 1890 work of Governor [[william-gilpin|William Gilpin]], [[abraham-lincoln|Lincoln]]'s former bodyguard and a global promoter of rail development.[1] In it Gilpin outlined the extension of rail lines through [[british-columbia|British Columbia]], Alaska, the Bering Strait and across Eurasia — a physical realization of the plan to join the American and Russian continental systems.[1]

The proposal followed directly from the 1863 Russian and American legislation to build telegraph lines connecting both continents through the Bering Strait, and from Lincoln's December 1864 message to Congress describing the "world-encircling communication" that the overland telegraph by way of Bering's Straits and Asiatic Russia would create.[1] Matthew Ehret presents Gilpin's design as the nineteenth-century ancestor of today's [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait tunnel]] rail corridor proposal.[1]
