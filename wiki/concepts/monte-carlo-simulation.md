# Monte Carlo simulation

Monte Carlo simulation is the computational technique at the heart of the predictive-supercomputing thesis advanced by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]].[1] In his description, a Monte Carlo simulator runs "massive amounts of scenarios" in order to find the most likely outcome, which he glosses as software that "can essentially predict future outcomes."[1]

## Use in finance

Carlson identifies the method as the engine of [[blackrock|BlackRock]]'s [[aladdin|Aladdin]] system, built in the 1990s on a supercomputer in [[wenatchee-washington|Wenatchee, Washington]] and used first to select stocks and other investments.[1] After BlackRock began licensing the software to outside customers in 1999, insurance companies applied it to estimate the risk of a person dying prematurely — informing underwriting decisions and premium pricing — while rival investment firms used it to allocate capital.[1]

The consequence, in Carlson's account, was that the financial system itself became progressively more predictable, and investment banks, believing they now understood their risks, accepted greater risk in exchange for greater potential reward.[1]

## Limits and the overconfidence problem

Carlson treats the [[2008-financial-crisis|2008 collapse]] as the demonstration of the method's limits: firms holding risky portfolios were caught out when markets turned.[1] He likens the failure to modern weather forecasting, whose accuracy leads people to leave home without a jacket or umbrella when a rare storm arrives — an over-reliance on simulated prediction rather than a defect in the arithmetic itself, aggravated by [[too-big-to-fail|"too big to fail"]] bailout expectations.[1]

## Broader application

Beyond finance, Carlson argues that both the [[black-hats|black hats]] and the [[white-hats|white hats]] feed scenarios into such simulators to forecast public reactions to political events, narratives and disclosures, and that the pace of both the alleged Deep State agenda and the countervailing awakening has been set by these simulations.[1]
