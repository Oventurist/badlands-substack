# Trump $2,000 Tariff Dividend Proposal

The $2,000 tariff dividend proposal was an idea floated by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in November 2025 for a rebate payment funded by tariff revenue and directed to low- and medium-income Americans.[1]

## The proposal

Trump announced the concept on Truth Social, writing: "People that are against Tariffs are FOOLS! A dividend of at least $2000 a person (not including high income people!) will be paid to everyone."[1] The proposal came amid sustained backlash to Trump's broad tariff program, with critics arguing that the duties raise prices for ordinary Americans while encouraging a trade war.[1] Reporting noted that a rebate of this kind could help Americans absorb higher prices on groceries and everyday necessities, but that congressional approval would likely be required before any money reached recipients.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] used the announcement as a springboard into a discussion of the precise phrase "USA Citizen," arguing that the capitalization and punctuation of such terms carry legal significance under the [[de-jure-republic-theory|de jure republic theory]] — namely that "the United States of America" denotes the organic federation of sovereign states under the 1787 Constitution, whereas the post-1871 "United States" denotes a federal municipal corporation.[1] He suggested that a president using "USA Citizens" could be read as acknowledging allegiance to the organic republic rather than the corporation, and remarked that those who dismiss the framing as a conspiracy theory "have mostly sifted themselves out and exposed themselves in the process."[1]
