# Executive Order 14201

**Executive Order 14201**, titled "Keeping Men Out of Women's Sports," is an executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] directing federal policy toward barring biologically male athletes from competing in women's sporting categories.[1]

## USOPC compliance

In July 2025 the [[us-olympic-and-paralympic-committee|U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Committee]] updated its policies in a manner suggesting it would comply with the order.[1] The USOPC's transgender eligibility policy page was amended to include a note reading, "As of July 21, 2025, please refer to the USOPC athlete safety policy."[1] That athlete safety policy does not lay out clear guidelines regarding transgender inclusion in women's sports, but it contains one paragraph directly citing the executive order: "USOPC will continue to collaborate with various stakeholders with oversight responsibilities… to ensure that women have a fair and safe competition environment consistent with Executive Order 14201."[1]

The shift was reported by [[fox-news|Fox News]] in the context of USA Fencing changing its transgender policy and citing new guidance from the U.S. Olympic Committee, and was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 23, 2025.[1]
