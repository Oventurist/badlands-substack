# Feeding Our Future

Feeding Our Future is the name of a Minnesota nonprofit at the center of a pandemic-era child nutrition fraud scheme, described in the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026 as a "prominent $250 million pandemic-era child nutrition scheme."

The Brief presents the case as the best-known component of a much larger pattern of alleged fraud against federally funded welfare programs in Minnesota, in which prosecutors and investigators estimate that up to half of $18–20 billion disbursed over recent years was lost to fraud across Medicaid, food stamps, housing, childcare and nutrition programs. The scheme is treated in the corpus as an anchor example in the wider [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali fraud]] controversy, which by January 2026 had drawn congressional investigations led by [[james-comer|James Comer]], [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] and [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]], as well as the White House-backed [[scam-act|SCAM Act]] denaturalization proposal.
