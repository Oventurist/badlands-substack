# PROMIS software

PROMIS is a case-management and database program that, in the account advanced by Badlands writers, was covertly modified with a hidden backdoor and distributed internationally as an intelligence-collection instrument.[1]

## Distribution through Robert Maxwell

Citing research by the author Gordon Thomas, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] — father of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] — was dispatched by [[mossad|Mossad]] to sell PROMIS to entities across the world, including the Swiss bank [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].[1]

## Claimed intelligence value

Because the distributed copies were equipped with a secret backdoor, PROMIS is said to have allowed Mossad to monitor the activities of all governments running it.[1] Beyond governmental surveillance, the same access allegedly permitted tracking of money-laundering practices by organized-crime syndicates moving funds through the banking system, as well as arms deals conducted through weapons manufacturers.[1]

It was through this surveillance, the account continues, that Mossad learned the [[cia|CIA]] had been using Credit Suisse to launder revenue from off-the-book endeavors — knowledge that Robert Maxwell is said to have exploited to become the most prolific money launderer of the late 1980s.[1]
