# 2024 Biden D-Day 80th Anniversary Address

On June 6, 2024, President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] delivered a speech in Normandy, France marking the 80th anniversary of the D-Day landings, using the occasion to warn against a resurgence of American isolationism and to pledge that the United States would "not walk away" from Ukraine.[1]

## Content of the speech

Biden spoke before a crowd of aging World War II veterans, many over 100 years old and in wheelchairs, and pointed to the beaches around him as "a powerful illustration of how alliances make us stronger," which he called "a lesson that I pray Americans never forget."[1] Acknowledging that soon "the last living voices of those who fought and bled on D-Day will no longer be with us," he urged the country not to forget that the postwar democratic order was worth fighting for.[1] "We cannot let what happened here be lost in the silence of the years to come," he said, adding that "the fact that they were heroes here that day does not absolve us of what we have do today."[1]

## Context

The address came days after Biden authorized Ukraine to fire U.S.-supplied weapons across the Russian border, a decision that U.S. officials linked to Moscow's planned [[2024-russian-naval-deployment-to-cuba|air and naval exercises in the Caribbean]].[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] carried the speech as a bonus item in its June 7, 2024 news brief without extended editorial commentary.[1]
