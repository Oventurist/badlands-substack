# Golf Force One

**"Golf Force One"** is the press nickname given to a modified Polaris Ranger armored escort vehicle introduced by the [[united-states-secret-service|United States Secret Service]] to shadow President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during golf outings.[1] The vehicle was first sighted at Trump's Turnberry course in Scotland during the weekend preceding July 28, 2025.[1]

## Description

The all-black, military-style buggy trailed Trump as he played the expansive golf links; Trump himself rode in a standard golf cart while the armored Ranger followed closely behind.[1] According to reporting by RT, the vehicle features armored panels, tinted bulletproof windows, and visibly reinforced rear sections — a level of protection described as unusual for golf outings.[1] Security experts noted that its light frame allows high mobility across grassy terrain while still offering ballistic defense in the event of a crisis.[1]

## Commentary

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, treated the vehicle as an addition to Trump-era political folklore.[1] Commentary by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] joked that just as "Trumpa-a-Mania" appeared to be winding down with the passing of the wrestler [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]], the president had produced "some new lore," comparing the fleet of specialized presidential vehicles to Batman's assortment of "Bat" vehicles and speculating about future additions such as a presidential jet-ski, snowmobile, or paragliding go-kart.[1]
