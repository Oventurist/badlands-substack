# Working Families Tax Cuts Act

The Working Families Tax Cuts Act is legislation enacted under the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration described by the [[white-house|White House]] as delivering "landmark tax relief," including No Tax on Tips, No Tax on Overtime, No Tax on Social Security, and other savings.[1]

The law was highlighted in the administration's post-speech statement following the [[2026-state-of-the-union|2026 State of the Union Address]] as one of the accomplishments congressional Democrats declined to applaud.[1]
