# Mandate Cultivation

Mandate Cultivation is a term used in Badlands commentary, particularly by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], to describe the deliberate construction of visible public consent before a major political action is taken.[1] Rather than acting first and defending the action afterward, the theory holds that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and allied actors survey the "American battlespace" and wait for an opportunity to rapidly cultivate the public mandate needed to make a change durable.[1]

## Application

Discussing the Minnesota Somali fraud allegations, Burning Bright wrote that "Trump and team will look at the American battlespace, and they will wait for an opportunity to rapidly cultivate the very public mandate that they need to solidify an actual" outcome.[1] He characterized Mandate Cultivation as sitting "at the heart of patriot plans," and framed it as participatory — closing with the question to readers, "Are you doing your part?"[1]

The concept is closely related to Badlands' broader treatment of narrative warfare, in which independent reporting such as that of [[anthony-rubin|Anthony Rubin]] in Ohio generates the public awareness that makes federal enforcement politically survivable.[1]
