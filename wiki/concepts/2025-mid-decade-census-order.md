# 2025 Mid-Decade Census Order

In August 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that he was ordering a "new and highly accurate CENSUS," saying it would be based on the "information gained from the Presidential Election of 2024."[1]

## The announcement

Trump made the announcement in a Truth Social post on Thursday, August 7, 2025, writing that "People who are in our country illegally will NOT BE COUNTED IN THE CENSUS."[1]

The census is a constitutionally mandated count of every person in the United States every ten years, last conducted in 2020.[1] Politico reported that a full census had never been conducted mid-decade in this manner, nor had one ever excluded noncitizens from the count.[1] Censuses are consequential in American governance: each count determines how many House seats every state receives through apportionment, and the results help direct billions of dollars in federal, state and local funding.[1]

Trump had sought to include a citizenship question on the census since his first term, an effort the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] struck down on procedural grounds in 2019.[1]

## Badlands analysis

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded to claims that a president cannot order a census by noting that the first US Census in 1790 was authorized by [[congress|Congress]] and signed into law by President George Washington, effectively ordering the census to proceed, and that every decennial census since has been conducted under presidential authority — authorized by both congressional action and presidential proclamation.[1]

She also observed that off-cycle data gathering similar to a census, but without impact on apportionment, took place in 1915 and 1925, ordered by the Executive Branch alone without congressional authorization; Trump could do the same now, though it would not affect representation.[1] If Congress writes legislation authorizing a proper census and Trump proclaims it, she argued, "it's game on — no matter how mad that makes Politico and their co-conspirators," closing with the hope of obtaining legitimate population numbers.[1]
