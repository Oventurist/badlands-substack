# 1913

1913 stands as a pivotal year in the narrative advanced by Badlands Media, marking what the source describes as the moment a coordinated group of bankers and political operatives permanently altered the American monetary system. According to the article, the events of 1913—encompassing the ratification of the Sixteenth Amendment, the passage of the Revenue Act of 1913, and the enactment of the Federal Reserve Act—constitute the consummation of a long-term plan to place the United States under a fiat monetary system controlled by a small cabal of financiers. The article, authored by anon_fa_mous and published on November 29, 2022, frames 1913 as the year the "cabal enslaved America."

## Background

The article traces its narrative back to the founding of the United States, arguing that the nation's early economy was based on "hard money"—a system of tradable US-minted coins that represented "pure financial freedom" for citizens. Under this system, the value of money was, in the source's view, "astronomically more" than contemporary currency, and few citizens faced loans or debt. Banks initially operated as convenience markets where people stored their US-minted coins. Over time, however, the article claims that banks allowed their bank notes to be openly traded, creating a parallel economy over the official US hard money system. This practice transitioned from coins to paper, laying the groundwork for fractional reserve lending. The article asserts that the only mechanism to stop this practice was mass citizen demand for gold redemption, making the abandonment of the gold standard a priority for bankers.

## Hard Money vs. Paper Money

The article frames the 19th-century American economy as a battle between the Founding Fathers' "hard money" system and what it calls the cabal's "paper money" system. It describes the hard money system as "the perfect economic system for the American people," allowing citizens to own their money, homes, land, and freedom. This environment, the source contends, fostered an entrepreneurial spirit that "barely exists today." In contrast, the modern "corporate society" is said to crush small businesses and concentrate power among elites. The article draws a direct line from the hard money era to what it describes as [[Donald Trump]]'s attempt, beginning in 2016, to restore the old monetary system in a manner reminiscent of [[Andrew Jackson]].

## The Second Bank of the United States

When the Second Bank of the United States was established, [[Andrew Jackson]] recognized what the article characterizes as overreach by bankers acting as a "fourth branch of government run by the elite." The source claims Jackson understood that if bankers succeeded in their ultimate plan, they would "enslave every citizen in the country," and he therefore "sought to root them out." According to the article, Jackson favored hard money over paper money and remains the only US President in history to have completely paid off the US National Debt. The article notes bitterly that the cabal later achieved its goal through the Federal Reserve and, as an additional insult, placed Andrew Jackson on the $20 bill. Jackson is identified as [[Donald Trump]]'s favorite US President because "he took on the cabal bankers and won bigly," which the source says Trump has been attempting since taking office in 2016.

## The Gold Standard and the Long Depression

The article states that the [[Coinage Act of 1873]] ended the coinage of silver and placed the American economy on the gold standard for the first time, a period that lasted from 1873 to 1933. This shift caused deflation, which the source claims increased the buying power of America's currency. The article asserts that bankers hated the gold standard so intensely that by 1873 they began calling for the government to permanently adopt a fiat currency issued by a central bank. The source claims the gold standard "forced the bankers into a siloed depression while citizens flourished."

The article cites [[Murray Rothbard]]'s book _History of Money and Banking of the United States_ to argue that the so-called "Long Depression" was "only a misunderstood recession," since real wages and production were increasing throughout the period. Rothbard is said to have attributed falling prices to the deflationary gold standard in the aftermath of the Civil War. [[David Ames Wells]] is also cited, having written of technological advancements during the period from 1870 to 1890 and explained how deflation empowered citizens and increased productivity. Wells's direct quotation reads: "The economic changes that have occurred during the last quarter of a century—or during the present generation of living men—have unquestionably been more important and more varied than during any period of the world's history."

## The Populist Party and the Income Tax Movement

According to the article, during the late nineteenth century various groups—including the [[Populist Party]] (formally the People's Party)—favored the establishment of a federal income tax. The source claims the Long Depression was "doing more damage to the rich and powerful" and that they needed something to permanently suppress citizens. The Populist Party was formed by former slave owners in the aftermath of the Civil War. The article states that the party demanded a graduated income tax in its 1892 platform, mirroring the Democratic Party led by William Jennings Bryan, who advocated for the income tax law passed in 1894 and proposed an income tax in the Democratic Party's 1908 platform.

## The Jekyll Island Meeting

The article describes the [[Jekyll Island meeting]] as the site where the cabal's master plan was hatched. It states that after the Panic of 1907, [[nelson-aldrich|Nelson W. Aldrich]] set up the National Monetary Commission to investigate a better economic system for the US. The Commission concluded that the European model—which included the Central Bank of England—was superior, and its reports and recommendations became principal bases for the enactment of the Federal Reserve Act of 1913. The article claims that in 1910, leaders of the Populist Party convinced Aldrich, then leader of the Republican Party, to transform the entire monetary system of the United States, with the plan being hatched at the Jekyll Island Club.

The article lists seven attendees and their affiliations at the time:
- Senator Nelson Aldrich (his daughter married a Rockefeller)
- A.P. Andrews (Assistant Secretary of the United States Treasury Department)
- Paul Warburg (employee of Jacob Schiff and with family ties to the Rothschilds)
- Frank A. Vanderlip (President of the National City Bank of New York)
- Henry P. Davison (Senior Partner of J. P. Morgan Company)
- Charles D. Norton (President of the Morgan-dominated First National Bank of New York)
- Benjamin Strong (representing J. P. Morgan)

Together, the article claims these men represented about one quarter of the world's wealth at the time. It states they left Hoboken, New Jersey on a train in complete secrecy, dropping their last names in favor of first names or code names to avoid discovery. The source asserts that "secrecy is at the heart of the cabal's operations."

## The Titanic Connection

The article suggests a potential connection between the Jekyll Island plan and the sinking of the RMS Titanic in April 1912, one year prior to the signing of the Federal Reserve Act. It states that several prominent figures died on the Titanic, including American millionaire John Jacob Astor IV, industrialist Benjamin Guggenheim, Macy's owner Isidor Straus, and journalist and social reformer William Thomas Stead. The source notes that it "has long been suggested" that these men all opposed the Federal Reserve Act to various degrees.

The article highlights Isidor Straus in particular, stating that he wrote an article published by the _New York Times_ on October 25, 1911, titled "THE QUESTION OF RESERVES," in which he "argues for the Recognition of the Central Association's Notes as Reserve Money." Straus is identified as a Director of the Mutual Alliance Trust Company and one of its founding 13 directors, which the article says included Emanuel Lehman, William Rockefeller, and Cornelius Vanderbilt. The source frames Straus as an opposing figure to the cabal.

## Ratification of the Sixteenth Amendment and the Revenue Act of 1913

The article claims that with opposition removed, the necessary representatives, senators, and President were ready to sign off on the cabal's plan. It states that the Sixteenth Amendment was ratified in 1913 and Democratic leaders agreed to seek passage of the Revenue Act of 1913, which would dramatically lower tariffs and implement an income tax. Representative Oscar Underwood is said to have quickly moved the bill through the House of Representatives, with approval in the United States Senate following extensive lobbying by the Wilson administration. President [[Woodrow Wilson]] signed the Revenue Act of 1913 into law on October 3, allowing for the establishment of the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).

## The Federal Reserve Act

The Federal Reserve Act was passed by the 63rd United States Congress and signed into law by President [[Woodrow Wilson]] on December 23, 1913. The law created the Federal Reserve System, the central banking system of the United States that the source claims the country has been "stuck with ever since." The article claims that with these moves, Wilson "had successfully enslaved every American to the fiat monetary system." According to the source, every American thereafter had to pay income tax, face continued devaluation of their money, and submit to the Federal Reserve, which it describes as a privately owned bank that became the most powerful organization in the United States.

## The Pujo Committee

The article discusses the [[Pujo Committee]] as a rare institutional check on the cabal's power that ultimately failed. It states that Arsene Pujo formed a special committee to investigate the monopoly of J. P. Morgan and New York's other powerful bankers after the creation of the Federal Reserve and the IRS. The committee issued a unanimous determination: "We unanimously determined that a small CABAL of financiers had gained consolidated control of numerous industries through the abuse of the public trust in the United States."

The article also quotes the Pujo Report: "No less than eighteen different major financial corporations were under control of a cartel led by J.P Morgan, George F Baker and James Stillman. The Pujo Report singled out individual bankers including Paul Warburg, Jacob H. Schiff, Felix M. Warburg, Frank E. Peabody, William Rockefeller and Benjamin Strong Jr. The report identified over $22 billion in resources and capitalization controlled through 341 directorships held in 112 corporations by members of the empire headed by J.P. Morgan."

The article claims that Arsene Pujo left Congress in 1913 and that despite some support in following years, nothing came from the investigation. It states that "the cabal squashed the findings of the committee, and since they controlled the media, it never got out to the mass public." The source asserts that "the same tactics they used then are still in use today."

## Legacy and Modern Resonance

The article argues that while the cabal maintains some power, it is "slowly becoming less relevant" as more people abandon mainstream media and develop "extreme skills for discernment." It calls for the end of central banking and the fiat monetary system, advocating instead for a system with "inherent, underlying value and trust in each trade" that would "eradicate the possibility of corruption entering the system in the future." In light of what the source describes as reckless money printing prompted by the Covid-19 Crisis, the article states that every currency in the world is becoming less valuable and that a reversal to a system like the one from 1873 is needed—specifically, "a new deflationary period."

The source concludes with a vision of economic freedom: "Imagine buying a house for $5,000 or a car for $1,000, no debt and a full paycheck sans tax." It claims this is "completely realistic with no IRS or no Federal Reserve," and asserts that "the cabal subsists on our struggle."
