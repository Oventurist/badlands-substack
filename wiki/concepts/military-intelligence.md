# Military Intelligence

**Military intelligence** occupies a central place in the interpretive framework of the [[qanon|Q]] drops, where it is presented as the instrument by which a president can act against entrenched interests inside the civilian intelligence bureaucracy.[1]

Chapter 3 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] describes Chapters 2 and 3 of the series as carrying "a pretty heavy message of Military Intelligence and its importance in this operation."[1] Analysing drop #17 of October 31, 2017, the author sets out the claimed advantages of the military channel: it can bypass the various alphabet agencies, does not answer to them, and is not subject to congressional oversight.[1] Military investigations, on this account, can be brought directly to the President, and the resulting intelligence and evidence can then be used against various actors — potentially in [[military-tribunals|military tribunals]].[1]

The [[united-states-marine-corps|Marines]] are treated as the operational counterpart to this intelligence channel, since they answer directly to the President.[1] The author argues that this gives a president a means of working around the alphabet agencies, a tool for combating globalist surveillance of the United States, and, if required, a force to quell civil unrest at home.[1]

## Caution on tribunals

The same chapter includes an explicit caveat about claims of military tribunals having already taken place. The author states that anyone asserting they have personally witnessed tribunals, or that they know for a fact such proceedings occurred and what the punishments were, is in his opinion intentionally misleading their audience and telling them what they want to hear.[1] His stated reasoning is that operators do not divulge, and that if tribunals were occurring the public would not learn of them until well after the fact.[1]

## Special access programs and presidential access

Chapter 4 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series argues that nearly every [[unacknowledged-special-access-programs|special access program]] was changed or altered after the September 11 attacks, that intelligence became progressively more compartmentalized, and that the ability of agencies to work together expanded.[2] The author holds that this architecture, used benevolently, is a powerful protective tool, but that no president until [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] used it toward benevolent ends.[2] Because the military came to hold the same special access programs as the civilian alphabet agencies, he argues, Trump as Commander in Chief could see any and all information he desired, a capacity he grounds in the constitutional grant of complete presidential control and clearance over intelligence operations conducted under his watch.[2]

## "Military is the only way"

Drop #26 of November 1, 2017 is described in Chapter 4 as quite possibly the most misunderstood, misconstrued and misquoted Q drop of all time.[2] The author states emphatically that it does not posit, speculate or insinuate that the military is physically coming to save the public, and that it describes neither martial law, nor an Emergency Broadcast System alert, nor a simultaneous mass round-up of bad actors.[2] In his reading, "military is the only way" refers to military intelligence and the information gained from it, which — placed in Trump's hands — set off a chain reaction leading to the defeat of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] "by the book," through law and order.[2]

The reasoning offered is institutional: if the military simply came in to save the day, the author asks, how would that rebuild America or restore trust in government, its institutions and the justice system?[2] His answer is that it would signal only that those systems were so broken the military had to rescue them, further eroding public trust.[2] The military is described as involved, but not in the manner many readers would prefer at this point in time, and readers are told they are part of a military operation far bigger and more complex than even the most seasoned anon can comprehend.[2]

An anon reply featured in drop #28 addressed the earlier question from drop #11 — "What must be completed in order to engage MI over other 3 letter agencies" — and concluded that Trump and his team had infiltrated [[antifa|Antifa]] and dissenting local governments, to which Q responded "Well done. Picture being painted."[2] The chapter treats this as explaining why riots and uprisings were allowed to continue through 2020, on the theory that the bad actors involved were being tracked and watched.[2]
