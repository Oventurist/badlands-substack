# SEC Crypto Task Force Tokenization Roundtable

The SEC Crypto Task Force tokenization roundtable, formally titled "Tokenization — Moving Assets Onchain: Where TradFi and DeFi Meet," was a public event hosted by the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission]] on May 12, 2025.[1]

## Format

The roundtable formed part of the SEC Crypto Task Force's ongoing series of discussions on crypto asset regulation.[1] No registration was required to view the virtual webcast, and in-person walk-ins were welcomed, subject to security checks for visitors.[1]

## Agenda

Under a Sunshine Notice, the Crypto Task Force announced it would host the roundtable at 1 p.m. Eastern on May 12, 2025, open to the public.[1] The agenda focused on tokenization — the migration of traditional financial assets onto blockchain rails — and the interface between traditional finance and decentralized finance.[1] Members of the public were able to communicate directly with the Crypto Task Force on tokenization and other topics, and to request meetings with it.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[gmoney|GMONEY]] framed the roundtable as tokenization "getting the GREEN LIGHT," reading significance into the date proximity of May 13.[1]
