# Tesla Robotaxi Launch in Austin

The Tesla robotaxi launch in Austin was the June 2025 debut of a limited self-driving car service by [[tesla|Tesla]] in the Austin, Texas area, arriving after nearly a decade of anticipation.[1] Company executives, including [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], have described the autonomous vehicle technology behind the service as critical to Tesla's future.[1] The launch was reported by [[wired|Wired]].[1]

## Service details

The initial service was open only to early users invited by Tesla and comprised roughly twenty 2025 Model Y vehicles available for rides through a Tesla-built application between 6 a.m. and midnight.[1] Terms of service posted on X by invited riders indicated the service would be paused or limited during bad weather.[1] Musk posted on X that rides during the invite-only phase would carry a flat fee of $4.20.[1]
