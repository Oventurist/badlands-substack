# Energetic Purge

**Energetic purge** is the term used in the June 26, 2025 Badlands Media essay "Signal Collapse" for the claim that global instability is not collapse but release — a planetary equivalent of somatic trauma discharge.[1] The essay opens the argument with the formulation "A body that heals shakes. So does a nation. So does a planet."[1]

## Body as prototype

The essay draws a point-by-point correspondence between individual trauma release — the body trembles, the breath unlocks, stored patterns of suppression surface — and planetary events: protests erupt, corruption is exposed, and buried truths re-emerge.[1] This is described as "not coincidence" but "correspondence."[1] The work of [[peter-levine|Peter Levine]] is invoked for the proposition that trauma is frozen energy and that healing requires a return to motion through completion of an interrupted survival response; the author extends this claim to nations and civilizations, asserting that "the entire geopolitical field has been in a freeze response" and now trembles.[1]

The essay develops a second analogy from fascia, the connective tissue said to hold both the body together and trauma within it. Societies are described as developing their own fascia — networks of memory, culture, and myth — which lock up when collective trauma infiltrates them, producing psychological, spiritual, and economic stagnation.[1] Biolabs are characterized in this framing as "symbolic fascia nodes," embedded sites of blocked planetary energy, whose destruction the author attributes to the [[white-hats|White Hats]] in Ukraine, Iran, and "soon elsewhere."[1]

## Planetary chiropractic

Under the heading "Planetary Chiropractic," the essay describes [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] not merely as national leaders but as "alignment agents — global chiropractors correcting decades of unnatural contortion," with each post, strike, and silence supposedly timed to release pressure in a collective field.[1] The essay argues that such a field "cannot be healed with policy" and must instead "be felt… shaken… heard."[1]

It further claims that Trump's public statements typically follow rather than precede events because they are "affirmations that the energy has moved," and that the frequency of sovereignty "is not imposed — it is revealed."[1] The [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] is accordingly reframed as "a physiological event" rather than an idea.[1]

## Assessment

The energetic-purge model is a metaphorical and spiritual reading of world events rather than a testable claim; it treats military strikes and civil unrest as therapeutic phenomena without supporting evidence, and Badlands Media states that features represent the views of contributing authors.[1]
