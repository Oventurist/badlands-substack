# Kennedy Curse

The **"Kennedy Curse"** is the popular name for the long series of premature and violent deaths in the [[kennedy-family|Kennedy family]]. In [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series, the pattern is read not as misfortune but as evidence of sustained [[deep-state|Deep State]] hostility toward a family the author places outside the cabal.[1]

## The catalogue of deaths

Citing [[frederick-dodson|Frederick Dodson]]'s article "The Hidden Trump-Kennedy Connection," the article lists the following:[1]

- 1944 — [[joseph-p-kennedy|Joseph P. Kennedy]] killed in a plane crash.[1]
- 1948 — Kathleen Kennedy killed in a plane crash.[1]
- 1963 — [[john-f-kennedy|JFK]] assassinated.[1]
- 1968 — [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]] assassinated.[1]
- 1969 — Ted Kennedy mysteriously drove his car off a bridge, which the author judges more likely than not an assassination attempt.[1]
- 1984 — David Kennedy died of a drug overdose, described as "very sketchy."[1]
- 1997 — Michael Kennedy died in a skiing accident.[1]
- 1999 — [[jfk-jr|JFK Jr.]] and Carolyn Kennedy died in a plane crash in the ocean.[1]
- 2011 — Kara Kennedy died of a sudden heart attack; the author notes the CIA has possessed a "heart attack gun" for decades.[1]
- 2012 — Mary Richardson Kennedy died from an official ruling of suicide, which the author treats skeptically.[1]
- 2018 — Christopher Kennedy died from a heart attack.[1]
- 2019 — Saoirse Kennedy Hill died from a drug overdose.[1]
- 2020 — Maeve Kennedy Townsend McKean disappeared during a canoe trip; the author remarks that "Kean" is very similar to "Ken."[1]

## Interpretation

The author asks whether it is normal for so many members of one family to die of unnatural causes, and argues it is reasonable to surmise that the Deep State strongly dislikes the Kennedys.[1] He treats the deaths as beginning well before, and continuing long after, JFK's attempt to wrest the power of money creation from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and return it to the people — an act described as unambiguously positive for the country and an unambiguous rebuke to the cabal.[1]

On this reading the Kennedys, like [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], stand outside the cabal machine: both families are hated by the Deep State but loved by the People.[1] The author extends the argument to the family's recent video endorsing [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], suggesting this does not prove they have "turned" — they may be reacting to life-threatening background pressure, or serving as a "positive elite" goalie filling the opening where the other team might otherwise score.[1]
