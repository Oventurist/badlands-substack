# Bering Strait Tunnel

The Bering Strait tunnel is a proposed 200 km rail tunnel connecting Russia and Alaska, and thereby the Eurasian and North American rail networks. In the Badlands Media corpus it is the emblematic project of a cooperative world development paradigm and the alternative to great-power war.

## Nineteenth-century roots

The idea is traced in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s [[avoiding-wwiii|Avoiding WWIII]] to the world land-bridge conception of [[william-gilpin|William Gilpin]], the Lincoln ally and Colorado governor who published *The Cosmopolitan Railway* in 1890 as an extension of the American transcontinental rail model into Eurasia.

## Modern proposals

The article reports that the project was first proposed in its modern form by Russia in August 2007 and re-emphasized by the Russian government in 2011, with the announcement carried in Business Insider. Both George W. Bush in 2007 and [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] afterwards ignored the Russian offers. On May 7, 2014, the Chinese government, speaking through the state-owned Beijing Times and China Daily, gave its support to the century-old project — which Ehret says means the proposal "can no longer be ignored."

## The Alaska–Canada rail link

On September 26, 2020, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced federal support for a 2,570 km railway connecting Alaska for the first time with Canada and the lower 48 states, a project the article treats as the North American precondition of the tunnel. The $17 billion project was assigned to the Alaska–Alberta Railway Development Corporation. Its fate depends on Environmental Impact Assessments and the Federal Government of Canada, which Ehret describes as run by "anti-development Rhodes Scholars and technocrats committed to depopulation and world government." If built, he argues, it would unlock Arctic resources, create tens of thousands of direct and millions of indirect jobs, and orient North America toward Asian markets — breaking the West free of both anti-development environmentalism and pro-militarization right-wing views in favour of "win-win cooperation" with Eurasian partners.
