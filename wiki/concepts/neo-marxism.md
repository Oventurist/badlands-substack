# Neo-Marxism

Neo-Marxism, in the Badlands Media corpus, denotes the transposition of classical Marxist class conflict into cultural and identity categories — race, gender, sexuality and environment — pursued through institutions rather than revolution. It is closely related to the broader treatment of [[communism|communism]] elsewhere in the corpus.

The essay [[ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo|AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO]] treats [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]], [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and COVID mandates as "covert forms of Neo-Marxism designed to achieve the strategic objectives of the New World Order," and asks [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to model their introduction into public education on that basis. The [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]] is folded into the same category, prompted as a means of reinforcing "collectivism, Neo-Marxism, the loss of individual rights and the loss of national sovereignty."

The temporal logic of this subversion is drawn from Soviet defector [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]], whose account of ideological subversion unfolding over numerous generations [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] asked the model to incorporate. In the resulting synthesis, Neo-Marxist ideologies are not imposed but seeded — through curricula, activist movements, media capture and [[esg|ESG]] incentives — so that a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] can be installed slowly across generations.
