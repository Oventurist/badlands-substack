# Neo-Marxism

**Neo-Marxism** is the category under which "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" places the ideological content held to be infiltrating public education.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] instructed ChatGPT to include the infiltration of public education using [[critical-race-theory|CRT]], [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and [[covid-mandates|COVID mandates]] as covert forms of neo-Marxism designed to achieve the strategic objectives of the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The defining feature in this usage is covertness. Rather than an open programme of class revolution, neo-Marxism is presented as operating through cultural and educational institutions, substituting identity categories for economic class while preserving the collectivist priority of group over individual.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) In the model's synthesis this appears as ideologies that emphasise collective rights and group identity over individual freedoms, gradually eroding the concept of individual rights, and as [[esg|ESG]] pressure rewarding organisations aligned with those ideologies.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Deschamps also asked that the [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]] be treated as neo-Marxist warfare reinforcing collectivism and the loss of national sovereignty, and that the whole be read through the multigenerational [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]] framework attributed to [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]], in which subversion unfolds across generations rather than in a single upheaval.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
