# Q

"Q" is the pseudonymous poster whose anonymous message board posts — known within the community as "drops" — form the textual basis of the movement often labeled QAnon. Within the Badlands Media corpus, Q's drops are treated less as prophecy than as a research prompt: cryptic pointers whose subsequent corroboration in public reporting is taken as evidence of foreknowledge.

## Function in the corpus

In An SOS From Ground Zero, [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] deploys a drop in exactly this way. Having published an article on November 14, 2022 about [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]], the [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] ballot vendor, she notes as a sidebar: "Interesting that Q's latest drop on November 18 mentioned Runbeck, isn't it?" The mention functions as validation of her independent line of inquiry rather than as its source, illustrating the corpus's characteristic use of Q material — a citizen journalist's own research is presented first, and the drop is offered afterward as convergence.

Readers of the corpus generally encounter Q alongside the broader themes of information warfare, distrust of institutional media, and the claim that [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and similar outlets constitute an alternative news ecosystem: "We are the news now."

## Anons as the crew: the 2023 White Squall reading

The September 2023 essay "Are Jack Smith and Fani Willis in on the Plan?" frames the prosecutions of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as the possible fulfilment of a Q comm. Its author, [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], describes Q as "an anonymous insider on 4chan" who first appeared in October 2017, and reads the film [[white-squall|White Squall]] as a metaphor for the journey of Trump, Q and anons since then. In the film's closing trial the captain is redeemed by his surviving crew; in the same manner, the essay argues, "loyal Anons are stepping into their role as the New News ('you are the news now' – Q)," shaping public opinion so that Trump receives a fair hearing. The piece closes with the movement's slogan, [[wwg1wga|WWG1WGA]].
