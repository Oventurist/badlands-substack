# Q

Q is the pseudonymous source of the "Q drops," a body of posts that Badlands contributors treat as the communications of a legitimate insider describing a long-running plan to expose corruption to the public.[1]

## Role in the Badlands framework

[[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] writes that, assuming Q is a legitimate insider, the visible backfiring of cabal propaganda during the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel-Palestine conflict]] represents the part of the plan in which the people are being "shown" what is going on — an outcome described as an extremely positive development in the wider information battle.[1]

Although many Badlands readers know the specifics of the drops, the author observes that for the average person the Q movement is loosely associated with a small number of ideas, the most prominent being that everything the public sees is scripted and fake.[1] The spread of that single idea matters strategically: it gives people "a place to go when their old narratives break down," providing an alternative framework at the moment established narratives collapse.[1]

## Pain incoming

Q is said to have warned many times of "pain incoming."[1] American Hypnotist glosses this in psychological terms: having one's reality torn asunder can in some cases be comparable to literal torture, and readers are urged to keep this in mind when they see people breaking down, and to be glad if they still have their wits.[1] People generally do not change unless they are in pain of some kind, and the pain of social ostracization in particular drives continual self-modification to conform to shifting norms — a dynamic known in marketing and persuasion as the "compliance ladder."[1] The spell breaks, on this account, when the social environment can no longer deliver coherent instructions to its members; a program that ceases to be executable will "short circuit," and the experience for the person undergoing it can be primordially terrifying.[1]
