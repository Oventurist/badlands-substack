# Emergency Broadcast System

The Emergency Broadcast System (EBS) is a national alerting mechanism that, within [[q-operation|Q]] interpretive circles, has been treated as a potential instrument for delivering mass disclosure directly to the American public. The system is referenced in Q drop #34, published November 1, 2017, and has been "a large topic of discussion ever since this drop was made," according to the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" commentary on drops #31–40.[1]

## Role in the Q drops

Drop #34 pairs the EBS reference with language about a presidential declaration of "temporary military control," framing the alert system as the communications half of a contingency in which ordinary institutions are bypassed.[1] The Badlands commentator, [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]], places the passage in the [[future-proves-past|"future proves past"]] category, writing that whether an EBS activation will actually occur "remains to be seen."[1]

## Interpretation

The principal argument advanced for the EBS in this reading is circumvention of the legacy press: "dropping an EBS to everyone when or if this operation goes hot would be a hell of an effective way to circumvent the MSM."[1] The same chapter argues that mainstream outlets are themselves implicated in the corruption Q describes, and that some media figures "will be forced to face justice for the continued misleading of the public," which supplies the rationale for a broadcast channel that does not pass through them.[1]

The commentary is careful to hedge, noting alongside the EBS discussion that some Q material may be "misinformation from Q to keep the [[deep-state|Deep State]] off kilter."[1]
