# New Axis

The "New Axis" is the term used in the Badlands Media corpus for the quintet of great powers — the United States, Russia, China, Iran and North Korea — that Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argues are the real players in the transition from a hegemonic to a multipolar world order.

## Origin of the term

According to the July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief, Burning Bright has referred to this set of five powers as the "New Axis" since July 2025 — two months before *The Guardian* referred to the same set as the "Axis of Upheaval." The Brief's commentary on NATO Secretary-General [[mark-rutte|Mark Rutte]]'s Ankara summit remarks notes that Rutte, alongside praising President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for triggering a surge in European nationalism and defense spending, cited the kinetic and economic campaigns waged by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] — and threw in [[iran|Iran]] and [[north-korea|North Korea]] for good measure — as proof of Trump's underlying premise "that we are moving from a hegemonic world to a multipolar one, and that the only language of negotiation in that world is power."

## Place in the corpus's geopolitics

The term belongs to the same framework as the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]]: a reading of world politics in which the unipolar hegemony is being supplanted by a multipolar order, with the five powers of the New Axis as its principal poles. Burning Bright's commentary frames Rutte's acknowledgment of the quintet as evidence that even the NATO leadership understands "the real game Trump is playing, and who's playing it with him."
