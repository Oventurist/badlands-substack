# 2025 ICE Enforcement Surge

The 2025 ICE enforcement surge refers to the wave of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] raids and arrests carried out across the United States following President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration in January 2025.[1]

## Arrest and crossing figures

Daily arrests soared from an average of 311 to 956 on Sunday, January 26 and 1,179 on Monday, January 27.[1] The crackdown also had a chilling effect on the U.S.–Mexico border: crossings in the first days of Trump's second term ran at less than half the level of the final days of the Biden administration, with just 600 people crossing on Sunday compared to roughly 1,400 daily crossings in December 2024, and the Del Rio sector recording only 60 crossings against a peak of more than 4,000 in December 2023.[1] Matt Elliston, director of the ICE Baltimore field office, said there is "no free pass" for illegal immigrants under the new rules, while noting that officers continued to prioritize migrants who had committed crimes or posed a public safety threat.[1]

## New York City raids

Heavily armed federal immigration agents swept through New York City on Tuesday, January 28, beginning in the Bronx — where Trump's support surged 35 percent between 2020 and 2024 — targeting migrants with warrants for murder, kidnapping and other serious crimes.[1] Among those arrested was Anderson Zambrano-Pacheco, 25, an alleged ringleader of the Venezuelan prison gang [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]], taken into custody at an Ogden Avenue apartment.[1] Residents of Hispanic and black communities that had backed Trump in large numbers in the 2024 election publicly welcomed the raids.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary credited Trump, DHS Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]], Border Czar [[tom-homan|Tom Homan]], ICE and [[us-border-patrol|U.S. Border Patrol]] with delivering measurable results, praising transparency on detentions and detainers and rejecting "weaponized empathy" and emotional blackmail.[1]
