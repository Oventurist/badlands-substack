# Werewolf Units

**Werewolf units** were the name given to the [[stay-behind-units|stay-behind]] cells that [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] established throughout the territories conquered by the Axis powers, so called because their members were "citizens by day and killers by night."[1] Colonel [[roxane-watkins|Roxane Watkins]] identifies them as the direct organizational ancestor of the postwar networks generically called [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]].[1]

## Command and structure

Hitler used intelligence officers including General [[reinhard-gehlen|Reinhard Gehlen]] and General [[karl-wolff|Karl Wolff]] to set up the network.[1] Each unit consisted of roughly 10 to 15 citizens with a primary and a secondary leader; the command structure knew only those two, never the full roster, which preserved plausible deniability.[1] Hidden caches supporting each cell contained at minimum firearms, ammunition, explosives, communication devices and money.[1]

## Purpose

Every territory the Axis acquired had such units emplaced.[1] The reasoning was that if the territory were later lost, these forces would generate chaos behind the new enemy lines and improve the chances of regaining it at a future date.[1] Sustaining the network required extensive training in weapons, explosives and radio equipment, a role filled in part by SS officer [[otto-skorzeny|Otto Skorzeny]].[1]
