# Modern-Day Nazis

"Modern-day Nazis" is the term [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] uses in *A Virus, or a Metaphor?* (17 July 2023) for the actors he identifies as the true adversary behind the events described in the article — the group he elsewhere calls the [[cabal|Cabal]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Origin of the term in the article

Carlson introduces the phrase through Tom Clancy's *[[the-sum-of-all-fears|The Sum of All Fears]]*, a novel the [[qanon|Q]] team referenced frequently.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Its premise is that a group of modern-day Nazis plays Russia against the United States: they unleash a bioweapon on Ukraine, attack a US naval ship, and detonate a nuclear bomb in Baltimore, all in the hope of getting the two powers to destroy each other.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Carlson reads Q's use of the story as a warning that the real modern-day Nazis are trying to create a war between Russia and the United States, and that the public should not fall for it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He adds that the two people the Nazis have never been able to control are [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], and promises a further post explaining who the Nazis really are.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Relationship to Communism

Carlson is explicit that Communism is not itself the ultimate enemy but a tool — "an axe that is being swung by an axe-murderer."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) The real enemy is the person swinging the axe: the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]], whom he identifies as the modern-day Nazis.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) On this framing, fighting the [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist takeover]] is a matter of keeping the Cabal from getting their hands on the axe, denying them the instrument with which they intend to destroy the country.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)
