# Narrative Warfare

**Narrative Warfare** is one of the core analytical frames used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators, referring to conflict waged through competing stories and information operations rather than — or alongside — kinetic force.[1] It was named as one of three central subjects of the twelfth episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], "The Fake and Gay, but Also Real and Serious War," published 28 March 2026.[1]

## Treatment in The Blitz Episode 12

In that episode, hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the contradictory public accounts of the Iranian Ayatollah — characterised in the episode's framing as "fake and gay, but also real and possibly not gay," and, per [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], "actually probably gay, according to what he's been told" — as a case study.[1] The episode's premise is that a story can be simultaneously a manufactured psychological operation and a real, serious event with real consequences, and that distinguishing the layers is the practical work of narrative warfare analysis.[1]

The hosts presented the discussion as a corrective for a world, and specifically a [[truth-community|Truth Community]], that they argue has "quite forgotten" narrative warfare along with [[sovereign-disentanglement|sovereign disentanglement]] and [[first-principles|first principles]].[1]
