# September 2023 Saudi-Russian Production Cut Extension

The September 2023 Saudi-Russian production cut extension was a coordinated announcement by [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[russia|Russia]] on September 5, 2023 that their voluntary oil supply reductions would run through the end of the year, sending crude to a new 2023 high.[1]

## Announcement

Just days after analysts noted that Saudi Aramco was floating the sale of up to $50 billion in new stock — read as a signal that oil was about to rise sharply — prices exploded higher when Saudi Arabia and, moments later, Russia surprised markets by extending production cuts well beyond the one month widely expected.[1] Just after 9am ET, Saudi Arabia said it would extend its voluntary cut of 1 million barrels per day for another three months, from October until the end of December.[1] The Saudi press agency SPA noted that the decision would be reviewed monthly to consider deepening the cut or increasing production, and that the extension was intended to reinforce the precautionary efforts made by [[opec|OPEC]] countries to support oil market stability.[1] The announcement came as a shock because 20 of 25 traders and analysts surveyed by Bloomberg the previous week had predicted only one additional month of cutbacks.[1]

Seconds after the Saudi decision, Russian deputy prime minister [[alexander-novak|Alexander Novak]] said Russia would extend its reduction of oil exports until the end of the year, cutting output by 300,000 barrels per day in voluntary reductions through December 2023.[1] Like the Saudis, Russia said its decision would be reviewed monthly with the possibility of deepening or reversing the reduction depending on world market conditions.[1] The report was carried by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]
