# Americana Brands

"Americana brands" is the category [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] uses for the corporations he considers most representative of American culture, and which sit at the centre of the boycott question raised by [[andy-frisella|Andy Frisella]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The list

Asking himself which brands are the most "American," Carlson names, in no particular order: Coca-Cola, [[disney|Disney]], Levi's, Hershey's, [[budweiser|Budweiser]], and [[mcdonalds|McDonald's]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He acknowledges the list will differ from person to person, but says these brands say "Americana" to him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The paradox

Carlson observes that other than perhaps McDonald's, all of these companies "have gone out of their way to upset their customer base through 'wokeness'."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) This leads to the article's central inversion: what if the Communists are forcing these companies to go woke in order to destroy them, and what if consumers boycotting them are unwittingly helping to remove Americana from America?1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He adds a further speculation — that McDonald's may be allowed to prosper by staying "woke-free" because it is being used to make Americans sick and unhealthy.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

Carlson does not advise abandoning boycotts of companies pushing wokeness, Satanism or the grooming of children, but insists readers consider that they may be doing the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s bidding, and that the easiest explanation may not be the only factor in play.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The frame connects the brand question to the broader [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist takeover]] thesis and to [[woke-capital|woke capital]] pressure from investment portfolios.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)
