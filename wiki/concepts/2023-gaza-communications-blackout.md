# 2023 Gaza Communications Blackout

The 2023 Gaza communications blackout refers to the shutdown of all internet and phone networks across the [[gaza|Gaza Strip]] on Thursday, November 16, 2023, which the main Palestinian telecom provider attributed to a dire lack of fuel in the besieged territory.[1] The outage effectively cut Gaza off from the outside world.[1]

The blackout coincided with signals that [[israel|Israel]]'s ground invasion could soon expand into southern Gaza: Palestinians in parts of the south reported receiving evacuation notices the same day.[1] Most of Gaza's 2.3 million residents were by then crowded into the south, including hundreds of thousands who had heeded earlier Israeli calls to evacuate the north ahead of the offensive against [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

## Humanitarian conditions

The [[united-nations|United Nations]] reported on the same day that nearly every person in the Gaza Strip lacked sufficient food and that more than two out of every three people lacked clean drinking water.[1] Residents described bread as scarce and supermarket shelves as bare, while central electricity and running water had been out for weeks.[1]

These conditions formed the backdrop to eroding U.S. public support for the Israeli campaign measured in polling that same week.[1]
