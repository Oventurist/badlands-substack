# Patriot Act

The USA PATRIOT Act is cited in Badlands Media's free-speech coverage as the archetypal example of Americans surrendering longstanding rights in exchange for promised safety.[1] The article describes one of the most destructive mistakes after 9/11 as allowing politicians to convince the public that rights had to be relinquished, a bargain that produced the Patriot Act and mass, warrantless spying on Americans by the intelligence community.[1]

## Relevance to present censorship debates

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argues the same playbook is now being run with "antisemitism" replacing "terrorism" as the cudgel used to justify censorship.[1] Under the older framing, opposition to the war in Afghanistan made one pro-terrorist, opposition to intervention in Libya made one pro-Gaddafi, and opposition to Ukraine funding made one pro-Putin; today criticism of Israel makes one pro-Hamas and antisemitic.[1] The contemporary legislative analogues to the Patriot Act, in this reading, are the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]] and [[executive-order-14188|Executive Order 14188]].[1]
