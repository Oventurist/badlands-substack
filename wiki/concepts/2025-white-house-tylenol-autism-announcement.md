# 2025 White House Tylenol and Autism Announcement

On Monday, September 22, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] used a White House news conference to promote unproven and, in some cases, discredited ties between [[tylenol|Tylenol]], vaccines, and autism, as his administration announced a wide-ranging effort to study the causes of the disorder.[1]

Trump instructed pregnant women roughly a dozen times not to take Tylenol — known generically as acetaminophen in the United States and paracetamol in most other countries — and urged mothers not to give the drug to their infants.[1] He also revived long-debunked claims that ingredients in vaccines, or the timing of shots close together, could contribute to rising rates of autism in the United States, without providing medical evidence, according to AP News.[1] The announcement appeared to rely on existing studies rather than significant new research.[1]

The event came as the [[make-america-healthy-again|Make America Healthy Again]] movement pushed for answers on the causes of autism; the coalition supporting Health Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] includes several anti-vaccine activists who have long spread claims that immunizations are responsible.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Jordan Sather argued that Trump was using Tylenol as a "gateway drug" to expose vaccines as a cause of autism.[1] Ashe in America mocked the coverage, noting that headlines uniformly deployed the words "unproven," "not backed by science," "unfounded," and "discredited" while the presenters had cited studies for an hour, including the head of Harvard's health sciences, and suggested readers use the phrasing to identify pharma-funded outlets.[1] Burning Bright placed the episode in a broader 2025 pattern he called "Disclosure disguised as Discovery," alongside de-nuclearization turning to re-nuclearization and the brink of war turning into unprecedented peace deals.[1]
