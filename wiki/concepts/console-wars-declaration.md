# Console Wars Declaration

The console wars declaration refers to a [[white-house|White House]] social media post of Sunday, October 26, 2025, in which the administration proclaimed an end to the long-running rivalry between video game console platforms.[1]

## The post

The White House social media account posted an AI-generated illustration of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] holding an energy sword while wearing Master Chief's assault armor from the Halo franchise, saluting a flag bearing only 40 stars.[1] The White House appears in the background, flying its own flag, and depicted with the then-demolished east wing intact.[1]

The caption read "Power to the Players," quoting a statement by [[gamestop|GameStop]] declaring the console wars "over" in response to the reveal that Halo would come to PlayStation in 2026.[1]

## Reception

Reporting framed the post as part of Trump's campaign for the Nobel Peace Prize, noting he has asserted that he ended seven wars — which would make the console wars an eighth.[1] The post triggered a surge in GameStop's stock and a frenzy on the meme stock's associated subreddit.[1] Badlands commentary responded mainly with lament that no sequel to Halo Wars was ever produced.[1]
