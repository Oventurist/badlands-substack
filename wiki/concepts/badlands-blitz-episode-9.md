# Badlands Blitz Episode 9

**Badlands Blitz Episode 9** is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly recap and dissection series published on the Badlands Media Substack. Dated March 7, 2026, the episode carries the subtitle "The New World Order is Dying … The New World is Beginning," and had registered 237 likes at the time of the source capture.

## Format and purpose

Addressed, as usual, to "Badlanders!", the post reiterates the standing remit of the series: The Blitz is "intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." Episode 9 therefore functions as a synthesis of the week's daily output rather than as freestanding reporting.

## Hosts

The episode marks the return of [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who, in the post's own framing, comes back "at the turn of the tide" and rescues the audience "as well … mostly from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]" — a joke consistent with the running banter between the show's regular contributors. Burning Bright is joined by [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] for the discussion.

## Themes

The write-up situates the conversation against a week in which, as the article puts it, "the world seems to be on the brink of both kinetic and financial catastrophe." Against that backdrop, the two hosts say they take "a 40k-foot view in order to reconcile the seeming contradictions at the heart of the transition between the [[new-world-order|New World Order]] … and the New World." The framing presents the apparent global instability not as simple breakdown but as the friction of a systemic transition — the dying of one order and the emergence of another — which is the interpretive thread the episode's title advances.

## Subscription appeal

As in other Blitz entries, the post closes with a note on the outlet's funding model. All Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz, but the article states that the only way the team is able to pay its people for their time is through the generous support of readers. Those who value the Substack team's daily work and "the step-up in said work for 2026" are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time. The post signs off with the recurring benediction to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
