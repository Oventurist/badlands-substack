# Entente Cordiale

The **Entente Cordiale** was an Anglo-French agreement signed on April 8, 1904, characterized in Badlands Media commentary as a secret mutual security pact rather than a mere colonial settlement.[1] In the account of [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the entente was the first structural pillar of a British strategy to encircle Germany with a hostile coalition, and none of the world wars of the twentieth century would have been possible without it.[1]

## Origins

The article attributes the diplomatic construction of the entente personally to [[edward-vii|King Edward VII]], citing a 1996 EIR report stating that the work "was masterminded and carried out by King Edward VII personally, with the various British ministers, cabinets, round tables, and other apparatus merely following in his wake."[1] Edward's stated geopolitical vision, in that telling, was "the encirclement of Germany with a hostile coalition, followed by a war of annihilation" in which nominal British allies such as France and Russia would also be decimated and crippled.[1]

The strategic motive advanced by the article is the threat posed to the [[british-empire|British Empire]] by the spread of [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]]'s system of nationalist political economy — the [[american-system|American System]] — through the German Zollverein, Meiji Japan, and the Russia of [[sergei-witte|Sergei Witte]] and the [[trans-siberian-railway|Trans-Siberian Railway]].[1] Witte's proposed "Continental League" of Russia, Germany and France, explained to Kaiser [[wilhelm-ii|Wilhelm II]] in 1897, is presented as the alternative order the entente was designed to destroy.[1]

## Expansion into the Triple Entente

By 1907 the weakened Russia of Czar [[nicholas-ii|Nicholas II]] secretly joined the Anglo-French pact, producing the [[triple-entente|Triple Entente]].[1] The article cites a December 14, 1909 *Daily Mail* editorial acknowledging that the king and his councillors had "strained every nerve to establish Ententes with Russia and with Italy; and have formed an Entente with France, and as well with Japan. Why? To isolate Germany."[1]

Kaiser Wilhelm II, writing in August 1914, described the encirclement as a fact "slowly and cleverly set going by Edward VII," concluding that "Edward VII is stronger after his death than am I who am still alive!"[1]

## The 120th anniversary, 2024

On April 8, 2024, UK Foreign Secretary [[david-cameron|David Cameron]] and French Foreign Minister [[stephane-sejourne|Stéphane Séjourné]] co-wrote an op-ed in the *Daily Telegraph* marking the entente's 120th anniversary, writing that "we must do even more to ensure we defeat Russia. The world is watching – and will judge us if we fail."[1] French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] said that "our Entente Cordiale remains as relevant as ever," framing it around support for Ukraine.[1] For the first time in history French troops participated in the changing of the guard at Buckingham Palace, and British troops marched in a military parade at the Élysée Palace to "God Save the King" and "La Marseillaise."[1]

Ehret argues that when Cameron or [[king-charles-iii|King Charles III]] champion the 1904 entente as a "bedrock of world peace," they are in fact championing the program that produced both world wars and that would make a third inevitable.[1]
