# Florida Civil Probe into the SPLC

On May 5, 2026, Florida Attorney General [[james-uthmeier|James Uthmeier]] announced a civil investigation into the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] and issued a subpoena demanding extensive records by May 25, 2026.[1]

## Focus of the investigation

The probe targets the SPLC's charitable fundraising practices, focusing on allegations of deceptive and unfair conduct.[1] Uthmeier stated that the organization "appears to be running a deceptive organization that pays informants to manufacture racism on its behalf," warning that consequences would follow if the allegations were confirmed.[1] The civil probe centers on whether the SPLC properly disclosed its practices to donors and complied with charitable solicitation rules, amounting to an official state-level examination of the nonprofit's operations and its transparency regarding informant payments and fundraising claims.[1]

## Subpoena scope

The subpoena requires detailed documentation of SPLC fundraising and operations in Florida, including disclosures to Florida donors about the use of informants; marketing and fundraising materials and their usage timelines; all names or aliases used in solicitations; and documentation of changes to SPLC communications regarding donations, fund allocation, and informant use.[1] It also seeks all websites used to solicit donations, annual donation totals from Florida donors, disbursements tied to informants, internal policies governing informant payments, and the share of the SPLC's budget allocated to such programs.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the SPLC subpoena as potentially "another overt act in the treasonous conspiracy," situating it in what she called the leftist [[lawfare|lawfare]] playbook of forwarding claims of racism to generate headlines and funding.[1] She raised the possibility that the investigation could converge with scrutiny of political fundraising platforms such as [[actblue|ActBlue]] and WinRed.[1]
