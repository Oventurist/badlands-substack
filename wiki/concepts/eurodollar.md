# Eurodollar

Eurodollars are US dollars held overseas, usually in offshore accounts.[1]

## Characteristics

Because Eurodollars are held outside the United States, they are neither taxed nor regulated, which in the account given by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] makes them the preferred vehicle for criminal wealth.[1] [[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.*]] argues that when [[richard-nixon|Richard Nixon]] moved against the drug infrastructure he was unknowingly "taking on the entire British oligarchy and the entire underpinnings of the Eurodollar market and the People's Republic of China."[1]

## Contemporary policy dimension

Carlson has argued that Trump-appointed Federal Reserve Chair [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] raised interest rates not to fight inflation but to kill the Eurodollar market and thereby sever the control the [[city-of-london|City of London]] holds over the US financial system.[1] Trump-appointed New York Fed head [[john-c-williams|John C. Williams]] created [[sofr|SOFR]] (the Secured Overnight Financing Rate), which in June 2023 fully replaced [[libor|LIBOR]] as the basis for bank lending rates.[1] Rising US rates cause Eurodollars to lose value, confronting offshore holders with a choice between continued depreciation and repatriating funds into a taxed and transparent US system.[1]
