# Info War Victory Laps

**Info War Victory Laps** is a term used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators for the practice, common among independent researchers and commentators in the [[information-war|Info War]], of publicly claiming vindication once a previously derided claim or prediction appears to be borne out by events.[1]

The concept received extended treatment on the second episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], published 17 January 2026 under the subtitle "Info War Victory Laps & the Dismantling of Legacy Media."[1] In that episode the hosts both endorsed and criticised the idea of taking victory laps, treating the practice as simultaneously a legitimate marker of research that proved correct and a temptation that can degrade the quality of the wider [[information-war|Info War]] effort.[1]

## Examples cited

Having laid out the general argument, the hosts took what they described as a couple of victory laps of their own, one concerning what the post terms "Venezuelan Proxy Princesses" and another concerning high-stakes United States court cases.[1]

## Relation to the evolution of the Info War

The victory-lap discussion was framed within a broader assessment of how the [[information-war|Info War]] had evolved and, in the hosts' phrasing, "Devolved" in places, together with a discussion of what participants could do to correct the drift.[1] The same episode paired the theme with the dismantling of [[legacy-media|legacy media]].[1]
