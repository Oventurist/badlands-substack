# Value Immune System

The **value immune system** is a term used by Badlands Media for the condition in which a social group or individual carefully discusses, analyzes and evaluates ideas against the accumulated body of objective morals and ethics present within the culture.[1] On this account morals and ethics evolve and improve with each ethical question debated, in the same way a biological immune system grows stronger and wiser through exposure to its environment.[1]

## Function

Religion is treated as a social institution rather than a cure or a cause: all social institutions are vulnerable to social contagion and [[mass-psychosis]] when the group's value immune system stops working.[1] The safeguard is not the institution itself but a consciously defined, enumerable set of principles of right and wrong that individuals can name and apply.[1]

## Failure and its exploitation

Industrial-grade mass psychosis in the form of [[communism]] is said to require both the destruction of true spiritual connection — the basis of real morality and ethics — and the deliberate dumbing-down of the population.[1] Once this cultural degradation has occurred, the population is easily convertible into a mass mob with no "true north" or moral compass, the precondition for [[menticide]].[1] The article argues that the secularization of Western society, meaning the removal of active philosophy and theism, is well underway, and suggests evaluating Hollywood output for how much theism and questioning of secular authority survive in its stories.[1]
