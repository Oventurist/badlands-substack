# Idaho House Bill 515

Idaho House Bill 515 is legislation passed by Republican members of the Idaho House of Representatives on Tuesday, February 13, 2024, that would allow the death penalty in cases of lewd conduct with children under the age of 12 where aggravating circumstances are present.[1]

## Provisions and debate

Under existing Idaho law the death penalty is available only in first-degree murder cases.[1] Co-sponsor Rep. [[bruce-skaug|Bruce Skaug]], R-Nampa, told the chamber that capital punishment under the bill would be reserved for the most heinous offenders, such as those who reoffend, saying: "There is a deep, dark, dark side in our culture. And it's our job to protect the children."[1]

## Constitutional questions

The bill runs against [[kennedy-v-louisiana|Kennedy v. Louisiana]], the 2008 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] decision that blocked the death penalty for child rape.[1] Florida had enacted a comparable child-rape death penalty law the previous year, setting up a potential vehicle for revisiting the precedent.[1] Asked about the constitutional objection, Skaug replied that "there's constitutional and there's constitutional. Depends on the court of the day," suggesting the present Court might rule differently.[1]
