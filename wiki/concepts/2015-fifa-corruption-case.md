# 2015 FIFA Corruption Case

The 2015 FIFA corruption case refers to the criminal proceedings in which several officials of [[fifa|FIFA]], world football's governing body, were arrested in a large-scale corruption scandal involving money laundering. The case is cited in the Badlands Brief as background context for suspicion of FIFA's institutional integrity.

## Use in Badlands commentary

In its coverage of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] final, the Brief's contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked the 2015 case alongside a newly announced [[fbi|FBI]] investigation into the Argentine football association for money laundering in the United States, arguing that the organization's record makes fan allegations of match manipulation more credible. He explicitly qualified the comparison, however, noting that the 2015 corruption scandal "was related to marketing and advertising money, not play on the field." That distinction is presented in the source as a clarification rather than a retraction of the broader suspicion that commercial incentives shape the sport's officiating.
