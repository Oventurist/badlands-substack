# How to Lie with Statistics

How to Lie with Statistics is a 1954 book by Darrell Huff on the misuse of statistical presentation, invoked by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] in his June 5, 2025 Badlands Media essay on climate science.[1] Discussing a chart of 600,000 years of ice-core data showing a connection between [[carbon-dioxide|carbon dioxide]] and temperature, Ehret concedes the correlation but insists that correlation is not causation, quoting Huff's remark that "a well-wrapped statistic is better than Hitler's Big Lie; it misleads, yet it cannot be pinned on you."[1]

The citation anchors the essay's broader claim that the modern climate consensus rests on a statistical mode of analysis rather than demonstrated physical causation, and that ordering the CO2 and temperature series by lead and lag reverses the assumed direction of causality.[1]
