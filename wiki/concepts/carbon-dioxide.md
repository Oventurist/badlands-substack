# Carbon Dioxide

Carbon dioxide (CO2) is the trace atmospheric molecule at the center of modern climate policy. In a June 5, 2025 Badlands Media feature, [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argued that CO2 has been "falsely framed for murder" by a statistical mode of climate analysis that, in his words, was designed to indict "a wonderful molecule named carbon dioxide" for genocide.[1] The essay set out to rehabilitate and defend CO2 before turning to what Ehret calls a true science of climate premised on [[astroclimatology]].[1]

## Biological role

Ehret notes that children are taught that CO2 is an integral part of the ecosystem and that plants love it.[1] Photosynthesis, which evolved with the advent of the chlorophyll molecule, requires constant infusions of carbon dioxide, which is broken down along with water, releasing oxygen back into the biosphere; over time this free oxygen formed the ozone layer and fueled the rise of higher life forms that depend on this "plant waste" for life.[1]

## Atmospheric proportions

Large amounts of carbon dioxide are generated regularly by biotic and abiotic activity — living animals, decaying biomass and volcanoes that constantly emit CO2 and other greenhouse gases — with only a surprisingly small portion attributable to human economic activity.[1] Taking the entire composition of greenhouse gases together, the article states that water vapour makes up 95 percent of the bulk, carbon dioxide 3.6 percent, nitrous oxide 0.9 percent, methane 0.3 percent, and aerosols about 0.07 percent.[1] Of the 3.6 percent carbon dioxide share, approximately 0.9 percent is said to be caused by human activity — restated by the author as human CO2 constituting less than 1 percent of the 3.6 percent of total greenhouse gases influencing climate.[1]

## The 400 PPM "ideal"

During the mid-20th century, Ehret writes, a belief emerged among some fringe climate scientists that the roughly 400 parts per million average of atmospheric carbon dioxide is the "natural and ideal amount," such that any upset of this mathematical average would destroy biodiversity.[1] The same modelers presumed the biosphere could be defined as a closed system governed by entropy, ignoring that ecosystems are open — connected to oceans of cosmic radiation from other stars, galaxies and supernovae, and mediated by nested arrays of electromagnetic fields.[1] Filmmaker [[adam-curtis|Adam Curtis]] documented how this closed-system belief migrated from the fringe into mainstream thinking.[1]

Against the 400 PPM ideal, the article points to commercial carbon dioxide generators routinely purchased by greenhouse managers, which raise CO2 concentrations to as high as 1,500 PPM, producing healthier, greener plants and vegetables.[1]

## Greening of the earth

Findings from the Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) instruments aboard [[nasa|NASA]]'s Terra and Aqua satellites, published February 11, 2019 in Nature Sustainability, found that global green leaf area increased by 5 percent since the early 2000s — an area equivalent to all of the Amazon rainforests — with at least 25 percent of that gain coming from China.[1] Ehret attributes the 2000–2017 greening largely to Chinese and Indian poverty-eradication programs involving reforestation, desert greening such as the South-to-North Water Diversion megaproject, agricultural innovation, and general industrial growth policies of the kind the West abandoned during the "post-industrialism" turn of the early 1970s.[1] The simple mechanism linking industrial growth to planetary greening, he answers, is carbon dioxide.[1]

## Temperature lag

The article contends that no one ever proved the claim that CO2 drives climate. Ice core records spanning 600,000 and 70,000 years show CO2 and temperature correlated, but with CO2 peaks and troughs following rather than preceding temperature — including an 800-year lag identified about 130,000 years ago.[1] The author cites Darrell Huff's [[how-to-lie-with-statistics|How to Lie with Statistics]] to the effect that "a well-wrapped statistic is better than Hitler's Big Lie."[1] Records further indicate that during many past ice ages CO2 stood as much as 800 percent higher than current levels, with zero human contribution.[1] Related anomalies include the absence of warming during the industrial expansion of 1940–1977 — a period when scientists warned of an impending ice age — the [[global-warming-pause|global warming pause]] of 1998–2012, and the [[medieval-warm-period|medieval warming period]].[1]

## Planetary comparison

Venus, with an atmosphere of 96.5 percent CO2, averages 467 degrees Celsius (872 Fahrenheit) and is routinely invoked as a warning; yet Mars, whose atmosphere is 95 percent CO2, averages minus 125 degrees Celsius (-195 Fahrenheit), which the article treats as evidence against CO2 as the controlling variable.[1] Ehret closes by urging readers to absolve CO2 of its accused sins and celebrate it as an ally, rather than accept the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] program of [[mass-decarbonization|mass decarbonization]].[1]
