# Great Joe Biden Replacement Theory

The **Great Joe Biden Replacement Theory** is the name given by the *[[daily-mail|Daily Mail]]* to persistent speculation during the 2024 campaign that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would be swapped out as the Democratic Party's nominee before the election, whether voluntarily or under pressure.[1]

## Reported plot

A June 2024 report described a supposed secret Democratic plan under which [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] and [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] would move to topple the aging president, including the anticipated timing of such a move.[1]

## Triggering incidents

Speculation was fueled by repeated public appearances. Obama was seen reaching for Biden's hand and appearing to guide the 81-year-old off the stage at a Los Angeles fundraiser, and days earlier Biden stood motionless and stared blankly for a full minute at a Juneteenth celebration at the [[white-house|White House]] while others sang and danced around him, until Philonise Floyd, brother of George Floyd, noticed and wrapped an arm around him.[1] White House spokesperson [[andrew-bates|Andrew Bates]] denied that Biden froze, calling the viral video a "cheap fake memo" spread by the media.[1]

Polling analyst [[nate-silver|Nate Silver]] wrote on social media that "dropping out would be a big risk. But there's some threshold below which continuing to run is a bigger risk," adding that while he did not know whether that threshold had been reached, "it's more than fair to ask."[1]
