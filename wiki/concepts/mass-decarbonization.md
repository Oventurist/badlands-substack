# Mass Decarbonization

Mass decarbonization is the policy program of eliminating carbon-based energy and industry from the world economy, characterized by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] in his June 5, 2025 Badlands Media essay as an "unscientific policy" that threatens to radically undermine civilization for countless generations.[1] Writing 76 years after the end of the Second World War, Ehret laments that this policy is tolerated despite the lasting contributions of [[vladimir-vernadsky|Vladimir Vernadsky]] to human knowledge.[1]

## Mechanism of consent

Unlike the fascism and imperialism of the past, Ehret argues, today's self-implosion of civilization is occurring by the consent of those intended to perish under a [[great-reset|Great Reset]], secured through collective guilt for the crime of simply being human.[1] He asserts that it has become normal for most children to think of themselves not as members of a species made in the image of a Creator, but as a parasitic race guilty of sinning against nature — a theme he connects to the pagan Gaia earth-mother revival treated in his earlier feature on the [[depopulation-agenda|depopulation agenda]] and its links to the Anglo-Dutch royal families, [[prince-bernhard-of-the-netherlands|Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands]] and [[prince-philip|Prince Philip Mountbatten]].[1]

## Alternative paradigm

Against decarbonization, Ehret calls for re-introducing truth into climate science and for nations to choose an open-system paradigm of life and anti-entropy over a closed-system world of decay and heat death.[1] He identifies this positive paradigm of cooperation, scientific and technological progress and cultural optimism with Russia, China and other nations joining the international New Silk Road — the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]] — and with the industrial and desert-greening programs that he credits for the measured greening of the earth.[1]
