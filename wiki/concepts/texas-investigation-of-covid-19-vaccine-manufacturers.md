# Texas Investigation of COVID-19 Vaccine Manufacturers

The Texas investigation of COVID-19 vaccine manufacturers is an inquiry announced on Monday, May 1, 2023, by Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] into three major pharmaceutical companies — [[pfizer|Pfizer]], [[moderna|Moderna]], and [[johnson-and-johnson|Johnson & Johnson]] — for alleged deceptive practices in representing the safety and efficacy of their vaccines.[1]

## Scope

Paxton said his office would examine whether "profit motive or political pressure" played a role in any "pandemic interventions forced on the public."[1] The stated purpose was to determine whether any company had illegally taken advantage of consumers during the pandemic or compromised people's safety in order to increase profits, and whether public health policy had been developed on the basis of flawed or misleading research.[1]

In the announcement Paxton framed the vaccine makers' knowledge and representations as matters "of profound interest to the public's health and welfare," and said the catastrophic effects of the pandemic and the interventions that followed deserved intense scrutiny, with his office "pursuing any hint of wrongdoing to the fullest."[1]

## Reception

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief that carried the announcement treated it as an early and partial corrective. Contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that Americans had been "asleep at the wheel," letting lawmakers and elected officials repeatedly ignore the public's wishes in violation of their oaths, and had allowed Big Pharma to escape accountability for manipulating studies and concealing dangers.[1] He characterized the Texas action as "a good start" while insisting there was "much, much more to be done."[1]
