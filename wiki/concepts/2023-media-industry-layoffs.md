# 2023 Media Industry Layoffs

In January 2023 a slew of media companies and technology firms began announcing sweeping job cuts as the economy continued to face uncertainty, a wave that [[axios|Axios]] described as looming over the year ahead.[1] For millions of workers across both industries, the layoffs were said to feel like whiplash.[1]

## Scope

The cuts followed a rough winter of reductions at entertainment giants including [[warner-bros-discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]] and [[walt-disney-company|Disney]].[1] Digital media brands such as Morning Brew and [[vice-media|Vice Media]], and newspaper groups such as Gannett, also announced layoffs, and more media companies began the year with similar news.[1] Coverage drew a comparison to the pandemic period, when an unprecedented number of jobs were cut early on in response to what were then framed as temporary advertising headwinds — fears that appeared to be trickling back.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the layoffs as a serendipitous companion to the news that [[meta-platforms|Meta]] would reinstate [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], observing that the same Big Tech world that had acted as "a constant anti-MAGA cudgel" was now reopening its gates to him amid the largest layoffs its companies had seen in over a decade.[1] He predicted a period of massive corporate consolidation and shrinkage across [[facebook|Facebook]], [[instagram|Instagram]], Twitter and the Media Industrial Complex, arguing that it would pave the way for independent journalists and platforms while former gatekeepers were "relegated to the place they've always belonged: the trash heap."[1]
