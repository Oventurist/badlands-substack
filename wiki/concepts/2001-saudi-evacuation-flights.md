# 2001 Saudi Evacuation Flights

The **2001 Saudi evacuation flights** refer to the departures of Saudi nationals — including members of the [[bin-laden-family|Bin Laden family]] — from the United States on and immediately after September 11, 2001, a subject raised in Q Drop #67 and expanded upon in Chapter 8 of the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series.[1]

## The 9/11 departure

Although all flights were grounded nationwide after the attacks, the series states that one flight nonetheless left the country, and that FOIA material obtained by [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] — surfaced by the Substack writer Patriots In Progress — shows the traveler granted leave was a Saudi national.[1] That flight is said to have departed from Atlanta, a detail the author flags as becoming relevant later because an incident at Atlanta Hartsfield-Jackson airport may be tied to it.[1]

## The Bin Laden family departures

In the days after 9/11, dozens of members of the Bin Laden family were allowed to leave the United States, and the series argues that such flights had to have been approved at the highest level of government.[1] The author contrasts this with President [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] publicly denouncing [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] while, in the author's telling, helping shuttle his relatives out the back door.[1]

## Interpretation

The episode is presented as evidence both of the lengths the [[deep-state|Deep State]] will go to and of the complicity of the [[saudi-royal-family|Saudi Royal Family]] in harming the United States.[1] It is discussed alongside the post-9/11 tightening of the [[no-fly-list|no-fly list]] and the use of small private airfields such as the [[maryland-three-airports|Maryland Three]] by well-connected travelers.[1]
