# Hibernation Theory

"Hibernation Theory" is a term used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to describe the establishment media's behavior in the immediate aftermath of the November 2024 US presidential election.[1] Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], he argued that legacy outlets were "attempting to chart a rapid retreat along Overton's Goalpost in a bizarre bicameral attempt to simultaneously shame their audiences while apologizing to them."[1]

## Context

The concept was advanced alongside commentary on [[harry-enten|Harry Enten]]'s [[cnn|CNN]] analysis that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had "broken history" with his demographic gains.[1] Burning Bright held that the most glaring net effect of what he called the "inflection election" was a complete bifurcation between a media-engineered consensus mechanism and the public mandate.[1] He stressed that this observation had nothing to do with the vote totals themselves, which he characterized as still unverifiable within an inherently fraudulent election system, but was instead a reading of the mass psychological response to the story of the election as it was told to the people.[1]

By contrast, he wrote, the response to the 2020 election result had been mass chaos and strife — some of it, in his view, engineered — accompanied by an establishment media victory parade that further alienated an increasingly silent majority.[1] He described the 2024 retreat as being recognized worldwide and as irreversible, "much like Awakening."[1] The framing overlaps with the wiki's treatment of the [[overton-window|Overton window]].[1]
