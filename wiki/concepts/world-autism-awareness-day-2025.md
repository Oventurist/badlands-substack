# World Autism Awareness Day, 2025

World Autism Awareness Day, 2025 was marked by a presidential proclamation issued by [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] through [[white-house|the White House]] on April 2, 2025, recognizing the millions of Americans living with autism spectrum disorder (ASD).[1] The proclamation praised the "proud perseverance" of those overcoming hurdles as emblematic of American strength and grit, and extended appreciation from the President and First Lady to the families, friends, and communities supporting Americans with ASD.[1]

## Prevalence claims

The proclamation stated that autism now affects 1 in 36 children in the United States, calling this a staggering increase from the 1980s, when the disorder was found in only 1 to 4 out of every 10,000 individuals.[1]

## Policy framing

Trump tied the observance to the [[make-america-healthy-again|Make America Healthy Again]] Commission, which he created to investigate and address the root causes of what the proclamation called an escalating health crisis, with a focus on childhood disorders like autism.[1] The administration said it was prioritizing gold-standard research and increasing transparency to gain new insights to aid those with ASD.[1]
