# Trump Passport Gender Marker Supreme Court Petition

In September 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] filed a petition with the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] seeking intervention in litigation over gender identity options on American passports.[1] In a Friday filing, government attorneys asked the justices to block a lower court decision permitting passport applicants to select "male," "female," or an "X" designation regardless of the sex listed on their birth certificate.[1]

The contested policy marked a departure from the traditional reliance on birth-certificate sex markers for official documentation and had generated significant public debate.[1] Reporting noted that the former Biden administration had issued parallel guidance allowing immigrants filing Form N-400 for naturalization to select the "X" designation without supporting documents.[1] That guidance stated: "You do not need to provide supporting documentation to select X as your gender initially or to change your gender selection for Form N-400," adding that "the gender you select does not need to match the gender listed on your other immigration documents or on supporting identity documents, such as your birth certificate, passport, or state identification."[1]
