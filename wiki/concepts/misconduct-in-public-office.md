# Misconduct in Public Office

Misconduct in public office is a common law offence in the United Kingdom that applies to abuse or neglect of power by individuals in government and public service roles. According to the [[crown-prosecution-service|Crown Prosecution Service]], as cited in the Badlands corpus, it carries a maximum sentence of life imprisonment.

## The Prince Andrew arrest (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the former Prince Andrew — referred to in the Brief as "Andrew Mountbatten, formerly known as Prince Andrew" — was arrested on suspicion of misconduct in public office on February 19, 2026, his 66th birthday, by [[thames-valley-police|Thames Valley Police]], who searched his properties in Berkshire and Norfolk and released him under investigation the same evening. The arrest came after years of scrutiny over his ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterizes the charge as a "classic reputation and process crime": one that generates headlines and a sense of progress "without ever requiring the system to confront the prosecutable heart of the Epstein operation," in his reading a deliberately narrow basis for the prosecution of a high-profile but expendable figure.
