# Misconduct in Public Office

Misconduct in public office is a common-law criminal offence in England and Wales covering the abuse or neglect of power by individuals holding government and public service roles.[1] According to the [[crown-prosecution-service|Crown Prosecution Service]], the offence carries a maximum sentence of life imprisonment.[1]

## Application to Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor

The offence entered global headlines in February 2026 when [[thames-valley-police|Thames Valley Police]] arrested [[prince-andrew|Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor]] on suspicion of misconduct in public office, searching properties in Berkshire and Norfolk on the morning of his 66th birthday.[1] He was released under investigation the same evening.[1]

## Critique as a "process crime"

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described charges of this type as "classic reputation and process crimes" that "generate immediate headlines, viral clips and a widespread sense of progress without ever requiring the system to confront the prosecutable heart of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein operation]]," particularly crimes against children.[1] In that framing, the choice of offence is a deliberate feature of the [[deep-state-burn-cards|burn card]] strategy rather than an artifact of evidentiary limits.[1]
