# Miyazaki Airport Bomb Explosion

On Wednesday, October 2, 2024, a buried United States bomb dating from the Second World War exploded at Miyazaki airport in south-western Japan, creating a large crater in a taxiway and causing the cancellation of more than 80 flights, though no injuries were reported.[1]

## Details

Land and transport ministry officials said no aircraft were nearby when the bomb detonated.[1] An investigation by Japan's self-defence forces and police confirmed the explosion was caused by a 500-pound U.S. bomb and that there was no further danger; officials were still determining what caused its sudden detonation.[1]

Video recorded by a nearby aviation school showed the blast spewing pieces of asphalt into the air like a fountain, and footage broadcast on Japanese television showed a crater in the taxiway reported to be about seven metres in diameter and one metre deep.[1] Japan's chief cabinet secretary, Yoshimasa Hayashi, said more than 80 flights had been cancelled at the airport, which hoped to resume operations the following morning.[1]

The incident was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of October 3, 2024.[1]
