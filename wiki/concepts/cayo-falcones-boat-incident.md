# Cayo Falcones Boat Incident

The Cayo Falcones boat incident was a fatal shooting on 25 February 2026 in which four people aboard a Florida-registered speedboat were killed by Cuban border guards in Cuban territorial waters near Cayo Falcones, off Villa Clara province.[1]

## The incident

According to authorities in [[cuba|Cuba]], the vessel entered Cuban waters and border guards opened fire after the boat shot at them first.[1] Cuba's Interior Ministry said ten people were aboard and that six were wounded in addition to the four killed.[1] Cuban officials stated that the group possessed assault rifles, handguns, Molotov cocktails or other incendiary devices, body armor or homemade ballistic vests, telescopic sights, and camouflage gear.[1] They described the occupants as Cuban nationals residing in the United States and characterized the voyage as an attempted infiltration intended to "unleash terrorism" on the island.[1]

## US response

Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] said the United States had no information beyond what Cuban authorities had released, and that agencies including the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] and the [[us-coast-guard|Coast Guard]] were investigating whether any of the occupants were US citizens or lawful permanent residents.[1] US authorities did not independently confirm who fired first, the number of people aboard, the reported six wounded, or the identities of those killed.[1]

Florida Attorney General [[james-uthmeier|James Uthmeier]] ordered state prosecutors to work with federal, state, and local partners on an investigation, saying the Cuban government "cannot be trusted."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] mocked the Cuban government as "half-century-behind island people" while questioning why Uthmeier — an ally of [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] — was making definitive public statements before the facts were established.[1]
