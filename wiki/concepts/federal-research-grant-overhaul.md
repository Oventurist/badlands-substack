# Federal Research Grant Overhaul

The federal research grant overhaul is a set of changes advanced by the Trump administration in July 2026 to the process by which federal agencies award scientific grants.[1]

## Proposed changes

Under the proposal, agencies would be required to place greater emphasis on national priorities, transparency, and practical outcomes when awarding scientific grants.[1] The plan drew criticism from many researchers and university groups, who argued it could reduce funding for basic research and increase political influence over grant decisions.[1]

## Climate office companion measure

Separately but concurrently, the administration established a new [[white-house|White House]] climate office led by climate policy critic [[david-legates|David Legates]], a former [[national-oceanic-and-atmospheric-administration|NOAA]] official who has questioned aspects of mainstream climate modeling.[1] Officials said the office would review federal climate science and policy to ensure analyses are objective and free from political bias, while critics contended the move could reshape how climate research informs federal policymaking and weaken existing climate initiatives.[1]
