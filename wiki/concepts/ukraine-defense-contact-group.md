# Ukraine Defense Contact Group

The Ukraine Defense Contact Group is a US-led multinational body that coordinates the shipment of weapons and military aid to Ukraine.[1] It was launched in the early weeks of the Russia–Ukraine war by Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] and then-Joint Chiefs Chairman Gen. [[mark-milley|Mark Milley]], and is credited with accelerating tens of billions of dollars in equipment, weapons, and other aid that proved critical in staving off Russian forces.[1]

## Proposals to move the group into NATO

In April 2024, Politico reported that the United States and other Western countries were considering transferring the group to [[nato]] control, one of several proposals intended to preserve the arms flow to Kyiv under a possible second [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] presidency — an effort characterised in the coverage as "Trump-proofing" weapons for Ukraine.[1] Officials were expected to discuss a range of options at the NATO foreign ministerial meeting in Brussels, with the aim of finalising any move at the NATO leaders' summit in Washington in July.[1]

A second US official described an alternative proposal that would give NATO a more formalized seat at the table within the Ukraine group rather than placing the group under alliance control, the stated intent being to strengthen NATO–Ukraine ties for long-term support.[1] The deliberations were described by three European officials and a US official with knowledge of the internal discussions.[1]
