# World Press Freedom Day

World Press Freedom Day is an annual observance, marked on May 3, dedicated to journalism and the defense of a free press.[1] In the Badlands corpus it appears chiefly as the occasion for two events on May 3, 2023 that the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated as ironic.[1]

Former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] released a video — recorded earlier for a conference on democracy held by the Columbia Journalism School in late April 2023 — lecturing about "widespread disinformation" and the need for journalists to create "an information environment" supportive of democracy, touting the importance of journalism in the struggle for democracy and ways to defend the truth.[1] [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] responded that Obama, "as the king of misinformation himself," was uniquely unsuited to deliver such a speech.[1]

The same day, Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] appeared at a Washington Post event previewing the 2023 World Press Freedom Index, where he dismissed Russian claims about the [[2023-kremlin-drone-attack|Kremlin drone attack]], saying he would take anything from the Kremlin "with a very large shaker of salt."[1]
