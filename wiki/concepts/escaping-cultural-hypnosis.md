# Escaping Cultural Hypnosis

**Escaping Cultural Hypnosis** is a book recommended by the Badlands Media contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] as further reading on the neuroscience behind hypnosis.[1]

The reference appears in the article's discussion of brain physiology and political disposition. Arguing that the *anterior cingulate cortex* rather than the amygdala governs fear responses to adverse stimuli, the author notes as an aside that the anterior cingulate cortex figures prominently in hypnotic states when measured under various instruments, and directs readers to *Escaping Cultural Hypnosis* for a fuller treatment of that neuroscience.[1]

The citation supports the author's broader thesis that [[leftism]] is sustained by "nonstop hypnotic, abusive messaging" and that leftist psychology is unusually susceptible to programming that he equates with hypnosis.[1]
