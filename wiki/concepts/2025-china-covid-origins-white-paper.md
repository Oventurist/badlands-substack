# 2025 China COVID-19 Origins White Paper

On Wednesday, April 30, 2025, the Chinese government published a white paper accusing the United States of originating COVID-19 and claiming that Washington was attempting to "shift the blame" after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] declared the disease had leaked from a Wuhan laboratory.[1]

## Contents of the paper

Beijing argued that "substantial evidence" suggested COVID might have come from the United States earlier than the outbreak in China, while insisting that the [[lab-leak-theory|lab leak theory]] was "extremely unlikely."[1] Chinese officials wrote that "the US government, instead of facing squarely its failure in response to Covid-19 and reflecting on its shortcomings, has tried to shift the blame and divert people's attention by shamelessly politicizing SARS-CoV-2 origins tracing."[1] The report also claimed that COVID-19 had been circulating in the United States earlier than officially determined.[1]

## Context

The accusations followed the White House's revamping of its COVID-19 information earlier that month to promote the assertion that the virus had leaked from the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary interpreted China's move as the opening of "a narrative deployment" that could incrementally lead back to Ukraine, where — according to [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] — the United States had been funding covert biological research facilities whose materials the [[state-department|State Department]] feared might be seized by Russian forces.[1] The commentator argued that if the story led back to those Ukrainian labs it would implicate the Biden family, citing connections involving [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], [[metabiota|Metabiota]], and [[rosemont-seneca|Rosemont Seneca]].[1]
