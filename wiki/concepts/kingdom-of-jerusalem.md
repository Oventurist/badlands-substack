# Kingdom of Jerusalem

The Kingdom of Jerusalem, which reigned from 1099 to 1291, was frequently managed by the [[knights-templar|Templars]] and ranged widely in size across several bloody Crusades against the Muslims.[1]

## The "Kingdom of Heaven"

The article notes that "Kingdom of Heaven" — the phrase [[leo-amery]] used to describe the British Empire ("something like the Kingdom of Heaven within ourselves") — was the name of the Templar Kingdom of Jerusalem.[1] The Franciscan Minorite Order, established by Francis of Assisi under a papal order in 1209, organized extensively in the Kingdom and selected a peculiar emblem for itself.[1] Godfrey of Bouillon is identified as the first crusading king of Jerusalem, whose flag design closely resembles a symbol [[aleister-crowley]] placed extreme importance on in his Baphomet studies.[1]

## Modern revival

[[cecil-rhodes]]' secret society, established in his last will and testament as "a Church of the British Empire" modeled on the Jesuit Constitution, was explicitly based on the Grail Myths of the Knighthood of the Round Table — myths the article says were designed in the 13th century to promote the Templar-managed Crusades and the reconstruction of the Crusader kingdom of Jerusalem.[1] [[henry-kissinger]]'s Round Table allegiances are read by the article as "an admission of allegiance to a new Templar order."[1]
