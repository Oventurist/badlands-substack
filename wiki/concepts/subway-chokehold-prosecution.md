# New York Subway Chokehold Prosecution

The New York subway chokehold prosecution is the case of a former Marine who restrained a violent man aboard a New York City subway train and subsequently faced criminal charges — an episode [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] reads as a deliberate deterrent message.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

Carlson describes the incident as "the ex-marine who tried to take down the violent street person, with a violent criminal record, who threatened people on a New York subway," and notes that the veteran "now faces possible jail time."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

He draws a general conclusion from the prosecution: "Anyone who gets in the way of them destroying our country will be punished."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) The case is presented as "a clear message: 'don't try to stop the destruction of the country—if you do, you will be punished'," and Carlson again poses his recurring rhetorical challenge — "what other explanation could there possibly be?"[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

The episode appears in the article immediately after the discussion of the [[retail-theft-wave|retail theft wave]] and functions as its enforcement counterpart within the wider [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist takeover]] thesis.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)
