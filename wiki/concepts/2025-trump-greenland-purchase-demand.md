# 2025 Trump Greenland Purchase Demand

The 2025 Trump Greenland purchase demand refers to a telephone call in mid-January 2025 in which then President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] pressed Danish Prime Minister [[mette-frederiksen|Mette Frederiksen]] over his demand that the United States buy [[greenland|Greenland]] from [[denmark|Denmark]].[1]

## The call

According to senior European officials speaking to the Financial Times, Trump spoke with Frederiksen for 45 minutes and was described as aggressive and confrontational about her refusal to sell the territory.[1] Five current and former senior European officials briefed on the call said the conversation "was horrendous."[1] One person said: "He was very firm. It was a cold shower. Before, it was hard to take it seriously. But I do think it is serious and potentially very dangerous."[1] Another said: "The intent was very clear. They want it. The Danes are now in crisis mode," while a third reported that "The Danes are utterly freaked out by this."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that the Greenland deployment was being sold on the back of pragmatism — strategic defense and frictionless trade — but argued it also served as "a cognitive gateway to one of the key revelations of the Golden Age," namely that "We've been lied to about the nature of our world," concluding: "No. Limits."[1]
