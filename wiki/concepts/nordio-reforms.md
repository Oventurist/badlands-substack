# Nordio Reforms

The Nordio Reforms are a package of Italian constitutional measures pushed by the government of Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] that would separate judges and prosecutors into distinct career tracks within the Italian judiciary.[1]

## Content and rationale

Supporters regard the separation of careers as prudent following a number of high-profile scandals that exposed backroom dealing over senior prosecutor appointments, in a judiciary widely described as highly politicized and polarized.[1] The reforms would additionally create new systems of accountability for judges who engage in misconduct.[1]

## Referendum defeat

A referendum on the Nordio Reforms was held in March 2026 and failed.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the defeat as Meloni's first major failure as prime minister, notable because her government otherwise enjoys broad popularity across the Italian political spectrum and was on track to become the longest-serving government in post-WWII Italian history on September 4, 2026.[1]

The episode is cited in Badlands analysis as evidence that Meloni's populist support among the public "has not been reflected in the Italian political system," motivating her parallel push for the [[premierato|Premierato]] reform.[1]
