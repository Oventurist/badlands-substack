# Multipolar Devolution

"Multipolar Devolution" is a term coined by Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as a play on Jon Herold's [[devolution|devolution]] theory, referring to the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]]'s operations against what he calls the Globalist Unipolar Hegemony.[1]

Where devolution describes the contingency-based continuity of a legitimate American government operating outside visible institutions, Multipolar Devolution extends the logic internationally: sovereign states quietly restructuring their relationships and economies in ways that erode the unipolar order, often through arrangements set in motion years earlier.[1] GhostofBasedPatrickHenry offered the [[wonsan-kalma-tourist-zone|Wonsan-Kalma tourist zone]] in [[north-korea|North Korea]] as an illustration, presenting the resort's construction as the delayed fulfillment of promises exchanged between [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at their 2018 summit, when Kim pledged a "Major Change" to his country.[1]

The concept is tied to the recurring Badlands theme that "the War of Sovereignty must be won," and that prosperity — not coercion — is the mechanism by which peace is delivered "not just for the American People, but for all mankind."[1]
