# Young Plan

The Young Plan was the German debt repayment scheme instituted in 1928 by [[owen-young|Owen Young]], a JP Morgan asset who headed [[general-electric|General Electric]].[1] It gave rise to the [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]] and consolidated an international cartel of industrialists and financiers on behalf of the City of London and Wall Street.[1]

## Cartelization

Under the Young Plan, Rockefeller's [[standard-oil|Standard Oil]] created a new international cartel alongside [[ig-farben|IG Farben]] in 1929.[1] The largest of these cartels saw Henry Ford's German operations merging with IG Farben, Dupont industries, Britain's Shell and Standard Oil.[1] The associated 1928 cartel agreement allowed Standard Oil to transfer all patents and technologies for the production of synthetic gasoline from coal to IG Farben, underwriting Germany's wartime fuel supply.[1]

## Legacy

The BIS created under the plan was placed under the effective control of [[montagu-norman|Montagu Norman]] rather than its nominal steward [[hjalmar-schacht|Hjalmar Schacht]], and became, in Ehret's account, the key mechanism by which international financiers funded the Nazi machine.[1]
