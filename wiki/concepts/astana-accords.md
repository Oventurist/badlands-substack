# Astana Accords

The Astana Accords are the diplomatic framework, brokered among [[russia|Russia]], [[turkey|Turkey]] and [[iran|Iran]], through which the post-Assad political settlement in [[syria|Syria]] was arranged.[1]

## Installation of al-Sharaa

Badlands Media treats the Accords as the mechanism by which Syrian President [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed al-Sharaa]], formerly known as al-Jolani, was installed in power — a decision attributed jointly to President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]], President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and the Iranians.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is described as having played a pivotal role in normalising al-Sharaa as a legitimate world leader, stating in numerous [[truth-social|Truth Social]] posts that Erdogan was responsible for toppling [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] and that al-Sharaa "is his guy."[1]

## Significance in Badlands analysis

The Accords supply the causal backbone of the Brief's April 2026 energy analysis. Because al-Sharaa owes his position to the Astana signatories rather than to [[israel|Israel]], his March 31 proposal in Germany to route all Persian Gulf pipelines to Syrian ports — and Iraq's April 2 resumption of pipeline shipments to Syria — are read as coordinated moves by the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] against Israeli ambitions to become the regional gatekeeper of oil.[1] Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]]'s call for al-Sharaa's assassination, following the Syrian president's refusal to comply with Israeli demands on sovereignty and territorial control, is presented as the counter-reaction to that alignment.[1]
