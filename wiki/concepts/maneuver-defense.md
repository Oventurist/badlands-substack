# Maneuver Defense

**Maneuver defense**, also referred to in Badlands Media commentary as "shoulder defense," is a military tactic developed by Russian forces to counter the German blitzkrieg during the Second World War, and used by Badlands writers as an analytical frame for interpreting the fall of Damascus in December 2024.[1]

## The tactic

Blitzkrieg operates by having a large attack element maneuver rapidly into enemy territory, overwhelming defenses with speed and firepower.[1] The Russians learned that blitzkrieg could be beaten by ostensibly retreating from the defense lines, drawing the attacking element deeper into their own territory.[1] Because blitzkrieg was conducted in waves, the attacking force contained a finite number of soldiers and tanks; the defender's aim was to get the entire wave of enemy troops into friendly territory as quickly as possible, allowing the invading group to form a "head."[1] The defender would then close the "shoulders," trapping the mass of enemy troops behind the defensive lines and encircling them — with destruction following if they did not surrender.[1]

According to the Badlands account, shoulder defense became so effective that by the end of 1943 Hitler denied ever using the term "blitzkrieg," dismissing the whole concept as a silly ploy.[1]

## Application to Syria (December 2024)

On the night of December 8, 2024, Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] drew an analogy between maneuver defense and [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]]'s ostensible retreat from [[syria|Syria]], a comparison developed further by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[1] On this reading, Assad and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] must have known that the globalist hegemony was planning a major assault using its collection of proxy forces, and rather than engaging in a conflict that would have cost many Syrian and Russian lives, Assad retreated and allowed the enemy to occupy territory far less advantageous than the occupiers might believe.[1]

The commentary presented this as a "narrative ambush" set over many years by many different players, all acting in their own interest but potentially as part of a grand design to liberate the world from what the writer called a ruthless oligarchy and to restore sovereignty to nation-states — a soap opera branded [[trumpamania|TrumpaMania]] and produced, in the writer's framing, by President [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]].[1] The Badlands newsletter notes it has covered the history of Russian maneuver defense extensively.[1]

## Application to Putin's 2024 NATO remarks

In June 2024 [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] applied the concept to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s dismissal of claims that Russia planned to attack [[nato|NATO]], describing the rhetorical posture as Russian maneuver defense.[2] He recounted the doctrine's origins as the Soviet answer to the German Blitzkrieg: feign retreat, draw advancing tanks deep so that a "head" forms well past the defensive lines, then use flanking positions the Russians called the "shoulders" to envelop the point of penetration — the "neck" — and decapitate the attacking force so it can be encircled and destroyed.[2] The technique was known as "Shoulder Defense," and the commentary claims it was so successful that Hitler denied ever using the term "Blitzkrieg," calling it a silly word.[2] The conclusion drawn was that although the Germans invented maneuver warfare, the Russians became its masters.[2]
