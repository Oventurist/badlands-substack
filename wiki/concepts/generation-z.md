# Generation Z

Generation Z (also "Zennials" in some commentary) is the American generational cohort that became the focus of debate in late 2023 over draft anxiety, military recruitment failures, and political disengagement.[1]

## Draft anxiety and recruitment

Commentary carried by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] described "irony and hypocrisy... dripping across social media" as young adults began to grasp that their support for establishment positions might carry personal consequences.[1] The piece characterized Gen Z as the cohort most inclined to vote for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and most inclined to support war against [[russia|Russia]] in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], including funding hundreds of billions of dollars in American weapons to prolong what it called a losing war.[1] It argued that the same voices which applauded sacrificing Ukrainian draftees as a cheaper alternative to American troops lost their "blood thirst" once personally exposed to the [[military-draft|draft]].[1]

A cited [[pentagon|Pentagon]] study found that up to 77% of Gen Z did not meet preliminary physical and mental qualifications for service or could not pass criminal background checks, contributing to years of poor recruitment; the Department of Defense reportedly built pre-boot "fat camps" to prepare recruits physically for boot camp.[1]

## Alternative assessment

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] rejected the contemptuous tone of that analysis, likening it to Boomer condescension toward Gen X.[1] She described Gen Z as the most educated generation in American history with the greatest access to information, which in her view accelerated their rejection of the establishment rather than reflecting frivolity.[1] She argued that they abstain from voting because they view elections as theater, decline enlistment because they reject the premise of "the banker wars," and ignore the news because they judge it fake — and that although they have been more emotionally manipulated than prior cohorts, they are correspondingly more comfortable resisting emotional tactics.[1] Writing as the parent of three members of the generation, she concluded that these young people "have the potential to be the best of us" if older Americans stop mocking them and help them out of the [[information-war|war of stories]].[1]
