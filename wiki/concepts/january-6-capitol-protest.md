# January 6 Capitol Protest

The events of January 6, 2021 in Washington, D.C. are treated in Badlands Media commentary as a paradigm case of a public event whose appearance was engineered to differ sharply from its substance.[1]

## Badlands account of the day

Writing in November 2023, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] described roughly a million people arriving in Washington to show support for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and to peacefully protest what they believed to be a stolen election.[1] He wrote that it was quickly discovered that anti-Trump activists were present disguised as Trump supporters — including members of [[antifa|Antifa]] and [[black-lives-matter|BLM]] wearing MAGA hats and T-shirts "fresh out of their plastic bags, creases and all" — causing and encouraging violence, vandalism and entry into the Capitol building.[1] He further alleged that the [[fbi|FBI]] had agents and assets doing the same, and that [[us-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] were opening doors and encouraging protesters to come inside.[1]

## Claimed narrative function

Carlson argued that despite the implausibility of the scene, the general public accepted the framing that Trump supporters had attempted to overthrow the government.[1] He asserted that a majority of Americans now regard January 6 as a political stunt by Trump's enemies coordinated by the FBI and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], but that the operation had already served its purpose: it diverted public attention from claims of a stolen election.[1] Like any big lie, he wrote, the January 6 lie eventually unravelled — and a majority of Americans now also believe there was election interference in 2020 — yet the damage was already done.[1] These characterisations reflect the author's position and the [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]] rather than adjudicated findings.[1]

Carlson used the episode as the precedent for his suspicion that other filmed confrontations, such as pro-Hamas and pro-Palestinian street protests, might include paid or planted participants holding inflammatory signs in order to discredit the wider crowd.[1]
