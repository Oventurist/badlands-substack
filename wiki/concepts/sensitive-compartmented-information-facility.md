# Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF)

A sensitive compartmented information facility, or SCIF, is a secured space certified for the handling and discussion of classified national security information. The term became central to the classified-documents prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in August 2023, when Trump's lawyers asked the court to establish a SCIF in which the former president and his legal team could discuss the classified material at issue in the case.[1]

## The 2023 dispute

Prosecutors working for special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] objected on August 14, 2023, in a filing before U.S. District Judge [[aileen-cannon|Aileen Cannon]], arguing that the request sought "special treatment that no other criminal defendant would receive and that is unsupported by law or precedent."[1] They further contended that Trump's motion rested on a "misrepresentation" of the proposed protective order issued the previous week by Judge [[tanya-chutkan|Tanya Chutkan]], which barred Trump and his lawyers from disclosing sensitive materials — recordings, transcripts and interview reports — to anyone outside the defense.[1]

The government's position was narrow but consequential: "The proposed protective order requires that any discussion of classified information between Trump and his attorneys take place in a sensitive compartmented information facility ('SCIF'); it does not require, as Trump maintains, that all discussions take place in the same SCIF where classified discovery will be housed."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that the matter had been examined at length on the [[devolution-power-hour|Devolution Power Hour]], where [[just-human|Just Human]] highlighted the absurdity that Trump might be permitted to discuss his defense inside a SCIF when Smith's case rests on the premise that documents held at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] were improperly handled outside a certified facility.[1] Burning Bright framed the exchange as part of the broader [[lawfare|lawfare]] campaign, in which both sides bait one another: "Smith may be wise to Trump's SCIF bait, but as is usually the way with Trump, once you've recognized one Narrative or legal trap, you've already fallen headfirst into three more."[1]
