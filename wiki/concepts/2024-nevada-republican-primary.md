# 2024 Nevada Republican Primary

The 2024 Nevada Republican presidential primary was a state-run nominating contest held on Tuesday, February 6, 2024. It is chiefly remembered for producing a result in which the only major candidate on the ballot, [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], finished second to the ballot line "None of These Candidates."[1]

## Result

Haley failed to win the primary despite the absence of any other Republican candidate on the ticket, coming in second by roughly 22,000 votes and receiving less than a third of the total.[1] The race was called at 9:00 p.m. Pacific time, with more than 60 percent of participants voting for no one; 2,705 voters cast ballots for [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]], who was no longer a candidate.[1] Badlands commentary put the figures at 26,038 votes for "none of these candidates" against 14,328 for Haley — a margin of about 27 percent of the vote.[1]

## Turnout

Nevada has approximately 1.9 million active voters, of whom 559,743 were registered Republicans according to the Secretary of State's office, yet only 42,702 voters participated in the primary.[1] That amounted to 7.6 percent of registered Republicans and 2.2 percent of total voters, a figure Badlands writers characterized as embarrassing for the state party.[1]

## Relationship to the caucuses

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was not on the primary ballot because Nevada was simultaneously holding a party-run caucus, scheduled for the following day, in which most Republican voters in the state were expected to participate instead.[1] Commentators argued that the primary's outcome, contrasted against caucus turnout, could amount to a rebuke of the nationwide push to abolish caucuses and centralize ballot access away from party members.[1] Colorado was cited as a state to watch on the same question, since it was also holding a caucus after its March 5 primary while facing Initiative 117, a measure seeking to abolish caucuses and establish jungle primaries and [[ranked-choice-voting|ranked choice voting]].[1]
