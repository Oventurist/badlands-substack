# 2024 Trump Mar-a-Lago Bitcoin Mining Meeting

On Tuesday, June 11, 2024, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] met with leading [[bitcoin]] mining executives at his Mar-a-Lago residence, engaging with them on policy issues affecting the industry.[1] The meeting was reported as the latest step in the presumptive Republican nominee's newfound embrace of the cryptocurrency industry and his effort to push it into the 2024 election conversation.[1]

## Participants

The mining industry's "Power 30" was in the room — nine to eleven executives from firms collectively representing about 30% of the total computing power on the Bitcoin network, according to Jayson Browder, a senior vice president at [[marathon-digital|Marathon Digital]], one of the largest publicly traded US mining firms.[1] Executives from Riot Platforms, Terawulf, CleanSpark, Core Scientific, Arkon Energy, Cholla Energy, and Exacore were among those attending, per CNBC.[1] Trump held an Auradine ASIC chip and reacted approvingly — "He was like, 'Made in America.' Great," Browder said — and Browder described the former president as prepared and informed.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that the meeting made substantial headlines in the crypto world and had ruffled establishment feathers, with outlets taking roughly forty-eight hours to attempt to process it and still failing to understand it.[1] He identified patterns in the coverage that cut against both normies who have not engaged with what true decentralization means and Bitcoin maximalists who believe they are the ones leading Trump to Bitcoin.[1] Citing the Axios summary in which Browder said Trump "understood how bitcoin mining would support these things like AI," he argued the episode is encouraging because it shows the public narrative does not move Trump and patriots — they move the public narrative, and observers merely get to front-run it.[1]
