# America's Talent Strategy

America's Talent Strategy: Equipping American Workers for the Golden Age is a joint federal workforce development report published by the [[us-department-of-labor|U.S. Department of Labor]], the [[department-of-commerce|Department of Commerce]], and the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]].[1]

## Content

The report presents a vision and set of concrete actions intended to transform the federal government's approach to workforce development, and serves as the Trump administration's blueprint for preparing the workforce to seize opportunities under [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s America First economic agenda and to power what the administration calls a new Golden Age of economic growth.[1]

The strategy consists of five strategic pillars describing how the administration will empower more Americans to access good-paying jobs, build pipelines of skilled talent for critical industries, prepare the workforce system for an AI-driven economy, and position the United States as the dominant global economic leader.[1]

## Statements

Secretary of Labor Lori Chavez-DeRemer credited Secretaries [[howard-lutnick|Lutnick]] and [[linda-mcmahon|McMahon]] for working with the Labor Department on the strategy, saying it "lays the foundation for the future of our nation."[1] She cited expanding proven workforce models such as Registered Apprenticeships and improving AI readiness, and said the plan would put the American Worker First and prepare the workforce to fill hundreds of thousands of good-paying, in-demand jobs.[1]
