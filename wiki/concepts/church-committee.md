# Church Committee

The Church Committee was the United States Senate investigation into the intelligence community whose recommendations for reform became a live political question in the 1976 presidential transition.[1]

## Influence on the Carter transition

[[walter-mondale|Walter Mondale]], who had been a leading member of the committee, became [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter's]] vice president and had already won a commitment from Carter to try to implement many of the committee's recommendations for reforming the intelligence community.[1] That commitment is offered as the explanation for why Carter declined to keep [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] on as Director of Central Intelligence, ending Bush's tenure after only a year, even though Bush tried to convince Carter to retain him.[1]

In the Badlands Media account by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], the committee's reform agenda represents one of the few moments when elected officials attempted to constrain the [[cia|CIA]] and the [[shadow-government|shadow government]] it was building — and the refusal to retain Bush was "a big blow" that Bush is said not to have expected.[1]
