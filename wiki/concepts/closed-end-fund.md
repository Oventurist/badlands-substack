# Closed-End Fund

A closed-end fund (CEF) is a pooled investment vehicle whose shares trade on an exchange and whose holders, like shareholders of a company, vote annually on its board of directors.[1]

## 2024 BlackRock CEF fight

Ten [[blackrock|BlackRock]] closed-end funds became the target of a 2024 control campaign by [[saba-capital|Saba Capital]], the hedge fund run by [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]].[1] Saba argued that BlackRock had rigged [[shareholder-proxy-voting|shareholder proxy voting]] so that incumbent directors could not be unseated.[1] For BlackRock Capital Allocation Trust, Saba proposed a slate of [[shavar-jeffries|Shavar Jeffries]], [[jennifer-raab|Jennifer Raab]], and [[alexander-vindman|Alexander Vindman]].[1] Badlands Media argued the campaign was not genuine governance reform but an attempt to "churn and burn" the funds at investors' expense — one [[deep-state|Deep State]] faction preying on another.[1]
