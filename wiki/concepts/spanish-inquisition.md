# Spanish Inquisition

The Spanish Inquisition, beginning in 1478, is cited in Badlands Media commentary as a historical instance of a region expelling the entrenched power network that writer Erik Carlson calls the Cabal.[1]

## Badlands framing

Carlson presents the Inquisition as "one such act of removing the Cabal from Spain," and argues that in the historical record that followed, the removers were demonized while the Cabal itself became the perceived victims.[1] He states that after the expulsion the Cabal relocated to Prussia and to the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[1]

This episode is used to support a broader cyclical thesis: that for centuries the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] has moved from one stronghold to another, forced to leave one city only to reestablish itself in another city in another country.[1] In Carlson's account, when a region has had enough, the Cabal simply packs up and relocates, treating reestablishment elsewhere as a routine part of its operating process; eventually every region grows tired of the Cabal and it wears out its welcome.[1] He argues the United States has finally reached that point.[1]
