# Distraction Operations

**Distraction operations** is the term used in Badlands Media commentary for the deliberate manufacture or amplification of sensational stories in order to move public attention away from a consequential development occurring simultaneously. The analytic habit associated with the concept is a reflex question: when it appears that peak craziness has been reached, and when every other post in one's social media feed contains the same specific, usually strange story or narrative, the reader should immediately ask, "what am I being distracted away from?"[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

## Diagnostic signature

The proposed tell is repetition and saturation rather than the content of any single story. A timeline filled with the same narrative over and over — even one that makes the opposing political side look bad — is treated as evidence of orchestration rather than of organic interest.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) A second tell is the emotional register: if the dominant reaction being produced is anger, disgust, or fear, the operator is presumed to want exactly that reaction, since a divided nation is a weak nation and a divided world is a dangerous world, and divided, distracted people are more easily controlled.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

## The outrage-without-action cycle

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] observed that the dissident community is very good at finding things to be outraged over but rarely does anything about it in the end; some have protested school board meetings and others have boycotted with their wallets, but most simply get upset and move on to the next thing.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) The cycle is therefore self-sustaining: each new outrage discharges the energy of the previous one without converting it into durable political pressure.

## Case study: summer 2024

The article catalogues the shocks of mid-2024 — the presidential debate exposing [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s diminished mental state, the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempt on Donald Trump's life]], Biden contracting Covid, Biden stepping down, [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] taking over, and the [[paris-olympics-2024-opening-ceremony|Olympic opening ceremony]] — and argues that all of them were designed to distract and divide.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) Within a single week of the shooting, Sheila Jackson Lee died, Biden stepped down, and [[kimberly-cheatle|Kimberly Cheatle]] testified before [[congress|Congress]] regarding Jackson Lee's oversight of the [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

The dividends of division were itemized: 30% of liberals were said to believe the Trump shooting was staged by Trump; most Americans were said to find Harris neither likeable nor competent; and people around the world — Jews, Christians, Muslims, Hindus, Buddhists, and agnostics alike — were offended by the opening ceremony.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) Fear was added to the mix by uncertainty over a Harris presidency, since Americans already knew how bad things would be under Biden while Harris represented a new unknown, and unknown situations are the scariest.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

## The alleged concealed subject

What the operations were said to conceal was the systematic destruction of the old-guard financial system. While the Olympic controversy dominated coverage, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was speaking at the [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] Conference, stating that he saw Bitcoin as an important part of the American economy going forward and that the US Government should stockpile it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) Carlson linked this to [[tax-free-tips|tax-free tips]] as a subtle signal of a chain running from capped income tax to the abolishment of the [[federal-income-tax|income tax]] and thence to the destruction of the financial system as presently constituted.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)

## Counter-practice

The prescription is not disengagement but recognition. Carlson wrote that he was as offended as anyone when he first saw the opening-ceremony posts, but that by the next day the saturation itself told him he was being manipulated; once he recognizes manipulation he turns off his anger and asks why.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) Readers are told not to support anything they find morally repulsive — they should vote with their dollars, their conscience, and at the ballot box — but to refuse the manipulation and seek out what the anger is concealing.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted) Admitting to having been played, he argued, is itself progress: being upset for a week is a marked improvement over two years of Covid or ten years of the aftermath of 9/11.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-we-being-distracted)
