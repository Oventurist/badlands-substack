# Divine Intervention

**Divine intervention** is the organising claim of the [[the-white-pill-emporium|White Pill Emporium]] series published at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]: that God is actively involved in freeing mankind from what the series calls enslavement to the [[cabal|cabal]], and that the [[q-operation|Q operation]] is one of the more visible symptoms of that involvement.[1]

## The argument from improbability

Part 2 of the series, published 2 November 2024, presents the miraculous facts surrounding the Q operation as reasonable evidence of Divine intervention, while expressly disclaiming any suggestion that Q or [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is a savior.[1] The essay points to Trump's reputation as "Teflon Don" — surviving accusations of collusion with Russia, multiple impeachments and what it calls absurd levels of lawfare clearly meant to destroy him or incapacitate him as a threat to the [[deep-state|Deep State]] — and to the televised assassination attempt in which he dodged a bullet, arguing that he continues to dodge unseen "bullets" every day.[1] The question the essay poses is what tipped the scales when the cabal held all the power in the world.[1]

## Human cooperation

Invoking the maxim that God helps those who help themselves, the author argues that Divine intervention is made possible by the existence of a sufficient number of human beings willing to learn the truth as best they can and do the right thing.[1] He tells readers who have been researching and speaking the truth that they deserve their fair share of credit for placing humanity on what he describes as a very positive timeline, and that their part in the process is of critical importance and witnessed by God.[1]

## Metaphysical mechanism

The series grounds the claim in the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|CTMU]] rather than in scripture. Because human beings are held to be aspects of a divine metalanguage, they are said to possess powers of creation, and the closer one draws to God the greater the flow of communication in both directions and the more one's perspective comes to resemble God's — a perspective in which eternity and now are reconciled, so that the present or past may be altered from the future.[1] The author counters the charge of blasphemy by arguing that the true blasphemy is artificially scrambling the channels of communication otherwise available between God and His creations.[1] From this he speculates that Trump, Q and the [[white-hats|white hats]] can access intelligence across potential timelines, which he connects to Q's maxim [[future-proves-past|future proves past]].[1] He also asserts that the universe is alive and hears those who get close enough to it, treating this as the true source of phenomena usually attributed to the [[law-of-attraction|law of attraction]] and synchronicity.[1]
