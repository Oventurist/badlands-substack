# UN Partition Plan for Palestine

The UN Partition Plan for Palestine of 1947 is the [[united-nations|United Nations]] instrument through which the modern state of Israel was officially created.[1]

## Badlands framing

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that the modern Zionist state of Israel — which he treats as a wholly separate entity from the Bible's House of Israel — owes its legal existence to the 1947 partition, and that this origin explains the enduring institutional entanglement between Israel and the UN system.[1] He notes that President Harry Truman initially opposed the concept, as did the entire American military leadership of the World War II generation, on the grounds that it resembled the same brand of despotic imperialism they had just defeated.[1] In his telling, the politicians in Washington were nonetheless bought and paid for by the Zionist lobby, and as the ostensible "voice of the American People" they held the decisive leverage.[1]

## Continuity argument

The same commentary holds that nothing structural has changed since 1947: [[congress|Congress]] remains, in his framing, owned by the Zionist lobby and prepared to bankrupt the United States to advance a foreign agenda.[1] This historical claim underpins his reading of contemporary events — the [[knesset|Knesset]]'s ban on [[unrwa|UNRWA]], the letter from more than one hundred members of Congress threatening to slash U.S. funding to the UN if Israel's status is downgraded, and the indictments issued against [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] by international courts — as stages in what he predicts will be the unravelling of the UN-centered globalist order.[1] See also [[zionism]].
