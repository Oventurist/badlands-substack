# Kekistani Code Talkers

**"Kekistani Code Talkers"** is the framing concept of an April 2024 Badlands Brief essay by the contributor Patriots in Progress, which asks whether the online research community that has learned to read the symbolic language of [[qanon|Q]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] constitutes a modern analogue of the wartime [[navajo-code-talkers|Navajo Code Talkers]].[1] The piece opens and closes with the greeting "Shadilay!" and is subtitled "Understandably Irregular, Totally Misunderstood ... Entirely Expected."[1]

## Argument

The essay proposes that a cryptographic key unlocks what Q, in drop #269, called the "door of all doors," a door the drop clarifies parenthetically is informational in nature.[1] The information behind that door is said to concern the root cause of, and solution to, present tyranny; the related notion of the "stone" is defined by Q as the capability of projecting power, which the author links to the [[softwar|Softwar]] thesis.[1]

Following the instruction to "follow the money" given in drops #5 and #6, the author argues that an honest inquiry into what money is reveals the central control of currency (fiat) and money (gold) as the root cause of a great deal of evil, an affliction spanning millennia that can only be corrected by eliminating the root cause.[1] [[covfefe|Covfefe]] is then presented as both ciphertext and cryptographic key, unlocking static information for understanding the war and dynamic information for discerning it — the latter role nicknamed the "Rug Sniffer."[1]

## Rhetorical close

The article ends with a set of open questions: what Q meant by "symbolism will be their downfall"; whether cryptographic keys, ciphers, or codes will be the downfall of the adversary; whether the true meaning of Covfefe has genuinely been established; and, if so, whether understanding the language makes its readers Kekistani Code Talkers.[1] The essay carries the standard notice that Badlands Media articles represent the opinions of contributing authors rather than of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] itself.[1]
