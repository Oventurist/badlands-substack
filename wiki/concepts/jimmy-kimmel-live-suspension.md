# Jimmy Kimmel Live! Suspension

The Jimmy Kimmel Live! suspension was [[abc-news|ABC]]'s decision in September 2025 to indefinitely halt production of late-night host [[jimmy-kimmel|Jimmy Kimmel]]'s program following remarks he made about the death of conservative activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1] Kimmel was photographed looking downcast as he exited his Hollywood studio on Wednesday evening, hours after the announcement.[1]

## The monologue

During Monday's monologue, Kimmel implied that Kirk's alleged murderer, 22-year-old [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]], could have been a pro-Trump MAGA Republican.[1] "The MAGA Gang (is) desperately trying to characterize this kid who murdered Charlie Kirk as anything other than one of them and doing everything they can to score political points from it," Kimmel said, adding that "in between the finger-pointing, there was grieving."[1]

## Affiliate revolt

ABC's decision to halt the program came after several affiliate stations controlled by [[nexstar-media-group|Nexstar Media Group]] and [[sinclair-broadcast-group|Sinclair Broadcast Group]] announced they would indefinitely cancel broadcasts of Kimmel's show over his remarks about Kirk's death.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Chris Paul commented tersely on the episode, remarking that it was as if the sequence of events were "all scripted or something."[1]
