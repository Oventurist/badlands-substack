# Shadow War

The **Shadow War** is a term used in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary for the covert, largely unpublicised conflict said to be underway between the sitting American administration and the entrenched interests its writers describe as the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State cabal]].[1] In Badlands usage the Shadow War is treated as the operational or kinetic counterpart to the [[information-war|Information War]], the latter being the contest over public narrative and perception; developments in one arena are read as signals about the state of the other.[1]

## Phase shift of July 2026

Episode 25 of The Blitz, published on 18 July 2026 under the subtitle "It's Treason, Then ...", was devoted in its entirety to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election fraud speech delivered the previous Thursday night, which hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterised as seismic.[1] The two argued that the speech carried significant implications for the Deep State cabal and that it represented the beginnings of a true phase shift in both the Shadow War and the Information War at once — that is, that an event in the open narrative arena also marked a change in the covert conflict.[1]

## The game board in late 2022

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s November 23, 2022 essay "Death Blossom" framed the Shadow War as a "movable, often-obscured game board" on which moves and countermoves are actioned by both the globalist side and the emerging sovereign coalition.[2] He argued that the correct analytical questions are always the same: what moves have appeared on the board of late, who actioned them, and who merely reacted.[2] By that measure he judged [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to have been the active player through November 2022, while the enemy reacted — including, in his reading, by attempting to attribute the [[2022-poland-missile-strike|Poland missile strike]] to [[russia|Russia]].[2]

## The Shadow War in the Unreality Paradox (November 2022)

In "The Unreality Paradox," [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the Shadow War as the theater in which a very real political, administrative, clandestine and sometimes-kinetic conflict plays out in the background while "Normie" Americans passively absorb mind-control-inducing lies from the [[media-industrial-complex|Fake News Media Complex]], calling the term "apt" for that particular theater on the modern game board.[3] He stated that nothing he had seen in the years since first encountering the [[q-drops|Q Drops]] refrain had disabused him of that reading, but that taking it purely literally caused him to miss part of the picture — namely that the fake news is itself part of the real war, the insight he named the [[unreality-paradox|Unreality Paradox]].[3]
