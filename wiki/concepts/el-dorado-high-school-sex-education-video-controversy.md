# El Dorado High School Sex Education Video Controversy

The El Dorado High School sex education video controversy arose in June 2023 when video circulated of an educator at El Dorado High School in Placentia, California giving students explicit classroom instruction about deriving pleasure from anal sex and about where to purchase sex toys that stimulate the prostate gland.[1]

## The video

The footage, posted to the Instagram account "edhscaughtlacking," showed the educator sitting atop a desk in front of a classroom of students, discussing why anal sex is pleasurable for gay men, with an enlarged image of a prostate gland displayed behind her.[1] Reporting on the video noted that the instructor directed students to [[target|Target]] as a place to buy sex toys, and situated the episode alongside other California school controversies over gender and sexuality curricula.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] called the episode "Babylon 2.0," arguing that the classroom is not an appropriate venue for such instruction and that the traditional boundary of parentally consented sex education with an opt-out right had been discarded.[1] He contended that if such material is being presented in high school, comparable content is likely reaching middle and elementary students, and warned that "hyper sexualization" and the normalization of pedophilia in education would provoke a backlash from parents.[1]
