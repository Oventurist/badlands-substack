# Bulldozer Revolution

The Bulldozer Revolution is the name given to the campaign that removed [[slobodan-milosevic|Slobodan Milosevic]] from power in Serbia in September 2000, carried out through the youth movement [[otpor|Otpor!]].[1]

According to the Guardian figure cited by Badlands Media, the U.S. government officially spent $41 million organizing and funding the year-long operation to get rid of Milosevic, beginning in October 1999 — with the comparable Ukrainian figure for the [[orange-revolution|Orange Revolution]] said to be around $14 million.[1] The money flowed through the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] and allied bodies.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] describes the outcome as "a sort of January 6th scenario on steroids — trained operatives leading hundreds of thousands of civilians to overthrow their government."[1] Milosevic was apprehended in 2001 for war crimes and sent to The Hague.[1] The Serbian episode subsequently became the reference model exported to Georgia, Ukraine and beyond through Otpor veterans and [[canvas|CANVAS]].[1]
