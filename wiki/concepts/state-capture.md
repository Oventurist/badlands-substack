# State Capture

State Capture is the analytical frame used by Badlands commentators to describe the usurpation of the United States government by what they characterize as a transnational cartel operating above and through formal constitutional institutions.[1]

## The Badlands framing

Writing in July 2025, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the only story that ultimately matters is "how our government was usurped by a transnational cartel" and how it "fell into State Capture."[1] Under this framing, all other reform projects — including election integrity — are described as meaningless pursuits if the captors are not first exposed and removed, because even nominally free and fair elections cannot produce sound outcomes while intelligence services are conducting psychological warfare operations against the domestic population.[1]

## Illustrative cases

Two July 2025 news items were combined to illustrate the thesis: former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s admission that clemency decisions issued under his name were executed by autopen, and reporting that [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] was willing to testify before [[congress|Congress]] about the Epstein files but had never been asked.[1] The argument holds that a captured deep state can simply "use the Autopen to implement their will" regardless of electoral outcomes, and that disclosure of autopen-executed pardons for deep state figures signals that something significant is imminent.[1]

Related commentary in the same brief connected the frame to questions of constitutional legitimacy raised by sanctuary-city defiance of federal immigration enforcement, noting that if the president has sole discretion to determine whether an invasion exists, questions of whether a government is legitimate and operating with the consent of the governed rest in comparable hands.[1]
