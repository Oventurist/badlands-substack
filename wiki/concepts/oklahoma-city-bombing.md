# Oklahoma City Bombing

The Oklahoma City bombing was the 1995 attack on the Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building in Oklahoma City, carried out using ammonium nitrate, a chemical used both as fertilizer and as an ingredient in explosives.[1] The explosion killed 168 people and injured approximately 850.[1]

## Relevance to the 2023 railcar disappearance

The bombing was invoked repeatedly in coverage of the April 2023 disappearance of roughly 30 tons — about 61,000 pounds — of ammonium nitrate shipped by [[dyno-nobel|Dyno Nobel]] from Cheyenne, Wyoming, to a rail stop in the Mojave Desert in California.[1] Badlands Media commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that ammonium nitrate "was a key ingredient in the 1995 Oklahoma City bombing" while questioning the shipper's theory that the entire load had leaked out of a sealed railcar in transit.[1]
