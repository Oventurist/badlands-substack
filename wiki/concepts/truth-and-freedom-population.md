# Truth and Freedom Population

The **truth and freedom population** is the loose label [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] applies, in "Parenting For Freedom" (May 12, 2023), to the one population of people he says has willingly chosen to go against the grain and embrace what he calls the full, horrible truth of this world.[1]

## Definition and composition

Deschamps is explicit that the group is not coextensive with a political faction: its members are not just conservatives and Trump supporters, although many fall within those camps.[1] They are, in his description, those who dared to seek the truth and attempt to do what is right despite the challenges of life, a rigged system, and a thoroughly controlled population.[1]

## Social cost

Going against the grain takes courage, Deschamps argues, because seeking truth undermines one's relationships with deceived peers — the very people the truth-seeker is usually trying to help — who reject them, often cruelly.[1] He frames the journey in mythic terms: only courage and faith in a good outcome can assist the one who, like the hero, wanders into the wilderness of the unknown, cold and alone, uncertain of the path forward yet still walking.[1] Eventually such a person finds a superior truth and a better way of life and wishes to bring the treasure home, only to be denied again by their fellows, as "the bitter cure is rejected in favor of sweet poison."[1]

## Generational function

Deschamps concludes that not everyone who has walked this path has emerged untouched, but each attempt helps pave the way for others.[1] Children are watching, thinking, and observing; they may not act today or next year, and some may never step into the truth, but for every one who does, the ranks of truth-inspired people increase, making it more likely that others will choose the path of courage in the future.[1]

The concept depends on the article's account of [[courage-and-radical-acceptance|courage and radical acceptance]] and on the function of the [[role-model|role model]].[1]
