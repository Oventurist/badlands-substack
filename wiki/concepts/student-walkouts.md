# Student Walkouts

The 2026 student walkouts were a wave of school walkout protests by American high school and middle school students in opposition to immigration enforcement and deportations, covered in the Badlands corpus in February 2026.

## The February 2026 wave

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that student walkouts in protest of immigration enforcement spread across Pennsylvania, Colorado, Illinois, Maryland, Florida and other states in the preceding weeks. Hundreds walked out in some districts, often leaving campus unauthorized, prompting disciplinary warnings for attendance and policy violations. The students reportedly opposed deportations and feared impacts on immigrant families, though critics of the protests noted professional signage and apparent professional coordination.

In Florida, a student near Palm Beach Lakes High School was struck by a vehicle, suffering non-life-threatening injuries; the driver cooperated with police.

## Commentary in the corpus

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] defended the right to protest — "our right to raise our voices in advocacy of the world we want to build is profoundly protected in this country" — while criticizing the specific cause ("sanctuary for criminal invaders") and assigning blame for the student struck by a car first to the schools and second to the parents, whom she accused of preferring "virtue signaling in the streets" over scholastic excellence.
