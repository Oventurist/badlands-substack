# Cabal

"The cabal" is the article's term for the network of bankers and financiers held to have captured the American monetary and political system, culminating in the events of 1913.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The economy in America during the 1800s is described as a battle between the Founding Fathers' [[hard-money|hard money]] system and the cabal's paper money system.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]] is said to have recognized that if the bankers succeeded in their ultimate plan they would enslave every citizen in the country.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) The cabal eventually got what it wanted in the form of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

Secrecy is described as being at the heart of the cabal's operations, exemplified by the 1910 [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] meeting whose participants travelled under first names or code names.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) The article also suggests the cabal removed opponents, noting the deaths of [[isidor-straus|Isidor Straus]] and others aboard the [[rms-titanic|Titanic]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The term received apparent institutional confirmation in the article's view from the [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]], which in 1913 stated that "a small CABAL of financiers had gained consolidated control of numerous industries through the abuse of the public trust in the United States."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) The cabal is said to have squashed the committee's findings and, controlling the media, kept them from the public — tactics the article claims are still used today.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The article closes by arguing that the cabal subsists on public struggle: it does not want people to earn their own money but to tax it, does not want people to own land or property but to own it itself, and does not want the currency people trade to be backed by anything but its promises.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) It concludes that while the cabal retains some power, it is becoming less relevant as people stop watching mainstream media and develop skills of discernment.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

## The cabal in fifth-generation warfare commentary

A different application of the term appears in [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" (22 January 2024), where the cabal is described as globalist and satanic rather than merely financial, and is identified with the group that the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]] record as calling itself the "6.6.6."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Deschamps argues that the cabal knows it cannot really hide the truth, because truth is coherent and the mind is a natural truth-discovering machine.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) What it can do is either get people not to look — through distraction, time-wasting, video games, media consumption and sports — or, when they do look and take possession of a piece of truth, use the power of culture to make them believe it is not valid.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) He identifies this second mechanism with the technique cults use on their members, as demonstrated in the [[asch-conformity-experiments|Asch Conformity Experiments]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

When [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] was asked to model this adversary within a fictional film script, it renamed the cabal the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]] and mapped its campaign toward a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] across the strategic, operational and tactical layers of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Deschamps characterises the adversary as "an ancient death cult" whose attacks on families and on independent thought are ever-covert and incorporated into daily life.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## The dollar as Keystone

"Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?" characterizes the U.S. dollar as the cabal's Achilles heel and Keystone: set up as the world's reserve currency and tied to oil via Saudi Arabia, it made the dollar dominant in global trade and allowed the cabal to weaponize finance against disobedient countries.[3] Joe Lange argues that [[donald-trump|Trump]] took control of the most dominant nation militarily and financially, and is using the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and the dollar to bankrupt the global debt system, because a system must be collapsed before it can be replaced.[3]

Against the common claim that [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDCs]] are the cabal's instrument of total control, Lange responds that the cabal already controlled everything financially and is now merely reacting: [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and [[blockchain|blockchain]] decentralization, and the [[brics|BRICS]] parallel system, were forced upon it.[3] He identifies the structure being dismantled with "the Rothschilds global debt system" and predicts a decentralized, auditable financial order designed to prevent future cabal capture.[3]

## Cultural provocation as a cabal tool

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] extended the cabal concept from finance to culture, arguing that [[transgender-athletes|transgender participants]] in the [[paris-olympics-2024-opening-ceremony|Paris Olympics opening ceremony]] were being used by the Cabal to upset and distract the world — without understanding that they were being used, believing instead that they were being appreciated — while spectators failed to grasp that they were being purposely manipulated by the show.[4] He described the [[international-olympic-committee|Olympic Committee]] as having been commissioned by its controllers to make people angry.[4] The underlying purpose alleged is the same as in the financial sphere: a divided and distracted population is more easily controlled, and the anger was intended to conceal the dismantling of the old-guard financial system that [[donald-trump|Trump]] signalled through [[tax-free-tips|tax-free tips]] and his [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] Conference remarks.[4]
