# Q Plus Symbols (+, ++, +++)

The plus symbols "+", "++" and "+++" are a shorthand notation appearing in the early Q drops of November 2017 and decoded within the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as designations for three families or power blocs.[1]

## Introduction in the drops

Drop #123, posted November 6, 2017, contains a "+++" alongside Q's assertion that nothing is random and everything has meaning.[1] Chapter 20 of Q for Dummies reads that assertion not as an invitation to decode every message from every figure associated with Q, but as an instruction to pay attention and to be willing to consult many different avenues for information.[1]

Drop #124, posted November 7, 2017, supplied all three markers — "+", "++" and "+++" — without explanation, and their meaning was not understood at the time.[1] The chapter notes that the resolution came later in drop #133, where "+" is given as [[george-soros|Soros]], "++" as [[rothschild-family|Rothschild]], and "+++" as the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]].[1]

## The +++ Q proof

The series treats the "+++" of drop #123 as the basis of a [[q-proofs|Q proof]].[1] On November 6, 2017 at 4:15 p.m., [[donald-trump|President Trump]] tweeted a "+++"; at 5:07 p.m. Q posted drop #123, also containing "+++"; and at 6:03 p.m. Trump tweeted in reference to the [[saudi-purge|Saudi Arabian purge]].[1] Because drop #133 identifies "+++" as the House of Saud, the author concludes that Q Team had made clear that this particular player had been removed from the playing field.[1]

## Deletion and repost

Drop #126, posted November 7, 2017, calls out the deletion of drop #124, in which the plus symbols had appeared.[1] No reason was ever given for the removal of the post from the boards, but Q quickly reposted the message, and the reposted drop was not removed.[1]
