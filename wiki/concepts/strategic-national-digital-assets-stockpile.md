# Strategic National Digital Assets Stockpile

The **strategic national digital assets stockpile** is the US government crypto reserve announced by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in early 2025. As described in Erik Carlson's Badlands Media essay on financial-system replacement, the stockpile includes the cryptocurrencies [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], Ethereum, XRP, Solana and Cardano.[1]

Carlson stresses that the crypto strategic reserve is intended not to replace the US dollar but to strengthen and back it.[1] He explicitly rejects a Bitcoin-only reading of the policy, arguing that "Bitcoin maximalists are doing us a disservice" and that the new financial system has many layers, incorporating multiple cryptocurrencies alongside other hard assets and the dollar itself.[1]

## Replacement rather than destruction

The stockpile is presented as one component of a deliberately gradual transition away from the [[city-of-london|City of London]]-based system. Carlson argues that those calling for the whole financial system to be blown up are unhelpful, since nuking rather than systematically replacing the system piece by piece would produce "an Armageddon-like situation that most wouldn't survive" — effectively, he writes, a depopulation agenda.[1]

He points to macroeconomic outcomes as evidence the transition is being managed well: the United States has so far avoided a massive recession or depression, whereas the 2008 financial crisis peaked at 10% unemployment and the Great Depression of the 1930s left 25% of the population without work, while the current US unemployment rate stands at roughly 4% and inflation has not tipped into hyperinflation.[1]

Related infrastructure changes in the same narrative include the replacement of [[libor|LIBOR]] with [[sofr|SOFR]] under [[john-c-williams|John C. Williams]] at the [[federal-reserve-bank-of-new-york|New York Fed]].[1]
