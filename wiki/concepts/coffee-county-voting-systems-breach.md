# Coffee County Voting Systems Breach

The Coffee County voting systems breach refers to the alleged unauthorized access of election equipment in Coffee County, Georgia, following the 2020 presidential election. It formed one strand of the racketeering indictment brought by [[fani-willis|Fulton County District Attorney Fani Willis]] against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and eighteen co-defendants over alleged efforts to overturn the 2020 election results in the state.[1]

## Role in the prosecution

Atlanta bail bondsman [[scott-hall|Scott Hall]] was charged by Willis with crimes related to his alleged involvement in the breach.[1] Hall became the first of the co-defendants to surrender, turning himself in at the [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] jail on August 22, 2023, after signing a bond agreement with Willis the day before; he was assigned an inmate identification number and released after roughly an hour, in a process expected to set the pattern for the remaining defendants.[1]
