# Government Funded Media Label

The "government funded media" label was an account designation applied by Twitter under [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] to news organizations receiving state financing.[1]

In April 2023 Twitter applied the label to the [[bbc|BBC]], prompting friction that culminated less than a week later in Musk's impromptu interview with BBC reporter [[james-clayton|James Clayton]] at Twitter headquarters.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] noted that Musk had been "slapping Government Funded Media labels on fake news media outlets for weeks" and framed the practice as a counterweight to what he described as years of speech restriction by mainstream and social media in partnership with globalist NGOs.[1]

Deschamps argued that outlets operating under such funding arrangements had developed a habit of recasting benign commentary as [[hate-speech|hate speech]], and that the labeling controversy exposed the absence of any stable definition of the term.[1]
