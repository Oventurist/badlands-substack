# 50501 Protests

The **50501 protests** were a wave of demonstrations held across the United States on Wednesday, February 5, 2025, opposing the early actions of the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], the policy blueprint known as Project 2025, and the role of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in restructuring the federal government.[1]

## Organization

The protests grew out of a movement organized online under the hashtags #buildtheresistance and #50501, the latter standing for "50 protests, 50 states, one day."[1] Websites and social media accounts issued calls to action with messages such as "reject fascism" and "defend our democracy."[1]

## Events

Demonstrators gathered at state capitols in California, Minnesota, Michigan, Texas, Wisconsin, Indiana, and elsewhere.[1] At a protest outside the Statehouse in Columbus, Ohio, participant Margaret Wilmeth said, "I'm appalled by democracy's changes in the last, well, specifically two weeks — but it started a long time ago. So I'm just trying to put a presence into resistance."[1]

The street mobilization coincided with a parallel campaign by congressional Democrats, who staged protests outside the [[usaid|U.S. Agency for International Development]] on Monday, the Treasury Department on Tuesday, and near the Capitol and outside the Labor Department on Wednesday, as they sought tools to check Trump's use of executive power from the congressional minority.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized the mobilization as "one last Color Revolution on American soil, for old time's sake."[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] contrasted the intensity of the foreign-aid protests with the absence of comparable outrage over the slow rollout of [[hurricane-helene|Hurricane Helene]] relief in Western North Carolina.[1]
