# Maduro's Secret Economic Concessions Offer (2025)

In October 2025 it was reported that Venezuela had offered the United States sweeping economic concessions — including a potential agreement to allow American companies to take a major stake in its oil sector — in secret talks that had lasted for months.[1] Washington was said to have rejected the deal, with the political future of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] identified as the key sticking point.[1]

## Context

Over the preceding weeks, Washington had conducted strikes off Venezuela's coast against what it called "drug boats," killing more than two dozen people and expanding the US military presence in the region.[1] US officials accused Maduro of having links to narcotics networks, a charge he denies, while Caracas accused Washington of seeking regime change, which US officials denied.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the report as "soft disclosure" that Maduro had been negotiating with the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] for months, even while Elon Musk and Maduro were publicly trading threats on X about fist fights and singeing mustaches from space.[1] He raised the possibility that the negotiations had in fact succeeded and that the military activity represented [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] helping Maduro strike back at the region's real bad actors — the cartels and the [[color-revolution|color revolution]] military operators allegedly aiding [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] in overthrowing the Venezuelan government.[1]

In support, he pointed to [[operation-gideon|Operation Gideon]], the failed 2020 paramilitary coup attempt for which former Green Berets were arrested in Venezuela, several of whom were released in a 2023 prisoner exchange, and to the October 2025 effort by a federal prosecutor in Florida to jail the operation's leader as a flight risk ahead of a February trial.[1] He predicted that the trial would bring out "juicy details regarding the true nature of these regime-change operations."[1]
