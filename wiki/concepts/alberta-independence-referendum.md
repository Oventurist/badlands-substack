# Alberta Independence Referendum

The Alberta independence referendum is a provincial vote scheduled for October 2026, announced by Premier [[danielle-smith|Danielle Smith]]. According to the Badlands Brief edition "[[badlands-brief-3d4|Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns]]," the referendum asks whether the province of [[alberta|Alberta]] should begin the legal process toward a future binding independence vote — that is, it is a preliminary or enabling ballot rather than a direct vote on secession from [[canada|Canada]].

## Federal opposition

Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] has warned Alberta separatists against repeating what he calls the "dangerous bluff" of [[brexit|Brexit]], invoking his experience as governor of the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]] during the United Kingdom's 2016 vote to leave the [[european-union|European Union]]. Carney argued that Alberta voters should be wary of the claim that separation is merely a negotiating tactic with Ottawa, drawing a direct comparison to Britons who were told they could vote for a "soft" exit and negotiate afterwards, only to face years of political and economic fallout.

## Contested framing

Critics of Carney noted the irony of his intervention, since he was among the most prominent establishment voices warning Britain against Brexit in 2016 and was accused by pro-Brexit conservatives of running "Project Fear." Alberta conservatives have made the parallel argument that federal elites are attempting to frighten voters away from asserting provincial leverage and sovereignty. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] endorsed the separatist effort, describing Carney as "the crown's man" and voicing support for what she called "Canexit," while conceding she is "not optimistic it will happen."
