# Waco Siege

The Waco siege — referred to in the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI" as the WACO massacre — was the 1993 federal operation against the Branch Davidian community led by [[david-koresh|David Koresh]] in Texas.[1] Together with [[ruby-ridge|Ruby Ridge]], it forms the pair of episodes the essay's author cites as his own introduction to the pattern he describes.[1]

## Interpretation in the article

The article does not narrate the siege in detail. It uses Waco as evidence for a general proposition: that when a group forms which is anti-government, for limited government, or simply demands that government be held accountable, the [[cia|CIA]] and [[fbi|FBI]] infiltrate it and try to turn it into a hate group, or at least make it appear to be one.[1] In some cases, the article claims, the agencies do not infiltrate at all but instead create such groups outright, in order to generate the appearance that anti-government people are violent and crazy.[1]

Koresh is listed among those suspected of involvement in the [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]] program, and the article assigns him, with [[timothy-mcveigh|Timothy McVeigh]], the function of making anti-government groups look violent and crazy.[1] This is described as what the agencies do to any group they see as a threat to their power, whether the group is legitimate or a collection of unstable people: they need the public to think anti-government sentiment equals hate groups.[1]

## Relation to January 6

Waco supplies the precedent by which the essay reads [[january-6-capitol-protest|January 6]] as a staged event intended to portray [[donald-trump|Trump]] supporters as violent and unbalanced.[1] The author insists there is nothing new under the sun, and that what has been happening lately has been going on since the FBI and CIA were formed.[1]
