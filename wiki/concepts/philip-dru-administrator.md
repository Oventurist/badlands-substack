# Philip Dru: Administrator

Philip Dru: Administrator is a novel written by [[edward-mandell-house|Edward Mandell House]] several years before the First World War.[1]

Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" states that House is claimed to have given a copy of the work to [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] to read on a trip to Bermuda.[1] In the novel, House describes a clandestine effort in the United States to establish a central bank, a graduated income tax, and control of both political parties.[1] Within two years of publication, the article notes, two of these objectives — if not all three — had already been accomplished, an outcome it treats as evidence of House's role in the creation of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1]

The article also observes that House described himself as a Marxist socialist while his actions reflected the more subversive Fabian socialism.[1]
