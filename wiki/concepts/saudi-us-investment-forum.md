# Saudi-US Investment Forum

The Saudi-US Investment Forum was a business and diplomatic summit held in Riyadh on May 13, 2025, and served as the principal venue for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s appearances during the [[2025-trump-middle-east-tour|2025 Trump Middle East tour]].[1]

## Agreements announced

Speaking at the forum, Trump unveiled a $142 billion defense agreement with Saudi Arabia and a $600 billion Saudi investment package directed at artificial intelligence, energy and infrastructure in the United States.[1] Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] told the same forum that the Kingdom had signed deals with the United States worth more than $300 billion, and said Saudi Arabia was examining $600 billion in investment opportunities that he hoped would eventually rise to $1 trillion.[1]

The crown prince described the United States as among the largest partners of the [[saudi-vision-2030|Saudi Vision 2030]] reform agenda, and said joint investment was one of the most important pillars of the bilateral economic relationship.[1] He noted that the United States is "a major destination for the [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], accounting for approximately 40 percent of the fund's global investments," and stressed that cooperation with Washington extended beyond economics to "establishing peace in the region and the world."[1]

## Political announcements

The forum also hosted Trump's statement of his "fervent hope" that Saudi Arabia would join the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]], and his announcement that the United States would lift sanctions on Syria — see [[syria-sanctions-relief|Syria sanctions relief]].[1]

## November 2025 Washington forum

The economic momentum generated by the November 18, 2025 [[white-house|White House]] meeting between Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] shifted the following day to the US-Saudi Investment Forum at the Kennedy Center in Washington, D.C.[2] Hosted by the Ministry of Investment of Saudi Arabia under the theme "Leadership for Growth: Strengthening the Saudi-US Economic Partnership," the forum brought together senior government officials, investors and CEOs from some of America's most powerful firms, and rapidly translated the government-level agreements announced in the Oval Office and at the previous evening's gala dinner into concrete private-sector deals worth billions.[2]

Trump announced at the forum that $270 billion of Saudi investment had already been successfully negotiated and locked in for the US economy, representing the first portion of the $1 trillion the crown prince had promised to procure.[2] It was also at this forum that Trump said he would begin "working" on the war in [[sudan|Sudan]] at the crown prince's request, and that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] made his claim that humanoid robots would eliminate poverty.[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the same organization had hosted the event where Trump spoke in Riyadh on May 13, 2025, and predicted more deals would be announced in the following weeks.[2]
