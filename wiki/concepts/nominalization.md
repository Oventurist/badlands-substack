# Nominalization

In hypnosis and neuro-linguistic terminology as used by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], a nominalization is a non-tangible noun — a word naming something that cannot be put in a wheelbarrow or examined under a microscope.[1]

## Definition and mechanism

The illustrative example given is "fascism": because no common definition of the term is agreed upon, it functions as an empty cup into which almost any meaning can be poured.[1] While the definition of a tangible noun such as a chair or a rock can in theory be disputed, disputing an intangible noun is far easier, and in the absence of shared meaning the word loses all utility except as a hypnotic trigger causing certain types of people to blindly vilify and assault certain other types of people.[1] The same distortion is said to apply to positive terms — there is no shortage of people who act monstrously toward others and call it "love."[1]

## Role in propaganda

The article poses the question of how to keep two or more groups fighting forever, and answers that one simply keeps them fighting over words they cannot define in the first place, which may also require silencing the truth-tellers in their midst.[1] Nominalizations and hypnotic repetition together are described as two of the main pillars of modern propaganda used by the [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird Media]]: attach maximum emotion to an undefined word and repeat it until the learned response becomes automatic, in the way a person responds instantly to hearing their own name.[1]

The stated countermeasure is definitional discipline — having "a sensible understanding of what words SHOULD mean" — and learning to recognize nominalizations as they are spoken in real time, which the author claims makes a listener relatively invincible to Mockingbird propaganda.[1]
