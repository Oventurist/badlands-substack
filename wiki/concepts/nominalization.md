# Nominalization

In hypnosis and persuasion terminology as used by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], a **nominalization** is a non-tangible noun — a word naming something that cannot be put in a wheelbarrow or examined under a microscope.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) "Fascism" is offered as the canonical example.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Role in propaganda

Because tangible nouns such as a chair or a rock have relatively stable referents, disputes over their meaning are hard to sustain; nominalizations, by contrast, can be contested indefinitely.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author frames the technique as an answer to the question of how to keep two or more groups fighting forever: make sure they fight over words they cannot define, and silence the truth-tellers who might settle the matter.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Absent an agreed definition, such a word loses all utility except as a hypnotic trigger causing certain people to blindly vilify and assault others.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) A nominalization is likened to an empty cup into which almost any meaning can be poured — the author notes that many people act monstrously toward others and call it "love."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Repetition and automaticity

Nominalizations and *hypnotic repetition* are described as the two main pillars of modern propaganda: attach as much emotion as possible to a word for which no common definition exists, then repeat it endlessly until the learned response becomes automatic, however illogical or self-destructive it may be.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author compares this automaticity to the speed with which a person responds to hearing their own name.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Learning to recognize nominalizations in real time is presented as the way to become relatively invincible to all forms of [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] propaganda, and having a sensible understanding of what words *should* mean is described as very important for one's sanity.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)
