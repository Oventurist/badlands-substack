# Reichs Bible

The **Reichs Bible** was a Nazi-era rewriting of Christian scripture produced in 1939 under the direction of the National Socialist regime, described in the third installment of Matthew Ehret's series "Real Patriots Don't Think Like Hitler" as evidence that [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] and the broader [[schutzstaffel|SS]] leadership were pagan occultists who despised Christianity rather than the Christian nationalists some modern conservative and alt-media commentators imagine them to have been.[1]

## Origins

According to the article, the Reichs Bible was the product of a deeper infiltration of German culture by a sub-branch of Theosophical (characterized in the piece as Luciferian) followers of [[helena-blavatsky|Madame Helena Blavatsky]], whose movement had spearheaded the Rosicrucian revival in England and the United States during the nineteenth century.[1] While the esoteric inner circles of the Nazi elite were occupied by Germanic branches of this occult current, an exoteric pseudo-Christian front was promoted for the uninitiated masses in the form of the [[german-christians|German Christians]] movement, which emerged with Hitler's 1933 acquisition of totalitarian power.[1]

## Revised Commandments

In the Nazi rewriting, two of the Ten Commandments — the prohibitions on killing and stealing — were removed, while several new commandments were added, including an injunction to honor the Führer.[1] The revised list, as reproduced in the article, reads: honor God and believe in him wholeheartedly; seek out the peace of God; avoid all hypocrisy; holy is your health and life; holy is your well-being and honor; holy is your truth and fidelity; honor your father and mother, for your children are your aid and your example; keep the blood pure and your marriage holy; maintain and multiply the heritage of your forefathers; be ready to help and forgive; honor your Führer and master; and joyously serve the people with work and sacrifice.[1]

## De-Judaization of scripture

Hitler removed the entire Old Testament from the Reich Bible, severing any connection to Jewish culture, and all New Testament passages referencing the Jewish heritage of Jesus or his apostles were rewritten to recast them as Aryan supermen.[1] Biblical accounts implying Jewish ancestry for Jesus were altered to the point of asserting that Jesus was not the son of God but the product of Mary's midnight tryst with a pure-blooded Roman soldier.[1] The historian Susannah Heschel, in *The Aryan Jesus: Christian Theologians and the Bible in Nazi Germany*, is quoted in the article observing that Nazi theologians invented a narrative in which Galilee was populated by Assyrians, Iranians, or Indians forcibly converted to Judaism, making Jesus "actually a secret Aryan, who was opposed and killed by the Jews."[1]

The article notes that the Reichs Bible has been treated in Luke Harrington's "The Truth About Nazi Germany's Very Different Version Of The Bible," published in Grunge Magazine in 2021.[1]

## Significance

The Reichs Bible is presented as one element of a wider program that also rebranded Christmas as a solstice "Jul Fest," replaced church weddings with SS "marriage consecrations" (Eheweihe), and substituted Christian baptisms with name-giving ceremonies officiated by Nazi officers before altars bearing Hitler's portrait.[1] Taken together with Hitler's private statements that "National Socialism and religion cannot exist together" and his stated intent to "wash off the Christian veneer and bring out a religion peculiar to our race," the rewritten Bible is offered as proof that the regime intended to supplant Christianity entirely rather than defend it.[1]
