# Opium Production in Afghanistan

One of the side effects attributed to [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]] was the transformation of [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] into the world's largest supplier of opium.[1]

At the start of the [[soviet-afghan-war|Soviet-Afghan War]], Afghanistan produced roughly 100 tons of opium annually; by the time Operation Cyclone had concluded, the country was producing upward of 2,000 tons a year, accounting for roughly 80% of the global opium trade.[1] Badlands Media notes that this rise in production coincides — "perhaps uncoincidentally" — with longstanding allegations of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s complicity in the global drug trade.[1]
