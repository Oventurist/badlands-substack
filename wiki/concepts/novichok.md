# Novichok

Novichok is a class of highly toxic nerve agents developed by the Soviet Union, and for decades attributed exclusively to Russia whenever it has been used in attacks.[1]

## Kirillov's findings

Russian Lieutenant General [[igor-kirillov|Igor Kirillov]], the Russian military's top official on weapons of mass destruction until his assassination in Moscow on December 17, 2024, investigated the attribution question.[1] Through his research he concluded that Western nations had developed techniques to synthesize compounds nearly identical to Novichok, which could conceivably be used in false-flag incidents designed to frame Russia.[1] This was one of three major investigative threads associated with Kirillov, alongside claims about bioweapons laboratories in Ukraine and the alleged chemical attacks in Syria attributed to [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

## Cultural reference

Badlands commentary noted that Netflix released a film, *Carry On*, on December 13, 2024, whose plot centers on a TSA agent thwarting a false-flag terrorist attack aboard a commercial airliner using Novichok.[1] In the film Russia is the intended scapegoat, while the unnamed actual culprits are implied to be special interests within the military industrial complex.[1]
