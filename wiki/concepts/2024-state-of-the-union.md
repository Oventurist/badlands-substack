# 2024 State of the Union Address

The 2024 State of the Union address was delivered by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] on the evening of Thursday, March 7, 2024, and became an immediate focus of commentary within the alternative-media "Truth Community" covered by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1] The speech arrived at the opening of the general-election phase of the 2024 presidential contest, days after [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] suspended her campaign and left [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as the presumptive Republican nominee.[1]

## Badlands Media coverage

Badlands Media produced a special live broadcast on Rumble, "Badlands Media Special Coverage – State of the Union 2024," hosted by a panel that included [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], [[the-kate-awakening|The Kate Awakening]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] and [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]].[1] The stream was pitched as a way for viewers to "get through" the address itself, and it paired the speech with live fact-checking drawn from Trump's posts on Truth Social.[1] The Badlands broadcast also carried [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s live response immediately following the address; commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] credited Carlson with going "hard on election fraud," further than she had previously heard him go on the subject, while adding that it "still wasn't far enough."[1]

## Badlands commentary on the speech

Ashe in America's reaction, published in the Badlands News Brief, was sharply hostile.[1] She wrote that the address "made me pretty ragey" and referred to Biden throughout by the derisive acronym "SCROTUS" — the "so called ruler of the United States" — arguing that he "demonized the majority of Americans" while "our nation's most despicable criminals cheered."[1] She highlighted what she characterized as tacit threats directed at the justices of the U.S. Supreme Court, summarizing the moment with the line "SCROTUS threatened SCOTUS at the SOTU," a formulation she noted instantly trended on Twitter.[1]

Other criticisms in the Badlands commentary included Biden's misnaming of murder victim [[laken-riley|Laken Riley]] as "Lincoln Riley"; his argument that easing migrant entry would reduce migrant crime; his invocation of what the commentary called "the myth of the Insurrection"; and claims that he was responsible for policy outcomes credited by the Badlands writers to Trump's agenda and record.[1] The commentary also disputed Biden's economic message, questioning the assertion that Americans could again afford homes at a time when, in the writer's telling, ordinary shoppers struggled to afford milk or meat, and noting that the president simultaneously unveiled "a truckload of new spending priorities."[1] The piece closed with the hope that the 2024 address would be the last State of the Union Biden delivered, and the sign-off "Trump 2024."[1]

## Context

Coverage of the address in the same News Brief was interleaved with Trump's call, posted on Truth Social the previous evening, for debates with Biden "ANYTIME, ANYWHERE, ANYPLACE," which Badlands writers framed as a test of whether Biden could perform without a handler or prepared notes.[1] The Badlands treatment of the speech thus functioned less as a report on policy content than as an argument about presidential capacity and narrative control heading into the November rematch.[1]
