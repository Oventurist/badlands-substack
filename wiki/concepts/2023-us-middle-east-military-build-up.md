# 2023 US Middle East Military Build-Up

The **2023 US Middle East military build-up** was a reinforcement of American naval and air forces in the Middle East during mid-2023, undertaken by the administration of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to deter [[iran|Iran]] from seizing commercial shipping in the Persian Gulf and adjacent waters.[1]

## Red Sea deployment

On Monday, August 7, 2023, upwards of 3,000 US sailors and Marines sailed into the Red Sea after transiting the Suez Canal in a pre-announced deployment, according to a statement by the [[us-navy|US Navy]]'s Fifth Fleet.[1] The reinforcements were carried aboard the warships USS *Bataan* and USS *Carter Hall*, which the statement said provided "greater flexibility and maritime capability" to the Fifth Fleet.[1]

Their arrival followed an American announcement the previous month that it would deploy a destroyer, F-35 and F-16 warplanes, and an Amphibious Readiness Group/Marine Expeditionary Unit to the Middle East, specifically to deter Iran from seizing ships in the Gulf.[1]

## Rationale

The US military said Iran had either seized or attempted to take control of nearly twenty internationally flagged ships in the region over the preceding two years.[1] The Biden [[white-house|White House]] sought additional forces in the region so they would stand ready to engage Iran if required, as [[breitbart|Breitbart News]] had previously reported in connection with Iranian Revolutionary Guard boarding operations in the Strait of Hormuz.[1]
