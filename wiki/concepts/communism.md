# Communism

Communism is the political and economic ideology treated in the Badlands Media corpus as the primary instrument of internal subversion directed against the United States. In the July 2023 essay A Virus, or a Metaphor?, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues that Communism should be understood as something deliberately introduced rather than organically emergent: "It is not organic; it is a contrived plan," unleashed on the United States "to destroy it from within."

## The virus analogy

The essay's central claim is that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] uses the word "virus" as a coded substitute for Communism. On this reading, when Trump described COVID as having been intentionally created in a lab and released on the world, he was simultaneously describing the political narratives and agendas circulating in America as having been "essentially created in a lab and dropped on us with the intention of destroying America and the world." Readers are urged to revisit Trump's speeches and posts replacing "virus" with "Communism."

## Structure of a communist society

The article characterizes Communism as producing a strictly two-tier society: a ruling elite and a working-class poor, with "nothing in the middle." In its formulation, "You are the 'served' or the 'server.' You are the slave or the master." The lockdowns of the COVID 19 pandemic are read through this lens: being classified as a non-essential worker is suggested to be an indicator that the ruling elite "have no place for you in their two-tiered system," with the historical comparison drawn that when the Soviet Union no longer had a place for people, "they faced a firing squad."

## Historical origin and modern application

The corpus traces Communism's political birth to the October 1917 [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik uprising]], in which the far-left Bolsheviks overthrew the Russian monarch, murdered his family and founded the Soviet Union, largely through propaganda and chaos and by fomenting civil war between "whites" and "reds." The essay argues America is being subjected to the same method: division, discouragement and demoralization by narrative. The [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] is presented as the contemporary state vehicle of this infiltration, which the author sees encoded in Trump's phrase "Chyna virus."

## Communism as a tool

The essay's final qualification is that Communism is not itself the ultimate adversary but an instrument: "Communism is just a tool—a tool used by the [[cabal|Cabal]] to control. It's like an axe that is being swung by an axe-murderer. The real enemy is the person swinging the axe." Fighting Communism, in this framing, is a way of keeping the Cabal — described as modern-day Nazis — from wielding the tool.

## Humanism as camouflage

"Anthony Fauci: Soldier for the Communist Cause" argues that "humanists are most often indistinguishable from Communists" and that use of the humanist label is "just word warfare." [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] traces the drift from religious formation to secular humanism through the example of liberation theology in South America, and describes the incoming Trump administration of 2017 as assembling "an invasion force to root out secret adherents to a theology (Communism) masquerading as an ideology (Socialism)."
