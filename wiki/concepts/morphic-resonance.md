# Morphic Resonance

**Morphic resonance** is the term used in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for the mechanism by which one person's inner state is held to influence others.[1]

The essay attributes the concept to [[lynne-mctaggart|Lynne McTaggart]], author of *The Field*, reporting her position that intention and emotion do not remain confined within the brain but radiate into the quantum field and affect other people through morphic resonance.[1]

The idea does specific work in the article's argument about the [[liminality|liminal]] state. Although liminal spaces "often feel lonely," the essay holds that they are "energetically anything but," because a person resting in stillness is "broadcasting coherence" rather than merely waiting.[1] The claim is reinforced with [[konstantin-korotkov|Konstantin Korotkov]]'s [[biofield-imaging|biofield imaging]] findings on coherent heart-centred states, and summarised in the maxim "You are not doing nothing. You are seeding the future."[1] The essay concludes the section by calling the liminal pause "energetic preparation for quantum contribution."[1]

## In "The Shift"

The same concept, there attributed directly to [[rupert-sheldrake|Rupert Sheldrake]], underpins the manifestation argument of the March 22, 2025 feature "The Shift."[2] That essay holds that because thoughts and emotions ripple into a collective field, a person's embodied state of gratitude, purpose or love strengthens a pattern that others unconsciously tune into, so that "the universe does not respond to wishes; it responds to your energetic state."[2]
