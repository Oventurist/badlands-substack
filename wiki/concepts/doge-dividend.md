# DOGE Dividend

The "DOGE dividend" is a proposal, floated by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in February 2025, to return a share of the savings generated by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] directly to American taxpayers in the form of checks.[1]

## The proposal

Trump raised the idea publicly on February 19, 2025 during a Miami Beach speech to a nonprofit funded by Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund — the [[future-investment-initiative|Future Investment Initiative]] Priority Summit.[1] "There's even under consideration a new concept where we give 20% of the DOGE savings to American citizens and 20% goes to paying down debt," he said. "The numbers are incredible, Elon, so many billions — hundreds of billions — and we're thinking about giving 20% back to the American citizens."[1]

The remarks came a day after Musk posted that he would "check with the President," responding to investor James Fishback, who had been advocating the concept.[1] It was unclear precisely how much DOGE had saved to date, though Musk had stated a goal of $1 trillion in annual cost cuts.[1] Press coverage framed the potential refund as putting thousands of dollars in the pockets of Americans struggling under record inflation.[1]

## Related fiscal proposals

The dividend sits alongside other February 2025 fiscal initiatives: Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] said Trump's goal was to abolish the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] in favor of an [[external-revenue-service|External Revenue Service]] funded by tariffs, and asked directly by [[jesse-watters|Jesse Watters]] whether DOGE savings would go back to the American people, Lutnick pivoted to that abolition plan.[1]
