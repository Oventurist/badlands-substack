# Physical Domain

In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] usage, the **physical domain** is the embodied, local, sensory world, contrasted with the **digital domain** in which the majority of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] is said to occur.[1] Depictions of 5GW such as *[[the-citizens-guide-to-fifth-generation-warfare|The Citizen's Guide to 5GW]]* by General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] and [[boone-cutler|Boone Cutler]] hold that most of the conflict takes place digitally, which is why the book's authors advocate the instruction to "dominate the physical domain" as a direct means of cutting through mind control and censorship.[1]

## Rationale

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] grounds the injunction in neurology, citing *[[the-master-and-his-emissary|The Master and His Emissary]]* on the "homeless mind" and the depth of human attachment to place: the evolutionary roots of the integrated emotional system behind social attachment may lie in ancient animal attachments to place, with some animals bonding as much with their nest sites as with their mothers.[1] If human bodies and brains are meant to be integrated with their physical surroundings, he argues, it is worth asking what it means that most daily cognition now occurs at the behest of the digital domain.[1]

Engaging with the world primarily through digital means perpetuates the replacement of genuine experience with symbolic tokens, a substitution Esler sees advancing further with devices such as the [[apple-vision-pro|Apple Vision Pro]].[1] His closing prescription pairs the cultivation of right-hemisphere capacities with domination of the physical domain through embodiment, family, community and local in-person networking.[1]
