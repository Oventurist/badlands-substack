# 2025 Istanbul Russia–Ukraine Talks

The **2025 Istanbul talks** were direct negotiations between Ukraine and Russia planned for Thursday, May 15, 2025, in Istanbul. The prospect of the meeting derailed a European effort to bring the United States on board with new sanctions on Russia in the event that Moscow refused an immediate 30-day ceasefire.[1]

## Background

European officials had detected a greater degree of skepticism toward Russia's intentions from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s team and were conferring with US officials on a sanctions package when President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s offer of direct talks changed the conversation.[1] According to nine European, Ukrainian, and US diplomats and officials speaking anonymously, the surprise meeting threw the sanctions plans into question.[1]

While the talks were seized upon as a possible breakthrough toward ending the war, Russia disclosed only late on Wednesday that it would send a low-level delegation of deputy ministers. After senior US envoys said they would attend, Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] said there were no plans to meet them.[1]
