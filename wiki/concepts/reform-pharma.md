# Reform Pharma

Reform Pharma is an initiative announced by [[childrens-health-defense|Children's Health Defense]] at the organization's second annual conference in November 2023.[1] The endeavor aims to restore the integrity of healthcare by identifying and dismantling the avenues that permit corruption to seize hold of and dominate the healthcare system.[1]

## Objectives

The initiative's stated objectives include the separation of pharma and state; the prohibition of [[gain-of-function-research|gain-of-function research]]; accountability for industry actors; liability for vaccine manufacturers; and a restoration of medical freedom.[1]

## Rationale

Advocates of the initiative point to the scale of pharmaceutical industry penalties and iatrogenic harm as justification. Since 2000 the pharmaceutical sector has been fined more than $100 billion for a variety of criminal offenses including fraud, unsafe product claims, and off-label or unauthorized medical product advertising.[1] Annual harms cited include up to 220,000 deaths on "properly prescribed" medications, approximately 2.7 million hospital admissions, and around 81 million adverse reactions.[1] Since 1988, 26,789 petitions for vaccination-related deaths and injuries have been submitted to the [[national-vaccine-injury-compensation-program|National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program]], only a small portion of which have resulted in compensation, though payouts to affected families exceed $5 billion and continue to grow.[1]

## Reception

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the world has been in need of [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]] reform for decades, and that the volume of fines imposed on major pharmaceutical companies both erodes public trust and demonstrates the need for stricter regulation and oversight.[1] He praised the initiative as a set of actual objectives rather than repeated talking points, while withholding judgment on whether it would produce results.[1]
