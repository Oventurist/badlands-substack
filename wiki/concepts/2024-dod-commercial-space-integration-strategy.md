# 2024 DOD Commercial Space Integration Strategy

The 2024 DOD Commercial Space Integration Strategy is a formal [[pentagon|Pentagon]] strategy document, released in April 2024, that outlines the role companies such as [[spacex|SpaceX]] have in United States national security.[1] Defense One reported that industry's usefulness under the plan will be determined by how well the Department of Defense adapts to it.[1]

## Content

The document outlines 13 key mission areas, including missile warning, electromagnetic warfare, and command and communications, and was described as a first for the Defense Department — a signal of how important space-based technology such as satellite communications has become to the military.[1]

John Plumb, the Pentagon's space policy chief, said in a news briefing that "the degree to which commercial space capabilities and services can benefit U.S. national security will ultimately be measured by how well the department can actually integrate commercial solutions into the way we operate, not just in peacetime, but also in conflict."[1]

Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] wrote in the strategy's prologue that "as the Department continues to prioritize the resilience of our national security space architecture in the face of new threats, we owe it to the joint force and the American people to leverage all tools available," adding that integrating commercial solutions rather than merely augmenting existing government systems would require a shift in departmental approach.[1] Austin called for defense leaders at all levels to remove barriers to integrating space capabilities, including "preconceived notions of how the commercial sector can support national security."[1]
