# Alfred E. Smith Memorial Foundation Dinner

The Alfred E. Smith Memorial Foundation Dinner is an annual white-tie charity banquet held in New York City that, in presidential election years, traditionally hosts both major-party nominees for an evening of self-deprecating humor and bipartisan levity on the campaign trail.[1] The 79th edition of the dinner was held on the evening of Thursday, October 17, 2024, and became a notable campaign moment because only one of the two 2024 nominees attended.[1]

## The 2024 dinner

Former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] delivered the keynote at the 79th dinner, cracking jokes, taking shots at Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] for skipping the event, and vowing to correct what he described as "serious problems" in the United States.[1] Trump and former first lady [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] were welcomed to the event just after 8 p.m., with Trump shaking hands with high-profile guests including Speaker of the House [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]]. Comedian Jim Gaffigan served as emcee.[1]

Trump's material focused heavily on Harris's absence. "If you really wanted Vice President Harris to accept your invitation, I guess you should have told her the funds were going to bail out the looters and rioters in Minneapolis, and she would have been here, guaranteed," he quipped, referencing Harris's promotion of a bail fund during the 2020 riots.[1] At another point he joked, "I must say I was shocked when I heard that Kamala was skipping the Al Smith dinner. I'd really hoped that you would come, because we can't get enough of hearing her beautiful laugh. She laughs like crazy."[1] Harris sent a pre-recorded video message in lieu of attending.[1]

## Commentary and interpretation

Badlands Media commentary treated the dinner as a recurring node in a longer narrative. [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that when Haiti re-entered the news cycle in a "majorly memetic" way, it reminded many observers of an earlier Al Smith Dinner at which Trump delivered what the writer characterized as his most pointed rhetorical deployments against [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the corrupt apparatus she was said to represent.[1] In this framing, Harris's decision to skip the 2024 dinner was purposeful avoidance of "the infamous dinner where Trump set the target firmly on the last almost-female president's back," and evidence that her campaign was unraveling while the [[deep-state|Deep State]] prepared an October surprise.[1]
