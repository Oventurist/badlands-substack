# 2025 Potomac Midair Collision

The 2025 Potomac midair collision was a fatal aviation accident involving a regional passenger jet and a U.S. Army helicopter in the airspace over Washington, D.C., on the evening of January 29, 2025. The collision occurred while the airliner was on approach to Runway 33 at [[ronald-reagan-washington-national-airport|Reagan Washington National Airport]] at around 9 p.m. local time, and rescue efforts were concentrated near the Woodrow Wilson Memorial Bridge on the Potomac River.[1]

## Aircraft involved

The two aircraft were a PSA Airlines Bombardier CRJ700 regional jet, operating as [[american-airlines|American Airlines]] Flight 5342 with 60 passengers and four crew members aboard, and a U.S. Army Sikorsky UH-60 helicopter.[1]

## Investigation

The [[federal-aviation-administration|Federal Aviation Administration]] and the [[national-transportation-safety-board|National Transportation Safety Board]] opened investigations into the crash, according to a statement released by the FAA.[1]

## Commentary and reception

Badlands Media commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] urged readers to observe the "72-hour rule" — the practice of withholding judgment in the immediate aftermath of a major event — noting that video of the collision circulated rapidly and that speculative theories emerged almost immediately, making official sources the safer reference point in the first hours.[1] She emphasized the gravity of a midair collision involving a military aircraft over what she characterized as arguably the most secure airspace in the nation, and possibly the world, calling for temperance in public reaction.[1]
