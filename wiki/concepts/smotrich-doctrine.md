# Smotrich Doctrine

The "Smotrich Doctrine" is the informal name given to a manifesto authored by Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]], formally titled "Israel's Decisive Plan."[1] The document sets out Smotrich's programme for resolving the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israeli–Palestinian conflict]] on maximalist Israeli terms.

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], writing as [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], encouraged readers to read the text directly, characterizing it as reading like "a Jewish version of Shariah Law."[1] The commentary linked the doctrine to remarks by Colonel [[douglas-macgregor|Douglas Macgregor]] in a May 2024 interview with Judge [[andrew-napolitano|Andrew Napolitano]] concerning "Jewish Supremacy" and [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s political circle, arguing that while Macgregor's characterization might not reflect the sentiments of ordinary Israelis, it did accurately reflect the finance minister's published manifesto.[1] The brief further connected the doctrine to what it described as hate speech laws in 38 Republican-controlled American states prohibiting criticism of the Israeli government, and to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s December 2021 remark to Israeli journalist Barak Ravid that roughly 20 percent of the Israeli public were "just bad people."[1]
