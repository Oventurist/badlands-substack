# Operation Gideon's Chariots

Operation Gideon's Chariots is the name given to the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]]'s expanded ground operation in Gaza, which began over the weekend preceding May 21, 2025.[1]

Speaking at his first press conference in five months, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] said the fledgling operation was meant to "complete the war, the work" in the enclave.[1] He told reporters and live television cameras at the Prime Minister's Office in Jerusalem that Israel has a "very organized" plan to achieve its war aims, which he listed as "To defeat [[hamas|Hamas]], which carried out the atrocities of October 7; to bring back all of our hostages; and to ensure that Gaza does not present a threat to Israel."[1] He added that Israeli forces were "landing powerful blows that will get stronger against Hamas strongholds that still exist in Gaza," promising that by the end of the operation "all the territory of Gaza will be under Israeli security control, and Hamas will be totally defeated."[1]

At the same press conference, Netanyahu for the first time named implementation of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s "revolutionary" plan to relocate Gaza's civilians as a condition for ending the conflict.[1]
