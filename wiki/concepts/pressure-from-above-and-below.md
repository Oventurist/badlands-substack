# Pressure From Above and Below

"Pressure from above and pressure from below" is a strategy the article says has been employed by both establishment and anti-establishment forces throughout history to effect societal change.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The "pressure from above" comes from elites who outwardly appear to support and sympathize with the plight of the downtrodden, who supply the "pressure from below."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

Labor unions — like the activist networks discussed in the author's related work — are cast as the pressure from below.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) When a cause is seen as championed by the working class and then taken up by a constellation of individuals among the ruling class, the article argues, the perfect situation has been created to convince the masses that their voices are making a difference, even if that is only true in appearance.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The cooperation of the labor unions and the activist network is described as critical to maintaining this facade.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

[[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s 2008 campaign is given as the exemplary case.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
