# Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind

Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind (CVOW) is an offshore wind project developed by [[dominion-energy|Dominion Energy]] off the coast of Virginia, described as the largest project of its kind in the United States.[1]

## December 2025 halt

On Monday, December 22, 2025, the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration halted CVOW along with four other East Coast projects under construction, in what was characterized as a devastating blow to the American wind industry.[1] Dominion Energy shares fell nearly 4 percent on the news.[1] The other affected projects were Vineyard Wind 1 off Massachusetts, Revolution Wind off Rhode Island, Sunrise Wind off Long Island and New England, and Empire Wind 1 south of Long Island, whose leases were paused.[1] Their developers said the projects together would have supplied enough power for more than two million homes.[1]

Interior Secretary [[doug-burgum|Doug Burgum]] attributed the lease pauses to national security concerns identified by the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], a coastal resident, recalled years of local yard signs demanding that beaches be "saved" from the project and his own 2023 prediction that Trump would cancel it.[1]
