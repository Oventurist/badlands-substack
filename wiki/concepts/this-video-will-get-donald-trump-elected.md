# This Video Will Get Donald Trump Elected

"This Video Will Get Donald Trump Elected" is the title of a widely circulated 2016 campaign video compiling remarks by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], treated in Badlands Media commentary as a foundational statement of the modern American populist movement. The Badlands essay "Revolution Founded at the Crossroads" (October 31, 2024) uses the video as its central political text, describing it as "packed with so much truth and wisdom regarding our current tyranny" and as making "a strong case for peaceful revolution."[1]

## The crossroads framing

The video's most-quoted passages, as reproduced in the essay, define the 2016 election as a civilizational inflection point rather than a routine contest: "Our campaign represents a true existential threat, like they haven't seen before"; "This is not simply another 4-year election"; "This is a crossroads in the history of our civilization that will determine whether or not We the People reclaim control over our government"; "This is a struggle for the survival of our nation, and this will be our last chance to save it"; and "Our great civilization has come upon a moment of reckoning."[1]

The author reads "crossroads" in the dictionary sense of "a crucial point especially where a decision must be made," arguing that Trump is describing a brand-new, binary, history-altering reckoning with extremely high stakes — and that such moments are rare.[1] "Reckoning" is likewise glossed in its accounting sense: "a settling of accounts."[1]

## Description of the tyranny

The essay draws on the video to characterize the incumbent order. Trump's lines include the claim that "the Washington establishment and the financial and media corporations that fund it, exists for only one reason, to protect and enrich itself"; that the political establishment behind "disastrous trade deals, massive illegal immigration and economic and foreign policies" had "brought about the destruction of our factories and our jobs"; and that "it's a global power structure that is responsible for the economic decisions that have robbed our working class, stripped our country of its wealth."[1] The video also alleges that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] "meets in secret with international banks to plot the destruction of US sovereignty in order to enrich these global financial powers, her special interest friends, and her donors."[1] The author summarizes these as "taxation without representation."[1]

## Description of the revolution

The essay treats one line as the video's revolutionary thesis: "Our movement is about replacing a failed and corrupt political establishment, with a new Government controlled by you, the American people."[1] The phrase "new Government" is read as a deliberate allusion to the [[declaration-of-independence|Declaration of Independence]] clause holding that when government becomes destructive of its ends, "it is the Right of the People to alter or to abolish it, and to institute new Government."[1] On that reading, Trump is invoking revolution against tyranny by irregular and unconventional means.[1]

## Call to participation

The closing passages quoted concern personal cost and mass agency: Trump's statement that opponents "would stop at nothing to try to stop me," that "I take all these slings and arrows gladly for you," that "the only thing that can stop this corrupt machine is you," and that he entered politics despite having "built a great company" because "this country has given me so much."[1] The essay pairs the video with [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]]'s "#BTC for the #Trump Train" tweet of March 21, 2016 — posted seven months earlier — as evidence linking the movement to [[bitcoin]].[1]
