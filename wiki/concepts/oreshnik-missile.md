# Oreshnik Missile

The Oreshnik is a Russian hypersonic missile system for which Russia says there is no defense.[1] It entered the December 2024 news cycle through Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]]'s interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], published Thursday, December 5, 2024, after Carlson traveled to Moscow.[1]

Referring to Russia's recent use of the weapon, Lavrov said he hoped Kiev's backers had taken the new system "seriously," framing it explicitly as a signal: "We are sending signals and we hope that the last one, a couple of weeks ago, the signal with the new weapons system called Oreshnik... was taken seriously."[1] The remark accompanied his broader message to Washington and its allies, who "must understand that we would be ready to use any means not to allow them to succeed in what they call strategic defeat of Russia."[1]

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read Lavrov's overall posture as deliberately two-tracked, distinguishing between a hybrid war Russia considers itself to be fighting against the United States and an America for which Russia retains respect, and argued that the escalation on display is narrative rather than kinetic in its ultimate character.[1]
