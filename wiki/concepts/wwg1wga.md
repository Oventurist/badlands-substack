# WWG1WGA

WWG1WGA — an initialism of "Where We Go One, We Go All" — is the signature slogan of the [[q|Q]] movement and a common sign-off in the Badlands Media corpus. It functions as an expression of collective solidarity among anons and supporters of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], asserting that the movement advances or falls together.

## Use in the corpus

The slogan closes the September 2023 essay [[are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in|Are Jack Smith and Fani Willis in on the Plan?]], where it follows the author's suggestion that the prosecutions of Trump by [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] and [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] may be part of a long-planned operation. In that context the phrase reinforces the essay's reading of the film [[white-squall|White Squall]], in which a captain on trial is redeemed by a crew that refuses to abandon him — the crew's collective loyalty being, in the author's interpretation, precisely what WWG1WGA describes.
