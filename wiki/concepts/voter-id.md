# Voter ID

**Voter ID** refers to legal requirements that voters present identification in order to cast a ballot. The absence of such a requirement is one of the [[eight-cardinal-sins|Eight Cardinal Sins]] of election administration identified by analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]].[1]

Keshel included "No Voter ID" among the eight practices he tracks precisely because it is not uniform across the fifty states, making it usable as a comparative measure of a state's exposure to manipulation.[1] Within his [[smart-goal-setting|SMART goal-setting]] framework for election reform, states are ranked by how many of the eight demerits they carry, and reform effort is directed first at the jurisdictions where they are most concentrated.[1]
