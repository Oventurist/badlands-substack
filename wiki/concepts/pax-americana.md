# Pax Americana

Pax Americana, or "American Peace," is the doctrine of global order underwritten by unchallenged United States military supremacy. The Badlands corpus treats it less as a description of a peaceful world than as the rhetorical cover for serial regime change.

## In the corpus

[[assad-day-in-damascus]] describes Pax Americana as "essentially the pretext used by the U.S. to effect regime change across the globe in the name of 'Democracy.'" The concept is bound up with the old American mythos invoked by the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]], whose report [[rebuilding-americas-defenses|Rebuilding America's Defenses]] warned that "even a global Pax Americana will not preserve itself" and therefore required "a secure foundation on unquestioned U.S. military preeminence."

Understanding this doctrine, the article argues, is a prerequisite for correctly interpreting Western media coverage of events such as the fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] — coverage that presents each intervention as a discrete humanitarian episode rather than as an instalment in a continuous imperial project stretching from Vietnam through Iraq, Libya and [[syria]].
