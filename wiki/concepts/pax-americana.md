# Pax Americana

Pax Americana, or "American Peace," is the doctrine of U.S. global primacy that Badlands Media identifies as the organizing pretext for American regime-change operations worldwide.[1]

The concept is central to the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]], whose vision of a "New American Century" encompassed the takeover of countries including Libya, Iraq, Iran, and Syria in order to achieve Pax Americana — described as the pretext used to effect regime change in the name of "Democracy."[1] PNAC's report *Rebuilding America's Defenses* declared that "The American peace has proven itself peaceful, stable, and durable. Yet no moment in international politics can be frozen in time: even a global Pax Americana will not preserve itself," and concluded that preserving it required "unquestioned U.S. military preeminence."[1]

The article also links the doctrine to the maintenance of domestic consent, arguing that the U.S. security state turned the media into a psychological conditioning engine — from the CIA's Mighty Wurlitzer and [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]] onward — especially after the United States emerged as the "police of the world" in service of PNAC's New American Century.[1] In this reading, the December 2024 fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] is another iteration of the same template.[1]

## The January 2026 cascade

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] identified January 2026 as the moment the projected Pax Americana began actualizing, pointing to a simultaneous cascade across the hemisphere and the Arctic: the reported extraction of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] in Caracas, [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s ultimatum to [[cuba|Cuba]], and European scrambling over [[greenland|Greenland]].[2] He described [[greenland|Greenland]] as a strategic linchpin and potential flashpoint in the new order — "as logical as it is shocking to the calcified western psyche, long rooted in its post-war paradigm."[2]

In his reading, the entire realignment was catalyzed by a single narrative whose factual particulars could be debated indefinitely, and its universal translation was that institutions built to constrain American power now respond with instinctive deference to the power that underwrote their existence.[2]
