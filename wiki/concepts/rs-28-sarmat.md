# RS-28 Sarmat

The RS-28 Sarmat, known in Western reporting as "Satan II," is a Russian heavy intercontinental ballistic missile described as the world's longest-range ICBM.[1]

## Characteristics

The missile weighs 208 tons and is roughly the size of a fourteen-storey tower block.[1] It is reported to travel at 15,880 mph and to be capable of striking a target 11,200 miles away, with claims that it could reach the United Kingdom in as little as three minutes.[1] It is said to carry ten or more nuclear warheads — enough, according to the reporting, to wipe out territories the size of England or France in a single strike.[1] Russian claims have also described a 16,000 mph hypersonic variant capable of destroying the [[united-kingdom|UK]].[1]

## Deployment announcement (June 2023)

On June 21, 2023, [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] warned that the Sarmat would be placed on combat duty "in the near future," speaking to military graduates in the Grand Kremlin Palace.[1] Putin framed the deployment as a threat to the West, describing the rocket as "unstoppable," though the program had previously suffered embarrassing development delays.[1]

## Truth Community assessment

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the announcement as a return to the Cold War, noting Putin was posturing readiness for nuclear war and claiming missiles capable of reaching the US homeland.[1] She observed that [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and its Western backers had been escalating tensions with [[russia|Russia]] for months, making Putin's moves unsurprising, while cautioning that "nothing is as it seems" and that the announcement — true or not — hands warmongers talking points for the next multibillion-dollar aid package.[1]
