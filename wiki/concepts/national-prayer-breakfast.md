# National Prayer Breakfast

The National Prayer Breakfast is an annual religious gathering held in Washington, D.C. and traditionally attended by the President of the United States and members of Congress.[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] spoke at a pair of events surrounding the National Prayer Breakfast on Thursday, February 6, 2025.[1] At those events he announced the creation of a [[task-force-to-eradicate-anti-christian-bias|task force to root out anti-Christian bias]] led by Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], and hours later signed an executive order formally directing the task force's work.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the appearance as an illustration that "President Trump never takes a break."[1]
