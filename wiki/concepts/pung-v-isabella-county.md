# Pung v. Isabella County

Pung v. Isabella County is a property-rights case before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] arising from a Michigan family's decades-long fight over a property seizure.[1] The case represents the latest high-stakes challenge to how counties nationally handle property tax foreclosures.[1]

The Pung family filed their opening brief in December 2025, represented by attorney [[phil-ellison|Phil Ellison]] and the [[pacific-legal-foundation|Pacific Legal Foundation]].[1] The case asks the Court to decide whether local governments must compensate homeowners based on fair market value; the plaintiffs also argue that government should not be allowed to seize properties worth far more than is needed to satisfy a tax debt.[1] A decision is expected in 2026.[1] PLF Senior Attorney [[christina-martin|Christina Martin]] discussed the case in an exclusive interview with The Center Square.[1]
