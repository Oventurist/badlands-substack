# 2025 Polish Presidential Election

The **2025 Polish presidential election** produced the victory of rightwing nationalist [[karol-nawrocki|Karol Nawrocki]], an outcome that shocked Brussels and placed billions of euros in [[european-union|EU]] funds earmarked for Warsaw, as well as the bloc's trade ambitions, at risk.[1]

## Outcome and reaction

According to the Financial Times, [[poland|Poland]]'s "lurch to the right" imperiled the EU's trade agenda and cast doubt on funds designated for Warsaw, while boosting a growing Eurosceptic trend across central Europe.[1] EU officials feared the result would fatally wound the pro-EU government of prime minister [[donald-tusk|Donald Tusk]], stymie the reforms required to unlock EU funding, and prompt Warsaw to help block a landmark trade deal with the South American Mercosur countries.[1]

Nawrocki, a former football hooligan with no prior political experience, campaigned under the slogan "Poland First" and criticized EU policy on climate change, Ukraine and social issues.[1] "Let's help others, but let's take care of our own citizens first," he said on the campaign trail.[1] He was backed by US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Chris Paul framed the result as another failure by what he calls the Regime to fully capture Poland's government, and questioned why the EU should be permitted to cancel the transfer of money that had already been appropriated.[1]
