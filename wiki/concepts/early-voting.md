# Early Voting

**Early voting** is the practice of allowing ballots to be cast in the days or weeks preceding Election Day. Election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] treats the extension of the voting window as a structural enabler of election manipulation rather than a neutral convenience.[1]

In his February 13, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay, Keshel described time as "the key ingredient" in his model of manual election corruption: more early voting means more time to get ballots in.[1] He characterized the practice in blunt terms as "God-forsaken," arguing that its purpose is to give harvesters enough time to collect and turn in all assigned ballots in a given precinct.[1] It therefore interlocks with the other elements of his sequence — expanded [[mail-in-voting|mail-in balloting]], [[ballot-harvesting|ballot harvesting]] and corrupted [[voter-registration|voter registration]].[1]

Early voting also functions as a hard deadline in his reform timeline. Counting roughly 300 days of committee work, executive action, litigation and Supreme Court review from February 2025 produced a date of December 10, 2025 — less than eleven months from Election Day 2026 and less than ten months from the start of early voting for the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]].[1]
