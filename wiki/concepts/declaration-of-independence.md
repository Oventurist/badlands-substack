# Declaration of Independence

The Declaration of Independence is the 1776 founding document of the United States. In the Badlands Media article "A Startling Contradiction at the Heart of 'Our' Legal System" (May 16, 2023) it serves as the article's touchstone for both American values and the ultimate source of governmental authority.

## Role in the article

Early in the piece, [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] anticipates the question of what "true American values" are and answers that the preamble to the Declaration of Independence "spells it out quite clearly," offering no further gloss and treating the text as self-explanatory.

The Declaration returns in the article's discussion of the chain of authority. Arguing that there must be a hierarchy of legitimacy above any corporation or agency, the author states that according to the Declaration of Independence the authority of the government of America ultimately comes from God, and refers readers to a companion Badlands feature, "God and the Declaration of Independence," for further treatment of that theme. On this basis the article holds that both the federal United States and the corporate United States — see [[united-states-federal-corporation|United States as a Federal Corporation]] — derive whatever authority they possess from the people and from God, and that removing "the foundation of law—being God and the people—" would leave nothing else standing.

The Declaration's principle of popular sovereignty is presented as consonant with the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s language in [[chisholm-v-georgia|*Chisholm v. Georgia*]] concerning "the voluntary and deliberate choice of the people," and underwrites the article's call for citizens to step into their "true position as the de jure civilian authority."
