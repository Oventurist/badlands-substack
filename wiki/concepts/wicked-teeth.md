# Wicked Teeth: The Secret History of Werewolves

***Wicked Teeth: The Secret History of Werewolves*** is a book cited in part five of [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series as one of the few detailed sources available on the [[koryos|Koryos]].[1]

The reference appears where the article summarises the series' background premise for newcomers: that extensive research into history, language, occultism and psychology has revealed the existence of an ancient cult originating in the region known today as Ukraine, which the author believes remains in operation and lies behind the phenomenon many call the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1] The Koryos are identified as almost certainly the original source of werewolf symbolism and as having retained some degree of global influence over mankind for many thousands of years.[1]

Although the article states that the Koryos will not be discussed at length in that particular installment, it tells readers that knowing of their existence is important for full context, and points them to *Wicked Teeth* as the detailed source on the subject.[1]
