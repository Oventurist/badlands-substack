# 2024 Solingen Knife Attack

The 2024 Solingen knife attack was a stabbing rampage in the western German city of Solingen late on Friday, August 23, 2024, that left three people dead and four seriously wounded at a city festival.[1] The [[islamic-state|Islamic State]] group claimed responsibility for the attack.[1]

## Suspect and charges

German prosecutors named the suspect as Issa Al H., a Syrian accused of being "a member of a terrorist organisation abroad," with prosecutors saying he shared the ideology of Islamic State.[1] He was charged with three counts of murder, attempted murder, and dangerous bodily harm in eight cases.[1] Authorities said he had been arrested before and had previously applied for asylum in Germany.[1]

## Manhunt and arrest

Police arrested the suspected attacker early on Sunday as the manhunt threatened to enter a second day; the 26-year-old man turned himself in to the authorities and said he was responsible for the stabbings.[1] Herbert Reul, interior minister of North Rhine-Westphalia, said: "The person we have been searching for all day has been detained a short while ago."[1]
