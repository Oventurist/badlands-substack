# Presidential Policy Directive 40

Presidential Policy Directive 40 (PPD-40) is the framework governing [[continuity-of-government|continuity of government]], providing for the maintenance of constitutional government during a national emergency.[1]

## Role in the sovereign reset thesis

Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* groups PPD-40 with [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]] and [[executive-order-13848|Executive Order 13848]] as the legal architecture activated and extended following the 2020 election and the rollout of COVID-19 emergency powers.[1] The article contends that these instruments do not merely freeze assets but "unlock operational authority to restructure governance under military continuity protocols if national security is compromised."[1]

The compromise alleged in the article consists of foreign-backed disinformation, election interference achieved through institutional capture, and coercive public-private censorship, with [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] described as a central rather than marginal participant.[1]
