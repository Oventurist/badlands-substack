# Election Reform Committee

An **Election Reform Committee** is a proposed executive-branch body that election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] has urged President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to establish in order to organize federal action on election integrity.[1]

As of the publication of Keshel's February 13, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] guest essay, no such committee had been announced.[1] Keshel described its function as isolating the "vast sea of electoral problems" into manageable chunks — of the kind catalogued in his [[eight-cardinal-sins|Eight Cardinal Sins]] — and concentrating all available and legal executive powers on solving them.[1]

## Projected timeline

Keshel used the hypothetical committee as the starting point of a [[smart-goal-setting|time-bound]] schedule: a 90-day committee cycle to present solutions to the President; roughly 21 days for Trump to review them, seek legal counsel and issue executive actions; approximately 90 days of restraining orders, judge shopping, stays, injunctions and hearings; and a further 90 days for the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] to take up an appeal from either party and rule.[1]

That sequence totals close to 300 days — by his reckoning an estimate on the conservative side — before the actual swap of bad systems for good could even begin, itself a further Herculean task.[1] Counting 300 days from the article's publication produced a date of December 10, 2025: less than eleven months before Election Day 2026 and less than ten months before the start of [[early-voting|early voting]].[1]
