# DOJ Strike Force on ODNI Disclosures

The **DOJ Strike Force on ODNI Disclosures** is an investigative unit announced by the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] on July 23, 2025, tasked with assessing evidence publicized by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and determining what legal steps might follow from her disclosures.[1] The Department framed the move as a response to alleged weaponization of the intelligence community, stating that it takes such allegations "with the utmost seriousness."[1]

## Announcement

Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pamela Bondi]] announced the Strike Force's formation with a statement praising Gabbard's cooperation: "The Department of Justice is proud to work with my friend Director Gabbard and we are grateful for her partnership in delivering accountability for the American people. We will investigate these troubling disclosures fully and leave no stone unturned to deliver justice."[1]

## Strike force model

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] emphasized the significance of the "strike force" designation itself, noting that the model was originally created "to pursue illegal racketeering by organized crime syndicates" and that a strike force "emphasizes the pooling of criminal intelligence data among investigative agencies."[1] The implication drawn was that the DOJ was treating the conduct described in Gabbard's disclosures as organized, multi-agency criminal activity rather than isolated misconduct.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Jon Herold argued in connection with the announcement that "there is substantially more evidence that Obama's intelligence community has worked to influence United States elections than any other foreign country ever has," positioning the Strike Force as a vehicle for a broader reckoning over the origins of the Russia collusion investigation.[1]
