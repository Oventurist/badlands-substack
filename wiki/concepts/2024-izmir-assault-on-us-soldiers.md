# 2024 Izmir Assault on US Soldiers

The 2024 Izmir assault was an incident on Monday, 2 September 2024 in which a mob of Turkish men attacked American service members walking the streets of Izmir, in western Turkey, while on liberty from the [[uss-wasp|USS Wasp]], which had made a port call there.[1]

## Incident

Social media footage circulated showing a group of men surrounding one US soldier and violently constraining him, then pulling a white bag over his head in what observers described as an attempt to humiliate and possibly abduct him; a short clip showed other American troops intervening to shove the attackers away.[1] Reuters confirmed the incident, reporting from the US Embassy in Turkey and the local governor's office that a nationalist Turkish youth group had assaulted two US soldiers and that fifteen assailants were detained.[1] Five additional US soldiers came to their fellow servicemember's aid before local police intervened; the embassy confirmed the Americans were safe and reported no injuries.[1]

## Aftermath: Izmir port protests

Following the assault, Turkish citizens in Izmir began protesting against the American military and vowed to continue until the US Navy amphibious assault ship [[uss-wasp|USS Wasp]] departed Izmir's port, according to the Lebanese outlet Almayadeen as relayed by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[2] "Protesters have issued a firm warning to the Izmir Governorship, stating they will remain at the port until the ship departs," Almayadeen wrote, adding that they also condemned the United States for its role in causing suffering and violence in Iraq, Syria and the broader West Asia region.[2] The USS Wasp was in regional waters as part of efforts to "deter potential threats to Israel" amid the Gaza war and the looming potential for escalation with Hezbollah and Iran.[2]

Anti-Israel sentiment in Turkey had exploded in the ten months since the Gaza war began, with outrage directed at Washington as Tel Aviv's biggest military backer, and American personnel targeted despite Turkey's NATO membership and hosting of US bases.[2] The incident itself involved a mob from a hardline nationalist political party throwing a bag over the head of one soldier, whom several men briefly detained while shouting "Yankee go home!" before local police intervened and the troops escaped to safety.[2] Protesters' grievances reached back to the US invasion of Iraq and charged Washington with being an imperial power that frequently attacks the people of the Middle East; the US military's support for the Syrian Kurds, hated by Turkey, was a further irritant.[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] again read the coverage as a "Turkey Bad" information deployment, calling the assault staged and ultimately harmless and arguing that it had precisely nothing to do with President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Tayyip Erdoğan]]'s petition to join the expanding [[brics|BRICS]] alliance while remaining inside the [[nato|NATO]] bloc.[2] He noted it would not be bad for the proxy and sanction war efforts of the Western hegemon if a major cultural and economic centre straddling the Middle East and Asia appeared too reasonable and conciliatory to the Collective Mind.[2]
