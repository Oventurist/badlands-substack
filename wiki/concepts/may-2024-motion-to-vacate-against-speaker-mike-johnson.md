# May 2024 Motion to Vacate Against Speaker Mike Johnson

The May 2024 motion to vacate was an attempt by Representative [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]] (R-Ga.) to strip [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] (R-La.) of the Speakership of the [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]], which failed decisively when Democrats joined Republicans to shelve it.[1]

## The vote

On 8 May 2024 the House overwhelmingly voted to block the measure.[1] Greene forced the vote after meeting with the Speaker twice that week to discuss her grievances and demands.[1] House Majority Whip [[steve-scalise|Steve Scalise]] (R-La.) then offered a motion to table Greene's resolution, and Democrats joined Republicans to approve the shelving by 359 to 43, with 11 Republicans voting to move forward with the ouster attempt.[1]

Speaking on the House floor during what was intended to be the final vote of the week, Greene delivered a litany of complaints against Johnson and was met with loud booing from members present when she brought the resolution forward.[1] She was accompanied by Representative [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] (R-Ky.), one of only two Republicans who had openly expressed support for the measure.[1]

## Democratic protection of the Speaker

House Democratic leaders had pledged in advance to help protect Johnson in the event of an ouster vote, citing his role in passing $95 billion in foreign aid for [[ukraine|Ukraine]], [[israel|Israel]] and the Indo-Pacific.[1] Johnson had previously denounced Greene's effort as a "dangerous gambit."[1] The episode was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and carried as a bonus item in the Badlands Media news brief for that day.[1]
