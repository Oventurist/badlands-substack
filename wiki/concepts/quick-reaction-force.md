# Quick Reaction Force

A "quick reaction force" (QRF) is a military unit held at high readiness for immediate deployment on short notice. In the American domestic context of 2025, the term came to describe specialized [[national-guard|National Guard]] units ordered by the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] to respond to riots and civil unrest inside the United States.[1]

## The October 2025 directive

Internal War Department memos reported at the end of October 2025 showed that the Pentagon had directed every state and territory National Guard to establish quick reaction forces by January, trained and equipped for domestic civil disturbance response.[1] Each unit could include up to 500 soldiers prepared for rapid deployment, with personnel receiving nonlethal training in crowd control, handling detainees, and the use of batons, stun guns, body shields, and other equipment.[1]

The [[national-guard-bureau|National Guard Bureau]] was tasked with training and equipping the forces for "civil disturbance operations," supplying each unit with 100 sets of crowd control gear — face and body shields, expandable riot batons, and shin guards — for crowd control and checkpoint operations.[1]

Readiness standards required units in all 50 states, plus Guam, the Virgin Islands, and Puerto Rico, to field 25% of personnel within hours, 50% within 12 hours, and 100% within 24 hours.[1] Washington, D.C., separately maintained a specialized military police battalion for civil unrest response.[1] The initiative followed an August 2025 directive from [[donald-trump|President Trump]] to War Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], with memos providing guidance for implementation on an expedited, large-scale timeline.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the directive as the culmination of a sequence in which Trump targeted NGO funding networks in the summer of 2025, the [[no-kings-protests|No Kings]] mobilizations tested and mapped readiness, and Trump then ordered quick reaction forces for 2026.[1]
