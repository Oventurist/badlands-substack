# 1619 Project

The 1619 Project is the journalistic project authored by [[nikole-hannah-jones|Nikole Hannah-Jones]], a staff writer at the [[new-york-times|New York Times]].[1]

The Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1" mentions the project only in identifying Hannah-Jones, whom it criticizes for asserting that "equality in Cuba is because of socialism."[1] The essay groups her with [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] as American commentators it accuses of praising the Cuban government while overlooking its repression of the [[2021-cuban-protests|2021 protests]].[1]
