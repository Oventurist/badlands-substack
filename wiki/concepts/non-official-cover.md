# Non-Official Cover

A "non-official cover" officer, or NOC, is a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operative who works under a private commercial identity rather than diplomatic status. In "A Family Affair," the international divisions of banks and investment firms are described as "the usual branch where CIA NOCs were placed."[1]

[[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] is presented as an optimal cover for such activity, and [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] as its most consequential NOC: hired into the international division at his father's request through [[james-baker|James Baker]], trained in overseas banking by CIA operative Robert Gambino, and posted to Caracas where he reported to his "official cover" counterpart attached to the U.S. embassy as a State Department diplomat.[1] The contrast between the two cover types — commercial versus diplomatic — structures the article's description of how the network operated abroad.[1]
