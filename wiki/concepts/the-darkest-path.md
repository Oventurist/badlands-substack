# The Darkest Path

"The Darkest Path" is a term used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to refer to the crimes against children narratives — and the underlying actuals — that many in the [[truth-community|truth community]] have investigated since the advent of the [[q-operation|Q Op]] in 2017.[1]

## Usage

Burning Bright treats the Darkest Path as one of the most persistent macro narratives in the [[collective-mind|collective mind]], with the trifecta of the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] arrest, Epstein's death in custody, and the [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] arrest sitting at the top of the heap in terms of public attention.[1] Beneath those events, he argues, smaller narratives crop up periodically and refuse to let the subject die — a dynamic he describes using the [[shark-fin-template|shark fin template]].[1]

Examples cited in November 2023 included the summer box-office dominance of the film *Sound of Freedom*, the continued appearance of Epstein's name in major legal cases against central bankers, and ongoing trafficking ring busts around the nation such as the [[massachusetts-high-end-brothel-network-case|Massachusetts high-end brothel network case]].[1] Burning Bright wrote that while he believes major disclosures and exposures regarding [[child-trafficking|crimes against children]] still await the collective mind, there are already a great many wins to track for those paying attention.[1]
