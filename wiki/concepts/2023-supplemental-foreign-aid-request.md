# 2023 $100 Billion Supplemental Foreign Aid Request

The 2023 supplemental foreign aid request was a roughly $100 billion package drafted by the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in October 2023, combining assistance for [[israel|Israel]] with funding for [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and other stated security priorities.[1]

## Contents and process

According to two people familiar with the details cited by ABC News, details of the spending request were still being finalized ahead of an official request to be sent by the [[white-house|White House]] to [[congress|Congress]] that week.[1] One person said the "bulk" of the assistance would likely go toward Ukraine, while others said the package included $10 billion for Israel; the officials spoke on condition of anonymity because the details had not yet been announced.[1] Speaking from Tel Aviv during his visit to Israel, Biden affirmed that he would ask Congress "this week" for "unprecedented" Israel aid.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] rejected the package outright, calling it "taxation without representation" and arguing that the "criminal regime" was bankrupting future generations of Americans.[1] She contended that the country was "bankrupt by every measure, except the magic of fiat and central bank deception," and that bankrupting the United States was the administration's goal as a prerequisite for realizing the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and a [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1] Anticipating the argument that congressional paralysis would block the deal, she cited National Security Council spokesman [[john-kirby|John Kirby]]'s statement that "Nothing in the halls of Congress will impact our ability to support Ukraine," and assumed the same logic would apply to Israel.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that by pledging to tie the Ukraine and Israel aid packages together, Biden was "directly converging the narrative templates of Ukraine and Israel into the actual," mapping onto claims about Western funding of proxy states and proxy wars long advanced within the Truth Community.[1]
