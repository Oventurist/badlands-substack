# Black Swan Event

A "black swan event" is a high-impact occurrence that is very difficult to predict in advance. The term entered mainstream US political commentary at the end of 2023 when [[cbs-news|CBS News]] investigative reporter [[catherine-herridge|Catherine Herridge]] used it to describe what she expected of the coming year.[1]

## Herridge's 2024 prediction

During a roundtable discussion, host Margaret Brennan asked Herridge what could be expected to unfold over the coming year. Herridge replied, "Well, mine's a little dark," and continued: "I just feel a lot of concern that 2024 may be the year of a black swan event. This is a national security event with high impact that's very hard to predict."[1] The remark was picked up by Modernity News and circulated in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as a bonus item, where it was read against a backdrop of escalating conflicts in Gaza, the [[red-sea-shipping-crisis|Red Sea]] and Iraq during the same news cycle.[1]
