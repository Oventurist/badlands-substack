# 2025 G7 Summit

The 2025 G7 summit was the annual gathering of Group of Seven leaders, hosted by Canada at a mountain resort in the Canadian Rockies in mid-June 2025.[1] It was the first such gathering since [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] returned to the [[white-house|White House]].[1]

## Context and expectations

Reporting ahead of the summit framed the event as a "delicate dance" for the other leaders, recalling that the last time Canada hosted the G7, in 2018, Trump blew up the final communiqué after departing on Air Force One and called then-Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] "very dishonest & weak."[1] In 2025, [[june-2025-israeli-strikes-on-iran|Israel's attack on Iranian nuclear sites]] threatened to crowd out plans to make progress on trade and security.[1]

Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] hosted what coverage described as an eclectic gathering involving a clutch of high-level guests, including Ukraine's [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]], India's [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]], and Mexico's [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]].[1] Australia's [[anthony-albanese|Anthony Albanese]] was identified as another potential flash point, as he faced pressure over Trump's consideration of scrapping a critical submarine deal.[1]
