# Strategic Defense Initiative

The Strategic Defense Initiative was a missile-defense concept announced by President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] in 1983.[1] In Badlands Media commentary it is described not merely as a weapons program but as the original template for a jointly controlled U.S.-Russia defense system based on shared scientific advance rather than mutual deterrence.[1]

## Revival as Strategic Defense of Earth

In 2011 the former head of Roscosmos, [[dmitry-rogozin|Dmitry Rogozin]], revived the concept under the title Strategic Defense of Earth, proposing that humanity's arsenal of atomic weapons be turned away from each other and toward the grave danger of asteroid collisions.[1] The proposal responded to the two-fold threat of [[nato|NATO]] military encirclement on Earth and asteroid impacts from abroad, a hazard for which the article says the world is woefully unprepared.[1]

## Significance

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats the initiative as an illustration of [[open-systems-vs-closed-systems|open-system]] strategy, arguing that folding asteroid defense into U.S.-Russia arms control working groups would yield non-linear benefits beyond conventional measurement, and noting that China, along with the European and Japanese space agencies, has echoed the vision.[1]
