# Transhausen by Proxy

**"Transhausen by proxy"** is an informal term coined to describe narcissistic parents who push so-called "gender transitioning" on their children, offered by some psychiatrists as a partial explanation for the sudden rise in the number of transgender-identifying children.[1]

## Origin and definition

The term is not an officially recognized psychological condition; it is a play on Munchausen Syndrome by Proxy (MSBP), an official condition also sometimes called medical child abuse or factitious disorder imposed on others.[1] MSBP is a mental illness exhibited mostly by women who seek attention by exaggerating or fabricating an illness in children or others in their care.[1]

## Cultural context

Experts told [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] that transhausen by proxy has very real effects on society, pointing to celebrity culture as an amplifier.[1] Celebrities are increasingly in the limelight with announcements about children who come out as transgender or nonbinary — nonbinary individuals identifying as neither male nor female.[1] Cited as an example was a May headline on Pride.com, "15 Celebs Who Are Out & Proud of Their Trans & Nonbinary Kids," which praised stars including Cher, Sade, Jennifer Lopez and Charlize Theron for supporting children who reject their biological sex.[1]
