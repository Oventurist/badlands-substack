# Fair Housing Act

The Fair Housing Act is the federal statute prohibiting discrimination in housing on the basis of protected characteristics including race. It is enforced principally by the [[department-of-housing-and-urban-development|Department of Housing and Urban Development]].[1]

## 2026 Minneapolis probe

In early 2026, HUD invoked the Act — together with the Civil Rights Act — in opening an investigation into [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]] housing programs, alleging that the city's race-based prioritization of certain ethnic groups in the allocation of housing benefits constitutes unlawful discrimination.[1] The probe ran alongside federal immigration-enforcement and obstruction investigations in Minnesota and was framed by the administration as part of a broader effort against fraud and corruption tied to state policies.[1]
