# Paradise Papers

The Paradise Papers are a leak of offshore financial records that exposed the hidden dealings of wealthy individuals and corporations. In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, the leak is cited as the source revealing that Israeli mining magnate [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] was disseminating funds from his Congolese mining operations to fellow oligarchs.[1]

## Relevance to the Gertler network

Gertler, described as scion to the Israeli Diamond Cartel, was targeted by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]] in December 2017 over the use of child slaves to mine cobalt and copper in the Congo.[1] The Paradise Papers disclosures are presented as documentary evidence of how proceeds from that operation circulated within a broader Israeli oligarchic network connected to the followers of [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]].[1] The mining rights seized through EO 13818 were later legally stripped from Gertler through prosecution by the Biden Justice Department in 2022, with the rights ultimately acquired by [[mohammed-bin-zayed|Mohammed bin Zayed]] of the UAE as well as China and Russia.[1]
