# Blockchain

Blockchain is the distributed ledger technology underlying [[bitcoin]] and, in the account given by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] in [[are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom|"Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?"]], the technical foundation of every digital currency including [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]]. The article treats it as the decisive variable in the transition away from what it calls the global debt slavery system.

## Technical description

Following Investopedia, the article defines blockchain as a type of [[distributed-ledger|distributed ledger]]: a database consensually shared and synchronized across multiple sites, institutions, or geographies and accessible to multiple parties, in which transactions have public "witnesses," each node can hold an identical copy of the record, and any change is reflected to all participants within seconds or minutes. This is contrasted with the centralized ledger used by most companies, which the source describes as more vulnerable to cyber attack and fraud because it has a single point of failure.

## Significance in the article's argument

Three properties carry the argument. First, decentralization: because blockchain distributes rather than concentrates control, Lange argues it cannot have been the [[cabal]]'s design and instead undermines it. Second, speed: settlement in minutes rather than the days required by the [[automated-clearing-house|ACH]] network makes peer-to-peer transfer possible without a third party able to reverse or block payment, and — critically for the article's thesis — makes it feasible to move the world into a new gold-backed system fast enough to limit the duration of a post-collapse depression. Third, auditability: a decentralized ledger, the article concludes, "will also force governments and banks to have honest bookkeeping."

The article also observes that digitization is not confined to money, noting that "everything related to data is being digitalized, and whoever doesn't keep up will be left behind and at a disadvantage." [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] is quoted describing a cross-border payment system built on "digital currency technologies and distributed registries" that would be independent of banks and third-party interference.
