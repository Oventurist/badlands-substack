# Blockchain

**Blockchain** is a type of distributed ledger technology underpinning cryptocurrencies and digital currencies generally. In the Badlands Media article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?", [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats blockchain as the technical foundation of all digital currency and as an inherently decentralizing force.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## Distributed ledgers

Quoting Investopedia, the article defines a distributed ledger as a database consensually shared and synchronized across multiple sites, institutions, or geographies and accessible to multiple people, allowing transactions to have public "witnesses."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Each node can hold an identical copy of the record, and any change is propagated to all participants within seconds or minutes.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) This contrasts with the centralized ledgers most companies use, which are more vulnerable to cyber attack and fraud because they have a single point of failure.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Blockchain is described as the specific type of distributed ledger used by [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## Significance in the article's argument

Lange argues that because blockchain is decentralized, open, and transparent, it is "the opposite of what the cabal wants, and clearly not their plan," and that its emergence therefore cannot be attributed to the [[cabal|cabal's]] design.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) He contends the timing of Bitcoin and blockchain was not coincidental but part of a plan to bring down entrenched financial control.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

Operationally, the article credits blockchain with enabling peer-to-peer settlement in minutes rather than the days required by the [[automated-clearing-house|ACH]] system, removing third-party ability to cancel transactions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Lange claims this speed is what makes a rapid transition from a [[fiat-currency|fiat]] system to an honest, [[gold-standard|gold-backed]] money system feasible without a prolonged depression, and that a decentralized ledger forces governments and banks into honest bookkeeping that is easy to audit.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) He further notes that digitization driven by blockchain extends beyond money to all data, and that jurisdictions failing to keep pace will be left at a disadvantage.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)
