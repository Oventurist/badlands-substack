# Why We Vote

**Why We Vote** is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program focused on election integrity, co-hosted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[canncon|CannCon]] and streamed on Rumble.[1] The show serves as the venue in which Badlands' election-related reporting and analysis is developed at length, with the weekly episode frequently previewed or referenced in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].[1]

## Episode 162

In the February 16, 2026 Brief, Ashe in America noted that she and CannCon had discussed attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]]'s posture with guest Rachel Alexander on the Friday edition of the program (Episode 162).[1] The discussion centered on Elias's proposals regarding federal, state and local roles in election administration, which the hosts read as an attempt to shield state election machinery from outside scrutiny.[1]

Ashe also used the Brief to preview upcoming coverage, writing that arrests connected to election cases were in fact occurring and would be discussed on the following Friday's episode, while pushing back on online commentators who claimed "nothing is happening" because no high-profile arrests had been announced.[1] Her argument was that arrests are not the objective — convictions are — and that cutting procedural corners to satisfy "internet lynch mobs" would ensure that defendants ultimately walk free.[1]

## Editorial posture

The program's stated position, as expressed in the Brief, is that the American justice system is deliberately designed to prioritize the rights of the accused, that equal justice under law requires due process even for defendants the audience believes are plainly guilty, and that every search, seizure, grand jury and prosecutorial step must be executed "meticulously by the book" if convictions are to survive appeal.[1] Ashe framed patience as a virtue in this context, arguing that a legitimate process necessarily takes time.[1]

## Coverage of the USPS ballot report (2023)

On June 5, 2023 [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[canncon|CannCon]] announced a deep dive on Why We Vote into the newly surfaced [[united-states-postal-service|USPS]] final report on contract driver [[jesse-morgan|Jesse Morgan]]'s account of hauling completed ballots from New York to Pennsylvania in October 2020.[2] The episode was scheduled for 5 p.m. that day on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s Rumble channel.[2]

## Internet-connected voting equipment and the CrowdStrike outage

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the program's reporting in her July 2024 commentary on the [[2024-crowdstrike-global-outage|CrowdStrike global outage]], writing that "everyone knows that electronic voting equipment is connected to the internet, as we've repeatedly shown on Why We Vote."[3] She argued that those still accepting official assurances about "air gapped machines" had received "a loading dose of reality" from the outage.[3]
