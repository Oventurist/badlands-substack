# Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative

The Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative is a [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] enforcement program that pursues federal contractors over alleged misrepresentations regarding the cybersecurity of systems they provide to the government.[1] The initiative was overseen by then-Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]].[1]

The program became central to a May 2026 referral filed by the [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]], which contended that the initiative targeted other contractors for allegedly less serious misconduct while no comparable case was ever brought against [[microsoft|Microsoft]], despite intrusions between 2019 and 2023 attributed to Russian and Chinese hackers that reportedly compromised sensitive US government systems.[1] The referral asked whether Microsoft could face [[false-claims-act|False Claims Act]] liability of the kind the initiative was designed to pursue.[1]
