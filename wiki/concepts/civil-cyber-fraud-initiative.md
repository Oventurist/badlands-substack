# Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative

The Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative is a [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] enforcement program that uses the [[false-claims-act|False Claims Act]] against government contractors who make false or misleading representations about their cybersecurity. In the Badlands corpus it appears in the May 2026 referral against [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]], who oversaw the initiative as Biden Deputy Attorney General.

## Allegations of selective enforcement

The [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]] alleged that under Monaco's oversight the initiative targeted other contractors for less serious alleged misconduct while no comparable case was brought against [[microsoft|Microsoft]], despite multiple cyber intrusions between 2019 and 2023 tied to Russian and Chinese hackers that reportedly compromised sensitive US government systems.

The foundation's referral asked DOJ inspectors general to investigate whether Microsoft could face liability under the False Claims Act for representations made to federal agencies about the security of its systems, and whether Monaco and other officials violated ethics, recusal, or revolving-door restrictions by later taking senior positions at the company.
