# State Secrets Privilege

The state secrets privilege is a legal doctrine allowing the government to shield certain information from disclosure in court on national security grounds. In the Badlands Media framework advanced by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], it functions as the legal mechanism concealing an ongoing military-intelligence operation against domestic "bad actors."[1]

Q drop 553 (January 19, 2018) asked: "How can we listen in, track, and monitor American citizens [bad actors]?… What must we LEGALLY demonstrate in order to gain such warrants? FISA? Do we TRUST the FISA JUDGES? MIL INTEL? State Secrets?"[1] Lange reads this as peeling back the curtain: military intelligence was running the operation, hidden legally under the state secrets designation, which prevented the courts from revealing the existence of the investigations.[1]

He argues that obtaining [[foreign-intelligence-surveillance-act|FISA]] warrants against figures such as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] required demonstrating treason at the Supreme Court level, and that a president and Secretary of State committing treason changes the legal rules entirely — the targets becoming "enemy combatants" rather than ordinary criminal defendants.[1] Q drop 17 (October 31, 2017) similarly asked how military intelligence could be applied to prosecute bad actors while avoiding corrupt agencies and judges.[1]

Lange further claims that at least two Supreme Court justices — he believes [[clarence-thomas|Clarence Thomas]] and Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]], the latter because he oversees the FISA courts — were informed of and legally authorized these operations, and that the three justices later appointed by [[donald-trump|Trump]] are also aware.[1] He cites Trump's success on the [[supreme-court-emergency-docket|emergency docket]] as evidence.[1] Q also frequently stated that "FISA goes both ways."[1] These assertions are Badlands Media interpretation and are not established fact.
