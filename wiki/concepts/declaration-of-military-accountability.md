# Declaration of Military Accountability

The Declaration of Military Accountability is an open letter published on January 1, 2024 and signed by more than 200 active-duty service members and veterans seeking accountability for the alleged harm caused by the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]]'s implementation of the since-rescinded COVID-19 vaccine mandate.[1]

## Contents and signatories

The letter is addressed to the American people but names specific senior military leaders whom the signers accuse of enabling lawlessness and betraying the Constitution, including former and current joint chiefs of staff, service academy commandants, service inspectors general, and service surgeons general.[1]

The signatories wrote that "in the coming years, thousands within our network will run for Congress and seek appointments to executive branch offices, while those of us still serving on active duty will continue to put fulfilling our oaths ahead of striving for rank or position."[1] They further pledged that "for those who achieve the lawful authority to do so, we pledge to recall from retirement the military leaders who broke the law and will convene courts-martial for the crimes they committed."[1]

A number of signatories were veterans running for [[congress|Congress]] and for state-level office, and they pledged to introduce legislation reducing the alleged perpetrators' retirement income to zero.[1] Many of the 231 signers remained on active duty at the time of publication, and several said they were accepting significant personal risk to defend rights they believed had been trampled.[1] The letter was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]].[1]
