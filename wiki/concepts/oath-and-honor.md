# Oath and Honor: A Memoir and a Warning

**"Oath and Honor: A Memoir and a Warning"** is a book by former Rep. [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]], released on Tuesday, December 5, 2023.[1]

## Content and context

The book focuses on the fallout of the 2020 presidential race and the events around January 6, describing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as "the most dangerous man ever to inhabit the Oval Office."[1] Its release coincided with Cheney's disclosure that she was weighing a third-party presidential bid, and with her claim that Trump's return to power in 2024 could mean the end of American democracy.[1] Cheney had helped run the [[january-6-committee|House select committee]] that investigated the Jan. 6 Capitol attack, and her criticism of Trump contributed to the end of her congressional career when she was defeated in a 2022 Wyoming primary by Trump-backed [[harriet-hageman|Harriett Hageman]].[1]
