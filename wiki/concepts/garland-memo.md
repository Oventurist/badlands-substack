# Garland Memo

The "Garland memo" is the October 4, 2021 directive issued by Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] instructing federal law enforcement to address a purported rise in threats against school board members and school officials — a document critics say effectively labeled concerned parents as "domestic terrorists."[1] In July 2025, [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] released records it said conclusively proved the memo was a politically orchestrated operation coordinated with the Biden [[white-house|White House]].[1]

## The 2025 document release

America First Legal described the newly disclosed records — obtained after three and a half years of litigation and requests — as "explosive," saying they exposed both the Biden White House's role in pushing a weaponized [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and strong internal dissent from career attorneys in the Civil Rights Division, who warned there was no federal authority or legal basis to target parents whose speech was protected by the First Amendment.[1]

The documents allowed the group to complete, for the first time, the timeline between the [[national-school-boards-association|National School Boards Association]] memo to DOJ and the publication of the October 4 memo.[1] As America First Legal had predicted on October 7, 2021, the normal process was not followed: political leadership circumvented and disregarded the advice of DOJ lawyers.[1] The NSBA memo had urged the DOJ to use the Patriot Act against parents objecting to school mask mandates and Critical Race Theory.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary noted that the weaponization of government is usually discussed in terms of attacks on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], but that thousands of ordinary Americans were affected by what it called the totalitarian abuses of the Biden administration — abuses now documented in the AFL records.[1]
