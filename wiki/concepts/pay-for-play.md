# Pay-for-Play

"Pay-for-play" is the term Badlands Media uses for the alleged system in which access to and favors from the United States government were exchanged for donations, fees and business arrangements benefiting officials and their associates. The author summarizes the Clinton-era version of it as a "PAY-FOR-PLAY SPIDER WEB" and, more broadly, as "America for sale."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Mechanics as described

The claimed structure had three interlocking nodes: the [[state-department|State Department]] under Secretary [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], which could grant favors; the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], which received donations; and [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]], the consulting firm and investment bank that served as the intermediary and collected fees.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] is described as the liaison between Foundation donors seeking favors and the Secretary's office, and [[doug-band|Doug Band]] as the counterpart operating from the Clinton side.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]]'s unpaid envoy post is presented as a means of generating corporate relationships that produced both Teneo accounts and Foundation donations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Illustrative transactions cited include [[ubs]]'s escalation of donations and speaking fees after its tax settlement, [[fifa]] and the Qatar 2022 committee's [[clinton-global-initiative|CGI]] sponsorships preceding [[qatar]]'s World Cup award, and Teneo's $2.5 million annual retainer to [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], approved by the State Department while his wife led it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Related mechanism

The article extends the concept beyond the Clintons to the Obama [[department-of-justice|DOJ]], where the [[doj-slush-fund|settlement slush fund]] allegedly directed roughly $1 billion from bank settlements to favored activist groups — a variation in which regulatory jeopardy rather than diplomatic favor was the currency.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The author's conclusion is that "our government was no longer working for the people. They were only working for themselves."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The Comey case in "The Biggest Boomerang In History"

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] uses "pay to play" as the organising description of the financial web he says compromised [[james-comey|James Comey]] before he ever investigated [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[2] Comey's brother [[peter-comey|Peter Comey]] worked at [[dla-piper|DLA Piper]], one of Clinton's top ten career campaign donors and the firm that prepares the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]]'s taxes and performed its 2015 audit; property records reportedly show James Comey held the mortgage on his brother's Virginia house.[2]

Q drop 4782 of September 28, 2020 is quoted as describing the mechanism directly: a supporter's family member is hired by a Foundation-engaged law firm, the Foundation donates predominantly foreign funds to that firm under nominally legal payments, DLA Piper pays the family member salary and bonuses, and the family member transfers value back to the supporter.[2] Lange also cites Comey's more than $6 million in final-year compensation from [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed Martin]] — which became a Clinton Foundation donor and Clinton Global Initiative member in 2010 and won 17 contract approvals from Clinton's [[state-department|State Department]] that year — and his 2013 board seat at [[hsbc|HSBC Holdings]], a routine Foundation partner.[2] He argues Clinton had been grooming officials across the DOJ and FBI who were all part of this spiderweb, installed to preserve a [[two-tier-justice-system|two tier justice system]] of protection.[2] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
