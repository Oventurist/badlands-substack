# TikTok US Joint Venture Deal

The TikTok US joint venture deal is the arrangement President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was expected to approve in late September 2025 to restructure [[tiktok|TikTok]]'s American operations under majority US ownership while placing all user data in a secure US-based cloud, according to a senior White House official.[1]

Under the agreement, TikTok's US business would operate as a new joint-venture company, with [[bytedance|ByteDance]] limited to under 20% ownership.[1] The board of directors would include seven members, most of them US citizens with national security and cybersecurity expertise, though ByteDance would be allowed to appoint one seat.[1]

[[oracle|Oracle]] would serve as the platform's trusted security partner, monitoring data storage and overseeing operations in cooperation with the US government, with all American user data housed in a dedicated Oracle-run cloud isolated from foreign access and protected by advanced security controls.[1] The deal also requires TikTok's algorithm for US users to be retrained, secured, and operated entirely outside ByteDance's control: ByteDance would duplicate the algorithm and lease it to the joint venture, with Oracle responsible for retraining and continuously monitoring it to prevent manipulation or surveillance.[1]

Officials said the arrangement would allow TikTok to remain interoperable globally while ensuring US users' data stays protected and the American platform independently enforces its own terms of service against harmful content.[1] Trump was expected to sign an executive order approving the agreement later that week.[1]
