# Draft Nuclear Construction Executive Orders (2025)

In May 2025 the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] was reported to be weighing a set of executive orders designed to accelerate the construction of nuclear power plants in the United States in order to meet rising electricity demand.[1]

## Contents of the drafts

According to drafts reviewed by The New York Times, the orders stated that the United States had fallen behind [[china|China]] in expanding nuclear power and called for a "wholesale revision" of federal safety regulations to make new plant construction easier.[1] They envisioned the Department of Defense taking a prominent role in ordering reactors and installing them on military bases.[1]

The drafts would also set a goal of quadrupling the size of the nation's nuclear fleet, from nearly 100 gigawatts of electric capacity at the time to 400 gigawatts by 2050 — one gigawatt being enough to power nearly one million homes.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[gmoney|GMONEY]] summarized the reporting and speculated that the administration already had "some fun new tech just waiting to be magically deployed."[1]
