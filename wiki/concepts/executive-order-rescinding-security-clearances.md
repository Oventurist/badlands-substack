# Executive Order Rescinding Security Clearances from Specified Individuals

In March 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed a presidential memorandum titled "Rescinding Security Clearances and Access to Classified Information from Specified Individuals," which Badlands Media treats as an unprecedented public designation of targets in an ongoing criminal investigation.[1]

## Content

The memorandum, addressed to the heads of executive departments and agencies, stated that it was no longer in the national interest for a named list of individuals to access classified information, and directed every department and agency head to revoke any active clearances they held, rescind their access to classified information, and revoke their unescorted access to secure United States government facilities.[1]

The individuals named were Antony Blinken, Jacob Sullivan, Lisa Monaco, Mark Zaid, Norman Eisen, Letitia James, [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]], Andrew Weissmann, [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], Elizabeth Cheney, Kamala Harris, [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]], Fiona Hill, [[alexander-vindman|Alexander Vindman]], Joseph R. Biden Jr., and any other member of Biden's family.[1]
