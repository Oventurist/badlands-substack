# Yellow Vest Movement

The Yellow Vest movement (*gilets jaunes*) was a mass French protest movement
beginning in 2018 in which, as Badlands described it, millions of city-dwelling
French people took to the streets for months — years, even — to express their
contempt for their ruling political class, and specifically for the elitist
green agenda.[1]

## Legacy and the threat of a larger revolt

The movement remains the benchmark against which subsequent French unrest is
measured. In April 2024, [[david-djaiz|David Djaïz]], a former advisor to
President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]], warned that France risked "a
social revolt compared to which the Yellow Vests and the farmers are
appetizers," alongside the possibility of a huge public finance crisis.[1]
Populist uprisings have been a defining feature of the government of Macron, a
former Rothschild banker branded the "president of the rich."[1]

Badlands commentary linked the Yellow Vests to the more recent farmer protests
across western Europe, describing governments plotting to requisition
privately-owned farmland and halt food production as orchestrating the
starvation of Europe.[1] The convergence of conservative rural farmers with
liberal-leaning city-dwellers against the [[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]]
agenda was presented as a case of [[horseshoe-theory|Horseshoe Theory]].[1]
