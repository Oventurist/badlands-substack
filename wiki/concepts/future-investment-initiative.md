# Future Investment Initiative

The Future Investment Initiative (FII) is an international forum affiliated with Saudi Arabia's [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], focused on global economic trends and cross-border investment.[1] Its organizers convene participants from multiple countries to discuss capital flows and economic opportunity.[1]

## FII Priority Summit, Miami 2026

The FII Priority Summit was held in Miami in February 2026, with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] delivering remarks to an audience of global investors, policymakers and business leaders.[1] Trump emphasized economic policy and the United States as a destination for international investment.[1] Reporting around the event noted scrutiny in Florida of ties between U.S. political figures and foreign-backed investment initiatives.[1] Badlands commentary highlighted Trump's freewheeling manner at the summit — including telling the audience they could ask him about "anything" — and his teasing remarks about Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], read as affectionate rather than hostile.[1]

### Miami Priority Summit 2025
On February 19, 2025, President Donald Trump opened the Future Investment Initiative Institute Priority Summit Miami 2025 at the Faena Forum on Miami Beach, telling the audience that it was "a tremendous honor, to become the first American president to address the Future Investment Initiative Institute."[2] The Miami Herald described the appearance as a sign that the Saudi oil giant was increasingly reaching the American mainstream with a signature event designed to promote the country as a global actor seeking to diversify its economy.[2] The event was backed by the Public Investment Fund, Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, which Bloomberg estimated held $925 billion in assets under management; the audience included Elon Musk as well as Trump's chief of staff and national security adviser.[2]

Badlands commentary described the institute as the antithesis of the World Economic Forum — the Sovereign Alliance's "answer" to Davos. When the Public Investment Fund created it in October 2018 it was branded "Davos in the Desert," a label said to have angered Klaus Schwab and his associates.[2] The inaugural edition was overshadowed by the Jamal Khashoggi affair, launched weeks earlier and characterized in that reading as a psychological operation against Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman that gave would-be attendees pause.[2] It was at this 2025 summit that Trump floated returning 20 percent of DOGE savings to American citizens.[2]
