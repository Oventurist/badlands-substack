# Breaking History

**Breaking History** is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program hosted by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], streamed on Rumble, that examines historical and financial structures underpinning contemporary events.[1]

## Coverage of FinCEN and the Egmont Group

Writing in the February 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], the host noted that recent episodes — including Episode 136 — had been devoted to the [[financial-crimes-enforcement-network|Financial Crimes Enforcement Network]] (FinCEN) and the [[egmont-group|Egmont Group]].[1] The program's framing is that FinCEN is the financial-crimes intelligence unit of the United States government, while the Egmont Group is an international network of more than 170 comparable units drawn from nearly every government on earth, created to share that intelligence across borders.[1]

The show connected this structure to the sanctioning of Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] by the U.S. Treasury Department, noting that the sanctioning document accused Petro of having caused Colombia's financial-crimes intelligence unit to be suspended from Egmont for allegedly sharing information he had collected from Egmont servers.[1] The host observed that both Petro and [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] have accused the most powerful banks in the United States and Europe of literally functioning as the cartels, with Petro arguing that institutions like the Egmont Group allow that criminality to continue by covering for it.[1]

## Analytical method

The program leans heavily on forensic accounting as an investigative model, invoking the "follow the money" refrain as a staple of both the Q Research community and the wider cultural ethos of criminology, on the grounds that money flow is objective and traceable in a way that testimony and narrative are not.[1]
