# The Evolutionary Psychology Behind Politics

**The Evolutionary Psychology Behind Politics** (abbreviated **EPBP** in Badlands Media commentary) is a book that the contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] credits with demonstrating "conclusively, in my opinion" that [[conservatism]] and [[liberalism]]/[[leftism]] amount to two survival and replication strategies optimized for different environments.[1]

## Role in the Argument

After reading EPBP, the author states, he became certain that there is "simply no better theory in the world" than [[r-k-selection-theory|r/K selection theory]] for explaining the bifurcation of humanity into conservative and left/liberal camps.[1] His article on predators and prey is presented as the simplest breakdown of the book's material that he could muster, and he cautions that r/K selection theory cannot be done justice in a single article.[1]

## Claims Drawn From the Book

The article draws several specific claims from EPBP. Among them is the treatment of the amygdala controversy: the author accepts the statistical observation that conservatives have larger amygdalas but, following the book, argues this correlates with a stronger ability to correctly perceive threats rather than with paranoid bigotry.[1] He further attributes to EPBP the identification of the *anterior cingulate cortex*, rather than the amygdala, as the brain region responsible for fear responses to adverse stimuli — noting as an aside that the anterior cingulate cortex figures prominently in hypnotic states as measured by various instruments.[1]

The book also supplies the trait tables reproduced in the article listing r- and K-selected characteristics as they manifest in humans, and underwrites the author's argument that the psychological and political differences between left and right are observable physiologically and in some cases neurologically.[1]
