# Counterparty Protocol

The **Counterparty protocol** is a layer built on top of the [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] blockchain that allows arbitrary assets and data to be issued and recorded using Bitcoin transactions. Badlands Media commentary presents it as a working demonstration that data can be permanently anchored to Bitcoin at a verifiable real-world cost.[1]

## Use as a proof of "severe physical cost"

In the April 2023 essay "Don't Bring Shiny Rocks to Digital Gunfights," [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] described creating an asset on "Bitcoin, Layer 2, Counter Party Protocol" carrying both an animation and an audio track, and presented it as proof that Bitcoin can already be used to mitigate the hazard of [[artificial-intelligence|AI]]-generated data.[1] The author noted that observers can independently verify that a price was paid to store the data on Bitcoin, when the action occurred, and which wallet performed it, among other information.[1]

The demonstration supported the essay's central proposal: associating bits of data with an energy cost through micro-transactions creates an auditable, immutable record of which data has "paid the cost," a filter that would be trivial for humans but cost-prohibitive for AI systems generating content at scale.[1] The author acknowledged that this filtration does not certify the truth of the surviving data, only that a scarce resource was expended to gain access to the record.[1] The same essay linked the approach to Major [[jason-lowery|Jason Lowery]]'s [[softwar|Softwar]] thesis and to a Counterparty-issued asset card titled "THE PEPE ZONE."[1]
