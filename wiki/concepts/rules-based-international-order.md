# Rules-Based International Order

The "rules-based international order" is the term used by Western governments for the post-war international system. In Badlands Media commentary it is treated critically as an arbitrary substitute for international law proper.[1]

## Critique

The [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]]'s first principle — total respect for the sovereign equality of nations — is presented explicitly in contrast to "the completely arbitrary 'Rules Based International Order.'"[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues that the order invoked by unipolarists is "a very different creature" from the [[united-nations|UN Charter]], which premises international law on the sacredness of national sovereignty, non-military interference of one state in another, and mutual cooperation.[1]

The article warns that no attack on Russia or China could be carried out without vicious retaliation "that would take out every major city across the Rules Based International free world."[1]
