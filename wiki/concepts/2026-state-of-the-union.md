# 2026 State of the Union Address

The 2026 State of the Union Address was delivered by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on the evening of Tuesday, February 24, 2026, before a joint session of [[congress|Congress]]. It was described as his first State of the Union of his second term, and the President characterized the preceding year as a "transformation" and a "turnaround for the ages."[1]

## White House framing

Shortly after midnight Eastern Time, the administration published a statement on the [[white-house|White House]] website headlined "Democrats Showed Whose Side They're On — And it's Not the American People." The statement declared that Trump had "proudly declar[ed] the dawn of a new Golden Age after just one year of transformative leadership in his second term," and enumerated claimed accomplishments including a fully secure border, plummeting inflation, falling gas prices and crime rates, surging stock markets and 401(k) balances, tax relief for working Americans, and a foreign policy of "peace through strength."[1]

The statement then catalogued a long list of items that, in the administration's telling, congressional Democrats had refused to applaud. These included the assertion that "the first duty of the American government is to protect American citizens, not illegal aliens"; the grieving families of Americans killed by illegal aliens, including the mother of [[iryna-zarutska|Iryna Zarutska]]; border security; unifying against political violence; subsiding inflation; keeping violent criminals incarcerated; lower prescription drug prices; the military action that brought indicted narcoterrorist [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] to justice; protections for minors against irreversible sex-change procedures; multi-year lows in gas prices; renewed respect for law enforcement; [[trump-accounts|Trump Accounts]] for American newborns; election-integrity measures barring illegal aliens from voting; a ban on large corporations purchasing single-family homes; the end of fraud schemes such as the [[minnesota-somali-fraud-scandal|$19 billion Somali fraud scandal]] in Minnesota; [[warrior-dividends|Warrior Dividends]] for military personnel; a record 56% one-year reduction in fentanyl flows; the largest single drop in the murder rate in 125 years; growth in retirement accounts; the [[working-families-tax-cuts-act|Working Families Tax Cuts Act]]; the recognition of a World War II veteran who helped liberate the largest internment camp in the Philippines; the ending of eight wars; American military deterrence; economic growth; and "total victory over terrorists abroad."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reproduced the White House statement in full in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], arguing that the list functions as a usable inventory of the President's own claimed achievements heading into the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]]. She observed that a claim of "total victory over terrorists abroad" should logically obviate continued funding for fighting terrorists abroad, and raised a caution about the phrase "unifying against political violence" — noting the recent era in which speech about protected groups was itself characterized as violence.[1] She described the speech as "a good time," singling out the spectacle of political opponents chanting "USA! USA!" for Team USA.[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted Trump's remark during the address that only the [[european-union|European Union]] was seeking to renegotiate the tariff agreement after the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] struck down Trump's tariff program as unconstitutional, reading it as evidence of an escalating confrontation between Washington and Brussels.[1] He also noted that during the address multiple European cities, including Brandenburg and Paris, illuminated monuments in Ukrainian colors in solidarity with [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]
