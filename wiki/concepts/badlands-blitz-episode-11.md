# Badlands Blitz Episode 11

**Badlands Blitz Episode 11**, subtitled "Iran, NATO and the Invisible Enemy," is the eleventh installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly recap program published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on its Substack. Dated March 21, 2026, the episode had accumulated 233 likes at the time of the source posting. Like earlier entries in the series, it is presented to the audience — addressed as "Badlanders" — as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."

## Hosts and format

The episode is hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], both regular contributors to the Badlands Substack. The announcement post links to each host's Substack profile and follows the standard Blitz template: a short framing of the week's dominant storyline, followed by the hosts' extended discussion.

## Themes

According to the source, the two hosts "use the spiraling narrative of the Iran War to map the strategic pincer that appears to be in the offing by the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] against the true [[invisible-enemy|Invisible Enemy]]." The framing treats the escalating conflict involving [[iran|Iran]] — and, by implication, [[nato|NATO]] as named in the episode subtitle — not as a self-contained regional war but as the visible surface of a wider covert struggle, in which an alliance of sovereignty-minded actors is said to be closing on a supranational adversary.

The second half of the episode turns to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and, in the source's words, "the dangers of playing white hat-black hat games with the characters that orbit him on the fifth-generation game board." This is a caution to the audience against confidently sorting the figures around Trump into heroes and villains, a habit the hosts treat as a hazard specific to the ambiguity of [[5th-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]], where perception management makes allegiances difficult to read in real time.

## Publication and support model

The post repeats the standard Badlands appeal: all Badlands content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free, and the team is able to pay itself only through readers who choose to support the work directly. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time, in recognition of the daily output and what the post calls the "step-up in said work for 2026." The episode closes with the outlet's customary sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
