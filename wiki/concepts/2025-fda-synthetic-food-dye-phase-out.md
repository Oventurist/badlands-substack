# 2025 FDA Synthetic Food Dye Phase-Out

The 2025 synthetic food dye phase-out was an initiative announced by the [[food-and-drug-administration|U.S. Food and Drug Administration]] on 22 April 2025 to remove several petroleum-derived colour additives from the American food supply by the end of the following year.[1]

## Background

Health Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] had made tartrazine — known as Yellow 5 — a signature target, warning in a YouTube video posted in autumn 2024 that many Americans were unknowingly eating this and other "poisons."[1] The lemon-yellow dye tints junk foods such as Skittles and Mountain Dew and also appears in chicken bouillon, pancake mix and pickles.[1] In Europe, products containing Yellow 5 must carry a label warning that it "may have an adverse effect on activity and attention in children," while for two decades the FDA had declined to ban the dye, citing inconclusive evidence.[1]

## The announcement

The FDA said it would move to rid the food supply of Yellow 5 along with other synthetic dyes including Red 40, Blue 1 and Green 3 by the end of 2026.[1] The measure was not a formal ban: Kennedy, who oversees the agency, said at a press conference that he had reached an "understanding" with food companies to phase the dyes out, though he provided scant detail on the specifics.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] praised the move as part of a broader [[make-america-healthy-again|MAHA]] campaign that had already taken on fluoride and vaccines, but argued that the "big toxic elephant in the room" remained genetically engineered food — chiefly corn, soy, canola and alfalfa.[1] He noted the political difficulty of that fight given that agribusiness firms such as Monsanto, Syngenta, DuPont and Dow Agro dominate the crop market and that many conservative-leaning farmers depend on their products for a living.[1]
