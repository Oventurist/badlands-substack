# Soros Playbook

The "Soros playbook" is Badlands Media's name for a reconstructed, multi-step method by which [[george-soros|George Soros]] is alleged to topple nations and manipulate global markets — extrapolated to the United States from what Soros has been accused of doing in other countries.[1] The author concedes that not all details may prove perfectly correct, but argues the basic outline of a plan for "revolution" in the USA has been slowly revealing itself over the last twenty years, and was seemingly expedited in response to the [[donald-trump|Trump]] presidency.[1]

## The steps

The outline given proceeds as follows.[1] Establish organizations — foundations and non-profits — within the United States, and fill them with people indoctrinated and activated by progressive ideologies.[1] Once firmly established, those organizations are used to place believers and leaders into federal government positions, including by supporting campaigns and paying for advertisements to influence elections.[1] The organizations are then used to purchase and influence media outlets on every front possible: television, radio, print, internet, social media and pop culture.[1]

The compromised media is used to shape narratives and frame reality, achieved by continually pounding a ceaseless drumbeat of the same messaging across all mediums; narrative formation is practised and refined over years or decades.[1] Infiltrating the election process is described as a prime objective, the more infiltrated the more directly manageable, with Soros said to be directly linked to voting-machine use in several states.[1] Finally, Soros co-opts or creates cultural figures to push agendas — the example given being [[al-gore|Al Gore]], with $10 million annually reportedly directed into Gore's Climate Reality Project, formerly the Alliance for Climate Protection at the time of the cash injections.[1]

## Second phase

Once the first phase is accomplished, operatives move to weaken the foundations of the state by casting doubt on the integrity and validity of legal structures and of the election process itself.[1] The article says this was on full display in 2016, when Soros and company ordained that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] would ascend to the Oval Office and the [[soros-shadow-network|shadow network]] worked to secure her the nomination.[1] [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] material is cited as showing Clinton's team working with Soros and his organizations, including his media organizations, leaving her both dependent on and indebted to them.[1] A whistleblower's leak showed the [[democratic-national-committee|DNC]] rigging the primary against [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]].[1]

Three narratives are identified as the compromised media's principal products that cycle: that Hillary would win easily and Trump could not win; that Trump was evil, racist, bigoted and dangerous, such that no sane or good person could like him; and that Russia and Trump were working together and Russia would influence or help steal the election.[1]

## Third phase

The subsequent step is the creation of a legion of foot soldiers — [[antifa|Antifa]] and [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] — funded by the network and amplified by sensationalist media, with the primary goal of casting doubt on the legitimacy of the police and the legal system.[1] In [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth Generation Warfare]] terms, the target audience for this operation was centrist and left-leaning individuals.[1]
