# Global North and Global South

The "Global North" and "Global South" are terms used to divide the world's nations by the state of their respective economies, and they form the analytical framework used by Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] to interpret suspected [[undersea-cable-sabotage|undersea cable sabotage]] and broader great-power conflict in early 2025.[1]

## Definition

Industrialized nations are considered part of the Global North, while "developing economies" are considered part of the Global South. In the Badlands framing, the Global North consists of Russia, Europe, and the Anglosphere — Britain, the United States, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand — with every other country falling into the Global South. Russia is counted as part of the Global North because of its industrialized economy, but is described as deeply allied with the Global South.[1]

## Critique of globalism

The article argues that the true intention of globalism is to keep the Global South under-developed so that its citizenry can be exploited via slave labor, while politicians sell citizens of the Global North on "free-trade zones" enforced by organizations such as NAFTA.[1]

Unrestricted trade with foreign countries is presented to the public as a virtuous concept, but is described as a lose-lose proposition for the citizens of both hemispheres. Citizens of the Global South are said to be subjected to a form of feudalism, coerced into sweat shops and work camps managed by Global South oligarchs and producing cheap goods for Global North corporations. Citizens of the Global North are in turn effectively coerced into working for those corporations, as small business opportunities are marginalized by cheap imported goods and by stifling domestic regulations imposed by governments controlled by Global North oligarchs.[1]

On this reading globalism is a system of feudal slavery that artificially makes economics a zero-sum equation, falsely asserting that prosperity requires "haves" and "have-nots." The counter-argument offered is that oligarchs are merely extracting wealth from an economic cycle that naturally produces wealth; zero-sum theory would require that labor simultaneously produce and destroy wealth, and if that were true the middle class would never have emerged and all people would still be feudal serfs.[1]

## Application to Russia and China

Despite being part of the Global North, Russia is described as having been targeted for destruction by Western oligarchs since at least 1917, while China is treated as a slave colony with the help of Chinese oligarchs who betray both their own people and the rest of the Global South. The article states that one could hardly blame these nations for engaging in asymmetric warfare against the Global North, while adding that more evidence would be needed to conclude that China and Russia are behind the cable attacks. Their main adversary is identified as the intelligence community — the Deep State — of the Global North, and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] are said to have publicly expressed awareness of this dynamic.[1]
