# United Nations Security Council Resolution 2803

United Nations Security Council Resolution 2803 is the measure endorsing the [[comprehensive-plan-to-end-the-gaza-conflict|Comprehensive Plan to End the Gaza Conflict]], the framework under which the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] operates as a transitional administration for [[gaza|Gaza]].[1]

## Content and function

The plan endorsed by the resolution seeks to secure a permanent ceasefire, aid Gaza's reconstruction, and promote a lasting peace grounded in Palestinian self-determination and statehood, while advancing regional security and stability.[1] The resolution supplies the international legal basis cited by the foreign ministers of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], [[turkey|Turkey]], [[egypt|Egypt]], Jordan, Indonesia, Pakistan, [[qatar|Qatar]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] when they announced in January 2026 that their countries would join the Board of Peace at President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s invitation.[1] In a statement released through the Saudi Foreign Ministry's X account, the ministers reaffirmed support for Trump's peace efforts and pledged to help implement the Board's mission under the plan.[1]
