# 2025 Nepal Protests

The 2025 Nepal protests were a violent uprising in Kathmandu and other parts of Nepal that began on Monday, September 8, 2025, driven by anger over official corruption and a government ban on social media platforms including WhatsApp.[1]

## Events

On September 8, government forces opened fire on protesters, killing at least nineteen people.[1] The following day, rampaging demonstrators attacked [[nepal|Nepal]]'s political elite in both government offices and private homes: smoke billowed from fires set at Parliament and the Supreme Court in Kathmandu and at the residences of current and former lawmakers, and the wife of one former official was badly burned.[1] Hotels and airports were also attacked.[1] The violence continued even after Prime Minister [[kp-sharma-oli|K.P. Sharma Oli]] resigned and the government retreated from the social media ban.[1]

## Military deployment

The heads of Nepal's main security agencies issued a joint statement appealing for calm and urging political parties to find a peaceful resolution, but after the resignation of Oli and other top officials it was unclear who, if anyone, was in charge.[1] Late on Tuesday, September 9, with the death toll at twenty-two, the army said it would step in and began deploying troops in Kathmandu to restore order.[1]

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] remarked that it was strange how readily audiences accept "protests" broadcast by global propaganda media as legitimate when they occur in countries the audience knows little about — a framing consistent with the [[color-revolution|color revolution]] skepticism common in Badlands analysis.[1]
