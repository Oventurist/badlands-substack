# Timber Sycamore

Timber Sycamore is the codename of the [[cia|CIA]] program that armed and trained militant groups in [[syria]] with the aim of pressuring the government of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].

## In the corpus

assad day in damascus treats the program as public knowledge and cites it as direct evidence that American involvement in the Syrian conflict long predated the 2024 rebel offensive. The article distinguishes the CIA's effort from that of the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], whose Syrian activity was focused more explicitly on fighting [[isis]], principally through support for the predominantly Kurdish [[syrian-democratic-forces]].

The article notes that the CIA program was terminated in 2017 under President [[donald-trump]], a decision described as a shift in U.S. policy toward counter-terrorism rather than regime change. Timber Sycamore is placed by the source in the same lineage as the CIA's earlier construction of the [[mujahideen]] against the Soviets — an approach in which localized proxy groups substitute for the direct deployment of American troops, a pattern the article says has characterized most conflicts since the Second World War.
