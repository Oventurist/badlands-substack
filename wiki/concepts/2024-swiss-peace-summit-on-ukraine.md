# 2024 Swiss Peace Summit on Ukraine

The Swiss-hosted "peace conference" on Ukraine was scheduled to open in Switzerland on Saturday, June 15, 2024, immediately following the [[2024-g7-summit-in-italy|G7 summit in Italy]], and was intended to build international backing for [[volodymyr-zelensky|Ukraine's]] terms for ending the war.[1] Russia was not invited, a decision that shaped the summit's attendance problems.[1]

## Attendance collapse

Roughly 160 nations were originally invited.[1] According to the Ukrainian service of Radio Liberty, citing unidentified EU diplomats, the number of participating nations fell from 93 to 78 as countries withdrew in the days before the opening; the diplomats did not name the defectors and said further withdrawals were possible.[1] Sergey Nikiforov, spokesman for President Zelensky, declined on Rada television to state a final participant number, saying only, "We would not like to state the final number."[1] Badlands commentary counted 82 official declines against the original invitation list and noted that many of the remaining 78 had still not formally committed on the eve of the conference.[1]

The most prominent absentee identified was Indian Prime Minister [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]], who joined China and Brazil in refusing to take part in a process that excluded Russia — a refusal the article called a particularly pointed snub given that Modi was already next door in Italy for the G7.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Writing for Badlands, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the Swiss conference and the G7 summit as twin humiliations inflicted on the "Globalist Empire" by what he called the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] in a single week.[1] He noted that [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] and the 57-nation Ummah had adopted the same position and planned to host their own peace conference in the fall that would include Russia, China, Brazil, and India.[1]
