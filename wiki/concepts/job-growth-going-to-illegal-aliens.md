# Job Growth Going to Illegal Aliens

"Job growth going to illegal aliens" refers to the claim — advanced persistently by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and, by June 2024, conceded in parts of Wall Street research — that essentially all net U.S. employment gains over the preceding year accrued to illegal immigrants rather than native-born workers.[1]

## The claim

ZeroHedge argued that the U.S. labor market had been "appallingly weak," and that most of the jobs recorded in 2023 as evidence of a strong Biden "recovery" were poised to be revised away, pointing to admissions first by the Philadelphia Federal Reserve and later by Bloomberg that payrolls had been overstated by at least a million.[1] More significantly, the outlet contended that all of the job growth of the preceding few years had gone to illegal aliens, a point it said it had first made more than a year earlier and repeated on multiple occasions as native-born employment fell while foreign-born employment reached record highs.[1]

The outlet also highlighted a specific immigration loophole it said allowed illegal immigrants to work in the United States for up to five years, and said it had repeatedly fact-checked the [[joe-biden|Biden]] White House on the subject.[1] Its central complaint in June 2024 was that the topic of most or all U.S. jobs going to illegal immigrants had still not become "the biggest political talking point" of the campaign.[1]

## Context

The item was carried in a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief as a bonus item alongside coverage of the [[trump-hush-money-case|Trump hush money case]], the [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] gun trial, and Biden's Normandy D-Day address, in a framing that treated official economic statistics as unreliable and politically managed.[1]
