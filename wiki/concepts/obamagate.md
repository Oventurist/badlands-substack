# Obamagate

"Obamagate" is the label Badlands Media applies to the alleged Obama-administration role in the conspiracy to surveil, frame and remove President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] In the article "Do You Believe in Coincidences?" it appears alongside [[spygate|Spygate]] as the subject matter of investigations handed from the House of Representatives to the [[united-states-senate|Senate]] after the 2018 midterms, where the Senate Judiciary Committee's oversight of the [[us-department-of-justice|DOJ]] and [[fbi|FBI]] allowed inquiry into "the individual traitors involved."[1]

## Cast of alleged participants

The article names former Attorney General [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]], [[james-comey|James Comey]], [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]] and [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] as figures against whom [[bruce-ohr|Bruce Ohr]] was said to be testifying.[1] Quoted Q material further suggests that Lynch's cooperation would expose the tarmac meeting with Bill Clinton, the handling of the Clinton email case, instructions from Barack Obama regarding that case, and coordination by John Brennan and John McCain "to frame POTUS."[1] Badlands presents these threads as converging into a single narrative of institutional wrongdoing that the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller investigation]] was intended to obscure.[1]
