# 345 Park Avenue Shooting

The **345 Park Avenue shooting** was a deadly mass shooting that took place on Monday, July 28, 2025, at 345 Park Avenue in Midtown Manhattan, one of the most exclusive corporate addresses in New York City.[1] The tower, commonly known as the "Blackstone Building," houses the headquarters of the financial giant [[blackstone|Blackstone]], the [[national-football-league|National Football League]], and the accounting firm [[kpmg|KPMG]], among a range of other corporate tenants.[1]

## The incident

The gunman, identified as [[shane-tamura|Shane Tamura]], opened fire inside the building and was subsequently found dead on the 33rd floor, according to law-enforcement sources cited by the *New York Post*.[1] That floor houses offices for the building's owner, the Rudin real-estate organization, along with other tenants.[1]

## Media coverage and the "white shooter" narrative

Coverage of the shooting was reported in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, under the heading "Inflationary Fact Checks & Golf Force One."[1] Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that when the news first broke, multiple television anchors reported that the shooter was "possibly white," a characterization contradicted by the images of the actual suspect that later circulated.[1] Ashe described the episode as "a fabricated narrative distraction," arguing that the resulting media fight — in which commentators focused on "dunking on CNN for suggesting he was white" — displaced more substantive questions about the gunman's motive, planning timeline, and apparent target list.[1] The commentary characterized the effect as intentional, likening audiences to "cats and laser pointers."[1]
