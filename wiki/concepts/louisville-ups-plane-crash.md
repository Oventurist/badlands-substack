# Louisville UPS Plane Crash

The Louisville UPS plane crash was the crash of a [[united-parcel-service|UPS]] cargo aircraft on Tuesday, November 4, 2025, shortly after it took off from Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport in Louisville, Kentucky, killing at least seven people.[1]

## Crash and casualties

The aircraft was carrying three crew members and struck two businesses near the airport, according to Kentucky Governor [[andy-beshear|Andy Beshear]], who warned that the death toll could rise.[1] Louisville Mayor [[craig-greenberg|Craig Greenberg]] said four of the dead had been on the ground and that eleven other people were injured.[1] The plane exploded into a fireball and left a fiery trail through an industrial area near the airport, and a thick cloud of smoke rose over what is one of the largest air cargo hubs in the United States.[1] Officials issued a shelter-in-place order for people living nearby.[1]

## Reaction

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described dash cam video of the crash and overhead footage as "apocalyptic," called the event a tragedy, and offered prayers for the injured and the families of the deceased while expressing hope that the cause would eventually be determined.[1]
