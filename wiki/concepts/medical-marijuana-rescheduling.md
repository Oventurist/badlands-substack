# Medical Marijuana Rescheduling

Medical marijuana rescheduling refers to the April 2026 decision by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration to move state-licensed medical marijuana from Schedule I to Schedule III under federal drug policy.[1]

## The action

The reclassification did not legalize marijuana at the federal level but eased restrictions on a substance that had occupied the most restrictive category since the 1937 Marijuana Tax Act.[1] Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] said the change allows research on the safety and efficacy of medical marijuana, ultimately providing patients with better care and doctors with more reliable information.[1] He credited President Trump's decisive leadership and referenced a December 2025 executive order that removed federal hurdles to medical marijuana and CBD research while directing an expedited rescheduling process.[1] Blanche announced plans for a new expedited hearing with set deadlines to fully reschedule marijuana in the near future.[1]

## Effects

The change legitimizes medical marijuana programs operating in 40 states, allows tax deductions for state-licensed companies, and establishes a system for state-licensed producers to register with the [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]].[1] It followed years of stalled efforts under the prior administration and aligned with Trump's campaign promises to improve American healthcare through reform.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] situated the move on the heels of the administration's greenlighting of psychedelic research, describing it as easing regulation and enabling mainstream financial and tax changes for THC-based businesses.[1] She predicted that states with marijuana programs would respond by re-regulating, pointing to Colorado, where more than a decade of legal cannabis has been accompanied by annually increasing regulation and taxation of consumers, producers, and retailers.[1] In her view the matter should never have been federal, and the action was a strong indicator of the trajectory on both healthcare reform and "literal liberty."[1]

## Planned rescheduling executive order (December 2025)

In December 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was reported to be preparing to push the federal government to dramatically loosen restrictions on marijuana, reducing oversight of the plant and its derivatives to the same level as some common prescription painkillers and other drugs, according to six people familiar with the discussions.[2] Trump discussed the plan with House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] during a Wednesday phone call from the Oval Office, and was expected to ease access through an executive order directing federal agencies to pursue reclassification.[2]

The move would not legalize or decriminalize marijuana, but it would ease barriers to research and improve the finances of legal cannabis businesses.[2] Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] characterized the plan as a shift from Schedule I to Schedule III and predicted it would "blow so many left wing AND right wing minds."[2]
