# Divine Right of Kings

The Divine Right of Kings is the doctrine that the monarchs of Europe were directly chosen by God to rule humanity — described in [[patriot-chronicles|The Patriot Chronicles]] as the psychological paradigm carefully curated for humanity by "the masters of the Old World."[1]

## Rejection by the American colonists

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]] argued that nearly 170 years of frontier life had forged the American colonists into a new people whose self-reliance and fierce independence burned away the shackles of servitude and empowered them to shed that inherited doctrine.[1] On this reading, [[thomas-jefferson|Thomas Jefferson]]'s [[declaration-of-independence|Declaration of Independence]] was more than a letter of intent for self-governance: it was a signal to the world that the Divine Right of Kings was a lie, and therefore that the Age of Monarchy must end, because no man needs a master.[1]

The essay drew a direct line from that repudiation to the present, holding that America has been fiercely hated ever since by "narcissistic sociopaths with aspirations to rule over others," and suggesting that the enemy defeated in the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]] may be the same one confronting patriots today.[1]
