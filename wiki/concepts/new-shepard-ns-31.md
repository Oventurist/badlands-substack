# New Shepard NS-31

New Shepard NS-31 was a suborbital space tourism flight operated by [[blue-origin|Blue Origin]], scheduled to launch at 9:30 a.m. on Monday, 14 April 2025 from the company's west Texas base, and notable for carrying an all-female crew.[1]

## Crew

The flight's passengers included pop star [[katy-perry|Katy Perry]], [[lauren-sanchez|Lauren Sanchez]], and broadcaster [[gayle-king|Gayle King]], joined by crewmates Amanda Nguyen, Aisha Bowe and Kerianne Flynn.[1] Blue Origin's founder is former Amazon chief [[jeff-bezos|Jeff Bezos]], who was sending his fiancée Sanchez to space as part of the mission.[1]

## Reception

Coverage emphasised the "glammed-up" presentation of the historic all-female tourism flight.[1] Badlands commentary was dismissive, holding that such flights make "spacemen seem unserious."[1]
