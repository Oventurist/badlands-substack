# The Kybalion

The Kybalion is a comparatively recent Hermetic text which, in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]], gives a concise summary of the seven Hermetic principles — the same substance he finds beneath the flowery language and distorted translations of the [[divine-pymander|Divine Pymander]].[1]

## The seven Hermetic principles

As set out in the Kybalion, the principles are:[1]

1. **The Principle of Mentalism** — "The all is mind; the universe is mental."[1]
2. **The Principle of Correspondence** — "As above, so below, as below, so above. As within, so without, as without, so within."[1]
3. **The Principle of Vibration** — "Nothing rests; Everything moves; Everything vibrates."[1]
4. **The Principle of Polarity** — "Everything is dual, everything has poles and everything has its pair of opposites; Like and unlike are the same; Opposites are identical in nature, but different in degree; Extremes meet; All truths, are but half-truths; All paradoxes may be reconciled."[1]
5. **The Principle of Rhythm** — "Everything flows, out and in; Everything has its tides; All things rise and fall."[1]
6. **The Principle of Cause and Effect** — "Every cause has its effect; Every effect has its cause."[1]
7. **The Principle of Gender** — "Gender is in everything; Everything has its masculine and feminine principles."[1]

## Interpretation

DeLarme notes that these principles have been expanded upon by countless commentators, but suggests it is up to the individual to determine what they mean, since any one of them can be applied to almost anything in life and meditated upon.[1] He characterises the study as "an adventure best spent inward, and not tainted by the distortions of others."[1] The second principle restates the dictum "As above, so below" associated with the [[emerald-tablet|Emerald Tablet]] and [[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]].[1]
