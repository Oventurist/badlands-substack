# 2025 US Strikes on Venezuelan Boats

The 2025 US strikes on Venezuelan boats were a series of American military attacks on vessels off [[venezuela|Venezuela]] alleged to be operated by drug cartels, which President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] described as "an act of kindness."[1]

## The strikes

The United States destroyed at least four boats in international waters beginning in September 2025, as Trump continued to accuse Venezuela's left-wing government of using "narco-terrorists" to smuggle drugs into the country.[1] Trump said the operation had saved thousands of lives at home and had eliminated a key sea route used to traffic fentanyl and other drugs into the United States, adding that "nobody wants to go into the water anymore."[1]

## Norfolk remarks

Speaking at the [[us-navy|US Navy]]'s 250th anniversary ceremony in Norfolk, Virginia, on Sunday, October 5, 2025, Trump praised the military for supporting efforts "to blow the cartel terrorists the hell out of the water."[1] "It's a pretty tough thing we've been doing, but you have to think of it this way," he said. "Every one of those boats is responsible for the death of 25,000 American people and the destruction of families. So when you think of it that way, what we are doing is actually an act of kindness."[1]

## Venezuelan response

Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] rejected the allegations that his government runs cartels, saying it had "eliminated all major trafficking networks and vanquished prominent gangs," and accused Washington of using the fight against cartels as a pretext to topple his government and seize Venezuela's natural resources.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the campaign as a war between Trump, the US military and the drug cartels, arguing that anyone who threatens the cartels' cash flow is normally killed, and that Trump was now destroying their vessels and their men in open water.[1] The same commentary saluted retired Chief Petty Officer [[felix-maurizio|Felix Maurizio]], the 100-year-old World War II veteran Trump singled out during the Norfolk speech.[1]
