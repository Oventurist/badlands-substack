# Q Post 238

Q Post 238 is a [[qanon|QAnon]] drop published on November 30, 2017, whose contents name figures and institutions including the Obamas, the Clintons, George Soros, 9/11 and the law firm [[sidley-austin|Sidley Austin]], ending with the phrase "happy hunting."[1]

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote an extended treatment of the drop in September 2024 after a reader asked what he made of it, admitting he had never researched it and that it had gone "in one ear and out the other" when first published.[1] He attributes the oversight partly to having discovered the Q posts about a month after this one appeared, forcing him to skim two months of backlog, and partly to having no personal association with Sidley Austin — unlike the other names in the drop.[1]

## Interpretation

Carlson notes that his instinct on reading "happy hunting" was to look for corruption, but observes that hunting is really about searching for something good, and uses the ambiguity to argue for approaching the drop with an open mind.[1] Following the Sidley Austin thread led him to [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], who worked at the firm's Columbus office, and from there to an extended web of pre-political connections between Vance and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] He does not claim to know what the drop was pointing at, allowing that Q may have been flagging Vance as early as 2017 or may have been leading readers toward something he never found.[1]

The post is also used in the article as a case study in cognitive bias: Carlson argues that readers' attention spikes for names they recognise and slides past those they do not, and that everyone — including members of the "truth community" — protects existing beliefs and remains susceptible to being fooled.[1]
