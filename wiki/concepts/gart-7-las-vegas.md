**GART 7: Las Vegas** was the seventh stop on the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live event series staged by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in Las Vegas, Nevada in the first week of March 2025.[1] It was promoted by Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] in a March 2, 2025 announcement headlined "Last Call for GART: Las Vegas," which billed the weekend as the first GART "of the new American Golden Age."[1]

## Framing

Addressing readers as "Badlanders," Burning Bright wrote that many of them would be joining their favorite Badlands hosts in Las Vegas for the tour's seventh stop.[1] He characterized the GART events generally as a great opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots, and as creating "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[1]

## Ticketing

At the time of the announcement, VIP and full weekend general admission tickets remained available, and a limited number of single-day tickets were offered for attendees close to the area who wished to attend on one or two of Friday, Saturday or Sunday.[1] In-person tickets were sold through the network's TicketSpice storefront, where the pricing breakdown and the contents of each package were listed.[1]

A separate virtual ticket was offered to those who had never attended a GART event in person, providing a stream of the entire event including the Friday, Saturday and Sunday panels.[1] Virtual purchases carried long-term DVR access to panel replays following the weekend, so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss the programming.[1] The virtual ticket also granted access to an exclusive GART Telegram chat, described as a place to spend time with hundreds of fellow Badlanders and Badlands hosts, view behind-the-scenes photos and videos, and "engage in virtual shenanigans galore."[1]

## Reception and sign-off

The announcement closed on behalf of the whole Badlands Media team, expressing hope of seeing familiar faces and, as had happened at every GART to that point, plenty of new ones, and ended with Burning Bright's customary sign-off, "Stay Bright!"[1]
