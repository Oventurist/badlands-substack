# COVID Mandates

**COVID mandates** are treated in "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" as an instrument of ideological warfare rather than as a purely epidemiological measure.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] grouped them with [[critical-race-theory|CRT]] and [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] when instructing ChatGPT to model the infiltration of public education as a covert form of [[neo-marxism|neo-Marxism]] serving the strategic objectives of the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

In the model's synthesis the mandates illustrate the manufactured conflict between individual and community rights: the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]] uses [[esg|ESG]] scores to pressure corporations into supporting policies that prioritise collective safety over individual rights, and COVID mandates and similar measures are presented as necessary for the greater good, thereby advancing the Consortium's agenda.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) This framing is an application of the [[satanic-inversion|principle of inversion]], in which restrictions on liberty are presented as acts of protection.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
