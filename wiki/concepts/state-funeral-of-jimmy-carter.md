# State Funeral of Jimmy Carter

The state funeral of former President [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]] was held on Thursday, January 9, 2025, at the Washington National Cathedral in Washington, D.C., and became notable less for its liturgy than for the rare assembly of all five living American presidents in a single pew.[1]

## Attendance

President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] joined the other four living American presidents at the service, placing him side by side with Democratic predecessors who had publicly described him as a threat to democracy.[1] Trump had long been an outsider among the living presidents and had previously skipped events attended by other members of what has been called the world's most exclusive club: he did not attend the 2020 services for civil rights icon and former Representative John Lewis of Georgia, who was eulogized by former Presidents [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]]; Carter, then 95, also did not attend but sent a letter that was read aloud.[1] Obama, Bush and Clinton attended the 2018 services for the late Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] of Arizona, to which Trump was not invited, and Trump did not attend the 2018 funeral of former first lady Barbara Bush, where then-first lady [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] joined the Obamas, the Clintons, George W. Bush and [[george-hw-bush|George H. W. Bush]].[1]

All living former first ladies attended the Carter funeral except [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]], whose spokesperson cited "scheduling conflicts."[1] Journalist Jeff Zeleny reported on CNN that she remained in Hawaii, and her office stated that she sent her "thoughts and prayers to the Carter family, and everyone who loved and learned from the remarkable former President."[1] Because of her absence, Barack Obama was seated between Trump and former first lady Laura Bush, with the former presidents and first ladies occupying the second row of the cathedral.[1]

## The Trump–Obama exchange

Video footage from before the service showed Barack Obama and Trump smiling and laughing together, imagery that commentators suggested would likely not have occurred had Michelle Obama been present in the adjacent seat.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry offered a shot-by-shot reading of the funeral footage, arguing that the body language told the real story.[1] In that account, Trump shook hands with former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] while Karen Pence avoided eye contact with both Donald and Melania Trump; the Trumps sat in silence as the surrounding group of peers ignored them; and Obama shook hands with everyone except Trump before sitting next to him, whereupon Trump engaged him in conversation and made him laugh.[1] The commentary described [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and Laura Bush walking past Trump without acknowledgment, [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] exchanging pleasantries while [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] sat stone-faced, and an awkward arrival by [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and her husband Doug Emhoff that visibly discomfited the group.[1] The writer praised Melania Trump's composure over the preceding decade and contrasted the atmosphere with the funeral of [[george-hw-bush|George H. W. Bush]], where attendees were cordial with the Trumps before the widely discussed distribution of mysterious white envelopes, noting that while Mike Pence received an envelope at that earlier service, Jimmy Carter did not.[1]
