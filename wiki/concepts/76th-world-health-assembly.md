# 76th World Health Assembly

The 76th World Health Assembly (WHA) was the annual governing meeting of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], held in Geneva, Switzerland from May 21 to May 30, 2023.[1] It concluded without ratifying either the proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] or the package of amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]] (IHR).[1]

## Proceedings

Although neither instrument was adopted, the meetings included announcements of new WHO bodies created to respond to pandemic threats and new calls to "restrict personal liberties" during health emergencies.[1] A revised "bureau's text" of the pandemic treaty dated May 22, 2023 was circulated at the assembly, while no updated document for the proposed IHR amendments was made available.[1] WHO Director-General [[tedros-adhanom-ghebreyesus|Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus]] urged member states to reach agreement on both instruments by 2024.[1]

## Criticism

Independent journalist [[james-roguski|James Roguski]], who has tracked the treaty and IHR negotiations closely, described the assembly as "a big theatrical production" and "a dog and pony show," warning that focusing on procedural minutiae obscures the fact that the documents are still being negotiated.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that the proposed amendments would give the WHO the ability to supersede the authority of national governments during a future pandemic, and argued the can had simply been kicked down the road to 2024.[1] He speculated that if a new pandemic arrived near the 2024 election, adopting the instruments shortly beforehand would leave little time for public scrutiny or pushback.[1]
