# Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974

Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 is the U.S. statutory authority under which the [[office-of-the-united-states-trade-representative|Office of the United States Trade Representative]] investigates foreign acts, policies and practices that burden or restrict American commerce and imposes responsive trade measures.[1]

In July 2026 the Trump administration invoked Section 301, at the direction of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], to impose a 25% tariff on certain Brazilian imports after a yearlong USTR investigation found several Brazilian policies unreasonable and harmful to American businesses, workers and farmers.[1] The statutory process included a determination in June 2026, review of more than 360 public comments, and a July hearing with 77 witnesses before the tariffs were finalized.[1] U.S. Trade Representative [[jamieson-greer|Jamieson Greer]] presented the action as an application of the administration's "America First" trade policies.[1]
