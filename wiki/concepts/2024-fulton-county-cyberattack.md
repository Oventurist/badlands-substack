# 2024 Fulton County Cyberattack

The 2024 Fulton County cyberattack was a cybersecurity incident that struck government systems in [[fulton-county|Fulton County]], Georgia, over the weekend of January 27–28, 2024, affecting the office of the district attorney prosecuting former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on election interference charges.[1]

All desktop phones, the county intranet and devices relying on county servers went down across every department, including the office of District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]], according to a county official with knowledge of the situation.[1] County employees received an email notification about the outage on Monday.[1]

Fulton County Board of Commissioners Chairman [[robb-pitts|Robb Pitts]] confirmed at a Monday afternoon news briefing that the outage — which affected the county's phones, courts and tax systems — "was a result of a cybersecurity incident" and that law enforcement was investigating.[1] "We do not yet have a specific time frame for when these systems will be restored," Pitts said, adding: "At this time, we are not aware of any transfer of sensitive information about citizens or employees, but we will continue to look carefully at this issue."[1]
