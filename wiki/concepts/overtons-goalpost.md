# Overton's Goalpost

Overton's Goalpost is an analytical framework advanced by Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] in 2023 to describe how the establishment is compelled to abandon previously held narrative territory as what he calls the Collective Mindscape continues to shift.[1]

## The framework

In the model, each concession made by institutional actors — each [[deep-state-burn-cards|burn card]] played, each sacrificial figure paraded before the cameras, each hill of rhetorical ground conceded under the banner of "justice served" — represents permanently lost terrain in what Burning Bright identifies as the true battlespace: the Collective Mind itself.[1] The enemy "can no longer deny the existence of the web," so the admission comes "in the most limited, theatrical and carefully-calibrated fashion possible," while the establishment fights to dictate the terms, scope and direction of that admission.[1] The framework's central claim is that "retreat is retreat, and Overton's Goalpost is not a process that can be reversed."[1]

## Application to the Andrew arrest

Burning Bright applied the framework to the February 2026 arrest of [[prince-andrew|Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor]], arguing that although the [[misconduct-in-public-office|misconduct in public office]] charge amounts to a controlled admission rather than genuine accountability, the strategic picture remains optimistic because the ground conceded cannot be reclaimed.[1] The concept is closely related to the wider Badlands treatment of the [[information-war|information war]] and the [[truth-community|Truth Community]]'s reading of managed disclosure.[1]

## Application to Nord Stream admissions (November 2023)

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] applied the concept in November 2023 to mainstream reporting on the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream]] sabotage, writing that "any Media admissions regarding NS2 and Ukraine are examples of Overton's Goalpost, so it's a net positive."[2] At the same time he warned that the incremental concession should not be allowed to become a vehicle for pinning intelligence-agency operations onto proxies, concluding that "Deflection = Defeat."[2]

## Application to the Durham Report (May 2023)

Following the release of the [[durham-report|Durham Report]] in May 2023, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] restated the framework as a five-step sequence: assert the thing is false and suppress evidence to the contrary; admit aspects of it are true while dismissing the whole picture; admit it is mostly true but insist it does not matter; admit it is entirely true and damning while claiming everyone already knew and that conspiracy theorists were right only by accident; and finally deploy kinetic or psychological distraction operations.[3] He argued the dialectic was being deployed in full force in coverage of the Durham Report, and told readers not to be upset because its appearance meant the Truth Community was winning.[3]

## Application to the June 2023 Ukraine coverage

In June 2023 [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked the term to describe the near-simultaneous mainstream admissions that Ukrainian fighters wore Nazi insignia and that Ukrainians were behind the attack on [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream 2]], after more than a year of unqualified media defense of Ukraine and [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]].[4] He characterized this "admission" phase as a retreat from the collectivists' previously approved public stance, one the enemy makes "only when they're forced to," while cautioning that it does not deliver the whole truth — noting that Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]] had publicly blamed the [[cia|CIA]] for the Nord Stream sabotage.[4] He tied the dynamic to [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] and the obfuscation of information itself, which he has elsewhere called the "War of Stories," and argued the episode showed that the Truth Community's side has "ghosts in the machine" too.[4]

## Application to COVID-19 messaging (2023)

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked Overton's Goalpost in September 2023 to describe establishment messaging on "COVID-23," mask mandates and lockdowns.[5] In the same week, [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] claimed masks do not work "for the public at large" but do work on an "individual basis," while [[deborah-birx|Deborah Birx]] told [[newsmax|Newsmax]] "We don't need to mandate" and called for empowering families to decide for themselves.[5] Burning Bright treated the contradictory positioning as a managed retreat of the acceptable-opinion boundary rather than a genuine concession, summarizing it as "Retreat is Defeat in the Mind War."[5]

## Application to Ukraine and Israel coverage (2023)

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued in December 2023 that the Media Machine had taken more than 18 months to begin moving the goalposts on the Ukraine war, but under two months to do so on Israel's campaign in Gaza — an acceleration he read as evidence of lost narrative control and of mass-psychological metrics inverting in favor of the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]].[6]

## Covid origins as a moving goalpost (2023)

Commenting on the [[covid-19-origin-act-of-2023|COVID-19 Origin Act of 2023]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the continual shifting of the officially acceptable position on the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]] as "the clearest example yet of Overton's Goalpost," arguing that each forced concession of previously withheld intelligence signals a loss of ground in the Mind War.[7]
