# Gallup Political Ideology Gender Gap

The **Gallup political ideology gender gap** refers to findings published by [[gallup|Gallup]] in February 2024 showing that American women have become substantially more liberal since the 1990s while men's ideological self-identification has remained largely stable.[1]

## Findings

Gallup's national figures continued to show a center-right country, with more Americans identifying as conservative (36 percent) than liberal (25 percent) and the remainder describing themselves as moderate (36 percent).[1] The liberal share, however, had inched up over three decades and stood one percentage point shy of its all-time high, a shift driven by contraction of the moderate category while the conservative share varied narrowly around its long-term average near 38 percent.[1]

Breaking the trend down by age and gender revealed that the leftward movement was concentrated among women in certain age groups.[1] The findings rested on annual averages of Gallup telephone surveys generally encompassing 12,000 or more interviews with US adults each year, with at least 500 adults in each gender-by-age subgroup, and analyzed each age group at the time of survey rather than tracking cohorts over time.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] read the data as "statistical evidence that we actually have a Karen problem," describing a bloc of angry middle-aged liberal women unknowingly fostering a communist revolution.[1] He pointed to fathers such as Harrison Tinsley and Adam Vena, both engaged in protracted legal battles with ex-wives seeking to transition their young sons, as concrete consequences of engineered ideological extremism spreading through the female population.[1]

Esler's practical question was strategic rather than dismissive: how might the data be used to win hearts and minds, given that most of these women believe they are acting kindly and virtuously rather than advancing Neo-Marxism.[1] Setting aside the ideologically captured, he argued the information could become strategically important for those focused on local politics ahead of the 2024 election.[1]
