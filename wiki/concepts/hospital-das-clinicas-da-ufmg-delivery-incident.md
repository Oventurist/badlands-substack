# Hospital das Clínicas da UFMG Delivery Incident

The Hospital das Clínicas da UFMG delivery incident refers to allegations, reported in May 2023, that an obstetrician at the Hospital das Clínicas da UFMG in the Santa Efigênia neighborhood of Belo Horizonte — the capital of Minas Gerais state in [[brazil|Brazil]] — decapitated a baby during delivery while the child's father watched.[1]

## Allegations

According to reports, 33-year-old Ranielly Coelho Santos was admitted to the hospital on April 28, 2023, and her labor was induced before the unnamed obstetrician arrived in the delivery room.[1] The obstetrician reportedly called the father closer to observe the birth, then climbed onto the pregnant woman's belly and pulled the baby's head off during the delivery.[1] The doctor was placed under investigation.[1] The account was carried by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and reproduced in the *[[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]]*.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] called it "one of the most horrific headlines I have ever encountered," and linked it rhetorically to abortion legislation in American blue states that he characterized as permitting killing "up to and after birth," describing the Brazilian case as a further step "towards a Babylonian agenda."[1] He raised concern about hospitals seeking waivers and liability release forms from parents that could, in his view, cover up and bury such crimes, and argued that "if it's happening in Brazil, it can happen here or anywhere."[1] He closed by urging a hard look at what he described as the destruction of youth worldwide, writing that children "are our most important asset, which we must always fight for," and that "we have the power to end it."[1]
