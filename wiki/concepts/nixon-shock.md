# 1971 Abandonment of the Gold Standard

In 1971 the United States ended the convertibility of the dollar into gold, terminating the gold peg established under the [[bretton-woods-agreement|Bretton Woods Agreement]] and converting the world's reserve currency into a [[fiat-currency|fiat currency]] backed by nothing.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

In the January 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Master and Commander," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] asserts that the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]] were behind the US decision to abandon the [[gold-standard|gold standard]] in 1971.[1] He argues that at that moment they gained full control of the printing of currency and the issuance of debt.[1] US debt has done nothing but grow since, in his account reaching almost 32 trillion dollars, with the rest of the world likewise drowning in obligations that were never going to be repaid.[1]

## Significance in the debt-collapse thesis

The 1971 break is presented as the structural precondition for the [[global-debt-system|global central bank debt system]]'s eventual bankruptcy: without a metallic anchor, borrowing could expand indefinitely until interest payments alone became unpayable.[1] It also underpins the essay's reading of the renewed central bank and [[brics|BRICS]] gold buying of 2022–2023 as a movement back toward hard-asset backing.[1]
