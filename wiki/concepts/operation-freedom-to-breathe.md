# Operation Freedom to Breathe

"Operation Freedom to Breathe" was an initiative launched by the Biden administration in September 2021 that placed opponents of COVID-19 mask mandates on federal watchlists, according to internal documents reported by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and summarized in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of 2 October 2025.[1]

## Scope

The ironically named operation targeted 19 mask-mandate opponents, more than half of whom were barred from flying despite facing no criminal charges.[1] Some individuals remained on the list until the federal transportation mask mandate ended in April 2022.[1] Officials at the [[transportation-security-administration|Transportation Security Administration]] warned internally that the effort appeared to punish political expression rather than address transportation security.[1]

## Methodology and errors

Emails showed that the TSA relied on academic databases and social media rather than intelligence agencies to identify targets, which led to errors including the wrongful inclusion of a National Guardsman and the wife of an air marshal.[1]

## Related watchlisting

The same internal documents showed that the TSA and the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] placed roughly 280 people linked to the January 6 Capitol protests on watchlists, with at least five placed on the no-fly list typically reserved for suspected terrorists — described by TSA sources as the agency's most sweeping use of travel restrictions against U.S. citizens.[1] Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] has said she was placed on the TSA's "Quiet Skies" watchlist after criticizing then–Vice President Kamala Harris.[1]

## Aftermath

Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] denounced the actions as an abuse of power and referred the matter to the Department of Justice and to [[congress|Congress]].[1] Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] commented that the truly targeted parties would eventually be revealed as the same set of influencers he associates with the Epstein Files binder, Twitter Files and TPUSA operations.[1]
