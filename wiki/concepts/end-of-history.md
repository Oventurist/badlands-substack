# End of History

"The end of history" is the proposition, associated with post–Cold War liberal triumphalism, that the collapse of Soviet communism marked the final resolution of ideological conflict and the universal destination of liberal democracy and market integration.

## Rubio's repudiation at Munich

The February 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0a2|Badlands Brief]] reports that Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] made the phrase the organizing target of his address to the [[munich-security-conference|Munich Security Conference]], arguing that it named not an insight but a delusion.

In the passage quoted by the Brief, Rubio traced the arc from the conference's 1963 founding — amid a divided Germany, a two-year-old Berlin Wall, and the recent memory of the Cuban Missile Crisis — through the fall of that wall and the reunification of East and West. He then argued that "the euphoria of this triumph led us to a dangerous delusion: that we had entered, quote, 'the end of history;' that every nation would now be a liberal democracy; that the ties formed by trade and by commerce alone would now replace nationhood; that the [[rules-based-international-order|rules-based global order]] – an overused term – would now replace the national interest; and that we would now live in a world without borders where everyone became a citizen of the world."

The formulation bundles four distinct claims that Rubio rejects together: the inevitability of liberal democracy, the substitution of commerce for nationhood, the primacy of procedural international order over national interest, and the dissolution of borders into world citizenship. In place of these he advanced an emphasis on national sovereignty, border security, and allied burden-sharing within [[nato|NATO]].

## Significance in the corpus

Commentators described the speech as a formal articulation of a [[maga|MAGA]] foreign-policy doctrine. Within the Badlands corpus the repudiation of the end-of-history thesis connects to longstanding themes of [[sovereign-disentanglement|sovereign disentanglement]], criticism of the [[new-world-order|New World Order]], and the emergence of a [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar]] system replacing unipolar globalism.
