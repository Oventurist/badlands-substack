# COVFEFE Act of 2017

The **COVFEFE Act of 2017** — formally the Communications Over Various Feeds Electronically For Engagement Act of 2017, introduced in the 115th Congress as House Bill 2884 — is legislation concerning the treatment of electronic communications, named after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[covfefe|covfefe]] Tweet.[1]

## Role in the COVFEFE Dig

In the Badlands Media feature "The COVFEFE Dig," [[gmoney|GMONEY]] cites the bill as a piece of the wider puzzle precisely because it "talks about communications."[1] The author uses it as a hinge to introduce the article's communications thesis: if there truly was a plan to end the central bankers' and Rothschild-controlled [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] system, the participants would have needed a channel that was un-hackable and could not be intercepted, since Trump could not simply pick up a telephone and call people.[1]

From that premise the dig argues that [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is not only a monetary system but a secure communications system, and that messages could be embedded in ordinary files through steganography — the practice of hiding secret data inside a non-secret file or message to avoid detection — perhaps in an image of [[pepe-the-frog|Pepe]].[1] The author points to [[qanon|Q]] drop 2712, which features a Pepe image and discusses secure communications, as corroboration.[1]
