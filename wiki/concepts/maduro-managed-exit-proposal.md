# Maduro Managed Exit Proposal

The Maduro managed exit proposal refers to reporting in November 2025 that Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] would be open to leaving power and going into exile if the United States guaranteed amnesty for him and his top lieutenants.[1]

## Background

The previous week, Maduro had reached out to [[russia|Russia]], [[china|China]] and [[iran|Iran]] for possible military aid after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] mulled a military attack amid a massive buildup of American forces in the region.[1] The Washington Post reported that the request to Moscow took the form of a letter intended for President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], to be delivered during a visit to the Russian capital by a senior aide that month, and that Maduro also wrote to Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] seeking "expanded military cooperation" to counter "the escalation between the U.S. and Venezuela."[1]

## The reported offer

The Atlantic subsequently reported that Maduro would be open to a managed exit or exile provided that the United States offered amnesty for him and his top lieutenants, lifted its bounties, and facilitated a comfortable exile.[1] A person with dealings in both countries was quoted saying that "if there is enough pressure, and if there is enough candy in the dish... everything is on the table with Maduro."[1] ZeroHedge cautioned that the managed-exit reporting should be viewed with a grain of salt given the source and timing.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that audiences were "being prepped for a Syria re-run in [[venezuela|Venezuela]]," in which a leader he characterized as aligned with a sovereign alliance would be warmly exiled so as to permit kinetic operations against the terror cells he had been fighting.[1] He noted that both storylines involve the Russians facilitating the exile, concluding: "Same same."[1]
