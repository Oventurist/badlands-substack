# Stockton 7-Eleven Shoplifting Takedown

The **Stockton 7-Eleven shoplifting takedown** was an August 2023 incident in Stockton, Northern California, in which a convenience store clerk physically subdued a man who was threatening staff and filling a barrel with stolen goods, and was subsequently placed under criminal investigation for assault by police.[1]

## The incident

According to reporting cited by Summit News, the would-be thief had visited the 7-Eleven three times on the evening in question, each time threatening the shopkeepers and stealing from them.[1] On the third attempt the clerk took him down with a stick, an episode captured on video in which the man had threatened to pull a gun while loading a barrel full of products.[1] Rather than the thief facing charges, police stepped in to criminally investigate the shopkeeper for assault.[1]

The video went viral, with public support running overwhelmingly in favor of the shop owner, who became known online as "stick guy."[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that for those angry about the decriminalization of crime, "stick guy" might not be the hero they expected or wanted, but was "most certainly the hero we need."[1] She noted that the thief had stolen from the store twice earlier that day and had threatened the owners with a gun before the takedown.[1]

Ashe observed that in California shop owners are not permitted to stop shoplifters, so the owner was placed under investigation while, under the law, retailers must absorb the losses because theft has effectively been decriminalized.[1] She questioned whether a rule that "protects the criminal and harms the victim" can properly be called a law, and argued that if there is to be any hope of the public respecting the rule of law, the shop owner must be absolved and the shoplifter prosecuted — while adding that she was "not holding my breath."[1]
