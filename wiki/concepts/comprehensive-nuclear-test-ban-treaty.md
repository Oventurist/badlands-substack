# Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty

The Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty (CTBT), adopted in 1996, bans all nuclear explosions anywhere in the world, but has never been fully implemented because a number of key states have not ratified it.[1] As of late 2023 the treaty remained unratified by the United States, China, India, Pakistan, North Korea, Israel, Iran and Egypt.[1]

## Russian de-ratification, 2023

On Thursday, November 2, 2023, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] signed a bill revoking Russia's ratification of the treaty, a step Moscow said was necessary to establish parity with the United States.[1] Putin had argued that rescinding ratification would "mirror" the American position, since Washington signed but never ratified the accord.[1] Both houses of the Russian parliament had voted the previous month to revoke ratification.[1]
