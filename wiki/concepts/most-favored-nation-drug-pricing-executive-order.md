# Most-Favored-Nation Drug Pricing Executive Order

The Most-Favored-Nation drug pricing executive order was a policy initiative announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in May 2025 that sought to tie United States prescription drug prices to the lowest price paid by any other country in the world.[1]

## Announcement

Trump said he planned to sign an executive order cutting prescription drug prices by mandating that the United States pay the same price for drugs as whichever country pays the lowest price globally.[1] In a social media post on his Truth Social platform, he stated he would sign the order at 9 a.m. Monday, Washington time, and predicted that pharmaceutical prices could fall by 30% to 80%.[1]

## Ambiguities

Trump's post did not detail how the order would function in practice.[1] It also did not specify potential limits on the policy — for example, whether it would apply only to government programs such as Medicare and Medicaid, or whether the White House saw a mechanism for applying the benchmark more broadly across the commercial market.[1]
