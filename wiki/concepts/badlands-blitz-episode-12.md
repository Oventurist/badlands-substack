# Badlands Blitz Episode 12

**Badlands Blitz Episode 12**, subtitled "The Fake and Gay, but Also Real and Serious War," is the twelfth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly recap program published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on its Substack. The post is dated March 28, 2026 and had accumulated 210 likes at the time of the source posting. As with earlier episodes, the announcement addresses readers as "Badlanders" and describes the program as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."

## Hosts

The episode is co-hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], both regular contributors to the Badlands Substack, whose Substack profiles are linked from the announcement post.

## Themes

The framing text is written in a deliberately arch register. According to the source, the hosts use "the dueling narratives of the fake and gay, but also real and possibly not gay (but also, according to Trump, actually probably gay, according to what he's been told,) Ayatollah as a jumping off point." The reference is to competing claims circulating about the condition and status of the Iranian Supreme Leader, [[ali-khamenei|Ayatollah Ali Khamenei]], during the escalating conflict involving [[iran|Iran]] — claims that the post presents as unresolved and mutually contradictory, with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] himself cited as relaying secondhand information ("according to what he's been told"). The phrase "fake and gay" is longstanding online shorthand within the community for a story judged to be staged or fabricated; the episode's subtitle plays on the possibility that a war can be simultaneously theatrical in its presentation and materially real in its consequences.

From that starting point the episode turns to what the source calls "an otherwise very serious and real conversation about [[narrative-warfare|Narrative Warfare]], [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]] and [[first-principles|First Principles]] in a world—and a [[truth-community|Truth Community]]—that has quite forgotten them." The construction is pointedly self-critical: the post suggests that the alternative-media audience the outlet serves has itself lost hold of the analytical discipline it claims, and that the confusion surrounding the Ayatollah story is an illustration of that failure rather than merely an example of enemy deception.

## Publication and support model

The post repeats the outlet's standard appeal: all Badlands content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free, and the team is able to pay itself for the time only through readers who choose to support the work directly. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription, cancellable at any time, in recognition of the daily output and the "step-up in said work for 2026." The episode closes with the customary sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
