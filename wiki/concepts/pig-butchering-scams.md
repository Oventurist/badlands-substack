# Pig Butchering Scams

"Pig butchering" is the common name for a category of cryptocurrency investment fraud in which perpetrators cultivate a relationship with a victim over time before inducing a large transfer of funds that is then stolen.[1] The term featured centrally in the October 2025 federal indictment of [[chen-zhi|Chen Zhi]] and the civil forfeiture action against assets of [[prince-holding-group|Prince Holding Group]].[1]

## Mechanics

According to Justice Department filings, malicious actors contact unwitting victims through messaging or social media applications and convince them to transfer [[bitcoin|cryptocurrency]] to specified accounts on false promises that the funds will be invested and generate profits.[1] In reality the funds are stolen and laundered for the benefit of the perpetrators.[1] The scam operators frequently build relationships with victims over an extended period, earning their trust before taking the money — the "fattening" stage that gives the practice its name.[1]

## Forced labor dimension

A defining feature of the industry as described by prosecutors is that many of the individual scammers are themselves trafficking victims. The [[fbi|FBI]] alleges that Prince Group carried out its schemes by trafficking hundreds of workers and forcing them to work in compounds in [[cambodia|Cambodia]] and execute the scams, often under the threat of violence.[1] Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] characterized the resulting enterprise as "a criminal empire built on forced labor and deception."[1]

## Scale

Prosecutors said the schemes stole billions of dollars from victims in the United States and around the world, and that the industry is "reaching epidemic proportions."[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] speculated that widely circulated Telegram solicitations impersonating public figures — including fake accounts posing as Badlands contributors — fit the same pattern.[1]
