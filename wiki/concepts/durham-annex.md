# Durham Annex

The Durham annex is the classified annex to the report of Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]], containing underlying intelligence related to the origins of the Russia collusion investigation and long anticipated by researchers as a pending declassification.[1]

## Ratcliffe's account

In a Fox News "Sunday Morning Futures" interview with Maria Bartiromo in July 2025, CIA Director [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]] said that material released the previous week had shown how [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], [[james-clapper|James Clapper]] and [[james-comey|James Comey]] pushed the known-fake [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]] into intelligence community assessments, forming the basis for [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]].[1] He said the underlying intelligence he had spent months making declassification recommendations on — sent to the [[justice-department|Department of Justice]] — would come out in the Durham report classified annex.[1]

Ratcliffe said the intelligence shows that part of the effort was "a [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] plan," and part was an FBI plan to act as an accelerant to the fake Steele dossier and the fake Russia collusion claims by "pouring oil on the fire, by amplifying the lie and burying the truth of what Hillary Clinton was up to."[1] He added that Clinton testified before Durham under oath in 2022 and that much of that testimony is "frankly completely inconsistent" with the underlying intelligence about to be declassified in the annex.[1]

## Anticipated release

[[john-solomon|John Solomon]] said on Fox News on Tuesday, July 29, 2025, that the annex — reported to include foreign intelligence effectively "calling" Russiagate before it happened — might be released as early as the following day.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] speculated about what competing news events might be used as distraction from the release, given the simultaneous Pacific tsunami and Manhattan shooting.[1]

## Related disclosures

Commentary linked the annex to Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]]'s declassification of the [[clinton-annex|Clinton Annex]], said to reveal thumb drives of Obama-era secrets and shields erected by the DNC, with Comey's FBI prioritizing cover-ups over justice.[1]

## Declassification and contents (July 31, 2025)

The annex was declassified on Thursday, July 31, 2025.[2] Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] said on Fox News that day that the newly released annex reveals the [[fbi|FBI]] had information a decade ago "that they never followed up on," and that the agency "had a hand in" attempts to "stop Trump" through the Russia collusion hoax.[2] Grassley suggested the annex "brings attention to the fact that there was either a Clinton conspiracy to make this happen, or Russian disinformation," but that "either way, it was an attempt to stop Trump."[2]

According to the annex, FBI officials found two Russian-language memos and emails purportedly hacked by "individuals affiliated with Russian intelligence services" to be "likely not be credible."[2] One of those documents indicated that the Clinton campaign was routinely obtaining information from Attorney General [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] about "the plans and intentions of the FBI."[2] The record as quoted in Badlands commentary states that the FBI was fully alerted to the possibility that some of the information it was receiving about the Trump campaign originated either with the Clinton campaign and its supporters or with Russian disinformation, yet appears to have dismissed the "T1" information as not credible without taking any investigative steps to corroborate or disprove the allegations.[2]

Also surfaced in connection with the release was an email from [[leonard-benardo|Leonard Benardo]], presented as showing that Hillary Clinton approved the plan to vilify [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]; intelligence analysts assessed the Benardo emails as likely authentic and confirmed that Russian services had hacked them.[2] The email's stated goal was to build enough attention around Russian interference for it to be treated as a critical infrastructure threat, enabling the Obama administration and the intelligence community to "speed up searching for evidence that is regrettably still unavailable" — a sequence that matched the Department of Homeland Security's January 6, 2017 designation of election infrastructure as critical infrastructure, and the email's prediction that CrowdStrike would "supply the media."[2]

## Reaction

The declassification came one day after an op-ed by [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] and [[james-clapper|James Clapper]] defending the 2017 intelligence community assessment, which Badlands commentary characterized as an ill-timed public relations move amounting to damage control.[2]
