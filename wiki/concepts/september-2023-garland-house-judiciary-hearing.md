# September 2023 Garland House Judiciary Hearing

The September 2023 Garland House Judiciary hearing was an oversight session held on Wednesday, September 20, 2023, at which Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] testified before the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] for roughly five hours.[1] Republican lawmakers used the high-stakes hearing to attack the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s handling of criminal investigations into former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s son [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], the events of January 6 and other high-profile matters.[1]

## Republican allegations

Chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] (R-OH) opened the hearing by declaring, "The fix is in," adding that "even with the face-saving indictment of Hunter Biden last week, everyone knows the fix is in."[1] Jordan repeatedly accused Garland of "slow walking" the Hunter Biden investigation for the benefit of President Biden.[1]

Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] (R-KY) confronted Garland in a fiery exchange over statements that appeared to conflict with his prior testimony.[1] Rep. [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] (R-FL) pressed the Attorney General on the relationship between Hunter and Joe Biden, focusing on the art-dealing arrangement; Garland confirmed that he had neither asked questions about nor investigated the matter, which [[badlands-media|Badlands]] commentary characterized as bearing the classic hallmarks of influence peddling and money laundering.[1] Rep. [[kevin-kiley|Kevin Kiley]] (R-CA) pinned Garland down on the origins of the [[doj-school-board-memo-2021|school board memo]], with the Attorney General citing mainstream media coverage as the cause for scrutiny of parents at school board meetings and confirming that he had not consulted [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] before issuing it.[1]

## Garland's defense

In his opening statement Garland took criticism of his tenure head-on, arguing that Republican efforts to target career officials were "dangerous" at a time when threats against public servants were rising.[1] "We will not be intimidated," he said. "We will do our jobs free from outside interference. And we will not back down from defending our democracy."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that listening to Garland speak raised her blood pressure but that Republican members went hard at the Attorney General, generating several viral moments in which Garland fumbled over his words and appeared to perjure himself relative to prior statements.[1] She concluded that while the hearing was theater, it was gratifying to see Garland sweat, and a reminder that he had not been confirmed to the Supreme Court — a slight about which, she argued, he remained bitter.[1]
