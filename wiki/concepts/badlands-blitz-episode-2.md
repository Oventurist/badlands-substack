# Badlands Blitz Episode 2

"Badlands Blitz Episode 2," subtitled "Info War Victory Laps & the Dismantling of Legacy Media," is the second installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video program published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack. Dated January 17, 2026, one week after the show's debut, the accompanying post records 311 likes.

## Purpose and format

The announcement reiterates the program's stated remit, describing The Blitz as a show "intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." The episode marks the Blitz debut of [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as a host, and the article says the two presenters use her first appearance to discuss "the original intentions behind the recent changes made around these parts," how the editorial vision for both the Brief and the Blitz might evolve, and how the audience fits into that evolution.

On running time, the post states that the show is "intended to get CLOSE to being a 60-minute show," while allowing the hosts "a bit of a buffer" as they solidify the format and incorporate audience feedback. As in the debut, a recurring segment involves dipping into the newsletter's comments section to review what the article calls the "beautiful savages" of the readership have been saying during the week.

## Content of the episode

According to the source, the hosts "both endorse and criticize the idea of Info War Victory Laps" — the practice among independent commentators of publicly claiming vindication when events appear to confirm earlier predictions. The article says the hosts then take "a couple of our own" such victory laps, specifically regarding what it terms "Venezuelan Proxy Princesses" and "high-stakes US court cases." The episode also covers the hosts' assessment of how the [[information-war|information war]] has evolved and, in the article's phrasing, "Devolved in places," together with a discussion of what participants can do about that deterioration.

## Subscription model and appeals

The post closes with the network's standard framing of its business model. It states that Badlands Media intends to serve the Substack audience with exclusive content during 2026, describing those subscribers as among the outlet's "most loyal and engaged." All Badlands content, including The Blitz, is said to remain free; the article states that the only way the organization can pay its contributors for their time is through voluntary reader support, and invites readers to upgrade to a paid subscription, cancellable at any time. Readers are also asked to share the show and to comment. The post signs off with the network's recurring exhortation to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright."
