# Michigan State University Shooting

The Michigan State University shooting was a mass shooting that took place on the evening of Monday, February 13, 2023, when a gunman opened fire inside an academic hall on the university's campus in East Lansing, Michigan.[1] Initial police reports cited by FOX News indicated that at least five people were wounded in the attack.[1]

## Immediate aftermath

All five victims — some of whom sustained wounds described as life-threatening — were transported to Sparrow Hospital for treatment.[1] As of 11:30 p.m. on the night of the shooting the suspect remained at large, and police described him as a short Black male wearing black pants, a blue jacket, and red shoes.[1] Hundreds of law enforcement officers drawn from several different agencies participated in the manhunt, and the university issued a shelter-in-place order for the campus while the search was underway.[1]
