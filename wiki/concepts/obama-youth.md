# Obama Youth

**"Obama Youth"** is a polemical term coined by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in a November 3, 2023 article to describe Americans who came of age during the presidency of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and who, in his argument, were shaped by a state-sanctioned propaganda environment analogous to that of the [[hitler-youth|Hitler Youth]].[1] The article's subtitle framed the comparison directly: "It's Getting Harder to Tell the Difference".[1]

## The polling evidence cited

Carlson's case rested on a [[harvard-harris-poll|Harvard/Harris]] survey of 2,116 Americans taken October 18–19, 2023 — eleven to twelve days after the initial Hamas attacks on Israeli citizens and one to two days after the alleged hospital bombing in Gaza.[1] Asked whether they sided more with Israel or [[hamas|Hamas]], 48% of the 18–24 age group sided more with Hamas, against 5% of respondents aged 65 and over.[1] Asked whether the Hamas killing of 1,200 Israeli civilians could be justified by Palestinian grievances, 51% of the 18–24 group said it could, against 9% of the 65+ group.[1] Asked about the [[al-ahli-baptist-hospital-explosion|Gaza hospital explosion]], 45% of the youngest cohort attributed it to an Israeli airstrike, against 13% of the oldest.[1]

Carlson emphasised that all other age cohorts polled tracked closer to the older group than to the 18–24 group, which he read as evidence that the youngest cohort had been intentionally targeted through propaganda.[1] He noted that media reporting on October 17 initially treated Israeli responsibility for the hospital strike as verified fact, that by that evening a Hamas rocket falling short appeared more likely, and that by the next morning it was clearer still that a rocket had struck a parking lot outside the hospital — yet 3.5 times more young respondents than old still blamed Israel.[1] He left open whether the younger cohort disbelieved the correction or never encountered it.[1]

## The propaganda-legalisation argument

Carlson tied the divergence to the repeal of the [[smith-mundt-act|Smith-Mundt Act]] under Obama, which he described as making propaganda against Americans effectively legal again shortly after Obama's re-election, and observed that transgender bathroom policy entered mainstream conversation immediately afterward.[1] He cited a 2023 [[gallup|Gallup]] poll finding that 20.8% of Generation Z identifies as LGBTQ+ against 2.6% of Boomers, and 2.1% of Gen Z as transgender against 0.1% of Boomers, arguing that changes of that speed cannot be organic.[1]

## The father-figure thesis

The article's central psychological claim is that Obama functioned as a surrogate father to a [[fatherless-households-in-america|fatherless generation]].[1] Carlson asserted that Obama is a CIA-created character and argued it is no great stretch to think he was at least partly created to serve as a father figure for young Americans in need of one — present in their homes nightly on television, and never drunk, absent, or violent.[1] Members of the 18–24 cohort in the 2023 poll were three to nine years old when Obama took office and eleven to seventeen when he left, which Carlson called formative years.[1]

## Alleged consequences

Carlson wrote that during Obama's presidency media, social media and teachers glorified his character and diplomacy while overlooking that his administration dropped 26,172 bombs on seven nations in 2016 alone, along with drone strikes, [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]], [[operation-fast-and-furious|gun-running to Mexican cartels]], and the IRS targeting of libertarians — despite the claim of a scandal-free presidency.[1] He contended that without Obama's presidency the United States would likely not have men using women's bathrooms, child body modification, drag performances for children in public libraries, comparable racial tension, or athletes kneeling during the national anthem.[1]

He described the most destructive element of Obama's tenure as the deliberate damage done to American youth, arguing that some of it is being repaired as young Americans encounter truth that makes more sense than the propaganda, while some of the damage is permanent.[1] Carlson described his own children in a blue state as "sojourners in their own country", obliged to affirm climate, pharmaceutical and historical claims they reject in order to pass their classes and to stay silent when a social studies teacher calls Trump a racist and a fascist.[1]

## Framing and remedy

Carlson defined "Nazi" for his own purposes as a fascist who forces beliefs on others and uses all available resources to compel compliance, and argued that Americans who complied with mandates and woke policy for mere comfort — absent the threat of death Germans faced — cannot look down on wartime Germans.[1] He noted a report that the Star of David was again being painted on Jewish homes in [[2023-paris-star-of-david-graffiti|Paris]], echoing Nazi practice.[1] His proposed remedy was to end what is taught in public schools and universities, mount a deliberate effort to undo the damage already done, and above all to put God back into America.[1]
