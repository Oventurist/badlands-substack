# Title 42

Title 42 refers to the coronavirus-era restrictions on asylum that allowed the United States to quickly turn back migrants at the U.S.–Mexico border for three years.[1] The authority derives from Title 42 of a 1944 public health law that permits curbs on migration in the name of protecting public health.[1]

## Expiration

The Title 42 restrictions lifted at 11:59 p.m. EDT on Thursday, May 11, 2023.[1] Ahead of that deadline, the United States put new restrictions into place at its southern border intended to stop migrants from crossing illegally and to encourage them instead to apply for asylum online through a new process.[1]

## Replacement policies

The [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration put in place a series of new policies cracking down on illegal crossings, saying it was trying to stop people from paying smuggling operations to make a dangerous and often deadly journey.[1] Under the new framework, migrants caught crossing illegally would not be allowed to return for five years and could face criminal prosecution if they attempted to do so.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that amid panic in both mainstream and right-wing media over the surge of migrants at the southern border, an encouraging development was passing largely unnoticed: the Biden administration was not only fighting to keep Trump-era policies in place but to strengthen them.[1] The expiration of Title 42 would be harmful if nothing replaced it, but the administration appeared to be imposing even stricter restrictions on crossings in a manner "oddly similar" to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s rhetoric from 2015 onward.[1]

Burning Bright connected the observation to the [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] thesis, noting that those who believe such a plan is in place are often accused of "hopium" given the regime's destructive agenda, but that it is possible to hold a rational conviction of the former while acknowledging, tracking and preparing for the latter.[1] The commentary summarized the posture as: the enemy is in a death spiral, but will produce a "Death Blossom" on the way down.[1]
