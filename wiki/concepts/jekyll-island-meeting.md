# Jekyll Island Meeting

The 1910 meeting on Jekyll Island, Georgia, is the secret gathering of bankers and legislators that produced the blueprint for the American central banking system and, ultimately, the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1] In Badlands Media's framing it stands as the reference point for monetary upheaval: the digitization of financial assets is described as "one of the most transformative and impactful moments in money since the 1910 meeting on Jekyll Island."[1]

The comparison anchors the article's central argument — that the rise of [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and other digital assets after the [[2008-financial-crisis|2008 financial crisis]] represents the first serious challenge to the century of central banking that Jekyll Island inaugurated, with early adopters functioning as a pilot program for whatever monetary order comes next.[1] The open question the article poses is whether Bitcoin can free people from what it calls the regime and its private banking cartel, or whether apathy, fraud, and the appetite for power will instead accelerate the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] toward a [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1]
