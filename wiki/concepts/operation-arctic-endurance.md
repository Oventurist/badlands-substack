# Operation Arctic Endurance

Operation Arctic Endurance is the name announced by France for its contribution of military "units" to the reinforcement of [[greenland|Greenland]] amid the January 2026 escalation between the United States and [[denmark|Denmark]] over the territory's status.[1]

## Composition and context

The deployment came after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] renewed his insistence that US control of Greenland is necessary for American national security, and after Danish officials — meeting Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] in Washington — reiterated that Greenland is not for sale.[1] Denmark subsequently began moving military resources into Greenland, with other European [[nato|NATO]] allies reportedly following.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] catalogued the resulting force as strikingly small: thirteen Germans for reconnaissance, two Norwegians, one Briton, and an unknown number of Swedes, alongside the French units under the Arctic Endurance banner, with Finland, Canada and the Netherlands still weighing contributions.[1] She read the choice of the word "endurance" as a signal that the effort was expected to be long and costly, comparing the naming convention to "Operation Enduring Freedom."[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Trump was deliberately using the threat of Russia and China to induce NATO to divert resources away from Eastern Europe and Ukraine toward Greenland, where in his assessment they would be strategically useless, and tied the alliance's weakness to the [[german-industrial-crisis-2026|German industrial crisis]] and Europe's broader economic decline.[1]
