# Ensuring American Space Superiority

"Ensuring American Space Superiority" is an executive order issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Thursday, December 18, 2025, described as the first major space policy move of his administration's second term.[1]

## Contents

The order enshrined the United States goal of putting humans back on the Moon by 2028 and committed the government to defending space assets against weapon threats.[1] It called on the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and US intelligence agencies to produce a space security strategy, urged efficiency among private space contractors, and sought demonstrations of missile-defense technologies under the [[golden-dome|Golden Dome]] program.[1]

A widely quoted passage directed the "near-term utilization of space nuclear power by deploying nuclear reactors on the Moon and in orbit, including a lunar surface reactor ready for launch by 2030."[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] singled out that provision, asking how the deployment of nuclear reactors on the Moon was not a larger story, and questioning the cost of the resulting space infrastructure "not just in dollars."[1]

## Reorganization of space policy coordination

The order reorganized national space policy coordination under Trump's chief science adviser, [[michael-kratsios|Michael Kratsios]].[1] It appeared to cancel the [[national-space-council|National Space Council]], the White House's top space policy-coordinating body and a panel of cabinet members that Trump had revived during his first term and reportedly considered abolishing during 2025.[1] An administration official said the council would not in fact be cancelled, suggesting instead that it would continue under the White House Office of Technology Policy with a different structure in which the president, rather than the vice president, would serve as chairman.[1]

Badlands commentary framed that structural change in personal terms, remarking that Trump had become "the chairman of going to the moon" and had ousted Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] from the position.[1]
