# Pfizer mRNA Flu Vaccine Trial Results

The Pfizer mRNA flu vaccine trial results refers to phase 3 clinical trial data for [[pfizer|Pfizer]]'s messenger-RNA influenza vaccine, reported in late November 2025 as having failed in elderly participants and performed poorly on a risk-benefit basis in younger adults.[1]

## Background

Two years earlier, Pfizer had anticipated that sales of its rebound-prone COVID-19 oral antiviral Paxlovid and its fully approved mRNA COVID vaccine Comirnaty would plummet.[1] Good news appeared on the horizon when the company's mRNA flu vaccine performed well in phase 3 trials in adults aged 18 to 64, and Pfizer said it expected to release results from the 65-and-up cohort "later this year."[1]

## Findings

When the results finally emerged, they showed that compared with a standard flu shot the mRNA vaccine was associated with six times as many adverse events as infections prevented in adults under 65.[1] In elderly people the product outright failed, being associated with more deaths and with both minor and serious injuries ranging from injection-site swelling to kidney problems.[1] Coverage characterized the company as having buried the data rather than publicizing the delayed elderly-cohort results it had earlier promised.[1]
