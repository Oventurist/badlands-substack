# 2025 Greenland General Election

The 2025 Greenland general election was a snap parliamentary contest in the Danish semi-autonomous territory of [[greenland|Greenland]], held in March 2025 and dominated by two intertwined questions: independence from Denmark and U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s pledge to take over the island.[1]

## Result

Greenland's centre-right opposition won a surprise victory. The Democratic party, which favours a gradual approach to independence from Denmark, took roughly 30% of the vote according to near-complete results.[1] Party leader [[jens-frederik-nielsen|Jens-Frederik Nielsen]] told local media that "Greenland needs us to stand together in a time of great interest from outside," adding that "there is a need for unity, so we will enter into negotiations with everyone."[1] His party was required to negotiate with other parties in order to form a coalition.[1]

## Context

Greenland — the world's biggest island, lying between the Arctic and Atlantic Oceans — has been controlled by Denmark, nearly 3,000km (1,860 miles) away, for about 300 years.[1] The territory governs its own domestic affairs, while decisions on foreign and defence policy are made in Copenhagen.[1] Five of the six main parties contesting the election favoured independence from Copenhagen, differing chiefly over the pace at which it should be achieved.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterised the outcome as Greenlanders voting "to throw off their monarchs in Copenhagen" by electing a centre-right party that ran on independence, and argued that although Greenlanders had long wanted independence, the subject was absent from American and international conversation until Trump dragged the territory into the narrative.[1] She paired the result with the contrasting situation in [[romania|Romania]] as evidence that "the people struggle to be free all over the world," while urging Americans to demand verifiably accurate elections at home.[1]
