# Warrior Board

The "warrior board" is a proposed panel of retired senior military personnel described in a draft executive order under consideration by the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] transition team in November 2024, which would review three- and four-star officers and recommend the removal of any deemed unfit for leadership.[1]

## Draft executive order

According to a draft reviewed by [[wall-street-journal|The Wall Street Journal]], approval of the order could fast-track the removal of generals and admirals found to be "lacking in requisite leadership qualities."[1] Reporting noted that the measure could also create a chilling effect among senior officers, given the president-elect's past vow to fire "woke generals" — a reference to officers seen as promoting diversity in the ranks at the expense of military readiness.[1]

As commander in chief, Trump can dismiss any officer at will, but an outside board whose members he appoints would bypass the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s regular promotion system and signal across the military an intent to purge a number of generals and admirals.[1] The draft states that the review would focus "on leadership capability, strategic readiness, and commitment to military excellence," without specifying what officers would need to present in order to demonstrate that they meet those standards.[1]

The draft originated with one of several outside policy groups collaborating with the transition team and was one of numerous executive orders under review, a transition official said.[1] The board would be composed of retired generals and noncommissioned officers, who would forward their recommendations to the president; those identified for removal would be retired at their current rank within thirty days.[1]
