# 2025 Myanmar Earthquake

The **2025 Myanmar earthquake** was a magnitude 7.7 quake that struck central [[myanmar|Myanmar]] on March 28, 2025, causing widespread destruction and mass casualties.[1]

## Casualty estimates

The [[united-states-geological-survey|United States Geological Survey]] estimated on Friday, March 28, that the death toll could surpass 10,000.[1] The USGS issued a red alert for estimated fatalities, a designation indicating "high casualties and extensive damage."[1] Myanmar's military government initially reported at least 144 deaths, a figure expected to rise substantially as rubble was cleared and bodies recovered.[1]

## Compounding conditions

Myanmar, one of Asia's poorest nations, was already reeling from a civil war sparked by the 2021 military coup.[1] Weak infrastructure left the country ill-equipped to respond to major natural disasters, and swathes of territory were run by a patchwork of militias, making reliable information extremely difficult to gather.[1] The military also regularly cut communications in conflict zones.[1]

The shaking was felt far beyond the epicentre: a building in Bangkok, Thailand, collapsed despite being more than 600 miles away.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Chris Paul framed coverage of the disaster sceptically, observing that "according to the global Regime's State media, the earthquake disaster will be compounded by the fact that the Regime was kicked out of Myanmar in 2021" — a reference to the 2021 coup that displaced the internationally aligned civilian government.[1]
