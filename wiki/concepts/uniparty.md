# Uniparty

"Uniparty" is the term used in the Badlands corpus for the alleged functional merger of establishment Republicans — described as RINOs and Neocons — with Democrats into a single governing bloc that produces continuity of policy regardless of which party holds nominal power.[1]

## The debt ceiling as demonstration

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] raised the [[2023-debt-ceiling-agreement|2023 debt ceiling agreement]] as a possible deliberate demonstration of the Uniparty's existence, asking whether "more evidence of a 'Uniparty' need[s] to occur to wake more people up."[1] In this framing, [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]]'s decision to side with the RINOs, Neocons and Democrats was not simply a failure of nerve but potentially the intended mechanism for exposing the alignment.[1]

## Exposure as strategy

Carlson argued that the [[america-first|America First]] congressmen had already secured procedural measures making it easier to impeach the Speaker, positioning McCarthy's capitulation as a trigger rather than an endpoint.[1] He extended the same logic to [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], holding that whether or not DeSantis was cooperating with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], his candidacy was exposing the RINOs, Neocons and the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1] Carlson expected any eventual DeSantis withdrawal to transfer Neocon campaign money to Trump — money those donors would never have given him directly.[1]
