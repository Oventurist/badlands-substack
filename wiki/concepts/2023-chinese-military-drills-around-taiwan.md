# 2023 Chinese Military Drills Around Taiwan

The **2023 Chinese military drills around Taiwan** were a major show of force conducted by the [[peoples-liberation-army|People's Liberation Army]] in April 2023 in the wake of a meeting in California between Taiwanese President [[tsai-ing-wen|Tsai Ing-wen]] and US House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]].[1] The exercises were described by the PLA as a rehearsal for "sealing off" [[taiwan|Taiwan]].[1]

## Conduct of the exercises

On Sunday, April 9, 2023, at least 70 Chinese aircraft breached the island's air defense identification zone and eleven military ships encircled it, including a group of J-15 fighter jets whose participation was described as rare if not unprecedented for that advanced Chinese aircraft.[1] By Monday, April 10 — the third day of wargames — reports cited at least 58 planes buzzing the island along with nine warships approaching its territorial waters.[1]

According to a PLA statement, aircraft, ships and troops were sent into "the maritime areas and air space of the Taiwan Strait, off the northern and southern coasts of the island, and to the island's east," and the drills included simulated "maritime blockades" and "targeted ambush assaults on enemy mooring vessels."[1] State broadcaster CCTV announced on Monday that the drills had "simulated joint precision strikes against key targets on Taiwan island and surrounding waters," adding that forces "continued to maintain the situation of closely encircling the island."[1] The military statement cited in the CCTV report said forces in the command were "ready for combat at all times, and will resolutely destroy any type of 'Taiwan independence' separatist or foreign interference attempts."[1]
