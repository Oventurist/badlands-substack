# 3:02 Comms

**3:02 comms** is the term used within the [[truth-community|truth community]] for a recurring pattern of references to the time 3:02 in Q drops and in later public statements and posts by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his allies.[1] The pattern is treated at length in Chapter 21 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series by [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]].[1]

## Origin in drop #131

The reference point is Q drop #131 of November 10, 2017, in which Q states that a National Security briefing is to occur at 3:02 a.m.[1] The purpose of that meeting was never publicly established — Fleuret assumes it was classified — but he argues the specificity of the timestamp is itself the signal, asking why the drop did not simply say "as of 3:00."[1] Drop #2491 contains a further mention of 3:02.[1]

## Later recurrences

Fleuret catalogues several subsequent appearances of the same figure. In an interview about the [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election]], Trump asked, "What happened at 3:02 in the morning? What happened," a window corresponding to the ballot dumps during which, in this account, his apparently insurmountable lead vanished.[1] [[michael-flynn|General Flynn]] has also used the marker, including in a retweet of a Trump cease-and-desist letter concerning the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 presidential election]].[1]

Other instances collected in the chapter include a Truth Social post in which the clock outside Trump Tower reads 3:02; a video clip posted by Trump at 3:02; and a clip in which Trump recounts that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] telephoned him at "3:02 in the morning" to concede in 2016.[1] A further clip has him again discussing the disappearance of his 2020 lead while mentioning 3:02.[1]

## Interpretation

Fleuret concludes that he does not know what took place in the November 2017 National Security meeting, but that because the 3:02 comms have never stopped, whatever occurred was probably far larger than is currently understood, and is likely to become a [[future-proves-past|future proves past]] confirmation during Trump's second administration.[1]
