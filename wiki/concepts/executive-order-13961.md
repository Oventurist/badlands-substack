# Executive Order 13961

Executive Order 13961, issued in the closing weeks of Donald Trump's first term, established a Federal Mission Resilience framework directing agencies to ensure the continuity of essential government functions during disruption.[1] Within the Badlands and broader Truth Community it is widely referred to as the "Devo EO," because it sits at the heart of the [[devolution|devolution]] thesis — the argument that continuity-of-government machinery was activated to preserve legitimate authority across the 2021–2025 period.[1]

## Federal Mission Resilience Strategy

The order was released in conjunction with a Federal Mission Resilience strategy document whose language concerned the preservation of the continuity and stability of the American system of government.[1]

## Echo in the 2025 Inauguration Theme

In January 2025 Badlands writer Jon Herold noted that the stated theme of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second inauguration invoked "the Founder's commitment to future generations of Americans to preserve the continuity and stability of our democratic system of government," wording he described as very similar to that of the Federal Mission Resilience strategy released with EO 13961.[1] He read the parallel as a deliberate signal, concluding that the "Devo term is almost coming to an end."[1]

## Relationship to the end of the Q drops

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describes EO 13961 as the continuity of government act, and equates it with [[devolution|Devolution]]: the order, he writes, gave [[donald-trump|Trump]] the power to put together a small group of people to oversee and preserve the national essential functions of the government if there was evidence of election interference.[2] He attaches particular significance to the date, noting that [[qanon|Q]] made its final pre-hiatus post on December 8, 2020, the day after the order was created, and reading the sequence as evidence that the mechanism was in place before the drops paused.[2] The small group so constituted is presented as an invisible safety net beneath the years that followed.[2]
