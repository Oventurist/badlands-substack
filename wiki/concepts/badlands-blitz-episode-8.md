# Badlands Blitz Episode 8

**Badlands Blitz Episode 8** is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly recap and dissection series published by the Badlands Media Substack. Dated February 28, 2026, and subtitled "On the Reclaiming of American Pride & the Combating of Anti-MAGA FUD," the episode registered 195 likes at the time of the source capture.

## Format and purpose

The article restates the standing purpose of the series: The Blitz is "intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." Episode 8 therefore functions as a summary companion to the daily Brief rather than as standalone reporting.

## Hosts and production notes

The episode was hosted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who, in the article's phrasing, "hold down the fort" in the absence of [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]. Burning Bright — described tongue-in-cheek as their "liege lord, best friend, mentor and overall inspiration—not to mention final editorial overseer, which has nothing to do with this paragraph in any way" — was occupied trying to get his home back in order after what the article calls a historic blizzard.

The write-up notes with evident humor that the episode "clocks in as the shortest episode—and so, most in keeping with the original intent—of the Blitz yet," adding that this is "pure coincidence, and all of you are very upset about it."

## Subscription appeal

As with other Blitz entries, the post closes with a note on the outlet's funding model. All Badlands content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free; the article states that the only way the team can pay its people for their time is through voluntary reader support. Readers who value the daily output and the "step-up in said work for 2026" are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time. The post signs off with the recurring benediction to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
