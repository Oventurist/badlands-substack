# Biological Weapons Anti-Terrorism Act of 1989

The Biological Weapons Anti-Terrorism Act of 1989 is the United States statute implementing the Biological Weapons Convention in domestic law. It was drafted by international law professor and bioweapons expert [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]].[1]

## Significance

Boyle's authorship of the act has been repeatedly cited as the basis for his authority in later public-health and biosecurity controversies, including his criticism of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]]'s proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and the amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]], which he described as "an attempt to take over the United States of America by using the WHO as a front for that purpose."[1]
