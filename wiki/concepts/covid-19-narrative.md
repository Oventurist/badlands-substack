# COVID-19 Narrative

The "Covid narrative" is [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s term for the publicly promoted account of the pandemic, which he distinguishes sharply from the pathogen itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Origin claims

Carlson writes that he does believe some sort of bioweapon was released from a lab in Wuhan, China, but that he does not think this is where it originated.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) In his account it originated in a lab in North Carolina and was moved to [[wuhan-lab|Wuhan]] to maintain secrecy and control, a program he alleges was funded and run by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], who had to move it overseas once [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] came into office.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He further believes other bioweapons were being created in biolabs elsewhere in the world, specifically in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and possibly Taiwan.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Trump, he says, publicly stated that Covid was intentionally created in a lab and released on the world.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## "Chyna virus"

Trump referred to the virus as the "Chyna virus" and was called racist for doing so and for insinuating it came from a Chinese lab.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Carlson argues Trump knows perfectly well where the bioweapon originated, and that the "Chyna virus" formulation is therefore a double meaning pointing at Communism: China is a communist country, and Trump is saying that the CCP is infiltrating America.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) In this reading the [[chinese-communist-party|CCP]] forced a bioweapon on the world that proved less deadly than hoped, but the communist-controlled media and government officials could achieve the same destructive effect simply by continuing to push the narrative that it was extremely deadly.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Function

The narrative, rather than the pathogen, is credited with the outcomes: it forced the [[covid-19-lockdowns|lockdowns]], produced an overreaching control of people and an abandonment of human rights, and justified mail-in ballots, by which means Carlson says a duly-elected President was overthrown and removed from power.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He invites readers to consider that what America is experiencing is itself like a man-made virus designed to destroy everything and everyone — narratives and agendas essentially created in a lab and dropped on the population.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)
