# Hero's Journey

The **Hero's Journey** is the mythic template associated with [[joseph-campbell|Joseph Campbell]] and invoked in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] to give ancient precedent to the [[liminality|liminal]] state.[1]

The essay identifies the liminal specifically with the *Belly of the Whale* stage of Campbell's sequence — the interval in which the protagonist is swallowed, stripped of former bearings, and remade before re-emerging.[1]

## Parallel archetypes

The article sets the Belly of the Whale alongside a set of cross-cultural analogues: Inanna descending into the underworld; the Buddha sitting beneath the Bodhi tree while Mara whispers temptations; Isis in the Egyptian mythos piecing together the body of Osiris, read as a metaphor for gathering scattered fragments of the self; and, in the Norse tradition, Odin hanging upside down on the World Tree, "sacrificing certainty for wisdom."[1]

The purpose of the comparison is normative rather than scholarly: the essay uses it to tell readers that they walk "the same path mystics, healers, and seekers have walked for centuries," that they are "not broken" but "being initiated," and that the old self is stripped bare in this passage "not because it is wrong. But because it is no longer true."[1]
