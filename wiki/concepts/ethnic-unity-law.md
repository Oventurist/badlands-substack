# Ethnic Unity Law

The Ethnic Unity Law is a proposed piece of [[china|Chinese]] legislation, advanced through the [[national-peoples-congress|National People's Congress]] in March 2026, aimed at strengthening what the government describes as national cohesion among the country's 56 officially recognized ethnic groups.[1]

## Provisions and stated purpose

The proposal is intended to formalize policies promoting ethnic integration and unity across the country.[1] Chinese officials say the legislation is meant to promote unity, stability, and development in minority regions, which Beijing has long described as a national priority.[1]

## Criticism

Critics contend the law could codify policies that pressure minority groups to assimilate into Han Chinese cultural and political norms, particularly in regions with large minority populations.[1] Human rights organizations have raised concerns that the legislation could expand existing programs affecting minority communities, including the [[uyghurs|Uyghurs]] and Tibetans.[1]
