# Gas Stoves

The campaign against **gas stoves** is treated in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]] as a case study in the sudden manufacture of an environmental villain.[1]

Carlson notes that gas stoves were demonized "overnight," becoming what he calls "the new aerosol can" — a reference to the earlier campaign warning that aerosols were destroying the ozone layer.[1] Under the new framing, owning a gas stove marks a person as an enemy of the planet.[1]

He identifies two prongs to the messaging: that gas stoves harm the environment, and that they are actively killing people.[1] The second claim, in his reading, serves a distinct purpose — reassigning blame for excess deaths away from Covid-19 vaccines and toward household appliances, which he summarizes as "It's not the vaccines, it's the gas stoves. Done and done; now they are no longer liable for the vaccine deaths."[1]

Within the essay's larger argument, the gas stove campaign is one channel of a broader push to eliminate direct consumer access to fuels — natural gas, gasoline, oil, coal, and nuclear power — in favor of electricity delivered through a single controllable grid.[1] Carlson satirizes the resulting moral framing: the good citizen cooks on an electric range, though he adds that eggs themselves have been discouraged despite evidence he cites that they counter Covid and vaccine side effects, with the preferred meal being "a soy burger cooked in vegetable oil on an electric stove."[1]
