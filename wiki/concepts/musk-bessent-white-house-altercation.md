# Musk–Bessent White House Altercation

The Musk–Bessent White House altercation was an alleged physical confrontation in mid-April 2025 between [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] inside the West Wing, recounted second-hand by Trump political advisor [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and first reported by the Washington Post in June 2025.[1]

## Account

According to Bannon, citing what he had been told by others, the two men had been with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the Oval Office pitching their respective preferred picks for acting IRS commissioner.[1] Earlier reporting by the New York Times indicated that Bessent was irate that Musk had gone around him to install [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] in the role despite the [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] reporting to the Treasury Department; Bessent told Trump he wanted Deputy Treasury Secretary Michael Faulkender in the slot, and Trump agreed, capping a chaotic period in which the IRS was overseen by three different acting commissioners in a single week.[1]

As they left the office and headed down a West Wing hallway, the two began insulting each other, with Bessent ridiculing Musk's claim that he would identify over a trillion dollars in government waste, fraud and abuse and telling him, "You're a fraud. You're a total fraud!"[1] That allegedly prompted Musk to slam his shoulder into Bessent's ribs, hitting him hard "like a rugby player," in Bannon's telling.[1] Bessent physically retaliated before bystanders outside the national security adviser's office intervened to break up the fight, and Musk was reportedly escorted from the West Wing.[1] "President Trump heard about it and said, 'This is too much,'" Bannon said.[1] In the following days Musk announced that he would ease back from his role in the administration to give more attention to his businesses.[1]

## Context

The account surfaced amid the wider [[trump-musk-feud-2025|Trump–Musk feud]], with Trump saying he had no desire to patch things up and assuming the relationship — which by all accounts played a decisive role in his return to power — was over.[1]
