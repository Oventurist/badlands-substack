# DOGE Acts

The "DOGE Acts" are a package of bills unveiled in early December 2024 by Senator [[marsha-blackburn|Marsha Blackburn]] (R-Tenn.) aimed at cutting federal spending and freezing federal hiring and salaries.[1] A spokesperson for Blackburn said the package was intended to hold the federal government "more accountable for managing taxpayer dollars" and that it coincided with the plan by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] to make the federal government more efficient.[1]

According to the statement, the legislation would cut discretionary spending, move federal agencies "out of the swamp," freeze federal hiring and salaries for one year, begin a merit-based compensation structure for federal employees, and require agencies to bring employees back into the office.[1] Specific bills in the package would create a commission focused on relocating some federal agencies away from Washington, D.C.; impose a yearlong pause on federal hiring and salary increases; and establish a pilot program compensating civilian employees according to merit.[1]

The package was framed as the legislative companion to the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] (DOGE), the advisory effort President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced in November 2024 with Musk and Ramaswamy at its head, whose stated aims were to "slash excess regulations, cut wasteful expenditures" and restructure federal agencies.[1]
