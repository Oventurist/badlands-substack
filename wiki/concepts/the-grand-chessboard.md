# The Grand Chessboard

*The Grand Chessboard* is a 1998 book by [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] setting out a strategy for American primacy in Eurasia.[1] In Badlands commentary, the work is cited for Brzezinski's warning that the only thing capable of potentially threatening the emerging New World Order would be a triumvirate alliance of Russia, China, and Iran.[1]

That framing underpins the movement's interest in [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], whose territory sits inside the triangle formed by the capitals of those three powers, and in the Eurasian rail corridors associated with the [[kazakhstan-united-states-locomotive-deal|$4 billion US–Kazakh locomotive deal]] announced in September 2025.[1] Commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry used the Brzezinski thesis to argue that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s railroad master plan — linking Azerbaijan, India, Pakistan, Iran, the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation, China, and Russia — represents a counter-move against Western hegemony precisely along the axis Brzezinski feared.[1]
