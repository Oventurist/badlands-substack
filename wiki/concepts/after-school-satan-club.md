# After School Satan Club

The After School Satan Club is a children's program sponsored by [[satanic-temple|The Satanic Temple]] and pushed into public school districts that already host Christian after-school clubs.[1]

## Legal basis

The club's promoters argue that a district which allows one religion to host events must allow all of them, a position litigated with the support of the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]].[1] In May 2023 a federal judge ruled that Pennsylvania's [[saucon-valley-school-district|Saucon Valley School District]] must allow the club to meet in district facilities; the ACLU announced the ruling in a tweet as "a victory for free speech and religious freedom."[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands Media commentators read the ruling less as a church-state technicality than as a front in a metaphysical conflict, with [[absolute-1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] writing that a federal judge ordering Pennsylvania to permit Satanic after-school clubs was "further proof that we are in a spiritual war more than anything else."[1]
