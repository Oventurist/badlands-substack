# Electoral Integrity

Electoral integrity refers to the accuracy, transparency and honesty of the machinery by which a republic transfers power. In Badlands Media commentary it is treated as the non-negotiable precondition on which every other institutional reform depends.[1]

## Framing in Badlands commentary

Writing in August 2023 on the first anniversary of the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago raid]], [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]] argued that the bedrock of democracy is the peaceful transition of power based on the will of the people, and that imprisoning political opponents disrupts that mechanism, casting doubt on the legitimacy of elections and eroding the foundation of democratic governance.[1] After enumerating remedies including [[judicial-independence|judicial independence]], transparency bodies, law enforcement reform, whistleblower protection and a free press, he concluded that all such efforts are futile without securing fair and honest elections, making electoral transparency and accuracy an unequivocal necessity for safeguarding the republic's future.[1]

The argument connects electoral integrity to the wider claim that [[politically-motivated-imprisonment|politically motivated imprisonment]] fuels polarization, suppresses dissent and damages a nation's international reputation — harms that compound when citizens no longer believe elections can correct them.[1]
