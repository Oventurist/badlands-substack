# WHCA Dinner Assassination Attempt Prosecution

The **WHCA Dinner assassination attempt prosecution** is the federal criminal case against a man accused of attempting to attack President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the [[white-house-correspondents-association-dinner|White House Correspondents' Association dinner]].[1]

## Plea and charges

The defendant, identified in reporting by the surname Allen, pleaded not guilty on a Monday in May 2026 to federal charges including attempted assassination and assaulting a federal officer.[1] He appeared briefly in federal court, where one of his attorneys entered the plea on his behalf.[1] He also faces additional firearms charges and a count accusing him of assaulting a federal officer with a deadly weapon.[1] The attempted assassination charge alone carries a possible life sentence upon conviction.[1]

## Conflict-of-interest motion

Allen's legal team sought to remove at least two senior Justice Department officials from involvement in the prosecution, arguing that Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] and US Attorney [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]], both of whom attended the event, could be considered victims or witnesses in the case and therefore present a possible conflict of interest.[1] Defense attorney Eugene Ohm indicated the request could expand to seeking the recusal of Pirro's entire office.[1] US District Judge [[trevor-mcfadden|Trevor McFadden]] did not immediately rule and asked the defense to further explain the scope of the request.[1]

## Schedule

Allen was scheduled to return to court on June 29.[1]
