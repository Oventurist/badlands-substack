# Truth Is a Force of Nature

**"Truth is a force of nature"** is the organizing thesis and subtitle of the Badlands Media essay "The Metaphysics of 5G War," published April 14, 2023 by the author writing as [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]].[1] It holds that truthfulness is not merely a moral preference but a structural property of reality, so that falsehood is inherently unstable and self-destroying.[1]

## The argument

The essay opens from the familiar adage that a lie travels halfway around the world before the truth has put its shoes on, and disputes it: the appearance of speed is an artifact of captured institutions, above all the media, which supply an "artificial support and propagation network" for weaponized narratives and outright lies.[1] Nonetheless, the author argues, "all lies eventually perish, just as surely as a building without a foundation is doomed to crumble," leaving only the questions of when and with how much collateral damage.[1]

The underlying reason offered is that narratives are "nothing more than linguistic constructs," and the lasting power of any narrative depends on how closely it conforms to reality.[1] The principle is said to extend even to fiction: stories endure across generations only insofar as they illuminate matters of genuine human importance — relationships, the nature of God and existence, war and peace, the meaning of love — and stories offering no such illumination are quickly forgotten.[1]

## Metaphysical grounding

The claim is grounded in the [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe]] of [[chris-langan|Chris Langan]], on which reality is a self-contained, self-processing language; if reality itself is linguistic, then the durability of linguistic constructs is a physical question and truth-conformity is what confers durability.[1] The corollary drawn for participants in the [[5g-war|5G war]] is that adherence to truth "exerts a *more* powerful effect on the metaphysical universe from which the physical universe is derived" than lies do, because, in the essay's formulation, God does not endorse lies — only truth.[1]

## Practical upshot

From this the essay derives its counsel to [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]]: bearing witness to truth helps "manifest" a reality reflecting that truth, an outcome the author insists is a matter of "verifiable metaphysics" rather than a New Age sentiment.[1] Readers are urged to be as honest as they know how to be, with an explicit exception for situations of legal duress, in which culpability rests with the party who created the coercion rather than the person forced to act against conscience.[1] The essay closes by observing that the most famous man in history began with only twelve followers, and repeating its title claim: "Truth is a force of nature, indeed."[1]
