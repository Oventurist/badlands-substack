# 2024 Zelensky Assassination Plot

The **2024 Zelensky assassination plot** was an alleged Russian conspiracy to kidnap and kill Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] that Ukrainian intelligence announced it had foiled in May 2024.[1]

## Allegations

The [[security-service-of-ukraine|SBU]] State Security Service said it had caught Russian agents embedded within the Ukrainian state guard plotting the kidnapping and assassination of the president and other senior government officials.[1] Vasyl Malyuk, head of the SBU, said the failed operation was intended as a "gift" to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] ahead of his inauguration that Tuesday.[1]

The service said it had arrested two suspected "moles" whom it believed had been recruited by Russia before the invasion.[1] Writing on Telegram, the SBU claimed a Russian secret agent had passed on coordinates of a safe house ahead of a planned rocket attack, and that Russia had planned to destroy all traces of the drone used to target the president by firing a second missile.[1] Without providing evidence, the agency stated: "The enemy was actively developing plans to eliminate President Volodymyr Zelensky."[1] The account was reported by The Independent.[1]

## Commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] read the announcement as a display of "the hegemony's hubris," arguing that it made sense in light of Ukrainian war weariness and that presenting the affair as a victory betrayed recognition behind closed doors that dissent within the ranks was reaching untenable levels.[1]

He drew a parallel to the 1863 New York City draft riots as depicted in Gangs of New York — the subject of an episode of Badlands Story Hour hosted by [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] — in which the U.S. government responded to resistance against conscription by firing on citizens in the streets and shelling them from warships.[1] He connected that history to videos emerging from Ukraine of the government and military dragging men from their homes to fight, and asked how soon a genuine mutiny would materialize against Ukrainian leadership and what he described as its masters, NATO and the CIA.[1]
