# Softwar

*Softwar: A Novel Theory on Power Projection and the National Strategic Significance of Bitcoin* is a thesis and book by United States Space Force Major [[jason-lowery|Jason Lowery]], cited in the Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]].

## Argument as cited

The article describes *Softwar* as "an extensive and well researched thesis on how [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is much more than simply 'magic internet money.'" Drawing on it, [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] asks what follows if Bitcoin was released as a military weapon and digital counterinsurgency operation intended to address the root cause that has plagued humanity with an endless cycle of destruction and bloodshed for millennia: the centralized control of money. The article supports this framing by noting that every element of Bitcoin's underlying technology — TCP/IP, TOR, SHA-256 and GPS — was built by the military for nuclear-resistant, wartime application.

The work's key concept for the article's purposes is physical restraint: Lowery is cited for the proposition that Bitcoin *physically restrains* actors in cyberspace. The article uses this to build its central analogy with Revelation's angel who binds Satan with a key and a chain, observing that Bitcoin restrains the "god-kings" using public and private keys and a decentralized ledger known as the timechain, thereby underpinning the [[millennial-reign|Millennial Reign]] reading.
