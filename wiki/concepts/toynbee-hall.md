# Toynbee Hall

Toynbee Hall was the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]]'s London settlement house experiment, founded in 1884.[1]

The experiment was an attempt to replace charity with government programs and is described as essentially a blueprint for the modern welfare system.[1] [[jane-addams|Jane Addams]], who ran the American temperance movement that produced [[prohibition|Prohibition]], studied the Toynbee Hall model.[1] In the argument advanced by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the 1884 experiment failed in the same manner as the contemporary welfare system: it does not lift people out of poverty, but is a great success if the objective is to keep the poor perpetually in poverty — one strand of an ongoing effort to enslave Americans alongside debt through the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and dependence on both illegal drugs and overconsumed pharmaceuticals.[1]
