# Operation Epic Fury

Operation Epic Fury is the name used in the [[badlands-brief-52f|Badlands Brief]] of March 3, 2026 for the coordinated United States and Israeli air and missile campaign against [[iran|Iran]] launched at the end of February 2026.

## Course of the campaign

As summarized in the Brief, the United States and [[israel|Israel]] struck Iranian military leadership, missile systems, and nuclear-related infrastructure. Supreme Leader Ayatollah [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] was killed in the attacks, and US officials said more than 1,000 targets had been struck since the campaign opened. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] described the objective as halting Iran's nuclear and long-range missile capabilities, said the operation could last several weeks, and indicated he remained open to talks with Tehran. Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] said no ground troops were intended but declined to rule them out, and characterized the campaign as directed at military objectives rather than at governance change.

Iran responded with missile and drone attacks on US and allied positions across the region. Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] called externally imposed regime change "mission impossible" while signaling interest in de-escalation. Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] subsequently told reporters that Washington acted preemptively because it had concluded Israel would strike with or without American participation, and that an Israeli strike would draw Iranian retaliation against US forces.

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argues that the operation "launched with the full cinematic weight the Collective Mind has been primed to expect," giving the surface appearance of a classic regime-change war, but that it is in fact an application of the [[venezuela-model|Venezuela Model]] — removal of a figurehead followed by accommodation with a surviving governing structure, without occupation or ground forces. In this reading the campaign is an instance of [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]], and the discomfort of neoconservative figures such as [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] is evidence that the expected "Forever War" is not materializing.

The Brief also notes speculation, drawing on remarks by retired Israeli general [[benny-gantz|Benny Gantz]], that a ground invasion by Israel or the United States remains possible, potentially via the [[davids-corridor|David's Corridor]] route and with [[kurds|Kurdish]] assistance.
