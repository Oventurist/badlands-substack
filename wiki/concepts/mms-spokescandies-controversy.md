# M&M's Spokescandies Controversy

The M&M's spokescandies controversy was a January 2023 consumer backlash against [[mars-incorporated|Mars]]'s redesign of the M&M's mascot characters, which the company had altered as part of an effort to make the brand more inclusive.[1]

Following what was described as a massive pushback over the politicization of the candy brand, Mars announced on January 23, 2023, that it was pulling its "increasingly woke" spokescandies and replacing the decades-old mascot team with the actress [[maya-rudolph|Maya Rudolph]].[1] The announcement was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and republished by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the headline as groan-inducing, noting the absurdity of a debate over whether M&M candies were "not inclusive enough."[1] He argued that the universality and speed of the backlash from an "increasingly-restless and outspoken American populace" demonstrated to the [[truth-community|truth community]] that ideological messaging campaigns had overreached, and that "the boomerangs are coming."[1]
