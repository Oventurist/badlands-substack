# 2024 Democratic Party Platform

The 2024 Democratic Party Platform is the policy document released by the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]] in August 2024 ahead of the [[2024-democratic-national-convention|Democratic National Convention]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] examined the document in "The Candidate Who Isn't There," published August 24, 2024, after arguing that [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] had offered the public no discernible policy platform of her own — her campaign website carrying a biography, merchandise and slogans such as "Together, we can win this!" but no mention of politics.[1]

## Written for a Biden second term

DeLarme's central observation was that the platform had clearly been drafted with a second [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] term in mind, referring to Biden as the presidential candidate throughout the text.[1] He counted nearly 300 instances of the name "Biden" in the document against 32 instances of "Harris."[1]

He read this as confirmation of a point he made throughout the article: that whichever Democrat occupies the Oval Office is irrelevant to the ruling class, because "they are all hollow vessels for the establishment."[1] He also read it as evidence of laziness and arrogance, since editing the document would not have taken long, but the party was confident its voters would not read it and that the few journalists who did would not criticize it.[1]

DeLarme concluded that although Harris had not stated her goals directly, the platform provided sufficient proof that they were identical to Biden's — that a Harris presidency would amount to "Biden administration 2.0."[1]
