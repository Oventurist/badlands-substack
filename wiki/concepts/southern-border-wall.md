# Southern Border Wall

The southern border wall — the barrier along the United States–Mexico frontier promised by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during his 2016 campaign — is treated in Badlands Media's [[qanon|Q]]-decode literature not merely as an immigration measure but as a national security operation executed by the military over the objections of [[congress|Congress]].[1]

## Congressional obstruction

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] identifies [[john-mccain|John McCain]] as Trump's single biggest obstacle in Congress to building the wall, alongside [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] and [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]]; McCain is also described as a perennial sponsor of amnesty legislation.[1] Lange characterizes the Democratic Party's standing objective as open borders, the elimination of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]], and support for illegal immigration, and argues Trump's task was to demonstrate that his opponents' professed commitment to border security was a scam.[1]

## Military funding and construction

CNN reported on March 27, 2018 that Trump had privately floated funding wall construction through the U.S. military budget, a proposal press secretary Sarah Sanders declined to discuss at the daily briefing while insisting that "the continuation of building the wall is ongoing."[1] Lange argues that Trump's success in securing more than $700 billion in new military spending was never solely about rebuilding the armed forces, and that the plan from the outset was for the military to build the wall because it was a national security issue within the president's constitutional powers.[1]

## The "RED CASTLE" decode

Q's instruction to "Think Green/RED CASTLE" and "Think MILITARY" is decoded by Lange as a reference to the [[us-army-corps-of-engineers|US Army Corps of Engineers]], whose insignia is a red castle, with "Greencastle" pointing to Greencastle, Indiana.[1] He notes that Q first hinted at the plan in March 2018, that a follower produced the decode a day later, and that Q confirmed it by including the material in drop #2582 of December 11, 2018; a Trump tweet is offered as further confirmation and as proof of a link between Q and Trump.[1]

## The shutdown and the narrative war

Lange presents the December 11, 2018 televised White House meeting and the ensuing government shutdown as a trap that forced Pelosi and Schumer into public opposition, culminating in their joint televised rebuttal in which Pelosi called the wall "expensive and ineffective" and Schumer declared that "the symbol of America should be the Statue of Liberty, not a thirty-foot wall."[1] In Lange's account the episode gave Trump control of the border security narrative, made the wall more popular, and left border security among the top issues of the subsequent election cycle.[1]
