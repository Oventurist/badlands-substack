# War Profiteering

War profiteering, in the framing advanced by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], describes a recurring cycle by which Western intervention in foreign conflicts generates wealth for a protected ruling class at the expense of taxpayers and civilians.[1]

## The four-phase pattern

DeLarme argued that over the last century a clear pattern has emerged each time a significant war breaks out in which the West intervenes directly or indirectly.[1] First, the public is bombarded with propaganda showing atrocities and rhetoric meant to ignite righteous indignation while key context is ignored.[1] Second, taxpayer dollars fund an unacceptable number of civilian casualties — he cited the killing of two [[reuters|Reuters]] reporters by a U.S. Apache attack helicopter as revealed by WikiLeaks.[1] Third, once sufficient wealth has been generated and unspoken goals achieved, media coverage shifts from high-intensity war propaganda toward balance and eventually criticism, as though the press had never fueled public support.[1] Fourth, financial interests circle the war-torn region to secure lucrative reconstruction contracts.[1]

## Ukraine as the fourth phase

DeLarme identified the conflict in Ukraine as entering that fourth phase. [[alexander-soros|Aleksander Soros]] had recently met with Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]] to reaffirm the commitment of his [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], described as Ukraine's largest independent financier.[1] Alongside Open Society, Wall Street institutions including [[blackrock|BlackRock]] and [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan]] were reportedly preparing to mobilize capital from private and public sector investors toward rebuilding the Ukrainian economy in anticipation of a windfall when post-conflict reconstruction begins.[1]

## Application to Gaza

He extended the analysis to the war between [[israel|Israel]] and [[hamas|Hamas]], asking why it should be treated differently from any other recent war and why House Republicans regarded the campaign in Gaza as more worthy of American tax dollars than Ukraine.[1] Noting that many in the America First movement understand that war is a racket and a powerful tool for a governing class, he expressed disappointment that the same crazed zeal seen after 9/11 — including calls from [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] and [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] to "flatten Gaza" — had reemerged on the Right.[1] The argument frames the [[israel-october-7-foreknowledge-allegations|October 7 foreknowledge allegations]] as a financial-motive question rather than merely an intelligence one.[1]
