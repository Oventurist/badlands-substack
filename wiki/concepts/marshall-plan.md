# Marshall Plan

The Marshall Plan was the post-war American aid program for Europe, described in the article as "nefarious and notorious."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) While the Department of Defense was formally focused on military reconstruction, the article says it indirectly supported the campaign against communist-led labor through the Marshall Plan's labor provisions, which conditioned aid on anti-leftist union reforms in recipient countries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

This conditionality is presented as an early version of a mechanism later used by the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]], whose grant agreements tie funding to alignment with U.S. foreign policy objectives.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) It also complemented the AFL's [[free-trade-union-committee|Free Trade Union Committee]] operations in France and Italy and the sabotage of the [[world-federation-of-trade-unions|World Federation of Trade Unions]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
