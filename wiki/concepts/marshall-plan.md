# Marshall Plan

The Marshall Plan was the post-war American aid program for Europe, described in the article as "nefarious and notorious."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) While the Department of Defense was formally focused on military reconstruction, the article says it indirectly supported the campaign against communist-led labor through the Marshall Plan's labor provisions, which conditioned aid on anti-leftist union reforms in recipient countries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

This conditionality is presented as an early version of a mechanism later used by the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]], whose grant agreements tie funding to alignment with U.S. foreign policy objectives.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) It also complemented the AFL's [[free-trade-union-committee|Free Trade Union Committee]] operations in France and Italy and the sabotage of the [[world-federation-of-trade-unions|World Federation of Trade Unions]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

## Official history

Citing the National Archives, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounts that on April 3, 1948, President [[harry-truman|Harry Truman]] signed the Economic Recovery Act of 1948, which became known as the Marshall Plan after Secretary of State [[george-marshall|George Marshall]], who in 1947 proposed that the United States provide economic assistance to restore the economic infrastructure of postwar Europe.[2] Marshall made the proposal in a speech at Harvard University on June 5, 1947, asking European nations to draw up their own plan for reconstruction with American funding.[2] Truman sent Congress a message following Marshall's ideas on December 19, 1947, Congress overwhelmingly passed the Economic Cooperation Act of 1948, and over the next four years appropriated $13.3 billion for European recovery.[2] The official framing holds that the plan provided markets for American goods, created reliable trading partners, supported stable democratic governments in Western Europe, and extended wartime bipartisanship into the postwar years.[2]

## Revisionist critique

Carlson calculates that the $13.3 billion of 1948 equates to roughly $169 billion today, a figure he compares to what the United States will likely end up giving Ukraine over the course of its conflict with Russia.[2] He argues both cases follow the same pattern: American taxpayers' money is sent overseas, the bulk of it laundered back into the pockets of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] while corporations earn billions rebuilding the war-torn region.[2] He describes Americans as a fish that swallowed a three-pronged hook — taxes sent abroad rather than spent at home, inflation the public is forced to absorb, and factories and production moved overseas that strip jobs and leverage from American workers.[2]

The distribution of funds is Carlson's central evidence against the humanitarian reading. The United Kingdom, France and West Germany received 55% of the money while fifteen other Western European countries divided the remaining 45%, and the Eastern Bloc received nothing.[2] He notes that the Soviet Union suffered an estimated 24 million dead, Poland 5.6 million, Yugoslavia one million, Romania 833,000, Greece 800,000, Hungary 580,000 and Italy 457,000, whereas France (567,000 deaths) and England (450,000) together took 44% of the funds despite representing roughly 2% of European lives lost.[2] West Germany, which he calls "the friggin' enemy," received 11%; East Germany received nothing.[2] The countries he identifies as most controlled by the Cabal — the UK, France and Germany — are precisely the ones that received over half the money.[2]

## Legacy

Carlson concludes that the Marshall Plan was not humanitarian relief but the consolidation of power, the creation of [[nato|NATO]], and the filling of offshore accounts with [[eurodollar|Eurodollars]].[2] In his account it marked the beginning of a new globalist world in which multinational corporations became the new borderless monarchs and elected politicians their puppets, with international business conducted in secret locations by secret groups belonging to secret societies, and anyone who resisted removed from power or from the world.[2] Working backwards from the desired end state — a unified Europe run by corporations — he argues the destruction of Europe in the Second World War was required to make the [[european-union|European Union]] possible, with [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] and the Nazis funded and promoted as the beyond-evil enemy that justified it.[2]

Carlson believes the Cabal attempted to run another Marshall Plan on the world when the Ukraine Conflict began, but that it is not working out because one side is acting against the Deep State rather than cooperating with it.[2] Without full cooperation the plan can only be strung along while funds are shifted into offshore accounts; it will ultimately fall apart, but in the meantime mainstream media claims about the war's progress and politicians' insistence on funding it are moving billions of dollars into the same Eurodollar system the original Marshall Plan created.[2]
