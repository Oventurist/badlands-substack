# Marshall Plan

The Marshall Plan (officially the European Recovery Program) was a United States initiative, announced in 1947, that provided large-scale economic aid to help rebuild Western European economies after World War II. In the Badlands Media article [[a-more-perfect-labor-union|A More Perfect (Labor) Union]], the Marshall Plan's labor provisions are cited as a mechanism that conditioned aid on anti-leftist union reforms.

## Labor provisions

The article states that the [[pentagon|DoD]] ("though focused on military reconstruction") indirectly supported the crackdown on communist-influenced labor via "the nefarious and notorious Marshall Plan's labor provisions that conditioned aid to anti-leftist union reforms in recipient countries." The article frames the Marshall Plan as part of the broader post-war U.S. effort, alongside the coordination under Secretary of State [[edward-stettinius|Edward Stettinius]], to ensure post-war labor movements aligned with American free-market ideals and to prevent Soviet-style worker councils from taking root.
