# Executive Order 13692

**Executive Order 13692** was issued by President Barack Obama in 2015, declaring [[venezuela|Venezuela]] an "unusual and extraordinary threat to the national security" of the United States.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterizes the designation as "laughable on its face," noting that Venezuela's military budget at the time was smaller than that of the New York City Police Department.[1] The order nonetheless unlocked the legal machinery for asset seizures and financial isolation, providing the statutory foundation for the escalating sanctions regime that followed.[1]

The order is presented as a turning point between the earlier, narrowly targeted measures imposed on Venezuelan officials accused of human rights abuses and drug trafficking, and the later economy-wide strangulation pursued under the Trump administration.[1] It also supplied the legal basis for the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]]'s freezing of $7 billion in [[citgo|Citgo]] assets and the transfer of control to [[juan-guaido|Juan Guaidó]]'s parallel government in 2019.[1]
