# Transgender Military Ban

The **transgender military ban** is a policy of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration that presumptively disqualifies transgender people from service in the United States armed forces.[1]

## Supreme Court order (2025)

On a Tuesday in May 2025 the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] allowed the administration to enforce the ban while legal challenges proceeded.[1] The court acted in a dispute over a policy that presumptively disqualifies transgender people from military service and could lead to the expulsion of experienced, decorated officers.[1] The court's three liberal justices said they would have kept the policy on hold; neither the justices in the majority nor those in dissent explained their votes, which is not uncommon in emergency appeals.[1]

## Executive action

Just after beginning his second term in January, Trump moved aggressively to roll back the rights of transgender people.[1] Among his actions was an executive order claiming that the sexual identity of transgender service members "conflicts with a soldier's commitment to an honorable, truthful, and disciplined lifestyle, even in one's personal life" and is harmful to military readiness.[1]

## Commentary

*Badlands News Brief* contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] commented on the Associated Press account, noting that one service member described in the piece as a decorated military veteran transitioned after Trump's ban took effect.[1]
