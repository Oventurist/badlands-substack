# United Airlines Flight 202 Wheel Well Death

On Christmas Eve 2024, a dead body was discovered in the wheel well of a [[united-airlines|United Airlines]] aircraft after it flew roughly 4,000 miles from Chicago to Maui, prompting a police investigation.[1]

## Incident

United Airlines Flight 202 departed Chicago's O'Hare airport at 9:50 a.m. on Tuesday and landed at Maui's Kahului airport at 2 p.m. local time.[1] Upon arrival, a body was found in the wheel well of one of the main landing gears of the Boeing 787-10.[1] The airline said the wheel well "was only accessible from outside of the aircraft" and that "at this time, it is not clear how or when the person accessed the wheel well," adding that United was working with law enforcement authorities on the investigation.[1] The deceased individual was not named.[1]

Maui Police said an active investigation into the circumstances was ongoing, stating that the department was "currently conducting an active investigation regarding a deceased individual discovered on an incoming flight from the mainland this afternoon" and that no further details were available.[1]
