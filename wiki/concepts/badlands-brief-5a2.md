# Badlands Brief: Tina Peters' Sentence Commuted; ISIS Killed in Africa

## Governor Polis commutes Tina Peters' sentence

The opening segment reports that Colorado Governor [[jared-polis|Jared Polis]] commuted the prison sentence of former Mesa County Clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]], who was convicted in 2024 for allowing unauthorized access to county voting systems tied to efforts to challenge the 2020 presidential election results. Peters was expected to be released after serving about two years in prison. She had been sentenced to roughly nine years after prosecutors said she enabled unauthorized access to [[mesa-county|Mesa County]] election equipment and data that later circulated among election-denial activists. An April 2026 Colorado appeals court decision upheld her convictions but vacated the sentence and ordered resentencing, citing issues with the trial judge's sentencing comments.

Polis said the commutation reflected concerns that Peters, a first-time nonviolent offender, received an unusually severe sentence, and that the decision did not endorse her conduct or false claims about the 2020 election. The case had become a national rallying point among supporters of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who repeatedly called for Peters' release and claimed in 2025 that he had pardoned her, despite state officials saying presidential pardons do not apply to state convictions. Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] and other critics said the commutation risks weakening deterrence against election-system tampering and could damage public trust in election administration.

Ashe in America's commentary adds detail on the clemency grant, reported on the Friday before publication: Polis cut Peters' sentence in half, moved up her eligibility date, and granted parole, with the conditions of parole to be determined by a governor-appointed parole board. She reports having hugged Peters on the Saturday after the grant, and describes Peters' priorities upon release: seeing her 97-year-old mother, reclaiming her health, eating fresh food, restoring her body, and reuniting with her dog Minka. Peters planned to eat a thick steak and a salad with real tomatoes and avocado, and told Ashe the first time she slept properly since being incarcerated was the Friday night after the grant of clemency. Asked whether she was coerced into issuing a statement of contrition, Peters said she approved the statement and stands by it. Ashe notes "June 1 can't come soon enough for Tina Peters."

## Drone strike near the Barakah nuclear plant and Trump's warning to Iran

The second segment reports that a drone strike caused a fire near the [[barakah-nuclear-power-plant|Barakah nuclear power plant]] in the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] on May 17, 2026, according to Emirati authorities, who said there were no injuries or radioactive leaks. The UAE defense ministry said three drones entered the country from the western border direction, with two intercepted and one striking an electrical generator outside the plant's inner perimeter in the Al Dhafra region. The UAE blamed [[iran|Iran]] or allied proxy groups; presidential adviser [[anwar-gargash|Anwar Gargash]] called the strike a "dangerous escalation" that violated international law and norms. [[international-atomic-energy-agency|International Atomic Energy Agency]] Director General [[rafael-grossi|Rafael Grossi]] said military activity threatening nuclear safety was "unacceptable" after discussions with UAE officials, while the UAE's nuclear regulator said there was no impact on reactor operations or public safety. [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] condemned the attack and reported intercepting three drones approaching from Iraqi airspace in a separate incident. President Trump warned Iran that "the clock is ticking" in a social media post amid stalled negotiations tied to the broader Iran conflict. The [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] had reported earlier in the month that [[israel|Israel]] built and defended a secret base in [[iraq|Iraq]] during the Iran war to support operations against Tehran.

The third segment reports that Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] was scheduled to visit Beijing on May 19–20, 2026 for talks with Chinese leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], according to a Kremlin announcement released on Saturday, May 16.
