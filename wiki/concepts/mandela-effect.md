# Mandela Effect

The **Mandela Effect** is the name given to the phenomenon in which large numbers of people share detailed memories of a past that does not match the documentary record. In [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]], [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] invokes the Mandela Effect as evidence that neither the past nor the future is "set in stone."[1]

## Explanation offered

The essay argues, following [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|the CTMU]], that time does not "move" anywhere; rather, human beings live eternally in the present moment and generate time through their minds' interaction with the terminal (physical) and non-terminal (metaphysical) domains.[1] On this account, timelines are constantly generated and refined according to the influence of all minds that participate in them, so that a new timeline can be produced within the non-terminal domain and then "take over" from where the old one left off.[1]

The Mandela Effect is presented alongside widely reported experiences of synchronicity as a class of observation that materialism cannot readily explain, but which follows naturally once the non-terminal domain is admitted.[1] The essay groups it with telepathy, retrocausality, and instantaneous medical remissions as phenomena that "cease to be mysterious" under the CTMU framework.[1]
