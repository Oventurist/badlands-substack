# 2025 National Health Care Fraud Takedown

The 2025 National Health Care Fraud Takedown was a coordinated enforcement action announced on July 1, 2025 by the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and federal partners, described at the time as the largest healthcare fraud investigation in DOJ history as measured by financial losses.[1]

## Scope and results

The investigation spanned fifty federal districts. According to Deputy FBI Director [[dan-bongino|Dan Bongino]], it uncovered nearly $3 billion in false claims and more than 15 million illegal distributions of pills. Authorities seized $245 million and charged 324 defendants, among them 96 medical professionals. The intended losses attributable to the schemes approached $15 billion.[1]

## Framing as public corruption

Bongino presented the takedown as part of a broader campaign against public corruption rather than as an isolated healthcare enforcement matter. "Public corruption will not be tolerated as the Director and I vigorously pursue bad actors who violated their oaths to all of us," he wrote, adding that the theft of public funds was viewed in the same light because "it's a crime against all of us."[1]

He closed with a warning framed as a statement of intent: "Results matter. Talk is cheap. And this is not even the beginning of the beginning. If you're stealing from the public, or violating your oath to serve, then we're coming for you too."[1]

## Reception in independent media

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the announcement as a signal rather than merely a statistic. Contributor CannCon, who covered the matter at length on the network's Daily programme, singled out Bongino's phrase "This isn't even the beginning of the beginning," writing that the statement constituted "a message. And a big one!"[1]
