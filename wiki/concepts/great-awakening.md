The Great Awakening is a theme within the [[truth-community]] served by [[badlands-media]], referring to a perceived collective political and spiritual awakening.[1] The network's Raising a Nation show (Saturday, 9pm ET), hosted by The Kate Awakening and Defender of the Republic, centers on "all things Great Awakening from the perspective of parents trying to raise the next generation of sovereign citizens."[1]

## Badlands commentary, December 2023

Writing in the December 7, 2023 News Brief, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] contended that the narrative shifts on Ukraine and Gaza indicated mass-psychological metrics had "inverted, and have now gone parabolic in service of the Great Awakening."[2] He further argued that the Biden administration and family had objectively done more to accelerate the Great Awakening than any patriot-engineered disclosure to that point, and that events made still more sense if the administration's own moves were reframed as patriot-engineered.[2]

## Trump 2.0 as narrative translation

Writing after [[ufc-309|UFC 309]] in November 2024, Badlands Media argued that the advent of Trump 2.0 constitutes a narrative translation layer of the Great Awakening and of the transition to what it calls the Golden Age, staged as a series of apparently organic convergences.[3] The outlet pointed to the "de facto MAGAvengers" — [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] — entering a modern-day colosseum as the culmination of a public narrative arc in which several mass psychological pincers had closed around the central narrative.[3]

## Framing in "Biden, Inc. Flips"

A December 2024 Badlands feature presented the Great Awakening as the intended outcome of a staged [[declas|declassification]] program, describing the strategic release of information as a psychological process as much as a legal one.[4] By exposing alleged truths incrementally, the author argued, the [[white-hats|White Hats]] guide the public through a journey of discovery that keeps revelations both comprehensible and actionable.[4]

The piece framed high-profile pardons and minor legal maneuvers as deliberate signals designed to awaken attentive observers, invoking "system of systems" thinking and the slogans "If it is over the top, it is an Op!" and "We are watching a movie!"[4] It attributed a central role to [[anons|Anons]] and patriots as citizen researchers healing and awakening the wider public, and described the anticipated endpoint as a restoration of trust and a "Golden Era" of accountable, constitutional governance.[4]

## Diffusion model (2025)

In "Countering an Ocean of Deceit," [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] described the Awakening as a gradual diffusion process rather than a single event: "it drips and it drips and it drips some more… and then comes the flood," with only a few people noticing at first, then a few more, and then the Great Awakening itself.[5] He argued that its apparent nearness is no reason to disengage, since there is never a point at which learning the truth is a poor use of one's time, and that the objective is not to reach everyone but to reach the reachable, of whom there are enough to make the critical difference.[5] The minimum public understanding he set out was threefold: that the cabal or [[deep-state|deep state]] is real, that it is evil, and that it cannot be left undefeated — summarised in the slogan "We are the media now."[5]

## "Inflection Point" (January 2023)

In "Inflection Point" (January 6, 2023), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] addressed the charge that Badlands writers who say the "pain" of the Great Awakening is far from over are engaged in "dooming" or spreading cynicism and despair, arguing that the opposite is true.[6] He framed continued turbulence as the necessary cost of awakening more people by the day, and mass psychological tension as not merely a side effect of the transition to a paradigm of sovereignty, freedom and morality but "a key component and engine OF that transition," likening it to birthing pains as a collective sheds "our old skin of subjugated serfs."[6]

He told readers that if 2023 appeared poised to make prior years "seem tame by comparison," they should take it as a sign not of losing but of having reached the summit of the [[information-war|Information War]] and begun the descent toward a valley representing "a new and lasting vision for peace, prosperity and goodness" built on the present struggle — an [[inflection-point|inflection point]] rather than an ending.[6]
