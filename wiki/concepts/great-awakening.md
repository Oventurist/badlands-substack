The Great Awakening is a theme within the [[truth-community]] served by [[badlands-media]], referring to a perceived collective political and spiritual awakening.[1] The network's Raising a Nation show (Saturday, 9pm ET), hosted by The Kate Awakening and Defender of the Republic, centers on "all things Great Awakening from the perspective of parents trying to raise the next generation of sovereign citizens."[1]

## Badlands commentary, December 2023

Writing in the December 7, 2023 News Brief, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] contended that the narrative shifts on Ukraine and Gaza indicated mass-psychological metrics had "inverted, and have now gone parabolic in service of the Great Awakening."[2] He further argued that the Biden administration and family had objectively done more to accelerate the Great Awakening than any patriot-engineered disclosure to that point, and that events made still more sense if the administration's own moves were reframed as patriot-engineered.[2]

## Trump 2.0 as narrative translation

Writing after [[ufc-309|UFC 309]] in November 2024, Badlands Media argued that the advent of Trump 2.0 constitutes a narrative translation layer of the Great Awakening and of the transition to what it calls the Golden Age, staged as a series of apparently organic convergences.[3] The outlet pointed to the "de facto MAGAvengers" — [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] — entering a modern-day colosseum as the culmination of a public narrative arc in which several mass psychological pincers had closed around the central narrative.[3]

## Framing in "Biden, Inc. Flips"

A December 2024 Badlands feature presented the Great Awakening as the intended outcome of a staged [[declas|declassification]] program, describing the strategic release of information as a psychological process as much as a legal one.[4] By exposing alleged truths incrementally, the author argued, the [[white-hats|White Hats]] guide the public through a journey of discovery that keeps revelations both comprehensible and actionable.[4]

The piece framed high-profile pardons and minor legal maneuvers as deliberate signals designed to awaken attentive observers, invoking "system of systems" thinking and the slogans "If it is over the top, it is an Op!" and "We are watching a movie!"[4] It attributed a central role to [[anons|Anons]] and patriots as citizen researchers healing and awakening the wider public, and described the anticipated endpoint as a restoration of trust and a "Golden Era" of accountable, constitutional governance.[4]

## Diffusion model (2025)

In "Countering an Ocean of Deceit," [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] described the Awakening as a gradual diffusion process rather than a single event: "it drips and it drips and it drips some more… and then comes the flood," with only a few people noticing at first, then a few more, and then the Great Awakening itself.[5] He argued that its apparent nearness is no reason to disengage, since there is never a point at which learning the truth is a poor use of one's time, and that the objective is not to reach everyone but to reach the reachable, of whom there are enough to make the critical difference.[5] The minimum public understanding he set out was threefold: that the cabal or [[deep-state|deep state]] is real, that it is evil, and that it cannot be left undefeated — summarised in the slogan "We are the media now."[5]

Badlands contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] treated the [[2023-trump-cpac-speech|2023 Trump CPAC speech]] as the start of "the next stage" of the Great Awakening, arguing that claims which were shocking in 2015-2017 had become "accepted fact" by 2023 and that [[red-pilling|red-pilling]] was spreading "from Twitter to TikTok, at family gatherings, online, offline, and beyond, as the Truth has grown impossible to escape."[9] He framed the period as "a great crossroads in the history of Mankind," writing that a century hence these years would be seen either as the beginning of "a New Globalist World Order run by unelected officials who view people as faceless workers" or as "the moment that Freed the planet and returned it to the People" — adding the maxim "How America Goes, so goes the World."[9]

## "Inflection Point" (January 2023)

In "Inflection Point" (January 6, 2023), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] addressed the charge that Badlands writers who say the "pain" of the Great Awakening is far from over are engaged in "dooming" or spreading cynicism and despair, arguing that the opposite is true.[6] He framed continued turbulence as the necessary cost of awakening more people by the day, and mass psychological tension as not merely a side effect of the transition to a paradigm of sovereignty, freedom and morality but "a key component and engine OF that transition," likening it to birthing pains as a collective sheds "our old skin of subjugated serfs."[6]

He told readers that if 2023 appeared poised to make prior years "seem tame by comparison," they should take it as a sign not of losing but of having reached the summit of the [[information-war|Information War]] and begun the descent toward a valley representing "a new and lasting vision for peace, prosperity and goodness" built on the present struggle — an [[inflection-point|inflection point]] rather than an ending.[6]

## Guidance for newcomers (November 2022)

In "New to the Great Awakening? Making Sense of It All" (November 18, 2022), [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] wrote that every participant "has walked our own unique path of awakening," with some awake for years or decades, some since [[qanon|Q]] first appeared in 2017, and more converted with each election cycle whose outcome defied expectations.[7] Addressed to newer arrivals, the piece set out a series of assurances: that the reader is not crazy but the events and their authors are; that "Evil is real" and has "infiltrated every institution we trusted"; that a war of Good versus Evil is under way in which "GOD HAS ALREADY WON THE FIGHT"; that questioning everything one was taught is normal; that critical thinking is a skill to be re-sharpened; and that one should be kind to oneself about years spent asleep and patient with those slower to wake.[7] It closed the list with "You are not alone. WE ARE THE MAJORITY."[7]

Gillespie described the awakening as a process of accumulating disparate and overwhelming information — [[gain-of-function-research|gain of function]] research, the suppression of [[hydroxychloroquine|hydroxychloroquine]] and [[ivermectin|ivermectin]], deplatforming, [[transhumanism|transhumanism]], child sex trafficking, [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]], [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] and Satanism — that only cohered once she organised it into categories on a chart she made on October 6, 2021.[7] Her framework named the adversary as "The Cabal/Globalists/Illuminati/[[freemasonry|Freemasons]]/[[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]" and grouped its methods into six "buckets": central banks, [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]]/Big Med, [[big-tech|Big Tech]], mind control, military/defense and corruption.[7] She recommended three introductory videos, including "'[[great-reset|The Great Reset]]' Is Happening NOW" with [[catherine-austin-fitts|Catherine Austin Fitts]] and Janet Ossebaard's *Fall of the Cabal*, and urged prayer for discernment in choosing whom to follow, warning that the road ahead would be "very hard" and "very scary."[7]

## Immigration as an unaddressed precondition (January 2025)

In "On Golden Bridges and the Art of War," published January 28, 2025, [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] argued that there can be no Great Awakening so long as the use of immigration policy by the [[deep-state|Deep State]] to destabilise Western societies remains unaddressed in a comprehensive manner.[8] He identified reducing the popularity of immigration among Western publics as a key step on the path to the Awakening, and read the [[h-1b-visa-program|H-1B visa]] controversy as part of the [[qanon|Q]] strategy of bringing the people to the precipice by making their disenfranchisement undeniable.[8]

The author also invoked the claim attributed to Q that the [[cabal|cabal]]'s crimes against children would be the key catalyst, holding that the exposure of the United Kingdom's [[grooming-gangs|grooming gangs]] had begun that process and stood one step from topics such as [[pizzagate|pizzagate]] and [[adrenochrome|adrenochrome]].[8] He characterised the Great Awakening as doubling as a "Great Cleansing," in which everyone who excused the disenfranchisement of Western citizenry would be made to answer for supporting treason — while allowing that some such figures would turn out to have been actors playing a role in exposing the criminals.[8]

## Q For Dummies framing

The Badlands Media "Q For Dummies" series by [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] describes the [[qanon|Q]] drops as partly responsible for triggering the period many refer to as the Great Awakening.[10] In Chapter 13, the author argued that the awakening was visibly accelerating, writing that after seven years of work the movement had exposed a great deal and woken a great many people, and concluding "it truly is a great awakening."[10]

The chapter tied the process to two mechanisms named in Drop #96: the eventual coherence of the drops — "it will all make sense," described as a puzzle coming together — and the displacement of legacy media by citizen journalists and content creators, whom the author judged more effective at cutting through noise than many had thought possible.[10] It also cited Trump-era transparency initiatives, including [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s remarks about declassifying files on 9/11, JFK and [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]], as evidence that transparency is the only way forward.[10]

## As a physiological and fractal event (June 2025)

The June 26, 2025 essay "Signal Collapse" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] recast the Great Awakening in somatic terms, arguing that it "is not just an idea — it is a physiological event," and that public purges from protests to awakenings are "somatic discharges of a collective body returning to coherence."[12] Elsewhere in the same piece the phenomenon is described as "a neural reboot of human consciousness" rather than a movement, broken open not by opposition but by pattern recognition across Iran, Ukraine, [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]], elections and censorship.[12]

The essay situates the Great Awakening within a [[fractal-of-awakening|fractal model]] in which individual and civilizational awakening follow the identical sequence of dissonance, fracture, memory and alignment, and links it to a planetary [[energetic-purge|energetic purge]] and the [[canon-of-collapse|Canon of Collapse]].[12] It insists the awakening "isn't new — it's ancient," and is only now scaling from self to family, tribe, nation and global body.[12]

## Anticipated psychological cost (October 2024)

Writing before the 2024 election, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that a [[donald-trump|Trump]] victory would usher in a mass exposure exceeding Covid in its effects — "Covid times ten" — and asked whether it would come like the ripping off of a Band-Aid or more gradually.[13] He held that Trump allowed the 2020 election to be stolen because the American public was not yet ready for the mass awakening required to dismantle the system, and suggested the transition into a new system might require baby steps and patience.[13] Carlson described the awakening in terms of a [[five-stages-of-grief|grief process]] and a [[mass-awakening|flash-flood model]] of absorption, and urged those already awake not to gloat but to help the recoverable while ignoring the permanently lost.[13]
