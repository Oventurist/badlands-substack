# Post-COVID Mass Shootings and State Gun Laws

In January 2023, Just the News published an analysis finding that many of the worst mass shootings in the United States since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic occurred in states with tight gun laws.[1] The report was prompted by a mass shooting in California that left 10 people dead that weekend.[1]

## Findings

The single worst mass shooting since the start of the pandemic occurred in [[uvalde-school-shooting|Uvalde, Texas]], where 21 elementary school students were killed; the Giffords Law Center to Prevent Gun Violence gives Texas an F grade for gun laws, as the state allows permitless carry and does not restrict assault weapons.[1] However, of the five worst shootings in the United States since 2020, four occurred in states holding at least a "B" grade from Giffords.[1] Excluding murder-suicides within a family, half of the ten deadliest mass shootings over the period occurred in states that received at least an "A-" gun law grade.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that the public is repeatedly told guns are a serious problem and gun control the answer, yet the places where gun control has been in effect for years see the most mass shootings.[1] He predicted that "the globalists will try to spin this in some way — they are master spinsters," but maintained that "the truth is plain to see."[1]
