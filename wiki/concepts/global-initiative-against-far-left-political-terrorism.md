# Global Initiative Against Far-Left Political Terrorism

The Global Initiative Against Far-Left Political Terrorism is a multinational effort launched in Washington in July 2026 by the second [[donald-trump]] administration to coordinate action against what it describes as transnational far-left political terrorism.

## Launch

As reported in the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, Secretary of State [[marco-rubio]] convened officials from more than 65 countries at the [[state-department]] to inaugurate the initiative. The administration alleged that violent left-wing extremist networks have become a growing global security threat and announced plans to expand international cooperation, terrorist designations, sanctions, and visa restrictions.

White House Deputy Chief of Staff [[stephen-miller]] said the administration was entering an "action phase" pairing diplomatic, law enforcement, and financial tools against organizations that use violence or intimidation to advance political objectives. Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent]] said the [[department-of-the-treasury]] would intensify efforts to investigate and disrupt the funding networks behind such groups.

Administration officials maintained that the initiative targets violent transnational organizations rather than lawful political activity. Critics argued it risks conflating extremist violence with broader political movements.

## Badlands framing

[[burning-bright]] read the announcement as an appetizer to the President's prime-time address the same evening, and argued that the partisan "far-left terrorism" framing understates what the administration is actually confronting. In his account, infiltration is the preferred method of asymmetric warfare against a United States that cannot be defeated conventionally: subversion concentrated on the left but pervasive across American government and society, running from the proliferation and siloing of non-governmental organizations to anti-American rhetoric and policy inside the judicial and legislative branches.

On this reading the campaign against election fraud and foreign interference and the campaign against what he calls the terrestrial Marxist and [[communism|communist]] [[deep-state]] are not separate efforts but "twin fronts of the same existential conflict," with fixing the election system a necessity and the public exposure and punishment of the underlying ideological cultivation the true endgame.
