# 2025 Green Card Processing Pause

In March 2025 the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] suspended the finalization of certain applications for lawful permanent residence — commonly known as green cards — in order to conduct additional vetting of applicants.[1] [[uscis|U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services]], the component of the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] that handles citizenship, legal status, and other immigration benefits, implemented the pause to comply with an executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in January 2025.[1]

## Executive order basis

The order, signed January 20, 2025 and titled "Protecting the United States from Foreign Terrorists and Other National Security and Public Safety Threats," directed agencies to "vet and screen to the maximum degree possible all aliens who intend to be admitted, enter, or are already inside the United States, particularly those aliens coming from regions or nations with identified security risks."[1]

## Scope

CBS News reported on March 25, 2025 that USCIS had directed staff to conduct additional vetting of refugees and of people granted asylum who had applied for legal permanent residency.[1] A DHS spokesperson said the agency was "placing a temporary pause on finalizing certain adjustment of status applications pending the completion of additional screening and vetting."[1]
