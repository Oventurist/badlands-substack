# Israel Lobby

The "Israel Lobby" is the term used in Badlands commentary for the network of advocacy organizations, donors, elected officials, and media figures said to press US policy toward maximal support for [[israel|Israel]]. In the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]], [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invokes the concept as the principal obstacle to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s regional peace efforts.

## Argument advanced in the Brief

The commentary assembles a set of episodes as evidence: Ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]]'s "Nile to the Euphrates" remarks and the international condemnation they drew; [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]]'s declaration that Israel will occupy [[gaza|Gaza]] and rebuild settlements; [[zeev-elkin|Ze'ev Elkin]]'s refusal to fund Gaza reconstruction through the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]]; the formal rejection of the Board of Peace by senior officials in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s government; [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] doubling down on her attacks against Emir [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Tamim]] of [[qatar|Qatar]] after Trump publicly asked her to stop; [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] repeatedly posting "Get Khamenei!"; and Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] comparing Trump to [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and calling him a "wuss" should he decline to strike [[iran|Iran]].

From this the author concludes that the figures involved "are not earnestly seeking peace" but are "bloodthirsty sociopaths fawning for war," and argues that the strongest possible demonstration of leadership Trump could offer his Arab partners would be "to stand up to the Israel Lobby and the NeoCons, and stop their warmongering plans." The claim is explicitly interpretive and is stated as the commentator's own judgment rather than reported fact.
