# 2024 Dueling Border Visits

The **2024 dueling border visits** were simultaneous appearances at the U.S.–Mexico border on Thursday, February 29, 2024 by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], made some 300 miles apart in Texas.[1]

## The visits

Biden challenged his predecessor to "join me" in securing the country's southern frontier, while Trump blamed the president for lawlessness at the border.[1] The remarks came at a moment of political peril for Biden, who had faced criticism from both parties as the number of people crossing into the United States reached record levels, with migrant encounters more than double those of the Trump years.[1] Both men sought to leverage what was likely to become the most volatile policy dispute of the 2024 campaign.[1]

Speaking in Brownsville, a border city in the Rio Grande Valley, Biden called on Trump to help pass a bipartisan bill in [[congress|Congress]] that would significantly crack down on border crossings.[1] Republicans, at Trump's urging, had torpedoed the [[bipartisan-border-bill-2024|bill]] — legislation they themselves had demanded — saying it was not strong enough.[1] "Instead of telling members of Congress to block this legislation, join me," Biden said, adding: "You know and I know it's the toughest, most efficient, most effective border security bill this country has ever seen. Instead of playing politics with the issue, why don't we just get together and get it done."[1]

The New York Times characterized Biden's words as a political dare, but also as an acknowledgment of Trump's power over the Republican Party on the border at a time when many Americans named immigration as their top concern and doubted Biden was addressing it.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that "it's definitely an election year," with Democrats talking about deporting people while Biden pretended to be strong on border security and blamed the invasion of the US on Republicans.[1] She contrasted the two appearances, noting that Biden "babbled on about climate change" while Trump spoke about [[laken-riley|Laken Riley]], a contrast summarized in a side-by-side clip circulated by Colin Rugg.[1] She also cited a clip posted by Raheem Kassam in which migrants could be heard chanting Trump's name — "They like Trump, can you believe it?" the former president laughed — and concluded, "The whole world wants Trump back. Can't come soon enough."[1]

Biden made remarks on the Gaza aid convoy killings outside the [[white-house|White House]] on the south lawn as he departed for the border trip.[1]
