# Black Music Month Proclamation (2023)

On May 31, 2023, President [[joe-biden|Joseph R. Biden Jr.]] issued a presidential proclamation designating June 2023 as Black Music Month.[1] The document, released through the [[white-house|White House]] briefing room, paid homage to "legends of American music, who have composed the soundtrack of American life," and credited their creativity with giving rise to distinctly American art forms that influence contemporary music worldwide.[1]

The proclamation called on public officials, educators, and all the people of the United States to observe the month by honoring Black musicians and by raising awareness and appreciation of Black music, and framed the occasion as a recommitment to "expanding the promise of dignity and opportunity for all Americans."[1]

## Context and commentary

The proclamation was one of several issued the same day. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that June 2023 had simultaneously been proclaimed Black Music Month, National Caribbean-American Heritage Month, Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer, and Intersex Pride Month, National Immigrant Heritage Month, National Ocean Month, and National Homeownership Month.[1] The accompanying commentary treated the proliferation of overlapping designations satirically, presenting them as a menu of competing virtue signals rather than as substantive policy.[1]
