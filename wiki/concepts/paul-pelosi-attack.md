# Paul Pelosi Attack

The Paul Pelosi attack refers to the October 2022 assault on [[paul-pelosi|Paul Pelosi]], husband of then-U.S. House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], at the couple's San Francisco home — an incident that became a persistent subject of dispute in the Truth Community over the withholding of police body camera footage.[1]

## Release of the footage

In late January 2023 court officials announced that the police body camera video showing the attack would be released to the media on Friday morning.[1] A statement released by the San Francisco Superior Court said the video and audio in the case of *The People v. [[david-depape|David DePape]]* would be made "available to accredited media members" at 9 a.m. Friday, in accordance with Judge Stephen M. Murphy's order issued Wednesday.[1]

Media representatives were able to pick up CDs containing the footage and audio recordings from clerks at the Hall of Justice upon providing identification, a completed criminal records request form, and a $25 payment.[1]

## Commentary

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that many at [[badlands-media|Badlands]] had been calling for the release of the first responder's body cam footage from the "attacker" fiasco and that the release appeared to grant that wish, but urged readers to temper their expectations.[1] He argued that the officials involved "have long been masters of covering up their crimes," questioned whether the footage would be doctored or authentic, and reasoned that having had ample time to assess the recording, they "likely wouldn't be releasing it if it was" damning.[1]

## Alleged coincidence with the Musk Twitter acquisition

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] highlighted in August 2024 that the attack on Paul Pelosi occurred on October 28, 2022 — the same date [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] officially completed his purchase of [[twitter|Twitter]].[2] Carlson presented the shared date as more than coincidence, suggesting it could represent [[deep-state|Deep State]] retaliation and a warning to any figure in [[big-tech|Big Tech]] contemplating a break with the Cabal, while leaving the conclusion to readers.[2] He also raised the question of which side [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] was playing for, and for how long, in the context of the same realignment.[2]
