# Nature of Civilizations series

The Nature of Civilizations series is a planned sequence of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] features by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] examining the underlying mechanics by which civilizations cohere, thrive, or fall under tyranny.[1] Its first installment, "The Steering Wheel of Civilization," was published on February 24, 2023 and drew 105 likes.[1]

## Framing questions

The opening feature poses two guiding questions: how tyrants and deceivers gain control over the "steering wheel" of a civilization, and why a stable anchor at the center of society acts as a shield against tyranny while propelling humanity toward lasting freedom and prosperity.[1]

## Core arguments of the first installment

The essay holds that at the heart of any social grouping — family, town, city or nation — is a central focus, a steering wheel, which guides collective labor.[1] When guided by objective truth, beauty and goodness, civilization thrives and its people are free of needless pain and toil.[1] It introduces the framework of [[parallel-duties-and-perpendicular-freedoms|parallel duties and perpendicular freedoms]] as the "physics of civilization," and argues that deceivers long ago learned to obtain the rewards of others' labor without doing their share, becoming over time the [[parasitic-elite|parasitic elite]].[1]

A second theme is [[common-purpose|common purpose]] as the glue of coordinated group activity, along with the role of social inclusion psychology and [[identity-politics|identity politics]] in maintaining in-group preference.[1] A third is the definition of the [[shadow-men|Shadow Men]], drawn from [[anthony-napoleon|Anthony Napoleon]]'s book, as the psychological profile shared by parasitic elites across otherwise unrelated groupings.[1] The final section develops "value mechanics," arguing that values are the steering wheel and that a conflicting value system disables individuals and societies alike, while a harmonized one makes near-superhuman productivity possible.[1]

The installment culminates in the claim that the grandest and most sweeping common truth and value available to humanity is a spiritual one, and that the quest to seek, know and strive to be like the Creator of all things constitutes an [[ultimate-value|ultimate value]] or value singularity capable of incorporating all other good and true values.[1]

## Planned continuation

Deschamps states that future installments will explore more of the civilizational mechanics intentionally hidden from the masses by the parasitic elite, and that a full treatment of the Shadow Men psychological profile will appear in a later post.[1] He frames the rediscovery of this knowledge as preparation for the day when the freedoms many died to protect can truly be kept.[1] The article carries Badlands Media's standard disclaimer that features represent the opinions of contributing authors rather than the views of Badlands Media itself.[1]
