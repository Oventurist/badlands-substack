# Bundibugyo Virus

**Bundibugyo virus** is a species of ebolavirus first identified in Uganda and named for the district where it emerged. It is one of the strains capable of causing [[ebola|Ebola]] virus disease in humans.

According to the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026, the Bundibugyo strain was responsible for the outbreak centered in Ituri province in northeastern the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of Congo]] that spread into North Kivu and South Kivu provinces and into [[uganda|Uganda]], reaching major population centers including Goma and Kampala. The article emphasizes that no vaccines or treatments are available for this strain, a fact that shaped the response of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] and the [[international-rescue-committee|International Rescue Committee]] and their calls for intensified surveillance, isolation and rapid funding.
