# Liberal International Order

The liberal international order, commonly referred to as the "international rules-based order," is the post-1940s framework of international institutions, alliances and norms that Badlands Media coverage identifies as the track along which the American empire has run and from which populist movements now threaten to derail it.[1]

## The globalist–populist division

The article asserts that while many political, economic and cultural factions exist, "the globalists and the populists are the only two games in town right now."[1] The globalists are defined as the international billionaires and private and political operatives who populate exclusive think tanks such as the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]; the article contends that this class has lost favour with common people in many nations.[1] The populist alternative is described as a more nationalist form of politics comprising right-wing and independent figures around the world.[1]

## Institutional pillars

The "establishment" side of the contest is described as encompassing the World Bank, the International Monetary Fund, the military-industrial complex, NATO, the United Nations and the U.S. interagency bureaucracy.[1]

## Method of maintenance

The article characterizes a "typical globalist play" as creating a problem and then presenting oneself as the hero by offering a meagre solution to that problem, always in exchange for something — citing [[peter-brabeck-letmathe|Peter Brabeck-Letmathe]]'s stance on water commodification as illustrative.[1] It argues that the reason ordinary people hear little about these actors is that, until recently, they enjoyed near-total dominance in the information war, waged in the [[cognitive-warfare|cognitive battlespace]].[1]
