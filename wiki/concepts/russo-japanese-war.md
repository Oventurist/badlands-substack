# Russo-Japanese War

The Russo-Japanese War of 1904–05 is treated in Badlands Media coverage as a financially engineered blow against Imperial Russia at precisely the moment Russia was positioned to join a global program of rail and industrial development.[1]

In 1905, as the [[trans-siberian-railway|Trans-Siberian Railway]] was completed and American engineers finished plans for the [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait]] rail project, Wall Street financier [[jacob-schiff|Jacob Schiff]] gave $200 million to the Japanese to assist their victory over the Russians.[1] The war crippled the Russian state and military, with the Russian navy wiped out in the fighting.[1] Schiff was awarded the Medal of the Rising Sun at the Meiji Palace in 1907 for this assistance.[1]

The war also supplied the human material for revolutionary agitation. The journalist [[george-kennan|George Kennan]] visited Russian prisoners of war held in Japan, distributed revolutionary literature with Japanese permission and Schiff's financing, and claimed that tens of thousands of Russian officers and men returned home as "ardent revolutionists."[1]
