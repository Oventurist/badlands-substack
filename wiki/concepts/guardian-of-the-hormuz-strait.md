# Guardian of the Hormuz Strait

"Guardian of the Hormuz Strait" is the role President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced in July 2026 that the United States would assume over the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]

## Announcement

In a post on [[truth-social|Truth Social]], Trump said the United States would be known as the "Guardian of the Hormuz Strait," providing security for the strategic waterway while allowing all countries — except [[iran|Iran]] and its customers under the "Iranian Blockade" — to continue using it.[1] He said the United States would be reimbursed through a proposed 20% fee on all cargo shipped through the Strait to cover the costs of providing security, and that the process would begin immediately.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] paired the announcement with a June 23 comment from Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and with reports that Iran had struck multiple bases in Kuwait, Jordan and Bahrain.[1] He restated a previously discussed theory that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] had used weather modification technology in advanced radar systems in [[qatar|Qatar]] to dry out the region, specifically targeting Iran with historic drought, and suggested that the apparent skirmishes between Trump and Iran might be cover for a more sophisticated plot to wipe out those radar stations.[1] He also questioned Trump's use of the word "They," proposing that it might refer not to Iran but to a transnational cartel with assets everywhere, including in Tehran and Tel Aviv.[1]
