# 2024 Bryansk Region Incursion Attempt

The **2024 Bryansk Region incursion attempt** was a reported cross-border raid into Russia's Bryansk Region from Ukraine, repelled by Russian forces and announced by regional governor Aleksandr Bogomaz on Sunday, October 27, 2024.[1]

## The incident

According to Bogomaz's statement on Telegram, the Russian military, border guards and National Guard units together repelled an attempted incursion.[1] An "armed group" sought to cross the Russian–Ukrainian border near the village of Manev, a small settlement located directly on the border that has been abandoned for years.[1] Border guards, military and security forces opened fire on the intruders and forced them to retreat.[1]

The size of the would-be incursion force and its potential losses remained unclear, and Bogomaz reported no Russian casualties.[1] He said the situation in the region remained stable and the border area was under the control of the regional operations headquarters.[1] Neither the Russian Defense Ministry nor the Ukrainian military commented on the incident.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Bryansk is directly adjacent to the Kursk Region, site of the earlier [[kursk-incursion|Ukrainian incursion]] that had reportedly been defeated.[1] He characterized the new attempt as reeking of desperation, coming in the same period that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and allied leaders convened in [[kazan|Kazan]] for the [[2024-brics-kazan-summit|BRICS summit]], and amid reports that elite Ukrainian special forces in Kursk were going out of their way to preemptively surrender to Russia's Chechen special forces.[1] He dismissed the notion that such raids could erode Russian domestic support for Putin.[1]
