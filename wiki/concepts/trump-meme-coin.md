# $TRUMP Meme Coin

**$TRUMP** is a cryptocurrency meme coin launched by President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Friday, January 17, 2025, in the days before his second inauguration. It represented Trump's latest venture into cryptocurrency and merchandise sales.[1]

## Launch and market performance

Trump announced the launch in a Truth Social post late on Friday, saying the cryptocurrency was celebrating "WINNING" the presidential election and his upcoming inauguration.[1] Shortly after launching, the price of $TRUMP rose by more than 300% by Saturday morning, and it continued rallying through Saturday night and early Sunday morning before paring back some of its gains later Sunday.[1]

The digital asset, hosted on the Solana blockchain, briefly surpassed $75 early Sunday morning, bringing the total market capitalization of all tokens in circulation to a peak of more than $15 billion. By 5:25 p.m. EST Sunday it was trading at just over $46, with a market cap of roughly $9.36 billion according to CoinMarketCap.[1]

The launch was carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief published on Inauguration Day, January 20, 2025.[1]
