# US Bitcoin Strategic Reserve

The US Bitcoin strategic reserve is a proposal for the United States government to accumulate and hold [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] as a sovereign reserve asset, argued by proponents as a defense against Chinese efforts to displace the US dollar as the global reserve currency.[1]

## The strategic argument

A CoinDesk opinion piece published in late December 2024 argued that finance is increasingly a weapon of war, and that US policymakers and their allies focus too narrowly on macroeconomic tools such as sanctions and dollar promotion while the modern front evolves — with the real battles being waged on smartphones and in global currency markets.[1] The piece described China as pursuing a multi-decade plan to displace the dollar, the United States' greatest asset, warning that without reserve-currency status American influence would weaken and its debt would become a larger problem — precisely the outcome sought by the Chinese Communist Party and the Kremlin.[1]

China and Russia have shed billions of dollars of US Treasury holdings while building their gold stockpiles, and sanctions designed to separate countries from the Western economic system are described as no longer a sufficient deterrent for states that can control financial activity within their borders and project power outward.[1] Authoritarian adversaries — including China, Iran and Russia — are said to be actively building parallel cross-border economic systems capable of pulling in not only neighboring countries but also US allies that trade heavily with them.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GMONEY quoted Max Keiser: "They're dumping their US treasury bonds, they're dumping whatever they can and they're putting it into Bitcoin… we're gonna see #Bitcoin be the major winner in this sovereign debt collapse, globally. That's how you get to 200…300…$400,000 per BTC."[1] GMONEY's own summary was that the central bank that prints money first to buy Bitcoin wins, framing the asset as "Bitpower" and a national stockpile.[1]

## Wintermute forecast

The crypto-trading firm [[wintermute|Wintermute]], in an annual review and outlook, predicted that the United States would begin consultations to create a strategic bitcoin reserve, with China, the [[united-arab-emirates|UAE]] and Europe following suit.[2] The forecast sat alongside other predictions that crypto would be more deeply integrated into traditional finance through ETFs and corporate holdings, that a major corporate acquisition or merger would be settled in stablecoins, and that a systemically important bank would offer spot cryptocurrency trading to clients.[2] Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] responded to the coverage with "You heard it here first!"[2]

## July 2024 Nashville conference speculation

In July 2024, rumors circulating on social media suggested that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] could announce [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] as a "strategic reserve asset" during a Bitcoin conference in Nashville later that month, a prospect analysts described as a potential "watershed moment."[3] [[dennis-porter|Dennis Porter]], co-founder and chief executive of the [[satoshi-action-fund|Satoshi Action Fund]], said he had the information from "credible" sources and wrote that "adding #Bitcoin as a 'strategic reserve' to the US Treasury is a no brainer and once the USA does it, the paradigm will shift and the world will understand they must also have a #Bitcoin position."[3]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] linked the speculation to Bitcoin's price strength over the weekend following the [[2024-crowdstrike-global-outage|CrowdStrike outage]], arguing that repeated threats to the global banking, investing and cyber security "system of systems" would create cognitive dissonance for those who believed Bitcoin could simply be "shut off" while the network remained "safe, reliable, and 'on.'"[3] He tied the pattern to what he saw as [[deep-state|Deep State]] groundwork for an election steal via a false-flag cyber attack, and noted that Trump had already referenced Bitcoin and Bitcoin mining as a national security matter.[3]
