# Generational Divide

The **generational divide** is the framing used on Badlands Media's weekly program [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] for the split in political outlook and expectation between Boomers, Millennials and Gen Z within the American electorate, and specifically for the balancing act it imposes on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Treatment on The Blitz

In Episode 23, subtitled "Generation America" and published on 3 July 2026, hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the divide as something Trump "is being forced to navigate" rather than a passive demographic fact — that is, as an active constraint on how his administration presents its agenda to three constituencies with different formative experiences and different appetites for institutional change.[1] The discussion was billed as frank and direct, and was paired in the same episode with the hosts' account of the intra-party civil wars afflicting both the Right and the Left sides of the [[uniparty|Uniparty]], the implication being that the generational split cuts across, and partly drives, those factional fights.[1]

The episode aired the day before Independence Day and was framed as an early start to the holiday's festivities, with the "Generation America" title tying the generational question to the broader theme of national continuity.[1]
