# 2025 Russian Drone Incursion into Poland

The 2025 Russian drone incursion into Poland refers to the entry of Russian drones into Polish airspace early on Wednesday, September 10, 2025, and the subsequent shoot-down of those drones by Polish forces backed by military aircraft from [[nato|NATO]] allies.[1] It was the first time a member of the Western military alliance is known to have fired shots during Russia's war in Ukraine.[1]

## Civil aviation consequences

The incursion reignited safety concerns over the vulnerability of civil air transport in Europe, according to aviation and insurance experts, and represented the latest upheaval facing airlines from escalating global conflict.[1] Warsaw Chopin and Modlin airports, along with Rzeszow and Lublin airports in eastern [[poland|Poland]], temporarily closed before resuming operations.[1]

## Precedent and scale

Countries bordering Ukraine had reported occasional Russian missiles or drones entering their airspace since Russia's 2022 invasion, but not on such a large scale, and they were not known to have shot them down previously.[1] The episode was reported by [[reuters|Reuters]].[1]
