# Wagner Group Mutiny

The Wagner Group mutiny was the short-lived June 2023 armed revolt launched by [[wagner-group|Wagner]] chief [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]] against the Russian military leadership.[1]

## Aftermath

One month after the failed mutiny, Prigozhin was photographed at a [[russia-africa-summit-2023|Russia-Africa summit]] in St. Petersburg hosted by President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], greeting visiting African officials on the sidelines of the event.[1] His public reappearance undercut Western expectations that he had been removed or eliminated following the revolt.[1]

## Military deception interpretation

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the "mutiny" was best understood as military deception (MILDEC): Prigozhin and Putin had, in his account, publicly engaged in very obvious MILDEC just one month prior during the defense of Bakhmut, a reading circulated across [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] for weeks beforehand.[1] He drew a parallel to the perennial mainstream claim that "the walls are closing in" on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], suggesting that audiences whipped into a frenzy about Putin's imminent downfall should check their underlying premises.[1]
