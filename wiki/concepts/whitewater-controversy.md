# Whitewater Controversy

The **Whitewater controversy** was one of the first major scandals of the [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] presidency, arising from the Clintons' real-estate dealings in Arkansas and generating years of federal investigation.[1] In Badlands Media's "[[irregular-warfare|Irregular Warfare]]," Whitewater is significant less for its own facts than as the origin point of a long relationship between the Clinton family and [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]].[1]

## The Rosenstein connection

Quoting the Rush Limbaugh Show, the article states that Rosenstein served on the Whitewater prosecution team, an investigation that ultimately found the Clintons had done nothing.[1] It further asserts that Rosenstein was in charge of the FBI travel office case — the matter in which the Clinton White House was found to have improperly obtained [[fbi|FBI]] files on White House travel office employees.[1] Shortly after Rosenstein cleared [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] in the Whitewater matter, his wife, [[lisa-barsoomian|Lisa Barsoomian]], represented Bill Clinton in a 1998–99 federal civil case.[1]

## Interpretation

Lange treats this sequence as evidence that Rosenstein repeatedly shielded the Clintons and that doing so was the mechanism by which he rose to the highest levels of the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]].[1] The alleged pattern is used to argue two things: that the Clinton network would trust Rosenstein to protect it when he was installed as Deputy Attorney General, and that his own record left him vulnerable to being leveraged.[1] Limbaugh's own summary of the arrangement — a Whitewater prosecutor who cleared Hillary, whose wife then represented Bill — is quoted in the article as "incestuous."[1]
