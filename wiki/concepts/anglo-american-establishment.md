# Anglo-American Establishment

The **Anglo-American Establishment** is the term coined by historian [[carroll-quigley|Carroll Quigley]] for what he described as a sprawling marriage of government and corporations spanning Britain and the United States.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] uses the concept as the organizing frame for his account of U.S. policy toward [[venezuela|Venezuela]], arguing that the establishment has spread itself into foreign countries through the [[imf|International Monetary Fund]], the [[world-bank|World Bank]], and intermediary consulting firms.[1] He explicitly frames his critique as directed not at any single administration but at the broader Western foreign policy establishment — politicians, corporations, and tax-exempt organizations that profit from and perpetuate NATO-style globalism, or what is often called on the left "Western imperialism."[1]

The mechanism described overlaps closely with the [[economic-hit-man|economic hit man]] playbook set out by [[john-perkins|John Perkins]]: seduce a resource-rich government with enormous loans, route the money to U.S. engineering contractors, and then demand privatization of state assets when the debt becomes unpayable.[1]
