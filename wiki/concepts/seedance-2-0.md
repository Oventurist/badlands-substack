# Seedance 2.0

Seedance 2.0 is an AI video-generation tool released in China by [[bytedance|ByteDance]] in February 2026. It produces short cinematic videos from text, image, audio, and video inputs.[1]

## Viral outputs and studio response

Within days of release, Seedance-generated clips featuring recognizably realistic portrayals of Hollywood actors and characters circulated online, some interpreted as resembling scenes from big-budget productions.[1]

[[disney|Walt Disney]] and [[paramount-global|Paramount]] sent cease-and-desist letters to ByteDance alleging unauthorized use of copyrighted material and characters. Disney's counsel described the model's outputs as a "virtual smash-and-grab of Disney's IP," calling it "willful, pervasive and totally unacceptable" and "just the tip of the iceberg."[1] The Human Artistry Campaign called the launch "an attack on every creator around the world," warning that "stealing isn't innovation" and that unauthorized [[deepfake|deepfakes]] and voice clones violate personal autonomy.[1] ByteDance promised improved IP safeguards.[1]

## Analysis

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed Seedance as a stress test of US and global intellectual-property enforcement, comparing it to Napster: attempts to suppress file sharing produced Kazaa, LimeWire, BitTorrent and a digital music revolution, and attempts to suppress generative video will accelerate rather than halt the transformation.[1] She argued that audiences have already detached from studios, theaters, and award shows but remain attached to characters, so the democratization of franchises — the studios' most valuable assets — is the real stake, and that litigation will arrive too late to reverse it.[1]
