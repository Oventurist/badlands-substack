# Narrative Seeding

Narrative seeding is a term used within alternative and citizen-journalist media to describe the deliberate advance placement of storylines in the press so that a later policy, conflict or crisis arrives with public expectation already prepared.[1]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked the concept in December 2023 while analysing Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]]'s pledge to draft "pre-invasion sanctions from hell" against [[china|China]] should it move on [[taiwan|Taiwan]].[1] He suspected that a China–Taiwan storyline was being positioned as a successor to Russia–Ukraine and Israel–Gaza in the rotation of headline conflicts.[1]

His central observation was that narrative seeding — once a term unique to anons and citizen journalists on the fringes of alternative media and the decentralized internet — had become widely recognized, called out on Truth Social, X and even in mainstream outlets.[1] Where it had once been treated as a conspiracy theory to suggest that much of modern war is waged in headlines, that claim had become broadly accepted, meaning that even the "normie layers" of what he calls the Collective Mind were growing wise to coming narrative deployments without conscious effort.[1] The consequence, in his framing, is diminished buy-in when such deployments are launched and a loss of the mass psychological energy on which the ruling establishment depends for its mandate.[1]
