# 2024 Rafah Border Shooting Incident

The 2024 Rafah border shooting incident was an exchange of fire between Egyptian and Israeli soldiers near the [[rafah-border-crossing|Rafah border crossing]] between [[egypt|Egypt]] and the Gaza Strip on Monday, May 27, 2024, in which a member of Egypt's security forces was killed.[1]

## The incident

Egypt's military spokesperson announced the death in a statement and said an investigation was under way.[1] The Israeli military had earlier said it was investigating reports of an exchange of fire between Israeli and Egyptian soldiers, stating, "A few hours ago (Monday), a shooting incident occurred on the Egyptian border. The incident is under review and discussions are being held with the Egyptians."[1] Later that day Egypt warned against compromising the security and safety of its security forces, with a high-level source telling the state-linked Al Qahera News that Egypt would take necessary measures to prevent a recurrence.[1]

According to two Egyptian security sources, an Egyptian soldier stationed on a watchtower reacted to seeing an armoured vehicle carrying Israeli troops cross a boundary line near the border while the soldiers pursued and killed several Palestinians.[1] The soldier opened fire and Israeli forces returned fire, killing him and sparking a broader exchange in which several Israeli soldiers were wounded, the Israeli force fell back, and one further Egyptian was wounded.[1]

## Context

Israel had seized control of the Rafah border crossing from the Gaza side earlier that month as it stepped up its military offensive in the area, drawing strong criticism from Egypt.[1] The shooting came days after the [[2024-rafah-tent-camp-strike|Rafah tent camp strike]] that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] described as a "tragic mistake."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the backstory of the incident "the most foreboding" element, noting that a soldier's death was "a quick way to revive the mid-century conflicts of yesteryear with Egyptian President Nasser, back when Egypt was the premiere power in the Arab world."[1] The commentary asked why the Egyptian soldier felt obliged to engage the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] at all, reasoning that Egyptian troops would be trained to identify Hamas fighters and presumably operate under stand-down orders in that case, and inferred that the Palestinians being pursued may have been civilians.[1] It closed by noting that [[josep-borrell|Josep Borrell]] and the EU were scheduled to meet Saudi Foreign Minister [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] and his "Team Peace" of Arab foreign ministers and diplomats.[1]
