# Project Verde

Project Verde — Spanish for "Green" — was [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]]'s portion of the firm-wide effort inside [[lehman-brothers|Lehman Brothers]], known internally as "Project Green," to save the bank during its 2008 slide into insolvency by raising new capital and disposing of bad assets.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Bush's assignment was to convince the world's richest man, Mexican billionaire [[carlos-slim|Carlos Slim]], to make a potentially life-saving investment in the firm. Slim was already a Lehman client and had lost a great deal as the bank's stock collapsed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The effort failed: as the Valukas examiner's report records, "On July 5, 2008, Bush reported that the meeting had been unsuccessful because Slim 'did not express interest in jv (joint venture) or stock purchase.'"[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
