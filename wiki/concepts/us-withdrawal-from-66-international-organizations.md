# US Withdrawal From 66 International Organizations

In January 2026 President [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]] signed a Presidential Memorandum directing the United States to withdraw from 66 international organizations that his administration said no longer served American interests.[1]

## Terms of the memorandum

Under the order, US participation and funding end for 35 non-UN bodies and 31 [[united-nations|United Nations]] entities judged contrary to US national interests, security, economic prosperity or sovereignty.[1] The [[white-house|White House]] said the move would stop taxpayer support for entities it deems wasteful or promoting "globalist agendas" and refocus resources on domestic priorities.[1] The administration presented the action as reflecting its stated focus on American sovereignty, fiscal restraint and national security priorities.[1]

## Companion defense-contracting order

The memorandum was signed the same day as a separate Executive Order on [[defense-contractor-buyback-restrictions|defense contractor payouts]], and the two were announced together as measures reshaping US global engagement and defense industry priorities.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the withdrawal as the kind of observable evidence one would expect if the United States were genuinely undergoing a transformation program addressing generational corruption in American institutions.[1] She urged readers to zoom out from the NGO question and consider the second-order effect of tightening Department of Defense contracts, writing that — assuming the changes were real — "the transformation is observable now."[1] She drew an analogy to the earlier cuts to [[usaid|USAID]] funding, after which formerly "free" outlets and NGOs began soliciting money from their audiences, and concluded: "Let's SEE what happens."[1]
