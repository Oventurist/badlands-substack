# Thompson v. United States

*Thompson v. United States* is a [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] decision cited in the June 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2c0|Badlands Brief]] for its holding that the federal statute governing statements to banks applies only to false statements, and not to statements that are merely misleading.

## Effect on the SPLC prosecution

The Brief reports that the superseding indictment obtained by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] against the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] reportedly removes language referring to "false or misleading" statements to banks, a revision attributed directly to the *Thompson* ruling. The change narrows the government's theory on the bank-fraud counts while leaving in place the eleven counts of wire fraud, bank fraud, and conspiracy to commit money laundering that the organization faces. The case therefore appears in the corpus chiefly as a procedural constraint shaping how prosecutors framed the charges.
