# California SB 1174

California SB 1174 is a state law signed by Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] on Sunday, September 29, 2024, barring local authorities from requiring photo identification to vote where such a requirement would exceed the state's own standards.[1]

## Provisions

The law "would prohibit a local government from enacting or enforcing any charter provision, ordinance, or regulation requiring a person to present identification for the purpose of voting or submitting a ballot at any polling place, vote center, or other location where ballots are cast or submitted, as specified."[1] Its text asserts that "voter identification laws have historically been used to disenfranchise low-income voters, voters of color, voters with disabilities, and senior voters" — a claim [[breitbart|Breitbart]] reported as made without evidence.[1]

## Context

California is one of 14 Democrat-run states that do not require any identification to vote, despite requiring it for many other purposes.[1] Voters at polling places are checked off against voter rolls without further proof, while mail-in ballot envelopes carry signatures machine-checked against those on file in voter registration records.[1]

The measure directly targeted the City of Huntington Beach, described as one of the last conservative bastions in the state, which had planned to require photo ID for voting in 2024 after a ballot initiative to that effect passed in March 2024.[1]
