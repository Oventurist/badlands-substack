# Transhumanism

**Transhumanism** is characterized in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary not as a technological program but as a religion to be combated.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s feature "Combating the Transhumanist Religion," published 1 November 2025 and highlighted in the 7 November 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] retrospective, is the second installment of a series.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) Its first part introduced readers to the "above ground" and "below ground" movements that Ehret contends have been carefully sculpted to induce as many humans as possible to participate, quite literally, in the willful self-destruction of industrial civilization.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) The distinction between overt and covert movements is central: the transhumanist religion is presented as operating simultaneously through publicly respectable institutions such as the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and the [[great-reset|Great Reset]], and through subterranean currents including [[eco-terrorism|eco-terrorism]] and the anti-industrial philosophy Ehret associates with [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)
