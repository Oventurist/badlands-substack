# Restrict Act

The Restrict Act is proposed U.S. legislation that critics in the alternative media characterized as a domestic vehicle for the same speech and device controls being enacted abroad.[1]

## Criticism

In April 2023 commentary comparing the bill to [[ireland|Ireland]]'s hate speech legislation, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that although America is popularly regarded as a beacon of free speech, the Restrict Act would allow "exactly" the sort of criminalization being adopted in Ireland.[1] The core objection was structural rather than textual: because the wording is so vague, the act would give the government discretion to define its own terms after the fact.[1]

DeLarme placed the Restrict Act in a lineage with the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]], describing it as following the same totalitarian lines and noting the irony that the earlier law has been used to "legally" target patriots.[1]
