# New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen

*New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen* is the United States Supreme Court decision issued in June 2022 that Badlands Media coverage describes as a "watershed" ruling, in whose "widening wake" a series of lower-court decisions have struck down firearms restrictions.[1]

## Downstream effect

The February 2023 Oklahoma ruling by U.S. District Judge [[patrick-wyrick|Patrick Wyrick]] in favor of [[jared-harrison|Jared Harrison]], which held the federal ban on gun ownership by marijuana users unconstitutional as applied, was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] as the latest of many such post-*Bruen* rulings against gun restrictions.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the ruling to argue a broader constitutional theory: that the Constitution enumerates rather than grants rights, that rights are inalienable and independent of any government or religion, and that only the abuse of a right justifies community or governmental restraint.[1] He argued that Americans have become "right-illiterate," that globalists deliberately dumbed down the population so that government overreach meets little resistance, and that restoring freedoms would require "a total and complete audit and review of the legal system," including the careful abolishment of regulations, policies, statutes, and codes that unlawfully deprive citizens of fundamental rights.[1] In his framing, because inalienable rights cannot truly be removed, a government claiming to take them is in fact making a coercive offer, and public acquiescence constitutes consent — "a bargain with a liar and a cheat."[1]
