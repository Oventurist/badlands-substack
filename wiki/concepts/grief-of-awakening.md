# Grief of Awakening

The **grief of awakening** is the third stage described in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], presented under the heading "Emotional Integration."[1]

## Description

The article observes that readers commonly hear about the expansion of awakening but rarely about the grief that accompanies it, calling that grief "a quiet storm."[1] A person may mourn the job that once defined them, friendships that fade as their frequency shifts, or the innocence of a world they thought they knew; the essay likens it to "the ache of a caterpillar dissolving in its chrysalis, necessary, yet disorienting."[1] For some it surfaces as tears over a lost routine, for others as a deeper existential ache for a humanity still asleep.[1]

[[andrew-huberman|Andrew Huberman]]'s research on neuroplasticity is cited for the claim that the brain resists transformation and that letting go of old belief systems triggers the same neurological responses as physical pain.[1] Against this the essay sets a line attributed to Rumi — "The wound is the place where the light enters you" — casting grief as the crack in the old self that makes space for the true one, "a trembling plank on your bridge that holds the promise of strength."[1]

## Navigating emotional cycles

The article's recommended practices are: acknowledging and processing grief by naming it aloud ("I'm letting go of who I was") to externalise the emotion; using breath to regulate emotional waves, pairing a long exhale with a hand on the chest because touch amplifies the body's sense of safety; recognising that identity is fluid, including by writing a letter from one's future self describing what is blooming from the release; and finding conscious community, since even one conversation with a like-minded person shifts isolation into connection.[1]

The stated key takeaway is that emotions are guideposts and that honouring them strengthens the bridge to new awareness.[1]
