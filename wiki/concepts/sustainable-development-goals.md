# Sustainable Development Goals

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are the United Nations development framework that succeeded the UN Millennium Goals of 2015.[1] In Badlands Media commentary the SDGs are treated as the programmatic core of [[esg|Environmental, Social, and Governance]] investing and, more broadly, of what the outlet calls global corporate communism.[1]

## Badlands critique

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the Millennium Goals "became the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG)" and that global communism subsequently "started showing up in US elementary school classrooms."[1] In this reading, globalists — described as "our invisible governors" — sought to raise children as good global citizens, while parents and educators who resisted the shift felt they were "screaming into the void while the curriculum changed and the money flowed in."[1]

With [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2024 victory and the collapse of ESG's political standing, the commentary predicted that ESG and its SDGs would be remembered as "that failed season" of global communism and studied by future generations as a cautionary tale about outsourcing national sovereignty to foreign interests.[1] It forecast that the subversive bureaucracy would be "rapidly ripped out by its roots" over the following eighteen months.[1]
