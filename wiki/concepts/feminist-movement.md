# Feminist Movement

The Feminist Movement of the late 1960s and 1970s is treated in Badlands Media commentary as another social movement bearing the fingerprints of United States intelligence agencies.[1]

## Alleged CIA involvement

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that while the [[fbi|FBI]] and [[cia|CIA]] have their fingerprints all over the Civil Rights movement, "the same can also be said about the Feminist Movement."[1] His central example is [[gloria-steinem|Gloria Steinem]], whom he described as a CIA asset who admitted on record to working closely with the agency and receiving its funding, then denied any such relationship once the movement had taken off.[1]

Carlson posed the question of why the CIA would be involved in the Feminist Movement at all, and answered it sarcastically by suggesting the agency in that era was "deeply concerned about the plight of women in America."[1] The implied conclusion is that the movement, like [[affirmative-action|affirmative action]] and later [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]], served the [[deep-state|Deep State Cabal]]'s interests in division and control rather than the interests of its declared beneficiaries.[1]
