# 2023 China-Brokered Saudi-Iran Normalization

On March 11, 2023, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[iran|Iran]] announced the restoration of diplomatic ties in an agreement brokered by China — a peace deal of sorts in the Middle East, not between Israel and the Arabs, but between two powers that had been at each other's throats for decades, and mediated not by the United States.[1]

Contemporary reporting described the development as among the topsiest and turviest anyone could have imagined, a shift that left heads spinning in capitals around the globe and upended alliances and rivalries that had governed diplomacy for generations.[1] According to what the Saudis told the Americans, the key to the agreement was a commitment by Iran to stop further attacks on Saudi Arabia and to curtail support for militant groups that had targeted the kingdom.[1] The two states had effectively fought a devastating proxy war in [[yemen|Yemen]], where [[houthis|Houthi]] rebels aligned with Tehran had battled Saudi forces for eight years.[1]

## American reaction

The agreement blindsided Washington. [[cia|CIA]] director [[william-burns|Bill Burns]] told Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] that the United States felt "blindsided" by Riyadh's moves to restore ties with Syria and Iran as part of the kingdom's increasingly independent foreign policy streak.[1]

## The Badlands interpretation

Badlands Media presents the normalization as proof that the Middle East had been completely transformed by the removal of what the author regards as the region's controlling figures: [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Al Waleed bin Talal]], arrested in the November 2017 Saudi purge, and [[qasem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]], killed by an American drone strike in January 2020.[1] In this account, taking down the puppet master first left the puppets weaker and more vulnerable, and eliminating Soleimani freed Iran from control by Al Waleed and the CIA, leaving the cabal's crimes in the Middle East exposed and prompting particular panic at the CIA.[1] The article concludes that peace is breaking out in the Middle East.[1]
