# Podesta Emails

The **Podesta emails** are the correspondence of Clinton campaign chairman [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] published by [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] in the autumn of 2016, a few months after the [[dnc-email-leak|DNC email leak]].[1]

## Content

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describes the emails as "extremely strange," noting that they involved a great deal of what the FBI considers pedophile code words.[1]

## Aftermath

The Podesta release was the only one of [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]]'s publications that prompted the Ecuadorian government to cut his internet access at the [[ecuadorian-embassy-in-london|Ecuadorian Embassy in London]], and then for only six weeks; the standard justification offered was the ongoing US election.[1] Carlson asks why this release alone was deemed worthy of that response, given that Assange went on to publish [[vault-7|Vault 7]] the following March without comparable interference.[1]

He further argues that, as with the Clinton material, nothing durable came of the disclosures — neither [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] nor Podesta went to prison — and that the pair may have been designated [[cabal|Cabal]] scapegoats, leaving open the question of whether Assange was a whistleblower, a black hat, or simply seeking attention.[1]
