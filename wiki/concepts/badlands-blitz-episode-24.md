# Badlands Blitz Episode 24

**Badlands Blitz Episode 24**, subtitled "The Trump Tornado Touches Down at NATO", is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] published on the Badlands Substack on July 11, 2026. At the time of archiving the post had accumulated 161 likes. As with every entry in the series, it is presented as a weekly recap and dissection of the work the team produces Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].

## Format and hosts

The episode is co-hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], both linked in the post to their respective Substack profiles. The written note opens with the program's customary greeting to "Badlanders!" and identifies the episode as the twenty-fourth in the series.

## Content

According to the source, the two hosts discuss [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s "latest (super rapid) world tour," an itinerary the subtitle associates in particular with a stop at [[nato|NATO]]. The post argues that the tour "represents a total inversion of everything the System has taught us about the Axis and Allies" — that is, that the diplomatic alignments on display contradict the conventional postwar division of the world into friendly and hostile blocs that the article attributes to establishment narrative-setting.

The note extends the argument by claiming that the greatest difficulty for what it calls the System is "the increasingly-apparent fact that they actually bought into their own story." In the source's framing, the architects of that narrative came to believe it themselves, leaving them unprepared for a realignment that Trump is described as "untangling in real time, and with some unlikely friends." The reference to unlikely friends signals the episode's interest in cooperation between the United States and states conventionally coded as adversaries, a recurring preoccupation of Badlands commentary on the [[cabal|System]] and on shifting world order.

The post does not itself enumerate the countries visited or the specific outcomes of the NATO stop; the written note functions as an introduction to the audio discussion rather than a transcript of it.

## Subscription model

The note restates the outlet's standard publishing model: all [[badlands-media|Badlands]] content will continue to be free, including The Blitz, and "the only way we're able to pay ourselves for the time is due to those of you who choose to support it directly." Readers who value the team's daily output and what the post calls "the step-up in said work for 2026" are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time.

The post closes with the program's standard sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
