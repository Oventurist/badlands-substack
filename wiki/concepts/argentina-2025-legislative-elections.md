# Argentina 2025 Legislative Elections

Argentina held legislative elections on Sunday, October 26, 2025, in which the party of President [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]] secured more than 40 percent of the vote.[1]

## Results

Milei's party won 64 of the 127 seats contested in the lower chamber and 13 of the 24 seats in the upper chamber — enough to prevent the legislative branch from overturning his vetoes.[1] The party had been forecast to win roughly 30 percent of the seats, according to Bloomberg.[1]

## US involvement

The outcome was characterized as a major victory for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had staked US financial support on the success of the libertarian Latin American ally.[1] Asked about the results aboard Air Force One, Trump said: "I love that question because that was a big win in Argentina," adding that he had given Milei "a very strong endorsement" and that "people thought it would be hard to win. And not only did he win, he won by a lot."[1]
