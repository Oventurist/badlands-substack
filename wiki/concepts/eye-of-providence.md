# Eye of Providence

The **Eye of Providence** is the single-eye-in-a-triangle emblem that appears on the reverse of the Great Seal of the United States and in a wide range of Masonic and civic iconography. In the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series it is treated as the central symbol of the power structure Q describes, and as the referent of Q's statement in Drop #133 that many governments "feed the eye."[1]

## Q Drop #133 and the pyramid

Drop #133, dated November 11, 2017, is presented by the author as Q's lesson in "what we are up against, and the pyramid that is being dismantled."[1] Q asks who the puppet masters are and, in the author's reading, answers the question by naming the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]], the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]], and [[george-soros|George Soros]] as the three sides of the pyramid, before stating that their publicly disclosed wealth is false and that they are worth far more than the public has been led to believe.[1]

The author acknowledges that "the eye" could be read as a reference to [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] intelligence sharing, and notes that "double meanings do exist," but concludes that within the context of the rest of the drop the intended referent is clearly the Eye of Providence.[1]

## Claimed perversion and the Eye of Horus

While allowing that the original meaning of the Eye of Providence was "benevolent enough," the article argues that the symbol has been perverted and is now used as cover for what it really represents: the [[eye-of-horus|Eye of Horus]].[1] The author points to the prevalence of left-eye symbolism among elites as supporting evidence, and stresses that the Eye of Horus represents sacrifice.[1] [[darpa|DARPA]] is cited as a modern institution whose logo features the Eye of Providence.[1]

## Bloodlines and cult claims

Q's instruction to "follow the bloodlines" is treated as an extension of the same crumb set.[1] The author notes the December 17 death of an ABC news anchor surnamed Orsini and remarks that Orsini is one of the bloodline families referenced in the drop, while expressing sympathy for the family and declining to press the point further.[1] The drop then asks whether Satan exists and who worships him; the author answers that he does and that most of the world's elites and governments do, framing the arrangement as a cult whose members include those who traveled to [[little-saint-james|Epstein's island]].[1]

## Colors and related symbolism

Q's question about the significance of colors is answered by the author as follows: blue signifies fertility, white represents the purity stolen from children, and yellow or gold represents eternal life.[1] Horus himself is described as represented by blue and gold, which the author uses to explain the color scheme of the temple on [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s island, and by extension to question the similar palette used by [[disney|Disney]] Cruise Lines and the set of [[ellen-degeneres|Ellen DeGeneres]]'s program.[1] The chapter closes its treatment of Drop #133 by noting Q's reference to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] calling out the New World Order Satanists said to run the world.[1]
