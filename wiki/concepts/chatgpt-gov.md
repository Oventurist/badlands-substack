# ChatGPT Gov

ChatGPT Gov is a tailored version of ChatGPT announced by [[openai|OpenAI]] on Tuesday, January 28, 2025, intended to accelerate government use of the tool for non-public sensitive data.[1]

## Capabilities

The service can run in either the Microsoft Azure commercial cloud or the Azure Government cloud, giving federal agencies increased ability to use OpenAI frontier models.[1] OpenAI said the product is designed to make it easier for agencies to satisfy certain cybersecurity and compliance requirements while exploring applications of the technology.[1] Through ChatGPT Gov, federal agencies can access GPT-4o along with other OpenAI tools and build custom agency-developed search and chat systems; an administrative console allows IT teams and chief information officers to manage usage, and agencies remain bound by OpenAI's universal usage policies.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary flagged the placement of AI tooling inside government cloud infrastructure as concerning, particularly given contemporaneous reports of China gaining an AI advantage with DeepSeek.[1] The commentator recounted professional experience producing a regulatory paper on a "cloud exit strategy" for a U.S. financial institution facing a foreign regulator, in which an admittedly theoretical and impractical rollback plan was approved without question — offered as evidence that technologists make jargon-laden promises to technically illiterate regulators and then do as they please, a dynamic described as merely amusing in the private sector but gravely concerning in national security matters.[1]
