# Credit Suisse Collapse Inquiry

The Credit Suisse collapse inquiry is the Swiss parliamentary investigation into the failure of the investment bank [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]], notable for a decision to seal its files for an unusually long period.[1]

## 50-year secrecy

Reuters reported in July 2023, as relayed by [[western-journal|The Western Journal]], that the parliamentary investigation into the collapse would keep its files closed for 50 years — described by Reuters as "a level of secrecy that has triggered concern among Swiss historians."[1] The 50-year period, noted in a parliamentary committee document, is two decades longer than the 30 years of secrecy standard in comparable cases, and the moratorium was justified as ensuring that "high levels of confidentiality apply to the investigation."[1]
