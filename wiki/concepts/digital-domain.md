# Digital Domain

The **digital domain** is the online, screen-mediated environment which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary identifies as the principal theatre of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[1] *[[the-citizens-guide-to-fifth-generation-warfare|The Citizen's Guide to 5GW]]* by General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] and [[boone-cutler|Boone Cutler]] holds that the majority of 5GW occurs there, and for that reason recommends dominating the [[physical-domain|physical domain]] as a countermeasure to mind control and censorship.[1]

## An imitation of an imitation

In "How Your Western Brain Exposes You to 5G Warfare," [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argues that the digital domain amplifies existing modern conditioning because it is a virtual representation of an already modernized world: it imitates the post-industrial, left-hemisphere environment, which was itself an imitation of the natural world before being "uploaded."[1] The world, broken down into a left-hemisphere interpretation, is thereby reflected and reinforced a second time, constituting an additional left-brain ritual layered on top of the conditioning produced by [[disembedding-mechanisms|disembedding mechanisms]].[1]

Constant engagement with symbolic tokens of modern imitations of life, curated by the rules and dimensions of the digital environment, caters to the left hemisphere's preference for artificially consistent patterns that are easier to comprehend than nature's asymmetry, originality and ambiguity.[1] Esler calls this "the matrix itself, ensnaring our cognition in ways that evade perception through unrelenting normalization."[1]

## You are what you scroll

Esler reframes the proverb "you are what you eat" for the digital age, holding it more accurate for content than for food, since digital media affects the consumer both through its contents and through the engineering built into the social media environment in which it is consumed.[1] The circulation of kinetic war content is his quintessential example: whether authentic or manipulated, it impacts cognition by rejecting everything the right hemisphere offers, sacrificing metaphor, intuition and embodied experience as technology "devolves us out of our bodies."[1]

He points to the intersection of smartphones and 4K cameras with the war in Gaza, where severed limbs and casualties are transported globally in high resolution into the palm of the hand; repeated exposure to such decontextualized, emotionally charged events desensitizes viewers and erodes the genuine emotional responses such events should evoke.[1] He asks when partisans on either side of that conflict stop to consider that they are emotionally inflamed by symbolic tokens of the war rather than the war itself, and argues that it is from this "place of existential obscurity" and alienation that populations are then operated on psychologically, directed toward policies that do not serve them, and sorted into political tribes fed the certainty that both sides crave as "Western-brained, left-hemisphere addicts."[1]
