# 2024 US Virgin Islands Republican Caucuses

The **2024 US Virgin Islands Republican caucuses** were held on Thursday, February 8, 2024, and were won by former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], continuing what NBC News described as his dominant march toward the Republican presidential nomination.[1]

## Result

The victory was Trump's third in as many contests, following the Iowa caucuses and the New Hampshire primary, and dealt another defeat to former United Nations ambassador [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], his last well-known challenger.[1] Trump was expected to net all four delegates the Republican National Committee planned to allocate from the territory at that summer's convention in Milwaukee.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported that 182 people cast votes for Trump in [[stacey-plaskett|Stacey Plaskett]]'s district, giving him 74 percent of the vote, while Haley received 64 votes, or 26 percent.[1] The tiny turnout drew wry commentary, with Ashe noting that virtually no one noticed the contest was happening amid an otherwise overwhelming news cycle.[1]

## Ranked-choice voting controversy

[[us-virgin-islands|US Virgin Islands]] GOP Chairman Gordon Ackley previewed the caucuses with a statement claiming that "going third-in-the-nation with an unrigged caucus using ranked-choice voting has given every candidate a fair and equal playing field and resulted in unprecedented attention for the Virgin Islands," and predicting that the territory would "decide the trajectory of the Republican nomination."[1] Badlands rejected the framing, arguing that "the regime is pushing RCV like they pushed the vaccines" and urging readers to reject [[ranked-choice-voting|ranked-choice voting]] outright.[1]
