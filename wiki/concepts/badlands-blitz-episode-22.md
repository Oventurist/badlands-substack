# Badlands Blitz Episode 22

**Badlands Blitz Episode 22**, subtitled "A Weave Through a Week of Weird Stories", is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] published on the Badlands Substack on June 20, 2026. At the time of archiving the post had accumulated 164 likes. As with every entry in the series, the post presents itself as a weekly recap and dissection of the work produced Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].

## Format and hosts

The episode is co-hosted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], both linked in the post to their Substack profiles. The written note serves as a companion to the recorded conversation and opens with the customary greeting to "Badlanders!", identifying the episode as the twenty-second in the series.

## Content

According to the source, the two hosts "embark on a discombobulating weave through the week of stories that felt like a 31-day month" — a characterisation of a news cycle unusually dense with developments. Three themes are named as defining the episode: "America rising", "Iranian peace", and "an unexpected, extended weave about COVID".

The first theme continues a recurring Badlands framing of an American national revival. The second concerns a de-escalation in the confrontation involving [[iran|Iran]], following the outlet's treatment in the preceding installment, [[badlands-blitz-episode-21|Badlands Blitz Episode 21]], of what it called the "Fake Iran War". The third, described as unexpected and extended, revisits the [[covid-19-pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]]. The post adds a timing caveat of its own: "the COVID declas hadn't yet dropped when we recorded" — indicating that a declassification release concerning COVID became public after the recording, so the discussion did not incorporate it.

## Subscription model

The post reiterates the outlet's standard publishing model: all [[badlands-media|Badlands]] content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free, and the team is able to pay itself for its time only through readers who support the work directly. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time, in recognition of the daily output and the "step-up in said work for 2026."

The note closes with the program's standard sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
