# Rare Pepe Directory

The **Rare Pepe Directory** is the catalogue of Rare Pepe trading cards issued as tokens on [[counterparty|Counterparty]], the second-layer protocol that operates through the OP_RETURN code on the [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] network.[1] In "The COVFEFE Dig," [[gmoney|GMONEY]] describes it as a full directory of 1,774 cards created as collectibles to help protect the image of [[pepe-the-frog|Pepe]], and attributes the first card to an anonymous person known only as "Mike," who remains unidentified.[1]

## Cards cited in the dig

The founding card, [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]], was minted on 9/9/2016 and is verifiable on Bitcoin block 428,919.[1] The author reads the card's on-chain figures as containing the digits 3, 1 and 5, the recurring signal of the dig.[1]

A second card, GAMESOFTRUMP, depicts [[donald-trump|Trump]] riding a dragon and carries a heart value of 17, a shield value of 17, and an initial supply of 1717.[1] Because Q is the seventeenth letter of the alphabet, GMONEY treats the card as anticipatory, stressing that it was minted 58 days before the first [[qanon|Q]] drop and is verifiable on block 482,855.[1]

The 315th card of the series is PEPERARE, described as the mirror of RAREPEPE, and Series 3, Card 15 is PUTINPEPE, which the author notes wears the same smug expression as the Pepe image Trump originally posted.[1] PUTINPEPE is used to pivot into the article's geopolitical argument about Russia's removal from Western financial systems.[1]
