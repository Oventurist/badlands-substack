# Campbell v. Clinton

Campbell v. Clinton is a 2000 decision of the United States Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit concerning the scope of presidential war powers.[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] cited the case in February 2025 in response to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s post that "[[he-who-saves-his-country-does-not-violate-any-law|He who saves his Country does not violate any Law]]," arguing that a president exercising wartime powers likewise does not violate any law.[1] As summarized in the brief: "Importantly, the Court made clear that it would not dispute the President on measures necessary to repel foreign aggression. The President alone must determine what degree of force the crisis demands."[1]
