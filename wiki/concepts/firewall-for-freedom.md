# Firewall for Freedom

"Firewall for Freedom" is the name given by [[american-civil-liberties-union|ACLU]] executive director [[anthony-romero|Anthony Romero]] to a plan, described publicly in December 2024, for using state and local officials to obstruct federal immigration enforcement under the incoming second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Description

Romero outlined the plan on [[rachel-maddow|Rachel Maddow]]'s [[msnbc|MSNBC]] program after the 2024 election but before the inauguration, at a moment Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described as the establishment still wargaming how to impede the presidency.[1] Romero said that "local governments, you know, the states Attorney's General, the Governors, the Mayors — we have this whole plan around what we call a 'firewall for freedom,'" and that these local officials could play an important role.[1]

The mechanism he described was logistical rather than strictly legal. Romero acknowledged that federal authorities possess the legal power to conduct raids and to detain and deport hundreds of thousands, up to a million, people; the constraint is that such an operation requires mayors, governors or city councils to grant or withhold access to police officers and jail space.[1] He added that his organization was "preparing executive orders" and "organizing our folks to put pressure on these elected officials."[1]

## Interpretation

DeLarme treated the segment as a clear illustration of his thesis: knowledge of the law used in tandem with textbook [[color-revolution|color revolution]] tactics to prevent an elected president from carrying out what he called the most popular promise of the campaign.[1] He argued that whether one supports or opposes mass deportations is beside the point, because the policy is what voters chose, and that an NGO tied to what he called the college of deep political institutions working to obstruct it is repugnant to the concept of democracy those institutions claim to defend.[1] He placed the episode within the broader vocabulary of the [[rule-of-law|"rule of law"]] and the [[language-of-control|language of control]].[1]

## Relation to "the Opposition"

A second Badlands article situated the firewall for freedom as a central tactic of [[the-opposition|the Opposition]], the post-2024 successor to [[the-resistance|the Resistance]].[2] In that telling, Romero paired the firewall with a commitment that "litigation is going to be key" while conceding the movement "can't run the same playbook," indicating a combination of [[lawfare|lawfare]] and sub-federal obstruction.[2] Romero was also described as encouraging local officials to place prisons and jails off limits to federal immigration enforcement and to devise means of pardoning illegal immigrants.[2]

The same account emphasized that both Romero and [[rachel-maddow|Rachel Maddow]] told viewers that the absence of large post-election street protests reflected the marshaling of resources for the most opportune moment — the launch of mass deportations — implying that protest infrastructure comparable to 2016 and 2020 had already been prepared.[2]
