# Socialism in One Country

"Socialism in one country" was the doctrine associated with [[joseph-stalin|Joseph Stalin]], holding that the Soviet state should consolidate and develop socialism within Russia rather than subordinate itself to a program of continuous international revolution.[1]

Badlands Media coverage presents the doctrine as the direct antithesis of [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]]'s commitment to global socialist revolution, and identifies the clash between the two positions as the heart of an unbridgeable divide between the two revolutionaries throughout the years.[1] The article argues that an over-simplified reading of Russian history has obscured why the battles between Trotsky and Stalin during the first twenty post-revolutionary years were fought as life-and-death struggles.[1]
