# Operation Northern Arrows

Operation Northern Arrows was the Israeli military offensive announced on September 23, 2024, aimed at undermining the military infrastructure of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[1]

## Course of the operation

The Israeli leadership announced that the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] had launched the operation, under which the Israeli Air Force carried out massive airstrikes across Lebanon, with dozens of settlements in the south and east of the country targeted.[1] Hezbollah responded by intensifying rocket attacks on military facilities inside Israel.[1] Lebanese Foreign Minister [[abdallah-bou-habib|Abdallah Bou Habib]] estimated that half a million people had been displaced within Lebanon during the escalation.[1]

The offensive followed almost a year of war against [[hamas|Hamas]] in [[gaza|Gaza]], with Israel shifting its focus to its northern frontier.[1]

## International reaction

The [[russian-foreign-ministry|Russian Foreign Ministry]], through spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]], strongly condemned the attacks and called for an immediate cessation of hostilities.[1] US President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], addressing the [[united-nations|United Nations]] General Assembly, sought to calm tensions, saying full-scale war was in no one's interest and that a diplomatic solution remained possible — remarks Bou Habib judged "not strong" and "not promising."[1] The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] announced it was moving a small number of additional US personnel into the region out of "an abundance of caution."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary questioned the logic of responding to the October 7 Hamas attack from Gaza by escalating against a different faction in a different country, and speculated that Russia might lead any counteraction by the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[1]
