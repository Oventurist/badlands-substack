# Badlands Blitz Episode 20

**Badlands Blitz Episode 20**, subtitled "Redefining ... or Remembering the MAGA Mission?", is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] published on the Badlands Substack on June 6, 2026. At the time of archiving the post had accumulated 172 likes. Like every entry in the series, it is presented as a weekly recap and dissection of the work produced Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].

## Format and hosts

The episode is co-hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], the two writers who anchor the weekly program. The post itself functions primarily as a companion note to the recorded discussion, opening with the customary greeting to "Badlanders!" and framing the episode as the twentieth in the series.

## Content

According to the source, the hosts begin with what the post describes as "a diagnostic of the fake diagnostics" — that is, a critical examination of media and institutional narratives claiming that conditions in the United States are deteriorating badly under the renewed presidency of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. The framing treats those negative assessments as themselves artificial or manufactured rather than as accurate measurements of national conditions.

From that opening, the article says the conversation "dovetails" into a broader discussion of American sovereignty, political representation, and what the hosts call re-anchoring around the original [[maga|MAGA]] mission that Trump originally laid out. The post presents this original mission as the animating force that "first energized so many of us in this community," positioning the episode as an act of remembrance and recovery of founding purpose rather than of redefinition — a tension captured directly in the episode's subtitle.

## Subscription model

As with other Blitz posts, the note reiterates the outlet's publishing model: all Badlands content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free, and the team is able to pay itself for the time invested only through readers who choose to support the work directly. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time, in support of the daily output and what the post calls the "step-up in said work for 2026."

The post closes with the program's standard sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
