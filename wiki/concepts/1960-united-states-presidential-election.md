# 1960 United States Presidential Election

The 1960 U.S. presidential election, won by [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], is treated in Badlands Media commentary by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as the defining turning point in the capture of the American election system — one of his four pillars of government control.[1]

## The alleged Chicago bargain

Some believe the Chicago Mob cut a deal with [[joseph-p-kennedy|Joseph Kennedy]], father of John and Robert, in 1960 to throw the election in his son's favor; in return, the Justice Department would take it easy on the Mafia during Kennedy's presidency.[1] It is also believed the Kennedys did not keep their side of the bargain, which is offered as the reason for Mafia involvement in the assassination of JFK three years later and of Robert F. Kennedy five years after that.[1] Carlson notes it is fairly common knowledge that the Mafia had been working with the FBI and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] (and its predecessor, the OSS) since World War II, and that both agencies are believed to have been involved in the Kennedy assassinations.[1]

The Mafia, which some believe still wields influence over the [[teamsters|Teamsters Union]], is said to have steered the largest union in America to vote for Kennedy on a national level.[1]

## The numbers

Ten states in 1960 were decided by fewer than 10,000 votes.[1] Kennedy carried Illinois by less than 9,000 votes while holding a 450,000-vote advantage in Cook County, the county in which the Chicago Mob resides.[1] Kennedy won the national popular vote by 113,000, meaning the Cook County landslide delivered not only Illinois but the popular vote itself.[1]

## Interpretation

Carlson writes that he finds it very difficult to believe the 1960 election was not stolen, and that the theft theory lends credence to why the Mob was involved in the Kennedy assassinations.[1] He argues the ease and effectiveness of stealing that election led the [[deep-state|Deep State]] to wonder why it would ever allow a fair election to occur again, with the appearance of fairness maintained at all times — perpetually narrow splits of 52/48 or 49/51, eight years with one party in power before the other takes over, both parties controlled by the same people until [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] In the same framework he asserts 2016 was rigged for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] but with too few votes stolen, 2020 was blatantly stolen, and 2024 was manipulated to make Trump appear to win only narrowly while down-ballot seats were taken.[1]
