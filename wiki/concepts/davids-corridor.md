# David's Corridor

"David's Corridor" is the name given by the Badlands Media contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] to a hypothesized Israeli strategic objective: the establishment of a contiguous land bridge running east from [[israel|Israel]] across Syria to the Euphrates River. The concept is interpretive and has not been confirmed by any government.

## The argument

In commentary first advanced in April 2025 and restated in the March 3, 2026 [[badlands-brief-52f|Badlands Brief]], GhostofBasedPatrickHenry contends that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s interest in controlling Syrian territory was aimed at securing this corridor. His stated reasoning is logistical: "once you cross the river, you are in Kurdistan, and the [[kurds|Kurds]] can help get soldiers and equipment into Iran." The corridor, on this account, would make a ground campaign against [[iran|Iran]] feasible for Israeli or American forces.

He presents remarks by retired IDF general and Israeli politician [[benny-gantz|Benny Gantz]] — allowing for the possibility of a ground invasion of Iran by Israel or the United States — as confirmation of the theory, and pairs it with reporting that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had begun asking the Kurds to assist the war effort. The Brief also recalls that a ground invasion is "the one thing that [[erik-prince|Erik Prince]] said we should never do."
