# Holocaust

The Holocaust was the genocide carried out by the Nazi regime under [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]], resulting in the deaths of approximately six million Jewish people. It provides the historical context for the Swiss banking segment of [[badlands-brief-314|the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026]].

## Relevance to the Credit Suisse investigation

The Brief reports that an independent investigation by [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]] uncovered 890 previously undisclosed accounts at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] with potential links to the Nazi regime, held by entities including the [[german-foreign-office|German Foreign Office]] and the [[schutzstaffel|SS]]. Beyond the existence of the accounts themselves, the probe found that the bank expropriated funds from accounts belonging to Jewish individuals during the Nazi era and transferred them to Nazi clients, and that it participated in schemes assisting Nazis in fleeing to [[argentina|Argentina]] after the war.

## Restitution history

Credit Suisse and [[ubs|UBS]] had previously reached a global settlement in 1999 that resolved all Nazi-era claims, including future ones, with both banks issuing apologies. The renewed inquiry — initiated in Congress by Senator [[sheldon-whitehouse|Sheldon Whitehouse]] at the prompting of the [[simon-wiesenthal-center|Simon Wiesenthal Center]], and later publicized by Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] — indicates that the historical record was substantially incomplete at the time of that settlement. UBS Americas President [[robert-karofsky|Robert Karofsky]] stated that the company accepts and deeply regrets the dark history of Swiss banking during the Second World War.
