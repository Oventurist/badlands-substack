# America for Sale — Part 1

"America for Sale - Part 1," subtitled "Betrayal and Treason," is a Badlands Media essay published on December 19, 2024 by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]. It advances the thesis that the sex scandal involving [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and White House intern [[monica-lewinsky|Monica Lewinsky]] functioned as a media shield that buried two far graver stories of the same period: the transfer of American missile and satellite technology to the People's Republic of China, and the quiet arrival in 1996 of a second intern — [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] — whom the author presents as a [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] placement inside the Clinton orbit.

## Argument: infidelity as cover

The article opens by asking readers to recall the scandal that "consumed the media narrative for several years" beginning in 1996. It argues that the Clinton administration was "inundated with scandal from the very moment he took office," and that the volume of scandals is itself evidence of a two-tier justice system. The Clintons are described as "the modern day Ahab and Jezebel." In the author's framing, the Lewinsky affair was survivable precisely because it was merely embarrassing: "Infidelity was NEVER going to end the Clinton administration." What would have ended it, the article asserts, was treason — and while the press focused on the affair, "all the other scandals just went away and were swept under the rug."

## Satellite and missile technology transfers to China

The essay quotes an April 16, 1998 *New York Times* report that "[s]everal Congressional committees are investigating whether the Administration's policy of exporting space satellite technology to China has helped China and other countries to develop and use nuclear missiles." The same report cited a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] finding that the reliability of Chinese nuclear missiles "was significantly advanced in 1996" after scientists working for American satellite companies gave Beijing expertise on guidance systems, noting that the technology used to place a commercial satellite in orbit resembles that which "guides a long-range nuclear missile to its target." Senator Thad Cochran, a Mississippi Republican, is quoted saying: "I'm not blaming China. We're talking about our own Government policy-makers responsible for the transfer of this technology, and this case is a glaring example."

The article states that the transfers were enabled by waivers signed by President Clinton in 1996 — the same year the Lewinsky affair began — and adds that Clinton also permitted the sale of supercomputers to China, which the author claims advanced China's nuclear missile program by roughly twenty years. It draws a parallel to the later Obama-era pattern, writing that "[w]e were funding and arming our enemies back in the 90s, just like we were doing during the Obama administration, with Hillary as Secretary of State in [[benghazi|Benghazi]]."

## Ron Brown and the Commerce Department

Central to the essay is Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]], described as the "liaison between China and the Clinton administration" who received his appointment because he had been a major fundraiser for Clinton's first presidential campaign. Citing a March 14, 1998 *New York Times* article, the piece recounts that a nine-count federal indictment of Dallas entrepreneur [[nolanda-hill|Nolanda Sue Hill]], 53, and her financial aide Kenneth C. White, 49, of Cumberland, Maryland, for conspiring to defraud the [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] and the FDIC between 1989 and 1995, grew out of an inquiry whose "original target" was Brown himself. Attorney General [[janet-reno|Janet Reno]] had said in the spring of 1995 that Brown apparently failed to properly report large payments from Hill.

A federal appeals court named Miami attorney [[daniel-pearson|Daniel S. Pearson]], 64, a former state judge and federal prosecutor, as independent counsel — the fourth special counsel then probing Clinton administration officials, according to the *Greensboro News and Record*. Legal sources quoted in that report expected the Brown investigation to run "well into the 1996 presidential campaign." Brown died in a plane crash near Dubrovnik, Croatia on April 3, 1996, and the inquiry was transferred to the Justice Department, which the author says removed the target from the administration: "Cover-up secured."

## The trade-mission fundraising scheme

Quoting Buchal.com, the article reports that thirty-four people accompanying Brown on the trade mission also died, and that through the work of [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] "we now know beyond a reasonable doubt that seats on Brown's Commerce Department trade missions were sold to raise funds for the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] and the 1996 Clinton/Gore Campaign," with "overwhelming evidence that Bill and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] knew of and approved this improper and illegal fundraising scheme." The same source states that [[bernard-schwartz|Bernard Schwartz]], head of [[loral-corporation|Loral Corporation]] and a major DNC donor, accompanied Brown on a 1994 trade mission to China, where Brown arranged a meeting between Schwartz and a Chinese official that led to the missile-technology transfer later investigated by Congress. The essay places these alongside the practice of "renting out" the Lincoln Bedroom to donors, concluding that under Clinton "EVERYTHING WAS FOR SALE."

At the time of his death Brown was under subpoena to produce trade-mission documents in Judicial Watch's suit against the Commerce Department; Hill testified under oath that Brown had shown her such documents in an ostrich-skin portfolio, which were withheld in violation of the subpoena and a FOIA request. The article quotes Brown as having said of his legal troubles shortly before his death: "I am too old to go to jail. If I go down, I'll take everyone else down with me."

## The pathology dispute

The essay devotes substantial space to claims of foul play in Brown's death. Citing Buchal.com, it reports that "a perfectly round .45 inch inwardly beveling hole was discovered in the top of his head" while his body was processed by the [[armed-forces-institute-of-pathology|Armed Forces Institute of Pathology]]. The story broke on November 24 in a report by Christopher Ruddy in the *Pittsburgh Tribune-Review*, in which Lt. Col. [[steve-cogswell|Steve Cogswell]], an AFIP deputy medical examiner, questioned the official finding of death by multiple blunt-force trauma, basing his doubts on x-rays and photographs. Cogswell also noted that original x-rays showed possible metal fragments in the brain consistent with a high-velocity gunshot wound, and that the two head x-rays were missing from the file. On December 5, 1997, AFIP imposed a gag order on Cogswell.

On December 9, 1997, Lt. Col. [[david-hause|David Hause]], described as one of AFIP's leading experts on gunshot wounds, corroborated the account. Hause was present when Chief Petty Officer Kathleen Janoski exclaimed, "Wow, look at the hole in Ron Brown's head," and verified that the wound penetrated the skull and exposed brain matter; he said it "looked like a punched-out .45-caliber entrance hole." After Hause spoke to Ruddy, the gag order was broadened to cover all AFIP personnel. The author concludes that Brown "wasn't supposed to survive the plane crash," and that the plane, which flew into what was then considered a war zone, suffered "suspicious navigational failures."

## The second intern: Huma Abedin

The article's second half turns to Huma Abedin as "the other INTERN" of 1996. Citing *Vanity Fair*, it notes that Abedin "began working for Hillary in 1996, when she was a 19-year-old intern fresh from [[george-washington-university|George Washington University]] assigned to the First Lady's office," and rose to be Clinton's "body woman," traveling chief of staff, senior adviser, deputy chief of staff at the [[state-department|State Department]], and vice chair of the 2016 campaign — throughout functioning as "confessor, confidante, and constant companion." A former Bill Clinton adviser called her "a mini Hillary"; campaign chair John Podesta called her "an integral part of the team." The author asks pointedly what team was meant, and observes that "Huma controls a lot of that dynamic" of access to Clinton.

*Newsweek* is quoted describing Abedin's rise "from girlhood in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, to lowly intern during the Monica Lewinsky scandal, to campaign vice chairman and possible White House chief of staff." Born in Kalamazoo, Michigan in 1976 to Pakistani and Indian Muslim academics, she moved to Jeddah at age two and returned to the United States at eighteen. As deputy chief of staff she was "arranging meetings with world leaders, scanning Clinton's talking points for those meetings and being cc'd on sensitive email traffic." The article stresses Jeddah as a nexus, noting it is the birthplace of [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] and the base of the Bin Laden family: "So many roads lead to Jeddah."

## The Muslim Minority Affairs network

*Newsweek* is further quoted on Abedin's parents' careers at Saudi universities: her mother Saleha is a sociologist and vice dean at a women's college in Jeddah, and her late father Syed helped start the *[[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]]*, at which Abedin worked part time between 1998 and 2009. It also notes former Representative Michele Bachmann's 2012 accusation that Abedin might be a Muslim Brotherhood sleeper, and Abedin's service on the board of the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] chapter at George Washington University, later advised by Al-Qaeda-affiliated cleric Anwar al Awlaki.

Drawing on Andrew C. McCarthy's PJMedia reporting, the article argues that the Abedin family's connection runs through [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]], who established the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]] and recruited Abedin's parents to run it. Naseef, a former vice-president of the Shura Council, president of King Abdul Aziz University, and secretary-general of the [[muslim-world-league|Muslim World League]] from 1983 to 1993, remained on the *JMMA* masthead until 2003, when he was named a defendant in the civil suit brought by 9/11 victims. PJMedia states that Abedin "was assistant editor of the journal for a dozen years — from the time she started interning at the Clinton White House in 1996 until right before she joined the State Department in 2009," overlapping with Naseef for seven of them. The author calls this a conflict of interest and questions how Abedin obtained a security clearance, noting she was also Hillary Clinton's closest aide during her Senate service on committees overseeing the armed forces, education, and the budget.

The essay characterizes the *JMMA* as "an international propaganda journal for the Muslim Brotherhood" and part of the Brotherhood's "civilization/stealth jihad" strategy: promoting acceptance of sharia law in non-Muslim countries through university curricula and student organizations. PJMedia is quoted that the MSA, with "hundreds of chapters" across the United States and Canada, is the foundation of this infrastructure, and that Abedin sat on the executive board of the GWU chapter, heading its Social Committee. The article notes that al-Awlaki was the GWU chapter's spiritual guide in 2001 while ministering to future 9/11 hijackers, that he had earlier led the chapter at Colorado State University, and that in the 1980s Wael Jalaidan ran the MSA at the University of Arizona before helping [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] found al-Qaeda and partnering with Naseef to establish the Rabita Trust, a formally designated terrorist organization under U.S. law. The Pakistani character of the Rabita Trust is treated as significant given Abedin's Pakistani background. The mission, as PJMedia puts it, is "incrementally to infiltrate sharia principles in our law, our institutions, and our public policy."

## Q drops and the Alwaleed thesis

The article reads two Q posts as confirmation. Q drop #111 (November 5, 2017) asks "What have you learned about HUMA? What organization is HUMA? Which US President is affiliated w/ HUMA?" and "What just happened in SA? Who was arrested? Funds frozen." The author identifies HUMA as the Harvard University Muslim Alumni association, notes that the drop appeared the day Prince Alwaleed was arrested and his funds frozen in Saudi Arabia, and repeats a claim made in his earlier article "Bad Actors" that Alwaleed paid for [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s Harvard education — offering this as the reason Obama's school records remain sealed.

Q drop #4750 (September 23, 2020) asks who "wields significant influence" over the Clinton and "Hussein" wings of the Democratic Party, who paid for Obama's Harvard law degree, why his records are sealed, and identifies "competing factions [each with a horse in the race] seeking direct WH control," "[INFILTRATION not invasion][VJ _HA]." The author decodes "VJ_HA" as [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] and Huma Abedin, calling them the "handlers" of Obama and Hillary Clinton respectively and both connected to Alwaleed, whom he describes as "the puppet master who controlled the democrat party under both Obama and Hillary" pursuing a "sixteen year plan" to destroy America and impose sharia law.

## Q, media, and the closing

The article frames the [[q-anon|Q operation]] as a response to this history: "Trump and the military needed an alternative media source to fight the narrative war against the CIA controlled media," with General Michael Flynn's call for "a digital army of truth tellers" credited with destroying mainstream media credibility — "We are the news now." It cites a Q claim that seven of every ten plane crashes are "targeted kills." The piece ends by previewing Part 2, promising deeper coverage of the links among Abedin, Hillary Clinton, Obama, and Jarrett, and quoting the Q markers "11.3 - PODESTA indicted" and "11.6 - HUMA indicted."
