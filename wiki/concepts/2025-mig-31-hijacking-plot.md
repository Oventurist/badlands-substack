# 2025 MiG-31 Hijacking Plot

The **2025 MiG-31 hijacking plot** was an alleged Ukrainian-British operation to steal a Russian MiG-31 fighter jet armed with a Kinzhal hypersonic ballistic missile, which the Russian Federal Security Service ([[fsb|FSB]]) said it foiled.[1]

## Alleged plan

According to the FSB, Ukrainian agents unsuccessfully attempted to persuade the aircraft's pilots to defect, but their actual objective was for the aircraft to be shot down in Romanian airspace, provoking an international incident with [[nato|NATO]].[1] The agency said the operation was organized by Ukraine's military intelligence service, the [[main-directorate-of-intelligence|HUR]], in coordination with the United Kingdom's [[mi6|MI6]].[1]

## Recruitment attempt

The FSB said a Russian MiG-31 pilot was contacted the previous year by a man introducing himself as Sergey Lugovsky, described as a researcher for the open-source investigation group [[bellingcat|Bellingcat]] — characterized in the account as a NATO OSINT front group linked to Western arms contractors and spy agencies.[1] Lugovsky initially sought information and later offered money for defection.[1] After the pilot declined, a Ukrainian agent approached the aircraft's navigator, offering $3 million and a foreign passport in exchange for flying the plane over an air base near Constanta, Romania — NATO's largest European airfield.[1] Kiev has previously offered money and assistance to defectors.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry likened the plot to the plot of the film "Top Gun: Maverick," in which characters played by [[tom-cruise|Tom Cruise]] and Miles Teller steal an enemy F-14 after being shot down behind enemy lines.[1] In his framing, Ukraine and NATO were caught out, with the intended endgame being to fly the aircraft toward a NATO member state and allow the crewman to be killed.[1] Contributor Ashe in America used the Top Gun reference to promote on-demand panels from GART 10: Cocoa Beach, an installment of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]].[1]
