# Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity

The Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity is a proposed transit corridor through [[armenia|Armenia]] announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in May 2026, which he said the United States and Armenia would soon "break ground together" on.[1]

## Announcement

In a Truth Social post, Trump praised Armenian Prime Minister [[nikol-pashinyan|Nikol Pashinyan]] as "a great friend and Leader" who is making his country "strong, wealthy, and very secure," and said Pashinyan completely shared his vision of peace and prosperity for Armenia and the entire South Caucasus region.[1] Trump noted that Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] had just travelled to Armenia, where he advanced several deals for both countries, and stated that the route would transform the South Caucasus and "help our wonderful American Energy Companies gain access from Central Asia all the way to the United States."[1] The post closed with Trump's complete and total endorsement of Pashinyan for re-election on June 7, 2026, and the slogan "Make (Armenia) Great Again — MAGA!"[1]

## Analysis

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] examined the geography of the claim, noting that Armenia sits at the center of the triangle formed by Georgia, [[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]], and [[iran|Iran]], and is technically located in West Asia rather than Central Asia — the territory between the Caspian Sea and [[china|China]].[1] On that reading, Trump's reference to "Central Asia" most likely pointed to [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], with which Trump had previously announced a $4 billion railroad deal that he touted as "the largest Railroad Equipment Purchase in History."[1]

The commentary concluded that Trump was insinuating that the United States intends to join the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]]'s [[north-south-transport-corridor|North-South Transport Corridor]], most likely tying into the network through Kazakhstan and across eastern [[russia|Russia]] to Alaska — an idea consistent with revived speculation about a rail bridge or tunnel connecting Russia and Alaska after the Trump–[[vladimir-putin|Putin]] meeting in Alaska.[1] In that framing, Trump appears to be aligning with Putin and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] against the old guard of Europe, specifically the Paris-based [[suez-company|Suez Company]] and its affiliates in the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[1]
