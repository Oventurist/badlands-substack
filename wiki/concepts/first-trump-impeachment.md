# First Trump Impeachment

The first impeachment of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], often referred to as the Ukraine impeachment, arose from a 2019 whistleblower complaint concerning the President's dealings with [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and led to proceedings in the House of Representatives and a trial in the Senate.

## The whistleblower complaint and Witness 2

According to reporting summarized in the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, the complaint attributed to [[eric-ciaramella|Eric Ciaramella]] was advanced by Intelligence Community Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]], and the 2019 claims of an official designated in the impeachment documents only as "Witness 2" were critical in helping Atkinson push it forward. That witness has since been identified as [[gavin-wilde|Gavin Wilde]], a co-author of the [[2017-intelligence-community-assessment|2017 Intelligence Community Assessment]] who served on the National Security Council in 2018 and 2019.

The same reporting states that Wilde's [[russiagate|Russiagate]]-linked biases were concealed from House investigators during the impeachment process. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected that concealment to the obstruction described by [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] in *Government Gangsters*, and treated the overlap of personnel between [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] and the impeachment as evidence of a continuous campaign against Trump rather than two separate episodes.
