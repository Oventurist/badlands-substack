# Executive Order 14339

Executive Order 14339 is a presidential directive cited as the legal basis for the creation of the [[260th-special-purpose-brigade|260th Special Purpose Brigade]] within the [[district-of-columbia-national-guard|District of Columbia National Guard]].[1]

The order, together with a directive from the [[department-of-war|Department of War]], called for a trained and equipped National Guard unit to support public safety in the nation's capital and to integrate with federal and local security partners.[1] The resulting brigade, activated in March 2026, is the US Army's only formation dedicated to coordinating military support to civil authorities and protecting critical infrastructure in Washington, DC.[1]
