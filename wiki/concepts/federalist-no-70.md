# Federalist No. 70

Federalist No. 70 is an essay arguing for a strong, energetic single executive.[1] It was invoked in July 2024 by [[kevin-roberts|Kevin Roberts]], president of the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]], in support of his claim that the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s [[presidential-immunity|presidential immunity]] ruling was "vital" and would reinforce a [[second-american-revolution|second American Revolution]].[1]

The source report, published by [[the-hill|The Hill]] and quoted in the Badlands News Brief, attributes the essay to Alexander Hamilton and dates it to 1877 — a date inconsistent with the Federalist Papers' actual late-1780s publication, an apparent error carried in the original reporting.[1]
