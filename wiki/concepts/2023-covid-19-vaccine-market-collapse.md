# 2023 COVID-19 Vaccine Market Collapse

The 2023 COVID-19 vaccine market collapse refers to the sharp contraction in projected demand for COVID-19 booster shots that hit vaccine makers' valuations in September 2023.[1]

## Moderna's share plunge

Although [[moderna|Moderna]]'s drug portfolio extends beyond COVID-19 shots, those products still made up a major part of its business — enough that news about the size of the market prompted investors to walk away, taking more than 9 percent of the company's market capitalization at one point during Monday afternoon trading.[1]

## Pfizer's booster estimate

The trigger came from fellow biotech [[pfizer|Pfizer]], which said only about 24 percent of eligible Americans were going to get a COVID-19 booster shot.[1] That implied a maximum market of roughly 82 million people, substantially less than the earliest figures.[1] Analysts including Chris Schott of JP Morgan expected COVID-19 treatment sales to level off and become more predictable starting in 2024.[1]

## Outlook

Even as the COVID-19 sales boom wore thin — especially among potential customers who by then regarded the disease as little more than a trumped-up cold — some analysts projected that Moderna's next five years could outperform the last five, citing a run of announcements about new products spanning oncology, rare diseases, and beyond.[1] Such programs carry the standard biotech burden of taking years or decades to produce returns, but the company retained a solid cash runway built from COVID-19 sales.[1]
