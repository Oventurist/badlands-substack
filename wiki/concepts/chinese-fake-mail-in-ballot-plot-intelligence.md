# Chinese Fake Mail-In Ballot Plot Intelligence

The Chinese fake mail-in ballot plot intelligence refers to a set of August 2020 US intelligence reports, declassified and delivered to [[congress]] in June 2025, raising concerns that [[china]] had mass-produced counterfeit US driver's licenses as part of a scheme to hijack the 2020 election with fraudulent mail-in ballots cast for Democrat [[joe-biden]].[1]

## Declassification and transmittal

On the evening of Monday, June 16, 2025, [[fbi]] Director [[kash-patel]] turned the intelligence report over to Congress.[1] The documents went to Senate Judiciary Committee Chairman [[chuck-grassley]], Republican of Iowa, who had first raised concerns to the bureau that the intelligence had never been fully vetted and had instead simply been dismissed despite evidence of the fake licenses.[1]

"Thanks to the oversight work and partnership of Chairman Grassley, the FBI continues to provide unprecedented transparency at the people's Bureau," Patel told Just the News in a statement, adding that the bureau had located documents Grassley requested detailing "alarming allegations related to the 2020 U.S. election."[1]

## Handling in 2020

Officials told Just the News that the newly declassified reports were neither corroborated nor fully investigated, and were instead recalled from intelligence agencies at roughly the same time that then-FBI Director [[christopher-wray]] testified that there were no known plots of foreign interference ahead of the 2020 election.[1] Biden defeated [[donald-trump]] in that election.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright]] framed the disclosure as a step toward a mass public narrative exposing that US elections had been "entirely fake the whole time," asking whether those who had said so all along would still be accused of "dooming," and concluding that "the plan never relied on cheerleading. It relied on truth."[1]

[[ashe-in-america]] connected the release to a January 7, 2021 letter by then-Acting Director of National Intelligence [[john-ratcliffe]] — later CIA Director — in which he objected to the intelligence community's consensus on election security and described the withholding of information about Chinese threats to US elections.[1] She noted that Jon Herold breaks down the significance of the DNI letter in [[devolution|Devolution Part 7]], and asked whether the intelligence emergency-declassified by Patel was part of what Ratcliffe had been referring to in early 2021.[1]
