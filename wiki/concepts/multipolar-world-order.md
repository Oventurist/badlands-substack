# Multipolar World Order

The **multipolar world order** is the term used by Russia and allied states for an international system in which power is distributed among multiple sovereign centers rather than concentrated in a single hegemon.[1] It is presented as the alternative to what its advocates call the "unipolar world order" dominated by the United States and its financial and military institutions.[1]

## Promotion at SPIEF 2024

At the [[2024-st-petersburg-international-economic-forum|St. Petersburg International Economic Forum]] in June 2024, attending nations reported the ongoing success of their operation to defect from the unipolar world order and reaffirmed their intent to establish the multipolar world order that had been discussed for years.[1] The forum's headline outcome, according to Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], was a consensus among attending nations to [[de-dollarization|de-dollarize]] their national economies away from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]'s fiat U.S. dollar.[1] The event coincided with the fiftieth anniversary of the 1974 [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] agreement between [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] and Saudi King Faisal.[1] African participants stated that a multipolar world served their interests.[1]

## Framing as a two-faction conflict

Badlands commentary frames contemporary geopolitics as a contest between the ruling Unipolar Globalist Empire and a rebelling Multipolar [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[1] On this reading, the [[2024-swiss-peace-summit-on-ukraine|Swiss peace summit]] of June 2024 was the first major "battle" fought on a world stage, presenting participating states with a binary choice: of roughly 160 invited countries only 78 formally accepted and signed the summit declaration, with none of the [[brics|BRICS]] states signing, an outcome interpreted as more than a hundred countries in rebellion against unipolar hegemony.[1]

## "The multi-polar world order isn't coming. It's here."

The phrase became a signature refrain of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who used it to open and close his August 29, 2023 essay "BRIC by BRIC: Emergence of the Multi-Polar World," written in the wake of the [[2023-brics-johannesburg-summit|BRICS summit in Johannesburg]].[2] He argued that the bloc's expansion — particularly the accession of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and other Persian Gulf states — meant that the rebalancing was no longer a forecast but an accomplished condition to be embraced rather than feared.[2]

He treated the emergence of a rival bloc during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s absence from public power as circumstantial evidence for [[devolution|Devolution]], reasoning that a sitting Trump could neither have permitted kinetic incursions by the Russian Federation nor allowed a challenge to the dollar to grow unopposed without contradicting his own rhetoric.[2] He predicted that Trump would return to public power amid a "reborn and rebuilding international order," would be received as a deal-maker and problem-solver in the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom and South America, and that while the U.S. might not formally join BRICS it would join the multipolar project "on a philosophical level."[2]

## Putin at St. Petersburg and the defense of natural law

In a November 2025 essay, [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argued that while coherence with natural law has been dislodged in the western world over the past half century — giving way to a [[transhumanism|transhumanist]], neo-eugenicist pseudo-religion underlying a unipolar rules-based order — the torch has been picked up by leading statesmen across Eurasia resisting a neo-feudal dystopia.[3]

He quoted Russian president [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s address to the XXV St. Petersburg International Economic Forum, in which Putin described technological development as the cross-cutting area defining the century, called for building a "techno economy" with reform of engineering education and financing for fast-growing high-tech companies, and observed "an increasingly pronounced trend in favour of a multipolar growth model in lieu of globalisation."[3] Putin added that it is up to "the strong sovereign states, those that do not follow a trajectory imposed by others, to set the rules governing the new world order", warning that states lacking such sovereignty "are doomed to become or remain colonies devoid of any rights."[3]

Ehret contrasted this with [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]]'s unipolar vision of stasis and an end to history, in which a "high caste" of Davos elites presides over a global [[useless-eaters|useless class]] rendered incapable of revolt.[3]

## Answering the "controlled opposition" charge

In "Don't Be Symbol-Minded" (November 15, 2025), [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] responded to the argument, advanced by [[peter-duke|Peter Duke]] and [[hrvoje-moric|Hrvoje Moric]], that the [[brics|BRICS]]/multipolar alliance is merely a different flavor of the same totalitarian evil.[4] He conceded that at a purely pattern-recognition level — comparing banking mechanisms or the wording of diplomatic documents — similarities between Eurasian/BRICS nations and NATO/trans-Atlantic systems are real.[4] But from the standpoint of [[universal-principles|Universal Principles]], he argued, a pronounced and meaningful opposition of a non-Hegelian sort is visible, because one side is provably animated by an intention to eliminate 90–95% of the human species while the multipolar states pursue abundance creation, the elimination of scarcity, creative flanking maneuvers to stop wars and increased interconnectivity instead of divide-and-conquer.[4]

Ehret pointed to his earlier studies "BRICS+ VS. the WEF: The Clash of Two Green Paradigms" and "Communism, Capitalism and Feudalism: How Nominalism Makes us Fools" as documentation of [[china|China]]'s program to undermine Malthusian systems of control.[4] He acknowledged that agents and deep-state saboteurs exist within multipolar-leaning nations across Mexico, Latin America, Asia, Russia and Africa, citing the death sentence handed to a former Bank of China chairman that week and asking why comparable accountability never reaches American bankers.[4] His conclusion was that the Eurasian operating system is incompatible with the ancient oligarchical formula of depopulation, war and stupidification, and that its animating principles are identical in principle — though not in form — to those of [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]] in 1776 and of the [[ionian-revolt|Ionian rebellion]] of 499 BC.[4]
