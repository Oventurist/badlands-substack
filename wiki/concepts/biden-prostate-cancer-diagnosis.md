# Biden Prostate Cancer Diagnosis

In May 2025 former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was reported to have been diagnosed with an aggressive form of prostate cancer, disclosed days after the release of audio from his interviews with special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]].[1]

## Clinical picture

The disease was characterized by a Gleason score of 9 and Grade Group 5 with bone metastasis, a presentation with a five-year survival rate of between 30% and 40%.[1] Fox News senior medical analyst Marc Siegel described the cancer as "pretty far advanced" and said, "It's the most aggressive type. That means it has the highest risk of spread — which obviously he has had."[1] Siegel noted that Biden was also presenting with urinary symptoms, another indicator of advanced disease, and that prostate cancer is often asymptomatic in its early stages.[1]

## Political reaction

David Axelrod suggested that conversations about Biden's "mental acuity" would be more muted following the announcement.[1] Badlands commentary observed that House Republicans said nothing about the diagnosis on X while Senate Republicans issued thoughtful and prayerful statements, and asked where the demands for committee hearings had gone.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s allies at Badlands declined to celebrate. Jon Herold wrote that he "wouldn't wish [prostate cancer] on anybody" and was "praying he makes a full recovery so he can watch all his friends go to prison."[1] Ashe in America said she would neither gloat nor "pretend this is a brand new diagnosis," pointing out that Biden had said he had cancer in 2022 and that the media had dismissed the remark as a gaffe; she argued the diagnosis made the officials operating "the cancer-riddled dementia patient and his autopen" more culpable, not less, and called for [[jill-biden|Jill Biden]], the medical staff, the chief of staff, the cabinet, [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] and [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] to testify under oath.[1]

Chris Paul wrote that he had projected such an event more than four years earlier when describing Biden as "the Fall Guy," a scapegoat to be blamed for the regime's program and then written out of the story "as sympathetically as possible."[1] He predicted the character would be killed off at a moment when a distraction was needed, suggesting the week before the nation's 250th birthday celebration and military parade, and argued that the announcement would make further discussion of Biden's dementia and illegitimacy socially forbidden.[1] The episode is closely bound up with the [[autopen-scandal|autopen scandal]] and with the release of the [[hur-report|Hur]] interview recordings.[1]
