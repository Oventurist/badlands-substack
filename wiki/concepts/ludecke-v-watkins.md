# Ludecke v. Watkins

Ludecke v. Watkins is a 1948 United States Supreme Court decision addressing the scope of presidential authority to remove enemy aliens and the availability of judicial review over that authority.[1]

## Holding as cited

In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, the decision was quoted for the proposition that "the very nature of the President's power to order the removal of all enemy aliens rejects the notion that courts may pass judgement upon the exercise of his discretion... and every judge before whom the question has since come has held that the statute barred judicial review."[1]

## Invocation in the 2025 deportation dispute

The case was raised by [[canncon|CannCon]] in response to U.S. District Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]]'s demands for information about deportation flights carried out under the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]], with the argument that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] owed the judge no response at all because the statute forecloses judicial review of the removal power.[1] The same commentary called for the judge to be removed from the bench and prosecuted for obstruction.[1]
