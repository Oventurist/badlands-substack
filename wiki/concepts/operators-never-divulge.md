# Operators Never Divulge

**"Operators never divulge"** is a phrase from [[q-operation|Q]] drop #60, posted November 2, 2017, which Chapter 7 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] treats as one of the most important operational-security statements in the drops.[1]

## Content of drop #60

The drop states that fewer than ten people have the full picture and scope of the Q operation, and that of those fewer than ten, only three are non-military.[1] The Chapter 7 author presents this as non-negotiable — "Anyone saying any different is lying" — and explains the rationale in counterintelligence terms: the fewer people who hold the whole plan, the smaller the chance of it being co-opted or sabotaged, and the easier it is to compartmentalise objectives and contain leakers.[1] He links the military composition of the group to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s relationship with the military.[1]

## Game theory framing

The chapter reads the drop alongside a chess and game-theory analogy: by limiting an opponent's options from multiple angles, one can dictate their responses and have them defeated ten moves before they realise it, even while they continue to take pieces off the board.[1] The author applies this to the present, arguing that "Patriots are in control" and the adversary cannot win, but retains the ability to cause damage in its "death rattle."[1]

## Use against claimed insiders

The phrase's principal polemical use in the chapter is against figures in the "Truth Community" who claim direct inside information from the Q Team.[1] The author argues that operators, by definition, never divulge, and that such claimants build up hopes for events that never occur — predicted arrest dates, JFK Jr. becoming vice president, Medbeds, and a Quantum Financial System — none of which, he says, Q ever mentioned or confirmed.[1] He frames the resulting disillusionment of newcomers, who take "an unvomitable black pill and walk away from the fight," as precisely the outcome an enemy would want.[1]
