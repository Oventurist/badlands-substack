# Take Care Clause

The Take Care Clause is the provision of Article II of the United States Constitution requiring that the president "take care that the laws be faithfully executed."[1] In late December 2025 the clause became the subject of commentary by Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] on Devolution Power Hour, who advanced a reading of the clause centered on constitutional primacy rather than statutory obedience.[1]

## The constitutional-duty reading

Paul argued that the president's "duty is to the Constitution first and foremost," and that it "is not to all of the laws that have been passed at some point by a Congress, legitimate or not."[1] On this reading, where statutes stand "in direct conflict with the president's understanding of the original Constitution, that is a full explanation for why the president would not go ahead and execute those laws."[1] He went further, contending that executing unconstitutional laws would itself be a breach of the oath: "It would be treason for him to do otherwise."[1]

The framing was published alongside coverage of federal action against alleged fraud in state-administered programs, and functions within Badlands commentary as a justification for aggressive executive non-enforcement of laws deemed illegitimate.[1]
