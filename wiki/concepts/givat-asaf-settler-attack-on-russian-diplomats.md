# Givat Asaf Settler Attack on Russian Diplomats

On July 30, 2025, a [[russia|Russian]] diplomatic vehicle was attacked by [[israeli-settler-movement|Israeli settlers]] in the occupied West Bank, causing mechanical damage to the car.[1]

## The incident

[[russian-foreign-ministry|Russian Foreign Ministry]] spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] disclosed the attack on Tuesday, August 5, 2025, stating that Moscow considers it a violation of the [[vienna-convention-on-diplomatic-relations|Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations]].[1] According to Zakharova the incident took place near the illegal Israeli settlement of Giv'at Asaf, just east of Ramallah and roughly 20 kilometres north of Jerusalem.[1] The settlers also shouted threats at the diplomats.[1]

The vehicle was carrying members of Russia's diplomatic mission to the Palestinian Authority, personnel who are also accredited with the [[israeli-ministry-of-foreign-affairs|Israeli Foreign Ministry]].[1]

## Israeli military inaction

Zakharova criticized the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] for failing to intervene, calling their inaction "particularly puzzling" and saying that "the Israeli Defense Force soldiers didn't even bother to try and stop the attackers' aggressive actions."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that the odds of a Russian "special military operation"-style intervention regarding Israel had increased dramatically, and read the IDF's non-intervention as a deliberate message concerning Russia's long-held pro-Palestine position.[1] The commentary noted the historical irony that the original colonists of Israel came from Russia and that, according to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], some 20 percent of Israelis retain Russian citizenship, concluding that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] had "entered FAFO territory."[1]
