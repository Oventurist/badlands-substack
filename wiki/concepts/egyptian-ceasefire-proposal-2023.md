# Egyptian Ceasefire Proposal (2023)

The Egyptian ceasefire proposal was a detailed, multi-stage plan floated by [[egypt|Egypt]] in late December 2023 for ending the war in Gaza and shaping the post-war political settlement, published as [[israel|Israel]] simultaneously vowed to expand its Gaza campaign.[1]

## Terms

The plan, reported by several outlets, envisioned a staged cessation of hostilities culminating in the full withdrawal of Israeli forces from Gaza, together with a new technocratic government and a unified Palestinian administration overseeing both Gaza and the West Bank.[1]

In the first stage, [[hamas|Hamas]] and allied armed Palestinian groups would release all civilian captives held in Gaza in exchange for a negotiated number of Palestinians held in Israeli prisons, producing a truce and pause in fighting of between seven and ten days.[1] In the second stage, Hamas would free all female Israeli soldiers held captive while Israel released additional Palestinian prisoners, with a further week-long pause.[1] The final stage would see Hamas release the remaining captives and Israel free more detained Palestinians after negotiations conducted over a period of one month; Israel would then pull its forces back to the Gaza border and cease all aerial operations against the Strip.[1]

## Context

Coverage in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] paired the proposal with the parallel escalation in the [[red-sea-shipping-crisis|Red Sea]], where Houthi attacks on commercial shipping were disrupting global trade.[1] Commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Egypt was "being primed (and is priming themselves) for a key role in not only settling the Suez crisis, but the larger Gaza situation tangential to it."[1]
