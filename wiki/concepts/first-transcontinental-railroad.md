# First Transcontinental Railroad

The **first transcontinental railroad** was the rail line begun in 1863 and completed in 1869, linking a continent from east to west for the first time in history.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] emphasizes that the project was launched at the peak of the [[american-civil-war|Civil War]] itself, as part of the often-overlooked large-scale industrial and rail programs financed by [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]]'s monetary reforms.[1] [[greenbacks|Greenbacks]] and [[five-twenty-bonds|5:20 bonds]] paid not only for the arming, feeding and payment of soldiers but also for these development programs.[1]

The railway was financed through grants and subsidies made possible by the greenbacks, which Ehret states increased government spending power by 300 percent.[1] The project stands in his account as the practical demonstration of the [[american-system-of-political-economy|American System]] principle that money's value should be tied to the increasing powers of production of a society through manufacturing and internal improvements.[1]
