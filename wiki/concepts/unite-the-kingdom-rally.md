# Unite the Kingdom Rally

The "Unite the Kingdom" rally was a mass demonstration held in London in May 2026, organized by British activist [[tommy-robinson|Tommy Robinson]] and drawing tens of thousands of participants according to police estimates.[1]

US Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] publicly backed the rally's participants, saying it was "OK to defend your culture" and urging opponents of mass migration in the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]] to "keep on going," while arguing that Western governments have relied on "millions and millions of unvetted people" and framing tighter border controls as both an economic and a cultural issue.[1] Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] condemned the event as promoting "hatred and division," and police reported dozens of arrests, several tied to alleged hate-crime offenses.[1]

## Vigilante patrol claims

A related claim circulating alongside the rally held that UK towns were forming "vigilante" security groups to protect women and children from migrants.[1] Reporting and watchdog documentation tie this to anti-migrant activist networks and local protest groups that emerged amid tensions over asylum housing and Channel crossings, with some groups organizing patrols, monitoring migrant activity, or attempting to disrupt migrant boat crossings in northern France.[1]

Badlands contributor Ashe in America argued that the "vigilante" label is a character-assassination framing, reading the underlying facts as British and French citizens forming neighborhood patrols for community safety, and attributed the underlying migration dynamic to the "[[great-migration|Great Migration]]," which she described as a transformational change program pushed by the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and its partners over two decades.[1]
