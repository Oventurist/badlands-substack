# Imperial presidency

"Imperial presidency" is the framing used by [[axios|Axios]] in July 2024 to describe the second-term plans attributed to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by his advisers, and carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief.[1]

According to the report, Trump, if re-elected, planned to immediately test the boundaries of presidential and governing power, knowing that the restraints of [[congress|Congress]] and the courts were dramatically looser than during his first term.[1] The analysis pointed not only to the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s ruling that presidents enjoy substantial [[presidential-immunity|legal immunity]] for official acts, but also to a Cabinet and staff pre-vetted for loyalty and a fully compliant Republican coalition in Congress devoid of critics in positions of real power.[1]

The described program included turning the Justice Department against critics, deporting millions of people in the United States illegally, imposing 10% tariffs on thousands of products, and firing perhaps tens of thousands of government staff deemed insufficiently loyal.[1] Axios stressed that Trump stated these intentions "consistently and clearly," so the account was "not conjecture," and that thanks to the immunity ruling he could pursue them "without fear of punishment or restraint."[1]

The report also connected the prospect to Democratic anxieties, noting that the expectation of such a presidency was a major reason many Democrats worried that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was "making one of the biggest gambles in U.S. history" by staying in the race amid concerns about his age.[1] A related Axios piece reported that major Democratic donors were shifting money toward House and Senate races to build a Capitol Hill firewall around a second Trump term they viewed as increasingly likely.[1]
