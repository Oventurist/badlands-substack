# Illuminatus Trilogy

The *Illuminatus Trilogy* is a series of books co-authored by [[robert-anton-wilson|Robert Anton Wilson]] and his [[playboy|Playboy]] colleague [[robert-shea|Robert Shea]].[1]

Badlands Media's [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series is concerned with the trilogy's ending, in which a globally influential evil cult attempts to animate an army of zombies that have been held in "suspended animation" at the bottom of a giant lake.[1] [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] proposes that this plot device may amount to fictionalised disclosure regarding the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult and the [[black-sea|Black Sea]], noting that the Black Sea is the largest meromictic sea on Earth — its layers do not mix, so that its contents largely exist in a condition the author likens to suspended animation.[1]

The trilogy's authorship by Playboy editors is presented as part of a broader argument that 1960s counterculture publishing served both as a vehicle for deconstructing traditional Western values and as an occasional channel for occult signalling.[1]
