# Speech Laws

"Speech laws" is the shorthand used in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands Brief]] for the body of pending legislation and regulation that would restrict categories of expression, treated in the eighteenth episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] as an emerging front in the [[information-war|Info War]].[1]

## Coverage on The Blitz

The 23 May 2026 episode, titled "Tina Peters Coming Out; Speech Laws Coming In," juxtaposed the commutation of [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]]' sentence with what its hosts described as a coming ban on antisemitism and other speech laws.[1] GhostofBasedPatrickHenry — see [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]] — and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the two developments as opposing movements in the same episode title, one liberty restored and the other liberty constrained.[1]

The episode situated the speech-law discussion alongside geopolitics and expectations for the golden age, placing legal restrictions on expression within the broader arc of narrative and sovereignty questions the Brief tracks Monday through Friday.[1]
