# Higgins Memo

The Higgins memo is a May 2017 document written by Rich Higgins, then a National Security Council staffer, warning that the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] was "suffering under withering information campaigns designed to first undermine, then delegitimize and ultimately remove" the president.[1] The memo argued that the White House risked misreading these campaigns as "an elevated interplay of otherwise normal D.C. partisan infighting and adversarial media relations," and that its response reflected "a political advocacy mindset that is intensely reactive, severely under-inclusive and dangerously inadequate to the threat."[1] Higgins concluded that without a re-scoped response, the administration risked "implosion and subsequent early departure from the White House."[1]

## Continued invocation

[[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] cited the memo's opening paragraph on X in November 2025 in response to the [[seditious-six-video|"Seditious Six" video]], noting that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had read the memo in 2017 and arguing that he "needs to read it again."[1] Flynn's bottom line was that "very little has changed and this is still not 'politics as usual,'" describing the environment as "political warfare" and a color revolution.[1] Badlands writer Jon Herold has for nearly four years described the memo as "required monthly reading," and it is analyzed at length in Devolution Part 23 on the Patel Patriot Substack.[1] Higgins died prior to the 2025 commentary, which referred to him as "may he R.I.P."[1]
