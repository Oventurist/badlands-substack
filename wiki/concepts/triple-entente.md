# Triple Entente

The **Triple Entente** was the alignment of Britain, France and Russia formed when the Russia of Czar [[nicholas-ii|Nicholas II]] secretly acceded in 1907 to the Anglo-French [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]] of 1904.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents the Triple Entente not as a defensive arrangement but as the completed architecture of the encirclement of Germany designed by [[edward-vii|King Edward VII]].[1]

## Construction

According to the article, the pieces were set in place through a sequence of operations preceding and accompanying the entente treaties: the destruction of the Russian army by the Imperial Japanese army in 1904, the 1905 revolutionary attempt in Russia associated with [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] and [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]], and the Balkan Wars fomented through the [[young-turks|Young Turks]], described as a freemasonic agency tied to Giuseppe Mazzini's P1 lodge.[1] Only after Russia had been weakened and mismanaged did it enter the pact in 1907.[1]

The article notes that had [[william-mckinley|William McKinley]] survived the 1901 assassin's bullet, the United States would likely have worked against this bloc; instead, by 1907 [[theodore-roosevelt|Theodore Roosevelt]] had proclaimed the first Anglo-American "special relationship" under the charm of Edward VII.[1]

## Consequences

Military pacts known to every geopolitician of 1914 guaranteed Russian intervention on Serbia's side and German support for Austria, so that the killing of [[archduke-franz-ferdinand|Archduke Franz Ferdinand]] by the [[black-hand|Black Hand]] on June 28, 1914 set off a chain reaction leading a sleep-walking world into general war.[1] Kaiser [[wilhelm-ii|Wilhelm II]] wrote in August 1914 that "England, Russia, and France have agreed among themselves… to take the Austro-Serbian conflict for an excuse for waging a war of extermination against us."[1]
