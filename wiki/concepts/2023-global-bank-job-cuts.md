# 2023 Global Bank Job Cuts

Global banks eliminated more than 60,000 jobs during 2023, marking one of the heaviest years for cuts since the financial crisis and reversing much of the hiring undertaken as the industry emerged from the Covid-19 pandemic.[1]

According to [[financial-times|Financial Times]] calculations, twenty of the world's biggest banks cut at least 61,905 jobs over the year.[1] That figure compared with more than 140,000 positions slashed by the same lenders during the global financial crisis of 2007-08.[1] Investment banks suffered a second consecutive year of plummeting fees as dealmaking and public listings dried up, leaving Wall Street firms attempting to protect profit margins by reducing headcount.[1]

A substantial share of the reductions stemmed from consolidation: the takeover of [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] by [[ubs|UBS]] had already resulted in at least 13,000 fewer roles at the combined bank, with further large redundancy rounds expected in the following year.[1]

Lee Thacker, owner of the financial services headhunting firm Silvermine Partners, summarised the outlook bleakly: "There is no stability, no investment, no growth in most banks — and there are likely to be more job cuts," adding that "there are some very nice gifts being sent to bosses at the moment."[1]
