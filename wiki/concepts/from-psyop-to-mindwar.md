# From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory

*From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory* is a 1980 essay by [[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]], co-authored with Colonel [[paul-vallely|Paul Vallely]] and commissioned for the US military by Major General [[albert-stubblebine|Albert Stubblebine]].[1]

Ehret describes the essay as the directing manifesto of a "revolution in military affairs" that followed the template set forth by Zbigniew Brzezinski and Samuel P. Huntington.[1] The document is said to have introduced [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] trauma-based mind control studies, the SRI Stargate Project and [[esalen-institute|Esalen Institute]] drug experiments into basic military training, alongside an array of electromagnetic weapon programs, all designed to carry out asymmetric warfare on the minds of citizens both within and outside the United States.[1]

In this account, the resulting "New Templar takeover" of the US military produced a vanguard of psychological warfare "Jedi warriors" and modern Grail knights who came to dominate increasing sections of the armed forces and to oversee a new global Crusade or Clash of Civilizations — beginning with Operation Desert Storm and continuing through the bombing of Yugoslavia, 9/11 and the forever wars across the Middle East.[1]
