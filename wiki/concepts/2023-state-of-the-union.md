# 2023 State of the Union Address

President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] delivered the 2023 [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union]] address on Tuesday, February 7, 2023, before a Congress that Badlands Media described as more embattled and more publicly scrutinized than at any prior point in American history. The speech was repeatedly interrupted by heckling and booing from Republican members.[1]

## Content

ZeroHedge's summary of the speech catalogued Biden's villains — billionaires and stock buybacks (both to be taxed), Chinese balloons, big oil, big tech, guns, police shootings, and Vladimir Putin — against his claimed successes: falling inflation and gas prices, job growth, climate spending through the Inflation Reduction Act, COVID, immigration, and support for Volodymyr Zelensky.[1]

In his own response, former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said: "I am running for president to end the destruction of our country and to complete the unfinished business of making America great again," adding that the country would be made better than ever and that he would "always put America first."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Biden spent much of the first half of the speech touting Trumpian policy and rhetoric, advancing America First talking points and extolling nationalism — comments that sat awkwardly against his own record. That Democratic members were caught cheering for increased oil and domestic production, Burning Bright wrote, showed how quickly the [[overton-window|Overton Window]] can shift. The night's larger takeaway, in this reading, was the contrast between Biden's delivery and Trump's presentation of himself as a strong, clever and humorous alternative.[1]

## Security dispute

The address was also the occasion of a divided [[capitol-police-board|Capitol Police Board]] vote on temporary security fencing around the Capitol, which passed 2–1 over the objection of House sergeant at arms [[william-mcfarland|William McFarland]].[1]
