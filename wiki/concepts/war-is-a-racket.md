# War Is a Racket

***War Is a Racket*** is the thesis and book of the decorated United States general [[smedley-butler|Smedley Butler]], who argued in the 1930s that war is a racket and that the puppeteers play both sides against each other.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Use in Badlands commentary

An October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] presents Butler's thesis as the truth that the general public is about to rediscover as [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] conditioning breaks down under the strain of the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The essay predicts that once people "look under the hood" of a war that will not start cleanly, they will see what Badlands readers and similar groups have been seeing for years — the same truth Butler exposed nearly a century earlier.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)
