# 2023 House Judiciary Manhattan Crime Hearing

The **2023 House Judiciary Manhattan crime hearing** was a field hearing scheduled by Republicans on the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] in New York City focused on "victims of violent crime in Manhattan," announced on Monday, April 10, 2023.[1]

## Background and announcement

The committee's hearing notice specifically named Manhattan District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]], whose investigation of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had produced a historic indictment at the end of March 2023.[1] According to the notice, the hearing "will examine how Manhattan District Attorney Alvin Bragg's pro-crime, anti-victim policies have led to an increase in violent crime and a dangerous community for New York City residents."[1]

## Reaction

New York Democrats raged over the announcement.[1] A spokesperson for Bragg dismissed the hearing as a "political stunt" that would not "engage in actual efforts to increase public safety, such as supporting national gun legislation and shutting down the iron pipeline," and touted improvements in some categories of violent crime in Manhattan since Bragg took office.[1] Democrats promoted the claim that New York City is the "safest big city in America."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the political left's "superpower" is repeating a claim until it is believed without question, citing the "safest big city in America" line as an example repeated ad nauseam by media commentators.[1] He contended that New York City appears safer only because Bragg downgrades felonies to misdemeanors for violent crimes, quipping that "if it doesn't involve Trump, it's not a felony."[1] DeLarme also criticized [[jerrold-nadler|Jerrold Nadler]] for attacking [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] over Jordan's push to defund what DeLarme called the fully weaponized [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]], noting that Nadler had previously claimed [[antifa|ANTIFA]] was a myth while small businesses burned.[1]
