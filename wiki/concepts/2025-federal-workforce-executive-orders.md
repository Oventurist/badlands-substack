# 2025 Federal Workforce Executive Orders

The 2025 federal workforce executive orders were a set of directives issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] within hours of returning to power in January 2025, seeking to overhaul how the federal government operates — removing job protections, ending remote work, and implementing a hiring freeze.[1] Especially consequential for employees was the [[white-house|White House]] decision to eliminate diversity, equity and inclusion programs and place the staffers who ran them on administrative leave.[1]

## Effect inside the agencies

Reporting by [[politico|Politico]] described a wave of anxiety across the bureaucracy, with some federal workers contemplating quitting, others preparing to file union grievances, and still others moving communications with colleagues to secure platforms such as Signal.[1] Some employees, fearing they would be swept up in the purge of diversity programs, began leaving their names off memos and documents that might be labeled DEI-adjacent.[1] A staffer at the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] said they were cleaning out their inbox while waiting for information about early retirement and buyout programs, remarking: "Trump version 1.0 was bad. I'm already done with version 2.0."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands commentator Ashe in America questioned how real the reported distress was, arguing that Politico's reliance on anonymous sources — attributed to fear of retaliation or of drawing attention from efforts such as the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] — was equally consistent with the sources being fabricated, given what she characterized as the outlet's low credibility.[1] She wrote that the American public was out of empathy for federal employees complaining online about their mortgages, contrasting their situation with citizens who had been silenced, demonized and debanked for political opinions, and concluded that "Elections — even and especially fake elections — have consequences."[1]

## Related actions

The orders coincided with a communications freeze across the federal health agencies.[1] See [[2025-public-health-communications-freeze|the 2025 public health communications freeze]].[1]
