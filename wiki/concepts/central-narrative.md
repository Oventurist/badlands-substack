# Central Narrative

The "central narrative," in Badlands Media usage, is the unified storyline that institutions, media and entertainment advance across otherwise unrelated domains, and which Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer* is said to serve.[1]

## Three supporting themes

The review identifies three themes running through the film that map directly onto the current [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] narrative.[1] First, listen to the [[communism|communists]] — they are merely misunderstood and comprise some of the smartest minds in the world.[1] Second, AI, like nuclear weapons, is a world-destroying capability, framed through the [[nuclear-arms-race|nuclear arms race]] metaphor.[1] Third, [[global-governance|global governance]] is the only answer, because national interests are barriers to global safety and decision rights must be globalized.[1]

## Mechanism

Badlands treats the timing as non-coincidental: the film refreshes public memory of the race to build world-ending capabilities at the precise moment the regime is making the AI comparison and calling for the sacrifice of national sovereignty in favor of global decision rights.[1] The narrative's distilled message is rendered as: fear us and submit, for we have the power to destroy you.[1] The same convergence is claimed across climate change, migration, global health and pandemic response, and corporate [[esg|ESG]] initiatives, culminating in the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1]
