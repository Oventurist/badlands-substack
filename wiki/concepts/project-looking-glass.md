# Project Looking Glass

**Project Looking Glass** is the name given in [[q-operation|Q]]-adjacent commentary to an alleged exotic technology said to permit the viewing of timelines and possible futures.[1] In Part 1 of [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]] (22 October 2024), [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] cites it as "a famous example of this type of tech" while discussing how the Q team might have measured the spiritual readiness of the collective to receive certain ideas.[1]

## Treatment in the White Pill Emporium

The author raises Looking Glass within a metaphysical rather than an engineering frame. Reasoning from [[chris-langan|Chris Langan]]'s [[cognitive-theoretic-model-of-the-universe|Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe]] and its doctrine of observer participancy, he argues that reality cannot ultimately be separated into "mental" and "physical" halves, and that it is therefore fair to posit the existence of advanced or exotic technologies allowing operators to pry into things like timelines and collective thought-forms.[1] He treats the question of how the Q team could have gauged collective readiness as genuinely open, offering Looking Glass as one candidate mechanism rather than an established fact.[1]

Earlier in the same article the author lists Looking Glass among the "far out" parts of the Q operation that the CTMU, in his view, makes it possible to analyse intelligently — alongside what he calls the mind-blowing possibility that the [[trump-organization|Trump]] family may have figured out methods for manipulating time itself, a claim he acknowledges sounds crazy but says he is prepared to back up.[1] That argument is deferred to later installments of the series.[1]
