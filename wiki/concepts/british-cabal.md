# British Cabal

The "British Cabal" is the term used by some Badlands Media contributors for what they describe as the London-centered core of the global [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]], distinguished from national deep states that they hold to be its subordinate branches.[1]

## Claimed structure

In an August 2024 essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] asserted that the British Cabal runs the Deep State component of Israel in the same way it runs the Deep State component of Iran.[1] On this model, apparently opposed national intelligence structures are branches of a single controlling apparatus rather than independent adversaries.[1]

## Narrative operations

Carlson wrote that British Cabal-controlled assets had recently begun insinuating or directly pushing the claim that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is controlled by Israel, and that the purpose of this messaging is to shift blame away from the British Cabal onto Israel.[1] He argued that some influencers repeat the claim because they are easily misled — believing they have uncovered a secret they were not meant to find, when in fact they were intentionally led to it — while others are paid to turn on Trump.[1]

He extended the same analysis to commentators who describe Trump as "the lesser of two evils," characterizing them as either genuinely misled or paid shills, and advising readers to stop following them.[1] The strategic goal he attributed to this activity is expectation management: having conceded that Trump would win in the fall, these voices work to dampen hopes so that, by controlling the public's expectations, the Cabal can control the scope of change that actually occurs.[1] Carlson tied this to a broader claim that people are conditioned to be unable to imagine a world beyond the controlled and manipulated present, which is why so many are easily "black-pilled into hopelessness."[1]

## Control of American intelligence

In a December 2023 essay occasioned by the death of [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], Carlson argued that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] does not work for the American people but for the British Cabal families who control the United States, executing color revolutions, secret bombings, drug trafficking and human trafficking operations, and manufacturing wars and conflicts on the Cabal's behalf.[2] He cited Kissinger's 1995 knighthood by [[queen-elizabeth-ii|Queen Elizabeth II]] and the existence of an Israeli monument to CIA counterintelligence chief [[james-jesus-angleton|James Jesus Angleton]] as visible markers of that patronage, and claimed that the disinformation behind [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] originated in London.[2]

## Claimed origins of communism

Carlson set out three premises for understanding the Cabal's long game: that Karl Marx was a British puppet, that the [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolsheviks]] were British puppets, and that communism itself was created by the British Cabal — concluding that the contemporary communist takeover of America is being led by the British Deep State.[2] He named [[alfred-milner|Lord Alfred Milner]] as the "point man" between the Cabal and the Bolsheviks, organizing them and securing their funding before building channels to place British assets throughout the English-speaking world, an architecture Carlson links to the [[round-table-movement|Round Table]] and the modern [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] arrangement.[2]

Carlson characterized the Cabal as indifferent to race, nationality and religion in choosing assets — instancing the Klan-linked [[william-yandell-elliott|William Yandell Elliott]] mentoring the Jewish refugee Kissinger, who in turn mentored [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] — and as sincerely believing that stripping ordinary people of their God-given rights would produce a better world in which the population is controlled and culled.[2] He closed by arguing that the Cabal is losing its grip and that "the parasites of the world who feed off of humanity are being eradicated one by one."[2]

## The billionaire elite thesis (August 2023)

In "George Soros, A British Creation," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] applied the framework to the financial elite, arguing that [[george-soros|George Soros]] and the figures around him were created and controlled by a British Satanic Cabal rather than rising on merit.[3] He stressed repeatedly that the Cabal "is comprised of all people from all walks of life, races and religions," and that its members' allegiance is to their Satanic society rather than to race or religion — a point he made in anticipation of readers reading a Jewish conspiracy into the names involved.[3]

Recurring markers Carlson identified across the network include birth or wartime flight to England ([[james-goldsmith|Sir James Goldsmith]], [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]], Soros himself), service in the British Army, and the founding of hedge funds, private equity firms and wealth management houses in the early to mid 1990s — the period when the Soviet Union fell and China began its rise, which he reads as the Cabal transitioning from control of the Soviet Union to control of the Chinese Communist Party.[3] He also argued that the Cabal is the origin of the climate change hoax, with [[king-charles-iii|King Charles]] now its leading advocate, and that the British "pretty much create and perfect the mind control system we know today," speculating about [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]]-type activity in the British military.[3]

Carlson concluded that Soros and his associates manipulate financial markets, foment [[color-revolution|color revolutions]], control the media, possibly manipulate elections, and manufacture crises and epidemics culminating in mass migration, while blackmailing, censoring and working toward one-world government — and that whatever their nationality, they all have deep ties to England and specifically the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[3]

## The ringmaster thesis (August 2023)

In "The British Cabal: The Top of the Pyramid," published 7 August 2023, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] developed the framework through two extended metaphors: the puppet show, in which the audience fixates on the puppet, a few notice the strings, and almost no one sees the deliberately concealed puppeteer; and the circus, in which apparently unrelated acts are choreographed into a whole by a ringmaster whose role looks marginal but is decisive.[4] Carlson argued that the British Cabal is both puppet master and ringmaster, coordinating operations that appear unconnected on the surface in order to secure control over money, resources and people.[4]

On this account the [[deep-state|Deep State]] is not a cohesive unit: some of its component groups do not know they are being manipulated from above, some are genuinely at odds with one another without realising they are being used, and some only pretend to be at odds.[4] Carlson listed Hollywood, the media, corporations, Washington Republicans and Democrats alike, the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]], [[mossad|Mossad]] and the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]], the [[chinese-communist-party|CCP]], the [[soviet-union|Soviet Union]] and the American Communists as circus acts whose ultimate function, knowingly or not, is to advance the Cabal's agenda.[4] He offered illustrative allegiances: [[barack-obama|Obama]] as a CIA puppet with Muslim Brotherhood sympathies, [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] tied to the CCP with a Brotherhood-linked handler in [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], and [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] as a [[rhodes-scholarship|Rhodes Scholar]] and therefore, in his reading, directly Cabal-controlled.[4]

Carlson cited [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s "God save the Queen, man" remark in Connecticut as a tell, argued that Biden's abuse of power for profit had left the Cabal more exposed than ever, and suggested the Bushes were controlled through the CIA and the Military Industrial Complex.[4] He also treated Covid as a Cabal gamble that backfired, waking large numbers of people to media, government and health-industry manipulation.[4]

## Colonial and economic warfare claims

Carlson rejected the tea-tax account of the [[american-revolutionary-war|American Revolutionary War]], asking why, if Britain lost the war outright, American history books still fail to portray England as evil — which he took as evidence the Cabal retained control of the narrative.[4] He asserted that pre-war England forbade Americans from manufacturing finished goods of any kind, taking commodities such as Southern cotton for manufacture in England and reselling the products worldwide, and that after the war Britain dumped manufactured goods below cost to destroy the young American economy, prompting early American tariffs.[4] He drew a direct parallel to post-WWI terms imposed on Germany, noting that the Kaiser was King George's first cousin, and to China's 1990s rise on cheap exports, which he compared to Trump's tariff response.[4]

He argued that England created [[communism|communism]] and uses it to control the world, playing the Soviet Union off the United States and now China off the United States, switching enemies and rewriting history in the manner of *1984*.[4] As evidence that the Soviet Union served as a human shield and boogeyman, he contrasted WWII military death tolls — roughly 417,000 American, 383,000 British, 217,000 French, and 9–11 million Soviet — computing 23–29 Soviet deaths per British death among allies, and compared this to Vietnam's ratio of about 19 North Vietnamese dead per American.[4]

Carlson read Trump's 2017 world tour — meetings with Russia, Germany, France, Japan, Canada, Turkey, India, China, Saudi Arabia, Israel, England and the Pope — as a confrontation with Cabal-controlled capitals, noting the symbolism read into Trump walking ahead of the Queen, and observed that the [[brics|BRICS]] bloc and its aspirant members map closely onto the leaders Trump met.[4] He closed with the claim that England invented [[globalism|globalism]], formerly called Imperialism, created communism, runs drug and human trafficking, and built modern mind control and propaganda through [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock]], with [[satanism|Satanism]] the common bond at the top of the pyramid — refusing to accept that an empire ruling a quarter of the world simply gave it up undefeated, and noting that communism rose worldwide immediately afterward.[4]

## Control through intelligence agencies (March 2024)

In "The CIA is Being Thrown Under the Bus," Erik Carlson developed the Cabal's transition from open empire to covert rule in terms of its intelligence apparatus, holding that it created [[mi6|MI6]] in 1909 along with think tanks such as [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock]] and other influential organizations, and thereafter controlled the world from the shadows rather than through armies.[5] He argued that MI6 created the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]] in 1942 to control the United States during the Second World War, and that the OSS became the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] in 1947 while remaining under British elite control — making the agency the Cabal's enforcement arm ever since.[5]

On this reading the Cabal's grip on America lapsed between the Civil War and the First World War, a period of inward-facing industrial expansion, and was restored only when British intelligence agent Colonel [[edward-mandell-house|House]] groomed the unknown [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] into the presidency, after which came the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and American entry into both world wars.[5] After 1945 the Cabal changed method again, governing through a Cold War in which it controlled both the American and Soviet sides.[5]

Carlson's 2024 thesis is that the Cabal is now abandoning the CIA as a scapegoat and transferring power to [[nato|NATO]], the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and the [[european-union|European Union]] — specifically England and France — because those bodies lie beyond a returning [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s control.[5] Losing the agency, he wrote, does not destroy the Cabal but strips it of its most-used tool, leaving it able to scheme without the means to act; he characterized the pivot to fighting America from outside as an act of desperation rather than choice.[5]
