# British Cabal

The "British Cabal" is the term used by some Badlands Media contributors for what they describe as the London-centered core of the global [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]], distinguished from national deep states that they hold to be its subordinate branches.[1]

## Claimed structure

In an August 2024 essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] asserted that the British Cabal runs the Deep State component of Israel in the same way it runs the Deep State component of Iran.[1] On this model, apparently opposed national intelligence structures are branches of a single controlling apparatus rather than independent adversaries.[1]

## Narrative operations

Carlson wrote that British Cabal-controlled assets had recently begun insinuating or directly pushing the claim that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is controlled by Israel, and that the purpose of this messaging is to shift blame away from the British Cabal onto Israel.[1] He argued that some influencers repeat the claim because they are easily misled — believing they have uncovered a secret they were not meant to find, when in fact they were intentionally led to it — while others are paid to turn on Trump.[1]

He extended the same analysis to commentators who describe Trump as "the lesser of two evils," characterizing them as either genuinely misled or paid shills, and advising readers to stop following them.[1] The strategic goal he attributed to this activity is expectation management: having conceded that Trump would win in the fall, these voices work to dampen hopes so that, by controlling the public's expectations, the Cabal can control the scope of change that actually occurs.[1] Carlson tied this to a broader claim that people are conditioned to be unable to imagine a world beyond the controlled and manipulated present, which is why so many are easily "black-pilled into hopelessness."[1]

## Control of American intelligence

In a December 2023 essay occasioned by the death of [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], Carlson argued that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] does not work for the American people but for the British Cabal families who control the United States, executing color revolutions, secret bombings, drug trafficking and human trafficking operations, and manufacturing wars and conflicts on the Cabal's behalf.[2] He cited Kissinger's 1995 knighthood by [[queen-elizabeth-ii|Queen Elizabeth II]] and the existence of an Israeli monument to CIA counterintelligence chief [[james-jesus-angleton|James Jesus Angleton]] as visible markers of that patronage, and claimed that the disinformation behind [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] originated in London.[2]

## Claimed origins of communism

Carlson set out three premises for understanding the Cabal's long game: that Karl Marx was a British puppet, that the [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolsheviks]] were British puppets, and that communism itself was created by the British Cabal — concluding that the contemporary communist takeover of America is being led by the British Deep State.[2] He named [[alfred-milner|Lord Alfred Milner]] as the "point man" between the Cabal and the Bolsheviks, organizing them and securing their funding before building channels to place British assets throughout the English-speaking world, an architecture Carlson links to the [[round-table-movement|Round Table]] and the modern [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] arrangement.[2]

Carlson characterized the Cabal as indifferent to race, nationality and religion in choosing assets — instancing the Klan-linked [[william-yandell-elliott|William Yandell Elliott]] mentoring the Jewish refugee Kissinger, who in turn mentored [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] — and as sincerely believing that stripping ordinary people of their God-given rights would produce a better world in which the population is controlled and culled.[2] He closed by arguing that the Cabal is losing its grip and that "the parasites of the world who feed off of humanity are being eradicated one by one."[2]

## The billionaire elite thesis (August 2023)

In "George Soros, A British Creation," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] applied the framework to the financial elite, arguing that [[george-soros|George Soros]] and the figures around him were created and controlled by a British Satanic Cabal rather than rising on merit.[3] He stressed repeatedly that the Cabal "is comprised of all people from all walks of life, races and religions," and that its members' allegiance is to their Satanic society rather than to race or religion — a point he made in anticipation of readers reading a Jewish conspiracy into the names involved.[3]

Recurring markers Carlson identified across the network include birth or wartime flight to England ([[james-goldsmith|Sir James Goldsmith]], [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]], Soros himself), service in the British Army, and the founding of hedge funds, private equity firms and wealth management houses in the early to mid 1990s — the period when the Soviet Union fell and China began its rise, which he reads as the Cabal transitioning from control of the Soviet Union to control of the Chinese Communist Party.[3] He also argued that the Cabal is the origin of the climate change hoax, with [[king-charles-iii|King Charles]] now its leading advocate, and that the British "pretty much create and perfect the mind control system we know today," speculating about [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]]-type activity in the British military.[3]

Carlson concluded that Soros and his associates manipulate financial markets, foment [[color-revolution|color revolutions]], control the media, possibly manipulate elections, and manufacture crises and epidemics culminating in mass migration, while blackmailing, censoring and working toward one-world government — and that whatever their nationality, they all have deep ties to England and specifically the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[3]
