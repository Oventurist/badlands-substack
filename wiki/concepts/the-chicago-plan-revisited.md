# The Chicago Plan Revisited

**The Chicago Plan Revisited** is a report published by the [[international-monetary-fund|International Monetary Fund]] examining alternatives to the prevailing banking model.[1] In the IMF's own words the report describes [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]] as "problematic," and it discusses the perceived benefits of [[full-reserve-banking|full reserve banking]].[1]

Badlands Media cites the report as evidence that criticism of fractional reserve banking is not confined to fringe commentary but appears in the literature of a major international financial institution.[1] The full reserve model the report revisits would require banks to lend out deposits themselves rather than create new money on the basis of those deposits.[1]
