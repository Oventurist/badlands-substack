# Brooklyn Synagogue Tunnel

The Brooklyn synagogue tunnel was a January 2024 news story concerning the discovery of an unauthorised tunnel beneath a synagogue in Brooklyn, New York, which briefly became a focus of online speculation about child trafficking.[1] [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] examined it in his [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Poisoning the Well" as a possible instance of the technique the essay describes.[1]

## The story

As Carlson summarised the widely circulated footage, Orthodox Jews appeared to riot while police pulled a mattress from the tunnel and attempted to seal it off.[1] The imagery and the timing prompted immediate association with the child-trafficking allegations then dominating discussion of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and the unsealed court documents.[1]

## Carlson's competing readings

Carlson declined to settle on an interpretation and laid out several possibilities in parallel. It could be coincidence, and it could be entirely legitimate — that is, the people involved might genuinely have been trafficking and abusing children.[1] But it could equally be an instance of [[poisoning-the-well|poisoning the well]]: if the tunnel turns out to have been built so that Orthodox Jews could reach the synagogue during Covid-era lockdowns rather than for trafficking, then the collapse of the trafficking claim in this instance would damage the credibility of the broader child-trafficking narrative and seed doubt about the legitimacy of the [[epstein-files|Epstein]] disclosures generally.[1]

He raised a further possibility running in the opposite direction: that the story functioned as an attempt to frame child trafficking as a specifically Jewish phenomenon rather than an elite [[deep-state|Cabal]] one, deflecting attention from non-Jewish traffickers.[1] Carlson observed that Epstein and [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] appeared increasingly connected to government intelligence services — naming [[mossad|Mossad]], the [[cia|CIA]] and [[mi6|MI6]], and possibly their respective governments more broadly — and asked whether Orthodox Jews were being "thrown under the bus" through the synagogue story in order to cover for elites.[1]

The incident was one of three stories Carlson said he was holding "in an open hand," alongside the [[miami-mall-alien-rumor|Miami mall alien rumor]] and the [[billionaire-bunker-narrative|billionaire bunker reports]], on the ground that in each case there were inconsistencies between who was providing the information and whom it appeared to injure.[1]
