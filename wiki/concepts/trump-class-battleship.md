# Trump-class Battleship

The **Trump-class battleship** is a United States Navy surface-combatant program announced in late 2025, whose implementation drew shock and criticism in Western specialized defense publications on the grounds that it responded to the [[us-navy|U.S. Navy]]'s real challenges with essentially the opposite of the decisions those challenges dictated.[1]

## Cost and criticism

By published estimates, each Trump-class ship would cost between $10 billion and $15 billion, comparable to the $12.8 billion price of the newest nuclear aircraft carrier, the Gerald R. Ford.[1] Critics argued that while powerful, the design was an insufficient solution: for $20–30 billion, two unique ships would be built with relatively narrow strike capabilities but with a maximum construction-cost coefficient driven by small series production, size and the concentration of new technical solutions.[1] Analysts contrasted the program with the alternative of building roughly five times as many destroyers.[1]

## Strategic implications

Defense Express argued the decision could only please [[china|China]], which already fields more ships than the U.S. Navy and was projected to hold a 1.5-fold advantage in pennants by 2030.[1] [[russia|Russia]] would likewise welcome the diversion of resources into vulnerable, costly small-series ships, since Moscow's approach to countering the U.S. Navy has always relied on a submarine fleet intended to strike large formations such as carrier strike groups — formations of which Trump-class battleships would form part.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the story within the [[multipolar-war|Multipolar War]], describing it as a war for the future of mankind rather than for nation states, one that has passed beneath the conscious notice of most people — including many who have spent a lifetime studying a geopolitical landscape he argues has been completely inverted since the close of the World Wars — and one he contends is being won.[1]
