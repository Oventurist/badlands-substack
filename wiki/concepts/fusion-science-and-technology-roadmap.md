# Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap

The Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap is a finalized national strategy released by the U.S. [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] to accelerate the development and commercialization of fusion energy.[1] The plan targets the deployment of fusion pilot plants and commercial fusion power in the mid-2030s through coordinated efforts spanning science, technology, infrastructure, workforce development, and commercialization.[1]

## Development

The roadmap was developed with input from more than 800 scientists and engineers drawn from over 15 private companies, 10 national laboratories, and 70 universities, making it one of the broadest consultative exercises in the history of U.S. fusion policy.[1]

## Structure

The document is organized around three core pillars: building critical infrastructure to close materials and technology gaps; driving innovation through advanced research, high-performance computing, and [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]]; and expanding the domestic fusion ecosystem through public-private partnerships, supply chain strengthening, workforce growth, and commercialization pathways.[1]

## Implementation

The roadmap is to be implemented through the Department of Energy's newly established [[office-of-fusion|Office of Fusion]] and aligns with the department's [[genesis-mission|Genesis Mission]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the roadmap's release as evidence that "the Golden Age is upon us," arguing that the long-teased next technological revolution is real and arriving, and that the release of such technologies would draw out the interests that had suppressed them and stifled "the creative power of mankind."[1] He framed the moment as a call for defiance against aspiring tyranny, singling out those who call for the suppression of speech, the desecration of the Bill of Rights, and the tempering of Christian teaching.[1]
