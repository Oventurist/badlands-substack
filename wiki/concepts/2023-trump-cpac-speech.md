# 2023 Trump CPAC Speech

The 2023 Trump CPAC speech was an address delivered by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Saturday, March 4, 2023, at the [[conservative-political-action-conference|Conservative Political Action Conference]], and was characterized by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] as "one of his most raw speeches to date" — a "quotable masterpiece" that marked the opening of a new stage in what the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] community regards as an ongoing information war.[1] Getz's essay treating the speech, "President Trump's Call to Arms," was published on March 7, 2023.[1]

## Attack on the Republican establishment

The passage Getz singled out as the speech's most consequential was Trump's repudiation of the pre-2016 Republican leadership: "We had a Republican Party that was ruled by Freaks, Neocons, Globalists, Open Border Zealots, and Fools... But we are never going back to the party of Paul Ryan, Karl Rove, and Jeb Bush."[1] Getz argued that the significance of the line lay less in its rhetoric than in its reception: in 2015 such a statement would have been met with "a wall of boos and confusion," whereas in 2023 it drew "the loud cheers of an awake audience."[1] He wrote that the "infiltrated version of the Republican party is dead" — its officeholders might retain their seats, but "their days are numbered" — and cited the political exposure of figures such as [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] as evidence that there was "no going back."[1]

Getz framed the moment autobiographically. A supporter of the [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] family for nearly two decades, he described Trump's 2016 primary dismantling of the establishment — and specifically the exposure of [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]], the candidate Getz had expected to win the nomination — as "jarring to behold" but ultimately the groundwork for his own awakening.[1]

## Repudiation of foreign wars

A second quoted passage addressed war spending: "We are never going back to a party that wants to give unlimited money to fight foreign wars, that are endless wars, that are stupid wars!"[1] Getz argued that on a world stage such a statement "means EVERYTHING," because war is "possibly the biggest operation currently set in place to wash and re-distribute American taxpayer money," and publicly declaring an intention to end that system signals to the [[deep-state|Deep State]] that its function is understood.[1] He linked the theme to the [[forever-wars|forever wars]] critique and to [[qanon|Q]] posts that had asked years earlier who audits war spending.[1]

## Continuity, retribution and the "final battle"

Trump told the audience, "We are going to finish what we started... we are going to see this battle through to ultimate Victory. We are going to make America great again," and claimed to be the only president "in history who's ever taken on the entire corrupt establishment in Washington," adding that a 2024 win would allow him to do it "even stronger, faster and better" because he now knew the personnel of the capital: "I now know the good ones, the bad ones, the weak ones, the strong ones—I know em all!"[1] Getz read these lines as confirmation that Trump's first term had functioned as reconnaissance, with the president having "spent four years studying them up close and personal, right in the belly of the beast."[1]

Other quoted passages included Trump's claim of financial independence from donors — "They cannot stand that they do not own me... they will never, ever control me and they will never ever, therefore control you" — and his exhortation, "I alone will never retreat and that is why we must stand together and we must charge... full speed ahead."[1] In the closing montage Getz reproduced, Trump declared "THIS IS THE FINAL BATTLE," warned that "if they win, we no longer have a Country," promised that "if you put me back in the White House, their reign is over," and issued the formulation that became the speech's most-quoted line: "In 2016, I declared, 'I am your voice.' Today, I add, 'I am your warrior, I am your justice, and for those who have been wronged and betrayed, I am your retribution.'"[1] Trump also pledged, "I will totally obliterate the Deep State."[1]

## Interpretation

Getz concluded that the speech marked "the beginning of the next stage in this war," with the players defined, the lines drawn, and Trump no longer using "code words" for what he had previously described euphemistically as "bad deals."[1] He tied the speech to what he saw as accelerating [[red-pilling|red-pilling]] "across the world, from Twitter to TikTok, at family gatherings, online, offline, and beyond," and framed the 2024 contest as "more than just another 4-year election" but "a Battle for the True United States of America" against a [[globalist-hegemon|globalist]] power structure.[1] The essay closed on the maxim "How America Goes, so goes the World."[1]
