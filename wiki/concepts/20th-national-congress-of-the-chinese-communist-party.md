# 20th National Congress of the Chinese Communist Party

The 20th National Congress of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], held in October 2022, is remembered in Badlands Media commentary chiefly for the televised removal of former leader [[hu-jintao|Hu Jintao]] from the congress hall.[1]

## The removal of Hu Jintao

Joe Lange's "The Master Builder" describes the incident as [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s predecessor being removed publicly in humiliating fashion, live on television for the whole world to see, and asks why the removal was carried out on Xi's orders.[1] The article answers by citing Foreign Policy's assessment that during Hu's tenure corruption rose — and, more dangerously for the party, so did public coverage of corruption, online freedom of speech, and, to a limited extent, civil society groups and NGOs.[1]

## Interpretation

The article treats the episode as the most visible public moment in a long-running [[anti-corruption-campaign-in-china|anti-corruption campaign]], and links the growth of NGOs under Hu to [[george-soros|George Soros]], whose networks the piece suggests were entangled with officials Xi has since purged.[1]

## Aftermath as read by Badlands Media (2022)

In "The Two Chinas" (November 10, 2022), Badlands Media described the Congress and its fallout as solidifying [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s position as leader "not just of the China of today, but that of tomorrow," and as the venue at which his crystalizing and unifying vision of China emerged.[2] The article stressed that [[hu-jintao|Hu Jintao]] was led away from the chamber in very public and very humiliating fashion, presenting the episode as a break with the Hu-era accommodation of Western oligarchic interests.[2] It further noted that legacy outlets from [[the-hill|The Hill]] to the [[bbc|BBC]] and [[associated-press|AP News]] covered Xi's unprecedented third term with breathless alarm, and that [[george-soros|George Soros]] had criticized Xi in the months beforehand.[2]
