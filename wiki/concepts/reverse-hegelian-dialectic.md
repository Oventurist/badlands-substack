# Reverse Hegelian Dialectic

The **Reverse Hegelian Dialectic** is a term used by the Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to describe a countermeasure to the classic problem–reaction–solution pattern associated with the [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian dialectic]].[1] In the standard formulation, controlling interests are said to manufacture a crisis, cultivate a public reaction, and then supply a pre-planned solution that advances their agenda. In the reversed version, dissident actors allow those same engineered narratives to run their course so that they ultimately undermine the ends and the paradigm of the parties that seeded them.[1]

## Use in the China–Taiwan analysis

Writing in October 2022, the author argued that patriots in the west, together with "enigmatic allied sovereigns in the east," had engineered such a reversal on the controllers with respect to the [[china|China]]–[[taiwan|Taiwan]] confrontation.[1] The claimed evidence was that the establishment "doesn't know what to do with the China-Taiwan narrative," because the American public simultaneously does not want war — which it believes would follow if the west appears weak to leaders such as [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] — and does not want to be seen as losing should a conflict go kinetic.[1] The author characterised this bind as a "Hegelian death loop" and a "Chinese Finger Trap" for the establishment.[1]

A parallel example offered was Ukraine, where nearly a year of what the author called exhaustive and contradictory propaganda was said to have brought the country's standing as the "decades-long piggybank of the Western elite" into sharper focus rather than concealing it, alongside continuing coverage of Nazi-aligned [[azov-battalion|Azov]] forces presented as defenders of global democracy.[1]
