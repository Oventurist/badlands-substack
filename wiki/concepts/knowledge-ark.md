# Knowledge Ark

The **knowledge ark** — punningly rendered "KNOW-ah's Ark" — is a metaphor introduced in the Badlands Media essay "The Extraordinary Power of Digital Soldiers" (July 31, 2023) for the body of accurate information assembled and distributed online by [[anons]] in advance of [[the-storm|the Storm]].[1]

## The pun and its reasoning

The essay notes that Noah, like present-day researchers, received forewarning of an impending storm and flood.[1] Phonetically *Noah* is held to be close to *knower*; the author adds that in ancient Greek *gno* refers to knowing, so that to possess knowledge is to possess "gno-ledge."[1] Knowledge in turn implies discernment, which intrinsically involves the ability to say *no* to untrue statements — on this reading nothing can be known until the word "no" enters one's vocabulary.[1] Those who know what the dark occultists are doing are accordingly described as the people most vociferously saying "NO" to propaganda and mind control.[1]

## The ark as a collective project

Putting heads together to arrive at an accurate picture of reality is therefore likened to building an ark: by distributing true knowledge across the Internet, participants help the masses refuse propaganda.[1] The essay concedes that not everyone will listen and not every life can be saved from what it calls the oftentimes deadly effects of mainstream propaganda, but maintains that the ark is being built regardless and that millions of lives may already have been saved.[1]

In the conclusion the reader is addressed as part of the ark's construction crew: the bigger it is built, the more lives can be saved during the storm and the events leading to it, and every such effort is also said to strengthen the "field effect" produced when minds are coherently tuned in the right direction.[1] See also [[counter-spell|counter-spell]] and [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]].[1]
