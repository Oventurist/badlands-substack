# Erosion of the Postwar International Order

In January 2026 a cluster of European statements, US withdrawals from international bodies, and the American operation against Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] converged into an open debate over whether the postwar "rules-based international order" was collapsing.[1]

## European warnings

German President [[frank-walter-steinmeier|Frank-Walter Steinmeier]] said US policy under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was helping erode the global order, pointing to the Maduro episode among destabilizing developments and warning against turning global politics into "a den of robbers."[1] French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] lamented that the United States was turning away from its allies.[1] EU foreign policy chief [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]] said the bloc had discussed how it might respond if Washington advanced efforts to acquire [[greenland|Greenland]], calling US messaging "extremely concerning."[1] An [[european-union|EU Commission]] spokesperson reportedly said the bloc does not recognize the legitimacy of Venezuela's interim president [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] but would maintain "targeted engagement" with Venezuelan authorities.[1]

Slovakian President [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] stated directly that the Maduro operation represented the end of the post-World War II international order.[1]

## Ukraine and the Kadyrov proposal

Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] said the United States should intensify pressure on Russia and suggested Washington could pursue "some sort of operation" to remove Chechen leader [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]], explicitly invoking the Maduro precedent as a way to raise Moscow's costs and push President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] to reconsider the war.[1] Kadyrov responded by calling Zelensky a coward for refusing to attempt it himself.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that the chaos generated by Trump's actions was dismantling the "international rules-based order" — what is often called international law — and endorsed Putin's characterization of that system as malicious and deceptive.[1] On this reading, international law is an abstract idea because without a true means of enforcement there is no codified law; the Great Reset was supposed to supply that enforcement, but the world is instead moving away from consolidation of power and toward the decentralization of authority.[1] Commentary attributed to Kokomo Rodriguez described international law as flagrant, barbaric, and a "law of the jungle" in which might makes right — a description Badlands accepted as accurate about US behavior while arguing those days were ending.[1]

Badlands suggested that the prospect of the EU confronting the United States as an adversary over Greenland could lead to a diplomatic divorce between the two continents, which would mean the official end of the ambitions set forth in Agenda 2030.[1]

## US withdrawal from international bodies

Beyond rhetoric, Trump withdrew the United States from 66 international organizations, half of them [[united-nations|United Nations]] bodies, including the climate change platform and other forums.[1] UN Secretary-General's spokesman Stephane Dujarric in turn threatened to revoke the United States' right to vote over unpaid dues.[1]
