# 2024 Human Bird Flu Cases in the US and Australia

In May 2024, shares of vaccine companies surged after two reports of human bird flu infections, one in the United States and one in Australia.[1] The federal government was said to be closely monitoring the rising risk of human-to-human transmission, which had not yet been observed.[1]

## The US case

On Wednesday, May 22, 2024, the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services reported that a farmworker with high exposure to infected livestock had developed mild symptoms and recovered.[1] Michigan's chief medical executive, Dr. Natasha Bagdasarian, said in a news release that "the current health risk to the general public remains low" and added, "We have not seen signs of sustained human-to-human transmission at this point. This is exactly how public health is meant to work, in early detection and monitoring of new and emerging illnesses."[1]

## The Australian case

The second case was reported in Australia's southeastern state of Victoria and was the first human case of bird flu recorded in that country.[1]

## Market reaction

The reports drove a rally in vaccine stocks, a juxtaposition highlighted by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] in the headline "Vaccine Stocks Erupt After Bird Flu Cases Reported In US & Australia" and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
