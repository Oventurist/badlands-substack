# 2025 Red Sea Submarine Cable Cuts

In early September 2025, multiple international submarine communications cables in the Red Sea were severed, degrading internet routing between Europe, the Middle East and Asia.[1]

## Impact on Azure

[[microsoft|Microsoft Corp.]] said on Saturday, September 6, 2025, that clients of its Azure cloud platform might experience increased latency as a result of the cuts.[1] The company stated that traffic traversing the Middle East that originates or terminates in Asia or Europe was affected, and that its engineering teams were working to mitigate the situation.[1] Microsoft's status notice did not say how the cables had been severed.[1] "Undersea fiber cuts can take time to repair, as such we will continuously monitor, rebalance, and optimize routing to reduce customer impact in the meantime," the company said.[1]

## Strategic context

The Red Sea is a critical telecommunications corridor connecting Europe to Africa and Asia by way of Egypt.[1] Repairs in the area were expected to be difficult because Yemen's [[houthis|Houthis]] continued to attack vessels transiting the waterway.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized the incident as an "invisible, unprovable attack," suggesting that a deliberate "switch flipped" was the more likely explanation than accident.[1]
