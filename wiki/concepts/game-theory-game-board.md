# Game Theory Game Board

The **Game Theory Game Board** is the recurring metaphor used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] for the arena on which the conflict between the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] and the Globalist [[deep-state|Deep State]] is contested.[1] It frames political, administrative, media and kinetic engagements as moves in a single, very large game rather than as unrelated news events.[1]

## Rules of the board

Under the [[game-theory|Game Theory]] logic Burning Bright applies, a player does not need to win every political, administrative, rhetorical or even kinetic engagement in order to win the war; the objective is to control more of the board than the adversary.[1] He concedes that it is virtually impossible for observers to know how expansive the board actually is, while asserting that it is vast and encompasses virtually every political, military and media faction in the world.[1]

Because the board's extent is unknowable, he proposes a proxy measure of who is winning the [[information-war|Information War]]: observe which sides, factions and public players appear to be reacting to [[narrative-deployment|Narrative Deployments]] and which appear to be seeding them.[1]

## Actual and Potential moves

A distinguishing feature of the board in Burning Bright's usage is that moves need not be material. Potential Events — those previously relegated to the hypothetical, rhetorical or "media" realms — can provoke Actual Events on the board and retain real power of their own by shaping the directional thinking and therefore the directional momentum of the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]].[1]

He holds that patriots operate both in and behind the public levers of political, administrative and even military power, attempting to hem in the Deep State globalist collective using the tenets of Game Theory, while anons, analysts, citizen journalists and readers act as "active minds in the Mind War."[1] He also cautions that every participant, including the sovereign-minded community itself, remains susceptible to emotional manipulation by all sides on the board.[1]

## Reading the board in the War of Stories (December 2022)

In "The War of Stories" (December 30, 2022), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that much of the national and international news cycle — from [[potential-events|Potential and Narrative Events]] through to Actual Events — is obfuscated by all sides on the Game Theory Game Board.[2] Recontextualizing outsized public personalities as vehicles for paradigm-shifting narratives, he argued, renders the board "more clearly than before."[2] He predicted that readers who assess figures by the net effects of their deployments would come away sensing that "the winds of change are only blowing in one direction," and that the decentralized patriots of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] were taking more of the board with each of their own [[narrative-deployment|deployments]] and with each of the enemy's.[2]
