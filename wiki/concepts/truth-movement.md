# Truth Movement

The Truth Movement is the term used in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writing for the loose community of independent researchers and citizen journalists who consider themselves "awake" to what they describe as the fake reality constructed by legacy media and government narrative managers.[1] In "Horseshoe Theory - Part 1," [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]] characterized its members as engaging with topical information and news cycles using logic and reason as the foundation of understanding, with objective discernment serving as the vehicle for navigating unfolding narratives.[1]

[[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] has labelled participants "heterodox thinkers," a description the author called "a beautiful moniker."[1] Within this framework, Research and Reasoning are the two defining actions of the movement and the core functions of what the author called the Logical Mind, while proficiency in both has become the measure of a "Psy-soldier" in the Information War.[1] Other valued skills named in the article include meme-smithing, reporting, articulating, and ridicule.[1]

## Self-conception and limits

The article treats the movement's logical orientation as genuine but incomplete.[1] "If Logic is our weapon of choice, then Discernment is our body armor," the author wrote, framing the struggle as a war of attrition and a finite game in the sense described by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]].[1] He acknowledged that even the most battle-tested members are deceived at deeper levels than they realize, and that they too are sometimes triggered into emotional outbursts of the sort they ridicule in their political opponents.[1]

He also argued that the movement possesses "the greatest mental fortitude of any group I have encountered on the political battlefield," which is why he assigned to it the burden of first reforming its own perception of the world before attempting to win over politically opposed countrymen — a project developed under the heading of [[horseshoe-theory|Horseshoe Theory]].[1]
