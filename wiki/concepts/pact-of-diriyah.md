# Pact of Diriyah

The Pact of Diriyah was the 1744 alliance between [[mohammed-bin-saud|Mohammed bin Saud]], emir of the farming settlement of Diriyah, and the preacher [[muhammad-ibn-abd-al-wahhab|Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab]], confirmed by a bay'ah (mutual oath of loyalty).[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) It fused political and military authority with religious reform and founded what became the [[emirate-of-diriyah|First State of Saud]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Terms

Bin Saud proposed two conditions: that al-Wahhab settle in Diriyah and counsel him as an advisor, and that al-Wahhab not oppose the collection of taxation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Al-Wahhab accepted the first but, given his recent expulsion from Uyayna, rejected the second, arguing that taxation would create natural animus between ruler and people and that greater wealth would come from the conquest of enemies than from bleeding one's own subjects.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Bin Saud agreed; the article notes that to this day no tax is imposed or collected by the Saudi government.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## The oaths

Bin Saud declared: "This oasis is yours, do not fear your enemies. By the name of God, if all Nejd was summoned to throw you out, we will never agree to expel you."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Al-Wahhab replied: "You are the settlement's chief and wise man. I want you to grant me an oath that you will perform jihad against the unbelievers. In return, you will be imam, leader of the Muslim community and I will be leader in religious matters."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

The two men — described as champions of reason and debate — embraced as brothers and cemented the alliance by wedding bin Saud's son to al-Wahhab's daughter, a dynastic practice traditionally embraced by cultures worldwide.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Together they set out to restore their concept of morality and honor to Arabia, establishing the enduring church–state architecture of the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]] and [[wahhabism|Wahhabism]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
