# Executive Order 13959

Executive Order 13959, "Addressing the Threat From Securities Investments That Finance Communist Chinese Military Companies," was signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on November 12, 2020. The order targeted U.S. investment in Chinese companies identified as linked to military operations and national-security threats.[1]

## Purpose and effect

The order was designed to disrupt the flow of American capital supporting the military expansion of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], and in doing so to expose the ties between that expansion and American business interests.[1] Badlands Media commentary specifically names [[rosemont-seneca|Rosemont Seneca]], the investment firm associated with [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], among the entities whose exposure the order is said to have facilitated.[1]

By limiting investment channels and increasing regulatory scrutiny of cross-border securities holdings, the order is described as laying groundwork for broader financial disentanglement from adversarial foreign powers.[1]

## Role in the Continuity of Government framing

In the December 2024 feature "Biden, Inc. Flips," EO 13959 is presented as one of several executive orders forming the legal infrastructure of an ongoing [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] operation.[1] Within that framework it is assigned the specific function of targeting adversarial foreign influence — severing the financial ties that the author argues enable China's global ambitions — alongside [[executive-order-13818|EO 13818]] and [[executive-order-13848|EO 13848]], which are cast as instruments for exposing financial corruption, and [[executive-order-13919|EO 13919]], cast as the basis for military preparedness.[1] The article treats the survival of these orders across administrations as evidence of a coordinated [[white-hats|White Hat]] strategy operating quietly within legal authority.[1]

## Assessment

EO 13959 is a genuine, publicly issued executive order with documented effects on index inclusion and institutional investment in listed Chinese firms. The interpretive claims layered onto it in this literature — that it functions as part of a covert continuity-of-government program or was aimed at Hunter Biden's business vehicles — are not supported by the order's text or implementation record.[1]
