# The Grand Chessboard

*The Grand Chessboard* is a 1998 book by [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] that, in the reading advanced by Badlands Media commentary, lays out a blueprint for the future of the Middle East and Eurasia as understood by the American foreign policy establishment.[1]

## Contents as framed in Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that much of the alleged NeoCon and globalist strategy for the region can be traced to the book: building Iran into a legitimate military power — a process said to have occurred under the George W. Bush administration and to have been greatly expanded under Obama — and then precipitating a large war between the two main factions of Islam so that they would destroy each other.[1] The commentary compared this to the sectarian wars imposed on Christians in Europe over previous centuries.[1]

The mechanics described are: get the Sunni Arab states to rally around Israel; escalate Israeli–Iranian tensions into kinetic conflict; and draw the United States and NATO in on the Israeli and Sunni side, which would in turn pull Russia and its allies in on Iran's side — described in the piece as "the recipe for World War 3."[1]

## The triumvirate warning

A point emphasized in the commentary is that Brzezinski explicitly identified a triumvirate alliance between Iran, Russia, and China as the one development capable of defeating the plan, and stated that such an alignment must be prevented at all costs.[1]

## Omissions and the MBS factor

The Badlands reading notes that neither [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] nor his father appears in Brzezinski's book, and that Saudi Arabia scarcely features in its hypothetical future at all.[1] MBS's unanticipated rise to Crown Prince is therefore presented as an unmodelled variable that upended the design: he made peace with Iran after decades of conflict, brought Syria back into the Arab League, and rekindled relations between Turkey and the Gulf states.[1] Syria matters in this account because it is where the politics become actual warfare, and Turkey matters as the key to subverting both NATO and [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]].[1]

Related commentary held that the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] were viewed by the Deep State as an instrument for uniting the Sunni world against the Shia world along the Persian Gulf axis, and that the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] had been prognosticating precisely such a Sunni–Shia war.[1]
