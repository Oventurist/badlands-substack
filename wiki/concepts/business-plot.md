# Business Plot

The Business Plot refers to the 1934 coup d'état plans against President [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]] laid by the pro-fascist Wall Street establishment and thwarted by General [[smedley-butler|Smedley Butler]].[1]

Matthew Ehret situates the plot within a longer campaign that began the day the anti-fascist Roosevelt was elected in 1932.[1] An attempted assassination of Roosevelt in February 1933 had already failed; the coup plans of the following year were exposed by Butler, whom Ehret describes as a patriotic general.[1] With both the violent and the electoral routes closed, Wall Street's remaining strategy ran through organizations such as the [[american-liberty-league|American Liberty League]], which sought to keep America out of the coming war until Pearl Harbor made that position untenable.[1]
