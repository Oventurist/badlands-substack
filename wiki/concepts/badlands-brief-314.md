## Trump calls on Republicans to nationalize elections

The first segment reports that the administration of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] has launched multiple probes into the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]]. Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] is described as overseeing an intelligence community review through the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] examining potential voting machine vulnerabilities and foreign interference, running parallel to a [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] criminal investigation.

According to the source, Gabbard personally participated in an [[fbi|FBI]] raid on a Fulton County, Georgia election facility, where agents seized nearly 700 boxes containing 2020 physical ballots, tabulator tapes, ballot images and voter rolls, citing alleged violations related to record preservation and false ballots — an operation covered separately as the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County election raid]]. President Trump is said to have congratulated the agents by phone during the operation.

Concurrently, the Brief reports, Trump has publicly called on Republicans to "take over" and "nationalize" election administration in at least 15 states, arguing that this is needed to prevent undocumented immigrants from voting and to address what he describes as inaccurate vote counting. He has endorsed two bills: the [[save-america-act|Save America Act]], which would mandate proof of citizenship for voter registration and impose restrictions on mail-in voting processes, and the [[mega-act|Make Elections Great Again Act]], which would prohibit ranked-choice voting and universal mail ballots and establish national election audits.

Her verdict: "It's all very silly. We need better psyops."

She continues that the "hunt is heating up," arguing that the people who stole elections and weaponized government against political opponents now claim victim status because the public is learning what they did. She reports first-hand that Colorado clerks received "a talking-to" from [[matt-crane|Matt Crane]], whom she describes as their handler, after she broke the news — while the meeting was still in progress — that Ryan Macias was present at a Colorado clerks' meeting. Her stated reason for breaking the story is that Macias was pitching Colorado clerks to fund a new NGO at the same moment he was being implicated in alleged willful election fraud in Georgia, where recount totals reportedly failed to match until they were sent to Macias, who was then with The Elections Group. No specifics were released on the amounts in the accounts or their current status.

The two banks had reached a global settlement in 1999 resolving all Nazi-era claims, including future ones, with both issuing apologies. UBS Americas President Robert Karofsky stated that the company accepts and deeply regrets the dark history of Swiss banking during the war. The probe is expected to conclude by early summer 2026, with a final report anticipated by the end of the year.

## Trade realignment and the New START expiry

The third segment reports that Trump announced a U.S.–India trade agreement lowering tariffs on Indian imports from 25% to 18%, contingent on [[india|India]] ceasing purchases of Russian crude oil and increasing purchases of U.S. energy and goods. The Indian government has remained silent with no official confirmation or ratification, and Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] stated that Moscow has received no indication from New Delhi of any oil cutoff, emphasizing [[russia|Russia]]'s intent to maintain its strategic partnership with India despite U.S. pressure.

Separately, U.S. trade partners are said to be accelerating alternative agreements to mitigate the impact of tariffs and threats, including an India–EU free trade deal, an [[european-union|EU]]–Mercosur bloc covering over 700 million people, and other diversification efforts. Additional U.S. actions include threats of 100% tariffs on [[canada|Canada]] over electric vehicle policy alignment with [[china|China]] and increased tariffs on South Korea, contributing to reported declines in the dollar's value and shifts toward gold holdings by central banks.

On arms control, the [[new-start-treaty|New START treaty]] — the last remaining major U.S.–Russia agreement, limiting deployed strategic nuclear warheads to 1,550 and delivery systems to 800 per side — is set to expire imminently without renewal. The administration has not responded to Russia's September offer of a one-year extension without inspections, with Trump indicating he prefers a new deal including China and downplaying the risks: "If it expires, it expires. We'll just do a better agreement." Russia has cited demands for the involvement of Britain and France, modernization of uncovered systems, and prior suspension of U.S. inspections amid Ukraine tensions as complicating factors. Experts warn of arsenal expansions, a broader arms race involving China, loss of verification mechanisms and heightened instability.

Burning Bright argues that Trump is helping partners such as India and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] establish "Regions of Responsibility" on economic and kinetic fronts, and that the pattern is repeating among what he calls the would-be Globalists in the Collectorate. As America reasserts economic sovereignty, he writes, trade partners scramble "not into isolation, but into deepened deals among themselves," even adopting the term "shielding" to describe protectionism intended to protect them from Trump's protectionism — "which is exactly what he wants them to do." Trump's escalatory framing, in this reading, grants partners domestic political cover to go their own way while positioning them as stronger trade partners, "with Trump often rushing in to fill a void of his own making."

He extends the shielding logic to the nuclear theater, describing the biggest Region of Responsibility as the one set up by the Tripolar Alliance — the trinity of the United States, Russia and China under Trump, [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi]]. As treaties lapse, modernization programs accelerate on all sides, and the [[media-protectorate|Media Protectorate]] — "a significantly-lagging translation layer" — is only now producing headlines warning of a new arms race. He identifies a contradiction: globalists once thrived on the prospect of endless war because it justified their borderless control apparatus, proxy networks and parasitic financial flows, yet now they recoil. His explanation is that the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] is real, meaning no terminal kinetic escalation is coming, and that the "New Arms Race" is entirely narrative — escalatory framing deployed to grant shielding to all three poles for accelerated technological innovation, defense investment and sovereign pivots.
