# Logos

*Logos* is the classical term for the divine ordering principle of reality, invoked repeatedly by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Hijacked Consciousness - The Spell" (July 29, 2025) as the faculty that modern schooling is said to have destroyed.[1]

## In the essay's argument

The article contends that logos was "once understood as divine ordering principle" before being "compressed into grammar rules and sentence structure" by an education system that treated language as machinery for extracting information rather than for evoking presence.[1] Syllables were drilled and rhymes industrialized; the child learned "not to speak truth, but to repeat content," and not to ask what a word felt like but whether it was right or wrong on a test.[1] The author frames this as being "de-tuned" rather than educated.[1] See [[sacred-language|sacred language]].

The essay also invokes the Greek pairing of logos with *mythos*, arguing that the Greeks did not separate the two and understood that "the mind must dance with meaning, not dissect it to death" — logic, in that formulation, "walked with the muses."[1] The modern inversion, in which reason was crowned king over story, symbol, sensation, rhythm, dream and intuition, is treated as the core of the [[tyranny-of-logic|tyranny of logic]].[1]

## Persistence

Observing Consciousness insists that "the logos never dies, it waits," hiding in whispers, dreams and children's unschooled poems, and that its recovery feels like "remembering something you never learned - yet you always knew."[1] The restoration of that faculty is presented as the point at which the control spell begins to crack.[1]
