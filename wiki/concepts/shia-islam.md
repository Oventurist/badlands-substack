# Shia Islam

Shia Islam is the minority branch of Islam, comprising roughly 10–15% of all Muslims and found primarily in modern [[iran|Iran]] and throughout the region of Persia.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Doctrine of succession

The Shia position originated in the disputed interpretation of the word Mawla — variously 'Lord', 'trustee', and 'helper' — which the Prophet [[muhammad|Muhammad]] once applied to [[ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Upon Muhammad's death, some followers concluded that Ali, who had been like a son and brother to him, was his chosen heir, and that therefore only Ali's descendants qualify to lead Islam.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The [[sunni-islam|Sunni]] majority rejects this, holding that no heir was appointed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

Shia Muslims also take a narrow reading of "Ahl al-Bayt" (People of the House of the Prophet), limiting the group to Muhammad's cousin, daughter, son-in-law, two grandsons, and eleven wives, whereas Sunnis extend it to all of the Banu Hashim.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Geography

Iran — ancient Persia — is considered the homeland of the Shia, while Afghanistan and Iraq are mixtures of both branches, a condition the article characterizes as arbitrary and intentionally created by the British Crown and East India Company at the end of the First World War.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
