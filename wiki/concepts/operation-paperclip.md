# Operation Paperclip

Operation Paperclip was the post–World War II United States program under which German scientists, including former Nazis, were brought to America to work on military and aerospace projects.[1]

Its most prominent alumnus was rocket engineer [[wernher-von-braun|Wernher von Braun]], described in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as "one of the more prolific Operation Paperclip Nazi's."[1] Von Braun is cited in that commentary as having claimed that a global false flag operation involving projections of extraterrestrial craft in the sky — alongside projections of gods, mystics and other spiritual figures — had been planned as far back as the 1960s, if not earlier.[1] That claim is a foundational reference point for the [[alien-disclosure-op|alien disclosure operation]] thesis advanced in discussion of the 2023 [[david-grusch|David Grusch]] whistleblower testimony.[1]

## Part of the postwar fascist resurgence

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] places Project Paperclip within a wider postwar pattern in which many leading fascists in Germany, France, the Netherlands, Italy and Japan were quietly and quickly put to work overseeing the creation of [[nato|NATO]]'s secret armies in the form of [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]], the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg Group]] of globalists, Project Paperclip, and the US bioweapons complex now spread across the world.[2] In his account the European banking houses representing the old nobility of empire passed through this reconquest of the West without punishment.[2]
