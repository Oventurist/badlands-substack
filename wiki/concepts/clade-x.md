# Clade X

**Clade X** was a pandemic tabletop exercise held on May 15, 2018.[1] Together with [[event-201|Event 201]] of October 18, 2019, it is cited in "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" as one of the tabletop and gain-of-function exercises focused on pandemic response — covering masks, social distancing, vaccination, lockdowns and economic effects.[1]

The article's argument is that these exercises rehearsed in advance the specific measures later imposed during COVID, and that [[donald-trump|Trump]] therefore likely knew ahead of time not only that something would happen to enable what the author calls the hidden enemy to steal the 2020 election, but the particulars of how it would unfold.[1]
