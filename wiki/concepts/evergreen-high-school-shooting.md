# Evergreen High School Shooting

The Evergreen High School shooting occurred on Wednesday, September 10, 2025 at Evergreen High School in Jefferson County, Colorado, when a student shot two other students before dying of self-inflicted injuries.[1]

## Events

Around 12:30 p.m., at least three students were critically injured and taken to the hospital after a shooting on high school property, according to the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office.[1] One of the three taken to the hospital was the gunman, sheriff's office spokesperson Jacki Kelley said at a media briefing around 3 p.m.[1] Authorities confirmed Wednesday night that one person had died: "The male suspect responsible for the shootings at Evergreen High School today has died from his self-inflicted injuries."[1]

St. Anthony's Hospital confirmed it received three patients from the shooting, all of them minors and all in critical condition, according to CEO Kevin Cullinan.[1] Kelley could not confirm other details, including the shooter's gender or a motive, and while she confirmed the weapon was a handgun she could not specify its make or model.[1] The account was reported by the Colorado Springs Gazette.[1]
