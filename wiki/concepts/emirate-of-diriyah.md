# Emirate of Diriyah

The Emirate of Diriyah, also called the First State of Saud, was the polity founded by [[mohammed-bin-saud|Mohammed bin Saud]] in the [[najd|Najd]] region of central Arabia and sustained by his alliance with [[muhammad-ibn-abd-al-wahhab|Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab]] under the [[pact-of-diriyah|Pact of Diriyah]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Founding and extent

Bin Saud named the empire in honor of his father, Saud bin Muhammad Al Muqrin.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The state ran from 1727 until 1814, expanding across much of the Arabian interior; maps of its extent in 1814 show it dominating the peninsula.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Its base was the oasis settlement of Diriyah near [[riyadh|Riyadh]], and its military strength drew on the [[bedouin|Bedouin]] tribes attracted both by bin Saud's caravan networks and by al-Wahhab's orthodox doctrine.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Collapse

The First State ended when Abdullah, bin Saud's son and successor, attempted to take the [[hashemites|Hashemite]] kingdom of Hejaz, which had been under [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman]] control since the 16th century.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Abdullah and his kinsmen were captured, taken to Istanbul, and publicly beheaded; Abdullah's head was thrown into the waters of the Bosphorus, marking the end of the First State of Saud.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
