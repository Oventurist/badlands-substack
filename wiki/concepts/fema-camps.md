# FEMA Camps

**FEMA camps** is the term used in dissident and alternative-media circles for mass internment facilities allegedly prepared by the United States federal government for the detention of civilians. In [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]], the concept is central to the claim that [[operation-warp-speed|Operation Warp Speed]] averted a planned atrocity.[1]

## The claim

According to the account advanced by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], drawing on a video by the anon and painter [[kag-drogo|KAG Drogo]], the cabal's original COVID-era plan was to lock the population down for years on end and to send dissidents and "anti-vaxxers" to FEMA camps, presumably to be forcibly injected and/or put to death.[1] The essay asserts that Operation Warp Speed blocked this plan by collapsing the timeline on which extended lockdowns depended.[1]

The essay treats the aversion of this outcome as an event that turned "for the better against mind-bogglingly steep odds," and therefore as reasonable evidence of Divine intervention and of a change of timelines.[1] It further notes that while many blame [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for vaccine mandates, Trump never mandated that anyone take anything.[1]

The claim is a contested one and is presented in the source as interpretation rather than as documented fact.
