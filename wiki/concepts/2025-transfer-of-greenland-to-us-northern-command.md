# 2025 Transfer of Greenland to US Northern Command

The 2025 transfer of Greenland to U.S. Northern Command was an order by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] moving [[greenland|Greenland]] out of the area of responsibility of [[united-states-european-command|U.S. European Command]] and into that of [[united-states-northern-command|U.S. Northern Command]], the combatant command tasked with defending the homeland.[1]

## Announcement

The announcement, which had been expected, came on Tuesday, June 17, 2025, from chief [[pentagon|Pentagon]] spokesman [[sean-parnell|Sean Parnell]], who said the change "will strengthen the Joint Force's ability to defend the U.S. homeland, contributing to a more robust defense of the western hemisphere and deepening relationships with Arctic allies and partners."[1]

## Strategic context

The Trump administration has repeatedly highlighted the strategic significance of the island, one of the few territories — along with the United States — with direct access to the Arctic.[1] While attempting to strengthen U.S.–Greenlandic ties, the president declined to rule out using military force or coercion, a threat that rankled local leaders.[1] The Badlands News Brief framed the reassignment as a "status upgrade" for Greenland within U.S. defense planning.[1]
