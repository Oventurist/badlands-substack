# Responsibility to Protect

**Responsibility to Protect (R2P)** is the post-nation state security doctrine described in Badlands Media commentary as launched in 1999 by Soros-affiliated operatives including [[mark-malloch-brown|Lord Mark Malloch Brown]], [[strobe-talbott|Strobe Talbott]] and [[tony-blair|Tony Blair]].[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents R2P as the demand of the modern Anglo-American elite that took control of the United States over the dead bodies of [[warren-harding|Warren Harding]], [[franklin-d-roosevelt|FDR]] and John F. Kennedy.[1] Malloch Brown integrated the doctrine into the [[united-nations|United Nations]] while serving as Undersecretary General, and has spent recent years calling for the dissolution of the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] in order to remove "authoritarian nations" such as Russia and China from any role in global war-making decisions.[1]

The article contrasts R2P with the UN Charter's Article 1 and Article 2 principles of sovereign equality and non-intervention, and argues that the Security Council veto has blocked countless attempts to destroy Syria and saved millions of lives.[1]
