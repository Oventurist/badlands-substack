# TrumpaMania

TrumpaMania is a Badlands analytical frame holding that the theatrical, professional-wrestling quality of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public conduct is deliberate — a cartoonish soap opera functioning as narrative rope-a-dope against the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]

## Application to the Venezuela episode

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked TrumpaMania as the only explanation he could offer that addresses the contradictions and plot twists surrounding the capture of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and the subsequent US posture toward [[colombia|Colombia]].[1] Among the anomalies cited: Trump dismissed the idea of installing regime favorite [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] and instead backed Vice President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] on the grounds that she was "picked by Maduro," an odd deference to the preferences of a man he had just had arrested.[1] Trump also referred to Maduro as "President Maduro" on Truth Social exactly once — in the post announcing the arrest — despite years of posts about him.[1]

The frame extends to the theatrical exchanges with Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], whom Trump told to "watch his ass" and who responded by calling Trump a coward and saying "Come and get me," in a moment described as feeling like a professional wrestling soap opera.[1] Trump's characterization of Maduro as a "violent guy" followed immediately by an accusation that Maduro stole his dance moves was read as further evidence that the goofiness was engineered rather than accidental.[1]

## Origins of the term (2024)

The label was already in circulation during the 2024 campaign, deriving from [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]]'s shirt-tearing appearance at the [[2024-republican-national-convention|Republican National Convention]].[2] [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], hosting [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s War Room on Tuesday, September 3, 2024 while Bannon served a four-month prison sentence, wore a "Trumpamania" shirt and said explicitly that he was wearing it in honour of Hogan's RNC speech.[2] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated this as vindication of a thesis he had been advancing since the RNC, joking that Patel was "a closet Badlander."[2]

In this earlier formulation, Trumpamania 2024 cast global politics as a WWE pay-per-view event with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] acting as executive producer, "calling the shots and making the movie magic happen," populated by a cast of wrestling-style characters including "The Rocket Man," "The Gentleman," "The Red Dragon," "The Fallen Idol" ([[kanye-west|Kanye West]]), "Hollywood Hulk Hogan," "The Enigmas," "Vladimir the Great" ([[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]) and "The Dictator" ([[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]).[2] The author noted that horses were becoming a recurring theme and pointed to the pageantry of Putin's visit to [[mongolia|Mongolia]] that week as evidence that "[[genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]] is coming to Trumpamania 2024."[2] Regarding Kanye West, the piece claimed the video of his pro-Trump rant during a live SNL recording had been effectively wiped from the internet and that he had been subjected to MKUltra programming after being taken to a mental hospital.[2]

## Coinage at the 2024 Republican National Convention

The term was popularized on the final night of the [[2024-republican-national-convention|2024 Republican National Convention]], when [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]] used "Trumpmania" to describe the aura surrounding [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and "Trumpmaniacs" to describe the MAGA faithful, after stepping briefly out of character as Terry Bollea to describe his long friendship with Trump.[3] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] asked whether Americans were "about to witness Trumpmania 2024" — a battle royal elimination tournament to determine who would face Trump — drawing an analogy to WrestleMania IV in 1988 at Trump Plaza, the first [[world-wrestling-entertainment|WWE]] event to feature a fourteen-man battle royal.[3] He noted that [[dwayne-johnson|Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson]] had said in November 2023 that both parties had asked him to run for president.[3]
