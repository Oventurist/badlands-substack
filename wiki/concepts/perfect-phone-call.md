# Perfect Phone Call

"Perfect Phone Call" is the phrase [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has used repeatedly to describe his 2019 telephone conversation with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, the call that triggered his first impeachment.[1]

## Interpretation within the Truth Community

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Trump's constant references to the "Perfect Phone Call" signal considerably more than a simple assertion that the call was not incriminating.[1] In this reading, Trump exercised some measure of control over how the call would play out, anticipated that audio would be leaked, knew what narratives the media would build in its wake, and had material on the Biden family prepared to deploy once his opponents took the bait.[1]

Burning Bright drew an explicit parallel between this pattern and the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago Raid]], which he characterized as a reverse [[wrap-up-smear|wrap-up smear]], and extended the same framework to the June 2023 release by [[cnn|CNN]] of the Bedminster audio recording.[1] The general claim is that whenever what he calls the "gotcha media" believes it has caught Trump, "the boomerang comes back around with significantly more force than they were expecting."[1]
