# Engels Fuel Depot Strike

The Engels fuel depot strike was a Ukrainian drone attack on fuel storage facilities in the city of Engels, in Russia's Saratov region, carried out overnight on Tuesday in early January 2025.[1]

## The attack

Ukrainian drones attacked fuel depots in Engels, causing an explosion and a huge fire, and local authorities decided to introduce a state of emergency, as reported by the Interia news portal.[1] The targeted oil depot supplies fuel to the Engels-2 military airport, a facility described as crucial to Moscow's plan for victory in the war.[1]

The strike was confirmed in a statement by the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, which said that "numerous explosions were reported near the target and a large-scale fire broke out," that local authorities confirmed an "industrial facility" was hit, and that more detailed information on the results of combat operations was being clarified.[1]

## Context

The strike came during a period in which Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] was pressing Western backers for deeper involvement in the war ahead of the January 20, 2025 inauguration of President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had repeatedly vowed to negotiate a swift end to the nearly three-year conflict.[1]
