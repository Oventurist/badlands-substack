# Nova Music Festival Friendly-Fire Admission

The **Nova music festival friendly-fire admission** refers to the November 2023 acknowledgement, emerging from an Israeli police investigation, that an Israeli attack helicopter killed some of the attendees at the Nova music festival near the Gaza border during the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]].[1] The finding was reported by [[haaretz|Haaretz]] on Saturday, November 18, 2023 and summarized by The Cradle.[1]

## Findings

According to a police source, the investigation showed that an Israeli combat helicopter which arrived at the scene from the Ramat David base fired at [[hamas|Hamas]] fighters and other Palestinians who had crossed through the border fence from Gaza into Israel, but also fired on some of the Israelis attending the festival.[1] Police put the total number of people killed at the festival site at 364.[1]

## Significance

The [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli military]] and rescue services had previously claimed that 260 Israelis were killed at the festival, all of them by Hamas and Palestinians in a deliberate massacre; the police finding marked the first acknowledgement that Israeli forces killed some of their own at the site.[1]

Earlier reports in Israeli media had revealed a comparable episode at Be'eri, a settlement also near the Gaza border, where Hamas fighters were holding Israelis captive in homes and the Israeli military, on arriving, opened fire — including with tank shells — killing both Israeli captives and Hamas fighters.[1]
