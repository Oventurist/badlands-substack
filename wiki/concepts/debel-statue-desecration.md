# Debel Statue Desecration

The Debel statue desecration was an April 2026 incident in which an Israeli soldier in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]] smashed a statue of Jesus with a sledgehammer.[1]

## Verification

The [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] said on Monday that it had verified the authenticity of a widely circulated image showing the act, that the matter was being handled through the chain of command, and that appropriate measures would be taken.[1] The military said it viewed the incident with "great severity" and would work with the local community to restore the statue.[1] Arab media reports cited by AFP placed the statue in the Christian village of Debl (Debel) near the Israel-Lebanon border.[1]

Israeli Foreign Minister [[gideon-saar|Gideon Sa'ar]] described the act as "grave and disgraceful" and said he supported the military's investigation and disciplinary process.[1]

## Local response and commentary

The town posted an image of the statue as it stood before the desecration, captioned in Arabic with Luke 23:34 — "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do."[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the Israeli government issues such condemnations whenever acts of terrorism are committed by the IDF or by the [[israeli-settler-movement|Israeli Settler Movement]], and that nothing ever happens to those responsible.[1] He placed the episode within a wider pattern of [[jewish-terrorism|Jewish terrorism]] that also included the destruction of the Tomb of Saint Peter, described as accidental, and warnings from [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s government that Lebanese Christians should not return to their villages despite the ceasefire.[1]
