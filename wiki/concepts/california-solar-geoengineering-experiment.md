# California Solar Geoengineering Experiment

The California solar geoengineering experiment refers to a billionaire-backed research program in California that conducted a brief solar geoengineering test on San Francisco Bay and quietly planned a far larger follow-on study aimed at dimming sunlight.[1]

## Background and the aborted carrier test

A team of researchers in California drew notoriety in 2024 with an aborted experiment aboard a retired aircraft carrier that sought to test a machine for creating clouds.[1]

## The larger planned study

Behind the scenes, the same team was planning a much larger and potentially riskier study of salt water-spraying equipment that could eventually be used to dim the sun's rays — a multimillion-dollar project aimed at producing clouds over a stretch of ocean larger than Puerto Rico.[1]

Details outlined in funding requests, emails, texts and other records obtained by Politico's E&E News raised new questions about the secretive, billionaire-backed initiative that oversaw the earlier San Francisco Bay test.[1] According to the reporting, the researchers wanted to "avoid scaring" the public, and the records offered a rare glimpse into the vast scope of research aimed at countering the Earth's warming — work that has often occurred outside public view.[1]

The item was carried as a bonus story in the July 28, 2025 Badlands News Brief.[1]
