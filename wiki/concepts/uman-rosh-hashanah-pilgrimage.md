# Uman Rosh Hashanah pilgrimage

The Uman Rosh Hashanah pilgrimage is the annual journey of tens of thousands of Jewish worshippers — predominantly Breslov Hasidim and Israelis — to the Ukrainian city of Uman to pray at the gravesite of Nachman of Breslov, an eighteenth-century Jewish mystic (rebbe) who lived during the era of the American Revolution and died in the early 1800s.[1]

## October 2024 pilgrimage amid war

During the Rosh Hashanah observance of early October 2024, more than 50,000 Israelis travelled to Uman according to figures attributed to the Ukrainian government, even as [[israel|Israel]] was absorbing a 181-missile barrage from [[iran|Iran]] and prosecuting expanded operations against [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in Lebanon.[1] The Times of Israel characterised the phenomenon as pilgrims "trading one war for another," since Uman sits inside a country under sustained Russian attack.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] raised the pilgrimage in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as a corrective to apocalyptic readings of the Middle East escalation, urging readers not to reach immediately for the Book of Revelation.[1] He pointed to widely circulated video of tens of thousands of Israeli pilgrims celebrating in an obscure Ukrainian town while Ukrainian men were conscripted to the front lines, and asserted that the same sect of Judaism exercises political leverage over [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s cabinet.[1]

He tied the observation to a 2017 report of a Russian special operations commando photographed in Aleppo, Syria, wearing a [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] patch, arguing that Russia, Ukraine and Israel are elements of a single interconnected conflict rather than separate theatres.[1] The commentary appeared alongside the item on Foreign Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] barring UN Secretary-General [[antonio-guterres|António Guterres]] from Israel, which the same writer said fed a growing perception of the Israeli government as an international pariah.[1]
