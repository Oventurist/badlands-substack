# Luciferian Rites

**Luciferian rites** is the term used in the Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* for a claimed system of occult inversion said to spiritually protect the legal and financial architecture the essay describes.[1]

The essay associates these rites with black-cube symbolism and with Saturnian cultic systems it alleges were revived within [[vatican|Vatican]] cathedrals and Jesuit retreats, arguing that "what appears as Christian piety is often Saturnian ritual cloaked in canon."[1] It claims the Vatican's deep halls conceal not merely gold but "rite-bound binding ceremonies" encoded with planetary talismans and Kabbalistic systems.[1]

The same inversion current is traced in the essay to the [[sabbateanism|Sabbatean-Frankist]] doctrine of redemption through sin — the teaching that rule must be inverted to become divine — and to the [[khazarian-mafia|Khazarian mafia]] network the essay describes as its political and intelligence expression.[1] Black-cube Saturnian worship is said to be echoed in architectural placements across elite capitals.[1] [[lucis-trust|Lucis Trust]] is presented as the doctrine's channel into United Nations compacts.[1]

The essay also treats certain performance art as public signalling of these rites, naming [[marina-abramovic|Marina Abramović]] as the "ceremonial artist of the grid" and [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] as the bureaucratic bridge between ritual and policy.[1]

No mainstream evidence supports the existence of an organized Luciferian rite within these institutions; the framing is characteristic of esoteric-conspiracist interpretation.[1]
