# Peacemaker Theory

"Peacemaker Theory" is an interpretive framework used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to make sense of escalating global conflict. In this reading, the wars and crises of the period are not open-ended but are staged movements toward an eventual settlement brokered by a figure cast as the peacemaker, so that apparent escalation is a precondition for, rather than an obstacle to, resolution.[1]

## Application to Bakhmut

Burning Bright invoked the framework in his commentary on [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]]'s May 2023 declaration that the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] had achieved full capture of Bakhmut after 224 days of fighting.[1] He argued that the announcement, coming after weeks of apparent [[kayfabe|kayfabe]] between Wagner and the Kremlin over alleged heavy losses, produced "narrative whiplash on NATO simps" and paved the way for a fresh wave of [[nato|NATO]] escalation and desperation.[1]

Summarizing his conclusion as "Peacemaker on Deck," he wrote that "when you understand that this movie only ends with the Peacemaker Theory, all of the escalation and acceleration, from Bakhmut to Taiwan makes perfect sense."[1] The theory thus functions as a unifying lens applied across theaters — the Russia–Ukraine war and Taiwan alike — treating intensification in each as part of a single trajectory toward a negotiated end.[1]
