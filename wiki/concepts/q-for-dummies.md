# Q for Dummies

**Q for Dummies** is an introductory serialized primer on the [[q-operation|Q]] drops written by [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] and published through [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s Substack, beginning with Chapter 1.[1]

The [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] author recommended the series in his July 2024 essay "In Defense of Q - We Were Never Told to Sit Still," describing it as a way for readers to "gain a solid footing with regards to the drops themselves," and framing his own [[in-defense-of-q-series|In Defense of Q]] series as a supplement to such material rather than a replacement for it.[1] Where Q for Dummies is presented as grounding in the source drops, the In Defense of Q series was intended to supply tactical and psychological guidance for [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]].[1] The author explicitly noted that there are many excellent sources on Q and characterized his mention of the series as a recommendation rather than an exclusive endorsement.[1]

## Purpose and method

Chapter 1, subtitled "Intro & Drops #1-5," was published on December 5, 2023.[2] Fleuret — known in the movement as Absolute1776 and a host of [[eye-of-the-storm|Eye Of The Storm]] on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] alongside [[stormy-patriot-joe|Stormy Patriot Joe]] — explains that the series is deliberately the "direct opposite" of high-level Q analysis, a niche he says is already covered by [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots In Progress]].[2] He describes the gap he set out to fill as the absence of "low level takes" that break the drops down for newcomers, given a learning curve he compares to the slopes of the Matterhorn.[2]

Fleuret states that the opinions in the series are his alone, that he is not the ultimate source on Q, and that readers should always draw their own conclusions and connect their own dots.[2] His stated aim is for readers eventually to navigate the drops without relying on anyone else's understanding, accepting that some of his own takes may prove wrong.[2] Additional goals include making Q simple to digest, equipping readers to explain it to friends and family, and tying each set of drops to contemporary news and narratives.[2]

## Rebuttal of the "psyop" framing

Before turning to the drops, Chapter 1 addresses what Fleuret calls the most infamous misnomer about Q — that it is a psyop designed to keep people at home and inactive.[2] He states flatly that nowhere in the drops does Q say the military is coming to save anyone, and that the drops never tell people to refrain from peaceful action.[2] Those who claim otherwise, he writes, have either never read the drops, do not understand them, or are misrepresenting them with nefarious intent.[2]

## Content of Chapter 1

The chapter covers the first five drops, posted to the [[4chan|4chan]] board /pol/ on October 28 and 29, 2017.[2] Topics include the claimed detention rather than arrest of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], the insulation and relative power of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] against [[deep-state|deep state]] figures, [[military-intelligence|military intelligence]] as a counterweight to the three-letter agencies, [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]], the [[posse-comitatus-act|Posse Comitatus Act]], [[george-soros|George Soros]]'s transfers to his non-profits, [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]]'s meeting with Trump over the FBI directorship, [[john-mccain|John McCain]] (later "No Name"), and [[ms-13|MS-13]] as an alleged deep state hit squad.[2] It also introduces the maxims [[future-proves-past|future proves past]] and "follow the money," and the [[4-am-talking-points|4 AM talking points]].[2]

Fleuret announced a plan to cover seven to ten drops per article on a bi-weekly basis, and closed with the movement slogan "where we go one, we go all."[2] Badlands Media appends its standard notice that articles represent the opinions of contributing authors rather than the outlet itself, and directs readers to Fleuret's own Substack, Beyond the Veil.[2]
