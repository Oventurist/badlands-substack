# 2023 G20 New Delhi Summit

The 2023 annual summit of the [[g20|Group of 20]] was hosted by India in New Delhi in September 2023 under the leadership of Prime Minister [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]].[1] The gathering was overshadowed in the days before it convened by reports that China's president would not attend in person.[1]

## Xi Jinping's absence

On Thursday, August 31, 2023, Reuters and other outlets reported, on the basis of multiple diplomatic sources, that [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] would not attend the summit — the first time he had skipped an in-person G20 leaders' meeting since becoming president in 2013.[1] The report was widely characterized as a major setback for a summit already beset by unity problems, and as a deeply symbolic snub to India coming only a week after Xi's prominent role at the [[2023-brics-johannesburg-summit|BRICS summit in South Africa]].[1]

Commentators read the decision as a downgrading of the G20's importance in favor of the [[brics|BRICS+]] format at a moment when the "alternative to Western alignment" bloc was set to double in size with the admission of six new members.[1] Zhang Baohui, a professor at Lingnan University in Hong Kong, told the Financial Times that Xi "never missed a G20 meeting before because it's a vital occasion for China to try to shape the global narrative," adding that the "G20 offers China that platform to outcompete the American messages."[1] Premier [[li-qiang|Li Qiang]] represented China at the Indian capital instead, where other heads of state including US President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] gathered.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Badlands News Brief, [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] questioned the timing of the snub, asking whether it signaled an imminent Chinese move on Taiwan or something else entirely, and floating the speculation that Xi might be aligned with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in exposing the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1] He noted the coincidence of the decision with the Trump indictments and what he described as the collapse of Biden's "house of cards," concluding that "timing matters."[1]
