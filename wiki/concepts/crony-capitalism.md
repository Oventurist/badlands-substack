# Crony Capitalism

Crony capitalism is the term used in the Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1" to describe an economic system in which businesses thrive not as a result of free enterprise, as originally intended, but as a result of money amassed through collusion between the business class and the political class.[1]

Author [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] presents crony capitalism as "a negatively-polarized usage of the free market system" and treats it as the legitimate grievance driving younger Americans toward [[socialism|socialism]].[1] He acknowledges that crony capitalism "has caused great suffering in more subtle ways," but argues that first-world citizens remain free to denounce it, a freedom denied to those living under [[communism|communism]] in states such as [[cuba|Cuba]].[1]
