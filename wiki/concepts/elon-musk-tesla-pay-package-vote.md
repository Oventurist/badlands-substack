# Elon Musk Tesla Pay Package Vote (2024)

At its annual meeting on Thursday, June 13, 2024, [[tesla|Tesla]] announced that shareholders had approved [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s compensation package following weeks of campaigning by the company.[1]

Musk celebrated immediately — "Hot damn! I love you guys," he said — and took to the stage for a victory dance.[1] The company had, however, only won its first battle: despite investor approval, Musk would not receive the package right away, because Tesla must take the issue back to court.[1] The plan had originally been approved in 2018 but was struck down in January 2024 by a Delaware judge after a Tesla shareholder filed a lawsuit alleging the agreement was "beyond the bounds of reasonable judgment."[1] Business Insider framed the outcome as a win for Musk that nonetheless left him "not out of the woods yet."[1]
