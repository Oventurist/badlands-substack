# Türkiye F-35 Delivery Dispute

The Türkiye F-35 delivery dispute concerns Turkish demands that the United States deliver F-35 fighter jets that Ankara says it has already paid for, despite Türkiye's 2019 removal from the F-35 program.[1]

## Erdogan's remarks

On Wednesday, 1 October 2025, President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] said his government was pursuing delivery of the aircraft, emphasizing that Türkiye had already paid for them.[1] "We told them, 'We paid the money, you still haven't delivered the F-35s.' That's it. Now we are following up on it," Erdogan told reporters at a reception hosted by Parliament Speaker Numan Kurtulmus at the Grand National Assembly to mark the new legislative year.[1] His remarks came in response to a question about his recent meeting with U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], during which the F-35 issue was reportedly discussed.[1]

## Background

Türkiye was removed from the F-35 program in 2019 after purchasing Russia's S-400 air defense system, a move that drew sharp criticism from Washington and other [[nato|NATO]] allies.[1] The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] cited security concerns, arguing that the Russian system could compromise the advanced fighter jet's technology.[1]
