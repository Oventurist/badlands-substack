# Gabbard Russian Asset Allegation

The Gabbard Russian asset allegation is the recurring claim that [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] functions as an asset of the Russian government, revived in November 2024 after President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] nominated her as director of national intelligence.[1]

## Wasserman Schultz remarks

On a Friday broadcast of MSNBC's "José Díaz-Balart Reports" in November 2024, Representative [[debbie-wasserman-schultz|Debbie Wasserman Schultz]], Democrat of Florida, said Gabbard is "someone who is likely a Russian asset" and "considered to be, essentially, by most assessments, a Russian asset."[1]

Wasserman Schultz argued that Gabbard "has met with war criminals, violated the Department of State's guidance, and secretly, clandestinely, went to Syria and met with [[bashar-al-assad|Assad]], who gassed and attacked his own people with chemical weapons."[1] When host José Díaz-Balart pressed her on whether that was her own view, she replied, "Oh, yes. There's no question," adding that as DNI Gabbard would be responsible for managing the entire intelligence community, would hold the nation's most significant secrets, and "essentially, would be a direct line to our enemies."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media traced the Russian-asset framing to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and characterized Wasserman Schultz's revival of it as a misogynistic and defamatory attack timed to the confirmation process.[1] The commentary argued the smear exists because opponents cannot concede that Gabbard is a strong, capable, experienced veteran with the integrity to root out systemic corruption, and must instead cast her as a honey pot.[1]

The outlet offered as a rebuttal that if Gabbard were genuinely a Russian asset she would have been prosecuted by the Biden Justice Department to the fullest extent allowable — and, in its phrasing, beyond it — by [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]], [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]], and their handlers.[1] It concluded that if "Russia!" was the sum of the opposition's case, they had nothing, and predicted Gabbard would be confirmed.[1]
