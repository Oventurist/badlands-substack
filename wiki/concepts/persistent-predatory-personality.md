# Persistent Predatory Personality

"Persistent Predatory Personality" is a term attributed to researcher [[karen-mitchell|Dr. Karen Mitchell]] describing a single underlying disorder said to unify sociopathy, narcissism, sadism and other predatory personality types.[1] Rather than treating these as separate clinical labels, the concept holds that they are surface variations of one predatory essence.[1]

## Use in Badlands analysis

The Badlands Media article "Q is Our Best Fren" adopts the term as the psychological foundation for its portrait of the [[cabal]], arguing that [[psychopathy-and-parasitism|psychopathy is a terminally advanced form of parasitism]] and that clinical literature stops short of stating this bluntly.[1] The article asserts that individual predators differ in traits but not in essence, so a single profile can be applied across the network.[1]

Characteristics attributed to the type in the article include an inability to feel satiation except while actively harming others, the enjoyment of deception as a substitute for genuine social pleasure, a self-conception as gods above ordinary humans, and contempt for the morality of non-predators.[1] The more intelligent members are said to possess enough self-control to sustain long-term campaigns of deception, making them effective infiltrators of institutions.[1] Because promises hold no value among them, their cooperative arrangements are described as being enforced by mutual blackmail rather than trust.[1]

## Practical significance

Within the article's framework, learning to recognise the Persistent Predatory Personality is presented as a survival skill for [[anons]] and [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]]: an immune system cannot fight a pathogen it cannot identify, and a public unable to spot predators cannot resist them.[1] Recommended further reading includes Mitchell's doctoral thesis and the book *Psychopath Proof*, alongside [[anthony-napoleon|Anthony Napoleon]]'s [[shadow-men|Shadow Men]].[1]
