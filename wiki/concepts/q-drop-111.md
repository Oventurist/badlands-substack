# Q Drop #111

**Q drop #111** is a post dated November 5, 2017 that Chapter 16 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series treats as large enough to warrant an entire installment on its own, rather than being packaged with the four or five drops the series normally covers per chapter.[1] The author explains that the drop is "huge," that "there is a lot to unpack," and that he therefore chose to break it down section by section.[1]

## HUMA and the groomed president

The drop opens with questions about "HUMA," which the chapter identifies not as [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] but as [[huma-harvard-university-muslim-alumni|HUMA, the Harvard University Muslim Alumni]].[1] The chapter states that [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] shares connections with prominent and "not necessarily benevolent" Muslims, including figures with ties to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]].[1] Q's question about which president is affiliated with HUMA is answered as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[1]

The chapter recounts that [[khalid-al-mansour|Dr. Khalid Abdullah Tariq al-Mansour]] wrote to former Malcolm X attorney [[percy-sutton|Percy Sutton]], asking him to send a letter on Obama's behalf to help secure his admission to Harvard Law School.[1] The author remarks on the incongruity of a man he characterises as hating America and white people having stumped for Obama's admission.[1] Al-Mansour is described as having close ties to [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], who for a long period owned the majority stake in [[twitter|Twitter]] and also owned the Mandalay Bay, the hotel from which the [[las-vegas-shooting|Las Vegas shooting]] was carried out.[1]

The chapter's recap is that al-Mansour vouched for Obama in 1988 and got him into Harvard, while Alwaleed financed Obama and helped bring him up through the ranks — a timeline the author places "right around the time Pappy Bush and his fellow Saudi-loving cretins were taking power."[1] He argues that while Israel has exerted influence over American politicians, the extent to which the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]] controlled the U.S. government went unrecognised until recently, spanning administrations from Bush 1.0 through Clinton, Bush 2.0, Obama and [[joe-biden|Biden]], and that this is why the [[sword-dance|sword dance]] Trump received was so monumental.[1] Q's question as to why a former president would be funded in his pre-presidential days is answered simply: he was groomed from the start.[1]

## Saudi Arabia and the severed pipeline

Asked "what just happened in SA," the chapter points to the November 2017 [[saudi-purge|crackdown and purge of corruption]] in the Saudi royal family and government, in which Alwaleed was arrested and his funds seized — cutting off what the author calls the endless pipeline of corrupt money flowing from Saudi Arabia into the U.S. government.[1] The events in Saudi Arabia are described as important precisely because they cut the strings of control held over the American government by the Saudi royal family and, by extension, the Muslim Brotherhood.[1]

The chapter cites an October 2017 Politico report that [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] took an unannounced trip to Saudi Arabia; while it was not officially announced whom he met, speculation held that he met [[mohammed-bin-salman|MBS]], who ordered the takedown of the Saudi royal family and who, the author notes, Badlands influencers associate with the Tri-Sovereign Alliance.[1]

## The Twitter deactivation

Q's questions about POTUS's last tweet before the Saudi events and about why his account was taken down are addressed with a refresher on the [[2017-trump-twitter-account-deactivation|November 3, 2017 deactivation]] of [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s Twitter account.[1] The chapter asserts that Trump's final tweet before the purge was a coded message giving the green light for the Saudi takedown, and that the eleven-minute removal of his account in the early hours of November 3, 2017 — publicly blamed on a "rogue employee" — was in fact an attempt to stop that message from going out.[1] The author argues that taking down a sitting president's account to subvert an operation freeing the United States from foreign shackles could be considered an act of war, and repeats his speculation that the tweet may have been the "11.3" marker.[1] Q's question of who was taken down is answered as Alwaleed bin Talal, whose arrest and alleged subsequent torture the author regards as no coincidence.[1]

## Wahhabism and systemic corruption

A later section asks which faith HUMA, the MB and Huma Abedin represent; the chapter's answer is not simply "Muslim" but [[wahhabism|Wahhabi extremism]], described as a belief system in which non-adherents are viewed as infidels to be eradicated, with America as the primary target of an infiltration effort as long-running as that of the communists.[1] The bad actors listed include Obama, [[hillary-clinton|HRC]], [[john-mccain|No Name]], [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]], Huma Abedin, Alwaleed bin Talal and [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], all presented as involved with the Muslim Brotherhood and therefore supportive of Wahhabism.[1]

Q's question about who funds the majority of senior U.S. politicians is answered as the Royal House of Saud, whose funding is said to have ceased with the purge.[1] The drop's reference to "Fantasy Land" is read as Saudi Arabia and its corruption — corruption that had become America's own.[1] Asked whether the former president was groomed and whether such grooming is possible, the chapter answers both with an emphatic yes, citing Obama, George W. Bush and, in the present, [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1] The rhetorical question "Is the US political/election system corrupt?" is answered that it is corrupt to its core, which the author gives as part of the reason everything is taking so long.[1] He highlights the line "they never thought she would lose," arguing that Hillary's 2016 defeat was cataclysmic for [them] and that the same overconfidence is on display in 2024 with Harris.[1]

## Closing lines

The drop's statement that the complete picture would place 99% of the population in a hospital is read as meaning the evil involved is so dark that releasing all of it at once would break many people's psyche, justifying a slow release of truth.[1] Q's line "POTUS is our savior" is interpreted not as equating Trump with Jesus Christ but as identifying him as the incorruptible politician able to save the government from the corruption controlling it.[1] The drop asks readers to pray, states that operators are active, reinforces that the country is at war, and closes with "Goodnight BO," which the author reads as Q announcing that Obama's funding and the Muslim Brotherhood's reach into the U.S. government had been cut off.[1] The drop ends with renewed mentions of [[operation-snow-white|Snow White]] and [[the-godfather|Godfather III]], the latter of which the chapter flags for future reference given recent potential Godfather III comms.[1]
