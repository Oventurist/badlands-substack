# Core 5

The "Core 5" is a reported proposal for a five-nation great-power forum comprising the United States, Russia, China, India and Japan, intended to sideline the Western-dominated [[g7|G7]].[1] The concept surfaced in December 2025 in media reporting on an unpublished, longer draft of the Trump administration's National Security Strategy.[1]

## Reported contents

According to the Defense One news portal, a draft circulated before the [[white-house|White House]] published the official unclassified document proposed the Core 5 as a forum for dialogue among major powers outside the G7 framework.[1] Under the reported plan the grouping would hold regular thematic summits modelled on the G7, with Middle East security — and specifically the normalization of relations between Israel and Saudi Arabia — first on the agenda.[1]

The same unpublished version reportedly proposed downgrading Washington's role in European defense, pushing NATO toward a tougher "burden-sharing" model, and prioritizing bilateral ties with EU governments viewed as closer to the American outlook, such as Austria, Hungary, Italy and Poland.[1]

## Denial

Politico reported that White House spokesperson Anna Kelly insisted "no alternative, private, or classified version exists" beyond the official 33-page plan.[1]

## Context

The proposal emerged alongside President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s statement that Russia has no interest in rejoining the G7, which he described as a shrinking but still "important platform."[1] Badlands commentary read both stories as elements of a deliberate information operation aimed less at the general public than at the foreign and domestic opponents of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
