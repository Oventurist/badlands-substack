# JACOB Accords

The **JACOB Accords** are a satirical coinage used by Badlands Media contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] to describe what he presents as an expanding informal network of world leaders aligned against a globalist establishment. The acronym is glossed jokingly as "**J**ust **A** **C**ouple **O**f **B**ros" — a deliberate riff on the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] — applied to demonstrative displays of personal warmth between heads of state.[1]

## Origin and usage

The term is invoked chiefly in commentary on summit imagery. Following [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s June 2024 arrival in Pyongyang, where [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] foregoing a traditional handshake embraced Putin in a full hug on the tarmac, the writer described the scene as "a full-blown love fest" and as affirmation that the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] is real and that the JACOB Accords "are expanding at an accelerating rate."[1] The gloss offered in the same passage frames the leaders as "preparing to bring down an ancient death cult that wants to enslave humanity."[1]

The same commentary compared Pyongyang's decoration for Putin's visit — streets decked with Russian flags and posters of the Russian president — to the regalia with which King Salman decorated Riyadh for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2017 visit to the Saudi capital, the trip remembered for the sword dance.[1]

## Interpretation

The concept has no diplomatic or legal existence; it is a narrative device within Badlands commentary for reading leader-to-leader rapport as evidence of a covert multipolar realignment. It sits alongside the Sovereign Alliance framework in the same body of writing, and is used to reinterpret events that mainstream outlets treat as adversarial alignment — such as Moscow's sourcing of weapons from Pyongyang to sustain its war in Ukraine — as cooperative theater.[1]
