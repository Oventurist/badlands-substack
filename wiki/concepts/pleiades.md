# Pleiades

The **Pleiades** are a star cluster which, in [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], are said to play a major role in human history according to the linguistic research of [[erhard-landmann|Erhard Landmann]] as transmitted by [[frederick-dodson|Frederick Dodson]].[1]

## Names and syllables

The essay "On Languages and Cover-ups" reports that the Pleiades are often referred to as "Min" or "Maia."[1] Alongside these names it identifies two vessel-forms associated with mankind's claimed "starry ancestors": egg-shaped craft, denoted by the syllables *ai* and *eck*, and craft shaped as a pillar of light, denoted by *sal*, *sul* and related forms.[1] The author argues that with these elements in hand a great deal of history can be analysed from an entirely different perspective, offering the name Solomon — containing *Sal* and *Min* — as a possible record of travel to Earth from the Pleiades on a pillar of light.[1]

## Words for "path"

The strongest evidence the essay offers is a set of cross-linguistic words for *path*.[1] Tamil *patai* is read as *pat* (path) plus *ai* (egg); Arabic *masar* may contain *ma* for Maia; Amharic *meningedi* is glossed as "go to Min"; Xhosa *umendo* contains *men/min* in the middle of the word; Southern Sotho *tsela* contains the vessel element *sel*; and Catalan *cami* is identified as the Hebrew word for the Pleiades.[1] The author concludes that this is a great deal of overlap between world languages referring to paths by way of the Pleiades and of pillar- or egg-shaped vessels, and promises more on the subject in a following article.[1]

## Broadening of the series

The author remarks that just as he never predicted he would end up writing about werewolves, he never expected the Pleiades to become important for [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]] to learn about.[1] He describes the series as having reached a point where its context must broaden beyond ancient history to beyond the solar system, using the tools of etymology, symbolism and "mythology" — which he suggests may in fact simply be history.[1] He notes that a few stars appear prominently in mythologies worldwide, the Pleiades being a major one and [[sirius|Sirius]] another frequently discussed example.[1]
