# Christian Zionism

Christian Zionism is the theological and political current within Western, chiefly evangelical, Christianity that holds support for the state of [[israel|Israel]] to be a religious obligation. In the Badlands Media corpus it is treated as a faction whose loyalties are contested rather than as a neutral descriptor, and it is invoked directly in the April 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-282|Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks"]].

Discussing the polarised reception of [[kanye-west|Kanye West]]'s Easter-weekend comeback concerts, the Brief observes that many readers will "rebuke this love for Kanye, refusing to extend him grace, given his questionable public behavior and statements, at times," and adds what it calls an irony: "those who rebuke him the most tend to identify as Christians, and often as Christian Zionists." The commentary thereby frames Christian Zionism as the wing of American Christianity least willing to tolerate criticism of Israel or of Jewish institutional power, and contrasts it with the corpus's own position, which credits West with having "broke[n] the seal on the Israel Question" and forced the terms of the U.S.-Israel relationship into open cultural debate. The same edition's later commentary, which envisions the collapse of what it calls the Jewish supremacy political movement and the emergence of a secular Israeli state detached from Zionism, situates Christian Zionism among the ideological supports the corpus expects that shift to erode.
