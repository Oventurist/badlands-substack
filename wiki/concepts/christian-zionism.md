# Christian Zionism

Christian Zionism is the theological and political current within Western, chiefly evangelical, Christianity that holds support for the state of [[israel|Israel]] to be a religious obligation. In the Badlands Media corpus it is treated as a faction whose loyalties are contested rather than as a neutral descriptor, and it is invoked directly in the April 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks".

Discussing the polarised reception of [[kanye-west|Kanye West]]'s Easter-weekend comeback concerts, the Brief observes that many readers will "rebuke this love for Kanye, refusing to extend him grace, given his questionable public behavior and statements, at times," and adds what it calls an irony: "those who rebuke him the most tend to identify as Christians, and often as Christian Zionists." The commentary thereby frames Christian Zionism as the wing of American Christianity least willing to tolerate criticism of Israel or of Jewish institutional power, and contrasts it with the corpus's own position, which credits West with having "broke[n] the seal on the Israel Question" and forced the terms of the U.S.-Israel relationship into open cultural debate. The same edition's later commentary, which envisions the collapse of what it calls the Jewish supremacy political movement and the emergence of a secular Israeli state detached from Zionism, situates Christian Zionism among the ideological supports the corpus expects that shift to erode.

## The January 2026 Jerusalem Patriarchs rebuke

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the [[patriarchs-and-heads-of-churches-in-jerusalem|Patriarchs and Heads of the Churches in Jerusalem]] issued a public statement on January 17, 2026 affirming that "the flock of Christ in this land is entrusted to the Apostolic Churches" and declaring that "recent activities undertaken by local individuals who advance damaging ideologies, such as Christian Zionism, mislead the public, sow confusion, and harm the unity of our flock." The statement warned that such undertakings "have found favor among certain political actors in Israel and beyond who seek to push a political agenda which may harm the Christian presence in the Holy Land and the wider Middle East."

US Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] responded on X, rejecting the idea "that any sect of the Christian faith should claim exclusivity in speaking for Christians worldwide," and defending the label: "Christians are followers of Christ and a Zionist simply accepts that the Jewish people have a right to live in their ancient, indigenous, and Biblical homeland. It's hard for me to understand why every one who takes on the moniker 'Christian' would not also be a Zionist." Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] published the letter and framed the group's rebuke as a condemnation of political leaders — such as Huckabee — "who use their positions in government to promote war and undermine peace."
