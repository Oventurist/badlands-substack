# Florida Ukraine Peace Talks

The Florida Ukraine peace talks were negotiations held over the weekend of 29–30 November 2025 in Florida between senior [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] officials and a delegation from [[ukraine|Ukraine]], aimed at ending Russia's invasion of the country.[1]

## Participants

Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], special envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and adviser [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] were among the American officials taking part.[1] Ukraine sent a new negotiating team after its previous lead negotiator, [[andriy-yermak|Andrii Yermak]], resigned when prosecutors searched his home in connection with a corruption investigation tied to alleged embezzlement of tens of millions of dollars from the energy sector.[1] The reconstituted delegation was tasked with continuing talks with both U.S. and Russian representatives.[1]

## The 28-point proposal

A 28-point peace proposal was under consideration during the talks.[1] Supporters argued that it provided a workable path to ending the war, while critics contended that its terms were favorable to [[russia|Russia]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary focused less on the diplomatic mechanics than on the corruption backdrop, asking who was now leading the Ukrainian negotiating team given Yermak's departure amid allegations that he helped embezzle hundreds of millions of dollars.[1] The writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry pressed a broader indictment of U.S. policy — why American taxpayers' money had been routed to Ukraine and why the Republican Party had participated — and argued these questions should be put to every member of [[congress|Congress]] in public.[1] The talks also coincided with [[nato|NATO]] military leadership publicly floating a more aggressive posture toward Russia, a juxtaposition Badlands read as an attempt to derail an imminent settlement.[1]
