# Coherence

**Coherence** is the organising concept of the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], where it names the state in which the recovered parts of a fragmented self begin to harmonize and stability becomes a default condition rather than a peak experience.[1]

## Definition in the essay

The article defines emergence as the moment "coherence becomes your default state, not your peak moment," describing it as "not the spark that ignites the fire, but the flame that endures."[1] Coherence in this usage is explicitly not transcendence: it is grounding, "where truth takes root," and it incorporates rather than erases the reader's fractures, which are said to be "woven into your foundation" and to "sing in harmony."[1]

## Physiological register

The essay insists that coherence is measurable rather than metaphorical, listing bodily markers — breath that is no longer shallow, an unclenched jaw, and the absence of bracing for betrayal at the first sign of closeness.[1] It calls the state "not just emotional" but "physiological. Chemical. Electromagnetic," and describes a nervous system that no longer lives on high alert.[1] [[andrew-newberg|Andrew Newberg]]'s work on the quieting of self-identification and spatial-orientation regions during contemplative practice, and [[mae-wan-ho|Mae-Wan Ho]]'s account of quantum coherence in living systems — in which each part of the body communicates with the whole in real time — are cited as scientific supports.[1] [[stephen-porges|Stephen Porges]]'s [[polyvagal-theory|Polyvagal Theory]] supplies the claim that coherence depends on felt safety: "Until the body feels safe, transformation is only conceptual."[1] The essay summarises the resulting state as "coherence made cellular."[1]

## Transmission and the collective field

Coherence in the essay is contagious as well as personal. [[shamini-jain|Shamini Jain]] is quoted that living in coherence means "transmitting healing to our environment," and [[rollin-mccraty|Rollin McCraty]] of the [[heartmath-institute|HeartMath Institute]] is cited for research indicating that a regulated heart field can stabilize others within its range.[1] [[joe-dispenza|Joe Dispenza]]'s "quantum signature" — the vibrational fingerprint created when thoughts, emotions and actions align — is offered as another framing.[1] The therapist [[vienna-pharaon|Vienna Pharaon]] is quoted to argue that coherence sorts relationships without confrontation: "your frequency does the sorting."[1]

Crucially, the essay maintains that coherence does not mean the absence of disturbance. Doubt still "whispers through your nervous system," but the practitioner pauses, feels, and recalibrates rather than fleeing — "coherence doesn't mean you never feel chaos again, it means you know how to stay steady in its presence."[1] Nor does it mean uniformity: "coherence doesn't mean sameness—it means harmony," a shared song "sung in millions of unique tones."[1]
