# 2025 Russian Victory Day Ceasefire Offer

The **2025 Russian Victory Day ceasefire offer** was a proposed 72-hour truce in the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia–Ukraine war]] announced by the [[kremlin|Kremlin]] in late April 2025 to coincide with Moscow's commemoration of the 80th anniversary of victory in the Second World War.[1]

## The offer

The Kremlin announced that [[russia|Russia]] would observe a 72-hour ceasefire the following weekend, running from 8 May until the close of 10 May, as Moscow prepared to mark the anniversary.[1]

## Ukrainian response

Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] accused Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] of "another attempt at manipulation" with the offer.[1] Writing on X, Zelensky said: "Russia has consistently rejected everything and continues to manipulate the world, trying to deceive the United States. Now, yet again, another attempt at manipulation: for some reason everyone is supposed to wait until 8 May before ceasing fire – just to provide Putin with silence for his parade."[1]

## Trump's reaction

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] sharpened his criticism of Putin after meeting Zelensky at the Vatican, saying he was "very disappointed" in Russia's continued bombardment of civilian areas in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] dismissed the arrangement drily, remarking that it was "totally normal to pre-plan your ceasefires in the middle of super serious conflicts."[1]
