# June 2023 Harvard CAPS-Harris Poll

The **June 2023 Harvard CAPS-Harris Poll**, taken 14–15 June 2023, measured net approval — favorable minus unfavorable — for a wide field of American political figures, and showed [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] topping all other politicians.[1]

## Results

The reported net approval ratings were: Robert F. Kennedy Jr. +21%; [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] +9%; [[tim-scott|Tim Scott]] +9%; [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] +8%; Ron DeSantis +5%; [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] +4%; Bernie Sanders −1%; [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Doug Burgum and Ted Cruz −3%; Marianne Williamson −6%; Kevin McCarthy, Mike Pence and Asa Hutchinson −7%; Chuck Schumer −8%; Kamala Harris, Joe Manchin and Gavin Newsom −10%; [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] −11%; [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] −12%; [[chris-christie|Chris Christie]] −16%; [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] −17%; and [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] −24%.[1]

Kennedy was tied with Trump on "favorable" at 45% each, but Trump carried a 48% "unfavorable" rating against Kennedy's 25%, leading the source outlet to argue that absent party affiliation Kennedy would be the most preferred person to become president.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] urged readers to take the poll with a grain of salt given its Harvard provenance, while noting the sweeping coverage of public figures, institutions, and issues.[1] She called net favorability a largely meaningless metric that nonetheless provided comic relief: Haley posting net-positive favorability, Christie and Ocasio-Cortez polling below Biden, Williamson still being "a thing," and Clinton outperforming only McConnell.[1] She observed that the poll also had Trump beating Biden by six points and found the U.S. military the most trusted institution by two dozen points, with police second — findings absent from the headline.[1]

On Kennedy, she judged him a formidable challenger to Trump in the political climate — a Kennedy representing a return to normalcy for the left, decorum, decency and an idealized Democratic Party that no longer exists — but argued the contest would be real only "if we had free and fair elections."[1] She placed Kennedy in the same category as [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]: figures the regime cannot allow to win, because Kennedy had vowed to revive the mission of his father and uncle and shatter the intelligence apparatus, spoken about accountability for past government abuses, and committed to righting the wrongs of the COVID pandemic and vaccines — third rails comparable to Lake's promises to investigate the southern border and election fraud.[1] She nonetheless declared herself "all in on Trump," calling Kennedy a big-government globalist wrong on guns, global warming, foreign policy and protections for liberty.[1]
