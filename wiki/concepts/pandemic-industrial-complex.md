# Pandemic Industrial Complex

The **pandemic industrial complex** is a critical term used in alternative and independent media to describe the network of public health officials, international health bodies, pharmaceutical interests, philanthropies, and biosecurity contractors whose funding and institutional relevance depend on the continued threat and management of infectious disease outbreaks.[1]

## Usage

The term was invoked in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of April 7, 2023 by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], responding to [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]]'s statement that "there will absolutely be an outbreak of another pandemic," possibly as soon as the following year.[1] DeLarme wrote that "they didn't spend years building up this Pandemic Industrial Complex to blow it all on one measly global pandemic," framing Fauci's remark as arriving "right on cue."[1]

He connected the statement to a claim attributed to Dutch virologist Marion Koopmans of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] that the WHO was preparing for "10 years of viruses" with 2020 as year one.[1] DeLarme argued that the same individuals who routinely war-game pandemic scenarios and have invested significant wealth in the sector are consistently able to predict when nature will supposedly unleash a pandemic, without ever explaining the basis for those conclusions.[1]

## Political implications

Commentators in the same brief linked renewed pandemic warnings to electoral consequences, with the contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] remarking that he could "already hear the mail-in ballot printers firing up" — tying anticipated outbreaks to the emergency voting procedures at the center of [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]].[1]

## "The WHO's Last Stand" (2023)

Badlands Media argued that because none of the errors of the COVID-19 pandemic were sufficiently addressed and virtually no accountability followed, the machinations of the Pandemic Industrial Complex would be able to proceed unimpeded under the banner of "global health" when the next emergency arrives.[2] The article observed that outrage dissipates over time as public attention shifts to a war in Eastern Europe, a culture war at home, the persecution of former presidents and a looming financial collapse — leaving the complex's incentives intact.[2]

It characterized the [[who-pandemic-accord|WHO CA+]] and the proposed amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]] as the next phase of a long-planned global coup d'etat, advanced by globalist institutions including the [[united-nations|United Nations]], the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and especially the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[2]
