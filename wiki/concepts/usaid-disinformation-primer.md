# USAID Disinformation Primer

The "Disinformation Primer" is an internal [[usaid|USAID]] document that drew heavy criticism in February 2025 for explicitly promoting and endorsing censorship strategies.[1]

## Contents and criticism

According to critics who reviewed the document during the first month of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term, the primer advocated pressuring advertisers to financially throttle disfavored media outlets, and endorsed narrative-management techniques including Google's "Redirect Method" and "prebunking."[1] Prebunking seeks to inoculate audiences against claims before they encounter them, while the Redirect Method uses search advertising to divert users toward counter-messaging content.[1]

The primer is cited alongside USAID's roughly $473 million in support for [[internews|Internews]] as evidence that the agency operated as a node in the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship industrial complex]] rather than as a purely humanitarian body.[1] Commentators objected that the only thing more objectionable than a government censoring its own citizens is using those citizens' tax dollars to do it.[1]
