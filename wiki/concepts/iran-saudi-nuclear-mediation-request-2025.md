# Iran Saudi Nuclear Mediation Request (2025)

In November 2025, [[iran|Iran]] asked [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] to persuade the United States to revive stalled nuclear talks, a request that underlined Tehran's anxiety over a possible repeat of Israeli airstrikes and its deepening economic woes, according to two regional sources with knowledge of the matter.[1]

## The Pezeshkian letter

A day before Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s visit to the White House that week, Iranian President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] sent a letter to the de facto Saudi leader, as reported by Iranian and Saudi media.[1] In it, Pezeshkian said Iran "does not seek confrontation," wants deeper regional cooperation, and remains "open to resolving the nuclear dispute through diplomacy, provided its rights are guaranteed."[1] Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei said that Wednesday that Pezeshkian's message to the Saudi crown prince was "purely bilateral."[1]
