# 2024 Election Certification

The certification of the 2024 presidential election took place in [[congress|Congress]] on Monday, January 6, 2025, sealing the political comeback of President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] exactly four years after the January 6, 2021 riot at the Capitol.[1]

## The vote

Reporting described the certification as a full 180-degree turn from four years earlier, when Trump sat in the White House as his supporters violently stormed the Capitol in an attempt to halt the certification of his defeat to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]. In the intervening four years Trump repeatedly sought to recast the events of that day, calling it a "day of love" on the campaign trail, referring to those arrested for their actions as "hostages," and vowing to pardon them immediately upon taking office.[1]

The certification was framed as a moment of vindication for Trump's team as he prepared to return to office, and as another blow to critics who had hoped voters would treat his inaction during the riot and his subsequent rhetoric as disqualifying.[1]

## Badlands coverage and take

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] ran special live coverage of the certification vote. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the proceeding as political and procedural theater — uneventful and anticlimactic.[1]

She argued that the regime was attempting to paint the lack of drama as a return to normalcy, when in fact Democrat drama is the norm and the quiet certification was the exception, citing the Washington Times observation that "it was the first time congressional Democrats did not raise a challenge to a Republican winner of the presidential election since certifying George H.W. Bush's victory in 1989." Whether Democrats would show the same restraint on Inauguration Day was left as an open question.[1]
