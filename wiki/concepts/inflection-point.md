# Inflection Point

"Inflection Point" is a macro-analytical concept advanced by the Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] in an essay of the same name published on January 6, 2023 and subtitled "An 'Eventful' Start to the Year."[1] The piece uses the term to characterize what its author saw as a decisive change of direction in the trajectory of American — and possibly global — society at the opening of 2023, and to argue that rising social and psychological tension should be read as a sign of progress rather than defeat.[1] The article recorded 197 likes on publication.[1]

## Definition

Burning Bright presented two dictionary senses of the phrase: the mathematical sense, "a point of a curve at which a change in the direction of curvature occurs," and the business sense, "a time of significant change in a situation; a turning point."[1] Rather than adopting the business definition for expedience, he argued that the mathematical definition better captured the sociopolitical moment, because what mattered was not merely that change was occurring but that the *direction of curvature* had reversed.[1]

An inflection point, in his usage, signals neither an ending nor a defeat but a midpoint — specifically the moment a climber reaches a summit, sees the whole landscape at once, and begins the descent, changed as much by the climb as by the new vantage.[1] The concept therefore carries a dual promise: a temporary increase in tension, and confirmation that society "has been guided along its current trajectory as far as it is going to go."[1]

## Micro and Macro

The essay is built around the distinction between the Micro — the granular analysis of individual narrative and kinetic deployments on what Burning Bright calls the Game Theory Game Board — and the Macro, the projection of those deployments' net effects on the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]].[1] He argued that the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] excels at Micro analysis, forming "a sort of open-source hivemind of decentralized citizen intelligence" capable of dissecting complex sociopolitical situations, but warned of a paradox at the heart of the [[information-war|Information War]]: in a conflict whose objective is to locate signal within noise, digging too far into detail can leave an analyst lost in the very "informational obfuscation matrix" he is trying to dismantle.[1] His standing advice to readers is to zoom out as often as, or more often than, they zoom in.[1]

He further distinguished "Actual Events" from "Narrative Events" — occurrences that exist principally as messaging deployed by each side of the board — and urged readers to weigh both categories at the Macro level.[1]

## The events of early 2023

Burning Bright cited three concurrent developments as evidence that a threshold was being crossed: the protracted contest over the nomination for Speaker of the House in the newly seated U.S. Congress; the threatened continuation of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] releases; and the "public, dramatic collapse of a young athlete in one of the most-watched sporting events of the past six months" — a reference to the on-field cardiac arrest of Buffalo Bills safety [[damar-hamlin|Damar Hamlin]].[1] Taken together, he wrote, these placed the Collective Mind "on the tip of a knife's edge," with "many genies" being unleashed in open public debate that "can't simply be put back into their respective bottles."[1]

## Tension as engine

A distinguishing claim of the essay is that mass psychological tension is not merely a side effect of the transition to a new paradigm of sovereignty, freedom and morality, but "a key component and engine OF that transition."[1] Burning Bright invoked the analogy of birthing pains, conceding it was well-worn, to describe a collective shedding "our old skin of subjugated serfs."[1] He acknowledged that the Republic established by the founding fathers had not been kept, but said many were resolved to "take it back legally, peacefully and constitutionally, or to die trying."[1]

He framed the preceding years — [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s entry into politics and the backlash from the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]], the culture war over gender norms, and the weaponization of identity politics into a "gladiatorial arena" — as the ascent phase of the curve.[1]

## Relation to the Great Awakening

Burning Bright wrote that when Badlands writers say the "pain" of the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] is far from over, they are not "dooming" or spreading cynicism, but describing the shape of the curve.[1] By his reckoning the pendulum in the Mind War had reached the zenith of its arc and had begun to swing back "in the direction of truth, discernment and ultimately of the justice we all want to see visited upon the enemies of humanity," though he declined to predict how quickly.[1] Readers finding 2023 more turbulent than prior years should read that turbulence as descent from the summit rather than as losing.[1]

The essay closed with his standard sign-off — "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright" — and the assurance that "the best is yet to come," followed by a pointer to his own Substack, Burning Bright, where he focuses on Narrative Deployments and Macro Concepts in [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]].[1]
