# Democratic Backsliding

"Democratic backsliding" is one of several terms Badlands Media identifies as "CIA-speak" — vocabulary deployed in the white papers and public talks of government-funded [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]] institutions to make regime-change advocacy sound like civic scholarship.[1]

## Definition offered

The article defines democratic backsliding as a term employed specifically against a democratically elected government that the establishment wishes to overthrow — ironically, in the name of "strengthening democracy."[1]

## Related terminology

Two companion terms are given in the same glossary. "Nonviolence scholars" describes operatives in the nonviolence space, with "scholars" chosen to imply mere academic standing rather than government-funded work directing civil unrest for political ends.[1] "Strengthening democracy" is glossed as strengthening the Liberal International Order — the CFR, [[nato|NATO]], the UN, the EU and similar bodies — and advancing the international ruling class's long-term agendas behind an outward projection of faux liberalism.[1]

The article cites [[maria-stephan|Maria Stephan]] as a speaker who showcases this terminology, and argues that without a key to the unspoken portion of these definitions, audiences are psychologically manipulated into believing the project is about strengthening democracy.[1]
