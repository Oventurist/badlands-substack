# Ken Paxton Impeachment Trial

The Ken Paxton impeachment trial was the 2023 proceeding in which Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] was tried by the [[texas-senate|Texas Senate]] on twenty articles of impeachment and acquitted on all counts that reached a vote.[1]

## Background

The impeachment campaign was leveled against Paxton by establishment Republicans who, in the account carried by The National Pulse, partnered with left-wing "impeachment managers" in an effort to remove the pro-Trump attorney general from office.[1] The effort was described as a Trump-style impeachment proceeding brought by partisan investigators.[1]

## Charges and verdict

The articles included "disregard of official duty," "misapplication of public resources," "constitutional bribery," "false statements in official records," "conspiracy and attempted conspiracy," "dereliction of duty," "unfitness for office," and "abuse of public trust."[1] On Saturday morning, September 16, 2023, Texas state senators voted to acquit Paxton on all sixteen charges put to a vote. Four original articles had been held in abeyance and were dismissed by the Senate in a vote conducted after the acquittal.[1] Paxton was not present for the verdict, and his wife, Senator [[angela-paxton|Angela Paxton]], was prohibited from voting.[1]

## Aftermath and commentary

With the court of impeachment dissolved, Paxton was officially restored to his position.[1] The presiding member urged legislators to amend the law in the next session to prevent such a flawed and politicized process from recurring; immediately after the vote, calls to "Impeach Dade Phelan" circulated on X, an apparent rebuke of that plea.[1]

Badlands Media commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the outcome as a rare justice win in a reality where justice is elusive, describing the trial as a show trial and a comedy of errors for the House managers, with Speaker [[dade-phelan|Dade Phelan]] leading the charge, and as further proof that when the law is weaponized, the process is the punishment.[1] The piece grouped the acquittal with the [[whitmer-kidnapping-plot-trials|Whitmer kidnapping plot acquittals]] as evidence that the tide might be turning on justice in America.[1]
