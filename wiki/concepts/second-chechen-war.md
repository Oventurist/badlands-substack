# Second Chechen War

The Second Chechen War began in 1999 and lasted ten years.[1] Unlike the [[first-chechen-war|First Chechen War]], the Chechen forces were divided during the second conflict, with roughly half of them siding with Vladimir Putin and the Russian government.[1]

## Origins

Badlands Media dates the outbreak of the war to the infiltration of Dagestan by highly trained radical Islamic terrorists who bombed an apartment building, killing 300 Russians.[1] The outlet asserted that the chances these terrorists were trained and deployed by the [[cia|CIA]] were "incredibly high."[1]

## Kadyrov's defection

[[akhmad-kadyrov|Akhmad Kadyrov]], Chief Mufti of the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria, switched sides and offered his services and his men to Putin; he was named President of Chechnya in 2003 and was assassinated by the same radical Islamists in March 2004.[1] At the onset of the war in 1999 Kadyrov had formed the 141st Special Motorized Regiment, the origin of the [[akhmat-special-forces|Akhmat Special Forces]].[1]

[[apty-alaudinov|Apty Alaudinov]] was in law school when the war began; he graduated in 2001 and became a prosecutor in the Interior Ministry's organized crime unit, a body that [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]] later restructured for kinetic operations.[1]
