# Babylon

Babylon, in the [[book-of-revelation|book of Revelation]], is the corrupt world city whose fall is announced by an angel. In the Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]], [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] reads the Babylon passages as "simply a more detailed look into the [[the-beast|Beast]] system during [[great-tribulation|the Tribulation]]."

## Cause of the fall

The announcing angel attributes Babylon's fall to its rejection of God and its having become "a dwelling place for demons, a prison for foul spirits, and a cage for birds." The article concludes that Babylon falls from within, due to unchecked, festering evil, and that the angel identifies Babylon as the vehicle through which the entire world is corrupted.

## The fiat parallel

The article finds an uncanny resemblance between Revelation's Babylon and what it calls today's [[fiat-currency|fiat slave system]]. The description highlights the kings of the earth who "sleep with the Beast," furthering Babylon's enslavement of humanity while enriching themselves; when Babylon falls these kings weep and lament as "the music stops," God's economy is established, and their earthly treasures are rendered worthless. The author notes that everything so far goes to zero against [[bitcoin]] over time, and calls it unique that Bitcoin is the first tool allowing treasures to be stored "in heaven where moths and vermin do not destroy, and thieves do not break in and steal" (Matthew 6:19–21), while conceding that cyberspace is not heaven and that the verse concerns the alignment of hearts and priorities rather than an asset class.

He also flags the passage's explicit mention of a single hour as interesting, asking whether this is where the concept of a "god-candle" comes into play, and closes on Babylon's description as a global marketplace for every kind of food, drink, trinket, commodity and service — "even bodies and souls."
