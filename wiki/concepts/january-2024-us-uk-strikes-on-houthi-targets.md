# January 2024 US–UK Strikes on Houthi Targets in Yemen

On Thursday, January 11, 2024, the United States and its allies launched air and missile strikes against [[houthis|Houthi]] rebel targets across [[yemen|Yemen]], responding to repeated drone and missile attacks by the Iran-backed militia on commercial shipping transiting the Red Sea.[1] The operation was reported by Politico and carried in the Badlands News Brief of January 12, 2024.[1]

## The operation

The joint assault involved U.S. aircraft, ships and submarines, and followed weeks of warnings from Washington and its partners demanding that the Houthis halt their attacks on vessels in the commercially critical waterway.[1] The strikes were conducted by the United States and the United Kingdom with support from Australia, Canada, the Netherlands and Bahrain.[1]

President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] framed the action as a collective response, saying that "the response of the international community to these reckless attacks has been united and resolute" and that the targeted strikes were "a clear message that the United States and our partners will not tolerate attacks on our personnel or allow hostile actors to imperil freedom of navigation in one of the world's most critical commercial routes."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] argued that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had "signed off on a shadow war against [[iran|Iran]] before he left office in Jan 2021," and read the January strikes as [[centcom|CENTCOM]] commander General [[michael-kurilla|Michael Kurilla]] and the regional coalition hitting targets — noting with satisfaction that press reports weeks earlier had claimed the coalition was falling apart.[1] In this reading, a shadow war against Iran was proceeding at the same time that Iranian proxies such as [[hamas|Hamas]], [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] and the Houthis were being battered by Israeli, American and other forces, described as the "[[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]/[[mossad|Mossad]]/Islamo-Fascist Frankenstein" being put down.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] added that media coverage tends to conflate the actions of nation states with those of terror organizations and proxies, observing that while it had been a bad time to run or fund a terrorist organization such as [[isis|ISIS]] when Trump was publicly in office, it appeared to be an even worse time once he was publicly out of office.[1] He characterized the pattern as a "Sovereign Alliance" operating through "Regions of Responsibility" in a multi-polar world order, adding that "coordination doesn't always take the form of parallel kinetic action."[1]

## Related events

The strikes occurred amid broader escalation in Middle Eastern waterways. On the same day, Iran's navy seized an oil tanker in the Gulf of Oman that had been at the center of an earlier U.S.–Iran dispute, and U.S.-led retaliatory strikes on Houthi positions followed early Friday.[1]
