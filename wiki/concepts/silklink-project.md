# SilkLink Project

SilkLink is a telecommunications infrastructure program worth nearly $1 billion agreed between [[syria|Syria]] and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] as part of a package of multibillion-dollar investment accords signed on February 7, 2026.[1]

The project was one element of a broader Saudi effort to revitalize the Syrian economy after years of civil war and following the lifting of Western sanctions on Syria's interim leadership.[1] Companion agreements in the same package included a new Saudi-Syrian low-cost airline, a $2 billion commitment to redevelop two airports in Aleppo through a Saudi-backed investment fund, and further accords covering energy, real estate and water desalination cooperation.[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the investments as the Saudis using their wealth — alongside [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the Turks — to pull Syria out of the condition into which the [[deep-state|Deep State]] had cast it, describing Syria as "a Rubix cube that President Trump, MBS, and Erdogan are solving together."[1]
