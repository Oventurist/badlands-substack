# Iranian Blockade

The Iranian Blockade is the name given in the July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief to the restriction under which [[iran|Iran]] and its customers are barred from using the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] that the United States would be known as the "Guardian of the Hormuz Strait," providing security for the strategic waterway while allowing all countries — except Iran and its customers under the Iranian Blockade — to continue using it. Trump said the United States would be reimbursed through a proposed 20% fee on all cargo shipped through the strait to cover the costs of providing security, adding that the process would begin immediately.
