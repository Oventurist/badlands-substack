# China Joe

"China Joe" is a political nickname applied to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], asserting that his family and administration are compromised by financial and political ties to the People's Republic of China. [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] described it in November 2023 as "one of the most infamous names the pretender-in-chief enjoys not just within MAGA, but within the larger sociopolitical zeitgeist."[1]

## Origin and function

Like many of the political monikers circulating in the [[information-war|Information War]], the label was coined by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], whom Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] has long characterized as a master of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]].[1] In that reading, Trump's nicknames serve an obvious surface-level purpose — drawing attention to a negative or corrupt aspect of the subject, or highlighting a systemic U.S. problem by proxy — while often carrying a second layer of meaning beneath the surface.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Burning Bright argued in the November 14, 2023 News Brief that while "China Joe" was a fitting name, or had been, the Biden administration was by then "completely hemmed in on the game board" by the [[devolution|Devolution]] operation.[1] He paired this with the claim that a [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] exists that includes nationalist interests in China headed by [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], working to destabilize the globalist [[deep-state|Deep State]] even, and perhaps especially, within its own lands.[1]

The apparent contradiction — a compromised "China Joe" alongside a Xi-led nationalist faction opposing globalist power — was reconciled through a maxim Burning Bright attributed to [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]]: "everything is two things," and every nation has an "evil twin."[1] On this account "China Joe" has been "neutered," his connections to Deep State elements abroad severed, but the narrative retains utility because the education phase of the Information War remains ongoing and Anons are years ahead of the Normie Layers within the Collective Mind.[1]
