# Pskov Drone Attack

The Pskov drone attack was a large overnight drone strike on an airport in the north-western Russian city of Pskov during the early hours of Wednesday, August 30, 2023, described as the biggest drone attack on Russia since the war in Ukraine began.[1]

## The strike

The airport at Pskov hosts military aircraft, and the strike came amid broader drone attacks across six Russian regions.[1] Russia's TASS agency reported that four Il-76 transport planes were damaged: "As a result of the drone attack, four Il-76 aircraft were damaged. A fire broke out, and two planes burst into flames."[1] Given apparent direct hits on four large military planes, ZeroHedge characterised the operation as one of the biggest and most successful cross-border attacks attributed to Ukraine since the war began.[1] Crimea had also come under waves of drone attacks in the preceding days, and Bryansk Oblast, Moscow and other locations were targeted overnight.[1]

## Geography and speculation

Pskov lies only about 20 miles from the border of [[nato|NATO]] member Estonia and more than 400 miles from Ukraine itself, which fuelled internet rumours that Estonia might have been involved; Sputnik reported that Russia was seeking to determine whether the attack had been launched from Latvia or Estonia.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] detected "some interesting False Flagian vibes emanating from the Baltic region over the last 48 hours."[1] He noted that on the Tuesday, Poland and the Baltic states had announced emergency measures in case of a "critical incident" involving Russia, including rapid troop movements particularly around Belarus to shore up a NATO response, and that in the early hours of the following morning Russia was struck by coordinated drone strikes allegedly emanating from the Baltic region.[1] His conclusion was posed as a question: "NATO getting desperate?"[1]
