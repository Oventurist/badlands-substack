# First Chechen War

The First Chechen War took place between 1994 and 1996 and was, in Badlands Media's description, an intense and very violent conflict.[1]

The war is remembered in Badlands coverage principally through its effect on the family of [[apty-alaudinov|Apty Alaudinov]], later the commander of the [[akhmat-special-forces|Akhmat Special Forces]]. Although Alaudinov did not himself participate, he lost twenty close relatives who fought in it, including his uncle, his brother, and his father, who had been an officer in the Soviet Army.[1] The conflict was followed three years later by the [[second-chechen-war|Second Chechen War]].[1]
