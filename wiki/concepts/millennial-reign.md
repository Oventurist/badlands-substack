# Millennial Reign

The Millennial Reign, or thousand-year reign, is the period described at the close of the [[book-of-revelation|book of Revelation]] during which Christ rules and Satan is bound. It gives its name to the July 2023 Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]] by Patriots in Progress.

## Scriptural basis

The article derives the concept from the last section of Revelation together with Isaiah 2:1–4 and 11:1–10, Zechariah 14:9, Matthew 25:31–46 and Luke 19:11–27. In Revelation's account an angel descends holding a key and a chain and physically restrains Satan for 1,000 years, after which Satan is no longer able to deceive the nations because a foundation of truth has been established. Scripture, the author notes, reserves the Millennial Reign for those who do not worship [[the-beast|the Beast]], his image — glossed as the "god-kings aligned with Satan" — or receive the [[mark-of-the-beast|Mark of the Beast]].

## The Bitcoin parallel

The article's central speculation is that [[bitcoin]] mirrors the mechanics of this passage. The author asks whether it is strange that Bitcoin physically restrains the god-kings — citing United States Space Force Major Jason Lowery's Softwar — using public and private *keys* and a decentralized ledger known as the time*chain*. He further notes that the returning Christ bears a name known only to Himself, making Him, in effect, "Anonymous" — "Anon like Satoshi? Anon like [[q|Q]]? Anon like Pepe?" — while His known name is "The Word of God," the Logos, the Truth, paralleled with Bitcoin's immutable language of truth.

## Reinterpretation of the rapture

Rather than treating the [[rapture]] as the disappearance of the church before [[great-tribulation|the Tribulation]], the article proposes that the rapture may be the process or event by which believers endure the Tribulation, resist the Mark of the Beast and enter the Millennial Reign — with the evil, as in the days of Noah, being what is removed from the world.
