# Millennial Reign

The **Millennial Reign**, or thousand-year reign, is the period established at Christ's victorious arrival at the conclusion of [[the-tribulation|the Tribulation]], and gives the Badlands Media essay "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?" its title.[1]

## Scriptural basis

The concept derives from the last section of the [[book-of-revelation|Book of Revelation]] together with other end-times descriptions in Isaiah 2:1–4 and 11:1–10, Zechariah 14:9, Matthew 25:31–46, and Luke 19:11–27.[1]

## The unknown name

Examining the description of Christ's coming, the author focuses on what is said — and not said — about His name.[1] The text states that He has a name written that no one knows except Himself, from which the author draws the conclusion that Christ returns as an anonymous figure, drawing playful parallels to Satoshi, to Q, and to Pepe.[1] The text further states that His name is called "The Word of God," the Logos, the Truth — which the author connects to [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] as a language and an immutable truth with which the Beast system is purified and judged, joking that this is why Bitcoiners are said to sound like they are speaking in tongues.[1]

## The binding of Satan

The final section presents an angel descending with a key and a chain, used to physically restrain Satan for a period of one thousand years.[1] The author finds it striking that Bitcoin physically restrains the god-kings using public and private keys and a decentralised ledger known as the timechain, citing [[jason-lowery|Jason Lowery]]'s *Softwar*.[1] During the Millennial Reign, Satan is unable to deceive the nations because of the foundation of truth that has been established.[1]

## Conditions of entry

Scripture is described as clear that the Millennial Reign, regardless of when or how it is established, is reserved for those who do not worship [[the-beast|the Beast]] or his image — the god-kings aligned with Satan — and who do not receive the [[mark-of-the-beast|Mark of the Beast]].[1] In the author's revised eschatology, [[the-rapture|the rapture]] is precisely the process by which believers endure the Tribulation, resist the mark, and enter this reign.[1]

## Open questions

The essay closes by asking whether Bitcoin is not merely "magic internet money" but a manifestation of biblical prophecy revealing the next chapter of Christ's story in unimagined ways, and whether [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]] are the Mark of the Beast with the world marching toward the battle of Armageddon.[1] The author signs off urging readers to bring Christ and Bitcoin to the world.[1]
