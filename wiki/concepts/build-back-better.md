# Build Back Better

"Build Back Better" was the slogan attached to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s domestic agenda, and in Truth Community commentary it is read as a globalist watchword rather than an ordinary policy brand.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

In "Balance, Necessary for Control" (February 14, 2023), [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that "Build Back Better didn't refer to We, the People … it referred to [their] system" — the bracketed pronoun being the community's standard shorthand for the [[cabal|Cabal]].[1]

Carlson set the slogan against a reversal narrative. Policies enacted under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that, in his telling, helped the country and the people and displaced arrangements designed to enrich the [[deep-state|Deep State]] Cabal were reversed as soon as Biden entered the White House.[1] He listed the crippling of domestic oil production and the burdening of American businesses with climate-change agendas as measures whose purpose was to enrich Deep State players.[1] Maintaining the illusion of balance, he argued, was what made this rollout possible.[1]

## Energy provisions and EV mandates

In a May 15, 2023 Badlands Media essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s Build Back Better policies were put into place quickly once he began residing in the [[white-house|White House]], and treated them as the vehicle for what he called [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]].[2] Among the measures he cited was the requirement that all new cars sold in certain liberal states be electric by 2035, a timetable he described as so unachievable as to be comical given that the power grids cannot accommodate the proposed changes.[2]

Carlson offered California as illustration: he wrote that on the very day [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] made the announcement the state suffered a brownout, and Californians were told not to charge their electric cars in the evening.[2] He argued that the timing and absurdity of the coincidence "could not happen organically."[2]
