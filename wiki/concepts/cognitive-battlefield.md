# Cognitive Battlefield

The **cognitive battlefield** is the term used in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary for the terrain on which [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] is fought: human perception and cognition itself.[1] In the March 2024 essay "How Your Western Brain Exposes You to 5G Warfare," [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argues that a war composed primarily of weaponized stories and influence operations is by definition cognitive in nature, and that an effective enemy will therefore have studied and exploited the particular weaknesses of the Western brain.[1] His central claim is that "it is our very Western brains that are being operated on," not merely that psychological operations surround us.[1]

## Field, enemy and weapons

Esler insists that the field be distinguished from the enemy and from the weapons.[1] The ambient conditioning produced by [[disembedding-mechanisms|disembedding mechanisms]], industrialization and digital life is, on his account, antecedent to any psychological operation: "you are being severely limited by your modern conditions before you are being operated on psychologically by the enemy."[1] Just as armies survey terrain to establish perimeters and site entrenchments, he argues, citizens should survey their own battlefield in ways suited to its nature, now that the line between citizen and soldier has faded.[1] He characterizes left-hemisphere entrenchment as "the trenches of modern warfare, cognitive trenches."[1]

## Vulnerability of excess certainty

Drawing on *[[the-master-and-his-emissary|The Master and His Emissary]]*, Esler identifies excess certainty as the key vulnerability: habitual reliance on the left hemisphere leads people to avoid uncertainty in order to remain within a comfortable worldview, and this attachment to being sure meets an environment saturated with weaponized narratives designed to manufacture "designer forms of certainty."[1] He asks how much of the current cultural struggle is fallout from the Western neurological condition rather than from targeting — posing the thought experiment of what struggles would remain if every psyop vanished, or if the [[deep-state|Deep State]] were purged entirely today.[1]

## Right-hemisphere countermeasures

The essay closes by listing the capacities Esler regards as the hidden weapons of the citizen-turned-soldier: doubt, uncertainty, intuition, contradiction, metaphors that strive for meaning beyond language, holistic thinking, relationship with the divine, and domination of the [[physical-domain|physical domain]] through embodiment, family, community and local in-person networking.[1] These are hidden, he writes, not by the enemy but by a natural lack of awareness and maturity in a developing technological society, and by "the bribery of convenience and distraction."[1] He warns readers not to underestimate how much harder they become to control by stepping outside as "a divine, intuitive being, comfortable with not knowing what is happening across the world," and asks whether the globalist dream is precisely to capture everyone in the story of a global collective struggle at the expense of local dilemmas.[1]
