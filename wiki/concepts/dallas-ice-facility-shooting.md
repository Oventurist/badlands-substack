# Dallas ICE Facility Shooting

The **Dallas ICE facility shooting** was a sniper attack on an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] facility in Dallas, Texas, on the morning of Wednesday, 24 September 2025, in which one detainee was killed and two others were critically injured.[1]

## The attack

Officials said the facility was "showered with bullets" beginning around 6:20 a.m., when the gunman "fired indiscriminately" at the building, striking a van in the sallyport with detainees inside.[1] No ICE agents were injured in the attack.[1] The suspect was identified as [[joshua-jahn|Joshua Jahn]], a 29-year-old Texan, who was found dead on the roof of a nearby immigration attorney's office with a self-inflicted gunshot wound.[1]

## Investigation

[[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], Director of the [[fbi|FBI]], said on X that one unspent shell casing recovered from the scene had "ANTI ICE" written on it.[1] The exact motive of the shooting remained unclear.[1] Jahn's family struggled to process the news; his brother Noah Jahn told The Independent "I'm still trying to..." before hanging up, and told NBC he did not believe his brother had "strong feelings about ICE."[1]
