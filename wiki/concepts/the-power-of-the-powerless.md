# The Power of the Powerless

*The Power of the Powerless* is a 1978 essay by Czech dissident [[vaclav-havel|Václav Havel]] analyzing how authoritarian systems sustain themselves through mass participation in rituals that participants privately recognize as false.[1]

## Core argument

Havel's thesis, as quoted at Davos, holds that the communist system "did not persist because of violence, alone," but rather "through the participation of ordinary people in rituals they privately knew to be a lie," a condition Havel labeled "living within a lie."[1] The corollary is that "the system's power comes not from its truth, but from everyone's willingness to perform as if it were true."[1]

## Use at Davos 2026

Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] invoked the essay in his address to the January 2026 [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meeting, in the same speech in which he described the breakdown of the [[rules-based-international-order|rules-based international order]] as a "rupture, not a transition."[1] Badlands writers read the citation as an ironic self-indictment of the Davos class, since the same framework applies to the legitimacy crisis facing global institutions.[1]
