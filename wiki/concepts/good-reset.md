# Good Reset

The "Good Reset" is a Badlands analytical framework describing what its authors see as a sovereign, nation-building inversion of the globalist [[great-reset|Great Reset]] — a set of partial nationalizations, reshoring mandates and asset-backed pivots intended to dismantle the fiat facade and foster abundance through decentralized, multipolar trade.[1]

## Origins in the Putin template

In an October 2025 Badlands News Brief analysis, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] traced the model to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s subordination of the [[boris-yeltsin|Yeltsin]]-era oligarchy after 2000: taking state stakes in private companies, especially those pertinent to strategic defense and production.[1] [[yukos|Yukos Oil]] was dismantled through tax-evasion charges and absorbed by [[rosneft|Rosneft]]; [[gazprom|Gazprom]] reclaimed control over natural gas; aerospace and nuclear energy saw outright state takeovers.[1] This de-privatization was framed inside Russia not as communism's return but as the reclamation of national destiny from Western-backed predators.[1]

## Application to Trump and Xi

The analysis argued that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] are not waging economic war against one another but against the [[deep-state|Deep State]], and that Trump is copying a playbook Putin arguably wrote.[1] Sanctions, tariffs and supply-chain chokepoints are described as weapons of the enemy repurposed from globalist tools of control into instruments of liberation.[1]

## Narrative constraints

The "actuals" of the Good Reset are presented as necessary, but the accompanying narratives are described as tightropes: Trump cloaks Putinesque moves in MAGA exceptionalism, Xi in Chinese resilience, and Putin himself in anti-Western defiance, all to avoid the stigma of "socialism" or "authoritarianism" that could unravel carefully cultivated domestic support.[1] Burning Bright concluded by naming Putin a core member — and possibly the unofficial founder — of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], invoking the theme of [[righteous-russia|Righteous Russia]] and pointing readers to the essay [[multipolar-margin-call|Multipolar Margin Call]].[1]

## Earlier usage: reversing the Hegelian trend

The term predates the 2025 economic framing. In his August 2023 essay "BRIC by BRIC," [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used "the 'Good' Reset" to name what he described as the turning of [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian]] narrative techniques back against their originators, citing a longer treatment published at his own Burning Bright Substack.[2] In that usage the Good Reset is primarily a narrative and psychological inversion — the recapture of storytelling power in the [[war-of-stories|War of Stories]] — of which the economic and monetary transition away from the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] is one expression.[2]
