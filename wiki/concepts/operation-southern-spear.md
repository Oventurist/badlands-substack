# Operation Southern Spear

Operation Southern Spear is a United States military operation against alleged "narcoterrorists" in the Western Hemisphere, announced on Thursday, November 13, 2025 by Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] in a post on X.[1]

## Announcement and command structure

Hegseth wrote that the mission would be "led by Joint Task Force Southern Spear and [[southcom|SOUTHCOM]]," and that it "defends our homeland, removes narco-terrorists from our hemisphere, and secures our homeland from the drugs that are killing our people."[1] He added that "the Western Hemisphere is America's neighborhood – and we will protect it."[1]

The announcement came amid sustained tensions with Venezuela and an ongoing campaign of strikes on alleged cartel vessels in international waters of the Caribbean Sea.[1] Hegseth did not specify whether Southern Spear would expand upon those strikes.[1] Since September 2025, the United States had destroyed at least 20 boats in the campaign, killing 80 people.[1] Coverage of the announcement also described the initiative as a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] operation to fight drug traffickers.[1]

## Reaction

Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] denounced the associated strikes in an NBC News interview, calling [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "a barbarian" who "wants to frighten us," and arguing that suspected traffickers "should be seized and detained" under due process rather than killed.[1] Petro said he could not rule out that some vessels were cartel-linked — "maybe or maybe not, we do not know" — but characterized the dead as "poor boatmen" hired by cartels, and had previously said that at least some victims were ordinary fishermen.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed Southern Spear as the operation to "finish off the cartels," completing work begun by [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and Petro, and advised watching for reports of Maduro's "arrest" while questioning whether such an event would be an arrest or an exfiltration.[1] The same commentator read Petro's hostile rhetoric as theater within a dialectic constructed by the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] to cause the cartels to misjudge their position — a pincer movement in which Petro and Maduro appear to oppose Trump while cooperating to corner traffickers in the jungle.[1] Supporting that reading, the commentary noted that Petro had sent diplomats to Qatar in September to negotiate the surrender and disbandment of the [[gulf-clan|Gulf Clan]], Colombia's largest cocaine cartel, and had arrested thousands of cartel figures over the previous year, including Carlos Lehder, [[medellin-cartel|Medellín Cartel]] co-founder and partner of Pablo Escobar.[1]
