# Red-pilling

"Red-pilling" is the term used within the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] milieu for the process by which a person comes to reject the mainstream narrative and adopt a dissident understanding of political and institutional power. The metaphor derives from the 1999 film *The Matrix*, in which the protagonist chooses a red pill to see reality as it is.

In "President Trump's Call to Arms," [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] described red-pilling as accelerating worldwide following the [[2023-trump-cpac-speech|2023 Trump CPAC speech]], writing that "people are being red-pilled across the world, from Twitter to TikTok, at family gatherings, online, offline, and beyond, as the Truth has grown impossible to escape."[1] He argued that concepts "once difficult to comprehend become more and more believable to the masses" as each successive revelation lands, and that claims that were shocking in 2015, 2016 and 2017 had become "accepted fact" by 2023.[1]

Getz offered his own conversion as a case study: a supporter of the Bush family for nearly two decades, he found [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 primary demolition of [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] and the Republican establishment "jarring to behold," but described it as having "laid the groundwork for my true Awakening."[1] He similarly cited a 2017 [[qanon|Q]] suggestion that 70% of elected politicians were corrupt as "a tall statement at the time, but after all we've seen since 2015, easily believable," noting that whereas the public had previously known only of Democratic machinations involving [[barack-obama|Obama]] and [[george-soros|Soros]], Trump had "drew out the 70% like snakes" on the Republican side as well.[1]
