# Parasitic Elite

"Parasitic elite" is the term used by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] in the February 24, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "The Steering Wheel of Civilization" for those who obtain the rewards of civilization without performing a corresponding share of its labor.[1]

## Origin of the term

The essay holds that deceivers and manipulators figured out long ago that they could lie, cheat and otherwise manipulate honest people in order to receive the rewards of others' labor without doing their fair share.[1] Over time these parasites became the tyrannical elites, spending their abundant free time devising ways to control the steering wheel of civilizations so that ordinary people would unwittingly serve a secret agenda.[1]

## Relationship to duty and freedom

Within the framework of [[parallel-duties-and-perpendicular-freedoms|parallel duties and perpendicular freedoms]], the parasitic elite are defined by an imbalanced ratio: they receive far more of civilization's perpendicular freedoms than anyone else while contributing little parallel duty.[1] That surplus of time and focus is said to have allowed them to develop increasingly sophisticated methods of defrauding people of their labor while persuading those people that the arrangement is justifiable or even necessary.[1] Because unfairness is easier to conceal in large groupings than in small ones, the essay argues the parasitic elite favor large stratified populations.[1] It further notes that the monetary system invented by advancing civilizations to measure each member's contribution can be hijacked by this class for unfair advantage.[1]

## Composition and aims

The essay identifies the parasitic elite with the so-called Illuminati, cabal, globalists and [[new-world-order|New World Order]] types, and characterizes their shared psychological profile as that of the [[shadow-men|Shadow Men]].[1] It asserts that their desired ends — including mass genocide and population control, human experimentation, and abuse of children — are not palatable to most sane and normal people, which creates their central problem of how to obtain mass cooperation, answered by mass mind control and propaganda.[1]

Globalists are described as masters of honing personal and social values, using coercion and violence to force their own people into compliance and similar techniques on others to keep their control over society effective for their agendas.[1] The essay states that today's large-scale civilizations were themselves set up and organized by parasitic elites, and frames the problem of keeping everyone working together as common to abject tyrannies such as Nazi Germany and Communist China as well as to freedom-centric civilizations like the United States.[1]
