"SATE nations" is a coinage used in Badlands Media's China series to designate the grouping of Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Egypt, treated as a second bloc of emerging sovereign states alongside the [[brics|BRICS]] nations of Brazil, Russia, India and South Africa.[1]

The December 14, 2022 essay "Mirror, Mirror" invoked the term while arguing that [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s open and strengthening economic and military ties with both the BRICS and SATE groupings were more indicative of a sovereign cooperative he seeks to help forge than of the collectivist ambitions attributed to him by Western commentators.[1] In the same passage the author contrasted these ties with Xi's antagonistic posture toward [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] and the bureaucracy of the [[european-union|European Union]] in Brussels.[1]

Within the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework, the SATE bloc functions as evidence for the "war on the globalists" quadrant: a set of institutional relationships through which nations are said to be decoupling from the collectivist system represented by the American oligarchy and European administrative power.[1]
