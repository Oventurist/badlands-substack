# 2024 Met Gala Pro-Palestine Protest

The **2024 Met Gala pro-Palestine protest** was a demonstration on May 6, 2024 in which more than 1,000 protesters marched through upper Manhattan toward the Metropolitan Museum of Art while it hosted the annual Met Gala, resulting in mass arrests by the [[nypd|NYPD]].[1]

## The march

Demonstrators moved down Fifth Avenue toward the event, blocking traffic.[1] Police intervened at the East 79th Street Transverse at Central Park and began making arrests, according to the New York Post.[1] One officer attempting to control the crowd was heard telling his partner, "This is an exercise in futility at this point. There's nowhere for them to go."[1]

Protesters chanted "Disclose, divest, we will not stop, we will not rest" while waving Palestinian flags and wearing keffiyeh face coverings.[1] The event was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], used the arrests to argue that there are "hard lines for the police" that reveal whose interests are protected: leftist protesters, she wrote, could harass ordinary Americans, occupy buildings, establish autonomous zones, and vandalize property with relative impunity, but engaging "the beautiful people and their decadence display" produced mass arrests.[1] She noted the irony that the demonstrators had been stopped from reaching what she characterized as their most vehement supporters, asking why police needed to be involved between the Gala and Gaza crowds at all, and suggested that if defunding the police remained a goal, "this special event detail is a good place to start."[1]
