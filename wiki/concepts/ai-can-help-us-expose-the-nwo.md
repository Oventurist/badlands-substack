# AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO

"AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO," subtitled "Using the Weapons of the Enemy Against Them," is a Badlands Media essay published on January 22, 2024 by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], with research and editorial assistance from [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]]. The piece documents an extended experiment in which the author fed passages from [[toronto-protocols|the Toronto Protocols]] into [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] and asked the model to analyse them through the framework of military strategy, producing what the article presents as a detailed map of the warfare allegedly being waged by a globalist cabal to install a [[new-world-order|New World Order]]. The post carries Badlands Media's standard disclaimer that articles represent the opinions of contributing authors.

## The epistemological argument

The article opens by defending the value of having an [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] system articulate "the agenda." Its first argument is drawn from epistemology: truth is coherent, so "a properly identified fact in one area of reality should and does correspond to a properly identified fact in another area." The second is that large language models cannot operate in a world of pure fiction, because "all lies are themselves woven with threads of truth"; an intellectually honest and rigorous inquirer will therefore inevitably formulate true statements about a subject, which the article says is "why people wake up at all."

From this the author concludes that the [[cabal|cabal]] cannot truly conceal the truth. Instead it relies on two mechanisms: preventing people from looking at all — through distraction, time-wasting, video games, media consumption and sports — or, when a person does take possession of a piece of truth, using cultural power to make them believe it is not valid. The article identifies this second mechanism as the technique cults use to stop members from placing value on true facts they encounter in ordinary life.

Third, the essay describes the human mind as a "truth discovering machine" that naturally generates accurate impressions of reality. It illustrates this with the example of counting apples on a table, and then invokes the Asch Conformity Experiments to show that when an entire social group insists on a wrong answer, the individual will suppress their true perception in favour of social conformity. The relevance to AI, the author argues, is that even engineers who might wish to build a system serving the cabal's agenda "can't ignore reality itself."

Finally, the article treats the AI's own evasiveness as evidence. That a model asked direct questions about the globalist agenda did not give clear, undistorted answers demonstrates, in the author's reading, two possibilities: that the AI has been manipulated to produce "globalist"-curated answers, and that the curation itself is evidence the truth is obvious to any honest observer, including the machine.

## The Toronto Protocols as input

The author states he has studied [[toronto-protocols|the Toronto Protocols]] for a long time, describing the collection as a candid resource for investigating the warfare waged by what the documents themselves allege is a globalist, satanic cabal. He values them as "a very accurate information set for mapping out our enemy's battle, from a military standpoint."

Originally written in French as "Les Protocoles de Toronto," the documents were leaked by Canadian journalist [[serge-monast|Serge Monast]]. One English translation describes the material as "a document compiled by a clique of senior financiers who met in Canada in 1967 and then in 1985." The article notes that the group identified itself as the "6.6.6.," which is why the author's prompts to the AI were built around the satanic idea of [[satanic-inversion|inversion]]. It also quotes translator Brian Nugent's observation that "Monast himself concluded that it is up to each individual reader to make their own mind up on whether to believe this document, by, he recommends, seeing if it matches the politics, and recent political history, of the last few years." The author reports that the more he has applied this test, the more the documents have proven their accuracy to him.

## Method: strategic, operational and tactical layers

Deschamps explains that one of the simplest ways to understand the architecture of warfare is to examine the strategic, operational and tactical layers of a campaign. Because [[5th-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] is by nature covert and woven into daily life, he found this lens especially practical for uncovering how populations are being attacked. He therefore fed ChatGPT passages from the Protocols and instructed it to explain them using these fundamentals of combat architecture, then refined the outputs using his own understanding of the war and of the adversaries' observed methods.

Reflecting a long-standing focus on what he calls the war on the family, his first prompts drew on the 6.6.6.'s emphasis on the family and its annihilation as a strategic requirement for implementing the New World Order.

## The initial exchange

The opening prompt asked the model to respond "as a General, studying fifth generation warfare," and to break the stated intentions of a quoted passage into Primary Strategic Objectives, Secondary Strategic Objectives, Operations and Tactics. The quoted passage reads:

> "To succeed with certainty to the construction of a World Government, [A New World Order Community] where all individuals without exception, will be subject to the 'World State' of the 'New Order', we must, first, remove the 'Family' (which will, in turn, lead to the loss of ancestral religious teachings), and secondly, levelling all individuals by removing the 'Social Classes', in particular, the 'Middle Classes'. But we must proceed so that all these are seen as being from the popular will, that they have the appearance of 'Democracy'."

ChatGPT prefaced its analysis by calling the rhetoric "a hypothetical scenario rather than a real-world strategy," then supplied the requested breakdown. The primary strategic objective was to establish a world government superseding national governments. The secondary objectives were to eradicate the family structure — weakening social bonds and allegiances not tied to the world government and "making individuals more dependent on the central authority" — to eliminate social classes and especially the middle class, since "middle-class individuals often possess resources and education that could challenge such a system," and to maintain the appearance of democracy, producing "a system that appears to be based on the will of the people while potentially concentrating power in an authoritarian manner."

Under Operations, the model listed psychological manipulation, legal and political manoeuvring, and social engineering. Under Tactics, it listed propaganda, legislation and economic policies. It closed with a caution that the objectives described were highly controversial, raising concerns about authoritarianism, social engineering and the erosion of individual freedoms, and that in reality such a project would meet opposition from those who value democracy, liberty and cultural diversity.

## Reframing as a film script

The author notes that this pattern of disclaimers continued throughout the conversation, with warnings appended at the beginning and end of each response. To remove what he describes as the model's bias toward protecting him from dangerous conspiracy theory, he experimented with framing the exercise as research for a sci-fi dystopian film script. He reports that this produced clearer and, in his view, more truthful results, deepening the accuracy of the mapping.

Believing it was writing a screenplay, ChatGPT invented a new dystopian name for the cabal: the Global Harmony Consortium. Since the Protocols explicitly describe using globalist institutions such as [[united-nations|the United Nations]], the author added the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] to the scenario.

The article then lists the successive refinement prompts. These asked the model to synthesise the warfare identified so far, to supply real-world examples, to incorporate the satanic principle of inversion together with social media monopolies and corporatocracy, to add the WEF, WHO, UN and the [[esg|ESG]] system, to add [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s [[great-reset|Great Reset]], to include the infiltration of public education via [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]], [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and COVID mandates as covert forms of Neo Marxism, to detail ESG scoring within the corporatocracy, to add the climate change narrative as a driver of collectivism and lost national sovereignty, to refocus everything on fifth-generation warfare principles, and finally to fold in the multigenerational model of communist ideological subversion described by Soviet defector [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]].

## The synthesised output

The final and most comprehensive output, which the author notes the AI still labelled "highly speculative," describes the Global Harmony Consortium pursuing a single primary strategic objective: to establish a New World Order, "a unified world government that promotes peace and prosperity while consolidating power and control."

Its secondary objectives, as generated, include weakening traditional family structures by using the principle of inversion to portray them as oppressive and regressive, aided by CRT and Queer Theory challenges to binary gender roles; undermining moral principles and religious values, with ESG scores rewarding organisations that promote secular and globalist values and reinforcing moral relativism; promoting sexual freedom by using the climate narrative to frame overpopulation as a threat and rewarding firms that promote contraception and population control; manipulating the concept of individual rights by using ESG pressure to elevate collective rights and group identity above individual freedoms; influencing women's movements by rewarding organisations that deconstruct traditional family roles; empowering women to break from tradition through workplace incentives and curricula that frame career over family as the path to empowerment; creating conflict between individual and community rights, with COVID mandates presented as necessary for the greater good; and leveraging the climate change narrative to promote collectivism, global governance and the erosion of national sovereignty through international agreements.

At the operational level the output describes fifth-generation warfare conducted through decentralised networks, psychological operations and misinformation campaigns spanning decades; long-term research and development of AI-driven information warfare and opinion-shaping algorithms; the infiltration of academia, media and politics and the shaping of laws and international agreements; the slow transformation of cultural norms through cultural and educational programmes; and collaboration with foreign governments and influential organisations to build a global network.

The tactics listed include generational capture of major media outlets and the marginalisation of critical voices; incremental legislative change and long-term legal warfare eroding sovereignty; patient psychological manipulation integrating CRT, Queer Theory and climate narratives into education; the funding and guidance of activist movements across generations; the staging of high-profile international events that present global governance as inevitable and desirable; and the use of ESG scoring as a warfare tool, with environmental scores driving climate policy and social and governance scores rewarding alignment with Queer Theory and CRT.

## Collective situational awareness

The essay concludes with a section titled "Collective Situational Awareness." Identifying the ways populations are assaulted by fifth-generation warfare waged by what the author calls "an ancient death cult" is complex, he writes, because attacks on families and on independent thought are covert and embedded in daily life. Using AI to assemble the exposed elements of that warfare nevertheless confers a distinct advantage: "We can make visible that which has been designed to remain invisible." It is from this position of collective situational awareness, the article argues, that collective action genuinely informed by what the enemy is trying to hide becomes possible.
