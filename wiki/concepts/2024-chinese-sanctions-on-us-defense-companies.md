# 2024 Chinese Sanctions on US Defense Companies

In January 2024, [[china|China]] announced sanctions on five American defense-related companies in response to United States arms sales to [[taiwan|Taiwan]] and to US sanctions imposed on Chinese companies and individuals.[1]

## Measures

The sanctions, announced on a Sunday by China's Foreign Ministry in an online statement, freeze any property the targeted companies hold in China and prohibit organizations and individuals in China from doing business with them.[1] The five firms named were BAE Systems Land and Armaments, Alliant Techsystems Operations, AeroVironment, Viasat and Data Link Solutions.[1] The practical impact was unclear, since such measures are often largely symbolic — American defense contractors generally do not sell into the Chinese market.[1]

## Context and commentary

The announcement came days before Taiwan's presidential election, with tensions already escalating ahead of the vote.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] argued that tensions in the Taiwan Strait were unlikely to ease regardless of the outcome: an incumbent-party victory would drive anti-China rhetoric upward, while an opposition victory more amenable to Chinese incursions or reunification would produce severe internal conflict within Taiwan's political and military spheres.[1] He further contended that by sanctioning US defense firms and accusing them of obstructing Chinese unification, Beijing was deliberately tying the United States and Western powers into the narrative campaign, consistent with the escalation and acceleration that characterized the [[joe-biden|Biden]] era, and suggested the next "Current Crisis" main event was ramping up.[1]
