# Biological Weapons Convention

The Biological Weapons Convention is described in Badlands commentary as the 1975 instrument that explicitly outlawed the "development, production, acquisition, transfer, stockpiling and use" of an entire category of weapons of mass destruction — the first multilateral treaty to do so.[1]

## Relationship to chemical weapons law

In the account offered by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], the Geneva Protocol of 1925 first outlawed the use of chemical weapons, and the [[chemical-weapons-convention|Chemical Weapons Convention]] of 1997 augmented that prohibition, while development and possession remained lawful until the 1975 convention closed that gap.[1] On that chronology, the United States government's 1980 transfer of chemical weapons to [[saddam-hussein|Saddam Hussein]] for use against Iran during the [[iran-iraq-war|Iran-Iraq War]] constituted a war crime.[1]
