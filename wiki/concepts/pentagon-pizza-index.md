# Pentagon Pizza Index

The "Pentagon pizza index" is an informal open-source-intelligence heuristic holding that spikes in pizza deliveries to restaurants near the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] may signal imminent military operations or late-night crisis sessions at the United States military headquarters.[1]

## Structure and following

The idea circulates largely as an online meme. The X account Pentagon Pizza Report has over 254,300 followers, and a website called Pentagon Pizza Index tracks six pizza shops near the Pentagon using Google Maps activity data.[1]

## Hegseth's response

In a Fox News interview on Sunday, October 5, 2025, US War Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] addressed the meme, saying "I'm aware of that account."[1] Asked by a Fox News reporter whether Pentagon staff had considered simply eating at the cafeteria before an airstrike, Hegseth laughed and replied: "I haven't thought of just going to the cafeteria. I've thought of just ordering lots of pizza on random nights" — that is, deliberately injecting noise into the signal to confuse those tracking delivery patterns.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the response "brilliant" and a demonstration of the Trump administration's evolution in strategic thinking, arguing that citizens should likewise evolve by refusing to wait for "trusted authorities" to validate their theories and by subjecting all ideas — save God — to challenge and scrutiny.[1]
