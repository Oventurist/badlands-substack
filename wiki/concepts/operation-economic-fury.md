# Operation Economic Fury

**Operation Economic Fury** is the name given to a United States financial pressure campaign against [[iran|Iran]] conducted in 2026 by the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|US Department of the Treasury]].[1]

## Sanctions action

Under the campaign, the Treasury imposed sanctions on 35 individuals and entities tied to Iran's "shadow banking" network, a system used to move tens of billions of dollars globally to evade sanctions and fund military and proxy operations.[1] The action targeted shell companies, exchange houses, and front operations, including networks linked to the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]].[1]

## Relationship to the naval blockade

The escalation came alongside the [[naval-blockade-of-iran|US naval blockade of Iran]] initiated in April 2026, which targets shipping and oil exports and has intercepted or redirected dozens of vessels.[1] The combination of financial sanctions and maritime enforcement marked a significant intensification of US economic and military pressure during the [[iran-war|Iran war]].[1] At the same time, diplomatic efforts stalled, with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] rejecting Iran-backed proposals and insisting nuclear issues be addressed immediately as a condition for negotiations.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Trump "is using one cartel to break another," describing the campaign as narrative cover for a deeper operation against the Western hegemon's energy infrastructure and financial rails rather than a simple confrontation with Tehran.[1] In this reading, the real architecture being dismantled is not Iranian but the [[shadow-banking-empire|shadow banking]] mechanism that has served as the plausible-deniability layer of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and the unipolar order.[1] The commentary credited Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] — dubbed "The Wolf of Main Street" — with delivering the sanctions salvo, and characterized the "Iranian" label as a narrative veil making the actual operation politically palatable.[1]
