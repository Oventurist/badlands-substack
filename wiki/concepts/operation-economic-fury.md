# Operation Economic Fury

**Operation Economic Fury** is the name given to the United States pressure campaign, run out of the [[department-of-the-treasury|Department of the Treasury]] under Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]], aimed at severing [[iran|Iran]]'s access to international finance during the 2026 conflict.

## The April 2026 designations

Under the Economic Fury banner, Treasury imposed sanctions on 35 individuals and entities tied to Iran's [[shadow-banking|shadow banking]] network — a system of shell companies, exchange houses and front operations used to move tens of billions of dollars globally, evade sanctions, and fund military and proxy operations, including those of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]].

## Relationship to the naval blockade

The financial campaign runs alongside the [[iranian-blockade|US naval blockade of Iran]] initiated in April 2026, which targets shipping and oil exports and has already intercepted or redirected dozens of vessels. With diplomacy stalled — [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] having rejected Iran-backed proposals and insisted that nuclear issues be addressed immediately as a precondition for negotiations — the combination of sanctions and maritime enforcement marks a significant intensification of pressure on Tehran. Badlands commentary notes that Iran's oil storage facilities are nearing critical mass as exports are choked off.

## Badlands interpretation

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] reads Economic Fury as an operation whose stated target is not its real one: "Trump is using one cartel to break another." On the Narrative level Trump is the Hollywood heel drawing a hard line against the Iranian regime and its nuclear program; on the Actual level, in this reading, he is at war with the Western Hegemon itself — the energy infrastructure and financial rails sustaining the [[deep-state|globalist machine]]. Because shell companies and plausible-deniability structures are the feature rather than the bug of that architecture, squeezing nodes labelled "Iranian" is said to expose and sever the broader financial sinews of the unipolar order. Burning Bright, who nicknamed Bessent "The Wolf of Main Street" in 2025, treats the word "proxy" as the key term in the Treasury release: the network being dismantled, in his account, predates and exceeds Iran, tracing to Prussian origins fused with British imperial precedent.
