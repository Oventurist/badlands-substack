# Ghosts in the Machine

"Ghosts in the Machine" is the title of a recruiting video released in 2022 by the United States Army's [[4th-psychological-operations-group|4th Psychological Operations Group]], a unit that describes itself as the "Masters of Influence" and as practitioners of information warfare.[1] The highly controversial clip went viral immediately upon release.[1]

## Content and reception

The video was crafted to look like a psychological thriller, complete with unsettling imagery and references to real-life uprisings.[1] Described as designed to target psychological vulnerabilities and create confusion in adversary organizations, it brazenly asked the question: "Who's pulling the strings?"[1] Reporting characterized it as a direct call to join an elite, secretive team functioning as a puppeteer, controlling the actions of hot-button national security threats such as the Chinese and Russian governments, and confirming that "the war of tomorrow is fought entirely within the human mind."[1]

Coverage of the clip has been recycled under headlines such as "The Ghost Puppeteers: US Army's Secret Video Confirms Who's Pulling the World's Strings."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GMONEY highlighted the appearance of a Pepe image on the "KOKO the Clown" figure in the video, visible at roughly the 57-second mark of the 1:17-long clip, and connected it to a Trump Bitcoin post.[1] GMONEY also riffed on the video's tagline "Words are our weapon" as an allusion to a cryptocurrency seed phrase, and asserted that "Bitcoin is Digital 1776."[1]
