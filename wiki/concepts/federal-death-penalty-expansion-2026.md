# Federal Death Penalty Expansion (2026)

The 2026 federal death penalty expansion refers to an announcement by the U.S. [[justice-department|Justice Department]] that it was broadening federal execution procedures to include firing squads, as part of a wider effort to strengthen and accelerate use of the federal death penalty.[1] The department framed the action as restoring its duty to carry out lawful capital sentences once condemned inmates have exhausted their appeals.[1]

## Contents of the directive

The Justice Department said it directed the [[federal-bureau-of-prisons|Federal Bureau of Prisons]] to broaden execution protocols to allow additional methods, including the firing squad, and to readopt the lethal injection protocol used during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first administration.[1] It also said internal processes would be streamlined to expedite capital cases.[1] Reuters reported that the administration also intends to add electrocution and gas asphyxiation as alternative federal execution methods.[1]

According to the Associated Press, the move marks the first explicit authorization of firing squads by the federal government.[1] AP also reported that three inmates remain on federal death row after former President Joe Biden commuted 37 death sentences.[1] Quoting Fox News, Badlands noted that the DOJ is additionally directing the Bureau of Prisons to look into expanding federal death row and constructing additional execution facilities.[1]

## Badlands commentary

She also drew a personal contrast, noting that four years earlier the government had sought to strip her co-defendant Col. Shawn Smith (USAF, Ret.) of his rights for opining that people involved in election fraud "deserve to hang," a statement later folded into a civil suit against them — while now the government itself is expanding execution methods and lawmakers are openly advocating hangings.[1] She concluded that even if justice never fully arrives, "the narrative of justice is changing," that a golden age cannot exist without a justice phase, and that the essential condition is due process.[1] She reiterated her position that globalism can constitute treason depending on the facts and conduct, and closed by quoting Ecclesiastes 8:11.[1]
