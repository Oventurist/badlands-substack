# Pepe the Frog

**Pepe the Frog** is an internet cartoon character that became a central meme of the 2016 United States presidential campaign and, in Badlands Media commentary, a symbol woven into a wider interpretive framework linking [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], [[qanon|Q]], [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], and [[covfefe|Covfefe]].[1]

## The 2016 sequence

The Badlands Brief article "Pepe, the Trump Train, and Hillary's Automobile" presents a dated timeline of the meme's entry into mainstream politics.[1] It begins on 10/13/15, when Trump posted an image of himself as Pepe the Frog at 1:53 AM.[1] On 9/9/16 [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] delivered her [[basket-of-deplorables|"basket of deplorables"]] remark, describing half of Trump's supporters as racist, sexist, homophobic, xenophobic and Islamophobic.[1] The same day, an anonymous party identified only as "Mike" minted the [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]] token on [[counterparty|Counterparty]].[1]

On 9/10/16 [[donald-trump-jr|Don Jr]] posted "The Deplorables" meme, a movie-poster parody featuring Trump-as-Pepe among the campaign's figures.[1] On 9/11/16 Clinton appeared to faint during what was described as a "medical episode," an event the article annotates simply with "KEK."[1] On 9/12/16 the Clinton campaign published an explainer on her official website attacking Pepe and associating the meme with white supremacists.[1]

## The Trump Train link

The article's central claim is that the [[trump-train|Trump Train]] and Pepe the Frog are directly connected, evidenced by a [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] Tweet of 10/9/16 that mentions the Trump Train, quotes Trump's reply to the deplorables remark, and states "WE LOVE OUR DEPLORABLES!!!"[1] A closing section observes that RAREPEPE was minted thirty days before that Tweet, and compares the interval "[30 days]" against Q drop #36 of 11/2/17.[1] The PEPETRAIN token, another Counterparty-issued Rare Pepe card, is presented as a further convergence of the two symbols, its series and supply figures said to encode the Covfefe signal and its card number #47 read as pointing to 11/5/24.[1]

## Cultural register

The piece is written in the idiom of Pepe-adjacent internet culture, addressing readers as residents of Kekistan requiring a "PEPEPASSPORT," signing off with "Shadilay," and inviting VIPs to "scream PEPE at the top of your lungs" for another cup of Covfefe.[1] It describes the exploration of interconnections between Q, Trump, Bitcoin, Pepe, John F. Kennedy, and Covfefe as "infinitely deep."[1]
