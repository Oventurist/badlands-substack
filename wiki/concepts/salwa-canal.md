# Salwa Canal

The Salwa Canal was a proposed Saudi waterway along the land border with [[qatar|Qatar]], announced during the 2017 Gulf crisis as an escalation of the Saudi-led embargo.[1]

## Origins

After being elevated to Crown Prince in June 2017, [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] launched an embargo against Qatar backed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Emirati President [[mohammed-bin-zayed|Mohammed bin Zayed]], along with Jordan, Egypt, Yemen, Bahrain, and several African nations.[1] Having closed the border and effectively turned the peninsula into an island, MBS then sought to make it a literal island by announcing his intention to construct — or expand an existing — canal along the frontier, issuing a Request for Proposal to regional contractors.[1]

## Rationale and abandonment

Badlands Media reported MBS's stated motive as a belief, shared with Trump, that Qatar was a terrorist haven allowing the [[cia|CIA]] and ISIS to conduct psychological warfare operations through outlets such as [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]].[1] The plan was described as an attempt "to wrap his diplomatic fingers around Qatar's peninsular neck and choke the life out of it," with the net effect of driving Qatar deeper into relations with [[iran|Iran]], a country with which it had also been feuding.[1] MBS ultimately abandoned the canal project, though the embargo itself persisted until January 4, 2021, when Trump negotiated a reconciliation two weeks before leaving office.[1]
