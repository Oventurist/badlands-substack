# 2025 Fourth of July Event Cancellations

In late June 2025, growing concerns over federal immigration raids led several cities and communities across Los Angeles County to cancel or postpone their Fourth of July celebrations.[1]

## Scope

Events that typically draw thousands of families — complete with fireworks shows, concerts, and parades — were scaled back or called off entirely out of what organizers described as an abundance of caution.[1] In the preceding weeks, federal immigration raids had ramped up across California, prompting protests and, at times, clashes with law enforcement.[1] Newsweek produced a map showing where Independence Day festivities had been affected by immigration enforcement concerns.[1]

## Competing patriotism commentary

The cancellations coincided with a broader argument over the meaning of patriotism heading into the holiday. A USA Today opinion column argued that with an unpopular president behaving in what the author called myriad un-American ways, Americans should question whether patriotism means saluting the flag and respecting the office of the presidency while looking past the actions of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his administration.[1] The column asserted that Trump's second term had shown that fealty to him was all that mattered, citing congressional allies proposing bills to put his face on currency or Mount Rushmore.[1]

Badlands contributors responded in the opposite direction. One urged readers to remember the foundation on which the country was built — "an eternal God."[1] Another, Ashe in America, argued it was absurd to cancel Independence Day festivities for citizens because a city was so overrun with illegal immigrants that celebrations would draw immigration raids, and questioned the premise that raids are undesirable, noting that [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] Denver was arresting child molesters and murderers nearly every day.[1] She contended Los Angeles, being larger and in a border state, likely held far more such offenders as well as trafficked children and indentured women, and asked why Democratic city leaders were protecting them.[1] She pointed to [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]]'s April remarks describing what Democratic immigration policies did to a 14-year-old girl, blaming the Biden administration and calling for accountability for the officials who crafted and executed those policies.[1] She dismissed the USA Today column as Trump Derangement Syndrome, calling America amazing and exceptional as the nation entered its 250th year.[1]
