# Canola Oil

Canola oil is a highly processed seed oil developed in Canada, its name derived from the phrase "Canadian Oil, Low Acid."[1] It occupies an unusual position at the intersection of North American trade politics and the American public-health debate.[1]

## Health controversy

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described canola oil as a highly processed seed oil linked to chronic inflammation, obesity and other health effects she associated with the broader American public health crisis.[1] She framed Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s campaign against highly processed seed oils as, in effect, an economic war on Canada, and endorsed it.[1]

## Trade dimension

Canada is the largest global exporter of canola, accounting for an estimated 75 to 80 percent of world supply.[1] Canola featured directly in the preliminary Canada–China arrangement announced in January 2026, which paired reduced Canadian duties on Chinese electric vehicles with lower Chinese tariffs on Canadian agricultural exports including canola.[1] That arrangement in turn triggered President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s threat of a 100 percent tariff on Canadian imports should Ottawa move to a full free trade agreement with Beijing.[1]
