# 2023 Vilnius NATO Summit

The **2023 Vilnius NATO summit** was a gathering of [[nato|NATO]] leaders in Vilnius, Lithuania, in July 2023, dominated by the question of Ukraine's bid for alliance membership.[1]

## Zelensky's outburst

Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskiy]] was "running hot" ahead of his sit-down with NATO leaders on Tuesday evening, having been angered earlier in the day by what he called an "absurd" reluctance to give his country a clear timeline on membership.[1] The outburst, delivered on social media without advance warning to the United States, riled partners that had funneled billions of dollars of weaponry and aid into Ukraine's defense against the Russian invasion.[1] UK Defense Secretary [[ben-wallace|Ben Wallace]] responded the following morning that "whether we like it or not, people want to see gratitude."[1]

## Threading the needle

Reporting based on interviews with more than a dozen diplomats and officials involved in the summit described leaders attempting to thread a needle on Ukraine's membership bid: seeking language that looked like progress, that Ukraine could sell as progress, but that fundamentally left the alliance no closer to being dragged into a war with nuclear-armed Russia.[1]
