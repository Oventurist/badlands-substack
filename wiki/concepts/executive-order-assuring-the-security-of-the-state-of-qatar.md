# Executive Order "Assuring the Security of the State of Qatar"

The executive order titled "ASSURING THE SECURITY OF THE STATE OF QATAR" is a U.S. presidential directive signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] pledging to use all measures — including U.S. military action — to defend the energy-rich state of [[qatar|Qatar]].[1] The text of the order became available on the [[white-house|White House]] website on Wednesday, 1 October 2025, though it was dated the preceding Monday.[1]

## Content

The order cites the two countries' "close cooperation" and "shared interest," and vows to "guarantee the security and territorial integrity of the state of Qatar against external attack."[1] It states that "the United States shall regard any armed attack on the territory, sovereignty or critical infrastructure of the state of Qatar as a threat to the peace and security of the United States," and that in the event of such an attack "the United States shall take all lawful and appropriate measures — including diplomatic, economic, and, if necessary, military — to defend the interests of the United States and of the state of Qatar and to restore peace and stability."[1] Reporting noted that it remained unclear what practical weight the pledge would carry.[1]

## Context

The order appeared to be a further measure by Trump to reassure the Qataris following [[israel|Israel]]'s surprise strike on Qatari territory targeting [[hamas|Hamas]] leaders as they weighed accepting a ceasefire with Israel over the war in the Gaza Strip.[1] It apparently came during a visit to Washington on Monday by Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]; Trump organized a call by Netanyahu to Qatar during that visit in which Netanyahu "expressed his deep regret" over the strike, which killed six people including a member of the Qatari security forces.[1]
