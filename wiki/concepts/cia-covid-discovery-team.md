# CIA COVID Discovery Team

The COVID Discovery Team was an internal [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] analytic group convened to assess the origins of SARS-CoV-2. It became the subject of congressional scrutiny in September 2023 after whistleblower testimony alleged that the agency had offered payments to team members to change their conclusions.[1]

## Whistleblower allegations

According to whistleblower testimony provided to Congress and reported on 12 September 2023, a senior-level CIA officer told House committee leaders that the agency had tried to pay off six analysts who found that SARS-CoV-2 likely originated in a laboratory in Wuhan, China, on condition that they revise their position to say the virus had jumped from animals to humans.[1] The panel chairmen summarised the whistleblower's account: "According to the whistleblower, at the end of its review, six of the seven members of the Team believed the intelligence and science were sufficient to make a low confidence assessment that COVID-19 originated from a laboratory in Wuhan, China."[1]

## Congressional response

Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic chairman [[brad-wenstrup|Brad Wenstrup]] and House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence chairman [[mike-turner|Mike Turner]], both Ohio Republicans, sent a letter dated 12 September 2023 to CIA Director [[william-burns|William Burns]] requesting all documents, communications and pay information relating to the COVID Discovery Team by 26 September.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary framed the disclosure as vindication for a hypothesis that had once been treated as a banned conspiracy theory, observing that the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]]'s suppression now appeared to have been purchased.[1] The writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] added that the more surprising feature was that the testimony was public at all, since "we never get this much information about the CIA," and pressed the further question of whether the event was "not a leak, but a launch," urging mainstream examination of Event 201.[1]
