# New Jersey Executive Order No. 12

Executive Order No. 12 is a [[new-jersey|New Jersey]] sanctuary directive issued by Gov. [[mikie-sherrill|Mikie Sherrill]] that restricts federal immigration enforcement inside certain state-controlled facilities.[1]

The order bars [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] from conducting secure arrests of criminal illegal aliens in nonpublic areas of state property, including correctional facilities.[1]

## Federal lawsuit

The [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] filed suit on Monday, February 23, 2026, against New Jersey and Governor Sherrill challenging the order. Federal officials argued that the policy obstructs immigration enforcement and interferes with efforts to detain and remove individuals already in custody. The complaint contends that the order results in the release of individuals otherwise eligible for removal, including illegal aliens convicted of aggravated assault, burglary, and drug and human trafficking offenses.[1]
