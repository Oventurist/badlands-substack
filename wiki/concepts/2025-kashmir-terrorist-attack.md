# 2025 Kashmir Terrorist Attack

The 2025 Kashmir terrorist attack was an assault in southern, India-administered Kashmir in April 2025 that killed 26 people — most of them tourists, including a visitor from Nepal — and injured dozens more.[1]

## Indian response

Speaking at a rally in the state of Bihar on Thursday, April 24, 2025, Indian Prime Minister [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] condemned the assault and vowed a firm response.[1] "I say to the whole world, India will identify, trace, and punish every terrorist and their backers. We will pursue them to the ends of the Earth," he said, adding that "every effort will be made to ensure that justice is done."[1] Modi also called for national unity, stating that "the entire nation is firm in this resolve" and that "India's spirit will never be broken by terrorism."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] speculated that the attack was the work of [[isis-k|ISIS-K]], recalling that the group carried out the Abbey Gate bombing at Kabul Airport during the Afghanistan withdrawal, killing 13 U.S. soldiers, and was subsequently hunted by the Taliban in line with commitments made to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] under the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] to rid Afghanistan of what the commentary called State-Department-funded terrorists.[1] The writer named "bad actors in the US [[state-department|State Department]] (the [[cia|CIA]])" as suspected backers.[1]

The commentary emphasized Kashmir's strategic position at the intersection of India, [[pakistan|Pakistan]] and China, noting that its western border lies less than 60 miles from Islamabad and about 150 miles from the Afghan border, and describing it as one of the most disputed regions in history.[1]
