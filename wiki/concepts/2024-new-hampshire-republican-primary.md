# 2024 New Hampshire Republican Primary

The 2024 New Hampshire Republican presidential primary, held on Tuesday, January 23, 2024, was the second nominating contest of the Republican presidential cycle and resulted in a victory for former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], his last remaining major rival for the nomination.[1]

## Result

Fox News projected Trump the winner just minutes after the final polls closed in New Hampshire.[1] The win came eight days after Trump crushed the field in Iowa's low-turnout Republican caucuses.[1] "I'm very honored by the result," Trump said in a statement to Fox News Digital.[1] Speaking to supporters in Concord, Haley conceded the night's contest, saying, "I want to congratulate Donald Trump on his victory tonight. He earned it and I want to acknowledge that."[1]

## Commentary

[[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], argued that the New Hampshire primary functions structurally as an establishment corrective to Iowa: because the GOP establishment historically permits Democrats to vote in the New Hampshire primary, the state can be used to reverse the "much-harder-to-rig" results of the Iowa caucuses.[1] In his framing, the resulting narrative casts Iowa's outcome as the product of "wacky Evangelicals," allows the establishment to claim a New Hampshire win for a RINO, and manufactures momentum ahead of larger contests such as South Carolina.[1] He concluded that "elections are rigged every cycle, everywhere."[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] extended that reading through a Net Effects lens, describing Trump as a cypher through which systemic patterns are exposed to the "Normie hivemind" of the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]].[1] He argued that Trump was using the concessions of intra-GOP rivals as stepping stones toward the appearance of a mandate while [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] continued to falter in the War of Stories, and suggested that the momentum Trump had accumulated as early as January of the cycle amounted to a bold bluff designed to provoke the System into shedding "the last vestiges of its legitimacy mask" through an even more transparent subversion of the democratic process.[1]
