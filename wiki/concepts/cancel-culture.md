# Cancel Culture

Cancel culture describes the practice of destroying a person's reputation or employment over expressed opinions, and was a principal grievance of the American right during and after the 2016–2020 period.[1] Figures such as [[bari-weiss|Bari Weiss]], [[dave-rubin|Dave Rubin]] and [[christopher-rufo|Christopher Rufo]], along with outlets including the [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]] and the Babylon Bee, billed themselves as opposed to cancel culture and campaigned against the policing of public discourse, particularly when conservatives were fired for their beliefs.[1]

## Illustrative cases

Rufo tweeted on December 20, 2021 in defense of Tony Kinnett, an Indianapolis teacher fired after posting a video about critical race theory in public schools, calling the firing "outrageous."[1] In February 2021 the Daily Wire's Tim Pierce quoted [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] on the firing of actress [[gina-carano|Gina Carano]] by Disney, with Shapiro calling it "indicative of where we are in the culture" and part of "a mass social movement to expel half of the American population from the body politic."[1] Weiss, writing in Commentary Magazine in 2021 under the title "We Got Here Because of Cowardice. We Get Out With Courage," argued that persuasion had been replaced with public shaming, debate with de-platforming, and forgiveness with punishment.[1]

## The reversal

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argues these positions were abandoned after October 7, 2023, when Americans including [[maura-finkelstein|Maura Finkelstein]], nurse Hesen Jabr, and dental hygienist Nikki Noor Aytoglu lost their jobs over statements about Israel and Jews without any comparable objection from right-wing free-speech advocates.[1] The advocacy group [[stopantisemitism|StopAntisemitism]] was the primary force pressuring those employers, and [[chaya-raichik|Chaya Raichik]] amplified its posts with comments such as "Consequences for hate are necessary" — despite having toured the media warning of the dangers of doxxing after being doxxed herself.[1]
