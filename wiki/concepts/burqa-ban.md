# Burqa Ban

A burqa ban is legislation prohibiting the wearing of Islamic full-face or full-body coverings in public spaces.[1] In October 2025 [[italy|Italy]]'s ruling [[brothers-of-italy|Brothers of Italy]] party announced plans for a nationwide ban framed as a bill against "Islamic separatism."[1]

## Italian proposal

The burqa is a full-body garment covering a woman from head to toe and includes a mesh screen over the eyes; a niqab leaves the area around the eyes clear.[1] The proposed Italian ban would prohibit the wearing of garments covering the face in all public spaces, including shops, schools and offices, with violators subject to fines of €300 to €3,000.[1]

Lawmaker [[andrea-delmastro|Andrea Delmastro]], one of the bill's initiators, wrote in a Facebook post on Wednesday, October 8, 2025: "Religious freedom is sacred, but it must be exercised in the open, in full respect of our constitution and the principles of the Italian state."[1]

The measure forms part of a broader legislative package intended to address what the right-wing party of Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] has described as "cultural separatism" associated with Islam.[1] The same package also targets mosque funding.[1]
