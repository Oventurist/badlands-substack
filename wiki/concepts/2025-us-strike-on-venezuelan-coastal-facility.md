# 2025 US Strike on Venezuelan Coastal Facility

The 2025 US strike on a Venezuelan coastal facility was a military action announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in late December 2025, in which he said American forces destroyed a "big facility" along the shore of [[venezuela|Venezuela]] — apparently the first known US land strike inside the country.[1]

## Announcement

Trump first mentioned the action on Friday, December 26, 2025, in an interview on WABC radio, saying, "We just knocked out… a big plant or big facility where the ships come from. So we hit them very hard."[1] Speaking at Mar-a-Lago alongside Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] on Monday, December 29, he described the target as an "implementation" area allegedly linked to drug trafficking, adding that "There was a major explosion in the dock area where they load the boats up with drugs."[1] Details surrounding the operation remained scarce.[1]

## Commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], writing for the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, juxtaposed the strike announcement with contemporaneous footage of Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]'s activities and concluded that it was "hard to take any of this seriously."[1] He grouped the Venezuela bombing with an alleged assassination attempt on [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and threats of war against [[iran|Iran]] as evidence of a foreign policy at odds with America First principles.[1]

## CNN attribution to the CIA

In a report published December 29, 2025, CNN said sources familiar with the matter had confirmed that the [[cia|CIA]] carried out the drone strike on a port facility on the Venezuelan coast earlier that month, marking the first known US attack on a target inside [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[2] The strike targeted a remote dock that the US government believed was being used by the Venezuelan gang [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] to store drugs and load them onto boats; no one was present at the time and there were no casualties.[2] Two sources said US Special Operations Forces provided intelligence support, but Col. Allie Weiskopf, a spokesperson for [[united-states-special-operations-command|US Special Operations Command]], denied it, saying, "Special Operations did not support this operation to include intel support."[2]

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] was dismissive, noting that although CNN claimed the details had not previously been reported, "the details still are not reported" and the story amounted to "we're told something happened."[2]
