# RAREPEPE

RAREPEPE is described in Badlands Media commentary as the first Pepe non-fungible token minted on [[concepts/bitcoin|Bitcoin]], created on September 9, 2016 by a party who has remained anonymous.[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

A Badlands Brief essay on the fifteenth anniversary of the [[concepts/bitcoin-genesis-block|Genesis Block]] noted that [[concepts/qanon|Q]] drops #3716 and #3717 were posted at the same moment with identical text, and that the resulting duplication of the "Marker [9]" line could be read as [9][9] — the date 9/9 — which the author connected to RAREPEPE's minting date.[1] The essay observed that Q, [[entities/satoshi-nakamoto|Satoshi Nakamoto]], and "Mike" are all anonymous figures, concluding with the characteristic aside "probably nothing."[1]

Readers interested in the wider web of associations between Q, [[entities/donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Bitcoin, Pepe, and John F. Kennedy were directed to the COVFEFE Dig authored by [[entities/gmoney|GMONEY]] for Badlands Media.[1]

## The 9/9/16 minting and the deplorables timeline

The Badlands Brief article "Pepe, the Trump Train, and Hillary's Automobile" places the RAREPEPE mint on 9 September 2016 — the same day [[entities/hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] delivered her [[concepts/basket-of-deplorables|"basket of deplorables"]] remark — within a tight sequence of events that also includes [[entities/donald-trump-jr|Don Jr]]'s "The Deplorables" meme on 9/10/16, Clinton's apparent fainting episode on 9/11/16, and her campaign website's attack on [[concepts/pepe-the-frog|Pepe the Frog]] on 9/12/16.[2] The token was issued on [[entities/counterparty|Counterparty]], the second-layer protocol on top of Bitcoin, and its transaction record is cited directly.[2]

A closing "dasting dig" observes that RAREPEPE was minted exactly thirty days before the [[entities/dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] Tweet of 10/9/16 that tied the [[concepts/trump-train|Trump Train]] to the deplorables meme, and compares "[30 days]" against Q drop #36 of 11/2/17 — a date read as 11 11, and therefore as a [[concepts/covfefe|Covfefe]] connection because 11/11 is the 315th day of a non-leap year.[2] The related PEPETRAIN token is highlighted for series and supply figures said to encode the same Covfefe signal, and for a card number of #47 read as pointing to 11/5/24.[2]
