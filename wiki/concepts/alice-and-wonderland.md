# Alice & Wonderland

"Alice & Wonderland" is one of the recurring code phrases, or "comms," used in the [[qanon|Q]] drops of late 2017. In the drops covered by Chapter 11 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series — drops #81 through #90, all posted on November 5, 2017 — Q used the phrase repeatedly alongside references to [[hillary-clinton|HRC]] and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[1]

## Decoding in the drops

Drop #82 stated that things must be solved before readers can understand what is going to happen, and pointed again toward HRC and Saudi Arabia, framing the subject as "Saudi Arabia — The Bloody Wonderland."[1] Drop #83 repeated the prior drop's material deliberately, as a teaching exercise: Q broke the comm down to its simplest form so that readers could draw the correlation directly — Alice corresponds to Hillary Clinton, and Wonderland corresponds to Saudi Arabia.[1]

Chapter 11's author argues that the correlation is apt in a second sense as well: both are "very deep rabbit holes," and researchers who begin digging into Clinton and the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] quickly encounter allegations of human rights violations and war crimes connected to Saudi Arabia.[1] Drop #86, in which Q commended an anon for locating a book whose closing pages compiled Clinton quotations, was read the same way — partly as a pointer to Clinton's hypocrisy, and partly as a reminder that the Hillary–Saudi Arabia thread demands sustained research.[1]

## Related comms

Alice & Wonderland appeared in the same cluster of drops as other Q signatures, including [[snow-white-supercomputers|Snow White]], "Wizards & Warlocks," and "Godfather III."[1] Drop #87 stated that Q's closing signatures were themselves nods toward incoming events, lending weight to the [[future-proves-past|future proves past]] refrain.[1]
