# 16-Year Plan to Destroy America

The **16-year plan to destroy America** is a recurring claim in the [[qanon|Q]] drops holding that a two-term presidency of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] followed by a two-term presidency of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was intended to constitute a sixteen-year programme for dismantling the United States from within.[1]

Chapter 3 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]], discussing drop #15 of October 31, 2017, presents the plan as the frame that explains the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s reaction to the 2016 election.[1] The author writes that the Deep State "never thought they would lose the Presidency again" as it had with [[john-f-kennedy|JFK]] or [[ronald-reagan|Reagan]], that Hillary Clinton was supposed to win, and that [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s election "threw a wrench" in the sixteen-year plan.[1]

The same drop is read as conveying several related assurances: that there are more good people than bad, a point the author stresses because it is easy to be drawn into believing that everyone in government is corrupt and that nobody is fighting for good.[1] He cites the number of people within government who have since spoken out as evidence that Q was not lying on this point.[1] The drop is also read as referencing the toppling of a family — identified by the author as the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]] — as flagging [[paul-manafort|Paul Manafort]] and other "plants" allegedly inserted into Trump's team to sabotage or frame him, and as indicating that [[george-soros|Soros]] was targeted.[1]

The author further connects the drop to the wave of congressmen and women who resigned or declined to seek re-election in that period, arguing that Q told readers those decisions were not their own and were part of the "cleaning" Q described.[1]

## The nuclear-war endgame

In "We Are Watching a Movie," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] restates the plan in its starkest form, drawing on Q drop #570 of January 21, 2018: eight years of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] as president meant to degrade the country and the constitution, in preparation for eight years of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and World War III.[2] The plan, he argues, was not only to destroy America but also Russia.[2]

Lange contends the war would have been a controlled nuclear exchange eliminating millions of people in order to make the world's remaining population more governable by the puppet masters, after which neither the United States nor Russia would remain a superpower.[2] Eight years of Obama was bad enough, he writes, but the country would not have survived eight years of Clinton - which in his account is why the military was prepared to do the unthinkable to prevent her becoming president.[2] He identifies the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] and the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran nuclear deal]] as the operational mechanism, and credits [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with preventing the war via the [[2018-us-strikes-on-syria|April 2018 strikes on Syria]].[2] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
