# FY2027 National Defense Authorization Act

The FY2027 National Defense Authorization Act is the annual defense policy bill for fiscal year 2027, a specific instance of the recurring [[national-defense-authorization-act|National Defense Authorization Act]]. In July 2026 the [[us-house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] passed the measure, which includes provisions to expand cooperation between the United States and [[israel|Israel]] on defense technology and industrial production.[1]

## Israel technology and supply-chain provisions

The bill directs the Defense Department to explore greater integration of certain defense supply chains, joint research and development, and expanded collaboration on military technologies with Israel.[1] It also includes funding for existing US-Israel missile defense programs and other security initiatives.[1] After House passage the legislation moved to the [[united-states-senate|Senate]], where lawmakers will consider their own version before any final bill can be sent to the president.[1]

## Badlands reaction

Commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the measure as "subversive treason," arguing that there is no reason the US military should ever merge with a foreign power and that supporting such integration amounts to placing a foreign power above the United States.[1] He called for everyone who voted for the bill — and, more broadly, everyone in [[congress|Congress]] — to be investigated for foreign ties, while cautioning that speculation about the bill's passage serving as a predicate to shut down and investigate Congress was likely "hopium."[1]

The commentary also criticized what it described as a childish assumption within the movement that because [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is president, Republican legislators must be earnest patriots, and closed with the formulation that "America First doesn't mean Israel First."[1]
