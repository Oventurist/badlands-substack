# 2023 House GOP January 6 Security Report

The 2023 House GOP January 6 security report was an investigative report released in early June 2023 by a group of Republican members of the United States House of Representatives, presenting emails and text messages indicating that the office of House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] was directly involved in the creation and editing of the Capitol security plan that failed during the January 6, 2021 riot.[1]

## Authorship and scope

The report was compiled by Representatives [[rodney-davis|Rodney Davis]], [[jim-banks|Jim Banks]], [[troy-nehls|Troy Nehls]], [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] and [[kelly-armstrong|Kelly Armstrong]], and reflected months of investigation into evidence the lawmakers said had been ignored by the Democrat-led [[january-6-committee|January 6 committee]].[1] House Minority Leader [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] had authorized the members to conduct their own probe.[1] The internal communications were made public on a Wednesday in the week preceding June 5, 2023.[1]

## Findings

The report concluded that the Capitol was left vulnerable on January 6 as a result of failures by Democratic leadership in the House and by law enforcement leaders in the [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]], who allowed concerns about the "optics" of visible armed officers and National Guardsmen to override the need for enhanced security.[1] Security officials later stated that they had been "denied again and again" the resources needed to protect the Capitol.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that every successive report on January 6 reveals further deception and coverup, describing the accumulated revelations as a damning indictment of officials in the Capitol.[1] The commentary cited the pre-event [[january-5-2021-pipe-bombs|pipe bomb]] incidents, the withholding of information about their perpetrator by the Biden Justice Department and FBI, the Capitol Police escort of [[jacob-chansley|Jacob Chansley]], the fabricated narrative around [[brian-sicknick|Brian Sicknick]]'s death, the killing of an unarmed [[ashli-babbitt|Ashli Babbitt]], the accidental gassing of officers and peaceful protesters that preceded the breach, the presence of federal agent provocateurs in MAGA attire, and federal protection of [[ray-epps|Ray Epps]].[1]

The piece concluded that Pelosi and the wider Washington establishment were the organizers of what it called a fake insurrection, that reliance on elected representatives has produced reports without accountability, that January 6 prisoners remain unjustly jailed to prop up the government's narrative, and that leaders continue advancing the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and the [[new-world-order|New World Order]] — with the oath of office reduced to "meaningless theatre."[1]
