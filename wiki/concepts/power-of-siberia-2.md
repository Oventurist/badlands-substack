# Power of Siberia 2

Power of Siberia 2 is a long-discussed natural gas pipeline project intended to carry Russian gas from Siberia to [[china|China]], seen by Moscow as critical for redirecting energy exports away from Europe. In the Badlands corpus it appears in the May 2026 reporting on the [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]–[[xi-jinping|Xi]] summit in Beijing.

## The Beijing summit setback

At the May 2026 summit where the two leaders signed the Multipolar World Declaration, Putin reportedly failed to secure final Chinese approval for the pipeline. Pricing and long-term dependency concerns reportedly remained unresolved, with China wary of becoming over-reliant on Russian gas. The episode was highlighted in the Badlands Brief as exposing limits in the Russia-China partnership even as the two countries signed more than 40 agreements covering trade, technology, energy, media cooperation, and strategic coordination.
