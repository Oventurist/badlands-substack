# Power of Siberia 2

Power of Siberia 2 is a long-discussed natural gas pipeline project intended to carry Russian gas to China, which Moscow views as critical for redirecting energy exports away from Europe.[1]

At the May 2026 Beijing summit, [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] reportedly failed to secure final Chinese approval for the project despite the meeting producing more than 40 agreements covering trade, technology, energy, media cooperation, and strategic coordination.[1] Pricing and long-term dependency concerns reportedly remained unresolved, and the impasse was cited as evidence of the limits of the Russia–China partnership even as the two governments signed a joint declaration calling for a [[multipolar-world|multipolar world]].[1]
