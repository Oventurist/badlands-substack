# Coinage Act of 1873

The **Coinage Act of 1873** ended the coinage of silver and placed the American economy on the [[gold-standard|gold standard]] for the first time.[1]

According to Badlands Media, the Act caused deflation, which increased the buying power of America's currency and benefited ordinary citizens.[1] Bankers, by contrast, are said to have hated the gold standard so much after 1873 that they began calling for the government to permanently adopt a fiat currency issued by a central bank, since the gold standard forced them into a siloed depression while citizens flourished.[1] This period was labeled by bankers the [[long-depression|Long Depression]].[1]

The article argues that in light of the money printing prompted by the Covid-19 crisis, the world needs a reversal mirroring what happened in 1873 — "a new deflationary period."[1]
