# Coinage Act of 1873

The Coinage Act of 1873 ended the coinage of silver and put the American economy on the [[gold-standard|gold standard]] for the first time.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The article states that this caused deflation, which increased the buying power of America's currency.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) Bankers hated the gold standard so much from 1873 onward that they began calling for the government to permanently adopt a [[fiat-currency|fiat]] currency issued by a central bank, because the gold standard forced them into a siloed depression while citizens flourished.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The resulting era, labelled the [[long-depression|Long Depression]] by bankers, is presented by the article as a model for the future: it argues that after the money printing of the Covid-19 crisis there must be a reversal mirroring what happened in 1873, and that "we need a new deflationary period."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
