# 2025 Iberian Peninsula Power Outage

The **2025 Iberian Peninsula power outage** was a widespread blackout that struck [[spain|Spain]] and parts of Portugal and [[france|France]] on Monday, 28 April 2025, which Portugal's grid operator [[redes-energeticas-nacionais|Redes Energéticas Nacionais]] (REN) attributed to a "rare atmospheric phenomenon."[1]

## Impact

The outage brought public transport to a standstill, caused significant traffic jams and delayed flights, disrupting millions of people.[1] Matches at the Madrid Open tennis tournament were suspended, Portuguese banks and schools closed, and hospitals in Spain were forced to run on generators.[1]

## Stated cause

REN told Sky News that the outage was caused by a fault in the Spanish electricity grid, which in turn was caused by a "rare atmospheric phenomenon."[1] The operator said that as a result of extreme temperature variations in Spain there were "anomalous oscillations" in extremely high-voltage lines, a condition it described as "induced atmospheric variation."[1] Those oscillations caused synchronization failures between systems, leading to successive disturbances across the interconnected European network.[1] Given the complexity of the issue, REN said it could take as long as a week for the network to return to normal.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] noted that officials were blaming the outage on a "rare atmospheric phenomenon," and said that if that explanation were true — and the event was not actually related to renewable energy generation or to a cyber attack — he would venture that the cause of the phenomenon was related to the Earth's weakening magnetic field and subsequent shifting magnetic poles.[1] He argued it was "pretty important to get our shit prepped," because the planet's magnetic field is undergoing cyclic changes that cannot be stopped.[1]
