# 2023 Pro-Palestinian Capitol Occupation

In October 2023, pro-Palestinian activists occupied a US Capitol building in Washington, DC, accusing lawmakers of having Palestinian blood on their hands as protests over the war in [[gaza|Gaza]] spread across the United States.[1]

## The occupation

ZeroHedge reported that far-left pro-Palestinian protests were continuing across the country, with activists occupying the Capitol and directing their accusations at members of [[congress|Congress]].[1] The item was framed with the pointed headline question "Insurrection?", noting that Democrats had spent the preceding years accusing conservatives of insurrection over the occupation of the Capitol.[1]

## Commentary

The accompanying analysis argued that the first goal of such efforts is a narrative shift in which those who triggered the conflict are recast as victims, with the [[hamas|Hamas]] fighters who launched the October 7 attacks "dismissed down the memory hole" and the focus placed solely on Palestinian tragedy.[1] It held that the most logical course for America was to stay out of the conflict entirely, but suggested that visible leftist involvement on the side of Hamas might push a majority of Americans toward full support for [[israel|Israel]], simply because "any cause backed by woke organizations is a cause worth obstructing."[1]
