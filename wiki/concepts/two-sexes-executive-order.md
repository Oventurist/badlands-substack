# Two Sexes Executive Order

The "two sexes" executive order is a day-one directive of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, signed in January 2025, requiring federal agencies to recognize only two sexes — male and female.[1]

## Contents

The order states that gender identity "does not provide a meaningful basis for identification," and defines sex as referring to "immutable biological classification as either male or female."[1] It further specifies that "Female means a person belonging, at conception, to the sex that produces the large reproductive cell" and that "Male means a person belonging, at conception, to the sex that produces the small reproductive cell."[1] Trump previewed the policy in his inauguration speech, declaring, "As of today, it will henceforth be the official policy of the United States government that there are only two genders, male and female."[1]

## Reaction

Transgender people and LGBTQ+ advocates in Maryland, speaking to WJZ, described the order as an attack on their community.[1]

## Pro-life implications

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the order delivered an unintended bonus to the pro-life movement through the phrase "a person belonging, at conception, to the sex that produces…," since official US government policy now describes personhood as beginning at conception.[1] Many believe life begins at conception and that the rights of personhood should attach at that moment; activists had sought such federal recognition for decades, and Trump accomplished it on day one.[1] She paired the observation with commentary on the death of [[cecile-richards|Cecile Richards]], the former head of [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]], immediately before the inauguration.[1]
