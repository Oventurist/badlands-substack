# Israel–Palestine Conflict

The **Israel–Palestine conflict** is treated in October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary less as a military or territorial dispute than as a mass-psychological event — a battle whose principal effect, from the author's vantage, is on "the minds of the masses."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Framing in Badlands commentary

[[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] argues that because the conflict is quasi-religious in nature, receives nonstop air time, and reaches back thousands of years, it is guaranteed to affect people very deeply, raising fundamental questions of right and wrong and of truth and falsehood for millions simultaneously.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The timing is described as extraordinary because it coincides with a general collapse of faith in the press, evidenced by [[rasmussen-reports|Rasmussen]] polling.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

The conflict is characterized as a wrecking ball for tribal loyalty on both the right and the left: the internal coherence of both political camps is said to be crumbling, since party affiliation no longer predicts where a person stands, and "peer pressure doesn't work if you don't know what you're supposed to do about it."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The author invokes the physics of an avalanche — a few patches of snow slipping before the whole structure comes down at once — and wonders whether the conflict marks the point of no return for [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] programming.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Author's stated position

The author declines to take a side, holding that criminal enterprises masquerading as government are always wrong and that innocent civilians must not be held personally culpable for their governments' actions; the goal should be removal of criminal elements rather than the decimation of either side.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Predicted consequences

The essay predicts that a third world war will be "like a car that won't start," prompting people to look under the hood, since no amount of rage-fuel can repair a broken engine.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) Those who look are expected to rediscover what [[smedley-butler|Smedley Butler]] said in the 1930s — that [[war-is-a-racket|war is a racket]] and the puppeteers play both sides against each other.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) While many forces drive this transformation, the author predicts the conflict will be the final straw for a great many people.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Badlands analysis of October 2023

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s essay "Women, Children ... and Terror," published October 13, 2023, argued that the conflict is manipulated on both sides and that its purpose is to produce a bigger war.[2] He traced the structure of the conflict to the [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]] and the 1948 removal of Palestinians from the land, calling the placement of [[israel|Israel]] amid the Arab states the equivalent of a hornet's nest struck periodically with a Louisville Slugger, with the resulting tension "100% intentional."[2]

Carlson said he had no idea how much of what was being seen or heard was real, speculating that some was real and some propaganda but all part of an overreaching plan.[2] He called the situation a [[false-flag-operation|false flag]] — meaning coordinated or manipulated, not that nobody died — and argued that factions within both the Israeli government and [[hamas|Hamas]] were wittingly or unwittingly doing the bidding of Deep State overlords.[2] He warned that American military aid to Israel could be used to justify terrorist attacks inside the United States by people who entered illegally in preceding years.[2]

His prescription was epistemic discipline: keep collecting information, do not tell people "everything about the Israel situation is fake" because no one will listen again, and instead ask how the attack happened undetected by [[mossad]] and Israel's defense systems, or say one supports the people of Israel and considers the violence evil while opposing escalation into war.[2]
