# Civil Rights Act of 1960

The Civil Rights Act of 1960 is a US federal statute whose provisions include requirements for the retention and federal inspection of election and voter registration records.[1]

## Invocation in Georgia, January 2026

On January 22, 2026, the Georgia State Senate Ethics Committee passed Resolution 563, urging the Georgia Secretary of State to fully comply with a Justice Department request for Georgia voter registration information in order to meet the state's obligations under the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act]], the [[help-america-vote-act|Help America Vote Act]], and the Civil Rights Act of 1960.[1] The resolution followed an eleven-hour [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] hearing at which investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]] presented evidence of alleged intentional human intervention in the 2020 election.[1]

## Use in the 2025 Fulton County records demand

The Act's provision authorizing the Attorney General to review and copy preserved election materials was invoked in November 2025 when the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s Civil Rights Division, through Assistant Attorney General [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]], demanded that [[fulton-county|Fulton County]], Georgia produce all election records responsive to a [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] subpoena "in its entirety and most original form."[2]
