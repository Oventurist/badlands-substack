# 2020 George Floyd Riots

The riots that spread across the United States in 2020 following the death of [[george-floyd|George Floyd]] caused, over the course of eight weeks, some $2 billion in damage and 29 deaths.[1]

Badlands Media attributes the destruction to Soros-funded radicals and reads the episode as a deliberate replay of the race riots that nearly tore the nation apart after the murder of [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]] in 1968.[1] The comparison is the article's organising device: 1968 and 2020 are treated as the same operation run twice, with a nonviolent civil rights tradition displaced by funded and directed street violence.[1]

The 2020 unrest is then set against 2025, when the [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination of Charlie Kirk]] again awoke the spectre of social chaos, this time amidst a more advanced economic collapse of the trans-Atlantic bubble economy.[1] Race riots had not yet begun in that later period, but Badlands argues the fissures between the radical left and radical right had never been greater.[1]
