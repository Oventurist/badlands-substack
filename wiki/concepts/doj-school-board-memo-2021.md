# 2021 DOJ School Board Memo

The 2021 school board memorandum was a [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] directive that focused federal law-enforcement attention on threats against school board members and educators.[1] It became a major political controversy during the Biden administration because critics argued it chilled the speech of parents objecting at local school board meetings.[1]

## Internal warnings disclosed in 2026

Newly released DOJ documents reported in June 2026 show internal concerns among department officials about the memorandum at the time it was issued.[1] The records indicate that some officials raised questions about how the memo could affect parents speaking out at school board meetings.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the disclosure to a similar "internal warnings were raised and ignored" pattern reported in recent weeks concerning the Mar-a-Lago raid, suggesting the public is being prepared to recognize [[weaponization-of-government|weaponized government]] as a sweeping and long-running phenomenon rather than a series of isolated incidents.[1]
