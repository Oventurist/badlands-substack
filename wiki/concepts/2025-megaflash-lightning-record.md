# 2025 Megaflash Lightning Record

In July 2025, researchers confirmed that a single lightning flash recorded in 2017 had set a world record for length, stretching 515 miles across the Great Plains from eastern Texas to Kansas City, Missouri.[1]

The record was documented in a new report published in the Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society, and it displaced the previous title holder, a 477-mile bolt observed in April 2020.[1] Randy Cerveny, an Arizona State University professor who worked on the study, said in a statement, "We call it megaflash lightning and we're just now figuring out the mechanics of how and why it occurs."[1]
