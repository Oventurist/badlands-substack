# FBI Retribution Lawsuit

The FBI retribution lawsuit is a federal suit filed in September 2025 by three former senior [[fbi|FBI]] officials against ranking Trump administration law enforcement officials over what the plaintiffs describe as a political purge.[1]

## Allegations

In a complaint filed in federal court on Wednesday, September 10, 2025, [[brian-driscoll|Brian Driscoll]], [[steve-jensen|Steve Jensen]], and [[spencer-evans|Spencer Evans]] alleged they were dismissed the previous month as part of a "campaign of retribution" carried out by FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] and Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]].[1] The complaint accuses both officials of knowingly violating the law to appease political demands to remove agents tied to past investigations into President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

According to the lawsuit, "Patel explained that he had to fire the people his superiors told him to fire, because his ability to keep his own job depended on the removal of the agents who worked on cases involving the President."[1] The complaint also alleges Patel privately told Driscoll that "the FBI tried to put the president in jail and he hasn't forgotten it," and that removing certain agents, while "likely illegal," was necessary to save his own job.[1] All three plaintiffs played varying roles in previous Trump-era investigations but deny any political motivation in their work.[1]

## Parties and relief sought

The former officials are represented by [[abbe-lowell|Abbe Lowell]], previously an attorney for Hunter Biden, and are seeking back pay and a formal declaration that the firings were unlawful.[1] The suit names the FBI, the [[justice-department|Department of Justice]], and the Executive Office of the President as defendants.[1] The case was reported by the [[washington-examiner|Washington Examiner]].[1]
