# 2025 Romanian Presidential Election Ban

In March 2025 Romania's electoral bureau barred the far-right frontrunner in the country's presidential vote from running in the May election, an explosive decision that sparked violence in Bucharest.[1]

## The decision

After a 10-4 vote, Romania's electoral bureau (BEC) said on a Sunday that it was invalidating [[calin-georgescu|Calin Georgescu]]'s candidacy due to his "failure to comply with the electoral regulations."[1] The ruling came despite pressure from Washington to "listen" to voters' demands, and just over a week after Romanian prosecutors opened a criminal investigation accusing Georgescu of crimes including attempting to subvert the constitutional order and establishing a fascist organization.[1] When Georgescu left the courthouse where he was questioned the previous month, he made what many interpreted to be a Nazi salute to supporters outside.[1]

## Reaction

Georgescu said the decision had dealt "a direct blow to the heart of democracy worldwide."[1] His supporters took to the streets of Bucharest, with some tearing up asphalt from the roads to pelt police massed to guard the BEC building, according to CNN affiliate Antena 3.[1]
