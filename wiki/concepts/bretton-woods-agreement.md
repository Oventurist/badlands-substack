# Bretton Woods Agreement

The Bretton Woods Agreement was the post-World War II monetary arrangement that pegged the US dollar to gold and established it as the world's reserve currency.[1]

## Background

According to the January 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Master and Commander," the international [[gold-standard|gold standard]] had fallen apart during World War I as countries hoarded their gold rather than fulfilling contractual obligations to one another.[1] After World War II the United States held the largest gold reserves in the world, which paved the way for a new standard built around the dollar.[1]

## Terms and effect

Touted as a means of re-creating a global gold standard for currencies, the agreement pegged the US dollar to gold and made it the world's reserve currency.[1] In the essay's framing, this was the moment at which the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] became the most powerful bank on the planet.[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argued that because the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]] controlled the Federal Reserve, control of the world's reserve currency gave them control of everything else in the [[global-debt-system|global debt system]].[1] He described the arrangement as the culmination of a financing strategy stretching back to the Napoleonic Wars, including the funding of both sides of the First and Second World Wars.[1] The essay treats the end of dollar-gold convertibility in 1971 (see [[nixon-shock|the 1971 abandonment of the gold standard]]) as the moment the arrangement became a pure [[fiat-currency|fiat currency]] system backed by nothing.[1]
