# Badlands Blitz Episode 21

**Badlands Blitz Episode 21**, subtitled "Exploring Trump's Trap ... And Who's Fallen Into It", is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] published on the Badlands Substack on June 13, 2026. At the time of archiving the post had accumulated 198 likes. As with every entry in the series, the post presents itself as a weekly recap and dissection of the work produced Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].

## Format and hosts

The episode is co-hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], both of whom are linked in the post to their Substack profiles. The written post functions as a companion note to the recorded discussion, opening with the customary greeting to "Badlanders!" and identifying the episode as the twenty-first in the series. The source characterises the tone of the conversation with a joke on protest-coverage language, saying the two hosts "get a little fiery, but mostly peaceful."

## Content

According to the source, the discussion covers three linked themes. The first is what the post calls "the ongoing and amplified effects of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s glorious discombobulator" — a recurring Badlands framing in which Trump's unpredictable public conduct is treated not as chaos but as a deliberate instrument that disorients his political opponents and induces them to expose themselves. The episode's subtitle, "Exploring Trump's Trap ... And Who's Fallen Into It", extends that framing by suggesting that identifiable actors have already been caught by the manoeuvre.

The second theme is what the hosts term "the real reason for the perpetuation of the Fake Iran War." The phrasing signals the outlet's position that the escalating confrontation involving Iran is, in its presentation to the public, substantially performative or manufactured, and that the reasons for sustaining it differ from those officially stated.

The third theme is the construction of what the post calls the Multipolar World — the argument, developed elsewhere in the Badlands corpus under the heading of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]], that a new international order is being assembled in plain view. The source qualifies this claim with the conditional "if we cultivate the eyes to see it," placing the burden of perception on the reader rather than treating the shift as self-evident.

## Subscription model

The post reiterates the outlet's standard publishing model: all Badlands content, including The Blitz, will continue to be free, and the team is able to pay itself for its time only through readers who support the work directly. Readers are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time, in recognition of the daily output and the "step-up in said work for 2026."

The note closes with the program's standard sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
