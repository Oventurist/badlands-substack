# Florida HB 269

Florida HB 269 is a state law creating new offenses related to harassment, threats or intimidation targeting religious or ethnic groups.[1] It was sponsored by then-state senator [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]].[1]

## Content and criticism

The bill was publicly presented as a measure against graffiti and comparable forms of harassment, but Badlands writers argue a key provision criminalized the distribution of flyers and pamphlets critical of [[israel|Israel]] — because the [[ihra-definition-of-antisemitism|IHRA definition of antisemitism]], adopted through legislation and executive orders in 38 states, equates antisemitism with anti-Zionism.[1] The law also imposes harsh prison sentences on offenders.[1]

Fine and Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] made what was described as a "secret trip" to Jerusalem, where DeSantis signed the bill into law — an arrangement Badlands writers flagged as anomalous, a governor and state senator travelling to a foreign country to enact state legislation.[1]

## Place in the speech war

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presents HB 269 as the most egregious example of what he had warned in 2023 would become a beachhead for expansion: the use of antisemitism statutes as a Trojan Horse for dismantling the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]], a trajectory he argues leads eventually to criminalizing criticism of the U.S. government itself on the theory that it incites violence against politicians.[1]
