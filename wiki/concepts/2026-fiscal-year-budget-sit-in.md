# 2026 Fiscal Year Budget Sit-In

On Sunday, April 27, 2025, House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] and New Jersey Senator [[cory-booker|Cory Booker]] held a livestreamed sit-in protest and discussion on the steps of the United States Capitol in opposition to the Republican budget plan for fiscal year 2026.[1]

## Format and participants

The event was billed as an "Urgent Conversation with the American People" and was timed to precede Congress's return to session on Monday, as Democrats sought to stall the Republican economic legislative agenda.[1] Other Democratic lawmakers joined through the day, including Senator [[raphael-warnock|Raphael Warnock]], who spoke as the sit-in passed the ten-hour mark.[1]

## The budget dispute

The proposed fiscal 2026 budget was reported to include cuts to programs supporting childcare, health research, education, housing assistance, community development, and the elderly.[1] In a joint statement, Booker and Jeffries said that "Republican leaders have made clear their intention to use the coming weeks to advance a reckless budget scheme to President Trump's desk that seeks to gut Medicaid, food assistance and basic needs programs that help people, all to give tax breaks to billionaires."[1]

Badlands commentary treated the demonstration dismissively, with contributor Jon Herold noting the spectacle of Democrats hosting a "sit-in."[1]
