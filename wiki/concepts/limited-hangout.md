# Limited Hangout

A "limited hangout" is an intelligence tradecraft term, widely used within the [[truth-community|Truth Community]], for the deliberate release of a partial or curated set of true information in order to conceal a larger truth — "to disguise lies with cleverly-gilded bits of Truth."[1]

## Use by the Deep State apparatus

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] describes the tactic as a staple of the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s power projection apparatus, executed specifically through the CIA-controlled [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird Media]].[1]

## The JFK example

Burning Bright cites the JFK assassination "conspiracies" that circulated from the 1960s through the 1980s as perhaps the most famous instance: narratives such as the "shooter on the grassy knoll" were, on this account, inserted directly by the [[cia|CIA]] in order to distract from genuine investigative leads into the actual conspiracy behind the murder of a sitting president.[1]

## Application to the Epstein unsealing

The concept was invoked in January 2024 to frame the anticipated release of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]-related court documents. While acknowledging that "some tantalizing bits of Truth" about Epstein's operations and clients would likely be disseminated, Burning Bright argued that the mainstream media had been amplifying the coming information dump "a little too much," and warned that victims and targets of the Epstein influence operation were as likely to be smeared alongside the guilty.[1] His prescribed posture was to "Watch & Discern."[1]
