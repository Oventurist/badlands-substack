# National Security Presidential Memorandum 8

**National Security Presidential Memorandum 8 (NSPM 8)** is a directive issued by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in October 2025 addressing the use of available [[department-of-war|Department of War]] funds for military pay and allowances during a lapse in annual appropriations.[1]

## Background

The memorandum was issued during a lapse in appropriations that, at the time of signing, was in its fifteenth day.[1] The lapse included the annual appropriations that [[congress|Congress]] provides for the pay and allowances of military personnel.[1] Congressional leaders had indicated that political negotiations had stalemated and that passage of the necessary bipartisan legislation to end the lapse was unlikely to occur before active duty military personnel were scheduled to receive their next paychecks on October 15th.[1] The memorandum characterized this potential outcome as "a serious and unacceptable threat to military readiness and the ability of our Armed Forces to protect and defend our Nation."[1]

## Provisions

Acting as Commander in Chief of the Armed Forces under Article II of the United States Constitution, Trump directed the Secretary of War, in coordination with the Director of the [[office-of-management-and-budget|Office of Management and Budget]], to use for the purpose of pay and allowances any funds appropriated by Congress that remained available for expenditure in Fiscal Year 2026.[1] The directive covered the scheduled disbursement of military pay and allowances for active duty military personnel, as well as for Reserve component military personnel who had performed active service during the relevant pay period.[1]

Funds used during the lapse were to be those the Secretary of War determined were provided for purposes bearing "a reasonable, logical relationship to the pay and allowances of military personnel," consistent with applicable law including 31 U.S.C. 1301(a), the statutory purpose restriction on appropriations.[1] At the conclusion of the lapse, the memorandum instructed that every effort be made, as authorized by law, to adjust applicable funding accounts within the Department of War so as to ensure continuation of departmental operations and activities consistent with pre-lapse planned expenditures.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] summarized NSPM 8 as having been issued to provide "military pay and allowances for active duty military personnel, as well as for Reserve component military personnel who have performed active service during the relevant pay period," and noted that the contents of NSPM 6 remain unknown or classified.[1] The memorandum sits within a numbered series of national security presidential memoranda issued by the second Trump administration, of which [[national-security-presidential-memorandum-7|NSPM 7]] is another publicly known member.[1]
