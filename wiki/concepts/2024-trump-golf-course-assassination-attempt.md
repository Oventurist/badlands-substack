# 2024 Trump Golf Course Assassination Attempt

The 2024 Trump golf course assassination attempt was an apparent attempt on the life of Republican presidential nominee [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Sunday, September 15, 2024, at his golf course in West Palm Beach, Florida. Trump was unharmed in what authorities investigated as a potential assassination attempt after a man pointed a rifle into the course where the former president was playing.[1]

## The incident

Following usual security procedure, a [[us-secret-service|Secret Service]] agent had moved one or two holes ahead of the former president, some 300 to 500 yards away, according to Palm Beach County Sheriff Ric Bradshaw.[1] Around 1:30 p.m., between the fifth and seventh holes, the agent noticed a rifle muzzle poking through the tree-lined chain-link fence surrounding the golf course.[1] The agent opened fire and Trump's protective detail rushed him to a holding room.[1] The gunman fled in a black Nissan, leaving behind an AK-47-style rifle, two bags and a camera mounted to the fence to record events.[1] "With a rifle and a scope like that, that's not a long distance," Bradshaw said.[1]

Police arrested [[ryan-wesley-routh|Ryan Wesley Routh]], a 58-year-old man most recently living in Hawaii who had spent recent years trying to join the war in Ukraine.[1] The FBI searched his car on Sunday as officers looked for clues to a possible motive.[1]

## Security context

It was the second time in just over two months that a man had a high-powered rifle within range of Trump, renewing concerns about his safety at the height of the campaign leading up to Election Day on November 5, 2024.[1] The Secret Service has long viewed golf courses as especially challenging venues because they are open to parts of the public and sit near heavily trafficked areas.[1] The agency had repeatedly denied requests from the Trump campaign for additional security measures at his public events, with officials citing limited resources.[1]
