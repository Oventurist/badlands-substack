# 2025 AfD Candidate Deaths

The 2025 AfD candidate deaths refers to a cluster of four sudden and unexpected deaths of [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative für Deutschland]] candidates in the weeks before local elections in North Rhine-Westphalia (NRW), Germany, scheduled for 14 September 2025.[1]

## The cases

AfD's direct candidate for the council in Blomberg, 66-year-old Ralph Lange, died on 28 August 2025, as reported by German outlet WDR.[1] His death automatically invalidated polling cards and postal ballot documents already sent out, pressuring the city administration to reprint and resend them; the city council decided the Blomberg election would proceed as a by-election on the planned date of 14 September 2025.[1]

Stefan Berendes, the AfD candidate for the city council in Bad Lippspringe, also died unexpectedly, triggering the same administrative procedures.[1] AfD candidates in Schwerte and Rheinberg died just days before scheduled elections, bringing the total to four in a very short period.[1] In both the Blomberg and Bad Lippspringe cases, the party had to decide whether to nominate successors.[1]

The Badlands News Brief reported that the four deceased were fact-checked as Ralph Lange (66, Blomberg), Wolfgang Seitz (59, Rheinberg), Wolfgang Klinger (71, Schwerte), and Stefan Berendes (59, Bad Lippspringe).[1]

## Reaction

German economist [[stefan-homburg|Stefan Homburg]] called the coincidence "statistically almost impossible" in a post on X.[1] German authorities, including police, stated there was no evidence of foul play, and natural causes were assumed in at least some cases.[1]

Badlands commentator Ashe in America observed that the story circulated mostly in foreign press of questionable origin, but noted the underlying deaths were verified; she questioned why officials from a rival party would rule out foul play with reportedly no investigations under way, and framed the AfD slogan "Deutschland; aber normal" ("Germany; but normal") as "possibly a deadly position."[1]
