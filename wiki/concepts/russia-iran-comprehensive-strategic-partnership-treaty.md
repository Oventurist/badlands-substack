# Russia–Iran Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Treaty

The **Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Agreement** is a bilateral treaty between Russia and [[iran|Iran]], signed in January 2025 by President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and his Iranian counterpart [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]].[1] Putin ratified parliamentary approval of the treaty and implemented it on Monday, April 21, 2025.[1]

## Contents

The agreement formalizes a commitment to building stronger relations across multiple areas, spanning national security, peaceful nuclear energy, and joint resistance against unilateral sanctions.[1]

## Diplomatic context

Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] emphasized in the week before ratification that the treaty was being finalized "despite the challenging regional and global landscape and attempts to exert pressure on our nations" by third parties.[1] Ratification came while [[donald-trump|President Trump]] was threatening military action against Iran unless it curtailed its nuclear activities sufficiently to guarantee that they could not be used to develop weaponry, an aspiration the Islamic Republic has vehemently denied holding.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry read the timing as significant, arguing that Putin was "placing himself firmly between [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] and Tehran, and daring Bibi to pull the trigger."[1] The commentary noted that Trump had already made clear the emerging US deal would grant Iran a civil nuclear energy program, something no faction in Israeli politics appeared prepared to accept, and it closed by asking how many nuclear weapons Israel itself illegally possesses before further aid is sent.[1]
