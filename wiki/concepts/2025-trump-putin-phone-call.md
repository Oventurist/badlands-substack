# 2025 Trump–Putin Phone Call

The 2025 Trump–Putin phone call was a two-hour telephone conversation held on Monday, May 19, 2025 between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], centered on efforts to end the war in Ukraine.[1] The call produced conspicuously warm personal language but no announced breakthrough on a ceasefire.[1]

## The call

Trump said afterward that "the tone and spirit of the conversation were excellent," describing Putin as a "nice gentleman."[1] Putin adviser Yuri Ushakov said the two leaders addressed each other by their first names and that "neither of them wanted to end the conversation."[1]

NBC News framed the exchange as confirming that the long-running Trump–Putin rapport is genuine while leaving open the question of what the bonhomie actually delivers for either the Kremlin or the White House.[1] The report noted that Putin has shown little desire for any ceasefire that does not satisfy his maximalist goals, and that Russian forces continued to make slow but steady battlefield advances in eastern Ukraine according to independent monitors including the [[institute-for-the-study-of-war|Institute for the Study of War]].[1] The talks also touched on the parallel negotiating track involving Volodymyr Zelenskyy and Turkey.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In the May 21, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], the item ran under the headline "Friends Without Benefits: How the Long-Running Trump-Putin Bromance is Still Not Delivering."[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] responded that "once you understand that the enemy matrix runs almost entirely on the basis of reality construction through narrative, you can reverse engineer that process in search of something much closer to the truth," and added that anti-Russia propaganda had been his own "gateway into the Info War."[1] The Badlands reading treats the framing of the call as a diplomatic failure less as reporting than as narrative construction to be decoded.[1]
