# July 2026 House Vote on Military Aid to Israel

On Wednesday, July 15, 2026, more than 100 House Democrats voted in favor of ending the United States' annual military assistance to [[israel|Israel]], exposing a growing divide within the Democratic caucus over US policy toward the longtime ally.[1]

The amendment, introduced by Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] (R-Ky.), would have eliminated approximately $3.3 billion in annual military aid but failed by a vote of 314-104.[1] A total of 103 Democrats joined Massie in supporting the measure, while 98 Democrats voted against it and 10 voted "present."[1] House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] opposed the amendment, while House Minority Whip [[katherine-clark|Katherine Clark]] and former Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] voted in favor, underscoring divisions within Democratic leadership.[1]

The amendment's defeat meant US military assistance to Israel remained unchanged.[1] The vote nevertheless marked one of the largest congressional showings of Democratic support for ending military aid to Israel and reflected the party's continuing debate over US policy amid the ongoing war in Gaza.[1]
