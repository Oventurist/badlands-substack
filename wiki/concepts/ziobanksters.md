# Ziobanksters

"Ziobanksters" is a term used in Badlands Media commentary to designate the financier class the author holds responsible for the creation of the modern state of [[israel|Israel]] and for the twentieth century's principal political upheavals.[1] The writer of "On Infiltration and Israel" states that he borrows the coinage from Chris Langan and adopts it "for expediency's sake."[1]

## Scope of the term

The term is applied to figures such as [[max-warburg|Max Warburg]], who is said to have financed a publishing house for Lenin in 1916 and championed [[richard-coudenhove-kalergi|Count Coudenhove-Kalergi]], and to the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]], to whose Lord Rothschild the [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]] was addressed.[1] The financial history the author relies on is drawn from [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]]'s [[wall-street-trilogy|Wall Street Trilogy]], which he reads as showing Wall Street funding communists and Nazis alike.[1]

## Distinction from Jewish people generally

The article is emphatic that the category is not coextensive with Jews. The author writes that the idea all Jews work in lockstep with the ziobanksters makes as much sense as the idea that all White people are active participants in a permanent system of oppression, and that both narratives act as blinders preventing anyone from solving the problems the ziobanksters cause.[1] He poses as the article's sharpest question how anyone can claim the ziobanksters look out for ordinary Jews when their own actions place many Jewish lives in grave danger by provoking backlash.[1]

## Function in the argument

The concept allows the author to attribute both the [[kalergi-plan|Kalergi Plan]] and the founding of Israel to a single actor while rejecting ethnic collective guilt, and it underpins his broader thesis of [[infiltration|infiltration]] as the mechanism by which nations, religions, and secret societies have been captured.[1]
