# Nothing Can Stop What Is Coming (#NCSWIC)

**"Nothing Can Stop What Is Coming"**, commonly abbreviated to the hashtag **#NCSWIC**, is among the most favored mantras of the Make America Great Again movement.[1]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in March 2023, [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] used the slogan as the frame for an argument that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s team had deployed [[schedule-f|Schedule F]] as the "Tip of the Spear" of an organizing philosophy he described as American to its very core, and unstoppable.[1] The phrase appears in the column both as a rhetorical bookend and as a literal claim: White closed by asserting that "the hashtag is true," and that nothing can stop what is coming.[1]

White attached the hashtag to his January 2023 tweet arguing that Trump had secured the speakership for [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] while empowering the [[house-freedom-caucus|Freedom Caucus]], a post amplified by commentator Pasquale "Pat" Scopelliti, who said it resolved for him the puzzle of why Trump had backed McCarthy.[1] Scopelliti had died by the time the column was published; White noted his passing while insisting the fight continues.[1]
