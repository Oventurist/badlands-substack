# Missile Comms

**Missile comms** is the term used by hosts of [[eye-of-the-storm|Eye of the Storm]] for a category of apparent signalling — in drops, posts and public statements — involving missiles, nuclear weapons and ICBMs.[1]

Writing in Chapter 21 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] in December 2024, [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] reported that the show had been tracking these comms for some time and that they had "only escalated in frequency," while acknowledging that their intended referent was not clear.[1] He linked the escalation to the sharp increase in nuclear and ICBM rhetoric surrounding the Russia–Ukraine war in late 2024.[1]

Fleuret's working hypothesis is that the missile comms point toward the event he calls [[the-kick|the kick]]: a nuclear launch and the apparent onset of a third world war, averted by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before his inauguration, consistent with Trump's repeated pledge to "end WWIII before he takes office."[1]
