# Vaxzevria

**Vaxzevria** is the brand name of the Oxford-[[astrazeneca|AstraZeneca]] COVID-19 vaccine, a viral vector product developed with the University of Oxford and deployed widely during the pandemic before being withdrawn from world markets in 2024.[1]

## Withdrawal

The vaccine was withdrawn worldwide in May 2024, months after AstraZeneca admitted for the first time in court documents that it could cause a rare and dangerous side effect.[1] It could no longer be used in the European Union after the company voluntarily withdrew its "marketing authorisation" — an application filed on March 5, 2024 that came into effect on Tuesday, May 7.[1] AstraZeneca said similar applications would follow in the coming months in the United Kingdom and in other countries that had approved the shot.[1]

The company attributed the withdrawal to commercial factors, stating that the vaccine was no longer being manufactured or supplied, having been superseded by updated vaccines targeting newer variants.[1] The decision ended use of a product that Boris Johnson had once heralded as a "triumph for British science" and that had been credited with saving more than six million lives.[1]

## Reported psychiatric effects

A peer-reviewed systematic review published in Frontiers in Psychiatry on April 12, 2024 examined 21 articles describing 24 cases of new-onset psychosis following COVID-19 vaccination, and linked the viral vector AstraZeneca vaccine to psychotic symptoms in 25 percent of those cases, with the [[pfizer|Pfizer]] mRNA vaccine implicated in 33.3 percent.[1] See [[covid-19-vaccine-associated-psychosis|COVID-19 vaccine-associated psychosis]].[1]

## Commentary

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] described the withdrawal as "a good first step" toward broader public recognition that pharmaceutical companies and vaccine manufacturers had defrauded the general population, biotech researchers, and medical professionals alike.[1]
