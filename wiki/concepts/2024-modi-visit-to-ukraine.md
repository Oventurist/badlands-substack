# 2024 Modi Visit to Ukraine

In August 2024, the Office of the President of [[ukraine|Ukraine]] confirmed that Indian Prime Minister [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] would make an official visit to Ukraine on Friday, August 23, 2024, Ukraine's National Flag Day.[1]

## Announcement and agenda

The Office of the President announced the visit on Telegram, clarifying that it was the first visit by an Indian head of government to Ukraine in the history of bilateral relations.[1] Modi was scheduled to meet Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], with the two expected to discuss issues of bilateral cooperation and a number of documents between Ukraine and India expected to be signed.[1]
