# Shilling

**Shilling**, in the vocabulary of the [[qanon|Q]] movement and the imageboard culture from which it emerged, refers to paid or organised posting intended to combat, derail, or discredit a narrative. The Badlands Media series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] defines shills as "people who are paid to combat narratives," characterising them as actors who know that "every word they say is a lie."[1]

## Sliding

One named shill tactic discussed in the drops is **sliding**. Chapter 3 of Q for Dummies, analysing drop #17 of October 31, 2017, explains that "sliding" is "a very well-known and effective shill tactic" — the practice of flooding a discussion board with unrelated or provocative posts so that a thread containing unwanted information is pushed off the front page and effectively buried.[1] The author reads Q's mention of the term as serving two purposes at once: alerting readers that the [[q-operation|Q operation]] was already attracting organised opposition fewer than twenty drops in, and publicly signalling to those actors that their methods had been identified — in the author's paraphrase, "we see you, and we see what you are doing."[1]

## Shilling as confirmation

Within the interpretive framework of the series, the volume of hostile activity is treated as evidence of the material's significance rather than of its falsity.[1] Discussing drop #21 of November 1, 2017, the author writes that within a span of less than four full days the board was being hit hard by shills and trolls, with "numerous bot farms, shills, and trolls" deployed upon every new drop or thread.[1] He describes this as "a telltale sign that the information being shared contains a lot more truth than lies, and a surefire sign that what is being shared is over the target," and cites it as the moment he ceased to regard the posts as a [[larp|LARP]].[1]
