# GART 4: Dallas

**GART 4: Dallas** was the fourth stop of the Great American Restoration Tour, the travelling live-event series staged by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held April 18–21, 2024 in Dallas, Texas.[1] It was the tour's first event in Texas, and [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] announced that on Thursday, April 18 the network would "take over Dallas, Texas, as the Great American Restoration Tour hits the Lone Star State for the first time."[1] The conference took place at the [[hilton-dallas-southlake|Hilton Dallas Southlake]] in Southlake Town Square.[1]

## Format

Badlands promoted GART 4 as a way to "experience everything you love about Badlands in the form of a live, ticketed event," with in-person speakers and meet-and-greets running all weekend alongside food and social programming.[1] Attendees could watch live, in-person versions of Badlands Media shows, take part in live question-and-answer sessions with Badlands panels, and meet the network's show hosts in person.[1]

## Speakers

The expected speaker list comprised [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], [[kate-buckley|Kate Buckley]], [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]], [[just-human|Just Human]], [[canncon|CannCon]], [[gmoney|GMoney]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]], [[absolute-1776|Absolute1776]] and [[ghost-of-bph|Ghost of BPH]].[1]

## Tickets

General Admission tickets included a Welcome Reception with Badlands Media on Friday night, breakfast and lunch across the weekend, and the opportunity to spend time with other "Badlanders" and with show hosts throughout the event, framed by the organisers as an attempt "to level up in the Information War together."[1] A VIP package, described as available in extremely limited quantity, added a separate Thursday night dinner and meet-and-greet with Badlands show hosts and personalities to open the weekend; a small number of additional VIP places were released shortly before the event because some attendees had been forced to change plans.[1] Single-day tickets were also released in limited quantity for those with less time available, and all in-person tickets were sold through a TicketSpice page.[1]

## Virtual attendance

For those unable to attend in person, Badlands sold virtual tickets streaming the entirety of GART 4.[1] Virtual purchasers received access to the full weekend live stream, entry to an exclusive [[telegram|Telegram]] chat shared with other virtual and in-person attendees, and the ability to watch recorded replays of all GART 4 panels both during and after the event.[1]
