# Soros District Attorney Funding

Soros district attorney funding refers to the practice, attributed to billionaire [[george-soros|George Soros]], of financing the election campaigns of progressive prosecutors across the United States.[1]

A January 2023 report said Soros had spent more than $40 million getting far-left district attorneys elected across the country, prosecutors described as having agendas to decriminalize a number of crimes.[1] The figure was published in the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] by author Matt Palumbo and carried by [[breitbart|Breitbart]]; Palumbo concluded that "Soros-backed DAs (and their ideological allies) now represent at least one-fifth of Americans."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote sarcastically that Soros had "a completely harmless habit of installing district attorneys across the U.S." — attorneys who "always seem eager to toe the party line and lend a helping hand when BLM or Antifa start wild'n out."[1] He noted that the pattern was not new, pointing out that Politico had reported on Soros's prosecutor spending as early as August 2016.[1]
