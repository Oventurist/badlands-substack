# Operation Midway Blitz

**Operation Midway Blitz** is the name given to a federal immigration-enforcement campaign carried out in Chicago, in which [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] officers swept the city for illegal migrants.[1]

## Dispute over military protection

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said that a deployment of the [[national-guard|National Guard]] to Chicago was needed to protect the federal immigration officers conducting the operation.[1] In December 2025 the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] temporarily blocked that deployment, holding that the government had failed to identify a source of authority permitting the military to execute the laws in Illinois and that the President had not invoked any statutory exception to the [[posse-comitatus-act|Posse Comitatus Act]].[1]

Justice [[samuel-alito|Samuel Alito]] dissented alongside Justices [[clarence-thomas|Clarence Thomas]] and [[neil-gorsuch|Neil Gorsuch]], writing that regardless of one's view of the administration's immigration enforcement or of how ICE had conducted its operations, protecting federal officers from potentially lethal attacks should not be thwarted.[1] Badlands commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] read the episode as part of a broader public-relations war rather than a straightforward legal contest.[1]
