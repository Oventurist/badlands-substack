# Project Stargate

Project Stargate is described in the Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]] as a plan rolled out by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for the United States to invest $500 billion in [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] infrastructure, in partnership with [[oracle|Oracle]], [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[softbank|SoftBank]].

The article introduces Stargate to establish the prominence of [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]] in the administration's plans, noting that at the same time Trump suggested either [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] or Ellison buy [[tiktok|TikTok]]. [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] concludes from this that "Ellison is a big part of Trump's plan going forward," a claim that in turn underwrites his broader argument that the Ellison family's acquisition of [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]] through [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]] is politically significant rather than merely commercial.

Stargate also anchors the essay's closing argument about AI itself: that "just because AI can be used for evil, it doesn't mean it can't also be used to do good," and that America rather than its adversaries should be the power "wielding the AI sword."
