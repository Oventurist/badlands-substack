# Project Stargate

Project Stargate is the plan, rolled out by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], for the United States to invest $500 billion into artificial intelligence infrastructure in partnership with [[oracle|Oracle]], [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[softbank|SoftBank]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

In "A New Hollywood," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites Stargate as evidence that Oracle cofounder [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]] is "a big part of Trump's plan going forward," noting that Trump simultaneously suggested Ellison or [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] buy [[tiktok|TikTok]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) Carlson connects the AI buildout to Hollywood output, reading *Mission Impossible: Dead Reckoning* — in which the team must stop a powerful AI from falling into the wrong hands — as a signal that America is being told it must be the power wielding "the AI sword," and not its adversaries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) He argues that just as AI can be used for evil, it can also be used for good.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

## Announcement and the Musk–Altman dispute (January 2025)

Stargate was formally announced by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 21, 2025 as a $500 billion artificial-intelligence infrastructure project funded by [[openai|OpenAI]], [[oracle|Oracle]] and [[softbank|SoftBank]], which Trump described as "a resounding declaration of confidence in America's potential."[3] The venture was to begin with an initial $100 billion private investment, with SoftBank's [[masayoshi-son|Masayoshi Son]], Oracle's [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]] and OpenAI chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] at the helm.[3]

The rollout triggered a public clash between [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and Altman.[3] After OpenAI posted on X that it would begin deploying $100 billion immediately, the [[spacex|SpaceX]] chief executive replied "They don't actually have the money," later adding, "SoftBank has well under $10B secured. I have that on good authority."[3] Altman answered first diplomatically, then sharply: "Wrong, as you surely know. Want to come visit the first site already under way? This is great for the country. I realize what is great for the country isn't always what's optimal for your companies, but in your new role, I hope you'll mostly put [the United States] first."[3]

Badlands commentator [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] characterized conservatives turning on Trump over the exchange as falling victim to an anti-Trump psyop, while [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] observed that the project had put Altman and Ellison in the spotlight and prompted the internet to begin digging into both men.[3] The $500 billion figure was also read alongside Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s pledge that same week of $600 billion in expanded investment and trade with the United States.[3]

## Port Washington campus and local backlash (2026)

A proposed $15 billion, 1.3-gigawatt AI data center campus described as part of the Stargate megaproject — backed by [[openai|OpenAI]], [[oracle|Oracle]] and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — became the target of a municipal ballot referendum in [[port-washington|Port Washington]], Wisconsin, in April 2026.[2] The measure, placed by the nonprofit [[great-lakes-neighbors-united|Great Lakes Neighbors United]], would require voter approval before city leaders could award tax incentives to major projects, and could be the first such referendum restricting data center development in the United States.[2] Backers touted 4,000 construction jobs, roughly 1,000 long-term positions, $175 million in local infrastructure upgrades and a closed-loop cooling system running on zero-emissions energy so residents' utility bills would not rise.[2] Oracle spokesman Michael Egbert said the company was "paying our own way on energy so ratepayers' bills and electric grid reliability are not impacted by our data center."[2] The dispute was described as part of a broader national backlash, with at least three other communities preparing similar measures in 2026.[2]

## White House unveiling and health-AI claims

CNBC reporting on the January 21, 2025 announcement described Stargate as a joint venture between [[openai|OpenAI]], [[oracle|Oracle]] and [[softbank|SoftBank]] to invest billions of dollars in artificial intelligence infrastructure in the United States, unveiled at the White House by [[donald-trump|Trump]], SoftBank chief executive [[masayoshi-son|Masayoshi Son]], OpenAI chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] and Oracle co-founder [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]].[4] The executives committed to an initial $100 billion and up to $500 billion over four years, with the project structured as a separate company.[4] "What we want to do is we want to keep it in this country," Trump said of AI, noting that China is a major competitor in the nascent industry.[4] Stargate's first joint venture would be the construction of data centers in Texas, an effort Ellison said in the Roosevelt Room was already under way.[4]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called the investment a good thing but flagged Ellison's remarks about using AI to diagnose cancer and generate a customizable "vaccine" or treatment within 48 hours, questioning whether such rapid, targeted biological capabilities would be used only for benevolent purposes.[4] She observed that Oracle is a prominent US defense and intelligence contractor and that there are obvious defense applications for such capabilities.[4] While criticizing the branding choice of calling the product a "vaccine," she concluded that "cancer disclosure appears to be imminent," and warned that the public may have passed the point of keeping tech oligarchs in check.[4]
