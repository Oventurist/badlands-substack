# Hatch Act

The Hatch Act is the federal statute that bars the use of federal government resources and the official activity of federal employees for partisan political purposes. In Badlands Media coverage it is invoked as the legal standard against which the Biden administration's use of federal agencies for voter-registration work was measured.[1]

## The 2024 SBA voter-registration controversy

At a June 2024 congressional subcommittee hearing on "Weaponizing Federal Resources," House Republicans examined an agreement between the [[small-business-administration|Small Business Administration]] and the state of Michigan under a March 2021 executive order directing federal agencies to "get out the vote."[1] The administration insisted the effort was bipartisan; Republicans used the hearing to argue otherwise.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported that the "first of its kind" federal agreement with Michigan targeted must-win areas for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and did not appear to pursue voter registration in the redder parts of the state, and that to the subcommittee's knowledge the administration had not approached any state or jurisdiction that was must-win for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Her conclusion was that the executive order and its execution in Michigan constituted a violation of the Hatch Act, amounting to the use of the federal government as campaign infrastructure — unlawful conduct for which Republicans had multiple avenues of redress in the courts.[1]

The hearing was largely overshadowed the same day by Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]]'s testimony before the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] and by a heavily attended abortion hearing.[1]

## Application to the Jack Smith review

The Office of Special Counsel's Hatch Act Unit, which enforces prohibitions on political activity by government employees, opened a 2025 review into former special counsel Jack Smith after Senator Tom Cotton alleged he used his office to influence the 2024 election.[2]
