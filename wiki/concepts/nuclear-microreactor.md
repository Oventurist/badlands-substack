# Nuclear Microreactor

A nuclear microreactor is a small, transportable reactor designed to supply power to remote or military locations. The [[pentagon|Department of War]] has pursued such systems through a demonstration program intended to prove that reactors can be safely relocated in the field.[1]

## First air transport, February 2026

In February 2026 the United States conducted its first air transport of a nuclear microreactor prototype, a joint [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] and Department of War operation. The demonstration established that the reactor system can be safely moved by aircraft.[1]

The program traces to an executive order signed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on May 23, 2025 directing the deployment of advanced nuclear reactor technologies for national security purposes.[1] Energy Secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]] called the flight "the next chapter for U.S. energy" and cast it as fulfillment of Trump's energy dominance pledge.[1]

## Strategic context

The test occurred as nuclear "arms race" rhetoric intensified following the expiration of the [[new-start-treaty|New START]] treaty, which Senator [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]] described in *The Wall Street Journal* as an overdue correction rather than a diplomatic failure. No new nuclear weapons policy accompanied the demonstration.[1]
