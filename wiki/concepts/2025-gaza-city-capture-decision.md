# 2025 Gaza City Capture Decision

In August 2025, nearly two years into the war in the Gaza Strip, Israel's cabinet formally signed off on the capture of Gaza City.[1]

## Stated objectives

The Israeli government laid out five official objectives for the operation: dismantling [[hamas|Hamas]]'s military capabilities, securing the return of all living and deceased hostages, demilitarizing the enclave, expanding Israeli control over its territory, and installing a new civil administration answering to neither Hamas nor the Palestinian Authority.[1]

## Political framing

RT reported that behind the bullet points lay a more calculated play: for Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] the operation was not only about battlefield gains but about political survival, and — according to some analysts — about setting the stage for something more permanent.[1] Analysts quoted said the timing of the Gaza City push was anything but random.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the decision as Netanyahu launching "yet another military campaign to stay in power."[1] He reported that the Ultra-Orthodox community was threatening to declare a Jewish civil war against Netanyahu and the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] by engaging in boycotts and bank runs intended to bankrupt Jewish-owned banks and corporations.[1]

The commentary argued that [[israel|Israel]] is tearing itself apart while the world turns against it over its belligerence in Gaza, attributing this to Netanyahu facing three felony charges brought against him in 2019 and being saddled with a security cabinet of Ultra-Orthodox warmongers — naming [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]], [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]], and [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] — as the far right pressures him to crush Hamas and subjugate Gaza.[1] The conditions were described as set such that every actor can act in earnest and the likely outcome is Israel facing a geopolitical and, hopefully, cultural reckoning; all Netanyahu has to do is continue acting in his own personal self-interest.[1]
