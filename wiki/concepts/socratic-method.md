# Socratic Method

The **Socratic method** — teaching by question rather than by answer — is presented in the Badlands Media essay "The Extraordinary Power of Digital Soldiers" (July 31, 2023) as the operative technique of the [[qanon|Q]] drops and as the reason those drops were able to confer genuine authority on their readers.[1]

## Q's use of the method

The essay poses its central question directly: what happens when an anonymous poster on [[4chan]] initiates a Socratic dialogue with [[anons]] and leads them to assemble a comprehensive and accurate picture of the world?[1] The answer given is that it induces anons to put the puzzle together themselves, which amounts to the authoring of true information; one of Q's missions, the author concludes, appears to have been to train anons to become genuine authorities.[1]

Being skilled with language in this context means being able to generate true, or at least coherent, ideas from one's own mental processes.[1] The essay distinguishes this from cleverness with words or a good memory for what one has been told, which make for good test-takers and parrots but not for creation or discernment.[1]

## Necessity rather than expedient

Q is described as constrained by national security concerns from giving anons the answers outright, but the essay insists that even absent that tactical necessity the Socratic process would still be required: inducing anons to generate correct answers from within and amongst themselves is presented as the only way to ensure they receive the training needed to become authorities in their own right.[1] Related claims are developed at [[authority-as-authorship|authority as authorship]].[1]
