# 2025 EU WTO Challenge to US Tariffs

In May 2025 the [[european-union|European Union]] declared its intention to contest the United States' reciprocal tariff policy at the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]], while simultaneously preparing a retaliation package reported at 95 billion euros.[1] The announcement was made at a press conference held by EU Commissioner [[maros-sefcovic|Maros Sefcovic]] at the EU Council building on 7 May 2025.[1]

## Measures

The [[european-commission|European Commission]] launched a public consultation on possible countermeasures targeting U.S. imports valued at $107.4 billion in the event that negotiations with Washington failed.[1] The proposed list spanned a wide array of agricultural and industrial goods, and notably included bourbon and tequila — long-standing points of contention in transatlantic trade disputes.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had previously threatened to impose a 200 percent tariff on EU alcohol imports, escalating tensions ahead of the announcement.[1]

The EU's dispute was to begin formally with a consultation request, the Commission asserting that the U.S. tariffs contravened fundamental WTO rules.[1] In its statement the Commission said its objective was "to reaffirm that internationally agreed rules matter and these cannot be unilaterally disregarded by any WTO member, including the US."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer Chris Paul asked whether the EU's action would be the trigger for an American exit from the WTO altogether.[1]
