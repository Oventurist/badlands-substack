# Climate Change Narrative

The "climate change narrative" is the Badlands Media corpus's term for the political and rhetorical apparatus built around environmental crisis, understood as an instrument for advancing collectivism and centralised global governance rather than as a scientific question.

In [[ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo|AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] asked [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to "implement the use of the climate change narrative to further reinforce collectivism, Neo-Marxism, the loss of individual rights and the loss of national sovereignty." The model's synthesis assigns the narrative two principal functions in the campaign of the fictional Global Harmony Consortium.

First, it is used to frame overpopulation as a global threat, a framing subtly linked to traditional family values and to calls for reduced reproduction; [[esg|ESG]] scores then reward companies promoting contraception and population control. Second, it is leveraged directly "to promote collectivism, global governance, and the erosion of national sovereignty," shaping international agreements toward centralised control over environmental policy. Environmental scores within the ESG system are described as the tactical mechanism by which climate policy is enforced on corporations, paralleling the way social and governance scores enforce [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] and [[critical-race-theory|Critical Race Theory]]. The narrative is thereby folded into the wider programme of [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxist]] [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]] said to serve the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].
