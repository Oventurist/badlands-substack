# NHS Antisemitism and Racism Review

The **NHS antisemitism and racism review** is an urgent review of [[antisemitism|antisemitism]] and all forms of racism within the [[nhs|National Health Service]] ordered by the British Prime Minister and announced by the UK government in October 2025.[1]

## Announcement

The government said the review was part of wider efforts to tackle discrimination in the health service, and that it followed recent incidents of antisemitism from doctors which drew stark attention to problems of culture and of regulation in the health system.[1] Lord [[john-mann|John Mann]] was appointed to lead the review, examining how to protect patients and staff from racism and how to hold perpetrators to account.[1]

Alongside the review, the government announced the immediate rollout of strengthened mandatory antisemitism and antiracism training across the health service, and said [[nhs-england|NHS England]] would review its uniform guidance so that patients and staff always feel respected in NHS settings.[1]

## Context

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] paired the announcement with a column in [[the-scotsman|The Scotsman]] arguing that a seeming nonchalance about antisemitism in the United Kingdom is a problem for everyone, drawing parallels between the denial patterns of the UK "gender wars" and the downplaying, denial or justification of rising antisemitism amid a wider atmosphere of anti-Israeli fervour since October 7, 2023.[1] The Brief attributed the government announcement to "The British Crown."[1]
