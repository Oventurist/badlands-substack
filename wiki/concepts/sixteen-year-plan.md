# Sixteen Year Plan

The "sixteen year plan" is the term used across the Badlands Media corpus, and specifically in the second installment of the America for Sale series, for an alleged scheme by [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]] to control the United States presidency for sixteen consecutive years and thereby destroy the country.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Under the plan as the article describes it, Alwaleed installed [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] for eight years and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was to be installed for eight more.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Clinton's role is presented as already active during the first half: she was Secretary of State while Alwaleed ranked among the biggest donors to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] served as Alwaleed's liaison between him and the Clintons.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article's terse summary of why the scheme failed is that "Hillary wasn't supposed to lose."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

The plan is linked to the broader claim, drawn from Q drop 299 of December 7, 2017 — "SA Controls (assigned) US / UK POLITICIANS / Tech Co's (primary)" — that Saudi Arabia through Alwaleed had controlled many important U.S. and U.K. politicians for several decades.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) It is also tied to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s alleged penetration of the federal government, which the article characterises as "an infiltration not an invasion," permitted after Alwaleed installed Obama.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The election of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2016 is treated as the interruption that broke the sequence.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The plan as script: war, reset and collapse

The article "Bad Actors" elaborates the plan as an enemy "script" designed to destroy America and the Constitution, of which a central act was the initiation of a Third World War between the United States and Russia.[2] Such a war was intended to eliminate a large portion of the world's population, making the population easier for the cabal of elites to control, while removing the two greatest threats to that control — Russia and the United States Constitution.[2] The underlying motive is described as financial: the worldwide debt system based on Federal Reserve Notes is going bankrupt, and war has historically served as both the distraction for the public and the excuse for a new [[great-reset|global reset]], as it did in both previous world wars.[2] In Joe Lange's account this time the war will not come; [[donald-trump|Trump]] is purposely bankrupting the system while the [[brics|BRICS+]] nations build a decentralized replacement based on honest money backed by gold or other commodities and protective of national sovereignty.[2]

## Obama's role and the 2016 interruption

[[barack-obama|Obama]] is said to have played his part of the plan by weakening the United States and damaging the Constitution, after which it was to be [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s turn to finish the job; the cabal never intended to relinquish the office of Commander in Chief and, having lost it, no longer controls the greatest military in the world.[2] Lange asserts that the plan was controlled by a single figure, [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], who controlled both Obama and Clinton, and that this is why both were tied to the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] and the Iran nuclear deal alike.[2] [[john-mccain|John McCain]] is likewise said to have played a part, having taken a secret trip to a foreign country in furtherance of the scheme.[2] The exposure of [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]]'s role in financing Obama's Harvard education is characterised as the type of revelation that could have derailed the plan outright.[2]

Lange distinguishes the plan from what followed 2020: what [[joe-biden|Biden]] is doing is not part of the sixteen year plan, since the intended U.S.–Russia war is not going to happen regardless of the rhetoric, and instead Biden is destroying the Democratic Party.[2]
