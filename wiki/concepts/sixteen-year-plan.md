# Sixteen Year Plan

The "sixteen year plan" is a term drawn from the [[q|Q]] drops and used extensively in the Badlands Media corpus, notably in the article "Bad Actors," to describe an alleged scheme to destroy the United States and the [[united-states-constitution|Constitution]] over sixteen years — eight years under [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] followed by eight under an anticipated [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] presidency.

## Content of the alleged plan

In the account given by the article, Obama's role was to weaken the United States and erode the Constitution, after which Clinton would "finish the job." A central element was the initiation of a Third World War between the United States and [[russia|Russia]], intended to eliminate a large portion of the world's population so that the [[cabal]] could more easily maintain control, and to remove the two greatest obstacles to that control: Russia and the U.S. Constitution. The war would also supply both the distraction and the pretext for a new [[great-reset|Great Reset]] to replace a [[fiat-currency|fiat]] debt system based on [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Notes that the article describes as going bankrupt. The article claims both world wars served the same dual function of population culling and monetary reset.

## Collapse of the plan

The plan is said to have been destroyed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 victory, which removed the "most powerful position in the world" — and with it command of the United States military — from the cabal's control. The article asserts that Clinton's defeat was a shock because the election had already been rigged, that the fraud proved insufficient, and that Obama's attempt to prevent Trump from taking office was stopped by a military prepared to carry out what Q called "the UNTHINKABLE."

## Direction and personnel

The article argues the plan was directed not by Obama or Clinton but by [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], who is said to have controlled both figures through financing — Obama's Harvard education by way of [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]], and Clinton through donations to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]]. [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] are described as the respective "handlers." Both the [[uranium-one|Uranium One]] deal and the Iran nuclear deal are presented as products of the same arrangement. [[john-mccain|John McCain]] is named as another participant. The article distinguishes the sixteen year plan from the actions of the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration, arguing that what Biden is doing is not part of it and that the intended U.S.–Russia war will not occur.
