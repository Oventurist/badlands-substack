# Trump Corollary to the Monroe Doctrine

The "Trump Corollary" to the [[monroe-doctrine|Monroe Doctrine]] is a doctrinal formulation set out in the [[2025-national-security-strategy|2025 National Security Strategy]] of the second [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] administration.[1]

## Content

The corollary prioritizes American-led cooperative efforts to combat mass migration, drug trafficking, and "hostile foreign incursion or ownership of key assets" in the Western Hemisphere.[1] It sits alongside the strategy's pledge that the United States "will reassert and enforce the Monroe Doctrine to restore American preeminence in the Western Hemisphere," after what the document calls years of neglect.[1] Reporting placed the corollary in a long line of presidential reinterpretations of the Monroe Doctrine, which have been used to argue both for non-intervention and for American imperialism.[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands host Burning Bright read the corollary as a deliberate instrument against the [[european-union|European Union]], asserting U.S. dominance in the Western Hemisphere while limiting European interference in regional affairs and criticizing Europe's trajectory as misaligned with American interests.[1] He linked it to Trump's 28-point Ukraine plan — crafted with input from Steve Witkoff, Marco Rubio, and [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] — which offers EU membership to Kiev without NATO expansion while conceding eastern territories to Russia, sidelining European leaders such as Emmanuel Macron, Friedrich Merz, and Alexander Stubb.[1] In this reading, the doctrine exposes the EU's ineffectiveness and positions it as a barrier to conflict resolution and migration control.[1]
