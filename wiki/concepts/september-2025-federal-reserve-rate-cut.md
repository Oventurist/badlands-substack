# September 2025 Federal Reserve Rate Cut

The September 2025 Federal Reserve rate cut was the [[federal-reserve|U.S. Federal Reserve]]'s decision on Wednesday, September 17, 2025 to lower its benchmark federal funds rate by 25 basis points to a range of 4.00–4.25 percent, in line with market expectations.[1]

## Significance

The cut marked the first rate reduction of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term and the first since December 2024, following five straight meetings at which the central bank held rates steady.[1]

## Projections

Projections released with the decision showed two more quarter-point cuts anticipated in 2025 — 50 basis points in total — alongside one cut in 2026 and another in 2027.[1] While most officials foresaw gradual easing, one policymaker projected as much as 125 basis points of rate reductions by December.[1]
