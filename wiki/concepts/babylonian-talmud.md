# Babylonian Talmud

The Babylonian Talmud is a foundational rabbinic text that appears in Badlands commentary chiefly through the 2015 purchase of its first printed complete edition by financier [[leon-black|Leon Black]] for $9.3 million.[1]

## Treatment in Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] states that the Talmud is said to have been written 500 years after Christ.[1] He acknowledges that he has not read the text himself, but reports having encountered multiple accounts critical of its writings, including the claim that it holds it legal for a priest to marry a girl as young as three years old.[1] Carlson declines to elaborate further and instead encourages readers to research the subject on their own.[1]

## Context

The reference to the Talmud sits inside a broader argument about the shared traits of billionaire financiers associated with [[george-soros|George Soros]] and [[jacob-rothschild|Jacob Rothschild]].[1] Anticipating accusations of antisemitism, Carlson states explicitly that the alleged British Satanic Cabal "is comprised of all people from all walks of life, races and religions," and that its members' allegiance is to their society rather than to any race or faith.[1]
