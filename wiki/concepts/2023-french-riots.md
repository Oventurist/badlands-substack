# 2023 French Riots

The 2023 French riots were a wave of nationwide civil unrest in France sparked by the police killing of a teenager of North African descent in late June 2023. The violence continued for a fifth consecutive night into the weekend of July 1–2, 2023, and spread to neighboring countries.[1]

## Course of the unrest

Rioters burned vehicles and buildings and looted stores across France for five straight nights. According to the French interior ministry, police arrested 719 people by the early hours of Sunday, July 2. Reuters characterized the overnight chaos as "less intense" than the previous night, when 1,300 people were arrested, suggesting the riots were subsiding after more than 45,000 police officers and dozens of military armored vehicles were deployed nationwide to suppress the violence.[1]

President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] canceled a planned visit to Germany and met with officials on Sunday to devise strategies for containing what was described as the worst violence France had seen since the 2018 [[yellow-vest-movement|"Yellow Vest"]] protests.[1]

## Commentary and interpretation

The unrest was framed in coverage cited by Badlands as a vindication of remarks made years earlier by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had said, "I wouldn't go to France. Because France is no longer France … This world better be very careful and they better get very tough and very smart."[1]

Commentator Paul Joseph Watson rejected the explanation offered by French officials, who blamed social media for the spread of the unrest. Watson argued instead: "This is what happens when you absorb millions of migrants into a European county at an accelerated rate pace — with zero plan — with zero prospects for ever integrating them."[1]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that "the enemy thrives on chaos, especially when that chaos is kinetic," and contended that while patriots had used game theory and [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] tactics within information warfare, [[color-revolution|color revolutions]] are "ALWAYS the work of Globalist power structures." He compared the French riots to similar destabilization attempts in Israel months earlier and suggested the episode reflected desperation on the part of those forces, questioning how long the French people and military would tolerate it.[1]
