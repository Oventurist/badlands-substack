# Quantum Computing

Quantum computing is a class of computing technology that the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration has identified as a strategic national priority alongside artificial intelligence and biotechnology.[1]

## The 2026 national quantum effort

In June 2026, the [[office-of-science-and-technology-policy|White House Office of Science and Technology Policy]] announced a national effort aimed at developing a quantum computer capable of ushering in what it called the "era of quantum-enabled intelligence."[1] The initiative was designed to accelerate United States leadership in quantum computing and related fields, and was folded into the administration's "Golden Age of Innovation" framing by OSTP Director [[michael-kratsios|Michael Kratsios]] and other officials, who described the technologies as critical to economic competitiveness and national security.[1]

President Trump signed a quantum executive order in connection with the effort, using the occasion to reference his uncle [[john-g-trump|John G. Trump]] and, by extension, the legacy of [[nikola-tesla|Nikola Tesla]].[1]

## "Q" messaging overlap

The quantum push coincided with two posts from the official [[white-house|White House]] social media account that generated substantial online activity about "Q" messaging; commentators disputed whether the posts constituted genuine Q references or simply shitpost-style marketing.[1] Badlands contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] argued that the quantum signing itself was the more substantive development, and speculated that hidden technologies might be publicly introduced under the quantum label.[1]
