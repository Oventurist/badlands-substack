# Universal Principles

"Universal Principles" is the term [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] uses for the higher realm of metaphysics and Platonic ideas which, in his analysis, governs history more decisively than the lower realm of mechanisms, materialism and contingent arrangements.[1] The distinction is the analytical core of his November 15, 2025 essay "Don't Be Symbol-Minded."[1]

Ehret argued that from a purely pattern-recognition standpoint — looking only at banking mechanisms or the words on diplomatic documents — similarities can certainly be found between Eurasian/[[brics|BRICS]] nations and NATO/trans-Atlantic systems.[1] But once the higher realm of universal principles is taken into account, he wrote, a pronounced and meaningful conflict of a "none-Hegelian sort" becomes visible, despite agreement on similar mechanisms of digital banking and the like.[1] What matters more than every other consideration is intention: one side in the present struggle is, in his account, provably animated by an intention to kill off 90–95% of the human species and to keep the surviving 5–10% as stupid, degenerate and decadent as possible.[1]

The corresponding failure mode is what Ehret calls symbol-mindedness or bottom-up empiricism, in which analysts without an appreciation for the higher realm of causality — discussed at length, he says, by Plato, Cicero, St. Augustine, Leibniz and [[lyndon-larouche|Lyndon LaRouche]] — conclude that everything is controlled and wired to destroy and enslave the people.[1] He held that neither ChatGPT nor Grok will ever be able to reach conclusions of the sort universal-principle reasoning yields, despite their correctness.[1] The same framework underpins his claim that the principles animating the [[ionian-revolt|Ionian rebellion]] of 499 BC, the American founding of 1776 and the [[multipolar-world-order|multipolar alliance]] today are identical in principle though not in form.[1]
