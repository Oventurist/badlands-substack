# Fluoride

Fluoride is the additive that Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] identified as "the most notable example" of a harmful substance deliberately introduced into public consumption, describing it as an extremely neurotoxic chemical added to the public water supply in most municipal areas.[1]

## Claimed effects

According to the article, fluoride is known to interrupt the basic function of nerve cells in the brain, producing more docile and submissive behavior as well as what DeLarme termed "IQ devastation."[1] The piece further asserts that fluoride has never been proven to prevent tooth decay.[1]

## Robert Carton's assessment

That claim is attributed to Dr. [[robert-carton|Robert Carton]], a former scientist for the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]], who is quoted as saying: "Fluoridation is the greatest case of scientific fraud of this century, if not all time."[1]

## Context

Fluoride is presented as the leading illustration of a wider pattern the article describes, in which the [[american-medical-association|AMA]], the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]], and chemical and pharmaceutical companies — together with the colleges and media outlets they fund — supply the public with reassurance rather than evidence.[1] The article emphasizes that fluoride is "not the only harmful additive that exists in our food and water — not by a long shot."[1]
