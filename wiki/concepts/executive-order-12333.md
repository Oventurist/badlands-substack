# Executive Order 12333

Executive Order 12333 is the United States executive order governing the conduct of the intelligence community, including limits on intelligence activities directed at U.S. persons. In the Badlands Media article "Countering an Ocean of Deceit," the order appears as one of three legal authorities cited by the Q critic [[17th-special-citizens-operations-group|17SCOG]] in arguing that the [[q-operation|Q operation]] would necessarily be illegal.[1]

## Use in the Q legality argument

17SCOG's contention, as summarized in the article, is that the legal standards required for the military to conduct covert influence operations upon American citizens have not been met, and he grounds that contention in Title 50 of the U.S. Code § 3093, DoD Directive 3600.01, and Executive Order 12333.[1] The article's author accepts that these authorities govern the question but declines to argue the statutory detail, stating an intention to read further on the relevant law before speaking at length.[1]

The counter-argument offered instead is jurisdictional rather than statutory: that the United States has been infiltrated by a cabal that stole an election and imported a hostile foreign army, and that under the DoD [[dod-law-of-war-manual|Law of War Manual]] the resulting conditions amount to hostile occupation, in which the normal operation of the rule of law — and therefore the ordinary application of instruments such as Executive Order 12333 — is displaced.[1]
