# November 2023 NBC News Poll

The **November 2023 NBC News poll** was a national survey, released in the week of November 19, 2023, that found President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s approval rating had declined to the lowest level of his presidency — 40 percent — as strong majorities of all voters disapproved of his handling of foreign policy and the Israel–Hamas war.[1]

## Findings

The poll found Biden trailing former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for the first time in a hypothetical general-election matchup, although the deficit was well within the poll's margin of error for a contest still more than eleven months away.[1] The erosion for Biden was most pronounced among Democrats, a majority of whom believed Israel had gone too far in its military action in Gaza, and among voters aged 18 to 34, of whom a full 70 percent disapproved of his handling of the war.[1]

Respondents quoted by [[nbc-news|NBC News]] echoed those themes: "I do not support his support of Israel," said Meg Furey, 40, a Democrat from Austin, Texas, while Democrat Zico Schell, 23, of San Diego cited "failed promises, student loans, foreign policy in general."[1]

The survey was conducted by Democratic pollster Jeff Horwitt of Hart Research Associates together with Republican pollster Bill McInturff of Public Opinion Strategies.[1] Horwitt said that "Joe Biden is at a uniquely low point in his presidency, and a significant part of this, especially within the Biden coalition, is due to how Americans are viewing his foreign policy actions."[1] McInturff said he could not recall another time when foreign affairs not involving U.S. troops had transformed the American political landscape.[1]
