# 2023 Bilderberg Meeting

The 2023 [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] meeting was held in Lisbon, Portugal, with artificial intelligence as the conference's primary topic.[1] The Badlands Media article "Dark Equation" by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treated the gathering as a pivotal event, noting that [[openai|OpenAI]] chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] attended only days after testifying before a US Senate Judiciary subcommittee on AI regulation.[1]

## Attendees

DeLarme highlighted a cross-section of the attendee list: [[stacey-abrams|Stacey Abrams]], Pfizer chief executive [[albert-bourla|Albert Bourla]], [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] President [[borge-brende|Børge Brende]], and [[jen-easterly|Jen Easterly]], director of the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency.[1] Altman was joined by fellow AI executives including [[microsoft|Microsoft]]'s [[satya-nadella|Satya Nadella]], [[deepmind|DeepMind]]'s [[demis-hassabis|Demis Hassabis]], and former [[google|Google]] chief executive [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]], who chairs the Bilderberg Group.[1] Also present were NATO Secretary-General [[jens-stoltenberg|Jens Stoltenberg]], Ukrainian Foreign Minister [[dmytro-kuleba|Dmytro Kuleba]], BP chief [[bernard-looney|Bernard Looney]], and PayPal and [[palantir|Palantir]] co-founder [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]].[1] [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], who has attended Bilderberg meetings on and off since 1957, was also in attendance.[1]

## Interpretation

The article characterizes the Bilderberg Group as one of several policy-creation bodies functioning as a modern global shadow government, with closed-door discussions later surfacing publicly at the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] in Davos the following year.[1] DeLarme criticized the "severe lack of transparency" around the meeting, noting the public receives a list of talking points but almost no substantive account of the discussions.[1] He surmised that Altman's presence would "bode well for his career and the continued success of OpenAI," and linked the conference's AI focus to [[larry-page|Larry Page]]'s reported interest in an "A.I. god" and to what he described as a controlled demolition of the US and world economy.[1]

## Deschamps commentary

Writing days after the gathering, Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described it as the 69th annual meeting of the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg Group]], noting that the published agenda covered "transnational threats, artificial intelligence, and America's leadership in world affairs."[2] He judged the timing of the [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] item favorable to the globalists, linking it to the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] initiative and the [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]] he expected to become the new economic standard, both resting on the [[fourth-industrial-revolution|fourth industrial revolution]] concept popularized by [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[2]

Deschamps quoted [[ryan-delarme|Ryan Delarme]]'s "The Men Behind the Curtain – Part 4" on the group's composition and alleged bloodline lineage, and treated the meeting as the occasion for a broader legal argument that such gatherings violate the [[sunshine-act|Government in the Sunshine Act]] and are shielded only by the [[chatham-house-rule|Chatham House Rule]].[2]
