# 2025 Global Tariff Announcement

The 2025 global tariff announcement was a sweeping [[white-house|White House]] trade action, unveiled at the end of July 2025, that set new United States import duties affecting virtually every nation and cemented what CNN described as a breakaway from decades of free trade toward a new protectionist era.[1]

## Terms

Under the framework, a "universal" tariff of 10% remains for countries with which the United States runs a trade surplus — most of them — while nations with which the United States runs a trade deficit face a 15% floor.[1] Deals with China and Mexico remained pending at the time of the announcement.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] also said he was raising tariffs on Canada to 35%, though the White House said goods compliant with the United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement would not be subject to the higher rate, blunting the impact.[1]

## Legal challenge

In a notable hearing held alongside the rollout, a skeptical federal appeals court heard oral arguments on whether the president overstepped his legal authority in imposing many of the sweeping tariffs.[1] Tariff rates for numerous countries had shifted repeatedly since Trump's April 2 announcement.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright argued that beneath the headlines a clear trend was emerging, describing the outcome as the "Globalist Superstate" capitulating to Trump and bending the knee to weaponized sovereignty, and characterizing the agreements as "not deals" but "disclosures."[1] The same analysis was applied to the [[european-union|European Union]]'s acceptance of a tripling of tariffs to 15% on most of its exports to the United States, negotiated by European Commission chief [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]].[1]
