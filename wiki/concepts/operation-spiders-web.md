# Operation Spider's Web

Operation Spider's Web was a Ukrainian drone operation conducted on Sunday, June 1, 2025, in which Ukrainian forces destroyed forty of Russia's most powerful bomber jets and surveillance aircraft in a series of coordinated strikes penetrating deep into Russian territory.[1] Russia's defence ministry confirmed the attack, saying five airfields had been struck.[1]

## Aftermath and the Trump–Putin call

On Wednesday, June 4, 2025, [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] said he had spoken by telephone with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], who told him that Russia would have to respond to the attack on its warplanes.[1] Trump described the call as lasting about one hour and fifteen minutes and wrote on Truth Social that it was "not a conversation that will lead to immediate Peace."[1] He added: "We discussed the attack on Russia's docked airplanes, by Ukraine, and also various other attacks that have been taking place by both sides… It was a good conversation, but not a conversation that will lead to immediate Peace. President Putin did say, and very strongly, that he will have to respond to the recent attack on the airfields."[1]
