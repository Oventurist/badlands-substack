# Operation Spider's Web

Operation Spider's Web was a Ukrainian drone operation conducted on Sunday, June 1, 2025, in which Ukrainian forces destroyed forty of Russia's most powerful bomber jets and surveillance aircraft in a series of coordinated strikes penetrating deep into Russian territory.[1] Russia's defence ministry confirmed the attack, saying five airfields had been struck.[1]

## Aftermath and the Trump–Putin call

On Wednesday, June 4, 2025, [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] said he had spoken by telephone with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], who told him that Russia would have to respond to the attack on its warplanes.[1] Trump described the call as lasting about one hour and fifteen minutes and wrote on Truth Social that it was "not a conversation that will lead to immediate Peace."[1] He added: "We discussed the attack on Russia's docked airplanes, by Ukraine, and also various other attacks that have been taking place by both sides… It was a good conversation, but not a conversation that will lead to immediate Peace. President Putin did say, and very strongly, that he will have to respond to the recent attack on the airfields."[1]

## Contemporaneous reporting

Russian state media reported on June 1, 2025, that military airfields in five Russian regions — Murmansk in the north, Ivanovo and Ryazan in central Russia, Irkutsk in Siberia and Amur in the Far East — had been targeted in coordinated drone attacks orchestrated by Kiev, according to the Russian Defense Ministry.[2] Moscow said most of the strikes were repelled, with some causing material damage but no casualties.[2] Ukrainian media described it as a major operation against Russian strategic aviation and reported that the airfields housed Tu-95 and Tu-22M strategic bombers as well as an A-50 airborne early warning and control aircraft, claims the Russian Defense Ministry did not confirm.[2] Western reporting put the figure at 41 Kremlin nuclear bombers and other aircraft destroyed or damaged across at least four Russian air bases.[2]

## Diplomatic response

Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] told Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] on Sunday, June 1, that both sides needed to engage in direct talks "to achieve a lasting peace," according to the [[state-department|State Department]], doubling down on [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s demand for negotiations even as the war escalated with intense bombings inside both countries over the weekend.[2]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Kiev's escalation gave Trump the justification he needed to walk away from the conflict entirely and gave [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] justification to seek the elimination of what he called the Banderite regime in Kiev, whether by prosecuting its leaders for war crimes against Russian-speaking Ukrainians or by conventional warfare.[2] "The acceleration is real," he wrote.[2]
