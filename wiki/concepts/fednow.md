# FedNow

FedNow is the instant payment service built by the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]. In [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s article [[are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom|"Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?"]] it is explicitly distinguished from a [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]]: FedNow, the article states, "is a peer-to-peer payment system, not a digital currency."

Despite that distinction, the article treats FedNow as an important part of the same plan. The Fed, it argues, has built a peer-to-peer platform that every financial service in the country can access, and in doing so has eliminated the fees charged by existing intermediaries. Combined with round-the-clock settlement, this is presented as a direct improvement over the [[automated-clearing-house|ACH]] network, where transfers take days, pass through a third party, and pause for bank weekends and holidays. In the article's broader framing, this infrastructure is one of the rails over which a future gold-backed digital dollar could move quickly enough to shorten the disruption of a collapse in the fiat system.
