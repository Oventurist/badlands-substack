# FedNow

**FedNow** is an instant payment service built by the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]. In the Badlands Media article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?", [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] stresses that FedNow is a peer-to-peer payment system and *not* a digital currency, a distinction he says is widely confused in commentary about [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDCs]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

Lange nonetheless calls FedNow an important part of the broader plan, because the Fed has built a peer-to-peer platform that every financial service in the country can access and which eliminates fees charged by other intermediaries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) He contrasts it with the [[automated-clearing-house|ACH]] network, where settlement takes days and a third party retains the ability to cancel transactions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Together with round-the-clock digital settlement, FedNow is presented as infrastructure that returns control and speed to individual users.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)
