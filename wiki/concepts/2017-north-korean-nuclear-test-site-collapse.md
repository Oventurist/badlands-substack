# 2017 North Korean Nuclear Test Site Collapse

The **2017 collapse of a North Korean nuclear testing facility** refers to the destruction, in October 2017, of a nuclear test site in [[north-korea|North Korea]], an event reported at the time by USA Today with casualty estimates of up to 200 killed.[1] Badlands Media's "[[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]]" series treats the collapse as the real-world referent of [[qanon|Q]] drop #41 of November 2, 2017.[1]

## Context

At the time of the collapse the summit between [[donald-trump|President Trump]] and [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] had not yet taken place, and rhetoric and "saber rattling" from the North Korean leadership were at what the chapter calls an all-time high.[1] The collapse effectively brought North Korean nuclear testing to an end, and not long afterwards Kim's posture towards the region and towards the United States shifted markedly.[1]

## Q interpretation

The chapter reads Q's treatment of the event as sarcastic, observing that no one publicly pointed to the United States as the cause of the collapse.[1] The author argues that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] would have welcomed a war at that point, so the absence of finger-pointing requires explanation, and that informed parties within North Korea would have known the event was not a simple accident.[1]

The explanation offered is that "white hats" who destroyed the complex made the destruction appear to be the consequence of a nuclear test, thereby arousing no suspicion.[1] The chapter presents this as one of many examples of "patriots" thwarting the Deep State.[1] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.

## Connection to drop #50

Chapter 6 links the episode to [[qanon|Q]] drop #50, which it reads as asserting that the Deep State has controlled North Korea since at least the Korean War, using it as a puppet state to keep tensions high in the region and to hold a war option in reserve.[1] In that account both the country and Kim himself were hostages, and Kim's change of attitude once Trump took office is offered as further evidence that he had been used as a puppet.[1] The same drop is read as asking how North Korea procured uranium, with the answer given as the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One]] deal.[1]
