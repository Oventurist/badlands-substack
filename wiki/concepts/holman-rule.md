# Holman Rule

The Holman rule is a House of Representatives procedural rule that permits appropriations legislation to be used to reduce the salary of specific federal government officials.[1]

## Restoration in the 118th Congress

Restoring the Holman rule was one of the concessions [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] granted to the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]] on 7 January 2023 in exchange for the votes that made him Speaker on the fifteenth ballot.[1] Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson groups the restoration with the other structural changes of that agreement — single-subject appropriations bills, a 72-hour review window, spending caps at fiscal 2022 levels, and a one-member [[motion-to-vacate|motion to vacate]] — as tools that collectively strip the entrenched bureaucracy of protection and make federal officials individually accountable to the House.[1] In his reading, the package meant "the Deep State Cabal was both spayed and neutered," because the corruption of the administrative state could now be investigated and defunded down to the level of named officials.[1]
