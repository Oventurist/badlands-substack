# Unacknowledged Special Access Programs

Unacknowledged special access programs (USAPs) are the most tightly compartmented category of United States classified activity, in which the existence of the program itself is denied rather than merely its contents.[1]

Commentary published in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] argued that USAPs grant the intelligence community "well-nigh limitless power to lie, manipulate, cover up, and otherwise limit the release and public recognition" of [[unidentified-anomalous-phenomena|unidentified anomalous phenomena]], whether terrestrial or extraterrestrial in nature.[1] That framing underpinned the argument that whistleblower [[david-grusch|David Grusch]]'s allegation of covert craft-retrieval programs was at least plausible, since the military-industrial complex has repeatedly advanced technologies denied to exist and only later acknowledged.[1]
