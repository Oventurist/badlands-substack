# Magdeburg Christmas Market Attack

The Magdeburg Christmas market attack was a vehicle-ramming attack in the German city of Magdeburg in December 2024 that killed five people and injured more than 200.[1]

## Suspect and proceedings

Taleb Al Abdulmohsen, a 50-year-old Saudi citizen who had lived in Germany for more than a decade and worked to help Saudis leave his home country, is accused of driving a car into the busy market.[1] Following a late-night court appearance on a Saturday, a judge ordered him held in pre-trial detention.[1] A police statement issued early Sunday said "the magistrate ordered pre-trial detention for five counts of murder, several counts of attempted murder and several counts of dangerous bodily harm," and that "the accused was taken to a correctional facility accordingly."[1]

## Motive and criticism of authorities

The motive for the attack remained unclear.[1] On social media the suspect had been a fervent critic of Islam, and prosecutors suggested he may have become embittered with how Germany treats Saudi refugees.[1] His recent messages grew increasingly threatening, including one stating: "if Germany wants to kill us, we will slaughter them, die, or go to prison with pride."[1] German authorities faced growing accusations that they could have done more to prevent the attack.[1]
