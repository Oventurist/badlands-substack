# Penisgate (2026 Winter Olympics)

**"Penisgate"** is the informal name given in Badlands commentary to an alleged ski-jumping equipment scandal at the [[2026-winter-olympics|2026 Winter Olympics]].[1]

## Allegation

According to headlines circulated on X and described by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in the February 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], ski jumpers were alleged to be injecting hyaluronic acid into their penises to inflate them before their bodies were scanned for suit fitting.[1] The purported benefit is that once the swelling subsides before competition, the athlete's suit contains extra fabric, which acts "like a sail" during flight and improves distance.[1] The Brief reported that the anti-doping league was investigating the claims.[1]

## Reception

Ashe in America introduced the story by noting that commentator Karli Bonne' had posted a video compilation of Olympic headlines that were "so outlandish, I had to look and see if they were real," and described being unable to stop laughing at this one.[1] She later tied it back to the [[2026-olympic-curling-double-touch-controversy|Canadian curling double-touch controversy]] with the closing quip that officials should "check their lockers for Hyaluronic acid."[1] The item is opinion-driven aggregation of viral social-media claims rather than verified reporting.[1]
