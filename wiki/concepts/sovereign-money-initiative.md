# Sovereign Money Initiative

The **Sovereign Money Initiative** was a Swiss ballot proposal to reform the country's monetary system, with [[full-reserve-banking|full reserve banking]] as a prominent component.[1] Swiss voters rejected it overwhelmingly in a 2018 referendum.[1]

Badlands Media cites the result as evidence that, despite recurring academic and activist interest in ending [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]], no country has adopted a full reserve requirement across its primary credit institutions; Iceland has considered the idea but not implemented it.[1]
