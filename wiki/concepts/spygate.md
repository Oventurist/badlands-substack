# Spygate

"Spygate" is the term used across Badlands Media commentary for the alleged surveillance of the 2016 [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] campaign by federal law-enforcement and intelligence agencies, and for the broader claim that the Russian collusion story was a manufactured pretext for that surveillance.[1] In the article "Do You Believe in Coincidences?", Spygate is presented as the object of investigations that were deliberately moved from the House of Representatives to the [[united-states-senate|Senate]] after the 2018 midterm elections so that the Senate Judiciary Committee's direct oversight of the [[us-department-of-justice|DOJ]] and [[fbi|FBI]] could be brought to bear on individual participants.[1]

## The alleged mechanism

According to the article, the surveillance rested on the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]], an unverified opposition-research product funded by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and compiled by [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]], which was carried into the Bureau through [[bruce-ohr|Bruce Ohr]] and [[nellie-ohr|Nellie Ohr]] and [[crowdstrike|CrowdStrike]].[1] That material became the basis of four FISA applications against [[carter-page|Carter Page]], signed by officials including [[james-comey|James Comey]], [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]], [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]], [[dana-boente|Dana Boente]] and [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]].[1] Badlands argues that Page was in fact a CIA asset rather than a Russian one, that the FBI knew this, and that FBI attorney [[kevin-clinesmith|Kevin Clinesmith]] altered an email by inserting the phrase "and not a source" to conceal it — a fact later established in the [[john-durham|Durham]] investigation and entered into court records.[1]

## Public introduction of the narrative

House investigators [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]], [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] and [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] are described as having received purposely declassified material from Trump and used it to introduce the American public to the claim that the Trump campaign had been spied on and framed by the Obama administration.[1] Their subsequent resignations are read by the article as a coordinated handoff, converting them from House investigators into "expert witnesses" who could guide Senate inquiries toward the key players.[1] Badlands links Spygate closely with [[obamagate|Obamagate]], treating the two labels as different faces of the same alleged operation.[1]

## Evidence surfaced by the Durham prosecutions

Between 2022 and the close of the [[durham-special-counsel-investigation|Durham Special Counsel investigation]], the [[michael-sussmann|Sussmann]] and [[igor-danchenko|Danchenko]] trials placed on the public record a body of Spygate evidence that had been withheld for more than five years.[2] These disclosures included the existence of a private [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] campaign spy ring staffed by cybersecurity contractors with access to federal agency databases; the campaign's provision of a target list of Trump associates; the theft of classified and non-public data used to construct the [[alfa-bank-hoax|Alfa Bank Hoax]] under the supervision of [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]]; the delivery of that product to the press, the [[fbi|FBI]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]; and the February 2017 approach to the CIA using material taken from [[executive-office-of-the-president|Executive Office of the President]] servers after [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s inauguration.[2] Testimony also revealed that FBI personnel were themselves subjects of ongoing investigations, and that more than 80 percent of the attorney–client privilege claims made by the campaign, Perkins Coie, [[fusion-gps|Fusion GPS]] and [[rodney-joffe|Rodney Joffe]]'s contractors to resist grand jury subpoenas were facially bogus.[2] Badlands contributor [[brian-cates|Brian Cates]] argued that this sequencing was deliberate — that [[john-durham|Durham]] had to dismantle the [[russiagate-hoax|RussiaGate]] narrative publicly before any major indictment for theft of classified information, espionage or treason could be tried fairly.[2]

## The Five Eyes dimension

In "Moves and Countermoves," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] frames Spygate as a foreign-intelligence operation rather than a purely domestic one, arguing that the [[barack-obama|Obama]] intelligence community led by the [[cia|CIA]] tasked [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] partners with surveilling [[donald-trump|Trump]] associates in order to evade the legal bar on CIA spying on Americans and to avoid congressional oversight.[3] On this account [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] was only one component of a broader treasonous conspiracy that also swept up candidate [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]], members of [[congress|Congress]] and the media, and the [[steele-dossier|dossier]]-based FISA warrants on [[carter-page|Carter Page]] and [[george-papadopoulos|George Papadopoulos]] were a retroactive cover for surveillance that had already occurred.[3] Lange points to the Obama administration's 2013 wiretapping of the Associated Press, flagged at the time in a [[donald-trump|Trump]] tweet linking it to [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]] and the IRS scandal, as evidence that the practice long predated the 2016 campaign, and he argues the scandal is larger than [[watergate|Watergate]].[3] Admiral [[mike-rogers|Mike Rogers]]'s November 2016 warning to Trump and [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]]'s cooperation with grand juries are cast as the countermoves that forced the conspirators into exposing errors.[3]

## SPYGATE in "The Biggest Boomerang In History"

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s article "[[the-biggest-boomerang-in-history|The Biggest Boomerang In History]]" defines SPYGATE as the original offence: a massive spy operation by the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration intended to influence the 2016 election and ensure a [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] victory, which he calls the biggest scandal in American history.[4] In his framing it precedes and underlies [[fisagate|FISAGATE]], which was the cover-up mounted after Clinton lost.[4]

Lange stresses that the spying was already under way long before the [[fbi|FBI]] applied for a [[foreign-intelligence-surveillance-act|FISA]] warrant on [[carter-page|Carter Page]]: the administration was using allied intelligence services in the United Kingdom and Australia through [[five-eyes|FVEY]] to monitor the Trump campaign and many others.[4] He asserts Obama personally ordered the operation twice over — through [[john-brennan|John Brennan]]'s [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] using FVEY partners, and through [[james-comey|James Comey]]'s FBI in the form of a counterintelligence rather than criminal investigation, so the material could be kept classified.[4]

The trap laid for [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] is presented as the point at which SPYGATE surfaced: Comey could only have known Flynn was lying about his call with the Russian ambassador if transcripts already existed, so the entrapment exposed the underlying surveillance.[4] Lange adds that a paper trail led straight to Obama because nobody expected Clinton to lose, that White House visitor logs for the FISA judge and related evidence were classified and moved to the Obama Presidential Library, and that Obama's own 2009 Executive Order forbade classifying information to conceal violations of law.[4] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
