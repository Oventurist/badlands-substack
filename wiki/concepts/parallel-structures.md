# Parallel Structures

**Parallel structures**, and the "parallel society" they collectively form, are a tactic attributed to [[vaclav-havel|Václav Havel]], the political dissident under Soviet Communist rule who later became president of Czechoslovakia.[1] Badlands Media presents the concept as one of the principal remedies against [[menticide]] and [[totalitarianism]].[1]

## Definition

A parallel structure is any form of organization, business, institution, technology or creative pursuit that exists inside a totalitarian society but is fundamentally opposed to it.[1] Typically such structures are the inversion of tools the totalitarian state already controls.[1] They must be aligned with moral and ethical principles, and those principles must be consciously known and wisely managed by the counterculture that uses them.[1] Because the would-be controllers peddle ignorance to entrap the masses, the solution must do the reverse: raise up the masses with key knowledge and understanding, and enshrine virtue, morals and ethics as primary and sacred.[1]

## Examples

The article cites uncensored [[rumble|Rumble]] as the parallel structure to censorship-centric YouTube, Signal as the encrypted parallel to data-harvesting Facebook Messenger, Protonmail as the parallel to Gmail, [[dan-bongino|Dan Bongino]]'s [[parallel-economy|"parallel economy"]] as an alternative to PayPal or Venmo, and cryptocurrency as a possible parallel to the deteriorating US dollar.[1]

## Effectiveness

In Communist Czechoslovakia, Havel observed that parallel structures were more effective at combating totalitarianism than any form of political action.[1] When enough of them are created, a counterculture or parallel society spontaneously forms and functions as an enclave of freedom and sanity within a totalitarian world.[1] The article closes by insisting that what is required above all is action by as many people as possible, warning against gamifying the resistance through popularity contests over followers or knowledge that pit patriots against patriots.[1]
