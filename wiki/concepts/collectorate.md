# Collectorate

"Collectorate" is a term used by Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] for the transnational governing apparatus — the collection of financial, corporate, intelligence and media institutions — that he treats as the real adversary in the [[multipolar-war|multipolar]] conflict, distinct from any single national government.[1]

## Usage in the June 2026 Iran analysis

In commentary on Iran's suspension of talks with Washington, Burning Bright argued that the Collectorate "seems to be panicking even as we're told the perpetuation of the Iran War narrative is actively destroying the mandate of the very movement that represents an existential threat to their existence."[1] The claim is that its "corporate Western Hegemonic propagandists" understand [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s true endgame: that the [[iran-war|Iran War]] is a story whose resolution will bring about a new vision for geopolitics, global governance, finance and trade.[1]

The Collectorate is thus positioned as the entity with the most to lose from the process Burning Bright calls the Trump Transition, and the longer that process runs, the more impossible it becomes for the Collectorate to undo the damage to its system.[1]
