# Full Spectrum Dominance

Full Spectrum Dominance is the military doctrine associated in Badlands Media's account with the consolidation of a new Pax Americana following the Cold War.[1] The article argues that the policy doctrine adopted by the administration of [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] was not that of the American patriots surrounding Representative [[curt-weldon|Curt Weldon]], but that of a network of unipolarists who sought to keep the world as divided and suppressed as possible while consolidating American possessions under a program of Full Spectrum Dominance.[1]

Between 2007 and 2016 the western unipolarists doubled down on Full Spectrum Dominance, despite the fact that the contours of world politics had drastically changed with the emergence of a Russian–Chinese alliance that became the bedrock of Eurasian integration.[1] During this period other nations were swept into turmoil by a western-manipulated [[arab-spring|Arab Spring]], followed by the 2011 bombing of Libya and the targeting of Syria for similar treatment.[1] In the Pacific, the Clinton–Obama Asia Pivot accelerated US military commitment across China's perimeter, with THAAD missiles in South Korea and roughly 100,000 troops spread across allied Asian governments.[1]

The doctrine's intellectual foundations are traced to the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]] and to [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]]'s [[grand-chessboard|Grand Chessboard]], both of which treated Russia and China as intrinsic enemies rather than potential partners.[1]
