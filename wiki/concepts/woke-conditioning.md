# Woke Conditioning

"Woke conditioning" is the term [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] uses in "A New Hollywood" for the ideological reprogramming he says has displaced heroic storytelling in [[hollywood|Hollywood]] over the past decade.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

Where earlier films featured brave heroes standing up to corrupt systems, Carlson argues that standing up to the system has been replaced with succumbing to the system.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) Even when a brave hero is the protagonist, he writes, the character must often arrive in a "woke version" — female instead of male, black instead of white, gay instead of straight, transgender instead of cisgender.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) Boys are taught their masculinity is toxic while girls are taught their femininity is weak, with both conditioned to exist somewhere in the middle, which Carlson calls "a very Baphomet concept."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

He contrasts this with a scene in [[lioness|Lioness]] in which a father rebuts the charge of transphobia by noting that a phobia is an irrational fear and that disagreement is not fear, while his [[cia|CIA]]-agent wife defends the right to challenge another's point of view — which Carlson calls the most anti-woke thing he had seen out of Hollywood in years, if ever.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)
