# Haavara Agreement

The **Haavara Agreement** was a 1933 arrangement, crafted in Matthew Ehret's account by [[adolf-eichmann|Adolf Eichmann]] with the British Empire, establishing Nazi support for the transfer of Jews from Germany to British-Mandate Palestine in preparation for the creation of a Jewish state.[1] Nazi Germany minted a commemorative coin in 1933 celebrating the agreement.[1]

## Scale and effects

The Jewish Virtual Library, quoted in the article, notes that "the Haavara transfer was a major factor in making possible the immigration of approximately 60,000 German Jews to Palestine in the years 1933–1939, and together with the money invested by the immigrants themselves, in providing an incentive for the expansion of agricultural settlement and for general economic development," adding that it "also served as a model for a similar arrangement with the Czech government and the immigration of several thousand Jews on the eve of World War II."[1]

## Interpretation

Ehret presents the agreement as part of the Nazi leadership's treatment of [[zionism|Zionism]] as an instrument of geopolitical control rather than a contradiction of its antisemitism, framing it alongside Eichmann's stated admiration for Zionism, [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]]'s alliance with Grand Mufti Haj Amin al-Husseini, and the fascist-admiring networks around Zeev Jabotinsky's [[irgun|Irgun]] as elements of a single formula for the management of chaos in a strategically important region where major civilizations intersect.[1]
