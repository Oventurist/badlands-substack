# World Health Assembly

The World Health Assembly (WHA) is the decision-making body of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], convening its member states in Geneva, Switzerland.[1]

## Seventy-sixth session, May 2023

The seventy-sixth World Health Assembly was scheduled to convene May 21–30, 2023, in Geneva to discuss the proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and a package of amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]].[1] Many analysts warned that ratification of either or both instruments would diminish or completely strip away sovereignty from nation-states, placing public health decision-making power in the hands of the WHO and its director-general.[1]

Precisely how this would operate in the United States was difficult to determine, in part because of the secrecy surrounding the negotiations and in part because of the complex interaction of domestic and international law that would have to occur for U.S. sovereignty to be affected.[1] Attorney [[reggie-littlejohn|Reggie Littlejohn]] of the [[sovereignty-coalition|Sovereignty Coalition]] said passage would "basically be the end of our national sovereignty," while professor [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]] called the instruments a front for a takeover of the United States backed by the CDC, [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]], Chinese authorities, Big Pharma, and the biowarfare industry.[1]

## Counterargument

Fact-checkers and establishment health journals argued that the accord would not compromise U.S. sovereignty. [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] disputed those assurances, pointing to the [[us-senate|Senate]] supermajority requirement for treaty ratification and to [[reid-v-covert|Reid v. Covert]] for the principle that the Constitution supersedes any ratified treaty.[1]
