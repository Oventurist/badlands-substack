# Badlands Blitz Episode 10

**Badlands Blitz Episode 10** is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly recap program produced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and published on its Substack newsletter. Dated March 14, 2026, and recorded with 244 likes at the time of the corpus snapshot, the episode carries the subtitle "On the Total Discombobulation of the Truth Community." As with previous entries in the series, the show is presented as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."

## Format and hosts

The episode is hosted by the Badlands writers [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]. The announcement text notes a deliberate departure from the program's usual structure: rather than working through the week's individual news items, the two hosts "leave micro topics to the side" for what is described as "a fiery, but mostly peaceful conversation." The stated aim of that conversation is to "lay out the prime sifting currently defining the Truth Community and the [[information-war|Info War]] as a whole circa 2026." The write-up concedes that listeners may "feel a little friction in the process," framing internal disagreement within the movement as the subject rather than an accident of the recording.

## Themes

The episode is organized around three questions posed in the announcement. The first asks whether one "can trust Trump implicitly while questioning his public moves" — a formulation of the long-running tension inside pro-[[donald-trump|Trump]] commentary between deference to the president's strategy and independent scrutiny of his decisions. The second question invokes the Badlands slogan that "the News is Fake and the War is Real" and asks whether Truthers are "actually looping back around to becoming Normies again," suggesting that segments of the alternative-media audience have re-adopted the credulity they originally organized against. The third asks "what do people get wrong when it comes to remembering the Founding Fathers," extending the discussion of self-mythology from the present movement to its historical model.

The announcement's shorthand for these interlocking subjects is "All the little things."

## Publication and support model

Consistent with the rest of the series, the post reiterates that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," and that the team is compensated only through voluntary paid subscriptions to the Substack newsletter, which readers may cancel at any time. It closes with the network's customary sign-off urging readers to "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
