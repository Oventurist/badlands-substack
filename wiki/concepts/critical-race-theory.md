# Critical Race Theory

Critical Race Theory (CRT) is an academic framework analysing law and society through the lens of racial power. In the Badlands Media corpus it is characterised not as scholarship but as a covert form of Neo Marxism introduced into public education to advance the strategic objectives of a [[new-world-order|New World Order]].

In AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] prompted [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to "include the infiltration of public education using CRT, Queer Theory and COVID mandates as covert forms of Neo-Marxism designed to achieve the strategic objectives of the New World Order," and to interweave the satanic principle of inversion throughout.

The model's synthesis assigns CRT several roles in the campaign of the fictional Global Harmony Consortium. Together with [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] it is used to portray traditional family structures as oppressive and regressive and to challenge binary gender roles and family norms, "subtly sowing the seeds of doubt within society." It supplies the ideological content that [[esg|ESG]] social and governance scores reward, pressuring corporations to adopt social justice framings that elevate collective rights and group identity over individual freedoms. And, as a tactic, it is slowly integrated into education systems as part of a patient, generational psychological operation that gradually shifts societal perspectives.


## Louisiana v. Callais (2026)

Following the Supreme Court's April 2026 ruling in [[louisiana-v-callais|Louisiana v. Callais]], Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that critical race theory is "dead – legally," since the decision removes race as a permissible engine for drawing electoral maps under the [[voting-rights-act-of-1965|Voting Rights Act of 1965]]. She framed the outcome as a gut punch to the progressive effort to expand civil rights statutes, while noting that political gerrymandering remains untouched.
