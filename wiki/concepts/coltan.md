# Coltan

**Coltan** is a rare metal ore essential to the production of electronics such as laptops and cellphones.[1] The [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] is the world's leading producer of coltan, having produced roughly 700 tons in 2021.[1] The global market for coltan was valued at $1.5 trillion in 2019 and was expected to be worth nearly $2 trillion by the end of 2026.[1]

## Strategic significance in the eastern Congo conflict

Badlands commentary on the January 2025 offensive in eastern Congo emphasised the proximity of coltan deposits to the city of Goma, which was seized by [[m23|M23]] rebels allegedly backed by the Rwandan military.[1] A resource-distribution map circulated in the brief highlighted a coltan mine near Goma, framing the fighting as a contest over mineral wealth rather than a purely ethnic or political conflict.[1]

The DRC's broader precious-metals market was estimated at more than $24 trillion in value.[1] Mineral rights in the country had long been concentrated through the relationship between former president [[joseph-kabila|Joseph Kabila]] and Israeli oligarch [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]], whose partnership effectively created a monopoly over Congolese precious metals.[1] After a British court ruling in 2023 stripped the mineral rights from Gertler and his partners, an investment firm from the United Arab Emirates signed a multi-billion dollar agreement with the DRC government to take over mining operations in the eastern region.[1]
