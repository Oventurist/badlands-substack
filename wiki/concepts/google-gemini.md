# Google Gemini

Gemini is the artificial intelligence tool developed by [[google|Google]], which generated substantial controversy in the United States in early 2024 over what critics described as pronounced progressive bias in its outputs on political, historical, racial and gender questions.[1]

## Bias controversy

Rather than delivering impartial results, Gemini was found to produce responses reflecting clear "woke" ideological influence, including image generation that declined to show favorable depictions of white people.[1] Senior Google officials publicly characterized the ideological slant of the model's responses as a "mistake."[1]

## Former employee accounts

Several former Google employees told The Free Press that the design of the tool was deliberate — a product of the progressive culture inside the company — and that its programming reflected "a corporate culture that prioritizes the ideology of diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) over excellence and good business sense."[1]

Shaun Maguire, a partner at Google Ventures, the company's investment arm, from 2016 to 2019, said: "I was not shocked at all… When the first Google Gemini photos popped up on my X feed, I thought to myself: 'Here we go again.' And: 'Of course.' Because I know Google well. Google Gemini's failures revealed how broken Google's culture is in such a visually obvious way to the world. But what happened was not a one-off incident. It was a symptom of a larger cultural phenomenon that has been taking over the company for years."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] traced Google's arc from a company whose brand equity rested on accurate search results — so dominant that its name became a verb — to one that by 2015 was visibly manipulating those results, a fact she described as indisputable by the time of President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s inauguration.[1] She argued that the willingness of companies to compromise their product stacks for ideological reasons disproved the traditional capitalist assumption that markets would punish such behavior, and that this realization pointed to the [[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]] "running on a different set of rules."[1] The presence of Marxist assumptions coded into Gemini, she concluded, was "the logical conclusion."[1]

## Loss of trust (February 2024)

By late February 2024 the controversy had produced what ZeroHedge described as a wholesale loss of trust in [[google|Google]], reporting that the "woke mess" of Gemini surprised no one who had been paying attention.[2] The outlet argued that between Google's internal culture and the fact that large language models largely reflect their creators, Gemini was the predictable digital poster-child for racist, anti-white, anti-conservative historical revisionism, rather than the neutral purveyor of information the company claimed to offer — "mask-off, as they say."[2]

The reporting highlighted Mario Juric, director of the DiRAC Institute at the University of Washington, Seattle, who wrote a lengthy post on X saying that despite the "many good individuals working there," he was "done with Google."[2]
