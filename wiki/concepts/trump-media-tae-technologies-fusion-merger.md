# Trump Media–TAE Technologies Fusion Merger

The Trump Media–TAE Technologies fusion merger is an all-stock transaction valued at more than $6 billion, announced on Thursday, December 18, 2025, combining [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]] with fusion energy developer [[tae-technologies|TAE Technologies]] to create a publicly traded company focused on nuclear fusion power.[1]

## Terms

Shareholders of each company are expected to own roughly 50 percent of the combined entity on a fully diluted basis.[1] Under the agreement, Trump Media will provide up to $200 million in cash at signing, with an additional $100 million available upon the initial filing of merger documents with the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]].[1] [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]], chairman and chief executive of Trump Media, and Dr. [[michl-binderbauer|Michl Binderbauer]], chief executive and director of TAE, are expected to serve as co-CEOs of the merged company.[1] The merger is expected to close in mid-2026, pending customary shareholder and regulatory approvals.[1]

## Assets and buildout

The combined entity will include Trump Media's platforms, such as [[truth-social|Truth Social]], alongside TAE's businesses, including TAE Power Solutions and TAE Life Sciences.[1] The companies plan in 2026 to select a site and begin construction on what they describe as the world's first utility-scale fusion power plant, with an initial output of 50 megawatts subject to regulatory approvals, and intend to develop larger facilities of 350–500 megawatts in subsequent phases.[1]

## December 2025 market reaction

The fusion pivot announced on December 18, 2025 produced a 41.93% single-day surge in [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]] shares, which traded near $15.32 the following morning after a 58% year-to-date decline.[2] Coverage framed the transaction as recasting a social media company as a diversified technology and energy conglomerate combining nuclear fusion with artificial intelligence infrastructure.[2] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that the merger made DJT the first publicly traded fusion company in history, with plans to build the first commercial fusion reactor beginning in 2026.[2]
