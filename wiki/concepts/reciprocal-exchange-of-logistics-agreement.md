# Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement

The **Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement (RELOS)** is a defense logistics pact between Russia and [[india|India]], announced by the Russian Ministry of Defense on Tuesday, February 18, 2025.[1] It is intended to enhance coordination between the two countries' armed forces in joint military exercises, disaster relief, and other joint operations.[1]

## Signing

The agreement was signed following a meeting between Russian Deputy Defense Minister Colonel-General Alexander Fomin and India's ambassador to Russia, Vinay Kumar.[1] Both sides emphasized the importance of the pact for advancing military cooperation and reaffirmed their commitment to what the Russian Ministry of Defense described as a "particularly privileged strategic partnership."[1]

## Significance

The pact is expected to improve interoperability between the Russian and Indian armed forces, particularly in exercises and in humanitarian or disaster relief operations.[1] Badlands commentary framed RELOS alongside the simultaneous [[india-qatar-strategic-partnership|India-Qatar strategic partnership]] as evidence of Indian Prime Minister [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] pursuing bilateral arrangements outside globalist multilateral structures, and argued that — combined with a more limited short-term agreement Modi signed with Xi Jinping the previous year — the defense pact with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s Russia effectively neutralizes hostility between India and China, since neither would readily provoke the other while both hold defense arrangements with their shared northern neighbor.[1]
