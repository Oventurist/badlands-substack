# J.D. Vance Baby-Face Memes

The J.D. Vance baby-face memes were a March 2025 viral phenomenon in which images of Vice President [[jd-vance|J.D. Vance]] were Photoshopped to give him cartoonishly chubby cheeks.[1]

## The phenomenon

In the edited images Vance appears as "a bearded baby or Humpty Dumpty," sometimes holding a lollipop and wearing a child's baseball cap with a propeller on top.[1] Variants added lusciously curly locks or changed his skin tone to a gentle purple hue, making him resemble "a Willy Wonka–inspired human blueberry"; in every image, [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]] wrote, Vance was "reanimated as an utter doofus."[1]

The Atlantic observed that while people commonly meme political adversaries as hapless, that was not quite what was happening here: the images were going viral on the left-wing internet but were equally or more popular on the right, with explicitly pro-Trump accounts on X circulating embarrassing memes of their own party's second in command.[1] The magazine concluded that the right had not turned on Vance, who uniquely appeals to various factions across the party, and that the simplest explanation offered was that the memes were "just funny."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the memes hilarious and said the fact that "the dorks over at The Atlantic don't understand them – or why Trump supporters would also be making/circulating them – is too perfect."[1] He argued that the magazine's writers "truly don't understand internet culture, memetics, or humor in general," all of which "embrace self-deprecation as one of the highest forms of comedy," and that "the self-righteous pillow-biters at The Atlantic are too prideful and low-energy to ever get it."[1] He concluded: "This is why we won the Meme War of 2016, and why we will win every Meme War for the rest of time."[1]
