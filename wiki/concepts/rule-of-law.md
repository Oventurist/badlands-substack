# Rule of Law

The rule of law is the principle that a society is governed by knowable, generally applicable rules rather than by arbitrary power. The [[a-startling-contradiction-at-the|Badlands Media article "A Startling Contradiction at the Heart of 'Our' Legal System"]] (May 16, 2023) devotes a section, headed "True Rule of Law," to arguing that the United States no longer satisfies this standard.

## The article's definition

The piece advances a strong accessibility criterion: "The rule of law does not exist when the citizens are unable to articulate the very laws under which they are required to operate." Stated more broadly, "you cannot have a free society if the very rules that govern that society are indecipherable by the citizens of that society." Where the purpose of law is subverted to serve any aim other than the defense of legitimate rights, the article warns, "the law itself becomes the greatest source of tyranny."

Measured against this standard, the article finds the contemporary American system wanting. It notes that the body of enacted law grows by tens of thousands of pages regularly, beyond the capacity of even intelligent and diligent citizens to follow; that lawyers and judges themselves routinely violate laws or codes unknowingly; and that the volume of the [[united-states-code|United States Code]] makes real-time enforcement — by, for example, agents of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] applying Title 26 — practically impossible. The resulting condition is likened to "a twisted game of Russian roulette," in which almost any action or omission might be characterised as illegal.

## Proposed remedy

The article calls for "a renaissance in civic awareness" as the means of restoring America and other nations. Rather than expecting individuals to master the system alone, it proposes that citizens collectively assemble a database of the legal system's "most blatant absurdities," making the present arrangement indefensible to any person of sound mind. The author illustrates the collective nature of the task with the image of passengers on a leaking boat locating and plugging the holes together. Public ignorance, the article contends, is the operating condition of the [[deep-state|Deep State]], and dispelling it is framed as ground-level work complementing political action attributed to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[q-anon|Q]] team.
