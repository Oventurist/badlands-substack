# Greater Israel Project

The "Greater Israel Project" is the term used in the Badlands corpus for an alleged long-range strategy to expand Israeli regional dominance through the fragmentation and destabilization of neighbouring states.

## In the corpus

assad day in damascus invokes the concept in the form of a rhetorical question the author says Western corporate media will never ask: "is it only a coincidence that this entire spectacle plays into the Greater Israel Project so nicely?" The fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] is thereby framed as advantageous to [[israel]], which the article notes has been targeting Iranian assets in [[syria]] and which stands to benefit from the weakening of [[iran]]'s influence through [[hezbollah]] and Syrian territory.

The article does not develop the concept at length, treating it instead as one of several beneficiary hypotheses raised in its closing speculation about who gained most from the timing of the November 2024 offensive — a question the author says only time will answer, while adding that his money "is not on that being the Syrian people."


## April 2026: Mount Hermon, the Forward Defense Zone, and Gaza

The Brief of April 20, 2026 details the project's on-the-ground progress: the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] moved northeast toward [[mount-hermon|Mount Hermon]], capturing Lebanese lands bordering the [[golan-heights|Golan Heights]] and [[syria|Syria]], and created a [[forward-defense-zone|"Forward Defense Zone"]] extending 5-10 kilometers into [[lebanon|Lebanon]], positioning Israel to annex southern Lebanon and southern Syria and to shift the maritime boundary to take the [[qana-gas-field|Qana Gas Field]]. [[bezalel-smotrich|Smotrich]] renewed calls to occupy [[gaza|Gaza]] and establish settlements, while settler founder [[pinchas-wallerstein|Pinchas Wallerstein]] condemned the rise of "Jewish terrorism" as treason. Contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argues [[donald-trump|Trump]] is deliberately "diffusing the Greater Israel Empire" and that only the emerging five-country security architecture can stop it.
