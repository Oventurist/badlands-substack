# Vatican Border Penalties Decree

The Vatican Border Penalties Decree is a January 2025 measure by which the Vatican City State enacted stiffer penalties on anyone entering its territory or violating its airspace without permission.[1]

## Provisions

Through the decree, signed by Cardinal Fernando Vérgez Alzaga, president of the Pontifical Commission for Vatican City State, violators face monetary fines ranging from 10,000 to 25,000 euros and prison sentences of one to four years — increases to both monetary sanctions and prison terms for breaches of the city-state's tight security regulations, as reported by Catholic News Agency.[1]

## Context

The [[vatican|Vatican City State]] is the world's only completely walled-in sovereign territory.[1] It has several entry points, all but one of which — the Saint Anna gate — are locked from dusk until dawn.[1] The gates are manned by the Swiss Guard, with gendarmes carrying firearms stationed not far off.[1] Badlands Media carried the item as a bonus story under the framing that the Vatican promises stiff penalties for illegal aliens crossing its border, an implicit contrast with papal advocacy on migration in other jurisdictions.[1]
