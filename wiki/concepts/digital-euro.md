# Digital Euro

The digital euro is a proposed central bank digital currency (CBDC) of the [[european-central-bank|European Central Bank]], intended to provide a euro-denominated digital means of payment issued directly by the central bank.[1]

## Rationale as articulated in 2025

In March 2025, ECB Chief Economist [[philip-lane|Philip Lane]] reiterated the need for the digital euro, emphasizing its role in mitigating risks from [[stablecoin|stablecoins]] and reducing European reliance on U.S. payment firms.[1] Lane described the CBDC as essential to ensuring Europe's monetary and financial autonomy amid increasing geopolitical fragmentation, and said it would "limit the likelihood of foreign-currency stablecoins gaining a foothold as a medium of exchange in the euro area."[1] He further argued that the digital euro could resolve fragmentation in European retail payments and act as a unifying framework for collaboration among banks and payment service providers.[1]

## Contested framing

Badlands commentators treated the ECB's digital euro campaign as a defensive reaction to the American stablecoin agenda advanced under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick.[1] In that reading, dollar stablecoins must be backed by Treasuries rather than conjured by decree, making them "more real" than fiat central bank notes and — in the commentary's conclusion — a mechanism that ends central banking altogether.[1]
