# Wrap-Up Smear

The "wrap-up smear" is a term for a political-media technique publicly described by then-House Minority Leader [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] in 2017, and treated in Badlands commentary as a core mechanic of the information war.[1]

## Mechanics

The dialectic involves first seeding claims or rumors into the media apparatus, and then citing the resulting reporting as the basis for making public comments about those same claims — even when the speaker was the one who planted them in the first place.[1]

The benefit of the practice runs to both parties involved. The politician gets to cite "reporting" as an independent source, while the media outlet gets to attribute its story to an "unnamed source close to the situation," so each side shields the other.[1]

## Application to the 2024 Democratic ticket

Writing in August 2024, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the American people were being shown the same dialectic applied to an intra-party civil war over the Democratic ticket, as the party's Old Guard all but admitted it had staged a political coup against [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] He contended that patriots including [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] understood this, which was why they were signaling the return of the #OurBoyBlue theme into the central narrative — posing the question of what it would mean if Biden was not, in fact, the establishment's man.[1]
