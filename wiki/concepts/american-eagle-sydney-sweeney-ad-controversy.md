# American Eagle Sydney Sweeney Ad Controversy

The American Eagle Sydney Sweeney ad controversy was a cultural and financial episode in late July and early August 2025 surrounding a divisive [[american-eagle-outfitters|American Eagle]] denim commercial starring actress [[sydney-sweeney|Sydney Sweeney]].[1]

## Market reaction

American Eagle's stock price rose nearly 23% as of 3 p.m. EDT on Monday, August 4, 2025, a steep increase that began hours earlier after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] posted on Truth Social praising the commercial, which the company had debuted the previous week.[1]

## Trump's remarks

Trump wrote of the campaign: "It's for American Eagle, and the jeans are 'flying off the shelves.' Go get 'em Sydney!"[1] He also hailed Sweeney's apparent voter registration, following multiple weekend reports indicating she is registered as a Republican voter in Florida.[1] Trump contrasted Sweeney favorably against "Woke singer [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]]," whom he had previously said he "hates" after the singer endorsed his election opponent, Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], in October, calling Swift "NO LONGER HOT."[1]

## Commentary

In the Badlands News Brief, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] joked that he was not claiming the president had watched [[the-narrative|The Narrative]] the preceding Sunday night, but noted that he had run a segment on "hot chicks in jeans," Jaguar, and Bud Light — in that order.[1] He framed the episode as evidence that "Cultural Whiplash is in motion," restating the Badlands maxim that "Politics is not downstream from culture; it IS culture."[1]
