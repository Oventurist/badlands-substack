# Civilization/Stealth Jihad

"Civilization jihad" or "stealth jihad" is the term used in the Badlands corpus, following writers such as Andrew C. McCarthy, for a strategy attributed to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] of advancing Islamic law in Western societies through institutional infiltration rather than armed force.

## Description in the corpus

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]] describes the strategy as twofold. Publicly, the Brotherhood denounced violence — in the article's reading, as a shield against criticism and against designation as a terrorist organization in the United States — while continuing to use global charities to fund groups such as Hamas, Hezbollah, and [[al-qaeda|al-Qaeda]]. Privately, its second track was "to infiltrate the governments of non-Muslim countries by recruiting from within." The article repeats the slogan "Infiltration, not invasion."

Universities are identified as the principal mechanism. The article states that the goal was "to indoctrinate young Muslims at universities in order to place them at the highest positions within our government," on the logic that "[i]f you control the government, you can force [[sharia-law|sharia law]] on the population." The publishing arm of the effort is the *[[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]]*, produced by the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]] and underwritten by [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]] of the [[muslim-world-league|Muslim World League]]; the campus arm is the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]].

Quoting PJMedia, the article summarizes the aim of the "Saudi-constructed, Brotherhood-conducted Islamist infrastructure" in the West: it "seeks to grow an unassimilated, aggressive population of Islamic supremacists, who will gradually but dramatically alter the character of the West," with the goal "incrementally to infiltrate sharia principles in our law, our institutions, and our public policy." This mechanism enables Muslims "to create enclaves — in the physical community, on campus, and in major social institutions, including government — in which sharia is honored as basic law."

## Application to Huma Abedin

The article treats [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] as "a perfect example of this strategy": simultaneously an intern to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], an assistant editor at the *JMMA*, and a member of the MSA executive board at [[george-washington-university|George Washington University]]. It also links the strategy to Q drop #4750, which contains the bracketed phrase "[INFILTRATION not invasion][VJ _HA]," read by the author as naming [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] and Abedin as coordinated handlers within the [[barack-obama|Obama]] and Clinton operations.
