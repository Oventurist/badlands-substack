# Civilization/Stealth Jihad

"Civilization/stealth jihad" is the term the Badlands account uses for the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s long-term strategy of advancing sharia acceptance in non-Muslim countries by non-violent means — summarized in the article's recurring slogan, "Infiltration, not invasion."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The strategy is described as twofold. Although the Brotherhood publicly denounced violence — a posture the article calls a shield against criticism and against designation as a terrorist organization in the United States — it continued to use its global charity network to fund groups such as Hamas, Hezbollah and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The second element was to infiltrate the governments of non-Muslim countries by recruiting from within, chiefly through universities, which serve as recruiting grounds for future politicians and bureaucrats.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The stated aim, in the words of the PJMedia analysis quoted at length, is to grow an unassimilated, aggressive population of Islamic supremacists who will gradually but dramatically alter the character of the West, and "incrementally to infiltrate sharia principles in our law, our institutions, and our public policy."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The mechanism is enclave-building — in the physical community, on campus, and in major social institutions including government — where sharia is honored as basic law and Muslims are given safe haven from pressures to assimilate.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Two institutional vehicles carry the strategy in the article's telling: the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]], published by the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]] and used to supply universities worldwide with Brotherhood-aligned scholarship, and the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]], with hundreds of campus chapters across North America.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]] underwrote both.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] is offered as the exemplary product of the strategy, and the article claims it culminated in the installation of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
