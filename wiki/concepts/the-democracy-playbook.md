# The Democracy Playbook

*The Democracy Playbook* is an annually updated publication of the [[brookings-institution|Brookings Institution]] edited by [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]], which Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterizes as a domestic [[color-revolution|color revolution]] planning guide rather than a neutral work of democratic scholarship.[1] The most recent edition cited in his 2025 article is the January 2025 version published on the Brookings website.[1]

## Context and companion works

DeLarme situated the *Playbook* within a body of Eisen's output that he read as operational rather than academic. Eisen is described as having written a "classic color revolution regime change manual" conveniently titled *The Playbook*, and Brookings also published the 300-page volume *Overcoming Trumpery* with Eisen as editor.[1] Eisen is said to chair the color revolution planning arm of Brookings itself.[1]

## Interpretation

In DeLarme's framing, publications of this kind belong to the "language of control": a body of jargon in which terms such as "democracy," [[democracy-promotion|"democracy promotion"]] and [[rule-of-law|"rule of law"]] carry a public meaning that reassures ordinary readers and an operational meaning understood within the establishment.[1] The techniques the guide is said to systematize — contesting electoral legitimacy, organizing mass protest and civil disobedience, and leveraging sympathetic media coverage — were, on his account, developed for use against foreign governments before being turned inward against American political movements.[1] He argued the same methodology would be applied to any anti-establishment figure, including a hypothetical [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]]-style Democrat, and not only to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
