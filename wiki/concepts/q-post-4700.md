# Q Post 4700

**Q post #4700** is a drop cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] as the doctrinal cornerstone of his account of American politics since 2016.[1]

## Contents

According to Lange, the post came in two parts.[1] The first part set out what an [[insurgency|insurgency]] is, drawing on the [[counterinsurgency-guide|Counterinsurgency Guide]]'s description of insurgent political strategy — persuasion, subversion and coercion.[1] The second part explained how the insurgency was to be defeated, presenting the doctrine of [[counterinsurgency|counterinsurgency]].[1] Lange reproduced images of both portions in his December 28, 2022 essay "What's Taking So Long?".[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

Post #4700 is one of four Q drops — alongside #4008, #4363 and #4364 — that link to the Counterinsurgency Guide.[1] Lange argued that Q did this for a reason: it was important both to know what the military strategy was and to understand why justice has taken so long to arrive.[1] On his reading the post establishes that the United States is fighting an insurgency that has infiltrated government at every level and society more broadly, and that the response is a comprehensive, simultaneous civilian and military campaign.[1]
