# India-Russia Working Group on Modernization and Industrial Cooperation

The India-Russia Working Group on Modernization and Industrial Cooperation is a bilateral mechanism through which New Delhi and Moscow coordinate industrial and technological collaboration.[1] On August 6, 2025, at Vanijya Bhawan in the Indian capital, the two governments sealed the "Protocol of the 11th Session" of the working group.[1]

## Scope of the protocol

The accord reaches into strategic domains that the outlet EuroSiberia described as pillars of sovereignty.[1] Beyond traditional industries such as aluminium and rail transport, the protocol addresses cooperation in aerospace, rare earth and critical minerals extraction, underground coal gasification, and the creation of advanced industrial infrastructure.[1] EuroSiberia framed these sectors as the material and energetic foundations that allow a civilization-state to shape its own path, casting the signing as geopolitics conducted as sacred craft rather than an ordinary treaty.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] summarized the development in two words — "Trade Routes" — and argued that public controversy over Russia-India oil tariffs functions as a cover story for an underlying decoupling and rerouting of global trade, dismissing engagement with the tariff narrative as misguided.[1]
