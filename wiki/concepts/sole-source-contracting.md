# Sole source contracting

**Sole source contracting** is a federal procurement method in which an award is made to a single vendor without competition, on the justification that no other provider is capable of performing the work.[1]

An October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] article by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the [[red-dawn-iii|Red Dawn III]] delivery order to [[icr-inc|ICR, INC.]] was awarded sole source, meaning no other bids were considered.[1] The same justification — that ICR alone could deliver — appears in the vendor's records going back to 2016.[1]

## Criticism

The article argued that the use of sole source contracting could indicate corruption, of the kind alleged in election systems.[1] It described a pattern in which government builds a process around a specific vendor and then avoids competitive bidding by claiming only that vendor can do the work, citing as an example the many counties that contract ballot printing to [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]] on a sole source basis.[1] The piece also raised the question of whether shipboard defense systems genuinely required no custom programming during the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, when the supposedly irreplaceable vendor received no awards.[1]
