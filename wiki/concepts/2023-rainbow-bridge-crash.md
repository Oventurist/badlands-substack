# 2023 Rainbow Bridge Crash

On Wednesday, 22 November 2023, a speeding car crashed in flames on the Rainbow Bridge, which links New York State and Ontario at Niagara Falls, killing the vehicle's two occupants and triggering a security scare that closed four U.S.–Canadian border crossings.[1]

## Incident

Security-camera video posted to X by [[customs-and-border-protection|U.S. Customs and Border Protection]] showed the car traveling at high speed from the U.S. side, striking an object, flying into the air and exploding in flames on landing.[1] The driver and a passenger died; a CBP officer suffered minor injuries, was treated at a hospital and released.[1]

## Investigation

Federal and state authorities said hours later that investigators had found no evidence of terrorism, though the circumstances remained murky and it was undetermined whether the crash was accidental or intentional.[1] New York Governor [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]] told reporters that evening, "At this time, there is no indication of a terrorist attack" or threat to the public, and federal and local law enforcement officials echoed her at a separate news conference.[1] The [[fbi|FBI]] said it had concluded its investigation, stating in a post on X that "a search of the scene revealed no explosive materials, and no terrorism nexus was identified."[1]

Authorities did not identify the two people killed; CNN reported the driver was a 56-year-old man traveling in a Bentley with his wife to attend a KISS concert.[1]
