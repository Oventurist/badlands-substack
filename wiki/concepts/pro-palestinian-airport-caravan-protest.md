# Pro-Palestinian Airport Caravan Protest (New York, 2023)

At the turn of 2024, a caravan of pro-Palestinian demonstrators snarled traffic around John F. Kennedy International Airport and later LaGuardia Airport in New York on one of the busiest travel days of the year.[1]

## Conduct of the protest

Videos obtained by [[fox-news|Fox News]] Digital showed protesters with a bullhorn chanting: "NYPD, KKK, IDF they're all the same!"[1] Video posted to social media showed dozens of vehicles waving Palestinian flags, honking horns, and shouting at police officers.[1]

## Response

The Port Authority subsequently shut down all entrances to JFK for people without a boarding pass, and Airtrain services were closed, with cars unable to enter the terminal area.[1] The protest group said the airport was "swarming with all kinds of law enforcement," adding that "organizers were forcibly removed from Terminal 4."[1] The chant directed at the [[nypd|NYPD]] equated the department with the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]].[1]
