# Decentralized Autonomous Organization

A **Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO)** is cited in Part 4 of the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series as a working example of a "sovereign [[holon|holon]]" — a self-organizing structure that uses the recursive logic of control systems for the opposite purpose.[1]

## Description in the article

The essay describes DAOs as built on blockchain infrastructure, with members voting, funding, and governing without intermediaries.[1] They are grouped with Earthship communities — self-sustaining, off-grid living environments — regenerative eco-villages that operate on feedback from the land and their members rather than bureaucracies, and homeschooling co-ops described as micro-holons replicating education outside the matrix without top-down standardization.[1] All are said to remain on the periphery.[1]

## Role in the exit thesis

These structures illustrate the article's claim that the threshold for exiting fifth-dimensional control is coherence rather than confrontation.[1] When individuals, communities, and technologies synchronize around decentralization, transparency, and feedback alignment, the essay argues, systems theory predicts a bifurcation point at which the existing attractor can no longer dominate and a new self-organizing structure arises.[1] In a later section the same logic is extended to "Holonic Value Networks," community-led blockchain currencies, and time-based economies, framed as early signals of an economy in which trust is the currency and external enforcement becomes unnecessary.[1]
