# Solana

Solana is a [[proof-of-stake]] blockchain network cited in Badlands Media's coverage of digital assets as an example of the centralization risks built into stake-weighted consensus.[1] Independent journalist [[ryan-matta|Ryan Matta]] examined the project in an October 2022 video, "What is Solana?? Solana Price Predictions," in which he detailed how stake-based validator selection can create barriers to fair access for retail investors.[1]

Matta's broader critique was that in projects of this type the founding team retains enough stake at launch to control and change the rules of the blockchain — behavior that would constitute antitrust violations and market manipulation in traditional markets.[1] The article's closing assessment grouped Solana with the Saitama token affair and IMF pressure on nation-state monetary policy as reasons its author remained skeptical of the promised decentralized utopia.[1]
