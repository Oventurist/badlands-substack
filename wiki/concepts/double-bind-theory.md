# Double Bind Theory

The double bind is a concept formulated by [[gregory-bateson|Gregory Bateson]] describing the mental trap produced when a subject is confronted with contradictory imperatives.[1] Bateson observed that a victim will often break down into a schizophrenic state when presented with contradictory messages, an effect that could be intensified with the assistance of hallucinogenic cocktails.[1] The article gives the illustrative example of a person taught from an early age to "be good to others" who is then presented with information demanding that, in order to be good, they must kill their fellow humans — producing predictable mental dissonance.[1]

## Application to the ecological debate

Badlands Media argues that the modern environmental movement is structured as a mass double bind.[1] Some subjects conclude that they must save nature from humanity — as outlined in [[morges-manifesto|Sir Julian Huxley's Morges Manifesto]] — by influencing the system from above to shut down industrial civilization, thereby killing billions.[1] Others encounter the same false claim that technological growth equals evil and express their goodness by declaring war on industrial civilization from below, in the manner of [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]] and groups such as [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]] and Deep Green Resistance.[1] Either way, the article contends, the effects lead to the same techno-feudal dystopia, making the apparent choice a false dichotomy.[1]

The proposed escape from the bind is to change one's fundamental axioms: to recognize that technology is neither intrinsically good nor evil and that overpopulation is not a genuine problem, but that tolerance of evil ideas shaping the behavior of technology and political systems is what turns those systems fascist and collapses them into dark ages.[1]
