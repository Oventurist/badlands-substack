# State Department Dissent Memos on Israel Policy

In November 2023, scathing internal "dissent memos" criticizing White House policy toward the Israel–Gaza war began circulating within the U.S. government, collecting many hundreds of signatures chiefly from [[state-department|State Department]] and [[usaid|USAID]] staff.[1]

## Background

A revolt was described as brewing within the Biden administration over the [[white-house|White House]]'s handling of the war, as the civilian death toll and mass Palestinian displacement in [[gaza|Gaza]] soared and as President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s top officials continued to say that "no conditions" had been placed on how Israel uses U.S.-supplied weapons.[1] Pressure from the press pool was also mounting, with near-daily spats and antagonistic exchanges in the State Department and White House briefing rooms.[1]

## Content of the memos

A primary theme of the internal pushback was that Biden must change course on the Gaza crisis.[1] On the Monday of that week, [[politico|POLITICO]] obtained and published portions of a memo issued by State Department personnel offering a blistering critique, which the publication reported argued, among other things, that the United States should be willing to publicly criticize the Israelis.[1]

## Commentary

The story was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of November 10, 2023.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] presented the revolt as "Exhibit A" of why the Israel crisis was backfiring on the [[deep-state|deep state]] establishment, noting that Biden's own administration — State Department personnel and White House aides — regarded his handling of the situation as a disaster.[1] He argued that the key takeaway was concern over damage to the standing and reputation of the United States on the world stage, and observed the irony that most of the dissenting figures had risen in direct opposition to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and were now likely looking back on his administration with envy.[1]
