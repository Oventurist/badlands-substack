# Las Vegas Cybertruck Explosion (January 1, 2025)

On the morning of Wednesday, January 1, 2025, a Tesla Cybertruck exploded outside the Trump International Hotel in Las Vegas, Nevada, killing one person and injuring at least seven others.[1] Government officials told ABC News that the explosion was being investigated as a "possible act of terror."[1]

## Investigation

Sheriff Kevin McMahill of the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department said at a news conference that authorities "believe this to be an isolated incident," while cautioning that they had not ruled out a connection to the [[new-orleans-bourbon-street-attack]] earlier the same morning, which killed at least 15 people.[1] "There is no further threat to the community," McMahill said.[1] As of the afternoon of January 1 there was no indication that the Las Vegas explosion was connected to [[isis]], which President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] said had inspired the New Orleans attack, though the investigation remained ongoing.[1]

## Musk's response and the vehicle

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] confirmed that "large fireworks and/or a bomb" had detonated in the vehicle, and posted on X: "The evil knuckleheads picked the wrong vehicle for a terrorist attack. Cybertruck actually contained the explosion and directed the blast upwards. Not even the glass doors of the lobby were broken."[1] Badlands noted that despite Musk's claim about the vehicle's structural performance, Tesla stock still fell in after-hours trading on Wednesday.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the Badlands News Brief, Ashe in America summarized the casualty count and the containment claim, and relayed Patrick Gunnels' argument on the Devolution Power Hour (episode 315) that the only reason to use a Cybertruck in a terrorist attack was to send a message to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Elon Musk.[1] Jon Herold added that more terror events were likely before Inauguration Day.[1]
