# Good Friday Agreement

The Good Friday Agreement is the 1998 accord that established the political settlement underpinning peace in Northern Ireland.[1]

In April 2023 US President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] traveled to Northern Ireland to mark a quarter-century since the agreement, beginning with events in Belfast.[1] The visit was overshadowed by a security incident in which a document outlining police deployments for the trip was found on a street by a member of the public, prompting an investigation by the [[police-service-of-northern-ireland|Police Service of Northern Ireland]].[1]

Badlands Media's coverage used the anniversary trip as a hook for commentary by [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on an earlier, less-publicized Biden visit to Ireland in September 2017, during which [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[jim-biden|Jim Biden]] met representatives of [[cefc-china-energy|CEFC China Energy]].[1]
