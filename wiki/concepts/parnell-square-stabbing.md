# Parnell Square Stabbing

The Parnell Square stabbing was a knife attack carried out on Parnell Square East in Dublin, Ireland, in November 2023, in which several young children and a creche worker were wounded outside a school.[1]

## The attack

One child aged five remained in critical condition in hospital the day after the attack, while two other young children and a female creche worker in her thirties were also treated for injuries.[1] The creche worker suffered serious stab wounds to her body when she tackled the knifeman on the Thursday afternoon of the attack, and was described in reporting as heroic.[1] A Brazilian Deliveroo driver was hailed as "a true hero" for helping to stop the attacker during the incident.[1]

## Investigation

Gardaí ruled out a terror-related motive and held a man aged in his forties who was himself injured during the incident and who also remained in hospital.[1] The school attended by the injured children released a statement on the victims as the girl remained in critical condition.[1]
