# DHS Targeted Violence and Terrorism Prevention Grants

The Targeted Violence and Terrorism Prevention grant program is a [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] anti-terrorism funding initiative that became the subject of controversy in May 2023, when documents showed it was financing a university program that had explicitly grouped the Republican Party and Christian and conservative organizations into the same category as Nazis.[1]

The [[media-research-center|Media Research Center]], a conservative watchdog group, obtained the documents through Freedom of Information Act requests and shared them exclusively with Fox News Digital, publishing its findings in a report arguing that the conduct uncovered warranted criminal prosecution.[1] MRC founder and president [[brent-bozell|Brent Bozell]] said the terrorism task force was "engaged in an active effort to demonize and eliminate Christian, conservative, and Republican organizations using federal taxpayer dollars."[1]

## Commentary

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, observed that some readers treated the disclosure as a declaration of civil war by the Biden administration, though he suggested it was likely occurring without President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s knowledge given that he did not appear to be a significant decision-maker.[1] Drawing on historical parallels, DeLarme argued that when a government labels part of its own population as terrorists or applies another dehumanizing designation, it has typically served as a pretext for action against the target group, in its most extreme form genocide; he stopped short of predicting that outcome but said the regime appeared indifferent to — and might even welcome — the worst case.[1]

DeLarme urged readers to repair distortions in their relationships with family, friends and neighbors as momentum built toward what he described as engineered open conflict between subgroups of the citizenry, recommending the book *The Citizen's Guide To Fifth Generation Warfare - An Introduction To 5GW* by [[michael-flynn|LTG Mike Flynn]] and [[boone-cutler|SGT Boone Cutler]] as an accessible primer on [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth generation warfare]].[1] He closed by adapting Flynn's maxim that "local action creates national impact" to interpersonal relationships, arguing that personal connection can outweigh propaganda.[1]
