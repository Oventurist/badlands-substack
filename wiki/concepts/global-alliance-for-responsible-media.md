# Global Alliance for Responsible Media (GARM)

The Global Alliance for Responsible Media (GARM) was a cross-industry advertising initiative that, according to the [[federal-trade-commission|Federal Trade Commission]], served as a vehicle for collusion among major advertising firms to blacklist center-right media outlets.[1]

## FTC findings

In its June 2025 review of the [[omnicom|Omnicom]]–[[interpublic-group|Interpublic Group]] merger, the FTC noted that both advertising giants were founding members of GARM, and that through the initiative there was collusion to deny advertising revenue to outlets including Breitbart News, Fox News, and the Daily Wire.[1] FTC Chairman [[andrew-ferguson|Andrew Ferguson]] cited that history of "collusion in the [advertising] market for media-buying services" as grounds for imposing a behavioral remedy as a condition of merger approval, requiring the combined firm not to discriminate on political or ideological viewpoint.[1]

## Significance

Critics of the advertising cartel argue that GARM-style coordination functioned as a privatized censorship mechanism: outlets that challenged official narratives — on subjects such as vaccine safety and election integrity — were financially starved regardless of the accuracy of their reporting.[1] Badlands commentary compared the potential impact of unwinding this arrangement to overturning Citizens United in the elections context.[1]
