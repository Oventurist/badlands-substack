# Berlin to Baghdad Railway

The **Berlin to Baghdad Railway** was begun with the intention of unleashing a bold program of modernization across southwest Asia, spearheaded by the German rail builder George von Siemens.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites the project as evidence that not even the [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman Empire]] remained untouched by the inspiration for progress associated with the [[american-system|American System]] of political economy, which was spreading through Germany's Zollverein, Meiji Japan, and [[sergei-witte|Sergei Witte]]'s [[trans-siberian-railway|Trans-Siberian Railway]].[1] It was precisely this diffusion of sovereign industrial development that the article identifies as the threat driving the [[british-empire|British Empire]] and [[edward-vii|Edward VII]] toward the [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]].[1]
