# Canada's Red Shadow

*Canada's Red Shadow* is a documentary film shot by [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] dealing with ideological capture in Canada.[1]

Esler has said that while shooting the film he experienced directly the phenomenon by which young people described as "trans youth" come to believe that a "trans genocide" is currently underway.[1] He treats this as an instance of the staged propaganda mechanics later described by a student in [[the-coddling-of-the-american-mind|The Coddling of the American Mind]], who recounted being taught that efforts to cure autism constitute an "autism genocide" — the two narratives, held side by side, being mutually revealing.[1] The film informs Esler's broader argument about [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]] and the testimony of [[detransitioners|detransitioners]].[1]
