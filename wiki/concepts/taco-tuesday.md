# TACO Tuesday

"TACO Tuesday" refers to the market-and-media shorthand — derived from the acronym "Trump Always Chickens Out" — used by traders and commentators who assume that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] will reverse aggressive policy positions, particularly tariff threats, before they take effect. In [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] Episode 14, the hosts devoted a segment to "what the 'TACO Tuesday' crowd gets wrong about Donald Trump."[1]

## Badlands critique

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the TACO framing misreads Trump's negotiating behavior, mistaking deliberate sequencing and leverage for capitulation.[1] The segment sat within an episode otherwise framed around institutional accountability, and was presented as another instance of conventional analysis failing to model Trump's actual method.[1]

## April 2026 Iran ceasefire application

The label resurfaced in April 2026 when Trump unilaterally extended a fragile US–Iran ceasefire rather than resuming strikes, prompting renewed cries of "Trump Always Chickens Out" from the intellectual dark web, alt-media skeptics, and forever-war advocates.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] responded that the refrain is "completely, spectacularly wrong" because critics project onto Trump an end goal — a war with [[iran|Iran]] — that he never held.[2]

In that reading, the real objective is energy: rewiring global routes and flows and breaking the "scarcity cartel's" grip on supply chains and pricing does not happen through a single dramatic strike, but through controlled pressure, calibrated signaling, and a slow squeeze that forces allies and adversaries alike into realignment with an emerging multipolar order.[2] Evidence offered included surging rather than collapsing American energy exports and calibrated sanctions relief flowing toward Russia and China while the "Hegemon" is squeezed out.[2] The same sequence culminated in Trump's invocation of the [[defense-production-act|Defense Production Act]] on April 20, 2026.[2]

## Hormuz toll reversal (July 2026)

The Badlands Brief of 15 July 2026 was headlined "TACO Tuesday Returns as the War of Stories Gets Weird," applying the label to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s abandonment of the [[hormuz-transit-fee-proposal|20% Hormuz transit fee]] in favor of trade and investment deals with Gulf states.[3] Contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the reversal not as a retreat but as "geopolitical jiu-jitsu," noting that it unwound during a [[white-house|White House]] meeting with Iraqi Prime Minister [[ali-al-zaidi|Ali al-Zaidi]] and was converted into an announcement of free trade in the Persian Gulf and an investment opportunity benefiting the American people.[3] The episode illustrates the Badlands counter-reading of the TACO framing: what critics score as chickening out, the movement scores as a bargaining sequence that ends in a better deal.[3]
