# Soros Shadow Network

The "shadow network" is the term Badlands Media uses for a group of private organizations said to work together to exercise a powerful hidden influence over the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic Party]].[1] Named members include the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]], [[actblue|ActBlue]], the [[gill-foundation|Gill Foundation]] and [[moveon-org|MoveOn PAC]].[1]

## Thesis

The article contends that the Democratic Party maintains only the veneer of an honest party made up of individuals with differing perspectives, and that in reality a subversive network operates within the Democratic machine to ensure compliance with [[uniparty|Uniparty]] agendas.[1] Its author explicitly frames this as one arm of a two-sided structure, noting that comparable subversive Republican networks built around the Bush, Romney, McCain, Cheney, Murdoch and Koch families feed the same beast, and that the two-party system is the result rather than the cause of the problem.[1]

The network came about, per the article, through a series of political, legal and financial maneuvers that gave an amalgamation of political activists and billionaires de facto control over the party, particularly its campaign apparatus.[1] Its kingpins are described as literal kingmakers who hand-select candidates before the people have spoken — the [[democratic-national-committee|DNC]]'s rigging of the 2016 primary against [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] in favour of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] being the paradigm case.[1] The article predicts [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|RFK Jr.]] would learn the same lesson in the following year's primary.[1]

To function, the network must operate as stealthily as possible, using secretive, manipulative and extra-constitutional means: infiltrating bureaucracies, corrupting officials and manipulating the press.[1]

## Intellectual lineage

[[saul-alinsky|Saul Alinsky]], an early mentor of Hillary Clinton and [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and of many other network operatives, is credited with identifying the real pathways to power in American politics — teaching that radicals could achieve sweeping change without majority support so long as they understood the rules of the game and how to exploit them.[1] The article's summary: "Alinsky wrote the rules, and Soros mastered the art."[1]

## 2004 and the boast

The network's pervasiveness became far more evident in the 2004 cycle, when it contributed over $300 million to the Democratic war chest through a cadre of leftist billionaires, using donations and independent media campaigns to shape the narrative and mold a new generation of voters.[1] On 9 December 2004, [[eli-pariser|Eli Pariser]] of MoveOn PAC told members: "Now it's our party. We bought it, we own it."[1]

## Operatives and offshoots

[[zack-exley|Zack Exley]] — former MoveOn organizing director, later a consultant to [[justice-democrats|Justice Democrats]] — is presented as a connective figure, moving between MoveOn, the Sanders campaign, [[john-kerry|John Kerry]]'s campaign, the UK Labour Party and the [[wikimedia-foundation|Wikimedia Foundation]].[1] Justice Democrats, founded in 2017, endorsed Rashida Tlaib, Ayanna Pressley and Ilhan Omar and recruited [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]].[1]

## Foot soldiers

After narratives take root, the article says, the next phase is to create a legion of foot soldiers: [[antifa|Antifa]] and Black Lives Matter, funded by the network and driven by Soros operatives, with sensationalist media backing.[1] The primary goal was to cast doubt on the legitimacy of the police and the legal system, an operation whose target audience — in [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth Generation Warfare]] terms — was centrist and left-leaning individuals.[1]

## Caveats

The article closes by describing itself as one contributor's dig, opening the subject to community additions, and Badlands Media notes that its articles represent the opinions of contributing authors.[1] Several strands — notably the speculation about hierarchy above Soros — are explicitly labelled by the author as unresolved.[1]
