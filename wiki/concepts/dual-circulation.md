# Dual-Economic Circulation

"Dual-economic circulation" is the Chinese Communist Party policy of reorienting China's economy away from its historical reliance on exports toward a more balanced model that strengthens domestic industries while continuing to engage in international trade.[1]

## Function within the fortress economy

The policy sits at the center of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s [[fortress-economy|"fortress economy"]] strategy, which seeks national self-sufficiency and resilience against external shocks including protracted armed conflict.[1] By fostering robust internal economic activity, the CCP hopes to mitigate the impact of global disruptions on China's economy while preserving the benefits of foreign trade.[1]

Analysis of official CCP speeches and policy documents, published on July 30, 2024 by Jimmy Goodrich of the University of California Institute on Global Conflict and Cooperation, situated dual circulation alongside food and energy security, supply-chain robustness, civil defense mobilization, and strategic reserve infrastructure as components of Xi's national security agenda.[1] The strategic rationale drew on the U.S.-China rivalry, the Russian invasion of Ukraine, and the COVID-19 pandemic, each of which exposed the fragility of interconnected global supply chains.[1]
