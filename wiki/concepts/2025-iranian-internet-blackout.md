# 2025 Iranian Internet Blackout

The 2025 Iranian internet blackout was a near-total communications shutdown inside Iran during the June 2025 war with Israel.[1] By the time the conflict reached its one-week mark, Iranians had spent nearly half of the war in a communications blackout, unable to reach either the outside world or neighbours and relatives elsewhere in the country.[1]

## Effects

Civilians were left unaware of when and where Israel would strike next, even though Israeli forces were issuing warnings through Persian-language online channels that most Iranians could not receive.[1] When missiles landed, disconnected phone and web services meant families often did not learn for hours or days whether relatives or friends were among the victims.[1] Many people turned to whatever social media applications still functioned, producing only a fragmentary glimpse of conditions in a nation of more than 80 million people and an uneven external picture of the war.[1] The blackout was reported by [[abc-news|ABC News]].[1]
