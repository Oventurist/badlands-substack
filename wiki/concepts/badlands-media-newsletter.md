# Badlands Media Newsletter

The **Badlands Media Newsletter** is the Substack publication of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], announced in a "Coming soon" launch post dated October 9, 2022.[1]

## Launch

The announcement post identified the publication simply as "the Badlands Media Newsletter" and described its publisher as a collective of citizen journalists committed to producing the best content possible while remaining grounded in honesty and integrity.[1] The post closed with a subscription prompt, the standard mechanism by which Substack publications build a mailing list before regular publishing begins.[1] The post accumulated 132 likes.[1]

## Significance

As the first entry in the corpus, the post establishes the newsletter's framing: an independent, reader-supported outlet operating on a subscription newsletter platform rather than through an established media company.[1]
