# Eastern Sentry

Eastern Sentry is a [[nato|NATO]] air defense operation launched in September 2025 to reinforce the alliance's eastern flank following incursions of Russian drones into Polish airspace.[1]

## Composition

Under the initiative, British fighter jets were to conduct air defense missions over [[poland|Poland]].[1] The [[royal-air-force|RAF]] Typhoons involved were based in Lincolnshire and were to work alongside forces from Denmark, France and Germany to strengthen NATO's eastern defenses, with a Voyager refuelling aircraft supporting the missions.[1] The operation's stated aim was to deter potential aggression from [[russia|Russia]].[1]

## Political framing

British Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Sir Keir Starmer]] emphasized that "Russia's reckless behaviour is a direct threat to European security."[1] Defence Secretary [[john-healey|John Healey]] stated that NATO had responded with unity to the challenge posed by Moscow.[1]

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] offered a sceptical reading, remarking that the allies were "not defending Poland as hard as they didn't defend 'Ukraine' against Putin's Very Brutal Invasion."[1]
