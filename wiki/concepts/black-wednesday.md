# Black Wednesday

Black Wednesday is the name given to 16 September 1992, the day on which the British pound collapsed and the United Kingdom was forced out of the European Exchange Rate Mechanism (ERM) — the currency exchange-rate system that prepared the way for the euro.[1] Badlands Media presents the episode as the signature demonstration of [[george-soros|George Soros]]' willingness to engage in morally dubious currency speculation for personal gain.[1]

## The trade

In 1992 Soros concluded that the pound would have to be devalued, on the grounds that it had entered the ERM at too high a rate.[1] He spent months building a gigantic trading position aimed at profiting on a colossal scale, reportedly borrowing around 6.5 billion British pounds and converting the holdings into a mixture of German Deutschmarks and French francs.[1] On 16 September the gamble paid off.[1]

Over the following days Soros unwound his positions, causing a tidal wave of selling of the pound on foreign exchanges; the currency's value plummeted and Britain was forced out of the ERM.[1]

## Aftermath

Soros made a profit of around £1 billion and became known as "the man who broke the Bank of England."[1] Defending the pound against his bets cost Britain roughly £3.3 billion, and the resulting financial turmoil was widely viewed at the time as a national disaster.[1] To Soros, the article notes drily, massive currency-speculation gambles for personal gain are all in a day's work.[1]

He is said to dabble daily in currency markets on a vast scale, and his alleged role in sparking the 1997 Asian financial crisis led a group of Southeast Asian countries to call for him to be prosecuted as a criminal.[1]

## Interpretation

Within the article's larger framework, Black Wednesday is not merely a trade but evidence for the thesis advanced by William Engdahl in a 1996 Executive Intelligence Review piece: that Soros robs from rich Western countries in order to loot more savagely from the East under the cloak of philanthropy.[1] This connects the market activity to the [[soros-shadow-network|shadow network]]'s political operations as two faces of one predatory system.[1]
