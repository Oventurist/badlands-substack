# China-Pakistan Economic Corridor

The China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC) is the flagship mechanism through which Pakistan and much of Southwest Asia are increasingly integrated into China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]].[1]

## Context

The corridor is cited as part of the wider infrastructure build-out associated with the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]], which has undertaken more than twenty large-scale projects in transportation, energy and telecommunications.[1] Twenty Arab states have signed onto the BRI, and much of Latin America has joined with hundreds of billions of dollars of infrastructure projects.[1] Pakistan is itself a full SCO member.[1]
