# Karmic Retribution

"Karmic retribution" is a term that entered circulation in the online "truther" community in early 2023 and was described by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in his January 24, 2023 Badlands Media essay "The World is a Vampire" as a concept he had not previously encountered.[1]

As Carlson defines it, the concept refers to the practice of telling a victim in advance that they are going to be victimized before the abuse takes place.[1] The advance warning does not necessarily mean the victim approves of or complies with the abuse; rather, it establishes that the abuser gave notice, and thereby posits that the victim bears part of the blame for the abuse subsequently received.[1] Carlson writes that the "Elites" of what he calls the Satanic [[cabal|Cabal]] are said to believe they will incur less blame or spiritual responsibility for their abuses if they warn their victims of them, directly or indirectly, in advance.[1]

## Relationship to predictive programming

Carlson presents karmic retribution as the missing motive behind [[predictive-programming|predictive programming]].[1] Using entertainment media to push an agenda or narrative is, in his framing, straightforward propaganda; what karmic retribution explains is the otherwise puzzling decision to warn the intended victims of their own future abuse — the volume of advance signalling he identifies around events such as 9/11 and COVID.[1] His answer to why this occurs is a single word: ideology, which he characterises as sick ideology.[1]

## Relationship to the vampire metaphor

The concept underpins the central analogy of the essay. Carlson notes that in folklore a vampire cannot enter a home unless invited in, and argues that this explains the effort expended through entertainment media to tell audiences what the Cabal intends to do and how.[1] The Cabal, in his reading, requires the public's "buy-in" or tacit approval, believing this allows them to avoid the full brunt of karmic retribution.[1]
