# Unrestricted Warfare

*Unrestricted Warfare* is a book of Chinese military doctrine frequently cited in Badlands Media analysis as evidence that the Chinese Communist Party has held a sophisticated understanding of multi-domain and [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] since the 1990s.[1]

## Doctrinal themes cited

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] quoted the text on the blurring of categories in modern conflict: "[T]he progressive breaking down of the distinction between military and technology and civilian technology and between the professional soldier and the non-professional warrior the battlespace will overlap more and more with the non-battlespace serving also to make the line between these two entities less and less clear."[1]

Esler also drew on the book's treatment of coalitions and motive, quoting its argument that "so-called common interest has become merely the war equation's largest common denominator that can be accepted by every allied party participating in the war effort," that different countries and even domestic interest groups pursue distinct agendas within the same war, and that consequently "the biggest difference between contemporary wars and the wars of the past is that, in contemporary wars, the overt goal and the covert goal are often two different matters."[1]

## Applications in Badlands analysis

Esler invoked the text twice in the February 16, 2024 Badlands News Brief. In assessing [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s remarks that Russia would prefer a [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] victory over [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], he used the overt/covert distinction to argue that Putin could simultaneously be a self-interested nationalist seeking America's dethroning and be invested in the fall of what Badlands writers call the American Deep State.[1]

In discussing the [[paperwall|Paperwall]] network of fake local-news websites exposed by [[citizen-lab|Citizen Lab]], Esler argued the crudeness of the campaign was inconsistent with the doctrine's sophistication, suggesting such visible operations may be couched within, or serve to distract from, covert multi-domain activity.[1]
