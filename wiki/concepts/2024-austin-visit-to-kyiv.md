# 2024 Austin Visit to Kyiv

In October 2024, U.S. Secretary of Defense [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] arrived in Ukraine's capital by train on an unannounced visit, at a moment when Ukrainian forces were being steadily pushed back in the east and President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] was expressing frustration at the lack of large-scale new military aid.[1] The trip came shortly before the U.S. presidential election.[1]

"It's been absolutely remarkable that Ukraine has been able to do what it's done," Austin told reporters as he entered Ukraine on Sunday night. "It's been able to do that, of course, because of the fact that we have supported them from the very beginning, and we've rallied some 50 countries to be a part of that support."[1]

In Kyiv, Austin announced $400 million in new arms for Ukraine but did not accede to the Ukrainians' principal request — permission to strike Russia with U.S.-supplied weapons.[1] The Wall Street Journal emphasized that it was one of the smaller aid packages announced by the Biden administration and included no new types of weapons systems.[1] The item was carried as a bonus entry in the Badlands News Brief, sourced to [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]
