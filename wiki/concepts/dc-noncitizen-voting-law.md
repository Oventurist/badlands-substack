# D.C. Noncitizen Voting Law

The **D.C. noncitizen voting law** was a District of Columbia measure granting noncitizen residents the right to vote in municipal elections, which the U.S. House of Representatives voted to overturn in February 2023.[1]

## Congressional action

On Thursday, February 9, 2023, the [[us-house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] voted 260–173 to overturn the law, with 42 Democrats joining Republicans.[1] Under the Constitution, Congress has the ability to block laws passed in the District, a power available because the District is not a state.[1] For the repeal to take effect, it also had to pass the Democratic-led Senate and be signed by the President; while President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] said he opposed the D.C. law, he had not stated whether he would veto the repeal.[1] Jason Snead, executive director of Honest Elections Project Action, applauded the vote, saying the House had acted "to ensure that foreign spies and illegal immigrants are not voting in the nation's capital" and calling the D.C. measure "extreme and antidemocratic."[1] The Federalist framed the vote as "a win for election integrity."[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], opened with a sarcastic rendering of the expected partisan reaction before arguing the policy was self-evidently unwise.[1] He invoked the fall of the Roman Empire, attributing it in part to mass migration caused by the Huns' invasion of Europe in the late fourth century, and predicted Democrats would "instruct these migrants how to vote" after providing benefits, swelling the populations of sanctuary cities such as San Francisco and Los Angeles and thereby increasing their federal funding.[1] DeLarme listed further causes of Rome's collapse — economic troubles, military overspending, government corruption, political instability, loss of traditional values, weakening of the legions, and the rise of a competing empire in the East — and asked whether the United States is "the perfect modern mirror" to pre-collapse Rome, concluding that "patriots aren't letting it happen without a fight."[1]
