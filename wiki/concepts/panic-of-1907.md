# Panic of 1907

The **Panic of 1907** was the American financial crisis that, in the Badlands Media narrative, furnished the pretext for central banking reform.[1]

After the Panic, Senator [[nelson-w-aldrich|Nelson W. Aldrich]] set up the [[national-monetary-commission|National Monetary Commission]] to investigate a better economic system for the United States. The Commission concluded that the European model, which included the Central Bank of England, was superior, and its reports and recommendations became one of the principal bases for the enactment of the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act of 1913]] and the creation of the modern [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve System]].[1]
