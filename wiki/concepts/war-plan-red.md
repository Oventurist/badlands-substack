# War Plan Red

**War Plan Red** was a United States military contingency plan for war against the British Empire, presented in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as evidence that leading American military figures once regarded British Canada as a hostile power rather than a friendly neighbour.[1]

## Origins

Ehret cites the 2010 LaRouche PAC documentary *"The Special Relationship is for Traitors"* as showing that during the early 20th century leading American military figures such as Brigadier General [[billy-mitchell|Billy Mitchell]] understood Britain's role in supporting the Confederacy and Britain's manipulation of global wars.[1] Mitchell is described as having fought against the Anglo-American "special relationship" tooth and nail, and as having led the military to create War Plan Red and War Plan Orange to defeat Britain in the context of an eventual war between the English-speaking powers.[1]

The plans became official US military doctrine in 1930.[1] In Ehret's account they were crafted in response to Anglo-Canadian plans to invade the United States that had been drafted ten years earlier, known as Defence Scheme 1.[1] He notes that even the most ardent anti-American historians admit that the Canadian plans were entirely offensive in nature, as no prior US plan to invade Canada then existed.[1]

## Abandonment

War Plan Red was only taken off the books, Ehret writes, when America decided it was more important to put down "London's Fascist Frankenstein threat" than to fight Britain head on in the Second World War.[1]
