# Jesus Is King

Jesus Is King is the 2019 gospel album released by [[kanye-west|Kanye West]], described by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as the most commercially successful gospel album of all time.[1] Badlands treated the record — alongside [[mel-gibson|Mel Gibson]]'s The Passion of the Christ — as evidence that individual artists can spread Christian content to mass audiences despite what the outlet calls a Satanic cabal controlling American culture.[1] The accompanying tour included an October 2019 performance by West and his gospel choir on a CBS late-night program.[1]
