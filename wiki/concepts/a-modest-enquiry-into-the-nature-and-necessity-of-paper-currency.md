# A Modest Enquiry into the Nature and Necessity of Paper Currency

*A Modest Enquiry into the Nature and Necessity of Paper Currency* is the 1729 pamphlet in which, according to the Badlands Media essay "In Defense of Benjamin Franklin," [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]] drafted some of the most important policies leading to sovereign control over currency.[1]

The essay describes the work as codifying a system of banking tied not to the worship of money or markets but to internal improvements, and as arguing for the creation of colonial scrip not controlled by private central bankers.[1] These insights are said to derive from Franklin's studies of Colbertist dirigism, and to have preceded the later work of his protégé [[alexander-hamilton|Alexander Hamilton]], who established the American System of political economy in his reports of 1790–91.[1]
