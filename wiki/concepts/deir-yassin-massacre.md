# Deir Yassin massacre

The **Deir Yassin massacre** took place in April 1948 in the Arab village of Deir Yassin, just outside Jerusalem, and is described by Badlands Media as the culmination of the Zionist terror campaign known as the [[nakba|Nakba]].[1]

## Events

According to the Badlands account, fighters of the [[irgun|Irgun]] and the [[lehi|Lehi]] — characterized as the two most notoriously violent and ruthless of the Zionist paramilitary groups — entered the village with the tactical support of the [[haganah|Haganah]] and [[palmach|Palmach]], rounded up over 100 women and children, and gunned them down in cold blood.[1]

## Aftermath

Badlands writes that the act was sufficient to inspire the surrounding Arab population to pack up and leave, achieving the depopulation objective of the wider campaign.[1] One month later, the four militias formally combined to create the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli Defense Force]].[1] The massacre is offered as the explanation for the depth of Palestinian hostility toward the IDF.[1]
