# START I

The Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty (START I) was signed by the United States and [[russia|Russia]] in 1991.[1]

Among its effects, the treaty was supposed to have eliminated the possibility of nuclear weapons being transported aboard surface ships, although many observers have often questioned Moscow's adherence to it.[1] The point resurfaced in August 2024 when leaked documents reported by the [[financial-times|Financial Times]] indicated that Russia remained evidently capable of transporting nuclear weapons on surface ships — a practice experts say carries significant risk of escalation or accident — as part of the reporting on alleged [[russian-nuclear-strike-plans-leak|Russian nuclear strike plans against NATO targets]].[1]
