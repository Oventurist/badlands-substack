# Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons

The **Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT)** is the international agreement governing the spread of nuclear weapons technology and the right of signatory states to pursue peaceful nuclear programs. It appears in Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s analysis of the [[twelve-day-war|12-Day War]] as the legal basis for [[iran|Iran]]'s uranium enrichment activity.[1]

## Relevance to the Iran dispute

DeLarme notes that as an NPT member, Iran has the right to do what it is doing under international law — namely enriching uranium, currently to a level of 60 percent, short of the roughly 90 percent that constitutes weapons-grade material.[1] This makes Iran a [[threshold-state|threshold state]] rather than a nuclear-armed one.[1] DeLarme cites Professor [[john-mearsheimer|John Mearsheimer]] and others who have said there is no evidence Iran was pursuing nuclear weapons, a position he says has been supported by the International Atomic Energy Agency; he allows that contrary intelligence may exist but observes that it is not open source and therefore cannot be tested by the press or the general public.[1]
