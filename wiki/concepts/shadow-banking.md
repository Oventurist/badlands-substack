# Shadow Banking

**Shadow banking**, in the sense used in the Badlands corpus and in US Treasury sanctions language, refers to networks of shell companies, exchange houses, trading firms and front operations that move money outside the regulated correspondent banking system. In the [[iran|Iranian]] case, such networks are described as moving tens of billions of dollars globally to evade sanctions and to fund military and proxy operations, notably those of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]].

## Sanctions target

The April 2026 [[operation-economic-fury|Operation Economic Fury]] designations by the [[department-of-the-treasury|Department of the Treasury]] hit 35 individuals and entities described as nodes in this network. Treasury's framing emphasizes terrorist financing and "Iranian proxies."

## Badlands reading

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argues that the shadow banking architecture is not distinctively Iranian but is "the ancient mechanism by which the Shadow Banking Empire has operated since its Prussian origins" — fused with British imperial precedent and perfected across the twentieth century into what he calls the invisible rails of the modern [[deep-state|Deep State]]. On this reading shell companies constitute the plausible-deniability layer that allows the hegemon to project power without showing its hand, and the "Iranian" label functions as a narrative veil that makes a much broader dismantling politically palatable.
