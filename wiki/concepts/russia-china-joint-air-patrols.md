# Russia-China Joint Air Patrols

Russia-China joint air patrols are combined bomber flights conducted by the Russian and Chinese militaries, which have expanded steadily since 2019.[1]

## Expansion since 2019

According to a Newsweek mapping of the flights, the two militaries have widened their joint patrols since 2019, with bombers — including aircraft capable of carrying nuclear weapons — flying beyond East Asia into the broader Pacific and near Alaska.[1] Liu Pengyu, spokesperson for China's Embassy in Washington, DC, told [[newsweek|Newsweek]] that the maneuvers were part of annual cooperation intended to demonstrate the two sides' determination and capability to jointly address regional security challenges.[1]

The tenth joint air patrol took place on December 9, 2025 near Japan.[1] The Russian Defense Ministry had previously said that flight was part of the Russia-China military cooperation plan for the year and denied that it was directed against third countries.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the patrols in terms of great-power transition, writing that "in order to have a [[multipolar-world|Multipolar World]], you must first win a Multipolar War."[1] He referenced his continued writing on the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], a term he coined, and a long-form essay arguing that the alliance had already won and that the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s "doom is at hand."[1]
