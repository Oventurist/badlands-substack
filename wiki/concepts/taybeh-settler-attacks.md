# Taybeh Settler Attacks

Taybeh is a Christian town in the West Bank that was the target of a violent assault in July 2025, prompting a joint condemnation from the Patriarchs and Heads of Churches in Jerusalem.[1]

## The attack

Several vehicles were set ablaze and hateful graffiti was sprayed in what church leaders described as an unambiguous act of intimidation directed at a peaceful and faithful community rooted in the land of Christ.[1] Days earlier, settlers had forcibly entered Taybeh, herding livestock into the heart of the town; masked individuals, some armed and others on horseback, roamed the streets spreading terror.[1] Fire reached the walls of the town's ancient church, described as a living testament to the enduring Christian presence in the Holy Land.[1]

## Church response

In a statement issued through the [[latin-patriarchate-of-jerusalem|Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem]], the church leaders said the incident was not isolated but part of an alarming pattern of settler violence against West Bank communities, their homes, sacred spaces, and ways of life.[1] They expressed grave concern at a prevailing climate of impunity that undermines the rule of law and jeopardizes peaceful coexistence, warning that the lack of accountability threatens not only Christian communities but the moral and legal foundations of peace and justice for all.[1]

The statement demanded that the Israeli government act with moral clarity by holding those responsible accountable without delay, ensuring effective and consistent protection for the people of Taybeh and all vulnerable communities, and upholding its obligations under international law with guarantees of equality before the law.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the Patriarchs were openly accusing masked Israeli settlers of terrorizing Christian towns in the West Bank while Israeli police and the Israeli government downplayed and covered up the incidents.[1]
