# Artificial General Intelligence

Artificial general intelligence (AGI) is a hypothetical technology that could perform most tasks at or above the level of a human.[1] Such systems do not currently exist, but the leading AI laboratories are working toward them, and many observers expect AGI to arrive within five years or less.[1]

## Security framing

A U.S. government–commissioned report by [[gladstone-ai|Gladstone AI]], published in March 2024, warned that the rise of advanced [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] and AGI has the potential to destabilize global security in ways reminiscent of the introduction of nuclear weapons, and in the worst case could pose an "extinction-level threat to the human species."[1] The report urged the U.S. government to move "quickly and decisively" to address these risks.[1] Its authors interviewed more than 200 government employees, experts, and staff at frontier AI companies including [[openai|OpenAI]], Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta.[1]

Commentary in the Badlands News Brief questioned the framing, arguing that the report ignored foreign, weaponized AI already being used in psychological operations against the American public.[1]
