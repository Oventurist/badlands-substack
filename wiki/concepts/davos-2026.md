# Davos 2026

**Davos 2026** refers to the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] Annual Meeting scheduled for January 19–24, 2026 in Davos, Switzerland. The gathering was expected to bring together more than 3,000 leaders, including nearly 60 heads of state, under the theme "A spirit of dialogue," which organizers framed as especially significant amid a fragmented global landscape.[1]

## Agenda and leadership

Andre Hoffmann, the forum's Interim Co-Chair, said the key discussions would center on growth, geopolitics, innovation and climate, and emphasized public-private cooperation as the mechanism for tackling interconnected global challenges.[1] The meeting was also slated to address the role of artificial intelligence and its governance alongside what organizers described as urgent climate priorities, with Hoffmann framing the forum's goal as identifying pragmatic pathways to sustainable prosperity "without harming the planet."[1]

The 2026 meeting marked the first Davos gathering held without the leadership of forum founder Professor [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]].[1] The anticipated attendance of U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was presented by the forum as consistent with its continued support for U.S. leadership in addressing worldwide issues.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the moment in terms of an ongoing conflict against the [[media-industrial-complex|media industrial complex]], the administrative state, the central banking cartel and the [[globalist-hegemon|globalist hegemon]] — "a War for Justice, a War for Peace" — and assessed that his earlier forecast for the year had held up reasonably well.[1]
