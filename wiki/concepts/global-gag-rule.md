# Global Gag Rule

The **Global Gag Rule** is a United States policy that bars foreign organizations receiving US global health funding from providing, counselling on, or advocating for abortion services.[1] It was reinstated by the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in early 2025.[1]

## 2025 reinstatement

The reinstatement accompanied a broader disruption of US foreign assistance. On 20 January 2025, Trump signed an executive order imposing a 90-day freeze on all US foreign aid, a decision that the [[international-commission-of-jurists|International Commission of Jurists]] and allied civil society groups said had produced immediate consequences including clinic closures, suspension of life-saving disease treatment programmes, disruption of human rights and rule-of-law initiatives, and a funding crisis for NGOs worldwide.[1] Critics argued the gag rule further threatened access to sexual and reproductive healthcare services.[1]

## Reaction and commentary

More than 60 civil society organizations joined an urgent appeal calling on European Union leaders to address the resulting global development aid crisis, urging the EU — described as the world's largest foreign aid provider — to prioritize funding for reproductive rights, gender equality, and LGBTIQ rights, and to press Washington to reverse course.[1] Badlands commentary treated the appeal as confirmation that the funding freeze was working as intended, arguing that American voters had rejected such spending at the November 2024 election and that whether to sustain these programmes was now a question for Europeans alone.[1]
