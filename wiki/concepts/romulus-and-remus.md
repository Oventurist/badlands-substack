# Romulus and Remus

**Romulus and Remus** are the brothers of the Roman foundation myth, cited in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — Creation Myth Clues" as an instance of ancient werewolf symbolism.[1]

The article recounts that the creation story of [[roman-empire|Rome]] holds the city to have been founded by the brothers, who as young children were suckled by a she-wolf.[1] The author treats this detail as remarkable because the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult practiced child grooming and had been riding from the Eurasian steppes into Western and Southern Europe in the centuries prior to Rome's founding.[1]

The essay adds that the Koryos arrived in waves over many centuries, bringing with them a culture of debauchery, drug use, demon worship and child grooming, and notes that the Roman aristocracy was itself famous for its love of debauchery.[1] The author observes that the word *debauchery* derives from [[bacchus|Bacchus]], the Roman god of wine-making, ecstasy and ritualized insanity, and asks what the odds are that these correspondences are coincidental.[1] He pairs the Roman myth with the Mayan [[popol-vuh|Popol Vuh]], whose founding figure travels with a woman who can shapeshift into a wolf, as evidence that Roman and Mayan traditions share a common source.[1]
