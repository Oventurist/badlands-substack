# Moho Discontinuity

The **Moho Discontinuity** (abbreviated MD) is presented in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — Creation Myth Clues" as evidence of hidden structures beneath the Earth's surface.[1] The article states that the phenomenon was discovered in 1909 by Andrija Mohorivivic, and asserts that "city-sized caves" exist roughly 30 kilometers underground "literally everywhere on Earth."[1]

## Alleged sabotage of research

According to the article, a large research project intended to study the Moho Discontinuity was disbanded — "or, more likely, sabotaged" — immediately before it was due to begin.[1] The author writes that the project "died on arrival" and was abandoned abruptly, and reasons that if saboteurs were involved they chose the most expensive possible moment to halt the work, perhaps in order to drain as many resources as possible before pulling the plug.[1] He concedes that sabotage cannot be established with certainty.[1]

## Interpretation within the series

The essay ties the Moho Discontinuity to an earlier installment of the series, which proposed that a portal to hell may be located near or within the [[black-sea|Black Sea]].[1] The author argues that the MD "gives us further evidence that there is SOMETHING going on beneath our feet," in the same place where many of the world's tribes and religions locate hell.[1] He states that the coverups of history and of the Moho Discontinuity both point to a situation in which humanity is "not being permitted to know the truth about our realm."[1]

The article further notes that the presence of demons is traditionally associated with the smell of sulphur, and that the Black Sea contains an unusual amount of sulphur, making it deadly to most living creatures.[1] Because the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult is said to hail from that region, the author suggests that something subterranean may have "surfaced" there, which he offers as an explanation for the emergence of the wolf cult in that part of the world.[1]
