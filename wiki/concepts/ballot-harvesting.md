# Ballot Harvesting

Ballot harvesting is the practice by which completed absentee ballots are collected by a third party and delivered to a polling office or election authority on the voter's behalf. The practice is strictly prohibited in at least six US states, and many other states impose partial limits on who may submit a ballot other than the voter.[1]

## Legal status

Because election administration is governed state by state, the legality of third-party ballot collection varies widely. Some states have no policy restricting the practice, while others — including Pennsylvania and Alabama — have statutes that expressly prohibit it. Mass collection of ballots by an organization therefore risks violating the law in some jurisdictions even where the same activity is lawful in others.[1]

## 2024 Israel case

Ahead of the November 5, 2024 US presidential election, a [[jerusalem-post|Jerusalem Post]] investigation reported a "potentially illegal" ballot-harvesting operation in central Israel run by [[ivoteisrael|iVoteIsrael]], a group that promotes absentee voting by American immigrants living in Israel. The report raised the prospect that ballots gathered en masse abroad could have been returned to states where such collection is barred.[1]

Badlands Media commentary treated the episode as a sovereignty question rather than a partisan one, asking readers why an overseas harvesting operation aimed at a US election would be treated with more indulgence than the same conduct by any other foreign country.[1]

## Related election-integrity themes

Discussion of ballot harvesting in Badlands coverage sits alongside broader arguments about the structural vulnerability of US election systems, including [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]]'s call for the incoming administration to reform election systems outright rather than rely on layered "defenses in depth" to protect results.[1]

## Lara Trump's 2024 pledge

In February 2024, ahead of her election as co-chair of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]], [[lara-trump|Lara Trump]] told [[epoch-times|The Epoch Times]]: "We need to have the biggest legal ballot harvesting operation this country has ever seen."[2] The report noted that ballot harvesting is legal in many states, allowing people to deliver other voters' absentee or mail-in ballots to election officials, that some fear the practice facilitates election fraud, and that Republicans have long frowned upon it while Democrats have taken advantage of it.[2] Of Democratic practice she said, "Whether or not they do it legally, that's up for discussion," arguing it was time for Republicans to start "attacking the game differently."[2]
