# Twenty-Fifth Amendment

The Twenty-Fifth Amendment to the United States Constitution governs presidential succession and the removal of a president deemed unable to discharge the powers of the office. In Badlands coverage it functions primarily as a recurring instrument in schemes to remove [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from office.[1]

## The 2018 removal talk

In September 2018 *The New York Times* reported that Deputy Attorney General Rod Rosenstein had discussed invoking the amendment against Trump. Q posted about the amendment for the first time on the day of that report, September 21, 2018, sharing the Times link and later pairing it with an article about Omarosa Manigault Newman conspiring against the president; Q would return to the 25th Amendment many times thereafter.[1]

## The Bannon–Epstein exchange

A text exchange dated December 31, 2018 between [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], surfaced amid the 2026 Epstein disclosures, included Bannon's line "I think it's beyond borderline — 25 amendment."[1] Mainstream reporting treated the message as evidence Bannon wanted Trump removed; Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued instead that Bannon was describing the Democrat and Rosenstein-linked removal strategy already in public circulation, calling the resulting coverage slander rather than revelation.[1]

## The 2024 threat against Joe Biden

The amendment resurfaced in July 2024 when investigative journalist [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]] reported, citing a senior Washington official, that former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] had used the threat of a 25th Amendment removal to force [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] out of the 2024 presidential race.[2] According to that account, Obama telephoned Biden after breakfast on July 20 — in the wake of an incident in Las Vegas that outwardly appeared to be a serious fall with a blow to the head — and said, "Here's the deal. We have Kamala's approval to invoke the 25th Amendment."[2] [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] and [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] were reportedly directly involved in the effort.[2]

The [[conservative-treehouse|Conservative Treehouse]] characterized the episode as a coup and argued it tracked with the broader pattern of the Obama team's control inside the Biden White House, noting Hersh's source saying Obama "had an agenda and he wanted to seek it through to the end, and he wanted to have control over who would be elected."[2]

## Speculation after the June 2024 WSJ report

Following The Wall Street Journal's June 2024 report on [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s behavior in closed-door meetings, Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] asked whether the [[mockingbird-media|mockingbird media]] were seeding a Twenty-fifth Amendment deployment, recalling [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s remark that "the 25th would be a problem for Crooked Joe."[3] She judged that removing or replacing Biden before or during the Democratic convention was unlikely as matters then stood.[3]

## Invoked in the 2023 Biden competency debate

The amendment featured in commentary surrounding the May 2023 letter in which 61 House Republicans led by Rep. [[ronny-jackson|Ronny Jackson]] demanded that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] take a mental competency test or withdraw from the 2024 race.[4] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] asked why, given widespread doubts about Biden's cognitive state, there were no accompanying calls to invoke the 25th Amendment or to file articles of impeachment, and quoted [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 12, 2021 remark that "the 25th Amendment is of zero risk to me but will come back to haunt Joe Biden and the Biden administration."[4]
