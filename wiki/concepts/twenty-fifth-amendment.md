# Twenty-Fifth Amendment

The Twenty-Fifth Amendment to the [[united-states-constitution|United States Constitution]] governs presidential succession and disability, including a mechanism by which the Vice President and a majority of the principal officers of the executive departments may declare a sitting president unable to discharge the powers and duties of the office.

## Use as a political instrument during the first Trump term

In the Badlands corpus the amendment appears chiefly as a proposed instrument for removing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from office during his first term, and as a marker within the [[q|Q]] posting record.

The February 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0a2|Badlands Brief]] recounts that [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] reported in September 2018 that Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] had discussed invoking the amendment against Trump. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] notes that Q posted about the amendment for the first time on September 21, 2018 — the day the story was first reported — linking the *Times* article, and hours later reposting the same link together with an article about [[omarosa-manigault-newman|Omarosa Manigault Newman]] conspiring against Trump. Q would go on to reference the amendment many times.

## The Bannon–Epstein exchange

The same article reports that a December 31, 2018 text exchange between [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], surfaced in the Epstein disclosures, included Bannon writing "I think it's beyond borderline — 25 amendment" after Epstein described Trump as "really borderline." The exchange occurred shortly after the midterm elections shifted control of the House of Representatives.

The disclosure produced competing readings. Mainstream coverage treated it as evidence that Bannon had contemplated Trump's removal. GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued the opposite: that Bannon was describing an adversary strategy already in public circulation because of the Rosenstein reporting, and that the exchange should be read in light of what he regards as a staged Trump–Bannon estrangement dating to August 2017.
