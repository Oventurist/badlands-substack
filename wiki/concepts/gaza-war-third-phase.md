# Gaza War Third Phase

The **third phase of the Gaza war** refers to the wind-down stage of Israel's ground campaign in the Gaza Strip that Israeli media reported in December 2023 was being prepared for the following weeks.[1]

## Reported plan

The Israeli Public Broadcasting Corporation reported on Friday, December 22, 2023, citing unnamed sources, that the Israeli army "is preparing to move to the third phase of the fighting in Gaza during the coming weeks, in accordance with operational achievements."[1] According to those sources, the third phase would include ending the ground operation in the Gaza Strip, reducing army forces and demobilizing reserves, shifting to reliance on air strikes, and establishing a buffer zone along the border between Israel and Gaza.[1]

The report claimed the army had taken control of most of the northern Gaza Strip while facing great difficulty advancing in the south.[1]

## Context and commentary

The reported de-escalation appeared alongside news of continued escalation on Israel's northern front, where the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] struck a [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] command center in Lebanon in response to cross-border attacks.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] paired the two headlines deliberately, arguing that globalist media outlets "can never seem to get their stories straight" and that their projections routinely diverge from reality.[1] In his reading, the juxtaposition allowed the establishment to signal a planned de-escalation — earning credit for humanitarian restraint — while obtaining tacit permission to intensify bombing following reported escalation by whichever adversary was at hand.[1] He argued that the real signal amid the noise was U.S. kinetic and financial involvement in the conflict, which he expected to become a unifying concern for both the [[america-first-movement|America First movement]] and the broader American citizenry.[1]
