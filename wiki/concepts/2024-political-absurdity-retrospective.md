# 2024 Political Absurdity Retrospective

The 2024 political absurdity retrospective refers to a year-end accounting of the most politically absurd episodes Americans endured in 2024, published by The Federalist and answered with an expanded month-by-month list from Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].[1]

## The Federalist's list

The Federalist argued that 2024 demonstrated there is no bottom in matters of the politically absurd, citing the decision to have [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] debate [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Fani Willis's insistence that her paramour Nathan Wade was worth every dime, and [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]]'s belief that her "Old Yeller moment" would be endearing — followed by the Trump transition team's judgment that the dog-killing South Dakota governor and close personal friend of Corey Lewandowski would make a fine Homeland Security secretary.[1]

## Ashe in America's expansion

Ashe in America called the premise fun but unsatisfying, noting that by November 2024 it had become indisputably obvious that Biden did not receive 81 million votes, yet Americans were still expected to pretend, and that The Federalist would not go there.[1]

Her own month-by-month additions ran as follows: in January, Ron DeSantis ended his presidential run and endorsed Trump; in February, Nikki Haley lost to "none of these candidates" in Nevada; in March, [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] won his Senate primary, which she said proved California elections are hopelessly corrupted; in April, the total solar eclipse closed schools and government buildings and threatened ongoing primaries; in May, Trump's civil fraud trial proceeded while CNN reported there was "no evidence" to support his charge that the indictments were a Democratic plot; in June, the Trump–Biden debate ended years of assertions that Biden was capable and cogent; in July, the elevation of candidate [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]; in August, [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] dragged Trump into court again amid teased indictments out of Georgia; in September, [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] was indicted after speaking out against what she called the Biden-enabled criminal invasion; in October, the media insisted there were no October surprises while Nazi rhetoric turned up to eleven, along with GarbageGate; in November, the absurdity of Harris's turnout numbers, with 21 million voters missing until counting finished a month later; and in December, the [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] rerun.[1]

She noted she had focused only on political stories, skipping business, science, "fake wars" and pop culture, and concluded that the entire central narrative had jumped the shark, that the situation was amusing but also enraging, and that there must still be accountability for the deception and corruption — urging that "politics as usual" be made to include integrity by dropping the hammer when it does not.[1]
