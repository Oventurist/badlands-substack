# Polar Silk Road

The Polar Silk Road is the Arctic extension of China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], developed in conjunction with Russian northern and Far Eastern development programs.

## Description in the corpus

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s Avoiding WWIII states that since 2018 Russia's eastern development ambitions have increasingly merged with China's northern extension of the BRI, which has amplified the growth of railways, roads, telecommunication hubs, ports, energy projects and sea corridors "through regions long thought inhospitable to human civilization." The article treats the Polar Silk Road as one arm of a "Russian Manifest Destiny" aimed at extending civilization into the Siberian Far East and Arctic and beyond Central Asia, Mongolia, Japan and China.

It is closely tied to Russia's [[northern-sea-route|Development Plan for the Northern Sea Route]], adopted in 2019, and to the modernization of the [[trans-siberian-railway|Trans-Siberian Railway]]. Ehret argues that these projects, together with the proposed [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait tunnel]], constitute the practical content of a twenty-first-century [[manifest-destiny|Manifest Destiny]] carried out by [[russia|Russia]] and [[china|China]] rather than the United States.
