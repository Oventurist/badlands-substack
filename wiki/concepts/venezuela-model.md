# Venezuela Model

The **Venezuela Model** is a term used in Badlands Media commentary — particularly by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] — for a template of American intervention in which the visible head of a hostile government is removed while the surrounding governing structure is left intact, stabilized, and made amenable to negotiated deals, in contrast to occupation-based regime change.[1]

## Definition and mechanics

As described in the March 2026 Badlands Brief, the model consists of "the appearance of decisive action against the top figurehead, while the majority of the governing structure remains intact, stabilized, pragmatic and suddenly open to the deals that sovereignty and America First demand."[1] In [[venezuela|Venezuela]], the operation removed [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] as the visible lightning rod, cut off cartel pipelines said to feed globalist proxies, installed continuity under a sovereignty-aligned figure, and moved directly into energy cooperation and stability narratives — with no occupation and no ground troops.[1]

Burning Bright contrasted this outcome with the alternative allegedly preferred by globalist actors, an NGO-backed regime change scenario centered on [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]].[1] He coined the term **Sovereign Disentanglement** for the resulting posture, describing it as "wearing the mask of the very thing the enemy had spent years demanding."[1]

## Application to Iran

Badlands commentary applied the model to [[operation-epic-fury|Operation Epic Fury]], the US–Israeli air campaign against [[iran|Iran]], arguing that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] told *The New York Times* repeatedly that he was following the Venezuela template.[1] Under this reading, the surface story supplies neoconservatives with the optics of a new forever war, while the actual trajectory is a rapid pivot toward negotiated stabilization — a shift said to explain why figures such as [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] were sounding alarms rather than celebrating.[1]

## Codification for the Iranian transition (March 2026)

After the death of [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he had to be involved in appointing Iran's next leader "like with Delcy [Rodríguez] in Venezuela," dismissing Khamenei's son as "a lightweight" and calling for a figure who would bring harmony and peace.[2] Badlands analyst [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that "the Venezuela Model is in full effect," treating Trump's explicit references to it as the clearest positive signal in his handling of what he called the Iranian Kobayashi Maru.[2]

In that reading, the template delivered functional continuity in Caracas by removing the primary lightning rod while preserving core governing structures under a pragmatic, US-aligned hand, and applying it to Iran converts what globalists envisioned as a forever-war catalyst into an internal realignment that maintains Iranian sovereignty while cutting the strings that made it an asset for regional destabilization.[2] Burning Bright cited the fracturing of transatlantic support — [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] declaring the strikes outside international law and a Spanish MEP calling for withdrawal from [[nato|NATO]] — as a reverse indicator that the outcome does not feed the perpetual war machine.[2] He described the result as an engineered off-ramp "wearing the credible optics of its opposite."[2]

## Application to Iran (2026)
[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued in June 2026 that the Iranian confrontation is the Venezuela Model applied at larger scale, citing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s own comparisons: "just like the results in Venezuela… the results in Iran will be amazing," and "this is being perfectly executed, on the scale of Venezuela, just a bigger, more complex operation."[3] Because the template is understood, he claimed, the process in [[iran|Iran]] "can be mapped in detail" rather than merely cheered from the sidelines or glimpsed on the macro in the Q Drops.[3]

## April 2026 invocation

After President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] simultaneously threatened to nuke [[iran|Iran]] and to make peace with Iran on a Monday in April 2026 — a juxtaposition [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] said "absolutely discombobulated everybody regardless of their position on the Iran War and President Trump" — the Badlands commentator concluded that Iran could now be expected to become one of America's greatest allies, with the narrative of Iranian malevolence "disarmed and dismantled."[4] He labelled the sequence simply: "The Venezuela Model."[4] The topic dominated that day's pre-recorded edition of Badlands Daily with [[brian-lupo|Brian Lupo]].[4]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] extended the parallel, describing Venezuela as a "masterclass" in which the regime change narrative shielded the real continuity of government that was rapidly translated into sovereign-coded stewardship, and arguing Iran was receiving the same off-ramp with its continuity preserved.[4]
