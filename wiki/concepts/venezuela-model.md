# Venezuela Model

The "Venezuela Model" is an interpretive framework advanced by the Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to describe what he argues is the Trump administration's actual template for military intervention: the visible, theatrical removal of a head-of-state figurehead, combined with the deliberate preservation of the surrounding governing structure, followed by rapid movement toward energy cooperation, stability, and negotiated deals — without occupation or ground forces.

## Origins in Venezuela

As set out in the March 3, 2026 Badlands Brief, the model takes its name from the [[venezuela|Venezuelan]] operation, in which, on Burning Bright's account, the United States removed [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]] as "the visible lightning rod," severed the cartel pipelines said to be feeding "the actual Globalist proxies," installed continuity of government with "a sovereign-aligned hand at the wheel," and then pivoted directly into energy cooperation and stability narratives. He contrasts this explicitly with the outcome he says globalist actors had wanted — a wholesale regime change delivering power to [[maria-corina-machado|Maria Corina Machado]] and what he characterizes as her NGO-backed opposition movement.

Burning Bright describes the result as [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]] "wearing the mask of the very thing the enemy had spent years demanding": intervention in form, withdrawal from entanglement in substance.

## Application to Iran

The Brief reports that in an exclusive interview with The New York Times, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said repeatedly that he was following the Venezuela template in [[iran|Iran]]. Burning Bright reads [[operation-epic-fury|Operation Epic Fury]] accordingly: the strikes and the death of Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] supply the optics of regime change, while the Iranian state apparatus is left standing and steered toward negotiation consistent with [[america-first|America First]] priorities.

His principal supporting evidence is the reaction of [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], whom he calls "the walking, talking mascot of the entire NeoCon Collective." Rather than celebrating, Bolton warned of "turmoil and bloodshed" and complained of insufficient consultation with the Iranian opposition and the absence of a plan for full decapitation of the regime — behavior Burning Bright takes as proof that neoconservatives recognize they are not getting the Forever War they wanted.

The framework is interpretive and contested; it is presented in the source as commentary rather than as reported fact.
