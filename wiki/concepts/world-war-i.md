# World War I

The First World War appears in Badlands Media commentary primarily as a financial event rather than a military one.[1] In Joe Lange's November 2022 essay "Money and Oil," the war is described as the first major global conflict in which central banks controlled by the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]] funded both sides while millions died and participating countries went into massive debt.[1]

## Aftermath and the road to the Second World War

Lange summarises the post-war settlement as "all the same playbook": Germany loses and is then bankrupted, its companies and resources are bought up for pennies on the dollar, the value of its money collapses, and rampant poverty sets the stage for [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] and [[world-war-ii|the Second World War]].[1] The essay also notes that Germany's post-war need to rebuild, combined with its lack of domestic petroleum, carried directly into the strategic logic of the next war.[1]

## Rise of oil

The war is credited in the essay with the rise of a commodity that remains as valuable as gold — one for which countries willingly trade gold.[1] That commodity is [[oil|oil]], whose military and economic centrality Lange treats as the second of the two levers, alongside money, by which the world is controlled.[1]

## 1914 and the banker-warlord system

Badlands Media commentary states that 1914 gave the world its first global war and a war-like system that still dominates today, in which wars are used to take control of civilizations while enriching the financial backers of those wars.[2] It alleges that the same Wall Street and [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] bankers have financed both sides of every major war for more than a century — including the Nazis and the Allies, the operators of WWII concentration camps, those who intentionally starved millions of Soviet Ukrainians in the 1930s, and those in charge of the killing fields in Cambodia.[2] WWI, in this framing, was the start of the perfect marriage between bankers who control the money and warlords who create wars to take over civilizations and economies.[2]

## The homeland bargain (Badlands account)

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that while Britain was losing World War I badly to the Germans, Prime Minister [[david-lloyd-george|David Lloyd George]] proposed to Jewish leaders in England that if they could bring the United States into the war, the English elite would promise the Jews their own homeland.[4] The Americans were staying out at the time, a sizable percentage of the population being of German descent; after the proposal, Carlson says, Jewish-American bankers began lending toward the British war effort, anti-German propaganda calling the Germans "baby killers" was pushed in Jewish-American-run media, German products were removed from Jewish-American-owned retail stores, and the [[sinking-of-the-lusitania|Lusitania was sunk]] as a false flag, after which the US entered the war.[4] The [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]] of 1917 was the promise kept.[4] Carlson estimates 17 million deaths from the war.[4]
