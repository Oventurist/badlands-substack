# Great Depression

The **Great Depression**, beginning with the financial collapse of 1929, appears in the Badlands Media "Structures of Control" series as the earliest of four worked examples of the [[problem-reaction-solution-outcome|Problem-Reaction-Solution-Outcome]] cycle operating at generational scale.[1]

## Treatment in the series

In the article's framing, the problem was a sudden and catastrophic financial collapse; the reaction was panic as breadlines formed and families lost everything; the solution was the emergence of centralized banking systems such as the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] in the role of saviors; and the outcome was the normalization of generational dependency on those institutions together with an erosion of sovereignty as centralized authority gained legitimacy.[1]

The article adds a feedback stage absent from conventional accounts: it claims the public's desperate acceptance of centralized banking became the template for future economic crises and informed the design of subsequent cycles, linking 1929 forward to the [[2008-financial-crisis|2008 financial crisis]] and the pandemic-era economic response.[1] The episode is thus used to illustrate the series' contention that [[4d-timeline-control|4D control]] uses historical outcomes to refine future cycles, producing an [[illusion-of-inevitability|illusion of inevitability]] around centralized financial authority.[1]
