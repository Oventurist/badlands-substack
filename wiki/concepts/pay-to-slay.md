# Pay to Slay

"Pay to slay" is the name commonly given to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]]'s system of payments to Palestinians imprisoned for terrorism offences and to the families of those killed carrying out attacks. The Badlands Media article "Bad Actors" cites the program to argue that American foreign aid released in the final hours of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration was directed to a body that pays for violence as a matter of statute.[1]

## Legal basis

Quoting Newsweek, the article states that the PA "has long incentivized violence with the infamous 'pay to slay' program" and that the program is "even enshrined in its laws."[1] In 2004 the PA passed a law guaranteeing salaries for convicted terrorists and their families, with the legislation specifying that "prisoners and released prisoners are a fighting sector and integral part of the fabric of Arab Palestinian society."[1] In 2010 a series of resolutions stipulated that every Palestinian inmate in an Israeli prison convicted of a "terrorism-related offense" receive a monthly payment.[1] In 2013 Prime Minister Rami Hamdallah amended the law to add benefits upon release: any male ex-prisoner incarcerated for ten or more years, and every female ex-prisoner who served five, became entitled to a position within the PA.[1]

## Relevance to US foreign aid

Joe Lange notes that these arrangements had been law and public knowledge for many years before Obama, hours before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration and over the objections of [[congress|Congress]], directed $221 million in taxpayer funds toward Gaza recovery programs through [[usaid|USAID]] at the instruction of Secretary of State [[john-kerry|John Kerry]].[1] He characterises the recipient as "a governing body in Gaza that has a 'pay to slay' program to pay terrorists and their families for violence against the Israeli people," and treats the transfer as a repayment of a political debt rather than humanitarian assistance.[1] The Trump administration froze the package shortly afterwards.[1]
