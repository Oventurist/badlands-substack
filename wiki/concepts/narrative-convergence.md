# Narrative Convergence

Narrative Convergence is an analytical concept developed by Badlands Media commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to describe moments when establishment and independent media narratives arrive at the same underlying observation from opposite framings.[1] It is used as an interpretive tool within the [[information-war|Information War]] analysis practiced on Badlands programming.[1]

## Definition and forms

Burning Bright distinguishes two forms of convergence. Mirrored convergence occurs when patriots — in his framing, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his allies — appear to advance messaging that runs parallel to that of the Truth Community.[1] Inverted convergence occurs when establishment media narratives run in parallel with independent analysis while starting from the opposite premise about causes and villains.[1]

## Application to the 2024 Biden campaign coverage

The concept was illustrated in June 2024 through a feature in [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]] on the [[joe-biden|Biden]] campaign's escalating fight with journalists.[1] Burning Bright argued the article converged with Badlands' own thesis that the Biden team was mismanaging the campaign and making a spectacle of the president during the 2024 cycle, but that the more revealing element was the magazine's framing of why: that the Biden administration was increasingly seen as hostile toward the press itself.[1]

He interpreted this as evidence that the "crabs in a bucket" nature of the establishment was already cracking what he called the thin, eggshell veneer over the System of Systems, and that the previously mutually reinforcing relationship between the administration and legacy media had been reverse-engineered into a primary weakness.[1] Badlands treated outlets such as [[politico|Politico]] and The Atlantic as "treasure troves of reverse indicators" for this reason.[1]
