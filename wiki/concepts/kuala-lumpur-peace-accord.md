# Kuala Lumpur Peace Accord

The Kuala Lumpur Peace Accord is an agreement between Cambodia and Thailand signed on October 26, 2025, on the sidelines of the 47th ASEAN Summit, ending an outbreak of hostilities between the two Southeast Asian neighbors.[1]

Malaysian Prime Minister [[anwar-ibrahim|Anwar Ibrahim]] mediated the settlement and drew sustained praise for it, including from U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who told the APEC CEO Summit in Korea that Malaysia had been "fantastic" in assisting with the signing of the deal.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright argued that the accord, like the investment agreements Trump signed with Japan and South Korea and his meeting with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], had been negotiated long before the announcements: "NONE were decided this week. The deals are done. This is about the show."[1]
