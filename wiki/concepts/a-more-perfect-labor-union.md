# A More Perfect (Labor) Union

*A More Perfect (Labor) Union* is an article by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] published on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on October 23, 2025, subtitled "The Role of Labor Unions in Covert Geopolitics." The piece argues that labor unions, far from being bulwarks of the working class, have functioned as covert agents of the U.S. foreign policy establishment — specifically through the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]], America's largest labor federation, and its international arm the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]].

## Central thesis

The article claims that the AFL-CIO and the Solidarity Center use taxpayer funds to promote political and geopolitical objectives on behalf of the U.S. foreign policy establishment, using the rhetoric of labor solidarity as cover. It traces the relationship back to Cold War covert operations and argues it has since been integrated into the modern "democracy promotion" agenda — a term the author says NATO and "friends of Anglo-American imperialism" use to justify warping foreign nations into "open societies."

The author defines "democracy promotion" as conforming a nation to the will of the international foreign policy establishment by installing NATO-approved leaders, flooding the country with American corporate franchises, disseminating establishment propaganda, altering the legal system via "rule of law programs," and manipulating labor unions to bring a workforce behind Western-sponsored causes.

## Place in the corpus

The article links this labor-focused analysis to the broader Badlands framework of a "deep government" — a network of unelected career bureaucrats within agencies like the [[cia|CIA]], [[state-department|State Department]], [[pentagon|DoD]], and the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|NED]] that exerts influence through the "plausible deniability network." DeLarme situates the piece alongside his earlier work on how this network influences political parties, the university system, the legal system, the international drug trade, information flows, NGOs, and activist networks.

The central metaphor is "pressure from above and pressure from below": labor unions, like activist networks, are the "pressure from below" that gives the elite's agenda the appearance of grassroots legitimacy. The article uses [[barack-obama|Barack Obama's]] 2008 campaign as the "perfect example" of this deception, arguing that his substantial support from labor unions and leftist activist groups duped the lower class into thinking they had achieved a social victory while Obama functioned as "another hollow, albeit well-spoken, vessel for the political establishment."

## Case studies

The article presents case studies of the Solidarity Center's operations in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] (2014 Maidan uprising), [[hong-kong|Hong Kong]] (2019–2020 protests), [[myanmar|Myanmar]] (post-2021 coup), and Africa's resource wars in the Democratic Republic of Congo and Zambia, arguing in each case that unions were used as geopolitical pawns to advance U.S. strategic objectives rather than worker interests.

## Sources

The article cites a range of sources including *The Nation*'s "Labor's Cold War" by Tim Shorrock (2003), *Jacobin*'s coverage of the "AFL-CIA," a *Monthly Review* piece on "Labor Imperialism Redux," a 1967 *New York Times* series, and academic and leftist outlets documenting the AFL-CIO's role in covert statecraft.
