# Antalya Diplomacy Forum

The Antalya Diplomacy Forum is a Turkish-hosted international conference that, in April 2026, served as the venue at which [[turkey|Turkey]] hosted [[egypt|Egypt]], [[pakistan|Pakistan]], and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] to discuss the [[iran|Iran]] situation.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identified those four states as the core of an emerging Middle Eastern "security architecture," and predicted Iran would eventually join it.[1] In his framing, Egypt is the traditional regional military power, Pakistan is the sole Muslim nuclear-weapons state, Turkey is a NATO member with the largest army in the region, and Saudi Arabia is the lynchpin binding them together alongside the United States.[1]

He argued that this bloc — backed by Russia, China, and a Europe increasingly souring on [[israel|Israel]] — represents the only force capable of halting the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]]'s campaign toward [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]], and that the conflict could prove the crucible forging the Middle East into a stable region with common interests.[1]
