# Qana Gas Field

The Qana Gas Field is an offshore natural gas prospect associated with Lebanese maritime waters that [[israel|Israel]] has long sought to control, historically through lawfare over the maritime boundary.[1]

In April 2026, Badlands analysis argued that by pushing the effective Israeli line north toward Tyre and designating the resulting strip a "Forward Defense Zone," Israel could justify shifting the maritime boundary and annexing the Qana field.[1] The gas field is thus presented as an economic motive layered beneath the stated security rationale for operations in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[1]
