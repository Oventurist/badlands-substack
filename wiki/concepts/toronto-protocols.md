# Toronto Protocols

The **Toronto Protocols**, originally circulated in French as "Les Protocoles de Toronto," are a collection of documents leaked by the Canadian journalist [[serge-monast|Serge Monast]] and treated by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] as a primary resource for investigating what the documents themselves allege is a globalist, satanic [[cabal|cabal]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Provenance

According to one English translation, the leak provides "a document compiled by a clique of senior financiers who met in Canada in 1967 and then in 1985."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The group in question identified itself as the "6.6.6." — a self-designation that led Deschamps to build his AI prompts around the satanic idea of inversion.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Brian Nugent, among the best-known English translators, records Monast's own position that it is up to each individual reader to decide whether to believe the document, by testing whether it matches the politics and recent political history of the preceding years.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Content and use

Deschamps describes the Protocols as a candid and unusually accurate information set for mapping an adversary's battle from a military standpoint, and says his appreciation for them has grown as what he calls the war escalated.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The documents place heavy emphasis on the family, which the 6.6.6. is said to be fixated on annihilating as a strategic requirement for the [[new-world-order|New World Order]]'s implementation.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) They also make explicit the intention to use globalist institutions such as the [[united-nations|United Nations]] to achieve their aims.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The passage Deschamps quotes at length reads: "To succeed with certainty to the construction of a World Government, [A New World Order Community] where all individuals without exception, will be subject to the 'World State' of the 'New Order', we must, first, remove the 'Family' (which will, in turn, lead to the loss of ancestral religious teachings), and secondly, levelling all individuals by removing the 'Social Classes', in particular, the 'Middle Classes'. But we must proceed so that all these are seen as being from the popular will, that they have the appearance of 'Democracy'."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Deschamps fed such passages to ChatGPT and asked it to decompose them into primary strategic objectives, secondary strategic objectives, operations and tactics, using the layered architecture he associates with [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The output of that exercise became the substance of his article.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
