# Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe

The **Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe** (CTMU) is a reality theory authored by [[chris-langan|Chris Langan]] and cited repeatedly in Badlands Media metaphysical commentary.[1] The essay "The Extraordinary Power of Digital Soldiers" (July 31, 2023) describes Langan as a reality theorist who "managed to demonstrate that reality quite literally is a *living language*," and points readers to the CTMU paper and to the earlier Badlands article "The Metaphysics of 5G War" for the supporting argument, which the author says is too large a topic to reproduce in full.[1]

## Reality as a living language

The consequence the essay draws from the CTMU is that if reality is a living language, then a human being is also a piece of that same living language and shares a common identity with the whole of reality by virtue of being made of, and participating in, the language that governs all existence.[1] English, Spanish and other tongues are described as sub-languages of the "One Language" — glossed as the *Uni-Verse* — that governs reality proper; what matters for the argument is language in the generalized sense of meaning-making, which the author says includes the whole of mathematics.[1]

## Descriptive proximity

A specific CTMU claim invoked in the essay is that there are multiple kinds of proximity: physical proximity and linguistic or descriptive proximity, with the latter largely unaffected by the former.[1] The author presents this as an "immanently plausible mechanism whereby symbolic rituals could affect the real world," and thus as the theoretical basis for a non-solipsistic [[law-of-attraction|law of attraction]] in which the universe talks about itself and thereby creates itself.[1] Embedding human consciousness in reality by means of this common metalanguage is also offered as a way to make sense of quantum entanglement, paranormal phenomena, telepathy, premonitions and synchronicities.[1]

## Origin and role in defining metaphysics

The earlier Badlands essay "The Metaphysics of 5G War" (April 14, 2023) supplies the fuller account on which the later article relies, identifying the CTMU as a paper published in 2002 by "a relatively obscure reality-theorist named Chris Langan."[2] The essay's claim is that until the CTMU the field of metaphysics was fraught with interpretive difficulty because the concept itself was never well defined — glossed loosely as "the physics of physics" — and that Langan's paper finally permits metaphysics to be defined in relation to physics as it is known.[2] Its central proposition is quoted as: "reality is a self-contained, self-processing language."[2]

### Cognition and self-observation

The essay connects the CTMU to the double-slit experiment in quantum physics, in which human observation appears to affect the behaviour of photons, arguing that this had long supplied scientific grounds for supposing that cognition can affect material reality without explaining the mechanism.[2] Langan's contribution, on the author's reading, was to prove logically that cognition or awareness provides the universe with a means to observe itself and thereby to process the feedback required to generate new events — summarized in the essay's aphorism that "time itself is a team sport."[2] The universe is described as perpetually holding a conversation with itself, in which each person participates by virtue of the capacity to use language in the broadest sense.[2]

### Metaphysical and physical reality

On this framework metaphysical reality is described as the universal "language" from which the laws of physics are derived, and also as the region in which a person's Spirit operates as an entity distinct from the physical body.[2] The two are held to be aspects of a single unified reality, both derivable from linguistic transformations emanating from an *a priori* meta-language.[2] The essay argues that no scientific theory of everything is complete unless it relates metaphysical and physical reality in a coherent, self-consistent framework, and credits the CTMU with achieving precisely that.[2] The takeaway drawn for participants in the [[5g-war|5G war]] is that God not only creates but *uses* human experience and cognition to structure reality, a claim the author says extends the meaning of WWG1WGA beyond what most had considered.[2] The full paper is acknowledged to be extremely dense and technical, with readers directed to a simplified explanation at Hology.org.[2]
