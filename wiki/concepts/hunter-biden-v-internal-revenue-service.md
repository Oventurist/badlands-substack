# Hunter Biden v. Internal Revenue Service

Hunter Biden v. Internal Revenue Service is a civil lawsuit filed on Monday, September 18, 2023, in federal court in Washington, D.C., in which [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] alleged that agents of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] illegally released his tax return information and that the agency failed to protect the confidentiality of his private records.[1]

## Claims and relief sought

The complaint alleged that the IRS unlawfully disclosed the president's son's tax return information and that the agency did not establish adequate safeguards to ensure the confidentiality of his records.[1] Among other things, the suit sought all documents relating to the disclosure of the tax information, statutory damages of $1,000 for each unauthorized disclosure, and attorneys' fees.[1]

## Whistleblowers at the center of the case

The lawsuit did not name as defendants the two IRS agents turned whistleblowers, [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] and [[joseph-ziegler|Joseph Ziegler]], but it was centered on disclosures the two agents and their lawyers made in public statements, congressional testimony, and interviews.[1]

## Judge and context

The case was assigned to Judge Timothy Kelly, an appointee of Donald Trump.[1] It was filed amid a swirl of other legal problems for Hunter Biden, who had been indicted the previous week by special counsel [[david-weiss|David Weiss]] on three felony gun charges and who potentially faced additional tax charges from Weiss.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], treated the suit as an instance of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] in which the templates built during the "Witch Hunt" against Donald Trump were being turned back on the Deep State.[1] He argued that the media had spent years demanding transparency over Trump's tax returns and financial records but was now working to explain why the same standards applied to the Bidens were unfair and illegal — "Boomerang, indeed."[1]
