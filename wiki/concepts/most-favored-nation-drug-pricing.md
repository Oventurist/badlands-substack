# Most Favored Nation Drug Pricing

"Most favored nation" drug pricing is a policy concept that would peg prices paid for prescription drugs in the United States to the lower prices paid in other developed nations. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] moved toward the policy in May 2025 with an executive order aimed at lowering U.S. drug prices, described in press coverage as a "bombshell" and a step toward the pharmaceutical industry's worst-case scenario.[1]

## Industry impact

Analysts framed the order as either the beginning of enormous global disruption for the pharmaceutical industry or, alternatively, the least-bad version of the options that had been on the table, depending on the observer.[1] The persistent risk of a full most-favored-nation regime was characterized as a massive threat to drugmakers' bottom lines, particularly on top of the pharmaceutical tariffs Trump had said he wanted to impose.[1] Some critics argued the industry could have done more to avoid the outcome, given Trump's sustained focus on drug prices and the populist current within the Republican Party.[1] Open questions included whether the policy would ever come to fruition at all.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the order as an example of what he called the Trump Dialectic — "the inversion of generations of deeply-held, calcified (engineered) belief" — writing that "from neutering Big Pharma to the MIL Complex, it turns out ... yeah, you actually CAN just do the thing, and stop the game."[1]
