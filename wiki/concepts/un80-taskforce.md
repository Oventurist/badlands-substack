# UN80 Taskforce

The UN80 Taskforce is a reform initiative launched in March 2025 by [[antonio-guterres|Secretary-General António Guterres]] to review how the [[united-nations|United Nations]] Secretariat implements thousands of mandates and to reduce administrative overload.[1] It was created as the organization approached its 80th anniversary amid what commentators described as a deepening identity crisis.[1]

## Findings on reports and meetings

A report released by the taskforce in 2025 found that the Secretariat published 1,100 reports in the previous year — a 20% increase since 1990 — and supported 27,000 meetings involving 240 entities.[1] Most of the more than 1,000 reports produced annually are barely read: the top 5% of reports were downloaded more than 5,500 times, while one in five received fewer than 1,000 downloads.[1]

"The sheer number of meetings and reports is pushing the system – and all of us – to the breaking point," Guterres said at a briefing, adding that many reports are not widely read and that "downloading doesn't necessarily mean reading."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary treated the study as unintentional satire, joking that the matrix had become self-aware in generating a UN report documenting that nobody reads UN reports.[1]
