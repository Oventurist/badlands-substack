# AZT

AZT (azidothymidine) is the drug deployed as the principal treatment for AIDS beginning in the 1980s. Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describes it as a harsh chemotherapy drug that had been taken off the market in the 1960s because it was killing too many people.[1]

## Role in the AIDS epidemic narrative

Carlson notes that the AIDS epidemic began in 1981 — the same year [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] became president — and that [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] was put in charge of researching, treating and eradicating the disease.[1] Carlson states that he, like many others, believes it was the treatment rather than the disease that caused most of the deaths, singling out AZT.[1]

He draws a direct parallel to the Covid era, where he argues the treatments — Remdesivir and ventilators — and later intentionally tainted batches of vaccines caused most of the deaths, alongside the demonization by government agencies and media of successful, reliable and inexpensive remedies.[1] From this parallel Carlson asks whether Fauci was used twice to derail an America First president and remove a chief executive hostile to the [[deep-state|Deep State]], noting that he does not recall Fauci being used to propagandize epidemics under presidents he regards as Deep State puppets.[1]
