# 2025 16 Billion Credential Leak

The 2025 sixteen-billion-credential leak was a mass exposure of login credentials tied to [[apple|Apple]], [[facebook|Facebook]], [[google|Google]] and other social media and government services, described by researchers as the largest data breach ever recorded.[1]

## Scope

Reporting in June 2025 stated that sixteen billion login credentials and passwords had been exposed.[1] The disclosure prompted Google to tell billions of users to change their passwords, and the [[fbi|FBI]] to warn Americans against opening suspicious links in SMS messages, according to a *Forbes* report published that Thursday.[1] Researchers at [[cybernews|Cybernews]], who had been investigating the leak, found "30 exposed datasets containing from tens of millions to over 3.5 billion records each," and noted that all but one of those datasets had never previously been reported as exposed — meaning the impacted data was all considered new.[1]

"This is not just a leak – it's a blueprint for mass exploitation," the researchers said, warning that the credentials were ground zero for phishing attacks and account takeover: "These aren't just old breaches being recycled... this is fresh, weaponizable intelligence at scale."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] contrasted the breach with the assurances of the elections industry, noting the incongruity of a reality in which every major tech platform suffers a catastrophic data breach while the public is told American elections are impenetrable — an argument she said rested on the testimony of a lawyer with an undergraduate degree in environmental science.[1] She pointed to [[j-alex-halderman|Alex Halderman]] citing [[chris-krebs|Chris Krebs]] as an authority in congressional testimony the prior week as evidence that she was not exaggerating.[1]
