# Harlem Riot of 1935

The Harlem race riots of 1935 in New York City are cited in the Badlands Media essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]] as the earliest entry in the article's chronology of recurring racial unrest.

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] mentions them in a single line — "In 1935, there were the Harlem race riots in New York City" — as the anchor point of a sequence continuing through the [[watts-riots|Watts riots]] of 1965, the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] beating of 1991 and the [[los-angeles-riots-1992|1992 Los Angeles riots]], and the [[george-floyd|George Floyd]] protests of 2020. Roughly thirty years separate each entry, a spacing the article treats as evidence of deliberate scheduling by the [[cabal|Deep State Cabal]] rather than coincidence.
