# Port Sudan Drone Attacks (2025)

In early May 2025 drones struck the airport and targeted an army base in **Port Sudan**, marking the third straight day of attacks on the seat of power of the Sudanese government, which is aligned with the Sudanese army.[1]

## Course of the attacks

The country's main fuel depot was hit on a Monday, causing a massive blaze just south of the eastern city that until the preceding Sunday had been considered a safe haven for hundreds of thousands of displaced people fleeing a two-year war.[1] An Agence France-Presse correspondent reported loud explosions at dawn on Tuesday and plumes of smoke over the coastal city, one rising from the direction of the port and another from a fuel depot to the south.[1]

One drone struck the civilian section of the Port Sudan airport, an airport official told Agence France-Presse, two days after the facility's military base was first attacked in drone strikes that the army blamed on the paramilitary [[rapid-support-forces|Rapid Support Forces]].[1] All flights were grounded at the country's main international port of entry.[1]

## Russian naval base context

Commentary in the *Badlands News Brief* by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Port Sudan was being attacked because in January the Sudanese government finalized a deal with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] allowing Russia to establish a naval base there — described as the first Russian military base on the African continent.[1] The preliminary deal was first announced as accepted in concept in February 2023, despite threats from the Biden-era [[state-department|State Department]] against the Sudanese government; two months later Sudan was plunged into a civil war from which it has yet to escape.[1] The brief cited a more recent article in which the State Department again threatened the Sudanese government with further pain and punishment for cutting a deal with Putin, with an anonymous official relaying the message to Fox News — behavior the commentary characterized as a reason the world despises the U.S. government.[1]
