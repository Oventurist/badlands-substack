# 2025 Minneapolis Church Shooting

The 2025 Minneapolis church shooting was a mass shooting that occurred on the morning of Wednesday, August 27, 2025, during Mass at the [[annunciation-catholic-church|Annunciation Catholic Church]] in Minneapolis, Minnesota.[1]

## Events

Officials identified [[robin-westman|Robin Westman]], described in reporting as a trans-identifying male in his twenties, as the alleged gunman.[1] Two children were killed and at least seventeen other people were injured in the attack.[1] Authorities named the suspect during a press briefing held the same day.[1]

## Political reaction

The shooting immediately became a focal point in the American gun control debate. Democratic officials called for expanded gun control measures, and a number of commentators argued that "thoughts and prayers" were insufficient to prevent such attacks.[1] Former White House press secretary [[jen-psaki|Jen Psaki]] drew significant backlash after writing on social media that prayers are "not freaking enough," then weeping on air during her MSNBC program "The Briefing" while discussing the victims.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked the "72-hour rule" — a caution against accepting early reporting on horrific crimes — and criticized Psaki sharply for attacking prayer in the aftermath of an attack that occurred inside a church.[1] The commentary framed the incident as part of what it called an epidemic of trans violence directed at deliberately softened targets, and argued that calls for disarmament during a crime wave were illegitimate.[1] Readers were pointed to [[brad-zerbo|Brad Zerbo]]'s recap of the incident on the program WWG1WGA After Dark.[1]
