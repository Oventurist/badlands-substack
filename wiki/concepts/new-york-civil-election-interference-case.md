# New York Civil Election Interference Case (1:23-cv-03773)

The case docketed 1:23-cv-03773 is a lesser-known New York civil matter concerning election interference involving [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], described by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as one of the parallel proceedings crowding the 2024 primary calendar.[1]

## Remand to state court

The case was remanded from the Southern District of New York to the New York Supreme Court in November 2023 by Judge [[alvin-hellerstein|Alvin Hellerstein]], who held that "Trump has failed to show that the conduct charged by the Indictment is for or relating to any act performed by or for the President under color of the official acts of a President," and that "Trump also has failed to show that he has a colorable federal defense to the Indictment."[1] On either or both grounds, Hellerstein granted the People's motion to remand and directed the clerk to send the case file to the New York Supreme Court, New York County.[1]

## Schedule

Trial was scheduled to begin Monday, March 25, 2024, with a hearing on motions including dismissal set for Thursday, February 1, 2024.[1] Ashe in America placed the case alongside the [[trump-january-6-indictment|federal January 6 prosecution]], the [[new-york-civil-fraud-trial|New York civil fraud trial]], the [[state-of-georgia-v-trump|Georgia racketeering case]], the [[trump-classified-documents-case|classified documents case]] and the [[e-jean-carroll-defamation-judgment|E. Jean Carroll damages trial]] as evidence that novel civil and criminal [[lawfare|lawfare]] had become the new normal for political opponents of the incumbent administration.[1]
