# Iran-Contra Affair

The Iran-Contra affair was a covert operation conducted during the Reagan administration in which the United States sold weapons to [[iran|Iran]] — using [[israel|Israel]] as the middle man — and directed the proceeds to mercenaries in Nicaragua who were trained to overthrow governments.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked the affair in an August 2025 obituary note for [[william-webster|William Webster]], the only person ever to lead both the [[fbi|FBI]] and the [[cia|CIA]].[1] The contributor listed Iran-Contra alongside the Gulf War, the collapse of the USSR — which he linked to the creation of Ukraine — and the crack epidemic as the events bracketing Webster's tenure atop the two agencies, concluding that the record "seems very notable."[1]

Webster led the CIA from 1987 to 1991 under Presidents Ronald Reagan and George H. W. Bush, and said upon taking the post that CIA and FBI directors should be ready to resign if asked to do something they know is wrong.[1]
