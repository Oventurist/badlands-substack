# October 2025 Federal Reductions in Force

The October 2025 federal reductions in force (RIFs) were mass layoffs of federal employees carried out by the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] during the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|October 2025 government shutdown]].[1] More than 4,000 federal employees received layoff notices on Friday, October 10, 2025, according to a court filing, as part of a broad effort to reshape the government while it remained shut down.[1]

The filing gave greater insight into an announcement made earlier that day by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s budget chief, Office of Management and Budget Director [[russ-vought|Russell Vought]], that the administration had begun government-wide reductions in force.[1] Such cuts had been anticipated since federal funding lapsed on October 1.[1] "The RIFs have begun," Vought posted on X, without elaborating on how many federal workers had received notices.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the layoffs as the predictable output of a corporate-style restructuring, writing that since the Department of Government Efficiency was announced she had theorized the federal government would be restructured through a business transformation.[1] In such a transformation, once strategy and goals are defined, the organization moves through cycles of assess, design, test, and release, and most cycles involve reductions in force aimed at making the enterprise more profitable, less costly, and more efficient by cutting what she termed dead weight talent.[1]

She argued that the government is full of dead weight talent precisely because it has always been nearly impossible to fire government workers, calling RIFs in government "a miracle" that never happen.[1] Acknowledging that people with family and friends affected would find it hard, she maintained that the transformation must continue in order to "make American great again and realize the Golden Age," noting that the federal government amounts to roughly 25 percent of GDP, which is how it acquired the resources and manpower to be excessively involved in Americans' lives.[1] She closed with the formulation that "everyone gets a hair cuts, and some heads get shaved. Accelerate."[1]
