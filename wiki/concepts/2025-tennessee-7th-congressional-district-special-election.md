# 2025 Tennessee 7th Congressional District Special Election

The December 2025 special election for a Tennessee U.S. House seat was won by Republican [[matt-van-epps|Matt Van Epps]] over Democrat [[aftyn-behn|Aftyn Behn]], but drew national attention because Behn significantly cut into Republican margins in a deep-red district.[1]

## Result and context

Van Epps won the seat on Tuesday, December 2, 2025, continuing Republican control, while Behn's performance continued a streak of Democratic overperformances through the year.[1] National outside groups poured millions of dollars into the race as Republicans grew concerned about a potential upset.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had carried the district by 22 points the previous fall, but voters' dissatisfaction with the cost of living helped Behn trim that margin.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked the media framing of the outcome as evidence that "MAGA is dead" and that the midterms would be a disaster, writing that "a Republican won a fake election in Tennessee, but Politico wants to make sure you know that their fake margin of victory is more narrow than it was in the last fake election."[1] She argued that elections cannot become real until Americans are honest that they are fake, warned that proposed remedies such as blockchain voting, [[ranked-choice-voting|ranked choice voting]] and mobile voting would reproduce existing problems while adding new ones, and insisted that accountability and a justice phase must precede any reform.[1]
