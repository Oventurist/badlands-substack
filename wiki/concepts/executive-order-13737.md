# Executive Order 13737

Executive Order 13737 is described in Badlands Media commentary as "Revoking Executive Orders That Relied on the Antiquities Act," an order said to have reversed specific Obama-era measures.[1]

## Claimed significance

The article characterizes EO 13737 as the least discussed of the executive orders it surveys, but assigns it a symbolic role: curbing potential executive overreach and setting a precedent against the use of executive authority for politically motivated purposes.[1] It is listed alongside [[executive-order-13818|EO 13818]], [[executive-order-13848|EO 13848]], [[executive-order-13959|EO 13959]], and [[executive-order-13919|EO 13919]] as part of what the author calls the legal infrastructure supporting [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] operations attributed to the [[white-hats|White Hats]].[1]

## Assessment

The description given in the source should be treated with caution: the numbering and subject matter attributed to this order do not match a well-documented Antiquities Act revocation, and the claim appears within a broader interpretive framework whose central assertions are unsubstantiated. Readers should verify the citation against the Federal Register before relying on it.[1]
