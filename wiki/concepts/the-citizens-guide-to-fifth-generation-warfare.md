# The Citizen's Guide to Fifth-Generation Warfare

***The Citizen's Guide to Fifth-Generation Warfare*** (also cited as *The Citizen's Guide to 5GW*) is a book by General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] and [[boone-cutler|Boone Cutler]] that functions within [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as the standard reference text for analyzing information operations.[1]

## The digital domain thesis

The book states that the majority of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] occurs in the [[digital-domain|digital domain]].[1] From that premise its authors advocate the instruction to "dominate the [[physical-domain|physical domain]]" as a direct means of cutting through mind control and censorship.[1]

In the March 2024 essay "How Your Western Brain Exposes You to 5G Warfare," [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] pairs this doctrine with [[iain-mcgilchrist|Iain McGilchrist]]'s neurological argument, treating the physical-domain instruction as the practical counterpart to recovering right-hemisphere cognition on the [[cognitive-battlefield|cognitive battlefield]].[1]
