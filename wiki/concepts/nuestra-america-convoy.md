# Nuestra America Convoy

The "Nuestra America Convoy" was a humanitarian aid convoy to [[cuba|Cuba]] in March 2026 that delivered roughly 20 tons of goods to Havana.[1] It was organized with help from [[codepink|CodePink]], the anti-war group co-founded by [[medea-benjamin|Medea Benjamin]].[1]

## Federal investigation

Federal investigators subpoenaed streamer [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]] and Benjamin as part of a [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] probe into their participation in the convoy, seeking financial, logistical and communications records.[1] More than 40 Americans are reportedly under scrutiny by the Treasury's [[office-of-foreign-assets-control|Office of Foreign Assets Control]] for potential violations of U.S. sanctions law arising from the trip and similar efforts.[1]

The probe came amid intensified [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration pressure on Cuba, including a tightened blockade, Cuba's designation as a state sponsor of terrorism, and the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] indictment of [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]].[1] Badlands framed the convoy inquiry as an early instance of accountability reaching the NGO and influencer ecosystem rather than a narrow sanctions matter.[1]
