# Nuestra America Convoy

The **Nuestra America Convoy** was a humanitarian aid convoy to [[cuba|Cuba]] in March 2026 that delivered roughly 20 tons of goods to Havana. According to the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026, it was organized with help from [[codepink|CodePink]], the anti-war group co-founded by [[medea-benjamin|Medea Benjamin]].

## Federal investigation

The convoy became the subject of a [[department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] probe conducted through the [[office-of-foreign-assets-control|Office of Foreign Assets Control]] into potential violations of U.S. sanctions law. Subpoenas were served on participants including streamer [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]] and Benjamin, seeking financial, logistical and communications records related to the trip, and more than 40 Americans are reportedly under scrutiny.

The article situates the investigation amid heightened Trump administration pressure on Cuba, including a tightened blockade implemented the previous year, the designation of Cuba as a state sponsor of terrorism, and a Justice Department indictment of former Cuban president [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]]. Badlands commentary treats the probe as the opening of a broader campaign against activist NGOs, a contested interpretive framing.
