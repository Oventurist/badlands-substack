# Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections

"Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections" is an executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in March 2026 that forms the legal basis for an expanded federal role in voter verification and mail-in ballot administration.[1]

## Provisions

The order directs the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] to develop a "state citizenship list"; instructs the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] to prioritize prosecution of state and local officials who distribute ballots to ineligible voters; and calls on the [[united-states-postal-service|US Postal Service]] to draft rules requiring states to provide voter registration lists in order to process mail-in ballots.[1]

## Implementation

By May 2026 the effort was advancing through coordinated interagency discussions held at the [[white-house|White House]], involving DOJ Civil Rights Division head [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]] and her deputies, USPS chief executive [[david-steiner|David Steiner]], and DHS election integrity official [[heather-honey|Heather Honey]].[1] Internal disclosures indicated that additional participants included Deputy Homeland Security Advisor Anthony Salisbury, DOJ associate deputy attorney general Aakash Singh, principal associate deputy attorney general Trent McCotter, and counselor to the attorney general Henry Whitaker.[1]

USPS said it was reviewing the order and working on draft regulations, while DHS and [[uscis|USCIS]] officials described internal deliberations as ongoing.[1] Election integrity advocates within the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] orbit treated the interagency push as a landmark moment for the movement.[1]
