# Qatari Boeing 747 Gift

The Qatari Boeing 747 gift refers to reports in May 2025 that the government of [[qatar|Qatar]] intended to provide a luxury Boeing 747-8 jumbo jet to the United States for use as [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s Air Force One, a transfer that drew scrutiny under the strict rules governing presents to American presidents.[1]

## Reports and reaction

Trump defended the plan on Sunday, May 11, 2025, after reports emerged that he would accept the luxury Boeing jet from Qatar.[1] He called the aircraft a "flying palace."[1] ABC News, which first reported the story, described the jet — gifted by the Qatari royal family — as possibly the most expensive gift ever received by the American government.[1]

## Controversy

The controversy surrounding the jet, and Trump's boast that it was coming "free of charge," built upon existing questions the president faced over potential conflicts of interest involving his family businesses and his use of public office.[1] In a social media post late that Sunday that made no mention of Qatar, Trump went on the offensive, claiming the plane was a temporary "gift" that would go to the Defense Department and would replace an existing airframe roughly four decades old.[1]

The episode was carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief covering the weekend of May 10–11, 2025, alongside coverage of Trump's forthcoming Gulf tour.[1]
