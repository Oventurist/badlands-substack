# Badlands Blitz Episode 6

**Badlands Blitz Episode 6**, subtitled "The Info War Ouroboros," is the sixth installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly video program published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack. It was posted on February 14, 2026, and had accumulated 200 likes at the time the accompanying text was archived.

## Format and stated purpose

The post opens with the program's customary greeting to readers — "Badlanders!" — and restates the show's remit: The Blitz is "intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]." For this episode the host is joined by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], continuing the show's rotating co-host arrangement.

## Topics covered

The write-up describes the episode as one in which the two hosts "derail our own conversation from the very start," spending most of the runtime discussing what the post calls "Sand Bunkers, Squared Circles and Ouroborosian Rugs." These figurative motifs are applied to subjects ranging from "the never-ending Epstein Saga" — the outlet's continuing treatment of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein narrative]] — to what the post terms the Election Reform Campaign. The ouroboros image supplies the episode's subtitle and its organizing conceit: an information war that continually consumes and regenerates its own material, so that coverage of a story becomes indistinguishable from the story itself.

The host adds a self-aware aside on the digression, remarking that "I think it was such a good discussion, it almost feels like we planned it that way" — a nod to the program's recurring suggestion that apparent accident and design are difficult to separate in the information environment it covers.

## Subscription appeal

As in previous installments, the post makes the outlet's standing appeal to its audience. It states that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," but that "the only way we're able to pay our people for their time is due to your generous support, should you choose to provide it." Readers who value "the work this Substack team does on a daily basis and the step-up in said work for 2026" are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription, cancellable at any time.

The post closes with the program's habitual valediction: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
