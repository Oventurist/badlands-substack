# Minuteman III

The Minuteman III is a United States intercontinental ballistic missile (ICBM) that forms part of the land-based leg of the American nuclear deterrent. Reported performance figures place its speed above 15,000 miles per hour, sufficient to strike any target worldwide roughly 30 minutes after launch.[1]

## November 2024 test launch

The US military scheduled a test launch of an unarmed Minuteman III for the hours immediately after polls closed on Election Day, November 5, 2024. The launch window ran between 11:01 p.m. and 5:01 a.m. Pacific Time from [[vandenberg-space-force-base|Vandenberg Space Force Base]] in California, with the missile flying approximately 4,200 miles to Kwajalein Atoll in the North Pacific — a flight of about 22 minutes.[1]

Some Americans expressed concern about a nuclear-capable weapons test taking place on the same day the country voted for its next president. Military officials responded that the "test is routine and was scheduled years in advance," and said its purpose was to demonstrate the "readiness of nuclear US forces" and provide "confidence in the nation's nuclear deterrence" amid fears of a wider global conflict.[1]

Coverage of the launch noted the geographic context of US deterrence planning: Moscow lies roughly 5,900 miles from California and Beijing about 6,000 miles, the two nations most frequently characterized as strategic threats to the United States.[1]
