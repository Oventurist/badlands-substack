# 2024 US–Russia Prisoner Swap

The **2024 US–Russia prisoner swap** was the largest prisoner exchange between the United States and Russia since the Cold War, completed on August 1, 2024 after more than a year of secret negotiation.[1]

## The exchange

American journalist [[evan-gershkovich|Evan Gershkovich]] and former US Marine [[paul-whelan|Paul Whelan]] returned to the United States on August 1, 2024, hours after being freed from Russian detention.[1] The deal encompassed 24 prisoners in total, of whom 16 were returned to the West from Russia while eight moved to Russia from the West.[1]

## Mediation

Speaking at the [[eastern-economic-forum|Eastern Economic Forum]] in Vladivostok on September 5, 2024, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] said he was grateful to Saudi Crown Prince and Prime Minister [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] for helping arrange the swap: "Saudi Arabia's Crown Prince played an active role in the initial stages of this work. We are very grateful to him, as it led to the return of our citizens to the homeland."[1] Putin also thanked Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] for providing the venue for the exchange, and said several other Arab countries had facilitated the deal without naming them.[1]

Reporting on the announcement noted that Putin and Mohammed bin Salman have fostered a close personal relationship since 2015, when the crown prince first visited Russia, and that this rapport helped the leaders of the world's two biggest oil exporters conclude and maintain the OPEC+ energy deal.[1]
