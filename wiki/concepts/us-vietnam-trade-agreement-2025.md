# US–Vietnam Trade Agreement (2025)

The US–Vietnam trade agreement of July 2025 is a bilateral tariff arrangement announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on July 2, 2025, setting a 20 per cent United States tariff on imports from [[vietnam|Vietnam]] plus a 40 per cent duty on goods deemed to be transshipped through the country.[1]

## Background

In early April 2025 the administration had announced a 46 per cent "reciprocal" tariff on Vietnamese goods, then paused it for 90 days, creating three months of uncertainty for manufacturers based in the country.[1]

## Reaction

Chinese manufacturers operating in Vietnam reacted with relief, describing the deal as "better than expected" and telling the South China Morning Post that the final rate was manageable; analysts said most Chinese exporters were likely to continue operating in Vietnam rather than relocate.[1] Trump described the agreement as "a great deal of cooperation between our two countries."[1]
