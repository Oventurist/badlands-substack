# Earth's Inner Core Shape Change

In February 2025, scientists who had months earlier confirmed that Earth's inner core recently reversed its spin reported a further finding: the inner core's **shape** is changing.[1]

## Findings

Earth's innermost layer is a hot, solid ball of metal surrounded by a liquid metal outer core.[1] For decades planetary scientists suspected the solid inner core deformed over time as it spun, and researchers have now found the first evidence of such changes occurring over the past 20 years.[1] Signs of the core's deformation appeared in waves from earthquakes strong enough to reach Earth's center.[1]

The research team used the same earthquake data in a 2024 study that resolved a longstanding debate over the inner core's rotation, concluding that the inner core once spun faster than the planet itself but that beginning around 2010 its spin slowed, and that it is now revolving backward relative to the rest of the planet.[1] One scientist quoted in coverage described the result as "almost science fiction."[1]

## Commentary
