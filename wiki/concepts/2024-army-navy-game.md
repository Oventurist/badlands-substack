# 2024 Army–Navy Game

The **2024 Army–Navy game**, the annual college football meeting between the United States Military Academy and the United States Naval Academy, is treated in Chapter 22 of the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series as a dense cluster of intentional military "comms" — described by the author as "about as blatant as I have ever seen."[1]

## Palantir's game-day messaging

The author points first to a [[palantir|Palantir]] post with what he calls "dasting" wording, and then to an advertisement the company ran during the broadcast.[1] His first reaction to the ad was the Q phrase "Watch the water."[1] At the seventeen-second mark a group of drones appears; earlier in the same spot ships are shown stopped by a geofence.[1] The author's inference is that if geofencing can halt vessels in an advertisement, the government plainly has the capability to geofence areas against drones, and yet is not doing so amid the ongoing drone sightings.[1]

The author adds a note on [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], observing that Thiel has been a long-time supporter of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and offering the view that Thiel may not be the monster he has been portrayed as — possibly instead a "white hat" option deployed to subvert what the tech world had planned.[1]

## Guantanamo Bay broadcast segment

During the game, soldiers at a base were shown watching the broadcast; the base, the author notes, was [[guantanamo-bay|Guantanamo Bay]].[1] This is said to have come one day before a delta of Q Drop #3692, which shows a photograph of Gitmo captioned "Operators are standing by."[1]

## Uniforms and the final score

The Army uniforms are described as having a "very familiar" look, and the Navy uniforms as sporting a skull and crossbones.[1] Navy won the game 31–13, which the author reads through the Q instruction "Think mirror."[1] He then adds 31 and 13 to reach 44 and connects that figure to [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] as the forty-fourth president, tying it to news breaking on December 17 that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had returned a Kenyan terrorist to Kenya after releasing him from Guantanamo.[1]

## Related events of the week

The chapter situates the game alongside Trump ringing the opening bell at the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] on December 12 — a date the author matches to deltas of Q Drop #2619 ("Gold shall destroy FED") and Drop #2598, which mentions a hammer and "more to find over time" — and alongside [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s visit to [[arlington-national-cemetery|Arlington National Cemetery]] the same day as the game.[1] Trump's post-bell stop at the [[the-citadel|Citadel]] booth, and a badge whose digits sum to 17, complete the set.[1]
