# MK Ultra

**MK Ultra** was the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] program of mind-control and behavioral-modification research that, in Badlands Media analysis, forms the operational reason elite [[child-sacrifice|child sacrifice]] and child trauma are among the most heavily protected secrets of the modern information battlefield.[1]

## Documented obsession with trauma

According to publicly available documentation cited in the article, the CIA was obsessed with finding ways to compartmentalize memories through trauma-based experiments "no matter how weird, inconclusive or unusual."[1] A 1956 CIA memo is quoted stating that experiments "too dangerous, too shocking, too unusual for routine testing would be of interest to us."[1]

## Targeting of indigenous and black children

In 2023, [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] posted newly surfaced documents indicating that MK Ultra targeted indigenous and black children in medical torture experiments.[1] Badlands Media treats this disclosure as validation of an earlier theory advanced by [[kevin-annett|Kevin Annett]], who reported an anonymous call from a self-described retired Canadian civil servant alleging that torture of indigenous people was tied to Department of National Defense projects, with the [[royal-canadian-mounted-police|RCMP]] delivering tortured victims into mind control programs directed from the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and originating in German research.[1] This raised the possibility that the [[canadian-residential-schools|Canadian residential school mass grave narrative]] functioned as a [[limited-hangout|limited hangout]] concealing MK Ultra programs.[1]

## The memory wars

The article argues that a parallel line of defense protects the program: the promotion of [[false-memory-syndrome|"False Memory Syndrome"]] as a means of discrediting victims of ritual abuse and trauma-based mind control.[1] Whether the founders of False Memory Syndrome organizations can be definitively linked to MK Ultra or to the intelligence agencies is treated as a moot point, since either those foundations originated in intelligence operations or they were created by people friendly to the mind-control cover-up whose theories were then amplified by a controlled media described as an arm of the CIA.[1]

## Strategic framing

Esler concludes that beyond cartoonish images of robed elites who simply enjoy the suffering of children, the sacrifice of children for mind control must be recognized as a closely guarded secret serving tactical, operational and strategic aspects of an enemy's quest for global domination.[1]

## Marilyn Monroe and Monarch programming

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argued that the likely reason for the murder of [[marilyn-monroe|Marilyn Monroe]] lies with her psychiatrist and her involvement in MK Ultra-connected Monarch operations, rather than with the Kennedy brothers.[3] He observed that in the voluminous literature alleging that [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]] killed Monroe — produced by [[fred-otash|Fred Otash]], [[anthony-summers|Anthony Summers]], [[mike-rothmiller|Mike Rothmiller]] and [[netflix|Netflix]] — he had yet to encounter a single reference to MK Ultra, its Tavistockian management, or the broader occult takeover of Hollywood underway during the 1960s.[3] Ehret also noted that the secret grand jury on Kennedy's own assassination, organized by Rothmiller, made no mention of MK Ultra.[3]

## Exposure by the Church Committee

MK ULTRA was brought out to the public in 1975 through the sessions of the [[church-committee|Church Committee]], where [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describes it as a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]-run mind control operation that created unwitting assassins through the use of drugs, torture and other psychological techniques.[2] The disclosure came from CIA Director [[william-colby|William Colby]]'s sworn testimony, which also revealed agency surveillance of American politicians and civil rights leaders, assassinations of foreign leaders and the techniques used to kill them, operations to overthrow foreign governments, and [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]].[2] The agency subsequently regarded Colby as a traitor for having told the truth under oath.[2]
