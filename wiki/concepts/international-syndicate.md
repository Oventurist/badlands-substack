# International Syndicate

The **International Syndicate** is a term coined by Badlands contributor Colonel [[roxane-watkins|Roxane Watkins]] in her primer on [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]] to describe the loose coalition of interests she holds responsible for a century of covert interference in foreign governments.[1] The syndicate, in her formulation, is made up of banking, industrial, religious and political interests rather than any single nationality, religion or ethnic group — a point she stresses against the tendency of commentators to blame one predefined group.[1]

## Origins in Wall Street and the City of London

Watkins locates the syndicate's operational centres in New York City — specifically 120 Broadway, home of the New York Federal Reserve Bank among other financial institutions — and in the City of London.[1] Drawing on [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]]'s research, she describes these forces as constantly interfering in the inner workings of foreign governments during the first half of the twentieth century, beginning with the Russian revolution.[1] The interference was conducted through "expeditions" mounted by nonprofits such as the Red Cross and by for-profit entities such as banks.[1]

## Method: labelling and overthrow

According to Watkins, the syndicate insisted on exploiting natural resources of third-world countries under agreements giving the host country 14% of net profit and the syndicate 86%.[1] Any leader who sought to renegotiate such contracts was labelled a communist; if he persisted or nationalized the industry, the [[nato|NATO]] paramilitary capability of Operation Gladio was activated to overthrow the government.[1] She cites Central America, where the syndicate's [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit]] held 42% of the country's farmable land, obtained by paying officials to confiscate it from middle-class coffee farmers, as the exemplary case.[1]

## Ideological lineage

Watkins traces the syndicate's worldview to the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] and its programme of world government, and describes the resulting doctrine of manufactured crisis as the [[strategy-of-tension|Strategy of Tension]].[1]
