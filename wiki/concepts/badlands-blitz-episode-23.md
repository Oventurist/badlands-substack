# Badlands Blitz Episode 23

**Badlands Blitz Episode 23**, subtitled "Generation America", is an installment of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]] published on the Badlands Substack on July 3, 2026. At the time of archiving the post had accumulated 137 likes. Like every entry in the series, it is presented as a weekly recap and dissection of the work the team produces Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].

## Format and hosts

The episode is co-hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], both linked in the post to their respective Substack profiles. The accompanying written note opens with the customary greeting to "Badlanders!" and identifies the episode as the twenty-third in the series.

## Content

According to the source, the two hosts "kick off the Independence Day festivities early" — the episode was published on the eve of the Fourth of July — with what the note calls "a wide-ranging episode." Two subjects are named as its organising themes.

The first is what the post describes as "the intra-party Civil Wars afflicting both the Right and Left sides of the Uniparty." This framing reflects the recurring Badlands contention, catalogued under [[uniparty|Uniparty]], that the two major American parties function less as genuine opponents than as two wings of a single governing establishment; the episode's interest lies in the internal factional conflicts said to be tearing at each of those wings simultaneously.

The second theme is introduced as "a frank, direct conversation about the sort of Generational Divide [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is being forced to navigate between Boomers, Millennials and Gen Z." The post thus casts Trump's political position as a problem of [[generational-divide|generational management]], with three distinct cohorts — the Baby Boomers, the Millennials and Generation Z — holding divergent expectations that the source presents him as having to reconcile. The subtitle "Generation America" gestures at the same preoccupation.

## Subscription model

The note restates the outlet's standard publishing model: all [[badlands-media|Badlands]] content will continue to be free, including The Blitz, and "the only way we're able to pay ourselves for the time is due to those of you who choose to support it directly." Readers who value the team's daily output and what the post calls "the step-up in said work for 2026" are invited to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time.

The post closes with the program's standard sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
