# Clean Air Act

The Clean Air Act is the U.S. federal statute under which the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] regulates air pollutant emissions.[1]

## Role in climate regulation

The EPA's 2009 [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]] — that greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide endanger public health and welfare — provided the foundational basis under the Clean Air Act for regulating emissions from vehicles, power plants, oil-and-gas facilities, and other sources.[1] The [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s 2026 rescission of that finding was accordingly presented as removing the statutory hook for federal greenhouse gas rules, beginning with motor vehicle emissions standards.[1]
