# WSJ Report on Elon Musk's Drug Use

The Wall Street Journal report on Elon Musk's drug use was a lengthy article published on Saturday night, January 6, 2024, accusing the [[tesla|Tesla]] and [[spacex|SpaceX]] chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] of illegal drug use to an extent that had worried executives and board members while potentially jeopardizing his various federal government contracts.[1]

## Allegations

The [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] piece relied heavily on anonymous sources described as "people who have witnessed his drug use and others with knowledge of it."[1] It reported that Musk had used LSD, cocaine, ecstasy and psychedelic mushrooms, often at private parties around the world where attendees signed nondisclosure agreements or surrendered their phones to enter.[1] Specific episodes cited included taking multiple tabs of acid at a party he hosted in Los Angeles in 2018, using magic mushrooms at an event in Mexico the following year, and taking ketamine recreationally with his brother Kimbal Musk in Miami at a house party during Art Basel in 2021; the article also said he had taken illegal drugs with current SpaceX and former Tesla board member Steve Jurvetson.[1]

## Criticism

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], which characterized the piece as a "Saturday night drive-by" smear, noted that many of the article's accounts dated back several years or more and that there were no specific descriptions of where or when Musk supposedly used cocaine or ecstasy.[1] As for ketamine, the 52-year-old Musk had previously said he was prescribed the drug for depression and had tweeted that it was a better avenue than antidepressants that "zombify" patients; in 2018 he famously shared cannabis on [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]]'s show.[1]
