# Pardongate

Pardongate is the scandal surrounding the clemency [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] granted on the final day of his presidency, January 20, 2001, when he issued 140 pardons and 36 commutations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article argues the sheer number of pardons was a purposeful distraction, intended to lessen scrutiny of one highly controversial act: the pardon of [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]], who had been indicted by the Department of Justice in 1983 for tax evasion, racketeering and wire fraud and was at the time the biggest tax evader in American history.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Rich had fled the United States owing nearly $50 million in back taxes and sat on the FBI's ten most wanted list, which is why he needed a pardon.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The pardon created a firestorm that launched congressional and criminal investigations and outraged most Americans, including Democrats.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article asks why Clinton would grant it knowing the uproar it would cause, and answers that the pardon was about one thing — money — money being, in the author's framing, a factor in everything the Clintons have ever done.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The specific hypothesis offered is that Rich helped the Clintons sell Iranian oil to [[north-korea|North Korea]] for a large profit in connection with the 1994 [[agreed-framework|Agreed Framework]], and that the Clintons took a share.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article further notes the irony that the Clintons themselves committed tax evasion — the same crime Rich was charged with — by failing to report millions in donations from [[ian-telfer|Ian Telfer]] revealed only through Canadian tax records.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) It also observes that after Rich's death in 2013, his business partners, lawyers, advisers and friends, including [[gilbert-chagoury|Gilbert Chagoury]] and [[sergei-kurzin|Sergei Kurzin]], showered millions of dollars on the Clintons in the decade and a half following the scandal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
