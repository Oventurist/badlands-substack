# Double Meanings Exist

"**Double meanings exist**" is an interpretive maxim drawn from the Q drops and used throughout the Badlands Media series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] to justify reading a single term or line in a drop as pointing to two referents at once.[1]

## Application in Chapter 9

Chapter 9 of the series, which covers drops #68 through #71 of November 3–4, 2017, describes drop #68 as "a perfect example of 'Double Meanings Exist.'"[1] The illustration is Q's capitalised "HUMA": the chapter argues that the term refers in part to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], whose family history the same drop goes on to probe, and in part to the [[huma-harvard-university-muslim-alumni|Harvard University Muslim Alumni]] association, whose connections — beginning with [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Al Waleed Bin Talal]] — the author says become central over the following chapters.[1] When Q repeats the instruction "Follow HUMA" in drop #71, the chapter maintains that both readings are intended simultaneously.[1]

## Relation to Q comms generally

Within the series the maxim functions as a general licence for layered decoding, sitting alongside the companion principle that [[disinformation-is-necessary|disinformation is necessary]] and runs in both directions.[1] Together the two rules allow a given drop to be read as literally true, deliberately misleading, or true on a second level, according to the interpreter's judgment — a flexibility that is a defining feature of the genre and a standard basis for outside criticism of it.
