# US Biological Warfare Presence in Africa

The alleged United States biological warfare presence in Africa is a body of claims advanced by the Russian Ministry of Defense that American military-biological research has expanded rapidly across the African continent following Russian efforts to publicise similar activity in Ukraine.[1]

## Russian December 2024 briefing

At a briefing in late December 2024, Major General [[aleksey-rtishchev|Aleksey Rtishchev]], Deputy Chief of Russia's Radiation, Chemical and Biological Protection Troops, said documents at the ministry's disposal confirmed that "the US biological warfare presence on the African continent is growing at a rapid pace," with research institutes of the US Department of Defense actively working in the region.[1]

According to Rtishchev, branches of the US National Naval Military Medical Center are stationed in Ghana and Djibouti, where work is carried out on natural disease foci and the isolation and sequencing of pathogens.[1] The US Army Military Medical Center in Kenya has deployed a network of field stations to monitor the spread of infectious diseases in Equatorial Africa, and in Nigeria a joint medical research centre and a military medical laboratory of the country's armed forces were established in 2024 with ten [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] specialists permanently on staff.[1]

Moscow named [[metabiota|Metabiota]] as one of the "real clients" behind these military-biological projects, saying its requests were directed to the US Army's Infectious Diseases Research Institute and concerned training infectious-disease specialists in Kenya and Uganda, while noting the company has ceased operations in Africa.[1]

## Earlier Kirillov disclosures

The claims build on statements by the late Lieutenant General [[igor-kirillov|Igor Kirillov]]. In February 2022 the Russian Defense Ministry said it had discovered thirty US-funded military biological laboratories in Ukraine, which it described as only a small part of a global network of over 300 similar facilities.[1] Kirillov said in October 2023 that Washington was "forced" to move dual-use biological research to Africa because of Russian efforts to expose such activity and the deteriorating epidemic situation near European bio-sites.[1] He identified key [[pentagon|Pentagon]] contractor activity in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]], Sierra Leone, Cameroon, Uganda and South Africa, naming the Defense Threat Reduction Agency, the National Security Agency and the [[state-department|US Department of State]] as customers on the part of the US government.[1]
