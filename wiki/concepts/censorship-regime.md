# Censorship Regime

The "censorship regime" is the term Badlands Media uses for the apparatus of information control that emerged in the West as a direct result of both Brexit and the rise of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], described as among the most powerful weapons wielded by the ruling class.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

## Character and effects

Despite its self-presentation, the regime is said to aim at controlling and manipulating public discourse by suppressing alternative viewpoints and dissenting voices, stifling free speech while perpetuating a narrative that serves the deep political establishment and further consolidates its power.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) A network of billionaire-funded information gatekeepers is described as having thoroughly marred civil discourse in the West and created a climate of fear around acknowledging the truth.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

## Mechanisms

Left-leaning nonprofits such as [[media-matters|Media Matters]] represent one of the primary aspects of this journalistic cancer.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) Rather than contesting opposing views through spirited debate — the mark of a healthy media environment — the modus operandi is to discourage and eliminate political debate altogether wherever it appears.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) Anything that even remotely threatens the liberal establishment and its orthodoxy becomes fair game, and on occasion even the establishment GOP falls under the regime's protection.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

Two specific techniques recur: the labeling of dissent as racist, homophobic, antisemitic, white-nationalist or transphobic; and the publication of dubious "studies" exploiting the weakened epistemological faculties of modern society, in which a large share of the population will believe almost anything preceded by the words "study finds."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) A third is advertiser pressure applied by groups such as Media Matters and the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]] against platforms including [[x|X]] and [[rumble|Rumble]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) The [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] are cited as evidence of the regime's governmental dimension.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

## Trajectory

The article argues that the number of people who recognize these organizations for what they are has been steadily increasing over the preceding seven years, and that the [[elon-musk|Musk]] lawsuit and the [[ken-paxton|Paxton]] investigation present an opportunity to expose the playbook more widely.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) Media Matters and organizations like it are characterized as symptoms of journalism's slow, agonizing death — "late-stage liberalism at its finest," where the orthodoxy devours everything in the name of liberation, here seeking to liberate the public from the burden of having its own thoughts.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)
