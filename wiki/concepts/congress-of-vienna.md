# Congress of Vienna

The Congress of Vienna of 1815 was the European settlement convened after two decades of Napoleonic wars had decimated Europe.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Assessment

Badlands Media commentary characterises the Congress as "a weak attempt" at a peace conference, contrasting it with the more durable framework established by the [[peace-of-westphalia|1648 Peace of Westphalia]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) It is placed within a lineage of postwar settlements that also includes the League of Nations and the [[united-nations|United Nations]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) The author notes that such conferences were sometimes shaped by honest agendas and sometimes not, but that human memory is weak and the Byzantine web of power politics in which the Anglo-American deep state operates is not easily transformed by agreements alone.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)
