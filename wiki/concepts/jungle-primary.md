# Jungle Primary

The "jungle primary" is an anomaly within the electoral system of California in which candidates from all parties appear on a single primary ballot and the two highest finishers advance to the general election regardless of party affiliation.[1]

## Exploitation by incumbents

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] identified the jungle primary as the structural feature that makes possible one of Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]]'s signature tactics: broadcasting advertisements that emphasize the conservative credentials of the Republican opponent the incumbent judges least capable of running a competitive general-election campaign.[1] Because the ads raise that candidate's name recognition among Republican primary voters, they help him finish second and lock out stronger rivals.[1]

In 2022 Newsom used this method against state legislator [[brian-dahle|Brian Dahle]], airing spots reminding voters that Dahle vehemently opposed abortion and advocated for gun rights.[1] Dahle was overwhelmingly supported by Republican voters who otherwise would have known little about him, secured the nomination, and was then buried by a [[culture-war|culture war]] onslaught, leaving Newsom to cruise to an effortless victory.[1]
