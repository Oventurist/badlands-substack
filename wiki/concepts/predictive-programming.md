# Predictive Programming

Predictive programming is the claim that entertainment media is used to prepare audiences in advance to accept future events, narratives and agendas. In "A New Hollywood," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues that [[hollywood|Hollywood]] has long used predictive programming to condition the public into accepting [[deep-state|Deep State]] narratives, and that the same mechanism is now being run in the opposite direction — to prepare the public for what [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has in store.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

Carlson's examples are drawn from [[paramount-global|Paramount]] and [[skydance-media|Skydance]] output. *Mission Impossible: Dead Reckoning*, whose antagonist is a world-conquering AI, appeared roughly six months after ChatGPT's release, with production necessarily predating the AI conversation — which he reads as a warning about the technology and an argument that America must wield "the AI sword" rather than its adversaries.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) [[lioness|Lioness]] season two, depicting a [[cia|CIA]] crew rescuing an American politician and trafficked children from a Mexican drug kingpin, he reads as preparation for Trump taking down the Mexican drug cartel.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) The volume of intelligence-agency programming from Trump allies leads him to infer that Trump does not intend to scatter the CIA into a thousand pieces but to reform it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

He also frames the reverse case: conditioning can be benign. The heroic films of his youth, he suggests, conditioned him "in a good way," instilling a need for justice and dumb courage.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

## Application to UAP disclosure

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] applied the concept to the extraterrestrial disclosure sequence in June 2023, arguing that fictional media reaching back to the 1950s placed the idea of extraterrestrials in the public mind and that "the same type of 'predictive programming' was used for 9/11, Covid, and other globalist-sponsored societal manipulation events."[2] On that basis he treated the [[david-grusch|David Grusch]] whistleblower story as fitting a historical trend of staged conditioning toward an [[alien-disclosure-op|engineered disclosure event]].[2]

## Cited in Structures of Control

Part 3 of Badlands Media's *Structures of Control* series lists predictive programming among the tools by which the [[deep-state|Deep State]] "weaponized the lower-dimensional planes," alongside manufactured synchronicities and AI-driven mass consciousness manipulation, to keep humanity in cycles of fear, division and programmed limitation.[3] The essay places it within a broader account of narrative control in which Hollywood storytelling and corporate news cycles craft hero-villain dichotomies that reinforce authority structures.[3]

## The Joker films

Carlson's September 2024 essay "The Deep State Has Already Lost" offers the [[joker-film-series|Joker films]] as his clearest case, defining predictive programming as "giving the target audience the permission to riot at a future point" — a kind of blueprint.[4] He notes the first film was released in the fall of 2019, before the Covid lockdowns and the BLM and [[antifa|Antifa]] riots, and argues it had to be released then rather than closer to the 2020 unrest because movie theaters were closed in 2020.[4] He reads [[joaquin-phoenix|Joaquin Phoenix]]'s emaciated, violent lead — a character who spurs rioting and revolt among others like himself — as the posterchild for Antifa.[4] The scheduled release of *Joker II* in the fall of 2024, less than a month before the presidential election, prompts him to ask whether Antifa-types were again being given a go-ahead to riot, disrupt the election or interfere with the transition of power.[4]

## Pandemic and occult release dates (Carlson, 2023)

In "The World is a Vampire" (January 24, 2023), [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] defined predictive programming as a term common in the "truther community" referring to the Elites' foretelling of future events in order to prepare the public to psychologically accept what is to come.[5] He distinguished programming aimed at specific events, such as 9/11 or COVID, from broader conditioning toward overarching narratives and agendas, naming the acceptance of pedophilia and transgenderism as examples of the latter.[5]

Carlson argued it was no coincidence that zombie films and series such as *The Walking Dead* became enormously popular over the preceding decade, reading them as preparation for a virus pandemic, and called *Contagion* a more obvious instance, noting its release on 9/9/11.[5] He observed that *The Walking Dead* premiered on 10/31/10, a date he described as dear to the occult, and that *World War Z* was released on 6/21/13, the summer solstice and another occult date.[5] He also cited the 2007 Will Smith film *I Am Legend*, released 12/14/07, in which the world's population is almost entirely wiped out by the ill effects of a cancer vaccine while the survivors become flesh-eating zombies.[5]

Beyond pandemic themes, Carlson pointed to *[[rupauls-drag-race|RuPaul's Drag Race]]*, which premiered in 2009 well before what he called the transgender hysteria, and to the 2013 film *[[elysium|Elysium]]* as anticipating the border crisis by roughly four years.[5] He offered *[[the-truman-show|The Truman Show]]* (1998) as an earlier demonstration of lives completely controlled and orchestrated without the subject's knowledge.[5] Carlson also identified a counter-current, crediting [[stanley-kubrick|Stanley Kubrick]] with embedding hidden messages intended to warn audiences rather than condition them.[5] He linked the whole practice to [[karmic-retribution|karmic retribution]], the claim that the elite believe advance warning of abuse reduces their share of blame for it.[5]
