# Swedish No-Go Zones

"No-go zones" is the term used in Badlands Media commentary for districts of Swedish cities that, since the mid-2010s, are described as areas police, social workers, firefighters and ambulances do not enter.[1] In this account the areas are populated largely by immigrant communities that draw on [[sweden|Sweden]]'s welfare system while refusing to abide by Swedish law or custom.[1]

## Origins in asylum policy

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], writing for Badlands Media in October 2023, traced the phenomenon to the scale and pace of Swedish asylum intake.[1] He wrote that between 1960 and 1982 Sweden accepted only about 20,000 asylum-seeking refugees, while between 1982 and 2024 it accepted roughly 278,000 — including 163,000 from Syria in 2015 alone, before cutting back.[1] Because Sweden is a country of about 10 million people, he argued, 278,000 asylum seekers is proportionally equivalent to the United States admitting roughly 9 million, and the single-year 2015 intake equivalent to about 5 million.[1] These figures cover asylum seekers only; substantially more non-asylum immigration occurred alongside them.[1] The asylum populations came predominantly from war-torn, Muslim-majority countries including Iran, Syria, Iraq, Afghanistan and Somalia.[1]

## Claimed effects

Carlson wrote that after the 2015 intake Sweden moved from having one of the lowest per-capita rates of deadly shootings in Europe to the highest.[1] Drawing on a 1994 visit to Stockholm, he described a city that had then been clean, safe and socially cohesive, with an immigrant share he estimated at around 5%, supported by a high-tax welfare state that could absorb that ratio.[1] Even then, he recalled, his cousin reported that immigrant men were sometimes seen beating their wives in public for being too "Swedish" — that is, for assimilating — and that drug and prostitution rings were often immigrant-run, with Swedes reluctant to act for fear of being called racist.[1] He also reported that many wealthy Swedes had relocated to England to escape Swedish tax rates.[1]

## As a template

Carlson advanced the view that Sweden served as a "test country" for a model of "how to destroy a country through mass immigration," declining to name the person he believed directed that project but stating that "his initials are 'G.S.'" — a reference readers were expected to take as [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1] He then applied the same template to the United States, arguing that admissions under the [[obama-administration|Obama administration]] settled America-hating populations in concentrated areas as part of a [[deep-state|Deep State]] plan.[1]
