# Executive Order 12608

**Executive Order 12608** is the executive order issued by President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] that revoked [[executive-order-11110|Executive Order 11110]] in its entirety.[1] In the Badlands Media feature "The COVFEFE Dig," [[gmoney|GMONEY]] presents the revocation as evidence that Kennedy's monetary order mattered, arguing that an inconsequential order would not have needed to be struck from the books.[1]

## The 9/9 date

Two details are emphasized. The first is that Reagan revoked the order shortly after he was shot, which the author offers as circumstantial support for the view that the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and the central-banking interests behind it had a stake in the outcome.[1] The second is the date: Reagan signed the revocation on September 9.[1] The 9/9 date is threaded through the rest of the dig, which links it to the minting of the first [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]] token on 9/9/2016 and to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s "basket of deplorables" speech delivered the same day.[1]
