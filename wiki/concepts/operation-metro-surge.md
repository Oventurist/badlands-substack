# Operation Metro Surge

Operation Metro Surge is the name of an aggressive federal immigration enforcement operation conducted in Minnesota by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] and [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] under the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] during the winter of 2025–2026.

According to the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]], the operation produced sustained clashes in Minneapolis and St. Paul and led to the fatal shootings of two United States citizen protesters: [[renee-good|Renee Good]] on January 7 and [[alex-pretti|Alex Pretti]] on January 24, 2026. The deaths became a focus of the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing of March 3, 2026, at which Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] defended the operation and blamed "violent protesters" for the disorder, while Democratic senators accused officers of excessive force and criticized Noem for describing the victims as "domestic terrorists."
