# COVID-19 Pandemic

The COVID-19 pandemic — referred to in the Badlands Media corpus as the "Covid Plandemic" — is treated less as a public health event than as a narrative operation. In [[a-virus-or-a-metaphor|A Virus, or a Metaphor?]], [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues that the significance of the episode lay in what the response accomplished politically rather than in the pathogen itself.

## Origin claims

The article states the author's belief that "some sort of bioweapon was released from a lab in Wuhan, China," but that Wuhan was not the point of origin. It claims the program originated in a laboratory in North Carolina and was relocated to the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan]] facility to maintain secrecy and control, and that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] funded and ran it, moving it overseas once [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took office. Additional bioweapon work is alleged to have taken place in biolabs elsewhere, specifically in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and possibly Taiwan. Trump's own statement that COVID was intentionally created in a lab and released on the world is cited approvingly.

## The lockdowns

The essay argues that the Cabal's plan was to keep America and the world shut down for a far longer period than actually occurred — "maybe up to five years," or whatever length of time was required to destroy the middle class and produce a two-tier society of working-class poor and elite, culminating in a one-world totalitarian government. The lockdowns, in this account, would have continued "until the [[deep-state|Deep State]] had complete control of everything."

The article contends the resulting Covid narrative "forced lockdowns, creating an overreaching control of people, and an abandonment of human rights, as well as a justification for mail-in ballots," through which it asserts "a duly-elected President was overthrown and removed from power." It further argues that the bioweapon itself "wasn't as deadly as they hoped," but that continued messaging by what it calls communist-controlled media and government officials would produce the same destructive effect regardless.

## Trump's response

The author's defense of Trump's handling of the pandemic is that he forced Big Pharma to accelerate development of the [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccines]] because their availability was the only mechanism capable of ending the lockdowns before permanent damage was done. Trump is described as weighing potential vaccine harms against the harms of extended lockdowns and concluding that the lockdowns were significantly worse.

## The "Chyna virus"

Trump's references to "Chyna" and the "Chyna virus," for which he was widely accused of racism, are interpreted in the article as coded references to [[communism|Communism]] and to infiltration of America by the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]]. The related claim that COVID was the worst thing to happen to America "since the Spanish Flu in 1917" is likewise read as intentional, pointing to the [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik uprising]] rather than to the [[spanish-flu|Spanish Flu]] of 1918.
