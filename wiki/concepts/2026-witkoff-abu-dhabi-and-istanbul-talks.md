# 2026 Witkoff Abu Dhabi and Istanbul Talks

In early February 2026, US Middle East envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] was scheduled to hold a series of diplomatic meetings across two regional hubs: talks with Russian and Ukrainian representatives in Abu Dhabi, and separate discussions with Iranian officials in [[istanbul|Istanbul]] focused on [[iran|Iran]]'s nuclear program.[1]

## Context

The meetings came amid heightened tensions involving Iran, [[russia|Russia]], and Ukraine.[1] Russian and Iranian officials had publicly acknowledged ongoing contacts with the United States regarding the nuclear deal framework, while US officials warned of potential military consequences if diplomacy failed.[1] Separately, after US warnings, Iranian authorities stated they had no plans to conduct live-fire naval exercises in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] at that time, a signal of temporary de-escalation as the diplomatic engagements proceeded.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the talks as evidence that Russia was "running interference on the Iran op," and contrasted them with the reaction of talk-radio host [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]], who publicly asked "Who the hell is in charge of this?"[1] The commentary answered that the person in charge was President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and characterized Levin's frustration as the reaction of a war-oriented commentator confronted with the prospect of peace: supportive of Trump so long as war with Iran appeared likely, hostile once peace appeared instead.[1]

The piece described Witkoff's Abu Dhabi track as "charting the course of the Golden Age" with Russian counterparts, while the Iranians met in [[turkey|Turkey]] with Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, the Qataris, and the Saudis — a grouping the author speculated was game-planning how to handle Benjamin Netanyahu and Levin.[1] He judged that the sequence was unlikely to conclude without some form of confrontation, adding that the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] was not yet operating at full capacity in the open, and noted a predicted smear campaign against [[mohammed-bin-salman|MBS]] materializing.[1]
