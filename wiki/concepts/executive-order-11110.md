# Executive Order 11110

**Executive Order 11110** is the 1963 executive order signed by President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] that, in the account given by [[gmoney|GMONEY]] in the Badlands Media feature "The COVFEFE Dig," represented an attempt to remove what the author calls the evils of the central bankers.[1] The order is described as an effort to transfer power from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] to the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|United States Department of the Treasury]] by replacing Federal Reserve Notes with silver certificates.[1]

## The monetary distinction

The dig frames the significance of the order through a simple contrast: a United States Note is currency issued without interest attached, whereas a Federal Reserve Note is currency issued with interest attached.[1] On that reading, moving note issuance back to the Treasury would have removed the interest burden that the author treats as the mechanism of central-bank power over the American state.[1]

## Reinterpretation as a merger, not a seizure

The article anticipates the standard objection that EO 11110 accomplished nothing and arguably enlarged the bankers' authority.[1] GMONEY concedes the point "to some degree," but argues that Kennedy's intention was to merge the Treasury with the Federal Reserve rather than to strip the Fed's power outright, on the theory that bringing down a corrupt system requires first tying its components together.[1] He supports the claim rhetorically by asking why, if EO 11110 was inconsequential, [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] revoked it shortly after being shot.[1]

## Revocation and the 11/11 motif

The order was revoked in full by [[executive-order-12608|Executive Order 12608]], issued on September 9.[1] The 9/9 date is treated in the dig as one of the recurring markers of the thesis, alongside the claim that 11/11 — the 315th day of the year — encodes the "FE FE" ending of [[covfefe|covfefe]] as 11 11.[1] The number 11110 itself is folded into that pattern, and 11/22, the date of Kennedy's assassination, is cited as a further node in the sequence.[1]

## Relationship to the Trump-era Fed–Treasury merger

The dig's central historical claim is that the merger Kennedy allegedly sought was completed under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the COVID-19 market crisis of 2020, when the Treasury under [[steven-mnuchin|Steve Mnuchin]] took equity positions in Federal Reserve special purpose vehicles.[1] The author asks directly whether Trump accomplished what Kennedy attempted but Reagan reversed.[1]
