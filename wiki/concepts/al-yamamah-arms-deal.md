# Al-Yamamah Arms Deal

Al-Yamamah was a major fighter-jet program between the United Kingdom and Saudi Arabia, long associated with allegations of commissions and illicit payments.[1]

The British arms broker [[douglas-leese|Douglas Leese]] — the man who introduced [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] to [[adnan-khashoggi|Adnan Khashoggi]] — is reported to have taken commissions on major deals including the Al-Yamamah program, alongside covert arms packages whose timelines overlapped the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] affair.[1] The program has been discussed in the UK Parliament's published proceedings in connection with those commission arrangements.[1]
