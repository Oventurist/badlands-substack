# Mianyang Fusion Research Facility

The Mianyang fusion research facility is a large laser-ignited fusion research centre that China appears to be constructing in the southwestern city of Mianyang, according to experts at two analytical organisations cited by Reuters in January 2025.[1]

## Design and implications

Satellite photos show four outlying "arms" that will house laser bays and a central experiment bay holding a target chamber containing hydrogen isotopes that the powerful lasers will fuse together to produce energy, said Decker Eveleth, a researcher at the U.S.-based independent research organisation CNA Corp.[1] Analysts said the development could aid nuclear weapons design as well as work exploring power generation.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary presented the Reuters report as confirmation of a predicted mainstream media pivot from Russia to China as the designated 2025 antagonist, mocking the emphasis on "laser bays and target chambers" and adding that nuclear technology is a good thing.[1]
