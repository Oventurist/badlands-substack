# Reichstag Fire

The Reichstag Fire is the 1933 burning of the German parliament building, an event that Badlands Media writers invoke as the template for the use of a crisis to eliminate a rival political party.[1]

## Badlands Media framing

Commenting on moves in the [[bundestag|Bundestag]] to ban the [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]] party, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the effort as a re-run of events in Germany 91 years earlier.[1] In his account, the National Socialist party won the majority of seats, installed [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] as Chancellor, and then staged the Reichstag Fire as a false flag which they blamed on the Communist Party of Germany (KPD) — at the time the second-most popular party in the legislature.[1] New elections followed, most seats went to the Nazis, and the resulting supermajority was used to merge the office of Chancellor with the recently vacated presidency, creating the title of Führer.[1]

The same commentary asserted that a paramilitary group of troublemakers called Antifaschistische Aktion, better known as [[antifa|Antifa]], was used alongside the Brown Shirts to intimidate and beat political rivals, the two groups forming what the writer described as a controlled-opposition dynamic.[1] The historical parallel was drawn to argue that the only substantive difference in 2024 was that the party facing prohibition was on the right rather than the communist left.[1] The writer further noted the irony that the [[social-democratic-party-of-germany|Social Democratic Party]] — which he characterised as a socialist faction historically entangled with the banned KPD — was accusing the conservative AfD of being National Socialists.[1]
