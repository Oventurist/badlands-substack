# Kahanism

Kahanism is the far-right Jewish nationalist ideology associated with Rabbi [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]], the founder of the [[jewish-defense-league|Jewish Defense League]] in New York City and later a member of the Israeli [[knesset|Knesset]].[1] Its core program included the imposition of Halachic Law — described in commentary as effectively the Jewish equivalent of Shariah Law, imbuing rabbinical leaders with lawmaking powers akin to those of Shariah clerics — the forced deportation of Arabs, and the banning of marriage and sexual relations between Jews and Gentiles.[1]

## Origins

Kahane was convicted of conducting or planning several terrorist attacks in the 1970s, including a plot to blow up the Iraqi embassy in Washington, D.C.[1] He was arrested in Israel in the 1980s and convicted of plotting to fly a small plane loaded with explosives into the Dome of the Rock, for which he served six months in jail; after his release he was elected to the Knesset, where he served four years and pushed the Kahanist agenda.[1] The Jewish Defense League he founded was accused of planning many violent attacks, some of which occurred, and was classified as a terrorist organization by law enforcement throughout America before ceasing operations in 2015.[1]

## Contemporary influence

Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] argues that the zealots serving in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s Security Cabinet — the highest positions in the Israeli government — are all Kahanists.[1] [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]] served as defense counsel for [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]], Kahane's protégé and political heir, after Gopstein was arrested in 2015 for publicly advocating the burning of all churches and the forced expulsion of Christians from Israel.[1]

The ideology returned to the center of Israeli political debate on November 12, 2025, when [[likud|Likud]] MK [[nissim-vaturi|Nissim Vaturi]] told the Knesset plenum that "Kahane was right" and that his party was wrong to ostracize him, prompting Yesh Atid MK Yorai Lahav Hertzanu to accuse Likud of degenerating into "a pathetic branch of Kahane Chai."[1]


## Lineage and contemporary adherents

Badlands Media traces Kahanism from [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]] — arrested by the Shin Bet in 1980 for plotting to blow up the Al-Aqsa Mosque and thereby ignite a holy war between Israel and Islam — through his followers to the present day.[2] Kahane won a [[knesset|Knesset]] seat in 1984 but was boycotted by all other members, prompting a law barring racial-supremacy platforms from office that the outlet says [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] effectively repealed in 2022.[2] Among Kahane's most devout followers was the physician [[baruch-goldstein|Baruch Goldstein]], who killed twenty-nine worshippers at the Cave of the Patriarchs in Hebron in February 1994 in outrage at the Oslo Accords, and whose gravesite has since become a destination for Kahanist extremists.[2] [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]] is described as Kahane's spiritual successor, and National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] — who defended Gopstein against incitement charges in January 2024 — is reported in Israel to display a portrait of Goldstein in his living room.[2]
