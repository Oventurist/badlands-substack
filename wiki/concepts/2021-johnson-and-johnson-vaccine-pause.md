# 2021 Johnson & Johnson Vaccine Pause

The **2021 Johnson & Johnson vaccine pause** was the temporary suspension of use of the single-dose [[johnson-and-johnson|Johnson & Johnson]] COVID-19 vaccine in the United States.[1] The [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] and the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]] paused the vaccine on April 13, 2021 after reports of very rare but dangerous blood clots, and lifted the pause ten days later on April 23, 2021.[1] NBC News reported the episode in connection with 28 identified clotting cases.[1] The vaccine, produced by [[janssen-pharmaceuticals|Janssen Pharmaceuticals]], had been approved on February 28, 2021.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] questions why the two agencies would pause the J&J product and then reverse within ten days.[1] Her proposed explanation is that the J&J vaccine had been created outside the "cabal's" control through the contracts signed with the company in December 2019 and February 2020, so that recipients may have been receiving saline or the Army's [[spike-ferritin-nanoparticle-vaccine|SpFN "supervax"]] rather than the intended product.[1] On this reading the pause was an attempt to tarnish the company's reputation and discourage uptake, which backfired by directing attention to clotting reports emerging worldwide in connection with the [[moderna|Moderna]], [[pfizer|Pfizer]] and [[astrazeneca|AstraZeneca]] vaccines, forcing a rapid walk-back.[1] These claims are the author's speculation and are presented as such under the standard Badlands disclaimer.[1]
