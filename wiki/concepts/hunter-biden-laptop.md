# Hunter Biden Laptop

The "Hunter Biden laptop" refers to a computer abandoned by [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] whose contents — principally an email archive, published in searchable form at bidenlaptopemails.com — have been used as documentary evidence in reporting on the Biden family's foreign business dealings.[1]

The archive underpins much of the record of Hunter Biden's relationship with the Ukrainian gas company [[burisma|Burisma]] and with the consulting firm [[blue-star-strategies|Blue Star Strategies]]. Searches for [[sally-painter|Sally Painter]] within it show contact with Hunter Biden dating to 4 March 2010 and her 2011 recruitment of him to the board of the [[truman-national-security-project|Truman National Security Project]].[1] The same archive contains the October–November 2015 thread "Revised Burisma Proposal, Contract and Invoice," in which [[vadym-pozharskyi|Vadym Pozharskyi]] set out the "ultimate purpose" of Burisma's contract with Blue Star as securing influential current and former US policy-makers to "close down for any cases/pursuits against Nikolay in Ukraine" — a reference to [[mykola-zlochevsky|Mykola Zlochevsky]].[1]

Further messages in the collection document [[eric-schwerin|Eric Schwerin]]'s confirmation that US officials' names were deliberately left out of the written scope, [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]]'s requests for calls between the Blue Star and Burisma teams, a 2 December 2015 conference call between Blue Star and [[white-house|the White House]] about the Vice President's Ukraine trip, and a 2016 message to [[rosemont-seneca|Rosemont Seneca]] noting "No help from our friend(s) however."[1]
