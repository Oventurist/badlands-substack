# 2024 Saudi–Pakistan Crown Prince Summit

On April 8, 2024, HRH Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman bin Abdulaziz Al-Saud]], Crown Prince and Prime Minister of the Kingdom of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], held an official meeting with Prime Minister of the Islamic Republic of [[pakistan|Pakistan]] Muhammad [[shehbaz-sharif|Shehbaz Sharif]] at Al-Safa Palace in Makkah Al-Mukarramah, documented in a joint statement issued by the Saudi Press Agency.[1]

## Bilateral content

The Crown Prince opened the meeting by congratulating Sharif on assuming office and extending warm wishes for his tenure; Sharif conveyed gratitude for the Kingdom's steadfast support and hospitality and reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to bolstering bilateral ties and economic cooperation.[1] Discussions centered on fortifying fraternal relations between the two nations and exploring avenues for enhanced collaboration across sectors, with emphasis on the Kingdom's supportive role in Pakistan's economy and a mutual desire to strengthen trade and investment ties.[1] Both parties affirmed their commitment to expediting a first wave of an investment package worth $5 billion that had been discussed previously.[1]

## Regional and Gaza positions

The two leaders exchanged views on regional and global developments of mutual interest, including what the statement called the worrying situation in Gaza.[1] They urged international efforts to halt Israeli military operations in Gaza and to mitigate the humanitarian impact, and underscored the imperative for the international community to pressure Israel to cease hostilities, adhere to international law, and facilitate unhindered humanitarian aid access.[1] They discussed advancing the peace process in accordance with relevant Security Council and General Assembly resolutions and with the Arab Peace Initiative, aimed at a just and comprehensive solution and the establishment of an independent Palestinian state with East Jerusalem as its capital.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the day as a "busy day in the Kingdom," noting that MBS and his attaché also hosted the newly appointed Palestinian Prime Minister Dr. [[mohammad-mustafa|Muhammad Mustafa]], whose appointment by President [[mahmoud-abbas|Abbas]] had been rebuked by [[hamas|Hamas]] because it was not first consulted.[1] He observed that Foreign Minister [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] and Mustafa were presumably discussing how best to resolve the Gaza conflict, and argued that both Hamas and the Biden administration were being boxed out of the peace process, with National Security Adviser Jake Sullivan sidelined in Washington nursing his ribs at what could have been a defining moment of his career.[1]

Faisal also fielded a call from UN Secretary-General [[antonio-guterres|António Guterres]], whose reaction the commentator attributed to Faisal's public meeting with Mustafa.[1] MBS was described as playing the role of Peacemaker in the absence of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], hosting Pakistan's leader, calling on India to resolve its ancient feud with Pakistan over Kashmir and Jammu, and affirming the $5 billion infrastructure investment commitment.[1]

MBS additionally took a call from Türkiye's President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]], which the commentator read as revealing the regional hierarchy: for months Erdoğan had capitalized on the Gaza situation with dramatic speeches and promises of decisive military action against Israel — promises carrying weight given that Turkey has the largest military in the Middle East and NATO backing — yet none had come to fruition, and he was now instead calling MBS to plead for action.[1] The take concluded that all of this represented a single day in the office of the Saudi Foreign Ministry, which the writer called "the most talented team of diplomats in the world."[1]
