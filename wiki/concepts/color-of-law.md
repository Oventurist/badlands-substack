# Color of Law

**Color of law** is the term used by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] in the Badlands Media feature "Trump Did It" to name a counterfeit legal order that displaces the true [[rule-of-law|rule of law]].[1]

## Definition

Deschamps defines the color of law as "a system that _looks like justice_ but is, in fact, anti-justice."[1] Where the rule of law is described as man's attempt to govern in accordance with God's will, the color of law preserves the outward forms and vocabulary of legality while inverting its function.[1] The article holds that when the rule of law is eroded and eventually replaced by the color of law, "freedom and prosperity break down in society."[1]

## Alleged deliberate use

The article contends that globalists "know all about this and they intentionally use a false, **color-of-law system**, knowing it will cause society to destabilize."[1] The claimed result is a society in which there is "no fair play, only cheaters can get ahead," producing "a might-is-right society... out of chaos—tyranny—where the weak are crushed under the feet of the strong, and fraud, deception, and coercion are the orders of the day."[1] Deschamps invites the reader to recognize this description in the present day, asking simply, "Sound familiar?"[1]

## Contrast with true justice

By contrast, the article says true justice must both attempt to make victims whole and motivate the perpetrator toward redemption, applied case by case rather than by rubber stamp.[1] Using a sports analogy, Deschamps argues that a referee's even-handed penalty restores the balance of play and allows the offended party to restrain the urge for revenge, and that a legal order that fails this test causes citizens "to lose faith in their society, motivating them to cheat to get what they need."[1]

## Related material

Deschamps states that the "spiritual, cultural, and sociological weapons developed by the globalists" would be examined further in future posts, situating the color of law within a broader series that includes his earlier feature "Trump Knows."[1] The concept is closely tied to the article's treatment of [[globalism]] and of the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]].[1]

## Announced as the subject of a sequel

Closing "Trump Knows," [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] announced that the next installment would break down precisely how the [[legal-system|legal system]] is not a [[rule-of-law|rule of law]] system but a color of law system.[2] He said he would argue that the ultimate weapon against the cabal is for the people to reclaim their knowledge of the law so as to build a true rule-of-law society, and that [[donald-trump|Trump]] and the patriots are preparing the world for that solution — possibly with an early version already available for use.[2]
