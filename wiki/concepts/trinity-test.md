# Trinity Test

The Trinity test was the first-ever detonation of an atomic bomb, conducted by the [[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]] in the New Mexico desert near Los Alamos.[1]

## In the film

Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer* renders the test as the most stunning visual sequence in the film, described by Badlands as beautifully shot and as providing badly needed updated imagery of the formidable power of nuclear weapons.[1] Immediately before the test, the film shows [[leslie-groves|Leslie Groves]] worrying about destroying the world and [[j-robert-oppenheimer|Oppenheimer]] answering that the chance of annihilating all life on Earth is near zero — prompting Groves's line that zero would be nicer.[1] In reality those concerns belonged to [[edward-teller|Edward Teller]], who feared atmospheric hydrogen fusion could set off a global chain reaction.[1]

## Narrative significance

Badlands argues the modernized mushroom-cloud imagery is the point: refreshing public memory of world-ending capability at the moment the same fear framework is being transferred to [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] and used to argue for [[global-governance|global governance]].[1]
