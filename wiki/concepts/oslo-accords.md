# Oslo Accords

The Oslo Accords are the agreements concluded in the 1990s between Israel and the Palestine Liberation Organization that established the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] and divided the [[west-bank|West Bank]] into zones of Palestinian and Israeli administrative and security control.

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] invokes the Accords as the legal benchmark against which the Israeli Security Cabinet's February 2026 West Bank measures are judged. Those measures — shifting building authority in Hebron, facilitating settler land purchases, repealing restrictions on non-Muslim real estate transactions, and expanding Israeli enforcement into Palestinian-controlled areas — are described in the source as actions that violate the Oslo Accords and that have drawn United States concern over regional stability. [[likud|Likud]] Energy Minister [[eli-cohen|Eli Cohen]] characterised the same measures as establishing "de facto sovereignty" precluding a Palestinian state, while President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] responded that he is "against annexation."
