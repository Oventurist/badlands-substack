# Digital Bill of Rights

The **Digital Bill of Rights** was the informal name given to the free-speech policy platform unveiled by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on December 15, 2022, as the first policy video drop of his 2024 presidential campaign.[1] The proposal was described by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] as a "history-making announcement" that "will change the WORLD."[1]

## Rollout

The policy video was published as an exclusive by [[jack-posobiec|Jack Posobiec]], who wrote that he had obtained a copy of Trump "unveiling the major planks of his official Free Speech Policy Proposal" and that it was the first in a planned series of video drops from the 2024 campaign.[1] Posobiec's post drew roughly 8,150 reposts and 19,700 likes, far outstripping the engagement on the mocking crypto-world commentary about the same day's NFT launch.[1]

## Overshadowed by the NFT launch

The Digital Bill of Rights was announced the same day as the [[trump-digital-trading-cards|Trump Digital Trading Cards]] NFT collection, and left-wing media coverage concentrated almost entirely on the NFTs while barely noticing the free-speech proposal.[1] Gillespie treated this not as a scheduling blunder but as intentional misdirection — the NFT drop was the "sizzle" and the Digital Bill of Rights the "steak" — and argued that pro-Trump commentators [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and [[steve-cortes|Steve Cortes]], who publicly denounced the same-day pairing as a "grievous error" warranting firings, were knowing participants in the diversion.[1] Her article deferred detailed analysis of the free-speech plan itself to other commentators, focusing instead on the monetary implications of the NFT drop.[1]
