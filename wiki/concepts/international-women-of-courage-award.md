# International Women of Courage Award

The International Women of Courage Award is an honor administered by the United States [[state-department]] recognizing women around the world for advocacy and personal courage. It is typically presented at a White House ceremony timed to International Women's Day.[1]

## 2023 controversy

On March 8, 2023, International Women's Day, First Lady Jill Biden presented an International Women of Courage Award at the White House to a recipient who was a biological male. The presentation drew widespread criticism on social media, where commentator Greg Price's post highlighting the ceremony accumulated thousands of reposts.[1]

The [[badlands-news-brief]] covered the episode as a short item, with commentary arguing that the "woke agenda" had become increasingly ridiculous, that a large majority of Americans did not agree with the framing, and that highly visible episodes of this kind ultimately worked against the agenda by prompting broader public skepticism.[1]
