# Executive Order 14344

Executive Order 14344, titled "Making Federal Architecture Beautiful Again," is an executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on August 28, 2025.[1] It was published in the Federal Register on September 3, 2025.[1]

## Context

The order forms part of a broader second-term effort to reshape the physical character of the federal capital, an effort that also encompassed the [[national-park-service|National Park Service]]'s multimillion-dollar program to repair Washington's fountains and water features — including the cascading fountain at [[meridian-hill-park|Meridian Hill Park]], which reopened on May 14, 2026.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the restoration agenda favorably but incompletely, writing that while it was pleasant for the capital to receive "a face lift," she would prefer that "the corruption and illegitimacy were getting nipped and tucked."[1] She argued that the highest value of the effort would be as a demonstration effect — that Washington should set "the acceptable aesthetic for the nation" and shame local leaders into cleaning up their own cities — while expressing doubt that this would happen.[1]
