# Controlled Demolition of the Global Debt System

"Controlled demolition" is a metaphor used in Badlands Media commentary to describe an alleged deliberate, staged dismantling of the global central-bank debt system, carried out in a way that replaces the old structure while limiting collateral damage to the societies dependent on it.[1]

## Origin of the metaphor

In "The Master Builder," Joe Lange draws the term from the construction industry, where controlled demolition is the process of bringing down an old skyscraper in order to replace it while preventing damage to surrounding structures.[1] The metaphor is chosen deliberately to fit the article's portrait of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a "master builder" — a real-estate developer who took over his father's business in 1971 and renamed it [[trump-organization|The Trump Organization]], and who is now said to be constructing something far larger than any building.[1]

## The claimed project

The article asserts that what is being demolished is the global central-bank debt system, and what is to replace it is a new global financial architecture no longer controlled by the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]] and an allied elite, in which people are no longer born into debt slavery.[1] It links this ambition to [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]], whose portrait Trump hung in the White House and who destroyed the central bank of his era, and quotes Jackson's warning about the "monied interest" deriving power from a controllable paper currency.[1] It similarly cites [[thomas-jefferson|Thomas Jefferson]]'s statement that banking establishments are more dangerous than standing armies.[1]

## Preconditions asserted by the article

The piece contends that two preconditions were already in place: Trump's claimed effective merger of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] into the U.S. Treasury, described in the companion article "Master and Commander," and the [[devolution|devolution]] of military command such that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] does not control the main functions of the U.S. military.[1] Because the debt system is global, the article argues that its removal requires a coalition of nations rather than action by the United States alone.[1]

## Mechanism of the demolition

According to the article, the demolition proceeds by squeezing the remaining seat of cabal power — Europe — where high inflation, a looming recession and rapid interest-rate increases by Trump's Federal Reserve are forcing central banks to seek bailouts.[1] Trump's insistence that [[nato|NATO]] members fund their own defense is presented as removing the American subsidy that propped up European economies and, through them, European central banks.[1] The [[russia-ukraine-war|war in Ukraine]] and the resulting [[sanctions|sanctions]] on Russia are described as a boomerang that damages European economies rather than Russia's, while the emergence of the [[brics|BRICS]] coalition supplies the replacement structure.[1]
