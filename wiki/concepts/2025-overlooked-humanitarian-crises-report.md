# 2025 Overlooked Humanitarian Crises Report

The 2025 Overlooked Humanitarian Crises Report was a study released in June 2025 by CEOWORLD magazine in partnership with the COSMOS Analytica Institute, identifying the world's ten most neglected humanitarian emergencies.[1]

## Findings

African nations again featured prominently, with the report naming countries such as Burkina Faso and the Democratic Republic of the Congo as facing significant challenges related to violence and displacement while receiving limited attention from international donors.[1] The study attributed the lack of assistance for millions enduring displacement, food insecurity or inadequate shelter to donor fatigue, insufficient media coverage and an absence of political resolution.[1] It categorised countries in crisis as those hosting or having more than 200,000 displaced individuals alongside a severe humanitarian emergency.[1]

The report described the world as witnessing unprecedented levels of displacement, driven by a combination of conflict, climate-related events, and political and economic instability.[1] It emphasised that many protracted crises are also neglected ones, with decreasing financial support, limited media attention and waning political commitment both heightening humanitarian needs and prolonging the emergencies year after year.[1]
