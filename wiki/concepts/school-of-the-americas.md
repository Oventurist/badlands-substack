# School of the Americas

The School of the Americas is the United States military training institution that Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] identifies as the mechanism through which the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and the [[shadow-government|shadow government]] installed and sustained dictatorships across Latin America.[1]

## Role in the Badlands account

In his earlier article [[school-of-assassins-and-dictators|School of Assassins and Dictators]], Lange described how [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and the CIA installed dictators throughout Central and South America, training those countries' militaries at the School of the Americas "to terrorize and murder their own people so they could maintain control of the governments."[1] The school is thus framed not as a conventional military academy but as the training arm of a system of control — the means by which a centralized armed force in each country could be turned against its own population on behalf of outside interests.[1]

## The Costa Rican exception

Lange returns to the subject in "Illusions of Democracy" to highlight the one country that did not follow the pattern. Unlike the other Central American states the CIA controlled, [[costa-rica|Costa Rica]] was not ruled by a dictator or brutalized by its own government, and remains among the happiest nations on earth.[1] His explanation is that [[jose-figueres-ferrer|José "Pepe" Figueres Ferrer]] disbanded the Costa Rican armed forces in 1949 and later saw that abolition written into the constitution.[1] Removing the centralized force removed the instrument the cabal would have used to take control away from the people — which Lange argues is "a big reason the CIA shadow government has not been able to install a dictator there."[1]

Figueres himself, per a [[new-york-times|New York Times]] editorial quoted in the article, bitterly opposed the Somoza regime in Nicaragua, broke with Fidel Castro when the Cuban revolution lurched leftward, and supported [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]]'s Alliance for Progress but not the CIA's clandestine wars against Cuba.[1] Lange reads that opposition as informed by firsthand observation of how much the CIA devastated the countries of Central America.[1]
