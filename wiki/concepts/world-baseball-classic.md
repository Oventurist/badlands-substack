# World Baseball Classic

The World Baseball Classic (WBC) is an international baseball tournament contested by national teams.

The March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] carried a bonus item on the 2026 final, in which [[venezuela|Venezuela]] defeated Team USA 3-2 on Tuesday night at LoanDepot Park in Miami to claim the country's first WBC title. The game was played before a heavily pro-Venezuela crowd energized by the large Venezuelan immigrant community in South Florida.

## The 2026 final

Venezuela took a 2-0 lead in the third inning on a sacrifice fly by Maikel Garcia that scored Salvador Pérez, followed immediately by a solo home run from Wilyer Abreu. Team USA tied the game in the eighth when Bobby Witt Jr. drew a leadoff walk and Bryce Harper hit a two-run homer. In the ninth, Luis Arráez walked, Javier Sanoja stole second base as a pinch runner, and Eugenio Suárez delivered the go-ahead RBI double. Closer Daniel Palencia retired the side in order, striking out Roman Anthony with a 99.7 mph fastball to end the game.

Venezuela had advanced after a pool-stage loss to the Dominican Republic by beating Japan and Italy. For Team USA the result marked a third consecutive appearance in the WBC final and a second straight loss, following defeat to Japan in 2023. Venezuela manager Omar López praised his team's unity amid the country's political tensions, saying the squad played "together with no color, political colors or ideology."
