# State Department Report on Cuban Support for Radical Left-Wing Activism

The [[state-department|State Department]] said in July 2026 that it was preparing to release a report alleging that [[cuba|Cuba]]'s communist government has spent decades supporting and cultivating radical left-wing movements in the United States and across the Western Hemisphere.[1] According to excerpts previewed ahead of its release, the report argues that the Cuban regime has provided ideological, organizational, and political support to networks that have influenced activist movements beyond Cuba's borders.[1]

The report is expected to examine organizations including [[codepink|Code Pink]] and to discuss financial backing from businessman [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]] for several left-wing groups, including Code Pink and the [[peoples-forum|People's Forum]].[1] It alleges these organizations have promoted positions aligned with the Cuban government and other authoritarian regimes opposed to US interests.[1]

The document arrived as Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] made countering transnational left-wing political influence a priority of US foreign policy.[1] The administration says the report is intended to highlight Cuba's historical role in supporting revolutionary movements throughout the Americas and to inform future US policy toward the Cuban regime.[1]
