# 2019 Trump–Kim Meeting at the DMZ

In June 2019, [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] met North Korean leader [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] at the Korean Demilitarized Zone, becoming the first sitting United States president to step into the DMZ.[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor AbsoluteTruth1776 described the meeting as a move that shocked many observers even though Trump had signalled it in advance, and characterized it as a demonstration of his unconventional approach to foreign policy.[1] The article argued that the encounter began a process of securing peace with [[north-korea|North Korea]] and induced Kim Jong Un to step back from his nuclear rhetoric.[1] The episode is cited among twenty government-related accomplishments used to rebut claims that Trump achieved little in office.[1]
