# Arctic National Wildlife Refuge

The Arctic National Wildlife Refuge (ANWR) is a protected area of northern Alaska that has been a decades-long flashpoint in United States energy politics, containing oil and gas acreage opened to leasing under the Trump administration and subsequently withdrawn under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## 2023 lease cancellation

In September 2023 the Biden administration confirmed it would cancel seven oil and gas leases in ANWR that had been legally awarded from a 2021 lease sale.[1] Interior Secretary [[deb-haaland|Deb Haaland]] said the department's analysis found the lease sale "seriously flawed and based on a number of fundamental legal deficiencies," and that Biden had directed the review on the first day of his administration.[1] Biden said in a written statement that the move would "help preserve our Arctic lands and wildlife, while honoring the culture, history, and enduring wisdom of Alaska Natives who have lived on these lands since time immemorial," and cited a "climate crisis" warming the Arctic more than twice as fast as the rest of the world.[1] A companion rule proposed protections for 13 million acres elsewhere in Alaska.[1]

## Criticism

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] characterized the cancellation as "illegal" and "reckless," placing it alongside the [[keystone-pipeline|Keystone XL]] cancellation and the drawdown of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve — part of which was sold to a Chinese energy firm linked to [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] — as evidence of policies that weaken the United States while geopolitical adversaries build energy independence.[1] The framing was summarized in the widely shared question "Who is Biden working for?"[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted the doomer tinge of such coverage and argued the same facts admit a Devolution-adjacent reading in which Biden is being used to execute a public collapse of Deep State legitimacy.[1]
