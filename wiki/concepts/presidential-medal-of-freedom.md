# Presidential Medal of Freedom

The Presidential Medal of Freedom is the highest civilian honor of the United States, awarded at the discretion of the president.[1]

## 2025 award to Rudy Giuliani

On Monday, September 1, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced on Truth Social that he would award the medal to [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]], describing him as "the greatest Mayor in the history of New York City, and an equally great American Patriot" and calling the medal "our Country's highest civilian honor."[1] Trump said details of a date and location for the ceremony would come later.[1] The announcement came the same day Giuliani was released from hospital after being injured in a highway crash in New Hampshire.[1]

Badlands commentator Ashe in America endorsed the award, arguing that Giuliani's disbarments in New York and Washington, D.C. in 2024 were punishment for challenging election results and that his conduct as mayor during the September 11 attacks merited the honor.[1]

## January 2025 White House ceremony

On Saturday, January 4, 2025, President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] awarded the nation's highest civilian honor to more than a dozen politicians, philanthropists, and cultural icons at a White House ceremony.[2] Nineteen individuals received the medal, including former Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and billionaire donor [[george-soros|George Soros]]; four were honored posthumously.[2] In his remarks Biden called the recipients "a collection of people, with different backgrounds and beliefs and talents, different generations and different agendas, using their remarkable gifts and unwavering passion to strengthen our resolve as one nation under god indivisible, liberty and justice for all."[2]

Badlands reaction was dismissive: [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] responded to the Clinton and Soros awards with a one-word "Kek," while [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] said she did not know whether all of the recipients were guilty of crimes against humanity or only some, adding that if all, "it's sad about Denzel."[2]
