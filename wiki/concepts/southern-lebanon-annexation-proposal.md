# Southern Lebanon Annexation Proposal

The southern Lebanon annexation proposal refers to a March 2026 call by Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] for [[israel|Israel]] to annex parts of southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]], holding the territory permanently as a security measure amid ongoing fighting with [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1]

## The proposal

Smotrich's remarks came as cross-border violence intensified, though the Israeli government had not formally adopted annexation as policy. The proposal was expected to draw international opposition, as annexation of territory by force is widely considered illegal under international law, and to risk further escalating an already volatile regional conflict.[1]

In practical terms the proposal would extend Israel's northern border to the [[litani-river|Litani River]], bringing the intervening territory within a [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]].[1]

## Military context

The [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] bombed seven bridges crossing the Litani River, cutting off access from Beirut and central Lebanon — a step consistent with severing the proposed annexation zone from the rest of the country.[1]
