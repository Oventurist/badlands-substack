# Trump Comms

"Trump comms" is the interpretive practice, described by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in his July 17, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] article *A Virus, or a Metaphor?*, of reading [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public statements as deliberately layered messages carrying more than one meaning at a time.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## The multiple-audience model

Carlson observes that most people in MAGA agree on three things about Trump: that he is a very smart man, that his messages can be extremely confusing, and that he probably loves his country more than any other politician alive.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Some in that audience further understand that many of the things he says carry double meanings — one meaning for one audience, a second for another, and a third for yet another.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He describes four audiences: one that is completely asleep and fully brainwashed; one that is half-awake, aware something is wrong with society but easily manipulated, discouraged or pulled back into conditioning; one that is fully awake, aware of the manipulation and rarely caught by the traps and rabbit holes dug to distract the motivated from the truth; and, many times, a fourth audience consisting of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## The critics' reading

Against this stands a body of former or reluctant supporters who find Trump exhausting and would prefer a less controversial candidate who is not attacked by the Deep State around the clock.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) This group does not approve of the Left but believes a better option must exist; it holds that Trump cannot control his tongue, that he constantly puts his foot in his mouth, and that it agrees with his policies but not with him as a communicator.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Others withdrew support because they believe he pushed the "jab" on Americans, because so many people in his inner circle appear to have turned on him, because they think his ego too large, or because they believe he is ultimately controlled by the same people who control Liberals, RINOs and Neocons.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

Carlson answers that these critics never explain why Trump is attacked so relentlessly if he is himself a member of the Deep State, or why he and his family would endure such abuse if he were not genuinely trying to take down the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) Trump, he notes, is a billionaire married to a supermodel with a well-adjusted family who adores him, and has no need to spend the twilight years of his life being abused from every angle.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) His net worth declined after becoming president; the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|SEC]] took the unprecedented step of holding up the DWAC–[[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media]] merger, blocking capital from reaching [[truth-social|Truth Social]]; and the entire mainstream media demonizes him.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) By Carlson's reckoning, if Trump were a corrupt member of the Deep State he does not appear to benefit from that membership in any way whatsoever, and claims that he is controlled by the banks, Wall Street or Israel come with literally no actual evidence.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)

## Decoding as a discipline

Carlson argues the practice cuts both ways: Trump either always speaks with meaning and strategy or he does not, and "he doesn't vacillate between being a genius and mentally challenged. He's always either one or the other."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He proposes going back through Trump's past speeches and posts substituting "Communism" for "virus" as a decoding exercise, and notes that hidden meanings are easiest to spot when a message is conspicuously random or strange, and hardest to find in statements that appear perfectly straightforward.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor) He also suggests Trump is humble enough to occasionally play "the fool" in order to advance the cause of taking America back from its captors.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-virus-or-a-metaphor)
