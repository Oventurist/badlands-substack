# 2023 Arlington, Virginia house explosion

The **2023 Arlington, Virginia house explosion** occurred just before 8:30 p.m. on Monday, December 4, 2023, when a home in Arlington County leveled itself in a blast felt for miles as police attempted to execute a search warrant.[1]

## Sequence of events

Police responded to the house at about 4:45 p.m. after receiving reports of shots fired.[1] Arlington County Police Chief Andy Penn said the homeowner, [[james-yoo|James Yoo]], 56, had fired a "flare-type gun" from inside the house into the neighborhood more than 30 times.[1] After attempts to communicate with Yoo were largely unsuccessful, officers obtained a search warrant; as they breached the door, multiple gunshots were fired from within the home.[1] The house then exploded, shooting flames and debris into the air.[1] Officials said Tuesday that Yoo was believed to have died in the blast.[1]

## Aftermath

Fire officials said an investigation into the cause of the explosion was ongoing, and police asked members of the public with photos or video of the area to share them with investigators.[1] Reporting also surfaced numerous grievances Yoo had aired against neighbors and others on social media and in lawsuits.[1]
