# The Blitz

**The Blitz** (also styled *Badlands Blitz*) is a weekly program produced by the Badlands Substack team. It is intended to serve as a weekly recap and dissection of the work published Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].[1]

## Format

Each episode is numbered sequentially; the sixth episode was published on February 14, 2026, under the title "The Info War Ouroboros".[1] Episodes take the form of a conversation between the host and a guest, ranging freely over the week's reporting rather than following a rigid segment structure. The host of Episode 6 noted that the discussion was derailed "from the very start", with most of the episode devoted to extended metaphors — "Sand Bunkers, Squared Circles and Ouroborosian Rugs" — applied to subjects ranging from the ongoing Epstein saga to the [[election-reform-campaign|Election Reform Campaign]].[1] The host remarked that the resulting discussion was strong enough that "it almost feels like we planned it that way."[1]

Episode 6 featured [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as guest.[1] The episode recorded roughly 200 likes on Substack.[1]

## Episode 5 (February 2026)

Episode 5, subtitled "The END of the START ...", was published February 7, 2026 and featured [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as guest.[2] The hosts described the episode as wading through the noise to find "Sovereign Signal galore," and organized it around two questions: "From Iranian Nuclear Rugs to the END of the [[new-start-treaty|New START]] treaty, when is escalation actually de-escalation?" and whether the right lessons were being taken from the proliferation and amplification of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein Narrative]].[2] The post recorded 290 likes.[2]

## Episode 7 (February 2026)

Episode 7, subtitled "Donald Trump, Deep State Burn Cards & the Return of Honor Culture," was published February 21, 2026 and featured [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as guest.[4] The host again described the programme's remit as hitting "the major Narrative and Actual Deployments of the week."[4] The episode opened with the expansion and subversion of the Epstein Web, which the hosts said led to the [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] inflection point, before turning to what they called [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s "Fake, but kind of Real" [[alien-disclosure-op|Alien Disclosure Op]].[4] It closed on the anticipated convergence between the United States and [[iran|Iran]], which the hosts assessed as "a whole lot of smoke, mirrors and a few juicy Actuals" signalling a major realignment along [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] terms.[4] The post recorded 252 likes.[4]

## Episode 4 (January 2026)

Episode 4, subtitled "Color Revolutions Come Home to Roost," was published January 31, 2026 and featured [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as guest.[3] The host framed the week as "absolutely full of System Sway," arguing that virtually every topic covered — the expansion and acceleration of the Minnesota [[color-revolution|Color Revolution]], the Fulton County Election Raid, and the ongoing pattern of Canadian Capitulation — converged to illuminate the same Macro System and its inevitable dissolution.[3] As is customary for the program, the hosts also dipped into the comments section to review what subscribers had been saying during the week.[3] The post recorded 279 likes.[3]

The host reiterated a standing production goal that the Blitz get close to a 60-minute runtime, noting that the two preceding episodes had nearly hit that mark.[3] He also described the program as part of a deliberate effort to serve the Badlands Substack audience with exclusive content in 2026, on the grounds that Substack subscribers are among the outlet's most loyal and engaged.[3]

## Episode 8 (February 2026)

Episode 8, subtitled "On the Reclaiming of American Pride & the Combating of Anti-MAGA FUD," was published February 28, 2026 and was co-hosted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[5] The post again described the programme as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."[5] The two hosts were said to be holding down the fort while [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] — described in mock-heroic terms as their "liege lord, best friend, mentor and overall inspiration," as well as the show's final editorial overseer — worked to get his home back in order after a historic blizzard.[5]

At the time of publication Episode 8 was the shortest instalment of The Blitz yet recorded, which the post noted made it the most in keeping with the programme's original intent; it insisted this was "pure coincidence" and no indictment of the absent Burning Bright, adding tongue-in-cheek that "all of you are very upset about it."[5] The post recorded 195 likes.[5]

## Episode 3 (January 2026)

Episode 3, subtitled "Donald Trump's Global Victory Tour," was published January 24, 2026 and featured [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as guest.[6] The host introduced it as "the THIRD episode of The Blitz," restating the programme's remit as a weekly recap and dissection of the work done Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]].[6] The pair characterised the week under review as "absolutely full of Sovereign Alliance proofs, Trumpian narrative deployments and Central Banker capitulation," tying the week's reporting to the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework and to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s information operations.[6] As is customary, the hosts also dipped into the comments section to review what subscribers had been saying during the week.[6] The host noted that the show is intended to get close to a 60-minute runtime and that this instalment "nearly hit the mark."[6] The post recorded 348 likes.[6]

## Episode 9 (March 2026)

Episode 9, subtitled "The New World Order is Dying … The New World is Beginning," was published March 7, 2026 and paired a returning [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] with [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[9] The post opened with Burning Bright announcing his return "at the turn of the tide," joking that he was rescuing the audience "mostly from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]," who had stood in during his absence the previous week.[9] Set against a week in which the world seemed to be on the brink of both kinetic and financial catastrophe, the two hosts deliberately took a "40k-foot view" in order to reconcile the seeming contradictions at the heart of the transition between the [[new-world-order|New World Order]] and the New World.[9] The post recorded 237 likes and repeated the programme's standing funding note and sign-off.[9]

## Episode 11 (March 2026)

Episode 11, subtitled "Iran, NATO and the Invisible Enemy," was published March 21, 2026 and paired [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] with [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[7] The post reiterated the programme's standing remit as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."[7] The two hosts used what the post called the spiraling narrative of the Iran War to map a strategic pincer said to be in the offing by the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] against the true [[invisible-enemy|Invisible Enemy]].[7] The episode also took up [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the dangers of playing "white hat-black hat games" with the characters who orbit him on the [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation]] game board.[7] The post recorded 233 likes.[7]

## Episode 25 (July 2026)

Episode 25, subtitled "It's Treason, Then ...", was published July 18, 2026 and paired [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].[8] The post again described the programme as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]."[8] Departing from the usual survey of the week's items, the two hosts spent the whole hour "and change" on a single subject: [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election fraud speech delivered on the Thursday night preceding publication, which the post characterised as "seismic" and insisted "actually was seismic."[8] The discussion covered the speech's implications for the [[deep-state|Deep State]] cabal and the hosts' contention that it represented the beginnings of a true phase shift in both the [[shadow-war|Shadow War]] and the [[information-war|Information War]] at once.[8] The post recorded 223 likes.[8]

The episode's framing tied Trump's public airing of [[2020-election-fraud-claims|election fraud claims]] to the programme's standing thesis that narrative deployments and actual operations move in tandem, with the speech read as a marker that the two theatres had begun converging.[8] The post retained the programme's customary funding appeal and sign-off.[8]

## Funding model

All Badlands content, including The Blitz, is distributed free of charge.[1][3] The team states that the only means by which it can pay contributors for their time is voluntary reader support, and episodes close with an invitation to upgrade to a paid subscription to the newsletter, cancellable at any time.[1][3] The publishers describe a "step-up" in the volume and quality of work for 2026 as part of the case for paid support.[1][3]

Episode 8 restated the same model verbatim in substance: all Badlands content will continue to be free, The Blitz included, and the only way the team can pay its people for their time is through the generous support of readers who choose to provide it.[5]

## Sign-off

Episodes and their accompanying posts close with the outlet's recurring exhortation to readers: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"[1][3] Posts also open with the salutation "Badlanders!"[3]

## Episode 13

After a short hiatus, Badlands Blitz returned with episode 13, in which [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] attempted to make sense of the "on-again, off-again, real, but also fake war" between the United States and [[iran|Iran]].[10] Burning Bright's line from that recording — "Donald Trump and Iran know how this story ends" — was quoted in the April 20, 2026 Brief, having been recorded before the [[mv-touska-seizure|M/V Touska seizure]] entered the info-war battlespace.[10]

## Scheduled breaks

The programme occasionally skips a week. The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] of 9 April 2026 confirmed, as announced the week before, that there would be no Badlands Blitz that weekend, coinciding with [[gart|GART]] 11 in Nashville.[11] Readers were directed to the outlet's Substack podcast playlist to catch up on earlier episodes, and to the GART Virtual Pass as the alternative "Badlands fix" in the interim.[11] A new episode of The Blitz was promised for the following Saturday.[11]

## Episode 19
Episode 19 featured [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] stepping back from direct analysis of events in [[iran|Iran]] to examine the grand strategy at play between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his "Frenemies."[12]


## Announcement (January 2026)

The programme was announced publicly on January 1, 2026, in a New Year's Day post that billed it as "Badlands Blitz, week-in-review news show EXCLUSIVELY here on Substack."[13] The announcement named [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] as host, with [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] alternating as co-host each week.[13] It described the format as a breakdown of the top stories from The Brief, augmented with bonus takes from the Brief writers and with selected reader comments and threads from the week featured on air.[13] The post added that the team was "excited" and that readers should be too, and noted that America would turn 250 during the year.[13]
