# Judengasse

The **Judengasse**, or "Jew lane," was the Jewish quarter of Frankfurt and, in Badlands Media's account of the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]], the birthplace and ancestral home of the dynasty.[1]

Ryan DeLarme's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 6" records that [[mayer-amschel-rothschild|Mayer Amschel Bauer]] was born there on February 23, 1744, at a time when Frankfurt is described as a hotbed of anti-Semitism stemming from the widely publicized philosophies of Immanuel Kant and Johann Fichte.[1]

## Origin of the Rothschild name

The quarter also supplied the family's name. In those days houses in the Judengasse were designated not by numbers but by signs displaying different symbols or colors, and the house the Bauer family occupied for many generations bore a red shield.[1] The German *zum rothen Schild* — "at the red shield," in the older spelling with "th" — became Rothschild when Mayer changed the family name.[1]

In 1785 the Rothschilds shared quarters in the district with a family named Schiff, whose descendant [[jacob-schiff|Jacob Schiff]] would emigrate to America in 1865 and rise to lead [[kuhn-loeb-and-co|Kuhn, Loeb & Company]].[1]
