# 2024 United States Presidential Election

The 2024 United States presidential election was the November 2024 contest in which [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] defeated Democratic nominee [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], returning Trump to the presidency for a second, non-consecutive term.

## Trump's account of the result

In his May 2026 [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement announcing the [[election-integrity-army|Election Integrity Army]] for the 2026 midterms, Trump described the 2024 election as his "Historic Election," in which he "won every single Swing State, and decisively won both the Electoral and Popular votes by wide margins." He credited the result in part to Republican election-monitoring operations: "the Republicans had an Election Integrity Army in every single State to preserve the sanctity of each legal vote."

## The Politico "stolen election" poll

A Politico poll highlighted in the May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief found widespread bipartisan distrust in the American election system, with only about 39% of respondents saying without reservation that the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 election]] was not stolen, and roughly 40% saying the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]] were unlikely to be stolen. Badlands commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that after the 2024 election, when "the blueanons went all 'stolen election'," Badlands was supportive of "getting to the bottom of all their allegations," reasoning that "inquiry into elections is always a good thing because elections are fake."
