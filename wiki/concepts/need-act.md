# NEED Act (HR 2990)

The **National Emergency Employment Defense Act**, known as the **N.E.E.D. Act** and introduced as HR 2990 by Representative Dennis Kucinich, was a monetary reform bill calling for a debt-free government money unit.[1] The legislation was penned by Stephen Zarlenga of the American Monetary Institute.[1]

Alongside its debt-free currency provision, the bill sought to provide credit through [[full-reserve-banking|full reserve banking]], removing the ability of commercial banks to create new money against deposits as they do under [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]].[1] Badlands Media lists the act among the practical reform proposals available to critics of the present system.[1]
