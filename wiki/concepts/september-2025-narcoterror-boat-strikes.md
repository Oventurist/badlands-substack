# September 2025 Narcoterror Boat Strikes

In September 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced additional lethal strikes against vessels accused of narcotrafficking in the [[southcom|USSOUTHCOM]] area of responsibility.[1]

In a statement, Trump said that on his orders the Secretary of War had directed "a lethal kinetic strike on a vessel affiliated with a Designated Terrorist Organization conducting narcotrafficking."[1] He stated that intelligence had confirmed the vessel was carrying illicit narcotics and was transiting a known narcotrafficking passage "enroute to poison Americans," that the strike killed three male narcoterrorists aboard, that the vessel was in international waters, and that no U.S. forces were harmed.[1] The statement closed with a warning in capital letters: "STOP SELLING FENTANYL, NARCOTICS, AND ILLEGAL DRUGS IN AMERICA, AND COMMITTING VIOLENCE AND TERRORISM AGAINST AMERICANS!!!"[1]
