# Morges Manifesto

The Morges Manifesto was published in 1961 and, according to Badlands Media, served as a constitution for the new ecology movement.[1] It was written by Sir [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]], and the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] was launched that same year by oligarchs including [[prince-philip|Prince Philip]] and [[prince-bernhard-of-the-netherlands|Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands]].[1]

The article contrasts the manifesto with the technological optimism of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]] and [[henry-c-carey|Henry C. Carey]].[1] Where those figures located the evil misshaping humanity in the imperialist system of exploitation, the hereditary elite behind the Morges Manifesto sought to persuade the world that technological progress itself was the intrinsic enemy of nature.[1] Badlands Media alleges that these oligarchical ecologists went on to oversee the murder of anti-Malthusian leaders during the 1960s while establishing new institutions of control such as the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg group]] and its junior partner founded at Davos in 1971 — the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1] Such organizations, the article says, became the controlling nodes of the cybernetic and [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] management of humanity over the following seventy years.[1]

The manifesto is also cited as one pole of the [[double-bind-theory|double bind]]: the belief that nature must be saved from humanity by shutting down industrial civilization from above, the mirror image of the [[ted-kaczynski|Unabomber's]] war on it from below.[1]
