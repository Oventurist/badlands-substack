# Desaparecidos

*Desaparecidos* — "the disappeared" — is the term used in the Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1" for people in [[cuba|Cuba]] who go missing as a result of political suppression.[1] A young Cuban-American woman who shared her experience of living in Cuba on TikTok said that anyone who dares to speak ill of the Communist Party risks becoming a *desaparecido*.[1]

## Documentation efforts

The essay reports that documents circulating on the internet list the names and details of the growing number of *desaparecidos*, keeping the data centralized and alive.[1] Continuing to share this material, DeLarme argues, keeps the reality fresh in the minds of those who care and demonstrates how internet access has shaken things up in Cuba — a key reason the state imposed a communications blackout during the [[2021-cuban-protests|2021 protests]].[1]
