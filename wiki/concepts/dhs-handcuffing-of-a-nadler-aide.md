# DHS Handcuffing of a Nadler Aide

The DHS handcuffing of a Nadler aide was an incident in late May 2025 in which [[department-of-homeland-security|U.S. Department of Homeland Security]] police handcuffed a staff member in the Manhattan district office of Representative [[jerrold-nadler|Jerry Nadler]].[1]

## The incident

The confrontation, captured on video and shared with the outlet Gothamist, was filmed by a person monitoring activity in immigration court; Nadler's district office sits in the same federal building as an immigration courthouse.[1] In the footage, DHS officers enter the office and accuse staff members of "harboring rioters," a Nadler staffer is seen crying and being handcuffed, and another officer attempts to enter a private area of the office while a staffer demands a warrant.[1]

DHS later said in a statement that "one individual" — the woman seen being handcuffed — had blocked police from performing a security check they intended to conduct on the basis of information that protesters were in the lawmaker's office.[1]

## Aftermath

Later the same day, protesters gathered outside the federal building to demonstrate against U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.[1]
