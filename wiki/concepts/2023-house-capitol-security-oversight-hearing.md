# 2023 House Capitol Security Oversight Hearing

The 2023 House Capitol security oversight hearing was a House subcommittee session held on Tuesday, September 18, 2023, formally titled "Oversight of United States Capitol Security: Assessing Security Failures on January 6, 2021."[1] Its central witness was former [[us-capitol-police|United States Capitol Police]] Chief [[steven-sund|Steven A. Sund]].[1]

## Proceedings

The hearing began nearly an hour late because the House was voting on defense spending, and it ran short.[1] Sund presented a condensed version of the account he had earlier given at greater length to [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], including his contention that [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] and Democratic leadership were culpable for the failure to secure the Capitol and that he had been forced out as chief to serve as a scapegoat.[1]

California Representative Norma Torres was described by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as the hearing's principal source of gaslighting, for asserting that the crowd was armed, that it intended to "hang Mike Pence," and that "if they didn't have a weapon, they found one."[1]

## Media reaction

[[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] argued that not a single mainstream outlet covered the hearing, contrasting this with the primetime network saturation given to the [[january-6-committee|January 6th select committee]], and claiming that apart from C-SPAN's routine coverage of congressional proceedings it was the only outlet reporting on the session.[1]

## Assessment

Badlands commentary concluded that the hearing "didn't really move any needles," but that it spotlighted two structural reform arguments: ending qualified immunity, and requiring legislators to speak under oath in public hearings, since as matters stand members face no legal consequence for making false statements to the American people from the dais.[1] The hearing was also cited as context for the timing of the disorderly-conduct charge filed against [[ray-epps|Ray Epps]] the following day, which Badlands writers characterized as an afterthought intended to distract from the hearing's exposure of the [[fedsurrection|FedSurrection]].[1]
