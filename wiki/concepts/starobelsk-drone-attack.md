# Starobelsk Drone Attack

The Starobelsk drone attack was a strike on a college dormitory in Starobelsk, a town in the Lugansk People's Republic within Russian-controlled territory, on the night of May 22, 2026. According to the account carried in the June 3, 2026 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] Brief, a Ukrainian drone hit the dormitory late at night while students were asleep, killing 21 people — most of them teenage girls — and injuring roughly 70 others.

## Russian response

Russia answered with a large-scale overnight missile and drone campaign employing precision weapons, including hypersonic missiles. The strikes targeted Ukrainian military airfields, defense industry facilities, fuel and transport infrastructure, and a drone production plant linked to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], across the Kyiv, Zaporozhye, Kherson, Dnipropetrovsk, Poltava, Khmelnytskyi, and Sumy regions. Officials of the Russian Defense Ministry stated that every designated target was hit and described the operation as a response to "terrorist attacks" by Kiev. President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] characterized the dormitory strike as a new chapter in Ukraine's "crime spree" that would bring "well-deserved and inevitable punishment."

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted reporting that the targeted drone plant had originally been a drone film-scouting company connected to associates of Zelensky before becoming Ukraine's principal producer of long-range drones, and grouped the episode with the drone strike on the [[zaporozhye-nuclear-power-plant|Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant]] and a drone incident in Romania as evidence of escalating recklessness and false-flag activity by Ukrainian leadership.
