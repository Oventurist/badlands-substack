# PURSUE (Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters)

**PURSUE**, the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters, is the U.S. government portal created in 2026 to release records on unidentified anomalous phenomena.[1]

## Launch

The [[department-of-war|Department of War]] announced on May 8, 2026 the initial release of new UAP files through PURSUE, saying the effort includes the White House, the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|ODNI]], the Department of Energy, the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, [[nasa|NASA]], the [[fbi|FBI]] and other intelligence components.[1] The department said the records would be housed at WAR.GOV/UFO with additional files released on a rolling basis; the portal lists Release 01 as cleared for release May 8, 2026.[1]

The department said the files were reviewed for security purposes, while many materials had not yet been analyzed for resolution of anomalies.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] reported the launch the same day, and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] responded to the release with "Have fun."[1]

## Reception

[[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] began a running thread on X cataloguing interesting data points contained in the administration's UFO file releases, noting that only war.gov/ufo was live while aliens.gov had not yet come up, and pledging to update as more files were uploaded.[1] The rollout fed the broader Badlands discussion of an [[alien-disclosure-op|alien disclosure operation]].[1]
