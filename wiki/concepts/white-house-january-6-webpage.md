# White House January 6 Webpage

The White House January 6 webpage is an official United States government web page published by the [[white-house|White House]] in January 2026 setting out the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's account of the events of January 6, 2021.[1]

## Publication

The page was released ahead of the fifth anniversary of the breach of the US Capitol and presents the administration's account of the day while disputing the "insurrection" conclusions reached by prior congressional investigations and court proceedings.[1]

## Content

The page states that the 2020 presidential election was stolen and criticises the actions of former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] relating to the certification of the 2020 election results.[1] It challenges the findings of the House [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]], references prosecutions arising from the Capitol breach, and emphasises that the majority of Americans present on January 6, 2021 were peaceful protesters.[1] The White House did not indicate whether additional revisions or related pages would be added.[1]
