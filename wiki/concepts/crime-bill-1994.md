# Crime Bill of 1994

The Crime Bill of 1994 is described by Erik Carlson as legislation devised by President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and Senator [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] that increased sentences for non-violent criminals — in his phrasing, drug dealers.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Context claimed

Carlson places the bill within the economics of [[prison-privatization|prison privatization]]: private companies had begun running American prisons for profit just as crime rates were falling, so those who benefited from full prisons needed the trend to reverse.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The bill supplied longer sentences; the [[cia|CIA]]'s alleged importation of drugs from South and Central America supplied the offenders.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

By the mid-1990s, Carlson writes, the stage had been set to fill the private prisons, with kickbacks flowing to the politicians who helped pass the new crime bills.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The same period, in his telling, saw the manufactured racial polarization of the [[la-riots-1992|1992 Los Angeles riots]], the [[oj-simpson|O. J. Simpson]] trial, and the promotion of [[gangster-rap|gangster rap]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
