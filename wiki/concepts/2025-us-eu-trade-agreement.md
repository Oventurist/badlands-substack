# 2025 US–EU Trade Agreement

The 2025 US–EU trade agreement was finalized on Sunday, July 27, 2025, during a meeting between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and European Commission President [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]].[1]

## Terms

The deal imposes a 15% tariff on most [[european-union|European Union]] exports to the United States, with no reciprocal tariffs imposed on US goods.[1] In addition, Brussels pledged to invest $600 billion in the US economy and to purchase $750 billion worth of American energy over three years.[1] Commentary in the brief noted reported restrictions that would prevent Europe from developing its own energy sector in the immediate term.[1]

## Reaction

White House official [[sebastian-gorka|Sebastian Gorka]], senior director for counterterrorism at the National Security Council, said the EU had "bent at the knee" before Trump and called the agreement a geopolitical victory for Washington.[1] Former Russian President [[dmitry-medvedev|Dmitry Medvedev]] remarked that "President Trump wiped the floor with the EU."[1] The French prime minister described the deal as "submission," while von der Leyen said "it was the best we could get," framing it as a means of staving off the 30% tariff Trump had threatened for months.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued the agreement would be remembered in retrospect as the moment continental Europe became an economic vassal of the United States.[1]
