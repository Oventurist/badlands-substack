# Cash Assistance Program for Immigrants (CAPI)

The Cash Assistance Program for Immigrants (CAPI) is a California state benefit program. According to the program's own website, CAPI is intended to provide monthly cash benefits to aged, blind, and disabled non-citizens who are ineligible for federal Supplemental Security Income.[1] The White House has claimed, by contrast, that the program provides benefits to illegal immigrants who cannot access Social Security benefits.[1]

## 2025 federal subpoenas

On Monday, May 12, 2025, the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] issued subpoenas to the government of California seeking records related to alleged disbursements of federal funds to illegal immigrants, targeting CAPI.[1] The subpoenas were filed by the Los Angeles office of Homeland Security Investigations (HSI), the investigative arm of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]].[1]

DHS Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] criticized California officials in a statement, saying that "radical left politicians in California prioritize illegal aliens over our own citizens, including by giving illegal aliens access to cash benefits," and that the administration was working to identify abuse and exploitation of public benefits so that people in the country illegally would not receive federal benefits or other financial incentives to stay.[1] Noem added: "If you are an illegal immigrant, you should leave now. The gravy train is over," noting that while the subpoena focused only on Los Angeles County, "it is just the beginning."[1]
