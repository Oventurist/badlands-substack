# Plan Ávila

**Plan Ávila** is the Venezuelan urban counterinsurgency blueprint originally employed by the state's security forces against the mass unrest of the [[caracazo|Caracazo]] in 1989.[1]

According to Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], the plan was quietly revived by U.S. military advisors during the multipronged Washington response to [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]]'s consolidation of power in the early 2000s, alongside the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]]'s quadrupling of its Venezuela budget.[1] Its reappearance is presented as evidence that the same repertoire of tools — street mobilization, "civil society" funding, and counterinsurgency doctrine — is recycled across decades and regimes in [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]
