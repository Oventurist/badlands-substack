# Operation Condor

Operation Condor is described by Badlands as the South American wing of [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]] — the Western covert network of stay-behind armies, coups and political violence operations conducted during and after the Cold War.[1]

## Badlands treatment

Cynthia Ehret, wife of Badlands contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], appeared on the program Breaking History to share her research into Operation Condor, arguing that the instrument used in the resulting coups was "almost always the Green Berets."[1]

Badlands invoked that research in September 2024 when Venezuelan authorities announced the arrest of six foreigners — including a US Navy SEAL — allegedly involved in a plot to kill President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], and when recalling the 2020 "Bay of Piglets" raid organized by former Green Beret [[jordan-goudreau|Jordan Goudreau]].[1] The publication treated the recurrence of US special-forces personnel in Latin American regime-change episodes as continuity with the Condor pattern rather than coincidence.[1]
