# National Defense Area

The National Defense Area is a restricted military zone established under the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration along the U.S.-Mexico border, forming part of the U.S. Army Garrison Fort Huachuca and marked by "Restricted Area" signs in both English and Spanish.[1]

## Purpose

Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] praised the establishment of the area, emphasizing its role in reducing illegal crossings, and warned during a visit earlier in 2025 that "when you cross illegally, you will be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law."[1]

## Dismissal of charges (May 2025)

In May 2025 a federal judge in New Mexico, Gregory B. Wormuth, dismissed charges against 98 illegal immigrants who had crossed into the restricted zone, ruling that they could not be prosecuted for entering because they were reportedly unaware it was military property.[1] "The United States provides no facts from which one could reasonably conclude that the defendant knew he was entering," Wormuth wrote, insisting that a requirement of intent to enter military land was necessary to avoid "absurd and unfair results."[1] The dismissed cases were among approximately 400 misdemeanor charges filed against migrants who entered the area.[1]
