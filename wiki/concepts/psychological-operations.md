# Psychological Operations (PSYOP)

Psychological operations, commonly abbreviated PSYOP, are operations intended to convey selected information and indicators to audiences in order to influence their motives and objective reasoning, and ultimately the behaviour of governments, organizations, groups and large foreign powers.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] quotes this definition in his essay on the [[smith-mundt-act|Smith-Mundt Act]], noting that the purpose of United States psychological operations is to induce or reinforce behaviour perceived to be favourable to U.S. objectives.[1]

## Doctrine

PSYOP is described as an important part of the range of diplomatic, informational, military and economic activities available to the United States, usable during both peacetime and conflict, and divided into three main types: strategic, operational and tactical.[1]

## Wartime applications

Carlson recounts that the U.S. military operated a psychological operations unit during the Second World War, working alongside the civilian [[office-of-war-information|Office of War Information]].[1] Such a unit could spread misinformation to the enemy to sow confusion; it could deploy inflatable tanks or aircraft to fool opposing forces into believing American firepower was greater than it was, or that an attack was coming from a different direction; it could demoralise enemy militaries and civilian populations; and it could even be used to overthrow governments or officials from within enemy states.[1]

## Postwar restriction

The wartime record convinced U.S. policymakers that psyops and propaganda were highly effective tools that should be used with care — retained for use against foreign enemies or potential enemies, but never turned on the American people.[1] That conclusion produced the [[smith-mundt-act|Smith-Mundt Act]] of 1948.[1] Carlson's larger thesis is that the tool never disappeared domestically: he argues that the [[cia|CIA]] continued to propagandise Americans through [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]], and that in the 2020s psychological operations were used by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his allies against the [[deep-state|Deep State]] — his subtitle for the essay being "Psyops Go Both Ways."[1]
