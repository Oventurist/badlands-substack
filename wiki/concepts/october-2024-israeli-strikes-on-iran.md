# October 2024 Israeli Strikes on Iran

The **October 2024 Israeli strikes on Iran** were a series of retaliatory air attacks conducted by [[israel|Israel]] against Iranian territory on Saturday, October 26, 2024, in response to Iranian missile attacks on Israel.[1]

## The strikes

The Israeli military announced at 2:30 a.m. that it was "conducting precise strikes on military targets in Iran" in response to more than a year of attacks on Israel by [[iran|Iran]] and its allies across the Middle East.[1] Just after 6 a.m., the military said the strikes had concluded.[1] Reporting at the time framed the exchange as raising fears that a long-brewing confrontation between two of the most powerful militaries in the Middle East could escalate into all-out war.[1]

The [[white-house|White House]] expressed support for the operation, calling it targeted and proportionate while stating its belief that it should mark the end of the direct military exchange between the two states.[1] An official who briefed reporters said the United States would again come to Israel's defense if Iran chose to retaliate.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the exchange as "the same old d--k measuring contest to see which side is more insecure," and criticized mainstream coverage for treating Iran as having no right to self-defense.[1] He argued that Iran was being blamed and "victim-shamed" within hours of being bombed, and that any Iranian response would be villainized even though it would mirror Israel's own conduct after October 7, 2023.[1]

He noted that [[brics|BRICS]] and other actors had been signalling that Iran would not respond, and argued that Iranian restraint would amount to winning the narrative war — particularly if [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] chose to pursue further kinetic conflict.[1] In his framing, Iran had so far demonstrated the discipline required for such restraint, something Israel had not.[1] He also highlighted, as an under-reported fact, that a Jewish member of the Iranian Parliament had publicly called for a strong response against the "Zionist regime," asking why Western audiences are never told that Iran has a Jewish lawmaker.[1]
