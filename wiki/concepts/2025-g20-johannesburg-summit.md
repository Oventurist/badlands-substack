# 2025 G20 Johannesburg Summit

The 2025 [[g20|Group of 20]] summit, hosted by [[south-africa|South Africa]] in Johannesburg and opening on November 22, 2025, became notable chiefly for the absence of the leaders of the world's largest economies.[1]

## Leader absences

China's Foreign Ministry announced on Thursday, November 13, 2025 that President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] would not attend and that Premier [[li-qiang|Li Qiang]] would lead Beijing's delegation, giving no reason for Xi's absence.[1] Xi had attended the previous year's G20 but has limited his international travel since the pandemic, appearing mainly at high-level gatherings such as his recent meeting with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in South Korea before the APEC summit.[1]

Xi's withdrawal meant the summit would convene without the leaders of the world's two largest economies, as well as Russia.[1] Trump had said the previous week that no U.S. officials would attend, accusing South Africa of allowing "genocide against White Afrikaners," and Argentine President [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]] was likewise boycotting the Johannesburg meeting.[1] Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] remained unable to travel because of an [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] warrant.[1] The combined absences were reported as another setback for host nation South Africa.[1]

## Carney remarks and the US boycott

Speaking to journalists on the sidelines of the summit, Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] said Washington should not overestimate its role in the international arena and that a wide range of issues can be resolved without US involvement, adding that the economic "center of gravity" is shifting away from America.[2] The United States skipped the meeting, held in Johannesburg, South Africa; President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had accused the country of perpetrating a genocide against white farmers, which he gave as the reason for his absence.[2] Washington also claimed that only a chairman's summary could be released following the summit because the US was not present, but the [[g20|G20]] issued a declaration on Sunday anyway.[2]
