# Israeli Arming of Gaza Anti-Hamas Militias

In June 2025, unnamed Israeli defense officials confirmed to the Times of Israel that the State of Israel, at the direction of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], was arming an [[isis|ISIS]]-aligned jihadist gang in Gaza.[1]

## Disclosure and confirmation

News of the arrangement first came via remarks by opposition [[knesset|Knesset]] member and former defense minister [[avigdor-lieberman|Avigdor Lieberman]] in an interview with Kan Bet public radio, in which he said the Israeli government was giving weapons to "a group of criminals and felons, identified with Islamic State, at the direction of the prime minister," and warned that "Ultimately, these weapons will be turned against us."[1] Netanyahu, while refraining from validating that characterization of the recipients, confirmed that Israel was "activating clans in Gaza that oppose [[hamas|Hamas]]."[1] He was accused of green-lighting the weapons handovers without securing his cabinet's permission.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] paired the story with the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]]'s withdrawal from Mali, framing a symmetry in which Ukraine — and behind it the United States — funds ISIS-affiliated terrorist groups in the Russia-aligned Sahel states of Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger while Netanyahu arms ISIS-aligned groups in Gaza.[1]

He highlighted a 2016 statement by former [[mossad|Mossad]] chief Efraim Halevy, who, asked why Israel was providing medical treatment to an injured Al Qaeda fighter in Syria, replied: "We have a different account with Hezbollah. A totally different account. [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]], to the best of my recollection, has not attacked Israel."[1] Similar statements, he noted, have been made by other Israeli officials regarding ISIS, leading him to suggest that "ISIS and Al Qaeda were created by Mossad and the CIA," to ask who the official narrative holds responsible for 9/11, and to conclude that the implications mark "the end of the establishment GOP, as well as Con Inc."[1]
