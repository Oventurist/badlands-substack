# Iron Dome

The Iron Dome is Israel's short-range air and missile defense system, treated in the Badlands corpus as the emblem of Israeli invulnerability to rocket and missile attack.

In the March 13, 2026 [[badlands-brief-559|Badlands Brief]], the system's performance is presented as a measure of how the war with [[iran|Iran]] is going. Citing [[haaretz|Haaretz]], [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reports that nearly a dozen smart missiles — each carrying dozens of submunitions with independent guidance systems — penetrated the Iron Dome and struck central Israel in a single night, as part of one of several salvos. He concludes that "Israel's defense systems have failed, and they are now vulnerable and taking a beating," a claim he pairs with reports that [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] sought a ceasefire from Iran on Israel's behalf and was refused. The argument advanced is that the penetration of the Iron Dome undercuts Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s ability to escalate toward regime change in Tehran.
