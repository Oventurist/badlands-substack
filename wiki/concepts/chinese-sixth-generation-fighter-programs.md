# Chinese Sixth-Generation Fighter Programs

China's sixth-generation combat aircraft programs came to Western attention at the end of December 2024, when high-resolution renderings and footage of a next-generation tactical fighter-bomber circulated widely.[1]

## December 2024 reveal

On December 26, 2024, footage of the aircraft — expected to replace the Xi'an JH-7 — surfaced on Chinese social media before spreading to X, and its diamond-shaped wing design left military observers across the West stunned.[1] The apparent ability of [[china|China]] to leapfrog current fifth-generation military aviation technology such as the West's F-22 Raptor and F-35 Lightning II prompted Deutsche Bank analyst Scott Deuschle to issue a note titled "A Wake-Up Call for Sixth Gen," characterizing China's rapid advances in sixth-generation combat aircraft as a serious wake-up call for the United States Air Force.[1] The item appeared as a bonus entry in the Badlands News Brief.[1]
