# Bay of Pigs Invasion

The Bay of Pigs invasion was the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s abortive 1961 operation against Cuba, carried out by exiled Cuban mercenaries. The agency's code name for it was OPERATION ZAPATA — a coincidence the Badlands article rejects, since [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Off-Shore Company]] belonged to [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]].[1]

Three ships owned by Zapata and financed by [[texas-national-bank|Texas National Bank]] — the *Houston*, the *Barbara J* and the *Zapata* — were loaned by Bush's firm to the CIA for the invasion.[1] A 1986 centenary book on [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] independently recorded that Zapata Off-Shore was linked to the operation.[1]

The article stresses that mainstream media has never mentioned Bush's connection to the invasion, and asks why the fact was kept from the American people; Bush, it argues, "was an untouchable of the highest order," denying CIA membership before his 1976 directorship while in fact having led clandestine operations since the early 1950s and commanding the Cuban exile force recruited for the invasion and later operations.[1]
