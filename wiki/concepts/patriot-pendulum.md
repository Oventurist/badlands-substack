# Patriot Pendulum

The **Patriot Pendulum** is an analytical concept advanced by the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], introduced in his January 12, 2023 essay "Right the Ship."[1] He defines it as "an offshoot of the [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian Dialectic]] strategy that I believe the Deep State 'Controllers' have wielded against the American people for generations."[1]

## Definition and mechanism

The concept holds that the ideological pole used to move a population is interchangeable, and that the swing between poles is itself the instrument of control.[1] Burning Bright introduced it while rebutting the view that it is sufficient to ignore what the ideological "Left" believes about the U.S. Administrative [[deep-state|Deep State]]; such a reductive viewpoint, he argued, minimizes the prevalence of the Patriot Pendulum.[1]

While conceding that the ideological Left was at that moment inflicting significantly more damage on the collective psyche of the American Mind than its counterpart on the Right, he posited that the opposite could have been argued twenty years earlier.[1] In that earlier period, unthinking adherence to the "Right" side of the aisle — along with its ensuing badges of patriotism, nationalism and "unity" — was weaponized against the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] to whip a majority of the nation into a "bloodthirsty, disconcerting zeal."[1] That zeal, in his account, produced the public mandate the Deep Administrative State needed in order to lie its way into a [[forever-wars|Forever War]] in the Middle East that was bloody and costly to the American public and highly lucrative to its architects.[1]

He held that reversing that war required the beginnings of the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]], the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] and the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]], all signed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Qualifications

Burning Bright was explicit that the concept is not a condemnation of conservative values, which he described as making up "a good bit of the bedrock of American idealism" — a strength at the heart of the nation that should not be easily relinquished.[1] The point of the framing, rather, is to remind readers of the fragility of the Collective Mind when subjected to emotional pushes, and of the forbidding and often-costly momentum of that Collective when it is guided, pushed or aimed in the wrong direction.[1]

He argued that the decentralized "hivemind of citizen intelligence" of the sovereign-minded community grows less likely by the day to be swept up into such costly emotional narratives, but cautioned that even that community remains susceptible to emotional manipulation by all sides on the Game Theory Game Board — including by its own.[1]
