# Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act

The Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act is a United States statute enacted in 2022 that explicitly bans dealings with the [[xinjiang-production-and-construction-corps|Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps]] and other entities tied to human rights abuses in [[xinjiang|Xinjiang]].[1]

## Application to Harvard

Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* invoked the Act, together with U.S. Department of State designations, as the legal basis for characterizing [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s training of XPCC officials through the [[harvard-china-health-partnership|Harvard-China Health Partnership]] as unlawful rather than merely unethical.[1] The article maintained that the allegations against Harvard "are not unfounded accusations" precisely because the statutory and sanctions framework already named the entity involved.[1]
