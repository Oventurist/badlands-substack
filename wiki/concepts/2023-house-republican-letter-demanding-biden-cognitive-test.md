# 2023 House Republican Letter Demanding Biden Cognitive Test

In May 2023, more than 60 Republican members of the [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] sent a letter to the [[white-house|White House]] demanding that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] take a mental competency test or withdraw from the 2024 election.[1]

## Content of the letter

The letter carried 61 Republican signatures and was led by Rep. [[ronny-jackson|Ronny Jackson]] of Texas, a former White House physician, who wrote that Republicans were concerned about Biden's "current cognitive state and ability to serve another term as President."[1] The signatories argued that "regardless of gender, age, or political party, all Presidents should document and demonstrate sound mental abilities."[1] They noted that Biden had undergone two physical exams during his presidency — on November 19, 2021 and February 16, 2023 — but that there was "no indication you have had any cognitive assessment, or if you have, such results were concealed from the public."[1]

The February 2023 examination by White House physician [[kevin-oconnor|Kevin O'Connor]] concluded that Biden was a "healthy, vigorous, 80-year-old male, who is fit successfully execute the duties of the Presidency, to include those as Chief Executive, Head of State, and Commander in Chief."[1] The letter responded that this was "a statement based on a physical exam that excluded the evaluation of your cognitive and mental abilities, which is where our concerns, and the concerns of the American public, lie."[1] Jackson pointed to earlier letters on the subject sent in June 2021, February 2022 and July 2022.[1] The demand followed Biden's April 25, 2023 announcement of his reelection campaign, and was reported by the Washington Examiner and Fox News.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the more revealing figure was the number of Republicans who had not signed: only about 60 of 222 House Republicans, roughly 27 percent, joined the letter.[1] The commentary asked why there were no accompanying calls to invoke the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]] or to file articles of impeachment, quoted [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 12, 2021 warning that the 25th Amendment "will come back to haunt Joe Biden and the Biden administration," and criticized Jackson's framing — that failing a test "will allow you to come to terms with the many failures of your administration" — as overtly political.[1]
