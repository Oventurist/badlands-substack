# Game Theory (strategy)

Game theory, in the sense used by the Badlands Media "Q For Dummies" series, is the branch of mathematics concerned with predicting and shaping the outcome of strategic interactions. Q asked readers to define the term at the opening of Drop #97, posted on November 5, 2017, and Chapter 13 of the series treated the request as the interpretive key to the entire drop.[1] (For the Badlands program of the same name, see [[game-theory|Game Theory (show)]].)

## Definition offered in the series

The chapter defined game theory in the simplest terms as a set of mathematics that can be used to dictate the outcome of a given situation.[1] Its illustrative example was that by presenting an opponent with a bounded set of possible actions, a strategist knows in advance which outcomes to prepare for, so that when the opponent acts the counter-response becomes second nature; repeated enough times, the process allows a strategist to mold an enemy into walking down a chosen path.[1]

## Application to the "deep state"

The relevance claimed for the concept is strategic necessity. The author argued that all-out warfare against the [[deep-state|deep state]] is not viable and would end in defeat, so the alternative is to rely on knowledge and on manipulation of the adversary's ego, causing opponents to walk into successive traps while believing they hold the upper hand.[1]

The chapter grounded the doctrine in classical sources, naming [[donald-trump|President Trump]] and [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] as proponents of [[sun-tzu|Sun Tzu]] and The Art of War, and quoting two of its maxims: "Let your plans be dark and impenetrable as night, and when you move — fall like a thunderbolt," and "Appear weak when strong."[1] It also cited the 4th Psychological Operations Group and the Second World War [[ghost-army|Ghost Army]] as instances of game theory in practice — methods that multiply the apparent moves available against an opponent and force that opponent into continual countermoves.[1] The author compared the resulting condition to an athlete driven onto his back foot, where playing not to lose eventually costs the victory.[1]

## Flynn and the FBI interview

The most concrete application in the chapter concerned the framing of [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] by the [[fbi|FBI]]. The author asserted that Flynn deliberately took the interview knowing he was being set up, precisely so that the bureau's corruption — and by extension that of the entire [[obama-administration|Obama administration]] — would be fully exposed, and described this as exactly what game theory is.[1] The same logic underlies the drop's statement that "disinformation is necessary," which the author read as a reminder that not everything Q publishes is aimed at followers or is literally true, since red herrings are required to keep the adversary off track.[1]
