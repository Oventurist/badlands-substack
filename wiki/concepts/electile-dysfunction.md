# Electile Dysfunction

**Electile dysfunction** is a satirical coinage used by election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] to describe what he characterizes as a chronic condition afflicting American national elections.[1]

Keshel introduced the term at the close of his February 13, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] guest essay on applying [[smart-goal-setting|SMART goal setting]] to election reform, warning that if the work of overhauling elections on a grand scale did not begin immediately, reformers would have to shift from salvaging the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]] to protecting the 2028 presidential election from the condition.[1]

He listed four primary symptoms: difficulty maintaining an election; an election lasting longer than four days; sudden, severe and notable loss of interest in voting; and, in worst cases, premature inauguration.[1] The framing is a deliberate parody of pharmaceutical advertising, but each symptom maps onto a substantive complaint in his broader analysis — respectively, administrative breakdown, prolonged counting (one of his [[eight-cardinal-sins|Eight Cardinal Sins]]), voter demoralization, and the seating of a candidate whose victory he regards as illegitimate.[1]
