# Sculptor Galaxy

The Sculptor galaxy, also catalogued as NGC 253, is a nearby spiral galaxy similar in many respects to the Milky Way, and the subject of an ultra-detailed image released in June 2025.[1]

## Characteristics and 2025 imaging

Sculptor is about the same size and mass as the Milky Way and shares a similar spiral structure, but unlike our own galaxy — which cannot be viewed in full from Earth because we sit inside it — Sculptor is perfectly positioned for a comprehensive look.[1]

Astronomers released the ultra-detailed image on Wednesday, June 18, 2025, based on 50 hours of observations with the [[european-southern-observatory|European Southern Observatory]]'s Chile-based Very Large Telescope, one of the world's biggest telescopes.[1] The image renders Sculptor in around 4,000 different colours, each corresponding to a specific wavelength in the optical spectrum.[1] Because various galactic components emit light differently across the spectrum, the observations provide information at unprecedented detail on the inner workings of an entire galaxy, from star formation to the motion of interstellar gas on large scales — far beyond the handful of colours available in conventional astronomical images.[1]
