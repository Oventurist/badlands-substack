# 2024 German Government Collapse

The 2024 German government collapse refers to the implosion of Germany's three-party ruling coalition on the evening of Wednesday, November 6, 2024, after Chancellor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]] announced he would fire Finance Minister [[christian-lindner|Christian Lindner]] over persistent rifts on spending and economic reforms.[1]

## Course of events

The coalition had been on the verge of collapse for months before the final rupture.[1] Scholz's dismissal of Lindner ejected the fiscally conservative [[free-democratic-party|Free Democratic Party]] (FDP) from the government, forcing Scholz to call a confidence vote that he said would take place on January 15.[1] Losing that vote was regarded as virtually certain, which would set a snap election for the end of March 2025.[1]

Scholz, described in the coverage as the weakest German chancellor in decades, said in a statement at the chancellery: "Dear fellow citizens, I would have liked to have spared you this difficult decision, especially in times like these, when uncertainty is growing."[1]

## Underlying dispute

Lindner and the FDP insisted the government stick to strict spending rules and cut taxes, while the coalition's left-wing partners wanted to maintain social spending and boost German industry through economic stimulus.[1] The rifts proved too great to overcome.[1]

## American context

The collapse came just hours after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s clear win in the U.S. election and the Republican sweep of Congress, a result that stunned German political leaders who depend on American military might for their country's defense and who feared Trump's tariff policies would hobble German industry.[1] The simultaneous defeat of [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and the German coalition's implosion were framed in Badlands commentary as part of a broader bad day for the Western establishment.[1]
