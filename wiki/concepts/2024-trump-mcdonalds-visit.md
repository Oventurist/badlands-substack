# 2024 Trump McDonald's Visit

The 2024 Trump McDonald's visit was a campaign event on Sunday, October 20, 2024, in which former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] cooked and served french fries at a [[mcdonalds|McDonald's]] restaurant in Pennsylvania while trading jabs at Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1]

Trump entered the restaurant and shook hands with the owner, announcing, "Hello, everybody. It's my first day at McDonald's, I'm looking for a job."[1] Thousands of supporters surrounded the restaurant as he spent the afternoon working as a fry cook, an appearance staged after he accused Harris of lying about having worked at the fast food chain.[1] Handing out orders through the drive-thru window, Trump said, "I've now worked for 15 minutes more than Kamala at McDonald's."[1]

Badlands Media treated the stunt as emblematic of an increasingly surreal news cycle, writing that the event produced "yet another iconic image that will help define the spectacular nature of this absurd moment in time."[1] The brief paired the visit with retired NFL wide receiver [[antonio-brown|Antonio Brown]]'s appearance at a Trump rally the previous day as twin examples of the campaign's cultural momentum.[1]
