# Canadian Capitulation

**Canadian Capitulation** is a phrase used by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators to describe what they present as a recurring pattern of concessions by the Canadian government in its dealings with the United States and with wider geopolitical pressure.

The term appears in [[badlands-blitz-episode-4|Badlands Blitz Episode 4]], published January 31, 2026, where the host describes "the ongoing pattern of Canadian Capitulation" as one of the week's threads. The episode groups it with the [[minnesota-color-revolution|Minnesota Color Revolution]] and the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County Election Raid]], arguing that these apparently unconnected stories converge to illuminate "the same Macro System, and its inevitable dissolution" — an example of the show's [[system-sway|System Sway]] framing.

Because the archived post names the pattern without elaborating on the specific Canadian policy moves involved, the entry reflects the outlet's characterisation rather than a documented sequence of events. The framing is interpretive and contested.
