# #BanTheADL campaign

**#BanTheADL** was a hashtag campaign that trended on [[x-twitter|X]] in September 2023, calling for the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] to be removed from the platform amid its public confrontation with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

The campaign gathered momentum after ADL chief executive [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]] posted a message that Musk and Badlands Media read as an implicit threat to declare X a vector of hate speech and thereby make it radioactive to advertisers.[1] Commentator [[eva-vlaardingerbroek|Eva Vlaardingerbroek]] noted the hashtag's trending status while criticising the censorship tactics she associated with such organisations, and Musk responded by floating the idea of running a poll to gauge public sentiment toward the ADL.[1]

The ADL replied that it was "unsurprised yet undeterred that anti-Semites, white supremacists, conspiracy theorists and other trolls have launched a coordinated attack on our organization. This type of thing is nothing new."[1] Badlands Media characterised that response as psychological bullying — an attempt to make any criticism of the organisation socially disqualifying by reducing critics to white supremacists, conspiracy theorists or trolls — and argued it is effective chiefly on the uninformed.[1]

The episode escalated further when Musk publicly raised the prospect of a defamation lawsuit against the ADL, an irony Badlands Media considered glaring enough to penetrate public apathy.[1] Musk also said on the [[all-in-podcast|All-In Podcast]] that the organisation had been instrumental in getting [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] removed from the platform, and the resulting advertiser "pause" caused a significant dip in X's western advertising revenue.[1]
