# Adrenochrome

Adrenochrome is a compound produced in the brain which, according to claims circulated within the Truth Community, is reputed to have psychedelic effects and is harvested by elite paedophile rings from children who are kidnapped, raped and murdered.[1] The compound is best known in popular culture from its appearance in the film Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas.[1]

## Role in the Sound of Freedom controversy

Adrenochrome claims became central to mainstream coverage of the 2023 anti-child-trafficking film [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]].[1] [[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]] critic [[miles-klee|Miles Klee]] attacked the film's lead actor, [[jim-caviezel|Jim Caviezel]], as "a prominent figure on the conspiracist right" and slammed his past statements about elite rings harvesting adrenochrome, treating those beliefs as grounds for dismissing the film's premise.[1] Aggregating the review, [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] countered that the film was inspired by the very real work of [[tim-ballard|Tim Ballard]] and [[operation-underground-railroad|Operation Underground Railroad]], and that Caviezel's separate beliefs did not invalidate it.[1]

Badlands commentators treated the adrenochrome framing as a rhetorical device: by attaching the most exotic claim available to any discussion of child trafficking, establishment outlets could categorise the entire subject as [[qanon|QAnon]] conspiracy theory.[1] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that destroying the very notion of a global child-sex trafficking network was a strategic necessity for the elites he associates with [[sabbateanism|Sabbateanism]] and [[frankism|Frankism]], so that even open admissions would not be believed.[1]
