# Founders & Foundations (series)

"Founders & Foundations" is a Badlands Media article series examining the origins of American political and financial institutions through the figures who shaped them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) The series is written by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] and published on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Substack.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)

## Installments

As described in the August 15, 2025 Badlands News Brief, the series' earlier parts focused on Alexander Hamilton, Abraham Lincoln, and Benjamin Franklin.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features) "The Birth of America's Deep State," published August 6, 2025, was presented as the third entry in the series, extending its scope from founding-era biography to the institutional emergence of what Badlands terms the [[deep-state|deep state]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features)
