# 2023 Debt Ceiling Agreement

The 2023 debt ceiling agreement was the deal struck between Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] and congressional Democrats to raise the federal borrowing limit, characterized in the Badlands corpus as an increase "with virtually no spending cuts."[1]

## Interpretive crisis

The agreement is treated by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as the event that broke his prior reading of McCarthy's speakership.[1] Having concluded that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s endorsement of McCarthy implied control over him, Carlson found himself compelled to rethink the endorsement entirely once the deal passed.[1]

He posed a series of alternatives rather than a conclusion: that an increased debt limit without cuts was itself part of Trump's plan; that more evidence of a [[uniparty|Uniparty]] was needed to wake people up; that the economy must worsen before Trump takes over again; and that the whole sequence was part of destroying the Cabal banking system said to control the country and the world.[1] A further possibility was that Trump endorsed McCarthy intending for him to be quickly ousted, with the capitulation serving to expose the RINOs and Neocons — made plausible by the America First bloc having secured measures easing the impeachment of a Speaker.[1]

## Trump's silence

Carlson noted as strange that on the morning before Sean Hannity's town hall, with the deal dominating the news cycle for weeks, he had not heard Trump mention the "monumentally-big debt ceiling deal" even once, spending his commentary instead on [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]].[1] He inferred from this that the deal likely needed to go through as written.[1]
