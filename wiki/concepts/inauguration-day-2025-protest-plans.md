# Inauguration Day 2025 Protest Plans

Ahead of the January 20, 2025 inauguration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], leftist groups announced plans to gather in Washington, D.C., and other large cities across the nation, saying their aim was to "defeat Trump's extreme right, billionaire agenda." The organizing was characterized by conservative media as a total failure to accept a clear electoral loss and was labeled an Inauguration Day "insurrection."[1]

The framing drew a direct contrast with the aftermath of January 6, 2021: the [[fbi|FBI]] was still prosecuting people who attended the Capitol riot, and the commentary asked whether the assembled leftists should likewise fear being jailed for being present and disagreeable — whether the FBI and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] would disseminate their photos, knock on doors, interview friends, drag them to court, and leave them to languish in prison. The answer offered was "probably not," on the grounds that conservatives do not throw the opposition in prison because doing so is un-American and unconstitutional.[1]
