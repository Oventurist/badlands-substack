# 2025 Lady Gaga Concert Terror Plot

The 2025 Lady Gaga concert terror plot was an alleged plan to attack the singer's record-breaking free concert on Copacabana Beach in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, before an audience of roughly 2.5 million people in early May 2025.[1]

## The alleged plot

Brazilian authorities said Luis da Silva, 44, planned to livestream the execution of children and to set up bombs close to the stage during the performance.[1] Rio de Janeiro Civil Police secretary Felipe Curi told reporters that da Silva "said that the singer was a Satanist and that he was going to perform a Satanist ritual too, killing a child during the show."[1] Da Silva and a 17-year-old boy were taken into custody hours before the concert proceeded, after investigators uncovered the alleged plot.[1] The pair are alleged to have used Discord to radicalise others, including teenagers, to carry out attacks on children and on LGBTQ+ concertgoers.[1]

## Deportation controversy

Brazilian authorities revealed that the suspect had been deported from the United States only the month before the alleged plot, and investigators were examining why he had been removed from the country.[1]
