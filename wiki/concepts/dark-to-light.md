# Dark to Light

"Dark to light" is a recurring phrase in the [[q-drops|Q drops]], used within the [[qanon|Q]] movement to describe the movement of hidden or captured institutions, countries and information into public view. [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] records that the phrase appears thirty-three times across the corpus of posts, and treats it as one of the operation's organising motifs rather than an incidental slogan.[1]

## Application to North Korea

Carlson's principal illustration of the phrase concerns [[north-korea|North Korea]]. He cites drop 888, dated March 8, 2018 — "Thank you Kim. Deal made. Clowns out. Strings cut. We took control. Iran next." — as the drop in which the transition is announced, reading it against Q's broader claim that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] created and controlled North Korea and used it as an imaginary threat to sustain agency power and the [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]].[1] On this reading the country's CIA strings were cut roughly a year into [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s presidency, with the [[2019-trump-kim-dmz-meeting|DMZ meeting]] between Trump and [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] serving as the visible confirmation.[1]

The wordplay Carlson draws out is that North Korea's initials, DPRK, closely resemble DARK — so that "dark to light" can be read as "DPRK to light," a nation freed from its [[deep-state|Deep State]] chains.[1] He extends the same framework to Iran, which the drops treat as subject to identical CIA control, and leaves open whether those strings have been completely severed.[1]

## Relation to "ascension"

The phrase sits alongside the final word Q posted, "ascension," on November 27, 2022, which Carlson glosses as the act of rising to a higher level.[1] He applies both terms to the [[anons|Anons]] themselves, arguing that discernment, understanding, hope, trust, courage and humility rose in those who followed the drops, and that the general public was affected in turn by the few who refused to be silenced.[1]
