# Disinformation Is Necessary

**"Disinformation is real, disinformation is necessary"** is a formulation appearing in Q drop #128 of November 9, 2017, and treated in Chapter 21 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as an explicit admission that some material posted by the so-called Q team was intentionally false.[1]

[[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] reads the line as Q telling followers in advance that not everything in the drops should be taken at face value.[1] In his account the practice is by design and serves two purposes: maintaining [[operations-security|operational security]] (OpSec) for ongoing operations, and keeping hostile actors uncertain about what the operation actually knows and intends.[1]

## The worked example

Immediately after the statement, Q supplies a worked example in the compressed stringer form "US ML NG (1) False," which Fleuret parses as a declaration that one of the drops concerning the United States military and the [[national-guard|National Guard]] was disinformation.[1] His guess is that the false item was the earlier post suggesting the National Guard had been called up, a drop that landed days before the [[saudi-purge|Saudi Purge]].[1]

The example is completed by the adjacent line "SA. True," which Fleuret takes as confirmation that everything dropped about the Saudi operation was accurate rather than seeded misdirection.[1] He suggests that while the American National Guard had not in fact been mobilised, members of the Saudi National Guard asked the same question on 30 October 2017 would likely have answered yes.[1]

## Rationale

Fleuret's explanation for the deliberate misdirection is that it prevented the [[deep-state|deep state]] from realising that [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] — described in the chapter as its "biggest honey hole" — was about to be taken from it.[1] He adds that Q later indicates the same sequence of events that occurred in Saudi Arabia will eventually occur in the United States.[1]

Drop #128 pairs the disinformation admission with a warning that a coordinated misdirection effort was being run by bad actors against Q and the boards, and with the instruction to learn to distinguish relevant from non-relevant news — a discipline Fleuret likens to [[michael-flynn|General Flynn]]'s repeated advice not to chase shiny objects.[1]

## Earlier statement in drop #70

The maxim appears well before drop #128. Chapter 9 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] notes that Q closed drop #70 of November 4, 2017 by reminding readers both that the crumbs being dropped would amount to the largest intelligence dump to the public domain in history and that disinformation is real.[2] The chapter's author draws the operative conclusion that disinformation "goes both ways," and that "our side utilizes it equally as often as the enemy" — a formulation that makes deliberate misdirection a tool of the [[white-hats|white hats]] as much as of their opponents.[2]

Placed alongside the companion rule that [[double-meanings-exist|double meanings exist]], the principle gives the series wide latitude in reading individual drops, allowing a statement to be treated as accurate, as planted, or as true on a second level.[2]
