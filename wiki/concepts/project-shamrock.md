# Project SHAMROCK

Project SHAMROCK was a [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] program identified by investigators for the 1975 [[church-committee|Church Committee]] as one of several intelligence operations that had never before been known to the American public.[1]

## Description

SHAMROCK, together with its companion program [[project-minaret|Project MINARET]], monitored wire communications to and from the United States and shared some of that collected data with other intelligence agencies.[1] Its exposure formed part of the committee's broader documentation of what Badlands Media calls the "silent war" on the privacy of American citizens waged by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], the [[fbi|FBI]] and the NSA — a capability [[frank-church|Frank Church]] warned could "at any time be turned around on the American people," leaving "no place left to hide if this government ever turned to tyranny."[1]
