# Google Veo 3

**Google Veo 3** is a generative video model released by [[google|Google]] and described by the company as a "state-of-the-art" system for producing synthetic video from text prompts. Following its launch in May 2025 the model spread rapidly across social media, with users generating clips realistic enough to be mistaken for authentic footage.[1]

## Public reception

Early adopters used Veo 3 for a wide range of subjects, including standup comedy routines, simulated street interviews, and — most consequentially — fabricated broadcast news segments. A series of clips depicting television news anchors delivering absurd information in an entirely straight, professional tone circulated widely, producing both amusement and alarm among viewers.[1]

One widely shared example was generated from the prompt instructing the model to produce "a British TV news anchor" reporting a fabricated story about a yacht sinking after an orca attack off the coast of Turkey. The user who posted it wrote, "General population is cooked. You can literally create news clips with Veo-3 now, what's even real anymore?"[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] framed the release as an information-warfare inflection point rather than a novelty. He argued that if consumer-grade tools already produce broadcast-quality synthetic video, the capabilities held by the world's militaries and intelligence services must be substantially further advanced, and asked how such actors might deploy them. His closing advice to readers was to "stay frosty."[1]

The commentary situates Veo 3 within a broader Badlands theme in which the authenticity of recorded media — long the evidentiary backbone of news reporting — can no longer be assumed, and in which narrative events delivered through video are treated with heightened skepticism.[1]
