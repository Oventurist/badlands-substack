# Gulf of America Renaming

The "Gulf of America" renaming refers to the second Trump administration's redesignation of the Gulf of Mexico, and the dispute that followed with news organizations that declined to adopt the new name.[1]

## Dispute with the Associated Press

Badlands Media reported that the administration denied [[associated-press|The Associated Press]] access to events because the wire service refused to adopt the administration's renaming of the Gulf of Mexico as the "Gulf of America."[1]

## Assessment in the free speech debate

The article placed the episode in its "The Bad" section, cataloguing instances where the administration's free speech record fell short of its campaign promises.[1] While acknowledging both the legal ambiguity and widespread antipathy toward the AP — the author admitted to "significant schadenfreude" — he argued the action was "trending in a certain direction."[1] He proposed a counterfactual: if Joe Biden had removed Fox News from a press event for refusing to acknowledge gender pronouns, conservatives would be up in arms.[1] The point raised was that people on the right were increasingly adopting the behaviours they had criticized on the left over the preceding four years.[1]

Together with the [[kristi-noem|Department of Homeland Security]]'s rhetoric about migrants who "hide behind the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]]" and campus speech enforcement, the AP episode was cited as evidence that the administration's application of free speech protections was "rather selective," even as it dismantled U.S. government elements of the international [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship apparatus]].[1]
