# Short Selling

Short selling, or shorting, is a trading practice in which investors wager that a particular stock will underperform and profit if their prediction proves accurate.[1] Contrary to conventional long trading, shorting is an uncommon practice, and numerous investment advisors caution against it under all circumstances because a breach of the projected poor performance can result in enormous losses.[1]

## Use as a forensic signal

Because shorting is comparatively rare and carries asymmetric downside, unusual concentrations of short interest ahead of a market-moving event can serve as forensic evidence of informed trading. This logic underpinned the 2023 study by [[robert-jackson-jr|Robert J. Jackson Jr.]] and [[joshua-mitts|Joshua Mitts]], which examined short selling of Israeli securities before the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 Hamas attack]].[1] The authors found a "significant spike" in short selling of listed Israeli companies, with volumes that far exceeded those recorded during other crises including the post-financial-crisis recession, the 2014 Israel-Gaza war, and the COVID-19 pandemic.[1]

A related instrument in the same analysis was the short-dated option: the researchers documented a sharp and unusual increase, just before the attacks, in trading of risky short-dated options on Israeli companies listed on U.S. exchanges, contracts that expired almost immediately after the attack began.[1] Short interest in the [[msci-israel-etf|MSCI Israel Exchange Traded Fund]] and dramatic increases in shorting on the [[tel-aviv-stock-exchange|Tel Aviv Stock Exchange]] rounded out the picture.[1]

The authors concluded that trading of this kind occurs in gaps in U.S. and international enforcement of legal prohibitions on informed trading, and recommended that the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]] and [[financial-industry-regulatory-authority|FINRA]] pursue the identities behind the trades.[1] See also [[israel-october-7-foreknowledge-allegations|Israel October 7 foreknowledge allegations]] and [[naked-short-selling|naked short selling]].
