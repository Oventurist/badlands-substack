# Sharm el-Sheikh Gaza Signing Ceremony (2025)

The Sharm el-Sheikh signing ceremony of October 2025 was the Egyptian event formalizing the Gaza peace deal brokered by the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], held as Israel prepared for the imminent release of the last 20 live hostages held by [[hamas|Hamas]].[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] told Axios in a phone interview from Air Force One en route to Israel that the deal "could be the biggest thing I was ever involved in."[1] The peace plan was expected to end a war that had killed more than 67,000 Palestinians according to the Hamas-controlled Gaza health ministry.[1]

## Trump's visit to Israel

Trump was scheduled to address the [[knesset|Knesset]], Israel's parliament, on the Monday morning following the ceremony.[1] Asked what his message to the Israeli people would be, he said: "Love and peace for eternity."[1] During the brief visit he was also to meet hostage families and Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], and he said he had watched parts of a Tel Aviv rally for the hostages held on the preceding Saturday.[1]

## Attendance controversies

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted two elements of the Axios interview, credited to reporter Barak Ravid.[1] The first was Trump drawing attention to a moment at the Tel Aviv rally when [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] mentioned Netanyahu's name before a crowd of 100,000 and the people booed loudly; Witkoff did not try to silence the crowd or change the subject but let the moment marinate, while [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] and [[ivanka-trump|Ivanka Trump]] were visibly laughing in the background as Netanyahu was humiliated.[1]

The second was that Netanyahu would not attend the signing ceremony in Egypt while Palestinian Authority President Mahmoud Abbas would, and that Trump called this a "great thing."[1] The commentary noted that Abbas had initially been blocked from attending, as he had been blocked from attending the UN General Assembly meeting in New York City the previous month, and asked who had reversed that decision.[1]

## Violence and dissent around the proceedings

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry offered "thoughts and prayers" to three Qatari diplomats conspicuously killed in a car crash in Egypt while on their way to the ceremony, and predicted that this would not be the last time violence was visited upon the proceedings.[1] He also noted that members of the Likud Party were boycotting Trump's Knesset speech as an act of defiance, angry that "their war of conquest is being shutdown," and warned that historically such moments are when "bad-twin" Israelis become exceptionally dangerous.[1] He closed by quoting Matthew 5:9: "Blessed are the Peacemakers, for they will be called sons of God."[1]
