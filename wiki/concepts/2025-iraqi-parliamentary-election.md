# 2025 Iraqi Parliamentary Election

Iraqis went to the polls on Tuesday, November 11, 2025, to elect a new 329-seat parliament from a field of more than 7,700 candidates — the seventh national vote since the fall of Saddam Hussein ushered in democratic rule more than two decades earlier.[1]

## Atmosphere and turnout

Across Baghdad's avenues and alleys, campaign posters lined the streets and created a festive air, but beneath the banners a mood of indifference prevailed, with passers-by ignoring the oversize candidate photographs.[1] The election unfolded in rare calm, yet widespread disengagement marked the political climate, a trend experts said could produce the lowest turnout since 2003.[1] One assessment quoted in reporting characterized the electoral mood as apathetic, noting that many Iraqis viewed the vote as unlikely to change entrenched power structures even as the country enjoyed relative calm.[1]
