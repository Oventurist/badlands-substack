# Battle of Parker's Ferry

The Battle of Parker's Ferry, fought on 30 August 1781 in South Carolina, was the engagement in which American troops led by [[francis-marion|Francis Marion]] defeated a combined British, Tory and Hessian force — described in the Patriot Chronicles as Marion's greatest victory as a guerrilla warfighter.[1]

## The ambush

Marion lined his men up on either side of the road at Parker's Ferry, hidden in the brush, and waited until the perfect moment to open fire.[1] The action was commemorated in an 1844 wood engraving reproduced in the article, alongside a modern photograph of the road by Chris M. Rogers.[1] The victory exemplifies the off-battlefield craft the article credits to Marion: careful attention to the morale of his men, attacking when spirits were high and regrouping to resupply when they were low.[1]
