# DOJ–Pentagon Media Leaks Task Force

The DOJ–Pentagon media leaks task force is a joint body established in July 2026 by the US [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] to identify and prosecute unauthorized disclosures of sensitive information to the news media.[1] Its creation was announced by War Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]].[1]

## Structure and authority

In a video posted to [[x|X]], Hegseth said he had delegated authority to the [[department-of-war|War Department]]'s Office of General Counsel to lead media leak investigations, empowering that office to request and receive all information, records and support across the department related to those investigations.[1]

## Context: New York Times subpoenas

The announcement came after the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] issued subpoenas to several [[new-york-times|New York Times]] journalists following the outlet's report on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s new Qatari-donated Air Force One.[1] The subpoenas sought to compel the reporters to testify before a federal grand jury in Manhattan.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the move outside conventional partisanship.[1] A 2004-vintage liberal, he wrote, would decry the trashing of the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] and the sanctity of the press as a check on the other branches, while a modern non-MAGA conservative would call it a sound step to preserve the sanctity of the US military against threats foreign and domestic — and both arguments have been heard before.[1]

Instead he described a battlespace defined by two realities: that illegal psychological warfare operations have been waged against the American people for generations with the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] serving as the main vector of psychological control, and that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] era — including the military intelligence operation many believe has been underway for a decade or more — represents a counter-insurgency, which presupposes a prior insurgency.[1] His conclusion was that the media is important but the Media Industrial Complex must be destroyed, the military is important but the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]] must be destroyed, and that both are aspects of the same complex now at war with the administration in a [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation war]].[1]
