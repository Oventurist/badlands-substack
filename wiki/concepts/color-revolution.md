# Color Revolution

A color revolution, as the term is used in the Badlands Media corpus, denotes a method of regime change in which "a small group takes over the control of a country by creating propaganda and chaos, through funding violence in the streets, buying off the media, and rigging elections." The definition is given by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in [[a-virus-or-a-metaphor|A Virus, or a Metaphor?]].

## The 1917 template

The essay's paradigm case is the October 1917 [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik uprising]], which overthrew and later murdered the Russian monarch and installed [[communism|Communism]] and the Soviet Union. The author explicitly labels that event a color revolution, noting that it was accomplished largely through propaganda and chaos and by dividing the country between "whites" and "reds" so as to foment civil war.

## Application to the United States

The article's argument is that the same method is being applied to America in the present day, and that this is what the [[q-anon|Q]] team's repeated references to [[the-hunt-for-red-october|Red October]] were meant to convey. In the author's account, the United States is "being taken down by narratives and agendas that divide and discourage and demoralize the country," with the lockdown and mail-in ballot arrangements of the [[covid-19-pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]] serving as the mechanism by which "a duly-elected President was overthrown and removed from power."
