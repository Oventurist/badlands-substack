# Magnificent Seven Stocks

The "Magnificent Seven" is a market label for the seven mega-capitalization US technology stocks that dominated index returns during the [[biden-administration|Biden administration]]: Microsoft, Apple, Alphabet (Google), Amazon, Meta (Facebook), Nvidia and [[tesla|Tesla]].[1] The grouping succeeded the earlier [[faang-stocks|FAANG]] acronym as the shorthand for concentrated [[big-tech|Big Tech]] market leadership.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In an August 2024 essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that the Magnificent Seven had soared under Biden in a continuation of the preferential treatment the [[obama-administration|Obama administration]] had extended to the FAANG names, which he said ensured those firms could monopolize their markets and become effectively irreplaceable.[1]

Carlson singled out Tesla as an anomaly within the basket, noting its share price was down roughly 33% over the previous two years and asking why it belonged on the list at all.[1] By his reading Tesla had not performed well under Biden — the opposite was the case — whereas the company had thrived under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] He tied the observation to his broader argument that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had broken with the forces that grew the sector and aligned with Trump.[1]

Elsewhere in the same essay Carlson extended his market skepticism to artificial intelligence equities, saying he had been critical of how AI companies' stock prices were "manipulated up" in what he could only interpret as a [[deep-state|Deep State]] money laundering operation.[1]
