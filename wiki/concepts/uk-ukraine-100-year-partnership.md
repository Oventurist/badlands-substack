# UK–Ukraine 100 Year Partnership

The UK–Ukraine 100 Year Partnership is a military and economic treaty between the United Kingdom and Ukraine announced on January 16, 2025, during an unannounced visit to Kyiv by British Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Sir Keir Starmer]].[1]

## Announcement

Starmer, making his first visit to Ukraine since becoming Prime Minister roughly six months earlier, announced his travel with the "obligatory train journey photographs made necessary by the war-imposed no-fly zone."[1] Ahead of meetings with President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], he said he planned to sign a 100-year treaty that day, hailed as a "historic 100 Year Partnership to continue to support Ukraine now and invest in our two countries for the future," formalising what he called an "unbreakable bond" between the two nations.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued the move echoed a pattern going back at least to the 19th century, when the British military adopted Prussian military standards and practices while the Prussian elite pursued unification under Otto von Bismarck, welding some 300 Germanic kingdoms into the nation-state of Germany.[1] The commentary described the rivalry between the Prussian elite and Russia as real and as involving the former [[pale-of-settlement|Pale of Settlement]] region — modern-day Ukraine.[1] It further asserted that most of those Prussian oligarchs eventually left the continent for London and joined the British aristocracy, some of them German, some Russian Jews from modern-day Ukraine, and in the case of the Rothschilds Austrian Jews, all folding into a banking cabal whose origins the author traced to Venice and deeper into history.[1]

On timing, the commentary held that the pragmatism of the treaty likely stemmed from fear that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would pull all Americans out of Ukraine and leave the coalition without men or resources, with all efforts being made to stoke and prop up morale.[1]
