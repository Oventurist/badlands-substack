# Confessions of an Economic Hit Man

*Confessions of an Economic Hit Man* is a book by [[john-perkins|John Perkins]] describing how developing nations are drawn into unpayable debt in order to strip them of resources and sovereignty.[1] In his April 27, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "The Economy: Weaponized," [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] cited Perkins and the book as confirmation that conquest and colonialism today are conducted through finance rather than through military occupation.[1]

Deschamps used the book as corroboration for his own seven-stage account of the [[economic-hit-man|economic hit man]] model, in which "modernization" loans from the [[international-monetary-fund|International Monetary Fund]] are structured to default, with land, resource rights and austerity commitments pledged as collateral.[1]
