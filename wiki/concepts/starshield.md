# Starshield

Starshield is a satellite program associated with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and used, according to Musk's own confirmation, by the United States for national defense.[1] It entered Badlands Media commentary in February 2025 as a possible explanation for the [[iron-dome|"Iron Dome"]] homeland missile-defense initiative directed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Relationship to the Iron Dome executive order

After experts told The Independent that Trump's plan for a next-generation homeland missile shield defied physics and practicality, Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] proposed an alternative reading: that Musk's confirmation of Starshield's defense role suggested the Iron Dome executive order was "just the narrative disclosure of Starshield already being in place."[1] The observation was paired with the reminder that "the DOD's biggest contractor is Elon Musk."[1]

In this framing, the executive order — which the White House said would "further the goal of peace through strength" — functions less as the initiation of a new program than as public acknowledgement of an existing orbital capability, sidestepping the terrestrial radar limitations identified by the [[congressional-research-service|Congressional Research Service]], which found that most ground-based radars cannot detect hypersonic weapons until late in flight.[1]
