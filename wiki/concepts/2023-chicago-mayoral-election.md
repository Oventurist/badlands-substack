# 2023 Chicago Mayoral Election

The 2023 Chicago mayoral election concluded with an April 4, 2023 runoff in which progressive candidate [[brandon-johnson|Brandon Johnson]] defeated moderate Democrat [[paul-vallas|Paul Vallas]] to become mayor of Chicago.[1]

## Campaign

Johnson, a self-professed progressive, ran on a platform that included defunding the Chicago Police Department and raising city taxes.[1] Vallas, a former chief executive of Chicago Public Schools, campaigned as a business-friendly, law-and-order alternative.[1] Johnson's campaign attacked Vallas as a racist who was "really a Republican."[1]

## Result

More than 500,000 Chicagoans cast ballots in the runoff.[1] Vallas led for most of election night until approximately 8 p.m., when Johnson began overtaking him and went on to hold a narrow lead; counting of absentee and mail-in ballots continued after Election Night.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that the election of another radical leftist in Chicago should surprise no one, but flagged the late surge in votes as a possible indicator of foul play, and speculated that the need for such measures in a reliably Democratic city might suggest Chicago had been at risk of flipping Republican.[1]
