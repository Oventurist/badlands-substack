# GART 6 (Myrtle Beach)

**GART 6** was the sixth stop along the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], the live event series staged by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina from November 14 to 17, 2024.[1] The event was announced in a promotional post published on October 18, 2024, roughly a month before the gathering, under the headline "GART Myrtle Beach is Almost Here!"[1]

## Framing

Badlands presented the tour stops as a chance for its audience — referred to as "Badlanders" — to meet and network with fellow patriots, and characterized them as creating "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[1]

The announcement drew attention to the stop's proximity to "a certain national event," the United States general election held on November 5, 2024, which organizers said would set the stage for discussion of "all manner of shenanigans … or red wave winnings."[1]

## Tickets

By the time of the October announcement, full weekend VIP and General Admission tickets — which included a Friday night welcome reception — had **sold out**, with the organizers thanking supporters for the response.[1] A limited number of single-day and discounted combo two-day tickets remained on sale, aimed at attendees local to the Myrtle Beach area who wanted to drop in for part of the conversations and festivities.[1] In-person tickets were sold through a Badlands TicketSpice page dedicated to the Myrtle Beach event.[1]

A separate virtual ticket was offered for those who had never attended a GART event in person, providing a stream of the entire event including panels on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.[1] Virtual purchases carried long-term DVR access to panel replays following the weekend, so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss the programming.[1] The virtual ticket additionally granted entry to an exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat, where holders could interact with hundreds of fellow Badlanders and Badlands hosts, view behind-the-scenes photos and videos, and take part in what the post called "virtual shenanigans galore."[1]

## Programming

The full schedule of panels and activities was not published with the announcement and was promised for later release.[1] Badlands did publish a list of the contributors and hosts who would appear in Myrtle Beach or on the stream, noting that the lineup covered "most of us."[1]
