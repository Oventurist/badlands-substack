# Phoenix Airport Migrant Bussing Story

The Phoenix airport migrant bussing story refers to a December 2023 wave of citizen-journalist reporting alleging that migrants from the southern U.S. border were being transported directly to Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport and flown onward to destinations across the country.[1]

## Social media claims

That week saw a surge of citizen journalist reports on the social platform X detailing how migrants from the southern border were being moved around the country through U.S. airports.[1] A post from X user Ashley St. Clair sparked the first major concern that the government and/or non-governmental organizations were flying migrants from processing centers onto Delta Air Lines planes at Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport and then across the nation.[1] Community Notes responded that "there is absolutely no evidence of the people at the gate being 'migrants.'"[1]

Another X user, Tony Ortiz, claimed he had noticed a large number of migrants being shipped from Phoenix Airport to different parts of the country, saying "these Guinea migrants were headed to New York via Catholic Community Services of Southern Arizona."[1]

## O'Keefe Media Group video

[[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]], founder of [[okeefe-media-group|O'Keefe Media Group]], was described by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] as having provided the best evidence yet that the travelers were in fact in the country illegally.[1] On Wednesday night he posted a video filmed at the Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport drop-off terminal, in which he interviewed a bus driver working for a limo service contracted by an unknown entity to haul migrants from the border to the airport.[1] Asked "Are these migrants from the border?", the driver answered, "Correct."[1]

The item ran under the headline "Public Has A Right To Know" as a bonus entry in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
