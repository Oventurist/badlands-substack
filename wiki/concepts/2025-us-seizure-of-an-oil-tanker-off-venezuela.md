# 2025 US Seizure of an Oil Tanker off Venezuela

On December 10, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said that the United States had seized an oil tanker off the coast of [[venezuela|Venezuela]] as tensions mounted with the government of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

## The seizure

Using U.S. forces to take control of a merchant ship is described as incredibly unusual, and the action marked the Trump administration's latest push to increase pressure on Maduro, who has been charged with narcoterrorism in the United States.[1] "We've just seized a tanker on the coast of Venezuela, a large tanker, very large, largest one ever seized, actually," Trump told reporters at the White House, adding that "it was seized for a very good reason."[1] He offered no additional details; asked what would happen to the oil aboard, he replied, "Well, we keep it, I guess."[1]

## Context

The seizure came amid the largest U.S. military presence in the region in decades and a series of deadly strikes on alleged drug-smuggling boats in the Caribbean Sea and eastern Pacific Ocean, a campaign facing growing scrutiny from [[us-congress|Congress]].[1] That scrutiny was reflected in the [[national-defense-authorization-act|National Defense Authorization Act]] provision withholding part of Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]]'s travel budget until he turned over unedited footage of the strikes.[1] Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] responded to the seizure by asking simply, "Whose tanker is it, anyway?"[1]
