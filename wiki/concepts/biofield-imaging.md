# Biofield Imaging

**Biofield imaging** is the research field associated in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] with [[konstantin-korotkov|Konstantin Korotkov]].[1]

The essay reports that Korotkov's biofield imaging work shows that when individuals enter coherent, heart-centred states, their energy fields extend further and more symmetrically than they otherwise would.[1]

Within the article's argument, the finding supplies an empirical-sounding basis for the claim that a person in the [[liminality|liminal]] state is doing measurable energetic work: by resting in stillness "without seeking external validation," they are said to be "anchoring a stable signal into a chaotic world."[1] The section pairs the research with [[lynne-mctaggart|Lynne McTaggart]]'s account of [[morphic-resonance|morphic resonance]] to argue that the liminal pause contributes to a collective field rather than withdrawing from it.[1]
