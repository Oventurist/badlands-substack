# Ides of March

The **Ides of March** is the 74th day of the Roman calendar, corresponding to 15 March, and was notable in Rome as a deadline for settling debts.[1]

## Significance in the COVFEFE Dig

The date is the numerological centrepiece of "The COVFEFE Dig," in which [[gmoney|GMONEY]] decodes the first three letters of [[covfefe|covfefe]] under a simple A=1 substitution as C=3, O=15, V=22 — that is, 3/15/22.[1] The Roman association with debt settlement is treated as thematically decisive, since the article's subject is the American debt burden; the author notes that United States debt had just passed $31.5 trillion.[1]

Further 3/15 correspondences are assembled: [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]], the destroyer of the central bank, was born on 3/15; 3 × 15 equals 45 and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is the 45th president; [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] posted a #COVFEFE "good morning" message on 3/15/22; the Senate condemned [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] as a war criminal on 3/15/22; and [[qanon|Q]] drop 314 is read as falling the day before 3/15.[1] The article also folds in 11/11 as the 315th day of the year, matching the "FE FE" ending of covfefe, which decodes to 11 11.[1]

The Roman framing extends to the article's opening image, a [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] graphic depicting Trump as Nero fiddling while Rome burned, paired with a Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis educational lesson on how currency debasement — the removal of silver content from Roman coinage — contributed to the fall of the Roman Empire.[1] A closing section invokes the Roman Triumvirate, the unofficial coalition of Julius Caesar, Pompey and Crassus formed in 60 BC, under the heading "Death to the Triumvirate."[1]
