# GART 8 Plymouth

GART 8 Plymouth was the eighth installment of the Great American Restoration Tour, the live conference series produced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], held in Plymouth beginning on the evening of Thursday, May 1, 2025.[1] The host city was Plymouth, Massachusetts, referred to within the network's community as "the Bright Lands".[2] Promotional material described the gathering as the second GART of what Badlands writers called the new American Golden Age.[2]

## Announcement

The April 30, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] news brief announced that the event would kick off the following night, noting that in-person tickets remained available and encouraging readers who were considering attending to do so.[1] Because many of the network's show hosts would be at the conference, readers were warned to expect changes to the regularly scheduled programming.[1] Those unable to attend in person were directed to a virtual ticket option so they could still take part in the action.[1]

A separate "last call" notice published the same day by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] repeated the appeal, telling readers that it was "almost time" and that many of them would be joining their favorite Badlands hosts in Plymouth that week.[2]

The event follows the pattern of earlier installments in the series such as [[gart-2|GART 2]] and [[gart-3|GART 3]], in which Badlands hosts gather with their audience for live shows and panels.[1]

## Ticketing

VIP passes and full-weekend general admission tickets remained on sale as the event approached, and a limited number of single-day tickets were released for attendees living near the area who wanted to attend only one or two of the Friday, Saturday or Sunday sessions.[2] In-person ticket packages, with a published breakdown of pricing and inclusions, were sold through a TicketSpice page dedicated to the Plymouth stop.[2]

A virtual ticket allowed remote viewers to stream the entirety of the event, including the panels held on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.[2] Virtual purchases carried long-term DVR access to panel replays after the weekend, so that buyers unable to watch live would not miss the content.[2] The virtual ticket also granted entry to an exclusive GART Telegram chat in which hundreds of audience members and Badlands hosts could interact, view behind-the-scenes photographs and videos, and participate in informal online activity around the event.[2]

## Purpose and framing

Badlands framed the GART events as an opportunity for attendees to meet and network with fellow patriots, describing the gatherings as "an awakening pressure cooker" in which a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place.[2] Closing the announcement on behalf of the whole Badlands Media team, the network said it hoped to see familiar faces alongside the many newcomers who had attended every previous GART.[2]
