# CUT (film)

*CUT* is a documentary film by [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], cited by its director as a case study in the difficulty of distributing work that dissents from mainstream narratives.[1]

Esler has reported that numerous distributors rejected the film as "dangerous misinformation" or as "too controversial," an experience he uses to argue that censorship-free distribution channels are urgently needed.[1] He contrasts this with [[substack|Substack]]'s distribution of [[the-coddling-of-the-american-mind|The Coddling of the American Mind]], noting that support of the arts has historically been a left-wing domain and that new avenues therefore matter disproportionately for films likely to be reframed by mainstream media as "far-right extremism."[1] The film is presented as part of the wider information war and of the emerging parallel economy for dissident media.[1]
