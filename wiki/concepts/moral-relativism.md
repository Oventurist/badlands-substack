# Moral Relativism

**Moral relativism** is the position that morality is subjective, resting on the beliefs of an individual or group.[1] Badlands Media characterizes it as an arbitrary system that favors one group so it can dominate another, and identifies phallogocentrism as a basis for Marxism within this framing.[1]

## Argument against

Because relativism reduces morality to a choice of one group using force to gain dominance, another system can always be imposed in its place, also by force — a dynamic the article identifies as the basis of [[communism]] as it gripped Russia and China.[1] Secular atheistic ideologies and cultures are therefore held to be more susceptible to [[mass-psychosis]] than religion-centric cultures, since religions are among the few institutions still founded on [[objective-morality|objective morality]].[1]

## Historical illustration

Before the Bolsheviks took over Russia it was a highly religious society with a clear if imperfect sense of morality and ethics.[1] When [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]] and his associates gutted Russia's moral foundation by popularizing secular atheism and Marxism, the article argues, genocide, rape, torture and the deprivation of property became justifiable and even necessary for the communist utopia.[1]

## Contemporary example

The article presents transgender ideology as an example of the disorienting power of removing an explicitly defined value system from society, arguing that it denies any objective male or female outside self-declaration and prescribes mass conformity to self-declared identities.[1] It contends that those who subscribe to it lack a moral compass or clear system of the principles of truth, right and wrong.[1]
