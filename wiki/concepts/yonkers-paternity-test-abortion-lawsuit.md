# Yonkers Paternity Test Abortion Lawsuit

The Yonkers paternity test abortion lawsuit is a Manhattan Federal Court case filed by an anonymous New York woman who terminated a pregnancy at roughly 20 weeks after receiving paternity test results she later learned were incorrect.[1]

## Background

The plaintiff, a 28-year-old administrative assistant, and her then-fiancé had struggled to conceive and broke up for three weeks in the summer of 2024, in part because of the "stress and aggravation" of their inability to get pregnant.[1] During the hiatus she had sex with another man, using a condom, she said.[1] She and her fiancé reconciled, and by August she was pregnant for the first time and confident her longtime partner was the father, saying "I was tracking my ovulation."[1]

Wanting certainty — and not wanting to disclose the encounter to her fiancé — she sought a paternity test with the other man.[1] Confident the test would exclude him, she hosted a gender-reveal party with her fiancé while awaiting results.[1] A Halloween call from the lab left her in "utter surprise, disappointment and frustration" when she was told the other man was the father with "99.99% certainty," according to court papers first reported by NBC News.[1]

## Termination and error

By then she was nearly 20 weeks pregnant and facing New York's 24-week deadline for legal abortion.[1] She disclosed the situation to her fiancé, who "just cried" and asked why she had gone through with the gender reveal.[1] During a grueling two-day termination procedure she had a change of heart, but doctors could not save the baby.[1] "I wish I could reverse this," she said.[1]

Months later she learned the results were wrong.[1] The suit names Winn Health Labs in the Bronx and Ohio-based DNA Diagnostics Center over the alleged error; the lab blamed an IT error.[1] The fiancé reportedly left her after Valentine's Day, and she entered therapy.[1] "My daughter would have been born on the 17th" of April, she told the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] through tears.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the woman had "attempted to play God" and that her "deceptively eugenicist plans blew up in her face," losing both the fiancé and the child the deception was meant to protect.[1] She questioned whether the gender-reveal party reflected genuine confidence or the keeping up of appearances, noted that a 20-week fetus is "a tiny but fully formed human," and said that while the abortion was legal she would not celebrate the plaintiff.[1] Her stated moral was that "deception kills," adding that "family planning" convinces mothers to treat their offspring as disposable accessories.[1]
