# Global Sumud Flotilla

The Global Sumud Flotilla was a convoy of vessels reportedly carrying about 430 international volunteers attempting to reach Gaza with humanitarian aid, which was intercepted by [[israel|Israel]].[1]

The interception triggered an international incident after Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] posted video of detained flotilla activists, including Colombians, kneeling and handcuffed.[1] Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] accused Ben-Gvir of acting "like a true Nazi" over the treatment of the detainees.[1] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] defended the interception itself but distanced himself from Ben-Gvir's handling of the detainees and ordered deportations expedited.[1]

## October 2025 deportations and follow-on interceptions

Israel deported around 170 activists, including the Swedish activist [[greta-thunberg|Greta Thunberg]], after they were detained from boats belonging to the flotilla that Israel had stopped.[2] During their detention, activists alleged mistreatment, and said in particular that Thunberg had been "paraded like a trophy wrapped in an Israeli flag," dragged by her hair and forced to kiss an Israeli flag.[2]

Days later the Israeli military intercepted and boarded further vessels headed to Gaza, including boats of the [[freedom-flotilla-coalition|Freedom Flotilla Coalition]] named *Thousand Madleens to Gaza* and *Conscience*.[2] In a statement posted on X, the Global Sumud Flotilla said that "at roughly 120nms away from Gaza, Israel has attacked our flotilla," that at least two boats had been boarded, that most livestreams had been disconnected, and that the military was trying to divert their route.[2] Coverage of the episode carried the activist refrain that "pirates are here again."[2]
