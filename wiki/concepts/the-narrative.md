# The Narrative

**The Narrative** is a recurring [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program released in episodic form and referenced within the Badlands Substack ecosystem as a companion to the outlet's daily written output.[1]

## Relationship to The Blitz

Episodes of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly recap and dissection of the Monday-through-Friday [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands Brief]], are used in part to preview forthcoming instalments of The Narrative.[1] In the sixteenth episode of The Blitz, published 9 May 2026 under the title "The White Pill Parade Is Only Just Beginning," co-hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] previewed the next episode of The Narrative by revisiting the preceding week's reporting.[1]

## Framing

The framing used for that preview was what the hosts described as the long, sometimes-slow and now rapidly accelerating march for American justice and accountability.[1] In their account, the week's developments amounted to substantial winning on multiple fronts, all of which appeared to be converging ahead of a 2026 inflection point.[1]

## Access

Like all Badlands content, including The Blitz, The Narrative is presented as free to the audience, with the production team funded by readers who voluntarily upgrade to a paid subscription to the Badlands Substack newsletter.[1]
