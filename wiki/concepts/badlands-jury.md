# Badlands Jury

The Badlands Jury is a civic-education experience created for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] events.

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that contributors [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[jonathan-drake|Jonathan Drake]] debuted the Badlands Jury at the recent Deadwood [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] "to smash reviews." The premise of the fictitious case was that Badlands was indicted for violating the "Electoral Confidence Preservation Act," a made-up law that criminalized doubting the delivery mechanism of democracy.

Ashe in America uses the construct as a lens on real-world politics: she compares the language of the [[shield-of-the-americas|Shield of the Americas]] joint statement on Colombia — declaring elections the sole foundation of legitimate government — to the fake law, arguing that "if elections are fake, then those holding public power are illegitimate" and that discrediting electoral authorities "with basis" requires public hearings.
