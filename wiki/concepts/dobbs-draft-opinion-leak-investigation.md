# Dobbs Draft Opinion Leak Investigation

The **Dobbs draft opinion leak investigation** was an internal inquiry conducted by the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]] into the disclosure to [[politico|POLITICO]] on May 2, 2022, of a draft majority opinion in *Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization*, the case that overturned the constitutional right to abortion.[1] The Court released a public report on the investigation on January 19, 2023.[1]

## Findings

The report concluded that investigators had been "unable to identify a person responsible by a preponderance of the evidence."[1] The inquiry involved 126 interviews of 97 personnel; devices were collected and analysed; employees provided call and text logs; internet search history was obtained from service providers; and printer logs, operating systems, and fingerprints were examined, while connections between employees and journalists were scrutinised.[1]

The Court stated that while investigators and its IT experts "cannot absolutely rule out a hack, the evidence to date reveals no suggestion of improper outside access," and that the possibility the draft opinion was inadvertently or negligently disclosed could not be eliminated.[1] If the leak was intentional, the report said, "that individual was evidently able to act without being detected by any of the Court's IT systems."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] analyst [[just-human|Just Human]] advanced the interpretation that the draft had been leaked deliberately by "patriots" or "white hats," so that the potentially violent reaction of left-wing groups could be assessed and defused ahead of the Court's formal release of the opinion in June 2022.[1]
