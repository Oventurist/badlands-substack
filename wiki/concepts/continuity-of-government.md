# Continuity of Government

Continuity of Government (COG) refers to protocols that preserve a state's decision-making capacity through decapitation, invasion or catastrophe by devolving authority away from its nominal head.[1]

## Iranian application

Badlands commentary applies the concept to [[iran|Iran]]. Commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] states his understanding that [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]] of the National Supreme Security Council implemented a COG plan in August 2025, removing the Ayatollah from the chain of command and making the office an "irrelevant figure head."[1]

The claim is the load-bearing element of the corpus's reading of the 2026 succession crisis: because authority had already devolved before [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]]'s death in the February 28, 2026 airstrike, the question of whether his successor [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] is alive, visible, or merely a [[cardboard-ayatollah|cardboard cutout]] is, on this account, strategically irrelevant — however much it dominates coverage.[1]

## American parallel

The same corpus applies a parallel logic to the United States through Jon Herold's Devolution research, which [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invokes in describing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s control of the U.S. military from 2017 through 2026.[1]
