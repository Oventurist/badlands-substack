# Continuity of Government

**Continuity of Government (COG)** refers, in its conventional sense, to contingency planning intended to preserve essential functions of the state during catastrophic disruption. Within Badlands Media commentary the term is used in a more expansive sense: as the operative condition the United States is presently understood to be in, and as the structural companion to [[devolution|Devolution]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## Purpose as described in Badlands commentary

Writers argue that if the country is in a Continuity of Government plan "for a reason," part of that reason is to provoke an awakening among the reachable middle of American society — those who have not yet joined the awakened or awakening ranks of the movement.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed) On this reading, the deliberate visibility of institutional failure serves a pedagogical function: the exposure of the Fiat Central Banking system led by the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], of an international cabal of globalist planners, and of the election systems themselves is treated as a planned unveiling before an increasingly weary and wary citizenry.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## Connection to the 2024 vote

Commentary on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s November 2022 announcement noted his remark that "the voting will be much, much different in 2024," alongside a warning that things would get "much worse" before people "see clearly."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed) Badlands writers read both statements as consistent with the precipice theories they had advanced for several years, and as compatible with — rather than contradicting — a COG framework, since a restored nation is held to require the mandate of a substantial majority of the population.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/all-assets-deployed)

## Venezuela, 2026

Venezuela's Supreme Court invoked continuity of government when it ordered Vice President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] to assume the acting presidency "in the absence" of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] following his detention by the United States.[2] [[nicolas-maduro-guerra|Nicolás Maduro Guerra]] publicly affirmed at a rally that COG was in effect and that the government was following directions left by his father.[2] Badlands writers treat the episode as a negotiated COG contingency plan analogous to [[devolution|Devolution]], described in detail as [[venezuelan-devolution|Venezuelan Devolution]].[2]

## Invocation in the Title 42 debate (2023)

Commenting on the May 2023 expiration of [[title-42|Title 42]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited the Continuity of Government thesis to explain why the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration appeared to be strengthening rather than dismantling Trump-era border restrictions.[5] He acknowledged that those who believe such a plan is in place are accused of "hopium" in light of what he called the Regime's destructive agenda, but argued it is possible to hold a rational conviction of the former while acknowledging, tracking and preparing for the latter — summarizing the posture with the warning that an enemy in a "Death Spiral" will still produce a "Death Blossom" on the way down.[5]
