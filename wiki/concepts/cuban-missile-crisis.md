# Cuban Missile Crisis

The Cuban Missile Crisis was the 1962 nuclear confrontation between the United States and the Soviet Union, cited in Badlands Media commentary as the archetypal example of leaders using private back-channel communication to resolve a crisis outside official diplomatic and media scrutiny.[1]

## Use as a precedent for back-channel diplomacy

In "The Master Builder," Joe Lange argues that dismantling a global debt system requires secrecy, and that the method available to leaders is back-channel communication "in a manner akin to what JFK did during the Cuban Missile Crisis with the Soviet Union," supplemented by secret face-to-face meetings with other world leaders.[1] The comparison is used to justify the article's reading of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s undocumented private meetings with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and of Beijing's search for a private channel to Trump through [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]].[1] The reference invokes [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] as a president who bypassed his own bureaucracy in a moment of existential danger.[1]
