# Forbidden City

The Forbidden City is the historic palace complex in Beijing that housed Chinese emperors and their families for almost five hundred years, and which served as the setting for an unusual diplomatic honor extended to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in November 2017.[1]

## The 2017 state dinner

CNN reported, in coverage quoted by Badlands Media, that Trump would receive an honor in Beijing not granted to any U.S. president since the founding of the People's Republic of China: an official dinner inside the Forbidden City.[1] On arriving in the Chinese capital, Trump and [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] joined [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and his wife Peng Liyuan for a tour of the palace.[1] Ambassador [[cui-tiankai|Cui Tiankai]] had promised what he called a "state visit-plus," and one commentator quoted in the coverage said Xi was "going to treat Trump almost like an emperor."[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands Media commentary

Joe Lange's "The Master Builder" treats the Forbidden City dinner and the accompanying red-carpet treatment as more than ceremony, reading it as a signal of an emerging alignment between Xi and Trump against the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1] The article pairs the ceremonial honors with the reported search for a private communications channel to Trump through [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], arguing that the public pageantry concealed a more consequential private understanding.[1]
