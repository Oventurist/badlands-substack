# Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Treaty (Russia–Iran)

The Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Treaty is a bilateral agreement between the Russian Federation and the Islamic Republic of Iran that formalizes the two states as strategic allies across military, transport, energy and diplomatic domains.[1] The text was finalized in June 2024 after editorial work that had lasted roughly two and a half years, making it a long-telegraphed rather than sudden development.[1]

## Signature decree

On Wednesday, September 18, 2024, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] accepted a proposal from the [[russian-foreign-ministry|Russian Foreign Ministry]] to sign the treaty, according to a decree published on Russia's official website for legal information.[1] The decree stated that the ministry's proposal had been "agreed with the interested federal government agencies and organizations," and that it was "advisable to realize the signing of the treaty 'at the highest level'" — that is, by the heads of state themselves.[1] Putin further authorized the Foreign Ministry to make changes to the draft during negotiations provided those changes did not alter the document's general points.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands writers treated the timing of the decree as significant rather than coincidental, arriving in the same news cycle as [[fbi|FBI]] claims that Iranian hackers had passed stolen [[donald-trump|Trump]] campaign material to the Biden–Harris campaign.[1] The commentary argued that because [[russia|Russia]] and [[iran|Iran]] were now formally "strategic allies," any alleged Iranian assistance to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] could be rhetorically reframed as Russian assistance — described as "a total inversion of the Russia, Russia, Russia psyop."[1] The authors emphasized that the treaty had been publicly negotiated for years and was reported in the press months earlier, so the Deep State could not credibly present it as a surprise.[1]

The partnership was also read through the lens of the Middle East escalation of September 2024, with the presence of [[russia|Russian]] military personnel and the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] alongside [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] cited as the true deterrent restraining an [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] ground invasion of Lebanon.[1]
