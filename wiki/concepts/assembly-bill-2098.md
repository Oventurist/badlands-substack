# Assembly Bill 2098

Assembly Bill 2098 (AB 2098) was a California state law empowering medical regulators to discipline physicians whose statements about COVID-19 departed from the "contemporary scientific consensus." It was blocked by a preliminary injunction in January 2023.[1]

## Provisions

Under AB 2098, the [[medical-board-of-california|Medical Board of California]] and the Osteopathic Medical Board of California could discipline physicians who "disseminate" information about COVID-19 that is not in line with the "contemporary scientific consensus."[1] The law was set to take effect on January 1, 2023.[1]

## Preliminary injunction

A California judge issued a preliminary injunction against the law, halting enforcement while the statute was challenged in court.[1] Coverage of the ruling emphasized that the decision also established the plaintiffs' standing — significant because an earlier challenge to AB 2098 had been dismissed for lack of standing.[1]

## Commentary

[[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] described AB 2098 as a bill passed and signed into law by [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] against "medical misinformation" that strictly forbade doctors from recommending against COVID vaccination and required them to stick to treatment protocol guidelines, with violations potentially costing physicians their medical licenses.[1] He argued the law violated patient–doctor confidentiality, the right to try, and the First Amendment, calling California "an Orwellian nightmare of continued forcible medical tyranny" and the litigation "a must win," lest a precedent be set that would "forever destroy the relationship between medical care providers and the people they serve."[1]
