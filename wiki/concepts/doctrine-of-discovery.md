# Doctrine of Discovery

The **Doctrine of Discovery** is the legal principle by which a discovering power acquires rights over land it claims to have discovered. Badlands Media cites it as a historical instance of ruling-class delusion translated into a system of domination.[1]

## Badlands treatment

In [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s January 20, 2023 essay on [[mass-psychosis]], the doctrine is introduced immediately after the papal bull [[unam-sanctam|Unam Sanctam]], which declared that "it is absolutely necessary for salvation that every human creature be subject to the Roman Pontiff."[1] Deschamps wrote that this declaration helped inaugurate the conquest of foreign lands under the Doctrine of Discovery.[1]

## Role in the argument

The example appears in a section arguing that the psychosis of [[totalitarianism]] almost always begins within a society's ruling class, and that bankers, CEOs, politicians and old-money patriarchs are prone to the delusion that they can and should control and dominate an entire society.[1] The doctrine illustrates how a religious ideology, once held by an elite, becomes the legal machinery for subjugating populations abroad — the same pattern the essay attributes in the modern era to communism, fascism and technocracy.[1] Deschamps described this game of domination and control, and of subjugating free thought, free speech and free enterprise, as being as old as human social organization for mutual protection.[1]
