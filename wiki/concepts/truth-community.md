# Truth Community

## Blind spot on artificial intelligence

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the term "Truth Community" in July 2026 to describe the alternative-media audience he addresses, arguing that the fight over [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source artificial intelligence]] is "the topic that almost nobody in the Truth Community is paying attention to right now," despite having more bearing on "alternatively starting or stopping the American Golden Age than almost anything else we spend our time arguing about."[1] He held that members of this audience can be broadly correct in their anxieties about the AI push, given how "serpentine" its core personalities are, while completely missing that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has staked much of his futurism agenda on AI's acceleration.[1]
