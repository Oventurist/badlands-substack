# EU Digital COVID Certificate

The EU Digital COVID-19 Certificate (EU DCC) is the interoperable vaccination, testing, and recovery credential established by the [[european-union|European Union]] during the COVID-19 pandemic to facilitate free movement within its borders.[1]

## Scope and adoption

By 2023, 80 countries and territories were connected to the EU DCC framework.[1] Thierry Breton, the EU Commissioner for Internal Market, said the certificate had "set a global standard," describing it as both an important tool in the pandemic response and a facilitator of international travel and tourism.[1]

## Basis for the WHO global network

The [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] agreed to build a global digital health certification network on the EU DCC's framework, principles, and open technologies, forming the technical foundation of the emerging [[digital-vaccine-passport|digital vaccine passport]] system.[1] Breton said he was pleased that the WHO would build on what he called the "privacy-preserving principles and cutting-edge technology" of the EU certificate to create a global tool against future pandemics.[1]

## Criticism

Badlands writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the EU DCC's extension into a WHO-run global system serves to normalize the marriage of biometric data to digital identification and to cement "public health" in the mass mind as a conduit for superseding national and personal sovereignty.[1]
