# FBI Subpoenaed Document Production to the House Judiciary Committee

In March 2025 the [[fbi|FBI]] produced hundreds of pages of subpoenaed material to the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] related to past Republican investigations, records committee members said they had never received from the Biden administration under Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]].[1]

## Contents of the production

An FBI assistant director wrote in a letter obtained by the Washington Examiner to the Republican-led committee that the tranche of documents concerned the bureau's inquiries into threats to school administrators, the pipe bombs discovered near the Capitol on January 6, 2021, and violent extremists' alleged ties to a faction of Catholicism.[1] The material also included records of the FBI's engagement with social media companies about foreign interference in the 2020 election.[1]
