# The Hero with a Thousand Faces

*The Hero with a Thousand Faces* is the book by [[joseph-campbell|Joseph Campbell]] described in Badlands Media commentary as the book many consider to be the primary text on the subject of the [[heros-journey|Hero's Journey]].[1]

## The quoted formula

The April 25, 2023 essay "The Significance of White Squall" by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] cites Campbell's summary of the monomyth: "A hero ventures forth from the world of common day into a region of supernatural wonder: fabulous forces are there encountered, and a decisive victory is won; the hero comes back from this mysterious adventure with the power to bestow boons on his fellow man."[1]

## Interpretation in Q commentary

From this formula the essay draws several points: that no hero's journey can take place within a comfort zone or familiar territory, and that the unknown is necessarily imbued with supernatural or magical qualities by virtue of its unfamiliarity.[1] The journey is undertaken because a compelling reason has presented itself — the "call" to adventure — and in some cases the hero must transgress the norms of their society to answer it, while in others the hero finds themselves on the journey without fully understanding how they got there.[1]

The framework is then applied to the film [[white-squall|White Squall]], which the essay treats as an unusual case in which the entire crew together with its leader [[skipper-sheldon|Skipper Sheldon]] constitutes the hero, and by extension to the situation of [[donald-trump|Trump]], [[qanon|Q]] and the [[anons|anons]].[1]
