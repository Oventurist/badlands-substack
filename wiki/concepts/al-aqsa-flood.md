# Al-Aqsa Flood

"Al-Aqsa Flood" is the operational name given by [[hamas|Hamas]] to its October 7, 2023 assault on southern Israel, the event that triggered the ensuing war in [[gaza|Gaza]].[1]

## Foreknowledge question

The Badlands Media essay "Our 'Greatest Ally'" argues that a fear of being labelled has suppressed public discussion of who may have possessed advance knowledge of the operation, noting reporting that Israeli officials had obtained Hamas's battle plan before the attack.[1] The author regards the "intelligence failure" explanation as difficult to accept, since it would require that [[mossad|MOSSAD]] — described in the article as arguably the most sophisticated intelligence service on earth — and the entire U.S. intelligence community, including the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], ODNI, DIA, NSA and DoD, all failed to detect preparations by a comparatively unsophisticated militant group.[1]

That skepticism is reinforced in the article by Gaza's status as one of the most heavily surveilled territories in the world, monitored in part through the [[gaza-reconstruction-mechanism|Gaza Reconstruction Mechanism]] and associated networks.[1] The essay concedes the foreknowledge thesis is speculation, while maintaining that aspects of the [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] government's subsequent response are not open to interpretation.[1]

## Framing

The article situates Al-Aqsa Flood within a broader claim that both Israelis and Palestinians have been "sold out" by an intelligence apparatus and [[deep-state|Deep State]] that benefits from polarization, and that the common denominator across the IDF, Hamas and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] is that apparatus rather than any one side's righteousness.[1]
