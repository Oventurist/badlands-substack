Within [[badlands-media]], central banking is portrayed critically as a corrupt system contrasted with decentralized alternatives such as cryptocurrency.[1] The network's Rugpull Radio show (Thursday, 10:30pm ET), hosted by GMoney and Patriots in Progress, educates viewers on "both the benefits and dangers of Bitcoin/Blockchain technology and the cryptocurrency space," then "shine[s] a light on the corrupt Central Banking system."[1]

## Q drops #135–138 on Rothschild-owned central banks

Chapter 22 of the Badlands "Q for Dummies" series covers drops #135 through #138 of November 11, 2017, in which Q lists what the author describes as all the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild]]-owned central banks in the world.[2] The author treats the list as self-explanatory given Q's earlier framing of the Rothschilds as one of the three sides of a pyramid controlling governments worldwide, and draws the operational conclusion: "Remove the Central Banks, and you remove [their] power."[2]

The chapter's proposed mechanism for that removal is decentralization of money — specifically [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] — with the author acknowledging that some readers will dislike the argument and directing them to [[rugpull-radio|Rugpull Radio]] and to research by the Substack writers Patriots In Progress and GMONEY.[2] As supporting evidence that central bank authority is already eroding, the chapter cites a 2022 Politico article on central banks facing a credibility crisis as losses mount at the European Central Bank and the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[2]
