# 2024 Kazan Drone Attack

The 2024 Kazan drone attack was a major Ukrainian drone strike on the Russian city of [[kazan|Kazan]], carried out on Saturday 21 December 2024, roughly 1,000 kilometres (620 miles) from the frontline of the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## The attack

Drones damaged residential buildings and forced the temporary closure of Kazan's airport in a city of more than 1.3 million people.[1] One drone smashed into a high-rise apartment block and another damaged a skyscraper; local officials reported no casualties.[1] Videos posted on Russian social media showed drones striking a high-rise building and setting off fireballs.[1] Russian foreign ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova said two drones hit a 37-storey apartment block and that Ukraine had been targeting an unspecified industrial facility, which suffered no damage.[1] Alongside the drones that hit the apartment block, three drones were shot down and three were suppressed by air-defence systems.[1]

Some Kazan residents were evacuated — Russian authorities did not release figures — and all major public events in the area were cancelled as a precaution.[1] The civil aviation authority Rosaviatsia temporarily closed Kazan international airport, one of the country's busiest, reopening it later the same day.[1]

Ukrainian president [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] said on the day of the attack, without specifically mentioning Kazan, that "we will definitely continue to strike at Russian military targets with drones and missiles."[1] The strike came a day after Russian attacks on Kyiv killed one person and wounded thirteen, and after five people were killed by a Ukrainian attack on the Russian border region of Kursk.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterised the strike as evidence that [[nato|NATO]] "is getting desperate," flying aircraft into residential skyscrapers more than 500 miles from the front, and described the footage as depicting "9/11-style attacks" — citing commentary by [[pepe-escobar|Pepe Escobar]] on [[andrew-napolitano|Judge Andrew Napolitano]]'s programme.[1] He connected the target city to the [[2024-brics-kazan-summit|BRICS summit held in Kazan]] in October 2024, and argued that Kiev and Tel Aviv were deploying provocations intended to bait the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] into an escalation toward world war.[1] In his assessment, Vladimir Putin was demonstrating "Taoist-like patience," and the restraint shown by world powers was an encouraging sign that the Sovereign Alliance is "very real, and very calculated."[1]
