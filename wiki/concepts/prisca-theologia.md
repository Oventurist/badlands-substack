# Prisca theologia

Prisca theologia is the doctrine that a single, true theology exists which threads through all religions, and which was given by God to man in antiquity and passed down through a series of prophets.[1] The doctrine is described in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]].[1]

Many Christian writers — including Lactantius, Augustine, [[giordano-bruno|Giordano Bruno]], [[marsilio-ficino|Marsilio Ficino]], Campanella and Giovanni Pico della Mirandola — considered [[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]] a wise pagan prophet who foresaw the coming of Christianity, and counted him among the prophets of the prisca theologia alongside Zoroaster and Plato.[1] In order to demonstrate the verity of the prisca theologia, Christians appropriated the Hermetic teachings for their own purposes.[1] On this account Hermes Trismegistus was either a contemporary of Moses or the third in a line of men named Hermes — Enoch, Noah, and the Egyptian priest-king known as Trismegistus.[1]
