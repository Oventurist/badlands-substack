# Badlands Blitz Episode 26

**Badlands Blitz Episode 26**, subtitled "On Colombian Hobbits, MAGA Ents and the Global Golden Age", is an installment of the weekly recap program produced by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and published to the Badlands Substack on July 25, 2026. At the time of archiving the post had recorded 124 likes. As with earlier editions, the announcement describes the program as "a weekly recap and dissection of the work we do Monday through Friday in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]," the outlet's weekday newsletter.

## Format and hosts

The episode is hosted by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], a pairing that alternates with other host combinations across the [[the-blitz|Blitz]] series. Both are identified in the announcement by their Substack profiles.

## Subject matter

According to the source, the hosts "discuss the advent of the [[global-golden-age|Global Golden Age]] from some unlikely angles," taking what the announcement calls "a circuitous route from American [[remigration|remigration]] to peace in the Middle East." The framing is characteristic of the program's method: rather than treating stories in isolation, the hosts trace a connective thread from domestic demographic and immigration policy through to geopolitical settlement abroad, presenting both as facets of a single unfolding transition.

The subtitle's whimsical imagery — "Colombian Hobbits" and "MAGA Ents" — is flagged in the body text as genuine rather than decorative: "And yes, we really do talk about Colombian Hobbits and MAGA Ents. You're welcome." The Tolkien allusions serve as shorthand for the episode's discursive, allegorical register, in which the hosts habitually reach for mythic and literary analogy to describe political actors and movements. The announcement provides no further elaboration on the content of either metaphor.

## Publishing model

The post closes with the outlet's standard subscription appeal. It affirms that "all Badlands content will continue to be free, including The Blitz," while noting that the team can only pay itself through direct reader support, and invites readers to upgrade to a paid subscription, "cancel at any time." The reference to "the step-up in said work for 2026" recurs from earlier episodes, indicating an expanded editorial cadence for the year. The article ends with the series sign-off: "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright!"
