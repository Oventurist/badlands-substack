# Republican Mail Ballot Deadline Lawsuits

In late 2023 and early 2024, Republicans challenged extended mail ballot receipt deadlines in at least two states, a legal maneuver with potentially widespread implications for mail voting ahead of the November 2024 presidential election.[1]

## The cases

A lawsuit filed in Mississippi in late January 2024 followed a similar action brought the previous year in North Dakota.[1] Both were filed in heavily Republican states before conservative federal courts.[1] The suits argued that state deadlines allowing mailed ballots to be received after Election Day, 5 November, violate federal law.[1]

## Potential consequences

Democratic and voting rights groups expressed concern about the impact beyond the two states in question, suggesting that a favorable ruling for the plaintiffs could produce a nationwide injunction comparable to the order in which a Texas judge temporarily paused the Food and Drug Administration's approval of the abortion pill mifepristone.[1] "This effort risks disenfranchising Mississippi voters, but we don't want that to also be precedent for other states," said Abhi Rahman, communications director of the Democratic Legislative Campaign Committee, in response to the Mississippi filing.[1]
