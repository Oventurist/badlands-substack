# 2023 Black Friday Retail Sales

The 2023 Black Friday shopping day, held in late November 2023, produced headline records in online spending but fell short of expectations for many major retailers, prompting ZeroHedge to characterize it as a "dud."[1]

## Bloomberg Second Measure findings

According to an analysis of Bloomberg Second Measure transaction data — derived from a sample of millions of U.S. credit and debit card users — the median decline in Black Friday sales was about 4% across a basket of 40 companies that draw a higher share of year-to-date sales from the shopping holiday than their peers.[1] The drop was sharper than that recorded for Black Friday 2022.[1]

Bloomberg noted that Black Friday represented more than five times an average day's worth of observed U.S. direct-to-consumer sales for the group, the highest level since 2019 — meaning those retailers were relying more heavily on a single shopping day even as the number of bargain hunters they attracted dwindled.[1]

## Interpretation

Adobe Analytics data showed online Black Friday spending up 7.5% year over year, and record online sales were fueled in part by "Buy Now, Pay Later" financing options.[1] The divergence between that figure and the Bloomberg data was read as evidence of consumers trading down toward discount retailers and shifting spending toward experiences and travel.[1] Consistent with that reading, Bloomberg Second Measure recorded rising sales at discounters including [[walmart|Walmart]], TJ Maxx, and Marshalls, while [[amazon|Amazon]] touted "record-breaking" sales to open the holiday season.[1]
