# Q Drops

The **Q Drops** are the anonymous coded messages associated with the [[qanon|Q Operation]], which first began appearing in the waning days of 2017.[1] They are the source in which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] first encountered the twin refrain "The News is fake. The War is real," although he has stated he does not know whether the phrases originated there.[1]

## Assessment in Badlands commentary

Burning Bright notes that responses to and impressions of the Q Drops range from staunch adherence, through a curious but agnostic middle ground, to derisive dismissal.[1] He argues that whatever one's verdict on their veracity, the drops cannot be disputed as representing, for better or worse, one of the most ubiquitous and impactful psychological operations in American — and perhaps world — history.[1]

## The twin refrain

The paired lines drawn from the drops form the basis of the [[unreality-paradox|Unreality Paradox]], the frame in which the manufactured character of the news is itself treated as an operational element of a genuine [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation]] conflict.[1] Rather than adjudicating the legitimacy of the Q Operation, Burning Bright passes the refrain "through my own cognitive filter" in order to expose that paradox and help readers orient themselves intellectually and emotionally within what he calls the modern Information Blitzkrieg.[1] He suggests the apparent contradiction in the two lines was highlighted deliberately by minds with greater knowledge of the war's foundational truth than ordinary observers possess.[1]

## Volume, chronology and terminal drops

The corpus comprises 4,958 posts made between October 28, 2017 and December 8, 2020, first on [[4chan|4chan]] and later on 8chan, after which the account fell silent for eighteen months.[2] Posting resumed on June 24, 2022 — the day [[roe-v-wade|Roe v. Wade]] was overturned, and 1,700 days after the first drop, a figure read as significant because Q is the seventeenth letter of the alphabet — and continued for six months until a final post on November 27, 2022 whose digits also sum to seventeen and whose last word was "ascension."[2] Although the drops sometimes appeared several times a day, gaps of days or weeks were common, and the material reached most readers not on the imageboards themselves but through decoders republishing it on YouTube and Twitter.[2]

Individual drops are frequently treated as load-bearing evidence. Drop 888, dated March 8, 2018, reads "Thank you Kim. Deal made. Clowns out. Strings cut. We took control. Iran next." and is cited as an assertion that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] had created and controlled [[north-korea|North Korea]] as a manufactured threat, with those strings severed a year into [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s presidency and Iran identified as the next such case.[2] The phrase "dark to light" appears thirty-three times across the drops, and the resemblance of DPRK to DARK has been read as a deliberate marker of that transition.[2]
