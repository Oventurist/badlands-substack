# Megatrends

"Megatrends" is the label Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] applies to a body of globalist forecasting circulated around 2015 that projected the major structural disruptions expected to reshape the coming decades.[1]

## Content of the forecasts

One prominent lane concerned pandemic preparedness — the anticipation of a pandemic that would disrupt global business — a theme Ashe in America has written about previously.[1] Other Megatrends focused on disruption to talent markets, with white-collar globalist authors producing extensive literature on how artificial intelligence and machine learning would displace significant portions of the workforce across industry sectors.[1]

## Assessment

In Ashe in America's reading, the forecasters were correct about the direction of travel but had a blind spot for the impact on their own class, a point she raised in connection with [[meta-platforms|Meta]]'s 2026 layoffs of roughly 8,000 workers as [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] accelerated the company's AI pivot.[1] Her one-word conclusion on the trend was "Accelerate."[1]
