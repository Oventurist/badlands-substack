# 1984 Los Angeles Olympics

The 1984 Summer Olympics were hosted by the United States in Los Angeles during the presidency of [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that the event brought with it a resurgence of American pride throughout the nation, and treats it as part of the broader Reagan-era restoration of national confidence.[1]

## Parallel to the Trump era

Carlson pairs the 1984 games with the [[2028-los-angeles-olympics|2028 Los Angeles Olympics]], which the same city will host during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s presidency, asking rhetorically "what are the odds?" and offering the repetition as one of the many Reagan–Trump correspondences he catalogues.[1]
