# Gaza Aid Convoy Killings (February 2024)

The **Gaza aid convoy killings** were an incident on Thursday, February 29, 2024 in which more than 100 Palestinians were killed near aid trucks in [[gaza|Gaza]].[1]

## The incident

Health authorities in Gaza said the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] fired on people waiting for aid near Gaza City on Thursday, killing 104 Palestinians and wounding 280, with one hospital saying it had received 10 bodies and dozens of injured patients, according to Reuters.[1]

## Biden's response

Speaking outside the [[white-house|White House]] on the south lawn as he departed for a visit to the US–Mexico border, President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] said he was reviewing reports of Israeli troops shooting people crowding for desperately needed food aid in Gaza, but was already clear that the deadly incident would complicate talks on a [[gaza-ceasefire|ceasefire]].[1] Asked by reporters whether the tragedy unfolding in Gaza would complicate negotiations, the president replied, "I know it will."[1]
