# Post-Hypnotic Triggers

A **post-hypnotic trigger** is, in the definition offered by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] in the [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series, a stimulus that has been associated with an intense experience such that presenting the stimulus again re-invokes that same experience in the same person.[1] The author compresses the mechanism into a single sentence — associate a stimulus with an intense experience and you can re-invoke the experience with the stimulus — and adds that because every mind is unique, an anchoring process that works on one person may not work on another; subconscious association is described as "the name of the game," with everything else being detail.[1]

## Intentional and incidental formation

Triggers can be installed deliberately by a knowledgeable hypnotist, but the author argues that more often life itself installs them.[1] Phobias are given as the standard example, characterised as over-zealous "safety" programs instantiated by the subconscious mind; he recounts developing an irrationally strong fear of heights after walking across the top of a waterfall as a young man and nearly falling a hundred feet onto jagged rock.[1] PTSD is described as working in a very similar manner, with an intense traumatic experience followed by triggered re-invocation.[1]

## Slow-formed triggers and media

Triggers are also said to develop through prolonged contact with demoralising people, situations and places — a mechanism the author attributes to the [[mockingbird-media|mainstream media]].[1] His prescribed remedy is to thoroughly discredit such sources in one's own mind using logic and empirical evidence, which he claims alleviates almost all media-induced demoralisation.[1]

## Extension to identity and to spirits

Because the author treats entire personalities as switchable by trigger, he presents [[dissociative-identity-disorder|dissociated identity]] as an extreme case of the same process, and connects it to the conditioning attributed to the [[koryos|Koryos]] and to [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]].[1] He further argues that such conditioning affects the dead as well as the living, so that triggers installed in a sacrificed child could remain active as [[earthbound-spirits|earthbound spirits]] — a proposition he regards as evidence that hypnosis works directly on the mind.[1] The article carries an explicit disclaimer that it is opinion and not professional medical or psychological advice.[1]
