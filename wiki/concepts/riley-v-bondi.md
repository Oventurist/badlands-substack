# Riley v. Bondi

Riley v. Bondi is a case argued before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] in March 2025 in which petitioner Pierre Riley invoked the Convention Against Torture — after the statutory deadline — in an effort to avoid deportation to Jamaica.[1]

## Coverage

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] covered the argument in the Badlands News Brief's running summary of the Supreme Court's March 2025 sitting, grouping it with the consolidated Louisiana redistricting cases and the week's [[2025-supreme-court-epa-venue-cases|EPA venue arguments]].[1] The Badlands framing emphasized the procedural question of a late-raised claim rather than the underlying torture allegations.[1]
