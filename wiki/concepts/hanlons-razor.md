# Hanlon's Razor

**Hanlon's Razor** is the aphorism "Never ascribe to malice that which is adequately explained by incompetence."[1] Ryan DeLarme opened "The WHO's Last Stand" with the maxim in order to reject it as applied to COVID-19, using it as a rhetorical frame for the article's central argument.[1]

## Use in Badlands Media analysis

As DeLarme summarized it, Hanlon's Razor suggests that at any given point in history there is no malicious ruling class of unelected leaders conspiring behind the scenes, and that all strife in human history results from the pure chaos of incompetence in high places.[1] Applied to COVID-19 and its response, he argued, the maxim would require accepting that the pandemic was "the most lucrative act of incompetence in human history," given the staggering consolidation of wealth and power that followed.[1]

That consolidation, he wrote, has fueled widespread speculation that the pandemic was no accident at all — a line of reasoning the article uses to move from Hanlon's Razor toward an intentionality thesis about the [[pandemic-industrial-complex|Pandemic Industrial Complex]] and the governance instruments pursued by the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1]
