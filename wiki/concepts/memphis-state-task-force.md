# Memphis Safe Task Force

The "Memphis State Task Force" — also referred to as the Memphis Safe Task Force — is a federal interagency crime-fighting body created by executive order of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Monday, 15 September 2025, to combat violent crime in [[memphis|Memphis]], Tennessee.[1]

## Composition and mandate

The task force was announced as part of a broader extension of federal law enforcement action to Memphis that also involved deploying [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops to the city.[1] Its membership was to include the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]], the [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]], [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] and the [[us-marshals-service|U.S. Marshals Service]].[1] The executive order did not set out a timeline for when the task force would be deployed.[1]

Trump characterized the task force as a "replica" of his August 2025 crackdown in Washington, D.C., according to the Associated Press.[1] The order asserted that violent crime in Memphis had "overwhelmed its local government's ability to respond effectively," though local police reported overall offences at a 25-year low.[1]

## Reception

Tennessee governor [[bill-lee|Bill Lee]] endorsed the task force and appeared with Trump at the White House signing ceremony, while the mayor of Memphis said he had not requested the National Guard and did not believe it was the way to drive down crime.[1] Trump had pressed for comparable federal-military policing arrangements in Baltimore and Chicago.[1]
