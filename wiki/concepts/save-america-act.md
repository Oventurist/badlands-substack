# SAVE America Act

The SAVE America Act is federal election-integrity legislation considered by [[congress|Congress]] and treated in the Badlands Media corpus as one of the principal legislative vehicles for tightening voter-eligibility and mail-balloting rules.

## Role in the 2026 election debate

[[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]] presents the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s June 2026 refusal to bar the counting of late-arriving absentee ballots as breathing "new life" into the SAVE America Act and into a companion mail ballot act sponsored by Representative [[clay-higgins|Clay Higgins]]. The logic advanced by commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] is that a Court declining to legislate from the bench necessarily returns the question to elected legislators, making passage of these bills the appropriate object of public pressure.

## Contested standing as an integrity measure

The same edition uses the Act as a marker of what Ashe in America calls establishmentarian "half measures." She criticizes Representative Jeff Crank of Colorado's fifth district for regarding himself as a champion of election integrity on the strength of his vote for the SAVE America Act, while, in her account, offering evasive answers on taxation and campaigning in 2026 chiefly on expansive spending priorities. The page therefore records the Act as both a rallying point for reform advocates and, within Badlands commentary, a credential whose sufficiency is disputed.
