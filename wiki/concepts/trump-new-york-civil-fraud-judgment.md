# Trump New York Civil Fraud Judgment

The Trump New York civil fraud judgment was a ruling issued in Manhattan Supreme Court on Friday, February 16, 2024, imposing a penalty of approximately $355 million on former president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the New York attorney general's civil fraud case concerning the valuation of his business assets.[1]

## Reaction

The size of the judgment provoked immediate backlash among Trump supporters, most visibly in an informal freight boycott of New York City organized over CB radio among long-haul truckers.[1] The trucker and social media influencer known as [[chicago-ray|Chicago Ray]] posted video claiming that drivers he had spoken with would begin refusing loads bound for New York City the following Monday.[1]

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] read the boycott as evidence that working-class Americans now understood the judgment as [[lawfare|lawfare]] — litigation deployed as a cover for an operation tied to election interference — rather than as an ordinary commercial ruling.[1] He compared the mobilization to Canada's [[freedom-convoy|Freedom Convoy]] and argued that such worker-led uprisings against elite power were occurring worldwide but were being carried out by avowedly anti-communist citizens, a fact he said left Marxist analysts unable to account for them.[1]
