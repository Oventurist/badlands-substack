# FTC v. Amazon Antitrust Lawsuit

The FTC v. Amazon antitrust lawsuit is a landmark monopolization case filed on 26 September 2023 by the United States government and 17 states against [[amazon|Amazon]], reflecting years of allegations that the e-commerce company abused its economic dominance and harmed fair competition.[1]

## The complaint

The suit was brought by the [[federal-trade-commission|Federal Trade Commission]] together with 17 state attorneys general and represented the government's sharpest attack to date on a company that began by selling books online and grew into "the everything store," expanding into a vast range of consumer products, a globe-spanning logistics network, and cloud computing.[1]

The 172-page complaint alleges that Amazon unfairly promotes its own platform and services at the expense of the third-party sellers who depend on its marketplace for distribution.[1] According to the FTC, Amazon harmed competition by requiring sellers to purchase its in-house logistics services in order to obtain the best seller benefits — "Prime" eligibility — and by anticompetitively forcing sellers to list products on Amazon at the lowest prices available anywhere on the web rather than offering lower prices on competing marketplaces.[1] That latter practice was already the subject of a separate lawsuit filed against Amazon by California's attorney general the previous year.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that the Amazon action followed an ongoing antitrust suit brought by the [[justice-department|Department of Justice]] against [[google|Google]] over monopolistic practices.[1] He argued that a restoration of American prosperity and sovereignty through a free market required that "the Deep State's monopolistic market-makers" be taken down, and read the simultaneous sieges on the Administrative State and American oligarchy — at a moment when they should have been enjoying unprecedented power — as a sign that those actors were "NOT in control."[1] He tied this to "The Switch" theory, which posits that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[agenda-47|Agenda 47]] was already underway.[1]
