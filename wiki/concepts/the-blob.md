# The Blob

"The Blob" is the term, attributed by Badlands Media to [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]], for the sprawling Western foreign policy and national security apparatus — what the article calls the "monstrosity" of names and acronyms that accompanies any study of the field.[1]

The article invokes the Blob to describe the institutional continuity linking the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]], the [[cia|CIA]], the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], and the [[state-department|State Department]] across administrations.[1] It describes the CIA and State Department together as a "two-headed dragon" behind cookie-cutter regime change operations using proxy armies, and says the Blob's standard method is to slander anyone seeking compromise as a "Russian agent" — as happened to [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]].[1] In the Ukraine case, the article holds that the State Department and its NGO blob orchestrated a color revolution and regime change in 2014.[1]
