# Military Industrial Complex

The Military Industrial Complex, paired throughout "A Bright Red Line" with the U.S. security state, is the article's name for the network of interests it holds responsible for instigating and sustaining American wars and for controlling their public explanation.[1]

The article argues that with Iraq, Afghanistan and nearly every other war of recent decades, the public is given only the explanation that best suits the Military Industrial Complex and the security state.[1] The same applies to Russia: readers are repeatedly told that "Putin is a mad dog" and expected to believe he takes joy in Ukrainian suffering, while a side of the story is denied them, as in every other conflict the complex has instigated.[1]

The article extends the pattern to the suppression of counter-narratives, citing the U.S. government forcing TikTok to remove the circulating Bin Laden letter, and to the [[nato-expansion|expansion of NATO]]: when the security state sets its sights on something it does not let go, but bides its time until the moment is right.[1] Neoconservative adherents are located in the security state, in [[congress|Congress]] and at the [[state-department|State Department]].[1]
