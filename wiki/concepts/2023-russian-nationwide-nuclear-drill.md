# 2023 Russian Nationwide Nuclear Drill

In early October 2023, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] ordered the country's first-ever nationwide exercise simulating widespread nuclear strikes on Russian territory, according to reporting by the Hungarian daily Magyar Hírlap relayed by [[remix-news|Remix News]].[1]

## Scenario

The one-day nuclear attack exercise was built on the assumption that [[nato|NATO]] would launch a nuclear strike on Russia, destroying 70 percent of Russian housing and life-support facilities. In the scenario, martial law is imposed across the country and a full-scale mobilization is ordered.[1] The exercise also modeled the secondary threat posed by damage to nuclear power plants and other key facilities, and required state and regional authorities to organize emergency rescue teams capable of providing food, medical supplies and protection against radiation.[1]

According to the preparatory document, there was a need to prepare for an escalation of war, including a global conflict involving nuclear powers.[1] Reporting at the time also linked the drill to preparations for a nuclear bomb test at the Arctic Circle.[1]
