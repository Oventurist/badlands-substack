# Revisionist Zionism

Revisionist Zionism — referred to in Badlands reporting as "the Revisionist Movement" — is a form of [[zionism|Zionism]] that calls for territorial maximalism on both sides of the Jordan River, with the ambition to annex both Palestine and Jordan as parts of Israel.[1] Its founding figure was [[zeev-jabotinsky|Ze'ev Jabotinsky]].[1]

[[nathan-mileikowsky|Nathan Mileikowsky]], grandfather of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], joined the movement after immigrating to Mandatory Palestine in 1920 and became one of its leading advocates under Jabotinsky; he and his sons affiliated with the [[irgun|Irgun]].[1] The movement's institutional descendants ran from the Irgun through the [[herut-party|Herut Party]] to the [[likud-party|Likud Party]], formed in 1977.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that Netanyahu is "the prodigal son of Ze'ev Jabotinsky and the Revisionist Movement," groomed for his campaign "since before he was born," and that the [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]] map displayed on Irgun propaganda posters is identical to the map that appeared on the podium at which [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] spoke in March 2023.[1]
