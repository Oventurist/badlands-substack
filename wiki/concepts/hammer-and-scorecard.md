# Hammer and Scorecard

"Hammer and Scorecard" is the name given to an alleged US government surveillance and vote-manipulation system said to have been invented by software contractor [[dennis-montgomery|Dennis Montgomery]]. Claims about the system became a central element of post-2020 election-fraud allegations promoted by [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]] and associates, and were examined at length in the defamation trial [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]].[1]

## Claims made in testimony

Montgomery, long treated as a near-mythical figure in election-fraud circles, testified by video deposition in the Coomer trial in June 2025 — appearing on the first day literally wearing a gray mask, and removing it on the second day.[1] In his testimony he claimed to have built a tool allowing the US government to steal elections around the world without detection, and asserted that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] was the main operator of the tool. Badlands noted internal contradictions in the account: he also said he was the only person who knew how to use the tool, and said both that the CIA told him they stole elections and that he personally watched them steal elections.[1]

## Financial entanglements

Testimony surrounding the alleged software also covered payments between Lindell and Montgomery. Lindell was reported in coverage of the trial to have paid $1.5 million for a "secret CIA software program to rig elections," and to have testified that he did more due diligence than anyone in history.[1] Accounts given at trial were inconsistent: Lindell was said to have traded Montgomery an interest in his business for a house in Naples, Florida; that Montgomery bought the house with $1.8 million Lindell gave him for the business; and that Montgomery took the money as a loan. The house is held in Montgomery's wife's trust, named the Gray Horse Family Trust — a name Badlands linked, pointedly, to the pale/grey horse of Revelation 6:8.[1]

## Assessment

Coverage of the claims has been sharply contested. A 2022 Reuters investigative special report profiled Montgomery, and the image used in that report was of the same man who testified in 2025.[1] Independent verification of the existence or capabilities of Hammer and Scorecard has not been established; the trial testimony was presented alongside that of election-security expert [[j-alex-halderman|J. Alex Halderman]].[1]
