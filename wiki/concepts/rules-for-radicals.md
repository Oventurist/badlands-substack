# Rules for Radicals

*Rules for Radicals* is the book by [[saul-alinsky|Saul Alinsky]] cited in Badlands Media's "Q for Dummies" series as the key to understanding the political methods attributed to Alinsky's protégés.[1]

## Use in the Q for Dummies analysis

While unpacking Q Drop #117, the series notes that both [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] were mentored by Alinsky, and argues that reading *Rules for Radicals* makes it "really isn't hard to figure out" what those figures were and are doing to the country.[1] The book is thus treated less as a historical artifact than as an operating manual whose tactics the series sees reflected in contemporary American politics.[1]

The reference appears alongside the drop's other material on Obama's influences, including [[jeremiah-wright|Jeremiah Wright]], [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]], and [[post-american-world|The Post-American World]].[1]
