# Millennials

Millennials are the American generation following [[generation-x|Generation X]], described in a December 2024 Badlands Media essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as being between 28 and 44 years old as of late 2024.[1] Carlson frames Millennials, alongside [[generation-z|Generation Z]], as raised during the era of peak political correctness and of the LGBTQ and [[transgender-movement|transgender movements]].[1]

By his timeline, Millennials were aged 5 to 20 when the September 11 attacks occurred, 16 to 31 when the trans movement began in 2012, and 24 to 39 when the COVID narrative was unleashed in 2020 — meaning each of these events landed during formative or early-adult years.[1] Carlson reports that 11.2% of Millennials identify as LGBTQ+ and 1% as transgender, figures well above those he gives for Gen X and Baby Boomers, and argues the rapid generational jump cannot be genetic.[1]

On technology, Carlson notes that the first iPhone was released in 2007, when Millennials were 11 to 26, and that many Millennials do not remember a time before the internet, contrasting them with Gen X, which he says found a "sweet spot" of technological fluency without dependence.[1] In the 2024 election, he cites 46% of Americans aged 30 to 44 — roughly the Millennial bracket — voting for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], short of the majority recorded among voters aged 45 to 64.[1]
