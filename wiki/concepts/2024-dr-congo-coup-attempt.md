# 2024 DR Congo Coup Attempt

The **2024 DR Congo coup attempt** was a failed effort in May 2024 to overthrow the government of the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]], for which around 50 people — including three Americans, a Belgian, a British national and a Canadian — were charged with criminal conspiracy, murder, terrorism and other offenses.[1]

## Prosecution and sentences

The three Americans, identified as Marcel Malanga Malu, Tylor Thomson and Zalman-Polun Benjamin, were among 40 defendants sentenced to death by a Congolese military court in September 2024.[1] Congolese President [[felix-tshisekedi|Felix Tshisekedi]] subsequently commuted the sentences of Malanga, Thomson and Benjamin to life imprisonment.[1]

## Repatriation to the United States (April 2025)

The DR Congo presidency announced on Tuesday, April 8, 2025 that the three convicted Americans had been returned to the United States to serve out their prison sentences.[1] "The three men… boarded a flight to the United States of America, where they will serve the remainder of their sentences," the presidency stated, describing the move as "part of a dynamic effort to strengthen judicial diplomacy and international cooperation in matters of justice and human rights between the two countries."[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] said he had speculated the previous week on his geopolitical program "Geopolitics with Ghost" that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] might leverage the DR Congo president's request for assistance in resolving the ongoing crisis with [[rwanda|Rwanda]] in exchange for custody of the American-born participants in the failed coup — and concluded that this was exactly what had happened.[1] He characterized the men as representatives of "one of the most evil schemes in modern political history: regime change," argued that none should receive clemency, and noted they would serve their remaining terms in a U.S. prison.[1] In his reading, the practical achievement of the deal was to place the convicted men in the hands of the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], which would then have leverage to extract the truth about who was behind the coup.[1]
