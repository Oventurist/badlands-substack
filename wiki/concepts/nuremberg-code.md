# Nuremberg Code

The Nuremberg Code is the set of research-ethics principles derived from the post-Second World War Nuremberg trials of Nazi doctors, of which the best known is the requirement of voluntary informed consent for medical experimentation. Within the Badlands Media corpus it is invoked chiefly as the standard against which COVID-19 era vaccine mandates are measured.[1]

## Invocation in COVID-19 mandate criticism

In "New to the Great Awakening? Making Sense of It All" (November 18, 2022), [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] described researching "Nuremberg" after her husband's employer threatened to fire him unless he was vaccinated, asking "What happened to informed consent?"[1] She reported that he was in fact fired, and placed the episode among the sequence of events — alongside the pressure campaign accompanying [[operation-warp-speed|Operation Warp Speed]] and the deplatforming of dissenting voices — that convinced her institutions she had trusted had been compromised.[1] The reference frames employer vaccine mandates as a coercion problem in the lineage of the consent violations the Code was written to prevent.[1]
