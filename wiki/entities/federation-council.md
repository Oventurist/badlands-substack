# Federation Council

The Federation Council is the upper house of the Russian parliament, whose responsibilities include consulting on and confirming senior executive appointments proposed by the president.[1]

## The May 2024 cabinet nominations

On Sunday evening, 12 May 2024, the Federation Council announced that President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had proposed replacing [[sergei-shoigu|Sergey Shoigu]] as Russian defense minister with acting First Deputy Prime Minister [[andrey-belousov|Andrey Belousov]], with Shoigu moving to become secretary of the [[russian-security-council|Russian Security Council]].[1]

The upper house announced that senators would hold consultations on the president's nominees during committee sessions on 13 May and at a Federation Council meeting on 14 May 2024.[1] No further alterations were made to the roster of cabinet candidates submitted by Putin, which included [[vladimir-kolokoltsev|Vladimir Kolokoltsev]] for interior minister, Alexander Kurenkov for minister of emergency situations, [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] for foreign minister and Konstantin Chuichenko for justice minister. [[denis-manturov|Denis Manturov]], previously deputy prime minister and head of the Ministry of Industry and Trade, was nominated for first deputy prime minister.[1]

The proceedings were reported by [[rt|RT]] and relayed in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief.[1]
