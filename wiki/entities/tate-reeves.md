# Tate Reeves

**Tate Reeves** is the Republican Governor of [[mississippi|Mississippi]].[1]

## Income tax repeal

In 2025 Reeves signed a controversial bill eliminating Mississippi's personal income tax, positioning the state as the first in the country to phase out an existing income tax entirely.[1] He hailed the measure as a "generational change" that would attract businesses and workers to Mississippi.[1]

The law he signed reduces the 4% income tax rate to 3% by 2031, with further reductions accelerated after a typographical error nullified the revenue-growth safeguards originally written into the bill.[1] Critics warned that the resulting revenue loss — an estimated $2.6 billion from a roughly $7 billion budget — could devastate public services in the nation's poorest state.[1] See [[mississippi-income-tax-elimination|Mississippi income tax elimination]].[1]
