# Alliance Israelite Universelle

The Alliance Israelite Universelle is a French Jewish organization founded in the 19th century. It appears in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account of the origins of [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]] through its head, [[adolphe-cremieux|Adolphe Crémieux]], who was simultaneously Grand Master of the Rite of Misraim and Grand Commander of the Supreme Council of France, overseeing the high degrees of the Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite within the Grand Orient of France.[1]

Crémieux's protégé [[maurice-joly|Maurice Joly]] wrote the 1864 *Dialogue in Hell Between Machiavelli and Montesquieu*, the text later plagiarized wholesale to produce the Protocols forgery.[1]
