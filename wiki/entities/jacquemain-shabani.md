# Jacquemain Shabani

Jacquemain Shabani is the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Interior and Security of the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

Speaking at a press conference in Kinshasa in early September 2025, Shabani accused the [[m23|M23]] rebels operating in eastern Congo of serious human rights violations, including the abduction and forced recruitment of young people.[1] He cited daily abuses against civilians in the conflict-hit North and South Kivu provinces, stating that "abductions and kidnappings of young people continue, to forcibly recruit them into armed movements."[1] Major Nestor Mavudisa, spokesperson for the Congolese army's third defense zone, said the rebels had arrested and detained several young people, including minors, using them at times as trackers and as human shields.[1]

The accusations came weeks after government representatives and rebels signed a declaration in Doha, Qatar, outlining a timeline for peace, under which the parties agreed to launch negotiations on August 8 and finalize a peace deal by August 18.[1]
