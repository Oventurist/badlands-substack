# Trump Tower

**Trump Tower** is the Trump family's landmark building on Fifth Avenue in New York City. In [[the-white-pill-emporium|The White Pill Emporium]], it features as a node in the alleged literary parallels between the Trump family and the 1890s novels of [[ingersoll-lockwood|Ingersoll Lockwood]].[1]

The essay observes that Lockwood's fictional Baron Trump lives in "Trump Castle," while the real [[barron-trump|Barron Trump]] is presumed to spend much of his time at Trump Tower.[1] It further notes that in Lockwood's *The Last President*, a riot takes place on Fifth Avenue at the exact location where the New York Trump Tower stands today.[1] These correspondences are offered as circumstantial support for the essay's broader argument about timeline manipulation and the singular role of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
