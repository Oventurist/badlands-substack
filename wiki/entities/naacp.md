# NAACP

The National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP) is one of the oldest and best-known civil rights organizations in the United States. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears principally through its Colorado/Montana/Wyoming Area Conference, which served as lead plaintiff in [[naacp-v-useip|NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.]]

That suit, filed on March 9, 2022 together with the [[league-of-women-voters|League of Women Voters of Colorado]] and [[mi-familia-vota|Mi Familia Vota]], alleged voter intimidation and suppression by the [[useip|United States Election Integrity Plan]] under Section 11(b) of the [[voting-rights-act-of-1965|Voting Rights Act of 1965]] and the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act of 1871]]. In the essay [[always-fight-back|"Always Fight Back"]], defendant [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues that the organization's participation in the case amounted to attaching the stigma of the Klan to lawful canvassers without evidence, and describes the court's July 2024 findings — that plaintiffs "failed to introduce any evidence that can remotely be perceived as intimidating or threatening" and that a witness had been prompted by counsel to name USEIP — as "a damning rebuke of both the civil rights organizations and their lawyers."
