# Silver Lake Partners

Silver Lake Partners is an American private equity firm specialising in technology investments.[1]

## Electronic Arts buyout

In September 2025 Silver Lake was named as one of three buyers in an agreement to acquire the video game company [[electronic-arts|Electronic Arts]], publisher of Madden NFL and The Sims, for $55 billion.[1] Its co-purchasers were Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, the [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], and Affinity Partners, the investment firm founded and led by [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], son-in-law of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Announced publicly on Monday 29 September 2025, the transaction was described as the largest private equity-funded buyout in history, with existing stockholders receiving $210 per share in cash — 25 percent above the stock's value the previous Thursday.[1]

The deal remained subject to regulatory approval because of Saudi Arabian involvement.[1] Reporting noted that the price of Electronic Arts stock had begun climbing on the Friday before the announcement as acquisition rumours surfaced.[1]

## TikTok connection

Silver Lake is also set to co-own TikTok's new United States business, placing the firm simultaneously at the centre of two politically sensitive technology ownership transitions.[1]
