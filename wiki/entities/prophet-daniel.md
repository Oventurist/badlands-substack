# Prophet Daniel

**Daniel** is the Old Testament prophet whose visions supply much of the interpretive scaffolding for the Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]]. The article dates his writing to roughly 600 BC.

## The seventieth week

Daniel 9 supplies the "seventieth week" from which many interpreters derive a literal seven-year [[great-tribulation|Tribulation]]. The article notes this reading alongside the three-and-a-half-year periods referenced in Revelation 11:2–3, 12:6 and 13:5, while acknowledging the debate over whether the period is literal or symbolic.

## Visions of the oppressive ruler

The article cites Daniel 7:19–27, 9:24–27 and 11:31–45 as Old Testament passages that some interpret as describing [[antichrist|the Antichrist]]. In them, a beast and oppressive ruler gathers forces and defiles the sanctuary, replacing sacrifices with abomination. Many are corrupted by flattery, but those who know God remain strong; they face persecution yet accomplish great feats. The ruler exalts himself, speaks blasphemies and prospers until the appointed time of wrath, worships a foreign god, conquers nations and faces opposition, until the Ancient of Days arrives, passes judgment, removes the ruler's dominion and grants the everlasting kingdom to the saints.

## Eyes like fire

Daniel is also one of the two writers — with [[john-the-apostle|John]] — who described Christ's eyes using the simile "like a flame of fire." [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] builds on this shared choice of simile, noting that both prophets knew exactly what fire looked like, to argue that a vision of laser-like eyes would have been describable only in those terms.
