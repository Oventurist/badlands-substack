# Ozzy Osbourne

**Ozzy Osbourne** (died July 22, 2025, aged 76) was the frontman of Black Sabbath and one of heavy metal's most iconic voices, widely known by the epithet "the Prince of Darkness."[1]

## Death

Osbourne died on the morning of Tuesday, July 22, 2025, at the age of 76.[1] His family said he died "surrounded by love," in a statement given to the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] shortly after his death: "It is with more sadness than mere words can convey that we have to report that our beloved Ozzy Osbourne has passed away this morning."[1] The statement came from his wife and four of his six children.[1] The Post marked his passing with the line "The Blizzard of Ozz has ended."[1]

## Reaction

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[canncon|CannCon]] noted the timing of the death relative to Osbourne's farewell performance: "RIP Ozzy…damn, his last show ever was just like two weeks ago or so!"[1]
