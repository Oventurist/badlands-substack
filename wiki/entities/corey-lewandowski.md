# Corey Lewandowski

Corey Lewandowski is a longtime political operative and adviser to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], associated in the second Trump administration with the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] and with Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]]'s circle.

He appears in the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]], which reports that during the March 3 [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing Senator [[thom-tillis|Thom Tillis]] repeatedly directed criticism at Trump allies including Lewandowski and [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]] while delivering what he called a performance review of Noem. The Brief renders his first name as "Cory."
