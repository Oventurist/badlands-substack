# Corey Lewandowski

Corey Lewandowski is an American political operative long associated with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s campaigns.[1] He served as Trump's 2016 presidential campaign manager and has remained an informal Trump adviser ever since.[2]

## Qatar lobbying

Politico reported in 2019 that [[qatar|Qatar]] had hired Lewandowski to help build ties with the Trump administration.[1] Badlands Media cited the arrangement as one strand of evidence that Doha's relationship with Trump world is more layered than partisan criticism of Qatar suggests, concluding that "Qatar remains an enigma, and Donald Trump—master of deception—is throwing smoke in every direction."[1]

## Return to the 2024 campaign

In August 2024 the Trump campaign expanded its ranks as the candidate sought to regain footing in the presidential race, bringing Lewandowski back on board to advise the campaign's senior leadership team.[2] The move was described by a person familiar with it who was granted anonymity to speak freely, and Lewandowski joined several other former Trump aides taking similar advisory roles.[2]

The staffing additions came as Trump trailed Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] in fundraising and in some swing-state polling, with Republicans voicing concern that the campaign had struggled to settle on a consistent line of attack against Harris.[2] Figures close to the campaign framed the hires not as a shakeup but as a buttressing of the leadership roster in the crucial final months before the election.[2]
