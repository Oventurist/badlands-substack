# Los Angeles City Council

The Los Angeles City Council is the legislative body of the city of Los Angeles, and figures in Badlands coverage of the [[skid-row-voter-fraud-investigation|Skid Row voter fraud investigation]] through its Housing and Homelessness Committee and the funding that committee directed to homeless-service providers.[1]

## Housing and Homelessness Committee grants

Reporting cited in the June 2026 Badlands Brief noted that a homeless services drop-in center in Venice had 185 registered voters associated with its address, and that the facility had previously received a $600,000 taxpayer-funded grant while Councilmember [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]] chaired the council's Housing and Homelessness Committee.[1] The same review found more than 7,600 registered voters linked to shelter and social-service addresses across the city, including roughly 1,160 registrations tied to the [[midnight-mission|Midnight Mission]] in Skid Row.[1]

## Council races and the fraud allegations

Recordings that surfaced after Election Day featured Skid Row residents claiming they had been paid to sign registration forms, forge signatures, and complete voter information, and that they were encouraged to support incumbent Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] and Councilmember Raman, while challenger [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] was not among the candidates allegedly promoted.[1] Those claims prompted the federal operation conducted on Skid Row by roughly twenty agents, including personnel from [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]].[1]
