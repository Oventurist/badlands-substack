# Los Angeles City Council

The Los Angeles City Council is the fifteen-member legislative body of the city of [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]], responsible for the municipal budget, land use, and the city's homelessness spending. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears principally through the grant-making powers of its committees.

## Housing and Homelessness Committee

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief notes that a homeless services drop-in center in Venice, reported to have 185 registered voters associated with its address, had previously received a $600,000 taxpayer-funded grant while Councilmember [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]] chaired the council's Housing and Homelessness Committee. The Brief uses the sequence — public money to service providers, service-provider addresses appearing as voter registration clusters, and those clusters allegedly favoring incumbents including Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] and Raman — as the structural core of its argument about the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|2026 mayoral election]] and the federal probe on [[skid-row|Skid Row]].
