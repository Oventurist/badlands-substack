# Korean Central News Agency

The **Korean Central News Agency** (KCNA) is the state-run news agency of [[north-korea|North Korea]] and the principal channel through which Pyongyang issues official statements.[1]

## Role in the Kursk deployment announcement

On 28 April 2025, KCNA carried the statement by North Korea's Central Military Commission that confirmed for the first time that North Korean troops had been fighting alongside Russia against Ukraine, and that they had helped Moscow retake control of the [[kursk-incursion|Kursk region]].[1] The statement said the deployment had been ordered by [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] under the mutual defence pact he signed with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in June 2024, a treaty that includes a pledge of mutual defence if either country is attacked.[1]

Through KCNA, the commission declared that the operation to regain control of Kursk had been "victoriously concluded" and that it was "an honor to have an alliance with such a powerful state as the Russian Federation."[1] The agency also carried Kim's characterisation of the deployment as intended to "annihilate and wipe out the Ukrainian neo-Nazi occupiers and liberate the Kursk area in cooperation with the Russian armed forces," and his statement that "they who fought for justice are all heroes and representatives of the honor of the motherland."[1]
