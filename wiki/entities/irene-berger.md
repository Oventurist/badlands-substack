# Irene Berger

**Irene Berger** is a judge of the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of West Virginia.[1]

## Castro ballot ruling

On December 21, 2023, Berger rejected a bid by presidential candidate [[john-anthony-castro|John Anthony Castro]] to remove former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the West Virginia ballot, ruling that Castro lacked standing to sue.[1] She sided with attorneys for Trump, Secretary of State [[mac-warner|Mac Warner]], and the [[west-virginia-republican-party|West Virginia GOP]].[1]

In her opinion, Berger wrote that the evidence Castro had submitted removed "any doubt that Mr. Castro's purported 'campaign' exists as a vehicle for pursuing litigation, not votes," and observed that he could not demonstrate any political activity in West Virginia other than the lawsuit he had filed.[1] The ruling was one of a series of dismissals of Castro suits premised on Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]
