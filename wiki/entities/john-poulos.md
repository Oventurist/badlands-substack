# John Poulos

John Poulos is the founder and chief executive of [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]]. He is cited in the June 9, 2026 *Badlands Brief* through a letter by [[john-c-bonifaz|John C. Bonifaz]] to the National Institute of Standards and Technology, which described Dominion as "a Toronto-based company with one listed key official, board members and employee, John Poulos, and under $18 million in sales for which the company obtains no income," per Dun & Bradstreet.[1]

The characterization is used by Badlands to argue that a company of minimal apparent substance was positioned, via the 2010 [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]-mandated [[premier-election-solutions|Premier]] divestiture and the subsequent purchase of [[sequoia-voting-systems|Sequoia Voting Systems]], to control a dominant share of the American voting-machine market.[1]

## Response to the Fox settlement (2023)

Poulos was the public face of Dominion's defamation litigation against [[fox-news|Fox News]], which the network settled for $787.5 million on April 18, 2023.[2] He called the settlement "historic," stating that "Fox has admitted to telling lies about Dominion that caused enormous damage to my company, our employees and our customers," and adding that "truthful reporting in the media is essential to our democracy."[2] The case and its reception in the Truth Community are covered at [[fox-dominion-defamation-settlement|the Fox–Dominion defamation settlement]].[2]
