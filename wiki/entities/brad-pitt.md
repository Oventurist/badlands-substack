# Brad Pitt

Brad Pitt is an American actor who appears in Badlands Media commentary both as a point of reference in the career of musician [[chris-whitley|Chris Whitley]] and as an alleged participant in an aborted effort to expose [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]].[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] stated that Pitt and music producer [[rick-rubin|Rick Rubin]] "are believed to be a part of this group as well" — referring to [[chris-cornell|Chris Cornell]], [[chester-bennington|Chester Bennington]], and [[avicii|Avicii]], who the essay says were producing a documentary on child trafficking before their deaths.[1] Carlson wrote that once Pitt and Rubin saw their friends die, "they got the point and stepped back in line," which he described as understandable.[1]

## Other mentions

The essay also notes that Whitley's 1991 debut included a song used in *Thelma and Louise* during Pitt's breakout love scene.[1] The claims about Pitt's involvement are presented as belief and rumor; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
