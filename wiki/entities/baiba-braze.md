# Baiba Braže

Baiba Braže is the foreign minister of Latvia. She appeared in coverage of the July 2024 [[2024-nato-washington-summit|NATO Washington Summit]] as one of the European officials pushing back on American domestic distraction and reframing allied attention toward Moscow.[1]

## NATO summit remarks

Asked about the questions dominating the summit — chiefly the mental acuity of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and the prospect of a returning [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — Braže said: "The Americans need to hear from us all that the problem is not Ukraine. The problem is Russia."[1]

Her remark was quoted alongside anonymous European diplomats who described the experience of watching the American president as "weird" and said allies found it "incredibly awkward" to be asked about Biden's condition. Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] rejected Braže's framing outright, arguing instead that "the problem is [[nato|NATO]]" and describing the alliance and its backers as parasitic elites driving the world toward a wider war.[1]
