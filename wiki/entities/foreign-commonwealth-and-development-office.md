# Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office

The Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) is the United Kingdom's foreign ministry, formed from the merger of the Foreign and Commonwealth Office with the Department for International Development.

In Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme's account of European war advocacy, the FCDO functions as one of the state funding mechanisms behind ostensibly independent civil-society organizations.[1] He noted that the [[institute-for-statecraft|Institute for Statecraft]] — founded in 2006 and granted UK charitable status in 2009 — received millions of pounds from the FCDO despite assurances that it was independent.[1] Documents released in the November 2018 [[integrity-initiative|Integrity Initiative]] leak indicated an annual Foreign Office budget of more than £2 million for the project, along with plans to influence public opinion and lists of participants in its clusters.[1]

DeLarme grouped the FCDO with [[usaid|USAID]] as the sort of governmental conduit that repeatedly turns up behind "pro-democracy" think tanks aligned with a permanent-war posture toward Russia.[1]
