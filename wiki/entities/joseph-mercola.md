# Joseph Mercola

Dr. Joseph Mercola is a natural health proponent, osteopathic physician and geopolitical commentator quoted by Badlands Media as a leading critic of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1]

## Critique of the WEF

Mercola's assessment, as summarized in the article, holds that the WEF's ultimate goal is to do away with the democratic process and hand all ownership and control to the deep state and the technocratic elite that controls it.[1] He argues the organization relies on fearmongering: a population governed by fear welcomes authoritarian "protections" such as expanded surveillance and digital identity systems, introduced under the guise of safety but ultimately eroding personal autonomy and freedom.[1] In his view the WEF's aim is not merely to control life on Earth but to fundamentally change it by hacking into humans and removing free will.[1] He further contends that the WEF, in partnership with the investment firm [[blackrock|BlackRock]], has infiltrated government and colludes in a corporatism in which an unelected corporate elite dictates policy top-down to the population.[1]

Badlands Media presents Mercola's reading as a counterweight to the sanitized Wikipedia description of the forum, leaving readers to judge which account — or which middle ground — is correct, while arguing that the end result is the same because the WEF does not hide its agendas.[1]
