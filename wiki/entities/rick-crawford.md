# Rick Crawford

Rick Crawford is a Republican member of the [[us-house-of-representatives|US House of Representatives]] and Chairman of the [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence]] (HPSCI).[1]

## Election monitoring activity

Badlands commentator [[canncon|CannCon]] highlighted Crawford's pattern of personally observing foreign elections, noting that in April 2025 he "monitored" elections in [[guyana|Guyana]], Suriname and Barbados in person — jurisdictions CannCon described as key narcotrafficking hubs.[1]

That observation was raised in the context of the disputed November 2025 election in [[honduras|Honduras]], where congressmen from HPSCI traveled to "monitor" the vote.[1] CannCon argued the pattern suggested "an attempt at regime placement in Honduras," noting that three weeks after the vote no winner had been certified and that the [[white-house|White House]] had revoked or denied visas for two officials of the [[libre-party|LIBRE party]] involved in the count.[1]
