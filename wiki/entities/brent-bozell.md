# Brent Bozell

Brent Bozell is the founder and president of the [[media-research-center|Media Research Center]], a conservative watchdog organization.[1]

In May 2023 Bozell publicized MRC findings, obtained through Freedom of Information Act requests, that a [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] counterterrorism grant program was funding a university program that had grouped the Republican Party together with Christian and conservative organizations in the same category as Nazis.[1] Bozell said, "This terrorism task force is engaged in an active effort to demonize and eliminate Christian, conservative, and Republican organizations using federal taxpayer dollars," and argued that "what we have uncovered calls for criminal prosecution," adding that the American people needed to know that officials abusing their federal positions would be held accountable.[1]
