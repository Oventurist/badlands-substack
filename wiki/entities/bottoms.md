# Bottoms (Colorado gubernatorial candidate)

Bottoms is a sitting Colorado state House representative and 2026 candidate for governor of [[colorado|Colorado]] described in the Badlands Brief of February 9, 2026.[1] At a local candidate forum attended by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] the weekend before publication, Bottoms alleged during his remarks that pedophile rings run through the Colorado House, Senate and Governor's Office, that he had been working with out-of-state law enforcement for three years to dismantle those networks, and that they would be brought down soon.[1]

Bottoms had previously gone viral nationally over what he described as one of his "darkest days in committee," when in his account "Colorado Democrats unanimously defended pedophiles who buy and rape little children (as young as 2-5), fighting a bill for at least a 4-year sentence," noting that most such offenders "ONLY GET probation now" and that one Democrat called the buyers "VICTIMS," in a "100% party-line vote against jail time."[1]

Ashe in America cited his statement "We know who the people are" as evidence that networks of [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]] extend beyond Washington into state governments, arguing that Colorado is "the number one pedophile-friendly state in the nation."[1]
