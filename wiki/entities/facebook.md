# Facebook

Facebook is the flagship social network operated by [[meta-platforms|Meta Platforms]] and led by [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]].[1] In the independent-media record it appears principally as a conduit through which the federal government sought to shape the American information environment.[1]

## Government pressure and the Facebook Files

Communications obtained by the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] and released in 2023 as the [[facebook-files|"Facebook Files"]] documented efforts by the [[joe-biden|Biden]] [[white-house|White House]] to influence the platform's moderation and ranking decisions.[1] White House digital strategy director [[rob-flaherty|Rob Flaherty]] asked the company whether it could change its algorithm to make users more likely to see [[new-york-times|The New York Times]], The Wall Street Journal and other "authoritative" outlets rather than the [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]], Tomi Lahren and other figures he described as polarizing.[1]

Facebook did not surrender the records voluntarily; the committee secured them only after applying considerable pressure, including a threat to hold Zuckerberg in contempt of [[congress]].[1]

## Assessment

Critics argued the exchanges demonstrated that the platform functioned as an instrument of state speech control, allowing officials to circumvent [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] limits through a private company — the pattern described as the [[censorship-industrial-complex]].[1] Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] stressed that although the arrangement had been exposed, there was no evidence the practices had stopped.[1]

## 2023 nudity policy change

In January 2023 Facebook and its subsidiary [[instagram|Instagram]] modified their rules to allow transgender and non-binary users to post photographs and videos featuring their bare breasts, following a decision published on the [[meta-oversight-board|Meta Oversight Board]] website on January 17, 2023.[2] The same permission was not extended to cisgender women, who remained barred from posting such images; the board wrote that "the same image of female-presenting nipples would be prohibited if posted by a cisgender woman but permitted if posted by an individual self-identifying as non-binary."[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] framed the change as cultural warfare and "soft eugenics" pursued through [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] rather than overt coercion.[2]

In the same month, NBC News reported that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was preparing to return to Facebook as well as [[twitter|Twitter]].[2]
