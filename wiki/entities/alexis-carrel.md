# Alexis Carrel

**Dr. Alexis Carrel** was a Nobel Prize–winning physician of the [[rockefeller-institute|Rockefeller Institute]] and one of the American medical establishment's most explicit public sympathizers with Nazi killing programs, according to Badlands Media.[1]

Carrel publicly applauded [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] for advocating the mass murder of mental patients and prisoners, and he himself advocated the use of poison gas to dispose of "useless eaters."[1] His position illustrates the article's central claim that the Rockefeller-reorganized [[medical-industrial-complex|Medical Industrial Complex]] was deeply intertwined with Nazi Germany and with the [[eugenics]] movement more broadly.[1]
