# Forough Hosseini

Forough Hosseini is a Florida philanthropist and donor, wife of homebuilding executive [[morteza-hosseini|Morteza "Mori" Hosseini]], and founder and chair of Food Brings Hope, a charity that provides assistance to homeless and economically disadvantaged students.[1] In 2019 Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] appointed her to serve on the Florida Children and Youth Cabinet.[1]

## Donor controversy

July 2023 reporting by [[the-national-pulse|The National Pulse]] identified the Hosseinis as major DeSantis benefactors who had also given tens of thousands of dollars to a political action committee funding Democratic leaders, including [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] and [[jamie-raskin|Jamie Raskin]].[1] The [[public-affairs-alliance-of-iranian-americans|Iranian American Political Action Committee]] associated with those donations also funded Friends of Hillary and Friends of Weiner, tied to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[anthony-weiner|Anthony Weiner]] respectively.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] that DeSantis could not claim plausible deniability regarding the Hosseinis, since he had personally appointed Forough Hosseini to a state post overseeing children and youth policy.[1] She asked why a Republican governor would appoint a well-known Democratic donor to a key position in his administration while campaigning on unifying the Republican Party, and why a network of what she called leftist NGOs should be allowed near children.[1] She emphasized that Hosseini was making policy decisions about homeless children in Florida while openly funding groups tied to Clinton and Weiner, and wrote that until this reporting she had considered DeSantis merely boring and overtly political rather than potentially "evil."[1]
