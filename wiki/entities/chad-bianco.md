# Chad Bianco

Chad Bianco is the sheriff of Riverside County, California, and a noted supporter of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Coachella rally arrest

In October 2024, Bianco's department arrested [[vem-miller|Vem Miller]], a 49-year-old Las Vegas man found with a shotgun, a loaded handgun, and a high-capacity magazine at a checkpoint roughly half a mile from the entrance to a Trump rally in California's Coachella Valley.[1] Miller was also carrying fake press and VIP passes, which authorities said suggested a possible attempt to gain closer access to the event.[1]

Bianco told Southern California News Group, "We probably stopped another assassination attempt."[1] His office nonetheless stated that the incident did not impact the safety of the former president or of rally attendees.[1] Miller was booked at the John J. Benoit Detention Center, released the same day on $5,000 bail, and scheduled for a January 2, 2025 appearance at the Indio Larson Justice Center.[1]
