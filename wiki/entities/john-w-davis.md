# John W. Davis

John W. Davis was an American lawyer who served as the founding president of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1]

Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" identifies Davis as the personal attorney of [[jp-morgan-jr|J.P. Morgan Jr.]], citing this relationship as evidence that the Council was, from its inception, closely tied to Morgan financial interests.[1] His installation as the organization's first president is grouped with the appointments of vice president [[paul-cravath|Paul Cravath]], who also represented Morgan properties, and first chairman [[russell-leffingwell|Russell Leffingwell]], a Morgan partner.[1] Because most early CFR members had Morgan connections, the article states, it has been said that the Council was heavily influenced by Morgan's interests.[1]
