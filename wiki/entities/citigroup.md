# Citigroup

Citigroup is a major American global bank.[1] It was one of three lenders — with [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]] and [[bank-of-america|Bank of America]] — slated to provide a $20 billion bank-led debt facility to [[argentina|Argentina]] as part of the [[argentina-bailout-package-2025|2025 Argentina bailout package]] backed by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

That facility was shelved in November 2025 in favor of a smaller, short-term loan package, after Argentina's October congressional elections delivered a resounding victory for President [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]]'s party and sent Argentine bonds and currency rallying.[1]
