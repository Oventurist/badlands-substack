# Howard University

**Howard University** is a historically black university in Washington, D.C., and the alma mater of Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1]

## Role in the 2024 election

The Harris campaign held its election-night watch party on the Howard campus on November 5, 2024.[1] Harris never addressed the crowd that night as initial optimism turned dour after midnight, and the returns broke decisively for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] She returned to the campus the following afternoon, November 6, to deliver her concession speech, telling supporters to "accept the results" while insisting that "while I concede this election, I do not concede the fight that fueled this campaign."[1]
