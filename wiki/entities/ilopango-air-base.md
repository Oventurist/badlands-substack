# Ilopango Air Base

Ilopango Air Base in El Salvador was the key transfer point for the covert airlift of weapons and supplies to the Nicaraguan Contras during the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra affair]].[1] [[felix-rodriguez|Felix Rodriguez]], described by Rolling Stone as a retired [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] agent of many pseudonyms, served as the logistics officer for airlifts from Ilopango to the jungle hide-outs of the rebels during the most violent years of the war in [[nicaragua]].[1]

[[southern-air-transport|Southern Air Transport]] flew airlift-command-contracted routes into Ilopango carrying construction materials, medical supplies, office supplies and spare parts, according to airlift command spokesman Lt. Jerry Todd.[1] The base was more than an occasional stop, however: it was a staging area for the Contras and a place where Southern Air frequently sent its mechanics to service the planes that carried guns and supplies into the field.[1]

Telephone records introduced during the investigation showed repeated calls to the home and business of [[richard-secord|Richard Secord]] from a "safe house" in El Salvador used by crew members of the secret air resupply operation, conducted at a time when [[congress|Congress]] had banned US military aid to the rebels.[1] It was from Ilopango that the C-123K carrying [[eugene-hasenfus|Eugene Hasenfus]] was dispatched before being shot down on October 5, 1986.[1]
