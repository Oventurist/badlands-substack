# Patrick Ho

Patrick Ho is a former Hong Kong official and executive associated with [[cefc-china-energy|CEFC China Energy]] who became a business contact of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]. Hunter Biden reportedly described him as "the fucking spy chief of China."[1]

## Charges and the call to James Biden

On December 18, 2017, Ho was charged with conspiracy to violate the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and with money laundering.[1] According to commentary compiled by [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], Ho's first telephone call after the charges was not to Hunter Biden but to [[james-biden|James Biden]], the president's brother.[1] James Biden said he believed the call had actually been intended for Hunter, adding, "There is nothing else I have to say. I don't want to be dragged into this anymore."[1]

Text messages later surfaced in which [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] wrote to Hunter Biden, "Nice quote from uncle jimmy... I hope you thanked him for that," to which Hunter replied, "yeah I'm delighted."[1] Reporting cited by Badlands indicated that Hunter Biden began "panicking" when the New York Times began asking why Ho had called James Biden looking for him, and that Hunter's attorney George Mesires boasted about steering Times reporter David Barboza away from the Bidens' involvement with CEFC.[1] A voicemail from [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to Hunter regarding the Times' inquiries into the "spy chief of China" reportedly told his son, "I think you're clear."[1]

## Significance

The Ho episode is central to arguments advanced by Badlands writers that the Biden family functioned as an integrated influence-peddling operation rather than a set of independent business careers, undercutting James Biden's testimony to the [[biden-impeachment-inquiry|House impeachment inquiry]] that his brother had no involvement in his deals.[1]
