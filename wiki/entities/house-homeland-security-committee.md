# House Homeland Security Committee

The **House Homeland Security Committee** is the standing committee of the United States House of Representatives with jurisdiction over the Department of Homeland Security.[1]

## Mayorkas impeachment articles

Early on Wednesday, January 31, 2024, the Republican-led panel approved two articles of impeachment against Homeland Security Secretary [[alejandro-mayorkas|Alejandro Mayorkas]], charging that he had "willfully and systematically" flouted federal immigration laws and breached the public trust.[1] The vote was 18–15 along party lines, and the articles affirmed that Mayorkas failed to detain migrants crossing the border before deciding whether to grant asylum.[1]

The articles further stated that the secretary had ignored records requests from the committee and misled [[congress|Congress]] about having "operational control" of the United States border and about his contention that it is "secure."[1] The marathon hearing ran for more than fifteen hours and featured several procedural attempts by Democrats to derail the impeachment effort, while Republicans insisted that their year-long investigation had produced sufficient evidence that Mayorkas violated his oath of office.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] situated the committee's vote within what he described as a cascade of border-crisis narratives converging in the early weeks of 2024, alongside the Eagle Pass controversy and a pending Texas appeal before the Fifth Circuit over federal authority at the border.[1] He argued that the timing bolstered the idea of patriot control in a fifth-generation warfare sense, tying it to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first major campaign promise resurfacing eight years later as his 2024 campaign began.[1]
