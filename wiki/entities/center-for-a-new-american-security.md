# Center for a New American Security

The Center for a New American Security (CNAS) is a Washington, D.C. foreign and national security policy think tank.[1] It appears in Badlands Media's "Deep State Spotlight" profile of [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] as one of the institutional way stations of her career: Nuland served as the organization's chief executive officer from January 2018 until early 2019, during the interval when she was out of government under the [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] administration.[1]

The profile places CNAS alongside the [[brookings-institution|Brookings Institution]], the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] and the Albright Stonebridge Group in a roster of establishment institutions that, in contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s reading, function as a holding pattern for [[state-department|State Department]] officials between administrations and as evidence of the durable network he associates with the [[deep-state|deep state]] and the [[neoconservatives|neoconservative]] foreign policy consensus.[1] Nuland returned to government under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] after her CNAS tenure.[1]
