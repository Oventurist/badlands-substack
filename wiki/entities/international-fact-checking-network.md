# International Fact-Checking Network

The International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) is the fact-checking accreditation body associated with the [[poynter-institute|Poynter Institute]].[1]

Badlands Media characterizes the IFCN and its "unholy merger" with the legacy media as essentially a response to anti-establishment populism.[1] That populist current is described as encompassing not only [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his followers but a wider range of people who have lost faith in official narratives, a pool that by late 2023 had expanded to include supporters of Robert F. Kennedy Jr., Cornel West and Vivek Ramaswamy, all of whom have expressed varying anti-establishment sentiments and all of whom have been targeted by [[media-matters|Media Matters]] coverage.[1]
