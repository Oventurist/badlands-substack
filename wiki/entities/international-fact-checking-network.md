# International Fact-Checking Network

The International Fact-Checking Network is a fact-checking body which, in Badlands Media's account, merged with the legacy media in what the article calls an unholy union essentially formed in response to anti-establishment populism.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

That populist constituency is described as comprising not only [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his followers but a wider range of people who have lost faith in official narratives, a pool that has expanded to include supporters of Robert F. Kennedy Jr., Cornel West and Vivek Ramaswamy — all of whom have been targets of [[media-matters|Media Matters]] coverage.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) The network's parent body, the Poynter Institute, is named among the institutions the author hopes will face legal judgment alongside the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]], the Southern Poverty Law Center and the Center for Countering Digital Hate.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

## Defense of the WHO

Badlands Media's "The WHO's Last Stand" described the International Fact-Checking Network as "highly biased" and grouped it with the [[associated-press|Associated Press]] as "damage control experts" who would have the public believe that the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] has purely altruistic intentions and only the best interests of humanity and the planet in mind — a claim the article called demonstrably false.[2]
