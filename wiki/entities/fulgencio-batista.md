# Fulgencio Batista

Fulgencio Batista (rendered "Fugencio Batista" in the source article) was the strongman whose 1950s rule constituted [[cuba|Cuba]]'s first dictatorship, following roughly half a century of republican government after Cuban independence in 1902.[1] The Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1" reports the rumor that Batista was backed by members of the American military-industrial complex.[1]

## Overthrow

Batista's regime was displaced by the revolution that installed [[fidel-castro|Fidel Castro]] and aligned Cuba with the Soviet Union in the late 1950s and early 1960s.[1] The article frames this transition skeptically, describing what was "propped up as a revolution" as merely "a new brand of dictatorship devouring the old," after which the sight of Cubans fleeing by boat toward the Florida coast became common.[1]
