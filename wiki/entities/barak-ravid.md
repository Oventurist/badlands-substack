# Barak Ravid

Barak Ravid is an Israeli journalist who covers foreign policy and the Middle East for [[axios|Axios]], and who is frequently the conduit for on-the-record remarks by senior American and Israeli officials.

In the July 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2b2|Badlands Brief]], Ravid is named as the Axios reporter to whom President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] spoke about the funeral of Iranian Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]]. Trump told Axios he was monitoring the funeral, that U.S.–Iran talks would pause for about a week, and that neither side would engage in hostilities during that period — including the widely circulated remark, "They are all there. One shot [and we can take them all out], but we are not going to do that because then we would have nobody to negotiate with." The Brief's commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads Trump's follow-up comment to Ravid about being surprised to see Iranians crying at the funeral as a deliberate use of the Streisand effect, designed to draw Western attention to the possibility that audiences have been misled about the Ayatollah's standing with the Iranian people.
