# Michael Every

**Michael Every** is a global strategist at [[rabobank|Rabobank]] known for macroeconomic commentary that fuses financial analysis with geopolitics, and whose notes are frequently republished by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]

## Analysis of the Macron China visit

Every wrote the April 2023 analysis titled "France Has Already Surrendered To The Coming Conflict," excerpted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, assessing the [[2023-macron-state-visit-to-china|2023 Macron state visit to China]].[1] He recounted that [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] had received a red-carpet reception while European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen was ushered in "via the tradesman's entrance," and quoted national-security experts calling the trip "one of the greatest blunders by a major European power since the end of the Cold War."[1] He noted the new coinage "to Macron," meaning "to deliberately increase one's dependency on China whilst lecturing European partners about naivety and the need to boost EU strategic autonomy."[1]

Every observed that Macron signed multiple investment deals, agreed to a new [[airbus|Airbus]] plant, promised [[huawei|Huawei]] fair treatment, and rejected von der Leyen's supply-chain "de-risking" call, while declaring "I am neither Taiwan nor the US" — signalling no French economic consequences for China over [[taiwan|Taiwan]].[1] He warned that Macron had sent the wrong signal to Beijing just as it announced inspections of cargo vessels entering Taiwan and stepped up encircling military exercises.[1] He described the [[european-union|European Union]] as divided between a Franco-German-Spanish bloc favouring China and northern and eastern member states plus the Dutch aligning with Washington, and argued Europe was already in a structurally weak, emerging-market-like position, referencing Rabobank's "Balance of payments — and power — crises."[1]
