# John Ratcliffe

John Ratcliffe is the Director of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration. He appears in the January 19, 2026 Badlands Brief as the most senior American official to visit Venezuela following the removal of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].

According to CBS News reporting summarised in the Brief, Ratcliffe travelled to Caracas to meet acting Venezuelan President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]]. The two-hour meeting focused on strengthening bilateral cooperation, including security matters and efforts to curb Venezuela's role as a haven for criminal networks. The Brief describes the visit as "a significant diplomatic development" and the highest-ranking U.S. official visit since Maduro's ouster.

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] — who in his commentary refers to him once as "Dan Ratcliffe" — treats the meeting as a signal that "several of the important players from [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s Devolution Theory seem to be involved in the diplomacy with the Maduro/Rodriguez government," which he argues is operating under a [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] protocol.
