# Times of Israel

The Times of Israel is an English-language Israeli news outlet cited in the Badlands Media corpus for reporting on Israeli government positions and regional diplomacy.

In the April 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-07c|Badlands Brief]], the Times of Israel is cited for the report that Washington is pursuing another meeting with [[iran|Iran]] in [[pakistan|Pakistan]], with anonymous officials described as optimistic about a deal, and for reporting that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] has said Israel is ready for a resumption of war with Iran while the United States gears up for another round of talks. The item is presented alongside the announcement by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] of a ten-day ceasefire between [[israel|Israel]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]].
