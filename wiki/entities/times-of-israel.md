# Times of Israel

The Times of Israel is an English-language Israeli news outlet cited in the Badlands Media corpus for reporting on Israeli government positions and regional diplomacy.

In the April 17, 2026 Badlands Brief, the Times of Israel is cited for the report that Washington is pursuing another meeting with [[iran|Iran]] in [[pakistan|Pakistan]], with anonymous officials described as optimistic about a deal, and for reporting that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] has said Israel is ready for a resumption of war with Iran while the United States gears up for another round of talks. The item is presented alongside the announcement by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] of a ten-day ceasefire between [[israel|Israel]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]].

## Gaza genocide polling and the High Court crisis (July 2026)

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief cites the Times of Israel for polling according to which one third of all American Jews say that Israel committed a genocide in [[gaza|Gaza]], and only 20% of all Americans say Israel did not commit a genocide and that the "complete obliteration of the Gazan civilization" was morally justified. The same edition cites the outlet for reporting that, for the first time in Israeli history, the government of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] openly declared it will not respect a [[israeli-high-court-of-justice|High Court of Justice]] ruling — sparking a constitutional crisis — and for the court's warning of anarchy and societal breakdown should officials flout its rulings. Commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues the polling's accuracy is irrelevant: "the winds have changed, and Israel has now become widely recognized in the US as a liability, if not an outright enemy."
