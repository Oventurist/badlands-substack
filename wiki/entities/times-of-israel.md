# The Times of Israel

The Times of Israel is an English-language Israeli news outlet whose reporting is cited in Badlands Media coverage as a primary source on Israeli military operations and on the diplomacy surrounding the June 2026 [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding|US–Iran memorandum of understanding]].[1]

## Coverage of the June 2026 Lebanon operation

The Times of Israel reported that the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] confirmed conducting a military operation overnight in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]], striking [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] targets, with sixteen fatalities reported in Lebanese accounts.[1] Badlands cited that confirmation alongside social-media reporting of heavy close-quarters fighting around [[nabatieh|Nabatieh]].[1]

## Coverage of the US–Iran agreement

The outlet's liveblog reported that Switzerland had confirmed the cancellation of planned US–Iran talks at Bürgenstock, effectively postponing the formal signing ceremony after the Israeli strikes.[1] The Times of Israel also reported Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]]'s defense of the deal and his criticism of what it characterized as an Israeli "freakout" over it, including his assertion that [[donald-trump|Trump]] was the only world leader who still liked [[israel|Israel]].[1] A further report carried Trump's statement that it was acceptable for [[iran|Iran]] to retain some ballistic missile capability as the agreement took force.[1]

## "Is Israel becoming a mafia state?" (2017)

Badlands commentary has repeatedly cited a 2017 Times of Israel article headlined "Is Israel becoming a mafia state?", which it says reported that 75 percent of [[knesset|Knesset]] members were involved in shady dealings.[2] A July 2024 Badlands News Brief used the piece as supporting context for its account of the [[israeli-mafia|Israeli mafia]], including the claim that a Jewish-Ukrainian criminal network migrated to Israel in 1989 and that organized crime infiltrated the [[likud|Likud]] central committee.[2]
