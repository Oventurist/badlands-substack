# Harvard-China Health Partnership

The Harvard-China Health Partnership (HCHP) is a program of the [[harvard-th-chan-school-of-public-health|Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health]] founded after a $350 million donation from the Chan family, Hong Kong real estate tycoons with extensive links to Beijing.[1]

## XPCC training controversy

Reporting by [[melissa-chen|Melissa Chen]] in April 2025 identified the HCHP as the vehicle through which Harvard trained officials of the [[xinjiang-production-and-construction-corps|Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps]] as late as October 2023, despite the XPCC having been sanctioned by the United States in 2020 for its role in the Uyghur genocide.[1] Harvard defended the arrangement by stating that the goal was to help China develop insurance programs.[1]

Badlands Media argued that the partnership placed Harvard in violation of the spirit — and potentially the letter — of the [[uyghur-forced-labor-prevention-act|Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act]] of 2022, and formed part of the university's legal exposure under [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]].[1]
