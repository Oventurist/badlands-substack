# Ralph Northam

Ralph Northam is a former Democratic Governor of Virginia.[1] Badlands commentary identifies him as the governor under whom [[justin-fairfax|Justin Fairfax]] served as Lieutenant Governor, and characterizes him as "the guy who talked about killing new born babies on the radio," a reference to his widely criticized remarks on late-term abortion and post-birth care decisions.[1] He is cited in coverage of the 2026 federal investigations touching Virginia Democrats as part of the network of officials who knew one another within the state party.[1]
