# Ralph Northam

**Ralph Northam** is a Democratic politician who served as Governor of Virginia from 2018 to 2022, with [[justin-fairfax]] as his lieutenant governor. A pediatric neurologist by training, he is remembered in the Badlands corpus chiefly as "the guy who talked about killing new born babies on the radio" — a reference to his January 2019 WTOP interview describing how an infant delivered in a late-term abortion case would be "kept comfortable" and "resuscitated if that's what the mother and the family desired" while a discussion took place, remarks widely read on the right as an endorsement of infanticide.

Northam's administration is invoked in the Brief's May 2026 survey of Virginia Democratic figures now implicated in scandal or tragedy, alongside [[louise-lucas]], [[steve-descano]] and Fairfax.
