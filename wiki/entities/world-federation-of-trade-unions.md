# World Federation of Trade Unions

The World Federation of Trade Unions (WFTU) was a short-lived attempt at a global labor union that included Soviet affiliates.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) By 1945 the American Federation of Labor had successfully sabotaged it by withdrawing and denouncing it as a communist front.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

The article treats this episode as the establishing pattern of the era: U.S. agencies would use unions to fracture international labor solidarity, prioritizing anti-communism — later rebranded as "[[democracy-promotion|democracy promotion]]" — over workers' rights.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The [[pentagon|Department of Defense]] indirectly supported the same objective through the [[marshall-plan|Marshall Plan]]'s labor provisions, which conditioned aid on anti-leftist union reforms in recipient countries.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The same logic later animated the [[free-trade-union-committee|Free Trade Union Committee]] and the foreign institutes of the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
