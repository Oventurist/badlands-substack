# Yuriy Sak

Yuriy Sak is an adviser to [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s defence minister who served as a frequent public voice for Kyiv's weapons requests during the war with Russia.[1]

## F-16 advocacy

In late January 2023, immediately after President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] announced that the United States would send 31 Abrams M1A2 tanks to Ukraine, Sak told Reuters that Kyiv would now press for Western fighter aircraft, saying "the next big hurdle will now be the fighter jets."[1] The remark set the terms of a subsequent debate in Washington, where [[pentagon|Pentagon]] Deputy Press Secretary [[sabrina-singh|Sabrina Singh]], pressed repeatedly at a briefing, declined to rule out transferring fourth-generation F-16s, saying only that "we haven't announced other packages yet."[1]

Sak's pattern of publicly naming the next capability sought after each incremental Western commitment was cited by critics of the aid programme as evidence of escalation by ratchet.[1]
