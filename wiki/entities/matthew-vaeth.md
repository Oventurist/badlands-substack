# Matthew Vaeth

Matthew Vaeth is an American budget official who served as acting director of the [[office-of-management-and-budget|White House Office of Management and Budget]] at the start of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## The federal funding freeze memo

Late on Monday, January 27, 2025, the Trump administration directed federal agencies to pause the disbursement of loans and grants while the government conducted a review to ensure that spending aligned with the President's agenda.[2] The memo issued by Vaeth as acting OMB director instructed agencies to temporarily pause "all activities related to obligation or disbursement of all Federal financial assistance."[2] "This temporary pause will provide the Administration time to review agency programs and determine the best uses of the funding for those programs consistent with the law and the President's priorities," the memo stated.[2] It indicated that Social Security and Medicare benefits should not be impacted, nor should assistance provided directly to individuals.[2]

## Rescission of the federal funding freeze memo

On Wednesday, January 29, 2025, Vaeth signed a memo rescinding a controversial OMB directive issued late the previous Monday that had frozen a wide swath of federal financial assistance, paralyzing many federal programs and causing an uproar on Capitol Hill.[1] The reversal came amid strong behind-the-scenes pushback from Republican lawmakers, according to a GOP senator apprised of the decision, and one Republican senator expressed relief, saying "chaos is never good."[1] GOP senators had been flooded with calls from community leaders confused and concerned about losing federal grants.[1]
