# Cindi Lee Yeager

Cindi Lee Yeager is a Cobb County, Georgia prosecutor who surfaced as a witness in the controversy over Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] and her hand-picked special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]].[1]

## Role in the Willis–Wade controversy

According to testimony given by defense attorney [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]] before the Georgia Senate Committee on Special Investigations, Yeager was physically present in September 2023 when Willis telephoned [[terrence-bradley|Terrence Bradley]] — Wade's former law partner and divorce attorney — and told him, "They are coming after us. You don't need to talk to them about anything about us."[1] Merchant presented the call as a central piece of evidence that Willis and Wade had engaged in efforts to influence a potential witness.[1]

Yeager subsequently provided a sworn declaration in the matter. In it she stated that she came forward after watching Bradley perjure himself, indicating that her decision to formalize what she had overheard was prompted by the divergence between Bradley's courtroom testimony and the conversation she witnessed.[1] Her declaration turned an allegation that might otherwise have rested on Bradley's own account into a corroborated one from a fellow prosecutor, and it was cited during the Senate hearing as evidence that the alleged tampering was "not just a 'feeling.'"[1]
