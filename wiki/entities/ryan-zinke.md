# Ryan Zinke

Ryan Zinke is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Montana and a former Secretary of the Interior. In January 2025 he publicly defended President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s slate of cabinet nominees during the [[senate-confirmation-process|Senate confirmation process]].[1]

## Defense of the Trump nominees

Speaking on [[newsmax|Newsmax TV]]'s "Newsline" on January 16, 2025 — the day of [[doug-burgum|Doug Burgum]]'s confirmation hearing for Secretary of the Interior — Zinke said two things would be clear from the proceedings: that Trump's slate "is enormously talented — there is no doubt," and that the nominees "were very well prepared."[1] He added that "in policy, they were better prepared than some of their democratic, you know, friends on the Senate side," calling the group "an enormous success of fielding this group of nominees" and predicting all of them would be successful.[1] Of Burgum specifically, Zinke said, "I can't think of a better individual for interior."[1]
