# Free Speech Systems

**Free Speech Systems** is the parent company of the [[infowars|Infowars]] media platform founded by [[alex-jones|Alex Jones]].[1]

## Bankruptcy proceedings

On Friday, June 14, 2024, a Texas bankruptcy judge ruled that Jones's personal assets would be liquidated to help repay the $1.5 billion he owes to the families of the victims of the Sandy Hook Elementary School shooting.[1] The same judge later ruled against liquidating the Infowars platform and Free Speech Systems itself, leaving the company intact even as Jones's personal holdings were ordered sold.[1] Jones had attempted to use bankruptcy protection to shield both his personal assets and his media operation from defamation damages awarded after he repeatedly claimed the shooting was a hoax, and in the days before the ruling told his audience the company was on the verge of being shut down while directing them to a new website owned by his father.[1]
