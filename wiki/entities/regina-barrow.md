# Regina Barrow

Regina Barrow is a Democratic member of the Louisiana State Senate.[1]

Barrow sponsored Senate Bill 371, which would sentence offenders aged 17 and older who are convicted of the rape of a victim under the age of 13 to surgical rather than chemical castration.[1] The bill passed the Louisiana House 74–24 and the Senate 29–9, and in early June 2024 headed to the desk of Republican Governor [[jeff-landry|Jeff Landry]] to be signed or vetoed.[1]
