# Roxane Watkins

**Colonel Roxane Watkins** is a contributing author to Badlands Media and the writer of "Operation Gladio – A Primer," published 13 June 2024, which opens a multi-part series arguing that a single covert apparatus links a wide range of twentieth-century events.[1] Her work also appears independently at The Colonel's Corner, a Substack publication.[1]

## Background and method

Watkins describes the primer as the product of research spanning weeks per topic, supplemented by roughly thirty years of travel to the places discussed in the series and by professional experience working inside the bureaucracy she examines.[1] She repeatedly urges readers not to accept her generalizations at face value but to independently verify each claim, framing the series as a research guide rather than a finished verdict.[1]

## Central arguments

Watkins contends that the United States' self-image as a purely liberating force in foreign affairs is historically false, and that her research into [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]] produced what she calls a troubling answer to that foundational question.[1] She credits [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]]'s work on Wall Street's interface with foreign affairs — including financing of the Bolshevik revolution, [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] and [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin D. Roosevelt]] — as her starting point, and [[cynthia-chung|Cynthia Chung]]'s book *The Empire Where the Black Sun Never Sets* as the key articulation of postwar [[stay-behind-units|stay-behind]] doctrine.[1]

She names the coalition behind these operations the [[international-syndicate|International Syndicate]], an array of banking, industrial, religious and political interests whose members, she stresses, do not fall neatly into any single religion or ethnic grouping.[1] She traces its ideological formalization to the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] of the late 1800s.[1]

Watkins also argues that the vocabulary of anti-communism has functioned as cover: that leaders labelled communists were often merely resisting extractive resource contracts, and that the Reagan- and Bush-era framing of the [[nicaraguan-contras|Contras]] as the good guys and the [[sandinista-national-liberation-front|Sandinistas]] as communists inverted the truth.[1] She closes by asking when media deception began, noting that [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]]-style manipulation predates the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] itself.[1]
