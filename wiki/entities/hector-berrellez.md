# Hector Berrellez

**Hector Berrellez** is a former [[drug-enforcement-administration|DEA]] agent who has publicly alleged that [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] contract pilots knowingly transported drugs into the United States, landing on military bases with impunity.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites Berrellez among the firsthand accounts that, alongside congressional reports and declassified documents, undercut official denials of intelligence-community involvement in narcotics trafficking.[1] Berrellez is the subject of the television series *The Last Narc* and the author of the memoir *The Last Narc: A Memoir by the DEA's Most Notorious Agent*, listed among the article's further-reading sources; DeLarme also points readers to a recorded interview with him.[1]
