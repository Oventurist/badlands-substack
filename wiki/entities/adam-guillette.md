# Adam Guillette

Adam Guillette is the president of [[accuracy-in-media|Accuracy in Media]] and an alumnus of the undercover journalism nonprofit [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]].[1] Under his leadership, Accuracy in Media adopted hidden-camera sting techniques aimed at public school districts, producing investigations in Idaho, Iowa and Tennessee before turning to Ohio.[1]

## Ohio investigation

Guillette narrated the January 2023 Accuracy in Media video showing employees of school districts around Columbus, Ohio, describing how they mislead parents who oppose [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]] and related concepts.[1] In the narration he said the recordings confirmed that "parents are playing an endless game of Whac-A-Mole," because districts rebrand the tenets of critical race theory or bury contested material inside programs with innocuous names.[1] Ohio districts were reported to be struggling to rebut the footage.[1]
