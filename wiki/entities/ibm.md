# IBM

IBM is a global technology provider that in October 2025 moved deeper into crypto and digital-asset infrastructure with the launch of a platform aimed at banks, governments, and large corporations.[1]

## Digital Asset Haven

On Monday, October 27, 2025, IBM announced the [[ibm-digital-asset-haven|IBM Digital Asset Haven]], a platform designed to let institutions manage digital assets securely across multiple blockchains.[1] The platform offers custody, transaction routing, and settlement across more than 40 public and private chains, together with compliance tooling such as identity verification and anti-money-laundering checks delivered through APIs and developer tools.[1]

A software-as-a-service version of the platform was expected to go live in the final quarter of 2025, with broader support planned for the second quarter of 2026.[1] The launch was reported against a backdrop of growing institutional interest in [[stablecoin|stablecoins]] and tokenized real-world assets as cheaper, faster cross-border alternatives to traditional banking rails.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the announcement as confirmation that "nothing can stop what is coming" is more than a slogan.[1]
