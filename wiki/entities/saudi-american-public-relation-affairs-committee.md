# Saudi American Public Relation Affairs Committee

The **Saudi American Public Relation Affairs Committee** (SAPRAC) is an organization established in Delaware and based in Washington, D.C. that became a prominent voice in the 2017 dispute between [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[qatar|Qatar]], running advertisements attacking Qatar over alleged terrorism ties.[1]

In August 2017 the [[podesta-group|Podesta Group]] registered with the Justice Department under the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]] as a foreign agent for SAPRAC.[1] The relationship had been unknown publicly until the Daily Beast first reported it the previous week.[1] SAPRAC agreed to pay the Podesta Group $50,000 per month, plus fees for producing or placing advertisements and other expenses, under a contract beginning near the end of June 2017.[1]

Badlands writer Joe Lange emphasizes the timing: the Mueller investigation launched in May 2017, and the Podesta Group's Saudi registration surfaced in August 2017, months before the firm abruptly closed.[1] He treats the SAPRAC contract as one of two Saudi money channels into the firm, the other being the reported $140,000 monthly retainer from the Center for Studies and Media Affairs at the Saudi Royal Court.[1]
