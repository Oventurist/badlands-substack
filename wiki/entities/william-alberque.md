# William Alberque

William Alberque is a former [[nato|NATO]] official who comments publicly on nuclear and arms-control matters.[1]

In August 2024 he was quoted in reporting on the [[russian-nuclear-strike-plans-leak|Russian nuclear strike plans leak]], saying of the alleged Russian target mapping that "there could be hundreds, if not thousands, of targets mapped across Europe, including military and infrastructure targets."[1]
