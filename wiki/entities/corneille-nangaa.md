# Corneille Nangaa

Corneille Nangaa is the leader of the Rwanda-linked Alliance Fleuve Congo, the political umbrella associated with the [[m23-rebels|M23]] armed group in eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

In late January 2026, Nangaa publicly denounced the US–DRC minerals partnership as unconstitutional, a claim the DRC presidency rejected.[1] Badlands described this as the M23 leader accusing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and President Félix Tshisekedi of violating the law through the implementation of their legal arrangement, and speculated about his alignment with [[rwanda|Rwanda]]'s longstanding ties to Israel.[1]

## Ceasefire violation claims (September 2025)

As political coordinator of the [[congo-river-alliance|Congo River Alliance]], Nangaa held a press conference in Goma in September 2025 accusing Congolese forces and their allies of attacking in South Kivu despite a pledge to cease hostilities.[2] "We are obliged to inform the Congolese people and the international community of the successive violations of the ceasefire, which are hindering the principle agreement," he said.[2]
