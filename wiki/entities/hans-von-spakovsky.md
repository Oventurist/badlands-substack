# Hans von Spakovsky

Hans von Spakovsky is manager of the Election Law Reform Initiative at [[heritage-foundation|The Heritage Foundation]] and a former career lawyer at the Department of Justice.[1]

## Commentary on noncitizen voting prosecutions

In September 2024 von Spakovsky told The Federalist that federal prosecutors have shown no appetite for pursuing cases of [[noncitizen-voting|noncitizen voting]].[1] "I spent four years at the Justice Department as a career lawyer, and I can tell you that the career ranks of the Justice Department are filled with left-wing ideologues, and they just had no interest — they have no interest in going after aliens to prosecute them," he said.[1]

He attributed the reluctance to ideological motive: "They think it is unfair that aliens can't register and vote. Plus, many of them thought, 'Well, if they're registering and voting, it will help our side.' I think that's clearly the motivation here. That's why they weren't interested in pursuing these cases."[1]

His remarks accompanied Bureau of Justice Statistics data showing the federal government pursued only 35 criminal cases of aliens voting in American elections from 2001 to 2021, a figure The Federalist contrasted with thousands of aliens registered to vote in recent years and at least hundreds flagged to the Justice Department as having actually cast ballots.[1]
