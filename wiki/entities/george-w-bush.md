# George W. Bush

George W. Bush was President of the United States during the Iraq War and the period of the New York Federal Reserve's cash airlift to Baghdad.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## The Iraq money and the Lebanon bunker

The article states that tens of billions of dollars were stolen during the [[iraq-war|Iraq War]], and that an investigation conducted during the Bush presidency discovered over $1 billion — elsewhere given as more than $1.5 billion — in a bunker in Lebanon, money the author says was destined to fund Hezbollah.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) Bush is said to have squashed that investigation so that nothing came of it, with the story swept under the rug and forgotten.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The article's judgement is blunt: the plan was always to steal the money, and Bush "didn't just turn a blind eye to the theft, he actually stopped an investigation."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) It further characterises Bush as one of [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]]'s biggest puppets.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## Libya policy

After 9/11, the article notes, [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]] treated Bush's war on terror as a serious threat, voluntarily surrendered his weapons of mass destruction programmes and made peace with Western nations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The Bush administration made a deal with Gaddafi under which Libya would take custody of [[abdelhakim-belhaj|Abdelhakim Belhaj]] and any other captured members of the [[libyan-islamic-fighting-group|Libyan Islamic Fighting Group]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## Teneo appearances and paid speeches

Bush figures in the America for Sale series as a headline attraction at events staged by the consulting firm [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) On September 20, 2012 — days after the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]] attack — he spoke alongside [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and former British Prime Minister Tony Blair at an unpublicised reception at the Essex House in New York City organised with the help of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], an event designed to impress potential Teneo clients with access to three former world leaders on a single stage.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article treats the line-up as evidence for its claim, drawn from Q drop 299, that [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]] controlled U.S. and U.K. politicians, calling all three men puppets.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Beginning in May 2011 Bush also appeared on stage with Clinton and [[ubs|UBS]] executive Bob McCann in a Teneo-arranged series of events titled "Revitalizing America," staged while the Swiss bank was under scrutiny for helping wealthy Americans evade taxes.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The Iraq cash airlift and the Bowen investigation

"Bad Actors" charges Bush with sending tens of billions of dollars in physical cash to Iraq after the 2003 invasion — $12 to $14 billion flown from Andrews Air Force Base to Baghdad aboard C-17s, with a further $5 billion wired — on the authorisation of proconsul [[paul-bremer|Paul Bremer]], and asks why the money was not simply transferred electronically, answering that cash is far harder to trace.[3] The [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury]] team working on Iraq's reconstruction said it did not know Bremer was flying in the cash and could see no reason for it.[3]

In 2004 Bush appointed his Texas friend [[stuart-w-bowen-jr|Stuart W. Bowen Jr.]] as special inspector general to investigate corruption and waste in Iraq, an appointment Joe Lange interprets as an attempt to make congressional investigations of the missing money go away; Bowen instead pursued the case seriously, tracing $1.2 to $1.6 billion to a bunker in rural Lebanon before being obstructed.[3] Lange writes that Bush "never really wanted to investigate the stolen cash" and that the exercise was a complete whitewash, noting that the Bush administration outside Bowen's office never investigated the reports at all.[3] He concludes that while Bush was constructing a public image of waging war on terrorism he was in fact funding the same terrorist groups, and that both Bush and [[barack-obama|Obama]] were puppets of [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], who controlled terrorist organisations through his control of the [[cia|CIA]].[3]
