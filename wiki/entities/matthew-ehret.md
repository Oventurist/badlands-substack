# Matthew Ehret

Matthew Ehret is a Canadian writer, historian and commentator who contributes to Badlands Media. According to the biography appended to his article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]], he is editor-in-chief of The Canadian Patriot Review, a Senior Fellow of the American University in Moscow, and director of the Rising Tide Foundation.

## Work

Ehret is the author of the four-volume Untold History of Canada series, the four-volume Clash of the Two Americas series, the Revenge of the Mystery Cult trilogy, and Science Unshackled: Restoring Causality to a World in Chaos. He co-hosts the weekly program Breaking History on Badlands Media and hosts Pluralia Dialogos, described as airing every second Sunday. He also publishes on Substack.

## Themes

Ehret's writing in the Badlands corpus advances a historical-strategic reading of contemporary geopolitics in which an "Anglo-American deep state" is contrasted with a rising [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]]. He treats the [[un-charter|UN Charter]], Franklin Roosevelt's Four Freedoms, the original Atlantic Charter and the original design of the [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods system]] as a coherent tradition of sovereign, development-oriented internationalism, and argues that this tradition is now being carried forward by the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]], the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]] and the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]] rather than by the West. He is a consistent critic of the [[rules-based-international-order|rules-based international order]] and of the Great Reset.

## Feature series (late 2025)

The November 7, 2025 Badlands News Brief roundup [[a-look-at-recent-features-84d|"A Look At Recent Features"]] listed four Ehret features from a five-week span: "Combating the Transhumanist Religion" (November 1, 2025), part two of a series on the "above ground" and "below ground" movements he says have been "carefully sculpted to quite literally, induce as many humans as possible to participate in the willful self-destruction of industrial civilization"; "On Ted Kaczynski and False Prophets" (October 25, 2025), which links [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]] to "the leading oligarchists running the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and [[great-reset|Great Reset]] today"; "The Rising Scourge of Eco-Terrorism" (October 21, 2025), which opens from the 2022 film How to Blow Up a Pipeline; and "Shadows, Sovereigns and Civil Wars - Part 2" (October 10, 2025).

## Features of mid-2025

The August 15, 2025 Badlands News Brief roundup [[a-look-at-recent-features|"A Look At Recent Features"]] highlights two Ehret features. "Trump, Putin, Alaska and the Future of Multipolarity" (August 12, 2025) previews the Alaska summit between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], noting that "what will be discussed between these two men and what arrangements have been decided are shrouded in mystery." "The Birth of America's Deep State" (August 6, 2025) is identified as the third entry in the Badlands Media "Founders & Foundations" series, following installments on Alexander Hamilton, Abraham Lincoln and Benjamin Franklin.
