# Matthew Ehret

Matthew Ehret is a historian, author and Badlands Media contributor who wrote "A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation," an analysis of the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit in Tianjin published on September 3, 2025.[1]

## Affiliations

Ehret is editor-in-chief of The Canadian Patriot Review, a Senior Fellow of the American University in Moscow, and Director of the Rising Tide Foundation.[1] He co-hosts the weekly program Breaking History on Badlands Media and hosts Pluralia Dialogos.[1]

## Writings

He is the author of the four-volume Untold History of Canada series, the four-volume Clash of the Two Americas series, the Revenge of the Mystery Cult Trilogy, and Science Unshackled: Restoring Causality to a World in Chaos.[1]

## Views

Ehret's Tianjin analysis argues that a new alternative security and financial architecture is coming online through the SCO, the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]] and the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]], and that the West should respond with humility and self-reflection rather than confrontation.[1] He frames the choice facing humanity as one between a technocratic [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and a cooperative reset modeled on Franklin Roosevelt's [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]], the [[united-nations|UN Charter]] and the original design of the [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods system]].[1] He warns that if the opportunity is missed, "the only peace the world may ever find will accompany a long nuclear winter."[1]
