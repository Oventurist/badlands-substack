# Matthew Ehret

Matthew Ehret is a Canadian writer and historian who contributes articles and features to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], where his work is presented as the opinion of the contributing author rather than the view of the outlet itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) He is the editor-in-chief of The Canadian Patriot Review, a Senior Fellow of the American University in Moscow, and Director of the Rising Tide Foundation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

Ehret has written the four-volume *Untold History of Canada* series, the four-volume *Clash of the Two Americas* series, the *Revenge of the Mystery Cult* trilogy, and *Science Unshackled: Restoring Causality to a World in Chaos*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) He co-hosts the weekly programme *Breaking History* on Badlands Media and hosts *Pluralia Dialogos*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Analytical framework

In "A Re-Alignment of World Systems," Ehret treats the removal of [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]] from Canadian government as a symptom of a broader collapse of the unipolar order.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) His framework holds that the post-1971 world was directed with the intention of destroying the moral-political and economic foundations of Western society, and that the collapse of the Soviet system in the early 1990s ushered in a unipolar order built on the European Union, [[nafta|NAFTA]], the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]] and the 1999 destruction of [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) He argues that Canada operates as a chess piece in a London-directed Great Game and that the countervailing force is the Russian–Chinese operating system shaped by the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) He has published reports on China's exposure to the trap of British Canada in 2012, 2017 and 2020.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

Ehret contrasts the protectionism, bank regulation, state credit and central authority exercised by the United States during the 1930s New Deal — and by the Eurasian New Silk Road policy today — with the [[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]] framework, which he says directs the same instruments only toward low energy flux density forms of energy that inherently lower the earth's population.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Cultural and art history

Ehret's December 26, 2025 Badlands essay "A New American Renaissance" examines the American-born painter [[benjamin-west|Benjamin West]] as an incubator and coordinator of a republican renaissance movement operating from inside the British Empire, reading West's canvases for deliberately embedded Platonic ironies and anomalies.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) The essay treats West's relationship with [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]]'s Philadelphia networks, his philosophical evenings with [[king-george-iii|King George III]] at Buckingham Palace, the [[penn-treaty-of-1682|Penn Treaty of 1682]] signed by [[william-penn|William Penn]], and the role of [[william-johnson|William Johnson]] and the Mohawks at the [[battle-of-quebec|Battle of Quebec]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) Ehret credits the Canadian-born historian [[pierre-beaudry|Pierre Beaudry]] with making the chapter possible and draws repeatedly on the work of the historian [[graham-lowry|Graham Lowry]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

The piece extends his broader framework into cultural history, arguing that British oligarchist influence within the United States was never extracted after 1783 and remained behind as a powerful fifth column over the following two centuries, and that the school of scientific thought running from Franklin through [[samuel-morse|Samuel Morse]] must be revived to overcome the paradoxes of twentieth-century cosmology and quantum mechanics.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## Commentary on the multipolar alliance

Ehret's essay "A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation" (3 September 2025) analyses the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit held in Tianjin, China on 31 August – 1 September 2025, at which [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]], [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] stood together with leaders representing nearly half the world's population and 20% of world GDP.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) He reads the resulting [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]] as the charter of a new financial and security architecture and treats the accompanying [[sco-development-bank|SCO Development Bank]] as its productive-credit component.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) He suggests the timing and character of the simultaneous fighting between Azerbaijan and SCO member Armenia, and between Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan, "smells of Anglo-Intelligence operations," while acknowledging the arsonists are not yet identified.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

The essay develops his recurring historical method of situating present events in a sequence of peace settlements: the 1648 [[peace-of-westphalia|Peace of Westphalia]], the 1815 [[congress-of-vienna|Congress of Vienna]], the [[league-of-nations|League of Nations]], and the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods]] settlements after the Second World War.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) He argues that the existence of atomic weapons makes it imperative that a comparable summit occur before rather than after the next war, and that its terms be built on [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]]'s [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]], the [[atlantic-charter|Atlantic Charter]], the UN Charter, the [[universal-declaration-of-human-rights|UN Declaration of Human Rights]] and the Bandung principles of the [[non-aligned-movement|Non-Aligned Movement]] rather than on an anti-humanist [[great-reset|Great Reset]].[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) He notes the irony that FDR's Four Freedoms are now being advanced by Eurasian powers widely regarded as the most "un-American" groups on earth, and warns that if the opportunity is missed the only peace the world may find will accompany a long nuclear winter.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Avoiding WWIII (2024)

In his 4 June 2024 Badlands essay "Avoiding WWIII: Reviving a Sane Manifest Destiny and Monroe Doctrine," Ehret argues that the origins of [[manifest-destiny|Manifest Destiny]] and the [[monroe-doctrine|Monroe Doctrine]] should not be discarded merely because "evil perverts justified evil things using them," and rejects both the reading of American history as all evil and the reading of it as all good.[4] He traces the republican strand of the doctrine from John Winthrop's 1630 "City on a Hill" speech through [[george-washington|George Washington]], [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]] and [[john-quincy-adams|John Quincy Adams]], and contrasts it with the southern perversion promoted by [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]], Jefferson Davis and Albert Pike.[4] He devotes substantial attention to rail development under [[abraham-lincoln|Lincoln]], to [[william-gilpin|William Gilpin]]'s *Cosmopolitan Railway*, to [[eco-colonialism|eco-colonialism]] and native reservations, and to the contemporary Eurasian revival of the doctrine through the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], the [[polar-silk-road|Polar Silk Road]], the [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait Tunnel]] and the [[alaska-canada-railway|Alaska-Canada Railway]].[4]

## Analysis of Canada and the central banking system

Ehret, the Canadian co-host of Breaking History, has argued that [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] is "perhaps the king of all central bankers" and was likely installed to defend Canada's surprising and largely hidden dominant position over the United States, a position rooted in Canada's high standing within the British Commonwealth.[5] He has also documented what he describes as subversive Deep State operations launched against the United States from Canada, including the plot that led John Wilkes Booth to assassinate President [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]].[5]
