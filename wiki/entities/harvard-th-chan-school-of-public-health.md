# Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health

The Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health is the public health faculty of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] and, in Badlands Media's account, the institutional hinge connecting the university to Chinese state-linked bodies and to global pandemic governance.[1]

## The Chan donation and the China partnership

The school bears the name of the Chan family, Hong Kong real estate tycoons with extensive links to Beijing, following a $350 million donation.[1] That gift underwrote the creation of the [[harvard-china-health-partnership|Harvard-China Health Partnership]], through which the school trained officials of the sanctioned [[xinjiang-production-and-construction-corps|Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps]] as late as October 2023.[1] Badlands Media contended that a donation of that scale does not sit passively: "You steer the curriculum. You shape the research agenda. You hire the faculty."[1]

## Rockefeller influence

The [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] has funded Harvard research centers since the early twentieth century, and the article argues that its fingerprints are especially visible in public health policy channelled through the Chan School.[1]

## Pandemic governance role

Alongside the [[harvard-global-health-institute|Harvard Global Health Institute]], the Chan School supplied advisors and operational models to the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] during the COVID-19 crisis, with WHO Strategic Preparedness and Response Plan documents citing Harvard-originated risk algorithms and response models.[1] These contributions included behavioral nudging frameworks to increase vaccine uptake, justification models for quarantine extension, and algorithmic impact assessments for misinformation response.[1] Many of the researchers involved were simultaneously under contract with [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]] initiatives, GAVI, or consulting arms such as the McKinsey Global Institute.[1]
