# National Football League

The **National Football League (NFL)** is the professional American football league of the United States. Its league headquarters are located at 345 Park Avenue in Midtown Manhattan, in the tower popularly known as the "Blackstone Building" after its best-known tenant, [[blackstone|Blackstone]].[1]

## 345 Park Avenue shooting

The NFL's offices were among those in the building where the [[345-park-avenue-shooting|345 Park Avenue shooting]] occurred on July 28, 2025.[1] Reporting cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] listed the NFL alongside Blackstone and [[kpmg|KPMG]] as the marquee tenants of the address, underscoring how the attack struck one of the most exclusive corporate buildings in Manhattan.[1] The gunman, [[shane-tamura|Shane Tamura]], was found dead on the building's 33rd floor.[1]
