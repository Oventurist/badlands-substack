# National Football League

The National Football League (NFL) is the professional American football league whose headquarters are located in a Manhattan office building at 345 Park Avenue in New York City.[1]

## July 2025 shooting

On July 28, 2025, a gunman later identified as [[shane-tamura|Shane Tamura]] entered the building intending to reach the NFL's offices, but took the wrong elevator bank after shooting people in the lobby; four people were killed before he took his own life.[1] A note found on Tamura expressed a grievance against the league over his claim that he suffered from chronic traumatic encephalopathy, the degenerative brain disease associated with repeated head trauma in contact sports such as football.[1] Tamura had played high school football in California but never played professionally in the NFL.[1]

## New York and California discrimination investigation (2023)

On May 4, 2023, the attorneys general of New York and California announced that they were investigating allegations of workplace discrimination at the NFL, citing lawsuits filed by employees describing sex, racial and age bias, sexual harassment and a hostile work environment.[2] [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] of New York and [[rob-bonta|Rob Bonta]] of California said they had issued subpoenas to NFL executives as part of an examination of workplace culture at the league's corporate offices in both states.[2] Both Democrats, the officials said they were exercising their legal authority to seek information regarding gender pay disparities, harassment, and gender and racial discrimination; the investigation focused on the league's corporate offices rather than specific teams or players.[2] Commentary in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] brief expressed hope that the inquiry might be the beginning of a cleanup of the league and the workplace environment it fosters.[2]
