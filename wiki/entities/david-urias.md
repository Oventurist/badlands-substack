# David Urias

David Urias is a United States District Judge for the District of New Mexico, appointed to the federal bench by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Ruling against the New Mexico carry suspension

On Wednesday, September 13, 2023, Urias blocked part of a public health order issued by Governor [[michelle-lujan-grisham|Michelle Lujan Grisham]] that suspended the right to carry firearms in public across New Mexico's largest metropolitan area.[1] He granted a temporary restraining order halting the suspension of gun rights until a further hearing scheduled for early October, siding with plaintiffs who argued the governor had trampled on constitutional rights.[1]

Urias said he did not blame Lujan Grisham for attempting to act "in the face of these terrible deaths," but that his duty was to decide the narrower question of the rights afforded under the U.S. Constitution.[1] The ruling was reported as a significant setback for the second-term Democratic governor, who said in a statement the same day that she would not be resigned to the status quo.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the swift rebuke of what she called an unconstitutional disarmament play was unsurprising but refreshing in its speed, and cautioned that progressive commentary praising the ruling on states'-rights grounds was laying groundwork to alter the [[second-amendment|Second Amendment]] rather than defend it.[1]

Additional reporting on the ruling noted that the emergency public health order had been imposed the preceding Friday and applied a statistical violent-crime threshold covering only Albuquerque and the surrounding metro area, and that Urias himself was appointed to the federal bench by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[2] His temporary restraining order blocked the suspension of gun rights until another hearing scheduled for early October, amid mounting criticism and widening political divides over the governor's action.[2] The broader dispute is covered at [[new-mexico-public-health-order-suspending-concealed-carry|the New Mexico public health order]].[2]
