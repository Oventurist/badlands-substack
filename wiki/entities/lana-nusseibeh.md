# Lana Nusseibeh

Lana Nusseibeh is an Emirati diplomat serving as a special envoy of the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], and one of the most senior Emirati voices on relations with Israel.[1]

## Warning against West Bank annexation

In September 2025, Nusseibeh delivered what The Times of Israel described as a shocking alarm bell from Abu Dhabi, warning Israel that annexing the West Bank would cross a "red line."[1] Speaking in an interview conducted at the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Abu Dhabi, she said: "Annexation would be a red line for my government, and that means there can be no lasting peace. It would foreclose the idea of regional integration and be the death knell of the two-state solution."[1]

The warning was issued two days before Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was scheduled to hold a major ministerial consultation on whether to advance [[west-bank-annexation|annexation]], and shortly before the fifth anniversary of the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] — the normalization framework the UAE initiated by becoming the first Arab country to establish relations with Israel in more than a quarter-century.[1]

Badlands commentary framed her intervention as a continuation of the UAE's historical role as the guarantor of the Accords, noting that Emirati officials had previously intervened to restrain Israeli settlement expansion in order to preserve the normalization process.[1]
