# Lord Miles Routledge

**Lord Miles Routledge** is a British traveler and internet personality who brands himself the "Last Great British Explorer" and is best known for his repeated trips to Taliban-controlled [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]].[1]

## Detention in Afghanistan (2023)

In 2023 Western media outlets reported that Routledge had been taken prisoner by the [[taliban|Taliban]], with whom he remained for eight months.[1] In an interview with Badlands host [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], Routledge described the experience as far closer to that of an honored guest than a captive: he said he was allowed to live in a house with servants, given a laptop with internet access, watched films such as *Titanic* with his hosts — one of whom cried — was served tea and delicacies, permitted to fire RPGs at a shooting range, and allowed to troll Western journalists on X.[1] He also said the Taliban gave him a private tour of the caves at Tora Bora, where Osama bin Laden is said to have hidden after the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], and that he filmed the visit for YouTube.[1]

## The "Talibros" account

Routledge refers to his hosts as his "Talibros" and has returned to Afghanistan repeatedly to visit them.[1] He recounted to Paine several episodes in which the Taliban captured members of [[isis|ISIS]] and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] and determined that these operatives — some of them Western nationals radicalized by online propaganda — were not practicing Muslims and had no understanding of the Quran.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented Routledge's testimony as evidence that Western corporate press coverage of the Taliban has been systematically deceptive, aligning it with the account of Mike Harris and with [[just-human|Just Human]]'s research on the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]].[1]
