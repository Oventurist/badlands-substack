# Independent Order of Odd Fellows

The **Odd Fellows Society** (OFS) is a fraternal organization discussed in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — What is Clown World?" as a link between circus symbolism and occult secret societies.[1]

## Three rings

The essay states that the three rings are a symbol of the Oddfellows Society, and that the showman [[pt-barnum|P. T. Barnum]] — originator of the "three ring circus" — was a prominent member, alongside figures the author lists as Burl Ives, King George and Charlie Chaplin.[1] The author asks why Barnum settled on three rings rather than two or four, and suggests that because dark occultists characteristically invert what is good, beautiful and true, the three-ring circus may point to an inverted trinity of some kind.[1]

## Claims about lodges

The article asserts that skeletons are frequently discovered at Odd Fellows lodges and gathering places, and that even relatively mainstream sources acknowledge as much.[1] It describes the order's self-presentation as a "fraternal and charitable" organization existing to assist members financially and professionally as a cover story, noting that dozens of secret societies make the same claim and that "charity" is the magic word used to justify the existence of secret societies and NGOs alike.[1]

## Relationship to other orders

The author observes that many people lump the Odd Fellows and the [[shriners|Shriners]] together with the [[freemasonry|Freemasons]], but cautions that these groups are often compartmentalized enough that it would be unfair to accuse every member of full awareness; he reports knowing several Freemasons who were inarticulate about deep occultism in a way suggesting genuine rather than feigned ignorance.[1] His overall conclusion is that such societies have been introducing demon worship to the masses as a form of entertainment for centuries.[1]
