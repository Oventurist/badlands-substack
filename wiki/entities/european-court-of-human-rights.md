# European Court of Human Rights

The **European Court of Human Rights** (ECHR) is the supranational court that adjudicates alleged violations of the European Convention on Human Rights, and appears in the Badlands corpus as the final avenue of recourse in the [[assange-extradition-case|Assange extradition case]].[1]

## Role in the Assange proceedings

According to Reuters reporting of February 20, 2024 cited by Badlands, if [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] won his High Court challenge a full appeal hearing would follow, but if he lost his only remaining option would be the European Court of Human Rights.[1] His wife [[stella-assange|Stella Assange]] said his lawyers would apply to the court for an injunction if that became necessary.[1] Supporters of Assange feared he could be flown to the United States before any such intervention could take effect.[1]
