# Sinaloa Cartel

## Regional reach and 2026 indictments

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recounted that in February 2025 Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] held a press conference with his generals announcing that the Colombian militias operating in the jungles against the government since the early 1960s were in fact proxy forces of the Sinaloa cartel, which operates out of [[mexico|Mexico]]; according to Petro, the Sinaloa cartel is "the head of the snake" in the cocaine trade.[1]

A year later, the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration indicted ten Mexican government officials accused of being connected to the Sinaloa cartel, a step the commentator described as "moving up the food chain" of cartel-controlled officials, and which coincided with Trump's accusation that Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] is a cartel puppet.[1]
