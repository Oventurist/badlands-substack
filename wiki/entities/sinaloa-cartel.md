# Sinaloa Cartel

The Sinaloa Cartel is a Mexican transnational criminal organization presented in the Badlands corpus as the apex node of the hemispheric cocaine trade.

## Alleged control of the ELN

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] recounts Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]]'s disclosure that the [[national-liberation-army|National Liberation Army]] (ELN) — active since 1963 and historically linked to [[pablo-escobar|Pablo Escobar]] and the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín Cartel]] — was in reality a proxy of the Sinaloa Cartel, dispatched to secure the smuggling routes through Central America for cocaine. In the corpus this inverts the conventional narrative, placing a Mexican organization rather than a Venezuelan or Colombian one at the center of the trafficking architecture.

## Context of US strike threats

The claim is used in the Brief to argue that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s focus on cartels operating in [[mexico|Mexico]] — including his statement that the United States may conduct land strikes there and that the cartels are running the Mexican government — is better founded than parallel allegations directed at [[venezuela|Venezuela]] and [[colombia|Colombia]].


## 2026 US indictment of Mexican officials

On May 1, 2026 the [[badlands-brief-579|Badlands Brief]] reported that federal prosecutors in New York had charged multiple Mexican officials — including the sitting governor of Sinaloa and nine other current and former officials — with conspiring alongside the cartel to traffic narcotics into the United States. The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] alleged a network of officials who used their positions to facilitate drug shipments, provide protection, and enable cartel operations across state institutions. The case, brought by the US Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York, was described as one of the most sweeping corruption prosecutions tied to Mexican governance in recent years.

Badlands commentary treated the indictments as confirmation that [[mexico|Mexico]], rather than [[venezuela|Venezuela]], is the true focus of US action in Latin America. Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] had a year earlier described Sinaloa as the "head of the snake" of the Colombian drug trade, accusing it of running the [[national-liberation-army|National Liberation Army]] (ELN) and other armed groups to secure cocaine routes into Mexico. Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] publicly defended the indicted governors and demanded that Washington produce proof of their crimes.
