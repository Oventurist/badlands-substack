# M23 Rebels

M23 is an armed group operating in eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]], described in Badlands coverage as a militia that crossed into the eastern Congo from [[rwanda|Rwanda]] and seized control of mining towns.[1] Its political vehicle is identified as the Rwanda-linked Alliance Fleuve Congo, led by [[corneille-nangaa|Corneille Nangaa]].[1]

Former Congolese President [[joseph-kabila|Joseph Kabila]] was accused in 2025 of aiding and abetting M23, charged with treason, and convicted in absentia of a failed coup with the death penalty granted.[1] Kabila was reportedly killed on January 25, 2026 in an explosion — described in Badlands commentary as a possible drone strike — while meeting M23 representatives in Uvira, a town the group had taken months earlier.[1]

Nangaa publicly denounced the US–DRC minerals partnership as unconstitutional, an accusation directed at President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and President Félix Tshisekedi that the DRC presidency rejected.[1] Congress was reported to be discussing sanctions against Rwanda over the conflict.[1]

## Advance on Uvira (January 2026)

In early January 2026, fighting intensified near the city of [[uvira|Uvira]] in eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] as M23 rebels tightened control over border routes, with clashes between Congolese government forces and rebels reported near strategic transport corridors and concerns raised over cross-border movement and humanitarian access.[2]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reported that M23 and its River Alliance Coalition had moved south from Bukavu, taken Uvira, and were expanding territory by attacking Congolese military bases in North Kivu, framing the campaign as the latest regime-change pressure on the government of [[felix-tshisekedi|Félix Tshisekedi]].[2] He characterized the Congo theater as "Conflict B: The War for Resources," with an estimated $24 trillion in rare earth minerals at stake.[2]
