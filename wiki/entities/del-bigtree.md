# Del Bigtree

Del Bigtree is the host of The Highwire, an independent broadcast program. He appears in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s November 15, 2025 essay "Don't Be Symbol-Minded" as the host of the segment "The Occult Messaging of the Olympics," in which Ehret presented his analysis of the [[2024-paris-summer-olympics|2024 Paris Olympics]].[1]

Ehret cited that appearance as prior documentation of his claim that the French games not only used a [[phrygian-cap|Phrygian cap]] as their mascot but featured a number of Mithraic themes throughout — a case he accepted as demonstrable even while rejecting the wider inference that every appearance of cult symbolism proves cult control of the institution displaying it.[1]
