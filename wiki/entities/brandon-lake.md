# Brandon Lake

Brandon Lake is an American Christian recording artist at the forefront of a growing Christian music movement in the United States.[1]

## Career and "King of Hearts"

In November 2025, Lake was profiled while on the road with his 2025 album "King of Hearts," a Christian release spanning pop, rock, country and hip-hop — a mixture not always associated with worship music — whose eclecticism was cited as one reason his music is connecting.[1] The album peaked at No. 7.[1] Interviewed backstage before a packed arena show in Henderson, Nevada, fifteen miles south of Las Vegas, he described the performance as an energetic, "family-friendly" show for thousands of people.[1]

## Genre growth

According to a midyear report from the industry data and analytics company Luminate, streams of new music released in the previous 18 months were slightly down year over year except in the case of Christian/gospel music.[1] Jaime Marconette, Luminate's vice president of music insights and industry relations, attributed the genre's growth to a "younger, streaming-forward fanbase" that is 60% female and 30% millennial, with artists such as Lake, Forrest Frank and Elevation Worship leading the charge.[1]

Lake framed his ambition in evangelistic rather than commercial terms: "I pray it's just the beginning, and I pray that it takes over. And not the genre of Christian music, but songs — whatever genre they are — that carry the message of the gospel."[1]
