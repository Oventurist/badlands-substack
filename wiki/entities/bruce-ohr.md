# Bruce Ohr

Bruce Ohr was a senior official at the [[us-department-of-justice|U.S. Department of Justice]] who, in Badlands Media's account of the Russian collusion affair, served as the intermediary linking former British intelligence officer [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]], the [[fbi|FBI]] and the cybersecurity firm [[crowdstrike|CrowdStrike]].[1] His role was described as central to the production and internal dissemination of the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]] within the FBI and DOJ.[1]

## Role in the dossier pipeline

According to the article, revelations in August 2018 established that Ohr functioned as the conduit through which dossier material moved between Steele, the Bureau and CrowdStrike, while his wife [[nellie-ohr|Nellie Ohr]] worked for CrowdStrike gathering the raw material that fed the dossier — an opposition-research product ultimately paid for by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[1] A Q drop cited in the piece asks when Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] first learned of the Ohrs' involvement, and what it would mean if he had known *before* signing a [[carter-page|Carter Page]] FISA application; the author reads the drop as asserting that Rosenstein knew of the dossier's provenance in advance.[1]

## Cooperation and testimony

Badlands states that Ohr subsequently "flipped" and began testifying against other Obama-era Justice Department figures involved in the alleged conspiracy, naming Rosenstein, [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]], [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]] and [[james-comey|James Comey]].[1] The article frames this cooperation as motivated by an effort to protect Nellie Ohr, and cites it as the trigger for Rosenstein's alleged panic and his repeated telephone contacts with Lynch in September 2018.[1]
