# Steve Kupka

Steve Kupka is a Washington, D.C.-based lawyer and partner at the law firm King & Spalding.[1]

Kupka's name entered the record of the [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] trial through the testimony of [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles "Chuck" Dolan Jr.]], who said Kupka asked him to become involved with the [[young-presidents-organization|Young Presidents' Organization]].[1] Kupka wanted to organize a conference in Moscow for October 2016 titled "Inside The Kremlin," and reached out to Dolan because of Dolan's extensive experience and connections in Russia — in particular his connections with the Russian government.[1] Dolan in turn suggested to the organizer that Danchenko "could be useful to us on this," drawing Danchenko into the conference planning during the same spring of 2016 in which he was collecting material for [[orbis-business-intelligence|Orbis Business Intelligence]].[1]
