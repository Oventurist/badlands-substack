# Rick Doblin

**Dr. Rick Doblin** is identified as the founder of MAPS — the Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies — in the March 29, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "Walking the Path: From Awakening to Integration" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay quotes him for the aphorism "Awareness is the seed; integration is the soil that lets it grow."[1] The line is used to carry the article's central distinction between awakening and [[psychological-integration|integration]]: seeing through old illusions is only the start, and the harder work is holding that awareness without being consumed by it.[1] Doblin's formulation appears in the "Grounding the Shift" section directly after Lisa Feldman Barrett's account of the predictive brain, and supports the essay's summary that awareness lights the path while integration is what makes it walkable.[1]

The citation is notable in that the author, whose sources are otherwise drawn from trauma psychology and neurobiology, reaches into the psychedelic-research field for the vocabulary of integration — a term of art in that literature for the process of translating an altered-state insight into ordinary life.[1]
