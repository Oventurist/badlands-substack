# Fouad Shokr

Fouad Shokr was the military chief of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], the Lebanese armed movement.[1] He was killed on July 30, 2024, when Israeli airstrikes targeted the Hezbollah stronghold in southern Beirut.[1]

His death, followed a day later by the killing of [[hamas|Hamas]] political bureau head [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]] in Tehran, marked a sharp escalation in the regional conflict.[1] In response to the two killings, the U.S. Defense Department deployed 12 warships to the Middle East, spanning the Persian Gulf and the Mediterranean Sea.[1] The escalation also prompted South Carolina Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] to introduce a Senate resolution asserting that any major Hezbollah escalation against Israel should be treated as an attack executed by [[iran|Iran]].[1]
