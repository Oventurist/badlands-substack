# Nobel Foundation

The Nobel Foundation is the Sweden-based institution that administers the funds bequeathed by Alfred Nobel and disburses the Nobel Prizes, including the Nobel Peace Prize.[1]

## Assange criminal complaint

In December 2025, WikiLeaks co-founder [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] filed a criminal complaint against the Nobel Foundation, accusing it of misusing Nobel Peace Prize funds by awarding them to Venezuelan opposition figure [[maria-corina-machado|Maria Corina Machado]], who has publicly called on the United States to attack her own country.[1] The complaint, dated Wednesday, December 17, 2025, was submitted to Sweden's Economic Crime Authority and War Crimes Unit in the jurisdiction where the foundation is based.[1]

Assange's filing argues that Alfred Nobel's will legally restricts Peace Prize funds to purposes that promote peace and bars their use to advance war or foreign military intervention, so that disbursing prize money to Machado violates that mandate and could constitute a crime under Swedish law.[1] Assange warned that the Nobel Peace Prize risks being used as an "instrument of war."[1] The complaint cites a series of public statements in which Machado endorsed potential US military intervention in [[venezuela|Venezuela]] and defended Washington's use of force amid its military buildup in the Caribbean, including strikes on boats off Venezuela's coast that had killed 95 people to date; the US maintains it is targeting "narco-terrorists," while the United Nations has condemned the strikes as "extrajudicial killings."[1]
