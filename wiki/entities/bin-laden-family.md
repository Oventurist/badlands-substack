# Bin Laden Family

The Bin Laden family is a wealthy Saudi dynasty whose fortune derives from the construction business built by the father of [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]]. Within Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series the family appears chiefly in connection with Q drop #118 and the aftermath of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1]

## Source of the fortune

Chapter 18 of the series answers Q's question about the origin of Osama bin Laden's wealth by pointing to his father, whose construction firm made the family one of the richest in the Kingdom.[1] The chapter adds that Osama bin Laden fought alongside the [[mujahideen]] in Afghanistan, presenting this as another instance of the United States funding, arming and equipping a figure who would later be cast as an enemy, and notes that the wider subject is a deep line of inquiry it does not attempt to exhaust.[1]

## Presence in the United States on September 11

Q drop #118 asks whether any members of the Bin Laden family were in the United States at the time of the attacks; the chapter answers yes and rejects the possibility of coincidence.[1] Drawing on a Judicial Watch archive of post-attack departures and an April 2004 Los Angeles Times article, the chapter observes that Saudi nationals were flown out of the country while civilian air traffic was grounded nationwide, with a notable share of those flights leaving from John F. Kennedy International Airport in the city that had just been struck.[1] Because most of the departures were private charters, to which the usual security checks do not necessarily apply, the author argues that anything or anyone could have been carried aboard.[1]

The chapter treats the identity of whoever authorised the departures as the decisive unanswered question, placing that decision somewhere within the [[george-w-bush|Bush]] administration while declining to accuse the president himself.[1] These are Badlands Media interpretations rather than established findings.

## Assessment

The underlying departure records and press reporting are matters of public record; the inferences drawn about complicity and about the family's role are the author's, and Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
