# Scott Kirby

Scott Kirby is the chief executive officer of United Airlines Holdings Inc., the parent of [[united-airlines|United Airlines]].[1]

## March 2024 safety message

On the morning of Monday, March 18, 2024, Kirby issued a message to passengers stating that the airline was reviewing a series of aviation incidents involving its commercial jets, including [[boeing|Boeing]]-made aircraft.[1] "Unfortunately, in the past few weeks, our airline has experienced a number of incidents that are reminders of the importance of safety," he wrote, adding: "While they are all unrelated, I want you to know that these incidents have our attention and have sharpened our focus."[1]

## Commentary

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], writing in the Badlands News Brief, rejected the "unrelated" characterization, arguing that something was "rotten in the airline industry" beyond DEI or ESG explanations and framing the cluster of incidents as the controlled demolition of the airline sector in service of the [[great-reset|Great Reset]].[1] He compared the pattern to the "generated instances" language of the [[operation-northwoods|Northwoods Memorandum]] and pointed to the death of a Boeing whistleblower as evidence of desperation among entrenched interests.[1]
