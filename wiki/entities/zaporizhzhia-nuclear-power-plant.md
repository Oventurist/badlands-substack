# Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant

The **Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant** is a nuclear facility in Ukraine that has repeatedly become a flashpoint in the Russia–Ukraine war.[1]

## August 2024 fire

On Sunday, August 11, 2024, a fire appeared to break out at the complex amid the ongoing [[kursk-incursion|Kursk incursion]] crisis.[1] Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] used the incident to warn of a looming nuclear disaster.[1] The United Nations' [[international-atomic-energy-agency|International Atomic Energy Agency]] subsequently stated that the incident — which may have simply involved tires being set alight at the complex — had no impact on nuclear safety.[1]

Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] cited the episode when rejecting negotiations with Kyiv, asking what talks were possible with people who "try to create threats to nuclear power facilities."[1]
