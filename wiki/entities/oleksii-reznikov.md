# Oleksii Reznikov

Oleksii Reznikov is a Ukrainian politician who served as Defence Minister of Ukraine during the war with Russia until his dismissal in early September 2023.[1]

## Dismissal

Reznikov was removed from office by President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] amid a slew of corruption scandals surrounding the Ukrainian war effort and a struggling counteroffensive against Russian forces.[1] Although Reznikov had not personally been tied to any of the specific corruption scandals, the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] reported that his resignation had "elevated the issue to the highest level of Ukrainian politics."[1] The same reporting acknowledged that the "enduring challenge of corruption in Ukraine" had "emerged as a rare area of criticism of President Volodymyr Zelensky's leadership."[1]

His departure followed an earlier round of purges in which nine senior Ukrainian officials were sacked over corruption allegations, including the purchase of military food supplies at vastly inflated prices and the taking of luxury vacations.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed Reznikov's dismissal alongside the arrest of oligarch [[ihor-kolomoyskyi|Ihor Kolomoyskyi]] within a 48-hour window, arguing that the two moves amounted to an anti-corruption purge occurring simultaneously with a public about-face on the failed counteroffensive.[1] In his reading, the legacy media and [[nato|NATO]] were beginning to turn on Zelensky publicly just as Zelensky appeared to be executing a turn of his own: "You don't have to be a 'white hat' for your strings to be cut. Control goes both ways."[1]

## Aftermath of his dismissal

Reznikov was sacked as Ukrainian defense minister over corruption in early September 2023 and replaced by [[rustem-umerov|Rustem Umerov]].[2] Two weeks later, six deputy defense ministers were dismissed in the [[2023-ukrainian-defense-ministry-purge|September 2023 defense ministry purge]], a move the New York Times attributed to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s desire to show Washington that Ukraine was not squandering aid before appealing to [[congress|Congress]] for a new multi-billion-dollar package.[2]
