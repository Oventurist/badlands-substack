# Sde Teiman

Sde Teiman is an Israeli military detention facility used to hold detainees captured during the war in Gaza. It became internationally known as the site of an alleged sexual assault on a male Palestinian prisoner by [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldiers, a case reported in the March 13, 2026 Badlands Brief.

## The abuse case

Israeli authorities opened an investigation after video footage of the alleged abuse surfaced, leading to the arrest of several soldiers and a broader public debate inside Israel over the treatment of Palestinian detainees. According to the Associated Press reporting summarized in the Brief, military prosecutors ultimately dropped the charges, citing evidentiary issues after months of investigation.

The Badlands commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describes the leaked security-camera video as showing a large group of soldiers dragging Palestinian prisoners into a corner of the facility and assaulting them while shielding themselves from the cameras with ballistic shields. Official reports cited in that account state the victim was sodomized with objects, including a knife, causing internal injuries that required surgery, and that the events were documented in IDF military police arrest reports and hospital medical records.

## Aftermath

The arrests triggered days of demonstrations in support of the accused soldiers — dubbed the "Right to Rape" protests — including an attempt by settlers, Knesset members, and National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] to storm the base holding them. Israeli media treated the four arrested soldiers as folk heroes; only [[meir-shitrit|Meir Shitrit]] appeared on camera unmasked. The [[knesset|Knesset]] later debated whether sodomizing prisoners with objects should be lawful. Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] is described as having welcomed the dropping of the charges. The Brief also notes that IDF lawyers who leaked the footage were later identified, fired, and prosecuted for what critics called a betrayal of Israeli values.
