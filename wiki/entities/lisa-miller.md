# Lisa Miller

**Dr. Lisa Miller**, author of *The Awakened Brain*, is cited in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay attributes to Miller the description of the [[liminality|liminal]] stage as a "spiritual lull," a neural shift in which brain regions responsible for higher meaning remain active even as the emotional centres undergo transformation.[1] She is said to liken the state to a cocoon phase in which perception deepens, intuition quietly builds, and purpose reorganises itself beneath the surface.[1]

Miller's citation appears in the article's "Living Without a Map" section, where it supports the claim that the emptying-out of goals, passions and even spiritual practice at this stage "is not failure."[1] It is followed immediately by [[dan-siegel|Dan Siegel]]'s formulation that differentiation must precede integration.[1]
