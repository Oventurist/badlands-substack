# 2018 Hawaii False Missile Alert

The Hawaii false missile alert was an emergency notification issued in error on Saturday, January 13, 2018, warning residents of an inbound ballistic missile.[1] Badlands Media contributor Joe Lange presents it not as bureaucratic error but as a deliberate provocation by entrenched interests trying to trigger war with [[north-korea|North Korea]].[1]

## The alert

Panic ensued in Hawaii for roughly 40 minutes after the notice — "BALLISTIC MISSILE THREAT INBOUND TO HAWAII. SEEK IMMEDIATE SHELTER. THIS IS NOT A DRILL" — was sent to thousands of mobile phones and appeared on television.[1] Hawaii's Emergency Management Agency quickly tweeted that there was no missile threat to the state, but it took 38 minutes before a second emergency alert was issued telling the public there was no danger.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Lange writes that "the enemy was trying to trigger a war as a last desperate move in North Korea, and they didn't care at all about scaring people in Hawaii or across America."[1] In his account the [[cia|CIA]]-controlled media was simultaneously promoting fear because the agency's control of North Korea was being removed by [[donald-trump|Trump]] and [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]].[1] He pairs the alert with a second alleged incident — an unauthorized missile launch near the Whidbey Island Naval Station in Washington State, which he believes was fired from a rogue submarine toward North Korea and shot down by F-16s, and which he links to the Q references to "The Hunt For Red October."[1] Both episodes are framed as failed last-ditch attempts to restart the war before the Trump–[[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] summit.[1]
