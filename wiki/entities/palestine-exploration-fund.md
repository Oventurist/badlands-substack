# Palestine Exploration Fund

The Palestine Exploration Fund (rendered "Palestinian Exploration Fund" in the source) was the British body that sponsored archaeological survey work in Ottoman Palestine, including the Jerusalem excavations of Sir [[charles-warren|Charles Warren]].[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes Warren's leadership of the Fund as having shaped modern [[zionism|Zionism]], presenting the organization as part of a broader effort to convince the Jews of Russia and Europe to leave for the desert.[1] Ehret pairs this role with Warren's Grand Mastership of the [[quatuor-coronati-lodge|Quatuor Coronati lodge]], which he says set the stage for reviving [[solomons-temple|Solomon's Temple]], and with Warren's simultaneous command of London's Metropolitan Police during the [[whitechapel-murders|Whitechapel Murders]] of 1888.[1]
