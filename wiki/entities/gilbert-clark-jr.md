# Gilbert Clark Jr.

Capt. Gilbert Clark Jr. is a US Navy officer who served briefly as commandant of midshipmen at the [[us-naval-academy|United States Naval Academy]].[1]

## Tenure and removal

Clark assumed the commandant role in June 2025 and was fired on Monday, November 24, 2025 — less than six months into the position — due to a "loss of confidence in his ability to effectively lead" the brigade of midshipmen.[1] The removal was ordered by Superintendent Lt. Gen. Michael Borgschulte.[1]

Neither the Academy nor the Navy provided further detail on the underlying reason, and the school's statement emphasized only that the "naval service maintains the highest standards for leaders and holds them accountable when those standards are not met."[1] "Loss of confidence" is the standard formulation the US Navy uses when relieving senior leaders.[1]
