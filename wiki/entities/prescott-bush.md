# Prescott Bush

Prescott Bush was an American banker and United States senator from Connecticut, and the patriarch of the Bush political dynasty — father of President George H. W. Bush and grandfather of [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]].

In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, Prescott Bush functions as the starting point of a multigenerational "infiltration" thesis. Writing on the [[2023-trump-cpac-speech|2023 Trump CPAC speech]], contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] argued that the war-making reputation of the modern American right was "a symptom of the Neo-con Bushes and their fellow CFR infiltrators," and that following the Bush family lineage back to Prescott shows that the process "took time and patience on their part."[1] Getz summarized the thesis in a single phrase: "Infiltration over invasion."[1] The claim links the family to the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and to the rise of [[neoconservatives|neoconservatism]] within the [[republican-party|Republican Party]].[1]

Getz contrasted this account with the record of the preceding century, in which, he noted, Democratic presidents had presided over the major American wars, and argued that it was only when neoconservatism blurred party lines and allowed [[rino|RINOs]] to emerge that the right followed suit.[1]
