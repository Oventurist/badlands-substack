# United States Park Police

The United States Park Police is a federal law enforcement agency with jurisdiction over national park lands and a long-standing policing role in Washington, D.C.[1]

## Role in the 2025 DC federal surge

When the [[white-house|White House]] announced on August 7, 2025 that federal officers would patrol the streets of Washington, D.C. for seven days beginning at midnight, the Park Police was designated to lead the operation.[1] The force was joined by officers and agents from the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]], the [[bureau-of-alcohol-tobacco-firearms-and-explosives|ATF]], divisions of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] and other agencies, all operating in marked units.[1]

The deployment followed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s statement that he was considering taking control of the D.C. police department after a violent attack on a former [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] staffer.[1] Press secretary [[karoline-leavitt|Karoline Leavitt]] said the surge was directed at protecting innocent citizens and that there would be "no safe harbor for violent criminals in D.C."[1]
