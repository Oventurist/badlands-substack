# Amanda Breshears

Amanda Breshears is a resident of North Lima, Ohio, who became a widely cited witness to the apparent downwind effects of the [[east-palestine-train-derailment|East Palestine train derailment]] and the controlled chemical release that followed.[1]

## Chicken deaths in North Lima

Residents across the Mahoning Valley, including Mahoning and Trumbull counties, reported smelling chlorine after the controlled release in East Palestine on Monday, February 13, 2023; officials said it was not dangerous.[1] Breshears, who lives a little more than ten miles from East Palestine, said the smell made her eyes water when she let her dog out and expressed skepticism about the official assurances.[1] On Tuesday morning she went to feed her five hens and rooster and found them all lifeless, positioned almost identically, with no sign that a predator had entered the enclosure.[1] Her account, reported by WKBN, was cited in the February 16, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as evidence that the chemical release was more harmful than authorities acknowledged.[1]
