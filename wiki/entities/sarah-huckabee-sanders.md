# Sarah Huckabee Sanders

Sarah Huckabee Sanders is the Republican governor of Arkansas and a former White House press secretary, cited in Badlands Media commentary as an example of a prominent GOP figure who advanced state-level adoption of the [[international-holocaust-remembrance-alliance|International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance]] definition of antisemitism.[1]

## State antisemitism legislation

Writing on the House passage of the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the IHRA had for years exerted influence over governors and state legislatures to expand the legal definition of antisemitism, with the result that criticizing a foreign government had become "hate speech" in 38 states.[1] Arkansas under Huckabee Sanders was listed among those states, alongside Texas under Greg Abbott, South Dakota under [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]], Virginia under Glenn Youngkin, and Florida under Ron DeSantis.[1] The commentary noted that all of these governors had publicly boasted about the accomplishment, and framed the trend as evidence that "special interest" foreign lobby groups exercise significant influence over celebrated Republican officeholders.[1]
