# Commerce Court

The Commerce Court was a specialized United States federal court that Congress abolished in 1913, redistributing its judges to the federal appeals court.[1] The court is cited as the leading historical precedent for Congress's power to eliminate lower federal tribunals it has created.[1]

## Significance in the 2025 judiciary debate

The Commerce Court's abolition was invoked in March 2025 reporting on Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]]'s suggestion that [[us-congress|Congress]] could eliminate entire federal district courts in response to rulings against the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1] Because Article III of the Constitution establishes only the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] and empowers Congress to "ordain and establish" inferior courts, the 1913 precedent demonstrates that such courts can be dissolved by ordinary legislation.[1] See [[mike-johnson-proposal-to-eliminate-federal-courts]].
