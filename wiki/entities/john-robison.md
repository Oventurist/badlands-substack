# John Robison

John Robison was the author of *Proofs of a Conspiracy Against all the Religions and Governments of Europe* (1798), cited under the abbreviation **JR** in the Badlands Media feature "Legacy of the Bavarian Illuminati – Part 2."[1]

Robison is drawn on for the account of how [[joseph-utzschneider|Joseph Utzschneider]] and his fellow professors delivered an internal [[bavarian-illuminati|Illuminati]] document and membership list to the Duchess Maria Anna, who alerted [[carl-theodore|Duke Carl Theodore]], and for the inventory of incriminating material recovered in the 1786 police search of [[xavier-zwack|Xavier Zwack]]'s residence — including the counterfeit government seals, the poison and secret-ink recipes, and the "infernal machine" designed to destroy secret papers.[1] His work stands alongside Augustin Barruel's *Memoirs Illustrating the History of Jacobinism*, published the same year, as one of the two foundational eighteenth-century exposés of the Order.[1]
