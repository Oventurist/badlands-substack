# Bacchus

**Bacchus** is the Roman god of wine-making, ecstasy and ritualized insanity, and the Roman counterpart of the Greek Dionysus.[1] He is invoked in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — Creation Myth Clues" as the etymological root of the English word *debauchery*.[1]

The article uses this etymology to link the culture of the Roman aristocracy — famous, the author writes, for its love of debauchery — to the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult, which is said to have migrated into Western and Southern Europe over many centuries carrying a culture of debauchery, drug use, demon worship and child grooming.[1] The author presents the survival of Bacchic practice inside Roman elite life as one of several correspondences, alongside the she-wolf of [[romulus-and-remus|Romulus and Remus]], that he argues are too numerous to be coincidental.[1]
