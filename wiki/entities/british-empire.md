# British Empire

The **British Empire** is treated in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the driving agency behind the alliance system that produced the First World War.[1]

At the opening of the twentieth century the empire faced what the article calls its major threat: the worldwide spread of [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]]'s [[american-system|American System]] of nationalist political economy, which was producing sovereign, cooperating industrial states in Germany, Japan, Russia, France, China and the [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman Empire]].[1] Leading members of the British elite, the article states, could see the stranglehold of empire evaporating as this model spread like wildfire, and the empire responded as any empire confronted with annihilation does — by burning the world.[1]

The instruments described include an unparalleled age of assassinations between 1880 and 1914, typically carried out by London-directed anarchists, which claimed Presidents Garfield and [[william-mckinley|McKinley]] in the United States, Czars Alexander I and II in Russia, and President Sadi Carnot in France in 1895.[1] Britain is further credited with annihilating the Russian army through the Imperial Japanese army in 1904, weakening Russia through the 1905 revolutionary attempt led by [[leon-trotsky|Trotsky]] and [[vladimir-lenin|Lenin]], and setting the Balkan Wars in motion via the [[young-turks|Young Turks]].[1] The [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]] and then the [[triple-entente|Triple Entente]] completed the encirclement of Germany designed by [[edward-vii|Edward VII]].[1]

After the war, the article argues, the [[league-of-nations|League of Nations]] was widely seen during the 1920s as the cover for a new British Empire, and the 1933 London Economic Conference organized under the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]] and the [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]] represented the same drive to strip nation states of the power to generate and direct credit.[1]
