# Maryland Democratic Party

The **Maryland Democratic Party** (MDP) is the state affiliate of the Democratic Party in Maryland. Among its internal bodies is the LGBTQ+ Diversity Leadership Council (DLC).[1]

## 2024 sting controversy

In June 2024 the head of the MDP LGBTQ+ Diversity Leadership Council, [[michael-knaapen|Michael Knaapen]], was recorded in a predator sting operation conducted by [[predator-poachers|Predator Poachers]], the vigilante organization founded by [[alex-rosen|Alex Rosen]].[1] Rosen said the group had "busted the head of the LGBTQ Dems of Maryland going after a 14-year-old," and posted video on X in which Knaapen described his role within the party body.[1]
