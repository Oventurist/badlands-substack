# Mike Evans

Dr. Mike Evans is an American evangelical figure, founder of the Friends of Zion Heritage Center in Jerusalem and of [[friends-of-zion|Friends of Zion]], and a confidant of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Role in the 2025 pastors' mission

Evans's organization sponsored the December 2025 delegation of 1,000 U.S. Christian pastors and influencers to [[israel|Israel]], organized in partnership with the [[israeli-ministry-of-foreign-affairs|Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs]], which was billed as the largest such group of American Christian leaders since Israel's founding.[1] Evans said most Evangelical Bible believers are Zionists, making them a key ally in efforts to counter antisemitism.[1] He told Fox News Digital that "these devils that hate Jews hate Christians just as much," and that what is being said against the state of Israel is "one hundred times worse than what the Nazis said on their party platform in 1920, and everyone is ignoring it."[1]

## Criticism

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] singled out Evans's remarks as evidence of a deliberate effort to conscript Christian clergy into Israeli state propaganda and to delegitimize critics who, in his account, merely seek sovereignty, self-governance, and an accountable government.[1] He argued that no pastor among the 1,000 had the temerity to preach against the enterprise, and that American Christians were being led astray in support of a material kingdom in a temporary world.[1]
