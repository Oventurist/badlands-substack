# Anthony Ornato

Anthony Ornato is a career [[united-states-secret-service|U.S. Secret Service]] official who was detailed to a security position in the White House, serving as Deputy Chief of Staff during the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] He became a central and contested witness in the investigation of the events of January 6, 2021.[1]

## Suppressed transcribed interview

Ornato's first transcribed interview with the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]] was conducted on January 28, 2022.[1] In it, he told [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] and committee investigators that he had overheard White House Chief of Staff [[mark-meadows|Mark Meadows]] press Washington, D.C. Mayor [[muriel-bowser|Muriel Bowser]] to request as many National Guard troops as she needed to protect the city.[1] Ornato further testified that President Trump had suggested 10,000 troops would be needed to keep the peace at the rallies and protests scheduled for January 6, 2021.[1] He also described White House frustration with Acting Secretary of Defense [[christopher-miller|Christopher Miller]]'s slow deployment of assistance on the afternoon of January 6.[1]

According to The Federalist reporting summarized in the Badlands News Brief, the committee neither accurately characterized this interview nor released the transcript, suppressing it from public review until 2024.[1] Committee allies additionally began publishing critical stories and conspiracy theories about Ornato ahead of follow-up interviews with him.[1]

## Significance

Because Ornato's account directly supported Trump officials' claims that the White House had sought a large National Guard presence, its suppression became a focal point of the [[house-administration-subcommittee-on-oversight|House Administration Subcommittee on Oversight]]'s preliminary findings on the committee's conduct.[1]
