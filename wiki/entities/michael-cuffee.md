# Michael Cuffee

**Michael Cuffee** is a former top campaign aide to Fulton County, Georgia district attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] who was accused of planning to misuse federal grant funds.[1]

According to former district attorney's office employee [[amanda-timpson|Amanda Timpson]], Cuffee intended to spend part of a $488,000 federal grant — earmarked for the creation of a Center of Youth Empowerment and Gang Prevention — on "swag," computers, and travel.[1] Timpson said she repeatedly told him that such expenditures were impossible under the terms of what she called a very specific grant, and that Cuffee had told colleagues the office would obtain MacBooks, swag, and travel money from the award.[1]

When Timpson raised the matter with Willis in a November 19, 2021 meeting, Willis said she respected Timpson's assessment and was not saying it was wrong, later apologizing and stating that Cuffee had "failed" her administration.[1] Timpson was nonetheless terminated less than two months afterward.[1]
