# Andrew Bosworth

Andrew "Boz" Bosworth is the [[meta-platforms|Meta]] executive who serves as the public voice of the company's [[reality-labs|Reality Labs]] and metaverse efforts.[1]

## March 2026 Horizon Worlds statements

In March 2026 Bosworth announced that Meta would not shut down [[horizon-worlds|Horizon Worlds]] in VR as previously planned, saying, "We will keep Horizon Worlds working in VR for existing games, to support the fans who've reached out."[1] He explained that users who already have games they like in Horizon Worlds will be able to download the Horizon Worlds app and use it in VR for the foreseeable future.[1] Bosworth also addressed what he described as misinformation about the decision, saying that the company's announcement that it was "moving away from Horizon Worlds in VR" had been turned into headlines claiming Horizon was dead: "It's not. And likewise, VR is not dead. We're continuing to invest tremendously."[1]
