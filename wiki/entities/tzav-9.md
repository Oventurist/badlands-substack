# Tzav 9

**Tzav 9** is an Israeli activist group described in Badlands Media coverage as a Jewish terrorist organization that has been sanctioned by the United States government for disrupting, attacking and blocking humanitarian aid convoys bound for [[gaza|Gaza]].[1] The convoys targeted include aid caravans sent by Jordan, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates carrying food and medicine, and video of the attacks circulated widely on social media.[1] The group is reported to be funded by non-governmental organizations controlled by Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]].[1]

## Domestic criticism

In a column published by the Israeli outlet [[haaretz|Haaretz]] under the title "If Israelis Don't Rise Up Now, We'll Have Nothing Left to Fight For," Zehava Galon wrote that Tzav 9 "is not just a radical organization composed of pimply youths from the hilltops" but a body whose members are afforded protection by the [[israeli-police|police]] and by the minister responsible for the police.[1] Galon paired this with a reference to [[benzi-gopstein|Benzi Gopstein]], who she noted moves freely in and out of the [[knesset|Knesset]] that bars entry to relatives of the hostages, and argued that mounting international sanctions on Israeli individuals and organizations were a warning signal being misread by the Israeli government as antisemitism.[1]

Badlands commentary cited the group's activities, and its treatment by Israeli authorities, in posing the question of which government in the region should properly be described as a terrorist state.[1]
