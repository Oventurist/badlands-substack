# Steven Seagal

Steven Seagal is an American action-film actor and martial artist who became a naturalized Russian citizen and an envoy for the Russian Foreign Ministry.[1] In June 2024, at age 72, he was awarded Russia's Order of Friendship by President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in a ceremony at the Yekaterininsky Hall of the Kremlin in Moscow, an event that went viral internationally.[1]

## Order of Friendship ceremony

Seagal received the honor for his "big contribution to development of international cultural and humanitarian cooperation" in his role as an envoy to the United States.[1] After Putin personally pinned the medal on his chest, Seagal claimed in his speech that "Before the special operation, Ukraine was known for human trafficking, organ trafficking, narcotrafficking, child sex trafficking, biochemical warfare labs, fascism, and Nazism."[1] He added, "We still try to make them our brothers. These poisons can affect the entire world, not just us," and warned that "the whole world is at risk of becoming dangerously close to entering World War Three."[1] [[daily-mail|The Daily Mail]], which described Seagal as an "actor-turned-Putin sycophant," reported the remarks.[1] Badlands noted that a 100-year-old tank-crew veteran of the Battles of Stalingrad and Kursk also received a medal at the same ceremony.[1]

## Russian diplomatic roles

Seagal has worked for the Russian Foreign Ministry since 2013, when Putin appointed him an honorary consul to broker peace talks between Russia and America — a proposal Putin reportedly raised with President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] at the G8 summit on June 18, 2013, drawing the response "You've got to be kidding me."[1] That same year Seagal brokered meetings between top-level diplomats of both countries in the wake of the Boston Marathon bombing.[1] Putin granted him Russian citizenship in 2016.[1] In 2017 Ukraine formally banned him from the country as a "national security threat" after he described Russia's 2014 annexation of Crimea as "very reasonable."[1] On August 5, 2018, Putin elevated him to Special Representative for Russia-US Cultural Links, Cultural and Historical Heritage, with an official brief to "facilitate relations between Russia and the United States in the humanitarian field."[1] As of 2024 he faced potential EU sanctions for attending Putin's recent inauguration and calling him the world's greatest leader.[1]

## Statements on Donald Trump

Seagal told [[piers-morgan|Piers Morgan]] in 2017 that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was "kicking the New World Order's ass," and in 2018 publicly rejected the Trump-Russia collusion narrative as propaganda.[1] He congratulated Trump on Twitter on inauguration day, citing the "wonderful speech."[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted as "weird" that on the very day Putin proposed Seagal as honorary consul — June 18, 2013 — Trump joked on Twitter about becoming Putin's best friend at the Miss Universe pageant then scheduled for Moscow that November.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry connected Seagal's trafficking allegations to an Israeli organ trafficking ring busted in Turkey the prior month, suggesting a possible Black Sea geographic link to Ukrainian trafficking networks.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that Seagal had been ridiculed by MMA media for a decade over his pro-Russia stances but that those stances "have aged quite well in the modern zeitgeist," particularly under the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework.[1] Burning Bright grouped the medal ceremony with Trump's hero's welcome at UFC 302 in Newark and his cage-side encounter with [[khabib-nurmagomedov|Khabib Nurmagomedov]] as a cluster of "Russia-Martial Arts-MAGA" signals, arguing that the [[overton-window|Overton Window]] was shifting as populism reached popular culture.[1]

## Remarks on Ukraine trafficking allegations

After being honored by President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] at a Kremlin award ceremony, Seagal gave a speech in which he stated that Russia's special military operation in Ukraine was about shutting down child trafficking and [[organ-trafficking|organ trafficking]] rings as well as ending Nazism.[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who had reported on the ceremony, read the framing as implying that the conflict is in some respects a continuation of the Second World War, and paired the remarks with Russian officials' claims about [[child-trafficking|child-trafficking]] rings running through Kherson and Mariupol.[2]
