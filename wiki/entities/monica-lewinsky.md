# Monica Lewinsky

Monica Lewinsky was the White House intern with whom President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] was found to have had an affair, despite her being roughly the same age as his own daughter.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The affair began in 1996 — the same year Clinton signed the waivers permitting the transfer of missile-related technology to [[communist-china|Communist China]], and the same year Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] died in a plane crash while under criminal investigation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Role in the narrative

The Badlands account treats the Lewinsky affair less as a scandal than as a shield. Clinton's administration was, in this telling, inundated with scandal from the moment he took office, and many pundits believed the intern story might end his presidency; the article argues that was never going to happen because the Clintons were insiders and part of a protected class, and that the affair was "all a big show."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Infidelity had already been well documented before Clinton became president and posed no genuine threat, whereas treason would have.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

By consuming the Washington media narrative 24/7, the Lewinsky story is said to have pushed every other Clinton scandal — including the congressional investigations into the sale of military technology to China and into money laundering — out of the headlines and under the rug.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The whole administration went into cover-up mode over the affair while the graver matters were quietly disposed of.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The article draws a deliberate parallel between Lewinsky and another intern of 1996: [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], who entered [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s office at the age of 19 in the same period and whose story, in contrast, received no attention at all.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Newsweek described Abedin as the "gofer in the girls' club forming a leak-proof shield around the humiliated first lady" when the sex scandal detonated in 1998.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
