# Karol Nawrocki

Karol Nawrocki is a Polish right-wing politician who was the presidential candidate of the [[law-and-justice|Law and Justice (PiS)]] party in Poland's 2025 presidential election.[1]

## Dispute with Zelensky

In January 2025 Nawrocki said that Kyiv could have no future in the European Union or NATO until Ukraine takes responsibility for the World War II [[volhynia-massacres|Volhynia massacres]], which are regarded in Poland as a genocide.[1] Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] responded during a visit to Poland that week: "If Ukraine is not in the EU and not in NATO, if there are no security guarantees for Ukraine, then Pan Nawrocki should start training, because it may turn out that he will have to take up arms."[1] The remarks sparked outrage in Poland, with PiS politicians accusing Zelensky of interfering in the upcoming Polish presidential election — an allegation notable for being directed at a NATO ally.[1]

## 2025 presidential victory

Nawrocki won the [[2025-polish-presidential-election|2025 Polish presidential election]], an outcome the Financial Times reported had shocked Brussels.[2] A former football hooligan with no prior political experience, he campaigned under the slogan "Poland First" and criticised [[european-union|EU]] policies on climate change, Ukraine and social issues.[2] "Let's help others, but let's take care of our own citizens first," he said on the campaign trail.[2] He was backed by US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]

EU officials said his victory imperiled the bloc's trade ambitions and cast doubt on billions of euros earmarked for Warsaw, fearing it would fatally wound the pro-EU government of prime minister [[donald-tusk|Donald Tusk]], stymie the reforms required to access EU funds, and prompt Warsaw to help block a landmark trade deal with the South American Mercosur countries.[2] His win was also read as boosting a growing Eurosceptic trend in central Europe.[2]
