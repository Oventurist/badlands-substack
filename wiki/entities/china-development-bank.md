# China Development Bank

The China Development Bank is a Chinese state policy bank whose leadership became a target of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s anti-corruption campaign.[1]

## Leadership corruption case

[[hu-huaibang|Hu Huaibang]], the bank's former party secretary and chairman, pleaded guilty in July 2020 to illegally receiving money and goods worth 85.5 million yuan — roughly $12.2 million — between 2009 and 2019, after being caught the previous year in Xi's sweeping anti-corruption drive.[1] The case was heard by a court in the northern city of Chengde.[1]

## Significance in Badlands Media commentary

Joe Lange's "The Master Builder" cites the China Development Bank case as one of several examples of senior Chinese bankers being prosecuted, presenting the pattern as evidence that Xi Jinping is dismantling a corrupt financial network inside the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] in parallel with what the article describes as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s campaign against the global central-bank debt system.[1]
