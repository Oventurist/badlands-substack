# Wagner Group

The Wagner Group is a Russian private military company that has operated in Syria, Ukraine and across Africa. It appears in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief in commentary on Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]]'s accusation that [[france|France]] is pursuing "revanchist" policies in Africa and attempting to overthrow nationalist governments in the Sahara–Sahel region.

Responding to Lavrov's remarks, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] writes that the campaign he calls "The War of Sovereignty" targets the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]], led by Burkinabè President Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]], and that the pressure comes not only from ISIS and coup plotters he attributes to the [[deep-state|Deep State]] but also from Ukrainian mercenaries. He states that it was the Russian military and the Wagner Group that trained the Sahelian forces in Syria to fight, and speculates about whether Russia will re-mobilize and redeploy to the Sahel in response.

The Brief's treatment thus casts Wagner as a training and force-generation vehicle underpinning the military capacity of the Sahel juntas, positioning Russian paramilitary involvement as the counterweight to French influence in the region.
