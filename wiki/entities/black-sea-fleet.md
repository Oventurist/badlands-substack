# Black Sea Fleet

The Black Sea Fleet is the Russian naval formation based at Sevastopol on the Russian-annexed Crimean peninsula.[1] During the war in Ukraine it became a repeated target of Ukrainian long-range missile strikes, and its headquarters and command staff featured prominently in competing claims about battlefield results.[1]

## September 2023 strikes on fleet headquarters

On 22 September 2023 the fleet's headquarters in Sevastopol was struck in a missile attack.[1] Ukrainian special forces subsequently claimed that the fleet's commander, [[viktor-sokolov|Viktor Sokolov]], had been killed along with 33 other officers, with 105 more wounded.[1] The claim collapsed the next day when the Russian defence ministry released footage of Sokolov attending a video conference with Defence Minister [[sergei-shoigu|Sergey Shoigu]] and other senior commanders.[1]

## Significance in the information war

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary presented the episode as evidence that reporting on the Ukraine war could not be taken at face value, placing it alongside other narratives — the "Ghost of Kiev," Nord Stream 2, the Wagner coup and the Kerch Bridge explosion — that Badlands writers regarded as propaganda.[1]
