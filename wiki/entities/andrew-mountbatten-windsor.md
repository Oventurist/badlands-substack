# Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor

Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor is the name assumed by the former [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] after [[buckingham-palace|Buckingham Palace]] announced on October 30, 2025 that he would no longer hold his royal title.[1]

## Loss of title and residence

The Palace said His Majesty had initiated a formal process to remove Andrew's Style, Titles and Honours, and that he would thereafter be known as Andrew Mountbatten Windsor.[1] Formal notice was served to surrender his lease on Royal Lodge — which had until then provided him legal protection to continue in residence — and he was to move to alternative private accommodation.[1] The censures were described as necessary notwithstanding his continued denial of the allegations against him.[1]

The action followed renewed scrutiny of his ties to convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], after he had already relinquished the Duke of York title earlier in October 2025.[1]

## Significance of the Mountbatten name

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Andrew is now "royal by blood but not by title," and that the crown's choice of Mountbatten was itself notable, explicitly linking the disgraced former prince to the Mountbatten descendants of Queen Victoria — "the grandmother of Europe" whose descendants took over the continent's royal houses.[1] The Mountbattens have been royal in blood but not in title since 1917.[1]
