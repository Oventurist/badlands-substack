# United States Department of Labor

The United States Department of Labor (DOL) is the federal executive department responsible for occupational safety, wage and hour standards, unemployment insurance, and the labor-certification processes that underpin employment-based immigration. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears principally through the enforcement work of its [[office-of-inspector-general|Office of Inspector General]].

## H-1B and PERM fraud investigation (July 2026)

As reported in the July 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0bd|Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles"]], the Department's OIG launched a major nationwide investigation into fraud and human trafficking tied to the [[h-1b-visa|H-1B visa]] and [[program-electronic-review-management|Program Electronic Review Management]] (PERM) systems — both of which run through Labor Department certification. The OIG press release stated that "these abuses undermine the integrity of Department of Labor programs designed to address genuine labor shortages — not to line the pockets of bad actors at the expense of American jobs," and described the effort as a commitment "to dismantle human trafficking and forced-labor networks that exploit the foreign guest worker visa system."

Inspector General [[anthony-desposito|Anthony P. D'Esposito]] framed the matter as more than administrative: "This isn't just paperwork fraud—it's the exploitation of vulnerable workers, forced labor, the displacement of American workers, and abusive human trafficking." The Department announced a reporting campaign urging US and foreign workers to report suspected abuses, with rewards potentially available for information leading to successful prosecutions.

## Significance in the Badlands reading

Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the investigation as a vindication of the 2024 public argument over H-1B visas, writing that the earlier controversy "educated and elevated the public mind to actually grasp the widespread corruption and criminality of US labor markets" and seeded support for accountability. In her framing, Labor Department enforcement is one of the practical tests of whether the [[america-first|America First]] agenda can be reconciled with existing guest-worker structures.
