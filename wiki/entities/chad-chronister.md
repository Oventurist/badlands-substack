# Chad Chronister

Chad Chronister is the Sheriff of Hillsborough County, Florida, the jurisdiction covering the Tampa metropolitan area.[1]

In March 2026 Chronister's agency, the [[hillsborough-county-sheriffs-office|Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office]], announced the arrest of 89 people in a multi-week undercover operation run between January 1 and February 6, 2026, which produced 1,217 felony charges related to human and sex trafficking and led to the rescue of a missing 17-year-old girl and the recovery of a 2-year-old child.[1]

Announcing the results, Chronister issued a direct warning to potential offenders: "If you are using a hotel room, a chat app, or a fake profile to pursue a child, we are there. Our detectives will follow the digital trail all the way to your door."[1] Badlands coverage cited the operation as part of a wider federal and local surge against [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]] since the start of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term.[1]
