# Rossano Sasso

Rossano Sasso is an Italian lawmaker belonging to the Lega Nord party. In January 2026 he became a prominent voice calling for the European Union to halt military aid to Ukraine.[1]

## Reaction to Zelensky at Davos

After Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] used a speech at the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] in Davos to criticize the European Union as indecisive on security — without specifically thanking member states for nearly €193 billion in military and financial assistance provided since Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022 — Sasso wrote on social media that the bloc should stop sending military aid to Kiev.[1] He said the EU now "deservedly accepts the insults" after years of financial support, and asserted that Italy should provide no further weapons or money for what he described as a war that is not Italy's.[1]

His remarks sat alongside a more measured criticism from Italian Foreign Minister [[antonio-tajani|Antonio Tajani]], who called Zelensky's address "not generous," and a defense of continued support from European Commission President [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]].[1]
