# Randy Guynn

Randy Guynn is a lawyer with Davis Polk & Wardwell who has long been among the most prominent Wall Street attorneys working on banking compliance.[1]

## Testimony on the STABLE Act

Guynn appeared as a pro-digital-assets witness at the [[house-financial-services-committee|House Financial Services Committee]] hearing on stablecoins held Tuesday, March 11, 2025, alongside a senior executive from BNY.[1] He argued that the safeguards contemplated by the [[stable-act|Stablecoin Transparency and Accountability for a Better Ledger Economy Act]] should place issuers of digital tokens under protections similar to those applied to banking.[1]

"If a permitted stablecoin issuer has a properly calibrated reserve of liquid assets, capital buffer and no material amount of liabilities other than its stablecoin liabilities, as contemplated by the STABLE Act, its payment stablecoins should be as safe as insured bank deposits and central bank money," his testimony stated.[1]

[[coindesk|Coindesk]] presented Guynn's participation — a career representative of Wall Street testifying in favor of crypto legislation — as evidence that the traditional financial system had begun putting its weight behind [[stablecoin|stablecoin]] regulation as congressional momentum toward crypto legislation increased.[1]
