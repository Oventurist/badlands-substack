# Nadav Argaman

Nadav Argaman is a former director of [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]], Israel's domestic security service, and the subject of a police complaint filed by Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] in March 2025.[1]

## Blackmail complaint

Netanyahu filed a police complaint accusing Argaman of blackmail after Argaman appeared on Israeli media and threatened to reveal information about the prime minister.[1] According to Badlands commentary, the threatened disclosures concerned Netanyahu's relationship with [[qatar|Qatar]] and money that was given to [[hamas|Hamas]] in 2018 and 2019.[1]

The complaint was described as separate from — though contemporaneous with — Netanyahu's effort to remove the sitting Shin Bet chief, [[ronen-bar|Ronen Bar]], a convergence that Badlands writers read as a broader confrontation between the prime minister and Israel's security establishment.[1]

## Blackmail allegation and the Netanyahu–Hamas funding claim (March 2025)

In March 2025, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] filed a police report alleging that Argaman, who directed the [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]] from 2016 to 2021, was blackmailing him, citing an interview in which Argaman cryptically threatened to expose certain truths regarding Netanyahu and the October 7 attack.[2] According to Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], Argaman explicitly referenced Netanyahu's previously exposed funding of [[hamas|Hamas]] in 2018–19 after the group lost the support of the Palestinian Authority — a factor said to explain Netanyahu's loss of support among moderate Zionists and his turn toward Kahanist and religious-zealot coalition partners who did not object to the funding because the faction being supported shared their appetite for war.[2] The episode preceded Netanyahu's announced dismissal of sitting Shin Bet chief [[ronen-bar|Ronen Bar]] later that month.[2]


## Claims regarding Netanyahu and Qatar (March 2025)

In an interview in mid-March 2025, Argaman, a former director of the [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]], claimed that he had knowledge of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s dealings with [[qatar|Qatar]] concerning the funding of [[hamas|Hamas]], and threatened to go public with it.[3] His claim surfaced alongside the cabinet's move to dismiss sitting Shin Bet chief [[ronen-bar|Ronen Bar]], who stated in a letter to the government that he was investigating Netanyahu's inner circle for corrupt ties to Qatar.[3]
