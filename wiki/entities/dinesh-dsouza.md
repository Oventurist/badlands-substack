# Dinesh D'Souza

Dinesh D'Souza is a conservative author and filmmaker.[1]

In July 2026 he was among the prominent conservative figures who condemned [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] after evidence from [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]]'s preliminary hearing was said to undercut her claims of a conspiracy in the murder of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], calling Owens a "malignant narcissist."[1] [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]], [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]], Catturd and Lauren Southern also condemned her.[1]
