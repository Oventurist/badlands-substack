# Dinesh D'Souza

Dinesh D'Souza is an American conservative author, filmmaker, and commentator.

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that D'Souza called [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] a "malignant narcissist" after evidence from [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]]'s preliminary hearing undercut her claims of a conspiracy in the murder of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]]. His remark came amid a wave of condemnation of Owens by prominent conservative figures.
