# Citizens Redistricting Commission

The Citizens Redistricting Commission is the California body responsible for drawing the state's congressional district maps independently of the legislature.[1]

## Role in the Proposition 50 fight

Under [[california-proposition-50|Proposition 50]], the legislatively approved constitutional amendment placed before California voters at the November 4, 2025 statewide special election, a "Yes" vote would implement new temporary legislative maps for the next election cycle, while a "No" vote would maintain the current maps drawn by the Citizens Redistricting Commission until the 2030 Census prompts new lines.[1]

The measure's text framed the temporary maps as a response to partisan [[mid-decade-redistricting|mid-decade redistricting]] efforts in Republican-led states including Texas, Florida and Ohio, with the stated goal of ensuring fair representation for California communities.[1] Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] and other supporters described it as a counter to efforts that could expand Republican control in Congress.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that by openly admitting its intentions for redrawing congressional districts before the midterms, California may have created a "judicially cognizable" situation of the kind the Supreme Court found lacking in [[texas-v-pennsylvania|Texas v. Pennsylvania]], and suggested Texas, voters, candidates and citizens should sue.[1]
