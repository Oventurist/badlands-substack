# Thomas Watson

**Thomas Watson** was the founder of [[ibm|IBM]] and, in Badlands Media's account of the mechanization of [[eugenics]], the man who supplied punch card computers to [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] and the Nazis for use in the death camps.[1]

The article dates the entry of computing into the eugenics field to 1928, when some of the first computers were put to use in the field, and notes that concentration camp inmates were tattooed with human ID numbers generated by the system. Readers are directed to the book *IBM and the Holocaust* for a fuller account.[1]
