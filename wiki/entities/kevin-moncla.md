# Kevin Moncla

Kevin Moncla is an investigator with Election Oversight who works on election-records analysis, particularly relating to Georgia's 2020 election.[1]

## Why We Vote appearance

On the January 2, 2026 edition of [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] (episode 156), Moncla joined former Maricopa County Recorder [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]] and hosts [[canncon|CannCon]] and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] for a discussion of Georgia's 2020 election and the 315,000 unsigned poll closing tapes.[1] The segment aired as election legitimacy became the dominant theme heading into the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].[1]
