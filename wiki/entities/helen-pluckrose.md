# Helen Pluckrose

Helen Pluckrose is a writer and critic of critical social justice scholarship, and one of the three participants in the [[grievance-studies-affair|Grievance Studies affair]].[1]

With mathematician [[james-lindsay|James Lindsay]] and philosopher [[peter-boghossian|Peter Boghossian]], Pluckrose produced a series of fabricated academic papers on intentionally absurd or immoral premises and succeeded in placing several of them in peer-reviewed journals.[1] The project — sometimes called "Sokal Squared" — was intended to demonstrate that entire fields of scholarship would publish nonsense provided it flattered the correct ideological priors.[1]

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] invoked Pluckrose's work when discussing the February 2024 retraction of an AI-generated paper by a leading cell biology journal, arguing that such operations exposing "false authorities" are "sorely needed" in a culture conditioned to substitute peer review for direct empirical experience.[1]
