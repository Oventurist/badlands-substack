# Ryan Chew

Ryan Chew is an elections consultant associated with [[the-elections-group|The Elections Group]] who figures in commentary about the administration of the 2020 presidential election in Wisconsin.[1]

## Role in 2020 election commentary

Writing in the Badlands Brief in May 2026, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Chew, along with former Milwaukee Election Commission director [[claire-woodall-vogg|Claire Woodall-Vogg]], "need to be interrogated" in connection with events at Milwaukee's [[milwaukee-central-count-facility|Central Count facility]] during the 2020 election.[1] The commentary noted Chew's affiliation with The Elections Group and drew a parallel to [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]] of the same organization, who was described as delivering "the answer needed" during Georgia's 2020 recount.[1] Ashe presented the overlap as evidence of "same sources and methods and actors across states," a recurring theme in [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]].[1]
