# Buckingham Palace

Buckingham Palace is the London residence and administrative headquarters of the British monarch, and the institutional voice through which official announcements of the Royal Household are made.[1]

## Removal of Prince Andrew's titles (October 2025)

On Thursday, October 30, 2025, Buckingham Palace announced that [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] would leave his residence at Royal Lodge and would no longer hold his royal title, becoming known as [[andrew-mountbatten-windsor|Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor]].[1] The move came amid renewed scrutiny of Andrew's ties to convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]; earlier that month the prince had relinquished his Duke of York title, saying that "continued accusations about me distract from the work of His Majesty and the Royal Family."[1]

The Palace statement said His Majesty had "initiated a formal process to remove the Style, Titles and Honours of Prince Andrew," noted that his lease on Royal Lodge had to that point provided him legal protection to remain in residence, and confirmed that formal notice had been served to surrender the lease so he would move to alternative private accommodation.[1] The statement added that the censures were "deemed necessary, notwithstanding the fact that he continues to deny the allegations against him," and that Their Majesties' "thoughts and utmost sympathies have been, and will remain with, the victims and survivors of any and all forms of abuse."[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that the crown's choice of the Mountbatten name was itself notable, explicitly linking Andrew to the Mountbatten descendants of Queen Victoria, and noting that the Mountbattens have been royal in blood but not in title since 1917.[1]
