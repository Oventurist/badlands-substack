# Venezuela

Venezuela is a South American state that, in the Badlands corpus, became the focal point of a 2025–2026 US operation resulting in the capture of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and the installation of an interim government.

## The Maduro operation and its aftermath

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] treats "the recent US operation involving Venezuela" as the reference event for the region: it prompted criticism from Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], it was cited by German President [[frank-walter-steinmeier|Frank-Walter Steinmeier]] as evidence that US policy is eroding the global order, and Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] invoked it as a template for a "Maduro-style" operation against Chechen leader [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]].

## Legal case against Maduro

The Brief reports emerging claims that the US government's case against Maduro hinges on the testimony of a single Venezuelan general, given as part of a plea deal for clemency. If accurate, the commentary suggests, the prosecution could collapse in court and Maduro could be publicly exonerated of allegations that he is a cartel boss. Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] publicly requested that Maduro receive a fair trial.

## Interim government and EU recognition

An EU Commission spokesperson reportedly said the [[european-union|European Union]] does not recognize the legitimacy of Venezuela's interim president [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] but will maintain "targeted engagement" with Venezuelan authorities. The Badlands commentary attributes this stance to European disappointment that [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] did not become Venezuela's leader.
