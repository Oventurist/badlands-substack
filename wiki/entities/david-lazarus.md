# David Lazarus

**David Lazarus** is a Special Agent with the [[united-states-capitol-police|United States Capitol Police]] whose testimony in the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]] seditious-conspiracy trial became the subject of a perjury allegation after the release of Capitol closed-circuit television footage from January 6, 2021.[1]

## Testimony and the CCTV discrepancy

Lazarus testified that he witnessed then-Capitol Police officer [[harry-dunn|Harry Dunn]] interacting with members of the Oath Keepers inside the Capitol on January 6, testimony that contributed to the convictions in that case.[1] Blaze Media investigative journalist [[steve-baker|Steve Baker]] reported in January 2024 that just-released time-stamped CCTV clips showed Lazarus in other parts of the Capitol complex at the time he claimed to have observed that interaction, and concluded that he had given false testimony.[1]

Baker had first raised the issue in an analysis published on October 4, 2023, which asked whether the security chief for then-Speaker Nancy Pelosi had perjured himself in the Oath Keepers trial.[1] At the time, journalists could review the January 6 footage only on House subcommittee terminals in Washington, D.C., in a limited and restricted capacity, and Blaze News was permitted to publish only a single screenshot of Lazarus after video permissions were suspended in the wake of House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]]'s ouster the previous day.[1]

## Congressional response

By early November 2023, Rep. [[barry-loudermilk|Barry Loudermilk]] (R-Georgia) released multiple January 6 video screenshots documenting Lazarus's movements in the Capitol, telling Baker in a statement that he did so because "an allegation of a Capitol Police officer lying under oath is very serious and must be fully investigated."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the Lazarus case as evidence that the withholding of January 6 footage was intended not to protect Capitol security but to prevent public recognition of perjury committed during the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]] proceedings and the ensuing prosecutions.[1] She noted that Badlands' own [[brian-lupo|Brian "CannCon" Lupo]] had reported on government abuses in the case of [[jeremy-brown|Jeremy Brown]], and called for the release of January 6 prisoners and accountability for those who lied under oath.[1]
