# Mehr News Agency

Mehr is a semi-official Iranian news agency.[1] In April 2024, amid expectations of an Iranian retaliation against Israel, Mehr became the focal point of an international information scare when it published — and then rapidly deleted — a report claiming that Iran was closing its airspace over Tehran.[1]

## The Tehran airspace report

The original post, carried on Mehr's official channel on X, cited the Iranian defense minister as saying that all air traffic over Tehran had been suspended "due to military drills."[1] Mehr subsequently removed the report and denied in a new post that it had ever published such news.[1]

The episode spread quickly through large influencer accounts, generating conflicting and largely unsourced claims. Open-source researchers described in Badlands commentary as "planefags" — anons who monitor live flight trackers — posted maps showing aircraft still flying over Iran, refuting pro-Iran accounts insisting the closure had occurred.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used the Mehr episode as a case study in the hazards of the modern information environment, noting how tedious it had become to trace sourced information amid a flood of influencer amplification.[1] He argued that audiences are living in a "live fifth-generation battlespace" and must exercise restraint before letting emotion carry them into breaking news that may be disinformation.[1] The incident is cited as an example of [[information-war|information warfare]] dynamics operating through ostensibly legitimate state-linked media.[1]
