# Paul Waldman

**Paul Waldman** is an American op-ed writer and the co-author, with University of Maryland political science professor [[thomas-schaller|Thomas Schaller]], of the 2024 book *[[white-rural-rage|White Rural Rage: The Threat to American Democracy]]*.[1]

## MSNBC appearance

Waldman joined Schaller in the [[msnbc|MSNBC]] "Morning Joe" studio on Thursday, February 29, 2024 to promote the book.[1] During the segment the pair repeatedly described whites living in the countryside and across the country as "racist" and "anti-democracy," prompting an online backlash in which commenters called it possibly the most overtly racist thing they had seen said on television.[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] treated the appearance as a signal of the media treatment rural voters could expect ahead of the November 2024 election.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] characterized the book and its promotional tour as a low-impact propaganda operation whose main function was to supply "fodder for these inane news clips," and argued its more subversive purpose was to redirect public attention toward constant preoccupation with racial identity.[1]
