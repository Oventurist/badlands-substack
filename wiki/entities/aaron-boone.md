# Aaron Boone

Aaron Boone is the manager of the [[new-york-yankees|New York Yankees]].[1]

Boone was among the Yankees personnel who greeted President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the team's clubhouse on September 11, 2025, before a home game played on the anniversary of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1]
