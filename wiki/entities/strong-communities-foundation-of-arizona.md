# Strong Communities Foundation of Arizona

The Strong Communities Foundation of Arizona is an Arizona organization on whose behalf [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] filed suit in August 2024 against [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] Recorder [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]].[1]

The suit, filed on Monday, August 5, 2024, was brought for the foundation together with a registered voter and naturalized citizen, and alleged that Richer refused to verify the citizenship of voters registered in the county and failed to perform the monthly list maintenance required by state law for registrants who have not provided proof of citizenship.[1] The complaint cited a rise in the number of registered voters without proof of citizenship from 21,595 in April to 26,108 in July.[1]
