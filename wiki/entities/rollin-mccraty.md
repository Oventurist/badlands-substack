# Rollin McCraty

**Dr. Rollin McCraty** is identified in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] as a researcher at the [[heartmath-institute|HeartMath Institute]].[1]

The essay states that McCraty "has shown that a regulated heart field can stabilize others within its range," and draws from this the conclusion that a coherent, embodied energy field becomes "an environmental offering" — effective "not because you try, but because you are."[1]

The citation appears in the section "The Field Awakens - From Grid to Humanity," where the essay moves from individual to collective [[coherence|coherence]], alongside references to [[clare-graves|Clare Graves]] on humanity's evolution from fear-based systems into collaborative synergy and [[dean-radin|Dean Radin]] on intention affecting outcomes at a distance.[1]
