# Tony Buzbee

Tony Buzbee is a Houston-based trial attorney who announced in October 2024 that he represented 120 accusers alleging sexual misconduct by the hip-hop mogul [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]], who was at the time awaiting trial on sex trafficking charges.[1]

## The Combs accusers

Speaking at a news conference in Texas, Buzbee said he expected lawsuits to be filed within the following month, with most anticipated in New York and Los Angeles.[1] He described the claimant pool as 60 males and 60 females, and said 25 were minors at the time of the alleged misconduct, with one individual alleging he was nine years old when abused.[1] The allegations were said to cover a period from 1991 through 2024.[1]

"This type of sexual assault, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation should never happen in the United States or anywhere else. This should have never been allowed to go on for so long. This conduct has created a mass of individuals who are injured, scared and scarred," Buzbee said.[1] Following the announcement, an attorney for Combs responded that the performer "cannot address every meritless allegation in what has become a reckless media circus."[1]

## Badlands framing

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary treats the wave of accusations Buzbee announced not as a distraction operation nor as the deep state sacrificing one of its own, but as the takedown of the entertainment arm of what the outlet describes as a transnational cartel.[1] The Brief links the litigation to broader prosecutorial activity by the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]].[1]
