# Warren Harding

**Warren G. Harding** was President of the United States from 1921 until his death in 1923, and is presented in Badlands Media commentary as the first federal-level victory of the American nationalist forces sometimes dubbed the "American System Caucus."[1]

According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], Harding's short-lived presidency brought "a return to sanity" and placed the United States in a hostile relationship with the [[league-of-nations|League of Nations]] and its Round Table affiliates within the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and Wall Street.[1] He maintained what the article calls a healthy belligerence toward the League's anti-national mandate and worked to initiate bilateral agreements with Austria, Germany, Hungary, Russia and China outside the League's authority.[1]

The article groups Harding with [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]] and John F. Kennedy as leaders over whose dead bodies the modern Anglo-American elite took control of the United States, and argues that despite neither Harding nor FDR fully realizing their goals, the later possibility of reviving the [[united-nations|United Nations]] charter under win-win cooperation would not exist without their intervention in history.[1]
