# Peter Schweizer

Peter Schweizer is an American author and investigative researcher best known for books examining the foreign financial entanglements of American political families, including the best-seller "Secret Empires," which addressed allegations concerning the Biden family.[1]

## Claims regarding Zelensky's circle

In a July 2023 appearance on Jesse Watters' Fox News program, Schweizer asserted that one of the top officials of Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] had been present in the room during meetings at which the bribing of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] was discussed.[1] The claim was reported by The Gateway Pundit and carried in the Badlands News Brief of July 26, 2023.[1]

Schweizer further suggested that Zelensky was in a position to use that knowledge as leverage over the Biden administration in seeking weapons transfers and billions of dollars in American assistance — in effect a blackmail dynamic sitting beneath the wartime relationship between Washington and Kyiv.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the report as confirmation of a suspicion long held within the community, while noting skepticism that accountability would follow, given that [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] had by then postponed his House Oversight appearance four separate times.[1]

Schweizer's broader body of work is frequently cited in Badlands coverage as source material for the claim that the Biden family's overseas dealings, particularly those involving [[burisma|Burisma]], constituted a systematic influence-selling operation rather than isolated business activity.[1]

## Disclosure of the Biden secret phone (June 2023)

On Sunday, June 25, 2023, Schweizer revealed that [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] had been paying for a secret global phone from [[at-and-t|AT&T]] at a cost of $300 per month.[2] Speaking with [[fox-news|Fox News]]' [[maria-bartiromo|Maria Bartiromo]], he said, "We know from the laptop that Hunter Biden's business paid for a private phone line that Joe Biden used while he was vice president," describing it as a global phone from which one could reach somebody anywhere around the world.[2] The disclosure was corroborated days later when journalist [[john-solomon|John Solomon]], having obtained the number from a leaked document, called the line and [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] answered.[2]

## Reporting on Kamala Harris and Catholic Church donations

In his book *Profiles in Corruption*, Schweizer documented the flow of Catholic Church-connected money into [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s campaign for San Francisco District Attorney, writing that Harris "had no particular ties to the Catholic Church or Catholic organizations, but the money still came in large, unprecedented sums."[3]

He identified lawyer [[joseph-russoniello|Joseph Russoniello]], who represented the church on a wide variety of issues including the handling of the abuse scandal and served on the National Review Board of the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops, as donating the maximum $1,250 with a further $2,250 from his firm Cooley Godward, and as sitting on Harris's advisory council.[3] He also noted that Bingham McCutcheon, which handled legal matters for the [[archdiocese-of-san-francisco|archdiocese]] concerning Catholic Charities, donated the maximum $2,825 despite having previously given only $650 total to two other San Francisco candidates, calling the support unusual.[3] Schweizer's work is also the source of the account of how [[willie-brown|Willie Brown]]'s machine turned on District Attorney [[terence-hallinan|Terence Hallinan]] on Harris's behalf.[3]
