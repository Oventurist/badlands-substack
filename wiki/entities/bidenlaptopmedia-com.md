# BidenLaptopMedia.com

BidenLaptopMedia.com is a website launched in mid-2023 that published a searchable archive of nearly 10,000 photographs and documents recovered from [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s abandoned laptop.[1] The site was intermittently unavailable after launch because of overwhelming traffic.[1]

## Origins and content

The project was the work of [[garrett-ziegler|Garrett Ziegler]], a former Trump White House aide who had served under economic adviser [[peter-navarro|Peter Navarro]] and who runs the research nonprofit [[marco-polo|Marco Polo]].[1] Ziegler said the site took months to complete, explaining that his team had to go through roughly 10,000 photos and redact genitalia, and that "the number one thing we're about… is truth and transparency."[1] He added that "if the American people want to know what their first family is like, they're going to get it," and said the project would not exclude photos that portray the Bidens favorably.[1]

Alongside the photographs — published with location metadata — the archive linked to emails, suspicious activity reports, material on influence peddling, a transcribed copy of a diary attributed to [[ashley-biden|Ashley Biden]], and extensive personal details.[1]

## Reception

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that the Hunter Biden scandals have remained near the forefront of the information war, spanning alleged foreign dealings conducted on behalf of his father [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] as well as his personal life, and that the story "just won't die" despite obfuscation by the political and media establishment.[1] The commentary suggested that the persistence of the story implies white hats retain control over whether justice is ultimately served, and that Hunter Biden might play a more active role in the downfall of his family than many observers assume.[1]
