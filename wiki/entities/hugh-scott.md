# Hugh Scott

Hugh Scott was the Senate Republican Leader at the time the [[church-committee|Church Committee]] was established in 1975.[1]

## Role in committee selection

Together with Majority Leader [[mike-mansfield|Mike Mansfield]], Scott carefully selected the members of the Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities, balancing experienced lawmakers with junior members and ensuring that the committee's membership represented a variety of political viewpoints.[1] The bipartisan selection process produced a panel chaired by Democrat [[frank-church|Frank Church]] with Republican [[john-tower|John Tower]] as vice-chairman.[1]
