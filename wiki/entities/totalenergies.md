# TotalEnergies

TotalEnergies (TTEF.PA) is the French multinational energy company and one of the world's largest traders of liquefied natural gas.[1]

## Yuan-settled LNG transaction

In March 2023 TotalEnergies became the counterparty to [[china|China's]] first yuan-settled LNG trade, dealing with the Chinese national oil company [[cnooc|CNOOC]] through the [[shanghai-petroleum-and-natural-gas-exchange|Shanghai Petroleum and Natural Gas Exchange]].[1] The cargo involved approximately 65,000 tonnes of LNG imported from the United Arab Emirates.[1] TotalEnergies confirmed to Reuters that the LNG had come from the UAE but did not comment further on the transaction.[1]

Because the deal placed a major Western energy company inside China's campaign to settle hydrocarbon trades outside the dollar, commentators in the Truth Community read it as a sign that France might be loosening its alignment with United States financial hegemony.[1] The trade was cited alongside [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia's]] partial accession to the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]] as part of an accelerating realignment away from Western monetary structures.[1]
