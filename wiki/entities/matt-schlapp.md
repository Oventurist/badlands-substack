# Matt Schlapp

**Matt Schlapp** is chairman of the [[american-conservative-union|American Conservative Union]], the organization behind the Conservative Political Action Conference.[1]

In commentary quoted by NPR and cited in a March 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] column, Schlapp explained the January 2023 revolt against [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]]'s bid for [[2023-speaker-of-the-house-election|Speaker of the House]] in ideological terms, telling Fox Business: "By and large, the reason why Kevin McCarthy has this problem in this conference is that these fiscal conservatives do not want to follow the Mitch McConnell Senate Republican strategy."[1] He added that Republicans "passed nearly a $2 trillion omnibus with winks and nods… because they don't have the stomach to reduce federal spending."[1]

Badlands contributor [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] credited Schlapp with having "done good work for our cause" but argued that his explanation represented the conventional wisdom of establishment politicos on the right, and that the true explanation was not a lack of stomach but the operation of a [[uniparty|UniParty]] in which congressional Republicans function as controlled opposition.[1]
