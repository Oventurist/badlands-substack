# Center for Countering Digital Hate

The Center for Countering Digital Hate (CCDH) is a watchdog organization named in Badlands Media coverage alongside [[media-matters|Media Matters]], the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]], the [[southern-poverty-law-center|SPLC]] and the [[poynter-institute|Poynter Institute]] as part of the apparatus policing online speech.[1]

The article groups the CCDH with organizations it describes as symptoms of journalism's slow, agonizing death, and expresses the hope that it will receive its own day of legal judgment in the manner of the lawsuit filed by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] against Media Matters.[1]
