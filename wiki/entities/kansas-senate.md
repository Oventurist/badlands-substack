# Kansas Senate

The Kansas Senate is the upper chamber of the Kansas Legislature. In February 2023 the Republican-controlled body passed a "women's bill of rights" defining a "woman" as a biological female under state law, by a vote of 26–10 without any Democratic support.[1]

The measure, Senate Bill 180, was introduced by Republican Sen. [[renee-erickson|Renee Erickson]], who framed it as a codification of the legal definition of sex rather than a measure addressing gender identity.[1] Reporting at the time indicated the legislation was expected to pass the Republican-controlled Kansas House, but that Democratic Governor [[laura-kelly|Laura Kelly]] was not expected to sign it into law.[1]

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] characterized the vote as evidence that "a return to sanity has come to some places in the country."[1]
