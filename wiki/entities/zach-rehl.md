# Zach Rehl

Zach Rehl is a [[proud-boys|Proud Boys]] leader prosecuted in connection with January 6, 2021 and the author of the Jailed 4 J6 Journal, a firsthand account of pre-trial detention.[1]

## Jailed 4 J6 Journal

In his second journal entry Rehl described being told by jail doctors that none of his medications were carried by the facility: he wrote that the Department of Veterans Affairs had prescribed him medication for three conditions over eight years, without which his anxiety would "go through the roof," he would be unable to sleep, and he would feel constant pain — and that another government agency's prescriptions would not be honored at all.[1]

Rehl also described being subjected to rapid transfers between facilities — the practice known as [[diesel-therapy|diesel therapy]] — recounting that he was "getting shuffled all over really fast" and, when he demanded to know his charges, was told only "conspiracy."[1] He further described the absence of attorney-client privacy, writing that a correctional officer insisted on remaining in the room during his legal call on the designated "legal phone," citing COVID protocols while assuring him that he heard case discussions all day and it went "in one ear and out the other."[1]

During the Proud Boys leaders' trial, the FBI was caught reading and sharing attorney-client privileged communications involving Rehl and his counsel.[1] These accounts were cited by Badlands Media in rebuttal to Congressman [[ken-buck|Ken Buck]]'s claims that January 6 defendants had not been denied medical treatment or access to counsel.[1]
