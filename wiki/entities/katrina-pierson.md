# Katrina Pierson

Katrina Pierson is an American political operative and longtime [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ally who was called as a witness in the 2023 Colorado trial over Trump's eligibility for the state's 2024 presidential ballot.[1]

## Colorado ballot trial

Pierson was scheduled to testify on the third day of the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification trial]], following [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s appearance for the Trump defense.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], which had been livestreaming the proceedings to roughly 8,600 viewers, shut down its stream out of an abundance of caution after petitioners' attorney Eric Olson raised the issue of unauthorized recording before the court; as a result the outlet was unable to carry Pierson's testimony before the court adjourned for the day.[1]
