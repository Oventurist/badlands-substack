# Johnny Ramone

Johnny Ramone was the guitarist of [[the-ramones]] and, according to bandmate [[marky-ramone|Marky Ramone]], the band's political conservative.[1] His running debates with the band's liberal singer Joey Ramone are cited by Badlands Media as evidence that early [[punk-rock]] accommodated a genuine range of ideologies rather than a single partisan outlook.[1]

Marky Ramone recalled that the arguments between Johnny and Joey "would go back and forth about why, what for, what's the reason," moving from generalities to specifics, and that the band treated the freedom to hold opposing politics as something worth valuing.[1]
