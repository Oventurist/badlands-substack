# John Eastman

John Eastman is an American attorney who advised [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on legal strategies contesting the 2020 presidential election result. He is named among the defendants in the Fulton County, Georgia racketeering indictment returned by an Atlanta grand jury on August 14, 2023.[1]

## Georgia indictment

The 98-page indictment charged Trump and eighteen allies with orchestrating what prosecutors described as a sweeping criminal enterprise involving more than a dozen felonies, undertaken in an unsuccessful attempt to overturn Trump's defeat in Georgia's 2020 election.[1] Eastman was listed alongside [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]], [[mark-meadows|Mark Meadows]] and [[sidney-powell|Sidney Powell]] as a defendant, while the charging documents additionally identified thirty unindicted co-conspirators.[1] The case is treated in this wiki as [[state-of-georgia-v-trump|State of Georgia v. Trump, et al.]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the most significant feature of the indictment was not the charges themselves but the breadth of the co-conspirator list, which he reframed as a roster of allies Trump would be compelled to bring with him into a public legal battle.[1] In that reading, a "Disclosure Defense" mounted by Eastman and the other defendants could expose election fraud and supply the template for a racketeering case turned back against the entrenched bureaucracy.[1]
