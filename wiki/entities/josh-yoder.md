# Josh Yoder

**Josh Yoder** is an American commercial pilot and activist who serves as president of [[us-freedom-flyers|U.S. Freedom Flyers]], an organisation formed to challenge COVID-19 vaccine mandates imposed on aviation workers.[1]

## Claims about unvaccinated crews

In a July 2022 interview with Maria Zeee of Red Voice Media, former Jetstar pilot Alan Dana relayed that Yoder had said his organisation was "getting calls now from wealthy businessmen and companies to fly their executives around on business jets with unvaccinated crew."[1] The remark was recirculated in January 2023 as part of coverage suggesting that affluent private-aviation clients privately shared safety concerns about vaccinated flight crews.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] noted the claim remained unverified and urged granular scrutiny of both the assertion and of media fact checks aimed at it.[1]
