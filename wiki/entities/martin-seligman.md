# Martin Seligman

**Dr. Martin Seligman** is the psychologist identified in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay as the researcher behind the theory of [[learned-helplessness|learned helplessness]].[1]

The essay summarizes his work as illustrating how repeated exposure to uncontrollable events creates a psychological state in which individuals cease to resist or even attempt change, and describes that passive resignation as becoming deeply ingrained until people accept external authority as the only viable solution.[1] The author applies this finding to [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]], arguing that engineered crises framed as too complex for individual or local resolution exploit precisely this mechanism to entrench dependence on centralized power.[1]
