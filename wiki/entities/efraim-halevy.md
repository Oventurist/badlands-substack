# Efraim Halevy

Efraim Halevy is a former chief of [[mossad|Mossad]], Israel's foreign intelligence service.[1]

In a 2016 exchange, asked why Israel was providing medical treatment to an Al Qaeda fighter injured in Syria, Halevy replied: "We have a different account with Hezbollah. A totally different account. [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]], to the best of my recollection, has not attacked Israel."[1] The quotation resurfaced in June 2025 Badlands commentary on the [[israeli-arming-of-gaza-anti-hamas-militias|Israeli arming of Gaza anti-Hamas militias]], where commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited it — alongside similar statements by other Israeli officials regarding [[isis|ISIS]] — as evidence for the proposition that both organizations were creations of Mossad and the CIA.[1]
