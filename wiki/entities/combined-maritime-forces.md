# Combined Maritime Forces

The Combined Maritime Forces (CMF) is a multinational naval partnership focused on maritime security operations, including counter-piracy and counter-terrorism, in and around the Middle East.[1] Its Task Force 153 is a partnership of 39 nations focused on security in the Red Sea.[1]

## Role in Operation Prosperity Guardian

In December 2023, the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] used the CMF framework to establish [[operation-prosperity-guardian|Operation Prosperity Guardian]], a new task force to protect commercial shipping from [[houthis|Houthi]] attacks in the Bab al-Mandab Strait and Red Sea.[1] Reporting by Politico's chief Pentagon correspondent Lara Seligman indicated that the operation would be conducted within the framework of the existing Combined Maritime Force 153.[1] Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] confirmed the announcement, describing the initiative as "an important new multinational security initiative under the umbrella of the Combined Maritime Forces and the leadership of its Task Force 153, which focuses on security in the Red Sea."[1]

The initiative followed reporting that the Pentagon was seeking to assemble the "broadest possible" coalition task force amid what had become daily Iran-backed Houthi attacks on commercial vessels, alongside Houthi rocket and drone launches into southern Israel that triggered US warship intercepts.[1]
