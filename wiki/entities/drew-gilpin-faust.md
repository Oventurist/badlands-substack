# Drew Gilpin Faust

**Drew Gilpin Faust** is a historian and former president of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]], listed in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" among the figures on the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board.[1]

The article notes her service as a board member of [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]], citing the overlap between Harvard's presidency, its endowment governance and major Wall Street institutions as evidence of the interlock it describes.[1] Faust appears in the roster the author calls "a financial politburo with global reach," presented as illustrating that Harvard's capital apparatus is staffed by finance-sector principals rather than academics within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
