# Adrien Arcand

Adrien Arcand was the leader of a Nazi-inspired political party in French-speaking Canada, known as the Quebec blue shirts, which [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes as being rapidly embraced in the early 1930s.[1]

Arcand's movement is placed alongside the English-Canadian League for Social Reconstruction — established by five Rhodes Scholars and, in Ehret's account, ready to take power in 1932 — as evidence that the fascist wave of the interwar period extended into Canada as well as the United States and Britain.[1] [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]] interfaced with nascent fascist organizations across the United States, Mexico, Canada and the United Kingdom during this period.[1]
