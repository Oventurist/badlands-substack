# Adrien Arcand

Adrien Arcand was the leader of a Nazi-inspired political party in French-speaking Canada, known as the Quebec blue shirts, which [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes as being rapidly embraced in the early 1930s.[1]

Arcand's movement is placed alongside the English-Canadian League for Social Reconstruction — established by five Rhodes Scholars and, in Ehret's account, ready to take power in 1932 — as evidence that the fascist wave of the interwar period extended into Canada as well as the United States and Britain.[1] [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]] interfaced with nascent fascist organizations across the United States, Mexico, Canada and the United Kingdom during this period.[1]

## Collaboration with Social Credit

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] identifies Adrien Arcand as the head of Quebec's official Nazi party and leader of the fascist movement known as the Blue Shirts.[2] He records that the leadership of the [[social-credit-party-of-canada|Social Credit Party]] in Quebec published the [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion]] in French and collaborated with Arcand for a time — the party being, in Ehret's account, the first and only Canadian political party to publish and widely distribute the forgery.[2] [[joshua-haldeman|Joshua Haldeman]], the grandfather of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], was a senior Social Credit officer in Saskatchewan during this period.[2]
