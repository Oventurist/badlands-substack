# Andrii Yermak

Andrii Yermak is the head of the Ukrainian presidential office and a close aide to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1]

## June 2025 Kyiv strike

Yermak accompanied Zelensky and Interior Minister [[ihor-klymenko|Ihor Klymenko]] to the site of a destroyed nine-story apartment building in Kyiv's Solomianskyi district on June 20, 2025, laying flowers and paying tribute to the 23 people killed there when a Russian missile brought down the structure.[1] The wider drone and missile attack on the capital killed 28 people and wounded 142, according to Kyiv Military Administration head [[tymur-tkachenko|Tymur Tkachenko]].[1] Zelensky used the occasion to argue that more pressure must be applied on Moscow to agree to a ceasefire.[1]
