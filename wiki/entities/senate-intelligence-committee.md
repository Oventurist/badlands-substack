# Senate Intelligence Committee

**Senate Intelligence Committee** (formally the United States Senate Select Committee on Intelligence, SSCI) is the [[senate|Senate]]'s standing oversight panel for the intelligence community. In the Badlands corpus it appears as a target of the revived [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] inquiry into the origins of [[russiagate|Russiagate]].

## The 2026 records dispute

The Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026, citing a Just the News report, says the Justice Department earlier in the year requested transcripts and records from two Senate committees involving former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], setting a February 23 deadline for compliance. As of the report's publication the Senate had not transmitted the requested materials, with the delay affecting part of an investigation based in Fort Pierce, Florida. The Brief adds that prosecutors had recently been allowed to review one Senate Intelligence Committee report in a secure room, citing unnamed officials — a sign of partial cooperation alongside the broader standoff over the committee's records.

## Significance

The committee's refusal to hand over its Russia investigation records is treated in the corpus as the institutional rear-guard action of the intelligence establishment against the accountability phase — the same dynamic the Brief identifies in the [[the-atlantic|Atlantic]]'s reporting on FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]].
