# Georgia State Election Board

The Georgia State Election Board is the state body responsible for promulgating election rules and reviewing complaints about election administration in Georgia.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief cites the board as one of the forums in which allegations about [[fulton-county|Fulton County]]'s handling of ballots from the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]] were previously examined. According to the source, many of the specific claims later recorded in [[fbi|FBI]] Special Agent [[hugh-evans|Hugh Evans]]'s search-warrant affidavits — such as discrepancies between ballot images and reported vote totals — had already been addressed through the manual recount ordered by Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]], reviewed by the State Election Board, and litigated in post-election challenges brought by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his legal team that courts dismissed for lack of standing or merit.

## The January 21, 2026 hearing

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the board held an eleven-hour hearing on Wednesday, January 21, 2026 to address election cases and consider referrals to the state's attorney general. During the hearing, local investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]] presented evidence of intentional human intervention in the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 election]], called on the SEB Chairman to resign, and asked the board to commit to criminal referrals.

Rossi's presentation centered on a report run at 12:06 p.m. and emailed at 12:13 p.m. on Thursday, December 3, 2020 to consultant [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]], and on the testimony of Fulton County elections director [[richard-barron|Richard Barron]], who said during certification week, "We knew we were short." Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the hearing as the moment "the State Election Board of Georgia finally heard rebuttal to their Secretary of State's election narrative," and, with [[canncon|CannCon]], recapped the key moments in a Badlands Media special coverage segment before carrying the [[georgia-senate-ethics-committee|Senate Ethics Committee]] meeting live the following day.
