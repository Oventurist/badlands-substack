# Georgia State Election Board

The Georgia State Election Board is the state body responsible for promulgating election rules and reviewing complaints about election administration in Georgia.

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] cites the board as one of the forums in which allegations about [[fulton-county|Fulton County]]'s handling of ballots from the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]] were previously examined. According to the source, many of the specific claims later recorded in [[fbi|FBI]] Special Agent [[hugh-evans|Hugh Evans]]'s search-warrant affidavits — such as discrepancies between ballot images and reported vote totals — had already been addressed through the manual recount ordered by Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]], reviewed by the State Election Board, and litigated in post-election challenges brought by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his legal team that courts dismissed for lack of standing or merit.
