# Federation of German Industries (BDI)

The Federation of German Industries (BDI) is [[germany|Germany]]'s main industry association, representing the country's manufacturers.[1]

## 2026 warnings

In late July 2026 the BDI reported that Germany's industrial sector was shedding roughly 15,000 jobs per month, and said the country's manufacturers faced a competitiveness crisis driven by high energy costs, weak investment, geopolitical pressures, and increased competition from [[china|China]]. Business leaders associated with the federation urged policies aimed at restoring industrial competitiveness and encouraging investment.[1]

The warning coincided with [[bmw|BMW]]'s announcement of several thousand job cuts in Germany and with broader restructuring across the German automotive sector. Badlands commentary situated the BDI figures alongside a report that Germany recorded its highest number of corporate bankruptcies in 21 years during the second quarter of 2026, and noted that German industry leaders blame the energy price shock — a trend said to have begun in 2018 and peaked in 2023 — for the collapsing economy.[1]

## Significance

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the BDI's data as evidence of a structural crisis in the backbone of the European economy: Germany holds Europe's largest manufacturing base, roughly 28% of its GDP, and was once the continent's production powerhouse.[1]
