# Bureau of Prisons

The Federal Bureau of Prisons is the agency within the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] responsible for the custody and care of federal inmates, including decisions about the security classification of individual prisoners and their transfer between facilities.[1]

## Maxwell transfer controversy

In 2025 the Bureau became the focus of political controversy over the decision to move [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], an associate of the convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, to a lower-security prison facility.[1] At a [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing in September 2025, FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] testified that the Bureau of Prisons had made the transfer decision independently.[1]

Senator [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] challenged that account directly, asking Patel whether he believed the American people were "stupid" enough to accept it rather than concluding that the move had been a politically motivated act by the White House.[1] The exchange escalated into a shouting match that briefly derailed the hearing, with Patel accusing Schiff of lying about Russiagate and January 6 and calling him "the biggest fraud to ever sit in the United States Senate."[1] The dispute left the question of institutional independence in the Maxwell transfer unresolved in public.[1]
