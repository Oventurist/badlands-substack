# Network of Central Banks and Supervisors for Greening the Financial System

The Network of Central Banks and Supervisors for Greening the Financial System (NGFS) is an international body of central banks and financial supervisors organized to help meet the goals of the Paris Agreement by integrating climate considerations into financial supervision and monetary policy frameworks.[1]

## US withdrawal, 2025

In early February 2025 the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|US Department of the Treasury]], acting through its Federal Insurance Office (FIO), notified the NGFS that it was withdrawing its membership.[1] Treasury described the withdrawal as one part of implementing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive orders titled Putting America First in International Environmental Agreements and Unleashing American Energy.[1]

Treasury stated that because the NGFS was organized to help meet the goals of the Paris Agreement — from which the United States had withdrawn — its initiatives were inconsistent with the administration's priorities of growing the US economy and American jobs, and that the network's role diverged from the traditional technical and coordinating functions of other international fora.[1] Treasury further noted that important parts of the NGFS's scope, including work on monetary policy frameworks, went beyond the FIO's core duties, and said the FIO would continue engaging with state insurance regulators and other stakeholders to promote US interests in international insurance matters.[1]
