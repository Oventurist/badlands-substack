# Gregory Carro

Gregory Carro is a New York State judge who presided over the state criminal case against [[luigi-mangione|Luigi Mangione]] in the killing of [[unitedhealthcare|UnitedHealthcare]] chief executive [[brian-thompson|Brian Thompson]].[1]

## Rulings in the Mangione case

In September 2025, Carro dismissed the terrorism charges against Mangione in New York State's case, while keeping in place the state's second-degree murder charges against him.[1] Mangione's lawyers had additionally argued that the New York state case and a parallel federal death penalty prosecution together amounted to double jeopardy; Carro rejected that argument, holding that it would be premature to make such a determination at that stage.[1]

The rulings meant that, at least for the time being, Mangione remained subject to prosecution at both the state and federal levels — a point Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] identified as the most interesting element of the decision.[1] The hearing marked Mangione's first court appearance in the state case since February of that year.[1]
