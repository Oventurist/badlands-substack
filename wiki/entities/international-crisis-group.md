# International Crisis Group

The International Crisis Group is an international non-governmental organisation that appears in Badlands commentary through the involvement of [[mark-malloch-brown|Mark Malloch Brown]].[1]

## Malloch Brown's role

Malloch Brown served as a member of the Executive Committee of the International Crisis Group, and in July 2014 became co-chair of its Board of Trustees.[1] The same year he became chairman of the board of directors of SGO Corporation Limited, the holding company whose primary asset is the voting machine manufacturer [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]], and he has also chaired the Royal African Society.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands thesis

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] presents this cluster of appointments — the [[united-nations|United Nations]], the [[world-bank|World Bank]], the Crisis Group and Smartmatic — as evidence that Malloch Brown is connected to every arm of what he calls the Cabal, functioning as "a Cabal Swiss Army Knife, going wherever the Cabal needs him."[1] Malloch Brown went on to become president of the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] on 1 January 2021.[1]
