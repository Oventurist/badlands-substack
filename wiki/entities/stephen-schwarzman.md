# Stephen Schwarzman

Stephen Schwarzman is the co-founder and chief executive of [[blackstone|Blackstone]]. "The Harvard Control Grid" names him among the Harvard-linked figures who routinely attend the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]'s annual Davos meeting, alongside [[mohamed-el-erian|Mohamed El-Erian]] and Blackstone CFO [[michael-chae|Michael Chae]].[1]

The article characterizes Davos as the venue "where public and private power synchronize," and argues that through panels, agenda-setting and behind-the-scenes deal-making the WEF promotes a top-down vision of stakeholder capitalism "that rebrands technocracy as 'equity.'"[1] Blackstone itself is described as "a central lever in global real estate, pharma, infrastructure, defense, and financial tech."[1]
