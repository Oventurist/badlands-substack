# Stephen Schwarzman

Stephen Schwarzman is the chairman and chief executive officer of Blackstone, the American private equity and alternative asset management firm he co-founded in 1985. He is one of Wall Street's most prominent dealmakers and a major figure in American political fundraising.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Schwarzman was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. His inclusion placed Blackstone alongside [[blackrock|BlackRock]]'s [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]] and [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]]' [[david-solomon|David Solomon]] in a delegation of American financial leadership prepared to lock in investment flows ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
