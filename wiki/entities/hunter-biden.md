# Hunter Biden

Hunter Biden is the son of former U.S. President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].

## Defamation award against Patrick Byrne (July 2026)

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Hunter Biden was awarded $1.7 million in punitive damages after U.S. District Judge [[stephen-wilson|Stephen Wilson]] ruled that former Overstock.com CEO [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]] defamed him by repeatedly promoting claims that Biden participated in an $800 million Iranian bribery scheme.

The lawsuit centered on Byrne's claims that Hunter Biden sought a bribe from [[iran|Iran]] in exchange for influencing his father to release $8 billion in frozen Iranian assets and reduce pressure during nuclear negotiations. Hunter Biden denied the allegations, arguing Byrne knowingly republished false claims. The judge found Byrne acted with "intentional misrepresentation" and "conscious disregard" for Biden's rights, ruling he continued amplifying the allegations even after the lawsuit was filed and encouraged others to spread them on social media. Biden was awarded $1 in nominal damages, as requested, and $1.7 million in punitive damages; Byrne was also ordered to pay nearly $35,000 in previously imposed court sanctions within two weeks or face an additional $1,000 daily penalty.
