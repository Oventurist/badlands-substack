# Paula Xinis

Paula Xinis is a United States judge in Maryland who has presided over litigation concerning the deportation of [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego Garcia]].[1]

## Third-country removal proceedings

On June 26, 2025, federal prosecutors told Xinis that the Trump administration would begin removal proceedings to deport Abrego Garcia to a country other than his native El Salvador if he were released from a Tennessee jail.[1] When Xinis pressed on timing — "30 seconds or 30 days or 30 months…" — a federal prosecutor would say only that removal was not "imminent."[1] Xinis scheduled a hearing on the matter for July 7.[1]

## Abrego García deportation ruling (April 2025)

On Sunday 6 April 2025 U.S. District Judge Paula Xinis issued a 22-page decision rebuking the administration of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for refusing to take steps to return [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego García]], a Salvadoran man wrongly deported to a mega-prison in El Salvador.[2] The ruling came two days after she ordered the government to arrange his release by 11:59 p.m. on the following Monday.[2] Xinis wrote that officials had offered "no evidence" that Abrego García was a gang member and cited records and official statements showing the United States had the power to bring him back but had chosen not to exercise it: "This is not about Defendants' inability to return Abrego García, but their lack of desire."[2] Her comments came as the administration pursued what reporting described as the largest mass deportation operation in U.S. history amid lawsuits alleging it was trampling immigrants' rights.[2]
