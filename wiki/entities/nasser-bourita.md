# Nasser Bourita

Nasser Bourita is the Foreign Minister of [[morocco|Morocco]].[1]

## April 2025 meeting with Rubio

Bourita met with US Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] on Tuesday, April 8, 2025, a meeting at which Rubio declared that Washington recognizes Moroccan sovereignty over [[western-sahara|Western Sahara]] and supports Morocco's "serious, credible, and realistic" autonomy plan as the only basis for resolving the longstanding dispute, according to a [[state-department|State Department]] press release.[1]
