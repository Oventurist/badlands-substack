# Dennis M. Lynch Arena

The Dennis M. Lynch Arena is a municipal ice arena in Pawtucket, Rhode Island, used for youth and high school hockey. In February 2026 it became the site of a mass shooting when a gunman opened fire during a crowded high school "Senior Night" hockey game.[1]

## February 2026 shooting

The attack took place on a Monday afternoon while spectators filled the arena to watch a senior-night contest.[1] [[robert-dorgan|Robert Dorgan]], a 56-year-old man who also went by Roberta Esposito, fatally shot his ex-wife Rhonda Dorgan and their 23-year-old son Aidan Dorgan in the stands while another of their sons played on the ice.[1] Rhonda Dorgan died of gunshot injuries at the arena itself, while Aidan Dorgan died later at Rhode Island Hospital in Providence.[1] Three other people — Rhonda's parents, Linda and Gerald Dorgan, and family friend Thomas Geruso — were critically wounded.[1] A bystander briefly disarmed the shooter inside the arena before he drew a second weapon and killed himself.[1]

Pawtucket police described the shooting as domestic-related and, at a press conference held in temporary City Council chambers, the city's police chief asked the public to respect the family's privacy.[1] The event is covered in greater detail at [[pawtucket-ice-arena-shooting-2026|the Pawtucket ice arena shooting]].
