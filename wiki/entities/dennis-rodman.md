# Dennis Rodman

Dennis Rodman is a former American basketball player known in political commentary for his unofficial contacts with North Korea. In the Badlands News Brief of February 10, 2025, contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry discussed Rodman while covering President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated desire to reestablish relations with North Korean leader [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong-un]].[1]

On an episode of The Book of Trump with Patrick Gunnels, the host examined Trump's reality television show The Apprentice and flagged what he called the peculiar timeline surrounding Rodman's first visit to North Korea in 2013 — it occurred just weeks after Rodman departed the show.[1] He also noted that the talent agent who booked the trip, which the agent claimed was made "in error" because he believed the destination was South Korea, was the same agent who negotiated Trump's 2022 NFT offering and who also represents Hulk Hogan.[1]

The commentary promised a full future episode on Rodman's role in establishing peace with North Korea, and speculated about how soon a US embassy might be established in Pyongyang and whether Rodman could be named ambassador.[1]
