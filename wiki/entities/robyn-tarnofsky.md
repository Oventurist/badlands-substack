# Robyn F. Tarnofsky

Robyn F. Tarnofsky is a United States magistrate judge in the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]] who presided over the bail proceedings of music executive [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] following his September 2024 federal indictment.[1]

## Denial of bail to Sean Combs

At a hearing on Tuesday, September 17, 2024, Judge Tarnofsky denied Combs's request for pretrial release after he pleaded not guilty to charges of sex trafficking, racketeering conspiracy, and transportation to engage in prostitution.[1] Combs's defense team had proposed a $50 million bond package, but Tarnofsky rejected the proposal, citing Combs's anger issues and his history of substance abuse, and ordered him detained pending trial.[1] Prosecutors, including Emily A. Johnson, had argued that Combs posed a danger to the community and that his personal wealth would make flight easy to accomplish undetected.[1]

The ruling meant that Combs would remain in federal custody while awaiting trial, a fact commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated as significant given widespread online speculation about his safety in detention.[1]
