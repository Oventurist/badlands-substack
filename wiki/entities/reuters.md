# Reuters

Reuters is an international news agency and wire service, frequently cited in the Badlands corpus both as a factual source and as an object of narrative criticism.

Badlands Brief: Conflicts, Cartels, & Clemency draws on Reuters reporting for its account of the United States joining [[israel|Israel]] in strikes on [[iran|Iran]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated rationale, Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s comments, and the reaction of "Russian hardliners" — a characterization the Brief explicitly attributes to Reuters and the wires. Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treats the agency's framing as part of a deliberate narrative campaign, writing that Reuters was "highlighting Russian hardliners rattled to the core—calling Trump a 'monster,' demanding he abandon US-brokered Ukraine talks and double down on the battlefield instead, while warning that the seeming systematic elimination of Moscow's allies (Syria, Venezuela, now Iran) means Russia is next on the docket."
