# Reuters

Reuters is an international wire service whose dispatches are cited in Badlands Media coverage as a source on Israeli military and political developments.[1]

## Reporting on Israeli deployments in Lebanon (June 2026)

In June 2026 Reuters reported that [[israel|Israel]] was in discussions with the United States about maintaining its troop deployments in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]] beyond previously anticipated timelines, according to officials.[1] The same reporting indicated that Israeli officials had advanced plans relating to border demarcation and security arrangements in the area despite ongoing diplomatic efforts — a posture Badlands framed as an escalation of Israeli defiance of [[donald-trump|President Trump]].[1]

Reuters coverage was also cited in connection with former Prime Minister [[naftali-bennett|Naftali Bennett]]'s announcement of plans for a new political movement and his call for major changes in Israeli leadership and governance, remarks that landed amid continued debate over Israel's security strategy and political future.[1]

## Coverage of Putin's Gaza warning

Reuters supplied the reporting behind the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] item on Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s October 2023 warning that Israel's conflict with [[hamas|Hamas]] could spread well beyond the Middle East.[2] The agency's account, drawn from a Kremlin transcript of Putin's meeting with Russian religious leaders, quoted his statement that "our main task is to stop the bloodshed and violence" and his warning of "grave and extremely dangerous and destructive consequences" if escalation continued, along with his criticism of unnamed Western forces seeking to draw more countries into the conflict.[2]

## Coverage of the North Korean balloon campaign (2024)

Badlands Media singled out Reuters for the scale of resources it devoted to covering [[north-korea|North Korea]]'s trash-balloon campaign against [[south-korea|South Korea]], writing that the agency meticulously tracked every "wave" of launches, established a running count of more than 6,000 balloons sent, mapped flight patterns and termination points, commissioned animated graphics of exploding cartoon balloons, and apparently sent investigators into the field to measure the size and variety of trash in the packages.[3] The outlet called the output impressive but misdirected, asking what would result if comparable effort were applied to election integrity, government corruption, or Jeffrey Epstein.[3]

## Reporting on the 2024 Trump Ukraine plan

Reuters reported in June 2024 that advisers [[keith-kellogg|Keith Kellogg]] and [[fred-fleitz|Fred Fleitz]] had presented [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with a plan to halt US military aid to Kyiv unless it entered peace talks with Moscow, describing it as the most detailed strategy yet produced by Trump associates.[4] The wire service also published the investigation into the Saudi Royal Purge cited in Badlands coverage of [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s seizure of the [[saudi-binladin-group|Bin Laden Group]].[4]
