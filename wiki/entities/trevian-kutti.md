# Trevian Kutti

Trevian Kutti — a surname pronounced like "cutie" — is a co-defendant in the Georgia election interference case brought in [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and associates.[1]

## May 2024 hearing

On Tuesday, May 27, 2024, Fulton County Superior Court Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] held court on two matters in the case, the second of which concerned Kutti.[1] Her appearance was a procedural matter relating to her new counsel.[1] Kutti told the court she had learned about the hearing on Twitter, and her new counsel had likewise been unaware of it; her new attorney's motion to appear had been filed on the wrong docket, an error the hearing was convened to remedy.[1]

Badlands coverage noted that this was not the first time defendants in the case had encountered problems with filings and the clerk's office, casting the incident as part of a broader pattern of administrative dysfunction in the [[state-of-georgia-v-trump|Georgia RICO prosecution]].[1] The first matter heard that day involved co-defendant [[harrison-floyd|Harrison Floyd]].[1]
