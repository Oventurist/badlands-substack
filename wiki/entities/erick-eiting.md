# Erick Eiting

Erick Eiting is a physician who serves as vice chair of operations for emergency medicine at Mount Sinai Downtown in New York City.[1]

## September 2023 comments on COVID-19 symptoms

In September 2023 Eiting told NBC News that the symptom profile of COVID-19 had shifted away from the earlier telltale signs of lost taste or smell and dry cough.[1] "It isn't the same typical symptoms that we were seeing before. It's a lot of congestion, sometimes sneezing, usually a mild sore throat," he said, adding that a sore throat generally appeared first, followed by nasal congestion.[1] He acknowledged that the only reason the illness was identified as COVID-19 was testing — otherwise it would have been taken for an ordinary cough or cold: "The only way that we knew that it was Covid was because we happened to be testing them."[1] His remarks were picked up by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and became the occasion for a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary on [[clinical-presentation-and-medical-diagnosis|clinical presentation and medical diagnosis]].[1]
