# Joe Ganim

Joe Ganim is the incumbent Democratic mayor of Bridgeport, Connecticut, and was the beneficiary of the 2023 Democratic mayoral primary result that a state judge later voided over ballot-fraud evidence.[1]

## 2023 primary overturned

In a ruling issued on Wednesday, November 1, 2023, Judge [[william-clark-connecticut|William Clark]] ordered a new Democratic primary after reviewing video evidence submitted by challenger [[john-gomes|John Gomes]].[1] The footage appeared to show Bridgeport Democratic Town Committee vice chair [[wanda-geter-pataky|Wanda Geter-Pataky]] depositing multiple ballots in a drop box; she pleaded the Fifth rather than answer questions about it.[1] Clark wrote that Ganim "was also correct to be 'shocked' at what he saw on the video clips in evidence that were shown to him while he was on the witness stand."[1]
