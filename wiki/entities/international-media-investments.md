# International Media Investments

International Media Investments (IMI) is a United Arab Emirates media investment company owned primarily by Emirati royal and UAE vice president [[mansour-bin-zayed-al-nahyan|Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan]].[1]

IMI was the major partner in RedBird IMI, the joint venture with [[redbird-capital-partners|RedBird Capital Partners]] led by former CNN president [[jeff-zucker|Jeff Zucker]] that bought [[the-telegraph|The Telegraph]] before the deal was derailed in 2023, when the Conservative government passed legislation curbing foreign state ownership of British media amid concerns about foreign influence over Britain's most prominent center-right outlet.[1] Under the May 2025 sale of the paper to RedBird Capital Partners for £500 million, IMI was to be reduced to a minority stakeholder, provided the [[labour-party|Labour]] government followed through on pending changes allowing foreign state-owned investors to hold up to 15 percent of British newspaper publishers.[1]
