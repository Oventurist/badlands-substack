# Cornel West

Cornel West is an American academic and left-wing political figure who mounted a third-party presidential campaign for the 2024 election cycle, initially under the banner of the Green Party.[1]

In September 2023 commentary on the position of [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] within the [[democratic-party|Democrat Party]], PJ Media used West's Green Party run as a benchmark for third-party risk to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s re-election prospects, arguing that whatever threat West posed should be magnified "by 10x, 100x" to approximate the damage a Kennedy third-party candidacy would inflict.[1] The same analysis suggested that a strong independent run by Kennedy might "literally break the Democrat Party for years or possibly forever."[1]

## CNN clash, July 2023

West's opposition to the NATO alliance and his contention that the West provoked war with Russia drew a critical on-air treatment from CNN's [[anderson-cooper|Anderson Cooper]] in July 2023, which [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]] rebutted in a widely circulated segment.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited the episode as evidence that political conflict in the United States was reorganizing around an Establishment-versus-anti-Establishment divide rather than the traditional Left-Right-Center paradigm.[2]

## Bernie Sanders' 2023 attacks on West's candidacy

An October 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] examined West's independent presidential candidacy and the response to it from [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]], one of West's longtime political allies.[3] West had been one of Sanders' most ardent supporters in previous elections, but in August 2023 Sanders became one of the first members of Congress to endorse [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] for re-election and went further by publicly attacking West.[3]

West has described himself as a "non-Marxist socialist," saying he does not view Marxism and Christianity as reconcilable, which underlies his interest in Christian socialist thought.[3] He is regarded as a left-wing populist with socialist leanings whose principal areas of interest include political philosophy, religious philosophy, ethics, race, caste, democracy, and liberation theology.[3] He is also a cultural figure who has recorded spoken word and hip-hop albums, appeared in feature films, and engaged constructively with conservative thought leaders — giving him, in the essay's assessment, more cultural weight than the average politician, which matters on a left where choices are heavily shaped by pop-culture association.[3]

The essay argued that as supporters of Sanders and [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] grow disillusioned with a Democratic Party that no longer contains a genuine oppositional faction, West should be the natural candidate to win them over — and that the powers within and behind the party, aware of this, deployed Sanders as an "attack dog" against him.[3] It also pointed to mainstream commentary treating third-party runs as a "threat to democracy," including remarks by Anderson Cooper and [[james-carville|James Carville]], the latter suggesting Jill Stein was an agent of the Kremlin, as part of a broader effort to delegitimize candidates outside the two-party structure.[3] The Hill's morning program Rising was cited as having covered the Sanders–West conflict comparatively fairly.[3]
