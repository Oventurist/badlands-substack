# Cornel West

Cornel West is an American academic and left-wing political figure who mounted a third-party presidential campaign for the 2024 election cycle, initially under the banner of the Green Party.[1]

In September 2023 commentary on the position of [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] within the [[democratic-party|Democrat Party]], PJ Media used West's Green Party run as a benchmark for third-party risk to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s re-election prospects, arguing that whatever threat West posed should be magnified "by 10x, 100x" to approximate the damage a Kennedy third-party candidacy would inflict.[1] The same analysis suggested that a strong independent run by Kennedy might "literally break the Democrat Party for years or possibly forever."[1]

## CNN clash, July 2023

West's opposition to the NATO alliance and his contention that the West provoked war with Russia drew a critical on-air treatment from CNN's [[anderson-cooper|Anderson Cooper]] in July 2023, which [[russell-brand|Russell Brand]] rebutted in a widely circulated segment.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited the episode as evidence that political conflict in the United States was reorganizing around an Establishment-versus-anti-Establishment divide rather than the traditional Left-Right-Center paradigm.[2]
