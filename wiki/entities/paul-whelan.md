# Paul Whelan

Paul Whelan is a former United States Marine who was imprisoned in Russia on espionage charges that he and the U.S. government denied.[1] Serving a 20-year sentence, he was reported on August 1, 2024 to be among those Russia had agreed to release in a major multi-country prisoner exchange with the United States, alongside Wall Street Journal reporter [[evan-gershkovich|Evan Gershkovich]].[1]

## 2022 exclusion and 2024 release

Whelan had been left behind in a previous prisoner swap — the December 2022 exchange that secured the release of WNBA player [[brittney-griner|Brittney Griner]], who had been convicted on drug charges in Russia.[1] That omission produced widespread confusion and disappointment among American observers at the time.[1] Under the 2024 arrangement, both men were reported to be en route to destinations outside Russia, though the identities of the Russian prisoners released in exchange and any additional U.S. concessions were not disclosed.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary framed the swap as a development tied to the anticipated return of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] rather than to Biden administration diplomacy, and called on journalists to demand disclosure of the negotiated terms from the [[white-house|White House]].[1]

## August 2024 release

Badlands commentary noted that the news that Whelan and [[evan-gershkovich|Evan Gershkovich]] were coming home was accompanied by media portrayals of a White House that appeared confused and caught unaware, and that the story vanished from headlines quickly.[2] Reporting on the negotiations suggested that Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] rather than President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was directing the process.[2]
