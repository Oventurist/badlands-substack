# Telefónica

Telefónica is Spain's dominant telecommunications operator, providing fixed-line, mobile, and internet services nationwide.[1]

## May 2025 nationwide outage

In May 2025 a nationwide phone-network failure struck Spain following network upgrade work carried out by Telefónica.[1] Landline telephones were the worst affected, though Spanish media reported that all of the company's voice services appeared to have been affected to some degree.[1] Emergency services in several regions were forced to publish alternative telephone numbers for people needing assistance after normal lines collapsed.[1]

A Telefónica spokesperson stated: "We have done some network upgrade work which has affected some companies' fixed communication services (voice and internet)."[1]

## Context

The outage came only weeks after major electricity blackouts had caused widespread disruption and heavy financial damage across Spain.[1] The May 21, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] carried the Independent's report of the telecom failure as a bonus item, placing it in a sequence of critical-infrastructure failures in Europe that the outlet tracks as a pattern rather than as isolated technical incidents.[1]
