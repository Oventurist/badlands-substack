# Samantha Hudson

**Samantha Hudson** is the stage name of Iván González Ranedo, a Spanish transgender influencer and performer.[1]

In March 2024, [[doritos|Doritos Spain]] terminated its relationship with Hudson after intense public backlash to a short promotional video titled "Crunch Talks," posted to Instagram on a Sunday and removed the following Monday.[1] A Doritos Spain spokesperson confirmed to Rolling Stone that the clip was not part of a broader brand ambassadorship campaign and that Hudson had been dropped over controversial comments made in the past, stating: "We have ended the relationship and stopped all related campaign activity due to the comments. We strongly condemn words or actions that promote violence or sexism of any kind."[1] Reporting noted Hudson had previously made provocative statements about capitalism, the family, and child sexual abuse, including tweets about doing "thuggish things" to a minor.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] framed the episode as a clarifying moment in what he describes as an ongoing cultural war, arguing that Hudson serves as a legible illustration of the dangers he attributes to "Neo-Marxist queer theory" — combining open advocacy against the traditional family with statements about child rape, two themes he says mainstream audiences rarely connect.[1] Esler argued Hudson is not an anomaly but an endpoint of that ideological trajectory, and appended a health-freedom aside that Doritos are "trashy, poisonous food" and therefore no loss to boycott.[1]
