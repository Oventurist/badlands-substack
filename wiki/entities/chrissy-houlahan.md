# Chrissy Houlahan

Chrissy Houlahan is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives and a veteran of the armed services or intelligence community.[1] She appeared alongside five colleagues in the 90-second video posted from Senator [[elissa-slotkin|Elissa Slotkin]]'s X account in November 2025, which addressed U.S. service members and called on them to uphold the Constitution and refuse "illegal orders."[1]

## Sedition controversy

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] accused the six lawmakers of sedition "punishable by DEATH" on November 20, 2025, amplifying reposts of the video with his own commentary.[1] Houlahan and the others — Slotkin, [[mark-kelly|Mark Kelly]], [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]], [[chris-deluzio|Chris Deluzio]] and [[maggie-goodlander|Maggie Goodlander]] — replied in a joint statement demanding that "every American must unite and condemn the President's calls for our murder and political violence."[1] Badlands coverage treats the group as the [[seditious-six|Seditious Six]].[1]
