# Palestine Chronicle

The Palestine Chronicle is a United States-based 501(c)(3) news outlet covering Palestinian affairs, founded by [[ramzy-baroud|Ramzy Baroud]], a U.S.-educated Palestinian-American journalist and former managing editor of both [[middle-east-eye|Middle East Eye]] and [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]].[1]

## June 2024 coverage of the Palestinian Authority

In June 2024 the outlet published "'Negative Neutrality' – Palestinian Authority 'Weak and Irrelevant'" by [[iqbal-jassat|Iqbal Jassat]], which argued that the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] under [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] had become undemocratic and unreformable, likened the PA to South Africa's apartheid-era Bantustans, and described the [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]] "peace pact" as a mirage.[1]

## Badlands assessment

Badlands Media contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used the Palestine Chronicle and its article as a case study in open-source intelligence analysis, contending that the outlet's founder and the article's author both matched a profile he associates with [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] media assets — a network he and Matthew Ehret have argued on Breaking History was created by British Intelligence in Cairo in 1928 to act as a proxy.[1] The commentary suggested the outlet was functioning as controlled opposition on behalf of Western intelligence agencies by calling for the rejection of Abbas and the embrace of armed resistance against [[israel|Israel]].[1] This characterization is Badlands Media opinion analysis rather than verified fact.
