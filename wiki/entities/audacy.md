# Audacy Inc.

Audacy Inc. is an American radio and podcast broadcasting company, described in February 2024 reporting as one of the largest and most heavily leveraged radio conglomerates in the United States.[1] The company entered bankruptcy proceedings, and its restructuring plan called for lenders to exchange their loans for equity in the reorganized broadcaster.[1]

## Bankruptcy and change of ownership

Bloomberg reported in February 2024 that [[soros-fund-management|Soros Fund Management]], the investment firm founded by [[george-soros|George Soros]], had accumulated more than $400 million of Audacy's highest-ranking debt according to bankruptcy court filings, making it by far the largest member of the creditor group set to convert debt into stock.[1] That position put the fund in line to become Audacy's biggest shareholder upon emergence from bankruptcy.[1]

In a statement, Audacy said: "The decision by our existing and new debtholders to become equity holders in Audacy represents a significant vote of confidence in our company and the future of the radio and audio business."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that converting distressed debt into equity would give the new owners direct influence over the conglomerate's business planning and, by extension, over the content and messaging distributed across its stations, framing the deal as part of a broader consolidation of information channels ahead of the 2024 US election.[1]
