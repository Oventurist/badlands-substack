# Kevin O'Leary

Kevin O'Leary is a Canadian businessman and television personality known for the program "Shark Tank." In coverage of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s New York civil real-estate valuation case, O'Leary told CNN's Laura Coates that the ruling had not "gone over well" with a real estate industry newly worried about becoming the next target.[1]

When Coates listed the findings against Trump — falsifying business records in the second degree, issuing false financial statements, insurance fraud and conspiracy, all arising from asset inflation — O'Leary replied that "everything that you just listed off is done by every real estate developer everywhere on Earth in every city. This has never been prosecuted."[1] The exchange was replayed by [[jon-stewart|Jon Stewart]] on "The Daily Show," who argued in response that such overvaluations were not victimless crimes; commentary at Badlands Media noted that Trump, O'Leary, developers and bankers had been making the same objection since the case was filed.[1]
