# Denis Manturov

Denis Manturov is a Russian government official who served as deputy prime minister and head of the Ministry of Industry and Trade during President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s previous term in office.[1]

## Nomination as first deputy prime minister

In the slate of cabinet nominations announced through the [[federation-council|Federation Council]] on 12 May 2024, Manturov was nominated for the post of first deputy prime minister, a position vacated as acting First Deputy Prime Minister [[andrey-belousov|Andrey Belousov]] was proposed as defense minister in place of [[sergei-shoigu|Sergey Shoigu]].[1] The nominations, which also included [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] for foreign minister and [[vladimir-kolokoltsev|Vladimir Kolokoltsev]] for interior minister, were scheduled for senatorial consultation on 13 and 14 May 2024.[1]
