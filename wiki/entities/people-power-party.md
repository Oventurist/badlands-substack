# People Power Party

The People Power Party is the conservative governing party of [[south-korea|South Korea]] and the party of President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]].[1]

## Reaction to the martial law declaration

Yoon's surprise imposition of martial law on December 3, 2024 blindsided the nation, his own People Power Party, and global allies including the United States.[1] The declaration — see the [[2024-south-korea-martial-law-declaration|2024 South Korea martial law declaration]] — triggered an outpouring of anger not only from the public but from Yoon's own party, contributing to widespread calls for his resignation even after he rescinded the decree hours later.[1] A bipartisan parliamentary vote, which by definition included members of the governing party, rejected military rule and forced the withdrawal of troops.[1]
