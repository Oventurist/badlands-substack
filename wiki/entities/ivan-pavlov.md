# Ivan Pavlov

**Ivan Pavlov** was a physiologist whose work on behavioral conditioning is cited in Badlands Media's account of [[menticide]] as a foundation for totalitarian psychological technique.[1]

## Conditioning and isolation

As recounted by the psychoanalyst [[joost-meerloo|Joost Meerloo]], Pavlov discovered that the conditioned reflex "could be developed most easily in a quiet laboratory with minimum disturbing stimuli."[1] Meerloo compared this to the practice of animal trainers, for whom isolation and the patient repetition of stimuli are required to tame wild animals.[1]

## Application by totalitarian regimes

Meerloo argued that "the totalitarians have followed this rule," knowing that political victims can be conditioned more easily when kept in isolation.[1] A population that is alone, confused and battered by waves of terror devolves into a hopeless and vulnerable state, at which point propaganda can convert rational minds into "playhouses of irrational forces."[1] Badlands Media raises the question of whether lockdowns, quarantine and the isolating effects of cell phones and social media perform the same function in the modern era.[1]
