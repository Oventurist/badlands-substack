# Council of Economic Advisers

The Council of Economic Advisers (CEA) is the White House body that supplies the president with economic analysis and policy advice. Its chairman is a senior presidential appointee, and in 2025 the post was held by [[stephen-miran|Stephen Miran]] under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Role in the 2025 Federal Reserve nomination

The CEA chairmanship served as the launching point for Miran's elevation to the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Board of Governors. On August 7, 2025, Trump announced that Miran, then chair of the Council, would be nominated to the board seat vacated by [[adriana-kugler|Adriana Kugler]].[1] Coverage of the move noted that, as the sitting chairman of the White House council, Miran was presumed to be an ally of the president and likely to share at least part of his dovish view of prevailing interest rates.[1]

The pick underscored how the CEA functions as a pipeline of economists aligned with an administration's macroeconomic preferences into independent institutions, a dynamic that drew commentary from Badlands writers who framed the appointment as part of a broader reshaping of monetary authority.[1]
