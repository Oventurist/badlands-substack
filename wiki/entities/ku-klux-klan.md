# Ku Klux Klan

The Ku Klux Klan is an American white-supremacist organization, historically associated with racial terrorism in the post-Civil War South and with symbolic practices including cross burnings and distinctive robed regalia. In the Badlands corpus it appears principally as the subject of monitoring and infiltration by advocacy organizations rather than as an active political force.

## Role in the SPLC indictment

The June 4, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that a superseding indictment obtained by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] against the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] in the Middle District of Alabama alleges that the SPLC used roughly $4.1 million in tax-exempt donations to pay individuals embedded in hate organizations. Prosecutors allege that those informants then took part in activities including recruiting members and obtaining materials linked to cross burnings and Ku Klux Klan regalia. The allegation is central to the government's theory that the SPLC defrauded donors and banks by routing funds through shell accounts to individuals inside the very groups it publicly opposed.

The Klan also figures in the corpus through the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]], the Reconstruction-era conspiracy statute that Ashe in America says was invoked in fabricated claims brought against her and her colleagues in 2022.
