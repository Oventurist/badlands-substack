# Ku Klux Klan

The Ku Klux Klan is an American white supremacist organization. In Badlands Media commentary on the origins of racial division in the United States, the Klan is described not as an independent grassroots movement but as an infiltrated and externally funded instrument of engineered conflict.[1]

## Alleged FBI infiltration and common funding

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that [[malcolm-x|Malcolm X]] discovered that the Klan and the [[black-panther-party|Black Panthers]] were both infiltrated by the [[fbi|FBI]] and were both being funded by the same people, including Texas oil tycoon [[h-l-hunt|H.L. Hunt]], with the aim of creating chaos, unrest and hate during the 1960s.[1] Carlson presented this discovery as the reason Malcolm X was murdered.[1]

## "Q for Dummies" Chapter 2 and Q drop #9

Badlands' "Q for Dummies" Chapter 2 describes the Klan as "a democrat-created institution" and points to the 1924 Democratic National Convention — nicknamed the "Klan Bake" — citing an article by The Federalist Papers on that convention and on "the truth behind who really wanted to abolish slavery."[2] The chapter identifies [[robert-byrd|Robert Byrd]], a high-ranking Klan figure, as [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s mentor, and notes that Clinton, [[barack-obama|Obama]], George W. Bush and Joe Biden delivered eulogies at his funeral.[2] The author calls this projection: a political side that brands its opponents racist while elevating a former Klansman within its own government.[2]
