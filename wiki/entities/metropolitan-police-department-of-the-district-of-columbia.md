# Metropolitan Police Department of the District of Columbia

The Metropolitan Police Department (MPD) is the municipal police force of Washington, DC. Officers of the department were deployed alongside the [[united-states-capitol-police|US Capitol Police]] during the January 6, 2021 Capitol breach, and the department's conduct that day is one of the two central subjects of the [[jan-6-excessive-force-lawsuit|Jan. 6 excessive force lawsuit]].[1]

## Role in January 6 litigation

A proposed class action filed in Florida alleges that MPD, together with the Capitol Police, deployed rubber bullets, chemical spray, flashbangs, and other crowd-control munitions in a reckless and indiscriminate manner, striking individuals who were not engaged in violence.[1] The plaintiffs, who seek tens of millions of dollars in damages and would represent dozens of people present on the Capitol's West Front, describe the response as a "wanton barrage" of non-lethal weapons.[1] The case is before US District Judge [[paul-byron|Paul Byron]].[1]

## DOJ Second Amendment lawsuit (December 2025)

On December 22, 2025 the [[department-of-justice|U.S. Department of Justice]], through the newly established Second Amendment Section of its Civil Rights Division, filed a [[doj-lawsuit-against-dc-firearm-laws|federal lawsuit]] against the MPD and the D.C. government.[2] The suit argues that D.C.'s firearm registration laws and MPD's enforcement practices effectively ban commonly owned semi-automatic rifles and other protected weapons, violating the [[second-amendment|Second Amendment]] rights of law-abiding citizens, and followed earlier DOJ allegations that the department and D.C. Code registration provisions were imposing a de facto ban on AR-15s.[2]
