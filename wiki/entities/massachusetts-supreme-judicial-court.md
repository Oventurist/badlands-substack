# Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court

The **Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court** (SJC) is the highest court of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.[1]

## 2024 Trump ballot eligibility case

On January 23, 2024, a bipartisan group of voters challenging [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s ballot eligibility brought their case before the SJC in what was described as a last-ditch effort to keep the former president's name off the March primary ballot in [[massachusetts|Massachusetts]]. The petitioners, represented by Boston attorney [[shannon-liss-riordan|Shannon Liss-Riordan]], had lost the previous week before a state ballot law panel.[1]

The petition asked the court to declare Trump constitutionally ineligible and to order Secretary of State [[william-galvin|William Galvin]] to remove him, on the theory that Trump had violated his oath to uphold the Constitution through a systematic effort to deny the 2020 election results and by fomenting the assault on the U.S. Capitol.[1]

## Relationship to the Colorado case

The Massachusetts action was filed against the backdrop of the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado Section 3 case]], set for argument at the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] on February 8, 2024. Badlands Media commentary argued that the SJC petition was legally dependent on Colorado prevailing, and that Trump's own 59-page SCOTUS brief — contending that the president is not an "officer of the United States" and that he did not "engage in insurrection" — anticipated precisely this proliferation of copycat suits.[1]
