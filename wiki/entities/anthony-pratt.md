# Anthony Pratt

Anthony Pratt is an Australian businessman whose name surfaced in reporting connected to the federal prosecutions of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Alleged classified-information claim

In October 2023, a story published by the New York Times reported claims that Trump had disclosed classified secrets to Pratt.[1] Trump denied the reports, and in posts on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] he accused the team of special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] of having leaked the story to the newspaper, describing Smith as "deranged."[1]

Those posts followed a decision by U.S. District Judge [[tanya-chutkan|Tanya Chutkan]] to grant a temporary hold on her earlier gag order in the 2020 election conspiracy case while Trump's legal team appealed it as unconstitutional.[1] The Pratt allegation therefore became one of the first subjects Trump addressed publicly once the restriction was lifted.[1]
