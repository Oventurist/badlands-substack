# Bill Hagerty

Bill Hagerty is a Republican United States Senator from Tennessee and a former ambassador to Japan, identified in Badlands Media's coverage of the 2024 vice-presidential selection as one of the figures favored by the hawkish wing of the Republican Party for a senior foreign-policy post in a second Trump administration.[1]

## 2024 cabinet speculation

Following Donald Trump's selection of [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] as his running mate in July 2024, [[politico|Politico]] reported that Republicans from the party's Reaganite, interventionist wing openly urged Trump to counter Vance's influence by staffing his national security team with more hawkish officials.[1] Hagerty was named — alongside [[robert-obrien|Robert O'Brien]], Trump's first-term national security adviser — as a preferred candidate for Secretary of State, while the same faction hoped to install [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]] at the Defense Department and [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]] at the CIA or inside the White House.[1]

Badlands Media framed this lobbying campaign as confirmation that the neoconservative establishment viewed Vance as a threat to its influence and was attempting to surround the incoming administration with personnel who would blunt his non-interventionist positions.[1]
