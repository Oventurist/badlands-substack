# Idris Elba

**Idris Elba** is a British actor who became the subject of controversy in February 2023 after declaring that he no longer calls himself a black actor, saying that people had become overly obsessed with race.[1]

## Race and identity remarks

Elba's comment was reported as having run afoul of what Breitbart called the "woke police," and he subsequently refused to back down from it.[1] Defending himself in a tweet posted on Saturday, February 11, 2023, he wrote that his work as an actor should not be defined by race: "There isn't a soul on this earth that can question whether I consider myself a BLACK MAN or not. Being an 'actor' is a profession, like being an 'architect,' they are not defined by race. However, if YOU define your work by your race, that is your Perogative [sic]. Ah lie?"[1] The remarks angered some commentators on the political left.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] said it could be shocking to hear an intelligent statement come out of [[hollywood|Hollywood]] and that he never tired of watching what he called white social-justice activists object to a black man who does not wish to be defined by the color of his skin.[1] DeLarme conceded that Elba had contributed to the problem by "feeding the woke monster" for several years, but rejected the argument that his past should outweigh his present position, noting that many people held liberal views in their youth — particularly during the George W. Bush administration — before moving toward conservatism.[1] He concluded by applauding Elba while withholding final judgement, invoking [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]]'s remark about Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] that trust is earned, and saying it remained to be seen whether the change of heart was sincere or simply pandering to a new demographic.[1]
