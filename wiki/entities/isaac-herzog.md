# Isaac Herzog

**Isaac Herzog** is the President of [[israel]], holding the country's largely ceremonial head-of-state office. Among the presidency's few substantive powers is the authority to grant pardons, which placed Herzog at the center of the political controversy surrounding the corruption trial of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu]].

## Refusal to pardon Netanyahu

Herzog has declined to issue a pardon that would end Netanyahu's prosecution. President [[donald-trump]] publicly attacked him for the refusal, saying Herzog "should be ashamed" and adding, "You have a president that refuses to give him a pardon. I think that man should be ashamed of himself ... he's disgraceful." The remarks came in the period surrounding Netanyahu's February 2026 visit to Washington.

## Badlands interpretation

*Badlands Brief* contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the conventional reading of Trump's pressure — that Trump is Netanyahu's instrument — inverts the actual dynamic. In his analysis, both possible outcomes benefit Trump: if Herzog grants the pardon, the optics are that Trump saved Netanyahu and Bibi owes him everything; if Herzog refuses, Netanyahu faces at least two likely convictions and a potential sentence of ten to fifteen years, with Israeli political divisions making a harsh sentence plausible as a way to "bury the Netanyahu era from view and memory." Either path, in this reading, increases Trump's leverage over the Israeli prime minister.
