# Ruben Gallego

Ruben Gallego is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Arizona's Congressional District 3. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears as a named defendant in the December 2022 election contest filed by Republican challenger [[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]] and [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Secretary of State]] candidate [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]].

## The 2022 contest

Gallego defeated Zink in the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]]. On December 9, 2022, within the five-day window that followed certification of the results by [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], Zink and Finchem filed a joint suit naming Gallego, [[adrian-fontes|Adrian Fontes]], and Hobbs. As described in "Arizona Earthquake!", the allegations in the complaint are directed at Hobbs's administration of the election rather than at any conduct by Gallego.

## Withdrawal of the Platner endorsement (July 2026)

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Gallego was among the prominent Democrats who quickly withdrew their endorsements of Maine Senate candidate [[graham-platner|Graham Platner]] after public sexual assault allegations from [[jenny-racicot|Jenny Racicot]] threw his campaign into turmoil. The Brief notes that Representative [[ro-khanna|Ro Khanna]] went further, calling the allegations serious and credible and urging Platner to drop out of the race against Republican Senator [[susan-collins|Susan Collins]].
