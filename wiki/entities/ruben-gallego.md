# Ruben Gallego

Ruben Gallego is the Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives for Arizona's Third Congressional District, who defeated Republican challenger [[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]] in the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]].[1]

Gallego was named as a defendant in the lawsuit that Zink filed jointly with Secretary of State candidate [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]] on December 9, 2022, which sought to overturn the results of the November election. The suit's substantive allegations were directed at Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], including claims that she failed to ensure the tabulation machines were properly certified and that she pressured county boards of supervisors to certify results.[1]

## Platner endorsement withdrawal (2026)

Gallego was among the prominent Democrats who withdrew their endorsements of Maine Senate candidate [[graham-platner|Graham Platner]] after a former romantic partner publicly accused Platner of sexual assault, a wave of withdrawals that also included Representative [[ro-khanna|Ro Khanna]].[2]

## 2026 calls to resign

In April 2026 Gallego faced calls to resign after failing to convince the press that he was not the second man appearing in a video circulating alongside the material implicating Representative Eric Swalwell, according to Badlands commentator Ashe in America.[3]
