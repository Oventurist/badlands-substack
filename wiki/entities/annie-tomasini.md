# Annie Tomasini

Annie Tomasini served as a deputy chief of staff in the [[white-house|White House]] under President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and was one of three senior aides subpoenaed by the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] in July 2024 over concerns about the president's mental fitness.[1]

## July 2024 subpoena

The Republican-led committee demanded depositions from Tomasini, first lady Jill Biden's top aide Anthony Bernal, and senior adviser Ashley Williams.[1] Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] argued the White House had shielded the three aides from testifying about Biden's handling of classified documents and asserted that reporting showed the same staffers were working to conceal the president's "declining cognitive state" inside the executive mansion.[1] Comer stated that Biden was "clearly unfit for office" and that his staff were "trying to hide the truth from the American people."[1]

The subpoenas were issued amid the post-debate crisis of summer 2024, in which Biden's June 27 performance triggered sustained scrutiny of his age and capacity and open calls within his party for him to step aside.[1]
