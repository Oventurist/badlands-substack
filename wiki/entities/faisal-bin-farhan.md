# Faisal bin Farhan

Faisal bin Farhan is the Foreign Minister of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]. Badlands commentary points to public reports of his meetings with Iranian counterparts in the run-up to the 2026 war, arguing that media accounts wrongly framed the encounters as Saudi threats against [[iran|Iran]] when they were in fact warnings delivered in good faith.[1]

That reading is offered as corroboration for Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]]'s account of a heated pre-war dialogue with an unnamed Gulf prince — a description [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] considers congruent with the conduct of Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]].[1] The broader point advanced is that the Saudis are playing an ever-expanding role in regional diplomacy, plausibly on behalf of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[1]


## Call to Lift Syria Sanctions (January 2025)

At a press briefing following high-level meetings on [[syria|Syria]] in Riyadh on Sunday, January 12, 2025, Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan called for the lifting of [[syria-sanctions|sanctions on Syria]], saying the curbs hindered the country's rebuilding and development.[2] "We stressed the importance of lifting unilateral and international sanctions imposed on Syria, as their continuation hinders the aspirations of the Syrian people to achieve development and reconstruction," he said.[2] The meeting drew representatives from Syria, Jordan, Lebanon and Türkiye as well as the UK and US, with officials from the GCC, EU and UN also taking part.[2] Prince Faisal said the gathering sought to advise Syria's new leadership in a way that respects its independence, and stressed continuing humanitarian and economic support, stability, reconstruction, and conditions for the return of Syrian refugees.[2]

## Riyadh diplomacy, September 2024

At the annual [[gulf-cooperation-council|Gulf Cooperation Council]] regional meeting held in Riyadh on September 9, 2024, Faisal bin Farhan — referred to in Badlands commentary by the initials "FBF" — scheduled stacked Joint Ministerial Meetings of Strategic Dialogue with both [[russia|Russia]] and [[india|India]], held in immediate sequence.[3] Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] attended, as did India's External Affairs Minister.[3] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the convergence of Russia and India in Riyadh as connected to Indian Prime Minister [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]]'s recent meeting with Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[3]

A widely circulated photograph from the meeting showed Faisal bin Farhan seated beside Kuwaiti Crown Prince [[mishal-al-sabah|Mishal Al-Sabah]].[3] During the same gathering, Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] publicly called for an emergency meeting of all Islamic world leaders; Faisal bin Farhan then departed the Riyadh meeting, went directly to the airport, and flew to [[egypt|Egypt]].[3] The Badlands commentary dubbed him "The Peacemaker," presenting his shuttle diplomacy as part of a broader healing of the Middle East and the formation of a strong alliance across the Islamic world that would frustrate efforts to set Sunni against Shia.[3]

## Postponed Washington trip (February 2025)

In February 2025 the Financial Times reported that Prince Faisal had postponed a scheduled trip to Washington after the US president announced his plan for Gaza, according to a person familiar with the matter.[4] The postponement came amid a ferocious [[saudi-arabia|Saudi]] state media campaign against Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[4]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who described Faisal as a capable and congenial diplomat who had earned friends for Saudi Arabia around the world, called the cancellation "a little alarming" and read it as a signal that the Saudis had lost patience with Tel Aviv — even while assuming the kingdom remained aligned with [[donald-trump|Trump]] ahead of an anticipated peace summit with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[4]
