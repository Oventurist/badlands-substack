# Group of Thirty

The **Group of Thirty (G30)** is an international body of senior economists and central bankers cited in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as one of the destinations of the [[princeton-university|Princeton]] central-banking pipeline.[1]

The article says Princeton produces "Federal Reserve governors, IMF and BIS strategists, and G30 economists," and that the pathway from the [[ivy-club|Ivy Club]] connects to the Group of Thirty, the [[brookings-institution|Brookings Institution]] and the [[international-monetary-fund|International Monetary Fund]].[1] The G30 also appears among the policy think tanks — with the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] — into which [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard|Vanguard]] and [[state-street|State Street]] are said to feed, and where Ivy-trained economists allegedly "dictate global economic narratives" on behalf of the [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
