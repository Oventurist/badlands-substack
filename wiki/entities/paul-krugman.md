# Paul Krugman

Paul Krugman is an American economist and commentator who for many years wrote a column for The New York Times and who, by early 2025, was publishing instead on his own Substack account.[1] In Badlands-adjacent coverage he appears as an emblem of establishment opinion journalism on public health and economics.[1]

## Criticism over the 2025 public health freeze

When the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration imposed a communications freeze across the federal public health bureaucracy in its first days, Krugman claimed that Trump wanted people to die — a charge the Brownstone Institute rejected outright, noting that he had reserved the "rather extreme view" for his Substack rather than a mainstream outlet.[1] See [[2025-public-health-communications-freeze|the 2025 public health communications freeze]].[1]

Writing for the [[brownstone-institute|Brownstone Institute]], the author recalled that Krugman had been "100 percent for lockdowns and all the rest including the fake science behind vaccine mandates," and that while much of the world was under lockdown restrictions he had proclaimed the dawn of the great reset; with that program now reversed, the piece argued, he had "reverted to form."[1]
