# Benjamin Disraeli

Benjamin Disraeli was a British Prime Minister quoted in Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" as an establishment figure who publicly acknowledged the existence of [[secret-society|secret societies]].[1]

As far back as 1856, Disraeli told the House of Commons that "it is useless to deny, because it is impossible to conceal, that a great part of Europe—the whole of Italy and France and a great portion of [then fragmented] Germany, to say nothing of other countries—is covered with a network of these secret societies."[1] He continued that their objects were not concealed: "They do not want constitutional government … they want to change the tenure of land, to drive out the present owners of the soil, and to put an end to ecclesiastical establishments."[1]

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites the statement alongside remarks by [[george-washington|George Washington]], [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]], [[john-f-hylan|John F. Hylan]] and [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin D. Roosevelt]] to argue that important historical figures knew secret societies were shaping world events.[1]
