# George W. Bush Institute

The **George W. Bush Institute** is the policy think tank of former President [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]].[1] It hosted the New York policy seminar in October 2017 at which Bush delivered a speech widely read as a veiled attack on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

According to the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]], Bush delivered his stinging rebuke of his fellow Republican as part of a larger survey of the political horizon at home and abroad, restating many of the principles that guided his presidency and were standard GOP orthodoxy until Trump upended the party.[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands Media commentary observes that the institute receives little public attention, and groups it with John McCain's institute and the Clinton foundation as organizations that raise money from foreign sources.[1] The article asserts that such foundations and institutes are "all just set up for receiving bribes and payoffs from their puppet masters," and promises a fuller treatment of these vehicles in a subsequent article.[1]

## Assessment

The seminar and speech details derive from quoted reporting; the bribery characterization is the author's allegation with no evidence presented in the article. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
