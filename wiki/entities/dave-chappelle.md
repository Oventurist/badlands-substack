# Dave Chappelle

Dave Chappelle is an American comedian whose stand-up special *Dreamer* ranked, alongside [[ricky-gervais|Ricky Gervais]]'s *Armageddon*, among the two top comedy specials in the world at the beginning of January 2024.[1]

## Dreamer and the 2024 comedy backlash

Both specials were reported to be generating a "furious backlash" while spreading virally across mainstream and dissident audiences, driven by their criticism of establishment politics, transgender ideology, and cultural Marxism.[1] Both were distributed by [[netflix|Netflix]], a platform not generally viewed as sympathetic to the truth movement.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the pairing as a possible distraction or limited hangout, but favored the interpretation that it reflected how far the [[overton-window|Overton Window]] had been pushed back toward objective reality, and that mainstream audiences were being granted the "social permission" to voice previously suppressed views.[1]

## Boston walkout (October 2023)

In October 2023, Chappelle — described by Badlands as the most famous comedian in the world — was subjected to a partial walkout during a Boston appearance, a reaction Badlands attributed to the extreme polarization surrounding the Israel-Hamas war.[2] His remarks angered ideological supporters of both Israel and Palestine, which Badlands contributor Burning Bright cited as a demonstration of the bind confronting the prevailing cultural regime.[2]
