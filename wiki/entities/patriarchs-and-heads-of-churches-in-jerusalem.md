# Patriarchs and Heads of the Churches in Jerusalem

The Patriarchs and Heads of the Churches in Jerusalem is the council of traditional, liturgical Christian church leaders — Greek Orthodox, Armenian, and other apostolic communions — responsible for the Christian holy places in the Holy Land. In January 2026 the body issued a public statement condemning [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionism]] as a "damaging" ideology.

## The January 17, 2026 statement

On January 17, 2026, the Patriarchs and Heads of the Churches in Jerusalem issued a public statement "on unity and representation of the Christian communities in the Holy Land." The statement affirmed that "the flock of Christ in this land is entrusted to the Apostolic Churches which have borne their sacred ministry across centuries with steadfast devotion," and warned that "recent activities undertaken by local individuals who advance damaging ideologies, such as Christian Zionism, mislead the public, sow confusion, and harm the unity of our flock."

The statement charged that such undertakings "have found favor among certain political actors in Israel and beyond who seek to push a political agenda which may harm the Christian presence in the Holy Land and the wider Middle East."

## The exchange with Ambassador Huckabee

US Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] responded to the Patriarchs on X with his own statement, saying he loved "my brothers and sisters in Christ from traditional, liturgical churches" and respected their views, but did not believe "any sect of the Christian faith should claim exclusivity in speaking for Christians worldwide." He argued: "If God can or would break His covenant with the Jews, then what hope would Christians have that He would keep His covenant with us?" and defended Christian Zionism as follows: "Christians are followers of Christ and a Zionist simply accepts that the Jewish people have a right to live in their ancient, indigenous, and Biblical homeland. It's hard for me to understand why every one who takes on the moniker 'Christian' would not also be a Zionist."

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] published a copy of the Patriarchs' letter, describing the group's assertion that Christian Zionism "misleads the public, sows confusion, and harms Christian unity," and their condemnation of political leaders — such as Huckabee — who use their positions in government to promote war and undermine peace. He characterized Huckabee's position as an awkward one for the pastor-turned-ambassador.
