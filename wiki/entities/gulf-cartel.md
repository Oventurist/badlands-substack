# Gulf Cartel

The Gulf Cartel is the Mexican drug trafficking organization led by [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]] during the period covered by the Badlands Media article.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Under García Abrego the cartel was transformed from a smuggling operation into a supplier: by renegotiating with the [[cali-cartel|Cali Cartel]] he secured 50 percent of every shipment out of Colombia as payment for delivery, in place of the US $1,500 per kilo previously received. The price of that arrangement was that the cartel had to guarantee any shipment from Colombia to its destination.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The article argues that this delivery guarantee is precisely why [[nafta|NAFTA]] mattered so much to the cartel's allies — easier border crossings made guaranteed delivery feasible.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

The [[salinas-family|Salinas family]] was connected to the cartel, and it was Gulf Cartel product that [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]] gave "green light protection" through Mexico.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The cartel paid [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] to bury the investigation into his own brother's murder, money he deposited at [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] in Houston, which was later discovered to have stashed $7 million in Gulf Cartel drug profits without legal consequence.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
