# Gulf Cartel

The Gulf Cartel was the Mexican trafficking organization led by [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]] and, in the Badlands account, the cartel to which the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]] was connected.[1] [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]] provided its shipments with "green light protection" through Mexico, and cartel money paid [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] to bury the investigation into his own brother's murder.[1]

Under García Abrego the cartel shifted from smuggling to supply, renegotiating with the [[cali-cartel|Cali Cartel]] to take 50 percent of every shipment out of Colombia as payment for delivery instead of the previous US$1,500 per kilo — at the price of having to guarantee any shipment from Colombia to its destination.[1] The article argues this guarantee requirement explains why [[nafta|NAFTA]] mattered so much to the cartel's protectors: easier movement across the southern border made delivery reliable.[1]

[[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] was later discovered to have stashed $7 million in Gulf Cartel drug profits without legal consequence.[1]
