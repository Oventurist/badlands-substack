# Gulf Cartel

The Gulf Cartel is a Mexican criminal organization based in Matamoros, Tamaulipas, historically involved in drug trafficking, money laundering, and political corruption. In the Badlands Media article a family affair, the Gulf Cartel is identified as the drug-trafficking partner of the salinas family in Mexico and as a key beneficiary of [[nafta]]-facilitated border movement.

## Leadership and transformation

According to wikipedia, [[juan-garcia-abrego]] transformed the Gulf Cartel from smugglers into cocaine suppliers by renegotiating with the [[cali-cartel]], securing 50 percent of every Colombian shipment as payment for delivery rather than a flat fee per kilo. This renegotiation required the cartel to guarantee delivery from Colombia to its destination, expanding its operational scope and influence.

## Relationship with the Salinas family

The article claims that the salinas family, specifically [[raul-salinas]] and President [[carlos-salinas]], provided "green light protection" for Gulf Cartel drug trafficking through Mexico. According to the source, Raul Salinas was the Salinas family's primary link to the cartel, and the money generated from drug trafficking was deposited into [[texas-commerce-bank]] in Houston by [[mario-ruiz-massieu]], the brother of a murder victim killed on Raul Salinas's orders.

## Money laundering through Texas Commerce Bank

According to *The Guardian* and U.S. grand jury testimony cited in the article, [[mario-ruiz-massieu]] made 26 cash deposits totaling nearly $10 million at [[texas-commerce-bank]] in Houston between December 1993 and February 1995. His bagman, [[jorge-stergios]], reportedly carried the money in cardboard boxes and suitcases on commercial flights from Mexico to Texas. U.S. prosecutors alleged these were bribes paid by drug traffickers in exchange for immunity from prosecution. The article notes that Texas Commerce Bank was later discovered to have stashed $7 million in drug profits for the Gulf Cartel, yet faced no legal consequences.

## Connection to NAFTA

The article argues that [[nafta]] was designed to facilitate Gulf Cartel drug trafficking by reducing border scrutiny, making it easier to guarantee delivery of cocaine shipments from Colombia to the United States. The source frames NAFTA as a corrupt bargain between the Bush family, the Salinas family, and the cartel.
