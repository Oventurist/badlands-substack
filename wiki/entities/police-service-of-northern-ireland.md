# Police Service of Northern Ireland

The Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) is the territorial police force responsible for Northern Ireland.[1]

## April 2023 security breach

In April 2023 the PSNI opened an investigation after a document detailing arrangements for US President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s visit to Northern Ireland was found lying in the street by a member of the public.[1] The incident was first reported by BBC Radio Ulster, part of the [[bbc|BBC]].[1] The PSNI confirmed it was "aware of a security breach" and stated that it takes "the safety of visiting dignitaries, members of the public and our officers and staff extremely seriously and will put the appropriate actions in place."[1]

The document appeared to set out police deployments for the visit, which opened with events in Belfast marking a quarter-century of the [[good-friday-agreement|Good Friday Agreement]].[1] The episode was reported by [[cnn|CNN]] and carried in the Badlands News Brief under the framing of "real leaks" contrasted with contemporaneous media coverage of classified-document allegations.[1]
