# Justin Sun

Justin Sun is a crypto billionaire and the founder of Tron.[1]

## World Liberty Financial lawsuit

In May 2026, [[world-liberty-financial|World Liberty Financial]] — the cryptocurrency venture co-founded by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his sons — sued Sun for defamation in Miami-Dade County, Florida, accusing him of a coordinated campaign to damage the company's reputation and seeking unspecified damages and a public retraction.[1] The company alleges Sun bet against its $WLFI token and used third parties to conceal purchases, prompting World Liberty to move to freeze assets tied to one of his firms.[1] The filing claims Sun then waged a "scorched earth" campaign of litigation threats and public attacks, spreading false claims amplified through influencers and bot accounts that generated millions of views.[1] Sun had not publicly responded to the allegations at the time of reporting.[1]
