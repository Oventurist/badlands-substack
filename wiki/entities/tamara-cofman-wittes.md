# Tamara Cofman Wittes

Tamara Cofman Wittes is an American foreign policy specialist who has served as president of the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]] (NDI) since 2024.[1] She previously worked in [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s State Department, and Badlands Media argues that her Middle East expertise aligns NDI with Democratic foreign policy priorities.[1]

Her appointment followed the tenure of [[derek-mitchell|Derek Mitchell]], who led the institute from 2018 to 2023.[1] In the article's account, the continuity of State Department alumni at the top of NDI is evidence that the institute functions less as an independent non-governmental organisation than as an extension of the establishment foreign policy consensus funded through the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] and, ultimately, the taxpayer.[1]
