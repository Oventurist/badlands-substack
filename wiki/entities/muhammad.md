# Muhammad

Muhammad, the Prophet of Islam, was the grandson of [[hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim]] and a member of the Banu Hashim within the [[quraysh|Quraysh]] tribe of Mecca.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Where Hashim stabilized the region, it was Muhammad who would "forever change the world."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Revelation and early followers

Muhammad made routine excursions into the caves around Mecca to meditate.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Emerging from one, he claimed that the archangel Gabriel — the same celestial being, he said, who visited Mary on the night of Jesus's birth — had brought him a message from God.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The only person who believed him at first was the ten-year-old [[ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], whose father had adopted Muhammad when he was orphaned as a child and whom Muhammad himself raised from the age of five.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Ali became the first Muslim, and Muhammad called him his brother, guardian and successor, and once a Mawla — an Arabic word variously meaning 'Lord', 'trustee', and 'helper'.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Governance and tolerance

In 622 Muhammad wrote the [[constitution-of-medina|Constitution of Medina]], a historic document corroborated by multiple sources, formalizing an agreement between his followers and the Jewish clans that had historically ruled Medina.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) It established Medina as a haram (sanctuary), prohibited violence between clans, and guaranteed religious freedom for all non-Muslims, specifically Jews.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) He later established similar laws in Jerusalem, where Jews had previously been banned from worship by the ruling Christian authorities — a record the article says defies the modern narrative on Islam and its rival religions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) His successors, especially under [[wahhabism|Wahhabism]], adopted a far less tolerant disposition.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

Muhammad defeated the [[banu-bakr|Banu Bakr]] in 627 and had their Christian emir tied to a pole and threatened with execution; when the emir, rather than renounce Christ, calmly recited a wisdom three times demonstrating his devotion to God and total lack of fear of death, Muhammad recognized him as a God-fearing man, released him, and embraced him as a brother — the emir later converting to Islam of his own volition.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Succession

Muhammad dictated no line of succession.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Most followers supported his most trusted companion, [[abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], initiating the [[rashidun-caliphate|Rashidun Caliphate]]; a minority held that Ali was the intended heir, producing the Shia–Sunni division.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The author counts Muhammad, with Hashim and the original [[mohammed-bin-saud|Mohammed bin Saud]], among the region's key figures who were men of honor and reason rather than ruthless ideologues.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The Quran, he notes, credits Jesus with at least six miracles while clarifying that Muhammad performed none.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The article also states that Muhammad had red hair and a fair complexion, as did many of his kin.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
