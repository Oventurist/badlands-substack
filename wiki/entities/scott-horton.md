# Scott Horton

Scott Horton is a historian and foreign-policy writer who was interviewed at length by [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] in early July 2025, in a conversation Badlands commentators treated as one of the most consequential media events of that week.[1]

## Carlson interview and its claims

Carlson gave Horton over two hours to deliver an extended historical account of the origins of Iran's purported conflict with the West. Badlands commentary described the revelations concerning post-1979 Iran as striking relative to the picture presented by outlets such as Fox News: the United States established immediate contact with the Ayatollah after the revolution, maintained that contact through the 1980s and into the mid-1990s, and shipped weapons to Iran while encouraging it to fight a war with Iraq.[1]

According to the account relayed in the brief, Israel also supplied Iran with weapons, and stopped only after 1993, when Prime Minister [[yitzhak-rabin|Yitzhak Rabin]] called for normalization with the Palestinians and the creation of a two-state solution. Leaning into the warhawk rhetoric espoused by figures such as [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], Rabin branded Iran as the enemy so that the Israeli citizenry would have a target for its "two minutes of hate," leaving Palestine and the Arabs open for earnest discussion. Iran, in this telling, became the "bad guy" so that Palestine could be publicly rebranded as a partner in order to sell the two-state solution to Israelis.[1]

Horton's interview also featured in Badlands' treatment of the Syrian file, where the bizarre character of the Western narrative around [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham|Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham]] and its leader was said to be well encapsulated in a clip from the Carlson–Horton discussion.[1]

Commentary on both interviews was written by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[1]
