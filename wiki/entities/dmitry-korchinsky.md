# Dmitry Korchinsky

Dmitry Korchinsky is a radical Ukrainian nationalist politician who leads the far-right Bratstvo ("Brotherhood") party. A longtime advocate of war with Russia, he has been compared by Western media to the Taliban on account of his ideology.[1]

## "Army of God" remarks

Speaking on his YouTube channel in early November 2025, Korchinsky argued that Kiev should transform its military into "an army of God" capable of conquering both Russia and China, claiming such a force would be rendered invincible by divine intervention.[1]

In the same remarks he criticized Ukrainians who had grown tired of the conflict with Russia, asserting that they had fallen for the narratives of the "enemy" and the "devil." Korchinsky acknowledged that war is "terrifying" but claimed it also brings "adventure" and "great joy," contrasting life at the front — where, he argued, every action becomes a meaningful sacrifice for others — with a dull civilian existence in which "we are unneeded, grey, and our very existence" irritates everyone.[1]

He has also called for Ukrainian children to be prevented from leaving the country during the conflict so that they may "grow up here hating the enemy."[1]
