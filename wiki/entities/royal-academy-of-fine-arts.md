# Royal Academy of Fine Arts

The English Royal Academy of Fine Arts was established in 1769 as the central institution of British artistic life, and is presented in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the platform from which the American-born painter [[benjamin-west|Benjamin West]] shaped the cultural, moral and aesthetic senses of the British Empire from its very heart.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## West's role

West became a founding member of the Academy in 1769, shortly after arriving in England and receiving the patronage of [[king-george-iii|King George III]], and in 1791 became its president.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) His 1795 painting *The Royal Academicians* places him at the centre surrounded by artists and artworks of the institution.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) Among those depicted is [[john-singleton-copley|John Singleton Copley]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

The Academy also functioned as the training ground for an entire generation of American painters whom West prepared for the mission of establishing a new American renaissance movement — one sensitive to resolving both the European versus Native American problem and the tension between personal liberty and the general welfare.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) Those trained by West in England included Charles Willson Peale, Rembrandt Peale, Gilbert Stuart, John Trumbull, Copley and [[samuel-morse|Samuel F. B. Morse]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## Establishment resistance

The Academy's surrounding establishment enforced the convention that history paintings be executed in Greco-Roman costume, and West was threatened that he would never receive another commission if he broke the rule with *The Death of General Wolfe*.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) He proceeded anyway and won the argument when George III purchased the painting.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) West remained president into 1816, by which time the king's final insanity had left him without royal sponsorship or patronage and quite isolated.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)
