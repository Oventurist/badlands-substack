# Mark Finchem

Mark Finchem was the America First Republican candidate for Secretary of State of Arizona in the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]]. He lost to Democrat [[adrian-fontes|Adrian Fontes]] and joined the wave of legal challenges filed by Republican candidates in the five-day window that followed the December 5, 2022 certification of the results.[1]

## The Finchem–Zink lawsuit

On Friday afternoon, December 9, 2022, Finchem joined America First congressional candidate [[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]] of Arizona's Third Congressional District in filing a suit to overturn the November results. The defendants were Fontes, Zink's opponent Representative [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]], and sitting Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]].[1]

The complaint alleged that Hobbs abused the powers of her office in several ways: by failing to have the tabulation machines properly certified; by threatening the boards of supervisors in Mohave and Cochise counties with criminal charges if they refused to certify the election; and by using her office to flag what it deemed misinformation on a Twitter account in January 2021.[1] Finchem and Zink also argued that Hobbs should have recused herself from her duties as Secretary of State while running for Governor.[1]

## Assessment in Badlands coverage

[[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], described Finchem as "a committed America First patriot, who has a deep conviction to oversee and administer secure and accurate elections in Arizona," and said his frustration with the state's election process over several years had been palpable.[1] She predicted he would eventually take the Secretary of State's office — first in the line of succession to a governorship held by [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] — but noted that this outcome depended on Lake prevailing in her own contest.[1] The [[kari-lake|Lake]] suit separately alleged that Maricopa County Recorder [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]] had founded a political action committee whose sole mission was to stop Lake and Finchem.[1]
