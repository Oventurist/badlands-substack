# Marlin Fitzwater

Marlin Fitzwater was the [[white-house|White House]] press spokesman during the [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] presidency.[1]

He appears in Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s account of the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan crisis]] as one of the three official voices insisting that [[neil-bush|Neil Bush]] had done nothing wrong in connection with the collapse of [[silverado-savings-and-loan-association|Silverado]] — the others being Neil Bush himself and his father the president.[1] The Orlando Sentinel set those denials against a bailout of federally insured deposits expected to cost $1 billion, and against reports that the White House had given clear marching orders to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] and banking investigators to lay off Neil Bush.[1]
