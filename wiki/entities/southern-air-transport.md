# Southern Air Transport

Southern Air Transport was a CIA-affiliated air carrier central to the logistics of the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] operation.[1]

After the scandal erupted in 1986, aircraft linked to the carrier were relocated to Ohio bases servicing the empire of [[les-wexner|Les Wexner]], with accounts from local investigators identifying [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] in the transfer.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described this evolution — from arms-related assets to private luxury fleets — as mirroring how covert infrastructure sometimes transitions into elite private use, and faulted the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] for avoiding the episode entirely in its December 2025 investigation.[1]
