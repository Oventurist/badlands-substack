# Keith Schiller

Keith Schiller is a longtime bodyguard and security aide to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] whose name became central to the defense case during Trump's New York "hush money" trial.[1]

## Role in the hush money trial

During cross-examination in May 2024, Trump defense attorney [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] confronted [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] over a pivotal October 2016 phone call that prosecutors used to connect Trump to the reimbursement of payments made to [[stormy-daniels|Stormy Daniels]].[1] Cohen had testified that the call was placed to Trump to reassure him that the Daniels payment was being handled.[1] Blanche produced text messages indicating that Cohen had in fact been communicating with Schiller — Trump's bodyguard — rather than with Trump himself, undercutting the prosecution's chain of evidence.[1]

Cohen appeared blindsided by this line of questioning and wavered in his recollection of the call before insisting, "I believe I was telling the truth!"[1] Commentators, including [[cnn|CNN]] host [[anderson-cooper|Anderson Cooper]], described the exchange as an extraordinary cross-examination in which Cohen was cornered in a lie.[1] Because the call was routed through Schiller, his role became the fulcrum on which a significant portion of the prosecution's narrative rested.[1]
