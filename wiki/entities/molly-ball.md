# Molly Ball

Molly Ball is an American political journalist best known in the Badlands corpus as the author of the February 2021 [[time-magazine]] article describing the "shadow campaign" that shaped the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election]].

That article recounted how a coalition of labor organizations, business groups, non-governmental organizations, technology platforms, and election officials coordinated to change voting rules, shape information flows, and secure acceptance of the result. Ball presented the arrangement as a patriotic effort to fortify the election. Badlands writers cite it constantly as an on-the-record admission of the very coordination they are accused of imagining.

In the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, [[ashe-in-america]] returned to a passage from the piece describing how "corporate America turned on" President [[donald-trump]] after the election, with hundreds of business leaders who had backed him calling on him to concede — an episode Trump called "all very, very strange." Reading it alongside declassified [[cia]] material published through the [[white-house-election-integrity-portal]] describing [[china]]'s pressure on American business figures and campaign donors, she argued that the sequence was neither natural nor organic: "Molly Ball and TIME want you to believe the public-private campaign after 2020 — the narratives and networks and corporations and NGOs and government officials — was virtuous and just... It wasn't."
