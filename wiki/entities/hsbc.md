# HSBC

The Hong Kong and Shanghai Banking Corporation (HSBC) is a British-founded bank established after the second Opium War of 1860.[1]

## Founding and role in the opium trade

According to [[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.*]], HSBC "became the central clearinghouse for all Far Eastern financial transactions relating to the black market in opium and its heroin derivative," at a time when Britain controlled seven-eighths of the vastly expanded opium trade into China.[1] The bank's creation followed directly from the settlement of the [[opium-wars|Opium Wars]], which gave Britain control of [[hong-kong|Hong Kong]] and secured legal access for opium imports into China.[1]

In the argument advanced by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], HSBC's origin illustrates the broader claim that the modern banking system was built in part on narcotics revenue rather than merely being exploited by it after the fact.[1] British opium refineries in Shanghai and Hong Kong, paired with British banks in Canada and the Caribbean, supplied and financed the narcotics traffic that entered the United States during [[prohibition|Prohibition]].[1]
