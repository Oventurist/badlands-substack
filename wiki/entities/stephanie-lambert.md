# Stephanie Lambert

**Stephanie Lambert** is an attorney and activist known for her work on election-integrity litigation.[1] She is described as a well-known election attorney who works with [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]].[1]

## Role in the Tina Peters clemency effort

In December 2025, attorney [[peter-ticktin|Peter Ticktin]] published a letter to President Donald Trump urging a pardon for former Mesa County clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]].[1] Appearing on [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s program to discuss the letter, Ticktin stated at the outset of the interview that the letter was the legal work of Lambert.[1]

The letter advanced a novel constitutional theory: that because the framers referred to the "united States" using plural pronouns — "they," "them," "their," as in Article III's treason clause — the presidential pardon power extends to all offenses, including state crimes, subject only to the impeachment exception.[1] Badlands contributor Ashe in America summarized the argument as "America Uses 'They/Them' Pronouns So Trump Can Pardon Tina Peters," and criticized the framing as effectively giving Trump a deadline to act outside his authority, setting up a narrative in which any failure to free Peters by Christmas would be blamed on the President or his appointees.[1]
