# Nicole Brown Simpson

Nicole Brown Simpson was the former wife of football star and actor [[oj-simpson|O.J. Simpson]].[1] She was killed in a knife slaying in Los Angeles in June 1994 along with her friend [[ronald-goldman|Ronald Goldman]].[1]

O.J. Simpson was acquitted of criminal charges in the killings in a trial that mesmerized the public and exposed divisions over race and policing in America, though he was later found liable for the deaths in a separate civil case.[1] The murders permanently reshaped his legacy, and his death from prostate cancer in April 2024 at age 76 renewed public attention to the case.[1]

## Kardashian and Jenner connections

Commentary published by Badlands Media noted that Nicole Brown Simpson was reportedly best friends with [[kris-jenner|Kris Jenner]], who was married to [[caitlyn-jenner|Caitlyn Jenner]] at the time of the killings, while Kris Jenner's former husband [[robert-kardashian|Robert Kardashian]] served on Simpson's defense team — placing the Jenner and Kardashian families, in the writer's words, "kind of on both sides of the OJ saga."[1]
