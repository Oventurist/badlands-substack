# Bob Levinson

Bob Levinson was a retired FBI agent who was contracted by the CIA to run a covert operations unit in Iran, where he was captured in 2007 and held hostage.[1] At the FBI he had specialized in Russian money laundering.[1]

## The rescue effort

Levinson was long presumed dead, but Air Force intelligence officer Bob Kent apparently obtained proof of life and organized a rescue attempt.[1] Kent contracted with Russian oligarch [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]] to fund the rescue mission at a cost of $20 million, in exchange for expedited green cards for Deripaska's family.[1] Former federal prosecutor [[david-mcgee|David McGee]] was brought in because he knew both Levinson and Deripaska, having worked in the Organized Crimes Task Force at the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], and [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]] served as the FBI point man on the arrangement.[1]

The rescue operation was under way during President Obama's first term, but on the eve of its execution the Clinton [[state-department|State Department]] was spooked by something and called the whole thing off.[1] Levinson remained a hostage and was later presumed dead.[1] In 2021 Kent concluded that Levinson was still alive and approached McGee with the idea of extorting Don Gaetz, the father of Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]], to finance a renewed rescue attempt.[1]
