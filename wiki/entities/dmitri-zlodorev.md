# Dmitri Zlodorev

Dmitri Zlodorev is a Russian-language contact who emerged at the 2022 trial of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] as the apparent connecting link between Danchenko and [[sergei-millian|Sergei Millian]].[1]

## Role in the Danchenko trial

On the second day of trial, [[john-durham|John Durham]] had FBI Supervisory Intelligence Analyst [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]] read into the record an email dated July 26, 2016 from Millian to Zlodorev, five days after Danchenko's July 21, 2016 email to Millian.[1] The message contained the forwarded Danchenko email of July 21 together with Millian's own July 26 response, and Auten then read Zlodorev's reply to Millian.[1] The correspondent covering the trial concluded from this exchange that Zlodorev was the link between Danchenko and Millian, and that it pointed toward how Danchenko came to know of Millian, why he attempted to contact him, and why he later lied to the [[fbi|FBI]] about having made contact.[1]

A further exhibit consisted of Russian-language messages between Danchenko and Zlodorev dated August 24–25, 2016, which Danchenko had himself provided to the FBI in January 2017 and which included the line "Sergei doesn't respond."[1] On cross-examination the defense introduced an additional Zlodorev-to-Millian email dated May 26, 2016 — a document the prosecution had earlier tried to admit over defense objection.[1] On redirect Durham returned to that May email to emphasize that the only forms of contact it mentioned were an email address and a telephone number, with no reference to any messaging application.[1]
