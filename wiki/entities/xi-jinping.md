# Xi Jinping

Xi Jinping is the President of the People's Republic of China and General Secretary of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]]. In the Badlands Media article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]] he appears as host of the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit in Tianjin, standing alongside [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] as representatives of the SCO's three founding nations.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s article credits China's "productive financial architecture" under Xi with unleashing more than $3 trillion in large-scale infrastructure investment through the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], increasingly integrated with the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]]. It also cites Chinese hypersonic missile technology as part of the strategic reality that, in the author's view, makes a Western first strike impossible.

The framing of Xi in this article contrasts markedly with other entries in the Badlands corpus, where the Chinese Communist Party is presented as a hostile actor engaged in subversion of the United States. Here, by contrast, China's leadership is portrayed as a partner in a cooperative, sovereignty-respecting order grounded in the [[un-charter|UN Charter]] and the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]].

## Putin's Beijing visit for the treaty anniversary (May 2026)

The May 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-5a2|Badlands Brief]] reports that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] was scheduled to visit Beijing on May 19–20, 2026 for talks with Xi, per a [[kremlin|Kremlin]] announcement released Saturday, May 16, coinciding with the 25th anniversary of the 2001 [[sino-russian-treaty-of-friendship|Sino-Russian Treaty of Friendship]]. The visit came less than a week after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] concluded a state visit to [[china|China]] that included meetings with Xi on trade, Taiwan, and the conflict involving [[iran|Iran]]. Chinese state media described the sequence of visits by the US and Russian presidents as unusual and indicative of Beijing's growing diplomatic influence. Ahead of the visit, Xi and Putin exchanged congratulatory messages, with Xi quoted as saying China-Russia cooperation had "continuously deepened and solidified."
