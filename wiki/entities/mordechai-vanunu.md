# Mordechai Vanunu

Mordechai Vanunu is an Israeli nuclear technician who in 1985 defected from [[israel|Israel]] to London carrying extensive evidence that Israel maintained a secret nuclear weapons program — the same kind of program Iran has long been accused of pursuing.[1]

## Disclosure and discrediting campaign

Vanunu spoke to news outlets in London while determining which was best suited to carry the story.[1] When [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] learned of the effort, he had his newspaper, [[daily-mail|The Daily Mail]], publish several hit pieces designed to discredit and impugn Vanunu's character; the stories were false but damaging enough that many dismissed his claims as the vindictive acts of a disgruntled scientist.[1]

## Abduction and imprisonment

Vanunu was lured to Italy by the [[mossad|Mossad]], where he was kidnapped and taken back to Israel to face a closed-door trial.[1] He was convicted and sentenced to 18 years in prison, much of which he spent in solitary confinement.[1] He now lives as a ward of the state, unable to travel or freely use the internet, and an X account he briefly maintained was shuttered by the Israeli government.[1]

His case is a central episode in the history of the [[israeli-nuclear-program|Israeli nuclear program]].[1]

## Later accounts

Later Badlands commentary dates Vanunu's defection to 1986 and describes him as an employee of the [[negev-nuclear-research-center|Negev Nuclear Research Center]] who went public with details of the facility, corroborating his claims with detailed photographs he had taken of the site and of the nuclear material housed within it.[2] By that account Vanunu was secretly drugged and captured by the Mossad in Italy and returned to Israel for a closed trial on treason charges, where he was convicted and sentenced to 18 years in prison, 11 of them in solitary confinement.[2] He converted to Christianity and, since his release in 2004, has advocated for the nuclear disarmament of Israel, though his activities and movements remain strictly controlled by the Israeli government.[2]
