# Maya Wiley

Maya Wiley is president and chief executive officer of the Leadership Conference on Civil and Human Rights, a coalition of American civil rights organisations.[1]

## Testimony before the weaponization subcommittee (2023)

Wiley was among the witnesses at the July 2023 hearing of the House [[house-weaponization-committee|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]], which examined federal censorship of Americans and the [[missouri-v-biden|Missouri v. Biden]] litigation.[1] She appeared alongside [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], [[d-john-sauer|D. John Sauer]] and [[emma-jo-morris|Emma-Jo Morris]] at a session marked by contentious interruptions and by an attempt from House Democrats to remove Kennedy as a witness.[1]
