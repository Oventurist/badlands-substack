# Eye of the Storm

Eye of the Storm is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Tuesdays at 10:30pm ET as of January 2023, co-hosted by [[stormy-patriot-joe|Stormy Patriot Joe]] and [[absolute-1776|Absolute 1776]].[1] The network describes the pair as two of the biggest and most knowledgeable analysts of the Q operation, and the show analyzes ongoing events through the lens of what it calls the largest military intelligence information dissemination program ever launched.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Eye of the Storm on Tuesdays at 10:30pm ET, pairing Stormy Patriot Joe and Absolute1776 to analyze events through the lens of what the network called the largest military intelligence information dissemination program ever launched.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Eye of the Storm in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]

## Format and expanded schedule

Writing in December 2023, co-host [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] — Absolute1776 — described Eye Of The Storm as airing Tuesday evenings at 10:30pm and Friday evenings at 9:00pm, an expansion from the single weekly slot listed in the 2022–2023 schedules.[5] He characterized the show's purpose as breaking down the [[q-operation|Q]] operation and taking a high-level look at the many comms and decodes associated with it.[5] It was that high-level orientation that led him to launch the deliberately low-level [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] written series as a complement aimed at newcomers.[5]

## Coverage cited in Q For Dummies Chapter 13

The Badlands Media "Q For Dummies" series pointed readers to Eye Of The Storm for its treatment of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s trial, citing two clip episodes — one framed around a claimed Q proof and the recurrence of "booms," the other a decode of the week — in which [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] and [[stormy-patriot-joe|StormyPatriotJoe]] highlighted what they called coincidences surrounding the case.[6] The chapter also invoked the show as evidence for its wider claim that the [[qanon|Q]] drops are resolving into clarity, telling readers that followers of the program would be well aware of how precisely certain things were snapping into place.[6]

## Promotion in Q for Dummies Chapter 5

Chapter 5 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series, published February 2024, again promoted the program, telling readers to check out Eye Of The Storm "every Tuesday and Friday night on Badlands Media" for higher-level takes and digs into [[q-operation|Q]].[7] The author described the format as dissecting the drops from the very beginning alongside [[stormy-patriot-joe|StormyPatriotJoe]] while covering current news and comms as they relate to the drops and the Q operation.[7] In the same chapter he cited the show as the venue in which he had argued that [[james-comey|James Comey]] was working on behalf of the operation.[7]

## Six-year delta framing

Chapter 4 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series, published January 19, 2024, cites the program directly, with the author writing that listeners of Eye Of The Storm will know that he and [[joe-lange|Joe]] both lean heavily toward many of the [[q-operation|Q]] drops being six-year deltas, and that a great deal appeared to be lining up with that framing.[8] He applies the reading to drop #24 and the wave of congressional retirements announced ahead of the 2024 elections, treating them as a [[q-markers|"future proves past"]] case.[8]
