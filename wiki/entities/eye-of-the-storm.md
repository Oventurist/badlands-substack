# Eye of the Storm

Eye of the Storm is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Tuesdays at 10:30pm ET as of January 2023, co-hosted by [[stormy-patriot-joe|Stormy Patriot Joe]] and [[absolute-1776|Absolute 1776]].[1] The network describes the pair as two of the biggest and most knowledgeable analysts of the Q operation, and the show analyzes ongoing events through the lens of what it calls the largest military intelligence information dissemination program ever launched.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Eye of the Storm on Tuesdays at 10:30pm ET, pairing Stormy Patriot Joe and Absolute1776 to analyze events through the lens of what the network called the largest military intelligence information dissemination program ever launched.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Eye of the Storm in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
