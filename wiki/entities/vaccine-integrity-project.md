# Vaccine Integrity Project

The Vaccine Integrity Project is an initiative housed at the University of Minnesota that, in 2025, became a partner in efforts by vaccine industry groups to build a "nongovernmental vaccine system" outside federal oversight.[1]

## Private coalition to bypass ACIP

Pharmaceutical companies, pharmacists, and medical associations with longstanding ties to vaccine manufacturers including [[merck|Merck]], Sanofi, and [[pfizer|Pfizer]] joined a new private coalition aimed at bypassing the CDC's [[advisory-committee-on-immunization-practices|Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices]] (ACIP), according to Washington Post reporting summarized by Badlands Media.[1] The coalition — which also includes state health officials and a new advocacy group — is working with the Vaccine Integrity Project to develop the alternative system.[1]

The effort followed Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]'s dismissal of all sitting ACIP members over conflict-of-interest concerns.[1] The coalition's plan involves ordering vaccines directly from manufacturers and following recommendations issued by medical associations rather than ACIP.[1]
