# David Hogg

David Hogg is an American gun control activist and prominent progressive political figure.[1]

## Position on the New Mexico carry suspension

In September 2023 Hogg was cited, alongside Representative [[ted-lieu|Ted Lieu]], as one of the prominent leftists who affirmed that states do not possess the power to strike down the [[second-amendment|Second Amendment]] — a response to Governor [[michelle-lujan-grisham|Michelle Lujan Grisham]]'s emergency order suspending public firearms carry in Albuquerque.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized these statements as tactical rather than principled, arguing that such figures oppose states' rights generally and want gun restrictions enacted at the federal level via constitutional amendment, mirroring the establishment's insistence on federal legislation after abortion policy was returned to the states.[1]
