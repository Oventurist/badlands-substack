# Adnan Khashoggi

Adnan Khashoggi was a Saudi businessman and arms dealer whose career placed him at the intersection of Gulf finance, Western intelligence, and international arms brokerage. In the Badlands corpus he appears as a figure connecting the financial architecture of what contributors call the transnational cartel to the early career of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].

## Recruitment of Jeffrey Epstein

The February 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0a2|Badlands Brief]] cites Khashoggi in [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s argument against the conventional portrayal of Epstein as a financial nonentity. Challenging the claim that Epstein "is this moron who never earned the kingdom he was given," the author asks: "if that were true then why did Adnan Khashoggi recruit Epstein out of Bear Sterns to become his personal accountant and money-launderer?"

From this premise the author develops a set of speculative questions: whether Epstein was in fact a financial expert who also worked for the [[deep-state|Deep State]] as a money launderer and financial consultant; whether he understood the financial architecture of the transnational cartel in granular detail; and whether that expertise, rather than scandal, explains the reported 16-plus hours of recorded interviews between Epstein and [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]. The author presents these as questions and theories rather than established claims.
