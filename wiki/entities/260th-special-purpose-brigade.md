# 260th Special Purpose Brigade

The 260th Special Purpose Brigade is a unit of the [[district-of-columbia-national-guard|District of Columbia National Guard]], activated in March 2026 as the US Army's only brigade dedicated to coordinating military support to civil authorities and protecting critical infrastructure in the nation's capital.[1]

## Formation

The brigade was created following [[executive-order-14339|Executive Order 14339]] and a directive from the [[department-of-war|Department of War]] calling for a trained and equipped National Guard unit to support public safety and integrate with federal and local security partners.[1] Brig. Gen. [[craig-maceri|Craig Maceri]] said the brigade provides a command structure capable of managing a wide range of missions while ensuring rapid coordination with federal and local agencies during major events, emergencies, and security operations.[1]

## Lineage and insignia

The unit carries the lineage of the 260th Military Police Command, a DC National Guard brigade that coordinated military police operations until its inactivation in 2011.[1] Its new patch incorporates elements from the Flag of Washington, DC — three red stars and two red bars drawn from George Washington's coat of arms — with an upright sword symbolizing its role in protecting all who live, work, and visit the capital.[1]

## Partners

One of the brigade's primary partners is the [[us-marshals-service|US Marshals Service]], which regularly works alongside the Guard in the capital.[1] The brigade reflects the unique structure of the DC National Guard, which reports directly to the President of the United States.[1]
