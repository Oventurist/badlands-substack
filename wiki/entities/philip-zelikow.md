# Philip Zelikow

Philip Zelikow is an American academic and government official who headed the 9/11 Commission, described in Badlands Media's account as notoriously misleading, and characterized there as an unrelenting supporter of [[israel]].[1]

In 1998 — three years before the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] — Zelikow co-authored an article titled "Catastrophic Terrorism: Tackling the New Danger," which imagined a "Transformative Event" that "could involve loss of life and property unprecedented in peacetime and undermine America's fundamental sense of security as did the Soviet atomic bomb test in 1949," and predicted that "[t]he United States might respond with draconian measures, scaling back civil liberties, allowing wider surveillance of citizens, detention of suspects and use of deadly force."[1] The article is cited alongside the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|PNAC]] "new Pearl Harbor" language as evidence of foreknowledge or anticipation of the policy shift that became the [[war-on-terror|War on Terror]].[1]
