# Jennifer Wright

Jennifer Wright is an Arizona attorney who served as an assistant attorney general assigned to the Election Integrity Unit of the Arizona Attorney General's Office under [[mark-brnovich|Mark Brnovich]]. She is best known in the Badlands Media corpus as the author of a four-page letter dated November 19, 2022 to [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]], Civil Division Chief of the [[maricopa-county-attorneys-office|Maricopa County Attorney's Office]], demanding an accounting of the county's administration of the 2022 general election.

## The November 19, 2022 letter

Wright opened the letter by reporting that hundreds of citizen complaints, including first-hand witness accounts, had raised concerns "regarding Maricopa's lawful compliance with Arizona election law." The letter addressed three areas. First, ballot-on-demand printer configuration settings: the county had acknowledged that "at least 60 voting locations had issues related to some ballot-on-demand (BOD) printers having printer configuration settings that were non-uniform," yielding ballots unreadable by on-site tabulators, and Wright requested "a comprehensive report detailing with specificity" the conditions at each location, including the method by which settings were corrected and whether technicians were deployed or a network administrator made universal changes. Second, election-day "check-out" procedures, where redirected voters were shown as already checked in at their original site. Third, the handling of the [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3"]] ballots, where Wright wrote that the county "appears to have failed to adhere to the statutory guidelines in segregating, counting, tabulating, tallying, and transporting" them, cited the county's admission of commingling, and referenced a sworn observer complaint that over 1,700 non-tabulated Door 3 ballots from one location were placed in black duffle bags meant for tabulated ballots.

The letter closed by stating that "Arizonans deserve a full report and accounting of the myriad problems" and, because the issues bore on the county's ability to lawfully certify results, requested a response before the county submitted its canvass to the [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Secretary of State]] — required under A.R.S. § 16-642(A) to occur on or before November 28, 2022. The full letter was published on the Arizona Attorney General's website.

## Background and profile

To describe Wright, [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] quotes an August 22, 2019 [[talking-points-memo|Talking Points Memo]] article headlined "GOP Voter Fraud Hawk Hired For Arizona AG's New 'Elections Integrity' Unit," which described her as "a Tea Party-linked voter fraud activist known for her vigilante approach to poll-monitoring" and "a former GOP mayoral candidate who once worked for the voter fraud alarmist group [[verify-the-vote|Verify the Vote]]." The article noted that Verify the Vote partnered with the Houston-based [[true-the-vote|True the Vote]], itself an outgrowth of the Tea Party group King Street Patriots. Gillespie, treating this critical portrait as a recommendation, responds: "Sounds like our kind of gal!"
