# Vladimir Saldo

Vladimir Saldo is the Russian-installed head of the Kherson administration in occupied southern Ukraine.[1]

## Statements on the Kakhovka dam

After the [[kakhovka-dam-destruction|Nova Kakhovka dam]] was destroyed early on Tuesday, June 6, 2023, Russia denied responsibility and Saldo publicly accused Kyiv of carrying out the attack.[1] He said the destruction "led to a large, but not critical amount of water flowing down the Dnieper," adding that "it will not prevent our military from defending the left bank."[1] He characterized the attack as an attempt by Kyiv to "divert attention" from failed counteroffensives.[1] His account was echoed by the Russian-installed mayor of Nova Kakhovka, Vladimir Leontiev, who said "night attacks" on the dam had "led to the destruction of the valves" so that water from the Kakhovka reservoir "began to uncontrollably be discharged downstream."[1] Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] blamed "Russian terrorists" for the breach.[1]
