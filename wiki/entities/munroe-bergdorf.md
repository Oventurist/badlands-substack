# Munroe Bergdorf

Munroe Bergdorf is a British model, broadcaster and transgender activist.[1]

## UN Women UK appointment

In December 2023, [[un-women|UN Women]]'s UK committee appointed Bergdorf as its "UK champion," prompting seventeen women's rights groups to sign a letter to the charity expressing "dismay and disappointment" at the selection of a transgender woman to represent British women.[1] The campaign group Fair Play For Women described the appointee as "a male who presents in a highly sexualised stereotype of womanhood" and argued the committee's credibility was "in tatters."[1] The dispute was reported by *The Times* and carried by Modernity News, which framed the appointment as an act of provocation toward women.[1] The item appeared as a bonus entry in the Badlands News Brief of January 4, 2024.[1]
