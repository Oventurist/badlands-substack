# Oracle

Oracle is an American technology company cofounded by [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]]. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]], Oracle appears in two capacities: as the source of the Ellison family fortune behind [[david-ellison|David Ellison]]'s [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]] and its pending merger with [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]], and as a named partner in Project Stargate, the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's announced $500 billion investment in U.S. [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] infrastructure alongside OpenAI and SoftBank.

The article also alludes to the general claim that "everyone within the American tech space has some past connection to DARPA and/or the [[cia|CIA]]," while arguing that such historical ties do not determine present loyalties.
