# Oracle

Oracle is the American technology company cofounded by [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) In "A New Hollywood," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites Oracle as one of the corporate partners in [[project-stargate|Project Stargate]], [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s plan for the United States to invest $500 billion in artificial intelligence infrastructure alongside [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[softbank|SoftBank]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

Oracle's significance in the article is chiefly familial and political: Ellison's wealth and Trump alliance underwrite [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]], run by his son [[david-ellison|David Ellison]], which is merging with [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) Carlson also notes that Trump suggested Ellison — or [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] — buy [[tiktok|TikTok]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) He acknowledges that critics point to the connections between American tech firms and DARPA or the [[cia|CIA]], arguing such history does not establish present disloyalty.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

## Port Washington AI data center (2026)

Oracle was named alongside [[openai|OpenAI]] and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a backer of a proposed $15 billion, 1.3-gigawatt AI data center campus in [[port-washington|Port Washington]], Wisconsin, tied to the [[project-stargate|Stargate]] megaproject and targeted by a municipal ballot referendum in April 2026.[2] Oracle spokesman Michael Egbert responded to residents' concerns by saying, "We're paying our own way on energy so ratepayers' bills and electric grid reliability are not impacted by our data center," and pledged a $175 million investment in local infrastructure upgrades including water mains, sewer lines, a new water tower and power.[2] He said the company takes "similar approaches at all of our AI data center locations to deliver long-term value to the community."[2]


## Role in the 2025 TikTok restructuring

Under the [[tiktok-us-joint-venture-deal|TikTok US joint venture deal]] that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was expected to approve in late September 2025, Oracle would serve as the platform's trusted security partner, monitoring data storage and overseeing operations in cooperation with the US government.[3] All American user data would be housed in a dedicated Oracle-run cloud isolated from foreign access, and Oracle would be responsible for retraining and continuously monitoring the US version of TikTok's algorithm to prevent manipulation or surveillance.[3]
