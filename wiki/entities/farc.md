# FARC

The Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC) is the Colombian militia group described in the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026 in the context of the earlier career of prosecutor [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]].

Before his appointment as Special Inspector General for the [[troubled-asset-relief-program|Troubled Asset Relief Program]] and his later work as independent ombudsman at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]], Barofsky served as an assistant U.S. attorney in New York, where, per the source, he "prosecuted 50 leaders of the Colombian militia group FARC on narcotics charges." Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites this record as giving Barofsky "the unique experience of prosecuting Colombian cartel members on narcotics charges," relevant to the Brief's broader argument connecting the global banking system to cartel finance. The same edition separately reports Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]]'s White House discussions of potential guerrilla threats and counternarcotics cooperation.
