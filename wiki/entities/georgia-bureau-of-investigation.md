# Georgia Bureau of Investigation

The Georgia Bureau of Investigation (GBI) is the state-level investigative agency of Georgia. Its role in the aftermath of the 2020 presidential election became contested in February 2026 following the unsealing of Fulton County special grand jury transcripts.[1]

## The 2020 election investigation question

Prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] asked former Senator [[david-perdue|David Perdue]] before the special grand jury whether he accepted that investigations by the Secretary of State's Office, the GBI, and "numerous investigations" had established that nothing illegal occurred at State Farm Arena on election night 2020.[1] Perdue's careful answer — "I agree that an investigation happened, yes" — did not endorse the conclusion.[1]

Perdue further testified that Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]] instructed GBI Chief Vic Reynolds to inform Perdue that the state was not going to investigate the election.[1] Badlands commentary drew the direct implication: "Every official that told the American people that the GBI investigated was lying."[1]

## Atlanta training center raid (January 2023)

On the morning of Wednesday, January 18, 2023, the Georgia Bureau of Investigation and other law enforcement agencies conducted a multi-agency raid on the so-called autonomous zone occupied by [[antifa|Antifa]] and other far-left activists at the site of the future Atlanta Public Safety Training Center south of Atlanta.[2] The GBI said a Georgia State Patrol trooper was shot and severely injured during the raid by a man camped in the area; officers returned fire and killed the gunman, who allegedly used a pistol.[2] Seven militants, all from out of state, were arrested and charged with domestic terrorism.[2] See [[stop-cop-city|Stop Cop City]].[2]
