# Melissa Hortman

Melissa Hortman was a Minnesota state legislator and Speaker of the Minnesota House of Representatives who was shot and killed at her home in Brooklyn Park, Minnesota, in the early hours of Saturday, June 14, 2025, in what authorities described as a targeted attack.[1] Her husband, Mark Hortman, was killed in the same attack.[1]

## Killing

Hortman and her husband were fatally shot at their Brooklyn Park residence in the Minneapolis metropolitan area, according to Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]], who characterized the shootings as "politically motivated" in a news briefing on the day of the attack.[1] Investigators reported that the shooter had been dressed as a police officer, a detail that shaped early speculation about planning and premeditation.[1] The attack on the Hortmans occurred alongside a second shooting the same morning that wounded State Senator [[john-hoffman|John Hoffman]] and his wife, Yvette, at their home in the nearby suburb of Champlin.[1]

Authorities identified 57-year-old [[vance-boelter|Vance Luther Boelter]] as the suspect sought in connection with the shootings; he was captured in Sibley County, Minnesota, on the evening of Sunday, June 15, 2025.[1] Investigators said they were examining whether the suspect had known Hoffman or Hortman personally, noting that Boelter had served on a state board with Hoffman for more than six years.[1] A stack of "No Kings" flyers was recovered from a vehicle connected to the suspect, according to the Minnesota State Patrol, linking the case in press coverage to the [[no-kings-protests|"No Kings" protests]] held nationwide that same Saturday; statewide "No Kings" events in Minnesota were subsequently canceled.[1]

## Commentary and reception

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary on the killings, published in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], offered condolences to the Hortman and Hoffman families while criticizing what it described as the immediate partisan attribution of the assassin to one political party or another, arguing that "political parties are fake" and that both major parties were "stoking the same fire."[1] The commentary also amplified social-media speculation questioning Boelter's background and possible institutional connections.[1]

## Aftermath and target list disclosures
[[vance-boelter|Vance Luther Boelter]], 57, accused of stalking and killing Hortman and her husband Mark and of shooting State Senator [[john-hoffman|John Hoffman]] and his wife Yvette, was arrested late on Sunday, June 15, 2025 after the largest manhunt in Minnesota history.[2] U.S. Representative [[veronica-escobar|Veronica Escobar]] said the [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] notified her that morning that her own name appeared on the shooter's target list, and she condemned political violence in a statement the next day.[2]
