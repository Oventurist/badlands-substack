# Henry Drummond

Henry Drummond was a British financier and co-founder of the New Apostolic Church, described in the article as one of three leaders — alongside Lord Anthony Ashley Cooper, 7th Earl of Shaftesbury, and [[john-nelson-darby]] — of a tightly knit network of Anglican and Jesuit intelligence operatives from which the [[plymouth-brethren]] emerged.[1]

## The Albury Conferences

Drummond oversaw the [[albury-conferences]] on prophecy, held between 1826 and 1830 at a vast estate he purchased in Surrey, England, featuring 70 bedrooms.[1] They drew leading figures of what the article calls London's gnostic intelligentsia, including the occultists Robert Haldane and Sir Thomas Carlyle, both of whom went on to become numbered among the twelve "apostles/prophets" of the New Apostolic Catholic Church created by Drummond and George Irving in 1830.[1] Lord Anthony Ashley Cooper was a follower of Drummond and devoted himself to "Premillennial Dispensationalism" soon after the conferences.[1]

## British Israelism

Drummond was a [[british-israelism|British Israelite]]. In 1834 he stated that "the majority of what was called the religious world, disbelieved that the Jews were to be restored to their own land, and that the Lord Jesus Christ was to return and reign in person on this earth."[1]
