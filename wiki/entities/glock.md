# Glock

Glock is a firearms manufacturer whose semiautomatic pistols are among the most widely distributed handguns in the United States, and which by 2024 had become a target of coordinated litigation and pressure from Democratic state law-enforcement officials.[1]

## 2024 attorneys general letter

On March 26, 2024, thirteen Democrat attorneys general sent a letter to Glock expressing concern over what they termed "machine-gun Glocks."[1] The signatories represented Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, New Jersey, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Vermont, Washington, and Washington, D.C.[1] The letter referenced a lawsuit brought by the city of Chicago against the company earlier that month, in which the city claimed Glock was liable for the ease with which a so-called "Glock switch" can convert its semiautomatic handguns to fully automatic fire.[1]

The Chicago suit pointed to China as one source of "auto sears" — the central component of a Glock switch — though it did not explain the extent to which China was allegedly committed to saturating the United States with such devices.[1]

## Document preservation demands

The attorneys general demanded that Glock preserve all documents relating to the conversion of its semiautomatic handguns into automatic weapons; the design and development of Glock pistols with respect to their semiautomatic function; the company's knowledge of state and federal laws concerning Glock switches and converted Glock machine guns; financial information relating to Glock pistols, including profit; and any public-facing marketing or advertising related to Glock pistols, among other categories.[1]

Coverage of the letter appeared in [[breitbart-news|Breitbart News]] and was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of March 27, 2024.[1]
