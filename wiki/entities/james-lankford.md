# James Lankford

James Lankford is a Republican U.S. Senator from Oklahoma and co-chair of the Senate's bipartisan antisemitism task force.[1]

## Antisemitism legislation

In May 2026, Lankford and Sen. [[jacky-rosen|Jacky Rosen]] (D-Nev.) unveiled a sweeping bipartisan bill aimed at combating rising antisemitism through new federal requirements in education, online platforms, and community security funding.[1] The measure had been more than a year in development and was backed by groups including the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]] and the American Jewish Committee.[1] Sponsors hoped to advance the package through vehicles such as the annual defense authorization bill, with a House companion bill expected soon.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the Lankford–Rosen bill as "the Trojan Horse that will dismantle the First Amendment," predicting it would draw the widest bipartisan support of any measure since the Patriot Act and citing that breadth as evidence of foreign influence over [[congress|Congress]].[1]

## Face of the 2024 border negotiations

In early 2024, Senate Minority Leader [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] assigned Lankford the task of serving as the public face of Republican negotiations on the [[bipartisan-border-bill-2024|bipartisan Senate border bill]].[2] Majority Leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] nonetheless made clear that McConnell called the shots for Republicans on the package, saying he had never worked more closely with the minority leader on any piece of legislation.[2] The bill drew fierce criticism from Republicans including Senator [[mike-lee|Mike Lee]], who called it a "disqualifying betrayal," and it was declared dead on arrival in the House by Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]].[2]
