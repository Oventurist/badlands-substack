# James Lankford

James Lankford is a Republican United States Senator from Oklahoma. In the Badlands corpus he is known chiefly as co-author, with Democratic Senator [[jacky-rosen|Jacky Rosen]] of Nevada, of a sweeping bipartisan antisemitism bill unveiled in May 2026.

## Antisemitism legislation

As reported in the May 20, 2026 Badlands Brief, Lankford and Rosen introduced the [[lankford-rosen-antisemitism-bill|antisemitism package]] on a Tuesday after more than a year of development. The measure would create new federal requirements across education, online platforms, and community security funding, including an antisemitism coordinator at the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]], regulations on federally funded colleges, $1 billion in nonprofit security grants for Jewish communities, and disclosure requirements for how online platforms moderate antisemitic content. Lankford co-chairs the Senate's bipartisan antisemitism task force with Rosen.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the bill as a "Trojan Horse" aimed at dismantling the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] and predicted it would attract the widest bipartisan support of any measure since the Patriot Act, treating that expected support as evidence of the [[israel-lobby|Israel lobby]]'s influence over [[congress|Congress]].
