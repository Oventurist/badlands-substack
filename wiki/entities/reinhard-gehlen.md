# Reinhard Gehlen

Reinhard Gehlen was a former Nazi intelligence officer who ran the Gehlen Organization, an intelligence agency established in June 1946 by US and British authorities to operate in post-war Germany under the supervision of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]].[1]

## The Gehlen Organization and Nazi recruitment

According to Badlands' account, after the Second World War [[stepan-bandera]] was contacted by British intelligence and recruited into the Gehlen Organization.[1] Gehlen and the CIA employed many former Nazis and other war criminals, including Bandera, and used them for [[operation-gladio]] missions over many years.[1]

The Badlands commentary characterizes this recruitment as common practice rather than an exception, asserting that half of the founding members of the CIA were former Nazi intelligence officers and making the same claim about [[nato]], NASA, the United Nations and other institutions it describes as globalist.[1] The article links to a CIA Studies in Intelligence document on Nazi-related files in support of the supervision claim.[1]
