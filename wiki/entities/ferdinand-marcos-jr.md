# Ferdinand Marcos Jr.

Ferdinand Marcos Jr. is the President of the Philippines. At a summit in Tokyo on May 28, 2026, he and Japanese Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]] announced plans to begin maritime delimitation talks concerning their two countries' exclusive economic zones and continental shelves.[1]

The initiative drew immediate objections. Chinese Foreign Ministry spokeswoman [[mao-ning|Mao Ning]] said the waters in question lie east of [[taiwan|Taiwan]] and that any delimitation negotiations there must involve China.[1] Taiwan also objected, saying the area overlaps with waters in which it claims rights and interests, and urged Tokyo and Manila to consult Taipei.[1] Japanese Chief Cabinet Secretary [[minoru-kihara|Minoru Kihara]] said any Tokyo–Manila agreement would not be legally binding on third parties.[1]

Shortly afterward China launched a "special maritime law enforcement operation" east of Taiwan, part of ongoing disputes involving China, Taiwan, Japan, and the Philippines over maritime claims and jurisdiction.[1]
