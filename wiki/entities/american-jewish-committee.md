# American Jewish Committee

The American Jewish Committee (AJC) is a Jewish advocacy organization that hosted the event underway at the [[capital-jewish-museum|Capital Jewish Museum]] in Washington, D.C. on the night of May 21, 2025, when two Israeli embassy staff members were shot and killed outside.[1]

The AJC event was described as a gathering for young Jewish professionals who work in foreign policy, and included a panel discussion focused on finding humanitarian solutions for Gaza.[1] The two victims were shot "at close range" as they were leaving the event; a 30-year-old Chicago man, [[elias-rodriguez|Elias Rodriguez]], was taken into custody.[1]

Badlands commentary by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] flagged the AJC panel's Gaza humanitarian focus as one of the details that made the incident feel to him like a possible false flag operation.[1]

## Progressive Jewish Thought and the New Anti-Semitism (2007)

In early 2007 the committee published [[progressive-jewish-thought-and-the-new-antisemitism|*Progressive Jewish Thought and the New Anti-Semitism*]], a paper by Indiana University English professor [[alvin-h-rosenfeld|Alvin H. Rosenfeld]] that characterized dissenting progressive American Jews — including Tony Judt, Tony Kushner and Richard Cohen — as standing alongside a new antisemitism denying the legitimacy of Israel's existence.[2] David Harris, then the committee's executive director, wrote in the introduction that "the most surprising—and distressing—feature of this new trend is the very public participation of some Jews in the verbal onslaught against Zionism and the Jewish state."[2]

The Badlands essay "Our 'Greatest Ally'" treats the publication as a high-water mark in the use of the [[antisemitism|antisemitism]] charge against critics of Israeli policy, noting that those named refuted the allegations and that Rabbi [[michael-lerner|Michael Lerner]] warned of the chilling effect such accusations have on members of Congress.[2]
