# American Jewish Committee

The American Jewish Committee (AJC) is a United States advocacy organization focused on Jewish communal affairs, antisemitism, and support for Israel.

## Support for the 2026 antisemitism bill

According to the May 20, 2026 Badlands Brief, the AJC was among the organizations backing the [[lankford-rosen-antisemitism-bill|bipartisan antisemitism package]] unveiled by Senators [[james-lankford|James Lankford]] and [[jacky-rosen|Jacky Rosen]], alongside the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]]. The bill, more than a year in development, would create federal requirements in education, online content moderation disclosure, and community security funding, including $1 billion in nonprofit security grants for Jewish communities. Badlands commentary treated the organized advocacy behind the measure as an illustration of the [[israel-lobby|Israel lobby]]'s influence over [[congress|Congress]].
