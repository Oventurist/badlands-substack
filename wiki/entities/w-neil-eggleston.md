# W. Neil Eggleston

W. Neil Eggleston served as White House Counsel to President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and is a central figure in a 2016 meeting timeline highlighted by Badlands Media.[1]

## The 2016 meetings

During the FBI's "Midyear Exam" investigation, Eggleston held two meetings with [[fbi|FBI]] officials — including Deputy Director [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]] and Deputy Attorney General [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]] — allegedly to discuss the contents of thumb drives connected to the probe. The meetings took place on September 20, 2016 and October 20, 2016.[1]

Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] noted that significant developments followed each meeting. On September 21, 2016, at the direction of [[fusion-gps|Fusion GPS]], [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]] briefed the New York Times, Washington Post, New Yorker, Yahoo! News and CNN; two days later Michael Isikoff published the first known article based on the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]].[1] On October 21, 2016, the FBI submitted its initial Carter Page FISA application, which the court granted the same day.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands narrative

Herold framed the open question as whether Obama's White House Counsel was effectively working with the Bureau, drawing an analogy to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton's]] use of the law firm [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]] to conduct politically sensitive work at arm's length.[1] The Eggleston meetings are presented as part of the causal sequence that Badlands commentators believe could anchor a racketeering theory of the [[russiagate-hoax|Russia collusion narrative]].[1]
