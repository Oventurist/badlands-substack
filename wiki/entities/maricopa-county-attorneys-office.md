# Maricopa County Attorney's Office

The Maricopa County Attorney's Office is the prosecuting and civil legal office for [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona. Its Civil Division represents county government, including the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Board of Supervisors]] and the county's election operation, in litigation and in correspondence over election administration.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

During the aftermath of the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]], the office's Civil Division Chief, [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]], became the county's public-facing legal voice. A volunteer for gubernatorial candidate [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] received a reply from Liddy that the Kari Lake War Room publicized on November 18, 2022 with the comment, "We've asked Maricopa County for answers. This is what we get in return."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) The exchange drew wide attention, and the episode was covered in national press reporting on accusations that Lake's RNC-affiliated lawyer had threatened a Republican county attorney.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

Liddy was also the recipient of the November 19, 2022 letter from Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] of the [[arizona-attorney-generals-office|Arizona Attorney General's Office]], which demanded a full accounting of the county's [[ballot-on-demand-printers|ballot-on-demand printer]] failures, its Election Day check-out procedures, and its handling of [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3" ballots]] before the county certified its results.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
