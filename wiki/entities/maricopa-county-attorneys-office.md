# Maricopa County Attorney's Office

The Maricopa County Attorney's Office is the prosecuting and civil legal authority for [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona. Its Civil Division represents the county and its officials in non-criminal matters, including election administration disputes.

## Role in the 2022 election dispute

During and after the November 2022 general election the Civil Division was headed by [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]]. The office was the recipient of the November 19, 2022 letter from Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] of the Arizona Attorney General's Election Integrity Unit, which relayed hundreds of citizen complaints about the county's compliance with Arizona election law and demanded detailed documentation on ballot-on-demand printer failures, election-day check-out procedures, and the handling of [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3"]] ballots before the county's canvass was submitted to the [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Secretary of State]].

The office also fielded inquiries from campaign representatives. [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]] cites a November 18, 2022 post by the Kari Lake War Room account — "We've asked Maricopa County for answers. This is what we get in return" — publicizing Liddy's reply to a [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] volunteer, an exchange that drew national coverage and accusations that Lake's side had been threatened or, conversely, that Lake's lawyer had threatened Liddy.
