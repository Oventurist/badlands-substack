# Kevin Diana

Kevin Diana is a US government human-resources official who served as acting chief human resources officer at the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]].[1] In early 2026 he received transfer orders as part of a broader wave of leadership changes at the agency.[1]

Those changes included the departure of chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]], who also received transfer orders before announcing his exit, and the naming of [[nick-andersen|Nick Andersen]] as acting director in place of [[madhu-gottumukkala|Madhu Gottumukkala]].[1]
