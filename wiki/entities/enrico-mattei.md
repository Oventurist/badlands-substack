# Enrico Mattei

Enrico Mattei was an Italian energy figure listed by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] among the pro-development leaders whose elimination between 1963 and 1968 created the political vacuum filled by the [[malthusianism|Malthusian]] revival of 1968–1972.[1]

## Context

Ehret groups Mattei with [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], [[charles-de-gaulle|Charles de Gaulle]], [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Bobby Kennedy]], arguing that after their removal establishment "hacks and cowards" ushered in a paradigm shift toward conservationism and rejected the industrial growth ethic that had defined western civilization up to that point.[1] The same passage frames this shift as the answer to [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]]'s 1946 call for world government and for making eugenic thinking "thinkable" again, and as the precondition for the founding of the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]] in 1968.[1] Ehret later refers to the "great green game" as having been created in the wake of the assassinations of anti-Malthusian leaders in the 1960s.[1]
