# Los Zetas

Los Zetas is a Mexican criminal organization founded by Miguel and Omar Morales, known respectively as Z-40 and Z-42.[1]

## 2025 extraditions

In February 2025, the two founders were among dozens of high-level cartel figures whom [[mexico|Mexico]] moved to extradite to the United States, alongside Guadalajara cartel figure [[rafael-caro-quintero|Rafael Caro Quintero]].[1] Mexican authorities said 29 people held in penitentiary centers were transferred to the United States because of their links to drug-trafficking organizations, at the request of the U.S. [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and in accordance with Mexico's Constitution and National Security Law.[1] The transfers occurred as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariff deadline for Mexico loomed, with cartel and fentanyl enforcement among his stated demands.[1]
