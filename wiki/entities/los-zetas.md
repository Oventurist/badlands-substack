# Los Zetas

Los Zetas is a Mexican criminal organization founded by Miguel and Omar Morales, known respectively as Z-40 and Z-42.[1]

## 2025 extraditions

In February 2025, the two founders were among dozens of high-level cartel figures whom [[mexico|Mexico]] moved to extradite to the United States, alongside Guadalajara cartel figure [[rafael-caro-quintero|Rafael Caro Quintero]].[1] Mexican authorities said 29 people held in penitentiary centers were transferred to the United States because of their links to drug-trafficking organizations, at the request of the U.S. [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and in accordance with Mexico's Constitution and National Security Law.[1] The transfers occurred as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariff deadline for Mexico loomed, with cartel and fentanyl enforcement among his stated demands.[1]


## Alleged CIA origins

"Profiting From War" asserts that the Zetas cartel was founded by Mexican special forces trained by [[central-intelligence-agency|the CIA]], and treats this as no coincidence.[3] Citing [[abc-news|ABC News]], the article notes that during [[operation-fast-and-furious|Operation Fast and Furious]] the northern Mexican states served as a battlefield between the Sinaloa and Juárez trafficking organizations and as expansion territory for the increasingly powerful Zetas, with nearly 175 weapons from the operation arming warring factions between October 2009 and the end of 2010.[3] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that drug trafficking is a valuable money flow to the CIA, [[hezbollah]] and [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], and that Fast and Furious armed agency-controlled cartels rather than exposing their leaders.[3]

## Links to Hezbollah's laundering network

"Panic in DC" describes Los Zetas as a partner of the [[ayman-joumaa|Ayman Joumaa]] network, the [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]]-linked organization investigated under [[project-cassandra|Project Cassandra]].[2] Politico reporting quoted in the article states that Joumaa's operation rang alarm bells in Washington when agents discovered he was working with the cartel to move multi-ton loads of cocaine directly into the United States, while washing roughly $200 million a month through some 300 used-car dealerships whose vehicles were shipped to Benin in West Africa.[2] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] characterizes Los Zetas as a deadly Mexican cartel and flags it for further treatment in a subsequent article.[2]
