# Timothy Courchaine

Timothy Courchaine is the United States Attorney for the District of Arizona.[1]

## Prosecution of the Arivaca border shooting

Courchaine's office announced charges against [[patrick-gary-schlegel|Patrick Gary Schlegel]], the Arizona man shot by a [[us-border-patrol|Border Patrol]] agent near Arivaca on January 27, 2026, after allegedly firing at agents and at a [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] helicopter.[1] Prosecutors said the anticipated charges included assault on a federal officer, human smuggling, and being a felon in possession of a firearm.[1]

In a public statement, Courchaine said: "There is no ambiguity here, shooting at any law enforcement officer—including federal agents—is a violent felony that attacks the rule of law and undermines universal American values. We will pursue every available charge to ensure the subject is held fully accountable under federal law."[1] He also credited interagency cooperation, adding that "the quick and coordinated reaction to the threat posed by this subject from all levels of Arizona law enforcement was key and demonstrates why local and federal cooperation keeps communities safer."[1]
