# Yunus-Bek Yevkurov

Yunus-Bek Yevkurov is a Russian Deputy Defence Minister who has served as Moscow's principal military envoy to the governments of the Sahel.[1] In late August and early September 2023 he led a Russian delegation to [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]], where he met the country's military leader, [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]], to discuss possible military cooperation.[1]

According to a communiqué from the Burkinabè presidency in Ouagadougou, the talks followed earlier discussions between Traoré and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] on the sidelines of the Russia-Africa summit held in St. Petersburg in July 2023, and covered "areas of cooperation that mainly concern the military sphere, including the training of Burkinabe cadet officers and officers at all levels, including pilots in Russia."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the delegation, described as headlined by the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]], had visited the Malian capital Bamako before Ouagadougou and then travelled to Bangui in the [[central-african-republic|Central African Republic]], forming part of a sequence of engagements that extended Russian influence in a land bridge across Africa from the Indian Ocean to the Atlantic.[1]
