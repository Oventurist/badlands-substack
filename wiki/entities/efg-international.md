# EFG International

EFG International is a Swiss private banking and asset-management group named among the financial institutions entangled in the corruption case surrounding Venezuela's state oil company, [[pdvsa|PDVSA]].[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], modelling how renewed litigation might unfold after Venezuela sanctions are lifted, reported an assessment that the private banks involved — the [[julius-baer|Julius Baer]] Group, EFG International, and [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] — were almost certain to be the first parties to cooperate with investigators, along with their compliance officers and internal auditors.[1] That expectation rests on the pattern established by [[matthias-krull|Matthias Krull]], whose cooperation in exchange for a drastically reduced sentence unlocked the broader [[operation-money-flight|Operation Money Flight]] case.[1]
