# Rima Hassan

Rima Hassan is a French Member of the European Parliament who was aboard the Gaza-bound civilian aid boat Madleen when Israeli naval forces intercepted it in international waters in June 2025.[1] She was detained along with climate activist [[greta-thunberg|Greta Thunberg]], Irish actor [[liam-cunningham|Liam Cunningham]] and the rest of the crew, and was towed with the vessel to the Israeli port of Ashdod before Israel's Foreign Ministry announced that the passengers were "expected to return to their home countries."[1] See [[madleen-flotilla-interception|Madleen flotilla interception]].[1]
