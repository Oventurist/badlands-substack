# Comedy Central

Comedy Central is an American cable television network best known for satirical programming including [[the-daily-show|The Daily Show]].[1]

## July 2024 programming pre-emption

In the immediate aftermath of the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination]] of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Butler, Pennsylvania, Comedy Central cancelled the Monday, July 15, 2024 airing of The Daily Show, a decision reported alongside [[msnbc|MSNBC]]'s cancellation of [[morning-joe|Morning Joe]] the same day.[1] The shooting left one rally attendee dead and two critically injured, and gunman [[thomas-matthew-crooks|Thomas Matthew Crooks]] was killed by [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] snipers.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the pre-emptions as evidence of a broader collapse in narrative coordination across the media, arguing that outlets which should have been "firming up their narrative positions" instead retreated into "a series of rhetorical bunkers."[1]
