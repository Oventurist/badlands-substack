# Omnicare

Omnicare is a long-term care pharmacy services provider founded in 1981 and acquired by [[cvs-health|CVS Health]] in August 2015 in a $12.7 billion transaction.[1] In July 2025 the unit became the subject of one of the largest pharmacy fraud judgments on record when a federal judge ordered it to pay $948.8 million in penalties and damages.[1]

## Fraudulent billing case

The federal lawsuit alleged that Omnicare fraudulently billed the government for invalid drug prescriptions dispensed to residents of long-term care facilities.[1] US District Judge [[colleen-mcmahon|Colleen McMahon]] of the Southern District of New York imposed a $542 million penalty for the filing of more than 3.3 million false claims over an eight-year period beginning in 2010, with additional damages bringing the total award to roughly $949 million.[1]

## Reaction

Badlands commentary on the ruling emphasized the absence of individual criminal liability, arguing that a monetary penalty against the corporate parent was an inadequate response to conduct that defrauded taxpayers of close to $1 billion.[1]
