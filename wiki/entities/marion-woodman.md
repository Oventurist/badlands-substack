# Marion Woodman

**Marion Woodman** is a psychologist quoted in the April 19, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Remembering: Fractured Selves and the Path Beyond Ego" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites her line, "The pain was necessary to know the truth, but we don't have to keep the pain alive to keep the truth alive."[1] It appears in the article's "Inner Reunion" section, where the author uses it to distinguish what integration preserves from what it releases: "what we keep alive through integration is not the wound, but the wisdom it gave us" — the ability to feel, to recognise others in their pain, and to be moved by the real.[1] The passage supports the essay's broader claim that inner reunion radiates outward, so that "as you become more whole, the world feels more whole in your presence."[1] See [[psychological-integration|psychological integration]].
