# Daniel Goodwyn

Daniel Goodwyn is a Texas resident and January 6 defendant whose fundraising became the leading example in 2023 reporting on Justice Department efforts to recover donations made to Capitol riot defendants.[1]

## Forfeiture effort

According to Associated Press reporting summarized by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] sought to seize more than $25,000 raised by Goodwyn, who had appeared on [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s former [[fox-news|Fox News]] show and promoted a website soliciting political donations.[1] The AP reviewed over 1,000 January 6 criminal cases and found that prosecutors had been asking judges to enhance fines on top of prison sentences in order to offset donations from supporters.[1]

Prosecutors acknowledged there was nothing wrong with defendants asking for help with attorney expenses, but questioned in some cases where the money was actually going, given that many of those charged had government-funded legal representation.[1] Most such fundraising appeared on [[givesendgo|GiveSendGo]], after defendants were barred from mainstream platforms including GoFundMe.[1]
