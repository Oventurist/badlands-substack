# Mongolia

Mongolia is a landlocked Central Asian state situated between Russia and China, and the historical heartland of the Mongol Empire founded by [[genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]].[1] In September 2024 it became the focus of geopolitical attention when Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] visited the country and publicly described it as a friendly nation and a decades-old ally of Russia.[1]

## Putin's September 2024 visit

Speaking at a meeting with Mongolian Parliament Speaker Dashzegviin Amarbayasgalan, Putin said he was "very pleased to be in Mongolia, which is friendly to us," and framed the trip around commemorating the shared victory at the Khalkhin-Gol River.[1] He characterised Mongolia as having proven "a very loyal, reliable ally of the Soviet Union and Russia" in the fight against Nazism and militarism, and said that relations between the two countries, maintained closely for decades, were "developing successfully."[1] Putin also stressed that Moscow attached great importance to inter-parliamentary interaction, calling it a significant contribution to contacts between executive authorities.[1] The remarks were reported by the Russian state news agency TASS.[1]

## Historical significance

Badlands Media commentary situated the visit within a much longer historical arc. Genghis Khan, who lived from roughly 1162 to 1227, conquered China and established the Yuan Dynasty, and dispatched his eldest son Jochi to conquer the Selenga River Valley, the borderland between modern Russia and Mongolia.[1] Jochi's son Batu subsequently founded the Golden Horde, whose siege of Kiev in 1240 ended the Kievan Rus.[1] While Kiev took centuries to recover from the Mongol siege, Moscow fared better: Muscovite princes curried favour with the Mongols and became intermediaries between the Mongols and the Rus nobility, and the Golden Horde thereby helped establish Moscow as the centre of influence in the emerging Russian state.[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

Writing for the Badlands News Brief, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the Putin–Mongolia meeting as "yet another major milestone in the War of Sovereignty," arguing that history was being revived and cultures were healing from the sins of the past as humanity forged new bonds against what he called the most ancient of evils.[1] He praised the pageantry of the welcome given to Putin in the Mongolian capital, writing that "the spirit of Genghis Khan lives."[1] Elsewhere in the same brief, the visit was folded into the "Trumpamania 2024" framing, with the author quipping that Genghis Khan was "coming to Trumpamania."[1]
