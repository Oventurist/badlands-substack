# Verify the Vote

Verify the Vote is an Arizona election-integrity group described in press coverage as a partner of the Houston-based [[true-the-vote|True the Vote]] and characterized by critics as a "voter fraud alarmist" organization. Like its larger affiliate, it grew out of Tea Party–era poll-watching activism.

## Role in the corpus

The group is mentioned in [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]] through a quoted [[talking-points-memo|Talking Points Memo]] article of August 22, 2019 headlined "GOP Voter Fraud Hawk Hired For Arizona AG's New 'Elections Integrity' Unit." That article reported that [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] — "a former GOP mayoral candidate who once worked for the voter fraud alarmist group Verify the Vote" — had been hired as an assistant attorney general focused on election integrity, as first reported by KPNX, the NBC affiliate in Phoenix.

[[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] reproduces this description not as an indictment but as a credential, framing Wright's activist background as the reason she could be trusted to press [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] for answers after the 2022 general election.
