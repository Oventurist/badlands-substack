# Verify the Vote

Verify the Vote is a voter-fraud watchdog group described in press accounts as a "voter fraud alarmist" organization and as a partner of the larger Houston-based group [[true-the-vote|True the Vote]], which itself sprang from the Tea Party organization King Street Patriots.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

The group is best known in Arizona through [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]], a former Republican mayoral candidate who worked for Verify the Vote before being hired in 2019 as an assistant attorney general assigned to election integrity in the [[arizona-attorney-generals-office|Arizona Attorney General's Office]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) A *Talking Points Memo* article of August 22, 2019 characterized Wright as a Tea Party-linked activist known for a "vigilante approach to poll-monitoring," reporting first published by KPNX, the NBC affiliate in Phoenix.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] cited that background as a point in Wright's favor when Wright challenged [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]]'s administration of the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
