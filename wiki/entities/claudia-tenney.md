# Claudia Tenney

Claudia Tenney is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from New York who sits on the [[house-ways-and-means-committee|House Ways and Means Committee]] and its Oversight Subcommittee, and who founded the House Election Integrity Caucus.[1]

## Investigation into nonprofits and election activity

In August 2023 Tenney announced that the Ways and Means Committee was opening a probe into whether liberal activists were misusing tax-exempt nonprofits to mobilize Democratic voters in battleground states, whether foreign money was being injected into American elections, and whether campaign finance laws were being evaded.[1] Speaking on the Just the News, No Noise television program, she said, "We're going to be investigating that as a member of the House Ways and Means Committee and the Oversight Subcommittee."[1]

Tenney argued that Democrats were substantially ahead of Republicans in running campaigns encouraging early and absentee voting, and that lawmakers were concerned foreign money might be seeping into that process.[1] She characterised her concern as "vote trafficking" — the use of ostensibly neutral registration infrastructure to drive only Democrat-supporting voters to the polls, funded by large liberal donors or foreign interests with a stake in electing Democrats.[1] Under [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] rules, 501(c)(3) organisations may conduct voter registration and get-out-the-vote drives only if those activities are carried out in a neutral, non-partisan manner without reference to any candidate or party.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that grassroots election investigators had already answered Tenney's question in the affirmative, and expressed "zero faith" that House investigators would deliver meaningful accountability.[1] Her principal criticism of the framing was that it treated the practice as Democrat-specific, when in her view the [[uniparty|uniparty]] establishment as a whole uses nonprofits to inflate voter rolls and to power what she called the lawfare machine — citing [[mi-familia-vota|Mi Familia Vota]], a voter registration nonprofit simultaneously suing in multiple states to keep [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] off the ballot.[1]

## Questions about the Chinese balloon

In February 2023 Tenney publicly questioned whether the [[chinese-spy-balloon-incident-2023|Chinese spy balloon]] shot down off the coast of South Carolina was a decoy for some other activity by the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]].[2] Interviewed on the Just the News, No Noise program on February 13, she said, "You have a president that is now getting inundated with these types of bizarre crossings," and asked, "are they surveillance? Probably. Do we know? No. But we should. The other thing is, are these decoys? Is there something else going on that we should know about that China is doing and the Chinese Communist Party is involved?"[2] She also noted that China had taken other steps to undermine US security.[2] Her remarks followed the shootdown of a suspicious object over Lake Huron and three earlier objects believed to be surveillance balloons downed within a single week.[2]

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that Tenney's willingness to question the official balloon narrative signalled a "new normal," in which scepticism previously confined to those dismissed as conspiracy theorists was being voiced by sitting members of Congress.[2]
