# Harriet Hageman

Harriet Hageman is a Republican politician from Wyoming who represents the state's at-large congressional district. She entered national prominence as the pro-Trump challenger who defeated incumbent Representative [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] in the August 2022 Republican primary for Wyoming's at-large seat, ending Cheney's congressional career after Cheney's role on the House Select Committee investigating January 6.[1]

Her primary victory has been widely cited as a demonstration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s continuing influence over Republican primary electorates, and Badlands Media commentary has invoked the race as context when describing Cheney's subsequent political marginalization — including Cheney's receipt of a preemptive pardon from [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] on Inauguration Day 2025 and her attacks on Trump's inaugural address.[1]
