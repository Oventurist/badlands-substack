# Signal Foundation

The **Signal Foundation** is the nonprofit organization that governs the [[signal|Signal]] encrypted messaging application.[1] Its board is chaired by [[katherine-maher|Katherine Maher]], who simultaneously serves as chief executive of [[npr|NPR]] and whose prior career included work for the National Democratic Institute, UNICEF, the World Bank, Access Now, the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]], and the U.S. State Department Foreign Policy Affairs Board.[1]

## Role in the Signalgate controversy

The foundation's governance drew attention during the [[signalgate|Signalgate]] affair, in which Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] used Signal to transmit updates about U.S. military strikes in Yemen.[1] Badlands host [[canncon|CannCon]] argued that Maher's chairmanship, combined with the possibility that the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] had recommended Signal to officials during the Biden administration shortly before President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took office, raised questions about whether the platform's adoption inside government was organic.[1]
