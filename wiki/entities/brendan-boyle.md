# Brendan Boyle

Brendan Boyle is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Pennsylvania.[1]

## Position on the overtime tax deduction

Amid Democratic division over how to respond to the popularity of the [[overtime-pay-tax-deduction|overtime pay tax deduction]] enacted in the Republican 2025 tax cuts, Boyle was among the members who voiced support for extending or enhancing the break in order to ease burdens on wage workers.[1] His stance contrasted with skeptics such as Rep. [[lloyd-doggett|Lloyd Doggett]], who called for more claims data and warned of possible abuse, and with Sen. [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]], who proposed alternative relief exempting workers earning under $46,000 from income taxes entirely.[1]

## Wealth tax legislation

In March 2024, Boyle joined Representative [[pramila-jayapal|Pramila Jayapal]] (D-WA) in introducing the House companion to Senator [[elizabeth-warren|Elizabeth Warren]]'s [[ultra-millionaire-tax-act|Ultra-Millionaire Tax Act]], which would levy 2 percent annually on households worth between $50 million and $1 billion and 3 percent on those above $1 billion.[2] Critics warned that the measure would normalize the confiscation of assets and could later be extended to lower wealth brackets.[2]
