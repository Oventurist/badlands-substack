# Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands

**Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands** was the convener of the founding meeting of the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] group in 1954 and a co-founder of the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]], according to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]].[1]

Ehret writes that Bernhard founded the WWF alongside UNESCO founder and Eugenics Society president Sir [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] and [[prince-philip|Prince Philip Mountbatten]], and that all three attended the 1954 Bilderberg founding meeting where, in his account, the "grand conversion of society into a willful self-extermination" was advanced.[1] In 1970 Bernhard and Philip co-founded the [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Nature Trust]] with 999 other wealthy backers to finance the environmental movement.[1]

Ehret argues the pair's conservationism was insincere, noting that Philip and Bernhard "have been recorded to have killed more endangered species on safari than most people have killed mosquitos."[1]

## Founder of the 1001 Trust

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that in order to finance the green paradigm shift, the [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Trust]] was founded in 1970 by Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands, under a simple plan by which each of the 1001 founding members put $10,000 into the trust.[2] Ehret describes Bernhard as a card-carrying Nazi and the founder of the Bilderberg Group in 1954, who had worked alongside his close misanthropic associates [[prince-philip|Prince Philip Mountbatten]] and Sir [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] to create the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] just a few years earlier.[2] Members of the resulting 1001 Club included international royalty, billionaires and technocrats, many of whom were also members of the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]].[2]
