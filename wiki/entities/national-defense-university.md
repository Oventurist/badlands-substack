# National Defense University

The National Defense University (NDU) is a Washington, D.C. institution providing advanced education and training in national security, warfighting, and military strategy.[1]

## Role on the USIP board

Badlands Media highlights NDU's statutory seat on the board of the [[united-states-institute-of-peace|United States Institute of Peace]]. USIP is governed by a bipartisan board of fifteen directors that must include the Secretary of Defense, the Secretary of State, and the president of the National Defense University; the remaining twelve members are appointed by the President and confirmed by the Senate.[1]

The article treats this composition as evidence against the claim that USIP is separate from government and therefore exempt from executive control — the central premise of Judge Beryl Howell's May 2025 ruling blocking the Trump administration's takeover of the institute.[1] It observes pointedly that a US institute for *peace* is required to be staffed by the Secretary of Defense and the president of a warfighting university, and that all funding for USIP's programs and salaries must come from the federal government.[1]
