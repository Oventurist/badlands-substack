# Lucasfilm

**Lucasfilm** is the film and television production company, owned by [[disney|Disney]], responsible for the *Star Wars* franchise and its streaming series, including *The Mandalorian*.[1]

## Carano litigation

Lucasfilm was named alongside its parent company as a defendant in the discrimination and wrongful-termination complaint filed by actress [[gina-carano|Gina Carano]] in California federal court on February 6, 2024.[1] Carano alleges that Lucasfilm fired her from *The Mandalorian* for voicing right-wing opinions on social media, and she asks the court to order the studio to recast her.[1] The lawsuit is funded in part by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] through [[x-corp|X]], which framed its support as a commitment to free speech.[1] Coverage characterized the dispute as one front in a wider struggle over political influence in Hollywood.[1]
