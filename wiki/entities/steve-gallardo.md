# Steve Gallardo

Steve Gallardo is the sole Democratic member of the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]] during the 2022 election cycle. He is named in his official capacity as a defendant in the election contest [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] filed on December 9, 2022 following the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]], together with [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]], [[clint-hickman|Clint Hickman]], [[jack-sellers|Jack Sellers]], and [[thomas-galvin|Thomas Galvin]].

The allegations against the Board, as reported in [[arizona-earthquake|"Arizona Earthquake!"]], concern its handling and public characterization of Election Day tabulator and printer failures in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]].
