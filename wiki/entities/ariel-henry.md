# Ariel Henry

Ariel Henry is a Haitian politician who served as Prime Minister of [[haiti|Haiti]] and was the outgoing premier during the country's 2024 collapse of state authority.[1]

## 2024 crisis

By March 2024, Henry was outside the country, and gang leader [[jimmy-cherizier|Jimmy "Barbecue" Cherizier]] declared that one of his express goals was to prevent Henry's return to the Caribbean nation, alongside capturing Haiti's police chief and government ministers.[1] Badlands Media commentary described Henry as the "former PM" whose return Cherizier was committed to blocking, while nearly 1.5 million Haitians faced famine and gang attacks on power substations left much of Port-au-Prince without electricity.[1]

Former Prime Minister [[laurent-lamothe|Laurent Lamothe]] publicly called for the restoration of law and order during the same period.[1]
