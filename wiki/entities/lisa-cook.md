# Lisa Cook

Lisa Cook is a governor of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] whose attempted removal by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] became the subject of litigation before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]].

## The removal case

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] reports that later that month the Supreme Court would hear the case involving Trump and Cook, in which Cook urged the Court to prevent the president from removing her. The case is commonly framed as a test of Federal Reserve independence.

## Badlands framing

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues that the independence framing "misses the point," and that the real question is constitutional structure: "in which constitutional box do these authorities reside? Can [[congress|Congress]] bypass the executive and insulate power from presidential control?" She contends that the Constitution vests executive power in the president alone, that nothing in it creates or protects an independent central bank, and that a "for cause" removal standard which effectively prevents presidential judgment undermines Article II. In her formulation, "serving at the pleasure of the President means cause is whatever he says it is. Loss of confidence is cause." She predicts that a ruling for Trump would "effectively neuter the 'independence of the federal reserve' narrative."
