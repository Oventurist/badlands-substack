# World Commerce Corporation

The World Commerce Corporation (WCC) was created by OSS chief [[william-donovan]] to serve as the financial conduit for the covert network designed by [[allen-dulles]], [[james-jesus-angleton]] and British Security Coordinator [[william-stephenson]].[1] Under the plan, money would be funnelled to Colonel [[paul-helliwell]], who would forward it to the WCC — an arrangement the Badlands Media account presents as the seed of [[operation-gladio]]'s off-books financing.[1]
