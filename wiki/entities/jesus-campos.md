# Jesus Campos

Jesus Campos was the Mandalay Bay security guard identified as an eyewitness to the October 2017 [[las-vegas-shooting|Las Vegas shooting]], and a central figure in claims that the official account of the attack was manipulated.[1]

## Shifting account and disappearance

Chapter 12 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series describes Campos as an eyewitness whose story changed multiple times before he vanished — in the author's phrase, "like a cat in a thunderstorm" — moments before a scheduled round of television interviews.[1] The chapter links to contemporaneous reporting on the disappearance as evidence that the account offered to the public was unreliable.[1]

## Place in the broader cover-up thesis

Campos is treated as one element of a wider pattern of witness attrition surrounding the shooting. The same chapter reports that eight eyewitnesses whose accounts contradicted the official narrative died shortly after the attack, allegedly while planning to organise as a group and publicise their version of events, and reads Q's questions on the subject as pointing to those individuals.[1] Within this framing the vanishing of Campos supports the argument, advanced across the chapter, that [[stephen-paddock|Stephen Paddock]] did not act alone and that [[ms-13|MS-13]] operatives assisted in the attack.[1]
