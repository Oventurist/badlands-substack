# Audrey Kurth Cronin

**Audrey Kurth Cronin** is an American scholar of strategy and terrorism who serves as director of the Carnegie Mellon Institute for Strategy and Technology.[1]

## Assessment of the Israeli campaign in Gaza (2024)

In a June 2024 op-ed for Foreign Affairs magazine, summarized by [[tass|TASS]] and carried in the Badlands News Brief, Cronin argued that Israel's military operation in Gaza was a "strategic disaster" because support for [[hamas|Hamas]] among Gazans was increasing rather than declining.[1] She held that while the operation's primary objective had been to dismantle Hamas, the Israeli armed forces had merely reduced the number of Hamas fighters.[1]

Cronin cited a March 2024 opinion poll by the Palestinian Center for Policy and Survey Research showing support for Hamas among Gazans topping 50%, a 14-point rise since December 2023.[1] "Simply put, despite some tactical victories, the Israeli war in Gaza has been a strategic disaster," she concluded.[1] She attributed the shift to the deaths of tens of thousands of civilians, including women and children, to Israeli restrictions on humanitarian aid entering Gaza, and to the Israeli army's operations in [[rafah-invasion|Rafah]] in southern Gaza.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] accepted Cronin's data while rejecting her framing, arguing that whether the campaign counted as a disaster depended on the endgame, and that inflating support for Hamas served [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s stated plan for a war lasting at least a decade.[1]
