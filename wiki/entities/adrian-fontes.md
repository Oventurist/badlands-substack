# Adrian Fontes

Adrian Fontes is an Arizona Democrat who defeated Republican [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]] in the 2022 election for [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Arizona Secretary of State]], the office that administers the state's elections. A former [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] Recorder, he is treated in the Badlands Media corpus chiefly as a defendant in post-election litigation.

## Named in the Finchem–Zink suit

On December 9, 2022, the final day of the five-day contest window that followed certification of the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]], Finchem and congressional candidate [[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]] filed a lawsuit seeking to overturn the November results. Fontes was named as Finchem's opponent, alongside Rep. [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]] and outgoing Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]]. As summarized in "Arizona Earthquake!", the substance of the complaint's allegations concerns Hobbs's conduct — the certification of tabulation machines, pressure on the Mohave and Cochise county boards of supervisors, her failure to recuse herself while running for governor, and her office's flagging of misinformation on a Twitter account in January 2021 — rather than any conduct attributed to Fontes himself.
