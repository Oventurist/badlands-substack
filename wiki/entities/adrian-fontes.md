# Adrian Fontes

Adrian Fontes is the Democratic politician who defeated Republican [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]] in the contest for Secretary of State of Arizona in the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]].[1]

Fontes was named as a defendant in the lawsuit that Finchem filed jointly with congressional candidate [[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]] on Friday, December 9, 2022, one of several suits brought by Republican candidates during the five-day contest window that followed certification of the results on December 5.[1] The other defendants were Representative [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]] and sitting Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]]; the substance of the complaint concerned Hobbs's alleged abuses of office rather than conduct by Fontes himself.[1]

## Named defendant in Lake v. Hobbs

[[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s original civil election contest brought seven counts against [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] personally as contestee, against Adrian Fontes in his official capacity as Arizona Secretary of State, and against [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]] in his official capacity as [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] Recorder.[2] The trial court ruled in Hobbs' favor and was affirmed on appeal, with only Count III — concerning [[signature-verification|signature verification]] — surviving to be retried in May 2023.[2]
