# Minnesota National Guard

The Minnesota National Guard is the state's militia force, placed on state active duty in January 2026 by Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] under Executive Order 26-01 amid demonstrations in Minneapolis over a fatal shooting by federal immigration agents.[1]

## Executive Order 26-01 deployment

The order authorizes the Adjutant General to place Guard personnel, equipment, and facilities on state active duty to coordinate and support public safety and security services.[1] The Guard's stated mission is a support role: protecting property, safeguarding critical infrastructure, and freeing local law enforcement to focus on community safety and investigations, while protecting freedom of speech by allowing peaceful demonstrations.[1] It also provides a planning and preparation tool for local and state law enforcement and emergency management agencies.[1] Alongside the Guard, 85 members of the Minnesota State Patrol Mobile Response Team were mobilized in the Twin Cities.[1]

## Federal-state conflict framing

Because the order's findings blamed federal immigration officials for the killing of Renee Nicole Good and for increasing tensions statewide, Badlands contributor Ashe in America asked whether Walz was "preparing for the National Guard to fight the Federal Government," reading the mobilization as directed against federal agents rather than against rioters.[1]
