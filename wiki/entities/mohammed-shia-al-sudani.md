# Mohammed Shia al-Sudani

Mohammed Shia al-Sudani is a former Prime Minister of [[iraq|Iraq]] whose political bloc was implicated in the June 2026 anti-corruption sweep in Baghdad.[1]

Associated Press reporting summarized in the Badlands Brief said that of the seven people arrested during the operation — including five members of parliament whose immunity had been revoked — some of those detained were linked to al-Sudani's political bloc.[1] The arrests stemmed from the investigation into former Deputy Oil Minister [[adnan-al-jumaili|Adnan al-Jumaili]], during which security forces locked down the [[green-zone|Green Zone]] and conducted raids with armored vehicles.[1]

Badlands commentary noted that although Iraq's new government was widely expected to align more closely with [[iran|Iran]] and turn away from the West, the arrests of former officials with Iranian ties suggested a different dynamic at work.[1]
