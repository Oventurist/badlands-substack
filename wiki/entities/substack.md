# Substack

Substack is the newsletter and subscription publishing platform on which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] distributes its written work, and it is treated in the essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening" as strategically essential infrastructure rather than a mere venue.[1] The article argues that because "anyone with a smart phone can make a news report like [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] or sing a peace ballad like [[john-lennon|John Lennon]] and share it with the world," censorship has become "the weapon being used against dissidents in the current era" — and that free-speech platforms "such as [[rumble|Rumble]] and Substack are so vital to keeping liberty alive."[1]

The piece situates Substack within what it calls the [[information-war|Information War]], in which independent writers contest narratives held by legacy media.[1] Badlands Media states in the article that it "will always put out our content for free," while inviting readers to support the outlet by becoming paid subscribers to its Substack newsletter, describing itself as "a collective of citizen journalists" working to "take back the narrative from the MSM."[1]

The author closes by naming the platform explicitly as part of the movement's method: "This is why we post features on Substack. This is why we post videos on Rumble."[1] Substack is thus framed as one half of a text-and-video distribution pair through which the writers present a choice of "Freedom vs. Tyranny" to a widening audience.[1]
