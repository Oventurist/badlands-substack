# David Schoen

David Schoen is an American attorney who served on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s defense team during his second impeachment, described in Badlands commentary as "the second fake impeachment."[1]

## Commentary on a post-presidency impeachment of Obama (July 2025)

In July 2025, amid the [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] declassifications and debate over whether [[presidential-immunity|presidential immunity]] would shield former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] from prosecution, Schoen commented publicly on the prospect of a post-presidency impeachment of Obama.[1] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] circulated the clip in the July 28, 2025 News Brief as an "Another Take" alongside [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s discussion of whether a president should be immune for official-duty crimes that impede an incoming president.[1]

The relevance of Schoen's perspective derives from the precedent set by Trump's own second impeachment, which established that a president may be impeached after leaving office — a route that, under Article I, Section 3, Clause 7 of the U.S. Constitution, would leave a convicted party liable to subsequent indictment, trial and punishment.[1]

## Defense of Trump over the Epstein flight logs

Schoen, who was hired to lead [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s criminal defense nine days before Epstein's death, publicly rebutted claims that Epstein held damaging information about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2] "I was hired to lead Jeffrey Epstein's defense as his criminal lawyer 9 days before he died," Schoen wrote on X. "He sought my advice for months before that."[2] He added: "I can say authoritatively, unequivocally, and definitely that he had no information to hurt President Trump. I specifically asked that!"[2]

Schoen's remarks followed a post by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] on X alleging that Trump was mentioned in the [[epstein-files|Epstein files]], made amid escalating tensions between the two men over Trump's "big beautiful bill," and coverage noting that Trump appears in Epstein flight logs seven times.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that if such evidence existed it would have been deployed during the thousands of prior attempts to stop Trump — raids, prosecutions, and what she characterized as fabricated crimes — and that it strained belief for the regime to have withheld its most damning material until after the uniparty establishment had lost its credibility.[2]
