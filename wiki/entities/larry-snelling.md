# Larry Snelling

Larry Snelling is the Superintendent of the Chicago Police Department, the official responsible for policing the streets around the [[2024-democratic-national-convention|2024 Democratic National Convention]] in Chicago.[1]

## Opening-day protests, August 2024

On the convention's opening day, thousands marched to the United Center to demand a ceasefire in the Israel–Hamas war, and dozens of protesters broke through a section of the outer security fence.[1] Snelling said that some of the protesters who took down the fence threw water bottles and other items at police officers.[1] He stated that officers de-escalated the situation without using their batons or chemical agents, and authorities confirmed that the inner security perimeter around the convention site was not breached and that there was no threat to attendees.[1] Several protesters who got through the fence were detained and handcuffed, and officers put on gas masks as demonstrators attempted to bring down a second fence erected in front of police lines.[1]
