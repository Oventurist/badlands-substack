# Jason Lowery

**Jason Lowery** is a United States Space Force major and the author of *Softwar: A Novel Theory on Power Projection and the National Strategic Significance of Bitcoin*.[1]

## Softwar thesis

The Badlands Media essay "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?" cites Lowery as having provided an extensive and well-researched thesis arguing that [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is much more than "magic internet money."[1] The author invokes Lowery's framework a second time when arguing that Bitcoin physically restrains the "god-kings" through public and private keys and a decentralised ledger known as the timechain — a parallel to the angel of Revelation who binds Satan with a key and a chain for a thousand years at the start of the [[millennial-reign|Millennial Reign]].[1]

## Context in the article's argument

Lowery's thesis supports the essay's broader contention that Bitcoin should be understood as a weapon and digital counterinsurgency operation rather than a payment novelty.[1] The author notes in the same passage that although Bitcoin was released as a peer-to-peer electronic cash system, every element of its underlying technology — TCP/IP, TOR, SHA-256, GPS — was built by the military for nuclear-resistant, wartime application.[1]

## Replacing kinetic power with electric power

A later Badlands Brief essay, published on the fifteenth anniversary of the Bitcoin [[bitcoin-genesis-block|Genesis Block]], quoted Lowery's [[softwar|Softwar]] directly: he invites readers to imagine that, instead of trying to replace physical power with abstract power, people accept there is no replacement for physical power and instead seek to replace kinetic power with electric power.[2] If a non-kinetic and therefore non-lethal form of warfare could still enable global-scale strategic power competitions to settle disputes, manage resources, and establish a pecking order, humanity "might be able to break the cycle."[2] The essay presented Lowery as describing the transition to a genuinely new war-fighting domain, and paired his argument with [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]]'s 2014 observation that cryptography can defend its users against "even the full might of the superpower."[2]

## Twitter exchanges on AI, 2023

In an April 2023 Badlands Media essay, the contributor known as [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] recounted following Lowery's Twitter activity while studying [[softwar|Softwar]], and being drawn thereby into the [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] question.[3] During one exchange, Lowery sent a tweet containing a large block of text that the author read as an ordinary human message; minutes later Lowery revealed in another thread that the text was a screenshot of [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] 4.0 output.[3] Investor [[preston-pysh|Preston Pysh]] replied an hour afterwards with a further ChatGPT output rewriting Lowery's summary in the style of comedian Kevin Hart, and subsequently posted a link to an AI voice rendition of that text delivered in Hart's voice — a sequence the essayist described as text and speech that literally no human wrote or said.[3] Lowery amplified the episode by sharing it alongside a photoshopped meme of Kevin Hart reading the Softwar book.[3]

Lowery was also cited in the same essay for the argument that the decisive countermeasure against AI bot farms is a mechanism able to impose "severe physical costs" on them — a resource the AI cannot replicate or acquire at scale — which the author identified as [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] micro-transactions recording an auditable energy expenditure.[3]

## Cited in Kekistani Code Talkers

The Badlands Brief article "Kekistani Code Talkers" cites Major Jason Lowery for his discussion of a transition to a new war-fighting domain in space and of the national strategic significance of [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], as set out in [[softwar|Softwar]].[4] The author links this thesis to Q's definition of the "stone" as the capability of projecting power.[4]
