# Jason Lowery

**Jason Lowery** is a Major in the United States Space Force and the author of [[softwar|*Softwar: A Novel Theory on Power Projection and the National Strategic Significance of Bitcoin*]].

## Role in the corpus

The Badlands Media feature "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?" cites Lowery as having "provided an extensive and well researched thesis on how [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] is much more than simply 'magic internet money.'" [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] uses Lowery's work to support the question of whether Bitcoin was released as a military weapon and digital counterinsurgency operation aimed at the centralized control of money — the root cause, in the article's framing, of humanity's endless cycle of destruction and bloodshed.

Lowery is cited a second time in the article's concluding argument, for the proposition that Bitcoin *physically restrains* the "god-kings" through public and private keys and a decentralized ledger known as the timechain. That claim of physical restraint supplies the article's analogy with the angel of Revelation who binds Satan with a key and a chain at the start of the [[millennial-reign|Millennial Reign]].
