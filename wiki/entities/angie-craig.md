# Angie Craig

Angie Craig is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Minnesota.[1]

## Call for Biden to withdraw, July 2024

On Saturday, July 6, 2024, Craig became the fifth Democratic member of Congress to publicly call on President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to abandon his re-election campaign, and the first incumbent in a competitive race to do so.[1] She wrote: "I do not believe that the President can effectively campaign and win against Donald Trump."[1]

Her statement came amid rapidly escalating pressure from within the party following Biden's debate performance and his interview with [[george-stephanopoulos|George Stephanopoulos]], and coincided with reports that Senate intelligence committee chairman [[mark-warner|Mark Warner]] was organizing Senate Democrats into a united front urging Biden to step aside.[1] Badlands Media framed these defections, together with a simultaneous revolt among mega-donors, as evidence of a "Panic Pattern" spreading through the political establishment.[1]
