# State Duma

The State Duma is the lower house of the Russian parliament and the chamber through which the Kremlin routes legislation requiring formal legislative assent.[1]

## Role in the suspension of New START

In February 2023, President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] announced during a speech to both houses of the Russian parliament that Russia would freeze its participation in the [[new-start-treaty|New START]] nuclear arms control agreement, using the same address to repeat accusations that the West was seeking to destroy Russia.[1] Later that day Putin submitted a draft law formalizing the suspension to the Duma, which was scheduled to consider the measure the following day, Wednesday, February 22, 2023, and to take an immediate decision on it.[1] Duma speaker [[vyacheslav-volodin|Vyacheslav Volodin]] announced the expedited timetable in a statement.[1]

The speed with which the chamber was expected to act — consideration and decision within a single session — illustrated the Duma's function as a ratifying body for executive decisions rather than an independent check on Kremlin foreign policy.[1] Moscow simultaneously said that Russia would continue to observe the treaty's limits on the number of deployed nuclear warheads despite the suspension, framing the legislative step as a political signal rather than an immediate change in force posture.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized the Russian escalation surrounding the Duma vote as "more theater than anything else," arguing that an actual nuclear exchange was unlikely and that the posturing served narrative rather than military purposes.[1]
