# Likud

Likud is the right-wing Israeli political party led by Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief identifies Likud through its Energy Minister, [[eli-cohen|Eli Cohen]], who praised the Israeli Security Cabinet's February 2026 [[west-bank|West Bank]] measures as establishing "de facto sovereignty" that rules out a Palestinian state. The package — shifting building authority in Hebron, facilitating settler land purchases, repealing restrictions on non-Muslim real estate transactions, and expanding Israeli enforcement into Palestinian-controlled areas — was implemented by executive action rather than legislation, and is described in the source as violating the [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]]. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly opposed the direction of the measures, declaring "I am against annexation."
