# Bank Leumi

Bank Leumi (ticker LUMI.TA) is the largest bank in [[israel|Israel]] and a constituent of the [[tel-aviv-stock-exchange|Tel Aviv Stock Exchange]].[1]

## Short selling before October 7

Leumi featured prominently in the 2023 study by [[robert-jackson-jr|Robert J. Jackson Jr.]] and [[joshua-mitts|Joshua Mitts]] into trading ahead of the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 Hamas attack]]. The researchers wrote that 4.43 million new shares of the bank were sold short between September 14 and October 5, 2023, and associated that additional short selling with profits of 3.2 billion shekels — roughly $862 million.[1]

The concentration of shorting in a single blue-chip Israeli name, alongside a sudden spike in short interest in the [[msci-israel-etf|MSCI Israel Exchange Traded Fund]] on October 2, formed part of the authors' conclusion that traders informed about the coming attacks profited from the events.[1] The [[israel-securities-authority|Israel Securities Authority]] subsequently said the matter was under investigation.[1] See [[israel-october-7-foreknowledge-allegations|Israel October 7 foreknowledge allegations]].[1]
