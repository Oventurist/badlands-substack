# Association of Southeast Asian Nations

The Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) is the regional bloc whose leaders' summit served as the setting for a round of high-stakes U.S. diplomacy in late October 2025.[1]

## The October 2025 Malaysia Summit

[[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] traveled to Malaysia for the ASEAN summit, arriving on a Sunday as the first stop of a five-day Asia tour.[1] The journey itself produced a diplomatic moment before it reached Asia: an unplanned refueling stop at Al Udeid Air Base in [[qatar|Qatar]], where Emir [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani]] boarded Air Force One to greet the president and discuss Gaza.[1]

On the sidelines of the summit, the United States and China agreed a framework for a trade deal, announced by Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]].[1] He said the framework would remove the threatened 100% tariffs on Chinese imports set to begin on 1 November and would include "a final deal" on the sale of TikTok in the United States.[1] The tour was expected to culminate in a face-to-face meeting between Trump and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] in South Korea later that week, with Trump saying after the summit talks, "I think we're going to have a deal with China."[1] Badlands commentary read the framework as a sign that the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] had the "Hegemon in a vice on multiple fronts."[1]
