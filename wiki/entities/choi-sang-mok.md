# Choi Sang-mok

Choi Sang-mok is South Korea's Finance Minister, who in April 2025 was invited to Washington for trade talks with U.S. Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]].[1]

## 2025 trade talks with the United States

Seoul's finance ministry announced on Wednesday, April 16, 2025 that Choi would meet Bessent the following week to discuss trade issues during a visit to the United States made at Bessent's suggestion.[1] Choi was also expected to attend a meeting of G20 finance ministers on the sidelines of the spring International Monetary Fund and [[world-bank|World Bank]] meetings in Washington.[1]

Bessent had said the U.S. was in talks with a handful of countries including [[south-korea|South Korea]] and Japan over tariffs announced by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and that there was a "first mover advantage" especially for allies that could reach "an agreement in principle" soon.[1] South Korea, under acting President [[han-duck-soo|Han Duck-soo]], engaged actively with senior Trump administration officials with the goal of lowering the 25% reciprocal tariff Trump had announced for the country and subsequently paused.[1]
