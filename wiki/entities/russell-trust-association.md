# Russell Trust Association

The Russell Trust Association is the corporate body under which Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society was officially incorporated in 1856, twenty-four years after the Order was first established on the [[yale-university|Yale]] campus in 1832.[1]

According to "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8," virtually all available information on the Order's early members indicates that the money required to sustain its campus affairs — and its broader role in placing members into key positions of influence after graduation — derived from the opium trade in the Far East.[1] That trade had been set up by the [[british-east-india-company|British East India Company]] and was flourishing by the time the Treaty of Paris was signed in 1783.[1] The founding families of Skull and Bones included the Russell and Perkins families, New England merchant houses drawn into the opium trade as junior partners under the sponsorship of Baring Brothers and the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]].[1]
