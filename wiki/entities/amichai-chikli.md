# Amichai Chikli

Amichai Chikli is the Israeli Diaspora Affairs Minister, known in mid-2026 for publicly warning that [[israel|Israel]] should prepare for the possibility of a future naval confrontation with [[turkey|Turkey]].[1]

## The July 2026 warning

Chikli argued that Ankara's expanding regional posture, together with tensions over Gaza and the Eastern Mediterranean, warranted Israeli planning for a possible clash at sea.[1] He called for strengthening Israeli naval capabilities and strategic planning, though he did not suggest that any confrontation was imminent.[1]

The comments came against a backdrop of elevated regional tension driven by the wars in Ukraine and Gaza. Turkey has maintained relations with both Ukraine and Russia while continuing to criticize Israeli military operations in Gaza, and despite increasingly sharp rhetoric between Ankara and Jerusalem the two states are not in direct military conflict.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands commentary treated Chikli's statement as corroboration of a longer-running thesis that a naval conflict could break out in the eastern Mediterranean between Israel and Turkey, potentially with [[ukraine|Ukraine]], Greece, or Cyprus aligning with Israel.[1] That reading connected Chikli's warning to reports of Ukrainian drone strikes on Turkish civilian vessels in the Black Sea and raised the question of whether [[article-5|Article 5]] of the NATO treaty might be invoked over an attack on a NATO member by Ukraine.[1]

## Campaign to shape US discourse on Gaza (2023–2024)

In November 2023, weeks into the war in Gaza, Chikli — then a 42-year-old [[likud|Likud]] minister described as brash — was called before the [[knesset|Knesset]] to brief lawmakers on how Israel might respond to rising anti-war protests among young Americans, particularly at elite universities.[2] "I've said it before, and I'll say it again now, that I think we should, especially in the United States, be on the offensive," he told the hearing.[2] He assured lawmakers that new budget money was available for a pushback campaign distinct from traditional public relations and paid advertising, including 80 programs already under way to be conducted "in the 'Concert' way."[2]

A June 2024 Guardian investigation reported that Chikli had since led a targeted push against critics of Israel, and uncovered evidence that Israel had relaunched a controversial entity as part of a broader campaign to target US college campuses and to redefine antisemitism in US law.[2] The "Concert" reference pointed to the relaunch of the Israeli government program originally known as Kela Shlomo, designed to carry out what Israel termed "mass consciousness activities" aimed largely at the United States and Europe; now operating as [[voices-of-israel|Voices of Israel]], it had previously worked with groups spearheading the campaign to pass state anti-BDS laws penalizing Americans for boycotting Israel.[2] The Guardian described its latest incarnation as part of a hardline and sometimes covert operation against student protests, human rights organizations, and other dissenting voices.[2]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, who had reported on the effort since September 2023, urged readers to read the investigation in full and argued that the macro claims were supported by the documentary paper trail.[2] He contended that while much of the MAGA movement had long accepted that the US government is influenced by foreign entities — typically naming China, Russia, Iran, or the World Economic Forum — many remain reluctant to include Israel, and framed the revelations as evidence of a transnational effort in which an Israeli deep state is a major component.[2]

## Call for the elimination of Ahmed al-Sharaa

On Tuesday, July 15, 2025, Chikli — Israel's Minister of Diaspora Affairs and Combating Antisemitism — publicly called for the elimination of Syrian President [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed al-Sharaa]] amid ongoing Israeli strikes on the Syrian regime and while diplomatic efforts toward a potential security agreement between the two countries continued.[3] "If it looks like Hamas, talks like Hamas, and acts like Hamas, it's Hamas!" Chikli declared, adding that Israel "must not stand idly by in the face of the Islamist-Nazi terror regime of Al-Qaeda in a suit and tie" and that anyone treating al-Sharaa as a legitimate leader was "gravely mistaken — he is a terrorist, a barbaric murderer who should be eliminated without delay."[3] He cited the massacre of the [[alawites|Alawites]], which he said was met with "deafening silence from European leaders," and the ongoing "slaughter and humiliation" of the [[druze]].[3]

Badlands commentary from [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the statement as a call for assassination and advanced the theory that al-Sharaa — formerly known as Abu Mohammed al-Jolani — had been trained and deployed by [[mossad]] to overthrow [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]], but had since been offered a better arrangement by [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Erdogan]] and Turkey and was refusing Israeli demands for control of Damascus and southern Syria and a corridor between the [[golan-heights|Golan Heights]] and the Euphrates.[3]
