# Tim Shea

Tim Shea is a former acting head of the Drug Enforcement Administration during the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

He was named of counsel to the [[torridon-group|Torridon Group]] in January 2024, joining alongside former White House counsel [[fred-fielding|Fred Fielding]] as the firm founded by [[bill-barr|William Barr]] and [[ted-ullyot|Ted Ullyot]] expanded into Washington.[1]
