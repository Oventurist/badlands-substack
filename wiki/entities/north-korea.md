# North Korea

North Korea, formally the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, appears in the Badlands Media essay [[america-for-sale-part-2|America for Sale — Part 2]] as the destination of a secret 2009 mission by former president [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] that the author believes concealed a larger purpose.

## The 2009 hostage mission

In 2009, with the blessing of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration, Bill Clinton traveled to Pyongyang and secured the release of two American journalists who had been sentenced to twelve years of hard labor for spying. The *Chicago Tribune*, quoted in the article, reported that the trip was planned in consultation with the administration's national security experts and involved Clinton, [[doug-band|Doug Band]], and two younger aides to the former president, Justin Cooper and Jon Davidson, along with several other advisers and friends. The article states that the mission took place at the request of North Korean leader [[kim-jong-il|Kim Jong Il]] himself.

Emails released by [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] show Band asking [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] at the [[state-department|State Department]] for diplomatic passports for the traveling party on July 27, 2009 — "Need get me/ justy and jd dip passports" — to which Abedin replied, "Okay. Will figure it out." None of the requesters were State Department employees.

## Interpretation

Q drop 227 (November 24, 2017) consisted solely of a photograph, which [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] identifies as taken during this trip, showing [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] behind Bill Clinton and Doug Band behind Kim Jong Il. The author argues that hostages have long served as cover for other operations — citing the Iran hostages and Iran-Contra, and hostages in Obama's Iran nuclear deal as cover for a secret money transfer — and asks what the 2009 North Korean hostage story was covering for. He connects the question to Q drop 228, to leadership changes in three other countries following Trump's 2017 inauguration, and to [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]], deferring the answer to a third installment.
