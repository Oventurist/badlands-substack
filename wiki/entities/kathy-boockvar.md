# Kathy Boockvar

Kathy Boockvar served as Pennsylvania's Secretary of the Commonwealth during the 2020 United States presidential election and was the named defendant in litigation over the state's voter-roll maintenance.[1] Badlands Media, which rendered her name "Kathy Bookvar," reported that the [[public-interest-legal-foundation|Public Interest Legal Foundation]] secured a voter-roll cleanup agreement against her office in Pennsylvania, paralleling similar agreements obtained against Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] and Michigan Secretary of State [[jocelyn-benson|Jocelyn Benson]].[1]

The article argued that if the Pennsylvania rolls were as pristine as officials claimed, the litigation would have been an opportunity to demonstrate it rather than to settle.[1] Pennsylvania featured heavily in the broader 2020 litigation record described in the piece, including rulings on poll observers at Philadelphia satellite offices, the counting of ballots lacking addresses on declaration envelopes, and the [[pennsylvania-supreme-court|Pennsylvania Supreme Court]]'s holding that voters should not be disenfranchised over advisory portions of the Election Code.[1]
