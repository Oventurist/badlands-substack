# Chris Hayes

Chris Hayes is an American cable-news host associated with MSNBC's liberal primetime lineup.[1]

## Role in the Opposition

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] identified Hayes, together with [[rachel-maddow|Rachel Maddow]], as one of the primary pundits championing the NGO institutions of [[the-opposition|the Opposition]] and keeping its morale up following the 2024 election of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] DeLarme wrote that he watched their programs specifically to understand what the Opposition was plotting, on the grounds that while the hosts would not always state their strategy outright, they dropped hints and booked guests deeply involved in that world.[1]
