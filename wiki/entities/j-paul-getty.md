# J. Paul Getty

J. Paul Getty was the American oil magnate whose fortune founded the [[getty-family|Getty family]] dynasty, an inheritance that Badlands Media coverage identifies as central to the rise of California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]].[1]

## The kidnapping and the Newsom connection

[[william-newsom|William Newsom, Jr.]], the governor's father, served as financial consigliere to the heirs of the Getty Oil fortune and personally delivered a ransom to the Italian mafia in exchange for the release of one of J. Paul Getty's grandchildren, who had been abducted.[1] Newsom, Jr. later successfully advocated for a change in California state law that expanded the beneficiaries' entitlements to the Getty trust fund.[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] urged readers interested in "the more clandestine aspects lurking behind American politics" to investigate the Getty family history in its own right.[1]
