# Norman Dodd

**Norman Dodd** was director of research for the House Select Committee to Investigate Foundations and Comparable Organizations.[1] He is best known in the literature on [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]] for a 1952 report in which he recounted that the president of the [[ford-foundation|Ford Foundation]] told him bluntly that the foundation was "operating under a directive from the White House" to "use our grant-making power so as to alter our life in the United States that we can be comfortably merged with the Soviet Union."[1]

Badlands Media cites this episode as evidence that the importance of the nonprofit sector in the machinations of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] "cannot be overstated."[1] Dodd's inquiry preceded the [[reece-committee|Reece Committee]] investigation of 1953–1955, on which [[rene-wormser|Rene A. Wormser]] served as general counsel.[1]
