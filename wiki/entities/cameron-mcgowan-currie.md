# Cameron McGowan Currie

Cameron McGowan Currie is a U.S. District Judge, appointed by President Bill Clinton, who in November 2025 issued the orders dismissing the federal criminal cases against former FBI director [[james-comey|James Comey]] and New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]].[1] Currie ruled that the federal prosecutor who brought the cases, [[lindsey-halligan|Lindsey Halligan]], had been illegally appointed by the [[justice-department|Justice Department]].[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] noted publicly that Currie's orders did not address the substance of the allegations against Comey or James, saying, "the court didn't say you couldn't bring the case, re-bring the case, or appeal the case. So they have a lot of options."[1] The administration announced on November 25, 2025 that the Justice Department would appeal the dismissals.[1]
