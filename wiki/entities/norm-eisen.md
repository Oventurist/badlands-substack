# Norm Eisen

**Norman "Norm" Eisen** is an American lawyer, election-law expert, and former Obama administration official who is identified in Badlands coverage as the central architect of both the 2020 election "fortification" effort and its 2026 successor.[1]

## 2020 election fortification

Eisen was quoted in the February 2021 *Time* magazine article that described a behind-the-scenes network coordinating to secure the 2020 election.[1] The article identified him as a prominent lawyer and former Obama administration official who recruited Republicans and Democrats to the board of the Voter Protection Program, and quoted him saying that the untold story of the election was "the thousands of people of both parties who accomplished the triumph of American democracy at its very foundation."[1]

## 2026 midterm planning

In June 2026 Eisen was among the experts convened by Senate Minority Leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] to help Democratic senators prepare for a set of possible election-disruption scenarios ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|midterms]], alongside former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], election attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], and the heads of [[democracy-forward|Democracy Forward]] and [[protect-democracy|Protect Democracy]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that "the current shadow cabal and 2020 shadow cabal are both the brain-children of Norm Eisen and his minions," treating his recurring presence as evidence that the [[shadow-cabal|2026 effort]] is a continuation rather than a coincidence.[1]
