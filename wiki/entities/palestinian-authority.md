# Palestinian Authority

The Palestinian Authority (PA) is the governing body established to administer Palestinian territories. In the Badlands Media article "[[bad-actors|Bad Actors]]" it is the recipient of a disputed $221 million payment made by the outgoing administration of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] hours before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration.

## The $221 million transfer

According to The Hill and the Associated Press as quoted in the article, the Obama administration sent $221 million to the Palestinian Authority on the Friday as the president prepared to leave the White House for the last time, formally notifying [[congress|Congress]] only hours before the inauguration. The Independent reported that Obama had overruled Congress to make the decision, that the money was intended to support good governance and humanitarian aid, and that the incoming Trump administration froze the package — Prime Minister [[rami-hamdallah|Rami Hamdallah]] was told by U.S. officials not to expect the payout. Acting [[state-department|State Department]] spokesperson Mark Toner said that in a final act, Secretary of State [[john-kerry|John Kerry]] had instructed [[usaid|USAID]] to release $220.3 million for Gaza recovery programs.

## The "pay to slay" program

Quoting Newsweek, the article describes the PA's long-standing practice of incentivising violence through the [[pay-to-slay|"pay to slay"]] program, which is enshrined in Palestinian law. A 2004 law guaranteed salaries for convicted terrorists and their families, with legislation specifying that "prisoners and released prisoners are a fighting sector and integral part of the fabric of Arab Palestinian society." In 2010 the PA passed resolutions stipulating a monthly payment to every Palestinian inmate in an Israeli prison convicted of a terrorism-related offense, and in 2013 Prime Minister Rami Hamdallah amended the law to offer additional benefits on release, including a position within the PA for any male ex-prisoner incarcerated ten or more years and any female ex-prisoner who served five.

## Interpretation in the corpus

The article notes that the PA is little discussed since Hamas took over Gaza, and argues that the last-minute payment was not routine policy but a repayment of political debt — money owed by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] to a donor who had expected her election victory. In the author's reading the transfer, routed through USAID, is an example of taxpayer money being used to fund enemies of the United States.
