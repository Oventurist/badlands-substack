# Tauqeer Hussain

Tauqeer "Toki" Hussain is a British man convicted as part of a child sexual exploitation gang in Keighley, England, whose case resurfaced in January 2025 amid renewed national attention to the UK's [[grooming-gangs|grooming gangs]] scandal.[1]

## Conviction

Hussain was found guilty of two counts of rape of one underage victim and one count of rape of a second underage victim, offences committed in 2012.[1] He was given a total sentence of 18 years; according to Guardian reporting at the time of the 2016 sentencing, he raped both girls in an underground car park.[1] Twelve men in total were sentenced in connection with the sexual exploitation of a Keighley teenager.[1]

## Police response controversy

A widely circulated account revived in January 2025 recounted that police caught a member of the rape gang in the act in 2012, had a brief conversation, and then drove away.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that although Hussain was eventually held accountable, the girl was still raped in front of what she described as disinterested or impotent and unarmed police, and that despite the convictions the gangs continued operating — with Conservative leader [[kemi-badenoch|Kemi Badenoch]] arguing that authorities still have no idea of the true extent of the problem.[1]
