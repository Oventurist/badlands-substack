# Mo Ivory

Mo Ivory is a Fulton County, Georgia commissioner representing District 4 (D-04).[1]

## 2026 FBI search of the Fulton County Election Hub

Ivory became a nationally cited source in January 2026 after the FBI executed a search warrant at the Fulton County Election Hub and Operations Center in Union City, Georgia, seeking records related to the 2020 General Election.[1] She publicly confirmed that federal agents removed roughly 700 boxes of ballots and related election records from the facility.[1] Her confirmation was among the earliest on-the-record accounts of the scale of the seizure, which encompassed physical ballots of all categories, absentee ballot envelopes, provisional ballots, ballot images, tabulator tapes from scanners, and 2020 voter rolls.[1]

As a sitting county commissioner, Ivory's statement carried weight because the FBI itself released no official details about the investigative purpose, targets, or findings of the operation, which was described as ongoing.[1] The raid occurred amid long-running disputes over access to [[fulton-county|Fulton County]]'s 2020 election records and subpoenas issued by the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]].[1]
