# John Bass

John Bass is an American career diplomat who, in March 2024, was announced as the temporary replacement for [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] as Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs, the number three position in the [[state-department|State Department]].[1] At the time of the announcement he was serving as Under Secretary of State for Management.[1]

## Afghanistan and Turkey

Bass is a former United States ambassador to Afghanistan who oversaw the American withdrawal from Kabul during the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration, an operation widely described as botched.[1] ZeroHedge observed that it was "somewhat ironic" that the official who had presided over the Kabul withdrawal would also oversee Ukraine policy at a critical juncture when Kiev was "clearly against the ropes."[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described Bass as [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s former ambassador to Afghanistan and noted that he had been serving as ambassador to [[turkey|Turkey]] in the interim before his elevation.[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

Burning Bright read the pairing of Nuland's exit and Bass's arrival as preparation for a "Botched Boogaloo 2.0" — a 2024 repeat of the 2021 Kabul withdrawal playing out in Syria and Iraq.[1] That reading is tied to [[just-human|Just Human]]'s thesis that the Kabul withdrawal represented the consummation of the [[doha-agreement|Doha Agreement]] between Trump and the Taliban, and to [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s [[devolution|devolution]] framework.[1]
