# Kayle Barrington Bates

Kayle Barrington Bates was a Florida death-row inmate executed by lethal injection on Tuesday, August 19, 2025, at the age of 67, for the 1982 murder of Janet Renee White.[1]

## Case and execution

White was murdered minutes after her husband, Randy White, kissed her goodbye for the last time.[1] She was described as the youngest of five tightknit siblings and a wife who dreamed of having children and running her own business.[1] Randy White, who was 27 when his wife was killed, witnessed the execution at the age of 70, forty-three years after the crime.[1]

White told USA Today after the execution that he had made a promise to his wife "right after it happened that I would be there for every trial, every hearing, every appeal, and that as long as I was living, I would seek justice for her," adding that he felt "a relief that I can mentally let Renee know that justice has finally been served for her."[1]

## Wider context

Beyond Florida, eight further executions were scheduled in other U.S. states by the end of 2025, putting the country on pace for at least 39 inmates put to death that year — more than in any year since 2013, and a 56% increase over the 25 executions carried out in 2024.[1]
