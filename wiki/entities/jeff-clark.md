# Jeff Clark

## Oversight Project role

Jeff Clark served as Vice President of Litigation at the [[oversight-project|Oversight Project]] when the organization published more than seventy hours of recordings of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] speaking with his memoir ghostwriter [[mark-zwonitzer|Mark Zwonitzer]] in July 2026.[1] Clark said the recordings should force a full reexamination of the decision to give Biden a pass for keeping and disclosing classified information, a decision that followed special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s investigation.[1]
