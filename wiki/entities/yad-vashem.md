# Yad Vashem

Yad Vashem is [[israel|Israel]]'s official memorial and research institution dedicated to the memory of the [[holocaust|Holocaust]] and its victims. Beyond commemoration, it functions as an authoritative voice in international disputes over Holocaust memory and the public honouring of wartime collaborators.

## Criticism of Ukraine's 2026 honours for Andriy Melnyk

As reported in the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," Yad Vashem criticised the Ukrainian state reburial ceremony held in Kyiv for [[andriy-melnyk|Andriy Melnyk]], a leader of the [[organization-of-ukrainian-nationalists|Organization of Ukrainian Nationalists]] who collaborated with Nazi Germany. The institution warned that celebrating leaders tied to movements that collaborated with Nazi Germany undermines Holocaust remembrance. Its statement accompanied a condemnation from Israel's Foreign Ministry, which said there is "no place for ignoring historical truth and the memory of the victims murdered by the Nazis and their collaborators." The ceremony was attended by President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] and senior [[ukraine|Ukrainian]] officials.

## Netanyahu's May 9, 2026 speech

On May 9, 2026, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] spoke at Yad Vashem, the World Holocaust Remembrance Center in Jerusalem, declaring: "If Israel is forced to stand alone - Israel will stand alone." The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief presents the speech as the prelude to Netanyahu's defiance of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over retaliatory strikes on [[iran|Iran]].
