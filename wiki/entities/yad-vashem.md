# Yad Vashem

Yad Vashem is Israel's official Holocaust remembrance institution, and functions as an authoritative voice in international disputes over the commemoration of Nazi collaborators.[1]

## 2026 Ukraine controversy

Yad Vashem criticized the Ukrainian state's May 2026 reburial honors for [[andriy-melnyk|Andriy Melnyk]], a leader of the [[organization-of-ukrainian-nationalists|Organization of Ukrainian Nationalists]] who collaborated with Nazi Germany, warning that celebrating leaders tied to movements that collaborated with the Nazis undermines Holocaust remembrance.[1] The institution's statement accompanied a condemnation from Israel's Foreign Ministry, which said there is "no place for ignoring historical truth and the memory of the victims murdered by the Nazis and their collaborators."[1]

## Netanyahu's "stand alone" speech

On May 9, 2026, Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] spoke at Yad Vashem — described in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] as the World Holocaust Remembrance Center, in Jerusalem — and declared: "If Israel is forced to stand alone - Israel will stand alone."[2] Exactly one month later, [[israel|Israel]] struck [[iran|Iran]] in defiance of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s urging, a sequence Badlands read as Netanyahu being baited into fulfilling his own Yad Vashem pledge.[2]
