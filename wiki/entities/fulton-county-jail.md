# Fulton County Jail

The Fulton County Jail is the county detention facility in Atlanta, Georgia, operated under [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] authority. It became a focus of national attention in August 2023 as the booking site for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his eighteen co-defendants in District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]]' election interference prosecution, [[state-of-georgia-v-trump|State of Georgia v. Trump]].[1]

## Role in the 2023 Georgia case

Unlike Trump's earlier federal and New York state proceedings, which were processed in courthouses and federal buildings, the Georgia case required defendants to report to the county jail itself for processing, including fingerprints and a booking photograph. All defendants were given until noon on Friday, August 25, 2023, to appear; Trump announced on his social media platform on the evening of August 21 that he intended to surrender on Thursday.[1]

## Conditions and public commentary

The facility's reputation for poor conditions featured prominently in coverage of the surrender. Appearing on [[msnbc|MSNBC]] on August 21, 2023, former Watergate prosecutor [[jill-wine-banks|Jill Wine-Banks]] described the jail as "not a clean, nice environment" and, "from what we hear from the press, a really dirty, dangerous, scary place," predicting the setting would produce "a very different picture" of Trump than his prior appearances.[1] She likened the potential effect to that on Watergate defendants who "really freaked out" in the D.C. jail and had to be relocated to an Army base to secure their cooperation.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] turned that description back on the system, noting the irony of a legal commentator citing squalid, terrifying conditions at a taxpayer-funded facility while celebrating the prospect of a political opponent being held there, and asking what had become of promised justice reform and humane conditions in Atlanta.[1]
