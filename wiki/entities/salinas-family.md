# Salinas Family

The Salinas family of Mexico is presented in the Badlands series as the Mexican counterpart of the [[bush-family|Bush family]] — the two were not merely connected but close friends, "Intimo Amigos."[1] The prior article in the series tied both families to a massive increase in drug trafficking across the Mexican border under the guise of free trade, arguing that [[nafta|NAFTA]] existed to expand smuggling and thereby CIA profits.[1]

[[carlos-salinas|Carlos Salinas]] served as president of Mexico; his brother [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]] was imprisoned over the 1994 murder of [[jose-francisco-ruiz-massieu|José Francisco Ruiz Massieu]], who had once been married to their sister Adriana Salinas.[1] The article states the family were all cartel members, connected to the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] of [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]], whose shipments Raúl gave "green light protection" through Mexico.[1] Raúl Salinas is also noted as having had a private banker at Citibank, a detail the author invokes when discussing Venezuelan president [[carlos-andres-perez|Carlos Andrés Pérez]]'s secret New York accounts.[1] [[carlos-slim|Carlos Slim]] and [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] were both friends of the family.[1]
