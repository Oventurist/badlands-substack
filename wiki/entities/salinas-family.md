# Salinas Family

The Salinas family of Mexico comprises President Carlos Salinas de Gortari, his brother [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]], and their sister Adriana. The Badlands Media account describes them as close friends of the Bush family — "Intimo Amigos" — and as cartel-connected participants in the drug trafficking expansion the author attributes to [[nafta|NAFTA]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Adriana Salinas had once been married to [[jose-francisco-ruiz-massieu|José Francisco Ruiz Massieu]], the secretary general and second-ranking official of Mexico's ruling [[pri|Institutional Revolutionary Party]], whom Raúl was convicted of masterminding the murder of in September 1994.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Late in his term, President Carlos Salinas appointed the victim's brother, [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]], as special prosecutor over the assassination — an appointment the article reads as a deliberate cover-up, since no one would question a verdict delivered by the victim's own brother.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

The family is described as connected to the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] of [[juan-garcia-abrego|Juan García Abrego]], whose shipments Raúl Salinas gave "green light protection" through Mexico.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Raúl is separately noted as having had a private banker at Citibank.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Carlos Salinas, popular at the outset like Venezuela's [[carlos-andres-perez|Carlos Andrés Pérez]], ended in self-exile — a pattern the author calls a CIA template.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Both [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] and [[carlos-slim|Carlos Slim]] were friends of the family.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
