# Army Protective Services Battalion

The Army Protective Services Battalion is a United States Army unit responsible for the personal security of senior military leaders.[1]

## Surveillance of dissent

According to a report published by [[the-intercept|The Intercept]] on 17 June 2023 and summarized by Badlands Media, the battalion is at the center of a U.S. national security surveillance strategy to covertly track, locate and identify anyone expressing dissent or even dissatisfaction with the actions of the U.S. military and its leadership.[1] The measures fall under the unit's remit of safeguarding top generals from "assassination, kidnapping, injury or embarrassment."[1]

Badlands highlights the inclusion of "embarrassment" in that remit as evidence that the surveillance mandate extends well beyond physical threats into the policing of speech and criticism, and treats the program as an example of the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s participation in domestic [[mass-surveillance|mass surveillance]].[1]
