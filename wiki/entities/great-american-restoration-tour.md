# Great American Restoration Tour

The Great American Restoration Tour, abbreviated GART, was a live conference organized by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and promoted heavily to the outlet's readership in April 2023.[1] In its April 17, 2023 news brief, Badlands Media issued a "last call" notice advising readers that the final batch of in-person tickets released the previous week had to be booked that day in order to guarantee a place at the conference.[1]

## Format and ticketing

The event was structured as a three-day conference. General Admission tickets covered the full run, but Badlands also offered cheaper single-day and individual event passes aimed at attendees who wanted a sampler of the programming but could not remain for the entire gathering.[1]

For supporters unable to travel, the organizers streamed the whole conference as an exclusive pay-per-view broadcast priced at a fraction of the in-person ticket.[1] Badlands framed the ticket revenue as directly funding the logistics of the event: flying the outlet's full speaker team out to Arizona, securing the venue, feeding guests, and underwriting the broader effort to "forge a new path in American citizen journalism."[1]

## Significance

GART functioned as both a fundraising vehicle and a physical gathering point for the audience Badlands calls "Badlanders," reflecting the outlet's model of distributing content free of charge while monetizing subscriptions, sponsorships, and live events.[1] The conference was held in the Arizona desert.[1]

## Inaugural stop: GART AZ (April 20–23, 2023)

The tour's first stop was held April 20–23, 2023 at the [[hilton-phoenix-chandler|Hilton Phoenix Chandler]] in [[chandler-arizona|Chandler, Arizona]], and was [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s first in-person, ticketed event, gathering Badlands personalities, show hosts, sponsors and readers.[6] A "Last Call for GART AZ!" notice published April 11, 2023 announced the release of a final batch of in-person tickets, including a small allocation of VIP Dinner places, and set a hard cutoff for in-person sales of 1pm EST on Monday, April 17.[6] Badlands expressed the hope that the Chandler gathering would be the first of "a long run of events."[6]

The promotion framed the tour as an extension of the slogan "We are the news, now," which the outlet described as a saying that had come to define a growing movement within the Truth and America First communities.[6] Badlands credited reader support with its growth into what it called one of the most-watched independent networks online, with dozens of citizen journalists, podcasters and personalities across its shows, and presented GART as taking that network "to the next level."[6]

Programming consisted of live in-person versions of Badlands Media shows, live Q&A sessions with Badlands panels, and meet-and-greets with hosts across the weekend.[6] In-person tickets included a Friday night Welcome Reception, breakfast and lunch throughout the weekend, and open time among fellow Badlanders and hosts as attendees sought to "level up in the Information War together."[6] The organisers stressed that General Admission guests would have plenty of opportunity to meet hosts, writers and personalities, the VIP dinner being an additional event only.[6] Tickets were sold through a TicketSpice page, with a separate pre-order for an exclusive live stream for those unable to travel.[6] The Chandler event was sponsored by [[firman-power-equipment|FIRMAN]], which offered Badlands readers 17% off its power equipment with the code BADLANDS.[6]
