# UK Ministry of Defence

The Ministry of Defence (MoD) is the United Kingdom government department responsible for the British armed forces, including the [[royal-air-force|Royal Air Force]].[1]

## Epstein flight records review

In February 2026 the Ministry of Defence launched a review of military flight records after revelations that the private jet of convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] may have landed at RAF bases, including RAF Northolt and RAF Marham, raising questions about whether those sites were used in connection with his criminal activities.[1]

Defence Secretary [[john-healey|John Healey]] ordered officials to search more than two decades of MoD records for evidence that Epstein used RAF facilities to traffic women into the United Kingdom.[1] Police were already examining related flight logs and airport movements at the time the review was announced.[1]

## Assange extradition conflict-of-interest reporting (2024)

Reporting published in February 2024 by *Declassified UK* and [[consortium-news|Consortium News]] found that Justice [[jeremy-johnson|Jeremy Johnson]], assigned with Dame [[victoria-sharp|Victoria Sharp]] to rule on [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]]'s extradition appeal, had represented the Ministry of Defence and the Secret Intelligence Service ([[mi6|MI6]]) before joining the bench.[2] The disclosure fuelled concerns about institutional conflicts of interest in the case.[2]
