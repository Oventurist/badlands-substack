# Ash Carter

**Ash Carter** was a United States Secretary of Defense who later led [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s [[belfer-center-for-science-and-international-affairs|Belfer Center for Science and International Affairs]], as noted in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion."[1]

The article describes the Belfer Center under Carter as functioning as "a sandbox for military-academic policy coordination," receiving grants and access from defense contractors and Department of Defense budgets and channeling students into [[pentagon|Pentagon]], [[darpa|DARPA]] and [[state-department|State Department]] fellowships.[1] Carter's tenure is offered as the clearest illustration of the article's claim that [[harvard-university|Harvard]] "operates more discreetly" than [[yale-university|Yale]] in its intelligence and defense integration within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
