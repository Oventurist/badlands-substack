# Joyce Beatty

Joyce Beatty is a member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Ohio.[1]

Beatty was the plaintiff in the litigation that produced the May 29, 2026 ruling by U.S. District Judge [[christopher-cooper|Christopher Cooper]] ordering the removal of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s name from the [[john-f-kennedy-center-for-the-performing-arts|John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts]] and blocking the venue's planned two-year renovation closure.[1] The court held that only [[congress|Congress]], which chartered and named the institution, had authority to rename it.[1]
