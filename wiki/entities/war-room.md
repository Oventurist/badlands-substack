# War Room

*War Room* is the daily political broadcast hosted by [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]], a program widely regarded within the populist right as a central organizing platform for activists, candidates and independent researchers.[1] The show functions as a venue where guests surface documents, reports and investigative claims that mainstream outlets have not covered, and its segments are routinely recirculated by allied independent publications.[1]

## Coverage in the Badlands News Brief

In April 2023, *War Room* hosted attorney [[elizabeth-yore|Elizabeth Yore]], who used the platform to publicize what she described as a [[united-nations|United Nations]] report calling for the normalization and decriminalization of pedophilia.[1] Yore's on-air statements — including her assertion that the UN was "declaring that pedophilia is a human right" — were the primary sourcing for subsequent write-ups by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and for the corresponding item in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief.[1] The episode illustrates the program's role as an upstream node in the independent-media information chain, where a broadcast interview becomes the basis for aggregation and commentary elsewhere.[1]
