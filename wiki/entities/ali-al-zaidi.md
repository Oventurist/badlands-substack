# Ali Al-Zaidi

Ali Al-Zaidi is identified in the Badlands Brief as the newly elected Prime Minister of [[iraq]]. He met President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at the [[white-house|White House]] in July 2026, an encounter at which, according to the Brief, Trump professed "his deep affection for the young leader." Following the meeting the United States announced support for an Iraq–[[syria|Syria]] oil pipeline routed to the Mediterranean, bypassing the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] and the Red Sea. The Brief situates the visit within a compressed sequence of diplomacy that also included Trump's meeting with Syrian President [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed Al-Sharaa]] at the [[nato|NATO]] summit in Ankara and reported Iran–Iraq cooperation on regional de-escalation.
