# Rafael Caro Quintero

Rafael Caro Quintero is a Mexican drug lord and former leader of the Guadalajara cartel.[1] He was involved in the 1985 kidnapping and murder of [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]] agent Enrique "Kiki" Camarena.[1]

## 2025 extradition

In late February 2025, [[mexico|Mexico]] began the process of extraditing dozens of high-level cartel leaders and members to the United States, with Caro Quintero among them.[1] The founders of [[los-zetas|Los Zetas]], Miguel and Omar Morales — known as Z-40 and Z-42 — were also slated for extradition.[1] Mexico's Attorney General's Office and Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection stated that 29 people held in various penitentiary centers were transferred to the United States because of their links to criminal organizations involved in drug trafficking and other crimes, with the custody, transfer and delivery conducted under institutional protocols, with respect for fundamental rights, and at the request of the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1]

The extraditions were carried out at the request of the U.S. government as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariff deadline approached; one of Trump's demands was that Mexico crack down on cartels and on fentanyl production and distribution.[1] Badlands commentary framed the sequence as an escalation cycle — tariff threats, Mexican objections over sovereignty, resumed tariffs, and finally the extraditions.[1]
