# Lega Nord

Lega Nord is an Italian political party whose lawmakers were among the sharpest critics of Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] following his January 2026 address to the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] in Davos.[1]

## Reaction to Zelensky's Davos speech

Zelensky used the Davos platform to criticize the [[european-union|European Union]]'s handling of security and its support for Ukraine, saying the bloc had been indecisive and must learn to better defend itself, and notably did not specifically thank EU member states for the nearly €193 billion in military and financial assistance provided since Russia's full-scale invasion began in 2022.[1]

Lega Nord lawmaker [[rossano-sasso|Rossano Sasso]] responded on social media by arguing that the EU should stop sending military aid to Ukraine, writing that the bloc now "deservedly accepts the insults" after years of financial support, and asserting that Italy should provide no more weapons or money for what he described as a war that is not Italy's.[1] The party's position contrasted with that of Italian Foreign Minister [[antonio-tajani|Antonio Tajani]], who merely called the speech "not generous," and with European Commission President [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]], who reaffirmed that the EU stands by Ukraine and that its actions "speak louder than words."[1]

## Salvini and the 2019 EU investigation

The party is led by [[matteo-salvini|Matteo Salvini]], Italy's Deputy Prime Minister and a longtime political ally of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2] In 2019 the European Union placed the party under investigation, accusing it of being funded and controlled by Vladimir Putin, although Salvini himself was not specifically targeted in that case.[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the episode as part of a wider effort by the Italian political elite to keep Salvini — despite his popularity — from the premiership, on the understanding that his ascent would align Italy with Russia and upset Europe's balance of power.[2]

## Trump branding and the 2022 coalition

In 2017 Matteo Salvini relaunched the party as Lega with branding modeled on the 2016 Trump campaign, including the templating of campaign signage, according to Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry.[3] Italian and European authorities later investigated Salvini and Lega over alleged allegiance to the Kremlin after party financing was allegedly connected to the Russian energy company Rosneft.[3] In 2022 Lega aligned with Giorgia Meloni's Brothers of Italy and other parties to elect Meloni Prime Minister of Italy.[3]
