# Kristen Nagle

Kristen Nagle is a neonatal nurse and Ontario practitioner of fourteen years who became an outspoken front-line critic of COVID-19 lockdowns and mandates in Canada.[1]

Nagle was embroiled in a legal battle with the government for four years and lost her case, leaving her saddled with more than $100,000 in fines.[1] In February 2024 a viral campaign circulated in her support, asking supporters for five-dollar donations over a two-week window to cover the fines in full — explicitly framed as a way of disrupting the [[justin-trudeau|Trudeau]] government's attempt to frighten others away from speaking out.[1]

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] cited Nagle's case alongside the [[emergencies-act|Emergencies Act]] ruling, the [[coutts-four|Coutts 4]], and the firing of podcaster BertaProudDad as evidence that both wins and losses in Canadian [[lawfare|lawfare]] were awakening ordinary Canadians to the politicization of the legal system.[1]
