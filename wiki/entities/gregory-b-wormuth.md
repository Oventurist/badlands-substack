# Gregory B. Wormuth

Gregory B. Wormuth is a federal judge in New Mexico.

## National Defense Area dismissals (2025)

In May 2025 Wormuth dismissed charges against 98 illegal immigrants who had crossed into the restricted [[national-defense-area|National Defense Area]] near the U.S.-Mexico border, ruling that they could not be prosecuted because they were reportedly unaware the land was military property.[1] "The United States provides no facts from which one could reasonably conclude that the defendant knew he was entering," he wrote, insisting that requiring intent to enter military land was necessary to avoid "absurd and unfair results."[1] The dismissals came out of roughly 400 misdemeanor charges filed over entries into the zone, which Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] had promoted as a tool for reducing illegal crossings.[1]
