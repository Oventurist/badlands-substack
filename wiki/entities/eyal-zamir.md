# Eyal Zamir

Eyal Zamir is the Chief of Staff of the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] in the 2026 reporting of the Badlands Brief, the career officer who publicly warned that the IDF could collapse under the strain of its multi-front war.

## Appointment and background

According to commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] in the March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief, Zamir was appointed by Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] after his predecessor became the scapegoat for the [[october-7|October 7]] attack. He is described as a career soldier who served on tank crews, and is therefore treated in the commentary as a credible authority on the perspective of the average IDF soldier.

## The "10 red flags" warning (March 2026)

The Brief reports, citing *[[jerusalem-post|The Jerusalem Post]]*, that Zamir warned during a security cabinet meeting that the IDF could soon collapse over its manpower shortage. "I am raising 10 red flags before the IDF collapses into itself," he told the cabinet.

His warning centered on the army's reliance on reservists for a war it was never built to fight. The IDF has reportedly been short anywhere from 10,000 to 15,000 enlisted soldiers since October 7, and the over-reliance on reserve troops has forced reservists to serve as many as six or seven combat tours since the attack. A total of 375,000 reservists have been mobilized since October 7, according to reporting cited in the Brief — a figure set to grow after the [[knesset|Knesset]] approved the mobilization of an additional 400,000.

## Strained-force analysis

Badlands commentary places Zamir's warning within a broader argument that the IDF, a relatively small fighting force unaccustomed to protracted war, is being hollowed out by an extended conflict: because reservists are Israeli citizens who make up the entire civilian economy, their repeated mobilization closes small businesses, breaks supply chains, and pushes citizens to flee the country. The commentary concludes that if the IDF does not collapse, it could mutiny against its own leadership — a risk amplified by Defense Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]]'s announced invasion of southern Lebanon and any escalation against [[iran|Iran]].
