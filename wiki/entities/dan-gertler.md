# Dan Gertler

Dan Gertler is an Israeli mining financier associated in the Badlands corpus with the capture of Congolese mineral assets through his relationship with then-president [[joseph-kabila|Joseph Kabila]].

## Congo dealings

In the [[badlands-brief-1ce|January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief]], contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recalls "the countless times I have cited Kabila, as he was the president of the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|DRC]] and in office when Dan Gertler bribed his way into a relationship with Kabila." The Gertler–Kabila relationship functions in the corpus as the origin point for the arrangements that the later United States–DRC minerals partnership under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and President [[felix-tshisekedi|Félix Tshisekedi]] is said to be displacing.
