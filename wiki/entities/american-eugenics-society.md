# American Eugenics Society

The **American Eugenics Society** was the principal organizational vehicle of the United States [[eugenics]] movement in the mid-twentieth century.[1]

In the 1950s the Rockefellers reorganized the US eugenics movement and added population control and abortion groups to it. During that same decade, Badlands Media reports, the American Eugenics Society was permitted to forcibly sterilize more than 64,000 "defective and undesirable persons."[1]

Facing backlash from its association with crimes against humanity, the society changed its name to the **Society for the Study of Social Biology**, which the article states remains active today.[1] Its work sat within a broader legal environment in which, by 1927, forced sterilization laws were on the books in 25 states.[1]
