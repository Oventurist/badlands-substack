# Jean-Marie Bockel

**Jean-Marie Bockel** is a French politician who serves as President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]]'s personal envoy for Africa.[1]

## Allegations by Niger

In a June 2025 interview with state broadcaster RTN, [[niger|Niger]]'s interim president, General [[abdourahmane-tchiani|Abdourahamane Tchiani]], accused [[france|France]] of establishing "French cells" in the Sahel region, one of them led by Bockel and tasked with countering "Sahelian influence."[1] Tchiani placed these alleged cells within a broader claimed campaign in which Paris infiltrated independence movements, then jihadist movements, and finally terrorist movements in order to destabilize Niger and impose a "new model of governance" against the will of the Nigerien people.[1]
