# Cheddi Jagan

Cheddi Jagan was the leftist leader of Guyana targeted by U.S.-backed labor operations in 1962–63.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) Graduates of the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]]'s [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]] led strikes that paralyzed his government, and CIA-funded AFL-CIO actions triggered a three-month general strike that paved the way for U.S.-backed regime change.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

The Guyana operation is presented alongside [[brazilian-coup-1964|Brazil's 1964 coup]] and the [[chilean-coup-1973|1973 Chilean coup]] as part of AIFLD's documented record in Latin America.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
