# Cheddi Jagan

Cheddi Jagan (1918–1997) was a Guyanese politician who served as Premier of British Guiana and later President of Guyana. A Marxist-leaning leader, he was a target of U.S. covert operations during the Cold War. In the Badlands Media article [[a-more-perfect-labor-union|A More Perfect (Labor) Union]], Jagan is cited as a victim of CIA-backed labor sabotage.

## The 1962–63 general strike

The article states that CIA-funded AFL-CIO actions — conducted through the [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]] — triggered a three-month general strike against Jagan's government in 1962–63, "paving the way for U.S.-backed regime change." The strike is presented as an example of AIFLD graduates using labor power to destabilize a leftist government.
