# Stephen Porges

**Dr. Stephen Porges** is identified in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] as the "architect of the [[polyvagal-theory|Polyvagal Theory]]."[1]

The essay, by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], cites Porges in its section on "The Intelligence of Embodiment" for the teaching that healing "doesn't begin with logic, it begins with safety," and that until the body feels safe, transformation remains "only conceptual."[1] When safety is established, the essay paraphrases, "the system opens, the heart entrains, and the soul emerges."[1]

His work is used to underwrite the article's claim that [[coherence|coherence]] is a physiological state — safety described as "the frequency you inhabit" rather than a destination — and appears alongside citations to [[mae-wan-ho|Mae-Wan Ho]], [[shamini-jain|Shamini Jain]], [[bruce-lipton|Bruce Lipton]] and [[rachel-naomi-remen|Rachel Naomi Remen]] in the same passage.[1]
