# Mute Egede

Mute Egede is the Prime Minister of [[greenland|Greenland]], the Arctic island and self-governing territory of the Kingdom of [[denmark|Denmark]].[1]

## New Year 2025 independence push

In his New Year speech at the start of 2025, Egede emphasized his desire to pursue independence from Denmark, Greenland's former colonial ruler — a shift the Wall Street Journal described as a significant change in the rhetoric surrounding the Arctic island's future.[1] The statement landed days after President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] reopened discussion of a US purchase of the rare-earth-rich island, writing on Truth Social that "for purposes of National Security and Freedom throughout the World, the United States of America feels that the ownership and control of Greenland is an absolute necessity," while announcing Ken Howery as US ambassador to the Kingdom of Denmark.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that globalist media had mocked Trump for raising a Greenland purchase and had tried to cast both Danes and Greenlanders as prospective Trump victims, a narrative she said took a hit when Egede used New Year's Day to declare his independence ambitions.[1] She characterized Egede as recognizing his leverage and negotiating "from a position of autonomy and strength," and speculated that future generations might view a Greenland Purchase the way Americans view the Louisiana Purchase.[1]
