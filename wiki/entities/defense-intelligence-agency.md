# Defense Intelligence Agency

The Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) is the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s military intelligence agency, responsible for producing and coordinating defense intelligence for the United States Department of Defense. In the Badlands corpus it appears as the institutional author of a disputed threat assessment concerning Israeli espionage against senior U.S. officials.

## "Critical" Israeli espionage assessment (June 2026)

The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing [[nbc-news|NBC News]] and the [[new-york-times|New York Times]], that the DIA recently elevated its assessment of the threat posed by Israeli espionage to a "critical" level. The reported assessment consisted of a seven-page document and an accompanying chart evaluating Israeli intelligence-gathering capabilities, and allegedly identified concerns about Israeli efforts to gather information regarding U.S. administration deliberations on [[iran|Iran]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]].

The reports, citing unnamed sources, named [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], [[elbridge-colby|Elbridge Colby]] and [[michael-dimino|Michael DiMino]] among officials allegedly targeted by intelligence collection efforts. The Pentagon declined comment. A [[white-house|White House]] official told NBC News that "the entire story is false," and a spokesperson for the Israeli Embassy in Washington dismissed the allegations as politically motivated, saying Israeli intelligence activities are directed at adversaries rather than allies.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats the reported assessment as a possible narrative deployment: whether or not the reporting is true, he argues, the timing "cannot be ignored" given the escalating rift between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], and public opinion in the United States turning against [[israel|Israel]].
