# James Rickards

James Rickards is an American author and commentator on monetary policy, currency markets and the international financial system.[1]

## Citation in Badlands commentary

In the January 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Master and Commander," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] embedded a short Rickards interview, describing it as a description "to perfection" of what [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had done in reshaping the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1] The reference is used to lend outside analytical weight to Lange's argument that Trump's appointments to the Board of Governors amounted to effective presidential control of the central bank.[1] The article does not quote Rickards directly or summarise his specific claims beyond the endorsement.[1]
