# Dave Smith

Dave Smith is an American comedian and a leader of the [[mises-caucus|Mises Caucus]], the right-wing faction that took control of the [[libertarian-party|Libertarian Party]] in 2022.[1]

Commenting on the party's contentious 2024 presidential nomination fight — in which the Mises-endorsed [[michael-rectenwald|Michael Rectenwald]] lost to [[chase-oliver|Chase Oliver]] after seven rounds of balloting — Smith told the Washington Post, "In a weird way, this is a microcosm of where the country is headed. Things are so polarized."[1]
