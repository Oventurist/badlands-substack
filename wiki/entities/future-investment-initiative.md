# Future Investment Initiative

The Future Investment Initiative (FII) is an international investment forum affiliated with Saudi Arabia's [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], the kingdom's sovereign wealth vehicle. Launched as a showcase for Saudi economic diversification, the FII convenes global investors, policymakers and business leaders to discuss capital flows, technology and cross-border economic opportunity. Its flagship gatherings are held in Riyadh, with a "Priority Summit" series staged in the United States.

## 2026 Miami Priority Summit

In February 2026, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] delivered remarks at the Future Investment Initiative Priority Summit in Miami, addressing an audience of global investors and officials. In his speech Trump discussed economic policy and emphasized the United States as a destination for international investment. The event drew political attention in Florida, with reporting noting scrutiny of connections between U.S. political figures and foreign-backed investment initiatives.

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that this was the second consecutive year Trump had attended and spoken at the forum, and that the Miami address closely resembled the speech he delivered in Riyadh the previous year — touting the successes of the Gulf Arab states and their intent to diversify their economies through investment in technology, infrastructure, tourism and the American economy.

Two moments from the summit circulated widely: Trump telling the audience they could ask him about "anything," including sex, and an exchange in which he appeared to needle Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], remarking that "he didn't think he would be kissing my ass" and that MBS "better be nice to me." Much of the internet read this as an insult; the Badlands commentary read it instead as evidence of unusual personal closeness, arguing that Trump would not earnestly humiliate a partner who had pledged trillions of dollars of investment into the U.S. economy at a forum that partner was hosting.
