# Jay Jones

Jay Jones is a Democratic politician and former Virginia delegate who was projected on November 4, 2025 to win election as Attorney General of Virginia, ousting Republican incumbent [[jason-miyares|Jason Miyares]] in the race for the state's top law enforcement post.[1]

## 2025 attorney general race

The contest was regarded as one of the more competitive statewide races in Virginia in the 2025 cycle.[1] Its final stretch was roiled by the resurfacing of violent text messages Jones had sent in 2022 about then-state House Speaker [[todd-gilbert|Todd Gilbert]].[1] In texts to Del. [[carrie-coyner|Carrie Coyner]], Jones wrote "Three people, two bullets," and "Gilbert, hitler, and pol pot," adding "Gilbert gets two bullets to the head."[1] He also wrote, "Spoiler: put Gilbert in the crew with the two worst people you know and he receives both bullets every time."[1]

Early voting in Virginia had already begun by the time the scandal broke in early October, and Jones staved off the controversy to win.[1] His victory came as part of a broader Democratic sweep in the first major elections of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term, alongside [[abigail-spanberger|Abigail Spanberger]]'s win in the Virginia governor's race and [[ghazala-hashmi|Ghazala Hashmi]]'s projected win as lieutenant governor.[1]

## Impact on the 2025 gubernatorial race

The fallout from the revelations continued to reverberate through Virginia's fall elections a week after they first came to light, upending the campaign at the top of the ticket.[2] During a chaotic gubernatorial debate in October 2025, Republican nominee [[winsome-earle-sears|Winsome Earle-Sears]] repeatedly called on Democratic nominee [[abigail-spanberger|Abigail Spanberger]] to tell Jones to drop out of the attorney general race, while Spanberger largely declined to engage.[2]
