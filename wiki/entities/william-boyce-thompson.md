# William Boyce Thompson

William Boyce Thompson was a director of the New York Federal Reserve who was installed as head of the [[american-red-cross|American Red Cross]] mission during the 1917 Russian revolution and was largely recognized as the true U.S. ambassador to the revolutionary government.[1]

Historian Kerry Bolton wrote that Thompson "set himself up in royal manner in Petrograd reporting directly to Pres. Wilson and bypassing U.S. Ambassador Francis."[1] According to Bolton, Thompson provided funds from his own money — first one million rubles to the Social Revolutionaries, and shortly afterward $1,000,000 to the Bolsheviks to spread their propaganda in Germany and Austria.[1]

Thompson's activity is placed alongside that of [[jacob-schiff|Jacob Schiff]] and [[alfred-milner|Lord Alfred Milner]] as part of a broader Anglo-American financing of the [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik Revolution]].[1]
