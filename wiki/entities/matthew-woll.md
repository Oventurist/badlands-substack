# Matthew Woll

Matthew Woll was a leader of the American Federation of Labor who, as Allied forces liberated Western Europe, viewed organized labor as a frontline in the ideological battle against communism.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) A staunch anti-communist, he understood — along with CIO president [[philip-murray|Philip Murray]] — that labor's collective strength could be leveraged to advance Western interests and counter Soviet influence in Europe.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

The article notes that Woll held key positions in two British intelligence fronts, the American Labor Committee to Aid British Labor and the League for Human Rights, and that historian Thomas E. Mahl suggests in his book *Desperate Deception* that Woll may have been a British intelligence contact.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) His outlook shaped the AFL's [[free-trade-union-committee|Free Trade Union Committee]] and the anti-communist orientation that the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]] carried forward after the 1955 merger.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
