# Jonathan Swan

Jonathan Swan is a reporter for The New York Times who covers the White House and national politics.[1]

## 2026 book with Maggie Haberman

Swan co-wrote a book with [[maggie-haberman|Maggie Haberman]] containing allegations about the internal dynamics of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1] Among the claims reported from the book was that Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] privately used derogatory language to describe Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] before an Oval Office meeting attended by Trump and Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], reportedly saying Zelensky "acted like Mr. Bean on crack."[1]

According to accounts of the book cited by RT and drawn from material published by The Guardian, the meeting was meant to finalize an agreement granting the United States access to Ukrainian mineral resources but deteriorated into a confrontation over U.S. policy toward Ukraine and Russia; Zelensky was asked to leave the White House and the minerals agreement was signed roughly two months later.[1]
