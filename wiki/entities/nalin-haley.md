# Nalin Haley

Nalin Haley is the Catholic son of Republican politician [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], the Indian-origin former Governor of South Carolina.[1] He emerged in 2025 as an online critic of fellow Republican [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]].[1]

## Dispute with Vivek Ramaswamy

At a Montana event hosted by [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]]'s [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]], Ramaswamy was asked about his Hindu faith and answered that he was not running to be a pastor of Ohio but its governor, describing his belief as an ethical monotheism found in Vedanta and comparing the relationship between one God and many forms to a Christian's belief in the Holy Trinity.[1]

Haley, who had previously criticized Ramaswamy over his conception of American identity, attacked the analogy, writing: "Just when you thought it couldn't get worse. Comparing the Holy Trinity to your 330 million gods is blasphemous, disrespectful, and a slap in the face to every Christian. If you're gonna run for governor in a state that is Christian, have the decency to learn our faith and not slander it."[1] Reporting noted the irony that Haley himself is the Catholic son of a family with Sikh grandparents.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary treated the exchange as a marker of identity politics inside the Republican coalition, comparing Ramaswamy's constructed political persona to those of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] and [[jd-vance|JD Vance]].[1]
