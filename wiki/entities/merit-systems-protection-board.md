# Merit Systems Protection Board

The Merit Systems Protection Board (MSPB) is an independent federal board overseeing civil service protections. In 2025 it was one of two independent labor boards at the center of litigation over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s power to remove members.[1]

## 2025 removal litigation

Trump fired MSPB member [[cathy-harris|Cathy Harris]]; a district court voided the removal and restored her to the board, as another court did for [[gwynne-wilcox|Gwynne Wilcox]] at the [[national-labor-relations-board|National Labor Relations Board]].[1] On April 9, 2025 the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] asked the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] for emergency relief, and Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] administratively stayed the lower court orders so the removals could take effect while the Court considered the case, with responses due by April 15.[1] Solicitor General [[d-john-sauer|D. John Sauer]] argued the orders harmed the presidency and the separation of powers.[1]
