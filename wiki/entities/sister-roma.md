# Sister Roma

Sister Roma is the drag persona of Michael Williams, a San Francisco–based performer and adult film director associated with the Sisters of Perpetual Indulgence, a drag troupe that adopts nun imagery and which critics characterize as anti-Catholic.[1]

## 2023 California Legislature honor

In June 2023, the California Legislature honored Sister Roma during its Pride Month celebration. State Senator [[scott-wiener|Scott Wiener]] invited the performer to the Legislature and said he was "proud of Sister Roma and her work in the community."[1] According to the Sacramento Bee, as relayed by the Washington Free Beacon, Democratic lawmakers "clapped, whooped, and cheered, giving Roma a prolonged standing ovation."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the Badlands News Brief, [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] questioned what an adult film director does "for the community" and argued that the honor left genuinely charitable people and organizations unrecognized.[1] He linked the episode to the broader backlash against drag-nun figures in California, citing the Los Angeles Dodgers Pride Night controversy, which drew objections from celebrities, players, and fans.[1] Johnny Q framed the honor as evidence that California politicians were advancing what he called an "agenda of madness," while asserting that Californians — first over COVID-era mandates on children, and then over what he described as the hypersexualization of children — were increasingly resisting.[1]
