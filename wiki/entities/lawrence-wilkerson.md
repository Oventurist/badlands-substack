# Lawrence Wilkerson

Colonel Lawrence Wilkerson is a retired United States Army officer and former [[state-department|State Department]] chief of staff who has become a frequent commentator on American foreign and military policy, appearing regularly in interviews with Judge [[andrew-napolitano|Andrew Napolitano]].[1]

## September 2024 commentary

In an interview with Napolitano titled "Can Ukraine Make It to Nov 5th?", Wilkerson discussed Ukraine, the proposed release of long-range missiles for strikes into Russia, and the September 13, 2024 meeting between President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and British Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]].[1] According to Wilkerson, Starmer was told to keep his target list in his bag because the missiles were not going to be deployed, per the [[pentagon]] — a reversal that came only about 24 hours after leaders of the State Department had publicly stated the deal was done and the missiles were coming.[1]

Wilkerson's account was independently corroborated the same day by [[scott-ritter|Scott Ritter]] and Colonel [[douglas-macgregor|Douglas Macgregor]] in separate Napolitano interviews.[1] Wilkerson also relayed that General [[michael-kurilla|Michael Kurilla]] of [[centcom]] had warned [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] the US would not back an Israeli-instigated ground war, and that the US would know if the IDF started the provocation.[1]

Badlands Media's [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used Wilkerson's account to argue that military officials had effectively placed the leaders of two of the most powerful nations on earth "into time out," and asked why a legitimate sitting president would not simply fire the [[pentagon]] officials responsible — pointing readers to [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s [[devolution]] series for the answer.[1] The clip was also played and discussed at length on Devolution Power Hour episode 286.[1]

## Napolitano interview and the usurpation thesis (2024)

In an interview with Judge [[andrew-napolitano|Andrew Napolitano]] widely circulated in September 2024, Wilkerson said that [[pentagon|Pentagon]] officials — specifically General [[michael-kurilla|Michael Kurilla]], Combatant Commander of [[centcom|CENTCOM]] — had usurped the authority of the [[white-house|White House]] and [[state-department|State Department]] and were effectively conducting US foreign policy.[2] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked the claim while rebutting reports of a US troop surge to the Middle East, arguing that the movements in question were routine and that nothing in them contradicted Wilkerson's account.[2]

## Commentary on the Kursk incursion (2024)

In late August 2024, Wilkerson told Judge Andrew Napolitano that the area of Russia targeted by the Ukrainian [[kursk-incursion|Kursk incursion]] is sparsely populated, an observation Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited in arguing that the operation was strategically pointless because the region is largely pasture, forest and a few small towns.[3]

## Claims about blocked long-range missile transfers

Badlands Media cited Wilkerson in November 2024 as having previously asserted that "certain officials" in the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] intervened in September 2024 to stop the provision and use of long-range missiles by Ukraine against targets inside Russia.[4] According to that account, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was visibly angry at being told he was not permitted to send the missiles.[4] The claim was invoked to question whether the [[biden-authorization-of-long-range-strikes-inside-russia-2024|November authorization]] was a genuine policy change or narrative escalation intended to provoke Moscow, and whether the same officials would intervene again.[4]
