# Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs

The Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs is Israel's diplomatic service. In July 2025 Badlands coverage it appears in two capacities: managing ambassadorial postings and financing public-diplomacy campaigns aimed at foreign audiences.[1]

## Personnel decisions

The ministry announced on X the appointment of Ambassador to Russia [[simona-halperin|Simona Halperin]] as deputy director-general for Europe, a move that ended her Moscow mission early. It did not specify whether she would be recalled immediately, and embassy staff indicated she would remain until at least October, with no successor named.[1] Badlands commentary argued that with Turkey posturing in Syria, the Russia–Israel relationship should be the top priority for [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s foreign ministry.[1]

## Influencer tour program

The ministry was reported to be funding a tour of Israel for U.S. social media influencers tied to the MAGA and America First conservative movements, seeking to improve Israel's image among young American conservatives by encouraging them to share messaging aligned with Israeli government policy.[1] The tour was to bring 16 U.S.-based influencers under 30 years old, each with hundreds of thousands to millions of followers, aiming to reverse a decline in pro-Israel sentiment among younger Americans. Officials noted that while older conservatives remain pro-Israel, "positive perspectives towards Israel are falling across all younger age groups."[1]

Badlands commentator Chris Paul compared the program to earlier Israeli hosting of Hollywood actors — citing comedian Bobby Lee's account of his own trip — and characterized it as giving propagandists "the star treatment in hopes they will further propagandize their followings," with participants taking money to appear in pro-Zionist commercials "as themselves."[1]

## Show Faith by Works FARA filing, 2025

Foreign Agents Registration Act filings disclosed in October 2025 identified the Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs as the principal behind [[show-faith-by-works|Show Faith by Works, LLC]], a group planning an influence campaign aimed at Christian churches and organizations in the Western United States using digital advertising, geofencing, and a "mobile museum."[2] The filing stated the group would "target and distribute pro-Israel information online and through targeted geofencing and digital online tools," reaching out to churches, pastors, and possibly Christian social media influencers, with the goal of encouraging Christians to view Israel more favorably and to visit for tourism.[2] [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] commentary condemned the effort as a propaganda campaign against Christian evangelicals and drew comparisons to twentieth-century televangelists' documented CIA ties.[2]
