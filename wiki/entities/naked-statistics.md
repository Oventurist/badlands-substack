# Naked Statistics

*Naked Statistics: Stripping the Dread from the Data* is a popular introduction to statistical reasoning cited in the Badlands Media corpus as a reference for how mainstream science and academia handle probability and hypothesis testing.[1]

## Use in Badlands commentary

In the June 27, 2024 essay "Q is Real, Because Math Says So," [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] points readers to the book when explaining the conventional 5% threshold for [[statistical-significance|statistical significance]] — the practice of rejecting a hypothesis whose likelihood of occurring falls below that level.[1] The citation is offered not as an endorsement of mainstream academia, toward which the author expresses open hostility, but to establish that the standard he is invoking against critics of the [[q-operation|Q operation]] is their own.[1]
