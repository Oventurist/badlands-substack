# Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra

The Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra is the historic monastery complex in Kyiv that became the focal point of tensions cited by Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] in his April 2026 accusation that parts of the European Union promote "rampant Satanism."[1] Ukrainian authorities have taken action at the site against clergy tied to Moscow, prompting Russian claims of religious persecution.[1]

Lavrov tied the Lavra dispute to what he described as more than a decade of persecution of the [[ukrainian-orthodox-church|Ukrainian Orthodox Church]], including churches forcibly taken over and vandalized and clergy and parishioners harassed.[1] [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and its Western allies characterize the measures as national security steps taken during the war rather than religious repression.[1]
