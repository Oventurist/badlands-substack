# Republican Jewish Coalition

The Republican Jewish Coalition (RJC) is a United States advocacy organization that promotes Jewish participation in the Republican Party and support for Israel within American conservatism. Its periodic summits function as a gathering point for Republican officeholders, donors and media figures.

In the Badlands corpus the RJC is noted as the venue at which, in November 2025, Florida politician [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]] described broadcaster [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] as "the most dangerous antisemite in America." The March 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] treats that statement as an early, public marker in the campaign that culminated months later in calls by Republican Congressman [[max-miller|Max Miller]] and activist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] for Carlson's prosecution.
