# Republican Jewish Coalition

The **Republican Jewish Coalition** (RJC) is a pro-Israel political organization within the [[republican-party|Republican Party]] identified by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a component of the right-leaning pro-Israel network whose backing he considers to have been decisive for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term.[1]

## Role in the 2024 election

In his November 2025 essay [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], DeLarme wrote that without the support of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the right-leaning pro-Israel network — including the [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] government, [[miriam-adelson|Miriam Adelson]], the RJC and [[bernie-marcus|Bernie Marcus]] — Trump's second term probably would not have materialized.[1] Pro-Israel donors are described as supplying more than $200 million, amplifying Trump's ground game in key states and solidifying his appeal among Jewish and evangelical voters.[1]

## Conditional support

DeLarme emphasized that "the RJC's infrastructure," alongside Adelson's money and Netanyahu's public and private endorsements, could as easily have been deployed against Trump had he taken a less accommodating line on [[israel|Israel]].[1] He argued that in 2024 the moment a Republican candidate showed even modest daylight on settlements, Gaza operations or confrontation with [[iran|Iran]], those dollars and that organizational muscle flipped from ally to adversary almost overnight — citing [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] in the primaries and earlier figures such as [[pat-buchanan|Pat Buchanan]] and [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]].[1] In that sense, he concluded, the support was "less a gift freely given and more a price of admission to remain viable on the Republican side in 2024."[1]
