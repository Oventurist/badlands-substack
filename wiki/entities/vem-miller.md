# Vem Miller

Vem Miller is a Las Vegas man who, at age 49, was arrested on Saturday, October 12, 2024, outside a [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] campaign rally in California's Coachella Valley while in possession of multiple firearms.[1]

## Arrest

According to a Riverside County Sheriff's Department press release, deputies found Miller carrying a shotgun, a loaded handgun, and a high-capacity magazine.[1] The arrest occurred at 4:59 p.m. at a checkpoint near Avenue 52 and Celebration Drive, roughly half a mile from the rally entrance, where Miller was stopped while driving a black SUV.[1] Authorities reported that Miller was carrying fake press and VIP passes, suggesting a possible attempt to gain closer access to the event.[1]

Riverside County Sheriff Chad Bianco, described as a noted Trump supporter, told Southern California News Group, "We probably stopped another assassination attempt."[1] The sheriff's office separately stated that "this incident did not impact the safety of former President Trump or attendees of the event."[1]

## Charges and release

Miller was booked at the John J. Benoit Detention Center on charges of possessing a loaded firearm and a high-capacity magazine.[1] He was released the same Saturday on $5,000 bail and was scheduled to appear at the Indio Larson Justice Center on January 2, 2025, according to the Riverside County Sheriff's Department inmate database.[1]
