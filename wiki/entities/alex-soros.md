# Alex Soros

Alex Soros is the son of [[george-soros|George Soros]] and his successor in the family's philanthropic and political operations. Badlands Media commentary treats his 2024 engagement announcement as a signal about internal Cabal politics.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Engagement to Huma Abedin

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] notes the announced engagement of Alex Soros and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], comparing it to the way "royal families marry into other royal families to keep peace and consolidate power."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) He asks whether the engagement was announced to calm the mayhem among the [[cabal|Cabal]] families, or whether the mayhem itself resulted from an ongoing power grab.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) The question is raised in the context of visible [[deep-state|Deep State]] disarray during July 2024, in which some figures stood by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] while others abandoned him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## Capital Research Center allegations (September 2025)

A September 2025 report by the [[capital-research-center|Capital Research Center]] stated that the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] — described as founded by [[george-soros|George Soros]] and "now run with his son Alexander" — had poured more than $80 million since 2016 into groups tied to terrorism or extremist violence.[2] Recipients named in the report included the [[center-for-third-world-organizing|Center for Third World Organizing]], the [[ruckus-society|Ruckus Society]], the [[sunrise-movement|Sunrise Movement]], the [[movement-for-black-lives|Movement for Black Lives]], and the West Bank NGO [[al-haq|Al-Haq]].[2]

## Maxed-out 2024 donation to Joe Biden

In late June 2023, Alex Soros contributed the maximum permissible $6,600 to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s re-election committee, matching an identical donation from his father, [[george-soros|George Soros]]; the pair's checks were their first involvement in the 2024 presidential cycle.[3] Neither had given to Biden's Victory Fund at that point.[3]

## Meetings with Democratic officials during the Trump trial (2024)

In May 2024, investigative reporter [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] reported that Alex Soros — described as the son and chosen successor of billionaire [[george-soros|George Soros]] — met with Michigan Governor [[gretchen-whitmer|Gretchen Whitmer]] and various Arizona Democrats after Whitmer's political action committee and the Arizona Democratic Party contracted [[authentic-campaigns|Authentic Campaigns]] as a vendor.[4] The firm is run by [[loren-merchan|Loren Merchan]], daughter of New York judge [[juan-merchan|Juan Merchan]], who was then presiding over [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s prosecution by District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]]; the meetings reportedly occurred while that trial was under way.[4] Soros has visited [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s White House at least twenty times.[4]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited the episode as illustrating how, in his view, billionaire oligarchs such as the Soros family use money and clout to control government and society, a dynamic he characterized as an organized crime syndicate.[4]

## Vatican audience with Bill Clinton (July 2023)

Soros accompanied former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] to a private audience with Pope [[pope-francis|Francis]] at the Casa Santa Marta papal residence on Wednesday, July 5, 2023, as part of a delegation of prominent Americans.[5] He had also travelled with Clinton in Albania on July 3–4, where Clinton received a public gratitude medal from the Albanian prime minister for his support of the country and for NATO's role in the Kosovo War.[5] Reporting identified Soros as the son of billionaire financier [[george-soros|George Soros]] and the new chairman of the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], the philanthropic giant his father founded.[5]
