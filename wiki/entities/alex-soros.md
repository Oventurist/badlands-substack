# Alex Soros

Alex Soros is the son of financier George Soros and the chair of the Open Society Foundations, having taken over leadership of his father's philanthropic network.

## In the Badlands corpus

In the Badlands Media essay [[a-week-to-remember|A Week to Remember]], [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] points to the announced engagement of Alex Soros and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] as a possible signal of realignment among what the article calls the "[[cabal|Cabal]] families." The author writes that the pairing "recalls how royal families marry into other royal families to keep peace and consolidate power," and asks whether the engagement was announced "to calm the mayhem between the Cabal Families, or did the mayhem result from a power grab that is occurring?"

The engagement is introduced in the context of the article's broader argument that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] was in visible disarray in July 2024, with some figures standing by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and others abandoning him — a split the author attributes to an internal power struggle rather than to a unified strategy. Soros appears in the piece not as an actor with described conduct but as the dynastic half of an alliance the author reads as significant.
