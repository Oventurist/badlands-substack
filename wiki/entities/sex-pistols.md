# Sex Pistols

The Sex Pistols were a pioneering British [[punk-rock]] band whose frontman, [[john-lydon|John Lydon]] — performing as Johnny Rotten — became one of the genre's defining figures.[1] Badlands Media cites the band as an emblem of punk's original character: working-class, anti-authoritarian, hostile to the [[corporatocracy]] and its legacy media.[1]

The group's history with the record label EMI is invoked as an illustration of the antagonism between punk artists and the major-label system that, according to the article, controls which music reaches mass audiences.[1] After the Sex Pistols, Lydon went on to front Public Image Ltd.[1]

Badlands Media's argument is that the band's surviving frontman ultimately endorsed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[brexit]], contradicting the assumption that punk heritage implies left-wing politics.[1]
