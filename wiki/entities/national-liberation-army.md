# National Liberation Army (ELN)

The National Liberation Army (Ejército de Liberación Nacional, ELN) is an armed group that has operated in Colombia and the surrounding region since 1963, historically in cooperation with [[pablo-escobar|Pablo Escobar]] and the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín Cartel]].[1]

## Petro's proxy disclosure

Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] made waves roughly a year before January 2026 when he disclosed that the ELN was in fact a proxy of the Mexican [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]], dispatched to secure the routes through Central America used for cocaine smuggling.[1]

## Maduro allegations

Badlands recorded that what it termed the NeoCons and Zionists have accused [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] of being the head of the ELN, while providing no evidence to support those claims.[1] The organization's leadership question is therefore central to the US case framing Maduro as a cartel figure — a case that Badlands reported may rest on the testimony of a single Venezuelan general operating under a plea deal.[1]
