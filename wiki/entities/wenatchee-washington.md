# Wenatchee, Washington

Wenatchee is a small city in Eastern Washington described by Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as having a population of around 35,000 and being located "out in the middle of nowhere."[1] Its economy is associated with apple orchard warehouses and processing facilities, and it is in that industrial setting that Carlson places a supercomputer built by [[blackrock|BlackRock]] during the 1990s.[1]

## Role in the Aladdin account

Carlson recounted visiting Eastern Washington on a family camping trip, which prompted him to think about BlackRock and the machine it sited there.[1] He assumed the firm chose Wenatchee because it wanted isolation while retaining access to adequate power grids — the same combination of cheap hydroelectric power and low population density that has historically drawn data-intensive operations to the Columbia River basin.[1]

On that supercomputer, in his telling, BlackRock developed [[aladdin|Aladdin]], a software system built around a [[monte-carlo-simulation|Monte Carlo simulator]] that runs vast numbers of scenarios to identify the most probable outcome, first used internally to pick stocks and other investments and then licensed to outside firms from 1999.[1] Wenatchee therefore functions in Carlson's argument as the physical, unremarkable location from which a globally consequential predictive capability was operated for decades without public attention.[1]
