# DLA Piper

DLA Piper is a Washington-headquartered international law firm that appears in the Badlands Media article "The Biggest Boomerang In History" as the alleged financial conduit linking the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] to the family of former [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[james-comey|James Comey]].[1]

## Clinton connections

Citing Big League Politics, [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] reports that DLA Piper is one of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s top ten all-time career campaign donors and that the firm prepares the Clinton Foundation's taxes.[1] When the Clinton Foundation scandal broke, DLA Piper performed the 2015 audit of the Foundation, which the article says was supposed to be an independent audit undertaken for the appearance of propriety.[1]

## Peter Comey's role

[[peter-comey|Peter Comey]], James Comey's brother, is described as serving at DLA Piper as "Senior Director of Real Estate Operations for the Americas," with a source confirming shortly before the 2016 election that the firm's Chinatown offices patched callers directly through to his line.[1] Because property records reportedly show James Comey holding the mortgage on his brother's Virginia house, Lange argues the FBI director had a direct financial relationship with a DLA Piper executive while investigating Clinton.[1]

## Q drop 4782 and the "pay to play" mechanism

Q drop 4782 of September 28, 2020, quoted in the article, sets out a four-step laundering pattern: a supporter's family member is hired by a Clinton Foundation–engaged law firm; the Foundation "donates" funds — primarily of foreign origin and under nominally legal payments — to the engaged firm, named in the drop as DLA Piper; the firm pays salary, bonus and other compensation to the family member; and the family member transfers value back to the supporter's campaign, foundation, assets or home.[1] Lange identifies the "supporter" as James Comey and characterises the structure as money laundering intended to conceal [[pay-for-play|pay to play]] schemes.[1] These are Badlands Media allegations rather than adjudicated findings.
