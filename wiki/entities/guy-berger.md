# Guy Berger

Guy Berger is the director of economic research at the [[burning-glass-institute|Burning Glass Institute]], a labor-analytics firm.[1]

In August 2024 Berger was quoted by [[business-insider|Business Insider]] on the deterioration of the United States labor market following the July employment report, which showed 114,000 jobs added against expectations of 176,000 and unemployment rising to 4.3 percent.[1] Characterizing the shift in the data, he said: "There was a lot of data that was sort of in the greenish area, and now there's a lot more data that's flashing yellow lights."[1] His comment was reproduced in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as part of its coverage of [[july-2024-us-jobs-report|the July 2024 US jobs report]] and of concerns that the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] had fallen behind the curve by holding rates steady.[1]
