# Jesus

**Jesus** appears in the Badlands Brief article "Kekistani Code Talkers" as the subject of its first historical case study, in which His parables are analyzed as cryptographic puzzles.[1]

## Parables as cryptographic puzzles

In the Bible, Jesus uses parables as relatable metaphors to communicate heavenly things to earthly beings.[1] Asked about their use, He delineates two groups: those around Him and His disciples, who could see His message within the parables, and those who are outside, who could not.[1] The article reads this as an indication that Jesus intentionally used parables knowing the former possessed the key to unlock His message — the key being a relationship with Him.[1] Other cryptographic keys in the Bible are suggested as well, including faith, prayer, and worship.[1]

## Information warfare reading

Being the Word (the Logos) of God and the Truth, everything about Jesus is characterized as [[information-warfare|information warfare]].[1] Had those outside understood His message at the time, the article argues, they would have moved more quickly to persecute and kill Him in an attempt to stop Him; the parables therefore functioned as a form of memetic warfare that simultaneously grew His network, propagated His message, and protected the network from attackers.[1] The author compares them to Biblical [[qanon|Q]] drops.[1]
