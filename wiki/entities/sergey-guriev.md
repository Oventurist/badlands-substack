# Sergey Guriev

Sergey Guriev is a Russian economist and outspoken critic of Russia's military operation against Ukraine who has lived outside Russia for years.[1]

## Terrorist and extremist designation

In November 2025, Russian authorities added Guriev, together with former Prime Minister [[mikhail-kasyanov|Mikhail Kasyanov]], to the official list of "terrorists and extremists."[1] The designation was published by the financial watchdog [[rosfinmonitoring|Rosfinmonitoring]] without any accompanying explanation.[1] Both men had been persistent public critics of the Kremlin's campaign against Kiev and were living abroad at the time of the listing.[1]
