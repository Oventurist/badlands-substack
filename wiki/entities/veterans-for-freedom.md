# Veterans for Freedom

**Veterans for Freedom** was a pro-war lobbying group led during the George W. Bush administration by [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], later nominated as Secretary of Defense by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after the 2024 election.[1]

## Role in the Iraq War debate

Badlands Media noted that Hegseth, then the head of Veterans for Freedom, appeared with [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] at the White House to support Bush's plan to escalate and extend the Iraq War.[1] Contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described the association as "not damning in itself, but something to consider going forward" when assessing Hegseth's suitability to lead the Pentagon.[1]

## Significance

The group's history features in the debate within the populist right over whether Trump's 2024 defense pick represented a genuine break with the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] or a continuation of interventionist habits.[1] DeLarme paired the Veterans for Freedom record with Hegseth's statements on the [[third-temple|Third Temple]] and on Israeli annexation of the West Bank, while acknowledging that Hegseth is strong on veterans' issues and that people he trusts vouch for him.[1]
