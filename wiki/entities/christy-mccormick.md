# Christy McCormick

Christy McCormick was a Republican commissioner of the U.S. [[election-assistance-commission|Election Assistance Commission]].[1]

McCormick resigned from the commission in July 2026, as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] moved on July 9 to clear out the agency's remaining membership.[1] Her departure, combined with the email terminations of Democratic commissioners [[thomas-hicks|Thomas Hicks]] and [[benjamin-hovland|Benjamin Hovland]] and the earlier voluntary exit of Republican [[donald-palmer|Donald Palmer]], left the four-seat body without a single sitting commissioner.[1]

Under the [[help-america-vote-act|Help America Vote Act of 2002]], the commission's four presidentially appointed and Senate-confirmed members must be divided evenly between the two major political parties, so McCormick's seat was one of the two reserved for Republicans.[1]
