# Elvira Nabiullina

Elvira Nabiullina is the governor of the central bank of [[russia|Russia]].[1]

## Role in Russia's crypto trade law

In late July 2024, Russian lawmakers passed a bill permitting businesses to use cryptocurrencies in international trade as part of an effort to circumvent Western sanctions imposed after the invasion of Ukraine.[1] Nabiullina was one of the backers of the new law, and she said the first transactions in cryptocurrencies would take place before the end of the year.[1] The law was expected to enter into force in September 2024.[1]

Under the legislation, the central bank Nabiullina heads was tasked with creating a new "experimental" infrastructure for cryptocurrency payments, the details of which had yet to be announced.[1] The measure formed part of a package that also covered the mining of cryptocurrencies and the circulation of other digital assets, while leaving intact the existing ban on cryptocurrency payments inside Russia.[1]

The move followed significant delays in Russia's international payments with major trading partners including [[china|China]], India and the United Arab Emirates, as banks in those countries grew more cautious under pressure from Western regulators.[1]
