# Kuwait-American Corporation

The **Kuwait-American Corporation** was an investment vehicle in which [[marvin-bush|Marvin Bush]], the youngest son of [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]], was a major shareholder from 1993 to 2000.[1] A fellow major shareholder was Mishal Youssef Saud al Sabah, a member of the Kuwaiti royal family.[1]

According to the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] material quoted in the article, the corporation held positions in several U.S. defense, aviation and industrial security companies.[1] Badlands Media commentary treats this as a case of a [[kuwait|Kuwaiti]]-linked entity holding stakes in firms of the American military-industrial complex while a presidential son sat on the shareholder register, presenting it as a channel by which the [[bush-family|Bush family]] profited from Middle Eastern war spending.[1]

## Assessment

The corporate facts are drawn from the quoted newspaper reporting; the inference of wrongdoing is the author's. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
