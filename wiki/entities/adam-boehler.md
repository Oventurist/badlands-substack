# Adam Boehler

Adam Boehler is the US hostage envoy in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, best known in March 2025 for conducting unprecedented direct talks between the United States and [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

## Doha meeting with Hamas (March 2025)

Boehler met in Doha with a senior Hamas delegation led by [[khalil-al-hayya|Khalil al-Hayya]] to discuss the potential release of American hostages held in Gaza.[1] The meeting was unprecedented, as the United States had historically refused direct negotiations with Hamas, and it proceeded without Israel being looped in beforehand.[1] Israel's Strategic Affairs Minister [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]] "lashed out" at Boehler in a phone call once Israel learned of the meeting, objecting in particular to Boehler's discussion of the number of Palestinian prisoners Israel would release in exchange for the five Israeli-American hostages still in Gaza — one alive and four believed dead — without Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s consent.[1]

Boehler responded that these were only initial discussions and that nothing would be finalized without Israel's approval.[1] Hours later, news of his meeting with al-Hayya was leaked to the press, a leak the United States attributed to Israel and which further degraded trust between the two governments.[1] The decision to exclude Israel followed Netanyahu's opposition when the concept was first raised with him a month earlier; Israel was especially opposed to any meeting held without preconditions for Hamas.[1] Israel avoided public criticism of Washington but issued a terse statement noting that it had "expressed to the United States its position regarding direct talks with Hamas."[1]
