# Clear Ballot

Clear Ballot is an American election technology vendor whose ballot scanning and tabulation equipment is used by a number of state and county jurisdictions, including in Colorado. The company drew attention in late October 2024 when partial BIOS passwords for voting equipment in Colorado were found to have been posted publicly on the Secretary of State's website.[1]

## Colorado password disclosure

The Colorado Secretary of State's office "improperly" published a spreadsheet on its public website that included passwords to some of the state's voting systems.[1] Reporting by Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who also covered the story for Colorado Free Press, noted that the exposure affected equipment from both [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] and Clear Ballot, and that explanations offered by Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] were contradicted by sworn testimony from her own staff.[1]

Ashe argued that the episode looked less like a simple administrative error than a set-up, suggesting the regime was "throwing Jena under the bus" and that the resulting saga would be used to fast-track [[ranked-choice-voting|ranked choice voting]] and elevate figures in Colorado government.[1] The incident occurred alongside the disclosure by Michigan Secretary of State [[jocelyn-benson|Jocelyn Benson]] of a nationwide programming issue with Dominion voter assist terminals, reinforcing a broader narrative that the election system was incapable of producing a result the public would accept as legitimate.[1]
