# Punchbowl News

Punchbowl News is a Washington, DC–based subscription news outlet focused on the internal workings of Congress and the legislative process. Its reporting is frequently cited by other outlets for early details on committee activity and leadership decisions.

In the March 26, 2026 Badlands Brief, Punchbowl is cited as the source for reporting that the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] had requested records from the [[house-intelligence-committee|House Intelligence Committee]] tied to former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]]. The cited passage notes that Brennan served as CIA director from 2013 to 2017 and "set up the counterintelligence investigation into Trump in 2016," and that [[donald-trump|Trump]] stripped Brennan of his security clearance in 2018 and again in 2025.
