# Paula Feroleto

Paula Feroleto is a Justice of the Erie County Supreme Court in New York State who issued a closely watched ruling permitting a lawsuit against several major social media companies to proceed.[1]

## The Buffalo social media ruling

On Monday, March 18, 2024, Feroleto denied a motion to dismiss a suit alleging that social media platforms contributed to the radicalization of [[payton-gendron|Payton Gendron]], the gunman who killed ten people at a Buffalo grocery store in 2022.[1] In her decision she wrote that "the Court has determined the complaint sufficiently pleads viable causes of action to go forward at this stage of the litigation."[1]

The ruling allowed plaintiffs to press claims against companies including [[meta-platforms|Meta]], [[google|Alphabet]], [[reddit|Reddit]] and [[4chan]], rejecting — at least at the pleading stage — the defense argument that the platforms are merely message boards displaying third-party content.[1] Critics in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] orbit warned that a plaintiff victory would supply a pretext for expanded platform censorship of conservative political speech.[1]
