# Anti-Defamation League

The **Anti-Defamation League** (ADL) is an advocacy organization that Badlands Media characterizes as one of the "watchdog" — in the author's framing, attack-dog — groups activated as part of the quadrennial censorship offensive.[1]

## Campaign against Elon Musk

After [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] refused to satisfy the ADL's demands, the organization mounted a pressure effort against him, comparable in kind to the advertiser-targeting campaigns waged against [[rumble|Rumble]] and Dave Portnoy.[1] Badlands states that its prior article on the ADL, "The Anti-Dissent League," is directly related to the censorship dynamics discussed here and forms part of the same coordinated offensive that recurs every four years.[1]
