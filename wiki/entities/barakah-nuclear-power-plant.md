# Barakah Nuclear Power Plant

The Barakah Nuclear Power Plant is the first nuclear power station in the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] and the first in the Arab world, located in the Al Dhafra region of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi. Within the Badlands corpus it appears as the target of a May 2026 drone strike amid the broader [[iran|Iran]] conflict.

## The May 17, 2026 drone strike

According to the May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, a drone strike caused a fire near the plant on May 17, 2026. Emirati authorities said there were no injuries or radioactive leaks. The UAE defense ministry said three drones entered the country from the western border direction, with two intercepted and one striking an electrical generator outside the plant's inner perimeter. The UAE blamed Iran or allied proxy groups for the incident; presidential adviser [[anwar-gargash|Anwar Gargash]] called the strike a "dangerous escalation" that violated international law and norms. [[international-atomic-energy-agency|International Atomic Energy Agency]] Director General [[rafael-grossi|Rafael Grossi]] said military activity threatening nuclear safety was "unacceptable," while the UAE's nuclear regulator said there was no impact on reactor operations or public safety.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that Israel "staunchly opposes any Muslim country possessing nuclear technology" and that "the intended target of the attack was the nuclear power plant in the UAE," presenting the strike as a provocation designed to set Muslim nations against one another.
