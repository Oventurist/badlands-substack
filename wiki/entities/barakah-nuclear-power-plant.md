# Barakah Nuclear Power Plant

The Barakah nuclear power plant is a civilian nuclear generating station in the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], located in the Al Dhafra region.[1]

## Drone strike of 17 May 2026

Emirati authorities reported that a drone strike caused a fire near the plant on 17 May 2026, with no injuries and no radioactive leaks.[1] The UAE defense ministry said three drones entered the country from the western border direction; two were intercepted and one struck an electrical generator outside the plant's inner perimeter.[1] The UAE's nuclear regulator said there was no impact on reactor operations or public safety, and investigations into the drones' origin continued.[1]

The UAE blamed [[iran|Iran]] or allied proxy groups, with presidential adviser [[anwar-gargash|Anwar Gargash]] calling the strike a "dangerous escalation" that violated international law and norms.[1] [[international-atomic-energy-agency|IAEA]] Director General [[rafael-grossi|Rafael Grossi]] said military activity threatening nuclear safety was "unacceptable" after speaking with UAE officials.[1]

## Contested attribution

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that because the drones came from the west — the direction of [[israel|Israel]] and [[iraq|Iraq]], not Iran — and because the intended target was a Muslim country's nuclear facility, which Israel staunchly opposes, the attack was more plausibly an attempt to provoke war among Muslim nations to Israel's benefit.[1] He linked the incident to reports of secret Israeli bases built in remote parts of Iraq, discovered by the Bedouin shepherd [[awad-al-shammari|Awad al-Shammari]], who was reportedly killed in a helicopter strike after reporting them.[1]
