# Corey Comperatore

Corey Comperatore was the rally attendee killed during the July 13, 2024 assassination attempt on former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Pennsylvania.[1]

## Death and commemoration

The shooting at the July 13 rally left one attendee dead and two others critically injured.[1] Badlands coverage identified Comperatore as the attendee who "tragically died from the assassin's bullet."[1]

At the [[house-homeland-security-committee|House Homeland Security Committee]] hearing of July 23, 2024, members observed a moment of silence for Comperatore and for those injured in the attack, following an initial moment of silence for the late Representative [[sheila-jackson-lee|Sheila Jackson Lee]].[1]

## Context

Comperatore's death became a central reference point in the accountability campaign that followed the attack, culminating the same week in the resignation of [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] Director [[kimberly-cheatle|Kimberly Cheatle]], who acknowledged that the agency had "fallen short" of its protective mission on July 13.[1]
