# Richard Marles

Richard Marles is the Defense Minister of Australia.[1]

In August 2024, Marles joined Australian Foreign Minister [[penny-wong|Penny Wong]] in meetings with US Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] amid intensive American diplomacy aimed at preventing a regional war between [[iran|Iran]] and [[israel|Israel]] after the assassinations of [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]] and [[fuad-shukr|Fuad Shukr]].[1] Speaking at the conclusion of that meeting, Blinken described Washington as "engaged in intense diplomacy with allies and partners," having conveyed de-escalation messages directly to both Tehran and Jerusalem.[1] The exchange was reported by the [[times-of-israel|Times of Israel]] and carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
