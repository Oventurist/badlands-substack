# Aaron Russo

Aaron Russo was an American filmmaker who, in the years before his death, became known in alternative-media circles for a series of interviews in which he described private conversations with members of the international financial elite about their long-term plans for population control.[1]

## The RFID "chipping" claim

In an interview given shortly before his death, Russo claimed that Nicholas Rockefeller — a figure he associated with the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], the [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]] and the International Institute of Strategic Studies — had told him as far back as the late 1990s that the goal of the international elite was to get everybody "chipped" with an RFID chip.[1]

Badlands Media frames Russo as an early whistleblower on what would later become the [[digital-identification|digital identification]] agenda, noting that he raised the subject nearly twenty years before it entered mainstream policy discussion.[1] At the time, anyone repeating the claim "would be met with smirks and jeers and told that 'it will never happen'"; the idea that Americans would accept being chipped seemed preposterous in the early 2000s.[1]

## Retrospective assessment

According to the Badlands account, the COVID-19 pandemic changed public receptivity dramatically: populations that had scoffed at the chipping claim became willing to defer to health authorities, the federal government and other perceived authority figures, producing a culture hostile to dissent in which constitutional overreach was tolerated and even celebrated.[1] The article cites a widely circulated social-media video in which a young user enthusiastically promotes having had a microchip implanted in her arm, presenting what was once decried as conspiratorial paranoia as a fashionable trend.[1]

Russo's claim is therefore treated by Badlands as a "multiple decades-old 'conspiracy theory' turned reality," and as an interpretive frame for reading later digital-ID initiatives by the [[european-union|European Union]], the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1]
