# David Banks

David Banks served as New York City's Commissioner of Schools during the administration of Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] and was identified in September 2024 as part of the cluster of city officials connected to a federal investigation that produced raids on officials' homes and the resignation of Police Commissioner [[edward-caban|Edward Caban]].[1]

Banks is the brother of [[philip-banks-iii|Philip Banks III]], the deputy mayor for public safety, and of [[terrence-banks|Terrence Banks]], who operates the Pearl Alliance consultancy.[1] He is engaged to Deputy Mayor [[sheena-wright|Sheena Wright]], a lawyer whose home was also raided.[1]

Badlands presented the Banks family's simultaneous control of the schools portfolio, the public-safety deputy mayoralty, a deputy-mayor fiancée, and a firm representing city contractors as an illustration of interlocking nepotism inside New York City government.[1]
