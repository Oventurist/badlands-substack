# Tzahi Hanegbi

Tzahi Hanegbi is an Israeli official who served as National Security Adviser to Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]

## June 2025 Washington visit

Hanegbi travelled to Washington in early June 2025 alongside Strategic Affairs Minister [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]] and [[mossad|Mossad]] Director [[david-barnea|David Barnea]].[1] During that visit the Israeli delegation conveyed reassurances to the [[white-house|White House]] that Israel would not launch an attack on Iran's nuclear facilities unless President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signaled that negotiations with Iran had failed.[1] The message responded to weeks of concern within the Trump administration that Israel was preparing a strike despite ongoing talks; Trump said he had cautioned Netanyahu against acting while negotiations continued, while also noting his stance "could change with a phone call" if he concluded the diplomacy was going nowhere.[1]
