# Frank-Walter Steinmeier

Frank-Walter Steinmeier is the President of [[germany|Germany]] and one of the European leaders quoted in the Badlands corpus as objecting to the direction of US foreign policy under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## Warnings about the global order

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Steinmeier said US policy under Trump is helping erode the global order, pointing to the operation against [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] among developments he described as destabilizing. He warned "against turning global politics into 'a den of robbers.'"

## Badlands response

The commentator [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] responds dismissively — "Perhaps, fraulein, it's always been that way? And President Trump is merely pulling back the curtain?" — treating Steinmeier's complaint as confirmation that the [[rules-based-international-order|rules-based international order]] is genuinely being dismantled, an outcome the author welcomes. Steinmeier's remarks are grouped in the Brief with those of French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]], EU foreign policy chief [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]], and Slovakian President [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]].
