# Frank-Walter Steinmeier

Frank-Walter Steinmeier is the President of Germany. In January 2026 he publicly warned that United States policy under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was helping erode the global order, citing the US operation that captured Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] among the developments he described as destabilizing.[1]

## "A den of robbers"

Steinmeier accused the United States of undermining the world order and warned against turning global politics into "a den of robbers."[1] His comments were paired in press coverage with those of French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]], who lamented that the US was turning away from its allies.[1]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry dismissed the complaint, suggesting that great-power politics had always operated on the basis of raw capability and that Trump was merely pulling back the curtain on an arrangement that predated him.[1]
