# Michael DiMino

Michael DiMino is a United States defense official serving as Deputy Assistant Secretary of War for the Middle East, a post in which he acts as the principal policy advisor on defense and security issues pertaining to the Middle East and is responsible for more than a dozen countries. He was sworn in at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] on January 20, 2025.

## Views on Israel

The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that DiMino became a target of scrutiny by the Israeli press upon his hiring, owing to his past rhetoric and his position that [[israel|Israel]] is a security liability for the United States and that the US should abandon it as an ally and withdraw all support. Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] notes that Badlands Media took interest in DiMino from the day of his swearing-in, and argues that his portfolio makes him a logical target of interest for [[mossad|Mossad]].

## Reported Israeli intelligence targeting

DiMino is named, alongside [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and [[elbridge-colby|Elbridge Colby]], among U.S. officials allegedly targeted by Israeli intelligence collection in the [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] assessment reported by [[nbc-news|NBC News]] and the [[new-york-times|New York Times]], which allegedly elevated the Israeli espionage threat to "critical." The [[white-house|White House]] called the story entirely false, and the Israeli Embassy in Washington dismissed the allegations as politically motivated. GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argues that the inclusion of DiMino makes the report "more than plausible" even if it originated as fake news, and reads the episode through the lens of the escalating rift between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].
