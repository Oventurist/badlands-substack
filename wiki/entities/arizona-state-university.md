# Arizona State University

Arizona State University (ASU) is a large American public research university identified as a central node in the modern intelligence/academia nexus.[1]

## Intelligence ties

ASU is cited alongside [[yale-university|Yale]] and Cambridge as an example of an institution hosting [[pentagon|Pentagon]]-, [[state-department|State Department]]-, or [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]-funded programs that analyze and target "disinformation."[1] The university's president, [[michael-crow|Michael Crow]], is also chairman of [[in-q-tel|In-Q-Tel]], the CIA's venture capital arm.[1]

## Center on Narrative, Disinformation & Strategic Influence

[[mike-benz|Mike Benz]], a former cybersecurity official at the State Department turned whistleblower and educator, has claimed that ASU's [[center-on-narrative-disinformation-and-strategic-influence|Center on Narrative, Disinformation & Strategic Influence]] functions as one of many CIA cut-outs, creating databases of pro-Trump social media content and developing "intelligence dashboards" used to shape censorship policies.[1]

ASU's role is presented as part of a broader pattern in which more than 60 U.S. universities receive roughly $100 million annually from the [[national-science-foundation|National Science Foundation]], [[usaid|USAID]] and other agencies to establish "disinformation studies" programs.[1]
