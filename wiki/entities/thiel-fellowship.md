# Thiel Fellowship

The Thiel Fellowship is a program of the Thiel Foundation, the philanthropic vehicle of [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], offering grants to young people to pursue technology ventures outside conventional higher education.[1]

[[jim-oneill|Jim O'Neill]] was a co-founder of the Thiel Fellowship and served as CEO of the Thiel Foundation from 2008 to 2012, overlapping with his tenure as a Managing Director at [[clarium-capital|Clarium Capital]].[1] The role features in Badlands commentary on O'Neill's trajectory from federal speechwriter to Thiel-world financier and back into government as Deputy Secretary of the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]].[1]
