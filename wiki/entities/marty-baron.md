# Marty Baron

Marty Baron is an American newspaper editor, formerly executive editor of the [[boston-globe|Boston Globe]] and later of [[washington-post|The Washington Post]].[1] He is best known for leading the Pulitzer Prize–winning Globe investigation into the cover-up of clerical child molestation in the Catholic Church.[1]

## Reaction to the 2024 Post non-endorsement

Baron emerged as one of the most prominent critics of The Washington Post's decision, announced October 25, 2024, not to endorse a candidate in that year's presidential election.[1] Writing on X, he called the move "cowardice, with democracy as its casualty," and added that "@realdonaldtrump will see this as an invitation to further intimidate owner @jeffbezos (and others). Disturbing spinelessness at an institution famed for courage."[1] His remarks were reported by the Boston Globe on October 25, 2024.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterised Baron's statement as treating the non-endorsement as a dereliction of the Post's supposed duty to support the Democratic establishment, and argued that his condemnation was hypocritical given that [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], as a San Francisco prosecutor, was in DeLarme's account complicit in the handling of multi-decade abuse allegations against the San Francisco Archdiocese — precisely the category of wrongdoing Baron's celebrated reporting had exposed.[1] DeLarme placed Baron within the broader wave of establishment media figures who reacted with outrage to the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|2024 newspaper non-endorsements]].[1]
