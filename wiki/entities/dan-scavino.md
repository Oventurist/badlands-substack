# Dan Scavino

Dan Scavino is a longtime aide to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and served as White House Deputy Chief of Staff for Communications during the Trump administration. He is best known within the Truth Community for his social-media posting, which followers parse for coded or anticipatory messaging.[1]

## Role in the "Biden returns" narrative (August 2024)

In August 2024, Trump posted on Truth Social speculating that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] — whom he called "Crooked Joe" and described as having had the presidency "Unconstitutionally STOLEN from him by Kamabla, Barrack HUSSEIN Obama, Crazy Nancy Pelosi, Shifty Adam Schiff, Cryin' Chuck Schumer, and others on the Lunatic Left" — might crash the Democratic National Convention and attempt to reclaim the nomination.[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the significance of the episode lay less in its plausibility than in who was seeding it, noting that both [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] and Scavino were amplifying the same narrative.[1]

In that reading, coordinated messaging by Trump, Flynn, and Scavino functioned as an instrument of psychological pressure on the Deep State rather than a literal prediction; if Biden actually did turn against his former allies, it would be treated as among the most definitive available evidence for [[devolution|Devolution]], short of explicit disclosure by Trump himself.[1] Even absent that outcome, the argument ran, the narrative deployment alone was sufficient to make opponents "think twice," and that hesitation might be enough to disrupt their plans around the election.[1]

## "Enter Sandman" post (2025)

In May 2025 Scavino posted a video of Metallica's "Enter Sandman" to X, a message widely read as a signal by Trump-aligned commentators.[2] Badlands contributor [[brad-cologero-getz|Brad Cologero Getz]] noted that Scavino is from New York, not far from the Bronx, where for nearly two decades the song played over the loudspeaker whenever the Yankees held a lead and were about to close out a game.[2] From 1996 to 2013 in New York, Getz wrote, "Enter Sandman" meant simply: "Game Over."[2]

## Trump Train posting

Scavino's long record of posts referencing the [[trump-train|Trump Train]] is the central corpus analysed in the Badlands Brief article "Pepe, the Trump Train, and Hillary's Automobile," which states that a survey of his account yields several dozen such data points.[3] Among the earliest are a Tweet of 3/11/16 referencing Periscope, the Twitter-based live-video service, and a Tweet of 3/21/16 declaring "BTC for the Trump Train! CHOO CHOO!" — read by the author as an early [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] signal, with the number 21 tied to Bitcoin's 21 million supply cap.[3] A Truth Social post of 3/15/24 resurfacing a Tweet from eight years earlier is presented as connecting the Trump Train to [[covfefe|Covfefe]], and a post of 5/26/24 referencing a Tweet from eight years prior is matched by timestamp to Q drop #3521.[3]

A post of 10/31/20 is highlighted for falling eleven years after the release of the Bitcoin whitepaper by [[satoshi-nakamoto|Satoshi Nakamoto]].[3] In September 2020 Scavino marveled at the length of the Trump Train passing through Laredo, Texas, an event reported as stretching more than fifteen miles along IH-35 with some 7,000 participants.[3]

## Oranges and the Jackson signal

Scavino's posts of 5/13/24, which classified images of oranges with rocket-ship and freedom symbols, are treated in the same article as a memetic reference to Bitcoin, the 5/13 date read against the Covfefe digits 1, 3 and 5.[3] The author notes he shared the post on X at 10:37 PM and on Truth Social at 10:38 PM, and maps those times onto [[qanon|Q]] drops #1037 and #1038, the former stating "BOOMs en route."[3] A separate Scavino post of 3/15/23 at 12:53 AM highlighted a speech [[donald-trump|Trump]] gave at [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]]'s Hermitage on 3/15/17, in which Trump says "BOOM" while discussing the Jackson portrait he hung in the White House.[3]

## Link to Pepe

The article's Pepe connection rests on a Scavino Tweet of 10/9/16 that mentions the Trump Train, quotes Trump's response to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s [[basket-of-deplorables|"basket of deplorables"]] remark, and declares "WE LOVE OUR DEPLORABLES!!!" — posted, the author observes, exactly thirty days after the [[rarepepe|RAREPEPE]] token was minted on [[counterparty|Counterparty]].[3]
