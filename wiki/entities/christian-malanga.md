# Christian Malanga

Christian Malanga was a Congolese-American mercenary who led a failed coup attempt in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] in May 2024.[1]

## The May 2024 coup attempt

Malanga attempted to lead a group of nearly 40 operators — including his 22-year-old son — into Kinshasa to overthrow the government.[1] Malanga was killed in action, while his son and the other participants were arrested and given life sentences in prison.[1] The younger Malanga and the three other Americans among the group were later extradited by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to serve out their sentences in the United States.[1]

Badlands commentary situates the episode within a longer sequence of contests over the eastern Congo's mineral belt, valued at over $24 trillion, that has unfolded in phases since Trump signed [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]] in December 2017.[1]

## Death in the May 2024 DR Congo coup attempt

Malanga, a Congolese-born "naturalized American" and former refugee, led a roughly 40-strong militia in an attempted coup against the government of the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] in the early hours of Sunday, May 19, 2024, in the Gombe area of northern Kinshasa near the Palais de la Nation housing the president's offices.[2] Congolese security forces thwarted the plot and "definitively neutralized" — shot dead — Malanga; the government said dozens of attackers were arrested and four killed.[2] His 21-year-old son [[marcel-malanga|Marcel Malanga]], who had accompanied him from their home in Utah, was among those arrested, as were two American co-conspirators including [[benjamin-zalman-polun|Benjamin Zalman-Polun]].[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] linked a video circulated on X purporting to show Malanga socializing with [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldiers on patrol in the West Bank, and argued the operation "reeks of the CIA and Operation Gladio."[2]
