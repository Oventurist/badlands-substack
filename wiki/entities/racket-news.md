# Racket News

Racket News is an independent journalism outlet best known for its investigative reporting on state and private censorship infrastructure.[1]

## Report on the Censorship-Industrial Complex

In May 2023 Racket News published "Report on the Censorship-Industrial Complex: The Top 50 Organizations to Know," a survey of the institutions comprising what it termed the [[censorship-industrial-complex|Censorship-Industrial Complex]].[1] The report argued that shortly after the election of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], it became "gospel" among the emerging leadership of that complex that interference by "malign foreign threat actors" and the vicissitudes of Western domestic politics must be linked, so that events from John Podesta's emails to Trump's Rust Belt primary victories to Brexit were reinterpreted as hybrid war events.[1]

Racket News contended that the Trump–Russia scandal would be remembered as a crucial moment in 21st-century history: what the Mueller investigation failed to accomplish in ousting Trump, it accomplished in birthing a vast public-private bureaucracy devoted to combating "mis-, dis-, and malinformation" while smoothing public acquiescence to new government agencies with information warfare missions.[1] Its summary formulation was that the Censorship-Industrial Complex "is just the [[military-industrial-complex|Military-Industrial Complex]] reborn for the 'hybrid warfare' age."[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] praised the report as a wealth of information, arguing that because information is power, censorship is essential to those who push corruption, and that understanding the major players in the information war is a prerequisite for countering it.[1]
