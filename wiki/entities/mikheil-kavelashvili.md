# Mikheil Kavelashvili

Mikheil Kavelashvili is a Georgian politician and former professional footballer who was sworn in as President of Georgia at the end of December 2024, allied with the ruling [[georgian-dream|Georgian Dream]] party.[1] His inauguration came during a critical political period after the government suspended Georgia's application to join the European Union, and drew thousands of protesters into the capital, Tbilisi.[1] Georgian Dream had won parliamentary elections in October 2024, a victory mired in allegations of fraud that sparked repeated street protests.[1]

Outgoing president [[salome-zourabichvili|Salome Zourabichvili]] refused to step down, declaring herself the "only legitimate president," though she ultimately vacated the presidential palace while branding her successor illegitimate.[1] Kavelashvili was sworn in at a private ceremony down the street, attended by Prime Minister [[irakli-kobakhidze|Irakli Kobakhidze]].[1]
