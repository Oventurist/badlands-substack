# César Jáuregui Moreno

César Jáuregui Moreno served as attorney general of the northern Mexican state of Chihuahua until his resignation in early May 2026.[1]

## Resignation over CIA deaths

Jáuregui resigned following a vehicle crash that killed two [[cia|CIA]] officers — labeled "instructors" — along with two Mexican officials, after a counter-narcotics operation in the El Pinal area of Chihuahua.[1] At the press conference announcing his resignation, he admitted there had been "omissions" regarding the presence of the American agents in the country.[1] He had initially claimed that the U.S. operatives were giving drone lessons in the mountains of Chihuahua and had coincidentally caught a ride with a police convoy.[1]

Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] had earlier ordered an investigation into why the U.S. agents were in Mexico, stating that "neither had formal accreditation to participate in operational activities" and that one had entered the country as a tourist.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the sequence — Sheinbaum demanding answers, followed immediately by Jáuregui's admission and resignation — as an indication that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration does not trust the Mexican president, given what he called the optics of her being a cartel puppet.[1] He also noted that the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] was the outlet that first disclosed the dead Americans were CIA, consistent with the Badlands view of the paper as an agency mouthpiece.[1] The resignation came amid broader fallout from the [[indictment-of-mexican-officials-in-the-sinaloa-cartel-case|U.S. indictment of Mexican officials]] tied to the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]].[1]
