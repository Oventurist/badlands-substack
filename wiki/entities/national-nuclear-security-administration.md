# National Nuclear Security Administration

The National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA) is the United States agency tasked with managing the nation's nuclear weapons stockpile, operating as a semi-autonomous body within the [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]].[1]

## February 2025 firings

On Thursday night, February 13, 2025, officials of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration fired more than 300 staffers at the NNSA as part of broader Energy Department layoffs, according to four people with knowledge of the matter cited by CNN.[1] Sources told CNN that the officials carrying out the terminations did not appear to know that the agency oversees America's nuclear weapons.[1]

An Energy Department spokesperson disputed the reported number of personnel affected, telling CNN that "less than 50 people" were "dismissed" from the NNSA and that those dismissed "held primarily administrative and clerical roles."[1] The agency began rescinding the terminations the following morning, Friday, February 14, 2025.[1] The episode unfolded amid the wider cost-cutting campaign associated with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that if the "nuclear deterrent" could be eliminated by firing people holding administrative positions at the agency watching the nuclear stockpile, then the deterrent itself is "a clown show."[1] He noted the irony of the timing, coming as Trump discussed "denuking" in coordination with Russia and China and as, in his framing, the regime was "nuke-hoaxing again in Chernobyl," asking how long until another "existential threat" becomes a joke about past credulity.[1]
