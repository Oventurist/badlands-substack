# Martin Heinrich

Martin Heinrich is a United States Senator from New Mexico who served as one of the Democratic questioners during the January–February 2025 confirmation hearing of [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] for [[director-of-national-intelligence|Director of National Intelligence]].[1]

## Role in the Gabbard confirmation hearing

Heinrich took the lead in pressing Gabbard on her 2017 trip to Syria, during which she met twice with Syrian President [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized Heinrich's questioning as part of a broader pattern in which senators of both parties demanded that the nominee affirm establishment foreign-policy orthodoxy rather than engage with the substance of her answers.[1]

In response to Heinrich's questioning, Gabbard defended the value of direct diplomatic contact, stating that leaders "whether you be in Congress or the POTUS, can benefit greatly by going and engaging, boots on the ground, learning and listening and meeting directly with people, whether they be adversaries or friends."[1] DeLarme contrasted the criticism of Gabbard's trip with the absence of comparable career consequences for [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] after her own 2007 meeting with Assad.[1]
