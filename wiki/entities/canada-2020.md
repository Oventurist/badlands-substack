# Canada 2020

Canada 2020 is a Canadian think tank described as the banner under which the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party of Canada]] was re-organized between 2011 and 2014 as it prepared to take power.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) It has been characterized in the Canadian press as the progressive think tank that "really runs Canada."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

[[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]] brought Western figures she counted among her close friends — including Larry Summers, [[george-soros|George Soros]] and Al Gore — into Canada during that period of Liberal Party reorganization under the Canada 2020 banner.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]], the Rhodes Scholar and former Ontario premier who served as interim controller of the Liberal Party during several of the Harper years, was a major player in the organization.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Founding and the North American Union agenda

Badlands contributor Matthew Ehret describes Canada 2020 as "the very think tank that selected and managed [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]]'s political career," and identifies [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] as one of its key architects.[2] Carney's wife, [[diana-fox-carney|Diana Fox Carney]], is a senior fellow at the organization.[2]

The think tank was co-founded by Privy Councillors John Manley and Thomas Axworthy, both of whom helped draft the 2005 Task Force report calling for a [[north-american-union|North American Union]]: a single currency modelled on the Euro, a harmonized regulatory regime, and an unelected class of managers ruling across borders.[2] Ehret argues these objectives were never abandoned, only delayed, and are resurfacing with new force.[2] He also situates Canada 2020 in the grooming of Trudeau during the years when [[michael-ignatieff|Michael Ignatieff]] and [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]] led the Liberal Party.[2]
