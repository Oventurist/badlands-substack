# John Durham Jr.

John Durham is a veteran federal prosecutor on Long Island who was named in January 2025 to lead the US attorney's office in Brooklyn, the Eastern District of New York.[1] He is the son of [[john-durham|John H. Durham]], the former special counsel who investigated possible government misconduct in earlier inquiries into [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Appointment

The appointment came within the first 24 hours of the second Trump administration, as the new administration reached into critical US attorneys' offices to install interim leaders — a break from past practice reflecting the importance of frontline prosecutors to the administration's plans.[1] Acting leaders were announced in three of the most important federal prosecutors' offices: two in New York City and one in Washington.[1] Alongside Durham, [[danielle-sassoon|Danielle Sassoon]] was named acting head of the US attorney's office in Manhattan.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted the appointment as notable, writing that "Special Counsel Durham's son just entered the chat as the new US Attorney in Brooklyn, Eastern District of NY. Following in dad's footsteps it seems. Just in time for the justice phase."[1]
