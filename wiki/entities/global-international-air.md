# Global International Air

Global International Air was an air charter company owned by [[farhad-azima|Farhad Azima]] and, in the account cited by Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], a component of [[oliver-north|Oliver North]]'s logistical network.[1]

[[indian-springs-state-bank|Indian Springs State Bank]], in which Azima was the fourth largest stockholder, made several unsecured loans to Global International totalling $600,000 in violation of the bank's $349,000 borrower limit.[1] Global International filed for bankruptcy in 1983 and Indian Springs followed in 1984.[1]

The company shipped arms for the U.S. government on several occasions, and a sister Azima firm, Race Aviation, delivered 23 tons of TOW missiles to Iran.[1] Global International's planes, based in Miami, were maintained by [[southern-air-transport|Southern Air Transport]], another [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] proprietary company.[1] According to Franck Van Geyso, an employee of Global International, the airline's pilots ferried arms into South and Central America and returned to Florida with drugs.[1]
