# Sharon Tate

Sharon Tate was an actress and the pregnant wife of director [[roman-polanski|Roman Polanski]], killed by followers of [[charles-manson|Charles Manson]].[1]

It was later discovered that Susan Atkins, the woman who carried out the stabbing of Tate, had been employed by [[anton-lavey|Anton LaVey]] as a topless vampire in his Witches' Sabbath ceremony.[1] Former [[church-of-satan|Church of Satan]] follower Jay Sebring was among the five victims killed that evening.[1] Ehret cites these overlaps as evidence that LaVey's church intersected with the Manson murders on numerous levels.[1]
