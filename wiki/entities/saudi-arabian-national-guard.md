# Saudi Arabian National Guard

The **Saudi Arabian National Guard**, referred to in Chapter 9 of the Badlands Media series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] as the **Saudi Republican Guard**, is the internal-security force that the chapter identifies as having maintained order inside the Kingdom during the November 2017 [[saudi-purge|Saudi purge]].[1]

## Role in the 2017 purge

According to the chapter, with [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] at a standstill, under martial law and on lockdown while the round-up of princes, ministers and businessmen proceeded, the force keeping the peace was this guard, which the author describes as the equivalent of the American [[national-guard|National Guard]].[1] The comparison is the point of the passage: the author argues that Q used the Saudi example to give readers a crash course in how the United States National Guard could operate in conjunction with the Marines during a comparable period of domestic unrest.[1]

The chapter treats the parallel as a possible template rather than a prediction, writing that whether such a scenario would be seen in the United States was unknown but that "Q did point to it for a reason."[1]

## Threat environment

The chapter notes that during the purge a missile launched from [[yemen|Yemen]] toward Riyadh was intercepted, which it interprets as an attempt by the [[deep-state|Deep State]] to eliminate patriot assets inside the Kingdom that was quickly shut down — an incident that frames the guard's stabilising role as operating under active external pressure.[1] The chapter separately identifies [[blackwater|Blackwater]] as the U.S. operator then present in Saudi Arabia.[1]
