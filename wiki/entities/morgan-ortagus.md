# Morgan Ortagus

Morgan Ortagus is a United States diplomat who, in September 2025, served as a U.S. representative at the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and personally delivered a American veto at the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]].[1]

## Gaza ceasefire veto (September 2025)

On Thursday, 18 September 2025, the United States vetoed a Security Council draft resolution calling for an immediate and permanent ceasefire in Gaza and for the lifting of all restrictions on humanitarian aid deliveries into the enclave.[1] The draft had been tabled by the ten elected members of the fifteen-member council and received fourteen votes in favor, failing solely because of the U.S. veto — a power the United States holds as one of the five permanent members.[1] It was the sixth American veto since the beginning of the war between [[israel|Israel]] and Hamas nearly two years earlier.[1]

The veto was delivered by Ortagus.[1] Washington's stated position was that UN ceasefire resolutions risk undermining peace negotiations on the ground as well as Israel's ability to act against Hamas and its "right to self-defense," while critics accused U.S. authorities of shielding Israel from international accountability.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry characterized the episode as a "humiliation ritual" for both the United States and the United Nations, arguing that the near-unanimous vote and lone veto demonstrated that international rules are applied selectively.[1] The same commentary argued that the UN is losing its authority and that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was allowing Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to expose the character of Zionism to a global audience.[1]
