# Tractor Supply Company

Tractor Supply Co. is an American rural lifestyle retailer that in 2024 rolled back its diversity, equity and inclusion programs following pressure from the campaign led by commentator and filmmaker [[robby-starbuck|Robby Starbuck]].[1]

## 2024 DEI reversal

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] listed Tractor Supply alongside Harley-Davidson Inc. and Deere & Co. among the companies that reversed or entirely eliminated [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]] programs after Starbuck's exposés of internal corporate initiatives.[1] The sequence continued in August 2024 when Starbuck announced [[jack-daniels|Jack Daniel's]] as his next target, prompting parent company [[brown-forman|Brown-Forman Corp.]] to cut its own training in advance.[1]
