# Emily Shilling

Commander Emily Shilling is a United States Navy officer and naval aviator who became the lead plaintiff in litigation challenging the [[transgender-military-ban|Trump administration's ban on transgender military service]].[1]

## Career

Shilling has served in the Navy for almost two decades and is a naval aviator with more than 60 combat missions to her credit.[1]

## Litigation

In May 2025, the Supreme Court ruled 6–3 in favor of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]], clearing the path for the administration to proceed with its plan to bar transgender people from serving in the military.[1] The plan would also remove currently serving transgender personnel while the lower courts continued to litigate the legality of the ban.[1] The Supreme Court order was not a final ruling on the issue but would remain in place as litigation proceeded, and the approximate number of service members affected was unclear.[1]

Shilling is the lead plaintiff suing the administration to overturn the ban, and described herself as "heartbroken" in coverage of the ruling.[1]
