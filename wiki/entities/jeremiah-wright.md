# Jeremiah Wright

Jeremiah Wright is the pastor of [[trinity-united-church-of-christ|Trinity United Church of Christ]], the Chicago congregation attended by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[1]

## Role in the Q drops

Badlands Media's "Q for Dummies" series raises Wright while unpacking Q Drop #117 and its questions about Obama's background and influences.[1] The series describes Trinity United Church of Christ as a predominantly black congregation and states that Wright has a history of espousing controversial and racial topics, particularly against white people.[1]

Wright is listed among Obama's mentors alongside [[saul-alinsky|Saul Alinsky]] and [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]], a group the series characterizes as figures who are not admirers of America or the American way of life.[1] That claim sits within the drop's broader argument that Obama was cultivated as a potential Saudi "Manchurian Candidate," with [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]] financing his Harvard schooling.[1]
