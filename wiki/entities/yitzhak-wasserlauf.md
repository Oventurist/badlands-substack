# Yitzhak Wasserlauf

**Yitzhak Wasserlauf** is an Israeli politician of the far-right [[otzma-yehudit|Otzma Yehudit]] party who served as Minister for the Negev, Galilee and National Resilience in the government of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]

## Resignation over the Gaza ceasefire

On Sunday, January 19, 2025, Otzma Yehudit quit the governing coalition after the government agreed to a ceasefire with [[hamas|Hamas]] in the Gaza Strip, following through on the party's standing threat. In a statement, the party said its three cabinet members — National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], Heritage Minister [[amichai-eliyahu|Amichai Eliyahu]], and Wasserlauf — had submitted resignation letters to Netanyahu.[1] Alongside them, MKs Zvika Fogel, Limor Son Har-Melech and Yitzhak Kroizer resigned their seats on the various Knesset committees on which they served. "From this time onwards the Otzma Yehudit party is not a member of the coalition," the party declared.[1]

The walkout removed five or six seats from Netanyahu's coalition, leaving him only a few seats short of collapse and raising the prospect of new elections should [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]]'s [[religious-zionism-party|Religious Zionism]] party follow suit.[1]
