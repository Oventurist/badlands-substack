# Derek Harvey

Derek Harvey is a former Trump administration official and former national security aide who, as of mid-2026, serves as a member of a White House task force assembled to gather intelligence documents related to the 2020 presidential election for declassification.[1]

## Role on the 2020 election document task force

Harvey works alongside *Just The News* journalist [[john-solomon|John Solomon]] and other former [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration officials on the task force, which operates in addition to ongoing [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] probes into the 2020 election.[1] The team has been collecting material from the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]], the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]], the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]], the [[fbi|FBI]], and the Justice Department.[1]

Lawyers attached to the effort have been reviewing the gathered documents for months, sometimes late into the night, working through thousands of pages drawn from US intelligence agencies with plans to declassify a portion of them.[1] The documents focus on areas including ballot irregularities, potential voting machine issues, and possibly other topics, and task force members have been encouraged to limit redactions even where that means retaining the names of government officials or other individuals.[1]

## Context

The task force's work intersects with long-running [[2020-election-fraud-claims|claims of irregularities in the 2020 election]] and with commentary in independent media treating the promised disclosures as a test of whether accountability is genuinely being pursued.[1] President Trump indicated that acting DNI [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] would serve only "a month or two" but is authorized to "declassify almost everything," a statement that frames the operating window for the review in which Harvey participates.[1]
