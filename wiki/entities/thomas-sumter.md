# Thomas Sumter

Thomas Sumter was a South Carolina militia commander of the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]], known by the nickname "the Gamecock."[1]

## Mention in the Patriot Chronicles

Sumter appears in the article as the alternative target [[banastre-tarleton|Banastre Tarleton]] chose after abandoning a seven-hour gallop in pursuit of [[francis-marion|Francis Marion]]: "Come my boys! Let us go back, and we will soon find the Gamecock [Thomas Sumter]. As for this damned Swamp Fox, the devil himself could not catch him!"[1] The exclamation is the moment in which, by the article's telling, Marion's Swamp Fox legend was born.[1]
