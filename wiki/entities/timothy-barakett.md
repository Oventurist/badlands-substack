# Timothy Barakett

**Timothy Barakett** is a financier identified in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as chair of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] and a co-founder of [[atticus-capital|Atticus Capital]] with [[nathaniel-rothschild|Nathaniel Rothschild]].[1]

The article places Barakett "at the epicenter of dynastic finance," presenting his Rothschild partnership and later chairmanship of Harvard's endowment arm as "proof of intergenerational elite continuity."[1] In the piece's three-degrees framework, he exemplifies the pathway running from Harvard's [[porcellian-club|Porcellian Club]] and comparable elite circles into Rothschild-affiliated financial houses and think tanks such as the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], and from there into global policy posts.[1] The article characterizes him as "a financial control agent" within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
