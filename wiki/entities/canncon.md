CannCon is a host and contributor on [[badlands-media]].[1] He co-hosts Badlands Daily (Monday–Friday, 10am ET) with [[jon-herold]], presenting news "without the Mainstream Media spin and corporate BS."[1] He hosts Why We Vote (Tuesday, 5pm ET), interviewing voting experts nationwide about election integrity and "repeated attempts at fraud and election theft."[1] He also co-hosts SITREP (Thursday, 9pm ET) with Alpha Warrior, covering the stories of military veterans and "why they took the oath."[1]

## September 2025 News Brief commentary

In the September 10, 2025 Badlands News Brief, CannCon celebrated the dismissal of the [[michigan-fake-electors-case|Michigan fake electors case]] as a "HUGE Victory in the Lawfare War," while warning that for the prosecutors "victory" is the process itself: the defense had already cost the electors millions in legal fees.[2] He urged allies to "go on offense."[2]

## November 2025 News Brief commentary

In the November 17, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], CannCon commented on reporting that phone-record seizures had become a pattern in the Justice Department's "grand conspiracy" weaponization case, writing that [[ed-martin|Ed Martin]] was qualified for "any other district" but not D.C. and that "I think we all know why."[3] He called Martin "a pitbull" who went from taking on an elephant as an assistant U.S. attorney to a lapdog as head of the Weaponization Group, and urged that he be given the resources to clean it up.[3] On the Epstein files, CannCon argued that every attempt to hurt President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with limited leaks "backfires royally" because the leakers "don't even do their homework" and bury more of their own in the process.[3]

## Analysis of October 7 helicopter testimony

CannCon, a Marine who served in Iraq, offered military analysis on [[badlands-daily|Badlands Daily]] in November 2023 when [[brian-lupo|Brian Lupo]] and [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] covered the public testimony of an Israeli helicopter pilot regarding October 7, 2023.[4] Reviewing imagery of the parking lot at the [[nova-music-festival|Nova music festival]], CannCon said the devastation shown was indicative of damage caused by hellfire missiles launched from helicopters and could not possibly have been caused by Hamas infantry armed with rifles and RPGs.[4]

## USPS ballot report coverage (2023)

On June 5, 2023 CannCon and [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] presented a deep dive on [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] into the [[united-states-postal-service|USPS]] final report concerning contract driver [[jesse-morgan|Jesse Morgan]] and the alleged interstate transport of completed 2020 ballots.[5]

## Honduras "regime placement" thesis (December 2025)

Responding to the [[state-department|State Department]]'s visa restrictions on two Honduran election officials, CannCon wrote that "there appears to be an attempt at regime placement in [[honduras|Honduras]]."[6] He noted that congressmen from the [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence]] had traveled to Honduras to "monitor" its most recent election, and that HPSCI Chairman [[rick-crawford|Rick Crawford]] had also monitored elections in person in [[guyana|Guyana]], Suriname and Barbados in April 2025 - all, he said, key narcotrafficking hubs.[6] Three weeks after the Honduran vote no winner had been certified, and the [[white-house|White House]] had revoked or denied visas for two [[libre-party|LIBRE party]] figures, one a member of the National Electoral Council and the other a magistrate with the Electoral Justice Tribunal.[6]

## December 2025 commentary

CannCon questioned the framing of the Signalgate report in the December 5, 2025 Badlands News Brief, asking what it would mean if CISA had advised officials to use Signal during the Biden administration about a month before Trump took office, and pointing to Katherine Maher's chairmanship of the Signal Foundation board alongside her NPR role and prior work for the National Democratic Institute, UNICEF, the World Bank, Access Now, the Atlantic Council, and the US State Department Foreign Policy Affairs Board.[7] He also noted that no screenshot had been produced showing how officials in the chat saw Jeffrey Goldberg's name, and was slated to join Ashe in America and Patrick Byrne on Why We Vote to discuss Venezuelan election fraud.[7]

## Commentary on the 2023 White House Pride display

In June 2023 CannCon responded on Twitter to the [[white-house|White House]] Pride Month flag arrangement, in which a rainbow flag was centered between two American flags, writing: "This is a disgrace. The US flag DOES NOT FLANK *ANY* FLAG. Especially not on OUR White House."[8] The post was cited in Badlands commentary as an example of the objection raised on [[us-flag-code|Flag Code]] grounds, much of which came from active-duty military personnel and veterans.[8]

## Inflation fact-check (July 2025)

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, CannCon fact-checked a Fox News appearance by Mike Nellis, stating that [[inflation|inflation]] had not been above 3% since President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took office.[9] The item accompanied reporting that analysts saw a 64% probability of a [[september-2025-federal-reserve-rate-cut|September Federal Reserve rate cut]].[9] CannCon was also referenced as co-host of the Badlands Book Club, whose third season, "The Fourth Turning," launched that evening.[9]


## Commentary in the August 8, 2025 brief

On the extension of junk-food waivers to six more states under [[snap|SNAP]], CannCon endorsed the policy with the line "You can still get soda...you just have to use YOUR money!", calling it "epic" and "100% on point."[10]
