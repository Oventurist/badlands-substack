# International Monetary Fund

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) is one of the two principal institutions created by the 1944 Bretton Woods conference, charged with overseeing the international monetary system and providing balance-of-payments lending.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

In the Badlands Media article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] names the IMF, together with the [[world-bank|World Bank]], as a Bretton Woods institution that was "hijacked by deep state operatives representing the worst oligarchical inclinations of humanity" in the years after the Second World War. The article contrasts this later record with the [[bretton-woods-system|original Bretton Woods design]], which it describes as premised on fixed exchange rates to block speculation and on long-term credit for large-scale development. Ehret argues that the noble origins of these institutions should not be dismissed on account of their capture, and situates the SCO Development Bank proposed in the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]] as a contemporary attempt to realise the development-credit function the IMF and World Bank were meant to serve.
