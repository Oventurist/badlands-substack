# Colorado Information Analysis Center

The Colorado Information Analysis Center (CIAC) is a state intelligence fusion center that compiles reporting from a range of agencies into threat assessments distributed to law enforcement partners.[1]

## 2024 election insider-threat bulletin

Ahead of the November 5, 2024 general election, the CIAC produced a U.S. intelligence memo warning that insider threats would "likely be an issue" at election centers and "could derail or jeopardize a fair and transparent election process."[1] The bulletin adopted the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] definition of an insider threat as someone who "will use his or her authorized access, wittingly or unwittingly, to do harm to an entity," and reasoned that the sheer scale of the electoral process — the very large number of people involved — created a correspondingly large pool of potential insider threats.[1] Press coverage of the memo tied it to warnings that supporters of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] were flagging problems with the voting process and that Trump allies had sought to install "election deniers" throughout the electoral system.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], treated the bulletin as an inadvertent concession: U.S. intelligence agencies were now conceding, in writing, that the election system is extremely vulnerable if bad actors hold positions inside it.[1] She contrasted the CIAC's framing of election-integrity advocates as "extremists" with the same institutions' earlier insistence that the 2020 election had been the safest and most secure in history, arguing the two positions cannot both be true.[1]
