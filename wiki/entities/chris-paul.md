# Chris Paul

Chris Paul is a contributor within the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective, cited by fellow writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] as the originator of the concept of "drafting" in the information war.[1]

## The "drafting" concept

Paul argues that, in the same way a driver drafting behind a larger vehicle conserves power before slingshotting past it at the moment of maximum vulnerability, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has stayed within the slipstream of the script that the media and institutional voices were deployed to protect — only to edit it from the inside.[1] Burning Bright applied the framework to Trump's June 2026 handling of the [[iran|Iran]] file, arguing that permitting the optics of tension with [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], headlines of regional friction and the specter of Hormuz served as narrative shielding while Trump inserted the hard pivots that redirected the story toward an off-ramp its original authors would never have written.[1] The result, in this reading, is not chaos for its own sake but the methodical humiliation of a machine whose own story beats are weaponized against it.[1]

## Commentary on the 2026 SCOTUS citizenship argument

Paul flagged an exchange from the April 1, 2026 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] argument in [[trump-v-barbara|Trump v. Barbara]] in which Justice [[ketanji-brown-jackson|Ketanji Brown Jackson]] indicated that language in Court opinions had been written to "help the public accept the outcome" rather than as serious legal reasoning.[2] Writing in his Substack chat, Paul called it "a rather shocking admission on the part of the Court" and argued that if precedent — the bedrock of the legal system alongside statutory law derived from the Reconstruction amendments — can amount to public relations, it is an unsound basis for determining what government may do.[2] He concluded acidly that the arrangement reflects a system in which "the government is our king, and we are its subjects."[2]

## Cuba and Iran commentary (February 2026)

Commenting on the [[cayo-falcones-boat-incident|Cayo Falcones boat incident]], Paul mocked the Cuban government as "based half-century-behind island people" while questioning why Florida Attorney General [[james-uthmeier|James Uthmeier]] — described as a servant of [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] — was making definitive statements and talking tough before US agencies had confirmed any facts.[3]

With [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on Devolution Power Hour Ep. 434, Paul argued that the renewed [[iran|Iran]] war narrative did not add up, since the earlier US strike was carried out with advance notice and killed nobody yet supposedly ended Iran's nuclear capability.[3] Their advice for televised war announcements was to reply "No, it isn't," wait a few days, watch the dumb stories, and keep saying "I don't think that's right," until the recognition dawns that people fake wars "so I'll support things I would never actually support."[3]

## Badlands Media programming (2023)

The January 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] schedule listed Chris Paul as host of [[what-we-missed-with-chris-paul|What We Missed with Chris Paul]], airing Tuesdays at 9pm ET, describing him as a former Hollywood insider who takes viewers through recent events in a style characterised as taking a sledgehammer to the mainstream media paradigm.[4]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[5]

## September 2025 News Brief commentary

In the September 10, 2025 Badlands News Brief, Chris Paul offered terse commentary on three items: the record [[bureau-of-labor-statistics|BLS]] downward revision ("How about that? Another agency is illegitimate?!"), [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s recall of the classified Venezuela report involving [[richard-grenell|Richard Grenell]], which he dismissed as a "clown show," and the [[2025-nepal-protests|Nepal protests]], where he questioned why audiences treat protests broadcast by global media in unfamiliar countries as automatically legitimate.[6]

## November 2025 News Brief commentary

Reacting to Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s designation of a European antifascist group as a terrorist organization under [[national-security-presidential-memorandum-7|NSPM-7]], Chris Paul argued that "eight years of Obama followed by eight years of Hillary was meant to be the Weimar phase."[7] In his reading, the establishment-conservative and neoconservative apparatus would have played its part in a Nazi storyline before blaming it on nationalists, populists, and Christians.[7] He concluded that "history repeats because there is only one human nature and only one narrative playbook."[7]

## Counter-narrative discussions (2023)

In the November 8, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described discussions with Chris Paul about the idea that the "Covid-23 Scariant" was effectively blunted by the mass counter-narrative deployments of the decentralized [[truth-community|Truth Community]] and the alternative media — a dynamic he then applied to the funding of proxy wars and to [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s postponed visit to Israel.[8]

## February 2023 commentary on McCarthy

In the February 22, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Chris Paul argued that Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] was "beginning to silence the doubters."[9] He described the turnover of [[january-6-capitol-security-footage-release|January 6 Capitol security footage]] to [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] as significant precisely because Carlson is "as big as it gets in mainstream media" and could therefore disrupt the mainstream media narrative on January 6 quickly.[9] Paul added that if McCarthy also succeeded in stripping [[ukraine-aid-funding-controversy|Ukraine funding]] out of must-pass bills in the House, "that's huge," reasoning that the Regime likely believed McCarthy lacked the political will to do so, but that the war was extremely unpopular and the public would be behind the effort.[9]

## Commentary in "Cartels, Crucibles, & Convictions" (August 2025)

Writing on the New York appellate court's reversal of the $527 million civil fraud penalty against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Chris Paul remarked, "Man, it's like the whole thing was fake from the beginning."[10] On the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s review of more than 55 million U.S. visa holders, he cited the Migration Policy Institute's estimate that temporary visa holders represent only 5% of total foreign-born U.S. residents, concluding that either the estimate is far off "or we have 1.1 billion illegal aliens here."[10]

## Commentary: No Deals on the Border (January 2024)

Writing in the Badlands News Brief in late January 2024, Paul rejected the bipartisan Senate border-and-Ukraine package outright, arguing that "the border problem could be solved without any legislation whatsoever."[11] In his reading, the legislation was not designed to solve the border crisis but "to increase the surveillance state while making the process of illegal alien entry more efficient," with an added $110 billion committed to what he called "fake foreign proxy wars."[11] His prescription was a flat refusal — "No deals. No deals on the border. No deals for foreign proxy 'wars.'" — on the grounds that full authority to solve the problem already exists and the crisis persists because both sides of the [[uniparty|Uniparty]] want it to.[11] He asked why Republican representatives were discussing new legislation rather than demanding the executive use the authorities the people had already transferred to the State.[11]

## Commentary in the Badlands News Brief (July 2025)

Paul contributed two extended takes to the July 3, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[12] In the first he criticized [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] as obsessed with the worldly, arguing that Thiel treats slowed technological advancement — "stagnation" — as the world's great evil, that this makes him progressive rather than conservative, and that his defense of FDR and the New Deal on the grounds that a socialist United States was good for Science sits oddly with professed opposition to centralization.[12] Paul countered that true freedom is the ability to make moral choices unobstructed, that freedom must be constrained by morality or else by a State that cares nothing for morality, and that Thiel's vision of self-transformation amounts to a worldly usurpation of God's order rooted in the Scientific Materialist inversion of that order.[12]

In the second he argued that no member of [[congress|Congress]] has a mandate to govern comparable to that of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], that most members are unknown even in their own districts and cannot prove their legitimacy because "our elections are fake," and that all 435 House members should support passage of the [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|One Big Beautiful Bill]] — closing by asking what mandate [[chip-roy|Chip Roy]] could claim.[12]


## Commentary in September 2025

Chris Paul commented on the media response to the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], writing that "all the TV Characters agree, despite not knowing what happened," that this "is not a naturally occurring phenomenon," and that the public was "being coerced into consensus, unifying with the enemy disguised as our friend."[13]

On the [[nato|NATO]] operation [[eastern-sentry|Eastern Sentry]] over [[poland|Poland]], he offered a sceptical read, remarking that the allies were "not defending Poland as hard as they didn't defend 'Ukraine' against Putin's Very Brutal Invasion."[13]

## Commentary in the August 2025 brief

Writing on [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s $5 million donation to the pro-Trump super PAC [[maga-inc|MAGA Inc.]], Paul argued that "the American political reality show is an info op," that the info op functions as negotiation leverage, and that the actual negotiation happens only behind the scenes.[14] He described American politics as "a very stupid and obviously fake reality show" whose meaning derives only from its audience's adherence to the ideology that keeps the system alive, urging readers to get involved locally, stop recognizing the fake government's legitimacy, and demand the end of the political soap opera by tuning out — while clarifying that the [[information-war|info op]] itself is entirely real and is the one layer on which meaningful engagement is possible.[14]


## Elections commentary (April 2025)

Writing in the Badlands News Brief of April 4, 2025 on the multistate lawsuit against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[2025-trump-elections-executive-order|elections executive order]], Chris Paul asked why "all the popular Attention Farmers downstream from [[turning-point-usa|TPUSA]]" had spent the week persuading "Standard-Issue Villagers on the Uniparty Right" that elections are real and that the remedy is more power and funding for TPUSA, when in his view "anyone with a semi-functional brain can see that elections are fake."[15] He closed by pointing to [[liz-harrington|Liz Harrington]]'s response with the remark that she "is not messing around."[15]

## "The Fake Election Loop" (December 2025)

Commenting on early polling for the [[2028-united-states-presidential-election|2028 presidential election]], Chris Paul offered a segment titled "The Fake Election Loop" on [[devolution-power-hour|Devolution Power Hour]] episode 417.[16] "As long as people keep giving their power away on the basis of fake elections, we're gonna be stuck in that cycle," he said, adding that "we have to know they're fake first before we can even get to the point of real elections."[16] He characterized the state of American political coverage bluntly: "This is crazy people stuff. If it were a show designed to make people stop paying attention, they couldn't be doing a better job."[16]

## Commentary, September 2025

Chris Paul responded to [[youtube|YouTube]]'s $24.5 million [[youtube-settlement-of-trump-lawsuit|settlement]] of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s deplatforming lawsuit with a single question — "Cool. When do the rest of us get paid?" — pointing to the absence of any remedy for ordinary censored users.[17] On the Gaza plan's clause barring further Israeli strikes on [[qatar|Qatar]], he noted drily that "The Uniparty Right Zionists told me Qatar supports terrorism," calling the arrangement "Weird."[17]

## December 2025 commentary

In the December 5, 2025 Badlands News Brief, Chris Paul dismissed the FBI's pipe bomb arrest by asking when observers would accept that there were never any real pipe bombs on January 6.[18] He also mocked the Michigan absentee ballot ruling, quipping that 'a few more of these improvements and people will believe our fake elections are even more legitimate than before,' and joked about Halle Berry's appearance at the DealBook Summit.[18]

## Commentary on the Rogan-Epstein story (July 2025)

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, Chris Paul rejected CNN's framing that [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]]'s criticism over the [[epstein-files|Epstein files]] posed a problem for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], noting that no Trump election was due for at least three and a half years and that "podcasters can't harm him."[19] He argued that "Standard-Issue Villagers" write as though Trump were uncertain about his own conduct, and characterized the whole affair as "a scripted psychodrama meant to keep you hooked to the Central Narrative," which he defined as "literally the thing that absolutely is not happening."[19]


## Commentary in the August 8, 2025 brief

Reacting to the nomination of [[stephen-miran|Stephen Miran]] to the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Board of Governors, Chris Paul noted that "the author of the Mar-a-Lago Accords will be the next Fed governor," and floated [[michael-saylor|Michael Saylor]] as a candidate for Fed chairman.[20]

## Commentary, May 2025

Reacting to reporting that Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] functioned largely as a televised messenger for the administration, Paul wrote: "Whoa! Crazy! The members of the administration are just TV people? Who could've ever thought this?" — adding that he had said as much in November of the previous year and had been told he "wasn't trusting the plan hard enough by very stupid people pretending to be truthers."[21]

He also criticized [[seth-dillon|Seth Dillon]] of [[babylon-bee|The Babylon Bee]] for joining what Paul described as establishment neoconservatives in labeling "conspiracy theorists" as "woke," calling the [[woke-right|woke right]] framing "a perfect new identifier and a proof of what I was showing people back in 2022."[21] Separately, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] named Paul, alongside [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], as having gamed out Saudi accession to the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]] as a theory of a potential future for years before the May 2025 Riyadh visit.[21]

## Commentary in the September 25, 2025 brief

Responding to the Dallas [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] facility shooting, Chris Paul quoted a statement about Scranton workers making Pennsylvania "the arsenal of democracy" and standing with Ukraine, noting that a year earlier "the illegitimate governor of Pennsylvania," [[josh-shapiro|Josh Shapiro]], had signed munitions.[22] He compared this to [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldiers signing ammunition after what he called the "Charlie Kirk" trauma event, asking who does such a thing, and mocked Fox News for inviting [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]] on to discuss "the latest ridiculous psyop."[22]
