# J.B. White

**J.B. White**, who writes online under the handle RattlerGator, is a contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and the author of the December 20, 2022 opinion column "Anthony Fauci: Soldier for the Communist Cause," his first written submission to the outlet.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He also publishes RattlerGator's Newsletter on Substack.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Entry into Badlands Media

White explained that the ongoing [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] revelations — which he characterised as exposing wrongdoing by a "RICO-worthy collection of fascistic rascals unworthy of American public service" — made it an appropriate moment to begin making regular submissions to the Badlands Media series on Substack.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He framed the Fauci column as a companion to a Twitter Files exposé on [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] that he expected would eventually appear.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

Before writing for Badlands, White had a prior connection to the outlet's orbit: he had delivered a 70-post thread on [[x-twitter|Twitter]] about what he called "the new kind of war," which was read aloud by his future *Trash Talk* co-host [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] in the *Reading Epic Threads* series.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Political outlook

White's writing combines religious and anti-communist framing. He argued that when Catholic training and tradition become "unmoored from 'men for God'" the result is an easy transition to what he called Crazy Street, pointing to the rise of liberation theology in South America as the model.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) In his view "humanists are most often indistinguishable from Communists," and use of the label is "just word warfare."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

On COVID-19, White drew what he described as an immediate line in the sand — "deaths, not cases" — and said he refused to credit hysteria over case counts absent credible evidence of extraordinary deaths.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He treated healthy skepticism as a default American mindset that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] had successfully subverted through tools applied "in plain sight, but through hidden means," chief among them [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He quoted at length from a column by J. E. Dyer arguing that the purpose of a campaign of political censorship is gaslighting, and that the more one censors, the more latitude one has to gaslight.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

White also described the 2020 presidential election as stolen through ballot stuffing in furtherance of a coup, expressed shock at what he called the ho-hum acceptance of the steal by family and friends, and listed the January 6th narrative alongside the "cases, cases, cases" hysteria as examples of lies of omission in 21st-century warfare as practiced by the CCP and those in its service.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He argued that in the 2016 cycle the Republican base tested the GOP establishment, which failed the test, and that the incoming [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration assembled "an invasion force" to root out adherents of Communism masquerading as Socialism.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) He called for all three branches of government to re-embrace inalienable individual rights, recommending the Claremont Review essay "The Tipping Point" and the American Mind memo "Abolish FISA, Reform FBI, & Break Up CIA."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Signature closing

White closed the column with the valediction he said he once used to end most email correspondence: "Patience. Preparation. Perseverance. Attention to Detail. Flexibility. Resiliency. Never think that God's delays are God's denials. Hold on; hold fast; hold out. Patience is genius."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)
