# J.B. White

J.B. White, who writes under the online handle RattlerGator, is a commentator and contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]. His column "Anthony Fauci: Soldier for the Communist Cause," published December 20, 2022, is described in its own text as his initial written submission to the outlet, undertaken because the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] disclosures had, in his view, made the moment ripe for regular commentary on the [[deep-state|Deep State]].

## Themes and style

White's writing combines political polemic with a moral and religious frame. In [[anthony-fauci|his Fauci column]] he argues that Jesuit-educated figures such as [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] can drift from "men for God" to a secular humanism that he regards as functionally indistinguishable from [[communism|Communism]], drawing a parallel to liberation theology in South America. He treats healthy skepticism as a default American mindset that has been eroded by subversion applied "in plain sight, but through hidden means," and insists that during the COVID-19 response the only meaningful metric was deaths rather than case counts.

He describes the 2020 presidential election as stolen through ballot stuffing "in furtherance of a coup," and argues that all three branches of government must re-embrace inalienable individual rights, with citizens understood as "First Sovereigns." He recommends the Claremont Review of Books essay "The Tipping Point: Douglas MacArthur versus the ruling class" and The American Mind's "Abolish FISA, Reform FBI, & Break Up CIA."

## Other activity

White is a co-host of the Badlands programme Trash Talk alongside [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]]. Before joining Badlands he was active on Twitter, where a 70-post thread of his was read aloud by Gunnels in the Reading Epic Threads series, producing roughly 26 minutes of video hosted on Odysee. He also publishes independently at RattlerGator's Newsletter on Substack. His columns close with a personal set of maxims: "Patience. Preparation. Perseverance. Attention to Detail. Flexibility. Resiliency," followed by "Never think that God's delays are God's denials," and the sign-off "Later, Gators."
