# Instagram

**Instagram** is a photo- and video-sharing social network operated as a subsidiary of [[meta-platforms|Meta Platforms]], alongside [[facebook|Facebook]].[1]

## 2023 nudity policy change

In January 2023 Facebook and Instagram modified their content regulations to permit transgender and non-binary users to post photographs and videos featuring their bare breasts, following a decision published on Meta's oversight board website on January 17, 2023.[1] The same rules were not extended to biological females: cisgender women — those who identify with their biological sex — remained barred from posting images of their bare breasts.[1] The board stated that "the same image of female-presenting nipples would be prohibited if posted by a cisgender woman but permitted if posted by an individual self-identifying as non-binary."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] read the policy as an instance of cultural warfare and "soft eugenics," arguing that globalist actors had turned to subtle [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] methods rather than overt population reduction to advance depopulation and control objectives.[1] He further contended that promoting transgender ideology produces a cohort of "truth-detached" people who can be weaponised against political opposition, while crowding freedom-oriented citizens out of the political space.[1]

## 2023 pedophile-network investigation

In June 2023, a joint investigation by the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] and the [[stanford-internet-observatory|Stanford Internet Observatory]] reported that Instagram had been home to an organized and massive network of pedophiles.[2] The investigation found that the platform's own recommendation algorithms actively promoted such content between users, because those systems excel at connecting people with shared niche interests.[2]

Researchers documented that accounts used coded emojis and phrases — an image of a map for "minor-attracted person," and "cheese pizza," sharing initials with "child pornography" — and that many described themselves as "lovers of the little things in life."[2] Instagram also permitted searches on explicit hashtags such as #pedowhore and #preteensex, which routed users to accounts advertising child-sex material for sale, with sellers signalling a child's purported age through phrases like "on chapter 14" or "age 31" paired with a reverse-arrow emoji.[2]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] contrasted [[meta-platforms|Meta]]'s tolerance of these networks with its banning of accounts in the freedom movement for posting anti-mainstream content, compared the situation to YouTube's Elsagate controversy, and noted that the pizza symbolism echoed the [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]] claims surrounding Comet Ping Pong.[2]
