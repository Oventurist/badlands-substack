# Prince Holding Group

Prince Holding Group, commonly referred to as Prince Group, is a multinational business conglomerate based in [[cambodia|Cambodia]], founded and chaired by [[chen-zhi|Chen Zhi]].[1] Federal prosecutors in the Eastern District of New York allege that the company, ostensibly focused on real estate development, financial services, and consumer services, secretly operated forced-labor scam compounds across Cambodia.[1]

## Structure

According to the October 2025 indictment, Prince Group has operated since approximately 2015 and comprises dozens of business entities in more than 30 countries.[1] Prosecutors allege that beneath this legitimate corporate surface, Chen Zhi and his top executives built the group into one of Asia's largest transnational criminal organizations.[1]

## Alleged criminal operations

Prince Group is alleged to have made enormous profits from scam compounds perpetrating fraudulent cryptocurrency investment schemes of the [[pig-butchering-scams|"pig butchering"]] type, in which operators cultivated trust with victims over messaging and social media before persuading them to transfer cryptocurrency that was then stolen and laundered.[1] The [[fbi|FBI]] states that Prince Group trafficked hundreds of workers and forced them to execute the scams in Cambodian compounds, often under the threat of violence.[1]

The victims and co-conspirators were global. One facilitation network operated in Brooklyn, New York, and helped transfer and launder millions of dollars on Prince Group's behalf from more than 250 victims in New York and across the country.[1]

## Enforcement action

On October 14, 2025, the [[us-attorneys-office-eastern-district-of-new-york|U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of New York]] and the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] National Security Division unsealed the indictment of Chen Zhi and filed a civil forfeiture complaint against approximately 127,271 [[bitcoin|bitcoin]] worth roughly $15 billion — the largest forfeiture action in Justice Department history.[1] Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] and U.S. Attorney Joseph Nocella both framed the case as a landmark strike against cyber-enabled fraud and human trafficking.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Prince Group's structure — a globally distributed conglomerate presenting itself as a professional-services firm while running offshore operations that defraud Americans — should be studied as a reusable "template" rather than dismissed as a foreign curiosity.[1]
