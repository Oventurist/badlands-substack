# Averell Harriman

Averell Harriman was an American financier and business partner of [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] in the Wall Street operations that, according to Matthew Ehret's account, financed the rise of German fascism during the 1920s–1940s.[1] Together with his younger brother E. Roland Harriman, he was central to the banking network that later became [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]].[1]

## Role in the financing of Nazism

Prescott Bush "made a name for himself funding Nazism alongside his business partners Averell Harriman and Averell's younger brother E. Roland Harriman," the latter of whom recruited Bush into [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] while the two were students at Yale.[1] The Harriman banking interests merged in 1931 with Montagu Norman's family bank, Brown Brothers, producing the entity through which the Nazi-linked [[union-banking-corporation|Union Banking Corporation]] business was conducted.[1]

## Eugenics patronage

The Harriman family co-sponsored the Third Eugenics Conference held in New York in 1932 alongside JP Morgan banker William Draper Jr.[1] The conference gathered leading eugenicists from around the world to study America's application of [[eugenics|eugenics]] laws, which had begun in 1907, and to discuss a coming age of "directed evolution of man."[1]
