# Averell Harriman

Averell Harriman was an American financier and business partner of [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] in the Wall Street operations that, according to Matthew Ehret's account, financed the rise of German fascism during the 1920s–1940s.[1] Together with his younger brother E. Roland Harriman, he was central to the banking network that later became [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]].[1]

## Role in the financing of Nazism

Prescott Bush "made a name for himself funding Nazism alongside his business partners Averell Harriman and Averell's younger brother E. Roland Harriman," the latter of whom recruited Bush into [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] while the two were students at Yale.[1] The Harriman banking interests merged in 1931 with Montagu Norman's family bank, Brown Brothers, producing the entity through which the Nazi-linked [[union-banking-corporation|Union Banking Corporation]] business was conducted.[1]

## Eugenics patronage

The Harriman family co-sponsored the Third Eugenics Conference held in New York in 1932 alongside JP Morgan banker William Draper Jr.[1] The conference gathered leading eugenicists from around the world to study America's application of [[eugenics|eugenics]] laws, which had begun in 1907, and to discuss a coming age of "directed evolution of man."[1]

## Skull and Bones and public career

"The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" lists Averell Harriman (Yale class of 1913) among the most notable members of [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] and calls him the "Elder Statesman" of the Democratic Party, active in the Order alongside his brother [[roland-harriman|Roland Harriman]].[2] His public career included service as an investment banker with [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]], director of the Lend-Lease program of the [[state-department|U.S. State Department]] (1941–1942), U.S. Ambassador to the Soviet Union (1943–1946), Governor of New York, Under Secretary of State for Asia (1961–1963), and presidential secret envoy to the Soviet leaders Stalin, Khrushchev, Brezhnev and Andropov.[2]

As minister to Great Britain in charge of Lend-Lease for Britain and Russia, Harriman was responsible for shipping entire factories into Russia, and according to researchers cited in the article he also oversaw the transfer of nuclear secrets, plutonium and U.S. dollar printing plates to the USSR.[2] The piece places this within a broader allegation that Bonesman-dominated banks — Brown Brothers Harriman and [[guaranty-trust-company|Guaranty Trust]] — financed both Communism and [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]]'s regime.[2]
