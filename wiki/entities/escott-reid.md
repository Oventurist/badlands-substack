# Escott Reid

Escott Reid was a Canadian diplomat and Rhodes Scholar credited by Badlands Media contributor Matthew Ehret with spearheading the creation of the [[nato|North Atlantic Treaty Organization]] in 1947.[1] He was one of the members of the Canadian branch of London's [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]], which was established by six Rhodes Scholars in 1931.[1]

Ehret situates Reid within a wider post-war pattern in which agents of the British imperial network built the institutions of global governance: agents took control of the UN Charter, Reid drafted NATO, Eugenics Society president Sir [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] created [[unesco|UNESCO]], Tavistock's Dr. G. Brock Chisholm established the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], Tavistock's Dr. John Rawlings Rees created the World Federation of Mental Health, and [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]] founder Sir [[alexander-king|Alexander King]] took over the [[oecd|OECD]].[1] In Ehret's account, "Everywhere you find technocracy in action, a Rhodes Scholar is nearby."[1]
