# Transport Logistics International

Transport Logistics International is the American trucking company that held the contract to transport Russian-owned uranium around the United States, and which became a focal point of the FBI's criminal investigation into the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]].[1]

## Role in the bribery scheme

According to court records cited in Badlands Media's account, [[vadim-mikerin|Vadim Mikerin]] — the [[rosatom|Rosatom]] official overseeing Russia's nuclear expansion inside the United States — awarded the firm the sensitive uranium transport contract in exchange for more than $2 million in kickbacks from some of its executives.[1] The FBI described the arrangement as a serious national security breach, since Moscow had compromised an American company entrusted with moving nuclear material across U.S. territory in violation of the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act.[1]

## Export licence workaround

Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that the export of American uranium was concealed from [[congress|Congress]] by adding Uranium One to Transport Logistics International's roster of companies authorised to export uranium, rather than issuing Uranium One its own export licence.[1] On this reading the bribes paid to company executives also served to buy their silence about the arrangement.[1]

## Prosecution

Executives of the trucking firm were arrested and charged alongside Mikerin and a Russian financier in 2014, but the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] and FBI publicised the case only through a brief press release in August 2015 noting that the defendants had reached plea deals.[1] The muted handling of the prosecutions is presented in the article as part of a broader effort to keep the Uranium One scandal out of public view.[1]
