# Michael Baden

Michael Baden is a forensic pathologist who observed the autopsy of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] on behalf of the Epstein family after Epstein's August 2019 death in federal custody in Manhattan.[1]

## Autopsy observations

According to [[mark-epstein|Mark Epstein]], speaking to [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] in January 2024, both the New York City pathologist and Dr. Baden "came out of the autopsy and they said this doesn't look like a suicide. It looks more like a homicide."[1] Mark Epstein said the findings included broken neck bones more consistent with strangulation or a physical attack than with hanging, and an absence of the physical signs expected on a body that had hung for an extended period.[1] He added that the autopsy photographs showed a ligature mark in the middle of his brother's neck running straight back, "as if someone put a rope around his neck and strangled him."[1]
