# Liz Murrill

Liz Murrill is the Republican attorney general of Louisiana.[1]

## January 2025 Fox News appearance

On January 1, 2025, just hours after the [[new-orleans-bourbon-street-attack|Bourbon Street vehicle-ramming attack]] in New Orleans, Murrill appeared on [[fox-news|Fox News]] to discuss the incident.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the appearance as an instance of prominent conservative figures folding an unrelated border-security narrative into coverage of the attack.[1]

DeLarme wrote that Fox News not only misreported that the suspect had driven the vehicle across the border, but that the vehicle in fact never crossed the border at all, having merely been driven around Eagle Pass, Texas, over a month earlier.[1] He noted that Fox News subsequently corrected the report.[1] DeLarme argued that manufacturing facts in service of a long-held cause actively damages that cause and reinforces the perception that conservatives seeking border enforcement are motivated by racism rather than evidence.[1] The perpetrator, [[shamsud-din-jabbar|Shamsud-Din Jabbar]], was a United States citizen and Army veteran.[1]
