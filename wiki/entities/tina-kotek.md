# Tina Kotek

Tina Kotek is an American Democratic politician serving as governor of Oregon, whose office coordinated the state's response to feared unrest around the 2024 general election.[1]

## 2024 election security

In a statement issued the Friday before Election Day 2024, Kotek said the Oregon [[national-guard|National Guard]] was standing ready as political leaders called for peaceful protests.[1] "The governor's office is closely monitoring and coordinating with local, state and federal agencies to ensure Oregon voters can safely cast their ballot," the statement said.[1]

The precaution followed the [[2024-pacific-northwest-ballot-box-fires|ballot drop box fires]] in Portland and across the Columbia River in Vancouver, Washington, in which hundreds of ballots were damaged or destroyed.[1] Oregon had previously deployed the Guard alongside a unified command for election security in 2020.[1]
