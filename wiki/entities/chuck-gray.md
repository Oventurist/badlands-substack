# Chuck Gray

Chuck Gray is the Secretary of State of Wyoming and a leading Republican advocate of state-level election-integrity legislation.[1]

## Wyoming noncitizen voting legislation

In January 2025 the Wyoming House of Representatives passed a package of bills aimed at preventing noncitizens from voting in elections, an effort Gray had made the centerpiece of his office's agenda.[1] "I want to commend the House's passage of these bills, which have been priority number one of our election integrity agenda," Gray said in a statement, adding that he looked forward to continuing to work with the Legislature "to achieve meaningful, conservative reform."[1]

One measure, HB 156, stipulates that "qualified elector[s]" must have "been a bona fide resident of Wyoming for not less than thirty (30) days before the date of the election at which he offers to vote."[1] The bill further specifies that county clerks "shall not" register an individual to vote "if the person's presented identification or proof of residence contains any indication that the person is not a citizen of the United States unless the person also presents proof of citizenship as specified by rule of the secretary of state" — placing the rulemaking authority for acceptable proof with Gray's office.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the Wyoming bills as part of a race to restore "accurate and verifiable elections before the midterms," contrasting Wyoming with neighboring Colorado, where she argued that stating that illegal migrant votes are not legal could bring accusations of bigotry, civil suits or prosecution, while in Wyoming it is "a simple fact of law."[1]
