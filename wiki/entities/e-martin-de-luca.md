# E. Martin De Luca

E. Martin De Luca is an American attorney with the law firm Boies Schiller Flexner who represented [[rumble|Rumble]] in its February 2025 federal lawsuit against Brazilian Supreme Court Justice [[alexandre-de-moraes|Alexandre de Moraes]].[1]

De Luca argued that [[allan-dos-santos|Allan dos Santos]], the Brazilian journalist targeted by Moraes's order and a legal resident of the United States, is entitled to compensation.[1] Framing the case as a defense of American jurisdiction, he stated that "Alexandre de Moraes is attempting to sidestep U.S. law entirely," and that the suit brought by Rumble and [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media]] "seeks the protection of a U.S. federal court to ensure that American businesses remain governed by American law, and that no foreign court can unilaterally dictate what speech is allowed on American platforms without proper authorization from the U.S. government."[1]

His firm's involvement placed a prominent litigation shop behind [[rumble-and-trump-media-v-moraes|Rumble and Trump Media v. Moraes]], the case that produced a Middle District of Florida ruling holding that Moraes's directives carry no legal force in the United States.[1]
