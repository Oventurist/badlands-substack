# Pieces Technologies

Pieces Technologies is an artificial intelligence company that entered into a settlement with the Texas Attorney General's Office over claims about the accuracy of its products.[1]

## Texas Settlement

An August 2025 Badlands Media essay described the Pieces Technologies settlement as ground-breaking because it forced transparency on accuracy claims and risk disclosures, embedding what the author called a permanent constraint into the AI development loop.[1] In the essay's game-theoretic reading, the agreement did more than resolve a single dispute: it changed the rules of play so that future rounds would unfold on terms set by the state rather than by the developer.[1]

## Place in the Broader Campaign

The settlement was presented as one element of Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]]'s coordinated approach to technology and artificial intelligence, alongside the $1.4 billion privacy settlement with [[meta-platforms|Meta]], the $1.375 billion settlement with [[google|Google]], the probe of [[character-ai|Character.AI]] over interactions with minors, and the investigation of [[deepseek|DeepSeek]].[1] The author argued that these actions together do not dismantle the technology sector but rewrite the conditions under which it operates, and that once such conditions are locked into precedent they become "the new gravity in the information battlespace."[1]
