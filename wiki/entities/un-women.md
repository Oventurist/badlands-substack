# UN Women

UN Women is the [[united-nations|United Nations]] body that bills itself as the "Entity for Gender Equality and the Empowerment of Women," describing its mandate as working "for gender equality and the empowerment of women."[1]

## 2024 UK champion controversy

In December 2023 the organization's UK committee, UN Women UK, appointed the model, broadcaster and transgender activist [[munroe-bergdorf|Munroe Bergdorf]] as its "UK champion," a decision that drew formal protest in early January 2024.[1] Seventeen women's rights groups signed a letter to the charity expressing their "dismay and disappointment" at the choice of a transgender woman to represent British women, as first reported by *The Times*.[1]

The campaign group Fair Play For Women issued a statement objecting that "In December the UN Women's UK committee appointed a male who presents in a highly sexualised stereotype of womanhood as an ambassador for women," and adding that "UN Women has made a point of demonstrating that it considers males can become women. It's disappointing to see the UK committee go so far as to select a male to represent women. Their credibility is in tatters."[1] Modernity News, whose report was carried in the Badlands News Brief, characterized the appointment as "a direct exercise in trolling women."[1]
