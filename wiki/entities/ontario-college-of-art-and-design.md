# Ontario College of Art and Design

The Ontario College of Art and Design is a Toronto art and design school referenced in commentary on the spread of ideological orthodoxy through Canadian post-secondary education.[1]

At a Toronto screening of [[the-coddling-of-the-american-mind|The Coddling of the American Mind]] organised by the [[heterodox-academy|Heterodox Academy]], a former instructor at the college reported that, because it was an art school, what he described as the woke mind virus had arrived there earlier than at conventional universities.[1] He said he had contended with a sudden spike in students submitting accommodation letters claiming they could no longer complete assignments on time — the same phenomenon separately described by [[leigh-rivers|Leigh Rivers]] of the [[university-of-toronto|University of Toronto]].[1] The account was cited as evidence that arts institutions function as leading indicators for ideological trends that later appear across the wider academy.[1]
