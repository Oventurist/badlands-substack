# World Wrestling Entertainment

World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE, formerly WWF) is the American professional wrestling promotion founded by Vince McMahon and long run by McMahon and his wife [[linda-mcmahon|Linda McMahon]], who served as its president and chief executive officer.[1] In Badlands Media commentary the organization is treated as a key reference point for understanding [[kayfabe|kayfabe]] — the wrestling convention of maintaining staged storylines as real — as a model for American political narrative.[1]

## Relationship with Donald Trump

Speaking on the final night of the [[2024-republican-national-convention|2024 Republican National Convention]], Linda McMahon described how [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], "for fun," joined what she called one of "the most compelling and highest-rated storylines in the World Wrestling Entertainment's history," and noted that she had gone on to serve in Trump's cabinet running the Small Business Administration.[1] Trump was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2013.[1]

In his induction speech Trump recounted that his relationship with the McMahons and the company began at WrestleMania IV, held in 1988 at Trump Plaza in Atlantic City.[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that this event featured the first battle royal elimination tournament in which fourteen wrestlers competed for a single title, won by Macho Man Randy Savage, and that Trump referred to his later on-screen feud with Vince McMahon as "The Battle of the Billionaires."[1] Trump also described meeting Andre the Giant, who headlined the event against [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]], and at points in the speech eased into a "heel" character to audience boos.[1]

## The 2024 RNC and Trumpmania

Badlands commentators argued that the scheduling of Linda McMahon and Hogan — appearing under his legal name Terry Bollea — within hours of each other on the convention's final day was a deliberate communications choice rather than coincidence.[1] Hogan demonstrated kayfabe on stage by breaking in and out of character: ripping off his shirt to reveal a "Trump/Vance 2024" tank top, then calmly reintroducing himself as Bollea to describe his long friendship with Trump, before returning to character to coin "[[trumpamania|Trumpmania]]" and "Trumpmaniacs."[1] The writer speculated that the 2024 campaign itself resembled a battle royal to determine who would face Trump, noting that Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson — among WWE's most famous heel characters — had claimed in November 2023 that he had been asked by both parties to run for president.[1]

## Merger with UFC

In April 2023, WWE agreed to merge with the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]] to form a new publicly traded combat sports and entertainment company controlled by [[endeavor|Endeavor Group]], in what became known as the [[wwe-ufc-merger|WWE–UFC merger]].[2] Endeavor took a 51 percent stake and WWE shareholders retained 49 percent, with WWE valued at $9.3 billion and UFC at $12.1 billion.[2] [[vince-mcmahon|Vince McMahon]] became executive chairman while chief executive [[nick-khan|Nick Khan]] remained president of the wrestling business under new chief executive [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]].[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] warned that WWE could be "doomed" under the same homogenizing directives applied to UFC, and more thoroughly so because its programming is scripted, leaving stories and interviews to whatever the writers determine.[2]
