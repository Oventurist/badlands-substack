# Alvin Hellerstein

Alvin Hellerstein is a United States District Judge sitting in Manhattan who, at a hearing on 22 April 2025, considered challenges to the removal of Venezuelan migrants under the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]] of 1798.[1]

Hellerstein appeared inclined to require the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration to give Venezuelan detainees at least 10 days' advance notice before deporting them under the eighteenth-century statute.[1] He said he understood the president's desire to move quickly on deportations, but that the administration must nonetheless afford migrants due process.[1] Summarizing his objection from the bench, Hellerstein said: "This is not a secret court, an inquisition in medieval times. This is the United States of America."[1]

His hearing ran in parallel with a ruling the same day by Denver-based Judge [[charlotte-sweeney|Charlotte Sweeney]], who imposed a 21-day notice requirement for migrants detained in Colorado; together the two decisions signaled that the wartime-law removal mechanism might not survive judicial review.[1] The litigation was brought with the involvement of the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]].[1]
