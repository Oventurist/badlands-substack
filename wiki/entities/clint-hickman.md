# Clint Hickman

Clint Hickman is a member of the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]] in Arizona. He is named, in his official capacity, as one of the defendants in the election contest [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] filed on December 9, 2022 following the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]], alongside fellow supervisors [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]], [[jack-sellers|Jack Sellers]], [[thomas-galvin|Thomas Galvin]], and [[steve-gallardo|Steve Gallardo]].

As set out in "Arizona Earthquake!", the allegations against the Board include that it wrongly claimed Election Day tabulation problems had been fixed by mid-afternoon when at least 34 vote centers experienced breakdowns after 3 p.m., and that it attempted to conceal how many vote centers had waits exceeding 90 minutes.
