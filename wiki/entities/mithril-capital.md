# Mithril Capital

Mithril Capital is the venture capital firm associated with [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], a Trump ally.[1] [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] joined the firm in 2015 after clerking for a couple of years following law school, and there learned the workings of Big Tech and venture capital.[1] He subsequently left to found his own fund, [[narya-capital|Narya Capital]].[1]

The firm takes its name from mithril, the mythical metal of Lord of the Rings lore described as light as a feather but hard as dragon scales — part of a consistent naming convention across Thiel's ventures noted in an editor's note to the Badlands article.[1]
