# Elizabeth Prelogar

Elizabeth Prelogar served as Solicitor General of the United States, the federal government's chief advocate before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]], during the administration of [[joe-biden]].[1]

## Role in the Tennessee gender-affirming care case

Prelogar argued on behalf of the federal government in the December 4, 2024 oral arguments challenging Tennessee's ban on gender-affirming medical treatments for minors, a case in which the Court's conservative majority appeared inclined to uphold the state law.[1] During the argument, Justice [[brett-kavanaugh]] pressed Prelogar directly on the downstream consequences of a ruling in the challengers' favor, asking, "If you prevail here, what would that mean for women's and girls' sports in particular?"[1] The question was widely noted because Kavanaugh, a [[donald-trump]] nominee, has spoken publicly about coaching his daughters' basketball teams.[1]

Badlands Media commentary framed the argument as a setback for the challengers Prelogar represented, arguing that Justice [[samuel-alito]]'s focus on whether transgender status is an "immutable" characteristic could ultimately narrow, rather than expand, the legal protections sought.[1]
