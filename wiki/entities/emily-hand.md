# Emily Hand

Emily Hand is a dual Irish-Israeli child who was taken hostage by [[hamas|Hamas]] during the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 attacks]] on Israel and returned to her family over the weekend of November 25–26, 2023 as part of the negotiated hostage-release framework accompanying the Gaza truce.[1]

Aged eight at the time of her release, Hand became the subject of an international controversy over language when Irish premier [[leo-varadkar|Leo Varadkar]] described her on X as having been "lost" and as having "now been found and returned," rather than as having been abducted and held captive by an armed group.[1] UFC champion [[conor-mcgregor|Conor McGregor]] publicly attacked the phrasing, calling Varadkar "a disgrace" and stating that the child had been "abducted by an evil terrorist organisation."[1] McGregor further accused the Irish government and what he termed its "paid for media affiliates" of "constantly down playing / attempting to repress horrific acts that happen to children."[1]

Her release occurred within the larger exchange in which 69 hostages had been freed by Hamas and 150 Palestinian women and children released from Israeli prisons by the time the initial four-day pause concluded.[1]
