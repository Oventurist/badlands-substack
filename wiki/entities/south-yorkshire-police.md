# South Yorkshire Police

**South Yorkshire Police** is the territorial police force responsible for the county of South Yorkshire in England, including the city of Sheffield.[1]

## Adam Johnson investigation

The force investigated the on-ice death of American ice hockey player [[adam-johnson|Adam Johnson]], who was killed on October 28, 2023 when a skate blade cut his neck during an Elite Ice Hockey League game between the [[nottingham-panthers|Nottingham Panthers]] and the [[sheffield-steelers|Sheffield Steelers]].[1] On Tuesday, November 14, 2023, the force arrested a man on suspicion of manslaughter in connection with the death.[1] Police declined to name the suspect or provide his age, stating only that he was in police custody.[1]
