# Martin Van Buren

Martin Van Buren was the eighth President of the United States and, in the account of [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] for Badlands Media, a principal operator of the Wall Street-dominated Democratic Party machine built by [[aaron-burr|Aaron Burr]] after his return to the United States in the months before the War of 1812.[1] Ehret argues that this machine would ultimately drive America into a Civil War five decades later.[1]

The federal takeover of the Burr machine under the combined presidencies of [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]] and Van Buren, ruling from 1828 to 1840, represented a massive defeat of the Hamiltonian networks then led by [[john-quincy-adams|John Quincy Adams]], William Harrison, Matthew Carey and [[henry-clay|Henry Clay]].[1] Under the two presidents protectionism was dismantled in favour of British free trade, speculation grew rampantly as did the southern slave power, and the south was cleansed of Cherokee under the [[trail-of-tears|Trail of Tears]] and given over to racist oligarchs.[1] With the National Bank destroyed in Jackson's last year in office, the country lost its only means of generating sovereign credit for development, producing the panic and depression of 1837 that dominated Van Buren's term, while all major infrastructure projects were cancelled under the banner of "paying the debt."[1]
