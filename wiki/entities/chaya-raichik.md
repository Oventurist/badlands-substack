# Chaya Raichik

Chaya Raichik is the operator of the social media account Libs of TikTok. Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] calls her "perhaps one of the most blatant of right-wing hypocrites."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

DeLarme writes that Raichik has consistently amplified [[stopantisemitism|StopAntisemitism]]'s posts about the firings of Americans critical of Israel, including the case of Abulhawa, with comments such as "Consequences for hate are necessary."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) The charge of hypocrisy rests on her own history: Raichik was doxxed by journalist Taylor Lorenz and subsequently went on a media tour warning of the dangers of doxxing, saying, "Every time I have to give my name for something, my heart skips a beat. It's unnerving."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

Her role in the essay is as an illustration of the broader [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] double standard, in which figures who objected to reputational destruction when it targeted allies endorse the same tactics when applied to Israel critics.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

## Libs of TikTok and the Lorenz doxxing

Raichik is the operator of the popular account [[libs-of-tiktok|Libs of TikTok]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm) Badlands Media reports that [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] gained a greater degree of notoriety with the American right after doxxing the account, and then went on a media tour complaining about online harassment in the aftermath — an episode the outlet cites as evidence of the mainstream press's inability to reflect on the hostility it invites.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)
