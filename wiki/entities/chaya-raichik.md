# Chaya Raichik

Chaya Raichik, known online by the handle **Libs of TikTok**, is an American conservative social media personality who runs the Libs of TikTok account, which reposts content by progressive activists. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," she is described as "perhaps one of the most blatant of right-wing hypocrites."

## Amplification of StopAntisemitism firings

The article reports that Raichik "has consistently amplified" the posts of the advocacy group [[stopantisemitism|StopAntisemitism]] about the firings of individuals for their views on Israel — including that of the writer Susan Abulhawa — with comments such as "Consequences for hate are necessary."

## Doxxing inconsistency

The source argues that Raichik's position is hypocritical because she herself was doxxed by Taylor Lorenz and then went on a media tour "warning of the dangers of doxxing," saying, "Every time I have to give my name for something, my heart skips a beat. It's unnerving." [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], the article's author, highlights this inconsistency as an example of figures "willingly [abandoning] their ethics and stance on an issue when the tables are turned."
