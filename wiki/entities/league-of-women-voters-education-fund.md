# League of Women Voters Education Fund

The League of Women Voters Education Fund is the educational and litigation arm of the League of Women Voters, a long-established American voter organization.[1]

## 2025 elections executive order lawsuit

The Education Fund was one of the organizations that sued over [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s March 2025 executive order restructuring the conduct of federal elections, alongside the [[league-of-united-latin-american-citizens|League of United Latin American Citizens]] and the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]].[1] The plaintiffs argued the order was unconstitutional.[1]

U.S. District Judge [[colleen-kollar-kotelly|Colleen Kollar-Kotelly]] granted partial relief on April 24, 2025, halting the proof-of-citizenship requirement for the federal voter registration form while leaving other portions of the order — such as a directive tightening mail ballot deadlines — in place pending further proceedings.[1] Her opinion emphasized that the power to regulate federal elections belongs to the states and Congress, and noted that Congress was itself considering proof-of-citizenship legislation.[1]
