# Asif Chaudhry

Asif Chaudhry served as United States Ambassador to [[moldova|Moldova]] from 2008 to 2011.[1] During that tenure he interfaced closely with the oligarch [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]] and acted as a power broker and mediator during the tumultuous period that saw the elimination of the pro-Russian Communist Party and the rise of the pro-European integration movement under the [[democratic-party-of-moldova|Democratic Party]] in the wake of the 2009 [[2009-moldovan-twitter-revolution|"Twitter Revolution"]].[1]

The article argues that the American [[state-department|State Department]]'s hand pervaded this story, noting that Chaudhry was connected closely to [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] according to WikiLeaks emails, and that he has described his work carrying out Moldovan democracy reforms during this period as the most rewarding experience of his career.[1] Chaudhry later sat on the board of Jashi's Trans Oil Group, which funded the Atlantic Council Moldova Program's stream of pro-NATO commentary.[1]
