# Cassie Ventura

Cassie Ventura is an American pop artist and the former girlfriend of [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]], whose testimony became central to the federal case against him in 2025.[1]

## Testimony

Ventura detailed a decade of abuse and forced sexual encounters involving political and corporate elites.[1] Her account formed part of a wider body of evidence in a trial that, as of May 2025, had begun to implicate talent agencies, record label executives, and retired intelligence officers in cover-ups.[1]

## Context

The charges against Combs include sex trafficking, racketeering, coercion, drug distribution, and criminal conspiracy, with more than a dozen witnesses testifying about "freak-off" parties, blackmail videos, and CIA-linked handlers.[1] Raids on properties in Los Angeles and Miami in March and April 2025 reportedly uncovered firearms with serial numbers removed, fentanyl and MDMA caches, and surveillance footage of high-profile political figures at underground events.[1] Badlands Media commentary treats the case as a doorway into an entire system of power and protection, with implications reaching back to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and forward into Hollywood.[1]
