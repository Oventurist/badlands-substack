# Max Azzarello

Max Azzarello was a 37-year-old Florida resident who died after setting himself on fire outside the New York City courthouse where [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s hush money trial was underway in April 2024.[1]

## Incident

According to the New York Police Department, Azzarello had arrived in New York City earlier that week. Witnesses reported seeing him pour liquid over his head and set himself on fire at 1:39 p.m. local time on Friday, April 19, 2024.[1] Police said he walked into Collect Pond Park, opened a book bag, and scattered papers and pamphlets on the ground before setting himself ablaze; the NYPD described the materials as "like a conspiracy-theory type of pamphlet."[1]

Azzarello then fell on a police barrier. Civilians and court officers used coats and extinguishers in an attempt to put out the fire, and the New York City Fire Department responded and finally extinguished it.[1] He later died of his injuries, police said.[1] The Badlands News Brief of April 22, 2024 carried the CBS News account as a bonus item.[1]
