# Derek Hines

**Derek Hines** is a federal prosecutor identified in June 2023 as one of the attorneys handling the [[hunter-biden-plea-agreement|Hunter Biden plea agreement]] under U.S. Attorney [[david-weiss|David Weiss]].[1]

## Alleged conflict of interest

[[mike-davis|Mike Davis]], former Chief Counsel to Senate Judiciary Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]], asserted that Hines had served as special counsel to former FBI Director [[louis-freeh|Louis Freeh]] from 2013 through February 2015, and that in April 2016 Freeh — described as a long-time Biden ally — gave $100,000 to trusts belonging to two of then-Vice President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s grandchildren.[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the claim as evidence that the prosecution could not credibly be described as unbiased, stressing that the payments were reported as six-figure gifts to family trusts rather than campaign donations.[1] The episode was folded into the broader Badlands argument about a [[two-tier-justice-system|two-tier justice system]].[1]
