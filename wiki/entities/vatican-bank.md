# Vatican Bank

The **Vatican Bank** is the financial institution of the Holy See. In Erik Carlson's Badlands Media essay on the replacement of the global financial system, the Vatican Bank appears as one of the strategic nodes of [[cabal|Cabal]] control that the Trump administration is said to have wrested away.[1]

Carlson writes that [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] visited the Pope just hours before his death, an act the author interprets as "letting the Cabal know that they no longer control the Vatican Bank."[1] He adds that [[donald-trump|Trump]] and [[prince-william|Prince William]] were the only figures to wear blue suits at the Pope's funeral, which he reads as a signal that they stood outside Cabal control.[1]

The article then notes the sequence of events by which England became the first country to sign a trade deal with the United States, with the new Pope announced minutes later — the first pontiff ever born in the United States, and only a week after the White House released AI-generated images of Trump as Pope.[1] Carlson concludes that regardless of one's view of the new Pope's past statements, control of the Vatican no longer rests with the Cabal.[1]

These interpretations are the author's opinion and are presented as symbolic reading of events rather than documented financial fact.

## CIA drug money

The Badlands Media Gladio funding article identifies the Vatican Bank as the venue of choice for the [[cia]]'s drug money, alongside the Miami shell company [[general-development-corporation]] through which the finances of "[[operation-x]]" were managed.[2]
