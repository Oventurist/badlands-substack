# Bitner Villegas

General Bitner Villegas is the personal pilot of Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], identified in Associated Press reporting summarized in the Badlands News Brief of October 29, 2025, as the target of a United States recruitment effort aimed at capturing Maduro.[1]

## Recruitment approach

Former Homeland Security agent [[edwin-lopez|Edwin Lopez]] met Villegas in the Dominican Republic in 2024 and allegedly offered him money and protection in exchange for diverting Maduro's aircraft to a location where US authorities could make an arrest on drug trafficking charges.[1] Villegas remained noncommittal but continued exchanging messages with Lopez for more than a year, including after Lopez retired in July 2025.[1] Lopez pointed to a US Justice Department announcement doubling the bounty for Maduro's capture to $50 million and urged the pilot to "be Venezuela's hero."[1] Villegas ultimately refused, calling Lopez a "coward" and severing contact.[1]

## Significance

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry noted that the pilot appeared to have been in contact with US officials since at least May 2024, suggesting the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and allied agencies had devoted considerable time and resources to the single recruitment, and cautioned that an extraction operation targeting Maduro could not yet be ruled out.[1]
