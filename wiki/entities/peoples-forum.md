# People's Forum

People's Forum Inc. is a New York 501(c)(3) organization identified in the 2026 federal grand jury investigation of [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]]'s funding network.[1]

## Funding

Reporting cited in the investigation attributed $22,440,000 in Singham-linked funding to People's Forum, placing it among six key nonprofits alongside People's Support Foundation Ltd., Justice and Education Fund Inc., [[tricontinental|Tricontinental Ltd.]], [[codepink|CodePink Women For Peace]], and Breakthrough BT Media Inc.[1] Those nonprofits reportedly channelled at least $223 million into a broader global network including the People's Welfare Association, the [[answer-coalition|ANSWER Coalition]], and the [[party-for-socialism-and-liberation|Party for Socialism and Liberation]].[1]

## State Department report (2026)

A [[state-department|State Department]] report previewed in July 2026 is expected to discuss People's Forum alongside [[codepink|Code Pink]] as recipients of financial backing from [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]], within a broader allegation that [[cuba|Cuba]]'s communist government has spent decades cultivating radical left-wing movements in the United States and the Western Hemisphere.[2] The report alleges the organizations promoted positions aligned with the Cuban government and other authoritarian regimes opposed to US interests.[2]
