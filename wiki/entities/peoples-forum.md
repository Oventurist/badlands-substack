# People's Forum

People's Forum Inc. is a New York-registered 501(c)(3) organization operating as a movement space and educational center for left-wing activism.

## Funding from the Singham network

[[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]] identifies People's Forum as one of six key nonprofits within the funding structure attributed to [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]], reporting that it received $22,440,000. That money is described as part of roughly $278 million routed from Shanghai through shell companies and the donor-advised philanthropy fund of [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]]. The Brief states that the recipient nonprofits collectively passed at least $223 million into a wider global network including the [[answer-coalition|ANSWER Coalition]] and the [[party-for-socialism-and-liberation|Party for Socialism and Liberation]], and that prosecutors under U.S. Attorney [[jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]] have subpoenaed bank records and financial documents from organizations in the network.
