# Jalaluddin Haqqani

Jalaluddin Haqqani was the Afghan militia commander who led the group that became the [[haqqani-network|Haqqani network]], described by American officials ten years into the occupation of [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] as the country's deadliest insurgent force.[1]

## CIA sponsorship

Badlands Media contributor Joe Lange characterizes Haqqani flatly as a [[cia|CIA]] operative, funded and supported by three intelligence agencies: the CIA, Saudi intelligence, and [[inter-services-intelligence|Pakistan's ISI]].[1] Author Steve Coll, of *Ghost Wars*, is quoted saying "We facilitated his rise — we and the Saudis and Pakistani intelligence."[1] Peter Tomsen, a former ambassador to Afghanistan, told PBS *Frontline* in 2006 that the CIA "gave Haqqani the infrastructure" to build his network.[1] Lange argues these three services were fomenting terrorism across the Middle East in order to create a never-ending war, and calls them the true "Axis of Evil."[1]

## Suicide bombing

Haqqani is said to have introduced suicide bombing as a tactic in Afghanistan.[1] The source article stresses that during the anti-Soviet campaign his radicalism and profoundly anti-Western views were treated by Washington as an asset rather than a liability.[1] Lange notes that this history — the CIA creating the network that later became public enemy number one — has been largely ignored by the media, and that fear of such enemies is the motivating factor used to sell war to the American people.[1]
