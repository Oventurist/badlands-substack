# Metropolitan Onufry

Onufry is the primate of the [[ukrainian-orthodox-church|Ukrainian Orthodox Church]], the Orthodox body in Ukraine that was historically affiliated with the Moscow Patriarchate and whose status became the subject of Ukrainian legislation in October 2023.[1]

## Position on the war

Onufry has insisted that his church is fully independent of Moscow and supportive of Ukraine's fight against Russian invaders, a position at odds with the widespread domestic suspicion that the church had not truly severed its ties to Moscow Patriarch [[patriarch-kirill|Kirill]].[1] Earlier in October 2023 he said that it was the "sacred duty" of every believer to defend Ukraine.[1]

His statements came as the [[verkhovna-rada|Verkhovna Rada]] voted 267–15 to advance legislation prohibiting the activities of religious organizations affiliated with management centers based in a state carrying out armed aggression against Ukraine — a measure widely read as targeting his church.[1] The church had formally declared its independence from Moscow in May 2022, three months after the Russian invasion began.[1]
