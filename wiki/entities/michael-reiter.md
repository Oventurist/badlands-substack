# Michael Reiter

Michael Reiter is a former chief of police of Palm Beach, Florida, whose department conducted the original investigation into [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that among the materials in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s early-February 2026 document release was a 2019 [[fbi|FBI]] interview with Reiter. In it he described a 2006 telephone call from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], during which Trump stated that "everyone" in New York and Palm Beach knew Epstein was engaging in illegal activities with teenage girls, praised the police for "stopping him," described [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] as "evil" and his "operative," and claimed that he had left Epstein's presence upon seeing teenagers and had subsequently banned Epstein from Mar-a-Lago.
