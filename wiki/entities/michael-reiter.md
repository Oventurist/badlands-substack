# Michael Reiter

Michael Reiter is the former Palm Beach police chief whose 2019 [[fbi|FBI]] interview was among the materials disclosed in the February 2026 [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] release of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] investigative files.[1]

## 2006 call from Donald Trump

Reiter's interview detailed a 2006 phone call from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], in which Trump said that "everyone" in New York and Palm Beach knew Epstein was engaging in illegal activities with teenage girls, praised the police for "stopping him," described [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] as "evil" and his "operative," and said he had left Epstein's presence upon seeing teenagers and subsequently banned Epstein from [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]].[1]
