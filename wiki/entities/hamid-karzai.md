# Hamid Karzai

Hamid Karzai is the former president of [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]], described in Badlands Media coverage as one of the few figures in a position of power who publicly identified the external sponsorship of the violence that consumed his country during the post-2001 war.[1]

## Confrontation with Washington over Pakistan

In an interview with Der Spiegel cited by Badlands Media, Karzai recounted that when extremist attacks began escalating in Afghanistan around 2005 and 2006, his government raised the causes of the violence directly with the United States. According to Karzai, Afghan officials knew that the attacks were being organized by intelligence in [[pakistan|Pakistan]] and carried out by the [[taliban|Taliban]], and that the United States independently confirmed both that the violence was coming from Pakistan and that the Taliban's sanctuaries were located there.[1]

Karzai said that instead of investigating and acting on that root cause, Washington began funding Pakistan's military — the same military that Badlands Media argues was facilitating the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] drug trafficking operation in the region.[1]

## "Supporting both sides"

Karzai framed American policy as a fundamental contradiction: the United States was dropping bombs on Afghan villages to fight the Taliban while simultaneously funding the very country accused of organizing Taliban terror campaigns. He noted that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had publicly stated that America paid $35 billion to Pakistan over nineteen years, and argued that this contradictory policy cost thousands of Afghan lives, both troops and civilians.[1]

Asked whether Pakistan's intelligence service or the Taliban would ultimately decide the question of war or peace, Karzai answered that the Taliban are also Afghans, expressed hope that they would stand up to foreign influences such as Pakistan, and appealed to all Afghans to take their fate into their own hands against all foreign powers.[1]

## Assessment in Badlands coverage

Badlands Media credits Karzai with calling out the hypocrisy of waging a "war on terror" in Afghanistan while funding the country promoting that same terrorism, and with genuinely caring about the Afghan people being sacrificed — but notes that, like most public figures, he stopped short of naming the CIA drug trafficking that the outlet argues was the true center of the war.[1]
