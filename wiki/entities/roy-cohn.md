# Roy Cohn

Roy Cohn was an American lawyer who served as chief counsel to Senator [[joseph-mccarthy|Joseph McCarthy]] during the Army–McCarthy hearings of 1954 and who assisted McCarthy in his investigations of suspected communists inside the United States government and military.[1] Decades later Cohn became the mentor of a young [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as Trump was establishing himself as a real estate developer in New York City.[1]

## Role in the McCarthy investigations

Cohn worked closely with McCarthy in the campaign to expose communist infiltration of American institutions during the 1950s, a period covered in the article's treatment of [[mccarthyism|McCarthyism]].[1] In the Badlands Media account, Cohn belongs to a lineage of Americans who confronted communist penetration of the federal government and were subsequently punished for it, whether by death or by reputational destruction.[1]

## Mentorship of Donald Trump

Cohn later served as Trump's mentor during Trump's early career as a New York developer.[1] By that stage of his life Cohn had been publicly labeled a "mob" lawyer, and some critics of Trump have cited the association as evidence that Trump himself was linked to organized crime and corruption.[1] The article argues the opposite reading: that the Cohn relationship is better understood as evidence that Trump assisted the FBI in dismantling the Mob during the 1980s.[1]

## Character assassination thesis

The article presents Cohn as a victim of character assassination in the same pattern it applies to McCarthy, [[james-forrestal|James Forrestal]], [[george-s-patton|George S. Patton]] and [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] — figures who fought [[communism|communism]] and whose reputations were subsequently destroyed in the historical record.[1] The Cohn connection is treated as a direct genealogical link between the anti-communist campaigns of the 1950s and Trump's political conflict with what the article calls the [[deep-state|Deep State]] Cabal, mirroring the way Forrestal mentored Kennedy.[1]
