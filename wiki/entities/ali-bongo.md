# Ali Bongo

Ali Bongo was the president of [[gabon|Gabon]] until soldiers seized power in the central African OPEC member in late August 2023, four days after disputed presidential elections.[1]

## 2023 election and ouster

Bongo had sought through the August 26, 2023 vote to extend his family's 56-year hold on power in the former French colony.[1] The opposition described the election as fraudulent.[1] Army officers subsequently appeared on Gabonese state television to announce that they had cancelled the vote and dissolved the country's institutions, according to BBC and Agence France-Presse reporting relayed by Bloomberg.[1] Gabon's dollar bonds fell on the news.[1]

The takeover, if it held, would have been the ninth in sub-Saharan Africa in three years and followed [[2023-niger-coup|the coup in Niger]] the previous month, part of a wave that Bloomberg traced to economic malaise and weak governance.[1] Badlands commentators including [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the end of the Bongo dynasty as part of a broader unwinding of French and Western influence across West and Central Africa.[1]
