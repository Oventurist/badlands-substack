# Eliezer Berland

Eliezer Berland is an Israeli rebbe and the leader of the [[breslov-hasidism|Breslov Hasidic]] sect, a figure who has claimed to be the Messiah and who has been convicted of multiple crimes.[1]

## Convictions and allegations

Berland was sentenced to 18 months in jail for rape, having been accused of raping many women, and in June 2021 received a further 18-month sentence for fraud.[1] He was subsequently arrested, along with others, in connection with two murders committed in the 1980s and 1990s that Israeli authorities allege were part of an occult ritual in which victims were lured to a specific location by Berland's followers and then killed.[1] Reporting on the case noted that another suspect was said to be the son of a former senior government minister, and that police had questioned some of those arrested over allegations of kidnapping, murder, and conspiracy to commit a crime, while cautioning that not all were suspected of direct involvement in the killings.[1]

## Uman pilgrimage dispute

In September 2022, after Berland had been released from one prison sentence but was awaiting trial on newer charges, Israel permitted him to leave the country to make the Rosh Hashanah pilgrimage to the gravesite of [[nachman-of-breslov|Rebbe Nachman of Breslov]] in Uman, Ukraine. Ukrainian authorities turned him away at the border, producing a diplomatic dispute between the two governments.[1]
