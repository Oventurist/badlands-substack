# Kendrick Lamar

Kendrick Lamar is an American rapper who was selected as the halftime performer for [[super-bowl-lix|Super Bowl LIX]] in New Orleans on February 9, 2025.[1]

Badlands contributor [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] called the booking "interesting" and speculated about whether Lamar would perform "Not Like Us" during the halftime show, noting that from the song's second verse onward Lamar "rips on child rapists."[1] The remark placed Lamar's set within the Badlands audience's broader interest in public messaging about child exploitation.[1]
