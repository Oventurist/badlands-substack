# Reggie Littlejohn

Reggie Littlejohn is an American lawyer and activist, co-founder of the [[sovereignty-coalition|Sovereignty Coalition]], founder and president of Women's Rights Without Frontiers, and co-chair of the Stop Vaccine Passports Task Force.[1]

## Opposition to the WHO instruments

Littlejohn emerged as a leading opponent of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]]'s proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and the amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]], which were scheduled for discussion at the World Health Assembly in Geneva from May 21–30, 2023.[1] She warned that "if these amendments and if this pandemic treaty are passed, then that would basically be the end of our national sovereignty."[1]

Her assessment was echoed by international law professor [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]], who characterized the instruments as an attempted takeover of the United States using the WHO as a front.[1]

## Quoted in "Behold, a Trojan Horse" (May 2023)

A May 2023 Badlands Media article, "Behold, a Trojan Horse," cited Littlejohn as a leading voice warning that the proposed [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and [[international-health-regulations|IHR amendments]] would abolish national sovereignty.[2] She was quoted stating: "If these amendments and if this pandemic treaty are passed, then that would basically be the end of our national sovereignty."[2] The article presented her warning — alongside that of [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]] — as evidence that concerns about the WHO instruments extended beyond "citizen journalist" circles to established legal and advocacy figures, noting her roles as co-founder of the [[sovereignty-coalition|Sovereignty Coalition]], founder and president of Women's Rights Without Frontiers, and co-chair of the Stop Vaccine Passports Task Force.[2]
