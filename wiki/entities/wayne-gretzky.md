# Wayne Gretzky

Wayne Gretzky is the Canadian hockey player who held the National Hockey League's all-time goal-scoring record until it was surpassed in April 2025.[1]

## Record surpassed

[[alexander-ovechkin|Alexander Ovechkin]] of the [[washington-capitals|Washington Capitals]] tied Gretzky's all-time scoring mark on Saturday, April 5, 2025, and broke it the following day, April 6, with his 895th career goal scored against New York Islanders goaltender [[ilya-sorokin|Ilya Sorokin]] at UBS Arena.[1] The moment drew celebration from both American and Russian fans and was framed in Russian coverage as a shared cultural achievement between Moscow and Washington.[1]

## Career and political speculation

Gretzky turned professional at age seventeen, captained four separate professional teams, and holds several of hockey's most durable records, including most career points (2,857) and most career goals (894) — many set with a wooden stick and before modern safety protocols or equipment advances.[2] He retired in 1999 and subsequently coached and owned teams and pursued business ventures including a Niagara-area winery, maintaining his personal brand and name recognition over decades.[2]

In December 2024, President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly urged Canadians to launch a [[draft-wayne-gretzky-movement|"DRAFT WAYNE GRETZKY" movement]] to replace Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]], saying Gretzky "would win easily" but "had no interest."[2] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Gretzky's stature made his name recognition a golden political asset and that a Trump–Gretzky alliance could raise [[canada|Canada]]'s geopolitical standing.[2]
