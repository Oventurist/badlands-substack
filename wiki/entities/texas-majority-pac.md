# Texas Majority PAC

Texas Majority PAC is a political committee positioning itself to turn Texas — an overwhelmingly Republican stronghold — blue for the Democratic Party.[1] Fox News Digital described it in August 2023 as a "mysterious under-the-radar group" and reported that it had received hundreds of thousands of dollars from Democracy PAC II, a vehicle associated with [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1]

State campaign finance records reviewed by Fox News Digital and reported in January 2024 showed Soros contributing over $3 million to at least five left-wing groups in Texas over the preceding year, aimed at building political infrastructure and helping Democrats make gains in the state; the financier was described as "single-handedly propelling" one of the progressive organizations pursuing those goals.[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected the spending to the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]]' record on immigration, arguing that money was flowing into "the embattled border state in an effort to make US society more open."[1]
