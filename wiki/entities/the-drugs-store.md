# The Drugs Store (Vancouver)

The Drugs Store is a mobile brick-and-mortar shop opened in Vancouver's Downtown Eastside in early May 2023 by [[jerry-martin|Jerry Martin]], described as the only known storefront in Canada or the United States openly selling heroin, cocaine, methamphetamine, MDMA, and other drugs.[1]

## Legal status

Although British Columbia has very progressive drug laws, the shop operates completely illegally.[1] The province had recently begun a three-year decriminalization pilot project covering possession of small amounts of opioids, cocaine, methamphetamine, and MDMA, but the sale of those substances remains prohibited.[1]

## Rationale and context

Martin told VICE News that he opened the store to give people drugs that have been tested and are free from adulterants, particularly fentanyl.[1] The Downtown Eastside has long been considered ground zero for Canada's overdose epidemic, and while fentanyl drives record overdose deaths in both Canada and the United States and is the primary street opioid used by Vancouverites, Martin sells heroin instead.[1]

## Commentary

[[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], characterized the store as evidence that "Canada's downward spiral continues its death rattle around the toilet bowl," noting that cocaine, heroin and methamphetamine were now effectively available for purchase in Vancouver.[1]
