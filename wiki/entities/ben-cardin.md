# Ben Cardin

Ben Cardin is a Democratic United States Senator from Maryland whose office was drawn into a December 2023 controversy involving a staffer and a sex tape filmed inside a [[us-senate|Senate]] hearing room.[1]

## Hart 216 staffer controversy

After the [[daily-caller|Daily Caller]] published leaked footage that appeared to show a congressional aide having sex in Senate room Hart 216 — the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Judiciary Committee]] hearing room — the staffer was publicly identified as [[aidan-maese-czeropski|Aidan Maese-Czeropski]], who was associated with Cardin's office.[1] A spokesperson for Cardin declined substantive comment, saying: "We have seen media reports emanating from the right-wing media. As this is a personnel matter and under review, we will not be commenting further at this time."[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the office's terse "right-wing media" framing as emblematic of the way institutional Washington handles embarrassing internal conduct, contrasting the muted response with the reverence political leaders express for the sanctity of government buildings when discussing January 6 prosecutions.[1]
