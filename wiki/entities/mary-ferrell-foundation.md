# Mary Ferrell Foundation

The **Mary Ferrell Foundation** is a nonpartisan nonprofit organization described as the nation's largest source of online records relating to the assassination of President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1] Its vice president, [[jefferson-morley|Jefferson Morley]], is a prominent assassination researcher who has sued the U.S. government to compel the release of additional records.[1]

## Role in the 2025 records disclosure

The Foundation's advocacy and litigation form part of the decades-long pressure campaign that culminated in [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 23, 2025 executive order demanding the release of all JFK assassination records.[1] In the review triggered by that order, the [[fbi|FBI]] located approximately 2,400 records — within roughly 14,000 pages — that had never been provided to the board tasked with reviewing and disclosing assassination documents.[1] Morley, speaking for the Foundation's perspective, called the discovery "huge" and evidence that "the FBI is taking this seriously."[1]
