# Natalie Winters

Natalie Winters is an American investigative journalist associated with the populist-right media ecosystem, known for reporting on federal health officials, Chinese influence operations and pandemic-era policy. Her work is frequently cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributors.

In the July 30, 2026 Badlands Brief, Winters is quoted for reporting posted to X concerning Anthony Fauci's private-sector activity: "Anthony Fauci quietly joined a stealth mRNA vaccine startup in 2023. Then the Biden administration awarded it more than $28 million to develop vaccines for viruses with pandemic potential. Now [[eli-lilly|Eli Lilly]] is buying it for up to $1.55 billion." The Brief used the report as evidence in its argument that Fauci's financial entanglements extend well beyond his tenure at the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, describing the accumulated allegations as a "portfolio of crime."
