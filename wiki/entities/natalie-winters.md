# Natalie Winters

Natalie Winters is an investigative reporter associated with the populist-right media ecosystem, known for research into pandemic-era officials and institutional corruption.[1]

## Reporting on Fauci's biotech ties

Following [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]]'s July 2026 Senate testimony on Covid origins, Winters reported that "Anthony Fauci quietly joined a stealth mRNA vaccine startup in 2023. Then the Biden administration awarded it more than $28 million to develop vaccines for viruses with pandemic potential. Now [[eli-lilly|Eli Lilly]] is buying it for up to $1.55 billion."[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the reporting as evidence that Fauci's "portfolio of crime is thick," arguing it strengthened the case for prosecution independent of the contempt-of-Congress question raised by Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]].[1]
