# Warren Davidson

Warren Davidson is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives representing a district in Ohio.[1] In June 2023 he emerged as one of the leading congressional critics of the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]] and of its chairman, [[gary-gensler|Gary Gensler]].[1]

## SEC Stabilization Act

On June 12, 2023, Davidson and House Majority Whip [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]] of Minnesota introduced the [[sec-stabilization-act|SEC Stabilization Act]], legislation that would remove Gensler from his post as SEC chairman and restructure the agency itself.[1] In announcing the bill, the two lawmakers pointed to what they described as Gensler's "long series of abuses that have been permitted under the current SEC structure."[1]

The bill would not merely change the agency's leadership. It proposed adding a sixth commissioner to the SEC and barring any single political party from holding more than three of the resulting seats, a structural change that commentators read as an acknowledgement that the commission had become excessively politicized.[1]

## Reception

Davidson's move was received enthusiastically in cryptocurrency circles, where hostility to Gensler's enforcement-first regulatory posture was intense. Following news of the bill, the $GENSLR protest token rose more than 25 percent and the hashtag #FireGaryGensler became a top-three trend on Twitter.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the bill as part of a broader accountability push, noting that it followed a set of House-passed bills amending the 1933 Securities Act with bipartisan support.[1]
