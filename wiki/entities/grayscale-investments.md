# Grayscale Investments

**Grayscale Investments** is a digital-asset management firm best known for its cryptocurrency trusts, including the Grayscale Bitcoin Trust (GBTC).[1] In late January 2025 Grayscale submitted a proposal to convert its XRP Trust into an exchange-traded fund listed on the New York Stock Exchange.[1] The filing was described as part of a broader effort by asset managers to introduce regulated investment vehicles for digital assets; if the Securities and Exchange Commission granted approval, the Grayscale XRP Trust — then holding approximately $16.1 million in assets — would transition into an ETF.[1] Grayscale argued that converting the trust into an ETF would enhance accessibility while ensuring compliance with regulatory standards under a national securities exchange.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands commentary was sharply hostile to the filing, characterizing Grayscale as "a parasitic financial firm that built its empire catering to Bitcoin maximalists, only to pivot to XRP once its original grift collapsed."[1] The commentary argued that for years Grayscale had been a stronghold pushing the Bitcoin-only narrative while dismissing XRP and other assets as "unregistered securities" or inferior technology, promoting what the author called the "Bitcoin cartel's" self-serving ideology and funneling institutions into GBTC while ignoring Bitcoin's scaling failures, centralization issues, and diminishing real-world utility.[1]

According to that commentary, Bitcoin's fee crisis, mining centralization, and lack of adoption had exposed the underlying delusion, prompting Grayscale to "ride the XRP wave" after previously dismissing XRP as a "banker coin" with no future.[1] The conclusion was that Grayscale are "not innovators; they are bandwagon investors," that the firm "represents everything wrong with institutional crypto adoption," and that its interest in XRP amounted to "desperation from failed maximalists looking for their next swindle."[1]
