# ADL Center on Extremism

The **Center on Extremism** is the research arm of the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] through which the organisation's 2021 partnership with [[paypal|PayPal]] was led.[1]

Under that initiative, reported by [[reuters|Reuters]] in July 2021, the Center focused on uncovering and disrupting the financial flows supporting white supremacist and anti-government organisations in the United States, examining how extremist and hate movements take advantage of financial platforms.[1] The information collected was to be shared with other firms in the financial industry, with law enforcement, and with policymakers.[1] ADL chief executive [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]] framed the work as an opportunity to understand how hate spreads and to generate insights for mitigating extremist threats.[1]

Badlands Media characterised the Center's work as a pretext for financial attacks on political dissenters, arguing that the language of combating extremism functions as cover for targeting opponents of establishment dogma, and situating it alongside the freezing of reader-funded outlets' fundraisers by platforms such as [[gofundme|GoFundMe]] and Patreon.[1]
