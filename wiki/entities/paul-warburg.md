# Paul Warburg

Paul Warburg was one of the seven participants in the secret 1910 meeting at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] that produced the plan to transform the monetary system of the United States.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The article identifies Warburg as an employee of Jacob Schiff who had family ties with the Rothschilds.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) He was later singled out by name in the [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]]'s report on the banking trade, alongside Jacob H. Schiff, Felix M. Warburg, Frank E. Peabody, William Rockefeller, and [[benjamin-strong|Benjamin Strong Jr.]][[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

## Jekyll Island, 1910

Badlands Media commentary names Paul Warburg among the six men — with [[nelson-aldrich|Nelson Aldrich]], A. Piatt Andrew, Henry Davison, Arthur Shelton and Frank Vanderlip — who met secretly at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] off the Georgia coast in November 1910 to write a plan to reform the nation's banking system.[3] The article says the group represented wealthy bankers including [[jp-morgan|J.P. Morgan]], the Rockefellers and the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]], and that their plan laid the foundation for the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve System]].[3]

## Architect of the Federal Reserve System

Paul Warburg was, at the time of the [[jekyll-island-meeting|Jekyll Island meeting]], earning a salary of $500,000 a year from [[kuhn-loeb-and-co|Kuhn, Loeb & Company]] — a firm Badlands Media describes as Rothschild-owned — ostensibly so that he could lobby for a privately owned central bank in America.[4] He was among the small group of wealthy and powerful men who boarded Senator [[nelson-aldrich|Nelson Aldrich]]'s private railcar in strict secrecy to draft a central banking plan.[4] The article stresses that because the Rothschilds, Warburgs and Schiffs were interconnected by marriage, the participants were essentially the same extended family.[4]

After [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]]'s election, [[jp-morgan|J.P. Morgan]], Warburg, [[bernard-baruch|Bernard Baruch]] and others advanced a new plan that Warburg himself named the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve System]], embodied in the Glass-Owen Bill — a measure hailed as totally different from the [[aldrich-plan|Aldrich bill]] though it was virtually identical.[4] The [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]] found that Warburg, along with Morgan, Rockefeller and [[jacob-schiff|Schiff]], had gained control of major manufacturing, transportation, mining, telecommunications and financial markets throughout the United States.[4] The article also lists the Warburg Bank of Hamburg and the Warburg Bank of Amsterdam among the institutions its paper trail identifies as owners of the Fed.[4]

## Role in the Russian Revolution

Warburg is named alongside [[jacob-schiff|Jacob Schiff]] and [[alfred-milner|Lord Alfred Milner]] as one of the financiers who always intended [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] to be the leader of the movement that would take control over the dead bodies of the Romanovs.[2]
