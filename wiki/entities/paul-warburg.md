# Paul Warburg

**Paul Warburg** was a banker present at the secret 1910 [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] meeting, described in the article as an employee of Jacob Schiff with family ties to the Rothschilds.[1]

Warburg was later singled out by the [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]] report among the individual bankers implicated in the cartel led by J.P. Morgan, George F. Baker and James Stillman, alongside Jacob H. Schiff, Felix M. Warburg, Frank E. Peabody, William Rockefeller and [[benjamin-strong|Benjamin Strong Jr.]][1]
