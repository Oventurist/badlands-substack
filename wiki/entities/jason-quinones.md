# Jason Quiñones

Jason Quiñones is the United States Attorney for the Southern District of Florida, named in October 2025 reporting as the official who directed the empanelment of a federal grand jury in Fort Pierce, Florida.[1]

## Fort Pierce Grand Jury (2025)

Conservative attorney [[mike-davis|Mike Davis]], an informal adviser to senior Trump officials including Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], said publicly that a federal grand jury had been empaneled in Fort Pierce to begin meeting in January and to investigate a decade-long conspiracy to undermine [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]], including whether top Democrats conspired to target him through the 2016 Russia investigation and the subsequent prosecutions.[1] Appearing on The Charlie Kirk Show, Davis described Quiñones as his "buddy" and said the U.S. Attorney directed the empanelment after Davis "pushed very hard" for an investigation.[1]

Court records confirmed that a federal judge had ordered a grand jury empaneled in Fort Pierce the previous month at the request of the Trump administration, and Quiñones's name appears on the administrative order establishing the panel.[1] Badlands commentary counted the Fort Pierce panel as one of two new grand juries and suggested that, if Davis's characterization of their purpose is accurate, 2026 could prove consequential.[1]
