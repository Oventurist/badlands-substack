# Miguel Recarey

Miguel Recarey is a Cuban-American businessman identified in the Badlands Media article [[a-family-affair]] as the perpetrator of the largest Medicare fraud case in U.S. history up to that time, and as a close associate of [[jeb-bush]] whose escape from prosecution the source attributes to shadow government protection.

## International Medical Centers fraud

According to [[tampa-bay-times]], Recarey built his Miami-based HMO, International Medical Centers, by "flexing political muscle." He contributed to Democrats and Republicans and hired well-connected lobbyists. From 1980 to 1987, the U.S. government gave Recarey "kid-glove treatment" and almost $1 billion from Medicare. The HMO eventually collapsed amid allegations of shoddy medical care, forged HMO enrollments, phony billings, and bribery of union officials, leaving $230 million in unpaid claims.

## Connection to Jeb Bush

Among Recarey's supporters, the source identifies [[jeb-bush]], who not only did business with Recarey but also intervened on his behalf when a federal agency sought to enforce a rule limiting Medicare enrollment to no more than 50 percent of an HMO's enrollees. According to the [[tampa-bay-times]], Jeb picked up the phone and lobbied the federal agency to give Recarey's HMO an exemption. After the intervention, Recarey's HMO was able to enroll far more Medicare patients, facilitating the fraud. Recarey subsequently paid Bush's real estate company $75,000, which the source describes as payment for the intervention, though Bush claimed it was for real estate work.

## Flight and escape

Recarey slipped out of the United States in 1987 and fled to Venezuela. He later moved to Spain, where he married a Spanish woman and became a Spanish citizen in 1989. After being tracked down and arrested in a Madrid jail, a Spanish court freed him and refused to extradite him. The article interprets this sequence as evidence of "shadow government" protection, noting that a Medicare fraud case of that magnitude should have resulted in extradition and severe punishment.
