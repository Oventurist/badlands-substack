# Prince Bernhard

Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands (rendered "Prince Bernhardt" in the source) is described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a leading SS operative who went on to create the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]], the private transatlantic forum that Ehret says has managed the takeover of nation states since 1955.[1]

Ehret notes reporting that a Nazi membership card proved Bernhard had joined Hitler's party despite repeated denials maintained until the day he died, even while nominally in charge of Dutch resistance forces in 1944.[1] Bernhard's postwar prominence is offered as evidence for Ehret's central claim that Nazi personnel were not punished after the war but recycled into the institutions of the Western alliance, alongside those absorbed by [[allen-dulles|Allen Dulles]], MI6 and NATO under [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]].[1]
