# Chanel Pfahl

Chanel Pfahl is a former Canadian high school teacher who, according to her biography on X (formerly Twitter), works to expose "wokism" in Canadian schools.[1]

## Dispute with Annie Ohana

In March 2024, Pfahl was identified by CTV News Vancouver as the person whose online posts targeted Surrey, B.C. teacher [[annie-ohana|Annie Ohana]] over the flags and posters displayed in Ohana's classroom at L.A. Matheson Secondary.[1] Ohana described the attention as online harassment and said it was the first time she had spoken publicly about such treatment.[1]

Asked for comment by the broadcaster, Pfahl responded in a direct message on X: "Any reasonable person who looks through (Ohana's) thousands of posts on X will see that she is indoctrinating kids with radical woke ideology."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argued that framing such criticism as "harassment" is a tactic used to shield teachers using what he characterized as communist ideologies, and that state media and state-funded organizations like the [[canadian-anti-hate-network|Canadian Anti-Hate Network]] are enlisted to portray any ideological opposition as dangerous.[1]
