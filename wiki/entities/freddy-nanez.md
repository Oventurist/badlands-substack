# Freddy Ñáñez

Freddy Ñáñez is Venezuela's Minister of Communication and Information, the government's principal spokesman on media matters.[1]

## Response to the US strike on a Venezuelan vessel

Following a US military strike in the Caribbean that destroyed a Venezuelan boat and killed 11 people the Pentagon identified as [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] narco-terrorists, Ñáñez dismissed video of the attack as AI-generated.[1] US Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] rejected the accusation directly: "I can tell you that was definitely not artificial intelligence. I watched it live. We knew exactly who was in that boat. We know exactly what they were doing, and we knew exactly who they represented."[1]
