Reclaim the Net is a digital civil-liberties news outlet that covers technology, surveillance, and free-speech issues.[1] A May 2023 Badlands Media article cited Reclaim the Net as reporting that the [[world-health-organization|WHO]], through proposed amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations (IHR)]], was "pushing to greatly expand its surveillance powers," including requiring states to build "collaborative surveillance networks."[1]

The article also drew on Reclaim the Net to note the WHO's desire for "increased influence over the targeting of 'misinformation' and 'disinformation'" via the amendments — a point the article presented as evidence the treaty framework would empower a "medical global police state."[1]
