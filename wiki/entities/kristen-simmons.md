# Kristen Simmons

Kristen Simmons is a Michigan district court judge who presided over the state's criminal case against the Republican presidential electors charged in the [[michigan-fake-electors-case|Michigan fake electors case]].[1]

## Dismissal ruling

On September 9, 2025, Simmons dismissed the felony forgery and conspiracy charges against the fifteen remaining defendants, holding that prosecutors had not provided "evidence sufficient to prove intent," a required element in fraud cases.[1] She told the courtroom that the case turned not on the intent of the attorneys who designed the alternate-elector strategy — including [[kenneth-chesebro|Kenneth Chesebro]] — but on the intent of the individuals who signed the certificates.[1] Of those signatories, Simmons said, "I believe they were executing their constitutional right to seek redress."[1] The ruling ended the prosecution brought by Attorney General [[dana-nessel|Dana Nessel]] and meant the electors would not face trial.[1]
