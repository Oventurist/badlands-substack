# Citizens Defending Freedom

**Citizens Defending Freedom** is an advocacy organization active on election-integrity issues, with state-level chapters including one in Georgia led by state director Holly Kesler.[1]

Following the reported vote-count discrepancies in the November 7, 2023 Georgia municipal elections, Kesler said that "the 2023 voting results prove that Georgia can no longer afford to use voting machines," positioning the organization alongside advocates of hand-marked paper ballots and against continued reliance on the state's electronic tabulation systems.[1] That position stood in direct contrast to that of [[all-voting-is-local|All Voting Is Local]], whose Georgia director attributed the variances to human error and criticized hand counts as a project of "bad-faith actors."[1]
