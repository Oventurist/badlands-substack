# Anthropic

Anthropic is an American artificial-intelligence company and a principal competitor of [[openai|OpenAI]] in the market for frontier language models.

According to the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]], Anthropic was the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s first choice for a contract supplying AI models for classified United States military operations, but negotiations collapsed when the company insisted on strict red lines prohibiting domestic surveillance of Americans and lethal autonomous weapons. The Pentagon then turned to OpenAI, whose announcement of the deal prompted internal protests and a subsequent rewriting of the contract by chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] to add comparable restrictions.
