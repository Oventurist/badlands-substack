# Anthropic

Anthropic is an American artificial intelligence company, led by [[dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]], that develops frontier AI models and has publicly advocated stronger safety guardrails on advanced systems.[1]

## Role in the open-source AI dispute

In the July 2026 fight over [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source artificial intelligence]], Anthropic was grouped with [[openai|OpenAI]] as a company that has "generally supported stronger safeguards for frontier AI systems," in contrast to open-source advocates who argue that excessive regulation would entrench incumbent firms and reduce America's ability to compete globally.[1] Venture capitalist [[chamath-palihapitiya|Chamath Palihapitiya]] warned that a government ban on open-source AI would tank the market and crater both Anthropic and OpenAI.[1] Trump-aligned technologists, including members of the administration's Innovation Council, argued that restricting open-source development would benefit foreign rivals, particularly [[china|China]], while letting a small number of companies control advanced AI capabilities.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the push by [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] and Amodei for government regulation was an attempt to slow and control the industry's acceleration, placing it "in collectivist, oligarchic hands," and that the two men are therefore "retardants" rather than "accelerants" to the AI agenda.[1] He cited former White House AI and Crypto Czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] as stating that Anthropic and OpenAI seek regulation chiefly to stave off competitors, and Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] as saying the administration aims to "protect open source AI."[1]
