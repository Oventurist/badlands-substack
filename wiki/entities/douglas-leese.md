# Douglas Leese

Douglas Leese was a British arms broker and defense contractor with extensive ties to the arms industry and the British government, and reported connections to former U.S. Attorney General John N. Mitchell.[1]

## Role

Leese introduced [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] to [[adnan-khashoggi|Adnan Khashoggi]] and other figures in the arms world, and the men collaborated on covert arms packages whose timelines overlapped the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] period.[1] Leese's activity included commissions on major transactions such as the Al-Yamamah fighter-jet program with Saudi Arabia.[1] Epstein's partnership with Leese positioned him as a financial fixer, handling discreet transfers and shielding assets amid embargoes and sanctions — work requiring offshore entities, phony invoices and shell companies, techniques Epstein later refined.[1]

## Media treatment

The [[new-york-times|New York Times]]' December 2025 investigation described Leese only as a "defense contractor with extensive connections in the arms industry and the British government," a characterization that Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] said stopped short of exploring Leese's documented role in major arms deals and omitted his mutual ties to Khashoggi.[1]
