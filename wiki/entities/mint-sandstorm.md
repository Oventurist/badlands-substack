# Mint Sandstorm

Mint Sandstorm is the designation used by [[microsoft|Microsoft]] for an Iranian state-linked hacking group connected to the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]].[1]

## 2024 US presidential campaign breach

On August 9, 2024, the Microsoft Threat Analysis Center (MTAC) reported that a high-ranking official on a US presidential campaign had been hacked by an Iran-backed group.[1] According to the MTAC report, Mint Sandstorm sent a spear-phishing email in June 2024 to a senior campaign official, using the compromised email account of a former senior campaign adviser.[1] MTAC noted that the same group had "similarly targeted a presidential campaign in May and June 2020, five to six months ahead of the last U.S. presidential election," and had also attempted, unsuccessfully, to breach an account belonging to a former presidential candidate.[1]

Microsoft did not identify the targeted official, but its threat assessment team linked the Iranian breaches to intensifying attempts to influence the November 2024 US presidential election, describing "cyber-enabled influence activity" arising from actors "conducting initial cyber reconnaissance and seeding online personas and websites into the information space."[1]

Following the report's release, the 2024 [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] presidential campaign confirmed it had been the target of a cyberattack in which campaign documents were stolen.[1] Campaign spokesperson Steven Cheung told Politico on August 10, 2024, that the breach was attributable to "foreign sources hostile to the United States."[1] [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] characterized the incident as a significant development in foreign interference in US elections.[1]
