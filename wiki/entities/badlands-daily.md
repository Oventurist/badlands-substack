# Badlands Daily

Badlands Daily is a weekday news program on the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] network, scheduled Monday through Friday at 10am ET as of the January 2023 schedule.[1] It is hosted by [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] and [[canncon|CannCon]], who analyze ongoing events in what the network describes as a traditional news broadcast style, but without what it calls the spin of the mainstream media and corporate framing.[1] The show aired live on the [[patel-patriot-rumble-channel|Patel Patriot channel]] on [[rumble|Rumble]], with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Badlands Daily Monday through Friday at 10am ET, with Jon Herold and CannCon analyzing ongoing events in a traditional news broadcast style stripped of mainstream media spin.[3]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Badlands Daily in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[4]
