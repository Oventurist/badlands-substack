# Nahuel Molina

Nahuel Molina is an Argentine defender who, according to commentary in the Badlands Brief, punched the [[spain|Spain]] team captain as the two players passed one another after the final whistle of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] final, triggering a brawl as the Spanish squad began celebrating. The Brief's contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the incident as "the cherry on top of an ostensibly dirty match," consistent with his broader argument that [[argentina|Argentina]] played the tournament with unusual physicality and escaped sanction for it. Separate reporting cited in the same edition, from the *Daily Mirror*, described a post-match confrontation involving midfielder [[leandro-paredes|Leandro Paredes]] and several Spain players.
