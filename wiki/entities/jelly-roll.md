# Jelly Roll

Jelly Roll is an American musician who used his acceptance speech at the [[68th-annual-grammy-awards|68th Annual Grammy Awards]] in February 2026 to preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, declaring that "Jesus is for everybody."[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the moment as the third of three encouraging developments at the ceremony — alongside [[bill-maher|Bill Maher]]'s mockery of celebrity virtue signaling and [[joy-villa|Joy Villa]]'s protest against [[scientology|Scientology]] — characterizing the speech as gospel proclaimed "into that place of great darkness."[1]
