# Free Speech For People

Free Speech For People is a legal advocacy organization that served as counsel to the plaintiffs in [[naacp-v-useip|NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.]] According to the essay [[always-fight-back|"Always Fight Back"]] by defendant [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], the group launched a campaign to impeach President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Inauguration Day, January 20, 2017, announcing the effort in a Washington Post article titled "The Campaign to Impeach President Trump Has Begun."

The article cites that history as evidence that the Colorado suit was politically motivated rather than a genuine voter-protection action, and notes that plaintiffs were collectively represented by two law firms and at least six attorneys. Judge [[charlotte-sweeney|Charlotte Sweeney]]'s finding that a plaintiff witness adopted her identification of the [[useip|United States Election Integrity Plan]] at the suggestion of plaintiffs' counsel is characterized in the essay as a rebuke of the lawyers as well as the client organizations.
