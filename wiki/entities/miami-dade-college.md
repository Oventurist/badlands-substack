# Miami-Dade College

Miami-Dade College is a public college in Miami, Florida. It entered national political coverage in 2026 as the institution that conveyed prime waterfront land to the [[trump-library-foundation|Trump Library Foundation]] for construction of the [[donald-j-trump-presidential-library|Donald J. Trump Presidential Library]].[1]

## The 2026 land transfer

Plans announced on March 31, 2026 by [[eric-trump|Eric Trump]] specified that the proposed 50-story library tower would be built on land transferred to the Trump Library Foundation by Miami-Dade College.[1] The arrangement drew criticism from historian [[marvin-dunn|Marvin Dunn]], who described it as a "land giveaway."[1] No construction timeline was released at the time of the announcement.[1]
