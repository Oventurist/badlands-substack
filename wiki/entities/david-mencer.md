# David Mencer

David Mencer is an Israeli government spokesman who appeared in the June 2024 dispute over American arms deliveries to Israel.[1]

## June 2024 briefing

At a briefing on Wednesday, June 19, 2024, Mencer acknowledged that a scheduled high-level meeting between American and Israeli security officials in Washington had been cancelled, while adding that U.S. Ambassador to Israel [[jack-lew|Jack Lew]] had reassured Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] that the weapons in question were being delivered.[1] His account sat alongside reporting from [[axios|Axios]] and Hebrew-language sources that the meeting had been called off following a social-media video in which Netanyahu criticised the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration over alleged delays in arms shipments.[1] The [[white-house|White House]] rejected that framing, saying the meeting had only been rescheduled for reasons of timing.[1]
