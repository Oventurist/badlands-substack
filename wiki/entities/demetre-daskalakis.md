# Demetre Daskalakis

Demetre Daskalakis is an American public health official who served as director of the National Center for Immunization and Respiratory Diseases at the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]].[1]

He resigned in late August 2025, one of several senior CDC officials to step down immediately after the [[white-house|White House]] fired CDC Director [[susan-monarez|Susan Monarez]].[1] The other departures included chief medical officer [[debra-houry|Debra Houry]] and [[daniel-jernigan|Daniel Jernigan]], director of the National Center for Emerging and Zoonotic Infectious Diseases.[1]

The resignations occurred amid controversy over the agency's handling of longstanding vaccine policy and tightened restrictions on who may receive the COVID-19 vaccine, changes that prompted pharmacy chains [[cvs-health|CVS]] and [[walgreens|Walgreens]] to restrict vaccinations.[1] Senator [[bill-cassidy|Bill Cassidy]], chair of the Senate health committee, called for the September meeting of the [[advisory-committee-on-immunization-practices|Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices]] to be delayed to allow for "significant oversight."[1]
