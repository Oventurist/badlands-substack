# Michigan Department of Civil Rights

The **Michigan Department of Civil Rights (MDCR)** is the Michigan state agency responsible for investigating discrimination complaints and enforcing the [[elliott-larsen-civil-rights-act|Elliott-Larsen Civil Rights Act]].[1] Its executive director as of 2023 was John Johnson Jr.[1]

## 2023 Studio 8 Hair Lab charge

On November 15, 2023, the department charged salon owner [[christine-geiger|Christine Geiger]] and her business, Studio 8 Hair Lab, with discrimination after investigating complaints filed over her public statements that she would not serve people who identify as anything other than a man or woman.[1] Announcing the [[studio-8-hair-lab-discrimination-charge|charge]] at a press briefing, Johnson said that "based on a thorough investigation" Studio 8 and Geiger "openly and repeatedly violated the Elliott-Larsen Civil Rights Act."[1]

The action drew criticism from commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], who placed it in a broader pattern of [[lawfare|lawfare]] against dissenting business owners — comparing it to the multi-year litigation against Colorado baker [[jack-phillips|Jack Phillips]] — and argued that such proceedings are designed both to punish opponents and to chisel new precedent into the law.[1]
