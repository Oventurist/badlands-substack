# Arnold Rothstein

Arnold Rothstein was an American organized crime boss of the Prohibition era.[1]

Agents of Rothstein and [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]] made their first trips to the Far East during the 1920s to buy heroin from the British, marking the entry of American organized crime into the international narcotics supply chain described in [[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.*]].[1] Britain's leading distilling companies sold bulk quantities of liquor to Rothstein and to [[joseph-p-kennedy|Joseph Kennedy]] during [[prohibition|Prohibition]].[1] Rothstein is the historical figure on whom the mob boss in the television series *Boardwalk Empire* is based.[1]
