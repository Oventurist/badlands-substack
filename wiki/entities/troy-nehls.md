# Troy Nehls

Troy Nehls is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives who participated in the House GOP's independent investigation of the security failures surrounding January 6, 2021.[1]

## January 6 security report

Nehls was one of five Republican representatives — alongside Rodney Davis, [[jim-banks|Jim Banks]], [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] and [[kelly-armstrong|Kelly Armstrong]] — who compiled a report released in early June 2023 containing emails and text messages indicating that the office of House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] was directly involved in creating and editing the Capitol security plan that failed on January 6.[1] The lawmakers had been authorized to conduct the probe by House Minority Leader [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]], and their months-long review focused on evidence they said had been ignored by the Democrat-led [[january-6-committee|January 6 committee]].[1]

The report concluded that the Capitol was left vulnerable because Democratic House leadership and [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] leaders allowed concerns about the "optics" of visible armed officers and National Guardsmen to override security needs, while security officials said they were "denied again and again" the resources they requested.[1]
