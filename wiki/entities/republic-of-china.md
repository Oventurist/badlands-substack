# Republic of China

The Republic of China was the republican government established in 1912 after the [[wuchang-uprising|Wuchang uprising]] brought down the [[qing-dynasty|Qing dynasty]], with [[sun-yat-sen|Sun Yat-sen]] — a former doctor — as its first president.[1] Its founding marked the first time in Chinese history that a republican government replaced the dynastic system.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that the balance between strong government and the people's will has never been perfectly achieved, and that Sun Yat-sen understood it to be America's struggle as well as a challenge for China as it threw off the dynastic system in 1912.[1] Sun's early death in 1924 created a leadership vacuum that none of his allies within the [[kuomintang|Kuomintang]] were even moderately qualified to fill; Ehret compares the KMT leadership's corruption and strategic incompetence to that of the elitist Federalist party of [[alexander-hamilton|Alexander Hamilton]], arguing it left the party unable to preserve the mandate of heaven.[1]

Sun remains a revered figure in both Taiwan and mainland China, and is honoured by the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] as the forebearer of its revolutionary legacy.[1]
