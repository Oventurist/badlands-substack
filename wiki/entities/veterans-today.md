# Veterans Today

**Veterans Today** is an American alternative-media outlet oriented toward military veterans and dissident foreign-policy commentary.[1] Its financial editor, [[mike-harris|Mike Harris]], a former 2006 Republican primary candidate for governor of Arizona, has for years traveled to [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] in an effort to help the [[taliban|Taliban]] build a domestic economy capable of insulating Afghans from recruitment by Western intelligence into mercenary groups such as [[isis|ISIS]] and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited his interview with Harris in June 2024 as corroboration for accounts of Taliban conduct that diverge sharply from Western corporate press coverage.[1]
