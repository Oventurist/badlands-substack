# Glenn Danzig

Glenn Danzig is an American musician who was the original frontman of the seminal horror-punk band [[the-misfits|the Misfits]] and who has pursued a successful solo career since parting ways with the group in 1983.[1] Badlands Media presents him as a second example of an original [[punk-rock]] figure whose views cut against the progressive politics associated with the modern scene.[1]

## Support for the travel ban

In 2017 Danzig voiced support for the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s so-called "Travel Ban."[1] In an interview with the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] that year he argued the policy was mischaracterized: "It's really not a travel ban … When you walk into the country, we want to see who you are and what you're doing. Well, when I go to every country right now, they look at me and they see whether I can come in or not. And I've been turned away from Canada and other places before. Where's my protest? Where's my parade?"[1]

A writer at the music blog Stereogum responded sarcastically — "Yes, because that's totally the same thing" — and added "But hey, at least he's pro-choice!"[1] Badlands Media characterizes that reply as a non-argument that ignored both the broader problems the article attributes to the southern border and Danzig's willingness to evaluate individual policies outside partisan lines.[1]

## Views on public opinion

Danzig, described in the article as pessimistic about the future, said: "I think people are being brainwashed into just accepting the status quo and their not thinking for themselves. And when the truth is put in front of them they just reject it because it's not their political affiliation."[1]
