# Omar García Harfuch

**Omar García Harfuch** is Mexico's top security official under President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]].[1]

## Record fentanyl seizure

On December 4, 2024, Mexican security forces announced the largest fentanyl seizure in the country's history, impounding 1,100 kilograms — roughly 1.2 tons — of the synthetic opioid in the state of Sinaloa.[1] Harfuch said in a statement that more than a ton of fentanyl had been seized, along with several guns, and that two men were arrested.[1] The narcotics were found at two properties in the municipality of Ahome after intelligence work and public tip-offs directed investigators there.[1] Harfuch pledged that "these actions will continue until the violence in the state of Sinaloa decreases," a reference to the fighting between factions of the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] that intensified after the capture of kingpin Ismael "El Mayo" Zambada in July 2024.[1] Sheinbaum described the operation as the product of a long-running investigation.[1] The seizure came days after a tariff warning from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
