# J. Anthony Lucas

J. Anthony Lucas was an American journalist whose 1971 New York Times examination of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] is quoted twice in Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1."[1]

Lucas wrote that "from 1945 well into the sixties, Council members were in the forefront of America's globalist activism," a line the article uses to characterize the CFR's role in shaping American policy during and after the Second World War.[1] He also reported that "analysts of the Soviet press say the Council crops up more regularly in Pravda and Izvestia than it does in the New York Times," which DeLarme cites as evidence that the Council's secrecy has been thoroughly protected by the Western media.[1]
