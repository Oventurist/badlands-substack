# Michael Atkinson

Michael Atkinson served as Inspector General of the Intelligence Community, the office responsible for receiving and evaluating whistleblower complaints arising within United States intelligence agencies.

## Role in the Ukraine whistleblower complaint

According to reporting summarized in the [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]], Atkinson's decision to advance the whistleblower complaint attributed to [[eric-ciaramella|Eric Ciaramella]] — the complaint that triggered the [[first-trump-impeachment|Ukraine impeachment]] of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — rested critically on 2019 claims made by the figure then known as "Witness 2," later identified as [[gavin-wilde|Gavin Wilde]]. The Brief quotes reporting that those claims "were critical in helping Intelligence Community Inspector General Michael Atkinson push Ukraine whistleblower Eric Ciaramella's complaint forward," and that Wilde's [[russiagate|Russiagate]]-linked biases were concealed from House investigators during the impeachment process.

Critics of the impeachment, including Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], have treated this sequence as evidence that the complaint was advanced by officials with undisclosed conflicts, a pattern she associates with the obstruction described by [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] in *Government Gangsters*.
