# Niall Ferguson

**Niall Ferguson** is a historian and biographer of the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]] cited in Ryan DeLarme's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 6" for his account of the dynasty's political proximity.[1]

## On proximity to power

Ferguson is quoted as writing that "from the earliest days, the Rothschilds appreciated the importance of proximity to politicians, the men who determined not only the extent of budget deficits but also the domestic and foreign policies," adding that Rothschild influence extended to royalty as well, and that [[nathan-rothschild|Nathan]] first came into contact with British royalty thanks to his father's purchase of outstanding debts owed by George, Prince Regent — later King George IV — and his brothers.[1]

DeLarme summarises Ferguson as tracing Rothschild influence through British royalty to Queen Victoria's prince consort Albert and his son, and notes that the British Rothschilds became close to most prominent Victorian politicians, including Lord John Russell, William Gladstone, Benjamin Disraeli, Arthur Balfour, Joseph Chamberlain and Lord Randolph Churchill, the father of Winston Churchill.[1]
