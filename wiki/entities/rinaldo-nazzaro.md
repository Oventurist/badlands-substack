# Rinaldo Nazzaro

Rinaldo Nazzaro is the American-born founder of the neo-Nazi organization [[the-base|The Base]] and, as of 2024, a Russia-based figure described in mainstream reporting as the subject of an [[fbi|FBI]] investigation and, in the words of one US government official, a Department of Justice "matter."[1]

## Background

Nazzaro, 51 at the time of the August 2024 reporting, was a former [[pentagon|Pentagon]] contractor and analyst at the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] before founding The Base.[1] A 2020 BBC profile described him as a military veteran, a former [[cia|CIA]] field officer, and a defence studies expert, and reported that he had praised al-Qaeda and posted on social media seeking "volunteers possessing various skills, including with weapons," for his new organisation.[1] He is not known to have set foot in the United States for years.[1]

## 2024 recruitment effort

In August 2024 The Guardian reported that Nazzaro had written on his personal Telegram account that he was seeking a stateside leader for his organization, offering a salary of up to $1,200 a month, as the presidential election season began in earnest.[1] The reporting situated the effort alongside wider Western concern over Russian sponsorship of far-right extremists following racially motivated riots in the United Kingdom.[1]

## Badlands analysis

Writing as [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], the commentator argued the story "glows" — that is, bears the marks of an intelligence construct — and proposed that The Base is a creation of the Five Eyes intelligence array.[1] Points cited included the group's name being the English translation of al-Qaeda, its operations across the US, Canada, Australia, South Africa and Europe, and its terrorist designation by Canada, the UK, Australia and New Zealand — four of the five Five Eyes states.[1] The commentary invoked [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]] as the historical template for state-sponsored false-flag extremism.[1]

The analysis also noted apparent inconsistencies in Nazzaro's biography: he appeared to be a devout Roman Catholic of Italian descent who attended a Catholic high school and Villanova University, with a college yearbook quote about Mary Magdalen being the disciple closest to Jesus, while his organization and affiliates such as the [[order-of-nine-angles|Order of Nine Angles]] were reported to engage in satanic rituals involving animal sacrifice.[1]

Further circumstantial points raised were The Guardian's report that three 10-acre blocks of undeveloped land in Washington state were purchased in December 2018 for $33,000 in the name of a Delaware LLC called "Base Global" — with 33 flagged as a masonic number — and that Nazzaro's security consulting firm [[omega-solutions-international|Omega Solutions International]] listed an address on [[k-street|K Street]] in Washington, DC.[1]

## FOIA record

A series of Freedom of Information Act requests filed on MuckRock by a person named Jason Wilson one week after the January 2020 stories, seeking records on Omega Solutions International and Nazzaro, produced no-records responses from every relevant agency — a result the Badlands commentary framed as irreconcilable with The Base being described as a major counterterrorism focus for the FBI.[1]
