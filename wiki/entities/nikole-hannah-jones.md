# Nikole Hannah-Jones

Nikole Hannah-Jones (given as "Nicole Hannah-Jones" in the source) is a reporter and commentator, author of the [[1619-project|1619 Project]] and a staff writer at the [[new-york-times|New York Times]].[1]

## Commentary on Cuba

In the Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1," Hannah-Jones is quoted as claiming that "equality in Cuba is because of socialism."[1] Cuban American author [[antonio-garcia-martinez|Antonio Garcia-Martinez]] mocked the claim, responding that "if you make everyone poor, then indeed inequality would be lower."[1] The essay places Hannah-Jones alongside [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] among American voices it accuses of praising the Cuban government for their own benefit while ignoring its repression, and notes that even the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] published reporting on the plight of Black Cubans during the protest period.[1]
