# Marvin S. Antelman

Marvin S. Antelman is cited by Badlands Media as the rabbi and author of *To Eliminate the Opiate*, a work of research into the origins of modern revolutionary movements.[1] In the essay "Trump Knows," [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] credited Antelman's diligent research with unearthing letters and correspondence between key figures of the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]].[1]

That evidence is used to support the article's claim that the idea of a populist revolution was developed by power brokers in Europe who intended to use it to overthrow established power structures, and that the Illuminati planned such an overthrow using the then-new technique of [[color-revolution|color revolutions]].[1] The [[french-revolution|French Revolution]], in this account, was a successful beta test of the method.[1] Antelman's findings thus underpin the essay's caution that popular uprisings have historically been engineered and then recaptured by elites — a warning applied to the American founding era and the [[panic-of-1792|Panic of 1792]].[1]
