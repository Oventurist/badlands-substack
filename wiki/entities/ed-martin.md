# Ed Martin

Ed Martin is a Justice Department figure in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration who became the subject of a viral controversy in early February 2026. [[badlands-brief-314|The Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026]] describes what commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] calls "the Martin madness": rumors that Martin had been removed from his authorities, demoted and "sent to Siberia (or something)," which she says "made the MAGA movement melt all the way down."

Ashe in America cautioned readers to give the story time, arguing that the accounts amplifying it are known for what she terms "gayotic narrative deployments," particularly since the beginning of the second Trump administration. She notes that Martin himself appeared unbothered, posting memes about lawfare and presidential handshakes while, in her words, "the internet tried to victimize him as proof of 'Dummy Trump.'" Her conclusion — "It's all very silly. We need better psyops." — frames the episode as an example of low-quality information warfare within the movement, running in parallel with the concurrent story of [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|ODNI]] election review.
