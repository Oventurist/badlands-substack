# Danielle Neuschwanger

Danielle Neuschwanger was a candidate for Governor of Colorado in the 2022 Republican primary cycle and the most visibly supported contender at the [[2022-colorado-republican-state-assembly|2022 Colorado Republican State Assembly and Convention]].[1]

## 2022 gubernatorial bid

Energy at the 2022 convention consolidated around three gubernatorial candidates — Greg Lopez, Heidi Ganahl, and Neuschwanger — with Neuschwanger showing the most support in t-shirts and signage throughout the World Arena.[1] The governor's race was the first contest decided using the party's discontinued [[edivote100-clickers|Infowhyse EdiVote100 clickers]], and the announced result shocked the room: Lopez 34%, Ganahl 32%, and Neuschwanger 27%.[1]

Because Colorado required 30% of the assembly vote to qualify for the primary ballot, Neuschwanger fell 3% — roughly 112 votes — short of ballot access.[1] Immediately after the vote, volunteers at her table were bombarded with claims from delegates that the clickers had not worked.[1] Reporting by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that with the exception of Neuschwanger, [[america-first-movement|America First]] candidates won the day at the assembly, making her exclusion the conspicuous outlier of the convention.[1]
