# Maria Stephan

Maria Stephan is an American political scientist and organizer specializing in nonviolent civil resistance, with a career spanning academia, government, and the non-profit sector.[1] Badlands Media identifies her as one of the primary contemporary "scholars" and organizers of what the article calls government-funded [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]] — figures characterized not as independent anti-establishment crusaders but as operatives trained and financed by the state and groomed for their roles.[1]

## Career and affiliations

Stephan served as Director of the Nonviolent Action Program at the [[united-states-institute-of-peace|United States Institute of Peace]] (USIP) from 2015 to 2021, overseeing both global and domestic programming on civil resistance.[1] She subsequently served as Co-Lead and Chief Organizer for the [[horizons-project|Horizons Project]], a US-focused initiative described as uniting social justice, peacebuilding, and democracy groups against "tyranny" and building coalitions for "inclusive democracy" while emphasizing nonviolent action.[1] The article reads "inclusive democracy" as shorthand for the inclusion of social groups adhering to establishment Democratic Party dogma.[1]

She is also a fellow at the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]], described in the source as an extremely militaristic NATO-aligned think tank, and has been a regular fixture at the [[state-department|State Department]].[1]

## Criticism

[[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] has covered and criticized Stephan's career extensively and has provided commentary on USIP's role in the same space.[1] The article highlights a clip in which Stephan discusses how the sitting, democratically elected leader of the United States — [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — should be removed through nonviolent action, framing this as the danger of a career official funded throughout her life by taxpayer-supported organizations advocating the overthrow of an elected government.[1]

A July 2025 webinar featuring both Stephan and [[erica-chenoweth|Erica Chenoweth]] reportedly drew more than 130,000 registrants, focused on "the moment and your mission" for peaceful resistance.[1] Badlands argues that both women are thoroughly integrated into the Western political establishment, and that leftist activists are routinely duped into believing they are participating in organic social causes when the civil-society arm of the political and military establishment is directing them.[1]

## Vocabulary

Stephan's public talks are cited as a showcase of the terminology the article dubs "CIA-speak," including "nonviolence scholars," "strengthening democracy," and "[[democratic-backsliding|democratic backsliding]]."[1]
