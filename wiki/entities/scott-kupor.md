# Scott Kupor

Scott Kupor is the Director of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, and became a focal point in the July 2026 dispute over the status of the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]].[1]

## Statements on DOGE's status

Kupor told [[reuters|Reuters]] that DOGE "doesn't exist," a remark widely cited by outlets reporting the initiative's demise.[1] He subsequently clarified on X that while DOGE "may not have centralized leadership," "the principles of DOGE remain alive and well," language echoed by other administration officials who described the effort as "alive in spirit" and now executed directly by individual departments and agencies.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated Kupor's clarification as a meaningful signal, arguing that if the DOGE principles genuinely persist, observers should expect additional prosecutions for waste, fraud, and abuse, and a continued reduction in both the size and cost of the federal government.[1]
