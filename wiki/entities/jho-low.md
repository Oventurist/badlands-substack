# Jho Low

**Low Taek Jho**, known as **Jho Low**, is the Malaysian financier at the center of a billion-dollar Malaysian scam that funneled money into United States politics.[1]

## Role in the Pras Michel case

The global foreign influence scandal led by Low resulted in the 2023 conviction of Grammy-winning Fugees rapper [[pras-michel|Prakazrel "Pras" Michel]] on money laundering and campaign finance charges.[1] In April 2023, a federal jury convicted Michel of ten counts, including conspiracy and acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government; the Washington, D.C. trial featured testimony from actor Leonardo DiCaprio and former attorney general Jeff Sessions.[1] In November 2025, Michel was sentenced to 14 years in prison for his involvement in the scheme, which included illegal donations to the Obama campaign.[1] [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] prosecutors said federal sentencing guidelines recommended a life sentence, writing that Michel "betrayed his country for money" and "lied unapologetically and unrelentingly to carry out his schemes."[1]

## The 1MDB embezzlement and the 2023 verdict

Low is the central figure of the [[1mdb-scandal|1MDB scandal]], described by Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] as a massive embezzlement operation to steal $5 billion from the Malaysian sovereign wealth fund 1Malaysia Development Berhad, implicating bankers, Hollywood, celebrities and government officials in ten nations across five continents.[2] The [[us-department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] said [[pras-michel|Pras Michel]] conspired with Low, [[elliott-broidy|Elliott Broidy]], Nickie Lum Davis, George Higginbotham and others to run undisclosed lobbying campaigns at Low's direction, and at that of the Vice Minister of Public Security for the People's Republic of China, to have the 1MDB embezzlement investigation and forfeiture proceedings dropped and a Chinese national returned to China.[2] Michel was convicted by a federal jury on 26 April 2023, the same day Swiss prosecutors indicted two [[petrosaudi|PetroSaudi]] executives as further 1MDB conspirators.[2]
