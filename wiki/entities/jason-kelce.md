# Jason Kelce

Jason Kelce is a retired National Football League center who played for the Philadelphia Eagles and, after retiring, became a prominent sports-media personality. He is the elder brother of Kansas City Chiefs tight end [[travis-kelce|Travis Kelce]].[1]

## Beaver Stadium incident

On November 2, 2024, Kelce appeared with the ESPN "College GameDay" crew at University Park, Pennsylvania, outside Beaver Stadium.[1] While walking through the crowd carrying a case of beer amid cheers from onlookers, a fan directed a homophobic slur at him in reference to his brother's high-profile relationship with [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]], shouting, "Kelce how does it feel that your brother is a f—–t for dating Taylor Swift?"[1] Kelce responded by taking the fan's phone and slamming it down, an exchange reported by the New York Post and widely circulated.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the confrontation as an entry point for a broader argument about celebrity influence rather than about Kelce himself, with writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry noting general disinterest in celebrity culture while conceding that it "commands the highest levels of clout" in modern society and therefore demands scrutiny.[1]
