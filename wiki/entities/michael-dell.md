# Michael Dell

Michael Dell is an American businessman who founded Dell Technologies, the personal computer and enterprise hardware company, and has served as its chairman and chief executive. His firm is a major supplier of computing infrastructure, including systems used in data center and artificial intelligence deployments.

Dell was named in March 2026 as an appointee to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], per the [[white-house|White House]] announcement summarized in the March 26 Badlands Brief. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] counted him among the appointees outside the [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] orbit.
