# Trevor Noah

Trevor Noah is a comedian and television host who served as host of the [[68th-annual-grammy-awards|68th Annual Grammy Awards]], broadcast live from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on February 1, 2026.[1]

During the ceremony Noah made on-air comments about President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], asserting that Trump and [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] had spent time on Epstein Island.[1] Trump responded on [[truth-social|Truth Social]], calling the statement false and defamatory, saying he had never been to the island "nor anywhere close," and describing Noah as "a total loser" and "almost as bad as Jimmy Kimmel at the Low Ratings Academy Awards."[1] Trump said he would send his lawyers to sue Noah, invoking prior litigation against other media figures and [[cbs|CBS]] as precedent.[1]
