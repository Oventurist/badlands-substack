# Capital Jewish Museum

The Capital Jewish Museum is a museum in Washington, D.C. that was the site of a fatal shooting of two Israeli embassy staff members on the night of Wednesday, May 21, 2025.[1]

## May 2025 shooting

Metropolitan Police Department Chief Pamela Smith said officers responded to multiple calls of a shooting near the museum at around 9:08 p.m.[1] Arriving officers found a man and a woman unconscious and not breathing; despite life-saving efforts by first responders, both were pronounced dead.[1] A 30-year-old man from Chicago, [[elias-rodriguez|Elias Rodriguez]], described as pro-Palestinian, was taken into custody and believed to have acted alone, with his firearm recovered at the scene.[1]

The two victims were shot "at close range" while an event hosted by the [[american-jewish-committee|American Jewish Committee]] was taking place at the museum.[1] The event was for young Jewish professionals working in foreign policy and featured a panel discussion focused on finding humanitarian solutions for Gaza.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that while any killing is a tragedy, elements of the story suggested to him a possible false flag, singling out the panel's Gaza humanitarian framing and the details released about the victims.[1] Israel's Ambassador to the US, [[yechiel-leiter|Yechiel Leiter]], said the two victims were dating and on the verge of engagement, and that the man had purchased a ring "with the intention of proposing to his girlfriend next week in Jerusalem" — a detail the commentary called "quite the narrative needle to thread."[1] The author said he would watch how Republicans, especially those in the administration, responded, and whether they would seek preemptive measures on the public in the name of combating antisemitism.[1]
