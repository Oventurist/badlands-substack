# Daniel Blake

Daniel Blake was Group Vice President for Marketing at [[anheuser-busch|Anheuser-Busch]], one of two senior marketing executives placed on leave during the corporate shakeup that followed the [[2023-bud-light-boycott|2023 Bud Light boycott]].[1]

## Departure from Anheuser-Busch

In late June 2023 a source inside Anheuser-Busch confirmed in text messages obtained by [[daily-caller|The Daily Caller]] that Blake and Bud Light Marketing Vice President [[alissa-heinerscheid|Alissa Heinerscheid]] were "gone gone" and no longer worked for the brand.[1] The company had publicly described the moves as a leave of absence rather than a firing; the source explained that "if we publicly announced the word 'fire' it opens up the potential for them to sue us," and that the wording was chosen for legal reasons.[1] Wholesalers were told in person by leadership that both executives were gone for good, and their direct reports had already been shifted to new managers and the head of marketing.[1]

The same source offered a partial defense of Blake, saying "Daniel Blake was actually awesome. I think he was just caught in cross fire. But also he did hire her… so that's a fault," referring to Heinerscheid.[1] Of Heinerscheid the source added that "the wholesalers would have had an absolute HAY DAY with leadership if they didn't remove her."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the removals changed nothing structurally, noting that whoever conceived the [[dylan-mulvaney|Dylan Mulvaney]] partnership, someone higher up had to approve it, and that Anheuser-Busch had doubled down by sponsoring the Toronto Pride Parade shortly afterward.[1] He framed the departures as a small victory demonstrating the power of consumers voting with their dollars against [[esg|ESG]]- and DEI-centric corporations.[1]
