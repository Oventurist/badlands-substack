# Friedrich Merz

Friedrich Merz is the Chancellor of Germany.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief describes Merz as remaining deeply unpopular domestically, part of a pattern of weak European incumbents alongside [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] and [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]], with far-right parties leading or near-leading polls in Germany, the United Kingdom, France and Italy amid inflation, economic stagnation and immigration backlash.

At the same time, the Brief identifies Merz as among the European leaders seeking to maintain dialogue with Washington, noting that he planned visits to the United States even as European officials increasingly described the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's America as a rival and pursued "de-risking" through diversified ties and a rethinking of defence arrangements within [[nato|NATO]] and the [[european-union|European Union]].


## Criticism of US Iran policy and the troop question (April 2026)

Merz's public criticism of United States policy in the Iran conflict prompted [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to suggest that Washington could reconsider its military presence in [[germany|Germany]], which hosts tens of thousands of US troops as a core component of [[nato|NATO]]'s European defense posture and logistics network. Trump framed continued deployments as contingent on allied support and burden-sharing. Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] responded by deriding European leaders for getting "lippy" with the power that funds and arms the alliance.
