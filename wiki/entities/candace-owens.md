# Candace Owens

Candace Owens is an American conservative commentator who appears in the Badlands Media corpus in connection with [[kanye-west|Kanye West]]'s 2022 rupture with the entertainment industry. The April 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks" recounts that West appeared at Paris Fashion Week in October 2022 alongside Owens, both wearing shirts reading "White Lives Matter."

The Brief treats that joint appearance as the proximate trigger for the backlash that culminated in West's "Defcon Three" remarks about "the Jews" and the campaign to cancel him. Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] defends the stunt, arguing that West and Owens were drawing attention to what he describes as psychological warfare operations directed at white populations in North America and Europe, and asserting that all identity groups should be regarded as equal rather than one elevated above another. The corpus does not otherwise detail Owens's media career in this article, but positions her within the same cultural current the Brief credits with forcing debate over [[israel|Israel]] and American foreign policy into mainstream discussion.
