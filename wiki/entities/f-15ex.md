# F-15EX Eagle II

The **F-15EX Eagle II** is a fourth-generation-plus fighter aircraft produced by [[boeing|Boeing]] and marketed aggressively for export in the Middle East, where the company has positioned it as a candidate for [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]'s next fighter jet review.[1]

## Saudi procurement competition

As reported by Aviation Week on November 16, 2025, Saudi Arabia was again at the center of Boeing's export strategy, with the company promoting the F-15EX ahead of the Dubai Airshow and aiming to secure new orders in the region.[1] The Kingdom remained a primary focus for both upgrades of existing F-15C/D airframes and possible new-build acquisitions.[1] The proposal sits within a broader Saudi search that also weighs the European Eurofighter Typhoon and the Dassault Rafale, as well as a potential approval for an [[f-35|F-35]] acquisition depending on political conditions.[1] Saudi interest in the F-15EX was publicly acknowledged for the first time in early 2024, during the World Defense Show in Riyadh, when Boeing confirmed that discussions with the Kingdom about a possible F-15EX procurement were already underway.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands host GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that the heavily publicized [[saudi-f-35-deal|F-35 deal with Saudi Arabia]] may prove to be a "shaggy dog" — a long, drawn-out storyline with an anticlimactic and inconsequential ending.[1] In his reading, the more meaningful signal is that the Kingdom appears set to buy more F-15EXs (60) than F-35s (48), noting that the F-15EX is, as he understands it, the most lethal fighter jet in the sky after the F-22, which is fielded only by the United States military.[1] He suggested this also explains why F-35s and F-15s flew together over the [[white-house|White House]] during Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s visit.[1]
