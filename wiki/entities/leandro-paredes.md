# Leandro Paredes

Leandro Paredes is an Argentine midfielder named in reporting on the aftermath of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] final. As summarized in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], the *Daily Mirror* reported that a confrontation involving Paredes and several [[spain|Spain]] players occurred on the field after the final whistle, following Spain's 1–0 extra-time victory over [[argentina|Argentina]]. The reported incident took place after Spain had already secured the championship, and it is described in the same edition alongside an account of defender [[nahuel-molina|Nahuel Molina]] punching the Spanish captain.
