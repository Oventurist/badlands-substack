# Blue Cross Blue Shield

Blue Cross Blue Shield is a major American health insurance organization, led by chief executive [[kim-keck|Kim Keck]].[1] In early December 2024 the company drew attention over a reported policy of no longer covering anesthesia for the full length of some surgeries.[1]

That reporting circulated within hours of the killing of UnitedHealthcare chief executive [[brian-thompson|Brian Thompson]] outside the Hilton in Midtown Manhattan, and became the hook for social media posts by former *Washington Post* reporter [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] that appeared to celebrate the killing — including the line "And people wonder why we want these executives dead," attached to a report on the anesthesia policy.[1] Lorenz subsequently defended the posts by arguing there is "very justified hatred" toward insurance company executives for what she described as the death and suffering caused by a broken system.[1]
