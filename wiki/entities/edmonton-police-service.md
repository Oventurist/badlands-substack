# Edmonton Police Service

The Edmonton Police Service (EPS) is the municipal police force of Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.[1]

## 2025 women's shelter assault case

On January 24, 2025, the EPS issued a press release concerning allegations that [[mika-lin-katz|Mika Lin Katz]], a 37-year-old trans-identified male also known as Michael Collins, had sexually assaulted multiple women while staying at a women's shelter in the city.[1] The release revealed that Katz had first come to the force's attention in August 2024, when two women reported having been victimized.[1]

Following an EPS investigation, Katz was arrested and charged with two counts of sexual assault, then released with conditions barring contact with the complainants, prohibiting possession of weapons, firearms or ammunition, and requiring that Katz remain at least 50 metres from any women's shelter in Alberta.[1]
