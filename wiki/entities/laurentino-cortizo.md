# Laurentino Cortizo

Laurentino Cortizo was the President of [[panama|Panama]] during the 2023 national protests against a copper mining concession granted to a local subsidiary of the Canadian company [[first-quantum-minerals|First Quantum Minerals]].[1]

## Response to the mining protests

Associated Press reporting from October 30, 2023 recorded that, as demonstrations extended into a second week and a broad cross-section of Panamanian society joined calls for the contract to be rescinded, the Cortizo administration sought to calm public anger by promising that Panamanians would decide the matter in a referendum.[1] The administration proposed sending congress a bill that would schedule the vote for December.[1] Panama's top electoral authority responded that such a vote could not be held before the presidential election due the following May.[1] A referendum date of December 17 was subsequently reported.[1]

Critics of the concession said it put Panama's environment and water supply at risk.[1] The protest movement was also the context for the November 8, 2023 killing of two demonstrators by [[kenneth-darlington|Kenneth Darlington]].[1]
