# David Koresh

David Koresh led the religious community at Mount Carmel outside Waco, Texas, destroyed during the 1993 federal siege referred to in the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI" as the WACO massacre.[1]

## Treatment in the article

The article names Koresh among the individuals suspected by many of having taken part in the [[cia|CIA]]'s [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]] mind-control program, alongside [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]], [[jack-ruby|Jack Ruby]], [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]], [[charles-manson|Charlie Manson]], Jim Jones, [[john-hinckley-jr|John Hinckley Jr.]], Mark Chapman, [[timothy-mcveigh|Timothy McVeigh]] and John Salvi.[1]

Author [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] sorts these figures by the function he believes each served. Koresh and McVeigh, in his account, were used to make anti-government groups look violent and crazy.[1] This is the same purpose the article ascribes to the alleged infiltration operations of the [[cia|CIA]] and [[fbi|FBI]] more broadly: when a group forms that is anti-government, pro-limited-government, or simply demands accountability, the agencies infiltrate it and attempt to turn it into a hate group — or into something that at least appears to be one.[1] In some cases, the article claims, the agencies do not infiltrate existing groups but start such groups themselves, in order to create the appearance that anti-government people are violent and crazy.[1]

Waco is paired throughout the essay with [[ruby-ridge|Ruby Ridge]] as the two episodes through which the author first learned this pattern.[1]
