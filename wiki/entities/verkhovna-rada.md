# Verkhovna Rada

The Verkhovna Rada is the unicameral parliament of [[ukraine]], responsible for legislation, budget approval, and the confirmation of the prime minister and cabinet.

Under martial law imposed after the Russian invasion, the Rada has continued to sit and to serve as the formal ratifier of President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]]'s wartime government, while elections have been suspended. Its confirmation votes therefore carry unusual weight as the only recurring public test of political support inside the Ukrainian state.

In July 2026, as reported in the Badlands Brief of July 17, the Rada voted 289–22 to confirm former [[naftogaz]] chief executive [[sergii-koretskyi]] as prime minister, replacing [[yulia-svyrydenko]]. The same reshuffle contemplated changes at the Defense Ministry and other senior posts. Opposition deputies argued that personnel rotation without structural reform would not address underlying governance problems, and critics circulated unsubstantiated reports linking businessman [[timur-mindich]] to incoming officials.

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] drew particular attention to the constitutional mechanics, observing that "it was the Parliament that appointed this guy as PM, not Zelensky" — a point he used to argue that power in Kyiv is more distributed among oligarchic and parliamentary factions than Western coverage of a single wartime president suggests.
