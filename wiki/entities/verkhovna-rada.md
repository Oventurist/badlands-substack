# Verkhovna Rada

The Verkhovna Rada is the unicameral parliament of [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## May 2024 prisoner mobilisation bill

On 8 May 2024 the Rada passed a bill enabling certain prisoners to serve in the armed forces as the country faced a critical shortage of manpower on the front lines.[1] Kyiv had long opposed such a measure and had criticised Moscow's own mobilisation of prisoners, but reversed course amid fresh Russian advances on the battlefield.[1] Under the legislation, mobilisation would be voluntary and open only to certain categories of prisoners; the bill required the signature of the chairperson of the Rada and of President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] before entering into force.[1]

MP Olena Shuliak, head of Zelensky's party, announced on social media that "the parliament has voted 'yes'," adding that the draft law "opens the possibility for certain categories of prisoners who expressed a desire to defend their country to join the Defence Forces."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary questioned the voluntariness of a choice framed as remaining in prison versus leaving it, and scrutinised the exclusion criteria — the ineligibility of former high-ranking officials and those guilty of serious corruption, and of prisoners who had killed two or more people.[1] The commentary tied its scepticism to Ukraine's record on corruption prosecutions, religious persecution allegations, and the cancellation of wartime elections.[1]
