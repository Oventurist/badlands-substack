# Mohamed El-Erian

Mohamed El-Erian is an economist and investment executive and a former head of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]].[1] "The Harvard Control Grid" cites him as an example of the traffic between Harvard's capital apparatus and the institutions of global economic governance.[1]

The article lists El-Erian among Harvard-linked individuals who routinely attend the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]'s Davos meeting, together with [[michael-chae|Michael Chae]] and Blackstone's [[stephen-schwarzman|Stephen Schwarzman]].[1] It also places him, with [[glenn-hutchins|Glenn Hutchins]], on the [[brookings-institution|Brookings Institution]], [[group-of-thirty|Group of Thirty]] and [[peterson-institute-for-international-economics|Peterson Institute]] circuit.[1]

In the section on intelligence-to-finance feedback loops, the article states that El-Erian, though no longer at HMC, sits on the Group of Thirty and "advised U.S. administrations on macroeconomic warfare — including sanctions and central banking pressure tactics."[1]
