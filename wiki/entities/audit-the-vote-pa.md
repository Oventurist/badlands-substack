# Audit the Vote PA

Audit the Vote PA is a Pennsylvania-based election integrity organization active on X, described by Badlands as among "our friends" in the movement to scrutinize voter rolls and election administration.[1]

## March 2026 statement on Scott Presler

In March 2026, amid the push to pass the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]], Audit the Vote PA published a widely circulated post criticizing activist [[scott-presler|Scott Presler]]. The group called him "a grifter and a conman" who "pushed policies that directly hurt election integrity and caused massive losses in municipal/special elections in major swing states like Pennsylvania and Wisconsin."[1] It argued that Presler had embraced the SAVE Act only after "his early voting and mail-in ballot push was a colossal failure and he needs to re-invent himself," while conceding that his platform was drawing useful attention to the bill.[1]

The statement was quoted approvingly by Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who used it to illustrate a rift between turnout-focused Republican operations and the election integrity community over what the SAVE America Act should accomplish.[1]
