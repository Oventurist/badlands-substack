# Dwight D. Eisenhower

Dwight D. Eisenhower (1890–1969) was the 34th President of the United States (1953–1961), previously the Supreme Allied Commander of Allied forces in Europe during World War II and the first Supreme Allied Commander of [[nato|NATO]]'s forces in Europe.

## Invocation at the Ankara summit (July 2026)

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that NATO Secretary-General [[mark-rutte|Mark Rutte]], in remarks delivered on the eve of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s arrival in Ankara for the July 2026 NATO summit, went so far as to invoke Eisenhower in relation to Trump's push to force the European allies to do their part in maintaining the alliance. Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] reads the invocation as ironic — arguing that the European allies have "only maintained [US hegemony] through proxy control over the US Presidents over the intervening generations since Eisenhower reigned," and that the world is not returning to the US-dominated order Eisenhower's era exemplified.
