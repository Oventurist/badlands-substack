# Dwight Eisenhower

Dwight Eisenhower was the American president who authorized the CIA operation that overthrew Iran's government in 1953.[1]

After [[mohammad-mosaddegh|Mohammad Mosaddegh]] nationalized Iran's oil industry and a worldwide embargo was launched against the country, Eisenhower in 1953 authorized the [[cia|CIA]] to orchestrate the [[1953-iranian-coup|coup]] that overthrew Mosaddegh and installed [[mohammad-reza-pahlavi|Mohammad Reza Shah]] as the new ruler in Iran.[1]

## The farewell address and the military-industrial complex

Eisenhower is credited in "War Pigs" with coining the phrase military-industrial-congressional complex (MICC) in his farewell address from the White House on 17 January 1961, a speech written by Malcolm Moos and Ralph E. Williams.[2] Before Eisenhower delivered it, the reference to Congress was edited out, leaving the more familiar formulation of the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]]; the same concept is also known as the [[permanent-war-economy|permanent war economy]].[2]

Badlands Media describes the speech as an important warning that was not given much attention at the time, presumably because the CIA's [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]] had already given the Deep State a high degree of control over the corporate media — the primary tool used to drum up support for war.[2] Despite growing public awareness of the complex's exploits, the article argues, Eisenhower's warning still falls on deaf ears whenever a new war arrives with a good marketing campaign.[2]
