# Horizon Worlds

Horizon Worlds is [[meta-platforms|Meta]]'s social virtual-reality platform and the centerpiece of the company's metaverse strategy.[1]

## 2026 reversal

Meta had announced that Horizon Worlds would no longer be accessible in VR after June 15, but subsequently backtracked and confirmed the product will continue to operate in VR.[1] The company is pivoting its metaverse experiences toward mobile and away from fully immersive virtual worlds toward mixed reality.[1] [[andrew-bosworth|Andrew Bosworth]] said in a social media post, "We will keep Horizon Worlds working in VR for existing games, to support the fans who've reached out," adding that people who already have games they like will be able to download the Horizon Worlds app and use it in VR for the foreseeable future.[1] He also pushed back on what he called misinformation about the decision: "We announced, 'hey, we're moving away from Horizon Worlds in VR,' and the headline is that Horizon is dead. It's not. And likewise, VR is not dead. We're continuing to invest tremendously."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[matthew-trump|Matthew Trump]] described [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s attempt to stake his company's future on a virtual reality avatar world "that almost no one wanted to explore" as perhaps the most mock-worthy episode of the social media era.[1] He suggested the reprieve may have been granted out of spite for critics who greeted the shutdown announcement with joy, and predicted that the virtual world imagined by Zuckerberg will eventually return in a new, less repulsive form.[1]
