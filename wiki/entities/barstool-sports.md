# Barstool Sports

Barstool Sports is a popular and financially successful American media company founded by [[dave-portnoy|Dave Portnoy]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) It is referenced in "A Glaring Double Standard" as the platform on which Portnoy, whom Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] describes as a leader in the effort to destigmatize humor in the age of political correctness, delivered a June 4 podcast segment objecting to jokes about American Jews.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

DeLarme uses the company's brand of anti-cancel-culture, "manosphere"-adjacent comedy as a test case: if edgy humor is defensible in general, he asks, why should jokes about Israel or Jews alone be forbidden.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) The episode is cited as an instance of the wider [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] double standard the essay documents.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)
