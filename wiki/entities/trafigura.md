# Trafigura

Trafigura is a multinational commodity trading company and one of the largest independent traders of crude oil and refined products in the world. It appears in the Badlands corpus alongside [[vitol|Vitol]] as a broker enlisted in the reopening of Venezuela's oil industry after the January 2026 capture of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].

## Venezuelan oil brokerage

According to the January 26, 2026 Badlands Brief, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] called on Trafigura and Vitol to begin brokering deals with potential buyers of Venezuelan crude as Venezuela's legislature moved to liberalize the sector under Acting President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]], reversing the state-control model established under [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]].

## Corruption cases

The Brief states that Trafigura was named as a defendant, along with Vitol, in a lawsuit filed by Maduro in both U.S. and Swiss courts in March 2018, and that the company has had executives arrested for corruption. It reports that Trafigura's former chief executive, identified as Mark Wainwright, was sentenced in Swiss court in February 2025 to 32 months in prison after being found guilty of paying over $5 billion in bribes to oil officials in Africa in order to secure contracts — conduct the commentary describes as connected to Maduro's 2018 suit.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] uses this record to argue that the resumption of Venezuelan oil flows through Trafigura and Vitol could trip anti-money-laundering controls and generate Suspicious Activity Reports, with potentially systemic consequences for transnational banking.
