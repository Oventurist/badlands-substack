# Trilateral Commission

The Trilateral Commission is a private international organisation listed in the Badlands Media essay [[and-iran-iran-so-far-away|"And Iran, Iran So Far Away"]] among the elite bodies that constitute what the author calls the foreign policy establishment.

The article names the Commission as an affiliation of [[jake-sullivan]], the policy-level architect of the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran Deal]], and of [[tom-donilon]], who is described as a regular fixture at "prestigious deep political think tanks" including [[bilderberg]], the [[council-on-foreign-relations]], the Trilateral Commission and the [[world-economic-forum]]. Such venues are characterised in the article as the places "where the heads of industry, finance, military, intelligence and politics convene."
