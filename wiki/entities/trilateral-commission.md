# Trilateral Commission

The **Trilateral Commission** is one of the elite policy forums Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] uses to map the membership of what he calls the foreign policy establishment.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

[[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]], who worked on the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran Deal]] at the policy level, is listed as a Trilateral Commission affiliate alongside his ties to the 7th Floor group, the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace|Carnegie Endowment]] and the Rhodes Scholarship.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) [[tom-donilon|Tom Donilon]] is likewise described as a regular fixture at the Trilateral Commission, together with [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]], the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)
