# Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department

The Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department (LVMPD) is the principal law-enforcement agency serving Las Vegas and surrounding Clark County, Nevada.[1]

## 2025 internet crimes against children sting

In August 2025 the LVMPD, working with other Nevada authorities, conducted a two-week undercover investigation aimed at online users seeking to sexually prey on children.[1] The department announced the results in a statement published on a Friday, disclosing that multiple suspects had been apprehended during the operation.[1]

Among those arrested was [[tom-artiom-alexandrovich|Tom Artiom Alexandrovich]], a 38-year-old Israeli government cybersecurity official identified online as an executive of the [[israel-national-cyber-directorate|Israel Cyber Directorate]], who was charged with felony luring of a child with a computer for a sex act.[1] Alexandrovich was released on $10,000 bail after an initial court appearance and returned to Israel, a sequence that drew heavy commentary and claims of political interference.[1] Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that charging and bail decisions in the case rested with local Nevada authorities rather than the federal government, which would have lacked jurisdiction to direct them.[1]
