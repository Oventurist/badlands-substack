# Cathy O'Brien

Cathy O'Brien is an American author known within alternative-media circles as a self-described survivor of [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]], the CIA mind-control programme.[1]

O'Brien is invoked in discussions of mind control as representing the archetype of the phenomenon in its overt, technological form — described as "robotic and distinct," involving trauma-based conditioning.[1] [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] has argued that this archetype, while real in its own terms, is too narrow a lens: modern [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]] should be recognised as a subtler contemporary form of mind control that requires neither sexual trauma rituals nor advanced neurological technology, achieving comparable results through slow generational conditioning.[1] On that reading, students at the [[2024-pro-palestinian-campus-protests|2024 pro-Palestinian campus protests]] who could not explain why they were present were exhibiting the output of a centralised system of control no less real for being diffuse.[1]
