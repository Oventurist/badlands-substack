# King Abdullah II

Abdullah II is the King of [[jordan|Jordan]], the Hashemite monarch whose government in April 2025 outlawed the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] and who conducted high-level diplomacy with [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] in the same period.[1]

## Jeddah meeting with Mohammed bin Salman

On Wednesday, April 23, 2025, King Abdullah met Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] during an official visit to Saudi Arabia.[1] The King arrived in Jeddah accompanied by Crown Prince Hussein and was received by the deputy governor of Makkah, Prince Saud bin Mishaal bin Abdulaziz, according to the Saudi Press Agency.[1] During the meeting he expressed pride in the historical relations between Riyadh and Amman and acknowledged the significant role of Saudi Arabia in supporting Arab and Islamic causes, as reported by the Petra news agency.[1] The two leaders discussed recent regional developments, particularly the situation in the Gaza Strip and the occupied West Bank.[1]

## Context with the Brotherhood ban

The Jeddah meeting occurred the same day that Jordan's Interior Ministry, under Interior Minister [[mazen-faraya|Mazen Faraya]], declared the Muslim Brotherhood dissolved and its membership illegal.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the timing as significant, reading it as part of a broader expulsion of Western intelligence-linked networks from the Middle East ahead of a planned visit by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to Riyadh.[1]

## Canceled Biden summit (October 2023)

On October 17, 2023 the [[white-house|White House]] canceled the Jordan leg of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s Middle East trip after consulting Abdullah II, and in light of the days of mourning declared by [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] President [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] following the [[al-ahli-baptist-hospital-explosion|al-Ahli Baptist Hospital explosion]] in Gaza.[2] The planned Amman summit was to have brought together Biden, Abdullah II, Abbas and Egyptian President [[abdel-fattah-el-sisi|Abdel Fattah el-Sisi]]; its collapse came in the same hour that Arab leaders announced they were unwilling to meet Biden given United States support for Israel.[2]
