# Tony Brown

Tony Brown is the county clerk of Laurel County, Kentucky, who became a figure in election-integrity coverage during the 2024 US general election.[1]

## Laurel County voting machine incident (October 2024)

On 31 October 2024 a video circulated widely showing a Laurel County voter whose selection of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on an electronic voting machine registered instead for [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1] Such clips had generally been treated with skepticism, but this case was distinguished by the fact that Brown issued a statement on the county clerk's official Facebook page confirming that the issue "did occur" — after having initially denied it.[1] Brown said the machine was taken out of service pending the arrival of a representative from the state attorney general's office.[1]

The episode was cited alongside other reported irregularities of the cycle — stolen ballots, leaked machine passwords, "known issues," unscannable ballots and ballot-marking-device misprints — in commentary arguing that the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 general election]] outcome was unverifiable.[1]
