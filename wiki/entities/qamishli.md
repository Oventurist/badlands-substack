# Qamishli

Qamishli is a Kurdish-majority city in northeastern [[syria|Syria]]. In early February 2026, Syrian government security forces entered the city under a U.S.-brokered deal designed to reintegrate Kurdish-controlled areas into central government authority, while Kurdish police continued to maintain day-to-day security in the city.[1]

The arrangement formed part of a broader reconciliation track in which U.S. envoy [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]] worked to bring the [[kurds|Kurds]] and Damascus together, running in parallel with Saudi investment in Syria's reconstruction and renewed U.S. airstrikes against [[islamic-state|Islamic State]] remnants elsewhere in the country.[1]
