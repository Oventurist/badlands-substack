# Michael Horowitz

Michael Horowitz is the Inspector General of the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]. In the Badlands Media essay America for Sale — Part 2, he is presented as the investigative half of a two-part strategy devised by Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] to work around a compromised [[fbi|FBI]] and Justice Department.

The article states that Sessions assigned Horowitz to conduct investigations instead of the FBI, stressing that the Inspector General's office is independent, that an IG office oversees every federal agency, and that IGs have "a broad reach to investigate all corruption involving our government." Q drop 1517 is quoted for the formulation "IG started long before Huber setting stage" and "Think: IG = FBI."

Breitbart, quoted in the essay, reported that Horowitz was working with U.S. Attorney [[john-huber|John Huber]] and commanded a staff of 470 investigators, giving Huber access to investigative resources far exceeding those of any special counsel. Sessions' March 29 letter to Congress is cited for two points about the scope of that jurisdiction: it includes "actions taken by former employees after they have left government service," and it extends "not only to allegations of legal violations, but also to allegations that Department employees violated established practices as well" — meaning an IG report can hold officials accountable for conduct that violates no specific statute.

The author's broader claim, that this arrangement produced hundreds of thousands of sealed indictments awaiting a future prosecution phase, is interpretive and contested.
