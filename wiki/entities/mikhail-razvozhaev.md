# Mikhail Razvozhaev

Mikhail Razvozhaev is the Russia-installed governor of Sevastopol, the Crimean city that hosts Russia's principal Black Sea naval port.[1]

Following the attack on the [[sevastopol-shipyard|Sevastopol Shipyard]] in the early hours of September 13, 2023, Razvozhaev announced on the Telegram messaging app that a Ukrainian air attack had sparked a fire at the yard and had injured at least 24 people.[1] He stated bluntly that "Our enemies attacked Sevastopol" and that "The air defense was at work," remarks that formed the basis for initial Reuters and CNN reporting attributing the blaze to a missile strike.[1] At least four of the injured were described as being in serious condition.[1]
