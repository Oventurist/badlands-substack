# Lebanon

Lebanon is a country in the Middle East. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," Lebanon is cited as the site of a bunker containing more than $1.5 billion in stolen Iraqi reconstruction cash, and as the ancestral homeland of [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] through his mother, Mona El Solh, the daughter of Riad El Solh, Lebanon's first Prime Minister.

According to the article, an investigation conducted during the [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush administration]] discovered the bunker and its contents, but Bush personally suppressed the investigation. The source claims the money ended up funding [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], the Shia Islamist militant group based in Lebanon. The article also connects Lebanon to the mysterious cash handler Basel, who was described as a naturalized American citizen of Lebanese descent.

The source frames Lebanon as a critical node in the covert network it describes, with stolen taxpayer money flowing from Iraq to bunkers in Lebanon and then to terrorist organizations. It presents the suppression of the bunker investigation as evidence of deliberate government complicity in the diversion of public funds to hostile actors.


## April 2026: occupation, desecration, and the Forward Defense Zone

The Brief of April 20, 2026 reports that [[israel|Israel]] ordered troops to use "full force" against threats in Lebanon despite the temporary truce, and that the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] published maps of a new deployment line placing dozens of villages inside an Israeli-controlled zone extending 5-10 kilometers from the border — the [[forward-defense-zone|Forward Defense Zone]] — where five divisions backed by naval forces operate. The IDF moved northeast toward [[mount-hermon|Mount Hermon]], capturing Lebanese lands bordering the [[golan-heights|Golan Heights]] and [[syria|Syria]], and the Brief argues the border shift toward Tyre is designed to enable annexation of the [[qana-gas-field|Qana Gas Field]]. It also documents the verified smashing of a statue of Jesus in the Christian village of Debl, the reported destruction of the [[tomb-of-saint-peter|Tomb of Saint Peter]], and warnings that Lebanese Christians should not return to their villages despite the ceasefire — incidents the commentary frames within the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel project]].
