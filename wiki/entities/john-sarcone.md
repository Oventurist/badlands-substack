# John Sarcone III

John Sarcone III served as US Attorney for the Northern District of New York until a federal judge disqualified him in January 2026.

## Disqualification

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that US District Judge Lorna Schofield, an Obama appointee, disqualified Sarcone in a 24-page opinion finding that he had been serving unlawfully, and invalidated subpoenas his office had issued to New York Attorney General Letitia James. Schofield wrote: "When the Executive branch of government skirts restraints put in place by Congress and then uses that power to subject political adversaries to criminal investigations, it acts without lawful authority."

## The appointment dispute

Sarcone's authority was challenged after what the Brief calls "unconventional moves" to keep him in office beyond his 120-day term. When local judges declined to reappoint him, Attorney General Pam Bondi named him a "special attorney" with an indefinite term.

The ruling was one in a series of decisions rejecting efforts by the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to install US attorneys without [[senate|Senate]] confirmation; judges had already removed US attorneys in New Jersey and Delaware, and others in Nevada, Los Angeles, and Virginia, where the dismissal of Lindsey Halligan invalidated indictments against James and former FBI Director James Comey.
