# Doug Band

Doug Band was [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]]'s closest aide and right-hand man, and in the article's account a recurring marker of where the money in Clinton world actually moved.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Band appears in the photograph of the 2009 North Korean hostage negotiation that [[qanon|Q]] posted without comment as drop #227 on November 24, 2017, standing directly behind [[kim-jong-il|Kim Jong Il]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) At the time he was working for the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] and the [[clinton-global-initiative|Clinton Global Initiative]], a fact the article calls a key data point: when Clinton travelled to [[north-korea|North Korea]] in 2009 with Band, the author argues, they were not merely there to free hostages but to make a deal.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The Clinton Global Initiative is described as the brainchild of Band, and the article states that almost all of the money flowing through CGI went through him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) It also notes that [[gilbert-chagoury|Gilbert Chagoury]] — the Nigerian businessman convicted of money laundering who pledged $1 billion to CGI in 2009 — attended Band's wedding, alongside his other appearances in Clinton social life.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article notes it had established Band's importance in a previous installment of the America for Sale series.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Biography and rise from White House intern

Douglas Jay Band was born on October 28, 1972, an American businessman and lawyer who became a founding partner and former president of [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]], a multinational C-suite advisory firm and investment bank.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) He was previously Deputy Assistant to President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] in the [[white-house|White House]] and later counselor to the former president, and is described as the "key architect" of the Clinton post-presidency and the creator of the [[clinton-global-initiative|Clinton Global Initiative]].2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) He later worked for the William J. [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] and travelled to [[north-korea|North Korea]] to orchestrate the release of two Americans and to Cuba to help secure the release of Alan P. Gross.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

A Sarasota native, Band earned a bachelor's degree in English at the University of Florida in 1995 and became a White House intern after graduating, working his way up to Clinton's personal aide.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article makes much of the parallel with [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]: both were interns inside the Clinton White House who stayed below the radar during the administration's scandals and emerged as the Clintons' closest aides.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The Atlantic is quoted saying that Band "was to Bill Clinton what Huma Abedin has been to Hillary" — he helped launch the Clinton Foundation, came up with the idea for CGI, brokered the paid speeches that enriched Clinton, and then started Teneo, which made the foundation, Clinton and Band himself wealthier.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Teneo and the Clinton money network

Band founded Teneo in June 2011 with [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] and [[paul-keary|Paul Keary]], the firm having been planned since 2009.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) While [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was Secretary of State she approved a paid consulting arrangement between Bill Clinton and Band, and the article states Teneo paid Bill Clinton $2.5 million per year as a client and paid adviser to the firm and its founders.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) During the two-year run-up to the launch, while Kelly served at the [[state-department|State Department]], Band was making the corresponding connections through the Clinton Foundation; he joined the board of directors of the USA bid committee for the World Cup in 2010, the same committee that hired Keary, while Bill Clinton lobbied [[fifa|FIFA]] to bring the tournament to the United States.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article characterises Teneo as an investment bank that "come[s] in real handy when you're trying to launder large sums of money."2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The 2009 North Korea trip and diplomatic passports

Band personally asked Abedin for a favour in 2009 while she was at the State Department.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) In a July 27, 2009 email released by [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] and reported by the Chicago Tribune, Band wrote, "Need get me/ justy and jd dip passports," referring to himself and Clinton aides Justin Cooper and Jon Davidson; Abedin replied, "Okay. Will figure it out."2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) None of them worked for the State Department, and the article argues the coveted diplomatic passports were sought to ease transit and avoid scrutiny ahead of the secret trip to Pyongyang planned in consultation with the Obama administration's national security experts.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Band appears in the Q drop 227 photograph standing behind [[kim-jong-il|Kim Jong Il]], with [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] behind Bill Clinton.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article names Band, alongside Abedin, as being at the centre of the treason evidence [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] is said to be holding.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)
