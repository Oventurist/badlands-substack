# Alex Thompson

Alex Thompson is a reporter for Axios.

## Book on Biden's decline (2025)

Thompson co-authored, with CNN host [[jake-tapper|Jake Tapper]], one of several new books examining the extent of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s diminished faculties while he served as president.[1] The book was set to be available in the same week that the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] published audio of Biden's October 2023 interviews with special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]], amid what the Journal described as a public reckoning among Democrats over the subject.[1]
