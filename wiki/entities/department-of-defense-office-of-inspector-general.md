# Department of Defense Office of Inspector General

The Department of Defense Office of Inspector General (DoD OIG) is the independent oversight arm of the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]], responsible for auditing and investigating the department's programs and spending.[1]

## Ukraine assistance oversight

In February 2023, as congressional scrutiny of aid to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] intensified, the DoD OIG released an "Oversight Update: Ukraine Assistance" document stating that the office was "currently planning five potential oversight projects related to Ukraine."[1] The release coincided with a letter from [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] Republicans, led by chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]], demanding that the Biden administration account for the billions of dollars [[congress|Congress]] had appropriated for Ukraine and describe safeguards against wasteful spending.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] characterized the OIG's work as pairing well with the congressional oversight effort and with the activity of other inspectors general, arguing that the combined push coincided with a shift in Congress and among the public against further Ukraine aid at existing levels.[1]
