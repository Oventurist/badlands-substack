# Aivo Peterson

**Aivo Peterson** is an Estonian politician and co-founder of the small conservative anti-NATO party [[koos-party|Koos]], sentenced in December 2025 to 14 years in prison for treason.[1]

## Conviction

On Thursday, December 11, 2025, Estonia's Harju District Court convicted Peterson of working on behalf of Russia to undermine national security and imposed a 14-year sentence. Associates Dmitri Rootsi and Andrei Andronov received 11 years and 11 years and six months respectively. All three denied wrongdoing and announced they would appeal.[1]

Prosecutors alleged the defendants spread "narratives supporting Russia's foreign and security policy" designed to erode public trust in [[nato|NATO]] and in Estonia's military aid to Ukraine, with State Prosecutor Triinu Olev-Aas asserting that they "deliberately assisted Russia in activities directed against the Estonian state and society."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the sentencing as an example of a Western regime prosecuting its political opposition amid what he described as NATO's collapsing credibility, while noting that the case did not itself validate the defendants' politics.[1]
