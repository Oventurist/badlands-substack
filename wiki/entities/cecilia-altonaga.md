# Cecilia M. Altonaga

Cecilia M. Altonaga is a United States District Judge who serves as chief federal judge in the Southern District of Florida.[1]

## Brennan grand jury dispute (December 2025)

In December 2025, lawyers for former [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] addressed a letter to Altonaga in her capacity as chief judge, asking her to intervene in a criminal grand jury investigation of their client in Miami.[1] The letter accused the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] of attempting to steer the matter to a favored judge.[1]

In that letter, Brennan's attorneys said U.S. Attorney [[jason-reding-quinones|Jason Reding Quiñones]] had informed them that the grand jury was examining Brennan's role in the [[2017-intelligence-assessment-on-russian-interference|2017 Intelligence Community Assessment]] on alleged Russian interference in the 2016 election.[1] They alleged that prosecutors were manipulating grand jury and case assignment procedures to ensure the matter would be overseen by U.S. District Judge [[aileen-cannon|Aileen Cannon]], and cited alleged leaks that they said violated grand jury secrecy rules.[1] The request placed Altonaga, as chief judge, in the position of arbiter over case-assignment and judge-shopping allegations in a politically charged prosecution.[1]
