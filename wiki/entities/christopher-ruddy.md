# Christopher Ruddy

Christopher Ruddy is an American journalist who, while reporting for the *Pittsburgh Tribune-Review*, broke the story of the suspicious head wound found on Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] after the April 1996 plane crash in Croatia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Reporting on the Ron Brown case

Ruddy's report, published on November 24, disclosed that Lt. Col. [[steve-cogswell|Steve Cogswell]], a deputy medical examiner at the [[armed-forces-institute-of-pathology|Armed Forces Institute of Pathology]], questioned the official finding that Brown had died of multiple blunt-force trauma in the crash.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Cogswell's suspicions rested on x-rays and photographs showing a round, inwardly beveling .45-inch hole in the top of Brown's head, and on the disappearance of the original head x-rays from Brown's file.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Ruddy subsequently spoke with a second AFIP pathologist, [[david-hause|David Hause]], who corroborated the account. Following that conversation, AFIP broadened its gag order from Cogswell alone to all institute personnel.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

In the Badlands framing, Ruddy belongs to a small group of contemporary "truth-tellers" who reported that Brown had a bullet hole in his head and did not die from the crash, and who were subsequently marginalized or silenced — a dynamic the author contrasts with the later emergence of independent citizen journalism.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
