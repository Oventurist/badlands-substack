# Taylor Budowich

**Taylor Budowich** was announced as Deputy Chief of Staff for communications and personnel in the incoming second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the November 2024 transition.[1]

## Context

Budowich's selection was one item in a rapid series of transition announcements catalogued by Badlands Media, which also included [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]] as Chief of Staff, [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] and [[james-blair|James Blair]] as deputy chiefs of staff, [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]] as Homeland Security Adviser, and [[william-mcginley|William McGinley]] as White House Counsel.[1]

Contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] framed the appointments phase as the second battle of the Trump restoration — after winning the White House came assembling the right team, and after that, getting the job done.[1]
