# Margaret Sanger

Margaret Sanger is described by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in the February 5, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Control Through Language" as the early "face" of abortion and of [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]].[1] Carlson places her in the same social circle as the British writers [[george-orwell|George Orwell]], [[h-g-wells|H.G. Wells]], [[aldous-huxley|Aldous Huxley]] and [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] during the first two decades of the twentieth century, and states that she is said to have been a former lover of Wells.[1]

He writes that at least some, if not all, of that circle supported [[eugenics|eugenics]] — the belief in controlling who reproduced and how often — and that Adolf Hitler drew many of his ideas from eugenics supporters in early twentieth-century America and England.[1]
