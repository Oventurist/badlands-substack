# Margaret Sanger

Margaret Sanger is described by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in the February 5, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Control Through Language" as the early "face" of abortion and of [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]].[1] Carlson places her in the same social circle as the British writers [[george-orwell|George Orwell]], [[h-g-wells|H.G. Wells]], [[aldous-huxley|Aldous Huxley]] and [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] during the first two decades of the twentieth century, and states that she is said to have been a former lover of Wells.[1]

He writes that at least some, if not all, of that circle supported [[eugenics|eugenics]] — the belief in controlling who reproduced and how often — and that Adolf Hitler drew many of his ideas from eugenics supporters in early twentieth-century America and England.[1]

## Association with the Fabian Society

In her primer on [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]], Colonel [[roxane-watkins|Roxane Watkins]] presents Sanger of Planned Parenthood as a product of the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] milieu, describing her role as culling what that circle regarded as an inferior herd.[2] Watkins situates this within the Society's broader embrace of [[eugenics|eugenics]] — the selective killing or euthanasia of non-productive people — driven by a wish that only strong members reproduce in order to maximize the elite's investments.[2]

## Rockefeller funding and the American Birth Control League

In "The Inversion of Health," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] records that the [[bureau-of-social-hygiene|Bureau of Social Hygiene]] — taken over by [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]] after he created the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] in 1913 — funded Sanger's proposal for birth control clinical studies by the [[american-birth-control-league|American Birth Control League]], the body that eventually became [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]].[3] He notes that she advocated limiting "dysgenic stocks" such as Black, Hispanic, American Indian and Catholic populations, as well as "slum dwellers" such as Jewish immigrants.[3]
