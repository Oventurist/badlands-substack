# Stripe

Stripe is an online payment processing platform. In June 2024 it took over identity-verification duties for [[x|X]] after the platform ended its relationship with the Israeli-founded identity firm [[au10tix|AU10TIX]].[1]

## Role in X identity verification

Users of X seeking payment features are still required to submit a government-issued photo ID in order to satisfy Section 326 of the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]], but under the new arrangement that verification is performed by Stripe rather than AU10TIX.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted in the Badlands News Brief that Stripe was already involved in processing bank wire transfers for the platform and therefore already possessed the information contained on users' government-issued IDs, making the verification step largely redundant.[1] He characterized the arrangement as "not ideal, but... better than working with Mossad," arguing that the redundancy alleviated some of the anxiety over potential foul play, and called on [[congress|Congress]] to repeal the Patriot Act and all ancillary legislation stemming from it.[1]
