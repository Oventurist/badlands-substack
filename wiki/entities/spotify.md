# Spotify

Spotify is the audio streaming and podcasting platform led by chief executive Daniel Ek. It became the subject of a congressional censorship investigation in July 2025.[1]

## House Judiciary Committee investigation (2025)

On July 29, 2025, the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] launched an investigation into whether the [[european-union|European Union]] and the Biden administration pressured Spotify to censor speech on its platform.[1] Censorship had already been a recurring point of tension for the company, which faced backlash for flagging COVID-19 content from podcaster Joe Rogan and for banning [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] from the platform.[1]

A source familiar with the probe told the New York Post that the more relevant concern was "the pressure we are seeing the EU put on companies to censor more."[1] In a letter to Ek, Representative [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] criticized recent EU and UK laws requiring social media platforms — including US-based ones — to censor "disinformation" and "harmful content" or face large fines.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that the entire platform is controlled by algorithms, including its play counts, and described it as part of the same propaganda and censorship apparatus.[1]
