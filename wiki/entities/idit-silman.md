# Idit Silman

Idit Silman is [[israel|Israel]]'s Environmental Protection Minister. In July 2026 she reclassified the Nile crocodile into a species category that permits security agencies to keep the animals under specified conditions, a legal step that could allow their use for security purposes at prison facilities.[1]

Israeli media reported the change was intended to facilitate a proposal backed by National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], and that the reclassification was made despite objections from the Israel Nature and Parks Authority and from the Environmental Protection Ministry's own legal adviser, who argued the proposal lacked sufficient legal and professional justification.[1]
