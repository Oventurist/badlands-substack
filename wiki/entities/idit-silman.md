# Idit Silman

Idit Silman is Israel's Environmental Protection Minister. As reported in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], Silman reclassified the Nile crocodile into a species category that permits security agencies to keep the animals under specified conditions — a legal step that could allow crocodiles to be used for security purposes at prison facilities in [[israel]].

Israeli media reported that the reclassification was intended to facilitate a proposal backed by National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], who has advocated surrounding prisons holding Palestinian security detainees with crocodiles as an escape deterrent, citing Florida's [[alligator-alcatraz|"Alligator Alcatraz"]] as inspiration. The change was made despite objections from the [[israel-nature-and-parks-authority|Israel Nature and Parks Authority]] and from the Environmental Protection Ministry's own legal adviser, who argued the proposal lacked sufficient legal and professional justification. Reporting indicated the regulatory change enables consideration of a pilot program but does not mean crocodiles have been deployed.
