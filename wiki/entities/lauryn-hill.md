# Lauryn Hill

Lauryn Hill is an American singer and rapper who appeared on stage with [[kanye-west|Kanye West]] during his April 2026 SoFi Stadium concert in Los Angeles, described as the first live collaboration between the two artists.[1] The appearance came during West's Good Friday and Easter Sunday shows, his first major U.S. performances in roughly five years.[1]
