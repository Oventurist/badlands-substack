# Lauryn Hill

Lauryn Hill is an American singer and rapper who appears in the Badlands Media corpus through the April 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-282|Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks"]]. According to that edition, Hill joined [[kanye-west|Kanye West]] on stage during his sold-out Easter-weekend concert at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles, before roughly 70,000 fans. The Brief describes the appearance as the first live collaboration between the two artists.

The performance formed part of West's return to major U.S. live performance after roughly five years away, a two-night run on Good Friday and Easter Sunday that the Brief reports grossed about $33 million in total. Beyond this appearance, the corpus does not discuss Hill's own career or public positions.
