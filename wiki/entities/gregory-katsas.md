# Gregory Katsas

Gregory Katsas is a judge on the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-dc-circuit|U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit]].[1]

## USAID funding cuts ruling (2025)

In August 2025, Katsas joined Judge [[karen-henderson|Karen Henderson]] in a 2–1 decision permitting the Trump administration to proceed with cutting billions in [[usaid|USAID]] funding that had already been approved by [[congress|Congress]].[1] The majority held that the nonprofits challenging the cuts lacked standing to sue under the [[impoundment-control-act|Impoundment Control Act]], reasoning that only the head of the [[government-accountability-office|Government Accountability Office]] may bring such a case, and the court declined to rule on the constitutionality of the cuts themselves.[1] The ruling reversed a February order by U.S. District Judge [[amir-ali|Amir Ali]].[1]
