# Patrick Gary Schlegel

Patrick Gary Schlegel is an Arizona man who, at age 34, was shot and wounded by a [[us-border-patrol|United States Border Patrol]] agent near Arivaca, Arizona, on January 27, 2026.[1]

## The incident

According to authorities, Schlegel fired at Border Patrol agents and at a [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] helicopter during an encounter close to the southern border, and agents returned fire.[1] He was airlifted to a hospital, underwent surgery, and was subsequently reported to be in serious but stable condition.[1]

## Investigation and charges

The [[pima-county-sheriffs-department|Pima County Sheriff's Department]] led a use-of-force review of the shooting at the request of the [[fbi|FBI]].[1] Federal prosecutors said Schlegel was expected to face charges including assault on a federal officer, human smuggling, and being a felon in possession of a firearm.[1]

US Attorney [[timothy-courchaine|Timothy Courchaine]] said: "There is no ambiguity here, shooting at any law enforcement officer—including federal agents—is a violent felony that attacks the rule of law and undermines universal American values. We will pursue every available charge to ensure the subject is held fully accountable under federal law."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the case as an illustration of escalating violence against immigration enforcement personnel, noting that the alleged human smuggling component undercut claims that opposition to enforcement is purely humanitarian.[1]
