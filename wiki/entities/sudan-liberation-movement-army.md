# Sudan Liberation Movement-Army

The Sudan Liberation Movement-Army (SLM-A) is a rebel group that controls parts of the Marrah Mountains region of Central Darfur in western [[sudan|Sudan]].[1]

## Role in the 2025 Tarasin landslide

The SLM-A was the primary source of information on the landslide that destroyed Tarasin village in Central Darfur's Marrah Mountains on Sunday, August 31, 2025, after days of heavy rainfall in late August.[1] The group reported that initial information indicated the death of all village residents — an estimated 1,000 people — with only one survivor, and said the village had been "completely levelled."[1] It appealed to the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and international aid organizations for assistance in recovering bodies.[1]

The disaster occurred amid Sudan's ongoing civil war, which has left much of Darfur, including the Marrah Mountains, largely inaccessible to the UN and aid organizations.[1]
