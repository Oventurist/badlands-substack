# Ali ibn Abi Talib

Ali, known simply as Ali, was the fourth of the caliphs (successors to the Prophet) of the [[rashidun-caliphate|Rashidun Caliphate]] and the figure around whom the Shia branch of Islam formed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Relationship to Muhammad

Ali's father had adopted [[muhammad|Muhammad]] when Muhammad was orphaned as a child, making Ali effectively Muhammad's step-brother; Ali was in turn raised by Muhammad from the age of five.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) When Muhammad emerged from one of his meditative excursions into the caves around Mecca claiming that the archangel Gabriel had brought him a message from God, it was the ten-year-old Ali who believed him when nobody else would.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Ali thus became Muhammad's first follower — the first Muslim — and Muhammad called him his brother, guardian and successor.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## The Mawla dispute

Muhammad once called Ali a Mawla, an Arabic word carrying a range of meanings including 'Lord', 'trustee', and 'helper'.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Because that word's interpretation was disputed, some of Muhammad's followers concluded upon his death that Ali — who was like a son and brother to him — was his chosen heir, and that therefore only descendants of Ali can qualify to lead Islam.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Those adherents are the [[shia-islam|Shia]], primarily found in modern Iran and throughout Persia, making up roughly 10–15% of all Muslims.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The remaining 85–90%, the [[sunni-islam|Sunni]], hold that Muhammad appointed no heir.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
