# Bernd Schaefer

Bernd Schaefer is the chief executive officer of [[eit-rawmaterials|EIT RawMaterials]], an EU-funded agency for key minerals.[1]

In a December 2025 interview with Reuters, Schaefer said Europe had finally taken major steps to build up its [[rare-earth-elements|rare earths]] sector to counter Chinese dominance, but warned that the region also needed to guard against being overwhelmed by the United States.[1] He welcomed the [[european-commission|European Commission]]'s REsourceEU action plan — which commits 3 billion euros ($3.49 billion) over a year to fast-track critical raw materials projects for electric cars, wind turbines and semiconductors — while cautioning that "it is certainly a real step change from ambition to delivery, but it's still missing a couple of things."[1]
