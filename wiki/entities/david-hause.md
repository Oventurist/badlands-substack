# David Hause

Lt. Col. David Hause was a pathologist at the [[armed-forces-institute-of-pathology|Armed Forces Institute of Pathology]] and, according to the account cited in the Badlands corpus, one of the institute's leading experts on gunshot wounds.

## In the Badlands corpus

America for Sale   Part 1 reports that on December 9, 1997, Hause came forward to corroborate the claims of Lt. Col. [[steve-cogswell|Steve Cogswell]] regarding the head wound of Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]]. Unlike Cogswell, Hause was physically present in the room during the examination of Brown's body. When Chief Petty Officer Kathleen Janoski exclaimed, "Wow, look at the hole in Ron Brown's head," Hause walked over and verified that the wound penetrated the skull and exposed brain matter, describing it as looking like "a punched-out .45-caliber entrance hole."

After Hause spoke to *Pittsburgh Tribune-Review* reporter Christopher Ruddy, AFIP broadened its gag order to cover all institute personnel. The article treats Hause's corroboration as the decisive element that made the dispute impossible to attribute to a single eccentric witness, noting that three separate military pathologists concluded there was a bullet hole in Brown's head before the department was silenced.
