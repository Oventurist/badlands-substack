# David Hause

Lt. Col. David Hause was a pathologist at the [[armed-forces-institute-of-pathology|Armed Forces Institute of Pathology]] (AFIP) and one of the institute's leading experts on gunshot wounds.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) On December 9, 1997, he came forward to corroborate the account of his colleague [[steve-cogswell|Steve Cogswell]] concerning the head wound found on Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Testimony on the Brown examination

Unlike Cogswell, Hause was present in the room while Brown's body was being examined. He described a commotion erupting when Chief Petty Officer Kathleen Janoski exclaimed, "Wow, look at the hole in Ron Brown's head."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Hause walked over and verified that the wound penetrated the skull, exposing brain matter, and characterized it as looking like "a punched-out .45-caliber entrance hole."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

His corroboration was significant because it made it difficult to dismiss Cogswell as an isolated eccentric willing to jeopardize his military career with unfounded accusations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) After Hause spoke to journalist [[christopher-ruddy|Christopher Ruddy]], the AFIP gag order originally imposed on Cogswell was broadened to include all AFIP personnel.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The Badlands account cites Hause as one of three military pathologists who concluded there was a bullet hole in Brown's head, evidence it treats as indicating Brown did not die from the crash itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
