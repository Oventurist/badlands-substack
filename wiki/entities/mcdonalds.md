# McDonald's

McDonald's is treated in Badlands Media commentary as the anomaly among [[americana-brands|Americana brands]] — the one company on [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s list of the most American brands that has not, in his assessment, gone out of its way to upset its customer base through "wokeness."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

Carlson lists McDonald's with Coca-Cola, [[disney|Disney]], Levi's, Hershey's and [[budweiser|Budweiser]] as the brands that most represent American culture to him.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He then singles it out: "Other than maybe McDonalds, all these companies have gone out of their way to upset their customer base through 'wokeness'."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

Rather than treat the exception as evidence against his thesis, Carlson offers a speculative explanation consistent with it — asking what if McDonald's "is being allowed to prosper by staying 'woke-free' because it is being used to make us sick and unhealthy."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) On that reading the company serves the same [[communist-takeover-of-america|takeover]] programme by a different mechanism, degrading public health instead of American cultural identity.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)
