# Office for Pandemic Preparedness and Response Policy

The **Office for Pandemic Preparedness and Response Policy** (OPPR) is a body announced by the Biden administration in July 2023 to coordinate United States preparation for future pandemics.[1]

## Russian accusations

In an August 2023 press briefing, Lieutenant General [[igor-kirillov|Igor Kirillov]], chief of the Russian Armed Forces' radiation, chemical and biological protection troops, cited the creation of the office as evidence that the United States had "started preparing for a new pandemic by searching for virus mutations."[1] Kirillov said the office's priority areas include work on creating vaccines and drugs to stop viruses and their genetically modified variants, and asserted that the leading role in the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s military biological program would be played by the [[united-states-army-medical-research-institute-of-infectious-diseases|United States Army Medical Research Institute of Infectious Diseases]].[1] He further claimed that documents uncovered during Russia's military operation in Ukraine confirmed that institution was involved in accumulating dangerous pathogens in various regions of the world.[1]

## Coverage

The accusations were relayed by Sputnik and reported by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], whose account framed the alleged effort as an attempt to muddy the waters ahead of the following year's presidential election and to derail Russian battlefield momentum in Ukraine, and linked it to the wider reassessment of COVID-19 origins then reaching the general public.[1] These claims are Russian government allegations reported in partisan media and are not independently established.[1]
