# Sean Casten

Sean Casten is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Downers Grove, Illinois.[1]

## Opposition to the Big Beautiful Bill Act

At an event in Geneva, Illinois, in July 2025, Casten attacked the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s recently passed [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|"Big Beautiful Bill Act"]], singling out its changes to [[supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program|SNAP]], the federal Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program that provides food assistance to low-income individuals.[1] "This bill is evil in the Biblical sense of that word," Casten said.[1] He appeared alongside fellow Illinois Representative [[bill-foster|Bill Foster]] and food bank leaders.[1]

The law, signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on July 4, includes $4.5 trillion in tax breaks originally enacted in 2017 during Trump's first term that would otherwise have expired, as well as new ones.[1] Its SNAP provisions raise the age through which participants must work to qualify from 55 to 65, with exceptions including parents caring for a child under 14.[1]
