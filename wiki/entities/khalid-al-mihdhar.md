# Khalid al Mihdhar

Khalid al Mihdhar was an [[al-qaeda|al Qaeda]] operative and one of the hijackers in the [[september-11-attacks|September 11, 2001 attacks]].[1] Together with [[nawaf-al-hazmi|Nawaf al Hazmi]] he entered the United States at Los Angeles shortly after an al Qaeda summit in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia in early January 2000.[1]

## Intelligence withheld from the FBI

According to the sworn declaration of Department of Defense investigator [[donald-canestraro|Donald Canestraro]], reported by [[florida-bulldog|Florida Bulldog]] in April 2023, senior [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] officials including Director George Tenet intentionally withheld from the [[fbi|FBI]] the knowledge that Mihdhar and Hazmi were inside the country.[1] Interviews with eleven former FBI agents and two former CIA officers support the theory that the Agency stayed quiet because it was working through its Saudi Arabian counterpart to recruit the two men as informants, since the CIA is legally barred from running intelligence operations domestically.[1]

Both men subsequently took part in hijacking the American Airlines jet flown into the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1] The declaration further alleges that after the attacks, FBI personnel in San Diego and at headquarters were instructed not to reveal to Congress the full extent of Saudi involvement with al Qaeda.[1]
