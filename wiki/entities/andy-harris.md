# Andy Harris

Andy Harris is a Republican member of the United States [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] from Maryland and chairman of the House Freedom Caucus.[1]

## June 2026 rule revolt

Harris was among the conservative members who drove the late-June 2026 rebellion that derailed the House's procedural rule and froze most floor business ahead of the July 4 recess.[1] The rule failed 224-198, with 14 Republicans joining Democrats in opposition, in an impasse that grew out of frustration over the stalled [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]].[1] Other members of the revolt included Reps. [[chip-roy|Chip Roy]], [[anna-paulina-luna|Anna Paulina Luna]], Mike Turner, and Victoria Spartz.[1]

The rebels' demands ranged from a promised vote on the House-passed immigration bill HR 2 to amendments touching defense policy, pensions for Delphi auto parts retirees, and the SAVE America Act itself.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had directed members not to blockade the floor, but some of the holdouts proceeded anyway.[1]
