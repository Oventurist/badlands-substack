# Institutional Revolutionary Party

The Institutional Revolutionary Party, known by its Spanish initials as the PRI, was the party that ruled Mexico during the period covered by "A Family Affair."[1] [[jose-francisco-ruiz-massieu|José Francisco Ruiz Massieu]] was its secretary general and second-ranking official when he was killed in September 1994, and [[carlos-salinas|Carlos Salinas]] governed as its president.[1]

The party appears in the article as an obstacle to accountability: [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] resigned as special prosecutor in the assassination case saying his investigation was being blocked by ruling-party power brokers, before investigators concluded he had himself altered witness statements protecting [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]].[1]
