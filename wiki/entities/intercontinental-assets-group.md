# Intercontinental Assets Group

Intercontinental Assets Group Inc. was the firm founded by [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] immediately after his 1981 departure from [[bear-stearns|Bear Stearns]], which followed a regulatory violation tied to insider-trading probes that the firm downplayed.[1]

Through the company Epstein marketed himself as a "bounty hunter" recovering stolen funds for wealthy clients.[1] Public records and contemporary accounts list his earliest major client as the Saudi arms broker [[adnan-khashoggi|Adnan Khashoggi]], a central figure in the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] scandal.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that "asset recovery" for such clients inherently involved disguising the origins of funds — skills honed in environments where financial opacity served geopolitical ends.[1]
