# Susan Collins

Susan Collins is a Republican United States Senator from [[maine|Maine]], first elected in 1996, and the incumbent whom Democrat [[graham-platner|Graham Platner]]'s 2026 Senate campaign was organized to defeat.

## The 2026 Senate race

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Platner's campaign stated its goal as defeating Collins, and that after public sexual assault allegations from [[jenny-racicot|Jenny Racicot]] threw the campaign into turmoil, Platner announced he was stepping back temporarily to reflect on the best path forward "for his campaign, his supporters, and the goal of defeating Republican Sen. Susan Collins." The Brief does not report any direct response from Collins to the allegations. The race is covered as part of the 2026 midterm cycle, with the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]] context in which several prominent Democrats, including Senator [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]] and Representative [[ro-khanna|Ro Khanna]], withdrew endorsements from Platner.
