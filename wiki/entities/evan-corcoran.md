# Evan Corcoran

**Evan Corcoran** is an attorney who represented [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the dispute over presidential records held at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]].

## May 2022 letter

The Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 reports that Corcoran authored a May 2022 letter, sent before the [[fbi|FBI]]'s August 8, 2022 search, asserting on Trump's behalf that the former president possessed broad declassification authority and had issued a standing order treating documents removed to his residence as declassified. The source presents these assertions as having been on the record before the [[mar-a-lago-raid|raid]] was approved, and as anticipating the doubts later voiced internally at the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] by [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]].
