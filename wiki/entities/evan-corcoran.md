# Evan Corcoran

Evan Corcoran is an attorney who represented [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the dispute over presidential records held at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]].[1]

In a May 2022 letter — sent months before the August 8, 2022 [[fbi|FBI]] search — Corcoran asserted on Trump's behalf that the former president possessed broad declassification authority and had issued a standing order treating documents removed to his residence as declassified.[1] That claim later formed part of the backdrop against which [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] officials, including [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]], privately questioned the raid's legal footing.[1]
