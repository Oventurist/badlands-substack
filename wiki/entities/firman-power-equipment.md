# FIRMAN Power Equipment

**FIRMAN Power Equipment** is a manufacturer of portable generators and power equipment.[1] In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as the presenting sponsor of the inaugural stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]].[1]

## Sponsorship of GART AZ

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] stated that the Great American Restoration Tour event in [[chandler-arizona|Chandler, Arizona]] was "powered by FIRMAN," and promoted a discount code, BADLANDS, giving readers 17% off all Firman Power Equipment purchases in the run-up to the April 2023 conference.[1] The arrangement is an example of the network's practice of pairing its live events and newsletter with commercial sponsors aligned with its audience, alongside the promotion of patriot-owned businesses elsewhere in its output.[1]

## Announcement-stage promotion (February 2023)

The sponsorship was disclosed as early as the February 25, 2023 post announcing the Chandler conference, which stated that "The Great American Restoration Tour in Chandler, AZ is powered by FIRMAN" and that readers could get 17% off all Firman Power Equipment with the discount code BADLANDS in the period leading up to the event.[2] The company's banner was displayed at the foot of the announcement, linking to its own storefront.[2]
