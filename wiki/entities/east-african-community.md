# East African Community

The East African Community (EAC) is a regional intergovernmental organization of East African states that took a leading diplomatic role in responding to the January 2025 escalation between the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Rwanda.[1]

## 2025 DRC–Rwanda crisis response

On Sunday, January 26, 2025, Kenyan head of state and EAC chairman [[william-ruto|William Ruto]] announced that the bloc would convene an extraordinary summit on the DRC–Rwanda crisis within 48 hours, at which Rwandan President [[paul-kagame|Paul Kagame]] would meet DRC President [[felix-tshisekedi|Félix Tshisekedi]].[1] "After consulting the heads of state of the EAC member states, we will convene an extraordinary summit in the next 48 hours to chart the way forward," Ruto said in an address posted to X; media reports later indicated the summit was expected to be organized for that Wednesday.[1]

The announcement followed reports that the leader of the alliance of rebel forces in the DRC, which includes the [[m23|M23]] group, had said the coalition captured the eastern city of Goma, a claim Congolese authorities had not confirmed.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the EAC's rapid convening as part of a broader pattern of regional self-reliance, following in the footsteps of the Arab League and the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, both of which count African states among their members.[1] In this reading, African states were neither looking west to the United States or the [[united-nations|United Nations]] nor east to Russia or China to restore order, a posture treated as indicative of an emerging [[multipolar-world-order|multipolar world order]].[1]
