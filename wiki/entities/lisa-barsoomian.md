# Lisa Barsoomian

**Lisa Barsoomian** is an American attorney, a longtime government litigator, and the wife of former Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]].[1] She is a recurring figure in Badlands Media's coverage of the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One]] and [[russiagate-hoax|Russian collusion]] narratives, where her legal career is presented as evidence of a conflict of interest at the top of the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]].[1]

## Career and cases

Barsoomian practiced in federal court in Washington, frequently alongside her supervisor R. Craig Lawrence.[1] The Badlands article states that the two represented a striking roster of officials and institutions, including [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]], [[james-comey|James Comey]], [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], and [[bill-clinton|Bill]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], as well as the [[fbi|FBI]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]].[1] Her specialty is described as defending the government against [[freedom-of-information-act|Freedom of Information Act]] lawsuits — that is, litigating to prevent the release of records.[1] Lange characterizes this as a role in hiding the crimes of a criminal syndicate installed atop the DOJ to protect the Clintons and Obama, part of what he calls a two-tier justice system shielding "untouchables."[1]

## The Hamburg v. Clinton matter

Citing the Rush Limbaugh Show, the article notes that Barsoomian represented then-President Bill Clinton in a 1998–99 civil case in federal court, shortly after Rosenstein — who had worked as a [[whitewater-controversy|Whitewater]] prosecutor — cleared Hillary Clinton.[1] [[qanon|Q]] drop 4784 (September 28, 2020) lists Barsoomian under the heading "follow the wives," identifying her as a former Bill Clinton attorney and Rosenstein's wife, and asserting that the Hamburg v. Clinton 1998 case and its 1999 appeal were "scrubbed from net."[1] Lange reports that his own searches turned up only basic docket information indicating a civil rights action brought by Al Hamburg in Wyoming, categorized as a nature-of-suit 440 civil rights violation — a residual category covering claims not falling under other specific codes, often used for allegations such as excessive force during an arrest.[1] Q drop 3035 (March 12, 2019) again pairs "LISA BARSOOMIAN = [RR] wife?" with "LISA BARSOOMIAN = rep BILL CLINTON?" under the label "PAY-FOR-PLAY SPIDER WEB."[1]

## Significance in the Badlands thesis

Lange argues that Rosenstein's failure to recuse himself from the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Special Counsel investigation]] is inexplicable given that his wife had represented Clinton, Mueller, and Comey in federal court — a conflict larger, in his telling, than the one that prompted [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] to recuse.[1] He further speculates that the couple's shared exposure made them leverageable: both were allegedly involved in covering up treason, both were trusted by the Clinton network, and both, as parents of two daughters, were placed in the same position he attributes to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] — cooperate or lose the family.[1] On that basis he concludes that Rosenstein, like Mueller, "flipped."[1] These claims originate with the author and Q drops rather than with documented court or DOJ findings.
