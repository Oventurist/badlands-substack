# Jeffrey Rath

Jeffrey Rath is a Canadian lawyer, co-founder and legal counsel of the [[alberta-prosperity-project|Alberta Prosperity Project]], the organization campaigning for [[alberta|Alberta]]'s independence from [[canada|Canada]].

## Washington meetings

According to *Financial Times* reporting summarized in the [[badlands-brief-1ce|January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief]], Rath attended meetings between APP representatives and [[state-department|State Department]] officials held three times in Washington since April 2025. He told the newspaper: "The US is extremely enthusiastic about a free and independent Alberta." The APP, which is seeking a $500 billion United States line of credit contingent on a successful independence referendum, planned to send a fourth delegation to Washington the following month.
