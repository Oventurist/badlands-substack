# Mark Houck

Mark Houck is a pro-life activist from the Philadelphia area whose home was raided by an FBI SWAT team, and who was subsequently acquitted by a jury "in barely an hour."[1] The raid became a widely cited example among conservatives of what they describe as the uneven application of federal law enforcement under Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]].[1]

## Political significance

Senator [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]], the ranking member of the Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on Criminal Justice and Counterterrorism, invoked the Houck raid in June 2023 while criticizing the federal indictment of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], asking how anyone could be surprised at the Justice Department's conduct after Garland "sics the FBI to raid ... a pro-life activist outside of Philadelphia, who's then acquitted by a jury of his peers in barely an hour."[1]
