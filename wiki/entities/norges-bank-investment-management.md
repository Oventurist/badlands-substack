# Norges Bank Investment Management

Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM) is the body that manages Norway's sovereign wealth fund, which at roughly $2 trillion in assets is the largest such fund in the world.[1]

## Israeli divestment, August 2025

On 11 August 2025 the fund announced it was rolling back Israeli investments over conditions in Gaza and the West Bank, selling stakes in some Israeli companies and terminating all contracts with Israeli asset managers handling its investments.[1] NBIM stated that it was divesting from 11 Israeli companies out of 61 held — specifically those not included in an equity benchmark index created by Norway's Finance Ministry — and that it would further strengthen its due diligence.[1] Chief executive [[nicolai-tangen|Nicolai Tangen]] framed the move as a response to investment exposure in "a country at war."[1]

## Reception

Badlands commentary treated the divestment as a market signal following a Saudi Foreign Ministry call for international action against Israel, predicting a ripple effect among other institutional and retail investors, and connected it to a Saudi–French declaration demanding that [[hamas|Hamas]] surrender Gaza to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]].[1]
