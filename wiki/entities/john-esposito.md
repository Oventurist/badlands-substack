# John Esposito

John Esposito is a professor at [[georgetown-university|Georgetown University]] and the director of the Prince Alwaleed Bin Talal Center for Muslim-Christian Understanding (ACMCU).[1]

The center he leads was renamed after Saudi [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] donated $20 million to it in December 2005, a gift reported as the second largest single donation in Georgetown's history.[1] In the Badlands Media feature "Transparency and Prosecution," quoting the Shoebat Foundation, Esposito is described as a renowned [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] apologist and as "Prince Alwaleed's guy at the University."[1]

Esposito's significance to the article is indirect but structural: he is listed among the faculty of the Department of Arabic and Islamic Studies in which [[elizabeth-obagy|Elizabeth O'Bagy]] earned her Master's in Arab Studies, and the author uses that overlap to argue that O'Bagy — like Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] — was operating within Alwaleed's sphere of influence while promoting intervention in Syria.[1]
