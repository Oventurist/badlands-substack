# The Lancet

The Lancet is a British medical journal described by Badlands Media as "once prestigious" and now, in its critics' view, "galloping onward in its woke agenda."[1]

## Climate–migration nexus article (April 2024)

In an April 2024 issue, The Lancet argued for an "inextricable" link between climate change and immigration, lamenting a lack of widespread appreciation for what it termed the "climate–migration–health nexus" and urging greater efforts to awaken the public to the problem.[1] The journal contended that "climate change is increasingly driving population movement, acting as a driver of and a barrier to migration and as a hazard experienced during journeys or on arrival at new destinations."[1]

Critics noted that the article failed to explain how climate — a variable measured in 30-year spans as opposed to daily weather — could act as a barrier or hazard to anything.[1]
