# Horizons Project

The Horizons Project is a United States–focused initiative that unites social justice, peacebuilding, and democracy groups against what it describes as "tyranny," building coalitions for "inclusive democracy" with an emphasis on [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]].[1] [[maria-stephan|Maria Stephan]] served as its Co-Lead and Chief Organizer after leaving her post as Director of the Nonviolent Action Program at the [[united-states-institute-of-peace|United States Institute of Peace]].[1]

## Role in domestic movements

Badlands Media asserts that the Horizons Project has been involved in nearly every major American movement and protest of the preceding eight years that can be traced back to what it calls the CIA/DoD NGO swarm, including the Black Lives Matter protests of 2020 and the [[no-kings-protests|"No Kings"]] coalition trainings of 2024–2025, which taught "nonviolent" strategies for countering perceived authoritarianism.[1]

## Interpretation

The article reads the project's stated goal of "inclusive democracy" as a euphemism for the inclusion of social groups that adhere to establishment Democratic Party positions, and treats Horizons as an example of how the [[plausible-deniability-network|plausible deniability network]] pushes formerly clandestine influence work down into ostensibly independent civil-society organizations.[1]
