# International Stabilization Force

The International Stabilization Force is the multinational military formation associated with the United States-brokered [[gaza|Gaza]] ceasefire framework and the second phase of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s peace plan.

The [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026 describes the force as drawing civilian and military personnel from a range of participating countries, coordinated through the [[civil-military-coordination-centre|Civil Military Coordination Centre]] established by the United States at Kiryat Gat in October 2025. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterizes the deployment as "a company of soldiers from across the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], led by [[turkey|Turkey]] and [[qatar|Qatar]]," and anticipates that Israeli settlers opposed to the arrangement — encouraged by Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]]'s call to dismantle the Kiryat Gat headquarters — may confront it directly.
