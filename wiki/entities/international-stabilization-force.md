# International Stabilization Force

The International Stabilization Force is the multinational military formation associated with the United States-brokered [[gaza|Gaza]] ceasefire framework and the second phase of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s peace plan.

The Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026 describes the force as drawing civilian and military personnel from a range of participating countries, coordinated through the [[civil-military-coordination-centre|Civil Military Coordination Centre]] established by the United States at Kiryat Gat in October 2025. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterizes the deployment as "a company of soldiers from across the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], led by [[turkey|Turkey]] and [[qatar|Qatar]]," and anticipates that Israeli settlers opposed to the arrangement — encouraged by Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]]'s call to dismantle the Kiryat Gat headquarters — may confront it directly.

## Composition and the southern Gaza base (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, via [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s summary of the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]]'s first meeting in Washington, that the force is planned to comprise 32,000 personnel — 12,000 police and 20,000 troops — drawn from countries including Indonesia, [[morocco|Morocco]], [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], Kosovo, and Albania, with training support from [[egypt|Egypt]] and [[jordan|Jordan]]. [[turkey|Turkey]] has also volunteered soldiers and police, a demographic President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he wants represented in the ISF; Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and other Israeli officials have "emphatically stated" that Turkish participation "will never be allowed."

Trump additionally announced plans to build a base for the ISF in southern [[gaza|Gaza]] that would house 5,000 soldiers and other personnel.
