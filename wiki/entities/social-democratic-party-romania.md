# Social Democratic Party (Romania)

The Social Democrats (PSD) are the largest political party in [[romania|Romania]].[1]

## Role in the May 2026 no-confidence vote

The PSD allied with far-right opposition groups, including the [[alliance-for-the-union-of-romanians|Alliance for the Union of Romanians (AUR)]], to oust the pro-EU minority government of Prime Minister [[ilie-bolojan|Ilie Bolojan]] in a no-confidence vote that passed with 281 votes against a threshold of 233.[1] The government the PSD helped bring down had itself been formed about ten months earlier to counter far-right gains, making the cross-spectrum alliance a notable realignment.[1]
