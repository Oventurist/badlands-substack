# Walter Mondale

Walter Mondale was [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter's]] vice president and, before that, a leading member of the [[church-committee|Church Committee]], the Senate panel that investigated abuses by the United States intelligence community.[1]

## Role in intelligence reform

Mondale had already won a commitment from Carter to try to implement many of the Church Committee's recommendations for reforming the intelligence community.[1] That commitment is presented as the reason Carter refused to retain [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] as Director of Central Intelligence after defeating [[gerald-ford|Gerald Ford]] in the 1976 election, despite Bush's efforts to persuade him.[1] Bush lasted only a year as CIA director.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats Mondale's influence on Carter as the first of several moves by which Carter attempted to "buck the system" of the emerging [[shadow-government|shadow government]].[1]
