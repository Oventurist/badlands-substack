# Frank A. Vanderlip

**Frank A. Vanderlip** was President of the National City Bank of New York and one of the men who attended the secret 1910 meeting at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] at which the plan behind the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act]] was drafted.[1]

Together with the other attendees, he represented roughly one quarter of the world's wealth at the time, and travelled from Hoboken, New Jersey by train in complete secrecy, using first names or code names to conceal the group's identity.[1]
