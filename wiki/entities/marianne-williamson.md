# Marianne Williamson

Marianne Williamson is an American author and self-help figure who mounted a challenge to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] for the 2024 Democratic presidential nomination.[1]

## 2024 primary campaign

Williamson suspended her presidential campaign in early February 2024.[1] Despite the suspension, she continued to draw votes: in the [[2024-michigan-democratic-primary|Michigan Democratic primary]] held on February 27, 2024, she took 3.0% of the statewide vote, narrowly ahead of the still-actively campaigning Representative [[dean-phillips|Dean Phillips]] at 2.7%, in a contest dominated by Biden's 81.1% and a 13.3% protest vote for "uncommitted."[1]
