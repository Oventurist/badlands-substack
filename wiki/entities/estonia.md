# Estonia

Estonia is a Baltic state and a member of [[nato|NATO]], which it joined in 2004.[1]

## Balticconnector incident

In October 2023, Estonia was directly implicated in the damage to the [[balticconnector|Balticconnector]] subsea gas pipeline and an accompanying telecommunications cable connecting the country with [[finland|Finland]] beneath the Baltic Sea.[1] Finnish authorities determined that the pipeline damage occurred in Finnish waters while the telecoms cable breach occurred in Estonian waters, and the investigation proceeded as a joint Finnish–Estonian effort.[1] Finnish President [[sauli-niinisto|Sauli Niinistö]] said the damage to both lines was likely the result of "outside activity."[1] See [[2023-balticconnector-pipeline-damage|2023 Balticconnector pipeline damage]].[1]
