# Tom Cotton

Tom Cotton is a Republican United States Senator from Arkansas who has served in senior roles on intelligence oversight and is closely associated with hawkish national-security positions.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports that Cotton introduced a provision to the 2027 Intelligence Authorization Act that would require the President to expand intelligence sharing with [[mossad|Mossad]] and prohibit the President from suspending or limiting intelligence sharing with [[israel|Israel]]. The Brief presents the measure as a statutory constraint on presidential discretion in favour of a foreign service, and comments acidly that despite such legislation "it is considered 'antisemitic' to suggest that maybe Israel controls our politicians and is plundering our government coffers for cash, and now highly classified intelligence." The provision is cited alongside the reactions of [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]], [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]], and [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] as part of what the newsletter treats as an organised pushback against [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s Iran diplomacy.
