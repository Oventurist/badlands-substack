# United States Judicial Conference

The **U.S. Judicial Conference** is the national policy-making body for the federal courts, headed in an official capacity by the Chief Justice of the United States.[1] Its administrative arm is the [[administrative-office-of-the-us-courts|Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts]].[1]

## America First Legal lawsuit (2025)

In May 2025 the [[america-first-legal|America First Legal Foundation]], a pro-Trump legal group founded by White House aide [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]], sued Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] in his capacity as official head of the Judicial Conference, along with [[robert-conrad|Robert J. Conrad]], director of the Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts.[1]

The complaint accuses both the Judicial Conference and the Administrative Office of performing certain regulatory actions that go beyond the scope of resolving cases or controversies, or administratively supporting those actions, which the plaintiffs argue are the "core functions" of the judiciary.[1] It further argues that records held by the Roberts-led Judicial Conference should therefore be subject to Freedom of Information Act requests.[1]

The suit was described as a long-shot move as Trump allies fought court rulings blocking key actions from the Oval Office.[1]

## Commentary

On *Badlands Daily*, [[canncon|CannCon]] and [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] discussed the suit, arguing that administrative entities created under the judiciary operate almost entirely free from any constitutionally-mandated oversight.[1]
