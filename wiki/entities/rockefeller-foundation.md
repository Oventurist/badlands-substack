# Rockefeller Foundation

The Rockefeller Foundation is a major American philanthropic institution which, in Badlands Media's account, functioned as a funder and sponsor of the postwar social-engineering apparatus.[1]

Reporting on the Harvard experiments that included [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]], Alston Chase noted in *[[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]]* that [[henry-a-murray|Henry A. Murray]] — chairman of Harvard's Department of Social Relations, a fanatical devotee of world government, and a man who used "science" to attempt to transform the very nature of humankind — worked for the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]], the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and the Rockefeller Foundation.[1] The article places the foundation alongside those agencies as part of the institutional scaffolding behind [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] and the broader effort to convert "National Man into World Man."[1]
