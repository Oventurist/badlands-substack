# Rockefeller Foundation

The Rockefeller Foundation is a major American philanthropic institution which, in Badlands Media's account, functioned as a funder and sponsor of the postwar social-engineering apparatus.[1]

Reporting on the Harvard experiments that included [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]], Alston Chase noted in *[[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]]* that [[henry-a-murray|Henry A. Murray]] — chairman of Harvard's Department of Social Relations, a fanatical devotee of world government, and a man who used "science" to attempt to transform the very nature of humankind — worked for the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]], the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and the Rockefeller Foundation.[1] The article places the foundation alongside those agencies as part of the institutional scaffolding behind [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] and the broader effort to convert "National Man into World Man."[1]

## Funding of the Peking Man expedition

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] reports that while living in China, the Jesuit [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]] spent some months in Peking, where he joined a Rockefeller Foundation-funded expedition that in 1926 uncovered the remains promoted worldwide as [[peking-man|Peking Man]], a new "missing link" between ape and man.[2] The hundreds of bone fragments were crated for shipment to the United States and mysteriously lost, never to be recovered — an outcome Ehret treats as a sequel to the [[piltdown-man|Piltdown Man]] hoax.[2]

## Eugenics and population control

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treats the Rockefeller Foundation, created in 1913, as the central funding engine of the twentieth-century American [[eugenics]] movement.[3] Shortly after founding it, [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]] took charge of the [[bureau-of-social-hygiene|Bureau of Social Hygiene]] and directed it to conduct "research and education on birth control, maternal health, and sex education," and through the National Research Council — described as the foundation's medical division — funded the sex research of [[alfred-kinsey|Alfred Kinsey]].[3]

The foundation is credited with creating the pseudoscientific specialty of "psychiatric genetics" and with financing both German Kaiser Wilhelm Institutes: the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-psychiatry|Institute for Psychiatry]] and the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-anthropology-eugenics-and-human-heredity|Institute for Anthropology, Eugenics, and Human Heredity]], run for the Rockefellers by [[ernst-rudin|Ernst Rudin]] with [[otmar-verschuer|Otmar Verschuer]] and [[franz-kallmann|Franz J. Kallmann]].[3] In the 1940s the foundation funded the [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock Institute for Human Relations]], which hired [[sigmund-freud|Sigmund Freud]] with the goal of determining the most effective ways to manipulate and control mass consciousness.[3]

In its 1968 annual report the foundation acknowledged funding the development of "anti-fertility vaccines" and their implementation on a mass scale, alongside other measures to reduce US fertility — encouraging increased homosexuality, "fertility control agents" in the water supply, encouraging women to work in order to break up the family, abortion and sterilization on demand, and universally accessible contraception.[3] It also funded [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]], whose national medical director [[richard-day|Richard Day]] reportedly told a 1969 Pittsburgh audience that cancer cures were being withheld at the [[rockefeller-institute|Rockefeller Institute]] to prevent population increase.[3]

## Harvard endowment overlap

"The Harvard Control Grid" places the Rockefeller Foundation, with the [[ford-foundation|Ford Foundation]], among legacy philanthropies that "historically served as conduits for soft power" rather than as purely charitable bodies, crediting it with funding early UN programs, transhumanist research and "the architecture of post-war liberal globalism."[4] [[rick-slocum|Rick Slocum]], chief investment officer of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]], sits on the foundation's investment committee, which the article says places him "inside a historic philanthropic engine long tied to covert influence and population control initiatives."[4] Through that role, it adds, Slocum interacts with UN and global health agencies "many of which share analytic data with Western intelligence."[4]

## The Harvard thread

"The Ivy Web" states that the Rockefeller Foundation has funded [[harvard-university|Harvard]] research centers since the early twentieth century, with its influence most visible in public health policy channelled through the [[harvard-th-chan-school-of-public-health|T.H. Chan School of Public Health]].[6] The Foundation is grouped with the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]] as an entity that heavily shaped the formation of the [[world-bank|World Bank]], WHO-aligned public health agencies, and digital health certificate and biometric tracking pilot programs — initiatives the article traces to white papers developed in Harvard research labs working in behavioral psychology, systems modeling and epidemiology.[6] Rockefeller ties are also listed among the affiliations of [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board members.[6]
