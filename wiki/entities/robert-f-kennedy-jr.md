# Robert F. Kennedy Jr.

**Robert F. Kennedy Jr.** is an American environmental attorney, author, and political figure who ran as an independent candidate in the **2024** U.S. presidential election. In the Badlands Media article *5GW Counter-Offensive – The Censorship Regime and the Road to 2024* (September 27, 2023), Kennedy is cited as a target of the **Trusted News Initiative's** electoral interference, illustrating the TNI's role as a gatekeeper during moments of electoral jeopardy.

## Lawsuit Against the Trusted News Initiative

The Badlands source asserts that the TNI — founded in **2019** by the **BBC** as a coalition of news organizations and tech firms — attempted to interfere with Kennedy's 2024 campaign. The article references a February 2023 *Daily Sceptic* report stating that Kennedy sued the BBC and major media companies for allegedly "hobbling online rivals" through the TNI framework. The source frames the lawsuit as evidence that the TNI functions as an active electoral censor, suppressing dissident candidates and alternative media platforms during election cycles.

The Badlands article quotes the TNI's self-description as *"a global coalition of news organizations and tech firms with the stated goal of combating fake news,"* noting that the initiative was set up *"to protect audiences and users from disinformation, particularly around moments of jeopardy, such as **elections**."* The source argues that Kennedy's lawsuit demonstrates the TNI living up to its role as gatekeeper by interfering with a candidate outside the two-party establishment consensus.

## Relationship to 5GW Censorship Framework

Within the Badlands corpus, Kennedy is positioned as a victim of the quadrennial censorship offensive that recurs during Western elections. The article connects his targeting by the TNI to the simultaneous **Online Safety Bill** campaign in the UK and the coordinated de-platforming of **Russell Brand**, arguing that these represent synchronized efforts across state and non-state institutions to control information flows during the 2023–2024 pre-election period.
