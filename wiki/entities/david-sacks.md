# David Sacks

David Sacks is an American venture capitalist and technology executive who served as White House AI and Crypto Czar in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration; the Badlands Brief renders his surname as "Sachs."[1]

## Open-source AI shadow war

In late July 2026 Sacks argued publicly that [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[anthropic|Anthropic]] pursue government regulation of frontier AI out of competitive self-interest rather than safety.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that Sacks laid out "almost beat for beat" his own framing from the previous week, "openly stating that he believes they're only seeking government regulation so they can stave off competitors."[1] The remarks were cited alongside Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]]'s reported statement that the administration aims to "protect open source AI" as evidence that the [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source AI]] dispute had become an explicit policy fight in the administration's orbit.[1]
