# Robby Starbuck

Robby Starbuck is a conservative pundit and activist known for a sustained public campaign pressuring major American corporations to abandon diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) programs.[1]

## Walmart campaign

Starbuck was the first to announce, in a post on X, that [[walmart|Walmart]] would discontinue its DEI efforts, declaring that "Walmart is ending their woke policies."[1] The retailer subsequently confirmed a set of sweeping changes: it would no longer consider race and gender when making offers to suppliers, would stop offering DEI trainings through the Racial Equity Institute, would not renew a racial equity center established under a five-year commitment addressing gaps in outcomes for Black and African American people in education, health, finance and criminal justice, and would remove LGBTQ+ merchandise from its stores.[1]

Guardian coverage framed the retreat as occurring amid escalating attacks on such programs from conservatives, while Walmart's own statement emphasized its founding purpose of helping people save money and live better and its intention to be "a Walmart for everyone."[1] Starbuck continued to attack DEI efforts as part of his ongoing campaign.[1]

## Jack Daniel's campaign (2024)

In an overnight post on X in August 2024, Starbuck announced that Jack Daniel's would be the next target of his campaign exposing internal DEI programs.[2] Unlike his previous reveals, the whiskey brand's parent company, Brown-Forman Corp., had been tipped off well in advance and moved to cut its woke training before the expose landed; Starbuck said, "They must have been tipped off by us going through employee LinkedIn pages," and posted an email from company executives to employees describing the "evolving" situation around its DEI program.[2] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] reported that Starbuck's anti-woke crusade had already led Harley-Davidson Inc., Tractor Supply Co. and Deere & Co. to reverse or eliminate [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|diversity, equity and inclusion]] programs.[2]
