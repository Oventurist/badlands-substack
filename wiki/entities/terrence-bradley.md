# Terrence Bradley

Terrence Bradley is an Atlanta attorney who served as the former law partner and divorce lawyer of Special Prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]], and who became a central witness in the effort to disqualify Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] from the Georgia election interference prosecution.[1]

## Compelled testimony

Late on Monday, February 26, 2024, Superior Court Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] notified attorneys by email that certain communications Bradley had with Wade were not protected by attorney–client privilege, meaning Bradley could be compelled to tell the court what he knew about Wade's relationship with Willis.[1] The ruling followed a closed-door meeting between the judge and Bradley on Monday that lasted nearly an hour and twenty minutes, and raised the prospect that Bradley could be back on the stand as soon as Tuesday afternoon.[1] A spokesman for Willis said the district attorney's office could not comment except in court.[1]

Bradley remained subject to cross-examination following an attempted impeachment of his testimony by prosecutor Anna Cross — an attempt that, in the reading of Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], produced the Wade–Bradley privilege defense in the hearing and, when it failed, "opened the door to a lot more of Mr. Bradley's story."[1] Ashe in America argued that the combination of Bradley's testimony and the newly disputed cellphone evidence appeared to establish that Willis and Wade had perjured themselves, which she described as a career-ending scandal given that the alleged perjurers were the top prosecutor in the county and the lead prosecutor in the case.[1]

Bradley's testimony was expected to feed directly into the summations in [[michael-roman|Michael Roman]]'s disqualification motion, which were scheduled for Friday, March 1, 2024.[1]

## February 27, 2024 testimony

Bradley returned to the stand on Tuesday, February 27, 2024, and testified that he had no knowledge of when the romantic relationship between Willis and Wade began, saying "I don't know when the relationship started" and that he "never witnessed anything" — an outcome described as a potential setback for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his co-defendants.[2] Defense attorneys had for weeks suggested Bradley could provide crucial testimony contradicting the prosecutors' account that the relationship began only after Willis hired Wade in November 2021.[2]

Lawyers for Trump and the other defendants hammered at Bradley's credibility, reading aloud January text messages in which he told defense attorney [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]] that the romance had begun before Nov. 1, 2021.[2] Asked by Merchant, "Do you think it happened before she hired him?", Bradley had replied, "Absolutely."[2] On the stand he claimed he had merely been speculating and could not recall why.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported that Bradley was impeached a number of times regarding his recollection, and that his testimony revealed the "convenience" of his memory where Wade was concerned.[2] The movants also established that Bradley had reviewed Merchant's motion, made edits and said "looks good" — though he later claimed he referred only to the inclusion of his edits — and that he advised Merchant of no defect beyond those edits, effectively approving the motion's allegations about the relationship.[2] Trump attorney [[steve-sadow|Steve Sadow]] pressed him repeatedly on why he would speculate about allegations against a friend to a lawyer he knew was filing them; Bradley had no good answers before the judge halted the questioning.[2] The hearing was set to resume on the record that Friday at 1 p.m. ET.[2]

## Role in the disclosure of the Willis–Wade relationship

Testimony by attorney [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]] before the [[georgia-senate|Georgia Senate]] Committee on Special Investigations on March 6, 2024 described Bradley, Nathan Wade's former law partner, as a key early source of information about Wade's personal circumstances.[3] According to Merchant, Bradley was upset over what happened in Wade's divorce and disapproved of how Wade had treated his wife, remarking that he himself would not leave a wife without alimony.[3] Merchant recounted Bradley telling her that Wade left his wife of nearly 30 years shortly after the couple dropped their youngest child off at college, and after Wade met Fulton County District Attorney Fani Willis.[3]

## CNN text-message reporting (February 28, 2024)

Hundreds of text messages obtained by CNN and published on February 28, 2024 revealed that Bradley's involvement in the disqualification effort ran far deeper than previously known.[4] The 413 texts between Bradley and [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]], an attorney for one of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s co-defendants, showed months of communication and underscored how extensively Bradley assisted Merchant's pursuit of evidence for claims that [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] and [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] had an improper romantic relationship.[4] The day before publication, Bradley had reluctantly testified for more than two hours about the relationship.[4]

The messages raised questions about Bradley's credibility and about the degree to which Merchant relied on claims she was then unable to substantiate elsewhere, and helped explain why his testimony failed to meet the expectations of defense attorneys who had billed him as their star witness.[4] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that attorney Phil Holloway broke the texts publicly and read them as showing that Bradley, Wade and Willis had perjured themselves, highlighting Bradley's statement that the affair began in 2020 "when she left the DA's office and was a judge in South Fulton."[4]

## February 16, 2024 impeachment attempt

Bradley's testimony consumed most of the February 16, 2024 hearing day, with defense attorney [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]] and other defense lawyers meeting objection after objection on grounds of legal privilege.[5] Earlier in the hearing the defense had attempted to admit text messages between Merchant and Bradley; the Willis team objected on privilege grounds and the judge agreed to review the material *in camera* after the hearing.[5] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the granting of that in camera review was likely the moment the prosecution decided to abandon Bradley.[5]

On cross-examination, Willis's attorney [[anna-cross|Anna Cross]] sought to impeach Bradley and discredit him before the court, despite his having protected [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]]'s account all day.[5] In doing so she may have opened the door for the defense to compel Bradley to answer questions he had previously refused on privilege grounds — that is, the privilege may have been forfeited by her line of questioning.[5] The impeachment included a public accusation that Bradley had committed sexual assault during the period he worked with Wade, and the calling of a further witness to describe "going to clubs" and other conduct; that witness was excluded as irrelevant and in violation of the rules.[5] Ashe in America characterized the sequence as a public humiliation ritual.[5]
