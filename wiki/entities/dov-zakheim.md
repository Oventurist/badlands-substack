# Dov Zakheim

Dov Zakheim is a rabbi and former United States defense official who served as comptroller of the [[pentagon]] at the time of the September 11, 2001 attacks.[1] On September 10, 2001, the day before the attacks, Defense Secretary [[donald-rumsfeld|Donald Rumsfeld]] announced that the Pentagon was missing $2.3 trillion from its books; Badlands Media notes that Zakheim was the official who would have had control over those funds.[1]

Zakheim was a member of both the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]].[1] He worked in the Pentagon during [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]] and then in the private sector from 1987 to 2001 as CEO of SPC International, a high-technology firm that manufactured equipment to remotely control aircraft — a detail the article treats as significant given the hijacking narrative of [[september-11-attacks|9/11]].[1]
