# Matthew Ruth

Matthew Ruth was a 24-year-old Pennsylvania man who ambushed five police officers in September 2025, killing three of them, before being killed in the ensuing gunfight.[1]

## The North Codorus Township shooting

Officers from the North York County Regional Police Department and the York County Sheriff's Office were attempting to serve an arrest warrant on Ruth when he opened fire with an assault-style rifle, according to York County District Attorney Tim Barker, who described the events on Thursday, 18 September 2025.[1] Five police detectives and a deputy sheriff had sought to arrest Ruth on suspicion of stalking his ex-partner, as well as prowling and trespassing.[1] The shooting unfolded at a farmhouse belonging to his ex-girlfriend and her mother in North Codorus Township, roughly 185 kilometres (115 miles) west of Philadelphia.[1]

## Background

Ruth's former partner, who was not named publicly, told police she believed he had set fire to her pick-up truck in August 2025.[1] She reported to police on Tuesday, 16 September, that he was lurking outside her home in camouflage clothing and peering through a window with binoculars.[1]
