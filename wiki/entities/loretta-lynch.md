# Loretta Lynch

Loretta Lynch served as United States Attorney General during the second term of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration, the period covering the [[fbi|FBI]]'s 2016 investigation of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s private email server.[1]

## Role in the Clinton email investigation

In intercepted Russian intelligence discussed alongside the declassified [[clinton-annex|Clinton annex]], Lynch is described as the instrument through which Obama pressured FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] to "essentially bury the email scandal."[1] Badlands commentator Jon Herold cited this material as evidence that the bureau's failure to pursue the investigation fully — including its decision not to examine thumb drives containing hacked [[state-department|State Department]] data linked to Obama — reflected political direction rather than mere incompetence.[1]

## Durham annex allegations (2025)

The [[durham-annex|Durham annex]], declassified on July 31, 2025, included among the documents FBI officials assessed as "likely not be credible" one indicating that the [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] campaign was routinely obtaining information from Attorney General Lynch about "the plans and intentions of the FBI."[2] Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] cited the annex as evidence the [[fbi|FBI]] never followed up on information it had a decade ago and "had a hand in" efforts to stop [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]

## Alleged cooperation during the Russiagate period

In "Do You Believe in Coincidences?", Badlands Media identifies Lynch as the "bird" that was already singing by September 2018.[3] Q material cited there claims that Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] telephoned her four times in eleven days as pressure mounted on him, that those calls were recorded, and that Lynch herself was talking to investigators.[3] The same drops suggest her cooperation would expose the truth about the tarmac meeting with Bill Clinton, [[james-comey|James Comey]]'s handling of the Clinton email case, Barack Obama's instructions regarding that case, and alleged coordination by John Brennan and John McCain to frame the president.[3] The article also reports that [[bruce-ohr|Bruce Ohr]] was testifying against Lynch alongside Rosenstein, [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]] and Comey.[3]

## Treatment in the Q drops

Lynch appears in Q drop #36 of November 2, 2017, which pairs her with former president [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and their private meeting.[3] Chapter 5 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] relays the claim that Lynch was offered a Supreme Court seat in exchange for adhering to what the author calls the Russiagate lie and playing her assigned part.[3] The same drop is read as indicating that the [[national-security-agency|NSA]], then led by Admiral [[mike-rogers|Mike Rogers]], possessed complete intelligence on the [[clinton-lynch-tarmac-meeting|tarmac meeting]] and related conduct.[3]

## The tarmac emails and the "flip" claim

In "Setting the Stage," Joe Lange writes that Lynch has flipped on [[barack-obama|Obama]], [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[james-comey|James Comey]], which he offers as the explanation for her disappearance from public view, citing a Q post on the subject.[4] The article also revisits the [[clinton-lynch-tarmac-meeting|tarmac meeting]] between Lynch, then attorney general, and former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] during the FBI's Clinton email probe, noting Newsweek's report that documents show [[david-bowdich|David Bowdich]] and Comey discussed the press coverage of the meeting by email while Comey sought the source of the leak.[4]

## The tarmac leverage and the Supreme Court offer

"[[the-biggest-boomerang-in-history|The Biggest Boomerang In History]]" places Lynch at the head of the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] alongside [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]] as one arm of the conspiracy [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] describes, with [[james-comey|James Comey]] reporting to her when he became FBI director under [[barack-obama|Obama]].[5] Lange notes that Lynch has since disappeared entirely from public view, and frames the article's subtitle question — how do you trap a very dangerous animal — around her.[5]

He argues Lynch distanced herself from [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s exoneration because something significant had been discovered during the investigation, and that some in the media asked at the time why Comey was stepping on her toes by exonerating Clinton publicly.[5] Q drop 36 of November 2, 2017 asked what was negotiated on the tarmac between [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and Lynch, suggested the [[wizards-and-warlocks|wizards and warlocks]] tipped off a local reporter to the supposedly unscheduled stop, and stated that the NSA under Admiral Mike Rogers had the meeting miscatalogued under a false identity so that bad actors could not locate it while being assured no logs existed.[5] The Inspector General report, Lange writes, proved the [[clinton-lynch-tarmac-meeting|meeting]] had been arranged in advance.[5]

Lange contends Trump knew the Clintons were offering Lynch a seat on the Supreme Court in exchange for dropping the Clinton investigation, citing Q drop 1147 of April 15, 2018, which juxtaposes a "Supreme Court/Loretta Lynch deal drop," a Trump tweet and the tarmac.[5] Because the tarmac story broke in June 2016, Comey was forced into the spotlight to exonerate Clinton in July while Lynch escaped most of the scrutiny — which Lange explains by asserting that Lynch flipped, taking a deal rather than going down as a traitor with Clinton, much as [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] did, and has since been supplying evidence to grand juries.[5] Q drop 3364 of July 8, 2019 is quoted asking what happens when Lynch can no longer provide legal cover for the Clintons.[5] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
