# Loretta Lynch

Loretta Lynch served as United States Attorney General during the second term of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration, the period covering the [[fbi|FBI]]'s 2016 investigation of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s private email server.[1]

## Role in the Clinton email investigation

In intercepted Russian intelligence discussed alongside the declassified [[clinton-annex|Clinton annex]], Lynch is described as the instrument through which Obama pressured FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] to "essentially bury the email scandal."[1] Badlands commentator Jon Herold cited this material as evidence that the bureau's failure to pursue the investigation fully — including its decision not to examine thumb drives containing hacked [[state-department|State Department]] data linked to Obama — reflected political direction rather than mere incompetence.[1]

## Durham annex allegations (2025)

The [[durham-annex|Durham annex]], declassified on July 31, 2025, included among the documents FBI officials assessed as "likely not be credible" one indicating that the [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] campaign was routinely obtaining information from Attorney General Lynch about "the plans and intentions of the FBI."[2] Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] cited the annex as evidence the [[fbi|FBI]] never followed up on information it had a decade ago and "had a hand in" efforts to stop [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]

## Alleged cooperation during the Russiagate period

In "Do You Believe in Coincidences?", Badlands Media identifies Lynch as the "bird" that was already singing by September 2018.[3] Q material cited there claims that Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] telephoned her four times in eleven days as pressure mounted on him, that those calls were recorded, and that Lynch herself was talking to investigators.[3] The same drops suggest her cooperation would expose the truth about the tarmac meeting with Bill Clinton, [[james-comey|James Comey]]'s handling of the Clinton email case, Barack Obama's instructions regarding that case, and alleged coordination by John Brennan and John McCain to frame the president.[3] The article also reports that [[bruce-ohr|Bruce Ohr]] was testifying against Lynch alongside Rosenstein, [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]] and Comey.[3]
