# Flagler County Sheriff's Office

The Flagler County Sheriff's Office is the county law enforcement agency serving Flagler County, Florida, led by Sheriff [[rick-staly|Rick Staly]].[1]

## 2025 nurse impersonation investigation

In August 2025 the agency arrested [[autumn-bardisa|Autumn Bardisa]], 29, alleging that she had treated 4,486 individuals at [[adventhealth|AdventHealth]] Palm Coast Parkway since July 2023 while lying about being a registered nurse.[1] The arrest took place on Tuesday, August 5, 2025, after hospital paperwork tied to a pending promotion exposed the missing credentials.[1] Sheriff Staly characterized the matter as among the most disturbing medical fraud cases the office had ever investigated.[1]
