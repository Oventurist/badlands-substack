# Ghost of Patrick Henry

"Ghost" — writing as Ghost of Patrick Henry — is the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor who authored "Arabian Nights: The Redemption Arc of the House of Saud," the first installment of a series on Arabia, the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]], and [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]].[1] He can be followed on Twitter and Truth Social under the handles GhostofBPH and Ghostof_PatrickHenry.[1]

## Personal background disclosed in the article

The author frames his interest in Saudi Arabia through personal history.[1] The 2017 [[sword-dance|Sword Dance]] took place on his birthday, exactly one week before his wedding in the Dominican Republic, and he recalls reading the apoplectic media coverage from the beach with satisfaction at having supported [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the moment he descended the escalator.[1]

A few months after the wedding his older brother's body was found in the Rappahannock River, having fallen off a boat and been left for dead — a case he compares to news out of the Obama camp concerning Tafari Campbell.[1] His brother was a staunch Republican who had voted for Trump; on Election Day 2016 the author had texted him and their father that General Flynn was engaged in a silent civil war against Hillary Clinton and her cohorts.[1] Roughly eighteen months earlier, the brothers had watched their younger brother die of a Stage 4 glioblastoma — the same affliction that purportedly killed [[john-mccain|John McCain]] — choking on his own mucus in their mother's arms.[1]

The man he had asked to give a scripture reading at the funeral, out of concern for his need for closure given his proximity to the death, later came under investigation for killing his older brother, beginning a five-year fight against what he describes as all facets of corruption; one of three judges on the case rejected a sweetheart plea deal struck between a corrupt prosecutor and an overprivileged defendant, which he likens to the rejection of Hunter Biden's plea deal.[1]

## Path to Q

Months after his brother's death the Las Vegas shooting occurred, and weeks later an anonymous poster appeared on 4chan offering cryptic musings — met with the same slurs and cynical rebuke as FBIAnon and WHInsiderAnon before him — carrying a message of promise, hope, optimism and Justice that the author admits he was vulnerable to receive.[1] What caught his eye was not the prophecy of arrests but the cryptic recounting of the Sword Dance in Saudi Arabia, which drew him into years of research on MBS, a man his own age to whom he came to feel a connection.[1] The article closes with the standard Badlands disclaimer that contributor pieces represent the opinions of their authors.[1]

## The Blitz

On 10 January 2026 Ghost of Patrick Henry — credited as GhostofBasedPatrickHenry — co-hosted the debut episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], the weekly Badlands Substack show recapping and dissecting the Monday-to-Friday output of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands Brief]].[2] Hosting duties were announced as alternating between Ghost, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], and the post's author.[2] After explaining the intention behind the new show, the hosts turned to the previous week's [[information-war|Info War]] developments, from "Mad-lad Maduro" to the "Minnesotamali Madness," and reviewed reader comments from the newsletter.[2]

### Episode 11

Credited as GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, he co-hosted the eleventh episode of The Blitz, published 21 March 2026 under the title "Iran, NATO and the Invisible Enemy."[6] With [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] he used the spiraling narrative of the [[iran|Iran]] War to map what the hosts described as a strategic pincer in the offing by the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] against the true [[invisible-enemy|Invisible Enemy]].[6] The episode also addressed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the dangers of playing white hat–black hat games with the characters that orbit him on the [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation]] game board.[6]

### Episode 13

Ghost of Patrick Henry, again credited as GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, co-hosted the thirteenth episode of The Blitz, published 18 April 2026 under the title "Is the Real Fake War Ending ... Or Only Just Beginning?"[3] He and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] spent the full hour dissecting [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s Friday-morning "blitzkrieg" of the Info War battlespace, arguing that the material remained relevant days later even though the global mindscape and the players involved had yet to accept that the ending of the 'war' with [[iran|Iran]] had been decided before it was fought.[3]

### Episode 15

Credited once more as GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, he co-hosted the fifteenth episode of The Blitz, published 2 May 2026 under the title "Dissolution of the Global Cartel."[4] With [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] he used the ongoing dispute between the Trump Administration and [[us-congress|Congress]] over the [[iran|Iran]] War to attempt a definition of when a war is and is not happening at the same time.[4] The pair then tied the "Actual" and "Narrative" threads together across the Middle East and South America, arguing that the [[global-cartel|Global Cartel]] is being dismantled in plain sight on kinetic, financial and energy grounds.[4]

### Episode 18
