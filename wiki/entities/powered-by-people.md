# Powered by People

Powered by People is a political action committee founded by former Texas congressman and Democratic candidate [[beto-orourke|Beto O'Rourke]].[1] In August 2025 it was placed under investigation by Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] over its alleged role in supporting Democratic state legislators who broke quorum to block Republican redistricting legislation.[1]

## Paxton investigation

Paxton opened the probe into Powered by People one day before announcing a parallel investigation into the [[texas-majority-project|Texas Majority Project]], a PAC founded by former O'Rourke staffers and reportedly funded by [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1] The attorney general alleged that both groups may have engaged in unlawful financial coordination, potential bribery of the Democratic lawmakers who left the state, and illegal fundraising.[1]

The underlying dispute concerned a Republican-backed redistricting bill designed to add five GOP congressional seats in Texas; Democratic members' departure from the state deprived the House of the quorum needed to conduct business and stalled the measure.[1]
