# Switzerland

Switzerland is a European country historically known for its neutrality, which in October 2025 drew criticism from free-speech commentators over the criminal prosecution of a man for statements about gender and human skeletal anatomy.[1]

## Neutrality and speech policy

Legal commentator [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] observed that Switzerland was famous — or infamous — for staying neutral in World War II, declining to take a side between the Nazis and the rest of the world, but argued that on free speech the country had "declared war" on anyone challenging certain orthodox positions, including gender policies.[1]

## The Brünisholz case

Turley reported that Emanuel Brünisholz was about to begin a 10-day prison term for voicing skepticism about claims that skeletons are transgender.[1] He noted that there was very little coverage of the case, adding that free-speech cases are often downplayed by European media and that only limited information was available, largely from conservative outlets.[1]
