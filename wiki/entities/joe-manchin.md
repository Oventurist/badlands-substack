# Joe Manchin

Joe Manchin is an American politician who served as United States Senator from West Virginia and was long regarded as one of the most conservative members of the Democratic caucus. In the aftermath of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s July 2024 withdrawal from the presidential race, Manchin was named among the alternative candidates that some party financiers preferred over Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1]

## 2024 succession speculation

Democratic megadonor [[john-morgan|John Morgan]], announcing that he would not fundraise for the party if Harris became the nominee, suggested that "any combination" of Manchin, [[andy-beshear|Andy Beshear]], [[gretchen-whitmer|Gretchen Whitmer]] and [[josh-shapiro|Josh Shapiro]] would win the general election, adding that [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]] "wins in a landslide with any of these picks."[1] Manchin's inclusion on that list reflected donor interest in a centrist ticket capable of competing with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in swing states, and underscored the disunity within the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]] that persisted even as delegates rapidly coalesced behind Harris.[1]
