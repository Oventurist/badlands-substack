# Pilgrims Society

The Pilgrims Society is an Anglo-American dining and networking society treated in Badlands Media coverage as one of the institutional expressions of the transatlantic establishment behind the [[anglo-american-special-relationship|special relationship]].[1]

## Reference in the British-influence thesis

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites "Pilgrim-Society member [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]" and the powerful influence Carlson holds among [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his supporters as one line of evidence that British intelligence influence over American politics continued into Trump's second term.[1] The claim is presented alongside two others: the description of [[sebastian-gorka|Sebastian Gorka]] as a British intelligence asset ahead of his appointment as Deputy Assistant to the President and Senior Director for Counterterrorism in 2025, and the characterisation of Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] as a Soros-connected financier.[1] Taken together, Ehret argues, evidence of continued British intelligence influence "abounds."[1]
