# August Belmont

August Belmont is described by Badlands Media as the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild]] family's United States emissary, who stood at the helm of the merger of trust companies that [[jp-morgan|J.P. Morgan]] engineered during the [[panic-of-1907|Panic of 1907]].[1]

## Role in the Panic of 1907

During the panic Morgan absorbed the Mercantile Trust and six additional trust companies and banks, including the two largest institutions hit hardest — The Trust Company of America and Lincoln Trust.[1] Morgan's Guarantee Trust and Manhattan Trust companies would control them, with Belmont at the helm of the merger.[1] The article treats this consolidation as one of the largest cases of corporate concentration in American history and as a step toward the banking centralization that produced the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1]
