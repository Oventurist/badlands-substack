# Diana Fox Carney

Diana Fox Carney is an economist and the wife of Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]].[1] She is a senior fellow at [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]], the Ottawa think tank that Badlands Media contributor Matthew Ehret describes as having groomed [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] and advanced the agenda of North American integration.[1]

Her position at Canada 2020 is cited by Ehret as evidence of the tight family and institutional overlap between the Carneys and the think-tank apparatus that shaped Liberal Party leadership selection in Canada.[1] Canada 2020 itself was co-founded by Privy Councillors John Manley and Thomas Axworthy, both of whom helped draft the 2005 Task Force report calling for a North American Union with a single currency modelled on the Euro, a harmonized regulatory regime, and an unelected managerial class ruling across borders.[1]
