# Andreas Malm

**Andreas Malm** is a Swedish academic at Lund University described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as an "eco-terror professor" who has built a career advancing the argument that human society's only salvation lies in destroying the "dirty energy infrastructure" that sustains modern life.[1]

Malm is the author of the 2021 book *How to Blow Up a Pipeline*, the sociological text that gave rise to the 2022 film of the same name.[1] Ehret notes that the book is assigned reading in hundreds of universities across Europe and North America, and argues that its penetration into mainstream academia means it cannot be dismissed as fringe thinking.[1]

## The moral case for sabotage

In a 2021 article published by *The Guardian*, Malm wrote: "We could destroy the machines that destroy this planet. If someone has planted a time bomb in your home, you are entitled to dismantle it… This is the moral case which, I would argue, justifies destroying fossil fuel property. That is completely separate from harming human bodies, for which there is no moral case… The days of gentle protest may be long over."[1]

Ehret situates Malm within a broader body of what he calls pseudo-intellectual books and peer-reviewed sociological essays that he regards as a precondition for a coordinated "bottom up" and "top down" assault on industrial civilization.[1]

## Reception

[[naomi-klein|Naomi Klein]], author of *The Shock Doctrine*, has praised Malm as "one of the most original thinkers on the subject" of climate change.[1] Ehret regards this endorsement as ironic, given his contention that Klein's embrace of radical degrowth and green energy would itself inflict a form of murderous economic shock therapy on the world's population.[1] Canadian broadcaster [[david-suzuki|David Suzuki]] repeated Malm's thesis at an [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]] event in November 2021.[1] Ehret also groups Malm with professor [[david-skrbina|David Skrbina]] as modern co-thinkers in the anti-industrial current he traces back to [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]].[1]
