# Ezra Cohen Watnick

Ezra Cohen Watnick is a U.S. intelligence official described in Badlands Media's account as General Michael Flynn's right-hand man, whom [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] prevented H.R. McMaster from firing.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

A [[qanon|Q]] drop asked who briefed [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] and [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] on classified intelligence regarding the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] and [[fbi|FBI]]; the author identifies Cohen Watnick as that person.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) According to the article, after Cohen Watnick had gathered sufficient evidence, Trump installed him at the DOJ for the express purpose of transmitting classified intelligence evidence to Goodlatte and Gowdy, and that evidence is now in the public records awaiting release at what the author calls the perfect time.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Career arc as described in "We Won't Forget"

Cohen-Watnick was brought onto [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s transition team and then onto the National Security Council by retired Lt. Gen. [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]], a leading critic of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] who had been his boss at the [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] and who preceded [[h-r-mcmaster|H.R. McMaster]] as national security adviser.[5] At 31 he served as senior director for intelligence programs, the liaison between the intelligence community and the President.[5] Politico reported that Trump overruled McMaster's decision to sideline him after he fell out of favor with some at the CIA, and that Cohen-Watnick and Flynn "saw eye to eye about the failings of the CIA human intelligence operations," with one consultant saying the agency "saw him as a threat, so they tried to unseat him and replace him with an agency loyalist."[5] He was also protected by Jared Kushner and Steve Bannon, and it took McMaster nearly six months to remove him.[5]

After his ouster in August 2017 he went to work for the national security company [[oracle|Oracle]] — a firm the article notes is tied to Trump and of interest to Flynn, who specializes in 5G warfare.[5] Trump then ordered the Department of Justice to hire him into an influential position advising Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] on counterintelligence and counterterrorism.[5] He had drawn public scrutiny for briefing Rep. [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] on classified intelligence concerning the Obama administration's incidental surveillance of Trump transition officials, and [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] adds that he also briefed [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] and [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] on classified material about the DOJ and FBI, making him the conduit between intelligence-community evidence and congressional treason investigations.[5]

He later served as acting undersecretary of defense for intelligence and security, working with Director of National Intelligence [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]] and [[christopher-miller|Chris Miller]]; in that role he announced the designation of the intelligence element of the [[united-states-space-force|U.S. Space Force]] as a member of the intelligence community, saying the change aligned the newest service with the rest of the Defense Intelligence Enterprise.[5] Lange reads the whole sequence — from criminal investigations of treason to the restructuring of the intelligence community — as "military planning at its finest."[5] These characterizations reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
