# P. T. Barnum

**P. T. Barnum** is described in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — What is Clown World?" as one of the most famous names associated with the circus and the originator of "the greatest show on Earth."[1] The essay credits him with conceiving the three-ring circus.[1]

The author treats the choice of three rings as significant rather than arbitrary, asking why not two or four, and offers two lines of explanation: that dark occultists habitually invert what is good, beautiful and true, so that a three-ring circus might encode an inverted trinity; and, more concretely, that the three rings are a symbol of the [[independent-order-of-odd-fellows|Odd Fellows Society]], of which Barnum was a prominent member alongside figures such as Burl Ives, King George and Charlie Chaplin.[1] Barnum thus functions in the essay as a bridge between mass popular entertainment and the secret societies the author accuses of seeding demon worship into culture.[1]
