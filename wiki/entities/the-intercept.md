# The Intercept

The Intercept is an investigative news outlet whose reporting on national security and press freedom is periodically aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news briefs.[1]

## Assange plea-deal reporting

In August 2023 The Intercept reported that the United States was considering a plea deal that would allow WikiLeaks founder [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] to return to Australia, following remarks by U.S. Ambassador [[caroline-kennedy|Caroline Kennedy]] to the *Sydney Morning Herald*.[1] The outlet noted that Assange had been held in a London prison since 2019 while contesting extradition, faced 18 U.S. charges — 17 under the [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]] — and that the 2010 files he published included footage of a U.S. airstrike in Baghdad that killed 18 civilians, among them journalists, along with hundreds of thousands of Iraq War field reports.[1] The Intercept reported that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] declined to comment and the [[state-department|State Department]] did not respond to its request for comment, and it obtained comment from Assange's brother Gabriel Shipton.[1]
