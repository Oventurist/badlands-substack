# Muckraker

Muckraker is an independent investigative journalism outlet founded by [[anthony-rubin|Anthony Rubin]].[1] The organization is known for direct, on-location field reporting rather than desk analysis, a style that placed it at the center of the December 2025 citizen-journalism surge described as the "[[nick-shirley-effect|Nick Shirley Effect]]."[1]

In late December 2025, Rubin took Muckraker to Columbus, Ohio, to knock on the doors of Somali-affiliated daycare facilities amid allegations of large-scale [[medicaid|Medicaid]] fraud in the state, publishing video and neighbor interviews from the first facility visited.[1] The work paralleled the broader scrutiny of daycare and nutrition programs in [[minnesota|Minnesota]] and Ohio that dominated the news cycle at the turn of the year.[1]
