# Freedom Party of Austria

The Freedom Party of Austria (FPÖ) is a right-wing populist Austrian political party led by [[herbert-kickl]].[1]

## 2024 election victory

In Austria's parliamentary election of September 29, 2024, preliminary results showed the FPÖ finishing first, making it the first far-right party to win a national Austrian election since the Second World War.[1] Kickl described the outcome as the beginning of a "new era."[1]

Analysts placed the win within a growing European trend of voters shifting toward anti-immigration and populist parties.[1] Despite topping the poll, the FPÖ was unlikely to lead a government, because Austrian law requires a coalition and all other parties had excluded cooperation with it — apart from Chancellor [[karl-nehammer|Nehammer's]] conservative People's Party (ÖVP), whose leader nonetheless refused to serve alongside Kickl on the grounds of his conspiracy beliefs.[1]
