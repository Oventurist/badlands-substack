# Wired

Wired is an American technology-focused magazine and news outlet.[1]

In June 2023, Wired obtained and reported on a newly declassified report prepared for Director of National Intelligence [[avril-haines|Avril Haines]] by her own panel of senior advisers, revealing that the United States government had been secretly amassing a "large amount" of "sensitive and intimate information" on its own citizens through purchases from commercial data brokers.[1] The magazine reported that Haines had first tasked those advisers in late 2021 with untangling a web of secretive business arrangements between commercial data brokers and members of the US intelligence community.[1]

The story was amplified by ZeroHedge and by Badlands Media commentary, which treated it as documentary evidence of a broader pattern of intelligence-community overreach.[1]
