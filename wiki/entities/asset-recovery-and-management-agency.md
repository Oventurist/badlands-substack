# Asset Recovery and Management Agency

The Asset Recovery and Management Agency (ARMA) is the Ukrainian state body responsible for tracing, seizing and managing assets recovered in criminal proceedings.[1]

## 2025 NABU investigation

In July 2025 Ukraine's [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau]] (NABU) confirmed that it had opened a probe into possible abuses involving ARMA.[1] The investigation followed a media investigation that tied the ex-husband of [[olga-stefanishina|Olga Stefanishina]] to questionable asset transfers handled through the agency.[1] No formal charges were filed, but confirmation of the pre-trial inquiry came only days before [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]] abruptly stripped Ukraine's anti-graft bodies of their independence.[1]

## Aftermath

The move against the anti-corruption agencies triggered an outcry from the European Union, warnings of an aid freeze, and widespread street protests, forcing Zelensky to restore autonomy to NABU and the [[specialized-anti-corruption-prosecutors-office|Special Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office]].[1] Anti-graft officials nevertheless maintained that "the damage has been done," citing a collapse in whistleblower cooperation and growing fears of political interference in cases such as the ARMA inquiry.[1]
