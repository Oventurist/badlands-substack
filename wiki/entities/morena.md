# Morena

Morena is the Mexican left-populist political party founded around [[andres-manuel-lopez-obrador|Andrés Manuel López Obrador]] and the vehicle for [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]]'s successful 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

In the June 2, 2024 general election, Sheinbaum — López Obrador's favored successor — won between 58.3 percent and 60.7 percent of the vote according to the National Electoral Institute, defeating opposition candidate [[xochitl-galvez|Xóchitl Gálvez]] and Jorge Álvarez Máynez.[1] Morena was also projected to hold its majorities in both chambers of Mexico's Congress, giving the incoming administration broad legislative control.[1] Sheinbaum pledged to continue the direction set by the outgoing populist leftist leader while offering a contrasting personal style.[1]
