# Kevin McCarthy

Kevin McCarthy is the American Republican politician who served as Speaker of the House of Representatives following a contested confirmation process, and who is treated in the Badlands corpus as an ambiguous figure whose loyalties could not be settled from his public conduct alone.[1]

## The speakership fight

Writing in June 2023, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recalled that his first impression of McCarthy during the speakership contest was that he was "just another weak Republican, maybe even a RINO," and that he sided with the [[america-first|America First]] congressmen opposing him.[1] Complicating that reading was the fact that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] endorsed McCarthy for the post while also endorsing the congressmen blocking him, which Carlson interpreted as evidence that Trump must hold leverage over McCarthy.[1]

After McCarthy was confirmed and his former opponents received significant leadership roles on congressional committees, Carlson concluded the sequence was staged: it needed to appear that McCarthy had been forced into those appointments in order to win confirmation, so that the Neocons and Democrats would accept the result and continue to believe he was working with them.[1] Carlson stopped short of trusting McCarthy but accepted that he was "playing ball" with Trump.[1]

## Evidence of Deep State reaction

Carlson cited the sudden intensity of events following McCarthy's elevation — the discovery of boxes of classified documents, train derailments, Chinese spy balloons and the uncovering of the Nord Stream pipeline sabotage — as indications that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] regarded the new speakership as a threat and had entered "full-on panic mode."[1]

## The debt ceiling and reassessment

The [[2023-debt-ceiling-agreement|2023 debt ceiling agreement]] overturned this reading. Carlson asked whether an increased debt limit with virtually no spending cuts was part of Trump's plan, whether further evidence of a [[uniparty|Uniparty]] was needed to wake more people up, and whether the economy needed to worsen before Trump returned to power as part of dismantling the Cabal banking system.[1] He raised a further possibility: that Trump had endorsed McCarthy with the intent of having him quickly ousted, and that McCarthy's caving to the Democrats was itself the mechanism for exposing the RINOs and Neocons.[1] Carlson noted that the America First congressmen had secured procedural measures making it easier to impeach the Speaker, which made this scenario impossible to ignore.[1]

He concluded that McCarthy had to be viewed from multiple angles at once: he could be working willingly with Trump, or working with Trump unwillingly as an instrument for exposing the Neocon wing.[1]
