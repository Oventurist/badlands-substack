# Kevin McCarthy

Kevin McCarthy is a U.S. Representative who became Speaker of the House in 2023 after a prolonged leadership fight. In the Badlands corpus, McCarthy is analyzed primarily as a case study in the broader theory of a Deep State-orchestrated destruction of American institutions. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Role in the "Communist Plot" Narrative

According to a June 2023 Badlands Media article by Erik Carlson, McCarthy's path to the Speakership and his subsequent actions are interpreted through a conspiratorial lens. The author initially viewed McCarthy as "just another weak Republican, maybe even a RINO," and stood with the Congressmen opposing his speakership. He noted that [[Donald Trump]] endorsed McCarthy but also endorsed the opposing Congressmen, leading him to conclude that Trump retained control over McCarthy regardless of the outcome. After McCarthy's confirmation and the appointment of opposing Congressmen to key committee positions, the author believed the arrangement served hidden purposes: McCarthy needed to appear to be working with Neocons and Democrats to maintain their trust, while secretly advancing a different agenda. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

The article identifies the subsequent escalation in events—classified document discoveries, train derailments, Chinese spy balloons, and the uncovering of Nord Stream pipeline sabotage—as evidence that the deep state had entered "full-on panic mode" in response to McCarthy's speakership. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Debt Ceiling Analysis

The debt limit agreement caused the author to reconsider his interpretation of McCarthy's role. He poses several alternative hypotheses: that an increased debt limit with virtually no spending cuts was part of Trump's plan to expose a "Uniparty," that economic deterioration was necessary before Trump could retake power, or that the agreement was designed to destroy the "Cabal banking system." A further possibility raised is that Trump intentionally endorsed McCarthy knowing he would be quickly ousted, and that McCarthy's concessions to Democrats on the debt ceiling were designed to expose RINOs and Neocons. The author notes that "America First Congressmen" had pushed for measures making it easier to impeach the Speaker of the House, and finds it notable that Trump had not mentioned the debt ceiling deal at the time of writing, focusing instead on [[Ron DeSantis]]. ^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]
