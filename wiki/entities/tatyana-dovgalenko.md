# Tatyana Dovgalenko

**Tatyana Dovgalenko** is a Russian Foreign Ministry official who has served as a public voice on Moscow's Africa policy.[1]

## Allegations against Ukraine in the Sahel

In late May 2025, Dovgalenko accused Ukraine of funneling Western-supplied weapons to militants operating across Africa and of training terrorist networks in the Sahel as part of what she described as "systematic efforts to destabilize the continent."[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited her allegation alongside [[niger|Niger]]ien president [[abdourahmane-tchiani|Abdourahamane Tchiani]]'s accusations against [[france|France]], suggesting a link to [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s disclosure that only half of the money sent to Kyiv actually arrived there.[1]
