# Mark David Chapman

Mark David Chapman, referred to in the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI" simply as Mark Chapman, is identified as the assassin of [[john-lennon|John Lennon]] and named among those suspected of having taken part in the [[cia|CIA]]'s [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]] mind-control program.[1]

## Function ascribed by the article

In the essay's classification of alleged patsies by purpose, Chapman occupies a category of his own: he "took out a man who was influential and vocal against the CIA agenda."[1] This distinguishes him from [[lee-harvey-oswald|Oswald]], [[jack-ruby|Ruby]], [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan]] and [[john-hinckley-jr|Hinckley]], described as used against people in power; from Jim Jones and [[charles-manson|Charlie Manson]], said to have been used to create fear and race wars; from [[david-koresh|Koresh]] and [[timothy-mcveigh|McVeigh]], said to have been used to make anti-government groups look violent; and from John Salvi, said to have been used to make pro-life supporters seem like violent extremists.[1]

## Context

MK ULTRA is characterised in the article as the CIA's alleged mind-control system, employing LSD along with physical and psychological torture to break subjects' minds and reprogram them as assets.[1] The essay's overall conclusion is that most alleged patsies both believed they were working with the CIA or [[fbi|FBI]] and had alleged ties to the agency's mind-control work.[1] No further evidence specific to Chapman is presented.[1]
