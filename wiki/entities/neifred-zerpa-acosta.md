# Neifred Zerpa-Acosta

Neifred Zerpa-Acosta is a man sentenced by an Arapahoe County judge to 12 years in prison in September 2025 for his role in two incidents at the Edge at Lowry apartments, at East 12th Avenue and Dallas Street in [[aurora-colorado|Aurora, Colorado]].[1]

## The viral video and arrest

Video recorded on August 18, 2024 from a resident's security camera inside the apartment complex went viral, showing armed men entering an apartment; Aurora Police identified Zerpa-Acosta as one of the men in the footage.[1] The video fueled claims that Aurora had been "taken over" by a Venezuelan gang and prompted President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to campaign in the city, where he told a crowd in October 2024, "I will rescue Aurora."[1]

Aurora Police arrested Zerpa-Acosta in December 2024 while responding to a report of a kidnapping that the police chief also described as torture.[1] Officers testified at another defendant's hearing that a wife was pistol-whipped on the head and her husband stabbed in the thigh with part of a multi-tool while the couple were held for more than two hours; the officers did not identify who committed those acts.[1]

## Plea and sentence

Zerpa-Acosta pleaded guilty to using a knife and firearm while committing or attempting to commit kidnapping, aggravated robbery, burglary and conspiracy.[1]

Badlands commentary from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the local NBC affiliate reporting the guilty plea had a year earlier accused Trump of exaggerating the scale of criminal migration into Colorado, and asserted that Zerpa-Acosta had been released by the Biden administration despite an outstanding deportation order.[1]
