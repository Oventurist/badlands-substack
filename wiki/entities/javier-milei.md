# Javier Milei

Javier Milei is the President of [[argentina|Argentina]]. In the Badlands Brief's coverage of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] final, Milei's foreign policy is offered as an explanation for the pattern of elite support behind the Argentine team. The Brief states that Milei "has been a great friend to [[israel|Israel]]," and that this alignment drew backing for Argentina from figures including [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] — described collectively in the source as "the entire Zionist Diaspora." The contrast drawn is with [[spain|Spain]], which the Brief credits with leading a European trend of "defecting from the longstanding tradition of supporting Israel no matter what," a divergence the author uses to explain why much of the online audience cheered for the Spanish side.
