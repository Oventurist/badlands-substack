# Javier Milei

Javier Milei is the President of [[argentina|Argentina]]. The Badlands Brief cited Milei's record as "a great friend to [[israel|Israel]]" as one of two reasons that pro-Israel political figures and commentators — the Brief named Benjamin Netanyahu and Mark Levin among what it called the "Zionist Diaspora" — publicly backed Argentina to win the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] final.[1] The other reason offered was that [[spain|Spain]] has led a trend of European countries breaking with unconditional support for Israel.[1]


## Rural land reform and the Patagonia fires

Amid the [[patagonia-wildfires-2026|Patagonia wildfires of 2026]], Milei's government pursued reforms that would significantly alter Argentina's land-ownership regime.[2] A legislative proposal developed by a government-aligned council would effectively remove longstanding limits on foreign ownership of rural land, overturning key provisions of the 2011 [[law-26737|Law 26.737]], and a further plan presented by the "Consejo de Mayo" envisaged broader liberalization of land purchases by private foreign investors.[2] A court ruling subsequently suspended the repeal, temporarily blocking the provisions that would have eliminated restrictions on foreign land purchases.[2] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reported that a December 9 executive order also repealed a 2020 law prohibiting the rezoning of rural land affected by forest fire for 30 to 60 years.[2]

## Austerity cuts and protests (December 2023)

Two weeks into his presidency, Milei faced [[argentine-austerity-protests-2023|street protests in Buenos Aires]] as socialist activists and workers' unions, carrying images of Che Guevara and Eva Perón, converged on the capital against his impending "austerity" cuts.[3] The measures formed part of a sweeping economic programme that would erase or rewrite over 300 rules regulating and restricting private enterprise in Argentina, which protesters said would favour big business while reducing welfare programmes and public protections.[3] In a televised speech from the presidential palace, Milei said the goal was "to start along the path to rebuilding the country... and start to undo the huge number of regulations that have held back and prevented economic growth."[3]

## December 2023 decree cutting state jobs

On December 26, 2023, shortly after taking office, Milei — an economist by training — signed a decree stating that his government would not renew the contracts of state employees hired since January 1 of that year, an estimated 5,000 workers, as part of a broader effort to cut government spending amid Argentina's troubled economy.[4] Published in the Official Gazette, the decree carved out exceptions for workers hired under "quotas regulated by law or other types of special protections," including those with disabilities and personnel deemed "indispensable."[4] It also provided that government contracts made before 2023 would undergo an "exhaustive review" within 90 days.[4] The Association of State Workers (ATE) union anticipated that as many as 7,000 employees could be affected.[4]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted the competing claims about Milei — sovereign savior versus "WEFian puppet" installed to rug-pull the sovereign movement — but assessed his net effects as positive so far, citing the job cuts as "a decidedly anti-globalist, anti-communist move," his public signaling that the central banking sector represents an existential threat to prosperity, and his explicit invocation of [[accelerationism|accelerationism]] in claiming that "gradualist programs" always end in failure.[4]

## Opening the Nazi ratline files (February 2025)

Milei promised officials of the [[simon-wiesenthal-center|Simon Wiesenthal Center]] his full cooperation in granting access to Argentine documents related to the financing of the so-called [[ratlines|"ratlines"]] that helped Nazis escape Europe after the Holocaust.[5] The commitment was made at the presidential palace, Casa Rosada, in Buenos Aires during a meeting with Milei and activists.[5]

For decades organizations including the Simon Wiesenthal Center had sought records relating to the unofficial escape routes taken by thousands of Nazis after the Second World War; up to 10,000 Nazis and other fascist war criminals escaped justice by fleeing to Argentina and other countries.[5] Rabbi Abraham Cooper, associate dean of the Center, told The Times of Israel that while some previous leaders had promised full cooperation, "Milei is the first to act with lightning speed to enable the SWC to uncover important pieces of the historic puzzle, especially as it related to involvement with Nazis before, during and after the Holocaust."[5] Badlands commentary linked the anticipated disclosures to the JFK files and to the deeper history of [[prussia|Prussia]] and German unification.[5]

## 2025 legislative elections

In the [[argentina-2025-legislative-elections|legislative elections of October 26, 2025]], Milei's party secured more than 40 percent of the vote, winning 64 of the 127 lower-chamber seats and 13 of the 24 upper-chamber seats — enough to shield his vetoes from being overturned, and far above the roughly 30 percent forecast.[6] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had staked US financial support on Milei's success, called the outcome "a big win in Argentina" and credited his own "very strong endorsement."[6] Badlands hosts contrasted Trump's support for Argentina with his pressure on Canada, while a dissenting Badlands take dismissed the result as another "fake election" won by "Lil Chainsaw Man."[6]

## Argentine Nuclear Plan

In December 2024 Milei unveiled the [[argentine-nuclear-plan|Argentine Nuclear Plan]], a programme intended to make [[argentina|Argentina]] a global energy powerhouse by building [[small-modular-reactor|Small Modular Reactors]] to power commercial sectors and other large-scale operations.[7] "After years of stagnation, nuclear energy is making a powerful comeback, and we are determined to lead, not follow," he declared, citing Argentina's natural resources, skilled workforce and Patagonia's cold climate as ideal for energy-intensive technologies such as AI, and calling nuclear "the only source that is sufficiently efficient, abundant and rapidly scalable to cope with the development of our civilization."[7] Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that opinion on Milei varies within the truth community, but that by the public narrative he had shrunk the federal government while increasing GDP and eliminating a large deficit — mirroring promises [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] made for his second term.[7]

## 2023 presidential victory

Milei won Argentina's presidential election in provisional results announced on 19 November 2023, wrenching the country to the right with a bombastic anti-establishment campaign that drew comparisons to that of former U.S. president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], all against the backdrop of one of the world's highest inflation rates.[8] His rival [[sergio-massa|Sergio Massa]] conceded the run-off on Sunday evening in a brief speech even before official results were announced, saying "Milei is the president elected for the next 4 years" and confirming he had already called to congratulate him.[8] Provisional results showed Milei with over 55 percent of the vote — 13,781,154 ballots — with more than 94 percent counted, according to the National Electoral Chamber, which had not yet declared an official winner.[8]

The victory marked an extraordinary rise for the former television pundit, who entered the race as a political outsider promising to "break up with the status quo" exemplified by Massa.[8] His campaign pledge to dollarize Argentina, if enacted, was expected to thrust the country into new territory, since no country of Argentina's size had previously turned over the reins of its own monetary policy to Washington decisionmakers.[8] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], recalling that Milei had stunned the world in August by taking the majority of primary votes as a third-party candidate, predicted that his connections with Russia would be immediately investigated and that fabricated scandals would follow, described his plan for government as "a Libertarian fever dream and a Hail Mary to save his nation," and asked whether the world would discover that "the Argentine Paradox was a feature, not a bug."[8] See [[2023-argentine-general-election|2023 Argentine general election]].[8]
