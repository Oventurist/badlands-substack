# Osprey Global Solutions

Osprey Global Solutions is a private security and logistics company founded by retired U.S. Army Major General David Grange, proposed in 2011 as a vehicle for delivering equipment to anti-Gadhafi forces in Libya.[1]

## The 2011 Libya proposal

On July 14, 2011, [[sidney-blumenthal|Sidney Blumenthal]] emailed [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] about a forthcoming trip to [[turkey|Turkey]] at which she could meet Grange, whom reporting described as "another arms broker."[1] Blumenthal pressed for the United States to sign a $114 million contract with Osprey to deliver equipment to anti-Gadhafi forces via a hospital ship, framing it as "a private contract" that "puts Americans in a central role without being direct battle combatants" and noting that "the TNC (National Transitional Council) wants to demonstrate they are pro-U.S."[1]

The structure closely resembled the parallel private-broker approach pursued by [[marc-turi|Marc Turi]] and [[turi-defense-group|Turi Defense Group]], and matched Clinton's own contemporaneous suggestion that "the idea of using private security experts to arm the opposition should be considered."[1] Blumenthal told the FBI he had ties to Osprey and stood to collect a finder's fee if the deal closed.[1] One day after his message, the Obama administration recognized the [[national-transitional-council|National Transitional Council]] as Libya's legitimate government, unlocking $30 billion in previously frozen Libyan assets.[1]
