# World Professional Association for Transgender Health (WPATH)

The World Professional Association for Transgender Health, commonly abbreviated WPATH, is described in Badlands Media coverage as the world's leading transgender health lobby group and the body responsible for issuing the [[wpath-standards-of-care|Standards of Care]] that guide clinical practice in gender medicine.[1]

## Jacobs controversy

In March 2024, [[reduxx|Reduxx]] reported that [[laura-a-jacobs|Laura A. Jacobs]], a WPATH-certified New York psychotherapist and trans-identified male, had influenced the development of WPATH guidance on "gender dysphoria" in adolescents and had served as an influential member of the committee that established the most recent Standards of Care.[1] Jacobs had previously contributed a chapter to an anthology promoting BDSM practices including "age play" and "genital torture," and had described himself as a "kinky, BDSM sexworker" who counsels clients from "adolescents to the elderly."[1]

The report, amplified by circulating footage of a TEDx talk in which Jacobs speculated about "artistic and creative" genital surgeries, intensified existing criticism of WPATH's internal standards-setting process and of the professional backgrounds of those shaping adolescent gender medicine guidance.[1]
