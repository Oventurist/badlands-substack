# Jack Teixeira

Jack Teixeira is a member of the U.S. Air National Guard alleged to have shared classified European intelligence reporting on the chat platform Discord, material that later formed the basis of reporting on advance knowledge of a Ukrainian plan to attack the Nord Stream pipeline.[1]

## The Discord leak and Nord Stream reporting

According to the [[washington-post|Washington Post]], European intelligence reporting indicating that the Ukrainian military had planned a covert attack on the undersea Nord Stream network was shared on Discord, allegedly by Teixeira, an Air National Guard member.[1] The Post obtained a copy of the material from one of Teixeira's online friends.[1] The document described a plan involving a small team of divers reporting directly to the commander in chief of the Ukrainian armed forces, and had been collected by a European intelligence service and shared with the [[cia|CIA]] in June 2022 — three months before saboteurs bombed the pipeline.[1]

The intelligence report was based on information obtained from an individual in Ukraine, and the source's information could not immediately be corroborated; the CIA nonetheless shared the report with Germany and other European countries, according to officials speaking on condition of anonymity.[1] The material provided some of the most specific evidence to date linking the government of Ukraine to the eventual attack in the Baltic Sea, which U.S. and Western officials described as a brazen and dangerous act of sabotage on Europe's energy infrastructure.[1] See [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream sabotage]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the emergence of the Teixeira-sourced reporting on the same day as the [[kakhovka-dam-destruction|Kakhovka dam]] story as evidence of what he calls [[overtons-goalpost|Overton's Goalpost]] — a forced retreat by legacy media from previously approved positions on Ukraine — while noting that [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]] had blamed the CIA rather than Ukraine for the sabotage.[1]
