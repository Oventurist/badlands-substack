# Jack Teixeira

Jack Teixeira is a member of the U.S. Air National Guard alleged to have shared classified European intelligence reporting on the chat platform Discord, material that later formed the basis of reporting on advance knowledge of a Ukrainian plan to attack the Nord Stream pipeline.[1]

## The Discord leak and Nord Stream reporting

According to the [[washington-post|Washington Post]], European intelligence reporting indicating that the Ukrainian military had planned a covert attack on the undersea Nord Stream network was shared on Discord, allegedly by Teixeira, an Air National Guard member.[1] The Post obtained a copy of the material from one of Teixeira's online friends.[1] The document described a plan involving a small team of divers reporting directly to the commander in chief of the Ukrainian armed forces, and had been collected by a European intelligence service and shared with the [[cia|CIA]] in June 2022 — three months before saboteurs bombed the pipeline.[1]

The intelligence report was based on information obtained from an individual in Ukraine, and the source's information could not immediately be corroborated; the CIA nonetheless shared the report with Germany and other European countries, according to officials speaking on condition of anonymity.[1] The material provided some of the most specific evidence to date linking the government of Ukraine to the eventual attack in the Baltic Sea, which U.S. and Western officials described as a brazen and dangerous act of sabotage on Europe's energy infrastructure.[1] See [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream sabotage]].[1]

## April 2023 arrest

On Thursday, April 13, 2023, Teixeira — then 21 years old — was arrested in connection with the leak of hundreds of classified documents, according to the [[washington-examiner|Washington Examiner]] and several other outlets.[2] He had been identified by the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] shortly before the arrest, which took place at his home in Dighton, Massachusetts.[2] Television footage appeared to show armed personnel leading a male wearing red shorts and a green shirt with his hands cuffed behind his back, the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] reported.[2]

Two police cruisers from the town of Dighton blocked the road near a residence associated with Teixeira and declined to say why, while a row of residents waited to pass; one neighbour said police began blocking off the street around noon, and at least one vehicle outside the blockade carried U.S. government license plates.[2] Officials told the Wall Street Journal that the leaked documents likely came from an [[air-national-guard|Air National Guard]] member.[2] See [[2023-pentagon-document-leaks|2023 Pentagon document leaks]].[2]

## Symbolic reading of the name

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] offered a symbolic reading of the case, noting that "Teixeira" derives from the Portuguese and Galician *teixo*, meaning "yew tree," a sacred tree of the Druids traditionally growing in the British Isles.[2] He observed that yew trees were used as symbols of immortality but also as omens of doom, with yew branches customarily carried on [[palm-sunday|Palm Sunday]] and at funerals, and that in Ireland yew was called "the coffin of the vine" because wine barrels were made of yew staves.[2] He added that "Jack" is a British name meaning "God is gracious."[2]

Deschamps argued that the shifting media coverage of the leak indicated an evolving narrative situation, since organic events require spin to be fitted into a narrative, whereas manufactured events are typically presented in a single settled form — citing the rapid naming of Islamic terrorists, new legislation and lock-step institutional reactions within days of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11, 2001 attacks]].[2] He suggested the leak could have been a white hat operation, or at minimum reflected sentiment within the young military-minded population, and concluded that whatever its origin it damaged false media coverage of the [[russia-ukraine-war|Russia-Ukraine conflict]].[2]
