# Michael Keaton

Michael Keaton is an American film actor. He appears in Badlands Media commentary only as an example of Hollywood hostility toward the second Trump administration.[1]

## Netflix talk show appearance

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that he had watched a Netflix talk show whose guests included Keaton and folk singer [[joan-baez|Joan Baez]], describing them as "Trump-hating."[1] During the episode Baez asserted that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] were attacking democracy, and the panel and audience applauded.[1] Carlson cited the segment as an illustration of celebrity culture amplifying mainstream-media narratives against the administration.[1]
