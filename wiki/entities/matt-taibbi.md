# Matt Taibbi

Matt Taibbi is an American journalist and author who became one of the principal reporters on the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]], the internal document releases published after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] acquired Twitter.[1] On March 9, 2023, Taibbi appeared alongside [[michael-shellenberger|Michael Shellenberger]] as a witness before the [[house-weaponization-committee|House Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]], where the pair testified about federal agency involvement in online content moderation.[1]

## Testimony before the weaponization subcommittee

The hearing was described as "full of fireworks, facts, and ad hominem friction," with Democratic Ranking Member [[stacey-plaskett|Stacey E. Plaskett]] opening by labeling Taibbi and Shellenberger "so-called journalists" and characterizing them as dangerous and a "threat" to former Twitter employees.[1] Plaskett argued that Republicans had brought in "two of Elon Musk's 'public scribes'" to release cherry-picked, out-of-context emails and screenshots designed to promote Musk's chosen narrative for partisan gain.[1]

Taibbi responded directly to the characterization, stating: "I'm not a so-called journalist. I've won the National Magazine Award, the I.F. Stone Award for Independent Journalism, and I've written 10 books."[1] The exchange was widely circulated on social media by commentators including Greg Price.[1]

In his substantive testimony, Taibbi summarized the Twitter Files findings as "a sweeping effort to reverse that promise, and use machine learning and other tools to turn the internet into an instrument of censorship and social control," adding that the U.S. government "appears to be playing a lead role" and that "effectively, news media became an arm of a state-sponsored thought-policing system."[1]

## Reception in independent media

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the 2023 congressional hearings, including this one, not as ineffectual "kangaroo courts" but as "massive exposure and disclosure operations" intended to keep the legacy media in a reactive and defensive posture before an increasingly skeptical public.[1] Taibbi's testimony was treated in that commentary as central evidence for the existence of a [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship-industrial complex]].[1]

## CCDH "kill Musk's Twitter" exclusive
In October 2024, Taibbi and journalist Paul D. Thacker published an election exclusive through Racket News and The Disinformation Chronicle, based on leaked internal documents from the UK's Center for Countering Digital Hate.[2] The documents showed the group planning in writing to "kill Musk's Twitter" while strengthening ties with the Biden/Harris administration and with Democrats including Senator Amy Klobuchar.[2] The report noted that CCDH's founder, British political operative Morgan McSweeney, was by then advising the Kamala Harris campaign, and it was amplified by ZeroHedge and answered by Elon Musk with the statement "This is war."[2]
