# The Jerusalem Post

*The Jerusalem Post* is the English-language Israeli newspaper cited in the March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief as the outlet that confirmed the remarks of [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] Chief of Staff [[eyal-zamir|Eyal Zamir]] during a security cabinet meeting.

## Confirmation of Zamir's warning

The Brief quotes the paper's reporting: "The IDF could soon collapse if there is no solution to the shortage of manpower, IDF Chief of Staff Lt.-Gen. Eyal Zamir warned in remarks during a security cabinet meeting held on Wednesday. 'I am raising 10 red flags before the IDF collapses into itself,' Zamir said during the cabinet meeting, *The Jerusalem Post* confirmed."

The outlet is also the vehicle for the same edition's reporting connecting claims about Iran's new supreme leader [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] to broader geopolitical narratives and internal Iranian power dynamics, as relayed in the context of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s remarks about the CIA's alleged intelligence on Khamenei.
