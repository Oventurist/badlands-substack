# Lily Phillips

Lily Phillips is an English [[onlyfans|OnlyFans]] performer who became internationally famous for filming sexual encounters with very large numbers of men at a single time.[1] In a March 13, 2025 article for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], the pseudonymous contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] treated her prominence, alongside that of fellow English performer [[bonnie-blue|Bonnie Blue]], as a subject of speculation connected to the [[grooming-gangs|UK grooming gangs scandal]].[1]

## Treatment in Badlands commentary

The article noted that Phillips is publicly described as being "supported by her family" in what she does, and characterised that framing as a cover story.[1] The author stated that her family financially benefits from her "sex work," and argued that this amounts to a family wanting her to be used by as many men as possible for money, concluding that she and Blue were "raised by pimps."[1]

American Hypnotist expressed the strong suspicion that Phillips is herself an abuse and grooming victim, and argued that she is not psychologically capable of making adult decisions, citing what he described as numerous tell-tale signs of dissociation, arrested development and abuse.[1] He pointed in particular to her reported statement that she has to watch her own videos in order to remember whom she has slept with, because she dissociates completely while making content for her OnlyFans account, calling this a profound symptom of deep trauma.[1] He argued more generally that the apparent enjoyment displayed by women in the sex industry cannot be taken at face value, since severe abuse can lead victims to reframe their situation as acceptable in order to survive.[1]

## Alleged narrative function

The article contended that Phillips and Blue were made famous precisely at the moment the UK grooming gangs were receiving international exposure, and that the timing could not be a coincidence.[1] In this reading their fame served to reinforce a stereotype of British girls as sexually loose, thereby bolstering the apologist claim that grooming victims consented to and enjoyed their abuse, and making justice harder to obtain for hundreds of thousands of victims.[1] The author speculated that, if his reading were correct, the families of both women might be connected to the [[cabal|cabal]] in some fashion.[1]
