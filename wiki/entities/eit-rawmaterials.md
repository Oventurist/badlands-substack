# EIT RawMaterials

EIT RawMaterials is an EU-funded agency focused on critical and key minerals for European industry.[1]

## Position on rare earths (December 2025)

In December 2025 the agency's chief executive, [[bernd-schaefer|Bernd Schaefer]], told Reuters that while Europe had finally taken major steps to build up its [[rare-earth-elements|rare earths]] sector to counter Chinese dominance, the region also needed to guard against being overwhelmed by the United States.[1]

Schaefer welcomed the release of a new EU action plan to secure critical raw materials used in electric cars, wind turbines and semiconductors.[1] The [[european-commission|European Commission]]'s REsourceEU plan includes investing 3 billion euros ($3.49 billion) over the following year to fast-track projects that could cut reliance on supplies from any single country.[1] Schaefer characterized the plan as "certainly a real step change from ambition to delivery, but it's still missing a couple of things."[1]
