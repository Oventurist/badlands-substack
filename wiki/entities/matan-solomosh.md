# Matan Solomosh

Col. Matan Solomosh is an Israeli military lawyer who recently completed his tenure as the Israel Defense Forces' chief military prosecutor before being detained by police in November 2025 in connection with the [[sde-teiman|Sde Teiman]] abuse video leak.[1]

## Detention

Israeli police detained Solomosh at the same time as former military advocate general [[yifat-tomer-yerushalmi|Maj. Gen. Yifat Tomer-Yerushalmi]].[1] Media reports indicated both were suspected of obstruction of justice over the leak of the Sde Teiman abuse video and an alleged subsequent cover-up in which false reports were given to officials about an internal investigation into the leak.[1] Police stated the pair, suspected of "leaking and other serious criminal offenses," would be brought before the Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court for an extension of their remand.[1]

## Context

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary noted that the disclosure of the pair's role in leaking the footage — which depicted IDF soldiers abusing Palestinian detainees — was followed by their immediate resignations and, in Tomer-Yerushalmi's case, a brief disappearance, before both were arrested by Israeli authorities.[1]
