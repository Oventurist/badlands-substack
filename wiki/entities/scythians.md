# Scythians

The **Scythians** were an ancient steppe people of the Black Sea region who, in the *Werewolf Game* series published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], are identified as the ethnic matrix out of which the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult emerged.[1] The series describes the Scythians as an ethnicity that "supposedly no longer exists," having gone extinct roughly a millennium ago.[1]

## Relationship to the Koryos

Drawing on *Wicked Teeth* by [[rogue-hypnotist|Rogue Hypnotist]], the article asserts that the Koryos were Scythian, and that while the total number of Scythian cult members is unknown, the cult constituted **the aristocracy of the Scythians**.[1] The decisive claim of the piece is that this aristocratic cult subsequently **interbred with the aristocracies of other peoples**, which the author offers as the explanation for why Koryos-style beliefs and rituals are allegedly practiced by "elites" the world over.[1]

## Claimed extinction

The article argues that Scythian disappearance is consistent with the cult's ideology: if the ruling class eschewed traditional family formation and normalized homosexuality, demographic collapse would follow.[1] The Koryos themselves, the piece says, asserted that they were possessed by demons who commanded them to love only members of their own sex.[1] Rather than preserving the Scythian nation, its rulers are described as having become "sadistic cultists" whose interbreeding with foreign aristocracies produced a worldwide, two-tier system of government grounded in dark occult practice.[1]

## Black Sea origin theory

The author suspects that a "major outbreak of evil" occurred around the Black Sea — an earlier installment of the series proposed that a literal portal to hell may be located in that region — and that this energy used the Scythian Koryos as a vector to spread chaos, violence, demon worship, and blood rituals across the world.[1] The article floats the wordplay that the Scythian Koryos may be the origin of the fictional "Sith."[1] This origin story is presented as the likely root of what the series calls the Dark Cabal or [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]

## Reception and status

These claims are the opinions of the contributing author and are not supported by mainstream archaeology or historiography, which treats the Scythians as a confederation of Iranian-speaking nomadic peoples without reference to a surviving worldwide occult aristocracy.[1]
