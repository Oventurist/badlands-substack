# William Stephenson

William “Little Bill” Stephenson was the British Security Coordinator and an [[mi6]] officer who met with [[james-jesus-angleton]] of the [[office-of-strategic-services|OSS]] and [[allen-dulles]] to discuss setting up the covert financing network that would underwrite [[operation-gladio]].[1] Under the plan, money would flow to Colonel [[paul-helliwell]] and onward to the [[world-commerce-corporation]] created by [[william-donovan]].[1]
