# Time Magazine

*Time* is an American weekly news magazine founded in 1923 and one of the most recognized titles in twentieth-century American journalism.

## 2024 layoffs

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] cites CNN reporting that in January 2024 Time laid off roughly 15 percent of its unionized editorial staff, "becoming the latest news outlet to slash its workforce." The magazine is listed among the widely recognized organizations that had experienced significant reductions in the weeks before the article's February 2024 publication, and [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]], quoted in the piece, notes that "Time Magazine also just laid off a ton of people." The corpus treats the episode as part of the systemic decline of [[mainstream-media|centralized media]] rather than an isolated business failure.
