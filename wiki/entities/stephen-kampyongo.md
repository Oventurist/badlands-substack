# Stephen Kampyongo

Stephen Kampyongo is a member of [[zambia|Zambia]]'s National Assembly representing the opposition Patriotic Front.[1] In an exclusive interview with [[rt|RT]] reported in the July 2024 Badlands News Brief, he stated that Zambia has no need for a United States military presence and questioned the relevance of security cooperation between Washington and Lusaka.[1]

Kampyongo made the remarks in response to regional concerns about the continued presence of [[africom|US Africa Command]] forces in Zambia and to demands from lawmakers for their withdrawal.[1] He said AFRICOM's objectives in the country are "not well spelled out," which he argued had sparked fears in Zambia and among its southern African neighbors, including [[zimbabwe|Zimbabwe]].[1]

Asked whether Washington was trying to impose its policies on Zambia's government, Kampyongo replied, "It's not desirable for… the United States... to impose an international request on us," adding that "Any international policy must be premised on mutual respect, appreciating the sovereignty of each state."[1] The Badlands commentary cited his statements as evidence that Zambia had told the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] to leave.[1]
