# George Doran

George Doran is the management theorist credited with creating the [[smart-goal-setting|SMART Goal Setting]] model in 1981, which specifies that goals should be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound.[1]

Doran's framework was invoked by election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] in a February 13, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] guest essay as the organizing structure for a realistic program of election reform under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], with Keshel citing Doran by name as the model's 1981 originator.[1]
