# Robert Anton Wilson

Robert Anton Wilson was an author and editor described in Badlands Media's [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series as a close colleague of [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]] and a significant figure in the reshaping of Western cultural attitudes during and after the 1960s.[1]

## Career

Wilson served as chief editor at [[playboy|Playboy]] magazine from 1960 to 1969.[1] He wrote numerous books from what the article characterises as a "post-modernist" or "deconstructionist" viewpoint — described there as a euphemism for writing intended to discredit traditional Western beliefs and values.[1]

## The Illuminatus Trilogy

Wilson co-authored the [[illuminatus-trilogy|Illuminatus Trilogy]] with his Playboy colleague [[robert-shea|Robert Shea]].[1] The article highlights the trilogy's conclusion, in which a globally influential evil cult attempts to animate an army of zombies held in suspended animation at the bottom of a giant lake, and proposes that this may constitute veiled disclosure concerning the [[koryos|Koryos]] cult and the [[black-sea|Black Sea]].[1]

## Interpretation in the series

Wilson's dual role — as a counterculture theorist connected to Leary and as an editor at a magazine the article treats as a hub for blackmail operations against U.S. politicians — is presented as a link between 1960s social engineering and the older occult currents the series traces.[1]
