# Mario Nawfal

Mario Nawfal is an online broadcaster known for hosting large, long-running Twitter Spaces on breaking news events.[1]

## Coverage of the Wagner mutiny

During the [[wagner-group-mutiny|Wagner Group mutiny]] of June 24, 2023, Nawfal hosted a twenty-four-hour live Twitter Space on the unfolding events.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the space featured "a stage full of neocons" who "damn near lost their minds playing pundit" while quoting alleged Wagner-run [[telegram|Telegram]] chats.[1] She recounted that at around 11am ET Nawfal himself declared, "Within the next few hours, we could see the Kremlin fall," which she characterized as the silliest theory of the weekend and an example of psychological operation-style commentary.[1] When [[wagner-group|Wagner]] columns turned around, she wrote, the assembled commentators could not hide the disappointment in their voices.[1]
