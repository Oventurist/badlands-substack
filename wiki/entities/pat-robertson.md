# Pat Robertson

Pat Robertson was an American televangelist and religious broadcaster identified in Badlands Media commentary as a key figure in the promotion of Christian Zionism in the United States.[1]

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that America underwent intense psychological conditioning during the latter half of the 20th century at the hands of figures such as CIA founding member [[john-singlaub|John Singlaub]] and the [[western-goals-foundation|Western Goals Foundation]], which he credited with elevating Robertson.[1] He asserted that if Robertson were alive in 2024 he would be urging his audience to stay silent and send money to [[israel|Israel]], and noted a personal connection — Robertson was a first cousin of the writer's grandmother — as the basis for his characterisation.[1] The remarks appeared in a broader argument that American Christians risk idolatry by "worshipping men on earth, in Israel," and should seek guidance from God rather than the secular authorities in Tel Aviv.[1]
