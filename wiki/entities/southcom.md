# United States Southern Command

United States Southern Command (SOUTHCOM) is the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] combatant command responsible for military operations in Latin America and the Caribbean.[1]

## Role in Operation Southern Spear

In November 2025 SOUTHCOM was named, alongside Joint Task Force Southern Spear, as co-lead of [[operation-southern-spear|Operation Southern Spear]], the counter-"narcoterrorist" campaign announced by Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]].[1] Hegseth described the mission as defending the homeland, removing narco-terrorists from the hemisphere, and securing the United States from drugs, declaring that "the Western Hemisphere is America's neighborhood."[1]

The command's area of responsibility encompassed the ongoing campaign of strikes against alleged cartel vessels in international waters of the Caribbean Sea, in which at least 20 boats had been destroyed and 80 people killed since September 2025.[1] Those strikes drew condemnation from Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], who argued the victims were "poor boatmen" and that suspects should be detained under due process.[1]
