# Richard Blum

Richard Blum was an American financier and the husband of California Senator [[dianne-feinstein|Dianne Feinstein]]. Following his death, the disposition of his estate became the subject of a family financial dispute that drew national attention in 2023.[1]

## Estate dispute

In August 2023, reporting indicated that Feinstein's daughter, [[katherine-feinstein|Katherine Feinstein]], had been granted power of attorney over the senator amid an ongoing financial dispute regarding Blum's estate.[1] The dispute unfolded alongside long-running questions about whether the senator, then 90 years old, remained capable of discharging her duties in the [[us-senate|United States Senate]].[1]

## Commentary on defense contracting

In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], commentator [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] alleged that Feinstein had consistently encouraged greater military spending while Blum profited as a military contractor, tying the couple to the broader dynamics of the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]].[1] DeLarme characterized the resulting family wealth as a "sizable and ill-begotten fortune" now at the center of an inheritance battle.[1]
