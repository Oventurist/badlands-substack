# Emanuel Fabian

Emanuel Fabian is an Israeli journalist who became the focus of international coverage in March 2026 after users of the prediction market platform [[polymarket|Polymarket]] threatened him over his reporting.[1]

## Polymarket threats (March 2026)

Fabian said Polymarket users threatened him over a routine report about a missile strike near Jerusalem, because the reporting could affect the resolution of a wager with more than $23 million at stake.[1] The episode was reported as intensifying scrutiny of [[prediction-markets|prediction markets]] at a moment when Democratic lawmakers were reportedly drafting legislation to rein them in following outrage over bets tied to war and other geopolitical events.[1]

Polymarket publicly condemned the threats, stating: "Polymarket condemns the harassment and threats directed at Emanuel Fabian, or anyone else for that matter. This behavior violates our terms of service and has no place on our platform or anywhere else. Prediction markets depend on the integrity of independent reporting. Attempts to pressure journalists to alter their reporting undermine that integrity and undermine the markets themselves."[1]

## Significance

The incident is cited as a concrete illustration of the argument that large event wagers create incentives for participants to pressure the reporters whose work determines a market's outcome, blurring the line between observing events and attempting to shape their coverage.[1]
