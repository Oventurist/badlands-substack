# Geoffrey Kennett

Geoffrey Kennett is a justice of the Federal Court of Australia who presided over the 2024 litigation between Australia's eSafety Commissioner and [[x-corp|X]] over the global removal of footage of the Wakeley church stabbing.[1]

## The eSafety injunction ruling

eSafety, headed by Commissioner [[julie-inman-grant|Julie Inman Grant]], had obtained a temporary injunction in April 2024 compelling X to block the stabbing footage, arguing that Australian geo-blocking was insufficient because users could evade it with a VPN. X argued that the regulator was seeking an effectively global ban that exceeded its jurisdiction.[1]

After a hearing on Friday 10 May 2024, and an extension of the expiring injunction to allow time for a decision, Justice Kennett ruled on the morning of 13 May 2024 that "the orders of the court will be that the application to extend … is refused." The injunction consequently lapsed, and a written decision setting out his reasoning had not yet been published at the time of reporting. eSafety indicated the matter would return for a case management hearing on 15 May 2024.[1]

The ruling was framed by commentators as a win for [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in a broader international dispute over the extraterritorial reach of national content regulators.[1]
