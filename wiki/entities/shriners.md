# Shriners

The **Shriners** are cited in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — What is Clown World?" as a second fraternal group connected to clown imagery, alongside the [[independent-order-of-odd-fellows|Odd Fellows Society]].[1]

The author writes that, much like the Odd Fellows, the Shriners present themselves as being "here to help," and remarks that for some inexplicable reason they like to dress up as clowns — again, he notes, under the banner of charity.[1] He places the Shriners within a broader argument that clown costumes are themselves a form of demon worship, or what the [[koryos|Koryos]] would have called ancestor worship, and that secret societies have for centuries been introducing demon worship to the public in the guise of entertainment.[1] At the same time, he cautions that groups such as the Shriners, the Odd Fellows and the [[freemasonry|Freemasons]] are often compartmentalized, so that individual members may have no awareness of any deeper occult content.[1]
