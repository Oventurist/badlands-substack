# Nissim Vaturi

Nissim Vaturi is an Israeli politician and a Member of the [[knesset|Knesset]] representing [[likud|Likud]].[1] On Wednesday, November 12, 2025, Vaturi told the Knesset plenum that "Kahane was right" and that his party had been wrong to "ostracize him," referring to the far-right nationalist rabbi [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]].[1]

## Knesset remarks

"I think Kahane was right — about many things. Absolutely not, he was not a terrorist," Vaturi said, despite Kahane having been convicted of terrorist activity in both Israel and the United States.[1] The exchange occurred during a Knesset debate on a bill to approve tax benefits for communities in the West Bank and the Gaza border area, in which MK Yorai Lahav Hertzanu of Yesh Atid attacked Likud's trajectory, telling the party that "from a proud liberal nationalist movement, you've turned into a pathetic branch of Kahane Chai."[1]

## Context

Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] situated Vaturi's defense of Kahane within a broader observation that the senior figures of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s Security Cabinet are Kahanists, and that "the worst people imaginable to represent Israel internationally have found themselves in key positions" of the government.[1]
