# Mubadala Investment Company

Mubadala Investment Company is an Emirati sovereign investment vehicle that was among the large investors approached by [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] in the weeks before his November 2023 ouster from [[openai|OpenAI]].[1]

Altman travelled to the Middle East to fundraise for an AI chip venture code-named [[tigris|Tigris]], intended to produce semiconductors competing with those of [[nvidia|Nvidia Corp.]], and held talks with Mubadala alongside [[softbank|SoftBank Group Corp.]] and Saudi Arabia's [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], seeking tens of billions of dollars across that project and a separate AI hardware device being developed with former Apple design chief [[jony-ive|Jony Ive]].[1] People familiar with the matter said the chip venture had not yet been formed and the investor talks remained in early stages.[1]
