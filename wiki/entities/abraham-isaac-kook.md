# Abraham Isaac Kook

Rabbi Abraham Isaac Cohen Kook is described in the article as a "Greater Israel champion, End Times cultist, Chief Ashkenaz Rabbi for British Mandate Palestine (1919–1935)."[1]

## Quoted doctrine

The article quotes Kook: "The Truth Concerning the Land is Revealed in Cabala. Jewish Mysticism (Cabala) militates for life in the Land of Israel. Rationalistic approaches to Judaism place no special value on the Land of Israel. In wars, national characters crystalize. Israel, as the universal reflection of mankind, benefits thereby. The heels of Messiah follow upon World Conflagration… At the hour of the downfall of Western civilization, Israel is called upon to fulfill its divine mission by providing the spiritual basis for a New World Order."[1]

## Assessment

The article groups Kook with [[theodor-herzl]] and "the army of gnostic Christian Zionist heirs of John Nelson Darby begging for a first strike onto Iran," arguing that this "genie of Greater Israelism" represents a zealotry that may spell disaster for much of humanity — with the distinction that, unlike most End Times cults, this one possesses a nuclear arsenal and is supported by rapture-believing [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionists]] in America.[1] Kook is also listed alongside Herzl and [[zeev-jabotinsky|Vladimir Jabotinsky]] as figures who likely believed Jewish cabalistic bankers were creating a [[greater-israel]] capital for a New World Order.[1]
