# Mark Green

Mark Green is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Tennessee who served as chairman of the House Homeland Security Committee.[1] In January 2024 Green led the effort to impeach Homeland Security Secretary [[alejandro-mayorkas|Alejandro Mayorkas]], announcing the release of two articles of impeachment with the statement: "These articles lay out a clear, compelling, and irrefutable case for Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas' impeachment."[1]

Green alleged that Mayorkas "has willfully and systemically refused to comply with immigration laws enacted by Congress," and that he "has breached the public trust by knowingly making false statements to Congress and the American people, and obstructing congressional oversight of his department."[1] The [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] rejected the effort, maintaining that no high crimes or misdemeanors had been committed under the Biden administration and dismissing the House investigation in a memo as unconstitutional and "evidence-free."[1]

## Resignation from the House (July 2025)

Green resigned from the House of Representatives in July 2025 amid a tight margin between the parties in the lower chamber.[2] "It's with a heavy heart that I say farewell. To my constituents across Tennessee's 7th District—thank you. The trust you put in me is humbling. I will look back fondly on my years of serving as your voice in Washington," he wrote in a post on X earlier that month, having entered the House in 2019.[2] His departure left 219 Republicans and 212 Democrats in the chamber — a seven-seat difference amid high inter-party tensions and heating 2026 midterm races in which Democrats sought to retake the House.[2]
