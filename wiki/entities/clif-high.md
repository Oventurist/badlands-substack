# Clif High

**Clif High** is an internet commentator and self-described predictive-linguistics researcher, known for "web bot" data-mining forecasts and for a large following on X (formerly Twitter), where he posts as @clif_high. He is a recurring voice in the alternative-media ecosystem in which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] operates.

He is quoted at the head of the Badlands essay [[a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine|"A Hypnotist's Take on Israel, Palestine, and Q"]] with a sardonic post about the pressure to take sides in the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]]: "Listen up, butthead! If you don't choose a side, you won't be able to have your emotions torqued & be manipulated!" The quotation, dated to an October 2023 post, sets up the essay's thesis that demands for immediate partisanship are themselves the delivery mechanism for [[operation-mockingbird|Mockingbird]] propaganda, and that emotional neutrality is the defense against hypnotic suggestion.
