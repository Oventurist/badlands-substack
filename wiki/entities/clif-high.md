# Clif High

**Clif High** is an internet commentator active on the platform X (formerly Twitter) whose remarks are cited in alternative-media commentary on wartime propaganda.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)

## Cited remark

An October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay by [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] opens with a satirical quotation attributed to High: "Listen up, butthead! If you don't choose a side, you won't be able to have your emotions torqued & be manipulated!"1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine) The line is used as an epigraph for the essay's argument that the demand to pick a side in the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel–Palestine conflict]] is itself the mechanism by which audiences are rendered emotionally manipulable.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine)
