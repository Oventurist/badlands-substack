# Clif High

Clif High is an online commentator known for predictive-linguistics work and for widely circulated social media commentary within alternative-media circles.[1]

## Cited commentary

An October 2023 post by High is used as the epigraph of [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s essay on the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel-Palestine conflict]]: "Listen up, butthead! If you don't choose a side, you won't be able to have your emotions torqued & be manipulated!"[1] The line is quoted satirically, as a compressed statement of the essay's thesis that the demand to pick a side is itself the mechanism by which audiences are made available to [[mockingbird-media|Mockingbird]] manipulation.[1]
