# Sports Talk (Badlands Media)

Sports Talk is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Mondays and Fridays at 12pm ET in the January 2023 schedule, hosted by [[j-b-white|JB White]] and [[absolute-1776|Absolute 1776]].[1] The hosts take what the network calls an unorthodox approach to sports coverage, cutting out politics from the coverage and focusing on what they describe as regaining America's pastime.[1] A December 2022 schedule post (week of December 5–11) carried Sports Talk in the same Monday-and-Friday 12pm ET slot, within the network's expanded slate for the truth community, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with replays on the Badlands Media Network.[2]
