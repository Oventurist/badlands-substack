# State Election Board

The State Election Board is Georgia's state-level body responsible for overseeing the administration of elections and the conduct of county election offices.[1]

## Fulton County subpoena and the 2020 anomalies

Georgia's board of elections described "unexplained anomalies in vote tabulation and storage related to the 2020 election" in [[fulton-county|Fulton County]], and the State Election Board issued a subpoena to the [[fulton-county-board-of-registration-and-elections|Fulton County Board of Registration and Elections]] seeking related records.[1] After the county did not comply, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] sent a letter on October 30, 2025 demanding that the county present for inspection all records responsive to that subpoena.[1]

The DOJ request, signed by Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]] on behalf of Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], relied on Title III of the [[civil-rights-act-of-1960|Civil Rights Act of 1960]], which empowers the Attorney General to demand preserved election records for inspection and copying.[1]
