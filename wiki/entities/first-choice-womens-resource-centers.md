# First Choice Women's Resource Centers

**First Choice Women's Resource Centers** is a [[new-jersey|New Jersey]] network of [[crisis-pregnancy-centers|crisis pregnancy centers]] that became the plaintiff in a 2026 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] case over state investigative subpoenas.[1]

## Litigation

New Jersey Attorney General [[matthew-platkin|Matthew Platkin]] issued a subpoena seeking the organization's donor and operational records.[1] First Choice argued the demand could chill speech and association rights protected under the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] and sued in federal court.[1]

The Supreme Court ruled that the center may proceed with its federal lawsuit challenging the state investigation, siding with its claim that constitutional rights were implicated.[1] The Court did not reach the merits of the subpoena itself but held the center can pursue its claims in federal court rather than being confined to state proceedings.[1] The Court wrote that "an official demand for private donor information is enough to discourage reasonable individuals from associating with a group," and that First Choice had established an injury to its First Amendment associational rights.[1]

Executive director [[aimee-huber|Aimee Huber]] said that because centers like First Choice do not perform or refer for abortion, they are targets for a government that disagrees with their views, adding that if the attorney general could bully them, the same could happen in other states.[1]
