# Susan Illston

Susan Illston is a United States District Judge based in California, appointed by President Bill Clinton, who on October 15, 2025 blocked the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] from laying off thousands of federal workers during the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|October 2025 government shutdown]].[1]

## Ruling on shutdown-era layoffs

Illston ruled from the bench following an afternoon hearing, finding that the administration was not following the legal requirements governing reductions in force (RIFs) and that officials had exceeded their authorities. She concluded that the layoffs appeared to be politically motivated and unlawfully targeted at Democrats, citing public comments about the shutdown made by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], and Office of Management and Budget Director [[russ-vought|Russ Vought]].[1]

In her bench ruling, Illston said the administration appeared to have "taken advantage of the lapse in government spending and government functioning to assume that all bets are off, that the laws don't apply to them any more and that they can impose the structures that they like."[1]
