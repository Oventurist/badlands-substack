# Mises Institute

The Mises Institute is an American think tank in the Austrian School tradition whose monetary analysis is frequently cited by independent media, including [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Money-supply reporting, 2023

In an article republished in part by the Badlands News Brief of April 25, 2023 under the headline "Money-Supply Growth Fell To A 50-Year Low In Feb. Will The Fed Panic?", the institute reported that money supply growth had fallen again in February 2023, moving further into negative territory after turning negative in November 2022 for the first time in twenty-eight years.[1] It noted that growth had slowed quickly since April 2021 from the unprecedented highs of the preceding two years, and that the previous episode of negative year-over-year change had begun in November 1994 and lasted fifteen months before turning positive again in January 1996.[1]

The institute reported that year-over-year money supply growth in February 2023 stood at –6.6 percent, down from –5.0 percent in January and from +6.9 percent in February 2022, and observed that at no point in at least sixty years had the money supply fallen by more than 5.6 percent in any month.[1] It linked the monetary slowdown to a considerably weakened economy, citing falling home prices, soaring credit card debt, rising consumer loan delinquencies, falling job openings and a deteriorating manufacturing outlook.[1]

## Reception at Badlands

Badlands writer [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the institute's data as the basis for an extended commentary describing the contraction as deliberate "monetary warfare" by oligarchic interests, arguing that credit expansion and contraction by the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] steer which industries live and die, and advocating a [[parallel-economy|parallel economy]] as the remedy.[1]
