# Pras Michel

Pras Michel is a musician known as a member of the Fugees and a figure in a federal conspiracy case tied to [[haiti|Haiti]], China, and the United States.[1]

## Conviction

Michel was found guilty on ten criminal counts in 2023 in a conspiracy case that linked Haiti, China, and the United States together through an illegal foreign lobbying scheme.[1] He has also been accused of outright stealing charitable funds intended for his native homeland.[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited his case alongside broader allegations that U.S. aid to Haiti was laundered through NGOs such as the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], noting that Haiti's collapse followed less than a year after his verdict.[1]

## Sentencing (November 2025)

In November 2025 the Grammy-winning rapper Prakazrel "Pras" Michel, a founding member of the 1990s hit trio the Fugees, was sentenced to 14 years in prison for his involvement in a billion-dollar Malaysia scam that funneled money into US politics, including illegal donations to the Obama campaign.[2] The 52-year-old was convicted of money laundering and campaign finance violations in 2023 in a global foreign influence scandal led by the Malaysian financier [[jho-low|Low Taek Jho]].[2] In April 2023 a federal jury convicted Michel of ten counts, including conspiracy and acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government; the Washington, D.C. trial included testimony from actor Leonardo DiCaprio and former attorney general Jeff Sessions.[2] [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] prosecutors said federal sentencing guidelines recommended a life sentence for Michel, who they said "betrayed his country for money" and "lied unapologetically and unrelentingly to carry out his schemes," arguing that his sentence "should reflect the breadth and depth of his crimes, his indifference to the risks to his country, and the magnitude of his greed."[2]

## April 2023 conviction and the 1MDB influence campaign

The [[us-department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced on 26 April 2023 that a federal jury had convicted Michel, then 50 and of Coconut Creek, Florida, for orchestrating an unregistered, back-channel campaign beginning in or about 2017 to influence the then-administration of the President of the United States and the Justice Department to drop the investigation of [[jho-low|Jho Low]] and others for embezzlement and other offences connected with 1Malaysia Development Berhad, and to send a Chinese national back to China.[3] The verdict also covered conspiring to make and conceal foreign and conduit campaign contributions during the 2012 U.S. presidential election.[3]

Prosecutors said Michel conspired with Low, [[elliott-broidy|Elliott Broidy]], Nickie Lum Davis, George Higginbotham and others to conduct undisclosed lobbying at the direction of Low and of the Vice Minister of Public Security for the People's Republic of China.[3] Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] wrote that Michel facilitated the illegal transfer of tens of millions of dollars from foreign donors into the Obama 2012 campaign and took part in an influence operation against the Trump administration and the DOJ to have the dissident [[miles-guo|Miles Guo]] extradited to China, and that he was convicted on ten counts including conspiracy, concealment, false records, witness tampering and [[foreign-agents-registration-act|FARA]] violations.[3] On the same day as the verdict, Swiss authorities indicted two executives of [[petrosaudi|PetroSaudi]] as further conspirators in the [[1mdb-scandal|1MDB scandal]].[3]
