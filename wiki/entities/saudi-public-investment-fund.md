# Saudi Public Investment Fund

The Public Investment Fund (PIF) is Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund and the principal vehicle for the kingdom's economic diversification program.[1]

## 2026–2030 strategy

Sources told Reuters in February 2026 that the PIF was expected to unveil a new 2026–2030 strategy that week.[1] The plan was set to focus on industry, minerals, artificial intelligence, and tourism, while scaling back some expensive mega-projects — including the linear city development known as "The Line" — as part of a broader adjustment to the kingdom's investment priorities.[1]

### Backing of the 2025 Miami FII summit
The Public Investment Fund backed the Future Investment Initiative Institute Priority Summit Miami 2025, held at the Faena Forum on Miami Beach and opened on February 19, 2025 by President Donald Trump.[2] Bloomberg estimated at the time that the fund held roughly $925 billion in assets under management, and Trump used the occasion to call on Saudi Arabia to invest $1 trillion in the United States.[2] The fund created the FII in October 2018, branding the event "Davos in the Desert" — a positioning read by Badlands commentators as the Sovereign Alliance's answer to the World Economic Forum.[2]
