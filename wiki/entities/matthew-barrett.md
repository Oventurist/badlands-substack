# Matthew Barrett

**Matthew Barrett** is the Colorado trial judge who sentenced former Mesa County Clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]] to incarceration.[1]

## Sentencing of Tina Peters

In sentencing Peters, Barrett declared: "Prison is for those folks where we send people who are a danger to all of us, whether it be by the pen or the sword or the word of the mouth."[1] Peters' federal habeas petition cites this and other inflammatory statements by the trial court about her incarceration as evidence that her detention is unconstitutional.[1] The question before U.S. Magistrate Judge [[scott-varholak|Scott Varholak]] is whether Barrett locking Peters up over what she might say violates her [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] rights.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described Barrett's sentencing monologue as "egregious," arguing that Peters has a strong appeal for bias and over-sentencing in the Colorado courts.[1] She noted that two of the three items in Barrett's list — the pen and the word of the mouth — are forms of speech, and contrasted the treatment of Peters with a court system she said releases pedophiles and rapists under the banner of criminal justice reform.[1]

## Sentencing of Tina Peters as described in March 2025

In a March 2025 summary of the case posted on X, Article III Project founder [[mike-davis|Mike Davis]] described Barrett as a Democrat judge, poorly rated by his community, who was "dumb enough to state on the record" that he sentenced the 69-year-old former Mesa County clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]] to nine years in prison because of her First Amendment-protected views on election integrity.[2] The remark was cited in the context of the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s announcement that it would review Peters's conviction.[2]
