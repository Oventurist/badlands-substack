# CNBC

CNBC is the business news cable channel owned by [[nbcuniversal|NBCUniversal]].[1]

In November 2024, [[comcast|Comcast]] announced it would proceed with a spinoff of its NBCUniversal cable networks, placing CNBC alongside [[msnbc|MSNBC]], USA, Oxygen, E!, Syfy and the Golf Channel in the divested portfolio. The assets collectively generated about $7 billion in revenue in the twelve months ended September 30, while Comcast retained Bravo, Peacock and the NBC broadcast network.[1]
