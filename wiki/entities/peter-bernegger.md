# Peter Bernegger

Peter Bernegger is an election-integrity researcher and fraud investigator whose statutory analyses of online political fundraising are cited in the Badlands corpus.

In the June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief, the writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] credits Bernegger with compiling the list of federal statutes she argues may have been violated by [[actblue|ActBlue]] and comparable operations. The list includes money laundering under 18 U.S.C. § 1956 and § 1957, false statements to federal agencies under § 1001, contributions in the name of another under 52 U.S.C. § 30122, wire and mail fraud under §§ 1343 and 1341, conspiracy to defraud the United States under § 371, identity and document fraud under § 1028, the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act at § 1030, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act at 15 U.S.C. §§ 6801–6809, and the Bank Secrecy Act at 31 U.S.C. § 5311 et seq. with its implementing regulations at 12 CFR 21.11 and 21.21. Ashe in America adds that state analogues of these offences could apply, "ostensibly for each transaction."
