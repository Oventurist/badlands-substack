# Cole Thomas Allen

Cole Thomas Allen is the man identified by the Associated Press as the suspect in the April 25, 2026 shooting at the White House Correspondents' Association Dinner at the Washington Hilton, an incident that prompted the United States Secret Service to evacuate President Donald Trump.[1]

## The April 25 incident

According to Reuters reporting summarized in the Badlands Brief, Allen was armed with a shotgun, a handgun and knives when he entered the event, fired at a Secret Service agent, and was subdued and arrested on scene.[1] CBS News, citing two law enforcement sources, reported that a Secret Service agent was struck by a round while wearing a bulletproof vest and was expected to recover.[1] Trump himself was unharmed.[1]

Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] said the suspect appeared to be targeting Trump administration officials, potentially including the president.[1] CBS News reported that officials said Allen left writings stating he planned to target administration officials "prioritized from highest-ranking to lowest."[1]

## Badlands commentary

The Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry described the evening as "bizarre" and mocked the apparent lack of planning behind the infiltration attempt, likening it to a "full blown Leeroy Jenkins" charge rather than a professionally executed operation.[1] He noted that Allen somehow ended up shirtless, as shown in a photograph posted by Trump on social media.[1]

The commentary's central claim is a pattern rather than a single fact: Badlands notes that ABC ran a story featuring Cole Thomas Allen back in 2017, when he was a student, and argues that a strange regularity has emerged in which the media and the intelligence community appear to have prior awareness of Trump's would-be assassins before they act.[1] The Brief characterizes the implications of that pattern as "dark and disturbing."[1]

## Aftermath

Trump wrote on Truth Social that the Secret Service and law enforcement had done a "fantastic job," and said the dinner would be rescheduled within 30 days.[1] Trump subsequently gave what Badlands called a strange answer for why he was not immediately whisked off stage by his protective detail, and used a 60 Minutes appearance with Norah O'Donnell the following night to attack the mainstream press.[1]
