# Bronfman Family

The Bronfman family is a Canadian dynasty whose fortune derives from the liquor trade.[1]

Like the [[kennedy-family|Kennedys]], the Bronfmans profited from the multibillion-dollar illegal alcohol and drug trade of [[prohibition|Prohibition]] and were financially positioned to make a smooth transition into the legalized liquor trade of the early 1930s.[1] Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] married into the Bronfman family fortune.[1]
