# John Cornyn

John Cornyn is a Republican United States Senator from Texas who sought renomination in the 2026 election cycle.[1]

## 2026 primary and runoff

In the [[2026-texas-primary-elections|March 2026 Texas primaries]], Cornyn failed to win outright renomination and was pushed into a Republican runoff against Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]], scheduled for May 26.[1] The eventual Republican nominee was set to face state Rep. [[james-talarico|James Talarico]], who won the Democratic primary by defeating Rep. [[jasmine-crockett|Jasmine Crockett]].[1]

Badlands commentary placed Cornyn's failure to clear the primary within a broader pattern of establishment incumbents in both parties being rejected by Texas voters in 2026.[1]

## Defeat in the 2026 primary runoff

Cornyn, a four-term senator, longtime Senate insider and former Majority Whip, lost the Republican primary runoff for his Texas seat to Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]], taking roughly 37% of the vote to Paxton's 63%.[2] The [[2026-texas-senate-primary-runoff|runoff]] followed a competitive March primary in which neither candidate won a majority, and the race featured heavy spending on attack ads in which Paxton cast Cornyn as out of touch with Texas voters.[2] [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s endorsement of Paxton days before early voting was described as decisive.[2]

## Resistance to the 2026 agenda pace

In June 2026, Cornyn was named among the Senate Republicans in tension with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the pace and strategy of advancing the administration's 2026 agenda.[3] With [[thom-tillis|Thom Tillis]] and [[bill-cassidy|Bill Cassidy]], he pushed back on the expectation that senators approve proposals without changes or extended debate, pointing to Senate procedures and narrow majorities as constraints while Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] emphasized working through the legislative process.[3] Despite the friction, the senators cited were described as generally supportive of many of Trump's policy goals.[3]

## March 2026: SAVE America Act over the filibuster

In March 2026, Cornyn published an op-ed asserting that the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]] was more important than preserving Senate procedural norms, arguing that election integrity should take precedence over maintaining the legislative filibuster.[4] The op-ed came as Senate Republicans debated whether to prioritize the bill, which [[donald-trump|President Trump]] had named a top priority ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]], and while Cornyn faced a tough primary challenge from Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]].[4]

## 2024 Senate GOP leadership race

Cornyn was one of two announced candidates — with [[john-thune|John Thune]] of South Dakota — in the race to succeed [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] as Senate Republican leader, both described by ZeroHedge as "quintessential establishment types."[5] When Kentucky Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] teased a rival bid, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] backed Paul and dared Cornyn and Thune to submit their names for consideration by X users; Paul's own poll gave him a 96 percent share with Cornyn and Thune each below 2 percent.[5]

## 2025 Texas primary threat

By April 2025 Cornyn's Senate seat had become the focus of an emerging Texas Republican primary that party figures feared would become a hugely expensive intraparty feud with ramifications across the following year's midterm map.[6] Behind the scenes, Senate GOP leaders personally asked President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to endorse Cornyn, who had held the seat for more than two decades and had narrowly lost his bid to become Senate majority leader the previous fall.[6] Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] was signaling loudly that he would enter the race, while Representative [[wesley-hunt|Wesley Hunt]] also began making moves toward a bid.[6] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] questioned why anyone would want "more of John Cornyn."[6]
