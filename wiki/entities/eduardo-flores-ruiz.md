# Eduardo Flores-Ruiz

Eduardo Flores-Ruiz is the immigration defendant at the center of the April 2025 Milwaukee courthouse incident that led to the federal obstruction prosecution of Milwaukee County Circuit Judge [[hannah-dugan|Hannah Dugan]].[1]

Prosecutors said that while federal immigration agents waited to arrest Flores-Ruiz at the Milwaukee County courthouse, Dugan directed him through a private jury door in an apparent effort to help him avoid the agents.[1] Federal agents nevertheless apprehended Flores-Ruiz outside the courthouse.[1] The episode became the factual basis for Dugan's felony obstruction conviction, and for the wider debate over judicial interference with federal immigration enforcement.[1]

## Underlying charge and Dugan's suspension

Flores-Ruiz, who is from Mexico, had been charged with battery for allegedly punching someone thirty times in the face after that person complained about his loud music.[2] An [[fbi|FBI]] affidavit filed in court stated that on April 18, 2025 Judge Dugan escorted him and his lawyer out of the courtroom through the jury door as a way to help avert his arrest by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]].[2] Dugan was taken into custody by the FBI on Friday, April 25 and was suspended by the [[wisconsin-supreme-court|Wisconsin Supreme Court]] on Tuesday, April 29, 2025.[2]
