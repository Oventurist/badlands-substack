# Eric Ciaramella

Eric Ciaramella is the individual identified in reporting as the Ukraine whistleblower whose complaint triggered the first impeachment of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## The whistleblower complaint

Reporting cited by Badlands states that 2019 claims by "Witness 2," later identified as [[gavin-wilde|Gavin Wilde]], were critical in helping Intelligence Community Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]] push Ciaramella's complaint forward.[1] Wilde's "Russiagate"-linked biases were concealed from House investigators during the impeachment process.[1]

## Criminal referral (April 2026)

In April 2026 the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] referred the whistleblower behind the 2019 impeachment complaint, along with former Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]], to the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] for potential criminal violations tied to 2019 briefings before the [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence]].[2] Badlands contributor Ashe in America framed the disclosure as evidence that a broad RICO case is progressing, noting that the public naming of Ciaramella was immediately followed by criminal referrals.[2] The commentary argued that [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]]'s 2019 insistence that the whistleblower's name could not be spoken was a purposeful lie that helped maintain what it called a Ukrainian money and secrets apparatus while undermining the elected president's agenda.[2]
