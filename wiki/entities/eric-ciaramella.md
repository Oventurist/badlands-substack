# Eric Ciaramella

Eric Ciaramella is the intelligence officer widely identified in conservative and independent media as the "Ukraine whistleblower" whose complaint precipitated the [[first-trump-impeachment|first impeachment]] of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2019.

## The whistleblower complaint

As summarized in the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, Ciaramella's complaint was advanced by Intelligence Community Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]], and reporting cited by the Brief holds that 2019 claims by the official then designated "Witness 2" — later identified as [[gavin-wilde|Gavin Wilde]] — were critical in helping Atkinson push the complaint forward. The same reporting states that Wilde's [[russiagate|Russiagate]]-linked biases were concealed from House investigators during the impeachment process, a fact that critics argue undermines the integrity of the complaint's handling.
