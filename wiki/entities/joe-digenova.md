# Joe diGenova

Joe diGenova is an American attorney and longtime ally of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. According to the June 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2c0|Badlands Brief]], he serves as counselor to acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] and leads a civil rights unit in Miami.

In that capacity diGenova supervises the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s "grand conspiracy" inquiry, run out of the US Attorney's Office for the Southern District of Florida under [[jason-reding-quinones|Jason Reding Quinones]], which is examining whether past investigations and prosecutions involving Trump amounted to criminal conspiracy. The review covers the Russian Collusion investigation tied to the 2016 campaign, cases brought by former Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], and the [[fbi|FBI]]'s 2022 search of [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]]. The Brief reports the hiring of [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]] as a senior attorney within that structure.
