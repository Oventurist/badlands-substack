# Joe diGenova

Joe diGenova is an American attorney and ally of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] serving as counselor to acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] and leading a civil rights unit in Miami.[1]

## Role in the grand conspiracy probe

diGenova supervises the [[grand-conspiracy-probe|grand conspiracy inquiry]] being developed out of the US Attorney's Office for the Southern District of Florida, which examines whether prior federal investigations and prosecutions of Trump amounted to a criminal conspiracy.[1] The probe reviews the [[russiagate-hoax|Russian Collusion investigation]] tied to Trump's 2016 campaign, cases brought by former Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], and the [[fbi|FBI]]'s 2022 search of Mar-a-Lago for classified documents.[1] The June 2026 hiring of attorney [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]] as a senior attorney in that office placed Olsen within this supervisory structure, reporting to US Attorney [[jason-reding-quinones|Jason Reding Quinones]].[1]

## Oversight of the Brennan probe

By May 2026 diGenova was overseeing the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] investigation into former [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]]'s role in the [[2017-intelligence-assessment-on-russian-interference|2017 intelligence assessment]] on Russian interference in the 2016 election, having taken over after the previous lead prosecutor was removed the prior month.[2] The matter had been referred to the Department by Republican Representative [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] in October, and under diGenova's supervision the [[fbi|FBI]]'s Miami field office questioned about a dozen CIA employees who worked on the assessment.[2]
