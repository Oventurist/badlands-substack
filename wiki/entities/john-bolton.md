# John Bolton

John Bolton is an American diplomat and national security official long associated with the hawkish wing of United States foreign policy.

## In the corpus

[[assad-day-in-damascus]] includes Bolton in its list of figures who populated the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]] after its founding in 1997 by [[william-kristol]] and [[robert-kagan]], alongside [[dick-cheney]], [[paul-wolfowitz]] and [[donald-rumsfeld]]. The article observes that fourteen of PNAC's twenty-five original signatories were appointed to positions in the George W. Bush administration.

Bolton's inclusion serves the article's thesis that the neoconservative faction of what [[mike-benz]] terms "the Blob" had targeted [[syria]] and its neighbours for regime change since the 1990s, in pursuit of the [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] described in [[rebuilding-americas-defenses|Rebuilding America's Defenses]].
