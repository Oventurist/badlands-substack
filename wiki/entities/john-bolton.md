# John Bolton

John Bolton is an American diplomat and national security official long associated with the hawkish wing of United States foreign policy.

## In the corpus

assad day in damascus includes Bolton in its list of figures who populated the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]] after its founding in 1997 by [[william-kristol]] and [[robert-kagan]], alongside [[dick-cheney]], [[paul-wolfowitz]] and [[donald-rumsfeld]]. The article observes that fourteen of PNAC's twenty-five original signatories were appointed to positions in the George W. Bush administration.

Bolton's inclusion serves the article's thesis that the neoconservative faction of what [[mike-benz]] terms "the Blob" had targeted [[syria]] and its neighbours for regime change since the 1990s, in pursuit of the [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] described in [[rebuilding-americas-defenses|Rebuilding America's Defenses]].

## The classified records plea deal

The June 5, 2026 Badlands Brief, citing CNN, reports that Bolton is expected to plead guilty later that month to a single count of illegally retaining sensitive national security documents. Under the anticipated agreement he would pay a fine exceeding $2 million; the charge carries a potential sentence ranging from no incarceration to five years, with a plea hearing scheduled for June 26. Federal prosecutors charged Bolton earlier in 2026, alleging he kept diary entries and other records from his time in the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration at his residence. The original indictment comprised eight counts related to transmitting national defense information and ten counts tied to retaining such material. Investigators further alleged that Bolton shared more than 1,000 pages detailing his daily activities through a personal email account with two unauthorized individuals — allegations not included in the single count to which he is expected to plead.

## Career chronology and the treason charge

Responding to the plea news, Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] asked, "In which job(s) did Bolton help plan coup d'état?" and set out his career in full: general counsel and then assistant administrator at [[usaid|USAID]] (1981–1983); partner at Covington & Burling (1983–1985); Assistant Attorney General for the Office of Legislative Affairs at the Department of Justice (1985–1989); Assistant Secretary for International Organization Affairs at the [[state-department|State Department]] (1989–1993); partner at Lerner, Reed, Bolton & McManus (1993–1999); vice president of the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]] (1997–2001); Under Secretary of State for Arms Control and International Security Affairs (2001–2005); U.S. Ambassador to the [[united-nations|United Nations]] (2005–2006); of counsel at Kirkland & Ellis and foreign policy adviser to [[mitt-romney|Mitt Romney]] in 2012 (2007–2018); and National Security Advisor to the President (2018–2019).

She asks whether the prosecution is "really about what this guy put in his book, or will there be accountability for Bolton's portfolio of work that sold out the American people so the rest of the world could rise" — concluding that the latter would be "literal treason."
