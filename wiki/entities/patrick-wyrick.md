# Patrick Wyrick

Patrick Wyrick is a judge of the United States District Court in Oklahoma who in February 2023 issued a ruling striking down, as applied, the federal prohibition on marijuana users owning or possessing firearms.[1]

## Harrison ruling

In a 54-page ruling handed down on Friday, February 3, 2023 in favor of the defendant [[jared-harrison|Jared Harrison]], Wyrick held that the government could not claim that Harrison's "mere status as a user of marijuana justifies stripping him of his fundamental right to possess a firearm."[1] The decision was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] as "another defeat for the gun-grabbers" and was described as the latest of many rulings against gun restrictions issued in the widening wake of the June 2022 Supreme Court decision in [[new-york-state-rifle-and-pistol-association-v-bruen|New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated the ruling as a step in the right direction while arguing that the Constitution does not create or grant rights but merely enumerates pre-existing inalienable rights.[1] He argued that no government may deprive a citizen of the right to purchase arms because they consume a substance unless that substance materially renders the consumer non compos mentis.[1]
