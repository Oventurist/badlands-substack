# Prairieland Detention Center

The Prairieland Detention Center is an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] facility in Alvarado, Texas, south of Fort Worth.

The facility became the site of a July 4, 2025 attack that produced the first federal prosecution of a self-identified [[antifa|Antifa]] cell. As summarized in the March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief, authorities said the incident involved fireworks, vandalism and gunfire directed at responding law enforcement; Alvarado Police Lieutenant Thomas Gross was shot and injured. A federal jury in Fort Worth subsequently convicted nine defendants, including [[benjamin-song|Benjamin Song]] on attempted murder and firearms charges, on counts ranging from rioting and use of explosives to providing material support to terrorists and conspiring to conceal documents. [[homeland-security-investigations|HSI]] Dallas Special Agent in Charge Travis Pickard described the case as a historic first for federal anti-Antifa enforcement.
