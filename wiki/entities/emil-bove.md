# Emil Bove

Emil Bove is an attorney who served on the criminal defense team of President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

On January 6, 2025, Bove and co-counsel [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] filed a motion urging Manhattan Supreme Court Justice [[juan-merchan|Juan Merchan]] to pause that week's sentencing in the [[trump-hush-money-case|"hush money" case]], arguing that their planned appeal to the Appellate Division of the state's trial court triggered what is known as an automatic stay of the proceedings. Merchan rejected the bid for a pause later the same day.[1]

The sentencing had been set for January 10, ten days before Inauguration Day, after Merchan rejected Trump's earlier claim that the verdict should be nullified because of presidential immunity. The underlying conviction covered 34 felonies for concealing a payoff that hid a sex scandal before the 2016 presidential election.[1]

## Order to drop the Eric Adams prosecution (February 2025)

As acting U.S. deputy attorney general, Bove issued a memo on Monday, February 10, 2025 ordering federal prosecutors in New York to drop the corruption case against New York City Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]], arguing in part that the prosecution hampered Adams's ability to tackle "illegal immigration and violent crime."[3] The directive triggered the resignation on Thursday, February 13 of [[danielle-sassoon|Danielle R. Sassoon]], the acting U.S. attorney for the Southern District of New York, along with two senior federal prosecutors in Washington, in what NBC News called a stunning public rebuke of the new [[justice-department|Justice Department]] leadership.[3] Sassoon had appealed the day before to Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] to express her alarm at the order.[3] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said publicly that he had not discussed the Adams case with the DOJ at all.[3]

## Nomination to the Third Circuit (May 2025)

On May 28, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced on Truth Social that he was nominating Bove to serve as a judge on the United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit.[2] Trump described him as a distinguished graduate of Georgetown Law who had served as an Assistant U.S. Attorney in the Southern District of New York for nearly a decade, where he was Co-Chief of the Terrorism and International Narcotics Unit.[2] Calling Bove "SMART, TOUGH, and respected by everyone," Trump said the nominee would "end the Weaponization of Justice" and "restore the Rule of Law."[2] Badlands contributor Chris Paul noted that Bove had been one of the people leading the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] before an attorney general was confirmed.[2]

## Memo directing dismissal of the Eric Adams case (February 2025)

As acting Deputy Attorney General, Bove directed federal prosecutors in February 2025 to dismiss the corruption charges against New York Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]].[4] In a two-page memo obtained by The Associated Press, Bove — himself a former prosecutor in the Manhattan office that brought the case — stated that the decision was made without evaluating the strength of the prosecution and was not intended to undermine the attorneys who filed it.[4] He wrote that the timing of the charges and "more recent actions" by former U.S. Attorney [[damian-williams|Damian Williams]] had compromised the integrity of the proceedings, citing prejudicial pretrial publicity that could influence potential witnesses and the jury pool.[4] Adams had been accused of accepting bribes in the form of free or discounted travel and of receiving illegal campaign contributions.[4]
