# Mark Brnovich

Mark Brnovich is a Republican politician who served as Attorney General of Arizona from 2015 through the disputed [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]] period.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) He sought the Republican nomination for the United States Senate in 2022 but finished third in the August primary.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Public profile

Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] characterized Brnovich's social media presence as one of "two comedy shows playing on Twitter" during the post-election crisis, faulting him for joking publicly "while Arizona is burning down all around him."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She cited a May 13, 2019 post in which he displayed nunchaku from storage, and an October 15, 2021 video posted after the Arizona audit of the 2020 election — "You want the nunchucks. You got the nunchucks." — which she read as an empty promise of action on election fraud.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) In her assessment, Brnovich "did NOTHING to fight election fraud after 2020," and was either compromised or "playing a specific role in this movie."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She noted he had spent considerable time with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before the Florence, Arizona rally the previous year.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Acting governor question

In November 2022 Brnovich was described as "acting governor," a designation Gillespie said no outlet had explained.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She noted that Article V, Section 6 of the Arizona Constitution places the Secretary of State first in the line of succession and the Attorney General second, and speculated that Governor Doug Ducey's travel out of state created a "temporary disability" — while asking why the role fell to Brnovich rather than Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], who was simultaneously positioned to certify her own election as governor.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Election Integrity Unit

Despite Gillespie's criticism of Brnovich personally, his office's Election Integrity Unit produced the November 19, 2022 demand letter to [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] written by Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]], which she called "a hint of governmental sanity."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
