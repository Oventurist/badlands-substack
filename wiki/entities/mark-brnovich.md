# Mark Brnovich

Mark Brnovich is an American attorney and Republican politician who served as Attorney General of Arizona from 2015 until January 2023. In the Badlands Media corpus he is a figure of derision: [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]]'s article [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]] characterizes his social media presence as one of "two new comedy shows playing on Twitter" and accuses him of having done "NOTHING to fight election fraud after 2020."

## Career and 2022 Senate bid

Brnovich became Arizona's attorney general in 2015. He sought the Republican nomination for the United States Senate in 2022 but placed third in the August primary. Gillespie attributes the loss in part to what she calls his "poor judgment about what Arizonans want to know about their Attorney General," citing a May 13, 2019 post in which he displayed his nunchaku and boasted of "my years of martial arts training," and an October 15, 2021 video posted after the Arizona audit of the 2020 election with the caption, "You want the nunchucks. You got the nunchucks." The article's verdict: "Oh, we got the nunchucks, all right."

## The "acting governor" episode

In November 2022 Brnovich was described as "acting governor" while Governor [[doug-ducey|Doug Ducey]] was apparently out of state. Gillespie observes that under Article V, Section 6 of the Constitution of Arizona — governing death, resignation, removal, disability, and temporary absence of the governor — succession runs first to the [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Secretary of State]] and only afterward to the Attorney General. She asks why the office passed to Brnovich rather than Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], speculating sardonically that Hobbs may have been "TOO BUSY trying to certify her OWN ELECTION," and complains that no journalist in the state posed the question.

## Assessment in the corpus

Gillespie speculates that Brnovich, as a Republican, is "either compromised or playing a specific role in this movie," noting that he spent considerable time with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before the Florence, Arizona rally of 2021. Despite her criticism, she credits his office's Election Integrity Unit — specifically Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]], whom she calls "my hero right now" — for its November 19, 2022 letter demanding answers from [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], writing, "Let's not hold working for Brnovich against Ms. Wright."
