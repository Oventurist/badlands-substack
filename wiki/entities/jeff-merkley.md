# Jeff Merkley

Jeff Merkley is a Democratic member of the United States [[senate|Senate]] from Oregon.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief identifies Merkley as one of the Democratic senators who pressed Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] during the February 10, 2026 Senate Appropriations Subcommittee hearing over his ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] as revealed in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s release of Epstein-related documents. Alongside [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]] and [[chris-coons|Chris Coons]], Merkley focused on discrepancies with Lutnick's prior claims that he had cut ties earlier and accused him of a lack of transparency.
