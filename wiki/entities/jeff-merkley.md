# Jeff Merkley

Jeff Merkley is a Democratic member of the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]].[1]

## Lutnick questioning

Merkley joined fellow Democratic senators in pressing Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] during his February 10, 2026, appearance before a Senate Appropriations Subcommittee over Lutnick's ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] revealed in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] file release, with some senators calling for his resignation.[1]

## Congressional stock trading ban (July 2024)

In July 2024 Merkley, a Democratic senator from Oregon, was one of four senators — alongside Republican [[josh-hawley|Josh Hawley]] and Democrats [[jon-ossoff|Jon Ossoff]] and [[gary-peters|Gary Peters]] — who negotiated and announced a bipartisan agreement to bar members of [[congress|Congress]] from trading stock.[2] The amendment would immediately forbid members of Congress, the president and the vice president from purchasing stocks and other covered investments and give lawmakers 90 days to divest existing holdings, and was the first such proposal to receive formal Senate committee consideration, set for July 24, 2024.[2]


## Change Corruption Act (December 2025)

In December 2025, Merkley and Sen. [[catherine-cortez-masto|Catherine Cortez Masto]] (D-Nev.) introduced the [[change-corruption-act|Change Corruption Act]], legislation barring any sitting or living former president from being featured on U.S. currency and intended to thwart the Treasury's plan for a commemorative $1 coin bearing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s image.[3] The bill was cosponsored by Sens. Ron Wyden (D-Ore.) and Richard Blumenthal (D-Conn.) and provides that "No United States currency may feature the likeness of a living or sitting President."[3]

Merkley said in a statement that "President Trump's self-celebrating maneuvers are authoritarian actions worthy of dictators like North Korea's Kim Jong Un, not the United States of America," calling for rejection of "his efforts to dismantle our 'We, The People' republic and replace it with a strongman state."[3]
