# Herb Brooks

Herb Brooks was the head coach of the 1980 United States Olympic hockey team that defeated the Soviet Union at Lake Placid in the game known as the [[miracle-on-ice|Miracle on Ice]].[1]

Badlands Media credits Brooks with a deliberate act of preparation: ten days before the Games began, he scheduled an exhibition game against the Soviets at Madison Square Garden, where Team USA was beaten 10–3.[1] The article argues Brooks "knew what he was doing" — the lopsided loss served to rid his young players of their nerves, and by the time the two sides met again in the Olympic medal round the Americans were a completely different team.[1] The resulting 4–3 win on February 22, 1980 is presented as a triumph of a group that won by working together, believing in each other, and believing in their coach.[1]
