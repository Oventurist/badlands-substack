# Mats Ljungqvist

**Mats Ljungqvist** is a Swedish public prosecutor who headed [[sweden|Sweden]]'s criminal investigation into the September 2022 sabotage of the Nord Stream gas pipelines.[1]

In April 2023 Ljungqvist announced that the investigation's "clear main scenario" was that a state-sponsored group was behind the explosions, a conclusion he based on the nature of the explosives used, which are rare and not widely available outside of governments and their militaries.[1] He stopped short of ruling out other scenarios, including the possibility of an independent group.[1]

Ljungqvist said investigators were "carrying out a number of concrete investigative precautions" and that "our hope is to be able to confirm who has committed this crime," while cautioning that confirmation would likely be difficult given the circumstances.[1] He described the crime as inherently hard to investigate because it took place roughly 80 meters (262 feet) beneath the surface of the Baltic Sea.[1] Two of the leaks caused by the explosions occurred within Sweden's economic zone, while [[denmark|Denmark]] pursued a parallel investigation.[1]

His statement was received by alternative media as corroboration of [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]]'s reporting on the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream sabotage]].[1]
