# Amy Chua

Amy Chua is a professor at [[yale-law-school|Yale Law School]] specialising in international business transactions and globalization.[1] In Badlands Media commentary she is presented as a pivotal figure in the personal history of [[jd-vance|JD Vance]]: as his mentor at Yale, she introduced him to his future wife [[usha-vance|Usha]].[1]

Chua is married to fellow Yale law professor [[jed-rubenfeld|Jed Rubenfeld]], and their daughter clerked for Supreme Court Justice [[brett-kavanaugh|Brett Kavanaugh]].[1] During Kavanaugh's contested Supreme Court confirmation — in which he was attacked by senators including [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] — Chua wrote a letter of support vouching for his character.[1] She was subsequently accused in the press of inappropriately socialising with students, which [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] characterises as a media character assassination of the type directed at figures who cross the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]

Carlson notes that Chua's academic specialty in globalization echoes the international orientation of [[sidley-austin|Sidley Austin]], the firm at which Vance later worked.[1]
