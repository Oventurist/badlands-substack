# Sam Brinton

Sam Brinton is a former United States federal official described by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in his January 24, 2023 Badlands Media essay "The World is a Vampire" as "the luggage-stealing Sam Brinton, the man [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] put in charge of the disposal of nuclear waste."[1]

Brinton appears in the essay in passing, as part of Carlson's discussion of the 2013 film *[[elysium|Elysium]]*: rewatching the film, Carlson remarked that a bald Matt Damon bears a striking resemblance to Brinton.[1] The reference is used for comic effect within a broader argument about [[predictive-programming|predictive programming]] in [[hollywood|Hollywood]] output, rather than as a substantive account of Brinton's tenure.[1]
