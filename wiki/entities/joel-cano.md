# Joel Cano

Joel Cano is a former New Mexico judge who was arrested by federal authorities in April 2025, along with his wife, on charges related to the reported harboring of an undocumented immigrant.[1] The immigrant in question was alleged to have ties to [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]], the Venezuelan prison gang designated by the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] as a principal transnational criminal threat.[1]

## Arrest and context

The Cano arrests occurred one day before the higher-profile arrest of Milwaukee Circuit Court Judge [[hannah-dugan|Hannah Dugan]], and the two cases were reported together as a pair of actions against sitting or former members of the judiciary.[1] Coverage framed the arrests as a sharp escalation in the administration's campaign to remove undocumented immigrants from the United States, and as a signal that judicial status would not insulate individuals from prosecution for interference with immigration enforcement.[1]

Border czar [[tom-homan|Tom Homan]] responded to both cases with a public warning: "Nobody should be surprised by the arrest of two judges... If you actively impede our enforcement efforts or if you knowingly harbor or conceal illegal aliens from ICE you will be prosecuted."[1]

## April 2025 arrest details

Fox News reporting identified Cano as the former Doña Ana County Magistrate Judge and stated that he and his wife [[nancy-cano|Nancy Cano]] were taken into custody on Thursday, April 24, 2025, after federal agents raided their Las Cruces home.[2] Both face charges of evidence tampering arising from allegations that they harbored [[cristhian-ortega-lopez|Cristhian Ortega-Lopez]], an alleged illegal alien believed to be a member of Venezuela's [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang.[2] The arrests followed a February incident in which Ortega-Lopez was apprehended at the Cano residence; court documents indicate he had first been hired by Nancy Cano for home repairs and was later given accommodation in the couple's guesthouse.[2]
