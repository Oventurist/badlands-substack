# Asim Munir

Asim Munir is a Pakistani Field Marshal and the country's senior military leader. He appears in the Badlands corpus as one of the two Pakistani officials who directly requested that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] extend the US–Iran ceasefire in April 2026.[1]

In a Truth Social post dated April 21, 2026, Trump stated that Munir and Prime Minister [[shehbaz-sharif|Shehbaz Sharif]] had urged additional time because [[iran|Iran]]'s government remained "seriously fractured" and was working to develop a unified negotiating proposal.[1] Trump granted the extension one day before the original ceasefire deadline of April 22, 2026, while directing U.S. forces to keep the [[naval-blockade-of-iran|naval blockade]] in place and remain fully prepared for further action until Iran presented a complete plan.[1] The request built on [[pakistan|Pakistan]]-mediated talks in Islamabad that had previously stalled over the blockade of Iranian ports and the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]
