# Asim Munir

Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir Ahmed Shah is the head of [[pakistan|Pakistan]]'s military and the country's most powerful political-military figure.

## Role in the 2026 Middle East negotiations

According to the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," Munir was among the leaders with whom [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] held talks on a comprehensive settlement with [[iran|Iran]]. Trump's [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement named Pakistan among the countries that should simultaneously sign onto the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]]. Pakistan also appears in the same Brief as one of the third countries reportedly under consideration by US negotiators as a destination for the transfer of Iran's enriched uranium stockpile, alongside Russia, [[turkey|Turkey]] and China.

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] included the Pakistanis, with the Turks, in the coalition he expects to emerge from an Arab–Persian peace, a bloc he argues would be "capable of challenging [[israel|Israel]] and keeping its aspiring hegemony in check."
