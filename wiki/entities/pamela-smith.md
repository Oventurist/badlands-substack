# Pamela A. Smith

Pamela A. Smith is the Chief of the Washington, D.C. [[metropolitan-police-department|Metropolitan Police Department]] (MPD).[1] In August 2025 she was cited by the department in response to allegations that MPD officials had manipulated crime statistics, with the department pointing to her statement that the MPD "is committed to upholding the trust and the confidence of the public."[1]

Smith's department declined to comment further on the allegations, saying that it does not comment on "active internal investigations and personnel matters."[1] The allegations centered on MPD commander [[michael-pulliam|Michael Pulliam]], who was placed on administrative leave and denied wrongdoing.[1]

## Context

The controversy over MPD crime data unfolded during an escalating confrontation between the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and the District of Columbia over control of the police department, with U.S. Attorney [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]] leading a federal investigation into whether crime statistics were falsified.[1]
