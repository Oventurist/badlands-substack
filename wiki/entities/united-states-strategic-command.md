# United States Strategic Command

United States Strategic Command (STRATCOM) is the unified combatant command of the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] responsible for the American nuclear deterrent, headquartered in the Omaha, Nebraska area.[1]

## Coverage in Badlands reporting

STRATCOM entered Badlands coverage in April 2024 through the case of [[doug-ward|Doug Ward]], who serves as deputy director of the Defense Department's Commander's Action Group and as Senior Nuclear Deterrence Advisor at the command.[1] Ward's wife, [[erin-ward|Erin Ward]], an Omaha substitute teacher, was charged with felony sexual abuse by a school employee after admitting to having sex with a 17-year-old student.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the episode to raise broader questions about personnel in what she called "the Biden nuclear domain," comparing it to a previous scandal involving a transgender nuclear official caught stealing luggage and asking "what other kinds of degenerate criminals we might find" in that portfolio.[1] She judged it a positive sign that the case reached the public rather than being suppressed to protect a senior official attached to the nuclear command.[1]
