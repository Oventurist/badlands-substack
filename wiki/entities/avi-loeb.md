# Avi Loeb

Avi Loeb is a Harvard astronomer described as one of academia's most controversial figures and as the university's "resident alien-hunter," known for arguing that objects arriving from interstellar space may be artifacts of extraterrestrial civilizations rather than natural bodies.[1]

## Claims about 3I/ATLAS

In August 2025 Loeb suggested that the interstellar object [[3i-atlas|3I/ATLAS]] could have been sent by an alien civilization to gauge human intelligence, functioning as a kind of "Turing Test" for humanity.[1] He contended that the scientific establishment — including NASA — erred by immediately declaring the object a comet, and pointed to new Hubble Space Telescope images showing a "glow" ahead of the object but "no prominent cometary tail behind it, as is the case for common comets."[1]

## Reception

Badlands commentary on the claim was dismissive, with contributor Chris Paul calling the coverage a "clown show" and questioning how anyone believes it.[1] Most astronomers, including those at NASA, continue to regard 3I/ATLAS as a comet.[1]
