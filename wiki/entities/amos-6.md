# Amos-6

**Amos-6** was an Israeli communications satellite destroyed before launch in a pad explosion at Cape Canaveral, Florida, on September 1, 2016.[1] The blast destroyed both the [[spacex|SpaceX]] Falcon 9 rocket and its payload.[1]

The satellite was owned by the Israeli satellite communications provider [[spacecom|Spacecom]], and a major user of Amos-6 was to have been [[facebook|Facebook]].[1] The mission formed part of Facebook's Internet.org program to deliver internet service to the developing world.[1]

In the analysis presented in "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]," the loss is treated as a striking anomaly: it was SpaceX's single failure against 273 successful launches, and the payload destroyed was a satellite intended to provide capabilities similar to those later delivered by [[starlink|Starlink]].[1] Video of the event shows the encapsulated payload falling at eleven seconds and triggering a secondary explosion that destroyed the satellite.[1]
