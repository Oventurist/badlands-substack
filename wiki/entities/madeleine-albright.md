# Madeleine Albright

Madeleine Albright was the United States Secretary of State under Bill Clinton and, in the account given by Badlands Media, one of the defining figures of the American "democracy promotion" apparatus that grew out of the post-[[church-committee|Church Committee]] restructuring of covert action.[1]

## Role at the National Democratic Institute

Albright chaired the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]] (NDI) for years, holding the position until her death in 2022.[1] The article characterises her as a heavyweight in Democratic foreign policy who shaped NDI's global outreach, work that it argues was frequently tied to United States interventionist agendas rather than to disinterested democracy assistance.[1] In the article's framing, NDI is one of two partisan offshoots of the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]], created in 1983 to give the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] deniable proxies for the political work it could no longer conduct directly.[1]

## Network affiliations

The article places Albright as a regular fixture in the circle around [[george-soros|George Soros]] and the Clintons, and in what it calls the deep politics of the United Nations.[1] Alongside fellow NDI board member [[stacey-abrams|Stacey Abrams]], she is presented as representative of the institute's progressive and electoral framing, in contrast to the more militaristic line associated with [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] figures such as [[john-mccain|John McCain]], [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]].[1]
