# Truong My Lan

Truong My Lan is a Vietnamese real estate tycoon and chair of the property company [[van-thinh-phat|Van Thinh Phat]] who was sentenced to death in April 2024 by a court in Ho Chi Minh City in what state media described as Vietnam's largest financial fraud case ever.[1]

## The fraud case

Lan, 67 at the time of sentencing, was formally charged with fraud amounting to $12.5 billion — nearly 3 percent of Vietnam's 2022 gross domestic product.[1] According to state media outlet VnExpress, she illegally controlled [[saigon-joint-stock-commercial-bank|Saigon Joint Stock Commercial Bank]] between 2012 and 2022 and permitted 2,500 loans that produced $27 billion in losses to the bank.[1] The court ordered her to compensate the bank $26.9 million.[1]

## Sentencing

Despite mitigating circumstances — the offense was her first and she had participated in charity activities — the court imposed a capital sentence, citing the seriousness of the case and finding that Lan had headed an orchestrated and sophisticated criminal enterprise with grave consequences and no realistic prospect of the money being recovered.[1] The judgment held that her actions "not only violate the property management rights of individuals and organizations but also push SCB into a state of special control; eroding people's trust in the leadership of the Party and State."[1]

Her niece, [[truong-hue-van|Truong Hue Van]], chief executive of Van Thinh Phat, was sentenced to 17 years in prison for aiding her aunt.[1]
