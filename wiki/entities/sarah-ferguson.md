# Sarah Ferguson

Sarah Ferguson, known as "Fergie," is the former wife of [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] and, at 66, a resident of Royal Lodge on the Windsor Estate at the time of reporting summarized in the Badlands News Brief of October 29, 2025.[1]

## Royal Lodge departure

The Sun reported that [[prince-william|Prince William]] threatened to strip Princesses Beatrice and Eugenie of their titles if Andrew and Ferguson refused to leave Royal Lodge, a 30-bedroom mansion in Windsor.[1] The couple were said to be in "advanced talks" over departing but still wanted a house each on the Windsor Estate; Ferguson expressed interest in Adelaide Cottage, formerly occupied by William and Kate, while Andrew sought Frogmore Cottage.[1] Emily Maitlis, the journalist behind Andrew's 2019 Newsnight interview, said Andrew was being pressured to move by his daughters after William's ultimatum.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America treated the episode as part of a broader reckoning inside the House of Windsor, describing the affair as a "planned rise" for William and framing the removal of disgraced family members as an encouraging development, while noting that the royals have little practical impact on Americans.[1]
