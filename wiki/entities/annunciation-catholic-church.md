# Annunciation Catholic Church

Annunciation Catholic Church is a Roman Catholic parish in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and the site of the [[2025-minneapolis-church-shooting|2025 Minneapolis church shooting]].[1]

## The August 2025 attack

On the morning of Wednesday, August 27, 2025, a gunman opened fire at the church during Mass.[1] Two children were killed and at least seventeen other people were injured in the attack.[1] Officials subsequently identified [[robin-westman|Robin Westman]] as the alleged shooter.[1]

## Significance in the ensuing debate

That the attack took place inside a church during a religious service became a central point in the political reaction. Badlands commentary emphasized the setting when criticizing [[jen-psaki|Jen Psaki]] for dismissing "thoughts and prayers" in the hours after the shooting, arguing that the venue made the attack on prayer particularly incongruous.[1]
