# Dartmouth College

**Dartmouth College** is an [[ivy-league|Ivy League]] institution cast in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the behavioral governance node of the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]

## Alleged role

The article describes Dartmouth as "entwined with technocratic control mechanisms" developed through partnerships with [[in-q-tel|In-Q-Tel]], [[palantir|Palantir]] and [[google|Google]] AI systems, and as serving the surveillance state by programming predictive policing systems and behavioral AI algorithms aligned with [[darpa|DARPA]] governance goals.[1]

## Sphinx Society

Dartmouth's alleged grooming portal is the [[sphinx-society|Sphinx Society]], described as "perhaps the quietest, but most dangerous of all," whose alumni are said to migrate into Palantir, In-Q-Tel and Google AI.[1] The article claims these behavioral governance platforms do not merely surveil populations but condition them, and that with DARPA-linked contracts and behavioral prediction capabilities "Dartmouth is building the digital cage."[1]

## Psychological warfare frontier

In the article's section on intelligence integration, Dartmouth is said to lead the frontier of digital behavioral control, with Dartmouth-linked initiatives focused on predictive modeling, social sentiment analysis and cognitive engineering — described as "weaponized neuroscience, coded into machine learning systems," designed "to guide, provoke, demoralize, and herd."[1]
