# Toni Atkins

Toni Atkins is a Democratic California state legislator representing San Diego who served as Senate President Pro Tempore.[1] In that role she was the legislator responsible for proposing the [[california-dream-for-all|Dream For All]] down-payment assistance program, enacted through Assembly Bill 140 in 2021.[1]

When the program's $300 million budget was exhausted in under 12 days and applications were paused in April 2023, Atkins responded optimistically rather than treating the shortfall as a failure.[1] In a statement dated April 14, 2023, she said it was "incredible and inspiring to see that the launch of the California Dream for All program has already been so successful," adding that the fact it had helped more than 2,400 first-time homebuyers with their down payments in its first two weeks was "terrific."[1]
