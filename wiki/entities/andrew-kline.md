# Andrew Kline

Andrew Kline is an American attorney and former [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]] lawyer who, according to Badlands reporting, was installed in March 2025 as deputy secretary of state running the office of Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]].[1]

## Career

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] traced Kline's record as follows: he served in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] focused on human trafficking cases during the George W. Bush administration from August 2003 to May 2009; he then became a policy advisor to Vice President Joe Biden from May 2009 to May 2010; and he joined the Executive Office of the President under Barack Obama from May 2010 to July 2011.[1] He was not in government on paper during Obama's second term until its final year, when he was installed as special counsel at the Federal Communications Commission, where he remained from January 2016 to May 2017.[1] After leaving the FCC he was again out of government and focused on cannabis advocacy.[1]

## Colorado appointment

In 2021 Kline joined Perkins Coie full time and remained there until March 2025, when — according to the Badlands account — he was secretly appointed to run Griswold's office.[1] There was no announcement of the appointment; his name simply began appearing on Colorado Department of State mastheads.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Ashe in America presented the appointment in the context of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s demands that Colorado release [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]], noting sardonically that Kline "was probably just the best guy for the job" and asking what that job actually was.[1] She observed that he now runs the office that tried to remove Trump from the ballot "for democracy," that persecuted Trump supporters as a matter of policy, and that was the allegedly injured party in the prosecution of Peters — "the whole corrupt temple."[1]
