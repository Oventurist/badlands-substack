# Anna Paulina Luna

Anna Paulina Luna is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Florida, known in the corpus for high-visibility oversight and transparency campaigns.

In the July 22, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Luna "attempted to appear heroic" over the weekend by amplifying [[kat-cammack|Kat Cammack]]'s H.Res. 500, which would require criminal background checks for congressional staffers and public disclosure of prior foreign-government payments and foreign nationality, in order to prevent foreign spies from becoming staffers.

The commentary treats the amplification as a public-relations misfire rather than a win: it "backfired spectacularly, as Americans everywhere reacted in shock that this wasn't already happening." The reaction is presented as part of a pattern in which [[congress|Congress]]'s attempts to advertise self-reform instead expose how minimal existing safeguards are — a reading applied equally to Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]]'s parallel proposal on staff relationships.
