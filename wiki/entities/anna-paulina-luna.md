# Anna Paulina Luna

Anna Paulina Luna is a member of the U.S. House of Representatives. In July 2026 she publicly amplified [[kat-cammack|Kat Cammack]]'s H.Res. 500, the resolution requiring criminal background checks for congressional staff and disclosure of foreign payments or nationality.[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued the effort backfired: rather than reading as heroic, the promotion drew widespread shock that such checks were not already mandatory, given the [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]]–Fang Fang affair and other cases of compromised staff.[1] The episode was folded into a broader Badlands argument that [[congress|Congress]] repeatedly demonstrates it "shouldn't be anywhere near power."[1]

## Role in the Swalwell scandal (2026)

During the April 2026 wave of sexual misconduct allegations against Representative Eric Swalwell, Luna held a hotel-room video circulated on X and verified as authentic by forensic analysis, showing Swalwell kissing an alleged sex worker.[2] She stated that the person who recorded the video was reportedly an underage female and that more damaging material would be released within 24 hours.[2] Luna also publicly accused Hill insiders and reporters of having known about the allegations for years without reporting them.[2]

## Leadership of the June 2026 rule revolt

Luna was among the conservatives who drove the late-June 2026 rebellion that defeated the House procedural rule 224-198 and froze floor business ahead of the July 4 recess, an impasse rooted in the stalled [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]].[3] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cautioned readers against excessive enthusiasm for the holdouts precisely because Luna was leading them, advising that they "asterisk your excitement" while still approving of the outcome.[3] See [[june-2026-house-rule-failure|the rule failure]] for fuller coverage.[3]

## JFK assassination tape

Luna called for the release of a purported NBC tape that could challenge the official narrative of Lee Harvey Oswald as the lone gunman in the assassination of President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[4] Her call came amid renewed controversy over the assassination, including a Glenn Beck-hosted discussion of an alleged tape suggesting Lyndon B. Johnson's involvement.[4] Coverage in The Guardian stressed the need for rigorous analysis of the tape's authenticity, while The Washington Post expressed skepticism that it would provide conclusive evidence.[4]

## Statement on WIC during the October 2025 shutdown

Luna said that the government shutdown that began on October 1, 2025 had cut aid to 6.7 million women, infants, and children.[5] "The WIC program is now on hold because these people can't put aside their egos and political games," she said, adding that the [[special-supplemental-nutrition-program-for-women-infants-and-children|WIC]] program "gives food to the most vulnerable in this country, and as a result American children are starving."[5] She urged lawmakers to "stop the nonsense and reopen these programs immediately."[5]

## Epstein files pressure campaign (2025)

As chair of a House task force on the "declassification of federal secrets," Luna made release of records from the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] investigation one of the task force's major priorities and led a bipartisan push in February 2025 urging Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] to release the alleged "client list."[6] Beginning Monday, February 24, 2025 she posted repeatedly on X directing her grievances at Bondi.[6] Badlands commentator Jon Herold was scathing about the effort's efficacy, writing that the task force's letter to the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] was ignored, that Bondi ignored her complaints, and that the eventual DOJ reply told her nothing — urging her instead to create a task force on the weaponization of media against Trump supporters.[6]

### Public demand to Bondi, February 24, 2025

On Monday, February 24, 2025, Luna took to X to ask publicly why the "files" on key crimes had not been released, writing that on February 11 and February 19 the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] had sent letters to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] asking for the status of releasing the Epstein files as well as the JFK records, and that the DOJ had not responded.[7] "Reaching out on X because we can't seem to get a response from the AG," she wrote, adding: "@AGPamBondi what is the status of the documents? These documents were ordered to be declassified."[7] Raw Story described Luna as a far-right lawmaker known for her outbursts on the House floor and framed the post as a MAGA representative demanding to know why the new attorney general had not kept a key Trump promise.[7] Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] responded that he stood by his criticism of the need for a declassification task force, acknowledged that readers were free to disagree, and said he had sent Luna a direct message inviting her onto his daily show, to which she had not responded.[7]

## Objection to the 2024 FISA reauthorization

In April 2024, immediately after the House voted 273 to 147 to reauthorize [[section-702|Section 702]] of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act through 2026, Luna objected to its passage and requested a vote on the motion to reconsider the legislation.[8] Her objection meant the bill could not be transmitted to the Senate until the House voted to table the motion to reconsider the following week, delaying the renewal of a program set to expire on April 19.[8] The maneuver came after an amendment to add a warrant requirement for searching Americans' data failed on a tied 212 to 212 vote.[8]
