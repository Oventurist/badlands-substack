# NativePath

**NativePath** is a nutritional supplement company that has advertised in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], the Substack newsletter of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1] Its collagen product was promoted to the "Badland Media Audience" through a dedicated landing page at getnativepath.com/badlands, offering readers 45 percent off the retail price of collagen.[1]

## Product claims

According to the sponsored placement carried in the July 4, 2025 edition of the Brief, NativePath Collagen is a single-ingredient formula containing no fillers, additives or artificial sweeteners.[1] The copy states that the product is made with only type 1 and type 3 collagen fibers and is third-party tested for heavy metals, which the advertiser presents as ensuring purity and safety.[1] Readers were invited to "discover NativePath Collagen and start your transformation today" via the Badlands-specific URL.[1]

The placement appeared under the Brief's standard "Sponsored" label, consistent with the newsletter's practice of running clearly marked sponsor slots between editorial items.[1]
