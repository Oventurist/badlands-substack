# Pierbattista Pizzaballa

Cardinal Pierbattista Pizzaballa is the Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem, the senior Roman Catholic prelate in the Holy Land and the head of the [[latin-patriarchate-of-jerusalem|Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem]].[1] In that capacity he is the principal Catholic authority responsible for liturgical celebrations at [[church-of-the-holy-sepulchre|the Church of the Holy Sepulchre]] in Jerusalem's Old City.[1]

## Palm Sunday 2026 exclusion

On March 29, 2026, [[israeli-police|Israeli police]] blocked Pizzaballa and other senior Catholic leaders from entering the Church of the Holy Sepulchre, preventing them from celebrating a private Palm Sunday Mass, according to church officials and media reports.[1] Catholic authorities described the episode as the first time in centuries that top church leaders were unable to conduct [[palm-sunday|Palm Sunday]] services at one of Christianity's holiest sites; the planned service had been limited to a small number of clergy and was not open to the public.[1] Israeli authorities attributed the decision to security concerns tied to the ongoing conflict with [[iran|Iran]], citing missile threats, limited shelter availability and restricted emergency access in the Old City.[1]

The Latin Patriarchate called the measure "a manifestly unreasonable and grossly disproportionate measure" that impeded freedom of worship.[1] U.S. ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] said that barring the Patriarch from a private ceremony was "difficult to understand or justify," noting that the four Catholic representatives were well below the Home Front Command's 50-person gathering limit.[1] Italian leader [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] said preventing the Patriarch of Jerusalem and the Custos of the Holy Land from entering constituted "an offence not only against believers but against every community that recognises religious freedom."[1] Following the international backlash, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] instructed authorities to allow access to the church.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writers treated the exclusion of Pizzaballa as the culmination of a multi-year escalation of restrictions at the Holy Sepulchre dating to the 2020 COVID-era closures.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] placed the episode in a sequence that began with an unprecedented Israeli property tax on Church properties, the freezing of Church bank accounts after refusal to pay, progressive access restrictions, and finally the barring of the Cardinal and clergy on Palm Sunday.[1]
