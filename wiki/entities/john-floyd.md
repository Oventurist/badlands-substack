# John Floyd

John Floyd is the father of Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] and a witness in the February 2024 evidentiary hearing on the motion to disqualify his daughter from the Georgia election interference case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his co-defendants.[1]

## Testimony

Floyd testified after former Georgia Governor Roy Barnes on the Friday session of the hearing.[1] He told the court that he had been aware of prior romantic relationships in his daughter's life — including one with a DJ — but that she had concealed her relationship with special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] from him.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the testimony as awkward and noted that the proceedings "got even weirder from there," with the bulk of the day consumed by the contested testimony of Wade's business partner [[terrence-bradley|Terrence Bradley]].[1]

The concealment described by Floyd was relevant to the defense theory that the Willis–Wade relationship predated Wade's hiring and had been deliberately hidden.[1]
