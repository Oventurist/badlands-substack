# Roy McClain

**Roy McClain** is the Secretary of the Board of Elections in [[spalding-county|Spalding County, Georgia]].[1]

Following the required post-election hand count of the November 7, 2023 municipal elections — in which observers reported that hand-count results failed to match machine counts in every race across every precinct — the Spalding County election board voted 2–1 to certify the results. McClain cast the lone vote against certification, aligning with those who wanted certification delayed until the root cause of the discrepancies could be identified.[1] He could not be reached for comment on his vote.[1]

See [[2023-georgia-municipal-election-discrepancies]] for the broader episode.[1]
