# Los Cuinis

Los Cuinis is a Mexican criminal organization closely aligned with the [[jalisco-new-generation-cartel|Jalisco New Generation Cartel]].[1]

## 2025 extradition of Abigael González Valencia

On August 12, 2025, Mexico agreed to send 26 high-ranking cartel figures wanted by the United States across the border as part of a major deal with the Trump administration amid the U.S. crackdown on drug smuggling.[1] Among them was [[abigael-gonzalez-valencia|Abigael González Valencia]], described in reporting on the transfer as a leader of Los Cuinis.[1] The Trump administration promised not to seek the death penalty for any of the transferred cartel members, and the handover followed the transfer of 30 other highly sought drug cartel members to the United States in February 2025.[1]
