# Metropolitan Jonathan of Tulchin

**Metropolitan Jonathan of Tulchin** is a senior clergyman of the [[ukrainian-orthodox-church|Ukrainian Orthodox Church]] who in August 2023 was sentenced to five years in prison in Ukraine after being accused of "justifying" Russian aggression against Ukraine.[1] Regional media identified him as the bishop at the centre of the case, and reported that property under his oversight would be confiscated as part of the sentence.[1]

## Context

The dominant Orthodox church in Ukraine to which Metropolitan Jonathan belongs has frequently criticized the Russian invasion but has not broken communion with the Moscow Patriarchate, the Russian Orthodox Church.[1] On that basis its clergy and monastics have been treated by the Ukrainian state as "sympathetic" to Moscow and have come under severe persecution, with authorities seizing several churches and monasteries on accusations of cooperating with Russia.[1] Other clergy members have been jailed, placed under house arrest, or harassed by mobs of far-right Ukrainian nationalists, in many cases for merely calling for peace between the two countries.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the sentence as an indictment of the government of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], remarking that "nothing says 'democracy' like jailing church leaders for praying for peace in the middle of a war."[1] She noted that the case was not the first of its kind and described the harassment of churches by what she called "far right Ukrainian brown shirts."[1] Because the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] operated under a policy of "nothing about Ukraine without Ukraine" — meaning the Ukrainian government drove strategy while the United States supported it — she argued that Washington was effectively on board with jailing church leaders for thought crimes, religious speech and prayer, and suggested the policy was consistent with the administration's record of politically targeting American Catholics.[1]
