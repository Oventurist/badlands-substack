# Gilbert Chagoury

Gilbert Chagoury is a Nigerian businessman described in the article as a close ally and business associate of [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]] and as one of the largest benefactors of the Clinton network after Rich's pardon.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Quoting the New York Post, the article notes that Nigerian media declared in 1999 that the "Gilbert Chagoury-Marc Rich alliance remains a formidable foe," and that the two sold oil on international markets together.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) In 2000 Chagoury was convicted in Geneva of money laundering and aiding a criminal organization in connection with the billions of dollars stolen from Nigeria during the reign of dictator Gen. Sani Abacha.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article adds that he got the conviction expunged in a plea deal, and asks whether [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] or [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] had a hand in that outcome.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Chagoury was, in the New York Post's phrasing, very generous to the Clintons in the years following the Rich pardon.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) He organized an event at which Bill Clinton was paid $100,000 to speak in 2003, donated millions to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], and in 2009 pledged $1 billion to the [[clinton-global-initiative|Clinton Global Initiative]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The Chagourys were also active in Hillary Clinton's 2008 presidential bid: Michel Chagoury, a relative in Los Angeles, was a bundler and served on her campaign staff, and numerous other relatives gave the maximum $4,600 each.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

In 2009 CGI gave Chagoury's company an award for sustainable development, and in 2013 Bill Clinton appeared in Nigeria for a public ceremony involving one of Chagoury's projects.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Chagoury was an invited guest at Clinton's 60th birthday party and attended the wedding of Clinton's longtime aide [[doug-band|Doug Band]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article draws the moral that it was not only America for sale: the Clinton Global Initiative put the whole world up for sale, since Chagoury could pledge a billion dollars only because he had stolen billions from Nigeria, after which Clinton travelled to Nigeria to celebrate one of his projects.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
