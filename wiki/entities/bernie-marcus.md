# Bernie Marcus

Bernie Marcus is an American businessman and philanthropist, best known as a co-founder of [[home-depot|The Home Depot]], who in November 2023 published a widely circulated open letter urging Americans to support [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s return to the presidency.[1] Writing at the age of 94, Marcus framed the essay as the reluctant re-entry of an elderly man who had hoped to "move to the sidelines and let younger generations continue the fight to preserve America's founding freedoms and values," but who concluded that "the stakes are just too high" to walk away.[1]

## The 2023 open letter

The letter, distributed through RealClear Wire, opened with the line that became its headline: "I am 94 years old and like many of you, I am exhausted by politics and saddened by what I see happening to America."[1] Marcus argued that the country's condition could not be left as an inheritance to "our children and grandchildren," citing what he described as once-great American cities that had "devolved into lawlessness with random violent attacks on innocent people, rampant looting, and large-scale homeless encampments."[1] He attributed the absence of consequences for offenders to prosecutors elected with backing from [[george-soros|George Soros]], writing that such prosecutors "across America refuse to prosecute" wrongdoers.[1]

Marcus reduced the 2024 choice to a pair of simple propositions. For Democrats, he wrote, the question was whether they felt better off than they had three years earlier; if so, they should vote for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] or whoever the party nominated.[1] For Republicans, he argued the calculus was equally simple: Trump would win the nomination, and the task was therefore to do everything possible to secure his victory in the general election.[1]

## Reception and Badlands commentary

Coverage of the letter divided sharply. In its News Brief treatment, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that the "Liberal and Conservative Establishment, also known as the Uniparty," had tried to dismiss the letter as "just another case of some corrupt rich guy stumping for another corrupt rich guy in Donald Trump," while it was nevertheless "making waves in all the right circles" from the Badlands perspective.[1]

Burning Bright acknowledged that Marcus "may not be a household name," but described him as "a mover and shaker in the world of finance and domestic commerce for decades."[1] The commentary argued that Marcus's age was itself evidentiary: at 94, "far closer to the end of the ride than the beginning," and having "made all the money he could ever need," his intervention lent credence to the view that he genuinely believed the nation required change — characterized as "progression through regression to a past mindset."[1] Burning Bright further emphasized what it called the "moral focus" of the letter, contending that choosing Trump in 2024 would be not merely the logical choice but "the RIGHT choice, in every sense of the word" as the awakening accelerated within what Badlands writers term the Collective Mind.[1]
