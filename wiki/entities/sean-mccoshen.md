# Sean McCoshen

**Sean McCoshen** is the chief executive officer of the [[alaska-alberta-railway-development-corporation|Alaska-Alberta Railway Development Corporation]] (A2A), the private initiative created in 2015 to advance the [[alaska-canada-railway|Alaska-Canada Railway]].[1]

McCoshen has described the rail link as "a world-class infrastructure project that will generate more than 18,000 jobs for Canadian workers at a time when they are most needed, provide a new, more efficient route for trans-Pacific shipping and thereby link Alberta to world markets."[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites the statement as evidence that the project's promise lies in connecting North America to Pacific and Eurasian development corridors rather than in narrow resource extraction.[1]

## Alaska summit essay

In his August 2025 essay on the [[2025-trump-putin-alaska-summit|Trump–Putin Alaska summit]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] again quoted McCoshen describing the [[alaska-canada-railway|Alaska–Alberta rail link]] as "a world-class infrastructure project that will generate more than 18,000 jobs for Canadian workers at a time when they are most needed," providing "a new, more efficient route for trans-Pacific shipping and thereby link Alberta to world markets."[2]
