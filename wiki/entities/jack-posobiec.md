# Jack Posobiec

Jack Posobiec, referred to in Badlands commentary as "Jack Poso," is a conservative media influencer who covered the pre-trial proceedings in the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] case in July 2026.[1]

Posobiec was among the influencers who emerged from the preliminary hearing declaring that the not-yet-held trial was effectively over and urging followers to pray for a speedy trial on behalf of [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]].[1] Together with [[benny-johnson|Benny Johnson]] and [[brandon-tatum|Officer Tatum]], he spoke of a very clear video said to prove definitively that the shooter was Robinson; Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported that Posobiec was almost moved to tears describing it, and that the group's message amounted to a claim that anyone who saw the footage would likewise condemn the accused before trial.[1]

The influencers began backing away from that claim after [[canncon|CannCon]] established that the purportedly secret video was visible on a defense laptop on the CourtTV feed and was the same video everyone had already seen; Posobiec did not respond to requests for comment.[1] Ashe in America concluded that Posobiec and Johnson were inverting the Constitution to sell a story and to influence potential jurors — behaviour she likened to the mob pressure surrounding the [[derek-chauvin|Derek Chauvin]] trial and described as an apparent psychological warfare campaign against the American public.[1]

## Reporting on the DeSantis Turning Point snub (2023)

A Turning Point USA contributor as well as a journalist, Posobiec broke the July 2023 report that [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] had "declined the invitation" to speak at a [[turning-point-action|Turning Point Action]] event in West Palm Beach, first in a Monday tweet and then on a Human Events broadcast on Real America's Voice the following day, noting that Turning Point had invited every presidential candidate.[2]

## Role in the Jankowicz departure

In March 2023 Posobiec posted that [[nina-jankowicz|Nina Jankowicz]] was asking for donations to fund a lawsuit, adding that this came "one year after I got her removed from the Dept of Homeland Security" — a reference to her short tenure leading the [[disinformation-governance-board|Disinformation Governance Board]] at the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]].[3] The post, dated March 3, 2023, drew roughly 1,740 reposts and 9,680 likes and was cited in Badlands commentary as an example of the mocking public reaction that greeted Jankowicz's fundraising appeal against [[fox-news|Fox News]].[3]
