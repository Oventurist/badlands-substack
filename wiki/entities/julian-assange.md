# Julian Assange

Julian Assange is the founder of [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]], listed in "A Bright Red Line" among the individuals whose efforts have allowed Western audiences to see a perspective on the Ukraine war that mainstream media omits.[1] The article names him alongside Tucker Carlson, Oliver Stone, Douglas Macgregor, RFK Jr. and the late Gonzalo Lira.[1]

Assange's specific contribution in the article's argument is the publication of the February 2008 diplomatic cable written by [[william-burns|William Burns]], which describes Ukrainian entry into [[nato|NATO]] as "the brightest of all redlines" for the Russian elite.[1]
