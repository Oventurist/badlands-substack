# Donald Trump Jr.

Donald Trump Jr. is the eldest son of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and a prominent surrogate within the America First political movement.[1]

## UFC 295

On November 11, 2023, Trump Jr. accompanied his father into Madison Square Garden for [[ufc|UFC]] 295, part of an entourage that also included [[kid-rock|Kid Rock]], [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], and UFC president [[dana-white|Dana White]].[1] The group took ringside seats moments before the pay-per-view event began, as the former president received a loud ovation from the sold-out crowd.[1]

## Exchange with Nayib Bukele (June 2024)

Following the inauguration of Salvadoran president [[nayib-bukele|Nayib Bukele]] for a second term on June 1, 2024, Trump Jr. congratulated him on the "amazing accomplishment" of winning reelection in a landslide with about 85 percent of the vote.[2] In the exchange he told Bukele, "And you do it all without having to jail your political opponents. Which, I liked in the speech," and noted that Salvadorans were "able to video because there's no more crime" and that no one was being thrown "in jail" for doing so.[2] Bukele responded, "We don't jail the opposition" — a remark widely circulated as a commentary on political prosecutions in the United States.[2]

## Public Square NYSE listing (2023)

Trump Jr. appeared at the podium on the floor of the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] alongside Kim Guilfoyle and [[michael-seifert|Michael Seifert]] in July 2023 as the conservative marketplace [[public-square|Public Square]] rang the opening bell for its public listing, an event accompanied by chants of "USA!" from the floor.[3]
