# Claire Woodall

Claire Woodall is the former Executive Director of the [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] whose conduct on Election Night 2020 became a focus of a federal investigation.[1]

Investigators from the [[fbi|FBI]] are reportedly examining an incident in which Woodall briefly left a flash drive inside a voting machine at Milwaukee's central count facility.[1] The review forms part of a broader FBI investigation into Wisconsin's 2020 election, in which agents prepared to interview multiple [[milwaukee-police-department|Milwaukee Police Department]] officers stationed at central count and at polling places.[1]

## Removal in May 2024

On May 6, 2024, Milwaukee Mayor [[cavalier-johnson|Cavalier Johnson]] announced in what the Associated Press characterized as a surprise move that Woodall would be replaced as executive director of the city's election commission by her deputy, Paulina Gutierrez.[2] The change came roughly six months before the November 2024 presidential election, in which Milwaukee — the largest city in the perennial swing state of Wisconsin — was again expected to draw national scrutiny; four of the previous six presidential contests in Wisconsin had been decided by less than a percentage point.[2]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary tied the ouster to long-running reporting on the state's "Vote at Home" controversy, noting that Woodall — also referred to as Woodall-Vogg — had recently resurfaced in coverage after emails emerged showing figures in what the commentary described as Wisconsin's "election cabal," a network of public and private actors administering the state's elections, joking about margins for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[2] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who covers the subject through [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] alongside co-host Brian, framed the removal as evidence that "the wheels of justice turn slowly," while cautioning that the underlying network remained in place and that Wisconsin's August 13 primary would provide further data ahead of November.[2]
