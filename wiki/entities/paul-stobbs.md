# Paul Stobbs

**Paul Stobbs** is the author of *The Nephilim Looked Like Clowns* and the host of the Understanding Conspiracy video channel, cited in the Badlands Media essay "[[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] — What is Clown World?".[1]

The Badlands author states that he has not yet read the book in full but has listened to several hours of Stobbs's videos, and summarizes the takeaway as follows: clown costumes are themselves a form of demon worship — or ancestor worship, as the [[koryos|Koryos]] would call it.[1] Stobbs is also the source of the essay's claim that [[joseph-grimaldi|Joseph Grimaldi]] was made to dress as a clown by a Freemason named Charles Dibden.[1]
