# JBS

JBS is a Brazilian-headquartered meat processing company and the largest meat packer in the world, with extensive beef, pork, and poultry operations in the United States.

## Antitrust investigation

As reported in the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, JBS is one of four companies — alongside [[cargill|Cargill]], [[tyson-foods|Tyson Foods]], and [[national-beef|National Beef]] — targeted by an intensified antitrust investigation announced by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and the [[united-states-department-of-agriculture|Department of Agriculture]], which cited price manipulation, foreign ownership risks, and supply chain problems.

Secretary of Agriculture [[brooke-rollins|Brooke Rollins]] singled out JBS and National Beef as foreign-owned packers constituting a national security threat amid reports of slave labor ties, stating that half the meatpacking giants under review, "including the largest meat packer in the world," are foreign-owned or subject to significant foreign ownership and control, making them "a threat not just to our cattle producers, but a threat to America itself." Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] disclosed that investigators had reviewed more than three million documents and conducted hundreds of rancher interviews.
