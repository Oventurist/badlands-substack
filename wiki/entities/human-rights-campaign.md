# Human Rights Campaign (HRC)

The Human Rights Campaign (HRC) is an American advocacy organization described in Badlands Media commentary as one of the principal enforcement bodies of the [[esg|ESG]] regime rather than as a conventional civil-rights group.[1]

## Alleged role in ESG enforcement

In his April 2023 essay "Get Woke, Go Broke," Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identified HRC as a Soros-run and Soros-funded organization that creates corporate credit scores, comparing the practice to the social credit system of the Chinese Communist Party.[1] According to Carlson, HRC and similar politically motivated organizations force corporations to comply with their demands or feel their wrath.[1]

The leverage, in this account, is financial rather than reputational: a company that does not toe the [[george-soros|Soros]] line is barred from the funds of the major investment managers — [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] and [[state-street|State Street]].[1] Because exclusion from ETFs and mutual funds reduces the number of holders of a stock, it depresses the share price and the company's overall valuation, giving scoring bodies such as HRC an effective veto over corporate conduct.[1]

Carlson placed this mechanism at the center of the dilemma named in his title: a corporation that adopts the agenda alienates its own customers, while one that refuses is excommunicated from the "club" and loses value regardless.[1] He also noted that the scoring standard is inherently unsatisfiable, with woke requirements continually ratcheted upward so that even the corporate face of ESG can be found non-compliant.[1]
