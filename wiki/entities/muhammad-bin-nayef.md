# Muhammad bin Nayef

Nayef was the would-be Crown Prince of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] displaced during the 2017 consolidation of power by [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] and [[king-salman|King Salman]].[1]

A cousin of MBS, he was deposed alongside the prince and minister then in charge of the Saudi military — also a cousin — with both arrested and stripped of all powers.[1] Nayef was charged with treason and later accused of plotting to murder King Salman.[1] The moves accompanied the [[ritz-carlton-purge|Ritz-Carlton Purge]] and MBS's coronation on November 4, 2017.[1] The article notes that many observers presumed these moves were made under the direct guidance of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a view the author says he once held but later rejected.[1]
