# Joseph Russoniello

Joseph Russoniello is a San Francisco lawyer identified by author [[peter-schweizer|Peter Schweizer]] as a central figure in the flow of Catholic-connected money into [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s campaign for San Francisco District Attorney.[1]

Russoniello represented the church on a wide variety of issues, including the handling of the church abuse scandal, and served on the Catholic Church's National Review Board of the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops, a body whose purpose was to review Catholic Church abuse cases.[1] He was also a partner in the San Francisco law firm Cooley Godward.[1]

Russoniello donated the maximum amount allowed by law to Harris's campaign, $1,250, and his law firm added another $2,250.[1] He additionally sat on Harris's advisory council during her tenure as San Francisco District Attorney.[1] Schweizer notes that Harris had no particular ties to the Catholic Church or Catholic organizations, yet the money arrived in large and unprecedented sums — a pattern echoed by the firm Bingham McCutcheon, which handled legal matters for the [[archdiocese-of-san-francisco|archdiocese]] concerning Catholic Charities and donated the maximum $2,825 despite having previously given only $650 total to two other San Francisco candidates.[1]
