# Simon Wiesenthal Center

The Simon Wiesenthal Center (SWC) is a Jewish human rights organization named for the famed Nazi hunter Simon Wiesenthal. In [[badlands-brief-314|the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026]] it is identified as the body that, in March 2020, first prompted Senator [[sheldon-whitehouse|Sheldon Whitehouse]] to open a congressional investigation into whether [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] had provided discreet banking services to [[schutzstaffel|SS]] officers fleeing Germany for [[argentina|Argentina]] after the Second World War.

That request set in motion the chain of events described in the Brief: Credit Suisse's 2021 hiring of independent ombudsman [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]], his firing in December 2022, his reinstatement by [[ubs|UBS]] after the 2023 acquisition following intervention by Whitehouse and [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]], and the eventual disclosure of 890 previously undisclosed Nazi-linked accounts. The Badlands commentary treats the Center's role as significant in part for its irony, noting that a Jewish human rights organization's inquiry ultimately converged with the [[pdvsa|PDVSA]] corruption investigations associated with [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].
