# Aaron Judge

Aaron Judge is a star outfielder for the [[new-york-yankees|New York Yankees]].[1]

On September 11, 2025, Judge was among the players who shook hands with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] when the president visited the Yankees clubhouse before a game held on the anniversary of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1] Players that day wore hats bearing "FDNY" and "NYPD" to honor first responders, and Trump told the assembled players and coaches, "You're going to win. You're going to go all the way."[1]
