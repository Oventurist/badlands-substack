# Johannes Steinhoff

Johannes Steinhoff was a Luftwaffe fighter pilot during the Second World War and a recipient of the Knight's Cross of the Iron Cross, the Nazi military's highest award.[1]

## Postwar NATO career

After the war Steinhoff served as Acting Commander of Allied Air Forces Central Europe in [[nato|NATO]] from 1965 to 1966, as Inspector of the West German Air Force from 1966 to 1970, and as Chairman of the NATO Military Committee from 1971 to 1974.[1] He is listed among the former Nazi officers who held senior NATO commands between 1950 and the 1980s, alongside [[adolf-heusinger|Adolf Heusinger]] — who chaired the same Military Committee from 1961 to 1964 — and [[hans-speidel|Hans Speidel]].[1]
