# John Connally

John Connally was a Texas politician — a close associate of President Lyndon B. Johnson and a former governor of Texas — who was riding in the limousine when President Kennedy was assassinated.[1] In 1980 he is alleged to have carried a message to Middle Eastern leaders intended to sabotage President [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter's]] re-election campaign.[1]

## The 1980 Middle East mission

According to [[ben-barnes|Ben Barnes]], his longtime political protégé, Connally took him to one Middle Eastern capital after another in the summer of 1980, meeting a host of regional leaders to deliver a blunt message to be passed to Iran: do not release the American hostages before the election, because Reagan will win and give you a better deal.[1] Shortly after returning home, Connally reported to [[william-casey|William J. Casey]], chairman of [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan's]] campaign and later Director of Central Intelligence, briefing him about the trip in an airport lounge.[1]

Records at the Lyndon Baines Johnson Library and Museum corroborate part of the account: an itinerary found in Connally's files indicates he left Houston on July 18, 1980, for a trip taking him to Jordan, Syria, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and Israel before returning to Houston on August 11, with Barnes listed as accompanying him.[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] writes that Barnes made this tour on behalf of [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and Casey, and that the trip opened the door to the later Casey meetings with Iranian representatives in Madrid — the core of the [[october-surprise|October Surprise]] allegations.[1]
