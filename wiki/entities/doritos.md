# Doritos

**Doritos** is a snack food brand whose Spanish arm, Doritos Spain, became the subject of a consumer boycott campaign in March 2024 after featuring the transgender influencer [[samantha-hudson|Samantha Hudson]] (Iván González Ranedo) in an Instagram promotional video.[1]

The short promo, called "Crunch Talks," was posted on a Sunday and pulled the following Monday amid backlash.[1] A company spokesperson confirmed to Rolling Stone that the video was not part of a wider brand ambassadorship campaign and that Hudson's relationship with the company had been terminated over controversial past comments, saying the company "strongly condemn[s] words or actions that promote violence or sexism of any kind."[1] Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] used the episode to argue both a culture-war point and a health-freedom one, dismissing Doritos as poor-quality food.[1]
