# Jonathan Conricus

Jonathan Conricus is a former spokesman for the Israel Defense Forces who has remained a prominent commentator on Israeli security and on the relationship between Israel and the United States.[1]

## 2026 Jerusalem News Syndicate summit remarks

Conricus spoke at the [[jerusalem-news-syndicate|Jerusalem News Syndicate]]'s 2026 International Policy Summit, an event at which several speakers criticized the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration over its diplomacy with [[iran|Iran]].[1] Badlands Media characterized Conricus as "one voice of reason on the stage," noting that he told the summit that [[israel|Israel]] remains entirely dependent on the United States and that Israeli politicians who claim the country can go its own way are delusional.[1]

Badlands framed the remark as a concession that Israel is, in its words, a "welfare queen" whose status quo cannot be maintained without continued American support — a dependency the outlet argued was unlikely to change in the near term.[1] Conricus's position stood in contrast to the more combative posture attributed at the same event to [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] and to remarks by [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] and [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]

## "Pearl Harbor moment" remark (October 2023)

On the day of the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 attacks]], Conricus — identified as former [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] international spokesperson — summed up the day's meaning for Israel by calling it the country's "Pearl Harbor" moment.[2] Speaking to [[cnn|CNN]] about the surprise invasion from [[gaza|Gaza]], he said: "The entire system failed. It's not just one component. It's the entire defense architecture that evidently failed to provide the necessary defense for Israeli civilians."[2] He added, "This is a Pearl Harbor type of moment for Israel, where there was reality up until today, and then there will be reality after today."[2]
