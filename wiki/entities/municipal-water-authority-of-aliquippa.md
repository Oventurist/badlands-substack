# Municipal Water Authority of Aliquippa

The Municipal Water Authority of Aliquippa is a small water utility serving Aliquippa in western Pennsylvania. It became nationally prominent in late 2023 when it discovered that its systems had been infiltrated by hackers affiliated with Iran.[1]

## November 2023 breach

The authority discovered the intrusion on November 25, 2023.[1] The attackers targeted an Israeli-made industrial control device in use at the facility, part of a broader campaign that U.S. and Israeli authorities said had compromised numerous organizations across multiple U.S. states.[1] Federal officials informed the authority's chairman, [[matthew-mottes|Matthew Mottes]], that the same group had also compromised four other utilities and an aquarium.[1]

An advisory issued jointly by the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]], the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] and [[israel-national-cyber-directorate|Israel's National Cyber Directorate]] stated that "the victims span multiple U.S. states," without specifying the number of compromised organizations or providing further detail.[1]

## Context and commentary

Although no evidence connected Iran to the October 7, 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack on [[israel|Israel]], cybersecurity analysts expected pro-Palestinian hacktivists and Iranian-backed hackers to intensify attacks on Israel and its allies during the Gaza war — an expectation the Aliquippa incident appeared to confirm.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treated the episode as a case study in narrative seeding, linking it to [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] cyberattack simulations held in 2020 and 2021 and to [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s warnings of a coming "cyber storm."[1]
