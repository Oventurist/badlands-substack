# Pod Save America

Pod Save America is an American political podcast hosted by former [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration staffers, among them former presidential speechwriter [[jon-favreau|Jon Favreau]].[1] The program is a leading outlet of Democratic-aligned commentary media.[1]

## 2024 campaign commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the show's host Favreau in January 2024 as an example of favorable establishment media reaction to [[biden-valley-forge-speech|Joe Biden's Valley Forge speech]], in which the president framed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as an existential threat to democracy.[1] DeLarme argued that outlets and commentators of this kind sustain an image of [[joe-biden|Biden]] as a heroic defender of democracy while declining to scrutinize his administration's record, including federal rulings over the pressuring of Big Tech to suppress dissent.[1]
