# Ripple

**Ripple** is a cryptocurrency and payments company whose chief executive, [[brad-garlinghouse|Brad Garlinghouse]], has publicly pressed for regulatory clarity in the United States rather than deregulation.[1]

## Regulatory posture

In a December 2024 segment on [[60-minutes|60 Minutes]], Garlinghouse said of the company's stance toward American authorities: "We haven't been asking to be deregulated. We've been asking to be regulated."[1] The remark was circulated by Badlands as a bonus item in its news brief, reflecting continuing attention within the alternative-media ecosystem to the relationship between the digital asset industry and US regulators.[1]
