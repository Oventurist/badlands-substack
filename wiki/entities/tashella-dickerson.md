# Tashella Dickerson

Tashella Sheri Amore Dickerson is an Oklahoma City activist who served as executive director of [[black-lives-matter-okc|Black Lives Matter OKC]] and who, in December 2025 at age 52, was charged in a federal indictment with wire fraud and money laundering.[1]

## Role at BLMOKC

According to the indictment, Dickerson served as executive director of the organization beginning at least in 2016, and in that capacity held access to BLMOKC's bank, PayPal, and Cash App accounts.[1] Because the group was not itself a registered 501(c)(3), donations were routed through the [[alliance-for-global-justice|Alliance for Global Justice]], which imposed restrictions on the use of funds, required full accounting on request, and barred real-estate purchases without consent.[1]

## Charges

A federal grand jury indictment was unsealed by U.S. Attorney Robert J. Troester of the Western District of Oklahoma.[1] It alleges that from June 2020 through at least October 2025 Dickerson embezzled funds from BLMOKC accounts for her personal benefit.[1] The case was carried in the Badlands News Brief's bonus items alongside other fraud and accountability stories of the week.[1]
