# Mayo Clinic

The **Mayo Clinic** is the American academic medical centre identified in [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] Chapter 7 as the hospital where Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] — referred to throughout the [[q-operation|Q]] drops only as "No Name" — underwent surgery.[1]

## Role in Q drop #52

Q drop #52, posted November 2, 2017, asked anons where "No Name" had had his surgery.[1] Chapter 7 of the Q for Dummies series answers that the procedure took place at the Mayo Clinic, and argues that the venue is itself relevant because the clinic's board of directors at the time included figures the author characterises as "rather swampy creatures."[1] The chapter notes that McCain was reported to have undergone surgery for a subdural hematoma and that he returned to work only days afterward, a timeline the author flags as noteworthy without elaborating, deferring fuller treatment of the McCain material to later chapters of the series.[1]

The Mayo Clinic question sits within a broader sequence in drop #52 concerning the unexplained wealth of senior officials and the use of charitable foundations as fronts, a sequence the drop closes with the phrase "[[alice-in-wonderland|Alice In Wonderland]]."[1]
