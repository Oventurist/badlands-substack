# Jordan Neely

Jordan Neely was a New York City subway rider who died in May 2023 after being placed in a chokehold by former Marine [[daniel-penny|Daniel Penny]] aboard an F train in Manhattan.[1] His death triggered a national controversy over subway crime, mental illness, and the limits of bystander intervention, and led the [[manhattan-district-attorneys-office|Manhattan District Attorney's Office]] to charge Penny with second-degree manslaughter.[1]

## Background and the incident

Neely had a documented history of mental illness.[1] A 66-year-old woman who witnessed the encounter told the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] that Neely was threatening passengers after boarding the F train in Manhattan.[1] Reporting cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] indicated that Neely had a criminal history including more than 40 arrests, that he was well known to the subway-riding community, and that he had been threatening to kill passengers on the train.[1] Commentary published the week of his death also noted reporting that on the night before he died Neely had threatened to push strangers onto the tracks.[1]

## Context and commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] situated the incident within a broader pattern of subway violence, noting that 2023 had already seen 26 passengers pushed onto the tracks, two of whom died.[1] The commentary argued that witnesses were emerging to debunk the state's account of the encounter, and that the media was portraying Neely sympathetically while omitting exculpatory detail favorable to Penny.[1] Video that surfaced the week after the death was said to show Penny placing Neely in the "recovery position" and Neely still breathing.[1]
