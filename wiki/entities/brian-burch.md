# Brian Burch

Brian Burch is the head of the right-wing Catholic advocacy group [[catholicvote|CatholicVote]] and was named by President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as his pick for United States ambassador to the Vatican.[1]

Burch is a critic of [[pope-francis|Pope Francis]], and his selection was reported as standing in stark contrast to the Pope's near-simultaneous appointment of Trump critic [[robert-mcelroy|Robert McElroy]] as archbishop of Washington, D.C. CatholicVote, the organization Burch leads, rallied Catholic support for Trump during the 2024 election.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the paired appointments as part of a broader realignment among the "city states" of Washington, D.C., the City of London, and Vatican City in the weeks before Inauguration Day.[1]
