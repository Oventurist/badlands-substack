# AdventHealth

AdventHealth is a hospital system operating facilities in Florida, including AdventHealth Palm Coast Parkway in Flagler County.[1]

## 2025 unlicensed nurse case

The Palm Coast Parkway hospital employed [[autumn-bardisa|Autumn Bardisa]], who according to the [[flagler-county-sheriffs-office|Flagler County Sheriff's Office]] treated 4,486 patients there beginning in July 2023 while falsely representing herself as a registered nurse.[1] Staff discovered the deception only when Bardisa was considered for a promotion and the associated paperwork was reviewed, roughly two years into her employment.[1]

Badlands commentary held that the institution came off worse than the impostor: the hospital had not identified any problem with her clinical work, and her performance had in fact put her in line for advancement, which commentators treated as an indictment of credentialism and the "trust the experts" posture of modern medicine.[1]
