# The Satanic Temple

**The Satanic Temple** is the organization that placed a Satanic display in the Iowa State Capitol building in December 2023.[1] The display consisted of a caped figure topped with a goat skull, an image commonly identified with Baphomet.[1]

## Iowa Capitol display and its destruction

[[michael-cassidy|Michael Cassidy]], described as a Christian veteran, beheaded the figure and threw the goat skull serving as its head into a bin.[1] Cassidy said he acted to "awaken Christians to the anti-Christian acts promoted by our government."[1] Rather than charging him with misdemeanor damage to property or vandalism, Polk County prosecutors charged Cassidy with felony third-degree criminal mischief, arguing the act was "in violation of individual rights" under Iowa's hate crime statute.[1] The Polk County Attorney's Office stated that evidence showed Cassidy made statements to law enforcement and the public indicating he destroyed the property because of the victim's religion.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] laid out both sides of the resulting [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] argument: on one reading the Baphomet display, however grotesque, was protected religious expression and private property housed on public grounds, making its destruction a vandalism motivated by religion and therefore a hate crime.[1] On the alternative reading, Cassidy as a follower of Jesus had a duty to stand against evil, the display's erection in a government building implied public consent to it, and his destruction of it was itself compelled religious expression — making his prosecution a government violation of the First Amendment.[1] She concluded that the ability to argue both sides is precisely why religious displays should be removed from government buildings, that the charges against Cassidy should be dropped, and that government should exit the business of regulating religion.[1]
