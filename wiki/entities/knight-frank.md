# Knight Frank

Knight Frank is a London-based real estate consultancy group whose research on Gulf construction markets is widely cited in business media.[1]

## Research on Saudi construction

In a research report published in June 2024, Knight Frank forecast that Saudi Arabia was set to become the world's largest construction market as the kingdom poured vast sums into projects intended to overhaul and diversify its economy.[1] The firm projected total Saudi construction output value at $181.5 billion by the end of 2028, nearly 30% above 2023 levels, driven largely by residential property and the so-called "giga projects" central to Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s [[vision-2030|Vision 2030]] program.[1] The firm noted the plan would need to accommodate a growing population and an expected influx of foreign tourists to succeed.[1]

Mohamed Nabil, Knight Frank's regional partner and head of project and development services for the Middle East and North Africa, said, "We are currently witnessing a historical transformation unfolding in Saudi Arabia with construction projects standing out in their design scale and value."[1] According to the firm's research, Saudi Arabia had launched projects worth more than $1.25 trillion in the eight years since Vision 2030 was introduced.[1]
