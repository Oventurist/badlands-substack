# Justice Democrats

Justice Democrats is a progressive political organization in the United States that recruits and backs primary challengers against incumbent Democratic members of [[congress|Congress]].[1]

## 2026 primary campaign

In 2026 the group backed [[melat-kiros|Melat Kiros]], who defeated Colorado Rep. [[diana-degette|Diana DeGette]] in the Democratic primary.[1] The upset prompted [[house-of-representatives|House]] Democrats to reassess their political strategy, with party members describing it as a warning sign that even established progressive incumbents are vulnerable to challenges from further left.[1] Progressive organizations, including groups operating in this space, pledged to continue targeting additional Democratic incumbents.[1]

## Commentary

Independent commentary in the Badlands Brief treated the Justice Democrats-backed victory as evidence of a socialist ascendancy within the Democratic Party, tying it to a broader claim that "around the nation, the commies are winning" heading into the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]].[1]

## Role in the "oppositional faction" critique (2023)

A 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized Justice Democrats as "political groomers" who took the [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] image and its outward philosophy and imbued [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] with it, creating a younger, female-of-color version of the Bernie brand in order to gain a foothold with Sanders' supporters.[2] The essay argued that when observers think of the supposed left-wing oppositional faction inside the Democratic Party, they think of politicians like Ocasio-Cortez and Sanders — and that the existence of any such faction was demolished when Sanders endorsed [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in August 2023 and publicly attacked the independent candidacy of [[cornel-west|Cornel West]].[2]
