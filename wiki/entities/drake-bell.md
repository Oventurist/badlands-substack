# Drake Bell

Drake Bell is an American actor who rose to prominence as a child star on [[nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]] programming. He was molested by dialogue coach [[brian-peck|Brian Peck]], a case that received renewed attention in March 2024 through an HBO documentary and subsequent court-record reporting on predators employed by the channel.[1]

Bell was later accused of grooming and sexually abusing a 15-year-old and was eventually convicted of distributing child pornography.[1] Badlands Media commentator [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] cited the trajectory from victim to offender as a clear example of how trauma travels from generation to generation, and argued that an industry which normalizes child abuse will eventually produce further abusers — a dynamic he associated with the multigenerational abuse patterns understood and engineered by MK Ultra programs.[1]
