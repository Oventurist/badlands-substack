# Kevin Annett

**Kevin Annett** is a Canadian author and researcher, cited by Badlands Media as the author of *Murder by Decree*, a book on abuses within the [[canadian-residential-schools|Canadian Residential Schooling system]].[1]

## Murder by Decree and the anonymous caller

In *Murder by Decree*, Annett describes an anonymous phone call from a man who claimed to be a retired member of the Canadian civil service.[1] The caller alleged that the torture of indigenous people was tied up in Department of National Defense projects, with the [[royal-canadian-mounted-police|RCMP]] delivering tortured victims into mind control programs.[1] He also pointed to the program's German origins, with its direction coming from the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

## Significance

Badlands Media presents Annett's findings as supplying a possible motive for the [[limited-hangout|limited hangout]] surrounding the 2022 mass grave narrative: that the coordinated story of murdered indigenous children served as cover for [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] programs.[1] The article holds that this theory was validated a year later when [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] published documents indicating MK Ultra targeted indigenous and black children in medical torture experiments.[1]
