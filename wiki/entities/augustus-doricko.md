# Augustus Doricko

Augustus Doricko is the chief executive of [[rainmaker-technology-corporation|Rainmaker Technology Corporation]], a cloud-seeding firm that drew intense public scrutiny after the [[2025-central-texas-floods|2025 Central Texas floods]].[1] He posts publicly on X under the handle @ADoricko.[1]

## Response to flood speculation

On the Saturday after the July 2025 flooding, Doricko posted that Rainmaker had suspended operations in accordance with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation's suspension criteria in order to ensure safety and prevent any risk of flooding from cloud seeding, and he published the full terms of those criteria.[1]

In the same thread, Doricko stated flatly that Rainmaker had conducted no operations that could have impacted the floods.[1] He described the company's final pre-flood mission as a brief cloud-seeding flight on the early afternoon of July 2 over the eastern portions of south-central Texas, in which two clouds were seeded and subsequently dissipated between 3:00 and 4:00 p.m. CDT.[1] He argued that because natural clouds rarely persist beyond a few hours and even durable storm systems seldom hold the same structure for more than 12 to 18 hours, the seeded clouds had dissipated more than a day before the storm complex responsible for the flooding rainfall formed.[1]

Badlands commentary noted that the resulting "debunking" was quickly repurposed by mainstream outlets to blame climate change and alleged NOAA funding cuts by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] instead.[1]

## Public denial after the Texas floods

Speaking out after the deadly flash floods that struck central Texas in early July 2025, Doricko said [[rainmaker-technology-corporation|Rainmaker]] — which works with Texas farmers during droughts — "unequivocally had nothing to do with the flooding."[2] The floods killed more than 80 people, with many still missing, as the Guadalupe River rose 20 to 26 feet in 90 minutes.[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] ridiculed Doricko for a subsequent analogy comparing "moving forward" after 109 deaths to football, declaring his political prospects finished.[2] See [[2025-central-texas-floods|2025 central Texas floods]].[2]
