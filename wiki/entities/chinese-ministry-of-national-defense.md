# Chinese Ministry of National Defense

The Chinese Ministry of National Defense is the government department of the People's Republic of [[china|China]] responsible for the external representation of the Chinese armed forces, including public statements on defense policy and military relations with foreign states.[1] Its spokesmen, drawn from the officer corps, are the principal channel through which Beijing signals its military intentions to foreign audiences.[1]

## Role in China–Russia military coordination (2023)

In late March 2023 the ministry's spokesman, Col. [[tan-kefei|Tan Kefei]], announced that China stood ready to deepen military cooperation with [[russia|Russia]] following the Moscow meeting between [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1] The ministry framed the deepening as implementation of the "important consensus reached by the two heads of state," with an emphasis on strengthened strategic communication and coordination.[1]

The ministry pointed to joint exercises and patrols as the practical vehicle for that cooperation, including the trilateral naval drills held with Russia and [[iran|Iran]] in the Gulf of Oman in mid-March 2023.[1] It simultaneously maintained that the relationship is not an alliance, describing it as governed by "non-alliance, non-confrontation, and non-targeting of third parties."[1]

The ministry's messaging featured in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of 3 April 2023, where it was contrasted with the internal preoccupations of the U.S. defense establishment under [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] and [[mark-milley|Mark Milley]].[1]
