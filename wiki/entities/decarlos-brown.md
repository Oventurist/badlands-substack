# Decarlos Brown

Decarlos Brown is the suspect charged in the August 2025 fatal stabbing of Ukrainian refugee [[iryna-zarutska|Iryna Zarutska]] aboard a Charlotte, North Carolina light rail train.[1]

## The Charlotte light rail stabbing

The unprovoked attack occurred shortly before 10 p.m. on August 22, 2025, according to the Charlotte-Mecklenburg Police Department, after a caller reported that a woman had been stabbed in the throat.[1] The incident was captured on video and is being investigated by the [[fbi|FBI]].[1]

Brown, 34, was taken to a hospital for treatment of a laceration and charged with first-degree murder.[1] He was homeless at the time of the stabbing, has a history of arrests according to court records, and suffered from mental health problems, family members told CNN.[1]

The case became a flashpoint in national debate over crime in large, predominantly Democratic cities as the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] vowed a crackdown.[1]
