# Børge Brende

Børge Brende is a Norwegian politician and international organization executive who served as president and chief executive officer of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] until his resignation in February 2026.[1]

## Resignation over Epstein connections

Brende stepped down on Thursday, 26 February 2026, after scrutiny of his past interactions with convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] Documents and releases tied to the "Epstein files" showed that Brende had dined with Epstein on multiple occasions and exchanged communications with him in 2018–2019, although Brende said he had been unaware of Epstein's criminal history.[1]

An internal review found no further concerns beyond what had already been disclosed, but Brende said he resigned so that the Forum could continue its work "without distractions."[1] [[alois-zwinggi|Alois Zwinggi]] was named interim chief executive while a permanent successor was sought.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry noted that the sequence began roughly three weeks earlier, when Brende himself requested an internal investigation into his own conduct following the release of the Epstein files.[1] He argued that the wave of European elite resignations, arrests, and attempted suicides was hard to interpret except as implying that those elites were guilty of something significant, though what exactly remained to be seen.[1]

## President of the WEF during the 2024 transition

At the time of [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s May 2024 step-back from day-to-day management, Brende was president of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], the office to which — alongside a managing board — full executive responsibility was being shifted under the Forum's planned governance evolution.[2]
