# Nawaf al Hazmi

Nawaf al Hazmi was an [[al-qaeda|al Qaeda]] operative and one of the hijackers in the [[september-11-attacks|September 11, 2001 attacks]].[1] He and Khalid al Mihdhar entered the United States in Los Angeles shortly after attending an al Qaeda summit meeting in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia in early January 2000, a fact known to the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and allegedly withheld from the [[fbi|FBI]].[1]

## The withheld intelligence

A sworn declaration by [[donald-canestraro|Donald Canestraro]], an investigator with the Department of Defense's [[military-commissions-defense-organization|Military Commissions Defense Organization]], gathered accounts from former FBI and CIA personnel alleging that top CIA officials including Director George Tenet intentionally kept this intelligence from the Bureau.[1] Weeks before the attacks, a New York FBI counterterrorism agent nearly "came over the table" at CIA officials blocking him from obtaining intelligence about the two men, later writing in an email, "Someone is going to die."[1] The pair took part in hijacking the American Airlines jet that was crashed into the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

The declaration supports the theory that the CIA remained silent because it was working with Saudi intelligence to recruit Hazmi and Mihdhar as informants — a channel used because the Agency is barred by law from conducting intelligence operations on US soil.[1]
