# Cracker Barrel

**Cracker Barrel** is an American restaurant chain that became the focus of a national branding controversy in August 2025 when it introduced, and then abandoned, a redesigned corporate logo.[1]

## The logo reversal

On Tuesday, August 26, 2025, Cracker Barrel announced that after listening to its customers it would scrap its new logo and retain the "Old Timer" figure.[1] In a statement to FOX Business the chain said: "We thank your guests for sharing your voices and love for Cracker Barrel. We said we would listen, and we have. Our new logo is going away and our 'Old Timer' will remain. At Cracker Barrel, it's always been – and always will be – about serving up delicious food, warm welcomes, and the kind of country hospitality that feels like family."[1] The company added that, "As a proud American institution, our 70,000 hardworking employees look forward to welcoming you to our table soon."[1]

## The "ConInc op" reading

Writing at his own Substack, Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] argued that the "Cracker Barrel rebrand controversy" was a [[con-inc|ConInc]] narrative operation rather than an organic consumer revolt.[1] He noted that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] responded to the controversy by preemptively taking personal credit for the good outcome that was sure to arrive, and contended that the praise lavished on Trump for "another amazing victory against 'the left'" would have been lavished on whatever candidate the GOP establishment picked in his place.[1] In Paul's account, the underlying idea was to sell a figure such as JD Vance, Ron DeSantis, or Vivek Ramaswamy as a leader capable of delivering "the rational, efficient change that can come from 'MAGA policies'" under someone more politically professional than Trump — a champion who could convince MAGA voters while still sounding like "the smart people" — and all of it would have worked in the prior paradigm.[1]

Paul described the episode as a narrative operation allowing Cracker Barrel "to gracefully accept punishment that sparks learning and movement toward a better tomorrow," so that "everyone moves forward," and observed that ConInc had run the same play with Bud Light, Target, Boeing "and the rest."[1] He declined to assert that these companies were innocent of what they were accused of, writing instead that there was "no reason to believe it just because Matt Walsh said so and Jesse Kelly backed him up and Donald Trump played along," and that the important focal point was why longstanding bad conduct was being surfaced now, in microcosm.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] commented that Paul's piece, though published under the guise of a treatise on the Cracker Barrel op, was in fact "a diagnosis of everything the MAGA and Truth Communities continually get wrong about how Donald Trump actually engages in Information Warfare."[1]
