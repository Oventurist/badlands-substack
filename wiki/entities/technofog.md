# TechnoFog

TechnoFog is a pseudonymous legal commentator and Substack writer whose work focuses on federal prosecutions, special counsel investigations, and what the writer characterizes as prosecutorial abuse by the United States Department of Justice.[1] TechnoFog's reporting is frequently excerpted by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news briefs.[1]

## Coverage of the Trump indictment

On June 8, 2023, TechnoFog published a piece titled "Donald Trump: 'I have been indicted'" reporting that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had announced on Truth Social that he had been indicted by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration in connection with the handling of government documents — a matter TechnoFog quoted Trump describing as "the Boxes Hoax" — and possibly for his response to a grand jury subpoena seeking documents bearing classification markings.[1]

TechnoFog wrote that although the indictment had been expected from a Justice Department that had instigated the investigation and appointed Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], it remained shocking because it represented new territory, adding that Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] had "done what his predecessors — Comey, McCabe, Mueller — could not."[1] The commentary concluded that, after years of what TechnoFog described as prosecutorial and investigative abuses, the Department of Justice had inserted itself into the 2024 presidential election and again disenfranchised millions of voters, calling it "a sad day for the country and a sobering day for those who wish for the equal administration of justice."[1]
