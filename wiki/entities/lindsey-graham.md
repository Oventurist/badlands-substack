# Lindsey Graham

Lindsey Graham is the senior United States Senator from South Carolina, a Republican long associated with an interventionist foreign policy. In the Badlands corpus he appears as a paradigm of the hawkish Republican establishment whose alignment with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is treated as tactical.

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Graham had been "radio silent" on the Iran–Israel conflict for a week or two because he faced a primary election that Tuesday, which he won after receiving Trump's endorsement. Once the primary was over and Trump announced plans to make peace with the Ayatollah, according to the Brief, "the knives are coming out," with Graham implying that Trump was a chickenhawk comparable to [[barack-obama|Obama]]. The commentary treats this as following the same attack vector used the previous day by other pro-Israel media figures, and as evidence that Graham's deference to Trump lasted only as long as his own electoral need for it.

## Middle East meetings, March 2026

The March 10, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Graham met Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] that month amid discussions of [[iran|Iran]] and regional security, urging stronger action against Iran including possible military strikes on its nuclear facilities. The meetings with Netanyahu prompted [[fox-news|Fox News]] host [[laura-ingraham|Laura Ingraham]] to question Sen. [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]] about what she called "secret meetings" between the senator and the Israeli leader; Cruz replied that such contacts are routine congressional diplomacy.

Commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats Graham as the clearest example of what he calls GOP figures who "have sold out our country to [[israel|Israel]]." Arguing that the Saudi royal family conducts diplomacy privately, he contends that publicizing a proposal on television and social media could not succeed, and that Graham's conduct "suggests a level of desperation that is not only dangerous, but likely doomed to fail." The same commentary notes Graham's claim that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been emboldened by his operation against [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]] in [[venezuela|Venezuela]], and reads Trump's posture as a deliberate trap that lured war advocates onto "the Forever War rug." In a separate item, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] writes that "despite Lindsey Graham and his donors' best efforts, the President always seems at least a few steps — or years — ahead."
