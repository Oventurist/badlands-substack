# Lindsey Graham

Lindsey Graham is the senior United States Senator from South Carolina, a Republican long associated with an interventionist foreign policy. In the Badlands corpus he appears as a paradigm of the hawkish Republican establishment whose alignment with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is treated as tactical.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports that Graham had been "radio silent" on the Iran–Israel conflict for a week or two because he faced a primary election that Tuesday, which he won after receiving Trump's endorsement. Once the primary was over and Trump announced plans to make peace with the Ayatollah, according to the Brief, "the knives are coming out," with Graham implying that Trump was a chickenhawk comparable to [[barack-obama|Obama]]. The commentary treats this as following the same attack vector used the previous day by other pro-Israel media figures, and as evidence that Graham's deference to Trump lasted only as long as his own electoral need for it.
