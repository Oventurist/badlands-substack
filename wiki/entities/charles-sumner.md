# Charles Sumner

Charles Sumner was a United States Senator present at the midnight session in the State Department on 29-30 March 1867 at which the treaty for the [[alaska-purchase|purchase of Alaska]] was drafted, alongside Secretary of State [[william-seward|William Seward]] and Russian Ambassador [[eduard-de-stoeckl|Eduard de Stoeckl]].[1]

In the [[alabama-claims|Alabama Claims]] affair of 1869-1871, Sumner and Seward both advocated that Britain surrender its Canadian possessions as payment for its wartime support of the Confederacy; weaker figures in America instead settled for $15 million in 1872.[1]
