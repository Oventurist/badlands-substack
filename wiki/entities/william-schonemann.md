# William Schonemann

William Schonemann, known in his community as "Pastor Bill," was a 76-year-old Arizona pastor murdered in his New River home in April 2025.[1]

## Death

Schonemann was found dead in his bed, covered in blood, on April 28, 2025 during a welfare check.[1] [[adam-christopher-sheafe|Adam Christopher Sheafe]], 51, later confessed in a jailhouse interview that he had crucified Schonemann inside the home, saying he drove from Phoenix and "executed him" around two in the morning on a Sunday night.[1] Sheafe characterized the killing as part of a self-described divine mission to punish religious leaders he believed were misleading their followers, and said he had assembled a broader hit list of clergy he intended to kill.[1]

The Badlands News Brief of June 25, 2025 carried the account of the confession as a bonus item alongside its coverage of the day's principal stories.[1]
