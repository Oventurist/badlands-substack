# Mid Vermont Christian School

Mid Vermont Christian School (MVCS) is a private Christian school in Vermont whose girls basketball team drew national attention in February 2023 when it withdrew from a state playoff tournament rather than play against a team with a transgender student-athlete.[1]

## 2023 tournament withdrawal

The MVCS girls basketball team withdrew from the Vermont Division IV state tournament because of its refusal to play an opponent with a transgender student-athlete.[1] The No. 12 seed Eagles had been scheduled to play a first-round game at No. 5 seed Long Trail; the withdrawal forfeited the game to the Mountain Lions.[1]

MVCS head of school [[vicky-fogg|Vicky Fogg]] wrote in an email to the Valley News, "We believe playing against an opponent with a biological male jeopardizes the fairness of the game and the safety of our players," adding that "allowing biological males to participate in women's sports sets a bad precedent for the future of women's sports in general."[1] Fogg declined a request for an interview.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] praised the forfeiture as "exactly what parents would've done the whole time in a braver world," arguing that a coach unwilling to take the same stand "doesn't care about your kid" and that parents should back coaches who do.[1] He acknowledged that families taking such a position would be called names and might forgo a championship, but held that refusing to sacrifice principle and player safety was "a bigger win long-term" on an issue worth that sacrifice.[1] The episode became a reference point in wider disputes over [[transgender-athletes|transgender participation in women's sports]].[1]
