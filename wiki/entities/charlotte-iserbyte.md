# Charlotte Iserbyte

**Charlotte Iserbyte** is cited in Badlands Media commentary as the author of [[the-deliberate-dumbing-down-of-america|The Deliberate Dumbing Down of America]], a book recommended by the contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] in his article on the left-right paradigm.[1]

The article credits her with compiling hundreds of references demonstrating that hardcore Soviet methods of brainwashing were in use in American public schools by the 1990s, including at the school the author himself attended.[1] Her work is invoked as evidence for the claim that the ideological formation of American schoolchildren — teaching them to hate their ancestry and to feel guilt for the destruction of the world — is a deliberate program rather than an incidental byproduct of curriculum.[1]
