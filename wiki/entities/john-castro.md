# John Castro

John Anthony Castro is a Texas tax attorney who sought the 2024 Republican presidential nomination and became known primarily for a nationwide campaign of lawsuits seeking to bar [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from appearing on the 2024 ballot.[1]

## Castro v. Trump

In August 2023 Castro petitioned the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] in *John Castro v. Donald Trump*, asking the justices to decide whether a political candidate may challenge the eligibility of another candidate of the same party seeking the same nomination "based on a political competitive injury in the form a diminution of votes."[1] The case was distributed to the justices for conference ahead of the term beginning October 2, 2023, with conference scheduled for September 26 and a decision expected on or before October 9.[1]

The substantive theory of Castro's suit rested on section three of the [[fourteenth-amendment|14th Amendment]], which disqualifies from public office individuals who have "engaged in insurrection or rebellion" against the United States.[1] Although Trump had not been charged with insurrection, Castro pointed to Trump's role in the January 6, 2021 Capitol riot as the basis for disqualification.[1] The filing was reported as part of a broader legal debate over whether Trump could lawfully appear on the 2024 ballot.[1]
