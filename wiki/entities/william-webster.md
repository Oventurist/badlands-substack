# William Webster

William Webster is a former Director of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] and of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]], serving under Presidents Ronald Reagan and George H. W. Bush.[1]

## 2024 endorsement of Kamala Harris

Webster signed the September 2024 open letter through which more than 100 Republican former national security figures endorsed Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and declared [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] unfit for public office.[1] See [[2024-republican-national-security-endorsement-of-harris|2024 Republican National Security Endorsement of Kamala Harris]].

## Career

Webster was the only US official ever to head both the [[fbi|FBI]] and the [[cia|CIA]].[2] Born on March 6, 1924, in St. Louis, Missouri, he served as a US Navy lieutenant in World War II and the Korean War, earned a law degree from Washington University in 1949, and worked as a federal prosecutor and district judge before entering intelligence leadership.[2] He became FBI Director in 1978 as the bureau reeled from corruption scandals over warrantless surveillance and break-ins; six weeks into his tenure a federal grand jury indicted former bureau officials for illegal raids targeting associates of far-left fugitives.[2] After nearly a decade at the FBI he led the CIA from 1987 to 1991 under Presidents Ronald Reagan and George H. W. Bush.[2] Webster said after accepting the CIA post that directors of the CIA and FBI should be ready to resign if asked to do something they know is wrong.[2]

## Death and Badlands commentary

Webster died on Friday, August 8, 2025, at the age of 101, his family announced.[2] They described him as a beloved husband, father, grandfather, great-grandfather, and patriot, and the FBI called him a "dedicated public servant" who spent more than 60 years serving the country and for whom it would be "forever grateful."[2]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the death as notable rather than merely ceremonial, cataloguing the events that bracketed Webster's tenure: CIA Director during the Reagan administration (with George H. W. Bush as vice president) and the Bush presidency, the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra affair]], the Gulf War, and the collapse of the USSR — which the contributor tied to the creation of Ukraine — as well as FBI Director during the crack epidemic.[2]
