# Jay Clayton

Jay Clayton is the US Attorney for the Southern District of New York.[1]

## Singham investigation

Clayton initiated the 2026 federal grand jury probe in Manhattan into [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]]'s funding network, an inquiry authorized by Acting US Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]].[1] The investigation examines possible wire fraud, bank fraud, money laundering, and related violations connected to roughly $278 million routed from Shanghai into US nonprofits and activist organizations, with subpoenas issued for bank records and financial documents from entities in the network.[1]

## DNI confirmation hearing (July 2026)

Walter "Jay" Clayton III, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nominee for Director of National Intelligence, faced pointed questioning before the [[senate-intelligence-committee|Senate Intelligence Committee]] on Wednesday, July 15, 2026, with Democratic lawmakers repeatedly pressing him on the 2020 presidential election.[2] Clayton acknowledged that the election was certified and that Joe Biden was certified as president, but rejected efforts to characterize him as an "election denier."[2]

The hearing also examined Clayton's record as US Attorney for the Southern District of New York, including his authorization of subpoenas targeting *New York Times* reporters during a leak investigation, which he defended as a national security matter rather than an attack on the press.[2] Senators questioned him about election-related investigations involving former DNI [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]], though Clayton said he had only recently been briefed on the matter.[2] The hearing marked another step in the Senate's consideration of his nomination to lead the US intelligence community.[2]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the grilling — over Clayton's refusal to disavow election denialism to senators' satisfaction, despite his stating he is not an election denier — as a bright signal of what "the machine" feared Trump might disclose in his [[july-2026-trump-prime-time-election-address|prime time address]] that evening.[2]

## Canceled DNI confirmation hearing (June 2026)

In June 2026 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that he was canceling Clayton's Senate confirmation hearing to become director of national intelligence, a move that further complicated efforts to reauthorize [[section-702|Section 702]] of the [[foreign-intelligence-surveillance-act|Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act]], which had lapsed the previous week.[3] Trump said he did not want to remove Clayton from his post as US attorney for the Southern District of New York until his preferred replacement, [[jamie-mcdonald|Jamie McDonald]], was confirmed, stating that the administration "will not be going forward until Jamie McDonald is approved to be US Attorney."[3] The decision left [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] on track to assume the role of acting director of national intelligence that Friday, following [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s departure.[3]
