# America Votes

America Votes is a major nonprofit voter mobilization organization in the United States.[1]

## Federal subpoena

Federal officials issued a subpoena to America Votes as part of a widening investigation tied to the [[ohio-organizing-collaborative|Ohio Organizing Collaborative]], according to sources familiar with the probe.[1] A former director and board member described the scope of the investigation as extensive and wide-ranging.[1] Badlands presented the subpoena as related to the broader federal scrutiny of voter registration operations that also produced the [[skid-row-voter-fraud-investigation|Skid Row voter fraud investigation]] in Los Angeles.[1]
