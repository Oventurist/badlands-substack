# America Votes

America Votes is a major nonprofit voter mobilization organization that coordinates progressive get-out-the-vote and voter-registration efforts across state affiliates.

## 2026 federal subpoena

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that federal officials issued a subpoena to America Votes as part of a widening investigation tied to the [[ohio-organizing-collaborative|Ohio Organizing Collaborative]], according to sources familiar with the probe. A former director and board member described the scope of the inquiry as extensive and wide-ranging. The Brief presents the subpoena as a national counterpart to the localized [[skid-row|Skid Row]] operation in [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]], framing both as strands of a single expanding [[fbi|FBI]] and [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] effort against what it calls the voting-rights network.
