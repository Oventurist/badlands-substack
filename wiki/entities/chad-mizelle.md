# Chad Mizelle

Chad Mizelle is the chief of staff of the United States [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] during the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Role in transgender medicine investigations

In July 2025, as the Department of Justice announced more than twenty subpoenas to doctors and clinics suspected of performing transgender medical procedures on minors, Mizelle confirmed that the administration was preparing draft legislation and "working with Congress" on existing criminal statutes related to female genital mutilation in order to "more robustly protect children from chemical and surgical mutilation."[1] His remarks came the same day the [[federal-trade-commission|Federal Trade Commission]] held a Washington, D.C. workshop on the "Dangers of 'Gender-Affirming Care' For Minors."[1] Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] separately said that medical professionals and organizations that "mutilated children in the service of a warped ideology" would be held accountable.[1]
