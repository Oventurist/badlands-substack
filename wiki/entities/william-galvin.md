# William Galvin

**William Galvin** is the Secretary of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, the state's chief elections officer.[1]

## 2024 Trump ballot challenge

In January 2024, a bipartisan group of Massachusetts voters represented by Boston attorney [[shannon-liss-riordan|Shannon Liss-Riordan]] petitioned the [[massachusetts-supreme-judicial-court|Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court]] to declare [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ineligible for the state's March presidential primary ballot and to order Galvin "to take all actions necessary to effectuate Trump's removal." The challengers had already lost before a state panel the previous week.[1]

Galvin's office was thus positioned as the ministerial actor who would have been required to strike Trump's name had the court ruled for the petitioners — a structure mirroring the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado case]], in which the state's secretary of state was similarly the target of a removal order.[1]
