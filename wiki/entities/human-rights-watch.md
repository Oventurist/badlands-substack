# Human Rights Watch

Human Rights Watch is an international human rights organization whose reporting on [[hong-kong|Hong Kong]] was cited by Badlands Media as documentation of the territory's transformation under Chinese control.[1]

## Reporting on Hong Kong

In a report published one year after the imposition of the [[hong-kong-national-security-law-2020|National Security Law]], Human Rights Watch noted that the Chinese government is pressing residents to pledge public loyalty to the government in Beijing, and that it is turning the police and courts into "tools of Chinese state control rather than independent and impartial enforcers of the rule of law."[1] The organization's findings were paired in the article with accounts that candidates considered insufficiently loyal to [[china|China]] have been barred from running for Hong Kong's electoral council, that academic freedom is under attack, and that websites have been blocked, museums harassed, films canceled, political slogans banned, and school curricula rewritten.[1]
