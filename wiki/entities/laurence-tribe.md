# Laurence Tribe

Laurence Tribe is a Harvard law professor and prominent liberal constitutional scholar who became one of the leading academic advocates for barring [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the ballot under Section 3 of the Fourteenth Amendment.[1]

## The disqualification theory

Tribe insisted publicly that the basis for barring Trump from office under the 14th Amendment was "unassailable."[1] In the February 2024 oral argument before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]], however, the justices gave the disqualification effort what legal commentator [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] described as "a chilly reception that bordered on the glacial," leaving open the possibility that the entire effort would be defeated unanimously.[1] Even liberal justice [[ketanji-brown-jackson|Ketanji Brown Jackson]] characterized the disqualification effort as anti-democratic.[1]

The gap between Tribe's confident framing and the Court's evident skepticism was cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as an example of the establishment's legal campaign against Trump collapsing under its own weight rather than being vindicated on the merits.[1]
