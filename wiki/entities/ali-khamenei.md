# Ali Khamenei

Ayatollah Ali Khamenei was the Supreme Leader of [[iran|Iran]] and the country's paramount political and religious authority. His reported death became one of the central and most contested claims of the opening week of the [[iran-war|U.S.–Israeli war on Iran]] in March 2026.[1]

## Reported death in the 2026 strikes

According to reporting summarized in the Badlands Brief of March 4, 2026, a major attack over the weekend of the campaign killed Khamenei, and Iranian state media confirmed his death. No official photograph of his body was released, a gap that fed intense speculation online.[1] [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]], speaking at an Oval Office press conference alongside German Chancellor [[friedrich-merz|Friedrich Merz]], confirmed that Khamenei had died in an Israeli strike and further asserted that most potential future Iranian leaders were already dead.[1]

## Information environment

The absence of visual confirmation made Khamenei's death a magnet for misinformation. AI-generated or recycled images purporting to show his body under rubble circulated widely alongside fabricated footage of destroyed U.S. ships and aircraft, downed jets, and Iranian missile impacts — much of it later debunked as old footage, video game clips, or outright fabrication.[1] Badlands contributor Burning Bright argued that this "fog" was not incidental but the defining feature of the conflict, describing a narrative haze in which even legacy outlets conceded that reporting out of Tehran was confusing at best and digitally manufactured at worst.[1]

## Death in the March 2026 strikes

Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei was killed in the coordinated United States and Israeli air and missile strikes on [[iran|Iran]] launched on Friday, February 27, 2026, as part of the campaign reported in Badlands commentary as [[operation-epic-fury|Operation Epic Fury]].[2] US officials confirmed that the campaign targeted military leadership, missile systems, and nuclear-related infrastructure, and that more than 1,000 targets had been struck since it began.[2] Following Khamenei's death, intelligence assessments warned of Iranian retaliatory attacks on US positions, and Iran subsequently launched missile and drone attacks against American and allied forces in the region.[2] Iranian Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] said that attempts at regime change through external force would be "mission impossible."[2]

## Succession

Khamenei's death in late February 2026, during Israeli military strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities, opened a leadership transition governed under Iran's constitution by the [[assembly-of-experts|Assembly of Experts]], the clerical body responsible for appointing and supervising the supreme leader.[3] Iran's clerical leadership was still working through the selection process in early March 2026.[3]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said the United States should be involved in determining Khamenei's successor, telling Axios: "We want someone that will bring harmony and peace to Iran. They are wasting their time. Khamenei's son is a lightweight. I have to be involved in the appointment, like with Delcy [Rodríguez] in Venezuela."[3] Badlands analyst [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the remarks as the codification of the [[venezuela-model|Venezuela Model]] for Iran's transition.[3]

## Earlier framing in the Venezuela template

In a January 11, 2026 essay cited again in June 2026 commentary, Burning Bright had asked whether Trump intended to "spirit away Khamenei, only to leave the rest of his ruling party in control," rug-pulling the globalist-planned version of an Iranian color revolution — a framing later invoked as evidence that the [[venezuela-model|Venezuela Model]] was being applied to Iran.[4]

## Warning to Trump during the Geneva talks (February 2026)

As the second round of indirect United States–Iran nuclear negotiations concluded in Geneva in February 2026, Khamenei warned President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that Iran possesses weapons capable of sending American warships to the bottom of the sea.[5] The warning came amid claimed diplomatic progress, with Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] reporting an understanding on core principles, and coincided with the launch of the [[maritime-security-belt|Maritime Security Belt 2026]] naval exercises by Iran, Russia and China in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[5]

## Protest crackdown and reported Moscow exit plan (January 2026)

During the [[2026-iran-protests|nationwide protests]] of early January 2026, demonstrators chanted "Death to the dictator," a direct reference to Khamenei.[6] In a weekend speech he condemned the protesters, saying "a number of agitated people, enemy mercenaries, had positioned themselves behind bazaar merchants and chanted slogans against Islam, against Iran and against the Islamic Republic."[6] He drew a distinction between dissent and disorder — "Protest is legitimate, but protest is different from rioting" — and instructed that "officials should speak with protesters. Speaking with a rioter is pointless. Rioters must be put in their place."[6]

British media, citing unnamed Iranian intelligence sources, reported that Khamenei planned to flee to Moscow if he lost control of the country, a claim Badlands flagged while noting that the identity of the report's author warranted consideration.[6] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] posted on Truth Social that if [[iran|Iran]] killed peaceful protesters the United States "will come to their rescue. We are locked and loaded and ready to go."[6]

## Statement on the October 7 attacks

On Saturday, October 7, 2023, the Supreme Leader of [[iran|Iran]] issued a statement on X saying, "God willing, the cancer of the usurper Zionist regime will be eradicated at the hands of the Palestinian people and the Resistance forces throughout the region."[7] X ruled that the post violated the platform's terms but allowed it to remain up.[7] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the statement alongside the [[iran-6-billion-fund-release|$6 billion fund release]] as evidence against the claim that unfrozen Iranian money would be confined to humanitarian uses.[7]

## Succession picture at the time of the Raisi crash (2024)

At the time of the May 2024 helicopter crash that killed President [[ebrahim-raisi|Ebrahim Raisi]], Badlands commentary described Khamenei as effectively in hospice care, making the question of his successor immediate.[8] Raisi — a conservative hardliner and protégé of Khamenei — had been widely expected to be selected as the next Supreme Leader by the [[assembly-of-experts|Assembly of Experts]], and his death was said to substantially change the political landscape of Iran.[8] Former President [[hassan-rouhani|Hassan Rouhani]] had once been the odds-on favorite for the post before Iranian courts disqualified him in January 2024.[8]

## Russian appeal for restraint (August 2024)

In August 2024, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] asked Khamenei for a restrained response to Israel's suspected killing of [[hamas|Hamas]] leader [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]] in Tehran, and specifically advised against attacks on Israeli civilians.[9] Two senior Iranian sources told Reuters that the message was delivered by Kremlin envoy [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]] in meetings with top Iranian officials while Iran weighed its response.[9] The appeal came amid a broader Western and regional diplomatic push, including direct messages from Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] to both [[iran|Iran]] and [[israel|Israel]], aimed at preventing all-out war.[9]

## Response to the June 2025 Israeli strikes

After Israel struck Iranian nuclear and military sites in Tehran on June 13, 2025, killing [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|IRGC]] commander [[hossein-salami|Hossein Salami]] and other senior figures, Khamenei warned that [[israel|Israel]] would face a "severe punishment" for its actions, and Iran vowed to respond harshly.[10] See [[june-2025-israeli-strikes-on-iran|the June 2025 Israeli strikes on Iran]].[10]

## The post-Raisi election dilemma (2024)

Following the death of President [[ebrahim-raisi|Ebrahim Raisi]] in May 2024, Khamenei faced a constitutionally mandated presidential election within 50 days and two options, each carrying risk.[11] He could open the field to all candidates from hard-liners to reformists, risking a competitive contest that might take [[iran|Iran]] in a direction he did not want, or he could again disqualify reformist rivals and even moderate, loyal opposition figures, risking the embarrassment of still lower turnout interpreted as a rebuke of his increasingly authoritarian state.[11]

## Order for a direct attack on Israel (July 2024)

According to a report on Wednesday, July 31, 2024, Khamenei commanded [[iran|Iranian]] forces to launch a "direct" attack against [[israel|Israel]], with three Iranian officials confirming the situation to The New York Times.[12] The order came hours after the assassination of [[hamas|Hamas]] leader [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]] in Tehran.[12] The White House had been unable to independently verify Haniyeh's death as of that Wednesday afternoon, while Tehran placed the blame on the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]]; Israel had struck Beirut the previous day but neither claimed responsibility for nor denied the Haniyeh killing.[12]

Khamenei's English-language X account said: "The criminal, terrorist Zionist regime martyred our dear guest in our territory and has caused our grief, but it has also prepared the ground for a severe punishment."[12]

## Threats of retaliation, April 2024

In a speech on April 10, 2024, Khamenei warned that the "evil regime" of [[israel|Israel]] "must be punished and will be punished" for the April 1 airstrike on the Iranian embassy compound in Damascus, which killed a senior general and six other officers of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Revolutionary Guards]].[13] The warning prompted U.S. officials to voice alarm over an imminent strike on Israeli interests and led President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to pledge "ironclad" American support for Israel, while Israeli Foreign Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] replied that if Iran attacked from its own territory, Israel would strike Iran in return.[13]

## Economic pressure in early 2024

As Iran waited on high alert in late January 2024 to see how [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would respond to the killing of three US servicemen by a Tehran-backed militia in Syria, the Iranian rial fell to its weakest level against the dollar in forty years, having lost 15 percent of its value since October 7, 2023.[14] With inflation running at 40 percent, Khamenei called for tighter controls on liquidity at a meeting with business leaders, reflecting his concern that inflation was crushing living standards and creating a difficult atmosphere ahead of nationwide parliamentary elections.[14]

## Response to the June 2025 Israeli campaign
During Israel's [[operation-rising-lion|Operation Rising Lion]] air campaign in June 2025, Khamenei said Iran would not surrender and warned that any US military intervention would bring irreparable consequences.[15] His statement came as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly repeated a demand for Iran's unconditional surrender while reportedly approving attack plans without issuing a final order.[15]

## Response to the Haniyeh assassination (2024)

Following the [[assassination-of-ismail-haniyeh|assassination of Ismail Haniyeh]] in Tehran during the inauguration of Iran's new president, Khamenei ordered retaliation directly against [[israel|Israel]], a response Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] attributed to how damaging the strike was to Iran's national honor.[16] DeLarme described the episode as an embarrassing security failure in what should have been one of the most secure locations in the country, and treated Khamenei's assurance of an imminent response — alongside [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] missile tests against targets in northern Israel — as evidence the region was moving toward a wider war.[16]
