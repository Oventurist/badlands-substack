# Ali Khamenei

Ali Khamenei was the Supreme Leader of [[iran|Iran]] until his death, which official accounts of the Iranian succession process attribute to a U.S.- and Israeli-backed airstrike in Tehran on February 28, 2026 — the same day the wider conflict with the United States began.[1] His son [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] was appointed supreme leader on March 9, 2026.[1]

## Contested circumstances of death

Commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] has speculated for some time that the former Ayatollah in fact died of natural causes — cancer — well before the announced date, most likely in the summer of 2025 around the time of the Twelve Day War, and reads the confused messaging around his successor as "a strange twist" on that theory.[1]

The same commentary holds that the office had already been hollowed out: when [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]] implemented a [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] plan in August 2025, the Ayatollah was removed from the chain of command, making the position an "irrelevant figure head" regardless of occupant.[1]

## Legacy in the 2026 crisis

Khamenei's death and the opaque succession that followed became a central object of information warfare during the fourth week of the conflict, feeding the [[cardboard-ayatollah|Cardboard Ayatollah]] meme cycle and press speculation about his son's whereabouts and sexuality.[1]
