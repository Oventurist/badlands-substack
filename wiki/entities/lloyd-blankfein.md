# Lloyd Blankfein

Lloyd Blankfein is a Harvard alumnus and the former chief executive of [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]].[1] "The Harvard Control Grid" cites him in connection with the appointment of former Harvard president [[drew-gilpin-faust|Drew Gilpin Faust]] to Goldman's board, noting that Blankfein — CEO at the time — described the relationship between the bank and the university as "symbiotic."[1]

The article uses the remark as evidence for its wider claim that Harvard's leadership and Wall Street's largest institutions operate as interlocking parts of a single structure rather than as separate spheres.[1]
