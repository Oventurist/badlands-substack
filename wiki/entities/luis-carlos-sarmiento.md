# Luis Carlos Sarmiento

Luis Carlos Sarmiento is a Colombian billionaire banker described in the Badlands Brief as worth roughly $10 billion.[1]

## Clash with Gustavo Petro

At a February 2026 press conference — the same event at which he disclosed an alleged assassination attempt — Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] publicly called out Sarmiento, telling the banker he could "go F yourself" if he intended to stand in the way of a pipeline project with Venezuela that Petro had discussed with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

The confrontation followed Petro's proposal to Trump for an international joint operation targeting the highest levels of organized crime — bankers, financiers, and corporate executives rather than street-level cartel figures — a framing under which figures such as Sarmiento sit near the top of the Badlands narrative about who ultimately resists such operations.[1]
