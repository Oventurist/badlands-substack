# Comcast

Comcast Corporation is an American telecommunications and media conglomerate, owner of NBCUniversal and therefore of [[nbc-news|NBC News]] and [[msnbc|MSNBC]].

## Role in the Big Six

A Swan Song for the MSM lists Comcast as one of the six conglomerates that, as of 2017, "lorded over all mainstream media, be it broadcast, print, internet, radio, or film," alongside [[time-warner|Time Warner]], [[walt-disney-company|Disney]], [[sony|Sony]], National Amusements (now [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]]) and [[news-corp|NewsCorp]]. The article argues that the appearance of competition among these firms is misleading because they share the same dominant institutional shareholders — [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]], [[state-street|State Street]] and [[berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] — and cites publicly available holder data for Comcast (CMCSA) in support. On this reading Comcast is one component of the "propaganda arm" of a single Western establishment rather than an independent commercial actor.
