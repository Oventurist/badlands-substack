# Starbucks Workers United

Starbucks Workers United is the labor union representing organized employees at [[starbucks|Starbucks]] stores in the United States.[1]

## June 2023 Pride decoration dispute

On June 13, 2023, NBC News reported that the union alleged Starbucks corporate management had asked workers in at least 21 states to take down Pride decorations over the preceding two weeks — a claim the company refuted.[1] According to the union, workers in Massachusetts were told there were not enough "labor hours" for employees to spend decorating, while in Oklahoma some were told "it was a safety concern to block windows with flags," citing threats against [[target|Target]] employees over that company's Pride displays as justification.[1]

Starbucks denied having a policy of reducing support for Pride observances, but the union published material it presented as evidence of corporate curbs on the displays.[1] The dispute was reported alongside claims that executives were focused on lifting the company's stock value following the Target and [[bud-light|Bud Light]] stock-market setbacks.[1]

Commentary published by Badlands treated the episode as largely anecdotal — "mostly anecdotal reporting about disgruntled employees" — while arguing that the more meaningful signal was the broader corporate retreat from public advocacy documented by the Wall Street Journal.[1]
