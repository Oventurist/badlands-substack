# In-Q-Tel

In-Q-Tel is the venture capital arm of the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]], created to invest agency money in emerging technology firms whose products may serve intelligence purposes.[1]

## Overlap with academia

In-Q-Tel is cited as direct evidence of the institutional fusion between the intelligence community and the modern university system.[1] [[michael-crow|Michael Crow]], the president of [[arizona-state-university|Arizona State University]], also serves as chairman of In-Q-Tel — a dual role that places the head of a major public research university at the top of the CIA's investment vehicle.[1] ASU is in turn identified as one of the institutions hosting [[pentagon|Pentagon]]-, [[state-department|State Department]]-, or CIA-funded programs that analyze and target "disinformation."[1]

## Significance

The In-Q-Tel/ASU overlap is presented as a case study in how the post-[[church-committee|Church Committee]] CIA operates: rather than acting directly, the agency works through cut-outs, venture vehicles, NGOs and universities, preserving plausible deniability while retaining influence over research agendas and technology development.[1]
