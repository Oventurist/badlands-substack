# Garuda Squad

The Garuda squad is an eagle-based counter-drone unit operated by the police of the southern Indian state of Telangana, described in January 2025 reporting as India's first and only bird squad and the world's second, after the Netherlands.[1]

## Formation and role

Police officials in Telangana successfully trained five eagles to identify and bring down rogue drones that could threaten the visits of Very Very Important Persons (VVIPs) or large public gatherings.[1] Named after the Garuda, a Hindu mythical sunbird, the squad took more than three years of painstaking effort to develop and now forms part of the [[telangana-police|Telangana police]]'s Intelligence Security Wing (ISW), the specialized force overseeing VVIP security.[1] Following the training's success, the state police's senior leadership received inquiries from the Indian Army, the Coast Guard and the paramilitary Border Security Force, each eager to establish similar bird squads.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the story simultaneously a meme — "a flock of eagles battling a swarm of killer drones is like something out of a Tolkien book" — and a genuine example of the creative ingenuity that could preserve humanity from a promised dystopian future.[1] He situated the eagle squads within the design discipline of [[biomimicry|biomimicry]], and closed by asking why India rather than the United States was innovating in the field, suggesting the American bald eagle could take out a swarm of Chinese drones.[1]
