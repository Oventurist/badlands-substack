# Abe Foxman

**Abe Foxman** is the longtime former chief of the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] (ADL), and is cited by Badlands Media as the leader under whom the organisation largely remained true to its historical priorities.[1]

Writing in the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] in 2016, Alex VanNess noted that under Foxman the ADL had kept faith with its founding mission of combating anti-Semitism, and contrasted this with the tenure of his successor [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]], under whom the group had shifted "onto a partisan course at odds with this core mission."[1] Foxman therefore functions in the article's argument as the dividing line between the ADL's earlier, more narrowly focused era and what Badlands Media describes as its transformation into an enforcer of the western [[censorship-regime|censorship regime]].[1]
