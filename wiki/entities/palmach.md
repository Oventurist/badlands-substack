# Palmach

The **Palmach** was one of the four Zionist paramilitary groups that Badlands Media names as responsible for the [[nakba|Nakba]], the 1947–48 campaign of violence against Palestinian civilians that accompanied the founding of Israel.[1]

## Role

Together with the [[haganah|Haganah]], the Palmach is described as furnishing tactical support to the [[irgun|Irgun]] and [[lehi|Lehi]] at the [[deir-yassin-massacre|Deir Yassin massacre]] in April 1948.[1] In May 1948 the Palmach combined with the other three militias to form the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli Defense Force]].[1]
