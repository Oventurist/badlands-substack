# Steve Jensen

Steve Jensen is a former senior official of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] who was dismissed in August 2025 and subsequently became a plaintiff in the [[fbi-retribution-lawsuit|FBI retribution lawsuit]].[1]

## Litigation

Jensen joined [[brian-driscoll|Brian Driscoll]] and [[spencer-evans|Spencer Evans]] in a federal complaint filed on September 10, 2025 alleging that their firings were part of a "campaign of retribution" carried out by FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] and Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] to remove agents tied to past investigations of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Like his co-plaintiffs, Jensen played a role in previous Trump-era investigations but denies any political motivation in his work.[1] The plaintiffs, represented by [[abbe-lowell|Abbe Lowell]], seek back pay and a declaration that the firings were unlawful.[1]
