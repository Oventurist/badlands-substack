# Scott Bessent

Scott Bessent is the United States Secretary of the Treasury in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, heading the [[treasury-department|Department of the Treasury]].

In the January 19, 2026 [[badlands-brief-02a|Badlands Brief]], [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identifies Bessent as the official who lifted United States sanctions on Venezuela following the removal of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]. He pairs this with Trump's January 9, 2026 executive order [[safeguarding-venezuelan-oil-revenue|"Safeguarding Venezuelan Oil Revenue for the Good of the American and Venezuelan People"]], which shields Venezuelan oil revenue from creditors, describing the combination as a coordinated "move against the global banking system."

Ghost argues that the lifting of sanctions is legally consequential because it reopens a civil action brought by Maduro against executives of banks, commodity trading houses, and oil companies, which had been dismissed without prejudice in 2019 on national-security grounds tied to the sanctions regime.

## Davos 2026

The January 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-577|Badlands Brief]] describes Bessent as playing a central diplomatic role at the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] in January 2026, working to steady global markets and allied governments amid the storm triggered by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s trade and foreign policy initiatives. Bessent repeatedly urged European leaders not to retaliate against Trump's threatened tariffs tied to the administration's pressure campaign over [[greenland|Greenland]], arguing that escalation would damage long-term relations, and sought to defuse speculation that European governments might sell U.S. Treasury holdings as leverage, dismissing such scenarios as exaggerated. On the sidelines he described U.S.–Europe relations as stronger than perceived and urged counterparts to "take a deep breath" about Washington's Greenland posture. He also publicly mocked California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]]'s criticism of Trump's diplomacy as lacking economic credibility, and suggested that [[alberta|Alberta]] could be a "natural partner" for the United States.

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the occasion to argue that Trump entered Davos "with two lions beside him" — Bessent and Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] — and cast Bessent as not merely the architect of Trump's "Golden Age" agenda but "the prime harbinger of the old System's demolition," recalling Bessent's early-2024 statement that he envisioned a "Global Economic Re-Ordering" and wanted to be part of it.
