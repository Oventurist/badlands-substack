# Scott Bessent

Scott Bessent is the United States Secretary of the Treasury in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, heading the [[treasury-department|Department of the Treasury]].

In the January 19, 2026 [[badlands-brief-02a|Badlands Brief]], [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identifies Bessent as the official who lifted United States sanctions on Venezuela following the removal of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]. He pairs this with Trump's January 9, 2026 executive order [[safeguarding-venezuelan-oil-revenue|"Safeguarding Venezuelan Oil Revenue for the Good of the American and Venezuelan People"]], which shields Venezuelan oil revenue from creditors, describing the combination as a coordinated "move against the global banking system."

Ghost argues that the lifting of sanctions is legally consequential because it reopens a civil action brought by Maduro against executives of banks, commodity trading houses, and oil companies, which had been dismissed without prejudice in 2019 on national-security grounds tied to the sanctions regime.
