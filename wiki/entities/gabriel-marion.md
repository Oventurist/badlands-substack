# Gabriel Marion

Gabriel Marion was the beloved nephew and adopted son of [[francis-marion|Francis Marion]], and a militiaman in his uncle's partisan band during the southern campaign of the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]].[1]

## Service and legacy

Gabriel fought alongside his legendary uncle from the age of fourteen, beginning before the signing of the Declaration of Independence and continuing well into manhood.[1] The character portrayed by Heath Ledger in [[the-patriot-2000-film|The Patriot]], "Gabriel Martin," was based on him; his arc in the film forms part of the Swamp Fox amalgamation, and the Patriot Chronicles promised to treat his story and heroic death in a future chapter.[1]
