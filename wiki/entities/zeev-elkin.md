# Ze'ev Elkin

Ze'ev Elkin is an Israeli cabinet minister associated with the Finance Ministry. He appears in the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]] as one of several Israeli officials whose public statements [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presents as evidence of disrespect toward President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## Statement on Gaza reconstruction

The Brief cites reporting that Elkin asserted [[israel|Israel]] will not financially contribute to Trump's [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], the US-backed postwar mechanism for [[gaza|Gaza]]. The commentator places this alongside Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]]'s declaration that Israel will ultimately occupy Gaza and reestablish settlements there, and the earlier formal rejection of the Board of Peace by senior officials in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s government, arguing that these positions collectively undermine the President's stated peace efforts in the region.
