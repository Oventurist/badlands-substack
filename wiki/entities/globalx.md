# GlobalX

**GlobalX** is a United States charter airline contracted by [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] to carry out deportation flights, and has been described as the Trump administration's airline of choice for removals.[1]

## Deportation flights to El Salvador

GlobalX was the carrier hired by the U.S. government to transport approximately 130 Venezuelan migrants to El Salvador on March 15, 2025, in an operation targeting alleged members of the [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang.[1] On May 1 a U.S. federal judge ruled that the reasoning behind those deportations was illegal.[1]

## Anonymous defacement

In early May 2025 the hacker collective [[anonymous|Anonymous]] defaced the landing page of a GlobalX subdomain, leaving a lengthy message protesting the removal of "alleged members of a Venezuelan gang" and referring to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a "fascist."[1] The hacktivists wrote "You lose again Donnie," alluding to the federal court ruling against the deportations.[1]
