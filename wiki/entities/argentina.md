# Argentina

Argentina is a South American republic that appears in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] corpus principally through its national football team and through the foreign policy of President [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]].

## 2026 World Cup final

Argentina entered the final of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] as the overwhelming favorite, defending the title it won in Qatar in 2022 and fielding [[lionel-messi|Lionel Messi]]. It lost 1–0 to [[spain|Spain]] after extra time at [[metlife-stadium|MetLife Stadium]], finishing the match with ten men after [[enzo-fernandez|Enzo Fernández]] received a second yellow card. Statistics circulated in the Brief showed the team without a shot on goal deep into the match.

Badlands commentary characterized Argentina's play as persistently and deliberately rough, alleging a years-long pattern in which referees permit Argentine players to push, shove and foul with impunity — a reputation the source says is widely recognized among soccer fans. The alleged motive attributed to [[fifa|FIFA]] is commercial promotion of Messi. The Brief also noted that the [[fbi|FBI]] had announced an investigation into the Argentine football association for money laundering in the United States. After the whistle, defender [[nahuel-molina|Nahuel Molina]] was described as punching the Spanish captain, and the *Daily Mirror* reported a confrontation involving midfielder [[leandro-paredes|Leandro Paredes]]. The commentary closed the section with the sardonic line, "Keep it classy, Argentina!"

## Political alignment

The Brief attributes elite support for Argentina in the final partly to Milei's close friendship with [[israel|Israel]], contrasted against Spain's leadership of a European trend away from unconditional support for the Israeli government.
