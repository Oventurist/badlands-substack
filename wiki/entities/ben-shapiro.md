# Ben Shapiro

Ben Shapiro is a conservative American political commentator, author, and media personality, widely described in the Badlands Media corpus as one of the most prominent figures in the "free speech" culture war and as a leading voice of the American right during the 2010s and 2020s.

## Role in the campus "safe spaces" controversy

The article "A Glaring Double Standard" recounts Shapiro as a central figure in the campus free speech disputes of the late 2010s. It states that when conservative personalities such as Shapiro appeared on college campuses they were greeted with "howls of execration" and demands that universities cancel their events, converting campuses into safe spaces not for minorities but "for anyone who disagreed with the speaker's opinions." The source reports that Shapiro had at least five major speaking engagements canceled by universities between 2017 and 2019, framing him as a principal target of the deplatforming dynamic it criticizes.

The article cites a February 2021 Daily Wire piece in which Shapiro commented on the firing by Disney of actress Gina Carano, saying the firing "is indicative of where we are in the culture, and it is a terrible moment for American culture," and that "we are now in the middle of a mass social movement to expel half of the American population from the body politic."

## Criticized for inconsistency after October 7

The author of "A Glaring Double Standard," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], writes that he generally agrees with Shapiro's free speech sentiments in the Carano instance, but argues that Shapiro's commitment to free speech was conditional. The article contends that conservative figures such as Shapiro, Bari Weiss, and Dave Rubin — who had earlier decried "safe spaces" and cancel culture — came to cheer or tacitly support the silencing of Israel critics after the October 7, 2023 Hamas attack, revealing, in the article's words, "that their commitment to free speech was always conditional, tethered to tribal loyalties rather than principle."
