# Ben Shapiro

Ben Shapiro is a prominent American conservative commentator and a co-founder of the [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]], described as having been at the forefront of the culture war over [[safe-spaces|safe spaces]].[1] Between 2017 and 2019 he had at least five major university speaking engagements cancelled amid student demands that campuses become safe spaces from opinions they disagreed with.[1]

## Anti-cancel-culture advocacy

In February 2021 the Daily Wire's Tim Pierce published "Shapiro: Gina Carano Firing Part Of A Movement To 'Expel' Half Of America," quoting Shapiro's response to Disney's firing of [[gina-carano|Gina Carano]] over an Instagram post comparing political hatred to pre-Holocaust Germany.[1] Shapiro called the firing "indicative of where we are in the culture," a "terrible moment for American culture," and part of "a mass social movement to expel half of the American population from the body politic."[1]

## Criticism

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] states he is not a Shapiro fan, objecting to a foreign policy outlook he equates with that of Joe Biden and John McCain, while agreeing with Shapiro's stated reasoning on safe spaces.[1] DeLarme names Shapiro alongside [[bari-weiss|Bari Weiss]] and [[dave-rubin|Dave Rubin]] as figures whose hypocrisy is revealed by their tacit support for silencing Israel critics after the [[october-7-attacks|October 7th attacks]], concluding their commitment to free speech was conditional and tethered to tribal loyalties.[1] [[eyal-yakoby|Eyal Yakoby]] is identified as one of Shapiro's biggest amplifiers.[1]
