# Saba Capital

Saba Capital is a hedge fund founded in 2009 by [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]] after his career as a credit derivatives trader at [[deutsche-bank|Deutsche Bank]].[1]

## Campaign against BlackRock closed-end funds

In 2024 Saba Capital sought to take control of the boards of directors of ten closed-end funds managed by [[blackrock|BlackRock]].[1] Weinstein's public rationale was that BlackRock had engineered an effectively unchallengeable board by manipulating the shareholder proxy process — counting every non-vote as a vote in favor of management's recommendations.[1] Because most fund shareholders never vote, and those who do generally know nothing about the directors and simply follow management's slate, incumbent boards are essentially never replaced.[1] The article likens the practice to counting every non-voter in a presidential election as a vote for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

For the board of BlackRock Capital Allocation Trust, Saba proposed [[shavar-jeffries|Shavar Jeffries]], chief executive of the [[kipp-foundation|KIPP Foundation]]; [[jennifer-raab|Jennifer Raab]], president of [[hunter-college|Hunter College]]; and Lieutenant Colonel [[alexander-vindman|Alexander Vindman]].[1]

## Interpretation

Badlands Media framed the campaign not as reform but as one [[deep-state|Deep State]]-aligned faction raiding another, arguing that Saba's real intention was to "churn and burn" the funds for profit at the expense of BlackRock and its investors.[1] The episode is presented alongside the [[london-whale|London Whale]] trade as evidence that establishment financial actors routinely betray one another while remaining densely interconnected.[1]
