# Andreessen Horowitz

Andreessen Horowitz is an American venture capital firm co-founded in 2009 by [[marc-andreessen|Marc Andreessen]] and [[ben-horowitz|Ben Horowitz]].[1]

## Political donations

In late 2023 the firm announced that its partners would begin making donations to political candidates they saw as "advancing technology," a departure from the firm's earlier posture of political non-involvement.[1] In July 2024, Axios reported that Andreessen and Horowitz would each donate to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s re-election effort, with The Information having reported earlier on the planned contributions to a pro-Trump political action committee.[1]

The firm's partners were described as "techno-optimists," and their move followed [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s endorsement of Trump and formation of a supporting PAC.[1] Axios framed the development as evidence that a significant chunk of the tech industry's money and power was lining up behind Trump, reversing Silicon Valley's long-standing Democratic tilt.[1] Badlands commentary linked this realignment to [[jd-vance|JD Vance]]'s selection as Trump's running mate and the longstanding backing of Vance by [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], suggesting a deliberate paradigm shift within Silicon Valley.[1]

## X shareholder disclosure (2024)

The venture capital firm was named among the nearly 100 entities holding a stake in [[x-corp|X]] when a federal judge ordered the shareholder list of X Holdings Corp. unsealed in August 2024, confirming its role in financing [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s $44 billion purchase of [[twitter|Twitter]].[2] Badlands noted that Andreessen and Horowitz had recently endorsed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], reading the timing of the disclosure as a deliberate signal in the closing stretch of the 2024 campaign.[2]
