# Steven Hoffenberg

Steven Hoffenberg was an American financier identified as one of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s early business partners.[1]

The [[new-york-times|New York Times]]' December 16, 2025 investigation into the origins of Epstein's wealth detailed his partnerships with figures including Hoffenberg and [[les-wexner|Les Wexner]], portraying Epstein as a college dropout who leveraged charm, lies and minor frauds to amass hundreds of millions of dollars.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that this framing reduced Epstein to a grifter while omitting his 1980s proximity to arms dealing, money laundering and intelligence-adjacent financial flows.[1]
