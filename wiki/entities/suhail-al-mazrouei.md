# Suhail Mohamed al-Mazrouei

Suhail Mohamed al-Mazrouei is the Energy Minister of the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]].[1] He was the official who publicly articulated the rationale for the Emirati withdrawal from [[opec|OPEC]] and OPEC+ announced on April 28, 2026.[1]

## OPEC withdrawal statement (April 2026)

Al-Mazrouei presented the withdrawal as a unilateral policy decision taken after a review of the country's energy strategies and production policies.[1] He cited frustrations over insufficient [[gulf-cooperation-council|Gulf Cooperation Council]] support against Iranian attacks and threats during the conflict with [[iran|Iran]], and acknowledged that the exit represented a blow to OPEC unity at a moment of historic energy shock caused by disruptions at the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]

The announcement coincided with U.S. Energy Secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]]'s assessment that the mined Strait could be reopened quickly by clearing a single shipping pathway, and with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s claim that Iran had described itself as being in a "State of Collapse."[1] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated al-Mazrouei's announcement as evidence of a broader nationalist realignment in global energy rather than a narrow wartime grievance.[1]
