# The Satanic Temple

The Satanic Temple is an organization that sponsors the [[after-school-satan-club|After School Satan Club]], a children's club promoted in public school districts that already host Christian clubs.[1]

## Legal strategy

The Temple's argument is that if a school district permits one religion to host events on its premises, it must allow all.[1] The organization has pursued this position with the backing of the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]], which filed suit on the Temple's behalf against Pennsylvania's [[saucon-valley-school-district|Saucon Valley School District]].[1]

In May 2023 a federal judge ruled that the district must allow the After School Satan Club to meet in district facilities; the ACLU announced the outcome on Monday as "a victory for free speech and religious freedom."[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[absolute-1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] cited the ruling as "further proof that we are in a spiritual war more than anything else."[1]

## December 2023 Iowa State Capitol display

In December 2023 the Temple installed a Christmas-season display featuring a Baphomet goat skull and a blood-red pentagram inside the Iowa State Capitol, prompting disbelief among Iowa residents and a public campaign against it.[2] Governor [[kim-reynolds|Kim Reynolds]], who had allowed the permitted installation, came under pressure to remove it, with state representative [[brad-sherman-iowa|Brad Sherman]] calling the display "evil," "disgusting" and "blasphemous" and seeking legislation to bar Satanic displays from the Capitol and all state-owned property.[2] The [[iowa-state-capitol-satanic-display-controversy|resulting controversy]] split commentary at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] between those who read it as deliberate occult provocation and those who defended the Temple's permit on First Amendment grounds.[2]

### Destruction of the display

On or about December 15, 2023, the pentagram-adorned Baphomet display was destroyed by [[michael-cassidy|Michael Cassidy]], a Christian military veteran who "beheaded" the caped goat figure and threw the skull in a bin, saying he acted "to awaken Christians to the anti-Christian acts promoted by our government."[3] Cassidy argued that "none of the founders would have considered government sanction of Satanic altars inside Capitol buildings as protected by the First Amendment."[3]

The Satanic Temple of Iowa acknowledged that the display had been "destroyed beyond repair" but said it was "proud to continue our holiday display for the next few days that we have been allotted," urging visitors to travel together for safety and to use the group's Seven Tenets "as a reminder for empathy, in the knowledge that justice is being pursued the correct way, through legal means"; the statement closed with "Hail Satan!"[3] Badlands writer [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] thanked Cassidy for the act, arguing that a comparable Christian shrine in the Capitol would have drawn immediate media demands for removal.[3]
