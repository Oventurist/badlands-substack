# Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan is a South Caucasus state closely aligned with [[turkey|Turkey]] and, as of early 2026, a destination for Turkish overseas air power.[1]

## Turkish F-16 deployment

Turkey's expansion of its defense footprint in early 2026 spanned Azerbaijan and [[somalia|Somalia]], with additional F-16 fighter jets deployed to both.[1] Badlands commentary described the pair as the northern and southern access points to the Middle East and as two critical trade corridors linking Asia to Europe, part of a wider Turkish repositioning away from [[nato|NATO]] and the EU and toward [[brics|BRICS]] and the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]].[1]

## Karabakh victory and the heroin corridor (Badlands analysis)

Badlands contributor Joe Lange presents Azerbaijan's victory in the [[second-karabakh-war|Second Karabakh War]] as one of the most consequential and least reported events of recent years.[2] Following the 44-day conflict, Azerbaijan regained control over a 130-kilometer (81-mile) section of its state border with [[iran|Iran]] that had been held by [[armenia|Armenia]] for about thirty years, and thereby blocked a drug trafficking route running from Iran through the Jabrayil district into Armenia and onward to Europe.[2] President [[ilham-aliyev|Ilham Aliyev]] reported that heroin seizures on other sections of the Azerbaijan-Iran border subsequently doubled, and said this proved that Armenia, in collusion with Iran, had used the occupied territories for drug trafficking to Europe.[2] Azerbaijani officials additionally stated they possessed concrete evidence that Armenian forces had employed terrorist fighters recruited from Lebanon, Syria and other Middle Eastern countries.[2] Azerbaijan liberated several cities and nearly 300 settlements before a Russian-mediated cease-fire on November 10, 2020, and later border clashes in September 2022 killed more than 200 people and prompted a snap visit to Armenia by [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]].[2]
