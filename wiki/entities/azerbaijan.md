# Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan is a South Caucasus state closely aligned with [[turkey|Turkey]] and, as of early 2026, a destination for Turkish overseas air power.[1]

## Turkish F-16 deployment

Turkey's expansion of its defense footprint in early 2026 spanned Azerbaijan and [[somalia|Somalia]], with additional F-16 fighter jets deployed to both.[1] Badlands commentary described the pair as the northern and southern access points to the Middle East and as two critical trade corridors linking Asia to Europe, part of a wider Turkish repositioning away from [[nato|NATO]] and the EU and toward [[brics|BRICS]] and the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]].[1]
