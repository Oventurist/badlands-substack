# Daily Caller

The Daily Caller is an American conservative news and opinion website whose reporting arm, the Daily Caller News Foundation, conducts original investigative work.[1] The outlet has featured prominently in coverage of the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]] controversy.[1]

## Hunter Biden laptop reporting

In October 2020 the Daily Caller News Foundation published a cybersecurity expert's authentication of a [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] email concerning [[burisma|Burisma]], more than a year before the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] and the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] acknowledged the laptop material's authenticity in March 2022.[1] The Daily Caller and other outlets subsequently used the material to report on the Biden family's extensive dealings with Chinese companies, including a presentation Hunter Biden gave promoting American shale and natural gas to Chinese businessmen.[1]

On 1 March 2023 the Daily Caller was first to obtain the 29 letters sent by [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] and [[house-intelligence-committee|House Intelligence Committee]] chairman [[mike-turner|Mike Turner]] to Central Intelligence officials who had discredited the laptop story, in which the lawmakers reiterated their requests for transcribed interviews and asked the officials to "comply promptly."[1]

## Reporting on CIA ethics referrals

In January 2024 the Daily Caller reported, on the basis of records it reviewed, that the [[cia|CIA]] appeared to have referred dozens of its employees to the Department of Justice over potential conflicts of interest during the Biden administration.[2] The outlet reported that between January 2021 and May 2023 the agency filed 32 OGE Form 202 conflict-of-interest referral notifications with the [[office-of-government-ethics|Office of Government Ethics]].[2] The records had been provided to the Daily Caller by the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]]'s [[oversight-project|Oversight Project]], which obtained information about the submissions through a Freedom of Information Act request.[2]
