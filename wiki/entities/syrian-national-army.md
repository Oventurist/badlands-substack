# Syrian National Army

The Syrian National Army is an umbrella group of Turkish-backed Syrian militias.[1] In the offensive that began in late November 2024, it fought alongside the jihadist group [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham|Hayat Tahrir al-Sham]] (HTS) in the sweep through northern and central Syria that produced the capture of much of [[aleppo|Aleppo]] and, on December 5, 2024, the central city of [[hama|Hama]].[1]

Its role illustrates the layered foreign sponsorship that Badlands commentary attributes to the Syrian conflict: alongside HTS, formerly the al-Qaeda affiliate Jabhat al-Nusra, Turkish-backed formations gave the offensive scale and coordination while the campaign advanced toward Homs and, beyond it, [[damascus|Damascus]].[1] Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] separately told [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] that Turkey was nonetheless working with Russia and Iran through the Astana process to stabilize the country, a tension Badlands commentary noted directly.[1]
