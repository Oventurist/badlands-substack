# Ynet

**Ynet** is an Israeli news website, described in Badlands Media coverage as Israel's largest online media outlet. It functions as the digital arm of the Yedioth Ahronoth publishing group and is one of the primary venues through which Israeli political and security officials speak to the domestic public.

## Role in the 2026 US-Iran peace coverage

Ynet features in the June 2026 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] reporting on the [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding-2026|US-Iran memorandum of understanding]]. Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited Ynet as the outlet where Israeli officials told reporters that they had "no input or influence" in the American-led peace process with [[iran|Iran]] — an admission the Badlands writer treated as significant evidence that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had negotiated an agreement that deliberately excluded [[israel|Israel]] from the table.

Ynet headlines were reproduced in the brief alongside reactions from Israeli political figures including [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], [[yair-lapid|Yair Lapid]], [[naftali-bennett|Naftali Bennett]] and [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]], who variously characterized the deal as a "strategic failure" and "terrible for Israel."

## Interpretive framing

In the Badlands reading, mainstream Israeli outlets such as Ynet are useful precisely because their domestic-facing coverage records elite frustration that is smoothed over in English-language reporting. The outlet is contrasted implicitly with what the brief calls "Mossad Media" in the United States — commentators such as [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]], [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] and [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] — whose reaction to the deal is read as an attempt to mobilize the [[israel-lobby|Israel Lobby]] and [[congress|Congress]] against it.
