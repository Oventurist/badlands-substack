# David Bell

**Dr. David Bell** is a public health physician, biotech consultant in global health, and Senior Scholar at the [[brownstone-institute|Brownstone Institute]].[1] He is among the most frequently cited technical critics of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]]'s pandemic preparedness and response proposals.[1]

## On the origin of lockdown policy

In an interview clip cited by Badlands Media, Bell argued that the COVID lockdown strategy did not originate with the WHO itself but with an outside source, stating: "The idea of lockdowns didn't come from the WHO, it came from these external influences."[1]

Ryan DeLarme used Bell's observation to support the article's broader contention that private funders exert decisive influence over WHO decision-making, since a majority of the organization's funding is "specified" — earmarked for particular programs rather than allocated where need is greatest.[1] In that framing, Bell's account of [[covid-19-lockdowns|lockdowns]] is presented as a documented case in which donor-driven priorities adversely affected public health outcomes.[1]
