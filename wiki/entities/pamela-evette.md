# Pamela Evette

**Pamela Evette** is the Lieutenant Governor of South Carolina and a candidate in the state's 2026 Republican gubernatorial primary, endorsed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Evette advanced to the run-off election scheduled for June 23, 2026.[1] Representative [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]], who finished last in the six-candidate first round with 12% of the vote after Trump endorsed one of her opponents, subsequently endorsed [[alan-wilson|Alan Wilson]] in the run-off, stating that she wanted to help him defeat the Trump-backed Evette.[1]
