# Pamela Evette

Pamela Evette is the Lieutenant Governor of South Carolina and a Republican candidate in the state's 2026 gubernatorial election.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] identifies Evette as the candidate endorsed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and reports that she advanced to the run-off scheduled for June 23, 2026. The Brief recounts that Representative [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]], who finished last in the six-candidate primary with 12 percent after Trump backed a rival, subsequently held a press conference endorsing [[alan-wilson|Alan Wilson]] specifically in order to help him defeat Evette — a step the newsletter presents as Mace's retaliation against Trump.
