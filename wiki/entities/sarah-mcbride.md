# Sarah McBride

Sarah McBride is the incoming member of Congress from Delaware and the first openly transgender person elected to that body.[1]

## Congressional bathroom dispute

McBride's election prompted a fight in Congress over restroom access in the Capitol.[1] In his [[time-magazine|TIME]] Person of the Year interview, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was asked whether he agreed with McBride's statement that lawmakers should be focused on more important issues; Trump answered, "I do agree with that. On that – absolutely. As I was saying, it's a small number of people."[1] Trump declined to engage with the bathroom question itself, saying the matter involved a very small number of people, had "ripped apart our country," and would have to be settled by whatever the law finally agreed.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treated the exchange as evidence that Trump chose not to exploit or demagogue a polarizing issue, viewing partisan culture-war disputes as secondary to what he framed as the real fight against entrenched institutional power.[1]
