# Naomi Klein

**Naomi Klein** is an author cited in the fourth and final installment of the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series, published by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] through [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on May 3, 2025.[1]

## Role in the article's argument

The essay invokes Klein's book *[[shock-doctrine|The Shock Doctrine]]* while describing what it calls the "4D" layer of control, in which the system shifts from governing space to governing time.[1] Klein is credited with documenting that disaster is the favored trigger for policy transformation: an event such as a pandemic, war, or economic collapse creates disorientation, and into that vacuum "a script is deployed… centralization, surveillance, and submission dressed as safety."[1]

The article places Klein's documentation alongside its claim that the [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]]'s own whitepapers describe crisis as an opportunity to install behavioral norms, arguing that in the fourth dimension the future is not predicted but scripted, and that populations are not merely reacting but rehearsing.[1]

## Framing

Klein is treated in the piece as a documentarian of mechanism rather than as an ally of its wider metaphysical thesis; her research is used as corroborating evidence for the "crisis-reaction-solution" pattern the series traces from the reflexive "2D" layer upward.[1] Badlands Media notes that contributed articles represent the opinions of their authors rather than the outlet itself.[1]

## Endorsement of Andreas Malm

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] notes that Klein became famous for her 2007 book *The Shock Doctrine*, which traced the origins of IMF shock therapy policies to the CIA's [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] program, in which victims were systematically broken down through electro-shock and other forms of psychological warfare.[2] Despite that subject matter, Klein has praised the Swedish eco-sabotage theorist [[andreas-malm|Andreas Malm]] as "one of the most original thinkers on the subject" of climate change.[2] Ehret regards this as an irony Klein has not reflected deeply upon, arguing that her ardent embrace of radical degrowth and green energy systems would itself deliver murderous economic shock therapy to the Earth's population.[2]
