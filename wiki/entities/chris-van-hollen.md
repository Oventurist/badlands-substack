# Chris Van Hollen

Chris Van Hollen is a Democratic member of the United States [[senate|Senate]] from Maryland.

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] names Van Hollen among the Democratic senators who questioned Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] at a Senate Appropriations Subcommittee hearing on February 10, 2026 concerning Lutnick's ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] as disclosed in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s document release. Together with [[chris-coons|Chris Coons]] and [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]], he pressed Lutnick on discrepancies between his testimony and his prior claims about when he had cut ties with Epstein, and accused him of a lack of transparency.
