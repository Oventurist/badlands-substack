# Vince McMahon

Vince McMahon is the founder of the professional wrestling promotion [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] (WWE/WWF) and the husband of [[linda-mcmahon|Linda McMahon]], who served as the organization's president and chief executive officer.[1]

## Relationship with Donald Trump

McMahon's promotion built a long-running on-screen relationship with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], beginning with WrestleMania IV, held in 1988 at Trump Plaza in Atlantic City.[1] The storyline later staged between the two men was billed as "The Battle of the Billionaires," a phrase Trump recalled in his 2013 WWE Hall of Fame induction speech and which Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read as foreshadowing of Trump's political career.[1] At the [[2024-republican-national-convention|2024 Republican National Convention]], Linda McMahon described Trump joining one of the "most compelling and highest-rated storylines" in the company's history, underscoring his continued ties to the McMahon family and professional wrestling.[1]

## WWE–UFC merger

In April 2023, McMahon's [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] agreed to merge with the [[ufc|UFC]] to form a new publicly traded company controlled by [[endeavor|Endeavor Group]].[2] Under the announced structure, McMahon would serve as executive chairman of the new company while [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]] served as chief executive.[2] The deal valued WWE at $9.3 billion and UFC at $12.1 billion and was expected to close in the second half of 2023.[2]
