# Manfredo Martin-Michael Madrigal III

Manfredo Martin-Michael Madrigal III is a former U.S. Army officer and attorney who was sentenced to 54 months in federal prison for willfully deleting Army materials, in a case announced by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and reported in January 2025.[1]

## Case

Madrigal, aged 38 at the time of sentencing, claimed the deletions occurred during a PTSD-related bout of alcoholism.[1] He also lied to authorities about contacting a foreign national, despite having previously called the Russian embassy in Washington, D.C.[1]

He had been assigned in February 2022 to the U.S. Army Judge Advocate General's (JAG) Legal Center and School, where he developed training materials.[1] Before that assignment he had served overseas on sensitive operations as an enlisted paratrooper with the U.S. 82nd Airborne Division.[1]

Madrigal was already under scrutiny at the time of the offense, having failed to report a previous arrest for driving under the influence; while the investigation into that omission was underway, and days after he began work at the JAG School, he maliciously deleted training materials and filmed himself doing so.[1]
