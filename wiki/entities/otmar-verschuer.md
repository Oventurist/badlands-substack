# Otmar Verschuer

**Otmar Verschuer** was a protégé of the Swiss psychiatrist [[ernst-rudin|Ernst Rudin]] and, with [[franz-kallmann|Franz J. Kallmann]], one of his chief assistants at the Rockefeller-funded Kaiser Wilhelm Institutes in Germany.[1]

Those institutes — the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-psychiatry|Institute for Psychiatry]] and the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-anthropology-eugenics-and-human-heredity|Institute for Anthropology, Eugenics, and Human Heredity]] — were financed by the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]], which Badlands Media also credits with creating the pseudoscientific specialty of "psychiatric genetics." Verschuer's circle produced the drafting work behind Germany's 1933 sterilization law, prepared under the "Task Force of Heredity Experts" chaired by [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]].[1]
