# William Pulte

William Pulte is Acting Director of National Intelligence.[1]

In July 2026 the Center to Advance Security in America (CASA), a government watchdog organization, asked Pulte to review the security clearances of former members of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigative team, citing recently released Justice Department records that it said raise questions about the handling of classified information.[1] CASA requested that the clearances be revoked if an official review determined the allegations warranted such action.[1] As of the publication of the report, no action by the Office of the Director of National Intelligence in response to CASA's request had been announced.[1]

## FHFA Director and the Letitia James referrals

Pulte also served as Director of the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]], in which capacity he repeatedly referred New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] for potential criminal prosecution.[2] In March 2026 he again referred James, citing allegations that she may have provided false information on a homeowner's insurance application, and sent letters to US Attorneys in Florida and Illinois requesting that they "authenticate and investigate" the claims.[2] The referral relied on analysis by attorney Mike Davis, who identified what he described as discrepancies in James' insurance representations in court filings.[2]

One application submitted to a Florida-based insurer reportedly stated that James' Norfolk, Virginia property would be unoccupied five months each year, contradicted by testimony that her niece, Nakia Thompson, had lived there for several years; a separate filing cited in the Illinois referral described the home as occupied by a single adult with no children, while public reporting indicated the niece and three children lived there.[2]

## Acting DNI and the June 2026 intelligence community purge

Pulte was selected by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to serve as acting Director of National Intelligence following the announced departure of [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]], scheduled to take effect June 30, 2026.[3] Trump indicated he did not plan to nominate Pulte to hold the intelligence post on a permanent basis after the acting appointment concluded.[3]

In a June 2026 interview with *The Wall Street Journal*, Trump said he had privately instructed Pulte to "start the process" of reducing personnel within the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]], which oversees 18 U.S. intelligence agencies and units and which Trump described as "unnecessary and/or too big."[3] *Reuters* separately quoted Trump saying, "I'd like to see it smaller. I think there are a lot of people in there that shouldn't be there."[3]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Pulte's selection only appears destabilizing if one assumes the intended end state is a restored and essentially unchanged intelligence apparatus; read instead as "the architecture of a controlled demolition and constitutional reset," an acting director drawn from outside the intelligence guild and given explicit license to cut headcount before a permanent hand takes over is the required feature rather than a bug.[3]

## Dual-hatting and the June 2026 appointment

President Trump formally appointed Pulte as Acting Director of National Intelligence in a post announced in early June 2026.[4] At the time Pulte was serving as director of the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]] and as chairman of [[fannie-mae|Fannie Mae]] and [[freddie-mac|Freddie Mac]], and the announcement specified he would continue holding all of those roles while serving in the acting DNI position.[4] Trump praised Pulte's qualifications by highlighting his experience overseeing some of the nation's most sensitive financial matters, including the safety and soundness of U.S. housing markets.[4]

The arrangement drew immediate commentary. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] questioned how leading ODNI — a clearing house across 18 intelligence agencies, including the Space Force element — could be a part-time job.[4] She allowed that if [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s tenure had genuinely transformed the intelligence community's people, process, and technology, then modernized oversight might plausibly be handled part time, and said she was principally looking forward to the "Justice phase" of accountability.[4]

## Fannie Mae terminations (April 2025)

Pulte was sworn in on March 14, 2025 after being appointed by President Trump, and within weeks moved against internal misconduct at [[fannie-mae|Fannie Mae]], where he served as chair of the board of directors.[5] On April 8, 2025 the enterprise announced it had fired more than 100 employees for unethical conduct: "Since my swearing-in, we fired over 100 employees from Fannie Mae who we caught engaging in unethical conduct, including facilitating fraud, against our great company," Pulte said, adding that "anyone who commits fraud against Fannie Mae does so against the American people."[5] He stated that under the Trump administration there would be no room for fraud, mortgage fraud, or "any other deceitful act that can jeopardize the safety and soundness of the housing industry."[5] Fannie Mae chief executive [[priscilla-almodovar|Priscilla Almodovar]] publicly thanked him for empowering the company to root out unethical conduct.[5]
