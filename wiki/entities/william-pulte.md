# William Pulte

William Pulte is identified in the Badlands corpus in two government roles: as Acting Director of National Intelligence, heading the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]], and as Director of the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]].

## CASA clearance request

According to the July 27, 2026 Badlands Brief, the [[center-to-advance-security-in-america|Center to Advance Security in America]] formally asked Pulte to review the security clearances of former members of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigative team. The watchdog organization cited recently released [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] records — internal communications made public by [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] — which it said raise questions about the handling of classified information, including allegations that members of Smith's office granted access to classified material without the required "need-to-know" authorization and that internal concerns were raised about security procedures involving a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility. CASA asked that the clearances be revoked if an official review determined the allegations warranted such action. As of the Brief's publication, no responsive action by Pulte's office had been announced.

## FHFA referral of Letitia James (March 2026)

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Pulte, as Director of the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]], again referred New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] for potential criminal prosecution, citing allegations that she may have provided false information on a homeowner's insurance application. Pulte sent letters to US Attorneys in Florida and Illinois requesting they "authenticate and investigate" the claims, citing analysis from attorney [[mike-davis|Mike Davis]] who points to court filings he says show discrepancies in James' insurance representations.
