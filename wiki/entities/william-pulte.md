# William Pulte

William Pulte is identified in the Badlands corpus in two government roles: as Acting Director of National Intelligence, heading the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]], and as Director of the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]].

## Appointment as Acting DNI (June 2026)

The June 3, 2026 Badlands Brief reported President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s appointment of Pulte as Acting Director of National Intelligence. Notably, Pulte was to retain his existing portfolio while serving in the intelligence post, remaining director of the FHFA and chairman of both [[fannie-mae|Fannie Mae]] and [[freddie-mac|Freddie Mac]]. Trump praised his qualifications by pointing to his experience overseeing some of the nation's most sensitive financial matters, including the safety and soundness of U.S. housing markets.

The dual-hatting drew scrutiny from Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who questioned how leadership of ODNI — the clearing house for eighteen intelligence agencies, including the Space Force element — could plausibly be a part-time job. She allowed that if [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s tenure had genuinely modernized the intelligence community's people, processes, and technology, oversight might have become a lighter lift, and framed the appointment as a step toward what she called the "Justice phase" of accountability for weaponized agencies.

## CASA clearance request

According to the July 27, 2026 Badlands Brief, the [[center-to-advance-security-in-america|Center to Advance Security in America]] formally asked Pulte to review the security clearances of former members of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigative team. The watchdog organization cited recently released [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] records — internal communications made public by [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] — which it said raise questions about the handling of classified information, including allegations that members of Smith's office granted access to classified material without the required "need-to-know" authorization and that internal concerns were raised about security procedures involving a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility. CASA asked that the clearances be revoked if an official review determined the allegations warranted such action. As of the Brief's publication, no responsive action by Pulte's office had been announced.

## FHFA referral of Letitia James (March 2026)

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Pulte, as Director of the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]], again referred New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] for potential criminal prosecution, citing allegations that she may have provided false information on a homeowner's insurance application. Pulte sent letters to US Attorneys in Florida and Illinois requesting they "authenticate and investigate" the claims, citing analysis from attorney [[mike-davis|Mike Davis]] who points to court filings he says show discrepancies in James' insurance representations.

## Acting DNI and the intelligence-community purge (June 2026)

In an interview with the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he had privately instructed Pulte to "start the process" of reducing personnel within the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]], which Trump described as "unnecessary and/or too big," telling [[reuters|Reuters]]: "I'd like to see it smaller. I think there are a lot of people in there that shouldn't be there." Pulte was selected by Trump to serve as acting DNI following the announced departure of [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]], scheduled to take effect June 30, 2026, and Trump indicated he does not plan to nominate Pulte for the post on a permanent basis. [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s commentary frames the appointment as part of "the architecture of a controlled demolition and constitutional reset" of an intelligence community that "became a node in a narrative war for domestic political enforcement.
