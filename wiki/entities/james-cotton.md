# James Cotton

James Cotton is the president of United Auto Workers Local 22, the union local representing workers at [[general-motors|General Motors]]' Factory Zero electric-vehicle assembly plant in Detroit.

In the July 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2b2|Badlands Brief]], Cotton is quoted from Crain's Detroit Business expressing the local's concern about GM's installation of 50 AI-integrated manufacturing robots at Factory Zero, coming after workforce reductions that the [[united-auto-workers|United Auto Workers]] says idled more than 1,000 employees at the plant. GM has described the machines as collaborative robots working alongside humans, principally attaching body panels to electric vehicles. Cotton's objection is presented in the Brief as the local-level face of a wider confrontation between organized labor and [[automation|automation]].
