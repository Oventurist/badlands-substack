# Devin Nunes

Devin Nunes is an American former congressman and media executive who served as Chief Executive Officer and Chairman of [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]] before stepping down to concentrate on his role with the President's Intelligence Advisory Board.[1] The company confirmed his departure on a Tuesday in April 2026, naming veteran executive [[kevin-mcgurn|Kevin McGurn]] as interim leader.[1]

## Congressional career

Before joining Trump Media, Nunes served as chairman of the House Intelligence Committee and later as its ranking member.[1] In those roles he led oversight efforts targeting the intelligence agencies during the Obama administration and played a central part in challenging the FBI's Russia investigation into [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Intelligence advisory role

Since taking his advisory post in 2025, Nunes has worked alongside [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]] on declassification efforts and intelligence reforms.[1] Reporting cited by Badlands noted that his team worked with Ratcliffe as the [[cia|CIA]] recalled more than a dozen intelligence analyses that failed to meet spy tradecraft standards — described as a powerful rebuke of past leadership at the agency.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America framed the move as "a notable development if you still believe in a plan," noting that Nunes is a key figure in [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s book *Government Gangsters* and served as a mentor of sorts to Patel as he navigated the Russia Gate hoax.[1] She wrote that despite loud online criticism of both men, she still considers them "core team."[1]

## Reviving the HPSCI Russiagate threads

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote in July 2025 that Nunes, the former head of the [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence]], is reviving the investigative plot lines left hanging in [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s book *Government Gangsters*.[2] Patel, who served as lead Russiagate investigator under Nunes at HPSCI, had written that the committee's findings could not be discussed publicly because they were classified rather than released.[2] The revival coincided with the [[fbi|FBI]]'s newly opened [[grand-conspiracy-probe|"grand conspiracy" probe]] into a decade of alleged election meddling against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]

## TAE Technologies merger

In the $6 billion all-stock merger between [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]] and fusion developer [[tae-technologies|TAE Technologies]] announced on December 18, 2025, Nunes — chairman and chief executive of Trump Media — is expected to serve as co-CEO of the combined company alongside TAE chief executive Dr. [[michl-binderbauer|Michl Binderbauer]].[3] The transaction, expected to close in mid-2026 pending shareholder and regulatory approvals, would place Trump Media's platforms including [[truth-social|Truth Social]] alongside TAE Power Solutions and TAE Life Sciences in a publicly traded fusion power company.[3]

## Appointment to the Intelligence Advisory Board (December 2024)

President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced on Saturday, December 14, 2024 that Nunes would serve as chairman of the [[presidents-intelligence-advisory-board|President's Intelligence Advisory Board]], a body tasked with offering independent evaluations of the intelligence community's operations and planning.[4] Nunes, then leading Trump's social media platform [[truth-social|Truth Social]], was to continue as its chief executive while taking on the advisory position, according to Trump's post on the platform.[4] Trump wrote that Nunes would "draw on his experience as former Chairman of the House Intelligence Committee, and his key role in exposing the Russia, Russia, Russia Hoax, to provide me with independent assessments of the effectiveness and propriety of the U.S. Intelligence Community's activities."[4] As committee chair during Trump's first term, Nunes had accused the [[fbi|FBI]] of conspiring against Trump during its Russia investigation.[4] The same announcement named [[troy-edgar|Troy Edgar]] deputy secretary of homeland security and [[bill-white|Bill White]] U.S. ambassador to Belgium.[4]
