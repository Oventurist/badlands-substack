# 20th Century Fox

20th Century Fox is an American film studio, historically one of Hollywood's major production and distribution houses, which was acquired by [[walt-disney-company|Disney]] in a landmark consolidation of the entertainment industry.[1]

## Role in the Sound of Freedom distribution dispute

The anti-child-trafficking film [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]] was originally financed by a 20th Century Fox subsidiary.[1] After Disney purchased the studio, the film was reportedly shelved indefinitely, prompting a years-long effort by [[angel-studios|Angel Studios]] to reacquire the distribution rights and bring the picture to theatres.[1] Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described these "backend connections to Disney" as one of the most fascinating aspects of the film's eventual box-office success, noting that once released the film began supplanting recent Disney tentpoles including the latest Indiana Jones instalment.[1]

The episode is cited in Truth Community commentary as an illustration of how studio consolidation allowed a single corporate owner to determine whether a completed film about real-life child trafficking busts would ever reach audiences.[1]
