# Jake Novak

Jake Novak served as broadcast media director of the Consulate General of Israel in New York and figures in the Matt Gaetz saga through his text exchanges with cartoonist [[scott-adams|Scott Adams]].[1]

In September 2021, after a federal grand jury indicted former prosecutor [[david-mcgee|David McGee]] for attempting to extort Don Gaetz, Adams posted screenshots of a text conversation with Novak.[1] Adams explained that the two had a relationship because Novak often sought to have pro-Israel messaging inserted into the Dilbert comic strip.[1] Adams told POLITICO that "people with connections to Israel had a high interest in me during the Trump days. Presumably to influence me," adding that he and Novak shared an interest in the mechanics of persuasion and in political stories with an Israel angle.[1]

Novak texted Adams attempting to convince him that [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] was guilty, citing sources he claimed to have inside the investigation.[1] Adams pushed back, saying the claims were not credible and that Gaetz's extortion allegations were credible and document-backed; when Novak argued the documents did not prove extortion, Adams refuted him.[1] The Israeli government was forced to distance itself from Novak, though it neither fired nor disciplined him.[1]
