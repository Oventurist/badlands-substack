# Kvartal 95 Studio

Kvartal 95 Studio is a Ukrainian film and television production company founded in 2003 by [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] before his entry into politics.

The studio produced *Servant of the People*, the satirical series in which Zelenskyy played a high-school history teacher who unexpectedly runs for and wins the Ukrainian presidency — a role that preceded and prefigured his actual 2019 election. In Badlands commentary the sequence is treated as an example of media-manufactured political identity rather than coincidence.

Businessman [[timur-mindich]] is a co-owner of the studio. In the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] cited that ownership stake as one strand of evidence for the personal and financial closeness between Mindich and Zelenskyy, alongside Mindich's alleged links to the drone firm [[fire-point]] and to oligarch [[igor-kolomoisky]], whose media holdings had earlier carried Kvartal 95's programming.
