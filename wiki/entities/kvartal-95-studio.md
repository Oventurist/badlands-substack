# Kvartal 95 Studio

Kvartal 95 Studio is a Ukrainian film and television production company founded by [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] in 2003.[1] The studio produced *Servant of the People*, the television series in which Zelensky starred as a high school history teacher who runs for president of Ukraine and wins — a role that preceded his real-world election to the presidency.[1]

Ukrainian businessman [[timur-mindich|Timur Mindich]], whose alleged ties to members of Ukraine's 2026 government reshuffle drew scrutiny, is a co-owner of the studio.[1]
