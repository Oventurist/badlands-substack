# HarrisX

HarrisX is an American polling and market research firm, whose chief pollster as of 2024 was Dritan Nesho.[1]

## Forbes/HarrisX July 2024 poll

A Forbes/HarrisX survey conducted July 19–21, 2024 — after the Republican National Convention but before President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] withdrew from the race — found Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] trailing former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by 11 points among likely voters, a worse margin than Biden's 10-point deficit.[1] Trump led Biden 49% to 39% with 12% undecided, and led Harris 51% to 40% with only 9% undecided.[1]

Nesho commented that "Kamala Harris starts her 2024 battle behind Trump, who is enjoying a strong post-convention bump," and warned that if the polls did not close, Biden's decision to step aside for Harris could prove "too similar, too late."[1] He allowed that Harris alleviated concerns among the Democratic base and was better positioned to sway undecided independents and suburban women.[1]

The result was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and contrasted in Badlands coverage with a Reuters/Ipsos poll showing Harris narrowly ahead, which Badlands criticized for oversampling Democrats.[1]
