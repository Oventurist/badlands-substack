# Siobhian Brown

Siobhian Brown is a [[scottish-national-party|Scottish National Party]] politician who served as the Scottish Government's community safety minister at the time the [[hate-crime-and-public-order-scotland-act-2021|Hate Crime and Public Order (Scotland) Act 2021]] came into force on April 1, 2024.[1]

## Statements on misgendering

Brown initially stated that calling a "trans woman" — described in reporting as a man — "he" would not be a crime under the new statute.[1] After the law came into force, however, she revised that assurance, saying: "It could be reported and it could be investigated. Whether or not the police would think it was criminal is up to [[police-scotland|Police Scotland]] for that."[1] Her admission was reported alongside the acknowledgment that author [[jk-rowling|J.K. Rowling]] could be prosecuted for "misgendering" transgender people under the Act.[1] The exchange was covered in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of April 2, 2024.[1]
