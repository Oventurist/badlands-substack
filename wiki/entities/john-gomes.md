# John Gomes

John Gomes is a Bridgeport, Connecticut mayoral candidate whose legal challenge to the city's 2023 Democratic primary resulted in the election being overturned.[1]

## 2023 primary challenge

Gomes posted video to Facebook on September 16, 2023 that appeared to show a woman — identified in the case as Bridgeport Democratic Town Committee vice chair [[wanda-geter-pataky|Wanda Geter-Pataky]] — stuffing ballots into an absentee drop box.[1] His legal counsel presented 180 pieces of evidence to the court, and Judge [[william-clark-connecticut|William Clark]] cited that record in a 37-page ruling ordering a fresh Democratic primary against incumbent Mayor [[joe-ganim|Joe Ganim]].[1]

The case was cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as an example of election fraud being exposed once voters became attentive to [[ballot-harvesting|ballot harvesting]] practices.[1]
