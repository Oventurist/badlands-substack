# Pepe Escobar

Pepe Escobar is a geopolitical journalist whose reporting on multipolar-world affairs is frequently cited in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary.[1]

## Reporting on Trump's Chinese reception

Following the July 13, 2024 [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination of Donald Trump]], Escobar reported that the event had made [[donald-trump|Trump]] a viral sensation on Chinese social media.[1] He noted that Trump has for years been known in China as *Comrade Jianguo* (建国同志), meaning "Comrade Build-Country," and by the related phrase *Chuān Jiànguó* (川建国), literally "Build the Country Trump" but commonly rendered as "Making China Great Again," reflecting a popular Chinese perception that Trump's actions ultimately benefit the Chinese working class.[1] According to Escobar, Chinese social media users were broadly wishing "Comrade Jianguo" a speedy recovery and praying for his safety.[1]
