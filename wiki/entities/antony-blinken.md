# Antony Blinken

Antony Blinken is the U.S. Secretary of State whose declaration at a summit of NATO foreign ministers that "Ukraine WILL become a member of NATO" is presented in "A Bright Red Line" as the moment the alliance's Ukraine policy passed from possibility to stated inevitability.[1] The article's subtitle pairs him with [[william-burns|William Burns]] as the two figures framing NATO's bid for Ukraine.[1]

## The language shift

The article emphasizes a change in official phrasing: officials no longer speak of being "in discussions" or of "negotiating the possibility of a MAP [membership action plan] for Ukraine"; the language now indicates that Ukrainian membership is decided and inevitable.[1] In the author's reading this is the one step that takes negotiations with Russia completely off the table — an outcome that Western neoconservatives in the security state, in [[congress|Congress]] and at the [[state-department|State Department]] are said to be aware of and to welcome.[1]

Blinken's statement is set directly against the earlier assurance, given to concerned onlookers, that Ukrainian NATO membership was merely "a figment of the paranoid Russian imagination" and a pretext invented by Russia to justify invasion.[1]
