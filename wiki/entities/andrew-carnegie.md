# Andrew Carnegie

Andrew Carnegie was an American industrialist and philanthropist who, in Badlands Media's account of the early twentieth century, worked alongside [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]] to reshape American medical education.[1]

Carnegie and Rockefeller are described as having started medical schools that taught only allopathic medicine, a program that accompanied Rockefeller's smear campaign against natural healing modalities and the formation of the [[american-medical-association|American Medical Association]], which would recognize only practitioners of allopathic, chemical-based medicine.[1] The result, in this telling, was that medicine shifted from largely homeopathic and natural sources to petrochemical treatment, and physicians who did not follow the new guidelines — a pill for every illness — could no longer practice in America.[1]
