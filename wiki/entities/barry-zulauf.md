# Barry Zulauf

Barry Zulauf is an intelligence community analytic ombudsman known for a report submitted to the [[senate-intelligence-committee|Senate Intelligence Committee]] concerning the handling of intelligence on foreign interference in the 2020 U.S. election.[1]

## Findings on analyst reluctance

Zulauf's report concluded that some analysts appeared reluctant to elevate evidence tied to [[china|China]] because of disagreements with [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration policies.[1] The finding is cited alongside statements by former Director of National Intelligence [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]], who said in early 2021 that intelligence analysts faced political pressure regarding assessments of Chinese interference efforts.[1]

The Zulauf report has been invoked as corroborating background for the later investigation launched by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] into allegations that agencies concealed foreign election threat evidence.[1]
