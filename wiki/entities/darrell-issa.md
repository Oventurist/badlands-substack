# Darrell Issa

Darrell Issa is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from California, known in the Badlands Media corpus primarily for his oversight-oriented interventions against executive branch agencies.[1]

## Oversight of State Department counseling sessions

In November 2024, following [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s victory in the 2024 presidential election, Issa criticized Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] over reports that the [[state-department|U.S. State Department]] had held therapy or counseling sessions for employees distressed by the election result. It had not been confirmed whether the sessions actually took place.[1]

In a letter to Blinken obtained by the Washington Free Beacon, Issa wrote that he was "concerned that the Department is catering to federal employees who are personally devastated by the normal functioning of American democracy through the provision of government-funded mental health counseling because [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] was not elected President of the United States."[1] He added that it was "disturbing that ostensibly nonpartisan government officials would suffer a personal meltdown over the results of a free and fair election, something the United States champions around the world."[1]

The letter posed a series of questions to Blinken, including how many sessions had been conducted, how many were planned, and what they cost the department.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America argued that State Department employees might reasonably need counseling given the prospect of losing their jobs and facing prosecution under a second Trump administration, while agreeing with Issa that taxpayers should not fund it.[1]
