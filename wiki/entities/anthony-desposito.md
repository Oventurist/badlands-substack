# Anthony P. D'Esposito

Anthony P. D'Esposito is the Inspector General of the US [[department-of-labor|Department of Labor]].[1]

## H-1B fraud and trafficking investigation

In July 2026, D'Esposito's Office of Inspector General launched a major investigation into fraud and human trafficking tied to the [[h-1b-visa-program|H-1B visa]] and Program Electronic Review Management (PERM) systems.[1] He said: "This isn't just paperwork fraud—it's the exploitation of vulnerable workers, forced labor, the displacement of American workers, and abusive human trafficking. As the Inspector General, my top priorities are exposing fraud, protecting American workers, and putting criminals in cuffs."[1]

Under his office, the OIG began a nationwide reporting campaign urging US and foreign workers to report suspected program abuses, with rewards potentially available for information leading to successful prosecutions.[1]

## Reception

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that D'Esposito "appears to be front-running a 'this is just paperwork' narrative from the administration's critics," and remarked on the press release's repeated references to human trafficking — asking of the phrase "abusive human trafficking," "is there any other kind?"[1]
