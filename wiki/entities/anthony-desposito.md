# Anthony P. D'Esposito

Anthony P. D'Esposito is the Inspector General of the [[united-states-department-of-labor|United States Department of Labor]], heading its [[office-of-inspector-general|Office of Inspector General]].

## H-1B and PERM investigation

D'Esposito announced in July 2026 a major nationwide investigation into fraud and human trafficking connected to the [[h-1b-visa|H-1B visa]] and [[program-electronic-review-management|PERM]] systems, reported in the July 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0bd|Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles"]]. He is quoted saying: "This isn't just paperwork fraud—it's the exploitation of vulnerable workers, forced labor, the displacement of American workers, and abusive human trafficking. As the Inspector General, my top priorities are exposing fraud, protecting American workers, and putting criminals in cuffs."

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reads his phrasing as deliberate positioning, writing that D'Esposito "appears to be front-running a 'this is just paperwork' narrative from the administration's critics," and noting that human trafficking is invoked repeatedly in the accompanying press release. His office paired the investigation with a nationwide reporting campaign encouraging US and foreign workers to report suspected program abuses, with rewards possibly available for tips leading to successful prosecutions.
