# Adel bin Ahmed Al-Jubeir

Adel bin Ahmed Al-Jubeir is a Saudi Arabian diplomat who serves as Minister of State for Foreign Affairs and as the kingdom's Climate Envoy.[1] He drew attention in January 2023 for remarks delivered in Switzerland during the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] annual meeting in Davos, where he described a future urban development without private automobiles.[1]

## "There will be no cars"

Speaking on a panel titled "The Evolution of Urban Life," Al-Jubeir said of [[neom|NEOM]]'s flagship project, The Line, that it "will revolutionize the way people look at cities and urban planning," calling NEOM an attempt to create a city that is environmentally friendly, sustainable, of very high quality of life, and with virtually no traffic.[1] He added: "There will be virtually no traffic. You could go to different places very efficiently without using cars. There are no cars. There are going to be different types of transportation that are environmentally friendly and based on renewable energy."[1] He further characterised private vehicle ownership as wasteful.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] noted the irony of a diplomat from oil-rich Saudi Arabia promoting a car-free "sustainable" smart city to an audience of billionaires at Davos.[1] DeLarme argued that removing private vehicles goes hand in hand with the removal of rights and other private property, described the presentation as consistent with half a decade of World Economic Forum messaging, and warned that a city built with slave labour in one of the most arid and uninhabitable places on earth could become a literal death trap for residents without long-distance transportation.[1]
