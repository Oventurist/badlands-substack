# Nils Gilman

Nils Gilman is an American think-tank executive identified as the co-founder and partner of [[rosa-brooks|Rosa Brooks]] in the [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]] (TIP), the election scenario-planning group formed in late 2019 ahead of the 2020 United States presidential election.[1]

## Institutional affiliations

Gilman serves as Senior Vice President of Programs at the [[berggruen-institute|Berggruen Institute]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] characterises the Berggruen Institute as an organisation involved in biotechnology that rearranges DNA to create new life forms, and as an institution engaged with artificial intelligence and transhumanism.[1] Gilman is also described as having ties to the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] and to Noema Magazine, a publication partnered with the Washington Post.[1]

## Role in TIP

Together with Brooks, Gilman helped convene the June 2020 exercises in which more than 100 current and former senior government and campaign figures war-gamed four possible outcomes of the 2020 election.[1] Carlson argues that the resulting 22-page report, though presented as bipartisan planning, in practice functioned as an effort to determine how [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] could be prevented from remaining president, and that the "bipartisan" label concealed a roster of Democrats, RINOs and neoconservatives.[1]
