# Alexander Rodnyansky

Alexander Rodnyansky is an economic adviser to Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] who drew attention in early 2023 for publicly signalling that Ukrainian forces might abandon the city of Bakhmut.[1]

## Bakhmut remarks (February 2023)

Speaking to CNN on Tuesday, February 28, 2023, Rodnyansky said Ukrainian troops may "strategically pull back" from Bakhmut, the focus of intense and brutal fighting for the preceding months.[1] He said the Ukrainian army had not yet pulled out of the city, but that Kyiv might soon decide the cost of holding it "outweighs the benefits."[1]

"Our military is obviously going to weigh all of the options," Rodnyansky said. "So far they've held the city, but if need be, they will strategically pull back because we're not going to sacrifice all of our people just for nothing."[1] Russian forces had assaulted [[battle-of-bakhmut|Bakhmut]] for months seeking to capture it and gain a foothold in Ukraine's eastern Donetsk region.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cautioned readers not to confuse a Ukrainian "strategic pull-back" with Russia's doctrine of maneuver defense, in which territory is voluntarily ceded in order to encircle and then destroy an advancing force.[1] He read the messaging from Rodnyansky, Zelensky and what he called their Media Industrial Complex cheerleaders as an admission that Ukraine was losing, and that the [[nato|NATO]] — in his framing, [[deep-state|Deep State]] globalist — proxy war had not gone according to plan.[1]
