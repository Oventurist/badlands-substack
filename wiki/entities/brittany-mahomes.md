# Brittany Mahomes

Brittany Mahomes is an American public figure who became a minor flashpoint in the 2024 presidential campaign's celebrity politics.[1] In September 2024, the Hollywood trade publication [[variety|Variety]] published a column pressuring [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]] to endorse [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], and, as [[breitbart|Breitbart]] reported, "called out Swift for having the audacity to socialize with Brittany Mahomes, whom former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has thanked for her support."[1]

The September 11, 2024 *Badlands News Brief* treated the episode as a sign of weakness in the Harris campaign, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] writing that "if you need a pop star to pretend you're popular, you're not popular."[1]
