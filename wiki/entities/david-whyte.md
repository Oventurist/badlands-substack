# David Whyte

**David Whyte** is the poet quoted in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites his line, "Let your solitude become a healing balm, not a wound that stings," in the section "When You Cannot Feel the Light, Be the Soil."[1] It is deployed to reassure readers that healing is not linear and that periods in which "fog has swallowed your intuition whole" require no performance of clarity or inspiration.[1]

Whyte's quotation immediately precedes the essay's closing image of the reader as soil — "dark and rich," with "something rooting in you" — and its counsel to "let yourself be in winter," since "creation will bloom from stillness."[1]
