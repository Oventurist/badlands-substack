# Tommy Pigott

Tommy Pigott is a spokesman for the United States [[state-department|State Department]] during the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Statements on Venezuela

Pigott said that the resumption of operations at the [[us-embassy-in-caracas|US Embassy in Caracas]] in late March 2026 represented a key milestone in implementing the administration's three-phase plan for [[venezuela|Venezuela]], a framework outlining stabilization, economic recovery, and political reconciliation leading toward democratic elections.[1]
