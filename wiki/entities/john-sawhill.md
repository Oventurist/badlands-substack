# John C. Sawhill

John C. Sawhill was an American economist and administrator identified by Badlands Media as a member of the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] whose career illustrates the Commission's policy-to-office pipeline.[1]

Sawhill authored an early commission report titled *Energy: Managing the Transition*, which made recommendations on how to manage a movement to higher-cost energy.[1] President [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]] subsequently appointed him deputy secretary of the Department of Energy, placing him in a position to implement the recommendations he had prepared on the Commission's behalf.[1]
