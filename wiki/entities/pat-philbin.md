# Pat Philbin

Pat Philbin is an American attorney who served as a deputy White House counsel in the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

In January 2024 he was named among the lawyers joining the [[torridon-group|Torridon Group]], the boutique firm founded by former Attorney General [[bill-barr|William Barr]] and [[ted-ullyot|Ted Ullyot]], as it built out a Washington office.[1] He joined alongside former White House counsel [[pat-cipollone|Pat Cipollone]] and fellow former deputy counsel [[kate-todd|Kate Todd]].[1]
