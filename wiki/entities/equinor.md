# Equinor

Equinor is the Norwegian energy company whose North Sea Rosebank development became a focal point of the United Kingdom's 2023 argument over energy security and net zero policy.[1]

## The Rosebank field

In late September 2023, Conservative Prime Minister [[rishi-sunak|Rishi Sunak]], citing energy security needs, walked back a set of UK net zero emissions targets and green-lighted Equinor's North Sea Rosebank field, described as one of the United Kingdom's biggest new oil and gas projects in recent times.[1]

The approval stood in direct contrast to the [[labour-party|Labour Party]]'s vow to stop any new offshore drilling in order to meet the country's 2050 climate goals if it won the next election.[1] Energy Secretary [[claire-coutinho|Claire Coutinho]] argued that without such new developments, output measured by the [[north-sea-transition-authority|North Sea Transition Authority]] would fall from a record low of 38 million tonnes in 2022 to 22 million tonnes by 2030, forcing Britain to import up to 80 percent of its oil and gas.[1]
