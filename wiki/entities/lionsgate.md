# Lionsgate

Lionsgate is a Hollywood film and television studio.[1]

In August 2023 Lionsgate announced and then rescinded a renewed COVID-19 mask mandate for its offices after public pushback, one of two high-profile reversals reported alongside the healthcare system [[kaiser-permanente|Kaiser Permanente]]'s revision of its own masking policy in Santa Rosa, California.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited both reversals as evidence that "peaceful noncompliance" and vocal opposition were effective against renewed pandemic restrictions, arguing that every attempt to reimpose emergency measures should be treated as "a game of whack-a-mole."[1]

## Imposition of the August 2023 mask mandate

The mandate that Lionsgate later rescinded was first reported in the third week of August 2023, when the studio emailed staff asking them to mask up on certain floors of its Santa Monica office after several employees contracted COVID-19, and also asked employees to conduct self-screening before coming in.[2] The Hollywood Reporter framed the move as one office of one studio, but noted it came amid national speculation that mask mandates might return as the EG.5 variant became dominant in the United States, with Los Angeles public health officials reporting rising cases and the CDC reporting hospital admissions up 14 percent nationally that week.[2] Experts quoted in the coverage cautioned that the omicron-descended variant, circulating domestically since April, had proved no more concerning than earlier variants and was not expected to produce a wave comparable to the original omicron surge.[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the episode as a test balloon rather than a public health measure, writing that the establishment had "raised the white flag officially on October 31, 2022" when it sought pandemic amnesty, and predicting that the eventual backlash to what he called the most damaging psychological operation ever launched on the world population would dwarf that earlier reaction.[2]
