# Lionsgate

Lionsgate is a Hollywood film and television studio.[1]

In August 2023 Lionsgate announced and then rescinded a renewed COVID-19 mask mandate for its offices after public pushback, one of two high-profile reversals reported alongside the healthcare system [[kaiser-permanente|Kaiser Permanente]]'s revision of its own masking policy in Santa Rosa, California.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited both reversals as evidence that "peaceful noncompliance" and vocal opposition were effective against renewed pandemic restrictions, arguing that every attempt to reimpose emergency measures should be treated as "a game of whack-a-mole."[1]
