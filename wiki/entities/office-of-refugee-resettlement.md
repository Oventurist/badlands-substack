# Office of Refugee Resettlement

The Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR) is the component of the U.S. [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] responsible for the custody and sponsor placement of unaccompanied alien children who arrive at the U.S. border.[1]

## Role in the unaccompanied minor pipeline

In April 2023 congressional testimony, HHS whistleblower [[tara-lee-rodas|Tara Lee Rodas]] identified ORR as the terminal link in what she described as a sophisticated trafficking network: recruitment in the child's home country, smuggling to the U.S. border, and finally ORR's delivery of the child to a sponsor.[1] Rodas testified that some of those sponsors are criminals, traffickers, and members of Transnational Criminal Organizations, and that some treat children as commodities and income-earning assets — a dynamic she blamed for an explosion of labor trafficking.[1]

## Congressional scrutiny

ORR's sponsor-vetting practices were the focus of the hearing "The Biden Border Crisis: Exploitation of Unaccompanied Alien Children," convened by the House Judiciary Subcommittee on Immigration Integrity, Security and Enforcement under the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]].[1] Rodas, who had been detailed to an Emergency Intake Site in Pomona, California, urged [[congress|Congress]] to crack down on the U.S. government's role in the network.[1]
