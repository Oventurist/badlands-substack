# Maurene Comey

Maurene Comey is an American federal prosecutor who worked on the criminal cases of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and his associate [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], and who was fired by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] on Wednesday, July 16, 2025.[1] She is the daughter of former FBI director [[james-comey|James B. Comey]], who was himself fired during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term.[1]

## Career

In addition to her work on the Epstein and Maxwell prosecutions, Comey served as a prosecutor in the trial of the entertainer [[sean-combs|Sean Combs]], known as Diddy.[1] Her portfolio therefore placed her at the center of two of the highest-profile sex-crime prosecutions of the era.

## Dismissal

The notice informing Comey of her termination did not provide a reason, instead citing the broad powers afforded to the president under the [[us-constitution|U.S. Constitution]], according to a person familiar with the matter who spoke on the condition of anonymity.[1] The notice was signed by Francey Hakes, a political appointee in the Justice Department who runs the Executive Office of U.S. Attorneys.[1] Reporting at the time noted that it was not known whether the firing was connected to the Epstein case or to her relationship to the former FBI director.[1]

## Commentary

Independent commentators associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the timing of the dismissal as significant rather than incidental. [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] noted the coincidence of Trump publicly suggesting that Democrats and "Deep Staters" had manipulated the [[epstein-files|Epstein files]] and planted fake evidence in them, alongside the claim that James Comey's daughter Maurene was the prosecutor who reviewed the footage surrounding Epstein's death in custody, and that she was fired the same week.[1]
