# Michelle Zajko

Michelle Zajko is a 32-year-old woman who became a focus of federal investigators in connection with the January 20, 2025 shooting that killed US Border Patrol agent [[david-maland|David Maland]] in northern Vermont.[1] Police say Zajko purchased the handguns used in Maland's shooting.[1]

Investigators expanded their focus to Zajko after the arrest of [[teresa-youngblut|Teresa "Milo" Youngblut]], who was charged with assaulting and discharging a firearm at a Border Patrol agent.[1] The wider case has been described by police as involving a "trans vegan cult" linked to multiple murders across the United States.[1]
