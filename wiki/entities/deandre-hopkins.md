# DeAndre Hopkins

**DeAndre Hopkins** is an American football wide receiver who played for the Kansas City Chiefs during the 2024–25 season, appearing in Super Bowl 2025.[1]

## Super Bowl 2025 officiating remarks

After the Chiefs' 40–22 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles, Hopkins publicly challenged the media narrative that NFL officials had been favoring Kansas City throughout the postseason.[1] "I saw a lot of things in the media about the refs, but what are you all going to say now about the refs and us?" he said. "There was a lot of touchy calls. Are you going to report that? Are you going to talk about the refs now?"[1]

Coverage of the game noted that the officiating crew was a "third team" under scrutiny, but that questionable calls went both ways and the lopsided score rendered them largely inconsequential.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] read the episode as an institutional legitimacy operation, arguing that the [[nfl|NFL]] was "running the same op in support of its own legitimacy as the Regime did with the 2024 election" — building weeks of national media stories alleging the league was rigged for Kansas City, then having Kansas City lose, then publishing pieces like the Hopkins story.[1]
