# Michelle Behnke

Michelle Behnke is the president of the [[american-bar-association|American Bar Association]]. She appears in the Badlands corpus as the ABA's principal defender during congressional scrutiny of the organization's accreditation powers.

## Testimony before the House

The July 27, 2026 [[badlands-brief-245|Badlands Brief]] reports that Behnke testified at a House hearing where she defended the ABA's approach to diversity, equity and inclusion. She testified that diversity initiatives strengthen the legal profession and stated that diversity and excellence are "not mutually exclusive."

Her testimony was met with criticism from Rep. [[brandon-gill|Brandon Gill]], who argued that the ABA's previous DEI requirements discriminated against certain students and who said afterward that he intends to pursue legislation removing the association's authority to accredit U.S. law schools. The Brief notes that the ABA has begun rescinding its DEI requirements for law schools following an executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].
