# Michelle Behnke

Michelle Behnke is the President of the [[american-bar-association|American Bar Association]].[1]

At a July 2026 House hearing, Behnke defended the association's approach to diversity, equity and inclusion, testifying that diversity initiatives strengthen the legal profession and that diversity and excellence are "not mutually exclusive."[1] Her testimony came as the ABA had begun rescinding its DEI requirements for law schools following an executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] addressing such mandates, and immediately preceded Rep. [[brandon-gill|Brandon Gill]]'s announcement that he would pursue legislation stripping the ABA of its law school accreditation authority.[1]
