# Jimmy Wales

Jimmy Wales is the founder of Wikipedia and a figure associated with the Wikimedia Foundation, which operates the encyclopedia.[1] Badlands Media notes that Wales is a member of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and characterizes him as fully beholden to the Davos agenda, offering this as a reason to treat Wikipedia's sanitized description of the forum with skepticism.[1] In the article's telling, Wikipedia's account of the WEF's founding — presenting it as a purely European creation by [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] — omits the American ties to the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] that dissenting researchers have documented.[1]
