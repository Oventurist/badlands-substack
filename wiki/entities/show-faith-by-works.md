# Show Faith by Works, LLC

Show Faith by Works, LLC is a United States entity identified in federal [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]] (FARA) filings as conducting an influence campaign on behalf of the [[israeli-ministry-of-foreign-affairs|Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs]], which is listed as the principal.[1]

## The influence campaign

According to FARA filings reported in October 2025, the group planned to launch a campaign targeting Christian churches and organizations in the Western United States using digital advertising and a "mobile museum."[1] The filing states: "We will target and distribute pro-Israel information online and through targeted geofencing and digital online tools," and adds that the group will "have teams of people reaching out to churches and Pastors and possibly even Christian social media influencers," while acknowledging that some elements remain "undecided and fluid."[1]

The stated goal of the campaign is to encourage Christians to hold a more favorable view of the nation of Israel and to encourage Christians to visit Israel for tourism purposes.[1]

## Geofencing methodology

One of the primary methods described is geofenced digital advertising, in which online ads are restricted to specific geographic locations — potentially down to individual buildings.[1] Geofencing is a common practice in digital advertising used to reach a specific audience or consumer base; in this case the group appears to intend to geofence its campaign to specific, predetermined churches and Christian universities.[1]
