# The Rolling Stones

The Rolling Stones are the British rock band that, alongside [[the-beatles|the Beatles]], led the 1960s British Invasion of American popular music.

## Alleged intelligence origins

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], in his September 17, 2024 essay "The Media is the Enemy of the People," named the Rolling Stones with the Beatles in the assertion that "the [[mi6|MI6]]/[[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] created" both bands, contrasting them with [[elvis-presley|Elvis Presley]], whom he credited to God.[1] The claim sits inside Carlson's broader argument that 1960s American culture — music groups, counterculture movements, and the civil rights and women's rights movements — was created and steered by the CIA to control the country through fear and division.[1]

Carlson's framing rests on the premise that only God creates while imitation is the work of the Devil, and he offered the British bands as derivative of Presley's originality.[1] He further asked whether entire genres have since been left "to rot on the vine" while pop and hip hop artists such as [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]] and [[beyonce|Beyonce]] are actively promoted.[1]
