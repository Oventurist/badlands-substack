# American Federation of Government Employees

The American Federation of Government Employees (AFGE) is the largest labor union representing employees of the United States federal government.[1]

In February 2025 the AFGE joined the [[alliance-for-retired-americans|Alliance for Retired Americans]] and the [[service-employees-international-union|Service Employees International Union]] in a lawsuit against Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]], alleging that he had illegally shared union members' personal information with the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] cost-cutting team assembled by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

Acting on that suit, U.S. District Judge [[colleen-kollar-kotelly|Colleen Kollar-Kotelly]] — an appointee of former President Bill Clinton — issued an order on Wednesday, February 5, 2025 confining access to the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] payment records system to Treasury officials and two DOGE employees, [[tom-krause|Tom Krause]] and [[marko-elez|Marko Elez]].[1]
