# Victor Rothschild

Lord Victor Rothschild, 3rd Baron Rothschild, was a British member of the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild]] banking dynasty who appears in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s account of the founding of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1]

DeLarme records that when [[prince-bernhard|Prince Bernhard]] married Princess Juliana of the Netherlands in 1937, the prince became a major shareholder and officer in Dutch Shell Oil alongside Rothschild.[1] After the German invasion of Holland the Dutch royal couple moved to London, and it was there after the war that Rothschild and [[jozef-retinger|Józef Retinger]] encouraged Bernhard to create the Bilderberg Group.[1]

Investigative reporter [[james-p-tucker|James P. Tucker]], quoted in the same article, argued that [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]] founded the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] but "shares power in the older Bilderberg group with Rothschilds of Britain and Europe."[1] DeLarme separately notes that later Bilderberg chairman [[peter-carrington|Lord Peter Carrington]] was linked to the Rothschild banking empire by both business connections and marriage, and lists France's Baron Edmond de Rothschild among noteworthy Bilderberg attendees.[1]
