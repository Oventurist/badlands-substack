# Joshua Mitts

Joshua Mitts is a securities law scholar associated with [[columbia-university|Columbia University]] and, in some accounts of the research, with [[new-york-university|New York University School of Law]], best known for empirical work on short selling and market manipulation.[1] With [[robert-jackson-jr|Robert J. Jackson Jr.]], he co-authored a 2023 study finding significant short selling of Israeli securities in the period preceding the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 Hamas attack]].[1]

## Research on pre-attack short selling

Mitts and Jackson wrote that "days before the attack, traders appeared to anticipate the events to come," citing short interest in the [[msci-israel-etf|MSCI Israel Exchange Traded Fund]] that "suddenly, and significantly, spiked" on October 2 based on data from the [[financial-industry-regulatory-authority|Financial Industry Regulatory Authority]].[1] They also documented that short selling of Israeli securities on the [[tel-aviv-stock-exchange|Tel Aviv Stock Exchange]] increased dramatically immediately before the attack, and that risky short-dated options on Israeli companies traded on U.S. exchanges rose abnormally and suddenly, with those options expiring almost immediately after the attack began.[1]

Their 66-page report concluded that the evidence is consistent with informed traders anticipating and profiting from the Hamas attack, and that trading of this kind occurs in gaps in U.S. and international enforcement of legal prohibitions on informed trading.[1] The authors noted that the October 2 shorting of the Israeli ETF was the 30th-highest daily shorting volume in the fund's preceding 3,570 trading days and significantly exceeded the shorting seen at the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic.[1]

Mitts and Jackson recommended that the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]] and FINRA, which would have access to trader identities, pursue the question of why and how financial markets may have anticipated October 7.[1] Their findings underpin much of the public case described at [[israel-october-7-foreknowledge-allegations|Israel October 7 foreknowledge allegations]].[1]
