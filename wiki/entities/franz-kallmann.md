# Franz J. Kallmann

**Franz J. Kallmann** was a protégé of [[ernst-rudin|Ernst Rudin]] at the Rockefeller-funded Kaiser Wilhelm Institutes and, with [[otmar-verschuer|Otmar Verschuer]], one of his principal assistants.[1]

In 1938 Kallmann wrote a book that Badlands Media says was used as a rationalization for the murder of over 250,000 mental patients and other "defective" people, many of them children.[1] His work belonged to the field of "psychiatric genetics," a specialty the article attributes to the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]], and sat alongside the sterilization law drafted by Rudin's group under [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]]'s Task Force of Heredity Experts.[1]
