# Enzo Fernández

Enzo Fernández is an Argentine footballer whose dismissal from the final of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]] is a focal point of the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]'s account of the match.

## Sending off in the 2026 final

With fewer than seven minutes remaining in regulation, the match scoreless and [[spain|Spain]] pressing, Fernández attempted to draw a foul by going to ground. Instead of winning a free kick he was cautioned — according to the Brief, for complaining that a foul had not been called. Regulation ended in a draw. In extra time he committed a second cautionable offense while challenging a Spanish player; the Brief's contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that although some would say Fernández was playing the ball, he "puts his feet together and forms a wall, of sorts, with his shins" and intended to trip the opponent, adding that Fernández "had been playing dirty the entire game." The commentary concedes in fairness that the contact was incidental enough not to warrant a straight red card.

Because a second yellow card constitutes a red, Fernández was ejected and [[argentina|Argentina]] finished the match a man down, unable to answer [[ferran-torres|Ferran Torres]]'s 106th-minute goal.
