# Pythagoras

Pythagoras is the Greek philosopher and mathematician cited in "Hijacked Consciousness - Sacred Origins" as an exemplar of learning conducted as spiritual practice.[1] The essay, the first installment of the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], places his teaching at around 500 BC in Greece.[1]

## Teaching in the article's account

Pythagoras is described as having taught the harmony of the spheres — "a music not heard with ears, but felt in the soul."[1] In his school, the article holds, mathematics was a spiritual discipline and geometry was not abstraction but revelation: to draw a circle was to invoke perfection, and to study number was to enter communion with the Logos, the underlying pattern of reality.[1]

## Significance in the series

The Pythagorean case is one of several the author uses to establish that pre-modern instruction unified intellect and spirit, alongside the temple schools of Egypt, the Vedic guru-shishya lineage, Mayan glyph schools and the Celtic bardic orders — treated collectively under [[ancient-sacred-learning-traditions|ancient sacred learning traditions]].[1] The subsequent split of mathematics from music is named as one of the specific separations attributed to [[the-enlightenment|the Enlightenment]], making Pythagoras a marker of what the series argues was lost.[1]
