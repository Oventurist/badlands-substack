# John Rendon

John Rendon is an American public-relations operative described in Badlands Media coverage as a "Pentagon PR guru."[1] He is best known in this literature for his appearance in the leaked internal documents of the [[integrity-initiative|Integrity Initiative]].[1]

## Integrity Initiative contacts

Memos leaked in January 2019 recorded meetings between Integrity Initiative director Chris Donnelly and a series of prominent Americans, with Rendon named alongside former Trump adviser [[sebastian-gorka|Sebastian Gorka]] and McCain Foundation director [[kurt-volker|Kurt Volker]].[1] The documents were presented as revealing the methods by which the anti-Russian British operation worked to penetrate American intelligence institutions, think tanks, and the media.[1]

## Significance

Rendon's inclusion is treated as notable because his professional specialism — the shaping of press narratives on behalf of the American defence establishment — sits precisely at the point where a foreign influence operation would seek leverage.[1] The episode forms one strand of the argument that British-directed networks have long conditioned the information environment in which American foreign policy is made.[1]
