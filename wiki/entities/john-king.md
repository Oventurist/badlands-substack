# John King

John King is an American television journalist and chief national correspondent for [[cnn|CNN]], best known for his electoral map analysis on the network's "Magic Wall."[1]

## July 2024 electoral map analysis

On Tuesday, July 16, 2024 — the first night of the [[2024-republican-national-convention|Republican National Convention]] — King used CNN's electoral map to argue that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was on course for a historic landslide victory in November, potentially reaching 330 or more electoral votes against the 270 required to win.[1]

King explained that the solid and leaning Republican states alone already gave Trump a path to 272 electoral votes.[1] He identified Wisconsin and Pennsylvania as toss-ups, described Michigan as leaning Republican, and noted that Georgia, Arizona, and Nevada — states [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had won or flipped in 2020 — had moved toward Trump.[1] "This is the dire strait for Joe Biden right now on the first night of the republican convention," King said, adding that with sixteen weeks remaining to Election Day, "it is tough to change a map like this in that amount of time."[1]

King said the warning to the White House was that if the race's current dynamics held, Trump could conceivably reach 330 or more electoral votes, while Biden had "essentially one, maybe two narrow paths to 270."[1] Badlands coverage highlighted the segment as a mainstream-media admission of Trump's standing amid the pressure campaign for Biden's withdrawal from the race.[1]
