# August Pfluger

August Pfluger is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas who has been active on homeland security and border matters.[1]

## Border security hearing, June 2023

At a June 21, 2023 hearing of the [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|Committee on Homeland Security]] titled "Threats Posed to the Homeland by Nation-State Actors in Latin America," held by the Subcommittee on Counterterrorism, Law Enforcement, and Intelligence, Pfluger voiced alarm about encounters at the southwest border with foreign nationals from countries of concern.[1]

"I am concerned that the chaos of the Southwest border could be taken advantage of by anti-U.S. regimes—not just can, but has been," Pfluger said, adding that "the PRC and our foreign adversaries are expanding their spheres of influence in Latin America, right in our backyard. … There are clear implications for U.S. Homeland Security."[1]

His remarks accompanied statements by House Republicans that [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] had intercepted a rising number of nationals from outside South and Central America, including Russians and several described as high-value targets from China.[1]
