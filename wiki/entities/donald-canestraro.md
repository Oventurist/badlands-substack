# Donald Canestraro

Donald Canestraro is an investigator for the Office of Military Commissions, part of the Department of Defense's [[military-commissions-defense-organization|Military Commissions Defense Organization]].[1] He is the author of a sworn declaration dated July 20, 2021 that collected accounts alleging a coverup by senior [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and [[fbi|FBI]] officials of intelligence failures preceding the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1]

## Declaration and investigation

Canestraro told the outlet [[florida-bulldog|Florida Bulldog]] that he is part of the defense team for Guantanamo detainee [[ammar-al-baluchi|Ammar al-Baluchi]], a Pakistani citizen awaiting trial with four other men accused of planning the 9/11 attacks.[1] His declaration compiles interviews with eleven former FBI agents, two former CIA officers, a CNN investigative journalist, former deputy National Security Advisor Richard Clarke, and former Senator Bob Graham of Florida, who co-chaired Congress's Joint Inquiry into 9/11.[1] Most of the interviews were conducted in 2016 and 2018.[1]

Among the accounts is that of a New York FBI counterterrorism agent who, weeks before the attacks, nearly "came over the table" at CIA officials blocking him from obtaining intelligence about two al Qaeda operatives who would go on to hijack an American Airlines jet and crash it into the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1] Shortly after the 2001 encounter the agent wrote in an email, "Someone is going to die."[1]

The 22-page report states that the CIA "used their liaison relationship with the Saudi Intelligence Services to conduct an operation on U.S. soil," and that "the Saudis were used as a go between as the CIA is forbidden by law to conduct intelligence operations with the U.S."[1] It further alleges that CIA officials hid this information from the FBI, and that after 9/11 the FBI's San Diego field office and headquarters instructed agents testifying to Congress "not to reveal the full extent of Saudi involvement with Al-Qaeda."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] noted that Canestraro is a veteran of the Drug Enforcement Administration hired as an investigator at the MCDO, which provides legal defense to accused 9/11 conspirators facing tribunals at Guantanamo Bay, and that he obtained his material both through discovery and through independent interviewing in the course of building the strongest possible defense for his client.[1] Just Human characterized the findings as mapping well onto other independent investigations of CIA and Saudi involvement in 9/11 — "not the full story of 9/11, of course, but it does expose a significant strand of it."[1]
