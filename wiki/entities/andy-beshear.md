# Andy Beshear

Andy Beshear is the Governor of Kentucky.[1]

## Louisville UPS crash response

Following the crash of a [[united-parcel-service|UPS]] cargo plane shortly after takeoff from Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport on November 4, 2025, Beshear said the plane had been carrying three crew members and had struck two businesses near the airport, adding that the death toll — at least seven at the time — could rise.[1] Mayor [[craig-greenberg|Craig Greenberg]] of Louisville reported that four of the dead had been on the ground and that eleven other people were injured.[1]
