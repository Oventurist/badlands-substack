# Bill McInturff

Bill McInturff is a Republican pollster with [[public-opinion-strategies|Public Opinion Strategies]].[1]

McInturff conducted the [[november-2023-nbc-news-poll|November 2023 NBC News national poll]] together with Democratic pollster [[jeff-horwitt|Jeff Horwitt]] of [[hart-research-associates|Hart Research Associates]].[1] Reflecting on the survey's finding that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s standing had fallen to the lowest point of his presidency amid the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel-Hamas war]], McInturff said he could not recall another time when foreign affairs not involving U.S. troops had transformed the American political landscape.[1]
