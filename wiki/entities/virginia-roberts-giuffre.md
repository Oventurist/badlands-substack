# Virginia Roberts Giuffre

Virginia Roberts Giuffre was the best-known public accuser in the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] sexual abuse case, and the woman whose allegations against [[prince-andrew|Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor]] kept the former prince at the center of the scandal for more than a decade.[1]

## Allegations

Giuffre alleged that Andrew had sex with her when she was 17 years old, an account set out in an extract from her memoir published by *The Guardian*.[1] Her claims formed the backbone of years of public scrutiny of Andrew's ties to Epstein.[1]

## Death and family statement

Giuffre is said to have died by suicide in 2025.[1] Following Andrew's arrest on suspicion of misconduct in public office in February 2026, her family issued a statement reading in part: "At last. Today, our broken hearts have been lifted at the news that no one is above the law, not even royalty. On behalf of our sister, Virginia Roberts Giuffre, we extend our gratitude to the UK's [[thames-valley-police|Thames Valley Police]] for their investigation and arrest of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. He was never a prince. For survivors everywhere, Virginia did this for you."[1]

## Significance in the Epstein narrative

Within the [[truth-community|Truth Community]], Giuffre's allegations are treated as the wedge that made a royal arrest politically possible, even as commentators such as [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the resulting process charges against Andrew function as a [[deep-state-burn-cards|burn card]] that avoids the prosecutable core of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein operation]].[1]
