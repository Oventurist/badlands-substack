# Clubhouse

Clubhouse is an audio-based social media platform that became popular in 2020.[1] Its rise prompted [[twitter|Twitter]] to launch its own live audio product, [[twitter-spaces|Twitter Spaces]], in December 2020 as a direct competitor.[1]

In a February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay on the growth of audio communities on the political right, Clubhouse is cited chiefly as the competitive origin point for Spaces, which later became the dominant venue for that audience after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] acquired Twitter and restored many banned accounts.[1]
