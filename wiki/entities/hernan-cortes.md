# Hernán Cortés

Hernán Cortés was a Spanish conquistador whose surviving manuscripts are treated as priceless cultural artifacts by the government of Mexico.[1]

## 2025 manuscript repatriation

On August 13, 2025, the [[fbi|FBI]] returned to the government of Mexico a stolen manuscript page signed by Cortés on February 20, 1527.[1] Special Agent Jessica Dittmer, a member of the Bureau's Art Crime Team embedded in FBI New York's FBI-NYPD Joint Major Theft Task Force, explained that the document "outlines the payment of pesos of common gold for expenses in preparation for discovery of the spice lands, so it really gives a lot of flavor as to the planning and preparation for unchartered territory back then."[1]

Authorities believe the page was stolen in the 1980s or 1990s, and the repatriation resulted from collaboration between the FBI, the [[nypd|New York City Police Department]], the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], and the Mexican government.[1] NYPD Detective Rigel Zeledon, who investigated the case with Dittmer, credited the Southern District of New York and the FBI's attaché office in Mexico for making the recovery possible.[1]
