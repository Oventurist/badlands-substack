# Conflict, Stability and Security Fund

The Conflict, Stability, and Security Fund (CSSF) is a United Kingdom government funding vehicle for overseas conflict, stability and security programming, identified in reporting on the [[mahmoud-khalil|Mahmoud Khalil]] case as one of the British programmes Khalil worked on during his employment at the British embassy in Beirut.[1]

## Role in the Khalil case

[[middle-east-eye|Middle East Eye]] reported that Khalil worked as a programme manager at the embassy's Syria Office from 2018 to 2022, serving both the [[chevening-programme|Syria Chevening Program]] and the CSSF.[1] The disclosure formed the basis of arguments that Khalil's profile was that of a British government operative rather than a purely independent student organizer.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the CSSF's name as sounding like "a money laundering Gladio front," linking it rhetorically to Cold War–era covert political operations.[1]
