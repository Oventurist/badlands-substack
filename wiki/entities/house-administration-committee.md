# House Administration Committee

The Committee on House Administration is a standing committee of the United States House of Representatives with jurisdiction over federal election administration, among other matters. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as the congressional body whose election observers generated the documentary record now underpinning federal scrutiny of ballot handling in Arizona.

## Observers at Runbeck Election Services

The March 10, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the [[fbi|FBI]]'s criminal investigation into election administration in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona, followed concerns raised by House Administration Committee observers during the 2024 election. Those observers inspected a third-party ballot processing facility used by the county — [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]], a private contractor hired to process ballots.

According to a memo cited in the reporting, the observers found that completed mail ballots and blank ballots were stored in the same room at the facility, that no state government officials were present while ballots were being handled, and that some pallets of blank ballots appeared to have torn wrapping with ballots missing or removed. These findings preceded a grand jury subpoena seeking "terabytes of data" and other materials related to voting systems and election procedures, and are described in the Brief as part of an ongoing federal investigation into election issues in multiple states. Rep. [[abe-hamadeh|Abe Hamadeh]] (R-AZ) separately asked the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] to investigate Runbeck's handling of ballots.
