# Mike Garcia

Mike Garcia is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from California who emerged in late October 2023 as the author of the House GOP's conditions for any further American assistance to Ukraine.[1]

## Ukraine funding demands (October 2023)

Garcia wrote the list of demands that newly elected Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] hand-delivered to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], informing the administration that [[congress|Congress]] would not authorize additional funds for [[ukraine|Ukraine]] unless it answered a dozen questions about the path forward.[1] Chief among those questions were how Biden and Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] intended to win the war against [[russia|Russia]], and how long victory might take.[1]

Defending the document, Garcia said that "failure to ask these questions, and a continued willingness by Congress to enable this carte blanche mentality to date, is, in my opinion, a dereliction of duty and a recipe for disaster that will enable a Ukrainian defeat and enhance Chinese aggression."[1] His demands formed part of the broader [[ukraine-aid-funding-controversy|Ukraine aid funding controversy]] and the fight over whether Ukraine and Israel aid should be bundled in a single package.[1]
