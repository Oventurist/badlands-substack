# Abercrombie & Fitch

Abercrombie & Fitch is an American clothing brand that became a mass-market cultural fixture in the 1990s and 2000s under chief executive [[mike-jeffries|Mike Jeffries]], who ran the company from 1992 to 2014.[1] The retailer re-entered the news cycle in October 2024 when Jeffries, its former CEO, was arrested on federal sex trafficking and interstate prostitution charges.[1]

## Association with the Jeffries case

The federal indictment unsealed in October 2024 concerns conduct alleged to have occurred between 2008 and 2015 — a span that overlaps substantially with Jeffries' tenure at the head of the company — and describes an "international sex trafficking and prostitution business" staging events in England, France, Italy, Morocco, St. Barts and New York.[1] Jeffries was arrested in West Palm Beach, Florida alongside Matthew Smith and James Jacobson, and the three men each face one count of sex trafficking and fifteen counts of interstate prostitution tied to fifteen alleged victims.[1]
