# The Philadelphia Inquirer

The Philadelphia Inquirer is the principal daily newspaper of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and a source of local investigative reporting cited in [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] coverage.[1]

## Reporting on the Josh Kruger killing

In October 2023 the Inquirer reported on the killing of local journalist [[josh-kruger|Josh Kruger]], including interviews with the family of the accused, [[robert-davis|Robert Davis]].[1] The paper's reporting carried the family's assertions that Davis and Kruger had been in a years-long relationship involving drugs beginning when Davis was 15, and that Davis said Kruger had threatened to post sexually explicit videos of him online.[1] The Inquirer also reported the details of the shooting itself and the investigative trail — surveillance video and family tips — that led detectives to name Davis as a suspect.[1]

The paper's coverage was the basis on which the story, initially framed nationally as the murder of a journalist, acquired significantly more complicated dimensions.[1]
