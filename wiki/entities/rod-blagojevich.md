# Rod Blagojevich

**Rod Blagojevich** is a former Democratic Governor of Illinois who served in office between 2003 and 2009 and was later convicted on an array of federal corruption charges, including an attempt to sell the U.S. Senate seat vacated by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] after Obama's 2008 presidential election victory.[1] He was impeached and removed from office, and ultimately served eight years in prison of a 14-year sentence.[1]

## Conviction, commutation and pardon

In 2020, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] commuted Blagojevich's 14-year sentence, releasing him after eight years behind bars.[1] On Monday, February 10, 2025, Trump signed a **full pardon** for Blagojevich, an action [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary described as bringing the earlier commutation "full circle."[1]

## Relationship with Trump

Following his impeachment and removal from office, Blagojevich appeared as a contestant on Trump's NBC program *The Celebrity Apprentice* in 2010, before his corruption trial began; when Trump "fired" him from the show, he used the occasion to praise Blagojevich.[1] Blagojevich subsequently endorsed Trump in the 2024 election and attended the Republican National Convention, and defended Trump after the New York hush-money indictment, comparing Trump's legal troubles to his own.[1] "I love Trump more today than ever!" he wrote on social media at the time, adding, "When you've lived through it yourself, you recognize when they do it to someone else."[1]

## Later commentary and speculation

Blagojevich appeared on [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]]'s podcast, where he described how Obama and others, in his telling, "raked him over the coals" because he refused to go along with their schemes.[1] At the time of the pardon, reporting and rumor suggested Trump might appoint Blagojevich as U.S. ambassador to Serbia.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] connected the pardon to a broader thesis he had explored with [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] on *The Book of Trump* — that *The Apprentice* functioned as a recruiting operation for Trump's later political career, a theory he said "more and more ... seems feasible."[1]
