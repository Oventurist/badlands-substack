# Demis Hassabis

Demis Hassabis is the chief executive of [[deepmind|DeepMind]], the United Kingdom-based artificial-intelligence research laboratory owned by [[google|Google]].[1] He attended the 2023 [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] meeting in Lisbon, Portugal, where artificial intelligence was the conference's primary agenda item.[1]

In the Badlands Media article "Dark Equation," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] grouped Hassabis with [[microsoft|Microsoft]] chief executive [[satya-nadella|Satya Nadella]], [[openai|OpenAI]]'s [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] and Bilderberg chair [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]] as members of an "A.I. brigade" present at the gathering — a concentration of AI industry leadership inside a forum the author describes as historically opaque and influential over world events.[1]
