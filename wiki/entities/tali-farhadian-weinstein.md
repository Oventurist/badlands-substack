# Tali Farhadian Weinstein

Tali Farhadian Weinstein is an American attorney and former prosecutor, and the wife of hedge fund manager [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]].[1]

## Background

Born into an Iranian-Jewish family that fled Iran when she was a child, she attended Yale and Oxford, where she was a Rhodes Scholar.[1] Badlands Media treats that credential trio — Yale, Oxford, Rhodes — as a marker of [[deep-state|Deep State]] cultivation.[1] She served as both a federal and a state prosecutor before entering electoral politics.[1] She married Boaz Weinstein in 2010.[1]

## 2021 district attorney campaign

Farhadian Weinstein was a candidate in the 2021 race for New York County District Attorney.[1] Had she prevailed, she — rather than [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]] — would have been the prosecutor bringing the "hush money" case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The article's author argues the case has gone badly for the prosecution and suggests Bragg was in fact permitted to win precisely so that the manipulation of the justice system would be exposed.[1]
