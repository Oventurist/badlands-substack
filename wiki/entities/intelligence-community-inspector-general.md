# Intelligence Community Inspector General

The Intelligence Community Inspector General (ICIG) is the internal oversight office responsible for receiving and evaluating whistleblower complaints arising within the U.S. intelligence community. Its handling of the 2019 [[ukraine|Ukraine]] complaint against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] became the subject of renewed scrutiny in 2026 after memos from its files were declassified.[1]

## The August 2019 memos

Newly declassified ICIG memos from August 2019, released by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] at the request of [[just-the-news|Just the News]], document that the whistleblower whose complaint triggered Trump's [[first-impeachment-of-donald-trump|first impeachment]] acknowledged potential perceptions of political bias, including prior work with then–Vice President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and being a registered Democrat.[1] The inspector general nonetheless concluded that those indications did not alter the agency's assessment that the complaint "appears credible."[1]

According to the memos, the whistleblower told investigators he had worked closely with Biden on Ukraine policy and had traveled with him to Ukraine, and the complaint itself was based on secondhand information rather than direct knowledge of the events described.[1]

The documents also describe a second intelligence official, identified as "Witness 2," who reviewed the Trump–Ukraine call transcript for situational awareness and assisted the whistleblower, citing a "moral and patriotic duty."[1] The memo states that this official had previously worked with former [[fbi|FBI]] agent [[peter-strzok|Peter Strzok]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued the memos demonstrate that the testimony triggering Trump's first impeachment was baseless, describing it as "a textbook example" of treason and contending that the disclosure implicates multiple intelligence agencies as well as both the Democratic and Republican parties.[1] He called for a wholesale reevaluation of the makeup of the intelligence community, suggesting agencies that claim ignorance be disbanded and that the military assume intelligence responsibilities under civilian control of DNI Gabbard.[1]
