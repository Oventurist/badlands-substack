# Tammany Hall

Tammany Hall was the New York political machine whose boss, William "Boss" Tweed, rose to power in 1863.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back) By 1871 Tweed's corruption and abuse of power had become widely known.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

Tweed biographer Kenneth D. Ackerman described the operation: "It's hard not to admire the skill behind Tweed's system… The Tweed ring at its height was an engineering marvel, strong and solid, strategically deployed to control key power points: the courts, the legislature, the treasury and the ballot box. Its frauds had a grandeur of scale and an elegance of structure: money-laundering, profit sharing and organization."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

Notably, Tweed punished those who questioned his electoral practices. He died in prison in 1878.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back) The episode is cited as evidence that what is now labeled [[election-denial|election denialism]] is often a rational response to actual election fraud.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)
