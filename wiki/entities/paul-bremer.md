# Paul Bremer

L. Paul "Jerry" Bremer is an American diplomat who served as the American proconsul in Iraq following the 2003 invasion, heading the [[coalition-provisional-authority|Coalition Provisional Authority]].

In the Badlands Media article "[[bad-actors|Bad Actors]]," Bremer is identified — via ShadowProof.com's summary of James Risen's reporting — as the official who, in the first days after the fall of Baghdad and continuing for over a year, "on his own" ordered $12 billion to $14 billion in cash to be flown to Iraq, with an additional $5 billion sent by electronic transfer; some sources put the total as high as $20 billion. The article notes the two-billion-dollar uncertainty in the airlift figure as itself indicative of the absence of controls.

The head of the [[treasury-department|Treasury Department]] team that worked on Iraq's financial reconstruction after the invasion is quoted as saying, "We did not know that Bremer was flying in all that cash… I can't see a reason for it." The article treats this as evidence that the airlift had no legitimate purpose other than to make theft easier, and argues that the cash flow into and out of Iraq was controlled by the [[cia|CIA]] and what the author terms the shadow government. Congressional investigators on the House Government Reform Committee charged in 2005 that U.S. officials "used virtually no financial controls to account for these enormous cash withdrawals once they arrived in Iraq." Part of the missing money was later traced by [[stuart-w-bowen-jr|Stuart W. Bowen Jr.]] to a bunker in [[lebanon|Lebanon]].
