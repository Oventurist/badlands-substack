# Lex Fridman

Lex Fridman is a podcaster known for long-form interviews with political and technical figures.[1]

In early January 2025 Fridman published a three-hour interview with Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] that the Ukrainian government-run United24 platform described as "bold" and candid. The conversation focused largely on the ongoing Russian invasion of Ukraine and on Kyiv's relationship with the United States under both [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

In that interview Zelensky lamented Biden's hesitation to preventatively sanction Russia before the 2022 full-scale invasion, describing the practice of threatening to sanction Moscow only after an invasion as "bullshit."[1]
