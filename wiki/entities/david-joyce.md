# David Joyce

David Joyce is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Ohio who played a procedural role in the October 2023 struggle over the speakership.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speaker fight

After [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] said on a Thursday in October 2023 that he would not hold a third ballot for speaker and would instead let interim speaker [[patrick-mchenry|Patrick McHenry]] hold the gavel until January, Joyce was reported to be drafting legislation that would grant McHenry the full powers of the office.[1]

The proposal angered many Republicans because McHenry was backed by Democrats; Representative [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]] said Republicans "should never allow a Democrat-backed coalition government," and [[steve-scalise|Steve Scalise]] and [[elise-stefanik|Elise Stefanik]] also opposed a Democrat-friendly speakership running into January.[1] Amid the backlash, Jordan reversed course and appeared open to a third vote, saying he wanted to talk to the 20 holdouts who had blocked his first two attempts.[1]
