# Robert A. Lovett

Robert Lovett was an American banker and defense official listed in "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" among the most notable public-facing members of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society.[1]

Lovett was a partner in [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]], served as Assistant Secretary of War for Air from 1941 to 1945, then as Deputy Secretary of Defense and as Secretary of Defense in 1950.[1] He was also a leading member of the New York [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1] His career is presented in the article as typical of the Order's placement pattern, in which Bonesmen move between the Wall Street investment banks dominated by the society — Brown Brothers Harriman and [[guaranty-trust-company|Guaranty Trust]] — and senior national-security posts.[1]
