# Royce Lamberth

Royce Lamberth is a United States District Judge appointed by President Ronald Reagan who, on Tuesday, April 29, 2025, ordered the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration to restore $12 million that [[us-congress|Congress]] had appropriated for [[radio-free-europe|Radio Free Europe]], an outlet described in reporting as at risk of going dark for the first time in 75 years.[1]

## The Radio Free Europe ruling

Lamberth's order was one of a series of judicial interventions against the administration's impoundment of appropriated funds during Trump's first 100 days.[1] Rather than confining himself to the funding question, Lamberth used the ruling to deliver what [[cnn|CNN]] characterized as a lesson on the three branches of government, cautioning that the system of checks and balances established by the U.S. Constitution must remain intact if the nation is to continue to thrive.[1]

He acknowledged directly the criticism aimed at the federal bench: "In interviews, podcasts, and op-eds, people from both inside and outside government have variously accused the courts — myself included — of fomenting a constitutional crisis, usurping the Article II powers of the Presidency, undercutting the popular will, or dictating how Executive agencies can and should be run."[1] Those notions, he wrote, reflect a "fundamental misunderstanding" of the role of the federal judiciary and of the Constitution itself.[1]

## Reception in independent media

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] dismissed the ruling curtly — "Okay, commie. Good luck with that." — reflecting the broader Badlands position that the President holds plenary authority over the Executive Branch and that judicial freezes of impoundment decisions are at best temporary.[1] That position was argued at greater length in the same brief in response to Democratic claims that Trump had targeted at least $430 billion in appropriated funding.[1]
