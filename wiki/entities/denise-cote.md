# Denise Cote

Denise Cote is a United States federal judge in New York who in October 2025 ruled that the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] must disclose what kinds of security clearances had been granted to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

## Ruling on Musk's security clearances

Cote issued the ruling on Wednesday, October 8, 2025, writing that "the public has an interest in knowing whether the leader of SpaceX and Starlink holds the appropriate security clearances."[1] The decision came in a suit brought by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] against the [[defense-counterintelligence-and-security-agency|Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency]] after the newspaper petitioned the agency under the [[freedom-of-information-act|Freedom of Information Act]] about Musk's clearances; the agency repeatedly rejected the request, arguing that disclosure could constitute an unwarranted invasion of Musk's privacy.[1]

In her ruling Cote held that Musk's public status as a "special government employee" leading the administration's [[doge|DOGE]] cost-cutting program, together with the billionaire's own public comments about his clearances and personal life, outweighed those privacy concerns.[1] She pointed specifically to disclosures Musk made to millions on social media, including a February post in which he wrote: "I've had a top secret clearance for many years and have clearances that themselves are classified."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary mocked the reasoning behind the order, suggesting Musk should respond that his clearance level is itself classified, and characterizing the judge's stated rationale — which the commentary tied to Musk's publicized cannabis use on a podcast — as superficial.[1]
