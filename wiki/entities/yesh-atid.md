# Yesh Atid

Yesh Atid is the Israeli political party led by opposition leader [[yair-lapid|Yair Lapid]].[1]

## Profile and positioning

The party brands itself as centrist and has lured away some centrist members of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s [[likud|Likud]] party, but it has also attracted a number of members from Israel's Labor Party, which promotes a platform of socialism that American observers would characterize as far to the left.[1] Lapid himself was raised by left-wing liberals.[1]

Badlands commentary situates Yesh Atid within a broader Israeli political spectrum in which both right- and left-wing parties embrace strong centralized government as a necessary apparatus to preserve the Jewish state — a dynamic traced to the socialist and communist milieu of the Eastern European ghettos of the [[pale-of-settlement|Pale of Settlement]] from which many of the pre-state colonists emigrated in the 1930s and 1940s.[1]

## 2021–2022 rotation government and 2025 attacks on Netanyahu

Lapid and [[naftali-bennett|Naftali Bennett]] served as co-Prime Ministers for 18 months between June 2021 and December 2022 — Bennett as PM for 12 months and Lapid for six, with the other serving as Alternate Prime Minister — in an effort to force Netanyahu out of power and end his political career.[1] Netanyahu nonetheless returned, striking a Christmas Day 2022 coalition deal with radical factions that required modifying a 1994 law barring racial supremacists from the [[knesset|Knesset]].[1]

In April 2025, Lapid joined Bennett in attacking Netanyahu after reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had blocked a proposed Israeli strike on Iran's nuclear facilities, with Lapid saying he had proposed attacking Iran's oil fields in October but that "Netanyahu was afraid, and stopped it."[1]
