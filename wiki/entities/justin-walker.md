# Justin Walker

Justin Walker is a judge on the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-dc-circuit|U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit]] appointed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

In April 2026 Walker joined [[neomi-rao|Neomi Rao]] in a divided panel decision ordering U.S. District Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]] to end his criminal contempt investigation of Trump administration officials arising from the March 2025 deportation flights of Venezuelan migrants to El Salvador under the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]].[1] The majority wrote that the district court's inquiry into executive branch deliberations on national security and diplomacy was "a clear abuse of discretion" that had reached "a legal dead end."[1] Judge [[michelle-childs|Michelle Childs]], a Biden appointee, dissented.[1]
