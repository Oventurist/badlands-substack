# Martha MacCallum

Martha MacCallum is a [[fox-news|Fox News]] anchor named, alongside [[bret-baier|Bret Baier]], as a moderator of the debate that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] proposed to hold against [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] on September 4, 2024.[1]

Trump announced the arrangement on Truth Social, saying the Fox News debate would be held in Pennsylvania at a site to be determined, with rules similar to those of his earlier debate against [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] but "WITH A FULL ARENA AUDIENCE."[1] He said the previously scheduled ABC debate had been terminated because Biden would no longer participate and because he was in litigation against the network.[1]
