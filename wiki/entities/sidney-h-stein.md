# Sidney H. Stein

Sidney H. Stein is a United States District Judge who presided over the federal bribery prosecution of former U.S. Senator [[robert-menendez|Bob Menendez]] in Manhattan.[1]

## Menendez sentencing

On Wednesday, January 29, 2025, Stein sentenced Menendez to 11 years in prison for accepting bribes of gold and cash and acting as an agent of Egypt.[1] Delivering the sentence in a packed Manhattan courtroom after Menendez tearfully told the court he had lost everything he cared about except his family, Stein said: "You were successful, powerful, you stood at the apex of our political system," adding, "Somewhere along the way, and I don't know when it was, you lost your way and working for the public good became working for your good."[1]
