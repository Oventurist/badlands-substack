# Dom Lucre

**Dom Lucre** is an online political commentator named by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as an example of what he calls the "content clowns" faction of the [[america-first-movement|America First]] coalition.[1]

## Placement in the coalition taxonomy

In [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], DeLarme defines the content-clown category impressionistically — "you know it when you see it" — and lists Lucre alongside [[ian-carroll|Ian Carroll]] and [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]], adding [[alex-jones|Alex Jones]] as an optional inclusion while conceding that Jones, whatever one thinks of him, was an instrumental part of the big-tent coalition that elected [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in 2024.[1] The category sits within DeLarme's broader argument that the coalition comprises numerous mutually antagonistic factions whose divisions have widened over the course of Trump's second term.[1]
