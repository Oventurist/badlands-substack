# Tymur Tkachenko

Tymur Tkachenko is the head of the Kyiv Military Administration, who reported the casualty figures from the deadliest Russian assault on the Ukrainian capital in 2025.[1]

## June 2025 Kyiv strike

Tkachenko stated that the Russian drone and missile attack on Kyiv early on Wednesday, June 18, 2025 killed 28 people across the city and wounded 142 others, making it the deadliest assault on the capital that year.[1] A nine-story apartment building in Kyiv's Solomianskyi district took a direct missile hit that brought down the structure, killing 23 people there.[1] Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], presidential office head [[andrii-yermak|Andrii Yermak]] and Interior Minister [[ihor-klymenko|Ihor Klymenko]] visited the site to lay flowers and pay tribute.[1]
