# Gary Shapley

Gary Shapley is an [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] supervisory agent who became one of two whistleblowers to disclose alleged political interference in the federal tax investigation of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]. He had come forward publicly to reveal his identity several weeks before the June 2023 release of the whistleblower testimony; contemporary coverage of the disclosures rendered his surname as "Shipley."[1]

## Allegations

According to the whistleblower testimony, the IRS was notified of potential evidence located "in the guest house of former Vice President Biden," but investigators were rebuffed by U.S. Attorney [[lesley-wolf|Lesley Wolf]], who reportedly said there was "no way" that such a search warrant "would ever get approved."[1] The same disclosures alleged that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] buried evidence of Hunter Biden's tax crimes and prevented U.S. Attorney [[david-weiss|David Weiss]] from bringing charges in two jurisdictions.[1]

## Ways and Means disclosures (November 2023)

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited disclosures from the [[house-ways-and-means-committee|House Ways and Means Committee]] based on documents and testimony from whistleblowers [[joseph-ziegler|Joseph Ziegler]] and Gary Shapley as central to the November 2023 revelations about the [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] tax investigation, including the once-secret IRS memos describing cooperating witness [[jeffrey-gelfound|Jeffrey Gelfound]].[2]

## Target of the 2023 Hunter Biden lawsuit

Although not named as a defendant, Shapley's disclosures were central to [[hunter-biden-v-internal-revenue-service|Hunter Biden's September 2023 lawsuit]] against the [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]], which alleged that agents illegally released his tax return information; the complaint focused on statements made publicly, in congressional testimony, and in interviews by Shapley, fellow whistleblower [[joseph-ziegler|Joseph Ziegler]], and their lawyers.[3]
