# Daniel Serwer

Daniel Serwer is identified by Badlands Media as the connection between the Serbian movement [[otpor|Otpor!]] and the [[united-states-institute-of-peace|United States Institute of Peace]], where he served as director of the institute's Balkans Initiative.[1]

The article states that Serwer "came right out of the CIA/DoD civil society pipeline," having been a Senior Fellow at the Foreign Policy Institute of the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies, and notes that he appears prominently in the documentaries the piece recommends on how "rent-a-riot" operations are conducted.[1]
