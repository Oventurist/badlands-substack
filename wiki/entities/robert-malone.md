# Robert Malone

Dr. Robert Malone is a physician and vaccine researcher who became one of the most widely cited critics of COVID-19 vaccine policy, publishing commentary through his Substack newsletter.[1]

## Writings on the pharmaceutical industry

In a January 2023 Substack essay on what happens to vaccine clinical trial participants, Malone described the pharmaceutical industry as "another common abuser of society."[1] Asked how he had been able to accurately predict the course of the pandemic, he answered each time that "all of this was just an escalation of what the industry had done previously."[1] He argued that over the years the industry had grown more brazen in pushing unsafe and ineffective medications onto the market and paying off the government to gaslight injured parties, rather than fulfilling its obligation to investigate and withdraw harmful medications.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that the public had been led to believe the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]] and other regulatory agencies double- and triple-check drugs and vaccines, but that the preceding years taught that "blind trust NEVER works."[1] He argued that trust requires follow-up, verification, validation and evaluation, and that the discovery of yet another vaccine program that was "a dumpster fire of proper processes" should prompt Americans to retake their role as "WE THE PEOPLE," keeping the institutions and branches of government they appoint on the straight and narrow.[1]
