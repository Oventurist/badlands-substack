# John Coleman

Dr. John Coleman was an author and former intelligence officer, best known for the book *Conspirators' Hierarchy: The Story of the Committee of 300*.[1]

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites Coleman in "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 4" for the claim that "The Bilderberg Conference is the creation of MI6 under the direction of the Royal Institute of International Affairs."[1] DeLarme follows the quotation by noting that the Royal Institute of International Affairs — [[chatham-house|Chatham House]] — is the British extension of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], and that the American intelligence connections around the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]]'s founding make it legitimate to argue the conferences were at least partially organised and sponsored by the [[cia|CIA]].[1]

## Claims about Tavistock and American culture

Coleman, described as a former MI6 intelligence agent, is the principal source for the claim that the [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock Institute]] and its subsidiaries have directly manipulated essentially every aspect of American culture over the past century.[2] He asserts that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] was founded and is run on guidelines established by Tavistock, and that all Tavistock and American foundation techniques share the single goal of breaking down the psychological strength of the individual so that he cannot oppose the dictators of the World Order.[2] The breakdown of the family, religion and patriotism, together with the promotion of sexually deviant behavior, are in his account tools of control, while Freudian psychotherapy techniques are used to create permanent mental illness in some subjects.[2]

Coleman also holds that the [[stanford-research-institute|Stanford Research Institute]], acting through Tavistock, controls America's [[national-education-association|National Education Association]], with the American education system designed to produce easily controlled people trained not to be independent thinkers.[2] He believes the [[1960s-american-counterculture-movement|1960s American counterculture revolution]] was created by Tavistock and funded and put into action by the CIA, with LSD moving from the [[tavistock-clinic|Tavistock Clinic]] into the CIA's [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] program in the 1950s before being loosed on young Americans in the 1960s.[2] He estimates Tavistock's annual budget in the billions and says it and its subsidiaries are fully funded by US taxpayers.[2]

His most controversial claims concern popular music: that [[the-beatles|the Beatles]], the Rolling Stones and the Grateful Dead were all Tavistock creations, and that [[theodor-adorno|Theodor Adorno]], a musicologist and leading member of the Tavistock/[[frankfurt-school|Frankfurt School]] milieu, wrote all of the Beatles' music and lyrics — the purpose being to manufacture the 1960s counterculture and the divisions it produced in America.[2]
