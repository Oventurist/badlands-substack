# John Coleman

Dr. John Coleman was an author and former intelligence officer, best known for the book *Conspirators' Hierarchy: The Story of the Committee of 300*.[1]

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites Coleman in "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 4" for the claim that "The Bilderberg Conference is the creation of MI6 under the direction of the Royal Institute of International Affairs."[1] DeLarme follows the quotation by noting that the Royal Institute of International Affairs — [[chatham-house|Chatham House]] — is the British extension of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], and that the American intelligence connections around the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]]'s founding make it legitimate to argue the conferences were at least partially organised and sponsored by the [[cia|CIA]].[1]
