# William Henry Harrison

**William Henry Harrison** was the ninth president of the United States, elected in 1840 as the first victory of the American nationalist grouping that rallied around the Whig Party of [[john-quincy-adams|John Quincy Adams]].[1]

## Program

Harrison took office on the basis of reviving Alexander Hamilton's national bank and re-launching protective tariffs and internal improvements.[1] Legislation for a Third National Bank passed both houses of Congress, but Harrison served only three months and died under mysterious circumstances before it could be signed into law.[1]

## Death

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] states flatly that both Whig presidents of this period — Harrison and [[zachary-taylor|Zachary Taylor]] — were poisoned.[1] Harrison is counted among the five presidents who made serious efforts to revive America's nationalist system after the killing of the Second National Bank in 1836 and who ended up dead while in office: Harrison in 1841, Taylor in 1850, [[abraham-lincoln|Lincoln]] in 1865, Garfield in 1880 and McKinley in 1901.[1]
