# William Henry Harrison

William Henry Harrison (1773–1841) was the ninth President of the United States, elected in 1840 as the first [[whig-party|Whig Party]] president and dead within three months of taking office.

## Program and death

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s Avoiding WWIII describes Harrison's 1840 victory as the first triumph of the nationalists, won on the basis of reviving Alexander Hamilton's national bank and re-launching protective tariffs and internal improvements. The article asserts that Harrison was poisoned, serving only three months and dying under mysterious circumstances before legislation for a Third National Bank — already passed by both houses of [[congress|Congress]] — could be signed into law.

Harrison is counted in the article as the first of five presidents who died in office after making serious efforts to revive America's nationalist system, alongside [[zachary-taylor|Zachary Taylor]] in 1850, [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]] in 1865, James Garfield in 1880 and William McKinley in 1901 — a sequence following the killing of the [[second-bank-of-the-united-states|Second Bank of the United States]] in 1836.
