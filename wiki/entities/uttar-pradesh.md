# Uttar Pradesh

**Uttar Pradesh** is a state in northern India with a population of more than 200 million residents.[1] In Badlands Media's coverage of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]]'s pandemic instruments, Uttar Pradesh is presented as a leading example of the benefits of non-compliance with WHO guidance.[1]

## COVID-19 response

According to the article, the government of Uttar Pradesh distributed [[ivermectin|Ivermectin]] and "squashed the Delta Wave," an outcome Ryan DeLarme argued would never have occurred had the state complied with the WHO's guidelines.[1] The case is cited alongside Florida's departure from federal COVID guidance and Sweden's refusal to lock down as evidence that jurisdictions retained real policy latitude during the pandemic.[1]

## Significance in the non-compliance argument

The article's closing argument holds that even if the [[who-pandemic-accord|WHO CA+]] and the proposed amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]] are ratified, WHO edicts that "fall on def ears" confer no practical control.[1] Uttar Pradesh functions in that argument as proof of concept at the scale of a very large population.[1]
