# Phil Gramm

Phil Gramm was a United States Senator from Texas, hailed as one of the biggest conservatives in Congress, and the author of the [[financial-services-modernization-act|Financial Services Modernization Act]] signed into law by President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]].[1]

Gramm led the charge to deregulate the banking system and to allow investment banks once again to sell risky securities to the American people through commercial banks, gutting the protections of the [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall Act]].[1] After leaving Congress he became vice chairman of the investment bank [[ubs|UBS]].[1] Joe Lange cites this career path as evidence of a rigged system in which politicians do the banks' bidding and are rewarded with high-paying jobs at those banks when they leave office.[1]
