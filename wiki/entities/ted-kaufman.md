# Ted Kaufman

Ted Kaufman is a longtime friend and constant adviser to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]. In June 2024, at age 85, he was identified by Axios as one of a very small group of decision-makers with real influence over whether Biden would remain the Democratic candidate for president.[1]

## Role in the Biden inner circle

Axios reported that Dr. [[jill-biden|Jill Biden]], Biden's younger sister Valerie Biden, and Kaufman — plus a small band of [[white-house|White House]] advisers — were "the only Biden deciders."[1] The outlet described this decades-long kitchen cabinet as functioning simultaneously as an extended family, a council of elders and a "governing oligarchy," holding sway over decisions big and small in Biden's life and presidency.[1] Following Biden's June 2024 debate performance, Axios reported that the only way he would step aside was if this same group of lifelong loyalists decided it was time.[1]
