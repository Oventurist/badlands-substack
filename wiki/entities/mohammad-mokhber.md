# Mohammad Mokhber

**Mohammad Mokhber** is an Iranian official who served as First Vice President of [[iran|Iran]].[1] He appears in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] record in connection with the Iranian government's response to the [[2024-kerman-bombings|2024 Kerman bombings]].[1]

## Response to the Kerman bombings

After the twin explosions at a memorial for [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] in the southeastern Iranian city of Kerman killed nearly 100 people and wounded 284 others, [[islamic-state|Islamic State]] claimed responsibility on January 4, 2024.[1] Speaking to reporters in Kerman, Mokhber vowed retaliation, declaring that "a very strong retaliation will be meted out to them by the hands of the soldiers of Soleimani."[1] Tehran more broadly pledged revenge for what was described as the bloodiest such attack since the 1979 Islamic Revolution.[1]
