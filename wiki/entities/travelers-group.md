# Travelers Group

The Travelers Group was an American insurance and financial services conglomerate whose 1998 merger with Citicorp created [[citigroup|Citigroup]], at the time the largest bank in the world.[1]

## An illegal merger, legalized after the fact

Under the [[glass-steagall|Glass-Steagall Act]], [[citibank|Citibank]] as a commercial bank was required to remain separated from any insurance company or investment bank, which made the Travelers combination unlawful when it was set in motion.[1] Badlands Media stresses the timeline: Citibank was already completing the merger before the law was changed, and the [[financial-services-modernization-act|Financial Services Modernization Act]] passed by Congress in 1999 gave the deal *retrospective* clearance.[1] The same act unleashed a broader wave of Wall Street consolidation later blamed for forcing taxpayers to spend billions bailing out the enlarged banks after the sub-prime mortgage crisis.[1]

Because the merger made Citicorp the biggest bank in the world, the combined institution was renamed Citigroup.[1] In the Badlands account the person positioned to benefit most from removing the legal obstacle was [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], by then the bank's largest individual shareholder.[1]
