# Rochelle Walensky

Rochelle Walensky is an American physician who served as Director of the U.S. [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] during the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration's management of the COVID-19 pandemic.[1] On Friday, May 5, 2023, she announced that she would step down from the role, with the agency confirming that she would leave at the end of June.[1]

## Resignation

In her resignation letter to President Biden, Walensky framed her departure around the formal winding down of the pandemic emergency, writing that "the end of the COVID-19 public health emergency marks a tremendous transition for our country, for public health, and in my tenure as CDC Director."[1] She added that she had taken the role at the president's request "with the goal of leaving behind the dark days of the pandemic and moving CDC — and public health — forward into a much better and more trusted place."[1] The resignation was first reported by [[fox-news|FOX News]].[1]

## Criticism from the Truth Community

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the departure as overdue, greeting it with "good riddance" while urging that all of her records and communications be retained.[1] Writing for the outlet, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Walensky had presided over multiple scandals involving COVID-19 case rates and death statistics, as well as vaccine mandates and school closures characterized as illogical, with some estimates holding that the closures set American students back by as much as twenty years.[1]

Critics seized on Walensky's own reflection that the pandemic "taught us and showed us is that now we need to talk to the American people," contrasting it with an argument that she had repeatedly misled the public and had prioritized the input of teachers' unions over that of parents in her policymaking.[1] Badlands commentary linked her exit to the concurrent media rehabilitation of [[american-federation-of-teachers|American Federation of Teachers]] president [[randi-weingarten|Randi Weingarten]], presenting the pairing as evidence that "the regime" was engaged in a broader project of COVID-19 revisionism.[1] The same commentary noted a countervailing trend: homeschooling had absorbed more than one million additional children relative to pre-pandemic levels, a figure expected to keep rising as parental confidence in government schooling eroded.[1]
