# Judy Shelton

**Judy Shelton** is an economist whom President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] nominated to the [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve]].[1]

Badlands Media states that Trump selected her because she was an advocate of bringing back the [[gold-standard|gold standard]], which in the United States spanned from 1873 to 1933.[1] Her nomination is presented as part of Trump's broader effort to revive the older [[hard-money|hard money]] system in a posture reminiscent of [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]].[1]
