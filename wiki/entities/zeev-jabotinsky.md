# Ze'ev Jabotinsky

Ze'ev Jabotinsky was the founding figure of the [[revisionist-zionism|Revisionist Movement]], a form of [[zionism|Zionism]] calling for territorial maximalism on both sides of the Jordan River, with the ambition of annexing both Palestine and Jordan as parts of Israel.[1]

[[nathan-mileikowsky|Nathan Mileikowsky]] — grandfather of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] — joined the Revisionist Movement after immigrating to Mandatory Palestine in 1920 and became one of its leading advocates under Jabotinsky.[1] Mileikowsky and his sons would affiliate with the [[irgun|Irgun]], the organization described in the Badlands account as a terrorist group that engaged in irregular warfare against Palestinian civilians throughout the 1930s and 1940s.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that Netanyahu is "the prodigal son of Ze'ev Jabotinsky and the Revisionist Movement," and that Netanyahu's long-held objective has been the fulfillment of Jabotinsky's dream of a [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]].[1] Under this reading, the invasion of Rafah was designed to provoke the Arabs and [[iran|Iran]] into attacking Israel, creating the conditions of war that could ultimately have resulted in annexation of lands expanding Israel's borders far beyond the Jordan River.[1]
