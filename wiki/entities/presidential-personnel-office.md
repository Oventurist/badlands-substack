# Presidential Personnel Office

The **Presidential Personnel Office** (PPO) is the White House office responsible for recruiting, vetting, and placing political appointees across the executive branch. During President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term it has been headed by [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]].[1]

## Role in the DOGE Wind-Down

The PPO figured in commentary about the November 2025 disbandment of the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]].[1] [[reuters|Reuters]] reported that the [[office-of-personnel-management|Office of Personnel Management]] had taken over many of DOGE's functions, but [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] questioned whether the true landing point for the effort was OPM or the PPO under Scavino.[1] Under that reading, the DOGE teams were largely automated and embedded within individual agencies, central DOGE was always temporary, and efficiency metrics would be reported through the agencies themselves — with only the White House, through the PPO, holding the aggregate picture.[1] The timing of any such reporting, in this framing, would be strategic, with public disclosure withheld until the identification, removal, and prosecution of waste, fraud, and abuse were complete.[1]
