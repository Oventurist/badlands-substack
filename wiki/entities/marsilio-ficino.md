# Marsilio Ficino

Marsilio Ficino was a Renaissance Christian writer counted, in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]], among those who considered [[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes Trismegistus]] a wise pagan prophet who foresaw the coming of Christianity.[1] He appears alongside Lactantius, Augustine, [[giordano-bruno|Giordano Bruno]], Campanella and Giovanni Pico della Mirandola.[1]

These writers believed in [[prisca-theologia|prisca theologia]], the doctrine that a single true theology threads through all religions, given by God to man in antiquity and transmitted through a series of prophets including Zoroaster and Plato, and appropriated the Hermetic teachings to demonstrate its verity.[1] Ficino formed his opinion on the epithet "Trismegistus" from the [[divine-pymander|Divine Pymander]], which states that "they called him Trismegistus because he was the greatest philosopher and the greatest priest and the greatest king."[1]
