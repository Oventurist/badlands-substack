# Ron Brown

Ronald H. Brown was United States Secretary of Commerce under President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], and, in the Badlands account, the single figure standing at the center of the alleged transfer of American missile and satellite technology to [[communist-china|Communist China]] during Clinton's first term.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Brown received the Commerce appointment because he had been a major fundraiser for Clinton's first presidential run, and he had previously served as chairman of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Criminal investigation

In the spring of 1995, Attorney General Janet Reno stated that Brown had apparently failed to properly report large payments from his business partner, Dallas entrepreneur Nolanda Sue Hill.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) A federal appeals court subsequently named Miami attorney Daniel S. Pearson — a former state judge and federal prosecutor — as [[independent-counsel|independent counsel]] to investigate Brown's financial affairs, making Pearson the fourth special counsel then probing officials of the Clinton administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Legal sources at the time expected the inquiry to run well into the 1996 presidential campaign.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The investigation outlived its target. A nine-count federal indictment later charged Hill, 53, and her financial aide Kenneth C. White, 49, with conspiring to defraud the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] and the [[federal-deposit-insurance-corporation|Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation]] between 1989 and 1995; the indictment never mentioned Brown by name although he had been the original target.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Brown's partners were indicted and convicted.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Trade missions and technology transfer

[[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] litigation established that seats aboard Brown's Commerce Department trade missions were sold to raise funds for the Democratic National Committee and the 1996 Clinton/Gore campaign, with the Badlands source asserting overwhelming evidence that Bill and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] knew of and approved the scheme.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) More serious than the sale of public property for campaign contributions, according to the same account, were technology transfers approved and overseen by Brown's Commerce Department that breached national security.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Bernard Schwartz, head of [[loral-corporation|Loral Corporation]] and a major DNC donor, accompanied Brown on a 1994 trade mission to China, during which Brown arranged a meeting between Schwartz and a Chinese government official — a meeting that led to a transfer of American missile technology later investigated by [[congress|Congress]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Brown is described as the liaison between China and the Clinton administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

At the time of his death Brown was under subpoena to produce documents on the sale of trade-mission seats in Judicial Watch's suit against the Commerce Department.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Hill testified under oath that Brown had shown her such a collection of documents in an ostrich-skin portfolio; the documents were withheld in violation of the subpoena and a FOIA request.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Brown reportedly said of his mounting legal troubles: "I am too old to go to jail. If I go down, I'll take everyone else down with me."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Death

Brown was killed on April 3, 1996, when an Air Force plane crashed into a mountainside near Dubrovnik, Croatia, then considered a war zone; thirty-four people accompanying him on the trade mission also died.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The aircraft suffered suspicious navigational failures.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) With Brown dead, the independent counsel inquiry was closed out and the matter transferred to the Justice Department under Janet Reno, since Brown's partners were not administration officials — an outcome the article characterizes as taking the target off the Clinton administration and securing the cover-up.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

### The head wound

A perfectly round .45-inch inwardly beveling hole was discovered in the top of Brown's head as his body was processed by the [[armed-forces-institute-of-pathology|Armed Forces Institute of Pathology]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The story broke on November 24 in a report by Christopher Ruddy in the *Pittsburgh Tribune-Review*, quoting Lt. Col. Steve Cogswell, an AFIP deputy medical examiner, who questioned the official finding of death by multiple blunt-force trauma based on x-rays and photographs.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Original x-rays reportedly showed possible metal fragments in the brain consistent with a high-velocity gunshot wound; those two head x-rays went missing from the file.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) On December 5, 1997, AFIP imposed a gag order on Cogswell.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

On December 9, 1997, Lt. Col. David Hause, one of AFIP's leading experts on gunshot wounds, corroborated Cogswell's account. Hause was present when Chief Petty Officer Kathleen Janoski exclaimed, "Wow, look at the hole in Ron Brown's head," and verified that the wound penetrated the skull and exposed brain matter, describing it as looking "like a punched-out .45-caliber entrance hole."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) After Hause spoke to Ruddy, the gag order was broadened to cover all AFIP personnel.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Interpretation

The Badlands narrative holds that Brown, panicked and facing an independent counsel rather than a friendly Justice Department, was vocal about not taking the fall and was therefore eliminated, after which the congressional inquiries into the sale of military technology to China were buried and Clinton was re-elected.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The article compares Brown's position to that of Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] in [[benghazi|Benghazi]] — "same playbook" — and notes the [[qanon|Q]] claim that seven of every ten plane crashes are targeted kills.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) It further argues that the [[monica-lewinsky|Monica Lewinsky]] scandal, breaking from the same year, consumed the media narrative and shielded the far graver allegations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
