# Within Our Lifetime

Within Our Lifetime (WOL) is a pro-Palestinian activist organization based in New York City, known for staging disruptive street demonstrations and for its founder, Nerdeen Kiswani.[1]

## Grand Central Terminal action, April 2025

On Monday, April 7, 2025, WOL organized a demonstration that began at 4:00 p.m. outside United Nations headquarters, timed to coincide with World Health Day and with Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s visit to the White House.[1] Protesters then moved into New York City's Grand Central Terminal, where dozens of demonstrators chanted and waved flags inside and at the entrance of the terminal.[1] Video of the action was posted to X by Kiswani.[1]

According to the group's account, the New York Police Department shut down multiple entrances to the terminal, bringing "the heart of NYC to a halt," and the demonstration also passed the United Nations and the Egyptian embassy.[1] Kiswani wrote that "We are just getting started," a phrase that supplied the headline for coverage of the protest.[1]
