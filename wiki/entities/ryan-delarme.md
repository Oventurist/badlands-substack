# Ryan DeLarme

Ryan DeLarme is a writer and contributor to Badlands Media, and the author of the essay "A Glaring Double Standard," which argues that the American right's commitment to free expression collapsed after the [[october-7-attacks|October 7th attacks]].[1] Readers are directed to more of his work at the Post-Liberal, published at postliberalnews.substack.com.[1]

## Argument and positioning

DeLarme writes from a self-consciously non-partisan free-speech position, describing himself as unmoved by claims of feeling unsafe whether they come from campus liberals or from pro-Israel advocates such as [[eyal-yakoby|Eyal Yakoby]].[1] He states he is not a fan of [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] — objecting to a foreign policy outlook he equates with that of Joe Biden and John McCain — nor of Douglas Murray, while agreeing with specific free-speech arguments each has made.[1] He disclaims any position on the Israel–Palestine conflict itself, saying the situation is outside his wheelhouse and that his concern is free speech in America.[1]

## Core thesis

His recurring formulation is that "the people who want to erode your freedoms do not care if you acquiesce for a left-wing cause or a right-wing cause, as long as the needle is moved."[1] He holds that the [[deep-state|interagency blob]] is party-agnostic, that [[hate-speech|hate speech]] is the universal pretext for [[government-sponsored-censorship|government-sponsored censorship]], and that the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]] and [[executive-order-14188|Executive Order 14188]] are the current American vehicles for it, following the template of the [[patriot-act|Patriot Act]].[1] Badlands Media notes that articles represent the opinions of contributing authors rather than the outlet itself.[1]
