# Maricopa County Tabulation and Election Center

The Maricopa County Tabulation and Election Center (MCTEC) is the central facility in which [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona processes, tabulates and verifies ballots, including the [[signature-verification|signature verification]] of mail-in ballot affidavit envelopes.[1]

## Testimony in the May 2023 signature verification trial

MCTEC's internal practices were the subject of extensive testimony in the remanded Count III trial of [[lake-v-hobbs|Lake v. Hobbs et al.]], held May 17–19, 2023.[1] The court first heard whistleblower testimony from two election workers employed at MCTEC, who described the rigor of the county's training and their adherence to the documented process, explaining meticulously how they compared each signature against exemplars in accordance with the law.[1]

Those workers further testified that Level 2 reviewers at the center were overwhelmed, and that "exceptioned signatures" — the term the county had adopted in place of "rejected signatures" — were repeatedly sent back down to Level 1.[1] This was not part of the process in which the workers had been trained, and it created confusion among signature verifiers at MCTEC.[1] The whistleblowers also testified that they had planned to work through an entire weekend, but when they arrived they found that hundreds of thousands of signatures had been approved overnight, while the staff assigned to signature verification had been sent home.[1]

Testimony from [[ray-valenzuela|Rey Valenzuela]], the county's Director of Elections for mail-in voting and election services, indicated that signatures were also approved at two other locations without observers present, including in the Clerk and Recorder's office.[1]
