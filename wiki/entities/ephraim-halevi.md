# Ephraim Halevi

Ephraim Halevi is a former director of [[mossad|Mossad]], Israel's foreign intelligence service.[1]

In April 2025 Halevi was among three former Mossad chiefs — alongside Danny Yotam and Tamir Pardo — who signed a letter, together with more than 250 Mossad veterans, calling for an immediate agreement to return all 59 remaining captives held in Gaza, "without delay, even at the cost of ceasing fighting."[1] The signatories declared that they "will not continue to stand by" and expressed full support for the earlier letter published by veteran Air Force pilots, as well as the parallel letter from [[unit-8200|Unit 8200]] intelligence personnel.[1] The Mossad veterans closed with the line, "The sanctity of life, Mr. Prime Minister, takes precedence over 'God of Revenge.'"[1]
