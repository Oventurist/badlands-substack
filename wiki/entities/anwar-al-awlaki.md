# Anwar al-Awlaki

Anwar al-Awlaki was an [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]]-affiliated cleric and operative who figures in the Badlands account as the link between American campus Islamist organizing and the September 11 attacks.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) In 2001 he served as spiritual guide to the [[muslim-students-association|Muslim Students Association]] chapter at [[george-washington-university|George Washington University]], while ministering to some of the eventual 9/11 suicide hijackers.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

Awlaki's own formation followed the same route: he led the MSA chapter at Colorado State University in the early 1990s.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The article cites him as evidence that the MSA has repeatedly served as an incubator of supremacist ideology, noting that he is far from the only jihadist to emerge from its confines.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) His later association with the George Washington chapter is raised in connection with [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], who sat on that chapter's executive board a few years before he advised it.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
