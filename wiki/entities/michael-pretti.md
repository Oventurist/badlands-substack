# Michael Pretti

Michael Pretti is the father of [[alex-pretti|Alex Jeffrey Pretti]], the 37-year-old former Minneapolis VA Medical Center ICU nurse who was fatally shot by [[customs-and-border-protection|U.S. Border Patrol]] agents on January 24, 2026, during anti-ICE protests in Minneapolis. He entered public reporting in the days after the shooting as the primary family source on his son's trajectory in the months preceding the confrontation.

## Statements to reporters

Speaking to reporters after the shooting, Michael Pretti described a son who had changed noticeably over the preceding period. He said the family had observed unusual behavior — his son pulling away from routine activities and speaking increasingly about "standing up" against what he perceived as federal overreach. Alex Pretti had left his nursing position in mid-2025; accounts differ as to whether he resigned or was terminated.

The father's most widely quoted remark was that his son "seemed to have joined some kind of group," and he added that the family had warned him to avoid direct confrontation with federal agents. Reporting on extracted cellphone data subsequently indicated involvement in coordinated anti-ICE protest networks, including encrypted Signal groups used to share the locations of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] agents and to plan crowd mobilization intended to obstruct enforcement operations — details that gave the father's impression of group affiliation a concrete referent.

## Colorado connection

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] emphasized a geographic detail: Alex Pretti's parents live in [[colorado|Colorado]], as did [[renee-good|Renee Good]], the other civilian killed in a Minneapolis-area federal enforcement confrontation. On the Monday following the shooting, Colorado legislators devoted floor time to mourning both Pretti and Good and to calls for resistance to federal authority.

In the Badlands reading, the convergence of two activist deaths with ties to the same state — and the immediate political mobilization of that state's legislature — is presented as evidence of an organized [[color-revolution|color revolution]]-style operation rather than coincidence, with Colorado characterized as its domestic center of gravity. See also [[minnesota-color-revolution|Minnesota color revolution]]. Michael Pretti's own statements are treated in that coverage as among the few relatively reliable data points in a rapidly shifting narrative environment, though only the separately reported detail of a broken rib sustained weeks earlier during interference with ICE enforcement had been independently confirmed at the time of writing.
