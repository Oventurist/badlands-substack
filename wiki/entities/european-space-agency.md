# European Space Agency

The European Space Agency (ESA) is Europe's intergovernmental space agency, responsible for the [[proba-3]] technology demonstration mission.[1]

## Proba-3 announcement

In June 2025 the agency published an announcement titled "Proba-3's first artificial solar eclipse," describing how the mission's two spacecraft — the Coronagraph and the Occulter — had flown 150 metres apart in perfect formation for several hours in March 2025 without any ground control, holding their relative position to within a single millimetre.[1] The agency explained that the pair aligned with the Sun so the Occulter's 1.4-metre disc blocked the solar disc for the Coronagraph, casting an 8-centimetre shadow on the ASPIICS optical instrument and yielding images of the solar corona.[1] The announcement was recirculated in the Badlands News Brief as a bonus item.[1]
