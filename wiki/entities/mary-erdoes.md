# Mary Erdoes

Mary Erdoes is chief executive of [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan]]'s asset and wealth management division and a central figure in unsealed correspondence concerning the bank's accommodation of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## The 2006 "google" email

In an email unsealed in August 2023 as part of the [[us-virgin-islands|U.S. Virgin Islands]] litigation against JPMorgan, Erdoes — then a rising star banker — instructed colleagues to build the [[google|Google]] founders' account team in New York rather than San Francisco.[1] Under the subject line "google," she wrote: "This is NOTHING to do with the abilities of the SF team. This is solely because of our need to have a NY team cover a NY person, Jeffrey Epstein (as the advisor to the partners)."[1]

The filing used the email to support the Virgin Islands' allegation that Epstein functioned as an investment advisor steering lucrative clients — including [[sergey-brin|Sergey Brin]], whose personal portfolio exceeded $4 billion — to the bank, and that senior JPMorgan executives structured coverage around him.[1]
