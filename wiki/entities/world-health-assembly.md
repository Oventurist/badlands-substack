The World Health Assembly (WHA) is the decision-making body of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] (WHO), composed of delegations from all WHO member states.[1] A May 2023 Badlands Media article noted that the 76th World Health Assembly was scheduled to convene in Geneva, Switzerland, from May 21 through May 30, 2023, to discuss the proposed [[who-pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations (IHR)]].[1]

The article cast the Geneva assembly as the venue at which globalist actors would advance instruments it argued would strip sovereignty from nation-states and concentrate public-health decision-making authority in the WHO and its Director-General, [[tedros-ghebreyesus|Tedros Ghebreyesus]].[1] The WHA's deliberations over the treaty and IHR amendments formed the focal event around which the article built its warning about the creation of "a new global authority" above national governments.[1]
