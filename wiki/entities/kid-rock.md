# Kid Rock

Kid Rock is an American musician and cultural figure closely associated with the populist right and with public appearances alongside [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## UFC 295 appearance

On November 11, 2023, Kid Rock was part of the entourage that accompanied Trump into Madison Square Garden for [[ufc|UFC]] 295, alongside [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], UFC president [[dana-white|Dana White]], and Donald Trump Jr.[1] Trump entered to a loud ovation, pumping his fist and waving to the sold-out crowd as Kid Rock's song "American Badass" played throughout the arena before the group took ringside seats.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that such appearances are not mere anecdotes but culturally significant events, noting that the UFC represents "THE counter-culture sport of the new century" and that Trump timed his 2023 UFC appearances to coincide with major narrative deployments, summarizing the point as "Politics IS Culture. Culture IS Politics."[1]
