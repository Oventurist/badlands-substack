# Windsor Castle

Windsor Castle is a Berkshire-based royal residence in the United Kingdom that made news in early March 2025 by hosting its first-ever Ramadan event inside St George's Hall, the castle's ceremonial room.[1]

## 2025 Iftar in St George's Hall

More than 360 guests from all backgrounds attended the Iftar, the fast-breaking evening meal observed by Muslims during Ramadan.[1] The residence had welcomed attendees for Iftar for the first time in British history the previous year, but the 2025 use of St George's Hall represented a significant upgrade from 2024, when the event was held in the Learning Centre.[1] The Royal Collection Trust website advertised the free-to-attend gathering with the invitation, "Break fast with us this Ramadan at Windsor Castle! Open to people of all faiths and none, join us for a visit to the majestic State Apartments, followed by a meal after sunset in the magnificent St George's Hall."[1] Guests began with a self-guided tour of the State Apartments.[1]

Badlands contributor Chris Paul read the event as evidence of institutional hollowing-out, arguing that there are "no more royals" and no more British Commonwealth, and that all that remains are "the sets, the characters," and onlookers fawning over wealth, status, and false decorum.[1]
