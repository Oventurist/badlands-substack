# Tom Fitton

Tom Fitton is the president of [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]], a conservative government-accountability group that pursues records through the Freedom of Information Act.[1]

## Biden–Hur audio dispute

In late April 2024, Fitton criticized the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] for refusing to release audio recordings of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s interview with former special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]], who investigated Biden's handling of classified documents.[1] "This is yet another brazen cover-up," Fitton said in a statement. "The Biden Justice Department's political gambit in asserting Joe Biden's privacy concerns in order to withhold audio of his criminal interviews with the special counsel really takes the cake."[1]

Judicial Watch, along with the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]] and [[cnn|CNN]], filed legal complaints seeking to enforce FOIA requests for the recordings, while House Judiciary Chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] and House Oversight Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] pressed the department from Congress, producing a tense impasse.[1]

## Criticism of the 2023 White House Pride display

In June 2023 Fitton criticized the [[white-house|White House]] Pride Month display, in which a rainbow flag was centered between two American flags, as a breach of the [[us-flag-code|United States Flag Code]].[2] He wrote that "to advance revolutionary transgender agenda targeting children, Biden violates basic tenet of US Flag Code and disrespects every American service member buried under its colors."[2]

## Mass Deportation Advocacy (2024)

Earlier in November 2024 Fitton, president of [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]], posted on Truth Social that President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was "prepared to declare a national emergency and will use military assets to reverse the Biden invasion through a mass deportation program."[3] Trump reposted the comment on November 18 with the caption "TRUE!!", making it the first confirmation of how the incoming administration intended to execute the plan.[3]

## Secret Service FOIA denials (2024)

After the [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] denied [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch's]] three July 16, 2024 FOIA requests for emails, videos and advance security assessments relating to the assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Fitton said: "The Biden Secret Service is in cover-up mode on its inexcusable and epic failure to protect former President Trump and other innocents."[4] He added that "for Secret Service leaders to promise transparency to Congress while hiding every possible FOIA record from the American people is yet another indictment of this corrupt and failing agency."[4] Under his leadership the organization said it was preparing for litigation and had over 25 FOIA and open-records requests pending on the shooting.[4]
