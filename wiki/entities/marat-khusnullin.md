# Marat Khusnullin

Marat Khusnullin is a Russian Deputy Prime Minister with responsibility for construction and regional development, including the reconstruction of Russia's border regions damaged during the war in Ukraine.[1]

## North Korean labor in Russian border regions

In June 2025 Khusnullin told journalists that construction workers from North Korea might take part in restoration work in Russia's borderline regions if major organizational issues could be settled.[1] "North Korea also wants to join and help with this issue," he said, while cataloguing the unresolved questions: "The timeframe is unclear and what does it mean that they are ready to arrive?"[1] He asked where the workers would be accommodated, who would be responsible for design and estimate documentation, which types of machinery would be used, and who would maintain contact with them, concluding that "this is also a major organizational scheme that needs to be managed."[1] The remarks were reported by [[tass|TASS]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary treated the announcement as another chapter in what it called the "redemption arc" of [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]], noting that North Korean troops had earlier been credited with driving Ukrainian forces from Russian territory and were now expected to help rebuild the communities destroyed in the fighting.[1] The commentary, attributed to [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], contrasted this with the United States' own role on the geopolitical stage, describing the American military apparatus as cast in the part of "Heel" while it continues Biden-era wars and government-toppling operations.[1]
