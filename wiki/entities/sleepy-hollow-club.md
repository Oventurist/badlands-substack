# Sleepy Hollow Club

The Sleepy Hollow Club was an elite New York social club that, in the account of [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], served from around 1910 as the physical and social base of a private, British-directed intelligence agency operating on American soil outside the control of the United States government.[1]

## Membership and ownership

The club was owned by William Rockefeller and drew its membership from the upper crust of America's brahmin families, including the Vanderbilts, Morgans, Cabots, Lodges and Lowells.[1] Senator [[nelson-aldrich|Nelson Aldrich]], father-in-law of John D. Rockefeller, was another leading member, as was President Wilson's handler [[edward-mandell-house|Edward Mandell House]]; both men played key roles in establishing the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and the income tax apparatus in 1913.[1] The club's manager was the financier [[thomas-fortune-ryan|Thomas Fortune Ryan]], who joined J.P. Morgan in financing [[nikola-tesla|Nikola Tesla]]'s towers before the outbreak of war.[1]

## Intelligence functions

The club's permanent resident was the British agent [[claude-dansey|Claude Dansey]], later deputy director of MI5, who was tasked with establishing a parallel security agency beyond the reach of the American government and who ran that agency from the club between 1911 and 1914 according to the historian [[anton-chaitkin|Anton Chaitkin]].[1] One of [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]]'s leading contacts and paymasters in New York before and during World War I was John Quinn, an Anglo-American agent of this private intelligence apparatus, as documented by Richard B. Spence in Secret Agent 666.[1] Crowley himself interfaced closely with the eastern establishment through the club while running ostensibly anti-British propaganda for [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]].[1]

Chaitkin characterizes the club, founded in 1910, as an instrument of British intelligence and "Deep State traitors," situating it within the broader 1909 restructuring of British intelligence by King Edward VII and Viscount Haldane into the branches later called MI5 and [[mi6|MI6]], and Home Secretary [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]]'s 1910 expansion of surveillance powers, subversive registries, mail interception, a drastic new Official Secrets Act and a recommended eugenical sterilization law.[1]
