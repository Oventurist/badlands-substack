# Northern Storm Brigade

The Northern Storm Brigade is described in the Badlands Media feature "Transparency and Prosecution" as the Al-Qaeda offshoot in Syria with which the wanted Syrian terrorist Muahmmad Noor was affiliated.[1]

Noor, who specialized in kidnapping American journalists, was one of the men circled in the photographs of Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]]'s May 2013 meeting inside Syria; he appears holding a camera alongside McCain and [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]].[1]

According to a World Bank biography of Moustafa quoted in the article, while working with the [[syrian-emergency-task-force|Syrian Emergency Task Force]] Moustafa led delegations to the Syrian border for journalists and lawmakers, including McCain's visit with the Northern Storm Brigade inside Syria, and the 2014 visit by Representatives [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]] and Holding with various [[free-syrian-army|Free Syrian Army]] commanders in Antakya, Turkey.[1] The article treats the brigade's Al-Qaeda lineage as evidence that the "rebel leaders" McCain met were not the moderates he presented them as.[1]
