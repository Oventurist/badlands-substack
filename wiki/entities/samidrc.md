# SAMIDRC

**SAMIDRC** is a regional military force dispatched by the Southern African Development Community (SADC) to the eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1] South Africa supplies the largest contingent to the mission.[1]

The deployment became a serious quagmire for the South African government during the [[2025-goma-offensive|2025 Goma offensive]]: thirteen South African soldiers were killed in the battle for Goma on 23 January 2025, launched by [[m23|M23]] rebels and their Rwandan backers.[1] Pretoria simultaneously faced domestic criticism, with the South African public questioning the wisdom of sending troops to fight in a far-off country, and threats from Rwandan president [[paul-kagame|Paul Kagame]], who rejected blame for the conflict and regarded the South African intervention as hostile.[1] South African forces under the mission fought alongside Congolese security forces in an effort to drive the rebels out of the east.[1]
