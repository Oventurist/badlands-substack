# Robert Kehoe

Robert Kehoe is the Deputy Administrator of the [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]] who was interviewed by federal agents in connection with the [[fbi|FBI]]'s investigation into Wisconsin's 2020 election.

## The FBI probe

According to the May 2026 Badlands Brief, federal agents spoke with Kehoe as part of the investigation into Milwaukee's 2020 election administration. They also attempted to question a top Milwaukee County election official at her home and contacted at least two individuals involved in administering Milwaukee's 2020 election. The probe includes the flash-drive incident involving former [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] Executive Director [[claire-woodall|Claire Woodall]] and the questioning of [[milwaukee-police-department|Milwaukee Police Department]] officers stationed at the central count facility on Election Night.
