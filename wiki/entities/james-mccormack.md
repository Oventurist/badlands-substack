# James McCormack

James McCormack is the global head of sovereigns at [[fitch-ratings|Fitch Ratings]]. In August 2023 he became the public voice of the agency's warning that China's A+ sovereign credit rating could be reassessed if the country's non-government debt liabilities continued to grow.[1]

## August 2023 remarks on China

Speaking to Bloomberg TV on Wednesday, August 16, 2023, McCormack said that if China chose to expand its balance sheet to support its faltering economy, "we might think again, because the debt-to-GDP ratio is a little bit on the high side for a single 'A' credit."[1] He did not forecast an imminent downgrade, but cautioned that increases in corporate and banking sector debt could become "real liabilities for the government."[1]

He added that recent evidence did not suggest such a balance-sheet expansion was expected, but that the agency would revisit its assessment if it occurred.[1] The remarks came one day after the [[peoples-bank-of-china|People's Bank of China]] injected the largest amount of short-term cash since February and cut interest rates amid deteriorating confidence in Chinese financial markets.[1]
