# Eyal Yakoby

Eyal Yakoby is a recent graduate of the University of Pennsylvania who gained prominence as a vocal advocate against antisemitism on American college campuses, and who is described in the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard" as one of [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]]'s "biggest amplifiers."

## Speech before House testimony

The article reports that Yakoby delivered a speech during a House of Representatives press conference held before the presidents of the University of Pennsylvania, Harvard, and MIT testified before [[congress|Congress]] about campus antisemitism. The event was facilitated by House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]], whom Yakoby praised for showing "moral clarity" on the issue.

## Quoted remarks and the article's criticism

The article quotes Yakoby's remarks at length, including his statement that "Since October 7th, American Jews have been under attack," his description of Penn as "a chilling landscape of hatred and hostility" where "Jewish students tiptoe through their days; uncertain and unsafe," and his pointed contrast of the strict COVID-era enforcement with the lack of resolute enforcement against intimidation of Jewish students: "As a student, despite what my university says, I do not feel safe, let me be clear; I do not feel safe."

The author of the article, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], argues that Yakoby "literally calls for COVID-grade guidelines and enforcement" to protect Jewish students — being protected from "hearing a dissenting opinion about the conduct of the foreign nation of Israel." The author writes that he is "just as unmoved and unwilling to scrap free expression" by Yakoby's repeated assertions of not feeling safe as he was by young liberals harassing conservative speakers on campus.
