# Eyal Yakoby

Eyal Yakoby is a University of Pennsylvania graduate who gained prominence as a vocal advocate against antisemitism on college campuses.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] describes him as one of [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]]'s biggest amplifiers.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

## House press conference

Yakoby delivered a speech at a House press conference held before the presidents of the [[university-of-pennsylvania|University of Pennsylvania]], Harvard, and MIT testified before [[congress|Congress]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) The event was facilitated by House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]], whom Yakoby praised for showing "moral clarity" on antisemitism.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

In the speech Yakoby said that American Jews had been under attack since October 7, and that Penn — "once renowned for groundbreaking discoveries like the mRNA vaccine" — had become "a chilling landscape of hatred and hostility" in which Jewish students "tiptoe through their days; uncertain and unsafe."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) He drew an explicit comparison to the pandemic: "During COVID, strict guidelines governed everything from class attendance to graduation walks, yet now, when students and faculty defy policies to intimidate Jewish students, where is the same resolute enforcement?"1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) He concluded, "I do not feel safe, let me be clear; I do not feel safe."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

## Criticism

DeLarme argues that Yakoby's speech literally calls for COVID-grade guidelines and enforcement to protect Jewish students from hearing dissenting opinions about the conduct of Israel.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) He contends that the "attacks" Yakoby invokes are, on inspection, largely the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]]-tallied incidents of harassment and [[hate-speech|hate speech]] that often amount to differences of opinion.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) DeLarme says he is as unmoved by Yakoby's repeated assertions of feeling unsafe as he was by young liberals demanding [[safe-spaces|safe spaces]] on campus.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)
