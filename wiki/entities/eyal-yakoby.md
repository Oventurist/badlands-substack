# Eyal Yakoby

Eyal Yakoby is a University of Pennsylvania graduate who gained prominence as a vocal advocate against antisemitism on college campuses, and is described as one of [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]]'s biggest amplifiers.[1]

## House press conference

Yakoby delivered a speech at a House press conference held before the presidents of the University of Pennsylvania, Harvard and MIT testified before [[congress|Congress]].[1] The event was facilitated by House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]], whom Yakoby praised for showing "moral clarity" on antisemitism.[1] In the speech he said that since October 7th American Jews had been under attack, that Penn — once renowned for discoveries such as the mRNA vaccine — had become "a chilling landscape of hatred and hostility" where Jewish students "tiptoe through their days; uncertain and unsafe," and repeated, "I do not feel safe, let me be clear; I do not feel safe."[1]

## Criticism

The most cited passage is Yakoby's question about COVID-era rules: "During COVID, strict guidelines governed everything from class attendance to graduation walks, yet now, when students and faculty defy policies to intimidate Jewish students, where is the same resolute enforcement?"[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] reads this as a literal call for COVID-grade guidelines and enforcement to shield Jewish students from hearing dissenting opinions about the conduct of Israel, and as a direct mirror image of the [[safe-spaces|safe space]] demands conservatives had mocked.[1] DeLarme notes that the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]]'s tally of campus "attacks" consisted overwhelmingly of harassment and [[hate-speech|hate speech]] classifications rather than physical assault.[1]
