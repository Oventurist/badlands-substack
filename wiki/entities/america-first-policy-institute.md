# America First Policy Institute

The America First Policy Institute (rendered in the Badlands Brief's quoted source as the "American First Policy Institute") is a policy organization aligned with the [[america-first-movement|America First movement]] and staffed heavily by veterans of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first administration.[1]

The institute appeared in the Brief's July 2026 coverage of the death of Sen. [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] through [[fred-fleitz|Fred Fleitz]], the former National Security Council chief of staff who serves as the institute's vice chair of American security and who described Graham's role as a constant informal adviser to Trump on national security policy.[1] The organization's prominence in that coverage illustrated how America First-aligned institutions were positioned to fill the advisory space left by the traditional Republican foreign policy establishment.[1]
