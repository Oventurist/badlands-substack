# Decoding Symbolism

**Decoding Symbolism** is a WordPress-hosted website whose stated purpose is to decode [[cabal|cabal]] and Q symbolism in news and popular culture with thorough reference to Q drops.[1] It is recommended — though explicitly not endorsed — in the Badlands Media essay "Expand Your Thinking" as a training ground for developing symbolic, associative thinking.[1]

## Description

The essay's author, writing as [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], describes the site as among the most in-depth he has found working on this task, and says its anonymous author has clearly spent years searching for associations and correspondences between symbols used in news and pop culture, producing work he calls among the most original he has seen.[1]

## Qualified recommendation

The author states that he does not agree with everything posted on the site, and that some of its claims stand in diametric opposition to things he believes to be true about the present [[5g-war|5G war]].[1] He argues that this is beside the point: what matters is the methodology, and the essay explicitly declines to officially endorse the site.[1] The stated goal is instead to learn how to expand one's thinking, for which the site is presented as an extremely useful resource for training the mind to see symbolic correspondences in unlikely places while remaining within the framework of the Q drops.[1] The author adds that even the most erudite researchers have blind spots that may seem irreconcilable with one's own conclusions.[1]

## Rationale

The recommendation rests on the [[qanon|Q]] assertion that the cabal's "need for symbolism will be their downfall."[1] If the cabal communicates in the open through symbols without giving itself away to the masses, the essay reasons, then decoding those symbols is sufficient to end the deception — easier said than done, but a way forward.[1] Right-brained, associative and linguistic thinking is therefore framed as a vital key for defeating the cabal, and studying the site is offered as a way for [[anons|anons]] to exercise that faculty and further the mission at the same time.[1]
