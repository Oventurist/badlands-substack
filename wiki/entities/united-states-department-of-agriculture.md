# United States Department of Agriculture

The United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) is the federal executive department responsible for agricultural policy, food safety, rural development, and oversight of livestock and meatpacking markets.

## Role in the 2026 beef packer investigation

As reported in the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, the department joined the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] in announcing an intensified antitrust investigation into the four largest beef packers — [[jbs|JBS]], [[cargill|Cargill]], [[tyson-foods|Tyson Foods]], and [[national-beef|National Beef]] — citing price manipulation, foreign ownership risks, and supply chain problems.

Secretary of Agriculture [[brooke-rollins|Brooke Rollins]] characterized foreign-owned packers such as JBS and National Beef as a national security threat amid reports of slave labor ties, saying: "Half of these meatpacking giants, including the largest meat packer in the world, are either foreign-owned or have significant foreign ownership and control, making them a threat not just to our cattle producers, but a threat to America itself." The Brief notes that the probe follows a November 2025 directive from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and coincides with a forthcoming settlement on poultry and meat prices, at a time when United States beef herds have fallen to their lowest levels since the 1950s because of drought and a declining number of ranches.
