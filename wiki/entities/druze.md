# Druze

The Druze are a religious and ethnic community of the Levant, concentrated in southern [[syria|Syria]], Lebanon, and Israel.[1]

## Role in the Syria balkanization thesis

Badlands commentary on the Israeli–Turkish deconfliction talks of April 2025 identified the Druze, alongside the [[alawites|Alawites]] and the [[kurds|Kurds]], as one of the competing ethnic groups whose historic conflicts would be exploited under the [[davids-corridor|David's Corridor]] strategy to balkanize Syria and prevent those communities from unifying into a coalition comparable to [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in southern Lebanon.[1]
