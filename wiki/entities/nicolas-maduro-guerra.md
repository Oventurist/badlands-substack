# Nicolás Maduro Guerra

Nicolás Maduro Guerra is the son of Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] and a public figure in the political aftermath of his father's detention by the United States.[1]

Speaking at a rally in early January 2026, Maduro Guerra affirmed that a [[continuity-of-government|continuity of government]] plan was in effect and that Venezuela's leadership was following the directions left behind by his father.[1] He was also the person holding the Bible when Vice President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] was sworn in as acting president; Rodríguez immediately clarified that Nicolás Maduro remained the one true president of Venezuela, a position Maduro himself asserted during his court appearance in New York.[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites these details as supporting evidence for his theory that Venezuela executed a negotiated continuity-of-government arrangement analogous to [[devolution|Devolution]], rather than suffering an ordinary regime collapse.[1]
