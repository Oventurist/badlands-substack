# Michael Kovats

Michael Kovats was a Hungarian colonel who served alongside Count [[casimir-pulaski|Casimir Pulaski]] among the European "horse lords" who brought professional cavalry experience to the Continental Army during the American Revolution.[1]

Badlands identifies Kovats as one of the officers whose expertise was necessary to counter the British dragoons led by [[banastre-tarleton|Banastre Tarleton]], at a time when [[george-washington|George Washington]] had little or no experience employing cavalry as a set piece in battle.[1] That deficiency stemmed from Washington's military background in the French and Indian War, fought in the mountainous terrain of the northern colonies and Canada rather than on open ground suited to mounted maneuver.[1]

Kovats' contribution is presented as part of a continuity linking the [[winged-hussars|Winged Hussars]] of Poland — heroes of the 1683 [[battle-of-vienna|Battle of Vienna]] — to the eventual U.S. Cavalry.[1]
