# David McIntosh

David McIntosh is a conservative political operative who headed the [[win-it-back-pac|Win It Back PAC]], the anti-Trump advertising vehicle closely tied to [[club-for-growth|Club for Growth]].[1]

## The 2023 donor memo

In a memo dated September 28, 2023 and obtained by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]], McIntosh acknowledged to the group's donors that its multimillion-dollar advertising campaign against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had failed.[1] After testing more than forty anti-Trump television spots, he wrote, "all attempts to undermine his conservative credentials on specific issues were ineffective," despite more than $4 million spent in Iowa and nearly $2 million in South Carolina.[1] The candour of the admission was widely noted, and it signalled to the rest of the Republican field that no tested message had been found capable of eroding Trump's lead.[1]

Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited McIntosh's memo as documentary evidence that establishment donors were burning resources against their own party's most popular candidate rather than contesting the general election.[1]
