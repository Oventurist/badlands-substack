# IBM Cognos Analytics

**Cognos Analytics** is a business-intelligence and analytics product sold by [[ibm|IBM]].[1] IBM markets it as delivering AI-powered automation and insights so that everyone in an organisation can unlock the potential of their data, with a natural-language AI assistant that builds data visualisations on request and answers questions or tests hypotheses posed in plain language.[1] The company describes it as a new generation of business intelligence that not only presents an accurate and complete picture of a business but forecasts future conditions, predicts outcomes and explains why they may occur.[1]

## Defense contracting context

An October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] investigation argued that the Department of Defense parent contract named [[cognition-cognos-support|Cognition Cognos Support]] — the program containing the [[red-dawn-iii|Red Dawn III]] delivery order to [[icr-inc|ICR, INC.]] — most likely refers to this IBM product.[1] On that reading, the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] is applying IBM artificial-intelligence tooling to a research program on defense-system electronics and communication equipment, with subprojects such as Red Dawn III supplying custom programming for shipboard defense systems.[1]
