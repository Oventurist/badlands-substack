# Kitty Gillespie

Kitty Gillespie is an Arizona-based writer and contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] who also publishes independently at Kitty's Newsletter on Substack. She is the author of the November 22, 2022 article [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]], a first-person account of the disputed administration of the 2022 general election in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]].

## Background

Gillespie writes that she has lived in Arizona since 1984, having "fled Chicagoland" that year, and that she resides in the Phoenix metropolitan area. Her writing blends personal narrative — including her affection for the state's landscape and climate — with close reading of Arizona statutes, official vote-count data, and correspondence from state officials. She notes in "An SOS From Ground Zero" that she previously worked with [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]] "back in the day when he was a radio talk show host," suggesting a background in or adjacent to broadcast media.

## Election coverage

Gillespie produced a sequence of Arizona election articles in late 2022. On November 5, 2022 she published "Why Kari Lake Will Lose in Arizona," in which she warned that manipulation of the governor's race would require flipping the entire Republican down-ballot ticket to remain plausible. On November 14 she published "The Plot Thickens in Maricopa County," concerning [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]]; she later noted that a [[q|Q]] post dated November 18 also referenced Runbeck. "An SOS From Ground Zero" followed on November 22, summarizing the letter from Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] to the [[maricopa-county-attorneys-office|Maricopa County Attorney's Office]] and arguing that statewide vote totals showing the State Treasurer race outdrawing the U.S. Senate, gubernatorial, secretary of state, and attorney general contests were evidence of tampering.

Her stance is openly partisan and sympathetic to [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]], and sharply critical of Attorney General [[mark-brnovich|Mark Brnovich]], Board of Supervisors chairman [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]], and Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]].

## "Arizona Earthquake!" (December 2022)

Gillespie wrote [[arizona-earthquake|"Arizona Earthquake!"]], published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on December 12, 2022, a survey of the post-certification election lawsuits filed by Arizona Republican candidates after the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]]. The piece reproduces [[liz-harrington|Liz Harrington]]'s summary of [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s 70-page contest, covers the recount in the [[abe-hamadeh|Abe Hamadeh]]–[[kris-mayes|Kris Mayes]] attorney general race and the [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]]–[[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]] suit, and remarks on the apparent silence of [[blake-masters|Blake Masters]]. It closes with her prediction that the case will be rejected by Arizona's courts and eventually decided by the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]].
