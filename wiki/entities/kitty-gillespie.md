# Kitty Gillespie

Kitty Gillespie is an Arizona-based contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and the author of Kitty's Newsletter on Substack, who covered the disputed [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]] from inside [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Background

Gillespie has lived in Arizona since leaving the Chicago area in 1984, and opens her November 22, 2022 article "An SOS From Ground Zero" with an extended appreciation of the state — the Grand Canyon, Tombstone, Antelope Canyon, Sedona, Bisbee, Jerome, the Petrified Forest, Route 66 — before pivoting to what she calls an appallingly broken elections system.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She frames the piece as an SOS: "Save Our State."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) Her rendering of Arizona life includes the "haboob," the local term for a dust storm, which she describes as visually dramatic but practically harmless.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Reporting on the 2022 election

Gillespie had published "Why Kari Lake Will Lose in Arizona" on November 5, 2022, warning that the left could not credibly flip only the governor's race and would need to flip the entire Republican down-ballot ticket.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She had also written "The Plot Thickens in Maricopa County" on November 14, 2022, concerning [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]], and noted with interest that a Q drop dated November 18 mentioned Runbeck.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

In "An SOS From Ground Zero" she analyzed statewide vote totals showing that the low-profile State Treasurer contest, won by Republican [[kimberly-yee|Kimberly Yee]] with 55.7 percent, drew more votes than the U.S. Senate, Governor, Secretary of State and Attorney General races, treating the treasurer figures as an unmanipulated baseline.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She mocked the public conduct of Board of Supervisors chairman [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]] and Attorney General [[mark-brnovich|Mark Brnovich]] as "two comedy shows playing on Twitter," and lamented the absence of "any real journalists in Arizona" willing to ask why Brnovich rather than Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] was named acting governor.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

She reported as a voter as well as a journalist: she and her husband were directed to deposit their ballots in a receptacle labeled "BOX 3" because their polling place had no working tabulator, an experience she connected to the wider [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3"]] controversy.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She praised Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] as "my hero right now" for the November 19 demand letter to [[tom-liddy|Tom Liddy]], and disclosed that she had worked with Liddy years earlier when he was a radio talk show host, adding only "No comment."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She urged readers to follow independent outlets such as Badlands Media through the canvass week.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## Role at Badlands Media

By March 2023 Gillespie had also taken on a commercial role within the network: the launch announcement for [[badlandsmedia-tv|BadlandsMedia.tv]] directed readers wishing to advertise with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] to "Connect with Kitty about Advertising" through the new site.[2]

## Personal awakening narrative

In "New to the Great Awakening? Making Sense of It All," published November 18, 2022, Gillespie addressed newcomers to the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] and recounted her own path in detail, writing that some readers had been awake for years or decades, others since [[qanon|Q]] first appeared in 2017, and still more with each election cycle in which anticipated outcomes did not materialise.[3] She offered a list of reassurances: that the reader is not crazy but the events are, that "Evil is real" and has "infiltrated every institution we trusted," that God has already won the fight, that questioning everything one was taught is normal, that returning critical thinking should be sharpened, that one should be kind to oneself about "the years you spent asleep" and patient with others slow to wake, and that "WE ARE THE MAJORITY."[3]

She dated her own awakening to December 2019, when she was listening to [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s WarRoom: Impeachment and heard discussion of a virus that had shut down [[wuhan|Wuhan]] in Hubei province, China — place names she had not previously encountered.[3] Acting on unease, she shopped for supplies at Costco on January 24, 2020, spending more than $600 on food and staples, prompting a cashier to ask whether she was "having a party" and her husband to ask whether she was "building a bunker next."[3] The ensuing [[covid-19-lockdowns|lockdown]] gave her time to research questions about [[gain-of-function-research|gain of function]] work, viral transmission and mask efficacy, and whether the government could be believed.[3]

Gillespie disclosed that she had spent her career in the mainstream media, and said the ferocity of its hostility to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — a figure it had embraced as a billionaire on *The Apprentice* — and its attacks on [[hydroxychloroquine|hydroxychloroquine]] and [[ivermectin|ivermectin]], both on the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] list of essential medicines, triggered her suspicion that inexpensive effective drugs were being demonised because [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]] could not profit from them.[3] She researched [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] and [[deborah-birx|Deborah Birx]], learning that both had been major figures in the AIDS epidemic, and watched creators she followed be deplatformed, asking what had become of the Bill of Rights.[3] She went on to encounter material on [[transhumanism|transhumanism]], child sex trafficking, [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]], gender confusion, [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] and Satanism, and described feeling overwhelmed by disparate pieces of information.[3]

She wrote that she prayed for discernment about the voices she followed on Telegram and [[rumble|Rumble]], concluding that those who worked to divide the patriot community or sold "Trump coins" were not genuine.[3] Her husband was fired by a company he loved after refusing [[covid-19-vaccines|vaccination]], which led her to research [[nuremberg-code|Nuremberg]] and the question of informed consent.[3]

## The October 2021 chart

Gillespie recounted that in October 2020 she recalled Trump's May 2020 Oval Office remarks, delivered while signing a National Nurses Day proclamation, that the country had suffered "the worst attack we've ever had… worse than Pearl Harbor… worse than the World Trade Center," which "should've been stopped in China," and only then understood that "we were in the midst of World War III."[3] This raised the question of who the [[invisible-enemy|"invisible enemy"]] Trump had invoked thousands of times actually was — the virus or the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]].[3]

Describing herself as an inveterate list-maker, she began categorising the information she had gathered and produced a visual chart on October 6, 2021, which she credited with clawing her way "back to sanity."[3] She identified the enemy not as a single actor but as an interconnected set she labelled "The Cabal/Globalists/Illuminati/[[freemasonry|Freemasons]]/[[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]," and their agenda as eugenics and [[depopulation-agenda|depopulation]] (citing the [[georgia-guidestones|Georgia Guidestones]]), transhumanism, centralised economic and political control, and a slavery system.[3] She sorted the mechanisms into six "big buckets": central banks, [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]]/Big Med, [[big-tech|Big Tech]], mind control, military/defense, and corruption, and, describing herself as solutions-oriented, included candidate solutions on the chart.[3]

For newcomers she recommended three videos: "The Great Awakening by Bonfire Guy," "'[[great-reset|The Great Reset]]' Is Happening NOW" with [[catherine-austin-fitts|Catherine Austin Fitts]], and the first part of Janet Ossebaard's ten-part *Fall of the Cabal* series.[3] She closed with religious encouragement, quoting 2 Timothy 1:7 on a spirit "of POWER and of LOVE and of a SOUND MIND" — asking who, if not God, plants fear — and 2 Chronicles 7:14, and warned readers that events would get "very hard" and "very scary."[3] The piece carried the standard Badlands disclaimer that contributors' views are their own, and pointed readers to Kitty's Newsletter.[3]

## "Operation Wake Up America 2.0" (December 2022)

On December 2, 2022, Gillespie published the Badlands Media essay "Operation Wake Up America 2.0," subtitled "All the World's a Stage," which set out her framework of [[operation-wake-up-america|Operation Wake Up America]].[4] Opening with the line "I'm sensing another covert operation underway," she built her argument on the premise that [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]] had never made a promise he failed to keep or a prediction that failed to come true, a record she treated as impossible for an ordinary person; she listed "Project Looking Glass," particle acceleration and time travel among the theories offered to explain it but declined to adjudicate, leaving the question "to bigger brains than mine."[4]

Gillespie described her own evolving view of Trump — from a swaggering New York real-estate developer and "rich playboy" known through *The Art of the Deal* to the figure she came to know via [[the-apprentice|The Apprentice]], whose treatment of [[donald-trump-jr|Don Jr.]], [[ivanka-trump|Ivanka]] and [[eric-trump|Eric]] made viewers see him as a great father.[4] She argued his persona, like [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s, had been "carefully crafted over the years" and that both had been chosen for "the role of a lifetime" by parties who would likely never be known.[4]

The essay laid out a 2020 national playbook — [[devolution|devolution]] for continuity of government, dismantling the [[deep-state|Deep State]] through lawfare and clandestine operations, awakening "normies," and capturing the mainstream media narrative — followed by an Arizona sequel in which Lake would run a shadow governorship after [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]] was installed.[4] Gillespie predicted that [[abe-hamadeh|Abe Hamadeh]] would win his attorney-general runoff and serve as "a highly-placed and powerful operative deep inside the machine."[4] She closed by asserting that "American history is being written right now" and with the exhortation "GIDDY UP!", and directed readers to her own outlet, Kitty's Newsletter.[4]

## "We Are 'The Plan'" (December 2022)

On December 9, 2022, Gillespie published "We Are 'The Plan'," subtitled "The Flywheel of Freedom Needs YOU!", a motivational essay responding to what she described as an undercurrent of weary, frustrated and impatient comments she had observed on Telegram, Truth Social, Substack and other "patriot platforms."[7] She quoted a reader who wrote that the corruption of the voting system must be remedied before 2024 and that the delays gave people "no reason to vote ever again," and answered that she must believe a plan exists for her own sanity, that the corruption is too deep to be undone at once, and that — echoing [[qanon|Q]]'s line that the truth "would put 99% of the people in the hospital" — the audience itself bears responsibility for waking the millions who remain asleep.[7] She characterised contemporary America as a "fast-food society" addicted to instant gratification.[7]

The essay used the *Titanic* scene in which many workers together move the ship as an illustration of collective effort, and cited [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]]'s description of events on the December 3, 2022 episode of [[devolution-power-hour|Devolution Power Hour]].[7] Its central device was the [[flywheel-of-freedom|Flywheel of Freedom]], drawn from Jim Collins's *[[good-to-great|Good to Great]]*, in which accumulated small pushes in a consistent direction eventually produce breakthrough momentum.[7] She invoked the [[tea-party-movement|Tea Party movement]] of 2009 as historical proof that a small, dedicated group can reshape the legislative branch.[7]

Gillespie relayed a Telegram post by Politick_Rick praising [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s [[war-room|Warroom]] for telling listeners to take back politics at the local level rather than to "trust the plan," and faulting the wider pundit class — including [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] — for cataloguing problems without proposing solutions.[7] She described Bannon as a "Field General" and "combatant commander" of the Revolution whose battle cry had drawn tens or hundreds of thousands of patriots into election work before the [[2022-midterm-elections|midterms]].[7]

Her list of practical actions included meeting and educating neighbours, joining a church community or community garden, volunteering (meals for the homebound, car repairs for single mothers, recording books for the blind, knitting blankets for seniors, sandwiches for the homeless), sharing [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] content, training for the 2024 election through the [[precinct-strategy|Precinct Strategy]] developed by [[dan-schultz|Dan Schultz]] and endorsed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in February 2022, listing businesses on [[publicsq|PublicSq]], and praying without ceasing.[7] She closed by quoting [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s "An Introduction to Devolution" on [[devolution|devolution]]'s two parts — the military and Trump having captured election-theft evidence, and We the People supplying the second — together with Trump's 2016 closing campaign line that "the only force strong enough to save our country is us," before signing off with her customary "GIDDY UP!"[7]

And GIDDY UP!", and directs readers to her own Substack, Kitty's Newsletter.[5]

## "Watch the Water" (November 2022)

On November 11, 2022, Gillespie published "Watch the Water," subtitled "Kari Lake's Unspoken Top Priority," written from Arizona three days after the midterm election.[6] She reported that only about 62,000 of [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]]'s ballots had been released in two full days of counting in a county of some 4.5 million residents, quoted [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] on unopened Republican envelopes, and wrote that "my precious state is currently the laughingstock, not only of the country, but of the WORLD."[6]

She explained Arizona's situation as a consequence of its long history as a [[mail-in-voting|mail-in ballot]] state — a convenience she glossed as "aka laziness" — followed by a post-2020 push by the [[arizona-republican-party|AZ GOP]] to get conservatives to vote in person, and recounted her own polling-place experience, including a worker who called her at-home reference ballot a "cheater ballot" and the absence of a working tabulator that forced her to use "Box 3."[6]

Gillespie summarised [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s stated Day One priorities — a declaration of invasion at the border, citing Lake's *Face the Nation* remarks on Article 4, Section 4 and Article 1, Section 10, and a special session on election reform, citing Lake's interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] — before advancing her own thesis that Lake's unspoken priority was to be "the key that will unlock the fraud of the 2020 election."[6] Citing a [[the-gateway-pundit|Gateway Pundit]] recap of the [[maricopa-county-forensic-audit|Maricopa County audit]] and canvass, she contrasted [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s 10,547-vote Arizona margin with 698,239 ballots identified as having issues.[6] She closed by invoking the Q phrase "[[watch-the-water|watch the water]]" as a pun on Lake's surname, adding "It's time to return the diamonds," "I love dominoes!" and her customary sign-off, "Strap yourself in, folks! And GIDDY UP!"[6]

## "Why Trump Pushed the Vax" (December 2022)

On December 29, 2022, Gillespie published "Why Trump Pushed the Vax," subtitled "The One Question We All Want Answered," her attempt to explain [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s continued public endorsement of COVID-19 vaccination.[8] She opened with the community backlash to Trump's endorsement of [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] for Speaker of the [[118th-congress|118th Congress]], which Trump explained in a Christmas Eve 2022 interview with [[wayne-allyn-root|Wayne Allyn Root]], and framed Trump as a "WARTIME President" obliged to make unimaginably hard decisions in "the most complex, deadly war our world has ever seen."[8]

Gillespie surveyed the standard truther explanation — that [[operation-warp-speed|Operation Warp Speed]] derailed a plan to kill half a billion people through vaccines and lockdowns — and then advanced her own extension of it, built around [[woody-johnson|Woody Johnson]], the [[johnson-and-johnson|Johnson & Johnson]] minority owner and Trump friend who served as ambassador to the United Kingdom.[8] She detailed the $905,645,089 in contracts awarded to [[janssen-pharmaceuticals|Janssen Pharmaceuticals]] beginning in December 2019, the [[2021-johnson-and-johnson-vaccine-pause|April 2021 pause]] of the J&J vaccine, and the [[walter-reed-army-institute-of-research|Walter Reed]] [[spike-ferritin-nanoparticle-vaccine|SpFN "supervax"]] reported by [[defense-one|Defense One]], proposing that Trump and Johnson may have distributed millions of vials containing saline or the Army formulation.[8]

She cited Trump's on-stage vaccine advocacy with [[bill-oreilly|Bill O'Reilly]] in December 2021, his introduction of Woody and Suzanne Johnson at the May 6, 2022 Greensburg Save America rally, and commentary on that rally by [[kim-iversen|Kim Iversen]] and [[robby-soave|Robby Soave]] of [[the-hill|The Hill]].[8] She attempted a quantitative check of Trump's "100 million lives" claim using Our World in Data, finding 66,885,169 J&J doses across 44 countries and the EU and speculating that the [[brics|BRICS]] states, including [[south-africa|South Africa]]'s 9,370,000 doses, closed the gap.[8] As supporting precedent she cited a Daily Mail report of December 1, 2022 on Antje T., a 39-year-old German Red Cross nurse given six months' probation for injecting some 8,600 elderly patients at the Schortens centre in Friesland with saline instead of the BioNTech-Pfizer vaccine.[8]

The essay closed with a quotation from a subscriber, Phil W., on the burden of deciding to lose innocent lives in order to save many more, an exhortation to keep praying for Trump and those executing "The Plan," and her customary "GIDDY UP!"[8] She recommended the MrTruthBomb film *President Trump Vs BIG PHARMA 2*, the documentary *Died Suddenly*, and her own article "Soccer Balls and High Fashion" at Kitty's Newsletter.[8] The piece carried the standard Badlands disclaimer that contributors' views are their own.[8]
