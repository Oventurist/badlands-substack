# Kennedy Family

The Kennedy family is an American political dynasty long associated with the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]]. Its political line was launched more than a century ago by John "Honey Fitz" Fitzgerald, Boston's first Irish Catholic mayor, and Patrick Kennedy, a Boston ward boss — described by [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] as his two great-grandfathers who together founded the family's role in public life.[1]

## Split over the 2024 independent campaign

On October 9, 2023, Robert F. Kennedy Jr. announced from in front of the National Constitution Center in Philadelphia that he would leave the Democratic primary and run for president as an independent in 2024.[1] In his remarks he said it was "painful for me to let go of the party of my uncles, my father, of my grandfather, and of both of my great-grandfathers," and compared his decision to the risk taken by the founders who signed the Declaration of Independence 247 years earlier, gesturing toward Independence Hall.[1]

Several of his siblings responded within hours. In a statement posted on Twitter/X, Kerry Kennedy, Rory Kennedy, Joseph P. Kennedy II, and Kathleen Kennedy Townsend said that while their brother carries the Kennedy name, he does not share the Kennedy "values, vision, or judgement," called his announcement "deeply saddening," and stated that they "denounce his candidacy."[1] The siblings simultaneously endorsed President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] characterized the denunciation as a play to "Kennedy Democrat" sentiment intended to paint RFK Jr. as unstable, and doubted it would work, arguing the Kennedy name is not strong enough to reimagine the state of American affairs.[1] The commentary added that for supporters of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] the family's rebuke amounted to the best possible endorsement — "A Kennedy that's not a Kennedy?" — and concluded that on net RFK Jr.'s move to independent status helps Trump.[1]

## In the Werewolf Game series

[[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series argues that the Kennedys are not part of the "dark elite" but rather royal heirs of a benevolent ancient civilization opposed to the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[2] The series holds that the family has been under attack since before [[john-f-kennedy|JFK]] attempted to wrest the power of money creation from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and return it to the people, an act described as an unambiguous rebuke to the cabal.[2] It catalogues the long run of untimely family deaths under the heading of the [[kennedy-curse|"Kennedy Curse"]] and treats them as evidence of that hostility.[2]

Addressing the family's video endorsing [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], the series cautions against reading it as proof that the Kennedys have "turned," suggesting instead that they may be reacting to life-threatening background pressures or acting as a "positive elite" serving as a goalie for "team blue."[2] The series further develops a claimed [[trump-kennedy-connection|Trump-Kennedy connection]] spanning friendship, co-investment, shared Scottish Gaelic geography, and possible Druidic royal ancestry.[2]
