# TikTok

TikTok is a short-form video platform owned by ByteDance. In the Badlands Media corpus, it is identified as a primary vector for [[Chinese Communist Party]] information operations targeting Western youth.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Algorithmic Manipulation

The June 2023 article "[[A Communist Plot to Take Over America]]" claims TikTok's algorithm is deliberately engineered to push "stupid and degrading" content to the top while burying intelligent material. Author [[Erik Carlson]] argues this is not organic engagement optimization but a [[Chinese Communist Party]]-directed strategy to reward degradation and maximize the platform's conditioning effect on young users, who seek "likes" and validation without realizing the equation is manipulated.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

The article links TikTok's algorithmic practices to broader [[artificial intelligence]] manipulation, arguing that social media interactions previously believed to be human-generated may in fact have been AI-driven, conditioning users' morals and values without their knowledge.^[raw/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america.md]

## Proposed American ownership

The Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]] (February 2025) reports that [[donald-trump|Trump]], at the same time he rolled out [[project-stargate|Project Stargate]], suggested that either [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] or [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]] buy TikTok.
