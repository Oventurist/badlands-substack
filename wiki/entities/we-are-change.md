# We Are Change

We Are Change is an independent alternative-media organization founded by journalist [[luke-rudkowski|Luke Rudkowski]], known for confrontational street reporting and criticism of American foreign interventionism.[1]

## Role in coverage of the 2024 ticket

Badlands Media referenced We Are Change in its July 2024 article on Donald Trump's selection of [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] as vice-presidential nominee, citing a post from Rudkowski to reinforce the argument that establishment hawks' visible distress over Vance signalled a genuine shift away from neoconservative dominance of Republican foreign policy.[1] The citation reflects Badlands' broader practice of drawing on independent outlets rather than legacy media when documenting antiwar sentiment on the American right.[1]
