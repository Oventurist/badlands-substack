# Dana Barnes

Dana Barnes is Vice President of Public Sector at the research and advisory firm [[forrester|Forrester]], where he advises federal agencies on cybersecurity architecture and technology modernization.[1]

## Advocacy for zero trust in government

In an October 2025 interview with FedScoop, Barnes argued for embedding [[zero-trust|zero trust]] principles into all government operations, including software development and system consolidation, rather than treating security as a bolt-on control.[1] He emphasized the need for a holistic approach spanning an entire organization rather than isolated pilot projects.[1]

Barnes framed the Trump administration's push to reduce the size of the federal workforce as simultaneously an opportunity and a challenge, arguing that a smaller workforce forces faster adoption of modern technologies such as artificial intelligence in order to maintain and increase efficiency.[1] At the same time, he warned of the risks posed by bureaucratic delays and stressed the necessity of a well-trained workforce capable of implementing these technologies safely and effectively.[1]

## Reception in alternative media

Barnes's remarks were carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of October 7, 2025, where commentators used the segment as a springboard for a broader argument that the federal government's own modernization rhetoric coexists with, and may accelerate, a public loss of faith in federal institutions.[1] Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] contended that state governments are no better managed than the federal government and cannot survive without federal money, and that under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s plan "the loss of faith is required."[1]
