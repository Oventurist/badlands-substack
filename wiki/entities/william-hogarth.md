# William Hogarth

William Hogarth was a satirical English painter cited in the Badlands Media essay "In Defense of Benjamin Franklin" for his portrait mocking [[francis-dashwood|Sir Francis Dashwood]] and the [[hellfire-club|Hell-Fire Club]].[1]

The article describes Hogarth as having "astutely captured this irony in his biting rendition of Sir Francis Dashwood dressed as St Francis of Assisi," except that the saintly attributes are replaced with their inversions: an erotic novel instead of a bible, a naked woman instead of Jesus, a Venetian masque, and the figure of fellow Hellfire leader Lord Sandwich as a lunar god in place of a halo — details taken to indicate the Dionysian orgiastic ceremonies performed at night by the initiated.[1]
