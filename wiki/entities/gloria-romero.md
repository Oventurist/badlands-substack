# Gloria Romero

Gloria Romero is a former California State Senator who served as Senate Majority Leader in the California legislature and who announced in September 2024 that she was leaving the [[democratic-party|Democrat Party]] and re-registering as a [[republican-party|Republican]].[1] Her defection was reported by [[breitbart|Breitbart]] and highlighted in the September 10, 2024 edition of the Badlands News Brief published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Departure from the Democratic Party

Romero announced her switch on a Wednesday in early September 2024, saying that the party she was leaving was "not the Democratic Party" she had "once championed."[1] In her statement she said: "I am now another near-life-long Democrat who is joining the growing number of people, including key groups like Latinos, who are leaving the Democratic Party. This is not the Democratic Party that I once championed. I do not recognize it anymore, and I cannot continue. I changed my voting registration today as the sun was rising, to Republican, which has, under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], become the champion of working people."[1]

## Criticism of Kamala Harris on education

The day after her announcement, Romero appeared on [[fox-news|Fox News]] with host [[laura-ingraham|Laura Ingraham]] and criticized Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s record on education during Harris's tenure as Attorney General of California, saying that Harris had done "nothing to change the schools."[1] Romero further noted that Harris had once sought to advance legislation "to jail mothers" of chronically truant students.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated Romero's defection as a visible instance of a broader trend, arguing that people "uncommitted to the new world order are walking away from central planning at quite a clip," with many defecting quietly while others, like Romero, announce it publicly.[1] Ashe argued that Romero was "correct that her former party is no longer the champion of working people," describing this as the standard trajectory of communism, in which "the workers of the world unite until they're no longer useful."[1] The commentary went further, asserting that the Democrat Party is "actually a communist front" and that the Republican Party is as well, concluding that "there is only one party" — one said to belong to Harris, Hakeem Jeffries, Liz Cheney, and [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]] alike, and characterized as "novel authoritarianism enabled by technology and fueled by weaponized empathy."[1]
