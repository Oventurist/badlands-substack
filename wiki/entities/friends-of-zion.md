# Friends of Zion

Friends of Zion is a pro-[[israel|Israel]] organization founded by [[mike-evans|Mike Evans]], which operates the Friends of Zion Heritage Center in Jerusalem and sponsors delegations of American Christian leaders to Israel.[1]

## December 2025 pastors' mission

In December 2025, a delegation of 1,000 U.S. Christian pastors and influencers — described as the largest group of American Christian leaders to visit Israel since the state's founding — arrived as part of a Friends of Zion initiative.[1] The trip was organized in partnership with the [[israeli-ministry-of-foreign-affairs|Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs]] and was designed to provide training and prepare participants to serve as unofficial ambassadors for Israel in their home communities.[1] Evans, a confidant of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], said most Evangelical Bible believers are Zionists, making them a key ally in efforts to counter antisemitism.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the mission as Israel "building a propaganda army" using the Christian Church, arguing that recruiting church leaders with material amenities and ego-stroking was intended to deploy them into congregations to build support for war.[1] He objected in particular to Evans's claim that rhetoric against Israel is "one hundred times worse than what the Nazis said on their party platform in 1920," calling it a gaslighting dog-whistle inviting retribution against critics of Israel.[1] He argued that what radicalizes people against Israel is not a secret brainwashing program but the rhetoric of Israeli leaders and ardent Zionists themselves, citing Congressman [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]]'s repeated "Starve them all" posts about the 2.5 million inhabitants of [[gaza|Gaza]].[1]
