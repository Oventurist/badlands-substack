# Alexander Dugin

**Alexander Dugin** is a Russian political philosopher known in mainstream Western media as "Putin's Brain," a label reflecting claims of his influence over Russian strategic thinking.[1]

## Commentary on MAGA

In January 2025, as the Kremlin signalled openness to talks with President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted recent remarks by Dugin about the MAGA movement as part of an argument that "something big" was being staged in US–Russia relations.[1] The brief presented Dugin's commentary alongside Trump's stated readiness to meet [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and the Kremlin's welcoming response.[1]

## Killing of his daughter

Dugin's daughter, the journalist and geopolitical commentator [[darya-dugina|Darya Dugina]], was killed in a targeted attack; an October 2023 [[washington-post|Washington Post]] report indicated that the [[cia|CIA]] had been running covert operations inside Russia that included her killing, carried out through Ukrainian directorates trained and equipped in partnership with the agency.[2]

## "An Even Deeper State and the Dark Enlightenment" (2025)

In April 2025, Dugin published an essay arguing that the rise of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] represented a deeper, tech-driven ideological shift aimed at accelerating humanity toward the singularity by dismantling liberal globalism through an alliance between populist and tech-right forces.[3] He wrote that Trumpists had declared outright war on the deep state and already won significant victories, such as the closure of [[usaid|USAID]], and that they define that deep state as a liberal-democratic ruling elite — both left-wing and neoconservative — entrenched in the U.S. government, backed by financial, military, and high-tech oligarchies, with networks permeating the intelligence agencies.[3]

Against that elite's commitment to globalism, unipolarity, and woke ideology, Dugin set the MAGA ideology of traditional values, binary gender, protection from mass migration, strengthened sovereignty, preserved nation-states, and a multipolar "Great Power Order" — a shift he characterized as an ideological and geopolitical revolution.[3] He attributed the relative ease of Trump's victory over deep-state resistance to the prior ideological conversion of the high-tech sector and parts of the security and intelligence communities along [[dark-enlightenment|Dark Enlightenment]] lines, and described Trump's decisiveness as a global plan to accelerate the progression of time itself — "philosophy, strategy, and even metaphysics."[3] Badlands commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] wrote that Dugin recognizes the deeper layer of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and the Regime's ongoing infiltration of all sides.[3]

## Cited in the "paper tiger" exchange (September 2025)

Badlands commentary on the September 2025 "real bear, not a paper tiger" exchange between Moscow and Washington cited posts from Dugin, described as a [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] adviser and political philosopher, alongside those of special envoy [[kirill-dmitriev|Kirill Dmitriev]], as Russian-side signal supporting the reading that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s pro-Ukraine rhetoric was strategic rather than a genuine change of posture.[4]
