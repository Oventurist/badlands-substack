# Mack Beggs

Mack Beggs is a Texas wrestler and mixed martial artist, a biological female who transitioned to identify as a transgender man, and who came to national attention through competition in gendered athletic categories.[1]

In May 2023, Beggs publicly accepted a challenge issued on Twitter by former UFC fighter [[jake-shields|Jake Shields]], who had offered to fight "the 10 toughest trans men in the world" consecutively without a training camp or rest between bouts.[1] Announcing the acceptance on Instagram, Beggs said: "We just going to make this an official call out, but Jake Shields I will take you up on your offer," while rejecting the format — "the offer isn't going to be 10 versus one... You're going to do a one-on-one type match."[1] Coverage reported that Beggs vowed to defeat Shields in crude terms.[1]

The exchange was reported by The Gateway Pundit and OutKick, and was treated by Badlands Media commentator [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] as an illustration of competition serving as a public test of contested claims about sex and gender.[1]
