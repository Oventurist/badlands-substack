# Article III Project

The Article III Project is an American conservative judicial advocacy organization founded and led by lawyer [[mike-davis|Mike Davis]], a prominent and combative commentator on federal court fights.[1]

## Role in the 2025 Alien Enemies Act dispute

In April 2025, Davis — described by Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as her "favorite legal shitposter" — used the group's platform to argue that the courts' legitimacy depends on public belief in their ability to deliver justice.[1] Reacting to the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court's]] middle-of-the-night order blocking removals of Venezuelan detainees under the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]], Davis wrote: "This is shameful lawlessness and cowardice from the conservative justices. You expect this nonsense from the three leftists. Are they trying to destroy the federal judiciary's legitimacy?"[1]
