# Universal Meditech Inc.

**Universal Meditech Inc.**, later operating as **Prestige Biotech**, was a medical-device company based in the Fresno and Reedley area of California, run by Chinese national [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]] with business partner [[zhaoyan-wang|Zhaoyan Wang]].

## Fraud conviction

The Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 reports that Zhu was convicted in federal court in California over the company's operations, on charges of conspiracy to commit wire fraud, multiple counts of wire fraud, distributing adulterated and misbranded medical devices, and making a false statement to the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]]. The company fraudulently sold more than one million faulty COVID-19 tests imported from China for nearly $4 million between 2020 and 2023.

## Reedley laboratory

The operation is also associated with an unlicensed laboratory discovered in Reedley in 2022. According to a report by the [[select-committee-on-the-chinese-communist-party|Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party]] cited in the article, vials found there were labeled as pathogens including HIV, tuberculosis, malaria, hepatitis and Ebola. The source suggests biological materials later found in the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas garage]] managed by [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]] may have been connected to these California operations.

Staffing for the company was drawn from inexperienced workers — including cellphone salespeople, supermarket workers and childcare workers — some of them recruited through the Fresno County Economic Development Corporation, a public body that places unskilled workers and provides subsidies to employers who hire them.
