# Bill Nye

Bill Nye is an American television science presenter and climate advocate known as "Bill Nye the Science Guy."[1]

In early December 2023, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] recounted seeing Nye interviewed on Denver's Channel 7 ahead of an event in Boulder, Colorado, in which he asserted that the single most important action an individual can take to combat climate change is to vote.[1] The Badlands commentary paired Nye's remarks with [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s claims at [[cop28|COP28]] about deaths from extreme heat, presenting both as evidence that "the new regime talking points have dropped" and that the climate narrative would intensify heading into the 2024 election season.[1]
