# Liberal Democratic Party

The **Liberal Democratic Party (LDP)** is Japan's ruling political party, whose leader serves as prime minister.[1]

## Taiwan diplomacy, December 2025

A senior LDP figure, [[koichi-hagiuda|Koichi Hagiuda]], met with [[taiwan|Taiwan]]'s President [[lai-ching-te|Lai Ching-te]] during a wave of visits to Taiwan by Japanese lawmakers, telling his hosts that Tokyo–Taipei relations were "the best they have ever been."[1] The visits underscored close ties between Japan and Taiwan and drew a sharp response from Beijing.[1]

The exchanges came at a period of heightened friction between Japan and [[china|China]]. LDP Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]] had said in November 2025 that Japan's military could theoretically be deployed if China attacked Taiwan, prompting Chinese retaliation including limits on Chinese tourists traveling to Japan and public criticism of Takaichi.[1]

## 2025 leadership election

On October 4, 2025, the LDP chose hardline conservative [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]] as its new leader in a historic first, after none of five candidates secured a first-round majority and Takaichi defeated Shinjiro Koizumi in a run-off.[2] The party faced the task of rebuilding public confidence and fending off opposition groups promising stimulus and clampdowns on migrants, with a parliamentary vote to replace [[shigeru-ishiba|Shigeru Ishiba]] expected on October 15 after the [[2025-liberal-democratic-party-leadership-election|2025 LDP leadership election]].[2]
