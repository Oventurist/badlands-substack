# Palestine Liberation Organization

The Palestine Liberation Organization (PLO) is the umbrella political body that has served as the internationally recognized representative of the Palestinian people in negotiations with Israel.[1]

## Oslo and the creation of the Palestinian Authority

In 1994, as part of the [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]], the PLO and the State of Israel signed the Gaza-Jericho Agreement and the Protocol on Economic Relations, commonly known as the [[paris-protocol|Paris Protocol]]. These agreements created the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]], granting it very limited powers of governance while Israel retained complete control over taxation in Palestine.[1]

The framework was explicitly designed as a temporary five-year arrangement, to expire when the Two-State Solution scheduled by the Oslo Accords was fully implemented. It has instead remained in force for decades, leaving the PA structurally dependent on Israeli-collected clearance revenues that account for roughly 70–75 percent of its income.[1]
