# Megyn Kelly

Megyn Kelly is an American broadcaster and independent media host.[1]

In July 2026, Kelly and [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] faced backlash from other prominent conservatives for continuing to stand by [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] amid the growing rift over Owens' conspiracy claims about the murder of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] and the preliminary hearing of [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]].[1]

## Virginia Tech exchange over Charlie Kirk's killing (September 2025)

On Wednesday, September 24, 2025, the former Fox News anchor took part in a [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] event at Virginia Tech University, following the death of the organization's founder [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], who was killed during a campus event at Utah Valley University earlier that month.[2] In keeping with Kirk's "Prove Me Wrong" format, the event invited audience members to engage speakers directly, producing a heated exchange between Kelly and a young attendee.[2] In an ABC News livestream, a man who appeared to be a student asked Kelly how she could support a president who "contributes to the rhetoric that got your friend Charlie killed," citing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s rally line "I hate my enemies" and similar remarks by White House Deputy Chief of Staff [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]].[2] Kelly dismissed the points as assuming "facts not in evidence" and "not true."[2]

Badlands contributor Burning Bright used the episode to discuss what he calls "Toxic Unity," arguing that "Con Inc." refines liberalism and communism into "rage porn" for monetization, that he is "on the lookout for the thing pretending to be me," and that genuine unification will come only from "all of the people that wind up in the right place, on their own, together."[2]
