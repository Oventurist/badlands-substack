# Megyn Kelly

Megyn Kelly is an American journalist and former television news anchor.

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Kelly, along with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], faced backlash for continuing to stand by [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] amid the growing conservative rift over her conspiracy claims in the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] case, in which Owens implicated [[israel|Israel]], Egypt, the US military, [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] staff, and [[erika-kirk|Erika Kirk]] in the murder of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].
