# Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant

The Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant (also rendered Zaporizhzhia) is the largest nuclear power facility in Europe, located in Ukraine and operated under Russian control since 2022 by the state nuclear corporation [[rosatom|Rosatom]].[1]

## 2026 drone strike

In late May 2026, a fiber-optic-guided drone struck the machine hall of the plant's sixth power unit, puncturing a hole in the building.[1] Rosatom described the strike as Kiev's first deliberate attack on the facility's main equipment and warned that any resulting explosion or fire could cut power and water supplies, creating conditions for a nuclear incident.[1] Rosatom chief executive [[aleksey-likhachev|Aleksey Likhachev]] stated that radiation released by such an event would primarily endanger [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and neighbouring European Union countries.[1] Ukrainian authorities denied involvement.[1]

## Context

The strike was cited by [[russia|Russia]] as part of the justification for a large-scale overnight missile and drone campaign against Ukrainian military and defense-industrial targets.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] pointed to the attack on the largest nuclear plant in Europe as evidence that Ukrainian leadership had grown "increasingly unhinged" and increasingly willing to attempt false flag operations.[1]
