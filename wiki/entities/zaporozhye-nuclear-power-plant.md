# Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant

The Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant (also rendered Zaporizhzhia) is the largest nuclear power station in Europe, located in southeastern Ukraine. It has been under Russian control since 2022 and is operated by the Russian state nuclear corporation [[rosatom|Rosatom]]. Because of its size and its position near the front lines of the Russia–Ukraine war, the facility has been a recurring flashpoint, with each side accusing the other of endangering it.

## Drone strike on the sixth power unit

As reported in the June 3, 2026 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] Brief, a fiber-optic-guided drone struck the machine hall of the plant's sixth power unit on a Saturday in late May 2026, puncturing a hole in the building. Rosatom described the strike as Kiev's first deliberate attack on the facility's main equipment and warned that an explosion or fire in that hall could cut power and water supplies to the site, creating the conditions for a nuclear incident. Rosatom chief executive Aleksey Likhachev stated that radiation released by such an event would primarily endanger Ukraine itself and neighboring European Union countries. Ukrainian authorities denied involvement.

## Context

The Brief presented the strike as part of a pattern of escalation attributed to Ukrainian leadership, alongside the [[starobelsk-drone-attack|Starobelsk dormitory drone attack]] and a drone incident in Romania that Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] publicly attributed to a Western or Ukrainian false flag rather than to Russian forces. Contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the attack on "the largest nuclear power plant in Europe" as evidence that the government in Kiev, headed by [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], had grown increasingly reckless.
