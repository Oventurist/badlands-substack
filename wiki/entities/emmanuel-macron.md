# Emmanuel Macron

Emmanuel Macron is the President of France.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief cites Macron as one of several European incumbents grappling with severe domestic unpopularity at a moment of transatlantic strain. The article reports that Macron registers just 16% approval amid far-right gains in France. He is grouped with U.K. Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]], who faces resignation calls over a scandal involving the [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]]-linked former ambassador [[peter-mandelson|Peter Mandelson]], and German Chancellor [[friedrich-merz|Friedrich Merz]], who remains deeply unpopular. The Brief notes that this weakness has driven far-right parties to lead or near-lead polls in Germany, the United Kingdom, France and Italy, propelled by inflation, economic stagnation and immigration backlash, even as [[european-union|European]] governments pursue "de-risking" from the United States.


## Refusal to join the Hormuz coalition (March 2026)

According to the March 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, Macron declared that France is "not party to the conflict" with [[iran|Iran]] and "will never take part in operations" to reopen the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], rejecting President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s call for an international coalition. France's refusal was echoed by Germany's [[boris-pistorius|Boris Pistorius]] and by Spain, Italy and Greece.
