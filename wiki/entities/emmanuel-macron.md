# Emmanuel Macron

Emmanuel Macron is the President of France.

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] cites Macron as one of several European incumbents grappling with severe domestic unpopularity at a moment of transatlantic strain. The article reports that Macron registers just 16% approval amid far-right gains in France. He is grouped with U.K. Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]], who faces resignation calls over a scandal involving the [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]]-linked former ambassador [[peter-mandelson|Peter Mandelson]], and German Chancellor [[friedrich-merz|Friedrich Merz]], who remains deeply unpopular. The Brief notes that this weakness has driven far-right parties to lead or near-lead polls in Germany, the United Kingdom, France and Italy, propelled by inflation, economic stagnation and immigration backlash, even as [[european-union|European]] governments pursue "de-risking" from the United States.
