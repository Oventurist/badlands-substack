# Joseph P. Kennedy

Joseph P. Kennedy was an American businessman and diplomat, patriarch of the [[kennedy-family|Kennedy family]] and father of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]].[1]

## Bootlegging

Britain's leading distilling companies sold bulk quantities of liquor to Kennedy and to [[arnold-rothstein|Arnold Rothstein]] during [[prohibition|Prohibition]].[1] Kennedy was a bootlegger who worked with organized crime to build his family's fortune.[1] His British liquor wholesaling rights during Prohibition were obtained through the [[duke-of-devonshire|Duke of Devonshire]], into whose family Kennedy later married his daughter Kathleen; Kathleen's husband died in the Second World War four months after their wedding.[1]

## Ambassadorship

Kennedy served as United States Ambassador to the United Kingdom from 1938 to 1940 — a posting [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] notes came one year before the United States joined England in the Second World War.[1]

## Transition to legal liquor

With the repeal of Prohibition, families such as the Kennedys and the [[bronfman-family|Bronfmans]] were financially positioned to move smoothly into the legalized liquor trade of the early 1930s.[1]
