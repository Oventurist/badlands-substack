# Sons of Liberty

The **Sons of Liberty** were the revolutionary-era American organization invoked in Chapter 10 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as an exemplar of the patriotism the series holds up as the nation's decisive strength.[1]

Commenting on the patriotic meme with which [[qanon|Q]] opened drop #75 of 5 November 2017 — eleven words the author calls powerful beyond measure — the chapter argues that patriotism is what drove George Washington, Thomas Jefferson, John and Samuel Adams, Benjamin Franklin and the Sons of Liberty to found the nation in the first place.[1] The same passage defines patriotism not as flag-waving or recitation of the Pledge of Allegiance but as standing up for one's rights and defending the [[united-states-constitution|Constitution]], and concludes that a patriotic American is the strongest weapon the nation possesses and the reason the [[deep-state|Deep State]] has been unable to finish it off.[1]
