# Charles Darwin

**Charles Darwin** was the English naturalist whose theory of natural selection supplied the intellectual raw material for what Badlands Media describes as "the applied pseudoscience of social Darwinism," better known as [[eugenics]].[1]

A significant amount of Darwin's thinking, the article argues, came from [[thomas-malthus|Thomas Malthus]], whose *Essay on the Principle of Population* held that human population grows exponentially while agricultural capacity grows arithmetically — implying an eventual "survival of the fittest" food crisis in which the poor and weak die off and the well-to-do survive.[1]

Darwin's half-cousin [[francis-galton|Sir Francis Galton]] coined the term eugenics and conducted an experiment in which the Galton, Darwin, Huxley and Wedgwood families bred exclusively among themselves in pursuit of a "superhuman" line; within two generations more than 90% of the resulting offspring died at birth or were physically or mentally disabled.[1]
