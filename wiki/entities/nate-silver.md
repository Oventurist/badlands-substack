# Nate Silver

Nate Silver is an American election statistician turned Substack writer, best known for building probabilistic forecasting models of US elections before moving to independent publishing.[1]

## Dispute with the Biden campaign

Silver appeared in Badlands Media's June 2024 coverage of an Atlantic feature on the [[joe-biden|Biden]] campaign's public feud with the press.[1] After Biden campaign senior communications adviser [[tj-ducklo|TJ Ducklo]] complained on X that MSNBC, CNN and other outlets had declined to carry Biden's remarks to roughly 1,000 mostly black voters in Philadelphia while continuing to cover [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s criminal trial, Silver responded that Democrats routinely complain that the media do *not* cover Trump's alleged misdeeds enough — an inconsistency in the campaign's media critique.[1]

Ducklo fired back that Silver's point "perfectly incapsulates [sic] the disconnect between the ivory tower/beltway know-it-alls and voters," arguing that Trump's trials affect only Trump while his policies affect ordinary Americans, and urging journalists to "stop covering polls & process."[1] Badlands treated the exchange as an illustration of establishment media and Democratic operatives turning on one another, part of what its writers describe as the internal fracturing of the [[old-guard|Old Guard]] media-political alliance.[1]
