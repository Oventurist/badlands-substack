# Jaytriot

Jaytriot is a personality associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], listed among the expected speakers for the network's [[gart-3|GART 3: Irvine]] conference held January 11–14, 2024.[1]

## Role at GART 3

Promotional material for GART 3 named Jaytriot alongside [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], Kate Buckley, [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[just-human|Just Human]], [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], [[gmoney|GMoney]], [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]], [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of BPH]] as expected speakers at the Irvine event.[1]

The same promotion singled out Jaytriot — with Burning Bright, Just Human, Chris Paul and Ghost — in a joking aside suggesting attendees might "trap" the hosts into unofficial lobby or bar socials lasting until 3am if they had whisky on hand, an illustration of the informal, community-facing role such personalities played at Badlands live events.[1]

Several GART 3 speakers, of whom Jaytriot was one of the announced roster, also participated in an exclusive pre-conference call-in live stream for ticket-buyers announced for December 7, 2023.[1]
