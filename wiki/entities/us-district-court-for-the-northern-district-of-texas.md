# U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas

The United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas is the federal trial court whose Fort Worth Division received the lawsuit filed by [[x-corp|X Corp.]] against [[media-matters|Media Matters for America]] in November 2023.[1]

## Role in X Corp. v. Media Matters

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] announced early on Saturday, November 18, 2023 that his social media platform would be "filing a thermonuclear lawsuit" against the left-leaning non-profit and "all those who colluded" for "completely misrepresenting" the real user experience on the platform, and said the filing would come the following Monday.[1] Sceptics on the platform, including a CNBC reporter who suggested "Musk may have been lying, like he has done before," doubted that he would follow through.[1] The complaint was in fact filed in the Northern District of Texas, Fort Worth Division, and alleged that the organization's tactics were manipulative and deceptive.[1]

The choice of venue placed the litigation in the same state whose attorney general, [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]], simultaneously opened an investigation into Media Matters for potential fraudulent activity under the Texas Business Organizations Code and the Deceptive Trade Practices Act.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the parallel federal suit and state investigation as "a lawsuit cascade" in the information war, and as the beginning of a template-setting campaign against those who had assaulted the constitutional right to free speech — one that would be fought both in court and in the court of public opinion ahead of a consequential election.[1]
