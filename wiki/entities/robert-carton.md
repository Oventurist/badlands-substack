# Robert Carton

Robert Carton is a scientist formerly employed by the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] who is cited in Badlands Media's survey of harmful food and water additives as a critic of municipal water [[fluoride|fluoridation]].[1]

Carton is quoted in the article for the assertion that fluoride has never been proven to prevent tooth decay, and for the broader judgment that "Fluoridation is the greatest case of scientific fraud of this century, if not all time."[1] Contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] invoked Carton as an example of the kind of credentialed specialist who, in DeLarme's framing, risks career suicide by publicly contradicting institutional consensus.[1]
