# John Fabbricatore

John Fabbricatore, known informally as "John Fabbs," is the former director of Immigration and Customs Enforcement for Colorado and a 2024 congressional candidate in Colorado's sixth district.[1] He spent decades in immigration-related government service and became one of the most prominent public warners about the spread of the Venezuelan gang [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] and cartel-sponsored activity inside American cities.[1]

## Warnings about Tren de Aragua

Fabbricatore was the principal law enforcement source in a September 2024 Daily Mail report on Tren de Aragua's takeover of apartment complexes in [[aurora-colorado|Aurora, Colorado]], telling the outlet: "There's about to be a big gang war."[1] He had been warning about TdA and related cartel activity for more than a year before that report.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] interviewed him on The Colorado Show in February 2024, when he warned that American cities were becoming overrun and that the cartels were exploiting sanctuary policies, and again on September 1, 2024, in a discussion of the scale of the problem and of solutions that would require federal government intervention.[1] Badlands credited his earlier warnings as having been vindicated by subsequent events and argued that his renewed warning of an imminent gang war should be heeded.[1]

## Congressional candidacy

Fabbricatore ran to replace Democratic Representative [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]] in Colorado's sixth congressional district, whom Badlands characterized as an "ignorant and arrogant Ukraine activist."[1] Ashe in America argued that a functional Congress committed to American interests requires sending people who "know how to do things," but conceded that effective government gaslighting in Colorado — producing what she called "weaponized empathy" — left Fabbricatore with an uphill battle.[1] She noted that the biennial House election cycle is a design feature allowing voters to remove poor representatives, and that the feature had never been more relevant.[1]
