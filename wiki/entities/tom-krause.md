# Tom Krause

Tom Krause is an official associated with the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] (DOGE), the cost-cutting effort led by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] during the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

In an order issued Wednesday, February 5, 2025, U.S. District Judge [[colleen-kollar-kotelly|Colleen Kollar-Kotelly]] temporarily restricted access to the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]]'s payment records system to Treasury officials and just two DOGE employees — Krause and [[marko-elez|Marko Elez]] — on a read-only basis.[1] The order additionally excluded anyone who was "not a Special Government Employee" from that access.[1]

Krause's counterpart Elez resigned on Thursday, February 6, 2025, after being linked to offensive social media posts, leaving Krause as the remaining named DOGE designee under the court's limitation.[1]
