# Greg Brockman

Greg Brockman is the president of [[openai|OpenAI]]. He was named in the relief sought by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI and chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]]: Musk asked for the removal of both Altman and Brockman, alongside as much as $134 billion in ill-gotten gains and a reversal of OpenAI's 2025 restructuring.[1]

A federal jury rejected Musk's claims in May 2026 after less than two hours of deliberation, and U.S. District Judge [[yvonne-gonzalez-rogers|Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers]] adopted the advisory jury's finding that Altman and OpenAI were not liable, leaving the requested leadership changes unrealized.[1]
