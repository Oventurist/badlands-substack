# Deena Hinshaw

Deena Hinshaw served as Alberta's Health Minister during the COVID-19 period and is identified in Badlands reporting as the official whose pandemic measures were ruled illegal by an Alberta court in early 2024.[1]

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] reported that the ruling paved the way for what appeared likely to become the largest class action in Alberta's history.[1] The decision was presented as part of a cluster of Canadian legal reversals — alongside the [[emergencies-act|Emergencies Act]] ruling and the [[coutts-four|Coutts 4]] charge withdrawals — indicating that Canadian [[lawfare|lawfare]] had become the primary front in disputes over pandemic-era government power.[1]
