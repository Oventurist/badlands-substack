# BadlandsMedia.tv

**BadlandsMedia.tv** is the central website of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], announced on March 28, 2023 in a Substack post titled "Introducing BadlandsMedia.tv!" and subtitled "Your One-Stop Hub for Everything Badlands."[1]

## Launch and purpose

The site was presented as a single destination for everything readers might want to know about Badlands Media, consolidating the network's shows, its Substack newsletter, its team roster, its programming schedule and an account of the organization's story in one place.[1] The announcement opened with the greeting "Hello Badlanders!" — the network's customary term for its audience — and described the launch as a point of pride for the organization.[1]

## Functions

Beyond acting as a directory of Badlands content, the portal was framed as a means of connection.[1] It links to the [[badlands-locals-community|Badlands Locals Community]], described as offering 24/7 live chat and exclusive content; it is the channel through which readers stay up to date on Badlands Media events; and it provides the point of contact with [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty]] for advertising inquiries.[1]

The site also hosts the [[badlands-marketplace|Badlands Marketplace]] at a marketplace subdomain, a directory of what the organization calls Patriot-owned and [[america-first-movement|America First]] businesses.[1]

## Positioning

Badlands Media characterized the launch as its next step "as an America First media company," expressing the hope that a central hub would give its readers, viewers and listeners a convenient place to keep up with the network "as we march forward."[1] The announcement recorded 116 likes on Substack.[1]
