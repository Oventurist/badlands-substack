# Bruce Swartz

Bruce Swartz is a longtime official of the U.S. [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] who served for many years as the head of the department's office of international affairs, the unit responsible for extradition requests, mutual legal assistance, and coordination with foreign law-enforcement bodies.[1]

## Reassignment, January 2025

In the first days of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second administration, the Associated Press reported — citing a person familiar with the matter — that Swartz had been reassigned along with as many as twenty other Justice Department staffers.[1] The move was reported as part of the same wave of day-one personnel changes that reshaped the senior ranks of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]], where [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] had already resigned, acting director Paul Abbate stepped down hours before the inauguration, and Brian Driscoll was installed as acting director pending the Senate confirmation of Kashyap "Kash" Patel.[1]

Fox News reported the reassignments under the framing that Trump's overhaul had put "untouchable" federal officials on notice, quoting a former agent, and tied the effort to Trump's campaign promise to root out corruption at both the FBI and the Justice Department after a term of repeated scandals under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]
