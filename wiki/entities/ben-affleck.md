# Ben Affleck

Ben Affleck is an American actor and director who features in Badlands Media commentary on the [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]] case chiefly through his 2024 divorce from singer and actress [[jennifer-lopez|Jennifer Lopez]].[1]

## Divorce from Jennifer Lopez

An October 2024 Badlands Media essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounted a rumor that Affleck left Lopez "because of her involvement in it all," meaning the allegations surrounding Combs.[1] Carlson laid out a timeline: on March 25, 2024, Combs's home was raided by the [[fbi|FBI]]; five months later, on August 20, 2024, Affleck and Lopez filed for divorce.[1] Citing rapper 50 Cent, the essay asserted that Affleck ended the marriage after learning of Lopez's participation in Combs's parties — described as conduct "even a celebrity in the Hollywood cesspool can't come back from."[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

The Affleck–Lopez separation is offered in the essay as one of several signs that entertainers connected to Combs were scrambling to distance themselves, alongside social-media scrubbing, resignations by music executives, and [[kevin-hart|Kevin Hart]]'s closure of restaurants co-owned with Combs.[1] The claims are presented as rumor and inference rather than established fact, and Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
