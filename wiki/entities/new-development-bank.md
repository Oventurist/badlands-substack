# New Development Bank

The New Development Bank is a Shanghai-based multilateral development institution created by the [[brics|BRICS]] countries — Brazil, Russia, India, China and South Africa — as an alternative to Western-dominated lenders.[1] It became a focal point of [[de-dollarization|de-dollarization]] discussion in April 2023 when Brazilian president [[luiz-inacio-lula-da-silva|Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva]] used a visit to the bank to call for a BRICS currency to replace the dollar in foreign trade.[1]

## Lula's April 2023 visit

Lula's remarks were made on a Thursday during a visit to the Shanghai-based institution, ahead of a planned meeting with Chinese president [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] in Beijing.[1] He called on BRICS nations to devise an alternative to the dollar for foreign trade, a position reported as supporting China's broader challenge to United States global economic dominance.[1]

## Leadership

Former Brazilian president [[dilma-rousseff|Dilma Rousseff]] became the bank's chief executive, a personnel choice that tied the institution more closely to Brazil's governing left and to Lula's economic diplomacy.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the bank's rising profile as evidence that the economic centre of gravity is shifting from the [[g7|G7]] toward the BRICS bloc, with the inflection point occurring during the COVID-19 period, and framed the transition as a planned demolition of the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] system rather than speculation.[1]
