# Greg Willis

Greg Willis is the District Attorney of Collin County, Texas.[1]

## Prosecution of Karmelo Anthony

On Tuesday, June 24, 2025, Willis announced that a grand jury had indicted [[karmelo-anthony|Karmelo Sincere Anthony]] for first-degree murder in the April 2, 2025 stabbing death of [[austin-metcalf|Austin Metcalf]] at a high school track meet in Frisco, Texas.[1] Both boys were 17 at the time of the incident; the Texas criminal justice system treats 17-year-olds as adults.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the public had been "gaslit" since Metcalf's death into believing his attacker was the true victim of institutionalized racism, and noted that recently released CCTV footage of the event disputed that narrative and, in the view of legal experts, undercut Anthony's defenses.[1]
