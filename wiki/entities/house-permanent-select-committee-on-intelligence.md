# House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence

The House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence (HPSCI) is the standing committee of the U.S. House of Representatives responsible for oversight of the Intelligence Community.[1] In Badlands coverage it appears principally as the vehicle through which the 2019 Ukraine whistleblower complaint was converted into impeachment proceedings.[1]

## Role in the first Trump impeachment

According to documents released by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] in April 2026, then-HPSCI Chairman [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] and then-Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] used a false, second-hand narrative to create media intrigue and ultimately spark the basis to impeach President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in December 2019.[1] The underlying complaint had been advanced by former Intelligence Community Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]], who — the Office of the Director of National Intelligence said — departed from standard inspector-general procedure, interviewed only four individuals, and relied on second-hand testimony while ignoring Department of Justice guidance to ensure the complaint reached [[congress|Congress]], the [[fbi|FBI]], and the press.[1]

Gabbard characterised the sequence as an instance of "deep state actors within the Intelligence Community" concocting a narrative "used by Congress to usurp the will of the American people and impeach the duly-elected President of the United States."[1] The committee is also the body from which Representative [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]] was removed following counterintelligence concerns, and it sits at the center of the [[first-impeachment-of-donald-trump|first impeachment of Donald Trump]].[1]

## 2019 briefings cited in criminal referrals

The criminal referrals sent by the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] to the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] in April 2026 against the impeachment whistleblower and former Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]] cite possible federal law violations tied specifically to 2019 briefings delivered before the committee.[2]

## Honduran election monitoring (2025)

Badlands commentator [[canncon|CannCon]] reported that congressmen from HPSCI traveled to [[honduras|Honduras]] to "monitor" that country's November 2025 election, and noted that committee Chairman [[rick-crawford|Rick Crawford]] had also monitored elections in person in [[guyana|Guyana]], Suriname and Barbados in April 2025 - jurisdictions he described as key narcotrafficking hubs.[3] CannCon presented the pattern, together with the [[state-department|State Department]]'s revocation and denial of visas for two [[libre-party|LIBRE party]] election officials while the count remained uncertified, as evidence of "an attempt at regime placement in Honduras."[3]
