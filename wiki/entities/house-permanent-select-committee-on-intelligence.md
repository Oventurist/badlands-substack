# House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence

The House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence (HPSCI) is a standing committee of the United States House of Representatives tasked with overseeing the intelligence community, including the [[cia|CIA]], NSA, and other intelligence agencies. In the Badlands Media corpus, it is cited as the congressional body that investigated the 2012 Benghazi attack and uncovered evidence of a cover-up by the Obama administration.

## Benghazi Investigation

According to the source, the HPSCI conducted hearings into the Benghazi attack, during which [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] pressed [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] about the requirement for CIA operatives to re-sign non-disclosure agreements. The article notes that two former CIA officials who fought in Benghazi testified before a subcommittee of the HPSCI, and that their testimony helped expose contradictions in the administration's narrative about the attack.

The source cites The Hill's reporting on the committee's findings, including the discovery that Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] had obstructed the direct order of the Commander in Chief to deploy military assets to rescue Americans under attack. The committee's report was chaired by [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]], who also investigated Clinton's use of a private email server.
