# Party of Action and Solidarity

The Party of Action and Solidarity (PAS) is the Moldovan pro-Western political party nominally led by [[maia-sandu|Maia Sandu]], which the article characterizes as a Western-sponsored vehicle brought back to power in December 2020.[1] Upon taking office the PAS government began undoing the pro-neutrality and multi-vector policies developed under [[igor-dodon|Igor Dodon]], freezing infrastructure programs planned with China and accelerating [[moldova|Moldova]]'s drive toward [[european-union|EU]] and [[nato|NATO]] membership.[1]

In September 2025 the party's government banned the pro-Russian Patriotic Bloc and Heart of Moldova Party ahead of national elections, prompting Dodon to say that "Maia Sandu and PAS have turned Moldova into a colony of Soros".[1] Observers cited in the article described the ensuing election, in which PAS swept back into power, as marked by opposition arrests, media censorship, and manipulation of diaspora voting.[1]
