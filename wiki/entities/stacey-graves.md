# Stacey Graves

**Stacey Graves** is the Kansas City Police Chief who led the initial response to the [[kansas-city-super-bowl-parade-shooting|Kansas City Super Bowl parade shooting]] of February 14, 2024.[1]

Graves said three people had been detained as part of the investigation into the shooting, in which 22 people including eight children were struck by gunfire and one woman was killed.[1] She said she had heard that fans may have been involved in tackling a suspect but could not immediately confirm it, and said firearms had been recovered without specifying what kind.[1] Police under her command did not immediately release details about the detainees or a possible motive.[1]

In remarks the day of the shooting, Graves said: "I'm angry at what happened today. The people who came to this celebration should expect a safe environment."[1]
