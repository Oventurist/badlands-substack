# John Griffin

John Griffin is a former [[cnn|CNN]] television producer from Stamford, Connecticut, convicted of luring a nine-year-old girl into illegal sexual acts.[1]

## Case and sentencing

Griffin pleaded guilty in federal court in December 2022 to using interstate commerce to entice and coerce a child to engage in sexual activity at his ski house in Ludlow, Vermont.[1] He had initially pleaded not guilty in 2021.[1] As part of the plea deal, the government dropped two remaining counts of enticement of a minor against Griffin, then 45 years old.[1]

According to the plea agreement, Griffin met a woman on a website during the summer of 2020 and persuaded her to bring her daughter to his Vermont home for sexual activity.[1] He paid the woman and girl to fly from Nevada to Boston, picked them up and drove them to the Vermont house, where the girl said Griffin sexually assaulted her, according to court records.[1]

On June 20, 2023, at a U.S. District Court hearing in Vermont, Griffin was sentenced to more than 19 years in prison plus an additional 15 years of supervised release, and was ordered to pay restitution to the victims.[1]
