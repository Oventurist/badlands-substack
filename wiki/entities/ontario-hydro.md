# Ontario Hydro

Ontario Hydro was the Canadian provincial electric utility that operated the core of Canada's nuclear power sector. Badlands Media commentary identifies it as the institution through which [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]] attacked the Canadian nuclear industry from the inside.[1]

## Strong's tenure, 1992–1995

After overseeing the 1992 [[rio-earth-summit|Rio Earth Summit]], Strong took up leadership of Ontario Hydro from 1992 to 1995.[1] The source states that in this post the "aging Malthusian went to war with the entire Canadian nuclear industry," using "every dirty trick to cancel refurbishments, designs for new constructions and all funding in research and development" in favour of "sustainable" energy pathways.[1]

## Legacy

Some of the damage was undone after Strong's departure, but the source describes the wounds as deep: Canada is said never to have fully recovered, having seen a consistent decay of its once-vibrant nuclear industry and the total destruction of its fusion energy efforts.[1] The episode parallels the American nuclear moratorium of 1977–2013 presided over by [[james-schlesinger|James Schlesinger]] at the Department of Energy.[1] Strong had earlier endorsed a nuclear moratorium while simultaneously serving as head of Petro Canada from 1976 to 1978.[1]
