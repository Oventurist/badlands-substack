# David Bar-Khalifa

David Bar-Khalifa is an [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] brigadier general named by [[south-africa|South Africa]] during May 2024 proceedings before the [[international-court-of-justice|International Court of Justice]] as among the Israeli figures allegedly responsible for war crimes in [[gaza|Gaza]].[1]

## Allegations at the ICJ

South Africa's legal team told the court that Bar-Khalifa ordered the execution of civilians, and asserted that he was subsequently rewarded with a promotion to head of the Personnel Division.[1] The claims were presented alongside evidence of mass graves said to contain men, women and children, many with their limbs bound in a manner indicating they had been taken prisoner and then "summarily executed."[1]

Bar-Khalifa is described as being known for a hand-written letter issued to IDF troops on the eve of the Gaza invasion and publicly released, in which he invoked "divine vengeance" and told the soldiers, "We have an iron will. I trust you."[1] South Africa used the letter as part of its argument that genocidal intent was expressed openly within the Israeli command structure.[1]
