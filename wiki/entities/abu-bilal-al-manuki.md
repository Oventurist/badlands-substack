# Abu-Bilal Al-Manuki

Abu-Bilal Al-Manuki, also known as Abu-Mainok (with Reuters variants spelling the name Al-Minuki), was a militant leader in West Africa identified by Badlands commentary as a former leader of [[boko-haram|Boko Haram]] who operated under the banner of [[isis|Islamic State]] in the Lake Chad Basin region.

## Killing in the joint US-Nigerian operation

According to the May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[nigeria|Nigerian]] and U.S. forces carried out a joint counterterrorism operation that killed Al-Manuki, with Nigeria's State House announcing on May 16, 2026 that early assessments confirmed Al-Manuki and several lieutenants were killed in a strike on his compound in the Lake Chad Basin. [[reuters|Reuters]] reported that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Nigerian President [[bola-tinubu|Bola Tinubu]] both said Al-Minuki was killed in northeastern Nigeria, and — citing the [[nigerian-army|Nigerian Army]] — that the strike took place in Metele, Borno State, in close coordination with [[africom|U.S. Africa Command]]. The Nigerian Army said the operation began about 12:01 a.m. and ended around 4 a.m. Saturday with no casualties or asset losses. The [[associated-press|Associated Press]], citing a U.S. official speaking anonymously, reported that Al-Mainuki was involved in Islamic State organizing and finance and had been plotting attacks against the United States and its interests.

## Boko Haram background

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describes Al-Manuki as "the former leader of Boko Haram," whose most famous exploit was the kidnapping of hundreds of schoolgirls in Nigeria in 2014 — girls who, in his account, were "most likely trafficked as sex slaves." He notes that 110 of these girls were rescued in Nigeria in March 2018 and that U.S. Special Forces were operating in that exact area, citing his analysis of the [[tongo-tongo-ambush|Tongo Tongo Ambush]]. He argues that by killing Al-Manuki, Trump did "a great service to Captain Traore and his Sahelian allies," though he predicts the mainstream media will frame it as Trump siding with [[ecowas|ECOWAS]] against the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]].
