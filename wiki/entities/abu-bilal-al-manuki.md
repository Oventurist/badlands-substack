# Abu-Bilal Al-Manuki

Abu-Bilal Al-Manuki, also rendered Al-Minuki or Al-Mainuki and known as Abu-Mainok, was an [[islamic-state|Islamic State]] figure in West Africa killed in a joint Nigerian–U.S. strike announced on 16 May 2026.[1]

## Death

[[nigeria|Nigeria]]'s State House said early assessments confirmed that Al-Manuki and several lieutenants were killed in a strike on his compound in the Lake Chad Basin.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Nigerian President [[bola-tinubu|Bola Tinubu]] both stated that he had been killed in northeastern Nigeria, and the Nigerian Army placed the operation in Metele, Borno State, in close coordination with U.S. [[africom|Africa Command]].[1] A U.S. official told the Associated Press anonymously that Al-Manuki was involved in Islamic State organizing and finance and had been plotting attacks against the United States and its interests.[1]

## Alleged background

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identified Al-Manuki as the former leader of [[boko-haram|Boko Haram]] and asserted that his most famous exploit was the 2014 kidnapping of hundreds of Nigerian schoolgirls, whom he said were most likely trafficked as sex slaves.[1] He argued that the killing benefited Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] and the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]] by degrading proxy groups used to destabilize the Sahel, while emphasizing that identifying the financing behind such groups mattered more than the strike itself.[1]
