# Polisario Front

The Polisario Front is an Algerian-backed separatist group that has fought a decades-long conflict with Rabat over [[western-sahara|Western Sahara]].[1]

## Position and conflict

The group has expressed support for a self-determination referendum in Western Sahara, initially proposed by the United Nations in 1991 as part of a ceasefire agreement but stalled since.[1] Its conflict with [[morocco|Morocco]] — which annexed the territory after Spain's 1975 withdrawal and controls roughly 80% of it — has been a persistent source of regional tension.[1] In April 2025 the United States, through Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], reaffirmed recognition of Moroccan sovereignty and endorsed Rabat's autonomy plan as the only basis for a settlement, a position also held by [[israel|Israel]] since 2023 and adopted by France.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writers declined to assign the Polisario Front a clear role in their globalist-versus-sovereignty frame, noting that it was unclear whether the Moroccan-backed side or the Polisario Front represented imperial interests in the information war.[1]
