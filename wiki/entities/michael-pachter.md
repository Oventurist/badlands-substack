# Michael Pachter

Michael Pachter is an equity research analyst at Wedbush Securities known for his long-running coverage of the video game sector, including [[gamestop|GameStop]].[1]

## Commentary on GameStop's bitcoin strategy

In March 2025, after GameStop announced a private offering of $1.3 billion in debt whose proceeds would fund purchases of bitcoin as a reserve asset, Pachter led a Wedbush note questioning the plan. He argued that the debt offering came at a large premium to the company's value and could exclude a substantial portion of GameStop's investor base who would not qualify for the offering under certain investor requirements.[1]

"We find it hard to understand why any investor would be pay more than two times cash value for the potential for GameStop to convert that cash into Bitcoin, particularly since the same investors can invest in Bitcoin or a Bitcoin ETF themselves," he wrote.[1]

GameStop shares slumped 22.1% on the news, a sharp reversal from an 11.7% gain earlier that week.[1]
