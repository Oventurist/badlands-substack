# Patrise Perkins-Hooker

**Patrise Perkins-Hooker** is an attorney with [[johnson-and-freeman|Johnson & Freeman, LLC]], the law firm serving as the "Administrative Partner" to [[fulton-county|Fulton County, Georgia]]'s election apparatus.[1]

## Role in the 2023 certification delay

On the Tuesday morning that Fulton County was scheduled to certify the November 7, 2023 municipal election results, Perkins-Hooker emailed county commissioners to report that "late last night after the Recount was conducted, we discovered that there was a discrepancy between the number of votes initially counted for Advance Voting Ballots, and the number reported during the recount." She stated that staff suspected a box of advance voting ballots had not been scanned into the system, that all advance voting ballots were therefore being retabulated that morning, and that as a result the Board of Registration and Elections would not be able to certify at 11 a.m.[1]

Later the same day she told [[just-the-news|Just the News]] that the recount would not delay certification, that the county would certify "in 45 minutes or so," that the second recount had been undertaken because the advance voting ballots did not agree with Election Day totals in the first recount, and that the count "was only 300 ballots off" due to a group of ballots in one box not being scanned.[1]

Her explanation drew criticism because sources observing the counts reported that the successive totals *declined* — 13,705, then 13,690, then 13,330 — whereas newly located and scanned ballots would be expected to raise the total.[1] Reporting for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Perkins-Hooker appeared to have given her statement to Just the News before the third count, which accounted for her apparently mistaken confidence that certification would proceed on schedule.[1]
