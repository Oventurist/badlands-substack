# Anthony D. Romero

Anthony D. Romero is the executive director of the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]] (ACLU), serving as the organization's chief spokesman in its litigation against executive branch policy.[1]

On the night of January 20, 2025, Romero issued the statement accompanying the ACLU's lawsuit challenging [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order seeking to end [[birthright-citizenship|birthright citizenship]] for children born in the United States to parents not lawfully present. Romero said that "denying citizenship to U.S.-born children is not only unconstitutional — it's also a reckless and ruthless repudiation of American values," arguing that birthright citizenship "is part of what makes the United States the strong and dynamic nation that it is" and that the order "seeks to repeat one of the gravest errors in American history, by creating a permanent subclass of people born in the U.S. who are denied full rights as Americans."[1]

Badlands Media commentary characterized the suit — filed alongside a separate challenge to the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] — as the opening move of a coordinated [[lawfare|lawfare]] campaign against the second Trump administration, launched within moments of the inauguration.[1]
