# Christian Democratic Union

The Christian Democratic Union (CDU) is Germany's center-right political party.[1] It featured in the September 2, 2024 Badlands News Brief as the principal loser of the eastern German state elections held that weekend.[1]

## 2024 state elections

In the [[2024-thuringia-state-election|Thuringia state election]], the CDU took around 24 percent of the vote, well behind the [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]], which secured 32 to 33 percent according to ARD and ZDF projections.[1] In Saxony, where the CDU had held power continuously since German reunification in 1990, projections showed a tight race with the AfD, with both parties hovering around 31 to 32 percent — see the [[2024-saxony-state-election|2024 Saxony state election]].[1]

Newsweek characterized the results as evidence of deepening discontent with the federal government of Chancellor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]], which had been mired in unpopularity and internal conflicts.[1]
