# Joseph Czuba

Joseph Czuba is the Illinois man charged in the October 14, 2023 stabbing death of six-year-old [[wadea-al-fayoume|Wadea Al-Fayoume]] and the wounding of the boy's mother at their home outside Chicago.[1]

## Charges

Czuba, aged 71 at the time of the attack, was the tenants' landlord.[1] He pleaded not guilty on Monday, October 30, 2023 to charges of murder, attempted murder and hate crimes, and the Department of Justice opened a hate-crime investigation into the killing.[1] The case was invoked by the [[white-house|White House]] as part of the rationale for the [[national-strategy-to-counter-islamophobia|National Strategy to Counter Islamophobia]].[1]
