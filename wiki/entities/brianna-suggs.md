# Brianna Suggs

Brianna Suggs is a campaign consultant and fundraiser who served as chief fund-raiser for New York City Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]].[1] She became publicly known in November 2023 when federal agents searched her home as part of a corruption investigation touching the mayor's 2021 election campaign.[1]

## FBI search and the Adams campaign investigation

On a Thursday in early November 2023, agents of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] conducted an early-morning raid at Suggs's Brooklyn home, an action that brought a previously non-public investigation into open view.[1] Reporting based on the search warrant described Suggs as a consultant "deeply entwined" with efforts to advance the mayor's agenda, placing her at the center of the fundraising operation under scrutiny.[1]

Federal prosecutors and the FBI were examining whether Adams's 2021 campaign had conspired with the Turkish government to receive illegal foreign donations.[1] Investigators also sought information about a Brooklyn construction company with ties to Turkey and about a small university in Washington, D.C. with ties both to Turkey and to Adams, and asked whether the campaign had kicked back benefits to the construction company's officials and employees and to Turkish officials.[1] Agents seized three iPhones, two laptop computers, papers, a "manila folder labeled Eric Adams," seven "contribution card binders" and other materials.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] folded the Suggs raid into a wider November 2023 run of election-integrity stories stretching from Bridgeport, Connecticut to Springfield, Massachusetts, arguing that figures most compromised by election irregularities were, by their exposure, helping to reveal the wider system.[1]
