# Ronan Farrow

Ronan Farrow is an American journalist whose work appears in [[new-yorker|The New Yorker]]. In Badlands Media's September 2023 coverage he features as the author of a profile of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] titled "Elon Musk's Shadow Rule."[1]

## "Elon Musk's Shadow Rule"

Farrow's article argued that Musk's "influence is more brazen and expansive" than previous "meddling of oligarchs and other monied interests in the fate of nations."[1] The piece appeared while Musk was weathering a flurry of legal attacks from the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and sustained negative press coverage.[1]

## Reaction

[[errol-musk|Errol Musk]], the entrepreneur's father, described the article to The U.S. Sun as "a hit job, a shadow government-sponsored opening salvo on Elon," likening it to "the artillery-like softening up of the enemy before the actual attack and preparing of the ever-submissive people for the attack."[1] Asked directly whether he feared a shadow government would attempt to assassinate his son, he answered "Yes."[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] considered Errol Musk's comments "relatively dramatic in context" but found it "tantalizing to see these sorts of narratives trotted out in the War of Stories."[1]
