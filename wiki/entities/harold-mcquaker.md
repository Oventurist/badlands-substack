# Harold McQuaker

Harold McQuaker is the mayor of Emo, a rural township of about 1,333 residents in Ontario, Canada, who was fined for declining to fly a Pride flag at his town hall and who publicly refused to pay the penalty.[1]

## The Pride flag dispute

McQuaker was ordered by the [[ontario-human-rights-commission|Ontario Human Rights Commission]] to pay damages after the township failed to proclaim Pride Month as instructed back in 2020.[1] The advocacy group [[borderland-pride|Borderland Pride]] had tasked Emo with declaring June as Pride Month and ordered municipal authorities to fly an "LGBTQ2 rainbow flag for a week of your choosing" — despite the fact that the township lacked an official flagpole — and asked Emo to email a copy of the proclamation or resolution once adopted and signed.[1] The township refused, and that rejection sparked a years-long arbitration process in which the tribunal found against Emo.[1]

## Refusal to comply

McQuaker said he would not be shamed into personally paying the $5,000 penalty, telling the *Toronto Sun*: "I utterly refuse to pay the $5,000 because that's extortion."[1] He also rejected orders to undertake LGBTQ2S+ "reeducation" training directed by the OHRC as an act of personal contrition, and insisted he would not yield to demands that he host a Drag Time story hour in the local library.[1] The case was detailed by *Toronto Sun* senior columnist Joe Warmington and reported by [[breitbart-news|Breitbart News]].[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] praised McQuaker's resistance, writing that the mayor of Emo "is fighting government mandates that he proclaim the rainbow communist religion from the town square" and that he had been fighting the retaliation of the "human rights" people in Ontario for years.[1] She argued that no state — "whether it's England, Canada or the UN" — gets to pick acceptable religions, claim them as science, and demand compliance, and urged the world to adopt the American [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1]
