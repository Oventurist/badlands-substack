# Collective Security Treaty Organization

The **Collective Security Treaty Organization (CSTO)** is a Russian-led military alliance of post-Soviet states, treated in Badlands Media coverage as the principal counterweight to [[nato|NATO]] expansion across Eurasia.[1]

## Armenian withdrawal

The June 13, 2024 Badlands News Brief reported that [[armenia|Armenia]] had announced it would withdraw from the CSTO, while simultaneously announcing that its top military brass would take part in NATO Military Committee meetings.[1] Badlands framed the move as a signal that one of Russia's oldest allies was abandoning it to join what the brief called the Western Hegemony, a shift reinforced by the announcement of a United States–Armenia Strategic Partnership Commission.[1] The commentary tied the realignment to a 2018 [[cia|CIA]]-led color revolution in Armenia which it judged to have succeeded.[1]

## Regional context

The brief situated Armenia's departure within a broader contest for the Caucasus, alongside pressure on neighboring Georgia after that country passed legislation impeding the [[state-department|State Department]]'s ability to work through foreign NGOs, threats against the Georgian Prime Minister, and individual sanctions issued by [[congress|Congress]] against Georgian leadership.[1] Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Mikhail Galuzin accused the European Union of using a "monitoring mission" in Armenia to spy on Azerbaijan, whose 2020-era conflict with Armenia saw a ceasefire broken by a 24-hour kinetic engagement in September 2023 that ended with Russian troops deploying to restore peace.[1] Badlands assessed these moves as an attempt by the hegemony to seize control of the Caucasus and sever the International North–South Transportation Corridor, a trade route associated with the [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]].[1]
