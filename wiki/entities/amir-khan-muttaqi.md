# Amir Khan Muttaqi

**Amir Khan Muttaqi** is the foreign minister of the [[taliban|Taliban]] government in Afghanistan.[1]

## Moscow format talks (October 2024)

Muttaqi attended the annual diplomatic forum on Afghanistan held in Moscow in early October 2024, at which Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] called on Western countries to lift sanctions on the Taliban.[1] "We once again appeal to Western countries with an urgent call to acknowledge responsibility for the post-conflict reconstruction of Afghanistan, lift sanctions restrictions, and return Kabul's appropriated assets," Lavrov said in his opening remarks, with Muttaqi present.[1] Lavrov also praised the current Afghan leadership for its campaigns against narcotics and the Islamic State, while not expressly naming the Taliban.[1]

The forum coincided with reporting that Russia had taken "a principle decision" to remove the Taliban from its list of terrorist organizations, according to President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s envoy for Afghanistan, [[zamir-kabulov|Zamir Kabulov]], who said the foreign ministry and security service were "putting finishing legal touches" on the removal.[1] The Taliban had been added to the Russian blacklist in 2003 for backing separatists in the North Caucasus.[1]
