# Glen Doherty

Glen "TB" Doherty was a former U.S. Navy SEAL and CIA security contractor who was killed during the September 11, 2012 terrorist attack on the U.S. consulate in [[benghazi|Benghazi]], Libya. He died alongside fellow SEAL [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]] while providing security at the CIA annex during the extended assault.

## Background and Service

Doherty had served as a Navy SEAL before working as a private security contractor for the CIA. On the night of the Benghazi attack, he was stationed at the CIA annex in Benghazi. When calls for help came from the nearby consulate compound, Doherty was part of the team that attempted to respond despite being instructed to stand down.

## Death and Memorial

Doherty and Woods were killed by a mortar round while defending the CIA annex during the second phase of the attack. Their deaths, along with those of Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] and [[sean-smith|Sean Smith]], brought the total American fatalities to four. The article cites a Weekly Standard report noting that surviving CIA officials who fought in Benghazi were asked to sign additional non-disclosure agreements during a memorial service at CIA headquarters honoring Woods and Doherty.
