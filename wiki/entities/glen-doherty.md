# Glen Doherty

Glen Doherty was a former Navy SEAL and CIA-affiliated security operator killed during the September 11, 2012 [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]], along with Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]], Sean Smith and [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Fox News reported that Doherty died during the extended assault by more than 100 Islamist militants that began at approximately 9:35 p.m. local time, roughly an hour after Stevens escorted the Turkish Consul General out of the consulate gate.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) In May 2013 the [[cia|CIA]] held a memorial service at its Langley headquarters honoring Doherty and Woods; The Weekly Standard reported that two surviving CIA officials were presented with additional [[non-disclosure-agreements|non-disclosure agreements]] at that service, shortly before their congressional testimony.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
