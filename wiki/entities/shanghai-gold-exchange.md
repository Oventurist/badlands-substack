# Shanghai Gold Exchange

The Shanghai Gold Exchange is China's principal marketplace for physical gold. In [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s article [[are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom|"Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?"]] it functions as the second half of a mechanism that the author says began the displacement of the [[petrodollar]].

Under that mechanism, oil producers selling crude for yuan on the [[shanghai-petroleum-and-natural-gas-exchange|Shanghai Petroleum and Natural Gas Exchange]], opened in March 2018, can convert the proceeds immediately into gold on the Shanghai Gold Exchange. The article presents this convertibility as what makes yuan-denominated oil sales attractive, effectively reintroducing a gold link into global energy trade and giving [[china|China]] and the wider [[brics]] bloc a route around dollar settlement. It is offered as an early instance of the broader return to a [[gold-standard|gold standard]] that Lange argues is now underway worldwide.
