# Quill and Dagger

**Quill and Dagger** is a senior honor society at [[cornell-university|Cornell University]] described in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as Cornell's pipeline into biotechnology and agricultural surveillance.[1]

The article says the society feeds "biotech innovation and agritech surveillance systems," with [[gates-foundation|Gates]]-funded research and [[darpa|DARPA]]-adjacent trials emerging from it.[1] Extending the chain outward, Quill and Dagger is said to feed into Gates Foundation partnerships and, by extension, [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] and [[united-nations|United Nations]] bio-surveillance systems — links the author argues became particularly visible during the COVID-19 era, when academic research, public health policy and digital identity initiatives allegedly converged on the same axis within the [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
