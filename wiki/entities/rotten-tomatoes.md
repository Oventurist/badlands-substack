# Rotten Tomatoes

Rotten Tomatoes is an American film and television review aggregation website that publishes separate scores for professional critics and for general audiences.[1] The divergence between those two figures is frequently cited in Truth Community commentary as a measure of the gap between establishment media opinion and popular sentiment.[1]

## Sound of Freedom scores (2023)

Following the July 2023 release of the anti-child-trafficking film [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]], [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] noted that the picture enjoyed a 99 percent user rating on Rotten Tomatoes against 75 percent from what it described as "'Hollywood type' professional reviewers."[1] That contrast was raised directly against [[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]] critic [[miles-klee|Miles Klee]]'s review, which had characterised the film as a "QAnon-tinged thriller."[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] made a parallel observation that audiences had made the film the top picture in America while critics were aligned in disparaging it.[1]
