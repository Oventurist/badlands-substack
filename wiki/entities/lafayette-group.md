# Lafayette Group

The Lafayette Group is a lobbying and consulting firm focused on law enforcement and national security, co-founded in 1994 by lobbyist [[scott-green|Scott Green]] and later headed by his son.[1]

According to a 2020 [[politico|Politico]] report cited in Badlands Media commentary, government contracts for niche support services to law enforcement and national security agencies have been central to the firm's business over the years.[1] While in the Senate, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] proposed appropriations for many of the niche areas in which the firm specialized, including "interoperable communications" for first responders and "fusion centers" for processing threat-related intelligence.[1]

From 2012 to 2018 the firm received contracts tied to at least one government program championed by Biden — the Nationwide Public Safety Broadband Network — and in total has received more than $10 million in contracts for work related to that network, now called FirstNet.[1]

Badlands writer [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] highlights that the firm was founded the same year as the [[crime-bill-1994|1994 Crime Bill]], legislation Biden pushed alongside [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and from which, in Carlson's reading, Green's new business stood to benefit considerably.[1]
