# Sanae Takaichi

Sanae Takaichi is the Prime Minister of [[japan|Japan]], who met President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at the White House in March 2026.[1]

## The Washington summit

The meeting drew criticism after Trump invoked Japan's role in the 1941 attack on [[pearl-harbor|Pearl Harbor]] while defending his decision not to notify allies before recent U.S. military actions involving [[iran|Iran]]; Japanese officials' reaction was initially muted before turning uneasy, and critics called the reference historically insensitive.[1] Takaichi reiterated Japan's limitations on military involvement under its pacifist constitution while affirming continued economic and strategic cooperation with the United States, and joined discussions of energy security tied to global instability including the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]

The summit produced a large package of Japanese investment in American energy: roughly $40 billion toward small modular reactor development, tens of billions more across natural gas and infrastructure, and cooperation on critical minerals and rare earths intended to reduce reliance on [[china|China]].[1]

## Badlands portrayal

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized Takaichi as an enthusiastic Trump ally who "absolutely loves Trump," skipping the handshake for a hug on arrival and commenting admiringly on Barron Trump's looks.[1] He described her as "a pretty cool lady who gets along famously with The Donald," and said that rapport is why Trump felt comfortable making his Pearl Harbor remark — a relationship he compared to Trump's bond with the late [[shinzo-abe|Shinzo Abe]].[1]
