# Andy Frisella

Andy Frisella is a commentator whose video remarks on corporate [[wokeness|wokeness]] and consumer boycotts prompted [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] to publicly revise a previously published Badlands position.[1]

## The boycott inversion argument

Frisella's starting premise matched Carlson's own earlier essay "Get Woke, Go Broke": American corporations are forced into woke messaging because refusing it costs them their standing in large investment companies' portfolios and can cost them funding and financing outright.[1]

The part Carlson had not considered was Frisella's inversion of the conclusion. Where Carlson had argued that boycotts work — that refusing to buy from woke companies costs them money, with Budweiser and Target as the obvious examples — Frisella suggested that consumers are being coerced into boycotting and destroying the very companies that represent [[americana|Americana]] and American culture.[1] On this reading, the campaign is a [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist plot]] to strip away American culture and usher in a communist state that eliminates the middle class.[1]

## Carlson's response

Carlson accepted the argument as at least a possible part of the equation, listing Coca-Cola, Disney, Levi's, Hershey's, Budweiser and McDonald's as the brands that most signify Americana to him, and observing that all but perhaps McDonald's had gone out of their way to upset their customer base.[1] He speculated that McDonald's might be permitted to prosper "woke-free" precisely because it serves to make Americans sick and unhealthy.[1] Carlson stopped short of telling readers to abandon boycotts of companies pushing wokeness, Satanism or the grooming of children, but insisted the possibility of doing the Deep State's bidding must be weighed, and admitted he was "slightly embarrassed" not to have considered Frisella's theory himself.[1]
