# Andy Frisella

Andy Frisella is a commentator whose video clip prompted [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] to publicly revise his analysis of corporate "wokeness" and consumer boycotts in June 2023.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## The argument attributed to him

Frisella is described as stating essentially what Carlson had already written in his earlier article "Get Woke, Go Broke": that American corporations are forced to be "woke," because otherwise they lose their standing in the portfolios of large investment companies and can even lose their funding and financing.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

The part Carlson says he had not considered is Frisella's further claim that the public is being coerced into boycotting and destroying the very companies that represent America — the [[americana-brands|Americana brands]] tied to American culture.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) On this reading the boycotts are not spontaneous consumer punishment but an engineered step in a [[communist-takeover-of-america|Communist plot]] to remove American culture and usher in a communist state that eliminates the middle class.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Reception

Carlson accepted the theory as at least "a part of the equation" rather than the whole explanation, allowed that Frisella's take might be off-base, and said he was "slightly embarrassed" that the possibility had never occurred to him — treating it as a reminder that the easiest explanation is not necessarily the only factor in play.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) He stopped short of advising readers to abandon boycotts of [[budweiser|Budweiser]], [[target|Target]], [[disney|Disney]] or other companies pushing what he characterizes as wokeness, Satanism or the grooming of children, urging only that they consider they may be doing the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s bidding.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)
