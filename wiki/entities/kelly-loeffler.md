# Kelly Loeffler

Kelly Loeffler is a former U.S. Senator from Georgia who was President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s pick to serve as administrator of the [[small-business-administration|Small Business Administration]] in his second term.[1]

## Confirmation

By mid-February 2025, six of Trump's 22 Cabinet-level picks remained for the [[united-states-senate|U.S. Senate]] to confirm.[1] The Senate voted on Thursday, February 13, to invoke cloture on two nominees — Loeffler for the Small Business Administration and [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] for [[department-of-commerce|Commerce Secretary]] — leaving both poised for confirmation votes when the chamber reconvened after the Presidents Day holiday.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] criticized the 30-hour post-cloture debate requirement that slowed such votes as "fabricating power obstruction," arguing that the Senate writes its own rules and could shorten the delay, including through the [[nuclear-option|nuclear option]], and that Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] should cut the 30 hours to two.[1]

## SBA confirmation timing (February 2025)

Badlands coverage of the Senate calendar for Wednesday, February 19, 2025 reported that the chamber was expected to confirm Loeffler's nomination to lead the [[small-business-administration|Small Business Administration]] that day before adjourning for party lunches.[2] The vote came amid a crowded floor schedule that also included the confirmation of [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] as Commerce Secretary, procedural votes on [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s nomination to lead the [[fbi|FBI]], and the start of debate on a budget resolution.[2]
