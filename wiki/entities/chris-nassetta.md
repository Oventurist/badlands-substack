# Chris Nassetta

Chris Nassetta is the chief executive officer of [[hilton-worldwide|Hilton]].[1]

Nassetta led the company's response to the January 2026 controversy over a Hampton Inn by Hilton franchise in Lakeville, Minnesota, that allegedly refused accommodations to Department of Homeland Security and ICE agents.[1] Hilton under Nassetta investigated the matter, found the franchisee was not meeting brand standards and removed the property from its systems, drawing praise from shareholder [[bill-ackman|Bill Ackman]] for the speed of the action.[1]
