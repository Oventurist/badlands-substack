# Liberty Union Party

The Liberty Union Party is a minor Vermont political party that served as the vehicle for [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]]' entry into electoral politics in the 1970s.[1]

## Role in Bernie Sanders' early career

Sanders began running for office in Vermont in the 1970s while remaining largely unemployed, mounting two campaigns for the United States Senate as a Liberty Union candidate.[1] His campaign rhetoric during this period has been described as "downright apocalyptic and conspiratorial."[1] Announcing his 1974 Senate bid on the Liberty Union line, Sanders warned: "I have the very frightened feeling that if fundamental and radical change does not come about in the very near future that our nation, and, in fact, our entire civilization could soon be entering an economic dark age."[1]

The same year, while associated with the party, Sanders sent an open letter to President [[gerald-ford|Gerald Ford]] warning of a "virtual Rockefeller family dictatorship over the nation" should [[nelson-rockefeller|Nelson Rockefeller]] be confirmed as vice president, and called for the immediate disbanding of the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] in the wake of revelations about the agency's misconduct.[1] A 2023 Badlands Media essay by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited these two positions as among the most defensible actions of Sanders' career, contrasting them with his later accommodation of establishment interests.[1]

Both Liberty Union Senate bids failed, after which Sanders shifted to municipal politics and ran successfully for mayor of [[burlington-vermont|Burlington]] in 1980.[1]
