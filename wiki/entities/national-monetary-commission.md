# National Monetary Commission

The **National Monetary Commission** was the body established by Senator [[nelson-w-aldrich|Nelson W. Aldrich]] after the [[panic-of-1907|Panic of 1907]] to investigate a better economic system for the United States.[1]

The Commission concluded that the European model was better, including the Central Bank of England. Its reports and recommendations became one of the principal bases in the enactment of the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act of 1913]], which led to the creation of the modern [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve System]].[1]
