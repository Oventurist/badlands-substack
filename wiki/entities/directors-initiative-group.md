# Director's Initiative Group

The Director's Initiative Group (DIG) was a review body established under Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and since disbanded.[1]

## Whistleblower allegations

Unclassified memos reviewed by [[just-the-news|Just the News]] show that the DIG learned of whistleblower allegations during a broader review conducted in the prior year. The allegations included claims that intelligence officials downplayed or omitted information about Chinese election interference from final assessments, congressional briefings, and the President's Daily Brief.[1] The resulting material was referred to Intelligence Community Inspector General [[christopher-fox|Christopher Fox]] for further review and possible declassification.[1]

Memos generated in connection with the group's work allege that the [[national-intelligence-council|National Intelligence Council]] concluded in 2020 that [[china|China]] had accessed multiple state voter registration databases — with estimates ranging from 12 to 18 states — far beyond what had previously been disclosed publicly.[1] The memos also cite concerns involving [[venezuela|Venezuela]]-linked election infrastructure, and allege that some officials withheld, de-emphasized, or omitted foreign interference findings because they believed public disclosure could politically benefit President [[donald-trump|Trump]].[1]
