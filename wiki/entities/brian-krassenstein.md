# Brian Krassenstein

Brian Krassenstein is an American social media commentator known for high-volume political posting on Twitter. In May 2023 he became the interlocutor in an exchange with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] over the reputation of [[george-soros|George Soros]], after Musk stated that Soros "hates humanity."[1]

## Exchange over Soros

Krassenstein's post, to which Musk was responding, defended Soros by invoking his survival during the Second World War and attributing "good intentions" to him.[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], who described Krassenstein as a "notorious intellectual failure," argued that merely existing during the war does not establish moral standing, and pointed to a 60 Minutes interview in which Soros recounted having been tasked with confiscating property from and helping deport fellow Jews, saying it had no emotional impact on him and comparing the experience to his conduct in financial markets.[1] DeLarme further questioned what the claimed "good intentions" amounted to, citing Soros' profits from shorting the pound sterling — which he said crashed the British economy and earned over £1 billion — the financial ruin of other countries, and what he characterised as eagerness for a third world war.[1]
