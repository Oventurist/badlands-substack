# Elena Lasconi

Elena Lasconi is a Romanian politician of the progressive Save Romania Union party who advanced to the second round of Romania's 2024 presidential election.[1]

## 2024 presidential election

Lasconi, described in coverage as a reformist, was set to face the sovereigntist candidate [[calin-georgescu|Călin Georgescu]] in a runoff scheduled for December 8, 2024, after Georgescu's surprise first-round victory.[1] Georgescu's win also displaced incumbent Prime Minister [[marcel-ciolacu|Marcel Ciolacu]] of the Social Democratic Party, leaving the ruling party without a candidate in the runoff for the first time in Romania's 35-year post-communist history.[1]

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated the runoff as a flashpoint in a wider confrontation between entrenched globalist power and popular sovereigntist movements, noting Romanian officials' subsequent demands for an election "redo" on the basis of alleged Russian interference.[1]
