# Robert Zoellick

Robert Zoellick is a former United States Trade Representative, having served under President George W. Bush.[1]

## 2024 endorsement of Kamala Harris

Zoellick was listed among the signatories of the September 2024 letter in which more than 100 Republican former national security figures endorsed Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], praising her championing of "the rule of law, democracy, and our constitutional principles" while faulting [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s national security decision-making.[1] See [[2024-republican-national-security-endorsement-of-harris|2024 Republican National Security Endorsement of Kamala Harris]].
