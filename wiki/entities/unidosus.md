# UnidosUS

UnidosUS is an American Latino civil rights and advocacy organization whose annual conference draws large numbers of Latino advocates and community leaders.[1]

## 2024 Annual Conference

The group's 2024 Annual Conference was held in Las Vegas, Nevada, and drew more than 1,500 Latino advocates and leaders in what was a crucial swing state for the 2024 presidential race.[1] President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was scheduled to address the conference on Wednesday, July 17, 2024, as he sought to rally supporters amid calls from Democrats in Congress for him to withdraw from the campaign.[1]

Biden abruptly cancelled the appearance after testing positive for COVID-19.[1] The organization's chief executive, [[janet-murguia|Janet Murguía]], announced the cancellation from the stage, telling attendees she had just spoken with Biden by phone and that he had shared his "deep disappointment" at being unable to attend.[1] The announcement was met with boos from the audience.[1] Murguía said Biden did not want to put "anybody at risk" and planned to address the conference at a later date, adding, "we've seen how COVID has still impacted many in our community still."[1]
