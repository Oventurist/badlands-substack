# Anatoly Antonov

Anatoly Antonov is a Russian diplomat who served as Russia's ambassador to the United States and who in September 2024 warned publicly that the United States would be unable to insulate itself from a nuclear conflict.[1]

## Nuclear war warning (September 2024)

Speaking to the Rossiya 24 channel, Antonov said he was surprised at the "illusion" that "if there is a conflict, it will not spread to the territory of the United States of America."[1] He added: "I am constantly trying to convey to them one thesis that the Americans will not be able to sit it out behind the waters of this ocean. This war will affect everyone, so we constantly say – do not play with this rhetoric."[1]

The remarks came as fears of escalation between Russia and [[nato|NATO]] over Ukraine intensified, with Western powers reportedly weighing whether to allow Kiev to conduct missile strikes deep inside Russian territory.[1]

## Criticism of US nuclear modelling

Antonov also observed that while Western countries accuse Russia of "sabre-rattling," the United States was itself commissioning research into the consequences of a nuclear strike on Eastern Europe.[1] He was apparently referring to a study ordered by the US Defense Department to simulate the impact of a nuclear conflict on global agriculture; according to a solicitation notice posted on a government procurement platform, the study focused on regions "beyond Eastern Europe and Western Russia," which in the simulation is the epicenter of the hypothetical nuclear attack.[1]
