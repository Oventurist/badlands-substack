# John Cleese

John Cleese is a British comedian and Monty Python member who, in May 2023, publicly refused demands that a scene be cut from the troupe's film [[monty-pythons-life-of-brian|The Life of Brian]], becoming a focal point in the wider argument over [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] and comedy.[1]

## The Life of Brian dispute

Legal commentator [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] framed the episode as the moment activists "met their match in a legend of comedy," noting that Cleese opposed cutting a scene in which the character Stan announces that he wants to become a woman named Loretta and have babies.[1] Activists objected that the scene mocked transgender people and demanded its removal; the sequence contains Stan's lines, "I want to be a woman… It's my right as a man. I want to have babies… It's every man's right to have babies if he wants them," followed by the retort "Don't you oppress me!" after Cleese's character protests.[1] Turley observed that the objection was not to the film's abundant jokes about Italians, Christians and Jews, but specifically to the gender-identity scene.[1]

## Framing in Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated Cleese's refusal as an act of heroism, arguing that tyranny depends on the abandonment of principled stances in favor of compliance with an ideologically dominant authority.[1] Deschamps quoted Jordan Peterson's line, "You know you're living in a tyranny because everyone is lying all the time," and argued that Cleese had "in epic synchronistic fashion" encapsulated the modern gender debate in a film made more than forty years earlier.[1] He grouped Cleese with J.K. Rowling as figures "refusing to bend the knee to the corporate-sponsored woke mob," predicting continued attacks on him and urging readers to speak against untruths in their own relationships, homes, communities, jobs, government, schools and churches.[1]
