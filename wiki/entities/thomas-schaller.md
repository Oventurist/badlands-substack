# Thomas Schaller

**Thomas Schaller** is a political science professor at the University of Maryland and co-author, with op-ed writer [[paul-waldman|Paul Waldman]], of the 2024 book *[[white-rural-rage|White Rural Rage: The Threat to American Democracy]]*.[1]

## MSNBC appearance

On Thursday, February 29, 2024, Schaller appeared with Waldman in the [[msnbc|MSNBC]] "Morning Joe" studio to promote the book, in a segment widely criticized as overtly racist toward rural white Americans.[1] Schaller said of the group: "They are the most racist, xenophobic, anti-immigrant, anti-gay, geodemographic group in the country," and added, "Second, they're the most conspiracist group. QAnon support and subscribers, election denialism, COVID denialism instead of scientific skepticism, Obama birtherism."[1] He also asserted that rural white Americans "don't believe in an independent press, free speech."[1]

Critics noted the segment applied sweeping characterizations to an entire demographic defined by race and geography, and observed that comparable generalizations about black urban residents would not be tolerated on mainstream television.[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] presented the appearance as an early example of the media hostility rural voters should expect in the months before the November 2024 election.[1]

## Reception

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] pointed to the heavy negative ratio Schaller received on his own X post announcing the book as a measure of the operation's ineffectiveness, arguing that such books mainly serve to generate short-lived news cycles that ultimately damage their authors.[1]
