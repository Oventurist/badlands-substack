# OceanGate

OceanGate is the company that operated the Titanic exploration submersible which lost contact with its surface support vessel in June 2023, touching off an international search.[1]

## Loss of contact and the Starlink claim

The submersible lost communication with its mothership on Sunday, June 18, 2023.[1] On June 20, the fact-checking site [[snopes|Snopes]] published an article asking whether the missing submersible had been using satellites belonging to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s company, implying that a [[starlink|Starlink]] failure was responsible for the loss of contact.[1]

A Twitter community note corrected the framing, observing that although OceanGate had used Starlink services, those services supported the company's surface vessels, not its submersibles, and that radio frequencies such as Starlink's do not work underwater.[1] Snopes subsequently downgraded its rating from its original assessment to "Unproven" and then to "False," acknowledging in an editor's note that while OceanGate said it was using Starlink satellites for the expedition, the submersible itself did not use the technology to communicate, and that the extent of the mothership's use of Starlink remained unknown.[1]

## US Coast Guard findings and whistleblower account (2025)

A report from the [[us-coast-guard|US Coast Guard]] published in August 2025 found that OceanGate's failures over safety, testing and maintenance were the main cause of the Titan disaster.[2] [[david-lochridge|David Lochridge]], sacked by the firm in 2018 after warning about safety issues, told the BBC he "just knew if they kept carrying on the way they were going and with that deficient equipment, then there would be an incident," and concluded that "people were sold a lie."[2] The submersible imploded in June 2023, killing all five people aboard including chief executive [[stockton-rush|Stockton Rush]].[2] See [[titan-submersible-implosion|the Titan submersible implosion]].[2]
