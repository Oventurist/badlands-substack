# G. Edward Griffin

**G. Edward Griffin** is the author of *The Creature from Jekyll Island*, a widely circulated critical history of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], cited in Ryan DeLarme's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 6" on the relationship between the houses of Morgan and Rothschild.[1]

## On Morgan and the Rothschilds

DeLarme raises the case of [[jp-morgan|John Pierpont Morgan]], described as one of the primary non-Jewish agents enlisted as a front for the "Rothschild organization," who made anti-Semitic remarks during the American Civil War while purportedly furthering [[rothschild-family|Rothschild]] goals in the United States.[1] Griffin is quoted at length on the apparent contradiction: "How much of Morgan's apparent anti-Semitism was real and how much may have been a pragmatic guise is, in the final analysis, of little importance … Regardless of one's interpretation of the nature of the relationship between the Houses of Morgan and Rothschild, the fact remains that it was close, it was ongoing, and it was profitable to both. If Morgan truly did harbor feelings of anti-Semitism, neither he nor the Rothschilds ever allowed them to get in the way of their business."[1]
