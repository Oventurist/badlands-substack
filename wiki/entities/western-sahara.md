# Western Sahara

Western Sahara is a disputed desert territory in North Africa, classified by the United Nations as a non-self-governing territory and claimed by [[morocco|Morocco]].[1]

## Dispute

Morocco annexed the territory shortly after Spain gave up control in 1975 and de facto controls around 80% of it, a claim that has fueled regional tension and a decades-long conflict with the Algerian-backed [[polisario-front|Polisario Front]].[1] The Polisario Front supports a self-determination referendum first proposed by the UN in 1991 as part of a ceasefire agreement, which has since stalled.[1]

## International positions

In April 2025 Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] told Moroccan Foreign Minister [[nasser-bourita|Nasser Bourita]] that Washington recognizes Moroccan sovereignty over Western Sahara and backs Rabat's autonomy plan as the only basis for a solution.[1] The United States had affirmed that position previously, [[israel|Israel]] followed in 2023, and France reversed years of neutrality to endorse the plan, with Emmanuel Macron calling it the only viable route to resolution.[1]

## Badlands framing

Badlands commentary suggested Western Sahara was itself "likely carved out by the imperial globalists generations ago," and proposed watching leaders such as [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traore]] of neighboring [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] to gauge which side genuinely represents anti-imperial interests.[1]
