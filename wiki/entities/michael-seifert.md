# Michael Seifert

Michael Seifert is the chief executive officer of [[public-square|Public Square]], the online directory of pro-freedom businesses that trades on the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] under the ticker $PSQH.[1]

## Public listing of Public Square

Seifert stood at the NYSE podium alongside [[donald-trump-jr|Donald Trump Jr.]] and [[kim-guilfoyle|Kim Guilfoyle]] when the company rang the opening bell on Thursday, July 20, 2023, amid chants of "USA!" from the exchange floor.[1] Announcing the listing, he said: "The largest network of non-woke businesses in America is officially public $PSQH."[1] At that point the platform reported more than 1.1 million consumer members and over 55,000 businesses, 90 percent of them small businesses, with free accounts for buyers and sellers alike.[1]
