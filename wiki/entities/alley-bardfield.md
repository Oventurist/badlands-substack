# Alley Bardfield

Alley Bardfield is an Illinois substitute teacher, aged 34 at the time of the allegations, accused in August 2025 of sexually abusing an 11-year-old male student during "play dates" at her home in Mount Zion, Illinois.[1]

## Allegations

According to a police statement cited by the New York Post, Bardfield exchanged messages and explicit photographs with the sixth-grade boy over Snapchat and sent him hundreds of dollars through CashApp over the course of several months.[1] The victim's mother reportedly discovered $700 in CashApp payments after checking her son's phone, having noticed a change in his behaviour following a stayover at the teacher's house.[1] In a police interview, Bardfield claimed the child had made sexual advances toward her first.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that, if convicted after due process, Bardfield should face capital punishment, noting that teachers are legally "trusted persons" and mandatory reporters whom society trains children to trust.[1] She also criticized the New York Post's framing, insisting that the only appropriate description of an adult teacher's conduct with an eleven-year-old is rape, and that the same case with genders reversed would have been reported that way universally.[1]
