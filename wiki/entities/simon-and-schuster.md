# Simon & Schuster

Simon & Schuster is a major American book publisher and, at the time of the events described here, a subsidiary of [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]].[1] The company published the work of journalist [[bob-woodward|Bob Woodward]], including the audiobook release "The Trump Tapes," which collected audio recordings of Woodward's interviews with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Role in the Woodward audiobook litigation

In late January 2023, Trump sued Woodward, Simon & Schuster and Paramount Global for at least $49,980,000, alleging that he had never consented to the sale of the audio of his interviews to the public.[1] The damages figure was derived from an estimate that "The Trump Tapes" audiobook had sold more than two million copies at $24.99 each.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized the arrangement as one in which Woodward and his publishing partners recorded Trump for the stated purpose of writing a book and then "doctored and sold the recordings," arguing that the words and their context had been altered.[1] Paul further contended that the most significant aspect of the suit concerned material omitted from the released recordings — in his account, a conversation amounting to a refutation of the premise of the first Trump impeachment over Ukraine.[1]

The suit placed Simon & Schuster, as publisher, and Paramount Global, as corporate parent, alongside Woodward as defendants, framing the dispute as one over the commercial exploitation of interview tapes rather than over the contents of the printed book.[1]
