# Daleep Singh

Daleep Singh served as deputy to National Security Adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] and was identified as one of the officials working behind the scenes on a proposal for a United States sovereign wealth fund during the final months of the Biden administration's term.[1]

## Sovereign wealth fund proposal

Bloomberg reported in September 2024 that aides to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had been crafting a proposal to create a [[sovereign-wealth-fund|sovereign wealth fund]] that would allow the United States to invest in national security interests including technology, energy, and critical links in the supply chain.[1] The reporting attributed the "behind-the-scenes work" to Sullivan and Singh, and noted that it mirrored, at least in spirit, a proposal floated by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] one day earlier in a speech to the Economic Club of New York.[1]

## Commentary

The Badlands brief characterized the timing as suspicious, arguing that the administration's plan surfaced only after Trump publicly proposed the idea, and that Bloomberg's framing of the work as having been underway "for months" was intended to blunt the appearance of imitation.[1] The item was presented as part of a pattern in which the Biden–[[kamala-harris|Harris]] administration adopted Trump policy ideas, following the elimination of taxes on tips and measures to stimulate new business creation.[1]
