# Interfax

Interfax is a Russian news agency whose reporting is widely used as a primary source for statements by [[russia|Russian]] government and defense officials.[1]

In May 2026, Interfax reported that Russia and [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] had signed a military-technical cooperation agreement on May 27 at an international security forum in the Moscow region, following a meeting between Russian Security Council Secretary [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]] and Afghan Defense Minister [[mohammad-yaqub|Mohammad Yaqub]].[1] The agency also carried Shoigu's May 14 remarks that Moscow had built a dialogue with the [[taliban|Taliban]] and was developing a full partnership with Kabul, as well as his call for Western states to unfreeze Afghan assets.[1]
