# Center on Narrative, Disinformation & Strategic Influence

The Center on Narrative, Disinformation & Strategic Influence is a research center housed at [[arizona-state-university|Arizona State University]].[1]

[[mike-benz|Mike Benz]], a former [[state-department|State Department]] cybersecurity official turned educator and whistleblower, has claimed that the center acts as one of many [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] cut-outs.[1] According to Benz, its activities include creating databases of pro-Trump social media content and developing "intelligence dashboards" that feed censorship policies.[1]

The center is presented as a representative example of how the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship industrial complex]] uses academic institutions to develop and legitimize censorship techniques while insulating the sponsoring agencies from scrutiny.[1] Its host university's president, [[michael-crow|Michael Crow]], is also chairman of the CIA venture arm [[in-q-tel|In-Q-Tel]].[1]
