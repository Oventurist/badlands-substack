# Alicia Menendez

Alicia Menendez is an American television host and anchor for [[msnbc|MSNBC]], where she conducts interviews with legal and political analysts on the network's weekend and daytime programming.[1]

## August 2023 interview on Trump's Georgia surrender

On August 21, 2023, Menendez hosted former Watergate prosecutor [[jill-wine-banks|Jill Wine-Banks]] and asked how the process of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] surrendering at the [[fulton-county-jail|Fulton County Jail]] in Georgia would differ from the previous indictments he had faced.[1] Wine-Banks answered that the Georgia booking would involve mug shots and fingerprints at a county jail rather than a federal building, describing the facility as "a really dirty, dangerous, scary place" — remarks that drew criticism from independent outlets and from commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], who read the exchange as relish rather than reporting.[1]
