# ABC

ABC is an American broadcast television network named by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in his call for the revocation of the broadcast licenses of networks that declined to carry his primetime [[white-house|White House]] address on election security.

## The 2026 broadcast license dispute

As reported in the Badlands Brief, ABC and [[nbc|NBC]] did not interrupt their primary broadcast schedules to carry the address live, though both streamed it on their digital news platforms. [[cnn|CNN]] also declined to air the speech on its main channel, while [[cbs|CBS]] and [[fox-news|Fox News]] carried it live. Trump criticized ABC and NBC by name and alleged that outlets refusing to carry the address were part of a "plot." The following day, [[federal-communications-commission|FCC]] Commissioner [[anna-gomez|Anna Gomez]] said the proposal was unconstitutional and that broadcasters are not subject to FCC penalties for declining to air a presidential speech; legal experts cited by Reuters noted that networks retain [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] discretion over programming and that revoking a license would require a formal administrative process. Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the episode as evidence of the subversive role he ascribes to the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]].
