# Marianna Budanova

Marianna Budanova is the wife of Lt. Gen. [[kyrylo-budanov|Kyrylo Budanov]], head of the [[main-directorate-of-intelligence|Main Directorate of Intelligence]] (GUR/DIU) of Ukraine's Ministry of Defense. In late November 2023 she was hospitalized after an apparent poisoning that Ukrainian officials and media treated as a suspected assassination attempt.[1]

## 2023 poisoning

Budanova fell ill after what Ukrainian media described as a "prolonged" period of not feeling well, and testing found heavy metals of unknown origin in her system. Ukrainska Pravda cited official sources saying she was "most likely poisoned through food."[1]

GUR spokesperson [[andriy-yusov|Andriy Yusov]] confirmed the reports to Reuters, saying, "Yes, I can confirm the information, unfortunately, it is true," without clarifying when the poisoning occurred. The BBC's Ukrainian service cited Yusov as saying several other GUR officials had experienced milder poisoning symptoms, suggesting a broader plot.[1] Ukrainian sources confirmed heavy metal poisoning through laboratory tests but did not identify a specific substance.[1]

The incident drew attention because General Budanov himself had reportedly survived roughly ten prior assassination attempts while directing major Ukrainian intelligence operations against Russian forces.[1]
