# Department of Justice Office of Inspector General

The Department of Justice Office of Inspector General (DOJ OIG) is the internal watchdog of the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], with jurisdiction over investigations of misconduct within the department and its components, including the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]].[1]

## Role in the 2026 FBI records disclosure

In March 2026 the DOJ OIG emerged as a gatekeeper in the dispute over a cache of up to 1.9 million pages of FBI records stored in a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility at bureau headquarters. Officials stated that many of the legacy records in the cache fall under the authority of the Office of Inspector General and must be reviewed in consultation with that office before any release can occur.[1]

The OIG consultation requirement was one of several procedural conditions — alongside digitization, classification review, and coordination with other federal agencies — that the FBI cited in projecting a 10-to-12-month timeline before it could determine which materials were responsive to a Freedom of Information Act request.[1]
