# Khalil al-Hayya

Khalil al-Hayya is a senior [[hamas|Hamas]] official who in March 2025 led the Hamas delegation in unprecedented direct talks with the United States.[1]

## Doha talks with the United States (March 2025)

Al-Hayya headed the senior Hamas delegation that met in Doha with US hostage envoy [[adam-boehler|Adam Boehler]] to discuss the potential release of American hostages held in Gaza.[1] The meeting was the first known direct engagement of its kind and was held without advance notification to Israel, which had opposed such contacts when the Trump administration first raised the idea the previous month.[1] Discussions reportedly touched on the number of Palestinian prisoners Israel would release in exchange for the five Israeli-American hostages still in Gaza, one of whom was alive and four believed dead.[1]

News of the al-Hayya–Boehler meeting was leaked to the media hours after a tense phone call between Boehler and Israeli Strategic Affairs Minister [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]]; US officials believed Israel was behind the leak, which further decreased trust between Washington and Jerusalem.[1]

## 2025 Israeli strike on Doha

During the [[2025-israeli-strike-on-doha|Israeli strike on Doha]] in September 2025, roughly 15 Israeli warplanes fired at least ten missiles at a [[hamas|Hamas]] compound in the Qatari capital, reportedly killing several Hamas members including al-Hayya's son.[2] Hamas said its top leadership survived the attack, which it characterized as an attempt to assassinate negotiators working on a potential settlement.[2]
