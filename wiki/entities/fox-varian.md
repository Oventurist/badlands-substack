# Fox Varian

**Fox Varian** is the plaintiff in a United States medical malpractice lawsuit arising from a double mastectomy performed on her at the age of 16, who later said she was compelled to undergo the surgery while still a minor.[1] A jury found both a psychologist and a surgeon liable for malpractice, awarding Varian $1.6 million for pain and suffering and $400,000 for future medical costs.[1]

## Reaction

The verdict prompted [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] to state on X that U.S. doctors who perform gender transition procedures on minors could face "thousands" of lawsuits, equating such doctors to "modern day Mengeles."[1] Musk also criticized schools, mental-health professionals and state officials for their roles in facilitating the procedures.[1] Reporting on the case noted that the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] has rolled back federal support for gender transition procedures for individuals under 19, and that several states have moved to restrict or ban such treatments for minors.[1]
