# John Konrad

**Captain John Konrad** is a mariner and the chief executive officer of [[gcaptain|gCaptain]], a website specializing in tracking the shipping industry.[1]

Konrad became a widely cited source during the aftermath of the [[francis-scott-key-bridge-collapse|Francis Scott Key Bridge collapse]] in Baltimore, when he stated that the 984-foot Singapore-flagged container ship [[dali|Dali]] was apparently "sitting atop a high-pressure underwater gas line."[1] Writing on the social media platform X, he reported that "Sources at ICS reports ship salvage effort will likely be delayed while line is surveyed and additional risk can be assessed."[1] His assessment complicated official expectations that the federally funded $60 million salvage effort would proceed smoothly.[1]

During the [[may-2024-g5-geomagnetic-storm|May 2024 G5 geomagnetic storm]], Konrad — identified as chief executive of Captain — again became a cited voice on infrastructure disruption, writing on X that "The solar storm has knocked out almost all long-distance shortwave radio."[2]
