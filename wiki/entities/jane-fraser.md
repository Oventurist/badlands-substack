# Jane Fraser

Jane Fraser is the chief executive officer of Citigroup, the American multinational bank. She became the first woman to lead a major Wall Street bank when she took the role in 2021, and has overseen a multi-year simplification of Citi's global operations.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Fraser was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. Her inclusion placed Citigroup in a delegation of American banking and financial leadership — alongside [[stephen-schwarzman|Stephen Schwarzman]] of Blackstone, [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]] of [[blackrock|BlackRock]] and [[david-solomon|David Solomon]] of [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] — traveling with the president ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
