# Jennifer Vasquez Sura

Jennifer Vasquez Sura is the wife of [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego García]], the Maryland resident deported by the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] to [[el-salvador|El Salvador]]'s [[cecot|CECOT]] megaprison in March 2025. She became the most visible public advocate for his return, appearing in media coverage and raising more than $200,000 in donations in the days after the case became a national story.[1]

## 2020–2021 protective order allegations

Court documents surfaced in April 2025 showed that Vasquez Sura had applied for a protective order against her husband in 2021, alleging that he punched, scratched and grabbed her, in some instances leaving her bruised and bleeding.[1] In a handwritten petition she described an incident on 4 May 2021 that occurred in front of their then-infant child: she wrote that when she declined to turn off her laptop he became angry, shut and threw the laptop to the floor, and after she pushed him away he punched and scratched her left eye, leaving her bleeding.[1] She further alleged that he ripped her shorts and shirt during a later argument the same day and stated that she was "afraid to be close to him."[1]

The petition also described earlier incidents in 2020, including an alleged assault in November in which he struck her with a boot and one in August that left her with a "purple eye."[1] Commentary in the Badlands brief noted that two protective orders — in 2020 and 2021 — had been sought, and contrasted those filings with her subsequent public campaign for his release.[1]
