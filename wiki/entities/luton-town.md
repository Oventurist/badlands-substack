# Luton Town

Luton Town is an English professional soccer club that competed in the [[premier-league|Premier League]] during the 2023–24 season after winning promotion through the Championship Play-Off Final in May 2023.[1] The club drew international attention in December 2023 when its captain collapsed during a top-flight match.[1]

## Lockyer incidents

Club captain [[tom-lockyer|Tom Lockyer]] collapsed during the May 2023 Championship Play-Off Final, an episode attributed at the time to atrial fibrillation.[1] He underwent an operation in June 2023 and was given the "all clear."[1] On December 16, 2023, Lockyer collapsed again in the 59th minute of Luton Town's Premier League match against Bournemouth; the club stated that he had suffered a cardiac arrest, and the fixture was abandoned.[1] Paramedics treated him on the pitch for approximately ten minutes before he was carried off, and he remained in hospital undergoing tests with the cause described as a "mystery."[1] The club made no confirmation that the two incidents were related.[1]
