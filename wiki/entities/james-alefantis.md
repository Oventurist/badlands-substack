# James Alefantis

James Alefantis is the owner of Comet Ping Pong, a pizza restaurant in Washington, D.C. that became the centerpiece of the [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]] controversy.[1]

## Connections described by Badlands Media

Badlands Media notes that Alefantis was for a long period the boyfriend of [[david-brock|David Brock]], the founder of [[media-matters-for-america|Media Matters for America]], the organization identified in the article as a lead attack dog against [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1] The article presents this relationship as one of the links binding together media-monitoring organizations, the Pizzagate allegations, and what the author calls the [[deep-state|Deep State]] Cabal.[1]

Comet Ping Pong is described as the restaurant at the center of Pizzagate — a story alleging child trafficking and abuse said to have been covered up by media organizations.[1] The article adds the observation that the Standard Hotel in Los Angeles also contains a ping pong-themed bar, and invites readers to research the names [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]], Ed Buck and Rachel Chandler in that connection.[1]

## Pizzagate origins

The Pizzagate story is traced in the article to emails belonging to [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] that were published by [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] under [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]], which critics argued contained trafficking code words such as "hot dogs," "walnut sauce" and "cheese pizza."[1]
