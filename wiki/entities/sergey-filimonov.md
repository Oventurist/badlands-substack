# Sergey Filimonov

Sergey Filimonov is a Ukrainian military officer who commands the elite 108th Separate Assault Battalion, a unit nicknamed the "Da Vinci Wolves."[1] In November 2025 he became a prominent voice warning publicly about the scale of desertion inside the Ukrainian armed forces.[1]

## Warnings on desertion

In a post on X published on Wednesday, November 5, 2025, Filimonov asked his subscribers how surprised they would be to learn that another newly formed brigade was being assembled from the ranks of the 150th — a formation that, while still at the stage of formation, already had roughly 3,000 personnel absent without leave.[1] His remarks were reported by [[rt|RT]] and framed as confirmation from a serving commander of a systemic manpower crisis.[1]

Filimonov's warning appeared alongside reporting by Telegraph contributor Owen Matthews, who estimated that as many as 20,000 Ukrainian service members desert or go absent every four weeks, that Ukrainian authorities had launched some 290,000 criminal cases for desertion since February 2022, and that Kiev's military might be 200,000 soldiers short of the minimum needed to hold back Russian advances.[1] Officers cited in that reporting described frontline units operating at half or even a third of required strength, and nearly 100,000 young men were reported to have left Ukraine after the government allowed men aged 18 to 22 to cross the border in August 2025.[1] Together these accounts define the contours of the [[ukrainian-mobilization-crisis|Ukrainian mobilization crisis]].[1]
