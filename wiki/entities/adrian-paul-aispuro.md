# Adrian Paul Aispuro

Adrian Paul Aispuro is the man identified by the [[lapd|Los Angeles Police Department]] as the armed suspect taken into custody at a Los Angeles campaign event for Democratic presidential candidate [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] on Friday, September 15, 2023. Aispuro, 44 at the time, was booked on felony charges and held on $35,000 bail.[1]

## The incident

According to a post by Kennedy on X, formerly known as Twitter, Aispuro allegedly wore shoulder holsters containing loaded pistols along with spare ammunition magazines, and carried a U.S. Marshal badge on a lanyard together with a belt-clip federal identification.[1] Kennedy said the man attempted to approach him and claimed to be a member of his security detail; the candidate's private security personnel detained the suspect until LAPD officers arrived.[1]

## Significance

The arrest occurred against the backdrop of Kennedy's repeated and unsuccessful requests to the [[white-house|White House]] for [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] protection, which he renewed the same day.[1] Badlands Media commentary treated the episode as evidence of the establishment's disregard for Kennedy's physical safety, and criticized attempts to attribute threats against him to the MAGA, America First and Q movements.[1]
