# Rupert Murdoch

Rupert Murdoch is the Australian-American media proprietor who serves as chairman of Fox Corp, the parent company of [[fox-news|Fox News]].[1] In April 2023, at the age of 92, he was among the network figures who would have been exposed to testimony at trial in the defamation suit brought by [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]]; the [[fox-dominion-defamation-settlement|$787.5 million settlement]] announced on April 18, 2023 spared him and on-air hosts [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]] and [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]] from being called to the witness stand and subjected to potentially withering questioning.[1]

Murdoch's centrality to the case reflects his position atop one of the world's top media companies, whose coverage of false vote-rigging claims about the 2020 U.S. election was the subject of the litigation.[1]

## The Carlson January 6 footage episode

In March 2023, after [[tucker-carlson]] aired an explosive Monday broadcast drawing on 42,000 hours of Capitol surveillance video and drew condemnation from Senate leaders [[chuck-schumer]] and [[mitch-mcconnell]], Carlson's Tuesday program was notably subdued and contained no further revelations.[2] Reporting by [[the-gateway-pundit]] suggested that Murdoch and the Fox News executive leadership had intervened to change the show's plan under pressure from the regime.[2]

Commentary published in the [[badlands-news-brief]] was scathing toward Murdoch, recalling that he had claimed [[donald-trump]] was losing his mind after the 2020 election and had publicly chastised his own employees for reporting on election fraud, and argued that media proprietors of his class treat party affiliation and political philosophy as markets to be cornered rather than causes to be served.[2]

## Stepping down, September 2023

In September 2023 it was announced that Murdoch was stepping down and handing over power to his son, [[lachlan-murdoch|Lachlan Murdoch]].[3] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the succession news broke the same day as the report that [[fox-business|Fox Business]] had aired a misleading graphic about a Trump–Biden poll, and characterized the heir as an "even-more-communist son."[3] The commentary argued that "misleading the audience is critical to manufacturing consent, and manufacturing consent is Fox's prime directive these days," and that the network had been in decline since the 2017 death of [[roger-ailes|Roger Ailes]].[3]

## Dominion litigation and the Carlson exit (2023)

Ahead of Fox's defamation trial, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — the owner and chairman of Fox Corp. being set to testify — had urged Murdoch to embrace his theories about voter fraud in a bid to win the [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] case in court.[4] Fox instead settled, a choice Trump publicly criticized days later while reacting to [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s ouster from [[fox-news|Fox News]], saying, "I was surprised they made a settlement in that case. I thought that was a case that should easily be won."[4]
