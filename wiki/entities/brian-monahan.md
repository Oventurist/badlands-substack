# Brian Monahan

Brian Monahan is the attending physician for the United States Congress, the in-house doctor responsible for the medical care of members of the House and Senate.[1]

## Role in the McConnell freezing episodes

Monahan came to broader public attention in September 2023 when he issued a letter clearing Senate Minority Leader [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] following a widely publicized episode in which McConnell froze while answering questions from reporters on August 30, 2023.[1] In the letter, released on Tuesday, September 5, 2023, Monahan wrote that his examination of McConnell "included several medical evaluations: brain MRI imaging, EEG study and consultations with several neurologists for a comprehensive neurology assessment," and concluded that there was "no evidence that you have a seizure disorder or that you experienced a stroke, TIA or movement disorder such as Parkinson's disease."[1] Monahan recommended no changes to McConnell's treatment protocols as the senator continued his recovery from a fall suffered in March 2023.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary treated the letter with skepticism, noting that four neurologists had signed off on McConnell's mental state and asking "who is paying these doctors," and observing that the assessment conspicuously did not address whether McConnell suffers from dementia.[1] The commentary framed McConnell as one of several ageing and allegedly incapacitated officials in Washington and argued for congressional term limits and independent cognitive testing as a prerequisite for elected office.[1]
