# Syed Abedin

Syed Abedin was an Indian-born Muslim academic, the father of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], and a co-founder of the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]] (JMMA) in [[jeddah|Jeddah]], Saudi Arabia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Career and the Journal

Abedin and his wife Saleha, both Muslim academics of Pakistani and Indian background, moved from Kalamazoo, Michigan to Jeddah when their daughter Huma was two years old, taking positions at Saudi Arabian universities.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) There Syed helped start an international journal devoted to the Muslim diaspora in non-Muslim lands, the JMMA.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The journal was published by the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]], an entity established by the Saudi financier [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]], who personally recruited the Abedin parents to run it in the late 1970s.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Naseef remained closely involved with the JMMA for decades, being removed from the masthead only in 2003, around the time he was named a defendant in the civil suit brought by victims of the September 11 attacks.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Legacy

In the Badlands account, the journal is characterized as an international propaganda vehicle for the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]], producing material used in universities worldwide to promote sharia acceptance and a Muslim-supremacist perspective as part of the [[civilization-stealth-jihad|civilization/stealth jihad]] strategy.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) After Syed Abedin's death, his widow took over the journal's operations, and Huma Abedin served as its assistant editor for roughly twelve years, from 1996 until she joined the [[state-department|State Department]] in 2009.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
