# Office of Strategic Services

The Office of Strategic Services (OSS) was the United States' wartime intelligence agency during World War II and the precursor to the [[cia|CIA]], established in 1942 and dissolved in 1945. In the Badlands Media article A More Perfect (Labor) Union, the OSS is identified as the origin point of the U.S. practice of collaborating with American labor unions for covert political purposes.

## Role in labor operations

The article states that "the exploitation of labor for political ends began well before the AFL-CIO even existed," tracing the practice back to the closing days of World War II, "when the U.S. Office of Strategic Services (OSS), the CIA's precursor, began collaborating with American unions to counter emerging communist influences in Europe." This early collaboration set the pattern that would later be institutionalized in the CIA's "AFL-CIA" partnership with the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]].
