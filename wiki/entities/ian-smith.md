# Ian Smith

Ian Smith is an American gym owner who became a national figure during the COVID-19 pandemic for defying New Jersey's lockdown restrictions by keeping his business open. He is the co-owner, with Frank Trumbetti, of Atilis Gym in Bellmawr, New Jersey.[1]

## Charges dropped

In a statement posted to X on May 18, 2024, Smith announced that the more than 80 charges filed against him and Trumbetti had been dropped with prejudice, meaning they could not be revisited or refiled.[1] The charges levied by the state included violations of a governor's order, public nuisance, disturbing the peace, and operating without a license.[1]

"The support we received locally, nationally, and internationally for our stand is something I will be forever grateful for," Smith said, adding that he was "thrilled to announce that we have achieved a major victory in the long, hard fight against the State."[1]

## Aftermath and criticism of the state

Smith framed the dismissal as an opening rather than an endpoint, saying the "victory opens the battlefield again and gives us options to continue to push back and bring justice to the treasonous actions of [[phil-murphy|Phil Murphy]] and his lackies [sic]," a reference to the New Jersey governor.[1] He also thanked his "fearless attorneys," noting that "some of the most high profile attorneys around the country ran from our case—knowing it would be a long, hard road and would make them a target of the state."[1]
