# Volodymyr Kudrytskyi

Volodymyr Kudrytskyi was the chief executive officer of [[ukrenergo|Ukrenergo]], Ukraine's power grid operator, until his removal by the company's supervisory board in early September 2024.[1]

His ouster followed Russia's August 26, 2024 drone and missile attack — the most massive of the full-scale invasion to that point — which targeted Ukraine's energy infrastructure and caused widespread electricity disruptions.[1] At a meeting of the Supreme Commander-in-Chief's Staff convened over the resulting crisis, President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] instructed Kudrytskyi to submit his resignation.[1] The board cited incomplete protective structures around Ukrenergo power facilities that allegedly failed to prevent blackouts, and four out of six supervisory board members voted in favor of his dismissal.[1]

The Badlands News Brief placed the removal within a broader pattern of Zelenskyy-era purges, alongside the arrest of [[igor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]], an anti-corruption probe that ensnared three Ukrainian Supreme Court justices and the chief justice, and actions against top military commanders.[1]
