# Chabad-Lubavitch

Chabad-Lubavitch is an Orthodox Hasidic movement headquartered in the Crown Heights neighborhood of Brooklyn, New York, with a worldwide network of emissaries and institutions. The Badlands corpus describes it as "an Ultra-Orthodox community in New York City with some dubious history."

The movement enters the March 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] because of a podcast published by [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] on March 5, 2026, examining Chabad-Lubavitch. The Brief reports that Florida politician [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]] responded by calling Carlson a "cancer," an escalation from Fine's earlier description of Carlson as "the most dangerous antisemite in America."

The Brief also recalls an episode roughly two years earlier in which the movement's Brooklyn headquarters were the subject of police attention after so-called "secret tunnels" were discovered beneath the synagogue complex, citing Times of Israel reporting that chaos ensued when the New York Police Department attempted to fill in the tunnel. The corpus treats the tunnel incident as part of the background that made Carlson's segment inflammatory.
