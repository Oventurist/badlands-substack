# Brian Sikes

Brian Sikes is the chairman and chief executive officer of [[cargill|Cargill]], the American agribusiness and food conglomerate and one of the largest privately held companies in the world.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Sikes was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. Cargill's inclusion tied the agricultural sector to a delegation of American industrial and financial leadership traveling ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]], with the administration aiming to secure business deals and purchase agreements.
