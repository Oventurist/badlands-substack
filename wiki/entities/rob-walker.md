# Rob Walker

Rob Walker is a business associate of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] who was subpoenaed by the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] as part of the congressional investigation into the Biden family's foreign business dealings.[1]

## Congressional testimony

Walker was scheduled to appear for closed-door transcribed interviews before both the House Oversight and [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary]] committees on the Friday of the week beginning January 22, 2024, as part of the House impeachment inquiry into President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] He was the second of two associates set to testify that week, following [[mervyn-yan|Mervyn Yan]].[1]

Financial records subpoenaed by the Oversight Committee indicated that members of the Biden family, including Hallie Biden and Sara Biden, received more than $1 million in payments from accounts connected to Walker and his Chinese business ventures with Hunter Biden.[1]
