# Spacecom

**Spacecom** is an Israeli satellite communications provider, operator of the Amos series of communications satellites.[1]

The company owned [[amos-6|Amos-6]], the satellite destroyed on September 1, 2016 when a [[spacex|SpaceX]] Falcon 9 exploded on its Florida launch pad during pre-launch operations.[1] A major intended user of the spacecraft was [[facebook|Facebook]], which planned to use it as part of its Internet.org effort to deliver internet access to the developing world.[1]

Spacecom's loss features in "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" chiefly because of what the destroyed payload represented — a would-be competitor to [[starlink|Starlink]] eliminated by SpaceX's only launch failure in the period examined.[1]
