# Gary Bettman

Gary Bettman is the commissioner of the [[national-hockey-league|National Hockey League]].[1]

## Comments on Pride Night

In late March 2023 a reporter with CTV Ottawa asked Bettman whether it had been a mistake to let each NHL team decide how to handle "Pride Night" promotions, after several players declined to participate.[1] Bettman signalled that the practice might be discontinued, saying, "I think it's something that we're going to have to evaluate in the offseason," and adding that it was "one issue where players for a variety of reasons may not feel comfortable wearing the uniform as a form of endorsement."[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] argued that targeting a sport composed largely of Christians with what the commentator called the trans agenda had been a strategic error, and that the initiative had been a persistent headache for the league.[1] The commentary listed the players and clubs who had resisted—Ivan Provorov, James Reimer, the Staal brothers, the Chicago Blackhawks and the Minnesota Wild—as evidence that the NHL had pushed back against the agenda more visibly than most institutions.[1]
