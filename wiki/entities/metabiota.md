# Metabiota

Metabiota is a private pandemic-risk and infectious-disease research firm that Russian military officials have identified as one of the "real clients" behind United States military-biological projects on the African continent.[1] In a December 2024 briefing, the Russian Ministry of Defense stated that Metabiota's research requests were directed toward the US Army's Infectious Diseases Research Institute and concerned the training of infectious-disease specialists in Kenya and Uganda.[1]

## Alleged role in the 2014 Ebola outbreak

Moscow's account, delivered by Major General [[aleksey-rtishchev|Aleksey Rtishchev]], cited a report by an international group of experts who took part in combating the 2014 Ebola outbreak in Sierra Leone. According to that report, Metabiota personnel concealed their engagement with [[pentagon|Pentagon]] employees while operating in the outbreak zone.[1] Russian officials characterised the primary goal of the activity as the isolation of virulent strains and their subsequent smuggling, alleging that Ebola virus samples were illegally exported by company representatives and handed over to the US Army's Infectious Diseases Research Institute.[1]

The same allegations had been made earlier by the late Lieutenant General [[igor-kirillov|Igor Kirillov]], who in an October 2023 briefing described Metabiota as "a key Pentagon contractor" whose "nontransparent practices immediately raised questions from the World Health Organisation," and said live Ebola samples ended up at the US Army Medical Research Institute of Infectious Diseases as a result of the illegal export.[1]

## Status and commentary

Russian officials stated that Metabiota has ceased its activities in Africa.[1] Badlands Media commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] linked the company to [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], describing Metabiota as "Hunter Biden's company" and arguing that Metabiota, the Pentagon and the [[state-department|State Department]] "have a lot of explaining to do" because, in his reading, the evidence suggests involvement in crimes against humanity.[1] He further noted that the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] experienced its largest Ebola outbreak in history in 2018, and that both Guinea and Uganda appeared on the list of countries Kirillov made public in September 2023.[1]
