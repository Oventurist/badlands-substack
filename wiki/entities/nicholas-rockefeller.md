# Nicholas Rockefeller

Nicholas Rockefeller is a member of the prominent Rockefeller family who appears in alternative-media accounts primarily through the testimony of filmmaker [[aaron-russo|Aaron Russo]].[1]

## Affiliations

Badlands Media identifies Rockefeller with a cluster of establishment foreign-policy and research institutions, including the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], the [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]] and the International Institute of Strategic Studies.[1]

## The Russo account

Russo stated that Rockefeller told him, as far back as the late 1990s, that the goal of the international elite was to get everybody "chipped" with an RFID chip.[1] The claim is presented by Badlands as an early and unusually explicit statement of what later became the mainstream push for [[digital-identification|digital identification]], and as evidence that the initiative long predates its contemporary policy rollout.[1] The account rests on Russo's testimony alone and is not independently corroborated in the article.[1]
