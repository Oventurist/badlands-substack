# Tim Pool

Tim Pool is an American political commentator and independent media figure whose treatment by media-ratings firms has been cited as evidence of ratings-based censorship of non-mainstream outlets.[1]

## NewsGuard dispute

In October 2023, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] publicly attacked [[newsguard|NewsGuard]] as a "scam" in a thread on X after the ratings company issued Pool a strike, in Musk's description, "because we ran 5 stories out of nearly 5,000 that quoted" former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The episode was reported alongside the lawsuit filed against NewsGuard and the Biden administration by [[consortium-news|Consortium News]], and was used by Badlands commentators to illustrate how a small number of disputed items can be leveraged to discredit an outlet's entire output.[1]

## Significance in Badlands coverage

Badlands framed the Pool strike as part of a pattern in which ratings intermediaries influence advertising revenue rather than reader trust, effectively demonetizing popular independent outlets whose audiences would otherwise be commercially attractive to advertisers.[1]

## Tenet Media indictment (2024)

Tim Pool was named in the federal indictment unsealed on September 4, 2024 as one of the conservative influencers associated with [[tenet-media|Tenet Media]], the Tennessee-based company alleged to have received nearly $10 million from employees of [[russia-today|Russia Today]].[2] The [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] stated that Pool and the other named commentators — [[benny-johnson|Benny Johnson]], [[dave-rubin|Dave Rubin]] and [[lauren-southern|Lauren Southern]] — had no idea they were taking Russian money and were deceived, having been told by Tenet founder [[lauren-chen|Lauren Chen]] that the funds came from a wealthy private investor named "Eduard Grigoriann."[2]

## Daily Wire sale rumors (December 2024)

In December 2024 an unsubstantiated rumor circulated widely on X that Pool had sold his entire podcast operation to [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] and [[daily-wire|The Daily Wire]].[3] Pool responded to critics by posting an Israeli flag, later saying he had been trolling.[3] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that if the sale were real it could explain why Pool had refrained from criticizing or questioning the Israeli government over the preceding fourteen months despite the prominence of the Israel–Gaza conflict on X, while allowing that the episode could simply be a troll.[3]

He noted the rumor surfaced the same day as reports that The Daily Wire was open to an acquisition offer from [[fox-corporation|Fox]], and recalled that in early September 2024, after the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] announced its investigation into [[tenet-media|Tenet Media]]'s Russian-linked funding, Pool told Shapiro on air that "Russia sucks."[3]


## Feud with Candace Owens (December 2025)

A public feud between Pool and [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] escalated in December 2025 after Pool accused Owens of benefiting from Charlie Kirk's murder.[4] Owens responded with a blistering rebuttal, accusing Pool of lacking "intellect," acting out of "weakness," and being "less than a man," and characterizing his on-air rant as "a display of estrogen" rather than toughness.[4] She said he would "never dare speak to a man like that," told him to "get [his] affairs in order," and denied having targeted him in her own content.[4] Badlands commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] joked only that it was "extra funny that these videos are delivered by a guy named 'Callin' Rug'."[4]
