# Max Warburg

Max Warburg was a German banker of the Warburg financial dynasty who appears in Badlands Media commentary as one of the financiers the author terms "ziobanksters" — a coinage the writer attributes to Chris Langan.[1] He is singled out as "particularly important" to the argument advanced in the article "On Infiltration and Israel."[1]

## Alleged activities

Badlands states that Warburg set up a publishing house for Lenin in 1916, tying him directly to the Bolshevik movement documented in [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]]'s [[wall-street-trilogy|Wall Street Trilogy]].[1] The article further asserts that Warburg championed the works of [[richard-coudenhove-kalergi|Count Coudenhove-Kalergi]], author of the [[kalergi-plan|Kalergi Plan]], whom the author characterizes as "a genocidal maniac."[1]

## Significance in the Badlands argument

Warburg's dual role — financing revolutionary politics in Russia while promoting a program for the demographic transformation of Europe — is used to illustrate the article's thesis that infiltration operates through a coordinated financial class rather than through any single ideology.[1] The same "ziobankster class" that funded and promoted the Kalergi Plan is said by the author to have been involved in the creation of [[israel|Israel]], connecting Warburg's activities to the [[balfour-declaration|Balfour Declaration]] and the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]].[1]
