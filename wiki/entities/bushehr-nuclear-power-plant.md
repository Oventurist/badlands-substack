# Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant

The Bushehr nuclear power plant is [[iran|Iran]]'s principal civilian nuclear generating facility. It was constructed by [[russia|Russia]] and continues to be maintained by Russian personnel, giving Moscow a direct material and human stake in the plant's physical security.[1]

## Strikes during the Iran War

During the [[iran-war|Iran War]], Israeli attacks on the Bushehr site drew explicit warnings from the Kremlin. Presidential spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] cautioned that [[israel|Israel]]'s strikes on the plant — which Russia built and maintains — could carry "irrevocable consequences."[1]

Badlands Media coverage places the Peskov warning alongside statements by Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] and Foreign Ministry spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] as part of a sustained Russian rhetorical campaign against Israel in the weeks preceding April 2026.[1] The plant's Russian staffing is treated as the key escalation risk: an attack on Bushehr is not merely an attack on Iranian infrastructure but a potential attack on Russian nationals and Russian-built nuclear assets.[1]
