# Sean Morgan

Sean Morgan is a host affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]. He co-hosts **Game Theory** with [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], which aired Thursdays at 12pm ET in the network's December 2022 schedule.[1]

Game Theory takes a macro view of national and international events, analyzing them through the frameworks of game theory and [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] in an effort, as the network puts it, to find the truth beneath the surface.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

Morgan also writes for his own outlet, the Sean Morgan Report, to which [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] directs readers of his News Brief commentary.[3]

## Commentary on grocery buy now, pay later (March 2023)

In the March 28, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Morgan responded to Adobe Analytics data showing that [[buy-now-pay-later|buy now, pay later]] usage in online grocery had grown 40% in the first two months of 2023 — the fastest pace of any retail category studied.[3] He wrote that Americans, and especially millennials, had "burned through all their savings and maxed out their credit cards" and now had to delay payment simply to buy food.[3] He described the trend as "a historic level of systemic vulnerability," warned that civil unrest becomes more likely when people cannot buy food, and urged readers to secure their assets and exit the traditional financial system.[3]
