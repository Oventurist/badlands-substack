# Melissa Hart

**Melissa Hart** is a justice of the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]] who wrote the majority opinion in the court's October 2024 decision declining to resolve the long-running dispute between Christian baker [[jack-phillips|Jack Phillips]] and transgender attorney [[autumn-scardina|Autumn Scardina]].[1]

## Masterpiece Cakeshop decision (October 2024)

On Tuesday, October 8, 2024, the Colorado Supreme Court voted 4–3 to dismiss on procedural grounds the case asking whether Phillips, owner of [[masterpiece-cakeshop|Masterpiece Cakeshop]] in Lakewood, could refuse to bake a pink-and-blue cake celebrating Scardina's gender transition on the grounds that doing so would violate his Christian faith.[1] Writing for the majority, Hart framed the underlying dispute as one that "has become the focus of intense public debate: How should governments balance the rights of transgender individuals to be free from discrimination in places of public accommodation with the rights of religious business owners when they are operating in the public market?" — and concluded, "We cannot answer that question."[1]

Scardina's attorney, John McHugh, criticized the ruling, saying the court "decided to avoid the merits of this issue by inventing an argument no party raised."[1] Phillips' attorney, Jake Werner of the [[alliance-defending-freedom|Alliance Defending Freedom]], said his client had been "dragged through courts for over a decade" and that "enough is enough."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media's [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the "technicality" invoked by the court was in substance the U.S. Constitution's protection of religious liberty and freedom of expression, and characterized the ruling as a notable shift for a court widely regarded as the most progressive state high court in the nation.[1] The 4–3 decision was described as the first contentious ruling issued since [[monica-marquez|Monica Márquez]] was appointed Chief Justice in September 2024, with Márquez joining the majority alongside Hart.[1]
