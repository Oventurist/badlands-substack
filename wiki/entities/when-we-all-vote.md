# When We All Vote

When We All Vote is a voter-registration and turnout organization founded by former First Lady [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]] in 2018.[1] In the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief of June 28, 2024, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the organization — not the gossip about the Obama and Biden families that dominated press coverage that week — was the genuinely newsworthy subject.[1]

## Badlands characterization

Ashe described When We All Vote as "the mother of all NGOs," contending that the other election-related non-governmental organizations operate underneath its umbrella and work together under the Obama banner.[1] She further noted the chronological proximity between the group's 2018 founding and remarks by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] about his campaign's "voter fraud" network, adding sardonically that she was "sure the two facts are completely unrelated."[1]

The Brief presented this as an example of the corporate press preferring interpersonal drama — in this case Michelle Obama's private frustration with the Bidens over the treatment of her friend Kathleen Buhle after Buhle's divorce from [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] — to structural questions about who organizes American election infrastructure.[1]
