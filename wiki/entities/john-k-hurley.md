# John K. Hurley

John K. Hurley is the U.S. Treasury Under Secretary for Terrorism and Financial Intelligence, the official overseeing the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]]'s sanctions and illicit-finance work, including the [[office-of-foreign-assets-control|Office of Foreign Assets Control]].[1]

## Iran–Venezuela sanctions

Announcing the turn-of-2026 designations targeting Iranian and Venezuelan defense and technology firms, Hurley said: "Treasury is holding [[iran|Iran]] and [[venezuela|Venezuela]] accountable for their aggressive and reckless proliferation of deadly weapons around the world."[1] He added, "We will continue to take swift action to deprive those who enable Iran's military-industrial complex access to the U.S. financial system."[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the statement as "tough talk against Iran's Military Industrial Complex" and asked whether the same energy could be matched against America's own [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]].[1]
