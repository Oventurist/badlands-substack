# Sundar Pichai

Sundar Pichai is the chief executive officer of [[google|Google]].[1]

Following the death of former [[youtube|YouTube]] chief executive [[susan-wojcicki|Susan Wojcicki]] on August 10, 2024, Pichai expressed condolences in a post on X, saying that Wojcicki was "core" to Google's history.[1] Wojcicki had been Google's 16th employee and worked at the company for nearly 25 years.[1]
