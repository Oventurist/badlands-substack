# Rashida Tlaib

Rashida Tlaib is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Michigan.[1]

## 2026 State of the Union

Following President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2026 State of the Union address, Trump criticized Tlaib and Rep. [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] in a [[truth-social|Truth Social]] post referencing disruptions and protest-related conduct surrounding the address.[1] In the post, Trump described the two members as having "screamed uncontrollably" during the address, called them "Crooked and Corrupt Politicians," and said they should be "sent back from where they came."[1] The same post extended his criticism to the actor Robert De Niro and to Rosie O'Donnell.[1]

## Israel–Hamas war and the 2023 backlash

In the autumn of 2023 Tlaib became a focal point of the Democratic Party's internal split over the Israel–Hamas war after calling for a cease-fire and criticizing the Israeli government.[2] On November 2, 2023 the pro-Israel group [[democratic-majority-for-israel|Democratic Majority for Israel]] released a six-figure television ad in Detroit, airing in Tlaib's district, attacking her over those positions.[2] The ad cited her 2021 vote against a bill to replenish Israel's [[iron-dome|Iron Dome]] missile defense system and her vote against a resolution to stand with Israel during the October 2023 war.[2]

Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] placed the episode within a broader fracturing of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s coalition, noting warnings from activists that Biden should "support a ceasefire now or don't count on us in 2024."[2]

## Role in the uncommitted movement

Badlands Media identified Tlaib, a Democrat congresswoman and founding member of "The Squad," as the figure who popularized the [[uncommitted-movement|uncommitted movement]] that emerged during the 2024 Democratic presidential primary.[3] The movement drew Arab-Americans, Muslims, young voters, and far-left progressives, and began in Michigan where more than 100,000 voters protested the Biden-Harris administration's handling of the Israel-Hamas war with uncommitted ballots.[3] By September 2024 figures associated with the movement, including Hamtramck Mayor [[amer-ghalib|Amer Ghalib]], were opening lines of communication with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[3]

## 2024 Michigan "uncommitted" primary campaign

In February 2024, Tlaib — an outspoken critic of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of the Israel–Hamas war and the first Palestinian American woman to serve in Congress — called on Michigan Democrats to vote "uncommitted" in the state's February 27 presidential primary.[4] In a video posted on X by the group [[listen-to-michigan|Listen to Michigan]], she said: "We feel completely neglected and just unseen by our government. If you want us to be louder, then come here and vote uncommitted."[4] The Listen to Michigan campaign comprised more than 30 Michigan officials who pledged uncommitted votes to demand that Biden back a ceasefire.[4]

Tlaib, a liberal firebrand representing parts of Detroit and its suburbs, had previously accused Biden of supporting a Palestinian "genocide," warning in a November 2023 video: "Mr. President, the American people are not with you on this one... We will remember in 2024."[4] Her push came as Biden faced growing intraparty discontent, with chants of "Genocide Joe" and "ceasefire now" trailing him on the campaign trail and Democrats worried that alienating Michigan's large Arab American and Muslim population could cost him a battleground state crucial to his 2020 win.[4] Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the effort as part of the wider [[uncommitted-movement|uncommitted movement]] and as evidence of a Democratic coalition fracturing simultaneously among unions, climate activists, and Palestinian-aligned voters.[4]
