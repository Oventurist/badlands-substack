# Rashida Tlaib

Rashida Tlaib is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Michigan and one of the most prominent progressive critics of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in [[congress|Congress]]. She is frequently paired in political commentary with Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] of Minnesota.

## 2026 State of the Union dispute

Tlaib appears in the February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief as a target of a lengthy [[truth-social|Truth Social]] post by President Trump following his 2026 [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union]] address. The post accused Tlaib and Omar of having "screamed uncontrollably" during the speech and described them as "Crooked and Corrupt Politicians" who should be sent "back from where they came — as fast as possible." Trump extended the same criticism to the actor [[robert-de-niro|Robert De Niro]] and the entertainer [[rosie-odonnell|Rosie O'Donnell]], suggesting the three should "get on a boat" together.

The post followed the arrest by the [[us-capitol-police|US Capitol Police]] of a guest whom Omar had invited to the address, an incident Omar attributed to immigration enforcement concerns. The Brief presents Trump's remarks as part of a broader confrontation between the administration and its congressional critics rather than as an isolated exchange.
