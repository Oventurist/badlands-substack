# Michael Roman

Michael Roman is a co-defendant in the Fulton County, Georgia election interference prosecution brought against former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and eighteen others, and the party who filed the initial motion accusing District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] and Special Prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] of maintaining an improper romantic relationship and of benefiting financially from the prosecution of Trump and his co-defendants.[1]

## Disqualification motion

Roman's filing set in motion the evidentiary proceedings before Superior Court Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] that dominated the case in February 2024, including disputes over Wade's cellphone records and the compelled testimony of Wade's former law partner [[terrence-bradley|Terrence Bradley]].[1] By late February 2024, summations in Roman's motion to disqualify Willis from prosecuting the racketeering case were scheduled for Friday, March 1.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the evidence surfacing through Roman's motion, together with Bradley's testimony, appeared to show that Willis and Wade had perjured themselves on the stand and in their representations to the court — a "devastating blow" to a hearing whose purpose was to assure the court and the public that there was not even an appearance of impropriety in the case.[1] She characterized the underlying prosecution as "a novel legal endeavor to persecute, I mean prosecute, a president and others for political speech and association."[1]

## Disqualification motion

Roman's attorney, [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]], authored the motion seeking to disqualify Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] over her romantic relationship with special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]], alleging the relationship created an untenable conflict of interest in the broader RICO case.[2] Text messages between Merchant and witness [[terrence-bradley|Terrence Bradley]] regarding when the romance began became central evidence at the February 27, 2024 hearing on the motion.[2]

## February 2024 disqualification hearing

Roman's motion produced a formal evidentiary hearing in Fulton County. On February 12, 2024, Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] confirmed that the hearing would proceed as scheduled that Thursday at 9:30 a.m. Eastern, and said it was "possible" the misconduct allegations "could result in disqualification" if evidence showed District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] had financially benefited from the case.[3] Willis, special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] and a number of employees of the district attorney's office had been subpoenaed to testify by Roman, whose motion filed the previous month sought to dismiss the election charges against him and disqualify Willis on the grounds that she had engaged in a "personal, romantic relationship" with Wade resulting in financial gain for both.[3] McAfee said the state had admitted a relationship existed, leaving the existence and extent of any financial benefit as the core question requiring an evidentiary record.[3]
