# Adam Smith

Adam Smith is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives and the ranking Democrat on the House Armed Services Committee.[1]

## Call for a Musk investigation

On October 25, 2024, following a [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] report that [[spacex|SpaceX]] chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had been in regular contact with Russian president [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] since 2022, Smith told Politico that the [[department-of-defense|Defense Department]] should investigate Musk to ensure his alleged actions were not "to the detriment" of United States national security.[1] His statement came alongside a similar call from [[nasa|NASA]] administrator [[bill-nelson|Bill Nelson]] and a widening group of Democratic lawmakers seeking a formal probe.[1] SpaceX publicly rejected the reporting as an "incredibly misleading story" resting on "completely unsubstantiated claims from unnamed sources."[1]
