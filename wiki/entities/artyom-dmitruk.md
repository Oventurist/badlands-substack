# Artyom Dmitruk

Artyom Dmitruk is a member of Ukraine's Verkhovna Rada and a prominent critic of the government of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1] He fled Ukraine in 2024 after being charged with assaulting a police officer; he denies wrongdoing and maintains that the prosecution is politically motivated.[1]

## Claims about Ukraine's mineral wealth

Speaking to [[rt|RT]] on Thursday, February 20, 2025, Dmitruk argued that Zelensky had wildly misled President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in boasting about Ukraine's mineral deposits.[1] "It is an issue on which Zelensky has once again fooled the whole world, and, more specifically, Donald Trump and his team," he said.[1] He raised two objections: that the rare-earth resources in question are located in territories with active combat, and that no one can say what the cost of extracting them would be.[1] "If these precious resources could have been mined so easily and on such a large scale as Zelensky promised, and if it would have been profitable, the companies in Ukraine would have started doing it long ago. It is yet another lie, another farce that Zelensky attempts to exploit," he added.[1]

His remarks followed Zelensky's offer to the United States of a partnership to develop Ukraine's minerals — "The Americans helped the most, and therefore the Americans should earn the most" — and his claim that Ukraine holds Europe's largest titanium deposits, alongside Prime Minister Denis Shmigal's op-ed assertion that Ukrainian subsoil contains 22 of the 30 minerals the EU lists as critical.[1]
