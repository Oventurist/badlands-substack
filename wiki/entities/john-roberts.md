# John Roberts

John Roberts is the Chief Justice of the United States, presiding over the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]].

## Mississippi mail ballot ruling (2026)

According to "Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes", Roberts joined Justice [[amy-coney-barrett|Amy Coney Barrett]] and the Court's three liberal justices in the 5–4 June 2026 majority holding that federal law does not bar states from counting absentee ballots postmarked by Election Day but received afterward. The decision upheld Mississippi's law permitting counting for up to five business days after Election Day and rejected a challenge from the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]]. Within the Badlands corpus, Roberts' vote is treated as an instance of the Chief Justice siding with the Court's institutionalist wing on election-administration questions, a pattern that election-integrity commentators such as [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read as pushing reform back toward [[congress|Congress]] and the states rather than the judiciary.
