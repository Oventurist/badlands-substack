# Marcus Allen

Marcus Allen is an [[fbi|FBI]] employee identified in congressional oversight material as one of three bureau personnel suspended in connection with the January 6, 2021 Capitol incursion.[1]

## Suspension and whistleblower testimony

An interim staff report released on May 18, 2023 by the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] and its [[select-subcommittee-on-the-weaponization-of-the-federal-government|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]] revealed that at least three FBI agents had been suspended or had their security clearances revoked over January 6, naming Allen alongside [[stephen-friend|Stephen Friend]] and [[brett-gloss|Brett Gloss]] as suspended while their cases were reviewed.[1] The report said the bureau had "weaponized the security clearance adjudication process to silence employees who fight against the politicized 'rot' within the FBI leadership," and included new whistleblower testimony from current and former agents describing "egregious abuse, misallocation of law enforcement resources and misconduct with the leadership ranks of the FBI."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] characterized the cases as evidence that the FBI was punishing agents who declined to follow leadership's political line, and noted that opponents had failed to rebut the whistleblower status that [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] had attributed to them.[1]
