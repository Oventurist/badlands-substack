# Marvin Bush

**Marvin Bush** is the youngest son of [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and the youngest brother of [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]].[1] In Badlands Media commentary he appears as one of four Bush sons whose business dealings are said to exemplify the family's commercial focus on the Middle East.[1]

## Kuwait-American Corporation

Citing the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]], the article states that from 1993 to 2000 Marvin Bush was a major shareholder in the [[kuwait-american-corporation|Kuwait-American Corporation]], alongside Mishal Youssef Saud al Sabah, a member of the Kuwaiti royal family.[1] The company held stakes in several U.S. defense, aviation and industrial security companies.[1] The article characterizes this arrangement as a scam in which a Kuwaiti vehicle held major positions in American firms tied to the military-industrial complex, and describes Marvin as "just another bagman for his father."[1]

## 1993 Kuwait trip

In 1993, after his father left the White House, Marvin travelled to [[kuwait|Kuwait]] with his parents, his brother [[neil-bush|Neil]] and former Secretary of State [[james-baker|James A. Baker III]].[1] Neil remained behind to lobby for business contracts after the rest of the party departed.[1]

## Assessment

The article presents these dealings as evidence that the [[bush-family|Bush family]] grew wealthier from wartime spending in the Middle East, routed through Kuwaiti entities.[1] The interpretive framing is that of the author; Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
