# Timur Mindich

Timur Mindich is a Ukrainian businessman and film-industry figure who has been described by critics as personally close to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]]. He is a recurring figure in Badlands commentary on corruption and money flows surrounding the war in Ukraine.

## Business and media interests

Mindich is a co-owner of [[kvartal-95-studio]], the production company founded by Zelenskyy in 2003 that produced *Servant of the People*, the television series in which Zelenskyy starred as a history teacher elected president of Ukraine. He is also alleged to be connected to [[fire-point]], the drone manufacturer that began by producing aerial platforms for scouting film locations and now supplies military drones. According to disgraced oligarch [[igor-kolomoisky]], Mindich is the former fiancé of Kolomoisky's daughter.

## July 2026 reshuffle allegations

In connection with the confirmation of [[sergii-koretskyi]] as prime minister, [[rt]] and some Ukrainian critics reported links between Mindich and members of the incoming government. As the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026 noted, these claims circulated in political commentary but were not substantiated by evidence presented by the Ukrainian government or by Reuters' own reporting on the cabinet changes.

## Badlands framing

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] used the Mindich reporting to argue that "the connections at this point between the Israeli elite and the Ukrainian elite are overwhelming," describing what he characterized as a transnational cartel laundering money appropriated by [[congress]] into Ukraine under cover of the war with Russia, with [[israel]] serving as a sanctuary for figures who come under investigation. He posed the questions of why Mindich would leave Ukraine at all if he were "Zelensky's guy," and why he would not feel safe there. These are contested interpretive claims rather than established findings.
