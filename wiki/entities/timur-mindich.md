# Timur Mindich

Timur Mindich is a Ukrainian businessman frequently described by critics as being close to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1] He surfaced prominently in coverage of Ukraine's July 2026 government reshuffle, when Russian outlet RT and some Ukrainian critics highlighted reports linking Mindich to members of the incoming government.[1] Those allegations circulated in political commentary but were not substantiated by evidence presented by the Ukrainian government or by Reuters' reporting on the cabinet changes.[1]

## Business ties
Mindich is alleged to be connected to [[fire-point|Fire Point]], a drone company that once produced drones for scouting filming locations and now manufactures them for warfare.[1] He is also a co-owner of [[kvartal-95-studio|Kvartal 95 Studio]], the film production company founded by Zelensky in 2003 that produced *Servant of the People*, the television series in which Zelensky played a high school history teacher who runs for president of Ukraine and wins.[1] According to disgraced oligarch [[ihor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]], Mindich is also the former fiancé of Kolomoisky's daughter.[1]

## Badlands commentary
Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the Mindich story illustrates what he described as overwhelming connections between the Israeli and Ukrainian elite, alleging a transnational cartel laundering money from Congress into [[ukraine|Ukraine]] under cover of the war with [[russia|Russia]] and then fleeing to [[israel|Israel]] for sanctuary when exposed.[1] He asked why Mindich would leave the country if he were "Zelensky's guy," why he did not feel safe in Ukraine, and why Israel was protecting him.[1]
