# Ibrahim Aqil

Ibrahim Aqil was a senior military commander of [[hezbollah]] who was killed in an Israeli airstrike on the Dahiyeh district in Beirut's southern suburbs on Friday, September 20, 2024.[1]

## Death

The Israeli military and a Lebanese security source both confirmed that Aqil had been killed alongside other senior members of an elite Hezbollah unit, sharply escalating the year-long conflict between Israel and the Iran-backed movement.[1] Hezbollah confirmed his death in a statement issued just after midnight, describing him as "one of its top leaders" without detailing how he died, and in a second statement characterized the strike as a "treacherous Israeli assassination."[1] Hezbollah said that Ahmed Wahbi — a commander who had overseen military operations of the Radwan special forces during the Gaza war until early 2024 — was also killed in the same strike.[1]

Lebanon's health ministry reported at least 14 dead, with the toll expected to rise as rescue teams worked overnight; the ministry did not say whether that figure included Aqil or other Hezbollah commanders.[1] Aqil had long been wanted by the United States in connection with the deadly 1983 Beirut embassy and Marine barracks bombings.[1]

## Context

The strike followed the coordinated remote detonation of pagers and other communications devices believed to be held by Hezbollah members, after which the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] began a sustained bombing campaign in southern Lebanon.[1] Badlands Media commentary by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that killing so many senior commanders was likely intended either to soften Hezbollah defenses ahead of a ground invasion or to provoke an overtly aggressive Hezbollah response, with less experienced successors more prone to sloppy escalation.[1]
