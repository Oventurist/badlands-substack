# Porsche

Porsche is a German sports car manufacturer headquartered in Stuttgart. It is listed in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] among the German automakers implementing major cost-cutting measures during the 2026 downturn in [[germany|Germany]]'s industrial sector.

The Brief grouped Porsche with [[volkswagen|Volkswagen]], [[mercedes-benz|Mercedes-Benz]] and [[bmw|BMW]] as manufacturers responding to declining demand, weaker sales in [[china|China]], the electric-vehicle transition, elevated energy costs and international trade pressures, in a period when the [[federation-of-german-industries|Federation of German Industries]] reported the loss of about 15,000 industrial jobs per month.
