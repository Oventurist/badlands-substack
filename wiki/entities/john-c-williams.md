# John C. Williams

**John C. Williams** is an American central banker who led the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco before being moved to head the [[federal-reserve-bank-of-new-york|New York Fed]]. In Erik Carlson's Badlands Media account, this transfer occurred during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term and is framed as a deliberate step in the long project of replacing the [[city-of-london|City of London]]-controlled financial order.[1]

According to the article, Williams created a new system for determining the interest rates used in lending money — [[sofr|SOFR]] — which would come from the US [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and replace the older, less transparent [[libor|LIBOR]] benchmark.[1] Carlson underlines the significance of the change with the observation that the "L" in LIBOR stands for London.[1]

In this framing, Williams's role is less that of a technocrat than of an instrument in a slow, deliberate transition of benchmark-setting authority from London to New York.
