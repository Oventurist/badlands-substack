# Reza Pahlavi

Reza Pahlavi is the son of the deposed Shah of Iran and the figure most frequently promoted in Western policy circles as a potential post-clerical leader of [[iran|Iran]].

## Trump's rejection

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], appearing on the Hugh Hewitt Show, said he did not think Pahlavi would be an appropriate pick to lead Iran. The Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] contrasts this with Trump's October 2024 campaign appearance on the Patrick Bet-David podcast, where he was asked whether he would support regime change in Iran.

## Badlands assessment

The commentary treats Trump's remark as significant: it "certainly throws cold water on the NeoCons and Zionists, who have been pushing this guy down our throats for months." The Brief points to Pahlavi's meeting with [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] during the past year, one of many trips to [[israel|Israel]], and to his performance of religious rituals there, as evidence in the author's view that he sought "the favor of the powers that be."

The author's own forecast is that regime change in Iran is unlikely, though he can envisage the religious elements of the state — the Ayatollah, the mullahs, and the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|IRGC]] — being expunged while the socially moderate political administration remains in power.
