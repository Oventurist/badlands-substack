# WinRed

**WinRed** is the principal online small-dollar fundraising platform used by Republican candidates and committees, functioning as the party's counterpart to the Democratic platform [[actblue|ActBlue]].[1]

## Congressional scrutiny

In June 2026 congressional scrutiny of both WinRed and ActBlue intensified as House Republicans held a hearing examining allegations of fraud, foreign contributions, and weaknesses in donor-verification systems on online political fundraising platforms.[1] Democrats on the panel argued that Republicans were using the hearing to target Democratic fundraising operations, and pointed out that similar concerns had also been raised about WinRed.[1] The hearing produced no new evidence of criminal wrongdoing by either platform, but confirmed that congressional investigations into online fundraising practices were ongoing and that donor-verification questions would persist into the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].[1]

## Bipartisan framing

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the alleged abuses as bipartisan in origin, writing that Republicans "got in on the operation" in 2012 with the Mitt Romney campaign, while noting that Barack Obama's PAC had received what she described as the largest campaign-finance-violation fine in history for so-called "primitive smurfing" during the 2008 cycle, a penalty imposed in 2013.[1] Her conclusion — "Prosecute every one of them" — was applied to both parties' operations rather than to Democrats alone.[1]

## Calls for parallel scrutiny in 2025

When House Republicans subpoenaed ActBlue officials in June 2025 over alleged widespread fraud, Badlands commentators immediately called for the same treatment of WinRed, the principal Republican online fundraising platform.[2] They cited investigator Peter Bernegger's claim that Senator Ted Cruz is the largest recipient of smurfed donations nationally, and argued that being in the party in power is no excuse for laundering foreign money through individual identities.[2]

## April 2025 straw donor memorandum

After President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] issued the [[2025-straw-donor-and-foreign-contributions-investigation-memorandum|April 2025 memorandum]] directing the Attorney General to investigate straw, dummy and foreign contributions routed through online fundraising platforms, Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] predicted that WinRed would "almost certainly" be among the many platforms drawn into the inquiry alongside [[actblue|ActBlue]], noting that the problem spans both sides of the aisle.[3]
