# WinRed

WinRed is the principal online small-dollar fundraising platform used by Republican candidates and committees, functioning as the party's counterpart to [[actblue|ActBlue]].

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that WinRed was examined alongside ActBlue in a House Republican hearing on allegations of fraud, foreign contributions, and weaknesses in donor-verification systems. Democrats on the panel argued that the hearing was intended to target Democratic fundraising operations while noting that comparable concerns about donor verification had been raised about WinRed. The reporting cited by the Brief concluded that the hearing produced no new evidence of criminal wrongdoing by either platform, but that congressional investigations into online political fundraising would continue into the 2026 midterm elections. The Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats bipartisan participation in such fundraising practices as aggravating rather than exculpatory, noting that Republicans "got in on the operation in 2012 with the Mitt Romney campaign."
