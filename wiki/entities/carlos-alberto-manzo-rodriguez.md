# Carlos Alberto Manzo Rodríguez

Carlos Alberto Manzo Rodríguez was the mayor of the Uruapan municipality in Mexico's western state of Michoacán. He was shot dead on Saturday, November 1, 2025, in the town's historic center in front of dozens of people who had gathered for Day of the Dead festivities.[1]

## Assassination

Manzo was rushed to a hospital, where he later died, according to state prosecutor Carlos Torres Piña.[1] A city council member and a bodyguard were also injured in the attack, and the attacker was killed at the scene, Federal Security Secretary [[omar-garcia-harfuch|Omar García Harfuch]] told journalists the following day.[1] Reporting on the killing noted that local politicians in Mexico are frequently victims of political and organized crime violence.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America used the killing to argue against open-border advocacy, writing that it is "madness" to press for an open border with a nation where politicians are murdered in the street and where "organized crime" typically means cartel activity, and calling instead for the border to remain closed and for unlawful entrants to be removed.[1]
