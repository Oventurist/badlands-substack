# United States Citizenship and Immigration Services

United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is the component of the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] responsible for administering lawful immigration, naturalization and citizenship records. Because it holds the federal government's authoritative data on who has become a citizen, it is central to any program that seeks to verify citizenship status for purposes other than immigration.

The [[badlands-brief-323|Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026]] reports that USCIS officials, alongside DHS, said internal deliberations were ongoing regarding implementation of [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s March 2026 executive order [[ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections|"Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections"]], which directs DHS to develop a "state citizenship list." That list would be assembled from the kind of records USCIS maintains and shared with states for use in voter-roll verification, work coordinated in [[white-house|White House]] interagency talks involving the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] and the [[united-states-postal-service|Postal Service]].
