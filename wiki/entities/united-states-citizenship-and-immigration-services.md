# United States Citizenship and Immigration Services

United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is the component of the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] responsible for adjudicating immigration benefits, including employment authorization documents.[1]

## 2025 end of automatic work-permit extensions

On October 29, 2025, DHS officials announced that the agency was ending a Biden-era policy that automatically extended work permits for millions of migrants in the United States.[1] Under the change, migrants seeking extensions must first undergo "screening and vetting" before an extension is granted.[1]

USCIS Director [[joseph-edlow|Joseph Edlow]] said former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s administration had prioritized migrants in the American labor market ahead of national security, stating that "USCIS is placing a renewed emphasis on robust alien screening and vetting, eliminating policies the former administration implemented that prioritized aliens' convenience ahead of Americans' safety and security."[1]
