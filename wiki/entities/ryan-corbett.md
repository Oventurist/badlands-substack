# Ryan Corbett

Ryan Corbett is the Chief Financial Officer of [[mp-materials|MP Materials]], the operator of the [[mountain-pass-mine|Mountain Pass Mine]] in California.[1]

## Public comments on Mountain Pass

In September 2025 reporting on the mine's role amid a growing trade war, Corbett described the deposit's formation as something that "probably happened many billions of years ago," calling the site "almost a freak of nature."[1] He explained that the way fault lines formed there brought rare-earth-bearing rock formations to the surface at a concentration and in a form "so easily leveraged and refined with our technology" that it is effectively unmatched worldwide.[1] He added that the ore is "so close to the surface and in a form that is easily digestible, if you will, in the downstream."[1]
