# Gina Raimondo

Gina Raimondo is the United States Commerce Secretary in the administration of [[joe-biden|President Joe Biden]]. In Badlands Media's coverage of deteriorating US–China relations in late 2023, she appears principally as the envoy behind an August 2023 diplomatic mission to China.[1]

## 2023 mission to China

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that regardless of the Biden administration's "hailing" of Raimondo's diplomatic trip, she was "as unfruitful and disrespected as all the other bureaucrats they've sent."[1] The commentary framed the visit as one more failed attempt to stabilize a relationship in which Beijing has accused Washington of "comprehensively containing" China through wars of tariffs, trade, technology, chips and rules.[1] Ashe in America also noted a widely circulated story involving children and Disney during Raimondo's time in China, remarking that the detail was "a little on the nose" — a nod to the symbolic register Badlands writers often read into official narratives.[1]

Her mission was cited in the same news cycle in which Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] declined to attend the [[g20|G20]] summit in India, a snub Badlands interpreted as evidence that established instruments of global governance were losing their hold.[1]
