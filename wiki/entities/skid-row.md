# Skid Row

Skid Row is a district of downtown [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]] with one of the largest concentrations of homeless residents in the United States. In the Badlands Media corpus it is the physical center of an alleged paid voter-registration scheme tied to the city's 2026 elections.

## Federal operation, June 18, 2026

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that roughly twenty federal agents, including personnel from [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]], moved through Skid Row shortly before noon on Thursday, June 18, as part of an investigation into alleged voter fraud connected to the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|Los Angeles mayoral election]] and the gubernatorial primary. Witnesses described agents in casual clothing fanning out across the streets, interviewing dozens of homeless residents and taking notes, with locals apparently directing them toward specific addresses and individuals. [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] amplified the operation on social media and, in the first comment on his post, linked the May 2026 [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] press release announcing the [[brenda-lee-brown-armstrong|Brenda Lee Brown Armstrong]] indictment.

## Allegations

Post-Election Day videos cited in the Brief show Skid Row residents claiming they were paid cash to sign registration forms, forge signatures, and complete voter information ahead of the mayoral race and primaries. Residents said they were encouraged to support incumbent Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] and Councilmember [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]]; one account stated that challenger [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] was not among the candidates promoted. A review cited in the reporting counted more than 7,600 registered voters linked to homeless shelter and social service addresses, including roughly 1,160 registrations tied to the [[midnight-mission|Midnight Mission]] in Skid Row.
