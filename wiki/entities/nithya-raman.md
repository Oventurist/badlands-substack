# Nithya Raman

Nithya Raman is a member of the [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]] City Council who advanced to the November 2026 runoff election for mayor of the city.

According to reporting summarized in [[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]], Raman entered the mayoral race shortly before the filing deadline and campaigned on housing affordability, homelessness, and city services. She finished ahead of former reality television personality [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] in the city's nonpartisan primary, in which voters chose among fourteen candidates, and will face incumbent Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] in the runoff. Public polling before the primary had shown Bass, Raman, and Pratt in a closely contested race.

Badlands commentators treat the outcome less as a judgment on Raman's campaign than as an occasion to question the integrity of Los Angeles's vote-counting apparatus, which [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] notes is the last in the United States to use [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] equipment.
