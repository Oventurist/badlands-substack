# Tiffany Trump

**Tiffany Trump** is the youngest daughter of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] She married Michael Boulos in 2022, with her father walking her down the aisle.[1]

## Connection to the Boulos appointment

Tiffany Trump's marriage to Michael Boulos made [[massad-boulos|Massad Boulos]] — a Lebanese-born billionaire businessman — her father-in-law, a relationship highlighted when President-elect Trump announced on December 1, 2024 that Boulos would serve as senior adviser on Arab and Middle Eastern affairs.[1] Badlands anticipated that mainstream coverage would frame the appointment as nepotism because of the family tie, and argued instead that Boulos's business record in West Africa and his prior service as Trump's personal liaison to Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas were the substantive reasons for the pick.[1]
