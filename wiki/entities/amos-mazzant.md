# Amos L. Mazzant

Amos L. Mazzant is a United States federal judge in Texas who issued the ruling compelling the [[fbi|FBI]] to disclose material from the laptops of murdered [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] staffer [[seth-rich|Seth Rich]].[1]

## Ruling in the Seth Rich FOIA litigation

In an opinion reported in late November 2023, Mazzant ordered "a timeline for the disclosure of information on Seth Rich's personal laptop, Seth Rich's work laptop, the DVD and tape drive within 14 days following issuance of this Memorandum of Opinion and Order."[1] The order was entered on a Wednesday morning and arose out of Freedom of Information Act requests, in litigation in which the court had previously addressed the adequacy of the FBI's responses in September 2022.[1]

Because the Rich case has been a fixture of [[truth-community|Truth Community]] research since 2016, the order drew immediate attention from independent commentators, who framed it as a potential turning point in a long-running document fight.[1]
