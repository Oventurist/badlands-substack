# Federal Correctional Institution, Tucson

The Federal Correctional Institution, Tucson (FCI Tucson) is a medium-security federal prison in Arizona operated by the [[federal-bureau-of-prisons|Bureau of Prisons]].[1]

## Security problems

The facility has been plagued by security lapses and staffing shortages, a record that drew renewed attention after a high-profile assault there in November 2023.[1]

## Chauvin stabbing

On a Friday in late November 2023, [[derek-chauvin|Derek Chauvin]], the former Minneapolis police officer convicted of murdering [[george-floyd|George Floyd]], was stabbed by another inmate and seriously injured at FCI Tucson.[1] The Bureau of Prisons confirmed that an incarcerated person was assaulted at the prison at around 12:30 p.m. local time, saying that responding employees contained the incident and performed "life-saving measures" before the inmate — whom the agency did not name — was taken to a hospital for further treatment and evaluation.[1] Details of the attack were provided to the [[associated-press|Associated Press]] by a person not authorised to discuss them publicly, who spoke on condition of anonymity.[1]
