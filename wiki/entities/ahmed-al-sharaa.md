# Ahmed Al-Sharaa

Ahmed Al-Sharaa is identified in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] as the President of [[syria]]. The Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] met Al-Sharaa on the sidelines of the [[nato|NATO]] summit in Ankara, Turkey, in July 2026, and that at the meeting Trump declared his intention to remove Syria from the United States list of state sponsors of terrorism. The encounter is presented as the opening move in a rapid sequence of Middle Eastern diplomacy that continued with Trump's White House meeting with Iraqi Prime Minister [[ali-al-zaidi|Ali Al-Zaidi]] and the announcement of American support for an [[iraq|Iraq]]–Syria oil pipeline bypassing the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].
