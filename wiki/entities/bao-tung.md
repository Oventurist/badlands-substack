# Bao Tung

Bao Tung was the principal secretary to Communist Party General Secretary [[zhao-ziyang|Zhao Ziyang]] and, in Matthew Ehret's Badlands account, one of Zhao's two right-hand men involved in managing [[george-soros|George Soros]]-affiliated Open Society think tanks in China.[1]

Soros described Bao Tung's role directly in his 1990 book *Underwriting Democracy*, writing that Bao Tung, "Communist party General Secretary Zhao Ziyang's reform-minded principal secretary, cut through the red tape and approved the foundation on the spot."[1] The foundation in question was set up on the Hungarian model with [[chen-yizi|Chen Yizi]]'s Institute for Economic Reform as Soros's partner.[1] Ehret situates this approval within a broader effort — alongside the 1986 "Fund for the Reform and Opening Up of China" and the Institute for Economic and Structural Reform — to embed free-market and Open Society influence within the Chinese state apparatus prior to the failed 1989 color revolution.[1]
