# Julie Miville-Dechêne

Julie Miville-Dechêne is an Independent member of the Senate of Canada who in 2021 brought forward [[bill-s-210|Bill S-210]], *An Act to restrict young persons' online access to sexually explicit material*.[1] The bill, which would require pornographic websites to verify the age of users, passed the Senate in April 2023 and moved to the House of Commons, where it cleared second reading in December 2023 and was referred for committee study on public safety and national security.[1]

Her legislation became the focus of controversy after [[pornhub|Pornhub]]'s owners called it the "wrong legislation" and floated blocking Canadian access altogether, while critics warned that the bill's failure to specify how verification would work created serious privacy and surveillance risks.[1]
