# Brad Zerbo

Brad Zerbo is a Badlands-affiliated filmmaker and Substack author, known as the creator of the documentary film "Codex 911," which examines the September 11, 2001 attacks.[1]

## Codex 911

The film was scheduled to premiere at [[gart|GART]] 9 in Deadwood, South Dakota, a few weeks before the 24th anniversary of the attacks.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] promoted the premiere alongside commentary on the July 2025 appellate ruling permitting the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] to withdraw from 9/11 plea agreements, arguing that "the truth of our shared trauma is critical to our national healing" and that the public may now be ready to evaluate and accept it.[1] Virtual tickets were offered for those unable to attend in person.[1]

## "September 11th 2001" essay

In July 2025 Zerbo published a personal Substack feature titled "September 11th 2001," recounting the morning of the attacks in first person.[2] He described being woken by his friend Sal — "Brad wake up!… a bomb just went off in one of the Towers" — and turning on the news to see one of the twin towers with a gaping hole spewing smoke and fire, an image he called awe-inspiring yet immensely sad.[2] As the two walked into the kitchen, United Flight 175 exploded into tower 2.[2]

"Everything changed," Zerbo wrote, describing the moment as an act of war and terrorism in which "the core foundation of our entire planet was rocked with the impact of Flight 175," the instant Flight 11 became no accident and "crisis became King."[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted the essay as the filmmaker's back story and the personal inspiration for [[codex-911|CODEX 911]], which premiered at GART Deadwood with virtual tickets available.[2]

## "September 11th 2001" essay and Codex 9/11 (July 2025)

In July 2025, Zerbo published a personal essay titled "September 11th 2001," recounting how he was woken by his friend Sal with news that "a bomb just went off in one of the Towers," and how he watched United Flight 175 strike the second tower as the two walked into the kitchen.[3] He described the morning as the point at which "everything changed" and "Flight 11 became no accident, and crisis became King."[3] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] presented the essay as the filmmaker's back story and the personal inspiration for his documentary [[codex-911|Codex 9/11]], which premiered at [[gart|GART]] Deadwood and was made available to virtual ticket holders through an early screening.[3]

## Reception alongside Tucker Carlson's 9/11 documentary (September 2025)

In September 2025, after [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] claimed on [[piers-morgan|Piers Morgan]]'s program that Israeli intelligence had foreknowledge of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Carlson's forthcoming documentary series could serve as "a great companion documentary" to Zerbo's [[codex-911|CODEX 9/11]].[4] He described Zerbo's film as definitively demonstrating that 9/11 was an inside job and as presenting the most compelling theory he had seen regarding the aircraft — namely remote-piloted drones — and argued that the zeitgeist had shifted such that the American people were finally prepared to confront hard truths about the attacks.[4]

## CODEX 911

Zerbo's original film *CODEX 911* was slated to premiere at the GART Deadwood event, and [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted the premiere as a leading reason to obtain tickets.[5] Viewers unable to attend in person were offered a virtual ticket option to take part in the premiere.[5]

## "September 11th 2001" feature and CODEX 911

In July 2025 Zerbo published a personal Substack feature titled "September 11th 2001," recounting being woken by his friend Sal on the morning of the attacks — "Brad wake up! … a bomb just went off in one of the Towers" — and watching United Flight 175 explode into the second tower as the two walked into the kitchen.[6] He wrote that "everything changed" in that moment, that it was when "Flight 11 became no accident, and crisis became King," and that the event would shape the rest of his life.[6] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] promoted the piece as the filmmaker's back story and the source of his personal inspiration for [[codex-911|CODEX 911]], which premiered at [[gart-9-deadwood|GART Deadwood]] and was available for early screening to virtual ticket holders.[6]

## CODEX 911 (2025)

Zerbo directed [[codex-911|CODEX 911]], an original film produced under the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] banner, which premiered at the GART Deadwood event and was promoted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025.[7] Badlands offered virtual tickets so remote audiences could take part in the premiere.[7]


## CODEX 9/11 trailer (August 2025)

Days before the Deadwood premiere, Zerbo released a new trailer for [[codex-911|CODEX 9/11]], which the August 8, 2025 Badlands News Brief promoted alongside the GART event, exclusive streams, panels and behind-the-scenes content, with virtual tickets available through Badlands Media.[8]
