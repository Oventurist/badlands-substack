# Tyler Cherry

Tyler Cherry is a communications staffer hired by the Biden administration in June 2024 as Associate Communications Director, a hire that drew immediate criticism over previously posted social media content.[1]

Reporting by Modernity News, aggregated as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief for June 24, 2024, said that shortly after the hire was announced it emerged that Cherry had a history of posts disparaging white people, comparing police officers to "slave patrols" and "lynch mobs," and calling for ICE to be abolished — posts that were subsequently deleted.[1] The account noted that Cherry holds a degree in gender studies and previously worked for Media Matters, and framed the appointment as a DEI hire by the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration.[1]
