# Nicusor Dan

Nicusor Dan is the President of [[romania|Romania]].[1]

## Role in the May 2026 government crisis

Following the successful no-confidence vote that toppled Prime Minister [[ilie-bolojan|Ilie Bolojan]]'s pro-EU minority government, constitutional responsibility fell to Dan to nominate a new prime minister, with Bolojan continuing in a limited interim role until a replacement government was approved.[1]

## Victory in the 2025 rerun

Dan, the pro-EU mayor of Bucharest, defeated Eurosceptic candidate [[george-simion|George Simion]] in Romania's presidential election rerun, taking 54% of the vote to Simion's 46% with more than 99% of ballots counted.[2] He thanked supporters for an "unprecedented mobilization" and wrote on X: "Starting tomorrow, we begin the reconstruction of Romania – a united and honest Romania, founded on respect for the law and for all its people."[2] Badlands commentary greeted the outcome with open skepticism, noting that the same authorities had annulled the previous election, cancelled its follow-up, and prosecuted the populist who had won it before a second populist candidate likewise fell short.[2]
