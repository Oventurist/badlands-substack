# Nicusor Dan

Nicusor Dan is the President of [[romania|Romania]].[1]

## Role in the May 2026 government crisis

Following the successful no-confidence vote that toppled Prime Minister [[ilie-bolojan|Ilie Bolojan]]'s pro-EU minority government, constitutional responsibility fell to Dan to nominate a new prime minister, with Bolojan continuing in a limited interim role until a replacement government was approved.[1]
