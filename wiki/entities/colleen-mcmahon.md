# Colleen McMahon

Colleen McMahon is a United States District Judge sitting in Manhattan who, in July 2025, presided over the federal false-claims litigation against [[cvs-health|CVS Health]]'s [[omnicare|Omnicare]] unit.[1]

## Omnicare ruling

McMahon ordered the company to pay $948.8 million in total penalties and damages, including a $542 million penalty for the filing of more than 3.3 million false claims across an eight-year period beginning in 2010.[1] The suit alleged that Omnicare had fraudulently billed the federal government for invalid drug prescriptions.[1] The judgment was reported as one of the largest of its kind against a pharmacy operator and drew commentary in alternative media calling for criminal prosecution of individual executives rather than corporate fines alone.[1]
