# Fumio Kishida

Fumio Kishida is a Japanese politician who served as Prime Minister of Japan.[1]

## Appearance at the April 2024 White House press conference

Kishida appeared alongside U.S. President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] at a Washington news conference in April 2024 during a visit to the United States.[1] It was at that joint appearance that Biden pledged "ironclad" American support for [[israel|Israel]] against threatened reprisals by [[iran|Iran]], repeating the word for emphasis and saying the United States would "do all we can to protect Israel's security."[1] Biden's remarks came in the aftermath of the April 1 strike that destroyed an Iranian consulate building in Damascus and killed members of Iran's [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Revolutionary Guards]], and while Kishida's visit was principally a bilateral U.S.–Japan occasion, the press conference became a vehicle for U.S. messaging on the Middle East crisis.[1]
