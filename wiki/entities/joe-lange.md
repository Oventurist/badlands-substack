# Joe Lange

Joe Lange is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor who writes feature essays for the outlet's Substack, where he maintains an author profile.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## Featured work

Lange's essay "Disinformation is Necessary," published 28 October 2025, was among the features collected in the 7 November 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] retrospective.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) The piece is framed around a single question — what would be the purpose of creating confusion? — treating [[disinformation|disinformation]] as a deliberate instrument with an identifiable strategic function rather than as mere noise.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## Financial series and CBDCs

Lange authored an extended Badlands series on the financial system, of which "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?" (June 2, 2023) is an installment.[2] In it he argues that the U.S. dollar was the [[cabal|cabal's]] "Achilles heel" and Keystone, that the global debt system is collapsing under unpayable debt, and that a [[great-reset|Great Reset]] was always inevitable — the open question being whose reset prevails.[2] Earlier pieces in the series, referenced within the article, include "Master and Commander," arguing Trump controls the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and the dollar, and "The Midas Touch," laying out evidence of a worldwide return to the [[gold-standard|gold standard]].[2]

Lange's distinctive position is that [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDCs]] should be welcomed rather than feared, because [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and [[brics|BRICS]] forced central banks to react rather than the reverse, and because blockchain settlement is the tool that can compress the suffering of the coming crash.[2] He credits [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] and [[j-christopher-giancarlo|J. Christopher Giancarlo]] as the American architects of the digital dollar.[2] The article carries Badlands Media's standard disclaimer that contributor opinions do not necessarily represent the outlet's views, and directs readers to Lange's own Substack for more of his work.[2]

## "Bad Actors" and the puppet master series

Lange is the author of "Bad Actors," an installment in a continuing series arguing that a cabal of elite puppet masters scripted a [[sixteen-year-plan|sixteen year plan]] to destroy America and the Constitution, and that [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] controlled both [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] as its executors.[3] The piece extends arguments made in his earlier articles "We Are Watching a Movie," "The Chosen One," "The Difference Between Friend And Foe" and "Profiting From War," and promises later installments on the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], Libya, and [[john-mccain|John McCain]]'s role in "The Sum of All Fears."[3] His method throughout is to pair Q drops with mainstream press reporting — The Hill, Newsweek, The Independent, Politico, Newsmax, The Boston Globe and ShadowProof — and read the latter as inadvertent confirmation of the former.[3] The article closes with his recurring refrain that "the flow of information is vital in a narrative war," and directs readers to his own Substack.[3]
