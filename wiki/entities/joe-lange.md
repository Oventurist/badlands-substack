# Joe Lange

Joe Lange is a contributing writer for Badlands Media and the author of a serialised body of work reinterpreting the 2011 Libyan intervention, the 2012 [[benghazi|Benghazi]] attack and the rise of [[isis|ISIS]] as elements of a single covert weapons-trafficking enterprise. He also publishes independently at Joe Lange's Substack.

## Work in the corpus

Lange is the author of [[a-dereliction-of-duty|A Dereliction of Duty]], published November 16, 2024 as a follow-up to his earlier feature "What Difference Does Benghazi Make?" His method combines mainstream press citations — the Washington Post, Fox News, Politico, The Hill, The Times, the New York Post, Investors Business Daily and the World Socialist Web Site — with readings of Q drops, notably drops 2640 and 1887, which he treats as an interpretive key to the documentary record.

His recurring argument is that the [[state-department|State Department]] under [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the [[cia|CIA]] ran a weapons pipeline from Libya through [[turkey|Turkey]] to Syrian rebel forces with [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s approval, that Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] was its liaison and was sacrificed when exposure became imminent, and that [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] managed both the battlefield approval bottleneck and the subsequent cover-up. At the close of the article Lange announces that a forthcoming piece will address why Obama, Brennan and [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] were installed, and why the administration was staffed as it was.

Badlands Media appends a standing disclaimer to his features noting that its articles represent the opinions of contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself.
