# Joe Lange

Joe Lange is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor who writes feature essays for the outlet's Substack, where he maintains an author profile.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## Featured work

Lange's essay "Disinformation is Necessary," published 28 October 2025, was among the features collected in the 7 November 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] retrospective.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) The piece is framed around a single question — what would be the purpose of creating confusion? — treating [[disinformation|disinformation]] as a deliberate instrument with an identifiable strategic function rather than as mere noise.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## Financial series and CBDCs

Lange authored an extended Badlands series on the financial system, of which "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?" (June 2, 2023) is an installment.[2] In it he argues that the U.S. dollar was the [[cabal|cabal's]] "Achilles heel" and Keystone, that the global debt system is collapsing under unpayable debt, and that a [[great-reset|Great Reset]] was always inevitable — the open question being whose reset prevails.[2] Earlier pieces in the series, referenced within the article, include "Master and Commander," arguing Trump controls the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and the dollar, and "The Midas Touch," laying out evidence of a worldwide return to the [[gold-standard|gold standard]].[2]

Lange's distinctive position is that [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDCs]] should be welcomed rather than feared, because [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and [[brics|BRICS]] forced central banks to react rather than the reverse, and because blockchain settlement is the tool that can compress the suffering of the coming crash.[2] He credits [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] and [[j-christopher-giancarlo|J. Christopher Giancarlo]] as the American architects of the digital dollar.[2] The article carries Badlands Media's standard disclaimer that contributor opinions do not necessarily represent the outlet's views, and directs readers to Lange's own Substack for more of his work.[2]

## "Bad Actors" and the puppet master series

Lange is the author of "Bad Actors," an installment in a continuing series arguing that a cabal of elite puppet masters scripted a [[sixteen-year-plan|sixteen year plan]] to destroy America and the Constitution, and that [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] controlled both [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] as its executors.[3] The piece extends arguments made in his earlier articles "We Are Watching a Movie," "The Chosen One," "The Difference Between Friend And Foe" and "Profiting From War," and promises later installments on the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], Libya, and [[john-mccain|John McCain]]'s role in "The Sum of All Fears."[3] His method throughout is to pair Q drops with mainstream press reporting — The Hill, Newsweek, The Independent, Politico, Newsmax, The Boston Globe and ShadowProof — and read the latter as inadvertent confirmation of the former.[3] The article closes with his recurring refrain that "the flow of information is vital in a narrative war," and directs readers to his own Substack.[3]

## "Clowns in America" and the Bush–CIA series

Lange is also the author of "Clowns in America" (August 14, 2023), subtitled "Admit Nothing; Deny Everything," which traces the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s capture of the presidency to [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]]'s eleven months as Director of Central Intelligence in 1976.[4] The piece argues that Bush secured CIA control of the [[presidents-daily-brief|President's Daily Brief]], enabling the growth of a [[shadow-government|shadow government]], and that the same instrument was later turned against the cabal when FBI analyst [[tonya-ugoretz|Tonya Ugoretz]] inserted evidence of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s private-server exposure into [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s brief — a trap Lange credits to General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]].[4]

The second half of the article develops Lange's drug-trafficking thesis, presenting [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Offshore]] and Bush's partner [[thomas-j-devine|Thomas J. Devine]] as CIA commercial cover, and the [[french-connection|French Connection]], the [[golden-triangle|Golden Triangle]] and the [[secret-war-in-laos|secret war in Laos]] — illustrated by the case of [[edgar-buell|Edgar Buell]] — as evidence that the agency became the world's largest drug trafficker.[4] He announced that subsequent installments would go deeper into global drug trafficking, wars and money laundering focused on the Bush family, eventually connecting to [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Al Waleed]], and promised a coming article on the Awan brothers.[4] As in his other work, the piece carries Badlands Media's contributor disclaimer and links to his personal Substack.[4]

## "Disinformation is Necessary"

Lange published "Disinformation is Necessary," subtitled "Deception and the Art of War," on 28 October 2025.[5] The piece opens with Sun Tzu's maxim that "the whole secret lies in confusing the enemy, so that he cannot fathom our real intent" and with Q drop 1318's question, "What would be the purpose of creating CONFUSION?"[5] Its central argument is that the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller special counsel investigation]] was an enemy operation that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] anticipated and turned against its authors by leveraging [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]] and [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]], installing [[geoffrey-berman|Geoffrey Berman]] at the [[southern-district-of-new-york|SDNY]], and using [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]]'s "insurance file" to introduce evidence legally.[5] The article is a continuation of his earlier feature "Do You Believe in Coincidences?", which covered the leveraging of Rosenstein and [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]]'s classified unrecusal letter to the Supreme Court.[5] It closes by promising a following installment connecting the Podestas, [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] and [[september-11-attacks|9/11]].[5] Lange's work also appears at Joe Lange's Substack.[5]

## "Master and Commander" (January 2023)

Lange's January 25, 2023 essay "Master and Commander," subtitled "The Collapse of the Central Banking System Has Begun," advanced his long-running claim that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] controls the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and that the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]] are no longer its puppet masters.[6] He argued Trump is a master of bankruptcy law who used [[exchange-stabilization-fund|the Exchange Stabilization Fund]] and the March 2020 Fed–Treasury merger to seize the enemy's greatest weapon and turn it against them.[6] The piece closed by promising a follow-up painting the picture of what would replace the bankrupt system, which he believed would grant unprecedented financial freedom.[6]
