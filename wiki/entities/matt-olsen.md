# Matt Olsen

Matt Olsen is an American national security lawyer who served as the [[justice-department|Justice Department]]'s assistant attorney general for national security, the senior official responsible for the department's counterterrorism, counterintelligence, and export-control enforcement portfolio.[1]

## Statements on Iranian assassination threats

In October 2024, Olsen was quoted in Politico reporting on Iranian plots targeting [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and former senior officials associated with the January 2020 drone strike that killed [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]]. Olsen characterized the threat picture in unusually stark terms, saying, "This is extraordinarily serious," and adding that "Iran has made it very clear that they are determined to seek retaliation against former officials in connection with the Soleimani strike."[1]

His remarks accompanied reporting that U.S. officials had concluded Tehran was not bluffing and was not likely to abandon its retaliation campaign, and that Iranian efforts against Trump and former national security officials were more extensive and aggressive than previously disclosed, according to roughly a dozen officials familiar with the matter.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated official statements of this kind with skepticism, arguing that a government genuinely alarmed by Iranian assassins would not simultaneously have loosened border controls, and suggesting that any would-be assassins might be working for authorities in Washington rather than Tehran.[1]
