# Luis De Javier

Luis De Javier is a fashion designer who made his [[new-york-fashion-week|New York Fashion Week]] debut in February 2023 with a collection created in collaboration with the pornography platform [[pornhub]].[1]

The show was praised in some fashion coverage as "a runway show that sexually liberated in style," but the [[national-center-on-sexual-exploitation|National Center on Sexual Exploitation]] countered that the collaboration normalized and glamorized a company complicit in mass sexual crime and urged the public to take action.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[absolutetruth1776]] treated the De Javier show as a successor to the [[balenciaga|Balenciaga]] scandal and as evidence that mainstream fashion was becoming more overt in its embrace of sexualized imagery.[1]
