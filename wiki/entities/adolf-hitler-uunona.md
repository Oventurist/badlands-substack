# Adolf Hitler Uunona

Adolf Hitler Uunona is a Namibian regional politician who was named after the Nazi leader but who rejects any association with his ideology.[1]

## Political career

Uunona, 59, is a member of the ruling left-wing [[swapo|SWAPO]] party and has served as a councilor in the Ompundja constituency of Namibia's Oshana region since 2004.[1] In November 2025 he stood for re-election in regional elections scheduled for November 26, a candidacy reported internationally after the German tabloid Bild picked up the story.[1] He was widely expected to retain his seat, having won roughly 85 percent of the vote in 2020.[1]

## Name and international attention

Uunona first drew international attention in 2020, when his full name surfaced in global media coverage of that year's election.[1] He has said that his father deliberately named him after Adolf Hitler but likely did not grasp the full history involved: "My father named me after this man. He probably didn't understand what Adolf Hitler stood for. As a child, I saw it as a totally normal name."[1] He has stressed that he does not support Nazi ideology, adding, "Only as I grew up did I realize: This man wanted to subjugate the whole world. I have nothing to do with any of these things."[1]
