# Cecile Richards

Cecile Richards was an American activist and the former president of [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]]. Her family announced her death in a social media post on Monday, January 20, 2025; she was 67.[1]

## Death

"This morning our beloved Cecile passed away at home, surrounded by her family and her ever-loyal dog, Ollie," the family's statement read, adding that "no words can do justice to the joy she brought to our lives."[1] Richards had been diagnosed with brain cancer in 2023.[1] Former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] said he was "deeply saddened" to learn of her death.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the timing of Richards's death — immediately preceding the second inauguration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the signing of an executive order defining sex from the point of conception — describing the coincidence as "fascinating" and remarking that "the former head of Planned Parenthood was unavailable for comment because she died suddenly, right before the inauguration."[1]
