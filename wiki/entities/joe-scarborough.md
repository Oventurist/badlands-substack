# Joe Scarborough

Joe Scarborough is an [[msnbc|MSNBC]] host cited in Badlands commentary as a participant in the March 2024 "bloodbath" media campaign against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## The "bloodbath" post and deletion

After corporate media outlets and Biden/Harris campaign social media accounts recast a passage of a Trump speech as a threatened "bloodbath," Scarborough posted his own version of the claim on X.[1] He deleted the post after being called out by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], who had responded to the broader coverage with the comment "Legacy media lies."[1] Badlands described the deletion as part of a wave of walkbacks that began almost immediately once the decentralized online response exposed the story's framing.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the episode to argue that the interval between the deployment of a media operation against Trump or the [[truth-community|truth community]] and its complete exposure had collapsed from years to months to minutes, and that the coordination visible on the other side of the exchange indicated Trump and Musk were themselves working in concert.[1]

## Claims about Obama's role in the 2024 pressure campaign

On July 11, 2024, Scarborough said on MSNBC's "Morning Joe" that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s campaign and many Democratic officials believed former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] was quietly working behind the scenes to orchestrate the growing calls for Biden to drop out of the 2024 race.[2] "One thing that we do have to underline here — just so viewers can follow what's going on behind the scenes — is the Biden campaign and many Democratic officials do believe that Barack Obama is quietly working behind the scenes to orchestrate this," he said.[2]

Scarborough added that Biden was "deeply resentful of his treatment under not only the Obama staff but also the way he was pushed aside for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]" in 2016.[2] His co-host and wife [[mika-brzezinski|Mika Brzezinski]] said Obama "has a lot of influence, and there's a lot there."[2] Scarborough himself had defended Biden against calls to bow out, even as former Obama aides became among the president's most vocal critics after his shaky debate performance against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]
