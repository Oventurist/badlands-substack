# Crooked Media

Crooked Media is an American podcast company. It was identified in February 2024 reporting as one of the media holdings in which [[soros-fund-management|Soros Fund Management]] had taken a position, with the fund having earlier acquired a minority stake in the company.[1]

## Context

The Crooked Media stake was cited by Bloomberg alongside Soros Fund Management's participation in the lender group that bought [[vice-media|Vice Media]] out of bankruptcy in 2023 and its position as prospective largest shareholder of the radio conglomerate [[audacy|Audacy Inc.]], as evidence of a pattern of media investments by the firm founded by [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1] Badlands Media commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described the podcasting investment as part of a spread across "information dissemination arms that hit all demographics" ahead of the 2024 US election cycle.[1]
