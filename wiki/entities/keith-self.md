# Keith Self

Keith Self is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas and one of the twenty-one holdouts in the January 2023 speakership election.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speakership fight

Self was among the conservatives aligned with the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]] whose votes forced the [[january-2023-mccarthy-concessions|January 2023 McCarthy concessions]] from [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]].[1] In the ensuing assignments he was placed on the [[house-foreign-affairs-committee|Foreign Affairs Committee]].[1]
