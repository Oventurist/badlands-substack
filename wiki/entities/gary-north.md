# Gary North

**Gary North** is a writer and economic historian cited by Badlands Media in its assessment of banking reform proposals.[1]

North argued that in a [[full-reserve-banking|full reserve]], gold-based banking system there would be no theft, because banks could not create money against deposits.[1] Badlands Media notes, however, that interest would remain: a borrower would still pay roughly $300,000 in interest over a $200,000 mortgage, whether settled in gold coin or in paper fully backed by gold.[1] For that reason the outlet treats interest, rather than money creation itself, as the core grievance of many [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve]] critics.[1]
