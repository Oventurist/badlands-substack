# Ronald Reagan

Ronald Reagan was the 40th President of the United States and, before that, a Hollywood actor. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]], Reagan functions as the historical precedent for the thesis that a president can consciously use the film industry as an instrument of national morale.

The article states that Reagan "took control of Hollywood during his eight years in office," pushing for movies that promoted strong American characters such as Rambo, and that the films of his presidency made the American military seem powerful — inspiring Americans while intimidating foreign adversaries. It notes that the original *Top Gun* was released in 1986, during Reagan's presidency, and describes it as "the biggest military recruiting tool ever created."

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] repeatedly calls [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "Reagan 2.0," clarifying that he means not a continuation of Reagan's efforts but "a vastly improved version — a version that doesn't include compromising with the enemy."
