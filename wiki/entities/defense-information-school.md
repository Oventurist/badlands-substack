# Defense Information School

The Defense Information School (DINFOS) is a Department of Defense training institution located at [[fort-meade|Fort Meade]], Maryland, that "fulfills the Department of Defense's need for an internal corps of professional journalists, broadcasters, and public affairs professionals."[1]

## Curriculum

Members from all branches of the U.S. military, DoD civilians, and international military personnel attend DINFOS for training in public affairs, print journalism, photojournalism, photography, television and radio broadcasting, lithography, equipment maintenance, and various forms of multimedia.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited DINFOS as evidence that Fort Meade functions as a hub of the information war, summarizing its graduates sardonically as "military-grade television characters" and grouping the school with the [[defense-information-systems-agency|Defense Information Systems Agency]] and the [[defense-media-activity|Defense Media Activity]].[1]
