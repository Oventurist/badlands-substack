# Patrick Yoes

Patrick Yoes is the National President of the [[fraternal-order-of-police|Fraternal Order of Police]], the largest police labor organization in the United States.[1]

## Testimony before the House Homeland Security Committee

Yoes testified before the [[house-homeland-security-committee|House Homeland Security Committee]] on July 23, 2024, during its examination of the July 13, 2024 assassination attempt on former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] He appeared alongside Colonel [[christopher-l-paris|Christopher L. Paris]], Commissioner of the [[pennsylvania-state-police|Pennsylvania State Police]], after the FBI and the Department of Homeland Security declined the committee's invitation.[1]

Badlands coverage characterized the session as a repeat of the previous day's disappointment: strongly worded statements from politicians and largely evasive answers from witnesses, who cited the ongoing investigation and the incomplete after-action review as grounds for declining to discuss timelines or the assignment of security resources.[1]
