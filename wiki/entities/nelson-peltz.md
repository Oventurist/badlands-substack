# Nelson Peltz

Nelson Peltz is a billionaire activist investor who in early 2024 waged a high-profile proxy campaign for a seat on the board of [[disney|The Walt Disney Company]].[1]

## Disney proxy fight

By late March 2024, Peltz had accumulated sizable shareholder support for his plan to seize a board seat and a measure of control at Disney at the company's shareholder meeting the following month.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the campaign as one of "twin deployments" that triggered a narrative crisis for the entertainment giant, noting it came immediately on the heels of Peltz's announcement that he would support [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the 2024 election.[1]

## Context

Badlands linked the Peltz campaign to a broader collapse of Disney's political position: a wave of media panic pieces including The Hollywood Reporter's declaration that "[[bob-iger|Bob Iger]]'s Invincible Era Is Over," Disney's settlement with the State of Florida over its special status disputes, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s support for Gina Carano's suit against the company, and the legal campaign by [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]].[1]

## 2024 Disney board defeat

Peltz's proxy campaign against Disney leadership culminated in defeat on April 3, 2024, when shareholders backed the board slate supported by chief executive [[bob-iger|Bob Iger]] and denied seats to Peltz and his ally [[jay-rasulo|Jay Rasulo]], a former Disney chief financial officer.[2] During the contest Peltz pressed the [[walt-disney-company|Walt Disney Company]] to adopt a concrete succession plan and criticized its push to make more diverse movies and television shows.[2] Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described Peltz as a Trump ally and had weighed whether his addition to the board would have amounted to resuscitation rather than controlled demolition of the company; after the loss he concluded the demolition would continue, with Disney left to Iger and institutional holders including [[vanguard-group|Vanguard Group]] and [[blackrock|BlackRock Inc.]].[2]
