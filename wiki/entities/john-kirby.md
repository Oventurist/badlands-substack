# John Kirby

**Adm. John Kirby** is a retired U.S. Navy officer who served as spokesman for the [[national-security-council|National Security Council]] during the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Role in the February 2023 shootdowns

On Friday, February 10, 2023, Kirby fielded reporters' questions after another unidentified "object" was shot down over Alaska.[1] According to [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], Kirby did not voluntarily offer information about the object, disclosing details only after a reporter raised the subject.[1] [[nbc-news|NBC]] host [[chuck-todd|Chuck Todd]] singled out Kirby and the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] for a lack of transparency in his February 12 broadcast, saying Kirby "did not volunteer this information about fighter jets being scrambled to shoot down another object until a reporter in that room asked."[1]

Kirby's handling of the briefings became part of a broader dispute over official candor during the [[2023-north-american-aerial-object-shootdowns|series of North American aerial object shootdowns]], which Badlands Media contributors read as evidence of a coordinated information operation rather than ordinary caution.[1]

## 2024 "handful of vets" email

On the anniversary of 9/11 in September 2024, Kirby — then serving as [[national-security-council|National Security Council]] communications adviser at the [[white-house|White House]] — dismissed the concerns of military veterans critical of the 2021 withdrawal from [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] in a "reply all" email that inadvertently included [[fox-news|Fox News]] Digital.[2] "Obviously no use in responding. A 'handful' of vets indeed and all of one stripe," Kirby wrote in the Wednesday afternoon chain that appeared intended only for White House staffers.[2]

Fox News Digital had earlier asked the White House for comment on criticism from four veterans, including Rep. [[cory-mills|Cory Mills]], R-Fla., who accused Kirby of providing "cover" for the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration's handling of the withdrawal during a Monday press conference.[2] The chain was forwarded to NSC staffers before Kirby replied to all, later telling a Fox reporter, "Clearly, I didn't realize you were on the chain."[2] He sent the message while travelling with President Biden on the 9/11 anniversary.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that while accidental reply-alls are common, the fallout is catastrophic when a professional communicator's slip reaches an adverse party such as a news organization.[2] She characterized Kirby's dismissal of veterans as "of all one stripe" as offensive on any day and exacerbated by the date, urged Republicans to exploit the episode given that a sitting congressman was among the disparaged veterans, and argued that because Kirby speaks for the administration and Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] "is the administration," his insult should be branded onto her campaign.[2]

## Reaction to the Putin-Xi embrace (2024)

As White House national security communications adviser, Kirby responded in May 2024 to the widely circulated embrace between Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] during the final day of Putin's two-day visit to China, in the host city of Harbin.[3] Asked about the rare hug — which Xi appeared to initiate, and which one regional expert noted is incredibly uncommon in Chinese culture, especially for senior officials — Kirby said somewhat sarcastically, "That's nice for them."[3]

He added that it was "no surprise" that Putin and Xi "continue to try to develop this burgeoning relationship," but that officials in both governments "aren't necessarily all that trustful" of each other.[3] "What they have in common is a desire to challenge the international rules-based order, challenge the network of alliances and partnerships that the United States enjoys," Kirby said, adding that the two are trying to "look for ways to bolster each other's national security interests as well."[3]

## Statements on Iran-backed militia attacks (October 2023)

As National Security Council spokesperson, Kirby said on Monday, October 23, 2023 that there was "a very direct connection between these groups" — Iran-aligned militias conducting drone and missile strikes on US bases in Syria and Iraq — and the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps, adding that the United States was "deeply concerned about the potential of any significant escalation of these attacks in the days ahead."[4] He further stated that "Iran's goal is to maintain some plausible deniability here, but we are not going to allow them to do that."[4]

His comments accompanied a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] assessment that Iran-backed groups were planning to intensify attacks on US forces, and reporting that US officials saw "red lights flashing everywhere," with [[iran|Iran]] said to be encouraging rather than explicitly directing the militias by signalling that continued attacks on US or Israeli targets would not cost them weapons resupply.[4]

## Austin hospitalization briefing (January 2024)

At a January 2024 press conference, Kirby — then National Security Council Coordinator for Strategic Communications — was grilled by Fox News reporter [[peter-doocy|Peter Doocy]] over the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s failure to disclose that Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] had been hospitalized at [[walter-reed-national-military-medical-center|Walter Reed]] on December 22, 2023 for prostate cancer surgery.[5] Asked "Why should we believe anything this administration tells us about anything ever again?", Kirby acknowledged that the Pentagon had faced a "challenge to credibility" by failing to quickly reveal the details of Austin's treatment.[5] Doocy pressed further on whether the same lengths might be taken to conceal problems with President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s health.[5]

## Response to the 2024 Israel-Iran intelligence leak

As White House National Security Council spokesman, Kirby said in October 2024 that President Joe Biden was "deeply concerned" about the leak of classified documents outlining the United States' assessment of [[israel|Israel]]'s plan to attack [[iran|Iran]].[6] One of the documents was compiled by the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]] and the other appeared to originate with the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]]; both were marked "top secret," carried the Foreign Government Intelligence marking, and had been shared within the [[five-eyes|Five Eyes]] alliance.[6] Kirby said there was no indication that any additional documents would find their way into the public domain, even as New Zealand's [[government-communications-security-bureau|Government Communications Security Bureau]] came under scrutiny in the investigation.[6]

## Promotion to White House national security communications adviser (February 2024)

In February 2024, Reuters reported that Kirby — President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s top national security spokesperson — was receiving a promotion and an expanded role at the [[white-house|White House]].[7] He took the new title of White House national security communications adviser and was elevated from deputy assistant to the president to assistant to the president, placing him in charge of communications coordination for national security across a variety of agencies.[7] Kirby had previously served as the top spokesperson at both the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and the State Department.[7]

Kirby was to preside over a team separate from the National Security Council's press office, which continued its daily operations under chief spokesperson [[adrienne-watson|Adrienne Watson]].[7] As one of the most visible faces of the Biden White House, Kirby had frequently joined the podium alongside press secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] to brief on national security matters, particularly after the Hamas attacks on Israel on October 7 and Israel's subsequent operations in Gaza; officials said his briefing-room appearances would continue whenever national security issues dominated the day's agenda.[7]

Days after the promotion, Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the "recently-promoted" Kirby told the Monday White House press conference that Biden had not spoken to Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] since his hospitalization, saying he was "not aware of any conversations," and replying "not at all" when asked whether Biden was concerned about Austin's ability to serve.[7]

## June 2023 Hunter Biden briefing walkout

At a White House press briefing in late June 2023, amid revelations that the [[justice-department|Justice Department]] had buried evidence of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s tax crimes and that Hunter had demanded a CCP-linked associate wire funds while he was "sitting here with my father," Kirby refused to answer questions and stormed away from the podium.[8] "I'm not going to comment further on this," he said, after being asked whether a WhatsApp message from Hunter appearing to demand payment from a Chinese businessman contradicted [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s earlier claims that he and his son had never discussed Hunter's business dealings.[8] After Kirby ducked out, press secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] attempted damage control and was bombarded with repeat questions.[8]

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described the briefing as "the perfect physical manifestation of the current state of panic and disarray" in the [[deep-state|Deep State]], writing that Kirby and Jean-Pierre "can barely hold it together in the face of the slightest scrutiny."[8]

## February 2024 Russia sanctions announcement

On the morning of Tuesday, February 20, 2024, Kirby announced that the [[white-house|White House]] would unveil a new "major sanctions" package that Friday "to hold Russia accountable" for the death of Russian opposition politician [[alexei-navalny|Alexei Navalny]].[9] "Whatever story the Russian government decides to tell the world, it's clear that President [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] and his government are responsible for Mr. Navalny's death," Kirby said, adding that the package would be announced "at President [[joe-biden|Biden]]'s direction."[9] He declined to detail its contents but noted the sanctions would also serve to hold Russia accountable for its ongoing war with Ukraine.[9]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued the announcement provided "the illusion of mandate" for sanctions that would not work, pointing to Russia's rising GDP and strengthening currency, and framed the episode as an application of the [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian Dialectic]] following Putin's interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]].[9]

## Claims of Russia–North Korea arms negotiations (August 2023)

On Wednesday, August 30, 2023, Kirby, as [[white-house|White House]] national security spokesman, accused Moscow of seeking North Korean weapons for use in its military operation in Ukraine at a moment when tensions with Pyongyang were mounting.[10] He stopped short of saying there was smoking-gun evidence of arms transfers already accomplished, instead strongly suggesting potential sales in the near future: "We urge the DPRK to cease its arms negotiations with Russia and abide by the public commitments that Pyongyang has made to not provide or sell arms to Russia."[10] Kirby referenced new intelligence which he said showed that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] were "actively advancing" high-level talks for more weapons, and claimed that Russian Defense Minister [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]], while in Pyongyang late the previous month, had been there "to try to convince Pyongyang to sell artillery ammunition to Russia."[10]

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that "the ruling junta in the US wants World War III," and recalled that Kirby had said earlier that year that congressional approval would not affect Ukraine war funding in any way.[10]
