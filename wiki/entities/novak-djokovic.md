# Novak Djokovic

Novak Djokovic is a Serbian professional tennis player who was ranked world No. 1 at the time of the events described here and who became an internationally prominent figure in disputes over COVID-19 vaccination requirements because he is unvaccinated against the disease.[1]

## Withdrawal from Indian Wells (2023)

In March 2023, tournament officials announced that Djokovic had formally withdrawn from the draw for the BNP Paribas Open at Indian Wells on the eve of the draw, a step ESPN reported as an indication that his application for a COVID-19 vaccine waiver to enter the United States had likely failed.[1] Djokovic had applied to the U.S. government the previous month for special permission to play at the ATP Masters events at Indian Wells and Miami.[1]
