# Snoop Dogg

Snoop Dogg is an American rapper who, in January 2024, publicly reversed his long-standing hostility toward [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a shift Badlands Media treated as a culturally significant defection from the corporate media narrative about the MAGA movement.[1]

## 2017 Feud and the "Lavender" Video

Snoop had been embroiled in a feud with Trump for years. In 2017 he released a music video for the song "Lavender," featuring BADBADNOTGOOD, in which he aimed a gun at a clown dressed like Trump and pulled the trigger to reveal a red flag reading "BANG."[1] The video provoked a reaction from the then-president, and Snoop repeatedly took aim at Trump's views in the years that followed.[1]

## 2024 Reversal

Speaking to The Times in an interview published Sunday, January 28, 2024, Snoop said: "Donald Trump? He ain't done nothing wrong to me. He has done only great things for me."[1] He specifically cited Trump's pardon of Michael Harris, co-founder of Death Row Records — Snoop's first label — who had been imprisoned on drug offences, adding: "So I have nothing but love and respect for Donald Trump."[1] Snoop also offered his own definition of MAGA, describing it as the period "in my lifetime" when "young Black men in impoverished areas organised to help their communities and to take care of their own because society basically left them for dead."[1]

## Badlands Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called the turnaround revealing, answering Snoop's definition with "Not society, Snoop. Democrats," and crediting the corporate press with successfully painting MAGA as a return to the Jim Crow South or the 1994 Crime Bill — both, she argued, Democratic legacies.[1] She placed the "Lavender" video alongside Madonna's remarks about blowing up the White House and Kathy Griffin's photo shoot with a model of Trump's severed head, describing all three as grotesque and escalatory but lawfully protected First Amendment expression that no one in MAGA sought to criminalize — a contrast she deployed against later claims that Trump's phrase "peacefully and patriotically" was a dog whistle for violence.[1] She argued that Trump-era measures including the First Step Act, Opportunity Zones and the Platinum Plan were aimed at benefiting Black Americans, and welcomed Snoop's willingness to say so publicly.[1]
