# United States Geological Survey

The **United States Geological Survey** (USGS) is the U.S. federal scientific agency responsible for, among other functions, monitoring seismic activity worldwide and issuing casualty and damage estimates after major earthquakes.[1]

## Role in the 2025 Myanmar earthquake

Following the magnitude 7.7 earthquake that struck central [[myanmar|Myanmar]] on March 28, 2025, the USGS estimated that the death toll could surpass 10,000.[1] The agency issued a **red alert** for estimated fatalities — its highest alert designation, indicating "high casualties and extensive damage."[1] At the time of the estimate, Myanmar's military government had reported at least 144 deaths, a figure expected to climb sharply as rubble was cleared.[1] See [[2025-myanmar-earthquake|2025 Myanmar earthquake]].[1]

## Arkansas lithium assessment (October 2024)

In October 2024 the USGS led a study, published in *Science Advances*, revealing a potentially massive lithium reserve beneath southwestern Arkansas that researchers said far exceeds current worldwide demand.[2] The agency estimated between 5 and 19 million tons of lithium in the region and stated in a press release that, if commercially recoverable, the supply could meet projected 2030 world demand for lithium in car batteries nine times over.[2] The research was conducted jointly with the Arkansas Department of Energy and Environment's Office of the State Geologist and used water testing combined with machine learning to estimate lithium concentrations in brines within the Smackover Formation.[2] See [[arkansas-lithium-discovery|Arkansas lithium discovery]].[2]
