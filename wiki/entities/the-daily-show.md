# The Daily Show

The Daily Show is a late-night satirical news program broadcast on [[comedy-central|Comedy Central]].[1]

## Cancellation after the Butler shooting

Following the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination]] of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at a Butler, Pennsylvania rally on Saturday, July 13, 2024, Comedy Central cancelled the Monday airing of The Daily Show, while [[msnbc|MSNBC]] cancelled the Monday edition of [[morning-joe|Morning Joe]].[1] Trump, 78, had been struck by a bullet in the right ear during the outdoor rally and was rushed from the stage by [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] agents before being treated in hospital for minor injuries; a rally-goer was killed and two others critically injured, and the shooter, 20-year-old [[thomas-matthew-crooks|Thomas Matthew Crooks]], was shot dead by snipers from a rooftop outside the event's security perimeter.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the simultaneous pre-emption of comedy and cable-news programming as a symptom of institutional paralysis, describing the "Deep State Archipelago" as "splintering & collapsing under a web of indecision and informational paralysis."[1] In this reading, the Media Industrial Complex normally deploys narratives in coordination with deeper layers of a criminal insurgency, and the sudden silence of its most reliable voices indicated that its outlets had "been cut off from each other" and "siloed."[1]
