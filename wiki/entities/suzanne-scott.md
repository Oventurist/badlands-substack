# Suzanne Scott

Suzanne Scott is the chief executive of [[fox-news|Fox News]], the cable network at the center of the American conservative media landscape.[1]

## Role in the Tucker Carlson departure

According to reporting excerpted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of April 25, 2023, the decision to part ways with prime-time host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] was made on the evening of Friday, April 21, 2023 by Fox Corporation chief executive [[lachlan-murdoch|Lachlan Murdoch]] and by Scott as chief executive of Fox News, according to a person familiar with the matter.[1] Carlson was informed of the decision on the morning of Monday, April 24, and the network announced the split later that day in a short statement that offered no explanation, saying only that it thanked him "for his service to the network as a host and prior to that as a contributor" and that his last show had been Friday, April 21.[1]

The announcement came within an hour of [[cnn|CNN]]'s firing of [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]], and Carlson — the highest-rated single host at Fox News — did not respond to requests for comment.[1] Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the executive decision exposed the network to its own audience, "kicking a hornet's nest of Carlson supporters" at a moment when the network was already under scrutiny for its call of Arizona for Joe Biden on November 3, 2020 and its settlement with [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]].[1]
