# Kamoto Copper Company

Kamoto Copper Company ("KCC") is a copper and cobalt producer in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] held within the [[glencore|Glencore]] group.[1]

## Ownership history and 2026 transaction

Deals brokered by [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] brought Kamoto under Glencore's control, with Gertler maintaining a stake.[1] Following Gertler's designation under [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]] in December 2017, Kamoto became entangled in frozen assets and foreign court proceedings that stripped mineral rights from the companies concerned.[1]

In February 2026, Glencore disclosed a non-binding Memorandum of Understanding with the [[orion-critical-mineral-consortium|Orion Critical Mineral Consortium]] for a 40% stake in Glencore's DRC interests, covering KCC and [[mutanda-mining|Mutanda Mining]], with a combined implied enterprise value of about $9 billion and continued Glencore management of the assets.[1]
