# Paul Cravath

Paul Drennan Cravath was an American corporate lawyer and the first vice president of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1]

Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" notes that Cravath, like founding CFR president [[john-w-davis|John W. Davis]], represented Morgan properties, part of a pattern in which the Council's earliest officers were drawn from the legal and financial orbit of [[jp-morgan-jr|J.P. Morgan Jr.]][1] The article uses this concentration of Morgan-affiliated men — Cravath, Davis, and first chairman [[russell-leffingwell|Russell Leffingwell]] — to argue that the Council was heavily influenced by Morgan's interests in its formative years.[1]
