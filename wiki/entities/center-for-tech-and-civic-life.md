# Center for Tech and Civic Life

The Center for Tech and Civic Life is a nongovernmental organization funded by [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] that poured more than $400 million into the administration of the 2020 United States election, a role that made it a persistent focus of election-integrity critics.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described it as Zuckerberg's "infamous NGO" and highlighted its connection to Colorado's 2022 Secretary of State race: [[pam-anderson|Pam Anderson]], the organization's Executive Director, took a leave of absence to run and won the Republican primary against [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]], who had carried 61% of delegates at the [[2022-colorado-republican-state-assembly|state assembly]].[1] Anderson subsequently lost the general election.[1] The episode was presented as evidence that the Republican candidate-selection process in Colorado produced nominees aligned with the very election-funding apparatus that grassroots activists had organized against.[1]
