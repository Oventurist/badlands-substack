# Center for Tech and Civic Life

The Center for Tech and Civic Life (CTCL) is an American nonprofit organization that provides technology, training, and funding to local election administrators. It became widely known during the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 United States presidential election]] for distributing large private grants to county and municipal election offices, funding substantially underwritten by [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] and Priscilla Chan. Critics on the right characterized the grants as private money steering the administration of a public election, a controversy that led several states to restrict or ban private election funding in subsequent years.

The organization is invoked in the March 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-143|Badlands Brief]] by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who cites CTCL in objecting to Zuckerberg's appointment to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], asking whether "we are just forgetting about all that" and arguing that "public trust is conditioned on accountability."
