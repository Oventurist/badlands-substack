# Supreme National Security Council

The Supreme National Security Council is [[iran|Iran]]'s highest coordinating body for defense and national security policy, bringing together the country's senior military, intelligence, diplomatic, and executive officials under the authority of the supreme leader.

## Wartime operation, 2026

According to the account given by Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] and reported in the July 22, 2026 [[badlands-brief-1ae|Badlands Brief]], the Council never convened in full at any point during the 2026 war. Araghchi explained that the simultaneous loss of its members — a realistic prospect given that the opening strikes of the conflict killed the supreme leader along with several senior military, security, and political figures — could collapse the system entirely. Meetings that did occur took place in underground tunnels.

Araghchi identified internal penetration by spies as the regime's single greatest vulnerability, and suggested that foreign intelligence activity extended beyond information gathering into "influencing the decision-making process itself." His disclosure of the Council's non-convening, together with details of wartime contingency planning such as the pre-agreed plan to close the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] upon any threat to the supreme leader, drew sharp criticism inside Iran from hardline commentators and outlets affiliated with the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Revolutionary Guards]], who accused him of exposing leadership vulnerabilities to foreign intelligence services.
