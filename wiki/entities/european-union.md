# European Union

The European Union is the supranational political and economic union of European states. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], the EU is treated as one of the principal institutional instruments of the post-Soviet unipolar order.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that the collapse of the Soviet system in the early 1990s "ushered in a unipolar world order with the European Union and [[nafta|NAFTA]], followed soon thereafter by the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]] and the 1999 destruction of [[glass-steagall-act|Glass-Steagall]]." Through these mechanisms, he argues, "the trans-Atlantic at last was converted into a cage of 'post-sovereign nations' that no longer had actual control of their own powers of credit generation," with [[nato|NATO]] subjecting even national militaries to technocratic control.

The EU also figures in the article's account of Ukraine: [[viktor-yanukovych|Viktor Yanukovych]] is described as having declined "to sacrifice Ukraine on the altar of the collapsing European Union," choosing instead the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]] in October 2013.
