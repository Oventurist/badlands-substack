# Lawrence Petroni

Lawrence Petroni is the Acting Chargé d'Affaires at the United States embassy in [[ecuador|Ecuador]].[1]

On the occasion of the opening of the first [[fbi|FBI]] office in Ecuador in March 2026, Petroni said the underlying agreement and the creation of a trusted FBI unit would expand the ability of US and Ecuadorian authorities to "identify, dismantle, and bring to justice those who traffic drugs, launder money, smuggle weapons, and finance terrorism."[1] The embassy described the opening in a statement posted on X as "another strategic and operational milestone in security."[1]
