# Afghanistan

**Afghanistan** is a South-Central Asian state governed since 2021 by the [[taliban|Taliban]]. In Badlands coverage of March 2026 it re-entered US headlines through a State Department designation targeting its detention of foreign nationals.[1]

## Designation as a State Sponsor of Wrongful Detention (2026)

On March 9, 2026, US Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] announced that the United States had designated Afghanistan a "State Sponsor of Wrongful Detention."[1] The [[state-department|State Department]] made the designation in response to the Taliban's detention of US nationals and other foreign citizens on grounds Washington considered unjustified or politically motivated.[1]

The classification is used to identify governments that detain foreign nationals in order to obtain diplomatic leverage or concessions, and it allows the US government to increase diplomatic pressure and coordinate efforts to secure the release of detainees.[1] Afghanistan's designation followed other countries labeled under the same framework.[1]

## In Badlands commentary

The designation was presented in the March 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] under the heading "War on Terror Reloaded?", alongside a Justice Department terrorism prosecution, as part of a cluster of re-emerging war narratives.[1] Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the pattern to the World Economic Forum's 2023 forecast of a coming "polycrisis," arguing that the globalists "always try to follow through" and that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was playing whack-a-mole with successive war narratives.[1] Badlands has elsewhere noted that the Taliban, like other regional actors, had at some point pledged loyalty to Trump.[1]
