# American Thinker

**American Thinker** is an American conservative online publication that carries opinion and blog commentary on politics and culture.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands Media

A November 2023 American Thinker blog item titled "A Trump-Tucker ticket?" was featured as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief.[1] The piece enthusiastically promoted a speculative [[trump-tucker-ticket|Trump-Tucker ticket]] pairing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] for 2024, describing it as a "double down" on American exceptionalism that would put globalist elites and the Deep State on notice.[1]
