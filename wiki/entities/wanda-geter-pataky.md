# Wanda Geter-Pataky

Wanda Geter-Pataky is a Connecticut Democratic Party official who served as vice chair of the Bridgeport Democratic Town Committee and became the central figure in the ballot-fraud dispute that led a judge to overturn the city's 2023 Democratic mayoral primary.[1]

## Bridgeport primary controversy

Video footage that emerged in the autumn of 2023 appeared to show Geter-Pataky depositing multiple ballots into an absentee drop box, conduct described in the case as ballot fraud.[1] She invoked her Fifth Amendment right against self-incrimination rather than testify about the footage.[1] On the basis of 180 pieces of evidence submitted by counsel for mayoral candidate [[john-gomes|John Gomes]], Judge [[william-clark-connecticut|William Clark]] issued a 37-page ruling ordering a new Democratic primary.[1] The judge wrote that the videos "are shocking to the court and should be shocking to all the parties," and noted that incumbent Mayor [[joe-ganim|Joe Ganim]] was "correct to be 'shocked'" at the clips shown to him on the witness stand.[1]

Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] published in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the Bridgeport case as evidence that [[ballot-harvesting|ballot harvesting]] schemes fail once the public is watching, and urged the America First movement to prioritize election integrity ahead of 2024.[1]
