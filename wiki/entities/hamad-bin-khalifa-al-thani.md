# Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani

Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani was the former Emir of Qatar and the father of the current Emir, [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani]].[1] He died on July 12, 2026, at the age of 74, one of several notable deaths reported in the same news cycle as that of Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]].[1]

## Reign and abdication

Hamad's reign over Qatar lasted from June 27, 1995 until June 25, 2013.[1] He ended it not through death or deposition but by abdication: in a brief televised address he handed the throne to his son Tamim.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that this abdication was later mirrored in the Gulf when, four years afterward, Saudi King Salman likewise stepped aside in favor of his son Mohammed — a pattern of generational handovers among Gulf monarchies treated in Badlands analysis as significant rather than coincidental.[1]

## Death and context

Hamad's death was reported alongside Graham's and alongside that of actor [[sam-neill|Sam Neill]], prompting the observation in the July 13, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] that "the Grim Reaper was busy yesterday."[1] The clustering of prominent deaths across politics, Gulf royalty and entertainment in a single 24-hour period formed part of the edition's framing of an unusually strange news cycle.[1]
