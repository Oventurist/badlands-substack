# Middle East Council on Global Affairs

The Middle East Council on Global Affairs is a think tank described by Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as [[deep-state|Deep State]]-aligned.[1]

## Saudi–Iranian détente publication

Nine days before the Iran–Israel conflict began in February 2026, the council published an article asserting that a détente with both [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] was a strategic imperative for [[iran|Iran]].[1] The commentary noted that the piece was well-sourced, citing June 2025 remarks by Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] during the [[twelve-day-war|Twelve Day War]] that "Saudi Arabia stands with its brothers in Iran and will spare no effort to support them" and that "the entire Islamic world is united in backing Iran."[1]

The publication's timing was presented as evidence of foreknowledge in an argument that subsequent drone attacks on the UAE and Saudi Arabia were false-flag operations designed to prevent Gulf–Iranian rapprochement.[1]
