# Middle East Council on Global Affairs

The Middle East Council on Global Affairs is a think tank focused on Middle Eastern geopolitics, described by Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as "Deep State-aligned."

## The Saudi-Iranian detente article

According to the May 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-5a2|Badlands Brief]], the Council published an article asserting that a detente with both [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] was a strategic priority for [[iran|Iran]] — published, in the commentator's telling, just nine days before the Iran-Israel conflict began in February 2026. The commentary cites the article as evidence that regional powers were moving toward rapprochement before the drone attack on the [[barakah-nuclear-power-plant|Barakah nuclear power plant]] on May 17, 2026, which it presents as a possible provocation intended to prevent that realignment.
