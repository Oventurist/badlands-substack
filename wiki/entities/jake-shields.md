# Jake Shields

Jake Shields is a former UFC fighter and one-time world title contender who became a prominent public critic of transgender ideology.[1]

## 2023 challenge

On April 20, 2023, Shields posted a challenge on Twitter: "Since trans men are real men I would like to challenge the 10 toughest trans men in the world to a fight I fight them with no training camp and no rest between each fight," adding, "Let's go, alphabet people get your 10 best and prove me wrong."[1] The challenge was framed as a test of the claim that transgender men are equivalent to biological men.[1]

Transgender wrestler and MMA competitor [[mack-beggs|Mack Beggs]] of Texas accepted, but proposed altering the terms to a single one-on-one bout rather than ten consecutive fights, and vowed to defeat Shields.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the episode to argue that individual combat sports expose real merit in a way team sports do not, since a single competitor cannot mask reduced effort behind teammates.[1] He wrote that in the octagon "it's just you, your training, your heart, your will, and the field," making such athletes well suited to identifying and confronting "subversive cons and deceptive social influences."[1] Deschamps described Shields as using the octagon as a symbolic stage for invalidating what he called the falsehoods of the transgender movement, and argued that open competition — like open debate — is a mechanism the deep state avoids precisely because "on the octagon of truth, the weapons of liars crumble to dust."[1]
