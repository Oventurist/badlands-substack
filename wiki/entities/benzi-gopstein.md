# Benzi Gopstein

**Benzi Gopstein** is the leader of the Israeli [[lehava|Lehava]] movement. He was arrested in 2015 for incitement to violence after calling for all Christians to be expelled from Israel and for churches to be burned.[1] His defense attorney at the time was [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], later Israel's National Security Minister.[1]

## 2026 viral video misattribution

In March 2026 a video circulated widely online that commentators described as showing Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir calling for increased Islamic migration into and takeover of the West.[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that the speaker in the clip was in fact Gopstein, with Ben Gvir standing in the background — using the misattribution to underscore the connection between Ben Gvir and Lehava's anti-Christian activism.[1]

The episode was presented within a wider argument that [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionist]] supporters of Israel misunderstand the religious commitments of the movements they align with, including those surrounding the [[third-temple|Third Temple]].[1]
