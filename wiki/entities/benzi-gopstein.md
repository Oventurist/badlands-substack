# Benzi Gopstein

Benzi Gopstein is the leader of the Israeli [[lehava|Lehava]] movement.[1] He was arrested in 2015 for inciting violence after calling for all Christians to be expelled from Israel and all churches to be burned; his defense attorney at the time was [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], who later became Israel's National Security Minister.[1]

In March 2026 a viral video widely attributed to Ben Gvir — purportedly calling for more Islamic migration and takeover in the West — was identified by Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as actually showing Gopstein speaking, with Ben Gvir standing in the background.[1] The writer used the misattribution to ask whether Christians understand with whom they are joining forces in Jerusalem.[1]

## Cited as Ben-Gvir's mentor (2024 coverage)

Badlands Media has repeatedly identified Gopstein as the mentor of Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], noting that Gopstein drew incitement charges in 2015 after calling for churches to be burned and for all Christians to be expelled from [[israel|Israel]].[2] The outlet invoked this record in August 2024 while arguing that Ben-Gvir's call to build a synagogue atop the [[al-aqsa-mosque|Al-Aqsa Mosque]] was the predictable outgrowth of a Jewish supremacist current hostile to Muslims and Christians alike.[2]
