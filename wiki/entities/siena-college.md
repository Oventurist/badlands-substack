# Siena College

Siena College is a New York institution whose research institute conducts political polling, most prominently in partnership with [[new-york-times|The New York Times]].[1]

## April 2024 poll on views of the Trump presidency

In April 2024 a New York Times/Siena College poll found that views of [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]]'s presidency had become more positive since he left office, bolstering his case for election and posing a risk to President [[joe-biden|Biden]]'s strategy of casting his opponent as unfit for the presidency.[1] While memories of what the Times described as Trump's tumultuous and chaotic administration had not significantly faded, many voters reported a rosier picture of his handling of the economy, immigration and maintaining law and order.[1]

Ahead of the 2020 election only 39 percent of voters said the country was better off after Trump took office; looking back in 2024, nearly half said he had improved things during his time as president.[1] The poll's findings underscored how a segment of voters had changed their minds about the Trump era, recalling those years as a time of economic prosperity and strong national security.[1] The shift came even as Trump faced dozens of felony counts and appeared in a New York courtroom on Monday, April 15, 2024 for jury selection in one of his four criminal trials.[1]
