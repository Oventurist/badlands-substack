# Mike Lindell

Mike Lindell is an American businessman, founder of MyPillow, and one of the most prominent private funders and promoters of efforts to investigate the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]].

The June 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2c0|Badlands Brief]] notes that attorney [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]] "spent the time between Trump Administrations as Mike Lindell's attorney, and he went everywhere the evidence was." [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] recalls Lindell's "unfortunate photo op" of January 15, 2021, in which Olsen's name was visible on papers carried under Lindell's arm outside the White House, and writes that she had long assumed this was "Olsen's origin story for how he got in the election fight." She revises that assumption in light of [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]]'s account that Olsen had already committed in December 2020 to taking election fraud "to the firing squad," and remarks that she does not recall where Lindell obtained those papers or who put Olsen's name on them — something she says "seems important."
