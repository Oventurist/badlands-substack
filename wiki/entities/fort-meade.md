# Fort Meade

Fort Meade is a United States Army installation in Maryland that served as the site of the classified [[project-stargate|Project Stargate]] remote-viewing program and that hosts a dense cluster of intelligence and information-warfare organizations.[1]

## Project Stargate

Project Stargate, the secret US Army unit established in 1977 to study anomalous phenomena including remote viewing, telepathy, and psychokinesis, was conducted at Fort Meade, recruiting men and women who claimed extrasensory perception to help uncover military and domestic intelligence secrets.[1]

## Intelligence and information-warfare tenants

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry noted that Fort Meade is home to the [[national-security-agency|NSA]] and to the combatant command [[us-cyber-command|CYBERCOM]], which plays a central role in Jon Herold's Devolution series.[1] The post also houses the Military Intelligence Civilian Excepted Career Program, described as "a Department of the Army Headquarters career management program administered by the US Army Field Support Center of the US Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM)" and tasked with "recruiting, training and developing a dedicated civilian intelligence workforce to conduct sensitive intelligence and counterintelligence operations missions worldwide."[1] The commentator linked this civilian-military intelligence workforce to the Department of Defense counterinsurgency (COIN) manual's insistence that successful counterinsurgency requires a civilian-military alliance.[1]

Other Fort Meade tenants highlighted include the [[defense-information-systems-agency|Defense Information Systems Agency]], the [[defense-information-school|Defense Information School]], and the [[defense-media-activity|Defense Media Activity]].[1] In 2009 the Army Corps of Engineers spent over $56 million constructing a 185,000-square-foot television production studio at Fort Meade, a fact the commentary connected to a Q drop referencing "Red Castle."[1]
