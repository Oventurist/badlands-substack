# Alexis Bloom

Alexis Bloom is the director of [[the-bibi-files|*The Bibi Files*]], the documentary assembled around leaked police interrogation videos of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] The film was produced by [[alex-gibney|Alex Gibney]] and premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2024.[1]

Bloom began working on the project before October 7, 2023; the leaked interrogation videos were subsequently provided to Gibney by a source and folded into the film.[1] The resulting documentary, described in press coverage as "urgent and incendiary," builds what reviewers called a rigorous and damning case that Netanyahu is prolonging the war in Gaza to avoid possible prison time stemming from corruption charges.[1]
