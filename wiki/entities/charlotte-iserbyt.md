# Charlotte Iserbyt

**Charlotte Iserbyt** is described in the opening installment of the Badlands Media series [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] as a former member of the Board of Education under President Ronald Reagan and the author of [[the-deliberate-dumbing-down-of-america|*The Deliberate Dumbing Down of America*]].[1]

## Claims attributed to her work

According to the essay, Iserbyt's book details the true origin of the American school curriculum, and what she learned was that Soviet mind control methods had been used on American children nationwide since at least the 1980s.[1] The article emphasises that she backs up her claims with hundreds of educational and official sources.[1]

## Role in the argument

Iserbyt's work is invoked in a section arguing that enormous resources are expended every day to keep a worldwide mass mind control apparatus functional.[1] Alongside the claim that roughly $365 billion was spent on advertising in America during the first half of 2023 — money the author characterises as spent on manipulating human consciousness — the educational system is presented as a second major arena in which mass consciousness is moulded.[1] The essay concludes from the scale of this expenditure that the cost of keeping mankind dumbed down is gargantuan, and that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] would not hide unless human beings genuinely had the power to defeat it.[1]
