# Qian Xuesen

Qian Xuesen was the space pioneer credited as the founding figure of China's space program.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that Premier [[zhou-enlai|Zhou Enlai]] ensured Qian received state support to create a Chinese space program, which began in earnest with Project 581 in 1958, an effort aimed at putting a satellite into orbit.[1] Although the program suffered many setbacks through the 1960s to the 1980s, Ehret notes that the Chinese "resiliently pushed forward," eventually becoming the third nation to send astronauts into space through their own efforts.[1] That trajectory culminated decades later in China's far-side lunar landings and its own orbital outpost, the [[tiangong-space-station|Tiangong]] station.[1]
