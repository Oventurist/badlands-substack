# Vanguard

**Vanguard** is one of the three large American asset managers — with [[blackrock|BlackRock]] and [[state-street|State Street]] — that the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" identifies as interlocked with [[ivy-league|Ivy League]] endowment capital.[1]

The article says Ivy endowments are "interlocked with BlackRock, Vanguard, and State Street portfolios," and that Vanguard specifically "benefits from the flow of academic grant programs into its passive index funds."[1] All three firms are described as feeding back into policy think tanks such as the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], the [[brookings-institution|Brookings Institution]], the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] and the [[group-of-thirty|Group of Thirty]], where Ivy-trained economists are said to dictate global economic narratives.[1]

In the article's collapse narrative, Vanguard is grouped with BlackRock and State Street as asset managers that "began walking back their most aggressive ideological stances," erasing ESG language, hiding DEI scorecards and rewriting DEI-linked credit clauses — a movement the author characterizes not as innovation but retreat.[1]
