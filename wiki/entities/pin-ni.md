# Pin Ni

Pin Ni is an executive at the Chinese automotive conglomerate Wanxiang Group and the head of the company's American subsidiary.[1] He was reported in August 2025 to be a member of the Chinese Communist Party.[1]

## Donations to Abigail Spanberger

Campaign finance records showed that Ni gave $50,000 in donations to the campaign of Virginia's Democratic gubernatorial candidate [[abigail-spanberger|Abigail Spanberger]] in April and May of 2025.[1] The Washington Free Beacon reported that the funding was legally perplexing: Virginia law forbids foreign nationals from making political contributions, while Chinese law typically bars CCP members from holding foreign citizenship or permanent residency.[1]
