# James Comer

James Comer is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Kentucky and chairman of the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|House Committee on Oversight and Accountability]].

In the Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026, Comer is identified as one of three Republican legislators leading investigations into the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal]], alongside Senate Homeland Security Chairman [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] and House Majority Whip [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]]. The Brief reports that these investigators describe the fraud as a "coordinated effort" and a "scam" intended to manipulate elections, congressional apportionment and federal funding flows in Democratic-run states, and that the probes were expanding beyond Minnesota to California, New York and Illinois while the Trump administration froze funds to high-risk programs.


## Subpoena of Pam Bondi (March 2026)

According to the March 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, Comer formally subpoenaed Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] on a Tuesday in March 2026, requiring her to appear for a deposition on April 14 regarding the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s handling of the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] investigation and compliance with the [[epstein-files-transparency-act|Epstein Files Transparency Act]]. The subpoena followed a committee vote prompted by Rep. [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]] and passed with every Democrat on the panel plus several Republicans.

Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the move as a "newsmaking political stunt" launched days after Bondi had offered to brief the committee, arguing it restored the Epstein story to the news cycle before the midterms and supplied lawmakers with "soundbites for campaign ads."
