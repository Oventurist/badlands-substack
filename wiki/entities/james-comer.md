# James Comer

James Comer is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Kentucky and chairman of the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|House Committee on Oversight and Accountability]].

In the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026, Comer is identified as one of three Republican legislators leading investigations into the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal]], alongside Senate Homeland Security Chairman [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] and House Majority Whip [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]]. The Brief reports that these investigators describe the fraud as a "coordinated effort" and a "scam" intended to manipulate elections, congressional apportionment and federal funding flows in Democratic-run states, and that the probes were expanding beyond Minnesota to California, New York and Illinois while the Trump administration froze funds to high-risk programs.
