# Ivy Club

The **Ivy Club** is an eating club at [[princeton-university|Princeton University]] described in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the institution through which Princeton grooms central bankers and members of the "financial aristocracy."[1]

According to the article, the Ivy Club "has birthed generations of central bankers," including former [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Chair [[ben-bernanke|Ben Bernanke]], and its onward pathway connects to the [[group-of-thirty|Group of Thirty]], the [[brookings-institution|Brookings Institution]] and the [[international-monetary-fund|International Monetary Fund]].[1] It is paired with the [[whig-cliosophic-society|Whig-Cliosophic Society]], which the article assigns the complementary function of training rhetorical masters and global NGO liaisons.[1] Through this channel Princeton is said to operate as "the philosophical core of financial globalization," carrying Fed doctrine outward into global monetary control within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
