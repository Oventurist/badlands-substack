# Department of War

The Department of War (DOW) is the name used in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration for the American military establishment historically and legally organized as the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] and headquartered at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]. The February 12, 2026 [[badlands-brief-405|Badlands Brief]] refers to the department under this name in reporting a counter-drone operation on the southern border.

## El Paso counter-drone operation

According to the Brief, the administration confirmed that cartel drones entered US airspace near El Paso, Texas, and were disabled through joint action by the [[federal-aviation-administration|Federal Aviation Administration]] and the Department of War. Transportation Secretary [[sean-duffy|Sean Duffy]] announced that "the FAA and DOW acted swiftly to address a cartel drone incursion. The threat has been neutralized, and there is no danger to commercial travel in the region."

The FAA had closed airspace above El Paso up to 18,000 feet within a ten-mile radius, initially for ten days, citing "special security reasons"; the restrictions were lifted Wednesday night. *[[business-insider|Business Insider]]* cited anonymous officials saying the drones were disabled using counter-drone measures, but the department did not publicly specify the technology employed. Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] denied any information about drone activity on the border, while Representative [[tony-gonzales|Tony Gonzales]] described such incursions as routine.

In the accompanying commentary, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the operation alongside the military buildup in the eastern Mediterranean, speculating that the latter may be a head-fake and that the department's real focus is cartel infrastructure in Mexico.
