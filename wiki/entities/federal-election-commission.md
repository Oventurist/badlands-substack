# Federal Election Commission

The Federal Election Commission (FEC) is the federal regulator charged with administering and enforcing the [[federal-election-campaign-act|Federal Election Campaign Act]] of 1971, the statute governing the financing of federal campaigns.[1]

The Commission was the respondent in [[nrsc-v-fec|NRSC v. FEC]], the December 2025 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] case testing the constitutionality of caps on coordinated spending between party committees and their candidates.[1] Republican plaintiffs, including the [[national-republican-senatorial-committee|NRSC]], the NRCC and Vice President [[jd-vance|J.D. Vance]], contended the FEC-enforced limits abridge the First Amendment, while liberal justices warned that eliminating them would invite corruption.[1]
