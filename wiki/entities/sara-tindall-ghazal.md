# Sara Tindall Ghazal

Sara Tindall Ghazal is the Democratic Party representative on the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]], the body responsible for setting rules governing the administration and certification of elections in Georgia.[1]

## Opposition to the 2024 certification-inquiry rule

In May 2024, Ghazal publicly objected as the State Election Board began drafting a new rule requiring a "reasonable inquiry" before county boards certify Georgia election results.[1] The rule was written at the request of a Republican county election board member who had refused to sign off on the state's 2024 presidential primary.[1]

Speaking at the board's Wednesday, May 8, 2024 meeting, Ghazal warned that if certification rules were changed, local election board members could invoke them as justification for voting against approving the results of upcoming elections.[1] "The risk is using pretextual reasons to fail to certify when folks are not pleased with the results," she said. "That is my concern — using excuses to fail to certify."[1]

Three Republican board members from Fulton and Gwinnett counties had voted against certifying the presidential primary results, saying they wanted more detailed documentation beyond a broad summary of votes cast; both [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had easily won their respective primaries.[1]
