# Palestinian Islamic Jihad

Palestinian Islamic Jihad (PIJ) is a Palestinian militant organization operating from the [[gaza|Gaza Strip]]. It took part alongside [[hamas|Hamas]] in the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 assault]] on southern Israel.[1]

Two days after the attack, as reported in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], town-by-town and farm-by-farm fighting was still underway, with Hamas and PIJ reportedly controlling swathes of southern territory and even military outposts inside Israel.[1] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] warned Gaza residents to leave and pledged that the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] would use all its strength to destroy Hamas, while his cabinet contemplated a high-risk rescue operation and potential ground incursion.[1]

PIJ is among the Iran-backed Palestinian factions in the region; [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] reporting cited by the Brief described meetings in [[beirut|Beirut]] attended by [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|IRGC]] officers and representatives of four Iran-backed militant groups, including Hamas and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1]

## Attribution of the al-Ahli hospital blast (October 2023)

Israel publicly attributed the October 17, 2023 [[al-ahli-baptist-hospital-explosion|al-Ahli Baptist Hospital explosion]] in Gaza to Palestinian militants, specifically Palestinian Islamic Jihad, rather than to an Israeli strike.[2] The attribution did little to slow the international reaction, which included worldwide protests, riots outside the United States Embassy in [[beirut|Beirut]] and the collapse of the Jordan leg of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s Middle East trip.[2]
