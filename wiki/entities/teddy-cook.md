# Teddy Cook

Teddy Cook is an Australian activist at the center of the 2024 takedown order that led [[x|X]] to sue Australia's [[esafety-commissioner|eSafety Commissioner]].[1]

Cook, 45, is a female-to-male trans man who has advocated for taxpayer-funded surgeries for all transgender Australians, and was controversially appointed to a [[world-health-organization|World Health Organisation]] expert panel.[1]

## The takedown dispute

Canadian activist [[chris-elston|Chris Elston]] wrote a post that misgendered Cook and made remarks described as "disparaging."[1] Australia's eSafety Commissioner ordered the post removed, threatening X with an $800,000 fine; X geo-blocked the post in Australia, after which it was re-shared in a different format and viewed hundreds of thousands of times.[1] X subsequently launched a legal challenge against the commissioner on free-speech grounds.[1]
