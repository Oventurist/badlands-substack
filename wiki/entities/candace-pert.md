# Candace Pert

**Dr. Candace Pert** is quoted in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites her line, "Your body is your subconscious mind," and applies it to the reader: "And your body is now coherent. Aligned. A beacon."[1]

The quotation closes the essay's section on resonance and leadership, following its citations of [[joe-dispenza|Joe Dispenza]] and [[bruce-greyson|Bruce Greyson]], and precedes the acknowledgement that off-centre days persist but that [[coherence|coherence]] has changed the reader's relationship to "the wobble" — pausing, feeling, recalibrating.[1]
