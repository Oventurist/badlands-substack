# Edwin Wilson

Edwin Wilson was a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operative who specialized in selling weapons.[1]

He was one of five men named in the 1983 report of the Australian Joint Task Force on Drug Trafficking, which told Parliament that [[ted-shackley]], [[richard-secord]], [[thomas-clines]], [[rafael-quintero]] and Wilson had backgrounds "relevant to a proper understanding of the activities of the [[nugan-hand-bank|Nugan Hand]] group and the people associated with that group."[1]

Wilson later became expendable when the public learned that he had sold Muammar Gaddafi explosives and was training Libyan terrorists with Green Berets.[1] He spent 25 years in prison before gaining access to CIA documents through the Freedom of Information Act, with which he proved he had been working for the agency; he was then released from prison.[1] Joe Lange presents his case as the exception that illustrates the rule of the untouchable/expendable divide within the [[shadow-government]] he describes.[1]
