# Uvira

Uvira is a city in eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] situated near strategic border and transport corridors.[1]

In early January 2026, fighting intensified near Uvira as [[m23-rebels|M23]] rebels tightened control over border routes, with clashes reported between Congolese government forces and rebel fighters near strategic transport corridors — developments that raised concerns about cross-border movement and humanitarian access.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reported that M23 had moved south from Bukavu to take Uvira and was subsequently expanding into North Kivu, attacking Congolese military bases.[1]

## Uvira offensive and the mineral trade (December 2025)

In December 2025 [[m23|M23]] fighters seized the city of [[uvira|Uvira]] in South Kivu without a fight, despite a high-profile US-brokered peace agreement signed the previous week.[2] The Congolese government reported more than 100 people killed and over 200,000 newly displaced in a "generalized offensive" on the Kamanyola–Uvira axis conducted by [[rwanda|Rwanda]]'s Defense Forces alongside the rebels, and said tactical weapons including suicide drones had been used, causing civilian casualties and trapping populations.[2]

Badlands analysis situated the capture in a campaign that began with Goma in December 2024 and Bukavu in February 2025, arguing the objective is control of the facilities where raw ore from the region's rare earth deposits — an estimated $24 trillion from this area alone — is refined for sale to Fortune 500 companies.[2] [[apple|Apple]] is being sued by the DR Congo government in a civil action over its role in the trade.[2] [[burundi|Burundi]], a rival of Rwanda and an ally of Kinshasa, was described as forming with the DRC the principal opposition to [[paul-kagame|Paul Kagame]]'s government.[2]
