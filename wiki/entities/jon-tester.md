# Jon Tester

Jon Tester is a Democratic United States Senator from Montana who, during the 2024 election cycle, was among his party's most vulnerable incumbents.[1]

## Draft registration vote pressure, 2024

In June 2024, Senate Democrats attached language to the annual defense authorization bill requiring women to register for the military draft.[1] Conservatives led by Senator [[roger-wicker|Roger Wicker]] (R-Miss.) were expected to force a vote to remove the provision — a vote reporting described as politically tough for Tester and for fellow at-risk Democrat [[jacky-rosen|Jacky Rosen]] of Nevada.[1] The controversy complicated the chances of moving the defense bill on the Senate floor before Election Day.[1]

The episode fit a broader pattern noted in mid-2024 political coverage, in which the Democratic Party's most vulnerable Senate incumbents were nonetheless running ahead of their rivals in key battleground states even as President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] struggled at the top of the ticket.[1]
