# King Salman

Salman is the King of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the father of Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]].[1]

Together with his son he made the moves that deposed the would-be Crown Prince [[muhammad-bin-nayef|Nayef]] and the prince then in charge of the Saudi military, both cousins of MBS, who were arrested and stripped of all powers; Nayef was charged with treason and later accused of plotting to murder the King.[1] MBS was formally coronated Crown Prince on November 4, 2017.[1]

The article stresses the closeness of father and son: where MBS's young relatives embraced an elitist, globetrotting lifestyle, MBS instead shadowed his father to learn about Arabia, and the deaths of two half-brothers between 2001 and 2002 brought the two men closer together, inspiring in MBS a deep love of country and altruism toward his people.[1]

## Health, May 2024

In May 2024 the Saudi state news agency reported that King Salman would undergo treatment at Al Salam Palace in Jeddah for a lung inflammation, hours after he underwent medical tests.[2] Citing the royal court, the agency said the 88-year-old king would be treated with antibiotics until the inflammation subsided; earlier that Sunday he had undergone medical tests at the royal clinics due to "high temperature and joint pain," and he had last been admitted to hospital in April for a routine checkup.[2] Because of the king's health issue, Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], the de facto Saudi leader, postponed a visit to Japan that had been scheduled to begin that Monday, as confirmed by Japan's Chief Cabinet Secretary Yoshimasa Hayashi.[2]

## Career and reputation

Salman is the 25th son of the founder of Saudi Arabia, Abdulaziz bin Abdul Rahman Al Saud.[2] In 1953 his father appointed him deputy governor of Riyadh, then a small town in the middle of the desert; he governed the region until becoming Crown Prince in 2011, a period during which Riyadh was transformed from a backwater village into one of the world's great cities.[2] Because Riyadh is the ancestral hometown of the Saudi family, the governorship also carried the duty of punishing relatives who disgraced the family name, and Salman was a diligent enforcer willing to imprison royals who financially preyed on the Saudi people — a record that made him popular with commoners and admired by regional and global leaders, though plenty of corruption went unpunished, leading to the [[saudi-purge|Royal Purge of 2017]].[2]

Badlands commentary describes Salman as a notably strict father who made his children read one book a week and quizzed them on it, teaching diligence and punctuality; Mohammed bin Salman chose to attend school in Riyadh rather than European boarding schools in order to stay close to him.[2] The commentary anticipates that Salman's legacy will be as the great reformer who "drove out the zealots, rounded up the traitors, and liberated the people from the tyranny of radical Wahhabi Islam."[2] He is also credited as the host who gave [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] the [[sword-dance|Sword Dance]], the seven-year anniversary of which fell in the same week.[2]

## September 2015 White House visit

King Salman traveled to Washington in September 2015, less than eight months into his reign, to discuss the recently signed Iran Nuclear Deal, accompanied by his son [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], then a 30-year-old defense minister.[3] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that nobody expected the king's young son to play lead negotiator, but MBS spoke out of turn and clashed directly with President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] over the deal and over Wahhabism.[3]
