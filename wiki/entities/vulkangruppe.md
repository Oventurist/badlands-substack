# Vulkangruppe

**Vulkangruppe** ("Volcano Group") is a far-left militant environmental group in Germany that claimed responsibility for an attack on the country's electricity infrastructure on March 5, 2024, paralyzing vehicle production at [[tesla|Tesla]]'s European Gigafactory near Berlin.[1]

Police in the state of Brandenburg said someone set fire to a nearby high-voltage transmission tower, causing a blaze that cut electricity to the Gigafactory and to more than 60,000 people in the surrounding area.[1] A Tesla spokesperson confirmed that the grid attack caused a power outage at the plant, halting production, and that the site was evacuated.[1]

In a roughly 2,500-word statement, the group wrote: "We sabotaged Tesla today. Because Tesla in Grünau eats up earth, resources, people, labor and spits out 6,000 SUVs, killing machines and monster trucks per week. Our gift for March 8th is to shut down Tesla," describing the destruction of the Gigafactory and the "sawing off" of "technofascists" like "Elend Musk" as a step toward liberation from patriarchy.[1]

Tesla chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] responded on X: "These are either the dumbest eco-terrorists on Earth or they're puppets of those who don't have good environmental goals. Stopping production of electric vehicles, rather than fossil fuel vehicles, ist extrem dumm."[1]
