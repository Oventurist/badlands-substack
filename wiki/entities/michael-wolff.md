# Michael Wolff

Michael Wolff is an American journalist and author who became the defendant in a $1 billion defamation action brought by First Lady [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]], and the target of a July 2026 motion for sanctions arising from his unsuccessful attempt to block that suit.[1]

## The Epstein claims and defamation suit

Wolff claimed that Melania Trump was heavily involved in the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] scandal.[1] Her legal team sent a pre-action notice demanding a retraction; Wolff ignored the notice and instead filed his own lawsuit seeking to head off her defamation claim.[1] A federal judge dismissed Wolff's case earlier in 2026, describing it as a "contorted" effort and stating that it was "not how the federal courts work."[1]

## Motion for sanctions

On Tuesday, July 21, 2026, Melania Trump filed a motion for sanctions against Wolff.[1] Her legal team argued that sanctions were warranted because Wolff had manufactured a jurisdictional dispute in order to keep the case in New York state court, used litigation for improper purposes, and advanced frivolous claims lacking evidentiary support.[1] The First Lady sought costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, arguing that Wolff's lawsuit functioned as a fundraising drive and political spectacle.[1] The filing stated that "Plaintiff and his counsel asserted a legally frivolous claim, propped it up with factual contentions that lacked any evidentiary support, and used them to run a political and commercial campaign."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] had used the case on The Daily Herold as a reference point for the future of frivolous lawfare, and suggested that the First Lady's filing may amount to a playbook for punishing those who weaponize the courts or use "litigation for improper purposes."[1]
