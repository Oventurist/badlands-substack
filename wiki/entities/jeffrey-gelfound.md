# Jeffrey Gelfound

Jeffrey Gelfound is a tax accountant with Edward White & Company who was hired by [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and later became a cooperating witness in the Internal Revenue Service criminal probe of the first son.[1]

## Role in the Hunter Biden tax investigation

According to once-secret IRS memos made public by [[congress|Congress]] and reviewed by Just the News, Gelfound was engaged to help Hunter Biden clean up a trail of delinquent taxes as [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] marched toward the presidency in 2020 on a promise to force the wealthy to pay their "fair share."[1] IRS agents subsequently determined that Hunter Biden was continuing to allegedly misrepresent his income and deductions to the very accountant he had hired to help.[1]

The documents indicate Gelfound cooperated with the IRS criminal investigation and was regarded as likely to be a key witness should Hunter Biden be charged with tax crimes by Special Counsel [[david-weiss|David Weiss]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Gelfound's testimony across multiple meetings with federal investigators "paints a detailed picture of the influence-peddling schemes in which the Biden Crime Family was engaged," particularly when combined with the Hunter Biden laptop, the testimony of business partner [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]], Suspicious Activity Reports from Hunter's banks, and "loan repayment" checks written directly to Joe Biden by family members.[1]
