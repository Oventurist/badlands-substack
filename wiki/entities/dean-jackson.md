# Dean Jackson

Dean Jackson is a disinformation researcher named in Badlands Media coverage among the "censorship industry insiders" who took part in a panel on the state of the field following the Trump administration's cuts to U.S. government information-control programs.[1]

## Affiliations

The article links Jackson to the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace|Carnegie Endowment]] and the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]], both of which it characterizes as CIA cut-outs.[1] He appeared alongside [[rebekah-tromble|Rebekah Tromble]] and [[katie-harbath|Katie Harbath]], the latter tied to the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]] and the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]].[1]

## Context

The panel's central theme, as reported, was that with U.S. federal support for counter-disinformation work being withdrawn by [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s [[state-department|State Department]] and allied officials, the European Union's [[digital-services-act|Digital Services Act]] had become the field's principal remaining source of leverage over online platforms.[1]
