# Paul Kessler

Paul Kessler was a 69-year-old Jewish man who died on November 6, 2023 after sustaining a head injury during an altercation at duelling demonstrations in Thousand Oaks, California, roughly 40 miles northwest of Los Angeles.[1] He had been demonstrating in support of Israel on November 5 when he became involved in a confrontation with a counter-protester attending a pro-Palestinian event nearby.[1]

## The incident

The altercation occurred at the intersection of Westlake Boulevard and Thousand Oaks Boulevard in Ventura County. Several citizens called 9-1-1 at about 3:20 p.m. to report a battery, and deputies from the Ventura County Sheriff's Office arrived to find Kessler suffering from a head injury.[1] Witnesses told police that Kessler fell backwards during the fight and struck his head on the ground. He was transported to an area hospital for treatment but succumbed to his injuries the following day, Monday, according to the sheriff's office.[1]

## Investigation

The Ventura County Medical Examiner's Office determined that Kessler died from blunt-force head injury and ruled the death a homicide; the county sheriff's office did not rule out a hate crime.[1] Kessler was identified as Jewish by the Jewish Federation of Greater Los Angeles.[1] Reporting on the case was carried by ZeroHedge, drawing on an account by Jill McLaughlin for The Epoch Times.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described Kessler's death as a tragedy and argued that while Americans have a right to protest, the recurring turn toward violence at American demonstrations was "a red flag — and a sign of things to come."[1] She criticized the media handling of the case, contending that framing the death as an accidental fall amounted to narrative shaping, and urged readers not to accept official characterizations of the incident as an accident.[1] The item appeared alongside related reports of a woman arrested after driving a car into an Indianapolis building she believed was an "Israel school" and of a major Democrat donor removed from a Philadelphia Eagles game over a pro-Israel display.[1]
