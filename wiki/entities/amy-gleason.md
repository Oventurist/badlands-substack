# Amy Gleason

Amy Gleason is a Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services advisor and administrator of the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) who participated in the July 2025 White House rollout of a federal health technology initiative.[1]

## Health Tech Ecosystem Initiative

Gleason appeared alongside President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Health Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], CMS Administrator [[mehmet-oz|Mehmet Oz]], and AI and Crypto Czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] at the White House announcement of the [[health-tech-ecosystem-initiative|Health Tech Ecosystem Initiative]], a program to expand interoperable digital health records across provider networks.[1]

Badlands commentary characterized the presentations by Gleason and Oz as "downright dystopian," arguing that the initiative was marketed to the public as giving patients control over their medical data and reducing "friction," a phrase the outlet treats as a recurring justification for surveillance and centralized control.[1] Officials stated that the system would be "entirely opt in" with "no centralized government run database," a claim Badlands contributor Chris Paul compared skeptically to the ostensibly voluntary health insurance system, asking whether providers would be permitted to opt out if patients declined.[1]
