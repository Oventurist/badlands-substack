# Office of Government Ethics

The Office of Government Ethics (OGE) is the federal body to which executive-branch agencies report alleged ethics violations that have been referred to the Department of Justice.[1] Agencies submit OGE Form 202, a "Notice of Conflict of Interest Referral," to record such referrals.[1]

## CIA conflict-of-interest referrals

Records reviewed by [[daily-caller|The Daily Caller]] and reported in January 2024 showed that between January 2021 and May 2023 the [[cia|CIA]] filed 32 Form 202 conflict-of-interest referral notifications with the OGE during the Biden administration, apparently reporting dozens of employees to the Justice Department over potential conflicts of interest.[1] The records were supplied to the Daily Caller by the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]]'s [[oversight-project|Oversight Project]], which had obtained information about the Form 202 submissions through a Freedom of Information Act request.[1]

The OGE cautioned the Heritage Oversight Project that multiple forms may be submitted in connection with a single referral, meaning the total number of Form 202s may not correspond to the total number of referrals made to the Justice Department.[1]
