# Church of Nigeria

The Anglican Church of Nigeria is a province of the [[anglican-communion|Anglican Communion]] and one of its largest and most theologically conservative constituent churches.[1] It is led by archbishop, metropolitan and primate [[henry-ndukuba|Henry Ndukuba]] and is a member of the Global Fellowship of Confessing Anglicans (GAFCON).[1]

## Rejection of Sarah Mullally

In October 2025 the Church of Nigeria formally rejected the appointment of [[sarah-mullally|Sarah Mullally]] as the first-ever female Archbishop of Canterbury.[1] Ndukuba characterized the election as "a double jeopardy," objecting both to the imposition of female headship on those who cannot accept it and to Mullally's support for same-sex marriage.[1]

The church "reaffirms [its] earlier stance to uphold the authority of the Scriptures" and rejects what Ndukuba called "the revisionist agenda" within parts of the Communion.[1] The statement declared that the election confirmed that "the global Anglican world could no longer accept the leadership of the Church of England and that of the Archbishop of Canterbury," signalling a further widening of the split between the Communion's African provinces and its Western leadership.[1]

## Badlands framing

[[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] commentary cited the Nigerian rejection as evidence that "churches in the third world and far east are far more based and loyal to God than the churches of the west," which contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued had been corrupted by wealth and dependence on government tax-exempt status.[1]
