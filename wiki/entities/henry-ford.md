# Henry Ford

Henry Ford was the American automobile industrialist who sponsored an American translation of [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]].[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] notes Ford's sponsorship in the course of tracing how the forgery — plagiarized from [[maurice-joly|Maurice Joly]]'s 1864 *Dialogue in Hell Between Machiavelli and Montesquieu* — spread from occult circles in Russia across Europe and into the United States, where it did more than any other piece of propaganda to inflame anti-Jewish racism in the decades leading up to World War II.[1]

## Quotation on the banking system

A February 2023 Badlands Media feature on [[fractional-reserve-banking|fractional reserve banking]] opened its case with a remark widely attributed to Ford: "It is well enough that people of the nation do not understand our banking and monetary system, for if they did, I believe there would be a revolution before tomorrow morning."[2] The article used the quotation to argue that the opacity of monetary mechanics is deliberate rather than incidental.[2]
