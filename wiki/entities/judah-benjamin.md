# Judah Benjamin

Judah Benjamin was the Confederate Secretary of State during the American [[american-civil-war|Civil War]].[1] Citing Barry Sheehy's *Montreal: City of Secrets*, Matthew Ehret writes that "the largest Confederate Secret Service base outside Richmond was located in Montreal," under Benjamin's direct control, and characterizes Benjamin as himself an asset of [[british-intelligence|British Intelligence]].[1]

Like [[jefferson-davis|Jefferson Davis]] and other Confederate rebels, Benjamin lived out his days in comfort in Britain, becoming an English barrister in London from 1865 until his death in 1884.[1] Ehret likens this pattern to the exiled Russian and Hong Kong oligarchs and traitors of the modern day.[1]
