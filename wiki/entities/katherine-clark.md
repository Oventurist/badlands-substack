# Katherine Clark

Katherine Clark is the House Minority Whip.[1]

In July 2026 Clark voted in favor of an amendment introduced by Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] (R-Ky.) that would have eliminated approximately $3.3 billion in annual US military assistance to [[israel|Israel]].[1] Her vote, along with that of former Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], placed her in opposition to House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]], underscoring divisions within Democratic leadership over US policy toward Israel.[1] The amendment failed by a vote of 314-104.[1]

## Impeachment infighting among House Democrats (December 2025)

In December 2025 House Democrats split over attempts to impeach Trump administration Cabinet members, after articles of impeachment were filed against Health Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] and Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]].[2] House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] dismissed the effort, telling Axios: "You know what I'm focused on? Making sure that the American people don't have their health care ripped away from them."[2] Representatives [[pete-aguilar|Pete Aguilar]] of California and [[katherine-clark|Katharine Clark]] of Massachusetts joined Jeffries in a joint statement denouncing the moves, arguing the process would require an immense amount of work to succeed.[2] Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] mocked the episode with a quip about aliens and lizard-people living among us.[2]

## Child's Antifa-related arrest (January 2023)

In January 2023, Clark's child [[jared-dowell|Jared Dowell]], also known as "Riley" Dowell, was arrested in Boston for Antifa-related violence, and identified in reporting as the offspring of the Democratic House Minority Whip representing Massachusetts's fifth congressional district.[3] Boston police charged Dowell with assault by means of a dangerous weapon, destruction or injury of personal property, and damage of property by graffiti or tagging, allegedly incurred while participating in rioting meant to show solidarity with [[antifa|Antifa]] rioting in Atlanta.[3] Badlands contributor [[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]] used the episode to argue that relatives of what he called subversive bureaucrats are often subversive operatives themselves.[3]
