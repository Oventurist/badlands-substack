# Minneapolis

Minneapolis is the largest city in [[minnesota|Minnesota]] and, in the Badlands corpus, the flashpoint of the January 2026 confrontation between federal immigration enforcement and organized street protest.

## Immigration enforcement crisis, January 2026

The January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief describes Minneapolis as the epicenter of [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]]-related enforcement and of the legal and political backlash against it. Chief U.S. District Judge [[patrick-schiltz|Patrick Schiltz]], sitting in Minneapolis federal court, ordered acting [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] Director [[todd-lyons|Todd Lyons]] to appear on January 30, 2026 to show cause on contempt over the agency's non-compliance with release orders such as that issued for detainee [[juan-tobay-robles|Juan Tobay Robles]].

On January 24, 2026, [[alex-pretti|Alex Pretti]], a 37-year-old former ICU nurse at the Minneapolis VA Medical Center, was fatally shot by [[united-states-border-patrol|U.S. Border Patrol]] agents during chaotic anti-ICE protests in the city. The shooting, following the earlier death of [[renee-good|Renee Good]], drove a national uproar that in turn fed the late-January 2026 federal funding standoff over [[department-of-homeland-security|DHS]] money.

## De-escalation measures

In response to the Minneapolis unrest, the Trump administration demoted [[customs-and-border-protection|CBP]] official [[gregory-bovino|Gregory Bovino]] from the Minneapolis operations and replaced him with border czar [[tom-homan|Tom Homan]]. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] personally telephoned Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] and Minneapolis Mayor [[jacob-frey|Jacob Frey]], and DHS Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] agreed to testify before the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] after months of refusal.

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the Minneapolis events through a [[color-revolution|color revolution]] frame, noting that both Pretti and Good had ties to [[colorado|Colorado]] and treating the coordinated protest networks as an engineered destabilization campaign rather than spontaneous civic reaction.
