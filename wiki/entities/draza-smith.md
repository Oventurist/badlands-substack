# Draza Smith

**Draza Smith** is an election-integrity researcher credited within the Truth Community with discovering what is known as "Edison Zero," an anomaly in election-night vote reporting data.[1]

## Role in third-party candidate analysis

In commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited Smith's work in arguing that third-party candidates are useful to the regime because they serve to mask real-time vote manipulation on election night, as she says was seen in 2020.[1] That argument was applied to reports that [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] was weighing a third-party presidential bid against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], with Ashe suggesting a Cheney run would function as "plausible deniability for election fraud" and that two third-party candidates would make such manipulation easier.[1]
