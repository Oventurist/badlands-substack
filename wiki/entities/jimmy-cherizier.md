# Jimmy "Barbecue" Cherizier

Jimmy Cherizier, widely known by the nickname "Barbecue," is a Haitian gang leader and former police officer who emerged as the dominant armed figure in [[haiti|Haiti]]'s 2024 collapse of state authority.[1]

## Bid for power (March 2024)

By March 2024, Cherizier was leading an armed campaign to seize power in Haiti while nearly 1.5 million Haitians stood on the brink of famine.[1] He claimed responsibility for a surge of attacks and stated that his goals were to capture Haiti's police chief and government ministers and to prevent outgoing Prime Minister [[ariel-henry|Ariel Henry]] from returning to the country.[1]

Gangs under this campaign attacked Haiti's electrical infrastructure, and the national power company announced that four substations in the capital and elsewhere "were destroyed and rendered completely dysfunctional," plunging swaths of Port-au-Prince into darkness — including the entrance to the U.S. embassy, the Cité Soleil slum, the Croix-des-Bouquets community, and a hospital.[1]

## Reputation

Cherizier's nickname and his reputation for burning people alive — an allegation he has long denied — preceded him and struck fear into many Haitians.[1] Badlands Media commentary sardonically referred to him as "PM BBQ," noting that while he denies the burning allegations there was "no update on the cannibalism," and treating his rise as emblematic of the total breakdown of order on the island.[1]

## Related coverage

Former Prime Minister [[laurent-lamothe|Laurent Lamothe]] responded to the crisis by declaring a "total void of law and order" and calling for its restoration.[1]
