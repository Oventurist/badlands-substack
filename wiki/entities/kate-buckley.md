# Kate Buckley

Kate Buckley is a personality associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], listed among the expected speakers for the network's [[gart-3|GART 3: Irvine]] conference held January 11–14, 2024 at the Marriott Irvine Spectrum in Irvine, California.[1]

## Role at GART 3

The December 2023 promotion for GART 3 named Buckley second on its speaker roster, after [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] and ahead of [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[just-human|Just Human]], [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], [[gmoney|GMoney]], [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]], [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of BPH]] and [[jaytriot|Jaytriot]].[1]

Speakers on that roster were also the pool from which participants were drawn for an exclusive pre-conference call-in live stream for GART 3 ticket-buyers, announced for December 7, 2023 at 12pm ET.[1]
