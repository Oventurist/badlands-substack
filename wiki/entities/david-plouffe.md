# David Plouffe

David Plouffe is an American Democratic political strategist who managed [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s successful 2008 presidential campaign and later served as a senior adviser to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s failed 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Deleted X account after 2024 loss

On Thursday, November 7, 2024, Plouffe, then 57, deleted his X account after appearing to blame President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and the "deep hole" he left Democrats in for Harris's historic loss.[1] In the since-deleted post, Plouffe wrote that it had been "a privilege to spend the last 100 days with Kamala Harris and the amazing staff led by @jomalleydillon who left it all on the field for their country," adding, "We dug out of a deep hole but not enough" and calling the result "a devastating loss."[1]

Plouffe explained his sudden online absence in a statement to Politico, saying he was "just taking a break as part of reimmersion into life and out of the campaign bunker."[1]

## Significance

His "deep hole" remark came as numerous Democrats were privately and publicly criticizing Biden for dropping out of the race so late, suggesting the delayed exit hindered Harris's chances.[1]
