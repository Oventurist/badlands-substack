# David Plouffe

David Plouffe is an American Democratic political strategist who managed [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s successful 2008 presidential campaign and later served as a senior adviser to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s failed 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Deleted X account after 2024 loss

On Thursday, November 7, 2024, Plouffe, then 57, deleted his X account after appearing to blame President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and the "deep hole" he left Democrats in for Harris's historic loss.[1] In the since-deleted post, Plouffe wrote that it had been "a privilege to spend the last 100 days with Kamala Harris and the amazing staff led by @jomalleydillon who left it all on the field for their country," adding, "We dug out of a deep hole but not enough" and calling the result "a devastating loss."[1]

Plouffe explained his sudden online absence in a statement to Politico, saying he was "just taking a break as part of reimmersion into life and out of the campaign bunker."[1]

## Significance

His "deep hole" remark came as numerous Democrats were privately and publicly criticizing Biden for dropping out of the race so late, suggesting the delayed exit hindered Harris's chances.[1]

## "It's all Biden" (2025)

In May 2025 Plouffe, a top campaign aide to former Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], blamed former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] for Harris's 2024 loss in a new book about Biden's apparent deterioration during the race, saying "it's all Biden."[2] His account underscored a frustration held by some Democrats that Biden's reluctance to remove himself earlier from the White House race sealed the fate of Harris's bid.[2] Discussing the impact of Biden's July 2024 withdrawal, Plouffe described the then-vice president's sub-three-month campaign as a "fucking nightmare," adding: "And it's all Biden…He totally fucked us."[2] Plouffe had previously managed [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s 2008 campaign and served as a senior adviser in his White House.[2]

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] noted that Plouffe wrote *A Citizen's Guide to Beating Donald Trump* ahead of the 2020 election and characterized him as the man who led election-rigging efforts for [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]'s Chan Zuckerberg Initiative and the Center for Tech and Civic Life.[2] He argued that the effort to make Biden the fall man for everything the regime had done was marching forward as projected in 2020.[2]
