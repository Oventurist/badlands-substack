# Belarus

Belarus is an Eastern European state bordering [[russia|Russia]], [[ukraine|Ukraine]], Poland, Lithuania and Latvia, governed since 1994 by President [[alexander-lukashenko|Alexander Lukashenko]]. It enters the Badlands Media corpus in June 2026 as the source of a widely reported claim about how Moscow was persuaded to invade Ukraine.

## Role in the Ukraine war narrative

The Badlands Brief of June 16, 2026 reports Lukashenko's assertion, made in an interview with *Al Arabiya*, that Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] received faulty information during the early stages of the conflict — specifically that a "Jewish lobby" in Ukraine had convinced Moscow that Russian forces would be greeted as liberators. Positioned between Russia and NATO's eastern flank, and having served throughout the war as a staging ground and diplomatic intermediary for Moscow, Belarus is treated in the coverage as a state whose leader speaks with unusual proximity to Russian decision-making.

The Brief also records Lukashenko's broader position that neither Russia nor Ukraine can win outright and that the war must end through negotiation and compromise — a stance consistent with Minsk's long-standing self-presentation as a potential venue for a settlement, while remaining Russia's closest formal ally.
