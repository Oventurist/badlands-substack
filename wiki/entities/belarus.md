# Belarus

**Belarus** is an Eastern European state and staunch ally of Russia that shares a sizable border with [[poland|Poland]], a NATO member which supports Ukraine.[1] In August 2023 it became a focal point of regional tension after fighters from Russia's [[wagner-group|Wagner]] private military company were relocated to its territory following their short-lived June mutiny in Russia.[1]

## 2023 border tensions with Poland

Poland's prime minister, [[mateusz-morawiecki|Mateusz Morawiecki]], said in early August 2023 that there were at least 4,000 Wagner fighters in Belarus, and warned against "provocations" and "sabotage actions" originating from Belarus by the relocated fighters.[1] That warning came days after two Belarusian helicopters breached Polish airspace, heightening jitters in the region.[1]

In response, Poland announced it would send an additional 2,000 troops to reinforce its border with Belarus, a deployment that deputy interior minister [[maciej-wasik|Maciej Wasik]] said was double what the country's Border Guard had requested and would arrive within two weeks.[1] There was no immediate response from Belarus to the announcement.[1]
