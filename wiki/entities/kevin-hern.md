# Kevin Hern

Kevin Hern (R-Okla.) is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives who was among the frontrunners for the speakership in October 2023.[1]

## 2023 speakership candidacy

After House Republicans abruptly withdrew [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] as the conference's nominee for Speaker, nearly a dozen members entered the race.[1] Press coverage identified Hern alongside [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]], [[byron-donalds|Byron Donalds]] and Austin Scott as the leading contenders in what was described as a high-stakes battle following three tumultuous weeks of Republican infighting that had brought [[congress|Congress]] to a halt.[1] The [[us-house-of-representatives|House]] was scheduled to reassemble the following Tuesday to attempt once again to elect a Speaker.[1]
