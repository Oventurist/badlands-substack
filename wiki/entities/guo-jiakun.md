# Guo Jiakun

Guo Jiakun is a spokesperson for the foreign ministry of the People's Republic of [[china|China]] who responded publicly to U.S. objections over Chinese trade arrangements with [[canada|Canada]] in January 2026.[1]

## January 2026 remarks on Canada trade

After [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] warned that the United States could impose 100% tariffs on Canadian goods if Ottawa pursued a free-trade agreement with China, China's foreign ministry said its trade arrangements with Canada were not aimed at any third party.[1] Responding to reporters on Monday, January 26, 2026, Guo said: "China holds that countries should handle relations with one another with a win-win rather than zero-sum mindset, and through cooperation rather than confrontation."[1]

The statement accompanied Canadian officials' own clarification that Canada had no intention of pursuing a free-trade deal with China, and Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]'s insistence that Canadian negotiations remained consistent with [[usmca|USMCA]]/CUSMA obligations.[1]

## Remarks on COVID-19 origins (February 2025)

As a Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesman, Guo Jiakun said on Wednesday, February 12, 2025 that China reiterated its opposition to political manipulation of coronavirus origins-tracing, urging the United States to reflect on itself rather than shift blame to others.[2] His remarks responded to US media reports claiming that [[usaid|USAID]] had used American taxpayer money to fund gain-of-function studies of the virus at the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]], allegedly causing the COVID-19 pandemic and the deaths of millions.[2]

Guo stressed that China had made clear many times that the Wuhan Institute of Virology had never engaged in gain-of-function studies of coronavirus and never designed, made, or leaked COVID-19.[2] He said it was extremely unlikely that the pandemic was caused by a lab leak, calling that the authoritative conclusion of the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]]-China joint mission.[2] Badlands contributor [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] dismissed both narratives, quipping that the [[lab-leak-theory|lab leak theory]] and the wet market theory were each made up and that "the virus is transmitted through the television."[2]
