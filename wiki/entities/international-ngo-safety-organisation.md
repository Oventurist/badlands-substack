# International NGO Safety Organisation

The International NGO Safety Organisation (INSO) is a Netherlands-based non-governmental organization that provides safety and security services to humanitarian organizations operating in conflict zones.[1] In October 2025 the government of [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] accused INSO of functioning as an espionage network within the country and arrested eight of its workers.[1]

## Burkina Faso espionage case

INSO had operated in the Sahel country since 2019 but was suspended on July 31, 2025, over allegations that it had been collecting sensitive data without authorization.[1] At a press briefing on Tuesday, October 7, 2025, Security Minister [[mahamadou-sana|Mahamadou Sana]] told reporters that authorities had monitored the organization's activities for some time and, thanks to the "contribution of citizens," had determined that the NGO "constituted an espionage network in Burkina Faso."[1]

Sana stated that despite the earlier suspension, "some officials continued to carry out clandestine or disguised activities such as gathering information and holding meetings in person or online."[1] The eight people arrested comprised four foreign nationals — two French citizens, one Czech and one Malian — along with four citizens of Burkina Faso.[1]

## Badlands commentary

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] read the case as evidence of Western intelligence activity conducted under humanitarian cover, with contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] writing that "the [[state-department|State Department]] ([[cia|CIA]]) has been busy, trying to overthrow yet another troublesome government."[1] The commentary asked what motive the United States could have for destabilizing a country where 80 percent of citizens lack access to electricity, suggesting the answer lay in Burkina Faso's newly signed nuclear deal with Russia to construct a nuclear power plant.[1]

The Brief extended the argument into a broader indictment of American and European foreign policy, singling out [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] and the [[republican-party|Republican Party]] for defending intelligence networks as necessary to maintain superpower status, and calling Graham a war criminal who should face a Nuremberg-style tribunal.[1] GhostofBasedPatrickHenry concluded that even with [[donald-trump|Trump]] as president, Americans remain unwilling to confront the GOP over imperialism, and that "we deserve the government that we will tolerate."[1]
