# Paulina Gutierrez

**Paulina Gutierrez** is the election official who was named executive director of the [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] in May 2024, replacing [[claire-woodall|Claire Woodall]].[1]

Gutierrez had served as Woodall's deputy at the commission when Milwaukee Mayor [[cavalier-johnson|Cavalier Johnson]] announced on Monday, May 6, 2024 — in what the Associated Press described as a surprise move — that she would take over the top post.[1] The change came roughly six months before the November 2024 presidential election, placing her in charge of election administration in Wisconsin's largest city at a moment of intense national attention on the state, where four of the previous six presidential contests were decided by less than a percentage point.[1]

Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated the leadership change primarily as a verdict on her predecessor and on Wisconsin's "Vote at Home" controversy, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] warning that the underlying network administering the state's elections remained intact regardless of personnel changes.[1]
