# Wall Street Journal

The Wall Street Journal is an American business and financial newspaper. It appears in the Badlands Media corpus as a source of reporting cited within the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] digest format.

In the April 17, 2026 edition, [[badlands-brief-07c|Supreme Conflicts, Criminal Probes, & A Ceasefire]], the Journal's national security reporting is cited for the disclosure that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] has approached automakers and manufacturers to boost weapons production — the factual basis for the Brief's discussion of an emerging [[war-economy|war economy]] built on the conversion of civilian industrial capacity to military supply.
