# Wall Street Journal

The Wall Street Journal is an American business and financial newspaper. It appears in the Badlands Media corpus as a source of reporting cited within the Badlands Brief digest format.

In the April 17, 2026 edition, Supreme Conflicts, Criminal Probes, & A Ceasefire, the Journal's national security reporting is cited for the disclosure that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] has approached automakers and manufacturers to boost weapons production — the factual basis for the Brief's discussion of an emerging [[war-economy|war economy]] built on the conversion of civilian industrial capacity to military supply.

## Trump on the ODNI (June 2026)

In an interview with the Journal, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he had privately instructed acting Director of National Intelligence [[william-pulte|Bill Pulte]] to "start the process" of reducing personnel within the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|ODNI]], which Trump called "unnecessary and/or too big," adding that some employees "should no longer be in their positions." Trump also said he does not plan to nominate Pulte for the post on a permanent basis.
