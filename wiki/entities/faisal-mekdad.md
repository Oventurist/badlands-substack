# Faisal Mekdad

Faisal Mekdad served as [[syria|Syria]]'s foreign minister under the Assad government.[1]

## Damascus embassy strike (April 2024)

Mekdad was seen at the site of the April 1, 2024 bombing of [[iran|Iran]]'s embassy compound in the Mezzeh district of Damascus, along with Syria's interior minister.[1] He condemned the attack, saying: "We strongly condemn this atrocious terrorist attack that targeted the Iranian consulate building in Damascus and killed a number of innocents."[1] The strike, attributed to suspected [[israel|Israeli]] warplanes, killed seven Iranian military advisers including senior [[quds-force|Quds Force]] commander [[mohammad-reza-zahedi|Mohammad Reza Zahedi]].[1] See [[2024-israeli-strike-on-iranian-embassy-in-damascus]].[1]
