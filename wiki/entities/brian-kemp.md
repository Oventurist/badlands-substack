# Brian Kemp

Brian Kemp is the Governor of Georgia, elected after previously serving as the state's Secretary of State. In February 2026 he became the focus of renewed scrutiny over the state's handling of 2020 presidential election fraud allegations, following the unsealing of special grand jury transcripts from the collapsed Fulton County racketeering case.[1]

## Perdue grand jury allegation

Under oath before the Fulton County special grand jury on September 6, 2022, former U.S. Senator [[david-perdue|David Perdue]] testified that Kemp had directed Georgia Bureau of Investigation Chief Vic Reynolds to tell Perdue that the state was not going to investigate the 2020 election, and that Perdue should be a "team player."[1] The transcript became public after Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] lifted a protective order on the special grand jury materials in January 2026.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that "being a team player in election fraud—either the crime or the cover up—is treason," and argued that Kemp "needs to resign so he can be properly prosecuted."[1] The Badlands commentary further noted that at least three officials tasked with investigating election fraud in Georgia allegedly killed the case and were subsequently rewarded with judgeships in the state, a pattern described as unproven in court but heavily suggestive.[1]

## Signature audit context

Attorney [[sidney-powell|Sidney Powell]] publicly connected Kemp's posture to the 2020 death of [[harrison-deal|Harrison Deal]], a young aide connected to Kemp, Senator Kelly Loeffler, and Perdue, noting that all three had called for a signature audit shortly before the fatal highway crash, and urging observers to "run the timeline on Kemp's change of heart."[1]
