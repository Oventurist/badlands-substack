# Dan Siegel

**Dr. Dan Siegel** is quoted in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites his statement that "integration is the foundation of health," using it to open the closing section "Living It Forward" and to support the claim that the reader is "no longer a puzzle to solve" but "the full image, visible and breathing."[1]

Siegel's framing underpins the essay's insistence that [[coherence|coherence]] "doesn't mean sameness—it means harmony," with mind, body and soul described as speaking "a common language."[1] His citation precedes the essay's quotation of [[jean-houston|Jean Houston]] on being "the ancestors of a future age."[1]

## "The Liminal" (April 2025)

Siegel is quoted in the earlier April 5, 2025 Badlands Media feature "The Liminal" for the formulation "Before integration, we must differentiate."[2] The author uses the line to name the [[liminality|liminal]] passage the differentiation stage, in which "the old narrative is fully disassembled so the new can be constructed from truth, not trauma."[2] The citation appears in the essay's "Living Without a Map" section, directly after [[lisa-miller|Lisa Miller]]'s description of a "spiritual lull," and grounds the article's insistence that the emptying of identity, goals and practice at this stage is not failure.[2]
