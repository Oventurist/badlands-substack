# Barbara Kay

Barbara Kay is a Canadian columnist who, together with [[jonathan-kay|Jonathan Kay]], was sued for defamation by the [[canadian-anti-hate-network|Canadian Anti-Hate Network]] (CAHN) after the two publicized the network's support for the far-left organization [[antifa|ANTIFA]].[1]

## The defamation action

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described the suit as a psychological operation on behalf of [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]]'s government that "met a disastrous end" when an Ontario judge upheld the Kays' characterization of CAHN.[1] In Deschamps's account, the ruling refuted CAHN's claim to be an independent nonprofit composed of Canada's leading experts on hate groups and hate crimes — a claim complicated further, he wrote, by a June 2022 Government of Canada news release confirming $268,400 in federal support for the network's "Anti-Hate Kits."[1]

Deschamps used the episode to illustrate his broader argument that state-funded intermediaries supply the [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] component of an [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric]] political operation, allowing the government to attack dissenters through a body positioned as an independent authority.[1]
