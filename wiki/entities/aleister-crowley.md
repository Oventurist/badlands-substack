# Aleister Crowley

Aleister Crowley was an English occultist invoked in the Badlands Media feature "Legacy of the Bavarian Illuminati – Part 2" as an exemplar of moral relativism in the occult tradition.[1]

Discussing the Shakespearean line "There is nothing either good or bad, but thinking makes it so" — which the article notes may have been written by Francis Bacon — [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] observes that such platitudes are popular with modern liberals and occult practitioners alike, and that "Crowley's declaration of 'Do as thou wilt' echoes the same sentiment."[1] What appears to be a liberating and progressive proclamation, he argues, can equally serve as an excuse to indulge in "all manner of unhealthy, self-serving behavior" — self-service being, in his account, a predominant trait of the secret societies that scheme behind the public's back to shape civilization in their favor.[1]
