# Kurds

The Kurds are an ethnic group of the Middle East whose armed and political organisations have long served as intermediaries in Western covert operations in the region. In the Badlands Media corpus they appear in the April 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-282|Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks"]], in connection with an admitted U.S. attempt to arm anti-government protesters inside [[iran|Iran]].

The Brief reports that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said the United States tried to send weapons to Iranian anti-government protesters earlier in 2026 — apparently the first public acknowledgement of such involvement. The weapons were routed through Kurdish intermediaries but, Trump said, largely failed to reach the intended recipients; he added that he believes Kurdish groups may have kept them instead. The underlying demonstrations, driven by economic conditions, lasted weeks and produced unrest across Iran.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the interception as deliberate and favourable. In his account the guns were dispatched by what he calls the "Zionist/NeoCon [[deep-state|Deep State]]" for operatives seeking to overthrow the Iranian government, and were stopped by Kurds who "have demonstrated loyalty to Trump and his Muslim allies in the Middle East." He presents this as evidence that the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] is succeeding in subverting Deep State plans, and as further indication that operations are being run against [[israel|Israel]] and the anti-Iran neoconservative faction in Washington.
