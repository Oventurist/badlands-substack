# Monica Márquez

**Monica Márquez** is the Chief Justice of the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]], appointed to that post in September 2024.[1]

The court's 4–3 decision of October 8, 2024, dismissing on procedural grounds the transgender-cake case against baker [[jack-phillips|Jack Phillips]] and [[masterpiece-cakeshop|Masterpiece Cakeshop]], was the first contentious ruling issued by the court since Márquez became Chief Justice, and she joined the majority.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the outcome as a notable shift for a court widely considered the most progressive state high court in the nation.[1]
