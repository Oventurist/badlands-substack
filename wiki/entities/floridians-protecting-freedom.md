# Floridians Protecting Freedom

Floridians Protecting Freedom (FPF) was the campaign group that sponsored Amendment 4, Florida's failed 2024 ballot measure to enshrine a right to abortion in the state constitution.[1]

## Signature fraud fines

In late 2024 the group paid more than $186,000 in fines arising from complaints and investigations alleging major fraudulent activity during its petition campaign.[1] According to a December 20, 2024 memo from Florida Deputy Secretary of State for Legal Affairs and Election Integrity Brad McVay, state election officials were "inundated" during 2023 and 2024 with signature fraud complaints concerning FPF.[1]

FPF needed a minimum of 891,523 valid public signatures to place Amendment 4 on the ballot and submitted 997,035.[1] In response to the complaints, the Florida Office of Election Crimes & Security opened more than 100 criminal investigations into FPF signature gatherers, involving allegations that paid gatherers signed petitions themselves on behalf of deceased individuals, forged voter signatures, used voters' personal identifying information without consent, and committed perjury or false swearing when submitting petitions.[1]
