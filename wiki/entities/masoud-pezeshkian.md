# Masoud Pezeshkian

Masoud Pezeshkian is the President of [[iran|Iran]], identified in Badlands commentary as part of a comparatively pragmatic Iranian leadership that includes Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]].[1]

In analysis of Araghchi's July 2026 interview, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] posed the counterfactual of whether Pezeshkian and Araghchi would hold office at all had [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] never been killed — suggesting Soleimani may have been powerful enough to prevent any "earnest benevolent actor" from taking power in Tehran.[1] The framing supports the broader [[devolution|Iranian devolution]] thesis advanced in Badlands coverage, under which hardline factions and possible foreign penetration of the chain of command work to prolong conflict against Iran's own interests.[1]

## Open letter to the American public (April 2026)

In an open letter to the American public issued on April 1, 2026, Pezeshkian questioned whether Washington was genuinely pursuing an "America First" agenda or instead operating as a proxy for [[israel|Israel]], and urged Americans to reject what he called coordinated "war propaganda" surrounding Iran.[2]

He argued that portrayals of Iran as a threat are the product of "manufactured narratives" driven by the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] and Israeli political interests, used to justify military presence and conflict, and said the United States has built extensive military capabilities around Iran despite Tehran posing no direct threat.[2] He accused Israel of shaping US policy to deflect attention from its own actions in the region and claimed Washington was being pushed into a broader war serving foreign rather than American interests.[2] Pezeshkian maintained that Iran has not sought war and is responding to continued aggression.[2]

He called on Americans to question official narratives, describing the depiction of Iran as an imminent threat as misleading and serving political objectives rather than the public interest, and pointed to growing domestic US frustration with foreign policy as evidence that ordinary citizens in both countries share an interest in avoiding escalation.[2]

## Wartime continuity measures (January 2026)

Amid escalating threats of US or Israeli attack in January 2026, Pezeshkian delegated expanded powers to Iran's provincial governors to permit rapid decision-making, accelerated imports, and barter trade conducted without foreign currency, as part of the [[iran-continuity-of-government-measures-2026|emergency continuity measures]] adopted by the Islamic Republic.[3] Badlands commentators described the package as a Persian analogue of [[devolution|Devolution Theory]].[3]

On January 27, 2026 Pezeshkian telephoned Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], telling him that "Muslim Unity" was the key to addressing the region's destabilizing events — language echoing MBS's own address to the [[organization-of-islamic-cooperation|Organization of Islamic Cooperation]] in Riyadh in November 2023.[3] Immediately after the call, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] announced that its airspace would not be used by any party to conduct operations against Iran, a development read in Badlands coverage as part of a pattern of Saudi interposition between Iran and the United States and Israel.[3]


## Defense of the Pope and openness to a deal (April 2026)

In April 2026 Pezeshkian publicly defended [[pope-leo-xiv|Pope Leo XIV]] against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s criticisms, while making clear that [[iran|Iran]] remained willing to make a deal under the correct conditions.[4] The intervention came after US–Iranian talks in Islamabad collapsed over Tehran's nuclear program and Washington announced a [[naval-blockade-of-iran|maritime blockade]].[4] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the episode as evidence against Western narratives of uniform Muslim hostility to Christianity, and argued that any demand for a total ban on uranium enrichment would be a non-starter for the Iranian government.[4]

## January 2026 protests and backchannel de-escalation

As nationwide protests inside [[iran|Iran]] appeared to subside following a security crackdown in mid-January 2026, Pezeshkian warned publicly that foreign interference would be met with a response while simultaneously calling for internal calm.[5] Iranian authorities accused the United States and Israel of encouraging the unrest.[5] During the same period, reporting attributed to [[backchannel-diplomacy|backchannel]] communications indicated that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had conveyed to Tehran that the US was not preparing to strike Iran.[5]

When mainstream outlets asserted that Iran was preparing to hang protesters, both Trump and Pezeshkian came forward to rebuke the claims — a convergence [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited alongside [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]]'s advocacy of diplomacy as evidence that the crisis was being defused rather than escalated.[5] Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] publicly contradicted those denials, insisting the Iranian government was still "killing protesters at an alarming rate."[5]

## False resignation report, May 2026

At the end of May 2026, a report originating with [[iran-international|Iran International]] claimed that Pezeshkian had submitted a letter of resignation "citing a lack of authority inside the country's ruling system," and had accused the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] of excluding him from major and vital decision-making.[6] The claim was subsequently proven false by the Iranian government.[6] The story was nonetheless amplified by [[fox-news|Fox News]], the [[jerusalem-post|Jerusalem Post]] and other outlets before being debunked.[6] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Pezeshkian's X account carried no mention of any resignation at the time the story broke, and described the episode as an attempt to convince audiences inside and outside Iran that IRGC hardliners had displaced the civilian government.[6] The Brief also noted in passing that Pezeshkian is a heart surgeon by profession.[6] The episode is documented in detail at [[pezeshkian-resignation-hoax-2026|the Pezeshkian resignation hoax]].[6]


## Comprehensive Strategic Partnership With Russia

Pezeshkian signed the [[russia-iran-comprehensive-strategic-partnership-treaty|Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Agreement]] with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in January 2025; Putin ratified the parliamentary approval and brought the treaty into force on April 21, 2025.[7] The agreement commits the two states to stronger relations across national security, peaceful nuclear energy and joint resistance to unilateral sanctions.[7]

## Tucker Carlson interview (July 2025)

In an interview with the American journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] aired on Monday, July 7, 2025, Pezeshkian charged that Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had spent decades trying to deceive US presidents into believing that Tehran was seeking nuclear weapons.[8] He said Netanyahu had pushed the narrative long before first becoming prime minister in 1996: "It was Netanyahu since 1984 who has created this false mentality that Iran seeks a nuclear bomb," referring to Netanyahu's role as Israel's envoy to the United Nations at the time.[8]

"He has put it in the minds of every US president since then… [that] we would like to have a nuclear bomb," Pezeshkian said, stressing that Iran has never developed such a weapon and that this is consistent with the religious decree issued by Iran's supreme leader; he also noted that the International Atomic Energy Agency had confirmed Iran was not developing nuclear weapons.[8] He further accused Israel of deliberately sabotaging negotiations with the United States on Iran's nuclear program when it launched a powerful strike on Tehran's nuclear infrastructure, military sites and top commanders on June 13.[8]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the interview had triggered much of the internet into an outcry, and credited Pezeshkian with successfully challenging the official narrative that Iran is governed by unhinged and murderous sociopaths — while judging Carlson's immediately preceding interview with historian [[scott-horton|Scott Horton]] more significant in content.[8]

## June 2025 diplomacy during the Israel–Iran war

In the aftermath of Israel's strike on [[iran|Iran]] in June 2025, Pezeshkian spoke by phone with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], who subsequently briefed Chinese leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] on the exchange and reaffirmed Moscow's readiness to mediate between Iran and Israel if necessary.[9] Both Moscow and Beijing maintained that there is no military solution to the situation or to issues related to Iran's nuclear program, and that the matter must be resolved exclusively through political and diplomatic means.[9] During the same period, Iran's deputy foreign minister warned the BBC that direct US involvement would cause "hell" in the region, saying "this is not America's war."[9]
