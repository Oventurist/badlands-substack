# Masoud Pezeshkian

Masoud Pezeshkian is the President of [[iran|Iran]], elected as a comparatively reformist figure and associated in the Badlands corpus with the diplomatic track pursued by Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]].

The July 22, 2026 [[badlands-brief-1ae|Badlands Brief]] does not report on Pezeshkian's own actions so much as invoke him in a counterfactual. Reflecting on Araghchi's claim that foreign intelligence services had penetrated Iranian decision-making, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] asks whether General [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] had been positioned to escalate conflict with [[israel|Israel]] through his proxy network and to prevent peace, or whether he was simply "so powerful that he could have prevented any earnest benevolent actor from even taking office." The question that follows — "Would Pezeshkian and Araghchi be in power if Soleimani had never been killed?" — casts the current Iranian leadership as a political formation made possible by the removal of the Quds Force commander.

Within that framing, Pezeshkian belongs to the faction the corpus treats as genuinely interested in de-escalation, in contrast to the hardline commentators and [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Revolutionary Guards]]-affiliated media that attacked Araghchi's interview.

## Solidarity with the Muslim world during the Twelve Day War

The May 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-5a2|Badlands Brief]] reports that, during the June 2025 [[twelve-day-war|Twelve Day War]] between [[israel|Israel]] and [[iran|Iran]], Pezeshkian and Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] expressed solidarity with the Muslim world, with Pezeshkian calling longtime rival [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] "our brothers."
