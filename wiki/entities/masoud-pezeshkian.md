# Masoud Pezeshkian

Masoud Pezeshkian is the President of [[iran|Iran]], elected as a comparatively reformist figure and associated in the Badlands corpus with the diplomatic track pursued by Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]].

The July 22, 2026 Badlands Brief does not report on Pezeshkian's own actions so much as invoke him in a counterfactual. Reflecting on Araghchi's claim that foreign intelligence services had penetrated Iranian decision-making, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] asks whether General [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] had been positioned to escalate conflict with [[israel|Israel]] through his proxy network and to prevent peace, or whether he was simply "so powerful that he could have prevented any earnest benevolent actor from even taking office." The question that follows — "Would Pezeshkian and Araghchi be in power if Soleimani had never been killed?" — casts the current Iranian leadership as a political formation made possible by the removal of the Quds Force commander.

Within that framing, Pezeshkian belongs to the faction the corpus treats as genuinely interested in de-escalation, in contrast to the hardline commentators and [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Revolutionary Guards]]-affiliated media that attacked Araghchi's interview.

## Solidarity with the Muslim world during the Twelve Day War

The May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that, during the June 2025 [[twelve-day-war|Twelve Day War]] between [[israel|Israel]] and [[iran|Iran]], Pezeshkian and Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] expressed solidarity with the Muslim world, with Pezeshkian calling longtime rival [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] "our brothers."

## Open letter to the American people (April 2026)

The Badlands Brief of April 2, 2026 reports that Pezeshkian issued an open letter to the American public questioning whether Washington is truly pursuing an "America First" agenda or instead operating as a proxy for [[israel|Israel]], while urging Americans to reject coordinated "war propaganda" surrounding [[iran|Iran]]. He argued that portraying Iran as a threat is the result of "manufactured narratives" driven by the military-industrial complex and Israeli political interests, claiming such messaging is used to justify military presence and conflict, and that the U.S. has built extensive military capabilities around Iran despite Tehran posing no direct threat. Pezeshkian accused Israel of shaping U.S. policy to deflect attention from its actions in the region, maintained that Iran has not sought war and is responding to what he characterized as continued aggression, and pointed to growing frustration within the United States over foreign policy decisions, suggesting ordinary citizens in both countries share an interest in avoiding further escalation.

## Emergency contingency measures, January 2026

The January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that, amid escalating threats of U.S. or Israeli attack, Pezeshkian delegated expanded powers to provincial governors to enable rapid local decision-making, accelerated imports, and barter trade conducted without foreign currency, as part of measures to ensure government continuity and essential supplies. In parallel the [[supreme-national-security-council|Supreme National Security Council]] designated successors for key military and civilian officials to prevent leadership gaps, drawing on past wartime protocols and on the framework of the newly created [[national-defense-council|National Defense Council]]. Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the arrangement as an Iranian [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] regime closely resembling [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s [[devolution|Devolution]] theory — "Persian Devolution."

## Call with Mohammed bin Salman

Citing the [[tasnim-news-agency|Tasnim News Agency]], the same Brief reports that Pezeshkian telephoned Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] on January 27, 2026, stating that "Muslim Unity" was the key to addressing the region's destabilizing events — echoing language MBS delivered to the [[organization-of-islamic-cooperation|Organization of Islamic Cooperation]] at the November 2023 Riyadh meeting convened with [[king-salman|King Salman]] after [[october-7|October 7]]. Immediately after the call, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] announced that its airspace would not be used by any party to conduct operations against [[iran|Iran]].
