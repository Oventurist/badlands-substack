# Transitional National Council

The Transitional National Council (TNC) was the governing body formed by the anti-Gaddafi movement during the 2011 Libyan uprising.[1] Politico reported that Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]], visiting the rebel stronghold of [[benghazi|Benghazi]], urged "every nation, especially the United States, to recognize the Transitional National Council as the legitimate voice of the Libyan people," adding that "they have earned this right, and Qadhafi has forfeited it by waging war on his own people."[1]

The Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest" argues McCain's push for recognition was financially motivated: recognizing the TNC as Libya's legitimate government would make it much easier for American taxpayer money to flow directly to the rebel groups.[1] It further notes that the TNC was the body with which lobbyist [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]] worked closely as Political Director of the [[libyan-emergency-task-force|Libyan Emergency Task Force]], linking the council to the same lobbying network later used for Syria.[1]
