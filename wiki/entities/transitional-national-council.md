# Transitional National Council

The Transitional National Council (TNC) was the governing body formed by the anti-Gaddafi movement during the 2011 Libyan uprising, based in [[benghazi|Benghazi]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

During his April 2011 visit to Benghazi, [[john-mccain|Senator John McCain]] urged that "every nation, especially the United States" recognise the TNC "as the legitimate voice of the Libyan people," arguing that the council had earned the right and that [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]] had forfeited it by waging war on his own people.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The Badlands account answers its own question about why McCain pressed so hard for recognition: formal recognition would make it far easier for American taxpayer money to flow directly to the rebel groups.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

The article also notes that the TNC was the body with which [[mouaz-moustafa|Mouaz Moustafa]] worked closely as Political Director of the [[libyan-emergency-task-force|Libyan Emergency Task Force]], connecting the lobbying apparatus in Washington to the nominal Libyan government-in-waiting, whose armed backbone included the [[libyan-islamic-fighting-group|Libyan Islamic Fighting Group]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)
