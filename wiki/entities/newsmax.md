# Newsmax

Newsmax is an American conservative news network whose television arm, Newsmax TV, carries political programming including the morning show "Wake Up America."[1]

## Coverage of the Trump indictments

In August 2023 Newsmax hosted attorney [[alina-habba|Alina Habba]], counsel to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and general counsel for the Save America PAC, who argued on "Wake Up America" that Georgia's decision to bring election-related charges against Trump in state rather than federal court was deliberate.[1] Habba said that anything involving election issues at the presidential level should "100% be in federal court" and that the state venue was chosen "by design" so that Trump, if elected president, could not pardon himself following a conviction.[1] She indicated the defense would likely seek removal to federal court and a change of venue.[1] The interview became a widely cited statement of the Trump team's [[lawfare|lawfare]] posture toward the [[state-of-georgia-v-trump|Georgia prosecution]] brought by [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]].[1]

## Van Susteren question to Netanyahu (September 2025)

In September 2025, Newsmax host [[greta-van-susteren|Greta Van Susteren]] asked Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] — citing "online conspiracy theorists" — whether Israel had any involvement in the [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination of Charlie Kirk]].[2] Badlands commentary called the exchange one of the more bizarre moments in recent memory, suggested Van Susteren appeared to have been fed the question, and judged Netanyahu's response unconvincing.[2]
