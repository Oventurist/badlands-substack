# Goya Foods

Goya Foods is an American food company best known in political commentary for the consumer controversy that surrounded it in July 2020, when chief executive [[robert-unanue|Robert Unanue]] publicly praised President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and provoked calls for a boycott from Trump's critics.[1]

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the Goya episode as the first occasion on which American conservatives discovered their collective market power.[1] Although Twitter posts pushing for a boycott outnumbered those urging consumers to buy more Goya products by 75 percent, net sales jumped 22 percent in the two weeks following the controversy, according to a study by the market research firm Numerator.[1] In this framing, Goya became the template for "buycott" behavior — deliberately purchasing from value-aligned businesses — that was later contrasted with the punitive boycotts of [[bud-light|Bud Light]] and [[target-corporation|Target]] during the [[target-pride-collection-boycott|Target Pride collection backlash]] of May 2023.[1]
