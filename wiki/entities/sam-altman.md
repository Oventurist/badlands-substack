# Sam Altman

## Regulation push and the AI shadow war

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued in July 2026 that the concerted effort by Sam Altman of [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]] of [[anthropic|Anthropic]] to seek government regulation for the nascent, rapidly-accelerating AI industry was not an attempt to supercharge the American push into an Age of Abundance but to slow and control that push, putting it "in collectivist, oligarchic hands."[1] In that framing, Altman and Amodei revealed themselves "not as accelerants to the AI agenda, but retardants to it."[1] Former White House AI and Crypto Czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] made a parallel argument, stating that the two companies seek regulation to stave off competitors — a position at odds with the administration's stated aim, voiced by Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]], of protecting [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open source AI]].[1]
