# Sam Altman

Sam Altman is the chief executive officer of [[openai|OpenAI]].

The March 4, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Altman responded to backlash over OpenAI's [[pentagon|Pentagon]] artificial-intelligence contract by calling the initial rollout "sloppy" and lacking clear communication. The deal, announced after negotiations with rival [[anthropic|Anthropic]] collapsed, would supply OpenAI models for classified United States military operations, and drew employee protests, chalk graffiti at company headquarters, and an open letter signed by nearly 900 workers at OpenAI and Google. Under Altman the company rewrote the contract to prohibit "intentional" use of its systems for domestic surveillance or monitoring of U.S. persons, including via commercially acquired personal data, and to exclude Pentagon intelligence agencies such as the [[national-security-agency|NSA]] pending separate agreements.
