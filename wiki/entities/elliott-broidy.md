# Elliott Broidy

**Elliott Broidy** is an American businessman and political fundraiser named by the [[us-department-of-justice|United States Department of Justice]] as a participant in the covert lobbying effort connected to the [[1mdb-scandal|1MDB scandal]].[1]

According to court documents and evidence presented at the trial of the entertainer [[pras-michel|Prakazrel "Pras" Michel]], Michel conspired with [[jho-low|Low Taek Jho]], Broidy, Nickie Lum Davis, George Higginbotham and others to conduct undisclosed lobbying campaigns.[1] Those campaigns were carried out at the direction of Low and of the Vice Minister of Public Security for the People's Republic of China, and sought both to have the 1MDB embezzlement investigation and forfeiture proceedings dropped and to have a Chinese national returned to China.[1]

Badlands Media commentary treated Broidy's involvement as one strand of a kleptocratic network spanning bankers, Hollywood figures, celebrities and officials across ten nations.[1]
