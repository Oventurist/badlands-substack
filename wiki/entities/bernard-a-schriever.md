# Bernard A. Schriever

**General Bernard A. Schriever** was the [[united-states-air-force|United States Air Force]] officer under whom the Western Development Division was established in 1954 as the first dedicated space organization within the US armed forces.[1]

Schriever's place in "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" is as a link in a chain of doctrinal succession: his commander was Henry H. "Hap" Arnold, and Arnold was one of the apostles of strategic air power, following the lead of General William "[[billy-mitchell|Billy]]" Mitchell.[1] The lineage is used to argue that recognition of a new warfighting domain typically begins with officers whose superiors resist them, and that the same dynamic now applies to space and cyberspace.[1]

Space activity in the US military predated Schriever's division, with the first Air Force space programs starting in 1945, and was later consolidated under Air Force Space Command from 1982 until the creation of the [[united-states-space-force|Space Force]].[1]
