# Andrii Sybiha

Andrii Sybiha is Ukraine's foreign minister, who in June 2025 accused Russia of stalling on peace negotiations while continuing its air campaign against Ukrainian cities.[1]

## Statements on ceasefire negotiations

On June 19, 2025, Sybiha said it had been 100 days of Russia "manipulating … opportunities to end the war," referencing Kyiv's acceptance of a US-proposed 30-day ceasefire in March 2025 and Moscow's refusal to agree to it.[1] His remarks came as [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] told international news outlets in St Petersburg that he was willing to meet Ukrainian president [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], but only for a "final phase" of talks intended to "put an end" to the conflict.[1] Ukraine and its Western backers characterized this as playing for time — appealing to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] while relentlessly bombing Ukraine, with dozens of civilians killed in a surge of Russian aerial attacks.[1]
