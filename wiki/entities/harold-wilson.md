# Harold Wilson

Harold Wilson was a British Labour politician who served as prime minister of the United Kingdom during the 1960s and 1970s.[1] He features in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s account of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]] through the testimony of [[kitty-little|Dr. Kitty Little]].[1]

Little, who said she worked for Britain's Ministry of Aircraft Production during the Second World War and later for the Atomic Energy Research Establishment, recounted attending a Labour Party "study group" at Oxford University in 1940.[1] The speaker that evening was a young man who claimed to be part of a "Marxist takeover" plot and a member of a nameless group — supposedly nameless by design, to make its existence harder to prove — that aimed to engineer Marxist control in Britain, Europe and parts of Africa.[1] He explained that because Britons distrusted extremists, group members would pose as moderates, allowing them to dismiss critics as right-wingers.[1] The speaker added that he had been selected to head the group's political section and expected one day to be named prime minister; that speaker, according to Little, was Wilson, who indeed later held the office.[1]

DeLarme writes that it is believed the group Wilson referred to is what is now colloquially called the Bilderbergers, and cites Wilson as one of several heads of state to have mingled with the group, alongside [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]].[1]
