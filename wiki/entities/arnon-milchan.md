# Arnon Milchan

Arnon Milchan is an Israeli-born Hollywood producer and former Mossad operative whose business and intelligence activities have intersected repeatedly with Israeli politics.[1]

## Film career

Milchan produced Oliver Stone's film *JFK*, among a long list of major Hollywood productions.[1]

## Ties to the Netanyahu family

Milchan is a central figure in the corruption case against Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]. Material circulated on Badlands' *Devolution Power Hour* and highlighted in the June 2025 news brief alleged that Milchan supplied a Bugs Bunny doll to [[yair-netanyahu|Yair Netanyahu]] at the request of the boy's parents, an anecdote cited as illustrating the gift relationship at the heart of the [[netanyahu-corruption-trial|Netanyahu corruption trial]].[1]

## Nuclear trigger allegations

Milchan has also been accused of helping to steal hundreds of krytron-style "triggers" used to detonate nuclear weapons.[1] The allegation is that he used his commercial and intelligence contacts to arrange the logistics for exporting the devices out of the United States to Israel in support of that country's illegal and undeclared nuclear weapons program.[1] Badlands commentators cited these allegations as one of several leverage points the Trump team may be applying to Netanyahu.[1]

## The Bibi Files (2024)

Milchan is a central figure in the documentary [[the-bibi-files|*The Bibi Files*]], which premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2024 and drew on police interrogation videos recorded between 2016 and 2018.[2] The footage includes Netanyahu addressing allegations that he and his wife [[sara-netanyahu|Sara Netanyahu]] accepted expensive champagne, Cuban cigars and jewelry from Milchan, with Netanyahu minimizing the champagne and cigars as gifts from a friend and denying knowledge of the jewelry.[2] Witnesses who worked for both men described regular gifts expected in exchange for favours, one such favour being a marginal tax break extension that benefited Milchan; Netanyahu argued his unusual interference on the tax break served the good of the state.[2] Milchan, described in the coverage as the *LA Confidential* producer, corroborated much of the witness testimony, though in one excerpt he gently asked police not to use the word "bribery" because it would make him look bad.[2]

## Nuclear procurement and the Apollo Affair

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Milchan, a former Israeli intelligence officer, "was responsible for procuring materials to advance the illegal Israel nuclear weapons program."[2] He connected Milchan to the [[apollo-affair|Apollo Affair]], in which the Mossad agent [[rafi-eitan|Rafi Eitan]] was effectively caught stealing materials for a nuclear weapon — including hundreds of kilograms of weapons-grade uranium — from a facility in Pennsylvania; Eitan was said to be working with Milchan to ship the materials back to Israel.[2]

This background is offered as the key to Milchan's conflict of interest, or motivation, in producing Oliver Stone's *JFK*, a film that explicitly blames the [[cia|CIA]] — and only the CIA — for the assassination of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[2] Badlands paired the claim with declassified JFK files indicating that outgoing President [[dwight-eisenhower|Dwight Eisenhower]] warned Kennedy the day before his inauguration that his biggest challenge would be stopping Israel from obtaining a nuclear weapon, concluding that contemporary alarm about Iran is projection.[2] Milchan had earlier been discussed by [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] on their *Badlands Story Hour* episode on Stone's *JFK*.[2]
