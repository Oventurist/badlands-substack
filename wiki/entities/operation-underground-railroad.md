# Operation Underground Railroad

Operation Underground Railroad (O.U.R.) is an anti-trafficking organisation founded by [[tim-ballard|Tim Ballard]] and dedicated to rescuing children from sex trafficking operations.[1]

The organisation's work reached a mass audience through the 2023 film [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]], which is based on Ballard's life and stars [[jim-caviezel|Jim Caviezel]].[1] The film's unexpected commercial success — including becoming the first independent film since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic to reach $100 million at the North American box office, according to [[variety|Variety]] — gave Operation Underground Railroad's cause a prominence its supporters attributed to grassroots demand rather than to industry promotion, since mainstream outlets such as Rolling Stone and Bloomberg News covered the film negatively.[1]

## Reception in July 2023

Following the release of [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described Ballard's work through Operation Underground Railroad as "without a doubt, exemplary," crediting the organisation's founder with first-hand knowledge of the insidious nature of the child trafficking network and with documentation supporting his accounts.[2] The organisation was cited in defence of the film after [[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]] critic [[miles-klee|Miles Klee]] dismissed the picture as a "QAnon-tinged thriller," with [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] noting that the film was inspired by the very real work of Ballard's group.[2]
