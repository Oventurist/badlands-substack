# Jaime Harrison

Jaime Harrison is the chair of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] who announced on Friday, August 2, 2024 that Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] had secured enough delegate votes to become the party's presidential nominee.[1] The announcement came before the party's online voting process was scheduled to end the following Monday, reflecting a campaign moving at speed after President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] ended his reelection bid and endorsed Harris less than two weeks earlier.[1]

Harrison pledged that Democrats "will rally around Vice President Kamala Harris and demonstrate the strength of our party" at the convention in Chicago later that month.[1] Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the process amounted to a redefinition of democracy, noting that party officials still planned a state-by-state roll call while openly conceding it was "purely ceremonial" because the outcome was already decided.[1]
