# Netherlands

The Netherlands is a Western European state whose parliament became, in September 2025, the first foreign legislature to follow the United States in formally pressing for the designation of [[antifa|Antifa]] as a terrorist organization.[1]

## Parliamentary motion on Antifa (September 2025)

On Thursday, 18 September 2025, the Dutch parliament adopted a motion from opposition leader [[geert-wilders|Geert Wilders]] calling on the Dutch government to classify Antifa as a terrorist organization.[1] The vote came one day after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that Antifa would be designated a "major terrorist organization" in the United States following the assassination of conservative activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1]

The motion alleged that Antifa cells operate inside the Netherlands and are responsible for threatening politicians, disrupting public events, and intimidating students and journalists.[1] Under Dutch practice, adoption of the motion obliged the government to issue a formal response and to determine whether to implement the requested designation.[1] Contemporary reporting observed that implementation would expand Dutch law enforcement authority over surveillance, monitoring, and prosecution of suspected Antifa activity.[1]
