# Cooley LLP

Cooley is one of the largest law firms in the United States. In October 2025 it was named among the participants in a network of more than 40 large law firms launched to provide free legal assistance to Jewish people facing antisemitism following the October 7, 2023, attack on Israel and the war in Gaza.[1]

The network, led by [[gibson-dunn|Gibson Dunn]] partner [[orin-snyder|Orin Snyder]], also included [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]], [[covington-and-burling|Covington & Burling]], and the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]], and operates through an app allowing individuals to report incidents for review by a steering committee.[1]
