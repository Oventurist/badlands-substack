# Showtime

Showtime is the American premium television network owned by [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) In "A New Hollywood," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites the new Showtime drama *The Agency* — about a [[cia|CIA]] officer who comes in from the cold and settles back into life after years undercover — as part of a cluster of recent intelligence-themed programming that prompted him to investigate who owns the network.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

Discovering that Showtime belongs to Paramount Global, run by [[sheri-redstone|Sheri Redstone]] and merging with [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]], led Carlson to conclude that [[hollywood|Hollywood]] output is being reshaped by Trump-aligned ownership.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)
