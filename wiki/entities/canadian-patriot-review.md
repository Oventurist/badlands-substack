# The Canadian Patriot Review

The Canadian Patriot Review is a publication edited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], who serves as its editor-in-chief.[1] Ehret is also a Senior Fellow of the American University in Moscow and Director of the [[rising-tide-foundation|Rising Tide Foundation]], and has written the four-volume *Untold History of Canada* series, the four-volume *Clash of the Two Americas* series, and *Science Unshackled: Restoring Causality to a World in Chaos*.[1]

Articles hosted at the Review are cited within "Real Patriots Don't Think Like Hitler — Part 1" as supporting documentation for Ehret's claims about Nazis embedded in the Trans-Atlantic banking system, the revival of eugenics, and the postwar CIA financing of the Ukrainian nationalist movement.[1]
