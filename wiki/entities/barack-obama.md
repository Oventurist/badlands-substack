# Barack Obama

**Barack Obama** served as President of the United States from 2009 to 2017. In Badlands Media's "A Dereliction of Duty," he is portrayed as the executive authority behind the covert Libya-to-Syria weapons operation and the non-response to the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]].[1]

## ISIS and the chain of command

The article asserts that Obama "protected and nurtured" [[isis|ISIS]] in Syria and Iraq, allowing its expansion and arming it with heavy weapons including [[surface-to-air-missiles|surface-to-air missiles]].[1] A central mechanism was his requirement that the military obtain [[white-house|White House]] approval for every action on the battlefield — a restriction the article, following Q drop 2640, treats as effectively a [[stand-down-order|stand down order]] protecting ISIS and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] while preserving lucrative weapons sales and money laundering.[1] Q drop 1887 is invoked to explain how [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] destroyed ISIS in under a year: he removed the White House from the battlefield chain of command and unleashed the military.[1] [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], as anti-terror czar running the drone program out of the White House, sat at the top of that approval chain.[1]

## Installation and staffing claims

The piece claims Obama was installed as president by [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed]], who groomed him, and that he was a puppet — an explanation offered for why he "installed traitors at the highest levels of government" across DOJ, FBI, CIA and national security, and filled the White House with people said to hate America.[1] It notes that Brennan, Obama and [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] share a Muslim background, flagging the point for a follow-up article.[1]

## Benghazi

Obama, with [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[john-mccain|John McCain]], is said to have installed the post-Gaddafi Libyan government knowing it was controlled by Al Qaeda.[1] On the night of the attack he convened a 5:00 p.m. White House meeting and directed that all resources be used to save lives, then absented himself from the 7:30 p.m. Deputies Meeting — which the article reads as a "cover your ass" play, since no assets ever deployed and Clinton was never disciplined for obstructing his order.[1] The administration then promoted the story of a protest over an online video as a "red herring."[1] Separately, the article argues [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] was a protected CIA asset who became expendable only when Obama's re-election looked threatened.[1]
