Lange asserts Obama gave the orders to spy on the Trump campaign twice over — to Brennan's [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] to use [[five-eyes|FVEY]] partners in the United Kingdom and Australia, and to Comey's FBI to open a counterintelligence rather than criminal investigation so the material could be kept classified.[60] Because nobody expected [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] to lose, he argues, they left a paper trail leading straight to Obama.[60]

The article states that White House visitor logs for the FISA court judge who approved warrants on [[carter-page|Carter Page]] on what Lange calls phony evidence and doctored emails were among material Obama designated classified and took with him to his presidential library — described as the routine way past presidents hid their crimes.[60] Lange claims [[donald-trump|Trump]] ordered a raid on the Obama Presidential Library office early in his administration, reported only by Q, and that Obama and the media have stayed "shockingly quiet" about it because Obama's own 2009 Executive Order prohibited classifying information to conceal violations of the law — making him, in Lange's phrase, caught violating his own order.[60]

Lange also writes that the entire Obama administration used private emails, secret servers and [[gmail-draft-communications|Gmail drafts]] to avoid the [[freedom-of-information-act|Freedom of Information Act]], citing Q drop 1504's question of why the whole administration used private email.[60] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.

## The Uranium One cover-up

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s March 2025 feature argues that the Obama administration approved the sale of [[uranium-one|Uranium One]] to the Russians and then concealed the fact that American uranium was permitted to travel to Canada and onward to Europe, having assured [[congress|Congress]] and the public that no American uranium would ever leave the country.[61] Reading [[qanon|Q]] drop #199 — "U1 - CA - EU - ASIA - IRAN/NK" — Lange contends the material ultimately reached [[iran|Iran]] and [[north-korea|North Korea]], and that the transfers were a precursor to Obama's Iran nuclear deal, which the administration classified at the highest level of state secrets so that not even a United States senator could review it.[61] In his account Iran was never a genuine threat but a controlled state, and the arrangement's purpose was money laundering.[61]

Lange also asserts, following Q's questions about who paid for Obama to attend Harvard and who was the largest donor to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], that [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] funded Obama's education and pre-political career.[61] He argues the administration hid the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] and the Iran deal from Congress, worked to prevent congressional investigations, and used allied media to brand critics conspiracy theorists.[61] The approval of [[vadim-mikerin|Vadim Mikerin]]'s 2010 U.S. work visa, granted while the FBI was investigating him, is offered as evidence that Obama knew of the criminal probe before the deal was approved.[61] Lange characterises the resulting arrangement as a [[two-tier-justice-system|two-tier justice system]] that swept the scandal aside.[61]

## Carlson's "Obama Youth" thesis (November 2023)

In a November 3, 2023 essay, Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that Obama is a CIA-created character and that his most destructive legacy is deliberate damage done to the youth of America.[62] Carlson contended it is not a great stretch to believe Obama was at least partially created to serve as a father figure for young Americans in need of one, given that 23% of American families lack a father in the home against 3% in China and 4% in India — Obama was not physically present in those homes, but appeared nightly on television, and would never get drunk, ignore them, miss a school event, or leave.[62]

Carlson tied the [[obama-youth|Obama Youth]] framing to Obama's repeal of the [[smith-mundt-act|Smith-Mundt Act]], which in his account made propaganda against Americans legal again immediately after Obama's re-election, with transgender bathroom controversies entering national conversation at once.[62] He noted that respondents in the 18–24 cohort of an October 2023 [[harvard-harris-poll|Harvard/Harris]] survey — the cohort that diverged most sharply from all others on the Israel/Hamas conflict — were three to nine years old when Obama took office and eleven to seventeen when he left.[62]

He wrote that during Obama's presidency the media, social media and teachers glorified his character and diplomacy while ignoring that his administration dropped 26,172 bombs on seven nations in 2016 alone, and disputed the claim of a scandal-free presidency by pointing to drone strikes, [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]], [[operation-fast-and-furious|gun-running to Mexican cartels]], and the IRS targeting of libertarians.[62] Carlson argued that without Obama's presidency the country would probably not have men using women's bathrooms, children modifying their bodies, drag performances for children in public libraries, comparable racial tension, or athletes kneeling during the national anthem, concluding that Obama promised to inspire change without specifying that it would be good change.[62]

## Libya, Benghazi and the arms pipeline

"What Difference Does Benghazi Make?" holds Obama responsible for turning Libya into what [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] called "a full blown training center for jihadists," arguing that the overthrow of [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]] gave [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] control of a state and a safe haven protected by a NATO no-fly zone.[66] After Gaddafi released [[abdelhakim-belhadj|Abdelhakim Belhadj]] and other LIFG prisoners as a goodwill gesture toward the incoming administration, Obama reduced U.S. weapons sales approvals to Libya in 2009 and later imposed a blanket weapons ban.[66]

Joe Lange argues the administration nevertheless authorized covert arms flows. Obama ordered air attacks on Gaddafi's military in March 2011, and one day after [[sidney-blumenthal|Sidney Blumenthal]]'s email to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] promoting an [[osprey-global-solutions|Osprey Global Solutions]] contract, the administration recognized the [[national-transitional-council|National Transitional Council]] as Libya's government and released $30 billion in frozen Libyan assets.[66] The *New York Times* reported in December 2012 that the administration "secretly gave its blessing to arms shipments to Libyan rebels from Qatar," later growing alarmed as evidence emerged that [[qatar|Qatar]] was passing weapons to Islamic militants.[66] Arms dealer [[marc-turi|Marc Turi]] said U.S. officials shut down his own pipeline because he was "getting in the way of the Obama administration's dealings with Qatar."[66]

Obama publicly denied that heavy weapons were moving from Libya to Syria and dismissed the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]] investigations as a "phony scandal" despite four American deaths.[66] Lange argues that ISIS was created and expanded during the Obama administration and that the Benghazi operation was focused on Syria as much as Libya.[66]
