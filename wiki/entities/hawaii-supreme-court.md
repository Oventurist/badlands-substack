# Hawaii Supreme Court

The Hawaii Supreme Court is the highest court of the state of Hawaii. It drew national attention when it ruled that the climate-change lawsuit brought by the city of [[honolulu]] against major fossil fuel producers, including [[exxonmobil|Exxon]] and [[chevron|Chevron]], could proceed to trial.[1]

## The Honolulu climate ruling

The suit, filed in 2020, invoked state-law theories such as public nuisance and trespass and sought billions of dollars in abatement funds for the effects of climate change.[1] By allowing the case past the pleading stage, the Hawaii Supreme Court created an appellate record that commentators described as alarming, and prompted many states to ask — in the words of one commentator, to outright beg — the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|United States Supreme Court]] to intervene and halt the litigation campaign before it spread nationwide.[1]

Because energy utilities are so heavily regulated that the lines between public and private are often indistinguishable, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the case sits inside a wider ecosystem of public-private partnerships in which non-governmental organizations supply an additional layer of plausible deniability when harm occurs.[1] She predicted that "the madness will continue, at least for a little while longer."[1]
