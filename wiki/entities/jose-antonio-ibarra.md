# Jose Antonio Ibarra

Jose Antonio Ibarra is a Venezuelan national and illegal alien convicted of the February 2024 murder of nursing student [[laken-riley|Laken Riley]] in Athens, Georgia. He was identified in reporting as a member of the [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang.[1]

## Entry into the United States

Ibarra crossed the U.S.–Mexico border near El Paso, Texas, on September 8, 2022. The [[joe-biden|Biden]] and [[kamala-harris|Harris]] administration's Department of Homeland Security released him into the U.S. interior on parole, citing a lack of available detention space even though more than 8,000 detention beds were reported to be available at the time. He was also granted a work permit.[1]

That same month Ibarra arrived in the sanctuary city of New York City, from which he requested a "humanitarian flight" to Georgia.[1]

## Conviction

On November 20, 2024, an Athens judge found Ibarra guilty on all counts of murdering Riley after stalking her on an early morning jog around the University of Georgia campus on February 22, 2024. He faced life in prison without the possibility of parole.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary alleged that a prior arrest of Ibarra for "acting in a manner that could injure a child" had been expunged so that he could remain in the country, and argued that his presence in Georgia was the product of calculated federal policy rather than oversight.[1]
