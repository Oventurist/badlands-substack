# Pete Ricketts

Pete Ricketts is a Republican United States Senator from Nebraska who has taken part in Senate Republican efforts to spotlight security conditions along the U.S.–Mexico border.[1]

## Border delegation of October 2023

In late October 2023, Ricketts joined a group of Republican senators — including [[john-barrasso|John Barrasso]] of Wyoming, [[john-cornyn|John Cornyn]] of Texas and [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]] of Texas — on a visit to the southern border. The delegation held a press conference on October 31, 2023, at which Barrasso disclosed that Border Patrol agents had apprehended illegal border crossers carrying explosive devices he described as "tailor-made for terrorism."[1] The senators asserted that more than eight million illegal border crossings had occurred during the presidency of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], and argued that the discoveries indicated an elevated risk of terrorist activity inside the United States.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] framed the border as a "third front" in what its writers described as a deliberately manufactured multi-front crisis benefiting the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]].[1]
