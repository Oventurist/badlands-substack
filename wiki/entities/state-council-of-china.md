# State Council of China

The State Council is the chief administrative authority of the People's Republic of China.[1] In the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" it appears as one of the institutional bodies stripped of authority during [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s consolidation of power.[1]

## Loss of control over the armed police

The article reports that the [[peoples-armed-police|People's Armed Police]], which guards internal security, previously reported jointly to the [[central-military-commission|CCP Central Military Commission]] and the State Council, but now reports to Xi personally.[1] The transfer is presented as part of a wider pattern in which formerly independent heads of state, sitting on the [[politburo-standing-committee|Politburo Standing Committee]], came to report to Xi directly on economic matters, reversing decades of institutionalised, division-of-responsibility rule.[1]
