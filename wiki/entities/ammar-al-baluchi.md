# Ammar al-Baluchi

Ammar al-Baluchi is a Pakistani citizen held as a detainee at Guantanamo Bay who is awaiting trial with four other men accused of planning the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1] His defense team, assembled through the Department of Defense's [[military-commissions-defense-organization|Military Commissions Defense Organization]], includes investigator [[donald-canestraro|Donald Canestraro]].[1]

## Significance to the 9/11 coverup allegations

Canestraro's work on al-Baluchi's defense produced a sworn declaration dated July 20, 2021 compiling interviews with former [[fbi|FBI]] and [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] personnel who alleged that senior CIA officials, including Director George Tenet, deliberately withheld intelligence from the FBI about future hijackers Nawaf al Hazmi and Khalid al Mihdhar.[1] Because the material was gathered in the service of al-Baluchi's defense, it entered the public record through the military commissions process rather than through congressional or commission inquiry.[1]
