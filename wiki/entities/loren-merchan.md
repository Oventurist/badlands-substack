# Loren Merchan

**Loren Merchan** is an American political consultant and the daughter of New York judge [[juan-merchan|Juan Merchan]], who presided over the Manhattan criminal prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by Democratic District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]].[1] She is the president and a partner of the political consulting firm [[authentic-campaigns|Authentic Campaigns]].[1]

## Firm and clients

Under her leadership, Authentic Campaigns was contracted as a vendor by Michigan Governor [[gretchen-whitmer|Gretchen Whitmer]]'s political action committee "Fight Like Hell," which paid the firm $412,000, and by the Arizona Democratic Party, which paid almost $160,000.[1] The firm has additionally worked for Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and for Representative [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]], and Loren Merchan has been reported as linked to the Democratic Attorneys General Association.[1]

## Role in the recusal dispute

Loren Merchan's professional ties to Democratic campaigns and committees formed the basis of repeated demands that her father recuse himself from Trump's hush-money prosecution.[1] Reporting also noted that she had previously posted images depicting Donald Trump behind bars.[1] Juan Merchan refused to step aside and imposed a gag order preventing Trump from discussing members of the judge's family.[1] The reported meetings between [[alex-soros|Alex Soros]] and clients of her firm, held while the trial was under way, intensified the controversy.[1]
