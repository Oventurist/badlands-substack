# Guy Edward Bartkus

Guy Edward Bartkus was a 25-year-old resident of Twentynine Palms, California, identified by FBI investigators as the suspect in the [[palm-springs-fertility-clinic-bombing|Palm Springs fertility clinic bombing]] of May 17, 2025.[1]

## The attack

Investigators say Bartkus packed his 2010 silver Ford Fusion with explosives and drove roughly an hour to Palm Springs, where he intentionally parked outside American Reproductive Centers, an IVF clinic.[1] He is believed to have died in the explosion, and the investigation continued at his Twentynine Palms home the following day.[1] Authorities were still reviewing a series of his online posts and writings, which appear to reflect anti-natalist beliefs — the view that people should not continue to have children.[1]

## Family account

His father, Richard Bartkus, told CBS News Los Angeles that he had not spoken to his son in more than a decade and that he no longer recognized him: "He tried to help people. After Twentynine Palms he just changed."[1] The father said he had been unaware both that his son was a suspect and that he was believed dead, learning of the death only from later reports.[1]
