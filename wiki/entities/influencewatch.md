# InfluenceWatch

**InfluenceWatch** is a research website that profiles nonprofits, foundations, advocacy groups and political operatives and their funding relationships. [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described it as an "immensely-useful website" and used its profiles as a primary source in his January 1, 2024 examination of the organizations behind the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification case]].[1]

## Use as a source

DeLarme quoted InfluenceWatch's profile of [[citizens-for-responsibility-and-ethics-in-washington|Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington]], which describes CREW as a legal advocacy group with previous connections to political strategist [[david-brock|David Brock]] that presents itself as a nonpartisan watchdog directing litigation against government corruption in the public interest.[1] The same profile places CREW within Brock's network of organizations, including the Democratic-aligned opposition research super PAC [[american-bridge-21st-century|American Bridge 21st Century]] and the media criticism organization [[media-matters-for-america|Media Matters for America]].[1]

He also drew on InfluenceWatch profiles to document CREW's funding from left-of-center foundations, including [[george-soros|George Soros]]' [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] and singer [[barbra-streisand|Barbra Streisand]]' Streisand Foundation.[1]
