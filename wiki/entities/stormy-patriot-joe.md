# Stormy Patriot Joe

Stormy Patriot Joe is a pseudonymous analyst and broadcaster associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

He co-hosts **Eye of the Storm** alongside [[absolute-1776|Absolute 1776]], which aired Tuesdays at 10:30pm ET in the network's December 2022 schedule. Badlands promotes the pair as "two of the biggest and most knowledgeable Q Operation experts and analysts," and frames the program as an examination of ongoing events through the lens of what the network calls the largest military intelligence information dissemination program ever launched.[1]

The show sits within Badlands Media's late-night block, a slot the network reserved for its most explicitly research- and theory-driven programming, including [[devolution-power-hour|Devolution Power Hour]] and Baseless Conspiracies.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]
