# US Embassy in Moscow

The **US Embassy in Moscow** is the diplomatic mission of the United States to the Russian Federation, operating under the [[state-department|State Department]].

On March 7, 2024, the embassy issued a public warning telling Americans in Russia that "extremists have imminent plans to target large gatherings in Moscow, to include concerts," and advising them to avoid large gatherings for 48 hours.[1] The advisory preceded the [[crocus-city-hall-attack|Crocus City Hall attack]] later that month, in which more than 140 people were killed at a concert hall outside Moscow.[1]

The same warning was also conveyed to Russian authorities privately, though [[fsb|FSB]] chief [[aleksandr-bortnikov|Aleksandr Bortnikov]] later said it was "of a general nature," and [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] reported that Washington had withheld further detail out of fear that Russian authorities might learn US intelligence sources or methods.[1]
