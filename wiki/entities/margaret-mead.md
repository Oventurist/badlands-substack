# Margaret Mead

Margaret Mead was an American anthropologist identified by Badlands Media as an [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] contractor and a close collaborator of Harvard's [[henry-a-murray|Henry A. Murray]].[1] Together with [[gregory-bateson|Gregory Bateson]], she is said to have driven a "human reprogramming" project organized around the use of LSD and psilocybin during the Cold War period in which [[ted-kaczynski|Theodore Kaczynski]] was experimented upon at [[harvard-university|Harvard]].[1]

The article treats Mead's involvement as one of the facts omitted from mainstream retrospectives of the Murray experiments, including the June 2000 *[[the-atlantic|Atlantic]]* account by Alston Chase, which described Murray's obsession with reprogramming humanity but not his network of collaborators.[1] In this telling, Mead belongs to the same postwar social-science apparatus — bound up with [[cybernetics|cybernetics]] and the ambition to convert "National Man into World Man" — that furnished the intellectual machinery for later [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] management of populations.[1]
