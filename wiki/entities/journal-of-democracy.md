# Journal of Democracy

The Journal of Democracy is an academic publication cited in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" as a source for characterisations of Chinese governance before and during the rule of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1]

## Cited assessments

The article quotes the Journal's observation that "Hu let senior CCP figures govern their own policy domains and build their own patronage networks," while "Xi Jinping has been systematically demolishing the organizational bases for patronage networks controlled by others."[1] It also cites the Journal's description of governance under [[hu-jintao|Hu Jintao]] as "collective leadership," defined as "a system with a division of responsibilities among individual leaders in an effort to prevent arbitrary decision-making by a single top leader."[1]

The author engages critically with these framings, conceding that phrases such as "arbitrary decision-making by a single top leader" appear to reflect Western values, but arguing the picture changes entirely if Xi's decade of centralisation is read as the systematic removal of legacy corruption from the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]].[1]
