# Nikita Khrushchev

Nikita Khrushchev was the leader of the Soviet Union during the early 1960s and the Soviet counterpart to [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] during the most dangerous phase of the Cold War.[1]

## Kennedy backchannels

Badlands Media recounts that the informal Kennedy advisory circle known as [[executive-committee-of-the-national-security-council|ExComm]] — dubbed the "Irish Mafia" by officials excluded from it — deliberately went around the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]] and the news media to establish direct backchannels to Khrushchev.[1] In the outlet's telling, these private lines of communication were the mechanism by which the two governments avoided the war that hardline factions in both countries were, in its phrasing, salivating over.[1]

The episode is cited in November 2024 commentary as a precedent for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s practice of building an advisory circle outside the established national security bureaucracy.[1]
