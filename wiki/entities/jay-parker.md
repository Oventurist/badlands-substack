# Jay Parker

**Jay Parker** is described in the [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series as an Illuminati whistleblower who says he was raised in an "elite" family.[1]

## Testimony cited

Parker is cited parenthetically in support of the article's claim that the [[koryos|Koryos]] and their descendant elites rejected love between the sexes: he reports that the family he was raised in did not believe true love could exist between men and women.[1] The article places this alongside its assertion that the Koryos claimed to be possessed by demons who commanded them to love only members of their own sex, and that the [[scythians|Scythian]] ruling class's abandonment of family formation explains that people's disappearance.[1]
