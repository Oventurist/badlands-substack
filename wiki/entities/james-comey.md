# James Comey

James Comey is a former Director of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]].

## Invalidated indictment

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that in Virginia the dismissal of US Attorney [[lindsey-halligan|Lindsey Halligan]] led to the invalidation of indictments against both Comey and New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]]. The collapse of those cases is presented in the Brief as part of a broader pattern in which courts rejected efforts by the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to install US attorneys without [[senate|Senate]] confirmation, with knock-on consequences for prosecutions those attorneys had brought — including the disqualification of [[john-sarcone|John Sarcone III]] in New York.


## Revived classified leaks investigation (May 2026)

The May 1, 2026 Badlands Brief, citing Bloomberg Law, reported that the Justice Department was weighing a new round of charges against Comey alleging that he leaked classified information. Prosecutors in the Eastern District of Virginia revived an investigation focused on Comey's alleged transmission of documents to Columbia Law professor [[daniel-richman|Daniel Richman]]. The probe had stalled after an initial review the previous year but regained traction after [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] was elevated to acting attorney general, with a small group of EDVA prosecutors meeting regularly with the deputy attorney general's office. No decision on venue had been made, with Eastern Virginia, where Comey resides, and New York, where Richman is based, both under consideration.

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] contrasted this line of inquiry favorably with the earlier "8647" indictment, which she and [[canncon|CannCon]] had publicly called "weak sauce" and likely protected by the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]]. She argued that a classified-leaks case was a far more legitimate use of prosecutorial resources, observing that depending on what the information was, who it went to, and why, the conduct of a sitting FBI Director "could arguably be treason."

## Amicus briefs barred in the criminal case (June 2026)

The Badlands Brief of June 16, 2026 reports that the federal judge overseeing the criminal case against Comey barred the filing of amicus ("friend of the court") briefs, limiting outside participation in the proceedings as the case moves forward.

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] places the ruling among four contemporaneous items she says all "involve weaponized government," and states the Comey matter in inverted form: "Comey claims the government is being weaponized against him because the government is investigating Comey's weaponization of the government against President Trump and his supporters." The other items in the sequence are the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s refusal to revive [[carter-page|Carter Page]]'s FBI lawsuit, the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] investigation of [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]], and the release of internal DOJ warnings over the 2021 school board memorandum. See [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of government]].
