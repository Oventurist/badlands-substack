# James Comey

James Comey is a former Director of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]].

## Invalidated indictment

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] reports that in Virginia the dismissal of US Attorney [[lindsey-halligan|Lindsey Halligan]] led to the invalidation of indictments against both Comey and New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]]. The collapse of those cases is presented in the Brief as part of a broader pattern in which courts rejected efforts by the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to install US attorneys without [[senate|Senate]] confirmation, with knock-on consequences for prosecutions those attorneys had brought — including the disqualification of [[john-sarcone|John Sarcone III]] in New York.
