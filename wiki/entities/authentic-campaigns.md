# Authentic Campaigns

**Authentic Campaigns** is a Democratic political consulting and digital campaign firm whose president and partner is Loren Merchan, the daughter of New York judge [[juan-merchan|Juan Merchan]], who presided over the Manhattan criminal prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] brought by District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]].[1] The firm's client roster and its family connection to the presiding judge made it a recurring focus of conservative and independent media scrutiny during the 2024 trial.[1]

## Contracts and clients

According to reporting by investigative journalist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]] cited by the National Pulse, Authentic Campaigns was contracted as a vendor by "Fight Like Hell," the political action committee of Michigan Governor [[gretchen-whitmer|Gretchen Whitmer]], and by the Arizona Democratic Party.[1] The Whitmer PAC paid the firm $412,000 and the Arizona Democratic Party nearly $160,000.[1] The firm has also worked for Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and for Representative [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]], and Loren Merchan has been reported as linked to the Democratic Attorneys General Association.[1]

## Soros connection

[[alex-soros|Alex Soros]] — the son and chosen successor of [[george-soros|George Soros]], and a frequent visitor to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s White House, reportedly at least twenty times — met with Whitmer and with various Arizona Democrats after those entities had retained Authentic Campaigns, and did so while Trump's trial before Judge Merchan was in progress.[1] Critics presented the sequence as evidence of financial flows connecting Democratic donor networks to the family of the judge overseeing the prosecution of the Republican presidential nominee.[1]

## Recusal controversy

Juan Merchan, a Biden donor, declined repeated requests to recuse himself over his daughter's work, and instead imposed a gag order that barred Trump from publicly discussing the judge's family members.[1] Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the arrangement as an illustration of how billionaire oligarchs use money and influence to control government and society, describing the resulting structure as an organized crime syndicate.[1]
