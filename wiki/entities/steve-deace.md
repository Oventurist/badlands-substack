# Steve Deace

Steve Deace is an Iowa-based conservative radio talk show host. In December 2024 he was reported to be considering a Republican primary challenge to Senator [[joni-ernst|Joni Ernst]] of Iowa, following Ernst's initial hesitance toward [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nominee for Secretary of Defense.[1]

Deace characterized the moment as a shift of grassroots energy toward the upper chamber, telling Politico that "the pressure valve is now pointed in the direction of the Senate" and that "the tea party was not nearly as organized as the Trump movement is."[1] His remarks were cited as evidence of the organized nature of the MAGA campaign against Republicans deemed insufficiently loyal to Trump — the phenomenon described by its participants as [[rino|RINO]]-hunting.[1]
