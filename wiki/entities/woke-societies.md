# Woke Societies

Woke Societies is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host who co-presented [[sauce-and-effect|Sauce and Effect]] with [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] on Mondays at 6pm ET as of the January 2023 schedule, analyzing the week's news with emphasis on documentary sourcing the hosts said the mainstream media omits.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]

## East Palestine reporting

In February 2023 Woke Societies led [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s citizen-journalist coverage of the [[east-palestine-train-derailment|East Palestine train derailment]], which [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] editors credited with "exposing the ongoing ecological disaster spilling out of Ohio while the media continues to focus the public's attention on everything BUT what's happening within its own borders."[3] His February 13 Twitter thread argued that "while the government is trying to psyop the country with downed UFO stories a domestic Chernobyl is happening in #EastPalestine Ohio since February 3rd," citing dead animals, sickened residents, arrested journalists and a mainstream media blackout.[3] The thread went viral, accumulating roughly 2,760 reposts and 5,040 likes.[3]
