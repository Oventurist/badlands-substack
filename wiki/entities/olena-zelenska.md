# Olena Zelenska

**Olena Zelenska** (rendered in some coverage as Elena Zelenskaya) is the wife of Ukrainian president [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] and Ukraine's first lady.[1]

## Bugatti Tourbillon report

In early July 2024, Russian outlet Mockba 24 reported, citing the French site Verite Cachee and an employee of the Bugatti showroom in Paris, that Zelenska had purchased a Bugatti Tourbillon hypercar for €4.5 million, becoming its first owner.[1] According to that account she was shown the vehicle two weeks before its official presentation and placed an order, with delivery expected in 2026 out of a planned production run of 250 cars.[1] The same report recalled earlier claims that she had spent $1.1 million at the Cartier boutique on New York's Fifth Avenue, and referenced an interview in which she said she sometimes felt close to psychological exhaustion.[1] The story circulated widely in alternative media despite originating with Russian-aligned outlets and a source later widely disputed.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] juxtaposed the hypercar report with Guardian reporting on Ukrainian men hiding in their apartments or fleeing the country to evade conscription, presenting the pair as a portrait of elite excess against mass suffering.[1] He wrote that Zelenska "would make Marie Antoinette blush," stressed that the money involved was Western taxpayer money, and characterized the entire arrangement as "a scam" that the world needed to see plainly.[1]
