# Lloyd Doggett

Lloyd Doggett is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Texas.[1]

## Position on the overtime tax deduction

Doggett emerged as a skeptic of the [[overtime-pay-tax-deduction|overtime pay tax deduction]], calling for more data on claims filed and warning of possible abuse once full reporting requirements take effect.[1] He noted the policy's origins in a broader GOP tax package that he and other Democrats opposed.[1] His caution stood against the position of Reps. [[brendan-boyle|Brendan Boyle]] and [[mike-thompson|Mike Thompson]], who backed extending the break.[1]

## First House Democrat to call for Biden's withdrawal

On Tuesday, July 2, 2024, Doggett became the first House Democrat to publicly urge President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to remove himself from the 2024 race following Biden's poor performance in the debate against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] the previous week.[2] Doggett, who had served in the House since 1995, said he had hoped the debate would "provide some momentum" to lift Biden's polling but that it had not.[2]

In his statement he said, "President Biden has continued to run substantially behind Democratic senators in key states and in most polls has trailed Donald Trump. I had hoped that the debate would provide some momentum to change that. It did not. Instead of reassuring voters, the President failed to effectively defend his many accomplishments and expose Trump's many lies."[2] Invoking his district's history, he added: "I represent the heart of a congressional district once represented by Lyndon Johnson. Under very different circumstances, he made the painful decision to withdraw. President Biden should do the same."[2] Doggett, then 77 and only four years younger than Biden, praised the president's legislative achievements while arguing the moment called for passing the torch within the Democratic Party.[2]
