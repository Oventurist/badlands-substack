# Igor Kolomoisky

Igor Kolomoisky is a Ukrainian oligarch and banking magnate, once among the wealthiest men in [[ukraine]], whose media empire carried the programming of [[kvartal-95-studio]] and helped launch the political career of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]]. He was later stripped of Ukrainian citizenship and prosecuted domestically, and is routinely described in Badlands coverage as a disgraced figure.

In the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, Kolomoisky appears as the source of a claim about [[timur-mindich]]: according to Kolomoisky, Mindich is the former fiancé of his daughter. The detail is used by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] to knit Mindich into the older oligarchic network that surrounded Zelenskyy before his presidency, and to support the broader argument that Ukrainian wartime finance, Ukrainian media, and elite networks tied to [[israel]] form a single interlocking system rather than separate spheres.
