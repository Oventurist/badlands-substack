# Igor Kolomoisky

Igor Kolomoisky is a Ukrainian oligarch and owner of the chemical producer Dneprazot, ranked among the wealthiest people in Ukraine as of September 2024.[1]

## Position among Ukrainian oligarchs

According to the outlet Ukrainian Focus, Kolomoisky placed fourth on the list of Ukraine's richest individuals, behind [[rinat-akhmetov|Rinat Akhmetov]] of SCM Group, [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]] of Interpipe Group and former president [[petro-poroshenko|Petro Poroshenko]], and ahead of Ferrexpo owner [[konstantin-zhevago|Konstantin Zhevago]].[1] Most of these figures have contributed to Kiev's war effort.[1]

## Musk sanctions proposal (March 2025)

Kolomoisky was implicitly among the targets of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s March 2025 proposal that sanctioning Ukraine's top ten oligarchs — "especially the ones with mansions in Monaco" — would end the conflict with Russia "immediately," a suggestion Musk offered on X in response to Senator [[mike-lee|Mike Lee]]'s call to halt US financial aid to Kiev.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] singled out Kolomoisky as "the notorious gangster" on the list and argued the named oligarchs were worth investigating for ties to other scandals.[1] He noted that the Ukrainian government had itself recently sanctioned many of these oligarchs, including Kolomoisky, which he said evoked [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s "Our Boy Z" theory.[1] He added that while reading a book about [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] published more than twenty years earlier, he found many of the Ukrainian-Israeli mafia names on his own mind map doing business with Maxwell in the 1980s and 1990s, and wondered how many of the oligarchs listed in the article were tied to that network.[1]
