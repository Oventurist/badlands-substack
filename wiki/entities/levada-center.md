# Levada Center

The Levada Center is an independent Russian pollster whose surveys of public opinion provide one of the few non-state measures of Russian attitudes toward foreign countries.[1]

## June 2025 survey on friendly and hostile countries

In a survey published on 5 June 2025, the Levada Center found that most Russians no longer regard the United States as their principal global enemy — the first time in twenty years of measurement that the U.S. had fallen from first to fourth place on the list.[1] The three countries rated most hostile were Germany (55 percent), the United Kingdom (49 percent) and Ukraine (43 percent).[1] Russians named their closest allies as Belarus (80 percent), China (64 percent), Kazakhstan (36 percent), India (32 percent) and North Korea (30 percent).[1]

The shift coincided with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s return to the White House and his repeated pledges to de-escalate and end the war in Ukraine, as well as his refraining from imposing new sanctions on Moscow while peace negotiations proceeded.[1] Over the same period Berlin and London grew more hawkish, urging a "coalition of the willing" to boost defense spending against Russia.[1] Past downturns in U.S. favorability in Levada polling followed the 2008 Russo-Georgian war and the 2014 annexation of Crimea together with the resulting Washington sanctions.[1]

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the results as evidence that "the Russians know who the puppet masters are," noting that Ukraine ranked third, far behind the governments of Germany and the United Kingdom.[1]
