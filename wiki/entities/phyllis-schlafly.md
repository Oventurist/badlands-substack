# Phyllis Schlafly

Phyllis Schlafly was an American conservative activist and author, cited in Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" as the coauthor, with Admiral [[chester-ward|Chester Ward]], of the 1975 book [[kissinger-on-the-couch|Kissinger on the Couch]].[1]

In that work, Ward and Schlafly documented the internal methods of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], arguing that once its ruling members settle on a policy for the U.S. government to adopt, the Council's research facilities are mobilized to produce intellectual and emotional arguments in support of it and to discredit any opposition.[1]
