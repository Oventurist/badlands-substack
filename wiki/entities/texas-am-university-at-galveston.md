# Texas A&M University at Galveston

Texas A&M University at Galveston is the Pelican Island campus of the Texas A&M system, located across the Pelican Island Causeway from Galveston, Texas.[1]

## 2024 bridge closure

Following the [[pelican-island-causeway-barge-collision|barge strike on the Pelican Island bridge]] on May 15, 2024, the university announced on Wednesday evening that its campus would be closed Thursday through Sunday, citing an "extended outage of the bridge through the weekend."[1] The university's marine unit rescued both barge operators involved in the incident — one who had fallen into the water and one who remained aboard the vessel.[1]
