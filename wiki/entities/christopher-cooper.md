# Christopher Cooper

Christopher Cooper is a judge of the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia, appointed by former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[1]

## Kennedy Center ruling, May 2026

On May 29, 2026, Cooper issued a decision ordering the removal of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s name from the [[john-f-kennedy-center-for-the-performing-arts|John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts]] and blocking a planned two-year closure of the venue for renovations.[1] He ruled that only [[congress|Congress]] has authority to change the institution's name, and that the center could not formally bear a name other than the one Congress established.[1] The case was brought by Rep. [[joyce-beatty|Joyce Beatty]] of Ohio, and the ruling halted a renovation plan approved by the center's Trump-appointed leadership.[1]

Trump responded on Truth Social by criticizing Cooper as an "Obama judge" and defending the renovation project, saying materials had already been ordered and that the building's rust, failing pipes, aging mechanical systems, pest problems, and structural issues required extensive work.[1]
