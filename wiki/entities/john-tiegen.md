# John "Tig" Tiegen

John "Tig" Tiegen is a former United States Marine and CIA Global Response Staff contractor who was part of the security team at the [[cia|CIA]] annex in [[benghazi|Benghazi]], Libya, on the night of September 11, 2012.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

Tiegen appears in [[a-dereliction-of-duty|A Dereliction of Duty]] as a first-hand witness to the disputed stand-down order. In an account given to Politico and quoted in the article, fellow contractor [[kris-paronto|Kris Paronto]] recalls that as the team stood outside their compound villa readying two military vehicles and listening to radio calls for help from [[state-department|State Department]] diplomats, Tiegen told the base chief that the security team was "losing the initiative; we need to go now." Paronto then heard the words "stand down" before the team "bucked" leadership and left on their own accord.

The article presents Tiegen and his colleagues as men who defied the order and went to the aid of fellow Americans, and contrasts their conduct with what The Hill's account calls the "dereliction of duty of so-called 'leaders' in D.C." It further notes that survivors of the annex fight were later required by CIA director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] to re-sign non-disclosure agreements — in two cases presented during a memorial service at Langley honouring [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]] and [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]] — which the article characterises as intimidation intended to suppress testimony before the [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence]].
