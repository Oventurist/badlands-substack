# Aaron Lukas

Aaron Lukas is an American intelligence official who served as principal deputy director of national intelligence in the second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Elevation to acting DNI

Following the announced resignation of Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] in May 2026, Trump stated in a Truth Social post that Lukas, then principal deputy director of national intelligence, would serve as acting DNI.[1] Gabbard's last day at the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] was reported as expected to be June 30, 2026, placing Lukas in line to lead the intelligence community during a period in which the administration was simultaneously negotiating a possible agreement with [[iran|Iran]] and pursuing renewed federal scrutiny of the 2020 election.[1]

## Significance

Because Gabbard was viewed within the Badlands commentary community as the cabinet official most committed to declassification and to what its writers call draining the swamp, the identity and disposition of her successor was treated as consequential for the fate of pending disclosure efforts.[1] Commentary published in the Badlands Brief expressed hope that Gabbard would declassify a substantial body of material before departing, implicitly framing the transition to Lukas as a moment of uncertainty for that agenda.[1]
