# First American Bankshares

First American Bankshares Inc. was Washington, D.C.'s leading banking institution, run by [[clark-clifford|Clark M. Clifford]], the prominent lobbyist and lawyer who represented the [[bank-of-credit-and-commerce-international|Bank of Credit and Commerce International]] (BCCI) when the bank first began buying banks in the United States.[1]

## BCCI's covert takeover

The Federal Reserve Board concluded that BCCI's use of front men ultimately enabled it to buy control of First American Bankshares.[1] The mechanism described in Badlands coverage was straightforward: BCCI extended loans to nominee purchasers that were never intended to be repaid, and when the borrowers defaulted, BCCI took control of the collateral — the bank itself.[1] Among the front men used in the scheme was [[kamal-adham|Sheik Kamal Adham]], the former chief of Saudi intelligence, who was described as guilty not only of money laundering but of acting as one of the front men BCCI used to purchase the biggest bank in Washington, D.C.[1]

## Alleged use as a laundering vehicle

Once under BCCI's effective control, First American was, in this account, used to launder money for drug traffickers and arms dealers, and is characterized by [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] as a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operation.[1] The broader BCCI structure into which First American was folded relied on phony loans, unrecorded deposits, secret files, and illicit share-buying schemes funneled through a global network of shell companies, friendly banks, and wealthy Arab front men.[1]
