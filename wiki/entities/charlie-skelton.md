# Charlie Skelton

Charlie Skelton is a journalist known for his long-running coverage of the annual [[bilderberg|Bilderberg Meeting]], the secretive gathering of political, financial, and media figures.[1]

## Coverage of the 2023 Bilderberg Meeting

Skelton travelled to Lisbon, Portugal, to cover the 69th Bilderberg Meeting in May 2023.[1] In an emailed statement to [[epoch-times|The Epoch Times]], he characterized the attendee roster as "a really high-octane list, leaning heavily into the Russia/Ukraine conflict and the future of [[nato|NATO]]."[1] His assessment aligned with the published agenda, which listed the geopolitical landscapes of Europe, China, India, Russia, and Ukraine alongside artificial intelligence, the banking system, energy transition, and industrial policy and trade.[1]
