# Defense Media Activity

The Defense Media Activity (DMA) is a Department of Defense organization headquartered at [[fort-meade|Fort Meade]], Maryland, which "provides a range of media and services that aim to 'inform, educate, and entertain Department of Defense audiences around the world.'"[1]

## Fort Meade production facility

In 2009 the Army Corps of Engineers spent over $56 million constructing a 185,000-square-foot television production studio at Fort Meade for the DMA.[1] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry pointed to the scale of the facility as a prompt for speculation about the military's role in broadcast production, invoking a Q drop reference to "Red Castle."[1]

## Context

The DMA appears in Badlands analysis alongside the [[defense-information-school|Defense Information School]] and the [[defense-information-systems-agency|Defense Information Systems Agency]] as part of a concentration of Pentagon media and information-warfare capability at Fort Meade, a site that also hosts the [[national-security-agency|NSA]] and [[us-cyber-command|CYBERCOM]].[1]
