# Erica Chenoweth

Erica Chenoweth is an American scholar of civil resistance identified by Badlands Media as another individual "firmly embedded" in the professionalized [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]] space alongside [[maria-stephan|Maria Stephan]].[1] The article groups Chenoweth among the contemporary "nonviolence scholars" whose public talks and white papers, it argues, deploy a specialized vocabulary that conceals government sponsorship behind the language of "strengthening democracy."[1]

## Public profile

Chenoweth is a frequent public lecturer on peaceful resistance strategy, and the article points readers to her talks as representative of the genre.[1] In July 2025, a webinar featuring both Chenoweth and Stephan drew more than 130,000 registrants, organized around the theme of "the moment and your mission" for peaceful resistance.[1]

## Criticism

Badlands argues that Chenoweth and Stephan are "as doctored into the Western political establishment as one can be," and that activists who follow their guidance rarely realize that the civil-society arm of the political and military establishment is directing the movements they join.[1] The article situates this critique within a broader account of how the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], and the [[state-department|State Department]] shifted covert influence work into ostensibly independent NGOs and academic institutions after the exposures of the 1970s.[1]

## Terminology

Terms associated with this milieu and flagged in the article include "nonviolence scholars," "strengthening democracy," and "[[democratic-backsliding|democratic backsliding]]" — the last of which the article says is applied selectively to democratically elected governments the establishment wishes to overthrow.[1]
