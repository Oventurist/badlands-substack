# Emma Vigeland

Emma Vigeland is a co-host of the online political program "The Majority Report," described by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as "a transcendentally cringe and unabashedly pro-DNC YouTube show."[1]

In "The Candidate Who Isn't There," published August 24, 2024, DeLarme identified Vigeland as the author of a post on X that he characterized as an outright fabrication: a claim suggesting that [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] was open to or considering an arms embargo against [[israel|Israel]].[1] DeLarme wrote that Harris had never suggested or even passively indicated any such position, and that "everything about this tweet is completely invented."[1]

According to DeLarme, the post offered comfort to liberal audiences torn between attending pro-Palestine demonstrations and supporting the Harris campaign, but it "further deceived already confused leftists who were having trouble reconciling why this candidate they've been told was so great was directing scorn at protestors."[1] He argued that no one with a modest amount of political awareness could have believed Harris would publicly endorse something as controversial as an arms embargo, given the power of [[aipac|AIPAC]] and the [[democratic-party|Democrat]] establishment.[1]

DeLarme noted an unintended consequence: the episode provoked what he described as the first official statement issued on behalf of the Harris campaign, a denial from Harris's national security adviser [[phil-gordon|Phil Gordon]].[1]
