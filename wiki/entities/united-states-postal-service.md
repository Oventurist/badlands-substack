# United States Postal Service

The United States Postal Service (USPS) is the federal agency responsible for mail delivery and, through its handling of mail ballots, a central actor in American election administration.[1]

## 2026 election order dispute

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order on election administration instructed the USPS to establish new standards for mail-in ballots, one of several provisions blocked in 23 Democrat-led states by a federal appeals court in July 2026.[1] Badlands commentary framed the resulting litigation as a conflict over decision rights: the plaintiff states sued on the theory that the administration cannot govern the Postal Service, yet states plainly have no authority over federal executive agencies either.[1] The Brief argued that if neither the states nor the President may direct the USPS, the question of who is constitutionally authorized to exercise its decision rights becomes unavoidable — and that clarifying such matters is important.[1]
