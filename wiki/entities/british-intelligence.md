# British Intelligence

British Intelligence is presented in the article as the original and central manipulator of the modern Middle East, and as the mentor institution of both the [[cia|CIA]] and [[mossad|Mossad]], described as its protégés.[1]

## Claimed role in the region

The author argues that understanding the Middle East requires grasping that British Intelligence has spent at least the past 200-plus years manipulating the region with the most nefarious intent, as it has elsewhere — and that many of these manipulations were gamesmanship between competing players within the British imperial hierarchy, each jockeying to improve its position at the others' expense.[1] For this reason, he writes, the Middle East has among the most complicated, tragic, and convoluted histories in the world, second only to Russia.[1]

The sectarian map is likewise attributed to this design: Afghanistan and Iraq being mixtures of [[shia-islam|Shia]] and [[sunni-islam|Sunni]] is described as an arbitrary condition intentionally created by the British Crown and the East India Company at the end of the First World War.[1]

## Promised sequel

The article's follow-up piece is to cover the British Cabal's betrayal of the [[hashemites|Hashemites]] and the nefarious usurpation of Hashemite stewardship over the Islamic holy lands by British Intelligence via Abdulaziz bin Abdul Rahman Al Saud, founder of the Kingdom of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and grandfather of [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] — a chain the author connects to the creation of the Ikhwan (Muslim Brotherhood), the exportation of [[wahhabism|Wahhabism]] across the Middle East, and the rise of radical Islamic terrorism in the latter 20th century.[1]
