# British Intelligence

British Intelligence is presented in the article as the original and central manipulator of the modern Middle East, and as the mentor institution of both the [[cia|CIA]] and [[mossad|Mossad]], described as its protégés.[1]

## Claimed role in the region

The author argues that understanding the Middle East requires grasping that British Intelligence has spent at least the past 200-plus years manipulating the region with the most nefarious intent, as it has elsewhere — and that many of these manipulations were gamesmanship between competing players within the British imperial hierarchy, each jockeying to improve its position at the others' expense.[1] For this reason, he writes, the Middle East has among the most complicated, tragic, and convoluted histories in the world, second only to Russia.[1]

The sectarian map is likewise attributed to this design: Afghanistan and Iraq being mixtures of [[shia-islam|Shia]] and [[sunni-islam|Sunni]] is described as an arbitrary condition intentionally created by the British Crown and the East India Company at the end of the First World War.[1]

## Promised sequel

The article's follow-up piece is to cover the British Cabal's betrayal of the [[hashemites|Hashemites]] and the nefarious usurpation of Hashemite stewardship over the Islamic holy lands by British Intelligence via Abdulaziz bin Abdul Rahman Al Saud, founder of the Kingdom of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and grandfather of [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] — a chain the author connects to the creation of the Ikhwan (Muslim Brotherhood), the exportation of [[wahhabism|Wahhabism]] across the Middle East, and the rise of radical Islamic terrorism in the latter 20th century.[1]

## The gold standard of clandestine operators (July 2023)

In "Who are the True Puppet Masters?", [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] described [[mi6|MI6]], "Britain's CIA," as the gold standard of clandestine operators, and British intelligence generally as superior to that of other governments.[2] He wrote that it trained the [[cia|CIA]] for decades before the CIA was officially formed, and that it also trained the KGB.[2]

Carlson used the James Bond image as a counterpoint to real practice: a genuine British intelligence officer, he argued, would be a pudgy middle-aged man losing his hair, driving an ordinary car in ordinary clothes, someone you would not notice was in the room — because British intelligence works by going unnoticed and controlling through others.[2] He cited [[roald-dahl|Roald Dahl]] and [[ian-fleming|Ian Fleming]] as officers sent west during the Second World War to spy on the United States, promote England and draw the Americans into the war.[2] Earlier, Colonel [[edward-house|Edward House]] is described as a British asset who handled [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]], and [[leon-trotsky|Trotsky]] as a British-controlled asset said to have carried on an affair with a cousin of [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]] who served in British intelligence.[2]

Carlson further attributed the origins of modern propaganda and mind control to the [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock Institute]] in England, from which came techniques for manipulating the masses through media and individuals through drugs, torture and psychiatry — Tavistock being, in his account, the predecessor of the CIA's [[mk-ultra|MKULTRA]].[2] He also alleged that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] used British intelligence to construct the [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] hoax against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after 2016, asking why America's greatest ally would take part and why the British would share her interests.[2]
