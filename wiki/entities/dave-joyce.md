# Dave Joyce

Dave Joyce is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Ohio and a co-chair of the [[congressional-cannabis-caucus|Congressional Cannabis Caucus]].[1]

## Veterans cannabis access

In June 2025 Joyce, together with fellow caucus co-chair Rep. [[brian-mast|Brian Mast]] (R-FL), sponsored an amendment approved by the full House that would increase veterans' access to state medical marijuana programs and strike the [[department-of-veterans-affairs|Department of Veterans Affairs]] directive prohibiting VA doctors from issuing cannabis recommendations.[1] The measure was attached to the Military Construction, Veterans Affairs and Related Agencies appropriations bill, alongside a separate amendment supporting psychedelics research and access.[1]
