# Founders Fund

Founders Fund is the venture capital fund of [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]].[1] In July 2025, the Financial Times reported that Founders Fund would be among the investors in [[erebor|Erebor]], a proposed United States bank led by [[palmer-luckey|Palmer Luckey]] and aimed at start-ups and cryptocurrency businesses left without a dedicated lender after the failure of [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]].[1] Other reported backers included [[joe-lonsdale|Joe Lonsdale]], founder of [[8vc|8VC]] and a co-founder of [[palantir|Palantir]].[1]

## Exit from Silicon Valley Bank, March 2023

Bloomberg reported that Founders Fund had no money with [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] as of Thursday morning, March 9, 2023, as the bank descended into chaos, having withdrawn millions of dollars from it.[2] The fund joined other venture firms that took dramatic steps to limit exposure to the soon-to-fail institution, and separately advised its portfolio companies that there was no downside to moving their money away from SVB even if the risk was judged low.[2] Also on that Thursday, Founders Fund began a capital call — the routine practice of asking limited partners to wire money for startup investments — and initially instructed those backers to transfer funds to its accounts at SVB, as it had done for years.[2]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterised the fund's exit, and the parallel moves by other blue-chip investors, as an "inside money stampede" amounting to a digital run on the bank.[2]
