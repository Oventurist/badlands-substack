# Owen Schroyer

Owen Schroyer is an American broadcaster who spent nearly ten years as a host at [[infowars|Infowars]].[1]

## Departure from Infowars

On August 31, 2025, Schroyer abruptly announced that he was leaving Infowars, effective immediately.[1] He cited a long-growing rift with [[alex-jones|Alex Jones]] over Schroyer's critical coverage of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s ostensible relationship with Israel.[1] Schroyer said he had offered to work an additional week and to appear on stream alongside Jones so that the departure could be announced on amiable terms; Jones declined the offer and told him never to return to the studio.[1]

## Context

The Badlands News Brief presented Schroyer's exit as one of several simultaneous ruptures in conservative media over Israel coverage, alongside a confrontation involving Alex Stein on [[tim-pool|Tim Pool]]'s program and the controversy surrounding [[james-lindsay|James Lindsay]] and [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]].[1] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that these platforms shared a record of dishonesty on the subject of Israel and that Trump was "breaking ConInc." by making the issue unavoidable.[1]
