# Ivan Provorov

**Ivan Provorov** is a Russian professional ice hockey defenseman who played for the Philadelphia Flyers of the [[national-hockey-league|National Hockey League]].[1] In January 2023 he became the focus of a national controversy in the United States after declining to take part in his team's "Pride Night" warmup, citing his Russian Orthodox Christian beliefs.[1]

## Pride Night refusal (January 2023)

The dispute erupted on Tuesday, January 17, 2023, when the Flyers held their "Pride Night" promotion at the Wells Fargo Arena in Philadelphia, an event that involved players wearing rainbow uniforms during pregame warmups.[1] Provorov declined to participate, citing his Russian Orthodox faith, and was attacked by LGBT activists and commentators for the decision.[1] The National Hockey League subsequently issued a statement on the matter, and its refusal to fully capitulate to activist demands drew further anger from critics.[1]

Coverage in alternative media framed Provorov as "getting the last laugh," noting a surge of public support — including fans buying his jersey — after he refused to abandon his religious convictions.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributors [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] and [[absolute-1776|Absolute1776]] argued that the episode showed America was "sick and tired of the woke agenda" and represented a moment when the silent majority becomes loud.[1] They described sports as having been converted from an escape into a virtue-signalling instrument, and characterised the backlash against Provorov as an instance of [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] attempting to silence dissenting voices over a matter of individual conscience and freedom of speech.[1] They warned that the NHL, like the NFL and MLB before it, was on a slippery slope that destroys the shared human experience of gathering to enjoy a game.[1]
