# Robert Storch

Robert Storch is a United States Inspector General invoked by Badlands commentary in July 2026 as the official best positioned to document alleged corruption in the flow of American tax dollars through [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## Calls for a Ukraine aid report

Writing after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] withheld $400 million in funding that [[congress|Congress]] had promised to [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] — with Trump saying the money would not be available "until the next president is in office" — Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that what he described as brazen attempts by Congress to launder and steal US tax dollars through Ukraine were now widely perceived.[1] He suggested the moment presented an ideal opportunity for an Inspector General report detailing that alleged corruption, addressing the appeal directly to Storch.[1]

The commentary appeared alongside coverage of Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s Manila meeting with Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] and of the administration's broader pause on portions of previously planned military assistance to Kyiv pending a review of US weapons stockpiles.[1]
