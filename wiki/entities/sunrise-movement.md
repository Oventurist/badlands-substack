# Sunrise Movement

The Sunrise Movement is an American activist organization named in a September 2025 [[capital-research-center|Capital Research Center]] report as a recipient of funding from the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]].[1]

## Stop Cop City endorsement

The report highlighted the Sunrise Movement's endorsement of the [[antifa|Antifa]]-linked [[stop-cop-city|Stop Cop City]] campaign, noting that activists connected to that campaign faced more than 40 domestic terrorism charges and 60 racketeering indictments.[1] The group was cited alongside the [[center-for-third-world-organizing|Center for Third World Organizing]] and the [[ruckus-society|Ruckus Society]] as part of a network the report said had received over $80 million from Open Society since 2016.[1]
