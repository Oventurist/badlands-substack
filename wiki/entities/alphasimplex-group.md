# AlphaSimplex Group

AlphaSimplex Group is a quantitative investment management firm whose market commentary featured in Bloomberg's April 2025 coverage of the U.S. Treasury rally.[1]

The firm's chief strategist and portfolio manager, [[kathryn-kaminski|Kathryn Kaminski]], cautioned that investors rushing into government debt on recession fears were underweighting the inflationary consequences of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariffs, and that the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] would face a difficult trade-off between cutting rates to support growth and containing tariff-driven inflation.[1]
