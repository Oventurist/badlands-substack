# Caitlyn Jenner

Caitlyn Jenner is an American former Olympic athlete and television personality who was married to [[kris-jenner|Kris Jenner]] at the time of the 1994 killings of [[nicole-brown-simpson|Nicole Brown Simpson]] and [[ronald-goldman|Ronald Goldman]].[1]

## Reaction to O.J. Simpson's death

When [[oj-simpson|O.J. Simpson]] died of prostate cancer in April 2024, Jenner reacted immediately by posting "Good Riddance."[1] Badlands Media commentary noted the personal history behind the remark: Jenner had vacationed with Simpson and their families before the killings, and Kris Jenner was reportedly best friends with Nicole Brown Simpson.[1] The commentary observed that while Kris Jenner has stayed silent about the case on social media, Caitlyn Jenner "still has beef" decades later.[1]

The same commentary highlighted that Kris Jenner's ex-husband [[robert-kardashian|Robert Kardashian]] served on Simpson's defense team, meaning the Jenner and Kardashian families were connected to both sides of the affair.[1]
