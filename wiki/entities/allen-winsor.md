# Allen Winsor

Allen Winsor is a United States District Judge sitting in Tallahassee, Florida.[1] In late January 2024 he dismissed the federal lawsuit brought by [[disney|The Walt Disney Company]] against Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] and state officials over the company's loss of its special governing status in the former Reedy Creek Improvement District.[1]

## Ruling in Disney v. DeSantis

Winsor found that Disney lacked standing to sue DeSantis, the secretary of the Florida Department of Economic Opportunity, and the newly constituted governing district.[1] The court concluded that the state law at issue was constitutional on its face, and that in consequence there was no standing for a First Amendment retaliation challenge.[1] Winsor noted that Disney is "not the district's only landowner, and other landowners within the district are affected by the same laws."[1]

Legal commentator [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]], who had criticized the original suit, wrote that the judge appeared to view the litigation as dimly as he did, describing the outcome as a major loss for the company.[1] Turley argued that even if retaliatory motives existed, there was a clear public-policy rationale for seeking uniformity of treatment among companies, and that judicial interference would represent a significant intrusion into legislative priority-setting.[1] A separate Disney lawsuit remained pending in state court in Orlando.[1]
