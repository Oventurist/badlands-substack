# KNOWiNK

KNOWiNK is an American election technology vendor that came under renewed scrutiny in October 2025 following the acquisition of [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] by [[liberty-vote|Liberty Vote]].[1] Both KNOWiNK and Dominion are associated with the same owner, but are described as separate businesses.[1]

## Relationship to Liberty Vote

Following the Dominion acquisition, a chart circulated by Pat Colbeck on X prompted the question of whether KNOWiNK should be included in the corporate diagram.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that KNOWiNK would belong on a "family tree" of the owner's Leiendecker businesses but not on a chart specifically depicting Dominion, since Dominion is understood to be "doing business as" Liberty Vote while remaining its own legal entity.[1]

If KNOWiNK were also to do business as Liberty Vote, that would mean two separate companies sharing a single brand — a configuration consistent with reporting from attendees of a Colorado clerks' meeting with the Liberty Vote CEO, who indicated the change was primarily about branding and reputation management amid a crisis of public trust in election vendors.[1]

## Open questions

Commentary raised unresolved operational questions about whether Dominion and KNOWiNK relationship managers would both be rebranded as Liberty Vote staff, how client portfolios overlap, whether a reduction in force would follow, and whether Liberty Vote would ever become more than a branded holding company.[1] The stated conclusion was that, as of mid-October 2025, the practical level of change to elections resulting from the ownership change appeared low.[1]
