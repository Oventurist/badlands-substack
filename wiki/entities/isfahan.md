# Isfahan

Isfahan is a province and city in central [[iran|Iran]], south of Tehran, that became the focal point of the reported Israeli retaliatory strike of April 18–19, 2024.[1]

## The April 2024 Strike

US officials confirmed that three large explosions were heard in Isfahan on the night of Thursday, April 18, 2024.[1] The [[natanz-nuclear-facility|Natanz nuclear facility]] is located in Isfahan, which lent the location outsized significance despite reporting that [[israel|Israel]] had indicated to Washington it would not attack nuclear targets.[1]

Iranian authorities minimized the event. Iran said its nuclear facilities remained unharmed, and PressTV reported that the situation in Isfahan was normal with no explosion having taken place on the ground.[1] Iranian officials and outlets attributed all explosions heard that night to air-defense interceptions, and the Iran Space Agency described the episode as "a failed and humiliating attempt by Israel aviation."[1] In the aftermath, Tehran's Imam Khomeini International Airport cancelled all flights and Iran established a no-fly zone over its western region.[1]

Fox News reported the strikes were carried out by unmanned aircraft, and US officials characterized the IDF action as limited — an assessment that helped global markets rebound after initial panic selling.[1]
