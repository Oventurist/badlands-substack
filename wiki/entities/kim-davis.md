# Kim Davis

**Kim Davis** is a former Kentucky county clerk known for refusing to issue same-sex marriage licenses, a refusal that generated hundreds of thousands of dollars in fines and damages against her.[1]

## Supreme Court appeal

Davis appealed to the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] seeking to mitigate those financial penalties. On Monday, November 10, 2025, the Court denied the appeal out of hand.[1] Because progressive lawmakers and activists had widely predicted that the Davis case would serve as the vehicle by which the Court's majority would revisit and overturn [[obergefell-v-hodges|Obergefell v. Hodges]], the denial was reported as the Court upholding the same-sex marriage precedent — at least for the time being.[1]

The expectation that Davis's case would trigger such a review rested largely on a brief passage in Justice Clarence Thomas's Dobbs concurrence; no other justice joined that sentiment.[1]
