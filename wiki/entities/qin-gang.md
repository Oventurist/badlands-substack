# Qin Gang

Qin Gang is a Chinese diplomat who served as foreign minister of the People's Republic of China until his dismissal in July 2023.[1] Before taking the post he had served as China's ambassador to the United States, and his removal was accompanied by speculation about a potentially compromising affair during that Washington posting.[1]

## Removal and the 2023 purges

Qin's dismissal was the first of two unexplained removals of senior Chinese officials in 2023, the second being the ouster of defense minister [[li-shangfu|Gen. Li Shangfu]], who was formally dismissed in October 2023 after nearly two months out of public view.[1] Both removals were carried out without official explanation and under a cloud of suspicion, and they were bracketed by an abrupt August 2023 shake-up in the leadership of China's nuclear rocket force — described as the highest-level upheaval in the Chinese military in recent years.[1]

Western outlets including [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] presented the sequence as evidence of the opacity and capriciousness of high-level politics under [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that legacy coverage of the Qin and Li removals reflexively framed Xi's long-running anti-corruption campaign as cover for eliminating disloyal officials, rather than considering that the purges in China, Russia and Ukraine might simply not be going the way Western institutions would prefer.[1]
