# Clare Graves

**Clare Graves** is identified in Part 4 of the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series as the originator of [[spiral-dynamics|Spiral Dynamics]].[1]

## Role in the argument

The article states that Spiral Dynamics was developed by Graves and later expanded by [[don-beck|Don Beck]] and [[ken-wilber|Ken Wilber]].[1] The model holds that consciousness evolves in waves, each stage transcending and including its predecessor, and that conflict emerges when one stage tries to dominate another instead of integrating it.[1]

The essay applies the framework to what it calls the eighth dimension, the level at which polarity is said to lose its charge and oppositional dichotomies collapse into pattern recognition.[1] Solutions at this level are described as emergent rather than oppositional: governance becomes open-source, economies become value-based, education becomes curiosity-driven, and institutions evolve through feedback and participation rather than dictation from above, with the Montessori method offered as an example.[1] The section concludes that at this level the system does not collapse from resistance but dissolves from disinterest.[1]
