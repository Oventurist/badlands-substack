# Chevening Programme

The Chevening Program is a prestigious United Kingdom government international scholarship scheme, described in reporting on the case of [[mahmoud-khalil|Mahmoud Khalil]] as part of Britain's "flagship soft power policy."[1]

## Syria Chevening Program

A dedicated Syria stream of the scheme was administered through the Syria Office of the British embassy in Beirut.[1] According to online records reviewed by [[middle-east-eye|Middle East Eye]], Khalil — the Palestinian Columbia University graduate later detained by US immigration authorities — worked as a local manager for the Syria Chevening Program between 2018 and 2022, alongside work for the [[conflict-stability-and-security-fund|Conflict, Stability, and Security Fund]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited Khalil's Chevening work, together with his associated British security clearance and Syrian NGO projects, as grounds to doubt the portrayal of him as an ordinary student activist.[1]
