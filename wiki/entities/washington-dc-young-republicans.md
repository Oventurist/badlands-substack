# Washington, D.C. Young Republicans

The Washington, D.C. Young Republicans (DCYR) is the District of Columbia chapter of the Young Republicans, a national network of Republican Party activists under the age of forty.[1]

## 2023 Trump endorsement

On Wednesday, May 24, 2023, the organization's board voted unanimously to endorse former President [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]] for the 2024 Republican nomination.[1] The endorsement landed the same day [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] launched his own presidential campaign in a glitch-plagued Twitter Spaces event, and was widely read as a symbolic blow to the DeSantis effort.[1] Among the board members backing the endorsement was Kingsley Cortes, daughter of DeSantis PAC spokesman [[steve-cortes|Steve Cortes]], who had himself defected from Trump's orbit to the DeSantis camp earlier that month.[1]

Coverage of the endorsement was carried by [[the-national-pulse|The National Pulse]] and summarized in the Badlands News Brief, where contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treated it as an early indicator of the primary's likely trajectory.[1]
