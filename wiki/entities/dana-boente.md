# Dana Boente

Dana Boente is an American prosecutor who served as Acting Attorney General during the early months of the Trump administration.[1] He is named in Badlands Media's account of the [[carter-page|Carter Page]] surveillance applications as one of the senior officials whose signature appears on the warrant paperwork later released to the public.[1]

## Signatures on the Carter Page FISA warrants

Citing Law & Crime's review of the released applications, the article reports that the warrants "flatly declare Page is a Russian agent, over and over again," and that the signature pages carry the names of [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]] (page 389), [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] (page 391), Boente as Acting Attorney General (page 271), [[sally-yates|Sally Yates]], and [[james-comey|James Comey]] (page 296).[1] Badlands treats those signatures as documentary evidence tying each signer to what it characterises as a conspiracy to frame President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], given that the applications rested on the unverified [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]].[1]
