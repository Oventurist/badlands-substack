# South Park

South Park is a long-running American animated satire series that in late 2023 became a marker in commentary about the cultural retreat of "woke" politics.[1]

## "Into the Panderverse" and the anti-woke turn

In October 2023 South Park aired the heavily promoted special *South Park: Into the Panderverse*, which lampooned Disney over woke casting shortly after Disney pushed its Snow White remake into 2025.[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] placed the special within a broader backlash that also included Arkansas Governor [[sarah-huckabee-sanders|Sarah Huckabee Sanders]]' executive order eliminating "woke, anti-women words" from state government use, a proposed "get woke go broke" ETF, an anti-woke AI chatbot, and the collapse of the Bud Light brand after a trans marketing campaign — evidence, in that framing, that corporations had realized going woke was killing profits.[1] On October 30, 2023, Twitter owner [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] declared that "The great wakening from woke has happened," which he called "good for civilization."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that many Anons and citizen journalists in the Truth Community complained that South Park's creators had arrived too late, having called out the woke mind virus themselves for nearly a decade.[1] While conceding that South Park and most of the mainstream comedy space only joke about such subjects once it is safe to do so, he argued the significant fact was that the prevailing persuasion of the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] had convinced multi-billion dollar corporations that the moment for such "controversial" comedy had arrived.[1] "The tide has turned on the woke mind virus," he wrote, "which, if politics is downstream of culture, means the tide has turned on [them.]"[1]
