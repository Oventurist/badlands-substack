# Water Island

Water Island is a small island in the United States Virgin Islands that provides natural protection for the bay housing the submarine base near St. Thomas, and which later became a tropical getaway destination.[1] In Badlands Media commentary by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the island's geography is treated as significant because the submarine base it shelters lies roughly eight miles from [[little-st-james|Little St. James]], the island purchased by [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] in 1998.[1]

## The Biden land transaction

Citing a January 2020 [[politico|Politico]] report, Carlson recounts that in 2005 [[james-biden|James Biden]], the brother of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], bought an acre of land with ocean views on Water Island for $150,000, divided it into three parcels, and the following year sold one parcel to lobbyist Scott Green — a longtime Biden associate — for what had been the cost of the entire property.[1] Green subsequently extended James Biden a private mortgage on the remaining parcels rather than the transaction going through a bank.[1] The property itself remained vacant and undeveloped, and the terms of the loan were not disclosed in property records.[1] An easement James Biden obtained granting road access before the sale may have raised the land's value, though it is unclear whether the substantially higher price reflected true market value.[1]

Politico framed the deal as furthering a pattern in which members of the Biden family engaged in financial dealings with people who had an interest in influencing the then-senator and later vice president.[1]

## Biden family visits

After the land deal, Joe Biden vacationed elsewhere on Water Island on at least three occasions.[1] Between election night 2008 and Barack Obama's inauguration, Biden and his family traveled to Water Island over the winter holidays, and he returned over the next two holiday seasons as vice president.[1] In the later years of the Obama administration the family shifted its annual Virgin Islands vacations to St. Croix, which a Water Island landowner attributed to the practicality of traveling with a Secret Service entourage.[1]

## Speculative significance

Carlson argues that owning property and vacationing repeatedly within 10 to 40 miles of Epstein's island would provide convenient cover for discreet visits, and speculates the purchase may have served a dual purpose of money laundering and establishing a reason to be in the area.[1] He notes that arriving by private or government aircraft to one's own property would keep a visitor off the so-called Lolita Express flight logs, or provide an innocent explanation if listed.[1] Carlson explicitly labels this speculation, stating he has no proof that any member of the Biden family set foot on an Epstein property, while maintaining that the proximity itself is indisputable.[1]
