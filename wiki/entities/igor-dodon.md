# Igor Dodon

Igor Dodon is a Moldovan politician and former President of [[moldova|Moldova]] who led the pro-Russian Socialist Party back into power after the collapse of the [[democratic-party-of-moldova|Democratic Party]] machine amid the fallout from the [[moldovan-billion-dollar-bank-fraud|"theft of the century"]].[1]

## Balanced foreign policy

Dodon wasted no time accelerating Moldova's observer status in the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]], which was made official on March 14, 2018 when he called for a policy that was simultaneously pro-Europe and pro-Russia — a position treated as blasphemy by some technocrats.[1] His orientation echoed the philosophy articulated by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], who described the Eurasian Union as an integral part of "a harmonious community of economies from Lisbon to Vladivostok."[1] In a February 2020 interview Dodon stated: "I believe in a big Europe, from Lisbon to Vladivostok. It was Charles de Gaulle who came up with this idea and its repeated often by various world leaders. I think this is the only solution to make Europe stronger."[1]

Dodon also worked to connect Moldova's economic destiny to China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], signing dozens of cooperation agreements, including two major infrastructure deals worth $400 million covering highways encircling Chisinau and connecting to Ukraine.[1] In March 2020 he praised Russia and China as the first countries to respond to Moldova's requests for pandemic assistance, and described as a major achievement that the country "got rid of the oligarch regime" while pursuing a balanced foreign policy.[1]

## Crackdown on Plahotniuc and subsequent lawfare

Dodon launched a relentless crackdown on [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]], with support from the Trump administration's [[state-department|State Department]], which preceded the oligarch's flight from the country in June 2019.[1] After a Western-sponsored government under [[maia-sandu|Maia Sandu]] came to power in December 2020, lawfare was deployed against Dodon himself, and he was arrested on corruption charges in 2022.[1] Following the September 2025 banning of the Patriotic Bloc and Heart of Moldova Party, Dodon said that "Maia Sandu and PAS have turned Moldova into a colony of Soros".[1]
