# Nord Stream 2

**Nord Stream 2** is a natural gas pipeline built to carry Russian gas directly to Germany beneath the Baltic Sea. Completed but never placed into commercial operation, it re-entered the news in March 2025 when the Financial Times reported that U.S. investors were backing a plan to activate the conduit, potentially using it as leverage in the ongoing Ukraine peace talks.[1]

## The 2025 activation plan

The reported deal was being assembled by Matthias Warnig, the former executive director of the Swiss-based Nord Stream 2 operating company, with unnamed American investors supporting the effort in what the Financial Times described as a "once unthinkable move that shows the breadth of President Donald Trump's rapprochement with Moscow."[1] During his first term, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been sharply critical of the pipeline and targeted it with sanctions, later boasting in an October interview that his actions had "killed" the project.[1] Since the start of his second term, however, Trump has pursued closer political and economic ties with Moscow and signaled that Washington could "at some point" lift sanctions.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the development as "sweet irony," recalling that Trump had castigated German Chancellor [[angela-merkel|Angela Merkel]] for simultaneously relying on Russia for European energy while positioning against Russia geopolitically.[1] He argued that after Nord Stream 1 was destroyed — which he described as a failed false flag pinned on the Russians — Europe destroyed its own access to cheap energy, leaving Trump little choice but to "turn on" Nord Stream 2 and leaving Europe little choice but to accept it if it wishes to avoid economic collapse.[1] See also [[nord-stream-sabotage|the Nord Stream sabotage]].
