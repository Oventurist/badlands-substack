# Steve Cogswell

Lt. Col. Steve Cogswell was a deputy medical examiner with the [[armed-forces-institute-of-pathology|Armed Forces Institute of Pathology]] (AFIP) who publicly questioned the official cause of death of Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] following the April 3, 1996 plane crash near Dubrovnik, Croatia.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Challenge to the official finding

Cogswell disputed the finding that Brown died of multiple blunt-force trauma sustained in the crash. Although he did not personally examine Brown's body, he based his suspicions on x-rays and photographs of the top of Brown's head, which showed what he regarded as a perfectly round .45-inch inwardly beveling hole consistent with a gunshot wound.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) He also noted that the original head x-rays showed possible metal fragments in the brain, consistent with a high-velocity gunshot wound, and that those two x-rays were missing from Brown's file — Cogswell suspecting they were never placed in the file at all.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

His account was first published on November 24 by journalist [[christopher-ruddy|Christopher Ruddy]] in the *Pittsburgh Tribune-Review*.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Gag order

On December 5, 1997, AFIP imposed a gag order on Cogswell, requiring him to refer all press inquiries about the Brown case to the institute's public affairs office.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) After a second AFIP pathologist, [[david-hause|David Hause]], corroborated his account, the gag order was broadened to cover all AFIP personnel.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Badlands account treats the silencing of Cogswell and his colleagues as the closing stage of a cover-up protecting the [[bill-clinton|Clinton]] administration from exposure over illegal fundraising and the transfer of military technology to [[communist-china|China]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
