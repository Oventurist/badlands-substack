# Wuhan University of Technology

The Wuhan University of Technology is a Chinese university in [[wuhan|Wuhan]] that appears in Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* as the institution financing the covert Chinese activities of Harvard chemist [[charles-lieber|Charles Lieber]].[1]

## The Lieber arrangement

Lieber secretly accepted approximately $50,000 per month from the Wuhan University of Technology, along with more than $1.5 million to establish a laboratory in China, while simultaneously holding over $15 million in [[national-institutes-of-health|NIH]] and Department of Defense research grants.[1] He was convicted in December 2021 on six felony counts arising from his concealment of these relationships, and in April 2023 was sentenced to time served, six months of home confinement, and a $50,000 fine — less than one month of his Wuhan salary.[1]

Badlands Media treated the arrangement as evidence that [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s compromise extended into biological and dual-use research funnelled toward Wuhan laboratories.[1]
