# J. E. Dyer

**J. E. Dyer** is a commentator who writes at The Optimistic Conservative, quoted at length by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] in his December 2022 column on [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

## Argument on censorship and gaslighting

White cited a December 16, 2022 Dyer column as the most prominent recent statement of the subversion tool he considers central to [[deep-state|Deep State]] operations: [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) In the passage quoted, Dyer argues that the goal of censorship is never merely to silence speech but "to prevent competition for someone's preferred speech, which is to be favored, fostered, enshrined, proclaimed and insisted to be valid and true, regardless of evidence," a condition that routinely devolves into a series of "Big Lies" as competing ideas and empirical reporting are driven out.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

Dyer holds that censorship and gaslighting always go together — "the more you censor, the more latitude you have to gaslight" — and that the purpose of a campaign of political censorship is gaslighting.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci) She adds that communication through the media purporting to come from government agencies increasingly offers no proof of being based on actual evidence or independently verifiable observations, and that accompanying "lies of omission" produce word salads leaving an unmistakable but unsupported impression.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)

White adopted this framework to characterise the "cases, cases, cases" coverage of COVID-19 and the narrative surrounding the January 6th event as instances of the same technique.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/anthony-fauci)
