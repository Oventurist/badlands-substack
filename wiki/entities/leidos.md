# Leidos

Leidos, Inc. is a United States defense and technology contractor. Badlands Media identifies it as one of two primary contractors selected to develop and manufacture computerized clothing for the intelligence community's [[smart-epants|SMART ePANTS]] program.[1]

## SMART ePANTS contract

[[iarpa|IARPA]], in partnership with the Naval Information Warfare Center, Pacific, awarded research contracts totaling more than $22 million to Leidos and [[nautilus-defense|Nautilus Defense]], according to an August 9, 2023 [[pentagon|Pentagon]] announcement.[1]

Reporting in [[childrens-health-defense|Children's Health Defense]]'s publication *The Defender* noted that the investment giants [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] and [[blackrock|BlackRock]] — which benefited from COVID-19 vaccine sales and hold stakes in technology companies developing vaccine passports and digital wallets — are listed among Leidos's top investors.[1]
