# Ken Calvert

Ken Calvert is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from California who chairs the Defense Appropriations Subcommittee, the panel that oversees military spending.[1]

## Reaction to the Vance vice-presidential selection

Calvert appears in Badlands Media's coverage of Donald Trump's July 2024 selection of [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] as his running mate, in which a group of hawkish congressional Republicans reacted with alarm to Vance's opposition to open-ended funding for the war in Ukraine.[1] Reporting by [[politico|Politico]], quoted at length in the Badlands piece, recounted that Calvert told an associate of the pick: "The Ukrainians better hurry up and win."[1] The remark was presented alongside another influential congressional Republican's anonymous comment that he was "scared to death" of the Vance selection.[1]

Badlands treated Calvert's reaction as evidence that the defense-appropriations wing of the Republican Party understood the Vance pick as a genuine break with the party's interventionist establishment rather than a symbolic gesture.[1] In the same reporting, Republicans of the self-described Reaganite wing urged Trump to counterbalance Vance by appointing more hawkish national security officials.[1]
