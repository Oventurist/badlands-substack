# Omeed Malik

Omeed Malik is a financier and co-owner of [[executive-branch-club|Executive Branch]], the ultra-exclusive Georgetown club created by and for the wealthiest members of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s circle.[1]

## Role in the Bessent–Pulte confrontation

At the club's inaugural dinner in early September 2025, Malik intervened when Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] confronted housing finance official [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] in an expletive-laden diatribe, according to three people familiar with the incident.[1] Bessent was reported to be unappeased and sought to have Pulte ejected, telling Malik, "It's either me or him. You tell me who's getting the fuck out of here."[1]
