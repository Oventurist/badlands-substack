# Jerusalem News Syndicate

The Jerusalem News Syndicate is an Israeli media organization that convenes policy conferences on Israeli security and diplomacy, including an annual International Policy Summit.[1]

## 2026 International Policy Summit

The syndicate's 2026 International Policy Summit became a focal point of criticism directed at the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration in the wake of the U.S.–[[iran|Iran]] peace framework.[1] United States Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] used a summit appearance to needle President Trump over his social media habits, remarks that Badlands Media read as evidence of a widening rift between the administration and pro-[[israel|Israel]] officials within it.[1]

Radio host [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] also addressed the summit, delivering warnings that Badlands quoted as "They will murder every single one of you... every woman in this room, they will rape you."[1] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] contributed remarks urging preemptive action against perceived enemies and calling on Jews everywhere to fight antisemites.[1] Former IDF spokesman [[jonathan-conricus|Jonathan Conricus]] offered a dissenting note at the same summit, arguing that Israel remains entirely dependent on the United States.[1]
