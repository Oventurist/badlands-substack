# U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of New York

The United States Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of New York (EDNY) is the federal prosecuting office covering Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island, and Long Island. In October 2025 it brought what the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] described as the largest forfeiture action in the department's history.[1]

## Prince Group prosecution

On October 14, 2025, an indictment was unsealed in federal court in Brooklyn charging [[chen-zhi|Chen Zhi]], founder and chairman of [[prince-holding-group|Prince Holding Group]], with wire fraud conspiracy and money laundering conspiracy for directing forced-labor scam compounds in [[cambodia|Cambodia]].[1] The office, together with the Justice Department's National Security Division, simultaneously filed a civil forfeiture complaint against approximately 127,271 [[bitcoin|bitcoin]] — about $15 billion — seized from unhosted wallets whose private keys the defendant held.[1]

United States Attorney Joseph Nocella said the defendant "directed one of the largest investment fraud operations in history, fueling an illicit industry that is reaching epidemic proportions," and that the case sent a message that prosecutors "will pursue you no matter where you are, no matter who you are, and no matter your insidious methods."[1] The office noted that one laundering network operating in Brooklyn had moved millions of dollars for Prince Group from more than 250 victims in New York and elsewhere.[1] Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] credited the [[fbi|FBI]] under Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] for the investigative work.[1]
