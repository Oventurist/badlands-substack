# Arizona Republican Party

The Arizona Republican Party is the state affiliate of the national Republican Party in Arizona. In January 2024 the organization drew national attention when its chairman, [[jeff-dewit|Jeff DeWit]], was recorded apparently attempting to induce [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] not to enter Arizona's U.S. Senate race.[1]

## 2024 Senate race controversy

An audio recording obtained by DailyMail.com and published on January 23, 2024 captured DeWit, described by the outlet as Arizona's top Republican official, asking Lake to name her price to remain out of politics for two years.[1] DeWit told Lake that "there are very powerful people who want to keep you out" and expressed his own view that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would lose the 2024 election, arguing it was time to make way for someone else.[1] The episode placed the state party at the center of a dispute between its establishment leadership and the populist wing of the Arizona Republican electorate that Lake represented.[1]

The recording was read within the Truth Community as evidence of coordinated establishment efforts to manage Republican primaries from above rather than allow grassroots preferences to determine nominations.[1]
