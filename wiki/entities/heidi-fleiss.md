# Heidi Fleiss

Heidi Fleiss is the former "Hollywood Madam" whose celebrity client list, preserved in a personal address book, is treated by Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] as evidence of how the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] acquired leverage over [[hollywood|Hollywood]].[1]

## The "little black book"

Citing the *New York Daily News*, the article reports that Fleiss's address book — the woman "who counted actor [[charlie-sheen|Charlie Sheen]] among her celebrity clients" — was put up for sale on eBay in 2015, a story first reported by TMZ.[1] According to the listing, the names in the book included celebrities, politicians, sports figures and musicians.[1] Lange stresses that this "little black book" was discussed publicly long before Jeffrey Epstein's own address book entered mainstream media coverage.[1]

## Interpretation as a compromise operation

In the article's reading, Fleiss did not merely run a prostitution business: she "helped the CIA control Hollywood," supplying the agency with the same kind of sexual-blackmail leverage it is said to have used to capture powerful people in other fields.[1] Lange argues the CIA reuses this "playbook" repeatedly because it has always worked.[1] He connects the resulting culture of impunity to the career of [[harvey-weinstein|Harvey Weinstein]], who he says became one of the biggest producers in Hollywood and remained above the law for decades — until his conviction and imprisonment during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s presidency, which Lange characterises as a deliberate message to Hollywood that "things have changed."[1] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
