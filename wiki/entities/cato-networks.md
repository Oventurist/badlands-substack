# Cato Networks

Cato Networks is a cybersecurity and networking company led by Israeli-American chief executive [[shlomo-kramer|Shlomo Kramer]].[1]

## Public profile

The company entered Badlands coverage on December 29, 2025, when Kramer appeared on CNBC in his capacity as Cato Networks CEO and argued for limiting the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] to "protect it" from AI-generated deepfakes and misinformation.[1] In the same appearance he promoted AI-driven "authenticity scores" for social media content and closer government-platform cooperation to verify information, presented as national security measures against rising cyber warfare risks — a framing consistent with the company's cybersecurity business.[1]
