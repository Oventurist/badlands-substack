# Thomas Fortune Ryan

Thomas Fortune Ryan was a powerful American financier who served as manager of the [[sleepy-hollow-club|Sleepy Hollow Club]], the elite New York institution that [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] identifies as the base of a British-directed private intelligence agency in the United States.[1]

Ryan joined J.P. Morgan in financing [[nikola-tesla|Nikola Tesla]]'s towers before the outbreak of World War I, a detail Ehret ties to the later claim by United States Naval Intelligence that German agents used the Long Island wireless installations to communicate with Berlin.[1]
