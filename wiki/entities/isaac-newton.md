# Isaac Newton

Sir Isaac Newton appears in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] survey of [[hermeticism|Hermeticism]] as one of the many scholars drawn into the study of the [[emerald-tablet|Emerald Tablet]].[1]

DeLarme notes that although the Emerald Tablet was not a large tome of instructive texts but a small batch of concise texts carved into a single emerald slab — readable in its entirety in a matter of minutes — many scholars dedicated their lives to puzzling over the text's meaning.[1] Newton is cited as the leading example: he devoted the later portions of his life to studying the tablet, a work regarded by history's greatest alchemists as harbouring the secret of the *prima materia* and the [[philosophers-stone|Philosopher's Stone]] and as the foundation of European [[alchemy|alchemy]].[1]
