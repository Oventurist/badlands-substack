# USS Spruance

**USS Spruance** is a United States Navy warship — an Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer named for Admiral Raymond A. Spruance, victor of the Battle of Midway — that appears in the Badlands corpus as the instrument of the April 2026 American naval interdiction of an Iranian cargo vessel.

## The M/V Touska interception

According to the Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026, [[u-s-central-command|U.S. Central Command]] reported that on April 19 the Spruance intercepted the Iranian-flagged cargo vessel M/V Touska in the north Arabian Sea as it headed toward an Iranian port, enforcing naval blockade measures. The command said the destroyer issued warnings and then fired into the vessel's engine room after the crew failed to comply, after which U.S. Marines boarded the ship and it remained in American custody. [[iran|Iran]] denounced the seizure as American "armed piracy" and signaled it would respond, while the incident threw into doubt a planned second round of negotiations that [[donald-trump|President Trump]] had said would move to [[pakistan|Pakistan]].

## Context in the corpus

The Brief presents the Spruance episode as the sharpest escalation in an on-again, off-again conflict cycle in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] region, and uses it to illustrate the difference between Trump's saber-rattling and genuine escalation — asking whether the administration is searching for an on-ramp or an off-ramp. The warship is thus treated less as a discrete actor than as a prop in what Badlands commentators describe as the engineered fog of a war meant to be believed but never fought.
