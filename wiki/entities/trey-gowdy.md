# Trey Gowdy

Trey Gowdy is a former Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives who chaired the [[house-select-committee-on-benghazi|House Select Committee on Benghazi]], the congressional panel created to investigate the September 11, 2012 attacks on U.S. facilities in Benghazi, Libya.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Role in the Benghazi investigation

According to Badlands Media, Gowdy and his Republican committee members conducted an investigation that was "delayed and repeatedly obstructed" by House Democrats and the Obama administration, yet still managed to assemble and document extensive factual findings about actions and inactions before, during, and after the attacks.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The committee's report, Part I, "Terrorist Attack on U.S. Facilities in Benghazi," opens with the words of a Diplomatic Security Agent under fire in Benghazi: "If you guys don't get out here, we're all going to f—-ing die."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Badlands Media characterizes the committee's central finding as a "smoking gun": that a direct order of the Commander in Chief was obstructed by Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The account credits Gowdy's committee with surfacing that conclusion in the same way it credits him with exposing Clinton's private email server used in secret during the Obama administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The committee's reconstructed timeline is a central pillar of the Badlands argument that the failure to respond to the Benghazi attack was a deliberate [[dereliction-of-duty|dereliction of duty]] rather than incompetence — documenting that senior diplomats in Tripoli learned of the attack within three minutes, that [[africom|AFRICOM]] learned of it within thirty minutes, and that the [[state-department|State Department]]'s Diplomatic Command Center in Washington knew immediately but took roughly an hour to notify the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## Role in the background investigations

The America for Sale series pairs Gowdy with House Judiciary Chairman [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] as the two members who played very important roles in criminal investigations proceeding out of public view before both resigned from Congress.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) It argues they remained active in those investigations after leaving, and that the work generated hundreds of thousands of sealed indictments awaiting unsealing.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Q drop 2462 asked who briefed "GOODLATTE & Gowdy" on classified intelligence regarding the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] and FBI; the article answers [[ezra-cohen-watnick|Ezra Cohen Watnick]], General Flynn's deputy whom [[donald-trump|Trump]] protected from dismissal by H.R. McMaster and later installed at the DOJ specifically to pass classified evidence to the two congressmen.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Gowdy, Goodlatte and Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] jointly requested the investigation that Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] assigned to U.S. Attorney [[john-huber|John Huber]], working with Inspector General [[michael-horowitz|Michael Horowitz]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Their resignations, together with that of Sessions, are presented as coordinated rather than coincidental — "military planning at its finest."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) After finishing their House investigations into Obama administration crimes, Gowdy and Goodlatte handed their evidence to Grassley as Senate Judiciary Chairman, evidence the article says concerns treason at the highest levels and is connected to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] and [[doug-band|Doug Band]].[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Trump publicly thanked both men, telling them what they had done "will never be forgotten."[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The House-to-Senate handoff

"Do You Believe in Coincidences?" places Gowdy among the House investigators — with [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] and [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] — who received purposely declassified material from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and used it to expose the Russian collusion hoax.[3] Q material quoted in the article repeats that "Goodlatte, Gowdy and Sessions all resigned," which the author interprets as a coordinated transformation of the investigators into "expert witnesses" who brought [[united-states-senate|Senate]] inquiries up to speed and pointed them toward the key players once the Senate Judiciary Committee's oversight of the [[us-department-of-justice|DOJ]] and [[fbi|FBI]] could be used.[3]

## Discovery of the Clinton server (Badlands account)

"[[the-biggest-boomerang-in-history|The Biggest Boomerang In History]]" credits Gowdy with discovering in 2015 that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] had used a [[hillary-clinton-private-email-server|private server]] as Secretary of State in order to avoid the [[freedom-of-information-act|Freedom of Information Act]].[3] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that [[james-comey|James Comey]] would never have opened an investigation into the server on his own initiative and was forced to do so once Gowdy's discovery, together with pressure from Congress and the Inspector General, made inaction impossible.[3] The article notes that the media helped Clinton present the subsequent deletion of some 33,000 emails as "no big deal."[3] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
