# Trey Gowdy

Trey Gowdy was a U.S. Representative from South Carolina who chaired the House Select Committee on Benghazi from 2014 to 2016. In the Badlands Media article "A Dereliction of Duty," Gowdy is identified as the leader of the congressional investigation that uncovered evidence of the Obama administration's obstruction of the military response to the 2012 Benghazi attack.

According to the article, Gowdy's committee "discovered the smoking gun" connecting the dots to a direct order of the Commander in Chief being obstructed by [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]. The source cites The Hill, quoting the committee's report as stating that Secretary of State Clinton "obstructed" the direct order to rescue Americans under attack by Al Qaeda. It further claims that Gowdy had previously discovered Hillary's illegal server that the Obama administration was using in secret.

The article asserts that Gowdy's committee documented "volumes of facts" recounting actions and inactions before, during, and after the attacks, and that the report starkly contrasted the heroism of GRS personnel, Diplomatic Security Agents, and Team Tripoli fighting to protect American lives in Benghazi with the "total incompetence and dereliction of duty" of leaders in Washington, D.C. According to the source, the committee's work was delayed and repeatedly obstructed by House Democrats and the Obama administration.

The article frames Gowdy as one of the few officials who attempted to expose the truth about Benghazi, and claims that his investigation revealed that the Obama administration had deliberately chosen not to attempt to rescue the 37 Americans under attack. It notes that despite the committee's findings, nobody faced consequences for defying the Commander in Chief's direct order.

## Classified briefings and the prosecution phase (America for Sale - Part 2)

[[america-for-sale-part-2|America for Sale — Part 2]] pairs Gowdy with [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] as the two congressmen who, in the author's account, conducted the substantive investigations into [[barack-obama|Obama]]-era conduct before resigning from [[congress|Congress]]. Citing Q drop 2462 — "Who briefed GOODLATTE & Gowdy on classified intel re: DOJ & FBI?" — [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] identifies [[ezra-cohen-watnick|Ezra Cohen Watnick]] as the briefer and claims [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] installed him at the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] for that purpose.

The article states that Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] launched the [[john-huber|John Huber]] investigation in response to a request from Gowdy, Goodlatte and Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]], and quotes Sessions' letter to Congress confirming that those matters fell "fully within the scope of [Huber's] existing mandate." It further asserts that Gowdy, Goodlatte and Sessions resigned around the same time as part of a coordinated plan, that Trump publicly thanked the congressmen for work that "will never be forgotten," and that Gowdy and Goodlatte handed their evidence to Grassley and the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]]. These claims are interpretive and contested.
