# Sigmund Livingston

**Sigmund Livingston** was the lawyer who created the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] (ADL) as a civil rights advocacy group in 1913.[1] The organisation he founded was formerly known as the Anti-Defamation League of B'nai B'rith, and its primary historical focus was fighting anti-Semitism.[1]

Badlands Media invokes Livingston's founding purpose as a benchmark against which the modern organisation is measured, arguing that the ADL was historically seen as a noble body committed to opposing genuine antisemitism and speaking for the interests of the Jewish community as a whole, rather than only for those who fund and support the liberal establishment.[1] The article contends that the current generation of leadership under [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]] has undone the work of the preceding generations that Livingston's founding mission set in motion, chiefly by trivialising the term "anti-Semitic."[1]
