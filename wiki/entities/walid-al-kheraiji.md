# Walid Al-Kheraiji

Eng. Walid Al-Kheraiji is the Saudi Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs.[1]

On Monday, November 18, 2024, Al-Kheraiji received Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister for Political Affairs, [[majid-takht-ravanchi|Dr. Majid Takht-Ravanchi]], at the ministry's headquarters in Riyadh.[1] The two discussed bilateral relations between [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[iran|Iran]] and ways to further develop them across various fields, as well as the latest regional and international developments.[1] The reception was attended by Saudi Ambassador to Iran Abdullah Al-Anzi, Iran's Ambassador to the Kingdom Ali Reza Enayati, and Director General of the Asian General Administration Nasser Al-Ghannoum.[1]

Badlands commentary read the Riyadh meeting alongside [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s contemporaneous contact with Iran's UN ambassador as evidence of concurrent diplomatic tracks between Iran and what the writers term the Sovereign Alliance.[1]
