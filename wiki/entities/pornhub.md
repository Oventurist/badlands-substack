# Pornhub

Pornhub is a major online pornography platform that anti-exploitation advocates have accused of complicity in mass sexual crime, including the hosting and monetization of material depicting trafficking and child sexual abuse.[1]

## New York Fashion Week collaboration

In February 2023, fashion designer [[luis-de-javier|Luis De Javier]] made his [[new-york-fashion-week|New York Fashion Week]] debut with a collection produced in collaboration with Pornhub.[1] Though the show was described in fashion press as "a runway show that sexually liberated in style," the [[national-center-on-sexual-exploitation|National Center on Sexual Exploitation]] argued that it normalized and glamorized a company complicit in mass sexual crime, and issued a call to action against the collaboration.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[absolutetruth1776]] described Pornhub as an online platform that knowingly facilitates and profits from child sex trafficking, and placed the collaboration in a sequence beginning with the [[balenciaga|Balenciaga]] advertising scandal.[1]
