# Jenny Racicot

Jenny Racicot is the former romantic partner of Maine U.S. Senate candidate [[graham-platner|Graham Platner]] whose public accusation of sexual assault threw his 2026 Democratic campaign into turmoil.

## The accusation

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Racicot accused Platner of sexually assaulting her in late 2021 after he showed up at her home uninvited while heavily intoxicated. She described him ignoring her protests, grabbing her forcefully, and proceeding with sex against her will, and said he claimed not to remember the incident the next morning. Racicot did not file a police report at the time, but later shared details with a therapist and others, and said she sent Platner a message weeks afterward stating the encounter was non-consensual.

Platner denied the allegations as "categorically false" and framed them as a coordinated political hit timed before Maine's candidate filing deadline; he stepped back from the campaign temporarily while Senator [[ruben-gallego|Ruben Gallego]] and Representative [[ro-khanna|Ro Khanna]] withdrew their endorsements, with Khanna calling the allegations serious and credible. The Brief's commentary (by Ashe in America) expresses skepticism of sexual assault allegations that surface in political campaigns and were not reported at the time, while questioning why the Democratic Party would treat the allegation as disqualifying at all.
