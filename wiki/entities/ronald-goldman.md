# Ronald Goldman

Ronald Goldman was a friend of [[nicole-brown-simpson|Nicole Brown Simpson]] who was killed alongside her in the June 1994 knife slayings in Los Angeles.[1]

[[oj-simpson|O.J. Simpson]], Nicole Brown Simpson's ex-husband, was acquitted of criminal charges in the killings but was later found liable for the deaths in a civil case brought by the victims' families.[1]

## Family reaction to Simpson's death

After Simpson died of prostate cancer in Las Vegas in April 2024 at age 76, Goldman's father, Fred, and his sister, Kim, released a statement acknowledging that "the hope for true accountability has ended."[1] They wrote that "The news of Ron's killer passing away is a mixed bag of complicated emotions and reminds us that the journey through grief is not linear."[1]
