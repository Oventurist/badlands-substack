# Eli Sharabi

Eli Sharabi is a former hostage who was freed from captivity in Gaza in February 2025 and who was reported to be traveling to Washington to meet US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in early March 2025.[1]

## Meeting with Trump

Sharabi's relative Sharon Sharabi said on Sunday, March 2, 2025, that his brother would meet Trump at the White House on Tuesday along with other released captives, whom he did not name.[1] Speaking to Channel 12 news, Sharon Sharabi said the delegation intended to impress upon the president the importance of all remaining hostages being freed.[1] He stated that the trip had been facilitated "due to the generosity" of [[miriam-adelson|Miriam Adelson]], a major financial backer of Trump's political campaigns.[1] At the time of reporting there was no confirmation of the meeting from officials in either the United States or Israel.[1]
