# Christopher Worrell

Christopher Worrell is a January 6, 2021 defendant whose treatment in the [[dc-jail|DC jail]] became the subject of a federal contempt finding.[1]

In 2021 a federal judge held DC jail officials in contempt over Worrell's treatment after officials failed for months to obtain care for his broken wrist and then obstructed an inquiry into the matter.[1] Congressman [[ken-buck|Ken Buck]] cited this episode in September 2023 as "at least one valid allegation of abuse concerning a January 6th defendant," while arguing there was no evidence the failure was widespread or that it occurred because of Worrell's status as a January 6 defendant.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] countered that Worrell was not only denied treatment for a broken wrist but was also denied cancer treatment, as reported by The Hill on November 3, 2021, in a case in which the judge ordered him released and stated that the "court has zero confidence" that the DC jail would provide proper treatment and not retaliate against him.[1]
