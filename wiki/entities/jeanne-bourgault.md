# Jeanne Bourgault

Jeanne Bourgault is the head of [[internews|Internews]], an international media-development nonprofit historically funded in large part by the [[usaid|United States Agency for International Development]].[1]

## WikiLeaks disclosure

In February 2025, [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] published an item — reproduced in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] under the heading "The Ultimate USAID Couple?" — alleging that Bourgault is secretly married to [[ray-jennings|Ray S. Jennings]], a high-profile regime-change specialist for USAID.[1] WikiLeaks stated that Bourgault paid herself $451,000 in compensation while telling her alma mater that she goes to the office only "once or twice a month."[1]

WikiLeaks said the marriage appears to have only a single obscure reference online, in a University of Washington alumni magazine feature titled "Alumna brings an information revolution to the developing world," and that it had never been disclosed by USAID, Internews, or the 4,291 media outlets connected to the organization.[1] Badlands framed the disclosure as evidence of undisclosed conflicts of interest in the taxpayer-funded media-development sector.[1]
