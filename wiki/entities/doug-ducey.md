# Doug Ducey

Doug Ducey was the Republican Governor of Arizona during the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]] and its disputed aftermath.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] surmised that Ducey left the state on Friday, November 18, 2022, creating the "temporary disability" contemplated by Article V, Section 6 of the Arizona Constitution and requiring another officer to take the helm.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) That designation reportedly fell to Attorney General [[mark-brnovich|Mark Brnovich]] rather than to Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], who under the constitutional order of succession stands ahead of the Attorney General — an anomaly Gillespie said no outlet in the state had explained.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
