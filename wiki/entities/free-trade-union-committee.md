# Free Trade Union Committee

The Free Trade Union Committee (FTUC) was formed by the American Federation of Labor in 1944 to target communist-led unions in France and Italy.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) It funnelled resources to splinter pro-communist groups and supplied intelligence to U.S. diplomats, operating in coordination with a [[state-department|State Department]] that under Secretary of State [[edward-stettinius|Edward Stettinius]] explicitly worked with unions to ensure post-war European labor movements aligned with American free-market ideals.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

By 1949 the [[cia|CIA]] was funnelling millions of dollars through the FTUC to split European labor federations along Cold War lines, funding non-communist rivals such as France's Force Ouvrière and Italy's non-CGIL unions.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The article characterizes this as operational sabotage rather than mere financial aid, noting that in Italy AFL operatives backed by CIA cash orchestrated strikes and propaganda against the CGIL, helping the Christian Democrats retain power in the 1948 elections.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The committee's work is presented as the direct precursor to the later foreign programs of the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
