# Alliance for Retired Americans

The Alliance for Retired Americans is a United States advocacy organization representing retired workers, closely tied to the organized labor movement.[1]

In early February 2025 the Alliance was one of three union-affiliated groups — alongside the [[american-federation-of-government-employees|American Federation of Government Employees]] and the [[service-employees-international-union|Service Employees International Union]] — that filed suit against Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]], accusing him of unlawfully sharing their members' personal information with the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] team led by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

The lawsuit produced a temporary order from U.S. District Judge [[colleen-kollar-kotelly|Colleen Kollar-Kotelly]] limiting access to the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]] payment system to Treasury officials and two named DOGE employees.[1] Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] characterized the litigation as lawfare and noted the involvement of [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]] in the effort to block Musk's access.[1]
