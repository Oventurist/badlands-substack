# Qasim al-Aboudi

**Qasim al-Aboudi** is the National Security Adviser of [[iraq|Iraq]], appointed in mid-2026 by Iraqi Prime Minister [[ali-al-zaidi|Ali al-Zaidi]].[1]

## Claims regarding Ukraine-backed militants

In late July 2026, al-Aboudi announced that Iraqi authorities had arrested a small number of militants who, under questioning, confessed to carrying out attacks inside Iraqi territory on behalf of [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] He was quoted as saying: "We arrested a limited number of groups that carried out attacks inside Iraqi territory and targeted several facilities. During questioning, they confessed to working for Ukraine."[1] Kyiv's Foreign Ministry rejected the claim as unfounded Russian propaganda.[1]

The statement was significant because it cut against the prevailing Western and Gulf attribution of drone attacks on [[saudi-arabia|Saudi]] energy infrastructure and [[us-military|U.S.]] forces to militias aligned with [[iran|Iran]] and the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|IRGC]].[1] Iran-backed militias operating in Iraq separately denied any involvement in attacks against Saudi Arabia.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated al-Aboudi's disclosure as a key data point in an argued pattern in which governments in the Middle East and Africa assert that Ukraine is supporting and arming militias tied to [[isis|ISIS]] and seeking to destabilize governments aligned with [[donald-trump|President Trump]], framing Ukraine as a proxy of the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
