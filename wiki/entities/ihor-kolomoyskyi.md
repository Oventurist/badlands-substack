# Ihor Kolomoyskyi

Ihor Kolomoyskyi is a Ukrainian oligarch and businessman widely described as the financial patron behind the political rise of President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1]

## Arrest

In early September 2023, Ukraine's justice authorities arrested Kolomoyskyi, a man Badlands Media described as "the man who funded his campaign and was seen as Ukraine's puppet master."[1] The arrest came within the same 48-hour period in which Zelensky dismissed Defence Minister [[oleksii-reznikov|Oleksii Reznikov]], a sequence Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized as Zelensky's own anti-corruption purge.[1]

## Significance in Badlands' framing

Burning Bright argued that the arrest of Kolomoyskyi, occurring alongside the removal of Reznikov and a public reversal on the failed counteroffensive, suggested a two-way severing of control between Zelensky and his backers. In his account, the Media Industrial Complex and globalist leadership had begun their "turn" on Zelensky and Ukraine just as Zelensky and Ukraine appeared to be turning on them, prompting the question of where the Ukrainian president's loyalties actually lay.[1]
