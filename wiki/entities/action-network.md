# Action Network

The Action Network is a left-of-center digital organizing platform described as a "tool" created in 2012 by [[john-kerry|John Kerry]] and Brian Young.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized Kerry as an Ivy League globalist and treated the platform as core infrastructure of the organized anti-Trump movement.[1]

## Role in protest organizing

According to DeLarme, the Action Network was behind many of the protests directed against the policies of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first term, ranging from the Dakota Access Pipeline demonstrations to the Women's March.[1] Its value lay in an infrastructure of apps and websites that allowed participating groups to communicate and coordinate on the fly.[1]

## Affiliated projects

[[the-comedy-resistance|The Comedy Resistance]], the celebrity-comedian activist group co-founded by Stephen Kessler and [[heather-podesta|Heather Podesta]], was described as a project of the Action Network and an extension of [[the-resistance|the Resistance]] movement.[1]
