# Brian Dahle

Brian Dahle is a conservative California state legislator who was the Republican nominee against Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] in the 2022 gubernatorial election.[1]

## The 2022 campaign and Newsom's ad strategy

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described Dahle's nomination as a product of Newsom's own advertising strategy rather than organic Republican enthusiasm.[1] In the run-up to the primary, Newsom broadcast advertisements emphasizing the conservative credentials of the Republican opponent he perceived as least likely to run a competitive campaign, reminding voters that Dahle vehemently opposed abortion and advocated for gun rights.[1]

The tactic worked because of California's [[jungle-primary|jungle primary]] system, in which all candidates run on a single ballot and the top two advance regardless of party.[1] Dahle was overwhelmingly supported by Republican voters who otherwise would have known little about him had it not been for Newsom's ads, securing the party's nomination; any genuine opposition to Newsom was eradicated by the ensuing [[culture-war|culture war]] onslaught, and the governor cruised to an effortless victory.[1]
