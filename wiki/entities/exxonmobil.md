# ExxonMobil

ExxonMobil, referred to in the source simply as Exxon, is an American oil and gas major listed in the Badlands Media essay [[and-iran-iran-so-far-away|"And Iran, Iran So Far Away"]] among the Western energy firms funding the [[atlantic-council]].

The article groups Exxon with [[chevron]], [[bp]] and [[shell]] as companies that "all donate to the Atlantic Council, and have a vested interest in the Iran deal." Through that funding, it argues, the energy majors gain influence over the [[state-department]], the [[cia]] and the [[pentagon]], and can lobby for policies that would open [[iran]] to Western investment on a far larger scale than Chevron's earlier deals with [[naftogaz]] in Ukraine.

The article also notes that [[blackrock]] holds considerable, if not majority, equity stakes in Exxon and its peers, tying the asset manager's interests — and those of BlackRock Investment Institute chairman [[tom-donilon]] — directly to the fate of the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|JCPOA]].
