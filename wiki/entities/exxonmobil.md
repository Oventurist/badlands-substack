# ExxonMobil

ExxonMobil is a major American integrated oil and gas corporation whose posture toward post-Maduro [[venezuela|Venezuela]] became a point of friction with the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration in January 2026.[1]

After the company's chief executive described Venezuela as "uninvestable," Trump indicated he might block ExxonMobil from investing in the country, according to a Reuters report carried by The Guardian.[1] The remark came as Trump publicly stated the United States would be "using oil" and "taking oil" from Venezuela and that U.S. oversight of the country's oil sector would last "much longer" than a year.[1]

ExxonMobil's caution stands in contrast to the position taken by the [[us-oil-and-gas-association|U.S. Oil & Gas Association]], which argued that the overwhelming show of American military force resolved producers' security concerns and cleared the way for a return to Venezuelan operations.[1]
