# Henrik Sass Larsen

Henrik Sass Larsen is a former Danish government minister and once a senior member of Denmark's ruling Social Democrats, having served as trade minister.[1]

## Conviction

On Monday, September 1, 2025, Larsen was sentenced to four months in prison for possession of child pornography, according to multiple media reports.[1] He admitted to having more than 6,000 photographs and 2,000 videos of child sexual abuse on his computer, but denied the criminal charges, insisting the files were connected to his own search for evidence of childhood abuse he had suffered.[1] A unanimous jury in the Copenhagen City Court rejected that explanation and delivered its verdict on Monday.[1]

Police uncovered the material during searches of his electronic devices in 2023 and 2024.[1] The case became public in March 2024 and cost him his membership in the ruling Social Democratic Party.[1]

In court, the 59-year-old — who spent part of his childhood in foster care before being adopted — said that in 2018 he received a video link that appeared to show him being abused as a toddler.[1] He told the judges that this file and another vanished after viewing, that he searched online to trace the perpetrators, and that he regretted not going to police.[1] Prosecutor Maria Cingari said she was "satisfied" with the verdict but called it sad that a person who had overcome a difficult start in life ended in such circumstances, stressing that nothing could justify possession of child pornography.[1]
