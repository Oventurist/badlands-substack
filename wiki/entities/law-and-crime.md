# Law & Crime

Law & Crime is a legal news outlet that covers courtroom proceedings and high-profile trials in the United States.[1]

Badlands Media cited Law & Crime's live-trial coverage of the February 2023 bail hearing for FTX founder [[sam-bankman-fried|Sam Bankman-Fried]], in which Senior U.S. District Judge [[lewis-kaplan|Lewis Kaplan]] pressed both prosecutors and defense counsel over the terms of a $250 million bond package.[1]
