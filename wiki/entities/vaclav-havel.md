# Václav Havel

Václav Havel was a Czech dissident writer and later president, best known in the context of the anti-communist opposition for his 1978 essay [[the-power-of-the-powerless|The Power of the Powerless]].[1]

## Invocation at Davos 2026

Havel's essay resurfaced in mainstream geopolitical discourse when Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] cited it in his address to the January 2026 [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meeting in Davos.[1] Carney quoted the argument that the communist system "did not persist because of violence, alone," but "through the participation of ordinary people in rituals they privately knew to be a lie" — what Havel termed "living within a lie" — concluding that "the system's power comes not from its truth, but from everyone's willingness to perform as if it were true."[1]

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the speech as surprisingly truthful and perhaps unintentionally aligned with the independent-media critique of institutional legitimacy, joking that it read as though written by Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]].[1]
