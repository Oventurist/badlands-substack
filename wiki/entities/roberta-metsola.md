# Roberta Metsola

Roberta Metsola is a Maltese politician who serves as President of the [[european-parliament|European Parliament]].[1]

## Role in the Migration Pact vote

Following the Parliament's April 2024 approval of the [[eu-asylum-and-migration-pact|EU Asylum and Migration Pact]], Metsola declared "History made" on social media, praising the package as a "robust legislative framework on how to deal with migration and asylum" and noting that the legislation had been "10 years in the making" but that the [[european-union|European Union]] had kept its word.[1] Her endorsement placed the Parliament's leadership firmly behind a compromise that drew criticism from both the left and the nationalist right, the latter arguing the pact transfers sovereign control over migration to Brussels.[1]
