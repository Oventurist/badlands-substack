# Joshua Jahn

**Joshua Jahn** (c. 1996 – 24 September 2025) was a 29-year-old Texan identified by authorities as the suspect in the sniper attack on an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] facility in Dallas, Texas.[1]

Authorities said Jahn "fired indiscriminately" at the building at around 6:20 a.m. on Wednesday, 24 September 2025, hitting a van in the sallyport in which detainees were held; one detainee was killed and two were critically injured, while no ICE agents were hurt.[1] Police said Jahn was found dead on the roof of a nearby immigration attorney's office with a self-inflicted gunshot wound.[1]

An unspent shell casing recovered from the scene bore the words "ANTI ICE," according to [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], though the exact motive remained unclear.[1] Jahn's brother Noah told NBC that he did not believe his brother had "strong feelings about ICE," and told The Independent he was still processing the news before ending the call.[1] The episode is covered in detail at [[dallas-ice-facility-shooting|the Dallas ICE facility shooting]].[1]
