# Upside Foods

Upside Foods is a California-based company producing cultivated ("lab-grown") chicken meat, and one of two firms whose products were cleared by the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] for human consumption.[1]

## Product and process

Upside Foods harvests cells from live animals and chicken tissue and uses those cells to grow meat in stainless-steel tanks known as bioreactors, rather than raising and slaughtering birds.[1] The resulting product is intended for mass production and sale on the American consumer market.[1]

## Regulatory clearance

In 2022 the FDA announced that Upside Foods' laboratory-derived chicken was "safe to eat," clearing the way for commercial-scale production.[1] The agency stated it had "no further questions" about the product's safety, and Commissioner [[robert-califf|Robert M. Califf]] declared it safe for human consumption.[1] Upside Foods was one of two lab-grown chicken products green-lit by the FDA, alongside [[good-meat|GOOD Meat]].[1]

## Criticism

Coverage in [[natural-news|Natural News]] framed the approvals as the corrupt federal regulator green-lighting "tumor nuggets" grown from cancer cells.[1] Badlands contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] argued that "there's real food and then there's 'imitation' food," that the food supply is tainted to an extraordinary degree, and that consumers should seek direct relationships with farmers rather than trusting big-chain grocery stores or restaurants.[1]
