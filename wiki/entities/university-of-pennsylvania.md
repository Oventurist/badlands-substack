# University of Pennsylvania

The University of Pennsylvania (Penn) is an Ivy League institution that became a focal point of the post–October 7 campus antisemitism controversy. Its president was among the university leaders — alongside those of Harvard and MIT — who testified before [[congress|Congress]] on the subject.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

Penn graduate [[eyal-yakoby|Eyal Yakoby]] spoke at a House press conference held before that testimony, facilitated by Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) Yakoby said that Penn, "once renowned for groundbreaking discoveries like the mRNA vaccine," had become "a chilling landscape of hatred and hostility" in which Jewish students "tiptoe through their days; uncertain and unsafe."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) He contrasted the university's strict COVID-era guidelines governing class attendance and graduation walks with what he described as an absence of comparable enforcement when students and faculty defied policies to intimidate Jewish students.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] reads Yakoby's Penn testimony as a demand for COVID-grade institutional enforcement against dissenting opinion about Israel, and as the clearest instance of the right adopting the campus [[safe-spaces|safe space]] logic it had previously ridiculed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)
