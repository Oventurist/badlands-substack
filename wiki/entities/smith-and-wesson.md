# Smith & Wesson

Smith & Wesson is an American firearms manufacturer whose publicly traded shares are cited in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as a market barometer of public fear and of demand for private gun ownership.[1]

## As an indicator of backlash against gun control

In "The Systematic Dismantling of the Deep State," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] used the company's share price to argue that the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s manipulation eventually rebounds against its own objectives.[1] He wrote that for decades the network has been "gunning" for Americans' guns, creating false flag events to traumatize the public into surrendering [[second-amendment|Second Amendment]] rights, with every school or mass shooting quickly followed by a push for more gun control.[1]

Carlson then set that campaign against the October 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack on Israel, which he characterised as a real or perceived plan initiated by the same "Cabal masters" who push gun control in America.[1] In his reading, the sponsors of activists who insist that "no one needs an assault rifle" had just handed Americans a vivid reason for owning one.[1]

He supported the point with two price moves: during June 2020, the month of the [[2020-george-floyd-riots|BLM riots]], Smith & Wesson stock rose 86 percent, and in the month following the Hamas attacks it rose 11 percent.[1] Carlson summarised the result in a single word — "Whoops!" — presenting it as an instance of lies and manipulation coming back to bite their authors.[1]
