# Young Republican National Federation

The Young Republican National Federation is the Republican Party's political organization for members between the ages of 18 and 40, with a reported membership of roughly 15,000.[1] In October 2025 the organization and its state affiliates became the subject of national controversy after leaked private messages from a Telegram group chat among Young Republican leaders were published by [[politico|Politico]].[1]

## Leaked Telegram chat

According to the reporting, leaders of Young Republican groups across the country worried about the consequences of their chat leaking but continued participating.[1] Participants referred to Black people as monkeys and "the watermelon people," mused about placing political opponents in gas chambers, discussed raping their enemies and driving them to suicide, and praised Republicans they believed supported slavery.[1]

William Hendrix, vice chair of the Kansas Young Republicans, used variations of a racial slur more than a dozen times in the chat.[1] Bobby Walker, then vice chair of the New York State Young Republicans, referred to rape as "epic."[1] Peter Giunta, at the time chair of the same New York organization, wrote in a June message that "everyone that votes no is going to the gas chamber," a remark referring to an upcoming vote on whether he should become chair of the Young Republican National Federation; he added, "Im going to create some of the greatest physiological torture methods known to man. We only want true believers."[1]
