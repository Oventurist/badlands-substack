# Global News

Global News is a Canadian news organisation cited in Badlands Media coverage of Canadian legislation and policy debates.[1] In February 2024 it reported on [[bill-s-210|Bill S-210]], the Senate bill requiring age verification on pornographic websites, detailing the bill's passage through the Senate in April 2023, its second reading in the House of Commons in December 2023, and the opposition of [[pornhub|Pornhub]]'s owners, who described the measure as the "wrong legislation" and raised the prospect of blocking Canadian users.[1]
