# Al-Shabaab

Al-Shabaab is a Somalia-based militant organization designated by the United States as a terrorist group. In late 2025 it became the focus of a U.S. domestic financial investigation after allegations surfaced that American tax dollars had been diverted to the group.[1]

## Minnesota diversion allegations

On December 1, 2025, Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] announced that the U.S. Treasury Department was investigating allegations that tax dollars from Minnesota may have been diverted to Al-Shabaab in Somalia.[1] Bessent framed the inquiry as targeting conduct that occurred "under the feckless mismanagement of the Biden Administration and Governor Tim Walz."[1]

The announcement followed remarks by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] describing Minnesota as a "hub of fraudulent money laundering activity" under Democratic Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]], itself an apparent response to unverified media reports circulated by several Republican lawmakers claiming Al-Shabaab had benefited from fraud committed in Minnesota.[1] Walz's office did not immediately respond to a request for comment, and he had previously accused Trump of demonizing the entire Somali community.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary treated the allegations as emblematic of a broader pattern in which, in the contributors' framing, "US TAXPAYERS are paying for it all" and the American taxpayer funds the very actors the government claims to be fighting.[1]
