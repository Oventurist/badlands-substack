# Bureau of African Affairs

The Bureau of African Affairs is the component of the United States [[state-department|State Department]] responsible for policy toward sub-Saharan Africa, led by an Assistant Secretary of State and charged with managing bilateral relations across the continent.

The bureau appears in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] as a public advocate of the Trump administration's campaign against the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]]. According to the Brief, the bureau posted on X welcoming [[chad|Chad]]'s announced withdrawal from the court and called on more of the ICC's 125 member states to withdraw and "reclaim their sovereignty from this flawed institution." The statement aligned the bureau with Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s declared campaign to dismantle what he described as the ICC's threat to national sovereignty, and with the February 2025 executive order by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] imposing sanctions on the court.
