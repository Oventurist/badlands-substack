# Brian Nugent

**Brian Nugent** is identified in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as one of the best-known English translators of the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]], the French-language document collection ("Les Protocoles de Toronto") leaked by the Canadian journalist [[serge-monast|Serge Monast]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

In "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" (22 January 2024), contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] cites Nugent's editorial note on how readers should approach the material. According to Nugent, "Monast himself concluded that it is up to each individual reader to make their own mind up on whether to believe this document, by, he recommends, seeing if it matches the politics, and recent political history, of the last few years."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Deschamps treats that instruction as a methodological invitation rather than a disclaimer, writing that the more he has put the exercise to the test, the more the documents have proven their accuracy to him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The article's central experiment — feeding passages of the Protocols to [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] and asking it to map them onto the strategic, operational and tactical layers of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] — is presented as an extension of the reader-verification test that Nugent attributes to Monast.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The English edition Deschamps references is published under the title *The Toronto Protocol: The Real Global Elite*, and he separately points readers to an archived scan of the underlying documents.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
