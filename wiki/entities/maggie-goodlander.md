# Maggie Goodlander

Maggie Goodlander is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives and a veteran of the armed services or intelligence community.[1] She was one of six lawmakers featured in the 90-second video first posted from Senator [[elissa-slotkin|Elissa Slotkin]]'s X account in November 2025, in which the group spoke directly to U.S. service members and urged them to uphold the Constitution and defy "illegal orders."[1]

## Sedition controversy

On November 20, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] accused Goodlander and her colleagues — Slotkin, [[mark-kelly|Mark Kelly]], [[jason-crow|Jason Crow]], [[chris-deluzio|Chris Deluzio]] and [[chrissy-houlahan|Chrissy Houlahan]] — of sedition "punishable by DEATH."[1] The lawmakers issued a joint statement calling on Americans to condemn "the President's calls for our murder and political violence."[1] The affair is covered in Badlands material as the [[seditious-six|Seditious Six]] episode.[1]
