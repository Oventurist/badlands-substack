# Frank Bisignano

Frank Bisignano is head of the [[social-security-administration|Social Security Administration]] and, as of October 2025, also the first-ever chief executive officer of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]].[1]

## Appointment as first IRS CEO

Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] appointed Bisignano to the newly created IRS chief executive role, a move that bypasses the Senate confirmation process; Bessent remained the IRS's acting commissioner.[1]

In an email to SSA employees on Monday, October 6, 2025, Bisignano wrote, "I am honored that Secretary Bessent has asked me to serve in this position," and highlighted his experience managing "multiple mission-critical organizations at the same time."[1] He pointed to his tenure at JPMorgan Chase in the 2000s, when he served simultaneously as co-chief operating officer and as chief executive of the bank's mortgage banking unit.[1]

Bessent described Bisignano as "a businessman with an exceptional track record of driving growth and efficiency in the private and now public sector," citing shared "customer service and technological goals" between the IRS and the SSA.[1]
