# Plum Island Animal Disease Center

The Plum Island Animal Disease Center is a United States government biological research laboratory located on an island off the coast of New York.[1]

## Siting rationale

Plum Island's offshore location was strategically chosen to ensure that the laboratory's highly dangerous research would not affect the American homeland — an isolation-based containment strategy.[1] The facility is owned and operated by the [[department-of-homeland-security|US Department of Homeland Security]].[1]

## Replacement by NBAF

Plum Island is being superseded by the [[national-bio-and-agro-defense-facility|National Bio and Agro-Defense Facility]] (NBAF), a $1.2 billion Level-4 maximum-biocontainment laboratory in Kansas whose completion was celebrated on May 24, 2023.[1] The transfer of the nation's most dangerous animal-pathogen research from an isolated island to the center of the US food supply — a decision first announced in 2008 — has been a persistent source of public criticism.[1]
