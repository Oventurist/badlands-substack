# Edmonton Oilers

The Edmonton Oilers are a National Hockey League franchise that lost the 2025 Stanley Cup Final to the [[florida-panthers|Florida Panthers]].[1]

## 2025 Stanley Cup Final

The Oilers were defeated 5-1 by the Panthers in Game 6 on Tuesday, June 17, 2025, allowing Florida to repeat as champions.[1] Coverage of the series emphasized the Panthers' "dirtbag hockey" — trash talk, hard checking, and the ability to goad opponents into losing composure — as the decisive edge.[1]
