# Never Back Down

Never Back Down is the super PAC allied with Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]]' 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Fundraising and 2023 campaign turmoil

DeSantis and Never Back Down self-reported large fundraising hauls, with the campaign saying it raised $20 million in the second quarter of 2023.[1] The reports came amid broader questions about the strength of the DeSantis campaign as it struggled to gain traction against former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

In July 2023, two senior campaign advisers — [[dave-abrams|Dave Abrams]] and [[tucker-obenshain|Tucker Obenshain]] — departed to help a pro-DeSantis outside nonprofit group, part of what the campaign described as a "DeSantis is everywhere strategy."[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the departures as "rats fleeing a sinking ship," describing the campaign as "a farcical operation covered by a thin veneer of False Trumpism."[1]
