# Character.AI

Character.AI is an artificial intelligence company whose conversational chatbot products became the subject of a Texas state investigation described in Badlands Media coverage of Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]]'s litigation campaign.[1]

## Texas Investigation

An August 2025 Badlands Media essay reported that Paxton's office had opened probes into Character.AI over predatory interactions with minors.[1] The essay grouped that inquiry with the investigation into CCP-linked [[deepseek|DeepSeek]], arguing that both target the human and geopolitical vulnerabilities inside the rise of artificial intelligence rather than the technology in the abstract.[1]

## Strategic Framing

The author placed the Character.AI probe within a wider pattern of state-level action intended to constrain what the essay called the "narrative machines" of the information battlespace, alongside the [[pieces-technologies|Pieces Technologies]] transparency settlement and the large privacy settlements with [[meta-platforms|Meta]] and [[google|Google]].[1] In that framing, child-safety enforcement is one of several intersecting pressure points — technical, ethical, and national security — where legal authority and narrative shaping can be applied simultaneously, an approach the essay identified with [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[1] The essay argued that AI safety rulings of this kind reshape platform moderation practices and feed back into data-privacy and training-input questions, producing reinforcing loops that constrain AI developers from several directions at once.[1]
