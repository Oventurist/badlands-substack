# Iran

Iran is the Middle Eastern state at the center of the conflict that began on February 28, 2026 and had entered its fourth week by Monday, March 23, 2026.[1]

## The 2026 conflict

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] threatened on March 22, 2026 to strike Iran's power plants unless Tehran fully reopened the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] within 48 hours.[1] Iran's [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Revolutionary Guards]] replied that the strait would be "completely" closed if the United States followed through, and Iranian officials warned that energy infrastructure in countries hosting U.S. military bases would become legitimate targets if Iranian power plants were attacked.[1] Iranian ballistic missile strikes hit Arad and Dimona in [[israel|Israel]] on March 22, injuring at least 31 people in Arad and five in Dimona, while Brent crude rose to $112.19 a barrel.[1]

Commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that threatening water and energy infrastructure is the strategically rational Iranian course — crippling an enemy society without directly attacking civilians — and that Iran, advised by [[russia|Russia]] and [[china|China]], seeks to hold the moral high ground, declining to mimic Israel's Gaza campaign in order to preserve its ability to characterize that campaign as genocide.[1]

## Leadership and succession

Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] was killed in a U.S.- and Israeli-backed airstrike in Tehran on February 28, 2026, and his son [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] was appointed supreme leader on March 9.[1] The new leader's near-total absence from public view produced the [[cardboard-ayatollah|Cardboard Ayatollah]] meme cycle.[1] Badlands commentary holds that after [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]] implemented a [[continuity-of-government|Continuity of Government]] plan in August 2025 the office was removed from the chain of command entirely.[1]

## Strategic reading

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s [[iranian-knot|Iranian Knot]] analysis casts Iran as undergoing "a form of precision disentanglement," with its most destabilizing elements removed through operations that "wear the mask of escalation while serving a deeper strategic function."[1]
