# Pulitzer Center on Crisis Reporting

The **Pulitzer Center on Crisis Reporting** is a journalism-funding nonprofit named by Badlands Media as one of the organizations engaged in "policing journalism."[1]

It appears alongside the [[national-press-foundation|National Press Foundation]], the [[international-center-for-journalists|International Center for Journalists]], the [[solutions-journalism-network|Solutions Journalism Network]] and the [[poynter-institute|Poynter Institute]], all of which the article says are connected to individuals belonging to one or multiple of the clandestine think tanks discussed in the series, and all of which it treats as instances of the influence of [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]] over the press.[1]
