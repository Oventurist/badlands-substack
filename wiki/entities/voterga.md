# VoterGA

**VoterGA** is a Georgia-based election integrity advocacy group best known for its litigation seeking public access to ballots cast in [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] during the 2020 presidential election.[1] Its cofounder is [[garland-favorito|Garland Favorito]].[1]

## Fulton County ballot litigation

A Georgia law that took effect on January 1, 2025, designed by Republican legislators, was intended to allow any member of the public to request high-resolution copies of ballots in order to review votes independently.[1] Fulton County nonetheless refused to release its 2020 ballots, continuing what reporting described as a four-year fight between skeptics of the 2020 result and the Democratic-run county government they accuse of wrongdoing.[1]

Favorito, who has sought access to Fulton's ballots through two pending lawsuits on behalf of VoterGA, said that "Fulton obviously has something to hide" and that the refusal proved "there was something seriously wrong about the 2020 election in Fulton County."[1] Multiple investigations have countered allegations of widespread fraud in Fulton County, and three vote counts showed that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] lost Georgia to Joe Biden by about 12,000 votes.[1]

## Advocacy on Georgia voting machine vulnerabilities

In June 2023, VoterGA's [[garland-favorito|Garland Favorito]] appeared on Bannon's [[war-room|War Room]] to review the newly unsealed [[halderman-report|Halderman Report]] on Georgia's [[dominion-imagecast-x|Dominion ImageCast X]] machines, arguing that the disclosed vulnerabilities could be used to alter the QR codes on printed ballots and thereby change voter selections without detection.[2] Favorito's own 2020 election lawsuit, which the Georgia Supreme Court remanded to a lower court after finding he had standing, was described at the time as one of the only such cases still alive in the country.[2]
