# David Friedman

**David Friedman** is a pro-[[israel|Israel]] figure associated with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Middle East diplomacy, cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as an example of a "smart, realist pro-Israel voice" who should be included in efforts to repair the fractured [[america-first-movement|America First]] coalition.[1]

## Proposed role in an America First Foreign Policy Council

In [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], DeLarme's third proposed remedy was the creation of an "America First Foreign Policy Council" inside the [[white-house|White House]] or the RNC.[1] He suggested seating [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]], [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]], [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and [[russ-vought|Russ Vought]] at the same table as realist pro-Israel voices such as [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] and Friedman, with a mandate to hammer out and publish a written doctrine covering where the United States stands with Israel, where red lines are drawn, how [[iran|Iran]] is handled, and what "no forever wars" means in practice.[1] DeLarme argued that publishing such a doctrine could attract 2026 and 2028 candidates to run on it, turning a shouting match into a governing platform.[1]
