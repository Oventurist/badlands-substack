# Nick Sorter

Nick Sorter is an independent journalist and commentator active in online right-of-center media.[1]

## SNAP influencer payments claim

In March 2025, amid a debate over proposals to ban the purchase of candy and soda with [[snap|Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program]] benefits, Sorter and others pointed out that conservative influencers defending soda's eligibility were reportedly being offered upwards of $1,000 per post to promote the position on behalf of soda companies.[1] Commentary by [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] cited Sorter's observation alongside the note that Coca-Cola earns billions of dollars per year from soda purchased with SNAP funds, giving the industry a strong lobbying incentive, and that a cluster of influencers had posted in defense of the status quo at roughly the same time.[1]
