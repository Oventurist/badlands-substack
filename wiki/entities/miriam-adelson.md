# Miriam Adelson

Miriam Adelson is a major financial backer of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s political campaigns.[1]

## Role in the 2025 hostage delegation

In March 2025, the family of freed Gaza hostage [[eli-sharabi|Eli Sharabi]] said that a trip bringing Sharabi and other released captives to meet Trump at the White House had been facilitated "due to the generosity" of Adelson.[1] The delegation's stated purpose was to press the president on the importance of securing the release of all remaining hostages.[1]

## Role in the 2024 election and the MAGA schism

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued in November 2025 that without the support of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the right-leaning pro-[[israel|Israel]] network — including the [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] government, Adelson, the [[republican-jewish-coalition|Republican Jewish Coalition]] and [[bernie-marcus|Bernie Marcus]] — Trump's second term probably would not have materialized.[2] He put pro-Israel donor contributions at over $200 million, including more than $100 million from Adelson alone, and credited that money with amplifying Trump's ground game in key states and solidifying his appeal among Jewish and evangelical voters, noting that without it the race could have been tighter in swing states such as Pennsylvania and Nevada, where her influence via her Las Vegas casino empire was pronounced.[2]

DeLarme emphasized that the same machine could have been deployed against Trump had he taken a less accommodating line on Israel, making the backing "less a gift freely given and more a price of admission to remain viable on the Republican side in 2024."[2] In his conclusion he warned that if Trump kept drifting toward what he called the Graham–Adelson axis while treating every critic as a traitor, the [[maga-coalition|MAGA coalition]] would shatter — a central theme of his essay [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]].[2]
