# DeepMind

DeepMind is a United Kingdom-based artificial-intelligence research laboratory that was acquired by [[google|Google]].[1] In the Badlands Media article "Dark Equation," the acquisition is presented as the trigger for the falling-out between [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and Google co-founder [[larry-page|Larry Page]]: at the time of the split Google had just bought DeepMind, and Musk characterized Google's approach to AI as having "great potential for good, but there's also potential for bad."[1]

DeepMind chief executive [[demis-hassabis|Demis Hassabis]] attended the 2023 [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] meeting in Lisbon, where artificial intelligence was the conference's primary topic, alongside [[openai|OpenAI]]'s [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]], [[microsoft|Microsoft]]'s [[satya-nadella|Satya Nadella]] and Bilderberg chair [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]].[1] The article treats DeepMind as one node in a small cluster of firms whose leaders simultaneously develop advanced AI and participate in the closed-door forums that shape policy toward it.[1]
