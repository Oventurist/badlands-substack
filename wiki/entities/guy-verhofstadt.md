# Guy Verhofstadt

Guy Verhofstadt is a Belgian politician, a former prime minister of Belgium and a sitting member of the [[european-parliament|European Parliament]].[1] He is among the most prominent federalist voices in [[european-union|European Union]] politics and is frequently cited in Anglophone media as a spokesman for the EU institutional establishment.[1]

## Role in the Tucker Carlson controversy

In February 2024, Verhofstadt emerged as a central figure in the European reaction to [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s trip to Moscow to interview Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]. Speaking to Newsweek, Verhofstadt argued that Carlson's work in Russia could place the former [[fox-news|Fox News]] host "in hot water" with the EU.[1] He called for the EU to explore imposing a "travel ban" on Carlson and described the broadcaster as "a mouthpiece" of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and of Putin.[1]

Verhofstadt framed the proposed measures in terms of the EU's existing sanctions architecture, stating: "As Putin is a war criminal and the EU sanctions all who assist him in that effort, it seems logical that the External Action Service examine his case as well."[1] The remarks were reported by [[newsweek|Newsweek]] alongside comments from current and former members of the European Parliament who likewise suggested Carlson could be targeted by EU lawmakers.[1]
