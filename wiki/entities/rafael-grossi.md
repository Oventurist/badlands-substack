# Rafael Grossi

Rafael Grossi is the Director General of the [[international-atomic-energy-agency|International Atomic Energy Agency]] (IAEA), the United Nations-affiliated body responsible for nuclear safeguards and the promotion of peaceful nuclear energy.

## Statement on the Barakah drone strike

According to the May 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-5a2|Badlands Brief]], Grossi said military activity threatening nuclear safety was "unacceptable" after discussions with UAE officials about the drone strike that caused a fire near the [[barakah-nuclear-power-plant|Barakah nuclear power plant]] in the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]] on May 17, 2026. The statement came as Emirati authorities reported that three drones had entered the country from the western border direction, with two intercepted and one striking an electrical generator outside the plant's inner perimeter; the UAE's nuclear regulator said there was no impact on reactor operations or public safety.
