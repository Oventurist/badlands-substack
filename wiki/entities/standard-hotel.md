# Standard Hotel

The **Standard Hotel** was a Los Angeles hotel that closed in 2022 and that features in citizen-researcher claims about elite [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]] networks.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] noted that while most people know of [[comet-ping-pong|Comet Ping Pong]], far fewer are aware of the Standard Hotel.[1]

## Allegations

Some citizen researchers have connected child trafficking to the area around the hotel and to the congressman referred to in Q-adjacent circles as "pencil neck" and "watermelon head," epithets applied to [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]].[1] It has been speculated that two hotel employees were preparing to disclose information about trafficking in the area and that both died together in a helicopter crash, an outcome Carlson compared to the death of a Boeing whistleblower shortly after testifying.[1]

Carlson further pointed out that the Standard Hotel also housed a ping pong bar, echoing the motif found at Comet Ping Pong.[1]
