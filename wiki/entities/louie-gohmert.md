# Louie Gohmert

Louie Gohmert is a Republican congressman from Texas who was among the earliest and most vocal critics of the special counsel investigation into alleged ties between Russia and the 2016 [[donald-trump|Trump]] campaign. He appears in the Badlands Media article "Setting the Stage" as a contemporaneous witness whose warnings about [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]] the author, Joe Lange, considers vindicated.[1]

## Criticism of the Mueller appointment

Speaking on Fox News, Gohmert said of Mueller: "I have said since day one, since he was appointed, he's bad news… He's out for a scalp. He would love to get Trump's scalp."[1] He added that Mueller "would love nothing more than to become a hero of the left," and questioned the special counsel's integrity and fairness, running through a list of reasons he doubted the legitimacy of the Russia probe.[1] Gohmert also said: "My impression is: Since the day Mueller went in, he has not been the upstanding, fair guy that people have painted him as being… If he cannot get the president, he will… take a Mike Flynn."[1]

## Assessment in the Badlands account

Lange judges Gohmert to have been correct: Mueller, he writes, instantly became a "hero of the left" as he attempted to take Trump's scalp, and the enemy had placed their own man in charge of the investigation into the Trump campaign while simultaneously pressuring Trump to appoint an approved replacement for [[james-comey|James Comey]] at the FBI.[1] Gohmert's prediction that Mueller would "take a Mike Flynn" if he could not get the president is treated as an early public read on the strategy that produced the case against [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]].[1]
