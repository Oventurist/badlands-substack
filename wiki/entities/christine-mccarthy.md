# Christine McCarthy

Christine McCarthy served as chief financial officer of [[walt-disney-company|The Walt Disney Company]] during the period in which the company removed large amounts of produced content from its streaming platforms and took a corresponding impairment charge.[1]

## Streaming content write-down

On Disney's earnings call in May 2023, McCarthy said the company expected to record a write-down of $1.5 billion to $1.8 billion in the June quarter as a result of removing content from its streaming platforms.[1] Disney subsequently disclosed in an SEC filing that it had removed "certain produced content" from its direct-to-consumer services on May 26, 2023 and would take a $1.5 billion impairment charge in its fiscal third quarter to adjust the carrying value of those assets to fair value, with up to roughly $400 million in additional charges anticipated.[1] By writing down the value of the content assets, Disney could remove them from its balance sheet and reduce its tax bill.[1]

Badlands commentary framed the purge as the removal of "the wokest of the woke" titles to generate tax write-offs offsetting streaming and theatrical losses, and grouped Disney with [[bud-light|Bud Light]] and [[target|Target]] as companies facing consumer backlash.[1]
