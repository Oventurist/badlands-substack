# Western Goals Foundation

The Western Goals Foundation was an American Cold War-era organisation cited in Badlands Media commentary as an instrument of long-running psychological influence operations directed at the American public.[1]

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry grouped the foundation with CIA founding member [[john-singlaub|John Singlaub]] as responsible for decades of "intense psychological brainwashing operations" in the second half of the 20th century, and credited it with elevating the televangelist [[pat-robertson|Pat Robertson]].[1] The reference formed part of an argument that American Christian support for the Israeli government is the downstream product of a deliberate conditioning campaign rather than of scripture.[1]
