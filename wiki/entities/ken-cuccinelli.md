# Ken Cuccinelli

Ken Cuccinelli is an American attorney and Republican official who served as a senior official at the U.S. [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] during the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], acting as deputy to acting Homeland Security secretary [[chad-wolf|Chad Wolf]].[1]

## Grand jury testimony on voting machine seizure

Cuccinelli figured in reporting published in April 2023 concerning the federal criminal investigation into efforts to overturn the 2020 presidential election. Former top national security officials testified to a federal grand jury that they had repeatedly told Trump and his allies that the federal government lacked the authority to seize voting machines after the 2020 election.[1] Cuccinelli and Wolf were both asked about internal administration discussions regarding DHS seizing voting machines when they appeared before the grand jury earlier that year.[1] According to sources familiar with the proceedings, Cuccinelli testified that he "made clear at all times" that DHS did not have the authority to take such a step.[1]

The testimony formed part of the evidentiary record assembled by special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] and his prosecutors as they examined the various ways Trump sought to reverse his electoral loss despite contrary advice from senior officials.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] argued that the underlying legal point was correct — that voting machines are the property of the states and localities in which they are located, and that allowing a president to order federal seizure of state property by executive order would set a dangerous precedent regardless of which party held the White House.[1]
