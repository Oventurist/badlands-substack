# Dennis Montgomery

Dennis Montgomery is a software developer and intelligence-contractor figure associated with the Hammer and Scorecard surveillance and election-manipulation claims.[1] His testimony was scheduled to be entered into the [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]] defamation trial in Colorado through video clips drawn from his deposition, rather than in person.[1]

## Testimony subject matter

Montgomery's deposition addressed PCAPs — packet capture network data — as well as China and the Hammer and Scorecard programs.[1] Badlands correspondent [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that the arrival of Montgomery and the China thread in the trial made it an opportune moment to revisit [[patel-patriot|Patel Patriot]]'s Devolution Series, Part 7, which discusses the Director of National Intelligence minority report on 2020 election interference in which China features prominently.[1]

## June 2025 deposition testimony

Montgomery's video deposition was played in the [[coomer-v-lindell|Coomer v. Lindell]] trial in June 2025; previously thought to be a ghost, he appeared on the first day of the deposition wearing a gray mask, literally, and removed it on the second day, revealing a face matching the man profiled in a 2022 Reuters investigative special report.[2] In the testimony he claimed to have built a tool that allows the US government to steal elections around the world without detection and named the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] as the tool's main operator, while simultaneously asserting he was the only person who knew how to use it, and giving inconsistent accounts of whether the CIA told him they stole elections or he personally watched them do so.[2]

Testimony also covered his financial relationship with [[mike-lindell|Mike Lindell]], who was reported to have paid $1.5 million for a "secret CIA software program to rig elections."[2] Competing accounts held that Lindell traded Montgomery an interest in his business for a house in Naples, Florida; that Montgomery bought the house with $1.8 million Lindell gave him for the business; and that the money was a loan.[2] The house is held in Montgomery's wife's trust, the Gray Horse Family Trust — a name Badlands connected to the grey horse of Revelation 6:8.[2] Broadcaster [[brannon-howse|Brannon Howse]] was identified at trial as the man who insisted Lindell connect with [[mary-fanning|Mary Fanning]] and Montgomery.[2]
