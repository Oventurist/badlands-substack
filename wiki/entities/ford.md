# Ford Motor Company

Ford Motor Company is one of the three legacy Detroit automakers and a major participant in the integrated North American vehicle manufacturing network built up across Canada, Mexico, and the United States over the preceding three decades.[1]

## 2025 auto tariffs

On 26 March 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced 25% tariffs on all vehicles and foreign-made auto parts imported into the United States. Analysts warned that, if maintained for an extended period, the levies could add thousands of dollars to the cost of an average US vehicle purchase and impede car production across North America, in part because of the deeply intertwined cross-border manufacturing operations of the industry. Research firm GlobalData reported that nearly half of all cars sold in the US the previous year were imported.[1]

Markets reacted sharply. Shares in Ford fell about 4.5% alongside US-traded shares of Chrysler parent Stellantis, while [[general-motors|General Motors]] slumped 8% in after-market trading. In Asia, shares in Toyota Motor, Honda Motor, and Hyundai Motor all fell between 3% and 4%, while [[tesla|Tesla]] — which builds all the cars it sells in the United States locally, though with some imported parts — was down 1.3%.[1]

Trump said the duties could be net neutral or even good for Tesla, adding that its chief executive and his close ally [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had not advised him on auto tariffs; Musk responded on X that the tariffs would also affect Tesla. US autoworkers, including the United Auto Workers union, praised the tariff plan.[1]

Badlands contributor Burning Bright framed the criticism as accurate but beside the point, writing that when opponents said the tariffs were bad for global democracy or for a given industry they were telling the truth — and that Trump and his supporters knew it and wanted the policy anyway.[1]
