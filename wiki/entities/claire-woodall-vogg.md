# Claire Woodall-Vogg

Claire Woodall-Vogg is the former director of the [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]], and a central figure in disputes over the administration of the 2020 presidential election in Wisconsin.[1]

## The 2020 flash drive incident

Reporting summarized in the Badlands Brief in May 2026 described a 2020 incident involving Woodall-Vogg and a flash drive containing absentee ballot tabulation data from Milwaukee's [[milwaukee-central-count-facility|Central Count facility]].[1] Woodall-Vogg had previously stated that the drive was left at Central Count before being retrieved and transported by police.[1] That account became one of the threads in a reported [[fbi|FBI]] inquiry into election-night ballot processing in Milwaukee.[1]

## Role in 2020 election fraud claims

In Badlands commentary, the writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described Woodall-Vogg as "an important character in the 2020 story," identifying her as the official who delivered "just the margin needed at 3am" — a reference to the late-night reporting of Milwaukee absentee results that shifted Wisconsin's statewide tally.[1] The same commentary referred to an alleged 4:30 a.m. infusion of roughly 138,000 votes.[1] Ashe argued that Woodall-Vogg and Ryan Chew should be interrogated, linking the Wisconsin events to the work of [[the-elections-group|The Elections Group]] and to [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]]'s role in Georgia's 2020 recount, and concluding that the same "sources and methods and actors" recurred across states.[1] These claims form part of the broader body of [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]].[1]
