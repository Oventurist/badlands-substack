# Blaise Metreweli

Blaise Metreweli is the head of the British Secret Intelligence Service, commonly known as [[mi6|MI6]], having recently assumed the post as Britain's new intelligence chief.[1] She delivered her first public speech in the role on December 15, 2025, speaking from MI6 headquarters in London and setting out an assessment of the global threat environment.[1]

## Maiden public speech

In the speech, previewed by the British government ahead of delivery, Metreweli argued that the United Kingdom is operating in an era in which "the front line is everywhere."[1] She characterised [[russia|Russia]] as an "aggressive, expansionist, and revisionist" power determined to export instability across Europe and beyond, and said that Russia's campaign against [[ukraine|Ukraine]] together with its wider hybrid operations pose an acute and enduring danger to Britain and its allies.[1]

Metreweli said that "the export of chaos is a feature, not a bug in the Russian approach to international engagement, and we should be ready for this to continue until Putin is forced to change his calculus," a direct reference to Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1] She further argued that as Russia and other hostile actors rewrite the rules of conflict through cyber operations, information warfare and covert sabotage, the global threat environment is becoming increasingly complex and interconnected.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated Metreweli's remarks as an "unwitting admission" from what he described as the crumbling battlements of the Western globalist hegemon, arguing that Russia has indeed become aggressive, expansionist and revisionist — but framing each of those traits as legitimate: aggressive in defence of its borders, expansionist in reclaiming cultural and historical spheres, and revisionist in challenging what he called the false chronicles imposed on the West for generations.[1] In that reading, the new spy chief's alarm was "panic disguised as warning," reflecting a globalist order that trembles as Russia reasserts a sovereign identity rooted in faith, family and nation.[1] The Badlands framing situated this shift within what it described as a renewed American awakening catalysed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s opposition to endless wars, [[deep-state|Deep State]] machinations and the Ukrainian grift.[1]

Metreweli's speech was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and referenced in the Badlands News Brief under the headline motif of "The Ice Maiden."[1]
