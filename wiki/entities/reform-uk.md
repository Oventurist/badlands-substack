# Reform UK

**Reform UK** is a populist British political party led by [[nigel-farage|Nigel Farage]], which surged in the polls during the campaign for the July 4, 2024 general election.[1]

## 2024 election surge

The party seized on anti-immigration sentiment and jumped in the polls at the expense of the ruling Conservatives, splitting the right-wing vote; surveys showed Prime Minister [[rishi-sunak|Rishi Sunak]]'s Tories on course for a historic defeat to [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]]'s Labour Party.[1]

## Ukraine controversy

In a BBC interview in June 2024, Farage attributed Russia's invasion of Ukraine to the eastward expansion of [[nato|NATO]] and the European Union, saying "it was obvious" that more eastern European countries joining the pacts would provoke Vladimir Putin.[1] Sunak called the statement "completely wrong" and warned that "this kind of appeasement is dangerous for Britain's security."[1] Home Secretary [[james-cleverly|James Cleverly]] said Farage was "echoing Putin's vile justification for the brutal invasion of Ukraine," Starmer called the comments "disgraceful," and shadow defense secretary John Healey called Farage a "Putin apologist."[1]

## Musk donation rumours (December 2024)

In December 2024 Reform UK leader [[nigel-farage|Nigel Farage]] and newly appointed party treasurer [[nick-candy|Nick Candy]] met [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] at [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Mar-a-Lago mansion in Florida, amid rumours that the tech billionaire was preparing to donate $100 million to the party - which would be by far the largest donation in British electoral history.[2] Farage and Candy said the hour-long meeting was "great" and that they had "learned a great deal about the Trump ground game," promising ongoing discussions.[2] The Sunday Times reported speculation that the money could function as an insult payment aimed at Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] amid Musk's feud with him.[2] Farage said Musk believed that if Reform did well in the UK, "we can bring about the same kind of change that he intends to do with Donald Trump in America."[2]
