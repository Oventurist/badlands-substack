# Bernie Sanders

Bernie Sanders is a United States Senator from Vermont, a self-described democratic socialist, and the figurehead of the progressive wing of the Democratic coalition.

In the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]], Sanders appears as a patron of insurgent primary challengers in the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm]] cycle. Candidates backed by Sanders and [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] have largely refused to commit to supporting House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]]. Among the March 3 primaries cited, Sanders backed [[nida-allam|Nida Allam]] in her challenge to Representative [[valerie-foushee|Valerie Foushee]] in North Carolina. The Brief associates this insurgent current with the wider movement represented by the [[democratic-socialists-of-america|Democratic Socialists of America]].
