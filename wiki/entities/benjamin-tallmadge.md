# Benjamin Tallmadge

**Benjamin Tallmadge** was a Continental Army major who organized the [[culper-spy-ring|Culper Spy Ring]] in 1778 to gather information on British troop movements, fortifications, and military plans in the New York area.[1]

Under his direction, informants used fake names and a numerical code book of seven hundred and sixty-three numbers representing words, names, and places, a system that proved essential in protecting the ring's communications and the identities of its members.[1] The network was a central element of [[george-washington|George Washington]]'s reliance on secret intelligence during the Revolution, cited in the Badlands Brief article "Kekistani Code Talkers" as a case study in [[information-warfare|information warfare]].[1]
