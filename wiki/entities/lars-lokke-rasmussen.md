# Lars Løkke Rasmussen

Lars Løkke Rasmussen is the Danish Foreign Minister who, in late August 2025, formally protested to the United States over an alleged American influence operation aimed at pushing [[greenland|Greenland]] toward secession from the Kingdom of [[denmark|Denmark]].[1]

## Summons of the US Chargé d'Affaires

Danish media reported that at least three Americans with ties to US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had allegedly been working to weaken relations between Greenland and Denmark and to recruit participants for a secession movement.[1] Rasmussen said on Wednesday, August 27, 2025, that such attempts were unacceptable, and he ordered US Chargé d'Affaires Mark Stroh to appear at the Danish foreign ministry for talks.[1]

The [[state-department|State Department]] confirmed that Stroh met Rasmussen and characterized the exchange as a "productive conversation" that "reaffirmed the strong ties" between Greenland, Denmark and the United States.[1] The [[white-house|White House]], however, declined to confirm whether any influence campaign had been underway, with an official telling CBS News that "we think the Danes need to calm down."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the Badlands News Brief, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the Danish protest as the reaction of a declining power, noting that Trump had not ruled out using military force to acquire Greenland and that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] had shifted Greenland's jurisdiction from [[united-states-european-command|European Command]] to [[united-states-northern-command|Northern Command]].[1] The commentary framed Denmark's summons of the American envoy as an expression of weakness rather than leverage.[1]
