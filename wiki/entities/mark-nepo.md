# Mark Nepo

**Mark Nepo** is a poet quoted in the closing passage of the April 19, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Remembering: Fractured Selves and the Path Beyond Ego" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites his line, "To be broken is no reason to see all things as broken."[1] It follows the article's stained-glass image, in which each pane — cracked, vibrant, or muted — is illuminated by a light that "does not judge," and precedes the author's conclusion that the reader is "not broken" but "beauty, assembled."[1] The quotation caps the essay's argument for [[psychological-integration|integration]] as wholeness achieved through the fractures rather than in spite of them.[1]
