# Clinton Global Initiative

The Clinton Global Initiative (CGI) was, in the article's description, the brainchild of [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]]'s right-hand man [[doug-band|Doug Band]], and the vehicle through which almost all of the money flowing in Clinton world passed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Band was working for CGI and the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] in 2009 when he accompanied Clinton to [[north-korea|North Korea]] for the hostage negotiations with [[kim-jong-il|Kim Jong Il]] — a detail the article calls a key data point supporting its claim that the trip concerned a business deal rather than the hostages.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

CGI's most striking donor in the article is Nigerian businessman [[gilbert-chagoury|Gilbert Chagoury]], a close associate of [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]] who was convicted in Geneva in 2000 of money laundering and aiding a criminal organization in connection with billions stolen from Nigeria under Gen. Sani Abacha.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) In 2009 Chagoury pledged $1 billion to CGI, and that same year CGI gave his company an award for sustainable development.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) In 2013 Bill Clinton appeared in Nigeria for a public ceremony involving one of Chagoury's projects, and Chagoury attended Doug Band's wedding.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article draws its title's moral from this relationship: it was not just America for sale — the Clinton Global Initiative put the whole world up for sale, since Chagoury could pledge a billion dollars only because he had stolen billions from Nigeria.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Creation by Doug Band and the FIFA membership

Part 2 of the series states plainly that Band created CGI, citing both Wikipedia and The Atlantic, and describes it as the instrument that took the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] money operation worldwide.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Band went from being a [[white-house|White House]] intern for [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] to helping launch the foundation and then inventing CGI.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Among CGI's participants was [[fifa|FIFA]], which reportedly gave the Clinton Foundation between $50,000 and $100,000 as a membership fee to take part in the initiative, together with a commitment to build 20 community centers in South Africa as part of its decision to hold the 2010 World Cup there.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The Qatar 2022 Supreme Committee gave between $250,000 and $500,000 as a CGI sponsor in 2013, while the government of [[qatar|Qatar]] has given between $1 million and $5 million to the foundation overall.2(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## 2022 meeting and Sam Bankman-Fried

Bill and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] invited crypto mogul [[sam-bankman-fried|Sam Bankman-Fried]] to speak at the 2022 Clinton Global Initiative in September, roughly five months after Bankman-Fried allegedly paid [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] some $250,000 to address his Crypto Bahamas Conference.[3] Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued the invitation suggested the Bahamas event had served as a vetting exercise, and placed the [[ftx|FTX]] operation within what he described as the Clinton network's orbit.[3]
