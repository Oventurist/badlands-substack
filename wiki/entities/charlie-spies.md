# Charlie Spies

Charlie Spies is a Republican election lawyer who briefly served as chief counsel of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] in 2024. His departure after roughly two months in the post was reported by NBC News in early May 2024 and covered by Badlands Media as a signal of continuing turbulence inside the party apparatus.[1]

## RNC tenure and departure

Spies was hired in March 2024 to lead the committee's legal efforts during a broad leadership overhaul, which followed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s endorsement of [[michael-whatley|Michael Whatley]] to helm the organization — a change that effectively reshaped the RNC in Trump's image.[1] Within two months he was parting ways with the committee; a source familiar with the move told NBC News that Spies had been "pushed out."[1]

The RNC's public account differed. Spokesperson Danielle Alvarez said in a statement that "Charlie approached RNC Chief of Staff, Chris LaCivita, about potential time commitment conflicts and it was agreed that, while we appreciate and value Charlie's expertise and professionalism, he cannot do this role full time and still maintain the obligations to his law firm that he has spent years successfully building."[1] Spies did not immediately respond to a request for comment.[1]

## Reputation

A campaign official quoted in the reporting described Spies as "the best at what he does, knows the FEC loopholes better than anyone," while also calling him a "gun for hire."[1] Reporting noted that Spies was not a supporter of the former president, a fact widely read as central to the brevity of his tenure.[1]
