# Dodi Al Fayed

Dodi Al Fayed was the companion of [[diana-princess-of-wales|Diana, Princess of Wales]] at the time of her death, and appears in Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] Chapter 14 within the discussion of Q drop #100.[1]

## Claim in the Q for Dummies series

The article asserts that Diana's impending marriage to Al Fayed "was a front" — that he was in reality attempting to help her get away from the royal household, and that the engagement supplied the public excuse for doing so.[1] No documentary support for this reading is offered in the source; it is presented as the author's interpretation of the drop.[1]
