# Stephen Paddock

Stephen Paddock is the man officially identified as the sole gunman in the October 2017 [[las-vegas-shooting|Route 91 massacre]] in Las Vegas, the deadliest mass shooting in American history.[1]

## Disputed official account

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treats the official "lone shooter" account as untenable. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the Stephen Paddock lone shooter story is "a cover up of epic proportions," citing 911 recordings from that night that describe shootings at multiple locations along the Las Vegas strip, a helicopter hijacking, and other evidence she says was suppressed.[1] She further noted that many people investigating the incident subsequently turned up dead or went silent in the days after the tragedy.[1]

## Continued relevance

The dispute over the Paddock narrative resurfaced in August 2023 when it was reported that [[john-pelletier|John Pelletier]], the incident commander during the Las Vegas response, was serving as Maui police chief during the [[2023-maui-wildfires|Maui wildfires]], prompting commentators to link the two events as instances of official information control.[1]
