# Count Mirabeau

Count Honoré Gabriel Riqueti Mirabeau (1749–1791) was a figure of [[french-revolution|French Revolution]] fame who, according to [[john-robison|John Robison]], was a member of the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]].[1] He is not to be confused with his father Victor (1715–1789).[1]

Within the Order, Mirabeau carried the code name Cornelius Scipio, part of a system of aliases drawn largely from classical Greek and Roman personalities known for ruthlessness and cynicism — the same system under which [[adam-weishaupt|Weishaupt]] was Spartacus, [[baron-von-knigge|Knigge]] was Philo, and Nicholai was Lucian.[1] Robison's final chapter devotes attention to Mirabeau's intrigues in the political occurrences of the Revolution.[1]
