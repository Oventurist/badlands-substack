# Theo Von

**Theo Von** is an American comedian and host of the podcast "This Past Weekend" who in autumn 2025 was featured, without his approval, in a [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] video promoting deportation efforts.[1]

## DHS deportation video controversy

The department posted the video to its various social media platforms on September 23, 2025.[1] It showed [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] officials detaining immigrants alongside a clip of Von saying, "I heard you got deported, dude. Bye."[1]

Von objected in a since-deleted post on X the same day, writing: "Yooo DHS i didnt approve to be used in this. I know you know my address so send a check. And please take this down and please keep me out of your 'banger' deportation videos."[1]

## Offer of security

Recounting the online harassment he received over the video during a Thursday episode of "This Past Weekend," Von said he was growing "paranoid" and had even closed the curtains in his house.[1] He said that a "high government official" subsequently offered him protection: "I woke up the next morning to a text from a high government official saying, 'Hey, if you need some extra security in your neighborhood, or some extra police cars on patrol, let me know,'" Von recounted, adding, "And I'm like, what? What are you talking about?"[1]
