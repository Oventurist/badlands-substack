# Carlos Lehder

Carlos Lehder is a Colombian drug trafficker and co-founder of the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín Cartel]], described as the partner of Pablo Escobar.[1]

## Arrest in Colombia

Lehder was cited as among the high-profile cartel figures arrested by the government of Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], who over the course of the year preceding November 2025 detained thousands of cartel criminals.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the Lehder arrest, together with Petro's September 2025 dispatch of diplomats to Qatar to negotiate the surrender and disbandment of the [[gulf-clan|Gulf Clan]], as evidence that Petro's public denunciations of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the Caribbean strikes were theater masking cooperation against the cartels.[1]
