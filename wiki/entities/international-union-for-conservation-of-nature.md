# International Union for Conservation of Nature

The International Union for the Conservation of Nature (IUCN) is described by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as one of the world's first environmental organizations, founded by [[julian-huxley|Sir Julian Huxley]].[1]

Ehret groups the IUCN with its off-shoot the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] and with [[unesco|UNESCO]] — which Huxley founded in 1946 — as the institutional machinery built by a man who also served as President of the [[british-eugenics-society|British Eugenics Society]].[1] In the UNESCO manifesto Huxley explicitly called for reviving [[eugenics|eugenics]] as the most important of all sciences while inducing humanity to accept world government, a program Ehret treats as the secular twin of [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]]'s religious project.[1]
