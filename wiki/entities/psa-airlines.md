# PSA Airlines

PSA Airlines is a regional carrier that operates flights on behalf of [[american-airlines|American Airlines]] under the American Eagle regional brand, flying Bombardier CRJ-series regional jets on short- and medium-haul routes in the eastern United States.[1]

## 2025 Potomac midair collision

PSA came to national attention on January 29, 2025, when one of its Bombardier CRJ700 regional jets, operating as American Airlines Flight 5342 with 60 passengers and four crew aboard, collided in midair with a U.S. Army Sikorsky UH-60 helicopter while on approach to Runway 33 at [[ronald-reagan-washington-national-airport|Reagan Washington National Airport]] at roughly 9 p.m. local time.[1] The [[federal-aviation-administration|FAA]] and the [[national-transportation-safety-board|NTSB]] opened investigations into the accident, which is described in detail at [[2025-potomac-midair-collision]].[1]
