# Loretta Preska

Loretta Preska is a United States District Court judge for the Southern District of New York who has presided over the litigation governing the unsealing of records connected to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Role in the Epstein document unsealing

On Monday, January 8, 2024, Preska issued an order directing that newly released images depicting Epstein, his associate [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], and young girls and women on Epstein's Caribbean property be redacted, after the court had already disclosed them in an unsealed filing.[1] Her order instructed the Clerk of the Court to strike docket entry 1332-7 in the case "because it contains inadvertently disclosed images that should have been redacted," and required counsel to re-file the stricken document "as soon as is practicable."[1] The photographs, dated to 2006 and taken at Epstein's Little St. James Island, had been provided by Epstein accuser [[sarah-ransome|Sarah Ransome]] and were reported alongside the filings by the New York Post.[1]

## Commentary

Commentators associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the accidental release and immediate resealing as evidence that the Epstein disclosures were a managed or "controlled" process rather than genuine transparency, asking why a court would seal material it had already made public when public trust in the Epstein matter was at stake.[1]
