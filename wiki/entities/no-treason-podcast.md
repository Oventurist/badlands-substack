# The No Treason Podcast

**The No Treason Podcast** is a programme on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] broadcast at 8pm EST on Sundays.[1] Its launch was announced in a Badlands Media essay published 5 October 2025, "On Nations and Natural Law," which the author offered as supplemental material to help listeners make sense of the terms and ideas to be discussed on the show.[1]

## Focus

The programme initially focuses on [[lysander-spooner|Lysander Spooner]] and his prolific writings on matters pertaining to [[natural-law|Natural Law]].[1] Its title alludes to Spooner's body of work of the same name. The host's stated goal, framed against the backdrop of the [[information-war|information war]], is to return Natural Law to the forefront of the language of political debate, on the argument that modern political discourse is "wholly devoid" of such concepts and instead discusses rights without any attempt at philosophical context.[1]
