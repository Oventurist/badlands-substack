# Max Burns

**Max Burns** is a Republican member of the [[georgia-senate|Georgia State Senate]].[1]

## State Election Board oversight bill

Burns sponsored 2024 legislation to remove Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]] from his role on the [[georgia-state-election-board|State Election Board]] and to permit that board to investigate him. The measure cleared the Georgia Senate Ethics Committee on January 23, 2024, despite objections from Raffensperger's counsel [[charlene-mcgowan|Charlene McGowan]], who called it a dangerous grant of unchecked power to unelected bureaucrats.[1]

Defending the bill at a hearing, Burns said: "We're trying to provide reasonable oversight of a process that affects every citizen of Georgia." Supporters denied the proposal was unconstitutional.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary framed the bill as a test of what checks exist against a secretary of state, urging Georgia voters to weigh the gains against the constitutional impact and arguing that election reform should prioritize decentralization of both oversight and administration down to the counties.[1]
