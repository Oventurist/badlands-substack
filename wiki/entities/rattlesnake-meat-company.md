# Rattlesnake Meat Company

Rattlesnake Meat Company is a family-owned American beef producer that has advertised as a sponsor of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] publications, marketing itself as "family-owned, American-raised, and 100% independent."[1]

## Marketing and positioning

The company's sponsored copy in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] positions its product against imported beef and the "Big Four" meatpackers, drawing an analogy to the 1773 Boston Tea Party: "In 1773, patriots dumped tea to fight tyranny. Today, it would be imported beef from the Big Four. Same control, different packaging."[1] The advertisement promoted a discount code, PATRIOT20, offering $20 off for orders placed by June 30, 2025 in time for the Fourth of July.[1]

## Pasture-raised chicken line (September 2025)

By September 2025 the company was also advertising a pasture-raised chicken line in Badlands News Brief sponsor slots, contrasting its product with "Big Ag" grocery-store chicken that it said is raised in crowded barns, pumped with water or broth to increase weight, and run through chlorine baths to hide contamination.[2] The advertisement argued that labels such as "cage free" or "natural" do not always mean what consumers think, and stated that Rattlesnake Meat Company chicken is raised on clean ground, fed non-GMO, soy-free feed, and never injected or chemically treated, promoting a $10 discount with the code CLEAN10.[2]


## April 2025 Badlands promotion

In the Badlands News Brief of April 4, 2025, Rattlesnake Meat Company offered two exclusive deals to Badlands readers: promotional code BAD100 for $100 off the "Whole Beef + Freezer" package and BAD50 for $50 off the "Half Beef + Grill" package.[3] The sponsored placement contrasted the company's stated commitment to quality and transparency with "Big Food and Big Government," which it said do not care about either.[3]

## TRUSTED20 promotion (June 2025)

A sponsored placement in the June 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] promoted Rattlesnake Meats with the offer "Trusted20 Gets $20 Off American Beef," directing readers to badlandsmedia.tv/meat1 and the discount code TRUSTED20.[4] The copy argued that four mega-packers control 85% of the beef sold in stores — "not a choice or a free market, that's a cartel" — and that their beef "can come from anywhere and wear whatever flag they want."[4] By contrast, the company's product was described as 100% American-raised and 21-day-aged, with "no imports, no globalist garbage," under the slogan "take back control of your food."[4]

## June 2025 sponsorship

A sponsored placement in the Badlands News Brief of June 20, 2025 promoted Rattlesnake Meat Company with an explicit anti-import framing: "In 1773, patriots dumped tea to fight tyranny. Today, it would be imported beef from the Big Four. Same control, different packaging."[5] The advertisement described the company as family-owned, American-raised and 100% independent — "No suits. No foreign beef. No tyrants." — and urged readers to order by June 30 to receive product in time for the Fourth of July, offering $20 off with the code PATRIOT20.[5]
