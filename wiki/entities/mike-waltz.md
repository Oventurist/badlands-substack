# Mike Waltz

Mike Waltz is a former United States National Security Advisor in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, subsequently removed from that cabinet position. He is treated harshly in the Badlands corpus as an example of officials whose loyalties are alleged to run to foreign governments rather than to American citizens.

## The Danon list controversy

The June 5, 2026 Badlands Brief recounts an episode in which [[danny-danon|Danny Danon]], Israel's Ambassador to the [[united-nations|United Nations]], appeared to suggest compiling a list of Americans who criticize the Israeli government so that they might be targeted with some form of retribution. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who encountered the clip through a repost on [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]]'s X feed, writes that Waltz was seen on stage "nodding along like a complete dumbass" as the suggestion was made.

The commentary draws an explicit historical comparison, asking "A list? Like the lists that the Nazis used to make?" and stating that "the former National Security Advisor is nodding in agreement to the suggestion by a foreign diplomat that a list be compiled of Americans who criticize that diplomat's government." On that basis the author concludes that "it would appear that Mike Waltz is a traitor—like so many others in our government—and is fully prepared to support the targeted persecution of American citizens by a foreign government." He characterizes the targeting of Americans for exercising their [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] right to criticize a foreign government as symbolic of a corrupt and irredeemable political class, calls Waltz "a sniveling coward" for remaining on the stage, and expresses relief that he was fired from the cabinet while urging that "the other rats that remain" be rooted out.
