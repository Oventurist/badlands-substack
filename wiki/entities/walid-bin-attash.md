# Walid bin Attash

Walid bin Attash is one of two alleged accomplices of [[khalid-sheikh-mohammed|Khalid Sheikh Mohammed]] charged in the capital military-commission case at [[guantanamo-bay|Guantánamo Bay]] over the September 11, 2001 attacks.[1]

On July 31, 2024, bin Attash signed a pretrial agreement with retired Brigadier General [[susan-escallier|Susan K. Escallier]] trading a guilty plea for a sentence of at most life in prison.[1] Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd J. Austin III]] withdrew from that agreement on August 2, 2024, stripping Escallier of oversight of the case and reinstating it as a death-penalty prosecution.[1] The [[pentagon|Pentagon]] announced the reversal with the release of Austin's memorandum.[1]
