# Tommy Corcoran

Thomas “Tommy the Cork” Corcoran was a lawyer and member of the [[office-of-strategic-services]] who, with Lieutenant General [[claire-chennault]], created the [[flying-tigers]].[1] That organisation transitioned into [[civil-air-transport]], forming the beginnings of the [[cia]]'s own air fleet and, in the Badlands Media account, part of the logistical backbone of [[operation-gladio]].[1]
