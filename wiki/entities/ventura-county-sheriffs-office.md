# Ventura County Sheriff's Office

The Ventura County Sheriff's Office is the county law enforcement agency serving Ventura County, California. It became the lead investigating agency in the November 2023 death of [[paul-kessler|Paul Kessler]], a 69-year-old Jewish demonstrator injured at duelling pro-Israel and pro-Palestinian rallies in Thousand Oaks.[1]

## Kessler investigation

The office responded to the altercation at the intersection of Westlake Boulevard and Thousand Oaks Boulevard after several citizens called 9-1-1 to report a battery at about 3:20 p.m. on Sunday, November 5, 2023.[1] Deputies arrived to find Kessler suffering from a head injury; witnesses told police he had fallen backwards during the fight and struck his head on the ground.[1] Kessler was taken to an area hospital and died of his injuries the following Monday, as the sheriff's office reported.[1]

The Ventura County Medical Examiner's Office determined that Kessler died from blunt-force head injury and ruled the death a homicide; the sheriff's office did not rule out a hate crime and continued its investigation.[1] Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] criticized the official and media framing of the incident as an accidental fall.[1]
