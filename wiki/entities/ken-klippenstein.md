# Ken Klippenstein

Ken Klippenstein is an American independent journalist and blogger who publishes on Substack. He previously worked as a reporter for The Intercept and was associated with the progressive commentary web show The Young Turks, and is described in conservative media as a figure of the far left.[1]

## Publication of the Vance vetting dossier

In September 2024, Klippenstein published on his Substack an internal Republican campaign vetting dossier concerning [[jd-vance|J.D. Vance]], then the party's vice-presidential nominee and a sitting senator from Ohio.[1] The material was believed to have been obtained through a hacking operation attributed to the Islamic Republic of Iran.[1] The National Pulse, which reported on the episode, declined to link to the document because of the volume of personal information it contained.[1]

The dossier included Vance's Social Security number, personal phone number, physical addresses and private email addresses, and Klippenstein made no effort to redact other private details or contact information — a decision critics said potentially constituted a threat to Vance and his family.[1]

## Contents of the document

The 271-page file was described as a fairly typical product of any campaign's vetting process, raising concerns about past policy positions and public statements and weighing how they might affect the presidential ticket.[1] It noted Vance's early opposition to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 campaign and questioned aspects of his conservative credentials.[1] Reviewers observed that the vetting materials portrayed Vance as considerably less radically right-wing than he had been depicted by national Democrats and the Kamala Harris campaign, presenting him instead as thoughtful and open to a wide array of policies and ideological propositions.[1]
