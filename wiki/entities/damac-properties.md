# DAMAC Properties

DAMAC Properties is a Dubai-based real estate developer, part of the DAMAC Group headed by Emirati billionaire [[hussain-sajwani|Hussain Sajwani]].[1] The firm has a long-running commercial relationship with [[trump-organization|The Trump Organization]], with which it has developed golf courses in the United Arab Emirates and Oman and planned further projects in the region.[1]

## US data center investment

On January 7, 2025, President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] that Sajwani's company would invest at least $20 billion to build new data centers in the United States, with a first phase targeting Texas, Arizona, Oklahoma, Louisiana, Ohio, Illinois, Michigan and Indiana.[1] Trump said the investment could "go double, or even somewhat more than double, that amount of money," and pledged to accelerate the environmental and permitting process for the projects.[1]

The announcement was reported alongside a statement by [[dar-global|Dar Global]] — the international arm of Saudi Arabia's Dar Al Arkan Real Estate Development Company — pledging expansion into the US and Greece following its own deals with the Trump Organization.[1] Badlands Media commentary treated the two announcements together as evidence of deepening Gulf capital flows into US projects under the incoming administration, and warned that such construction plans would be jeopardized by continued war in the Middle East.[1]
