# Cincinnati Children's Hospital

Cincinnati Children's Hospital is a major pediatric medical center in Ohio. In February 2025 it drew national attention over its vaccination requirements for organ-transplant candidates.[1]

## Adaline Deal transplant dispute

According to a report in The Cincinnati Enquirer relayed by One America News Network and republished in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], 12-year-old Adaline Deal — a distant relative of Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] by marriage through his half-siblings — was denied a spot on a heart transplant list because she had not been vaccinated against COVID-19 and influenza.[1]

Adaline was born with two rare heart conditions that her family knew would eventually require a transplant, her mother Janeen Deal told the outlet, and she had been treated at Cincinnati Children's for nearly ten years, where her parents hoped she would eventually receive her transplant.[1] The parents said the hospital now requires patients to be vaccinated against COVID-19 and the flu in order to receive a transplant, and declined to make an exception even after they explained that their religious beliefs as non-denominational Christians left them uncomfortable proceeding.[1]

Badlands carried the story as its "bonus item," presenting it as an illustration of institutional rigidity around vaccine mandates persisting into the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]
