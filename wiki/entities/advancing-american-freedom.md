# Advancing American Freedom

Advancing American Freedom is the political advocacy group founded by former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]].[1] The organization entered national news coverage in January 2023 when Pence's team, responding to the discovery of classified material at his residence, conducted searches of both his Carmel, Indiana home and the offices of Advancing American Freedom.[1] Those searches followed revelations that classified documents from President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s tenure as vice president had been found at the Penn Biden Center think tank and at Biden's Wilmington, Delaware property.[1]
