# Electronic Frontier Foundation

The **Electronic Frontier Foundation** (EFF) is a digital civil liberties organization that campaigns on encryption, surveillance and online speech issues.[1]

## Opposition to the UK Online Safety Bill

EFF opposed the United Kingdom's [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]] for years before its passage by the [[uk-parliament|UK Parliament]] in September 2023, arguing that the law would produce a more censored, locked-down internet for British users and could undermine the privacy and security of internet users worldwide, not just U.K. residents.[1]

The organization focused particular criticism on the clause allowing [[ofcom|Ofcom]], the British telecom regulator, to require technology companies to scan all of their users for child abuse content even in end-to-end encrypted messages and files — a mandate EFF described as forcing companies to build a backdoor.[1] EFF has characterized such client-side scanning systems as "bugs in our pockets," noting that a group of leading computer security experts independently reached the same conclusion that the technique breaks end-to-end encryption and undermines security for everyone.[1]

EFF's broader argument is that having a private conversation is a basic human right, one that matters most for the most vulnerable people; if the U.K. exercises its new scanning powers, lawmakers will damage the security individuals rely on to protect themselves from harassers, data thieves and authoritarian governments — risks created, paradoxically, in the name of online safety.[1]
