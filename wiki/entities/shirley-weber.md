# Shirley Weber

Shirley Weber is the Secretary of State of California and a Democrat, serving as the state's top election official during the 2024 presidential primary cycle.[1]

## 2024 ballot access decision

Weber kept former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on California's 2024 presidential primary ballot despite contrary decisions in Colorado and Maine that had sought to remove him.[1] On 28 December 2023 she certified the list of candidates ahead of the 5 March 2024 Presidential Primary Election ballot.[1]

Her decision followed pressure from California's lieutenant governor, [[eleni-kounalakis|Eleni Kounalakis]], who in a letter of 20 December 2023 — one day after the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]]'s disqualification ruling — urged Weber to explore any and all legal options to keep Trump off the ballot.[1] Kounalakis accused Trump of insurrection in connection with the events of 6 January 2021 and argued that this disqualified him under Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1] Weber declined to act on that request.[1]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] praised the outcome, quoting Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]]'s position that California defeats disfavoured candidates at the polls rather than through administrative removal, and argued that bureaucrats should never be able to unilaterally neutralise political opposition.[1]
