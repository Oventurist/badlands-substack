# Michael Flynn Jr.

**Michael Flynn Jr.**, referred to in Badlands Media commentary simply as "Flynn Jr," is a political commentator and the son of [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]].[1]

## Commentary on the MAGA schism

In his November 2025 essay [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited a Flynn Jr. post as capturing the mood of the fracturing [[america-first-movement|America First]] coalition, writing that Flynn Jr. "expressed what a lot of people are feeling."[1] DeLarme characterized him as having proven himself "much more immune to partisan orthodoxy and blind allegiance than a vast majority of the 'new media,'" placing him among the commentators willing to criticize the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration rather than defend it reflexively.[1] The citation appeared alongside [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]]'s quip, "Can whoever removed a chromosome from MAGA this week put it back already?", in a passage describing how the coalition's fragmentation had gone "into overdrive" in the weeks before publication.[1]
