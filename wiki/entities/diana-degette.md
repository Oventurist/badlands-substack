# Diana DeGette

Diana DeGette is a Democratic member of the United States [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] from Colorado who, in 2026, lost her party's primary to [[justice-democrats|Justice Democrats]]-backed challenger [[melat-kiros|Melat Kiros]].[1]

## 2026 primary defeat

DeGette's loss prompted House Democrats to reassess their political strategy, with party members describing the upset as a warning sign that even established progressive incumbents are vulnerable to challenges from their left.[1] Progressive organizations pledged to continue targeting additional Democratic incumbents following the result.[1] The defeat came amid reports that former Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] had renewed outreach to Democratic donors and allies as she weighed her next political moves within the party's evolving leadership landscape.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing from [[colorado|Colorado]], framed the primary as part of a broader pattern in which "the commies are winning" nationally, noting that Colorado voters backed change down ballot while keeping existing party leadership in place — with [[phil-weiser|Phil Weiser]] securing the gubernatorial nomination and [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] the nomination for attorney general.[1] The same commentary identified DeGette as the congresswoman who distributed the Ukraine flags waved on the House floor when [[congress|Congress]] passed Ukraine funding, described by the author as "their money laundering."[1]
