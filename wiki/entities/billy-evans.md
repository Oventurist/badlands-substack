# Billy Evans

Billy Evans is the partner of [[elizabeth-holmes|Elizabeth Holmes]], the imprisoned founder of [[theranos|Theranos]].[1] In May 2025 it was reported that Evans had raised millions of dollars for an artificial-intelligence startup called [[haemanthus|Haemanthus]], which hoped to introduce a product usable in medical testing and other settings.[1]

The reporting was based on two sources with direct knowledge of the endeavor who could not speak publicly because the company had not yet officially launched.[1] Those sources said Holmes had been advising Evans on the startup from the federal facility in Bryan, Texas, where she is serving an 11-year sentence, though the precise nature of her support was unclear.[1]
