# KCBS 740 AM

KCBS 740 AM is a San Francisco Bay Area radio station described in reporting as owned by billionaire [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1]

## Disclosure of ICE agent locations

On January 26, 2025, a KCBS 740 AM host publicly revealed the locations of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|US Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] agents operating undercover in San Jose, California.[1] Host [[bret-burkhart|Bret Burkhart]] detailed the types of unmarked vehicles used and the specific locations where ICE agents were present.[1]

Burkhart cited updates from social media posts made by the [[rapid-response-network|Rapid Response Network]], an organization opposing deportation efforts.[1] Reporting noted that the broadcast might have endangered the agents' safety by potentially exposing their covert operations.[1]
