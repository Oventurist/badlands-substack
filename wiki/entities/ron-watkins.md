# Ron Watkins

Ron Watkins, known online by the handle "Codemonkey," is the imageboard administrator most closely associated with the technical operation of the boards on which the Q drops were posted.[1]

## Role in the Q phenomenon

In Badlands Media's "Q for Dummies" series, Watkins is identified as the person who was "in control of the actual boards Q was posting to," and is offered as the likely answer to Q's question in Drop #112 asking readers who owns the boards.[1] The author of the series notes that while the identity of the actual owner of [[4chan]] is not something he can recall, Watkins' operational control of the boards makes him the figure Q was most plausibly pointing toward.[1]

Drop #112 addressed the security posture of those boards directly: Q stated that they were safeguarded but neither fully secure nor immune from attack, and warned that forthcoming information could trigger an operation to shut down 4chan in order to silence the Q postings.[1] That framing places Watkins at the intersection of the drops' distribution channel and the censorship risk Q repeatedly told readers to guard against by archiving material offline.[1]
