# Eric Rice

Eric Rice is described in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief as a friend of the Badlands commentariat whose question shaped the discussion of the reported cartel drone incursion near El Paso, Texas.

Writing about the incident, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] relays that "our friend, Eric Rice, presented an interesting question: Who sold Mexico the drones?" The commentator treats the question as a productive line of inquiry, citing a 2015 article on drone sales that he concedes concerns the Mexican government rather than the cartels — before asking whether, in practice, there is any difference between the two. The exchange forms part of the Brief's broader argument that the [[mexico|Mexican]] state and organized crime are intertwined, an assessment the commentary attributes to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as well.
