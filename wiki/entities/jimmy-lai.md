# Jimmy Lai

Jimmy Lai is a [[hong-kong|Hong Kong]] media proprietor and free-press advocate whose arrest, according to Badlands Media, "proved that even the head of a multi-million dollar company could not stand against the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]]."[1]

## Criticism of the CCP

Lai had been overtly vocal about the CCP's dealings worldwide.[1] He criticized the Catholic Church for signing a treaty or "provisional agreement" with the CCP that, in his view, furthered the parties' control over religious freedoms, and he criticized [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] prior to the election for kowtowing to party leader [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1]

## Apple Daily and imprisonment

Lai's newspaper, [[apple-daily|Apple Daily]], was destroyed by the CCP's use of the [[hong-kong-national-security-law-2020|National Security Law]]; Badlands Media wrote that "it didn't take long for the CCP to completely annihilate Lai's free press."[1] Lai himself has been jailed since late 2020 without any substantial convictions and, the article concluded, will likely remain there indefinitely.[1]
