# Benjamin Strong

**Benjamin Strong** was one of the participants at the secret 1910 [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] meeting, attending as a representative of J.P. Morgan.[1]

He was later named in the [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]] report — as Benjamin Strong Jr. — among the individual bankers singled out in connection with the cartel that controlled eighteen major financial corporations, alongside [[paul-warburg|Paul Warburg]], Jacob H. Schiff, Felix M. Warburg, Frank E. Peabody and William Rockefeller.[1]
