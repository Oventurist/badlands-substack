# Benjamin Strong

Benjamin Strong attended the secret 1910 meeting at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]] as a representative of [[jp-morgan|J. P. Morgan]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

Strong, listed in the [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]] report as Benjamin Strong Jr., was among the individual bankers singled out in that committee's findings on the banking trade, alongside [[paul-warburg|Paul Warburg]], Jacob H. Schiff, Felix M. Warburg, Frank E. Peabody, and William Rockefeller.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
