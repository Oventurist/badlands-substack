# Jewish Agency

The Jewish Agency is the quasi-governmental Israeli body responsible for facilitating Jewish immigration to Israel and for managing absorption of new arrivals.[1]

## Role in post-2022 migration from the former Soviet space

In coverage of the demographic ties between Russia and Israel, the Jewish Agency was cited as reporting that more than 40,000 Jews had immigrated to Israel from Ukraine since the start of the Ukraine war, and that the agency expected a further 100,000 people to apply for Israeli citizenship from Ukraine, Russia and Belarus.[1] Badlands Media commentary emphasized that most of these arrivals would hold dual citizenship, and used the figures to argue that of roughly 7 million Jews living in Israel, as many as 1.5 million have Russian or Ukrainian heritage.[1]

That demographic overlap, the commentary argued, gives [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] a distinct form of leverage over [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] at a moment when Russia was reported to be under consideration as a broker of an Israel–[[hezbollah]] ceasefire.[1] The historical root of that overlap was traced to the [[pale-of-settlement|Pale of Settlement]] and to the eastern European origins of much of the settler population organized under the [[world-zionist-organization|World Zionist Organization]] in the interwar period.[1]
