# Leah Hoopes

Leah Hoopes is a Pennsylvania-based election integrity researcher and commentator who has appeared on Badlands Media programming.[1]

## Badlands appearances

In late March 2025, Hoopes joined [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] on an episode of *The Daily Herold* for a segment discussing the recent Pennsylvania state Senate special election, in which Democrat [[james-malone|James Malone]] flipped a district that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had carried by roughly 15 points the previous November.[1]
