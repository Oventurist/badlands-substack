# GoFundMe

**GoFundMe** is a crowdsourced fundraising platform that became a focal point of debate over financial deplatforming after its handling of donations to the 2022 Canadian trucker protests.[1]

## Role in the Freedom Convoy

In the first installment of the [[money-wars|Money Wars]] series, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] cites GoFundMe as a leading example of money weaponized against a political movement.[1] During the [[freedom-convoy|Freedom Convoy]] protests in Ottawa, the platform unilaterally confiscated over $10 million in trucker donations, announcing an intent to redirect the money to charities of its own choosing on the claim that the funds were going to support "white supremacy and hate."[1]

Deschamps places GoFundMe alongside the Canadian government under [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] and the banks that froze protestors' accounts as actors guilty of what he describes as one of the highest crimes known to man — breach of trust and contract, a violation of fiduciary duty, or complicity in such a violation.[1] In his account, the episode illustrates how total control of money and finance can be used to override the will of the people when lawful protest cannot be shut down directly.[1]
