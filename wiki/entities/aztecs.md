# Aztecs

The **Aztecs** are one of several widely separated cultures in which the [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series claims to find traces of the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult, alongside Ukraine, Romania, Africa and New Zealand.[1]

## Red-haired progenitors

A stated characteristic of the cult is that its progenitors were red-haired albinos — with the explicit caveat that this does not mean all people of that description are members.[1] The article notes that Montezuma of the Aztecs is depicted as having red hair, which it calls highly unusual for the indigenous peoples of Mexico at that time.[1]

## Ritual sacrifice

The Aztecs practiced ritual sacrifice in a style the article describes as highly reminiscent of the Koryos.[1] The spilling of blood was held to be extremely important, being considered food for the "gods" — the article pointedly asks which gods — and in exchange for blood these entities were said to confer material abundance and worldly power upon the cult.[1]

## Trans-Pacific contact claim

The same section reports that the Māori of New Zealand say a race of red-haired, pale-skinned cannibals inhabited the islands before their own arrival, a claim the article attributes to Frederick Dodson's *The Secret History of Polynesia*.[1] Because the article asserts extensive overlap between the Māori and Aztec languages, rooted in the [[ancient-german-language-theory|Ancient German language]], it concludes that the Koryos and other peoples engaged in trans-Pacific travel possibly thousands of years before mainstream history allows.[1]
