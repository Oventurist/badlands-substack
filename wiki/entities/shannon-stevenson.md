# Shannon Stevenson

**Shannon Stevenson** is the Colorado Solicitor General who argued on behalf of Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]] in [[anderson-v-griswold|Anderson v. Griswold]] on February 8, 2024.[1]

## Supreme Court argument

Griswold's side was allotted ten minutes of argument time, which Stevenson used.[1] Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted an unusual moment in which Stevenson objected to Justice [[clarence-thomas|Clarence Thomas]], prompting Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] to clarify that she appeared on Griswold's behalf.[1] Roberts then challenged her to defend the determination of "insurrection" and pressed her to better define the applicable standards.[1]

According to Badlands, the exchange made the differences between Colorado's election laws and those of other states unmistakable, and Stevenson struggled to articulate how a single state disenfranchising its own voters in a federal election — implicating equal protection — could remain a constitutional act.[1] Ashe in America credited Stevenson with one constructive contribution: urging the justices to review the findings of fact and conclusions of law regarding insurrection, a review Badlands writers also favored on the theory that the evidentiary record would not survive scrutiny outside Colorado's courts.[1]
