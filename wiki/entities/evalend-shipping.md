# Evalend Shipping

Evalend Shipping is a Greek maritime transport company identified in Badlands Brief reporting as the owner of the tanker Kiku, a vessel reportedly struck by Iranian forces in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] in late June 2026 after it attempted to bypass agreed-upon traffic lanes.[1] The company is controlled by the Greek shipping magnate [[kriton-lentoudis|Kriton Lentoudis]].[1]

## Alleged sanctions exposure

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Evalend was a commercial counterparty to [[vs-tankers-fze|VS Tankers FZE]], formerly known as AISSOT, leasing that company many of its ships.[1] VS Tankers FZE was in turn named by the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|U.S. Treasury]] as part of a network run by the Iraqi-British national [[salim-ahmed-said|Salim Ahmed Said]], which allegedly blended Iranian and Iraqi oil for sale to Western buyers through [[iraq|Iraq]] or the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]].[1]

On the basis of that commercial relationship, the Brief's analysis argued that the Kiku and her owners form part of a network laundering money and oil out of [[iran|Iran]] through Iraq and the UAE, a pattern the author linked to executive orders and sanctions issued by the Trump administration dating back to 2018.[1] The article presented the vessel strikes and the simultaneous Iraqi anti-corruption sweep as connected events rather than coincidences.[1]
