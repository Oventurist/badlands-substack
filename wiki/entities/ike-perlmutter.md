# Ike Perlmutter

Ike Perlmutter is the former chairman of Marvel Entertainment and a longtime acquaintance of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] During Trump's first term he quietly helped shape policy at the [[department-of-veterans-affairs|Department of Veterans Affairs]] without holding any formal government position, a role documented in reporting on the so-called "shadow rulers" of the VA.[1]

## Relevance to a possible Musk advisory role

In May 2024 The Wall Street Journal reported that Trump had floated an informal advisory role for [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in a prospective second administration, and characterized the arrangement as similar to the one Perlmutter had occupied at the VA — influence exercised through personal access rather than an appointed office.[1] The comparison made Perlmutter a reference point for how unofficial outside advisers can direct federal policy.[1]
