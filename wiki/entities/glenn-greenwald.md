# Glenn Greenwald

Glenn Greenwald is an author and independent journalist cited in Badlands Media coverage as a critic of the transformation of [[david-brock|David Brock]].[1]

Commenting on Brock's shift from conservative attack journalist to Democratic operative, Greenwald said: "I don't know what happened to David Brock, I don't know what was done to him, but overnight he went from one of the most psychotic Hillary haters to someone who treats [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] like a deity."[1]
