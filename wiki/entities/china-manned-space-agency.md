# China Manned Space Agency

The China Manned Space Agency (CMSA) is the [[china|Chinese]] government body responsible for the country's human spaceflight program, including operation of the [[tiangong-space-station|Tiangong space station]] and the Shenzhou crew transport missions that service it.[1]

## Role in the 2025 stranding

In November 2025 the agency was the official voice on the crisis in which three astronauts of the Shenzhou-21 mission were left aboard Tiangong without a dedicated return vehicle after the Shenzhou-20 capsule was damaged by suspected space debris.[1] CMSA stated that the Shenzhou-20 spacecraft "does not meet the requirements for the astronauts' safe return and will remain in orbit to continue relevant experiments."[1] The agency indicated that a new Shenzhou-22 spacecraft was expected to be launched to retrieve the crew, without announcing a specific date.[1] The episode is treated in full at [[2025-shenzhou-space-station-stranding|the 2025 Shenzhou space station stranding]].[1]
