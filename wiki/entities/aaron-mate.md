# Aaron Maté

Aaron Maté is an independent journalist known for his critical reporting on the Russia investigation and on mainstream press coverage of it. In February 2023 he authored a RealClear Investigations piece titled "Unchastened by Russiagate, the NY Times Doubles Down in Its Special Counsel Coverage," which examined how the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] framed the work of Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]].[1]

## RealClear Investigations analysis of Times coverage

Maté's article argued that a widely circulated Jan. 26, 2023 Times report — bylined by Charlie Savage, Adam Goldman, and Katie Benner and based on a "monthslong review" including interviews with more than a dozen current and former officials — declared that Durham's team had gone "unsuccessfully down one path after another" and had "failed to find wrongdoing in the origins of the Russia inquiry," even though Durham had not yet issued his final report.[1] Maté contended that the trio's reporting continued the same journalistic behavior that had made the paper "a reliable disseminator of discredited innuendo" about a conspiracy between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russia, omitting countervailing information and distorting available facts.[1] In his reading, the article did not set the record straight but instead attempted to write off the Durham probe before its findings were released while whitewashing key [[fbi|FBI]] and Clinton campaign actors in the [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] affair.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] praised the piece as an "excellent article by Aaron Mate" in the February 16, 2023 edition of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], pairing it with his own Substack commentary on what move Durham might make next.[1]
