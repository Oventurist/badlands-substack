# Marvel Studios

Marvel Studios is the film production company behind the Marvel Cinematic Universe, operating as a unit of [[disney|Disney]] and led by studio head [[kevin-feige|Kevin Feige]].[1] Over more than a decade the studio built an interconnected franchise beginning with *Iron Man* (2008), with executive [[victoria-alonso|Victoria Alonso]] serving as one of its principal architects in charge of physical production, post-production, visual effects and animation.[1]

## 2023 crisis

By March 2023 the studio was the subject of intense scrutiny. Disney fired Alonso abruptly and "for cause," a decision made by a committee of Disney executives which Feige declined to intervene in, and which quickly drew threats of litigation and public accusations that she had been silenced over LGBTQ+ advocacy.[1] In the same period, [[jonathan-majors|Jonathan Majors]] — the actor cast as the studio's next tentpole villain — was arrested in New York on charges of strangulation, assault and harassment following an alleged domestic dispute.[1]

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized these events as part of a pattern of "escalation and acceleration hitting some of the biggest woke entertainment brands in the world," noting that Marvel's most recent films had lost the company hundreds of millions of dollars and that the Disney+ streaming service was reporting increasing net losses.[1] He declined to assert coordination behind the pattern but summarized it with the phrase "Pride ... meet Fall."[1]
