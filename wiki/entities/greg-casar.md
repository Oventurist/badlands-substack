# Greg Casar

Greg Casar is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas who has been a vocal critic of state-level border enforcement measures pursued by Governor [[greg-abbott|Greg Abbott]].[1]

## Call to federalize the Texas National Guard

In late January 2024, amid the standoff between the State of Texas and the federal government over razor-wire barriers along the Rio Grande, Casar publicly endorsed a proposal advanced by fellow Texas Democrat [[joaquin-castro|Joaquin Castro]] that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] should assume federal control of the [[texas-national-guard|Texas National Guard]].[1] Casar stated that he agreed with Castro that Biden should take control of the Guard, which had continued installing concertina wire in defiance of a [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] order permitting federal agents to remove it.[1]

The call placed Casar among the Democratic legislators arguing that Abbott's invocation of Article I, Section 10, Clause 3 of the Constitution — the state self-defense provision — amounted to an unlawful challenge to federal supremacy, an argument commentators at Badlands Media characterized as one side of an escalating federal-versus-state confrontation.[1]
