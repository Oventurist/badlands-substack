# Condé Nast

Condé Nast is an American mass media company and magazine publisher whose titles include GQ, Vogue, The New Yorker and Vanity Fair.

## 2024 strike

In the TikTok address quoted in [[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]], [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] reports that "hundreds of workers at Condé Nast, the parent company of pretty much every major magazine from GQ to Vogue to The New Yorker to Vanity Fair, are on strike because they're also facing impending layoffs." The article cites this alongside the collapse of [[sports-illustrated|Sports Illustrated]] and cuts at [[time-magazine|Time Magazine]] as evidence that the magazine sector, like newspapers and digital media, is in structural decline.
