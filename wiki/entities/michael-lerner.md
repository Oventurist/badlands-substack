# Michael Lerner

Michael Lerner is an American rabbi associated with the progressive Jewish magazine Tikkun and a critic of Israeli government policy.[1]

## Response to the 2007 AJC paper

Following the publication of [[alvin-h-rosenfeld|Alvin H. Rosenfeld]]'s [[american-jewish-committee|American Jewish Committee]] paper [[progressive-jewish-thought-and-the-new-antisemitism|*Progressive Jewish Thought and the New Anti-Semitism*]], Lerner emphasized the chilling effect of unfounded accusations of antisemitism on elected officials.[1] He is quoted in the Badlands Media essay "Our 'Greatest Ally'" as saying:

> "When we talk to Congressional representatives who are liberal or even extremely progressive on every other issue, they tell us privately that they are afraid to speak out about the way Israeli policies are destructive to the best interests of the United States or the best interests of world peace—lest they too be labeled anti-Semitic and anti-Israel. If it can happen to Jimmy Carter, some of them told me recently, a man with impeccable moral credentials, then no one is really politically safe."[1]

The reference to [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]] invokes the backlash the former president faced over his own criticism of Israeli policy.[1] Lerner's remarks are used in the article to support its central contention that the [[antisemitism|antisemitism]] charge operates as a silencing device within American politics.[1]
