# Matthew Reeves

Matthew Reeves is the director of archaeology at [[montpelier|Montpelier]], the Virginia estate of President [[james-madison|James Madison]].[1]

## Reported remarks

Reeves drew congressional attention in May 2023 when Reps. [[bob-good|Bob Good]] and [[eli-crane|Eli Crane]] cited [[washington-examiner|Washington Examiner]] reporting that Reeves "said something to the effect that staff members had no interest in honoring a 'dead white president and a dead white president's Constitution.'"[1] Reeves was also reported as saying that he had to act "less like a bulldozer and more like a termite that undermined a building's foundation, destroying it from within before tearing it down."[1] The lawmakers used those reported remarks to support their demand that the [[national-trust-for-historic-preservation|National Trust for Historic Preservation]] account for the direction of Montpelier's exhibits and for the use of federal funds.[1]
