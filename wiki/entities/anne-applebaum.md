# Anne Applebaum

Anne Applebaum is an American-born journalist and author who has served as a board member of the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]], and who is described in Badlands Media coverage as a neoconservative propagandist embedded in the democracy-promotion establishment.[1]

## Criticism of Donald Trump

Applebaum has repeatedly characterized [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as an authoritarian, comparing him in her books and articles to figures such as Vladimir Putin and Hungary's Viktor Orbán.[1] Weeks before the 2024 presidential election she published an *Atlantic* piece titled "Trump Is Speaking Like Hitler, Stalin, and Mussolini."[1] Critics cite her writing as evidence that the NED, presented as a bipartisan institution, has aligned itself with anti-Trump and anti-populist political agendas.[1]
