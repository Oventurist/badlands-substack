# Montezuma

**Montezuma** was the Aztec ruler encountered by the Spanish at the time of the conquest of Mexico. He appears in part five of [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series as a single but pointed piece of evidence in the essay's argument for ancient contact between Mesoamerica and the Germanic world.[1]

## Red hair and the Koryos

The essay observes that Montezuma is portrayed as having red hair, and immediately notes that the [[koryos|Koryos]] — the werewolf cult the series identifies as the ancient origin of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] — are likewise known for red hair.[1] The author does not resolve what the coincidence means, asking instead whether the Saxons were an ancient counterbalance to the Koryos, whether the two groups are related in some sense, or whether both are partly true.[1]

## Context in the Saxony correspondences

The Montezuma observation is placed directly after the essay's two proper-name correspondences: the Aztec *Sacatlan*, said to mean "ancient Mexico," rendered in ancient German as *Saschenland* or [[saxony|Saxony]], and the Aztec phrase *Ani Sazi*, said to mean "ancient ancestors who built the cities," rendered as *Ahnen Sachen*, "ancestors from Saxony."[1] Read together, these claims are used to suggest that the Aztec ruling world and the Saxon world were branches of a single earlier culture that spoke [[ancient-german-language-thesis|ancient German]].[1] The author qualifies the whole line of inquiry by saying he is still researching these matters and will refrain from saying anything too conclusive too soon, while adding that the "good guys" and "bad guys" appear to be more closely related than one would assume.[1]
