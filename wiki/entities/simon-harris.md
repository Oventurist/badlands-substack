# Simon Harris

Simon Harris is the Deputy Prime Minister and top diplomat of [[ireland|Ireland]], who in September 2025 led an effort among European Union member states to impose sanctions on [[israel|Israel]] over the conflict in Gaza.[1]

## Push for EU sanctions on Israel

Irish public broadcaster RTÉ reported on Monday, 15 September 2025 that Harris was reaching out to [[european-union|EU]] foreign ministers to press for sanctions.[1] He asked his EU counterparts, described as already considering unilateral trade measures, to co-sign a letter to the bloc's foreign policy chief [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]] urging a swift decision on sanctions.[1]

The letter reportedly stated: "As Member States, we stand ready to work with you immediately to overcome the current obstacles and ensure that sufficient pressure is applied to the Israeli government to change its course and meet its obligations according to international law."[1]

Harris's initiative followed a speech in Strasbourg by European Commission president [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]], who called the situation in Gaza "unacceptable" and urged member states to impose sanctions on Israel's "extremist ministers and on violent settlers," pledging that the Commission would put bilateral support to Israel on hold and proposing a partial freeze of the EU–Israel free trade agreement.[1] Ireland was among the member states — alongside Spain, Sweden, Denmark and the Netherlands — calling for suspension of that deal, while Germany, Hungary and the Czech Republic opposed sanctioning Israel.[1]
