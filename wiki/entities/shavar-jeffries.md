# Shavar Jeffries

Shavar Jeffries is the chief executive officer of the [[kipp-foundation|KIPP Foundation]], the national organization behind the KIPP network of charter schools.[1]

## Nomination to BlackRock fund board

In 2024 Jeffries was one of three candidates that [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]]'s [[saba-capital|Saba Capital]] sought to install on the board of directors of BlackRock Capital Allocation Trust as part of its campaign against ten [[blackrock|BlackRock]] closed-end funds.[1] Badlands Media described him as "plenty sketchy," questioned what in his background qualified him to serve on an investment fund board, and characterized charter schools generally as a mechanism for drawing government funding without transparency about how money is spent or how students perform.[1] The author further asserted that Jeffries has extensive ties to other [[deep-state|Deep State]] figures and organizations.[1]
