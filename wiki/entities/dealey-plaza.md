# Dealey Plaza

Dealey Plaza is the public square in Dallas, Texas where President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] was assassinated. It appears in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] corpus principally through the personal narrative that opens Chapter 11 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series.[1]

## In the Q for Dummies series

Writing in April 2024, [[absolutetruth1776|AbsoluteTruth1776]] recounted visiting Dealey Plaza with his wife following the GART gathering in Dallas, describing the visit as a mind-numbing and surreal experience.[1] He characterized the site as "the altar" on which the Republic was sacrificed, and said the heaviness and darkness he felt there would stay with him permanently.[1]

The author noted that within the preceding eight months he had visited both the Kennedy Space Center and Dealey Plaza, and had been given the opportunity to speak on a JFK panel at GART in the very city where Kennedy was murdered.[1] He described the Kennedy assassination as the subject that first sent him down the rabbit hole at age twelve and as the reason he does the work he does.[1]

The visit prompted him to announce his largest project to date: a full-length documentary on the JFK assassination and the surrounding events that, in his framing, led to the downfall of the Republic as it had been known.[1]
