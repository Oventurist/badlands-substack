# Wanxiang Group

Wanxiang Group is a Chinese automotive conglomerate with an American subsidiary operating in the United States.[1] The company drew political attention in August 2025 when [[pin-ni|Pin Ni]], an executive at the group and head of its US arm, was reported to have donated $50,000 to the Virginia gubernatorial campaign of [[abigail-spanberger|Abigail Spanberger]] in April and May of that year.[1]

Reporting by the Washington Free Beacon highlighted the tension between Virginia's prohibition on political contributions by foreign nationals and Chinese rules that typically bar Chinese Communist Party members — of which Ni was said to be one — from holding foreign citizenship or permanent residency.[1]
