# Elizabeth Oyer

Elizabeth Oyer is a lawyer who served as the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s top pardon attorney and was fired after refusing to grant the restoration of gun ownership rights to the actor [[mel-gibson|Mel Gibson]].[1]

Following her dismissal, Oyer became a prominent critic of how the department was being run under Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]]. She described an unusual concentration of authority outside the department itself: "The decisions are being made at the White House, and then they're being pushed down to the Department of Justice, which is very, very atypical."[1] Of Bondi personally, Oyer said, "It feels like she is just performing a part. She is like an actor, in a way."[1] Her remarks appeared in reporting drawing on interviews with 20 current and former officials about Bondi's role as a public messenger for the administration.[1]
