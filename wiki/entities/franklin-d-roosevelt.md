# Franklin D. Roosevelt

Franklin Delano Roosevelt (FDR) was the United States president whose wartime vision of the postwar order is treated in Badlands Media commentary as the authentic alternative to the unipolar system that followed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## The Four Freedoms

In 1941 Roosevelt outlined the [[four-freedoms|Four Freedoms]] — freedom from want, freedom from fear of war, freedom of speech and freedom of conscience — which the author describes as universal and as forming the backbone of the Atlantic Charter, the [[united-nations|UN Charter]] and the original designs of the [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods system]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Vision for the United Nations

The UN as envisioned by Roosevelt, or by his vice president Henry Wallace, was never meant to be a world government but a platform for dialogue and the harmonization of security and economic interests among civilizations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Bretton Woods was similarly premised on emitting long-term credit for large-scale projects in order to internationalize the New Deal during what was envisioned as an era of multipolar cooperation and win-win development.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Legacy

The author notes the irony that Roosevelt's Four Freedoms are now being advanced by Eurasian powers regarded by many as the most "un-American" forces on earth, pointing to the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|SCO]] summit in Tianjin as evidence.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) He also argues that the noble origins of the institutions Roosevelt helped design were later hijacked by deep state operatives, though [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] attempted to revive them.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)
