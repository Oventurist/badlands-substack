# Maggie Haberman

Maggie Haberman is a reporter for The New York Times known for her coverage of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his administrations.[1]

## 2026 book with Jonathan Swan

Haberman co-authored a book with fellow New York Times reporter [[jonathan-swan|Jonathan Swan]] that alleged Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] had privately used derogatory language about Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] ahead of a contentious Oval Office meeting involving Trump and Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]].[1] Excerpts from the book, cited by RT and attributed to material published by The Guardian, described Bessent's concerns about Zelensky's behavior before the meeting, including the characterization that Zelensky "acted like Mr. Bean on crack."[1]

The reporting placed Bessent inside the negotiation of the U.S.–Ukraine minerals arrangement and present during the discussions that collapsed into confrontation.[1]
