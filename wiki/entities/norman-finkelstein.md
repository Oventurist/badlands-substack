# Norman Finkelstein

Norman Finkelstein is an American political scientist, author and activist known for his sustained criticism of the State of Israel and of what he describes as the political exploitation of the Nazi Holocaust.[1] In the Badlands Media essay "Our 'Greatest Ally'," Ryan DeLarme presents Finkelstein as "another prime target of the Israel machine," a figure whose scholarship made him a lightning rod for accusations of antisemitism despite his own Jewish background and family history.[1]

## The Holocaust Industry

Finkelstein's book *The Holocaust Industry* is identified in the article as the work that "certainly put a target on his back."[1] DeLarme cites the sustained campaign against Finkelstein — including treatments of him in literature on the so-called "new antisemitism" — as evidence that the antisemitism charge is deployed against Jewish critics of Israeli policy as readily as against non-Jews.[1]

## Role in the article's argument

Finkelstein appears in a section of the essay devoted to the labelling of dissenting Jews as antisemites, alongside [[american-jewish-committee|the American Jewish Committee]]'s 2007 publication [[progressive-jewish-thought-and-the-new-antisemitism|*Progressive Jewish Thought and the New Anti-Semitism*]] and the response of Rabbi [[michael-lerner|Michael Lerner]].[1] The article also references a recent interview in which Finkelstein "very slowly lays out his view of the conflict," offered to readers as an example of an argued rather than bigoted critique.[1]

DeLarme's broader claim is that the charge of [[antisemitism|anti-Semitism]] has, by 2024, "essentially lost all weight and meaning" through overuse, and that the treatment of figures such as Finkelstein demonstrates that the label functions as a silencing mechanism rather than a description of bigotry.[1]
