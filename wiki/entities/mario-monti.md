# Mario Monti

**Mario Monti** is an Italian economist who served as prime minister of Italy and, earlier, as a European commissioner.[1]

The Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* groups Monti with Giuseppe Conte as technocratic Italian prime ministers who "continued the legacy of [[mario-draghi|Draghi]]'s ecclesiastical economic engineering."[1] The essay describes Monti as a former EU commissioner and member of the Trilateral Commission who was "groomed under Vatican-linked financial networks."[1]

In the essay's account, Conte — presented publicly as an outsider — preserved the Jesuit-aligned legal frameworks binding Italy to European Central Bank and [[vatican|Vatican]] concordats, and the two men together exemplify how "spiritual compacts and monetary policy remain fused in post-monarch Europe."[1] They appear in a passage that also names Giovanni Bisignani and Jean-Claude Trichet as elite financial intermediaries linking European banking to Vatican diplomatic channels.[1]
