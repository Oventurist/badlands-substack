# Elizabeth II

Elizabeth II was the long-reigning British monarch and niece of [[edward-viii|Edward VIII]].[1]

In the Channel 4 documentary *Edward VIII: Britain's Traitor King*, drawn from declassified Royal Archives material and the work of historian [[andrew-lownie|Andrew Lownie]], it is reported that her uncle taught the young Elizabeth how to perform a proper "sieg heil."[1] Matthew Ehret cites this as one of many uncomfortable links between the [[house-of-windsor|House of Windsor]] and Nazism.[1]
