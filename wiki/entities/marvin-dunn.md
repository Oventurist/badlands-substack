# Marvin Dunn

Marvin Dunn is a historian associated with the study of Florida and Miami history who emerged as a critic of the [[donald-j-trump-presidential-library|Donald J. Trump Presidential Library]] project announced on March 31, 2026.[1]

## Criticism of the Miami land transfer

Dunn characterized the transfer of prime waterfront land from [[miami-dade-college|Miami-Dade College]] to the [[trump-library-foundation|Trump Library Foundation]] as a "land giveaway," framing the arrangement as a transfer of public educational assets to a private foundation associated with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] His objection was reported alongside broader mockery of the project's gold cladding and monumental statuary, including comparisons drawn by the office of California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] between the golden Trump statue and effigies of authoritarian leaders.[1]
