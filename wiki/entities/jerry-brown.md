# Jerry Brown

Jerry Brown is a California politician who served as Governor of California and is referenced in Badlands reporting on [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] as one of the powerful state figures who came out of the [[jesuits|Jesuit]]-run [[st-ignatius-college-preparatory|St. Ignatius College Prep]] in San Francisco.[1]

His significance in that account is contextual rather than direct: the clergy abuse documents that District Attorney [[terence-hallinan|Terence Hallinan]] obtained from the [[archdiocese-of-san-francisco|Archdiocese of San Francisco]] touched on institutions tied to the California power structure, including St. Ignatius, whose alumni network encompassed Brown — then serving as governor — along with members of the [[getty-family|Getty family]].[1] The article uses these ties to argue that the suppression of the records served entrenched interests.[1]

## Views on water and population

Matthew Ehret cites Brown, as former Governor of California, among the adherents of the view that it was a mistake to have used science and technology to populate the west coast of the United States over the past 120 years.[2] In this reading, such figures regard the great dams of Franklin Roosevelt's New Deal — which made the Imperial Valley possible — as an abomination of nature, and conclude that humanity should adapt to the deserts nature created and depopulate places like California, whose population exceeds that of all Canada by more than seven million people.[2] Ehret classes this position as one of the two forms of [[zero-sum-thinking|zero-sum thinking]] blocking a real solution to the [[north-american-water-crisis|North American water crisis]].[2]
