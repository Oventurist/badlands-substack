# Tom Brady

Tom Brady is a retired American football quarterback widely regarded as one of the greatest players in NFL history. He appears in Badlands Media coverage both as an athlete and as a rare example of a mainstream sports figure who publicly backed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Brady led the Tampa Bay Buccaneers to a Super Bowl title in 2020 with [[antonio-brown|Antonio Brown]] on the roster, completing what Badlands described as Brown's comeback story after his 2019 departure from the league.[1]

Badlands characterized Brady as the only professional athlete from a "mainstream" sport to have endorsed Donald Trump up until the 2024 cycle, an endorsement made during the 2016 campaign.[1] That framing was used to underline how unusual Brown's own 2024 political turn appeared.[1]
