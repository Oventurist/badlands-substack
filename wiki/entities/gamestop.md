# GameStop

GameStop (ticker GME) is the American video-game retailer whose stock became the emblematic "meme stock" of the retail-investor movement, and which returned to prominence in mid-2024 amid renewed activity by [[keith-gill|Keith Gill]].[1]

## 2024 meme-stock revival

Gill, known online as Roaring Kitty, bought a large volume of GameStop options through [[e-trade|E*Trade]] shortly before reigniting the meme-stock craze in May 2024.[1] By early June he disclosed screenshots showing GameStop shares valued at roughly $140 million plus additional near-dated options, with total gains of $85.5 million.[1] The stock rose 21 percent on Monday, June 3, 2024, and had gained more than 60 percent since Gill's reappearance.[1] The rally revived debate over whether influencer-driven buying constitutes market manipulation, prompting internal discussions at E*Trade and [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]] and an inquiry by the Massachusetts securities division.[1]

## Symbolic significance

Badlands commentary treated GameStop as carrying multiple meanings in the information war: it argued the saga had red-pilled tens of millions of ordinary people to the view that the financial system is rigged in favor of institutions and against retail investors, since institutional money can effectively pause one side of the game.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] contended that the retail "Apes" were still in the reactive phase of the Hegelian dialectic and that the only winning move was to exit the rigged venue for sound, decentralized, finite money such as [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]].[1]

## Console wars statement (2025)

In October 2025, GameStop issued a "statement" declaring the console wars "over" in response to the reveal that Halo would come to PlayStation in 2026.[2] The [[white-house|White House]] social media account quoted the company's slogan "Power to the Players" alongside an AI-generated image of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Master Chief's armor, an episode covered at [[console-wars-declaration|console wars declaration]].[2] The post sparked a surge in GameStop's stock and sent the associated meme-stock subreddit into a frenzy.[2]

## Bitcoin treasury adoption (2025)

On Tuesday, March 25, 2025, GameStop announced its long-anticipated move into [[bitcoin|bitcoin]], and shares rose more than 6% in extended trading.[3] In a short statement the company said its board had unanimously approved an update to its investment policy adding bitcoin as a treasury-reserve asset, a move that followed weeks of bitcoin speculation surrounding the retailer and chief executive Ryan Cohen.[3] In a Form 10-K filing, GameStop said that a portion of the company's cash or of future debt and equity issuances may be invested in bitcoin, adding: "We have not set a maximum amount of bitcoin we may accumulate, and may sell any bitcoin we may acquire."[3]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the convergence of the GameStop storyline with the bitcoin storyline would provide "retroactive confirmation" of long-term strategic planning and of the role mass narrative seeding had played in it, closing with the maxim "He that makes the money makes the world."[3]
