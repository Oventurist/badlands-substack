# Ingo Gerhartz

**Ingo Gerhartz** is the head of the Luftwaffe, the German air force, whose taped conversation with fellow senior officers was published by Russia in March 2024, triggering a political crisis in Berlin.[1]

## Intercepted call

On Friday, March 1, 2024, [[margarita-simonyan|Margarita Simonyan]], head of the Russian state broadcaster RT, posted a recording on her Telegram channel in which Gerhartz discussed the possibility of deploying German Taurus missiles in Ukraine with other senior officers.[1] In the 30-minute conversation the officers also discussed whether Ukraine could attack Russian targets without the participation of German soldiers, and how many missiles could be delivered to Ukraine.[1]

## Fallout

German chancellor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]], speaking on the sidelines of a meeting with Pope Francis in Rome, promised a full investigation and described the incident as a "very serious affair," saying, "It will be investigated very carefully, intensively and quickly. It is also necessary to do so."[1] Politicians from parties in Scholz's coalition expressed concern that Russia may have eavesdropped on other sensitive conversations and that government communications might no longer be adequately protected.[1] Scholz had to that point refused to deliver Taurus missiles to Kyiv.[1]
