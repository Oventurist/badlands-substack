# Scott Skavdahl

**Scott Skavdahl** is a judge of the U.S. District Court for the District of Wyoming.[1]

## Ruling on Wyoming's proof-of-citizenship law

On Tuesday, July 22, 2025, Skavdahl dismissed a federal lawsuit challenging [[wyoming-house-bill-156|Wyoming House Bill 156]], the state law requiring voters to provide proof of U.S. citizenship and state residency to register to vote.[1] The suit had been brought by the Equality State Policy Center through its attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]] against Wyoming Secretary of State [[chuck-gray|Chuck Gray]].[1] Gray publicly celebrated the dismissal as a vindication of the statute and of his office's defense of it.[1]
