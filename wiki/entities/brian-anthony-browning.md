# Brian Anthony Browning

Brian Anthony Browning (c. 1994 – June 22, 2025) was a 31-year-old man from Romulus, Michigan, who was killed while attempting to carry out a mass shooting at CrossPointe Community Church in Wayne, Michigan.[1]

## Attack and death

Browning was seen at about 11 a.m. on Sunday, June 22, 2025, driving a silver SUV erratically in the parking lot of the church, roughly 25 miles west of Detroit.[1] He parked and emerged wearing camouflage and a tactical vest, armed with an AR-15 style rifle carrying more than a dozen fully loaded magazines, a semi-automatic handgun with an extended magazine, and hundreds of rounds of ammunition.[1] Several church staff members confronted him as he began firing, and a parishioner rammed him with a Ford F-150 pickup truck while Browning shot at the vehicle repeatedly.[1] After a security guard was shot in the leg, two staff members shot and killed Browning.[1] See [[crosspointe-community-church-shooting|CrossPointe Community Church shooting]].

## Background

Police said Browning had no criminal history or prior police contact that would have indicated a turn toward violence.[1] Wayne Police Department Chief Ryan Strong said his motivations were unknown but that he appeared to have been suffering from a mental health crisis.[1] Browning's mother was a member of the congregation but was not inside the building at the time of the attack; Browning had attended a few services in the previous year but was not himself a member.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] urged readers to pray for Browning's mother, describing her situation as "unfathomably hard."[1]
