# Vlad Filat

Vlad Filat is a Moldovan politician and oligarch who partnered with [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]], [[ilan-shor|Ilan Shor]] and [[vyacheslav-platon|Vyacheslav Platon]] in the [[moldovan-billion-dollar-bank-fraud|billion-dollar Moldovan bank fraud]] committed between 2011 and 2014.[1] In 2016 Filat was, in the account of the source, sacrificed by his former partner Plahotniuc and sentenced to three years in prison for taking bribes and participating in the heist.[1]

## MCC compact and arrest

As prime minister of [[moldova|Moldova]], Filat presided with U.S. Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] over the January 22, 2010 signing of a five-year, $262 million [[millennium-challenge-corporation|Millennium Challenge Corporation]] compact, the agency's first under CEO Daniel Yohannes.[2] Reuters reported that he was arrested on October 15, 2015, taken from parliament by masked officers of Moldova's anti-corruption bureau after lawmakers voted to strip his immunity at the prosecutor's request, amid mass protests over the theft of $1 billion from the banking system.[2] Forbes later reported that Filat, a former member of [[vlad-plahotniuc|Plahotniuc]]'s party, was serving a nine-year prison term for his involvement in the theft of National Bank reserves, with a Moldovan investigator describing the system as "rigged by Plahotniuc, with Vlad Filat in his corner."[2] Badlands writer Joe Lange highlights the contrast between the 2010 ceremony and the 2015 arrest as evidence that American aid money entrenched rather than curbed Moldovan corruption.[2]
