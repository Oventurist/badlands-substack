# Nervis Villalobos

Nervis Villalobos is a former Venezuelan energy official associated with [[pdvsa|PDVSA]], the state oil company. The Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026 cites an Argentine press report naming Villalobos among Venezuelans accused of looting PDVSA who held accounts at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]], and notes that he was arrested in Spain in 2018.

Villalobos also features in a 2022 investigation by the [[occrp|Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project]] as part of the Suisse Secrets project on Venezuelan elites hiding stolen oil money in Credit Suisse, in which cooperating witness [[mathias-krull|Mathias Krull]] provides commentary on him. The Brief observes that the PDVSA-linked Credit Suisse accounts were all opened between 2004 and 2015 — the same corruption timeline cited in the RICO lawsuit filed by [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] in March 2018, although that lawsuit named no banking institution.
