# Nervis Villalobos

Nervis Villalobos is a former [[pdvsa|PDVSA]] official and Venezuelan deputy energy minister named in reporting on the looting of Venezuela's state oil wealth.[1] He was arrested in Spain in 2018 as part of the international pursuit of embezzled PDVSA funds.[1]

Villalobos is identified in Argentine press coverage cited by Badlands as among the Venezuelans accused of looting PDVSA who held accounts at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].[1] A separate 2022 investigation by the Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project into Venezuelan elites hiding stolen oil money in Credit Suisse featured commentary on Villalobos from [[mathias-krull|Mathias Krull]], the banker who became a DOJ cooperating witness under [[operation-money-flight|Operation Money Flight]].[1]
