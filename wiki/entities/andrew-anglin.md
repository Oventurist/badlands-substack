# Andrew Anglin

**Andrew Anglin** is an American far-right internet figure described by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as one of the "genuine neo-Nazi types" occupying the extreme end of the anti-Israel far right.[1]

## Placement outside the coalition

In [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]], DeLarme sketches a spectrum within the far-right anti-[[israel|Israel]] crowd running from the [[nick-fuentes|Nick Fuentes]]/[[groypers|Groyper]] brand of [[america-first-movement|"America First"]] to figures such as Anglin.[1] He states that he hesitates to include these types in the [[donald-trump|Trump]] coalition at all, both because he believes they have had a more deleterious than positive effect on the movement and because he is not certain they see themselves as part of the coalition.[1]

## The purity-spiral problem

Anglin's cohort features in DeLarme's fifth proposed remedy for the coalition's fracture: that "the groypers and neo-Nazis have to be dealt with in the public square, whether you like it or not."[1] He argues they are a liability that hands the media and the neocons a perfect cudgel, but adds that they are mostly young and that few people hold their youthful political views throughout life — "today's liability could be tomorrow's voters."[1] He pairs this with the reciprocal demand that the "criticize Israel and you're an anti-Semite" crowd be told publicly and repeatedly that policy criticism is not hate speech.[1]
