# Ashish Jha

Ashish Jha is a physician and public health official who served as the White House COVID-19 response coordinator under President Joe Biden.[1]

## Criticism of the ACIP overhaul

In June 2025, Jha publicly criticized Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] for firing all 17 members of the [[advisory-committee-on-immunization-practices|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention vaccine advisory panel]].[1] Kennedy had announced the removals in a *Wall Street Journal* op-ed, arguing that "a clean sweep is needed to re-establish public confidence in vaccine science."[1]

In an interview with CNN's Wolf Blitzer, Jha called the op-ed "a series of nonsense about a group of individuals, experts … who shape what vaccines, if any, are going to be available to the American people," adding that the move was "very concerning" and "a step in the wrong direction."[1] He said he would be watching who Kennedy appointed next and expressed broader concern about what the purge signaled regarding the secretary's vaccine agenda.[1]
