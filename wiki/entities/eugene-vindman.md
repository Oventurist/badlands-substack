# Eugene Vindman

Eugene Vindman is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Virginia (D-Va.) who previously served on the National Security Council (NSC) during the first administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] He is the twin brother of [[alexander-vindman|Alexander Vindman]], the NSC official whose account of a July 2019 call between Trump and Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] became the basis of Trump's first impeachment.[1] Eugene Vindman himself played a key role in exposing details of that call.[1]

## Demand for release of the Trump–MBS call transcript (November 2025)

In November 2025, Vindman publicly called on President Trump to release the transcript of what he described as a "shocking" telephone call between Trump and Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] that took place in the aftermath of the assassination of Washington Post journalist [[jamal-khashoggi|Jamal Khashoggi]].[1] Vindman said a second call with the Saudi crown prince exists that rivals the Zelensky call as "the most problematic," telling viewers "you will be shocked."[1]

Speaking on CNN's "News Central" on Wednesday, November 19, 2025, Vindman said the call with the de facto Saudi leader "was about the murder of an American resident — a Virginia resident — and a Washington Post reporter."[1] In a floor speech the previous day he stated: "During my tenure on Trump's White House National Security Council staff, I reviewed many of Trump's calls with foreign leaders. Of all the calls I reviewed, two stood out as the most problematic: The first, we all know, it was between President Trump and President Zelensky, which resulted in President Trump's first impeachment."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Ashe in America responded to the episode by arguing that "the time for restraint has passed," contending that presidential restraint had emboldened the Vindman brothers after what she characterized, "in my opinion based on the public record," as participation in insurrection, seditious conspiracy and treason.[1] She asked why such officials "are still walking around free," and concluded that "if there is to be a Golden Age, there must be a Justice Phase."[1]
