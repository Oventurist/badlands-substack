# Thomas Haldenwang

**Thomas Haldenwang** is the head of Germany's domestic intelligence agency, which monitors extremist movements within the country.[1]

In February 2024, the agency reported that the number of far-right extremists in Germany had been rising, reaching 38,800 in 2022, of whom 14,000 were considered potentially violent.[1] Haldenwang said the numbers were believed to have risen again the following year.[1]

His agency's findings underpinned proposals by Interior Minister [[nancy-faeser|Nancy Faeser]] to make it easier to trace right-wing extremists' financing and to establish an "early recognition unit" for detecting far-right and foreign disinformation campaigns.[1] The debate followed large protests against the far right and reporting that extremists had met to discuss deporting millions of immigrants, with members of the [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]] party present.[1]
