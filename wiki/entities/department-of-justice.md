# Department of Justice

## 2026 election order litigation

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order on election administration directed the Justice Department to prioritize investigations into state and local election officials who issue federal ballots to people deemed ineligible to vote.[1] The Department subsequently asked a federal appeals court to lift the injunction blocking key portions of the order in 23 Democrat-led states; the court denied that request in late July 2026, leaving Judge [[indira-talwani|Talwani]]'s injunction intact and setting up a likely [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] fight over presidential authority in federal elections.[1]
