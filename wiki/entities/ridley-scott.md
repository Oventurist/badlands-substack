# Ridley Scott

**Ridley Scott** is a film director identified in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as the director of the 1996 film [[white-squall|White Squall]], starring Jeff Bridges, from which the [[q-drops|Q]]-associated slogan [[wwg1wga|"Where we go one, we go all"]] is said to derive.[1]

## Treatment in Badlands commentary

Writing on February 14, 2024, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] noted that he had recently written about Scott on his own Substack and stated his belief that Scott, along with his brother Tony Scott and other filmmakers including Mel Gibson, Christopher Nolan and Stanley Kubrick, have attempted to communicate with audiences through their films by embedding coded messaging about various conspiracies.[1]

In *White Squall*, Scott depicts a group of young men sailing a ship under Bridges's captain who encounter a massive storm that appears out of nowhere; the crew, previously disjointed, selfish and undisciplined, is forced to set aside its disagreements and work as a cohesive team to keep the ship afloat.[1] Carlson treats this narrative as the template for the unity message he reads into WWG1WGA.[1]
