# Michael McAuliffe

Michael McAuliffe is a former federal prosecutor and former elected state attorney in Florida who has commented publicly on the legal reach of the state's actions against [[disney]].[1]

In August 2023 he told [[newsweek|Newsweek]] that the [[central-florida-tourism-oversight-district|Central Florida Tourism Oversight District]]'s announcement that it had summarily ended all diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives within its jurisdiction "appears to be a mixture of political posturing and legal implementation."[1] He added that Disney, like many other American companies, was "likely revamping its DEI initiatives to identify ways to promote a productive and vibrant workforce without running afoul of the newly announced prohibition on race as an explicit remedial factor in its business decisions."[1]

His assessment was cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators as evidence that the policy change might produce less substantive effect at Disney than its political framing suggested.[1]
