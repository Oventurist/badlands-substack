# Hua Chunying

Hua Chunying is a Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson who joined chief epidemiologist [[zeng-guang|Zeng Guang]] in redirecting scrutiny over COVID-19's origins toward the [[pentagon|Pentagon's]] globally extended array of bioweapons laboratories.[1]

In her widely reported statement she said that if the United States "truly respects facts, it should open the biological lab at [[fort-detrick|Fort Detrick]], give more transparency to issues like its 200-plus overseas bio-labs, invite WHO experts to conduct origin-tracing in the United States, and respond to the concerns from the international community with real actions."[1] The demand mirrored the reciprocal transparency logic Chinese officials deployed against the United States [[state-department|State Department]] fact sheet of January 15, 2021, which had accused the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] of systematically preventing a transparent investigation into the pandemic's origin.[1]

Hua's intervention followed the earlier public questioning of the natural-origin narrative by fellow spokesman [[zhao-lijian|Zhao Lijian]], and forms part of the pattern that [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites in arguing that the [[lab-leak-theory|Wuhan lab leak hypothesis]] functions as misdirection away from American biodefense infrastructure.[1]
