# Eurasian Times

The Eurasian Times is an online publication covering defense, geopolitics, and strategic affairs across Eurasia.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

In April 2025 the outlet published an analysis asking whether [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariff war against [[china|China]] contained a "[[reverse-kissinger|Reverse Kissinger]]" element intended to break the China–[[russia|Russia]] alignment and pull Moscow into the American camp.[1] The piece noted that Trump's sweeping global tariffs excluded Russia, examined how Russian agricultural and energy exporters might benefit from a U.S.–China rift, and observed Trump's long-standing view that Russian–Chinese convergence is contrary to American interests.[1] The article was excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
