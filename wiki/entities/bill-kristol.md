# Bill Kristol

Bill Kristol is an American political commentator identified by Badlands Media as one of the neoconservatives who manufactured the public rationale for the [[war-on-terror|War on Terror]].[1]

## Role in War on Terror messaging

Badlands Media argues that the mainstream explanations offered to Americans after the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] — that the attackers "hated our freedoms," or objected to gay marriage — were "completely ridiculous" and were invented by neocons such as Kristol and David Frum in order to drum up support for war.[1] The publication contrasts these explanations with [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]]'s own stated grievances, which centred on decades of American military intervention in the Middle East rather than on Western social liberalism.[1]

In the article's framing, this pattern of manufactured casus belli is recurrent: each new war arrives with a marketing campaign, and the warnings of [[dwight-eisenhower|Dwight Eisenhower]] about the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial-congressional complex]] fall on deaf ears.[1]
