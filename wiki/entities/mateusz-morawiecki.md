# Mateusz Morawiecki

**Mateusz Morawiecki** is a Polish politician who served as prime minister of [[poland|Poland]].[1] In early August 2023 he said that there were at least 4,000 fighters from Russia's [[wagner-group|Wagner]] private military company present in neighboring [[belarus|Belarus]], following the group's relocation there after its short-lived June mutiny in Russia.[1]

Morawiecki warned against "provocations" and "sabotage actions" from Belarus by the relocated Wagner fighters.[1] His caution came days after two Belarusian helicopters breached Polish airspace, heightening jitters in the region, and preceded Warsaw's decision to reinforce the Belarusian border with an additional 2,000 troops announced by deputy interior minister [[maciej-wasik|Maciej Wasik]].[1]

## Announcement ending arms supplies to Ukraine (2023)

As Polish prime minister, Mateusz Morawiecki announced on September 20, 2023 that Poland would no longer arm Ukraine in order to focus on its own defense, a statement reported by AFP and issued only hours after Warsaw summoned Ukraine's ambassador over the dispute over blocked grain exports.[2] The declaration marked a dramatic reversal for a government that had been Kiev's most vocal backer since the beginning of the Russian invasion, and came against the backdrop of Polish parliamentary elections weeks away and mounting anger among Polish farmers over cheap Ukrainian wheat imports.[2]
