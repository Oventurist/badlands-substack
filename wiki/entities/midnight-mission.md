# Midnight Mission

The Midnight Mission is a long-established homeless services organization located in [[skid-row|Skid Row]], downtown [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]].

## Role in the 2026 voter registration allegations

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief cites a review of voter registration records that linked more than 7,600 registered voters to homeless shelters and social service organizations in Los Angeles, of which approximately 1,160 registrations were connected to the Midnight Mission's address. The Brief presents these clustered registrations as circumstantial context for the federal probe into alleged paid registrations ahead of the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|2026 Los Angeles mayoral election]]. The article does not allege wrongdoing by the organization itself; it treats the address-clustering as an indicator warranting scrutiny.

A separate homeless services drop-in center in Venice was reported to have 185 registered voters associated with its address; that facility had previously received a $600,000 taxpayer-funded grant while Councilmember [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]] chaired the [[los-angeles-city-council|Los Angeles City Council]]'s Housing and Homelessness Committee.
