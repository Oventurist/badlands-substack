# Midnight Mission

The Midnight Mission is a homeless services organization located in the Skid Row district of downtown Los Angeles.[1]

## Voter registration records

Reporting cited during the [[skid-row-voter-fraud-investigation|Skid Row voter fraud investigation]] found that more than 7,600 registered voters were linked to addresses of homeless shelters and social service organizations in Los Angeles, including approximately 1,160 registrations connected to the Midnight Mission's address.[1] The concentration of registrations at shelter addresses was a central element of the reporting that accompanied the federal operation on Skid Row and the associated allegations of paid registration activity.[1]
