# Ivor Caplin

**Ivor Caplin** is a former British Labour Party politician who served as Member of Parliament for Hove from 1997 to 2005 and held office as a defence minister.[1]

## Arrest

On Saturday, January 11, 2025, Caplin, then 66, was arrested in Brighton following a sting operation conducted by online "paedophile hunters" who broadcast the encounter on a Facebook live stream.[1] Videos circulated on social media showed him in handcuffs, wearing a bright red hat and black top, flanked by two officers.[1] A Sussex Police spokesman confirmed that a local 66-year-old man had been detained on suspicion of engaging in sexual communication with a child and remained in custody.[1]
