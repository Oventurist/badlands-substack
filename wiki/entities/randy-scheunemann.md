# Randy Scheunemann

Randy Scheunemann is a Republican foreign policy strategist described by Badlands Media as a neoconservative operative associated with the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] (IRI).[1] The article lists him among the figures who have featured on IRI's board, alongside Reagan and Bush aide Richard S. Williamson, while noting that sitting senators are the institute's most active operatives.[1]

Scheunemann appears in the article's account of the [[tea-party-movement|Tea Party]]'s transformation after 2009. It argues that IRI heavyweights such as Scheunemann and [[john-mccain|John McCain]] — men experienced at steering "people power" movements abroad — saw an opening in the movement's grassroots energy and turned the institute's toolkit inward, running training sessions for Republican candidates and issuing strategy briefs for activists funded by [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] money.[1] By 2012, in the article's telling, the movement's anti-war, small-government spine had been swapped for neoconservative priorities including endless wars, surveillance and corporate tax cuts.[1]
