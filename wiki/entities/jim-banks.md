# Jim Banks

Jim Banks is a Republican member of the [[us-house-of-representatives|US House of Representatives]] from Indiana who in April 2024 introduced legislation to strip federal funding from [[npr|National Public Radio]].[1]

## Defund NPR Act

On Friday, April 19, 2024, Banks introduced a bill titled the [[defund-npr-act|Defund NPR Act]], a copy of which was first obtained by the Daily Caller.[1] The measure would cut off any federal funds to NPR.[1]

The bill followed a public controversy at the broadcaster. NPR editor [[uri-berliner|Uri Berliner]] had published an essay alleging deep political bias at the outlet, including the claim that its Washington, D.C. bureau employed 87 registered Democrats and zero registered Republicans; Berliner resigned after NPR suspended him over the piece.[1] Scrutiny also fell on NPR's new chief executive [[katherine-maher|Katherine Maher]], whose past statements resurfaced in the same period.[1]
