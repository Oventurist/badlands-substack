# Niki (political party)

Niki is a right-wing Greek political party led by [[dimitris-natsios|Dimitris Natsios]].[1]

## Role in the Netflix Alexander dispute

In February 2024 the party, through its president, became one of the loudest domestic critics of the [[netflix|Netflix]] series "The Making of a God," which depicted [[alexander-the-great|Alexander the Great]] as homosexual.[1] Natsios called the series "deplorable, unacceptable and unhistorical" and pressed Culture Minister [[lina-mendoni|Lina Mendoni]] on whether the Greek state would act against the streaming service, which she said would be unconstitutional.[1] The episode was carried in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of February 23, 2024.[1]
