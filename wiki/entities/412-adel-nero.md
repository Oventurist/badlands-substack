# 412 Adel Nero

412 Adel Nero is a host in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective.[1]

## Badlands Media role

He co-hosts [[taking-it-back|Taking It Back]] with [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]] and [[frankie-val|Frankie Val]], airing Thursdays at 4pm ET as one of the new programs announced in the January 16–22, 2023 Badlands schedule.[1] The show discusses current events from the perspective of the America First movement and its effort to take back the Republic from the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
