# What We Missed with Chris Paul

What We Missed with Chris Paul is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Tuesdays at 9pm ET in the January 2023 schedule.[1] It is hosted by [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], described by the network as a former Hollywood insider, who walks viewers through recent events in a style the schedule characterizes as taking a sledgehammer to the mainstream media paradigm.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed What We Missed with Chris Paul on Tuesdays at 9pm ET, with the former Hollywood insider reviewing recent events and taking "a sledgehammer to the Mainstream Media paradigm."[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed What We Missed with Chris Paul in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
