# Gershon Hacohen

Maj.-Gen. (Res.) Gershon Hacohen is a former Commander of the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] General Staff Corps and a frequent Israeli commentator on military affairs.[1]

## Statements on a Lebanon ground operation

On Monday, September 23, 2024, Hacohen said that "it is entirely possible that the IDF will need to enter Lebanon on the ground," speaking against the backdrop of Israeli preemptive strikes on targets deep inside [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and concern over the response of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1] With northern Israel on high alert, he speculated about the direction of Israeli operations and the possibility of a ground maneuver.[1]

Speaking to Israeli radio station 103FM, Hacohen argued that returning evacuated northern residents to their homes was non-negotiable: "There is no choice; we must, by any means, return the evacuated residents to their homes."[1] He said the state had struggled to achieve this amid continual fighting with Hezbollah and that Israel had realized its prior actions could not bring residents home, and so must "change tack."[1]

## Assessment of the United States

Hacohen characterized Washington's posture as restraining. He said that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was "still trying to make all sides de-escalate," adding that "the Americans are afraid of a conflict that could destabilize the region and lead to Iranian intervention. They want us to show restraint. While American interests are respected, there whole of Israel is expecting a solution."[1]
