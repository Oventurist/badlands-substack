# John Martinis

John Martinis is an American experimental physicist known for his work on superconducting quantum computing. He led Google's quantum hardware effort during the period in which the company announced a quantum computational milestone, and has continued research and entrepreneurial work in the field, including through startup ventures and academic appointments.

Martinis was named in March 2026 among the appointees to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], as reported in the March 26 [[badlands-brief-143|Badlands Brief]]. His inclusion reflects the council's stated remit covering frontier research priorities alongside artificial intelligence and national security technology.
