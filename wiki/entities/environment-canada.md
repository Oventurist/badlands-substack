# Environment Canada

Environment Canada is the Canadian federal environmental agency whose creation Badlands Media contributor Matthew Ehret attributes directly to the network around the Canadian Club of Rome, founded in 1970 under Prime Minister [[pierre-trudeau|Pierre Elliott Trudeau]].[1]

According to Ehret, the agency applied systems analysis in order to transform what had been a policy of water and energy development centred around a national mission into a policy of "ecosystems management."[1] This shift formed part of a broader wave of cybernetic restructuring of the Canadian federal government undertaken after Trudeau took office, aimed at reorganizing governance around a logic of "adapting to limits."[1]

Ehret links the agency's founding assumptions to the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]] framework — financed by David Rockefeller and [[prince-bernhard|Prince Bernhard]] and brought into the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] in 1973 — which posited a strict dualism between a changing civilized humanity and the "unchanging pure equilibrium" of nature.[1]
