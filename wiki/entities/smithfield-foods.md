# Smithfield Foods

Smithfield Foods is a major American pork producer that has been cited in commentary about foreign and corporate acquisition of American agricultural assets.[1]

## Role in farmland and food-supply commentary

In an August 2023 edition of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] carried a segment on remarks by [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] concerning why [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] and Chinese interests were purchasing large quantities of farmland in the United States.[1] In the accompanying commentary, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] pointed to "the case with Smithfield Pork" as an illustration of how an entire segment of the population can be economically displaced when it does not understand the monetary and economic system it inhabits.[1]

Deschamps argued that American farmers, never taught to defend their families, businesses, and communities against predatory policies and practices, effectively "let the fox into the hen house," and framed the episode as part of a broader pattern in which control of land, credit, and markets functions as a mechanism of societal control.[1]
