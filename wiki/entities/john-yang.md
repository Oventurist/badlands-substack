# John Yang

John Yang is an American broadcast journalist who anchored PBS News Weekend, the weekend edition of PBS NewsHour on [[pbs|PBS]].[1]

## Final broadcast and departure

Yang anchored the program's final live broadcast on January 11, 2026, twelve years after PBS News Weekend launched in 2013.[1] During that episode he announced that PBS had canceled the program "at least for the foreseeable future," attributing the decision to the loss of federal funding for public media, and said he would depart the network at the end of January.[1]

The cancellation followed a $1.1 billion reduction in federal appropriations for the [[corporation-for-public-broadcasting|Corporation for Public Broadcasting]] enacted by the Republican-controlled [[congress|Congress]] in 2025, a response to [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s campaign to defund PBS and [[npr|NPR]] over allegations of liberal bias.[1]
