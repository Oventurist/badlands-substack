# Defending Digital Campaigns

**Defending Digital Campaigns** is a nonprofit organization described in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as a "non-aligned" body that receives funding from Google, Facebook, [[microsoft|Microsoft]] and other entities, and whose ostensible objective is to enhance the security of campaign data.[1]

## Board composition and criticism

In his January 1, 2024 essay, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] questioned the organization's claimed neutrality by pointing to its board of directors, which he said comprised former Obama administration and then-current Department of Homeland Security officials, alongside former Romney and Hillary Clinton campaign managers Matt Rhoades and [[robby-mook|Robby Mook]] respectively, and the chairman of DigiDems — an organization financially supported by the Democratic Party — as well as the law firm [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]] of "Russiagate" fame.[1]

DeLarme cited the group as part of a wider apparatus of institutions that adopt the vocabulary of protecting democracy while, in his account, serving a particular partisan and establishment interest, a pattern he also traced through [[microsoft|Microsoft]]'s [[democracy-forward|Democracy Forward]] initiative, the media rating service [[newsguard|NewsGuard]], and the [[protecting-our-democracy-act|Protecting Our Democracy Act]].[1]
