# Fernwood Foundation

The Fernwood Foundation is the family charity of [[ian-telfer|Ian Telfer]], the Canadian chairman of [[uranium-one|Uranium One]], and the conduit through which the article says millions of undisclosed dollars reached the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] while the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] was under United States government review.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Undisclosed donations

Clinton Foundation disclosures listed Telfer as the only Uranium One official to donate, in an amount described as relatively small — no more than $250,000, given in 2007, before talk of a Rosatom deal began.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article treats that accounting as false.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Citing the New York Times, it reports that a review of tax records in Canada, where the Fernwood Foundation is registered, shows Telfer donated millions of dollars more during and after the period when the foreign investment committee was reviewing his deal with the Russians.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The itemized Fernwood donations were $1 million reported in 2009 — the year Telfer's company appealed to the American Embassy for help keeping its mines in [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]]; $250,000 in 2010, the year the Russians sought majority control; $600,000 in 2011; and $500,000 in 2012.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

These undisclosed gifts came in addition to reported contributions of between $1.3 million and $5.6 million from a constellation of people tied to Uranium One or [[urasia-energy|UrAsia Energy]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Significance

Because [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] had struck an agreement with the Obama White House to publicly identify all Clinton Foundation donors upon becoming Secretary of State, the article argues the Fernwood channel represents a deliberate evasion of that commitment and amounts to the same offense — tax evasion — for which [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]] was indicted and later pardoned by [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
