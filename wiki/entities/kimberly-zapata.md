# Kimberly Zapata

Kimberly Zapata is a former deputy director of the [[milwaukee-election-commission|City of Milwaukee Election Commission]] in Wisconsin who was convicted in 2024 in a scheme involving fraudulently requested military ballots.[1]

## The 2022 military ballot scheme

In November 2022, Wisconsin state Rep. [[janel-brandtjen|Janel Brandtjen]], then chair of the Assembly Committee on Campaigns and Elections, issued an election fraud alert after receiving three authentic military ballots at her home addressed to "Holly," a woman who had never lived there.[1] Brandtjen believed this was part of a scheme to steal votes in Wisconsin.[1]

Later that week, Wisconsin radio host Dan O'Donnell broke the news that Zapata had been fired for committing election fraud.[1]

## Conviction (March 2024)

On Wednesday, March 20, 2024, Zapata was found guilty on all counts against her for illegally requesting military ballots.[1]

## Coverage context

[[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], which reported the verdict, tied the case to its broader reporting on an alleged 2020 military-ballot fraud pattern across battleground states, claiming military ballots went almost entirely to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and asserting evidence of the same in Michigan, Arizona, and Georgia.[1]
