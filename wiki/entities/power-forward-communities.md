# Power Forward Communities

Power Forward Communities is a nonprofit coalition that received a $2 billion award from the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] for residential decarbonization projects, funded through the [[inflation-reduction-act|Inflation Reduction Act]]'s green energy programs.

## Controversy

According to the June 3, 2026 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] Brief, the organization became a focal point of EPA Administrator [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]]'s criminal referrals to the agency's inspector general and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]. Zeldin's office highlighted that Power Forward Communities — described as tied to [[stacey-abrams|Stacey Abrams]] — had reported only $100 in revenue in the year prior to receiving the $2 billion grant, a disparity cited as evidence that the award had gone to a thinly capitalized "pass-through entity" rather than an organization with the operational capacity to deploy the funds.

The $2 billion grant was among roughly $29 billion in green energy awards that Zeldin canceled or paused, alongside the $20 billion routed through the [[greenhouse-gas-reduction-fund|Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund]]. Zeldin characterized the broader 2024 distribution as "blatant waste and abuse" that "feels criminal" and centered on self-dealing among former Obama–Biden officials and politically connected recipients.
