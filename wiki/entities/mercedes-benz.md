# Mercedes-Benz

Mercedes-Benz is a German luxury vehicle manufacturer based in Stuttgart and one of the pillars of the country's automotive industry. It is named in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] as one of several German carmakers announcing major cost-cutting measures during 2026.

According to the Brief, Mercedes-Benz joined [[volkswagen|Volkswagen]], [[porsche|Porsche]] and [[bmw|BMW]] in restructuring as the sector faced declining demand, weaker sales in [[china|China]], the shift to electric vehicles, elevated energy costs and international trade pressures — conditions the [[federation-of-german-industries|Federation of German Industries]] described as a competitiveness crisis for [[germany|Germany]] as a whole.
