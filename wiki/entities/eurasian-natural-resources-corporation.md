# Eurasian Natural Resources Corporation

The Eurasian Natural Resources Corporation (ENRC) is a London-listed mining company that became entangled in the corruption controversies surrounding Israeli billionaire [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] and his operations in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

## Exit from the Congo copper deal

A 2012 article in The Guardian, "ENRC ends Congo mining deal with controversial billionaire Dan Gertler," reported that after multiple institutions opened investigations into Gertler and his African mining operations, ENRC spent over $550 million to buy its way out of a Congo copper-mining contract with him.[1] The same reporting noted accusations that Gertler had made most of his $2.5 billion fortune from "looting the Congo at the expense of its people."[1]
