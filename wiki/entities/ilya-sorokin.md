# Ilya Sorokin

Ilya Sorokin is a Russian goaltender for the New York Islanders of the National Hockey League.[1]

## Role in the 2025 NHL scoring record

On April 6, 2025, at UBS Arena in New York, Sorokin conceded the goal by fellow Russian [[alexander-ovechkin|Alexander Ovechkin]] of the [[washington-capitals|Washington Capitals]] that became the 895th of Ovechkin's career and set the all-time NHL record previously held by [[wayne-gretzky|Wayne Gretzky]].[1] The moment was celebrated by both American and Russian fans in the arena.[1]
