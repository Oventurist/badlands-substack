# Muslim Students Association

The Muslim Students Association (MSA) is a campus organization with, according to the sources cited in the Badlands corpus, hundreds of chapters at universities across the United States and Canada.

## In the Badlands corpus

America for Sale   Part 1 describes the MSA, quoting PJMedia, as "the foundation of this infrastructure" of the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]'s "civilization/stealth jihad" — a network intended to create enclaves in the physical community, on campus, and in government institutions "in which sharia is honored as basic law." The article calls the MSA "the key to the civilization/stealth jihad" and its chapters "breeding grounds for not just terrorists, but also infiltrators within our own government."

It emphasizes that [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] served on the executive board of the MSA chapter at [[george-washington-university|George Washington University]], heading its Social Committee, during the same years she interned for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and served as assistant editor of the *[[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]]*. The article traces a radical lineage through MSA chapters: Anwar al Awlaki was spiritual guide to the GWU chapter in 2001 and had earlier led the Colorado State University chapter, while in the 1980s Wael Jalaidan ran the University of Arizona chapter before helping [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] found al-Qaeda.
