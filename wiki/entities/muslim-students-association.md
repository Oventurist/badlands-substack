# Muslim Students Association

The Muslim Students Association (MSA) is described in the Badlands account as the foundation of the Saudi-constructed, [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]-conducted Islamist infrastructure in the West, with hundreds of chapters on campuses across the United States and Canada.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) It is characterized not as a routine cultural fraternity but as the key instrument of the "civilization/stealth jihad" strategy, enabling Muslims to create enclaves — in the physical community, on campus, and in major social institutions including government — where sharia is honored as basic law and members are shielded from pressure to assimilate.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Abedin and George Washington University

In the mid-to-late 1990s, while she was an intern at the Clinton White House and assistant editor at the [[journal-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs]], [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] served on the executive board of the MSA chapter at [[george-washington-university|George Washington University]], heading its Social Committee.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) That chapter had what the article calls a radical history: in 2001 its spiritual guide was [[anwar-al-awlaki|Anwar al-Awlaki]], the Al-Qaeda operative then ministering to several of the eventual 9/11 hijackers.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Jihadist alumni

Awlaki had himself led the MSA chapter at Colorado State University in the early 1990s, and researcher Patrick Poole has documented that he is far from the only jihadist to hone a supremacist ideology within the MSA.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) In the 1980s Wael Jalaidan ran the MSA at the University of Arizona before helping [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] found [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]]; he also partnered with the Abedins' patron [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]] to establish the [[rabita-trust|Rabita Trust]], formally designated a terrorist organization under U.S. law.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The Badlands narrative concludes that these associations make campus MSA chapters breeding grounds both for terrorists and for infiltrators of the U.S. government.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
