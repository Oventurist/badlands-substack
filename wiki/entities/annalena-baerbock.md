# Annalena Baerbock

Annalena Baerbock is the German Foreign Minister who, in January 2023, publicly stated that Western allies were "fighting a war against Russia" — remarks that Badlands Media and other outlets treated as an inadvertent admission of direct belligerency by [[germany|Germany]] and its partners in the conflict in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## The PACE remarks

Baerbock made the statement during a debate at the Parliamentary Assembly of the [[council-of-europe|Council of Europe]] (PACE) amid discussions over sending Leopard 2 tanks to Ukraine.[1] She introduced the comments by saying, "And therefore I've said already in the last days – yes, we have to do more to defend Ukraine. Yes, we have to do more also on tanks," before asserting: "But the most important and the crucial part is that we do it together and that we do not do the blame game in Europe, because we are fighting a war against Russia and not against each other."[1]

## Reaction

Reporting relayed by Badlands noted that Baerbock's words were largely ignored in mainstream media, while a number of commentators on social media argued with alarm that the German foreign minister had essentially declared war on Russia.[1] The remarks contradicted the long-standing position of other German officials, who had emphasized that their country was not a party to the conflict out of fear of uncontrollable escalation.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the episode to argue that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s war had exposed the core mechanisms of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] system more than almost any other recent deployment, describing Ukraine as "a Globalist proxy state" and asserting that [[nato|NATO]] countries such as Germany were growing nervous that the arrangement was ending.[1]
