# Massachusetts Registry of Motor Vehicles

The Registry of Motor Vehicles (RMV) is the Massachusetts state agency responsible for issuing driver's licenses, learner's permits and vehicle registrations.[1] It became a focus of national attention in July 2023 as the agency charged with implementing the state's new law extending driving privileges to persons in the country illegally.[1]

## Role in the 2023 license expansion

RMV data, published by Chris Van Buskirk of the [[boston-herald|Boston Herald]], showed that approximately 100,000 requests for learner's permit appointments were placed after the [[massachusetts-drivers-licenses-for-illegal-aliens|new licensing law]] took effect on July 1, 2023.[1] The agency hired 52 additional employees to handle the resulting demand.[1] State officials projected that close to 300,000 illegal aliens in Massachusetts would apply for licenses in the law's first four years, with implementation expected to cost taxpayers at least $28 million.[1] Critics in the Truth Community pointed to the RMV's expanded workload as an example of how new statutes generate additional bureaucracy and administrative complexity.[1]
