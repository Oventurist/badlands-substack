# National Rifle Association

The National Rifle Association (NRA) is the American gun-rights organization that endorsed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at its annual meeting in Texas in May 2024.[1]

Trump followed the endorsement with a speech to thousands of NRA members stoking fears that the government under [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was "coming for your guns," alongside a monologue set to dramatic music that The Daily Beast described as resembling a song favored by [[qanon|QAnon]] adherents.[1] In the address Trump hailed the reversal of *Roe v. Wade* as an "amazing thing," compared himself to Al Capone, and insisted that "genius" runs in his bloodline.[1]

As the speech wound down, sentimental instrumental music began to play and Trump delivered a grim message that America is "a failing nation": "Now we are a nation in decline. We are a failing nation. We are a nation that has the highest inflation in 58 years, where banks are collapsing, and interest rates are skyrocketing."[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] focused on one aside from the NRA speech — "You know there is a genius to what I'm doing. You do know that? I go from here to there, and it all comes back into a beautiful picture." — treating it as direct acknowledgement that Trump's public speaking style is highly intentional.[1]
