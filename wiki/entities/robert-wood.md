# Robert Wood

Robert Wood is an American diplomat who served as a United States envoy to the United Nations, representing Washington in sessions of the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] during the later years of the Ukraine war.[1]

## Warning to North Korea (October 2024)

Wood drew international attention in late October 2024 when he responded to reports that North Korean personnel had been deployed to Russia for possible use against Ukrainian forces. Speaking before the UN Security Council, he warned bluntly that if Pyongyang's forces "enter Ukraine in support of Russia, they will surely return in body bags."[1] He added that he "would advise Chairman Kim [Jong-un] to think twice about engaging in such reckless and dangerous behaviour."[1]

His remarks paralleled statements by US defence secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]], who called on North Korea to withdraw its troops from Russia and said such forces would be treated as co-belligerents, and by the [[white-house|White House]], which said Pyongyang's soldiers would become "legitimate military targets" if they fought against Ukraine.[1] Washington's estimate at the time was that roughly 10,000 North Korean troops had been deployed.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary treated Wood's statement as an example of what its writers described as the performative belligerence of Western officialdom, arguing that publicly advertising an eagerness to kill is a marker of weakness rather than strength, and contrasting it with what the outlet characterized as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s more restrained but credible willingness to use force.[1] The same commentary framed the moment within a broader thesis that the world is coalescing not against a nation but against what the writer called an entrenched transnational criminal syndicate.[1]
