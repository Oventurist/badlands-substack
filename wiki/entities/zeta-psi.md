# Zeta Psi

**Zeta Psi** is a fraternity named in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as part of the [[university-of-pennsylvania|University of Pennsylvania]]'s alleged financial-enforcement pipeline.[1]

The article states that Penn "trains financial enforcement operatives through Zeta Psi and the Wharton Inner Circle," producing everything "from ESG auditors to WEF policy designers" and forging what it calls the new technocracy.[1] Together with the [[wharton-school|Wharton School]], Zeta Psi is described as a staging ground for [[blackrock|BlackRock]]-trained executives later embedded in ESG compliance, central bank digital currency strategy and [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] think tanks within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
