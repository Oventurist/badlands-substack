# Leon County Sheriff's Office

The Leon County Sheriff's Office is the county law enforcement agency serving Leon County, Florida, which includes Tallahassee.[1]

## Connection to the 2025 FSU shooting

The agency drew national attention after the April 17, 2025 shooting at [[florida-state-university|Florida State University]], in which two people were killed and six injured.[1] Police said the suspected shooter, 20-year-old [[phoenix-ikner|Phoenix Ikner]], used a weapon belonging to his mother, Jessica Ikner, a deputy with the Leon County Sheriff's Office.[1] Authorities said she had served as an officer for over 18 years and had done a "tremendous job" in her position.[1]
