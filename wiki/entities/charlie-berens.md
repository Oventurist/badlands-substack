# Charlie Berens

Charlie Berens is an internet-famous American comedian associated with Wisconsin regional humor and a staunch public critic of artificial intelligence.[1]

In 2026 Berens's "impassioned coverage" was credited with helping the largely social-media-organized protest campaign against the [[port-washington|Port Washington]] AI data center go viral.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited his involvement as evidence that the anti-data-center movement was influencer-driven rather than organic, and wondered aloud whether "the internet-famous comedian knows or cares" that the affected towns appeared abandoned and in need of industry.[1]
