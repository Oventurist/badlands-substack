# Tides Foundation

The Tides Foundation is a left-wing grantmaking organization that functions as a pass-through and fiscal sponsor for a wide range of progressive projects and nonprofits.[1]

## Role in the USAID funding controversy

During the first month of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term, scrutiny of [[usaid|USAID]] produced reporting that the agency had been a significant funder of nongovernmental organizations with contentious domestic political agendas, including [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] and organizations linked to the George Floyd riots, with money routed through the Tides Foundation and its associated Tides Center.[1] Critics argued that the implications were staggering: taxpayer money appropriated for international development was instead alleged to be fueling domestic political unrest.[1]

The Tides structure — in which a single grantmaking intermediary sponsors and disburses to numerous downstream projects — is central to the criticism, because it obscures the line between the original public funding source and the ultimate recipient.[1]
