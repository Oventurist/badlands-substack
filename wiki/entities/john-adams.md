# John Adams

John Adams was an American founder and the second president of the United States, invoked in Badlands commentary as the archetypal exponent of the rule of law.[1]

## Invocation in rule-of-law commentary

Writing about the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s decision clearing the way for dismissal of [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s [[contempt-of-congress|contempt of Congress]] case, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] said she could not recall the exact words Adams used about the rule of law, but that "the spirit of John Adams" animated the question she posed: do we have rule of law or don't we?[1] She paired the invocation with a caveat — that the rule-of-law framing was itself, in her reading, an operation designed to stifle emerging ideas of individual liberty in their infancy — while still holding that prosecution should never be political.[1]
