# Borderline Dance Team

The Borderline Dance Team is a troupe of line-dancing women that was effectively booted out of a Seattle dance contest in April 2024 after some members of the audience complained that they had been "triggered" by the performers' stars-and-stripes outfits.[1]

## Ejection from the 2024 contest

The team was scheduled to take part in a contest organized by the [[rain-country-dance-association|Rain Country Dance Association]] on the preceding Saturday, but was told before the event that its star-spangled suits, seen in promotional material, "were offensive to some of the convention goers." In a social media post, the team said it had been informed that the complainants were "associated with the situation in Palestine and the Trans community" and that they felt "triggered and unsafe" because of the outfits.[1]

The story was reported by Modernity News and circulated widely, generating substantial public backlash against the organizers, who subsequently reported online hostility and a threat of violence deemed credible by Seattle police.[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the incident as emblematic of activist-left inconsistency, noting the contrast with the celebrated US-flag costuming in the Pitch Perfect film franchise.[1]
