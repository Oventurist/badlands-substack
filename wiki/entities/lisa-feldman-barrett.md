# Lisa Feldman Barrett

**Dr. Lisa Feldman Barrett** is a neuroscientist and the author of *How Emotions Are Made*, cited in the March 29, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "Walking the Path: From Awakening to Integration" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay quotes her for the proposition that "Your brain doesn't just react to the world, it predicts it, shaped by past experiences."[1] The author uses the line to explain the physical and perceptual signs of early [[spiritual-awakening|awakening]] — heightened sensitivity to light, sound and food, emotional swings, and a sense that reality is recalibrating — as the mind breaking free of conditioned patterns.[1] On this reading, awakening begins precisely when the brain's habitual predictions falter, so that the reader questions not only the world but the trained manner of seeing it.[1]

Barrett's predictive account is placed at the head of the article's "Grounding the Shift" section, immediately before quotations from Rick Doblin, and functions as the essay's bridge from felt symptom to mechanism: [[psychological-integration|integration]] is said to start in those cracks, in the choice to pause and breathe and let the disturbance settle into a workable rhythm.[1]
