# Roger Stone

Roger Stone is a longtime American political consultant and self-described "political icon" whose predictions about presidential nominating contests have periodically drawn attention within the alternative-media ecosystem.[1]

## 2024 Democratic nomination prediction

Speaking at the Turning Point Action Conference in West Palm Beach, Florida, in July 2023, Stone predicted that incumbent president [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would not be the Democratic nominee for the 2024 general election, and that former first lady [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]] would instead secure the nomination.[1] Stone further claimed that the vice-presidential slot on the ticket would effectively be "bought."[1] He publicized the prediction on social media after making it from the conference stage, and The Western Journal characterized the claim as bold but "not unheard of."[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated Stone's prediction as plausible, arguing that Obama would be the Democratic candidate with the most political capital and the fewest public scandals, while also being the opponent that populist and pro-Trump audiences would most welcome given accumulated hostility toward what the commentary called the Obama–Clinton–Biden establishment.[1]

## Reception

The Western Journal described Stone as a "savvy political operator," reflecting his continued status as a widely quoted, if polarizing, figure in Republican politics.[1]
