# Roger Stone

**Roger Stone** is a longtime American political operative and advisor to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], known for his "ratfucking" style of campaign politics, his conviction and pardon in the Russia investigation, and his continued presence in conservative media as a commentator on the [[russiagate|Russiagate]] accountability movement.

## The steak dinner bet

In the Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026, contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reports that [[john-solomon|John Solomon]] is "doubling down" and has bet Stone a steak dinner on indictments landing by June 20, 2026 — the end of spring — in connection with the [[rico-grande|RICO Grande]] prosecution narrative. The wager is cited as evidence that the figures driving the accountability storyline expect the Justice phase to arrive quickly, and it is celebrated in the Brief with the Deadwood-inflected refrain "Indictments by Deadwood? Let's go!"

## Role in the corpus

Stone functions in the Badlands narrative as one of the veteran figures of the original Russia-collusion fight — a man who was himself indicted and convicted in the investigation the corpus regards as a hoax, and whose willingness to bet on new indictments is read as a signal of confidence in the reversal of fortunes.
