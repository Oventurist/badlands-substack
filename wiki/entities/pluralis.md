# Pluralis

Pluralis is a Dutch media-investment company backed by the Soros Economic Development Fund, part of the philanthropic network of [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1]

In 2021 Pluralis acquired a minority stake in the Polish media group [[gremi-media|Gremi Media]] — publisher of the daily Rzeczpospolita and the financial daily Parkiet — with an option to purchase additional shares.[1] In August 2023 it exercised that option, buying a large proportion of owner Grzegorz Hajdarowicz's holding and raising its stake to 57 percent, which gave it control of the company.[1] [[remix-news|Remix News]] reported that the takeover raised concerns over far-left media bias in Poland given the politics of the fund's billionaire backer.[1]
