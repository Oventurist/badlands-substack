# Mark Passio

Mark Passio is a former satanist priest turned whistleblower and occult researcher whose testimony forms the interpretive backbone of the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult."[1] He is the source of the claim that a recurring forty-day "season of sacrifice" governs the timing of many notable acts of mass violence.[1]

## Recruitment into the Church of Satan

Circa 1995, as a teenager, Passio was recruited into the [[church-of-satan|Church of Satan]] after being noticed for his ability to write ideologically accurate satanic song lyrics.[1] According to the account, [[anton-lavey|Anton LaVey]] personally appointed Passio to the position of high priest and granted him authority over a Grotto in the Philadelphia area, where he was tasked with attending and overseeing midnight meetings of the group.[1]

Passio reportedly expected to encounter obstinate punk kids like himself; instead he found politicians, lawyers, teachers, policemen, priests and other influential people in attendance.[1] Grotto attendees spoke of an ancient tradition and expressed pride in the ideology of Satanism, which they said bound together a diverse group of the movers and shakers of society.[1] The group claimed to have orchestrated almost every major event of history, reaching back into ancient pre-history, and told Passio that its mind control over the general population was so profound and near-absolute that even a public press conference announcing its identity and agenda would not be believed.[1]

## Chaos sorcery and the dark occult

Disgusted and disturbed by what he encountered, Passio dedicated his life to exposing the group and its agenda.[1] Central to his exposition is the doctrine he calls [[chaos-sorcery|chaos sorcery]] — the intentional destabilization of aspects of society ranging from the economy and religious institutions to the family unit, the sciences and politics.[1] In this framework the [[false-flag-operation|false flag]] is only one among many cultural-warfare weapons developed by what Passio terms the dark occult groups of the world, a category he extends to the so-called Illuminati, globalists and allied factions pursuing a mutually agreed agenda of total control over the human population.[1]

## The season of sacrifice

Passio is credited in the article with defining the forty-day [[season-of-sacrifice|season of sacrifice]] running from approximately March 19th to May 1st, derived by combining the Minor Zodiacal Cross point of the spring equinox with the Grand Cross point of Walpurgisnacht.[1] The article's author, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], reproduces an excerpt from his own 2015 summary of a Passio presentation laying out the [[astrotheology|astrotheological]] basis of false flags and the season.[1] Passio is cited alongside Santos Bonacci as a researcher concluding that an astrotheological basis for shaping world events remains active in the present day.[1] A 2015 appearance at the "De-Mystifying the Occult" event in Manchester, Connecticut, and a subsequent three-hour-plus presentation from 2016 are referenced as sources for his testimony.[1]

## The masses as "the walking dead"

Badlands Media's [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series cites Passio for the claim that the higher-ups in the [[church-of-satan|Church of Satan]] view the general population as the *walking dead*.[2] On this reading the term "consumers" is a form of occult mockery: to the initiated the masses are zombies who wander aimlessly about the world consuming as they go, an idea the series treats as approximated by the phrase "useless eaters."[2] [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] builds on this to propose a three-tier symbolic hierarchy in which vampires represent the cult's royalty, werewolves its mercenaries and zombies everyone else — as though an infernal hierarchy had been instantiated in this realm.[2]
