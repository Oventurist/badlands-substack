# Joe Gebbia

**Joe Gebbia** is an American entrepreneur and a co-founder of Airbnb who, during the second term of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], took on a role in the federal government's technology and design reform efforts.[1]

## National Design Studio

Gebbia heads the [[national-design-studio|National Design Studio]], a body created through an executive order Trump signed in August 2025.[1] Trump's order directed Gebbia to beautify government websites — a mandate centered on the visual design and user experience of federal digital services.[1]

## Connection to DOGE

Gebbia's role gained wider notice in November 2025, when [[reuters|Reuters]] reported that the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] had disbanded with eight months left on its charter, and that at least two prominent DOGE employees had moved into the National Design Studio.[1] The Studio thus became one of the successor destinations for staff from the centralized DOGE effort, alongside the [[office-of-personnel-management|Office of Personnel Management]], which absorbed many of DOGE's functions.[1]
