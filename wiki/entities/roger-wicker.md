# Roger Wicker

Roger Wicker is a Republican United States Senator from Mississippi and a leading Republican voice on defense policy and the annual defense authorization process.[1]

## Opposition to female draft registration, 2024

In June 2024, Senate Democrats added language to the annual defense authorization bill requiring women to register for the military draft, prompting a backlash from Republicans and social conservatives and complicating the bill's prospects on the Senate floor before Election Day.[1] Conservatives led by Wicker were described as certain to attempt to strip the provision requiring women to register.[1]

Reporting noted that such an amendment vote could prove politically difficult for Democratic senators in tight reelection races, including [[jon-tester|Jon Tester]] of Montana and [[jacky-rosen|Jacky Rosen]] of Nevada.[1] Republican Senate candidate [[sam-brown|Sam Brown]], an Army veteran running against Rosen, was already campaigning on the issue.[1]

## Push for ATACMS transfers to Ukraine (2023)

In September 2023 Wicker joined Senators [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]], [[susan-collins|Susan Collins]] and [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] in writing to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to urge the transfer of [[atacms|MGM-140 Army Tactical Missile Systems]] to Ukraine, contending that withholding them would "only prolong the war."[2] The letter followed the [[senate-armed-services-committee|Senate Armed Services Committee]]'s unanimous approval for the Department of Defense to send long-range missiles to Kyiv.[2]
