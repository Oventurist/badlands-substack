# Evgeny Popov

Evgeny Popov is a Russian television host associated with Russian state broadcasting, notably the program *60 Minutes* (distinct from the American CBS program of the same name).[1] In April 2023 his commentary on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] drew Western press attention.[1]

## April 2023 commentary on Trump and Ukraine

During an episode of *60 Minutes*, Popov discussed Trump's recent interview with Fox News host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], in which Trump suggested Ukraine "cannot win" the war with Russia and appeared to argue the United States should stop sending military aid to Kyiv.[1] While playing segments of the interview, Popov said the former president had proposed that the United States "surrender" and stop assisting Ukraine's fight against Russia.[1]

Popov said that "according to Trump, America is tired of fighting Russia," and asserted that Western politicians had imposed more sanctions on Russia than on Iran and North Korea combined.[1] He argued they had promised a quick victory and the collapse of Russia's economy, but that instead the U.S. banking system was falling apart and the European economy was sliding into recession.[1] He concluded that "according to Trump, Moscow has already won the economic war," that Republicans understood they would have to make a deal, and that "Donald is laying the groundwork."[1]

## Reception

[[newsweek|Newsweek]] reported the segment under the framing that Russian state television claimed Trump was laying the groundwork for a deal to end the war in Ukraine.[1] Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that Newsweek adopted a mocking tone toward "Russian State TV" while noting that the network was advancing the same talking points as Trump himself, and argued that reading hostile outlets is valuable for mapping the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]].[1]
