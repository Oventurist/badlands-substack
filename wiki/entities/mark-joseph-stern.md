# Mark Joseph Stern

Mark Joseph Stern is a legal correspondent for Slate who covers the U.S. Supreme Court and the federal judiciary. His commentary on the June 27, 2024 decision in [[sec-v-jarkesy|SEC v. Jarkesy]] was reproduced at length in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief, where [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described him as a "SLATE globalist, corporatist, liberal shill" and invited readers to put on "Bicameral glasses" and read Stern's alarm about the erosion of federal agency power as an encouraging sign rather than a warning.[1]

The Brief's argument was that Stern's account of the damage the ruling would inflict on the administrative state amounted to an unintentional confirmation that the "promised neutering of the (Deep) Administrative State is WELL underway," with writers like Stern being among those most affected.[1] The item closed with the aside "Don't threaten us with a good time, Mark."[1]

Stern's inclusion illustrates a recurring Badlands technique of quoting hostile mainstream legal commentary in full and letting the reader invert its framing, rather than rebutting it point by point.[1]
