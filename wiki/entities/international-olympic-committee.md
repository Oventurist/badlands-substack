# International Olympic Committee

The International Olympic Committee is the governing body of the modern Olympic Games, responsible for awarding host cities, setting eligibility rules, and approving the content of ceremonies. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as a compromised institution acting on behalf of external controllers rather than as an independent sporting authority.

## Portrayal in the corpus

In [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s August 2024 essay Are We Being Distracted?, the committee is charged with having staged the [[paris-2024-olympics-opening-ceremony|Paris opening ceremony]] for the purpose of provoking global anger. Carlson writes that the committee "doesn't care about Transgenders or gays; they have been commissioned by their controllers to make people angry," and that transgender participants are "being used to divide, distract and anger the people" on behalf of the [[cabal|Cabal]].

## The eligibility argument

Carlson's central evidence for this claim is the committee's own competition rules. According to the article, the Olympic Committee "essentially banned Transgenders from participating in the Olympics": unless an athlete has fully transitioned by the age of twelve, they may not compete, the committee having determined that anyone transitioning after that age retains an unfair advantage. Carlson treats the coexistence of this restrictive rule with a ceremony celebrating transgender imagery as proof of bad faith — "if the Olympic Committee truly cares about Transgenders, and Transgenders' rights, why don't they allow them to compete in the Olympics?"

He extends the point to the asymmetry he observes in transgender competition generally: athletes transitioning from female to male do not, in his account, compete against men, while athletes transitioning from male to female "tend to dominate" women's fields — a pattern he reads as consistent with an intent to upset rather than to include.
