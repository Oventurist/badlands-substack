# SOR Party

The SOR party was a Euroskeptic Moldovan political party that was banned by the authorities of [[moldova|Moldova]] in 2023.[1]

## Prosecutions arising from SOR financing

Moldovan prosecutors alleged that funds from an organized criminal group were funnelled into the SOR party's campaign coffers.[1] On that basis, [[evgenia-gutsul|Evgenia Gutsul]], the leader of the autonomous region of [[gagauzia|Gagauzia]], was convicted of illegal party financing and sentenced to seven years in prison, plus a 40 million Moldovan lei ($2.3 million) payment to the state.[1] Gutsul was also accused of paying people to attend anti-government protests organized by SOR in 2022.[1]

Svetlana Popan, a former secretary in the SOR Central Office, was sentenced to six years' imprisonment in the same proceeding; both sentences remain subject to appeal.[1] Badlands commentary treated the party's banning and the ensuing prosecutions as an illustration of European Union-aligned governments suppressing electoral opposition.[1]
