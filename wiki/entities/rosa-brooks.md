# Rosa Brooks

Rosa Brooks is a Georgetown University Law Center figure who founded and facilitated the [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]], the election scenario-planning group that conducted war games ahead of the 2020 United States presidential election.[1]

## 2020 election writings

Brooks authored a Washington Post op-ed in advance of the 2020 election titled "What's the worst that could happen? The election will likely spark violence — and a constitutional crisis," in which she described assembling "some of the most accomplished Republicans, Democrats, civil servants, media experts, pollsters and strategists" to imagine responses to a range of election and transition scenarios.[1] The exercises produced findings that "election night" was no longer an accurate concept, that a determined campaign could contest the outcome into January 2021, and that the administrative transition itself might be highly disrupted.[1]

## Oversight scrutiny

In March 2024, Representative Pete Sessions wrote to Brooks on behalf of the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]], opening an investigation into TIP and describing it as an "intensely partisan exercise" intended "to sew dissension and subversion regarding the American election system and the institution of the president of the United States."[1] Sessions noted particular concern that the exercise appeared to have been administered adjacent to the Georgetown University Law Center.[1]
