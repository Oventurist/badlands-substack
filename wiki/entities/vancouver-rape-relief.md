# Vancouver Rape Relief

Vancouver Rape Relief (VRR), located in the province of British Columbia, is Canada's oldest rape crisis shelter and, according to reporting cited by Badlands, the only known single-sex shelter in the country despite Canada's vast size.[1]

## 2019 defunding

In 2019, Vancouver Rape Relief was stripped of its city funding after asserting that it had a right to provide services exclusively to females.[1] Transgender activists successfully lobbied the Vancouver city government to make the shelter's funding contingent on accepting males who identified as transgender.[1]

## Significance in the 2025 Edmonton shelter case

The VRR case was invoked in Badlands commentary on the January 2025 charging of a trans-identified male in Edmonton, Alberta, over the alleged sexual assault of multiple women at a women's shelter.[1] Badlands host [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the defunding of VRR illustrated a Canadian policy environment in which demanding women-only spaces is treated as transphobic, leaving women without safe single-sex refuge.[1]
