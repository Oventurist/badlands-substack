# Jocelyn Benson

Jocelyn Benson is the Secretary of State of Michigan and the defendant in an election integrity lawsuit filed by the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] in March 2024.[1]

## RNC lawsuit

On March 28, 2024, the RNC sued Benson, alleging that she had worked to covertly undercut Michigan's absentee voting safeguards.[1] At issue was a memorandum Benson sent to election officials ahead of the state's presidential primary encouraging them to apply a "presumption of validity" to signatures on absentee ballot requests, which the RNC contended violates the state constitution's signature matching requirement.[1]

"This new rule, however, is directly inconsistent with the plain language of the Michigan Constitution and the Michigan Election Law," the suit stated.[1] The complaint further highlighted a rule Benson promulgated granting election officials broad leeway to account for discrepancies between signatures on return envelopes and signatures on file, which the RNC described as permitting verification based on "mere speculation."[1]
