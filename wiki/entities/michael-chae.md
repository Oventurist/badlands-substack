# Michael Chae

**Michael Chae** is the chief financial officer of [[blackstone|Blackstone]] and, according to the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion," a member of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board.[1]

The article describes him as a senior member of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and a board member at the [[asia-society|Asia Society]] alongside Saudi intelligence chief [[turki-al-faisal|Prince Turki al-Faisal]].[1] He is listed first among the HMC figures the author cites as evidence that the endowment's governance constitutes "a cartel — a financial politburo with global reach" rather than a body of academics, and as a node linking Harvard's capital to private equity, foreign policy circles and foreign intelligence figures within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
