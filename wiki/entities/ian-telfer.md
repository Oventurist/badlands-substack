# Ian Telfer

Ian Telfer is the Canadian businessman who became chairman of [[uranium-one|Uranium One]] and whose undisclosed donations to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] the article treats as decisive evidence that the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] was a purchased approval.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Telfer became chairman after [[frank-giustra|Frank Giustra]]'s UrAsia Energy merged in 2007 with Uranium One, then a South African company, in a $3.5 billion transaction; the combined company was controlled by UrAsia investors including Telfer.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Disclosed and undisclosed giving

The Clinton Foundation was required, to avoid the perception of conflicts of interest and beyond its ban on foreign government donations, to publicly disclose all contributors.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) By those disclosures, the New York Times reported, the only Uranium One official to give was Telfer, and the amount was relatively small — no more than $250,000, given in 2007, before talk of a [[rosatom|Rosatom]] deal began percolating.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article stresses that this donation preceded any discussion of a Russian purchase, and asks whether anyone trusts the Clintons' tax records.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

A review of tax records in Canada, where Telfer has a family charity called the Fernwood Foundation, showed he donated millions of dollars more during and after the critical period when the foreign investment committee was reviewing his deal with the Russians.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) His Fernwood donations included $1 million reported in 2009 — the year his company appealed to the American Embassy for help keeping its mines in [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]] — $250,000 in 2010, the year the Russians sought majority control, $600,000 in 2011 and $500,000 in 2012.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) These undisclosed gifts came in addition to between $1.3 million and $5.6 million in reported contributions from a constellation of people tied to Uranium One or UrAsia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article concludes that Telfer gave millions the Clintons never reported, that this constitutes tax evasion — the same crime for which [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]] was charged and pardoned — and that the pattern undermines [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s claim to have had no role in approving the sale.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Chairman of Uranium One at the time, Telfer is separately described in the article's imagery caption as having made large donations to the Clinton Foundation.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
