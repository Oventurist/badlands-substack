# Eric Olson

Eric Olson is a Colorado attorney who served as lead petitioners' counsel in the 2023 lawsuit seeking to disqualify [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state's 2024 presidential ballot under the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]]'s disqualification clause.[1]

## The livestream interruption

On the third day of the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification trial]], Olson halted the proceeding to inform the court that someone was recording the livestream of the hearing without permission.[1] When Judge Sarah B. Wallace asked whether the person was part of the expanded media coverage arrangement, Olson said they were not, identifying the stream as belonging to "Ashley Epp," whom he described as "a conservative activist in Colorado who has been very involved in efforts urging people to question — and reject — the results of the 2020 election."[1]

The person named was [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] host who writes and broadcasts under that name and goes by Ashe Epp or Ash Epp personally and professionally.[1] She noted that the name Olson used appears only in government records and in the NAACP-led voter intimidation lawsuit filed against her and two co-defendants, and observed that Colorado Secretary of State Jena Griswold — a witness for the plaintiffs in that case — is also the respondent in the ballot disqualification suit.[1] She characterized Olson's statement as a lie to the judge about the nature of the stream, noted that the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] feed carried roughly 8,600 live viewers at the time, and said the incident felt personal and intimidating, concluding that "Eric Olson owes me an apology."[1] Out of caution, the Badlands team ended the livestream, missing Katrina Pierson's testimony before adjournment.[1]

The interruption came immediately after [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s testimony for the Trump defense, during which the exchanges between Patel and petitioners' counsel became openly hostile.[1]
