# Sarah A. Boardman

Sarah A. Boardman is a British-born, Colorado-based portrait artist known for her official portraits displayed in the Colorado State Capitol.[1] She painted the portrait of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that has hung in the Capitol since 2019, during Trump's first term, as well as the Capitol's portrait of President Barack Obama.[1] Her portraits are based on photographs rather than live sittings.[1]

## 2025 controversy

In March 2025 Trump publicly demanded the immediate removal of Boardman's portrait of him, calling it "truly the worst" and "purposefully distorted to a level that" he had "never seen before," while describing her Obama portrait as "wonderful."[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that while the painting demonstrated genuine technique, its lack of contrast between lights and darks made it look flat and beneath the standard expected of a presidential portrait in a state capitol.[1] See [[colorado-capitol-trump-portrait-dispute]].
