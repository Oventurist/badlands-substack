# Solyndra

Solyndra was an American solar panel manufacturer that received $535 million in federal support from the administration of President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] to produce specialized solar panels, and subsequently went out of business.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited Solyndra as the case that first made apparent her contention that the green new deal functions as a money laundering operation rather than an environmental program.[1] She noted that the scandal was quickly memory-holed after the company's collapse.[1]

Solyndra is invoked in the Brief as the direct antecedent of [[crescent-dunes|Crescent Dunes]], another federally supported solar project dating to the Obama administration and likewise characterized as a money-laundering operation — with the added aggravating factor that the [[joe-biden|Biden]] [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] moved on election night 2024 to dismiss the [[qui-tam|qui tam]] lawsuit that sought to recoup taxpayer losses.[1]
