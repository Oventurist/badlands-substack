# Kirill Dmitriev

Kirill Dmitriev is a Russian financier and official described in the Badlands corpus as Russia's Special Envoy to the United States.

In the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026, Dmitriev is reported to have confirmed on X that President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had accepted an invitation to join President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], making Putin the first high-profile invitee publicly identified. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the confirmation as placing Trump and Putin figuratively "shoulder to shoulder at the Gaza Border." The Brief notes in parallel that Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] described the invitation as still under review, with Moscow seeking to "clarify all nuances" with Washington.
