# Kirill Dmitriev

Kirill Dmitriev is a Russian financier and official described in the Badlands corpus as Russia's Special Envoy to the United States.

In the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026, Dmitriev is reported to have confirmed on X that President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had accepted an invitation to join President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], making Putin the first high-profile invitee publicly identified. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the confirmation as placing Trump and Putin figuratively "shoulder to shoulder at the Gaza Border." The Brief notes in parallel that Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] described the invitation as still under review, with Moscow seeking to "clarify all nuances" with Washington.

## The Bering Strait tunnel

Dmitriev is also the principal Russian promoter of the proposed [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait tunnel]] between Chukotka and Alaska. The June 5, 2026 [[badlands-brief-58d|Badlands Brief]] reports his statement that Russia and the United States would sign an agreement that day to continue the tunnel's design phase: "Regarding the tunnel. We will have news tomorrow: we are signing an agreement to continue the tunnel design. The tunnel will be built." He described the project as among the most significant infrastructure initiatives envisioned between the two countries and as a fixed land link between Asia and North America symbolising expanded economic cooperation.

Dmitriev first introduced the proposal in October of the previous year, estimating that a conventional build could exceed $65 billion but suggesting that costs could fall below $8 billion using construction methods associated with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s [[the-boring-company|The Boring Company]]. Badlands commentary treats the signing as the public debut of what it calls the Sovereign Alliance.
