# Kirill Dmitriev

Kirill Dmitriev is Russia's Special Envoy to the United States.[1]

## Board of Peace announcement

In January 2026 Dmitriev confirmed on X that President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had accepted an invitation to join President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], making Putin the first high-profile name to emerge publicly as an invitee.[1] Separately, Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] said the invitation was under review and that Moscow was seeking to "clarify all nuances" with Washington before responding.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the acceptance as placing Putin and Trump figuratively shoulder to shoulder at the Gaza border, standing between Palestine and Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]

## Bering Strait tunnel advocacy

Dmitriev has been the principal public promoter of a fixed link between Russia and North America.[2] He first introduced the idea in October 2025, estimating that a conventional build could exceed $65 billion while suggesting that costs could fall below $8 billion if construction methods associated with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s [[the-boring-company|The Boring Company]] were used.[2] On June 4, 2026 he announced that Russia and the United States would sign an agreement the following day to continue the design phase of the [[bering-strait-tunnel|Bering Strait tunnel]] linking Russia's Chukotka region with Alaska: "Regarding the tunnel. We will have news tomorrow: we are signing an agreement to continue the tunnel design. The tunnel will be built."[2] He described the project as one of the most significant infrastructure initiatives envisioned between the two countries and Russian officials have promoted it as a symbol of expanded economic cooperation with Washington.[2]

## "Putin & Trump will prevent WWIII"

Dmitriev, a presidential aide on international economic affairs and head of the Russian Direct Investment Fund (RDIF), Russia's sovereign wealth fund, argued in September 2025 that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] were bringing the end of the Ukraine conflict closer and that their diplomacy could prevent a third world war.[3] Writing on X on Saturday, September 6, he declared: "Stalin, Roosevelt & Churchill won WWII. Putin & Trump will prevent WWIII," accompanying the post with a photograph of the Soviet, American, and British leaders taken at the 1945 Yalta Conference that laid the foundations of the postwar order.[3] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called Dmitriev "based" and "pure signal," linking his messaging to the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework discussed alongside [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orban]]'s warnings of European Union collapse.[3]


## Accusations against EU "warmongers" (September 2025)

In September 2025 Dmitriev accused the [[european-union|European Union]] of deliberately undermining US-led peace efforts in Ukraine, after media reports that Washington increasingly believed European leaders were obstructing negotiations.[4] In a series of posts on X, he said Brussels was "sabotaging a real peace process" by encouraging Kiev to pursue what he called "impossible demands," writing: "EU warmongers exposed… Even Washington now sees it – EU leaders are prolonging the conflict in Ukraine with impossible demands."[4] He urged the bloc to "drop Biden's failed logic" and "stop sabotaging a real peace process."[4] His remarks followed reporting in Axios and The Atlantic that the White House was growing frustrated with EU governments for undermining President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s peace initiative.[4]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], reporting an impromptu live dig on Dmitriev conducted on Breaking History, noted that Dmitriev has worked directly with Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] on Russia–Saudi trade and investment, despite MBS not holding a cabinet position until he became Minister of Defense in late January 2015.[4] He also observed that Dmitriev had come to the defense of US Special Envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], and in doing so was isolating what he characterized as the rotten European elite and the corrupt multi-continental intelligence apparatus aligned with them — public behavior he argued had brought the [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] and Trump teams together as the European establishment spiraled.[4]
## April 2025 claims of sabotage against US-Russia talks

Speaking to reporters on Thursday, April 17, 2025, Dmitriev — investment envoy to Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] — claimed that numerous foreign actors were attempting to sabotage the dialogue between Moscow and Washington.[5] He was discussing his talks the previous week in St. Petersburg with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s special envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], which focused on finding a settlement to the Ukraine conflict.[5]

Dmitriev called the negotiations "extremely productive" but said that "a lot of people, structures, and countries are trying to disrupt our dialogue with the United States."[5] He added that "there is a very active propaganda campaign against Russia in the United States through various media, so it's very important to communicate Russia's position directly," describing a useful dialogue proceeding "in very difficult conditions - constant attacks and constant misinformation."[5]

Badlands commentary framed the episode as a test of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], arguing that all available assets would be deployed to prevent a rapprochement between Trump and Putin because an alliance between Russia and MAGA would mean the end of the unipolar world order.[5]

## Cited in the "paper tiger" exchange (September 2025)

In coverage of the September 2025 exchange in which the Kremlin insisted Russia was "a real bear" rather than a paper tiger, Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited a post from Dmitriev, described as Russian Special Envoy to the United States, as signal from the Russian side supporting the thesis that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was deliberately pushing Ukraine and NATO into a confrontation with the Russian military.[6]
