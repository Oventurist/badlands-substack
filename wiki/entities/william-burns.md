# William Burns

William Burns is a U.S. diplomat and intelligence official who served as the author of a February 2008 diplomatic cable from Moscow warning that Ukrainian and Georgian NATO aspirations constituted the "brightest of all redlines" for Russia. As of the date of the source article (May 2024), Burns served as the Director of the Central Intelligence Agency, having been appointed by President Joe Biden.

## The 2008 Moscow Cable

While serving in the State Department's intelligence bureau, Burns wrote a cable dated February 2008 that assessed Russian elite and popular reactions to the prospect of NATO expansion to include Ukraine and Georgia. The cable, never intended for public consumption, was later published by Wikileaks. In it, Burns stated that the two countries' NATO aspirations "not only touch a raw nerve in Russia, they engender serious concerns about the consequences for stability in the region." He noted that Russia perceived "encirclement" and feared "unpredictable and uncontrolled consequences which would seriously affect Russian security interests."

The cable's most quoted passage reads: "Consequences of a premature MAP offer, especially to Ukraine. Ukrainian entry into NATO is the **brightest of all redlines** for the Russian elite (not just Putin)." Burns further observed that across more than two and a half years of conversations with Russian officials spanning the full political spectrum, "from knuckle-draggers in the dark recesses of the Kremlin to Putin's sharpest liberal critics," he had encountered no one who viewed Ukraine in NATO as anything other than "a direct challenge to Russian interests."

The cable also predicted specific consequences of a premature Membership Action Plan (MAP) offer: Russian-Ukrainian relations would enter a "deep freeze," Moscow might raise gas prices to world market levels, impose clampdowns on Ukrainian workers in Russia, and meddle in Crimea and eastern Ukraine. The source article presents this cable as evidence that the U.S. government possessed foreknowledge of the risks of NATO expansion yet proceeded with that policy nonetheless.

## Role in the Biden Administration

As CIA director under the Biden administration, Burns occupies a central position in the U.S. intelligence community. The article cites his 2008 cable to argue that the policy he now serves as a senior intelligence official for represents a continuation of the trajectory he once warned against. The piece characterizes the cable as a warning to an incoming administration that was "at least outwardly cultivated the appearance of a desire for a tenable relationship with Russia."

## Related Figures and Concepts

Burns' cable is a foundational document in the Badlands Media corpus's analysis of the Russia-Ukraine conflict. It connects him thematically to [[anthony-blinken]], the Secretary of State who later declared that Ukraine "WILL become a member of NATO," and to [[victoria-nuland]], who discussed NATO expansion to Ukraine during the Bush administration. The cable itself is analyzed as a leak that exposed internal government knowledge contradicting public propaganda.
