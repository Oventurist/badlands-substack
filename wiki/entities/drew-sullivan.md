# Drew Sullivan

Drew Sullivan is an entrepreneur and journalist identified as the co-founder of the [[organized-crime-and-corruption-reporting-project|Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project]] (OCCRP), an international NGO of journalists operating in six countries.[1]

The organization Sullivan co-founded was reported in December 2024 to have received 52 percent of the funds it spent between 2014 and 2023 from the U.S. federal government, chiefly the [[state-department|Department of State]], along with roughly $15 million from European governments and backing from [[george-soros|George Soros]]'s [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]].[1] OCCRP's reporting on [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]]'s Ukraine activities was cited multiple times in the whistleblower complaint that triggered the [[first-impeachment-of-donald-trump|first impeachment of Donald Trump]].[1]
