# Gulf of Oman

The Gulf of Oman is the body of water connecting the Arabian Sea to the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] and, through it, to the Persian Gulf. It became a focal point of US–Iranian confrontation in April 2026.[1]

## 2026 US maritime blockade

Following the collapse of negotiations with [[iran|Iran]] in Islamabad, the United States moved ahead with a maritime blockade in key Middle Eastern waters.[1] According to [[centcom|US Central Command]], the blockade would be enforced in the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea, targeting vessel traffic east of the Strait of Hormuz.[1] Military guidance stated that any unauthorized ship entering or leaving the restricted zone could face interception, diversion, or capture, while neutral transit through the strait and humanitarian shipments would still be permitted under inspection.[1]

The geography of the measure is significant: by concentrating enforcement east of the strait rather than inside it, the blockade targeted traffic to and from Iranian ports while nominally preserving the international transit corridor.[1] The operation forms the maritime component of the wider [[naval-blockade-of-iran|naval blockade of Iran]].[1]
