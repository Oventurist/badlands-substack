# Zhong Shaojun

Zhong Shaojun is a Chinese military official named in January 2026 reports of arrests among the senior ranks of the People's Liberation Army.[1]

## Alleged detention

Social-media reports amplified by an account quoted by General Michael Flynn claimed that [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]], [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]] and Zhong Shaojun had been arrested simultaneously.[1] A subsequent post from the same account stated that it was [[wang-xiaohong|Wang Xiaohong]]'s special service bureau that arrested military personnel including Zhong Shaojun, who — according to that account — had only just been released before being detained again, as part of a sweep of seventeen people.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the arrests as the collapse of a failed military coup and as evidence of a definitive break between [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and the CCP's "red families."[1] The originating account noted that the sourcing could not be verified.[1]
