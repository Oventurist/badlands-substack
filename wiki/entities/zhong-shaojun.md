# Zhong Shaojun

Zhong Shaojun is a senior Chinese military official long associated with the office of the Central Military Commission and with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s personal staff apparatus. He is named in the January 2026 reports of a purge — described by some accounts as an attempted coup — within the Chinese military leadership.

## Reported arrests, January 2026

The January 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] ([[badlands-brief-577|"ICE Getting Boiled, Chinese Coup Chaos, & Carney Bends The Knee"]]) quotes an X account, relayed by General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]], asserting that Zhong was arrested simultaneously with generals [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]] and [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]]. A subsequent post from the same account added a further detail specific to Zhong: that it was the special service bureau of Wang Xiaohong which arrested military personnel "including Zhong Shaojun, who had only just been released, and he was arrested again," in a sweep said to have taken seventeen people at once.

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry groups Zhong with the other two named officers as members of the "red families," the founding lineages of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] said to control most state-owned natural resources and infrastructure, and reads the episode as evidence of a decisive factional break between Xi Jinping and that aristocracy. The account is explicitly unverified in the source, which advises letting "the bullet fly a little longer."
