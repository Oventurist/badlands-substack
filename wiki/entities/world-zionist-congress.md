# World Zionist Congress

The **World Zionist Congress** is the deliberative body of the [[world-zionist-organization|World Zionist Organization]], created by Theodor Herzl in 1897 and frequently described as "the parliament of the Jewish people." It is responsible for budgeting more than $1 billion a year in allocations to a range of Jewish and Israeli causes.[1]

## 2025 election and fraud allegations

The 2025 Congress elections were conducted in part through a two-month online vote in the United States, which drew a record 238,000 ballots. As tallying proceeded, investigators examined widespread allegations of voter fraud, and the number of ballots considered suspicious rose steadily. When voting closed on May 4, 2025, roughly 10,200 votes were believed likely to be disqualified; by late May sources indicated that more than 15,000 ballots were likely to be thrown out — more than six percent of all votes cast.[1]

Results of the contested online election were due at the end of May 2025, though sources indicated they would probably be delayed by the ongoing investigation. Preparations were simultaneously underway for other countries to hold their own local Congress elections.[1]
