# Chunghwa Telecom

Chunghwa Telecom is Taiwan's principal telecommunications carrier and the operator of subsea communications infrastructure serving the island.[1]

On January 3, 2025, at approximately 12:40 PM, Chunghwa Telecom informed Taiwan's [[taiwan-coast-guard-administration|Coast Guard Administration]] that a subsea communications cable near Keelung, off Taiwan's northern coast, had been severed. The notification triggered the interception of the Hong Kong-owned freighter Shunxin-39, which had been operating near Yehliu at the time.[1]

The company's role as the reporting party made it the initial detector of an incident that Badlands and international outlets linked to the wider pattern of [[undersea-cable-sabotage|undersea cable sabotage]] previously seen in the Baltic Sea.[1]
