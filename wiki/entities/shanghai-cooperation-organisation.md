# Shanghai Cooperation Organisation

The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) is a Eurasian political, security and economic bloc whose 2025 annual summit was held in Tianjin, China from August 31 to September 1, 2025.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) The Badlands Media commentary "A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation" treats the Tianjin gathering as a watershed event in the construction of an alternative security and financial architecture.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Tianjin summit (2025)

At the summit the leaders of the three founding nations behind the SCO — Narendra Modi of India, Xi Jinping of China and Vladimir Putin of Russia — stood together, joined by other heads of state representing nearly half of the world's population and roughly 20% of world GDP.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Collectively they advanced a new financial and security architecture premised on the principles set out in the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Declared principles

The article summarises the summit's commitments as: total respect for the sovereign equality of nations, in explicit contrast to the [[rules-based-international-order|Rules Based International Order]]; rejection of unilateral coercive measures; creation of a new SCO Development Bank to provide productive credit to all members; resistance to what the author describes as the rehabilitation of Nazism growing throughout the West; and the position that a just settlement of the Palestinian question is the only way to ensure peace and stability in the Middle East.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Alleged destabilisation

The author argues that the emergence of this architecture explains the timing of "convenient fires" — bloody confrontations launched by Azerbaijan against SCO member Armenia, and clashes between SCO members Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan, both of which host Russian bases and Chinese [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road]]-connected infrastructure.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) While conceding that the identity of the "arsonists" is not established, he writes that the timing and character of the conflicts "smells of Anglo-Intelligence operations."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Framing

The discussions at the summit and within the broader multipolar alliance are described as organised around the common good, common security and the improvement of living conditions of peoples and sovereign nations as outlined by the [[united-nations|UN Charter]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) The author contends that Western leaders with political influence remain largely unaware of the epoch-making decisions being taken, and that the multipolar states would prefer cooperation with the trans-Atlantic community over war, even as events have forced them to prepare for the latter.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Russia-China coordination mechanism

In a congratulatory letter to Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] dated November 20, 2023, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] identified the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation, alongside [[brics|BRICS]], as one of the multilateral mechanisms through which Moscow and Beijing coordinate positions to resolve major international issues and promote what he called "a more just and democratic international order."[2] The letter marked the tenth meeting of the dialogue mechanism between the two countries' ruling parties and described the Russia-China comprehensive strategic partnership of coordination as being at its highest level in history.[2]
