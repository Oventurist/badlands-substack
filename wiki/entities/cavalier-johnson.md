# Cavalier Johnson

Cavalier Johnson is the mayor of Milwaukee, Wisconsin.[1]

## Response to the 2026 federal election inquiry

In May 2026, Johnson criticized reports that the [[fbi|FBI]] was preparing to interview as many as 30 Milwaukee police officers as part of an investigation into the administration of the 2020 presidential election in Wisconsin.[1] Together with Milwaukee County Executive [[david-crowley|David Crowley]], he characterized the reported federal inquiry as "an act of intimidation."[1] Investigators were reported to be examining whether police assigned to Milwaukee's [[milwaukee-central-count-facility|Central Count facility]] had been instructed to avoid certain areas while election operations were underway, with the officers described as potential witnesses rather than targets.[1]

## Replacement of the Milwaukee election director (2024)

On Monday, May 6, 2024, Johnson announced that he would replace [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] Executive Director [[claire-woodall|Claire Woodall]] with her deputy, [[paulina-gutierrez|Paulina Gutierrez]], a step the Associated Press described as a surprise move.[2] The change came just six months before Wisconsin's largest city would again be in the spotlight in a presidential swing state where four of the past six presidential contests had been decided by less than a percentage point.[2] Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] linked the removal to long-running scrutiny of Wisconsin's "Vote at Home" practices and to emails showing state election figures joking about margins for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[2]
