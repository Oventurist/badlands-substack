# Jeff Buckley

Jeff Buckley was an American singer and guitarist best known for his 1994 debut album *Grace* and his rendition of Leonard Cohen's "Hallelujah," later familiar to audiences from the film *Shrek*.[1] He drowned in the Mississippi River in 1997.[1]

## Death

An October 2024 Badlands Media essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] questioned the circumstances of Buckley's death, describing the accepted account as a physically fit 30-year-old man pulling his car over in Memphis, deciding to take an impromptu swim fully dressed, and drowning — days after proposing to his fiancée.[1] Carlson noted that he personally watched [[chris-whitley|Chris Whitley]], Buckley's close friend, perform in a small Seattle club on the night Buckley washed up on the river's shore.[1]

## Context

Buckley is one of several musicians — with [[kurt-cobain|Kurt Cobain]], [[chris-cornell|Chris Cornell]], [[chester-bennington|Chester Bennington]], and [[avicii|Avicii]] — whose deaths the essay treats as suspicious, offered as evidence that the [[cabal|Cabal]] eliminates entertainers who refuse or abandon its bargain.[1] Like Whitley and the Canadian band [[the-tragically-hip|The Tragically Hip]], Buckley is also described as an artist with a strong debut who never received the sustained industry backing his talent warranted.[1] Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
