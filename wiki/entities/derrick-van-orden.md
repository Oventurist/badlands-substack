# Derrick Van Orden

Derrick Van Orden is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Wisconsin (R-WI). He appears in the Badlands Media corpus as the House Republican who offered the most sympathetic interpretation of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election-reform demands during the February 2026 Politico reporting summarized by the Badlands Brief.[1]

## Interpretation of Trump's position

Rather than distancing himself from the President, Van Orden recast Trump's opposition to mail voting as narrowly targeted at ineligible voters: "If you're sick and you can't get to the polling [place], he wants you to have a ballot. If you're a military member, he wants you to have a ballot. Who he doesn't want to have a ballot is the illegal alien that registered or even a lawful alien who got a driver's license to be registered to vote and get a mail-in ballot."[1] The formulation links election integrity to immigration enforcement and to motor-voter style registration pathways.[1]

## Significance in the corpus

Van Orden's comments sit at one end of the spectrum the Brief documented, with [[mike-lawler|Mike Lawler]]'s explicit support for mail-in voting at the other.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the overall picture as Republicans "playing politics on election reforms," noting that changing election rules during an election year threatens the incumbents who benefit from the existing system.[1]
