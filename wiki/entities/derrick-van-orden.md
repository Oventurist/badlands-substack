# Derrick Van Orden

Derrick Van Orden is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Wisconsin.[1]

## Interpretation of Trump's election reform position

In February 2026, Van Orden offered an interpretation of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s position on mail-in balloting that sought to narrow it to questions of eligibility rather than method: "If you're sick and you can't get to the polling [place], he wants you to have a ballot. If you're a military member, he wants you to have a ballot," he said. "Who he doesn't want to have a ballot is the illegal alien that registered or even a lawful alien who got a driver's license to be registered to vote and get a mail-in ballot."[1]

The remark appeared in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]]'s survey of Republican responses to the [[election-reform-campaign|election reform campaign]].[1]
