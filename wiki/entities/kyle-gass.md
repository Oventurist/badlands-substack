# Kyle Gass

Kyle Gass is an American musician and comedian, one half of the comedy rock band [[tenacious-d|Tenacious D]] with [[jack-black|Jack Black]].[1]

## Sydney birthday remark

During a Tenacious D performance in Sydney on Sunday, July 14, 2024, Gass was presented with a birthday cake by what appeared to be a robot, and when asked to make a wish he blew out the candles and said, "Don't miss Trump next time" — a reference to the gunman who had opened fire at a [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|Trump rally in Pennsylvania]] the previous day, killing one person and injuring several others including [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] himself.[1] The Sydney crowd reacted with a mix of boos and cheers.[1]

The remark triggered immediate backlash. Black cancelled the band's world tour two days later, saying he had been "blindsided" and would never condone hate speech or political violence, and placed all future creative plans on hold.[1] Australian leaders and members of the public called for the deportation of the Tenacious D duo.[1] Badlands commentary observed, sardonically, that the strongest condemnation of the "we missed" sentiment was coming from Australia rather than from the United States.[1]
