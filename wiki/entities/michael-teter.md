# Michael Teter

Michael Teter is an American attorney who in March 2023 was retained by [[ray-epps]] in connection with Epps's dispute with [[fox-news]] and its former host [[tucker-carlson]].[1]

## Background and Perkins Coie ties

Teter was formerly of [[perkins-coie]], the law firm described in [[badlands-media]] coverage as notorious for its role in helping the Clinton campaign originate the Steele dossier and for collaborating with the [[fbi|FBI]] in advancing what Badlands writers call the Trump–Russia hoax.[1] That association was, in the alternative-media framing, treated as evidence that Epps's legal strategy carried establishment backing.[1]

## Representation of Ray Epps

Immediately upon being retained, Teter sent a letter to Carlson demanding retraction of what he characterized as "false and defamatory statements" that Epps had been a January 6 government plant.[1] The demand letter preceded the defamation lawsuit Epps filed against Fox News in July 2023, in which his counsel disclosed that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] had notified Epps in May 2023 of its intention to charge him criminally over January 6.[1]
