# European People's Party

The European People's Party (EPP) is the powerful center-right political family that constitutes the largest group in the European Parliament and is the political home of [[european-commission|European Commission]] president [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]].[1]

In February 2024 the EPP spearheaded the changes that gutted the [[eu-2040-climate-proposal|EU 2040 climate proposal]], removing the mandated 30 percent cut to agricultural production by 2040, the citizen lifestyle-change mandate, and the push to end fossil fuel subsidies, in response to the [[2024-european-farmer-protests|farmer protests]] sweeping the continent.[1] The party's influence was also visible in the scrapping of plans to halve EU pesticide use by 2030 under the Green Deal.[1]

## Support for the EU Migration Pact

The European People's Party supplied a large share of the votes that carried the [[eu-asylum-and-migration-pact|EU Asylum and Migration Pact]] through the [[european-parliament|European Parliament]] in April 2024, alongside the Socialists and Democrats and [[renew-europe|Renew Europe]].[2] MEPs across these groups were urged to set aside their criticisms of the scheme in favor of the compromise legislation, and some center-right lawmakers publicly acknowledged voting for the package despite regarding it as flawed.[2]
