# Andrius Kubilius

**Andrius Kubilius** is a Lithuanian politician, former prime minister of Lithuania, and Member of the European Parliament who was appointed the [[european-union|European Union]]'s first Commissioner for Defense and Space in 2024.[1] He has long been known for a hardline stance on relations with Moscow.[1]

## "Prepare for war" remarks (October 2024)

In the first days of October 2024, shortly after his appointment, Kubilius said the EU and Russia could face one another on the battlefield in less than a decade and that the bloc must ramp up its defense production and stock up reserves to be able to "defend itself."[1] Speaking as his European People's Party group presented its strategic paper on defense on Wednesday, October 2, he invoked the maxim "if you want peace, prepare for war" in commenting on the group's initiative calling for a "European Defense Union" and encouraging investments in the "defense technology of the future."[1]

"We must be ready to meet Russia militarily in six to eight years," Kubilius maintained, adding that "the EU, in complementing [[nato|NATO]] and together with its Member States, must boost defense production, stock up reserves and continue helping Ukraine."[1] He said Brussels' adversaries were "watching … closely" whether the bloc would "succeed or fail" in the endeavor.[1]

## Reception

Badlands paired Kubilius's militarization push with [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]]'s warning that the EU "could die" economically, treating the two statements as evidence that the bloc's leadership was simultaneously admitting economic collapse and committing to a costly rearmament program.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] rejected the framing of an earnest inflection point, describing the EU instead as "a menagerie of wannabe aristocrats" attempting to re-enact the 1815 Treaty of Vienna.[1]
