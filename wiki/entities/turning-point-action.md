# Turning Point Action

Turning Point Action (TPA) is the political-activism affiliate of [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]], the conservative youth organization founded by [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1] In July 2023 the group hosted a conference in West Palm Beach, Florida, and extended speaking invitations to every declared Republican presidential candidate.[1]

## 2023 West Palm Beach conference and the DeSantis snub

The event became a news story when Florida Governor and presidential candidate [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] was reported to have turned down his invitation — a move that left conservatives perplexed given that DeSantis had spoken at Turning Point events in the past.[1] Journalist and Turning Point USA contributor [[jack-posobiec|Jack Posobiec]] broke the report in a tweet on Monday, July 10, 2023, stating that DeSantis "declined the invitation," and reaffirmed the account during a Human Events broadcast on Real America's Voice the following day.[1] Charlie Kirk shared Posobiec's tweet with the comment, "Too bad!", while TPA Chief Operating Officer [[tyler-bowyer|Tyler Bowyer]] retweeted Posobiec and questioned the DeSantis campaign's strategy.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that declining a friendly appearance in DeSantis's own home state was one more misstep in a campaign that, in her view, never had a realistic chance against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the primary.[1]

## 2023 West Palm Beach conference

In July 2023, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] delivered what Summit News called a rousing speech at the Turning Point Action conference in West Palm Beach, telling young American voters that the upcoming election would decide "whether your generation inherits a fascist country or a free country—whether you will have the rule of tyrants or the rule of law."[2] The venue underscored the organization's role as a mobilization vehicle for young conservative voters ahead of the 2024 cycle.[2]

## July 2024 West Palm Beach rally

On Friday, July 26, 2024, Turning Point Action hosted a rally in West Palm Beach, Florida — described by The Guardian as an event of the "far-right Christian advocacy group" — at which [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] told the crowd, "Christians, get out and vote! Just this time – you won't have to do it any more."[3] He added, "You know what? It'll be fixed! It'll be fine. You won't have to vote any more, my beautiful Christians," and later, "In four years, you don't have to vote again. We'll have it fixed so good, you're not gonna have to vote."[3] The remarks, delivered not far from Trump's Mar-a-Lago resort, drew immediate alarm from critics, including constitutional and civil rights attorney Andrew Seidel, who wrote on X that "this is not subtle Christian nationalism. He's talking about ending our democracy and installing a Christian nation."[3]
