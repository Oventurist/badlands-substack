# Boston Herald

The Boston Herald is a daily newspaper published in Boston, Massachusetts, covering state politics, local government and regional news.[1]

## Coverage of the Massachusetts license law

In July 2023 the Herald's Chris Van Buskirk published data obtained from the [[massachusetts-registry-of-motor-vehicles|Registry of Motor Vehicles]] showing that roughly 100,000 learner's permit appointment requests had been placed since the state's [[massachusetts-drivers-licenses-for-illegal-aliens|driver's license law for illegal aliens]] took effect on July 1, and that the RMV had hired 52 additional employees to cope with the demand.[1] The Herald's reporting was subsequently picked up by [[breitbart|Breitbart]] and circulated in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as evidence of the scale of the policy's implementation.[1]
