# Terryl Farnsworth

Terryl Farnsworth is a contracted camera operator associated with [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] who became a target of threats during the organization's public feud with [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]].[1]

According to a Politico Magazine investigation published July 11, 2026, Farnsworth and his family went into hiding for more than a week after he received multiple death threats.[1] Politico attributed the account to four anonymous people described as familiar with the security response.[1] His case was reported alongside Turning Point's decision to increase security at its Phoenix headquarters and at the homes of several employees after staff received threats following Owens' broadcasts questioning the circumstances of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]]'s killing.[1]
