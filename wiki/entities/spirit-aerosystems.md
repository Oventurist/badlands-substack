# Spirit AeroSystems

Spirit AeroSystems is an aerostructures manufacturer based in Wichita, Kansas, and a major supplier to [[boeing|Boeing]], including for the 737 MAX production line.[1]

## Quality controversies and whistleblowers

The company drew scrutiny over alleged manufacturing defects on the 737 MAX. [[josh-dean|Joshua Dean]], a former quality auditor at Spirit, was among the first whistleblowers to allege that Spirit leadership had ignored such defects.[1] Dean gave a deposition in a Spirit shareholder lawsuit and filed a complaint with the [[federal-aviation-administration|Federal Aviation Administration]] alleging "serious and gross misconduct by senior quality management of the 737 production line."[1]

Spirit fired Dean in April 2023; he subsequently filed a complaint with the [[department-of-labor|Department of Labor]] alleging retaliatory termination for raising aviation safety concerns.[1] Dean died on April 30, 2024, at age 45 after a sudden, fast-spreading infection, and Spirit spokesperson Joe Buccino called the loss "stunning news here and for his loved ones."[1]
