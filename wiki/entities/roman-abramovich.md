# Roman Abramovich

Roman Abramovich is a Russian businessman, formerly the owner of the English football club Chelsea, who was sanctioned by the United Kingdom in March 2022 over alleged Kremlin ties — allegations he has denied.[1]

## Sale of Chelsea and sanctions

Abramovich was forced to sell Chelsea to a US-led consortium shortly after the escalation of the Ukraine conflict, with the UK government imposing sanctions on him in March 2022.[1] He has acted as a mediator between Moscow and Kiev on several occasions.[1] His case is documented in the book *Sanctioned: The Inside Story of the Sale of Chelsea FC* by Nick Purewal.[1]

## Withdrawal from football

In a June 2025 interview excerpt cited by the *Daily Mail*, Abramovich said he no longer intends to be involved in professional football or to own a club again, stating he has "no interest in any role in a football club, certainly not a professional role" and that "as for ownership or a professional role at a club, I am done with that in this lifetime."[1] He said he still hopes to attend a Chelsea match to "say a proper goodbye," and expressed willingness to support initiatives for underprivileged youth if a meaningful opportunity arises.[1]

## 2023 US enforcement campaign

In March 2023, Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] listed Abramovich among the most significant targets of the United States effort against smuggling and money laundering networks supporting the Russian regime, alongside [[viktor-vekselberg|Viktor Vekselberg]], [[andrei-derkach|Andrei Derkach]] and [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]], describing them collectively as members of "the international Swamp."[2] The broader [[us-sanctions-campaign-against-russian-oligarch-networks|sanctions campaign]] had by that point resulted in the seizure of more than $500 million in assets and the indictment of more than 30 individuals and two corporate entities for offences including sanctions evasion and money laundering.[2]
