# Kim Ju-ae

Kim Ju-ae is the daughter of [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong-un]], believed to be in her early teens, whose growing public profile has fueled speculation that she is being groomed to become the first female ruler of [[north-korea|North Korea]].[1] The North Korean regime has never publicly confirmed her name or age.[1]

## International debut

In September 2025, Ju-ae made her debut on the international stage when she appeared in China ahead of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s "victory day" parade in Beijing.[1] Photos released by North Korean state media showed her stepping off her father's heavily armoured train in the Chinese capital, where she was greeted by Chinese officials.[1]

## Domestic profile and succession speculation

Ju-ae is no stranger to the spotlight domestically, having accompanied her father on numerous official duties around North Korea, including ballistic missile test launches and, more recently, the opening of Wonsan Kalma, a resort on the country's east coast.[1] If, as many analysts believe, she is her father's preferred successor, she would become the fourth member of the Kim family to rule North Korea since the state was founded by her great-grandfather, Kim Il-sung, in 1948.[1]
