# Mohammed Nuru

Mohammed Nuru was a confidant of San Francisco political boss [[willie-brown|Willie Brown]] and served as deputy director of operations in the city's Department of Public Works.[1] Among the programs he ran was the San Francisco League of Urban Gardeners (SLUG), a group of city workers who performed gardening and cleanup work around San Francisco.[1]

## SLUG and political mobilization

According to internal reports cited in the article, SLUG served a second, more clandestine purpose as a resource deployed by the Willie Brown machine.[1] SLUG employees were said to have been dispatched on the public's dime to fill in sparse crowds at rallies for both [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]], preserving the appearance of mid- to lower-class public support, and were told to vote for both candidates.[1] Harris's campaign manager denied the accusations and nothing came of them.[1] These allegations formed part of a broader pattern in which employees of certain businesses and city workers reported being told by their bosses to attend Harris campaign events.[1]

## Federal conviction

Nuru was eventually sentenced to seven years in federal prison for receiving bribery money and kickbacks during his tenure at the Department of Public Works, which he ultimately led as director.[1]
