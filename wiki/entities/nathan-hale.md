# Nathan Hale

**Nathan Hale** was an American officer hanged without trial in New York City in mid-September 1776.[1] British authorities caught Hale as he was returning to his regiment after penetrating the British lines to gather information.[1]

His death illustrated the grave dangers inherent in spying for the rebels, a point the Badlands Brief article "Kekistani Code Talkers" uses to frame the necessity of the coded communications later adopted by the [[culper-spy-ring|Culper Spy Ring]] under [[george-washington|George Washington]].[1]
