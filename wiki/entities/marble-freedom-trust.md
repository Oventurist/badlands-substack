# Marble Freedom Trust

The Marble Freedom Trust is a conservative non-profit organization associated with the legal activist [[leonard-leo|Leonard Leo]], described as being prepared to push both the government and the private sector in a conservative direction.[1]

## $1 billion campaign against "liberal dominance"

In September 2024 it was reported that Leo, a conservative billionaire activist credited as the driving force behind the movement to reshape the American legal system, had launched a $1 billion campaign through the trust to "crush liberal dominance" in American corporations, mass media and the entertainment industry.[1] Leo told the Financial Times: "We need to crush liberal dominance where it's most insidious, so we'll direct resources to build talent and capital formation pipelines in the areas of news and entertainment, where leftwing extremism is most evident."[1]

The stated method was to support anti-woke organizations. "Expect us to increase support for organizations that call out companies and financial institutions that bend to the woke mind virus spread by regulators and NGOs so that they have to pay the price for putting extreme leftwing ideology ahead of consumers," Leo said.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] endorsed the effort, writing that reclaiming the institutions is a noble goal that much of the movement has abandoned, and that journalism — telling the people the truth regardless of who it implicates — remains a noble institution even where its current practice is corrupted.[1] The commentary paired Leo's ambition with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s campaign to shrink government and eliminate waste, arguing that a Fourth Estate reclaimed on conservative principles would help keep the surviving institutions necessary and constitutional.[1]
