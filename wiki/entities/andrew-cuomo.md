# Andrew Cuomo

**Andrew Cuomo** is a former Governor of New York who ran as an independent candidate in the 2025 New York City mayoral election.[1]

## 2025 NYC mayoral campaign

During the first general mayoral election debate in October 2025, Cuomo launched a series of attacks on Democratic nominee [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]] over his positions on Israel and Gaza, criticizing him for refusing to denounce [[hamas|Hamas]] and the phrase "Globalize the intifada."[1] "The Assemblyman will not denounce Hamas," Cuomo said during the debate, adding, "The assemblyman will not denounce [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]], who said America deserved 9/11."[1] Cuomo also seized on Mamdani's response to the ceasefire deal brokered by the Trump administration, quoting Mamdani as having said "Well, it depends on occupation."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] questioned the framing of the race, arguing that "based'n'redpilled" influencers had been reduced to calling people antisemitic in hopes of beating "the left" in what he characterized as fake elections.[1] Paul also raised a separate question about why Cuomo was speaking with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at an assassination-related event.[1]

## COVID-19 nursing home deaths inquiry

As Governor of New York during the COVID-19 pandemic, Cuomo presided over a policy requiring nursing homes to readmit recovering COVID-19 patients in order to prevent hospitals from becoming overwhelmed, a decision for which his administration faced heavy criticism; state fatality figures were additionally accused of undercounting the true number of deaths.[2] In early September 2024 it was reported that Cuomo had agreed to appear publicly before the [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]] on September 10 to answer questions about the deaths of thousands of residents in New York's nursing homes.[2] Subcommittee chairman [[brad-wenstrup|Brad Wenstrup]] of Ohio said that "Andrew Cuomo owes answers to the 15,000 families who lost loved ones in New York's nursing homes during the Covid-19 pandemic."[2]

The scheduled public appearance followed a closed-door session in June 2024, in which Cuomo spoke for seven hours before House Republicans after being subpoenaed.[2] On the Tuesday before the public hearing, the subcommittee posted a series of statements on X accusing Cuomo of showing "little remorse" and being "shockingly callous" during that testimony, quoting him as having said, "…let's say there's a 3,000 [deaths] differential, 2500. Who cares? What difference does it make in any dimension to anyone about anything?"[2] Cuomo has shifted blame to the federal government, stating in June that the Trump administration should be held accountable for pandemic-related deaths and that "I think the federal government failed this nation, and it was abysmal," while criticising delays in testing and misleading statements by President Trump.[2]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterised Cuomo as unrepentant and sticking to the position that dead grandparents in his state and under his policies were "Trump's fault."[2] She argued that Trump had provided medical ships and the Javits Center, and that a field hospital in Central Park stayed largely empty, while Cuomo chose instead to overload nursing homes with sick people in a decision that resulted in many unnecessary deaths.[2] Her broader concern was the timing of the hearing on the eve of an election, writing that playing politics with the lives of Americans is not acceptable regardless of which party does the politicking, and concluding that "Cuomo should be behind bars."[2]

## Trump endorsement (November 2025)

On the eve of the [[new-york-city-mayoral-election-2025|2025 New York City mayoral election]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] endorsed Cuomo, telling New Yorkers in a Truth Social post that they "must vote for" the former governor to prevent the victory of "Communist" candidate [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]].[3] Although Cuomo was running as an independent and Republican [[curtis-sliwa|Curtis Sliwa]] remained in the race, Trump argued that a vote for Sliwa was a vote for Mamdani, adding that he "would much rather see a Democrat, who has had a Record of Success, WIN, than a Communist with no experience."[3] Badlands host Ashe in America questioned whether Cuomo — or anyone — could prevail in what she characterized as a "fake election."[3]
