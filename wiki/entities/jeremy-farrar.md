# Jeremy Farrar

**Dr. Jeremy Farrar** is a British medical researcher who serves as Chief Scientist at the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] and who, in March 2023 congressional findings on the origins of COVID-19, was identified as having been heavily involved — though not credited as an author — in the drafting of the [[proximal-origin-paper|"Proximal Origin of SARS-CoV-2"]] paper.[1]

## Role in the "Proximal Origin" paper

According to the [[house-select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic]] memo released in March 2023, [[anthony-fauci|Dr. Anthony Fauci]] "prompted" the creation of the paper by a group of scientists in order to disprove the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]] of COVID-19's origins.[1] The paper followed a February 1, 2020 conference call involving Fauci, [[national-institutes-of-health|NIH]] Director [[francis-collins|Dr. Francis Collins]] and at least eleven other scientists, several of whom warned that the virus may have leaked from a lab in Wuhan and may have been intentionally genetically manipulated.[1] Three days later, participants including [[kristian-andersen|Kristian Andersen]], Edward Holmes, [[robert-garry|Robert Garry]], Andrew Rambaut and Ian Lipkin drafted the paper and sent it to Fauci and Collins; Farrar was described as also heavily involved yet not credited.[1]

## Position at the World Health Organization

At the time of the March 2023 disclosures Farrar was the sitting Chief Scientist at the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], a position he took up after having been a lead UK lockdown advisor during the pandemic.[1]
