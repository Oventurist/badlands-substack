# Runbeck Election Services

Runbeck Election Services is a Phoenix-area private vendor that provides ballot printing, mailing, and envelope-scanning services to election jurisdictions, including [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona. Its involvement in the county's 2022 election processing made it a focal point of election-integrity scrutiny in the Badlands Media corpus.

## Role in the corpus

[[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] devoted a November 14, 2022 article, "The Plot Thickens in Maricopa County," to Runbeck. In [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]] she notes as a sidebar that a [[q|Q]] post dated November 18, 2022 also mentioned Runbeck, treating the coincidence as significant corroboration of her line of inquiry.

The company is also implicated in the county's own post-election messaging: a November 19, 2022 video posted by chairman [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]] to the @MaricopaCounty account addressed "the transport trucks that run between" the county recorder's office and "our vendor" — the chain-of-custody question that Runbeck's role raises. Gillespie's broader complaint is that the county's explanations of these arrangements are reassurances rather than documentation, a concern echoed in the detailed records requested by Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]].
