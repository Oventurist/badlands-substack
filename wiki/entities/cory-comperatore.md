# Cory Comperatore

Cory Comperatore was a spectator killed during the July 2024 attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s life at a campaign rally in Butler, Pennsylvania.[1] In [[reuters|Reuters]]' October 2024 accounting of American political violence, his death and that of the gunman, [[thomas-matthew-crooks|Thomas Crooks]], were the only two fatalities recorded among the incidents catalogued for that year.[1]

Badlands commentary cited Comperatore's death in arguing that the [[2024-us-political-violence-surge|2024 political violence narrative]] rested on a small number of genuine fatalities surrounded by a much larger body of low-level assaults and vandalism, and that the underlying driver looked more like an untreated mental health crisis than an organized political movement.[1]
