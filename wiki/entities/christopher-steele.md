# Christopher Steele

Christopher Steele is a former British intelligence officer whose opposition-research dossier, produced through [[fusion-gps|Fusion GPS]], became a central artifact of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]]/[[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] investigation.[1]

## Durham records litigation

In January 2026, [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] announced a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit against the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] seeking unredacted records from former Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s probe into the origins of Crossfire Hurricane.[1] The request explicitly covers communications involving Steele, along with [[british-intelligence|British Intelligence]] head Sir [[richard-dearlove|Richard Dearlove]], U.S. intelligence officials and [[stefan-halper|Stefan Halper]] — grouping Steele among the foreign-linked figures whose contacts with American agencies remain incompletely disclosed.[1]
