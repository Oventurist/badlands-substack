# Christopher Steele

Christopher Steele is a former British intelligence officer whose opposition-research dossier, produced through [[fusion-gps|Fusion GPS]], became a central artifact of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]]/[[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] investigation.[1]

## Durham records litigation

In January 2026, [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] announced a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit against the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] seeking unredacted records from former Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s probe into the origins of Crossfire Hurricane.[1] The request explicitly covers communications involving Steele, along with [[british-intelligence|British Intelligence]] head Sir [[richard-dearlove|Richard Dearlove]], U.S. intelligence officials and [[stefan-halper|Stefan Halper]] — grouping Steele among the foreign-linked figures whose contacts with American agencies remain incompletely disclosed.[1]

## July 5, 2016 first report

Badlands commentary places Steele's delivery of his first report to the FBI on July 5, 2016, the same day as the closure of the Clinton email investigation and a private Obama–Clinton meeting aboard a plane.[2]

## The dossier and the FBI corroboration offer

Evidence placed on the record by Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]] at the October 2022 [[igor-danchenko|Danchenko]] trial showed that the [[fbi|FBI]] had verified none of the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]] allegations it used in the FISA warrant on [[carter-page|Carter Page]].[3] With the 2016 election approaching and no corroboration in hand, the Bureau in October 2016 offered Steele payment of up to $1 million to substantiate the reporting; Steele was unable to verify a single allegation.[3] At the time the Page warrant issued, Steele had still not identified his primary sub-source to the Bureau, which supposedly did not learn until late December that the sub-source was Danchenko, a man previously investigated for suspected espionage.[3] The trial also revealed that longtime Democratic operative [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles H. "Chuck" Dolan]] was among the dossier's sources.[3]
