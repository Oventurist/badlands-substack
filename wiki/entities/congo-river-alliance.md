# Congo River Alliance

The Congo River Alliance (AFC) is a coalition of armed groups in the eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]], including the [[m23|M23]] rebel movement.[1]

## Doha framework agreement

Kinshasa and the AFC signed a framework peace agreement on November 15, 2025 in Doha, after months of mediation by [[qatar|Qatar]], the United States and the [[african-union|African Union]].[1] The document lays out eight protocols to be negotiated, including an immediate cessation of hostilities, disengagement of forces, "unimpeded, safe, and sustained humanitarian access," conditions for the return of displaced civilians, and the "full restoration of state authority over the entire national territory," including areas currently held by the rebels.[1]

[[russia|Russia]] welcomed the agreement, with Foreign Ministry spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] calling it another step toward "unblocking the conflict" and consistent with commitments previously made by the warring parties.[1]

## Accusations against Kinshasa (September 2025)

At a press conference held at a hotel in Goma in September 2025, AFC political coordinator [[corneille-nangaa|Corneille Nangaa]] accused the Congolese government of breaching agreements intended to end the long-running conflict in eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|DR Congo]], saying Congolese forces and their allies were carrying out attacks in South Kivu despite a pledge to cease hostilities.[2] "We are obliged to inform the Congolese people and the international community of the successive violations of the ceasefire, which are hindering the principle agreement," Nangaa told journalists.[2] The coalition includes the [[m23]] rebel group, which had seized Goma earlier that year.[2]
