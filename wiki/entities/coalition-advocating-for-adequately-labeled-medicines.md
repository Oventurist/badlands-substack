# Coalition Advocating for Adequately Labeled Medicines

The Coalition Advocating for Adequately Labeled Medicines (CAALM) is a group of experts that in early February 2023 filed a citizen petition with the [[food-and-drug-administration|U.S. Food and Drug Administration]] seeking revisions to the labeling of the [[pfizer|Pfizer]] and [[moderna|Moderna]] COVID-19 vaccines.[1]

## Petition

The coalition, whose signatories included University of Maryland School of Pharmacy associate professor [[peter-doshi|Peter Doshi]] and eight other experts, asked officials to update the vaccine labels to acknowledge limitations in the clinical trials — in particular to state clearly that the phase III trials that led to clearance did not provide evidence of efficacy against death.[1] The petition warned that "incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading labeling of any medical product can negatively impact the health and safety of Americans, with global ramifications considering the international importance of FDA decisions."[1] The FDA had authorized the vaccines in late 2020 and granted full approval in 2021, and as of the petition still stated on its website that "the scientific community does not yet know" whether the vaccines reduce transmission.[1]
