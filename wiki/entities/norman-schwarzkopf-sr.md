# Herbert Norman Schwarzkopf Sr.

Major General Herbert Norman Schwarzkopf was a United States Army officer who, in the mid-1950s, was one of a permanent team of five career CIA officers assigned to Iran to build the Shah's new intelligence apparatus. He is credited with having "trained virtually all of the first generation of [[savak|SAVAK]] personnel."[1]

## Role in Iran

Following the [[1953-iranian-coup|1953 coup]] that removed Prime Minister [[mohammad-mosaddegh|Mohammad Mosaddeq]], a U.S. Army colonel working for the [[cia|CIA]] was sent to Persia in September 1953 to work with General Teymur Bakhtiar, the military governor of Tehran, who began assembling the nucleus of a new intelligence organization.[1] In March 1955 that colonel was replaced by a team of five career CIA officers specializing in covert operations, intelligence analysis and counterintelligence — Schwarzkopf among them.[1] The organization he helped build became notorious for widespread torture and for the arrest and killing of thousands of Iranians perceived as threats to [[mohammad-reza-pahlavi|the Shah's]] rule.[1]

## Family and legacy

Schwarzkopf Sr. was the father of General [[norman-schwarzkopf|Norman Schwarzkopf Jr.]], who decades later commanded Operations Desert Shield and Desert Storm under President [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats this lineage as evidence that the Schwarzkopf family were members of the "club" — that those who play the cabal's game are promoted to positions of power and become more valuable instruments of the [[shadow-government|shadow government]].[1]
