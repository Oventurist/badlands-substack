# John Manning

John Manning is a legal scholar at Harvard Law School cited for his exposition of the [[absurdity-doctrine|Absurdity Doctrine]] in coverage of the Colorado litigation over [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2024 ballot eligibility.[1]

According to Manning, the Absurdity Doctrine recognizes that, despite legislators' best efforts to make the law coherent, statutes can produce unintended consequences, or society may evolve such that applying a law yields unexpected or unfair outcomes.[1] The doctrine allows the legal system to address unforeseen issues without undermining the overall authority of the legislation.[1] His account was invoked when justices and attorneys before the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]] debated whether Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]] reaches the Office of the President.[1]
