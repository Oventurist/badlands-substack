# Benjamin Hovland

Benjamin Hovland was a Democratic commissioner of the U.S. [[election-assistance-commission|Election Assistance Commission]].[1]

Hovland was removed from his post on July 9, 2026, when President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] terminated the last three sitting members of the commission, leaving the four-seat bipartisan agency with no commissioners at all.[1] He and fellow Democratic commissioner [[thomas-hicks|Thomas Hicks]] learned by email that their positions had been terminated effective immediately.[1]

The removals were defended by a [[white-house|White House]] official on the grounds that the president may remove officials not aligned with the administration's election-security priorities, invoking [[trump-v-slaughter|*Trump v. Slaughter*]] as precedent.[1] The vacancies prompted debate over whether the commission should be repopulated with election-integrity advocates or dissolved outright, with Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] arguing for dissolution, investigation and prosecution rather than replacement.[1]
