# The Hershey Company

The Hershey Company is an American confectionery manufacturer and the owner of the Reese's Peanut Butter Cup brand. It appears in the Badlands Media corpus as the subject of a February 2026 bonus item on product reformulation.[1]

## The Brad Reese open letter

Brad Reese, grandson of the inventor of Reese's Peanut Butter Cups, published an open letter to The Hershey Company accusing it of "quietly replacing" the ingredients in the candy and declaring that the product's "identity is being rewritten."[1] Reese wrote: "My grandfather built REESE'S on a simple, enduring architecture: Milk Chocolate + Peanut Butter. Not a flavor idea. Not a marketing construct. A real, tangible product identity that consumers have trusted for a century."[1] He alleged that "REESE'S identity is being rewritten, not by storytellers, but by formulation decisions that replace Milk Chocolate with compound coatings and Peanut Butter with peanut-butter-style crèmes across multiple REESE'S products."[1]

## Significance in the corpus

The Brief carried the item under its "Bonus Items" heading, where it functions as a consumer-facing instance of a recurring Badlands theme: the quiet substitution of authentic substance with cheaper synthetic equivalents while the branding remains unchanged.[1]
