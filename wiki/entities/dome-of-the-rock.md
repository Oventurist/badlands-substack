# Dome of the Rock

The Dome of the Rock is the shrine on Jerusalem's [[temple-mount|Temple Mount]] built atop the stone on which, by tradition, [[abraham|Abraham]] nearly sacrificed his son before being stopped by divine intervention.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] notes that this "Holy of Holies" sits on the same rock that Sir [[charles-warren|Charles Warren]] excavated on behalf of Edward VII, and which Warren presumed to be the point at which the blood of all mass sacrifice victims of the ancient pagan syncretic cult system of Israel had flowed.[1] Beneath the shrine, Warren mapped sub-caverns and tunnels which he believed had been designed to send human blood into the surrounding soil of Jerusalem.[1]

For Ehret, the site therefore stands at the intersection of two opposed traditions: the Old Testament intervention against child sacrifice embodied in the Abraham story, and the occult fascination with the rock as an altar of mass sacrifice.[1]
