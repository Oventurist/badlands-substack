# Pascal Tigri

Lieutenant Colonel Pascal Tigri is the Beninese officer named by mutinous soldiers as chairman of the self-declared Military Committee for Refoundation (CMR) during the December 2025 coup attempt in [[benin|Benin]].[1] The appointment was announced over the national television channel SRTB, alongside declarations suspending state institutions and the Constitution of Benin and closing the country's air, land and maritime borders; the channel's signal was cut shortly afterward.[1]

The attempt failed. Forces loyal to President [[patrice-talon|Patrice Talon]], assisted by [[nigeria|Nigerian]] airstrikes that immobilized mutineer armored vehicles, retook control of the situation.[1]
