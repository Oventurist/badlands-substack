# Mette Frederiksen

Mette Frederiksen is a Danish politician who served as Prime Minister of [[denmark|Denmark]] and leader of the Social Democrats.[1]

## Resignation

Frederiksen resigned following major electoral losses for the Social Democrats, an event reported in March 2026 as part of a broader wave of political instability across Europe.[1] Badlands commentary placed the resignation alongside [[germany|Germany]]'s industrial stagnation and [[hungary|Hungary]]'s curtailment of natural gas supplies to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] as evidence of "political dominoes" toppling in Europe, describing Denmark as a place where "the old guard fractures under the weight of their own illusions."[1]

Contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] situated her departure within a claimed continent-wide populist uprising also visible in Germany, the United Kingdom, and Spain, and read it as one symptom of the fragility of what he termed the "Globalist Hegemon."[1]

## Davos 2026 warning

Earlier, at the January 2026 [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meeting in Davos, Frederiksen was among the Danish and Greenlandic leaders who emphasized respect for territorial integrity in the face of the U.S. campaign to acquire [[greenland|Greenland]], warning that "the worst may still be ahead."[2] The [[greenland-tariff-dispute-2026|tariff threats issued against eight nations backing Denmark's sovereignty]] over the Arctic territory dominated the forum and prompted a united European front.[2]
