# Mette Frederiksen

Mette Frederiksen is the former Prime Minister of [[denmark|Denmark]] whose resignation is reported in the March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief as one of the political dominoes toppling across Europe.

## Resignation after Social Democrat losses

The Brief reports that Frederiksen resigned as prime minister following major electoral losses for her Social Democrats, citing ZeroHedge reporting. Her fall is presented within the broader European political instability item of the edition, which links it to Germany's industrial stagnation, Hungary's energy confrontation with Ukraine, and populist uprisings in Germany, the United Kingdom and Spain.

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treats Frederiksen's departure as evidence that "the old guard fractures under the weight of their own illusions" — part of his argument that the European political establishment is collapsing while the United States, Russia and China operate from positions of leverage.
