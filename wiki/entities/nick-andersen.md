# Nick Andersen

Nick Andersen is a US cybersecurity official who was named acting director of the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] in early 2026, replacing former acting director [[madhu-gottumukkala|Madhu Gottumukkala]].[1]

His appointment came during a period of broad leadership churn at the agency, which also saw chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]] announce his departure after 18 years at the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] and acting chief human resources officer [[kevin-diana|Kevin Diana]] receive transfer orders.[1] The nomination of [[sean-plankey|Sean Plankey]] to lead CISA on a permanent basis remained stalled at the time.[1]
