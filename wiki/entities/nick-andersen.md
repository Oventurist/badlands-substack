# Nick Andersen

**Nick Andersen** was named acting director of the [[cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] (CISA) in early 2026, replacing former acting director [[madhu-gottumukkala|Madhu Gottumukkala]].[1]

His appointment came during a period of significant leadership churn at the agency. Chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]] announced his departure in March 2026 after receiving transfer orders, acting chief human resources officer Kevin Diana also received transfer orders, and the nomination of [[sean-plankey|Sean Plankey]] to serve as CISA's permanent director remained stalled — leaving the agency under acting leadership amid congressional scrutiny from House Homeland Security Chairman [[andrew-garbarino|Andrew Garbarino]].[1]
