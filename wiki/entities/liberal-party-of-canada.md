# Liberal Party of Canada

The Liberal Party of Canada is one of Canada's two historically dominant federal political parties, in government under [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] from 2015 and subsequently under [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]. In the Badlands Media essay A Re Alignment of World Systems, the party is portrayed as the vehicle through which a transnational network placed [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]] at the centre of Canadian government.

## Reorganization, 2011–2014

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes a period of "re-organization of the Liberal Party (2011-2014)" carried out under the banner of the [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]] think tank as the party prepared to take power. During this period, the article says, Freeland brought her close contacts — Larry Summers, [[george-soros|George Soros]] and [[al-gore|Al Gore]] among them — into Canada. [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]] served as interim controller of the party during several of the Harper years and gave up his seat to Freeland, ensuring her entry into Trudeau's inner circle.

## The source's broader claim

A footnote to the article argues that both the Liberals and the NDP retain a "rotten core tied to the highest echelons of the British oligarchy," while conceding that "good people have found themselves members of the NDP and Liberals over the years."
