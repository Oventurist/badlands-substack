# Ze'ev Elkin

Ze'ev Elkin is an Israeli politician associated with the country's Finance Ministry who featured in Badlands coverage of February 2026 as one of several Israeli officials publicly distancing their government from US-led postwar arrangements in Gaza.[1]

## Refusal to fund Gaza reconstruction

Elkin asserted that [[israel|Israel]] would not financially contribute to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], the US-backed postwar Gaza panel.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the statement as one item in a catalogue of Israeli disrespect toward Trump's peace initiative, alongside Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]]'s declaration that Israel would ultimately occupy Gaza and reestablish settlements, and the formal rejection of the Board of Peace by top officials in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s government the previous month.[1]
