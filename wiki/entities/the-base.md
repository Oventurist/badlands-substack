# The Base

The Base is a US-founded neo-Nazi organization created by [[rinaldo-nazzaro|Rinaldo Nazzaro]], described in Western reporting as one of the longer-standing US-based neo-Nazi terror groups.[1] It was treated as a domestic terrorism threat in the lead-up to the 2020 presidential election campaign.[1]

## Ideology and reach

The group adheres to accelerationism, a doctrine calling on followers to hasten the collapse of society through acts of terrorism.[1] It is reported to operate in the United States, Canada, Australia, South Africa and Europe, and has been designated a terrorist organization by Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand.[1]

## 2024 activity

In August 2024 The Guardian reported that The Base was attempting to continue covert activities as the presidential election season began, with Nazzaro advertising on Telegram for a stateside leader at a salary of up to $1,200 a month.[1] The same reporting noted that far-right extremists were targeting [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] as she took over the Democratic ticket.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], argued the group is a construct of Western intelligence, noting that "the Base" is the English rendering of al-Qaeda's name and that the set of states designating it as terrorist overlaps almost exactly with the Five Eyes alliance.[1] The commentary compared the arrangement to [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]] and observed that even The Guardian's investigative team had openly speculated as to whether Nazzaro is a federal agent and whether The Base amounts to a honeypot designed to catch unwitting actors.[1] Affiliation with the [[order-of-nine-angles|Order of Nine Angles]] and reports of satanic rituals involving animal sacrifice were cited as further anomalies.[1] The broader concern raised was that such an organization functions to subvert messaging and frame citizen journalists as terrorists.[1]

Property records reported by The Guardian showed three 10-acre blocks of undeveloped land in Washington state bought in December 2018 for $33,000 through a Delaware LLC named "Base Global."[1]
