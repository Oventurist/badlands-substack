# Chris Elston

Chris Elston is a Canadian activist whose social-media post triggered the 2024 confrontation between [[x-corp|X]] and Australia's [[esafety-commissioner|eSafety Commissioner]].[1]

## The 2024 takedown order

Elston wrote a post in which he misgendered and made remarks characterized as "disparaging" about Australian citizen [[teddy-cook|Teddy Cook]].[1] Australia's eSafety Commissioner ordered the post removed, and X faced an $800,000 fine if it did not comply.[1] X geo-blocked the post within Australia, but the action backfired when the post was re-shared in a different format and viewed hundreds of thousands of times.[1] X then announced a legal challenge against the commissioner to "protect its user's right to free speech."[1]
