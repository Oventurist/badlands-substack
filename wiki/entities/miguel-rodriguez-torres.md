# Miguel Rodríguez Torres

Miguel Rodríguez Torres is a retired Venezuelan general named in reported transition proposals for Venezuela following the U.S. operation that detained President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

According to a Miami Herald report archived online and summarized in the Badlands Brief, Qatari intermediaries presented proposals to the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration in April and September that envisaged a transition described as "Madurismo without Maduro."[1] Under that framework, Vice President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] would remain as a continuity figure while Rodríguez Torres led a transitional government, in exchange for access by U.S. companies to Venezuela's oil and mining industries.[1]

The same reported channel — negotiated through [[qatar|Qatar]] with Special Presidential Envoy for Special Missions [[richard-grenell|Ric Grenell]] acting as intermediary — is central to the [[devolution|Devolution]]-style [[continuity-of-government|continuity of government]] theory advanced by Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[1]
