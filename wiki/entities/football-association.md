# Football Association

The Football Association (FA) is the governing body of football in England, responsible for enforcing the laws of the game — including a prohibition on religious messaging appearing on jerseys and equipment.[1]

## Guehi armband case

In December 2024 the FA was understood to be preparing to contact [[crystal-palace-fc|Crystal Palace]] captain [[marc-guehi|Marc Guehi]] to remind him that religious messaging is banned from equipment, after the message "I love Jesus" was added to his Premier League-issued LGBTQ+ campaign rainbow captain's armband during a weekend match.[1] The laws of football prohibit any religious messaging on jerseys, but a formal reminder would avoid the need for disciplinary action; Palace was also to be contacted to be reminded of the regulations.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] criticized the enforcement as arbitrary, arguing that religious messaging is banned "unless the religion is rainbow communism," and that only state-approved religious messaging is permitted in public spaces.[1]
