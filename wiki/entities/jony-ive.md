# Jony Ive

Jony Ive is the former design chief of [[apple|Apple Inc.]] who, as of late 2023, was developing an artificial-intelligence-focused hardware device in tandem with [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]].[1]

Reporting published shortly after Altman's ouster from [[openai|OpenAI]] revealed that he had been raising money for the Ive collaboration alongside a separate AI chip venture code-named [[tigris|Tigris]], holding talks with [[softbank|SoftBank Group Corp.]], Saudi Arabia's [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]], [[mubadala-investment-company|Mubadala Investment Company]] and others as he sought tens of billions of dollars for the new companies.[1]
