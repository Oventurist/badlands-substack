# Peter Szijjarto

Peter Szijjarto is the Hungarian Minister of Foreign Affairs and Trade, serving in the government of Prime Minister [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]].[1]

## Washington engagement and Russia ties

Szijjarto featured in an October 2022 discussion on bringing "peace in Ukraine," co-sponsored by the [[conservative-partnership-institute|Conservative Partnership Institute]], a Washington organization described as a nerve center for incubating policy for a second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1] Attendees — conservative policy and national security officials and GOP strategists — were given pamphlets containing what POLITICO described as unabashedly pro-Russia talking points, calling for US aid to Ukraine to be severely constricted and for President Zelensky to be encouraged toward an armistice and Ukrainian neutrality.[1]

Months before that event, President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] awarded Szijjarto the Order of Friendship, the highest Russian state decoration that can be conferred on a foreigner.[1] The episode was cited by former Republican officials raising concerns over Trump's embrace of Orbán.[1]

## 2024 comments on a second Trump term

In July 2024, Szijjártó told Reuters that [[hungary|Hungary]] believed a second [[donald-trump|Trump]] presidency would boost the chances of achieving peace in Ukraine, calling Trump "the man of peace."[2] The remarks came as Prime Minister [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]] pursued his self-styled "peace mission," which included meetings with Vladimir Putin, Xi Jinping, and Volodymyr Zelensky before a Mar-a-Lago visit to Trump, drawing objections from Hungary's NATO allies and EU leadership.[2]
