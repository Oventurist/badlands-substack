# Jason Galanis

Jason Galanis is an American financier and convicted fraudster who was a business associate of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]. He is serving a 14-year federal prison sentence.[1]

## House Oversight testimony (March 2024)

On Wednesday, March 20, 2024, Galanis testified virtually before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] as part of the congressional Republicans' impeachment inquiry into President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and his family's alleged influence peddling. He appeared by video from Federal Prison Camp in Montgomery, Alabama, a minimum-security facility for male inmates, where he is serving his sentence.[1]

Galanis was one of two former Hunter Biden business associates to testify publicly at the hearing; the other was [[tony-bobulinski|Tony Bobulinski]], a U.S. Navy veteran and former Hunter Biden business partner who appeared in person on Capitol Hill.[1] Hunter Biden himself declined to attend the hearing.[1]

## Context

The hearing, which ran nearly six hours, was characterized in Badlands Media commentary as a proceeding in which Republicans recapped evidence of alleged Biden family corruption while Democrats redirected the discussion toward former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Democrats separately invited Lev Parnas — see [[lev-parnas]] — as their own witness.[1]
