# Amnesty International

Amnesty International is an international human rights organisation, characterised in [[badlands-media|Badlands]] coverage as one of the "leftist human rights organizations" that intervene against socially conservative legislation in Europe.[1]

## Opposition to the Slovak constitutional amendment

In September 2025 Amnesty International condemned a constitutional amendment passed by [[slovakia|Slovakia]]'s parliament recognising only two sexes, male and female, and restricting adoption rights to married couples.[1] In a statement the organisation said: "Today, the Slovak government chose to follow the lead of countries, such as Hungary, whose policies have led to an erosion of human rights."[1] Its objection was echoed by the [[council-of-europe|Council of Europe]]'s Commissioner for Human Rights, Michael O'Flaherty, who argued the amendment denied "the realities of trans and intersex people."[1]
