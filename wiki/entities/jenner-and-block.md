# Jenner & Block

Jenner & Block is a large American law firm at which [[andrew-weissmann|Andrew Weissmann]] became a partner in its New York office after leaving the Enron Task Force at the end of 2005.[1] Weissmann remained in private practice there until 2011, when he returned to the FBI to serve as General Counsel under [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]].[1]

## Executive Order 14246

On March 25, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] issued Executive Order 14246, titled "Addressing Risks From Jenner & Block," which suspended security clearances for the firm's lawyers and restricted their access to government buildings, government officials, and federal contracting work.[1]

The Badlands Media feature "Enjoy the Show" argues that the order was not aimed at the firm in the abstract but at Weissmann personally, drawing a direct parallel to Trump's earlier executive action against [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]], which the article reads as a strike at [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] through the firm that retained [[fusion-gps|Fusion GPS]].[1] In the article's framing, targeting a law firm is a way of targeting the individual conspirator sheltered inside it.[1]
