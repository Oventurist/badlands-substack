# The Federalist

The Federalist is an American conservative online magazine frequently cited by Badlands Media for its coverage of election administration and legislative developments in the states.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

In March 2024 The Federalist reported on the passage of [[louisiana-senate-bill-101|Louisiana Senate Bill 101]] through the state Senate, legislation prohibiting [[ranked-choice-voting|ranked-choice voting]] in Louisiana elections, and provided the account of sponsor Blake Miguez's arguments that "complex tabulated ballots" are "un-American."[1] The outlet had previously covered Louisiana's constitutional amendment banning "Zuckbucks," which Miguez also sponsored, as well as the State Freedom Caucus Network's influence on Republican state legislatures.[1]
