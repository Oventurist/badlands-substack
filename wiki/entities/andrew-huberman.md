# Andrew Huberman

**Andrew Huberman** is cited in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for his research on neuroplasticity.[1]

## Role in the article

The essay states that Huberman's work reveals that the brain resists transformation, and that letting go of old belief systems triggers the same neurological responses as physical pain.[1] This is used to explain the grief that the article says frequently accompanies awakening but is rarely discussed — a "quiet storm" in which a person may mourn a job that once defined them, friendships that fade as their frequency shifts, or the innocence of a world they thought they knew.[1]

Huberman's neuroplasticity finding is paired in the text with a line attributed to Rumi, "The wound is the place where the light enters you," to frame grief as a crack in the old self that makes space for the true self.[1] The article also draws on neuroplasticity in its practical guidance, recommending readers replace a limiting thought such as "I can't" with an expansive one such as "I'm learning how."[1]
