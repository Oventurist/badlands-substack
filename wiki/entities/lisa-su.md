# Lisa Su

Lisa Su is a Taiwanese-born American electrical engineer and business executive who serves as chair and chief executive of Advanced Micro Devices (AMD). She is credited with reversing AMD's competitive decline and positioning the company as a significant supplier of central processors and accelerators for data center and artificial intelligence workloads, competing with [[nvidia|Nvidia]].

Su was named in March 2026 among the appointees to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], as reported in the March 26 [[badlands-brief-143|Badlands Brief]]. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] placed her among appointees outside the [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] network.
