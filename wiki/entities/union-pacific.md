# Union Pacific

Union Pacific is a major American freight railroad.[1]

## 2023 missing ammonium nitrate shipment

Union Pacific was one of the parties investigating the disappearance of approximately 61,000 pounds of ammonium nitrate shipped by rail from Cheyenne, Wyoming, to Saltdale, California, in April 2023.[1] The railcar, loaded and sealed by [[dyno-nobel|Dyno Nobel]], arrived at its Mojave Desert destination still sealed but completely empty.[1] Alongside Union Pacific, the [[federal-railroad-administration|Federal Railroad Administration]], the [[california-public-utilities-commission|California Public Utilities Commission]] and Dyno Nobel itself examined the loss, and the railcar was routed back to Wyoming for a thorough inspection.[1] The car reportedly made multiple stops during its two-week journey, which Badlands commentary argued provided opportunity for foul play.[1]
