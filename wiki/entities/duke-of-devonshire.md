# Duke of Devonshire

The Duke of Devonshire is a title of the British aristocracy that appears in accounts of the Prohibition-era liquor trade.[1]

The Duke was responsible for [[joseph-p-kennedy|Joseph Kennedy]] obtaining his British liquor wholesaling rights during [[prohibition|Prohibition]].[1] Kennedy subsequently married his daughter Kathleen into the Devonshire family; her husband was killed in the Second World War four months after their wedding.[1]
