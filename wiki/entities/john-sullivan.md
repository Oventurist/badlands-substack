# John Sullivan

John Sullivan, known online as "JaydenX," is an American activist and videographer who was present at the United States Capitol on January 6, 2021 and who figures in [[badlands-media]] commentary as a companion figure to [[ray-epps]] in arguments that federal authorities treated certain January 6 participants very differently from ordinary defendants.[1]

## Association with Ray Epps

According to commentary published by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in the [[badlands-news-brief]], on the night of January 5, 2021 Epps shouted at a public gathering — in Sullivan's company — that "Tomorrow, we're gonna go into the Capitol!", prompting the surrounding crowd to chant "Fed! Fed! Fed!"[1] Badlands commentary treats the pairing of the two men as significant because both were, in its telling, conspicuously spared the aggressive federal treatment applied to other participants.[1]

## Capitol footage and aftermath

Sullivan filmed himself inside the Capitol Rotunda declaring "We did it!" during the riot, yet Badlands commentary asserts he was effectively ignored by federal investigators despite that recording.[1] The same commentary states that Sullivan was paid more than $70,000 for licensing his January 6 video footage and subsequently worked as a war correspondent in Ukraine — a trajectory the author cited sardonically as fitting the pattern of protected actors within the January 6 narrative.[1]
