# Christopher L. Paris

Colonel Christopher L. Paris is the Commissioner of the [[pennsylvania-state-police|Pennsylvania State Police]].[1]

## Testimony on the Butler assassination attempt

Paris appeared before the [[house-homeland-security-committee|House Homeland Security Committee]] on Tuesday, July 23, 2024, as part of the committee's assessment of the July 13, 2024 assassination attempt on former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at a rally in Pennsylvania.[1] Because the FBI and the Department of Homeland Security declined to appear, congressional questioning was concentrated on Paris and on Patrick Yoes, National President of the [[fraternal-order-of-police|Fraternal Order of Police]].[1]

Badlands coverage characterized the hearing as unproductive: witnesses were limited in what they would answer, citing the ongoing investigation and the incomplete after-action review, and "I can't comment" and "I can't respond" were reported as the two most common responses.[1] No specific timelines were provided and no details were given on the assignment of security resources.[1]

The hearing opened with moments of silence for [[sheila-jackson-lee|Sheila Jackson Lee]], for [[corey-comperatore|Corey Comperatore]] — the rally attendee killed by the shooter — and for those injured in the attack.[1]
