# Bob Menendez

**Bob Menendez** is a Democratic United States senator from New Jersey.[1]

## Departure from the 2024 cycle

Chapter 4 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series lists Menendez among the members of Congress not seeking re-election in 2024, noting that he was under indictment for accepting bribes.[1] The chapter groups him with [[george-santos|George Santos]], who had been expelled from Congress, at the head of a long list of retirements and departures spanning both parties.[1] The author treats the scale and partisan balance of that list as a "future proves past" confirmation of Q drop #24 of November 1, 2017, which had spoken of politicians stepping away, and observes that a number of the names are not America First or MAGA — while stopping short of claiming that all of them are corrupt or being compelled to leave.[1] The reading is tied to the series' broader argument that many drops function as six-year [[q-markers|deltas]].[1]

## Cited in the October 2023 "coincidences" list

Badlands writer [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] included Menendez in a list of events he refuses to treat as coincidence in the month preceding the October 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack on [[israel|Israel]], noting that "Senator Menendez has been charged with taking bribes and acting as a foreign agent to Egypt."[2] The item sat alongside [[joe-biden|Biden]] freeing $6 billion to [[iran|Iran]], Iran's claimed responsibility for the [[1983-beirut-barracks-bombings|1983 Beirut bombing]], and reports of people on the terrorist watch list crossing the US-Mexican border.[2]
