# Morgan McSweeney

Morgan McSweeney is a British political operative and the founder of the [[center-for-countering-digital-hate|Center for Countering Digital Hate]] (CCDH), who by October 2024 was reported to be advising the [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] presidential campaign.[1]

## The "kill Musk's Twitter" leak

McSweeney's dual role became the pivot of an October 2024 story reported by journalists Paul D. Thacker and [[matt-taibbi|Matt Taibbi]] through The Disinformation Chronicle and Racket News, and amplified by ZeroHedge.[1] Leaked internal documents from CCDH showed the organization planning, in writing, to "kill Musk's Twitter" while strengthening its ties to the Biden/Harris administration and to Democrats including Senator [[amy-klobuchar|Amy Klobuchar]], who has introduced multiple bills to regulate online "misinformation."[1] Klobuchar's office did not respond to a request for comment.[1]

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] responded to the reporting with the two-word statement "This is war."[1] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the leak as confirmation that a foreign political operator embedded in a U.S. presidential campaign was working to dismantle an American speech platform — "The British are coming, to meddle in our elections!" as the original report put it.[1]
