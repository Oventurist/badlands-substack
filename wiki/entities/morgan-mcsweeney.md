# Morgan McSweeney

Morgan McSweeney is a British political operative who served as chief of staff to Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]]. He is best known as one of the principal architects of Starmer's control of the Labour Party and of the party's return to government.

## Departure amid Epstein-related scrutiny

The February 10, 2026 edition of the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], "[[badlands-brief-4ec|Criminal Invader Crashouts & Honeypot Hoaxes]]," reported that Starmer had lost senior aides — McSweeney among them, together with communications director [[tim-allan|Tim Allan]] — amid scrutiny tied to associations with the late financier [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]. The Brief cited media reporting, including in *The Economist*, indicating that Starmer was under pressure over questions of judgment, vetting, and institutional accountability following the departures. No charges have been reported against Starmer or any of his staff, and the Brief presented the resignations as part of a broader transatlantic pattern of [[epstein-narrative|Epstein-related]] political fallout unfolding in parallel with the stalled congressional inquiry in the United States.
