# Ralph Baric

Ralph Baric is an American virologist and professor at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, known for decades of research on coronaviruses, including gain-of-function experiments on SARS and related pathogens. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears as one of the principal targets of the 2026 COVID origins reckoning against scientists affiliated with the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]].

## Federal debarment referral (April 2026)

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that in April 2026 Baric was referred for a three-year federal debarment over paperwork inconsistencies and decade-old gain-of-function experiments. The referral is presented as part of a series of legal and administrative actions — debarments, indictments, arrests, removals from leadership and congressional subpoenas — pursued by proponents of the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]], including figures in the Trump administration, against scientists they claim hid evidence of a possible laboratory origin of COVID-19 in Wuhan, China. The Brief groups Baric's case with those of [[david-morens|David Morens]], [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]], [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]] and [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] as five cases centered on the small community of NIAID-affiliated scientists, with enforcement shifting toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].
