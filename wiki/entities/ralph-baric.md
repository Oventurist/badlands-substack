# Ralph Baric

Ralph Baric is an American virologist known for coronavirus research, including gain-of-function experiments.[1] In April 2026 he was referred for a three-year federal debarment over paperwork inconsistencies and decade-old gain-of-function experiments.[1]

The referral was among the first in a series of legal and administrative actions taken against U.S. scientists tied to COVID-19 research and alleged efforts to obscure the virus's origins, a campaign advanced by proponents of the lab-leak theory including figures in the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1] Related actions touched scientists affiliated with the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]], including [[david-morens|David Morens]], [[jeffrey-taubenberger|Jeffrey Taubenberger]], and [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]].[1]
