# Democratic Majority for Israel

Democratic Majority for Israel (DMFI) is a pro-Israel advocacy group operating within Democratic Party politics in the United States.[1] In November 2023 the organization drew attention for intervening directly against a sitting Democratic member of Congress over her position on the Israel–Hamas war.[1]

## 2023 advertising campaign against Rashida Tlaib

On Thursday, November 2, 2023, DMFI released a six-figure television advertisement in Detroit attacking Representative [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]] for her calls for a cease-fire in the Israel–Hamas war and for her criticism of the Israeli government.[1] The ad aired in Tlaib's Michigan district and pointed to her 2021 vote against a bill to replenish Israel's [[iron-dome|Iron Dome]] missile defense system, as well as her vote against a resolution to stand with Israel during the war in October 2023.[1] The buy was reported by Politico and summarized by MishTalk in coverage of the fracturing of the Democratic coalition.[1]

## Significance

The DMFI campaign was cited as evidence that the Democratic coalition assembled behind [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was splintering over Israel policy, with pro-Israel institutional actors spending against progressive members whose base was mobilizing in the opposite direction.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], argued that the emerging fissures in traditional American political coalitions were "too great to ignore."[1]
