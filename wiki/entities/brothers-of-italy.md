# Brothers of Italy

Brothers of Italy (Fratelli d'Italia) is the ultraconservative Italian political party led by Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]].[1]

## Ties to the American right

Before becoming prime minister, Meloni forged links with MAGA ideologues at Republican gatherings, and her party apparatus has quietly worked behind the scenes to reinvigorate ties with the Republican Party.[1] Antonio Giordano, a Brothers of Italy MP who attended the 2024 Republican National Convention, said Meloni would be the "natural interlocutor" for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] if he "wants to understand how to better deal with Europe."[1] While most European leaders were anxious about a Trump return to the White House, Meloni's allies believed she would emerge as his go-to European partner, boosting her standing with EU peers with whom she has had fractious relations.[1]

## Domestic messaging

In September 2024 the party posted on X: "We have formed an alliance with businesses and citizens, choosing to walk alongside those who wanted to do something and supporting investments and employment. By betting on the potential of this nation we have demonstrated that every challenge is within our reach."[1]
