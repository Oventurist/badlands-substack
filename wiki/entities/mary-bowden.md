# Mary Bowden

Mary Bowden is an American physician and one of three doctors who sued the [[food-and-drug-administration|U.S. Food and Drug Administration]] in 2022 over the agency's public messaging discouraging the use of [[ivermectin|ivermectin]] as a treatment for COVID-19.[1]

## Litigation against the FDA

The suit filed by Bowden and two other doctors in 2022 was initially dismissed, but the case received new life when the 5th Circuit Court of Appeals revived it and remanded it back to the lower court.[1] On review, the lower court determined that only Bowden, of the original plaintiffs, had standing in the matter.[1]

Bowden emphasized that the FDA article titled "Why You Should Not Use Ivermectin to Treat or Prevent COVID-19" failed to mention that doctors retained the legal authority to prescribe ivermectin despite the agency's discouragement of its use.[1]

## Outcome

In March 2024 the FDA agreed to take down the social media posts and online materials cautioning against ivermectin use, including the post reading "You are not a horse. You are not a cow. Seriously, y'all. Stop it," which linked to the disputed article.[1] The agency was given a three-week deadline to remove the material and posts from various platforms.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the outcome as vindication for the thousands of doctors who had told the public that effective early COVID-19 treatments existed and who were, in her account, banned from the public square, stripped of medical licenses, or in at least one case imprisoned.[1]
