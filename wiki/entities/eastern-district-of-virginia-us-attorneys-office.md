# U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Virginia

The **U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Virginia** is the federal prosecutor's office that in late 2025 brought criminal charges against former FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] and subsequently experienced significant internal upheaval.[1]

## October 2025 departures

Two senior federal prosecutors departed the office in early October 2025, adding to what reporting described as mounting turmoil inside the office handling the Comey case.[1] [[michael-benary|Michael Ben'Ary]], who led the office's national security section, was reportedly fired after conservative journalist [[julie-kelly|Julie Kelly]] publicized his prior service as senior counsel to former Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]].[1] Kelly framed the departures as necessary to the de-weaponization of the Justice Department promised by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], arguing that officials who advised "the very architects of the lawfare against the president" could not remain in powerful positions.[1]

Kelly suggested Ben'Ary might have belonged to an "internal resistance" to the Comey prosecution, though a person familiar with the matter told the *Associated Press* that he had no involvement in the case.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright treated the churn inside the office as part of a broader convergence, writing that "Comey is the beginning of something" and asking how one would tell the story of a RICO investigation "in a way that's going to be digestible."[1] He characterized the moment as "pure narrative warfare."[1]
