# George Simion

George Simion is the leader of the Alliance for the Union of Romanians (AUR), a nationalist Romanian political party.[1]

## March 2025 Bucharest rally

Simion's party organized the rally in Bucharest's Victory Square on Saturday, March 1, 2025, at which tens of thousands of demonstrators demanded that the second round of the annulled Romanian presidential election be held rather than a new vote.[1] The rally drew participants from across the country, and both Simion and [[calin-georgescu|Calin Georgescu]] were in attendance; organizers claimed nearly a million people turned out, although several media outlets reported lower figures.[1] Badlands commentary characterized the peaceful assembly as an act of defiance against the Romanian "Deep State."[1]
