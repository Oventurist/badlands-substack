# George Simion

George Simion is the leader of the Alliance for the Union of Romanians (AUR), a nationalist Romanian political party.[1]

## March 2025 Bucharest rally

Simion's party organized the rally in Bucharest's Victory Square on Saturday, March 1, 2025, at which tens of thousands of demonstrators demanded that the second round of the annulled Romanian presidential election be held rather than a new vote.[1] The rally drew participants from across the country, and both Simion and [[calin-georgescu|Calin Georgescu]] were in attendance; organizers claimed nearly a million people turned out, although several media outlets reported lower figures.[1] Badlands commentary characterized the peaceful assembly as an act of defiance against the Romanian "Deep State."[1]

## January 2025 protest against the annulled election

At a mass demonstration in Bucharest on Sunday, January 12, 2025, Simion, leader of the [[alliance-for-the-union-of-romanians|Alliance for the Unity of Romanians]], addressed tens of thousands protesting the Constitutional Court's annulment of the presidential election won in its first round by [[calin-georgescu|Calin Georgescu]].[2] "We are protesting against the coup d'état that took place on Dec. 6," Simion said. "We are sorry to discover so late that we were living in a lie and that we were led by people who claimed to be democrats, but are not at all."[2] Demonstrators waved Romanian flags and carried placards reading "Democracy is not optional" and "We want free elections," with many demanding the race resume from the second round.[2]

## Defeat in the 2025 rerun

Simion, the Eurosceptic candidate, lost Romania's presidential election rerun to pro-EU Bucharest mayor [[nicusor-dan|Nicusor Dan]], receiving 46% of the vote to Dan's 54% with more than 99% of ballots counted.[3] Badlands commentary framed his defeat as suspicious given that he had emerged as the successor to an earlier populist candidate whose victory was annulled and who was subsequently arrested, and who had appeared to draw that entire base of support.[3]

## Challenge to the rerun result

In a post on X on Tuesday, May 20, 2025, Simion said he had "officially" asked [[romania|Romania]]'s top court to annul the result of that Sunday's runoff "for the very reasons the December elections were annulled," claiming the vote had been compromised by foreign interference and flagging France and Moldova in particular.[4] The runoff had seen pro-EU Bucharest mayor [[nicusor-dan|Nicusor Dan]] defeat Simion with 54% of the vote, in a rerun ordered after Romania's Constitutional Court annulled the November election in which [[calin-georgescu|Calin Georgescu]], an EU and NATO critic, finished first with 23%; authorities had cited "irregularities" and intelligence reports alleging Russian interference, which Moscow denied.[4]

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] agreed that external influence had shaped the outcome but identified its source differently, writing that Georgescu had dominated the first round only to see the results annulled, the follow-up cancelled, and himself arrested and barred from Romanian elections by the courts.[4] Simion, "the next man up in the nationalist-populist movement," then dominated pre-election optics and polling before losing under strange circumstances to a candidate who is pro-EU, anti-Russia, and aligned with the globalist uniparty agenda — leading him to conclude the interference most likely came from the U.S. [[state-department|State Department]] and the [[european-union|European Union]].[4]
