# Jagmeet Singh

Jagmeet Singh is the leader of Canada's New Democratic Party (NDP), a social-democratic party positioned to the left of the governing Liberals.[1]

## 2025 federal election campaign

During the opening day of the [[2025-canadian-federal-election|2025 Canadian federal election]] campaign, Singh cast the NDP as the political underdog and argued that his party was best positioned to take on U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], framing the contest as a battle to protect workers' livelihoods amid the economic disruption caused by American tariffs on [[canada|Canada]].[1] His pitch contrasted with the tax-cut messaging offered the same day by Liberal leader [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] and Conservative leader [[pierre-poilievre|Pierre Poilievre]].[1]

## Electoral reform planning with Trudeau

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described Singh as controlled opposition and reported that his team and that of [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] had been quietly preparing [[electoral-reform-in-canada|electoral reform legislation]].[2] The reported package would allow an expanded three-day voting period in general elections, permit voters to cast ballots at any polling place within their riding, and revise the [[mail-in-voting|mail-in ballot]] process in the name of accessibility and integrity.[2] Deschamps argued these measures would sound disturbingly familiar to Americans critical of the [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election]], and would make it easier to preserve the minority-government asymmetry that has kept Trudeau in power.[2]
