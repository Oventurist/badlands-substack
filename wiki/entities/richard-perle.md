# Richard Perle

**Richard Perle** is an American defense policy advisor identified by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as one of the key members of the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] (PNAC), the neoconservative think tank active from 1997 to 2006 that DeLarme describes as the primary architect of the U.S.-led forever wars of the 1990s and 2000s.[1]

## Role in the neoconservative network

DeLarme describes Perle as a Jewish defense policy advisor who co-authored the 1996 "Clean Break" paper and PNAC letters advocating regime change in Iraq.[1] He characterizes Perle as a vocal supporter of [[israel|Israel]], framing the country as a bulwark against terrorism.[1] Perle appears in the essay alongside [[william-kristol|William Kristol]], [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]], [[paul-wolfowitz|Paul Wolfowitz]], [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]], [[elliott-abrams|Elliott Abrams]] and [[norman-podhoretz|Norman Podhoretz]] as figures remembered by contemporaneous reporters as the quintessential neocon warmongers, most of whom had strong ties to Israel and helped reinforce the U.S.-Israel relationship now under heavy scrutiny.[1]

## Relevance to the MAGA schism

Perle's inclusion in the essay serves DeLarme's broader argument in [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]] that [[neoconservatives|neoconservatism]] and [[zionism|Zionism]] are deeply intertwined with little meaningful separation between them, and that the apparent alignment of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration with the neocons of yesteryear sits at odds with Trump's stated commitment to ending forever wars.[1]
