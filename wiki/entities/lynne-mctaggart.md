# Lynne McTaggart

**Lynne McTaggart**, author of *The Field*, is cited in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay attributes to McTaggart the position that intention and emotion do not remain confined within the brain but radiate into the quantum field, influencing others through what the article calls [[morphic-resonance|morphic resonance]].[1] From this the author draws the conclusion that a person in the [[liminality|liminal]] state is "not just waiting in silence" but "broadcasting coherence."[1]

McTaggart's citation opens the essay's section "The Collective Grid – Why Your Frequency Matters Now More Than Ever," which argues that liminal isolation is only apparent, and is followed by [[konstantin-korotkov|Konstantin Korotkov]]'s [[biofield-imaging|biofield imaging]] research.[1]
