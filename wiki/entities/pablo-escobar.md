# Pablo Escobar

Pablo Escobar headed the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín cartel]], which in the late 1980s was the most powerful drug-trafficking organization in the world.[1] The article names him among [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]]'s cartel connections and states that his money helped finance the Nicaraguan contras in exchange for CIA-supplied weapons.[1]

As Colombian authorities intensified their pursuit and violence rose through the decade, Escobar and the Colombian government entered indirect negotiations by 1988, with cartel leaders proposing a deal to preserve their wealth and end the manhunt.[1] In 1989 they sent Vallejo to Miami to approach a lawyer working with corporate attorneys headed by Jeb Bush, hoping he would persuade the president's son to raise the proposal privately with his father.[1]

The negotiation collapsed after one of the most notorious political killings in Colombian history: the August 1989 assassination of [[luis-galan|Luis Galán]], the leading presidential candidate, who had promised to extradite traffickers to the United States. The killing was orchestrated in part by Escobar and reportedly at the urging of Galán's political rivals, and the article argues it is what made Escobar expendable.[1]
