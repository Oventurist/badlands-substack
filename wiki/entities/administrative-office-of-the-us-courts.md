# Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts

The **Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts** is the agency that provides administrative support to the federal judiciary and serves as the operational arm of the [[united-states-judicial-conference|U.S. Judicial Conference]].[1] As of 2025 its director was [[robert-conrad|Robert J. Conrad]].[1]

## 2025 litigation

The Administrative Office was named as a defendant, through Conrad, in a lawsuit filed by the [[america-first-legal|America First Legal Foundation]] against Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]].[1] The complaint accuses the office and the Judicial Conference of undertaking regulatory actions beyond the scope of resolving cases or controversies or administratively supporting that work, which the plaintiffs identify as the judiciary's "core functions."[1] The suit also contends that records held by these bodies should be subject to Freedom of Information Act requests.[1]
