# Donda Sports

Donda Sports is the sports fashion brand created by the musician and designer [[kanye-west|Kanye West]].[1] The organization operates as a sports-focused extension of West's broader Donda branding.

Badlands Media noted in October 2024 that retired NFL wide receiver [[antonio-brown|Antonio Brown]] serves as the current president of Donda Sports, meaning that Brown — then publicly campaigning for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and offering to serve in a future Trump administration — works for West.[1] Badlands treated the connection as a notable convergence of two cultural storylines, writing that the "Yevolution" was "not only back on the menu, but merging with the Trumpamania storyline."[1]
