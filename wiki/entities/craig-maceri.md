# Craig Maceri

Brig. Gen. Craig Maceri is a US Army general officer associated with the [[district-of-columbia-national-guard|District of Columbia National Guard]].[1]

On the activation of the [[260th-special-purpose-brigade|260th Special Purpose Brigade]] in March 2026, Maceri said the brigade provides a command structure to manage a wide range of missions while ensuring rapid coordination with federal and local agencies during major events, emergencies, and security operations.[1]
