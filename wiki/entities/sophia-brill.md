# Sophia Brill

**Sophia Brill** is an attorney with the National Security Division of the [[department-of-justice|United States Department of Justice]], identified in the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 as the recipient of an internal email sent two days after the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago raid]].

## The Stemler email

According to the source, [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]] wrote to Brill on August 10, 2022 raising concerns about whether [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had declassified the documents seized by the [[fbi|FBI]] on August 8, 2022, and observing that a president acting as Commander in Chief is not necessarily bound by standard declassification procedures. Brill's role in the reported exchange is as the National Security Division contact to whom those doubts were addressed; the article does not describe her reply.
