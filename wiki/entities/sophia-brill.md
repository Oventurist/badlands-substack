# Sophia Brill

Sophia Brill is an attorney in the National Security Division of the U.S. [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]].[1]

Brill is identified as the recipient of an August 10, 2022 internal email from senior DOJ official [[patty-stemler|Patty Stemler]], sent two days after the [[fbi|FBI]] raid on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] residence, in which Stemler questioned whether Trump had declassified the seized documents and observed that the president as Commander in Chief is not necessarily bound by standard declassification procedures.[1] The exchange, obtained by Just the News, surfaced during the Department's review of federal law enforcement actions tied to the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of government]] investigation.[1]
