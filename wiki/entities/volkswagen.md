# Volkswagen

Volkswagen is a German automobile manufacturer and the largest carmaker in [[germany|Germany]] by output. It appears in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] among the German manufacturers undertaking major cost-cutting programmes during the 2026 contraction of German industry.

The Brief reported that Volkswagen, along with [[mercedes-benz|Mercedes-Benz]], [[porsche|Porsche]] and [[bmw|BMW]], had announced significant restructuring as the sector contended with declining demand, weaker sales in [[china|China]], the transition to electric vehicles, elevated energy costs and international trade pressures. The reporting placed these measures against the backdrop of the [[federation-of-german-industries|Federation of German Industries]]' estimate that German industry was losing roughly 15,000 jobs per month.
