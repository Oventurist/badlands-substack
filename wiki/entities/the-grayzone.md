# The Grayzone

The Grayzone is an independent American news website known for adversarial reporting on U.S. foreign policy.

## In the Badlands corpus

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] cites The Grayzone as an example of the decentralized media whose subject matter the corporate press will not touch: "you won't see the corporate hacks at [[cnn|CNN]] reporting on things you might see on The Grayzone or [[consortium-news|Consortium News]]."

The outlet is also offered as evidence that opposition to independent journalism extends into the financial system. The article notes, citing Al Jazeera, that GoFundMe froze donations to The Grayzone, an episode it presents alongside platform-level restrictions as part of the broader [[censorship-industrial-complex|Censorship Industrial Complex]] arrayed against [[citizen-journalism|citizen journalists]].
