# Jeff Booth

**Jeff Booth** is a technology entrepreneur and the author of *The Price of Tomorrow*, a book cited in Badlands Media commentary as a framework for understanding the exponential pace of technological change and the economic hazards that accompany it.[1]

## *The Price of Tomorrow* in Badlands commentary

In the April 2023 essay "Don't Bring Shiny Rocks to Digital Gunfights," [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] recounted reading Booth's book at the same time as Major [[jason-lowery|Jason Lowery]]'s [[softwar|Softwar]], describing the two works as converging rabbit holes.[1] Booth, the essay noted, treats the technological topics at issue in greater technical detail and discusses further hazards on the horizon — deflation among them — that societies must learn to control.[1]

The essay quoted Booth's summary of Moore's law: that most people are generally aware of the observation that the number of transistors on a printed circuit board continues to double every two years, producing a corresponding doubling of computing power.[1] From this the Badlands author drew the inference that technological change is exponential, with small-scale effects early and large-scale effects late, and that the world has now entered the later stages where effects become dramatically more noticeable while newly matured technologies add further chaos.[1]

On [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] specifically, the essay quoted Booth's observation that "the explosion in knowledge and positive feedback loop from learning is accelerating to the point that we are finding it hard to keep up with the changes."[1] The author used the passage to argue that people can either come up to speed on these technologies by choice now or be forced to do so later by necessity, far behind the learning curve, since history shows there is no stopping such technologies.[1]
