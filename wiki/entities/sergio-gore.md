# Sergio Gore

Sergio Gore is a United States diplomat serving as Presidential Special Envoy for South and Central Asia in the second [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1]

On Monday, September 22, 2025, Gore met in New York with Kazakh President [[kassym-jomart-tokayev|Kassym-Jomart Tokayev]], who congratulated him on his appointment and pledged support for his mission.[1] At the same meeting Tokayev reaffirmed [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]]'s commitment to advancing the Kazakh-American strategic partnership and emphasized his country's role as Washington's main economic partner in Central Asia.[1] The meeting occurred the same day President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced a $4 billion Kazakh purchase of American locomotives and rail equipment, and coincided with Tokayev's meetings alongside a [[meta-platforms|Meta]] vice president.[1]
