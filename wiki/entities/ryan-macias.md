# Ryan Macias

Ryan Macias is an election-administration consultant who has become a recurring figure in Badlands coverage of the NGO layer surrounding American election operations.[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported, while a Colorado Clerks meeting was still in progress, that Macias was present at the meeting pitching the clerks to fund a new NGO of his — at what she describes as "literally the same moment" he was being implicated in willful election fraud in Georgia.[1]

The Georgia allegation concerns a recount in which the totals reportedly did not match; the numbers were allegedly sent to Macias, after which they matched.[1] At the time of that allegation Macias was affiliated with The Elections Group, another election-services NGO.[1]

The reporting of his presence prompted a reprimand of Colorado clerks by Matt Crane, described by Ashe as their "handler," on the stated grounds that "we're not safe if people know where we are."[1] Ashe noted that she had never disclosed the meeting's location, only that Macias was there, and argued that embarrassment rather than any genuine safety fear motivated the reprimand.[1] Sources familiar with the reprimand told her Colorado clerks are no longer permitted to post to social media during their meetings.[1]

## December 3, 2020 email (Rossi presentation)

At the January 21, 2026 [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] hearing, investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]] testified that a report generated at 12:06 p.m. on Thursday, December 3, 2020 was emailed at 12:13 p.m. to Macias, whom Rossi described as "maybe a consultant that worked for The Election Group."[2] Rossi said he did not know why the report was sent to Macias but speculated the message conveyed that Fulton County was "only at 511" and short of the needed count.[2] He linked the exchange to testimony from Fulton County elections director [[richard-barron|Richard Barron]] that officials "knew we were short" and had "called the state."[2]
