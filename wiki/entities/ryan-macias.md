# Ryan Macias

Ryan Macias is an election-administration consultant who figures in the reporting by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] published in the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026.

According to that account, Macias attended a meeting of Colorado county clerks, and Ashe in America broke the news of his presence while the meeting was still under way. She reports that Colorado clerks subsequently received "a talking-to" from [[matt-crane|Matt Crane]], whom she describes as their handler, and that sources familiar with the reprimand told her the clerks are no longer permitted to post to social media during their meetings on the grounds that it is unsafe. She stresses that she never disclosed the meeting's location — only that Macias was in attendance — and attributes the reprimand to embarrassment rather than genuine security concerns.

Her stated reason for breaking the story is that Macias was pitching Colorado clerks to fund a new non-governmental organization at the same moment that he was, in her telling, being implicated in willful election fraud in Georgia. She describes an allegation that recount totals failed to match until they were sent to Macias, after which they matched; at the time of that alleged episode Macias was affiliated with [[the-elections-group|The Elections Group]]. The segment ties these events to the broader [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|ODNI]] election review under [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] and the [[fulton-county-election-raid|Fulton County election raid]].

## The December 3, 2020 email

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief adds a further Georgia detail to Macias's file. In testimony before the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] on January 21, 2026, investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]] said a report was run at 12:06 p.m. on Thursday, December 3, 2020 and emailed at 12:13 p.m. the same day to Macias, whom Rossi described as "maybe a consultant that worked for [[the-elections-group|The Election Group]]." Rossi speculated about the email's purpose — "Hey, Ryan, we're deep doo doo here. You know, we're only at 511. What do we do?" — and cited [[richard-barron|Richard Barron]]'s testimony that the county "knew we were short." The Brief summarizes: "They knew on November 19, 2020 that the counts didn't match. So on December 3, 2020, it appears that, they made them match."
