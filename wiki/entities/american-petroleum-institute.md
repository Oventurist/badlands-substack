# American Petroleum Institute

The American Petroleum Institute (API) is the principal trade association of the United States oil and natural gas industry.[1]

## Opposition to the LNG permit pause

In January 2024 API chief executive Mike Sommers publicly criticized the [[biden-lng-export-permit-pause|Biden administration's pause on new liquefied natural gas export approvals]], saying in a statement that "there is no review needed to understand the clear benefits of U.S. LNG for stabilizing global energy markets, supporting thousands of American jobs, and reducing emissions around the world by transitioning countries toward cleaner fuels."[1] He characterized the decision as "nothing more than a broken promise to U.S. allies," adding that "it's time for the administration to stop playing politics with global energy security."[1] API's position aligned with energy analysts who argued the delay benefited America's adversaries — notably Russia and Iran — while harming European allies that had turned to U.S. supply to reduce dependence on Moscow.[1]
