# France terre d'asile

France terre d'asile is a French non-governmental organization working on asylum and migration, and one of the small group of publicly funded NGOs authorized to provide legal assistance inside France's migrant detention centres.[1]

In May 2025 the organization was named alongside [[cimade|Cimade]] as a target of a Senate bill tabled by [[les-republicains|Les Républicains]], which sought to end their exclusive role advising undocumented migrants awaiting expulsion in the Centres de Rétention Administrative.[1] Interior minister [[bruno-retailleau|Bruno Retailleau]] argued that responsibility should pass to the [[french-office-for-immigration-and-integration|French Office for Immigration and Integration]] or to a magistrate in order to restore impartiality, contending that publicly funded activists were obstructing government deportation policy.[1]
