# Otto Kahn

Otto Kahn was an international banker and financier listed by Badlands Media among the primary funders of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1]

"The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" places Kahn alongside [[paul-warburg|Paul Warburg]], [[jacob-schiff|Jacob Schiff]], [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]] and representatives of the family of [[jp-morgan-jr|J.P. Morgan Jr.]] as backers of the newly incorporated Council in 1921.[1] The article observes that these same funders had all been intimately involved with both the [[panic-of-1907|Panic of 1907]] and the ensuing creation of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[1]
