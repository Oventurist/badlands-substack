# Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan

Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan is an Emirati royal and vice president of the United Arab Emirates.[1]

He is the primary owner of [[international-media-investments|International Media Investments]], the UAE firm that partnered with [[redbird-capital-partners|RedBird Capital Partners]] in the RedBird IMI venture headed by [[jeff-zucker|Jeff Zucker]] to acquire [[the-telegraph|The Telegraph]].[1] His ownership of IMI was central to British concerns about foreign state influence over the newspaper, prompting the Conservative government to pass legislation in 2023 curbing foreign state ownership of British media and derailing the RedBird IMI purchase.[1]
