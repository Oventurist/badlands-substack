# Paul Wolfowitz

Paul Wolfowitz is a neoconservative official listed by Badlands Media among the members of the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]], the think tank founded in 1997 by [[william-kristol|William Kristol]] and [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]] with ties to the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]].[1]

Alongside [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]], [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], and [[donald-rumsfeld|Donald Rumsfeld]], Wolfowitz belonged to the group whose signatories heavily staffed the George W. Bush administration.[1] The article ties PNAC's program of [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] and its designs on Iraq, Iran, Libya, and Syria to the events that ended in the ouster of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] in December 2024.[1]
