# Paul Wolfowitz

Paul Wolfowitz is an American foreign policy official closely identified with the neoconservative movement and the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]].

## In the corpus

[[assad-day-in-damascus]] names Wolfowitz among the figures who populated PNAC following its 1997 creation by [[william-kristol]] and [[robert-kagan]], listing him alongside [[dick-cheney]], [[john-bolton]] and [[donald-rumsfeld]]. The article notes that fourteen of the group's twenty-five original signatories obtained posts in the George W. Bush administration.

Wolfowitz's presence in that roster is cited as evidence for the article's argument that the takeover of Libya, Iraq, Iran and [[syria]] in pursuit of [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] was a stated ambition of the American national security establishment long before the 2024 collapse of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]]'s government.
