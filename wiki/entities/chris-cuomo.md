# Chris Cuomo

Chris Cuomo is a former [[cnn|CNN]] anchor who later joined NewsNation.[1] Aged 53 in 2024, he was among the most prominent television figures associated with COVID-era coverage.[2]

## COVID vaccine injury disclosure

In a segment on his NewsNation program reported in early May 2024, Cuomo disclosed that he was himself suffering from a health condition following a COVID-19 vaccination.[2] The revelation came while he was interviewing Shaun Barcavage, a nurse practitioner who said he had suffered vaccine injuries and received little help or recognition.[2] "We know that vaccines can have unintended consequences, AKA side effects, but nobody's really talking about it because they're too afraid of blame, and they just want it to go away," Cuomo said, adding that "people like Shaun—and me—and millions of others who still have weird stuff with their bloodwork and their lives and their feelings—you know, physically—are not going away."[2] He said plainly, "I'm sick myself, but I'm working with people who are working with this," and offered to share his doctor's information with Barcavage.[2] Cuomo did not detail his symptoms or identify the brand of vaccine he had received.[2] The disclosure was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]].[2]

### Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the admission through what he calls "Overton's Goalpost," a framework he describes as a spin on the Hegelian dialectic intended to help information warriors track progress amid demoralizing psychological operations.[2] Rather than asking whether legacy media is telling the truth — which he argued will not happen until it is forced to — the framework tracks the collective narrative retreat of media institutions from psychological ground they once held firmly in the public mind.[2] Cuomo's backpedal, in this reading, represented a "Covid-famous" mind-mover attempting to realign himself with the vaccine-injured and mandate-averse MAGA majority, getting ahead of a pendulum swing that had already passed him.[2] Burning Bright closed with the observation that "treason doesn't pay well in the end," adding that what remained to be determined was how complicit some media figures had been in knowingly illegal acts.[2]

## Ivermectin reversal (May 2024)

In May 2024, Cuomo reversed his position on [[ivermectin]] as a therapeutic for COVID-19, telling the PBD Podcast hosted by [[patrick-bet-david|Patrick Bet-David]] that he was "taking … a regular dose of Ivermectin" to manage an ongoing inflammatory response and "brain fog" tied to long COVID.[3] He had disclosed in January that he was suffering from long COVID.[3] "Ivermectin was a boogeyman during COVID. That was wrong. We were given bad information about Ivermectin. The real question is, why?" he said.[3] The about-face was notable because as a CNN anchor Cuomo had said that anyone promoting the drug should be "shamed," and he and colleague [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] had ridiculed its use after [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]] disclosed he had taken it.[3]

Cuomo argued that the deeper issue was institutional rather than personal: "the entire medical community knew that Ivermectin couldn't hurt you. They knew it … I know they knew it," he said, explaining that he had since spoken with clinicians who were overwhelmed during the pandemic and said nothing at the time.[3] Ivermectin, first used in the 1970s as an antiparasitic primarily for animals, had come into widespread human use in later decades — particularly as a malaria prophylaxis and treatment — and is considered very safe, though during 2020 and 2021 it was instantly denounced as a "horse dewormer."[3]

### Badlands response

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] rejected Cuomo's framing that no one knew the truth at the time, writing that independent researchers had been talking to the same clinicians all along while Cuomo was demonizing the FLCCC and mocking ivermectin as "horse paste."[3] She argued that patients resorted to veterinary formulations from farm-supply stores because doctors would not write prescriptions and pharmacists would not fill them under government pressure and propaganda — pressure that worked only because media figures carried it.[3] She contended that many lives could have been saved with ivermectin, hydroxychloroquine and Vitamin D, and that thousands died after being denied such treatments and instead given remdesivir and placed on ventilators.[3] She characterized Cuomo's recasting of himself as brazen, noting that he and his brother had found integrity only after being discarded by the regime.[3]

## Criticism of media coverage of threats against Trump

In September 2024, Cuomo publicly expressed shame at the media for advancing the narrative that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had only himself to blame for attempts on his life.[1] Cuomo said he had telephoned Trump personally after the second assassination attempt to tell him he was "really sorry that this is going on and it's being dealt with this way," adding, "I called him today because I am ashamed of how we are responding and not responding to the threats on him."[1]

Cuomo said he felt for Trump's family and rejected the dismissal that Trump "asked for it," arguing that commentators had to "get out of the judgment business, unless it's judging ourselves."[1] He also said he did not know how Trump stayed in the race after "being shot in the head," told those attempting to minimize the shooting that they "need to check yourself," and noted that Trump got up pumping his fist and barely spoke of it afterward.[1] While qualifying that he was not a Trump supporter, Cuomo said he was worried about the country and ashamed at the relative lack of concern surrounding the threats.[1] The remarks were reported by [[modernity-news|Modernity News]] under the headline "Cuomo Red-Pilled."[1]
