# Xóchitl Gálvez

Xóchitl Gálvez was the principal opposition candidate in Mexico's June 2, 2024 presidential election.[1]

According to the president of Mexico's National Electoral Institute, Gálvez received between 26.6 percent and 28.6 percent of the vote, far behind winner [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]], who took between 58.3 percent and 60.7 percent, while Jorge Álvarez Máynez received between 9.9 percent and 10.8 percent.[1] Sheinbaum's Morena party was also projected to retain its majorities in both chambers of Mexico's Congress, leaving the opposition Gálvez represented in a substantially weakened position.[1]
