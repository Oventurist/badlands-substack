# Harmeet Dhillon

Harmeet Dhillon is an American attorney and Republican Party figure known for litigation on election law, civil liberties, and free-speech matters, and for founding the Dhillon Law Group and the Center for American Liberty.

## Role in the corpus

In An SOS From Ground Zero, [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] reports that in the weeks after the November 8, 2022 election "lawyers are swarming again" in Arizona and singles out Dhillon among "some savage lawyers fighting for election integrity in Arizona," calling her "our beloved Harmeet Dhillon." The reference situates her within the broader post-election legal effort surrounding [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s campaign and the demands for documentation from [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], alongside the parallel inquiry pursued by Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] within the Arizona Attorney General's Election Integrity Unit.

## Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights

By January 2026 Dhillon was serving as Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]. The Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026 reports that after activists stormed [[cities-church|Cities Church]] in St. Paul, Minnesota, during a Sunday service, Dhillon stated that the incident could violate the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]] and the [[face-act|FACE Act]]. She emphasized a policy of zero tolerance, said that disrupting religious services is not protected speech, and confirmed that the Civil Rights Division and the [[fbi|FBI]] were investigating. Her statements accompanied the DOJ's decision to place former CNN host [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] "on notice" for potential federal charges.
