# Exxon Mobil

**Exxon Mobil Corporation** is one of the largest American oil and gas companies.[1] Among its long-running legal claims are those arising from property confiscated by the Cuban government after the 1959 revolution.[1]

## Exxon Mobil Corp. v. Corporación Cimex, S.A.

On June 23, 2026, the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] decided Exxon Mobil Corp. v. Corporación Cimex, S.A. (Cuba) (No. 24-699), holding in a divided decision that the [[helms-burton-act|Helms-Burton Act]] itself abrogates the sovereign immunity of Cuban agencies and instrumentalities for private suits alleging trafficking in confiscated United States property.[1] The ruling opened a clearer path for American claimants to pursue Cuban state entities in federal court.[1]
