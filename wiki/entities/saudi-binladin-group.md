# Saudi Binladin Group

The Bin Laden Group is the largest construction firm in Saudi Arabia and one of the central corporate vehicles of the kingdom's building boom.[1]

During the Royal Purge, Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] seized control of the Bin Laden Group along with [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]]'s Kingdom Holdings, a roughly $12 billion investment fund.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presents that consolidation — together with the recruitment of Dubai-based developer [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] as foreign minister to sell the [[vision-2030|Vision 2030]] program to global investors, and back-channel contacts with [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration — as evidence that MBS was building the industrial base for his transformation plan rather than merely filibustering to skeptical American officials.[1]
