# The Earth Chronicles

The Earth Chronicles is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Wednesdays at 3pm ET in the January 2023 schedule, hosted by [[david-whitehead|David Whitehead]] and [[josh-reid|Josh Reid]].[1] The two discuss ongoing sociopolitical events from a global perspective.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed The Earth Chronicles on Wednesdays at 3pm ET, with hosts David Whitehead and Josh Reid discussing ongoing sociopolitical events from a global perspective.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed The Earth Chronicles in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
