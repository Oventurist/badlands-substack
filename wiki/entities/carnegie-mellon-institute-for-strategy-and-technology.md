# Carnegie Mellon Institute for Strategy and Technology

The **Carnegie Mellon Institute for Strategy and Technology** is a research institute at Carnegie Mellon University focused on the intersection of technology and national security strategy.[1] Its director is [[audrey-kurth-cronin|Audrey Kurth Cronin]].[1]

## June 2024 Gaza assessment

The institute entered Badlands coverage in June 2024 when Cronin, writing in [[foreign-affairs|Foreign Affairs]] and summarized by [[tass|TASS]], argued that Israel's Gaza campaign amounted to a "strategic disaster" that had reduced the number of [[hamas|Hamas]] fighters without dismantling the movement, while driving popular support for it above 50% among Gazans.[1]
