# Roger Carstens

Roger Carstens served as the special presidential envoy for hostage affairs during the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration, the official responsible for coordinating US efforts to recover Americans detained abroad.[1]

Carstens featured in reporting on the negotiations that secured the release of Wall Street Journal reporter [[evan-gershkovich|Evan Gershkovich]] from Russian prison.[1] According to the account, he instructed Gershkovich's mother, Ella, to approach President Biden at the White House Correspondents' Dinner and urge him to telephone German Chancellor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]].[1] Biden claimed he had already made the call, but Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] corrected him; ultimately a signed letter was sent to Scholz instead of a call.[1]

Badlands commentary framed the scene — the mother of an American prisoner waiting in a handshake line to relay a message from the president's own hostage negotiator — as evidence that Biden did not grasp the situation and that others were managing US diplomacy.[1]
