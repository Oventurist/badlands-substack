# Whig Party

The Whig Party was an American political party of the mid-nineteenth century. In the Badlands Media corpus it is described as the principal vehicle of the nationalist, anti-slavery grouping that opposed the Anglophile [[deep-state|Deep State]] and the southern slave power.

## Role in the American System tradition

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s [[avoiding-wwiii|Avoiding WWIII]] states that for decades the patriotic grouping seeking to reclaim the nation from the oligarchical slave power rallied primarily around the Whig Party of [[john-quincy-adams|John Quincy Adams]]. Its program centred on reviving Alexander Hamilton's national bank, re-launching protective tariffs, and funding internal improvements — the policy complex the corpus elsewhere calls the American System.

## Two victories, two deaths

The party won two presidential victories the article regards as decisive opportunities. The first, in 1840, brought [[william-henry-harrison|William Henry Harrison]] to office on a platform of reviving Hamilton's national bank; the second came with [[zachary-taylor|Zachary Taylor]] in 1848. Ehret asserts that both Whig presidents were poisoned. Harrison served only three months and died under mysterious circumstances before legislation for a Third National Bank, passed by both houses of [[congress|Congress]], could be signed into law; Taylor's death was officially attributed to "too many cherries and cold milk."

The Whigs' defeat is presented alongside the killing of the [[second-bank-of-the-united-states|Second Bank of the United States]] in 1836 and the passage of the [[kansas-nebraska-act|Kansas–Nebraska Act]] of 1854 as stages in the ascent of the slave power before the Civil War.
