# Omega Solutions International

Omega Solutions International is a security consulting firm associated with [[rinaldo-nazzaro|Rinaldo Nazzaro]], the founder of the neo-Nazi organization [[the-base|The Base]].[1]

Reporting cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that the firm listed an address on [[k-street|K Street]] in Washington, DC, the corridor where the capital's lobbying industry is concentrated.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized security consultancies of this kind as a common front for intelligence operations.[1]

A series of Freedom of Information Act requests filed on MuckRock by a person named Jason Wilson one week after the January 2020 press coverage of Nazzaro sought records on both Omega Solutions International and Nazzaro from every relevant agency; each agency responded that it had no information on the entities.[1] The Badlands commentary treated that outcome as inconsistent with the [[fbi|FBI]]'s stated posture that The Base was a major counterterrorism focus.[1]
