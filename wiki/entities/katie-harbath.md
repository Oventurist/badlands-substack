# Katie Harbath

Katie Harbath is a platform-policy and elections specialist identified in Badlands Media coverage as one of the "censorship industry insiders" appearing on a panel discussing the future of content moderation after the Trump administration began dismantling U.S. government elements of the censorship apparatus.[1]

## Institutional affiliations

The article associates Harbath with the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]] and with the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]], which it characterizes as a CIA cut-out.[1] She appeared alongside [[rebekah-tromble|Rebekah Tromble]] and [[dean-jackson|Dean Jackson]], the latter linked to the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace|Carnegie Endowment]] and the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]].[1]

## Position after the U.S. retrenchment

In the panel described by the article, the participants expressed dismay at the Trump administration's effort to bury the disinformation-research industry and placed their remaining hope in the European Union's [[digital-services-act|Digital Services Act]] as the instrument capable of restoring platform trust-and-safety staffing and re-establishing researcher access.[1] The Badlands framing treats this as American practitioners welcoming a foreign regulator's leverage — including the threat of multibillion-dollar fines — over U.S. companies such as X.[1]
