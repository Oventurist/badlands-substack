# Norway

Norway is a Nordic kingdom and founding member of [[nato|NATO]]. It occupies a central place in the January 2026 transatlantic crisis over [[greenland|Greenland]] described in the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]], both as an Arctic power and as the country whose Nobel Committee awards the Nobel Peace Prize.

The Brief reports that Norway was one of eight countries — with [[denmark|Denmark]], Finland, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Sweden and the United Kingdom — that deployed a small military contingent to Greenland to bolster Arctic security and demonstrate solidarity with Denmark, and that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] threatened all eight with additional 10–15% tariffs effective February 1 unless they supported a U.S. purchase deal for the island. In a letter to Prime Minister [[jonas-gahr-store|Jonas Gahr Støre]], Trump connected the Oslo-based Nobel Committee's refusal to award him the Peace Prize, despite his claim to have stopped eight wars, to his declaration that he "no longer feel[s] an obligation to think purely of peace." Støre replied by affirming Norway's backing for Danish sovereignty over Greenland, and Norway joined the eight-nation statement condemning the tariff threats as risking a "dangerous downward spiral."
