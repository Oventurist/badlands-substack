# Saint Anselm College

Saint Anselm College is a college in New Hampshire that regularly hosts presidential campaign events.[1]

## 2023 CNN town hall

On May 10, 2023, the college hosted the [[cnn|CNN]] town hall with former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], moderated by [[kaitlan-collins|Kaitlan Collins]] before an audience of New Hampshire GOP primary voters.[1] It was at this venue that Trump declined to say who he thought should prevail in Russia's war against Ukraine, saying instead that he wanted "everybody to stop dying" and that he would have the conflict settled in 24 hours.[1]
