# Michael Aquino

Colonel Michael Aquino (1946–2019) was a United States Army psychological warfare officer and founder of the [[temple-of-set|Temple of Set]], described by Matthew Ehret as one of the most important Satanists to make [[wewelsburg-castle|Wewelsburg Castle]] the centre of an occult Nazi revival after the Second World War.[1]

## Vietnam and the Phoenix Program

Aquino was initiated into a Satanic California coven led by [[anton-lavey|Anton LaVey]] in the early days of the Vietnam War, joining LaVey's [[church-of-satan|Church of Satan]] in 1966.[1] In 1968 he was employed in Operation Phoenix directly under the command of psychological warfare specialist [[edward-lansdale|Edward Lansdale]], where, in this account, he helped oversee the assassination of over 40,000 civilians whose existence supposedly aided the Vietcong.[1] Operation Phoenix specialized in psychological warfare, torture, terror and mass murder, innovating the philosophy of "burning the village to save the village."[1] Researcher Anthony Forwood writes that the program created "legions of cold-blooded psychopathic killers" who returned to the United States transformed, many becoming involved in Satanism, with Aquino positioned to lead them into it amid a rise in satanically tinged serial murders in southern California.[1]

## The Aeon of Set

During the Summer Solstice of 21 June 1975, Aquino conducted a "magical working" and explained in his book *The Coming Forth by Night* that Satan appeared to him disguised as Set and called forth the Aeon of Set.[1] Satan reportedly told Aquino that this Aeon had begun in 1904 during [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley's]] magical working in Cairo, when Satan appeared in the form of Aiwass; the age of indulgence Crowley called "The Age of Horus" would give rise to what Aquino called "The Age of Set."[1] In Egyptian mythology Set was the third of five original deities, representing chaos, violence, destruction and confusion, and the first murderer, who killed his brother Osiris and sought to kill Horus.[1] In second-century Gnostic literature popularized by Carl Jung and his Eranos followers, Set is rebranded as the deity animating Sodom and Gomorrah and incarnating in a dark version of Jesus who liberates disciples from the material world, deifying initiates free to "do what thou wilt."[1]

Aquino became convinced he was the new dark messiah of the New Age and the reincarnation of Crowley; he cut his hair into a widow's peak, tattooed "666" under his scalp and groomed his eyebrows in homage to a photograph of Crowley.[1] He instituted the inverted pentagram as his group's symbol and founded the Temple of Set as a breakaway body, taking 28 members of LaVey's Church of Satan with him as founding priests.[1] He adopted degrees modelled on the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and took the title of Ipsissimus, with his personal witch Lilith Sinclair as the cult's high priestess.[1] Asked by Oprah Winfrey in 1988 to describe the god he worshipped, Aquino answered: "We are not servants of some God; we are our own gods."[1]

## The Wewelsburg Working

In 1982, then a Major in the US Army and an overseer of the "revolution in military affairs," Aquino described his own dark rituals in Himmler's Wewelsburg Castle in an essay titled "The Wewelsburg Working," dated 19 October, 3:00–4:30 pm, in the Hall of the Dead / "Walhalla" of the North Tower.[1] He wrote that because the Wewelsburg had been conceived by [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] as the "Mittelpunkt der Welt," the Hall of the Dead was the Gate of that Centre from which to summon the Powers of Darkness at their most powerful locus, and reported an inflow of dormant knowledge and a "vast, spiraling dialectic" clearing away sectarian debris in search of the true Powers of Darkness.[1] Occult writer Peter Levenda records that Aquino visited Wewelsburg in the early eighties and performed a ritual in the North Tower designed to unleash the castle's power on the world and jump-start the next phase of human evolution.[1] A 1987 *San Francisco Chronicle* article noted that in his book *Crystal Tablet of Set* Aquino wrote that he performed the rituals to recreate an order of knighthood for followers of Satan.[1]

## Military career and MindWar

Aquino served with the Green Berets and in the 1970s was a NATO liaison officer in several European countries, while remaining a specialist in psychological warfare for military intelligence.[1] Ehret states that he played a direct role overseeing a coup within the US military under Major General [[albert-stubblebine|Albert Stubblebine]] and the emergence of a Mithraic revival of neo-Templars.[1] This revolution in military affairs followed the template of Zbigniew Brzezinski and Samuel P. Huntington and received its directing manifesto in Aquino's 1980 publication [[from-psyop-to-mindwar|From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory]], commissioned by Stubblebine and co-authored with Colonel [[paul-vallely|Paul Vallely]].[1] That essay introduced [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] trauma-based mind control studies, the SRI Stargate Project and [[esalen-institute|Esalen Institute]] drug experiments into basic military training, along with an array of electromagnetic weapon programs designed to wage asymmetric warfare on the minds of citizens inside and outside the United States.[1]

Despite nominally breaking from LaVey's Church of Satan, both organizations supported each other and promoted Crowley's Thelemic doctrine, "Do What Thou Wilt Shall be the Whole of the Law."[1]
