# Melania Trump

Melania Trump is the First Lady of the United States, wife of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]].

The January 19, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the Office of the First Lady posted on social media that she encouraged people to "use AI to unlock new parts of your imagination" while warning against allowing artificial intelligence to replace personal thinking. She urged users to remain creative and thoughtful in their engagement with the technology, stressing that individuals should not "surrender your thinking to AI."

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] endorses the message in her commentary, writing that "the First Lady has exactly the right message. AI is a tool — be smart with it, turn your brain on and use the tool to unleash it, but don't outsource your critical thinking. Use the tool. Don't be used by the tool." She extends the point into an argument that the deeper question raised by artificial intelligence is "about decision rights to determine what's true," which she urges readers never to give away.

## Hosting the Xi visit (March 2026)

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the First Lady and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] will host Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and Madame Peng for a reciprocal visit in Washington, D.C. later in 2026, following Trump's Beijing meeting with Xi on May 14 and 15. Press Secretary [[karoline-leavitt|Karoline Leavitt]] said the date of the Washington visit is still to be determined.
