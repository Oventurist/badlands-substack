# Judy Mikovits

Judy Mikovits is a virologist identified as one of the early prominent scientific voices arguing that SARS-CoV-2 bore signs of laboratory manipulation rather than natural evolutionary emergence.[1] She is grouped with fellow virologist [[luc-montagnier|Luc Montagnier]] and bioweapons scholar [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]] among the specialists who joined Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesman [[zhao-lijian|Zhao Lijian]] in the first months of the pandemic in rejecting the natural-origin position advanced by the [[world-health-organization|WHO]], Nature Magazine and the editors of [[the-lancet|The Lancet]].[1]

The shared conclusion of this group was that the virus's apparent gene sequencing implied human handiwork; what remained contested among them was whether the responsible laboratory was Chinese or under United States control, and whether any release was an accident or a conscious deployment.[1] That second question was treated as a live possibility given the long normalization of pandemic war-game exercises in western geopolitical life, from Dark Winter in 2000 through the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation's]] Lock Step scenario to the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum's]] [[event-201|Event 201]].[1]
