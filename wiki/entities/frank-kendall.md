# Frank Kendall

Frank Kendall served as Secretary of the Air Force, the top civilian official of the Department of the Air Force, during the Biden administration.[1]

## 2024 reorganization

In February 2024 Kendall joined the other senior civilian and uniformed leaders of the Air Force and Space Force in unveiling a slate of 24 initiatives at the Air and Space Forces Association's Air Warfare Symposium, the product of a months-long department-wide review begun the previous fall.[1] The package — described as among the most significant reorganizations of the service since the end of the Cold War — included a new capabilities planning command, a refocused training enterprise, and changes to how airmen deploy.[1]

Kendall framed the effort as a demonstration of the department's competitive resolve toward U.S. adversaries, naming Russia and China in particular, and stressed the need for urgency.[1] "We can no longer regard conflict as a distant possibility or a future problem that we might have to confront," he said. "The risk of conflict is here now, and that risk will increase with time."[1]
