# Rachel Levine

Rachel Levine is an American public health official who served as Assistant Secretary for Health in the Biden administration and holds the rank of admiral in the U.S. Public Health Service Commissioned Corps.[1]

## As a figure in the transgender policy debate

Levine became a recurring reference point in conservative criticism of the Biden administration's transgender policies. In commentary on the July 2023 [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] memo detailing benefits for service members who identify as transgender, Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] asked rhetorically whether Levine was "the gold standard example of an 'indefinite waiver for physical fitness standards,'" using the reference to argue that the policies had degraded [[military-readiness|military readiness]] and the standards of the uniformed services.[1]

## Invoked in the 2023 military readiness debate

Following Lt. Gen. [[deanna-burt|DeAnna Burt]]'s June 2023 Pride remarks at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited Levine — the first female four-star admiral in the history of the US Public Health Service Commissioned Corps — as a marker of institutional decline, writing that "when you make a mockery of a once serious institution, don't be surprised when people find that institution seriously mockable."[2]
