# Seth Moulton

Seth Moulton is an American politician and former U.S. Marine Corps officer who has served as a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Massachusetts since 2015. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," he is named among the supporters of the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]] whom the source ties to the "deep state."

## Role in the article

The article describes Moulton as "both a CIA stooge and a Young Global Leader at the World Economic Forum," and lists him among the bill's "biggest supporters" who were "born and bred from the interagency blob, or 'deep state,' political spooks." Its characterization of Moulton forms part of the source's argument that supporters of the Antisemitism Awareness Act included figures connected to intelligence and globalist networks.
