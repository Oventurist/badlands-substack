# Elijah Boyd

Elijah Boyd is a Libertarian candidate who ran for Alabama House District 10 in the 2022 general election, receiving 3.4% of the vote in a race won by Republican [[david-cole|David Cole]] with 51.6%, ahead of Democrat Marilyn Lands at 45%.[1]

Boyd subsequently challenged Cole's eligibility, alleging that Cole had claimed a residence in District 10 when declaring for the race while tax documents showed his residence in District 4.[1] On November 22 he filed a complaint contesting the election in Madison County Circuit Court, arguing that Cole was not a resident of the district and had not been a resident for the year preceding the November 8 general election, as required by Alabama's constitution.[1] Those long-standing complaints preceded Cole's August 2023 arrest on charges of voting at multiple or unauthorized locations.[1]
