# Graham Lowry

Graham Lowry was an American historian, described in the Badlands Media essay A New American Renaissance as "the late historian" and "the great historian," and cited for his magnum opus *How the Nation Was Won: Volume 1 (1630–1757)*.

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] draws on Lowry for two central claims. First, that the networks led by [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]] had been planning the conditions for a declaration of independence for decades before 1776, so that the fall of Quebec at the Battle of Quebec in 1759 formed part of a long-prepared strategic sequence. Second, that [[william-penn|William Penn]]'s colonial work in Pennsylvania — his commitment to developing culture, economy and population rather than extracting wealth — is examined at length in *How the Nation Was Won*.
