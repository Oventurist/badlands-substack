# Matuš Šutaj Eštok

Matuš Šutaj Eštok is the Interior Minister of [[slovakia|Slovakia]].[1]

In May 2024, following the shooting of Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] in Handlová, Šutaj Eštok told reporters that the theory of a broader assassination plot was supported by the fact that the assailant's social media communications had been erased by another person about two hours after the shooting.[1] His remarks accompanied the Slovak police's announcement that cooperation with domestic and foreign intelligence services had led them to broaden the probe beyond the initial "lone-wolf" framing to include the possibility that an unidentified group was linked to the crime.[1]
