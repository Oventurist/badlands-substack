# Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf

Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf is the Speaker of the Iranian Parliament and, according to the White House account reported in the Badlands Brief of June 16, 2026, one of the three signatories to the United States–[[iran|Iran]] agreement concluded that month.

## Signing of the 2026 agreement

The Brief, citing the *Times of Israel*, reports that according to the [[white-house|White House]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and Ghalibaf digitally signed the agreement on Sunday, following negotiations aimed at ending the recent conflict. Administration officials described the deal as a pathway toward regional de-escalation and a broader restructuring of Middle East security arrangements.

Ghalibaf's role as the Iranian signatory is notable in the reporting because he is a parliamentary rather than executive figure, and because the digital form of the signing — together with the divergent accounts offered by participants — became part of the controversy surrounding the agreement's terms. American Jewish organizations and Israeli commentators cited in the same edition said key details remained unclear and warned that any arrangement must prevent Iran from advancing its nuclear capabilities. The official signing ceremony was scheduled for June 19 in Switzerland.
