# Matt Rosendale

Matt Rosendale is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Montana and one of the twenty-one conservatives who withheld votes from [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] in the January 2023 speakership contest.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speakership fight

Rosendale belonged to the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]]-aligned holdout bloc that produced the [[january-2023-mccarthy-concessions|January 2023 McCarthy concessions]], and he continued to serve on the House Natural Resources Committee afterward.[1] Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson identifies natural resources as a strategically important panel, arguing that energy dependence was among the first consequences of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] taking office.[1]
