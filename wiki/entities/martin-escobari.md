# Martín Escobari

Martín Escobari is co-president of [[general-atlantic|General Atlantic]] and a member of the advisory board of the [[david-rockefeller-center-for-latin-american-studies|David Rockefeller Center for Latin American Studies]] at Harvard.[1] "The Harvard Control Grid" lists him among the stewards of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]].[1]

The article argues that Escobari's advisory role positions him inside Harvard's Latin America arm — an institution funded and named for [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]] — and that he is linked to Latin American business and political networks including "globalist-aligned oligarchs, major U.S. investors, and transnational donor circuits."[1] He is cited again in the article's enumeration of Harvard's overwhelming ties to the Rockefeller dynasty.[1]
