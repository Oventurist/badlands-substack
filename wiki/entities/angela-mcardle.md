# Angela McArdle

Angela McArdle is the chair of the [[libertarian-party|Libertarian Party]] of the United States, a role in which she became a figure in speculation about third-party options for the 2024 presidential race.[1]

## Meeting with Robert F. Kennedy Jr.

The New York Times reported in September 2023 that McArdle had met [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] in July of that year at a conference in Memphis.[1] According to McArdle, Kennedy "emphasized that he was committed to running as a Democrat but said that he considered himself very libertarian," and she added that "we're aligned on a lot of issues," specifically citing the threat of the "[[deep-state|deep state]]."[1]

McArdle characterized the relationship as an open channel rather than a commitment: "My perspective is that we are going to stay in touch in case he does decide to run. And he can contact me at any time if that's the case."[1] The prospect alarmed Democratic strategists — Matt Bennett of Third Way said it "would be very bad" if Kennedy ran on the Libertarian line.[1] Kennedy ultimately was reported to be preparing an independent, rather than Libertarian, candidacy, with an announcement planned for October 9, 2023 in Philadelphia.[1]
