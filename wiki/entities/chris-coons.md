# Chris Coons

Chris Coons is a Democratic member of the United States [[senate|Senate]] from Delaware.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief lists Coons among the Democratic senators who questioned Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] at the February 10, 2026 Senate Appropriations Subcommittee hearing about his ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] revealed in the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] document release. With [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]] and [[jeff-merkley|Jeff Merkley]], he challenged Lutnick over inconsistencies with his earlier statements about when the relationship ended and accused him of failing to be transparent, part of a line of questioning that produced calls from some Democrats for his resignation.
