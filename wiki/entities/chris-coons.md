# Chris Coons

Chris Coons is a Democratic member of the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]].[1]

## Lutnick questioning

Coons was among the Democratic senators who, at a Senate Appropriations Subcommittee hearing on February 10, 2026, pressed Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] on his ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] as disclosed in the February 2026 [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] document release, challenging his transparency and earlier account of when the relationship ended.[1]
