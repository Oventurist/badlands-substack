# Donald Palmer

Donald Palmer was a Republican commissioner of the U.S. [[election-assistance-commission|Election Assistance Commission]].[1]

Palmer voluntarily left the commission earlier in 2026, before President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s July 9 removal of the remaining members.[1] His prior departure meant that when Democratic commissioners [[thomas-hicks|Thomas Hicks]] and [[benjamin-hovland|Benjamin Hovland]] were terminated by email and Republican [[christy-mccormick|Christy McCormick]] resigned, the statutorily four-member agency was left entirely vacant.[1]
