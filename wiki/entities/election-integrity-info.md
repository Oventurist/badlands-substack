# ElectionIntegrity.Info

ElectionIntegrity.Info is a research project that compiled an extensive public report cataloguing the lawsuits filed in connection with the 2020 United States presidential election.[1] Its report on 2020 election cases collects filings, pleadings, and outcomes for the litigation that followed the November 2020 vote, allowing readers to review the record case by case rather than relying on summary characterizations in the press.[1]

Attorney and independent journalist [[rachel-alexander|Rachel Alexander]] leveraged the ElectionIntegrity.Info report — together with case summaries from the [[healthy-elections-project|Healthy Elections Project]] — to build her widely circulated index of 2020 election cases.[1] That index underpinned the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] argument that, of 92 lawsuits specifically concerning the 2020 election, 30 were decided on the merits and Trump-aligned plaintiffs prevailed in 22 of them.[1]
