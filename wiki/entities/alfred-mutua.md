# Alfred Mutua

Alfred Mutua is a Kenyan politician serving as a Cabinet Secretary in the government of [[kenya|Kenya]]. He features in the Badlands corpus chiefly for his role in the government's August 2023 confrontation with the biometric identity venture [[worldcoin|Worldcoin]].[1]

## Opposition to Worldcoin

After the Kenyan Ministry of the Interior suspended Worldcoin and similar firms from operating in the country, Mutua issued a statement condemning the company's practice of exchanging roughly $50 in crypto tokens for iris scans taken by its "Orb" device.[1] He was described as enraged over the technology, declaring: "Let us support the stoppage of Kenyans being used as guinea pigs and their data being harvested."[1] His remarks accompanied inquiries by Kenyan security, financial services and data protection agencies into the legality of the data collection and the intended use of the harvested biometrics.[1]

Commentary by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited Mutua's stance as evidence that African publics and officials were beginning to reject what she characterized as the global surveillance state.[1]
