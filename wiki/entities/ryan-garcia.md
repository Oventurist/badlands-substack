# Ryan Garcia

Ryan Garcia is an American professional boxer who, in April 2024, publicly endorsed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for president and became a figure of interest in the Truth Community after speaking out about child sex trafficking.[1]

## Trump endorsement

Garcia announced from Mar-a-Lago that he would vote for Trump, and offered his help to the campaign, telling the former president: "Hey you're my President. If you need somebody to troll Barack Obama, I could do it really well."[1] The announcement was circulated by TheStormHasArrived, described by Badlands as a friend of the outlet.[1]

## Haney fight and surrounding controversy

Garcia shocked the boxing world in the weekend before the endorsement by defeating the previously undefeated [[devin-haney|Devin Haney]].[1] According to commentary by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], Garcia spent the month before the bout "partying," ranting about the Deep State and behaving erratically. He then missed weight by three pounds and paid Haney $500,000 per pound — $1.5 million in total — before the two entered the ring.[1]

The press tour left Garcia closing as a 5- or 6-to-1 underdog on fight night depending on the bookmaker. He responded by betting on himself, flooring Haney three times over twelve rounds to notch a major upset, then conducted himself normally at the post-fight press conference and suggested the entire lead-up had been a psychological operation.[1]

Burning Bright summarized the sequence as a fighter who "has been talking about child trafficking among elites, 'pretending' to be crazy only to turn up and put on career performances against world champions," concluding with the note "Eyes on."[1] Garcia had also been, in Badlands' framing, "dragged through the mud by the Fake News Media" for his trafficking comments.[1]
