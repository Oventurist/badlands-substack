# Michael Collins (National Intelligence Council)

Michael Collins was the acting chair of the [[national-intelligence-council|National Intelligence Council]] until May 2025, when Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] fired him.[1]

## Dismissal

Gabbard removed Collins along with his deputy, Maria Langan-Riekhof, as part of what her office described as the Trump administration's goal to "end the weaponization and politicization of the intelligence community."[1] A spokesperson for Gabbard's office said, "The Director is working alongside President Trump to end the weaponization and politicization of the Intelligence Community."[1]

The firings came shortly after the National Intelligence Council published a report suggesting that the Maduro regime in [[venezuela|Venezuela]] plays no role in [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang members illegally immigrating to the United States — an assessment at odds with the administration's stated position.[1]
