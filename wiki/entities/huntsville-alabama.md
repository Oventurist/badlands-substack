# Huntsville, Alabama

Huntsville is a city in northern Alabama long associated with American rocketry and space research, a heritage that earned it the decades-old nickname "Rocket City" because of its early role in NASA and the U.S. space program.[1]

## Selection as Space Command headquarters

On September 2, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that [[united-states-space-command|U.S. Space Command]] would be headquartered in Huntsville, reversing a Biden-era decision to keep the command at its temporary headquarters in [[colorado|Colorado]].[1] "The U.S. Space Command headquarters will move to the beautiful locale of a place called Huntsville, Alabama, forever to be known from this point forward as Rocket City," Trump said, flanked in the Oval Office by Republican members of Alabama's congressional delegation.[1] "We had a lot of competition for this and Alabama's getting it," he added.[1]

Trump said Huntsville won the race in part because "they fought harder for it than anybody else."[1] The decision capped a four-year tug of war between Alabama and Colorado, an intense fight because the headquarters is expected to be a significant boon to the winning region's local economy.[1] Reuters reporting framed the move as benefiting a state that overwhelmingly supported Trump's three Republican presidential bids at the expense of one that opposed them.[1]

Badlands commentary noted that the relocation to Huntsville had originally been initiated in the closing days of Trump's first term, on January 13, 2021, before the Biden administration cancelled it — a cancellation attributed at the time to Alabama's restrictive abortion law.[1]
