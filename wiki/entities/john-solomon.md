# John Solomon

John Solomon is an American investigative journalist associated with the outlet Just the News, and a principal public communicator on the Grand RICO prosecution track.[1]

In February 2026 Solomon said that 2020 election fraud was included in the Grand RICO case.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed in April 2026 that Solomon appeared to be deploying a phased communications plan as the case advanced, and that he was doubling down publicly — betting Roger Stone a steak dinner that indictments would come by June 20, the end of spring.[1]

Solomon's outlet also reported that the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] had requested transcripts and records from two Senate committees concerning former [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], and that former U.S. Attorney [[joseph-digenova|Joseph diGenova]] was scheduled to begin work as a lead prosecutor on Russia-collusion matters.[1]

## White House 2020 election document task force (2026)

By mid-2026 Solomon had temporarily stepped aside from his role at *Just The News* to assist a government document review, joining a [[white-house|White House]] task force gathering documents related to the 2020 presidential election for declassification.[2] The task force operates in addition to ongoing [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] probes into the 2020 election and includes former [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration officials such as former national security aide [[derek-harvey|Derek Harvey]].[2]

The effort collects material from the [[cia|CIA]], the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]], the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]], the [[fbi|FBI]], and the Justice Department, with lawyers reviewing gathered documents for months, sometimes late into the night.[2] The documents focus on ballot irregularities, potential voting machine issues, and possibly other topics, and members have been encouraged to limit redactions even where that means retaining the names of government officials.[2] The initiative involves thousands of pages, with plans to declassify some of them; Trump said acting DNI [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] would serve only "a month or two" but is authorized to "declassify almost everything."[2]

## Temporary unpaid government role (2026)

In June 2026, Solomon announced that he would temporarily step aside from his editorial role at [[just-the-news|Just the News]] to assist the government in an unpaid capacity identifying documents, according to a video posted by the outlet.[3] Just the News described the assignment as temporary and unpaid, and stated that Solomon's work would involve identifying classified or withheld documents concerning what it described as "weaponization and election integrity."[3] No additional details regarding the government entity involved, the duration of the assignment, or the scope of the document review were provided on the page.[3]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Solomon had made clear he was not being paid, asking what the value exchange was given that "nothing is free."[3] She wrote that she likes Solomon but has always thought he was operating, that the timing of his predictions has not been great though he trends toward justice, and that his access to the administration makes him newsworthy rather than merely a news deliverer.[3] She said she looked forward both to hearing Solomon's stories after his government stint and to the promised justice about which he has always seemed so certain, adding that "the golden age depends on it."[3]

## Reporting on Biden's autopen use (2025)

In early September 2025, Solomon reported for Just the News on newly released documents that he said exposed [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s excessive use of the autopen.[4] He stated that "for the first time, we're seeing documents produced by the National Archives that show that Biden White House lawyers believed that Joe Biden had an obligation to personally sign pardons," and that Biden may have delegated decisions on matters such as legislation and pardons to other people, with "no record of him being in the room when certain things were signed."[4]

Solomon called the material "bombshell documents" that even surprised him about what was being discussed behind the scenes at the Biden [[white-house|White House]], noting that all of it had been dug out of the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]].[4] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] previewed the report as due to drop that day and remarked on the relentless pace of such disclosures.[4]
