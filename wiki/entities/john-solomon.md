# John Solomon

**John Solomon** is an American investigative journalist, founder of [[just-the-news|Just the News]] and a former vice president of The Hill, who has become the central figure in the Badlands corpus's account of the push for accountability over [[russiagate|Russiagate]] and the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of government]].

## The RICO Grande

The Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026 treats Solomon as the main mouthpiece of the [[rico-grande|RICO Grande]] — the prospective federal racketeering case he announced in February 2026, which he has said includes the 2020 election fraud allegations. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observes that Solomon "sounds like he is deploying a phased communications plan," and that he is "doubling down" by betting [[roger-stone|Roger Stone]] a steak dinner on indictments by June 20, 2026. The Brief presents Solomon's public confidence as the reason a contemporaneous [[the-atlantic|Atlantic]] report on FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] is suspected of being a hit piece intended to muddy the justice phase.

## Role in the corpus

Solomon's reporting — on Hunter Biden, the Steele dossier, and the intelligence community's conduct during the 2016-2020 period — supplies much of the evidentiary basis that Badlands commentators cite for their expectation of prosecutions. He is portrayed as the journalist who kept the documentary record alive through the years when mainstream outlets refused to publish it.
