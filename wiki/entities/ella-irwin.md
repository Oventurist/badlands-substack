# Ella Irwin

Ella Irwin served as head of safety at [[twitter|Twitter]] under [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s ownership and was the company's public voice on its handling of child sexual abuse material in early 2023.[1]

In a February 2023 [[new-york-times|New York Times]] investigation relayed by [[breitbart|Breitbart]], Irwin claimed that she was quickly resolving the problem of child sexual abuse material on the platform, which she described as having been widespread under Twitter's previous owners.[1] The Times reported that such content nonetheless remained present on the platform, including widely disseminated material that authorities considered simple to identify and remove.[1]
