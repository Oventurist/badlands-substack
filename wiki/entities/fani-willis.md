# Fani Willis

Fani Willis is the District Attorney of Fulton County, Georgia, who brought the state racketeering indictment against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his associates over their efforts to contest the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]] result in Georgia. She is examined in the Badlands Media essay [[are-jack-smith-and-fani-willis-in|Are Jack Smith and Fani Willis in on the Plan?]], published September 20, 2023.

## The Fulton County indictment

The article reports that Willis's indictment contains 41 counts, and that only a handful of them are unrelated to alleged "false statements" or to acts said to have arisen from those statements. The solicitation-of-violation-of-oath counts, the source notes, depend on the prior existence of the alleged false statements. Count 1 is the RICO charge, which lists 161 separate "acts" that are said together to prove the existence of an organized criminal enterprise. The author argues that a significant portion of these acts "amount to things like tweets or normal conversations that officials might have if they were investigating election fraud," and that what makes them criminal is only "the presumption that they were carried out in a fraudulent manner by people who intended to overthrow the election."

## Assessment in the source

As with Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], the article's principal criticism of Willis is that her case rests on an assertion about Trump's inner knowledge — that he knew he had lost and lied about it anyway. The essay concludes that both prosecutors have proceeded "based on the belief that they can somehow read his mind," and describes the indictments as "downright pitiful."

The author further speculates that Willis's prosecution may inadvertently or deliberately serve Trump, since it grants him powers of discovery and a venue in which evidence of election fraud — evidence the essay says has never been heard on the merits, 50 of 55 election cases having been dismissed on [[legal-standing|standing]] — could finally be placed on the record. He also argues the case improves Trump's optics, noting that "despite being a billionaire, he too has a mugshot."
