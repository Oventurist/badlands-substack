# Teresa Youngblut

Teresa "Milo" Youngblut is a 21-year-old woman charged in connection with the shooting death of US Border Patrol agent [[david-maland|David Maland]] in northern Vermont on January 20, 2025 — Inauguration Day.[1] Police have linked the case to what has been described as a "trans vegan cult" connected to multiple murders across the United States.[1]

## The Vermont shooting

Maland, 44, and other agents pulled over Youngblut and German citizen [[felix-bauckholt|Felix "Ophelia" Bauckholt]] near the Canadian border.[1] Youngblut quickly opened fire on the agents and a back-and-forth shootout followed.[1] Maland and Bauckholt were killed in the incident, and Youngblut was injured in the crossfire.[1]

## Arrest and charges

Youngblut was arrested in Washington state in late January 2025.[1] She faces one count of using a deadly weapon while assaulting a United States Border Patrol agent and one count of using and discharging a firearm during that assault.[1] A judge ordered her held without bail until a preliminary hearing on February 7.[1]

Federal investigators subsequently expanded their focus to 32-year-old [[michelle-zajko|Michelle Zajko]], whom police say purchased the handguns used in Maland's shooting.[1]
