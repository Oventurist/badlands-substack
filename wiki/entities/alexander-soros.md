# Alexander Soros

Alexander Soros is the son of [[george-soros|George Soros]] and, since June 2023, the controlling figure at the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], the philanthropy that holds most of the assets of his father's roughly $25 billion family office.[1]

## Takeover and retrenchment

Soros the younger assumed control of OSF in June 2023 and promptly announced a headcount reduction of at least 40% at the foundation.[1] By August 2023 Bloomberg reported that OSF would end a large part of its operations inside the European Union, citing a "radical shift of strategic direction" and an operating model requiring "significant further restructuring" and the "closing all regional and global programs."[1] A letter to grantees in Hungary, quoting a note sent to staff at OSF's Berlin headquarters, said the new direction "provides for withdrawal and termination of large parts of our current work within the European Union," and that funding inside the bloc would be "extremely limited" going forward.[1] The stated rationale was that EU institutions and governments were already allocating significant resources to human rights, freedom and pluralism.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the succession as George Soros handing his son "a somewhat unceremonious inheritance that may be more of an albatross," and urged readers to look past the schadenfreude to what the retreat implies about a shifting worldwide Overton window.[1] In his framing, defections from the enemy's narrative apparatus matter more than conversions: "In order to win an Info War, you don't need your psychological enemy to adopt your paradigm; you only need him to abandon his own."[1]
