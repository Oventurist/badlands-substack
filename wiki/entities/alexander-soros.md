# Alexander Soros

Alexander Soros is the son of [[george-soros|George Soros]] and, since June 2023, the controlling figure at the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], the philanthropy that holds most of the assets of his father's roughly $25 billion family office.[1]

## Takeover and retrenchment

Soros the younger assumed control of OSF in June 2023 and promptly announced a headcount reduction of at least 40% at the foundation.[1] By August 2023 Bloomberg reported that OSF would end a large part of its operations inside the European Union, citing a "radical shift of strategic direction" and an operating model requiring "significant further restructuring" and the "closing all regional and global programs."[1] A letter to grantees in Hungary, quoting a note sent to staff at OSF's Berlin headquarters, said the new direction "provides for withdrawal and termination of large parts of our current work within the European Union," and that funding inside the bloc would be "extremely limited" going forward.[1] The stated rationale was that EU institutions and governments were already allocating significant resources to human rights, freedom and pluralism.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized the succession as George Soros handing his son "a somewhat unceremonious inheritance that may be more of an albatross," and urged readers to look past the schadenfreude to what the retreat implies about a shifting worldwide Overton window.[1] In his framing, defections from the enemy's narrative apparatus matter more than conversions: "In order to win an Info War, you don't need your psychological enemy to adopt your paradigm; you only need him to abandon his own."[1]

## Announcement of the succession (June 2023)

The handover was made public in June 2023, when [[george-soros|George Soros]], then 92, was reported to be transferring control of his $25 billion empire to his 37-year-old younger son.[2] In his first interview after his selection, given to the Wall Street Journal, Alex Soros said "I'm more political" than his Hungarian-born financier father, and indicated he would broaden the family's liberal goals to include voting and abortion rights and "gender equality."[2] He said he felt bound to involve himself in day-to-day national political affairs ahead of the 2024 election, adding, "As much as I would love to get money out of politics, as long as the other side is doing it, we will have to do it, too" — a remark taken to signal a significant financial role for the Soros foundations in 2024.[2] The elder Soros said of his son, "We think alike," and "He's earned it."[2]

[[breitbart|Breitbart]] reported that Alex Soros had visited the [[white-house|White House]] at least 17 times in just over a year and had met with prominent Democrats.[2] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] noted the unusual publicity of the transfer given his father's preference for discretion.[2] Contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] wrote that Alex Soros was concerned about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] retaking the White House, was positioned on the far left on abortion and transgender issues, had taken an interest in District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]]'s New York case and commented on the classified-documents case assigned to Florida, and intended to further advance the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]].[2]
