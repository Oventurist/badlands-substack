# Marco Rubio

Marco Rubio is the United States Secretary of State in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and the head of the [[state-department|State Department]].

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] notes that Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] planned to meet Rubio on the morning of his Washington visit, before his meeting with President Trump. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads this scheduling as significant given Trump's public opposition to the Israeli Security Cabinet's [[west-bank|West Bank]] measures — "I am against annexation" — suggesting that Netanyahu wanted to "gameplan how to deal with him" before the presidential meeting on [[iran|Iran]] and [[gaza|Gaza]].


## Campaign against the International Criminal Court

The July 27, 2026 [[badlands-brief-245|Badlands Brief]] reports that Rubio, as Secretary of State, announced a campaign aimed at dismantling the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]], arguing that the tribunal threatens U.S. sovereignty and improperly targets American military personnel and officials. The effort includes encouraging other countries to distance themselves from the court. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] dates the announcement to July 13 and characterizes it as a sweeping "whole-of-government" campaign.

The initiative is presented as the opening move in a sequence that also included New York City Mayor [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]]'s declaration that he would seek the arrest of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], and [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s withdrawal from the [[rome-statute|Rome Statute]], which the [[state-department|State Department]] welcomed while calling on other countries to follow suit.
