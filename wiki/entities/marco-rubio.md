# Marco Rubio

Marco Rubio is the United States Secretary of State in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and the head of the [[state-department|State Department]].

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] notes that Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] planned to meet Rubio on the morning of his Washington visit, before his meeting with President Trump. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads this scheduling as significant given Trump's public opposition to the Israeli Security Cabinet's [[west-bank|West Bank]] measures — "I am against annexation" — suggesting that Netanyahu wanted to "gameplan how to deal with him" before the presidential meeting on [[iran|Iran]] and [[gaza|Gaza]].
