# George Kent

George Kent is a career [[state-department|State Department]] official who served in Ukraine-related roles and testified in the first House impeachment inquiry into [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Role in the Burisma record

Badlands cited government memos obtained by [[just-the-news|Just the News]] as undercutting the narrative Democrats sustained since the 2019 impeachment scandal, under which [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s threat to withhold a $1 billion loan guarantee from Ukraine to force the firing of Prosecutor General [[viktor-shokin|Viktor Shokin]] was official U.S. policy.[1] In commentary on that record, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] asserted that Kent lied under oath during the first House impeachment inquiry, as did then-Ambassador [[marie-yovanovitch|Marie Yovanovitch]].[1]

The same commentary argued that exculpatory evidence was withheld from Trump's defense team during the inquiry and the ensuing Senate trial, and that the State Department and the broader federal establishment had spent years covering up what officials knew about [[burisma|Burisma]] and [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1]
