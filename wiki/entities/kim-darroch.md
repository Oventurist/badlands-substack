# Kim Darroch

Kim Darroch is a British diplomat who served as the United Kingdom's ambassador to the United States during the first presidency of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] He is best known for the 2019 disclosure of confidential diplomatic cables he had authored, a leak that ended his ambassadorship and became a focal point for critics who argue that British diplomatic and intelligence structures actively sought to shape American policy from within.[1]

## The 2019 email leaks

In the summer of 2019, a cache of Darroch's email and cable traffic became public.[1] In the reading advanced by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the leaked material revealed that the [[british-foreign-office|British Foreign Office]] had been working to "flood the zone" around Trump — that is, to saturate the new administration's advisory environment with British-aligned voices — in order to influence the direction of his presidency.[1] Ehret contends that the aim of this effort was to nudge the administration toward a war footing with Russia, Iran, and other Middle Eastern states, rather than toward the détente Trump had campaigned on.[1]

## Significance in the British-influence thesis

Darroch's leaked correspondence is treated in this literature as one of three converging 2019 disclosures — alongside the internal memos of the [[integrity-initiative|Integrity Initiative]] and the emerging account of the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]]'s provenance — that together indicated a "very British pedigree" behind the American deep state.[1] The episode is cited less for the individual conduct of the ambassador than as documentary evidence of an institutional practice: the routine treatment of American internal deliberations as a legitimate arena for British diplomatic management.[1] That practice is presented as continuous with the pattern [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] himself described in 1981, when he said he had kept the British Foreign Office better informed than the American [[state-department|State Department]].[1]
