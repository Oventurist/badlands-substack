# Bill Richardson

**Bill Richardson** was an American Democratic politician who served as Governor of New Mexico and was a longtime ally of the Clintons.[1] He was nominated by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] to serve as Commerce Secretary but was forced to withdraw his nomination after a story emerged exposing corruption during his governorship.[1]

## North Korea involvement

In the Badlands Media analysis by Joe Lange, Richardson appears as one of two Americans identified in [[qanon|Q]] drop imagery as assisting [[north-korea|North Korea]] with its computer systems, alongside [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]].[1] The author poses the question of why a prospective Commerce Secretary would need to be intimately involved with North Korean computing infrastructure, and treats the pairing of a veteran Democratic political operator with a Silicon Valley executive as evidence that American figures were profiting from access to national secrets — "it wasn't just the Clintons."[1]

The article situates this involvement within its broader claim that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operated supercomputer systems inside North Korea — three of them, later taken offline under CIA director [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]] — and that these systems were the mechanism by which [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] and the North Korean state were controlled from outside.[1]
