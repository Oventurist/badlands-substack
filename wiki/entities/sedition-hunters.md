# Sedition Hunters

The "Sedition Hunters" are a loosely organized group of online tech sleuths, described as having anti-Trump leanings and known foreign ties, who assisted the [[fbi|FBI]] in identifying suspects from the January 6, 2021 Capitol riot.[1]

## Paid FBI informant arrangement

Memos reviewed by *Just the News* showed that the FBI under the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration paid members of the group more than $150,000 to help identify suspects in the January 6 investigation and in the [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]] probe.[1] Some informants used facial recognition and spyware to locate American citizens for potential arrest.[1] Payments included $20,000 in the summer of 2023, approved by a deputy to then-Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]], for video analysis linking Trump supporters at the Ellipse speech to the Capitol riot.[1] The group's toolkit reportedly included facial recognition applications, Capitol riot maps and databases of suspects.[1]

## Policy concerns and response

The arrangement raised concerns that bureau policies on informant bias, foreign influence and secrecy may have been violated, particularly because the Sedition Hunters openly acknowledged assisting the FBI online despite the general requirement that confidential human sources remain secret.[1] FBI officials said all supervisors involved had been dismissed and that an audit was underway to ensure no other cases were missed.[1] FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] told *Just the News* he planned to work with [[congress|Congress]] to create safeguards against similar entanglements in the future.[1]
