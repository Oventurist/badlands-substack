# Christian Lindner

Christian Lindner is a German politician who served as Federal Finance Minister in the three-party coalition government led by Chancellor [[olaf-scholz|Olaf Scholz]], and who leads the fiscally conservative [[free-democratic-party|Free Democratic Party]] (FDP).[1]

## Dismissal and coalition collapse

On the evening of Wednesday, November 6, 2024, Scholz announced that he would fire Lindner over persistent rifts within the coalition regarding government spending and economic reforms.[1] The dismissal ejected Lindner's FDP from the coalition, collapsing the government and paving the way for a snap election expected by the end of March 2025.[1] Scholz said he would call a confidence vote for January 15, a vote he was widely expected to lose.[1]

## Fiscal position

Lindner and the FDP insisted that the German government adhere to strict spending rules and cut taxes, positions that clashed directly with left-wing coalition partners who wanted to maintain social spending and stimulate German industry through economic measures.[1] Badlands Media coverage framed the resulting rupture as an impossible battle in which Lindner was caught in the middle.[1]

## Context

The collapse occurred just hours after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s decisive win in the 2024 U.S. presidential election, a result that reportedly stunned German political leaders who depend on American military power for their country's defense and who feared Trump's tariff policies would hobble German industry.[1]
