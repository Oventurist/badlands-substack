# Justin Eichorn

Justin Eichorn is a Republican state senator from Minnesota who was arrested in March 2025 on a felony charge of soliciting a minor to practice prostitution.[1] At the time of his arrest he was 40 years old, and his biography on the Minnesota Senate website described him as married with four children.[1]

## Arrest

Eichorn was arrested and booked on Tuesday, March 18, 2025, by authorities in Bloomington, Minnesota.[1] Police alleged that Eichorn believed he was communicating with a 17-year-old female, when in fact he was corresponding with detectives from the Bloomington police department conducting an undercover operation.[1] Booker Hodges of the Bloomington police department said in a statement: "As a 40-year-old man, if you come to the Orange Jumpsuit District looking to have sex with someone's child, you can expect that we are going to lock you up."[1]

## "Trump Derangement Syndrome" bill

The arrest drew heightened media attention because Eichorn had shortly beforehand introduced legislation seeking to create a mental illness category for liberals said to be obsessed with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — a formalization of the popular epithet "Trump Derangement Syndrome."[1] The juxtaposition of that bill with the solicitation charge made the case a prominent national story.[1]
