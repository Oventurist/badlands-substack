# Barry Goldwater

Barry Goldwater was a United States senator from Arizona and a presidential candidate whose criticism of the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] is quoted in Badlands Media's "The Men Behind the Curtain – Part 2."[1]

Writing at a time when the perception that U.S. policy was being directed by Rockefeller-dominated organizations had spread widely, Goldwater put the case succinctly: "What the Trilaterals truly intend is the creation of a worldwide economic power superior to the political government of the nation-states involved. As managers and creators of the system they will rule the world."[1] The statement is presented alongside similar complaints from Secretary of State Edmund Muskie and former ambassador to Iran William Sullivan about [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]]'s conduct of foreign policy, and preceded [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]]'s 1980 *Wall Street Journal* defense of the Commission.[1]
