# Commonwealth of Nations

The Commonwealth of Nations is an international association of 56 states, largely composed of former territories of the British Empire, with the British monarch at its symbolic head.[1]

## Proposed U.S. associate membership

In March 2025, British press reported that plans were allegedly in the works to make the United States the association's next "associate member," and that [[king-charles-iii|King Charles III]] might extend such an invitation to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during a state visit, following a letter from the King hand-delivered by Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]].[1] The idea had first been floated during Trump's first term by the Royal Commonwealth Society with the blessing of Queen Elizabeth II, and was revived in his second term, with a source telling the Daily Mail that it was "being discussed at the highest levels."[1] Reporting linked the proposal to the tariff conflict that began in February 2025 between the United States and [[canada|Canada]] — a Commonwealth realm of which the King is head of state — after Trump signed orders imposing near-universal tariffs on Canadian goods and suggested Canada could avoid higher taxes by joining the United States as a 51st state.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary treated the reported offer skeptically. [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] framed it as a "collapsing British commonwealth" seeking to bring in the United States, and noted that Trump had that same week placed the Declaration of Independence on display in the Oval Office.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the proposal outright, writing that associate membership was "not a thing we're doing."[1]
