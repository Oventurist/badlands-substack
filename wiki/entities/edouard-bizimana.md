# Edouard Bizimana

**Edouard Bizimana** is the Foreign Minister of [[burundi|Burundi]].[1]

## Accusations against M23

In December 2025, Bizimana publicly accused the [[m23|M23]] rebel movement of carrying out a cross-border bombing on Burundian territory.[1] Writing on X on a Friday, he stated: "The terrorists of the AFC/M23, supported by [[rwanda|Rwanda]], dropped a bomb on Burundian territory on [Thursday]. Such provocation is unacceptable, and appropriate actions will be taken to protect the Burundian population."[1]

The alleged strike targeted Burundi's Cibitoke province, which borders the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]]; local reporting indicated several civilians, including children, were injured when two projectiles fell near a market in Rugombo, though the precise casualty count was not confirmed.[1] The accusation marked an escalation in regional hostilities coming only a week after a peace agreement was signed between the presidents of the DR Congo and Rwanda.[1]

The rebel [[alliance-fleuve-congo|Congo River Alliance]] (AFC), which includes M23, issued counterclaims the same day through spokesperson Lawrence Kanyuka, alleging that bombs had fallen "continuously" from Burundian territory for over three days.[1]
