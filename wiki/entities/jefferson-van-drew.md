# Jefferson Van Drew

Jefferson Van Drew is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from New Jersey. In December 2024 he became a central figure in the controversy over unexplained drone sightings over New Jersey when he claimed on Fox News that Iranian drones were flying over New Jersey skies, launched from an Iranian "mothership" positioned off the United States coast, and urged military action in response.[1]

The claim was publicly denied the same day by the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]. Deputy Press Secretary [[sabrina-singh|Sabrina Singh]] told reporters on Wednesday afternoon, hours after Van Drew's Fox News appearance, that "there is not any truth to that," adding: "There is no Iranian ship off the coast of the United States ... and there is no so-called 'mothership' launching drones towards the United States."[1] Singh further stated that the objects "were not US military drones" and that "these are not drones or activities from a foreign entity or foreign adversary."[1]
