# South Pars Gas Field

The South Pars gas field is an enormous offshore natural gas field in the Persian Gulf lying between [[qatar|Qatar]] and [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## March 2026 strike

In March 2026 [[israel|Israel]] attacked the field, an event that became a central flashpoint in the widening [[iran-war|Iran war]].[1] [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took to Truth Social to blame Israel, stating that Israel acted on the strike "without American coordination," and warned that further attacks on the field would cease unless Iran struck Qatar again, threatening an overwhelming U.S. response on his own terms.[1] Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] subsequently told a press conference that Israel "acted alone" in attacking the gas field and that he would comply with Trump's request to refrain from additional attacks.[1]

## Second-order effects

The strike drove immediate regional consequences: Turkish foreign minister Hakan Fidan publicly blamed Israel for the "unprecedented crisis," and the fragile new Gulf alignments Trump had been forging showed visible strain.[1] [[qatar|Qatar]] and [[turkey|Turkey]] held a joint press conference in which Qatar blamed Israel for the war and argued that the Arab states need to reconcile with Iran to bring peace to the region.[1] The strike also coincided with the resignation of counterterrorism chief [[joe-kent|Joe Kent]] over the U.S. decision to attack Iran, deepening the foreign-policy split inside the [[america-first-movement|America First]] coalition.[1]
