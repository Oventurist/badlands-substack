# Narya Capital

Narya Capital is the venture capital firm founded by [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] after he left [[mithril-capital|Mithril Capital]], the fund run by his mentor [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]].[1] Narya provided funding to [[rumble|Rumble]], the free-speech video platform widely regarded as an alternative to YouTube and closely integrated with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[truth-social|Truth Social]].[1]

The firm's name is glossed in Badlands commentary as meaning "a powerful person, manly."[1] An editor's note appended to the article observes that Thiel's ventures are consistently named from Lord of the Rings lore: Mithril is the mythical light-but-durable metal, while Narya is one of the elven rings of power, of which Gandalf becomes steward.[1]
