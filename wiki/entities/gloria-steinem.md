# Gloria Steinem

Gloria Steinem is an American feminist writer and organizer. In Badlands Media commentary she is cited as evidence that the [[feminist-movement|Feminist Movement]] was shaped by United States intelligence rather than arising spontaneously.[1]

## Alleged CIA relationship

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that Steinem "was an asset of the CIA," that she admitted on record to working closely with the [[cia|CIA]] and receiving funding from the agency, and that after the Feminist Movement took off she later denied having had any relationship with it.[1]

Carlson used the episode to pose the rhetorical question of why the CIA would involve itself in the Feminist Movement at all, dismissing with sarcasm the suggestion that the agency in the late 1960s and early 1970s was "deeply concerned about the plight of women in America."[1] The implied answer is consistent with the article's central claim that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] promotes social movements to divide and control populations rather than to secure rights.[1]

## Role in the Women's Movement

A December 21, 2024 Badlands essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] again described Steinem as a [[cia|CIA]] asset, writing that the [[womens-movement|Women's Movement]] of the 1960s and early 1970s was "led by CIA assets like Gloria Steinem" and encouraged women to leave their husbands and join the workforce.[2] Carlson linked that movement to the devaluation of the traditional family, the treatment of stay-at-home mothers as second-class citizens, rising divorce rates, and the emergence of the [[latchkey-generation|Latchkey Generation]] among [[generation-x|Generation X]] children.[2] He argued the movement's real beneficiaries were the banking, real estate, retail and service industries rather than women themselves.[2]
