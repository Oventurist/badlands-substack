# Peter Doocy

Peter Doocy is an American television correspondent who covers the White House and regularly questions the president at press availabilities. He appears in the Badlands corpus as the questioner who prompted a widely noted shift in President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public posture toward Iran's leadership.

## The June 2026 Iran exchange

According to the June 5, 2026 Badlands Brief, Trump was responding to a question from Doocy about whether he would be willing to meet Supreme Leader [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] when he delivered remarks that Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called "a jaw-dropping breakthrough in public diplomacy." Trump began by saying "I don't *want* to meet," then immediately shifted to say he would be "honored" to meet the Supreme Leader, adding that "some people have suggested it."

The commentary treats Doocy's question as the occasion for what it describes as a rebranding of the Ayatollah as a reasonable actor, executed "with brilliant tact," and as a signal that Washington would not pursue the overthrow of [[iran|Iran]]'s existing system of government.

## The February 2026 aliens exchange

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Doocy asked President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for comment during a press gaggle on [[air-force-one|Air Force One]] on Thursday, February 19, 2026, after [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] went viral for conflicting statements about extraterrestrial life. Doocy: "Barack Obama said aliens are real. Have you seen any evidence of non-human visitors to Earth?" Trump replied: "He gave classified information. He's not supposed to be doing that... He made a big mistake. He took it out of classified information." When Doocy noted "The President can declassify anything he wants to," Trump responded: "Maybe I'll get him out of trouble. I may get him out of trouble by declassifying." Hours later Trump posted on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] directing the Secretary of War to begin releasing government files on alien life, UAPs and UFOs.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the exchange as a "controlled opposition dynamic" between Doocy and Obama designed to trap Trump in a dialectic about classified materials — "right on the heels of the Epstein File deployment" — which Trump instead "rugs" by declassifying everything.
