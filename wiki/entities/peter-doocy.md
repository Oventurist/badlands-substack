# Peter Doocy

Peter Doocy is a White House correspondent for [[fox-news|Fox News]] whose questions during presidential press gaggles have repeatedly generated widely circulated news cycles.[1]

## Air Force One alien exchange (February 2026)

In February 2026, Doocy questioned President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during a press gaggle aboard Air Force One about remarks that former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] had made concerning extraterrestrial life, asking, "Barack Obama said aliens are real. Have you seen any evidence of non-human visitors to Earth?"[1] Trump's response redirected the question toward the handling of classified material, saying of Obama, "He gave classified information. He's not supposed to be doing that."[1] When Doocy pressed on whether aliens are real, Trump replied that he did not know, but repeated that Obama "made a big mistake" by taking the claim "out of classified information."[1] Doocy then noted that "the President can declassify anything he wants to," to which Trump answered, "Maybe I'll get him out of trouble. I may get him out of trouble by declassifying."[1]

A few hours after the exchange, Trump posted on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] that he would direct the Secretary of War and other departments and agencies to begin identifying and releasing government files on alien and extraterrestrial life, unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP), and unidentified flying objects (UFOs).[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the Doocy–Obama sequence as a "controlled opposition dynamic" intended to trap Trump in a dialectic about classified and sensitive materials at a moment of enormous public interest, coming "right on the heels of the Epstein File deployment," and argued that Trump defused the maneuver by moving to declassify everything.[1] That reading places the episode within the broader [[alien-disclosure-op|alien disclosure operation]] framing used across the [[truth-community|Truth Community]].[1]

## Questioning over Biden's health (January 2024)

In January 2024, Doocy reacted to the revelation that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] had kept secret the details of Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]]'s cancer treatment by asking whether a cover-up also surrounded President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s health.[2] He grilled National Security Council Coordinator for Strategic Communications [[john-kirby|John Kirby]] at a press conference, demanding to know, "Why should we believe anything this administration tells us about anything ever again?"[2] After Kirby conceded that the Pentagon had faced a "challenge to credibility," Doocy shot back: "If the administration's going to go to such great lengths to keep secrets about the defense secretary's health, how can anybody be certain that the administration would not go to the same lengths to keep secret problems with President Biden's health in the future?"[2]
