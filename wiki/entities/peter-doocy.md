# Peter Doocy

Peter Doocy is an American television correspondent who covers the White House and regularly questions the president at press availabilities. He appears in the Badlands corpus as the questioner who prompted a widely noted shift in President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public posture toward Iran's leadership.

## The June 2026 Iran exchange

According to the June 5, 2026 [[badlands-brief-58d|Badlands Brief]], Trump was responding to a question from Doocy about whether he would be willing to meet Supreme Leader [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] when he delivered remarks that Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called "a jaw-dropping breakthrough in public diplomacy." Trump began by saying "I don't *want* to meet," then immediately shifted to say he would be "honored" to meet the Supreme Leader, adding that "some people have suggested it."

The commentary treats Doocy's question as the occasion for what it describes as a rebranding of the Ayatollah as a reasonable actor, executed "with brilliant tact," and as a signal that Washington would not pursue the overthrow of [[iran|Iran]]'s existing system of government.
