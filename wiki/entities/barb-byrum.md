# Barb Byrum

Barb Byrum is the Ingham County, Michigan clerk and a Democrat.[1]

## Response to the 2025 voting integrity executive order

Byrum publicly warned that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election integrity executive order could cause voting delays or render some votes ineligible to be counted.[1] "This will impact every voter in some way," she said.[1] She argued specifically that the order could create problems for married women who have not changed their name on their birth certificate, lengthen lines at polling places, or result in votes not counting at all.[1]

The order tied federal election funding to state compliance; Michigan received over $20 million in federal election funding in 2020 according to the U.S. Election Assistance Commission.[1] The GOP-led Michigan state House separately introduced a constitutional amendment requiring proof of citizenship in state elections.[1]
