# Anita Dunn

**Anita Dunn** is a Democratic political strategist who served as a senior adviser to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], frequently described as having the president's ear.[1] She is married to [[bob-bauer|Bob Bauer]], Biden's personal attorney.[1]

## 2024 debate fallout

Following Biden's poor performance in the June 2024 debate against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], members of the Biden family privately criticized the president's top campaign advisers during a weekend gathering at Camp David and urged him to fire or demote members of his political high command, with Dunn among those blamed alongside Bauer and former chief of staff [[ron-klain|Ron Klain]].[1] Family complaints reported by Politico included that Biden had not been prepared to pivot to attack, that he dwelt too much on defending his record instead of laying out a second-term vision, and that he was overworked and under-rested.[1] There was no immediate expectation that Biden would act on the advice; campaign spokesperson Kevin Munoz said the aides who prepped the president had been with him for years or decades and that Biden maintained strong confidence in them, while a senior aide called it "not true" that frustration was directed at Dunn, Bauer, and Klain.[1]
