# Balticconnector

The Balticconnector is a subsea natural gas pipeline running beneath the Baltic Sea between [[finland|Finland]] and [[estonia|Estonia]], with a length of roughly 77 kilometres (48 miles).[1] It is operated on the Finnish side by the state-linked grid operator Gasgrid.[1]

## 2023 damage

The pipeline was shut down early on Sunday, 8 October 2023, amid concerns that gas was leaking from a hole in the line.[1] Finnish authorities subsequently concluded that the damage — along with damage to a parallel telecommunications cable operated by Elisa — was likely the result of "outside activity," raising suspicion of deliberate sabotage.[1] Gasgrid said repairs could take months or more.[1] The pipeline damage was believed to have occurred within Finnish waters, while the cable breach occurred in Estonian waters.[1] The episode is described in detail at [[2023-balticconnector-pipeline-damage|2023 Balticconnector pipeline damage]].[1]

Because gas represents only about 5 percent of Finland's energy supply, Finnish Prime Minister [[petteri-orpo|Petteri Orpo]] said the country's overall energy supply remained stable despite the shutdown.[1]
