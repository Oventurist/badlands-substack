# Henri de Castries

Henri de Castries is a French businessman identified in [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s March 2023 article as the then-current chairman of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1]

DeLarme places him at the end of a line of chairmen that began with [[prince-bernhard|Prince Bernhard]], who personally chaired the group until his 1976 resignation over payoffs from Lockheed, and continued with Britain's [[peter-carrington|Lord Peter Carrington]], the former cabinet minister, NATO secretary-general and president of the Royal Institute of International Affairs.[1] Under de Castries's chairmanship, DeLarme writes, the group continues to meet annually at plush resorts around the globe with its activities cloaked in secrecy, while broader public attention has shifted toward more visible bodies such as the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1]
