# Vernon L. Stauffer

Vernon L. Stauffer, Ph.D., was the author of *New England and the Bavarian Illuminati* (1918), whose "Chapter III: The European Illuminati" is the most frequently cited source in the Badlands Media feature "Legacy of the Bavarian Illuminati – Part 2," abbreviated there as **VS**.[1]

Stauffer supplies the article's biographical backbone for [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]]: his guardianship under [[johann-adam-ickstatt|Baron Johann Adam Ickstatt]] and free run of his godfather's library, his 1768 graduation and years as tutor and catechist, his assumption of the chair of canon law in 1773, his promotion to dean of the faculty of law in 1775, and the death of his wife in 1780.[1]

He is also the source for the Order's takeover of the Munich Lodge Theodore of Good Counsel by Weishaupt's "Insinuators" by mid-1779, for [[baron-von-knigge|Baron von Knigge]]'s 1780 initiation, restructuring and broadened recruitment — which brought membership to "between two and three thousand" by 1784 — and for Knigge's 1784 resignation and formal agreement of silence.[1] Stauffer further documents the defection of [[joseph-utzschneider|Joseph Utzschneider]], the edicts of [[carl-theodore|Duke Carl Theodore]], the judicial inquiries at Ingolstadt, Weishaupt's flight to Regensburg and Gotha, the publication of the seized [[xavier-zwack|Zwack]] documents, and the text of the final 1787 edict prescribing death by the sword for continued recruiting.[1]
