# Charles Kushner

**Charles Kushner** is an American real estate developer and philanthropist, founder of the firm Kushner Companies, and the father of [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], son-in-law of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] In late November 2024, President-elect Trump announced that Kushner would serve as United States Ambassador to France.[1]

## Nomination as ambassador to France (2024)

In a Truth Social post published on Saturday, November 30, 2024, Trump wrote that he was pleased to nominate Kushner, of New Jersey, to serve as U.S. Ambassador to France, describing him as "a tremendous business leader, philanthropist, & dealmaker, who will be a strong advocate representing our Country & its interests."[1] Trump added that together they would strengthen America's partnership with France, "our oldest Ally, & one of our greatest."[1]

French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] had congratulated Trump quickly after his election win and said he was ready to work with the incoming administration, writing on X: "Ready to work together as we did for four years. With your convictions and mine. With respect and ambition. For more peace and prosperity."[1] Macron also said the role of the European Union was not to comment on whether Trump's election was good or bad, since "he was elected by the American people, and he will defend the interests of the Americans."[1]

## Background and 2005 prosecution

Kushner is known for founding Kushner Companies and for his earlier work as an attorney; he was appointed to the U.S. Holocaust Memorial Council and serves on the Board of Trustees of New York University, where Trump's youngest son Barron Trump attends school.[1] In 2005 he was sentenced to two years in prison for illegal campaign contributions and witness tampering in a case prosecuted by [[chris-christie|Chris Christie]], who described Kushner's conduct as among "the most loathsome, disgusting crimes" he had ever prosecuted.[1] Kushner was disbarred as a result of the prosecution and was pardoned by President Trump in December 2020.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the Kushner announcement, together with the [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] FBI nomination, functioned as [[narrative-shielding|narrative shielding]] around the simultaneous appointment of [[massad-boulos|Massad Boulos]] as senior adviser on Arab and Middle Eastern affairs, since media attention would gravitate to Jared Kushner's father.[1] He further noted reports that Jared visited his father by private jet every weekend of his imprisonment, and — pointing to Christie's reputation as a favored spokesman for AIPAC — speculated about what role the Israeli oligarchy may have played in the prosecution and whether Jared harbors a grudge.[1]
