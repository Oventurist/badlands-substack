# David Garrow

David Garrow is an American historian and biographer of former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[1] His research drew renewed attention after he revealed that Obama had told an ex-girlfriend that he "repeatedly fantasizes about making love to men."[1]

## Impact of the disclosure

The disclosure fueled sustained online discussion of Obama's sexual orientation and revived long-running internet speculation about [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]].[1] It also formed the backdrop for the September 2023 interview in which [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] hosted [[larry-sinclair|Larry Sinclair]], who has alleged drug-fueled sexual encounters with Obama in 2008.[1] Coverage of the interview by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] explicitly framed Garrow's reporting as the point at which "the internet has been abuzz" about the subject.[1]
