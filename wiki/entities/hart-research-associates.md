# Hart Research Associates

Hart Research Associates is an American public opinion research firm that conducts the Democratic side of the bipartisan [[nbc-news|NBC News]] national poll.[1]

## Role in the November 2023 NBC News poll

Democratic pollster [[jeff-horwitt|Jeff Horwitt]] of Hart Research Associates conducted the [[november-2023-nbc-news-poll|November 2023 NBC News survey]] jointly with Republican pollster [[bill-mcinturff|Bill McInturff]] of [[public-opinion-strategies|Public Opinion Strategies]].[1] The poll found President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s approval rating at 40%, the lowest of his presidency, with strong majorities of voters disapproving of his handling of foreign policy and of the [[israel-palestine-conflict|Israel-Hamas war]].[1]

Summarizing the findings, Horwitt said that "Joe Biden is at a uniquely low point in his presidency, and a significant part of this, especially within the Biden coalition, is due to how Americans are viewing his foreign policy actions."[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] acknowledged that polling carries a deserved poor reputation within the [[truth-community|Truth Community]], but argued that mainstream polls conducted by firms such as Hart Research nevertheless serve as a way to discern narrative intentions on the part of the [[uniparty|Establishment Uniparty]] and to read internal consistency within the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]].[1]
