# Anouska De Georgiou

Anouska De Georgiou is one of the survivors of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s abuse who has publicly campaigned for the release of government records relating to his crimes.[1]

## Call for congressional action

In a September 2025 interview with ABC News' Linsey Davis, De Georgiou appeared alongside six other women who say Epstein sexually exploited them, pleading with [[congress|Congress]] and the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration to release the hundreds of thousands of remaining files related to the sex offender.[1] The survivors described years of legal setbacks, limited accountability, and delayed justice, and said full disclosure would show the public the scope of Epstein's actions and allow victims to heal.[1]

"Now it is the responsibility of Congress to pass a bill so that we don't have to carry the baton of being the ones to enforce this," De Georgiou said. "This is not our job. We have done our job. We've been responsible in reporting, so I want transparency."[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the campaign was being channelled in a legally counterproductive direction by Representative [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]], who proposed naming alleged abusers on the House floor under the speech and debate clause rather than in court.[1]
