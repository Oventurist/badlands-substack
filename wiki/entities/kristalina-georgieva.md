# Kristalina Georgieva

Kristalina Georgieva is the managing director of the [[international-monetary-fund|International Monetary Fund]].[1]

## Global CBDC platform

During a conference in Morocco, Georgieva announced that the IMF is "working hard on the concept of a global CBDC platform."[1] Badlands Media treats the statement as confirmation that [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]] are being coordinated at the supranational level rather than left to individual national central banks, placing the IMF alongside the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and the [[european-union|European Union]] among institutions calling for the elimination of paper money and a move toward a cashless society in which every transaction can be tracked.[1]
