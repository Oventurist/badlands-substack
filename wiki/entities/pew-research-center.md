# Pew Research Center

The Pew Research Center is an American nonpartisan polling and social science research organization whose surveys are widely cited in political and foreign-policy reporting.[1]

## Polling on Israel and Netanyahu

In the July 2026 debate over whether Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had damaged [[israel|Israel]]'s standing with the American public, Pew findings served as the empirical basis of the argument: unfavorable views of Israel and declining confidence in Netanyahu had risen since 2022, with opinion worsening across demographic groups and among younger voters in both major parties.[1] Commentators used these results to argue that Israel had shifted from a largely bipartisan issue in American politics to an increasingly polarizing one.[1]
