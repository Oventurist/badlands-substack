# Conspiracy of Truth

Conspiracy of Truth is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program introduced as a new addition to the network's lineup in the January 16–22, 2023 schedule.[1] It airs Thursdays at 5pm ET.[1]

## Format and hosts

Co-hosted by [[praying-medic|Praying Medic]] and [[mary-grace|Mary Grace]], the show discusses news, current events, and spiritual growth through the lens of faith and freedom.[1] It is one of the more explicitly religious programs on the schedule, alongside the faith-inflected morning show [[rattlergator-report|RattlerGator Report]].[1]

## Distribution

The program streams live and in replay on the main Badlands Media [[rumble|Rumble]] channel.[1]
