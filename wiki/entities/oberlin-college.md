# Oberlin College

Oberlin College is the Ohio institution at which the American temperance movement was founded following the Civil War.[1]

According to the account in [[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.*]] as relayed by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], Oberlin was founded by British "Christian missionaries," required all students to become vegetarians, and was organized around the principle that the material world was wholly evil.[1] The temperance movement that emerged there was, in its early period, characterized as a violent cult known as "Organized Motherlove," whose members made headlines during the 1910s through assaults on saloons throughout the Ohio Valley.[1] Carlson argues these details cohere only if the elite financiers of the [[womens-christian-temperance-union|Women's Christian Temperance Union]] were funding an anti-pleasure cult because [[prohibition|Prohibition]] would create the organizational infrastructure for trafficking bootleg liquor, drugs, and possibly humans.[1]
