# Serina Baker-Hill

Serina Baker-Hill is a United States [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] official who, at age 55, served as Director of CBP's Center for Excellence and Expertise over Automotive and Aerospace Engineering.[1]

## Federal charges (March 2025)

On March 12, 2025, the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] announced that Baker-Hill had been charged with federal offences including a scheme to defraud the [[fema|Federal Emergency Management Agency]] through disaster aid and lying to federal agents.[1] The charges stemmed from an application for FEMA funds submitted in 2023 following the Detroit floods.[1] A federal criminal complaint charging her was unsealed that day, announced by Acting United States Attorney Julie A. Beck.[1]
