# Conservative Partnership Institute

The Conservative Partnership Institute (CPI) is a Washington-based organization described as a nerve center for incubating policies for a second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## October 2022 Ukraine discussion

CPI co-sponsored a discussion in October 2022 on how to bring "peace in Ukraine," featuring Hungarian Minister of Foreign Affairs [[peter-szijjarto|Peter Szijjarto]].[1] Attendees included conservative policy and national security officials and Republican strategists, who on being seated were handed pamphlets pushing what POLITICO characterized as unabashedly pro-Russia talking points.[1]

The document warned that "Russia has the will, strength, and patience to continue war," and urged that "U.S aid to Ukraine must be severely constricted and Ukrainian President Zelensky should be encouraged by U.S. leadership to seek armistice and concede Ukraine as a neutral country."[1] It further argued that continued US enablement of the war would destroy Ukraine and provoke further Russian aggression toward the West, "with the potential for nuclear conflict."[1]

Months before the event, President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had awarded Szijjarto an Order of Friendship medal, the highest Russian state decoration available to a foreigner.[1] Former Republican officials subsequently sounded the alarm over Trump's embrace of Hungarian Prime Minister [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]].[1]
