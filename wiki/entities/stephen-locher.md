# Stephen Locher

Stephen Locher is a United States District Judge in Iowa, appointed by President Joe Biden.[1]

## 2024 election ruling

On Sunday, November 3, 2024, Locher issued the decision in the [[iowa-noncitizen-voter-challenge-ruling|Iowa noncitizen voter challenge]] brought by the [[american-civil-liberties-union|ACLU]] and a group of naturalized citizens against Secretary of State [[paul-pate|Paul Pate]].[1] He declined to grant the requested injunction, noting that the Supreme Court had allowed Virginia to continue automatically purging voters while state officials appealed a lower court's order blocking the program, and citing the Supreme Court's guidance that courts should "act with great caution before awarding last-minute injunctive relief into how Iowa officials handle election issues."[1]

Locher wrote that whatever concerns the plaintiffs might have about the nature and timing of Pate's letter, it would not be appropriate for the court to respond with relief that "effectively forces local election officials to allow ineligible voters to vote" — a line Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted as the core of the decision.[1]
