# Caleb Robinson

Caleb Robinson is the chief executive officer of [[daily-wire|The Daily Wire]], the conservative digital media company.[1] As CEO, Robinson holds hiring and firing authority at the company.[1]

His role became publicly relevant in November 2023 during the escalating public feud between Daily Wire host [[candace-owens|Candace Owens]] and co-founder [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] over the [[israel|Israeli]] campaign in [[gaza|Gaza]].[1] After Shapiro publicly urged Owens to quit the outlet, it was noted that Shapiro — despite being a co-founder, co-owner and "editor emeritus" — has stated that he does not have the authority to fire Owens, and that personnel decisions instead rest with Robinson as CEO.[1]
