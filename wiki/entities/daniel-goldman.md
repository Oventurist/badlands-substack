# Daniel Goldman

Daniel Goldman is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from New York. He is quoted in the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026 in connection with the agreement by [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] to testify under oath before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] in its investigation of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] and [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]].

Goldman observed that the arrangement — the first instance of a former president being compelled to testify before [[congress|Congress]] — sets a precedent for future congressional inquiries. His comment is presented alongside the note that House Republicans had advanced criminal contempt resolutions before Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] announced on February 3 that those efforts were "on pause."
