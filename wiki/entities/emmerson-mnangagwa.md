# Emmerson Mnangagwa

Emmerson Mnangagwa is the President of [[zimbabwe|Zimbabwe]].[1] He is quoted in the July 2024 Badlands News Brief in the context of Zimbabwean outreach to Russia over the perceived American military buildup in neighboring [[zambia|Zambia]].[1]

"The United States is making us feel lonely," Mnangagwa remarked, a statement the Brief reads as indicating a perceived encirclement of Zimbabwe by Western powers.[1] His comments are placed alongside prior Badlands coverage of a fertilizer deal between [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and Zimbabwe credited with delivering a historic crop yield, and are used to frame the [[africom|AFRICOM]] presence in the region as a destabilizing wedge between two historically close allies.[1]

## Remarks at SPIEF 2024

Speaking to [[tass|TASS]] on the sidelines of the [[2024-st-petersburg-international-economic-forum|St. Petersburg International Economic Forum]] in June 2024, Mnangagwa said [[zimbabwe|Zimbabwe]] was grateful to Russia for supplies of grain and fertilizer and that the country would have "an incredible harvest" that year "because we have enough fertilizer."[2] He added that Zimbabwe would need more supplies, that "Russia gives us lower prices and convenient payment methods than anywhere else," and that his government was "happy with the existing agreements and opportunities."[2] At the same forum he declared that "Africa will soon roar like a lion."[2]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the remarks as evidence of the power of bilateral agreements over what he called elitist trade organizations that skim off the top, arguing that any competitor wishing to take that market must now offer Zimbabwe better value.[2]
