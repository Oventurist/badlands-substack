# Emmerson Mnangagwa

Emmerson Mnangagwa is the President of [[zimbabwe|Zimbabwe]].[1] He is quoted in the July 2024 Badlands News Brief in the context of Zimbabwean outreach to Russia over the perceived American military buildup in neighboring [[zambia|Zambia]].[1]

"The United States is making us feel lonely," Mnangagwa remarked, a statement the Brief reads as indicating a perceived encirclement of Zimbabwe by Western powers.[1] His comments are placed alongside prior Badlands coverage of a fertilizer deal between [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and Zimbabwe credited with delivering a historic crop yield, and are used to frame the [[africom|AFRICOM]] presence in the region as a destabilizing wedge between two historically close allies.[1]
