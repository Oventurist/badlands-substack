# United States District Court for the District of Colorado

The United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the federal trial court of general jurisdiction for the state of Colorado. In the Badlands Media corpus it is best known as the forum for [[naacp-v-useip|NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.]], the 2022–2024 voter-intimidation suit brought by the [[naacp|NAACP]], the [[league-of-women-voters|League of Women Voters of Colorado]], and [[mi-familia-vota|Mi Familia Vota]] against the [[useip|United States Election Integrity Plan]].

The essay [[always-fight-back|"Always Fight Back"]] presents the court's handling of that case as a corrective to what its author, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], calls the "leftist lawfare machine." Presiding judge [[charlotte-sweeney|Charlotte Sweeney]] cut the trial short in July 2024 under Rule 52(c) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, entering judgment for the defendants on partial findings and awarding costs. The article frames the ruling as a vindication of [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] protections for election skepticism and door-to-door canvassing, while also questioning how a case the court found devoid of intimidating evidence survived the procedural checks against tortious abuse built into the Federal Rules.
