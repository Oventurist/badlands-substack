# Satya Nadella

Satya Nadella is the chief executive officer of [[microsoft|Microsoft]].[1] He was among the artificial-intelligence industry leaders who attended the 2023 [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] meeting in Lisbon, Portugal, a gathering at which artificial intelligence was the primary topic of discussion.[1]

The Badlands Media article "Dark Equation" lists Nadella alongside [[deepmind|DeepMind]]'s [[demis-hassabis|Demis Hassabis]], [[openai|OpenAI]] chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] and Bilderberg chair [[eric-schmidt|Eric Schmidt]], presenting their joint attendance as evidence that the commercial developers of AI systems and the transnational policy elite were converging on a shared agenda behind closed doors.[1]
