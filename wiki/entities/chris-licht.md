# Chris Licht

Chris Licht is a television executive who became chief executive of [[cnn|CNN]], where he publicly stated that his goal was to steer the network in a less partisan direction.[1]

## Handling of the Don Lemon controversy

In February 2023, anchor [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] said on air that presidential candidate and former United Nations ambassador [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] "isn't in her prime," a remark made in response to Haley's call for politicians over the age of 75 to take mental competency tests. Licht said he was "disappointed" by the comments.[1]

Lemon was absent from the CNN morning show on the Friday following the remarks and, according to reporting cited by The Post Millennial, was removed from the Monday lineup as well; the revised schedule paired Poppy Harlow with Sara Sidner and [[kaitlan-collins|Kaitlan Collins]]. A network source described conversations about Lemon's future as "ongoing" and called him "a constant distraction."[1]

Badlands Media commentary framed the episode as evidence that Licht was following through on his stated depoliticization agenda, noting that Lemon had been demoted for attacking a Republican politician, and asking whether the move signaled a broader shift at CNN away from its previous editorial posture.[1]
