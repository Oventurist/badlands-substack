# Uyghurs

The Uyghurs are a Turkic Muslim ethnic minority in [[china|China]], concentrated in the country's northwest, and one of the 56 ethnic groups officially recognized by the Chinese state.[1] They are among the communities most frequently cited in international criticism of Beijing's minority policy.[1]

## Ethnic Unity Law concerns, 2026

When China's [[national-peoples-congress|National People's Congress]] moved in March 2026 to advance a proposed [[ethnic-unity-law|Ethnic Unity Law]], human rights organizations raised concerns that the legislation could expand existing programs affecting minority communities, naming the Uyghurs and Tibetans specifically.[1] Critics argued the law could codify policies pressuring minority groups to assimilate into Han Chinese cultural and political norms, particularly in regions with large minority populations.[1] Chinese officials maintained the measure was intended to promote unity, stability, and development in minority regions.[1]

## The XPCC and Harvard

In Badlands Media's "The Ivy Web," the [[xinjiang-production-and-construction-corps|Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps]] is described as a Chinese paramilitary corporate entity sanctioned by the United States in 2020 for its role in the Uyghur genocide, and as a force that suppresses the Uyghurs through land seizures and armed control.[2] The article criticises [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] for training XPCC officials as late as October 2023 despite those sanctions and the protections of the [[uyghur-forced-labor-prevention-act|Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act]] of 2022.[2]

## Selective outrage argument (Carlson, 2023)

In "The Systematic Dismantling of the Deep State," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] used the treatment of the Uyghurs as his central example of manipulated public attention.[3] He wrote that one to two million Uyghur Muslims were forced into "re-education camps" by the [[chinese-communist-party|CCP]] beginning in 2014, and that it became apparent they were being tortured, raped, forced into labour and made to renounce Islam, with some having their organs harvested or being murdered.[3]

Carlson asked where [[black-lives-matter|BLM]], [[the-squad|"the Squad"]], college protesters and the mainstream media were during that period, answering that they were preoccupied with outrage at [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over a past locker-room comment and allegations of caging children at the border — in other words, outraged about whatever the media instructed them to be outraged about.[3] His explanation was that the Uyghurs are not part of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] Cabal's theatre while Palestinian Muslims are, so the public is conditioned to demand war over the death and displacement of thousands while ignoring that of millions.[3] He added that China receives a pass so long as it delivers consumer goods such as the iPhone 15 in time for Black Friday.[3]
