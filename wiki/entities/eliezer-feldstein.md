# Eliezer Feldstein

Eliezer Feldstein is an Israeli political aide to Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] who was arrested in November 2024 over the leak of classified Israeli military intelligence to foreign media.[1]

## Arrest and leak allegations

Israeli police arrested Feldstein and four others for allegedly leaking classified information to foreign outlets, according to court documents released on Sunday, November 3, 2024.[1] The names of the four other detainees were withheld by Israel's military censors.[1] The leaked intelligence allegedly claimed that [[hamas|Hamas]] was planning to smuggle Israeli captives from Gaza to Egypt.[1]

The material was passed to two foreign publications — the *Jewish Chronicle* in the United Kingdom and *Bild* in Germany — both of which published stories based on it; the *Jewish Chronicle* later retracted its account.[1] Court documents said the information had been taken from the Israeli military's systems and "illegally issued," and may have damaged Israel's ability to free the captives held in Gaza.[1]

## Political context

Opposition leaders said the intelligence had been leaked in order to relieve pressure on Netanyahu to conclude a ceasefire deal with Hamas that would return the roughly 100 Israeli captives still held by the Palestinian resistance, of whom an estimated 70 remained alive.[1] Netanyahu was described as having repeatedly sabotaged ceasefire talks since the start of the war on October 7, 2023, despite heavy pressure from captives' families.[1]
