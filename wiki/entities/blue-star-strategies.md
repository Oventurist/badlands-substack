# Blue Star Strategies

Blue Star Strategies is a Washington, D.C. consulting and government-relations firm co-founded by [[sally-painter|Sally Painter]] and [[karen-tramontano|Karen Tramontano]]. The firm figures prominently in emails recovered from [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden's abandoned laptop]], where it appears as the outside consultancy retained by the Ukrainian gas company [[burisma|Burisma]] in late 2015 as Ukrainian prosecutors pursued the company's owner, [[mykola-zlochevsky|Mykola Zlochevsky]].[1]

## Retention by Burisma

The earliest documented link between Painter and Burisma in the laptop archive is an email dated 23 October 2015 titled "Schedule for Next week," which listed [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s calendar for the week of 26 October 2015 and showed a meeting placed on hold between Hunter, [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]], [[vadym-pozharskyi|Vadym Pozharskyi]] and Painter.[1] One week later, an email chain titled "Revised Burisma Proposal, Contract and Invoice" circulated among Painter, [[rosemont-seneca|Rosemont Seneca]] and Pozharskyi, in which Blue Star transmitted a "revised proposal, contract and initial invoice for Burisma Holdings."[1]

Pozharskyi replied with detailed "observations" on the Blue Star documents. He complained that the proposal lacked "tangible results" and named no "top US officials in Ukraine" as targets who could improve Zlochevsky's position, while allowing that the omission may have been deliberate in order to stay "on the safe and cautious side" — something he said he could understand so long as everyone grasped the "true purpose" of the "BS" engagement.[1] He asked for deliverables including "meetings/communications resulting in high-ranking US officials in Ukraine (US Ambassador) and in US publicly or in private communication/comment expressing their 'positive opinion' and support of Nikolay/Burisma" to Ukraine's president, chief of staff and prosecutor general.[1] The final portion of the email described the "ultimate purpose" of the contract as organising a visit of "widely recognized and influential current and/or former US policy-makers" to Ukraine in November 2015 in order "to close down for any cases/pursuits against Nikolay in Ukraine."[1]

[[eric-schwerin|Eric Schwerin]] wrote to Archer and Hunter Biden confirming that the omission of US officials' names was "definitely done deliberately to be on the safe and cautious side and that Sally and company understand the scope and deliverables."[1] Archer asked Hunter to relay that message; Hunter told Pozharskyi he would schedule one more call with Blue Star but that "I trust Sally and Karen implicitly so I believe we are all aligned."[1] Pozharskyi answered that if Hunter and Archer were "comfortable that they will deliver what in real terms we are talking about, we should disregard the wording of the scope," and Hunter told him to "go ahead and sign."[1] The contract was signed in mid-November 2015.[1]

## Activity around Joe Biden's December 2015 Ukraine trip

Beginning 23 November 2015 a series of "Burisma/Blue Star" strategy emails and calls were exchanged.[1] An email of 2 December 2015 recorded that Blue Star Strategies took part in a conference call with [[white-house|the White House]] "regarding the Vice President's upcoming trip to Ukraine."[1] [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] travelled to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] on 7–8 December 2015 and addressed the Verkhovna Rada on 9 December; on 7 December Archer requested a call between "the Blue Star and Burisma teams."[1] Biden later said publicly that he had threatened to withhold $1 billion in US loan guarantees unless prosecutor general [[viktor-shokin|Viktor Shokin]] was removed.[1]

## Contacts with the State Department

In a December 2020 transcribed interview with the [[senate-homeland-security-and-governmental-affairs-committee|Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs]] and Finance committees, [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] disclosed contacts with Painter, including a 27 June 2016 email in which Painter, having spoken with him at a [[truman-national-security-project|Truman]] event, sought a meeting to discuss "troubling events we are seeing in Ukraine."[1] About a month later a Blue Star message to Rosemont Seneca noted "No help from our friend(s) however."[1]

## Investigation and closure

In June 2021 it was reported that Blue Star Strategies was under investigation for illegal lobbying, and that Ukrainian prosecutor general [[yuriy-lutsenko|Yuriy Lutsenko]] had been "pitched" by the Hunter Biden– and Burisma-linked firm with offers of "access" to "high levels" of the Clinton campaign in the weeks before the 2016 presidential election.[1] In May 2022 it was reported that the [[us-department-of-justice|Justice Department]]-led investigation into the firm had been closed with no finding of wrongdoing.[1]
