# Jack Ciattarelli

Jack Ciattarelli is a New Jersey Republican politician and repeat candidate for governor of the state. He is best known nationally for his 2021 gubernatorial campaign, in which he came within roughly three percentage points of unseating New Jersey's incumbent Democratic governor — an unexpectedly narrow result in a state that had been considered safely Democratic.[1]

## 2025 gubernatorial campaign and Trump endorsement

On May 12, 2025, [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] inserted his influence into the New Jersey governor's race by endorsing Ciattarelli ahead of the June 10 Republican primary.[1] Writing on Truth Social, Trump called Ciattarelli "a terrific America First Candidate running to be the next Governor of a State that I love, NEW JERSEY!" and said Ciattarelli was "strongly supported by the most Highly Respected Leaders in New Jersey" and would, as governor, "work closely with me and the Trump Administration."[1] Reporting at the time judged that the public backing of the president was likely to solidify Ciattarelli's odds in the primary.[1]

## Commentary

Commentary carried in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief was skeptical of the endorsement, framing Ciattarelli as "the guy who lost in the fake gubernatorial election of 2021" — the same cycle in which [[glenn-youngkin|Glenn Youngkin]] won the Virginia governorship on what the commentator characterized as a "ConInc anti-woke campaign."[1] The Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] used the endorsement to satirize intra-movement pressure to "trust the plan," noting that many Trump supporters were conspicuously unwilling to defend this particular endorsement.[1]

## 2025 New Jersey gubernatorial election

Ciattarelli, the Republican nominee, was defeated by Democrat [[mikie-sherrill|Mikie Sherrill]] in the November 2025 New Jersey general election.[2] Shortly after the win, Sherrill said she was considering stopping the state from paying federal taxes in protest of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's policies.[2]
