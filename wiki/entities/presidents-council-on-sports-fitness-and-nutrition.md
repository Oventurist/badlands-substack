# President's Council on Sports, Fitness and Nutrition

The President's Council on Sports, Fitness and Nutrition is a federal advisory panel that encourages healthy eating and physical activity.[1] President Joe Biden renewed the council through September 30, 2025.[1]

The body entered the December 2024 news cycle when [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]] suggested in a Fox News interview with Brian Kilmeade that President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] might give him a role in the incoming administration.[1] Hogan recounted a conversation at Trump's October rally at Madison Square Garden in which, as the former professional wrestler told it, Trump said: "You know something, you'd be great to run the President's Council on Physical Fitness."[1] Hogan said the suggestion arose from a backstage discussion about Robert Kennedy, nutrition, and the observation that many foreign countries will not allow their populations to eat the food sold in America, which he said had "poisoned a generation of kids."[1]
