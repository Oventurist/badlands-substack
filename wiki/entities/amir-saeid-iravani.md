# Amir Saeid Iravani

Amir Saeid Iravani is Iran's ambassador to the United Nations, who in November 2024 met privately with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] at the ambassador's residence in New York.[1]

The meeting was first reported by The New York Times, which said two Iranian officials described it as a discussion of how to defuse tensions between the United States and [[iran|Iran]].[1] According to CBS News reporting, there was no indication that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s national security transition team was aware of the meeting, nor was it clear that Iran hawks [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]] — Trump's picks for secretary of state and national security adviser — knew of it, or that Trump himself did.[1] Iran quickly rushed to deny reports of the meeting, which Badlands commentary treated as expected and immaterial to the public narrative.[1]
