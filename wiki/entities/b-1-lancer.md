# B-1 Lancer

The Rockwell B-1 Lancer is a long-range United States Air Force heavy bomber that became the focus of an October 2025 escalation of American military pressure on [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

## Capabilities

The B-1 can fly at supersonic speeds and carry 75,000 pounds of bombs, more than any other U.S. bomber, and can also conduct maritime surveillance.[1] Because of the aircraft's range, the bombers were not repositioned to bases in the Caribbean region — a defense official noted they can reach anywhere in the Caribbean directly from the continental United States.[1]

## The October 2025 Venezuela flights

According to The Wall Street Journal, two B-1 Lancers took off from Dyess Air Force Base in Texas on Thursday, October 23, 2025, and flew near Venezuela while remaining in international airspace, per a U.S. official and flight-tracking data.[1] The flights came only days after other American warplanes carried out an "attack demonstration" near the South American country, further ratcheting up pressure on President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] denied the report, telling reporters: "No, that's not accurate. But we're not happy with Venezuela."[1]
