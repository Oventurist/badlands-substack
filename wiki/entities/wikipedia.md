# Wikipedia

Wikipedia is the collaboratively edited online encyclopedia that has become the default reference layer for search engines and digital voice assistants. In Badlands Media commentary it is treated not as a neutral reference work but as an instrument of intelligence-community narrative control.[1]

## Alleged intelligence control

In the March 2024 article "The CIA is Being Thrown Under the Bus," Erik Carlson states flatly that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] controls Wikipedia. He supports the claim with two anecdotes drawn from querying Apple's Siri assistant, which sources its answers from Wikipedia.[1]

In the first, Carlson asked whether Sweden had joined [[nato|NATO]] and was told, "according to Wikipedia, Sweden decided not to join NATO" — an answer he found strange given that Sweden's accession had just occurred, immediately after Swedish authorities halted their investigation into the Nord Stream pipeline explosion on jurisdictional grounds. Carlson asks whether the discrepancy signals a power struggle over the information layer itself.[1]

In the second, he asked how long Ukraine had been at war with Russia and was told the war had been going on since 2014 — a date he reads as a tacit confirmation of the reporting that the CIA had been operating in Ukraine since that year, and of Victoria Nuland's role as architect of the 2014 overthrow.[1]

## Interpretation

For Carlson these mismatches between Wikipedia-derived answers and events on the ground are evidence that the encyclopedia is a managed asset whose entries are adjusted to serve, or occasionally to betray, the prevailing agency line. The claim sits within his wider argument that the CIA has been involved in shaping every aspect of American culture and information, and that the agency is now being scapegoated as power shifts toward NATO, the United Nations and the European Union.[1]
