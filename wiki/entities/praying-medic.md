# Praying Medic

Praying Medic is a commentator and host in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective.[1]

## Badlands Media role

He co-hosts [[conspiracy-of-truth|Conspiracy of Truth]] with [[mary-grace|Mary Grace]], a Thursday 5pm ET program added to the Badlands lineup in the January 16–22, 2023 schedule.[1] The show covers news, current events, and spiritual growth through the lens of faith and freedom, reflecting the religious emphasis Praying Medic brings to the network's commentary.[1]
