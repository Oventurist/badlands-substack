# White House

## Cartel policy and AI (2026)

Amid reporting that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration was preparing options for military action inside [[mexico|Mexico]], the White House maintained that its focus is dismantling transnational criminal organizations responsible for fentanyl trafficking, human smuggling and violence affecting the United States, with officials emphasizing cooperation with Mexico where possible while declining to rule out additional measures against cartel leadership.[1]

On technology policy, White House Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] reportedly said the administration aims to "protect open source AI," and former White House AI and Crypto Czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] argued that [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[anthropic|Anthropic]] seek regulation to stave off competitors.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] separately framed [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s repeated efforts to secure White House visits as a recurring pattern that Trump answers by elevating [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Erdogan]].[1]
