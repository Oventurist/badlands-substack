# Transgender Michigan

**Transgender Michigan** is a Michigan-based transgender advocacy organization headed by [[rachel-crandall|Rachel Crandall]].[1] The organization is chiefly known outside Michigan as the institutional origin of the [[transgender-day-of-visibility|Transgender Day of Visibility]], which Crandall established in 2009 and first held on March 31 of that year.[1]

Because the observance has been fixed to March 31 since its founding, the organization's original scheduling decision became a point of reference in the 2024 dispute over President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s Transgender Day of Visibility proclamation falling on Easter Sunday.[1]
