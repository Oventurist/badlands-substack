# Chuck Todd

**Chuck Todd** is an American broadcast journalist associated with [[nbc-news|NBC]], where he hosted the Sunday political program "Meet the Press."[1]

## February 2023 criticism of the Biden administration

On Sunday, February 12, 2023, Todd opened his program by criticizing the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] over what he characterized as a lack of transparency surrounding the Chinese spy balloon episode and the subsequent "high-altitude object" shot down over Alaska.[1] Todd faulted National Security Council spokesman [[john-kirby|Adm. John Kirby]] and the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] specifically, noting that Kirby had not volunteered information about the shootdown until a reporter in the briefing room asked about it.[1] "For some reason, the spokesperson, John Kirby, did not volunteer this information about fighter jets being scrambled to shoot down another object until a reporter in that room asked," Todd said.[1] An accompanying "Meet the Press" social media post said that in the previous 48 hours U.S. fighter jets had downed two more high-altitude objects over Alaska and Canada, that the military had shared little detail, and that "the White House and Pentagon missed opportunities for transparency."[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated Todd's coverage skeptically, grouping him with "DS media outlets" that were quick to report the shootdowns while emphasizing that Biden had taken "decisive action."[1] Deschamps argued the coordinated media treatment of the [[2023-north-american-aerial-object-shootdowns|aerial object shootdowns]] suggested "some agenda is being pushed," whether an operation to damage Biden ahead of the 2024 Democratic nomination or a "white hat" effort to expose long-running intelligence activities.[1]
