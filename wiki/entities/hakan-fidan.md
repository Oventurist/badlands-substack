# Hakan Fidan

Hakan Fidan is the Turkish foreign minister and former head of Turkey's national intelligence service. He is a central figure in [[turkey|Turkey]]'s diplomacy toward the Middle East and a prominent voice in the regional response to the 2026 Iran–Israel war.

## Role in the 2026 Iran–Israel war

The Badlands Brief edition [[badlands-brief-52b|"Golgotha & The Metaverse"]] (March 20, 2026) reports that following Israeli strikes on the [[south-pars-gas-field|South Pars gas field]], Fidan publicly blamed [[israel|Israel]] for what he described as an "unprecedented crisis." Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited Fidan's statement as one of the immediate second-order effects of the strike, alongside visible strain in the Gulf alignments [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been assembling — strain that, in his reading, gave Trump additional room to adjust his own geopolitical positioning.

The Brief also notes a subsequent joint press conference held by [[qatar|Qatar]] and Turkey, at which Qatar blamed Israel for the war and called for Arab reconciliation with [[iran|Iran]] as the route to regional peace.
