# Hakan Fidan

Hakan Fidan is the Turkish foreign minister and former head of Turkey's national intelligence service. He is a central figure in [[turkey|Turkey]]'s diplomacy toward the Middle East and a prominent voice in the regional response to the 2026 Iran–Israel war.

## Role in the 2026 Iran–Israel war

The Badlands Brief edition "Golgotha & The Metaverse" (March 20, 2026) reports that following Israeli strikes on the [[south-pars-gas-field|South Pars gas field]], Fidan publicly blamed [[israel|Israel]] for what he described as an "unprecedented crisis." Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited Fidan's statement as one of the immediate second-order effects of the strike, alongside visible strain in the Gulf alignments [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been assembling — strain that, in his reading, gave Trump additional room to adjust his own geopolitical positioning.

The Brief also notes a subsequent joint press conference held by [[qatar|Qatar]] and Turkey, at which Qatar blamed Israel for the war and called for Arab reconciliation with [[iran|Iran]] as the route to regional peace.

## The Board of Peace ceremony at Davos (January 2026)

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Fidan appeared on stage with Saudi Foreign Minister [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] at the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] charter ratification ceremony in Davos. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the pairing as "a strategic symbol" of Saudi-Turkey rapprochement — the two countries having just aided President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in negotiating a peace deal with Syrian President [[abu-mohammad-al-jolani|Al Jolani]] — and noted that Turkey and Qatar were also filling the vacuum in Gaza created by Israel's snub of the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]], per the Times of Israel's reporting.
