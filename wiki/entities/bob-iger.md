# Bob Iger

Bob Iger is the returned chief executive officer of The Walt Disney Company and a prominent figure in the American entertainment industry.[1]

## Role in the 2023 strikes

During the [[2023-hollywood-labor-strikes|2023 Hollywood labor strikes]], Iger emerged as what [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] called the villain of the narrative around collective bargaining.[1] Burning Bright described this as one of the most tantalizing trends of the actors' strike, noting that Iger had been widely rumored as being primed for a Democratic presidential run as recently as 2020 and was beloved by leftists and industry globalists.[1]

According to that commentary, Iger's nine-figure salary-and-bonus structure and his derogatory comments about what Burning Bright termed the "Hollywood slave class" were causing rank-and-file supporters — whom he called "useful idiots" — to turn on their "communist masters."[1]

## End of the "Invincible Era"

In March 2024, The Hollywood Reporter ran the headline "Bob Iger's Invincible Era Is Over," which Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited as evidence that the media establishment was "pinning the tail on the Igerian donkey" amid a wave of panic pieces and about-faces about the [[disney|Disney]] empire.[2] Burning Bright described Iger as "being thrown to the wolves in a media he once dominated as a Dem presidential hopeful," listing his fall alongside [[nelson-peltz|Nelson Peltz]]'s board campaign, Disney's settlement with Florida over its special status, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s support for Gina Carano's lawsuit, and [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]]'s legal offensive.[2]
