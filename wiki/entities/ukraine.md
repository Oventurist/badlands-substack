# Ukraine

Ukraine is a country in Eastern Europe whose 2014 Maidan uprising and subsequent trajectory toward [[nato|NATO]] are examined in the Badlands Media article [[a-more-perfect-labor-union|A More Perfect (Labor) Union]] as a case study of labor unions being used as geopolitical pawns.

## The 2014 Maidan uprising

The article describes the 2014 Maidan uprising as "a textbook example of labor unions as geopolitical pawns." It states that the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]], through [[national-endowment-for-democracy|NED]] grants exceeding $500,000, supported pro-Western labor groups that amplified strikes and protests against the Yanukovych government. These actions, the article argues, "dovetailed with State Department and DoD objectives to pull Ukraine into NATO's orbit." Ukrainian unions, trained in "nonviolent resistance" tactics derived from Gene Sharp's manuals, are described as "foot soldiers in a U.S.-orchestrated regime change operation."

Post-Maidan, the article claims the Solidarity Center continued funding to reshape Ukraine's labor laws, aligning them with EU standards — "less about worker protections and more about integrating Ukraine into Western economic structures."

The article also notes that leaked and declassified State Department cables from the 2010s reveal that union organizers in Ukraine were tasked with reporting on local conditions via secure apps linked to U.S. embassies.
