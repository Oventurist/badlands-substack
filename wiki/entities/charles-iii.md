# Charles III

Charles III is the reigning monarch of the United Kingdom and head of state of the Commonwealth realms, including Canada. In March 2025 he was undergoing ongoing medical treatment for cancer.[1]

## March 2025 hospital admission

On Thursday 27 March 2025, Buckingham Palace announced that the King had been admitted to hospital after experiencing adverse side effects connected to his cancer treatment. He was discharged from the London Clinic following what the Palace described as "a short period of observation," but was forced to cancel a series of engagements scheduled to take place in Birmingham the following day.[1]

The Palace sought to play down the severity of the episode, with sources indicating that the King's recovery path continued in a positive direction. In a rare public statement about his health, Buckingham Palace said that "following scheduled and ongoing medical treatment for cancer this morning, The King experienced temporary side effects that required a short period of observation in hospital," and that his afternoon engagements were therefore postponed.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributors linked the timing of the episode to the concurrent friction between the United States and Canada. Chris Paul remarked sardonically that the hospitalization came "right as he was about to offer us that coveted place in the collapsing British commonwealth," calling the timing strange.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] added a pointed aside offering "thoughts and prayers to all the Canadians concerned about the health of their monarch," a jab at the Canadian sovereignty rhetoric then being deployed by Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] amid the tariff dispute with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
