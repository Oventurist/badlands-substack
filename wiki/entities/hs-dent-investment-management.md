# HS Dent Investment Management

HS Dent Investment Management is the investment firm owned by economist [[harry-dent|Harry Dent]], whose research focuses on consumer spending and demographic cycles.[1]

In December 2023, speaking to Fox Business, Dent used the firm's platform to issue a warning that 2024 would be "the biggest single crash year we'll see in our lifetimes."[1] He argued that since 2009 the economy had been sustained by "100 percent artificial, unprecedented money printing and deficits" totaling $27 trillion over fifteen years, that US markets had been in a bubble beginning in late 2021 amid the COVID-19 pandemic, and that the coming decline would resemble the 1929–1932 collapse rather than a correction.[1] He advised investors to exit for six to twelve months, reasoning that the downside of being wrong was missing modest additional gains while the upside was avoiding massive losses and reinvesting at very low prices a year or so later.[1]

[[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] carried the remarks as a bonus item in the December 22, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
