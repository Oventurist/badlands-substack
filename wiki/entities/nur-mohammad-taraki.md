# Nur Mohammad Taraki

Nur Mohammad Taraki was the leader of the [[peoples-democratic-party-of-afghanistan|People's Democratic Party of Afghanistan]] (PDPA) and head of the communist government that took control of [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] in 1978.[1]

## Overthrow and death

Taraki led the PDPA's Khalq faction alongside his deputy [[hafizullah-amin|Hafizullah Amin]], who served as second in command and controlled most of the military. Amin, dissatisfied with the party's rule after the coup, outmaneuvered Taraki by staffing the PDPA Politburo with his own loyalists.[1]

Soviet leader [[leonid-brezhnev|Leonid Brezhnev]] tried to prevent Taraki's murder, but Amin had him killed in October 1979 — a move that deeply rattled the Soviet leadership. Badlands Media notes that Amin was torturing and killing tens of thousands of Afghans before he led the military coup that removed Taraki and took control of the country.[1]

## Significance

Taraki's killing is presented in Badlands Media's account as one of the immediate triggers of the [[soviet-union|Soviet Union]]'s December 1979 invasion, which the outlet argues was a defensive reaction to [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] penetration of Afghanistan rather than an act of communist expansion.[1]
