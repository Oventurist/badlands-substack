# Arizona Secretary of State

The Secretary of State of Arizona is the state's second-highest constitutional officer and its chief election official. Because Arizona has no lieutenant governor, the office stands first in the line of succession to the governorship under Article V, Section 6 of the Constitution of Arizona, which governs "death, resignation, removal or disability of governor; succession to office; impeachment, absence from state or temporary disability."

## Role in the 2022 election dispute

The office was held during the 2022 election cycle by [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], who was simultaneously the Democratic nominee for governor — a dual role that [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] treats in [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]] as an unaddressed conflict of interest. Gillespie questions why Attorney General [[mark-brnovich|Mark Brnovich]] rather than Hobbs was described as "acting governor" during Governor [[doug-ducey|Doug Ducey]]'s absence, given the constitutional order of succession, and suggests Hobbs may have been occupied "trying to certify her OWN ELECTION."

The office publishes statewide returns, including the "2022 Unofficial General Election Results" page from which Gillespie drew the vote totals for the U.S. Senate, gubernatorial, secretary of state, attorney general, and State Treasurer races. It is also the recipient of each county's official canvass, which under A.R.S. § 16-642(A) had to be submitted on or before November 28, 2022 — the deadline invoked by Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] in demanding answers from [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]].

## The 2022 succession

The office was held during the [[2022-united-states-midterm-elections|2022 midterm elections]] by [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], who certified the general election on December 5, 2022 while herself a candidate for governor — a conflict that features in both election contests filed on December 9. Republican [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]] lost the race for the office to Democrat [[adrian-fontes|Adrian Fontes]] and joined [[jeff-zink|Jeff Zink]] in suing to overturn the result. The Badlands Media article [[arizona-earthquake|"Arizona Earthquake!"]] notes that the Secretary of State stands first in the line of succession to the governorship.
