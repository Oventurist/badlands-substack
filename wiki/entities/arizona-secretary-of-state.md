# Arizona Secretary of State

The Arizona Secretary of State is the state's chief election officer and, under Article V, Section 6 of the Constitution of Arizona — entitled "Death, resignation, removal or disability of governor; succession to office; impeachment, absence from state or temporary disability" — stands first in the line of succession to the governorship, ahead of the Attorney General.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

During the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]] the office was held by [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], who was simultaneously the Democratic candidate for governor, an arrangement critics regarded as an unresolved conflict of interest.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) When Governor [[doug-ducey|Doug Ducey]] apparently left the state on Friday, November 18, 2022, creating a "temporary disability," Attorney General [[mark-brnovich|Mark Brnovich]] rather than Hobbs was described as acting governor. Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] said she had heard no one in mainstream or independent media explain the deviation from the constitutional order, and speculated that Hobbs was "TOO BUSY trying to certify her OWN ELECTION."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

The office publishes statewide returns; its "2022 Unofficial General Election Results" page was the source Gillespie used to compare turnout across statewide races.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) She found that 1,321,435 votes were cast for U.S. Senate, 1,287,322 for Governor, 1,320,059 for Secretary of State, and 1,254,096 for Attorney General, while 1,389,215 votes were cast for the low-profile office of [[kimberly-yee|State Treasurer]] — a race that outpolled every marquee contest, by 67,780 votes over Senate, 101,893 over Governor, 69,156 over Secretary of State, and 135,119 over Attorney General.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) Gillespie treated the anomaly as evidence that manipulation had not extended that far down the ballot.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

County governing bodies must submit their official canvass to the Secretary of State within the window set by A.R.S. § 16-642(A) — for the 2022 election, on or before November 28, 2022.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
