# Tim Allan

Tim Allan is a British communications professional who served as communications director to Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]].

## Departure amid Epstein-related scrutiny

According to the February 10, 2026 edition of the Badlands Brief, "Criminal Invader Crashouts & Honeypot Hoaxes," Allan was among the senior aides Starmer lost amid scrutiny connected to associations with the late financier [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], departing alongside chief of staff [[morgan-mcsweeney|Morgan McSweeney]]. The Brief noted that media reporting had placed Starmer under pressure over questions of judgment, vetting, and institutional accountability, and emphasised that no charges had been reported against the Prime Minister or his staff. The departures were presented as the British half of a wider pattern of [[epstein-narrative|Epstein-related]] political fallout occurring simultaneously on both sides of the Atlantic.
