# Vietnam

Vietnam is a Southeast Asian nation that serves as a major manufacturing base for exporters, including Chinese firms that relocated production there amid United States–China trade friction.[1]

## 2025 US–Vietnam tariff agreement

On July 2, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced via social media that the United States and Vietnam had reached a trade agreement under which US tariffs on Vietnamese imports would be set at 20 per cent, with a 40 per cent duty on goods deemed to be transshipped.[1] Trump called the arrangement "a great deal of cooperation between our two countries."[1] The rate was significantly lower than the 46 per cent "reciprocal" tariff on Vietnamese goods that Trump had announced in early April before pausing it for 90 days.[1]

Chinese manufacturers operating in Vietnam described the outcome as "better than expected," telling the South China Morning Post that the final rate was manageable and that most exporters were likely to keep operating in the country, ending three months of uncertainty.[1]

## Putin's June 2024 state visit

In June 2024, Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] visited Hanoi as the second stop on a tour that began in [[north-korea|North Korea]].[2] Meeting with National Assembly Chairman [[tran-thanh-man|Tran Thanh Man]], Putin said that cooperation between the parliaments of Russia and Vietnam helps strengthen relations in security, economy and humanitarian development, and that legislative-level decisions create "a solid and reliable basis for our interaction."[2] He described the Vietnamese direction in Russian foreign policy as "one of the unconditional priorities," reported productive talks with Vietnam's president, prime minister and the secretary general of the Communist Party of Vietnam Central Committee, and congratulated Tran Thanh Man on his election as speaker the previous month.[2]

## Badlands historical framing

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary treats Vietnam, alongside North Korea, as one of two states that served as critical proxy theaters for the [[state-department|State Department]] and the [[cia|CIA]] in the post-World War II period.[2] In this account, Vietnam suffered under French colonial rule, which established a large-scale opium operation of the same kind Britain and France had used to subjugate China during the nineteenth-century Opium Wars.[2] When France was driven out by Soviet and Chinese-backed forces after the Korean War, the [[gulf-of-tonkin-incident|Gulf of Tonkin]] false flag was staged to bring the United States into the conflict; the CIA relocated its opium operation to Laos while American soldiers fought the Vietcong, and the [[vietnam-war|Vietnam War]]'s main heroin customer base was said to be American GIs serving in country.[2] On this reading, Putin's Hanoi visit formed part of a deliberate mirroring of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2017 foreign tour and of an effort to build a worldwide network of sovereign-minded allies.[2]
