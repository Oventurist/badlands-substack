# Redes Energéticas Nacionais

**Redes Energéticas Nacionais** (REN) is Portugal's national electricity and gas grid operator, responsible for the country's high-voltage transmission network and its interconnection with the wider European grid.[1]

## Role in the 2025 Iberian blackout

REN became the primary public source of explanation for the [[2025-iberian-peninsula-power-outage|widespread power outage]] that hit [[spain|Spain]] and parts of Portugal and [[france|France]] on 28 April 2025.[1] The company told Sky News that the outage was caused by a fault in the Spanish electricity grid, which was itself caused by a "rare atmospheric phenomenon."[1]

REN said that as a result of extreme temperature variations in Spain there were "anomalous oscillations" in extremely high-voltage lines, a condition it labelled "induced atmospheric variation."[1] According to the operator, those oscillations produced synchronization failures between systems, leading to successive disturbances across the interconnected European network, and the complexity of the fault meant restoration to normal operation could take as long as a week.[1]

REN's explanation was received skeptically in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary, which weighed the official account against alternative explanations involving renewable energy generation, cyber attack, or geophysical changes in the Earth's magnetic field.[1]
