# Smiths Detection

Smiths Detection is a for-profit defense and security-screening company with operations in the Southern California congressional district represented by [[adam-schiff|Rep. Adam Schiff]].[1]

## Role in the Schiff earmark controversy

A September 2023 Politico investigation, amplified by ZeroHedge and covered by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], found that Schiff had steered $6 million in congressional earmarks to Smiths Detection, one of two in-district defense firms singled out in the reporting alongside Phasebridge, Inc., which received $3 million.[1] Both transactions would have been barred under earmark reforms adopted in 2010.[1]

The funding coincided with Schiff receiving $8,500 in contributions from the PMA Group PAC and from two family members of Paul Magliocchetti, the founder and owner of the lobbying firm retained by both defense companies.[1] Magliocchetti was sentenced in 2011 to 27 months in prison for making illegal campaign contributions.[1]
