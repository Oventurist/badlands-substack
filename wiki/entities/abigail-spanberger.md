# Abigail Spanberger

Abigail Spanberger is the Governor of Virginia and a former CIA officer, described in Badlands commentary as the "CIA-soccer-mom-turned-governor."[1] State Senate President pro tempore [[louise-lucas|L. Louise Lucas]], whose Portsmouth office was searched by the [[fbi|FBI]] in May 2026 as part of a federal corruption investigation, is identified as a close ally of Spanberger.[1] Lucas publicly tied the federal action to Virginia's recent congressional redistricting dispute, a fight closely associated with the Spanberger administration's political position.[1]

## 2025 election as Virginia governor

Spanberger won the Virginia gubernatorial race on November 4, 2025, becoming the state's first female governor.[2] Her victory came as part of a Democratic sweep of the first major elections of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term, alongside [[mikie-sherrill|Mikie Sherrill]]'s win in New Jersey, [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]]'s projected win in the New York City mayoral race, [[ghazala-hashmi|Ghazala Hashmi]]'s projected election as Virginia lieutenant governor, and [[jay-jones|Jay Jones]]'s projected win over Attorney General [[jason-miyares|Jason Miyares]].[2] Badlands contributors disputed the legitimacy of the results, with [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] asserting that "elections are fake" and that the winner "was installed."[2]

## October 2025 gubernatorial debate

Spanberger entered the campaign's only scheduled debate, held in October 2025, as the race's front-runner.[3] The meeting was described as chaotic: Republican nominee [[winsome-earle-sears|Winsome Earle-Sears]] repeatedly interrupted her and pressed her to demand that Democratic attorney general nominee [[jay-jones|Jay Jones]] withdraw over his violent text messages, while Spanberger mostly did not engage directly, often staring straight ahead and remaining silent.[3] Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] framed the contest as "the ultimate narrative destroyer," describing it as "a literal CIA agent running for the democrats against a Black woman and Marine for the GOP."[3]


## CCP-linked campaign donations (2025)

Campaign finance records reported by the Washington Free Beacon in August 2025 showed that Spanberger's Virginia gubernatorial campaign received $50,000 from [[pin-ni|Pin Ni]], an electric vehicle executive at the Chinese conglomerate [[wanxiang-group|Wanxiang Group]] and head of its American subsidiary, who is a member of the Chinese Communist Party.[4] The donations were made in April and May of that year.[4] The reporting noted the legal tension involved: Virginia law prohibits foreign nationals from making political contributions, while Chinese law typically bars CCP members from holding foreign citizenship or permanent residency.[4]
