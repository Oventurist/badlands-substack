# Fisker

Fisker is an American electric-vehicle startup that went public during the early-2020s wave of EV listings.[1] In December 2023, a Wall Street Journal analysis of company filings placed Fisker among at least 18 EV and battery startups at risk of exhausting their cash by the end of 2024.[1]

Fisker, like [[nikola|Nikola]], had attracted investors with bold promises to transform the automotive industry and fight climate change through electric trucks and SUVs, only to stumble amid rising costs and manufacturing problems.[1] Its difficulties formed part of the broader [[2023-electric-vehicle-startup-shakeout|EV startup shakeout]], in which the median newly public EV stock fell more than 80% from its debut price and tens of billions of dollars in market value evaporated.[1]
