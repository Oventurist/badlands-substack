# Igor Krasnov

**Igor Krasnov** is the Prosecutor General of [[russia|Russia]], who in December 2024 ordered prosecutors to make wider use of artificial intelligence in the fight against corruption.[1]

## AI anti-corruption directive (December 2024)

The directive came on Sunday, December 8, 2024, a day before International Anti-Corruption Day, and was described by Krasnov in an interview with the news agency TASS.[1] AI-based technology would be employed to check existing regulations: "The [work] requires excellent knowledge of many laws… My subordinates have all this. But at the same time, I have instructed [them] to make greater use of AI capabilities," he said.[1]

Krasnov explained that the aim is to prevent corruption by finding and closing loopholes that allow officials to make decisions benefiting themselves.[1] He cited a ministry regulation that had let the heads of subordinate entities determine the size of their own bonuses; the regulation was amended so that bonus payments must now be approved by the ministry.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the directive as a "narrative inversion," observing that for years fearmongers had warned that technocrats would use [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] to enslave the public, whereas Russia was now using AI to hunt down would-be technocrats and corrupt politicians.[1] The commentary asked when the technology could be brought to America by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], concluding: "Hunters become The Hunted."[1]
