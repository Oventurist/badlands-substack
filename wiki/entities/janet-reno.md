# Janet Reno

Janet Reno was Attorney General of the United States under President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and appears in the Badlands account at both ends of the [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] investigation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

In the spring of 1995 Reno stated publicly that Brown had apparently failed to properly report large payments from his business partner, Nolanda Sue Hill — the disclosure that triggered the appointment of an independent counsel to examine the Commerce Secretary's financial affairs.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) After Brown was killed in a plane crash on April 3, 1996 while the inquiry was still in its early stages, the investigation was transferred from the independent counsel to Reno's Justice Department, on the reasoning that Brown's remaining partners were not members of the Clinton administration and no outside counsel was therefore required.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The article treats that transfer as the decisive move that took the target off the Clinton administration, contrasting a genuinely independent inquiry with what it calls Clinton's corrupt DOJ.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
