# Dmitry Kiselev

Dmitry Kiselev is the head of the Rossiya Segodnya (Russia Today) media group, the Russian state media organization to which [[sputnik|Sputnik]] belongs.[1]

## Response to the 2025 Baku arrests

After Azerbaijani authorities detained two senior Sputnik editors and a Ruptly journalist in Baku on June 30, 2025, Kiselev described the arrests of the Russian reporters as an "injustice."[1] He insisted that "there have been no objections to the journalistic work of Sputnik Azerbaijan," and said Russian and Azerbaijani officials had agreed during a meeting in Moscow in early April to resolve the "formalities" of the outlet's operations in the country — a process he said Baku had allegedly stalled.[1]

The detentions led Russia's Foreign Ministry to summon Azerbaijani ambassador [[rakhman-mustafaev|Rakhman Mustafaev]].[1]
