# Ministry of Justice of Russia

The Ministry of Justice of the Russian Federation is the federal executive body responsible for legal policy, registration of organizations, and the maintenance of Russia's registries of "extremist" and "foreign agent" entities.[1]

## Role in the 2023 LGBTQ extremism case

The Ministry filed the motion that produced the [[supreme-court-of-russia|Supreme Court of Russia]]'s November 30, 2023 ruling declaring the "international LGBTQ movement" an extremist organization.[1] In its filing the Ministry claimed that the LGBTQ community risked "inciting social and religious discord," in violation of Russia's Law on Countering Extremism.[1] The Ministry was the only party present at the four-hour closed-door hearing, and the case materials were classified.[1] A United Nations statement condemned the resulting decision.[1]
