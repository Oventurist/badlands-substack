# Stuart Varney

Stuart Varney is the host of *Varney & Co.* on [[fox-business|Fox Business]].[1] In September 2023 his program was accused of deliberately altering a graphic to misrepresent the findings of a [[fox-news|Fox News]] presidential poll.[1]

## The polling graphic controversy

The poll, conducted September 9–12, 2023, showed former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] leading incumbent [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] 48 percent to 46 percent.[1] According to [[the-national-pulse|The National Pulse]], the graphic aired by *Varney & Co.* on the following Monday used what the outlet described as a purposefully confusing labeling method, creating the appearance that Trump trailed Biden 46 to 48.[1]

The inverted presentation generated confusion on social media, with some users citing the graphic as evidence that Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] was better positioned to win in 2024 — the opposite of what the underlying poll indicated.[1] Varney was scheduled to serve as a co-moderator of the second Republican primary debate on September 27, 2023.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the story broke the same day as the announcement that [[rupert-murdoch|Rupert Murdoch]] was stepping down in favor of his son, and argued that "misleading the audience is critical to manufacturing consent, and manufacturing consent is Fox's prime directive these days."[1]
