# Kenneth Good

Kenneth Good was a Denver businessman and the second of [[neil-bush|Neil Bush]]'s Colorado partners.[1]

In 1984 Good gave Bush $100,000 to invest as a loan; the investment failed and the loan was then "forgiven," in Bush's own description.[1] In 1986 Bush asked [[silverado-savings-and-loan-association|Silverado]], of which he was a director, to approve a $900,000 line of credit for Good, and because he considered Good a business partner he abstained on votes that approved $34 million in loans to him.[1]

Good and [[william-walters|William Walters]] together defaulted on Silverado loans totalling $106 million.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] tallies roughly $130 million in loans plus a near-million-dollar line of credit obtained by the pair through Bush's influence, part of the pattern of self-dealing that produced the $1 billion Silverado bailout in the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan crisis]].[1]
