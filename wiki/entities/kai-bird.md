# Kai Bird

Kai Bird is the co-author, with Martin J. Sherwin, of *American Prometheus: The Triumph and Tragedy of J. Robert Oppenheimer*, the Pulitzer Prize-winning biography that inspired Christopher Nolan's film.[1]

Sherwin died in 2021, but Bird consulted on the film and said he hoped it would start a societal conversation — Badlands cites this as evidence that *Oppenheimer* prioritizes narrative value over entertainment value.[1] Speaking at a mid-May 2023 seminar at Princeton's Institute for Advanced Study, where [[j-robert-oppenheimer|Oppenheimer]] served as director from 1947 to 1966, Bird said he hoped the film would stimulate a national and even global conversation about how to live in the atomic age and with the bomb, about McCarthyism, about what it means to be a patriot, and about the role of scientists in speaking out on public issues in a society drenched in technology and science; the remarks were reported by the Union of Concerned Scientists in July 2023.[1] Badlands reads this as intentional support for [[global-governance|global governance]].[1]
