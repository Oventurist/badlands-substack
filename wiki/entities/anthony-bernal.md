# Anthony Bernal

Anthony Bernal is a longtime aide to First Lady Jill Biden who served as her top staffer inside the [[white-house|White House]] during the presidency of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] He became a subject of congressional scrutiny in July 2024 when the Republican-led [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] issued subpoenas to three senior White House aides, demanding they sit for depositions concerning the president's mental state.[1]

## July 2024 subpoena

Fox News confirmed that the Oversight subpoenas covered Bernal, deputy chief of staff Annie Tomasini and senior adviser Ashley Williams.[1] Committee chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] said in a press release that the White House had "shielded three key aides from testifying about President Biden's mishandling of classified documents," and that reporting indicated the same aides were "also seeking to cover up President Biden's declining cognitive state inside the White House."[1] Comer added that "President Biden is clearly unfit for office, yet his staff are trying to hide the truth from the American people."[1]

The subpoenas landed during the political crisis that followed Biden's June 27, 2024 debate performance, when questions about the president's age and cognitive capacity dominated coverage and prompted calls from Democrats for him to withdraw from the race.[1] Badlands Media commentary framed the episode as part of a wider collapse of the media and political apparatus surrounding the Biden White House.[1]
