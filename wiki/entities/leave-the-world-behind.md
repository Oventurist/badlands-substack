# Leave the World Behind

**Leave the World Behind** is a [[netflix|Netflix]] feature film released in December 2023, produced under the production arrangement between Netflix and former president [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and his wife.[1] Its plot concerns a catastrophic cyber-attack on the United States and the resulting societal breakdown.[1]

## Tesla scene and Badlands commentary

A prominent sequence in the film depicts [[tesla|Tesla]] vehicles' autopilot systems malfunctioning and causing widespread havoc.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] highlighted the film's release as occurring in the same week that federal regulators and the press attacked Tesla's Autopilot feature and that the [[federal-communications-commission|FCC]] declined to restore roughly $900 million in [[starlink]] subsidies.[1]

Carlson argued that the convergence was not coincidental, reasoning that films are not produced overnight and that the timing therefore indicated the campaign against [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had been under preparation for a considerable period.[1] He placed the film within a sequence of December 2023 events that also included Musk telling advertisers attempting to pressure him to "go f*** themselves," [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s interview with [[alex-jones]] on X, and Jones's reinstatement on the platform.[1]
