# Perplexity AI

Perplexity is a software and artificial intelligence company that builds search products designed to provide direct, contextually accurate answers with transparent citations.[1]

## Partnership with Trump Media

In August 2025, [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media and Technology Group]] announced that it had begun public beta testing of Truth Search AI, a new AI search feature on the [[truth-social|Truth Social]] platform, powered by Perplexity.[1] The company said the feature was intended to enhance Truth Social and "exponentially increase the amount of information available to its users."[1] Trump Media CEO and Chairman [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] said the company was "proud to partner with Perplexity" and framed the launch as making Truth Social "an even more vital element in the Patriot Economy."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] noted that Perplexity had months earlier been reported as a potential merger partner for TikTok, and that it was now partnering with Truth Social instead.[1] He argued this supported his long-running speculation that TikTok would eventually end up under Trump Media, pointing to [[jeffrey-yass|Jeff Yass]] as an investor with money in both — a structure he compared to the Twitter "sale."[1]
