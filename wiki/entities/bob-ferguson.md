# Bob Ferguson

**Bob Ferguson** is a Democratic politician serving as Governor of Washington state.[1]

## Flag order for the 2025 inauguration

In January 2025, Ferguson ordered flags in Washington state to fly at full-staff on Monday, January 20, for the inauguration of President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], despite the official order issued by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] after the death of former President [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]] on December 29 directing that flags at all government and public buildings and grounds across the country fly at half-staff for a 30-day mourning period — a period that included Inauguration Day.[1]

Ferguson joined California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] and Colorado Governor [[jared-polis|Jared Polis]] as Democrats who broke with the mourning-period tradition and ordered flags raised for Trump's inauguration before the period ended on January 28. Their orders followed those of several Republican governors — Idaho's Brad Little, North Dakota's Kelly Armstrong, Florida's Ron DeSantis, Alabama's Kay Ivey, Tennessee's Bill Lee, Iowa's Kim Reynolds and Texas's Greg Abbott — while House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] ordered flags at the U.S. Capitol flown at full-staff on Inauguration Day.[1]

The Badlands News Brief noted that more than 11 governors had ordered full-staff flags in defiance of Biden's order, including three Democrats all rumored to be considering presidential runs in 2028, and suggested that which officials and businesses kept flags at half-staff would serve as a revealing political "tell" at the most local levels.[1]
