# Heritage Foundation

The Heritage Foundation is a conservative public policy think tank based in Washington, D.C. In the Badlands corpus it appears principally as a litigant and transparency actor rather than as a policy shop.

## Oversight Project and the Hur recordings

Through its investigative arm and its associated attorney [[mike-howell|Mike Howell]], Heritage was one of the requesters to whom the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] agreed to release redacted audio recordings and transcripts from Special Counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s investigation into [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of classified documents. The recordings captured Biden's 2016–2017 conversations with ghostwriter [[mark-zwonitzer|Mark Zwonitzer]].

As reported in the July 22, 2026 [[badlands-brief-1ae|Badlands Brief]], the Justice Department had initially withheld most of the records under FOIA exemptions before agreeing to the redacted release to Heritage, Howell, and the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]]. Biden intervened in the litigation to block disclosure. After a lower court denied a preliminary injunction, the [[dc-circuit-court-of-appeals|D.C. Circuit]] denied his emergency appeal, holding that he had not shown a likelihood of success and that there was a substantial public interest in allowing assessment of whether the government had gone easy on a former vice president.

The outcome positioned Heritage alongside congressional oversight bodies as a principal vehicle for forcing disclosure of the Hur record, a role Badlands writers treat as advancing the accountability phase they associate with the [[autopen-scandal|autopen scandal]].
