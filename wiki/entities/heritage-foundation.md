# Heritage Foundation

The Heritage Foundation is a conservative policy institute in Washington, D.C. In the litigation over Special Counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s investigative materials, Heritage was one of the requesters to whom the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] agreed to release redacted audio recordings and transcripts of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s conversations with ghostwriter [[mark-zwonitzer|Mark Zwonitzer]], alongside [[mike-howell|Mike Howell]] and the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]].[1]

The Justice Department had originally withheld most of the records under FOIA exemptions before reversing course; Biden then intervened to block the disclosure, and the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-dc-circuit|D.C. Circuit]] denied his emergency request in July 2026.[1]

## 50th anniversary and the Carlson speech (2023)

In April 2023 the Heritage Foundation marked its 50th anniversary with an event at which [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] delivered what became one of his most discussed addresses, arguing that the central conflict in American life was no longer political but a struggle over "the very moral foundations of civilization."[2] Carlson described the new ideological movements as built on "old and dark premises," motivated solely to destroy what they could in order to take what they could.[2] Because his ouster from [[fox-news|Fox News]] was announced days later, [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and other outlets asked whether the Heritage speech was the proximate cause of his removal, suggesting he had been "getting a little too close to the truth."[2]

## The Oversight Project and the Biden autopen (2025)

Heritage's Oversight Project first uncovered the circumstances surrounding former President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s use of an autopen device to approve many of his executive orders, prompting questions from critics about whether Biden was fully mindful of the documents signed in his name.[3] The finding led Missouri Attorney General [[andrew-bailey|Andrew Bailey]] to question the legitimacy of executive orders signed by the 82-year-old former president given concerns about his cognitive decline.[3] Oversight Project investigators said that to determine whether Biden actually ordered the signature of the relevant legal documents — or had the mental capacity to do so — they must learn who controlled the autopen and what checks were in place.[3] See [[autopen-scandal|the autopen scandal]].[3]

## Shapiro–Carlson controversy

The foundation became the venue for an internal conservative rupture in December 2025, when [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] used a Heritage stage on Wednesday, December 17 to denounce [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] by name and to urge the institution hosting him to enforce what Shapiro called "ideological border control" against a strain of conservatism drifting toward conspiracy theories and antisemitism.[4] The event was hosted by Heritage president [[kevin-roberts|Kevin Roberts]], who had come under fire in preceding months for publicly defending Carlson after Carlson interviewed [[nick-fuentes|Nick Fuentes]], a white nationalist and Holocaust revisionist.[4] Roberts' comments triggered resignations and criticism from Jewish leaders and former Heritage affiliates, and two more trustees of the foundation resigned that week over his support for Carlson.[4]

## Davos criticism

Heritage Foundation president [[kevin-roberts|Kevin Roberts]] publicly rebuked attendees at a [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] Davos meeting, telling them it was 'laughable' to describe Davos as protecting liberal democracy — an exchange cited in Badlands commentary as evidence of shifting elite dynamics ahead of [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s 2024 departure from executive management.[5]

## Internal fallout over the Carlson controversy

The Heritage Foundation was convulsed in November 2025 by internal criticism of president [[kevin-roberts|Kevin Roberts]] over a video in which he defended [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]].[6] At a leaked all-staff meeting on Wednesday, November 5, 2025, Roberts apologized to employees — "I made a mistake, and I let you down, and I let down this institution. Period. Full stop." — and faced brutal criticism from the think tank's scholars over his handling of the matter, with video of the meeting published by the Washington Free Beacon.[6] Roberts afterward recorded a further statement saying that everyone has a responsibility to speak up against antisemitism "no matter the messenger," and that Heritage would do so "even when my friend Tucker Carlson needs challenging."[6]

## Oversight Project FOIA on CIA ethics referrals

In January 2024 the foundation's [[oversight-project|Oversight Project]] supplied [[daily-caller|The Daily Caller]] with records showing that the [[cia|CIA]] had filed 32 conflict-of-interest referral notifications with the [[office-of-government-ethics|Office of Government Ethics]] between January 2021 and May 2023, apparently reporting dozens of employees to the Justice Department during the Biden administration.[7] Heritage had obtained information about the Form 202 submissions through a Freedom of Information Act request, and the OGE advised the project that multiple forms may correspond to a single referral.[7]
