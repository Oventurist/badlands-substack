# Rising Tide Foundation

The Rising Tide Foundation is an organization directed by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], editor-in-chief of the Canadian Patriot Review and Senior Fellow of the American University in Moscow.[1]

The foundation is cited in Badlands Media reporting as the publisher or host of source material used in Ehret's critique of [[transhumanism|transhumanism]], including the collected writings of the American economist [[lyndon-larouche|Lyndon LaRouche]], the essays of [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]], [[friedrich-schiller|Friedrich Schiller]]'s 1791 comparison of the legislations of Lycurgus and Solon, and analyses of what determines a limit to growth in response to the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]].[1]
