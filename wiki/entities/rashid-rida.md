# Rashid Rida

Rashid Rida — rendered "Rida Rashid" in Badlands commentary — was an Islamic cleric identified as one of the figures responsible for the proliferation of Salafi Islam.[1]

## Alleged British sponsorship

According to commentary from Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], Rida was plucked from obscurity by British Intelligence and employed as head imam of a British-funded Salafi school in Cairo established in the 1880s under [[evelyn-baring|Evelyn Baring]].[1] The commentary asserts that Rida was literally on the payroll of British Intelligence and that his students went on to create the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]], among them [[hassan-al-banna|Hassan al-Banna]], who founded the organization in Cairo in 1928.[1]
