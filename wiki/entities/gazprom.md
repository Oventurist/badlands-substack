# Gazprom

Gazprom is [[russia|Russia]]'s state-controlled energy company and the operator of the country's principal natural gas export infrastructure. It appears in the Badlands Media corpus as the source of reporting on a Ukrainian drone strike against Russian gas export facilities serving Türkiye.

## Krasnodarskaya compressor station strike (July 2026)

As reported in the July 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0bd|Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles"]], Gazprom said that Ukrainian drones targeted the Krasnodarskaya compressor station, a facility serving the [[blue-stream-pipeline|Blue Stream]] natural gas pipeline that carries Russian gas to Türkiye across the Black Sea. According to the company, the attack caused minor damage to a building at the facility but did not interrupt gas deliveries, because repairs were carried out promptly and backup measures kept supplies flowing. [[ukraine|Ukraine]] had not publicly commented on the strike at the time of reporting.

The incident placed Gazprom's export infrastructure at the centre of a wider dispute: Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] condemned the strike as a threat to critical international energy infrastructure and said Russia expected the countries served by the pipeline, including [[turkey|Türkiye]], to respond.

## Export routes

The Brief identifies Blue Stream as one of Russia's key gas export routes to Türkiye, and notes that the separate [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] pipeline also carries Russian gas through Türkiye onward to several European countries. Both routes have taken on additional strategic weight as Ukraine has expanded long-range attacks on Russian energy infrastructure and Russia has continued missile and drone strikes on Ukrainian cities and energy facilities.
