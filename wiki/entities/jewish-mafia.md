# Jewish Mafia

The "Jewish Mafia" is Erik Carlson's term for the organized-crime network he says sat above the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] in the American underworld, hidden from public view by Hollywood's promotion of the Italian gangster archetype.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Structure claimed

At the top of the Jewish Mafia, in Carlson's account, sat [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]]. Beneath Lansky, [[mickey-cohen|Mickey Cohen]] ran Hollywood and [[bugsy-siegel|Bugsy Siegel]] ran Las Vegas — until Siegel's good friend Lansky learned that Siegel's girlfriend was skimming money and had him killed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Concealment through The Godfather

Carlson reports discovering that the book *[[the-godfather|The Godfather]]* was published by a Jewish man, the film produced by a Jewish man, and the studio executive who signed off on the film also a Jewish man; Mickey Cohen's men even staged the shooting of the producer's car to suggest the Mafia opposed the production.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) He argues that had the Mafia genuinely opposed the film, it would not have been made, nor its sequels, and the producer would not have survived a shotgun attack unscathed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The film's success allowed the Jewish Mafia to disappear into the shadows while the Italian Mafia became synonymous with organized crime.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Position in the hierarchy

Carlson places the [[cia|CIA]] above the Jewish Mafia, noting that the agency could not allow the public to learn that Lansky ran its drugs out of the Golden Triangle during the Vietnam War.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) He then extends the same logic upward: just as the Jewish Mafia stood above the Italian Mafia, he suggests Jews and Israel may themselves be a scapegoat for the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]] centred on the [[city-of-london|City of London]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Gladio-era account

The Badlands Media Gladio funding article identifies a second American criminal network of the early 1900s consisting of Jewish mobsters including Louis "Pretty" Amberg, Moses Annenberg of Hearst Corp newspapers and media, Abe Bernstein of the Purple Gang, [[mickey-cohen]] in Los Angeles, [[meyer-lansky]] — who formed the National Crime Syndicate focused on Cuba and Las Vegas — and [[bugsy-siegel]] in New York and Las Vegas.[2] While they were involved in the same rackets as the Italian mafia, they were also used by large business enterprises that needed assistance making things happen with dock workers, newspapers and bankers.[2] Lansky went on to co-found [[the-commission-mafia|the Commission]] with [[lucky-luciano]] and attended the Cuba meeting that framed the [[operation-gladio]] drug network.[2]

## Carlson's Mafia model of the Deep State (October 2023)

In "Was the Israel-Hamas Conflict De-escalated?" (October 18, 2023), [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] used the Italian Mafia's rank structure — Boss or Godfather, Capos, Soldiers, Associates — as a model for the [[deep-state|Deep State]], arguing that a common error is to treat it as one cohesive unit when in reality its factions constantly stab one another in the back for power, as crime families do.[3] Mapping the ranks, he placed the [[cabal|Cabal]] as the Godfather, the Jewish Mafia as the Capos, the [[italian-mafia|Italian]] and other mafias as the Soldiers, and politicians, CEOs and intelligence agents as the Associates.[3]

Carlson repeated the claim that the Jewish Mafia never wished to be seen running organized crime and therefore built up the Italian Mafia's public persona through books and films.[3] He wrote that [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]] ran organized crime for decades with associates including [[mickey-cohen|Mickey Cohen]] running Hollywood while others ran Las Vegas, and that the network ran drug-smuggling operations with the [[cia|CIA]].[3] He characterised the [[french-connection|French Connection]] as in reality the CIA and Lansky moving Vietnam War-era drugs through France into the United States, and asserted that much of the resulting money went towards creating the State of [[israel|Israel]], "a hornet's nest right in the middle of the Middle East."[3] Israel, on his account, later had to distance itself from organized crime to appear legitimate enough to receive official US government funding.[3]

He added that when [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]] took down the New York Italian Mafia in the 1980s — with help, he believes, from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — the RICO case removed only lower tiers, with the Russian Mafia filling the vacuum while the true Capos, the Jewish Mafia, were never touched.[3]
