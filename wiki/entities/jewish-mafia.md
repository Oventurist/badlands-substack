# Jewish Mafia

The "Jewish Mafia" is the term used in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay for the organized-crime network he argues sat above the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] in the American underworld.[1]

## Described leadership

Carlson writes that [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]] sat at the top of the Jewish Mafia, with [[mickey-cohen|Mickey Cohen]] running Hollywood and [[bugsy-siegel|Bugsy Siegel]] running Las Vegas beneath him.[1] He adds that Lansky, Siegel's good friend, had Siegel killed after discovering that Siegel's girlfriend was skimming money.[1]

## Alleged concealment through The Godfather

Carlson states that having grown up on Italian Mafia films — The Godfather, The Godfather Part II, Goodfellas, and Casino — he believed the Italian Mafia ran American organized crime because Hollywood led him to believe it.[1] He argues [[the-godfather|The Godfather]] allowed the Jewish Mafia to disappear into the shadows, after which the Italian Mafia became synonymous with organized crime in the public mind.[1] He asserts that the Italian Mafia is real and genuinely criminal but that its power and standing are greatly exaggerated relative to the Jewish Mafia above it and the [[cia|CIA]] above that.[1]

## Alleged CIA connection

According to Carlson, the CIA could not allow the American public to know that "their guy," Lansky, was running the agency's drugs out of the Golden Triangle during the Vietnam War, and he notes the timing of the novel's 1969 publication and the film's 1972 release at the height of that war.[1]

## Position in the wider hierarchy

Carlson uses the Jewish Mafia's position over the Italian Mafia as the template for his broader claim that Jews and Israel serve as a scapegoat for the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] seated in the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[1]
