# Jewish Mafia

The "Jewish Mafia" is Erik Carlson's term for the organized-crime network he says sat above the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] in the American underworld, hidden from public view by Hollywood's promotion of the Italian gangster archetype.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Structure claimed

At the top of the Jewish Mafia, in Carlson's account, sat [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]]. Beneath Lansky, [[mickey-cohen|Mickey Cohen]] ran Hollywood and [[bugsy-siegel|Bugsy Siegel]] ran Las Vegas — until Siegel's good friend Lansky learned that Siegel's girlfriend was skimming money and had him killed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Concealment through The Godfather

Carlson reports discovering that the book *[[the-godfather|The Godfather]]* was published by a Jewish man, the film produced by a Jewish man, and the studio executive who signed off on the film also a Jewish man; Mickey Cohen's men even staged the shooting of the producer's car to suggest the Mafia opposed the production.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) He argues that had the Mafia genuinely opposed the film, it would not have been made, nor its sequels, and the producer would not have survived a shotgun attack unscathed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The film's success allowed the Jewish Mafia to disappear into the shadows while the Italian Mafia became synonymous with organized crime.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Position in the hierarchy

Carlson places the [[cia|CIA]] above the Jewish Mafia, noting that the agency could not allow the public to learn that Lansky ran its drugs out of the Golden Triangle during the Vietnam War.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) He then extends the same logic upward: just as the Jewish Mafia stood above the Italian Mafia, he suggests Jews and Israel may themselves be a scapegoat for the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]] centred on the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)
