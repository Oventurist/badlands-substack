# RipperSec

RipperSec is the name used by an anonymous pro-Palestinian hacking collective that claimed credit for a cyber-attack disrupting [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s live X "Spaces" interview with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in August 2024.[1]

## Claimed attack on the Trump–Musk X interview

The group, operating anonymously under the RipperSec name, asserted responsibility for the technical failures that delayed and degraded the Monday livestream of the Trump–Musk conversation on X.[1] A major cybersecurity firm characterised the incident as including a "highly targeted operation, specifically aimed at Donald Trump's personal Twitter [X] account," alongside a broader assault on the livestream itself.[1]

The attribution was contested from the outset. A London-based cybersecurity expert told DailyMail.com he would treat the firm's claims "with a pinch of salt for now," noting the murky nature of the technical difficulties.[1] Badlands commentary emphasised that the expert analysis alleging pro-Palestinian hackers operating out of [[iran|Iran]] had originated with a China-based firm, a provenance the outlet argued warranted skepticism.[1]

## Context

The RipperSec claim landed amid intensifying warnings about the digital vulnerability of the 2024 U.S. election cycle, including a reported [[fbi|FBI]] investigation, opened in June, into alleged Iranian hacking of both Republican and Democratic presidential campaigns.[1] Badlands writers situated the episode within a broader argument that heightened attention to Iranian cyber activity functioned as a distraction from domestic governance controversies rather than an organic response to Iranian behaviour.[1]
