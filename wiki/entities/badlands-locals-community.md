# Badlands Locals Community

The **Badlands Locals Community** is the subscriber community maintained by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on the Locals platform, promoted as one of the principal connection points reachable through [[badlandsmedia-tv|BadlandsMedia.tv]].[1]

## Offering

In the March 28, 2023 launch announcement for the network's website, the Locals community was described as providing 24/7 live chat together with exclusive content not distributed through the outlet's free channels.[1] It sits alongside event announcements and advertising contact as one of the ways the site invites audience members — whom the network calls Badlanders — to "CONNECT" with the organization.[1]

## Context

The community complements Badlands Media's other distribution surfaces, which include its Substack newsletter, its video programming and the [[badlands-marketplace|Badlands Marketplace]] business directory, all of which the website was created to consolidate.[1]
