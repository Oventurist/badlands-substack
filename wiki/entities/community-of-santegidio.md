# Community of Sant'Egidio

The Community of Sant'Egidio is a Catholic lay association known for peace advocacy and back-channel mediation, and the host of international gatherings that convene political and religious figures.[1]

## 'Imagining Peace' gathering, September 2024

On Sunday, September 22, 2024, French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] addressed the Catholic community of Sant'Egidio as part of the international "Imagining Peace" gathering in Paris, which brought together leading political and religious figures.[1] Speaking there, Macron called for reform of what he described as an "unjust" world order, arguing that "we must be imaginative enough to think about the peace of tomorrow, a peace in Europe in a new form" that is "neither quite the EU, nor resolutely NATO," and that Europe would have to "rethink our relationship with Russia" once the Ukraine conflict ends.[1]
