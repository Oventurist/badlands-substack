# Jesse Watters

Jesse Watters is a [[fox-news|Fox News]] host whose program carried Iran war commentary during the 2026 conflict.[1]

## Role in the suicide dolphins story

In early May 2026, Watters pushed the [[suicide-dolphins-narrative|"suicide dolphins" claim]] — that [[iran|Iran]] was preparing mine-equipped dolphins to attack U.S. ships — on Fox News, matching coverage that had already aired on [[cnn|CNN]].[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the overlap as evidence that the "Uniparty Right and Uniparty Left" were in lockstep pushing narratives to foment war rather than functioning as adversarial press.[1]

## 'No Kings' admission about his mother

In October 2025 Watters admitted on [[fox-news|Fox News]]' *The Five* that his liberal mother, Anne Purvis, had joined demonstrators at the anti-Trump [[no-kings-protests|'No Kings']] rally the previous Saturday, attending a march on Long Island.[2] "I know my mom was there. Can you believe my mom was there? Sometimes I think I was adopted," said Watters, 47, whose long-running on-air rivalry with his politically opposite mother — who reportedly did not invite him to Thanksgiving the previous year — is a standing joke among his co-hosts.[2]

He went on to argue that liberal protest movements of the Trump era had been ineffective: "They do these things — the Women's March, BLM, the Musk stuff. The issues change but the one thing that stays the same is Trump."[2] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the segment as a springboard for an essay on [[loosh]], the esoteric idea of engineered emotional discord harvested as energy.[2]

### Lutnick interview on abolishing the IRS
On February 19, 2025, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick appeared on Jesse Watters Primetime and said President Donald Trump's goal was to abolish the Internal Revenue Service in favor of an "External Revenue Service" funded by tariffs and foreign revenue.[3] Watters asked Lutnick whether the savings generated by DOGE cuts would be given back to the American people, prompting Lutnick's reply that Trump's goal was "to abolish the Internal Revenue Service and let all the outsiders pay."[3]

## Interview with RFK Jr. on Epstein (December 2023)

Watters interviewed independent presidential candidate [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] in December 2023 and asked whether he had ever flown on [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s "Lolita Express," prompting Kennedy to acknowledge two flights, one in 1993 and another with his family en route to Florida.[4] Kennedy told Watters he agreed that all information about Epstein and the high-level political figures connected to him should be released to the public.[4]
