# 1776 Commission

The 1776 Commission — formally the President's Advisory 1776 Commission — was an initiative of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] whose final report was published on the White House website in January 2021.[1]

## Coverage in The Patriot Chronicles

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]] cited the Commission's final report when introducing [[patriot-chronicles|The Patriot Chronicles]] in June 2023, linking directly to the archived PDF hosted at trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov.[1] He named two aspects of the Commission as those for which he was most excited: the [[garden-of-heroes|Garden of Heroes]], a proposed national park of monuments to American heroes, and the restoration of true Classical Architecture to federal buildings, which he described as his professional background.[1]

The Commission was presented in this framing as part of a wider effort by [[donald-trump|Trump]] to restore patriotism, national pride and a sense of purpose to American culture — an effort the author sought to advance in written form, describing his own series as "a digital kickstart" for that vision.[1]
