# Rising

Rising is a web series produced by [[the-hill|The Hill]], built around a cross-ideological co-hosting format.[1]

## Co-hosts and the 2023 Israel exchange

[[briahna-joy-gray|Briahna Joy Gray]], former National Press Secretary for [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]], co-hosted the programme alongside [[robby-soave|Robby Soave]].[1] Badlands Media cited Gray's on-air criticism of Sanders over his attacks on [[cornel-west|Cornel West]] as an example of the show's willingness to host intra-left dissent.[1]

In October 2023 the programme became a flashpoint when Gray and Soave clashed heatedly over her criticism of the Israeli government during the Israel–Palestine conflict.[1] Badlands Media used the exchange to illustrate how the war was fracturing alternative media on both the left and the right, and warned that emotionally driven responses to war — as after 9/11 — tend to end badly.[1]
