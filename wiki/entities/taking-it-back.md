# Taking It Back

Taking It Back is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program announced as a new addition to the network's lineup in the January 16–22, 2023 show schedule, airing Thursdays at 4pm ET.[1]

## Format and hosts

The show is hosted by [[412-adel-nero|412 Adel Nero]], [[zak-paine|Zak Paine]], and [[frankie-val|Frankie Val]].[1] It discusses current events from the perspective of the America First movement and what the network describes as that movement's quest to take back the Republic from the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]

## Distribution

Live streams and replays air on the main Badlands Media [[rumble|Rumble]] channel.[1]
