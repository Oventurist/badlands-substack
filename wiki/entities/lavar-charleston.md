# LaVar Charleston

LaVar Charleston is an American academic administrator serving as deputy vice chancellor for diversity and inclusion at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.[1] He is the husband of [[sherri-ann-charleston|Sherri Ann Charleston]], the chief diversity and inclusion officer at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]].[1]

## Role in the 2024 plagiarism complaint

LaVar Charleston figured centrally in a January 2024 plagiarism complaint filed against his wife with Harvard University and analyzed by the *Washington Free Beacon*.[1] According to the complaint, a 2014 peer-reviewed article coauthored by the couple recycled much of a 2012 study that LaVar Charleston had published on his own, presenting the older material as new research.[1] The 2012 study, which involved interviews with black computer science students, appeared in the *Journal of Diversity in Higher Education*; the 2014 paper, published in the *Journal of Negro Education* and also coauthored with Jerlando Jackson, reproduced the same methods, findings, and description of survey subjects.[1] Critics argued the arrangement allowed Sherri Ann Charleston to claim credit for her husband's earlier work.[1]
