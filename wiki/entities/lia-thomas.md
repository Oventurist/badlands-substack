# Lia Thomas

Lia Thomas is the transgender swimmer who dominated the 2022 [[national-collegiate-athletic-association|NCAA]] Swimming Championships while a student at the [[university-of-pennsylvania|University of Pennsylvania]], and who sits at the center of a March 2024 class-action lawsuit brought by female athletes against the NCAA.[1]

## Collegiate career

Thomas first competed for the University of Pennsylvania's men's swim team as a male from 2017 to 2020 but never reached the NCAA championships during that period.[1] After two years of hormone therapy, Thomas switched to the women's team and, according to the coverage, trounced female competitors in both sprint and endurance races.[1] In 2022 Thomas won the 500-yard freestyle title at the NCAA Division I Women's Swimming and Diving Championships and was named All-American in all three events entered.[1]

## The NCAA lawsuit

More than a dozen female athletes sued the NCAA for allowing transgender athletes to compete against them and to use female locker rooms in college sports, with Thomas's 2022 championship at the center of the complaint.[1] The suit alleges that both the NCAA and Georgia Tech, which hosted the event, knowingly violated [[title-ix|Title IX]].[1] The action — described as the first federal case of its kind — seeks to render biological males ineligible to compete against female athletes, to force the NCAA to revoke awards given to trans athletes in women's competitions and "reassign" them to female contenders, and to obtain damages for pain and suffering, mental and emotional distress, and related costs.[1]
