# Mac Warner

**Mac Warner** is the Secretary of State of West Virginia, the official responsible for the administration of elections and ballot access in the state.[1]

Warner was a defendant in the 2023 lawsuit brought by presidential candidate [[john-anthony-castro|John Anthony Castro]] seeking to bar former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the West Virginia presidential ballot under Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1] U.S. District Judge [[irene-berger|Irene Berger]] sided with attorneys for Trump, Warner, and the [[west-virginia-republican-party|West Virginia GOP]] in dismissing the suit for lack of standing.[1]
