# Gordie Howe International Bridge

The Gordie Howe International Bridge is a $4.6 billion crossing linking Windsor, Ontario, and Detroit, Michigan, scheduled to open in early 2026.[1]

## Trump's threat to block the opening

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] threatened to block the bridge's opening, posting on social media that the project featured "virtually no US content" and accusing Canada of unfairly benefiting while offering "Absolutely NOTHING" in return.[1] He tied the dispute to Canadian trade plans with China, warning that such plans could trigger 100% tariffs, a potential ban on ice hockey in Canada, and the elimination of the Stanley Cup.[1]

## Canadian response

Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] described a positive conversation with Trump, emphasizing Canada's full funding of construction, shared ownership with Michigan, and the involvement of both countries' steel and workers as a model of cooperation.[1] Local officials and former lawmakers highlighted that the project originated during Trump's first term.[1]
