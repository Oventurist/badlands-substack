# Gilad Erdan

Gilad Erdan is an Israeli politician and diplomat who served as Israel's Ambassador to the [[united-nations|United Nations]]. In August 2024, as his tenure came to a close, Erdan drew international attention for declaring that the United Nations headquarters building in New York was "unnecessary" and "should be closed and wiped off the face of the earth."[1]

## Departing remarks on the UN

Erdan's comments came in a snippet from an upcoming Hebrew-language interview with i24News, reported by the Times of Israel. He said he was leaving his post "with a feeling of satisfaction on one hand, of my battle and work here, but on the other hand with great distress and frustration with the fact that this building, which may look nice from the outside, is actually twisted and distorted."[1] In a video rant circulated alongside the interview, Erdan went further, asserting that "there is no place more corrupt and morally twisted than the UN, and we all must unite to spread this message worldwide, demanding the closure and dismantling of this organization."[1]

## Badlands interpretation

The Badlands News Brief treated Erdan's outburst as a significant narrative development rather than a diplomatic gaffe. Writing under the byline [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], the commentary framed the episode as "5D Chess": an Israeli official, angered by the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] and [[international-court-of-justice|International Court of Justice]] proceedings against Israeli leaders, publicly calling for the destruction of the very global institution that the Badlands writer argues created and long shielded the modern State of Israel.[1] The Brief noted that the modern state was born of the 1947 partition arrangements and that the UN had for decades blocked lawfare against Israel until South Africa's genocide complaint was filed in November 2023 and heard the following February — a shift the author read as evidence that outside forces had begun influencing the body.[1]

In this reading, "white hats" had redirected the vengeful instincts of Israel's ruling faction against its own globalist patrons in New York and Geneva, with the practical consequence that no army — the depleted IDF, NATO forces tied up in Ukraine, or UN and EU contingents routed in Africa by the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] — remained available for a Middle Eastern war against Iran.[1]
