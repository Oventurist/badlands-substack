# Lin Yu-ting

Lin Yu-ting is a Taiwanese boxer who competed in the women's featherweight division at the [[2024-paris-summer-olympics|2024 Paris Summer Olympics]], reaching the semi-finals alongside Algeria's [[imane-khelif|Imane Khelif]].[1]

Both fighters had previously been disqualified from the women's category by the [[international-boxing-association|International Boxing Association]] (IBA).[1] At a press conference held on Monday, August 5, 2024, IBA chief executive Chris Roberts said 2022 test results produced by an independent laboratory in Istanbul "demonstrated the chromosomes we refer to in competition rules that make both boxers ineligible."[1] Roberts stated that both boxers were informed they had tested positive for XY chromosomes and were offered the opportunity to appeal to the [[court-of-arbitration-for-sport|Court of Arbitration for Sport]], with the IBA offering to cover the costs; Lin chose not to appeal, while Khelif initially appealed and then withdrew.[1] Both boxers were re-tested in India during the 2023 Women's World Boxing Championships with the same results.[1]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the two fighters were guaranteed to leave the games with medals and criticized the [[international-olympic-committee|International Olympic Committee]]'s position, contrasting it with the IOC's deference to the sport's governing body over eligibility questions in men's volleyball.[1] Lin was scheduled to fight in the featherweight semi-final the day after Khelif's welterweight semi-final.[1]
