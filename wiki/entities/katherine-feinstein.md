# Katherine Feinstein

Katherine Feinstein is the daughter of the late California Senator [[dianne-feinstein|Dianne Feinstein]], and in 2023 she became a figure of national political interest after reporting indicated that she had been granted power of attorney over her mother's legal affairs.[1]

## Power of attorney reporting

In August 2023, coverage aggregated by MSN News reported that Katherine Feinstein, then 66 years old, had been given power of attorney over the 90-year-old senator amid an ongoing financial dispute regarding the estate of Dianne Feinstein's late husband, [[richard-blum|Richard Blum]], according to reporting attributed to The New York Times.[1] The disclosure raised further questions about whether the senator should remain in office, given that she appeared to no longer be in control of her own legal affairs while continuing to cast votes in the [[us-senate|United States Senate]].[1]

The reporting arrived amid long-running calls for the senator's resignation over concerns about her frail health and her ability to perform the duties of her office, including an extended absence from the Senate earlier in 2023 following hospitalization for shingles and a subsequent case of encephalitis.[1]

## Commentary

In [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], commentator [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] framed the episode as evidence of an American "gerontocracy," describing the situation as one in which a senator votes on matters of serious consequence while her family is embroiled in a heated legal battle over the inheritance of the Feinstein–Blum fortune "as if she were already dead."[1]
