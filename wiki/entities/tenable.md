# Tenable

Tenable is a cybersecurity "exposure management" company focused on modern analytics used to measure and communicate cybersecurity risk.[1] Its Deputy Chief Technology Officer for [[operational-technology|Operational Technology]] and the [[internet-of-things|Internet of Things]] is [[marty-edwards|Marty Edwards]], formerly the last director of the Department of Homeland Security's [[ics-cert|ICS-CERT]] and a program manager at the [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]]'s [[idaho-national-laboratory|Idaho National Laboratory]].[1]

## Role in federal critical infrastructure policy

Tenable's stated focus includes "furthering government and industry initiatives to improve critical infrastructure security," and its personnel have participated directly in federal advisory structures — Edwards served in 2022 as a working group lead on the National Security Telecommunications Advisory Committee.[1] The company was one of two commercial vendors, along with [[dragos|Dragos]], seated on the witness panel at the February 6, 2024 [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Homeland Security Committee]] hearing "Securing Operational Technology: A Deep Dive into the Water Sector," a composition the Badlands account read as characteristic of [[public-private-partnership|public-private partnership]] governance of [[critical-infrastructure|critical infrastructure]].[1]

Through Edwards, Tenable's contribution to the hearing emphasized the cross-sector reuse of identical programmable logic controllers and the absence of supply chain capacity to replace such devices at scale after a mass ransomware or malware event.[1]
