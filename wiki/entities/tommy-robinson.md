# Tommy Robinson

Tommy Robinson is a British anti-immigration activist who organized the "[[unite-the-kingdom-rally|Unite the Kingdom]]" rally in London in May 2026, which drew tens of thousands of demonstrators according to police estimates.[1]

The rally became an international flashpoint after US Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] publicly backed British anti-immigration activists, saying it was "OK to defend your culture" and encouraging opponents of mass migration in the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]] to "keep on going."[1] UK Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] condemned the event as promoting "hatred and division," and police reported dozens of arrests, including several tied to alleged hate-crime offenses.[1]
