# Tommy Robinson

Tommy Robinson is a British anti-immigration activist who organized the "Unite the Kingdom" rally in London in May 2026, the event at the center of Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]]'s public backing of British opponents of mass migration.

## The Unite the Kingdom rally

The rally, which police estimated drew tens of thousands of demonstrators, was publicly condemned by British Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] as promoting "hatred and division." Police reported dozens of arrests, including several tied to alleged hate-crime offenses. Vance said it was "OK to defend your culture" and encouraged opponents of mass migration in the UK to "keep on going," arguing that Western governments have relied on "millions and millions of unvetted people."
