# University of Washington

The University of Washington (UW) is an American public research university identified as a major hub of federally funded "misinformation" research.[1]

UW hosts the [[center-for-an-informed-public|Center for an Informed Public]] (CIP), which is funded by the [[national-science-foundation|National Science Foundation]] and other agencies and studies "misinformation" while developing rapid-response frameworks to counter online narratives.[1] NSF award #2120496 provided $2.25 million to UW in 2021 for disinformation research, according to the NSF's own award search.[1]

Because the NSF is widely regarded by critics as the civilian arm of [[darpa|DARPA]], the grant is characterized as a Pentagon-adjacent grantmaking body funding research that would lead to the mass censorship of Americans.[1] NSF funding cuts in 2025 may have reduced CIP's scope.[1]
