# Green Street

Green Street is a real estate data and analytics firm whose valuation indices are used to track commercial property price movements.[1]

During the 2023 [[commercial-real-estate-crisis|commercial real estate crisis]], Green Street reported that Washington, D.C. office space values had slid 36% through March 2023 compared with a year earlier, attributing the decline to rising vacancies amid the post-Covid rise of remote and hybrid work.[1] The figure was cited alongside [[kastle-systems|Kastle Systems]] occupancy data in coverage of [[brookfield|Brookfield]]'s $161.4 million default on twelve D.C. office buildings.[1]
