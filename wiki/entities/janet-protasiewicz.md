# Janet Protasiewicz

Janet Protasiewicz is a Wisconsin jurist who won election to the [[wisconsin-supreme-court|Wisconsin Supreme Court]] in the April 2023 spring election, a contest that set the previous vote record for a Wisconsin Supreme Court seat decided in a spring election.[1]

## Role in the 2025 turnout comparison

Election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]] used Protasiewicz's prior record as the benchmark for measuring turnout in the [[2025-wisconsin-supreme-court-election|April 2025 Wisconsin Supreme Court election]]. He wrote that [[susan-crawford|Susan Crawford]] "blew past" Protasiewicz's record by almost 300,000 votes while defeating [[brad-schimel|Brad Schimel]] by ten points, with Schimel simultaneously setting a new record on the losing side.[1] Keshel cited the escalation as evidence that Democrats have "mastered mail-ballot turnout in off-year elections," observing that roughly 600,000 votes had been sufficient to win comparable Wisconsin races in 2018 and 2019 in a state whose population is not growing by any meaningful numbers.[1]
