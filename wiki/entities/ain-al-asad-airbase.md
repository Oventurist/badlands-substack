# Ain al-Asad Airbase

Al-Asad, or Ain al-Asad, is a large airbase in western Iraq hosting United States and coalition forces under [[us-central-command|U.S. Central Command]].[1]

## October 2023 drone attacks

On October 18, 2023 — the same day as the drone attack on the [[al-tanf-garrison|al-Tanf garrison]] in southern Syria — four American personnel suffered minor injuries during two separate drone attacks against U.S. and coalition forces stationed at al-Asad, according to CENTCOM.[1] Together with the twenty wounded at al-Tanf, the strikes brought the total to two dozen American military personnel injured in the wave of attacks, a figure first disclosed publicly on October 24, 2023.[1] [[pentagon|The Pentagon]] had confirmed the attacks the prior week but had not previously released casualty numbers.[1]

The attacks were widely attributed to Iranian-aligned proxy forces operating in Iraq and Syria, though Badlands commentary cautioned that such attributions come from an administration and defense establishment whose information the Truth Community regards as unreliable.[1]
