# Bab el-Mandeb Strait

The Bab el-Mandeb Strait is a maritime chokepoint linking the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden and the Indian Ocean, treated in Badlands coverage as one of the strategic waterways [[iran|Iran]] and its allied resistance groups could use as leverage against Israel and the West.[1]

## Role in the June 2026 Iranian escalation threats

Reporting from [[tasnim-news-agency|Tasnim News Agency]] in June 2026 stated that, following Iran's suspension of indirect talks with the United States, options under consideration by Tehran and allied groups included efforts to close the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] and to activate pressure points along other strategic waterways, including the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.[1] The threat was framed as conditional: Iranian armed forces and resistance groups were described as committed to retaliatory measures unless demands regarding Gaza and [[lebanon|Lebanon]] were met.[1]

Pairing Bab el-Mandeb with Hormuz signals a two-chokepoint pressure strategy — one at the mouth of the Persian Gulf and one at the southern entrance to the Red Sea — capable of disrupting global energy and container traffic simultaneously.[1]
