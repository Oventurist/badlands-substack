# Le Cercle

Le Cercle is an exclusive transatlantic foreign-policy and intelligence forum described by Badlands Media as one of the two most exclusive gatherings of the "deep-state milieu," alongside [[bilderberg]].[1]

The group is invoked in the article's profile of [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]], who is identified as one of only 46 people known to have attended both Bilderberg and Le Cercle meetings — offered as evidence of his standing within the international think-tank class that shaped Cold War strategy and, through [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]], the arming of the [[mujahideen]].[1]
