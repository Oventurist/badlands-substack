# Sports Talk

Sports Talk is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program that aired Mondays and Fridays at 12pm ET under the network's January 2023 schedule, hosted by [[jb-white|JB White]] and [[absolute-1776|Absolute 1776]].[1] The show was described as taking an unorthodox approach to sports coverage, cutting out politics from athletics and focusing on what the hosts called regaining America's pastime.[1]

Like the rest of the network's slate, Sports Talk aired live on the [[patel-patriot|Patel Patriot]] [[rumble|Rumble]] channel, with replays archived in library format on the Badlands Media channel.[1]
