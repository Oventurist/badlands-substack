# Trash Talk

Trash Talk is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday at 1:30pm ET as of January 2023, hosted by [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] and [[j-b-white|JB White]].[1] The format is conversational, ranging across subjects from the information war to pop culture and entertainment.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Trash Talk on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 8am ET, a conversational morning show in which Patrick Gunnels and J.B. White ranged from the Information War to pop culture and entertainment.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Trash Talk in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
