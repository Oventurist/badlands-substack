# Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference Office

The Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference Office (FIMI) is a [[state-department|State Department]] office tasked with monitoring foreign "disinformation" online. It is the successor to the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]], which was launched in 2016 under President Obama.[1]

## Criticism

FIMI and its predecessor have drawn criticism for allegedly pressuring technology companies to suppress conservative media and opinions.[1]

## Attempted closure and injunction

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s April 2025 executive order instructed agencies to eliminate duplicative and unnecessary functions, including FIMI, and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] followed by announcing the office's closure.[1] A federal judge in California temporarily blocked the shutdown: the June 13, 2025 injunction issued by U.S. District Judge [[susan-illston|Susan Illston]] halted layoffs at the office and required the department to seek court approval before making further changes.[1] The ruling sided with the office's employees, who claimed their firings violated federal workforce protections.[1]
