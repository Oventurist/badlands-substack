# Rosemary Vrablic

Rosemary Vrablic is a former private banker at [[deutsche-bank|Deutsche Bank]] who handled the accounts of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his family.[1]

## Role in the New York civil fraud trial

During the defense phase of New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]]' civil fraud case against Trump in late November 2023, Trump's team called four current and former Deutsche Bank employees to the stand, including Vrablic as the family's former private banker. The strategy sought to "flip the script" on the state's version of events by showing that the bank had courted Trump's business and had made its lending decisions with full knowledge of, and adjustments to, his stated asset values.[1]
