# Anthony Enzor-DeMeo

Anthony Enzor-DeMeo is the chief executive officer of [[mozilla|Mozilla]], the open-source software company behind the [[firefox|Firefox]] web browser.[1]

Shortly after his appointment, Enzor-DeMeo announced that Mozilla would be "tripling down" on artificial intelligence. In a blog post published December 16, 2025, he stated that Firefox would become a "modern AI browser and support a portfolio of new and trusted software additions."[1]

The announcement triggered a substantial backlash from Firefox's user base, many of whom had chosen the browser specifically because it lacked AI integration.[1] The company subsequently issued a clarification on Mastodon stating that Firefox would offer an option to disable all AI features entirely.[1]
