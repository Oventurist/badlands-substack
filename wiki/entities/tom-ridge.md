# Tom Ridge

Tom Ridge is a former Governor of Pennsylvania and the first Secretary of the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, and sits on the advisory board of [[newsguard|NewsGuard]].[1]

## NewsGuard advisory role

Badlands Media's "The Information Imperium" names Ridge — described as a "former DHS honcho" — among the security-state veterans advising NewsGuard, alongside [[michael-hayden|Michael Hayden]], [[arne-duncan|Arne Duncan]] and [[richard-stengel|Richard Stengel]].[1] The article argues that the concentration of former intelligence and homeland-security officials on the board of a private company that decides which news sites are labelled trustworthy blurs the line between public security apparatus and commercial content policing.[1]

Ridge's inclusion supports the article's broader thesis that the "information imperium" no longer needs covert operations of the [[operation-mockingbird|Mockingbird]] type: the same institutional class now performs narrative management openly, through ratings, advertiser pressure and "misinformation" labelling.[1]
