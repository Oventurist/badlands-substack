# Heidi Beirich

Heidi Beirich is the former director of the Intelligence Project at the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] and a self-described expert on fascism and extremism. She is identified in Badlands reporting as "Employee-2" in the [[department-of-justice|U.S. Department of Justice]]'s superseding indictment of the SPLC filed on June 2, 2026.[1]

## Allegations in the superseding indictment

Prosecutors allege that Beirich directed more than $1.2 million in SPLC donor funds over roughly two decades to a confidential informant identified in court documents only as "F-9," who was embedded in the neo-Nazi [[national-alliance|National Alliance]].[1] According to the indictment, Beirich and F-9 were in a romantic relationship, shared a residence, and maintained joint bank accounts into which approximately $140,000 of SPLC donor money was deposited between 2015 and 2021 for personal expenses.[1] While receiving those payments, F-9 allegedly continued fundraising for and otherwise supporting the National Alliance's activities.[1]

The indictment forms part of a broader set of charges against the SPLC for wire fraud, bank fraud, false statements to a federally insured bank, and conspiracy to commit money laundering, with prosecutors contending that the organization financially supported genuine extremist groups while publicly denouncing them and designating conservative organizations as "hate groups."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] quoted reporting describing Beirich as "a 58-year-old fascism expert who was the director of intelligence at the Alabama-based anti-extremism nonprofit between 2012 and 2019," and observed that "the word expert is a bit of a stretch. Her expertise is based on intimate knowledge of the inner workings of fascism."[1] She called for the entire network around the case to be traced and prosecuted rather than resolved through "ceremonial arrests."[1]
