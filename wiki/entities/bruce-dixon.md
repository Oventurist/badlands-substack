# Bruce Dixon

Bruce Dixon is the chief executive of [[vice-media|Vice Media]], who in February 2024 announced that the company would stop publishing on its flagship website and cut hundreds of jobs.[1]

## The February 2024 restructuring memo

Dixon laid out the plan in an internal memo reviewed by [[wall-street-journal|The Wall Street Journal]], writing that "it is no longer cost-effective for us to distribute our digital content the way we have done previously" and that, as part of the shift, "we will no longer publish content on vice.com."[1] He said the company could instead partner with established media companies to distribute its content.[1]

Dixon added that [[refinery29|Refinery29]], Vice's women's lifestyle-focused site, would continue to operate as a stand-alone publishing business, and that the company was in advanced discussions to sell it, with an announcement expected "in the coming weeks."[1] On staffing he wrote: "With this strategic shift comes the need to realign our resources and streamline our overall operations at Vice. Regrettably, this means that we will be reducing our workforce, eliminating several hundred positions."[1] Affected employees were to be notified the following week.[1] The cuts followed a failed effort by owner [[fortress-investment-group|Fortress Investment Group]] to sell the publisher and its brands, and formed part of a plan to make the company profitable after new owners took control in June 2023.[1]
