# Jesse Weber

Jesse Weber is a legal contributor for the cable network NewsNation.[1]

In May 2025 Weber commented on the announcement by [[dan-bongino|Dan Bongino]], deputy director of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]], that several matters of "potential public corruption" from the [[joe-biden|Biden]] years were being reopened or given renewed investigative attention.[1] Weber argued that the American public "deserves answers" on those cases, which included the [[white-house-cocaine-incident|discovery of cocaine at the White House]] in 2023, the [[dobbs-draft-opinion-leak-investigation|Dobbs Supreme Court leak]] of 2022, and the pipe bombs placed outside the Democratic and Republican national committee headquarters in Washington, D.C.[1]

"Let's be very clear about something, these are all very serious, and this isn't a case where we say someone didn't commit a crime," Weber said. "No, there's evidence of a crime. You want to talk about federal government property, drug possession, and possession of an explosive device, particularly in this kind of area. There's no doubt about that."[1]
