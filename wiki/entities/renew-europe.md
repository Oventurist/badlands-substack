# Renew Europe

Renew Europe is a liberal political group in the [[european-parliament|European Parliament]].[1]

## Support for the Migration Pact

Renew Europe was one of the three groups whose votes carried the [[eu-asylum-and-migration-pact|EU Asylum and Migration Pact]] through the European Parliament in April 2024, alongside the [[european-peoples-party|European People's Party]] and the Socialists and Democrats.[1] MEPs in these groups were urged to swallow their criticisms of the scheme and back the compromise legislation.[1] The group's foreign affairs coordinator, [[hilde-vautmans|Hilde Vautmans]], defended the outcome by saying "The new legislation is not perfect but we can only make migration manageable and humane with one European solution."[1]
