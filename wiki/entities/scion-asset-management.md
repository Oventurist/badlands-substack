# Scion Asset Management

Scion Asset Management is the hedge fund founded and managed by investor [[michael-burry|Michael Burry]], through which he conducted the equity and options positions that made him a fixture of financial-media coverage in the 2020s.[1]

## 2025 positioning and deregistration

A Scion 13F filing disclosed in November 2025 showed that approximately 80 percent of the firm's put positions were concentrated in [[palantir|Palantir]] and [[nvidia|Nvidia]], two of the highest-flying artificial-intelligence-linked equities of the cycle.[1] The disclosure followed Burry's cryptic public warning on X — "Sometimes, we see bubbles" — and was read as confirmation that the fund was betting directly against the AI trade.[1]

On the Monday following that disclosure, Scion terminated its registration with the Securities and Exchange Commission, an action described in coverage as Burry liquidating the fund and quitting the market.[1] By the Thursday night afterwards Burry had clarified on X that the Palantir position consisted of 50,000 put contracts bought at $1.84 apiece — about $9.2 million in total premium, not the $912 million figure circulating online — struck at $50 with a 2027 expiry.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the deregistration as emblematic of a market that had "decoupled into a hall of narrative mirrors," in which valuations respond to storytelling rather than to actual production, and linked the episode to the broader [[multipolar-margin-call|Multipolar Margin Call]] thesis.[1]
