# Everpeak Hospitality

Everpeak Hospitality is the management company for the independently owned Hampton Inn by Hilton in Lakeville, Minnesota.[1]

After allegations emerged that the property canceled reservations made by Department of Homeland Security and ICE agents on official government email addresses and government rates, citing the guests' "immigration work," Everpeak claimed a commitment to non-discrimination and said it was resolving the matter.[1] [[hilton-worldwide|Hilton]] nonetheless determined that the franchisee was not meeting brand standards and terminated the franchise agreement, removing the hotel from its systems.[1]
