# Echoing Green

**Echoing Green** is a social entrepreneurship fellowship organization named in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as a destination for graduates of [[brown-university|Brown University]]'s alleged identity-politics pipeline.[1]

The article states that Brown's NGO ties "to Soros and Echoing Green run deep," and that the [[philermenian-society|Philermenian Society]] channels students into [[george-soros|Soros]]-affiliated NGOs and "social movement engineering foundations like Echoing Green."[1] Such organizations, the author argues, function as "domestic regime-change agents, destabilizing culture from within via grievance amplification and identity fragmentation" within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
