# Yuriy Lutsenko

Yuriy Lutsenko is a Ukrainian politician who served as prosecutor general of [[ukraine|Ukraine]], succeeding the office held earlier by [[viktor-shokin|Viktor Shokin]].[1]

Lutsenko features in reporting from June 2021 on the investigation into [[blue-star-strategies|Blue Star Strategies]] for illegal lobbying. According to that reporting, Lutsenko had been "pitched" by the [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]- and [[burisma|Burisma]]-linked lobbying firm with the promise of "access" to "high levels" of the Clinton campaign in the weeks leading up to the 2016 presidential election.[1] The [[us-department-of-justice|Justice Department]] investigation into the firm was reported closed in May 2022 with no finding of wrongdoing.[1]
