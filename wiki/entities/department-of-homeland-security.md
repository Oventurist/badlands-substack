# Department of Homeland Security

## Role in the 2026 election order

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order on election administration directed the Department of Homeland Security to compile lists of confirmed US citizens eligible to vote and provide them to the states, a citizenship-verification function at the heart of the order's proof-of-citizenship measures.[1] The provision was among those blocked in 23 Democrat-led states when a federal appeals court declined in July 2026 to lift Judge [[indira-talwani|Talwani]]'s injunction while litigation continues.[1]
