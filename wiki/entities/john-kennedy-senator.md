# John Kennedy (senator)

John Kennedy is a Republican United States Senator from Louisiana.[1]

## Commentary on the 2025 reconciliation megabill

In April 2025, as congressional Republicans began drafting the domestic policy megabill informally called the "Big, Beautiful Bill," Kennedy publicly voiced doubt that the party could resolve its internal divisions.[1] Referring to the negotiating process, he said: "I know the dialectic is supposed to produce a final result, but sometimes it doesn't — many times up here, it doesn't. We can spend an entire year getting nothing done."[1]

His remarks came after a House vote finalized a fiscal framework but exposed fissures between the House and Senate over tax cuts, border security, and energy provisions, with Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] having promised fiscal hawks steep spending cuts while Senate Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] preserved maximum flexibility for his own conference.[1]
