# Playboy

Playboy is an American magazine treated in Badlands Media's [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series as an institution that played a significant and, in the author's assessment, deleterious role in the reshaping of Western culture from its inception onward — a role likened to that of the Kinsey Institute.[1]

[[robert-anton-wilson|Robert Anton Wilson]] served as the magazine's chief editor from 1960 to 1969, and co-authored the [[illuminatus-trilogy|Illuminatus Trilogy]] with fellow Playboy staffer [[robert-shea|Robert Shea]].[1] The article asserts that there is substantial evidence the magazine served as a hub for blackmail operations against U.S. politicians, a claim it describes as already familiar to followers of Q.[1]

Playboy thus functions in the series as a bridge between the 1960s counterculture promoted by figures such as [[timothy-leary|Timothy Leary]] and the older occult lineage the author traces back to the [[koryos|Koryos]].[1]
