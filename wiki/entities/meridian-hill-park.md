# Meridian Hill Park

Meridian Hill Park, also known as Malcolm X Park, is a federal urban park in Washington, D.C. administered by the [[national-park-service|National Park Service]]. Its terraced lower plaza contains a cascading fountain that WJLA described as a 13-basin structure and one of the longest fountains in North America.[1]

## Fountain rehabilitation

The Park Service said the cascading fountain and reflecting pool reopened to visitors on May 14, 2026 after years of work at the lower plaza.[1] According to the agency, the fountain had been turned off in 2019 during rehabilitation, and additional repairs were later needed to replace critical components and repair damaged concrete.[1]

The reopening followed a broader repair campaign for the capital's water features. The Washington Post reported on January 2, 2026 that procurement records showed the Park Service spending at least $54.2 million to repair fountains and water features at seven locations in the city, including a $10.68 million project at Meridian Hill Park.[1] On May 18, 2026, the [[department-of-the-interior|Interior Department]] released video showing water running at the fountain.[1]

## Reception

The reopening was cited in the Badlands Brief as an emblem of the effort by the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to restore Washington's public spaces, an effort also expressed in [[executive-order-14344|Executive Order 14344]], "Making Federal Architecture Beautiful Again."[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that it was "cool that our nation's capital is getting a face lift" while adding that she would be happier if corruption and illegitimacy were addressed as well, and hoped the project would embarrass and inspire mayors and governors elsewhere to clean up their own cities.[1]
