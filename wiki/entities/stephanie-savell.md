# Stephanie Savell

Stephanie Savell is co-director of the [[costs-of-war-project|Costs of War Project]] at [[brown-university|Brown University]]'s Watson Institute for International and Public Affairs.[1]

## "Death Outlives War" report

Savell authored a report published in May 2023 estimating that the post-9/11 [[war-on-terror|War on Terror]] may have caused at least 4.5 million deaths across roughly half a dozen countries.[1] The report examined people killed indirectly by the wars in Afghanistan, Iraq, Libya, Pakistan, Somalia, Syria, and Yemen, showing "how death outlives war."[1]

In an accompanying statement, Savell said, "In a place like Afghanistan, the pressing question is whether any death can today be considered unrelated to war," adding that "wars often kill far more people indirectly than in direct combat, particularly young children."[1]
