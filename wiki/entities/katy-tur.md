# Katy Tur

Katy Tur is an American television news anchor for [[msnbc|MSNBC]], where she has covered national politics and the Trump era extensively.[1]

## July 2024 Bolton interview

Tur appears in Badlands Media's analysis of the [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] vice-presidential selection as the MSNBC host who interviewed former national security adviser [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] about the prospect of a second Trump term.[1] Introducing the Ukraine question, Tur characterized Vance as "somebody who believes that Ukraine needs to make a peace deal with Russia and cede territory if necessary," adding that he does not believe continued American funding is financially sound because he doubts Ukraine can win.[1]

Badlands observed that the settlement Tur described closely resembled the terms Ukraine was reportedly prepared to accept in the first months of the war, before those negotiations were, in the article's account, sabotaged by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[boris-johnson|Boris Johnson]].[1] The exchange between Tur and Bolton was presented by the author as a useful window into how both establishment wings — liberal media and Republican hawks — interpret the realignment of American foreign policy.[1]
