# Darrin Bell

Darrin Bell is a Pulitzer Prize-winning American editorial cartoonist known for his work with [[washington-post|The Washington Post]] who was arrested in January 2025 on child pornography charges in Sacramento County, California.[1]

## Arrest and charges

According to an entry on the Sacramento County Jail's website, Bell was booked on January 15, 2025, and charged with felony "possession/control of obscene matter depicting a person under 18."[1] The investigation was conducted by Sacramento Valley Internet Crimes Against Children (ICAC) detectives and reportedly began after a tip regarding 18 files containing child sexual abuse material (CSAM) uploaded to an online service, per a statement by the [[sacramento-county-sheriffs-office|Sacramento County Sheriff's Office]] cited by the outlet BNO.[1] Investigators are said to have ultimately identified a total of 134 videos linked to the same account, which was allegedly owned and controlled by Bell.[1] A search warrant was reportedly executed at Bell's residence, resulting in his arrest and booking into the Sacramento County Main Jail.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media's coverage in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] emphasized the irony that Bell had won a Pulitzer for work directed *against* critics of what the commentary characterized as the grooming of children.[1] Contributor Ashe in America catalogued recurring themes in Bell's published cartoons — deflecting legitimate criticism as bigotry, constructing emotionally charged false equivalencies, and projecting the cartoonist's own conduct onto others — arguing that the pattern made the allegations appear predictable in hindsight.[1] The commentary concluded with the observation that "public support for groomers has become a pretty reliable tell… for groomers."[1]
