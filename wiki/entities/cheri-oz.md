# Cheri Oz

Cheri Oz is a [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]] Special Agent in Charge of the agency's Phoenix field division.[1]

## Statements on the Arizona fentanyl trade

Amid reporting in February 2023 on cartel influence in Arizona, Oz was quoted saying, "I want to be crystal clear. The drugs that are flooding Arizona every single day are sourced primarily by one evil entity, the Sinaloa Drug Cartel."[1] Her statement accompanied accounts of Tempe police seizing over 30 million lethal doses of fentanyl and of the DEA nationally seizing more than 50.6 million deadly fentanyl-laced counterfeit prescription pills and over 10,000 pounds of fentanyl powder in 2022, roughly half of which came from Arizona.[1] The attribution of the Arizona supply to the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] was cited in coverage of allegations by [[john-thaler|John Thaler]] concerning cartel bribery of Arizona officials.[1]
