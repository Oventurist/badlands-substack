# Iván Duque

Iván Duque served as President of [[colombia|Colombia]], immediately preceding [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]].[1] He features in Badlands coverage chiefly as a reference point for the tax, monetary, and security policies that Petro alleges his successor intends to restore.[1]

## Allegations by Petro

Petro has accused president-elect [[abelardo-de-la-espriella|Abelardo de la Espriella]] of planning to restore Duque's tax and monetary policies.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that if de la Espriella does so, it would indicate that "La Tigre" is most likely a Deep State operator.[1]

Separately, days after the arrest of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], Petro held a large public rally at which he disclosed for the first time that the US Deep State had originally planned to invade Venezuela and foment a civil war, using Colombia as the staging ground, with Duque's blessing.[1]
