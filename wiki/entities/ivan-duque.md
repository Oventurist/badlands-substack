# Iván Duque

**Iván Duque Márquez** is a former President of Colombia and a political protégé of [[alvaro-uribe|Álvaro Uribe]]. His tenure was marked by sharp confrontation with the government of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] in neighboring [[venezuela|Venezuela]] and by close alignment with United States regional policy.

## Allegations of a Venezuela invasion plan

Duque figures in the Badlands corpus as an alleged conduit for American covert policy in the Andes. Maduro, in his January 2025 "Bolivarian Shield 2025" address, named Duque and Uribe as "public enemies number one" of Venezuela's armed forces and people, linking them to Colombian paramilitarism and drug trafficking, in the same speech in which he characterized [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] as a fabricated instrument for destabilizing Venezuela.

The Brief reports that in January 2026 — days after Maduro was reportedly taken by U.S. forces — sitting Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] publicly alleged that his predecessor Duque had collaborated with the American [[deep-state|Deep State]] and had intended to use Colombia as a staging ground for an invasion of Venezuela and the ignition of a civil war. Badlands commentary compares the alleged plan to the 2011 Syrian conflict and the subsequent rise of [[isis|ISIS]], presenting both as manufactured crises used to justify expanded intervention.
