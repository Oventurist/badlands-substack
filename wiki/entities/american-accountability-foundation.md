# American Accountability Foundation

The American Accountability Foundation is a conservative government watchdog organization that filed a May 2026 referral against [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]] with the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]].

## The Monaco referral

The foundation accused the former Biden Deputy Attorney General of shielding [[microsoft|Microsoft]] from cybersecurity enforcement actions before joining the company in 2025 as President of Global Affairs. Its referral alleged the Biden DOJ failed to pursue major action against Microsoft despite multiple cyber intrusions between 2019 and 2023 tied to Russian and Chinese hackers that reportedly compromised sensitive US government systems.

The group highlighted the disparity between the DOJ's [[civil-cyber-fraud-initiative|Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative]], which under Monaco's oversight targeted other contractors for less serious alleged misconduct, and the absence of a comparable case against Microsoft. It also cited former Biden administration cybersecurity officials who later took jobs with Microsoft, including [[john-p-carlin|John P. Carlin]] and [[bryan-vorndran|Bryan A. Vorndran]].

The foundation asked DOJ inspectors general to investigate whether Monaco and other officials violated ethics, recusal, or revolving-door restrictions, and whether Microsoft could face liability under the [[false-claims-act|False Claims Act]] for representations made to federal agencies about the security of its systems.
