# Sun Tzu

**Sun Tzu** is the ancient Chinese strategist cited in the Badlands Brief article "Kekistani Code Talkers" as an early theorist of [[information-warfare|information warfare]] from the perspective of an entirely analog battlefield.[1]

The article closes its argument by invoking his maxim that "all warfare is based on deception," using it to justify the claim that the irregular and misunderstood sources and methods of information control in a [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation war]] are entirely expected rather than anomalous.[1]
