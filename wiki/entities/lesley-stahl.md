# Lesley Stahl

Lesley Stahl is a CBS News correspondent and long-serving anchor on the network's newsmagazine "60 Minutes."[1]

## Greene interview (December 2025)

In an interview broadcast on "60 Minutes" in December 2025, Stahl pressed Rep. [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]] on whether she still identified with the MAGA movement.[1] Asked "Are you MAGA?", Greene answered, "I'm America first."[1] When Stahl followed up — "And that's not the same as MAGA?" — Greene replied that "MAGA is President Trump's phrase. That's his, his political policies," referring to the "Make America Great Again" slogan that propelled [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 campaign to victory, adding, "I call myself America first."[1] Stahl pushed once more — "But you're not saying you're MAGA. That's over" — and Greene shook her head, repeating, "I'm America first. Yep."[1]
