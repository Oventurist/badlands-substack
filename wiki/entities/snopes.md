# Snopes

Snopes is an American fact-checking website. It has been a recurring target of criticism within alternative-media circles, where its ratings are treated as partisan rather than neutral arbitration of fact.[1]

## Reputational controversies

Coverage by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] noted that a Snopes co-founder had been suspended and subsequently fired over mass plagiarism, following a staff revolt, and that he had also been the subject of allegations of financial and sexual misconduct.[1] The outlet used these episodes to argue that the "fact checker" industry consists of "otherwise-unemployable partisan hacks."[1]

## The Starlink and OceanGate fact check

On Tuesday, June 20, 2023, Snopes published an article headlined "Was the Missing Titanic Submersible Using Satellites from Elon Musk's Company?", implying that the [[oceangate|OceanGate]] Titanic exploration submersible had lost contact with its surface support vessel because of a [[starlink|Starlink]] failure.[1] After Snopes tweeted the article, a Twitter community note stated that while OceanGate had used Starlink services, those services supported the company's surface vessels rather than its submersibles, and that radio frequencies such as Starlink's do not function underwater.[1]

Snopes then amended the article, adding an update clarifying that it was addressing "the legitimacy of claims that the submersible itself used Starlink during the exhibition, not whether its company did," and noting that the extent of any reliance by the submersible on Starlink was unknown.[1] The rating was changed to "Unproven," and subsequently to "False," accompanied by a further editor's note explaining that while OceanGate said it was using Starlink satellites for the trip, the submersible itself was not using the technology to communicate.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the episode demonstrated that "Snopes' fact checks always need to be fact checked," describing the site as "a source for smooth-brained, narrative peddlers" and recommending readers keep the article on hand to rebut anyone citing Snopes.[1] She framed the original piece as an attempt to demean [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] that collapsed under crowd-sourced correction.[1]
