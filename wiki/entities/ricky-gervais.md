# Ricky Gervais

Ricky Gervais is a British comedian whose stand-up special *Armageddon* was, at the start of January 2024, one of the two top comedy specials in the world.[1] He is described in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as a favorite of the online research community, remembered in particular for a Golden Globes monologue that pointedly invoked [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] before a Hollywood audience.[1]

## Armageddon and the 2024 comedy backlash

*Armageddon* and [[dave-chappelle|Dave Chappelle]]'s *Dreamer* were reported to be causing a "furious backlash" while going viral across both mainstream and dissident audiences, owing to their harsh criticism of establishment politics, transgender ideology, and what commentators termed cultural Marxism.[1] Both specials were distributed by [[netflix|Netflix]], a platform generally regarded within the truth movement as unfriendly to it.[1]

Badlands commentary allowed that such releases could be read as distraction events, mollification, or limited hangouts, but argued they more likely indicated either sympathetic actors inside corporate media or a corporate acknowledgement of how far the [[overton-window|Overton Window]] had shifted back toward objective reality.[1] Citing [[just-human|Just Human]], the write-up concluded that audiences sometimes need "social permission" to speak the truth, and that in 2024 they were getting it.[1]
