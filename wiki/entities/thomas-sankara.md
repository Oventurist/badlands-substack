# Thomas Sankara

Thomas Sankara was the first president of [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]], serving from 1983 until he was killed in a coup in 1987, and is remembered as a Pan-Africanist icon.[1]

## Presidency and assassination

Sankara was branded a Marxist by Western authorities — a characterization Badlands commentary describes as a common trope, citing the research of Colonel Towner-Watkins — because he refused loans and financial assistance from the [[imf|International Monetary Fund]], which Badlands writers characterize as the preferred mechanism used by the globalist cabal to place nations in debt bondage.[1] In Badlands' reading, this refusal is the likely reason Sankara was violently overthrown by [[blaise-compaore|Blaise Compaoré]], who seized power in the 1987 coup during which Sankara was assassinated.[1]

## Legacy

Sankara's assassination remains the reference point for subsequent Burkinabè politics. Badlands coverage frames the 2015 coup attempt against the post-Compaoré transitional government, and the later amnesty granted by interim president [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] to 21 soldiers convicted over that plot, as episodes in a longer struggle over foreign influence in the country that began with Sankara's overthrow.[1]
