# Washington University Transgender Center

The Washington University Transgender Center at St. Louis Children's Hospital is a pediatric gender clinic in Missouri that came under scrutiny in 2023 following whistleblower allegations about its practices.[1]

## Whistleblower allegations and investigations

In 2023 the center tacitly confirmed some of the claims made by a whistleblower while pledging vague changes, even as it exonerated itself of "substandard care causing adverse outcomes for patients."[1] The pledges came amid investigations by Missouri Attorney General [[andrew-bailey|Andrew Bailey]] and Senator [[josh-hawley|Josh Hawley]] (R-Mo.).[1]

## Research controversy

Reporting on the clinic situated it within a wider controversy over the evidence base for [[pediatric-gender-transition-treatment|pediatric gender transition treatment]], including puberty blockers, cross-sex hormones, and the surgical removal of healthy breasts and genitals in minors.[1] A study published in the fall of 2022 in the Journal of the American Medical Association Pediatrics concluded that breast removal improves "chest dysphoria" in young women who identify as men or nonbinary, but inexplicably hid its results pertaining to gender dysphoria — suggesting those results did not align with the researchers' hypothesis.[1] An accompanying editorial proclaimed that so-called top surgery in adolescents and young adults is "effective and medically necessary."[1]

## Commentary

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] that the episode illustrates the vulnerability of the medical system to infiltration and manipulation because it is an "extrinsically based orthodoxy" — a belief system grounded in deference to external authority rather than personal critical assessment.[1] He contended that overly compartmentalized expert boards leave most doctors executing treatments they neither developed nor understand, producing socially reinforced compliance, and that the remedy at the level of the individual citizen is to train in critical thinking and epistemology so extrinsic beliefs become intrinsic ones.[1]
