# Kuhn, Loeb & Co.

Kuhn, Loeb & Co. was a New York investment banking house of German origin. It appears in Badlands Media reporting on the founding of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] through remarks entered in the Congressional Record by Congressman [[louis-t-mcfadden|Louis T. McFadden]].[1]

## Alleged role in the Federal Reserve

In the passage quoted by Badlands, McFadden states that shortly after the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act]] became law on December 23, 1913, "the German international bankers, Kuhn, Loeb & Co., sent one of their partners here to run it."[1] The claim is presented within McFadden's broader argument that the Act was secured through the influence of Wall Street interests operating on President [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] via [[edward-mandell-house|Colonel Edward Mandell House]], rather than through the independent judgment of Congress.[1]

The Badlands article uses the allegation to support its wider thesis that the true beneficiaries of the Federal Reserve arrangement are private international interests rather than the American public, even though public benefit is the premise on which the arrangement's legitimacy rests under [[trust-law|trust law]].[1]
