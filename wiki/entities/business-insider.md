# Business Insider

Business Insider is an American digital business and technology news outlet.

## 2024 layoffs

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] cites *Variety*'s January 2024 report on layoffs at Business Insider, listing the outlet alongside the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]], [[time-magazine|Time Magazine]], [[nbc-news|NBC News]], [[sports-illustrated|Sports Illustrated]] and [[national-geographic|National Geographic]] as evidence of an industry-wide contraction. In the Badlands reading, the vulnerability of a digital-native, venture-backed outlet such as Business Insider illustrates that the crisis of [[mainstream-media|centralized media]] is structural rather than a matter of print versus digital distribution.
