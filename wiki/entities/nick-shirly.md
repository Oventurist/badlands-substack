# Nick Shirly

Nick Shirly is an independent video journalist known for on-the-ground reporting on alleged fraud in publicly funded programs, particularly in Minnesota.

## Reporting on Minnesota fraud

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cites Shirly in the February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief, recalling the videos he published documenting [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Somali fraud in Minnesota]]. In her account, once the videos circulated the operators of the implicated programs began "freestyling on the solution": the government reported that the centers in question were closed, while operators trucked children into the facilities "to make the situation appear legit."

Ashe invokes the episode as an analogy for the reporting that federal grant money had flowed to Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] while she prosecuted [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], speculating that a similar pattern of maintaining appearances around a funded operation may have been at work.
