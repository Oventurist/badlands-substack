# Aaron Heitke

Aaron Heitke is a former chief patrol agent of the [[united-states-border-patrol|U.S. Border Patrol]]'s San Diego sector who publicly accused the Biden–Harris administration of concealing a rise in encounters with migrants tied to terrorism.[1]

## House testimony, September 2024

Testifying at a House hearing on Wednesday, September 18, 2024, Heitke said arrests of "significant interest aliens" — his term for people with ties to terrorism — had increased since Joe Biden took office, and accused the administration of trying to cover up the rising arrest statistics.[1] "At the time, I was told I could not release any information on this increase in SIAs or mention any of the arrests," he said. "The administration was trying to convince the public there was no threat at the border."[1]

According to Heitke, arrests of such individuals in the San Diego sector rose from 10–15 annually before 2021 to more than 100 in 2022, "well over that in 2023 and even more than that registered this year," adding pointedly that "these are only the ones we caught."[1] He further testified that he had been required to release people who crossed the border illegally into local communities that could not support them — an action criticized at the same hearing by San Diego County Supervisor Jim Desmond, who pointed to the costs of transporting those immigrants.[1]
