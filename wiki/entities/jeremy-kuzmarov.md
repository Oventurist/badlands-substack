# Jeremy Kuzmarov

Jeremy Kuzmarov is a writer for Covert Action Magazine who in 2021 authored what Badlands Media describes as the most comprehensive analysis available of the plot to kill Senator [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]].[1]

The piece, titled "New Evidence Implicates CIA, LAPD, FBI and Mafia as Plotters in Elaborate 'Hit' Plan to Prevent RFK From Ever Reaching White House," assembles the elaborate conspiracy into what Badlands calls a clear and concise demonstration of who carried out the murder, how the cover-up was managed and why it happened.[1] To the article's author's knowledge, no one has done a better job of the task.[1]

Kuzmarov's reconstruction implicates the [[cia|CIA]], the [[lapd|LAPD]], the [[fbi|FBI]] and organised crime acting in concert, with [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]] serving as the visible scapegoat rather than the actual killer.[1]
