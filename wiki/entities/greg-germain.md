# Greg Germain

Greg Germain is a lawyer and law professor at Syracuse University in New York who was cited in June 2024 commentary on the potential consequences of sentencing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to prison in his New York criminal case.[1]

## Commentary on a Trump prison sentence

Germain told *Newsweek* that imprisoning Trump would lead to many legal challenges, part of a broader argument by legal experts that jailing the presumptive Republican presidential nominee would create a constitutional crisis.[1] The remarks were made ahead of Trump's scheduled July 11, 2024 sentencing following his May 30 conviction on all 34 counts of falsifying business records in the [[trump-hush-money-case|hush money case]] involving payments to [[stormy-daniels|Stormy Daniels]].[1]
