# Claudia Sheinbaum

## Tensions with the Trump administration (2026)

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described growing public tension between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum, whom he characterized as reluctant to assist the US government in fighting the cocaine cartels — behavior that provoked Trump into accusing Sheinbaum of being a cartel puppet.[1] The friction accompanied the Trump administration's indictment of ten Mexican government officials accused of connections to the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa cartel]], part of what the commentator called "moving up the food chain" of cartel-controlled Mexican officials.[1]
