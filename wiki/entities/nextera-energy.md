# NextEra Energy

**NextEra Energy** is an American utility and renewable-energy company identified in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]] as one of the four largest solar companies in the United States.[1] Carlson arrived at the list by searching for "the biggest solar companies in the US," which returned NextEra, [[avangrid|Avangrid]], [[sunpower|SunPower]], and [[first-solar|First Solar]].[1]

Between [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s inauguration and May 2023, Carlson reported NextEra's share price down 7 percent — the mildest decline among the eight losers in his nine-company sample, but a decline nonetheless during a period of aggressive federal support for renewables.[1] The contrast with First Solar, which he reported up 108 percent, is the anomaly on which his favoritism argument rests.[1]
