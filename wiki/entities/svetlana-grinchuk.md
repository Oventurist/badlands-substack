# Svetlana Grinchuk

Svetlana Grinchuk is a Ukrainian politician who served as Energy Minister.[1]

She resigned following the November 2025 disclosure by Ukraine's [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau]] and the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office of a $100 million corruption scheme involving the country's state-owned nuclear energy operator.[1] The same scandal produced the resignations of Justice Minister [[german-galushchenko|German Galushchenko]] and presidential chief of staff [[andriy-yermak|Andrey Yermak]].[1]
