# Christopher Bedford

Christopher Bedford is the editor in chief of Blaze News, the news division of [[blaze-media|Blaze Media]].[1]

## Reporting on the January 6 pipe bomb case

Bedford led or oversaw the November 2025 Blaze News investigation reporting that a forensic gait analysis of former [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] officer [[shauni-rae-kerkhoff|Shauni Rae Kerkhoff]] produced a 94%–98% match to the suspect in the [[january-5-2021-pipe-bombs|January 5, 2021 pipe bomb]] case.[1]

While reporting the story, Bedford drove to Kerkhoff's residence in Alexandria, Virginia, which appeared to be under law enforcement watch on a Friday night; after stopping to observe the home, he was pulled over by local police and then allowed to leave.[1] The episode was cited in the Badlands News Brief as an indication of the sensitivity of the investigation.[1]
