# Information Liberation

Information Liberation is an alternative news website that aggregates and comments on mainstream reporting from a dissident-right perspective. It appears among the outlets excerpted in the Badlands News Brief compiled by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands Media

The June 16, 2023 brief reproduced Information Liberation's write-up of the jury verdict ordering [[starbucks|Starbucks]] to pay more than $25 million to [[shannon-phillips|Shannon Phillips]], a regional manager the site said was fired "for being white as a sacrificial offering to the [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] movement."[1] The outlet quoted [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] account of the 2018 Rittenhouse Square arrests and added its own prior reporting that Starbucks employees said they were forced to watch "video after video" of white police officers attacking Black people as part of the company's racial bias training.[1]
