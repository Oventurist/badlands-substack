# Annie Ohana

Annie Ohana is a teacher at L.A. Matheson Secondary School in Surrey, British Columbia, who spoke publicly in March 2024 about online harassment she said she had experienced over the political content of her classroom.[1]

## Classroom and curriculum

Ohana's classroom is decorated with colourful and thought-provoking posters and flags that form part of her curriculum and are meant to ignite dialogue among her students.[1] "We've had students tell us that those flags really mean something to them," she said. "It represents them."[1]

## Online dispute

While the flags and posters are embraced at the school, they were also the reason Ohana was targeted online by [[chanel-pfahl|Chanel Pfahl]], a former high school teacher whose X biography says she exposes "wokism" in Canadian schools.[1] Asked for comment, Pfahl said in a direct message on X that "any reasonable person who looks through (Ohana's) thousands of posts on X will see that she is indoctrinating kids with radical woke ideology."[1]

Ohana said it was not the first time she had experienced this kind of harassment, but the first time she had spoken publicly about it and asked for help online.[1] "I think really the call out is to make sure that other teachers know it's OK to ask for help," she said. "You don't have to suffer in silence."[1]
