# National Science Foundation

The National Science Foundation (NSF) is the U.S. federal agency that funds basic scientific research through grants to universities and private firms. In January 2023 it was identified as a principal funding channel for a new generation of automated "misinformation" detection tools directed at domestic American speech.[1]

## Misinformation grant program

Reporting cited by Badlands Media stated that the NSF had awarded several million dollars in grants to universities and private firms to develop tools "eerily similar" to those built in 2011 by [[darpa|DARPA]] under its [[social-media-in-strategic-communication|Social Media in Strategic Communication]] (SMISC) program.[1] DARPA had described the earlier tools as helping to "identify misinformation or deception campaigns and counter them with truthful information," work that began with the Arab Spring uprisings that preceded the rise of [[isis|ISIS]].[1]

The concern raised in the coverage was that military-grade artificial intelligence originally developed to silence ISIS was being adapted to identify and censor American dissent on subjects such as vaccine safety and election integrity.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]] framed the NSF grants as an instance of the Biden administration weaponizing government against domestic critics, describing artificial intelligence as a weapon of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] now "swiveled around at our own citizens," and calling the reporting a "smoking gun."[1] He argued that the two subjects the security state most feared public exploration of were vaccine safety and election integrity.[1]
