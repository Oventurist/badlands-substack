# Ralph Van Deman

Ralph Van Deman was a United States Army officer described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the protégé of the British intelligence officer [[claude-dansey|Claude Dansey]], who was then resident at New York's [[sleepy-hollow-club|Sleepy Hollow Club]].[1]

In 1918 Van Deman set up the shadowy military intelligence agency known as [[black-chamber|The Black Chamber]], an organization that excelled at signals intelligence and that was given a new name in 1930 under the banner of the [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]].[1] After World War I, Van Deman recommended his mentor Dansey for a Distinguished Service Medal "for guiding, planning and implementation of an American intelligence service," an endorsement Ehret cites as evidence that the American intelligence architecture was designed under British direction.[1]
