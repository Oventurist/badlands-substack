# Harrison Deal

Harrison Deal was a young Georgia political aide connected to Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]], Senator Kelly Loeffler, and Senator [[david-perdue|David Perdue]], who died in a highway crash in Georgia in the period following the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## Significance in election-integrity commentary

Attorney [[sidney-powell|Sidney Powell]] invoked Deal's death in February 2026, noting that it occurred after Kemp, Loeffler, and Perdue had all called for a signature audit of the 2020 results, and urging observers to "run the timeline on Kemp's change of heart."[1] Badlands closed its commentary on the Georgia grand jury revelations with "RIP, Harrison Deal. We may still get justice for you, yet."[1] No causal link between the crash and the election dispute has been established in any legal proceeding.[1]
