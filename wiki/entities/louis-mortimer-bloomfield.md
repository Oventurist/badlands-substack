# Louis Mortimer Bloomfield

Louis Mortimer Bloomfield was a Montreal figure who served as a Vice President of the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] and was a member of the [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Club]].[1]

## Claimed role in the Kennedy assassination

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that Bloomfield was the WWF Vice President later replaced by [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]], and that New Orleans District Attorney Jim Garrison discovered Bloomfield to be at the heart of the Montreal-based assassination of the anti-Malthusian President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] in 1963.[1] This claim is used in the article to connect the conservationist and 1001 Club milieu directly to the removal of pro-development leaders in the 1960s — a group Ehret also identifies as including Enrico Mattei, [[charles-de-gaulle|Charles de Gaulle]], [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Bobby Kennedy]] — whose elimination left the vacuum filled by the [[malthusianism|Malthusian]] revival of 1968–1972.[1]
