# Merrill Lynch

Merrill Lynch is an American investment firm that appears in Badlands Media coverage as an early stop in the career of hedge fund manager [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]].[1] Weinstein interned at the firm at age 15 and returned to work there after graduating from the University of Michigan in 1995, before moving to [[deutsche-bank|Deutsche Bank]] in 1998.[1]
