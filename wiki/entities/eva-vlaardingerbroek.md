# Eva Vlaardingerbroek

**Eva Vlaardingerbroek** is a commentator whose post on [[x|X]] helped escalate the September 2023 confrontation between [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]].[1]

In a tweet noting that the hashtag #BanTheADL was trending, Vlaardingerbroek criticised the censorship tactics routinely employed by organisations of the ADL's type.[1] Musk replied to her post by floating the idea of running a public poll to gauge sentiment toward the organisation, a suggestion the ADL answered by declaring itself "unsurprised yet undeterred that anti-Semites, white supremacists, conspiracy theorists and other trolls have launched a coordinated attack on our organization."[1]
