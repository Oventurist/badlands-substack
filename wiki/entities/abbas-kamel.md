# Abbas Kamel

Abbas Kamel is an Egyptian intelligence official who served as head of Egypt's General Intelligence Directorate and acted as one of the principal regional mediators in negotiations over hostages held in Gaza.[1]

## Role in Gaza hostage negotiations

In January 2024, sources told Reuters that Kamel would participate in talks in Europe on a second potential Gaza hostage deal alongside [[cia|CIA]] Director [[william-burns|William Burns]], [[mossad|Mossad]] chief [[david-barnea|David Barnea]], and Qatari Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani.[1] The administration of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had been attempting to secure the release of more than 100 remaining hostages taken during the [[hamas|Hamas]] attack of October 7, 2023, although a considerable distance remained between the two sides' demands.[1]

Kamel and his Qatari counterparts had previously met with the American and Israeli intelligence chiefs, helping to broker a short-lived truce in November 2023 during which more than 100 hostages were freed.[1] The recurrence of the same intelligence-service channel underscored that the Gaza hostage file was being handled principally through spy agencies rather than conventional diplomacy.[1]
