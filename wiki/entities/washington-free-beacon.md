# Washington Free Beacon

The **Washington Free Beacon** is an American conservative news outlet known for investigative reporting on political figures and institutions.[1]

In late January 2024 the Free Beacon reported that Fulton County district attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] had fired [[amanda-timpson|Amanda Timpson]], the employee who warned her about the mishandling of federal funds, while not denying Timpson's underlying allegations.[1] The outlet's reporting was based in part on a recording it reviewed of a November 19, 2021 meeting between Timpson and Willis, in which Timpson described a plan by campaign aide [[michael-cuffee|Michael Cuffee]] to spend part of a $488,000 federal youth gang prevention grant on swag, computers, and travel.[1]

The story was picked up by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news brief, where writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited it as evidence of what she called a stunning abuse of power by Willis.[1]
