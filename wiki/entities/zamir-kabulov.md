# Zamir Kabulov

**Zamir Kabulov** is the Russian special presidential envoy for [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] and director of the Second Asia Department of the [[russian-foreign-ministry|Russian Foreign Ministry]].[1]

## Role in Russia–Taliban normalization

Ahead of the June 2024 St. Petersburg International Economic Forum, Kabulov told [[tass|TASS]] that the visiting [[taliban|Taliban]] delegation would include not only Minister of Labor and Social Affairs Abdul Manan Omari but also the head of the Afghan Chamber of Commerce and Industry.[1]

Kabulov also said that Russia's foreign and justice ministries had reported to President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] that the Taliban could be removed from the country's list of banned organizations.[1] Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] said the initiative reflected objective reality.[1] The delisting effort formed part of a broader Russian opening toward the Taliban that also included reported cooperation against [[cia|CIA]]-backed terrorist networks.[1]
