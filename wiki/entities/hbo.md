# HBO

HBO is an American premium television network and documentary producer. In March 2024 it released a documentary examining alleged child abuse at [[nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]], which highlighted two offenders connected to the channel, including dialogue coach [[brian-peck|Brian Peck]].[1]

Subsequent reporting by the Daily Mail, drawing on court records, indicated that the involvement of convicted and accused pedophiles with Nickelodeon productions was far more extensive than the documentary had shown, with five convicted child molesters and two further accused individuals having worked on or with the channel's programming.[1]
