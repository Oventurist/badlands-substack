# American Liberty League

The American Liberty League was the Wall Street corporatist organization that campaigned to keep the United States out of the Second World War, in the hope that American financiers could co-lead a New World Order alongside Germany, France and Italy.[1]

That posture collapsed in 1941, when Japan's attack on Pearl Harbor polarized the American psyche so deeply that continued resistance to America's entry into the war became political suicide.[1] Wall Street's corporatist organizations had already been called out by [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]] in a 1938 speech in which the president reminded Congress that fascism is in essence "ownership of government by an individual, by a group, or by any other controlling private power," and warned that "a concentration of private power without equal in history is growing."[1]

The League's political project sat alongside the failed February 1933 assassination attempt on Roosevelt and the 1934 coup plot thwarted by General [[smedley-butler|Smedley Butler]].[1]
