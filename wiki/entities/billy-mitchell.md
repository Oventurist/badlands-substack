# Billy Mitchell

**Brigadier General Billy Mitchell** was an early 20th-century United States military officer cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as one of the leading American figures who understood Britain's role in supporting the Confederacy during the Civil War and Britain's subsequent manipulation of global wars.[1]

According to Ehret, Mitchell fought against the Anglo-American "special relationship" tooth and nail, and led the military to create [[war-plan-red|War Plan Red]] and War Plan Orange in order to defeat Britain under the assumption of an eventual war between the English-speaking powers.[1] Those plans became official US military doctrine in 1930 and were, in Ehret's telling, a response to the earlier Anglo-Canadian invasion plan known as Defence Scheme 1.[1]

Ehret's discussion of Mitchell draws on the 2010 LaRouche PAC documentary *"The Special Relationship is for Traitors."*[1]
