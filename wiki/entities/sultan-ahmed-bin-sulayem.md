# Sultan Ahmed bin Sulayem

Sultan Ahmed bin Sulayem is an Emirati businessman and the longtime chairman and chief executive officer of [[dp-world|DP World]], the Dubai-based global shipping and logistics group.[1]

## Ties to Trump business ventures

Bin Sulayem was involved in [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s original Dubai real estate venture.[1] He also served as executive chairman of the Emirati developer Nakheel at the time that company announced its partnership with the Trump Organization on the forthcoming Trump Tower in Dubai.[1] In Badlands analysis these connections are cited as evidence that Dubai-based commercial actors sit inside the network associated with the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], rather than with the established global regime.[1]

## Context: the emirate power struggle

His company's competition with the Abu Dhabi-based AD Ports is presented as a visible surface of a broader factional contest between Dubai and Abu Dhabi within the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], a federation in which each of the seven emirates retains its own sovereignty alongside the federal government.[1]
