# Masimo

Masimo is an American medical technology company led by chief executive [[joe-kiani|Joe Kiani]].[1]

The company entered political reporting in 2024 because of its CEO's ties to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]. Kiani, a substantial donor to Biden's campaigns and family foundation, was appointed in September 2021 to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], which advises the executive branch on policy areas involving Masimo's line of business — an overlap that drew ethics concerns.[1] In August 2024, Biden spent a five-day family vacation at Kiani's $37 million ranch in Santa Ynez, California, with the White House describing the Masimo executive as a "friend," indicating the stay was free of charge.[1]
