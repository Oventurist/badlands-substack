# Malcolm Roberts

Malcolm Roberts is an Australian politician who publishes commentary critical of international institutions on his personal website. In May 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] highlighted his article "Is the UN Trying to Groom Our Kids?", which questioned the [[united-nations|United Nations]]'s role in promoting sexual content and standards to children.[1]

## Badlands coverage

Badlands carried the Roberts piece without an excerpt, using it as the springboard for extended commentary by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] on the [[sexualization-of-children|sexualization of children]].[1] Deschamps argued that the intent behind such initiatives is to normalize adult sexual contact with minors as another sexual identity, and that this serves a broader design to destabilize the family and produce a controlled population.[1]
