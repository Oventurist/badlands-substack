# James Logan

James Logan was a Pennsylvania statesman and scholar, described in the Badlands Media essay "In Defense of Benjamin Franklin" as an advisor to Jonathan Spotiswood who played a special role in shaping the mind of the young [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]].[1]

Through Logan's vast library, Franklin was introduced to the writings of continental scientists such as Gottfried Leibniz.[1] The essay places this influence in the period after Franklin's return from England, when he quickly became a leading republican figure within the networks established by [[cotton-mather|Cotton Mather]], Jonathan Spotiswood and William Pitt.[1]
