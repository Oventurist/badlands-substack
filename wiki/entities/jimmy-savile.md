# Jimmy Savile

Jimmy Savile was a British DJ and broadcaster posthumously exposed as a prolific sex offender, described in the Badlands News Brief coverage as a "paedophile DJ" and "notorious sex beast."[1]

In February 2025 his former Scottish cottage, Allt-na-Reigh in Glencoe, featured in news coverage after a fire ripped through garages next to the house, with onlookers filming the blaze; police locked down the road and urged residents to avoid the area, and firefighters were called to the scene.[1] The property had become a morbid tourist attraction drawing thousands of walkers and selfie-takers to the famed Glencoe spot.[1] The Badlands brief carried the item under the headline "Burn In Hell."[1]

## Alleged occult dimension

Savile also appears in Badlands Media coverage of [[satanic-ritual-abuse|Satanic Ritual Abuse]]. A client of psychotherapist [[valerie-sinason|Dr. Valerie Sinason]] alleges to have been sexually abused by Savile and a group of hooded occultists during a Satanic ritual in the basement of a hospital where Savile volunteered and in which he even maintained quarters.[2] The account is cited in support of the argument that ritual abuse involving Satanism receives uniquely dismissive treatment compared with abuse attributed to other religions.[2]
