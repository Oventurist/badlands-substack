# DeAnna Burt

Lt. Gen. DeAnna Burt is a United States Space Force officer who, as of June 2023, served as the service's Chief Operating Officer.[1] She drew national attention that month for remarks delivered at a Pride event held at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

## Pentagon Pride event remarks (June 2023)

Speaking at the event, Burt criticized the proliferation of more than 400 state-level regulations she characterized as "anti-LGBTQ+," and argued that such laws posed a danger both to military readiness and to individual service members.[1] The mechanism of that danger was not spelled out in detail; Burt suggested that the laws would "distract" service members from their duties by preventing them from being their "true selves."[1] She further indicated, indirectly, that the asserted threat extended to the children of service members, on the theory that soldiers who are parents might feel unsafe if their transgender children lacked access to gender-affirming healthcare.[1]

## Reception

The remarks were reported by ZeroHedge and reproduced in the Badlands News Brief, where contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] disputed the readiness argument.[1] She contrasted Burt's framing with a Pentagon study cited in the underlying article finding that 77% of Gen Z candidates are ineligible for military service because of mental instability and physical limitations, arguing that if that figure were accurate the services faced a far larger readiness problem than state legislation.[1] Ashe also invoked the promotion of [[rachel-levine|Rachel Levine]] as an earlier marker of what she saw as the institutional decline of the uniformed services, and concluded that the armed forces appeared to be "being dismantled from within."[1] The episode was one of several in the brief grouped under the theme of institutional capture within the [[united-states-space-force|United States Space Force]] and the wider defense establishment.
