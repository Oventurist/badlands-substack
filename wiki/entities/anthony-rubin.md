# Anthony Rubin

Anthony Rubin is the founder of [[muckraker|Muckraker]], an independent investigative outlet, and a citizen journalist whose on-the-ground reporting has been credited with extending what commentators dubbed the "[[nick-shirley-effect|Nick Shirley Effect]]" — the wave of amateur documentary investigation touched off by [[nick-shirley|Nick Shirley]].[1]

## Columbus investigation

In late December 2025 Rubin travelled to Columbus, Ohio, home to the second-largest Somali community in the United States, to investigate daycare centers there.[1] The trip came less than a day after Ohio attorney [[mehek-cooke|Mehek Cooke]] said federal investigators were examining allegations that elements within Ohio's Somali community had defrauded millions of dollars from the state's [[medicaid|Medicaid]] system.[1]

Rubin wrote on X that "the first Somali-affiliated daycare facility that we knocked on after landing in Columbus, Ohio, today did not answer," posting video of the facility, Great Minds Learning Academy.[1] He added that "a neighbor across the street told us, 'I've never seen anybody come out of the building or go into the building.'"[1] The episode was reported by ZeroHedge under the framing that Rubin had immediately found the "first signs of potentially massive Somali fraud."[1] Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] commented on the affair simply, "This isn't what the founders intended."[1]
