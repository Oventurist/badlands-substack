# Michel Aoun

Michel Aoun is a Lebanese politician and former army commander who served as President of [[lebanon|Lebanon]] from October 2016 until his term ended in October 2022, leaving a presidential vacancy that lasted more than two years.[1]

## Presidency and succession

Having assumed office in October 2016, Aoun appointed General [[joseph-aoun|Joseph Aoun]] — no relation — as Commander of the Lebanese Armed Forces in March 2017, shortly after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] entered office in the United States.[1] Michel Aoun is described in Badlands commentary as a close confidant and ally of [[massad-boulos|Massad Boulos]], the father-in-law of Tiffany Trump whom President Trump appointed as Special Envoy to the Middle East.[1]

Lebanon's parliament required thirteen attempts before electing a successor to Michel Aoun, finally choosing Joseph Aoun in the [[2025-lebanese-presidential-election|January 2025 presidential election]].[1]
