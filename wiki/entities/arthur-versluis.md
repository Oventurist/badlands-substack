# Arthur Versluis

**Arthur Versluis** is a professor and author cited by Badlands Media for his definition of totalitarianism in *The New Inquisitions: Heretic-Hunting and the Intellectual Origins of Modern Totalitarianism*.[1]

## Definition of totalitarianism

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s January 20, 2023 essay quotes Versluis as writing that "totalitarianism is the modern phenomenon of total centralized state power coupled with the complete obliteration of basic, individual human rights. In a totalized state. There are those in power, and there are the objectified masses … the masses are transformed into dependent subjects of these pathological rulers, and take on a psychologically regressed and child-like status."[1]

## Place in the argument

The definition is deployed to specify what the [[totalitarianism|totalitarian]] variant of [[mass-psychosis]] looks like in practice, and is paired with Hannah Arendt's characterization of totalitarianism as "an attempted transformation of human nature itself."[1] Deschamps used both to argue that once an individual and their in-group have no clear and effective moral compass, the soil is ripe for triggering a mass psychosis, after which the population hands control of its own life over to a priest class, ideologues, politicians or bureaucrats who themselves suffer from delusion.[1] The book's subtitle framing — heretic-hunting as the intellectual origin of modern totalitarianism — parallels the essay's own comparison between the witch hunts and contemporary scapegoating.[1]
