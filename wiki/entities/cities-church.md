# Cities Church

Cities Church is a Christian congregation in St. Paul, Minnesota. It became a national flashpoint in January 2026 when, according to the Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026, dozens of left-wing activists stormed the church during a Sunday service.

The demonstrators were reportedly targeting a pastor believed to be connected to the local field office operations of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]], at a time when federal deportation operations in the Minneapolis–St. Paul sanctuary-city region had triggered sustained protests and riots, intensified by the fatal shooting of [[renee-good|Renee Good]] by an ICE agent.

The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] responded by opening an investigation through its Civil Rights Division, working with the [[fbi|FBI]]. Assistant Attorney General [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]] said the incident could violate both the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]] and the FACE Act, stressing that disrupting religious services is not protected speech. Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] spoke directly with the affected pastor and said intimidation of Christians and attacks on law enforcement would meet the "full force of federal law." [[white-house|White House]] officials and religious leaders condemned the action as a desecration of a sacred space. Former CNN host [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] was alleged to have taken part and was placed "on notice" by the DOJ.
