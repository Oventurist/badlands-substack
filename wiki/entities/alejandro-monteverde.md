# Alejandro Monteverde

Alejandro Monteverde is a film director best known for directing the 2023 anti-child-trafficking film [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]], which was made on a $14 million budget and starred [[jim-caviezel|Jim Caviezel]] as [[tim-ballard|Tim Ballard]].[1] The film became the 16th highest-grossing movie of 2023 at the time it passed the $100 million mark, and according to [[variety|Variety]] was the first independent film to reach that threshold at the North American box office since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic.[1]
