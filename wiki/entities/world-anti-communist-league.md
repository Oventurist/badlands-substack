# World Anti-Communist League

The World Anti-Communist League was one of the postwar vehicles through which, in [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account, the fascist sabotage networks of the interwar and wartime years were rehabilitated and redeployed after 1945.[1]

Ehret writes that the truths about the fifth columnists and occult underground seeking a new "thousand year reich" were not permitted to come to light after World War II, particularly following the death of [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]] on 12 April 1945 and the subsequent deaths of his strongest allies Harry Hopkins, [[harry-dexter-white|Harry Dexter White]] and Secretary of Defense [[james-forrestal|James Forrestal]], whose career ended when he went out of a window in 1949.[1] Without missing a beat, the networks that had sought an Anglo-Nazi special relationship to conquer the world reappeared in the form of the World Anti-Communist League and [[nato|NATO]]'s secret armies, codenamed [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]].[1] Ehret notes the irony that the rebranding of postwar fascism took the form of a movement that came to be called neoconservatism, steered by "former" Trotskyists and Satanists committed to reorganizing the world under a new managerial elite.[1]
