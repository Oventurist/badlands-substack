# Edward Kennedy

Edward "Ted" Kennedy was a United States senator and the surviving brother of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]]. In the lead-up to the 1980 elections he sought the presidency, and his chief of staff was [[allard-lowenstein|Allard Lowenstein]].[1]

According to [[paul-fitzgerald|Paul Fitzgerald]]'s account in [[valediction-three-nights-of-desmond|Valediction: Three Nights of Desmond]], Lowenstein described in a brief exchange his and Kennedy's intention to shed light on the [[cia|CIA]]'s involvement in the murder of the two Kennedy brothers.[1] Lowenstein was shot dead in his office two weeks later.[1]

The article notes drily, in a closing footnote, that based on his policy performance from that point forward one can only assume Ted Kennedy finally learned his lesson and decided it was infinitely easier to become a creature of the system.[1]
