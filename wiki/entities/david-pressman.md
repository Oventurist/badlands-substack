# David Pressman

David Pressman is the United States ambassador to [[hungary|Hungary]], described in reporting as a gay activist and a donor to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Carlson criticism

Pressman drew sharp criticism after he publicly lectured the Hungarian government over LGBTQ rights in remarks at a U.S. Embassy family pride event.[1] Speaking in Hungary in August 2023, [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] apologized to Hungarians on behalf of the United States, saying that "the behavior of the American ambassador to Hungary makes me want to apologize" and calling it "disgusting and inexcusable."[1] Carlson argued that "the point of diplomacy is not to hector other nations for its own sake" or "to show up in someone else's country and scream at them because they're different from you."[1] He characterized Pressman as "not a diplomat" but "a political activist and Biden donor" whose conduct "hurts the United States."[1]
