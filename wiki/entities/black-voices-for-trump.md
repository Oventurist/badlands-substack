# Black Voices for Trump

**Black Voices for Trump** was an outreach organization associated with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s political operation, aimed at building support for Trump among Black American voters.[1]

Its former executive director, [[harrison-floyd|Harrison Floyd]], is credited with expanding Trump's support among Black voters to unprecedented levels in the 2020 election.[1] Floyd was subsequently named as a defendant in District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]]'s [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] criminal proceeding against Trump and co-defendants — an involvement that, according to Floyd, originated in a mainstream news article that misrepresented his role in Georgia's elections.[1]
