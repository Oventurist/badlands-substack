# Safra Catz

Safra Catz is an Israeli-born American business executive best known for her long tenure in the senior leadership of Oracle Corporation, where she served as chief executive officer and previously as president and chief financial officer. She is regarded as one of the most prominent women in the American enterprise software industry.

In March 2026 Catz was named among the appointees to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], as reported in the March 26 [[badlands-brief-143|Badlands Brief]]. Her appointment placed her alongside Oracle co-founder [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]] on the same advisory council.
