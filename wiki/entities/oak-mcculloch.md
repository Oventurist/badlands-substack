# Oak McCulloch

Lt. Colonel Oak McCulloch is a retired U.S. Army officer and speaker associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] programming and its GART live events.[1]

## Commentary on borders

McCulloch has stated on many occasions, both at GART events and on stream, that changing the national boundaries on a map is a bloody endeavor.[1] The remark was cited by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] discussion of [[colonial-borders-in-africa|colonial borders in Africa]], where it framed the question of whether boundaries could ever be redrawn without war.[1]
