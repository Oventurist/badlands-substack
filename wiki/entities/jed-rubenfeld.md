# Jed Rubenfeld

Jed Rubenfeld is a professor at [[yale-law-school|Yale Law School]] whose field is constitutional law and First Amendment rights.[1] He is married to fellow Yale professor [[amy-chua|Amy Chua]], who mentored [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and introduced him to [[usha-vance|Usha Vance]].[1]

Rubenfeld has been publicly critical of the guilty verdict returned against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], describing it as dangerous and unconstitutional.[1] Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites this, along with the couple's daughter having clerked for Justice [[brett-kavanaugh|Brett Kavanaugh]], as part of a pattern of pre-political ties linking Vance's academic circle to Trump.[1]
