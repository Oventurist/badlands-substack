# Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers

Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers is a United States District Judge who presided over [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s lawsuit against [[openai|OpenAI]] and its chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]].[1]

After a federal jury rejected Musk's claims following less than two hours of deliberation, Gonzalez Rogers adopted the advisory jury's finding that Altman and OpenAI were not liable.[1] She additionally ruled that Musk's claims of breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment were dismissed as untimely, and dismissed the parallel aiding-and-abetting claims against [[microsoft|Microsoft]], an early OpenAI investor beginning in 2019.[1]
