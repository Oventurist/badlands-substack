# Citizen Lab

Citizen Lab is a digital watchdog and research group based at Canada's University of Toronto.[1] It investigates digital espionage, censorship and influence operations, and publishes technical reports on state-linked information campaigns.[1]

## Paperwall report

In a report dated February 7, 2024, Citizen Lab documented at least 123 Chinese websites disguised as local news outlets across 30 countries, disseminating pro-Beijing disinformation.[1] Senior researcher Alberto Fittarelli, who authored the report, named the campaign [[paperwall|Paperwall]] and described it as "a large, and fast growing, network of anonymous websites posing as local news outlets," calling it "an example of a sprawling influence operation serving both financial and political interests, and in alignment with Beijing's political agenda."[1]

The report identified the United States, South Korea, Japan, Russia, the UK, France, Brazil, Turkey and Italy among the targeted countries, and catalogued site names that borrowed local references — such as Eiffel Post and Provence Daily in French, British FT in the UK, Sendai Shimbum and Fujiyama Times in Japan, Daegu Journal and Busan Online in South Korea, and Roma Journal and Napoli Money in Italy.[1] The single site aimed at a US audience was UpdateNews.Info, a domain registered in July 2019 and the earliest Paperwall site on record.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] accepted the Citizen Lab findings but questioned their significance, noting that the report itself assessed the campaign's influence as small, and arguing that such comparatively unsophisticated operations may serve to draw attention away from more advanced Chinese activity consistent with the doctrine set out in [[unrestricted-warfare|Unrestricted Warfare]].[1]
