# Assembly of Experts

The **Assembly of Experts** is the Iranian constitutional body of clerics responsible for appointing and supervising the country's supreme leader.[1] Under Iran's constitution, selection of the supreme leader falls exclusively to this body rather than to the executive or the electorate.[1]

## Role in the 2026 succession

The Assembly's function became internationally salient after the death of supreme leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] in late February 2026 during Israeli military strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities.[1] The clerical leadership began the process of selecting a successor while Iran remained under military and diplomatic pressure.[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly asserted that the United States should be involved in determining who leads Iran, stating "I have to be involved in the appointment, like with Delcy [Rodríguez] in Venezuela" and dismissing Khamenei's son as "a lightweight."[1] Because the constitution vests the choice in the Assembly of Experts, Trump's stated intention placed the American position in direct tension with Iran's formal succession machinery.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the episode through the [[venezuela-model|Venezuela Model]], arguing that the objective was removal of a single lightning-rod figure while preserving core governing structures — an outcome requiring that the successor emerge from internal dynamics, including the Assembly's own deliberations, rather than by overt external imposition.[1]
