# Assembly of Experts

The Assembly of Experts is the body of clerics that, under [[iran|Iran]]'s constitution, is responsible for choosing, appointing, and supervising the country's supreme leader.[1]

## 2026 succession process

The Assembly's role became internationally prominent after the death of supreme leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] in late February 2026 during Israeli military strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities.[1] Iran's clerical leadership continued the process of selecting a successor through early March 2026.[1]

The process drew unusual external commentary when US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he believed the United States should be involved in determining who leads Iran, telling Axios: "We want someone that will bring harmony and peace to Iran... Khamenei's son is a lightweight. I have to be involved in the appointment, like with Delcy [Rodríguez] in Venezuela."[1] Badlands analyst [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read those remarks as codification of the [[venezuela-model|Venezuela Model]] — removing a lightning-rod figure while preserving core governing structures under a pragmatic, US-aligned hand — rather than the fracturing regime change sought by neoconservative and globalist actors.[1]

## Succession context after Raisi's death (2024)

The Assembly of Experts is the Iranian body responsible for selecting the Supreme Leader. Following the May 2024 helicopter crash that killed President [[ebrahim-raisi|Ebrahim Raisi]], Badlands commentary observed that Raisi had been widely expected to be chosen as the next Supreme Leader by the Assembly, with the sitting [[ali-khamenei|Ayatollah Ali Khamenei]] described as effectively in hospice care.[2]

Raisi had not always been the front-runner: his predecessor, former President [[hassan-rouhani|Hassan Rouhani]], was once the odds-on favorite for selection by the Assembly, until Iranian courts officially disqualified him from consideration in January 2024.[2] Raisi's death was therefore expected to substantially change the political landscape of Iran.[2]
