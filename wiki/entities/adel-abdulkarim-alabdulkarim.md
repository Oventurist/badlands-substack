# Adel Abdulkarim Alabdulkarim

Adel Abdulkarim Alabdulkarim is a British-Saudi film executive who owns [[volant-media|Volant Media]], the UK-based parent company of the Persian-language broadcaster [[iran-international|Iran International]].[1]

A 2018 report by the Financial Times of London accused Alabdulkarim of receiving funding from [[israel|Israel]] for the broadcaster; he denied the accusation.[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described Alabdulkarim as a Saudi royal holding British citizenship, and treated that profile — combined with Iran International's direct satellite penetration of the Iranian domestic television market — as evidence that the outlet operates as a state-aligned information operation rather than an independent newsroom.[1]
