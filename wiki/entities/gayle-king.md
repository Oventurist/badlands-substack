# Gayle King

Gayle King is an American broadcast journalist and CBS morning show host.[1]

## Blue Origin spaceflight

In April 2025, King was among the six passengers on the all-female [[blue-origin|Blue Origin]] suborbital flight, riding alongside singer [[katy-perry|Katy Perry]], former NASA engineer Aisha Bowe, astronaut Amanda Nguyễn, film producer Kerianne Flynn, and Lauren Sánchez, fiancée of Jeff Bezos.[1] The flight drew intense backlash over its brevity and celebrity character, and was mocked publicly by podcaster [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]].[1]
