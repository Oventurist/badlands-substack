# Gavin Wilde

Gavin Wilde is a former United States intelligence official and think tank analyst who, according to reporting summarized in the [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]], has emerged as a newly disclosed witness before congressional investigators examining how assessments of Russian election interference were developed and communicated.

## Intelligence career

Just the News reported that Wilde, who works at think tanks in Washington, co-authored the January 2017 [[2017-intelligence-community-assessment|Intelligence Community Assessment]] on Russian meddling in the 2016 election, and that he subsequently served on the Trump National Security Council in 2018 and 2019. He has also said he worked on United States intelligence assessments of alleged Russian influence efforts ahead of the 2020 election. The assessment he helped write is described by the Brief as having been produced at the direction of then-President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and overseen by FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]], CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], and Director of National Intelligence [[james-clapper|James Clapper]].

## Role in the Ukraine impeachment

Wilde was previously identified only as "Witness 2" in the documentary record of the [[first-trump-impeachment|Ukraine impeachment]] of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. According to the reporting quoted in the Brief, "the 2019 claims by Witness 2 were critical in helping Intelligence Community Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]] push Ukraine whistleblower [[eric-ciaramella|Eric Ciaramella]]'s complaint forward, and Wilde's 'Russiagate'-linked biases were concealed from House investigators during the impeachment process."

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the disclosure as significant, arguing that a figure who was "hands on keyboard in multiple overt acts" across both [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] and the impeachment has apparently become a cooperating government witness, and speculating that his cooperation likely predates its public disclosure. She linked the concealment of his biases from House investigators to the pattern of obstruction described by [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] in *Government Gangsters*.
