# Carla Hayden

Dr. Carla Hayden is an American librarian who served as Librarian of Congress for nearly a decade before being fired by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in May 2025.[1] She was the first woman and the first Black person to hold the Senate-confirmed post at the [[library-of-congress|Library of Congress]].[1]

Her dismissal, first reported by the Associated Press, sparked substantial backlash online.[1] Coverage placed the firing within the broader pattern of Trump's second term, in which the administration used executive orders and the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] to remove federal employees and programs it viewed as hostile to its agenda.[1] Reporting also linked the action to the administration's resistance to diversity, equity and inclusion initiatives, noting that thousands of government web pages highlighting the service of women, LGBTQ+ people, and people of color had been deleted.[1] Newsweek reported that it had contacted the Library of Congress for comment.[1]
