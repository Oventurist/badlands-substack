# Ernst Rudin

**Ernst Rudin** was the Swiss psychiatrist described by Badlands Media as a fascist and as the Rockefellers' chief executive of the two Kaiser Wilhelm Institutes in Germany — the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-psychiatry|Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Psychiatry]] and the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-anthropology-eugenics-and-human-heredity|Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Anthropology, Eugenics, and Human Heredity]], both funded by the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]].[1] He was assisted by his protégés [[otmar-verschuer|Otmar Verschuer]] and [[franz-kallmann|Franz J. Kallmann]].[1]

Rudin and his staff, working as part of the "Task Force of Heredity Experts" chaired by SS chief [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]], drafted Germany's sterilization law. Described as an "American model law," it was implemented in July 1933 and proudly reprinted in the United States in the September 1933 issue of *Eugenical News* bearing [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]]'s signature.[1]
