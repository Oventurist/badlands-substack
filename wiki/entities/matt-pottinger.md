# Matt Pottinger

Matt Pottinger is a former United States deputy national security adviser who served during the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and who was closely associated with the administration's China policy.[1]

## Briefing on the Chinese balloon program

In February 2023, following the shootdown of a Chinese high-altitude surveillance balloon over the United States, the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] briefed former Trump administration officials about the Chinese balloon program. Pottinger was among those briefed, along with former national security advisers [[robert-obrien|Robert O'Brien]] and [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], former Secretary of State [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]], and former Director of National Intelligence [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]].[1]

The briefings followed reporting that a small circle of intelligence officials at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] during the Trump years had monitored mysterious objects—now suspected to have been balloons—without escalating the matter to the [[white-house|White House]], because the nature of the objects was unclear at the time. Analysts at the time suspected the flights might be linked to China and to efforts by Beijing to test radar-jamming systems over sensitive U.S. military sites.[1]

Badlands Media commentary questioned why such information had not been passed up the chain of command during the Trump administration, and why the Biden administration considered it necessary years later to brief former officials such as Pottinger about incursions over Navy sites in Coronado, California; Norfolk, Virginia; and Guam.[1]
