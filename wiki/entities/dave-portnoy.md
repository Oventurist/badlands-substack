# Dave Portnoy

Dave Portnoy is a conservative-leaning social media personality and the founder of the media company Barstool Sports, described as a leader in the effort to destigmatize humor in the age of political correctness.[1]

## June 2025 podcast episode

On June 4th, Portnoy appeared on one of the Barstool podcasts and delivered what Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized as one of the most unhinged meltdowns he had witnessed on a podcast, driven by Portnoy's belief that jokes about American Jews — however light-hearted — would get Jews killed in the street.[1] DeLarme responded that he did not come away persuaded that Jews are so uniquely endangered that all jokes at their expense should be self-censored and socially proscribed.[1]

## Context in the double-standard argument

The episode is presented as evidence that the anti-[[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] "manosphere"-adjacent comedy world carved out a single protected exception.[1] DeLarme observes that jokes remain permissible about gays, trans people, Blacks, Hispanics, the homeless, drug addicts, the mentally challenged, amputees and dead Gazans, while only American Jews — specifically Zionist Jews — are treated as beyond ridicule, with Jews who do not support Israel dismissed as "self-hating."[1]
