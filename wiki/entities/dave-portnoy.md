# Dave Portnoy

Dave Portnoy is a conservative social media personality and the founder of the media company [[barstool-sports|Barstool Sports]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] credits him with leading efforts to destigmatize humor in the age of political correctness, while arguing that Portnoy exempts one category of humor from that principle.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

On June 4 — of 2025, in the run-up to the essay's publication — Portnoy appeared on a Barstool podcast and had what DeLarme calls "one of the most loony, unhinged meltdowns I've ever witnessed on a podcast," driven by his perception that jokes about American Jews, however light-hearted, will get them killed in the street.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)

DeLarme says the episode did not persuade him that Jews are so exclusively endangered that all jokes at their expense should be self-censored and socially proscribed, observing that jokes remain permissible about gays, trans people, Blacks, Hispanics, the homeless, drug addicts, the mentally challenged, amputees, and dead Gazans.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard) The Portnoy segment functions in the essay as the comedic counterpart to the campus [[safe-spaces|safe space]] inversion and to the broader elasticity of [[antisemitism]] as a category.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-glaring-double-standard)
