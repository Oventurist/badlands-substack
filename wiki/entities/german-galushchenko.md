# German Galushchenko

German Galushchenko is a Ukrainian official who served as Justice Minister and previously oversaw the energy portfolio.[1]

In November 2025, Ukraine's [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau]] and the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office uncovered a $100 million corruption scheme involving the country's state-owned nuclear energy operator.[1] The scandal led to the resignations of Galushchenko, Energy Minister [[svetlana-grinchuk|Svetlana Grinchuk]] and President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s chief of staff [[andriy-yermak|Andrey Yermak]].[1]

Ukrainian media reported in mid-February 2026 that Galushchenko had been detained while attempting to flee to Poland.[1]

## April 2024 grid attacks

In a Facebook post following Russia's April 11, 2024 missile and drone offensive, Energy Minister Galushchenko wrote that power plants near Kyiv, in the Kharkiv region in the east, Zaporizhzhia in the south, and Lviv in the west had been damaged.[2] The same wave of strikes hit the [[trypilska-power-plant|Trypilska plant]] south of Kyiv, the largest in the region, and underground natural gas storage sites across [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[2]

## Energoatom background and 2025 dismissal

Badlands commentary in November 2025 noted that Galushchenko, dismissed as Ukraine's justice minister the previous week after being implicated in a major graft probe described as a "$100 million kickback scheme," had been Vice President of [[energoatom|Energoatom]] before joining the government in 2021.[3] The scandal coincided with claims by lawmaker Vladimir Ariev that Ukrainian officials embezzled millions through procurement filed in the name of the Russia-held [[zaporozhye-nuclear-power-plant|Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant]].[3] There were also calls for President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] to dismiss chief of staff [[andriy-yermak|Andrey Yermak]] over alleged ties to the same affair.[3]
