# Kansas City Chiefs

The Kansas City Chiefs are a professional American football franchise in the [[nfl|National Football League]]. The team's colors are red and black, a detail that became central to a November 2023 media controversy involving a young fan.[1]

## 2023 headdress and face-paint controversy

A child fan attended a Chiefs game wearing a Native American–style headdress with his face painted in the team's red and black colors. [[carron-j-phillips|Carron J. Phillips]] of [[deadspin|Deadspin]] wrote an article accusing the boy of wearing "black face" and calling on the NFL to speak out against him, using a cropped image that hid the red-painted half of the child's face.[1]

The story unraveled when reporting indicated the boy was Native American and his father a tribal board member, and critics noted that the black and red paint simply matched Chiefs team colors rather than signaling any racial caricature.[1] The episode was cited in [[badlands-media|Badlands]] commentary as an example of manufactured racism narratives in sports journalism.[1]
