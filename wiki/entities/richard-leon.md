# Richard Leon

Richard Leon is a United States district judge on the US District Court for the District of Columbia.[1]

## Kelly retirement-benefits case

In early February 2026, Leon questioned [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] lawyers about the legal precedent underpinning the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s attempt to demote Senator [[mark-kelly|Mark Kelly]] and reduce his retirement benefits over the [[seditious-six|Seditious Six]] scandal.[1] Leon pressed the government to identify the Supreme Court or D.C. Circuit authority that would support punishing off-duty speech.[1]
