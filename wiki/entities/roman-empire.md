# Roman Empire

Rome is used in the February 24, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "The Steering Wheel of Civilization" as the principal historical case study in how a large, diverse civilization sustains a [[common-purpose|common purpose]].[1]

## Roman identity as common purpose

The essay states that during its height, both before and after the empire, Rome had a common purpose despite being a melting pot of different cultures, classes and socio-religious groups.[1] A Roman identity was grafted onto all of these groups in some fashion, producing what the author calls one of the most successful civilizations on earth — at least in terms of coordinating the group actions of individual member-citizens.[1]

## Mechanisms of cohesion

Rome is also presented as an example of a civilization tending toward tyranny, which uses force, deception and coercion to compel participation.[1] The Romans are described as having used conquest, violence and slavery, methods that in turn required a socio-religious foundation providing spiritual justification, along with a cultural reinforcement mechanism ensuring that all layers of society played their part.[1] The [[mystery-schools|mystery schools]] and the metropolitan lifestyle of a well-functioning civilization are identified as the institutions that helped foster this Roman identity.[1]
