# John Maynard Keynes

John Maynard Keynes was the British economist who, in Matthew Ehret's account, served at the 1944 [[bretton-woods-conference|Bretton Woods conference]] as the defender of the imperial financial order against the anti-colonial program of [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]].[1]

Ehret identifies Keynes as a [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] leader and a devout eugenicist.[1] While [[harry-dexter-white|Harry Dexter White]] fought to dissolve the [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]] and defended FDR's plan for a new anti-imperial system of finance, Keynes defended the Bank and pushed instead to redefine the post-war system around a one-world currency called the Bancor, to be controlled by the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]] and the BIS.[1]
