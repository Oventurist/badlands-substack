# Bill Foster

Bill Foster is a Democratic U.S. Representative from Illinois who joined Representative [[sean-casten|Sean Casten]] and food bank leaders at a July 2025 event in Geneva, Illinois, to criticize federal changes to the [[supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program|SNAP]] program.[1]

## Criticism of the budget bill

Foster called the [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|"Big Beautiful Bill Act"]] "devastating," saying that Illinois is "not exempt from the damage here."[1] The law, signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on July 4, 2025, extended $4.5 trillion in tax breaks first enacted in 2017 and altered SNAP eligibility by raising the working-age requirement from 55 to 65, with limited exceptions.[1]
