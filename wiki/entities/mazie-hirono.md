# Mazie Hirono

Mazie Hirono is a Democratic United States senator from Hawaii and a member of the Senate Judiciary Committee, where she has been a persistent questioner of Republican executive-branch and judicial nominees.[1]

## Bondi Confirmation Hearing (January 2025)

Badlands commentary on the January 15, 2025 confirmation hearing for attorney general nominee [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] listed Hirono, alongside [[dick-durbin|Dick Durbin]] and [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]], among the senators who repeatedly pressed Bondi to say whether Joe Biden won the 2020 election.[1] According to that account, the senators reclaimed their time rather than allow Bondi to describe what she said she had seen in Pennsylvania, using the exchange to register how shocking they found her refusal to affirm the 2020 result.[1]
