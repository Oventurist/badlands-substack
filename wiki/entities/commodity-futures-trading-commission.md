# Commodity Futures Trading Commission

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) is the United States federal agency responsible for regulating derivatives markets, including futures contracts. It enters the Badlands Media corpus through [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s article [[are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom|"Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?"]], which emphasizes that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] installed a committed [[blockchain]] advocate at its head.

That appointee was [[j-christopher-giancarlo|J. Christopher Giancarlo]], who served as acting chairman from Trump's inauguration on January 20, 2017, was nominated as full-time chairman on March 14, 2017, and was confirmed by the U.S. Senate on August 3, 2017. Under CFTC oversight the [[bitcoin]] futures market launched in December 2017, and Giancarlo told CNBC that throughout 2018 he routinely briefed Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]] on the operation and supervision of that market.

Lange draws a pointed conclusion from the appointment: Trump placed at the head of the commodities regulator "the very guy who has been pushing hard for a CBDC," years before the global rush toward [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]] began. The agency's role in the article is therefore less regulatory than emblematic — evidence, in the author's reading, that the American digital currency effort was seeded deliberately from within the Trump administration.
