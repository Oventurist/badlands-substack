# Karl Wolff

**General Karl Wolff** was a German intelligence officer of the Third Reich named by Colonel [[roxane-watkins|Roxane Watkins]], alongside General [[reinhard-gehlen|Reinhard Gehlen]], as one of the officers [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] used to establish his [[stay-behind-units|stay-behind]] network across Axis-held territory.[1]

## Role in the werewolf network

The cells Wolff and Gehlen organized were known as [[werewolf-units|werewolf units]] because their members were citizens by day and killers by night.[1] Each unit numbered roughly 10 to 15 people with a primary and secondary leader, and the command structure — Gehlen and Wolff — was told only the identities of those two leaders, never the whole cell, so that deniability could be maintained.[1] The weapons caches supporting the units held guns, ammunition, explosives, communications equipment and money.[1]

Watkins treats this network as the seedbed of what later became generically known as [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]], with training conducted in part by SS officer [[otto-skorzeny|Otto Skorzeny]].[1]
