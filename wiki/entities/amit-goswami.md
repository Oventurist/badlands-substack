# Amit Goswami

**Amit Goswami** is a theorist of consciousness quoted in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]].[1]

The essay cites his statement that "consciousness is the ground of all being" in its section on awareness as a weapon within [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]], pairing it with a quotation from [[joe-dispenza|Joe Dispenza]] to argue that by elevating awareness individuals tap into a profound potential to transform both themselves and their communities.[1] In the author's framing, this awakened state shatters illusions imposed by the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and empowers people to reclaim agency and reshape reality in alignment with liberation.[1]

The same quotation reappears in "Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field," the fifth installment of the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series published on August 9, 2025 by the same author, [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[2] There Goswami's line is used to argue that if consciousness is indeed the ground of all being, then every moment of coherence is not merely healing but an act of creation, supporting the article's claim that individuals who hold coherence become "tuning forks" within a [[resonance-field|resonance field]].[2]

## Cited in Structures of Control

Part 3 of Badlands Media's *Structures of Control* series cites Amit Goswami alongside [[walter-russell|Walter Russell]] for the claim that consciousness actively shapes reality rather than passively observing it.[3] The essay builds on this to assert that resonance amplifies truth, creating ripples of awakening that dismantle the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s manipulative structures, and that individuals aligned with such frequencies become "beacons of light" (see [[authentic-5d|authentic 5D]]).[3]
