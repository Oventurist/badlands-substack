# Sports Illustrated

*Sports Illustrated* is an American sports magazine, long one of the best-known titles in the U.S. periodical market.

## Collapse in early 2024

A Swan Song for the MSM opens by asserting that for some media organizations "the death certificates have already been signed," and links to CBS News reporting that the magazine's union announced layoffs of all or most of its staff after Authentic Brands Group, the owner of the trademark, was not paid its quarterly license fee. The article lists Sports Illustrated alongside the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]], [[time-magazine|Time Magazine]], [[nbc-news|NBC News]], [[national-geographic|National Geographic]] and [[business-insider|Business Insider]] among outlets that had suffered significant workforce reductions or been pushed to the verge of extinction. In the TikTok address quoted in the article, [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] states that Sports Illustrated "basically shut down last week."
