# Sports Illustrated

Sports Illustrated is an American sports magazine whose near-total collapse in January 2024 is used by Badlands Media as the opening example of the mainstream media's demise — a case in which "the death certificates have already been signed."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

The magazine's publisher laid off all or most of its staff after the brand's owner, Authentic, was not paid its quarterly license fee, effectively shutting the operation down.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm) [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] described it plainly in her February 2024 video: "Sports Illustrated basically shut down last week."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

Badlands places the collapse alongside cuts at the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]], [[time-magazine|Time Magazine]], NBC News, National Geographic and Business Insider, arguing that "there are few industries more worthy of such a gruesome and undignified demise as modern mainstream journalism."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)
