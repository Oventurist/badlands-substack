# Anthony St John

**Lord Anthony St John** is a British hereditary peer, merchant banker and director of multiple mining companies across South Africa, identified by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a patron of [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]].[1]

St John serves as a vice chair of the All Party Parliamentary South Africa Group in the House of Lords and is a leading patron of Television for the Environment, a project created in 1984 by the [[united-nations-environment-programme|United Nations Environment Programme]] and the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]]-UK.[1]

He also sits on the advisory board of "Successful Green" alongside Peter Merian, a director of the Basel Stock Exchange, and Marcelo Cavalho de Andrade, president of the Earth Council Alliance — a body created by [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]] in 1992.[1] Ehret cites this cluster of affiliations as illustrating the overlap between establishment finance, hereditary aristocracy and ostensibly radical climate activism.[1]
