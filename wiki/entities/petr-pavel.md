# Petr Pavel

Petr Pavel is the President of the Czech Republic, a retired general and former chairman of [[nato|NATO]]'s military committee.[1]

Speaking around the IISS Prague Defence Summit in November 2024, Pavel assessed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s pledge to end the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] "within 24 hours" as unrealistic, while allowing that Trump would make a genuine effort: "I don't think it is realistic, but at the same time I believe that he will make an effort to end this war and to make a deal with President [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]." He added that such a deal would "most probably not be in our interest and in the interest of Ukraine."[1]
