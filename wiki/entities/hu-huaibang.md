# Hu Huaibang

Hu Huaibang is a former Chinese banking executive who served as party secretary and chairman of the [[china-development-bank|China Development Bank]] and who was prosecuted as part of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s anti-corruption campaign.[1]

## Corruption case

According to reporting by Free Malaysia Today cited in Badlands Media's "The Master Builder," Hu pleaded guilty in July 2020 to illegally receiving more than $12 million, having been caught the previous year in what the outlet described as Xi Jinping's sweeping campaign against corruption.[1] A court in the northern city of Chengde heard that Hu had used his positions to illegally receive money and goods totaling 85.5 million yuan — roughly $12.2 million — between 2009 and 2019.[1] The same report noted that more than one million officials had been punished in Xi's crackdown, although critics argued the campaign also served to purge Xi's political rivals.[1]

## Significance in Badlands Media commentary

Joe Lange's article presents the Hu Huaibang case, alongside that of [[lai-xiaomin|Lai Xiaomin]] of [[china-huarong-asset-management|China Huarong Asset Management]], as evidence that Xi has been waging a genuine internal war against corrupt elements of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] — an argument used to support the broader thesis that Xi is confronting his own version of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] and cooperating with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] against the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1] The piece observes that such arrests have continued for many years with comparatively little coverage in Western media.[1]
