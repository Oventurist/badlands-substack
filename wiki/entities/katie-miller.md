# Katie Miller

Katie Miller is an American media figure and political spouse who hosts a podcast that has become a venue for interviews with senior Trump administration officials. She is the wife of White House aide [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]].[1]

## Bondi hate speech interview

In September 2025, Miller's podcast produced one of the more consequential interviews of the period when Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] appeared as a guest. Miller asked Bondi directly whether federal law enforcement would be deployed against groups engaged in "hate speech" in the aftermath of the killing of conservative political organizer [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1] Bondi replied that the Justice Department would "absolutely target you, go after you if you are targeting anyone with hate speech — and that's across the aisle," adding that there was no place for hate speech following Kirk's death.[1]

The exchange circulated rapidly online and triggered a broad backlash from across the political spectrum, including from figures who invoked Kirk's own past insistence that hate speech has no legal existence in the United States and that even ugly or evil speech is protected by the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1] Bondi backpedaled from the remarks the following day.[1] Because the comments were made on Miller's program, her podcast became the documentary record at the center of the controversy.[1]
