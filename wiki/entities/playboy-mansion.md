# Playboy Mansion

The **Playboy Mansion** is the Los Angeles estate associated with the Playboy publishing empire, referenced by Badlands Media as "yet another operation linked to elite child abuse."[1]

## Proximity to the Combs property

The mansion features in coverage of the allegations against [[sean-combs|Sean "P. Diddy" Combs]], whose property — reported to contain an underground tunnel — sits next door to it.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] cited this adjacency alongside Combs's alleged hundreds of hidden cameras and the underground tunnels attributed to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] as a cluster of parallels running through cases of alleged elite exploitation of minors.[1]
