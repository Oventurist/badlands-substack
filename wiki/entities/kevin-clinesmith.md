# Kevin Clinesmith

Kevin Clinesmith is a former FBI lawyer whose criminal case, arising out of the Russiagate investigations, was presided over by Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]] of the D.C. District Court.[1]

## Significance in the Badlands narrative

Badlands Media commentary invoked the Clinesmith case in March 2025 to argue that Boasberg's record demonstrated political bias. In coverage of the House Republican effort to impeach Boasberg over his ruling blocking deportation flights, Ashe in America noted that Boasberg was "the judge that presided over Kevin Clinesmith's case in #RussiaGate" and, separately, over the case of [[ray-epps|Ray Epps]] arising from January 6.[1] Reporting cited in the brief characterized the outcome of the Clinesmith matter as a "slap on the wrist" for the FBI lawyer.[1]

The pairing of the Clinesmith and Epps cases was used to support the argument that Boasberg is "a political animal" whose "bias is on the record," even as the Badlands take stressed that the legal basis of the impeachment rested on the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]] and presidential authority rather than on judicial bias alone.[1]
