# Otto Rahn

Otto Rahn was a German Grail researcher and Wagner enthusiast hired by [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] to lead the expedition in search of the Holy Grail — the cup from which Jesus was said to have drunk at the Last Supper, promising eternal life to whoever drank from it.[1]

Returning from excavations in Palestine that followed an earlier search of the Cathar knight castle of Montségur, Rahn composed a report for Himmler and the [[new-templar-order|New Templars]] titled *Lucifer's Court*.[1] The report identified the [[cathars|Cathars]] as a "pure" Christian sect that had practiced Luciferianism before being wiped out by the Catholic Church during the Albigensian Crusade of 1209.[1]

Rahn wrote: "The ancient god of love is also the lord of Spring as personified in the Greek myth of Apollo, who brought back the light from the sun, he is a light-bearer, or 'Lucifer'. According to the Apocalypse of John, Apollo was identified with the Devil… There is much more light in the world than in the houses of God — cathedrals and churches — where Lucifer is neither able nor desirous of entering because of the sombre stained-glass windows on which the Jewish prophets and apostles, the Roman [catholic] gods and saints are depicted."[1]
