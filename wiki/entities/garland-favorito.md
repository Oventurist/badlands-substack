# Garland Favorito

Garland Favorito is a Georgia lobbyist and election-integrity advocate.[1]

Favorito appeared as a supporter of [[georgia-house-bill-397|House Bill 397]] during the Georgia Senate Ethics Committee's late-night March 2025 hearing on the measure, which would require an election night hand count among other changes to state election administration.[1] Summarizing his case for the bill, Favorito said: "If the elections aren't right, what else do we have? We rely on the elections to elect the right people, to make the right decisions."[1]
