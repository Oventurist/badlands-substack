# Kris Jenner

Kris Jenner is an American television personality and matriarch of the Kardashian–Jenner family.[1]

## Connection to the O.J. Simpson case

Jenner was married to [[caitlyn-jenner|Caitlyn Jenner]] at the time of the June 1994 killings of [[nicole-brown-simpson|Nicole Brown Simpson]] and [[ronald-goldman|Ronald Goldman]], and was reportedly best friends with Nicole Brown Simpson.[1] Her former husband, [[robert-kardashian|Robert Kardashian]], was part of [[oj-simpson|O.J. Simpson]]'s criminal defense team, a circumstance Badlands Media commentary described as placing the Jenners and Kardashians "kind of on both sides of the OJ saga."[1]

Following Simpson's death from prostate cancer in April 2024, Caitlyn Jenner responded publicly with "Good Riddance," while Kris Jenner remained silent about the killing on social media.[1] Commentary also noted the broader observation that the Simpson story is tightly connected to the later Kardashian media phenomenon.[1]
