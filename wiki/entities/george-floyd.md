# George Floyd

George Floyd was the man whose 2020 death in police custody triggered nationwide Black Lives Matter protests in the United States.[1]

## Treatment in Badlands commentary

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describes the Floyd incident as "very Rodney King-like," producing a similar reaction: BLM protests that led to violence, destruction, and looting.[1] He notes that because the event is close to the present and because many people now question what they see, many readers believe the killing was completely staged or at least that something about it is not right.[1]

## Placement in the alleged riot cycle

Carlson uses Floyd as the most recent entry in an argued thirty-year cycle of manufactured racial unrest, preceded by the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] beating of 1991 and the [[la-riots-1992|1992 Los Angeles riots]], the [[watts-riots-1965|Watts riots of 1965]], and the [[harlem-riots-1935|Harlem riots of 1935]].[1] In his framework the cycle is maintained by the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] to prevent racism from fading from American life.[1]
