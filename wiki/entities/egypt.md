# Egypt

Egypt is an Arab republic and a leading state in Middle Eastern diplomacy. It appears in the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]] as one of the governments that publicly condemned remarks by US Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]].

## Response to the Huckabee remarks

After Huckabee told [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] that [[israel|Israel]] holds a biblical right to territory "from the Nile to the Euphrates" — a formulation that places Egyptian territory within the claimed boundary — Arab and Muslim governments issued condemnations over the weekend. Egypt is listed among them alongside [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and [[jordan|Jordan]], together with regional bodies including the [[arab-league|League of Arab States]] and the [[organization-of-islamic-cooperation|Organization of Islamic Cooperation]]. The Brief presents the breadth of this reaction, and the co-signing of the Saudi statement by states across multiple international blocs, as evidence that the remarks unified the Arab and Muslim world in opposition to the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel]] concept.
