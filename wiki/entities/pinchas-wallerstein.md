# Pinchas Wallerstein

**Pinchas Wallerstein** is an Israeli religious Zionist figure described in the Badlands corpus as a founder of the modern Israeli Settler Movement. His April 2026 interview with [[haaretz|Haaretz]] — the oldest operating newspaper in Israel — is cited in the Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026 as authoritative testimony that the movement he helped build has degenerated into what he himself calls terrorism.

## The Haaretz interview

According to the Brief, Wallerstein told Haaretz that he is "horrified" by the rise of Jewish terrorism in the region, labelling it "treason" against the State of Israel. The interview is invoked in the context of a wave of incidents — the destruction of a statue of Jesus in the Christian village of Debl in southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]], the reported destruction of the [[tomb-of-saint-peter|Tomb of Saint Peter]] "by accident," and the conduct of [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldiers and settlers documented across social media — which the Brief argues "can only be defined as terrorism."

## Significance in the corpus

Wallerstein's biography gives his condemnation particular weight in the Badlands argument: a founder of the settler enterprise itself denouncing its current trajectory as treason is presented as evidence that the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel project]] has escaped the control of its original architects. The Brief uses his words to frame the escalating abuses as a systemic cultural disease rather than isolated misconduct.
