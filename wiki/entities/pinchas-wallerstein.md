# Pinchas Wallerstein

Pinchas Wallerstein is a founder of the modern [[israeli-settler-movement|Israeli Settler Movement]].[1]

In an interview published in April 2026 by [[haaretz|Haaretz]] — the oldest operating newspaper in [[israel|Israel]] — Wallerstein said he was horrified by the rise of [[jewish-terrorism|Jewish terrorism]] in the region, labelling it "treason" against the State of Israel.[1] Badlands cited the remarks as evidence that criticism of settler and [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] violence was emerging from within the movement's own founding generation.[1]
