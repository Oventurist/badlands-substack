# Supreme Court of Russia

The Supreme Court of Russia is the Russian Federation's highest court of general jurisdiction. In November 2023 it issued a landmark ruling declaring what it termed the "international LGBTQ movement" an extremist organization and banning all activities associated with it inside the country.[1]

## The 2023 extremism ruling

The decision, handed down on Thursday, November 30, 2023, was expected to further erode the position of Russia's LGBTQ community, which had faced an intensifying crackdown in preceding years as President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] sought to shore up his image as a defender of traditional moral values against the liberal West.[1]

The court found in favour of a motion filed by the [[ministry-of-justice-russia|Ministry of Justice]], which argued that the LGBTQ community risked "inciting social and religious discord" in violation of Russia's Law on Countering Extremism — a characterization drawn from a United Nations statement condemning the decision.[1] Although no legally recognized LGBTQ community exists in Russia under the country's anti-gay legislation, the ruling stated: "The claims are to be satisfied: to recognize the international LGBT movement as an extremist organization and to prohibit its activities in Russia," according to state news agency RIA Novosti.[1]

The four-hour hearing was held behind closed doors with only the Justice Ministry present, and the case materials were classified. RIA Novosti reported that the decision took effect immediately.[1] The ruling was reported by [[cnn|CNN]].[1]
