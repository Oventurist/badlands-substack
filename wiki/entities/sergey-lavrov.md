# Sergey Lavrov

Sergey Lavrov is the Foreign Minister of the Russian Federation and the principal diplomatic voice of [[russia|Russia]] in its dealings with Western governments and with partners such as [[iran|Iran]].

## Caspian Sea incident

The July 27, 2026 [[badlands-brief-245|Badlands Brief]] reports that Iranian Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] raised the reported Ukrainian strike on an Iranian commercial vessel in the [[caspian-sea|Caspian Sea]] with Lavrov, as well as with European Union foreign policy chief [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]]. Araghchi described the strike, which killed one sailor and wounded another, as a violation of international law and warned that it "cannot go unanswered."

The consultation with Lavrov is presented in the Brief within a broader picture of naval escalation, alongside President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s Navy Day remarks instructing Russian commanders to respond decisively to attempts to seize Russian vessels while acting within international maritime law. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the diplomatic traffic among Moscow, Tehran and Ankara as pointing toward an eventual Russia–[[turkey|Turkey]]–Iran naval alignment.
