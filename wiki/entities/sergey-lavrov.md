# Sergey Lavrov

Sergey Lavrov is the Foreign Minister of the Russian Federation and the principal diplomatic voice of [[russia|Russia]] in its dealings with Western governments and with partners such as [[iran|Iran]].

## Caspian Sea incident

The July 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Iranian Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]] raised the reported Ukrainian strike on an Iranian commercial vessel in the [[caspian-sea|Caspian Sea]] with Lavrov, as well as with European Union foreign policy chief [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]]. Araghchi described the strike, which killed one sailor and wounded another, as a violation of international law and warned that it "cannot go unanswered."

The consultation with Lavrov is presented in the Brief within a broader picture of naval escalation, alongside President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s Navy Day remarks instructing Russian commanders to respond decisively to attempts to seize Russian vessels while acting within international maritime law. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the diplomatic traffic among Moscow, Tehran and Ankara as pointing toward an eventual Russia–[[turkey|Turkey]]–Iran naval alignment.

## Accusations against France in Africa

In a speech to the Russian parliament on Wednesday, February 11, 2026, reported by *[[rt|RT]]* and summarized in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief, Lavrov accused [[france|France]] of pursuing "revanchist" policies in Africa. He said France "is attempting to overthrow undesirable nationalist governments in the Sahara-Sahel region and other parts of Africa," and that although African nations "have long since abandoned their ties to their former metropolis," Paris has chosen to undermine them through opposition figures, "outright terrorist groups and Ukrainian militants." The Brief situates the remarks amid ongoing tensions between Russia and Western states over influence in Africa, noting France's stated respect for African sovereignty alongside its concern about unconstitutional changes of government.

[[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats the speech as confirmation of what he calls "The War of Sovereignty," a coordinated subversive campaign he attributes to a unipolar hegemony and maps as "Conflict A." He identifies the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]] under Burkinabè President Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] as the target, credits the Russian military and the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] with having trained those forces in Syria, and speculates about a Russian redeployment to the Sahel.

## Cuba as a "brotherly nation" (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Lavrov referred to [[cuba|Cuba]] as a "brotherly nation" in public remarks while criticizing US sanctions on the island. The remarks came as Cuba faced worsening blackouts and waste accumulation from large-scale power outages tied to strain on its electrical grid, with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] describing the country as "a failed nation" that lacks jet fuel.
