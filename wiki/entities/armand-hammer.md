# Armand Hammer

Armand Hammer (1898–1990) was the [[occidental-petroleum|Occidental Petroleum]] magnate identified in Badlands Media coverage as the most powerful Western figure granted access to buy up Russian assets under the [[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy]].[1]

## Role in the Soviet economy

Hammer was forced to leave Russia the moment [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] was expelled, and returned to dominance only after [[joseph-stalin|Joseph Stalin]]'s death in 1953.[1] Working closely with [[vladimir-lenin|Vladimir Lenin]] and especially Trotsky, he became the principal moderator of nearly every business deal struck between the Soviet government and Western corporations during the 1920s, a period in which Russia is described as sinking into brutal economic enslavement to foreign powers at a pace not seen again for more than sixty years.[1]

Later in life Hammer described Lenin telling him: "We do not need doctors, we need businessmen… communism is not working, and we must change to a New Economic Policy."[1]

## Legacy and comparison

The vast liberalization of the Russian economy in the 1920s is said to parallel closely the [[perestroika|Perestroika]] free-trade and privatization program of the 1990s, with the article noting that George Bush Sr. dubbed that later program of Balkanized looting "Operation Hammer."[1] The article suggests that anyone seeing a proto-[[george-soros|George Soros]] in the figure of Armand Hammer "would not be far off."[1]
