# Beirut

Beirut is the capital of [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and, in June 2026, the focal point of a planned Israeli military operation that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] reportedly intervened to stop.[1]

## June 2026 halted Israeli strike

According to [[axios|Axios]] reporting on a June 1 phone call, Trump directly intervened to stop a planned Israeli strike on Beirut, arguing that further escalation would undermine ongoing regional diplomacy and isolate [[israel|Israel]] internationally.[1] Israeli officials subsequently confirmed that plans for a Beirut operation had been shelved, although Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] publicly maintained that Israel would continue responding to [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] attacks and would keep military operations underway in southern Lebanon.[1]

Trump later stated on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] that he had held a "very productive" call with Netanyahu, that Israeli troops would not enter Beirut, and that any forces already en route had been turned back; he added that Hezbollah, through intermediaries, had agreed to halt all shooting as part of a de-escalation arrangement.[1]

The city's fate was bound up with the wider negotiating track: [[iran|Iran]] had suspended indirect talks with Washington over Israeli operations in Gaza and Lebanon, making Beirut a potential trigger point for a collapse of regional diplomacy.[1] See [[june-2026-trump-netanyahu-lebanon-call|the June 2026 Trump–Netanyahu Lebanon call]].[1]

## Embassy protests (October 2023)

On the night of October 17, 2023, the situation outside the United States Embassy in Beirut deteriorated sharply following the [[al-ahli-baptist-hospital-explosion|al-Ahli Baptist Hospital explosion]] in Gaza, with reports of riot police and tear gas deployed against large crowds waving [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] flags.[2] The unrest was part of a wider wave of demonstrations that [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] described as a global "day of rage."[2]
