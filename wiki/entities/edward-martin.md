# Edward Martin

Edward Martin is an American lawyer and political activist who was nominated to serve as U.S. Attorney for the District of Columbia during the second [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], and whose nomination collapsed in May 2025.[1]

## Background

Martin is a "Stop the Steal" advocate and MAGA political activist who was among the crowd outside the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021.[1] He defended Capitol riot cases, including the case of an accused Nazi sympathizer, and made frequent appearances on Russian-linked media outlets and on conspiracy theorist Alex Jones's program.[1]

## Failed nomination

The collapse of Martin's nomination as U.S. Attorney for the District of Columbia, and the surprise announcement of Fox News personality [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]] as his replacement, triggered a unique and mixed wave of reaction from former prosecutors, critics of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and people involved in the U.S. Capitol riot prosecutions.[1] The implosion of Martin's nomination for the top D.C. prosecutor post drew celebratory statements from Trump's critics and potentially softened some of the backlash against the choice of Pirro.[1]

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] addressed the episode on the Devolution Power Hour.[1]
