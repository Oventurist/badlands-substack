# SunPower

**SunPower** is an American solar company named as one of the four biggest US solar firms in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]].[1]

Carlson reported SunPower's stock down 76 percent between [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s inauguration on January 20, 2021 and May 2023, the steepest fall among the solar names he examined.[1] Alongside [[nextera-energy|NextEra]] and [[avangrid|Avangrid]], it forms the backdrop against which [[first-solar|First Solar]]'s reported 108 percent gain stands out, a divergence Carlson attributes to political connection rather than to any operational advantage.[1]
