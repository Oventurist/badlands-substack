# Fox News

Fox News is the American conservative cable news network associated with [[rupert-murdoch|Rupert Murdoch]] and the Fox/[[news-corp|News Corp]] media empire.

## In the Badlands critique

A Swan Song for the MSM refuses to treat Fox News as an alternative to the liberal press. The article states that what passes for journalism at [[msnbc|MSNBC]] is matched by "Fox News' catering to garden variety conservatives," and that the same emptiness was "true of most Fox News hosts." It argues that the audience is offered only a choice between "establishment democrat talking points or establishment republican talking points."

The essay also notes that "the Fox News crowd won't touch the difficult, but important topics you see thoroughly discussed here on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]," just as [[cnn|CNN]] will not cover the material published by [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]] or [[consortium-news|Consortium News]]. The conclusion drawn is that the two ostensibly opposing networks are "two heads on the same wretched snake" within a single system of [[mainstream-media|centralized media]].

## The Five interview on Iran (March 2026)

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] appeared on "The Five" and said the [[cia|CIA]] had told him that [[iran|Iran]]'s new supreme leader [[mojtaba-khamenei|Mojtaba Khamenei]] is gay, adding "or he's not, but a lot of people say" — remarks Badlands commentary reads as trolling the media narrative surrounding the Iran conflict.
