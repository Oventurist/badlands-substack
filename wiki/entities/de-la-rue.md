# De La Rue PLC

De La Rue PLC is a British firm best known for security printing and currency production. In the Badlands corpus it appears as the seller of [[sequoia-voting-systems|Sequoia Voting Systems]].

According to [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, De La Rue sold Sequoia to [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] in 2005 for $16 million, four years after investing $35 million in the company — a loss of $19 million on the transaction. The sale placed a significant American voting equipment vendor under the ownership of a company the Badlands account describes as Venezuelan-linked.
