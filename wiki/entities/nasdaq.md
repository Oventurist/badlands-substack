# NASDAQ

The NASDAQ is a United States stock exchange and index composed mostly of technology companies and growth-oriented companies.[1]

## 2022 performance

Its heavy weighting toward growth stocks made it markedly more volatile than broader benchmarks during the 2022 downturn: the [[sp-500|S&P 500]] was down 19.44% for the year while the NASDAQ was down 33.1%.[1]

## Context in the Badlands argument

In his February 2023 commentary, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] grouped the NASDAQ with the [[dow-jones-industrial-average|Dow Jones Industrial Average]] and the S&P 500 as measures of only the small publicly traded fraction of American enterprise, noting that most US companies are private.[1] He also observed that the technology companies concentrated on the NASDAQ were among those least disrupted by the [[covid-19-lockdowns|COVID lockdowns]], because their employees could work from home and their products and services remained purchasable during near-full lockdown.[1]
