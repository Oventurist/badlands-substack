# Apple Daily

Apple Daily was, for over two decades, [[hong-kong|Hong Kong]]'s largest independent newspaper and arguably one of the most outspoken critics of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] still in print.[1] It was founded by media proprietor [[jimmy-lai|Jimmy Lai]].[1]

## Destruction under the National Security Law

The [[hong-kong-national-security-law-2020|National Security Law]] imposed by Beijing was used to destroy the entire company.[1] Apple Daily was raided twice by Hong Kong police — once on August 10, 2020, and again on June 17, 2021.[1] Those raids and the subsequent freeze of the company's capital forced the 26-year-old newspaper to close its doors in June 2021.[1] Badlands Media presented the paper's closure as the clearest single illustration of the collapse of press freedom in the territory, a freedom that had once distinguished Hong Kong from mainland [[china|China]].[1]
