# Volodymyr Zelensky

Volodymyr Zelensky is the president of Ukraine, described in "A Bright Red Line" in the context of a manpower crisis driving Ukraine's war effort and its push for [[nato|NATO]] membership.[1]

## Manpower and the draft

The article states that a desperation for what it calls cannon fodder led Zelensky, already working with a vanishingly small conscripted army, to sign into law a lowering of the draft age to 25.[1] Even with a lowered draft age, it argues, Ukraine cannot sustain the war much further into the future, and certainly cannot beat back the Russians.[1] The article had earlier noted that Ukraine appealed to Westerners who claimed to support the cause to enlist and fight rather than merely display Ukrainian flags in their profile pictures, and that remarkably few Americans heeded the call.[1]

## NATO as necessity

Because the one-sided war of attrition cannot otherwise continue, the article argues Ukraine essentially must join NATO, at which point it could be the United States military shipping off to the front lines — supplying, in the author's phrasing, the manpower Zelensky requires.[1] The article also characterizes Ukraine as a country with "an installed leader who is subservient to the West," for whom membership in an alliance bound by [[article-5|Article 5]] is a natural objective.[1]
