# Raphael Warnock

Raphael Warnock is a Democratic United States Senator from Georgia.[1]

## 2025 Capitol steps sit-in

On Sunday, April 27, 2025, Warnock joined House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] and New Jersey Senator [[cory-booker|Cory Booker]] at a livestreamed sit-in protest on the steps of the U.S. Capitol against the Republican budget plan for fiscal year 2026.[1] He spoke as the demonstration passed the ten-hour mark.[1] The event, billed as an "Urgent Conversation with the American People," preceded Congress's return to session and was part of a Democratic effort to stall the Republican economic agenda.[1]

## Shutdown pay votes (October 2025)

During the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|October 2025 government shutdown]], Warnock joined fellow Georgia Democrat [[jon-ossoff|Jon Ossoff]] and Sen. [[john-fetterman|John Fetterman]] in crossing party lines on Thursday, October 23, 2025, to support the Republican-sponsored [[shutdown-fairness-act-of-2025|Shutdown Fairness Act of 2025]] to pay active-duty military and essential federal employees; the bill failed 54-45, short of the 60-vote threshold.[2] Both Georgia Democrats nevertheless continued voting against the stopgap funding bill to reopen the government.[2]
