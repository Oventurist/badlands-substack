# Drew Carey

Drew Carey is an American comedian and television host associated with the [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]] entertainment industry.

## Criticism of Spencer Pratt

In the coverage summarised by the Badlands Brief edition "[[badlands-brief-3d4|Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns]]," Carey publicly blasted mayoral candidate [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] as a "serial scammer," joining a broader wave of Los Angeles establishment criticism directed at Pratt's insurgent campaign in the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|2026 Los Angeles mayoral election]]. Other critics attacked Pratt's residency, hotel spending and MAGA-adjacent support. The Brief argued that such attacks tended to strengthen rather than weaken Pratt's outsider positioning against incumbent [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]].
