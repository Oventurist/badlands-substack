# Jeffrey Taubenberger

Jeffrey Taubenberger is an American virologist and pathologist best known for his work on influenza, including the recovery and sequencing of the 1918 Spanish flu virus. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears among the scientists targeted in the 2026 COVID origins reckoning at the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]] (NIAID).

## Removal as acting head of NIAID (2026)

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Taubenberger was removed from his role as acting head of NIAID shortly after the indictment of [[david-morens|David Morens]], the former adviser to [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]]. The removal is presented as part of a series of legal and administrative actions — debarments, indictments, arrests, removals from leadership and congressional subpoenas — pursued by proponents of the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]], including figures in the Trump administration, against scientists they claim hid evidence of a possible laboratory origin of COVID-19 in Wuhan, China. The Brief groups his case with those of [[ralph-baric|Ralph Baric]], Morens, [[vincent-munster|Vincent Munster]] and Fauci, noting that enforcement has shifted toward the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].
