# Jeffrey Taubenberger

Jeffrey Taubenberger is an American virologist who served as acting head of the [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]].[1] He was removed from that role in 2026, shortly after the indictment and arrest of former [[anthony-fauci|Fauci]] adviser [[david-morens|David Morens]].[1]

His removal was counted among the debarments, indictments, arrests, leadership removals, and congressional subpoenas that made up the post-April 2026 wave of accountability actions against NIAID-affiliated scientists over COVID-19 research and the question of the virus's origins.[1]
