# Democratic Socialists of America

The Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) is an American socialist political organization whose members have won municipal and congressional offices through Democratic primaries. In the Badlands corpus it is treated as the organizational core of the radical left that emerged after 2016.

## Treatment in the corpus

The March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]] devotes extended commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] to the DSA. She argues that the organization has been "waging a temu revolution on Americans since 2017" and is now at its end stage. In 2020 the DSA was "fire," she writes, occupying the cities and carried by students, idealists and "restless believers in a new order," and in her account its adherents "burned and looted and murdered" out of zeal for a communist utopia — zeal being "exactly what a party led by a corpse needed."

The commentary holds that the DSA failed to expand beyond a few blue strongholds and failed to make every city [[zohran-mamdani|Mamdani]]'s New York, and has therefore become inconvenient to the [[uniparty|uniparty]] left as it pivots toward the political middle before the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]]. Because that pivot requires appearing "stable and sane" in order to portray [[donald-trump|Trump]] and his supporters as extremists, the DSA is described as a liability whose purity tests, uncompromising rhetoric and eagerness to destroy — useful during COVID, BLM and the Gaza protests — are now risks.

Ashe in America places this within a historical pattern, invoking the Jacobins, the Bolsheviks and the Red Guard as cases in which "the idealists get handled when the phase of the revolution changes," since "the ingredients of destabilization are the opposite of those required for stability": stability demands hierarchy, and hierarchy demands control, whereas the DSA consists of uncontrollable ideologues.
