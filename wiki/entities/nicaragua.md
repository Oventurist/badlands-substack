# Nicaragua

Nicaragua is a Central American state named in January 2026 as a prospective target of United States pressure following the US-led [[operation-absolute-resolve|Operation Absolute Resolve]] in Venezuela.[1]

## Named as a target (January 2026)

Republican Senator [[rick-scott|Rick Scott]] said in a public interview that, following recent US actions in Venezuela, the United States would next "fix" [[cuba|Cuba]] and Nicaragua and would seek political change in [[colombia|Colombia]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the remark as evidence that neoconservatives had "taken the bait" laid by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], writing that the US government was arbitrarily deciding that Latin American governments were illegitimate for reasons that were not entirely clear and describing the rhetoric as "the essence of tyranny."[1]
