# Chris Pavlovski

**Chris Pavlovski** is the head of [[rumble|Rumble]], the free-speech video platform Badlands Media identifies as the censorship regime's likely primary target ahead of the 2024 election.[1]

## Position in the information war

Badlands notes that Pavlovski does not have even a fraction of the resources available to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], yet is challenging the censorship regime even more intensely than [[x-twitter|X]] is.[1] That asymmetry, in the author's assessment, leaves Rumble in a much more vulnerable position and possibly makes it a higher-priority target than X.[1] Because Rumble under Pavlovski has taken a hard stance against the regime, Badlands Media credits the platform with enabling its own operations and urges readers to protect and support it.[1]
