# Rinat Akhmetov

Rinat Akhmetov is a Ukrainian businessman and owner of the industrial conglomerate SCM Group.[1]

## Ranking and war effort

As of September 2024, according to the outlet Ukrainian Focus, Akhmetov topped the list of Ukraine's wealthiest people, followed by Interpipe Group owner [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]], former president [[petro-poroshenko|Petro Poroshenko]], Dneprazot owner [[igor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]] and Ferrexpo owner [[konstantin-zhevago|Konstantin Zhevago]].[1] Most of these individuals have contributed to Kiev's war effort against Russia.[1]

## Musk sanctions proposal (March 2025)

Akhmetov's position at the top of the oligarch rankings placed him at the center of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s March 2025 suggestion that placing sanctions on Ukraine's top ten oligarchs would swiftly end the conflict with Russia.[1] Musk, who leads the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]], made the proposal on X in reply to Senator [[mike-lee|Mike Lee]]'s call for the United States to halt financial aid to Kiev, without elaborating on the mechanism by which such sanctions would affect the war.[1]
