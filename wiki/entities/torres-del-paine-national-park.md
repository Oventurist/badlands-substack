# Torres del Paine National Park

Torres del Paine National Park is a Chilean national park in Patagonia that has repeatedly featured in reporting on wildfires started by foreign tourists.[1]

## Fire incidents and expulsions

A 2014 Ynet News article reports that four Israeli tourists were expelled from the park for violating fire restrictions after inspectors caught them starting an illegal fire.[1] In January 2012 a 23-year-old Israeli tourist, Rotem Singer, was arrested in Chile after a massive wildfire broke out in the reserve; authorities alleged that toilet paper he burned caused the fire to spread, and firefighters battled the blaze for days with four planes and a helicopter as tens of thousands of acres of forest were destroyed.[1] Singer denied the allegations and, after a month, reached a settlement with Chilean prosecutors and was released.[1]

In 2017 a group of three Israeli tourists was expelled from the park and fined $600,000 for starting a fire in an unauthorized area.[1] Afterward the director of Conaf in Magallanes, Elizabeth Muñoz, told Tele13 Radio that most expulsions from the park involve Israeli tourists, stating that since 2012 there had been 36 expulsions, 23 of them Israelis, and that "it seems they have a culture of disobeying and going against the rules."[1]

This history is cited in commentary surrounding the [[patagonia-wildfires-2026|Patagonia wildfires of 2026]] in neighboring Argentina.[1]
