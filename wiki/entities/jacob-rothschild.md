# Jacob Rothschild

Jacob Rothschild (1936–2024) was a British financier and a senior member of the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild]] banking dynasty, one of Europe's best-known financial families.[1] His death at the age of 87 was announced by his family on Monday, February 26, 2024.[1]

## Career

Born in England in 1936, Rothschild began his career at the family bank, N M Rothschild & Sons, in 1963.[1] He later co-founded J Rothschild Assurance Group, which became the London-based wealth manager St James's Place.[1] He also founded the investment trust RIT Capital Partners, which backed a wide range of ventures from hedge funds to clean-technology start-ups.[1]

In a statement to Britain's Press Association, his family called him "a towering presence in many peoples' lives" and described him as "a superbly accomplished financier, a champion of the arts and culture, a devoted public servant, a passionate supporter of charitable causes in Israel and Jewish culture, a keen environmentalist and much-loved friend, father and grandfather."[1]
