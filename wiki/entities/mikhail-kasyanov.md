# Mikhail Kasyanov

Mikhail Kasyanov is a former Prime Minister of Russia who led the government from 2000 to 2004 under [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1] He later became an outspoken critic of Russia's military operation against Ukraine and has resided abroad for years.[1]

## Designations

In November 2025, Russian authorities added Kasyanov, along with economist [[sergey-guriev|Sergey Guriev]], to the official list of "terrorists and extremists."[1] The notice was published by Russia's financial watchdog, [[rosfinmonitoring|Rosfinmonitoring]], on a Monday, and the entry offered no explanation for the designation.[1]

Moscow had previously designated Kasyanov a "foreign agent" in 2023, stating that he was "actively taking part in the creation and distribution" of materials by other foreign agents and spreading "false information aimed at tarnishing Russian authorities."[1] Officials also said Kasyanov opposed Moscow's military operation in Ukraine and participated in the Anti-War Committee of Russia, an expatriate organization created in early 2022 shortly after the outbreak of full-scale hostilities between Russia and Ukraine.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Putin had launched a criminal investigation into Kasyanov the previous month, accusing him of attempting a coup against Putin in 2022 following the invasion of Ukraine.[1] Reviewing Kasyanov's biography, he argued that Kasyanov's deep ties to London- and Washington-based think tanks suggested intelligence connections, pointing to a 2006 presentation Kasyanov gave to the [[international-institute-for-strategic-studies|International Institute for Strategic Studies]] in London in which — as a former member of Putin's administration — he claimed Putin had restructured the government and consolidated power, effectively branding him an autocrat.[1] He speculated that this presentation may have been an origin point for the "Putin is a strongman dictator" narrative.[1]
