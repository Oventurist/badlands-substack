# Tom Cole

Tom Cole is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Oklahoma and chairman of the [[house-appropriations-committee|House Appropriations Committee]].[1]

## Fiscal Year 2026 Interior and Environment markup

Cole appeared alongside subcommittee chairman [[mike-simpson|Mike Simpson]] at the July 15, 2025 markup of the Fiscal Year 2026 Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies appropriations bill, which the subcommittee approved.[1] The bill was framed by its authors as rolling back Biden-era [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]] regulation, limiting use of the Endangered Species Act, expanding access to hardrock and critical minerals, and addressing needs in Indian Country.[1] Badlands commentary criticized a provision described as granting "vaccine-like immunity" to pesticide manufacturers, calling it contrary to [[make-america-healthy-again]] priorities.[1]
