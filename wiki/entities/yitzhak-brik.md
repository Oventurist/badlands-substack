# Yitzhak Brik

Yitzhak Brik is a retired Israeli major general and former commander who has become one of the most outspoken internal critics of the Israeli military's conduct and readiness during the war in the Gaza Strip.[1]

## Appeal to Trump over the Gaza war

In an opinion piece published in the Haaretz newspaper and reported in early August 2025, Brik appealed to US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to intervene and compel Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to end the war in Gaza.[1] "For now, all we can do is to pray that Donald Trump will come to his senses and realize that he needs to force Benjamin Netanyahu to end the war, release the hostages, rebuild the country, and save it from collapse," he wrote.[1]

Brik ruled out the possibility of permanent Israeli military rule in Gaza, arguing that the army's ground forces "are very small and exhausted."[1] He further warned that annexing parts of Gaza would isolate Israel internationally and invite additional economic and diplomatic boycotts, calling such a course "political suicide."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited Brik's warning as evidence that former [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] generals and other analysts now believe Trump is permitting Netanyahu to lead Israel toward political self-destruction, compounded by a war-attrition economic collapse and a mental health crisis within the IDF.[1]
