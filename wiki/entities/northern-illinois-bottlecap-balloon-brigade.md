# Northern Illinois Bottlecap Balloon Brigade

The Northern Illinois Bottlecap Balloon Brigade (NIBBB) is an Illinois-based amateur balloonist club that flies small, long-duration "pico balloons" carrying lightweight radio payloads.[1]

## Missing pico balloon, February 2023

In February 2023 the club announced in a blog post that one of its balloons, designated K9YO, was "missing in action" and feared shot down by the [[united-states-air-force|United States Air Force]]. The 32-inch-wide pico balloon had been aloft for more than four months and had circumnavigated the globe seven times before its last reported position over Alaska on a Saturday. The Air Force said it had downed an unidentified object in the same region at approximately the same time. The club stopped short of directly blaming the USAF in its post.[1]

The incident occurred during the wave of [[2023-north-american-aerial-object-shootdowns|North American aerial object shootdowns]] that followed the destruction of a Chinese high-altitude surveillance balloon.[1]
