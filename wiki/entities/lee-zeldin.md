# Lee Zeldin

Lee Zeldin is the Administrator of the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Zeldin was to appear alongside President Trump at a [[white-house|White House]] ceremony marking the issuance of the final rule rescinding the EPA's 2009 [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]]. Zeldin and other administration officials described the action as the largest act of deregulation in United States history, projecting over $1 trillion in regulatory cost savings — chiefly through reduced vehicle compliance expenses averaging more than $2,400 per light-duty car, SUV or truck — and emphasising benefits for energy affordability, fossil fuel production, economic growth and national security.
