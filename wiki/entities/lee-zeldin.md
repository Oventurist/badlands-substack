# Lee Zeldin

Lee Zeldin is the Administrator of the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Zeldin was to appear alongside President Trump at a [[white-house|White House]] ceremony marking the issuance of the final rule rescinding the EPA's 2009 [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]]. Zeldin and other administration officials described the action as the largest act of deregulation in United States history, projecting over $1 trillion in regulatory cost savings — chiefly through reduced vehicle compliance expenses averaging more than $2,400 per light-duty car, SUV or truck — and emphasising benefits for energy affordability, fossil fuel production, economic growth and national security.

## Criminal referrals over green energy grants (June 2026)

The June 3, 2026 Badlands Brief reported that Zeldin made criminal referrals to the EPA inspector general and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] alleging self-dealing in the distribution of Biden-era green energy grants funded by the [[inflation-reduction-act|Inflation Reduction Act]]. He canceled or paused roughly $29 billion in such awards, including the $20 billion of the [[greenhouse-gas-reduction-fund|Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund]] routed through eight nonprofit "cutouts," and a separate $2 billion grant to [[power-forward-communities|Power Forward Communities]] for residential decarbonization.

Specific cases cited included former Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund director Jahi Wise personally overseeing a $5 billion grant to his prior employer; the $2 billion award to Power Forward Communities, a nonprofit tied to [[stacey-abrams|Stacey Abrams]] that had reported only $100 in revenue the previous year; and $20 million to Young, Gifted & Green, whose chief executive applied while serving on the White House Environmental Justice Advisory Council. Zeldin said the money went to "pass-through entities" connected to former Obama–Biden officials, Democratic donors, and politically connected individuals, calling the rushed 2024 distribution "blatant waste and abuse" that "feels criminal."

Badlands contributors treated the referrals as a test of accountability: [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described them as encouraging follow-through on what [[doge|DOGE]] had exposed and urged other agencies to file their own, while [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read them as confirmation of his [[scarcity-paradigm|Scarcity Paradigm]] thesis about the [[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]] era.
