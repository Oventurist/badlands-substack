# Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression

The Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression (FIRE) is a nonpartisan American nonprofit organization dedicated to defending and promoting individual rights on college campuses, primarily freedom of speech and expression. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," FIRE's survey data is cited to illustrate the campus climate around free speech.

## Survey data on campus self-censorship

The article cites FIRE's 2020 college free speech rankings, which found that 62% of college students believed shouting down speakers was acceptable to some degree, and that conservative students reported higher rates of self-censorship — 73% for strong Republicans versus 52% for strong Democrats. The source uses these figures to support its argument that conservative students and speakers faced event cancellations, disruptions, and self-censorship due to "social pressures or administrative actions."
