# NBC

NBC is a major American broadcast television network, cited by Badlands Media as the central illustration of the [[military-industrial-media-complex|military-industrial-media complex]].[1]

## General Electric ownership

For years [[general-electric|General Electric]] owned 49% of NBC.[1] Over the same period GE served as a subcontractor for Tomahawk cruise missiles and Patriot II missiles used extensively during the [[gulf-war|Gulf War]], and manufactured components for the B-2 stealth bomber, the B-52 bomber and the E-3 AWACS aircraft.[1] GE received around $2 billion in defence contracts tied to weapons used in the Gulf War and the 2003 invasion and occupation of Iraq.[1] Comcast subsequently acquired GE's common equity ownership interest in NBCUniversal.[1]

Badlands Media presents this overlap — a network's part-owner profiting directly from the wars that network covers — as one example among many of the historic marriage between defence contractors and corporate media, and as a structural reason for the press's reliable enthusiasm for military intervention.[1]
