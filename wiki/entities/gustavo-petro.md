# Gustavo Petro

## Claims about the Sinaloa cartel

In February 2025, Colombian President Gustavo Petro held a press conference with his generals in which he announced that the Colombian militias that had operated in the jungles against the Colombian government since the early 1960s were actually proxy forces of the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa cartel]], which operates out of [[mexico|Mexico]].[1] According to Petro, the Sinaloa cartel is the head of the snake when it comes to the cocaine trade — a claim [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited a year later as context for the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's indictment of ten Mexican officials over cartel ties.[1]
