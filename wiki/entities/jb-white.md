# JB White

JB White is a host and commentator affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], the citizen-journalist broadcasting collective whose programming airs live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media Network library channel.[1]

## Programs

White co-hosts **Sports Talk**, which aired Mondays and Fridays at 12pm ET as of the December 2022 schedule, alongside Absolute 1776. The show takes what Badlands describes as an unorthodox approach to sports coverage, "cutting through the BS, cutting out the politics and focusing on regaining America's Pastime."[1]

He also co-hosts **Trash Talk** with [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]], airing Tuesdays and Thursdays at 8am ET. Trash Talk is a conversational morning program in which the two hosts range across subjects from the Information War to pop culture and entertainment.[1]

White's dual placement on both a sports program and a general-interest morning show reflects Badlands Media's editorial approach of blending cultural commentary with political and information-war analysis rather than segregating the two.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]
