# James Blair

**James Blair** was named Deputy Chief of Staff for Legislative, Political and Public Affairs in the incoming second administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], announced during the November 2024 transition.[1] The Badlands Media account rendered his surname as "James Blaire" in its running list of appointments.[1]

## Context

Blair's appointment came amid a rapid wave of transition announcements that also included [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] as Attorney General, [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] as Director of National Intelligence, [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] as Secretary of State, [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] and [[taylor-budowich|Taylor Budowich]] as fellow deputy chiefs of staff, and [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]] as Chief of Staff.[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described the overall slate as "an odd mix of duds and dynamite" while reserving judgment on individual picks.[1]

These appointments were expected to face a confirmation environment shaped by the Senate leadership fight, in which [[john-thune|John Thune]] was elected majority leader to succeed the retiring [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]].[1]
