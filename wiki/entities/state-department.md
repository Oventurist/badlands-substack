# State Department

The U.S. Department of State is identified in "A Bright Red Line" as one of the institutional homes of the Western neoconservatives driving [[nato|NATO]] expansion into Ukraine.[1]

The article argues that stating Ukrainian membership as settled takes negotiations with Russia completely off the table, and that "the Western Neocons in the Security State, in Congress, and at the State Department" are not merely aware of this consequence but hungry for it.[1] The department is headed by Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]], whose declaration to a summit of NATO foreign ministers that "Ukraine WILL become a member of NATO" the article treats as the decisive statement of policy.[1] The article also notes that [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] and Condoleezza Rice were affirmatively discussing NATO expansion to include Ukraine during the Bush administration.[1] See also [[congress]].
