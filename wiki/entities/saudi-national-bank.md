# Saudi National Bank

The Saudi National Bank is a large Saudi Arabian financial institution which, as of March 2023, was the largest single investor in the Swiss lender [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].[1]

## Role in the March 2023 Credit Suisse crisis

On March 15, 2023, as Credit Suisse disclosed problems in its financial reporting and moved to draw up to $53.7 billion in emergency liquidity from the [[swiss-national-bank|Swiss National Bank]], the Saudi National Bank signaled that it would not be rushing in with additional cash to buttress the firm.[1] That refusal compounded Credit Suisse's difficulties, removing the possibility of a private-sector rescue by its biggest shareholder at precisely the moment confidence in the bank was eroding and European banking stocks were falling.[1]

The episode illustrated how the fate of a systemically important European bank had come to depend in part on the appetite of a Gulf sovereign-linked investor, a dynamic that commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] folded into their broader argument that the global fiat banking system was under accelerating strain following the failure of [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]].[1]
