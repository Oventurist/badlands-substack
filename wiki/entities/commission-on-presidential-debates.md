# Commission on Presidential Debates

The Commission on Presidential Debates is the body traditionally responsible for setting the terms of United States presidential debates. In May 2024 it became a focus of Badlands criticism over the negotiation of the 2024 debate schedule between [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Biden had demanded "very undemocratic debate conditions" — no audience, a mute button, and a hand-picked moderator team — and that the Commission's acquiescence to such terms "proves that we are living in a different America than we all believed possible."[1] The core objection was procedural: the commission, not the candidates, is supposed to set the terms, and the spectacle of one candidate setting all the terms was characterized as [[uniparty|uniparty]] manipulation "so transparent that only the intellectually dishonest can ignore it."[1]

The dispute arose after the Biden campaign rejected two further debate proposals plus a vice-presidential debate, having decreed strict conditions for two debates only — the June [[cnn|CNN]] and September [[abc-news|ABC]] events — while Trump accepted additional invitations from [[fox-news|Fox News]] and NBC/Telemundo.[1]
