# Predator Poachers

**Predator Poachers** is a Houston-based vigilante organization founded by [[alex-rosen|Alex Rosen]] that conducts sting operations nationwide against adults who seek sexual activities with young boys and girls.[1]

## Maryland Democratic Party sting

In June 2024 the group published video of a sting involving [[michael-knaapen|Michael Knaapen]], head of the [[maryland-democratic-party|Maryland Democratic Party]] LGBTQ+ Diversity Leadership Council.[1] Rosen wrote, "We just busted the head of the LGBTQ Dems of Maryland going after a 14-year-old. Groomers are about to be mad," and posted footage on X in which Knaapen discussed his role with the council.[1]
