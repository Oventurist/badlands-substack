# Murray Rothbard

**Murray Rothbard** was an economist cited by Badlands Media in support of its revisionist reading of the [[long-depression|Long Depression]].[1]

In his book *History of Money and Banking of the United States*, Rothbard argued that the Long Depression was only a misunderstood recession, since real wages and production were increasing throughout the period. He attributed falling prices to the deflationary [[gold-standard|gold standard]] in the United States in the aftermath of the Civil War.[1]
