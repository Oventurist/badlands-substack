# Susan Kokinda

Susan Kokinda is a commentator whose analysis of British imperial decline was highlighted by Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in January 2026.[1]

## "When the British empire loses control"

Ashe in America pointed to a Kokinda video discussing evidence of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s strategy to "demolish the hidden hand," which Kokinda opened by saying, "This is what it looks like when the British empire loses control."[1] Ashe noted that the video predated her post by about eleven days and that, amid the holidays and "all the color revolutions," she had missed Britain's response to what she described as the increasingly obvious moves of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] — perspective she considered timely given economic anxiety over oil futures.[1] Kokinda also spotlighted former Vice President Mike Pence in the same piece.[1]
