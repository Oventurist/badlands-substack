# Bree Fram

**Bree Fram** is a transgender colonel in the [[united-states-space-force|United States Space Force]] who in early 2024 attracted national attention for arguing that the use of pronouns in official email signatures would contribute to "winning war fighting strategies."[1]

## Remarks to the Air Force

During a speech to the U.S. Air Force reported on March 1, 2024, Fram called on service members to display symbols of pride and to share pronouns in their email.[1] "All too often, I hear leaders talk about providing everyone with dignity and respect like it's an aspirational goal, that's not good enough," Fram said.[1] "Dignity and respect is the bare minimum, it's the floor of where we can be, we must set our sights higher and focus on intentional inclusivity, because there are still far too many people out there, not just LGBTQ individuals, that feel marginalized, shut out or discriminated against."[1]

Fram continued: "So for all of you out there, I ask you to set out your symbols of pride, share your pronouns in your email, particularly if you're a person who doesn't think they need to, initiate difficult conversations about racial and gender barriers, and share a bit of your vulnerability in a way that draws others in."[1] Fram concluded that service members had "the power to take intentionally inclusive action to ensure the multiple perspectives that we know make us stronger as we devise winning war fighting strategies get heard."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the episode to argue that [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]]-style transformation of the U.S. armed forces is a deliberate weakening of the institution from within rather than a genuine inclusion strategy.[1] Citing McKinsey research that roughly 70 percent of corporate transformation programs fail primarily for lack of strategy or vision, she contrasted this with what she called the "rainbow cult," which she said does have a vision — to "trans the world through emotional blackmail and other behavioral tactics that wear people down and force compliance," weakening national resolve and forcing rejection of natural law.[1]

She invoked Proverbs 29:18 in the King James rendering — "Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he" — and asserted that the transgender community has the highest suicide rate of any intersectional subclass, including post-operatively.[1] Ashe argued that the transing of the military is uniquely coercive because refusal can lead to court martial or dishonorable discharge, so that a seemingly trivial demand for pronouns in email footers "will drive compliance in an institution that forbids dissent."[1] She concluded that forcing soldiers to "deny reality and participate in delusion" infects American warriors with a false reality at a moment of multi-theater conflict, and said of Fram that "Fram needs to be in a mental ward, finger painting a rainbow."[1]
