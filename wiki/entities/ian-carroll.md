# Ian Carroll

Ian Carroll is an independent journalist and researcher cited within the Badlands Media orbit for open-source investigations into financial and influence networks.[1]

## TikTok acquisition research

In March 2024, Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] credited Carroll with going "deep down this rabbit hole" on the proposed purchase of [[tiktok]] by an investor group assembled by former Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]], arguing that Carroll had assembled evidence that Mnuchin's bid represented Israeli interests rather than a purely commercial venture.[1] Esler further reported that Carroll's work cited evidence that TikTok's moderation systems suppressed anti-Israel content while leaving anti-CCP content comparatively untouched — a pattern Esler used to question how much of the "TikTok is a CCP weapon" framing functioned as cover for other state influence operations.[1] These claims are contested commentary rather than established fact.[1]

## Pizzagate video and Flynn's amplification

On January 3, 2024, [[michael-flynn|General Michael Flynn]] retweeted a video by Carroll which, in the account of Badlands Media contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], argued not only that [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]] was never debunked but that a sophisticated form of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] had been used to psychologically operate on the public in order to maintain the cover-up.[2] Esler assessed the video against *The Citizen's Guide to Fifth-Generation Warfare* by Flynn and [[boone-cutler|Boone Cutler]], concluding that Carroll's research aligned with the book's account of hybrid warfare, exposed an information feedback loop as an additional battlefield tactic, and validated the doctrine's description of shadow government involvement.[2]
