# John Pastore

John Pastore was the United States Senator who, in January 1975, introduced the resolution that created the [[church-committee|Church Committee]].[1]

## The 1975 resolution

Pastore's resolution called for the establishment of a select committee to investigate federal intelligence operations and to determine "the extent, if any, to which illegal, improper, or unethical activities were engaged in by any agency of the Federal Government."[1] The [[united-states-senate|Senate]] approved the resolution by a vote of 82–4, an overwhelming margin that reflected the post-[[watergate|Watergate]] climate and the impact of [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]]'s 1974 reporting on domestic [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] spying.[1]
