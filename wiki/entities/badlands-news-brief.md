# Badlands News Brief

The **Badlands News Brief** is the recurring newsletter published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on Substack, assembled by the outlet's collective of citizen journalists.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## Format and policy

The Brief is distributed free of charge, with readers invited to support the operation by becoming paid subscribers so that the collective can "take back the narrative"; its closing refrain is "We are the news now."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) Each edition carries a standing disclaimer that the opinions expressed are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the attitudes or positions of Badlands Media.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) Readers are routinely encouraged to share editions widely and to discuss them in the comments with "fellow Badlanders."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d) Editions also carry sponsored placements; the 7 November 2025 issue advertised the microdosing brand My Shroom Vibe with a "Badlands" discount code.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-look-at-recent-features-84d)

## Relationship to The Blitz

From 10 January 2026 the Brief's weekday output became the subject matter of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], a new weekly Badlands show described as a recap and dissection of the work the team does Monday through Friday in the Badlands Brief.[2] The programme also revisits the comment threads beneath Brief editions, sampling what readers have posted over the preceding week.[2]

The Brief's weekday reporting continued to anchor The Blitz through the spring of 2026: the programme's thirteenth episode, published 18 April 2026, was again introduced as a weekly recap and dissection of the work the team does Monday through Friday in the Badlands Brief, with hosts [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] devoting the hour to [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s Friday morning blitzkrieg of the [[information-war|Info War]] battlespace and the contention that the ending of the 'war' with [[iran|Iran]] had been decided before it was fought.[3] That post reiterated that all Badlands content, including The Blitz, remains free and is sustained only by readers who choose to support it directly.[3]

## Editorial evolution in 2026

The second episode of [[the-blitz|The Blitz]], published 17 January 2026, was again described as a weekly recap and dissection of the work the team does Monday through Friday in the Badlands Brief.[4] It devoted its opening segment to the original intentions behind the recent changes made to the publication, how the vision for both the Brief and the Blitz might evolve, and how readers fit into that plan.[4] The post also set out the aim of serving the Badlands Substack audience with exclusive content in 2026 while keeping all Badlands output, including The Blitz, free and funded by voluntary paid subscriptions.[4]

## Format and contributors

The Brief describes itself as "a uniquely curated selection of the stories dominating the info war — with context and commentary from Badlands hosts," offered as a way to track a news cycle that is impossible to follow in full.[6] A typical edition excerpts external reporting under linked headlines and appends an "Our Take" from a named host, occasionally adding an "Another Take" from a second contributor; recurring bylines include [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[canncon|CannCon]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], and [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[6] Editions also carry sponsored segments and a closing note that the opinions expressed are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the positions of Badlands Media, alongside an invitation to support the free newsletter through paid subscriptions under the banner "We are the news now."[6]
