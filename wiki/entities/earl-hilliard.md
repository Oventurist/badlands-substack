# Earl Hilliard

Earl Hilliard is a former Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Alabama.[1]

## Loss of his seat after pro-Israel backlash

In "The Exploitation of Charlie Kirk," Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited Hilliard (D-AL) as an example of the professional risk attached to criticizing U.S.-[[israel|Israel]] policy, noting that he lost his seat after significant backlash from pro-Israel groups.[1] DeLarme grouped him with [[cori-bush|Cori Bush]] (D-MO) and [[jamaal-bowman|Jamaal Bowman]] (D-NY), who met the same fate, and contrasted them with figures such as [[pat-buchanan|Pat Buchanan]], [[ron-paul|Ron Paul]], and John Hostettler, who openly criticized the relationship and defied the expectation of unwavering support.[1] The examples supported his broader claim that for decades a thriving career in American media or politics has often demanded outspoken loyalty to Israel.[1]
