# National Transportation Safety Board

The National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) is the independent United States federal agency charged with investigating civil transportation accidents, including rail accidents, and determining their probable cause.

## Determination on the East Palestine derailment

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the NTSB and the [[federal-railroad-administration|Federal Railroad Administration]] (FRA) determined that the February 3, 2023 [[norfolk-southern|Norfolk Southern]] derailment in [[east-palestine|East Palestine]], Ohio — in which 38 cars came off the rails, 20 of them carrying hazardous materials — was caused by a failed/overheated wheel bearing on a railcar. The finding anchors the Brief's account of the disaster, which frames the response and aftermath as a political issue taken up by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2026 recovery pledge.
