# Daniel Hodges

**Daniel Hodges** is a Washington, D.C. police officer who became a prominent public witness to the January 6, 2021 riot at the U.S. Capitol and whose testimony was used by petitioners in the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification case]] against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Role in the Colorado case

In closing arguments before a [[denver-district-court|Denver District Court]] judge on November 15, 2023, petitioners seeking to bar Trump from Colorado's Republican presidential primary ballot relied in part on the fear expressed by Hodges, described as an inexperienced Capitol police officer, as evidence bearing on whether Trump "engaged in insurrection" under the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

## Criticism

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], covering the trial, characterized the petitioners' reliance on Hodges as a weakness in their case, noting that he had been selling books and profiting from his experiences on January 6.[1] She placed his testimony alongside that of sociologist [[pete-simi|Pete Simi]] as the two pillars of a closing argument she described as "an overt assault on the Bill of Rights."[1]
