# Vaishnavi Jayakumar

**Vaishnavi Jayakumar** is the former head of safety and well-being at Instagram, whose testimony features in a major lawsuit against four social media companies.[1]

## Testimony on Meta's strike policy

According to a plaintiffs' brief unsealed in November 2025 in the Northern District of California, Jayakumar testified that when she joined [[meta-platforms|Meta]] in 2020 she was shocked to learn that the company operated a "17x" strike policy for accounts reportedly engaged in the "trafficking of humans for sex."[1] "You could incur 16 violations for prostitution and sexual solicitation, and upon the 17th violation, your account would be suspended," she reportedly testified, adding that "by any measure across the industry, [it was] a very, very high strike threshold."[1] Plaintiffs claim the testimony is corroborated by internal company documentation, and allege that sex trafficking on Meta platforms was both difficult to report and widely tolerated.[1]

The brief further alleges that Meta knew millions of adult strangers were contacting minors on its sites, that its products exacerbated mental health issues in teens, and that content related to eating disorders, suicide, and child sexual abuse was frequently detected yet rarely removed — and that the company failed to disclose these harms to the public or to [[congress|Congress]] while refusing to implement protective safety fixes.[1]
