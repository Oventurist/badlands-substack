# Netflix

Netflix is a streaming company that features in Badlands Media coverage as an advertiser targeted by media pressure campaigns.[1]

In March 2023 [[media-matters|Media Matters]] published an article claiming that "Ads for Netflix are appearing next to Holocaust denial videos on [[rumble|Rumble]]."[1] Rumble's own traffic data indicated that the Media Matters employee was the only human to view the instance of the video carrying the Netflix advertisement.[1] Despite the evident flaws in the claim, Netflix pulled its advertisements from Rumble out of fear of being labeled antisemitic, achieving the campaign's intended result and illustrating the leverage that advertiser flight gives pressure groups over a platform's survival.[1]
