# Jordan Bardella

Jordan Bardella is the French politician who leads the National Rally (Rassemblement National, RN), the anti-immigration party historically associated with [[marine-le-pen|Marine Le Pen]].[1] In the run-up to the June 2024 European Parliament elections, Bardella's RN emerged as the leading destination for young French voters, a shift that commentators treated as a marker of a broader populist realignment in Western Europe.[1]

## 2024 European Parliament campaign

An Ipsos survey published in March 2024 found that 31 percent of French voters aged 18 to 24 intended to back Bardella's National Rally in the June European Parliament elections.[1] The figure represented a rise of nine percentage points relative to polling conducted the previous December, indicating rapid consolidation of youth support behind Bardella's list.[1] Over the same period the rival hard-right party [[reconquete|Reconquête]], led by [[eric-zemmour|Éric Zemmour]] and fronted for the European contest by [[marion-marechal|Marion Maréchal]], fell from 8 percent to 3 percent among the same age cohort, suggesting that Bardella was absorbing rather than merely sharing the youth right-wing vote.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the French polling as evidence that disillusionment with establishment parties was pushing young voters toward both the populist right and the populist left, and argued that the French youth were "foundational in their populist uprising" in a way that American observers should study.[1] The same commentary framed party structures themselves — rather than any single party's platform — as the root obstacle to populist politics, citing the [[uniparty]] dynamic in the United States.[1]
