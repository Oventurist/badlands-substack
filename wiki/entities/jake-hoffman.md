# Jake Hoffman

Jake Hoffman is a Republican member of the Arizona state Senate and a leading legislative advocate of post-2020 election-integrity measures in the state.[1]

Hoffman sponsored [[arizona-senate-bill-1695|Senate Bill 1695]], a 2023 measure aimed at combating voter fraud in Arizona, which was approved by the Senate Government Committee on February 16, 2023.[1] The bill would bar certification of an election in which voters were disenfranchised because election laws had been violated, and would require a complete redo of any such election in Arizona counties with more than one million residents.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] characterized the measure as a step in the right direction that nonetheless exposed a deeper problem — that a remedy so basic was not already standard policy.[1]
