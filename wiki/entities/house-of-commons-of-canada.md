# House of Commons of Canada

The House of Commons of Canada is the elected lower chamber of Canada's Parliament. In September 2023 the chamber became the subject of international controversy after it gave a standing ovation to a veteran of a Nazi military unit during a state visit by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky.[1]

## The 2023 Zelensky visit

Zelensky addressed the House on Friday, September 22, 2023, as part of a Canadian visit that Badlands commentary linked to hundreds of millions of dollars in fresh Ukrainian war funding pledged by Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]].[1] Immediately after the address, Speaker [[anthony-rota|Anthony Rota]] recognized 98-year-old [[yaroslav-hunka|Yaroslav Hunka]], describing him as a war hero of the [[first-ukrainian-division|First Ukrainian Division]], and lawmakers rose to applaud.[1]

Rota apologized on September 24 after the unit's Nazi affiliation was reported, stating that he alone had arranged the recognition and that neither other members nor the Ukrainian delegation had prior knowledge of it.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writers used the episode to argue that Western legislatures now operate with little concern for the optics of their alliances.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] called the parliamentary ovation "perhaps the most memetic event of the weekend," noting it occurred amid economic strain at home.[1] [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] framed it as confirmation that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] "no longer cares about hiding what they truly are."[1]
