# Economic Freedom Fighters

The Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF) is a South African political party led by [[julius-malema|Julius Malema]], a defector from the [[african-national-congress|African National Congress]].[1]

Around the May 29, 2024 general election, in which the ANC fell below 40 percent of the vote, the EFF was reported to have formed an alliance with [[umkhonto-we-sizwe-party|Umkhonto we Sizwe]], the newly created party of former president [[jacob-zuma|Jacob Zuma]].[1] Badlands commentary noted that the EFF and MKP branded themselves as pro-multipolar and aligned with Beijing and Moscow, while also advocating the seizure of "white-owned land" and the nationalization of mines and banks.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that a nationalization drive would cut against years of work by what he calls the Sovereign Alliance to legally strip mining rights from corrupt foreign-obtained holdings, and therefore represented a subversion rather than a genuine multipolar turn.[1]
