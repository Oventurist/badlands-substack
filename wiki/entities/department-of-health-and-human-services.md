# Department of Health and Human Services

The Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) is the US federal department responsible for public health and social services programs, including federal child care funding streams administered through the states.

## Minnesota funding freeze

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that HHS "has already frozen federal child care funding for [[minnesota|Minnesota]]." The freeze accompanied a broader decision by the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to halt more than $10 billion in federal funding for social services programs in Minnesota, New York, California, Illinois, and Colorado, announced in the same week that Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] unveiled a new assistant attorney general role dedicated to investigating fraud in federally backed programs. The actions follow the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali fraud]] revelations and claims that taxpayers lost billions of dollars in the state.

## The WHO withdrawal (January 2026)

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the United States officially withdrew from the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] effective January 22, 2026, following the one-year notice period that began with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first-day executive order. HHS Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] issued a joint statement declaring that the WHO "abandoned its core mission and acted repeatedly against the interests of the United States," that the organization "obstructed the timely and accurate sharing of critical information that could have saved American lives," and that it "refuses to hand over the American flag that hung in front of it, arguing it has not approved our withdrawal." The statement said future US engagement with the WHO would be limited strictly to effectuating the withdrawal and safeguarding the health and safety of the American people. The exit triggered a financial crisis at the WHO, historically the largest beneficiary of US funding.
