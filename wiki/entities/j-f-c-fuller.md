# J.F.C. Fuller

Major General J.F.C. Fuller (1878–1966) was a British military theorist and innovator of tank warfare whose doctrines are still taught across military colleges to the present day.[1]

Fuller served as Sir Oswald Mosley's lieutenant and liaison with [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] within the [[british-union-of-fascists|British Union of Fascists]], which [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes as rising faster than ever while the British governing class, led by the royal family, backed Nazism.[1] In 1907 Fuller was initiated into [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]]'s Argentum Astrum Thelemic order, and Ehret — who labels him a Satanist — holds that he acted as a back channel between Crowley and Hitler before and during World War II.[1]
