# Urban Moving Systems

Urban Moving Systems was a New Jersey moving company that Badlands Media identifies as a [[mossad]] front company managed by the Israeli national Dominic Suter.[1]

The company's van was the vehicle used by the men known as the [[dancing-israelis|"Dancing Israelis"]], who were observed celebrating as the Twin Towers burned on September 11, 2001 and were subsequently questioned and arrested.[1] Explosive residue, box-cutters, and Arab clothing were reportedly found inside the van registered to Urban Moving Systems, and the five arrested men — Yaron Shmuel, Omer Marmari, Sivan and Paul Kurzberg, and Oded Ellner — are described in the account as Mossad agents.[1]
