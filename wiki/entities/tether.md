# Tether

Tether is described as the largest company in the digital assets industry and is the issuer of USDt, the world's largest stablecoin, which serves over 350 million users.[1]

## USDt on Bitcoin and the Lightning Network

In early 2025 Tether announced the integration of USDt into the Bitcoin ecosystem, covering both Bitcoin's base layer and its layer-2 Lightning Network.[1] The integration is supported by a new Taproot-powered protocol, Taproot Assets, developed by [[lightning-labs|Lightning Labs]], and combines Bitcoin's decentralization and security with the speed and scalability of the Lightning Network.[1]

Once fully integrated, USDt is intended to operate seamlessly on Bitcoin's base layer and on Lightning, enabling high-speed, low-cost transactions.[1] Tether framed the move as redefining how stablecoins can function within the Bitcoin ecosystem and positioned USDt as an essential component of Bitcoin-based financial systems given strong institutional and retail demand for Bitcoin.[1]

## Rumble integration

Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] by [[gmoney|GMONEY]] noted that [[rumble|Rumble]]'s integration of Tether and Bitcoin would give the platform lightning rails, enabling instant micro USD payments supported by the Bitcoin network.[1]

## Investment in Rumble

In December 2024 Tether disclosed a $775 million strategic investment in [[rumble|Rumble]], agreeing to purchase 103.3 million Rumble Class A common shares at $7.50 each and taking a minority position without board participation, with closing expected in the first quarter of 2025 subject to customary conditions and regulatory clearances.[2] Rumble's stock rose 76% to $12.66 after the announcement.[2] Tether CEO [[paolo-ardoino|Paolo Ardoino]] described the partnership as a means of encouraging decentralization and free speech and said the company wanted to explore opportunities with Rumble in cloud services, crypto payment solutions and advertising.[2] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized Tether not as a speculative crypto asset but as a digital token pegged to the U.S. dollar that permits near-frictionless peer-to-peer transacting outside traditional banking institutions, distinguishing it from a central bank digital currency.[2] See [[tether-investment-in-rumble|Tether Investment in Rumble]].[2]
