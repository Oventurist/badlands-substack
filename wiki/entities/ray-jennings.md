# Ray S. Jennings

Ray S. Jennings is described by [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] as a high-profile regime-change specialist for the [[usaid|United States Agency for International Development]].[1]

## WikiLeaks allegations

In a February 2025 item republished by the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], WikiLeaks alleged that Jennings is secretly married to [[jeanne-bourgault|Jeanne Bourgault]], head of the USAID-funded media-development nonprofit [[internews|Internews]].[1] According to WikiLeaks, Jennings has authored numerous papers recommending funding for his wife's methods, an arrangement the outlet characterized as an undisclosed conflict of interest.[1]

WikiLeaks reported that the relationship had only one obscure online reference — a University of Washington alumni magazine profile of Bourgault — and that neither USAID, Internews, nor the 4,291 media outlets connected to Internews had disclosed it.[1]
