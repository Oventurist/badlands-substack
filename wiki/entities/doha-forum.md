# Doha Forum

The Doha Forum is an annual high-profile international conference hosted in the Qatari capital of Doha, at which heads of state, foreign ministers and policy figures discuss global diplomacy, security and economic order.[1]

## December 2023 session

At the December 2023 edition of the forum, Russian foreign minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] addressed participants by video link rather than in person, expressing regret that unspecified "circumstances" had prevented him from travelling to Doha and hearing the discussions directly.[1] In that address Lavrov argued that the 500-year dominance of what he called the "collective West" was ending and being replaced by "a new polycentric world," language that departed from his more customary formulation of a [[multipolar-world|multipolar world]].[1]

Lavrov told the forum that Western hegemony had been "based on a diverse history, including ruthless exploitation of peoples and territories of other countries," and that the West had expected its own model of globalization to preserve its primacy indefinitely.[1] Instead, he said, other states had used the very principles and instruments of Western globalization to "beat the West on its own turf," building economies on national sovereignty and a balance of interests with other countries, so that new centers of economic growth and political influence were shifting the global balance of power "not to the West's liking."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the Doha address as a significant data point in tracking terminology, noting the shift from "multi-polar" to "polycentric" and arguing that the framework Lavrov described — sovereign powers exercising influence within their own orbits — mirrored the Sovereign Alliance thesis and the "Regions of Responsibility" macro discussed by [[just-human|Just Human]].[1] He pointed to Saudi Arabia's posture toward its neighbors as evidence of the pattern already operating.[1]
