# Ewen Cameron

**Dr. Ewen Cameron** was the psychiatrist who ran the CIA-funded [[mk-ultra|MKULTRA]] mind control program, described by Badlands Media as "infamous."[1]

He appears in the article's account of twentieth-century population and behavior engineering through his personal connection to the sex researcher [[alfred-kinsey|Alfred Kinsey]], who counted Cameron among his intimates.[1] The pairing is used to link Rockefeller-funded sex research to the intelligence community's mind-control work, both presented as branches of the same effort to reshape mass behavior — an effort the article also traces through the [[tavistock-institute|Tavistock Institute for Human Relations]].[1]
