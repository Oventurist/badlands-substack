# Anthony Blinken

Anthony Blinken is the U.S. Secretary of State in the Biden administration. In the context of the Badlands Media corpus, he is identified as the official who made a definitive public statement committing the United States to Ukrainian NATO membership, a declaration characterized in the source article as a significant escalation in rhetorical commitment.

## Declaration on Ukrainian NATO Membership

The article cites Blinken's statement at a NATO foreign ministers summit, in which he said unequivocally that "Ukraine WILL become a member of NATO." This language is presented as a marked departure from earlier diplomatic formulations that referenced "discussions," "preparations," or "negotiating the possibility of a MAP [Membership Action Plan] for Ukraine." The shift to categorical certainty—removing the conditional or prospective framing—is characterized in the article as rendering negotiations with Russia completely off the table. The piece argues that a Russian concession on NATO membership would constitute an existential geopolitical defeat, making the categorical commitment an effective barrier to any diplomatic resolution.

The article attributes this shift to deliberate intent among what it terms the "Western Neocons in the Security State, in Congress, and at the State Department," suggesting that these actors are "not only ... aware of this, they are actually hungry for it."

## Role in U.S. Foreign Policy

As Secretary of State, Blinken is the senior U.S. official responsible for articulating American positions within NATO and other multilateral forums. The article places his declaration within a longer continuum of U.S. policy stretching back through the Obama, Bush, and Clinton administrations, linking him thematically to [[victoria-nuland]], who discussed NATO expansion to Ukraine during the Bush era, and to [[william-burns]], whose 2008 cable warned of the consequences of that very policy.

## Related Figures and Concepts

Blinken's statement is analyzed in the article as the culmination of a policy trajectory that the [[burns-cable-2008]] had explicitly warned against. The declaration intersects with the broader Badlands corpus narrative regarding the military industrial complex and the security state, as well as the structural analysis of [[nato]] collective defense obligations under Article 5.
