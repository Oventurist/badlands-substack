# Adolphe Cremieux

Adolphe Crémieux was a 19th-century French statesman and Masonic dignitary who headed the [[alliance-israelite-universelle|Alliance Israelite Universelle]].[1] In the account given by historian [[david-livingstone|David Livingstone]] and relayed by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], Crémieux was also Grand Master of the Rite of Misraim and Grand Commander of the Supreme Council of France, responsible for managing the high degrees of the Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite within the Grand Orient of France.[1]

Crémieux's significance to the story of [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]] lies in his patronage of [[maurice-joly|Maurice Joly]], whose 1864 *Dialogue in Hell Between Machiavelli and Montesquieu* — an attack on Napoleon III — was later plagiarized to fabricate the Protocols.[1]
