# Alan Wilson

**Alan Wilson** is a South Carolina Republican who advanced to the June 23, 2026 run-off in the state's gubernatorial primary.[1]

Following her last-place finish in the six-candidate first round, Representative [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]] held a press conference publicly endorsing Wilson in the run-off, saying she wanted to help him defeat Lieutenant Governor [[pamela-evette|Pamela Evette]], who had been endorsed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Badlands coverage read the endorsement as part of Mace's stated campaign of revenge against Trump rather than as a substantive policy alignment.[1]

## Dispute with Nancy Mace (February 2025)

In her February 10, 2025 House floor speech alleging a rape and sexual-exploitation "ring" operating in her district, Rep. [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]] accused Wilson, as South Carolina Attorney General, of killing the investigation, protecting rapists, and threatening to investigate and arrest her.[2] Wilson's office publicly responded that Mace had never brought the matter to him and called her allegations "categorically false."[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that the confrontation was complicated by family ties: Wilson's adoptive father is Mace's House colleague, Rep. Joe Wilson (R-SC).[2]
