# Alan Wilson

**Alan Wilson** is a South Carolina Republican who advanced to the June 23, 2026 run-off in the state's gubernatorial primary.[1]

Following her last-place finish in the six-candidate first round, Representative [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]] held a press conference publicly endorsing Wilson in the run-off, saying she wanted to help him defeat Lieutenant Governor [[pamela-evette|Pamela Evette]], who had been endorsed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Badlands coverage read the endorsement as part of Mace's stated campaign of revenge against Trump rather than as a substantive policy alignment.[1]
