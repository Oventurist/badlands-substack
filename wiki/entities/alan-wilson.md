# Alan Wilson

Alan Wilson is a South Carolina Republican, long-serving as the state's Attorney General, who ran for governor in 2026 and advanced to the party's run-off election scheduled for June 23, 2026.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports that Representative [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]], eliminated in the first round of the primary with 12 percent of the vote, publicly endorsed Wilson at a press conference, saying she wanted to help him defeat Lieutenant Governor [[pamela-evette|Pamela Evette]], who had been endorsed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. The newsletter presents Mace's endorsement of Wilson as an act of revenge against Trump rather than as an expression of support for Wilson's platform.
