# Jay-Z (Shawn Carter)

Shawn Carter, known professionally as Jay-Z, is a rapper and music-industry executive who appears in Badlands Media coverage of the alleged entertainment-industry abuse network surrounding [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]].[1]

## Allegations reported by Badlands

The singer Jaguar Wright, who has performed on studio recordings with rappers including Jay-Z, has claimed that there are likely thousands of abuse victims harmed by Carter and his wife, Beyoncé Knowles.[1] "That couple is nasty," Wright said, visibly shaking and breaking down in tears, adding that she was already counseling hundreds of victims and believed thousands more existed.[1] These are allegations reported by the Brief and are not established findings.[1]

## Kanye West episode

On November 18, 2016 — ten days after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was first elected president — [[kanye-west|Kanye West]] interrupted his own concert to endorse Trump and address Jay-Z directly: "It's a new world, Hillary. It's a new world, Barack. It's a new world, Jay-Z."[1] West added, "Hey, bro, Jay-Z! I know you have killers, please don't send them at my head. Just call me! Talk to me like a man."[1] The Brief also cites reporting from March 2024, with video evidence, describing Jay-Z arriving at a West concert in Los Angeles seeking a private meeting, and West ignoring him.[1]
