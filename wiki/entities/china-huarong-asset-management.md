# China Huarong Asset Management

China Huarong Asset Management is a major Chinese state-controlled financial institution, described in reporting cited by Badlands Media as one of the country's top banks.[1]

## The Lai Xiaomin scandal

[[lai-xiaomin|Lai Xiaomin]], the company's chairman and its internal Communist Party chief, confessed to taking more than 200 million yuan — approximately $42 million — in cash bribes, which he stored in cabinets inside his apartment.[1] His confession was aired in a documentary on the Chinese state broadcaster CCTV.[1] The case became one of the most prominent financial-corruption prosecutions of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s campaign against graft.[1]

## Significance in Badlands Media commentary

In "The Master Builder," Joe Lange treats the Huarong case as part of a sustained purge of corrupt banking officials within the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], evidence in the article's argument that Xi is fighting a domestic faction analogous to the American [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
