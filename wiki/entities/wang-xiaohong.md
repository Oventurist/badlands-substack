# Wang Xiaohong

Wang Xiaohong is the Chinese security official whose "special service bureau" was identified in January 2026 social-media reporting as the body that carried out arrests of senior military personnel in Beijing.[1]

## Role in the alleged 2026 purge

According to an account quoted by General Michael Flynn and circulated widely on social media, it was Wang Xiaohong's special service bureau that arrested military figures including [[zhong-shaojun|Zhong Shaojun]], with seventeen people reportedly taken in a single operation.[1] The same reporting alleged the simultaneous arrest of [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]] and [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]], along with Zhang's family members.[1]

The use of a civilian security apparatus to detain uniformed officers was read by observers as a sign that [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and the CCP's "red families" had reached a point of no return.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] situated the arrests within a broader multi-year purge in which more than fifty senior military officers and defense-industry executives were placed under investigation or removed from office.[1]
