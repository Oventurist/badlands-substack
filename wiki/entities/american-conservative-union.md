# American Conservative Union

The **American Conservative Union (ACU)** is a long-established American conservative advocacy organization, chaired as of 2023 by [[matt-schlapp|Matt Schlapp]].[1]

The group appears in Badlands Media commentary chiefly through Schlapp's role as a spokesman for movement-conservative conventional wisdom. Discussing the fifteen-ballot January 2023 [[2023-speaker-of-the-house-election|Speaker of the House election]], Schlapp attributed the holdout bloc's resistance to [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] to fiscal conservatives' refusal to follow the Senate Republican strategy associated with [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]], and to a Republican unwillingness to actually reduce federal spending.[1] Badlands contributor [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] treated this as an accurate description of establishment right-of-center thinking while rejecting it as an explanation, arguing instead that the parties operate as a single [[uniparty|UniParty]] serving the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
