# Adelita Grijalva

Adelita Grijalva is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Arizona.[1] On Wednesday, November 12, 2025, immediately after being sworn into office on the House floor, Grijalva became the 218th and final signature on a [[discharge-petition|discharge petition]] to force a floor vote on a bill releasing the [[epstein-files|Epstein files]].[1]

## Discharge petition signature

Grijalva's signature gave the petition the majority required to compel action on the long-sought measure relating to the convicted sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] She joined all other House Democrats and four Republicans: [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] of Kentucky, [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]] of Georgia, [[lauren-boebert|Lauren Boebert]] of Colorado, and [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]] of South Carolina.[1] Democrats in the chamber cheered as she signed, while two Epstein survivors watched from the gallery.[1]
