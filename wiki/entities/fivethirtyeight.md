# FiveThirtyEight

FiveThirtyEight is an American data-journalism outlet best known for its probabilistic election forecasting model, whose "election simulator" was among the most closely watched forecasts of the 2024 United States presidential race.[1]

## Role in the 2024 forecast cycle

In early October 2024, FiveThirtyEight's simulator showed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] with a 47 percent chance of winning in November — his highest reading since August 11 — against a 53 percent chance for [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], down from a peak of 64 percent on September 18.[1] The model had projected Harris as the favorite continuously since August 8, roughly two weeks after [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] ended his re-election campaign and Harris became the Democratic nominee.[1] During that period national surveys had at times shown Harris leading Trump by as much as seven points, a reversal from Trump's earlier polling lead over Biden.[1]

## Badlands critique

Badlands Media contributor Ashe in America treated forecasts of this kind as instruments of what she called "the 50/50 country lie" — the premise, embedded in coverage by [[newsweek|Newsweek]] and Politico alike, that Americans are evenly divided on every issue that matters.[1] In her account this "fake foundational fact" is sustained by "fake polling," which explains why reported Trump gains across key demographics never translate into a corresponding polling advantage: "the narrative sets the polls, not the other way around."[1]
