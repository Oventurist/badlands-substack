# James McHenry

James McHenry served as Acting Attorney General of the United States at the start of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term in January 2025.[1]

## Dismissal of the Jack Smith prosecutors

On Monday, January 27, 2025, McHenry terminated the employment of more than a dozen prosecutors who had worked on former special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s team, gutting the office that had led two historic criminal prosecutions of Trump.[1] A Justice Department spokesman confirmed the firings to the Washington Examiner but did not disclose the names of those let go, saying McHenry had deemed them untrustworthy.[1]

In a letter to the affected lawyers, McHenry wrote: "Given your significant role in prosecuting the President, I do not believe that the leadership of the Department can trust you to assist in implementing the President's agenda faithfully."[1]

Smith had resigned as special counsel before Trump took office, having brought criminal charges against Trump in Florida and in Washington, D.C., relating respectively to Trump's handling of classified documents and his alleged attempts to overturn the 2020 election.[1]
