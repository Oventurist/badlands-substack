# Valerie Jarrett

Valerie Jarrett was a senior adviser to President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and appears in the Badlands account as the counterpart to [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] in an alleged coordinated infiltration of the United States government.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

The connection is drawn from [[qanon|Q]] drop #4750 of September 23, 2020, which described competing foreign factions each seeking direct White House control and concluded that they were "All working together [coordinated][INFILTRATION not invasion][VJ _HA]."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The article reads the bracketed initials as Valerie Jarrett and Huma Abedin, and asserts that both women were connected to [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] — Jarrett serving as Obama's "handler" as Abedin served as [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) A promised follow-up article was to explore the connections among Abedin, Hillary Clinton, Obama and Jarrett in greater depth.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Named as a handler in Q drop 1948

"Bad Actors" quotes the portion of Q drop 1948 of August 28, 2018 reading "All working together [coordinated][infiltration not invasion][VJ _HA]," expanding the initials to Valerie Jarrett and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]].[2] Joe Lange interprets the pairing as identifying the two women as the "handlers" for [[barack-obama|Obama]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] respectively, and states that he will demonstrate their connections to [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] in a subsequent article.[2] The same drop's framing — competing factions each seeking direct White House control, defined as command of the U.S. military plus trillions in overseas transfers of taxpayer funds under the guise of aid, military assistance and climate programs — underpins Lange's contention that what the United States faces is "an insurgency infiltration within our government, not an invasion."[2]

## Q drop #50 and the November 2017 Asia trip

Badlands' "[[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]]" Chapter 6 notes that [[qanon|Q]] drop #50 of November 2, 2017 closed by asking where [[barack-obama|Obama]] and Jarrett were.[3] The chapter answers that the two were travelling around Asia immediately before [[donald-trump|President Trump]] was due to depart for the region to meet several world leaders for the first time in his presidency, and concludes that Obama and his associates were attempting to undermine Trump.[3]
