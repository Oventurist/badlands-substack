It claims that Obama was "installed as president by Prince Alwaleed, who groomed him to become president," and that his administration's policies were designed to empower the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] and other Islamist networks. According to the source, Jarrett's presence in the White House was evidence that the Obama administration was not acting in the interests of the American people but was instead advancing the agenda of hostile foreign networks.

The article does not provide specific details about Jarrett's policy roles, but positions her as part of the broader pattern of Obama administration officials who were allegedly aligned with the Muslim Brotherhood and complicit in the covert operation to arm and fund terrorist organizations in Libya and Syria.
