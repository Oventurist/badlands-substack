# Virginia Giuffre

Virginia Roberts Giuffre is the American woman who accused the former Prince Andrew of sexual assault, becoming the central public accuser in the British royal dimension of the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] affair. In the Badlands corpus she appears through her family's statement following Andrew's February 2026 arrest.

## The accusation against Prince Andrew

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Giuffre alleged Andrew had sex with her when she was 17, according to an extract from her memoir published by *The Guardian*. The allegation, denied by Andrew, underpinned years of scrutiny over his ties to Jeffrey Epstein that culminated in the February 19, 2026 arrest.

## The family statement (February 2026)

After the former prince was arrested and released under investigation, the family of Giuffre — who the Brief reports is said to have died by suicide the previous year — issued a statement: "At last. Today, our broken hearts have been lifted at the news that no one is above the law, not even royalty. On behalf of our sister, Virginia Roberts Giuffre, we extend our gratitude to the UK's [[thames-valley-police|Thames Valley Police]] for their investigation and arrest of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. He was never a prince. For survivors everywhere, Virginia did this for you."
