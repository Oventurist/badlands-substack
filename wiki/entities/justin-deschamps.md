# Justin Deschamps

**Justin Deschamps** is a contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and the author of "AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO" (22 January 2024), an article describing his use of a large language model to map what he presents as the warfare architecture of a globalist agenda.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Readers are directed to more of his work on his own Substack, published under the name Justin Stillness.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Epistemological framing

Deschamps grounds the article in a theory of knowledge rather than in reporting. He argues that the nature of truth is coherence — that a properly identified fact in one area of reality corresponds to a properly identified fact in another — and that this principle is known in epistemology as "coherence."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) From this he reasons that large language models cannot operate in a world of pure fiction, because nothing can fully occupy a world of lies: all lies are themselves woven with threads of truth, so an intellectually honest and rigorous mind will inevitably formulate true facts when studying a subject.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) This, he writes, is why people "wake up" at all.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

He describes the mind as a "truth discovering machine" that naturally generates accurate impressions of reality by virtue of its information-absorbing abilities.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The obstacle is not perception but social pressure: invoking the [[asch-conformity-experiments|Asch Conformity Experiments]], he argues that a person who correctly counts five apples on a table will suppress that true perception if the surrounding group insists there are six.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) He identifies this as the mechanism cults use to prevent members from placing value on true facts they naturally encounter.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Applied to the [[cabal|cabal]], the argument becomes strategic: the cabal knows it cannot really hide the truth, but it can either get people not to look — through distraction, time-wasting, video games, media consumption and sports — or, when they do look and take possession of a piece of truth, use the power of culture to make them think it is not valid.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The same limit applies to AI engineers who might be driven to make a model serve the cabal's agenda: they cannot ignore reality itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Use of the Toronto Protocols

Deschamps writes that he has studied the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]] for a very long time, describing them as a candid resource for investigating the warfare waged by what the documents themselves allege is a globalist, satanic cabal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) As the war escalated around him, his appreciation for the records grew because they provide an accurate information set for mapping the enemy's battle from a military standpoint.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

His method rests on separating a campaign into its strategic, operational and tactical layers, which he calls one of the simplest ways to understand the architecture of warfare.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Because [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] is by nature ever-covert and integrated into daily life, he finds this architecture especially practical as a lens for uncovering how people are being attacked.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

He fed ChatGPT passages from the Protocols and instructed it to explain them using those fundamentals of combat architecture, then fine-tuned the outputs using his own understanding of the war, its known adversaries and the methods he had personally witnessed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Owing to what he calls his long-standing concentration on the [[war-on-the-family|war on the family]], his initial prompts were passages focused on the 6.6.6.'s fixation on the family's annihilation as a strategic requirement for the New World Order's implementation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Conclusions

Deschamps closes on the theme of collective situational awareness. Identifying how people are assaulted by fifth-generation warfare "by an ancient death cult" is complex, he writes, because the attacks on families and on the ability to think independently are ever-covert and incorporated into daily life.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Using AI to gather the exposed warfare confers a distinct advantage: "We can make visible that which has been designed to remain invisible."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) From that position of collective situational awareness, he argues, collective action becomes possible — action inspired by everything the enemy is trying to hide.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
