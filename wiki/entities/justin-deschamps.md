# Justin Deschamps

Justin Deschamps is a writer and [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor who also publishes independently on Substack at justinstillness.substack.com. He is the author of the January 22, 2024 essay AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO, in which he documents a long experiment using [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to analyse [[toronto-protocols|the Toronto Protocols]] through the framework of military strategy.

Deschamps's writing in that piece is grounded in epistemology, and he argues explicitly from the coherence theory of truth: because reality is coherent, a fact correctly identified in one domain will correspond to facts identified elsewhere. He treats the human mind as a "truth discovering machine" and cites the Asch Conformity Experiments to explain how social pressure suppresses accurate individual perception — a mechanism he attributes to cults and, by extension, to the [[cabal|cabal]].

He describes himself as having studied the Toronto Protocols "for a very long time" and as having a "long-standing concentration on the war on the family," which shaped the initial prompts he supplied to the AI. His analytical method divides a campaign into strategic, operational and tactical layers, a lens he considers especially suited to exposing [[5th-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]]. The article closes with his call for "collective situational awareness" as the precondition for effective collective action. [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] assisted him with the piece.
