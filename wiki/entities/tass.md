# TASS

TASS is Russia's state news agency and one of the principal channels through which statements by Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and other senior officials reach both domestic and international audiences.[1] In the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] corpus it is frequently cited as a primary-source outlet for Russian government positions that Western wire services either omit or paraphrase unfavorably.[1]

## Coverage cited in June 2024

TASS supplied two reports that anchored the June 21, 2024 edition of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1] The first carried Putin's declaration, following negotiations with North Korean leader [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]], that the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] sanctions regime against [[north-korea|North Korea]] — which he described as "inspired by the United States and its allies" — "must be revised."[1] In the same remarks Putin said that propaganda cliches reproduced by Western countries could no longer disguise their aggressive geopolitical plans in North-East Asia, and that Moscow and Pyongyang would continue to oppose "the practice of sanctions strangulation as a tool that the West is accustomed to using to maintain its hegemony."[1]

The second TASS report covered Putin's meeting in Hanoi with Vietnamese National Assembly Chairman [[tran-thanh-man|Tran Thanh Man]], at which Putin praised parliamentary cooperation between Russia and [[vietnam|Vietnam]] as a foundation for security, economic and humanitarian ties.[1] A third TASS item, on Kim Jong Un's gift of a set of dogs to Putin, was cited in passing by Badlands commentary.[1]
