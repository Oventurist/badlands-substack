# Mike Quigley

Mike Quigley is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Illinois (D-IL).[1]

## February 2023 spy balloon comments

Appearing on CNN's "CNN Newsroom" on Friday, February 3, 2023, Quigley said that the Chinese spy balloon then transiting American airspace was a serious issue, but cautioned that "the low orbit Chinese satellites have been there for years, they're there all the time."[1] He added, "I don't want the American people to think this is something new and that all of a sudden we have a concern that we didn't have before."[1] Quigley also argued that Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Tony Blinken]] should not have canceled his planned trip to China over the balloon, saying that "if you're angry about something, don't talk about it through the press, talk about it nose-to-nose and the ramifications, let them know we're serious about matters like this."[1] The remarks were reported by [[breitbart|Breitbart]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized Quigley's framing as "nothing to see here," conceding that low-orbit Chinese satellites had indeed long been overhead but asking whether that meant Americans should turn a blind eye, and whether United States airspace had become meaningless.[1] DeLarme suggested the balloons might serve purposes that satellites could not fulfill, and argued it was "not a good look" for a US politician to play devil's advocate for the nation's most competent adversary — adding that the remarks amounted to telling the public one was on the [[chinese-communist-party|CCP]] payroll without saying so, and that Illinois politics produced "a special breed."[1] The item appeared alongside the wider coverage of the [[chinese-spy-balloon-incident-2023|Chinese spy balloon incident]].[1]
