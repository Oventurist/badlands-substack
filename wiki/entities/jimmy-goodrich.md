# Jimmy Goodrich

Jimmy Goodrich is a fellow at the University of California Institute on Global Conflict and Cooperation and the author of a study on Chinese economic strategy.[1]

On July 30, 2024, Goodrich released a report — summarized by ZeroHedge and excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] — analyzing official Chinese Communist Party speeches and policy documents to assess Beijing's progress in implementing what he termed a [[fortress-economy|"fortress economy"]] policy across critical sectors.[1] The paper argued that the strategy was "designed to bolster national self-sufficiency and resilience against external shocks, and ultimately allow the nation to withstand 'extreme situations' including protracted armed conflict."[1]

Goodrich's report tied the shift to a series of global upheavals — rising US–China tensions, the Russian invasion of Ukraine, and the supply-chain disruptions of the COVID-19 pandemic — and identified [[dual-circulation|dual-economic circulation]] as the central organizing concept, alongside food and energy security, supply-chain robustness, civil defense mobilization, and strategic reserve infrastructure.[1] The findings were attributed to the leadership of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1]
