# Real America's Voice

Real America's Voice is a conservative American broadcast network best known for carrying [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s program The War Room and for field reporting on immigration and border issues.[1]

## Border coverage

The network's correspondent [[ben-bergquam|Ben Bergquam]] reported from Yuma, Arizona on May 15, 2023, describing hundreds of migrants crossing overnight from countries including Moldova, Egypt and Sudan, and reporting that [[us-border-patrol|Border Patrol]] agents were retrieving tagged luggage for illegal border crossers and delivering it to their destinations.[1] Bannon described the practice on The War Room as an "illegal alien concierge service," a characterization picked up by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news brief coverage.[1]
