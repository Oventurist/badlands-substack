# Joe O'Dea

Joe O'Dea was the Republican nominee for United States Senate from Colorado in 2022.[1]

## 2022 Senate primary

O'Dea did not seek the nomination through the [[2022-colorado-republican-state-assembly|Colorado Republican state assembly]], instead petitioning directly onto the primary ballot, a route available under Colorado law that allows candidates to bypass the party's voting delegates.[1] His primary opponent, State Representative [[ron-hanks|Ron Hanks]], had taken 39% of the assembly vote and was the only candidate to qualify from a crowded field.[1]

O'Dea won the primary and went on to lose the general election. Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the race as part of a pattern in which candidates who refused to face Republican voters at assembly, and who ran little visible campaign, nonetheless defeated [[america-first-movement|America First]] candidates who commanded the enthusiasm and public support of the party base, only to lose in November.[1]
