# Jay Inslee

Jay Inslee is an American Democratic politician who served as governor of Washington state, and in that role oversaw the state's security preparations for the 2024 general election.[1]

## 2024 election security activation

In a news release issued the Friday before Election Day 2024, Inslee announced that members of the Washington [[national-guard|National Guard]] were being placed on standby as a precaution against election-related civil unrest, and addressed the recent [[2024-pacific-northwest-ballot-box-fires|ballot drop box fires]] in the state.[1] "The southwest region of Washington state has already experienced specific instances of election-related unrest," he said.[1]

Inslee declined to disclose how many troops would be activated on Election Day, but said they would be available to support law enforcement from Monday through Thursday.[1] He added that the [[department-of-homeland-security|US Department of Homeland Security]] had warned that threats to "election infrastructure" remained high, and framed the activation in terms of preparedness: "Based upon general and specific information and concerns regarding the potential for violence or other unlawful activity related to the 2024 general election, I want to ensure we are fully prepared to respond to any potential additional civil unrest."[1]
