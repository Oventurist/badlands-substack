# Jia Cobb

Jia Cobb is a United States District Judge who presided over litigation brought by Democratic lawmakers against [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] over denied attempts to visit immigration detention facilities.[1]

## ICE detention access case

In October 2025, ICE informed Judge Cobb that, as a consequence of the government shutdown, no law remained requiring the Trump administration to accommodate congressional visits, since that requirement had been contained in expired government funding laws.[1] The agency told the court it was funding operations through appropriations made in the [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|One Big Beautiful Bill Act]], which imposes no congressional access obligation.[1] The dispute is covered further at [[ice-congressional-access-suspension|ICE congressional access suspension]].[1]

## Kalshi election-contracts ruling (2024)

In September 2024, Cobb issued a one-page order siding with the prediction-market startup [[kalshi|Kalshi]] and throwing out a 2023 decision by the [[commodity-futures-trading-commission|Commodity Futures Trading Commission]] that had blocked the company from listing contracts on control of the U.S. Congress.[2] The ruling cleared the way for Americans to place legal bets on the outcome of congressional elections and was described as potentially opening the door to legalized wagering on U.S. elections more broadly.[2] Regulators promptly filed an emergency motion seeking a two-week delay while they considered an appeal.[2]
