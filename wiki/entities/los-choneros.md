# Los Choneros

Los Choneros is a criminal organization involved in drug trafficking that the United States designated a Foreign Terrorist Organization in 2025.[1]

## Terrorist designation and FBI posture

In September 2025, [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] announced that the bureau was putting "ALL cartels on notice," stating that with Los Choneros and [[los-lobos|Los Lobos]] designated as Foreign Terrorist Organizations, "their drug money pipelines are drying up."[1] Patel framed the effort as offensive rather than defensive, saying the FBI intended to eliminate drug and cartel trafficking "at the source," and warned that "if you poison our communities, the FBI will find you, and we will end your criminal empire."[1]

The designation formed part of a broader Trump administration campaign against narcotics-trafficking organizations, which included military options under consideration against cartels operating in [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1] See also [[foreign-terrorist-organization|Foreign Terrorist Organization]].
