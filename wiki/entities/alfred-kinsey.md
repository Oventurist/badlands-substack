The article describes Kinsey's research in the harshest terms, alleging that he and fellow pedophiles sexually abused 2,000 infants and children in the course of it, and identifies him as a forefather of the modern "Gender Queer" and transsexual movements as well as a figure connected to [[eugenics]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] adds several biographical claims: that a year or two before his death Kinsey circumcised himself with a pocketknife; that among his intimates was Dr. [[ewen-cameron|Ewen Cameron]], the CIA-funded mind control doctor who ran the [[mk-ultra|MKULTRA]] program; and that another apparent influence on him was the occultist [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], "the Great Beast," known in the press as the wickedest man alive.[1]
