# Ilie Bolojan

Ilie Bolojan is a Romanian politician who served as Prime Minister of [[romania|Romania]] at the head of a pro-EU minority government until its collapse in May 2026.[1]

## Government and fall

Bolojan's government was formed roughly ten months before its collapse, assembled explicitly to counter gains by far-right parties.[1] It fell when a no-confidence motion passed with 281 votes in favor against a 233-vote threshold, after the [[social-democratic-party-romania|Social Democrats]] joined far-right opposition groups including the [[alliance-for-the-union-of-romanians|Alliance for the Union of Romanians]] to bring it down.[1] Bolojan remained in a limited interim role pending President [[nicusor-dan|Nicusor Dan]]'s nomination of a successor and parliamentary approval of a new government.[1]

Badlands commentary attributed the collapse to austerity measures demanded by Brussels colliding with Romanian domestic political realities.[1]
