# Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport

Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport, commonly referred to as Reagan National, is the commercial airport serving Washington, D.C., from the Virginia side of the Potomac River. Because of its proximity to the seat of the U.S. federal government, the surrounding airspace is among the most tightly restricted and monitored in the country, a point Badlands commentators stressed when a military aircraft was involved in an accident there.[1]

## 2025 midair collision

On the evening of January 29, 2025, an [[american-airlines|American Airlines]] regional flight operated by PSA Airlines collided in midair with a U.S. Army Sikorsky UH-60 helicopter while on approach to Runway 33 at the airport, at around 9 p.m. local time.[1] Rescue operations were concentrated near the Woodrow Wilson Memorial Bridge, and the [[federal-aviation-administration|FAA]] and [[national-transportation-safety-board|NTSB]] launched investigations.[1] The event is covered in detail at [[2025-potomac-midair-collision]].
