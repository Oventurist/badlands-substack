# Nike

Nike is an American athletic apparel and footwear corporation cited in Badlands Media commentary as an example of a consumer brand aligning its marketing with progressive social messaging under [[esg|ESG]] pressure.[1]

## Trans influencer sponsorship (2023)

In his April 2023 essay "Get Woke, Go Broke," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that he told his 13- and 15-year-old sons that the trans person famous on TikTok was now sponsored by Nike and [[bud-light|Bud Light]].[1] Their response — "who?", "why would you think we'd know who you are talking about?", and "nobody we know knows who that is" — surprised him, and he offered it as evidence that such figures may carry far less cultural weight than audiences are told.[1]

Carlson drew a broader inference from the exchange: that the bulk of Generation Z may deserve more credit than commentators give it, and that mentally well young people raised in religiously conservative households appear largely insulated from the messaging, with indoctrinated parents rather than corporate sponsorship being the decisive risk factor.[1]

## Corporate logic

Carlson placed the sponsorship within his general argument that corporations under ESG pressure upset the majority of their customer base in order to appease a tiny fraction of people who are not customers at all — behavior he said defies all logic and all business sense.[1] In his framing, brands accept this cost because refusal carries a heavier one: exclusion from funds managed by [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] and [[state-street|State Street]], and expulsion from the elite "club."[1]
