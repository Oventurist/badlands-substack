# William Howard Taft

William Howard Taft is described in the Badlands Media series "The Men Behind the Curtain" as one of the most prominent members of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society, and as the only man ever to serve as both President of the United States and Chief Justice of the United States Supreme Court.[1]

His father, [[alphonso-taft|Alphonso Taft]], was a founding member of the Order and served as Secretary of War under President Rutherford B. Hayes.[1] As president, Taft appointed his fellow Bonesman [[henry-stimson|Henry Stimson]] as Secretary of War in 1911, an episode [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]] used to illustrate the Order's "vertical chain" of self-perpetuating appointments.[1] Ryan DeLarme places the Tafts among the family lines that made [[yale-university|Yale]]'s senior societies a durable recruiting ground for careers in government, law and finance.[1]
