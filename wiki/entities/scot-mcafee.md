# Scot McAfee

Scot McAfee is the Fulton County Superior Court judge presiding over the Georgia election-interference case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his co-defendants.[1]

## Order on the Willis misconduct allegations

In January 2024, McAfee ordered District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] to respond in writing to accusations that she had hired her romantic partner, [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]], as a top prosecutor on the case. He set a February 2 deadline for the written response and scheduled a hearing on the matter for February 15.[1]

The order followed a motion by co-defendant [[mike-roman|Mike Roman]], who contended that Wade's appointment made the indictment "fatally defective" and sought both dismissal of the charges and the removal of the Fulton County District Attorney's office from the prosecution. By requiring a formal answer and setting a hearing date, McAfee placed the conduct of the prosecutors themselves — rather than the underlying election allegations — at the center of the case's early 2024 proceedings.[1]
