# William Pepper

William Pepper is an attorney who, together with co-counsel Laurie Dusek, has represented [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]] in efforts to overturn his conviction for the 1968 killing of Senator [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]].[1]

In a 2011 filing that led to a further appeal for Sirhan, Pepper and Dusek maintained that the bullet that struck Kennedy's neck was not fired from Sirhan's gun, and that the bullet held in evidence had been switched for another.[1] The claim rests in part on the autopsy findings of coroner Thomas Noguchi, who placed the muzzle of the fatal shot one to one and a half inches from the skin behind Kennedy's right ear, while witnesses put Sirhan at least eighteen inches away and in front of the senator.[1]
