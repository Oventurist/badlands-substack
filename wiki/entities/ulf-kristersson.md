# Ulf Kristersson

Ulf Kristersson is the Prime Minister of [[sweden|Sweden]] who completed his country's accession to the [[nato|North Atlantic Treaty Organization]] in March 2024.[1]

## NATO accession

On Thursday, March 7, 2024, Kristersson traveled to Washington and personally handed Sweden's final accession documentation to the United States government, the last formal step in a drawn-out ratification process that required the backing of every existing member of the alliance.[1] U.S. Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] received the documents, remarking, "Good things come to those who wait."[1] With the deposit of the instruments, Sweden became NATO's 32nd member, two years after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine prompted a reversal of the Scandinavian country's long-standing policy of non-alignment.[1] Under the alliance's common defence guarantee, an attack on Sweden is thereafter treated as an attack on all members.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the Kristersson visit primarily as a narrative event rather than a military one, arguing that the addition of a "relatively inert military into a relatively inert kinetic power projection bloc" mattered less than the framing used to sell it.[1] He contended that the "security rethink" language favoured by Western outlets read to a tense public as provocation rather than defence, and that Russia had warned against Swedish accession for years.[1]
