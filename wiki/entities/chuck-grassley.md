# Chuck Grassley

Chuck Grassley is a United States Senator and Chairman of the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]]. Badlands Media identifies him as the recipient of the evidence assembled by [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] and [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] in the House, and as a central figure in the anticipated transparency and prosecution phase.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## 2023 letter on FBI handling of Biden-Ukraine allegations

In a letter released in October 2023, Grassley revealed that the [[fbi|FBI]] had gathered evidence from more than 40 informants over years of investigation into alleged corrupt Biden family dealings in [[ukraine]], material that [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] and FBI officials sought to undermine.[13] Reporting by Just the News cited alongside the disclosure described a prosecutor as "livid" over the bureau's reluctance to pursue what were characterized as credible allegations — the same subject matter for which Democrats had impeached [[donald-trump|Trump]] for asking about.[13] Badlands framing contrasted the treatment of the Biden family with January 6 protesters imprisoned for participating in what it called a "fed-filled" march on the Capitol.[13]

## Oversight powers

The article enumerates the Senate Judiciary Committee's oversight functions: overseeing the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]], considering executive and judicial nominations, reviewing pending legislation, holding hearings and investigations, and exercising legislative jurisdiction.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Its legislative jurisdiction covers proposed constitutional amendments and legislation on federal criminal law, human rights law, immigration, intellectual property, antitrust law and internet privacy.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The author observes that these align closely with the Trump agenda: ending the two-tier justice system, an executive order on crimes against humanity, ending illegal immigration, protecting American intellectual property, breaking up monopolies, and an internet bill of rights.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Grassley's authority over the DOJ is described as especially significant given the extent to which the author believes Obama corrupted and weaponized that department.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The treason evidence

Grassley was one of the three legislators — with Goodlatte and Gowdy — whose request prompted Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] to launch the [[john-huber|Huber]] investigation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article asserts that Grassley is sitting on evidence of treason from the Goodlatte and Gowdy investigations, evidence connected to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] and [[doug-band|Doug Band]] and the alleged money-laundering operation running through [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

The author predicts Grassley will be placed at center stage to expose "treason at the highest levels," which he argues explains the extreme efforts to frame and remove Trump and to steal the 2020 and 2024 elections.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) He also expects Grassley to help transform the government through new legislation intended to safeguard the future.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## March 2026 DHS oversight hearing

At the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] hearing of March 3, 2026, Grassley's critical posture toward Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] was described by Badlands contributor Ashe in America as a surprise, with his opening statement setting a combative tone and his questioning going harder still.[2] The hearing was dominated less by Democratic complaints than by Republican attacks on the Secretary and on the conduct of immigration enforcement.[2]

## Release of Jack Smith records (2026)

As Chairman of the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]], Grassley released internal Justice Department communications concerning Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s office.[3] Those records formed the basis of a request by the [[center-to-advance-security-in-america|Center to Advance Security in America]] that Acting DNI [[william-pulte|William Pulte]] review the security clearances of Smith's former team; as described in reporting, the materials allege that members of Smith's office granted access to classified information without required "need-to-know" authorization and that internal concerns were raised about security procedures involving a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility.[3]

## Credit Suisse Nazi-era accounts (2026)

In February 2026 Grassley announced that an independent investigation had uncovered 890 previously undisclosed accounts at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] with potential links to the Nazi regime during World War II, held by entities including the German Foreign Office, the SS, and a German arms manufacturer.[4] The probe, conducted by [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]], also found that the bank expropriated funds from Jewish account holders and transferred them to Nazi clients, and developed further detail on schemes assisting Nazis fleeing to [[argentina|Argentina]].[4]

The underlying congressional inquiry had been initiated by Democratic Senator [[sheldon-whitehouse|Sheldon Whitehouse]] at the urging of the [[simon-wiesenthal-center|Simon Wiesenthal Center]], with Grassley serving as co-chair on the matter.[4] After learning that Credit Suisse had likely fired Barofsky in December 2022 to conceal the scandal's depth, Grassley and Whitehouse intervened and demanded his reinstatement, which [[ubs|UBS]] carried out following its 2023 acquisition of the bank.[4]


## Awaiting the Barofsky report (2026)

In April 2026 Badlands noted that a report from independent investigator [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]] on Nazi-era and money-laundering accounts at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] and its acquirer [[ubs|UBS]] was expected to be delivered to Grassley in the Senate later that year.[5] The report was awaited against a backdrop of allegations that UBS was blocking Barofsky from obtaining internal documents.[5]

## Report on Jack Smith's access to congressional communications (July 2026)

As chair of the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]], Grassley revealed in July 2026 that former special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s team had obtained and reviewed text messages from 44 members of [[us-congress|Congress]] — among them Grassley himself, 19 other senators, and top House Republicans including then-GOP Leader [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]], along with some Democrats — during the investigations into [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s role in the January 6 Capitol attack and the Mar-a-Lago classified documents case.[6] The messages had been sent to senior [[white-house|White House]] officials in the closing weeks of Trump's first term and reached prosecutors through subpoenaed White House records turned over by the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]] in August 2023.[6]

Grassley released a joint report with [[ron-johnson|Sen. Ron Johnson]], drawing on whistleblower information and internal Justice Department records, alleging that Smith's prosecutors bypassed standard document-filtering protocols and directly accessed communications outside the scope of the probes.[6] He announced plans to compel Smith to testify before the committee and framed the affair as evidence of prosecutorial overreach by the Biden-era Justice Department.[6]

## The Biden classified documents inquiry

Grassley and Senator [[ron-johnson]] jointly pressed the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]] on the handling of classified records tied to [[joe-biden]], sending a February 24 letter asking how and when the agency learned that records had been moved to Boston.[7] Acting Archivist [[debra-steidel-wall]] responded that NARA learned of the transfer on November 3, 2022 and revealed that nine boxes had been retrieved from the office of Biden attorney [[patrick-moore]] — a figure not previously public.[7] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] praised the pair for methodically building a documentary record on the [[biden-classified-documents-scandal|Biden classified documents scandal]].[7]

## Questions on immigration parole for an alleged Trump assassination plotter (August 2024)

In August 2024, Grassley posed a series of questions to the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] seeking clarity on the arrest of Pakistani national [[asif-raza-merchant|Asif Raza Merchant]], accused of plotting to assassinate high-ranking U.S. officials allegedly including former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[8] Grassley's concern centered on the fact that Merchant had been flagged on a federal watchlist yet was recently granted immigration parole.[8] In an August 9 letter to Secretary [[alejandro-mayorkas|Alejandro Mayorkas]], Grassley cited media reports that Merchant had been interviewed by the Joint Terrorism Task Force on arrival in the United States on April 13 because he was flagged as a "Lookout Qualified Person of Interest."[8]

## Declassification of the Clinton annex (July 2025)

On Monday, July 21, 2025, Grassley revealed new findings from the long-sought document known as the [[clinton-annex|Clinton annex]], showing major investigative failures by the [[fbi|FBI]] during its probe of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s private email server and her mishandling of highly classified information.[9] The annex is an appendix to the Justice Department Office of Inspector General's 2018 report on the Clinton email investigation, newly declassified at Grassley's request by Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] and FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]].[9]

Grassley had pressed for the document's release since 2018, including requests to former Attorneys General Bill Barr and to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], reiterating the request earlier in 2025 and crediting Bondi and Patel for honoring it.[9] Commenting on the disclosure that the bureau never fully examined thumb drives of hacked [[state-department|State Department]] data including messages linked to [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], Grassley told the Washington Examiner that the document "shows an extreme lack of effort and due diligence in the FBI's investigation of former Secretary Clinton's email usage and mishandling of highly classified information."[9]

## The Clinton email classified annex

As Senate Judiciary Committee Chairman, Grassley sought a classified annex to a years-old inspector general probe of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s improper email server.[10] The annex is believed to show that credible information about possible wrongdoing was intentionally ignored by the [[fbi|FBI]].[10] Reporting in July 2025 identified it as one of two sealed evidentiary tranches — the other identified by [[john-durham|John Durham]] in his final report — whose declassification by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] could significantly boost the FBI's [[grand-conspiracy-probe|"grand conspiracy" probe]] opened under Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]].[10] Both had remained sealed for nearly a decade and are highly classified because they reveal sensitive intelligence-gathering methods.[10]

## Demand for Russia sanctions (May 2025)

In late May 2025 Grassley publicly pressed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to sanction Russia after the Kremlin launched a massive drone and missile attack against Ukraine over the weekend — the largest aerial assault of the war to that point.[11] Writing on his X account on Monday 26 May, the fed-up senator demanded punitive action against Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]: "I've had enuf of Putin killing innocent ppl. Pres Trump Take action AT LEAST SANCTIONS."[11] Grassley had previously criticised Putin and suggested that Russia was playing the United States "as a patsy" as it continued its assault on Ukraine.[11] Badlands commentators read the episode as an instance of [[kayfabe|kayfabe]] escalation rather than a genuine rupture in the Trump–Putin relationship.[11]

## Release of the FBI informant form (July 2023)

On Thursday, July 20, 2023, Grassley released an unclassified FBI document in which a confidential human source laid out an alleged bribery scheme involving [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and the Ukrainian gas company [[burisma|Burisma]], which the source said had hired Hunter to gain access to his then–vice president father.[12] The disclosure came in the same week that [[james-comer|James Comer]] announced plans for multiple criminal referrals against Hunter Biden.[12]

## Release of the Biden Ukraine FD-1023s

Grassley, as Senate Judiciary Chairman, joined Senator [[ron-johnson|Ron Johnson]] of Wisconsin in releasing two new FBI FD-1023s — records of reporting from the bureau's confidential human sources — focused on allegations of Biden family corruption in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[14] The records match closely a memo Grassley released in 2023 containing similar claims, and he has pressed to determine why the [[fbi|FBI]] apparently failed to investigate them, telling Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] at a September 2025 hearing that "to date, the FBI has never answered Congress whether they investigated the text messages, audio files and financial records referenced in that 1023."[14]

## FBI destruction of CCP election-interference memo (2025)

In June 2025, Grassley alleged in a letter to the [[fbi|FBI]] that the bureau under former director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] had ordered the destruction of an intelligence document alleging that the Chinese Communist Party plotted to interfere in the 2020 presidential election.[15] The FBI had recently turned over to Grassley an intelligence report from its Albany Field Office alleging that China planned to interfere by creating fake mail-in ballots for Democratic candidate Joe Biden.[15] A statement noted that "Chairman Grassley is in receipt of an FBI document responsive to a request he made based on legally protected whistleblower disclosures," and that he was seeking additional information about the destruction decision.[15] Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] characterized the episode as evidence that the CCP foreign-interference story runs far deeper than commonly understood, calling it "coordinated disclosure."[15]

## October 2025 release of Arctic Frost subpoenas

On Wednesday, October 29, 2025, Grassley released additional FBI files showing that hundreds more Republican groups and individuals had been swept into what he called a "fishing expedition" by special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]].[16] At a press conference alongside other GOP senators, he made public the 197 subpoenas issued as part of the [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]] probe, which he said became "the vehicle by which FBI agents and DOJ prosecutors could improperly investigate the entire Republican political apparatus."[16]

Grassley said he had obtained the material "through legally protected whistleblower disclosures," and detailed that the subpoenas went to 34 individuals and 163 businesses, including financial institutions, seeking records and communications related to more than 430 individuals and organizations, all apparently aimed at Republicans.[16] He noted requests sent to the late conservative figure [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]]'s [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] and the [[republican-attorneys-general-association|Republican Attorneys General Association]].[16] The release intensified calls for the impeachment of Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]], with Senator [[mike-lee|Mike Lee]] publicly raising the question the same day.[16]

## Named in Patrick Byrne's 2006 recruitment claim

In the inaugural issue of [[capitol-times-magazine|Capitol Times Magazine]] in June 2023, [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]] wrote that in 2006 the Senate Judiciary Committee's chairman told him that the committee's power "transcended everything else within government," that systemic corruption was taking over the U.S. government, and that the country needed him to disrupt it.[17] Byrne named a bipartisan group of senators who engaged him for that purpose, a group he said included Grassley.[17]

## Secret Service oversight (July 2024)

At the Senate hearing of July 30, 2024 into the attempted assassination of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Butler, Pennsylvania, Grassley went hard at the communication failures that marked the [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] response on July 13, questioning Acting Director [[ronald-rowe|Ronald Rowe]] and FBI Deputy Director [[paul-abbate|Paul Abbate]].[21]

## Iran-backed assassination plot disclosures (September 2024)

## FD-1023 floor disclosure (June 2023)

Grassley added that the FBI record indicated then-Vice President Biden "may have been involved in Burisma employing Hunter Biden," referring to [[burisma|Burisma]] and its owner [[mykola-zlochevsky|Mykola Zlochevsky]].[24] Badlands commentators responded with the refrain "Trust Grassley," criticized the FBI's redactions to the document sent to Congress as lacking any plausible national-security justification, and read the disclosure alongside [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s federal indictment as evidence that investigations were converging.[24]
