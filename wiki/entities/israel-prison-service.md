# Israel Prison Service

The Israel Prison Service is the agency operating [[israel|Israel]]'s prison facilities, including those holding Palestinian security detainees. In connection with National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]]'s proposal to surround such prisons with Nile crocodiles as an escape deterrent, the service sent officials to a crocodile farm to examine the practical aspects of the plan.[1]

The proposal became legally possible after Environmental Protection Minister [[idit-silman|Idit Silman]] reclassified the Nile crocodile into a category permitting security agencies to keep the animals under specified conditions, though reporting indicated no crocodiles have been deployed at prison facilities.[1]
