# Israel Prison Service

The Israel Prison Service is the agency responsible for operating [[israel|Israel]]'s correctional facilities, including those holding Palestinian security detainees. As reported in the Badlands Brief, the service sent officials to a crocodile farm to examine the practical aspects of a proposal, backed by National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], to surround such prisons with Nile crocodiles as a deterrent against escape attempts. The proposal advanced further when Environmental Protection Minister [[idit-silman|Idit Silman]] reclassified the species to permit security agencies to keep the animals, over the objections of the [[israel-nature-and-parks-authority|Israel Nature and Parks Authority]] and the ministry's legal adviser. The concept was said to be inspired by Florida's [[alligator-alcatraz|"Alligator Alcatraz"]].
