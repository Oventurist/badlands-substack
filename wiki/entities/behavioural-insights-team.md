# Behavioural Insights Team

The Behavioural Insights Team, popularly known as the United Kingdom's "Nudge Unit," is a behavioural science organisation that designs psychological interventions intended to steer public behaviour.[1]

## Net zero recommendations

In early 2023, Reclaim the Net reported that the Nudge Unit had recommended a range of online psychological manipulations targeting people while they shop and travel, in service of pushing a "net zero society."[1] The team framed its problem as part of the climate change narrative, in which achieving "net zero" means eliminating greenhouse gas emissions.[1] According to the report, the unit showed little concern that the route to that goal ran through direct manipulation of people — specifically online, via prompts, or "nudges," toward choices that are not really their own but serve the agenda.[1] The choices at issue covered everyday life: what people wear, what and how much they eat, how they travel to work, whether that job is "climate-friendly," and how and where they travel generally, including holiday destinations.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that a proposal aligned with truth is generally accepted by those humble before the truth, while those who fear it — through delusion or self-interest — reject it; an agenda founded on deception, he wrote, cannot persuade truth-centric people because it is built on unreality.[1] He characterized the climate change agenda as based in fraud, a poorly conceived argument supported by cherry-picked data, and offered as an indirect test the question of whether one is permitted to challenge the basis of the agenda and its evidence — answering no.[1] Deschamps described the "nudgers" as deceivers and manipulators by definition and as the primary feature of their purpose, using manipulative means to push compliance with the climate agenda.[1] He concluded that society is being nudged throughout, partly because the capacity for critical thought has been so diluted, and that mastery of one's own mind plus expert knowledge of the climate evidence would serve as "a shield and sword" to cut through the lies.[1]
