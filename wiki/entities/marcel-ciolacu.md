# Marcel Ciolacu

Marcel Ciolacu is a Romanian politician of the Social Democratic Party who served as Prime Minister of Romania and stood as his party's candidate in the 2024 presidential election.[1]

## 2024 presidential election

Ciolacu was defeated in the first round of the November 2024 presidential election by [[calin-georgescu|Călin Georgescu]], a result that left the Social Democratic Party without a candidate in the runoff for the first time in Romania's 35-year post-communist history.[1] The runoff, scheduled for December 8, 2024, was to pit Georgescu against the progressive Save Romania Union candidate [[elena-lasconi|Elena Lasconi]].[1]

Following the upset, Romanian officials sought a rerun of the vote, citing a report by the Bucharest think tank Expert Forum alleging that Georgescu had benefited from "preferential treatment" on [[tiktok|TikTok]] and asserting Russian interference.[1] The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] characterized the effort as an establishment attempt to overturn an adverse popular result.[1]
