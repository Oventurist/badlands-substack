# Stop Mind Control and Ritual Abuse Today (S.M.A.R.T.)

Stop Mind Control and Ritual Abuse Today, generally abbreviated S.M.A.R.T., is a survivor-and-clinician network that maintains a database and conference archive on [[ritual-abuse|ritual abuse]] and trauma-based mind control.[1] Badlands Media identified the S.M.A.R.T. database as one of the three principal bodies of material underpinning its "Gazing Into the Abyss" series, alongside the published works of accomplished psychotherapists and the written testimony of survivors.[1]

Material associated with the network includes conference presentations such as Hal Pepinsky's account of bringing the voices of survivors of ritual violence and mind control experimentation into his classroom, and Neil Brick's writing on avoiding being mind controlled at a conference.[1] The organization's archives also host the 1991 report of the [[los-angeles-county-commission-for-women-ritual-abuse-task-force|Los Angeles County Commission for Women's Ritual Abuse Task Force]].[1]
