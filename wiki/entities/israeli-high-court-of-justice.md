# Israeli High Court of Justice

The High Court of Justice is the supreme judicial body of [[israel|Israel]], serving as both the country's highest court of appeal and its court of first instance for petitions against the state. In the Badlands Media corpus it enters as the flashpoint of Israel's first constitutional crisis over judicial defiance.

## The 2026 constitutional crisis

The July 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that, for the first time in Israeli history, the government of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] openly declared that it will not respect a High Court of Justice ruling, sparking a constitutional crisis. The Brief cites the [[times-of-israel|Times of Israel]] for the government's declaration, and reports that the High Court warned of anarchy and societal breakdown should officials pursue this course of defiance and flout its rulings.

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the crisis as evidence that the Israeli political elite is cornered — unable to allow peace to return to the Middle East because the world will "come seeking retribution" for Israel's conduct in Gaza — and predicts a similar "civil war, of sorts" among the political elite in the United States as Israel's control and influence over American politicians and institutions becomes increasingly obvious.
