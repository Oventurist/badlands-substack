# Israeli High Court of Justice

The **High Court of Justice** is [[israel|Israel]]'s supreme judicial body sitting in its capacity to review actions of the state and the military.[1]

## April 2026 ruling on IDF gender integration

In April 2026, the High Court of Justice ruled that the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] must proceed with the integration of women into armored units.[1] The ruling placed the court in direct conflict with the leadership of twelve [[hesder-yeshivas|Hesder yeshivas]], who warned that integration would drive religious soldiers out of the Armored Corps and threatened to stop sending students to those units.[1]

By June 2026 the IDF had responded by stating that men and women would not serve inside the same tanks, an accommodation that preserved some form of integration while conceding the yeshivas' central objection.[1] Badlands commentary framed the standoff as a case of judicially mandated social policy colliding with the manpower requirements of a military already strained by recruitment shortfalls and tension with the Ultra-Orthodox community.[1]

## Striking down the reasonableness law

On January 1, 2024, Israel's highest court struck down the central provision of the [[israeli-judicial-overhaul|judicial overhaul]] enacted in July 2023 by the government of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], which would have removed the court's power to abrogate government decisions it deems "unreasonable in the extreme."[2] The vote was eight justices in favor of striking the law down and seven against.[2] The decision was expected to reopen the political and social conflict of the previous year at a moment when [[israel|Israel]] was still absorbing the October 7 attack and fighting in [[gaza|Gaza]].[2]
