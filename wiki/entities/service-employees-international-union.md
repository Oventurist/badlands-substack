# Service Employees International Union

The Service Employees International Union (SEIU) is a large United States labor union representing workers in health care, public services and property services.[1]

The SEIU was one of three plaintiff organizations — with the [[alliance-for-retired-americans|Alliance for Retired Americans]] and the [[american-federation-of-government-employees|American Federation of Government Employees]] — that sued Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] in early February 2025 over the sharing of members' information with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] team.[1]

The suit resulted in U.S. District Judge [[colleen-kollar-kotelly|Colleen Kollar-Kotelly]] temporarily limiting DOGE access to the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]]'s payment system, restricting read-only access to two named DOGE staffers and Treasury personnel.[1]
