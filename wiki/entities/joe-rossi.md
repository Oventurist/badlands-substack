# Joe Rossi

Joe Rossi is a local Georgia election investigator known for his research into Fulton County's handling of the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## January 2026 State Election Board presentation

At an eleven-hour [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] hearing held on January 21, 2026, Rossi presented evidence he characterized as demonstrating intentional human intervention in the 2020 election.[1] He called on the board's chairman to resign and asked the board to commit to making criminal referrals to the state attorney general.[1]

The centerpiece of Rossi's presentation was a timeline built around a report run at 12:06 p.m. on Thursday, December 3, 2020, which was transmitted by email at 12:13 p.m. to [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]], whom Rossi described as possibly a consultant working for The Election Group.[1] Rossi said he did not know why the report had been sent to Macias but speculated aloud that the message amounted to an admission that the county was "only at 511" and did not know what to do.[1] He linked this to testimony from Fulton County elections director [[richard-barron|Richard Barron]], who stated during certification week that "we knew we were short" and that officials had "called the state" — which Rossi assumed meant the Secretary of State's office.[1]

## Significance in Badlands coverage

Badlands writers treated Rossi's presentation as a "bombshell," summarizing it as evidence that officials knew on November 19, 2020 that the counts did not match and that on December 3, 2020 they "made them match."[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected the finding to [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]], arguing he had been implicated for misleading President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on January 2, 2021 about whether the vote was "under what they thought they needed."[1] The hearing was recapped on Badlands Media by Ashe in America and [[canncon|CannCon]].[1]
