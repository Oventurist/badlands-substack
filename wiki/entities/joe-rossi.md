# Joe Rossi

Joe Rossi is a local investigator who presented evidence of alleged intentional human intervention in the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]] before the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] (SEB). His presentation, delivered during the board's eleven-hour hearing on Wednesday, January 21, 2026, was described by Badlands Media's [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as the most explosive part of the proceedings.

## Testimony before the State Election Board

According to the January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief, Rossi presented evidence of what he characterized as deliberate human intervention in the 2020 election, called on the SEB Chairman to resign, and asked the board to commit to criminal referrals to the state's attorney general. The hearing was held to address election cases and consider referrals to the Georgia Attorney General.

The centerpiece of Rossi's presentation concerned a report he said was run at 12:06 p.m. on Thursday, December 3, 2020, and emailed at 12:13 p.m. to [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]], a consultant Rossi said "maybe... worked for [[the-elections-group|The Election Group]]." Quoted in the Brief, Rossi said: "He runs that report at noon 06 (12:06) on Thursday, December 3rd... At noon 13 (12:13), he sends that report in an email. We've got the email to Ryan Macias... I could speculate saying, 'Hey, Ryan, we're deep doo doo here. You know, we're only at 511. What do we do?'"

Rossi also cited the testimony of Fulton County elections director [[richard-barron|Richard Barron]], who testified during certification week that the county was short of its expected count: "He said, 'We knew we were short.' He said, 'We called the state.' I'm assuming that's the Secretary of State's office." On the basis of this testimony, the Brief concludes: "They knew on November 19, 2020 that the counts didn't match. So on December 3, 2020, it appears that, they made them match."

## Context

Rossi's presentation came one day after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said people "are going to be prosecuted for 2020," and was framed by Badlands commentators as the first rebuttal heard by the SEB to the election narrative of Georgia Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]], who was separately implicated in the same reporting for his January 2, 2021 phone call with Trump. Ashe in America and [[canncon|CannCon]] recapped the hearing in a Badlands Media special coverage segment, and the pair also carried the subsequent [[georgia-senate-ethics-committee|Georgia Senate Ethics Committee]] meeting live.
