# Silvio Berlusconi

Silvio Berlusconi was the Prime Minister of [[italy|Italy]] under whose government the 2003 defence cooperation memorandum of understanding with [[israel|Israel]] entered into force in 2006.[1] That agreement — covering military procurement, training, the exchange of defence equipment and technology, and arms transit — renewed automatically every five years unless one party opted out, and remained the framework for Italian–Israeli defence cooperation until Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] suspended its automatic renewal in April 2026.[1] See [[italy-israel-defence-memorandum-of-understanding|the memorandum]] for details.[1]
