# The Libertarian Institute

The Libertarian Institute is an American non-interventionist media and commentary organization whose foreign-policy reporting is periodically excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Reporting on Slovakia and Ukraine arms transfers

The Libertarian Institute supplied the source material for the Brief's bonus item on [[slovakia|Slovakia]]'s decision to halt weapons shipments to [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] Its account reported that the new government in Bratislava had followed through on its campaign pledge to terminate arms transfers and was calling on the rest of Europe to adopt the same policy.[1]

The report quoted Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]] telling Slovak legislators that Bratislava was abandoning the West's proxy war against Russia, that he would "support zero military aid to Ukraine," and that "the EU should change from an arms supplier to a peacemaker."[1] It further recorded Fico's refusal to back additional sanctions on Moscow absent analyses of their impact on Slovakia, his remark that "the people in Slovakia have bigger problems than (dealing with) Ukraine," and the electoral victory of his left-wing Smer-SD party on a platform emphasizing an end to arms transfers.[1]

## Editorial orientation

The organization's output is consistently critical of American interventionism, NATO expansion, and economic sanctions regimes — positions that align with the antiwar strand of Badlands Media commentary on the Ukraine conflict and on escalation in the Middle East.[1]
