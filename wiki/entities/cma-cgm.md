# CMA CGM

CMA CGM is a French-headquartered container shipping and logistics group, one of the largest ocean carriers in the world.

## Suspension of Cuba operations

The [[badlands-brief-257|Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026]] identifies CMA CGM as one of the European shipping companies that suspended operations in [[cuba|Cuba]] after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s May 1, 2026 executive order imposed secondary sanctions on firms doing business with [[gaesa|GAESA]], the Cuban military-industrial conglomerate that controls the island's port and terminal infrastructure. Together with [[hapag-lloyd|Hapag-Lloyd]] and the Canadian miner [[sherritt-international|Sherritt International]], its withdrawal was presented in the source as evidence that the sanctions were rapidly severing Cuba's commercial links to global carriers — a key mechanism of the administration's "[[accelerationism|accelerationism]]" strategy, since a maritime economy dependent on imports is acutely vulnerable to carrier withdrawal.
