# Aspen Strategy Group

The Aspen Strategy Group is a foreign-policy program of the [[aspen-institute|Aspen Institute]] that Badlands Media identified as the vehicle for [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]]'s association with the institute.[1] The group's publication "American Purpose" lists Blinken as "Staff Director, U.S. Senate Foreign Relations Committee," and stood as essentially the only online documentation of the connection remaining at the time of publication.[1]

## Scrubbing claims

Badlands Media co-founder [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] used [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to probe the relationship after conventional searches stalled.[1] The model, whose stated knowledge cutoff was September 2021, said Blinken "has been a member of the Aspen Strategy Group since at least 2017" and cited coverage by CNN, The New York Times, [[politico|Politico]], Bloomberg, Reuters, Foreign Policy, and The Hill.[1] All 13 article links the model produced returned "404 not found," and none could be located in the Wayback Machine or other archives, which the article treated as evidence of deliberate obfuscation rather than model error.[1] The article acknowledged the alternative explanation that ChatGPT fabricated the links and text outright.[1]

Among the missing items was a Politico piece titled "Biden's Top Diplomat Has Close Ties to the President's New Favorite Think Tank" (November 23, 2020) and a Newsweek article connecting Blinken, then the secretary of state pick, to the Aspen Strategy Group.[1] Aspen Institute Form 990 filings back to 2013 show no membership relationship with Blinken.[1]
