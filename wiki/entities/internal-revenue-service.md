# Internal Revenue Service

The **Internal Revenue Service (IRS)** is the United States federal agency responsible for collecting taxes. In the Badlands Media corpus, the IRS is presented as a creation of the [[Revenue Act of 1913]], which established the federal income tax following ratification of the [[Sixteenth Amendment]]. The article "[[1913-the-year-the-cabal-enslaved-america|1913: The Year the Cabal Enslaved America]]" frames the IRS as one pillar of the [[1913]] consolidation of power, alongside the [[Federal Reserve]].

## The corpus's framing

According to the source, after the Sixteenth Amendment was ratified in 1913, Democratic leaders sought passage of the Revenue Act of 1913, which "dramatically lowered tariffs and implemented an income tax," thereby establishing the IRS. President [[Woodrow Wilson]] signed the Act into law on October 3, 1913. The article asserts that the income tax gave the state "a permanent claim on earnings" and, combined with the [[Federal Reserve]]'s removal of gold redeemability, "enslaved every American to the [[fiat-currency|fiat monetary system]]."

The source links the income tax to earlier advocacy by the [[Populist Party]] and William Jennings Bryan, and argues it was designed to "permanently suppress the citizens" after the [[Long Depression]] "was doing more damage to the rich and powerful."
