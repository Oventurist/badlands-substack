# Internal Revenue Service

The **Internal Revenue Service (IRS)** is the United States federal tax collection agency whose establishment was enabled by the [[revenue-act-of-1913|Revenue Act of 1913]], signed by President [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] on October 3, 1913.[1]

Badlands Media presents the IRS as the taxation arm of the 1913 settlement, paired with the [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve]] as its monetary arm: after 1913 every American had to pay income tax while watching the continued devaluation of their money.[1] The article's closing appeal invites readers to imagine a country "with no IRS or no Federal Reserve," in which a house might cost $5,000 and a car $1,000, with no debt and an untaxed paycheck.[1]

The [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]] was convened by Representative [[arsene-pujo|Arsene Pujo]] to investigate banking monopoly after the creation of both the Federal Reserve and the IRS.[1]
