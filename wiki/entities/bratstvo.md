# Bratstvo (Brotherhood Party)

Bratstvo, meaning "Brotherhood," is a small far-right Ukrainian political party led by the radical nationalist politician [[dmitry-korchinsky|Dmitry Korchinsky]].[1]

## Profile

The party is described as marginal in electoral terms but notable for the prominence of its leader, a longtime proponent of war with Russia.[1] In January 2025, Korchinsky used an interview with the Govorit Veliky Lviv YouTube channel to call for Ukraine to become a military dictatorship, diverting whatever resources the country still had toward the conflict with Moscow.[1]

Speaking in his capacity as the party's leader, Korchinsky argued that the open publication of sociological survey data during wartime benefits the enemy and should be banned, with those responsible imprisoned.[1] He further condemned criticism of Ukrainian military and political leadership and of enlistment officers, insisting that such conduct be punished and that offenders be targeted by the [[security-service-of-ukraine|Security Service of Ukraine]].[1]

Badlands commentary treated these positions as evidence of authoritarian tendencies within the Ukrainian political ecosystem being tacitly underwritten by Western media and the political establishment.[1]
