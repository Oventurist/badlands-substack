# Christine Fang

Christine Fang, widely known as "Fang Fang," is the woman the [[fbi|FBI]] investigated years ago as a suspected Chinese intelligence operative and whose past contacts with Rep. [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]] of California are the subject of a decade-old investigative file.[1]

## 2026 file-release push

FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] pressed in 2026 to release the file, directing agents in the FBI's San Francisco field office to accelerate redactions in preparation for possible public disclosure, despite no public evidence of wrongdoing by the lawmaker.[1] Current and former officials said the push raised concerns inside the bureau about departing from standard practice and politicizing investigative records; the FBI had previously examined the matter and brought no charges.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Swalwell was not removed from the House Intelligence Committee until the two-year investigation concluded, and questioned the official account — delivered, she wrote, by the Biden/Garland Justice Department — that he had a relationship with a foreign spy while remaining innocent on the espionage front.[1]
