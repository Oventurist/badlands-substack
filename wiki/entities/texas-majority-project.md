# Texas Majority Project

The Texas Majority Project (TMP) is a political action committee founded by former staffers of [[beto-orourke|Beto O'Rourke]] and reportedly funded by billionaire [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1] In August 2025 the group became the subject of a Texas state investigation over its alleged support for Democratic legislators who fled the state to deny the Texas House a quorum.[1]

## Paxton investigation

Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] announced the investigation into TMP in a news release on Thursday, August 7, 2025, one day after opening a separate probe into [[powered-by-people|Powered by People]], the PAC founded by O'Rourke himself.[1] Paxton claimed both organizations may have violated state law through unlawful financial coordination, potential bribery of Democratic legislators who left Texas to break quorum, and illegal fundraising activities.[1]

The walkout that prompted the inquiry was aimed at blocking a Republican-backed redistricting bill that would have added five GOP congressional seats; by leaving the state, Democratic members prevented the chamber from reaching the number of members required to conduct business and stalled the legislation.[1] Badlands commentary framed the episode around the question of whether elected officials face consequences for abdicating their duties, noting that Texas Democrats had used the same tactic in July 2021 over voting legislation.[1]
