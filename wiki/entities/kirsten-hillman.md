# Kirsten Hillman

Kirsten Hillman is the Canadian ambassador to the United States.[1] In early December 2024, following Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]]'s visit to President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Mar-a-Lago — one of the first in-person meetings between Trump and a foreign leader after his November victory — Hillman told reporters that "the message that our border is so vastly different than the Mexican border was really understood."[1]

The remark came as Canada and Mexico maneuvered for position ahead of a threatened 25 percent tariff on all imports from both countries, which Trump framed as a lever to curb migration and drug flows across U.S. borders.[1] Both governments criticized the proposed tariffs and raised the prospect of retaliatory taxes on U.S. goods, while sharpening rhetoric against each other in a bid to be seen as the stronger North American trading partner.[1] Trump described the Mar-a-Lago meeting as "productive," though no trade guarantees emerged from it.[1]
