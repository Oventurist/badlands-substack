# Joseph Chilton Pearce

**Joseph Chilton Pearce** is an author quoted in "Hijacked Consciousness - Resonance Field," the fifth installment of the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series published by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] through [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] on August 9, 2025.[1]

The article cites his remark, "To live a creative life, we must lose our fear of being wrong," in its closing section on why resonance was treated as a threat.[1] The author reframes the line as a working definition of resonance itself, asking "what is resonance but the courage to feel truth, even when it contradicts the curriculum?"[1] The quotation is used to reinforce the essay's argument that remembering happens by vibration and alignment rather than by concepts, and that the resulting coherence spreads by contact once it begins — a process the article describes as the [[resonance-field|resonance field]].[1]
