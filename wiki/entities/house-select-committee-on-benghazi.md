# House Select Committee on Benghazi

The House Select Committee on Benghazi, chaired by Rep. Trey Gowdy, produced the comprehensive report the article relies on for its timeline of the [[benghazi-attack|attack]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Part I of the report, "Terrorist Attack on U.S. Facilities in Benghazi," opens with a Diplomatic Security Agent's words under fire: "If you guys don't get out here, we're all going to f—-ing die."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) According to The Hill, the investigation was delayed and repeatedly obstructed by House Democrats and the Obama administration, but Gowdy and his Republican members documented volumes of facts about actions and inactions before, during and after the attacks, and catalogued the administration's excuses — notably the "tyranny of time and distance."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The report established that senior diplomats in Tripoli learned of the attack within three minutes, that [[africom|AFRICOM]] learned within thirty minutes, and that the [[state-department|State Department]]'s notice took about an hour to reach the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) It recorded [[leon-panetta|Leon Panetta]]'s testimony on identifying and ordering assets, and the 7:30 p.m. deputies meeting.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The Hill wrote that the report connected the dots to a "smoking gun": a direct order of the Commander in Chief obstructed by Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The article credits Gowdy's committee with this finding, as it had earlier exposed Clinton's private email server.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Separately, the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence — where Rep. Devin Nunes questioned CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] — took testimony from Benghazi survivors about their [[non-disclosure-agreements|non-disclosure agreements]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
