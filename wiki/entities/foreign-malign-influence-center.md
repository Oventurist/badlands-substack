# Foreign Malign Influence Center

The Foreign Malign Influence Center (FMIC) is a component of the [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Office of the Director of National Intelligence]] tasked with assessing foreign influence operations directed at the United States, including attempts to interfere with federal elections.[1]

## September 2024 election security update

On September 6, 2024, the FMIC published a two-page election security update stating that the intelligence community "has not observed any foreign actor seeking to interfere in the conduct of the 2024 elections."[1] The report stipulated that Russia "poses the most active foreign influence threat" to the election, but found no evidence of meddling in the conduct of the vote itself.[1]

The findings were first surfaced publicly by Natalie Winters, a co-host of the Steve Bannon WarRoom podcast.[1]

## Contrast with Justice Department statements

Coverage of the report emphasized that its conclusions appeared to undercut warnings issued days earlier by Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] about foreign influence targeting the election, warnings that had accompanied charges against Russian media figures and had been used, critics argued, to smear conservative commentators.[1] The Badlands brief carried the item as a bonus entry illustrating the gap between the assessments of the intelligence community and the public messaging of the Justice Department.[1]
