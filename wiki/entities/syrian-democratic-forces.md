# Syrian Democratic Forces

The Syrian Democratic Forces (SDF) are a predominantly Kurdish armed coalition in [[syria]] supported by the United States [[pentagon|Pentagon]].

## In the corpus

assad day in damascus uses the SDF to draw a distinction between the two principal strands of American involvement in Syria. Where the [[cia|CIA]], through [[timber-sycamore]], armed and trained groups aimed at pressuring the government of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]], the Pentagon's efforts "were more focused on fighting [[isis|ISIS]], particularly through support for the Syrian Democratic Forces, which is predominantly Kurdish."

The article also identifies Kurds among the factions whose position is unsettled by the collapse of the Assad government, alongside Turkish-backed groups and other opposition forces — a point of particular sensitivity given [[turkey]]'s hostility to Kurdish armed formations and its acknowledged aid to some Syrian rebel factions.
