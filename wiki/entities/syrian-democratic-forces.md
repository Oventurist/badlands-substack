# Syrian Democratic Forces

The Syrian Democratic Forces (SDF) is a predominantly Kurdish armed coalition in Syria supported by the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

Badlands Media distinguishes the Pentagon's SDF support, framed as focused on fighting [[isis|ISIS]], from the [[cia|CIA]]'s [[timber-sycamore|Timber Sycamore]] program, which armed and trained groups aimed at pressuring the government of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1] The termination of the CIA program in 2017 under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was read as a shift toward counter-terrorism over regime change.[1]

The article notes that the collapse of Assad's government in December 2024 raised questions about how the power transition would affect Kurds, Turkish-backed groups, and other opposition factions inside Syria.[1]
