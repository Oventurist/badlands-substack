# Satoshi Action Fund

The Satoshi Action Fund is a Bitcoin policy advocacy organization co-founded by [[dennis-porter|Dennis Porter]], who serves as its chief executive officer.[1]

## Role in the strategic reserve debate

In July 2024, Porter said that credible sources indicated [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] might declare [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] a "strategic reserve asset" at a Bitcoin conference in Nashville, a prospect analysts called a potential watershed moment for the asset.[1] Porter publicly argued that adding Bitcoin to the U.S. Treasury as a strategic reserve was "a no brainer" and that once the United States did so, other nations would follow — the core of the case for a [[us-bitcoin-strategic-reserve|US Bitcoin strategic reserve]].[1] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] connected the speculation to Bitcoin's price rise following the [[2024-crowdstrike-global-outage|CrowdStrike global outage]] and to Trump's earlier framing of Bitcoin and Bitcoin mining as a national security matter.[1]
