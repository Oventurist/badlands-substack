# Strobe Talbott

Strobe Talbott is an American journalist and diplomat who served as Deputy Secretary of State during the Clinton administration with particular responsibility for Russia policy. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]] he is identified as a Rhodes Scholar and as one of the Western managers of post-Soviet liberalization.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] lists Talbott — spelled "Strobe Talbot" in the article — among those involved in the Western side of Perestroika, alongside the IMF's [[jeffrey-sachs|Jeffrey Sachs]], Vice President [[al-gore|Al Gore]] and the Goldman Sachs banker [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]. His designation as a Rhodes Scholar connects him, in the essay's argument, to the [[rhodes-scholarship|Rhodes Scholarship]] cadre network descended from [[cecil-rhodes|Cecil Rhodes]] and working alongside the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]].
