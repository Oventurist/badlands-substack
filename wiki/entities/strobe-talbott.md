# Strobe Talbott

Strobe Talbott is an American foreign policy figure who served as Deputy Secretary of State during the Clinton administration.[1] Badlands Media's "Deep State Spotlight" profile of [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] identifies him as the official to whom Nuland served as chief of staff at the beginning of her career in American government, and notes that he, like Nuland's husband [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]] and much of the wider foreign policy establishment described in the piece, was a [[yale-university|Yale]] alumnus.[1]

The profile treats this posting as the launching point of Nuland's specialization in Russia policy: after working for Talbott she moved on to serve as deputy director for former Soviet Union affairs, a position that placed her in charge of U.S. policy toward Russia.[1] Contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] uses the Talbott connection as part of his argument that Nuland's career was shaped by a self-perpetuating network within the [[state-department|State Department]] whose personnel and priorities persist regardless of which party controls the executive branch.[1]
