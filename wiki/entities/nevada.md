# Nevada

**Nevada** is a U.S. state in the Mountain West whose largest metropolitan area, Las Vegas, sits in Clark County. In the Badlands Media corpus Nevada appears principally as the setting of the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas biolab case]] of 2026.

## Las Vegas biolab

The Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 reports that an unlicensed laboratory was discovered in the garage of an east Las Vegas rental home in late January and early February 2026 by the [[fbi|FBI]] and local police. The property manager, [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]], faced federal firearms charges that were dismissed in May 2026 by First Assistant U.S. Attorney [[sigal-chattah|Sigal Chattah]], while a Clark County charge for improper disposal of hazardous waste remained pending with a status hearing scheduled for June 4. Magistrate judge [[elayna-youchah|Elayna J. Youchah]] had released Solomon pending trial in February. The state-level hazardous waste case is the surviving prosecution in the matter.
