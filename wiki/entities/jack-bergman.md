# Jack Bergman

**Jack Bergman** is a U.S. Representative from Michigan and a retired Marine Corps officer.[1]

## Lake Huron shootdown

Bergman, together with Michigan Representative [[elissa-slotkin|Elissa Slotkin]], was among the first officials to report publicly that an unknown "octagonal object" had been shot down over Lake Huron on February 12, 2023, during the [[2023-north-american-aerial-object-shootdowns|February 2023 aerial object shootdowns]].[1] The disclosures from Michigan's congressional delegation contributed to criticism that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and White House were slower to inform the public than members of Congress were.[1]
