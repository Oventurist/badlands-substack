# Foxconn

Foxconn Technology Group is a Taiwan-headquartered contract electronics manufacturer and the largest single assembler of Apple iPhones.[1] It occupies a central place in debates over [[friendshoring|friendshoring]] because its Indian operations have absorbed a growing share of production shifted out of mainland China.[1]

## Role in Indian iPhone production

In the fiscal year ended March 2024, Foxconn assembled nearly 67 percent of the iPhones made in India, with Pegatron Corporation producing about 17 percent and the remainder coming from the Wistron plant in southern Karnataka state that the [[tata-group|Tata Group]] acquired in 2023.[1] That year Indian iPhone output topped $14 billion in value, representing roughly 14 percent — one in seven — of global iPhone production for [[apple|Apple]].[1] The pace of the increase was read as evidence that Apple was accelerating its effort to reduce dependence on Chinese manufacturing as relations between China and the West deteriorated.[1] China nevertheless remained the source of the largest share of iPhones.[1]

## Context

Foxconn's Indian expansion is part of the broader fracturing of global supply chains that followed the trade war launched against China by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during his first administration, with India emerging as the prime destination for relocated US manufacturing supply chains.[1]
