# Guido von List

**Guido von List** was an Austrian mystic and rune-obsessed poet who founded [[ariosophy|Ariosophism]], the occult sect that Matthew Ehret identifies as the belief system of the Nazi leadership.[1]

## Doctrine

Von List took the Theosophy of [[helena-blavatsky|Madame Helena Blavatsky]] and infused it with an Aryan racial-superiority twist and a heightened focus on Wotan (Odin) myths.[1] In his sect the swastika and other rune symbols — the Othala rune, the Ehlaz or life rune, the Sig runes later used by the [[schutzstaffel|SS]], and the wolfsangel — were treated as sacred images endowed with magic power.[1]

## The High Armanen Order

Von List organized his sect into an inner and outer core, with the "elect" learning a secret interpretation of the runes under an elite occult society called the High Armanen Order, where von List himself served as Grand Master.[1] Out of the High Armanen Order grew another secret occult organization, the [[thule-society|Thule Society]], whose leading members included Rudolf Hess, Hans Frank, Hermann Göring, Karl Haushofer, and Hitler's coach [[dietrich-eckart|Dietrich Eckart]].[1]

Alongside von List, [[lanz-von-liebenfels|Lanz von Liebenfels]] served as an Ariosophist high priest, publishing the Teutonic mystical magazine *Ostara* to which a young [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] was a devoted subscriber.[1]
