# Maria Langan-Riekhof

Maria Langan-Riekhof served as deputy to Michael Collins, the acting chair of the [[national-intelligence-council|National Intelligence Council]], until May 2025.[1]

## Dismissal

Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]] fired Langan-Riekhof along with Collins in May 2025, in a move her office framed as part of the Trump administration's effort to "end the weaponization and politicization of the intelligence community."[1] The dismissals followed the Council's publication of a report asserting that the Maduro government in [[venezuela|Venezuela]] played no role in the illegal migration of [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang members to the United States.[1]
