# Jacob DeWitte

Jacob DeWitte is an American nuclear engineer and entrepreneur, co-founder and chief executive of Oklo, a company developing small modular fission reactors intended to provide compact, distributed power generation. His work sits at the center of renewed US policy interest in advanced nuclear technology, including [[nuclear-microreactor|microreactors]] as a power source for data centers and remote installations.

DeWitte was named in March 2026 among the appointees to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], as reported in the March 26 Badlands Brief.
