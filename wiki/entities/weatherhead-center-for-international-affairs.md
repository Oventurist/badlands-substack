# Weatherhead Center for International Affairs

The Center for International Affairs at Harvard University — later the Weatherhead Center — is described by Badlands Media as "a bastion of the high Cold War defense, intelligence, and security establishment," widely nicknamed "The CIA at Harvard," a coincidence of initials the article calls "cheeky."[1]

[[thomas-schelling|Thomas Schelling]] recruited the young [[gene-sharp|Gene Sharp]] to the center in the mid-1960s, placing the future theorist of [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]] inside the same institution that nurtured [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], future National Security Advisor McGeorge Bundy and future [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] director Robert Bowie.[1]
