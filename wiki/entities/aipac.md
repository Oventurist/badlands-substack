# American Israel Public Affairs Committee

The American Israel Public Affairs Committee, known as AIPAC, is the principal pro-Israel lobbying organization in United States politics and a major participant in congressional primary campaigns.

The March 4, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that in the aftermath of the Gaza war, Democratic candidates' pro-Israel positions and their links to AIPAC were being weaponized by primary opponents in states including Illinois, Michigan and Texas, amid a shift in party sympathy toward Palestinians. The Brief presents this dynamic as compounding Democratic divisions over the American and Israeli strikes on Iran ahead of the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].
