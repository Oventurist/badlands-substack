# Julie Chavez Rodriguez

Julie Chavez Rodriguez served as manager of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s 2024 reelection campaign.[1]

In October 2023 she communicated the campaign's decision that Biden would not file for the New Hampshire Democratic presidential primary ballot, writing in a letter to New Hampshire Democratic Party chair Raymond Buckley that "while the president wishes to participate in the primary, he is obligated to comply" with party rules set by the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]].[1] In the same letter she stated that the president looked forward to having his name on New Hampshire's general election ballot as the Democratic nominee after formally securing the nomination at the 2024 Democratic National Convention, and that he would "tirelessly campaign to earn every single vote in the Granite State."[1] The episode is covered in detail at [[2024-new-hampshire-democratic-primary]].[1]

## Campaign funds after the 2024 debate

During a tense call between Biden campaign aides and donors in early July 2024, Chavez Rodriguez as campaign manager addressed the scenario of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] leaving the race, saying that if he dropped out — which she emphasized he had no intention of doing — Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] would control most of the money in the campaign account, which stood at $91.2 million as of May 31.[2] Campaign finance experts largely agreed with that reading, though they noted it likely applied only if Harris became the party's presidential nominee.[2]

## January 6 messaging pivot (January 2024)

As [[joe-biden|Biden]] campaign manager, Chavez Rodriguez was quoted in January 2024 as the campaign doubled down on January 6-related attacks against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] following the poor performance of the White House's [[bidenomics|"Bidenomics"]] messaging.[3] "Over the last three years, MAGA Republicans haven't shied away from the Big Lie — they've doubled down," she told NBC News, ahead of a Biden speech at Valley Forge, Pennsylvania framing Trump as a threat to democracy.[3]
