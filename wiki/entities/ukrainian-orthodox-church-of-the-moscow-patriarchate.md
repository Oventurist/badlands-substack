# Ukrainian Orthodox Church of the Moscow Patriarchate

The Ukrainian Orthodox Church of the Moscow Patriarchate (UOC-MP) is the Moscow-linked Orthodox church body operating in Ukraine. In August 2024 it became the effective target of Ukrainian legislation banning religious organizations with ties to Russia.[1]

## 2024 ban

On Tuesday, August 20, 2024, the [[verkhovna-rada|Verkhovna Rada]] approved the banning legislation in its second and final reading, with 265 lawmakers in favor and 29 against, according to MP Yaroslav Zheleznyak.[1] The law paved the way for Kyiv to end the church's activities on Ukrainian soil.[1] Ukrainian officials have long argued that the UOC-MP operates as an arm of the Russian Orthodox Church to undermine Ukraine and is complicit in Russia's full-scale war, and specifically accuse it of working closely with the FSB, Russia's security service.[1]

The bill required the signature of President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] to become law; in a meeting with Ukrainian religious leaders the preceding Saturday he signaled he would not hesitate to sign, saying Russia used the church to suppress the independence of other states and limit their citizens' freedoms and that "it is our common duty to guarantee Ukrainian spiritual independence."[1]

## Badlands framing

The Badlands News Brief used the ban as the entry point for an argument, by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], that the Ukraine conflict is at least in part a religious war — a claim the writer attributes to President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] — and then pivoted to a long history of mystical [[kabbalah|Kabbalistic]] Judaism in Poland and Ukraine, [[breslov-hasidism|Breslov Hasidism]], and the Uman pilgrimage.[1]
