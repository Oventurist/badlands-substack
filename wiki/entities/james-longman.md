# James Longman

James Longman is [[abc-news|ABC News]]' Chief International Correspondent.[1]

## Syria ISIS patch video

In December 2024, footage emerged of Longman in Syria advising 'rebel' fighters that the [[isis|ISIS]] logo displayed on their uniforms would be misunderstood by Western audiences.[1] One of the militants — possibly a member of the US-designated terror group [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham|Hayat Tahrir al-Sham]] or another hardline Islamist faction — attempted to claim the emblem did not represent ISIS, while nonetheless removing the patch for the camera; the fighters swore to Longman that they were not *Daesh* even while openly sporting its symbols.[1]

Ali Abunimah of Electronic Intifada, who speaks Arabic, said Longman did not explicitly tell the fighter to remove the patch in the clip, but that it was "not much of a 'confrontation' either," and that the fighters appeared to interpret his comments as friendly advice on presentation.[1] Abunimah compared the incident to Western media collusion in the rebranding of Ukraine's Azov Battalion, which those same outlets had accurately described as hardcore Nazis months earlier.[1]

ZeroHedge presented the episode as evidence of a trend of American journalists travelling to war zones to coach combatants on self-presentation, and of the long-standing mainstream myth of "moderate rebels" in Syria seeking to topple [[bashar-al-assad|Assad]] — which they finally accomplished in December 2024.[1]
