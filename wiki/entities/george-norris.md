# George Norris

George Norris was a Republican United States Senator from Nebraska who, alongside Senator [[peter-norbeck|Peter Norbeck]] of South Dakota, spearheaded the 1932 establishment of the U.S. Committee on Banking and Currency, the body that became the [[pecora-commission|Pecora Commission]].[1] Norris became known for publicly displaying charts illustrating the control that Wall Street and its conglomeration of corporations and shell companies exerted over the United States in the 1920s.[1]

His work came at the depth of the [[great-depression|Great Depression]], when unemployment had reached 25 percent, over 40 percent of banks had gone bankrupt, and more than half of America's industrial capacity had shut down.[1]
