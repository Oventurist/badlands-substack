# Eduardo Bolsonaro

Eduardo Bolsonaro is a Brazilian congressman and the third-oldest son of former Brazilian president [[jair-bolsonaro|Jair Bolsonaro]].[1]

## U.S. asylum claim (March 2025)

On March 18, 2025, Eduardo Bolsonaro, then 40, said he would seek political asylum in the United States, claiming he was the target of persecution for defending the right-wing movement embodied by his father.[1] He had been in the United States since late February 2025, just as Brazil's Supreme Court began weighing whether to seize his passport over accusations that he tried to interfere in the case involving his father's alleged role in a coup plot following the elder Bolsonaro's loss in the 2022 election.[1]

In a video posted on social media, he said he intended to remain in the United States and step back from his role as a federal lawmaker.[1] He has close ties to the Trump family and has visited [[donald-trump|President Trump]] at Mar-a-Lago, and he denounced Brazilian Supreme Court justice Alexandre de Moraes for targeting him.[1]
