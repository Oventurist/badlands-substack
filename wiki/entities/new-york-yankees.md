# New York Yankees

The New York Yankees are a Major League Baseball franchise based in the Bronx, New York.[1]

## Trump's 9/11 anniversary visit (2025)

On Thursday, September 11, 2025 — the anniversary of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] — President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attended a Yankees game in the Bronx, drawing a mix of cheers and jeers from the crowd.[1] Trump sat in a private box alongside team president [[randy-levine|Randy Levine]], and the stadium crowd offered loud cheers as well as some boos when his face appeared on the screens during the national anthem.[1]

Before the game Trump visited the Yankees clubhouse, shaking hands with star outfielder [[aaron-judge|Aaron Judge]], manager [[aaron-boone|Aaron Boone]], and others.[1] Players wore hats emblazoned with "FDNY" and "NYPD" to honor first responders on the anniversary.[1] Trump told the team he had been friends with the late [[george-steinbrenner|George Steinbrenner]], who owned the franchise for nearly 40 years, and predicted: "You're going to win. You're going to go all the way."[1]
