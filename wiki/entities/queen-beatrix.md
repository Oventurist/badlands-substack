# Queen Beatrix

**Beatrix** was Queen of the Netherlands and a member of the House of Orange; she is the daughter of [[prince-bernhard|Prince Bernhard]], a co-founder of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1]

The Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* describes Beatrix as "no ceremonial matriarch" but a Bilderberg originator in her own right, whose reign tethered Dutch royalty to supranational steering groups.[1] In the essay's account, the House of Orange acted as a covert convener of consensus control, and Bilderberg allowed the illusion of royal disengagement to be weaponized — real influence hidden behind diplomatic charm and soft-power forums.[1]

The essay summarizes this arrangement with the claim that "the Dutch crown did not fade, it dispersed," presenting it as an example of its broader thesis that monarchy was retained as emotional theater while sovereign authority migrated to central banks and covert compacts.[1] Beatrix appears in the same passage as figures including Henry Kissinger and [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] within the [[committee-of-300|Committee of 300]], Bilderberg, and [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]] milieu.[1]
