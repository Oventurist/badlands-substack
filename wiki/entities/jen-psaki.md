# Jen Psaki

Jen Psaki is an American political figure who served as White House Press Secretary in the Biden administration before moving to a hosting role at [[msnbc|MSNBC]].

## In the Badlands critique

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] pairs Psaki with [[joy-reid|Joy Reid]] as illustrations of the state of cable punditry, describing their MSNBC programs in dismissive terms and using them to argue that mainstream news now consists of "substance-free affirmations of the modern liberal cult-of-personality talking points." Her career trajectory from government spokesperson to network host is consistent with the article's broader thesis that "the media IS the establishment" rather than a check upon it.
