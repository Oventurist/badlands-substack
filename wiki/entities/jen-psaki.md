# Jen Psaki

**Jen Psaki** served as [[white-house|White House]] press secretary in the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration. On May 5, 2021, she issued what the [[brownstone-institute|Brownstone Institute]] characterized as a "mob-like warning" to social-media companies and information distributors generally, telling them in effect that they needed to begin censoring critics of COVID policy and to amplify government messaging.[1]

In her remarks, Psaki said that "the president's view is that the major platforms have a responsibility related to the health and safety of all Americans to stop amplifying untrustworthy content, disinformation and misinformation, especially related to Covid-19 vaccinations and elections," adding that Biden "also supports better privacy protections and a robust antitrust program" and that "there's more that needs to be done to ensure that this type of misinformation, disinformation, damaging, sometimes life threatening information is not going out to the American public."[1]

The pairing of censorship demands with an explicit reference to antitrust policy was later cited as context for the Justice Department's antitrust action against [[apple|Apple]], with the Brownstone Institute presenting the 2021 statement as the administration making good on its antitrust threats.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the episode to the Supreme Court's treatment of government coercion as mere "encouragement" in [[murthy-v-missouri|Murthy v. Missouri]].[1]

## MSNBC interview with Dan Goldman (November 2023)

In her post-White House role as an MSNBC host, Psaki interviewed New York Democratic Representative [[dan-goldman|Dan Goldman]] on Sunday, November 19, 2023.[2] During the exchange Goldman told her that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s rhetoric was "really getting dangerous" and that Trump "is not only unfit, he is destructive to our democracy, and he has to be eliminated" — a remark he retracted and apologized for the following day after critics argued it could incite political violence.[2]
