# Milwaukee Police Department

The Milwaukee Police Department (MPD) is the municipal police force of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. In the Badlands corpus it appears as a witness pool for the [[fbi|FBI]]'s investigation into Wisconsin's 2020 election.

## FBI interviews in the 2020 probe

The May 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the FBI is preparing to interview multiple MPD officers as part of its investigation into Wisconsin's 2020 election, citing sources in the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel. The interviews, which could begin as soon as the following week, are expected to focus on officers stationed at Milwaukee's central count facility and polling places on Election Night. Investigators are examining the flash-drive incident involving former [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] Executive Director [[claire-woodall|Claire Woodall]], and have also spoken with [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]] Deputy Administrator [[robert-kehoe|Robert Kehoe]].
