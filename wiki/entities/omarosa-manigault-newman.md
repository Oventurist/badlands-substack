# Omarosa Manigault Newman

Omarosa Manigault Newman is an American television personality and former White House official who first became publicly known as a contestant on *The Apprentice*, the reality program hosted by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## Role in Badlands analysis of the 25th Amendment narrative

The February 17, 2026 Badlands Brief discusses Manigault Newman in the course of [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s analysis of the 2018 [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]] narrative. He notes that on September 21, 2018 — the day [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] first reported that [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] had discussed invoking the amendment — [[q|Q]] posted the *Times* link, and hours later reposted it alongside an article about Manigault Newman conspiring against Trump.

Drawing on his study of *The Apprentice* for his ongoing Book of Trump series, the author argues that Manigault Newman "became a fixture for Donald Trump," a character he would "regularly deploy into the field to sow confusion and chaos among the contestants." He grounds this in Trump's repeated on-air statements that he liked to see how contestants handled pressure — a metric the author says Trump treated as equal to or more important than simple acumen — and in Trump's habit of conferring with her about her interactions with contestants, which the author likens to intelligence gathering. The passage serves in the article as a precedent for reading later Trump-world conflicts, including the Trump–[[steve-bannon|Bannon]] split, as deliberate kayfabe rather than genuine rupture.
