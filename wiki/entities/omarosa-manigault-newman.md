# Omarosa Manigault Newman

Omarosa Manigault Newman is a reality television personality and former White House aide who rose to prominence on the first season of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s program The Apprentice, before celebrities were introduced to the format.[1]

Though she did not win the contest, Omarosa demonstrated the kind of strong personality and charisma Trump is drawn toward, and she parlayed the appearance into a career as a tabloid celebrity, becoming notorious for her assertiveness and combative demeanor.[1] Trump later brought her back as both contestant and consultant on several celebrity seasons — a consultant role also filled at times by his children.[1] As a contestant she would intentionally seek conflict with the celebrities and escalate the drama; Trump would then pull her aside into his limousine, where she briefed him on the emotional intelligence of the other contestants, information he used in his formal evaluations to test their fortitude.[1]

After being sworn into office in January 2017, Trump hired Omarosa as his personal assistant and director of communication at the Office of Public Liaison.[1] She was fired by Chief of Staff John Kelly in December of that year.[1]

In [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s analysis, Omarosa is the clearest example of Trump's long history of using agent provocateurs to instigate conflict so that he can force resolution — a pattern the commentator dubs "The Trump Dialectic" and now sees playing out geopolitically, with [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] cast in the Omarosa role.[1]
