# Office of Pandemic Preparedness and Response Policy

The **Office of Pandemic Preparedness Response Policy** is a permanent White House office launched by the Biden Administration on 21 July 2023.[1]

## Badlands Media assessment

Ryan DeLarme presented the office's creation as confirmation that warnings of future pandemics issued by [[tedros-adhanom-ghebreyesus|Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus]], [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], and [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] "were not empty gestures," signaling to more discerning portions of the population that another global health emergency was being institutionally anticipated.[1] Reporting cited in the article noted that a retired general had been selected to lead the new pandemic response office.[1]

The article situated the office within a broader argument that the sitting U.S. President appeared "all too eager to facilitate the 'Public Health' agenda" rather than resisting what DeLarme characterized as the rapid encroachment of globalism.[1] Because the powers sought under the [[who-pandemic-accord|WHO CA+]] and the amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations]] can only be triggered by another declared global health emergency, the establishment of a permanent domestic preparedness bureaucracy was read as part of the same trajectory.[1]
