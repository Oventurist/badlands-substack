# Protect Democracy

**Protect Democracy** is a nonpartisan, nonprofit organisation focused on American democratic institutions and election-related legal and policy work.[1]

## 2026 midterm planning

The organisation's head took part in the June 2026 strategy session convened by Senate Minority Leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] to prepare Democratic senators for scenarios involving election disruptions, foreign interference, federal law-enforcement activity at polling places, and post-election legal disputes ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|midterms]].[1] Also present were former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], election-law expert [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]], and the head of [[democracy-forward|Democracy Forward]].[1] Badlands commentary framed the group's participation as part of a revived [[shadow-cabal|shadow cabal]].[1]
