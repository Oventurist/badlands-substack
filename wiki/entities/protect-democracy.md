# Protect Democracy

Protect Democracy is a nonprofit organisation, described in the reporting cited by the Badlands corpus as nonpartisan, that works on rule-of-law, executive-power, and election-administration issues.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports that the head of Protect Democracy was among the experts convened by Senate Democratic leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] in June 2026 — together with former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], the attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], the election-law expert [[norman-eisen|Norman Eisen]], and the head of [[democracy-forward|Democracy Forward]] — to help senators prepare for three hypothetical election-disruption scenarios ahead of the 2026 midterms and to strengthen coordination with the state and local officials who oversee election administration and turnout operations. Badlands commentary presents this convening as a continuation of the cross-institutional coordination described in the February 2021 [[time-magazine|Time]] article on the "fortified" 2020 election.
