# Rick Rubin

Rick Rubin is an American music producer who appears in Badlands Media commentary as an alleged participant in a suppressed effort to expose [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]] within the entertainment industry.[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] named Rubin, along with actor [[brad-pitt|Brad Pitt]], as believed members of the group that also included [[chris-cornell|Chris Cornell]], [[chester-bennington|Chester Bennington]], and [[avicii|Avicii]] — musicians the essay says were producing a documentary on child trafficking before their deaths.[1] According to Carlson, after seeing their friends die, Rubin and Pitt "got the point and stepped back in line."[1]

## Context

The account supports the essay's broader claim that the [[cabal|Cabal]] enforces silence in the entertainment industry through lethal reprisal, and that the prosecution of [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]] marked the beginning of that system's exposure.[1] The claims are presented as rumor and belief; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
