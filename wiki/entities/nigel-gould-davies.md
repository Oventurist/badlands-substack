# Nigel Gould-Davies

Nigel Gould-Davies is a former British ambassador to Belarus and a senior fellow for Russia and Eurasia at the [[international-institute-for-strategic-studies|International Institute for Strategic Studies]] in London.[1]

## Commentary on Navalny's death

In February 2024, following the death of [[alexei-navalny|Alexei Navalny]] in a Russian penal colony, Gould-Davies was quoted by the Associated Press saying that the death demonstrated [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s "complete ruthlessness and disdain … for both Western and international opinion."[1] He framed the timing — Russia announced the death on a Friday, just as Western leaders assembled at the [[munich-security-conference|Munich Security Conference]] — as Putin "throwing down a gauntlet to the West" and, ahead of the second anniversary of the war in Ukraine, "again testing Western resolve."[1]

Gould-Davies further argued that the death should serve as a "wake-up call" to U.S. Republicans opposing further aid for Ukraine in [[congress|Congress]], and should encourage European [[nato|NATO]] allies to bolster their assistance to Kyiv.[1]

## Badlands criticism

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited Gould-Davies's remarks as an example of how the Navalny story was being converted into leverage for the stalled $60 billion Ukraine aid package, arguing that the coordinated messaging across the corporate media and the political establishment pointed to an objective being sought rather than sincere mourning.[1]
