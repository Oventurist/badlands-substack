# Court of Arbitration for Sport

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) is the international arbitral body that hears disputes arising in sport, including challenges to eligibility rulings by governing federations.[1]

In its August 2024 press conference on the eligibility of boxers [[imane-khelif|Imane Khelif]] and [[lin-yu-ting|Lin Yu-ting]], the [[international-boxing-association|International Boxing Association]] said both athletes had been informed of their 2022 chromosome test results and were permitted to appeal those findings to the Court of Arbitration for Sport, with the IBA offering to cover the costs of the appeal.[1] Lin declined to appeal; Khelif filed an appeal and subsequently withdrew it.[1]
