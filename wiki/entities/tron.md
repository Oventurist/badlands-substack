# Tron

Tron is a public blockchain platform and cryptocurrency network founded by [[justin-sun|Justin Sun]], widely used for token issuance and stablecoin transfers.

## Mention in the World Liberty Financial suit

The [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]] identifies Sun as the founder of Tron in its account of the defamation lawsuit brought against him by [[world-liberty-financial|World Liberty Financial]], the cryptocurrency venture co-founded by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his sons. The suit alleges that Sun bet against World Liberty's $WLFI token, concealed purchases through third parties, and conducted a coordinated campaign of public attacks against the company; World Liberty is said to have moved to freeze assets tied to one of his firms.
