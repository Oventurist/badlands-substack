# Elias Rodriguez

**Elias Rodriguez** is a 31-year-old Chicago English major accused of the May 2025 shooting deaths of two Israeli Embassy diplomats outside the Capital Jewish Museum in Washington, D.C.[1]

## Attack and arrest

Rodriguez is accused of executing Yaron Lischinsky and Sarah Lynn Milgrim as they left an event at the Capital Jewish Museum on a Wednesday night in May 2025. After his arrest he chanted "Free Palestine."[1]

## Social media posts

Federal investigators worked to confirm whether a social media account containing a series of inflammatory posts was tied to Rodriguez, law enforcement sources said. The account reportedly posted "death to America" and "death to Israel," along with the statement "I voted for Hamas," and praised Luigi Mangione.[1]

## Context

The killings featured in Badlands commentary on narrative events. In the same [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], writer [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] was quoted arguing that readers who had changed their opinion on Israel and Gaza on the basis of "the reported antisemitic killing" were precisely the audience such narrative events are designed to capture, and cautioned against selectively treating only some events as false flags.[1]

## Capital Jewish Museum shooting (May 2025)

A 30-year-old man from Chicago identified as Elias Rodriguez was taken into custody after allegedly killing two Israeli embassy staff members who were leaving an event at the [[capital-jewish-museum|Capital Jewish Museum]] in Washington, D.C. on the night of Wednesday, May 21, 2025.[2] Metropolitan Police Department Chief Pamela Smith said officers responded to multiple calls of a shooting near the museum at around 9:08 p.m., found a man and a woman unconscious and not breathing, and pronounced both dead despite life-saving efforts.[2] Smith said Rodriguez, described as a pro-Palestinian man, was believed to have committed the act alone, and that his gun had been recovered.[2] Badlands commentary raised the possibility of a false flag, pointing to the [[american-jewish-committee|American Jewish Committee]] panel on humanitarian solutions for Gaza that was underway and to the emotive details released about the victims.[2]
