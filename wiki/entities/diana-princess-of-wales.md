# Diana, Princess of Wales

Diana, Princess of Wales, is treated in Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as a central figure in Q drop #100's questions about the British monarchy.[1]

## The Badlands reading of Diana's death

The Chapter 14 commentary proposes that Diana was "about to spill the beans" on the royal family and was killed to prevent that disclosure.[1] In support, the article points to a letter Diana wrote to her butler expressing fear that [[king-charles-iii|Prince Charles]] intended to have her removed by means of a staged car accident; the article links to a Telegraph report on that letter.[1]

The series further claims that Diana's impending marriage to [[dodi-al-fayed|Dodi Al Fayed]] was a cover story, and that Al Fayed was in fact assisting her in escaping the royal household, with the marriage serving as the pretext.[1]

## Context within the drop

Drop #100 is read as connecting the monarchy under [[queen-elizabeth-ii|Queen Elizabeth II]] to a wider pattern of institutional corruption, with Diana's death as one of its most visible outcomes.[1] The chapter's framing places these claims alongside allegations concerning Prince Andrew and the broader argument that the royal family is not what it publicly appears to be.[1]
