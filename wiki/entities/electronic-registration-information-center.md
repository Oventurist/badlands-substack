# Electronic Registration Information Center (ERIC)

The Electronic Registration Information Center, commonly referred to as ERIC, is a multi-state voter-roll data-sharing organization founded by [[david-becker|David Becker]] that member states use to compare and update their voter registration lists.[1] In Badlands coverage of election integrity, ERIC is treated as a central mechanism by which voter rolls are allegedly inflated rather than cleaned.[1]

## Litigation and discovery

Election researcher [[peter-bernegger|Peter Bernegger]] reported a "huge win in court" when the [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]]'s motion to dismiss was denied in Waukesha County Case Number 2024CV001544, *Janel Brandtjen v. Wisconsin Elections Commission, Meagan Wolfe*.[1] With the case proceeding to full discovery, Bernegger indicated plans to depose David Becker of the ERIC states.[1] Bernegger alleged that "the Election Commission and ERIC are unlawfully giving away voters['] personally identifiable information to CEIR (Becker's other company)," comparing it to a bank handing a nonprofit a customer's Social Security number, birthdate and driver's license number without notice, and calling the practice criminal.[1]

## Allegations of roll padding

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that in her opinion ERIC states are "intentionally padding the voter rolls across the country."[1] She cited the 2022 episode in which the Colorado Department of State under Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] "mistakenly" mailed postcards to nearly 30,000 foreign nationals encouraging them to vote, of whom only three were actually eligible.[1] Under oath during her own trial, she questioned Colorado's Deputy Secretary of State, who she says admitted that ERIC sends non-citizens to the states as "eligible but unauthorized" voters without telling the states which of those individuals are not actually eligible — a provision she says is contained in the ERIC contract.[1] She and [[canncon|CannCon]] have discussed the arrangement extensively on [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]], and she has shared the sworn transcripts with Bernegger, describing them as "an ERIC customer admitting knowledge of the 'potential' padding."[1]

Her summary of the pattern was: "Fake Voters. Fake Ballots. Fake Counts. Fake Elections."[1]

## Colorado disclosure litigation (2024)

In September 2024 the U.S. District Court for the District of Colorado ordered Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] to release ERIC reports suspected of containing dead registrants on the state's voter rolls, in a case brought by the [[public-interest-legal-foundation|Public Interest Legal Foundation]].[2] PILF president [[j-christian-adams|J. Christian Adams]] said the ruling had "knocked down ERIC's wall of secrecy in the voter list maintenance process," arguing that states cannot use third parties to hide election records the public has a right to see.[2] Judge [[philip-brimmer|Philip Brimmer]] set a November 1 deadline for disclosure of the 2021 ERIC reports and dismissed PILF's National Voter Registration Act claim once the settlement was reached.[2]

## Criticism of ERIC's design

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Colorado's 2022 mailing of voter-registration postcards to roughly 30,000 foreign nationals — officially attributed to a "data coding mismatch" between the Colorado DMV database and ERIC — implicates ERIC directly rather than the DMV alone, and that the same problem occurred in 2020.[2] She further asserted that ERIC routinely sends non-citizen records to member states without informing them that the individuals are non-citizens, and that this is part of the system's design, citing sworn testimony from Colorado Deputy Secretary of State [[chris-beall|Chris Beall]].[2] Ashe noted that similar "glitches" were being reported in California, still an ERIC member, and Iowa, which withdrew in June 2023 citing reduced data effectiveness as states left and disagreement with ERIC bylaws binding member states.[2] She concluded that a just end to the ERIC saga requires the criminal indictment of [[david-becker|David Becker]] and his inter-state collaborators.[2]
