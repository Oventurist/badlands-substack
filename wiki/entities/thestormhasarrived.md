# TheStormHasArrived

**TheStormHasArrived** (posting as @TheStormHasArrived17 on Truth Social) is a commentator within the online Truth Community whose analysis has appeared in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news briefs.[1]

## Commentary

In the June 8, 2023 Badlands News Brief, TheStormHasArrived summarized the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]]'s introduction of the [[ace-act|ACE Act]], noting that the bill would require voter identification, restrict ballot harvesting, guarantee public observation of the election process, secure mail-in ballots, clean voter rolls, and bar non-citizens from voting.[1] While observing that the legislation applied specifically to the District of Columbia, the commentator quoted [[james-comer|James Comer]]'s hope that the reforms would serve as a model for other states, and concluded: "This is what we need in every state (and more.)"[1]
