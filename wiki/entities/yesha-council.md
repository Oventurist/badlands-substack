# Yesha Council

The Yesha Council is the umbrella body representing Israeli settlements in the West Bank, functioning as the principal political organ of the settler movement.[1]

## Role in the 2024 settlement construction push

In May 2024, Israeli finance minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] convened settlement mayors at the Finance Ministry and warned them that the establishment of a Palestinian state was becoming a "tangible danger" driven by "international involvement" and the "consent of leftist elements" in Israel.[1] [[israel-ganz|Israel Ganz]], newly named chairman of the Yesha Council, attended the meeting along with the mayors of the largest West Bank settlements, including Beitar Illit, Modiin Illit and Maale Adumim.[1]

At the meeting Smotrich described his efforts to pressure Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] into convening a key administrative body that could authorize new settlement construction plans.[1] The push came against the backdrop of the United Nations General Assembly's vote granting Palestine new rights and reviving its membership bid.[1]
