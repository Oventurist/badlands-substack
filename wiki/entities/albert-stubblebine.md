# Albert Stubblebine

Major General Albert Stubblebine was a United States Army officer under whom, according to Matthew Ehret, [[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]] played a direct role overseeing a coup within the US military and the emergence of a Mithraic revival of neo-Templars.[1]

Stubblebine commissioned Aquino's 1980 essay [[from-psyop-to-mindwar|From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory]], co-authored with Colonel [[paul-vallely|Paul Vallely]], which served as the directing manifesto of the ensuing "revolution in military affairs" and introduced [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] mind control research, the SRI Stargate Project and [[esalen-institute|Esalen Institute]] drug experiments into military training.[1]
