# Frank Fahrenkopf Jr.

Frank Fahrenkopf Jr. is a former chairman of the Republican National Committee and a longtime co-chair of the [[commission-on-presidential-debates|Commission on Presidential Debates]], the bipartisan body that organized general-election presidential debates in the United States.[1]

## 2024 debate dispute

In April 2024, Fahrenkopf and fellow co-chair [[antonia-hernandez|Antonia Hernandez]] received a letter from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s co-campaign managers [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]] and [[chris-lacivita|Chris LaCivita]] demanding that the commission move its scheduled debates with President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] "much earlier" in the calendar and add additional matchups.[1] The commission had already announced three presidential debates and one vice-presidential debate to be held later in 2024, a schedule the Trump campaign argued left voters waiting too long, echoing a parallel appeal from television networks.[1]
