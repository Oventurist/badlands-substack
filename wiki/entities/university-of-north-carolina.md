# University of North Carolina

The University of North Carolina is a public flagship university and, like other land-grant institutions, provides an elite education subsidized by taxpayers for state residents.[1]

## Affirmative action litigation

UNC was one of the two pillars of American higher education whose admissions practices were before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] in the 2023 [[affirmative-action|affirmative action]] cases, alongside [[harvard-university|Harvard College]].[1] The university argued that, consistent with decades of Supreme Court precedent, a minority applicant's race could serve as an unenumerated plus factor that raised the applicant's chances of admission, and lower courts agreed, rejecting the lawsuits organized by [[edward-blum|Edward Blum]].[1] On June 29, 2023, the Supreme Court ruled 6–3 that the consideration of race in university admissions is unconstitutional, requiring UNC and institutions across American higher education to rework their admissions criteria.[1]
