# Port Authority of New York and New Jersey

The Port Authority of New York and New Jersey is the bi-state agency that operates major port, airport, and transportation infrastructure in the New York metropolitan region.[1]

## 2024 port strike preparations

Ahead of the threatened September–October 2024 dockworker strike, a Port Authority spokesperson said the agency was "coordinating with partners across the supply chain to prepare for any impacts" from a possible work stoppage by workers represented by the [[international-longshoremens-association|International Longshoremen's Association]], who were then negotiating with the [[united-states-maritime-alliance|United States Maritime Alliance]].[1] The spokesperson urged both sides "to find common ground and keep the cargo flowing for the good of the national economy," noting that $240 billion in goods move through its two ports annually and that such trade supports more than 600,000 local jobs.[1]

## Preparations for the 2024 dockworker strike

As a strike by dockworkers approached in late September 2024, a Port Authority spokesperson told CBS MoneyWatch that the agency was "coordinating with partners across the supply chain to prepare for any impacts" from a possible work stoppage by workers represented by the [[international-longshoremens-association|International Longshoremen's Association]] as they negotiated with the [[united-states-maritime-alliance|United States Maritime Alliance]].[2] "We urge both sides to find common ground and keep the cargo flowing for the good of the national economy," the spokesperson added, noting that $240 billion in goods move through the authority's two ports each year and that such trade supports more than 600,000 local jobs.[2]
