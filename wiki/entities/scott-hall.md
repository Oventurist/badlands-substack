# Scott Hall

Scott Hall is an Atlanta bail bondsman who became the first of the co-defendants in the Georgia election racketeering case to surrender to authorities.[1]

## Role in the Georgia case

Hall was charged by [[fani-willis|Fulton County District Attorney Fani Willis]] with crimes related to his alleged involvement in the Coffee County, Georgia, voting systems breach, one of the strands of the sprawling indictment that also named former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and seventeen other co-defendants.[1]

On Tuesday, August 22, 2023, Hall turned himself in at the [[fulton-county|Fulton County]] jail, becoming the first defendant in the case to do so; reporting treated the mechanics of his surrender as an early signal of how the remaining defendants would be processed.[1] He had signed a bond agreement with Willis the previous day, was given an inmate identification number, and was released after spending roughly an hour at the jail.[1] Coverage suggested his experience would likely demonstrate how most defendants in the case were to be treated.[1]

## Bond, August 21, 2023

On Monday, August 21, 2023, Fulton County Superior Court Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] set Hall's bond at $10,000 — the lowest among the defendants whose bonds were set that day, compared with $200,000 for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], $100,000 each for [[john-eastman|John Eastman]] and [[kenneth-chesebro|Kenneth Chesebro]], and $50,000 for [[ray-smith-iii|Ray Smith III]].[2] All defendants faced a noon Friday deadline to appear at the [[fulton-county-jail|Fulton County Jail]] for processing, and each bond agreement barred acts of intimidation against codefendants or witnesses.[2]
