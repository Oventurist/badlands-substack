# He Lifeng

He Lifeng is a Vice Premier of the People's Republic of [[china|China]] with responsibility for economic and trade affairs, and a participant in the January 2026 annual meeting of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] in Davos.[1]

## Davos 2026 remarks

At the Davos meeting, He Lifeng contributed to the forum's broader warnings against escalating trade conflict, stating that trade wars have "no winners."[1] His intervention came as the assembled leaders discussed the [[greenland-tariff-dispute-2026|U.S. tariff threats issued over Greenland]], the erosion of multilateral institutions, and calls for renewed cooperation on conflicts in Ukraine and the Middle East, with global markets falling amid fears of prolonged disruption.[1]

His remarks were notable in a session where European officials simultaneously floated deeper economic cooperation with China as a hedge against U.S. pressure.[1]

## 2023 economic talks with the United States

He Lifeng emerged as China's principal economic interlocutor with Washington in 2023, when he was described in U.S. reporting as the country's "new economic czar."[2] He was scheduled to meet U.S. Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]] on November 9 and 10, 2023, in advance of a possible meeting between [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and of the [[asia-pacific-economic-cooperation|APEC]] summit in San Francisco.[2] The Treasury Department announced the meetings on November 6, and Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesman Wang Wenbin confirmed that He would visit the United States from November 8 to 12.[2]

The announcement came shortly after Chinese state media revealed that He had become head of two separate Chinese Communist Party committees created by Xi in March 2023 — a restructuring widely read as bringing China's financial system under the Party's direct control.[2]
