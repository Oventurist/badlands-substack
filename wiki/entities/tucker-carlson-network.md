# Tucker Carlson Network

The Tucker Carlson Network is a subscription streaming service launched by former Fox News host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], announced in December 2023.[1] The service charges $9 a month — or $72 a year for annual subscribers — for interviews, documentaries, behind-the-scenes clips, and "access to Tucker's personal inbox to ask him anything that's on your mind."[1]

## Launch and rationale

In a video posted to his website on Monday, December 11, 2023, Carlson said the venture was prompted by a sense that "something big is coming," citing the 2024 presidential election and "entire populations" migrating to new locations.[1] He said "suddenly everything seems at stake — control of the world, and your soul," and downplayed his former employer [[fox-news|Fox News]] and its rivals on the grounds that "big media companies won't help" his audience understand current trends.[1]

## Background

The paid service followed Carlson's June 2023 launch of a free show on [[x|X]], the platform formerly known as Twitter, after his ouster from Fox News.[1] On X he interviewed figures including [[alex-jones|Alex Jones]] — who had been ordered by a court to pay almost $1 billion over his claim that the 2012 Sandy Hook school massacre was a hoax — and former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] With almost 11 million followers on X, Carlson was reported to be betting that a portion of that audience would pay for monthly programming.[1]
