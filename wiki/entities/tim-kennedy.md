# Tim Kennedy

**Tim Kennedy** is a veteran of the U.S. Army's 7th Special Forces Group who publicized his own account of government obstruction of volunteer rescue efforts in the aftermath of [[hurricane-helene|Hurricane Helene]] in 2024.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited Kennedy's report alongside that of South Carolina pilot [[jordan-seidhom|Jordan Seidhom]], writing that stories of this kind were "being reported all over social media" and reflected subversive actors within government at all levels working to prevent private citizens from risking their lives to save others.[1] The pairing of a former narcotics-unit commander and a Special Forces veteran was used to argue that the obstruction was not directed at unqualified amateurs but at capable operators filling a gap the federal response had left open.[1]
