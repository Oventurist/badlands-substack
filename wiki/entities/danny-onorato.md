# Danny Onorato

Danny Onorato is a defence attorney of [[schertler-onorato-mead-and-sears|Schertler, Onorato, Mead & Sears]] who, with [[stuart-sears|Stuart A. Sears]], represented [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] at his 2022 trial before Judge [[anthony-trenga|Anthony J. Trenga]] in Alexandria, Virginia.[1]

## Role on the opening day

Onorato raised a rule-of-completeness objection to the government's proposed use of a 26 July 2016 email between [[sergei-millian|Sergei Millian]] and a Mr. Zlodorev, telling the court that the prosecution had excised the final message from the chain and that the defence wanted the jury to see what Zlodorev had told Millian about his relationship with Danchenko at the time.[1] Defence counsel also resisted admission of the Millian and [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Dolan]] emails on the ground that the agents interviewing Danchenko had never specifically asked for them, arguing the government was "charging a false statement case by affirmative representation but then trying to backdoor a concealment theory, which would be very difficult to prove in this case because there was no duty to disclose."[1] [[john-durham|Durham]] answered that argument by pointing to the [[danchenko-immunity-agreement|immunity agreement]] requiring full and complete disclosure.[1]

Onorato delivered the defence's opening statement.[1] After the jury was excused, Durham brought to the judge Onorato's accusation that Assistant Special Counsel [[michael-keilty|Michael Keilty]] had lied in the prosecution's opening; Trenga did not accept the accusation, and refused to entertain it further when Onorato pressed the point.[1]
