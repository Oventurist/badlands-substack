# Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn

**Alexander Solzhenitsyn** was a Russian writer and dissident whose essay [[live-not-by-lies|"Live Not by Lies"]] is cited by Badlands Media contributors as a moral framework for resisting state falsehood.[1]

## Invocation in the 2026 midterm cycle

In June 2026 Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] told readers navigating the coming midterm elections to "take comfort and direction from Alexander Solzhenitsyn," quoting an extended passage from "Live Not by Lies."[1] The excerpt argues that nothing changes so long as people daily acknowledge and strengthen the lies that encircle them; that violence, having lost confidence in itself, must summon falsehood as its ally because it can conceal itself with nothing else; and that lies in turn can be maintained only by violence.[1] Its practical conclusion — that what the system demands is merely obedience to lies and daily participation in them, and that the accessible key to liberation is therefore personal non-participation in lies — was presented as directly applicable to American political life.[1]

Ashe reported that posting the excerpt drew a reply she used to sharpen the point, writing that the biggest lie is that elections are real and that Americans consented to be governed through a lawful process, and that this lie remains the foundation of all the others precisely because it is the most uncomfortable to confront.[1]
