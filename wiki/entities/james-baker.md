# James Baker

James Baker was a very close and loyal friend of [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and served in his administration.[1] His family founded [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]] in Houston, an institution the article describes as "an optimal cover for CIA activities."[1]

When Bush, then director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], wished to place his son [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] as a non-official-cover officer, all he had to do was call Baker to have him hired into the bank's international division — the usual branch where CIA NOCs were placed within banks and investment firms.[1]
