# Jason Smith

Jason Smith is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Missouri who played a public role in releasing testimony from [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] whistleblowers concerning the federal investigation of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]].[1]

## Role in the IRS whistleblower disclosures

In June 2023, Smith stated that U.S. Attorney [[david-weiss|David Weiss]] had sought to be appointed as a special counsel in the Hunter Biden case during the previous year and had been denied.[1] That claim formed part of a broader set of whistleblower allegations that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] had buried evidence of Hunter Biden's tax crimes and had reduced recommended felony charges to two misdemeanors.[1]
