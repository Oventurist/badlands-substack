# Minneapolis Police Department

The Minneapolis Police Department (MPD) is the municipal law enforcement agency serving [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]], Minnesota.[1]

## October 2023 pro-Palestinian rally incident

On Sunday, October 22, 2023, the department received several reports of disturbances surrounding a demonstration held in support of Palestinians near the intersection of Hennepin Avenue and Lyndale Avenue at approximately 3:23 p.m.[1] Various witnesses reported that a vehicle drove through the crowd, and one person reported hearing the sound of a gunshot.[1]

MPD stated that no injuries had been reported in connection with the demonstrations, that no victims had come forward, and that no arrests had been made, while follow-up investigations remained under way.[1] The rally had been organized with the help of the [[minnesota-anti-war-committee|Minnesota Anti-War Committee]], which announced a further protest for the following Wednesday outside the St. Paul office of Representative [[betty-mccollum|Betty McCollum]].[1]
