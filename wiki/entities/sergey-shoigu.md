# Sergey Shoigu

Sergey Shoigu is a Russian official who, as Defense Minister, has led a plan for the construction of five major Arctic cities each capable of supporting up to one million people — a program contrasted in the Badlands account with the West's failure to build any new cities in several generations.[1]

## Siberian masterplan

Reporting by Atle Staalesen in Arctic Today described Shoigu as viewing his masterplan for Siberia as closely connected to nearby Chinese markets, while also arguing that the new cities will be important for Arctic development.[1] Shoigu invoked the 18th-century scientist and writer Mikhail Lomonosov, who wrote that "Russian power will grow with Siberia and the Arctic Ocean," and argued that Lomonosov did not coincidentally connect the two regions: "They should be developed together and not separately," adding that "the focus on the development of the Siberian region is both timely and reasonable."[1]

The program is treated as part of the broader multipolar infrastructure agenda encompassing the [[international-north-south-transport-corridor|International North-South Transportation Corridor]], the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]] and the [[polar-silk-road|Polar Silk Road]].[1]
