# Fannie Mae

Fannie Mae — formally the Federal National Mortgage Association — is a government-sponsored enterprise that purchases and securitizes residential mortgages, making it one of the central institutions in the United States housing finance system. Together with its sibling enterprise [[freddie-mac|Freddie Mac]], it stands behind a large share of American mortgage credit, and both entities have operated under federal conservatorship supervised by the [[federal-housing-finance-agency|Federal Housing Finance Agency]] since the 2008 financial crisis.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

In the June 3, 2026 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] Brief, Fannie Mae appears in the context of the appointment of [[william-pulte|William J. Pulte]] as Acting [[office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence|Director of National Intelligence]]. Pulte, who serves as director of the FHFA, is also chairman of both Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, and the Brief noted that he would retain all of those existing housing-finance roles while serving in the acting intelligence position. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] cited Pulte's stewardship of "some of the nation's most sensitive financial matters, including the safety and soundness of U.S. housing markets," as evidence of his qualifications for the intelligence post.

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the overlap skeptically, questioning how leadership of the sprawling intelligence community — eighteen agencies coordinated through ODNI — could be handled part-time by an official who simultaneously chairs the two largest housing enterprises in the country.
