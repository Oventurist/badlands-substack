# Janette Nesheiwat

Janette Nesheiwat is an American physician who was President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s initial nominee for Surgeon General of the United States in 2025.[1] Her nomination was withdrawn in May 2025 over allegations that she had inflated her credentials by claiming she held a degree from the University of Arkansas School of Medicine.[1] According to accounts of the controversy, she had in fact graduated from a medical school in the Caribbean and completed her residency in Arkansas.[1]

Following the withdrawal, Trump nominated [[casey-means|Casey Means]] for the post, a choice that generated its own backlash and was publicly defended by Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]].[1]
