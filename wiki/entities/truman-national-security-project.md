# Truman National Security Project

The Truman National Security Project (also referred to as the Truman Center) is a Washington national-security organisation whose board of directors has included [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] and [[sally-painter|Sally Painter]] of [[blue-star-strategies|Blue Star Strategies]].[1]

Reporting in 2023 noted that Hunter Biden had served on a national security advisory board alongside Sullivan, who went on to become National Security Advisor.[1] Emails from [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden's abandoned laptop]] show that Painter recruited Hunter to the board: on 16 February 2011 she asked whether he would consider a position, and a March 2011 exchange titled "Truman Board" recorded Hunter saying he was "excited to get involved" while Painter reported he had been a "big hit" and expected a formal offer within three weeks.[1] Sullivan did not join the board until roughly 2017, after leaving the [[state-department|State Department]].[1]

The organisation also served as a venue for contacts between Painter and [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]]: in his December 2020 transcribed Senate interview, Blinken referenced a 27 June 2016 email in which Painter mentioned having spoken with him at a Truman event and asked to meet about "troubling events we are seeing in Ukraine."[1]
