# Dina Powell McCormick

Dina Powell McCormick is the president and vice chair of [[meta-platforms|Meta]], the parent company of Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. She previously served in the [[white-house|White House]] as deputy national security adviser for strategy under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and held senior roles in the State Department and Goldman Sachs.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a White House official, that Powell McCormick was among the executives expected to accompany Trump on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. Her presence placed Meta among a delegation of American technology, industrial and financial leadership — including [[tim-cook|Tim Cook]] of [[apple-inc|Apple]] and [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] of Tesla — traveling ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
