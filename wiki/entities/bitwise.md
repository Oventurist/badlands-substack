# Bitwise

Bitwise is a crypto-focused asset manager with roughly $5 billion under management that publishes periodic research on digital asset markets, including a quarterly crypto market report.[1]

## Q4 2024 Bitcoin ownership data

In an X post on 10 February 2025, Bitwise circulated an excerpt from its fourth-quarter 2024 crypto market report addressing the ownership structure of [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]]. The firm argued that the steady stream of headlines about institutional purchases — exchange-traded fund inflows and corporate treasury buying by companies such as MicroStrategy — had created a misleading impression that Bitcoin was becoming an institutionally controlled asset.[1]

According to the data Bitwise highlighted, as of 31 December 2024 individuals remained by far the largest holders of Bitcoin, controlling 69.4% of supply, compared with 6.1% held by investment funds, 4.4% by businesses, and just 1.4% by governments.[1] The implication drawn by commentators was that sovereign buyers seeking Bitcoin reserves would have to purchase from individual holders willing to sell, since no large uncommitted institutional float exists.[1] Badlands commentary summarised the finding as a structural advantage for retail holders, wishing "good luck to the rest of the world who will have to battle for a fraction of BitPower."[1]
