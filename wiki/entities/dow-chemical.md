# Dow Chemical

Dow Chemical is the American chemical corporation that, in the account given by the second installment of the America for Sale series, paid [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] millions of dollars while he simultaneously held an unpaid post at [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s [[state-department|State Department]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Politico is quoted reporting that while serving as Clinton's special envoy for economic affairs in Northern Ireland — reaching out to global corporations to solicit investment — Kelly was also working for two of those corporations as a private consultant, earning about $2.4 million from Dow Chemical, a longtime client of his and one of the firms that participated in Clinton's Ireland initiative.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The payments were routed through FTI Consulting, Kelly's former employer, between August 2009 and January 2011, during a period when his two-year non-compete agreement barred him from consulting independently but permitted continued part-time work for FTI on the Dow and Coca-Cola accounts.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

The article states that Kelly deliberately took no salary from the State Department and hired his own staff, which allowed him to retain the Dow income under "[[special-government-employee|special government employee]]" style arrangements and made it easier to obscure how he was being compensated.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Dow and Coca-Cola were relationships Kelly chose to keep, and both would later become among the first and biggest accounts of [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]], the firm he launched in June 2011 with [[doug-band|Doug Band]] and [[paul-keary|Paul Keary]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)
