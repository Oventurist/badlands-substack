# Phil Jones

Phil Jones is a British climate scientist associated with the University of East Anglia's Climatic Research Unit, named by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] in his June 5, 2025 Badlands Media essay on climate science.[1] Ehret states that the famous "hockey stick" temperature model, used by [[michael-mann|Michael Mann]] to erase the [[medieval-warm-period|medieval warming period]], was crafted with Jones's help.[1]

The East Anglia unit's role places Jones within the broader controversy the article gestures toward — including the [[climategate]] affair over the handling and presentation of temperature data — which Ehret treats as evidence that the climate consensus rests on statistical manipulation rather than physical demonstration that [[carbon-dioxide|carbon dioxide]] drives temperature.[1]
