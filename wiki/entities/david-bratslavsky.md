# David Bratslavsky

David Bratslavsky is a technology entrepreneur named among the defendants charged in the 2026 global insider trading case brought by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1]

## Background and charges

Bratslavsky is described as having served as executive director of the U.S.-Israel Business Council and as associate for policy and planning at the Conference of Presidents of Major American Jewish Organizations.[1] He was charged as part of the alleged criminal group, with the DOJ claiming the defendants worked together and that much of their time together was documented on film.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted public remarks in which Bratslavsky advocated for Jewish people to set aside differences and work together, offering it as commentary on the alleged scheme.[1] These characterizations are the analyst's interpretation rather than allegations contained in the indictment.[1]
