# Oversight Project

The Oversight Project is an American government-accountability and litigation organization that pursues transparency actions against federal officials and agencies.[1]

## Release of the Biden ghostwriter tapes

In July 2026 the Oversight Project published more than seventy hours of recordings of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] speaking with his memoir ghostwriter [[mark-zwonitzer|Mark Zwonitzer]], obtained during special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s classified documents probe.[1] The 2016 and 2017 recordings were released after a lengthy legal fight in which Biden's attorneys spent months attempting to block publication before abandoning their lawsuit; a judge accepted the dismissal on the preceding Monday.[1] In them Biden warns, "Some of this may be classified … So, be careful," and elsewhere says, "this is classified."[1] The organization's Vice President of Litigation, [[jeff-clark|Jeff Clark]], said the recordings should force a full reexamination of the decision to give Biden a pass for keeping and disclosing classified information.[1]
