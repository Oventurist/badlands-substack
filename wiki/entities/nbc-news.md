# NBC News

NBC News is a major American broadcast news organization whose reporting features in coverage of the 2026 [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] crisis.[1]

## Reporting on Project Freedom

A Middle Eastern diplomat told NBC News that the United States coordinated only with [[oman|Oman]] after President [[donald-trump|Trump]] publicly announced [[project-freedom|Project Freedom]], the naval escort mission designed to protect commercial shipping through the strait; the same diplomat said Omani officials were "not upset or angry" over the sequence of events.[1] The [[white-house|White House]] disputed claims that Gulf allies had been caught off guard, stating that regional partners were informed in advance.[1]

An NBC News report also cited a Saudi source saying Trump and Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] "have been in touch regularly," with Saudi officials coordinating as well with Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], [[centcom|U.S. Central Command]], and Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]].[1]

## Coverage of Kurt Olsen (June 2026)

In June 2026 NBC News reported that election-focused nongovernmental organizations were raising concerns about [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]]'s appointment to a senior [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] position because of his post-2020 election litigation.[2] The report described Olsen as "an election denier who tried to overturn Donald Trump's 2020 election loss" while characterizing his principal critic, [[free-speech-for-people|Free Speech For People]], as a nonprofit focused "on free and fair elections and rooting out corruption in government."[2] Badlands writers cited that framing as an example of asymmetric labeling, noting that the same organization had campaigned to impeach [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] the day after Inauguration Day in 2017.[2]

## November 2023 national poll

In November 2023 NBC News published a national poll, conducted by Democratic pollster [[jeff-horwitt|Jeff Horwitt]] of [[hart-research-associates|Hart Research Associates]] with Republican pollster [[bill-mcinturff|Bill McInturff]] of [[public-opinion-strategies|Public Opinion Strategies]], showing President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s approval rating at 40 percent — the lowest of his presidency — amid broad disapproval of his handling of foreign policy and the Israel-Hamas war.[3] The survey was the first NBC poll to show Biden trailing former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a hypothetical general-election matchup, though within the margin of error.[3]

## March 2023 Crimea dispatch

In March 2023 an NBC News correspondent broadcasting from inside [[crimea|Crimea]] described the peninsula as a militarized town locked in "a very, very dangerous standoff," questioned the feasibility of the demilitarization sought by [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]], and reported that Crimeans regard themselves as Russians.[4] A companion NBC written report noted that Kyiv, its Western allies and the [[united-nations|United Nations]] deem the 2014 annexation illegal and that [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] had vowed to retake Crimea, while profiling a 73-year-old resident who "speaks Russian, feels Russian and lives here."[4] Ukrainian officials and pro-Kyiv pundits were reported to be outraged at the segment.[4] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] joked that NBC had "accidentally sent a based media correspondent to the Crimea," and treated the coverage as a sign the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] was losing control of the Ukraine narrative.[4]

## Ronna McDaniel hiring reversal

In March 2024 NBC News dropped former Republican National Committee boss [[ronna-mcdaniel|Ronna McDaniel]] as a paid contributor less than a week after announcing her hiring, in what the New York Post described as a humiliating turnaround following a mutiny among the network's stars.[5] MSNBC prime-time headliner [[rachel-maddow|Rachel Maddow]] and the co-hosts of "Morning Joe" were among those who sparked the revolt over bringing the [[donald-trump|Trump]]-backing politico on board.[5] NBCUniversal chairman [[cesar-conde|Cesar Conde]] took "full responsibility" for both hiring and firing McDaniel in a memo to furious staffers obtained by the Post.[5]
