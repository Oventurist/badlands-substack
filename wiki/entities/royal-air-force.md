# Royal Air Force

The Royal Air Force (RAF) is the aerial warfare service branch of the United Kingdom's armed forces, operating under the [[uk-ministry-of-defence|Ministry of Defence]].[1]

## Epstein trafficking inquiry

In February 2026 the RAF's parent department opened a review of military flight records after revelations that [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s private jet may have landed at RAF bases, including RAF Northolt in west London and RAF Marham in Norfolk.[1] The review sought to determine whether those sites had been used in connection with Epstein's criminal activities, in particular the trafficking of women into the United Kingdom.[1]

Defence Secretary [[john-healey|John Healey]] directed officials to search more than two decades of records, while police separately examined flight logs and airport movements.[1]


## Eastern Sentry patrols over Poland (September 2025)

In September 2025 RAF Typhoons based in Lincolnshire were assigned to conduct air defense missions over [[poland|Poland]] as part of the [[nato|NATO]] initiative [[eastern-sentry|Eastern Sentry]], launched after Russian drones breached Polish airspace.[2] The aircraft operated alongside forces from Denmark, France and Germany to strengthen NATO's eastern defenses, with a Voyager refuelling aircraft supporting the missions.[2] Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Sir Keir Starmer]] said Russia's "reckless behaviour is a direct threat to European security," while Defence Secretary [[john-healey|John Healey]] said NATO had responded with unity.[2]

## Strategic bombing doctrine under Harris

The Badlands News Brief's discussion of the September 2024 [[darryl-cooper|Darryl Cooper]] interview recounted that, after British forces lost their foothold on the continent at Dunkirk, [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]] directed the RAF into continuous firebombing runs against cities in Germany and the Nazi-occupied Netherlands.[3] In one incident the RAF mistakenly bombed a Dutch town, killing over 500 civilians, when the intended target was an adjacent forest storing Nazi V-2 rockets.[3] Churchill had his Marshal of the Royal Air Force, [[arthur-harris|Arthur Harris]], draw up the new doctrine that guided the "strategic bombing" campaigns for all six years of the war, superseding the [[just-war-theory|Just War Theory]] tradition of protecting civilian life.[3]
