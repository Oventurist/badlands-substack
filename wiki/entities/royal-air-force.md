# Royal Air Force

The Royal Air Force (RAF) is the aerial warfare service of the United Kingdom, operating under the [[uk-ministry-of-defence|Ministry of Defence]]. Its network of airbases includes RAF Northolt in northwest London, historically used for government and VIP movements, and RAF Marham in Norfolk.

## Epstein trafficking inquiry

The February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that RAF facilities became the subject of a Ministry of Defence review after revelations that the private jet of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] may have landed at RAF bases, specifically naming RAF Northolt and RAF Marham. The disclosure raised the question of whether British military airfields had been used in connection with Epstein's criminal activities, potentially bypassing ordinary civilian border and customs scrutiny.

Defence Secretary [[john-healey|John Healey]] directed officials to search more than two decades of ministry records for evidence that Epstein used RAF facilities to traffic women into the United Kingdom, while police separately examined flight logs and airport movements.
