# Greg Gutfeld

Greg Gutfeld is a host on [[fox-news|Fox News]] known for a comedic, contrarian on-air style and for a long record of criticizing his own network.[1]

## Remarks on Tucker Carlson's removal

In early December 2023 Gutfeld said on his program that [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] had been pushed off Fox News by special interest groups tied to political interests.[1] He made the comments while discussing [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s public rebuke of advertisers who had attempted, in Musk's telling, to blackmail him — joking that such pressure was "like extorting Jerry Nadler with salad, or blackmailing sports fans by threatening to cancel PBS."[1]

Gutfeld also described Musk as the last man standing against what he called the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship-industrial complex]], which he characterized as a combination of government, media and technology forces.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that mainstream coverage treated Gutfeld's remarks as shocking, but argued that Gutfeld had criticized Fox for years and that such criticism is part of his brand, tolerated by the network so long as it does not touch the core narratives around the 2020 election or January 6, 2021.[1] In this reading, Fox did not capitulate to the censorship-industrial complex so much as constitute part of it, functioning as the narrative outlet for the right side of what Badlands writers call the uniparty.[1] The same commentary recounted Carlson's claim that he filmed an interview with former Capitol Police chief Steven Sund that was never aired.[1]
