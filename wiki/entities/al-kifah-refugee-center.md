# Al Kifah Refugee Center

The Al Kifah Refugee Center was a Brooklyn, New York organization that Badlands Media identifies as the recruiting ground used by the United States to draw American Muslims into the Afghan jihad during [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]].[1]

Egyptian double agent [[ali-mohamed|Ali Abdul Saoud Mohamed]] was sent to the center to recruit young American Muslims to go fight in [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]], preaching a message of holy war to bolster the ranks of the [[mujahideen]].[1] The center functioned in the article's telling as the U.S.-side counterpart to [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]]'s [[maktab-al-khidamat|Maktab al-Khidamat]], the overseas services bureau that channeled foreign volunteers into the war.[1]
