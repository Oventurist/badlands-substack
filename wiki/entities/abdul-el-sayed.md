# Abdul El-Sayed

Abdul El-Sayed is a Michigan Democratic Senate candidate backed by [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]], cited in June 2026 reporting on the ideological struggle within the Democratic Party.[1]

Amid a string of progressive primary successes across multiple states, El-Sayed said voters are increasingly receptive to progressive candidates.[1] Establishment-aligned Democrats responded by increasing support for rival candidates in key races, including Michigan's Senate contest.[1] The debate followed progressive victories in New York City, where left-leaning candidates won several Democratic contests and defeated two incumbent House members, prompting centrist strategists to warn of a broader ideological shift.[1]
