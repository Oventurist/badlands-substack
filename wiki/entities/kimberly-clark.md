# Kimberly-Clark

Kimberly-Clark is an American consumer products corporation, best known as the maker of Kleenex tissues and a range of paper and personal-care goods.[1]

## 2025 Kenvue merger and share collapse

In November 2025, Kimberly-Clark announced plans to acquire [[kenvue|Kenvue]], the producer of Tylenol, in a cash-and-stock transaction valued at approximately $48.7 billion — a deal widely reported as a roughly $40 billion merger. The announcement sent Kenvue shares up about 20 percent, while Kimberly-Clark shares fell sharply, trading down roughly 14.5 percent in late-afternoon trading.[1]

Had the losses held into the close, the decline would have marked the company's steepest one-day drop since October 16, 1987 — days before the Black Monday crash of October 19, 1987 — and left the shares at their lowest point since late 2019.[1]

Wall Street analysts were divided on the transaction. Some anticipated short-term pressure on Kimberly-Clark's stock, while others praised the combination as "strategically transformative."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host Burning Bright cited the merger as further evidence for a "Too Small to Fail" thesis he laid out in 2022, which posits that rather than a single total market crash, bloated corporations will collapse under the weight of their own debt, and that a wave of consolidation would precede that collapse.[1]
