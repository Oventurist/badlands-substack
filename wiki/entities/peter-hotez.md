# Peter Hotez

Peter Hotez is an American vaccine scientist and physician who became a prominent media spokesman on vaccination during and after the COVID-19 era. In June 2023, Badlands Media characterized him as the mainstream media's newly "anointed" vaccine-promoting talking head following the departure of Anthony Fauci from government service, describing Hotez as having "exited stage left" into the role Fauci vacated.[1]

## Joe Rogan podcast dispute

Hotez appeared on [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]]'s podcast in June 2023, where, according to the account relayed by Badlands, he criticized presidential candidate [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] over Kennedy's vaccine positions while simultaneously telling Rogan he would be "uncomfortable" debating him directly.[1] Rogan responded by offering to donate $100,000 to a charity of Hotez's choosing if he would debate Kennedy about the COVID-19 vaccines on The Joe Rogan Experience.[1] Kennedy's own response to the refusal was blunt and dismissive.[1]

Other public figures subsequently added pledges to the pot, and by the time of the Badlands brief the aggregate challenge exceeded $1.52 million, with individual pledges under $1,000 tracked but excluded from the running total.[1] Hotez did not accept.

## Commentary and framing

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that pharmaceutical-aligned influencers rallied to Hotez's defense by asserting that no credentialed scientist should ever debate an "uncredentialed science denier," and by deploying the label "anti-vaxxer" against critics.[1] She linked this rhetorical pattern to the term "conspiracy theorist," which she attributed to the [[cia|CIA]] in the aftermath of the assassination of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], contending that the same delegitimizing tactics were being applied to Kennedy's nephew decades later.[1] The episode was presented in the brief as emblematic of a broader contest over who is permitted to speak with authority about vaccine safety.
