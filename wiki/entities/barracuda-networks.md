# Barracuda Networks

**Barracuda** is a cybersecurity company cited in Part 8 of [[the-werewolf-game|The Werewolf Game]] series at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as the source of statistics on automated web traffic.[1]

## Bot attacks report

The article references a Barracuda bot-attacks report which it says found that approximately 64% of web activity is conducted by bots, and that 40% of all online activity consists of malicious bots running various forms of attack script.[1] The author states that, not being a computer expert, he can neither easily verify nor debunk those numbers, but that as a daily internet user they look about right to him.[1] The figures are used as the principal external evidence for the [[dead-internet-theory|dead internet theory]] and, in turn, for the essay's concept of [[cyber-necromancy|cyber-necromancy]].[1] Malicious bots are described in the article as able to run attack scripts, scrape private data and skew the feedback of information.[1]
