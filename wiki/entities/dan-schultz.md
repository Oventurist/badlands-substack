# Dan Schultz

**Dan Schultz** is the American activist credited with formulating and popularizing the [[precinct-strategy|Precinct Strategy]], a grassroots plan urging conservatives to fill vacant Republican precinct committeeman slots in order to control party organization from the bottom up.[1]

## Trump endorsement

Schultz's work received an explicit endorsement from [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] on February 27, 2022, in a statement quoted by Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]]: "The Precinct Strategy, as explained by Dan Schultz is a great way to restore our Republic by transforming our Party from currently at less than half-strength at the precinct level into a full-strength Get Out The Vote powerhouse—as it should be. Make America Great Again!"[1]

## Reception in the Badlands corpus

Gillespie points readers concerned about election integrity toward Schultz's Precinct Strategy training as a practical way to prepare for the 2024 election, treating it as one of the individual "pushes" that build the [[flywheel-of-freedom|Flywheel of Freedom]].[1]
