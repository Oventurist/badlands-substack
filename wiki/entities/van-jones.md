# Van Jones

Van Jones is an American political commentator and former Obama administration official who has been cited as an establishment-adjacent voice acknowledging elite capture of both major American political parties.[1]

## Critique of neoliberal capture

Speaking to YES! Magazine, Jones acknowledged the influence of economic elites on both major political parties, stating that both have been dominated by a neoliberal agenda in which corporations and Wall Street are more concerned about global trade deals than about rebuilding American cities and educating American children.[1]

Badlands Media commentary pairs this observation with [[chris-hayes|Chris Hayes]]'s discussion in Twilight of the Elites of "cognitive capture," the process by which sustained immersion in elite circles erodes journalistic independence and objectivity.[1] Together these statements are presented as evidence from within the establishment media of the influence of powerful entities on narrative formation.[1]
