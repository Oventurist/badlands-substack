# Silicon Valley

Silicon Valley is the San Francisco Bay Area technology corridor that houses the largest US software, semiconductor and platform companies together with the venture capital industry that finances them.[1] In Badlands commentary it functions less as a geographic label than as shorthand for the class of founders and investors whose political loyalties were visibly shifting during the 2024 election cycle.[1]

## Political realignment toward Trump

In an August 2024 essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the titans of [[big-tech|Big Tech]] and venture capital were seeking a working relationship, though not everyone in Silicon Valley was looking to become friendly with Trump — some, such as Google, were doubling down in an effort to keep him out of the White House.[1] Those holdouts, in his framing, remain loyal to the corrupt entities that in some cases created their technology, funded their businesses, and manipulated the system to let them cannibalize competitors.[1] Carlson identified [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], with his venture capital background, as the bridge figure who made the partnership credible.[1]

## Decay of the Bay Area

Carlson argued it is no coincidence that the Bay Area has been "decimated by woke, liberal, Deep State policies," since this is the region so many in Big Tech see every day.[1] Walking those streets and observing the visible human damage, he wrote, the tech leadership can see the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]]'s plan and how it is being implemented.[1] He asked pointedly who represents the area, and whether Trump had been telling Big Tech that the Cabal plans to offshore their businesses.[1]

His conclusion was blunt: if America falls, so does Silicon Valley — the wealth generated there is worthless if the restaurants close, cars are vandalized, and mansions become prisons.[1] He also noted the reports of tech billionaires building underground fortresses and suggested they may have been intended as protection from their own Cabal overlords rather than from angry mobs of citizens demanding justice.[1]

## Related events

Carlson raised the [[2023-silicon-valley-bank-run|Silicon Valley Bank]] bailout of early spring 2023 and the [[ftx|FTX]] collapse under [[sam-bankman-fried|Sam Bankman-Fried]] in the fall of 2022 as episodes whose relationship to the sector's political turn remained unexplained, floating the possibility that they were part of a white hat operation to take control of Big Tech and cryptocurrency.[1]
