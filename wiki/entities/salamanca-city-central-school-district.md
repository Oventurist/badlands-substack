# Salamanca City Central School District

The Salamanca City Central School District is a small rural public school district in western New York, located on the [[seneca-nation-of-indians|Seneca Nation of Indians]] reservation near the Pennsylvania border. It is the only public school district in the United States situated on Native American reservation land, and it drew national coverage in July 2026 over a proposal to place an AI-powered humanoid robot in its classrooms.

## The Realbotix pilot

As reported in the July 30, 2026 Badlands Brief, the district's board approved a purchase of nearly $60,000 from the Las Vegas company [[realbotix|Realbotix]] for a stationary humanoid robot named "Sally," intended to support high school robotics and technology students. The district's existing use of WozEd, a curriculum developed by Apple co-founder [[steve-wozniak|Steve Wozniak]], was cited as a reason Realbotix was considered a natural fit.

## Backlash and suspension

Parents, teachers and state officials objected on grounds of student data privacy and Realbotix's ties to a sister company producing hyper-realistic sex dolls. State Education Commissioner [[betty-rosa|Betty Rosa]] noted an inconsistency between the district's claim that the robot would not deliver instruction and a board presentation that described it as a "tutoring platform." The district paused the pilot, which remains on hold while it negotiates enhanced data-privacy agreements with the state. Badlands commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the episode as a case study in institutional detachment from community values, asking how the project survived beyond its first pitch.
