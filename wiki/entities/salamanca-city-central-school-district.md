# Salamanca City Central School District

Salamanca City Central School District is a small rural public school district in western New York, located on the [[seneca-nation-of-indians|Seneca Nation of Indians]] reservation near the Pennsylvania border.[1]

## AI robot pilot and backlash

In 2026 the district's board approved a purchase of nearly $60,000 from [[realbotix|Realbotix]] for a stationary AI-powered humanoid robot named "Sally," intended to support high school robotics and technology students. Sally can move her arms and hands but has nonworking legs.[1]

Parents, teachers, and state officials raised concerns about student data privacy and about Realbotix's ties to a sister company that manufactures hyper-realistic sex dolls. State Education Commissioner [[betty-rosa|Betty Rosa]] also noted a discrepancy in the district's account: officials said the robot would not deliver instruction, but a board presentation described it as a "tutoring platform."[1]

Following the backlash, the district paused the deployment. The pilot remains on hold while the district works out enhanced data-privacy agreements with the state.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked the district's insistence that Sally is "a professional educator" rather than a sex robot, satirizing an imagined exchange in which the district argues that disabling the legs and loading globalist education curriculum transforms the product, while parents reply simply, "Buy computers." She questioned how the project advanced past its first pitch at all. The district was reported to have found the Las Vegas-based company a natural fit because its robots are already programmed with WozEd, a curriculum from Apple co-founder [[steve-wozniak|Steve Wozniak]] that the district uses.[1]
