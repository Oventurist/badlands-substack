# The Recount

The Recount is a left-leaning, video-driven news website whose editor-in-chief until October 2023 was [[slade-sohmer|Slade Sohmer]].[1] Sohmer, who had previously served as managing editor of [[cnn|CNN]]'s now-defunct BEME video sharing app, left the outlet roughly a month before being charged in a Massachusetts court with possessing and disseminating "hundreds of child pornography images and videos."[1] He was released on $100,000 bail and pled not guilty to two counts of possession and two counts of dissemination.[1]

Commentary in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] situated the case within a run of prosecutions involving senior producers at [[abc-news|ABC]] and CNN over the preceding year, treating the pattern as evidence for arguments long associated with the [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]] controversy.[1]
