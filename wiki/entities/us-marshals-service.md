# US Marshals Service

The US Marshals Service is the federal law-enforcement agency of the Department of Justice responsible for fugitive apprehension, judicial security, prisoner transport, and asset forfeiture.[1]

In Badlands Media coverage of the March 2026 activation of the [[260th-special-purpose-brigade|260th Special Purpose Brigade]], the Marshals Service is identified as one of the brigade's primary partners, regularly working alongside the [[district-of-columbia-national-guard|District of Columbia National Guard]] in the capital.[1] The brigade was created to coordinate military support to civil authorities and to protect critical infrastructure in Washington, DC, integrating with federal and local security partners.[1]


## Memphis Safe Task Force (September 2025)

The U.S. Marshals Service was included in the [[memphis-state-task-force|Memphis Safe Task Force]] created by executive order of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on 15 September 2025, joining the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]] and [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] in a federal surge against violent crime in [[memphis|Memphis]], Tennessee, alongside [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops.[2]
