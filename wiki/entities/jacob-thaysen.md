# Jacob Thaysen

Jacob Thaysen is the chief executive officer of Illumina, the American biotechnology company that dominates the market for DNA sequencing instruments and reagents.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Thaysen was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. His inclusion placed Illumina in a delegation of American technology and industrial leadership traveling ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
