# Banastre Tarleton

Banastre Tarleton was the British officer who commanded the dragoons — mounted troops — opposing the Continental Army during the American Revolution, and is characterized by Badlands as "blood-thirsty."[1]

Tarleton is described as the villain both of the Badlands "Patriot Chronicles" article on Francis Marion and of Mel Gibson's film *The Patriot*.[1] Countering Tarleton's dragoons required cavalry expertise that [[george-washington|George Washington]] himself lacked, and which was supplied by Count [[casimir-pulaski|Casimir Pulaski]] and officers such as Hungarian Colonel [[michael-kovats|Michael Kovats]].[1]
