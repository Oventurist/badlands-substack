# Mykola Zlochevsky

Mykola Zlochevsky is a Ukrainian oligarch and the owner of the energy company [[burisma|Burisma]], the firm that paid [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] $83,000 a month to serve as a director.[1]

Zlochevsky appears in the account of a December 4, 2015 evening in Dubai that [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] was expected to describe in testimony to the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]].[1] Following a Burisma board dinner at the Burj Al Arab Hotel, Archer and Hunter Biden moved to the Four Seasons Resort Dubai at Jumeirah Beach, where senior Burisma executive [[vadym-pozharskyi|Vadym Pozharskyi]] telephoned to locate them because Zlochevsky urgently needed to speak with Hunter.[1] The episode forms part of the wider congressional inquiry into whether Hunter Biden monetised access to his father, then Vice President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]
