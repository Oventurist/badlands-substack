# Bezalel Smotrich

Bezalel Smotrich is the Israeli Finance Minister, identified in April 2026 Badlands coverage as a leading advocate for the formal territorial expansion of [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Expansionist proposals

Smotrich called for Israel to formally expand its borders to include all of Palestine as well as parts of [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and [[syria|Syria]].[1] His stated justification was that Israel's existing borders do not provide topography that is easy to defend, and that security therefore required redrawing them.[1] Badlands commentary noted that carrying out such a plan would mean erasing entire villages from the map and driving thousands of people from their homeland, and reported that Israel had already destroyed more than 62,000 structures, including apartment buildings and single-family homes.[1]

## Context

Smotrich's proposals were reported alongside Israel's formal acknowledgement of an "Advanced Defense Zone" on the Lebanese side of the Israel–Lebanon border, where the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] warned that anyone present would be shot.[1] Together these developments framed a period in which Israeli policy toward Lebanon and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] hardened even as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] extended the Israel–Hezbollah ceasefire.[1]

## "The border must be the Litani" (March 2026)

On March 23, 2026, Smotrich said Israel's border "must be the [[litani-river|Litani]]," signaling support for extending Israeli control northward into southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[2] Badlands commentary characterized the statement as a call for the annexation of southern Lebanon up to the river.[2] A day later, Defense Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] announced that Israeli forces would occupy territory up to the Litani and control bridges and a broader "security zone," turning Smotrich's rhetorical position into declared military policy.[2] [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] called any such occupation an "existential threat," and the United Nations described the prospect of a sustained Israeli presence as "very much concerning."[2]

## Call to dismantle the Kiryat Gat headquarters

In January 2026, as Israel weighed participation in President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], Smotrich said through his office that "the time has come to dismantle the headquarters in Kiryat Gat," referring to the [[civil-military-coordination-centre|Civil Military Coordination Centre]] established by the United States in October to host civilian and military personnel from countries participating in the [[international-stabilization-force|International Stabilization Force]].[3] He argued that dismantling the centre would let Israel remove representatives from "countries like Egypt and Britain that are hostile to Israel and undermine its security," and declined to explain why he singled out the United Kingdom.[3]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Britain and Egypt are, with the United States, Israel's two historic allies, and described Smotrich as a "stone cold sociopath" whose ultra-Orthodox settler constituency would be triggered by overt Israeli participation in aid to Gazans.[3] He predicted that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] would ultimately accept the Board invitation and persuade Smotrich that it is better to be at the table than outside it, while Smotrich would begrudgingly accept the situation and use it to catalyze fervor among the settlers — possibly to the point of protests or attacks outside the headquarters.[3]

## Declaration against the ICC and the UN (May 2026)

In May 2026, Smotrich released a recorded statement accusing the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] of seeking an arrest warrant against him and other members of the [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] government.[4] He characterized such acts as declarations of war and said Israel would respond appropriately, explicitly declaring war on the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] with new actions to follow — a posture Badlands read as effectively declaring war on the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and therefore on the entire world.[4]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the statement "the raving of a lunatic" and "the behavior of a wild animal that has been backed into the corner," arguing that Smotrich and his allies pursue aggressive conflict instead of diplomacy and are positioning [[israel|Israel]] to continue such conflicts regardless of what the United States does next.[4] He placed the episode within a wider argument that the Zionist leadership, sensing lost ground in public opinion, responds with interventions that only accelerate the shifting of the Overton Window — even as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], in his reading, intends to make peace with [[iran|Iran]].[4]

## Gaza occupation remarks (February 2026)

In February 2026, amid the backlash over US Ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]]'s "Nile to the Euphrates" comments, Smotrich told Kan public radio that he expected [[hamas|Hamas]] to be given an ultimatum to disarm "in the coming days," and that "in the end" Israel would occupy Gaza and reestablish Jewish settlements there.[5] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented the statement as a direct undermining of [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s peace efforts and part of a pattern that also included the earlier rejection of the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] by senior members of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s government, Smotrich among them.[5]

## Call to annex southern Lebanon (March 2026)

In March 2026 Smotrich called for [[israel|Israel]] to annex parts of southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]], saying the territory should be permanently held as a security measure amid ongoing fighting with [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[6] His remarks came as cross-border violence intensified, though the Israeli government had not formally adopted annexation as policy, and analysts expected the proposal to draw international opposition since annexation of territory by force is widely considered illegal under international law.[6]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the proposal would extend Israel's northern border to the [[litani-river|Litani River]], folding the intervening area into a [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]], and observed that the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] had bombed seven bridges crossing the Litani, cutting off access from Beirut and central Lebanon.[6] See [[southern-lebanon-annexation-proposal|southern Lebanon annexation proposal]].[6]

## April 2026 call to settle Gaza

In April 2026, Smotrich renewed his calls for [[israel|Israel]] to occupy and establish settlements in the Gaza Strip, saying the war there should conclude with territorial expansion.[7] Badlands characterized the demand as a call for the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] to reoccupy all of Gaza and for new settlements to be founded there, in direct opposition to [[donald-trump|President Trump]] and the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]].[7]

## Reaction to the US–Iran peace deal (June 2026)

Smotrich was among the Israeli officials whose statements Badlands cited in June 2026 as evidence of Israeli fury over the U.S.–[[iran|Iran]] peace framework, with Israeli politicians and military leaders calling the outcome a "strategic failure."[8] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] summarized the reaction as "cope," noting that violence had erupted in the West Bank from Jewish settlers in response to reports of the deal and that Israeli officials told the outlet Ynet they had no input or influence in the peace process.[8]

## Call to dismantle the Palestinian Authority (September 2025)

Following a shooting attack in Jerusalem on the morning of September 9, 2025, Smotrich called for the dismantling of the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]], tweeting that "the State of Israel cannot accept a Palestinian Authority that raises and educates its children to murder Jews."[10] He declared that "the Palestinian Authority must disappear from the map, and the villages from which the terrorists came should look like Rafah and Beit Hanoun," while praising the "bravery of the courageous soldier and Kollel student who engaged and neutralized the terrorists," which he said likely prevented a larger attack.[10] The statement came only days after Smotrich unveiled a proposal for Israel to annex 82 percent of the West Bank in response to plans by a range of Western countries to recognize a Palestinian state.[10] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry described a parallel online influence campaign against PA President [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] as an attempt to justify annexation of Palestinian territory.[10]

## Opposition to Palestinian statehood (November 2025)

Ahead of the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] vote on the next stage of the U.S.-brokered Gaza peace plan in November 2025, Smotrich, as Israel's finance minister, urged Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to "make it clear to the entire world" that a Palestinian state "will never be established."[11] His appeal echoed a lengthy X post by security minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] claiming that the Palestinian people do not exist and that the only real solution was "encouraging voluntary emigration."[11]

## Leverage within Netanyahu's security cabinet

Badlands commentary groups Smotrich with [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] and [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] as the radical zealots who control three of six seats in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s security cabinet and can therefore dissolve the government whenever they choose.[12] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that their standing threat to resign if the war ends explains Netanyahu's repeated sabotage of hostage and ceasefire deals, a pattern Israeli security and military officials were themselves disclosing by August 2024.[12]

## Coalition Threat Over the January 2025 Gaza Deal

As the ceasefire and hostage release framework with [[hamas|Hamas]] was announced on January 15, 2025, Smotrich threatened to bolt [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s governing coalition rather than accept it, prompting President [[isaac-herzog|Isaac Herzog]] to publicly appeal to the cabinet to approve the deal.[13] Badlands commentary held that formal Israeli ratification remained unclear because consternation within the Security Cabinet was at an all-time high, attributing the resistance not to the terms being unfair but to what the writer described as Smotrich's "deep and insatiable bloodlust that he claims is a righteous virtue of his faith."[13]

## Lauder criticism and Gaza aid remarks (May 2025)

In May 2025, [[ronald-lauder|Ronald Lauder]], president of the [[world-jewish-congress|World Jewish Congress]], criticized Smotrich at a Jerusalem conference, telling Foreign Minister [[gideon-saar|Gideon Sa'ar]] that "all the good things that Israel is doing are being destroyed by Smotrich" because his statements about starving the people and destroying Gaza "were played all over the world."[14] Lauder asked why Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had the chance to stop him and would not; Sa'ar replied only that he "took note."[14]

The immediate trigger for the renewed attention was footage of Smotrich publicly stating that, after blocking all aid for the preceding months, Israel would allow in only enough aid to prevent war crimes charges.[14] Badlands commentary argued Netanyahu is beholden to Smotrich because his [[religious-zionism-party|Religious Zionist Party]] holds 14 [[knesset|Knesset]] seats, whose loss would drop the coalition below the 60-seat threshold, whereas [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]]'s six-seat Jewish Power Party left Netanyahu a 62-seat majority when it briefly resigned in January.[14] The same commentary described Smotrich as the more cunning and ruthless of the two, with more political polish than the louder Ben-Gvir.[14]

## Rejection of the Trump Gaza plan (September–October 2025)

Israel's Finance Minister Smotrich commented negatively on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Gaza peace plan, saying that "[a]fter October 7 and two years of heroic sacrifice, returning to the old Oslo-era delusions — outsourcing our security to others, trading real battlefield gains for polished diplomatic theater, re-empowering [[qatar|Qatar]] as a central broker, and training a Palestinian police — is a tragic, backward step."[15] He warned the plan "risks repeating the same mistakes, forcing our children to fight in Gaza again."[15]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry called the reaction unsurprising, noting that the audience had "been learning about Bezalel Smotrich for nearly two years."[15] He observed that Trump had amplified both Smotrich's criticism and commentary from opposition leader [[benny-gantz|Benny Gantz]] in a Truth Social post, reading it as a sign that Trump expected [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s security cabinet to reject or subvert the deal.[15] In the same commentary, Smotrich — described as a formally trained rabbi — was characterized as the spokesman for a "hyper-zealot Ultra-Orthodox movement" said to seek a kingdom of Jewish supremacy stretching from the Nile to the Euphrates, framed as a recreation of the Neo-Babylonian Empire that besieged Jerusalem in 587 BC and destroyed Solomon's Temple.[15]

## March 2025 Washington visit

On Tuesday, March 4, 2025, Smotrich said he was making a brief visit to the United States to "strengthen economic cooperation" and "deepen the strategic alliance between our two countries," ahead of an expected announcement by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on the future of the West Bank.[16] Writing on X, he said he had taken off overnight and would meet US officials including Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]], and that he would emphasize Israel's determined stance in the fight against terror and the need for clear American support.[16] The far-right minister had threatened to topple the government should Israel withdraw from Gaza as part of a truce-hostage deal.[16]

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that [[west-bank-annexation|annexation of the West Bank]] would likely mean world war and would certainly foreclose a trillion-dollar Saudi investment, since Iran and the Arab states, backed by Russia and China, would go to war with Israel.[16] He described Smotrich as widely detested internationally for saying Palestinians are sub-human and should be starved to death, and as "low-hanging fruit" for Trump to bully in the manner of [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], being polarizing even within Israel.[16] He further identified Smotrich as a disciple of the late Knesset member [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]], noting that in 1994 the Knesset banned members whose platforms championed racial supremacy — a ban [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was forced to repeal in 2022 to retain enough votes to stay in office.[16] The visit's timing coincided with reports of [[russian-mediation-of-us-iran-nuclear-talks|Russian mediation of US–Iran nuclear talks]].[16]

## Pressure to resume the Gaza war, 2025

Ahead of the second phase of the Gaza ceasefire deal in February 2025, Badlands commentary identified Smotrich as among the members of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s Security Cabinet who wanted the ceasefire with [[hamas|Hamas]] broken as soon as possible.[17] Smotrich made clear that if Netanyahu failed to resume the war and instead proceeded with the second phase, he would resign from the government — a step that would collapse the coalition and remove Netanyahu as prime minister.[17]

## May 2024 warning on Palestinian statehood

In May 2024, Smotrich told a group of settlement mayors that the establishment of a Palestinian state had become a "tangible danger" as a result of international efforts advancing the cause.[18] According to his office, he described at the Finance Ministry meeting a "tangible, developing danger" that a Palestinian state would be created through "international involvement" and the "consent of leftist elements" in Israel.[18] Present were [[israel-ganz|Israel Ganz]], newly named chairman of the [[yesha-council|Yesha Council]], and the mayors of the largest West Bank settlements including Beitar Illit, Modiin Illit and Maale Adumim.[18] Smotrich also told the group he was pressing Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to convene a key administrative body capable of authorizing new settlement construction plans.[18] Days later the UN General Assembly voted 143-9 with 25 abstentions to grant Palestine new rights and revive its membership bid.[18]

## Badlands profile of Smotrich

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry compiled an extended profile of Smotrich in connection with the statehood vote, describing him as an ethnic Ukrainian who had repeatedly courted controversy with statements read as calls for genocide, including his March 2023 remark that "the [Palestinian] village of Huwwara needs to be wiped out. I think that the State of Israel needs to do that—not, God forbid, private individuals."[18] In April 2023 Smotrich was filmed speaking at an event promoting [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]], before a banner depicting an expanded Israel encompassing all of Palestine, all of Jordan and part of northwestern Saudi Arabia; in the speech he asserted that the Palestinian people were an invented concept and that his own children were the real Palestinians.[18]
The profile also recounted Smotrich's 2005 arrest by the [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]] over an alleged plan, with a five-man cell, to burn vehicles on major highways using 700 liters of gasoline in protest of Ariel Sharon's plan to evacuate Israeli settlers from Gaza.[18] It summarized his 2017 [[knesset|Knesset]]-era manifesto "Israel's Decisive Plan," now known as the [[smotrich-doctrine|Smotrich Doctrine]], which rejects the [[two-state-solution|two-state solution]] and holds that "there is only room for one expression of national self-determination west of the Jordan River: that of the Jewish nation."[18] The manifesto further characterizes non-Orthodox Judaism as a "fake religion" and proposes incorporating Torah law into the Israeli justice system, with separate provisions for gentiles under the Seven Laws of Noah.[18]

As finance minister, Smotrich has used the post to withhold tax revenue Israel collects on behalf of Palestinians, and in March 2024 announced the largest Israeli land seizure in the West Bank since 1993 together with approval to build 3,300 new settler homes.[18] Badlands commentary noted friction between Smotrich and Netanyahu, with the prime minister blocking some of the finance minister's planned settlement developments, and observed that international actors had long called for Smotrich's prosecution for war crimes, predating October 7.[18]

## Remarks on Palestinian displacement (November 2024)

In late November 2024 a video circulated on X, highlighted by journalist Barak Ravid, in which Smotrich, as Israeli finance minister, expressed his desire to see the incoming [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration use American leverage to coerce the Palestinian population into leaving their lands and surrendering their homes to a foreign government.[19] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterised the proposal as "pure evil" and argued that any American who supported it was no ally of the MAGA movement, since indulging such a policy would amount to profound hypocrisy and moral relativism for a movement built on the defence of private property and sovereignty.[19] The remarks were presented alongside statements from Heritage Minister Amichai Eliyahu describing a prospective Lebanon ceasefire as a mere "tactical delay" until Trump re-entered office, as evidence that the Netanyahu cabinet's zealots were "unwittingly pulling back the veil" on a longer-term plan.[19]

## Opposition to a Gaza ceasefire (July 2025)

Speaking at a press briefing on Monday, June 30, 2025, Smotrich said in his capacity as Israeli Finance Minister that the war in [[gaza|Gaza]] must continue until "a decisive victory" was achieved, rejecting calls for a ceasefire or a negotiated settlement.[20] He characterised Israel as being "in the midst of a campaign against a crushed terrorist organization" and warned that there would be "no greater danger" to the country's future than halting the operation prematurely.[20] "This war must end in a decisive victory for many generations to come, without agreements, without mediators," he said.[20]

The remarks came amid growing divisions within the Israeli cabinet over the future of Operation Gideon's Chariots, with Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] expected to reconvene his security cabinet after several failed attempts to reach consensus.[20] The timing was pointed: Smotrich spoke on the same day that [[donald-trump|President Trump]] announced Israel had "agreed to the necessary conditions" for a 60-day ceasefire to be delivered to [[hamas|Hamas]] by Qatari and Egyptian mediators.[20]

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry read the sequence as a clear signal of coming conflict between Trump and what he called "the radical zealots in Tel Aviv."[20] He argued that while [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] was the more boisterous mascot of the [[israeli-settler-movement|Israeli Settler Movement]], Smotrich was the more devious and cunning adversary — more calculated in his political manoeuvring and possessed of a calm disposition with an "eerie chill."[20] In this reading, Smotrich, Ben-Gvir and [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] faced an all-or-nothing moment, since no likely successor to Netanyahu would be desperate enough to ally with them, leaving them unlikely ever to return to the Security Cabinet.[20]

## Coalition threat over the July 2024 hostage deal

In July 2024, Smotrich threatened to leave [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s governing coalition if a hostage deal were reached with [[hamas|Hamas]].[21] On Wednesday, July 17, [[likud|Likud]] science minister [[gila-gamliel|Gila Gamliel]] called on Netanyahu to fire him "immediately," telling Channel 13 News that "there is no reason why this deal should not go through" and that "the very threat should have caused him to go home."[21]

Gamliel grouped Smotrich with his far-right coalition allies, principally National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], and recalled that during a previous hostage deal in November he had publicly claimed he would oppose the arrangement before voting in favor once he saw the details.[21] "'We will resign,' so resign! In any case, they are a burden on this government. It wouldn't hurt if they resigned," she said.[21]

Badlands commentary placed the episode within a broader argument that Netanyahu, having exhausted his moderate allies, was captive to radical partners whose ideology — associated with [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]], the [[smotrich-doctrine|Smotrich Doctrine]], and [[kahanism|Kahanism]] — would not permit any ceasefire with Hamas or [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[21]

## Support for the 2025 Gaza seizure plan

After the Israeli Cabinet approved plans in May 2025 to seize the [[gaza|Gaza Strip]] and remain in the territory indefinitely, Smotrich, as Finance Minister, instructed Israeli citizens to "embrace Gaza occupation," adding that nothing would stop the war — not even the release of the hostages.[22] His stance contrasted with the warning from [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] Chief of Staff [[eyal-zamir|Lt. Gen. Eyal Zamir]] that the operation could cost the lives of remaining hostages.[22]

## Gaza "security annexation" advocacy (July 2025)

On July 22, 2025, Smotrich endorsed a "security annexation" of the northern Gaza Strip and said Gaza would become an "inseparable part of the State of Israel."[23] He made the remarks at the [[gaza-riviera-conference|"Gaza Riviera – from vision to reality"]] conference in the [[knesset|Knesset]], at which participants presented plans for reestablishing Jewish settlements in the Gaza Strip.[23] Smotrich, who also serves as a minister in the Defense Ministry, claimed that [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] Chief of Staff [[eyal-zamir|Eyal Zamir]] favored the idea, and proposed beginning "with the northern border [area of the Strip] and establish three communities there."[23] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described him as one of "the wicked Pharisees who have returned to power in the Holy Land."[23]

## January 2025 threat to collapse the coalition

As negotiations approached over the second phase of the Israel-Hamas hostage deal in late January 2025, Smotrich, [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s finance minister, came under pressure from hardliners in his [[religious-zionism-party|Religious Zionism]] party and from the Israeli settler movement to resign from the government, a step that would collapse Netanyahu's coalition and force new elections.[24] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reported that Smotrich had threatened Netanyahu with following through if the Phase Two exchange was upheld and the war was not resumed, a threat President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was said to be well aware of when he dispatched envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] to [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[24]

## Crisis with Netanyahu over Gaza humanitarian ceasefires (July 2025)

An official told *The Jerusalem Post* on Monday, July 28, 2025, that Finance Minister Smotrich and Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] were in a "real" crisis, with relations between them "very, very tense and complicated," following Netanyahu's decision over the preceding weekend — taken without Smotrich's knowledge — to enact "localized humanitarian ceasefires" in [[gaza|Gaza]].[25] According to the official, who spoke on condition of anonymity, if Smotrich did not receive "real and concrete" guarantees from the prime minister regarding the defeat of [[hamas|Hamas]], his Religious Zionist Party could be "on its way out" of the government.[25] With the coalition numbering just 60 MKs after the departure of two haredi parties earlier in July, the exit of Smotrich's seven MKs would likely topple the government.[25] At press time on Monday evening the usually vocal Smotrich had not commented publicly on the tension or on the ceasefires, which the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] announced early Sunday morning.[25]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] paired the episode with National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]]'s description of Netanyahu as "morally bankrupt" for allowing food into Gaza, and reported that Smotrich was demanding guarantees that [[israel|Israel]] would begin annexing Gaza within days.[25]


## Coalition leverage in June 2024

Smotrich, alongside [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], repeatedly threatened to resign from [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s cabinet if the ultra-Orthodox were subjected to conscription after Israel's Supreme Court struck down their draft exemption in June 2024; their joint departure would legally dissolve the government and trigger new elections.[26]

## Reaction to the January 2025 ceasefire deal

On Monday, January 13, 2025, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] briefed Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich and National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] on the emerging Gaza hostage deal.[27] Smotrich left the meeting sounding thoughtful, and those who spoke with him that day came away with the impression he considered it a good deal, but by noon he announced that it was a catastrophe Israel should not accept.[27] His statement pointedly did not address the critical question of whether he regarded the deal as grounds to quit the coalition; observers noted that if he left he would drag Ben-Gvir with him and the government would fall, whereas mere opposition would be meaningless airtime.[27]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Smotrich and Ben-Gvir could not afford to "seek peace," because their parties form the base of the Israeli settler movement, which seeks to expand Israel's borders well beyond Palestine, and because the moment represented their best and perhaps last chance before international retribution.[27] Their support also rests heavily on Haredi voters affected by the end of the [[ultra-orthodox-conscription-in-israel|conscription exemption]].[27]

## Viral remarks about the Saudis (October 2025)

In late October 2025, amid the fallout from the [[knesset|Knesset]]'s preliminary [[west-bank-annexation|West Bank annexation]] vote, Israeli Finance Minister Smotrich went viral for disrespecting the Saudis.[28] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the episode as "the cherry on top of the proverbial sundae" following the Knesset vote, and predicted that with Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|MBS]] due in Washington the next month, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi]]–American relations would advance, potentially ahead of [[israel|Israel]] in the prioritization of US strategic partnerships.[28]


## August 2024 remarks on starving Gaza

Speaking at the Katif Annual Conference on Monday, August 5, 2024, about Israel's decision to allow humanitarian aid into [[gaza|Gaza]], Smotrich said that starving two million Gazans "to death" might be the "right and moral" course until Israeli hostages held in the territory were released, but that the "world won't let us."[29] The finance minister also argued against a deal with [[hamas|Hamas]], saying it would endanger Israel and return only a small number of captives: "We have a responsibility, we want to bring back the hostages, but a deal would only return a few captives and seal the fate of most of them to stay in captivity in Gaza."[29] Badlands Media juxtaposed the remarks with a billboard in Tehran proclaiming "Indeed, the Messiah, Jesus Christ, Son of Mary, is a messenger of God," presenting the pairing as "a tale of two civilizations" and asking which of [[iran|Iran]] and [[israel|Israel]] appeared more aligned with the Christian values of the West.[29]

## Withholding PA clearance revenues, May 2024

In May 2024 Israel's Finance Minister Smotrich was reported by Channel 12 to have held back NIS 170 million ($46 million) in tax revenue collected on behalf of the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] for nine days, in protest at PA efforts "fighting against the State of Israel" — an apparent reference to Ramallah's push for the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] to issue arrest warrants for top Israeli officials over alleged breaches of international law in Gaza.[30]

As a result the PA said on 12 May 2024 that it could pay only 50 percent of March public sector salaries, after Israel withheld the transfer due for April, continuing a payroll squeeze that had lasted months.[30] The Finance Ministry confirmed it had decided not to transfer tax revenues that month but declined to provide details.[30] The clearance mechanism giving Israel this leverage derives from the 1994 [[paris-protocol|Paris Protocol]].[30]
