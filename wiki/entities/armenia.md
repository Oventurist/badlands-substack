# Armenia

Armenia is a landlocked republic in the South Caucasus, bordered by Turkey, Georgia, [[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]], and Iran. Its modern politics have been dominated by its long conflict with Azerbaijan over Nagorno-Karabakh and the surrounding territories.

## Vance visit and the US-facilitated peace process

The February 10, 2026 edition of the Badlands Brief, "Criminal Invader Crashouts & Honeypot Hoaxes," reported, citing [[associated-press|AP News]], that United States Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] had arrived in Armenia, becoming the first sitting US vice president or president ever to visit the country. The Brief described the visit as following a United States–facilitated agreement and peace deal between Armenia and Azerbaijan, a process that included an August 2025 summit at the [[white-house|White House]] and a transit-corridor accord intended to advance regional peace.

The visit was reported as part of a wider international round-up in that edition, alongside Energy Secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]]'s planned trip to [[venezuela|Venezuela]] and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]'s forthcoming sovereign-wealth strategy, and was presented as evidence of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's active diplomatic footprint outside its core theatres.
