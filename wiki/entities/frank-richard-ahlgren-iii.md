# Frank Richard Ahlgren III

Frank Richard Ahlgren III is an early Texas [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] investor who became the subject of a landmark United States tax-fraud case involving the compelled surrender of cryptocurrency keys.[1]

## Conviction and restitution

Ahlgren falsely underreported capital gains on more than $3.7 million in Bitcoin sales between 2017 and 2019.[1] He was sentenced in December 2024 to two years in prison for tax fraud and owes the US government roughly $1.1 million in restitution.[1]

## Order to surrender private keys

On January 6, 2025, Austin federal court judge [[robert-pitman|Robert Pitman]] ordered Ahlgren — along with any family, friends, associates or representatives — to identify and hand over any physical devices used to store his cryptocurrency, together with any public keys, private keys, seed phrases or passphrases, as part of a restraining order in the $124 million landmark case.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[gmoney|GMONEY]] framed the order as an illegitimate demand, writing that he would refuse a judge's request for his seed phrase and declaring that "Bitcoin is Digital 1776."[1]
