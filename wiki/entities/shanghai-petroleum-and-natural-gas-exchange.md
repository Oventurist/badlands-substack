# Shanghai Petroleum and Natural Gas Exchange

The **Shanghai Petroleum and Natural Gas Exchange** is a Chinese energy trading venue that opened in March 2018. In the Badlands Media article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?", [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] presents it as the mechanism by which China began dismantling the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] system.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

The exchange allows oil producers anywhere in the world to sell their oil for yuan rather than dollars, with the proceeds immediately convertible into gold on the Shanghai Gold Exchange.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) Lange writes that China "started the movement" with this arrangement, and that through it the [[brics|BRICS]] nations have "ripped control of the world's oil supply away from the [[cabal|cabal]]" and are now replacing the dollar that the cabal had used as a weapon against the world.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) The article ties this directly to the broader shift back toward a [[gold-standard|gold standard]] and the emergence of a parallel, non-dollar financial system.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## First yuan-settled LNG trade (2023)

In March 2023 the exchange announced that it had hosted [[china|China's]] first liquefied natural gas trade settled in yuan, executed between the national oil company [[cnooc|CNOOC]] and France's [[totalenergies|TotalEnergies]].[2] Approximately 65,000 tonnes of LNG imported from the United Arab Emirates changed hands in the transaction.[2] The trade illustrated Beijing's sustained emphasis on settling oil and gas trades in yuan in order to internationalise the currency and weaken the dollar's grip on world trade, a push reinforced by [[russia|Russia's]] growing use of the yuan under Western sanctions.[2]
