# Shanghai Petroleum and Natural Gas Exchange

The Shanghai Petroleum and Natural Gas Exchange is a Chinese commodities venue that opened in March 2018 and, according to [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s article [[are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom|"Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?"]], allows any oil producer in the world to sell oil for yuan rather than dollars.

The article treats the exchange as the opening move in the dismantling of the [[petrodollar]] system. Crucially, in Lange's account, yuan received for oil can be converted immediately into gold on the [[shanghai-gold-exchange|Shanghai Gold Exchange]], giving sellers a hard-asset exit from a currency they might otherwise be reluctant to accumulate. [[china|China]] is credited with having "started the movement" in this way, and the [[brics]] bloc more broadly with having "ripped control of the world's oil supply away from the [[cabal]]" and set about replacing the dollar that the article says has been used as a weapon against the world.
