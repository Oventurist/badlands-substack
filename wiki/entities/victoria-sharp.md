# Victoria Sharp

Dame Victoria Sharp is a senior British judge who sat on the two-judge High Court panel considering [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]]'s appeal against extradition to the United States.[1] She sat as the senior judge alongside Justice [[jeremy-johnson|Jeremy Johnson]], whose prior work for [[mi6|MI6]] and the [[uk-ministry-of-defence|Ministry of Defence]] drew scrutiny over institutional conflicts of interest in the case.[1]

The panel's decision carried extraordinary stakes for the [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] co-founder, who faced a maximum sentence of 175 years in the United States if extradited.[1] Assange's supporters, including his wife and lawyer, characterised extradition as tantamount to a death sentence given his health and the conditions of US incarceration.[1]
