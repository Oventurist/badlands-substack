# International Organization for Migration

The International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the United Nations' migration agency, headquartered in Geneva.[1]

## 2025 Syria appeal

On Thursday, January 9, 2025, the IOM expanded its aid appeal for Syria to over $73 million as the country transitioned after years of civil war and decades of dictatorship.[1] The agency more than doubled an appeal launched the previous month, raising it from $30 million to $73.2 million, with the aim of assisting 1.1 million people across Syria over the following six months.[1]

IOM chief Amy Pope said in a statement that "IOM is committed to helping the people of Syria at this historical moment as the nation recovers from nearly 14 years of conflict," adding that the agency would bring its deep experience in humanitarian assistance and recovery to help vulnerable communities and work with all partners "to help build a better future for Syria."[1]

The Geneva-based agency said it was working to reestablish its presence inside Syria after exiting Damascus in 2020, building on two preceding decades of work there as well as on cross-border activities over the past decade to bring aid to northwest Syria.[1]
