# Jordan Goudreau

Jordan Goudreau is a former US Green Beret who ran a private security firm in Florida and who organized the failed 2020 amphibious raid on Venezuela that became known as the "Bay of Piglets."[1]

## The 2020 Venezuela raid

In 2020 Goudreau assembled a team for what became a failed amphibious raid by a ragtag group of soldiers launched out of Colombia against Venezuela's shores.[1] The Associated Press summarized that despite significant setbacks the plotters went forward "in a comical if tragic way" in an operation "widely ridiculed as the 'Bay of Piglets,'" a reference to the 1961 Cuban fiasco.[1] The group was easily mopped up by Venezuelan security forces, which had already infiltrated it.[1] Two of Goudreau's former Green Beret colleagues spent years in Venezuelan prisons until a prisoner swap that exchanged jailed Americans for a [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]] ally held in the United States on money laundering charges.[1]

Goudreau later faced federal charges for failing to hold an export license for assault rifles, ammunition, silencers, night vision goggles and other military items that had been sent to Colombia.[1]

## Significance in later coverage

ZeroHedge and Badlands invoked the Goudreau episode in September 2024 as an "eerily familiar" precedent when Venezuelan interior minister [[diosdado-cabello|Diosdado Cabello]] announced the arrest of three Americans, two Spaniards and a Czech citizen allegedly involved in a plot to assassinate Maduro.[1] Badlands connected the pattern to research by Cynthia Ehret, wife of [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], on Operation Condor — described as the South American wing of [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]] — in which the instrument of such coups was "almost always the Green Berets."[1]
