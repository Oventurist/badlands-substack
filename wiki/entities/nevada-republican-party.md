# Nevada Republican Party

The Nevada Republican Party is the state affiliate of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] in Nevada.[1]

## 2024 mail-ballot litigation

In June 2024 the Nevada Republican Party joined the RNC and the campaign of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in filing suit against the state of Nevada over the counting of mail-in ballots received the day after an election without postmarks.[1] The plaintiffs argued that Nevada law requires mail ballots to be postmarked on or before Election Day and that state election officials routinely counted non-postmarked late-arriving ballots in violation of that requirement.[1] RNC chairman [[michael-whatley|Michael Whatley]] said the practice created "a concerning and potentially fraudulent impact" on the state's elections.[1] The suit was one element of the party's broader [[mail-in-voting|mail-in voting]] challenges in the run-up to November 2024.[1]

## 2024 caucuses

For the 2024 presidential cycle the party opted to hold its own caucuses rather than rely on the state-run primary, making the caucuses the only contest that would allocate Nevada's 26 Republican delegates.[2] As a result [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] did not appear on the February 6 primary ballot and instead won the party's February 8 [[2024-nevada-republican-caucuses|caucuses]], easily defeating Ryan Binkley, while [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] contested the primary and finished behind "none of these candidates."[2]
