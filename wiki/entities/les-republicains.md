# Les Républicains

Les Républicains (LR) is the principal French centre-right political party, whose figures include interior minister [[bruno-retailleau|Bruno Retailleau]].[1]

In May 2025 the party tabled a Senate bill designed to dismantle what critics call the NGO-migrant complex in France.[1] The bill would end the closed system under which only a select few publicly funded non-governmental organizations — among them [[cimade|Cimade]] and [[france-terre-dasile|France terre d'asile]] — are permitted to operate inside the country's Centres de Rétention Administrative, the 26 secure holding facilities where migrants are detained prior to expulsion.[1] These centres are not prisons but closed facilities intended to prevent absconding while deportation orders are carried out, and in practice often hold individuals being expelled after serving prison sentences for violent crime.[1]

The measure formed part of a broader crackdown, with the party and government proposing that legal assistance be transferred to the [[french-office-for-immigration-and-integration|French Office for Immigration and Integration]] or to a neutral magistrate.[1]
