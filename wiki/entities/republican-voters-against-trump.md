# Republican Voters Against Trump

Republican Voters Against Trump is an anti-Trump advocacy group composed of Republican and former-Republican voters and operatives.[1]

In August 2024 it was identified, together with [[the-lincoln-project|The Lincoln Project]], as one of two Republican anti-Trump groups that publicly criticized former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after he posted on Truth Social that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] — whom he described as having had the presidency "unconstitutionally stolen" from him by [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], Nancy Pelosi, [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] and Chuck Schumer — might crash the Democratic National Convention to reclaim the nomination.[1] The episode was recounted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], which framed the reaction as evidence that the post had rattled establishment actors rather than as a successful rebuttal.[1]
