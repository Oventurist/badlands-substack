# Iván Cepeda

Iván Cepeda is a Colombian political figure who was a candidate in [[colombia|Colombia]]'s June 21, 2026 presidential election.[1]

Following the certification of Abelardo de la Espriella as the winner, outgoing President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] reportedly claimed that the real winner had been Cepeda, alleging that the result was manipulated using technology from California.[1] That claim prompted the July 10, 2026 [[shield-of-the-americas|Shield of the Americas]] joint statement by the United States and twelve regional partners rejecting attempts to delegitimize the certified mandate.[1]

## Refusal to recognize de la Espriella (June 2026)

Cepeda, a 47-year-old progressive senator and leader of the Historical Pact party, narrowly lost the June 21, 2026 runoff to [[abelardo-de-la-espriella|Abelardo de la Espriella]] by 48.7% to 49.6%, and initially delayed conceding until electoral authorities certified the results on June 24.[2] On June 30 he announced that he would not recognize de la Espriella as incoming president unless the president-elect renounced his U.S. citizenship and met other conditions.[2]

He demanded that de la Espriella clarify whether he is acting as a U.S. "agent," citing his past legal representation of a former paramilitary leader who was a [[drug-enforcement-administration|DEA]] informant, and called on him to halt any effort to extradite outgoing President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], who faces U.S. federal charges in Brooklyn related to alleged drug trafficker ties.[2] If the demands went unmet, Cepeda said he would lead "peaceful civil disobedience," declaring that "as the leader of the opposition, I will embark on the path of peaceful civil disobedience that implies not recognizing the authority of someone who will not defend our national sovereignty."[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Cepeda is Petro's chosen successor and that both men were accusing de la Espriella of being a [[cia|CIA]] stooge; see [[2026-colombian-presidential-election|the election]].[2]
