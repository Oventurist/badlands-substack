# Iván Cepeda

Iván Cepeda is a Colombian senator whom President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]] claimed was the real winner of the June 21, 2026 Colombian presidential election, according to the July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief.

The claim accompanied Petro's allegations that the result — certified for [[abelardo-de-la-espriella|Abelardo de la Espriella]] — was manipulated using technology from California. The [[shield-of-the-americas|Shield of the Americas]] coalition rejected any effort to delegitimize the certified mandate. See [[2026-colombian-presidential-election|2026 Colombian presidential election]].
