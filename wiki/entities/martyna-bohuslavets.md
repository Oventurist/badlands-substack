# Martyna Bohuslavets

Martyna Bohuslavets is a Ukrainian anti-corruption investigator who heads the Mezha Anti-Corruption Center.[1]

## "Where are the fortifications?" report

In May 2024, Bohuslavets published a report in Ukrainian Pravda titled "Where are the fortifications?", alleging that millions of dollars earmarked for the construction of defensive fortifications in Ukraine had instead been "transferred to Kharkiv OVA to front companies of avatars."[1] She reported that the Kharkiv Regional Military Administration paid out funds to fictitious companies during the fortification of the Kharkiv region, and that a total of 7 billion hryvnias had been spent there.[1]

The report landed as Russian forces launched the [[2024-kharkiv-offensive|2024 Kharkiv offensive]], advancing into northern Ukraine in an area where the missing fortifications had been budgeted, and while the United States continued funding the war effort.[1] Ukrainian reconnaissance commander [[denys-yaroslavskyi|Denys Yaroslavskyi]] separately told the BBC that there had been no first line of defense in the region, calling the failure "a betrayal."[1]

## Significance

Badlands commentary treated Bohuslavets's findings alongside a [[government-accountability-office|Government Accountability Office]] report on the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]]'s inability to track deliveries of defense articles to Ukraine, arguing that the two together suggested US aid flows were less transparent than officially presented.[1]
