# Dario Amodei

**Dario Amodei** is the chief executive officer of [[anthropic|Anthropic]], a US-based developer of advanced artificial-intelligence models and software systems.[1] In early 2026 he became the central figure in a public dispute between Anthropic and the US [[department-of-war|Department of War]] (DoW) over permissible military applications of the company's AI systems.[1]

## Dispute with the Department of War

The conflict became public in March 2026 after a leaked internal memo of roughly 1,600 words, written by Amodei to Anthropic staff, circulated outside the company.[1] The memo reportedly set out Anthropic's position in an ongoing disagreement with the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] over how the company's AI systems could be used in military programs.[1] Anthropic had previously worked with the Pentagon under an AI-related contract valued at roughly $200 million.[1]

Amodei has stated that Anthropic maintains "red lines" regarding certain military uses of artificial intelligence, and that the company is unwilling to allow its systems to be used for specific applications such as autonomous weapons or certain surveillance functions.[1] Pentagon officials allegedly threatened to cancel or reconsider Anthropic's defense contract if the company maintained those restrictions, and the DoW subsequently designated Anthropic a "supply-chain risk," a classification that can restrict or complicate participation in government contracting.[1]

Amodei afterward said Anthropic was attempting to "de-escalate" the dispute while continuing discussions about the company's participation in government AI programs.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the episode as the DoW demanding "unchecked war applications of AI" and treating dissent as a national-security threat.[1] She noted that because the objection originated from figures perceived as left-leaning, much of the "Truth Community" sided with the federal government and with [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]], effectively cheering mass domestic surveillance and AI-autonomous weapons — a posture she compared to the "principled conservatism" that produced the Patriot Act.[1]
