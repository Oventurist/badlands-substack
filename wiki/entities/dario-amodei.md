# Dario Amodei

Dario Amodei is the chief executive of [[anthropic|Anthropic]] and, with [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] of [[openai|OpenAI]], one of the two figures most identified with the industry campaign for government regulation of frontier AI systems.[1]

## Open-source AI dispute

In July 2026, as Trump-aligned technologists escalated a public argument over restrictions on [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source artificial intelligence]], Amodei's Anthropic was characterized as favoring stronger safeguards on frontier models, while open-source advocates argued such rules would entrench incumbents and weaken the United States against foreign competitors, above all [[china|China]].[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the Amodei-Altman regulatory push was designed to slow and control AI acceleration rather than supercharge an American Age of Abundance, making them "retardants" to the AI agenda, and cited [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] as making the same case publicly.[1]
