# Karen Bass

Karen Bass is the incumbent Mayor of [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]], a former member of Congress, and a candidate for re-election in the city's 2026 mayoral contest.

As reported in [[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]], Bass advanced from the city's nonpartisan primary to a November runoff against City Council member [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]]. The race drew national attention in part because of the candidacy of former reality television personality [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]], whose campaign centered on criticism of city leadership following the [[palisades-fire|Palisades Fire]], as well as on homelessness and crime. Public polling before the primary had shown Bass, Raman, and Pratt competing closely; Pratt ultimately finished outside the top two.
