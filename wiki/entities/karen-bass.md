# Karen Bass

Karen Bass is the incumbent Mayor of [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]], a former member of Congress, and a candidate for re-election in the city's 2026 mayoral contest.

As reported in Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, Bass advanced from the city's nonpartisan primary to a November runoff against City Council member [[nithya-raman|Nithya Raman]]. The race drew national attention in part because of the candidacy of former reality television personality [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]], whose campaign centered on criticism of city leadership following the [[palisades-fire|Palisades Fire]], as well as on homelessness and crime. Public polling before the primary had shown Bass, Raman, and Pratt competing closely; Pratt ultimately finished outside the top two.

## Contrast campaigning by challengers (May 2026)

During the pre-primary phase of the race, described in Badlands Brief: Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns, Bass became the target of a visual argument about municipal decay. [[spencer-pratt|Spencer Pratt]] power-washed campaign slogans into dirty sidewalks across the city, an approach coverage framed as a guerrilla advertisement whose force depended on the condition of the streets under Bass's administration. Badlands commentary contrasted Bass's presumed reliance on conventional, expensive campaign media with Pratt's viral low-budget stunts, and cited reporting that Pratt was leading in fundraising and polling within three points of the incumbent.
