# Al Green

Al Green is a long-serving Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas, representing a Houston-area district.

The March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]] lists Green among the incumbents facing serious primary challenges on March 3, 2026, in his case from Harris County Attorney [[christian-menefee|Christian Menefee]]. The Brief presents the contest as part of a costly Democratic "civil war" ahead of the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]], driven by generational change and anti-establishment frustration rather than by ideological disagreement alone.
