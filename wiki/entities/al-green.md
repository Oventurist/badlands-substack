# Al Green

Al Green is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas's 9th congressional district.[1]

## 2026 primary runoff

In the [[2026-texas-primary-elections|March 2026 Texas primaries]], Green did not lose outright but failed to secure a majority of the vote, sending him into a runoff against Christian Menefee.[1] The contest was described as an incumbent-versus-incumbent, redistricting-driven challenge.[1] Early reporting that Green had lost his seat outright was premature.[1]

The result was folded into broader Badlands commentary describing the Texas cycle as a revolt against sitting officeholders across both parties.[1]

## Removal from the 2025 joint session

During President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[2025-trump-address-to-joint-session-of-congress|address to a joint session of Congress]] on March 4, 2025, Green was removed from the chamber by the Sergeant at Arms after disrupting the speech, while other Democrats held up signs, jeered and walked out.[2] Badlands commentary described him as having been "thrown out for being shouty," and criticized Democrats for bringing shirts, signs and other props while refusing to applaud ordinary Americans honored in the gallery.[2]

## Impeachment resolution over the Iran strikes (June 2025)

On Tuesday, June 24, 2025, Green forced a House vote on a five-page impeachment resolution against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the unilateral U.S. strikes on Iranian nuclear sites, accusing him of "Disregarding the Separation of Powers" and "Devolving American Democracy into Authoritarianism by Unconstitutionally Usurping Congress's Power to Declare War."[3] The resolution emphasized that Trump had neither sought congressional authorization nor provided evidence of an "imminent threat" to U.S. forces.[3] The House voted 344 to 79 on a Republican motion to table the article, with 128 Democrats joining Republicans to kill the measure and 79 voting to bring it to the floor — an outcome described as the clearest signal yet of Democratic reluctance to pursue impeachment.[3] See [[2025-impeachment-vote-over-the-iran-strikes|the 2025 impeachment vote over the Iran strikes]].[3]
