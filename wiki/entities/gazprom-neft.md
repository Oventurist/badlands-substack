# Gazprom Neft

Gazprom Neft is one of Russia's major petroleum producers and a subsidiary of the state-linked gas giant [[gazprom|Gazprom]]. In January 2025 it became a central target of a sweeping package of United States sanctions imposed on the Russian energy industry during the closing days of the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration.[1]

## January 2025 sanctions

The sanctions announced by Washington targeted two major petroleum producers — Gazprom Neft and [[surgutneftegaz|Surgutneftegaz]] — along with their subsidiaries, including Naftna industrija Srbije (NIS), the Serbian refiner that handles deliveries of Russian oil to Serbia and neighboring European nations.[1] Related insurance providers, more than 30 oilfield service companies, and over 180 vessels used to deliver Russian oil were also placed under restrictions.[1]

Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] characterized the measures as an attempt by the outgoing administration to create problems for President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before his inauguration, arguing that the curbs simultaneously targeted Serbia, Russia, and Trump himself, who had expressed readiness to resume dialogue with Moscow over the Ukraine conflict.[1] "The Democrats have such a manner in American politics to spoil the whole thing for the next administration before the end of their mandate," Lavrov said.[1]

## Commentary
