# Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr

Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr is a former commander of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) who was appointed Iran's national security chief in March 2026 during a wartime leadership reshuffle in Tehran.[1]

## Appointment

Zolghadr's elevation came as Iran entered the fourth week of a heavy exchange of fire with Israel.[1] He replaced [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]], who had been assassinated.[1] The appointment occurred alongside continued Iranian missile and drone barrages against Israeli cities and US bases in the region, and Israeli airstrikes on Iranian military and energy infrastructure near Isfahan.[1]

## Context

The reshuffle unfolded as the United States pushed a one-month ceasefire proposal delivered through Pakistani intermediaries, which would require Iran to dismantle its nuclear capabilities, abandon its proxy militias, forswear nuclear weapons, and keep the Strait of Hormuz open in exchange for full sanctions relief and assistance with a civilian nuclear program.[1] Tehran had rejected negotiations with US envoys [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], branding them "backstabbers," and signaled a preference for dealing with Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] instead.[1]
