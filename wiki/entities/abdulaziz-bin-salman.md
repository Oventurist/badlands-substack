# Abdulaziz bin Salman

Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman is the Saudi Energy Minister and a senior member of the House of Saud. As the kingdom's principal official on oil, electricity, and atomic energy policy, he is the Saudi counterpart to the U.S. Secretary of Energy in bilateral negotiations over nuclear cooperation.

According to the July 22, 2026 [[badlands-brief-1ae|Badlands Brief]], Abdulaziz bin Salman was expected to sign, alongside U.S. Energy Secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]], the [[123-agreement|123 Agreement]] authorizing U.S. cooperation with [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] on civil nuclear technology. The signing followed a preliminary agreement reached in Riyadh the previous year, and the completed document was to be submitted to [[congress|Congress]] for its statutory review period.

The agreement he was to sign notably omits the [[gold-standard|Gold Standard]] restriction on enrichment and reprocessing, as well as the Additional Protocol expanding [[international-atomic-energy-agency|IAEA]] inspection authority — omissions that place his ministry's future enrichment options at the center of the nonproliferation debate surrounding the deal, and that connect to Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s stated position that the kingdom would match any Iranian nuclear weapon.
