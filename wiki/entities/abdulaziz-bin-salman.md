# Abdulaziz bin Salman

Abdulaziz bin Salman is the Energy Minister of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and a senior member of the House of Saud. He was expected to sign the U.S.–Saudi [[123-agreement|123 Agreement]] on civil nuclear cooperation alongside U.S. Energy Secretary [[chris-wright|Chris Wright]], following a preliminary agreement reached in Riyadh the previous year.[1]

The agreement, which the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration planned to send to [[congress|Congress]] in July 2026, omits both the [[gold-standard-nonproliferation|Gold Standard]] enrichment prohibition and the [[international-atomic-energy-agency|IAEA]] Additional Protocol.[1]


## SPIEF 2026 delegation

Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman Al Saud led [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]'s high-level delegation to the [[2026-st-petersburg-international-economic-forum|2026 St. Petersburg International Economic Forum]] in his capacity as Energy Minister, accompanied by officials from sovereign wealth funds and major state enterprises.[2] On the sidelines of the forum the Saudi and Russian sides signed approximately 30 agreements covering energy, investment, education, tourism and humanitarian cooperation.[2]
