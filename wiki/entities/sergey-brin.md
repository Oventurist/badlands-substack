# Sergey Brin

Sergey Brin is a co-founder of [[google|Google]] whose private wealth management relationship with [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan]] became a subject of litigation in the [[us-virgin-islands|U.S. Virgin Islands]] case against the bank over its dealings with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Epstein, JPMorgan, and the Google account

Court papers filed in Manhattan federal court in August 2023 alleged that Epstein played a key role as an investment "advisor" to Google and to Brin personally, whose portfolio of more than $4 billion was among JPMorgan's largest client accounts.[1] Newly unsealed emails from 2006 indicated that senior bankers organized coverage of the relationship around Epstein rather than around the client's geography: [[mary-erdoes|Mary Erdoes]] urged colleagues to staff the account from New York rather than San Francisco, where Google and Brin are based, writing that the decision was "solely because of our need to have a NY team cover a NY person, Jeffrey Epstein (as the advisor to the partners)."[1]

The same day, Ann Borowiec, then a managing director at JPMorgan's New Jersey Private Bank, circulated a message titled "big new business oppy" noting that a New York team had been chosen "to work with Jeffrey Epstein (advisor) on business oppy associated with GRATS that are terminating for Google founders."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that revelations tying Epstein to elite finance and Silicon Valley wealth, once electrifying to online researchers, had by 2023 become routine coverage — "just another Tuesday" — while insisting that the Epstein subject retains unique power to unite a broader public against what he calls the collectivist enemy.[1]
