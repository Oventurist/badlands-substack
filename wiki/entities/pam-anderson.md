# Pam Anderson

Pam Anderson was the Republican nominee for Colorado Secretary of State in 2022 and the former Executive Director of the [[center-for-tech-and-civic-life|Center for Tech and Civic Life]], the [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]]-funded NGO that distributed more than $400 million into the administration of the 2020 election.[1]

## 2022 Secretary of State race

Anderson took a leave of absence from the organization to run for Secretary of State.[1] She did not emerge from the [[2022-colorado-republican-state-assembly|state assembly]], where [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]] took a 61% majority of assembled delegates and lesser-known candidate Mike O'Donnell took the remainder; Peters faced both O'Donnell and Anderson in the June primary.[1]

Anderson won the Republican primary — an outcome Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted as emblematic of the cycle, noting that the Republican who won the Colorado primary for Secretary of State was the executive director of Zuckerberg's NGO.[1] She lost the general election.[1]
