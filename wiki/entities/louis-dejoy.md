# Louis DeJoy

Louis DeJoy is a North Carolina businessman and Trump campaign donor who was selected as Postmaster General of the [[united-states-postal-service|United States Postal Service]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) He was the first person chosen for the position who had not come up through the organization, making him an outsider to the postal bureaucracy.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)

## Selection

DeJoy was elected by the [[usps-board-of-governors|USPS Board of Governors]] after that body regained a quorum in August 2019 and Postmaster General Megan Brennan announced her retirement; the board picked DeJoy in May.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) At the time of his appointment the board was described as controlled five to one by loyalists to [[donald-trump|President Trump]], who had filled every seat after finding the board entirely empty on taking office in 2017.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)

## Tenure and reception

DeJoy arrived in June and moved to make sweeping cuts to postal operations.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) The political left, Badlands Media notes, has hated DeJoy from the beginning, and his appointment came amid one of the biggest controversies over election integrity in years.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift) In the article's framing, DeJoy's outsider status is a feature rather than a defect: he is portrayed as an instrument of a plan to repurpose the Post Office both to safeguard elections and to deliver a [[public-banking-option|public banking option]] through a revived [[postal-savings-system|Postal Savings System]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-seismic-shift)
