# Philip Banks III

Philip Banks III is a former [[new-york-city-police-department|New York City Police Department]] officer who was appointed deputy mayor for public safety in January 2022 under Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]].[1] In September 2024 he was among the senior New York City officials whose homes were raided by federal agents as part of an investigation into the Adams administration.[1]

## Family network

Banks is one of three brothers occupying overlapping positions of civic influence. His brother [[david-banks|David Banks]] served as Commissioner of Schools and is engaged to Deputy Mayor [[sheena-wright|Sheena Wright]]; a third brother, [[terrence-banks|Terrence Banks]], runs the Pearl Alliance, a firm representing companies holding city contracts worth hundreds of thousands of dollars.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the concentration of family members across policing, education, deputy-mayoral posts, and contract representation as evidence of nepotism and organized-crime-style patronage within New York City's public-private partnership structures, occurring while the mayor's office was "neck deep" in the scandal.[1]
