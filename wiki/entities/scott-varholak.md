# Scott Varholak

**Scott Varholak** is a U.S. Magistrate Judge in the U.S. District of Colorado who is presiding over the federal habeas corpus proceedings brought by former Mesa County Clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]].[1]

## Peters habeas proceedings

Varholak is considering Peters' [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] claims on the habeas corpus motion she filed in 2025, in which she argues that her detention is unconstitutional because she was denied bond pending appeal.[1] In July, Varholak ruled on Peters' expanded claims, narrowing the petition to the First Amendment issue; the question before him is whether trial judge Matthew Barrett's decision to jail Peters over what she might say violates her First Amendment rights.[1]

At a short motion hearing in October 2025, Varholak ruled immediately on several pending motions.[1] He granted admission of an amicus brief from Peters' supporters, deeming it novel but useful.[1] He also granted motions to strike foreign whistleblower testimony about U.S. election systems, deeming it irrelevant to the matter before the court — testimony that would have supported Peters' argument that what she might say is true.[1] The court additionally denied Peters' motion to take notice of the [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] sale as irrelevant.[1] Varholak indicated an expedited ruling on the full petition, possibly by early November.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that she could see the ruling both ways: the government argued the case concerns Peters' misrepresentation of credentials while securing forensic images of voting machines, making the validity of her findings irrelevant, while Ashe countered that since Varholak narrowed the petition to the First Amendment claim, the substance of the "danger" of Peters' speech should be in play.[1] She argued the court has broad discretion to remedy manifest injustice but that Varholak would not revisit the expanded claims he ruled on in July.[1]
