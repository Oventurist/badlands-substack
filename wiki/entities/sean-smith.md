# Sean Smith

Sean Smith was one of the four Americans killed in the September 11, 2012 [[benghazi-attack|attack on the U.S. mission in Benghazi, Libya]].[1] He died alongside Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] and the two former special operators [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]] and [[tyrone-woods|Tyrone Woods]].[1]

## In the Badlands account

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] frames his Benghazi coverage around a refusal to let the four dead Americans be forgotten, honoring Smith by name and pledging that justice will be served for their sacrifice.[1] The article argues that the public debate over Benghazi was deliberately narrowed to two questions — why the Obama administration did not send help and why security at the facility was so weak — and that both were "red herrings" that framed the deaths as the product of mistakes rather than of treason.[1] On that reading, neither President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] nor Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was ever in real danger of consequences, and the ten official inquiries served to bury the underlying reason the attack happened.[1]

These characterizations reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
