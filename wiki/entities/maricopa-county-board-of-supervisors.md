# Maricopa County Board of Supervisors

The Maricopa County Board of Supervisors is the five-member elected governing body of [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona. Among its statutory duties is the conduct and canvass of elections, making it the body legally responsible for certifying results in the state's largest jurisdiction.

## Role in the 2022 election dispute

The board was chaired in 2022 by [[bill-gates-arizona|Bill Gates]], who took to the county's official Twitter account after the November 8 election to answer voter questions in a series of videos — an effort ridiculed in [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]] as "Arizona Elections for Dummies with Mr. Bill."

As the "governing body holding an election" under A.R.S. § 16-642(A), the board was required to meet and canvass the election "not less than six days nor more than twenty days following the election," with the county's official canvass due to the [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Secretary of State]] on or before November 28, 2022. Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] requested a response to her list of concerns before that submission, on the ground that the issues bore directly on the county's "ability to lawfully certify election results." [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] draws attention to subsection C of the statute, which provides that if returns from any polling place are missing, the canvass "shall be postponed from day to day until all the returns are received or until six postponements have been had" — a scenario she calls "uncharted territory."
