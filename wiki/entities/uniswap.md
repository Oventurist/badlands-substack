# Uniswap

Uniswap is a decentralized exchange built on the [[ethereum|Ethereum]] network, where tokens are traded directly from wallets without a centralized intermediary or the identity-verification requirements imposed by custodial exchanges.[1]

Badlands Media's coverage of digital-asset fraud described Uniswap as the venue for the tradable supply of [[saitama-token|Saitama Token]]: 46.06 quadrillion of the token's 100 quadrillion initial supply was added to Uniswap at launch.[1] Investigators tracking the project alleged that after moving undisclosed team tokens through hundreds of sub-wallets and onto centralized exchanges such as Gate.io, Bitmart, and Lbank, the deployer was constrained by those exchanges' KYC limitations and therefore moved some holdings to private wallets and sold them on Uniswap instead.[1] The episode illustrates the article's broader theme that permissionless venues make market manipulation easy to execute but also, ultimately, traceable on-chain.[1]
