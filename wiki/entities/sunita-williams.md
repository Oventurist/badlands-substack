# Sunita Williams

Sunita "Suni" Williams is a [[nasa|NASA]] astronaut whose planned eight-day stopover on the International Space Station turned into a mission of nine and a half months.[1]

## Return to Earth

Williams returned to Earth on Tuesday, March 18, 2025, when the [[spacex|SpaceX]] Dragon capsule *Freedom* splashed down off the coast of Tallahassee, Florida, at 5:57 pm EDT.[1] She was accompanied by fellow long-duration crew member [[butch-wilmore|Butch Wilmore]], NASA astronaut Nick Hague, and Roscosmos cosmonaut Aleksandr Gorbunov.[1]

Conditions at splashdown were described as idyllic, with 78 degree Fahrenheit weather and a pod of curious dolphins swimming around the capsule as ground crews prepared to open it.[1] The capsule was lifted from the water and placed on a recovery ship, where the astronauts took their first breath of fresh air in over nine months.[1]
