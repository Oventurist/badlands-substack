# Emerson College Polling

Emerson College Polling is the survey research unit of Emerson College, whose national and state-level political polls are widely cited in American campaign coverage. In August 2023 the organization released a national poll of the Republican presidential field that became a focal point of commentary about the shape of the 2024 primary.[1]

## August 2023 national primary poll

The Emerson survey found Florida governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] tied for second place in the Republican presidential field with entrepreneur [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]], both at 10 percent.[1] Former president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] led the field with 56 percent, retaining a majority of Republican support heading into the first primary debate.[1] The poll's internal trend lines were the most discussed element of the release: DeSantis had fallen from 21 percent in June, while Ramaswamy had climbed from just 2 percent over the same interval.[1]

## Reception in independent media

The result was treated by ZeroHedge and by commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as evidence that the DeSantis campaign had collapsed and that Ramaswamy was absorbing the anti-establishment protest vote within the primary electorate.[1] Badlands writers used the numbers to argue that candidate "authenticity" rather than institutional backing was driving movement in the field, and that the polling shift coincided with a strategic pivot by establishment-aligned media figures.[1]
