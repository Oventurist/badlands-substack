# Felix Rodriguez

Felix Rodriguez was a Cuban exile recruited and trained by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and a member of the covert exile force [[brigade-2506]], which participated in the [[bay-of-pigs|Bay of Pigs]] invasion and later covert operations.[1] Joe Lange classes him among the network's "untouchables" — operatives shielded from prosecution regardless of exposure.[1]

The Washington Post reported in June 1987 that a money launderer for the major Colombian cocaine cartel had told Congress in secret testimony that he funneled nearly $10 million to the Nicaraguan Contras through Rodriguez.[1] That launderer, [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]], testified that the money was delivered in several installments after his own 1983 arrest, and that he passed the funds in Central America to couriers selected by Felix Rodriguez.[1] Lange treats this as evidence that the [[medellin-cartel]] was a CIA-connected cartel in every respect.[1]

Rodriguez served in the Miami-based brigade commanded by [[manuel-artime]], within the CIA station run under [[jmwave]] by [[ted-shackley]].[1]
