# Warren Buffett

Warren Buffett is the American investor and chairman of Berkshire Hathaway, long regarded as one of the most closely watched capital allocators in global finance. His trading in bank equities is routinely read by analysts as a signal about the health of the institutions involved.[1]

## 2024 sales of Bank of America stock

In September 2024, Buffett began what media accounts described as a "Dump-A-Thon" of [[bank-of-america|Bank of America]] shares, a sustained sequence of disposals that prompted questions from industry analysts about what he might be anticipating.[1] The timing became a focal point of alternative-media commentary after Bank of America disclosed in late October 2024 that federal regulators might bring an enforcement action over deficiencies in its [[bank-secrecy-act|Bank Secrecy Act]], anti-money-laundering, and sanctions compliance programs.[1]

Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] listed Buffett's share sales as one item in a chronology running from the [[toronto-dominion-bank|Toronto-Dominion Bank]] fentanyl laundering probe through [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s cartel asset seizure proposal, presenting the sequence as evidence of a convergence between cartel finance and the banking system.[1]
