# Jay Rasulo

Jay Rasulo is a former chief financial officer of the [[walt-disney-company|Walt Disney Company]] who, in 2024, ran as a dissident board candidate alongside activist investor [[nelson-peltz|Nelson Peltz]] in a proxy contest against Disney's incumbent leadership.[1]

## 2024 Disney proxy fight

Rasulo was Peltz's ally and running mate in the slate seeking seats on the Disney board, a campaign that pressed the company to produce a concrete chief-executive succession plan and criticized its efforts to make more diverse movies and television shows.[1] At the company's shareholder meeting, the challenge fell short: Disney shareholders approved the board members backed by current company leadership under chief executive [[bob-iger|Bob Iger]], denying seats to both Peltz and Rasulo.[1]

The defeat was reported as averting what would have been a stunning embarrassment for Iger, one of Hollywood's leading executives, at a moment when Disney sat at the center of wider American culture wars and faced dizzying changes in the media landscape, including the rise of streaming, the decline of traditional television and growing competition from social media.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the failed Peltz–Rasulo bid within a broader question about whether corrupted American institutions would be subject to "controlled demolition" or resuscitation, concluding after the vote that Disney had been left in the hands of Iger and a consortium of interests backed by [[vanguard-group|Vanguard Group]], [[blackrock|BlackRock Inc.]], T. Rowe Price, Norges Bank Investment Management and ValueAct Capital Management, and that "the Controlled Demolition continues unabated."[1]
