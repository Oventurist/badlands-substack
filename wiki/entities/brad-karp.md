# Brad Karp

Brad Karp is an American lawyer who served as chairman of the law firm Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison, one of the most prominent corporate litigation practices in the United States.

## Resignation amid the Epstein disclosures

The February 17, 2026 Badlands Brief, citing [[axios|Axios]], reports that Karp resigned as Paul Weiss chair as part of the reputational and career fallout following the latest disclosures of Epstein records. The Brief groups his departure with that of Hyatt heir [[tom-pritzker|Tom Pritzker]], who stepped down as executive chair, and with reported investigations and resignation pressure directed at Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]].

The disclosures at issue followed the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s production letter asserting compliance with the [[epstein-files-transparency-act|Epstein Files Transparency Act]]. Reporting cited in the Brief emphasizes that the DOJ's accompanying list of "politically exposed persons" is not itself an allegation, and that some individuals named may appear only in incidental material such as press clippings — a caveat echoed in [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s commentary on guilt by association within the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein narrative]].
