# Vassily Nebenzia

Vassily Nebenzia is the Russian ambassador to the United Nations and Moscow's principal voice at the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]].[1]

## Statements on Israel and Syria

Addressing the Security Council on Wednesday, January 8, 2025, Nebenzia said that Israel's "unlawful actions" in Syria posed a threat to that country's future, blatantly violated international norms, and contributed to a humanitarian crisis in the region.[1] He stated that the Israeli military had occupied a total of 500 square kilometres of Syrian territory, and described Israel's conduct as "a grave violation of the international legal decisions, including numerous [UN] Security Council and General Assembly [resolutions]."[1] He called on the international body to provide an "honest assessment" of the developments.[1]

Nebenzia warned that the campaign by West Jerusalem posed "a threat to Syria's territorial integrity," adding that Israel was attempting to present the results of its military action as a fait accompli.[1] His remarks followed the massive Israeli bombing campaign launched against Syrian targets after the fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar Assad]]'s government in late 2024, and the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]]' move into the UN-established buffer zone from the [[golan-heights|Golan Heights]] — territory internationally recognised as Syrian and occupied by Israel since 1967.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read Nebenzia's intervention as part of a coordinated "pincer move" by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] against [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], with Trump playing "good cop" by praising [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Erdogan]] and Turkey while Putin deployed his diplomats to cast Israel as the disruptive party.[1]

## Warning to Israel over Patriot transfers (July 2024)

In early July 2024, Nebenzia — Russia's permanent representative to the United Nations — warned that [[israel|Israel]] must be ready to face the consequences if it proceeded with reported plans to supply [[ukraine|Ukraine]] with U.S.-made air defense systems.[2] The Financial Times had reported that the United States, Israel and Ukraine were discussing the delivery of up to eight older Israeli Patriot systems to Kiev.[2]

Speaking on Monday as Moscow assumed the rotating presidency of the UN Security Council, Nebenzia said the transfer "could of course have certain political consequences" for relations between Israel and [[russia|Russia]].[2] He added: "The weapons, whoever they are sent by… to Ukraine, will eventually be destroyed, just like other Western and US weapons. That is obvious."[2] Badlands commentary paired the warning with Hezbollah's simultaneous de-escalation signals as evidence that the Russia–NATO confrontation was spilling into the Middle East.[2]

## Accusations against Ukraine over Syria (December 2024)

On Tuesday, December 3, 2024, Nebenzia formalised Moscow's allegation that Ukraine was supporting Syria's al-Qaeda spin-off [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham|Hayat Tahrir al-Sham]], issuing a statement at the United Nations accusing Kyiv of stoking the armed insurgency seeking to take over Syrian territory and threaten [[bashar-al-assad|Assad]].[3] "Ukrainian military instructors from the GUR are present… training HTS fighters for combat operations," he said, referring to the [[main-directorate-of-intelligence-of-ukraine|Main Directorate of Intelligence]].[3] He asserted that HTS had "not only not concealed the fact that they are supported by Ukraine, but they are also openly flaunting this," a claim accompanied by days of reports and images of Ukrainian equipment and drones in jihadist hands during the capture of Aleppo.[3] Nebenzia also criticised UN officials for being "unable to summon the courage to call a spade a spade and to condemn these terrorist attacks" against Syria.[3]

## April 2024 Security Council remarks on Iran

In April 2024, Nebenzia told members of the [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] that Western demands for the body to discuss the Iranian attack on [[israel|Israel]] amounted to hypocrisy, arguing that the council had helped give rise to the strike by failing to act over the illegal bombing that provoked it.[4] "What happened on the night of April 14 did not happen in a vacuum," he said. "The steps undertaken by [[iran|Iran]] became a reaction to the disgraceful inaction by this council."[4]

At issue was the April 1 missile strike that killed seven Iranian military officers, including two senior commanders, at Tehran's consulate in Damascus. Nebenzia said Western members of the council had stood in the way of rebuking Israel over the "egregious attack," despite it being a violation of international law on the inviolability of diplomatic installations.[4] He recalled that Russia had called on colleagues to "clearly and unequivocally condemn such reckless steps to make sure that they don't get repeated" and had warned that otherwise the risk of repetition and general escalation would grow manifold, concluding: "The outcome is now clear for everyone to see."[4]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Nebenzia had "eviscerated the UN and the Western Hegemony," arguing that the episode demonstrated both that the [[united-nations|United Nations]] is a toothless organization incapable of enforcing its own laws and that Iranian and Israeli reputations had effectively flipped in global public perception.[4]
