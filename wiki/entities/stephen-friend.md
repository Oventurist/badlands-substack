# Stephen Friend

Stephen Friend is a former [[fbi|FBI]] agent who became a whistleblower and was among three bureau employees identified in congressional oversight material as suspended in connection with the January 6, 2021 Capitol incursion.[1]

## Suspension and whistleblower testimony

The interim staff report issued on May 18, 2023 by the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] and its [[select-subcommittee-on-the-weaponization-of-the-federal-government|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]] disclosed that at least three FBI agents had been suspended or stripped of their security clearances over January 6, listing Friend together with [[marcus-allen|Marcus Allen]] and [[brett-gloss|Brett Gloss]] as suspended pending review of their cases.[1] The report found that the bureau "weaponized the security clearance adjudication process to silence employees who fight against the politicized 'rot' within the FBI leadership," and drew on new testimony from current and former agents alleging "egregious abuse, misallocation of law enforcement resources and misconduct with the leadership ranks of the FBI."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that the treatment of Friend and his colleagues showed the FBI persecuting agents who refused to conform to leadership's political demands, and that the failure of critics to contest the whistleblower designation applied by [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] lent weight to their claims.[1]
