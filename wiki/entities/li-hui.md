# Li Hui

Li Hui is China's special envoy for Eurasian affairs and a former Chinese ambassador to Russia, tasked with Beijing's shuttle diplomacy on the war in Ukraine.[1]

## Ukraine peace diplomacy

Beginning in early March 2024, Li conducted a European tour covering Ukraine, Poland, Germany, France and Russia to discuss the future of the conflict.[1] Officials told Politico that during this tour China amplified a message that it would boycott any Switzerland-hosted Ukraine peace talks if Russia were excluded, and that Beijing was pressing Europe to allow Russian participation.[1] The Chinese foreign ministry quoted Li as pledging support for "the timely convening of a peace conference with equal participation of all parties."[1]

The diplomacy was previewed alongside an announced trip by [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] to France in early May 2024.[1]
