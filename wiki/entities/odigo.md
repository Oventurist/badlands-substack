# Odigo

Odigo was an Israeli-owned Hebrew instant messaging service that figures in allegations of foreknowledge of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1]

Badlands Media reports that an anonymous tip-off was sent through Odigo warning users two hours before the attacks to avoid the Twin Towers.[1] The account notes that of roughly 4,000 Israeli nationals who lived and worked in and around the World Trade Center, the number of casualties ended up in the single digits — figures the article concedes are contested, while pointing to video footage of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] visiting ground zero and being briefed that only four Israelis died that day.[1]

Odigo cooperated with both Israeli and American law enforcement, including the FBI, but eventually refused to provide further information; a company spokesperson told the *Washington Post* that "[p]roviding more details would only lead to more conjecture."[1]
