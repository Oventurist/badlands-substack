# Civil Military Coordination Centre

The Civil Military Coordination Centre (CMCC) is a headquarters established by the United States in October 2025 at Kiryat Gat, Israel, to house civilian and military personnel from the various countries participating in the [[international-stabilization-force|International Stabilization Force]] in Gaza.[1]

## Smotrich's call to dismantle

In January 2026 Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] said through his office that "the time has come to dismantle the headquarters in Kiryat Gat."[1] He argued that by dismantling the centre, Israel would be able to remove representatives stationed there from "countries like Egypt and Britain that are hostile to Israel and undermine its security," and instead do what is best for the Jewish state; he declined to explain why he had singled out the United Kingdom.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that the two countries Smotrich singled out are, along with the United States, Israel's two historic allies: the modern State of Israel was formed out of British Mandate Palestine, fulfilling a promise made by British Lord Sir Arthur Balfour in 1917 to Sir Walter Rothschild, who had inherited the title Baron Rothschild in 1915, while Egypt was the first Arab nation to normalize relations with Israel in 1980.[1]

He further speculated that if Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] joined Trump's [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], Israeli settlers aligned with Smotrich might amass outside the headquarters in protest and even attack it, confronting soldiers drawn from across what he called the Sovereign Alliance, led by [[turkey|Turkey]] and [[qatar|Qatar]].[1]
