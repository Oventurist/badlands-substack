# Civil Military Coordination Centre

The Civil Military Coordination Centre (CMCC) is a headquarters established by the United States in October 2025 at Kiryat Gat, Israel, to host civilian and military personnel from the various countries participating in the [[international-stabilization-force|International Stabilization Force]] connected with the [[gaza|Gaza]] ceasefire framework.

According to the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026, Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] publicly called for the centre's closure, declaring that "the time has come to dismantle the headquarters in Kiryat Gat." Quoting the Times of Israel, the Brief records Smotrich's argument that dismantling the headquarters would allow Israel to remove representatives from "countries like Egypt and Britain that are hostile to Israel and undermine its security," while he declined to explain why he singled out the United Kingdom. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] notes that Egypt and Britain are two of [[israel|Israel]]'s historic allies, and speculates that Israeli settlers aligned with Smotrich could mass in protest outside the headquarters or even attack it as Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] weighs joining the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]].
