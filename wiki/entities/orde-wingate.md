# Orde Wingate

Colonel Orde Wingate was a British officer whose father, Colonel Charles Wingate, was a leading figure in [[john-nelson-darby|Darby's]] [[plymouth-brethren]] sect and who ensured his son would follow him as, in the article's words, "a deviant imperialist and Christian Zionist."[1]

## British Mandate Palestine

Wingate worked closely with Christopher Sykes, son of Mark Sykes of Sykes-Picot fame, and was sent to British Mandate Palestine in 1935 to train Zionist paramilitary groups.[1] There he created a network of elite "Night Squads" operating in tandem with [[zeev-jabotinsky|Jabotinsky's]] [[haganah]] paramilitary group.[1]

In a letter to his cousin, Wingate wrote: "The Jews are loyal to the Empire… Palestine is essential to our Empire, our Empire is essential to England, England is essential to world peace. We have the chance to plant here in Palestine and Transjordan a loyal, rich and intelligent nation, with which we can hold for us the key to world domination without expense or effort on our part."[1]

## Family connections

The article calls it a notable irony that Wingate had two influential "pro-Arab" orientalist cousins: E.G. Browne, sponsor of Jamal al-Din al-Afghani, the spiritual father of Salafiyyism; and T.E. Lawrence, whose manipulation of Bedouin Hashemite tribes drove the British Empire's first "Arab Spring" against the weakened Ottoman Empire during World War One.[1] It also notes that Wingate's "fellow British intelligence agent" and self-professed satanist [[aleister-crowley]] likewise emerged out of the Plymouth Brethren.[1]
