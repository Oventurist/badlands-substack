# Thomas Massie

Thomas Massie is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Kentucky, known within the corpus primarily for his role as a persistent critic of executive-branch secrecy and a leading congressional voice pressing for full disclosure of the Jeffrey Epstein records.

## Role in the Epstein files disclosure fight

In the February 17, 2026 edition of the [[badlands-brief-0a2|Badlands Brief]], Massie is reported to have appeared on ABC's *This Week* and stated that he does not have confidence in Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]]. Criticizing her conduct during a House Judiciary Committee hearing, Massie said, "I don't think she did very well," and added, "She came with a book full of insults, one for each congressperson. She obviously had one for me."

Massie argued more broadly that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] lacks accountability and that the department's work on the Epstein files is not finished. He pointed specifically to shifting redactions across successive productions and to the absence of explanatory context when names were unredacted — objections that arose in the wake of the DOJ's production letter asserting compliance with the Epstein Files Transparency Act. The Brief describes him as a former DeSantis surrogate.

His criticism placed him alongside Rep. [[nancy-mace|Nancy Mace]] among Republicans in [[congress|Congress]] publicly dissatisfied with the completeness of the disclosures, a dynamic Badlands commentators discussed in terms of the wider [[epstein-narrative|Epstein narrative]] and its effects on the [[maga|MAGA]] coalition.
