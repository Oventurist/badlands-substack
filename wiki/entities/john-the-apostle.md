# John the Apostle

**John the Apostle** is identified in the Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]] as the author of the [[book-of-revelation|book of Revelation]], as well as of the Gospel of John and the epistles 1–3 John.

## Life as described in the corpus

The article states that by the time he wrote Revelation, John had been exiled to the island of Patmos as a death sentence for his faith and ministry in Christ. It characterizes this as a light sentence compared with the fates of other apostles, such as Peter, who suffered various punishments up to and including being crucified upside down. The article dates John's writing to roughly 80 AD.

## His descriptions and the article's reading

Much of the feature turns on how John chose to render what he saw. Describing Christ at the second coming, John used the simile "eyes like a flame of fire" — a phrasing shared with [[prophet-daniel|Daniel]]. [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] argues that both writers knew exactly what fire looked like, and that if John was seeing visions of 2023 and beyond, when lasers (discovered only in 1960) and Bitcoiner "laser eyes" avatars existed, a simile of fire is precisely what one would expect him to write.

The article also notes John's strange descriptions of the Tribulation judgments — 7 seals, 7 trumpets and 7 bowls — as still causing "cussing and discussing to this day," and highlights John's statement that the returning Christ bears a name that no one knows except Himself, which the author reads as Christ returning "Anonymous."
