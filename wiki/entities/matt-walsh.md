# Matt Walsh

Matt Walsh is an American conservative commentator and cultural critic best known for his combative online presence and his work on gender ideology and social issues.[1]

## Reaction to the Bondi hate speech controversy

In September 2025, Walsh featured in Badlands Media commentary surrounding Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]]'s remarks about using federal law enforcement against groups engaged in "hate speech," comments she made on the podcast of [[katie-miller|Katie Miller]] and subsequently walked back.[1] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized Walsh as "spiraling" amid the online argument over speech policing, a dismissive assessment that reflected friction within the broader online right over how to respond to the killing of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] and to proposals for state action against speech.[1]

The episode placed Walsh within a wider dispute in which critics of Bondi cited Kirk's own position that hate speech "does not exist legally in America" and that ugly, gross, and evil speech alike remain protected by the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1] Badlands commentary framed the whole controversy as an exercise in the weaponization of emotion, arguing that provocations of this kind are most effective at steering public opinion precisely while they are happening.[1]
