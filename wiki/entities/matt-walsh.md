# Matt Walsh

Matt Walsh is an American conservative commentator and cultural critic best known for his combative online presence and his work on gender ideology and social issues.[1]

## "What Is a Woman?" (2022)

Walsh is the host and central figure of the [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]] documentary [[what-is-a-woman|"What Is a Woman?"]], released on June 1, 2022, in which he poses the title question to therapists, physicians, academics and members of the public.[2] Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], reviewing the film a year later, wrote that although she did not care for The Daily Wire she had "always appreciated the blunt honesty of Matt Walsh," recalling his earlier commentary on drag shows for children and on males being rewarded for turning womanhood into a caricature.[2] She said she was pleasantly surprised by the seriousness with which Walsh treats pronouns, non-surgical affirmation, women's sports and spaces, [[puberty-blockers|puberty blockers]], surgeries and the nature of truth, and concluded that she had been wrong to judge the book by its cover.[2]

Her criticisms were that Walsh and his team stopped short of indicting the global order and the financial establishment behind medical transition, and that the film underplayed parental rights: in retelling the [[loudoun-county-school-bathroom-assault-case|Loudoun County]] events it highlighted Walsh's publicity stunt of renting a basement to establish residency so he could address the school board, presenting him as the hero rather than centering the parents.[2] Walsh's stated principle in the film, drawn from his confrontation with therapist [[sophie-denbeau|Sophie D'Enbeau]] on "Dr. Phil," was "I care about the truth … Basic truth matters."[2]

In June 2023, as The Daily Wire prepared to stream the film on Twitter, Walsh tweeted that it "got 3,500 retweets in two minutes and then Twitter flagged it as hate speech and shut down retweets. They are terrified of this film."[2] A day later he wrote that all suppression had been lifted and that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] himself had tweeted the film and urged people to watch it, calling the episode "A huge win."[2]

## Reaction to the Bondi hate speech controversy

In September 2025, Walsh featured in Badlands Media commentary surrounding Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]]'s remarks about using federal law enforcement against groups engaged in "hate speech," comments she made on the podcast of [[katie-miller|Katie Miller]] and subsequently walked back.[1] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized Walsh as "spiraling" amid the online argument over speech policing, a dismissive assessment that reflected friction within the broader online right over how to respond to the killing of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] and to proposals for state action against speech.[1]

The episode placed Walsh within a wider dispute in which critics of Bondi cited Kirk's own position that hate speech "does not exist legally in America" and that ugly, gross, and evil speech alike remain protected by the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1] Badlands commentary framed the whole controversy as an exercise in the weaponization of emotion, arguing that provocations of this kind are most effective at steering public opinion precisely while they are happening.[1]
