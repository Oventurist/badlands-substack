# Elizabeth Yore

Elizabeth "Liz" Yore is an American attorney and child-protection advocate who appears frequently as a guest commentator on independent and conservative media programs, where she focuses on international institutions and their policies affecting children.[1]

## UN report controversy (April 2023)

In April 2023, Yore appeared on Steve Bannon's [[war-room|War Room]] program to voice her concerns about a report being promoted by the [[united-nations|United Nations]] that she said recommended the normalization and decriminalization of pedophilia.[1] According to Yore, the report treated pedophilia as a human right that "should not be criminalized or prosecuted," and she characterized its position as holding that "minors can consent to sex with an adult."[1]

Yore further argued that the underlying agenda was being advanced incrementally — a process she described as "softening the target" — through the grooming of children and teachers within school systems.[1] She framed the document as an unprecedented institutional endorsement of a long-standing elite objective, stating: "This is what the global elite have always wanted to legitimize pedophilia. I've never seen a report like this."[1]

Her remarks were amplified by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and subsequently by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], whose commentary accompanying the item argued that the transgender youth medicalization debate formed part of the same broader normalization campaign, and that only a small fraction of child abuse cases — cited as roughly 10 percent — are ever reported.[1]
