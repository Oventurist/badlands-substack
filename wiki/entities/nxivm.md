# NXIVM

NXIVM was a purported self-help organization, described in reporting and in federal prosecutions as a cult, led by [[keith-raniere|Keith Raniere]] and prosecuted in the U.S. federal courts on racketeering and sex-trafficking charges.[1]

## Prosecutions

Federal prosecutors described NXIVM as an organization in which women were recruited and groomed to be used as sexual partners by Raniere.[1] Raniere was sentenced in 2020 to 120 years in prison on charges related to sex trafficking.[1] Former *Smallville* actress [[allison-mack|Allison Mack]] pleaded guilty in 2019 to racketeering and racketeering conspiracy charges for her role in the group, was sentenced in 2021 to 36 months in federal prison, and was released in July 2023 after serving two years, having assisted prosecutors in the case against Raniere.[1]
