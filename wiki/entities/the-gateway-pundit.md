# The Gateway Pundit

**The Gateway Pundit** is an American conservative news outlet that publishes reporting and commentary on election-integrity litigation, among other subjects.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] writes for the outlet.[1]

## Reporting on Free Speech For People (June 2026)

In June 2026 The Gateway Pundit published a CannCon piece examining [[free-speech-for-people|Free Speech For People]]'s campaign against the appointment of attorney [[kurt-olsen|Kurt Olsen]] to a senior [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] role.[1] The article noted that FSFP co-founder and president [[john-c-bonifaz|John Bonifaz]] had previously raised concerns — in a letter still hosted on the National Institute of Standards and Technology website — about voting software from a "foreign-controlled company" with "ties to the Venezuelan government."[1] It contrasted that record with NBC News's description of Olsen as "an election denier" and of FSFP as a nonprofit focused on free and fair elections and rooting out government corruption.[1]

The outlet had also previously reported that FSFP was intimately involved in a post-2020 lawsuit against canvassers in Colorado alleging violations of the [[ku-klux-klan-act|Ku Klux Klan Act]], a case it said rested almost exclusively on the testimony of a single witness coached by attorneys, and which the defendants won in federal court on a directed verdict.[1]

## Dimmitt dairy explosion coverage

In April 2023 The Gateway Pundit reported the declaration of a mass casualty incident following an explosion and fire at South Fork Dairy in Dimmitt, Texas, in which nearly 20,000 cattle died.[2] Its account described smoke visible from as much as 80 miles away, multiple structures engulfed, an unknown cause, and a statement from Castro County Sheriff Sal Rivera that surviving animals injured badly enough would have to be destroyed.[2] The report was excerpted in the Badlands News Brief alongside commentary from [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]].[2]

## Coverage of the January 6 footage release

In March 2023 The Gateway Pundit provided close coverage of [[tucker-carlson]]'s broadcasts of Capitol surveillance video, reporting his claims regarding [[brian-sicknick]], [[jacob-chansley]] and [[ray-epps]] and characterizing his Monday program as "explosive."[3] The outlet subsequently reported that Carlson's Tuesday show was "tampered down," and raised the suggestion that [[rupert-murdoch]] and the [[fox-news]] leadership had pressured Carlson into changing his show plan.[3]

## Flynn interview (February 2023)

On February 1, 2023, Gateway Pundit contributors Jim and Joe Hoft interviewed retired general [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] about the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]].[4] In that interview Flynn said [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had travelled to Ukraine 12 or 13 times during his final year as vice president and argued that every trip warranted public investigation, remarks widely recirculated in alternative media including the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[4]

## USPS ballot reporting

The Gateway Pundit reported on the 2020 postal ballot allegations from December 2020 onward and, in June 2023, published the story that the [[united-states-postal-service|Postal Service]]'s final report vindicated contract driver [[jesse-morgan|Jesse Morgan]] by confirming he hauled a trailer of ballots from New York to Pennsylvania in late October 2020.[5] Its coverage carried the [[amistad-project|Amistad Project]]'s sworn declarations on ballots at issue in Arizona, Michigan, Georgia and Pennsylvania and attorney [[phill-kline|Phill Kline]]'s account of the Bethpage-to-Lancaster shipment.[5]

## Key Bridge collapse coverage

In March 2024 The Gateway Pundit carried independent journalist [[lara-logan|Lara Logan]]'s account of the Baltimore bridge collapse, presenting it as "very different from the reports in the mainstream media" and quoting her claim that intelligence sources described the event as an "absolutely brilliant strategic attack" on the nation's second busiest strategic roadway for hazardous materials.[6]

## Trump declassification letter (2023)

In June 2023, The Gateway Pundit reported that [[dc-draino|DC Draino]] had tweeted a signed letter from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] dated January 19, 2021, the day before he left office, declassifying all of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] documents.[7] The outlet framed the letter as explaining why the [[fbi|FBI]] raided Mar-a-Lago, describing the documents as proof that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], [[james-comey|James Comey]], [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], [[james-clapper|James Clapper]], the [[cia|CIA]], the [[state-department|State Department]] and the Deep State had spied on Trump.[7]

## Reporting on January 6 undercover operatives

In April 2023, [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] reported that court documents revealed the Biden [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] admitting to at least 40 undercover operatives with the Proud Boys on January 6, 2021 — a disclosure central to the [[fedsurrection|"Fedsurrection"]] thesis.[8] Badlands writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described 40 government confidential human source agents undercover that day in addition to 40 undercover operatives and 8 informants inside the group, with further assets within the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]], putting the confirmed total near 100 excluding non-federal anarchist provocateurs.[8]

## Reporting on Maricopa County ballot printers

Gateway Pundit reporter Jordan Conradson documented the shifting testimony of [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] Elections Director [[scott-jarrett|Scott Jarrett]] in the first [[lake-v-hobbs|Lake v. Hobbs]] trial, reporting that Jarrett claimed "no knowledge" of a 19-inch ballot image being placed on 20-inch paper before changing his account the following day.[10] The outlet also reported on the county's [[maricopa-county-2022-ballot-on-demand-printer-investigation|Ballot on Demand printer investigation]], characterizing it as a sham independent inquiry released after the election day disaster.[10]
