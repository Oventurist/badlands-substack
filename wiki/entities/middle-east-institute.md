# Middle East Institute

The Middle East Institute is a Washington-based policy research organization whose analysts are frequently cited in reporting on U.S. relations with Gulf states.[1]

## Commentary on the US–Saudi "plan B"

In May 2024, Firas Maksad, the institute's senior director for strategic outreach, was quoted on Saudi efforts to decouple a bilateral U.S.–[[saudi-arabia|Saudi]] security and technology package from a broader Middle East settlement involving [[israel|Israel]] and the Palestinians.[1] Maksad argued: "There should be room for a less-for-less model, so the relationship with the US need not be held hostage to the whims of Israeli politics or Benjamin Netanyahu."[1] The remark encapsulated the rationale for Riyadh's fallback proposal, under which completion of the U.S.–Saudi agreements would not depend on assent from the [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] government.[1]
