# Owen Young

Owen Young was an American industrialist and financier described as a [[jp-morgan|JP Morgan]] asset who headed [[general-electric|General Electric]].[1]

In 1928 he instituted a German debt repayment plan — the [[young-plan|Young Plan]] — that gave rise to the [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]] and consolidated an international cartel of industrialists and financiers on behalf of the City of London and Wall Street.[1] The largest of the resulting cartels merged Henry Ford's German operations with [[ig-farben|IG Farben]], Dupont industries, Britain's Shell and Rockefeller's [[standard-oil|Standard Oil]].[1]
