# Niko House

Niko House is an American independent political commentator quoted in August 2024 alleging that Venezuelan opposition leader [[maria-corina-machado|Maria Corina Machado]] operates as a paid agent of the United States government.[1]

"She's been operating as a foreign agent on behalf of the United States. The [[cia|CIA]] literally writes her a paycheck," House said in remarks reported by Sputnik and reproduced in the *Badlands News Brief*.[1] House also pointed to Venezuela's electoral system, which he described as containing multiple checks and balances and permitting the opposition to monitor vote counting at precinct level, and argued that "the most simplistic explanation is the oil" behind Washington's interest in the election.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cautioned that House's accusation was not definitive proof even if it aligned with existing skepticism of the CIA, but credited him with "compelling logic," noting the parallel that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] has never called for an audit of the 2020 U.S. election while [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] has invited scrutiny of his own.[1]
