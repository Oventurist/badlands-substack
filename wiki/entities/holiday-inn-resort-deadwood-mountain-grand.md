# Holiday Inn Resort Deadwood Mountain Grand

The **Holiday Inn Resort Deadwood Mountain Grand** is a hotel and resort in [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood, South Dakota]] that served as the conference venue for [[gart-5-deadwood|GART 5]], the fifth stop of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]], held August 15–18, 2024.[1]

## Role in GART 5

Badlands Media named the property as the site of the GART 5 conference programme in its July 6, 2024 ticket announcement, linking directly to the hotel's booking page.[1] The network cautioned prospective attendees that, "due to the limited venue options in this exclusive area," the hosting hotel was already sold out at the time tickets went on sale.[1] Attendees were therefore advised that accommodations would need to be made at nearby hotels or short-term rental destinations in the area, and that ticket buyers were responsible for sourcing their own lodging and travel.[1]

The venue hosted the weekend's programme of live panels, in-person versions of Badlands shows, audience Q&A sessions and meet-and-greets with network hosts, along with a Friday-night Welcome Reception and breakfast and lunch service across the weekend included with in-person tickets.[1] A separate Thursday-night VIP dinner associated with the event had sold out before the July announcement.[1]
