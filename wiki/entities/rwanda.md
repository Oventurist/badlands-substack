# Rwanda

Rwanda is a central African state treated in the Badlands corpus as the external sponsor of the [[m23-movement|M23]] insurgency in the eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].

## Role in the Congo conflict

The [[badlands-brief-1ce|January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief]] describes the [[alliance-fleuve-congo|Alliance Fleuve Congo]], led by [[corneille-nangaa|Corneille Nangaa]], as "Rwanda-linked," and reports that militias crossing into eastern Congo from Rwanda have seized mining towns. Former Congolese president [[joseph-kabila|Joseph Kabila]] was convicted in absentia of treason for aiding these forces.

## Sanctions and alignment

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] notes that [[congress|Congress]] was discussing sanctions against Rwanda, and characterizes the country as one "which has long been an ally of [[israel|Israel]] and a faithful purveyor of the Zionist lifestyle." The commentary places Rwanda on the opposing side of the corpus's [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] framework in the contest over African mineral resources.
