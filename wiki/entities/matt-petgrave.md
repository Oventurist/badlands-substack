# Matt Petgrave

**Matt Petgrave** is an ice hockey player for the [[sheffield-steelers|Sheffield Steelers]] who was the other player involved in the October 28, 2023 incident in which American player [[adam-johnson|Adam Johnson]] died after his neck was cut by a skate blade.[1] He was 31 years old at the time.[1]

## The incident

Video of the play showed Johnson skating with the puck toward the Steelers net when Petgrave skated toward him and collided with another [[nottingham-panthers|Nottingham Panthers]] player.[1] As Petgrave began to fall, his left skate kicked up and the blade struck Johnson in the neck.[1] The incident reverberated around the hockey community and prompted moments of silence in the NHL.[1]

On November 14, 2023, [[south-yorkshire-police|South Yorkshire Police]] arrested a man on suspicion of manslaughter in the case but did not name the suspect or provide his age.[1]
