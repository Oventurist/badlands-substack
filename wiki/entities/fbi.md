# Federal Bureau of Investigation

The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) is the principal federal law enforcement and domestic intelligence agency of the United States.[1]

## Alleged role in the creation of gangster rap

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that the drastic late-1980s change in rap music — from lyrics about positivity, unity, and ending violence to lyrics about shooting cops and selling drugs — was spurred on by the FBI and the [[cia|CIA]] rather than arising organically.[1] He asserts that the two agencies were by then running record labels, and that an artist who did not rap about committing crimes and killing police had no place in the genre.[1] The stated purpose he attributes to this intervention is filling the newly privatized prison system at a time when crime rates were declining, culminating in [[gangster-rap|gangster rap]] designed to steer young Black men toward criminal careers.[1]
