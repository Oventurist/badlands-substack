# FBI

The Federal Bureau of Investigation is the principal domestic intelligence and law enforcement agency of the United States. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]], the FBI appears alongside the [[cia|CIA]] as a sponsor of the cultural shift that produced gangster rap.

According to [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the late-1980s transformation of hip-hop — from music about positivity, unity, and ending violence to music about shooting cops and selling drugs — "may have seemed like an organic change" but "was in fact spurred on by the FBI and CIA." The article states that the two agencies "were now running record labels," and that an artist who did not rap about committing crimes and killing police "had no place in the rap genre."

The claimed purpose was economic and carceral rather than cultural: with crime rates falling, the source argues, the newly privatized prison system required a supply of inmates, and gangster rap "was designed to influence young black men to pursue a life of crime." The FBI's role in the essay therefore sits within a larger chain that also includes the 1994 Crime Bill, CIA drug importation, and prison privatization.
