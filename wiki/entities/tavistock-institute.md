# Tavistock Institute

The Tavistock Institute is a London-based organization devoted to the study of human relations and group psychology. In Badlands Media commentary it is named as the origin point of the modern [[transgender-movement|Trans Movement]].[1]

## Alleged role in the Trans Movement

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] described the Trans Movement as "by far the cruelest" of the woke movements and wrote that it originated from the Tavistock Institute in London, England.[1] He added the aside that "anytime you hear something came from the City of London, pay close attention," and argued that the movement preys on those with psychological disorders.[1]

Carlson distinguished the alleged harms of Tavistock's project from those of other narratives, arguing that while many "narrative hoaxes" can be walked away from with little or no permanent damage, those affected by the Trans Movement may be physically and irreversibly altered.[1]

## Formation and institutional description

The Tavistock Institute of Human Relations is described as a British not-for-profit organization that applies social science to contemporary issues and problems; it was initiated in 1946, when it developed from the [[tavistock-clinic|Tavistock Clinic]], and was formally established as a separate entity in September 1947.[5] Its journal, *Human Relations*, is published on its behalf by Sage Publications, and the institute is located on Gee Street in Clerkenwell, London.[5] The institute engages in educational, research, consultancy, project evaluation and professional development work founded on methodologies drawn from the social sciences and applied psychology, and its own website frames its focus as how humans relate to each other and to non-human systems, and how people grow in character and are affected by learning, creativity and change.[5]

The transition from a clinic treating shell-shocked soldiers into an influential think tank, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes, could not have happened without major financial donations from the Rockefeller family, while the preceding clinic had been funded by the Rothschild and British Royal families.[5] Key figures who carried across from clinic to institute include the British military psychiatrist [[john-rawlings-rees|John Rawlings Rees]] and the German-American psychologist [[kurt-lewin|Kurt Lewin]], whose unfreeze–change–refreeze model of organizational change Carlson reads as a blueprint for social engineering.[5] Rees is quoted urging colleagues to "imitate the Totalitarians and organize some kind of fifth column activity."[5] [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] is identified as a student of Rees.[5]

## Closure of the Gender Identity Development Service

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] identified the shutting down of the Tavistock gender clinic in the United Kingdom as the first of three international developments that, in his account, should have discredited the [[gender-affirming-care|gender affirmative model]] in North America.[6] In December 2019 The Telegraph reported that the UK's largest children's transgender clinic had been hit by 35 resignations over a three-year period, with all of the departing staff warning of massive over-diagnosis of [[gender-dysphoria|gender dysphoria]] in children.[6]

A review of the clinic's practices by Dr. [[hilary-cass|Hilary Cass]] resulted in the clinic being shut down.[6] The clinic sought to deflect criticism, claiming its Gender Identity Development Service (GIDS) was closing only because the [[cass-review|Cass Review]] recommended a regional model instead of a single national service and not because of safety concerns, but Esler argued the resulting policy changes told a different story.[6] The review's interim report defined the affirmative model as "a model of gender healthcare that originated in the USA," and cited a sharp rise in referrals, poorly understood changes in the patient population, scarce and inconclusive evidence, and operational failures of the single-clinic model.[6]
