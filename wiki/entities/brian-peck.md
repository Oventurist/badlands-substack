# Brian Peck

Brian Peck is an American dialogue coach who worked in children's television and was convicted as a child molester. He was one of the figures highlighted in the March 2024 reporting on abuse connected to [[nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]], and one of two offenders featured in an HBO documentary about alleged child abuse at the channel.[1]

Peck molested the child star [[drake-bell|Drake Bell]].[1] Commentators noted that after his conviction as a child molester Peck was hired by [[walt-disney-company|Disney]], a sequence cited as evidence that major entertainment companies functioned as safe havens for convicted offenders.[1] Badlands Media writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] used the Peck–Bell case as an illustration of how trauma travels between generations within an industry that normalizes child abuse, arguing that such an industry will in time produce more abusers.[1]
