# Stephen Lynch

Stephen Lynch is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Massachusetts.[1]

## Support for Biden in July 2024

After a closed-door House Democratic meeting on July 9, 2024 convened to discuss President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s political future following the [[2024-presidential-debate|June 2024 presidential debate]], Lynch said he was backing Biden and that "I feel we've hit a turning point."[1] He added that, as the president had said, "it's been 10, 11 days or so since the debate and it's time to move on," and that many more members had arrived at that position than had a week earlier.[1] His remarks were reported alongside those of [[james-clyburn|James Clyburn]] as evidence that Biden had, at least temporarily, halted the flow of individual Democratic detractors from becoming a flood.[1]
