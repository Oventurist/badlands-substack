# Michael Crow

Michael Crow is the president of [[arizona-state-university|Arizona State University]].[1] He simultaneously serves as chairman of [[in-q-tel|In-Q-Tel]], the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]]'s venture capital arm.[1]

That dual role is cited as one of the clearest illustrations of the intelligence/academia nexus in its present form: the same individual leads a large public research university and chairs the investment vehicle of a foreign intelligence service's technology portfolio.[1] Under Crow, ASU hosts the [[center-on-narrative-disinformation-and-strategic-influence|Center on Narrative, Disinformation & Strategic Influence]], which [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] has characterized as one of many CIA cut-outs, building databases of pro-Trump social media content and developing "intelligence dashboards" for censorship policies.[1]
