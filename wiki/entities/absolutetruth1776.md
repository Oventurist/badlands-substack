# AbsoluteTruth1776

AbsoluteTruth1776 is a contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], writing commentary on finance, censorship and the [[deep-state|deep state]] from a Substack account of the same name.[1]

## Commentary on the March 2023 banking crisis

In the March 13, 2023 edition of the Badlands News Brief, AbsoluteTruth1776 responded to Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]]'s statement that the federal government would not bail out [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] by declaring that "the game is up" and that the [[fiat-currency|fiat]] system was "in its death rattle," on the grounds that one "can only print fake money with no worth for so long before the bubble pops."[1] He predicted the complete destruction of the federal banks and the fiat system as then constituted, adding that although the process would "hurt a bit," the cabal's "fiat slave system must be toppled and blown into the wind" if the cabal itself were to fall.[1]

Commenting on the failure of [[signature-bank|Signature Bank]] — the institution that had cancelled [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s personal accounts after the events of January 6, 2021 — he argued that as the fiat system cracked under the weight of its own corruption it was becoming apparent that the deep state was losing its grip on the monetary system it had used as a means of control since its inception, and forecast that SVB and Signature "will not be the last big banks to go under."[1]

## Commentary on censorship

Responding to Representative [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]]'s report that a Democratic senator had asked on an emergency banking call whether a program existed to censor social media speech, AbsoluteTruth1776 wrote that the "attack on the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] and blood-thirst for complete control of the narrative has become blatantly obvious."[1] He characterised the officials involved as "totalitarians occupying democrat seats" seeking greater control over speech and the flow of information by censoring posts they judged capable of creating a run on the banks, and concluded that there was "becoming less and less cover for these corrupt demons and their deeds to hide."[1]

## Commentary on the January 6 footage and vaccine policy

Responding to [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s March 2023 broadcast of Capitol security video appearing to show [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] escorting [[jacob-chansley|Jacob Chansley]] toward the Senate floor, AbsoluteTruth1776 wrote that "they lied. Again," arguing that for two years the public had received only edited and chopped videos that served to build a narrative against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his supporters.[2] He asserted that there had been no insurrection and that January 6 instead represented "one of the largest psyops ever run against the American people, and their sitting President in history," predicting that the narrative would continue to crumble as more footage was released.[2]

In the same brief he criticised the refusal to grant [[novak-djokovic|Novak Djokovic]] a vaccine waiver to enter the United States, noting the entry requirement was due to lapse on May 11 and contrasting the exclusion of the world's top-ranked tennis player with the admission of vaccinated [[antifa]] attackers from abroad who targeted an establishment in Atlanta.[2]

## "Voting matters" (February 2023)

In the February 23, 2023 Badlands News Brief, AbsoluteTruth1776 commented on the [[north-carolina-supreme-court|North Carolina Supreme Court]]'s decision to rehear two voting cases, opening with the assertion that "voting matters."[3] He recounted that the court had flipped from a Democratic majority to a Republican supermajority in November 2022 and that, on their way out, the Democratic justices had "legislated from the bench" by holding [[north-carolina-voter-id-law|voter ID]] unconstitutional and finding that Republicans had unfairly gerrymandered districts in [[harper-v-hall|Harper v. Hall]].[3] He wrote that neither assertion was accurate and argued that the high likelihood of both rulings being overturned showed that North Carolinians who went to the polls in November had successfully voted out justices he described as incredibly biased, if not corrupt.[3]

## Thanksgiving 2023 reflection

Writing as "Abs," the contributor supplied a short reflection to the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Thanksgiving post of November 23, 2023.[4] The message expressed thanks for "my faith and my family," for "my Badlands family, and for all of our Badlanders out there," and for the chance "to share such an amazing time in history with so many incredible people," ending with thanks for readers' love and support and wishes for a blessed Thanksgiving.[4]
