# Scientific American

*Scientific American* is an American popular science magazine. In Badlands Media coverage by [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], the publication is characterised as a propaganda outlet whose reporting on paediatric gender medicine functions as an instrument of information warfare aimed at left-wing cognition.[1]

## The May 2022 article

Esler singled out a May 2022 *Scientific American* article entitled "What the Science on Gender-Affirming Care for Transgender Kids Really Shows" as one of the best examples of a message targeting left-wing readers.[1] He argued that the most notable feature of the piece, from an information-warfare perspective, is that it relies almost entirely on American citations — a curation he called deeply intentional, because international sources would quickly undo nearly everything the article claims.[1] He suggested the comparison also indicates how captured American science and academia must be to operate in stark contrast to the rest of the world with such confidence.[1]

Among the statements Esler challenged was the article's claim that "a decade of research shows [gender affirmative] treatment reduces depression, suicidality and other devastating consequences of trans preteens and teens being forced to undergo puberty in the sex they were assigned at birth."[1] He contrasted this with the [[nhs|NHS]] protocol changes in England, which reframed [[gender-affirming-care|gender affirmative care]] as a model originating in the USA and restricted [[puberty-blockers|puberty blockers]] to research settings.[1]

Esler also contrasted the magazine's treatment of social transition — presented as harmless and as simply giving children "time to figure things out" — with new NHS guidance recognising social transition as a psychosocial intervention rather than a neutral act, and strongly discouraging it in children.[1]

A further passage he quoted warned that "more than a dozen state legislatures or administrations are considering—or have already passed—laws banning health care for transgender young people," and cited April 20 guidance from the Florida Department of Health withholding gender-affirming care including social transition.[1] Esler argued that the "American Model" so framed as "health care" had been outright banned in the United Kingdom.[1]

## Assessment in Badlands coverage

Esler concluded that outlets such as *Scientific American* must rely strictly on what he described as a corrupt network of pseudoscientific institutions for evidence, summarising the phenomenon in a single phrase repeated throughout his essay: "Information warfare."[1]
