# Dwayne Johnson

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson is an American actor and former professional wrestler, described in Badlands commentary as perhaps the most famous "heel" character in the history of [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]].[1]

## Speculation about a presidential run

In November 2023 Johnson told The Guardian that he had been asked "by both parties" to run for president of the United States.[1] Writing in July 2024, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited that claim while speculating that the post-[[joe-biden|Biden]] Democratic field resembled a wrestling battle royal to determine who would face [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in November — a scenario the author framed under the heading "[[trumpamania|Trumpmania]] 2024" and connected to the [[kayfabe|kayfabe]] symbolism on display at the [[2024-republican-national-convention|2024 Republican National Convention]].[1]
