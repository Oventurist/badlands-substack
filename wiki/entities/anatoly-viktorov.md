# Anatoly Viktorov

Anatoly Viktorov is the Russian ambassador to [[israel]], serving in Tel Aviv during the June 2025 escalation between Israel and [[iran]].[1]

## June 2025 remarks

Speaking to the television news channel Russia 24 on Monday, June 16, 2025, Viktorov said that Russian nationals in Israel, including diplomatic staff, faced significant danger because of the ongoing hostilities.[1] "The situation is nerve-wracking, I must admit," he said. "The threat to the lives and well-being of both diplomats and Russian citizens in Israel is not ephemeral."[1]

He described mounting difficulties confronting the embassy since tensions escalated following Israel's attacks on Iranian nuclear sites the previous week.[1] Shockwaves from nearby explosions had shattered windows in several embassy residences, though he noted that "thankfully, there were no direct hits, but the incidents were unfortunate."[1] Viktorov added that the US consular office in Tel Aviv had also recently sustained minor damage from flying debris.[1] Embassy workloads had increased sharply; the ambassador said he had managed only three hours of sleep the previous night, while still urging his employees to take their annual leave.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] read the embassy's guidance — that staff consider returning home to visit family and that travelers fly out through Egypt rather than Tel Aviv — as a signal that the Kremlin expected the situation to deteriorate further.[1] He argued this implied [[vladimir-putin]] would need to intervene forcefully to stop an escalation before something destabilized Iran's government, such as [[benjamin-netanyahu]] threatening to assassinate [[ali-khamenei|the Ayatollah]].[1]
