# Andriy Yusov

Andriy Yusov is a spokesperson for the [[main-directorate-of-intelligence|Main Directorate of Intelligence]] (GUR) of Ukraine's Ministry of Defense.[1]

## Confirmation of the Budanova poisoning

In November 2023, Yusov confirmed to Reuters the reports that [[marianna-budanova|Marianna Budanova]], wife of intelligence chief [[kyrylo-budanov|Kyrylo Budanov]], had been poisoned, stating: "Yes, I can confirm the information, unfortunately, it is true." He did not clarify when the poisoning took place.[1] The BBC's Ukrainian service further cited Yusov as saying that several other GUR officials had experienced milder symptoms of poisoning, which fueled speculation about a wider poisoning plot targeting Ukrainian intelligence personnel.[1]
