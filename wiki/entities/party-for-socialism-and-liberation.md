# Party for Socialism and Liberation

The Party for Socialism and Liberation (PSL) is a Marxist-Leninist political party in the United States, closely linked organizationally to the [[answer-coalition|ANSWER Coalition]] and to activists such as [[brian-becker|Brian Becker]].

## Placement in the Singham funding network

"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes" names the PSL among the downstream beneficiaries of the funding architecture attributed to [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]]. The Brief reports that six nonprofits receiving Singham money — including [[peoples-forum|People's Forum Inc.]], [[tricontinental|Tricontinental Ltd.]] and [[codepink|CodePink Women For Peace]] — passed at least $223 million into a wider global network comprising the People's Welfare Association, the ANSWER Coalition, the Party for Socialism and Liberation and other unidentified groups. These flows form part of the subject matter of the Manhattan federal grand jury inquiry led by U.S. Attorney [[jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]].
