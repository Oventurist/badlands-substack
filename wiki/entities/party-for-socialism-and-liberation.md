# Party for Socialism and Liberation

The Party for Socialism and Liberation (PSL) is a US socialist political organization identified as a recipient within the broader network described in the 2026 [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]] grand jury investigation.[1]

## Position in the network

Six nonprofits funded through Singham's channels reportedly directed at least $223 million into a wider global network encompassing the People's Welfare Association, the [[answer-coalition|ANSWER Coalition]], the PSL, and various unidentified groups.[1] The investigation, run by US Attorney [[jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]] and authorized by Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]], concerns alleged wire fraud, bank fraud, and money laundering in the funding of socialist, communist, and Marxist organizations in the United States.[1]
