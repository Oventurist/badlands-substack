# KGlobal

KGlobal is the Washington-area public relations firm associated with Democratic operative [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles "Chuck" Dolan Jr.]], whose emails and business records became a significant body of evidence at the 2022 trial of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]].[1]

## Records at the Danchenko trial

On the second day of trial, [[john-durham|John Durham]] produced a binder of exhibits consisting of KGlobal emails and business records for examination of FBI Supervisory Intelligence Analyst [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]].[1] The defense had filed a motion in limine against portions of these records and signalled it would object when specific items were offered; according to the trial correspondent, the record the defense most wanted excluded was one in which Dolan speculated that Danchenko might be KGB or a Russian agent.[1]

Among the admitted material was an August 19, 2016 email from Danchenko to Dolan — sent two months before the [[fbi|FBI]] met [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]] and five months before the Bureau interviewed Danchenko — together with Dolan's reply and further exchanges between the two men.[1] Durham used the sequence to demonstrate not merely communication but active cooperation between Dolan and Danchenko in gathering information on Donald Trump and members of his campaign, material that could then be fed to Steele for his dossier.[1]
