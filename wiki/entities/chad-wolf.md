# Chad Wolf

Chad Wolf served as Acting Secretary of the Department of Homeland Security during the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Special counsel interview (2023)

In February 2023 it was reported that Wolf had spent four hours being interviewed under oath a few weeks earlier as part of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigation into the events of January 6, 2021 and any attempts to interfere with the transfer of power following the 2020 presidential election.[1] The disclosure came alongside news that Smith had subpoenaed former Vice President [[mike-pence|Michael Pence]] and former National Security Advisor [[robert-obrien|Robert O'Brien]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] argued that as Smith investigated these matters he was likely to uncover evidence of election interference and insurrection — "but not by Trump and Team."[1]

## Grand jury testimony on voting machine seizure (April 2023)

In April 2023 CNN reported that former top national security officials had testified to a federal grand jury that they repeatedly told Trump and his allies that the government lacked the authority to seize voting machines after the 2020 election.[2] Wolf, as the former acting Homeland Security secretary, and his former deputy [[ken-cuccinelli|Ken Cuccinelli]] were asked about internal administration discussions around DHS seizing voting machines when they appeared before the grand jury earlier that year.[2] Cuccinelli testified that he "made clear at all times" that DHS did not have such authority.[2] Robert O'Brien separately recounted to prosecutors conversations about seizing voting machines, including during a heated Oval Office meeting in which Trump participated.[2]
