# Michael Bohnert

Michael Bohnert is an air warfare expert at the [[rand-corporation|RAND Corporation]] who commented in October 2024 on the expected attrition of Western-supplied weapons in Ukraine.[1]

## Remarks on Western weapons in Ukraine

Speaking to *Business Insider*, Bohnert argued that if Ukrainian forces used donated F-16 fighter jets and Abrams tanks to their full potential, few or none should be expected to survive the war: "If any of the F-16s make it to the end of the war, they probably weren't used hard enough."[1] He added, "I hate saying that, but we as the West have to realize every Abrams tank that was given, every F-16, if any of them make it through the war, they probably weren't used to their maximum extent."[1] Analysts quoted alongside him framed the conflict as an intense, protracted fight in which the small numbers of Western systems delivered limit Ukrainian options, and in which maximizing their use necessarily raises the risk of losing them.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized Bohnert as a "shill" for RAND and derided his framing as sociopathic, arguing that it amounted to demanding the destruction of every donated system in defense of what the author called a puppet state.[1] The commentary paired his remarks with reporting on Ukraine's [[ukrainian-desertion-crisis|desertion crisis]] to argue that Western war planning was detached from conditions on the ground.[1]
