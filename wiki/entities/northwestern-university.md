# Northwestern University

Northwestern University is an American private research university whose chief investment officer, [[amy-falls|Amy C. Falls]], is named in "The Harvard Control Grid" among the stewards associated with the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]].[1]

The article notes that Falls holds the Northwestern CIO post while having previously served as CIO of [[rockefeller-university|Rockefeller University]] and as a [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]] managing director, and that she also sat on Harvard's own investment committees — giving her, in the author's words, "simultaneous influence across multiple elite portfolios."[1] Northwestern thus appears in the piece as one node in a small, overlapping circuit of university endowment management.[1]
