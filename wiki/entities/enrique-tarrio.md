# Enrique Tarrio

Enrique Tarrio is the Afro-Cuban American chairman of the [[proud-boys|Proud Boys]].[1]

Tarrio's ethnicity has repeatedly featured in disputes over how the organization is characterized. In a May 2023 segment of [[the-view|The View]], co-host [[ana-navarro|Ana Navarro]] invoked Tarrio by name — "we all have to remember that the head of the Proud Boys, his name is Enrique Tarrio" — while arguing that the group is a white-nationalist organization and that Hispanic or Black identity does not preclude someone from holding white-supremacist views.[1] Independent commentators, including Badlands Media's [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]], rejected that reasoning as a rhetorical device for labeling political opponents of any ethnicity as racist.[1]

## Pardon and release (January 2025)

Tarrio was serving a 22-year sentence for his role in the January 6 Capitol riot when he was pardoned by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in January 2025.[2] Pictured for the first time since being freed, he called for those behind the mass convictions to "feel the heat" and "pay for what they did."[2] Speaking to a local outlet, Tarrio said: "I honestly think that the prosecutors, [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]], Matthew Graves, they should be investigated, and if they committed any crimes, they should be prosecuted."[2] He added: "I'm happy that the president's focusing not on retribution and focusing on success, but I will tell you that I'm not gonna play by those rules. The people who did this, they need to feel the heat. They need to be put behind bars, and they need to be prosecuted."[2]
