# Banamex

Banamex is the Mexican subsidiary bank owned by [[citibank|Citibank]].[1] Of Citibank's 2,649 branches across 19 countries, 1,494 are Banamex locations in Mexico — nearly twice the number of Citibank's 723 branches in the United States.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that this concentration of Citibank's global footprint in Mexico "is not a coincidence."[1] He writes that it was through Banamex that Citibank used currency exchange facilities to launder drug money for the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín cartel]] and the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]], a subject he treated at length in an earlier article titled "Follow The Money."[1] Banamex therefore sits at the intersection of the two operations Lange identifies as directly tied to the CIA and [[george-hw-bush|George H. W. Bush]]: the Medellín cartel laundering and the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]] drug trafficking network in Mexico.[1]
