# Germany

Germany is a leading member state of the [[european-union|European Union]] and, in the January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]], one of the loudest European voices warning that the United States is eroding the postwar international system.

## The Steinmeier intervention

German President [[frank-walter-steinmeier|Frank-Walter Steinmeier]] said US policy under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is helping erode the global order, citing the operation against [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] among destabilizing developments, and warned against turning global politics into "a den of robbers." The Badlands commentary treats these statements as evidence that the [[rules-based-international-order|rules-based international order]] is coming apart, and pairs them with French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]]'s lament that the United States is turning away from its allies.
