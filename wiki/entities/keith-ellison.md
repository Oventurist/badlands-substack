# Keith Ellison

Keith Ellison is the Democratic Attorney General of Minnesota and a former member of the United States House of Representatives.

In the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026, Ellison appears in coverage of the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal]]. The Brief reports that whistleblowers, including Minnesota state workers, provided affidavits claiming they had warned Ellison and Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] about fraud in federally funded welfare programs but that no action was taken. Both officials deny the allegations. The claims form part of the Republican case, advanced by [[james-comer|James Comer]], [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] and [[tom-emmer|Tom Emmer]], that Democratic state governments tolerated or facilitated large-scale entitlement fraud.
