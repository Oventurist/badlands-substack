# Maria Lvova-Belova

Maria Lvova-Belova is the Russian Commissioner for Children's Rights and one of two Russian officials named in the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]]'s first arrest warrants arising from the war in Ukraine.[1]

## ICC arrest warrant

In 2023 the Hague-based ICC issued arrest warrants for Lvova-Belova and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] over the alleged unlawful deportation and transfer of children from former Ukrainian territories.[1] Moscow does not recognize the court's jurisdiction, has denied the accusations, and has declared the warrants null and void.[1]

The ICC issued additional warrants a year later against senior Russian military commanders, including former Defense Minister [[sergei-shoigu|Sergey Shoigu]] and General [[valery-gerasimov|Valery Gerasimov]], accusing them of attacking civilian infrastructure in Ukraine.[1]

In April 2025 Italian media reported that the warrant against Putin would not expose him to arrest in Italy, because officials there never completed the procedures required to make it valid.[1]
