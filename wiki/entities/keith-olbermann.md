# Keith Olbermann

Keith Olbermann is an American broadcaster and political commentator known for combative left-leaning commentary and for his heavy use of social media. In late November 2023 he became the subject of conservative media coverage after announcing that he was leaving the platform [[x-corp|X]] in protest of its owner, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

## Departure from X (November 2023)

The trigger for Olbermann's announcement was a joke meme referencing [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]] that Musk posted and subsequently deleted. Olbermann characterized the post as an "endorsement" of Pizzagate and [[qanon|QAnon]], and responded with what RedState described as a "glorious meltdown," declaring that he was officially quitting the platform.[1]

Commentary in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] news brief treated the episode as an example of critics of Musk continuing to post prolifically on X while denouncing it, arguing that the persistence of such users on the platform reflected a craving for attention and affirmation rather than genuine objection.[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the episode to illustrate what he calls "Overton's Goalpost" — the idea that an ideological adversary retreats along predetermined and finite lines when caught in a moral dilemma, with the logical endpoint being convergence of the adversary's own narrative with the truth. In his framing, mainstream commentators eventually describe Trumpism, the "Q Op," and anons accurately as opponents of elite crimes against children, at which point the accusation ceases to be an insult.[1]
