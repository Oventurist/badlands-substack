# Maurice Mitchell

**Maurice Mitchell** is the national head of the [[working-families-party|Working Families Party]] (WFP), the left-of-center party and organizing vehicle that helped anchor the progressive coalition formed against a second [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] administration.[1]

## 2024 election-cycle statements

During the 2024 election cycle, commenting on the prospect of a second Trump term, Mitchell said activists were focused primarily on preventing Trump from winning reelection but would also take actions to respond to a second Trump term.[1] Quoted in reporting cited by Badlands Media, he described the movement's planning mindset: "A lot of folks are in the mind frame of, What can we learn from the past…How can we apply those lessons going forward? And how can we think through the various scenarios that might present themselves, and how might we leverage everything that we have?"[1]

## Context

Mitchell's remarks were cited by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as evidence that the post-election mobilization branded "Resistance 2.0" had been prepared in advance rather than improvised after Trump's victory — part of a broader argument that the political establishment anticipated [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s loss and had contingency plans ready.[1]
