# Roger Alejandro Pinate Martinez

Roger Alejandro Pinate Martinez is a co-founder and president of the voting machine company [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]].[1]

In August 2024, the United States [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] announced that Pinate, aged 49, had been charged along with two other Smartmatic executives with bribing the head of the Philippine election commission to obtain business during the 2016 Philippine election.[1] According to an indictment filed in the Southern District of Florida, between 2015 and 2018 Pinate and [[jorge-miguel-vasquez|Jorge Miguel Vasquez]], aged 62, together with others, allegedly caused at least $1 million in bribes to be paid to [[juan-andres-donato-bautista|Juan Andres Donato Bautista]], the former chairman of the Philippines' Commission on Elections ([[comelec|COMELEC]]).[1] The men allegedly overpaid contracts using a slush fund to conceal the bribes, and prosecutors said the co-conspirators then laundered the proceeds through bank accounts in Asia, Europe and the United States, including in the Southern District of Florida.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the indictment as a vindication of long-standing reporting on Smartmatic, arguing that as "key Smartmatic players get rolled up," US leaders were feigning shock about a history that is "well documented and fully verifiable."[1]
