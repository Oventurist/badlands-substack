# House of Saud

The House of Saud (Al Saud) is the ruling dynasty of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], whose modern head is Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The prefix "Al" in "Al Saud" is a dynastic designation referring to a specific family, distinct from the broader term "Ahl," as in "Ahl al-Bayt" (People of the House of the Prophet).[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Origins

The dynasty traces its roots to the [[banu-bakr|Banu Bakr]] clan and the Hanifa, an ancient tribe predating the Prophet [[muhammad|Muhammad]]; Arab genealogists count the Banu Bakr among the original Christian tribes of Arabia.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The family's home base was the farming settlement of Diriyah on the outskirts of [[riyadh|Riyadh]], in the [[najd|Najd]] region of central Arabia.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Riyadh does not appear on maps of Arabian tribes circa 600 AD because it was not yet a place of significance, its approximate location corresponding to al-Yamamah.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## The first state

The dynasty's founder, [[mohammed-bin-saud|Mohammed bin Saud]], allied with the preacher [[muhammad-ibn-abd-al-wahhab|Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab]] in the [[pact-of-diriyah|Pact of Diriyah]] of 1744, fusing political power with religious reform.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The resulting [[emirate-of-diriyah|Emirate of Diriyah]], or First State of Saud, lasted from 1727 until 1814, when Abdullah — son and successor of Mohammed bin Saud — attempted to seize the [[hashemites|Hashemite]] kingdom of Hejaz, then under [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman]] control since the 16th century.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Abdullah and his kinsmen were captured, taken to Istanbul, and publicly beheaded, with Abdullah's head thrown into the Bosphorus, marking the end of the First State.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Modern era

The article's promised sequel concerns Abdulaziz bin Abdul Rahman Al Saud — founder of the modern Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and grandfather of the current Crown Prince — whose rise the author attributes to [[british-intelligence|British Intelligence]] and a usurpation of Hashemite stewardship over the Islamic holy lands.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Sunni Islam's modern "throne" is thus held by a house that is neither sharif (descendant of Muhammad) nor Banu Hashim.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The article's overall thesis is that the House of Saud is undergoing a redemption arc under MBS.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

Notably, per the terms al-Wahhab set in 1744, no tax is imposed or collected by the Saudi government to this day.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
