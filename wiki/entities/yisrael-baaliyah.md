# Yisrael BaAliyah

Yisrael BaAliyah was an Israeli political party founded by former Soviet dissident [[natan-sharansky|Natan Sharansky]] to represent the interests of Russian immigrants in Israel, specifically the community known as "Refuseniks" — an unofficial term for Soviet Jews.[1]

## 1996 Knesset campaign

The party contested the 1996 [[knesset|Knesset]] elections with a campaign whose polling and strategy were designed by [[ron-dermer|Ron Dermer]], then a student at Oxford and later Israel's ambassador to the United States and minister of strategic affairs.[1] Yisrael BaAliyah was subsequently integrated into [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s [[likud|Likud]] party.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands commentary treated the party's constituency as significant because the Refusenik population traced to the [[pale-of-settlement|Pale of Settlement]], a recurring subject in Badlands writing and streams.[1] On that basis the commentary argued that Dermer's early campaign work tied him to Ukraine and to the criminal networks said by Badlands writers to connect Ukraine and Russia to Israel.[1]
