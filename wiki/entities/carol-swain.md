# Carol Swain

Carol Swain is an award-winning American political scientist and former professor whose scholarship was allegedly plagiarized by [[claudine-gay|Claudine Gay]], the president of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]].[1]

## Response to the Harvard plagiarism allegations

Swain spoke publicly on Tuesday, December 12, 2023, shortly after Harvard's governing board released a full-throated defense of Gay — an expression of support that came a week after Gay's congressional testimony on campus antisemitism and amid allegations that she had plagiarized portions of her dissertation, including from Swain's work.[1]

"I rarely get angry, but I am angry," Swain wrote on X, citing "the racial double standards that are TEMPORARILY giving [Claudine Gay] an opportunity to resign."[1] She added: "White progressives created her and white progressives are protecting her. The rest of us have had to work our rear ends off to achieve success. Some get it handed to them," tagging the incident with hashtags referencing DEI and affirmative action.[1]

In an interview with journalist [[christopher-rufo|Christopher Rufo]], who broke the plagiarism story with Christopher Brunet, Swain said the matter went beyond a few ripped-off paragraphs, contending that Gay's "whole research agenda, her whole career, was based on my work."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the Ivy League's "facade of intellectual superiority" crumbles when the head of such an institution appears incapable of original thought, and that the establishment's decision to protect Gay came at the expense of institutional credibility.[1] The commentary characterized the defense as "a deviation" and "panic," suggesting the episode could expose what it called the bigotry of white leftists, and closed by urging Swain to take the matter to court.[1]
