# Yale Law School

Yale Law School is the law school from which [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] graduated after attending Ohio State University, and where much of the network Badlands commentary associates with him was formed.[1]

At Yale, Vance met his future wife [[usha-vance|Usha]], introduced by their mentor professor [[amy-chua|Amy Chua]], a specialist in international business transactions and globalization.[1] Chua's husband [[jed-rubenfeld|Jed Rubenfeld]] is also a Yale law professor, working in constitutional law and First Amendment rights.[1] It was likewise at Yale, in 2011, that Vance met [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]], who took an immediate liking to him and became his business mentor.[1]

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats the school as the hinge of the Vance story: the point at which personal, judicial, and venture-capital ties that later converged on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] were first established, years before either man's political partnership became public.[1] After graduating, Vance clerked — including for Ninth Circuit judge [[alex-kozinski|Alex Kozinski]] — and worked at the Columbus, Ohio office of [[sidley-austin|Sidley Austin]].[1]
