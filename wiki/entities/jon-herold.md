Jon Herold is the founder of [[badlands-media]] and one of its most frequent on-air hosts.[1] He is described as the host of the Devolution Power Hour, "the show that started it all on the Badlands Media Network," through which he analyzes recent events via [[devolution]] and Continuity of Government theories.[1]

On the network's weekly schedule, Herold appears across several programs. He co-hosts Badlands Daily (Monday–Friday, 10am ET) with [[canncon]] in a traditional news-broadcast format "without the Mainstream Media spin and corporate BS."[1] He is the subject of Baseless Conspiracies (Monday, 10:30pm ET), in which [[zak-paine]] "takes Jon Herold to conspiracy theory school" on a new topic each week.[1] He hosts the Devolution Power Hour on Wednesday and Saturday nights at 10:30pm ET, joined by co-host [[burning-bright]] on Wednesdays.[1] He also co-hosts The Liberty Den (Friday, 10:30pm ET) with The Kate Awakening, a casual week-in-review conversation often featuring a special guest.[1]

## NPR coverage and Epstein document response (November 2025)

Herold was quoted in a November 2025 [[npr|NPR]] article headlined "Right-Wing Media Shrugs Off Latest Epstein Document Release," which described him as a pro-Trump podcaster who gained an audience after the 2020 election by spreading QAnon-adjacent conspiracy theories.[2] On his [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] Rumble livestream that week he said of the newly released [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]] material, "To me, these are nothingburgers. If they're even real."[2]

Responding in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] under the heading "NPR Melts Down Over Badlands Shrugging," Herold reiterated that spending time analyzing the material was "the dumb thing to do," said millions of people online had spent days on "something that's probably fake," and noted it was likely the first time Badlands had ever been mentioned by NPR.[2] He argued the outlet's headline confirmed that Badlands was "the only one not taking the bait" and that critics were "mad we didn't go hook, line, and sinker like literally everybody else on the internet."[2] He rebutted the "QAnon-adjacent" label by referring to a post number that does not exist.[2] Elsewhere in the same brief, reacting to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s endorsement of a congressional vote to release the Epstein files, Herold quipped: "Can whoever removed a chromosome from 'MAGA' last week put it back already?"[2]

## Ireland–CEFC analysis (2023)

For the April 2023 Badlands News Brief, Herold published a documentary analysis of a September 2017 Biden family trip to Ireland, prompted by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s visit to Northern Ireland for the [[good-friday-agreement|Good Friday Agreement]] anniversary.[4] Using flight itineraries, he showed that Joe, [[hunter-biden|Hunter]] and [[jim-biden|Jim Biden]] were all in Ireland from 11 to 17 September 2017, with travel arranged by aide [[kathy-chung|Kathy Chung]] — a detail he tied to oversight activity by Representative [[james-comer|James Comer]].[4]

Herold documented an off-site meeting on 13 September 2017 between the younger Bidens and members of [[cefc-china-energy|CEFC China Energy]] plus trade-group figures, held away from the main venue due to the "sensitivity of the discussion and participants," followed two days later by an email to Jim Biden from Sam Morison outlining two options including "the Rosneft transaction," a reference to [[rosneft|Rosneft]].[4] He closed by noting that the "office mates" email followed four days after the party's return and asking whether Joe Biden had attended the meeting.[4]

## Commentary in the March 2023 brief

Herold contributed two items to the March 9, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief]].[5] On the presentation of an [[international-women-of-courage-award|International Women of Courage Award]] to a biological male, he wrote that the woke agenda was growing increasingly ridiculous and egregious, that a large majority of Americans did not agree with it, and that the more absurd such stories became the greater their chance of waking people up.[5]

On the [[house-foreign-affairs-committee]] testimony of Marine sergeant [[tyler-vargas-andrews]] about the [[abbey-gate-bombing|Abbey Gate bombing]], Herold called it another example of [[joe-biden]]'s stunning incompetence, priced at the lives of thirteen American soldiers, and connected it to [[donald-trump]]'s claim that Biden's incompetence had brought the country to the brink of a third world war.[5]

## Commentary in "Cartels, Crucibles, & Convictions" (August 2025)

Reacting to the New York appellate court's ruling that the $527 million civil fraud penalty against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was excessive, Herold asked when Judge [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]] — whom he called "this gremlin" — would "get held accountable for allowing the excessive fine in the first place."[6] On the Wednesday edition of the Devolution Power Hour he and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] examined newly revealed evidence of [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] corruption, arguing that investigations into the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] were deliberately shut down to protect political elites.[6]

## Commentary in the Badlands News Brief (July 2025)

In the July 3, 2025 edition of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], Herold responded to CIA Director [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]]'s release of a declassified tradecraft review of the post-2016 [[intelligence-community-assessment|Intelligence Community Assessment]] by calling for the release of the classified report referenced within it.[8] He identified that document as the one [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] "collected in July" 2016 and kept highly classified, noting that July 26 was when [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] approved the plan to vilify [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and July 28 was when Brennan first met [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] to discuss "Russian malign influence" and create the inter-agency Fusion Cell.[8]


## Commentary on Antifa and the Kirk assassination (September 2025)

Writing in the Badlands News Brief in September 2025, Herold responded to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated willingness to designate [[antifa|Antifa]] as domestic terrorists by recalling the memo Trump issued on 5 January 2021 ordering an assessment of whether to classify Antifa as a terrorist organization — noting it came the day before what Badlands describes as the January 6 false flag.[9]

Commenting on the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], Herold observed that the intelligence community had updated its definition of "election interference" to include "assassinations" after the 2022 midterms, and that three attempts had followed, concluding "It's almost as if…"[9]

## Commentary on the One Big Beautiful Bill (May 2025)

In the May 22, 2025 Badlands News Brief, Herold responded to reporting on the [[house-rules-committee|House Rules Committee]]'s late-night approval of the [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|One Big Beautiful Bill]] with a single question: "Why should Americans continue to pay taxes?", linking to that day's full episode of The Daily Herold.[10]

## Commentary on the 2016 Obama–Brennan timeline

In the Badlands News Brief of August 5, 2025, Herold reconstructed a three-day sequence in July 2016 — [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]]'s approval of the plan to vilify [[donald-trump|Trump]] on July 26, [[barack-obama|Obama]]'s DNC remark that Trump "cozies up to Putin" on July 27, and [[john-brennan|Brennan]]'s briefing of Obama on the Clinton plan and the creation of the Fusion Cell on July 28 — and argued that Clinton likely approved the plan considerably earlier than the documented date.[11] His central question was why Obama would make the Putin claim publicly before being briefed on the underlying intelligence.[11]

## DOGE savings tracking (December 2025)

Herold tracked the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]]'s published savings figures and noted in December 2025 that the total had been frozen at $214 billion since October 4, observing that DOGE saved $190 billion in its first 160 days but only $24 billion in the 180 days that followed.[12] He published the full running tally back to February 17, when the figure stood at $55 billion.[12]

Commenting on polling for the [[2028-united-states-presidential-election|2028 presidential election]], he reflected on the cost of candor in media: "Saying 'speak the truth' is easy. Living it gets uncomfortable fast," asking what happens when protecting a platform matters more than calling things as they are, and pointed readers to The Daily Herold.[12]

## Commentary, September 2025

Reacting to the failure of White House shutdown talks on 29 September 2025, Herold wrote that he did not care who got blamed for the [[october-2025-government-shutdown|shutdown]] because "It's all political theatre," adding that he would cast blame upon whoever voted to avert it or to end one, and concluding that "All of congress sucks. They're all illegitimate."[13] He also flagged the Department of War's [[pentagon-shutdown-contingency-guidance-2025|contingency guidance]] as the department's continuity of operations plan for the shutdown.[13]

## Commentary on the Texas map ruling

In the December 5, 2025 Badlands News Brief, Herold commented on the Supreme Court's decision permitting Texas to use its Trump-backed congressional map in the 2026 midterms, telling readers to brace themselves because the midterms are coming, and linking to his Daily Herold episode covering 'Bombergait, Signalgate and other gayts.'[14]
