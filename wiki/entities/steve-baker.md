# Steve Baker

**Steve Baker** is an American journalist working for *The Blaze* who was arrested in 2024 on misdemeanor charges arising from his presence at the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021.[1]

## Arrest

Baker was arrested on Friday, March 1, 2024 and charged with four misdemeanors related to his January 6 coverage at the U.S. Capitol.[1] He turned himself over to the [[fbi|FBI]] in Dallas.[1]

The four charges were: knowingly entering or remaining in any restricted building or grounds without lawful authority; disorderly and disruptive conduct in a restricted building or grounds; disorderly conduct in a Capitol building; and parading, demonstrating, or picketing in a Capitol building.[1]

Baker was released from a Texas federal courthouse following his appearance.[1] His arrest was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and circulated widely as an example of the prosecution of journalists who documented the events of January 6.[1]

## Reporting on Capitol Police testimony

In January 2024, Baker reported that newly released U.S. Capitol closed-circuit television clips from January 6, 2021 showed that [[united-states-capitol-police|United States Capitol Police]] Special Agent [[david-lazarus|David Lazarus]] had given false testimony in the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]] seditious-conspiracy trial.[2] Baker had first raised the question in an October 2023 analysis for The Blaze, noting that time-stamped CCTV footage placed Lazarus in other parts of the Capitol complex at the moment he claimed to have witnessed then-Capitol Police officer [[harry-dunn|Harry Dunn]] interacting with Oath Keepers.[2]

Baker and other journalists were permitted to view the January 6 footage only on House subcommittee terminals in Washington, D.C., under limited and restricted conditions.[2] At the time of his October 4 analysis, Blaze News was allowed to publish just one screenshot, because video permissions were put on hold after House Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] was ousted the previous day.[2] By early November, Rep. [[barry-loudermilk|Barry Loudermilk]] released multiple screenshots of Lazarus's movements, telling Baker that "an allegation of a Capitol Police officer lying under oath is very serious and must be fully investigated."[2] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued the episode showed that the real purpose of withholding January 6 footage was to conceal perjury rather than to protect Capitol security.[2]
