# Swiss People's Party

The **Swiss People's Party** (SVP) is a right-wing nationalist political party and the largest group in the lower house of the Swiss parliament.[1] The party holds that neutrality is an integral part of Switzerland's prosperity and has initiated a referendum to embed the principle of neutrality in the Swiss constitution.[1]

## Opposition to the 2024 Ukraine summit

Leading SVP figures argued that Switzerland should not have hosted the June 2024 [[2024-swiss-peace-summit-on-ukraine|Ukraine peace summit]] at the Buergenstock luxury resort without Russia present, contending that the talks damaged Switzerland's traditional neutrality.[1] Nils Fiechter, chief of the SVP's youth wing, delivered a damning verdict on the talks to Russian broadcaster RT on the eve of the conference, saying "the conference will achieve nothing" and calling the whole thing "an absolute farce and an embarrassment for our country."[1] His comments were picked up by Swiss media that Sunday and reported by [[reuters|Reuters]].[1] The summit sparked heated debate over whether Switzerland should abandon neutrality, a position described as deeply rooted in the Swiss psyche.[1]
