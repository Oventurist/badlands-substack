# Democracy Forward

**Democracy Forward** is a left-leaning American legal advocacy organisation active in litigation against Republican administrations and in election-related legal work.[1]

## 2026 midterm planning

The head of Democracy Forward was among the participants in the June 2026 strategy session convened by Senate Minority Leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] to prepare Democratic senators for potential election disruptions ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|midterms]].[1] Other participants included former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], election attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], election-law expert [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]], and the head of [[protect-democracy|Protect Democracy]].[1] The session addressed litigation, communications, and response plans for a set of hypothetical scenarios and sought to boost coordination with state and local election administrators.[1] Critics compared the effort to the cross-institutional coordination of the 2020 [[shadow-cabal|"fortified" election]].[1]

## Microsoft initiative and the "healthy democracy" framing

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote in January 2024 about a tech initiative called Democracy Forward associated with [[microsoft|Microsoft]], citing it as an example of what he called linguistic hypnotism in the vocabulary of "[[danger-to-our-democracy|protecting our democracy]]."[2] At a conference devoted to digital campaign security, an individual affiliated with the project named Ethan Chumley described its work as "supporting the institutions we think are fundamental to a healthy democracy" — a phrasing DeLarme emphasized as revealing who decides which institutions qualify.[2] He reported that the initiative had also partnered with the media rating service [[newsguard|NewsGuard]], and grouped it with the funder-backed nonprofit [[defending-digital-campaigns|Defending Digital Campaigns]].[2]
