# Democracy Forward

Democracy Forward is a legal advocacy organisation, described in reporting cited by the Badlands corpus as "left-leaning," that litigates against federal executive actions and participates in election-related legal work.

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the head of Democracy Forward was among the experts convened by Senate Democratic leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] — alongside former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], the election attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], the election-law expert [[norman-eisen|Norman Eisen]], and the head of [[protect-democracy|Protect Democracy]] — to help Senate Democrats prepare litigation, communications, and response plans for possible election disruptions during the 2026 midterms, and to improve coordination with state and local election administrators. Badlands commentary treats the group's participation as part of a renewed "shadow cabal" modelled on the network described in [[time-magazine|Time]]'s 2021 account of the 2020 election.
