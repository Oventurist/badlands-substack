# Dusty Johnson

Dusty Johnson is a Republican member of the [[us-house-of-representatives|U.S. House of Representatives]] who commented publicly on his conference's handling of the November 2023 government funding deadline.[1]

## November 2023 stopgap funding

After House Republicans met privately on November 7, 2023 to weigh options for a [[continuing-resolution|continuing resolution]] ahead of the November 17 funding expiration, Johnson predicted that Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] would settle the matter for the conference: "He is going to make a play call, and I would expect that the overwhelming majority of the conference would back his play."[1] The comment reflected an expectation among members that the Speaker would unveil a stopgap measure within days, ensuring a floor vote early the following week.[1] Republicans said they feared being forced to back a rival funding measure originating in the Democratic-led [[us-senate|Senate]].[1]
