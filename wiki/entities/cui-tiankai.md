# Cui Tiankai

Cui Tiankai is a Chinese diplomat who served as the People's Republic of China's ambassador to the United States during the presidency of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and who acted as one of Beijing's principal interlocutors in arranging Trump's November 2017 state visit to China.[1]

## Role in the 2017 Trump visit to Beijing

Ahead of Trump's arrival in Beijing, Cui promised that the trip would amount to a "state visit-plus," a formulation quoted by CNN and reproduced in Badlands Media's reporting on the visit.[1] The centerpiece of that treatment was an official dinner inside the [[forbidden-city|Forbidden City]] — an honor CNN described as not having been granted to any U.S. president since the founding of the People's Republic of China.[1] Trump and [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] toured the palace complex with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and his wife Peng Liyuan, and one observer quoted in the coverage said Xi was "going to treat Trump almost like an emperor."[1]

## Currency manipulation and the search for a channel

In remarks reported by NPR and cited in the Badlands piece, Cui characterized the currency-manipulation dispute as a matter of apprising Trump of what he called the widely accepted fact that China was propping up the value of its currency rather than depressing it.[1] Cui also said that one of Beijing's tasks was to "find a channel through which to communicate with Trump," and identified [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] as that channel — Kushner having helped arrange the April 2017 summit between Xi and Trump at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]].[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands Media commentary

Joe Lange's article "The Master Builder" treats Cui's stated need for a private communications channel as a significant data point, arguing that the two leaders' inability to speak openly suggested a desire to evade surveillance by the American [[deep-state|Deep State]], by factions within the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], or by both.[1] In that reading, Cui's diplomacy formed part of the groundwork for an alleged coalition between Trump, Xi and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] aimed at dismantling the global central-bank debt system.[1]

## Call for renewed US–China cooperation

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites Cui, China's former Ambassador to Washington, as having acknowledged the threat of a world war between China and the United States while taking the high road and calling for reasonable dialogue: "China and the USA need to recapture the spirit of cooperation from WWII and join hands to confront our common enemies in the new era."[2] In the same speech Cui echoed [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s references to [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]] by quoting the martyred president's line that "the best way to predict the future is to create it."[2]
