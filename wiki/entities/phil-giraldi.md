# Phil Giraldi

Phil Giraldi is a former CIA and U.S. Army intelligence officer who appears frequently as a commentator on Middle East affairs.[1]

In September 2024, Giraldi spoke with Judge Andrew Napolitano and postulated that the previous week's pager attack against [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] had potentially been intended to occur at the onset of an Israeli ground invasion of southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]], but that concern Hezbollah had become wise to the scheme led the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] to detonate the devices before the invasion was ready.[1]
