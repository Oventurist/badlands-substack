# China Media Project

The China Media Project is a research organisation cited in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" for its reporting on party recruitment practices under [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1]

## Cited findings

According to the China Media Project as quoted in the article, the recruitment of new members into the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] now involves much stricter loyalty parameters, including official pledges and oaths to "The Peoples China."[1] The article places this alongside the transfer of judicial appointments away from local councils and the expansion of the [[central-commission-for-discipline-inspection|Discipline Commission]], presenting the tightened oath requirement as one element in a broad reordering of the party's internal loyalty structures.[1]
