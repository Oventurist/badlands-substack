# Herschel Walker

Herschel Walker is a Republican political figure who was named among the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration nominees awaiting Senate confirmation in September 2025.[1] He appeared on the list of 108 executive-branch nominees that the Republican-controlled [[united-states-senate|Senate]] moved to confirm in a single large batch by early October of that year, a process enabled by a rule change pushed through by Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]].[1]
