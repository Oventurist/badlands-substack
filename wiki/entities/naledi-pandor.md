# Naledi Pandor

Naledi Pandor is the South African Foreign Minister who served as a principal spokesperson for the host government during the [[2023-brics-johannesburg-summit|2023 BRICS summit]] in Johannesburg.[1]

## Announcement of BRICS expansion

On Wednesday, August 23, 2023, Pandor said that [[brics|BRICS]] leaders had "agreed on the matter of expansion" and that a detailed announcement would be made before the summit's close on Thursday.[1] Speaking to the state-run Ubuntu Radio, she said: "We have a document that we have adopted that sets out guidelines and principles, processes, for considering countries that wish to become members of Brics."[1]

The expansion question had dominated the three-day Johannesburg agenda and exposed rifts between members, with China seeking rapid growth of the bloc amid rising competition with the United States while India remained wary of its geopolitical rival's intentions.[1] South African officials said nearly two dozen countries had formally applied to join the group, which accounts for 40 per cent of the world's population and a quarter of the global economy.[1]

## Announcement of BRICS enlargement

On Wednesday, January 31, 2024, as South Africa's foreign minister, Pandor said that Egypt, Ethiopia, [[iran|Iran]], Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates had confirmed they were joining the [[brics|BRICS]] bloc after receiving invitations the previous year.[2] The five had been invited alongside Argentina at the August 2023 summit in Johannesburg; Argentina subsequently declined.[2]
