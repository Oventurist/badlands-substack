# Heinrich Svensmark

Heinrich Svensmark is a Danish physicist whose research on cosmic radiation and cloud formation is cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as key evidence for [[astroclimatology]].[1] According to Ehret's June 5, 2025 Badlands Media essay, Svensmark and his team in Denmark found strong correlations linking cloud formation, climate and cosmic radiation flux over time.[1]

Cosmic radiation flux into the earth is described as a continuous process mediated by the earth's magnetic field as well as by the oscillating magnetic field of the sun, which shapes the entire solar system as it revolves around the galactic center of the Milky Way every 225–250 million years.[1] Svensmark's discovery was outlined in the 2011 documentary The Cloud Mystery, which Ehret recommends to readers as an accessible presentation of the cosmic-ray hypothesis.[1] In the article's framing, this line of research supports the argument that climate is driven by galactic and solar forces rather than by human [[carbon-dioxide|carbon dioxide]] emissions.[1]
