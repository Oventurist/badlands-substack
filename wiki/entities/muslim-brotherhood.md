# Muslim Brotherhood

The Muslim Brotherhood — specifically the Syrian Muslim Brotherhood — is named in the article as an intermediary in the [[cia|CIA]]-directed smuggling of weapons into Syria.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

Citing the World Socialist Web Site's summary of a June 2012 New York Times report, the article states that CIA operatives deployed to [[turkey|Turkey]] directed a massive smuggling operation in which automatic rifles, rocket-propelled grenades, ammunition and some antitank weapons were funneled mostly across the Turkish border by way of "a shadowy network of intermediaries, including the Syrian Muslim Brotherhood," paid for by [[qatar|Qatar]], Saudi Arabia and Turkey.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The article closes by highlighting this detail — that the CIA's partner in Turkey for arming [[isis|ISIS]] was the Muslim Brotherhood — and links it to its recurring question about why [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] staffed his administration with figures the author describes as hostile to America, promising to address it in a subsequent piece.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

## The release of Abdelhakim Belhaj

"A Conflict of Interest" places the Brotherhood at the centre of the 2009 release of [[abdelhakim-belhaj|Abdelhakim Belhaj]] from Libyan custody: with the help of the Muslim Brotherhood preacher Ali Sallabi, Belhaj negotiated his liberation with [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]]'s son Saïf al-Islam.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) The article treats Sallabi's involvement as an important data point, arguing that the newly inaugurated Obama administration had asked Gaddafi to release Belhaj and other members of the [[libyan-islamic-fighting-group|Libyan Islamic Fighting Group]], and that Gaddafi complied to preserve goodwill with the incoming president.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest) It asserts that Obama has strong ties to the Muslim Brotherhood and promises to present that evidence in later articles.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-conflict-of-interest)

## Infiltration through Huma Abedin

Part 2 of the America for Sale series treats the Brotherhood as the organisation that "infiltrated" the U.S. government through [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], quoting Q drop 71 of November 4, 2017, which asked who connects HRC and the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] to Saudi Arabia, who the Muslim Brotherhood is, and who has ties to it.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Abedin is described as deeply connected to the Brotherhood and as having remained assistant editor of the Brotherhood journal her parents helped found in Jeddah throughout her service at the [[state-department|State Department]].[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Through her, the article argues, the Brotherhood was connected to everything she touched — the Clinton Foundation, [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]] and Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s office.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The author repeats the formulation that "it was an infiltration not an invasion," attributing the Brotherhood's access to the approval of [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]] after he installed [[barack-obama|Obama]] as president.[[3]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Jordanian ban (2025)

On Wednesday, April 23, 2025, [[jordan|Jordan]]'s Interior Ministry announced a sweeping set of measures against the Muslim Brotherhood, formally declaring the group dissolved and illegal.[4] Interior Minister [[mazen-faraya|Mazen Faraya]] said membership in or affiliation with the group was prohibited by law, that all of its offices across the Kingdom had been permanently closed under a judicial order, and that security forces had been deployed to enforce the decision and confiscate the organization's assets.[4] He described the group's continued activities as a threat to citizens, a barrier to national development, and a destabilizing force, alleging that members had planned to target sensitive sites, stored weapons and explosives in residential neighborhoods, and operated covertly to undermine public security.[4]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the ban came the same day [[king-abdullah-ii|King Abdullah II]] met Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], and framed the Brotherhood as an instrument of Western intelligence — quipping that Jordan had banned "MI6 — er, I mean the Muslim Brotherhood" — and casting the move as part of the [[cia|CIA]] and [[mi6|MI6]] being pushed out of the Middle East and Africa.[4]

## Pressure for a U.S. terrorist designation (2025)

In June 2025 reports spread that the Trump administration was preparing to designate the Muslim Brotherhood as a terrorist organization, but ten months into Trump's second term no designation had been issued.[7] The [[white-house|White House]] was reported to still be searching for a way to brand the Brotherhood a terrorist entity under a foreign terrorist designation, while saying it was coming soon.[7] The Brotherhood is already counted as a terrorist group in several countries, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Bahrain, and Russia, and is described as backing violent extremist forces across the Middle East and beyond — including Hamas, which was spun off from a Muslim Brotherhood affiliate in the 1980s.[7]

## Alleged British intelligence origins

Badlands commentary holds that "the Muslim Brotherhood is a creation of British Intelligence."[7] On this account the modern Brotherhood was created in 1928 in Cairo by [[hassan-al-banna|Hassan al-Banna]], a graduate of a Salafi Islamic school established by the British government in the 1880s under [[evelyn-baring|Evelyn Baring]], British Lord and Comptroller General of Egypt.[7] Al-Banna is said to have studied under [[rashid-rida|Rida Rashid]], a cleric central to the proliferation of Salafi Islam who was drawn from obscurity by British Intelligence and installed as head imam of the British-funded Cairo school, with his students going on to found the Brotherhood.[7]

## Proposed US terrorist designation (2025)

On August 12, 2025, Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] said the United States was "in the process" of designating the Muslim Brotherhood a terrorist organization, telling radio host Sid Rosenberg that such a step, along with possible action against the [[council-on-american-islamic-relations|Council on American-Islamic Relations]], was "in the works."[9] Rosenberg had raised the support he said both organizations were giving to New York mayoral candidate [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]].[9] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry responded that the Brotherhood should be understood as an arm of [[mi6|MI6]], and recalled that the previous congressional designation push in 2017 prompted the recruitment of former [[cia|CIA]] officer [[kevin-chalker|Kevin Chalker]] and his company Global Risk Strategies to run a psychological warfare campaign, [[operation-endgame|Operation Endgame]], to subvert the effort.[9] That project, revealed in documents leaked in January 2024, targeted UAE Ambassador to the United States [[youssef-al-otaiba|Yousef Al Otaiba]], likely for his role brokering ties between [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] and Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] and for advancing the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]].[9] Al Otaiba had published an op-ed in Israeli newspapers in the summer of 2020 warning that factions within Israel sought to derail the Accords and expand settlements in Palestine, a step he said would foreclose Arab-Israeli peace.[9] The Badlands commentary argued it was "past time" to examine what roles figures such as [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], [[john-mccain|John McCain]], and [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] played in cultivating the spread of radical Islamic terrorism.[9]

## Media assets and identification methodology

In a June 2024 Badlands News Brief, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that he and Matthew Ehret had established on Breaking History that the modern Muslim Brotherhood was created by British Intelligence in Cairo in 1928 to act as its proxy, and that the Brotherhood uses media assets to exert public influence — a common intelligence tradecraft practice documented by the U.S. government.[8] He set out a checklist he uses to assess whether a journalist may be Brotherhood-connected: affiliation with "Middle East" outlets based in Qatar, London, or the United States, such as [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]] — accused by critics of being a Brotherhood front and banned by Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Egypt and the UAE in 2017 — or [[middle-east-eye|Middle East Eye]], listed as a "Qatar/Muslim Brotherhood media asset" in a leaked document prepared for the Brotherhood by former CIA officer Kevin Chalkner, who had allegedly been hired to run Operation Endgame against Senator [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]] to thwart his efforts to designate the Brotherhood a terrorist organization.[8] Additional indicators included support for the 2011 Arab Spring uprisings, characterized as Brotherhood-coordinated color revolutions designed to overthrow Middle Eastern governments, and vehement endorsement of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s findings on the death of Jamal Khashoggi.[8]

Applying the checklist, he concluded that [[palestine-chronicle|Palestine Chronicle]] founder [[ramzy-baroud|Ramzy Baroud]] and contributor [[iqbal-jassat|Iqbal Jassat]] met every criterion.[8] The account described Baroud as a vocal supporter of Brotherhood operative Mohamed Morsi, who led the 2011 revolution overthrowing the Egyptian government; a counter-military coup led by General Abdel Fattah el-Sisi followed in 2013, Morsi was jailed, and he died in the courtroom during trial in 2019.[8]

## Arab Spring, Sharia and "civilization jihad"

Badlands contributor Joe Lange identifies the Muslim Brotherhood as the "common thread" linking the leaders toppled or targeted during the [[arab-spring|Arab Spring]] — Zine El Abidine Ben Ali of Tunisia, [[muammar-gaddafi|Muammar Gaddafi]] of Libya, [[hosni-mubarak|Hosni Mubarak]] of Egypt, Ali Abdullah Saleh of Yemen, [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] of Syria and the rulers of Bahrain — arguing that each was suppressing the Brotherhood in his own country and thereby standing in the way of the [[sixteen-year-plan|sixteen year plan]].[10] The Brotherhood is described as a movement seeking to establish [[sharia-law|Sharia law]] under a global caliphate.[10]

In Egypt, the overthrow of Mubarak led to an election, promoted by U.S. government-financed NGOs, that installed Brotherhood leader [[mohamed-morsi|Mohamed Morsi]]; he placed Brotherhood allies in powerful positions and began implementing Sharia law before millions of Egyptians took to the streets and the military removed him, after which the Brotherhood was outlawed again and its leaders arrested.[10] Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] made a trip to Egypt after the military takeover, in part to extract [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] members who were facing arrest and sheltering in the American embassy, and insisted on calling the June 30 Revolution a "military coup," a stance Egyptians saw as aligning U.S. leadership with the Brotherhood.[10] [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]] functioned, in this account, as the Brotherhood's international mouthpiece, prompting resignations by several of its correspondents.[10]

Citing the Center for Security Policy and Breitbart, the article describes the Brotherhood's doctrine of [[civilization-stealth-jihad|civilization jihad]] — a pre-violent, "stealth" phase of jihad consisting of cultural subversion, co-opting of senior leaders, influence operations and propaganda intended to insinuate Sharia gradually into Western societies.[10] The Brotherhood's *Explanatory Memorandum* describes the Islamic Movement in the United States as a "settlement process" leading to a "grand jihad" under Brotherhood direction.[10] Lange applies the Q formulation "infiltration, not invasion" to this program and points to Representative [[michele-bachmann|Michele Bachmann]]'s 2012 letters warning inspectors general about Brotherhood infiltration of the U.S. government, which named [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] and drew sharp criticism from McCain.[10] He also states that the Brotherhood helped the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] smuggle shoulder-fired missiles into Syria with the help of Turkey.[10]
