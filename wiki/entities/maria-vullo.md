# Maria Vullo

Maria Vullo is a former superintendent of the New York Department of Financial Services (DFS) and the named defendant in the Supreme Court case [[national-rifle-association-of-america-v-vullo|National Rifle Association of America v. Vullo]].[1]

The [[national-rifle-association|National Rifle Association]] alleged that Vullo violated the First Amendment by coercing DFS-regulated insurance companies and banks into cutting ties with the organization, in an effort to punish or suppress its gun-rights advocacy.[1] The [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] ruled unanimously against that conduct, holding through Justice [[sonia-sotomayor|Sonia Sotomayor]] that government officials cannot attempt to coerce private parties in order to punish or suppress disfavored views.[1] The decision was later invoked in debate over whether [[federal-communications-commission|FCC]] pressure on broadcasters could be unconstitutional.[1]
