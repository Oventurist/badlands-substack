# David DePape

David DePape is the defendant in the San Francisco criminal case arising from the October 2022 assault on [[paul-pelosi|Paul Pelosi]], husband of former U.S. House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], at the couple's San Francisco home.[1]

## Release of body camera footage

In late January 2023, the San Francisco Superior Court announced that police body camera video showing the attack would be released to the media. A statement from the court said the video and audio in the case of *The People v. David DePape* would be made "available to accredited media members" on Friday morning at 9 a.m., in accordance with an order issued Wednesday by Judge Stephen M. Murphy.[1]

Media representatives were required to collect CDs containing the footage and audio recordings from clerks at the Hall of Justice, on presentation of identification, a completed criminal records request form, and a $25 payment.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] noted that many at Badlands had been calling for the release of the first responder's body cam footage from what he termed the Paul Pelosi "attacker" fiasco, and urged readers to temper their expectations, questioning whether the footage would prove authentic or doctored and suggesting that officials who had held the material for months would be unlikely to release it if it were genuinely damning.[1]
