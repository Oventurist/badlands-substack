# Way of the Future

**Way of the Future** was a non-profit religious organisation founded in September 2015 by [[anthony-levandowski|Anthony Levandowski]], the engineer previously known for work on driverless cars at Uber.[1] Its existence was reported by *Wired* and subsequently covered by the *Independent* on 26 October 2017.[1]

## Mission

The organisation's stated mission was "to develop and promote the realization of a Godhead based on artificial intelligence and through understanding and worship of the Godhead contribute to the betterment of society."[1]

## Reception

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] spoke out against the creation of an AI god that humans could worship, saying its founder should not be allowed to develop digital superintelligence.[1] Commentary surrounding the group raised the prospect of an AI deity emerging by 2042 and writing its own scripture, with experts quoted predicting that AI would surpass humans at all tasks within 45 years.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] presented the organisation as evidence that the push toward [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] carries a religious and potentially nefarious dimension.[1]
