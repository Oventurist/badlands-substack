# Sheldon Whitehouse

Sheldon Whitehouse is a Democratic United States Senator from Rhode Island and a member of the [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]], where he initiated the congressional investigation into [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]]'s Nazi-era accounts.[1] Badlands commentary describes him as the senator who "viciously excoriated" President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Cabinet nominations, making his role in launching an inquiry that Badlands writers view as favorable to a "White Hat plan" an irony worth noting.[1]

## Credit Suisse investigation

Whitehouse opened the inquiry to determine whether Credit Suisse had helped Nazi SS officers flee Germany for [[argentina|Argentina]] by providing discreet banking services.[1] He was first put up to the task in March 2020 by the Simon Wiesenthal Center, a Jewish human rights organization named for the famous Nazi hunter.[1] When Credit Suisse fired independent ombudsman [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]] in December 2022, Whitehouse and committee co-chair [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] — who had heard the dismissal was an attempt to conceal the scandal's depth — intervened and demanded Barofsky's reinstatement, which [[ubs|UBS]] carried out after acquiring the bank in 2023.[1]

The resulting probe, announced by Grassley, identified 890 previously undisclosed accounts with potential Nazi links.[1] Badlands framing places Whitehouse's inquiry alongside [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]'s 2018 RICO lawsuit as converging lines of investigation into the Swiss banking system.[1]
