# Sheldon Whitehouse

Sheldon Whitehouse is a Democratic United States Senator identified in the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026 as the originator of the congressional investigation into Nazi-era accounts at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].

## Role in the Credit Suisse inquiry

According to the commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], Whitehouse opened the inquiry to determine whether Credit Suisse had helped Nazi SS officers flee Germany and escape to [[argentina|Argentina]] by providing banking services with extreme discretion. He was first put up to the task in March 2020 by the [[simon-wiesenthal-center|Simon Wiesenthal Center]], a Jewish human rights organization named for the Nazi hunter Simon Wiesenthal.

When Credit Suisse fired its independent ombudsman [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]] in December 2022, Whitehouse and his committee co-chair [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] concluded the dismissal was likely an attempt to conceal the depth of the scandal. They intervened and demanded Barofsky's reinstatement, which was carried out by [[ubs|UBS]] after its 2023 acquisition of Credit Suisse. The resulting probe produced the February 2026 disclosure of 890 previously unknown accounts tied to the Nazi apparatus.

## Characterization in the source

The Badlands commentary is hostile in tone toward Whitehouse personally, describing him as "the insufferable prick who viciously excoriated all of President Trump's Cabinet nominations last year," while simultaneously crediting his investigation as one of the "multiple lines of inquiry coinciding in strange and wonderful ways" that the author sees converging on the Swiss banking system.
