# Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI)

The **Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI)** is a UN-connected body concerned with certifying the responsible sourcing of minerals used in electronics and defense manufacturing.[1]

## Allegations of fraudulent certification

A report commissioned by the government of the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] and published by the law firm [[amsterdam-and-partners|Amsterdam & Partners]] accused technology companies of using organizations such as RMI and the [[international-tin-supply-chain-initiative|International Tin Supply Chain Initiative]] to legitimize illicit mineral supply chains with fraudulent certifications.[1]

The underlying accusation is that firms including [[apple|Apple]], Intel, Sony, and Lockheed Martin illegally smuggled rare earth minerals out of the Congo into neighbouring states — chiefly [[rwanda|Rwanda]] — so they could re-enter the global supply chain labelled "clean," i.e. free of child slave labour.[1] The report also named individuals such as Vincent Bolloré, arrested in 2018 and charged with fraud connected to African mining operations.[1]

Two months before late February 2025, Amsterdam & Partners filed suit against Apple on behalf of the DRC government.[1]
