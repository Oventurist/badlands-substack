# Alan Watts

**Alan Watts** is a writer on philosophy and consciousness quoted in the April 12, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "Transcending Ego: Meeting the Mirror at the Threshold," by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites his line, "The ego is like a radar on a ship, a useful tool. But we confuse it for the whole ship."[1] It appears in the section describing the ego as a guardian turned gatekeeper, immediately after a quotation from [[gabor-mate|Gabor Maté]] that "the ego is not who we are. The ego is who we think we are."[1] The two quotations together support the essay's account of the ego as a survival scaffolding built in childhood, useful for orientation but hardening over time into something that confines, filters out threats to its own architecture, and clings to titles, opinions and familiar stories to preserve an illusion of control.[1]

The author draws the Watts image out explicitly, writing that "this confusion becomes a cage. But for every cage, there is a key," and concluding that the ego is not to be destroyed but "evolved, transcended. Reassigned. Integrated" — the position developed at length under [[ego-transcendence|ego transcendence]].[1] The article carries the standard Badlands Media disclaimer that features represent the opinions of contributing authors rather than the outlet itself.[1]
