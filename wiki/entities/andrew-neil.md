# Andrew Neil

Andrew Neil is a British journalist and political commentator whose columns appear in the Daily Mail, where he writes on American as well as British politics.[1]

## Commentary on the 2024 Democratic crisis

In a mid-July 2024 Daily Mail column, Neil argued sardonically that the leadership of the Democratic Party had "either been infected by a death wish — or infiltrated by agents of President [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] with a mission to ensure [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s victory in November."[1] His reasoning turned on the party's handling of the biggest issue in American politics that summer: [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s age and his fitness to serve a second term, a question on which he said 80 percent of Americans had already concluded the president was unfit.[1]

Neil's central point was one of political self-harm. By refusing to reach a firm conclusion over whether Biden should stay in or leave the race, Democrats guaranteed that the fitness question remained "front and centre of every media outlet in every news cycle," and in doing so, he wrote, "the Democrats are doing Trump's work for him."[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] highlighted the column in its news brief as evidence that the corporate press was belatedly recognising a dynamic the Truth Community had already described. Writing under the handle [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], the commentator joked that the allegation Trump had colluded with Putin was "likely... true," though not for the reasons mainstream outlets claimed, and framed Biden's continued candidacy as functionally serving Trump's interests.[1]
