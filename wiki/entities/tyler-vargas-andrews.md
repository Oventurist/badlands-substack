# Tyler Vargas-Andrews

Tyler Vargas-Andrews is a sergeant in the United States Marine Corps who was deployed to Kabul during the 2021 evacuation of Afghanistan and was severely wounded in the August 26, 2021 suicide bombing at the airport that killed thirteen American service members.[1]

## Testimony before Congress

Vargas-Andrews testified in March 2023 before the [[house-foreign-affairs-committee]] at its first public hearing on the withdrawal operation. He told lawmakers that his sniper team had identified the suspected bomber and had an opportunity to kill him, but was not granted authority to act by military leadership.[1]

He stated that his team had been provided detailed information about the suspected bomber, including a physical description, and recounted asking intelligence personnel why the man had not been apprehended sooner. "I was told the asset could not be compromised," he testified.[1] Vargas-Andrews also described how the [[state-department]] under the Biden administration turned Afghans away from the airport, in his words "condemning them to death" at the hands of the [[taliban]].[1]

## Reception

Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief]] treated the testimony as evidence of the incompetence of the [[joe-biden]] administration, arguing that the cost of that incompetence was measured in the lives of thirteen American soldiers and connecting the episode to Donald Trump's recurring claim that Biden's handling of foreign policy had brought the United States to the brink of a wider war.[1]
