# Diosdado Cabello

Diosdado Cabello is the Venezuelan Interior Minister and a senior figure in the governing apparatus that survived the removal of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] in January 2026. He remains under United States sanctions and indictment.

The January 19, 2026 [[badlands-brief-02a|Badlands Brief]] reports, on the basis of sources cited by Reuters, that U.S. officials engaged in months of covert discussions with Cabello both before and after the January 3, 2026 American military operation that captured Maduro. In those talks, American personnel warned him to refrain from using security forces or militias against the political opposition.

[[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] connects this warning to a contemporaneous argument by [[erik-prince|Erik Prince]] on Steve Bannon's programme that the United States should avoid repeating its Iraq-era mistakes and should instead allow acting President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] to contract directly with private military contractors for security. Cabello's continued prominence is cited in the Brief's framing of the post-Maduro government as substantially intact.
