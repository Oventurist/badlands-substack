# Diosdado Cabello

Diosdado Cabello is the Venezuelan Interior Minister who was the counterparty to months of covert discussions with U.S. officials before and after the January 3, 2026 American military operation that captured [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

In those talks, U.S. personnel warned Cabello — who remains under U.S. sanctions and indictment — to refrain from using security forces or militias against the political opposition.[1] Badlands commentary connected this warning to [[erik-prince|Erik Prince]]'s public argument that acting president [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] should be allowed to contract directly with private military contractors to provide security, rather than repeating the Iraq disbandment model.[1]

## Announcement of the 2024 assassination plot arrests

On state television in September 2024, Cabello — described as Venezuela's powerful interior minister — announced the arrest of three Americans, two Spaniards and a Czech citizen accused of entering the country to assassinate President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[2] He said the foreign citizens were part of a CIA-led plot to overthrow the Venezuelan government and kill several members of its leadership, and displayed images of rifles he said had been confiscated from the plotters.[2] Cabello identified one detained American as Navy SEAL Wilbert Joseph Castañeda Gomez, who he said had served in Afghanistan, Iraq and Colombia.[2] The [[state-department|US State Department]] confirmed the detention of a US military member while calling any claims of US involvement in a plot against Maduro "categorically false."[2]
