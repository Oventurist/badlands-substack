# William McKinley

William McKinley was a President of the United States who implemented his own tariff program and was assassinated.[1]

## Invocation in the 2025 tariff debate

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited a McKinley quotation on the eve of Supreme Court oral arguments over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariffs, framing McKinley as a president who pursued a tariff program and "was assassinated for it," and casting the case as an attempt by the [[deep-state|Deep State]] to stop a tariff policy that its supporters argue would eliminate the need for an income tax and free the American economy from globalism.[1]
