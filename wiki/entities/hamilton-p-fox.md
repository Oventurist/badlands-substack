# Hamilton P. Fox III

Hamilton P. "Phil" Fox is the chief of the [[office-of-disciplinary-counsel|Office of Disciplinary Counsel]], the body that handles attorney discipline in Washington, D.C.[1]

## Role in the Ed Martin matter

In May 2025, Democratic lawmakers and advocacy groups lodged complaints with Fox against Justice Department official [[ed-martin|Ed Martin]], alleging that Martin had used the threat of prosecution to intimidate and chill the speech of perceived adversaries of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and adviser [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], and seeking suspension of his law license.[1] Martin publicly disclosed the resulting ethics investigation, case number 2025-D047, and claimed his confidentiality had been violated.[1]

Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] described Fox as "the Fox at the ABA's Disciplinary Action Board" going after Martin's law license, and noted that the same official had previously pursued [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]] and a number of other lawyers who brought election-related cases.[1]
