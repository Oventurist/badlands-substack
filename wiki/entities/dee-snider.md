# Dee Snider

Dee Snider is the front man of the American hard rock band Twisted Sister, best known for the 1984 anthem "We're Not Gonna Take It."[1]

## San Francisco Pride cancellation (2023)

In May 2023, Snider's scheduled performance at San Francisco Pride was cancelled after he voiced support for fellow rock musician [[paul-stanley|Paul Stanley]]'s rebuke of allowing children to undergo gender transitions.[1] The organization behind the event announced that Twisted Sister's planned performance of "We're Not Gonna Take It" was being pulled because of Snider's recent political stance, having originally intended the song to serve as the "unofficial rallying cry" of its June 2023 pride celebration.[1]

Commentary in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief observed that neither Snider nor Stanley had been champions of conservative causes — both had strongly promoted mask-wearing and vaccination — but that both commanded large platforms and had recently spoken out against drag shows aimed at children and what the commentary called the trans agenda being pushed on minors.[1] The writer suggested that Snider, now on the receiving end of cancellation, might "sing a different tune."[1]
