# Farhad Azima

Farhad Azima is an Iranian expatriate businessman, owner of the air charter company [[global-international-air|Global International Air]] and the fourth largest stockholder in [[indian-springs-state-bank|Indian Springs State Bank]].[1]

Indian Springs made several unsecured loans to Global International totalling $600,000, exceeding the bank's $349,000 borrower limit; Global International filed for bankruptcy in 1983 and the bank followed in 1984.[1] Global International Air formed part of [[oliver-north|Oliver North]]'s logistical network shipping arms for the U.S. government, and Race Aviation, another Azima company, shipped 23 tons of TOW missiles to Iran.[1]

Azima's relationship with the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] dates to the late 1970s, when he supplied air and logistical support to [[eatsco|EATSCO]], the Egyptian American Transport and Services Corporation owned by former CIA agents [[thomas-clines|Thomas Clines]], [[ted-shackley|Theodore Shackley]] and [[richard-secord|Richard Secord]], a firm prominently involved in the activities of [[edwin-wilson|Edwin Wilson]].[1] The assistant U.S. Attorney investigating the Indian Springs collapse was told to back off a key figure because of his CIA ties.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] concludes that Azima was a CIA operative and that his air transport company was controlled by the agency.[1]
