# Brian Sicknick

Brian Sicknick was a United States Capitol Police officer whose death following January 6, 2021 became one of the central emotional and political anchors of the official account of that day. Early reporting asserted that Sicknick had been murdered by protesters, a claim repeated in political rhetoric and in the framing adopted by the [[january-6-committee|January 6 committee]].[1]

## The Carlson footage claim

During his March 2023 broadcasts drawing on the approximately 42,000 hours of Capitol surveillance video, [[tucker-carlson]] presented video that he said showed Sicknick healthy and walking around inside the Capitol after the point at which he was alleged to have been fatally assaulted.[1] Carlson placed the Sicknick material among a group of claims — alongside those concerning [[jacob-chansley]] and [[ray-epps]] — that he argued demonstrated the committee's narratives to be "vicious lies."[1]

The dispute over Sicknick's death was described in the [[badlands-news-brief]] as a key reason the full footage archive should be released publicly, so that independent researchers rather than a single network could evaluate the primary evidence.[1] The broadcast prompted a rapid political response, with Senate leaders [[chuck-schumer]] and [[mitch-mcconnell]] both condemning Carlson and [[fox-news]] for airing the video.[1]
