# Liberty Strategic Capital

Liberty Strategic Capital is the private investment firm led by former U.S. Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]].[1]

## TikTok bid

In March 2024, Mnuchin disclosed on CNBC's "Squawk Box" that he was assembling an investor group to acquire [[tiktok]] from its Chinese parent [[bytedance|ByteDance]], stating: "I think the legislation should pass and I think it should be sold ... It's a great business and I'm going to put together a group to buy TikTok."[1] The announcement followed House passage of a bipartisan divest-or-ban bill that would force ByteDance to sell the app or see it barred in the United States.[1]

Reporting noted overlapping ownership interests between Liberty and ByteDance: Masayoshi Son's [[softbank|SoftBank]] Vision Fund invested in ByteDance in 2018 and is also a limited partner in Liberty Strategic Capital.[1] Badlands commentator [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]], citing independent journalist [[ian-carroll|Ian Carroll]], questioned whose interests the bid ultimately served.[1]
