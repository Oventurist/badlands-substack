# Revolver News

Revolver News is an American online publication associated with [[darren-beattie|Darren Beattie]] that Badlands Media credits with introducing the [[color-revolution|color revolution]] framework into mainstream populist political analysis in the United States.[1]

## Role in exposing Norm Eisen

In September 2020 Revolver News published an article titled "Meet Norm Eisen: Legal Hatchet Man and Central Operative in the 'Color Revolution' Against President Trump."[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that prior to that piece [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]] was little known to the public though well known in Washington, D.C., and that Beattie and [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] subsequently brought Eisen's name to public attention in a major way, shaping much of the narrative around his "rule of law" work.[1]

## Definition of a color revolution

DeLarme quoted a companion Revolver News article on the Transition Integrity Project for the outlet's working definition of the term: "A 'Color Revolution' in this context refers to a specific type of coordinated attack that the United States government has been known to deploy against foreign regimes, particularly in Eastern Europe deemed to be 'authoritarian' and hostile to American interests. Rather than using a direct military intervention to effect regime change as in Iraq, Color Revolutions attack a foreign regime by contesting its electoral legitimacy, organizing mass protests and acts of civil disobedience, and leveraging media contacts to ensure favorable coverage to their agenda in the Western press."[1]

DeLarme presented this definition as valuable because it supplies a precise and tangible heuristic for understanding operations against populism, more specific than the broader and more ambiguous term "coup."[1]
