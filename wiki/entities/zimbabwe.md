# Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a southern African country that appears in the July 2024 Badlands News Brief as the regional counterpart to [[zambia|Zambia]] in a story about American military presence and Russian engagement in Africa.[1]

The Brief recalls its prior coverage of a deal struck by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] supplying fertilizer to Zimbabwe, which it credits with producing a historic crop yield for the struggling nation.[1] It further reports that in June 2024 Zimbabwe reached out to Russia over concerns that the United States was attempting to consolidate its military strength in Zambia and to exacerbate a growing rift between the two historically close neighbors.[1]

Zimbabwean President [[emmerson-mnangagwa|Emmerson Mnangagwa]] is quoted remarking, "The United States is making us feel lonely," which the Brief interprets as expressing a perceived encirclement by Western powers.[1] Zimbabwe's stated concerns about [[africom|US Africa Command]] activity are presented as the precursor to Zambia's own call for a US withdrawal.[1]
