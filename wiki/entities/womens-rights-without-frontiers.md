Women's Rights Without Frontiers is a human-rights nonprofit founded and led by lawyer [[reggie-littlejohn|Reggie Littlejohn]].[1] A May 2023 Badlands Media article identified the organization in describing Littlejohn's credentials as a critic of the proposed [[who-pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and amendments to the [[international-health-regulations|International Health Regulations (IHR)]].[1]

Littlejohn, as the group's founder and president and as co-founder of the [[sovereignty-coalition|Sovereignty Coalition]] and co-chair of the Stop Vaccine Passports Task Force, was quoted warning that passage of the treaty and amendments "would basically be the end of our national sovereignty" — a claim the article presented as corroborating its thesis about the [[world-health-organization|WHO]]'s pursuit of global authority.[1]
