# Zoom Video Communications

Zoom Video Communications is the virtual meeting platform cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a leading beneficiary of the COVID-19 pandemic economy.[1]

DeLarme groups Zoom with Google Hangouts and Microsoft among the virtual meeting platforms that saw explosive growth while myriad small businesses were forced to close permanently, noting that Zoom in particular reached $663.5 million in revenue across the months of May through July.[1] He observes that [[blackrock|BlackRock]] and [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] are the largest shareholders of all of these companies, as they are of the [[walmart|Walmart]] and [[target-corporation|Target]] supermarkets that absorbed the customers of shuttered small-town grocers.[1]

The example forms part of his argument that the pandemic produced the largest upward transfer of wealth in human history, a claim he supports with the [[oxfam|Oxfam]] report "Power, Profits and the Pandemic" and with the doubling of the fortunes of the world's ten richest men.[1]
