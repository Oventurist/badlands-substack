# Ambrose Finnegan

2nd Lt. Ambrose J. "Bozey" Finnegan Jr. was a United States Army Air Forces officer in the Second World War and an uncle of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]. He became the subject of national controversy in April 2024 after Biden falsely claimed he had been eaten by cannibals.[1]

## Biden's Claim

Speaking in Pittsburgh on Wednesday, April 17, 2024, Biden said of Finnegan: "He was a hell of an athlete, they tell me, when he was a kid. He flew those single-engine planes as reconnaissance over war zones, and he got shot down in New Guinea. They never found the body because there used to be, there were a lot of cannibals, for real, in that part of New Guinea."[1] He added that "the government went back when I went down there and they checked and found some parts of the plane."[1] The account appeared in the official White House transcript of the remarks, and Biden made the claim twice.[1] He raised it while disparaging [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for skipping a 2018 visit to a military cemetery outside Paris, a cancellation that had in fact been made by the Navy on bad-weather grounds.[1]

## The Documented Record

As [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] pointed out, a survivor of the incident gave a detailed account describing how Finnegan and another man remained in the plane as it sank.[1] Nor was the aircraft shot down: it was a Douglas A-20 Havoc powered by two Pratt & Whitney R-985 Wasp Junior nine-cylinder radial engines, which suffered mechanical problems and crashed near New Guinea before sinking into the ocean.[1]

## Reaction

The story was fact-checked by the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the coincidence of cannibalism entering the news cycle twice in as many months — first through coverage of the Haitian gang leader known as "Barbecue," and now through "Uncle Bosey" — and speculated about which name might be attached to such a story next.[1]
