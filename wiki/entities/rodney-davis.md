# Rodney Davis

Rodney Davis is a former Republican member of the United States House of Representatives who led a Republican review of the security failures at the United States Capitol on January 6, 2021.[1]

## January 6 security report

Davis was among the Republican lawmakers — with [[jim-banks|Jim Banks]], [[troy-nehls|Troy Nehls]], [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] and [[kelly-armstrong|Kelly Armstrong]] — who compiled a report made public in June 2023 assembling emails and text messages showing that House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]]'s office was directly involved in the creation and editing of the Capitol security plan that failed on January 6.[1] The investigation was authorized by House Minority Leader [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] and reviewed evidence the lawmakers said the Democrat-led [[january-6-committee|January 6 committee]] had ignored.[1]

The report found that concerns about the "optics" of visible armed officers and National Guard troops, held by Democratic House leadership and [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] leadership, overrode the need for enhanced security, leaving the Capitol vulnerable while security officials were "denied again and again" the resources they sought.[1]
