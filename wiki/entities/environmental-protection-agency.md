# Environmental Protection Agency

The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) is the United States federal agency responsible for administering the [[clean-air-act|Clean Air Act]] and other environmental statutes, and the issuer of the 2009 [[endangerment-finding|endangerment finding]] that established the legal predicate for federal greenhouse gas regulation.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration is preparing to rescind that finding. The final rule was to be issued on the Thursday following a [[white-house|White House]] ceremony with the president and EPA Administrator [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]], and follows a proposed rescission announced the previous July together with an executive order issued on Inauguration Day directing a review of the finding's legality.

Administration officials, Zeldin among them, described the action as the largest act of deregulation in United States history, projecting over $1 trillion in regulatory cost savings — primarily through reduced vehicle compliance expenses averaging more than $2,400 per light-duty car, SUV or truck — and emphasising benefits for energy affordability, fossil fuel production, economic growth and national security. The rescission initially targets motor vehicle emissions standards, eliminating requirements to measure, report, certify and comply with greenhouse gas limits, while paving the way for potential future rollbacks of power plant and stationary-source rules.

Environmental organisations including [[earthjustice|Earthjustice]] and the [[environmental-defense-fund|Environmental Defense Fund]] condemned the move and pledged immediate court challenges grounded in settled science and Supreme Court precedent, notably [[massachusetts-v-epa|Massachusetts v. EPA]]. Democratic-led states signalled opposition and the possibility of independent emissions standards, while the fossil fuel industry welcomed relief from regulatory burdens. Legal experts anticipate prolonged litigation during which some rules may remain unenforced.
