# Thomas Schelling

Thomas Schelling was a Nobel Prize–winning nuclear theorist who, in the mid-1960s, recruited the 29-year-old [[gene-sharp|Gene Sharp]] to join the [[weatherhead-center-for-international-affairs|Center for International Affairs]] at Harvard.[1]

Badlands Media cites this recruitment as the moment Sharp entered the Cold War defense, intelligence and security establishment, describing the Harvard center — nicknamed "The CIA at Harvard" — as the institution that also nurtured [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], future National Security Advisor McGeorge Bundy and future [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] chief Robert Bowie.[1] The article uses the connection to support its argument that Sharp is better understood as a Cold War defense intellectual than as a people's champion.[1]
