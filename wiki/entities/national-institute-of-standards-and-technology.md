# National Institute of Standards and Technology

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) is a United States federal agency responsible for measurement standards and technical guidance, including standards work relevant to voting system certification through the Technical Guidelines Development Committee.

NIST appears in [[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]] as the recipient of a 2010 letter from attorney [[john-c-bonifaz|John C. Bonifaz]] warning that [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] — then described as the largest or second largest voting system company in the country — was foreign controlled and dependent on secret source code created and owned by [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]], a company the letter tied to the Venezuelan government of [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]]. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cites the letter as documentary evidence that concerns about the provenance of American election software were raised formally with federal standards authorities well before the disputes of the 2020s.
