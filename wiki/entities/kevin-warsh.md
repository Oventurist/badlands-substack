# Kevin Warsh

Kevin Warsh is an American financier and former Governor of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], serving on the Board of Governors from 2006 to 2011. In January 2026 he emerged as the reported frontrunner to replace [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] as Chair of the Federal Reserve.

## 2026 Fed chair candidacy

According to the [[badlands-brief-1ce|January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said at the premiere of the film *Melania* that he would announce Powell's replacement on Friday morning, January 30. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported that the shortlist had been vetted by Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] and that Warsh was "widely regarded as the frontrunner," having reportedly met with the President at the [[white-house|White House]] on Thursday, January 29.

The other names on the shortlist were National Economic Council director [[kevin-hassett|Kevin Hassett]], sitting Fed Governor [[christopher-waller|Christopher Waller]], and BlackRock's [[rick-rieder|Rick Rieder]]. The Brief noted that all of the candidates indicated support for cutting interest rates — the major point of contention between Trump and Powell.
