# Brewster Kahle

Brewster Kahle is the founder of the [[internet-archive|Internet Archive]], the San Francisco nonprofit that maintains the Wayback Machine and its collection of more than 900 billion preserved webpages.[1]

## Response to the 2024 breach

Following the October 2024 hack of the Internet Archive — in which attackers leaked the data of millions of users and defaced the site — Kahle spoke to the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] about the organization's decision to take the site and the Wayback Machine offline to prevent further leaks.[1] He noted that it was the first time in the Archive's almost 30-year history that it had suffered an outage lasting longer than a few hours.[1]

Kahle expressed bafflement at the motive for the intrusion, observing that the attackers appeared to have no agenda and made no ransom demand against what is a free repository of digital history: "I don't know. Why kick the cat?"[1]
