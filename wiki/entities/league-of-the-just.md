# League of the Just

The League of the Just, known in Germany as the Bund der Gerechten or simply "the Bund," was a secret society that, in the account of [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], paid and directed [[karl-marx|Karl Marx]] in the production of the ideas set out in the *Communist Manifesto*.[1] The organization later became known as the International Communist Party and is described as either the spiritual successor of the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]] or as a body working directly in conjunction with it.[1]

## Aims

The Bund is presented as existing not only to infiltrate and eradicate most religions but to target Judaism in particular, which at that time constituted a far more unified people than is the case today.[1] DeLarme argues it is easier to control and eventually destroy a religion by splintering it into many competing sects, and that this was one of the purposes the Bund inherited from the Illuminati.[1]

The true origin of the Bund appears to be lost in obscurity, but many political scientists believe it was influenced by the Bavarian Illuminati; political scientists remain divided as to the Illuminati's relationship with the Communist Party more broadly.[1]
