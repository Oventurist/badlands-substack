# Oliver North

**Oliver North** was the United States official whose Contra resupply network became a defining element of the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra scandal]] of the 1980s.[1]

In the account of Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], North's network provides the link between the covert arming of the [[nicaraguan-contras|Nicaraguan Contras]] and the alleged narcotics operation at [[mena-arkansas|Mena, Arkansas]].[1] The aircraft formerly flown by [[barry-seal|Barry Seal]] — the C-123 used in a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] sting against the Sandinistas — was by October 1986 tied to North's Contra resupply effort, and its crash in Nicaragua while carrying a load of guns dragged Mena into the scandal's harsh spotlight, as reported by PBS Frontline in 1987.[1]

North's operation existed because [[congress|Congress]] had cut off official Contra funding through the [[boland-amendment|Boland Amendment]], forcing the war's backers toward off-the-books financing.[1]
