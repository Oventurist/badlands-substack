# Sinclair Broadcast Group

Sinclair Broadcast Group is an American television broadcasting company that controls a large portfolio of local network affiliate stations.[1]

## Role in the Kimmel suspension

In September 2025, Sinclair-controlled affiliates announced they would indefinitely cancel broadcasts of [[jimmy-kimmel|Jimmy Kimmel]]'s show following his monologue remarks about the death of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1] The move, made in parallel with [[nexstar-media-group|Nexstar Media Group]], came before [[abc-news|ABC]] announced it would indefinitely halt production of the program.[1] See [[jimmy-kimmel-live-suspension|Jimmy Kimmel Live! suspension]].
