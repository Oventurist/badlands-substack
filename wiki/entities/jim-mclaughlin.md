# Jim McLaughlin

Jim McLaughlin is a pollster for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] who publicly predicted that the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]]'s December 2023 ballot-disqualification ruling would backfire politically on its authors.[1]

Speaking to Politico on the night of the decision, McLaughlin said, "The fact that it was a bunch of unelected, left-wing judges, and a lawsuit that was brought by a [George] Soros-funded organization, it's crazy."[1] He added that "the American people have a great sense of fairness, and these are the people that run around saying that Trump's a threat to Democracy, and they're getting him thrown off the ballot. They are not giving the American people the right to decide. This, I think, is going to backfire."[1]

The framing echoed a broader Republican argument that adjudicating the 2024 presidential campaign in courtrooms rather than at the ballot box would strain American governing institutions, though coverage noted that the constant legal controversy surrounding Trump has not been an unambiguous political benefit — many voters wearied of it in 2020, and President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] leaned heavily on critiques of Trump's fitness for office in his own reelection campaign.[1]
