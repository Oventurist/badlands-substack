# University of Ingolstadt

The University of Ingolstadt in Bavaria, Germany, was the institution where [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]] held a professorship of canon law and where the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]] was born.[1] Weishaupt's godfather, Baron Ickstatt, was curator of the university and arranged for the orphaned Weishaupt to be raised by the [[jesuits|Jesuits]] after the death of his father George Weishaupt in 1754.[1] Weishaupt would convert to Protestantism while at Ingolstadt before eventually rebelling against the Abrahamic religions altogether.[1]

Ingolstadt was also the geographic seat of the Illuminati movement, which the article pairs with Offenbach as the seat of the [[frankism|Frankists]] after 1786, noting that the two movements paralleled one another in both time and geography within Germany.[1] Following the exposure of the Order, Weishaupt was discovered to be its head, deprived of his professor's chair and banished from Bavaria, after which he went to Regensburg and continued his activities.[1]

At the time, the Jesuit monopoly over universities and colleges was being challenged by the explosion of natural philosophy and science, the resurgence of hermeticism and occult experimentation that characterized [[the-enlightenment|the Age of Enlightenment]].[1]
