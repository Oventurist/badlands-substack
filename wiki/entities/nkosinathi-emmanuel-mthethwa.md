# Nkosinathi Emmanuel Mthethwa

**Nkosinathi Emmanuel Mthethwa** was South Africa's ambassador to France and a former cabinet minister who was found dead in Paris in late September 2025 after reportedly falling from the 22nd floor of the Hyatt Regency hotel in the Porte Maillot area.[1] He was 58.[1]

## Death and investigation

French prosecutors launched an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the death.[1] Mthethwa had checked into the hotel, where investigators said a secure window appeared to have been forced open.[1] His body was discovered early on a Tuesday morning after a police search was initiated in response to concerns raised by his wife.[1] French authorities confirmed that his last known phone signal was traced to the Bois de Boulogne, a large park west of Paris, where search dogs were deployed during the initial phase of the investigation.[1] The South African government said in a statement that "[t]he circumstances of his untimely death are under investigation by the French authorities."[1]

## Political career

Mthethwa was a long-serving member of the [[african-national-congress|African National Congress]], South Africa's ruling party, formerly led by Nelson Mandela.[1] He had recently been implicated in a judicial inquiry probing alleged corruption and reported links between senior South African officials and organized crime.[1] He served as South Africa's Minister of Police from 2009 to 2014, a tenure that drew widespread criticism following the 2012 killing of 34 striking miners by police at Marikana.[1]
