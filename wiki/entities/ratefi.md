# RateFi

RateFi is a US-based mortgage lender reported in the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]] to have launched a crypto-backed mortgage qualification product.

## Crypto-backed qualification

According to the Brief, RateFi announced a mortgage product allowing borrowers to use cryptocurrency holdings — such as [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and Ethereum — toward borrower qualification in the underwriting process, without requiring liquidation of those assets. The item was published as a companion to the report that the average US 30-year fixed mortgage rate had fallen to 5.99% according to [[freddie-mac|Freddie Mac]] data, the first sub-6% reading since 2022, and is presented as an example of digital assets moving into mainstream mortgage underwriting.
