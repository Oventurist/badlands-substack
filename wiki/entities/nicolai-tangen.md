# Nicolai Tangen

Nicolai Tangen is the chief executive of the body managing Norway's sovereign wealth fund, the world's largest at roughly $2 trillion in assets.[1]

## 2025 divestment from Israeli holdings

In August 2025 Tangen announced that the fund was selling stakes in some Israeli companies and terminating all contracts with Israeli asset managers, citing the worsening situation in Gaza and the West Bank.[1] "We are invested in companies that operate in a country at war, and conditions in the West Bank and Gaza have recently worsened. In response, we will further strengthen our due diligence," Tangen said in a statement.[1] The manager, [[norges-bank-investment-management|Norges Bank Investment Management]], said it was divesting from 11 Israeli companies out of 61 held, specifically those not included in an equity benchmark index created by Norway's Finance Ministry.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] linked the decision to a Saudi Foreign Ministry statement calling on the international community to take effective action against Israel, arguing the divestment would ripple through markets as other institutional and retail investors avoided an increasingly toxic brand.[1]
