# John Kerry

John Kerry served as Secretary of State in the second term of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration. In the Badlands Media article "Bad Actors," his role is confined to a single decisive act: the authorisation, in the administration's final hours, of a $220.3 million disbursement to Gaza.[1]

## The final-hours Gaza disbursement

The Independent reported that the House was informed by then-Secretary of State John Kerry hours before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration.[1] Acting [[state-department|State Department]] spokesman Mark Toner told the paper: "In a final act, then-Secretary of State John Kerry instructed USAID to release $220.3 million for Gaza recovery programs."[1] Obama had overruled [[congress|Congress]] to permit the transfer, which was nominally intended to support good governance and humanitarian aid for the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]].[1] The incoming Trump administration froze the package.[1]

Joe Lange questions whether the money was truly destined for humanitarian purposes or for the Palestinian Authority at all, noting that Kerry routed it through [[usaid|USAID]], which he characterises as a [[cia|CIA]] front group and taxpayer slush fund.[1] In his reading, the disbursement was the mechanism by which a political debt owed to [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] was settled with public money.[1]
