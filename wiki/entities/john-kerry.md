# John Kerry

John Kerry served as Secretary of State in the second term of the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration. In the Badlands Media article "Bad Actors," his role is confined to a single decisive act: the authorisation, in the administration's final hours, of a $220.3 million disbursement to Gaza.[1]

## The final-hours Gaza disbursement

The Independent reported that the House was informed by then-Secretary of State John Kerry hours before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration.[1] Acting [[state-department|State Department]] spokesman Mark Toner told the paper: "In a final act, then-Secretary of State John Kerry instructed USAID to release $220.3 million for Gaza recovery programs."[1] Obama had overruled [[congress|Congress]] to permit the transfer, which was nominally intended to support good governance and humanitarian aid for the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]].[1] The incoming Trump administration froze the package.[1]

Joe Lange questions whether the money was truly destined for humanitarian purposes or for the Palestinian Authority at all, noting that Kerry routed it through [[usaid|USAID]], which he characterises as a [[cia|CIA]] front group and taxpayer slush fund.[1] In his reading, the disbursement was the mechanism by which a political debt owed to [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] was settled with public money.[1]

## Georgetown dinner with MBS, 2015

On the evening of the September 2015 Saudi delegation visit, Secretary of State John Kerry hosted an elegant dinner party at his Georgetown home for [[king-salman|King Salman]]'s party.[2] According to Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] — dismissed by DC elites as an unrefined homebody — approached Kerry's baby grand piano without asking permission and performed a rendition of Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata so moving that it left the assembled socialites speechless, explaining afterward that he was entirely self-taught.[2]

## The 1987–88 narcotics hearings

Badlands contributor Joe Lange credits Kerry with leading the Senate hearings that, a year before the [[iran-contra-affair]] investigations, dug into evidence of drug trafficking by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] — inquiries he says struck genuine fear in what he calls the [[shadow-government]].[3] The hearings, held before the Senate Subcommittee on Narcotics, Terrorism and International Operations and a Senate Foreign Relations subcommittee, produced the sworn testimony of [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]] about laundering billions for the [[medellin-cartel]] through [[manuel-noriega]]'s Panama and about his own work for the CIA.[3] Lange writes that the hearings received nowhere near the media attention of Iran-Contra and have been largely forgotten.[3]
