# John Kerry

John Kerry is an American politician who served as United States Secretary of State from 2013 to 2017 under President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], having previously been a United States Senator from Massachusetts and the Democratic presidential nominee in 2004.

In the Badlands Media article "Bad Actors," Kerry appears in the closing hours of the Obama administration. The Independent reported that the House was informed by Kerry hours before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration of a $221 million transfer to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]]. Acting [[state-department|State Department]] spokesperson Mark Toner told the paper: "In a final act, then-Secretary of State John Kerry instructed USAID to release $220.3 million for Gaza recovery programs."

The article uses Kerry's instruction to [[usaid|USAID]] as a link in its argument that the State Department and USAID function together as conduits controlled by the [[cia|CIA]], and that the payment was a last-minute repayment of political obligations rather than a routine humanitarian disbursement. It notes that Obama overruled [[congress|Congress]] to make the decision and that the Trump administration subsequently froze the funds.
