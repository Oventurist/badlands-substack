# BOTAS International

BOTAS International is a Turkish state-owned company that operates the [[baku-tbilisi-ceyhan-pipeline|Baku–Tbilisi–Ceyhan (BTC) pipeline]].[1] It was originally founded in 1974 to operate the Kirkuk–Ceyhan pipeline, which runs adjacent to the BTC and connects the Kirkuk oil fields in Iraq to the Turkish Mediterranean port of Ceyhan.[1]

## Role in Azerbaijani–Israeli oil flows

Azerbaijani crude accounts for a large share of the oil consumed by [[israel|Israel]], and that oil moves from Baku to Ceyhan through the BTC pipeline before onward shipment.[1] According to Turkish media reports cited in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief, the oil transiting the BTC pipeline is regulated under a trade agreement between [[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]] and Georgia rather than by [[turkey|Turkey]], which limits Ankara's formal ability to halt shipments.[1]

Following President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]]'s declaration that Turkey had cut off relations with Israel, the companies owning oil moving through the BOTAS-operated line were reported to have decided to respect that decision and to have refused shipments.[1] Turkey's Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources refuted claims of interference, stressing that BOTAS has "no authority or involvement in the purchase or sale of oil."[1] Turkish energy analyst Haluk Direskeneli argued in a contemporaneous op-ed that Turkey could not lawfully halt oil shipments from Azerbaijan to Israel absent United Nations sanctions.[1]

The Brief's analysis, written by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], speculated that a sustained interruption of these flows would produce fuel shortages for the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]], and that prospective [[united-nations|United Nations]] sanctions on Israel could give Erdogan legal cover to shut the BTC pipeline altogether.[1]
