# Red Dawn (1984 film)

**Red Dawn** is a 1984 American film depicting a World War III scenario reaching American soil.[1] Set in [[colorado|Colorado]], the story follows a group of teenagers who escape into the mountains as Soviet fighters descend by parachute and round up their parents, forcing the young protagonists to band together to defend their families, their town and their country.[1]

The film's rallying cry, "Wolverines!", and its central characters — Jed, Matt, Erica and Toni — became cultural touchstones for a generation of American children who imagined themselves waging unconventional warfare at home.[1]

## Relevance to Badlands reporting

An October 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] article by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked the film after news broke that paratroopers had descended over [[israel|Israel]] on October 7, arguing that every 1980s American child's first thought was "Wolverines!"[1] The piece went on to examine a Department of Defense delivery order literally named [[red-dawn-iii|Red Dawn III]], awarded to a Colorado firm, whose original completion date fell in October 2023.[1] The author repeatedly argued that naming a real defense project after such a specific invasion scenario was irresponsible regardless of whether the timing was coincidental.[1] The article closed with the exhortation "Stay vigilant, Wolverines."[1]
