# Andy Burnham

Andy Burnham is a British Labour politician who, in June 2026, emerged as the leading potential challenger to Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] for the leadership of the [[labour-party|Labour Party]].[1]

## Return to Parliament and leadership positioning

Burnham won the Makerfield by-election and was due to be sworn in as a member of Parliament on the Monday following his victory, according to a report published by The Observer.[1] He was expected to meet Starmer early that same week.[1]

Supporters of Burnham were reported to be claiming the backing of more than 201 Labour MPs for a potential leadership challenge in the event that Starmer did not step down voluntarily.[1] The Observer reported that Starmer had concluded his position was no longer tenable after discussions with cabinet ministers, advisers, trade union leaders and party donors, and was expected to announce a timetable for his departure from Downing Street as early as Monday.[1] Some Labour figures anticipated an orderly transition process potentially culminating in a leadership change later in the year, although a Downing Street source dismissed reports of an imminent resignation as "just speculation" and Starmer maintained he would stand in any leadership contest.[1]
