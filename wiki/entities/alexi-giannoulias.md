# Alexi Giannoulias

Alexi Giannoulias is the Secretary of State of Illinois, whose office administers driver's licensing and identification services in the state.[1]

## REAL ID Supercenter

Amid long lines outside Illinois Secretary of State facilities in Chicago's Loop as the deadline for [[real-id|REAL ID]] enforcement at the nation's airports approached, Giannoulias opened a downtown REAL ID Supercenter at 191 N. Clark Street in March 2025 without requesting additional taxpayer funding.[1] No appointment was required at the facility, and editorial commentary credited him with triaging the situation.[1]
