# Project for a New American Century

The Project for a New American Century (PNAC) was a neoconservative think tank co-founded in part by [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]], the husband of [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]].[1] The organization is described as having produced dystopic policy visions for the 21st century, most notably the September 2000 document [[rebuilding-americas-defenses|Rebuilding America's Defenses]].[1]

## Doctrine

PNAC's outlook treated both Russia and China not as potential allies but as intrinsic enemies to be destroyed if the planned global hegemony of the United States was to be ensured.[1] In direct opposition to the win-win cooperation envisioned by Representative [[curt-weldon|Curt Weldon]] and his allies, the unipolarist networks associated with PNAC anticipated a Hobbesian world order of struggle of each against all.[1]

*Rebuilding America's Defenses* forecast that although the process of military transformation might take several decades, combat would come to take place in new dimensions — in space, in "cyber-space," and "perhaps the world of microbes."[1] It predicted that air warfare might no longer be fought by pilots in tactical fighters but by long-range, stealthy unmanned craft; that space itself would become a theater of war as the distinction between military and commercial space systems, combatants and noncombatants, became blurred; and that information systems would become an important focus of attack for adversaries seeking to short-circuit sophisticated American forces.[1] Most controversially, the document stated that "advanced forms of biological warfare that can 'target' specific genotypes may transform biological warfare from the realm of terror to a politically useful tool."[1]

## Relationship to other networks

The thinking of grand strategist [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] is described as visceral in the pulse of ideologues such as Kagan, Nuland and other neoconservatives including [[paul-wolfowitz|Paul Wolfowitz]], [[richard-perle|Richard Perle]], [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], [[donald-rumsfeld|Donald Rumsfeld]] and [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]], who are said to have run the malleable presidency of [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]].[1] Brzezinski's 1997 [[grand-chessboard|Grand Chessboard]], which outlined the carving up of Russia under Washington diktat, could likewise be "smelled across the pages of the PNAC white papers."[1]

The article argues that the doctrine adopted by the Bush administration was not that of the American patriots surrounding Weldon, but that of this network of unipolarists seeking to keep the world divided and suppressed while a new Pax Americana consolidated its possessions under a program of [[full-spectrum-dominance|Full Spectrum Dominance]].[1]
