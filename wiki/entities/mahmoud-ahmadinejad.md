# Mahmoud Ahmadinejad

Mahmoud Ahmadinejad is an Iranian politician who served as President of [[iran|Iran]] from 2005 to 2013, when he was replaced by [[hassan-rouhani|Hassan Rouhani]].

## Reported house arrest (July 2026)

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Ahmadinejad was reportedly placed under house arrest following [[israel|Israel]]'s recent conflict with Iran, according to multiple media reports citing sources familiar with the matter. The reports allege that Iranian authorities — investigating suspected Israeli intelligence infiltration — were questioning him over alleged contacts with individuals accused of links to [[mossad|Mossad]]. Iranian authorities had not publicly confirmed the reports or announced any formal charges, and neither Ahmadinejad nor his representatives had publicly commented.

The reported house arrest came as Iran continued a sweeping internal security crackdown following its military confrontation with Israel, with numerous arrests and investigations targeting individuals accused of espionage or collaboration with Israel, and state media reporting executions in several espionage-related cases.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats the reports as validating his theory that Mossad had deeply infiltrated the Iranian government, framing Iran's leadership over recent decades as a "mixed bag of 'good twin/evil twin'" in relation to the global [[deep-state|Deep State]] and arguing that "a deeply complex web is being untangled and Iran is being purged (or has been purged) of the infiltrators."
