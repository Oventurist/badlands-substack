# Conscious Strength

Conscious Strength is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program hosted by [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]], scheduled for the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month at 12pm ET in the January 2023 schedule.[1] The program focuses on holistic self-development, discussing how individuals can craft themselves into physically and mentally stronger people.[1]
