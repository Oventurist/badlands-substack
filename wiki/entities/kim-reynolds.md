# Kim Reynolds

Kim Reynolds is the Republican Governor of Iowa. In December 2025 she responded to the deaths of two Iowa National Guard members killed in the [[palmyra-attack-on-iowa-national-guard-members|Palmyra ambush]] in Syria by ordering all flags in the state to fly at half-staff in their honor, saying, "We are grateful for their service and deeply mourn their loss."[1]

The soldiers, Sergeant Edgar Brian Torres-Tovar of Des Moines and Sergeant William Nathaniel Howard of Marshalltown, were killed in an attack attributed to the [[islamic-state|Islamic State]] that also killed a civilian U.S. interpreter and injured three other Guard members.[1]

## 2023 Iowa Capitol Satanic display

In December 2023 Reynolds came under pressure over the [[iowa-state-capitol-satanic-display-controversy|Satanic holiday display]] that [[satanic-temple|The Satanic Temple]] was permitted to install inside the Iowa State Capitol, featuring a Baphomet goat skull and a blood-red pentagram.[2] Iowa state representative [[brad-sherman-iowa|Brad Sherman]] led calls for her to remove the "blasphemous" installation, which he called unconstitutional and offensive to God, and demanded legislation barring Satanic displays from the Capitol and state property.[2] Badlands writer [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] said Reynolds "should be ashamed of herself" for allowing the display, while [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the group held a valid permit and warned that demanding removal risked eroding First Amendment protections.[2]
