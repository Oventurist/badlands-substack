# Burundi

Burundi is a landlocked state in Africa's Great Lakes region, bordering the eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] near Lake Tanganyika.[1]

## Role in the eastern Congo conflict

Burundi is a regional rival of [[rwanda|Rwanda]] and an ally of the DRC. Badlands analysis in December 2025 framed Burundi and the DRC together as the principal opposition to [[paul-kagame|Paul Kagame]]'s government in Kigali, which the same commentary characterized as a globalist proxy and close ally of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]

The city of [[uvira|Uvira]], seized by [[m23|M23]] rebels in December 2025, sits on Lake Tanganyika near the Burundian border and had served as an important military and administrative base for Kinshasa in South Kivu following the earlier fall of the provincial capital, Bukavu.[1] Its capture therefore brought the conflict directly to Burundi's frontier and, according to the Congolese government, displaced more than 200,000 people in a "generalized offensive" attributed to Rwanda's Defense Forces and the rebels.[1]
