# Kip Hawley

Kip Hawley is a former administrator of the [[transportation-security-administration|Transportation Security Administration]], cited in Badlands commentary for a 2010 statement explaining the agency's rationale for uniform passenger screening rules.[1]

## The 2010 equity statement

Hawley said: "We apply the same rules to everyone because it's more equitable and more effective than trying to guess who looks suspicious."[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] presented the remark as confirmation that the TSA's blanket shoe-removal requirement — imposed on all travelers from 2006 onward following the attempted shoe bombing by [[richard-reid|Richard Reid]] — was driven by an equity principle rather than by security effectiveness, on the premise that profiling for terrorists had been deemed racist.[1]

That framing was invoked again in July 2025, when reporting indicated that passengers at certain US airports were no longer required to remove their shoes, ending what the commentary called "a stupid and inconvenient era" fifteen years in the unwinding.[1]
