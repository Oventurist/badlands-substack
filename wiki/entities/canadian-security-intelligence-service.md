# Canadian Security Intelligence Service

The **Canadian Security Intelligence Service** (CSIS) is Canada's principal intelligence agency, described in reporting on the 2024 Winnipeg laboratory affair as "Canada's top intelligence service."[1]

## Assessment of the Winnipeg lab virologists

In 2024, secret documents authored by CSIS and viewed and published by The Counter Signal supplied the long-awaited explanation for the abrupt departure of two virologists from Canada's top biolaboratory.[1] While the Liberal government of [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] had cited the departure of Dr. [[xiangguo-qiu|Xiangguo Qiu]] from Canada's public health agency as a private "personnel issue," CSIS accused her of a long history of clandestine actions that put Canadians' health and security at risk and directly assisted military research in China.[1]

The service documented that Qiu sold deadly pathogens to Chinese authorities at the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]] for $75 and hid a Chinese bank account from CSIS.[1] Its assessment concluded that "Ms. Qiu developed deep, cooperative relationships with a variety of People's Republic of China (PRC) institutions and has intentionally transferred scientific knowledge and materials to China in order to benefit the PRC Government, and herself, without regard for the implications to her employer or to Canada's interests."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argued that the CSIS findings, and the government's suppression of them, pointed not to a security failure but to deliberate collaboration with the Chinese Communist Party at the political level, framing the matter as one of treason rather than incompetence.[1]
