# David Ellison

David Ellison is the head of [[skydance-media|Skydance Media]] and the son of [[oracle|Oracle]] cofounder [[larry-ellison|Larry Ellison]]. In the Badlands Media essay A New Hollywood, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identifies him as the executive who will effectively control [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]] once its merger with Skydance is complete, making him a key figure in what the article describes as the [[donald-trump|Trump]]-aligned takeover of Hollywood.

Under his leadership, Skydance produced *Top Gun: Maverick*, the *Mission Impossible* films and *Jack Reacher*, along with the television series *Reacher* and *Jack Ryan*.
