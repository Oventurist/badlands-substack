# Democratic Party of Korea

The Democratic Party is the main opposition party of [[south-korea|South Korea]] and the principal antagonist of President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]] during the [[2024-south-korea-martial-law-declaration|2024 South Korea martial law declaration]].[1]

## Role in the martial law crisis

Following Yoon's brief imposition of martial law on the night of December 3, 2024, the party urged the president to resign immediately or face impeachment.[1] On Wednesday it announced in a statement that it would pursue charges of treason and impeachment against Yoon, as well as against South Korea's defense minister and safety minister, alleging that the declaration of martial law was illegal.[1]

Party leader [[lee-jae-myung|Lee Jae-myung]] filmed a video from the backseat of his car declaring that Yoon was no longer president, and later livestreamed himself scaling the walls of the [[national-assembly-of-south-korea|National Assembly]]; other opposition lawmakers followed suit to gain access to the legislature while denouncing the move as a coup.[1]

## Electoral outlook

Rory Green of TS Lombard predicted that Yoon was "doomed to face impeachment," that a presidential election would probably follow early in the second quarter of 2025, and that the Democratic Party would be "strong favorites" to win it.[1]
