# Leonard Benardo

Leonard Benardo is an Open Society Foundations official whose hacked email correspondence became a central exhibit in the declassified annex to the [[john-durham|John Durham]] investigation, released on July 31, 2025.[1]

## Role in the Durham Annex

Material highlighted in the annex included an email attributed to Benardo which, in the reading advanced by Badlands contributors, showed that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] had approved a plan to vilify [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by tying him to Russia.[1] Intelligence community analysts assessed that "their best assessment was that the Benardo emails were likely authentic," and multiple analysts noted that Russian services had in fact hacked Benardo's email accounts.[1]

The stated goal described in the email was to generate sufficient public attention around Russian interference that election systems could be treated as a critical infrastructure threat, which would in turn allow the Obama administration and the intelligence community to "speed up searching for evidence that is regrettably still unavailable."[1] Commentators noted that Obama's Department of Homeland Security formally designated election infrastructure as critical infrastructure on January 6, 2017, matching the sequence the email anticipated.[1] The email further stated that, absent direct evidence, CrowdStrike would "supply the media" — an outcome that Badlands contributor Jon Herold argued is precisely what occurred.[1]

## Significance

Because the annex material was released one day after an op-ed by [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] and [[james-clapper|James Clapper]] defending the 2017 intelligence assessment, the Benardo emails were presented by Badlands commentary as evidence that the public defense of the assessment amounted to damage control rather than a rebuttal.[1]
