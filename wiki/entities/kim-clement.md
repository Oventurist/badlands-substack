# Kim Clement

**Kim Clement** was a Christian prophet whose prophecies are cited in the Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]] as one of the "unique" data points supporting its eschatological hypothesis. He died on November 23, 2016.

## Prophecies as described in the corpus

[[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] calls Clement's prophecies "remarkably accurate" and finds it incredible that he made prophecies about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] becoming president over the course of a decade, likely without ever having met him. The article mentions that Clement referred to a possible meeting in June 2015, but states the author could not confirm it.

The prophecy singled out for discussion is Clement's statement that "Trump shall become a trumpet." The author asks whether this was a reference to one of the seven trumpets of the [[book-of-revelation|book of Revelation]], whose judgments feature centrally in the article's reading of [[great-tribulation|the Tribulation]]. He also notes with interest Clement's mention of a "brilliant plan" that could only be "given by God," a phrase that resonates within the corpus with [[q|Q]]-adjacent language about "the plan."
