# Kim Clement

**Kim Clement** was a Christian prophetic minister whose predictions are described in the Badlands Media essay "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?" as remarkably accurate and as one of the essay's unique data points suggesting the present era is biblically distinctive.[1]

## Trump prophecies

The article's author finds it incredible that Clement made prophecies about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] becoming president over the course of a decade, likely without ever meeting him.[1] Clement mentioned a possible meeting in June 2015, which the author could not confirm.[1] Clement died on November 23, 2016.[1]

## "Trump shall become a trumpet"

The prophecy singled out for discussion is Clement's statement that "Trump shall become a trumpet," which the author asks whether it referred to one of the seven trumpets of [[book-of-revelation|Revelation]] and its associated judgements during [[the-tribulation|the Tribulation]].[1] The author also notes his interest in Clement's mention of a "brilliant plan" that could only be "given by God."[1]
