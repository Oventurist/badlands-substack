# Dianna Gibson

Dianna Gibson is a Utah 3rd District Court judge presiding over the state's court-ordered congressional redistricting case.[1]

As of October 2025 Gibson faced a November 10 deadline to select a new congressional map for use in the 2026 election.[1] A flurry of court filings flooded her docket in the preceding week, and she scheduled two days of evidentiary hearings for that Thursday and Friday.[1] In the filings, legislative attorneys and plaintiffs argued over their preferred maps, their dueling redistricting experts, and which statistical tools should and should not be used to determine partisan slant or gerrymandering — with each side calling the other's map a partisan gerrymander.[1]

Complicating the proceedings, the Utah Republican Party mounted an effort both to block the map advanced earlier that month by the GOP-controlled Utah Legislature — a map the party had itself encouraged Republicans to support as the least damaging option — and to pursue an "indirect initiative" to overturn the state's independent redistricting law.[1]
