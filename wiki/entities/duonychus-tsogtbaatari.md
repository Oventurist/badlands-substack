# Duonychus tsogtbaatari

Duonychus tsogtbaatari is a previously unknown dinosaur species whose remains were unearthed by paleontologists in Mongolia's Gobi Desert, described in research published in the journal iScience in March 2025.[1] The animal was distinguished by two-fingered claws, and reporting described its overall appearance as a bizarre mix of sloth, giraffe, and the film character Edward Scissorhands.[1]

## The claw

The find was unique because the keratin sheath of one digit was preserved intact, revealing that the claw itself was considerably longer than the underlying bones.[1] Paleontologist Darla Zelenitsky, an associate professor at the University of Calgary and a coauthor of the study, said the claw is "close to a foot in size" and "by far the biggest claw preserved for a dinosaur that has that keratinous sheath on it."[1] Researchers described it as the largest claw of its kind ever found fully preserved in this manner.[1]
