# Mustafa al-Hawsawi

Mustafa al-Hawsawi is an alleged accomplice of [[khalid-sheikh-mohammed|Khalid Sheikh Mohammed]] in the September 11, 2001 attacks and a co-defendant in the capital case before the war court at [[guantanamo-bay|Guantánamo Bay]].[1]

Al-Hawsawi was one of three defendants who signed pretrial agreements on July 31, 2024 with [[susan-escallier|Susan K. Escallier]], the retired brigadier general then serving as the convening authority for military commissions, exchanging guilty pleas for sentences capped at life imprisonment.[1] Defense Secretary [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd J. Austin III]] revoked the agreements days later, writing "Effective immediately, in the exercise of my authority, I hereby withdraw from the three pretrial agreements that you signed on July 31, 2024."[1]
