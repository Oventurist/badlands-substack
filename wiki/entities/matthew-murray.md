# Matthew Murray

Matthew Murray, also referred to as Matt Murray, is an American editor and the former editor-in-chief of [[wall-street-journal|The Wall Street Journal]].[1]

In the leadership announcement made by [[washington-post|The Washington Post]] on June 2, 2024, Murray was named interim successor to departing executive editor [[sally-buzbee|Sally Buzbee]].[1] He was to serve as executive editor until after the 2024 presidential election, at which point [[robert-winnett|Robert Winnett]] would replace him.[1] The appointment accompanied a restructuring of the [[jeff-bezos|Bezos]]-owned paper's newsroom into three parts: the main newsroom, the editorial page, and a separate "third newsroom" for service and social media journalism.[1]
