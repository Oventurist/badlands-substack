# Sidley Austin

Sidley Austin is an international law firm founded in Chicago in 1866 that has grown to roughly 2,300 lawyers across 21 offices worldwide.[1] In Badlands Media commentary by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the firm is treated as a significant node in the story of American elite formation, primarily because of the number of politically consequential figures who passed through it.[1]

## Q post reference

The firm's name appears in QAnon drop #238, published on November 30, 2017, alongside references to figures such as the Obamas, the Clintons and George Soros, and closing with the phrase "happy hunting."[1] Carlson has written that the post initially made no impression on him because, unlike the other names it contained, Sidley Austin meant nothing to him personally — an omission he later used to illustrate how readers filter information according to prior familiarity and belief.[1]

## Notable alumni

In the summer of 1988, [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] met his future wife [[michelle-obama|Michelle]] while interning at the firm's Chicago branch; Michelle Obama worked at Sidley Austin for three years and later said she was uncomfortable there because there were too few Black lawyers at the firm.[1] Carlson criticises that account as an early expression of what he calls illogical DEI thinking, noting that Black attorneys make up a small share of the profession generally.[1]

The other alumnus Carlson highlights is [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], who worked at the firm's Columbus, Ohio office after graduating from [[yale-law-school|Yale Law School]].[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

Carlson argues that a law firm with a large international footprint in key global cities is exactly the kind of institution a globalist operation would use — and equally, exactly the kind of institution an effort to dismantle the Deep State would seek to infiltrate.[1] He does not claim to have resolved which reading the Q drop pointed to, presenting the firm instead as a starting point for tracing connections between Vance and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
