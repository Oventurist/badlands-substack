# ANSWER Coalition

The ANSWER Coalition is a US antiwar and activist coalition named as a downstream beneficiary in the alleged [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]] funding network.[1]

## Position in the network

According to the 2026 grand jury coverage, six Singham-linked nonprofits funnelled at least $223 million, plus additional support, into a broader global network that included the People's Welfare Association — a 501(c)(4) channeling grants worldwide — the ANSWER Coalition, the [[party-for-socialism-and-liberation|Party for Socialism and Liberation]], and various unidentified groups.[1] The coalition has historically been associated with the organizing network of longtime activist [[brian-becker|Brian Becker]].[1]

## National March on Washington: Free Palestine

On Saturday, November 4, 2023, ANSWER — an acronym for "Act Now to Stop War and End Racism" — served as the coalition group behind a demonstration it called the "National March on Washington: Free Palestine," organizing buses to the capital from across the country.[2] Thousands of protesters gathered at Freedom Plaza near the White House to demand a ceasefire in Gaza and to denounce President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s policy toward the war, carrying placards reading "Palestinian Lives Matter," "Let Gaza Live" and "Their blood is on your hands."[2] The event opened with a moment of silence as demonstrators held up a large poster bearing the names of Palestinians killed since Israel's retaliation began, and ranked among the largest pro-Palestinian gatherings in the United States and among the biggest for any cause in Washington in recent years.[2] Other participants included the [[american-muslim-alliance|American Muslim Alliance]], whose national director [[mahdi-bray|Mahdi Bray]] framed the demand as "a ceasefire now."[2]
