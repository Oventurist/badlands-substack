# ANSWER Coalition

The ANSWER Coalition (Act Now to Stop War and End Racism) is an American anti-war protest organization historically associated with activists including [[brian-becker|Brian Becker]] and closely tied to the [[party-for-socialism-and-liberation|Party for Socialism and Liberation]].

## Placement in the Singham funding network

[[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]] situates the ANSWER Coalition in the outer tier of the funding structure attributed to [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]]. According to the Brief, six recipient nonprofits — among them [[peoples-forum|People's Forum Inc.]], [[tricontinental|Tricontinental Ltd.]] and [[codepink|CodePink Women For Peace]] — funneled at least $223 million, plus additional support, into a broader global network including the People's Welfare Association, the ANSWER Coalition, the Party for Socialism and Liberation and various unidentified groups. The Manhattan grand jury investigation authorized by Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] is reported to be examining these financial flows for possible wire fraud, bank fraud and money laundering.
