# ANSWER Coalition

The ANSWER Coalition is a US antiwar and activist coalition named as a downstream beneficiary in the alleged [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]] funding network.[1]

## Position in the network

According to the 2026 grand jury coverage, six Singham-linked nonprofits funnelled at least $223 million, plus additional support, into a broader global network that included the People's Welfare Association — a 501(c)(4) channeling grants worldwide — the ANSWER Coalition, the [[party-for-socialism-and-liberation|Party for Socialism and Liberation]], and various unidentified groups.[1] The coalition has historically been associated with the organizing network of longtime activist [[brian-becker|Brian Becker]].[1]
