# B. F. Skinner

B. F. Skinner was the American psychologist associated with operant conditioning, the shaping of behavior through rewards and punishments.[1] He is named in "Hijacked Consciousness - The Spell," the July 29, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]], as one of three behaviorists whose work is said to have supplied the machinery of the modern education system.[1]

The essay groups Skinner with [[edward-thorndike|Edward Thorndike]] and [[ivan-pavlov|Ivan Pavlov]] as figures who saw humans "not as beings of light and story, but as reactionary meat waiting to be conditioned," and states that their methods "were weaponized."[1] It argues that behavioral psychology edged out mythic development, that standardized testing replaced story, and that "Skinner's boxes" became classrooms — reducing "the mystery of the cosmos" to stimulus and response.[1] The claim forms part of the essay's wider account of the [[tyranny-of-logic|tyranny of logic]].[1]
