# Wewelsburg Castle

Wewelsburg Castle is a German castle that [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] leased for 100 years in 1934 and renovated to serve as the ceremonial centre of the projected new world Reich.[1] Swastikas and pagan symbolism were embedded throughout the structure, and it became the headquarters of Himmler's [[new-templar-order|New Templar]] high initiates before and during the Second World War.[1]

## Design and symbolism

On the floor of a central tower Himmler commissioned a marble [[black-sun|Black Sun]] symbol, located in the hall known as the Obergruppenführer Hall.[1] Himmler, obsessed with the Grail myths of Parsifal and King Arthur's Round Table, modelled his inner SS priesthood on the twelve Arthurian knights, who were to gather in a circular chamber called "The Crypt" around an eternal flame; two further chambers were named "The Grail" and "King Arthur."[1]

BP Perry, in "The Nazi Castle Designed to be the Center of the World," writes that Himmler envisaged a huge complex of buildings with the castle at its centre, to be called the "Centre of the World," where victorious Nazis would rule their empire, where high-ranking SS "knights" could perform occult rituals, and where the remains of fallen SS officers would be interred for eternity.[1] Oded Heilbronner, in "The Wewelsburg Effect," describes the castle as the secret inauguration centre for SS rituals, "the magical and spiritual centre of the Order, the magical centre of the new Thule … the nerve centre of the Black Order," and a laboratory from which a Germanic god-people would arise.[1]

## Destruction and reconstruction

As the war drew to a close Himmler ordered the castle demolished, and the demolition took place days before Allied forces could capture it, burying many of its secrets.[1] Reconstruction followed rapidly with Marshall Plan funding, and by 1950 the site had been turned into a museum and one of the largest boys' homes in Germany.[1] Ehret notes that many occult symbols and chambers were reconstructed and alleges the castle continued to serve as an occult centre where satanic rituals are performed to the present, raising unanswered questions about the presence of thousands of parentless young boys at the same location.[1]

## Postwar occult revival

Colonel [[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]] made Wewelsburg the centre of a postwar occult Nazi revival, performing "The Wewelsburg Working" in the Hall of the Dead / "Walhalla" of the North Tower on 19 October 1982, explicitly invoking Himmler's conception of the castle as the "Mittelpunkt der Welt."[1] Occult writer Peter Levenda records that Aquino visited in the early eighties, was impressed by Himmler's still partially intact occult fantasy, and performed a ritual designed to unleash the power of Wewelsburg on the rest of the world.[1]

## Headquarters of the Nazi occult priesthood

Matthew Ehret describes the occult secret society shaping the Nazi high command — the [[ariosophy|Ariosophist]] and [[thule-society|Thule Society]] priesthood descended from [[helena-blavatsky|Helena Blavatsky]]'s [[theosophy|Theosophy]] — as having organized a central headquarters at Wewelsburg Castle, whose beliefs, Templar revival, and satanic practices he reserved for a later installment of the series.[2]
