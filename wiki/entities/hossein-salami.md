# Hossein Salami

General Hossein Salami was the commander in chief of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]], Iran's Revolutionary Guard. Iranian state media reported that he was killed in an Israeli strike in the early hours of Friday, June 13, 2025 (local time), and Israeli military leaders reported his death on the evening of June 12.[1]

## Death in the Israeli strikes on Tehran

Salami was killed as part of a targeted Israeli military operation launched against Iran's capital, described by Israel as an effort to "roll back the Iranian threat to Israel's very survival."[1] Explosions were reported across Tehran as simultaneous raids struck nuclear and military sites as well as the homes of high-value targets with knowledge of the Iranian nuclear program; reporting indicated that "top Revolutionary Guard generals" and prominent scientists were killed alongside Salami.[1]

Israeli Defense Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] announced the strikes and declared a "special state of emergency" in Israel, warning of possible drone and missile retaliation against Israeli territory and civilians.[1] U.S. Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] stated that Israel had acted unilaterally and that the United States was "not involved in strikes against Iran."[1] Iran's Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ayatollah Ali Khamenei]] vowed that Israel would face "severe punishment" for the attack.[1]

## Context

Salami's killing came amid escalating tensions over stalled nuclear negotiations, during which Iranian Defense Minister General Aziz Nasirzadeh had warned that Iran could attack U.S. bases if talks with the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration broke down.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] framed the escalation as an information-war event whose on-the-ground footage was largely uncorroborated, and argued that [[israel|Israel]] was the aggressor in an unprovoked attack on a sovereign state.[1]

## Response to the March 2025 Yemen strikes

After President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] launched large-scale strikes on the [[houthis|Houthis]] in Yemen on March 15, 2025 and warned [[iran|Iran]] to halt support for the group, Salami — top commander of Iran's Revolutionary Guards — reacted on Sunday local time by saying the Houthis are independent and take their own strategic and operational decisions.[2] "We warn our enemies that Iran will respond decisively and destructively if they take their threats into action," he told state media.[2]
