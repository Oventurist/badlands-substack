# Jon Voight

Jon Voight is an American actor identified in the Badlands Media essay A New Hollywood as one of three figures recently appointed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as "ambassadors to Hollywood," alongside [[sylvester-stallone|Sylvester Stallone]] and [[mel-gibson|Mel Gibson]].

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats the appointment as evidence that Trump regards Hollywood as redeemable rather than irredeemably hostile: "Why would he do this unless he thought Hollywood has been and could be again an important part of what makes America America?"
