# Getty Family

The Getty family is described in Badlands reporting as a highly influential California dynasty whose members were associated with the [[jesuits|Jesuit]]-run [[st-ignatius-college-preparatory|St. Ignatius College Prep]] in San Francisco.[1]

The family appears in the account of the San Francisco clergy abuse records as part of the reason those documents were politically radioactive. The files that District Attorney [[terence-hallinan|Terence Hallinan]] extracted from the [[archdiocese-of-san-francisco|Archdiocese of San Francisco]] in 2002 touched on well-connected institutions at the heart of the California power structure, including the school from which both [[jerry-brown|Jerry Brown]] and the Getty family hailed.[1] After [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] replaced Hallinan as District Attorney, the effort to release the documents was ended and no priest was prosecuted.[1]

## Patronage of Gavin Newsom

The family's fortune also underwrote the political ascent of [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]], whose life was profoundly influenced by his interactions with the affluent elite of San Francisco and particularly by the Gettys.[2] Newsom's father, [[william-newsom|William Newsom, Jr.]], served as financial consigliere to the heirs of the Getty Oil fortune; in one act of ingratiation he personally delivered a ransom to the Italian mafia in exchange for the release of an abducted grandchild of [[j-paul-getty|J. Paul Getty]], and he later successfully advocated a change in California law expanding the beneficiaries' entitlements to the Getty trust fund.[2]

As a result of his father's allegiance, the younger Newsom was treated as an almost adopted member of the family, accompanying the Gettys on luxurious vacations and African excursions, and after college he and childhood companion Billy Getty established a prosperous hospitality enterprise encompassing resorts, wineries and nightclubs.[2] In the mid-1990s Mayor [[willie-brown|Willie Brown]], seeking to gain favor with the Getty family, appointed Newsom to a local traffic commission and then to a vacancy on the city's board of supervisors.[2] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] encouraged readers interested in the clandestine side of American politics to study the Getty family history directly.[2]
