# Hugh Hewitt

Hugh Hewitt is a conservative American radio host whose program serves as a frequent venue for Republican political figures.[1]

## Trump interview, October 2024

On Monday, October 7, 2024 — the anniversary of the Hamas attacks on Israel that killed more than 1,200 people and triggered a war that has killed tens of thousands of Palestinians — [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] phoned into Hewitt's show.[1] Asked whether Israel could fully recover from its war with Hamas, Trump said the country "would be good," then argued that Israel "ha[s] to get smart about Trump, because they don't back me," adding that he "did more for Israel than anybody" and that the relationship was "not reciprocal."[1] Trump also told Hewitt he would support Israel striking back at Iran following an Iranian missile attack the previous week, while speculating that a deal with Tehran might be in the works because "Iran's not looking so good."[1]
