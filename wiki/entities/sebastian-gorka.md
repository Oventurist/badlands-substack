# Sebastian Gorka

Sebastian Gorka is a White House official who serves as senior director for counterterrorism at the National Security Council in the second [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1]

## Comments on the 2025 US–EU trade deal

In late July 2025, Gorka publicly characterized the [[european-union|European Union]] as having "bent at the knee" before President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after Brussels accepted a sweeping trade agreement imposing a 15% tariff on most EU exports to the United States, alongside pledges to invest $600 billion in the US economy and purchase $750 billion in American energy over three years.[1] Speaking to Newsmax, Gorka said that even for someone who had known the president for a decade, it was hard to believe "that the whole European Union bent at the knee of America First and said: 'You got us, President Trump, and we are going to surrender to a 15% tariff.'"[1] He framed the agreement as a geopolitical victory for Washington.[1]

## "Imminent" Trump–Putin meeting claim (May 2025)

In May 2025, Gorka — described as a deputy assistant to the president — told a security summit organized by Politico that a meeting between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] to help finalize a Russia–Ukraine peace agreement was "imminent."[2] "Deals are all about timing," he said. "When the time is right, that's when the president is in the room with Putin."[2] He did not elaborate or provide further details about a possible meeting.[2]

The remarks came while Trump was touring the Middle East and had mulled travelling to [[turkey|Türkiye]] on Friday "if something happened," as Moscow's and Kiev's delegations were expected to hold discussions there following Putin's suggestion of resuming the Istanbul talks broken off three years earlier.[2] [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]] said he would personally fly to Türkiye and demanded that Putin do the same; the Kremlin replied that the Russian president had no plan to travel, and Trump then said Putin had no reason to go since he himself had not committed to attending.[2]
