# Three Mile Island Nuclear Power Plant

Three Mile Island is a shuttered nuclear power plant in Pennsylvania whose owner announced in September 2024 that it planned to restart the reactor so that [[microsoft|Microsoft]] could purchase the output to supply its data centers.[1]

## Role in the AI energy build-out

The restart plan was cited alongside October 2024 announcements by [[amazon|Amazon]] and [[google|Google]] that they were investing in [[small-modular-reactor|small modular reactors]], as major technology companies sought carbon-free electricity to meet demand growth driven by data centers and [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]].[1] All three firms had earlier invested in solar and wind generation and said they now needed additional firm, low-emission capacity to meet both load growth and their own emissions commitments.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the Three Mile Island restart and the parallel SMR announcements as signs that the technology sector's energy strategy was being reorganized under pressure rather than by design.[1]

## Microsoft power purchase and grid queue

On Friday, September 20, 2024, [[microsoft|Microsoft]] agreed to buy power from the shuttered Three Mile Island plant, intending to begin using the electricity by 2028 to run data centers; the reactor's output is enough to supply roughly 700,000 homes.[2] Pennsylvania Governor [[josh-shapiro|Josh Shapiro]] wrote to the leaders of [[pjm-interconnection|PJM Interconnection]] urging that the reactor "be allowed to come online as quickly as possible rather than waiting in the queue as if they were an entirely new development," warning that slow grid interconnection could delay the restart by years.[2] PJM replied that it was developing a "fast track" system to prioritize some electricity projects.[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the sudden establishment enthusiasm for an American nuclear renaissance as a "Switch Theory" signal, arguing that the Deep State was not converting to nuclear power but recognizing that it was coming, that the Green New Deal had been circumvented by an energy plan grounded in military disclosure and advancement through contractors operating as civilians, and that adversaries were now attempting to front-run and corner the emerging market.[2]

## Microsoft–Constellation restart deal (September 2024)

In late September 2024, [[microsoft]] and [[constellation-energy]] unveiled a $1.6 billion power purchase agreement to restart a dormant reactor at Three Mile Island in 2028, supplying electricity for AI data center operations.[3] The deal was reported alongside the [[department-of-energy]] loan backing the restart of the [[palisades-nuclear-plant]] in Michigan as evidence that a new U.S. nuclear age was dawning after years of false starts, driven by data centers' insatiable thirst for energy.[3] Badlands commentary placed the announcement within a broader [[nuclear-renaissance]] and a "convergence of nuclear narratives" in which federal energy and defense initiatives were rebuilding nuclear capacity while media output sustained a parallel fear campaign.[3]
