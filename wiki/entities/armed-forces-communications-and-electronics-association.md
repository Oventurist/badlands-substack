# Armed Forces Communications and Electronics Association

The Armed Forces Communications and Electronics Association (AFCEA) is a professional association linking military, government and industry in the fields of communications, intelligence and information technology. It publishes *SIGNAL Media*.[1]

Badlands Media cites AFCEA's coverage of the [[iarpa|IARPA]] wearables program, which stated that "SMART ePANTS could revolutionize the Internet of Things by collecting data to help intelligence, medical and sports communities." The article treats this framing as an example of how [[smart-clothing|smart clothing]] surveillance capability is presented to the public in benign, health-and-fitness terms.[1] The underlying program is [[smart-epants|SMART ePANTS]].[1]
