# Yury Pivovarov

Yury Pivovarov is Ukraine's ambassador to [[senegal|Senegal]], whose social media conduct in mid-2024 triggered a diplomatic backlash across West Africa.[1]

After [[main-directorate-of-intelligence|GUR]] spokesman [[andrey-yusov|Andrey Yusov]] said Ukrainian intelligence had helped plan an ambush on [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] contractors and Malian soldiers in northern [[mali|Mali]], the Ukrainian embassy in Dakar shared the interview on its Facebook page with a caption by Pivovarov that appeared to signal further actions targeting Wagner; the video was subsequently deleted.[1] Senegal's Ministry of African Integration and Foreign Affairs said it was "surprised" by the publication of what it called a propaganda video accompanied by the ambassador's unequivocal support for a terrorist attack carried out between 25 and 27 July 2024 by Tuareg rebels and members of the Support Group for Islam and Muslims ([[jnim|JNIM]]) against the Armed Forces of Mali, an attack it said caused significant civilian casualties.[1] [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]]'s Foreign Ministry viewed Pivovarov's words as a justification of terrorism.[1]
