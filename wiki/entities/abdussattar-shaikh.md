# Abdussattar Shaikh

Dr. Abdussattar Shaikh was an [[fbi|FBI]] informant in San Diego who shared his home with two of the September 11, 2001 hijackers, Nawaf al-Hazmi and Khalid al-Mihdhar.[1] He had been recruited as an informant in 1994, according to his handler, and was presented in the 9/11 Commission Report as "an apparently law-abiding citizen with friendly, long-standing contacts among local police and FBI personnel."[1]

## Unsealed civil-suit allegations

Newly unsealed evidence reported in September 2024 in litigation brought by plaintiffs suing [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] over its alleged role in the attacks accused Shaikh of having been "co-opted" by Saudi intelligence.[1] Plaintiffs contended that Shaikh was "hired" by Saudi intelligence agent Omar al-Bayoumi to house the two hijackers.[1] At a July 31 court hearing, a plaintiffs' lawyer argued that "it was a plan that had been carefully and meticulously developed by Bayoumi in conjunction with other Saudi officials," according to reporting by the Florida Bulldog.[1]

The allegation, circulated in Badlands coverage as a bonus item, is treated within the Truth Community as evidence that a federal informant played a key operational role in the attacks.[1]
