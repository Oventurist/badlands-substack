# Scott Perry

Scott Perry is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Pennsylvania who was placed under [[fbi|FBI]] physical surveillance in 2022 as part of the bureau's [[arctic-frost]] investigation into the 2020 election.[1]

## Arctic Frost surveillance

Unclassified records reviewed by the New York Post showed that the FBI surveilled Perry at his congressional office and at his private residence roughly a month before seizing his cell phone and demanding information for a sprawling probe into 2020 election interference.[1] The files formed part of Arctic Frost, an investigation that targeted nearly 100 Republican-aligned entities in the wake of Trump's 2020 electoral loss, and showed that Perry was trailed by bureau agents in July and August 2022.[1]

One FBI document stated that the "[g]oal from surveillance is to get a pattern of life, identify his vehicle, and possibly identify a location to serve him with a SW [seizure warrant]."[1] Agents watched for Perry and kept log notes on July 25 at his home just south of Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, and at two workplaces in Washington, D.C.; on August 4 the congressman was surveilled at a recreation center in the capital.[1] The records were first obtained by House Judiciary Committee Chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] as part of his oversight of the probe, which the newly uncovered FBI files described as a "sensitive election law investigation."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that her first reaction was that surveillance of a sitting congressman is unsurprising, arguing that the agencies surveil anyone they wish and that it is standard practice for those they are allegedly blackmailing, with the FBI operating as "the muscle for the criminal cartel that runs our government."[1] She noted that Perry and Jordan nonetheless appeared surprised and outraged, comparing the episode to earlier controversies in which official outrage proved most authentic when the officials themselves were the subject.[1]
