# Cyrus Hashemi

Cyrus Hashemi was an Iranian arms exporter and one of the "Hashemi brothers" named in U.S. arms-smuggling investigations of the early 1980s.[1]

Federal investigations and contemporaneous reporting indicate that [[j-stanley-pottinger|J. Stanley Pottinger]], a former Assistant Attorney General who shared office space with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], assisted figures such as Hashemi in circumventing U.S. embargoes on Iran using shell companies and phony invoices.[1] Those methods aligned with the covert pipelines that supplied Iran and funded the Nicaraguan Contras during the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] period.[1] FBI surveillance tapes recorded in 1980 captured discussions of circumventing embargoes through overseas fronts.[1]
