# Minnesota Anti-War Committee

The Minnesota Anti-War Committee is an activist organization that helps organize anti-war and pro-Palestinian demonstrations in the Minneapolis–St. Paul area.[1]

## October 2023 Minneapolis demonstration

The committee helped organize a pro-Palestinian rally held in [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]] on Sunday, October 22, 2023, near the intersection of Hennepin Avenue and Lyndale Avenue.[1] The Minneapolis Police Department received several reports of disturbances around the demonstration at approximately 3:23 p.m., with various witnesses reporting that a vehicle drove through the crowd and one person reporting the sound of a gunshot.[1] No injuries were reported, no victims came forward, and no arrests were made, with police stating that follow-up investigations were under way.[1]

The committee announced a further protest for the following Wednesday afternoon outside the St. Paul office of Representative Betty McCollum.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Burning Bright used the demonstration to argue that the activist coalition which mobilized during the 2020 unrest following the death of George Floyd was now rallying around Hamas-related causes, placing it at odds with the administration it had helped install.[1]
