# Michele Bachmann

Michele Bachmann was a member of Congress from Minnesota who in 2012 publicly demanded that the five national security agencies investigate the infiltration of the United States government by the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]].[1] Badlands Media presents her as one of the few officials who recognized the threat at the time.[1]

## The 2012 letters

Bachmann and four members of the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence and the House Judiciary Committee sent letters to the inspectors general of the Department of Defense, the [[state-department|Department of State]], the Department of Justice, the Department of Homeland Security, and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, warning about Muslim Brotherhood infiltration of the U.S. government.[1] She specifically named [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s closest aide and the wife of former Representative Anthony Weiner.[1]

In her letter, Bachmann questioned how Abedin had been able to receive a security clearance despite documented family connections to the extremist Muslim Brotherhood, writing that she was "particularly interested in exactly how... she was able to avoid being disqualified for a security clearance."[1]

## Backlash

Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and others roundly criticized Bachmann for raising the question, a defense of Abedin that Badlands Media characterizes as protecting "the enemy from within."[1] The episode was referenced directly in Q drop #58 of November 2, 2017, which recounted the letters and asked "Why is this relevant? Who took an undisclosed trip to SA? What was the purpose of a f2f v phone call?"[1] The article treats her warnings as an early, ignored alarm about what Q would later summarize as "infiltration, not invasion."[1]
