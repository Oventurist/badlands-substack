# Fan Yifei

Fan Yifei is a former deputy governor of China's central bank who was sentenced to death for bribery in October 2024, with a two-year reprieve.[1] His sentencing was cited alongside that of former [[bank-of-china|Bank of China]] chairman [[liu-liange|Liu Liange]] as part of the same wave of high-profile financial-sector prosecutions.[1]

## Context

Both sentences were reported as products of the widespread anti-corruption effort ordered by Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]], which has targeted the country's roughly $60 trillion financial sector.[1] The suspended nature of such sentences means that, absent further offenses during the reprieve period, they are typically commuted to life imprisonment.[1]

Badlands Media commentary treated the pattern of severe punishment for corrupt Chinese bankers as a data point supporting its [[sovereign-alliance|sovereign alliance]] framing of global politics.[1]
