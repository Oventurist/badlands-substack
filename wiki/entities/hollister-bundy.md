# Hollister Bundy

Hollister Bundy (Yale class of 1909) was a member of [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] who served as special assistant to Secretary of War [[henry-stimson|Henry Stimson]] and as Stimson's point man in the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] for the [[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]].[1]

His two sons, [[william-bundy|William Bundy]] (Yale 1939) and [[mcgeorge-bundy|McGeorge Bundy]] (Yale 1940), were likewise initiated into the Order and became highly active in governmental and foundation affairs; the Bundy family is described by conspiracy researchers cited in the article as one of particular importance.[1] From their positions in the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] and the [[state-department|State Department]], and as Special Assistants to Presidents Kennedy and Johnson, the brothers exercised significant impact on the flow of information and intelligence during the Vietnam War.[1] [[antony-sutton|Antony Sutton]] uses the Whitney–Stimson–Bundy succession as his central illustration of the Order's "vertical chain" of influence.[1]
