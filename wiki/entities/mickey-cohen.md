# Mickey Cohen

Mickey Cohen is described by Erik Carlson as the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]] figure who ran Hollywood under [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Role in the article

Cohen's principal act in the essay is a piece of stagecraft: his men are said to have staged the shooting of the car belonging to the producer of *[[the-godfather|The Godfather]]*, creating the impression that the Mafia did not want the film made.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) Carlson argues the ruse is transparent — had the Mafia genuinely opposed the film, it would never have been made, nor would its sequels, and the producer would not have survived a shotgun attack unscathed.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

The purpose he ascribes to the operation is concealment: the film's success let the Jewish Mafia recede into the shadows while the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] absorbed the public identity of organized crime.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Gladio-era context

Mickey Cohen (1914–1976) is listed in the Badlands Media account of [[operation-gladio]]'s funding among the Jewish mobsters operating in the United States in the early 1900s, with his base in Los Angeles.[2] The article groups him with Louis "Pretty" Amberg, Moses Annenberg, Abe Bernstein of the Purple Gang, [[meyer-lansky]] and [[bugsy-siegel]], noting that while they ran the same rackets as the Italian mafia they were also used by large business enterprises that needed help with dock workers, newspapers and bankers.[2]
