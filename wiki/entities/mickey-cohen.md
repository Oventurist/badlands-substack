# Mickey Cohen

Mickey Cohen is identified in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay as the gangster who ran Hollywood under [[meyer-lansky|Meyer Lansky]] within the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]].[1]

## Alleged role in The Godfather

Carlson writes that Cohen's men staged the shooting of the car belonging to the producer of [[the-godfather|The Godfather]] in order to create the impression that the Mafia did not want the film made.[1] He argues that if the Mafia had genuinely opposed the picture, neither it nor the later films would have been made, and the producer would not have survived a shotgun attack unscathed.[1] In Carlson's reading the episode was part of a publicity and misdirection effort that ultimately allowed the Jewish Mafia to recede from public view while the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] absorbed the public identity of organized crime.[1]
