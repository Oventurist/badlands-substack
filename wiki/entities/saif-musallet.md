# Saif Musallet

Saif Musallet was a Palestinian-American killed by Israeli settlers in the West Bank in July 2025, whose death became a focal point for shifting coverage of settler violence in American conservative media.[1]

## Coverage and significance

In what The Times of Israel described as a sign of shift, the [[one-america-news-network|One America News Network]] — a network generally aligned with the American right — aired a segment on Musallet's killing and on the wider phenomenon of unchecked Israeli settler violence in the West Bank.[1] The network's decision to cover a subject once largely ignored in conservative circles was read as evidence of a growing willingness among MAGA Republicans to criticize Israel.[1]

The segment was anchored by former Florida congressman [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]], [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s original pick for attorney general, who withdrew his nomination amid mounting scandals.[1] Gaetz referred to the West Bank by its biblical name of Judea and Samaria while excoriating the conduct of the Israeli settlers who live there.[1]

Badlands commentary placed the killing alongside the Israeli refusal of visas to American Christian groups and the shelling of Gaza's Holy Family Church as evidence that Christians and Palestinians alike were being targeted in the Holy Land, and argued that Evangelical supporters of Zionism would have to confront an inconvenient reality about the modern State of Israel.[1]
