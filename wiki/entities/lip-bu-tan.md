# Lip-Bu Tan

Lip-Bu Tan is the chief executive officer of the American chipmaker [[intel|Intel]].[1]

## US government equity stake

On August 22, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that the United States government had taken a 10% stake in Intel, saying he had negotiated the deal directly with Tan, whom he described as "the Highly Respected Chief Executive Officer of the Company."[1] Writing on Truth Social, Trump said "the United States of America now fully owns and controls 10% of INTEL, a Great American Company that has an even more incredible future," and that "the United States paid nothing for these Shares, and the Shares are now valued at approximately $11 Billion Dollars."[1] At various points during the day Trump valued the government's shares at $10 billion and $11 billion without providing supporting details.[1]

[[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] placed the Intel transaction alongside the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]]'s acquisition of a 40% stake in [[mp-materials|MP Materials]], arguing that the United States had federalized both rare earth minerals and the products made from them.[1]
