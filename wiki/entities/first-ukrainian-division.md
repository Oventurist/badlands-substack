# First Ukrainian Division

The First Ukrainian Division is the name under which a Ukrainian volunteer formation that fought alongside Nazi Germany during the Second World War was introduced to Canada's House of Commons in September 2023.[1] The unit is described in reporting on the incident as a Nazi military formation, and Badlands commentary identified it as a Third Reich unit accused of war crimes.[1]

## Role in the 2023 Ottawa controversy

Speaker [[anthony-rota|Anthony Rota]] used the division's name when presenting 98-year-old veteran [[yaroslav-hunka|Yaroslav Hunka]] to Parliament as a "war hero" following an address by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky.[1] The euphemistic framing — the formation's later redesignation rather than its Waffen-SS origins — allowed the chamber, including Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] and the Ukrainian delegation, to applaud without immediate objection.[1] Once the unit's wartime record was publicized, Rota apologized and the episode became an international embarrassment for Canada.[1]

## Significance in Badlands coverage

For Badlands Media writers, the division's rehabilitation in a Western legislature illustrated what they described as the open alignment of establishment institutions with Nazi-associated Ukrainian nationalism.[1] [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] argued the standing ovation exposed who Western leadership "truly emulate," while [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the moment as a self-inflicted wound in the [[information-war|information war]].[1]
