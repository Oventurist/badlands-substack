# Cybernews

Cybernews is a cybersecurity research and news outlet whose researchers investigated and publicized the [[2025-16-billion-credential-leak|2025 leak of sixteen billion login credentials]] tied to [[apple|Apple]], [[facebook|Facebook]], [[google|Google]] and other services.[1]

## Role in the 2025 credential leak

Cybernews researchers identified "30 exposed datasets containing from tens of millions to over 3.5 billion records each," and reported that all but one had never previously been disclosed, meaning the exposed data was considered entirely new.[1] They characterized the incident in stark terms: "This is not just a leak – it's a blueprint for mass exploitation," warning that the credentials were ground zero for phishing and account-takeover attacks and constituted "fresh, weaponizable intelligence at scale."[1] Their findings were amplified in a *Forbes* report and drove warnings from Google and the [[fbi|FBI]].[1]
