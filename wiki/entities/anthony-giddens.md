# Anthony Giddens

**Anthony Giddens** is a sociologist cited by [[iain-mcgilchrist|Iain McGilchrist]] as the originator of the concept of [[disembedding-mechanisms|disembedding mechanisms]], the modern processes whose effect is "to separate things from their context and ourselves from the uniqueness of place," what Giddens calls *locale*.[1] In the formulation quoted from McGilchrist's *[[the-master-and-his-emissary|The Master and His Emissary]]*, real things and experiences under these conditions are replaced by symbolic tokens, and expert systems replace local know-how.[1]

## Giddens on the media

Giddens is further quoted for the claim that the media "promote fragmentation by a random juxtaposition of items of information, as well as permitting the intrusion of distant events into everyday consciousness," which McGilchrist treats as another aspect of the decontextualization of modern life and of the resulting loss of meaning in the experienced world.[1]

## Use in Badlands Media commentary

In the March 2024 essay "How Your Western Brain Exposes You to 5G Warfare," [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] adopted Giddens' two-part account — decontextualization followed by random juxtaposition — as the analytical core of his argument that modern information consumption trains the population into left-hemisphere cognition and thereby prepares the ground for the [[cognitive-battlefield|cognitive battlefield]] of [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[1]
