# BAE Systems

**BAE Systems** is a British defense contractor.[1]

## Employment of Gina Haspel

BAE Systems appears in Badlands Media's [[purple-revolution|Purple Revolution]] analysis as the post-government employer of former [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] Director [[gina-haspel|Gina Haspel]].[1] The article emphasizes the point for effect — "the former CIA Director now works for a foreign defense contractor" — presenting it as evidence that many holders of senior American office ultimately serve the interests of foreign nations.[1] The claim is tied to the author's wider thesis that the Cabal is headquartered in the City of London, a thesis also supported in the article by Haspel's two tours as CIA station chief in London and by the assertion that the 2016 "Russia, Russia, Russia" narrative originated there.[1]
