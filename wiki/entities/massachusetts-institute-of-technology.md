# Massachusetts Institute of Technology

The Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) is a research university in Cambridge, Massachusetts, whose physics faculty features prominently in US plasma and fusion energy research.[1]

## December 2025 killing of an MIT professor

MIT physics professor [[nuno-loureiro|Nuno F. G. Loureiro]] was fatally shot at his home in Brookline, Massachusetts, on Monday, December 15, 2025.[1] Federal authorities attributed the killing to [[claudio-manuel-neves-valente|Claudio Manuel Neves Valente]], the suspect in the [[brown-university-shooting|Brown University shooting]], who was found dead by suicide in a New Hampshire storage unit on December 18, 2025.[1] Leah Foley, the US Attorney for the District of Massachusetts, announced the connection between the two cases.[1]

Badlands commentators framed the killing as "fusion-adjacent," juxtaposing the loss of an MIT plasma physicist with the same week's $6 billion [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media]]–[[tae-technologies|TAE Technologies]] fusion merger and questioning who stood to benefit from the professor's death.[1]

## Killing of Professor Nuno Loureiro, December 2025

[[nuno-loureiro|Nuno F. G. Loureiro]], a 47-year-old physics and nuclear science professor whom MIT hired in 2016 and who in 2024 became director of the university's [[plasma-science-and-fusion-center|Plasma Science and Fusion Center]], was shot multiple times at his home in Brookline, Massachusetts on the night of December 15, 2025 and died early the next morning.[2] The center is one of the largest laboratories at MIT, employing over 250 researchers across seven buildings and focused on clean energy technology.[2] As of December 17 no motive had been disclosed and no suspects were in custody, while Israeli officials were said to be examining unverified intelligence suggesting an Iranian connection.[2]
