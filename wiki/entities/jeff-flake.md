# Jeff Flake

Jeff Flake is a former Republican senator from Arizona who served alongside [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and was one of the eight senators who crafted the 2013 [[gang-of-eight-immigration-bill|Gang of Eight immigration bill]].[1]

## Role in the Trump–McCain feud

Flake appears in the early chronology of the feud between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and McCain. On July 10, 2015, *The Arizona Republic* published a story headlined "McCain, Flake want no part of Trump's Phoenix rally," pairing the two Arizona senators in public distancing from Trump's presidential campaign shortly after its launch.[1] Trump campaign sources subsequently confirmed that Trump was aware of the article, which quoted McCain criticizing Trump's remarks about Mexicans.[1]

## Immigration position

As a Gang of Eight member, Flake co-sponsored legislation that, according to the reporting cited by Badlands, would have granted amnesty to 11.5 million illegal immigrants and cost taxpayers trillions of dollars while containing no requirement that a border fence actually be built.[1] The Badlands account groups Flake with the Arizona Republican establishment that Trump targeted on immigration during his 2015 Phoenix campaign appearances.[1]
