# Dagestan

Dagestan is the southernmost province of the Russian Federation, located in the North Caucasus on the Caspian Sea. It is a predominantly Muslim region with a long history of separatist and militant violence, turbulence that has been further fanned by Russia's war in Ukraine, where ethnic minorities from the region have been disproportionately mobilized to fight.[1]

Alongside its Muslim majority, Dagestan is home to a substantial Jewish community and an Orthodox Christian population, a religious plurality reflected in the mix of churches, synagogues and mosques found in its cities.[1] Its regional capital is Makhachkala; the historic Caspian city of Derbent lies roughly 120 kilometers (75 miles) to the south.[1]

## June 2024 attacks

On Sunday, June 23, 2024, gunmen opened fire on places of worship in Derbent and Makhachkala in what appeared to be a coordinated assault, killing at least 15 police officers and four civilians, including an Orthodox priest.[1] Sergey Melikov, head of the Dagestan Republic, said at least six "militants" were also killed in the response to attacks on churches, synagogues and police posts.[1] Video and photographs showed heavy flames and smoke pouring from a synagogue in Derbent, while footage from Makhachkala showed black-clad unidentified gunmen firing on a police car in the street.[1]

No group claimed responsibility. Russian law enforcement agencies told the state news agency TASS that the attackers were "adherents of an international terrorist organization."[1] The assault came three months after [[isis-k|ISIS-K]] claimed the Crocus City Hall attack in Moscow, which killed more than 140 people and ranked among Russia's deadliest terrorist atrocities in years.[1]
