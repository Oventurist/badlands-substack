# Mahdi Bray

Mahdi Bray is an American Muslim activist who serves as national director of the [[american-muslim-alliance|American Muslim Alliance]].[1]

## November 2023 Washington ceasefire rally

Bray was among the speakers and organizers associated with the "National March on Washington: Free Palestine" held in the United States capital on Saturday, November 4, 2023.[1] Speaking for the demonstrators, he stated the movement's core demand plainly: "What we want and what we demand is a ceasefire now."[1]

The march took place while the Biden administration was rejecting demands to join calls for a blanket ceasefire in Gaza, and while thousands had been killed in the Israeli offensive that followed the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]] by [[hamas|Hamas]].[1] Protesters carried placards reading "Palestinian Lives Matter," "Let Gaza Live" and "Their blood is on your hands," and the event ranked among the largest gatherings for any cause in Washington in recent years.[1]
