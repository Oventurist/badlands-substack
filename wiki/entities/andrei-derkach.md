# Andrei Derkach

Andrei Derkach is a Ukrainian politician and businessman who has been sanctioned by the United States government in connection with activity supporting the Russian regime.[1]

## Role in U.S. enforcement actions

Derkach was named in Badlands commentary as one of the most significant targets of the United States campaign against smuggling and money laundering networks supporting Russia, alongside [[viktor-vekselberg|Viktor Vekselberg]], [[roman-abramovich|Roman Abramovich]] and [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]].[1] That campaign, reported by the [[organized-crime-and-corruption-reporting-project|OCCRP]], included the seizure of more than $500 million in assets over the preceding year and indictments of more than 30 individuals and two corporate entities on charges such as sanctions evasion and money laundering.[1]

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] described these figures collectively as "members of the Swamp, the international Swamp," and questioned whether the enforcement effort was aimed at Russia and its enablers or at that transnational network.[1]
