# George Shultz

George Shultz was the American official identified by [[john-perkins|John Perkins]] as the chief economic hit man of the postwar order.[1]

In 1971, as director of the Office of Management and Budget, Shultz orchestrated the removal of the U.S. dollar from the gold-reserve, fixed exchange rate system.[1] In the same year the Rothschild Inter-Alpha Group of banks was created to usher in a new age of globalization.[1] The floating of the dollar, in Ehret's account, ushered in a paradigm of consumerism, post-industrialism and deregulation that transformed once-productive western nations into speculative "post-truth" basket cases convinced that casino principles, bubbles and windmills were substitutes for agro-industrial economic practices.[1]
