# Tennessee

Tennessee is a U.S. state whose office of the Attorney General has taken a leading role in Republican-led legal challenges to environmental, social and corporate governance investing.[1]

## Litigation against BlackRock

In December 2023, Tennessee sued [[blackrock|BlackRock]], alleging that the asset manager breached state consumer protection laws by making "misleading" statements about its [[esg|ESG]] investment strategies.[1] The state's filing argued that BlackRock understated how far ESG considerations drive its investment decisions and how those considerations affect the financial performance and outlook of the companies in which it invests.[1] BlackRock rejected the allegation, saying it fully discloses its investment practices and proxy-voting approach.[1]

The case was brought under Attorney General [[jonathan-skrmetti|Jonathan Skrmetti]], who had earlier in 2023 demanded information from ten major asset managers on their climate-change policies and, with twenty other Republican state attorneys general, warned asset managers in March 2023 that their handling of environmental and social issues might breach fiduciary duties.[1]

## Political context

The Tennessee suit sits within a broader partisan divide over ESG: Democratic leaders including President Joe Biden have defended ESG investing — Biden used the first veto of his presidency to protect a federal ESG rule — while Republicans, many from energy-producing states, have mounted an increasingly organized challenge.[1]
