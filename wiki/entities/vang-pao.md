# Vang Pao

**Vang Pao** was a Laotian warlord allied with the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] during the Vietnam War, whose Hmong forces cultivated opium poppies as a generations-old tradition.[1]

Historians including [[alfred-mccoy|Alfred McCoy]] have argued that the CIA's airline, [[air-america|Air America]], became a conduit for moving opium and heroin precursors, with the profits helping to sustain anti-communist operations in Southeast Asia.[1] On page 318 of *The Politics of Heroin*, McCoy shows that [[usaid|USAID]] funded Vang Pao's own airline while purportedly aware that it would be used for transporting opium.[1]

The passage cited in the article records the CIA operative Tony Poe's charge that Vang Pao later used a DC-3 to fly heroin across southern Laos into central Vietnam, where the "number two guy to President Thieu would receive it"; Poe claimed that his opposition to the corruption led to his transfer from Long Tieng.[1] A former USAID employee is quoted as saying that officials involved realised any commercial activity at Long Tieng would involve opium, but supported the project anyway.[1]
