# Lauren King

**Lauren King** is a judge of the United States District Court who, on February 28, 2025, extended a temporary injunction blocking the Trump administration from withholding federal funds from institutions that provide gender-affirming care to transgender youth.[1]

King ruled that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive orders violate the Fifth Amendment's guarantee of equal protection under the law, writing that the orders would "prevent federally funded medical providers from providing necessary medical treatments to transgender youth that are completely unrelated to gender identity."[1] She illustrated the point by noting that a cisgender teenager could obtain puberty blockers from such a provider as part of cancer treatment, while a transgender teenager on the same cancer care plan could not.[1]

The underlying lawsuit was filed against the administration by Colorado, Minnesota, Oregon, and Washington, each led by Democratic attorneys general.[1]
