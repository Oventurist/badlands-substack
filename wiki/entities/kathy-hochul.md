# Kathy Hochul

Kathy Hochul is the Governor of New York. In the Badlands corpus she appears in connection with state legislation preserving New York's authority over vaccine policy in the face of federal changes under the Trump administration.

## 2026 vaccine legislation

The May 20, 2026 [[badlands-brief-311|Badlands Brief]] reports that Hochul signed two bills into law on Friday, May 15, 2026. One expands pharmacist authority to administer COVID-19 vaccines to children ages 2 to 18. The other requires health insurers to cover all vaccines recommended by the New York State Commissioner of Health, decoupling state policy from the recommendations of the [[advisory-committee-on-immunization-practices|Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices]], which had recently reduced routine recommendations from 17 diseases to 11. The bills also preserve state authority over school immunization schedules, newborn vaccination protocols, and related requirements.

Hochul and state health officials framed the measures as safeguarding public health tools against federal policy shifts and ensuring that New Yorkers retain broad access to immunizations. The laws followed earlier executive orders expanding vaccine administration. Critics quoted in the source argued the laws represent overreach or resistance to national policy adjustments, while other areas of federal–state public health cooperation continued.
