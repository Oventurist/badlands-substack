# Patrick Schiltz

Patrick Schiltz is the Chief Judge of the United States District Court for the District of [[minnesota|Minnesota]]. In January 2026 he became a central figure in the confrontation between the federal judiciary and federal immigration enforcement in [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]], as reported in the January 28, 2026 edition of the Badlands Brief.

## Contempt order against ICE leadership

Schiltz ordered acting [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] Director [[todd-lyons|Todd Lyons]] to appear in person in Minneapolis federal court on January 30, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. to show cause why he should not be held in contempt of court. The order arose from ICE's failure to comply with a January 14, 2026 directive requiring either a bond hearing or release within seven days for detainee [[juan-tobay-robles|Juan Tobay Robles]], who had been taken into custody by federal immigration agents earlier that month.

Schiltz characterized the violation as part of "one of dozens of court orders" ignored by ICE in recent weeks, writing that "The Court's patience is at an end... the extent of ICE's violation of court orders is likewise extraordinary, and lesser measures have been tried and failed." The Brief noted that the show-cause hearing could be canceled if Robles were released beforehand.

The order came amid escalating tension in Minneapolis over federal immigration operations, including the fatal shooting of [[alex-pretti|Alex Pretti]] by [[united-states-border-patrol|Border Patrol]] agents on January 24, 2026 during anti-ICE protests.
