# Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo

General Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo, known by the nickname "Hemedti" — glossed in Badlands coverage as "Little Mohamed" — is a Sudanese paramilitary commander associated with the [[rapid-support-forces|Rapid Support Forces]].[1] Badlands Media identified him as one of two men vying for control of the paramilitary group during the April 2023 fighting in [[sudan|Sudan]], the other being [[abdel-fattah-al-burhan|General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan]], head of the Sovereign Council and of the Sudanese military.[1]

## Moscow visit and Russian alignment

Badlands writer [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] highlighted Hemedti's February 23, 2022 visit to Moscow, noting that al-Burhan was conspicuously absent from the trip — a fact taken as evidence that the two generals may have had separate objectives and separate patrons.[1] DeLarme further observed that Hemedti's eight-day visit with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] coincided with the announcement of U.S. sanctions against Russia over Putin's "threatening actions" and "unprovoked aggression" in Ukraine, per a [[white-house|White House]] press release.[1]

## Proxy-war framing

The Badlands analysis treated Hemedti's Russian ties as central to the question of whose proxy the RSF represented after the group was reported to have seized a Sudanese bio-laboratory in Khartoum.[1] Russia's negotiated arrangement with Sudan for a naval base on the Red Sea coast was cited as a motive for outside powers to cultivate rival Sudanese factions.[1]
