# Richard Helms

Richard Helms was Director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] at the start of the Nixon presidency and is described in Badlands Media commentary as the official who blocked a sitting president from reviewing the agency's most sensitive files.[1]

## Conflict with Nixon

When [[richard-nixon|Richard Nixon]] took office in 1968, one of the first things he wanted to do was review the CIA's documents on the [[bay-of-pigs|Bay of Pigs]] and the assassination of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1] Helms was very reluctant to give Nixon access and, in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s account, essentially refused to allow the standing president to see them, even though such access was well within his rights as president.[1]

Carlson treats this refusal as evidence that the CIA understood Nixon was not its friend, and as the motive behind what he characterizes as the agency's badly botched [[watergate|Watergate]] break-in, run to remove Nixon from power.[1] George Bush Sr. is described as heavily involved in the plan to remove Nixon.[1]

## Succession

Nixon appointed [[william-colby|William Colby]] as the new Director of the CIA in 1973.[1] Colby's subsequent sworn testimony to the [[church-committee|Church Committee]] in 1975 exposed [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]], domestic surveillance, foreign assassination programs and [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]] — disclosures that led the agency to seek a loyalist successor in [[george-hw-bush|George H. W. Bush]] in 1976.[1]
