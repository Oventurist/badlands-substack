# Raul Castro

Raul Castro is a Cuban revolutionary and politician, the younger brother of [[fidel-castro|Fidel Castro]], who succeeded him as head of state and of the Communist Party of [[cuba|Cuba]] before formally ceding the presidency to [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Diaz-Canel]].

In the March 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], Raul Castro appears indirectly: his grandson, [[raulito-castro|Raulito Castro]], stood beside Diaz-Canel at the Havana press conference at which the Cuban government confirmed open negotiations with the Trump administration. The Brief notes that the long-standing neoconservative demand had been the expulsion of the Castro family from Cuba and possibly its prosecution for war crimes, making the family's reported role in a Washington-brokered succession a striking inversion of that position.
