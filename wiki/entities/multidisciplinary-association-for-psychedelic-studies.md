# Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies

The **Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies (MAPS)** is the organisation named in the March 29, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "Walking the Path: From Awakening to Integration" as the body founded by [[rick-doblin|Dr. Rick Doblin]].[1]

MAPS appears in the article only as the credential attached to Doblin when he is quoted that "Awareness is the seed; integration is the soil that lets it grow."[1] The reference nonetheless situates the essay's central term, [[psychological-integration|integration]], within the psychedelic-research tradition MAPS represents, where integration denotes the structured work of translating insight gained in a non-ordinary state into durable changes in ordinary life.[1] [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] applies that framing to [[spiritual-awakening|awakening]] more generally, arguing that the disorientation, grief and heightened sensitivity of an awakening require the same tending if they are to become a stable way of living rather than a cycle of euphoria and despair.[1]
