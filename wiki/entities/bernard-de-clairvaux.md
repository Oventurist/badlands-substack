# Bernard de Clairvaux

Bernard de Clairvaux is described by Matthew Ehret as the grand strategist who created the [[knights-templar|Templar]] cultists, and as an enemy of the Augustinian Platonist Peter Abelard.[1]

A figure of the Cistercian order, Clairvaux placed at least two popes into power during what the article calls his hyper-active lifetime.[1] He established the constitution of what Ehret terms a gnostic "Christian mercenary" cult that would soon control the majority of banking operations and inaugurate a new era of Christian-versus-Muslim and Christian-versus-Jewish religious violence shaping the centuries that followed.[1] The Templar fixation on Solomon's Temple — their headquarters sat beneath its presumed site — together with the Grail myth holding the royal family to be progeny of a marriage between Jesus and Mary Magdalene, is presented as the source of Rosicrucianism, modern masonry and political Zionism, and thus as an elemental cause of the present global crisis.[1]
