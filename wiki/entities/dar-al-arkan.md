# Dar Al Arkan

**Dar Al Arkan** is a Saudi Arabian real estate company described as a "mega-developer," and the parent of the majority-owned international subsidiary [[dar-global|Dar Global]].[1] Badlands coverage of the arrangement noted some ambiguity in press descriptions of the corporate structure, with one account describing Dar Al Arkan as a subsidiary of Dar Global and another describing Dar Global as the majority-owned international arm of Dar Al Arkan.[1]

## Deal with the Trump Organization

In 2022 Dar Al Arkan signed a licensing and management agreement with [[trump-organization|The Trump Organization]], announced publicly in November 2022, covering a resort project in Oman.[1] The resulting development, [[trump-international-oman|Trump International Oman]], is a $500 million villa, hotel, and golf project inside the AIDA development near Muscat with a projected opening in December 2028.[1] Under the deal the Trump Organization and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] earn brand-licensing fees plus additional fees for managing the hotel, golf course, and luxury villas to be launched over the following decade.[1] Trump's 2023 financial disclosure for calendar year 2022 recorded $5.35 million in licensing fees from the Oman golf project.[1] The Trump Organization team on the project is led by [[eric-trump|Eric Trump]].[1]
