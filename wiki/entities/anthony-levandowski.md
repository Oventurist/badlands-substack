# Anthony Levandowski

**Anthony Levandowski** is an American engineer, formerly a developer of driverless cars at Uber, who founded a non-profit religious organisation devoted to the creation of an artificial-intelligence deity.[1]

## Way of the Future

Levandowski established the organisation, called [[way-of-the-future|Way of the Future]], in September 2015, according to a report in *Wired* cited by the *Independent* in October 2017.[1] Its mission statement was "to develop and promote the realization of a Godhead based on artificial intelligence and through understanding and worship of the Godhead contribute to the betterment of society."[1]

## Musk's reaction

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] publicly objected, tweeting that Levandowski should be "on the list of people who should absolutely *not* be allowed to develop digital superintelligence."[1] Musk shared a VentureBeat article arguing that an AI god could emerge by 2042, write its own bible and be worshipped by humans, on the reasoning that people tend to trust and obey things that appear more powerful and worthy than themselves.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cited the episode within his broader warning about the direction of [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]].[1]
