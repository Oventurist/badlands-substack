# Teneo Holdings

Teneo Holdings is a multinational C-suite advisory firm and investment bank founded in June 2011 by [[doug-band|Doug Band]], [[declan-kelly|Declan Kelly]] and [[paul-keary|Paul Keary]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Badlands Media's "America for Sale — Part 2" treats Teneo as the commercial go-between in an alleged pay-for-play scheme linking corporate clients, the [[state-department|State Department]] under [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], and the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Origins

Former employees said planning for the joint firm began well before its incorporation: in 2009, several weeks before Kelly took an unpaid State Department post, he created his own private consulting firm and Keary created two others, all later merged under the Teneo umbrella.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The launch was delayed until 2011 because Kelly had signed a two-year non-compete agreement upon stepping down from FTI Consulting in July 2009.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The article argues Kelly used the intervening State Department years to build corporate relationships that later became Teneo accounts.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Clinton connections

Teneo employed numerous Hillary Clinton associates, including [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], and for a time retained [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] as "honorary chairman," giving clients rare access to the couple and their network of world leaders.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) With Hillary Clinton's State Department approval, Teneo paid Bill Clinton $2.5 million per year as a client and paid advisor to the firm and its founders.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Abedin earned a $15,000-a-month consulting fee from Teneo over a seven-month period while simultaneously drawing pay as a "special government employee" advising Clinton at the State Department.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) One project she worked on was a September 20, 2012 reception at the Essex House in New York City — days after the Benghazi attack — at which Bill Clinton, [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and former British Prime Minister Tony Blair appeared as speakers before potential Teneo clients.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Clients

Early and major accounts included Dow Chemical and Coca-Cola, relationships Kelly retained through FTI while serving at the State Department; Dow paid Kelly $2.4 million between August 2009 and January 2011.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Keary won the USA bid committee for the World Cup as a client in 2010 and later the Swiss bank [[ubs]], for whom Teneo helped arrange the "Revitalizing America" event series pairing UBS executive Bob McCann on stage with Bill Clinton and George W. Bush from May 2011.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

The article characterizes Teneo's status as an investment bank as significant, arguing that "investment banks come in real handy when you're trying to launder large sums of money."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)
