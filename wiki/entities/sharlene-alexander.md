# Sharlene Alexander

Sharlene Alexander is a Georgia election official and supporter of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] who was appointed to the Fayette County board of elections in February 2024.[1]

## Hand-count rule

Alexander introduced the rule, later passed by the pro-Trump majority of the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]], that would have required poll workers to hand-count the number of ballots cast.[1] The rule was blocked in October 2024 by Judge [[robert-mcburney|Robert McBurney]], who paused it pending further review on the grounds that mandatory hand-counting would create administrative chaos after early voting had already begun.[1]

Press accounts described Alexander as one of twelve so-called election deniers who had introduced more than thirty rules to the Georgia State Election Board since May 2024.[1] Badlands Media treated the coverage as an illustration of how the label "election denier" is applied to officials seeking additional procedural safeguards, and used the resulting ruling to argue that state election boards possess less real authority than the public is told.[1]
