# Brenda Lee Brown Armstrong

Brenda Lee Brown Armstrong, of Marina del Rey, California, aged 64 and also known as "Anika," is the individual identified by Badlands as the defendant in a Department of Justice voter fraud case announced on May 18, 2026.[1]

## Indictment

The DOJ release described the defendant as "a California woman who worked as a longtime signature collector for ballot initiatives" charged with paying individuals — including homeless people living in the Skid Row area of downtown Los Angeles — to register to vote.[1] The announcement came roughly two months after [[okeefe-media-group|O'Keefe Media Group]] published undercover footage of an LA registration drive involving money exchanged and fake names.[1]

## Speculation about cooperation

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] raised the question of whether Armstrong had "flipped" — cooperated with federal investigators — and whether the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|2026 Los Angeles mayoral election]] had functioned as a sting operation, given the sequence running from her indictment through the June primary to the federal [[skid-row-voter-fraud-investigation|Skid Row operation]].[1]
