# Brenda Lee Brown Armstrong

Brenda Lee Brown Armstrong, of Marina del Rey, California, aged 64 and also known as "Anika," is the California woman federally charged in May 2026 in connection with an alleged paid voter-registration scheme in [[los-angeles|Los Angeles]].

## Indictment

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that on May 18, 2026 the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced her indictment. The DOJ release described her as "a California woman who worked as a longtime signature collector for ballot initiatives" charged with paying individuals — including homeless people living in the [[skid-row|Skid Row]] area of downtown Los Angeles — to register to vote. The Brief situates the charge as following the March 2026 undercover footage published by [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] and the [[okeefe-media-group|O'Keefe Media Group]].

## Speculation in the Badlands framing

Writing after the [[fbi|FBI]]'s June 18 Skid Row operation, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] asks whether the [[2026-los-angeles-mayoral-election|Los Angeles mayoral election]] was itself a sting and whether Armstrong "flipped" — that is, cooperated with investigators — thereby seeding the wider probe. This is presented as an open question rather than an established fact.
