# Bryan Steil

Bryan Steil is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives who serves as Chairman of the Committee on House Administration, the panel with jurisdiction over federal election administration and the internal operations of the House.[1]

## Role in the ActBlue investigation

Steil is one of three committee chairmen — alongside House Judiciary Chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] and House Oversight Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] — who in June 2026 signed a letter to [[actblue|ActBlue]] CEO [[regina-wallace-jones|Regina Wallace-Jones]] warning that the fundraising platform could face [[contempt-of-congress|contempt of Congress]] proceedings for failing to comply with congressional subpoenas.[1] The subpoenas, issued on July 22, 2025, sought documents concerning alleged misconduct at ActBlue, retaliation against whistleblowers, and the mass departure of the organization's legal team after it ended voluntary cooperation with the committees.[1]

The chairmen argued that ActBlue's continued withholding of records — including a privilege log filed on June 5, 2026 covering at least 420 additional documents — was obstructing Congress's ability to investigate possible foreign interference in political donations and to consider election security legislation.[1]

Earlier in June 2026, Wallace-Jones appeared before Steil's Committee on House Administration and declined to answer questions about allegations that ActBlue facilitated foreign campaign donations to Democratic candidates in federal elections, invoking the Fifth Amendment.[1]
