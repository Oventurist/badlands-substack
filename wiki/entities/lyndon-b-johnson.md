# Lyndon B. Johnson

Lyndon B. Johnson was the 36th President of the United States and, in Badlands Media commentary, is treated less as a civil-rights reformer than as the architect of a policy machinery designed to divide Americans along racial lines.[1]

## Affirmative Action

Johnson put affirmative action into effect by executive order in 1965.[1] Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that Johnson "may be the most racist president America has ever known, at least in modern times," and rejected the premise that Johnson genuinely cared about black Americans.[1] In this reading, [[affirmative-action|affirmative action]] was not a remedial measure but a mechanism "designed to create hate, doubt and division," with [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]] later described as "Affirmative Action on steroids."[1]

## Alleged knowledge of the Kennedy assassination

Carlson wrote that, according to some accounts, Johnson and Texas oil tycoon [[h-l-hunt|H.L. Hunt]] were aware of the true motivations behind the JFK assassination, though he declined to develop the claim, calling it "a story for another day."[1]

## Cited as a successor puppet (December 2022)

In "The War of Stories" (December 30, 2022), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] invoked Johnson in his summary of the [[patriot-pendulum|Patriot Pendulum]]: "For every Kennedy that was taken from us, we had a Lyndon Johnson to take his place."[2] The line introduced a broader account of American presidential successions — Reagan to Bush, then the Clintons, the Bushes again and Obama — which he described as figures "groomed in succession and for succession" on behalf of interests operating behind them.[2]
