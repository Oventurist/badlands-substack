# Hugh Wilson

Hugh Wilson was an American foreign service officer and a member of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society.[1] He served as Counselor to Japan (1911–1921), U.S. Minister to Switzerland (1924–1927), Assistant Secretary of State (1937–1938), Ambassador to Germany (1938), Special Assistant to the Secretary of State (1939–1941), and in the [[office-of-strategic-services|Office of Strategic Services]] from 1941 to 1945.[1] His career is offered in the article as an example of the Order's placement of members across the [[state-department|State Department]] and the wartime intelligence apparatus.[1]
