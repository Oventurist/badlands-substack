# Darya Dugina

Darya Dugina was a Russian journalist and geopolitical commentator, and the daughter of the Russian philosopher [[alexander-dugin|Alexander Dugin]], who was killed in a targeted attack.[1]

## Reported CIA connection

In October 2023, while global attention was focused on the war in Gaza, [[washington-post|The Washington Post]] published a report stating that the [[cia|CIA]] had been actively running covert operations inside Russia, including operations connected to the killing of Dugina.[1] Coverage by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] framed the report as vindicating Moscow's longstanding accusations and as an example of a claim previously dismissed as "pro-Kremlin propaganda" being belatedly admitted as fact.[1]

The report described a "shadow war" in which missions were carried out by elite teams of Ukrainian operatives drawn from directorates that were formed, trained, and equipped in close partnership with the CIA.[1] According to current and former Ukrainian and US officials cited in the reporting, the agency had since 2015 spent tens of millions of dollars transforming [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s Soviet-formed services into potent allies against Moscow.[1]
