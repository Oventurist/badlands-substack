# Israel Hayom

**Israel Hayom** is described in Badlands coverage as the largest online media outlet in Israel.[1]

## Call for a global Jewish security architecture

In December 2025, following the [[bondi-beach-attack|Bondi Beach attack]] in Sydney, Israel Hayom published an opinion piece calling for a global Jewish security architecture that would connect Israeli security forces with local law enforcement — "especially in the U.S." — to identify instances of antisemitism and take action against them.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the proposal as an open call for worldwide tyranny controlled by the Israeli government, predicting that [[mossad|Mossad]] or IDF intelligence consultants would work with local sheriffs' offices to build databases of alleged antisemites, and that enforcement would extend from deplatforming to frozen bank accounts, ruined business relationships and character assassination.[1] He argued the ultimate goal was to criminalize the behavior in the collective mind before codifying it into law, with violent incidents attributed to "hate speech" serving to make speech restrictions appear pragmatic.[1]
