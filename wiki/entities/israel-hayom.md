# Israel Hayom

Israel Hayom is an Israeli newspaper described in the Badlands Media corpus as the largest online publication in [[israel|Israel]].[1]

## Role in the February 2026 Iran war narrative

In February 2026, Israel Hayom published a report stating that a US attack on [[iran|Iran]] was just "days away," citing an Israeli source.[1] The Badlands Brief placed this publication in a sequence: the narrative originated with an [[axios|Axios]] story by [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]], after which Israel Hayom amplified the claim with its own anonymously sourced timeline.[1] Israel Hayom also carried the Israel Defense Forces assessment that [[israel-defense-forces|the IDF]] expected Iran to direct retaliation at Israel if the United States carried out a strike.[1]

## Interpretation in the corpus

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the Israel Hayom report as part of an Israeli-origin pressure campaign pushing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] toward a war he was resisting in favor of a negotiated deal.[1] Steve Bannon's criticism of Ravid was cited in the same segment, and the Brief emphasized that no public announcement of an authorized strike, declaration of war, or confirmed US military action existed at the time of the reporting.[1]
