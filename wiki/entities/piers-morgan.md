# Piers Morgan

**Piers Morgan** is a British broadcaster and interviewer, host of the TalkTV program *Piers Morgan Uncensored*.[1]

In an interview aired on April 6, 2023, Morgan spoke with [[stormy-daniels|Stormy Daniels]] in her first comments after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] pleaded not guilty to charges arising from a hush-money payment scheme.[1] Pressed by Morgan on whether she would testify if asked, Daniels replied, "Oh, absolutely."[1] The exchange was widely recirculated and became the basis for coverage of her potential role as a witness in the [[trump-hush-money-case|Trump hush money case]].[1]

## Tucker Carlson 9/11 interview (September 2025)

In September 2025, [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] used an appearance on *Piers Morgan Uncensored News* to claim that Israeli intelligence had advance knowledge of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], remarks that were carried internationally and previewed a forthcoming documentary series by Carlson on the attacks.[2] Speaking to Morgan, Carlson rejected the framing that he had alleged "the Jews did it," saying such phrasing "is a way to discredit real questions," and pointed instead to [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s on-camera statements that the attacks were beneficial to United States–Israel relations.[2] Carlson also raised the [[israeli-art-students-affair|Israeli art students affair]] during the interview, saying public knowledge of the episode had remained very limited and that reporting on it ultimately vanished.[2]
