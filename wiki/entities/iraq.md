# Iraq

Iraq is a Middle Eastern state that appears in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] as a pivot of the regional realignment its contributors track.

## 2026 realignment

The Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] hosted newly elected Iraqi Prime Minister [[ali-al-zaidi|Ali Al-Zaidi]] at the [[white-house|White House]], professing "deep affection for the young leader," and subsequently announced United States support for an Iraq–[[syria|Syria]] oil pipeline that would bypass the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] and the Red Sea to deliver product directly to the Mediterranean. The meeting followed by less than a week Trump's encounter with Syrian President [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed Al-Sharaa]] on the sidelines of the [[nato|NATO]] summit in Ankara.

Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlights that Iraq and [[iran|Iran]] — "two bitter enemies who fought the bloodiest war of the 20th century in the 1980's" — were reported by Iranian state media to be working together on regional de-escalation and stabilization, treating the convergence as evidence of a deliberate and accelerating reordering of Middle Eastern alignments.
