# Reed Hastings

Reed Hastings is a co-founder of [[netflix|Netflix]] and one of the largest individual donors to Democratic candidates in the United States, having given more than $20 million to Democratic causes in recent years, including upwards of $1.5 million to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s 2020 presidential campaign.[1]

## Break with Biden in 2024

In early July 2024, following Biden's debate performance against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Hastings publicly called for the president to abandon his re-election campaign.[1] He reiterated that position after Biden's interview with [[george-stephanopoulos|George Stephanopoulos]], telling ABC News: "Biden is unfortunately in denial about his mental state. He needs to step aside to let a vigorous Democratic leader beat Trump."[1]

Hastings was among a cluster of mega-donors — alongside [[whitney-tilson|Whitney Tilson]] and [[bill-harris|Bill Harris]] — who not only closed their checkbooks but explored using political action committees to fund a "mini-primary" and a replacement nominee.[1] Badlands Media read the donor revolt as evidence that the "Panic Pattern" had spread beyond the media and political establishment into the elite funding class, while noting that Biden's existing campaign war chest could legally be transferred only to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1]
