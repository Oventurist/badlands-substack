# CNN

CNN is an American cable news network, historically part of [[time-warner|Time Warner]].

## In the Badlands critique

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] argues that CNN, together with [[msnbc|MSNBC]], had been "hemorrhaging viewers before Trump" and was temporarily rescued by the 2016 election, when "once-disinterested liberals became maniacally obsessed with politics in the age of 'the great tyrant' [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]" and flocked back to cable news. The article characterizes that recovery as a sugar high that inevitably waned.

It further charges CNN with a structural blind spot, noting that "you won't see the corporate hacks at CNN reporting on things you might see on [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]] or [[consortium-news|Consortium News]]," and places the network within a partisan duopoly with [[fox-news|Fox News]] in which the two outlets are "two heads on the same wretched snake." CNN's own business coverage is nonetheless cited by the article as a source for the January 2024 layoffs at [[time-magazine|Time Magazine]].
