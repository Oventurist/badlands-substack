# Moxila Upadhyaya

Moxila Upadhyaya is a United States magistrate judge for the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia.[1] She presided over the August 3, 2023 arraignment of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on the four-count federal indictment brought by Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] concerning the 2020 presidential election and the certification of electoral votes on January 6, 2021.[1]

At the hearing, Upadhyaya swore in the defendant and read aloud the charges and the penalties they carried, while Trump listened and nodded.[1] Defense counsel John Lauro waived the reading of the full 45-page indictment, and Trump entered a plea of not guilty.[1] The proceeding took place in the federal courthouse in Washington, D.C., a short distance from the Capitol building at the center of the allegations.[1]
