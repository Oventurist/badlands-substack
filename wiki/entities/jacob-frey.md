# Jacob Frey

Jacob Frey is the Mayor of Minneapolis, Minnesota. In January 2026 he emerged as one of the most prominent local critics of federal immigration enforcement operations conducted in his city.[1]

## Response to the Pretti shooting

After federal agents fatally shot 37-year-old U.S. citizen [[alex-jeffrey-pretti|Alex Jeffrey Pretti]] in Minneapolis on January 24, 2026, Frey joined Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] in condemning the operation as excessive, calling for the withdrawal of federal agents from the city and for accountability for those involved.[1] The two officials' criticism was answered directly by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who accused Minnesota officials of "inciting insurrection" and questioned the absence of local police from the operation.[1]

Badlands commentary framed the dispute as part of a broader argument over whether sanctuary jurisdictions and their officials are themselves operating outside federal law, invoking the "no one is above the law" standard that Democrats applied to Trump during his prosecutions.[1]


## Response to the Renee Nicole Good shooting

Following the fatal shooting of [[renee-nicole-good|Renee Nicole Good]] by an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] agent on January 7, Minneapolis Mayor Frey drew both support and criticism for his remarks, including an on-camera profanity-laced statement telling ICE to leave the city, which he later defended.[2] City officials described most of the ensuing demonstrations as peaceful despite some arrests and isolated clashes.[2]
