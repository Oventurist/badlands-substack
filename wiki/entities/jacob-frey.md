# Jacob Frey

Jacob Frey is the mayor of Minneapolis, Minnesota. He appears in the Badlands corpus in connection with the January 2026 confrontation between federal immigration authorities and local officials in the Twin Cities.

## Response to the Alex Pretti shooting

According to the January 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-577|Badlands Brief]], Frey was among the local leaders who issued sharp rebukes after federal immigration agents fatally shot 37-year-old U.S. citizen [[alex-pretti|Alex Jeffrey Pretti]] in Minneapolis on January 24, 2026. Alongside Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]], Frey decried the federal operation as excessive and called for the withdrawal of federal agents from the city and for accountability over the shooting. The [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] maintained that Pretti was armed and resisted disarmament, a position defended publicly by Border Patrol Chief [[gregory-bovino|Gregory Bovino]], while bystander video suggested Pretti had been filming officers, pepper-sprayed, and pinned before the shots were fired.

The Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] responded to the criticism from Minnesota officials by accusing them of "inciting insurrection" and by questioning the absence of local police from the scene — a pointed reference to the posture of city and state authorities toward federal enforcement in [[sanctuary-city|sanctuary]] jurisdictions.
