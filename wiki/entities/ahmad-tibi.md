# Ahmad Tibi

Ahmad Tibi is a Palestinian-Israeli politician.[1]

## Exchange with Ben-Gvir

Badlands Media highlighted an incident in October 2024 in which Israeli National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] reacted furiously to being called a "terrorist" by Tibi.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the response from Ben-Gvir's side was that Jews cannot be considered "terrorists," which the contributor presented as evidence of a sense of supremacy and of double standards within Israeli politics.[1]

The contributor framed the episode within a broader argument that [[israel|Israel]] has never been the liberal democracy it is presented as being, and that its future depends on whether the Israeli people repudiate Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and the oligarchy the contributor associates with him.[1] Tibi's willingness to make the accusation on the record was cited as grounds for hope that Israel could be "saved from itself."[1]
