# Lee Vogler

J. Lee Vogler, 38, is a Danville, Virginia city councilman who also works as director of sales at Showcase Magazine.[1]

## 2025 arson attack

On Wednesday, July 30, 2025, Vogler was doused with gasoline and set on fire at the Showcase Magazine office in Danville, sustaining burns across 50 to 60 percent of his body, according to the magazine's publisher, Andrew Brooks.[1] Police arrested Shotsie Michael Buck Hayes, 29, of Danville, and charged him with attempted murder.[1]

Vogler, a father of two, was airlifted to a hospital in North Carolina and was scheduled for surgery there; most of his burns were on his torso.[1] Brooks said several bystanders recorded Vogler with their phones after he ran out of the office covered in gasoline and was set alight, and that a passing motorist told him to drop to the ground and roll.[1]
