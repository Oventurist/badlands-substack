# Georgetown University

Georgetown University is a private Jesuit research university in Washington, D.C. In the Badlands Media feature "Transparency and Prosecution," the school appears as an institution whose Arab and Islamic studies programs are alleged to operate under the financial influence of Saudi [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]].[1]

## The Alwaleed endowment

In December 2005 Georgetown's Center for Muslim-Christian Understanding received a $20 million gift from Prince Alwaleed bin Talal, described by the university as an internationally renowned businessman and global investor.[1] The center was renamed the Prince Alwaleed Bin Talal Center for Muslim-Christian Understanding (ACMCU), and the endowed fund was reported as the second largest single gift in Georgetown's history.[1] The article, quoting the Shoebat Foundation, describes the ACMCU's director, Professor John Esposito, as a Muslim Brotherhood apologist and as "Prince Alwaleed's guy at the University," noting that Esposito is listed among the faculty of the Department of Arabic and Islamic Studies.[1]

## Relevance to the Syria narrative

The university enters the article's argument through [[elizabeth-obagy|Elizabeth O'Bagy]], who earned a Master's in Arab Studies at Georgetown and whose hiring announcement by the [[syrian-emergency-task-force|Syrian Emergency Task Force]] highlighted that degree.[1] O'Bagy was later dismissed by the [[institute-for-the-study-of-war|Institute for the Study of War]] after the institute confirmed she did not hold the Georgetown Ph.D. she had claimed.[1] For the author, the Georgetown-Alwaleed link is the connective tissue explaining how both O'Bagy and Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] came to advance the same interventionist line on Syria.[1]
