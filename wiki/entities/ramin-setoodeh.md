# Ramin Setoodeh

Ramin Setoodeh is an entertainment journalist and the author of Apprentice in Wonderland, a book about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the reality television program [[the-apprentice|The Apprentice]].[1] The book was scheduled for release in late June 2024, with [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]] obtaining an early copy for review.[1]

## Apprentice in Wonderland

Setoodeh reports that for at least six months after entering the 2015 presidential race, Trump insisted on keeping the Apprentice set intact on the 14th floor of Trump Tower, reasoning that if the campaign did not work out it would at least generate good publicity for the next season of The Celebrity Apprentice.[1] Trump's son Eric told Setoodeh that "there was a cognizant decision to leave the boardroom, and there was a possibility of it coming back"; campaign staffers took over the floor after the set was eventually torn down.[1]

The Atlantic's review characterized the book as a revealing look behind the scenes of what it called arguably the most consequential television show in history, arguing that the tough-but-fair executive persona Trump played from 2004 to 2015 was more reality-television invention than reality, and that Trump emerges as thoroughly steeped in the celebrity culture of the 2000s — a culture the magazine said remains influential on his politics.[1]

## Badlands response

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the persona thesis was not news to Anons, who had said for years that "The Donald" was a construct crafted or perfected on the show, and that what unsettled the establishment was its own belated recognition of the fact.[1]
