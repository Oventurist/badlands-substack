# Iran International

Iran International is a Persian-language news outlet covering [[iran|Iran]] from outside the country, widely read by Iranians and frequently cited in Western reporting on the Islamic Republic's internal politics.

The July 22, 2026 Badlands Brief uses an Iran International report as its primary source for coverage of Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]]'s nearly 90-minute podcast interview with establishment podcaster Javad Mogoei. The outlet reported Araghchi's statement that Iran's security breach "is not limited merely to infiltration and obtaining information," but that "sometimes it also exists in influencing the decision-making process itself. And even more importantly, it extends to shaping our psychological environment and public perception."

Iran International also carried analyst commentary from Omid Memarian of DAWN, who observed that one manifestation of such influence would be officials advocating policies that prolong the war despite the damage to the Islamic Republic's own interests: "Some of the decisions they are making are exactly the things that Israelis might wish for." The outlet's headline framing emphasized the backlash Araghchi's remarks provoked among Iranian hardliners and [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Revolutionary Guards]]-affiliated media, a detail [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlights in building his argument about factional struggle inside Tehran.
