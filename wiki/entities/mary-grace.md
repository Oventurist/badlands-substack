# Mary Grace

Mary Grace is a host in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective.[1]

## Badlands Media role

She co-hosts [[conspiracy-of-truth|Conspiracy of Truth]] with [[praying-medic|Praying Medic]], airing Thursdays at 5pm ET, one of the new shows announced in the network's January 16–22, 2023 schedule.[1] The program discusses news, current events, and spiritual growth through the lens of faith and freedom.[1]
