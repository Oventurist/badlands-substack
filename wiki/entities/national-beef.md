# National Beef

National Beef Packing Company is one of the four largest beef processors in the United States and, like [[jbs|JBS]], is subject to substantial foreign ownership.

## Antitrust investigation and national security framing

The [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]] reports that National Beef is named alongside JBS, [[cargill|Cargill]], and [[tyson-foods|Tyson Foods]] in the intensified antitrust investigation announced by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and the [[united-states-department-of-agriculture|Department of Agriculture]]. Secretary of Agriculture [[brooke-rollins|Brooke Rollins]] identified National Beef specifically, together with JBS, as a foreign-owned packer posing a national security threat amid reports of slave labor ties, arguing that such control makes the companies "a threat not just to our cattle producers, but a threat to America itself." Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] said the review encompassed more than three million documents and hundreds of interviews with ranchers.
