# National Beef

National Beef is a US beef processing company counted among the "Big Four" beef packers.[1]

## 2026 antitrust investigation

National Beef was named with [[jbs|JBS]], [[cargill|Cargill]], and [[tyson-foods|Tyson Foods]] in the [[beef-packer-antitrust-investigation-2026|antitrust investigation]] announced in May 2026 by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and [[department-of-agriculture|Department of Agriculture]].[1] Agriculture Secretary [[brooke-rollins|Brooke Rollins]] identified National Beef and JBS as foreign-owned packers posing a national security threat amid reports of slave labor ties.[1]
