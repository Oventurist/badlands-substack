# Dominion Energy

Dominion Energy is a US electric utility and the developer of the Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind (CVOW) project, the largest offshore wind project of its kind in the United States.[1]

## Trump administration halt to offshore wind

On Monday, December 22, 2025, the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration halted Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind along with four other projects under construction off the East Coast, a move described as a devastating blow to the wind industry.[1] Shares of Dominion Energy dropped nearly 4 percent on the news.[1] The administration also paused leases for Vineyard Wind 1 off Massachusetts, Revolution Wind off Rhode Island, Sunrise Wind off Long Island and New England, and Empire Wind 1 south of Long Island; taken together, the developers said, the projects would have provided enough power for more than two million homes.[1] Interior Secretary [[doug-burgum|Doug Burgum]] said the leases were paused because of national security concerns identified by the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], writing as a resident of a coastal town, framed the halt as the fulfillment of a 2023 prediction that Trump would cancel the project, producing "extreme cognitive dissonance" among coastal residents who had campaigned against the turbines while supporting the politics behind them.[1]
