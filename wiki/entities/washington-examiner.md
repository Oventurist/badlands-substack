# The Washington Examiner

**The Washington Examiner** is an American conservative-leaning news outlet covering national politics and policy.[1]

Its reporting was cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] for coverage of [[georgia-senate-bill-189|Georgia Senate Bill 189]], the election legislation approved by Georgia Republicans in March 2024 that clarified voter eligibility challenge standards, strengthened paper ballot security, and eased presidential ballot access for parties already qualified in twenty states.[1]
