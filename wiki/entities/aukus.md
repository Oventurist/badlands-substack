# AUKUS

AUKUS is a trilateral defense partnership between the United States, the United Kingdom, and Australia, formed in 2021 to strengthen defense cooperation in the Indo-Pacific region.[1] The pact is organized into two tracks: "Pillar One," which covers the development and transfer of nuclear-powered submarine capability, and "Pillar Two," which covers advanced technologies including artificial intelligence, cyber capabilities, and quantum systems.[1]

## Undersea drone program

In a June 2026 announcement made in Singapore, War Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] said the three AUKUS partners would jointly develop new undersea drones.[1] Hegseth described the effort as the first Pillar Two initiative focused specifically on uncrewed undersea vehicles (UUVs), framing it as a means of delivering "highly adaptable multi-mission UUV payloads" in order to maintain maritime advantage.[1]

## Political context

The undersea drone announcement followed a period of uncertainty about the pact's future, driven by concerns over a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] review of AUKUS.[1] Those concerns were later eased when President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] reaffirmed United States support for the arrangement.[1] Hegseth stated that the United States remains committed to the partnership and is moving quickly to expand submarine capabilities in the Pacific.[1]
