# Ahnenerbe

The Ahnenerbe — the Ancestral Heritage Research and Teaching Society — was created by [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] in 1935 as the vehicle through which he took full control of all branches of Nazi science, and which he led until the end of the war.[1] The agency reorganized entire fields of science, with a focus on anthropology, geology, agronomy and archaeology, around a pagan and Nazi-approved filter.[1]

## Expeditions

With nearly unlimited resources, Himmler deployed Ahnenerbe teams of anthropologists to locate the origins of the Aryan root race in Tibet. SS officer [[ernst-schafer|Ernst Schäfer]] illegally entered Tibet, befriended and mentored the young Dalai Lama, and rigorously measured the skulls of Tibetans.[1] Schäfer's team was also tasked with mapping sexual practices, the occult rites of esoteric Tibetan Buddhism and priestly rituals including the use of human skin for decorative furniture, and with tracking down the mythical Yeti.[1] Photographs record Dr. Bruno Beger and Dr. Ernst Schäfer being received by Tibetan dignitaries at Lhasa in 1938, and an Ahnenerbe phrenologist measuring a Tibetan face.[1]

Other Ahnenerbe-linked efforts sought the entrance to the hollow earth in Antarctica, Atlantean colonies in the South American Andes, the Theosophists' mythical Shamballah in Tibet, the Holy Grail — an expedition led by [[otto-rahn|Otto Rahn]] — and the Lost Ark beneath Solomon's Temple.[1]

## Personnel and ideology

Another leading Ahnenerbe figure was the Volkish archaeologist [[wilhelm-teudt|Wilhelm Teudt]], who identified the Frankish king [[charlemagne|Charlemagne]] as the cause of Germany's loss of its pure pagan spirit.[1]

## Himmler, Eichmann and Jewish affairs

In Matthew Ehret's account, it was during his time managing the Ahnenerbe that Reichsführer [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]] interfaced closely with [[adolf-eichmann|Adolf Eichmann]], the chief minister responsible for "Jewish Affairs" who oversaw the creation of the ghetto system in Germany and later led the crafting of the 1933 [[haavara-agreement|Haavara Agreement]].[2]
