# House Select Committee on Assassinations

The House Select Committee on Assassinations was a committee of the United States House of Representatives convened to reinvestigate the killings of President John F. Kennedy and [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]] It appears in the Badlands Media corpus through the April 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks", which cites its firearms panel in a comparison between the King case and the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].

As recounted in the Brief, the committee assembled a panel of firearms experts to examine the deformed .30-06 rifle slug recovered from King's body, designated exhibit Q64, against the Remington 760 Gamemaster rifle recovered near the crime scene, designated Q2. The panel found that the bullet's class characteristics matched the rifle — both exhibited six grooves and six lands with a right twist — but that the individual identifying characteristics were insufficient for a determination. The committee's conclusion was that it was "unable positively to identify or eliminate the Q64 bullet as having been fired from the Q2 rifle." The Brief also notes the committee's finding that no U.S. government agency participated in the assassination, a conclusion [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats with open scepticism, and draws a parallel with the [[bureau-of-alcohol-tobacco-firearms-and-explosives|ATF]]'s inconclusive ballistics in the case against [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]].
