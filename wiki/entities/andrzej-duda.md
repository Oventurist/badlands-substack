# Andrzej Duda

Andrzej Duda is the President of Poland, a NATO member state, and a figure who maintained a notably warm relationship with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during Trump's first term.[1]

## April 2024 Meeting at Trump Tower

On the evening of Wednesday, April 17, 2024, Duda met Trump at Trump Tower in New York City, where the former president was staying while attending his Manhattan criminal trial over an alleged hush-money scheme.[1] The meeting was one in a series of conversations Trump, then the presumptive Republican nominee, held with foreign leaders during the campaign.[1]

According to a readout issued by the Trump campaign, the two men spoke for two and a half hours and discussed the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and the conflict between [[israel|Israel]] and Hamas.[1] They also discussed Duda's proposal that NATO countries spend 3 percent of GDP on defense.[1] Trump praised his guest publicly, saying "He's doing a fantastic job. The people of Poland love him, they really do," and adding, "He's my friend, and we had four great years together."[1]

## Policy Divergence

Duda has been a consistent proponent of supporting Ukraine in its war against [[russia|Russia]], a position that sits uneasily with Trump's skepticism toward Ukraine aid and his criticism of the [[nato|NATO]] alliance of which Poland is a member.[1]
