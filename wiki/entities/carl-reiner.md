# Carl Reiner

Carl Reiner (1922–2020) was an American comedian, writer, and director described in Badlands commentary as "a founding father of modern American comedy."[1] He was the father of filmmaker [[rob-reiner|Rob Reiner]] and the patriarch of a family that commentators characterized as indisputably qualifying as "Hollywood royalty."[1]

## Legacy and the Reiner family

Rob Reiner (1947–2025) began his Hollywood career working with his father's contemporary Norman Lear (1922–2023) on *All in the Family*, where he played the liberal son of Archie Bunker, before going on to an outsized role in modern Hollywood storytelling.[1] Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the #MeToo era, whatever its faults, exposed the "perversions of tinsel town casting couches" and removed the public's inability to question what she called the aristocracy of narrative propaganda — a frame she applied to the Reiner dynasty following the December 2025 killings of Rob Reiner and [[michele-singer-reiner|Michele Singer Reiner]] and the arrest of their son [[nick-reiner|Nick Reiner]].[1]
