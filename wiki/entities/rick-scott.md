# Rick Scott

Rick Scott is a Republican United States Senator whose public statements on Latin America drew attention in January 2026 following the US-led [[operation-absolute-resolve|Operation Absolute Resolve]] in Venezuela.[1]

## Remarks on Cuba, Nicaragua and Colombia (January 2026)

In a public interview conducted after the removal of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] from power, Scott said that following recent US actions in Venezuela the United States would next "fix" [[cuba|Cuba]] and Nicaragua and would seek political change in [[colombia|Colombia]].[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

The remarks were cited by Badlands contributors as evidence that neoconservative and interventionist figures had been drawn into the open by the Venezuela operation.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had "chummed the waters and now the NeoCons have taken the bait," describing the resulting rhetoric — including comments by Senator [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]] — as "the essence of tyranny."[1] He argued that the US government was arbitrarily declaring Latin American governments illegitimate for reasons that were not entirely clear, and that American politicians had become "the henchmen of transnational oligarchs."[1]
