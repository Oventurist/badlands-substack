# Justin Bieber

Justin Bieber is a Canadian singer who rose to fame as a child performer and who appears in Badlands Media commentary as an alleged victim of abuse within the entertainment industry.[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] stated that it "appears singer Justin Bieber was the victim of much abuse in Hollywood while still a boy," and that he has since turned to Christianity to mend the damage done to him.[1] The essay noted that Bieber's father-in-law, actor [[stephen-baldwin|Stephen Baldwin]], has posted cryptic videos saying the public is in "the calm before the storm," which Carlson identified as a [[qanon|Q]] reference.[1]

## Context

Bieber's treatment in the essay supports its broader argument that the entertainment industry is controlled by a [[cabal|Cabal]] that compromises performers — sometimes as children — and that the prosecution of [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]] would begin to expose that system.[1] The claims are presented as opinion and inference; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the views of contributing authors.[1]
