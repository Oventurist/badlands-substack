# Katy Perry

Katy Perry is an American pop singer who, in April 2025, became the focus of widespread public backlash after participating in an all-female suborbital spaceflight operated by [[blue-origin|Blue Origin]], the rocket company owned by Jeff Bezos.[1]

## 2025 Blue Origin flight

Perry flew aboard the Blue Origin capsule alongside former NASA engineer Aisha Bowe, astronaut Amanda Nguyễn, CBS morning host [[gayle-king|Gayle King]], film producer Kerianne Flynn, and Lauren Sánchez, the fiancée of Jeff Bezos.[1] The trip was brief, and Perry brought a daisy with her, flashing the flower to cameras as the crew floated inside the capsule.[1]

## Rogan criticism

Podcaster [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]] mocked Perry over the flight on an episode released in April 2025, joking that she was "basically a guru now" after describing the experience as profound.[1] Rogan singled out the daisy for ridicule, remarking that "it shows you how quick the flight was" because "the dead daisy that's like snipped from its life source was still alive or still vibrant."[1] Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] dismissed the episode with a broader skepticism toward celebrity space deployments generally.[1]
