# Hilton Dallas Southlake

The **Hilton Dallas Southlake** — formally the Hilton Dallas/Southlake Town Square — is a hotel and conference venue in the Dallas–Fort Worth area of Texas.[1]

## Role in GART 4

The hotel served as the conference venue for [[gart-4-dallas|GART 4: Dallas]], the fourth stop of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s Great American Restoration Tour, held April 18–21, 2024.[1] In its final promotional notice for the event, published April 14, 2024, Badlands Media directed ticket-holders to the Hilton Dallas Southlake as the site of the conference, which combined live in-person versions of the network's shows, panel question-and-answer sessions and host meet-and-greets with meals and receptions provided across the weekend.[1] The venue accommodated the weekend's General Admission programming, including a Friday night Welcome Reception and weekend breakfasts and lunches, as well as a separate Thursday night VIP dinner and meet-and-greet.[1]
