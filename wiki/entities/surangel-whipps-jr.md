# Surangel Whipps Jr.

Surangel Whipps Jr. is the president of [[palau|Palau]], a Pacific archipelago nation of about 350 islands.[1]

In December 2025, Whipps's office announced that Palau had signed a memorandum of understanding with the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration to accept up to 75 "third country nationals" whom the United States cannot return to their home countries, in exchange for $7.5 million and other aid.[1] The statement from his office framed the arrangement as allowing people never charged with a crime to live and work in Palau, "helping address local labor shortages in needed occupations."[1]
