# Gina Carano

**Gina Carano** is an American actress and former mixed martial artist best known for her role in the Disney+ series *The Mandalorian*, from which she was fired after posting right-wing opinions on social media.[1]

## Lawsuit against Disney and Lucasfilm

On Tuesday, February 6, 2024, Carano filed a complaint in California federal court suing [[disney|Disney]] and [[lucasfilm|Lucasfilm]] for discrimination and wrongful termination, escalating a long-running standoff over her dismissal from *The Mandalorian*.[1] The complaint alleges that she was fired for voicing right-wing opinions on social media, and it seeks a court order that would force Lucasfilm to recast her in the role.[1]

The suit was reported as opening another front in a broader battle for influence over Hollywood that has drawn in corporate America.[1] [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] is helping fund the litigation through [[x-corp|X]], making good on an earlier promise to foot the legal bills of users who claim they were discriminated against because of their activity on his platform.[1] X's head of business operations, Joe Benarroch, said in a statement: "As a sign of X Corp's commitment to free speech, we're proud to provide financial support for Gina Carano's lawsuit, empowering her to seek vindication of her free speech rights on X and the ability to work without bullying, harassment, or discrimination."[1]

The case was carried in the [[the-hollywood-reporter|Hollywood Reporter]] and circulated in Badlands coverage as a bonus item in its February 7, 2024 news brief.[1]
