# Gina Carano

Gina Carano is an American actress and former mixed martial artist, known for her role in the television series *The Mandalorian*. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," her firing by Disney is cited as a prominent example of the right-wing "cancel culture" outrage that, the article argues, conservatives failed to apply consistently.

## The 2021 firing

The article recounts that Disney fired Carano in February 2021 over an Instagram image the actress posted depicting a Jewish woman running from Nazi guards, captioned with the observation that "Jews were beaten in the streets, not by Nazi soldiers but by their neighbors…even by children," and asking "How is that any different from hating someone for their political views?" The firing was reported in a [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]] piece by Tim Pierce titled "Shapiro: Gina Carano Firing Part Of A Movement To 'Expel' Half Of America."

[[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] commented on his podcast "The Ben Shapiro Show" that Carano's firing "is indicative of where we are in the culture, and it is a terrible moment for American culture," adding that "Social movements have consequences, and we are now in the middle of a mass social movement to expel half of the American population from the body politic."

## Use in the article's argument

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], the article's author, cites the Carano case as an instance where he agrees the right's free speech advocacy was justified — but argues the same figures "didn't suddenly stop believing this after October 7th, 2023" when the targets of firings became critics of Israel, such as Maura Finkelstein, Hesen Jabr, and Nikki Noor Aytoglu.
