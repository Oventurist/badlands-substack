# United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit

The United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit is the Manhattan-based federal appellate court with jurisdiction over the district courts of New York, Connecticut, and Vermont, including the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]].[1]

## Maxwell appeal

On Tuesday, September 17, 2024, a three-judge panel of the Second Circuit upheld [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]]'s conviction for helping the late financier [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] sexually abuse teenage girls, leaving in place the 20-year sentence she is serving in a Florida prison.[1] The panel rejected Maxwell's claim that Epstein's 2007 non-prosecution agreement with federal prosecutors in southern Florida shielded her from being charged in New York in 2020.[1] It also rejected her arguments that the trial was tainted because a juror failed to disclose that he had been sexually abused as a child, and that her sentence was excessive.[1] A lawyer for Maxwell signaled that she would appeal the decision to the U.S. Supreme Court.[1]

Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that the ruling landed on the same day, and in the same judicial district, as the arraignment and bail denial of [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] — a coincidence of timing they treated as narratively significant.[1]
