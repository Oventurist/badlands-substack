# Liridon Rexhepi

Liridon Rexhepi is a Kosovan national identified by the [[turkish-national-intelligence-organization|Turkish National Intelligence Organization (MIT)]] as the head of [[mossad|Mossad]]'s financial network in Türkiye.[1] Turkish security officials announced his arrest on 3 September 2024.[1]

Citing security sources, Turkish media reported that Rexhepi "was transferring money to Mossad's field agents in Turkiye who filmed Mossad targets with drones, led psychological operations against Palestinian politicians, and gathered intelligence about the Syrian field."[1] MIT reportedly discovered him after detecting irregularities in his financial accounts, which showed "countless" transfers to Mossad field agents in the country via Western Union; during interrogation he also admitted that funds from Kosovo were transferred to sources in Syria using cryptocurrencies.[1]

Rexhepi was placed under MIT surveillance upon entering Türkiye on 25 August, detained in Istanbul on 30 August in a joint operation with the Istanbul police, and formally arrested on Tuesday 3 September.[1]
