# Yair Lapid

Yair Lapid is an Israeli politician, former television news anchor, and leader of the opposition to [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s government. He appears in the February 24, 2026 Badlands Brief as one of two favorites — alongside [[naftali-bennett|Naftali Bennett]] — to win the Israeli prime ministerial election scheduled for October.

## Position on war with Iran

[[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites Lapid declaring that he would stand with Netanyahu in a war against [[iran|Iran]], and reports that Lapid went on in Hebrew to express sympathy for the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel]] concept articulated by US Ambassador [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] in his interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]. The Brief presents this as evidence for its broader interpretive claim that no genuinely pro-peace faction with a realistic path to power exists in Israeli politics, since even a "center-left" progressive former news anchor is described as willing to give Netanyahu whatever support a war with Iran would require.
