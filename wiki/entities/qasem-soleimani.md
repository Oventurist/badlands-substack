# Qasem Soleimani

Qasem Soleimani was the head of the Quds Force, a branch of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), and his killing became the stated motive behind a series of Iranian-backed assassination plots later disclosed to the American public.[1]

## Role in the IRGC

As commander of the Quds Force, Soleimani directed the external operations arm of the IRGC, the element of the Iranian security state responsible for activity beyond Iran's borders.[1] Reporting on his death consistently identifies him by that position, and it is in that capacity that his killing is treated as a trigger for Iranian retaliation planning.[1]

## Alleged retaliation plots

FBI records released by Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] in September 2024 indicated that Iranian-backed plotters sought to assassinate [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and other prominent American political figures in relation to Soleimani's killing.[1] The records, obtained through legally protected whistleblower disclosures, described potential targeting of "politicians, military people or bureaucrats," a category that reportedly included President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], Trump, and former presidential candidate [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]].[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor Burning Bright argued that disclosures of this kind should be read on several levels, framing "Iran" variously as a regime installed to destabilize the Middle East and supply a pretext for escalation, and as a catch-all attribution label for clandestine operations routed through Iranian servers and operators.[1] The commentary further contended that the inclusion of Biden alongside Trump on the alleged target list constituted evidence for the [[devolution|devolution]] thesis circulating in the Truth Community.[1]
