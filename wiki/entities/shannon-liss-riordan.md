# Shannon Liss-Riordan

**Shannon Liss-Riordan** is a Boston attorney who in January 2024 represented a bipartisan group of Massachusetts voters seeking to bar [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state's presidential primary ballot.[1]

## Massachusetts ballot challenge

After the challengers lost before a state ballot law panel, Liss-Riordan took the question to the [[massachusetts-supreme-judicial-court|Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court]], asking the state's highest court to declare Trump ineligible to appear on the March primary ballot and to order Secretary of State [[william-galvin|William Galvin]] "to take all actions necessary to effectuate Trump's removal."[1]

Her argument held that Trump violated his oath to uphold the Constitution when he "engaged in a systematic effort to deny the results" of the 2020 presidential election, culminating in what she described as "his fomenting of a violent assault" on the U.S. Capitol. In court filings she said obtaining a "timely, conclusive" answer on Trump's eligibility was of "paramount importance," warning that his appearance on a ballot for an office for which he was constitutionally ineligible would "violate Massachusetts law, undermine the integrity of the electoral process and the U.S. Constitution, and potentially engender political chaos."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the Massachusetts filing as the Bay State's case of "FOMO" ahead of the February 8 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] hearing on the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado disqualification case]], arguing that the Massachusetts suit was wholly dependent on Colorado prevailing and that the true purpose of such lawsuits was election interference.[1]
