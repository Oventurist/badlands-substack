# Daniel S. Pearson

Daniel S. Pearson is a Miami attorney, former Florida state judge and former federal prosecutor who was appointed by a federal appeals court to serve as [[concepts/independent-counsel|independent counsel]] investigating the financial affairs of Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ronald H. Brown]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) At the time of his appointment, Pearson — then 64 years old — became the fourth special counsel probing officials of the Clinton administration.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Mandate

Pearson's charge was to determine whether Brown had broken the law in his private financial dealings or in his public reporting of those transactions.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Legal observers noted at the time that an investigation of Brown, a former chairman of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]], would likely extend well into the 1996 presidential campaign — a politically dangerous prospect for a president seeking re-election.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Termination of the inquiry

The investigation was still in its early stages when Brown was killed in an April 3, 1996 plane crash near Dubrovnik, Croatia.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) With Brown dead, the matter was transferred from Pearson's independent counsel office to the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] under Attorney General [[janet-reno|Janet Reno]], on the reasoning that Brown's remaining business partners were not members of the Clinton administration and therefore did not require an independent counsel.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that this transfer removed the investigative target from the Clinton administration and effectively secured a cover-up of both the illegal fundraising scheme and the transfer of American technology to China.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
