# Tortuguita

Tortuguita was the adopted name of a 26-year-old environmental activist, described in reporting as a nonbinary person who used they/it pronouns, who was killed by authorities in Georgia in January 2023.[1] Officials said the activist shot a state trooper before being killed.[1] The death occurred in the context of the long-running [[stop-cop-city|Stop Cop City]] protest movement against a planned Atlanta police training facility.[1]

## Aftermath

On the Saturday night following the killing, hundreds of demonstrators gathered and marched up Atlanta's Peachtree Street to mourn the activist.[1] A subsection of the crowd, masked and dressed in all black, threw rocks and lit fireworks in front of a skyscraper housing the [[atlanta-police-foundation|Atlanta Police Foundation]], shattering large glass windows, setting a police cruiser on fire, smashing further windows and covering walls with anti-police graffiti as tourists scattered.[1] Solidarity actions followed elsewhere, including rioting in Boston for which [[jared-dowell|Jared Dowell]] was arrested.[1]

## Commentary

[[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] described the unrest as reminiscent of the 2020 "summer of love," characterising it as [[antifa|Antifa]]-led protests spurred by the police killing of what he called a far-left gunman.[1] He criticised FOX 5 reporter Tyler Fingert for describing the protests as "mostly peaceful" while a police cruiser burned in the background.[1]
