# Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation

The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) is an intergovernmental forum of 21 member states bordering the Pacific, convened around trade and economic policy.[1]

## 2023 San Francisco summit

The November 2023 APEC summit was held in San Francisco and served as the diplomatic backdrop for a potential meeting between the leaders of the world's two largest economies, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1] U.S. Treasury Secretary [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]] was scheduled to host the top finance officials and ministers of the 21 APEC member states on November 11.[1]

Ahead of the summit, Yellen was set to hold economic talks with Chinese Vice Premier [[he-lifeng|He Lifeng]] on November 9 and 10, meetings announced by the Treasury Department on November 6 and confirmed by China's Foreign Ministry, whose spokesman Wang Wenbin said He would visit the United States from November 8 to November 12.[1] The announcement came shortly after Chinese state media revealed that He had become head of two Chinese Communist Party committees created by Xi in March, a move interpreted as bringing the financial system under the Party's direct control.[1]

## 2023 San Francisco summit

President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was set to meet Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] on Wednesday, November 15, 2023, in the San Francisco Bay Area on the sidelines of the Indo-Pacific forum, APEC.[2] Senior administration officials said the agenda would include the U.S. relationship with the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], the importance of open lines of communication, managing the competition responsibly, and a range of regional, global and transnational issues.[2] Officials pointed to new outbound investment rules, updated semiconductor export controls and actions against PRC entities, arguing that "intense competition requires and demands intense diplomacy to manage tensions and to prevent competition from verging into conflict or confrontation."[2]
