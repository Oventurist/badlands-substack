# Barbara Holmes

Barbara Holmes is a United States Magistrate Judge of the U.S. District Court for Middle Tennessee, sitting in Nashville.[1] In June 2025 she ruled that [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego Garcia]] could be released on bail while awaiting trial on human smuggling charges.[1]

## Abrego Garcia bail ruling

Holmes found that the government had failed to prove Abrego Garcia was a flight risk, and ordered his pretrial release.[1] She nevertheless acknowledged a point on which both sides in the case agreed: Abrego Garcia would likely remain in custody regardless of whether she upheld the prosecution's motion for detention, so the ruling did not mean he would walk free.[1] Abrego Garcia, a Salvadoran native who had been living with his wife and children in Maryland, had been deported in March 2025 to El Salvador's [[cecot|CECOT]] prison despite a 2019 court order barring his removal to that country because of a fear of persecution.[1] The ruling was reported by Georgia Public Broadcasting and carried as a bonus item by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]
