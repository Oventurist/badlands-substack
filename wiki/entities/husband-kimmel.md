# Husband Kimmel

Admiral Husband E. Kimmel was the United States Navy commander at Pearl Harbor at the time of the Japanese attack of December 7, 1941. In Badlands Media's telling, he is the designated scapegoat of the episode: the Roosevelt-led US government blamed the Pearl Harbor fiasco on the incompetency of the military leaders stationed there, and Kimmel specifically.[1]

## Exoneration by the Naval Court of Inquiry

The [[attack-on-pearl-harbor|1944 Naval Court of Inquiry]], convened on the order of Secretary of the Navy [[james-forrestal|James V. Forrestal]], largely exonerated Kimmel.[1] The court found that his decisions had been correct given the limited information available to him, and concluded that "based upon the facts established, the Court is of the opinion that no offenses have been committed nor serious blame incurred on the part of any person or persons in the naval service."[1]

The court's report was withheld from the public until after the war ended, because its findings implicitly revealed that American cryptographers had broken the Japanese codes.[1] For [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], that suppression is the significant fact: if Washington had broken the codes and knew the attack was coming, Kimmel's blame was manufactured and the deaths at Pearl Harbor were permitted rather than merely suffered.[1]

## Significance

Kimmel's case is used in Badlands commentary as an early template for how blame is assigned after mass-casualty events that serve a policy end — a pattern the same writers apply to the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] and to intelligence failure claims surrounding the October 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack on [[israel|Israel]] and the role of [[mossad]].[1]
