# Bernard C. Cohen

Bernard C. Cohen was an American political scientist whose 1963 book *The Press and Foreign Policy* supplied one of the foundational statements of agenda-setting theory, quoted by Badlands Media.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)

Cohen wrote that "the press may not be successful much of the time in telling people what to think, but it is stunningly successful in telling its readers what to think about."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm) Badlands invokes the formulation to explain how the [[centralized-media|centralized media]] shepherded American attention for the better part of the twentieth century, and how the constant redirection of collective attention from one issue to another prevents the public from addressing complex social problems and government corruption.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-swan-song-for-the-msm)
