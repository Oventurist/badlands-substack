# Nir Oz

Nir Oz is a small kibbutz near Gaza in southern Israel that was among the communities worst affected by the attacks of October 7, 2023, with approximately one in four residents murdered or kidnapped.[1]

## October 7 and Omer Neutra

Israeli Foreign Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] said that [[omer-neutra|Omer Maxim Neutra]], the American-Israeli tank platoon commander long believed to be held hostage in Gaza, "fell in a heroic battle near Nir Oz on October 7."[1] The Israeli military announced on December 2, 2024, that Neutra had in fact been killed during the [[hamas|Hamas]]-led assault, while stating his body remained held in Gaza.[1] Nir Oz's casualty ratio was cited in that coverage to convey the scale of the attack on the Gaza-envelope communities.[1]
