# Dick Durbin

Dick Durbin is a Democratic United States Senator from Illinois and a long-serving member of the Senate Democratic leadership.

In the March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], Durbin appears as the leading Democratic critic of the [[save-america-act|SAVE Act]] during [[senate|Senate]] debate. According to the source, he argued that the bill's documentary proof-of-citizenship requirement would in practice force voters to obtain passports costing $186 and involving weeks of delay, and that it would create particular burdens for married women whose current identification does not match their birth certificates.

The bill's sponsor, Sen. [[mike-lee|Mike Lee]], answered these objections directly, pointing to free sworn attestations, state verification against existing records and interstate reciprocity agreements, and insisting the process "will not cost them a dime."
