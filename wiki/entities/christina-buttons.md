# Christina Buttons

Christina Buttons is a commentator and writer who describes herself as politically homeless and as a former leftist, and who writes frequently in criticism of [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]].[1]

## Reaction to the Schaffer poll

Buttons became a reference point in Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s analysis of hybridized asymmetric and narrative warfare.[1] After commentator Elijah Schaffer posted a Twitter poll on July 2 asking whether respondents would rather have a son join the [[ku-klux-klan|Ku Klux Klan]] or the LGBTQ community — a poll engaged by more than 65,000 users, of whom 74.8% chose the KKK and 25.2% chose LGBTQ — Buttons responded with an Instagram post emphasizing how disheartening she found the results and saying they reinforced her sense of political homelessness.[1] She characterized the outcome as "an extreme position" taken by conservatives.[1]

## Deschamps's counter-reading

Deschamps disputed that framing, arguing that a conventional socio-political lens is the wrong instrument for interpreting the poll and that a [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] lens is more appropriate.[1] In his reading, the poll's framing exploited real-time emotionality over the perceived targeting of children by propaganda and set it against the largely spent historical threat of the Klan, making the result unsurprising rather than evidence of resurgent racism or homophobia.[1] He argued the results reflected neither actual racism — the belief that one race is superior to another — nor the [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]] conception of racism, nor growing conservative opposition to same-sex rights.[1]

Deschamps further used Buttons as an illustration of a broader phenomenon, asking why "politically homeless" pundits attract such substantial followings, and suggesting the answer lies in [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] that entrenches audiences into engineered political camps while leaving thoughtful dissenters without a home.[1] He noted that many on the right remain unaware that large numbers on the left, including a growing share of gay and lesbian voters, also oppose the indoctrination of children with gender ideology, pointing to organizations such as [[gays-against-groomers|Gays Against Groomers]], the [[lgb-alliance|LGB Alliance]], LGB United, and [[lesbians-united|Lesbians United]].[1]
