# Hanwha Ocean

Hanwha Ocean is a South Korean shipbuilding giant that acquired the Philly Shipyard in Pennsylvania in 2024, making it a central player in American efforts to rebuild domestic shipbuilding capacity.[1]

## Role in US shipbuilding revival

The company is described as the linchpin of a broader US strategy to counter China's dominance in shipbuilding, a field in which the People's Republic holds a significant military and economic advantage over the United States, whose own capabilities are comparatively anemic.[1] A senior administration official, granted anonymity to discuss internal thinking, said US shipbuilding had diminished significantly over the previous couple of decades and that Washington welcomed Korean "know-how, their capital and the general collaboration."[1]

Shipbuilding was expected to feature in trade negotiations between the Republic of Korea and the United States, with Seoul leaning into the sector as it courted [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ahead of his arrival in South Korea, the third stop on a weeklong Asia trip.[1] Trump, speaking in Norfolk, Virginia at a celebration of the Navy's 250th birthday, touted overseas investment and said America would "soon revitalize our once great shipyards with hundreds of billions of dollars in new investments."[1] In Seoul he was expected to meet newly elected President [[lee-jae-myung|Lee Jae Myung]], address business leaders, and attend a dinner with other heads of state.[1]
