# Yuri Ushakov

Yuri Ushakov is a senior aide to Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in the [[kremlin|Kremlin]], serving as a foreign policy adviser in the Russian presidential administration. In the Badlands corpus he appears as the Kremlin official who characterized the January 2026 Putin meeting with American envoys [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]].

## The Davos-week Putin meeting

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Putin met with Witkoff and Kushner during the week of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] annual meeting in Davos, and that Ushakov told reporters the talks were "substantive, constructive and very frank."

Ushakov also confirmed, according to Russian media, that the first meeting of a Russia-US-Ukraine working group on security issues would be held in Abu Dhabi on January 23, 2026. His remarks came as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ratified the charter of the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] at Davos, an organization Putin had not yet joined at the time of publication — a fact Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted while predicting that when Putin does join, the rhetoric between him and Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] would not be "excessively cordial."
