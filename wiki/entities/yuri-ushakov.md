# Yuri Ushakov

Yuri Ushakov is a Kremlin foreign policy aide to Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1]

## Leaked call with Steve Witkoff

In November 2025, Bloomberg News published a transcript of a leaked telephone call between Ushakov and [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], the U.S. special envoy appointed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Critics of the administration seized on the transcript to accuse Witkoff of one-sidedness toward [[russia|Russia]] at a moment when the Trump administration was advancing a Kremlin-friendly framework to end the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

Putin publicly defended Witkoff over the leak, calling him "an intelligent man" who was properly representing the position of his country and its leader, and observing that since Witkoff was traveling to Moscow on Trump's orders to negotiate, "it would probably be surprising if he cursed us with obscenities in his conversations with Ushakov."[1] Putin made the remarks at a news conference in Kyrgyzstan during a state visit.[1]

## October 2025 call with Steve Witkoff

On October 14, 2025, Ushakov held a phone call of just over five minutes with [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s special envoy for peace missions; Bloomberg published a transcript on November 25, 2025.[2] Ushakov joked that only one problem needed solving — the Russian-Ukrainian war — and asked Witkoff whether it would be useful for "our bosses" to talk on the phone, a suggestion Witkoff endorsed.[2] When Witkoff mentioned that [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]] would be at the White House that Friday, Ushakov chuckled and said he already knew.[2]
