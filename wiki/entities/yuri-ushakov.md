# Yuri Ushakov

Yuri Ushakov is a Kremlin foreign policy aide to Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1]

## Leaked call with Steve Witkoff

In November 2025, Bloomberg News published a transcript of a leaked telephone call between Ushakov and [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], the U.S. special envoy appointed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Critics of the administration seized on the transcript to accuse Witkoff of one-sidedness toward [[russia|Russia]] at a moment when the Trump administration was advancing a Kremlin-friendly framework to end the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

Putin publicly defended Witkoff over the leak, calling him "an intelligent man" who was properly representing the position of his country and its leader, and observing that since Witkoff was traveling to Moscow on Trump's orders to negotiate, "it would probably be surprising if he cursed us with obscenities in his conversations with Ushakov."[1] Putin made the remarks at a news conference in Kyrgyzstan during a state visit.[1]

## October 2025 call with Steve Witkoff

On October 14, 2025, Ushakov held a phone call of just over five minutes with [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s special envoy for peace missions; Bloomberg published a transcript on November 25, 2025.[2] Ushakov joked that only one problem needed solving — the Russian-Ukrainian war — and asked Witkoff whether it would be useful for "our bosses" to talk on the phone, a suggestion Witkoff endorsed.[2] When Witkoff mentioned that [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]] would be at the White House that Friday, Ushakov chuckled and said he already knew.[2]

## Announcement of the 2025 Trump-Putin meeting

On Thursday, August 7, 2025, Ushakov told reporters from Russian state media that "at the suggestion of the American side, an agreement was made in principle to hold a bilateral meeting at the highest level in the coming days ... a meeting between President Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump."[3] The confirmation came as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] pressed [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] with tariff threats and a Friday deadline for a ceasefire in Ukraine.[3]

## Readout of the June 2025 Putin–Xi call

Ushakov briefed reporters on the roughly hour-long June 2025 telephone call between [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] on the worsening Middle East crisis, describing the conversation as "friendly and constructive."[4] He said Moscow and Beijing share "identical approaches" to the crisis and that both leaders "strongly condemn Israel's actions, which violate the UN Charter and other norms of international law."[4] Ushakov reported that Putin had briefed Xi on his calls with Iranian President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] and Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], and reaffirmed Moscow's "readiness to carry out possible mediation efforts, if necessary," an initiative Xi welcomed as a step that could defuse the conflict.[4] "Both Moscow and Beijing fundamentally believe that there is no military solution to the current situation or to issues related to Iran's nuclear program. This must be resolved exclusively through political and diplomatic means," the presidential aide stressed, adding that the two leaders had instructed their ministries and agencies to stay in close contact.[4]
