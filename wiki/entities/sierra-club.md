# Sierra Club

The Sierra Club is an American environmental organization that Badlands Media identified as a central non-governmental actor in the legal campaign against Mid-Atlantic natural gas infrastructure.[1]

According to Badlands commentary, the Sierra Club was among the NGOs that collaborated with state attorneys described as Soros-funded to file injunctions aimed at halting the [[mountain-valley-pipeline|Mountain Valley Pipeline]] and its sister project, the [[atlantic-coast-pipeline|Atlantic Coast Pipeline]].[1] Army Corps of Engineers verifications that the Mountain Valley Pipeline met all applicable criteria — issued December 22, 2017 and reinstated July 3, 2018 — were later documented in federal court cases between the pipeline's owners and the Sierra Club.[1]

Badlands framed the resulting delays, which pushed the Mountain Valley Pipeline years behind schedule and billions of dollars over budget, and the outright cancellation of the Atlantic Coast Pipeline in 2020, as the product of coordinated lawfare rather than ordinary environmental review.[1]
