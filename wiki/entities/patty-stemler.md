# Patty Stemler

Patty Stemler is a longtime U.S. [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] veteran described as an ally of then-Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] who had been consulted on matters relating to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## The August 10, 2022 email

Two days after the [[fbi|FBI]]'s August 8, 2022 raid on Trump's [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] residence, Stemler sent an email to National Security Division attorney [[sophia-brill|Sophia Brill]] raising internal concerns about the legal footing of the search.[1] In the message she questioned whether the former president had in fact declassified the seized documents, noting that although standard declassification procedures exist, "the President as Commander in Chief" is not necessarily bound by them — an argument she illustrated by analogy to the pardon power.[1]

The email was obtained by Just the News and was reportedly discovered during the Justice Department's own review of federal law enforcement actions and its investigation into the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization of government]].[1] Badlands commentary treated the document as evidence that the Biden-era Justice Department was less confident in the raid's legality than its public posture suggested.[1]

## Context

The search Stemler questioned had been personally approved by Garland, and it followed assertions by Trump's legal team — including a May 2022 letter from attorney [[evan-corcoran|Evan Corcoran]] — that Trump held broad declassification authority and had issued a standing order treating documents removed to his residence as declassified.[1]
