# Patty Stemler

**Patty Stemler** is a senior [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] official described in the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 as a longtime DOJ veteran and an ally of then-Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] who had been consulted on matters relating to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## August 10, 2022 email

The article reports that on August 10, 2022 — two days after the [[fbi|FBI]]'s [[mar-a-lago-raid|raid]] on [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] — Stemler emailed National Security Division attorney [[sophia-brill|Sophia Brill]] to question whether the former president had declassified the seized documents. She noted that while standard declassification procedures exist, "the President as Commander in Chief" is not necessarily bound by them, drawing an analogy to the pardon power.

The email was reportedly obtained by [[just-the-news|Just the News]] and discovered during the Justice Department's own review of federal law enforcement actions and of the [[weaponization-of-government|weaponization]] of those agencies. The source presents it as evidence of early internal doubt within the DOJ about the raid's legal footing, since it echoed arguments already advanced by Trump's attorney [[evan-corcoran|Evan Corcoran]] in a May 2022 letter.
