# Mustafa Kasubhai

Judge Kasubhai is a federal judge, appointed under the Biden administration, who in December 2025 delayed the Trump administration's effort to restrict [[supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program|SNAP]] benefits for migrants.[1]

## SNAP ruling

Ruling on Monday, December 15, 2025, in litigation brought by a coalition of 20 state attorneys general, Kasubhai held that the [[usda|US Department of Agriculture]] must allow states additional time to bring themselves into compliance with new guidance on SNAP eligibility.[1] The plaintiffs had argued that the administration failed to provide a legally required 120-day compliance period after issuing guidance on October 31, 2025 that would bar benefits for certain lawful permanent residents, including refugees and asylum seekers.[1]

Kasubhai wrote that "the inability to provide compliance in the time period in which they were forced to by virtue of the guidance contributed to an erosion of trust," and extended the grace period to April 9, 2026.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked the judge's reference to public trust, arguing that little such trust remains, while suggesting that challenging a four-month grace period was not the best use of administration resources compared with prosecuting what she called a treasonous conspiracy.[1]
