# Evgenia Gutsul

Evgenia Gutsul is the elected leader (Bashkan) of Gagauzia, the autonomous Turkic-speaking region inside [[moldova|Moldova]], and a prominent Euroskeptic political figure.[1]

## Conviction and sentence

In August 2025, a court in EU-candidate Moldova sentenced Gutsul to seven years in prison on charges of illegally financing a Euroskeptic political party.[1] She was convicted of receiving illegal campaign funds from an organized criminal group and funnelling them to the SOR party, which Moldovan authorities banned in 2023.[1] Prosecutors also accused her of paying people to take part in anti-government protests organized by SOR in 2022.[1]

In addition to the custodial sentence, Gutsul was ordered to pay 40 million Moldovan lei — roughly $2.3 million — to the state.[1] Svetlana Popan, a former secretary in the SOR Central Office, received a six-year sentence in the same case.[1] Both sentences can be appealed.[1]

Gutsul denied the accusations and characterized the prosecution as politically motivated.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry framed the jailing as evidence of what he called the European Union's "final form: liberal tyranny," invoking the adage that "in order to protect democracy, we must suspend democracy," and asking whether a political leader should be imprisoned for seven years "for the crime of winning an election."[1] The commentary situated Gagauzia geographically within Moldova and Moldova itself relative to Ukraine, arguing that the optics of the case were badly damaging to the neoliberal establishment in Europe.[1]
