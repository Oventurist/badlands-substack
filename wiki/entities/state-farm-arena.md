# State Farm Arena

State Farm Arena is an indoor venue in Atlanta, Georgia, which served as an absentee ballot processing location for Fulton County during the 2020 presidential election. Video from the arena on election night became one of the most-litigated pieces of evidence in disputes over the Georgia result.[1]

## Role in 2020 election disputes

Georgia officials maintained that investigations by the Secretary of State's Office, the [[georgia-bureau-of-investigation|Georgia Bureau of Investigation]] and others had established that nothing illegal occurred at the arena on election night 2020.[1] When Fulton County prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] put that proposition to former U.S. Senator [[david-perdue|David Perdue]] before the special grand jury on September 6, 2022, Perdue declined to endorse it, answering only, "I agree that an investigation happened, yes," and later, "Not to my knowledge. Not to my satisfaction."[1] Perdue's refusal to accept the official account led directly into his testimony about the alleged suppression of a GBI investigation by Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]].[1]
