# Live Nation Entertainment

**Live Nation Entertainment** is the American live-events conglomerate that owns [[ticketmaster|Ticketmaster]], the dominant ticketing platform in the United States.[1]

## DOJ antitrust lawsuit, May 2024

On Thursday, May 23, 2024, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced an antitrust lawsuit against Live Nation, accusing the company of unlawfully dominating the live-events market.[1] Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] said at a press conference: "We are not here today because Live Nation-Ticketmaster's conduct is inconvenient or frustrating. We are here because, as we allege, that conduct is anti-competitive and illegal. It is time to break it up."[1]

The complaint alleged that Live Nation and Ticketmaster had used their "power and influence to insert themselves at the center and the edges of virtually every aspect of the live music ecosystem," enabling them to stifle innovation and control how fans buy tickets and where artists can perform.[1] The filing further stated that this had given the companies "the opportunity to freeze innovation and bend the industry to their own benefit," with "a real cost to Americans."[1]

## Taylor Swift trigger

The suit followed Ticketmaster's botched late-2022 presale for [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]]'s Eras Tour, in which fans competed with bots for a limited pool of tickets and the platform crashed.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Ticketmaster's conduct was long-standing and familiar to fans of acts such as Dave Matthews Band and Dead & Company, asking why it took Taylor Swift's displeasure for the DOJ to act.[1] She linked the suit to the Biden administration's earlier campaign against Ticketmaster "junk fees" and speculated about who at the company had "crossed the Bidens."[1]
