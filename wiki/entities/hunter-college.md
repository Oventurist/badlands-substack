# Hunter College

Hunter College is a public college in New York City whose president, [[jennifer-raab|Jennifer Raab]], was nominated by [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]]'s [[saba-capital|Saba Capital]] to the board of BlackRock Capital Allocation Trust during its 2024 campaign against ten [[blackrock|BlackRock]] closed-end funds.[1] The institution appears in Badlands Media coverage chiefly as the source of Raab's public standing and institutional connections.[1]
