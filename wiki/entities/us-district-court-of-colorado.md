# United States District Court for the District of Colorado

The United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the federal trial court for the state of Colorado. In July 2024 it decided [[naacp-v-useip|*NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.*]], a voter-intimidation suit brought by three civil-rights organizations against election-integrity activists.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

Presided over by Judge [[charlotte-sweeney|Charlotte Sweeney]], the court entered judgement against the plaintiffs on partial findings under Rule 52(c), halting the trial at the close of the plaintiffs' case and awarding costs to the defendants — an outcome the prevailing pro se defendant described as shattering the narrative that "election deniers" are the greatest threat to democracy.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back) The court found no evidence in the record that could remotely be perceived as intimidating or threatening.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

The court's handling of the case is cited as evidence that the checks against tortious abuse built into the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure can eventually work, though in this instance only after two and a half years of motions practice and a trial.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)
