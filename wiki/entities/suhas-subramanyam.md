# Suhas Subramanyam

Suhas Subramanyam is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Virginia who sits on the [[house-oversight-committee]].

In the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, Subramanyam appears as a participant in the committee's questioning of former Obama White House counsel [[kathryn-ruemmler]] over her long association with [[jeffrey-epstein]]. Speaking to Fox News Digital, he said Ruemmler "knew that this wasn't a man of good character, certainly, and someone who is a sex offender and registered as such," but "she saw dollar signs, and she decided that she would overlook all that because he was rich and powerful."

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america]] singled out the remark as "super triggering," arguing that a member of [[congress]] policing whom private citizens choose to associate with runs against the [[first-amendment]] and the right of freedom of association. Her critique positioned Subramanyam's comment as emblematic of a broader willingness in Congress to substitute guilt by association for prosecution of actual conduct.
