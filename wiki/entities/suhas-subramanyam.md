# Suhas Subramanyam

Suhas Subramanyam is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Virginia who participated in the [[united-states-house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]]'s July 2026 questioning of former Obama White House counsel [[kathryn-ruemmler|Kathryn Ruemmler]].[1]

Speaking to Fox News Digital after the closed-door interview, Subramanyam said Ruemmler "knew that this wasn't a man of good character, certainly, and someone who is a sex offender and registered as such," but "she saw dollar signs, and she decided that she would overlook all that because he was rich and powerful."[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] singled out those remarks as "super triggering," arguing they showed disregard for the First Amendment right of free association and asking whether "guilt by association" had become acceptable.[1]
