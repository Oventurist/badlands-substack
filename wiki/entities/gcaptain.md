# gCaptain

**gCaptain** is a website specializing in tracking the shipping industry, led by chief executive officer Captain [[john-konrad|John Konrad]].[1]

The outlet's reporting figured prominently in coverage of the [[francis-scott-key-bridge-collapse|Francis Scott Key Bridge collapse]] at the Port of Baltimore, where Konrad's assessment that the container ship [[dali|Dali]] was sitting atop a high-pressure underwater gas line indicated the salvage operation would likely be delayed pending a survey of the line and further risk assessment.[1]
