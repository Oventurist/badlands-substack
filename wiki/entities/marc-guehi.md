# Marc Guehi

Marc Guehi is a professional footballer and captain for the English Premier League club [[crystal-palace-fc|Crystal Palace]] who became the subject of a religious-messaging dispute in December 2024 after writing a Christian message on his league-issued armband.[1]

## The "I love Jesus" armband

During a match over the preceding weekend, the words "I love Jesus" were written on Guehi's Premier League-issued LGBTQ+ campaign rainbow captain's armband.[1] The [[football-association|Football Association]] was understood to be preparing to contact Guehi to remind him that religious messaging is banned from equipment, since the laws of football prohibit any religious messaging appearing on jerseys.[1] The formal reminder would avoid the need for disciplinary action from the FA, and Crystal Palace was also to be contacted to be reminded of the regulations.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] that there should be no rules restricting peaceful expression of religious belief, framing the incident as evidence that "religious messaging is banned from the Premiere League Football Association, unless the religion is rainbow communism."[1] She linked the case to that of Ontario mayor [[harold-mcquaker|Harold McQuaker]] and argued that the globally acceptable state religion is [[scientism|scientism]], presenting as climate change or gender ideology.[1]
