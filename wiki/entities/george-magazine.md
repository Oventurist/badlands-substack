# George (magazine)

*GEORGE* was the politics-and-culture magazine founded by [[jfk-jr|John F. Kennedy Jr.]], and it occupies a central place in Badlands Media's essay "Heroes of the Great Awakening."[1] The article describes Kennedy Jr. as "the candidate who seemed poised to carry on the legacy of transparency a generation ago," and identifies the magazine — together with his projected run for a New York Senate seat — as the twin vehicles through which he might have risen to national power.[1]

In the author's reading, *GEORGE* was not merely a publishing venture but an instrument of the transparency politics associated with [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], whose warning that "the very word 'secrecy' is repugnant in a free and open society" the essay quotes at length.[1] A magazine run by a Kennedy heir, aimed at making politics legible to a mass readership, is presented as precisely the kind of enterprise that would have made him "a major threat to the establishment."[1]

## Revival

The article reports that in the present era "we have a new *GEORGE* Magazine in production" alongside "a new 'Great Awakening' in progress," treating the revival as part of a broader recurrence of 1960s-era themes.[1] The author pairs this with the publication of a best-selling book by [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|RFK Jr.]], described as "the world's preeminent thought-leader for medical freedom," to argue that "if history doesn't repeat itself, it definitely rhymes."[1]
