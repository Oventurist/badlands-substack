# Defense Counterintelligence Command

The **Defense Counterintelligence Command** (DCC) is a military intelligence organ of [[south-korea|South Korea]] that figured centrally in the December 2024 martial law crisis. At the relevant time it was led by Yeo In-hyeong, described in reporting as a high school classmate of then–Defense Minister [[kim-yong-hyun|Kim Yong-hyun]].[1]

## Role in the martial law plan

Democratic Party lawmaker Park Beom-kye alleged that the Command "planned the specifics" of an operation to send unmanned drones over Pyongyang, suggesting the plan "was conceived to offer a pretext for the invocation of martial law."[1]

A reference document on the operation of martial law troops and a joint investigation headquarters, issued in November 2024 under Yeo's orders and made public by the Democratic Party, showed that the Command had reviewed the possibility of declaring martial law and a state of united defense simultaneously, in order to execute military responses and control public order and security in the event of a crisis such as armed conflict with [[north-korea|North Korea]].[1] The document stated that martial law and united defense could be declared simultaneously in the case of "enemy infiltration, provocation and domestic circumstances."[1]
