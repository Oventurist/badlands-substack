# Kevin Morris

Kevin Morris is a Hollywood lawyer and financial patron of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], often described in press coverage as the first son's "sugar brother." He became a subject of congressional interest after a whistleblower alleged that the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] had blocked federal investigators from interviewing him.[1]

## CIA intervention allegation

According to a whistleblower account relayed to House impeachment leaders, the CIA stopped Internal Revenue Service and [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] investigators from interviewing Morris in August 2021, during a five-year probe into Hunter Biden's alleged tax crimes.[1]

The whistleblower informed [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight]] chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] (R-Ky.) and Judiciary chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] (R-Ohio) that two DOJ officials were summoned to CIA headquarters in Langley, Virginia, and told that Morris "could not be a witness" for their investigation.[1] The allegation was set out in a letter addressed to CIA Director [[william-burns|William Burns]].[1]

The episode was carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of May 23, 2024.[1]
