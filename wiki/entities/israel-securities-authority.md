# Israel Securities Authority

The Israel Securities Authority (ISA) is the statutory regulator of [[israel|Israel]]'s capital markets, with jurisdiction over trading conducted on the [[tel-aviv-stock-exchange|Tel Aviv Stock Exchange]].[1]

## October 7 trading investigation

In December 2023 the ISA became the lead Israeli body examining allegations, raised by U.S. law professors [[robert-jackson-jr|Robert J. Jackson Jr.]] and [[joshua-mitts|Joshua Mitts]], that traders with foreknowledge of the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 Hamas attack]] had shorted Israeli securities for profit.[1] When [[reuters|Reuters]] approached the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange about the research, the exchange referred the matter to the ISA, which issued a brief statement: "The matter is known to the authority and is under investigation by all the relevant parties."[1]

The regulator's spokesperson declined to elaborate, and Israeli police did not respond promptly to inquiries.[1] The researchers argued that regulators — including the [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]] and the [[financial-industry-regulatory-authority|Financial Industry Regulatory Authority]], which possess the identities of the traders involved — should press further to understand how markets may have anticipated the attack.[1] The investigation is a central strand of the [[israel-october-7-foreknowledge-allegations|Israel October 7 foreknowledge allegations]].[1]
