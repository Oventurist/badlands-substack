# Peter Mandelson

Peter Mandelson is a British Labour politician and former United Kingdom ambassador to the United States.

The February 11, 2026 Badlands Brief refers to Mandelson as an "[[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]]-linked former ambassador" whose associated scandal has generated resignation calls against Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]], who holds record-low approval ratings. The Brief places the affair within its broader account of European incumbents weakened by domestic unpopularity — alongside [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] and [[friedrich-merz|Friedrich Merz]] — and within [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s observation that the [[media-protectorate|Media Protectorate]] has been "rhetorically sacrificing some of their own overseas" while attacking [[donald-trump|Trump]] and his allies over the Epstein files.
