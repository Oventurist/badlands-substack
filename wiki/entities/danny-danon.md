# Danny Danon

Danny Danon is [[israel|Israel]]'s Ambassador to the [[united-nations|United Nations]]. He enters the Badlands corpus through a June 2026 social media controversy over remarks that Badlands writers interpreted as a call to compile lists of Americans critical of the Israeli government.

## The list remarks

The June 5, 2026 Badlands Brief describes Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] checking [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]]'s X feed for reaction to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s conciliatory comments about Iran's leadership. Levin had not addressed Trump's remarks, but the author found a repost of Danon in which the ambassador appeared to propose that a list be compiled of Americans who criticize his government, with those Americans to face some form of retribution.

The Brief's commentary responds with the question "A list? Like the lists that the Nazis used to make?" and treats the exchange as evidence of a foreign government seeking the persecution of American citizens for exercising [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] rights. Former National Security Advisor [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]], described as nodding along on stage as Danon spoke, is condemned in the same passage. The author frames the episode within a broader argument that international opinion is turning against Israel not because of Jewishness but because of what he characterizes as despotic conduct and the use of accusations of antisemitism to subjugate other nations.
