# Danny Danon

Danny Danon is Israel's Ambassador to the [[united-nations|United Nations]]. He enters Badlands Media coverage in June 2026 through a social-media repost in which the compilation of a list of Americans critical of the Israeli government was proposed, with those individuals to face some form of retribution.[1]

## The list proposal

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] encountered the repost while surveying reaction to [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s newly conciliatory posture toward [[iran|Iran]]'s leadership, noting that commentator [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] had not yet addressed Trump's remarks.[1] The writer likened the proposed list to Nazi-era practices and stressed that the conduct to be punished — criticism of a foreign government — is protected by the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1] Former National Security Advisor [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]], present on the same stage, was described as nodding along in agreement, which the author treated as evidence of a broader betrayal by American officials.[1]

## Broader framing

The episode was folded into a wider argument that international opinion is turning against Israel not for reasons of religion but because of the conduct of its government, described in the commentary as despotic and as relying on accusations of antisemitism to deflect criticism.[1]


## Response to the September 2025 recognitions

As Israel's ambassador to the [[united-nations|United Nations]], Danon responded to the wave of recognitions of [[palestinian-statehood|Palestinian statehood]] announced in New York on September 22, 2025 by telling reporters, "We will take action."[2] He declined to say whether that would mean, as some Israeli officials had suggested, expanded settlements or outright annexations in the territory envisioned as a Palestinian state, saying Israel's response would be announced by Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], who was to address the UN gathering and meet that week with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]

## Response to Abbas at the United Nations (2024)

As Israel's ambassador to the United Nations, Danon responded to [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]]'s September 2024 General Assembly speech with a statement accusing the Palestinian president of speaking about a peaceful solution only when addressing the United Nations, and of failing to condemn the October 7 attack by [[hamas|Hamas]] that triggered the war in the Hamas-run Gaza Strip.[3] Badlands Media disputed that characterization, noting that Abbas had publicly criticized Hamas for failing to inform the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] of the planned attack and had blamed the group for the destruction that followed.[3]
