# Rivian

**Rivian** is an American electric vehicle manufacturer cited in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]].[1] It was one of the first five electric car companies Carlson listed from memory when testing whether climate policy had in fact enriched the industry it ostensibly favored.[1]

Measuring from [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s inauguration on January 20, 2021 to the time of writing in May 2023, Carlson reported Rivian's stock down 90 percent — the second-worst performance in his sample after [[nikola|Nikola]], and part of a uniformly negative result across [[tesla|Tesla]], Rivian, [[polestar|Polestar]], and [[nio|Nio]].[1] Carlson treated the breadth of the decline as the significant fact: individual company-specific explanations could not account for every EV maker falling at once during a period of aggressive pro-EV policy, which he argued pointed to an agenda other than the stated one.[1]
