# John Wilkes Booth

**John Wilkes Booth** was the assassin of President [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]], described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as having been paid and directed by Confederate intelligence networks operating out of British Canada.[1]

Ehret writes that in the 19th century Canada was recognized as a principal enemy of the United States, because Confederate intelligence enjoyed vast patronage and support from the British Empire through its web of intelligence bases in Montreal and Toronto.[1] These networks not only coordinated terrorist operations against the Union from the north but additionally paid and directed Booth to murder Lincoln after the war's end.[1]

Ehret cites Barry Sheehy's 2017 book *Montreal: City of Secrets* as laying out these facts in detail.[1]
