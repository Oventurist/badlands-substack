# Jeremy Corbyn

Jeremy Corbyn is a British politician, a former leader of the Labour Party, and a sitting Member of Parliament in the British House of Commons. He is described as the figurehead of the pro-Gaza "Your Party" faction within the Commons, and is characterized in Badlands coverage as a figure of the far left of British politics.[1]

## Involvement in the 2025 New York City mayoral race

Corbyn drew attention in the United States in November 2025 when he joined a phone-banking session on November 2 in support of [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]], the Democratic candidate in the [[new-york-city-mayoral-election-2025|New York City mayoral election]] held on November 4. The event was organized by the New York City chapter of the [[democratic-socialists-of-america|Democratic Socialists of America]] using the Action Network platform.[1]

Corbyn's participation prompted the [[department-of-justice|U.S. Department of Justice]] to review concerns about possible foreign interference in the campaign. Under U.S. federal law, foreign nationals are prohibited from contributing money to campaigns or engaging in campaign decision-making, though [[federal-election-commission|Federal Election Commission]] guidelines permit unpaid volunteer work in limited circumstances. Legal experts quoted in reporting on the episode said Corbyn's participation likely did not violate federal law, and the Justice Department had not opened a formal investigation.[1]

Commentators framed the incident less as a clear legal violation than as an illustration of how organizations such as the Democratic Socialists of America blur the line between domestic political organizing and international collaboration.[1]
