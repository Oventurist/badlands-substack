# Tomb of Saint Peter

**Tomb of Saint Peter** — in the Badlands corpus, a religious site in [[lebanon|Lebanon]] venerated in connection with the Apostle Peter, reported to have been destroyed "by accident" by the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] during the 2026 campaign. The Brief of April 20, 2026 cites its destruction alongside the smashing of a statue of Jesus in the Christian village of Debl as incidents that "can only be defined as terrorism."

## Context

The destruction is presented in the Brief as part of a pattern of assaults on Christian sites and communities in southern Lebanon during the Israeli occupation: the desecration of the Debl statue (verified as authentic by the IDF, with Foreign Minister [[gideon-saar|Gideon Sa'ar]] calling it "grave and disgraceful"), the warning by [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]]'s government that Lebanese Christians should not return to their villages despite the ceasefire, and the claim that Christians are among the Palestinians tortured in Israeli custody. The repeated "accidental" destruction of religious sites is read by the corpus as a systematic feature of the campaign rather than a coincidence.

## Significance

For Badlands commentators the Tomb of Saint Peter episode is evidence for their broader claim that the IDF and the settler movement have abandoned the moral foundations of "Western Civilization (Christianity)" — the framing under which they argue the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel project]] has become a war on the region's Christian population.
