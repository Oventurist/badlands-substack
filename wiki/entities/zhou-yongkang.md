# Zhou Yongkang

Zhou Yongkang is a former Chinese security chief and member of the [[politburo-standing-committee|Politburo Standing Committee]] of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]].[1] The Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" lists him among six party leaders removed early in [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s rule for alleged coup attempts.[1]

## Removal

The article groups Zhou with [[bo-xilai|Bo Xilai]], top [[hu-jintao|Hu Jintao]] aide [[ling-jihua|Ling Jihua]], and generals [[xu-caihou|Xu Caihou]], [[guo-boxiong|Guo Boxiong]] and [[sun-zhengcai|Sun Zhengcai]] as figures whose removal marked the opening phase of Xi's consolidation of power.[1] The author reads these removals not as simple factional score-settling but as part of a systematic dismantling of patronage networks that had been permitted to flourish under the preceding administration.[1]
