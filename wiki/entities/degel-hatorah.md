# Degel HaTorah

Degel HaTorah is an Israeli ultra-Orthodox political party representing the "Lithuanian" (non-Hasidic) stream of Haredi Judaism.[1] Alongside the Hasidic Agudat Yisrael, it is one of the two factions that together constitute the [[united-torah-judaism|United Torah Judaism]] alliance in the Knesset.[1]

## Leadership and the 2025 draft crisis

The party's spiritual leader is Rabbi [[dov-lando|Dov Lando]], one of the most prominent rabbinic authorities of Lithuanian ultra-Orthodoxy.[1] On August 6, 2025, following the arrest of two Haredi draft evaders, a spokesman for Lando accused the Israeli government of waging war on the Haredi community and warned that "Haredi Judaism will embark on a global struggle like never before."[1]

The statement was issued after Lando convened an emergency consultation with Haredi activists at his home in Bnei Brak in order to build a consensus response across the various Haredi sub-groups.[1] The episode underscored the centrality of the conscription question to Degel HaTorah's political identity and to the stability of Israeli governing coalitions that depend on Haredi parties.[1]
