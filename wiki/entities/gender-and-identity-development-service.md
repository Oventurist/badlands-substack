# Gender and Identity Development Service (GIDS)

The **Gender and Identity Development Service (GIDS)** was the gender identity clinic operated under the [[tavistock-clinic|Tavistock]] umbrella within the British National Health Service.[1] As reported by *The Independent* on 11 August 2022 and quoted in the Badlands source, the clinic faced mass legal action over claims that children were misdiagnosed and rushed into transitioning at a young age.[1]

An independent review criticised the quality of care and services provided to patients, who were predominantly young teenagers expressing an interest in gender transitioning, and [[nhs-england|NHS England]] moved to shut the clinic down.[1] Staff, patients and parents raised concerns that young people using the service were placed on the pathway to transitioning too early and before proper assessment, with allegations that children were "rushed into taking life-altering puberty blockers without adequate consideration or proper diagnosis" while staff were under pressure to adopt an "unquestioning affirmative approach."[1] GIDS had treated 19,000 children with gender dysphoria since 1989.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] adds the observation that almost all of those 19,000 cases occurred between 2012 and the clinic's 2022 closure, characterising 1989–2012 as a research phase and 2012 as the point at which the transitioning push began in earnest.[1] He links the timing to the repeal at the end of 2012 of the [[smith-mundt-act|Smith-Mundt Act]] of 1948 by the Obama administration, which he says legalised domestic propaganda against Americans and was immediately followed by transgender bathrooms becoming a hot topic in the United States.[1]
