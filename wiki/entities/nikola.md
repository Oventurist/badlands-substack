# Nikola

Nikola is an American electric- and hydrogen-truck startup that went public during the electric-vehicle listing boom of the early 2020s.[1] A December 2023 Wall Street Journal analysis identified Nikola among at least 18 EV and battery companies at risk of running out of cash by the end of 2024 based on their most recent filings.[1]

The Journal grouped Nikola with [[fisker|Fisker]] as firms that attracted investors with bold promises to transform the industry and fight climate change with electric trucks and SUVs, before stumbling amid rising costs and manufacturing problems.[1] The median stock among the companies examined had fallen more than 80% from its market debut, part of a broader [[2023-electric-vehicle-startup-shakeout|EV startup shakeout]] that erased tens of billions of dollars in market value within a few years.[1]
