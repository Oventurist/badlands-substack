# Congressional Hispanic Caucus

The Congressional Hispanic Caucus is a caucus of Hispanic members of the United States Congress. It served as the venue for remarks by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in September 2023 in which he blamed Republicans for undermining border security.[1]

## The September 2023 Biden address

Addressing the caucus on Thursday, September 21, 2023, Biden claimed that Republicans were responsible for undermining the security of the southern border, despite what Summit News described as a massive surge in illegal immigration under his administration.[1] He further asserted that former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had "spent four years gutting the immigration system," and said he had "developed federal experts" to handle the influx.[1]

The remarks followed the refusal of Biden's press secretary to answer questions about the surge — including a single-day influx of more than 10,000 crossings that week — which she dismissed sarcastically.[1] The item was carried by [[summit-news|Summit News]].[1]
