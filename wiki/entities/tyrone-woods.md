# Tyrone Woods

Tyrone "Rone" Woods was a former U.S. Navy SEAL and CIA security contractor who was killed during the September 11, 2012 terrorist attack on the U.S. consulate in [[benghazi|Benghazi]], Libya. He is remembered as one of the heroes who responded to the attack despite being ordered to stand down.

## Background and Service

Woods had served for more than 20 years in the U.S. Navy as a SEAL before transitioning to private security work as a CIA contractor. On the night of the Benghazi attack, he was part of the CIA security team stationed at the agency's annex in the city. Along with [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]] and other team members, Woods heard the calls for help from the besieged consulate and attempted to mount a rescue operation.

## Death and Legacy

According to the source, Woods and Doherty were among four Americans killed in the Benghazi attack, along with Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] and information management officer [[sean-smith|Sean Smith]]. Woods and Doherty were killed by a mortar round while providing security at the CIA annex during the extended assault.

The article notes that CIA director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] forced surviving CIA operatives involved in the Benghazi rescue efforts to sign new non-disclosure agreements during a memorial service for Woods and Doherty at CIA headquarters in Langley, Virginia, more than six months after the attack.
