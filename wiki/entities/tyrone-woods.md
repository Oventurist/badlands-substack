# Tyrone Woods

Tyrone Woods was a former Navy SEAL and CIA-affiliated security operator killed during the September 11, 2012 [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]], alongside Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]], information management officer Sean Smith and fellow former SEAL [[glen-doherty|Glen Doherty]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The Weekly Standard reported that a memorial service was held at [[cia|CIA]] headquarters in Langley, Virginia in May 2013 honoring Woods and Doherty.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) It was at that memorial service that two former CIA officials who had fought in Benghazi — and who were due to testify before a subcommittee of the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence — were presented with additional [[non-disclosure-agreements|non-disclosure agreements]] to sign, more than six months after the attacks.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The article calls this the worst element of the cover-up, arguing that the lives lost mattered less to officials than concealing the scandal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
