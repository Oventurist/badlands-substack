# Gali Baharav-Miara

Gali Baharav-Miara is the Attorney General of Israel, the state's senior legal officer, whose intervention in the attempted firing of [[ronen-bar|Ronen Bar]] placed her in direct conflict with Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] in March 2025.[1]

## Conflict with Netanyahu

Baharav-Miara advised Netanyahu that he could not proceed with the dismissal of [[shin-bet|Shin Bet]] chief Ronen Bar unless a "factual and legal basis" for the removal could first be established.[1] On Monday, March 17, 2025, Netanyahu publicly accused her of abusing her authority in issuing that advice.[1]

Israeli press reporting indicated that the offices of both the prime minister and the attorney general were separately examining whether the government required approval from a commission that vets candidates for key civil service positions before it could fire Bar, a review that could have postponed the dismissal by weeks.[1]

## Investigation of Sara Netanyahu

On December 26, Attorney General Baharav-Miara and State Attorney [[amit-aisman|Amit Aisman]] announced that they had ordered an investigation of [[sara-netanyahu|Sara Netanyahu]] on suspicion of witness harassment and obstruction of justice.[2] The order followed a Channel 12 "Uvda" report alleging she had directed an aide to orchestrate protests and an online campaign against [[hadas-klein|Hadas Klein]], a key witness in the corruption trial of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[2] The resulting probe is being conducted by the Israel Police and the cybercrimes department of the State Attorney's Office.[2]

## Government vote to dismiss (August 2025)

In August 2025 the Israeli government voted unanimously to fire Baharav-Miara while she was prosecuting Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] for corruption; the [[israeli-high-court-of-justice|Israeli High Court]] immediately suspended the move.[3] Netanyahu and his supporters accused her of exceeding her powers by blocking decisions of the elected government, including an attempt to dismiss the head of [[shin-bet|Israel's domestic security agency]].[3] She called the dismissal "unlawful," vowed that her office would remain faithful to the law, and warned that the vote was "a fateful blow to the rule of law, equality before the law, human rights, and the ability of the law enforcement system to deal with government corruption."[3]

Badlands commentary noted that Baharav-Miara had been appointed by Prime Minister Naftali Bennett in 2022 on a nomination submitted in November 2021 by then Justice Minister Gideon Sa'ar, now Netanyahu's Foreign Minister, and argued she was targeted because of her investigation into corruption linked to Qatar and the flow of money to Hamas through Netanyahu's office, in which several aides had already been arrested and charged.[3] Though technically still in post, all departments were instructed not to cooperate with her — the tactic previously used against former Shin Bet Director [[ronen-bar|Ronen Bar]].[3] See [[2025-attempt-to-dismiss-israels-attorney-general|the 2025 dismissal attempt]].[3]
