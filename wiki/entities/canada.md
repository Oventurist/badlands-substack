# Canada

Canada is the United States' northern neighbour and, in the February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]], one of the allied states whose relations with Washington are described as strained by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s statements and actions.

The Brief reports that Trump threatened to block the early-2026 opening of the $4.6 billion [[gordie-howe-international-bridge|Gordie Howe International Bridge]] between Windsor, Ontario and Detroit, Michigan, posting that the project featured "virtually no US content" and accusing Canada of unfairly benefiting while offering "Absolutely NOTHING" in return. He connected the dispute to Canadian trade plans with China, warning that these could trigger 100% tariffs, a potential ban on ice hockey in Canada, and elimination of the Stanley Cup.

Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] responded by describing a positive conversation with Trump, emphasising that Canada had fully funded the bridge's construction, that ownership is shared with Michigan, and that steel and workers from both countries were involved — framing the project as a model of bilateral cooperation.
