# Laurene Powell Jobs

Laurene Powell Jobs is the founder of Emerson Collective and, according to Badlands Media, a member of the Board of Directors of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1]

"The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 1" lists her among current board members alongside [[blackrock|BlackRock]] CEO [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]] and Alphabet and Google CFO [[ruth-porat|Ruth Porat]], presenting the board as a "who's who of globalist financiers, establishment politicians, representatives of the military-industrial complex and heads of the world's largest corporations."[1] The article also describes her as a confidant of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]].[1]

## Friendship with Ghislaine Maxwell

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in February 2023, raised the relationship between Powell Jobs and [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] as a neglected thread in the [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]] scandal.[2] In a parenthetical aside within his essay "The Road Without Struggle," he asked why no one seems to know or care that Maxwell and Steve Jobs's wife were best friends, and whether [[apple]] is too big and important to tie to the scandal.[2]
