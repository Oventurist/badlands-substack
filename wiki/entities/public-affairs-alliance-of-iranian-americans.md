# Public Affairs Alliance of Iranian Americans

The Public Affairs Alliance of Iranian Americans (PAAIA) is the organization behind the Iranian American Political Action Committee (IAPAC), a political action committee whose donation history drew scrutiny in July 2023 reporting by [[the-national-pulse|The National Pulse]].[1]

## Donations and beneficiaries

The report documented contributions to the PAC from Florida homebuilder [[morteza-hosseini|Morteza "Mori" Hosseini]] between 2004 and 2011, made under his name and under the aliases "Morteza Hosseini Kargar" and "Morteza Hosseini-Kargar."[1] Over the same period, the PAC funded a range of Democratic groups, including Friends of Hillary, associated with [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], and Friends of Weiner, associated with [[anthony-weiner|Anthony Weiner]].[1] Money that reached Republicans came in far smaller sums and went to groups supporting former Republican leader [[paul-ryan|Paul Ryan]], [[dan-crenshaw|Dan Crenshaw]], and [[ben-sasse|Ben Sasse]].[1]

The article also linked the PAC's network to support for the Iran nuclear deal negotiated under the Obama and Biden administrations, and to criticism of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The reporting connected these donors to Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] through the Hosseinis' role as major benefactors.[1]
