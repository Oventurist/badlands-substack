# Eli Cohen

Eli Cohen is an Israeli politician serving as Energy Minister and a member of the Likud party.[1]

## West Bank sovereignty measures

In February 2026, Cohen praised the Israeli Security Cabinet's approval of [[west-bank-annexation|West Bank measures]] as establishing "de facto sovereignty" that rules out a Palestinian state.[1] His characterization stood in direct contrast to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s declaration, made ahead of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s Washington visit, that "I am against annexation."[1]
