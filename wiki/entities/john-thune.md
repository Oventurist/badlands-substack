# John Thune

John Thune is the Majority Leader of the United States [[senate|Senate]].

## Fraud prosecutor confirmation

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] announced the creation of a new assistant attorney general position focused on investigating fraud tied to federally backed programs, and that the position will require Senate confirmation. Vance said a nominee was expected within days and added that Thune had pledged swift confirmation. The initiative follows claims that taxpayers lost billions of dollars in [[minnesota|Minnesota]] through the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Somali-linked fraud]] schemes.


## April 2026: Brennan records release effort

The Brief of April 20, 2026, citing [[just-the-news|Just the News]], reports that aides to Senate Majority Leader Thune told the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] he was seeking unanimous consent to release records connected to former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], discussing the matter with Democrats to avoid a floor vote. The records had been requested by the Justice Department with a February 23 deadline and had not been transmitted as of the report.
