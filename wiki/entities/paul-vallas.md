# Paul Vallas

Paul Vallas is an American public administrator and politician, best known as a former chief executive of Chicago Public Schools and as a candidate for mayor of Chicago.[1]

## 2023 Chicago mayoral runoff

Vallas was the moderate Democratic candidate in the April 2023 runoff election for mayor of Chicago, facing the progressive candidate [[brandon-johnson|Brandon Johnson]].[1] He campaigned as a business-friendly, law-and-order candidate, in contrast to Johnson's platform of defunding the Chicago Police Department and raising city taxes.[1]

Vallas led Johnson for most of election night until roughly 8 p.m., when Johnson began overtaking him.[1] More than 500,000 Chicagoans cast ballots in the mayoral contest, and Johnson ultimately maintained a narrow lead, with counting of absentee and mail-in ballots continuing after Election Night.[1] During the campaign Johnson characterized Vallas as a racist who was "really a Republican."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] noted the late surge of votes that carried Johnson past Vallas, suggesting it might indicate irregularities, and asked whether Chicago had been closer to flipping Republican than commonly assumed.[1]
