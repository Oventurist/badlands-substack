# House Committee on Homeland Security

The Committee on Homeland Security is a standing committee of the United States House of Representatives with jurisdiction over border security, counterterrorism, and the Department of Homeland Security and its components.[1]

## June 2023 hearing on nation-state actors in Latin America

On June 21, 2023, the committee's Subcommittee on Counterterrorism, Law Enforcement, and Intelligence held a hearing titled "Threats Posed to the Homeland by Nation-State Actors in Latin America." Several lawmakers used the hearing to raise concerns about dangers posed to the United States by an unsecured southern border.[1]

House Republicans stated at the hearing that [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] had intercepted an increasing number of foreign nationals from countries outside South and Central America, including Russia, along with several high-value targets from China.[1] Representative [[august-pfluger|August Pfluger]] of Texas warned that "the chaos of the Southwest border could be taken advantage of by anti-U.S. regimes—not just can, but has been," and that the People's Republic of China and other adversaries were expanding their spheres of influence in Latin America.[1]

Commenting on the hearing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the findings to earlier reporting by [[sara-carter|Sara Carter]] of a 393 percent increase in Chinese migrants crossing the southern border, and argued that the administration's border posture amounted to something closer to troop movements than humanitarian migration.[1]
