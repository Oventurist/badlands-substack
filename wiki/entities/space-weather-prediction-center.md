# Space Weather Prediction Center

The **Space Weather Prediction Center** (SWPC) is the space weather forecasting arm of the [[national-oceanic-and-atmospheric-administration|National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration]], responsible for issuing warnings about solar activity that can affect satellites, radio communications, navigation systems, and electrical grids.[1]

The center classifies geomagnetic storms on a scale from G1 to G5, with G5 representing the most extreme category.[1] On the Friday night of the [[may-2024-g5-geomagnetic-storm|May 2024 G5 geomagnetic storm]], the SWPC warned that an "extreme" G5 storm was impacting Earth; the event lasted into the early morning hours and produced satellite orientation disturbances, degraded ionospheric radio communications, and GPS outages, but no significant US power grid failures.[1]
