# Harvard Global Health Institute

The Harvard Global Health Institute is a university-wide research body at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] that, according to Badlands Media, served alongside the [[harvard-th-chan-school-of-public-health|T.H. Chan School of Public Health]] as a conduit between Harvard research and international pandemic policy.[1]

## Role during COVID-19

The Institute provided advisors and operational models to the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] during the COVID-19 crisis, and documents published under the WHO Strategic Preparedness and Response Plan cite Harvard-originated risk algorithms and response models.[1] The contributions described include behavioral nudging frameworks intended to increase vaccine uptake, justification models for extending quarantines, and algorithmic impact assessments for misinformation response.[1]

Badlands Media argued that the researchers producing these recommendations "were not neutral scientists," noting contractual relationships with [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]] initiatives, GAVI, and the McKinsey Global Institute.[1] The article reported that one former Harvard fellow became chief data advisor for a European nation's digital health pass rollout, while another led creation of an AI-driven compliance index later embedded into multiple national reopening plans.[1]
