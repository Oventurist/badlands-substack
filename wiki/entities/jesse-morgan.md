# Jesse Morgan

Jesse Morgan is a former United States Postal Service contract truck driver who became a whistleblower after the 2020 general election, alleging that a trailer of completed ballots he hauled from New York to Pennsylvania subsequently disappeared.[1]

## Allegations

Morgan was tasked with delivering completed ballots to Pennsylvania from New York State on October 21, 2020.[1] He alleged that after he parked the trailer at the post office in Harrisburg and returned, the trailer — and allegedly hundreds of thousands of ballots — were gone.[1] Attorney [[phill-kline|Phill Kline]] stated that "130,000 to 280,000 completed ballots for the 2020 general election were shipped from Bethpage, NY, to Lancaster, PA, where those ballots and the trailer in which they were shipped disappeared."[1] Morgan spoke for roughly nine minutes about his ordeal at a December 2020 press conference held by the [[amistad-project|Amistad Project]] of the [[thomas-more-society|Thomas More Society]].[1]

## Aftermath and vindication

For coming forward, Morgan was mocked and demonized by both local and national media, Attorney General [[bill-barr|William Barr]] shut down a Senate-led investigation of the incident, and the story was memory-holed.[1] The [[united-states-postal-service|USPS]] conducted its own investigation, which reportedly concluded in June 2022; the resulting report was not published on the agency's website until December 2022 and has since been removed.[1] Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] held that the report's findings confirm Morgan's story that USPS transported ballots across state lines, that the document is so heavily redacted that its full findings are unknowable, and that it states it was submitted to federal prosecutors.[1] Ashe and [[canncon|CannCon]] covered the report in a deep dive on [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]
