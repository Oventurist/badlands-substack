# Trinity United Church of Christ

Trinity United Church of Christ is the Chicago church attended by [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and pastored by [[jeremiah-wright|Jeremiah Wright]].[1]

## Treatment in the Q drops

The congregation appears in Badlands Media's "Q for Dummies" analysis of Q Drop #117, which examines the formative influences on Obama.[1] The series describes the church as predominantly black and notes that its pastor has a record of preaching on controversial and racial themes, particularly directed against white people.[1]

Obama's attendance at Trinity is presented as one strand in a larger picture that also includes his mentors [[saul-alinsky|Saul Alinsky]] and [[khalid-al-mansour|Khalid al-Mansour]], his reading of [[post-american-world|The Post-American World]], and the claim that [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]] paid for his Harvard education through the network the drop identifies as HUMA.[1]
