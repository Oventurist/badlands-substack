# Jeff Crank

Jeff Crank is the US Representative for [[colorado|Colorado]]'s fifth congressional district and a co-plaintiff in the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]]'s 2026 lawsuit against Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] over overseas voting rules.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] criticized Crank as a legislator who "thinks he is a champion of election integrity because he voted for the Save America Act."[1] She wrote that he gave her "a word salad" when asked why citizens should be paying taxes, and that his 2026 campaign has centered mainly on expansive spending priorities.[1] She grouped him with what she called establishmentarians, identifiable by "a portfolio of half measures and an overt disdain for the People and their desired outcomes."[1]
