# Jeff Crank

Jeff Crank is the United States Representative for Colorado's fifth congressional district.

## Co-plaintiff in the Colorado overseas voting suit

As reported in "Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes", Crank joined the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] and Douglas County Clerk [[sheri-davis|Sheri Davis]] in suing Colorado Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] over guidance permitting certain never-resident U.S. citizens abroad to register and vote in Colorado under the [[uniformed-and-overseas-citizens-absentee-voting-act|Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act]].

## Assessment in Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterizes Crank as a congressman who "thinks he is a champion of election integrity because he voted for the [[save-america-act|Save America Act]]." She reports that he gave her "a word salad" when she asked why citizens should be paying taxes, and observes that his 2026 campaign has centered mainly on expansive spending priorities. In her reading he exemplifies the establishmentarian profile of half measures paired with disdain for the preferences of voters.
