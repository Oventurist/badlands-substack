# Gal Hirsch

Gal Hirsch is [[israel|Israel]]'s hostage envoy, responsible for the government's efforts to secure the release of hostages held in Gaza.[1]

## Safe-passage offer to Yahya Sinwar (September 2024)

In an interview given Tuesday, September 10, 2024, in the Bloomberg News Washington bureau, Hirsch disclosed that Israel had proposed granting [[hamas|Hamas]] leader [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]] safe passage out of Gaza in exchange for the group freeing the hostages it holds and giving up control of the strip.[1] "I'm ready to provide safe passage to Sinwar, his family, whoever wants to join him," he said, adding: "We want the hostages back. We want demilitarization, de-radicalization of course — a new system that will manage Gaza."[1]

Hirsch said he had put the offer on the table a day and a half earlier and declined to characterize the response so far, while reiterating that Israel would also be willing to release prisoners it holds as part of any deal.[1] He framed the proposal as part of an effort to devise new solutions as prospects for a cease-fire dimmed, noting that the United States, Qatar and Egypt were working to present Israel with a new cease-fire proposal but that Hamas had so far sought to dictate terms rather than negotiate.[1]

Reporting at the time judged it unclear whether Hamas would accept the proposal, particularly given Israel's history of operations targeting operatives abroad; Israel had not taken responsibility for the assassination of Hamas leader Ismail Haniyeh on July 31 in Tehran, though Iran said the bombing was Israel's handiwork.[1]
