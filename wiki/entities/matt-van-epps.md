# Matt Van Epps

Matt Van Epps is a Republican politician who won a Tennessee U.S. House special election on Tuesday, December 2, 2025.[1]

## Special election

Van Epps defeated Democrat [[aftyn-behn|Aftyn Behn]] to hold the deep-red seat, but Behn significantly cut into Republican margins in the district, continuing a streak of Democratic overperformances through 2025.[1] National outside groups poured millions of dollars into the contest as Republicans grew concerned about a potential upset.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had carried the district by 22 points the previous fall; voters' dissatisfaction with the cost of living helped Behn trim that margin.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the media narrative built around the narrowed margin, arguing the underlying electoral system itself remained unaccountable.[1] The race is covered in more detail at [[2025-tennessee-7th-congressional-district-special-election|the 2025 Tennessee special election]].[1]
