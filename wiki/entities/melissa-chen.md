# Melissa Chen

Melissa Chen is a journalist and commentator whose April 2025 investigative thread on X is credited in Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* with exposing [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s direct institutional ties to the [[xinjiang-production-and-construction-corps|Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps]] (XPCC).[1]

## Reporting on Harvard and the XPCC

Chen's findings indicated that Harvard's [[harvard-th-chan-school-of-public-health|T.H. Chan School of Public Health]] had trained XPCC officials as recently as October 2023 through the [[harvard-china-health-partnership|Harvard-China Health Partnership]].[1] She emphasized the moral contradiction involved, writing that "given its land seizures and function as a military force that suppresses the Uyghurs, the XPCC is by all accounts a repressive, colonial institution," and that the irony lay in "an elite institution that has spent decades posturing itself as a paragon of progressive values and a stalwart of social justice refusing to reckon with the moral implications of willingly training members of the XPCC."[1]

## Reception

Badlands Media presented Chen's work as corroborated rather than speculative, noting that her claims aligned with U.S. Department of State designations and with the [[uyghur-forced-labor-prevention-act|Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act]] of 2022.[1] The article's closing acknowledgments offered "special thanks to independent investigators like Melissa Chen, and Gain of Fauci, whose reporting helped uncover critical layers of the *Ivy Web*."[1]
