# Dawn Buckingham

Dawn Buckingham is the Texas Land Commissioner, a statewide elected office overseeing state lands and associated energy resources.[1]

## Response to the LNG permit pause

In January 2024 Buckingham publicly attacked the [[biden-lng-export-permit-pause|Biden administration's delay of new liquefied natural gas export terminal approvals]], writing on X that "this reckless move is nothing more than retaliation against Texas — for standing up to this administration over the border crisis."[1] Her statement reflected the widely held view among Texas officials and energy commentators that the pause, publicly justified on climate grounds, was in fact tied to the administration's escalating confrontation with Texas over enforcement at the southern border — a confrontation with direct economic stakes given Texas's position as a hub of the world's third largest LNG export industry.[1]
