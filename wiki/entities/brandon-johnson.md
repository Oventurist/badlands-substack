# Brandon Johnson

**Brandon Johnson** is the mayor of Chicago, whose rhetoric during the October 2025 federal deployment to Illinois drew sharp criticism from Badlands commentators.[1]

## 2023 election as mayor

Johnson won the April 2023 runoff for mayor of Chicago as the far-left, self-professed "progressive" candidate, campaigning on a plan to defund the Chicago Police and raise the city's taxes.[3] He defeated moderate Democrat [[paul-vallas|Paul Vallas]], the former Chicago Public Schools chief, who had run as a business-friendly, law-and-order candidate and whom Johnson's campaign labelled a racist who was "really a Republican."[3] Vallas led for most of election night until roughly 8 p.m., when Johnson began overtaking him; more than 500,000 Chicagoans cast ballots and Johnson held a slight lead as absentee and mail-in counting continued past Election Night.[3] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] flagged the late vote surge as a possible sign of foul play and wondered aloud whether Chicago had been at risk of flipping Republican.[3]

## "Civil War" remark

As [[texas-national-guard|Texas National Guard]] troops arrived in Illinois to protect federal personnel and property amid anti-immigration protests, Johnson was quoted invoking the phrase "Civil War."[1] Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] argued that Johnson had thereby said "the quiet part out loud," noting that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had never used those words and was "simply enforcing the law as ordered by Article IV Sect. 4," which requires the federal government to protect each of the states against invasion.[1]

CannCon characterized the statement as "violent rhetoric from the Commie 'Mayor'" coming as the Texas Guard moved to reinforce federal law enforcement, and noted the coincident order for the [[illinois-national-guard|Illinois National Guard]] to conduct "training" the same day.[1] He also questioned a reference to "farmers in urban cities," asking whether the phrase was code for drug dealers, and concluded that "shits about to get spicy."[1]

## March 2025 House Oversight testimony

On Wednesday, March 5, 2025, Johnson testified before the Republican-led [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] alongside fellow Democratic mayors [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] of New York, [[mike-johnston|Mike Johnston]] of Denver and [[michelle-wu|Michelle Wu]] of Boston, defending Chicago's [[sanctuary-cities|sanctuary city]] policies and its treatment of undocumented immigrants.[2] The mayors argued the policies were crucial to ensuring undocumented immigrants report crimes and access city services without fear of deportation, while Republicans accused them of defying President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s immigration agenda and releasing dangerous criminals onto the streets.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that all four mayors recited the same talking points — recasting their jurisdictions as "welcoming cities" and insisting they follow all applicable law — and concluded that they "lied — brazenly and under oath."[2]
