# Amir Ohana

Amir Ohana is an Israeli politician who serves as Speaker of the [[knesset|Knesset]], Israel's parliament. In that capacity he presides over legislative business and frequently uses the speaker's rostrum to make ideological declarations about Israeli sovereignty claims.[1]

## West Bank annexation motion

On July 24, 2025, Knesset lawmakers voted 71–13 in favor of a non-binding motion for the agenda supporting the annexation of the West Bank. Following the vote, Ohana declared that "the land of Israel belongs to the people of Israel," adding that "Jews cannot be the occupiers" of their own ancestral homeland.[1]

Ohana elaborated on the claim in explicitly historical and nativist terms: "This is our land. This is our home. The Land of Israel belongs to the people of Israel. In 1967, the occupation did not begin; it ended, and our homeland was returned to its rightful owners. We are the original first natives of this piece of land. Jews cannot be the 'occupier' of a land that for 3,000 years has been called Judea."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary treated Ohana's remarks and the accompanying vote as an admission that Israeli political ambitions extend beyond the stated war aims of the post–October 7 period. Commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry noted that the annexation declaration targeted the West Bank — where Hamas holds no political power or formal presence — rather than Gaza, undercutting the official framing that Israel's campaign was solely about dismantling Hamas and that Israel was the party pushing for a two-state solution.[1] See [[west-bank-annexation]].
