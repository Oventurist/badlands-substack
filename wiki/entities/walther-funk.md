# Walther Funk

Walther Funk was president of the Reichsbank in Nazi Germany and the author of a 1940 memorandum that features prominently in dissident accounts of the origins of the [[european-union|European Union]].[1]

## The 1940 memorandum

In 1940 Funk wrote a nine-page memo numbered H.S. 70 and titled "Economic Reorganisation of Europe," setting out Nazi post-war plans for the continent.[1] According to those plans, the Nazis intended to establish an economic zone spanning Europe and to implement a single currency capable of subjugating and undermining individual nation-states.[1]

## Claims of EU plagiarism

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry recounted that memes circulating on 4chan roughly a decade earlier placed Funk's memo side by side with the founding documents of the European Union — established in 1993 — and argued that the EU documents were plagiarized from the Nazi text, in some cases apparently copied verbatim.[1] He cited a 2020 article by Adam LeBor that called Funk a prophet who predicted the rise of the European Union, and advanced the argument that factions within the Third Reich merged with Allied counterparts to create the post-war "rules-based international order," making the EU "really just the Fourth Reich with different branding."[1]

The framing was offered in the context of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s February 2025 assertion that "the European Union was formed in order to screw the United States."[1]
