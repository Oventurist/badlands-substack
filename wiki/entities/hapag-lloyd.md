# Hapag-Lloyd

Hapag-Lloyd is a German container shipping line based in Hamburg and one of the world's major ocean carriers.

## Suspension of Cuba operations

According to the [[badlands-brief-257|Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026]], Hapag-Lloyd suspended its operations in [[cuba|Cuba]] following the May 1, 2026 executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that imposed secondary sanctions on companies doing business with [[gaesa|GAESA]], the conglomerate through which the Cuban military controls port and commercial infrastructure. The carrier's withdrawal, alongside that of [[cma-cgm|CMA CGM]] and the Canadian miner [[sherritt-international|Sherritt International]], is cited in the source as an early and consequential effect of the pressure campaign described internally as "[[accelerationism|accelerationism]]," with further departures expected among banks and hotel operators.
