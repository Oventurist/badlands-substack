# MS-13

MS-13 (Mara Salvatrucha) is a transnational criminal gang that originated in Los Angeles in the 1980s among Salvadoran immigrant communities and has since spread across Central America and the United States. It is frequently cited in U.S. immigration and criminal enforcement as one of the most violent street gangs in the country.

## Role in the Abrego Garcia case

In the Badlands corpus, MS-13 appears in the June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief as part of the criminal allegations against [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego Garcia]]. The May 21, 2025 indictment in the Middle District of Tennessee alleged that Abrego Garcia — an El Salvador citizen — was a member or associate of MS-13, and that he and others conspired from about 2016 to about 2025 to bring undocumented immigrants into the United States. U.S. District Judge [[waverly-crenshaw|Waverly Crenshaw]] dismissed the human-smuggling case on May 22, 2026 on vindictive-prosecution grounds, a dismissal that led to an ethics complaint against Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]].
