# Degem Systems

Degem Systems was an Israeli computer company acquired by [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] as part of the web of Israeli firms he purchased in the early 1980s, many of which were official "service providers" for [[mossad|Mossad]].[1]

## Cover for Mossad operations

According to Badlands Media, Degem had for years been used to hide Mossad killers who kidnapped and killed people in Africa and Latin America.[1]

## PROMIS distribution

Maxwell and his associates marketed the compromised [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] through Degem and the litany of other Maxwell-owned companies based in Israel and abroad.[1] Unlike [[rafi-eitan|Rafi Eitan]]'s earlier attempt to place the software through [[earl-brian|Earl Brian]] and [[inslaw|Inslaw]], Maxwell's distribution effort was so successful that Israeli intelligence soon had access to the innermost workings of countless governments, corporations, banks and intelligence services around the world.[1] The software was sold to the military dictatorships of Chile and Argentina, where it was used to facilitate the mass murder of dissidents.[1]
