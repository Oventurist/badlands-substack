# Tony Dofat

Tony Dofat is an American record executive and producer who worked alongside [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] on several projects from the early 1990s to the late 2000s.[1]

Shortly after the July 2, 2025 verdict in Combs's federal sex-trafficking trial, the 55-year-old Dofat told the New York Post that he would "definitely" work with Combs again.[1] Combs had been acquitted of racketeering and sex-trafficking charges but convicted on two lesser counts of transportation to engage in prostitution, and reportedly still faced 66 civil lawsuits connected to allegations of sexual impropriety.[1]
