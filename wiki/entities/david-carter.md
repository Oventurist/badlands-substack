# David O. Carter

**David O. Carter** is a United States District Judge for the Central District of California, appointed to the federal bench by President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]].[1] He came to prominence in coverage by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in January 2024 as the judge who dismissed the last surviving California lawsuit seeking to bar former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the state's Republican primary ballot.[1]

## Dismissal of the California ballot disqualification suit

On Wednesday, January 3, 2024, Carter granted a motion to dismiss the lawsuit "with prejudice," meaning the claim could not be submitted to the same court again, according to the court papers.[1] The plaintiff had argued that they suffered "emotional injury" from the breach of the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021, having watched the events unfold on television, on the radio and in various publications, and that the resulting "severe emotional distress" supported a suit to keep Trump off California's ballots.[1] Carter wrote that because the events occurred "more than two years before the plaintiff" filed suit, the claim fell outside the two-year statute of limitations.[1]

The ruling was posted online by former Republican National Committee for California chairwoman [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]], who wrote on X that "the remnants of the last California case to keep President Trump off the ballot here were dismissed today by Judge David O. Carter!!"[1]

## Reception

Coverage in *The Epoch Times* noted that Carter, a Clinton appointee, had ruled against Trump in a separate case, making the dismissal notable.[1] Badlands writer [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] highlighted the appointing president in his commentary — "Oh, the best part? It was a Clinton appointed judge" — and treated the outcome as evidence that the disqualification campaign lacked precedent or legal standing, describing such suits as "temper tantrums being thrown by a side that knows they are already beaten."[1]
