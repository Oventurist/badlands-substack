# Finland

Finland is a Nordic country and, as of April 2023, the newest member of the [[nato|NATO]] alliance, sharing a border of roughly 830 miles with the Russian Federation that also constitutes an external border of the European Union.[1]

## Border crisis with Russia (2023)

Following Finland's NATO accession, Russia vowed "countermeasures," and by late 2023 border tensions had heightened markedly.[1] Finland accused Moscow of weaponizing migrants to manufacture a crisis at its border crossings, with the Finnish Border Guard documenting what it described as a serious uptick in undocumented migrants arriving at crossings, most of them originating in Africa and the Middle East.[1] In response, the Finnish military moved to bolster the border wall.[1]

European Commission President [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]] joined Finnish Prime Minister [[petteri-orpo|Petteri Orpo]] in condemning what they termed Russia's "instrumentalization of migrants," with von der Leyen calling the practice "shameful."[1]

## Balticconnector pipeline damage (October 2023)

In October 2023 a subsea gas pipeline and a telecommunications cable connecting Finland and [[estonia|Estonia]] under the Baltic Sea were damaged in what the Finnish government said may have been a deliberate act.[2] The [[balticconnector|Balticconnector]] pipeline was shut early on Sunday, 8 October, over concerns of a leak from a hole in the 77-kilometre line, and Finnish operator Gasgrid said repairs could take months or more.[2] President [[sauli-niinisto|Sauli Niinistö]] said it was likely that the damage to both the pipeline and the cable resulted from "outside activity," with the investigation continuing jointly with Estonia.[2] Prime Minister Orpo called the damage "worrying" while stressing that Finland's energy supply remained stable — gas accounts for about 5 percent of that supply — and that national connectivity was unaffected.[2] [[nato|NATO]] Secretary General [[jens-stoltenberg|Jens Stoltenberg]] said the alliance was sharing information and stood ready to support the allies concerned.[2] The pipeline damage was believed to have occurred in Finnish waters and the cable breach in Estonian waters.[2] See [[2023-balticconnector-pipeline-damage|2023 Balticconnector pipeline damage]].[2]
