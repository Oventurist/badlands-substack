# Finland

Finland is a Nordic state and [[nato|NATO]] member whose president is cited in the Badlands corpus as an early signal that European leaders are already planning a security architecture with less reliance on Washington.

## "A more European NATO"

The Badlands Brief of April 2, 2026 reports that Finland's president said after speaking with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on April 1 that "a more European NATO" is taking shape. The remark — reported by Reuters — is presented as underscoring that European leaders are discussing a security architecture with less reliance on the United States, in the same week that Trump said he was "strongly considering" pulling the United States out of the alliance and dismissed NATO as a "paper tiger."

The commentary reads the Finnish statement as evidence that the alliance's Europeanization is being discussed openly, in parallel with the [[european-union|European Union]]'s entanglement in what the Brief characterizes as the hollowing out of the "Globalist Western Hegemon" architecture.
