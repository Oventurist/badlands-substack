# Paul Moreno

**Paul Moreno** is a historian who has written in defense of [[originalism]] in the pages of Law & Liberty.[1]

## Response to Jill Lepore

Moreno, together with legal scholar [[robert-natelson|Robert Natelson]], responded to Harvard historian [[jill-lepore|Jill Lepore]]'s Atlantic essay "How Originalism Has Killed the Constitution," which Law & Liberty described as a jeremiad of "constitutional despair" that fails to meet standards of scholarship.[1] Their rebuttal maintains that Lepore's central thesis — that originalism thwarts constitutional change and kills the amendment process — gets matters precisely backward, since originalism preserves the [[united-states-constitution|Constitution]] and its [[article-v|Article V]] amendment process as the lawful mechanism of change in a constitutional republic.[1]
