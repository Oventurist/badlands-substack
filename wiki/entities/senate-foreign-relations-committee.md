# Senate Foreign Relations Committee

The Senate Foreign Relations Committee is the standing committee of the United States Senate with jurisdiction over foreign policy, treaties, and the oversight of the [[state-department|State Department]].[1]

## 2026 Venezuela hearing

In January 2026 the committee received prepared remarks from Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] in what was his first congressional appearance since the January 3 US operation in Caracas that captured [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1] Rubio told the committee that the administration was prepared to use force to secure cooperation from [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s acting president, [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]], while signaling that Washington expected compliance through political and economic leverage first.[1]

Rubio stated that Rodríguez had agreed to open Venezuela's energy sector to US companies, grant preferential access to production, and direct oil revenue toward purchasing American goods.[1] "We are prepared to use force to ensure maximum cooperation if other methods fail," he said in the statement submitted to the committee. "It is our hope that this will not prove necessary, but we will never shy away from our duty to the American people and our mission in this Hemisphere."[1] Rubio was scheduled to meet Venezuelan opposition leader [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] at the State Department after the hearing.[1]
