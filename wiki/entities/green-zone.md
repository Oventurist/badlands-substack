# Green Zone

The Green Zone is the heavily fortified district of Baghdad that houses key Iraqi government institutions and foreign embassies.[1]

## June 2026 lockdown

In late June 2026, Iraqi security forces locked down the Green Zone and conducted raids inside the area as part of a corruption investigation linked to former Deputy Oil Minister [[adnan-al-jumaili|Adnan al-Jumaili]].[1] Video footage referenced in reporting showed tanks and other heavily armed vehicles deployed during the operation.[1] A security report obtained by the Associated Press said seven people were arrested, including five members of parliament whose parliamentary immunity had been revoked; some detainees were linked to the bloc of former Prime Minister [[mohammed-shia-al-sudani|Mohammed Shia al-Sudani]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the sweep as connected to the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|U.S. Treasury]] sanctions network of [[salim-ahmed-said|Salim Ahmed Said]] and to the concurrent tanker strikes in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], arguing that the arrests represented [[donald-trump|Trump]] and the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] dismantling [[deep-state|Deep State]] structures in [[iraq|Iraq]] that he speculated had been installed by Bush-era neoconservatives.[1]
