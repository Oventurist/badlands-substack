# Dade Phelan

Dade Phelan is a Republican Texas state representative who served as Speaker of the Texas House and was leaving that post amid the [[2025-texas-house-speaker-battle|2025 Texas House Speaker battle]].[1]

Phelan's top ally, state Representative [[dustin-burrows|Dustin Burrows]], sought to succeed him against [[david-cook|David Cook]], the state GOP's designated Speaker nominee. The contest exposed long-standing friction between the establishment wing Phelan represented and the rising far-right faction that had come to control most of Texas state government.[1]

## Role in the Paxton impeachment

As Speaker of the Texas House, Phelan led the chamber's effort to impeach Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] in 2023, sending twenty articles to the [[texas-senate|Texas Senate]] for trial.[2] Badlands Media commentary described the resulting [[ken-paxton-impeachment-trial|impeachment trial]] as a show trial and "a comedy of errors" for the House managers, with "drunken Dade Phelan leading the charge."[2] Immediately after Paxton's acquittal on all counts on September 16, 2023, calls to "Impeach Dade Phelan" circulated on X.[2]
