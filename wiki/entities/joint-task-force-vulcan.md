# Joint Task Force Vulcan

Joint Task Force Vulcan (JTFV) is a United States Department of Justice initiative created to coordinate federal prosecutions against the transnational gang [[ms-13|MS-13]]. Badlands Media described it as "a Trump and Barr-created effort," launched in August 2019 with the stated mission to "go to war against MS-13."[1]

## Leadership

The director of Joint Task Force Vulcan is John J. Durham, who Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] identified as a son of Special Counsel [[john-durham|John H. Durham]] — a detail treated in the Truth Community as a notable link between the anti-gang effort and the Durham investigation into the origins of the Trump–Russia probe.[1]

## Activity

JTFV's work was cited in connection with a four-count indictment unsealed in federal court in Central Islip charging thirteen of the highest-ranking MS-13 leaders in the world with directing the organization's criminal activities in the United States, El Salvador, Mexico, and elsewhere over the previous two decades. Charges included racketeering conspiracy, conspiracy to provide or conceal material support to terrorists, and narco-terrorism conspiracy, with four defendants also charged with alien smuggling conspiracy.[1]

Three defendants — Vladimir Antonio Arevalo-Chavez ("Vampiro de Monserrat Criminales"), Walter Yovani Hernandez-Rivera ("Baxter de Park View"), and Marlon Antonio Menjivar-Portillo ("Rojo de Park View") — were located by Mexican authorities on February 22, 2023, expelled from Mexico via the United States, and arrested on arrival at George Bush Intercontinental Airport in Houston by the [[fbi|FBI]] and [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]]. They made initial appearances in the Southern District of Texas pending removal to the Eastern District of New York.[1]

Badlands commentary framed the arrests as evidence that "the War on MS-13 continues."[1]
