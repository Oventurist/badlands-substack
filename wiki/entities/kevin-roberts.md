# Kevin Roberts

Kevin Roberts is the head of the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]], an American conservative think tank, and a participant at the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] annual meeting in Davos.[1]

## 2024 Davos panel

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] identifies the 2024 WEF Annual Meeting as the point at which the energy of the forum first noticeably shifted, citing Roberts's appearance on a panel discussing what to expect from a possible Republican administration.[1] On that panel Roberts predicted a populist reckoning — a forecast that, in the Badlands reading, was borne out when subsequent Davos meetings turned to the crisis of public trust in global institutions amid the resignation of [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]].[1]

## "You are the problem" (Davos, January 2024)

Speaking at the World Economic Forum on Thursday, January 18, 2024, Roberts told the assembled globalists directly that "you are part of the problem, you are not the solution."[2] The remarks came during a session titled "What to Expect from a Possible Republican Administration."[2]

"I will be candid," Roberts began, adding that "the agenda that every single person member of the [future Republican] administration needs to have, is to compile a list of everything that's ever been proposed at the [WEF], and object all of them wholesale."[2] He urged that "anyone not prepared to do that, and take away this power of the unelected bureaucrats and give it back to the American people, is unprepared to be a part of the next conservative administration."[2]

When the discussion turned to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], Roberts told the panel's host, [[robin-niblett|Sir Robin Niblett]], Distinguished Fellow of [[chatham-house|Chatham House]], that "it's laughable that you or anyone would describe Davos as protecting liberal democracy," and that it was "equally laughable to use the word 'dictatorship' at Davos and aim that at President Trump… I think that's absurd."[2] He closed by saying that "the very reason I'm here at Davos, is to explain to many people in this room and who are watching, with all due respect – nothing personal – that you are part of the problem."[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted the strangeness of Roberts and [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]] even being invited to speak at the WEF, writing that it was hard to imagine the Davos set commissioning so scathing and public a rebuke of its own worldview.[2] She characterised his target as "the infamous hypocritical self-avowed Marxists, private-jet environmentalists, and genocide-adjacent humanitarians" who wanted to hear from the Heritage Foundation how to rebuild trust with the everyday Americans against whom they had weaponized their institutions.[2] Observing that the line had been delivered at Davos itself, she added: "Remember when globalist control of US institutions was a conspiracy theory?"[2]

## "Second American Revolution" remarks (July 2024)

In July 2024, Roberts argued that the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]]'s ruling on [[presidential-immunity|presidential immunity]] would reinforce a [[second-american-revolution|"second American Revolution"]], which he said would "remain bloodless if the left allows it to be."[3] He made the comments on [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]]'s "War Room" podcast, framing the ruling — which largely shields former presidents from criminal prosecution for actions in office — as something that should encourage conservatives.[3]

"In spite of all this nonsense from the left, we are going to win. We're in the process of taking this country back. No one in the audience should be despairing," Roberts said, adding that "in spite of all of the injustice… we are going to prevail."[3] Calling the ruling "vital," he pointed to [[federalist-no-70|Federalist No. 70]], the essay arguing for a strong executive leader.[3]

## Fallout over defense of Tucker Carlson

Roberts came under sustained criticism in late 2025 for publicly defending [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] after Carlson interviewed [[nick-fuentes|Nick Fuentes]], a white nationalist and Holocaust revisionist; his comments triggered resignations and criticism from Jewish leaders and former [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]] affiliates, and two more trustees resigned in the week of December 15, 2025 over his support for Carlson.[4] Roberts nonetheless hosted [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] at Heritage on Wednesday, December 17, 2025, where Shapiro spent about an hour denouncing Carlson by name and calling on the institution to enforce "ideological border control."[4]

## Davos rebuke

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] recalled Roberts, head of the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]], scolding attendees at a [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meeting in Davos and telling them, 'It's laughable that you or anyone would describe Davos as protecting liberal democracy.'[5] She cited the episode as a marker that the rhetoric and energy around Davos had changed.[5]

## Apology over the Carlson defense, November 2025

At a leaked all-staff meeting on Wednesday, November 5, 2025, Roberts apologized to [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]] employees for the controversial video in which he had defended [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], and faced sharp criticism from the think tank's scholars over his handling of the matter.[6] "I made a mistake, and I let you down, and I let down this institution. Period. Full stop," Roberts said in opening the meeting, video of which was published by the Washington Free Beacon.[6] He followed the meeting with another direct-to-camera statement pledging to challenge Carlson: "Everyone has the responsibility to speak up against the scourge of antisemitism, no matter the messenger. Heritage and I will do so, even when my friend Tucker Carlson needs challenging."[6] The episode was reported by [[the-hill|The Hill]].[6]
