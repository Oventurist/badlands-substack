# Winthrop Rockefeller

**Winthrop Rockefeller** was a son of [[john-d-rockefeller-jr|John D. Rockefeller Jr.]] and, in Badlands Media's telling, the renegade of the family.[1]

## Early life and war service

Having dropped out of Yale in 1934, he moved to Texas and worked as an oil field roustabout, spending time in the trenches rather than in the prestigious posts occupied by his brothers.[1] In the Second World War he served as a combat infantryman in the Pacific Theater, earning a Purple Heart among other decorations.[1] Returning home, he developed a taste for drinking, women and New York cafe society.[1]

## Arkansas and the governorship

In 1953 he abruptly moved to Arkansas, where he was voted "Arkansas Man of the Year" in 1956.[1] His famous name helped him win the office of governor in 1967.[1] The article speculates that it was around this time that a young Arkansas Democrat, Rhodes scholar and DeMolay member named [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] may have come to Rockefeller's attention.[1]

## Death

Winthrop Rockefeller died of cancer in 1973, two months before his sixty-first birthday.[1]
