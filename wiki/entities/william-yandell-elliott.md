# William Yandell Elliott

William Yandell Elliott (May 12, 1896 – January 9, 1979) was an American historian and a political advisor to six US presidents, and is best known in Badlands commentary as the mentor of [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]].[1]

Born in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, Elliott served as an artillery battery commander in the First World War. He attended Vanderbilt University, where he belonged to the group of poets and literary scholars known as the Fugitives, and as a [[rhodes-scholarship|Rhodes Scholar]] he studied at Balliol College, Oxford. Hired by [[harvard-university|Harvard]] President Abbott Lawrence Lowell, he remained at Harvard for the next 41 years, advising a number of American presidents and presidential candidates. He was a member of Franklin Roosevelt's Brain Trust in the 1930s and, after the war, served on the National Security Council. As dean of the Harvard Summer School he established the Harvard International Seminar, which was directed by his student and protégé Kissinger.[1]

## Alleged Round Table role

The Executive Intelligence Review wrote in 2002 that throughout his career Elliott coordinated operations between his hometown circle — the "Tennessee Templar" of the Ku Klux Klan — and the Cecil Rhodes [[round-table-movement|Round Table]] movement, with which he became associated during his Rhodes Scholarship studies at Balliol College. His speeches, published work, and private correspondence emphasized that he viewed his life's work as extending the Round Table movement worldwide, and his direction of the Harvard Summer School, including Kissinger's International Seminar and the alumni associations it spawned around the world, were all directed to that purpose.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that Elliott recruited Kissinger into the Rhodes-funded secret society in 1951, and that Kissinger joined the American counterpart of the Round Table — the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] — in 1956. Carlson presented the pairing of a man with close ties to the Klan mentoring a Jewish American refugee as evidence that the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] is indifferent to race, nationality, or religion when selecting useful assets.[1]
