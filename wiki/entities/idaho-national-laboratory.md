# Idaho National Laboratory

Idaho National Laboratory is a national laboratory of the U.S. [[department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] whose portfolio includes cybersecurity research relevant to industrial control systems and [[critical-infrastructure|critical infrastructure]] protection.[1]

[[marty-edwards|Marty Edwards]] — later Deputy Chief Technology Officer for operational technology and IoT at [[tenable|Tenable]] and a witness at the February 6, 2024 [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Homeland Security Committee]] hearing on the water sector — served as a program manager focused on cybersecurity at Idaho National Laboratory before moving to the Department of Homeland Security's [[ics-cert|ICS-CERT]] and then to industry.[1] The laboratory therefore appears in the Badlands account as one node in the revolving-door pipeline between federal research institutions and the commercial vendors that testify on [[public-private-partnership|public-private partnership]] cybersecurity policy.[1]
