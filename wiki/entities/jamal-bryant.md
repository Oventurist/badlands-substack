# Jamal Bryant

The Rev. Jamal Bryant is a Georgia-based pastor who has led a nationwide boycott campaign against the retailer [[target|Target]] over the company's rollback of diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives.[1]

## Target boycott

In April 2025, Bryant called for the nationwide boycott of Target to continue, saying the company had not met his list of demands regarding DEI initiatives.[1] Newsweek reported that it had reached out to Target for comment.[1]

The dispute followed executive action by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after his January inauguration, including an executive order attempting to rid the federal government of DEI initiatives, which he called "wasteful" and "radical."[1] Following that order, Target announced it would end certain DEI programs, and the retailer subsequently faced backlash over the rescinded programs.[1]
