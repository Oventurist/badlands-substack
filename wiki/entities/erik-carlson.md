# Erik Carlson

Erik Carlson is a contributing author to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] whose essays present a first-person account of researching what he describes as layered or partial truths.[1] In the April 15, 2025 essay "A Journey of Ever-changing Truths," Carlson frames his own intellectual development as an ongoing process of discovering that conclusions he once accepted as complete were in fact only partial.[1]

## Method and argument

Carlson distinguishes between outright lies, complete truths, and partial truths, arguing that partial truths are the most effective instrument of public deception because they satisfy curiosity and cause inquiry to stop.[1] He illustrates the role of context with a hypothetical shooting that reads as cold-blooded murder without context and as justified defense with it, contending that the omission of context can reverse the moral meaning of a factual account without altering any of its facts.[1] He compares the search for truth to peeling an onion, in which each layer differs from the one above it.[1]

Carlson estimates that roughly 80% of Americans will accept claims delivered by a television news anchor or a person in a white lab coat, while the remaining 20% are more skeptical and are therefore served purpose-built half-truths that are technically accurate but stripped of decisive detail.[1]

## Substantive claims

Applying this method, Carlson argues that a recurring roughly thirty-year cycle of American race riots is engineered rather than organic, citing the [[harlem-riots-1935|Harlem riots of 1935]], the [[watts-riots-1965|Watts riots of 1965]], the [[la-riots-1992|Los Angeles riots of 1992]] that followed the [[rodney-king|Rodney King]] verdict, and the 2020 unrest following the death of [[george-floyd|George Floyd]].[1] He connects the early-1990s cycle to prison privatization, the [[crime-bill-1994|1994 Crime Bill]], the rise of [[gangster-rap|gangster rap]], and [[cia|CIA]] narcotics trafficking into South Central Los Angeles.[1]

Carlson further argues that the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] was elevated in public consciousness by [[the-godfather|The Godfather]] in order to conceal the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]] beneath it, and that both in turn sit below the CIA.[1] His ultimate conclusion is that neither Israel nor the Jewish people are the apex of the hierarchy but a scapegoat, and that the [[city-of-london|City of London]] sits atop the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]].[1]

Carlson publishes additional work at his own Substack, and Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors rather than the outlet itself.[1]
