# Covington & Burling

Covington & Burling is a major Washington-based American law firm. In October 2025 it joined a network of more than 40 large law firms created to provide free legal assistance to Jewish people confronting antisemitism after the October 7, 2023, attack on Israel and the ensuing war in Gaza.[1]

The initiative was led by [[gibson-dunn|Gibson Dunn]] partner [[orin-snyder|Orin Snyder]] and included [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]], [[cooley|Cooley]], and the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]].[1] Its launch coincided with heightened scrutiny of law firm pro bono practices by the Trump administration, under which several firms agreed to devote free legal work to causes the president supports, including combating antisemitism.[1]
