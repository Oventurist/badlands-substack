# Atomwaffen Division

Atomwaffen Division — referred to in the source as "Atomwaffen SS" — is a neo-Nazi organisation cited by Badlands Media as an example of a nominally right-wing group operating under intelligence-agency control.[1]

The article states that the group was run by [[fbi|FBI]] asset Caleb Sutter, and groups it with [[patriot-front|Patriot Front]] and Canada's Heritage Front as evidence of three-letter agency operations behind right-wing organisations.[1] Badlands reads the pattern as indicating a deliberate intention to inflame an already volatile American situation in the wake of the [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination of Charlie Kirk]].[1]

This is framed as a continuation of an older technique: as in the Bolshevik versus National Socialist chaos of the 1920s, both extremes are funded and steered while the hand of the London–Wall Street banking interests behind them is ignored.[1]
