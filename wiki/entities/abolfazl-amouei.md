# Abolfazl Amouei

Abolfazl Amouei is an Iranian politician who serves as spokesman for the National Security Committee of the Iranian Parliament.[1]

## April 2024 warning to Israel

In April 2024, amid expectations that [[israel|Israel]] would counter-strike after [[iran|Iran]]'s weekend barrage of more than 300 drones and missiles, Amouei said that "we are prepared to use a weapon that we have never used," urging Israel to "act wisely."[1] The statement, reported by Iran International, accompanied Tehran's broader warning that it would launch a further attack if Israel responded to the initial assault, which Iran framed as retaliation for airstrikes in Syria that killed [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] officials including Brig. Gen. [[mohammad-reza-zahedi|Mohammad Reza Zahedi]].[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], doubted the obvious nuclear interpretation of Amouei's remark, speculating that Iran might mean a non-kinetic capability such as a cyberwarfare weapon, and also entertained the view of [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] and [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] that no secret weapon existed and the claim was psychological warfare intended to bait Israel into overreach.[1]
