# Mario Lugones

**Mario Lugones** is the Argentine Minister of Health.[1] In May 2025 he issued a joint statement with U.S. Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] announcing and explaining each country's withdrawal from the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]].[1]

The joint statement argued that the WHO's handling of the COVID-19 pandemic had revealed serious structural and operational shortcomings that undermined global trust and highlighted the need for independent, science-based leadership in global health.[1] It cited well-documented concerns about the early management of the pandemic and the risks associated with certain types of research, and charged that the WHO had failed to provide timely transparency and critical access to information, impairing countries' ability to act swiftly and effectively.[1] The absence of meaningful reforms, disproportionate financial demands, and the increasing politicization of the organization were given as the reasons the United States and Argentina had both chosen to withdraw.[1]
