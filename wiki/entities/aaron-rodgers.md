# Aaron Rodgers

Aaron Rodgers is an NFL quarterback, described in coverage as outspoken, who in early 2024 was considered by [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] as a possible running mate for Kennedy's independent presidential campaign.[1]

## Vice-presidential consideration

Reporting in March 2024 indicated that Rodgers was out of the running for the vice-presidential slot, with a source close to the campaign saying the prospect of his selection had prompted concerns among donors — some of whom had reportedly threatened to abandon the campaign over the pick.[1] Kennedy was instead expected to name attorney and entrepreneur [[nicole-shanahan|Nicole Shanahan]], whose potential to help finance the campaign's ballot access initiative was cited as a key factor.[1]

## COVID-19 dissent

Rodgers, described as one of the best players in the NFL since he was drafted, took a public stand against the COVID-19 vaccine.[2] He also spoke on prominent podcast appearances about the benefits of [[ivermectin|Ivermectin]] and [[hydroxychloroquine|HCQ]] as alternative treatments for COVID-19 symptoms.[2] Badlands Media grouped him with [[kyrie-irving|Kyrie Irving]], [[novak-djokovic|Novak Djokovic]] and [[ivan-provorov|Ivan Provorov]] as athletes who had refused to comply with institutional orthodoxy despite pressure from the sports media establishment.[2]
