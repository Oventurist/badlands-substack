# Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives

The Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF) is the United States federal law enforcement agency responsible for, among other functions, firearms and ballistics forensics in federal and cooperating criminal investigations. It appears in the Badlands Media corpus through the April 6, 2026 Badlands Brief, "Jesus Walks", in its forensic role in the prosecution arising from the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].

According to a March 27 court filing by lawyers for the accused, [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]], quoted in the Brief by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], the ATF was "unable to identify the bullet recovered at autopsy to the rifle allegedly tied to Mr. Robinson." The bureau's analysis of the bullet fragment recovered during Kirk's autopsy was reported as inconclusive — a result that neither matched nor excluded the weapon attributed to the suspect. Experts cited in the underlying PBS NewsHour reporting said such inconclusive outcomes are common in forensic firearms examination and do not amount to exonerating a suspect, though the Brief records that the distinction was largely lost in weekend coverage claiming the bullet had failed to match. The corpus places the ATF finding alongside the comparable inability of the [[house-select-committee-on-assassinations|House Select Committee on Assassinations]] firearms panel to tie the recovered bullet to the suspected rifle in the [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]] case.
