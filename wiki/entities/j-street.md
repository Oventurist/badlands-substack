# J Street

**J Street** is a liberal advocacy organisation invoked by the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] editorial board as the model that the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] came to resemble under [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]].[1]

The editorial board wrote that the ADL "has essentially become another J Street — an arm of the Democratic Party's stable of pressure groups."[1] Badlands Media cited the comparison as part of a broader case that formerly non-partisan Jewish and civil-rights organisations have been absorbed into the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]]'s political apparatus.[1]
