# United States Department of the Treasury

The **United States Department of the Treasury** is the federal executive department responsible for federal finances, currency and the administration of economic sanctions through its [[office-of-foreign-assets-control|Office of Foreign Assets Control]].

## Cuba convoy probe

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 the Treasury appears as the agency behind a federal investigation into American participants in the March 2026 [[nuestra-america-convoy|Nuestra America Convoy]] to [[cuba|Cuba]]. Federal investigators subpoenaed streamer [[hasan-piker|Hasan Piker]] and activist [[medea-benjamin|Medea Benjamin]] for financial, logistical and communications records, with more than 40 Americans reportedly under scrutiny for potential sanctions violations. The source presents the Treasury action, alongside [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] measures such as the indictment of [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]], as part of a coordinated tightening of pressure on Cuba and on the activist networks that engage with it.
