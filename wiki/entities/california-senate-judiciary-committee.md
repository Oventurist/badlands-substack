# California Senate Judiciary Committee

The California Senate Judiciary Committee is the standing committee of the California State Senate responsible for reviewing legislation affecting the courts, family law, and civil procedure. In 2023 only two of its members were Republicans, among them [[scott-wilk|Scott Wilk]] of Santa Clarita.[1]

## AB 957 hearing

On June 13, 2023 the committee heard [[ab-957|AB 957]], a bill amended the previous week to insert "gender affirmation" into the California Family Code alongside a child's need for "health, safety, and welfare."[1] Democratic members of the committee told the assembled parents that the measure would only "give the judge more information," an assurance Wilk publicly disputed before advising parents to leave the state.[1] The hearing became a focal point in national coverage of parental rights and gender-transition policy, and was highlighted by [[the-daily-signal|The Daily Signal]] and subsequently by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]
