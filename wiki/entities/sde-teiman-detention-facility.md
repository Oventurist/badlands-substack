# Sde Teiman Detention Facility

Sde Teiman is an [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] military base and detention facility in southern Israel used to hold Palestinian detainees taken during the Gaza war.[1] The site became the focus of a national controversy in July 2024 after Israeli Military Police raided it and arrested nine IDF soldiers suspected of the serious abuse of a Palestinian detainee held there.[1]

## July 2024 raid and mob storming

The arrests on Monday, July 29, 2024 provoked an immediate outcry among far-right politicians in Israel.[1] Right-wing lawmakers — including National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben Gvir]], Heritage Minister Amichay Eliyahu, and [[religious-zionism-party|Religious Zionism]] MK Zvi Sukkot — urged supporters to gather outside Sde Teiman to protest the soldiers' detention.[1] A mob subsequently stormed the base, and hours later far-right activists broke into a second facility, the IDF's Beit Lid base in central Israel, where the detained soldiers had been taken for questioning.[1]

IDF chief of staff [[herzi-halevi|Herzi Halevi]] described the scenes as "bordering on anarchy" and said the break-ins harmed the army, Israel's security, and the war effort.[1] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], Halevi, Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]], and President [[isaac-herzog|Isaac Herzog]] all harshly criticized the rioting and appealed for calm, though their statements did not prevent the second break-in.[1] Several members of Netanyahu's ruling coalition were reported to have fomented the break-ins and in some cases personally taken part.[1]

Ben Gvir, whose ministry controls the [[israeli-police|Israel Police]] and the [[israel-prison-service|Israel Prison Service]], called the arrests shameful: "The spectacle of Military Police officers coming to arrest our best heroes at Sde Teiman is nothing less than shameful."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], framed the episode as evidence of a manipulated gameboard within the IDF's own ranks, noting that he and [[canncon|CannCon]] had covered the Military Police raid on Badlands Daily the previous day.[1] He characterized the storming as the work of supporters of the "hyper-radical" [[otzma-yehudit|Otzma Yehudit]] ("Jewish Power") and Religious Zionist parties, both of which he described as adherents of the teachings of [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]].[1] He argued that the specific nature of the alleged abuse — the sexual assault of male Palestinian prisoners — was being underreported, and connected the resulting "degeneracy" to what he described as the capture of Israel by organized crime syndicates, citing a 2002 U.S. Department of Justice document describing how the "tentacles of Ukrainian organized crime" had reached Israel during the 1990s.[1] He further tied the radicals' ideology to the antinomian mystical currents associated with [[sabbatai-zevi|Sabbatai Zevi]] and [[jacob-frank|Jacob Frank]] rather than to mainstream Torah observance.[1]
