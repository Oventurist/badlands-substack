# Stuart Symington

Stuart Symington was a United States Senator remembered in Badlands commentary chiefly for his attempts to subject the Israeli lobby in Washington to foreign-agent registration requirements.[1]

## AIPAC registration effort

In 1957 Symington attempted to bring legislation to the Senate floor that would have forced the [[aipac|American Israel Public Affairs Committee]] (AIPAC), described as the most powerful Israeli lobby group, to register as a foreign agent.[1] He failed to secure the necessary support, but one senator did back the proposal: [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1] As president, Kennedy went on to have a contemptuous relationship with AIPAC and the government of Israel, likewise seeking to have AIPAC registered as a foreign agent — as federal law requires of foreign lobby groups — because he believed it was working behind the scenes in Washington and subverting his pursuit of nuclear disarmament.[1]

## Nonproliferation amendment

Symington remained in the Senate and in the 1970s succeeded in passing an amendment to the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty framework prohibiting the United States from giving foreign aid to any government found in violation of the treaty.[1] According to Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], that provision has yet to be enforced against Israel.[1]
