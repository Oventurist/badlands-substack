# Port of Baltimore

The **Port of Baltimore** is the Maryland seaport spanned by the 1.6-mile Francis Scott Key Bridge, which collapsed in March 2024 after being struck by the container ship [[dali|Dali]].[1] The [[francis-scott-key-bridge-collapse|collapse]] left wreckage blocking the shipping channel and paralyzed the entire port.[1]

The federal government authorized $60 million for salvage efforts, and a massive floating crane described in reporting as CIA-linked arrived in Baltimore along with other cranes to begin clearing the mangled bridge from the channel.[1] Officials' hopes for a swift salvage operation were complicated by Captain [[john-konrad|John Konrad]]'s report that the Dali appeared to be sitting atop a high-pressure underwater gas line, with sources at ICS indicating the effort would likely be delayed while the line was surveyed and additional risk assessed.[1]
