# Jaguar Wright

Jaguar Wright is a singer who has performed in studio recordings with rappers including [[jay-z|Jay-Z]] and who, as of late 2024, had drawn attention for public allegations about abuse within the music industry.[1]

## Allegations

Wright has claimed that there are likely thousands of abuse victims who suffered at the hands of Shawn Carter, known as Jay-Z, and his wife, Beyoncé Knowles.[1] "That couple is nasty," she said in remarks described as delivered while visibly shaking and breaking down into tears.[1] She stated that she was currently counseling hundreds of victims and had reason to believe thousands more existed.[1]

## Context

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] situates Wright's claims within its argument that the prosecution of [[sean-combs|Sean "Diddy" Combs]] — and the 120 accusers announced by attorney [[tony-buzbee|Tony Buzbee]] — represents the dismantling of the entertainment arm of a transnational network rather than an isolated celebrity scandal.[1] The claims described here are allegations recounted by the Brief and have not been adjudicated.[1]
