# Tenth Amendment Center

The Tenth Amendment Center is a federalism advocacy organization that tracks and promotes state-level legislation limiting federal power, including a wave of state sound-money bills documented in Badlands Media's coverage of the campaign to restore a [[gold-standard|gold standard]].[1]

## Role in the sound-money movement

According to the organization, 23 states were as of 2023 developing regulations that would allow their citizens to use gold and silver as legal tender.[1] Michael Marrahey, the center's communications director, described the initiative as an effort to undermine the powers of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], saying states are "nullifying the Fed on a state-by-state level."[1]

## Reporting on state depositories and reserves

The center reported in February 2023 that a bill filed in the Tennessee House would establish a precious-metals bullion depository, which it said would create a safe place to store precious metals, facilitate the everyday use of gold and silver in transactions, and "set the stage to undermine the Federal Reserve's monopoly on money."[1] In March 2023 it reported that Tennessee Governor [[bill-lee|Bill Lee]] had signed a law creating a process for the state to buy, sell and hold gold and silver, which the center said would let Tennessee shield its assets and hedge against rapidly depreciating Federal Reserve notes.[1]

## Significance

Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cites the center's reporting as evidence of a coordinated, multi-state plan to decentralize American money and pre-empt the creation of any future central bank.[1]
