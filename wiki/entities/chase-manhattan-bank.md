# Chase Manhattan Bank

**Chase Manhattan Bank** was the New York banking institution through which [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]] exercised much of his financial and geopolitical influence.[1]

## Origins

David Rockefeller joined the staff of the Chase National Bank of New York after the Second World War, at a time when his uncle Winthrop Aldrich was chairman of the board and president and Chase had become the second-largest national bank in the United States.[1] In 1955 Rockefeller played a major role in the merger of Chase with the Bank of Manhattan Company, creating Chase Manhattan Bank.[1]

## Holding company and Rockefeller's chairmanship

In 1969 the bank became part of Chase Manhattan Corp., in line with the trend of establishing holding companies to sidestep banking laws prohibiting certain activities such as the acquisition of finance companies.[1] That same year David Rockefeller became the company's board chairman and chief executive officer.[1]

## Political reach

Badlands Media cites Gary Allen's claim that in 1973 alone David Rockefeller met with 27 heads of state, including the rulers of Russia and Red China, and notes that when Australian leader Malcolm Fraser visited the United States in 1976 he apparently conferred with Rockefeller before meeting President [[gerald-ford|Gerald Ford]].[1] Author Ralph F. Epperson is quoted calling this "truly incredible because David Rockefeller has neither been elected nor appointed to any governmental position where he could officially represent the will of the American people."[1] Researchers Anthony C. Sutton and Patrick M. Wood elsewhere described the bank as "ultrapowerful."[1]
