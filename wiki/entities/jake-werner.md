# Jake Werner

**Jake Werner** is an attorney with the Arizona-based [[alliance-defending-freedom|Alliance Defending Freedom]] who represented Christian baker [[jack-phillips|Jack Phillips]] in the litigation brought against him and his bakery, [[masterpiece-cakeshop|Masterpiece Cakeshop]], by transgender attorney [[autumn-scardina|Autumn Scardina]].[1]

## Colorado Supreme Court dismissal (October 2024)

After the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]] dismissed the case on procedural grounds in a 4–3 decision on October 8, 2024, Werner said his client had been pursued and mocked for years by people who disagreed with him.[1] "Enough is enough," Werner said. "Jack has been dragged through courts for over a decade. It's time to leave him alone."[1]

The court's majority opinion, authored by Justice [[melissa-hart|Melissa Hart]], declined to resolve the underlying constitutional question of how to balance public-accommodation protections for transgender customers against the free-exercise and free-expression rights of religious business owners.[1] Scardina's attorney, John McHugh, said he was weighing further legal options.[1]
