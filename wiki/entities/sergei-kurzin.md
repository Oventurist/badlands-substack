# Sergei Kurzin

Sergei Kurzin is a Russian investor whom the article presents as the second major link between [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]]'s network and the Clintons, alongside [[gilbert-chagoury|Gilbert Chagoury]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

Quoting the New York Post, the article reports that Kurzin worked for Marc Rich in the 1990s, travelling around Russia looking for suitable investment opportunities in the crumbled former Soviet Union.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) An engineer by training, he has been involved in lucrative deals in [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]] and other countries, including the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] that involved [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and [[frank-giustra|Frank Giustra]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The same account notes that Russia bought 20 percent of all uranium production capacity in the United States in a deal requiring sign-off from the [[state-department|State Department]] under [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], and that while the deal was going through Bill Clinton was paid $500,000 for a Moscow speech funded by a Russian investment bank promoting the uranium transaction.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Kurzin, meanwhile, donated $1 million to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article summarizes that Kurzin was in on the [[urasia-energy|UrAsia]] uranium deal in Kazakhstan and also made a great deal of money on the sale of [[uranium-one|Uranium One]] to Russia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) It stresses that Bill Clinton, not Hillary Clinton, got the ball rolling in September 2005 when he flew to Kazakhstan on Giustra's private plane and cut a deal for three of the best uranium mines in the world, benefiting himself, Giustra and their Russian partner Kurzin.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) That a Russian appears in the story surprises the author not at all, given that Uranium One was ultimately sold to the Russians.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
