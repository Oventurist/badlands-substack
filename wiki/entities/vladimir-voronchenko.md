# Vladimir Voronchenko

Vladimir Voronchenko, also spelled "Vladimir Vorontchenko," is a citizen of the Russian Federation and a legal permanent resident of the United States who was indicted in the Southern District of New York in February 2023 for sanctions evasion and money laundering.[1]

## Indictment

The unsealing of the indictment was announced by [[damian-williams|Damian Williams]], United States Attorney for the [[southern-district-of-new-york|Southern District of New York]]; Andrew C. Adams, Director of [[task-force-kleptocapture|Task Force KleptoCapture]]; Ivan J. Arvelo, Special Agent in Charge of the New York Field Office of [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]]; and Maged Behnam, Acting Special Agent in Charge of the Miami Field Office of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]].[1]

The charge alleged that Voronchenko participated in a scheme to make more than $4 million in U.S. dollar payments to maintain four American real properties owned by the sanctioned oligarch [[viktor-vekselberg|Viktor Vekselberg]], and to attempt to sell two of those properties. The indictment additionally charged Voronchenko with contempt of court arising from his flight from the United States after receiving a grand jury subpoena compelling his personal appearance and testimony.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] observed that although the prosecution might appear perfunctory because the defendant had already left the country, it signalled that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] was tracing the money outward from Vekselberg.[1]
