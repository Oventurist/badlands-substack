# Mao Ning

Mao Ning is a spokeswoman for the Foreign Ministry of the People's Republic of [[china|China]].

In [[badlands-brief-25c|Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi]], Mao is quoted responding to the announcement by [[japan|Japan]] and the [[philippines|Philippines]] of planned maritime delimitation talks. She said the waters under discussion lie east of [[taiwan|Taiwan]] and that any maritime delimitation negotiations in that area must involve China. Her statement preceded China's declaration of a "special maritime law enforcement operation" east of Taiwan.
