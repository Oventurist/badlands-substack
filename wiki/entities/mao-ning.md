# Mao Ning

Mao Ning is a spokeswoman for the Chinese Foreign Ministry. In response to the May 28, 2026 Tokyo summit at which Japanese Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]] and Philippine President [[ferdinand-marcos-jr|Ferdinand Marcos Jr.]] announced plans for maritime delimitation talks, Mao said the waters under discussion lie east of [[taiwan|Taiwan]] and that any maritime delimitation negotiations in the area must involve China.[1]

Her statement framed Beijing's legal claim ahead of China's subsequent "special maritime law enforcement operation" east of Taiwan, which Chinese authorities said was intended to exercise maritime administrative law-enforcement jurisdiction and protect national rights and interests.[1]

## Response to Biden's "dictator" remark (2023)

At a daily press briefing in Beijing on June 21, 2023, Mao Ning responded to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s description of [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] as a dictator, saying the comments were "extremely absurd and irresponsible."[2] She added: "It is a blatant political provocation. China expresses strong dissatisfaction and opposition."[2]
