# Gregory Bovino

Gregory Bovino is a senior official of the [[united-states-border-patrol|United States Border Patrol]] who became a nationally visible figure in the Trump administration's interior immigration enforcement operations, leading surge deployments of Border Patrol personnel into metropolitan areas well away from the southern border.

## Minnesota investigation

According to the March 3, 2026 [[badlands-brief-52f|Badlands Brief]], Hennepin County Attorney [[mary-moriarty|Mary Moriarty]] named Bovino among the federal agents whose conduct her office would investigate in connection with enforcement actions in [[minnesota|Minnesota]] earlier that year. The [[hennepin-county-attorneys-office|Hennepin County Attorney's Office]] said it would examine "potentially unlawful behavior" by Border Patrol agents and pursue charges where appropriate, and opened a portal for members of the public to submit evidence.

The [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] rejected the premise of the inquiry, maintaining that immigration enforcement is a federal responsibility and that state prosecutors have no authority to charge federal officers. Commentary in the Brief by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] defended the agents as "doing their job" and framed the probe as a political exercise.
