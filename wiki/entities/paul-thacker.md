# Paul Thacker

Paul Thacker is an investigative journalist who publishes The Disinformation Chronicle on Substack and who has reported extensively on the institutional machinery of online content moderation.[1]

## Twitter Files reporting

In July 2023 Thacker published a Twitter Files exposé via The Disinformation Chronicle revealing that pre-[[elon-musk|Musk]] [[twitter|Twitter]] employees had taken action against several conservative accounts after the [[center-for-countering-digital-hate|Center for Countering Digital Hate]] released a report alleging that just twelve accounts produced the majority of anti-vaccine disinformation on social media.[1] [[facebook|Facebook]] rejected the same report, stating that "there isn't any evidence to support this claim."[1] Thacker's reporting was cited in coverage of [[x|X Corp]]'s exploration of a lawsuit against CCDH and its chief executive [[imran-ahmed|Imran Ahmed]].[1]
