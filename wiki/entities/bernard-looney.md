# Bernard Looney

Bernard Looney was the chief executive of the British energy company BP.[1] He is listed in the Badlands Media article "Dark Equation" among the attendees of the 2023 [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] meeting in Lisbon, Portugal, where artificial intelligence dominated the agenda.[1]

Looney appears in the article as part of a roster of corporate and political figures — including NATO Secretary-General [[jens-stoltenberg|Jens Stoltenberg]], Ukrainian Foreign Minister [[dmytro-kuleba|Dmytro Kuleba]], Pfizer chief executive [[albert-bourla|Albert Bourla]], [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] President [[borge-brende|Børge Brende]], and [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] — cited by the author as evidence of the breadth of institutional power represented at the closed-door conference.[1]
