# Bandar bin Sultan Al Saud

Bandar bin Sultan Al Saud is a Saudi royal and former Secretary General of the Kingdom's National Security Council, described in Badlands Media's account of the Syrian Civil War as the man who took over the regime-change effort against [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

## The "Syria File"

According to a former Qatari defense minister who went public with details of the anti-Assad plot — an interview aired by [[kevork-almassian|Kevork Almassian]] on his podcast *Syriana Analysis* — the operation was originally run by Qatar as part of a coalition that included Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Jordan, Turkey and the West, with a budget said to total some $2 trillion.[1] In 2013, after months of failure, Bandar assumed control of what participants called "The Syria File," citing Qatari incompetence in completing the job.[1] Bandar, however, also failed to overthrow Assad.[1]

## Removal

Bandar was ousted from his post on January 29, 2015, the stated reason being that his office and his entire agency had been abolished.[1] That same date saw the death of Saudi King Abdullah and the accession of Crown Prince Salman, who immediately appointed his young son — [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] — as Defense Minister; MBS then reorganized the Saudi government from scratch within about ten days.[1]
