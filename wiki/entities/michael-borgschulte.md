# Michael Borgschulte

Lieutenant General Michael Borgschulte is the Superintendent of the [[us-naval-academy|United States Naval Academy]].[1]

## Dismissal of the commandant of midshipmen

On Monday, November 24, 2025, Borgschulte removed Capt. [[gilbert-clark-jr|Gilbert Clark Jr.]] as commandant of midshipmen, citing a "loss of confidence in his ability to effectively lead" the brigade.[1] Clark had assumed the post in June 2025, meaning he was relieved less than six months into the role.[1] The academy said in a statement that "the naval service maintains the highest standards for leaders and holds them accountable when those standards are not met," and did not provide further detail about the reason for the dismissal; the U.S. Navy commonly invokes "loss of confidence" when firing senior leaders.[1]
