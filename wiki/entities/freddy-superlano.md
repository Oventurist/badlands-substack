# Freddy Superlano

Freddy Superlano is a Venezuelan opposition politician who serves as national coordinator of the party [[voluntad-popular|Voluntad Popular]].[1]

On Tuesday, July 30, 2024, Voluntad Popular announced on X that Superlano had been detained by security services loyal to [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], in the days following Venezuela's disputed July 28 presidential election.[1] Reporting at the time attributed the detention to the opposition's continued insistence — voiced most prominently by candidate [[edmundo-gonzalez-urrutia|Edmundo González Urrutia]] — that the opposition had in fact won and that the official result was stolen.[1] The arrest came amid spreading protests, while at least nine Latin American countries withheld recognition of Maduro's third term and the [[white-house|White House]] said it was "reviewing" available data and saw "signs" that the official results did not reflect the will of the people.[1]
