# Penelope Convertino

Penelope Convertino is a 22-year-old transgender person who was arrested in August 2025 after allegedly threatening to kill U.S. Senator [[marsha-blackburn|Marsha Blackburn]] (R-TN).[1]

## Background reporting

The Tennessee Star reported that yearbook photographs it obtained showed Convertino had been a member of the Gay Straight Trans Alliance (GSTA) club at Riverdale High School in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, attended in 2019.[1] According to the outlet, the 2019 Riverdale yearbook referred to the student exclusively by the female first name Penelope, while the photographs showed traditionally masculine clothing — a military-style shirt in group photos and a tie-dye sweatshirt in the class photo.[1] The yearbook indicated participation in four school clubs that year, including the GSTA club.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] carried the story as a bonus item in its September 8, 2025 news brief.[1]
