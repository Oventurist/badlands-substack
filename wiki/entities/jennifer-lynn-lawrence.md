# Jennifer Lynn Lawrence

Jennifer Lynn Lawrence is an American political activist associated with the "We Build the Wall" campaign and, with her husband [[dustin-stockton|Dustin Stockton]], the co-founder of the organizing project [[america-mission|America Mission]].[1]

A February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay noted that Lawrence may be better known publicly for an incident in which Representative [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] presented an altered version of one of her tweets during the second impeachment trial of Donald Trump.[1]

Lawrence and Stockton use [[twitter-spaces|Twitter Spaces]] extensively for activism and for building a nationwide community of participants, hosting both agenda-driven organizing meetings and informal deep-dive discussions.[1]
