# Defense Priorities

Defense Priorities is a Washington foreign-policy think tank described in reporting as a Koch-funded isolationist organization founded by allies of libertarian Senator [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] (R-KY).[1] It advocates military restraint and a reduced U.S. footprint abroad, particularly in the Middle East.[1]

## Role in the DiMino appointment controversy

The institution drew attention in January 2025 when [[michael-dimino|Michael DiMino]] — a former [[cia|CIA]] military analyst and first-term [[pentagon|Defense Department]] official who had been a fellow at Defense Priorities — was appointed deputy assistant secretary of defense for the Middle East in [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second administration.[1] Jewish Insider reported that pro-Israel Republicans were alarmed by DiMino's record, which included describing Iran's missile attack on Israel as "fairly moderate" and urging the United States against bombing the [[houthis|Houthis]], calling instead for American pressure on Israel.[1]

In a February 2024 webinar hosted by Defense Priorities, DiMino declared that the Middle East does "not really" matter for U.S. interests, argued that "vital or existential threats" in the region are "best characterized as minimal to nonexistent," and contended that the American role there had not produced benefits.[1] He was sworn in on Monday, January 20, 2025.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the appointment as evidence that Trump's earlier selection of ardent Zionists such as [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] had been a feint intended to lull the Deep State and Tel Aviv, writing that "Mike Huckabee is noise, Michael DiMino is signal," and noting that DiMino was sworn in alongside roughly thirty other Pentagon appointees with almost no press coverage.[1]
