# Vicky Fogg

Vicky Fogg is the head of school at [[mid-vermont-christian-school|Mid Vermont Christian School]] in Vermont.[1]

## 2023 tournament withdrawal statement

In February 2023, after the school's girls basketball team withdrew from the Vermont Division IV state tournament rather than face an opponent with a transgender student-athlete, Fogg explained the decision in an email to the Valley News: "We believe playing against an opponent with a biological male jeopardizes the fairness of the game and the safety of our players."[1] She added that "allowing biological males to participate in women's sports sets a bad precedent for the future of women's sports in general."[1] Fogg declined a request for an interview.[1]

Her statement placed the school at the center of national argument over [[transgender-athletes|transgender athletes]] in girls' and women's competition, and was cited approvingly by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] as a model of institutional willingness to accept a competitive loss on principle.[1]
