# Rohit Chopra

Rohit Chopra served as director of the [[consumer-financial-protection-bureau|Consumer Financial Protection Bureau]] until he was fired by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in early February 2025, in what was described as the latest purge of a Biden administration holdover.[1]

## Tenure

Chopra was one of the more important regulators from the previous Democratic administration still on the job after Trump took office on January 20, 2025.[1] His tenure saw the removal of medical debt from credit reports and limits on overdraft penalties, measures grounded in the premise that the financial system could be made fairer and more competitive in ways that helped consumers.[1] Many in the financial industry viewed these actions as regulatory overreach.[1]

## Departure

In a social media post on Saturday about his departure, Chopra thanked people across the country who "shared their ideas and experiences" with the consumer financial watchdog agency.[1] "You helped us hold powerful companies & their executives accountable for breaking the law, and you made our work better," he wrote on X, above pictures of his letter announcing that he would no longer lead the bureau.[1]
