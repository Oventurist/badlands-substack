# USS Carl Vinson

The USS Carl Vinson is a United States Navy aircraft carrier that, as of mid-June 2025, was the sole US aircraft carrier operating in the Middle East region.[1]

## June 2025 posture

US defense officials told Fox News that as of Friday, June 13, 2025, the Carl Vinson strike group was the only carrier group present in the Middle East, after having been deployed for several months.[1] The [[uss-nimitz]] carrier strike group had been scheduled to replace it on a normal rotation, but instead sailed from the South China Sea toward the Middle East ahead of schedule, meaning both strike groups would be in the region simultaneously.[1] The doubling of US carrier presence coincided with the escalating exchange of strikes between [[israel]] and [[iran]] that began the previous week.[1]
