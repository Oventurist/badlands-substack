# Kosovo Liberation Army

The Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA) was the separatist Albanian armed group active during the breakup of [[yugoslavia|Yugoslavia]].[1]

Badlands Media notes that the KLA had previously been considered a terrorist group by the United States, and cites reporting that the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] "helped to train" the organization.[1] The Guardian article *CIA's bastard army ran riot in Balkans' backed extremists* is quoted to the effect that the agency "encouraged them to launch a rebellion in southern Serbia in an effort to undermine the then Yugoslav President Slobodan Milosevic."[1]

The KLA's insurgency forms the kinetic counterpart, in the article's account, to the later "nonviolent" campaign waged through [[otpor|Otpor!]] against [[slobodan-milosevic|Slobodan Milosevic]].[1]
