# Calcalist

Calcalist is an Israeli business and financial news outlet whose technology desk, CTech, publishes English-language coverage of the Israeli startup and high-tech economy.[1]

## June 2024 report on the Israeli high-tech sector

Calcalist published a widely circulated report documenting the pressure on Israel's technology industry during the Gaza War, headlined by the quotation from a senior official at an international company operating in Israel: "We are becoming a pariah state, a hated country, and this trickles down to high-tech as well. Even our friends no longer want to be associated with Israeli technology."[1]

The report drew on a viral personal post describing a startup vice president of development who enlisted on October 7, served repeated reserve rotations, and ultimately left his company by mutual agreement because it needed someone able to commit fully.[1] Calcalist argued that such cases reflected undercurrents extending beyond personnel costs and posed a real threat to the continued success of the industry, as more companies — including early-stage startups — chose to spread risk by recruiting workers abroad.[1] The article carried comments from [[fiverr|Fiverr]] chief executive [[micha-kaufman|Micha Kaufman]] on geographic dispersion of staff and on the difficulty Israeli firms faced raising capital, and reported on an industry letter to senior ministers warning against the flight of high-tech jobs overseas.[1]

The piece was cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] alongside coverage of the Senate investigation into [[affinity-partners|Affinity Partners]], as evidence for the argument that Saudi investment in Israeli technology was preemptively responding to an anticipated economic crisis.[1]
