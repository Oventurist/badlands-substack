# The New Yorker

The New Yorker is an American magazine known for long-form reporting and cultural criticism. In Badlands Media's coverage it appears as the publisher of [[ronan-farrow|Ronan Farrow]]'s September 2023 investigative profile "Elon Musk's Shadow Rule."[1]

## Coverage of Elon Musk

The magazine's piece argued that [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s "influence is more brazen and expansive" than earlier forms of oligarchic meddling in the fate of nations, publishing at a moment when Musk faced legal pressure from the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1] [[errol-musk|Errol Musk]] characterized the article as "a hit job, a shadow government-sponsored opening salvo" against his son, framing it as narrative preparation for a more direct attack.[1] Badlands cited the episode as an example of establishment media outlets being used as instruments in the [[information-war|Information War]].[1]
