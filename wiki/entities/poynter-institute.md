# Poynter Institute

The Poynter Institute is a journalism organization named in Badlands Media coverage among the institutions that comprise the modern fact-checking and content-policing apparatus.[1] It is the home of the [[international-fact-checking-network|International Fact-Checking Network]], whose merger with the legacy media the article characterizes as a response to anti-establishment populism.[1]

The article expresses the hope that Poynter, along with the [[anti-defamation-league|ADL]], the [[southern-poverty-law-center|SPLC]] and the [[center-for-countering-digital-hate|CCDH]], will receive its own day of legal judgment in the manner of the actions taken against [[media-matters|Media Matters]].[1]
