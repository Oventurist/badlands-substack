# Poynter Institute

The **Poynter Institute** is a journalism-training nonprofit listed by Badlands Media among the organizations it describes as "policing journalism."[1]

The article groups Poynter with the [[pulitzer-center-on-crisis-reporting|Pulitzer Center on Crisis Reporting]], the [[national-press-foundation|National Press Foundation]], the [[international-center-for-journalists|International Center for Journalists]] and the [[solutions-journalism-network|Solutions Journalism Network]], asserting that all of them are connected to individuals belonging to one or multiple of the clandestine think tanks examined across *The Men Behind the Curtain* series.[1] It presents this cluster as evidence of how [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]] shape the boundaries of acceptable reporting.[1]
