# Avangrid

**Avangrid** is an American energy company listed among the four largest US solar firms in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]].[1]

Carlson reported Avangrid's stock down 11 percent from [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s January 2021 inauguration through May 2023.[1] Its inclusion serves his broader point that the renewable sector as a whole failed to appreciate under an administration explicitly committed to climate policy, with the sole exception of [[first-solar|First Solar]] — the company Carlson associates with [[barack-obama|Obama]]-era political favoritism.[1] The other companies in the solar group were [[nextera-energy|NextEra]] and [[sunpower|SunPower]].[1]
