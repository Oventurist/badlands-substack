# Helen McCaw

**Helen McCaw** is a senior analyst at the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]] who, in January 2026, was associated with a public warning that financial markets could collapse if the U.S. government were to announce the existence of extraterrestrial life.[1]

The warning drew attention in the Badlands corpus chiefly for its timing. [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the article reporting McCaw's warning itself acknowledged that the disclosure trend had been years in the making, making it unclear why a senior central bank analyst would choose that particular moment to raise the alarm.[1] The proximity to the lifting of Venezuelan sanctions and the [[safeguarding-venezuelan-oil-revenue-executive-order|executive order shielding Venezuelan oil revenue]] from bank creditors led him to conclude that the timing "can't be a coincidence," and to fold the episode into the broader [[alien-disclosure-op|alien disclosure]] narrative thread.[1]
