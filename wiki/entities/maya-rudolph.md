# Maya Rudolph

Maya Rudolph is an American actress and comedian best known within political media coverage for her recurring portrayal of [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] on [[saturday-night-live|Saturday Night Live]].[1]

## Harris portrayal

Rudolph's Harris character was pushed into the spotlight on the late-night show as the November 2024 election approached, and drew extensive audience commentary.[1] In the mid-October 2024 episode, the show used a Family Feud–style sketch to lampoon Harris's trademark mannerisms, including her signature "word salad" style of speaking.[1]

Reaction to the portrayal was widely read as politically significant rather than merely comedic, with social media commentators arguing that mockery from SNL signaled that the show's producers considered the Democratic candidate's campaign to be failing.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] judged the sketches unfunny and safe, arguing the writing reinforced underlying narratives rather than pushing the comedic envelope.[1]
