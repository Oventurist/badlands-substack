# Zack Vorhies

Zack Vorhies is a former Google software engineer turned whistleblower, known for releasing internal company documents that he says demonstrate the deliberate shaping of public opinion through search results and content ranking.[1]

## Role in media-manipulation arguments

Vorhies is frequently cited in alternative-media analysis as documentary evidence that large technology platforms possess — and exercise — the ability to manipulate belief at scale. [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] invoked Vorhies's document trove in January 2023 while analysing the suppression of the [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]] undercover video featuring [[jordan-trishton-walker|Jordan Trishton Walker]], arguing that the throttling of the story and the deletion of coverage by the [[daily-mail|Daily Mail]] were consistent with the capabilities Vorhies had documented.[1] In that framing, Vorhies's disclosures sit alongside the [[asch-conformity-experiments|Asch conformity experiments]] and [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]]-style press coordination as components of a broader theory of manufactured consensus.[1]
