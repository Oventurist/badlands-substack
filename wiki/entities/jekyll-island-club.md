# Jekyll Island Club

The Jekyll Island Club was the site of the secret 1910 meeting at which, according to the article, the master plan to transform the entire monetary system of the United States was hatched.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The meeting followed the persuasion of Senator [[nelson-aldrich|Nelson W. Aldrich]] by leaders of the [[populist-party|Populist Party]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) The article lists the participants of the Jekyll Island group as:[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

- Senator Aldrich, whose daughter married a Rockefeller[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
- A. P. Andrews, Assistant Secretary of the United States Treasury Department[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
- [[paul-warburg|Paul Warburg]], an employee of Jacob Schiff with family ties to the Rothschilds[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
- Frank A. Vanderlip, President of the National City Bank of New York[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
- Henry P. Davison, Senior Partner of the [[jp-morgan|J. P. Morgan]] Company[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
- Charles D. Norton, President of the Morgan-dominated First National Bank of New York[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
- [[benjamin-strong|Benjamin Strong]], representing J. P. Morgan[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

Together these men represented about one quarter of the world's wealth at the time.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) They departed from Hoboken, New Jersey by train in complete secrecy, dropping their last names in favor of first names or code names so that no one would discover who they were.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) The article treats this episode as emblematic of its claim that secrecy is at the heart of the [[cabal]]'s operations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The plan produced at Jekyll Island fed into the [[national-monetary-commission|National Monetary Commission]] recommendations and ultimately the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act of 1913]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
