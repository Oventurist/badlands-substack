# Jekyll Island Club

The **Jekyll Island Club** was the site of the secret 1910 meeting at which, in the Badlands Media account, the plan to transform the United States monetary system was drafted.[1]

## The gathering

After leaders of the [[populist-party|Populist Party]] persuaded Senator [[nelson-w-aldrich|Nelson W. Aldrich]] to overhaul the monetary system, the master plan was hatched at Jekyll Island.[1] The article maps the participants as:[1]

- Sen. Aldrich, whose daughter married a Rockefeller[1]
- A.P. Andrews, Assistant Secretary of the United States Treasury Department[1]
- [[paul-warburg|Paul Warburg]], an employee of Jacob Schiff with family ties to the Rothschilds[1]
- [[frank-a-vanderlip|Frank A. Vanderlip]], President of the National City Bank of New York[1]
- [[henry-p-davison|Henry P. Davison]], Senior Partner of the J.P. Morgan Company[1]
- Charles D. Norton, President of the Morgan-dominated First National Bank of New York[1]
- [[benjamin-strong|Benjamin Strong]], representing J.P. Morgan[1]

## Secrecy

Together these men represented about one quarter of the world's wealth at the time. They left Hoboken, New Jersey by train in complete secrecy, dropping their last names in favor of first names or code names so that no one would discover who they were.[1] The article treats this episode as emblematic of its claim that secrecy is at the heart of the cabal's operations.[1]
