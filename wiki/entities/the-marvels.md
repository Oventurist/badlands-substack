# The Marvels

***The Marvels*** is a 2023 superhero film released by the [[walt-disney-company|Walt Disney Company]] as part of the [[marvel-cinematic-universe|Marvel Cinematic Universe]]. Its November 2023 debut was reported as the worst opening in MCU history and became a widely cited example of audience rejection of the studio's output.[1]

## Box office performance

Writing at [[breitbart-news|Breitbart]], [[john-nolte|John Nolte]] described the film as "a certified disaster," citing Deadline projections of an opening weekend between $47 million and $55 million.[1] Even at the top of that range, the film would rank as the worst opening of the MCU, a franchise launched 33 movies earlier in 2008 with *Iron Man*.[1] The prior low was held by 2008's *The Incredible Hulk*, starring Edward Norton, which opened at $55.4 million.[1]

Prospects for recovery through word of mouth were judged poor: die-hard fans turned out on Friday night and the film still earned only a weak "B" CinemaScore, a signal that repeat business and positive word-of-mouth advertising were unlikely.[1] Nolte contrasted the result with the MCU's historical record of roughly $30 billion in worldwide box office from 30 films, arguing the franchise had been ruined by ideological content.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that although many in the "Info War" avoid the MCU, Disney and Hollywood entertainment generally, those properties hold significant sway within what he called the collective mind of American society.[1] He characterized the film's failure as one of the biggest market wins the [[culture-war|Culture War]] had seen since 2016 and a genuine sea-change moment in both alternative and mainstream discourse around "the wokening."[1]

Burning Bright added that Disney, while not the only studio, was the largest, and had run some of the most male-centric and male-supported franchises into the ground through a mix of ideological programming and incompetence.[1] He suggested that South Park's satire had been only the beginning, that "open season" had been declared on the entertainment industry, and that whether these intellectual properties and their parent companies survive the [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]] remained to be seen.[1]
