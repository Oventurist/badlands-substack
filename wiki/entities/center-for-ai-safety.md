# Center for AI Safety

The **Center for AI Safety** (CAIS) is a research and advocacy organisation focused on reducing societal-scale risks from artificial intelligence. It came to broad public attention on May 30, 2023 when it published the [[statement-on-ai-risk|Statement on AI Risk]], a single-sentence declaration signed by dozens of AI industry leaders, academics and celebrities asserting that "mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside other societal-scale risks such as pandemics and nuclear war."[1]

## The 2023 statement

The statement drew signatures from [[openai|OpenAI]] chief executive [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]], AI pioneer Geoffrey Hinton, executives and researchers at Google DeepMind and [[anthropic|Anthropic]], [[microsoft|Microsoft]] chief technology officer Kevin Scott, cryptographer Bruce Schneier, climate writer Bill McKibben and the musician [[grimes|Grimes]].[1] CAIS director Dan Hendrycks compared the effort to atomic scientists issuing warnings about the very technologies they had created.[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the CAIS statement with ambivalence, welcoming any industry discussion of safety and ethics while suggesting the signatories were trying to "put the genie back in the lamp" after profiting from an unregulated build-out.[1] She emphasised that Hendrycks' atomic-scientist analogy carried an ominous timeline, since the 1939 warnings preceded the 1945 bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki by six years, and asked whether CAIS' warning of an [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] extinction event would prove similarly prophetic.[1]
