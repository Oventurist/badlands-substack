# Kermit Roosevelt Jr.

Kermit Roosevelt Jr. was the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] officer who headed the 1953 coup that removed democratically elected Iranian Prime Minister [[mohammad-mosaddegh|Mohammad Mossadegh]] from power, and was the grandson of President Theodore Roosevelt.[1]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in July 2023, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] presented Roosevelt's role as itself a piece of evidence about how deeply the covert apparatus is woven into the American establishment, remarking that readers who find it strange that the grandson of "one of the guys on Mount Rushmore" led a CIA coup in [[iran|Iran]] "are correct."[1]

## The Mossadegh operation

According to Carlson's account of the [[1953-iranian-coup|1953 Iranian coup]], the operation Roosevelt directed was triggered when Mossadegh nationalized the Iranian oil industry and expelled foreign companies, in particular British Petroleum, whose production depended heavily on Iranian fields.[1] The British, described in the article as the "puppet masters" of the American side of the operation, asked the United States — or rather the CIA — to step in and remove the popular prime minister and install a CIA-, US- and British-friendly leader in his place, [[mohammad-reza-pahlavi|the Shah of Iran]].[1] The CIA pushed propaganda, rigged Iran's election and paid thugs to create violence and chaos in the streets of Tehran in order to install a pro-American, pro-CIA government, a pattern Carlson compares directly to conditions in the United States in his own time.[1] Mossadegh was tried for treason and placed under house arrest, while the Shah ruled for a further 26 years before being ousted in the [[iranian-revolution|Iranian Revolution]] of 1979.[1]

The CIA publicly acknowledged its involvement in the Iranian government overthrow roughly ten years before the article was written.[1]
