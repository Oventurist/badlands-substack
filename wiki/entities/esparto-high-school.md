# Esparto High School

Esparto High School is a public high school in Yolo County, California, near Sacramento.[1]

In September 2023 the school became a national talking point after it canceled a football game, stating that six players had tested positive for COVID-19; it also said multiple players were injured and that it did not have enough players available for the game.[1] It was one of two California high schools to confirm cancellations of football games because of COVID-19 cases among students, a development reported amid concerns that virus-related rules might be reimposed in the coming months.[1]

District Superintendent Christina Goennier told FOX40 that "ensuring the well-being of students is not only a moral obligation but also a legal and ethical responsibility."[1] Aimee Sisson, the Yolo County health officer, said she supported the move, describing the health department's role as supporting schools in staying open for in-person instruction by providing guidance on what to do when cases arise and offering testing resources, with the goal of keeping children in school in person but safely.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the episode as an illustration of a deeper structural problem rather than an individual failing, arguing that the issue is not that particular officials are corrupt but that the [[administrative-state|administrative state]] that enabled what he called tyrannical COVID overreach should not exist at all.[1]
