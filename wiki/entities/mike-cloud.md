# Mike Cloud

Mike Cloud is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas who was one of the twenty-one holdouts in the January 2023 speakership election.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speakership fight

Cloud's vote was part of the bloc that extracted the [[january-2023-mccarthy-concessions|January 2023 McCarthy concessions]] from [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]].[1] In the settlement he won a new seat on the [[house-appropriations-committee|Appropriations Committee]], which controls federal spending, and McCarthy also named him to the [[select-subcommittee-on-the-coronavirus-pandemic|select committee investigating the origins of the Covid pandemic]].[1] Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson presents both assignments as evidence that the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]] bloc was positioned on precisely the panels handling the era's central controversies.[1]
