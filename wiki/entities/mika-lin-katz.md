# Mika Lin Katz

Mika Lin Katz, 37, also known as Michael Collins, is a trans-identified male in Edmonton, Alberta, charged in connection with the alleged sexual assault of multiple women at a women's shelter in the city.[1]

## Charges

On January 24, 2025, the Edmonton Police Service issued a press release on the incidents, revealing that Katz had first come to their attention in August 2024, when two women came forward to report that they had been victimized.[1] Following an investigation, Katz was arrested and charged with two counts of sexual assault.[1]

Despite the seriousness of the charges, Katz was released with conditions that included having no contact or communication with any of the complainants; possessing no weapons, firearms or ammunition; and remaining more than 50 metres away from any women's shelter in Alberta.[1]
