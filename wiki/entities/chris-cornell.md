# Chris Cornell

Chris Cornell was an American rock singer, frontman of Soundgarden and Audioslave, whose 2017 death is treated in Badlands Media commentary as a suspicious killing rather than a suicide.[1]

## Death and the "red scarf" claim

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounted that Cornell "hung himself from a doorknob after a concert in Detroit," and questioned the official account: readers are asked to look at the nearest door and note how low a doorknob sits, and are told that Cornell was a father of three who "was beaten to a pulp."[1] Carlson wrote that Cornell is not the only figure said to have hanged himself with a red scarf, and asserted that the red scarf is the [[cabal|Cabal]]'s way of signaling "this is what happens when you turn on the Cabal."[1]

## Alleged trafficking documentary

The essay claimed that Cornell, together with his friends [[chester-bennington|Chester Bennington]] of Linkin Park and the Swedish DJ [[avicii|Avicii]], was producing a documentary exposing [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]] at the time of his death, and that all three were subsequently said to have killed themselves.[1] Actor [[brad-pitt|Brad Pitt]] and music producer [[rick-rubin|Rick Rubin]] are described as believed participants in the same group who, having seen their friends die, "got the point and stepped back in line."[1]

## Interpretation

Cornell's death anchors Carlson's broader argument that entertainers who refuse the industry's bargain, or attempt to leave it, "usually find [themselves] the victim of an untimely death," in contrast to politicians who can instead be destroyed by character assassination.[1] The claims are presented as opinion; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the views of contributing authors.[1]
