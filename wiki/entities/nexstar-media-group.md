# Nexstar Media Group

Nexstar Media Group is an American television broadcasting company that owns and operates local station affiliates across the United States.[1]

## Role in the Kimmel suspension

In September 2025, affiliate stations controlled by Nexstar announced they would indefinitely cancel broadcasts of [[jimmy-kimmel|Jimmy Kimmel]]'s late-night program over remarks he made about the death of conservative activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1] That affiliate action, taken alongside a similar move by [[sinclair-broadcast-group|Sinclair Broadcast Group]], preceded [[abc-news|ABC]]'s own decision to indefinitely halt production of the show.[1] The episode illustrated the leverage that large affiliate groups hold over national network programming.[1] See [[jimmy-kimmel-live-suspension|Jimmy Kimmel Live! suspension]].
