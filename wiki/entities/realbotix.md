# Realbotix

Realbotix is a Las Vegas–based robotics company that manufactures AI-powered humanoid robots. It has corporate ties to a sister company that makes hyper-realistic sex dolls, a connection that became central to public controversy in 2026.[1]

## "Sally" and the Salamanca contract

The [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]] in western New York approved a purchase of nearly $60,000 from Realbotix for a stationary humanoid robot named "Sally," intended to support high school robotics and technology students. Sally can move her arms and hands but has nonworking legs.[1]

Parents, teachers, and state officials objected on grounds of student data privacy and the company's sex-doll affiliation, and the district paused the pilot. State Education Commissioner [[betty-rosa|Betty Rosa]] flagged an inconsistency between the district's claim that the robot would not deliver instruction and a board presentation describing it as a "tutoring platform."[1]

Reporting indicated the district considered Realbotix a natural fit because its robots are already programmed with WozEd, a curriculum from [[apple|Apple]] co-founder [[steve-wozniak|Steve Wozniak]] that the district already uses.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the episode as a rebranding exercise, writing that "the makers of Sally want to assure you that Sally is NOT a sex robot" and joking that Sally "might live a double life in Las Vegas."[1]
