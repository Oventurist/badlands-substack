# Realbotix

Realbotix is a Las Vegas–based robotics company that manufactures humanlike AI-powered humanoid robots. It became the subject of national attention in July 2026 when the [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]] in western New York approved a nearly $60,000 purchase of a Realbotix robot for classroom use, as reported in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]].

## The "Sally" robot

The robot purchased by the district, named "Sally," is a stationary humanoid unit intended to support high school robotics and technology students. Sally can move her arms and hands but has nonworking legs. The Brief noted that Realbotix was described as "a natural fit" for the district because its robots are already programmed with WozEd, a curriculum created by Apple co-founder [[steve-wozniak|Steve Wozniak]] that the district already uses.

## Controversy

Parents, teachers and state officials raised objections on two grounds: student data privacy, and Realbotix's corporate ties to a sister company that manufactures hyper-realistic sex dolls. State Education Commissioner [[betty-rosa|Betty Rosa]] additionally identified a discrepancy between the district's assurance that the robot would not deliver instruction and a board presentation describing it as a "tutoring platform." The pilot was paused pending enhanced data-privacy agreements with the state.

Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the episode as an attempted rebranding, satirising the company's insistence that Sally is "NOT a sex robot" but "a professional educator," and questioning how the project advanced past its initial pitch.
