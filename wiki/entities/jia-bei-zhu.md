# Jia Bei Zhu

**Jia Bei Zhu**, also known as Qiang "David" He and rendered in some reporting as Jiabei Zhu, is a Chinese national convicted in federal court in California in connection with an unlicensed laboratory and medical-device fraud operation, and linked to the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas biolab]] discovered in early 2026.

## Las Vegas connection

The Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 reports that Zhu owned the east Las Vegas rental property through an LLC and hired [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]] to manage it. Materials found in the property's garage — lab equipment, test kits, vaccines and influenza samples — may, according to the source, have been connected to Zhu's California operations.

## California conviction

Zhu was arrested in 2023 and later convicted for operating [[universal-meditech|Universal Meditech Inc.]] (later Prestige Biotech) in the Fresno/Reedley area of California. He was found guilty of conspiracy to commit wire fraud, multiple counts of wire fraud, distributing adulterated and misbranded medical devices, and making a false statement to the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]], after fraudulently selling more than one million faulty COVID-19 tests imported from China for nearly $4 million between 2020 and 2023.

According to a report from the [[select-committee-on-the-chinese-communist-party|Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party]] cited in the article, vials found in the 2022 Reedley lab were labeled as pathogens including HIV, tuberculosis, malaria, hepatitis and Ebola. The source further states that when Zhu and his wife were arrested in 2023, authorities realized he was already a fugitive in Canada, wanted for his connection to companies in the Chinese dairy industry. His business partner [[zhaoyan-wang|Zhaoyan Wang]] was indicted in the same scheme but fled to China and remains a fugitive.

Trial evidence cited in the article showed that Zhu and Wang hired inexperienced employees — cellphone salespeople, supermarket workers and childcare workers — who "would not ask any questions," some recruited through the Fresno County Economic Development Corporation, a public organization that places unskilled workers and subsidizes employers who hire them.
