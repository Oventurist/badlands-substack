# Jia Bei Zhu

Jia Bei Zhu, also known as Qiang "David" He and rendered in some reporting as Jiabei Zhu, is a Chinese national convicted in federal court in California over a fraudulent medical-device and biolab operation.[1]

## Las Vegas property

Zhu owned, through an LLC, the east [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas]] rental home where an illegal biolab was discovered in late January and early February 2026, and he hired [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]] to manage it.[1] Materials found in the Las Vegas garage may have been connected to Zhu's California operations.[1]

## California conviction

Zhu operated [[universal-meditech|Universal Meditech Inc.]] — later Prestige Biotech — in the Fresno/Reedley area.[1] He was found guilty of conspiracy to commit wire fraud, multiple counts of wire fraud, distributing adulterated and misbranded medical devices, and making a false statement to the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]], after fraudulently selling more than one million faulty COVID-19 tests imported from China for nearly $4 million between 2020 and 2023.[1]

According to a report from the [[select-committee-on-the-chinese-communist-party|Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party]], vials found at the 2022 Reedley lab were labeled as pathogens including "HIV," "tuberculosis," "malaria," "hepatitis," and "Ebola."[1] Trial evidence showed that Zhu and business partner [[zhaoyan-wang|Zhaoyan Wang]] hired inexperienced employees — cellphone salespeople, supermarket workers and childcare workers — who "would not ask any questions," some of them recruited through the Fresno County Economic Development Corporation, a public body that places unskilled workers and subsidizes employers who hire them.[1]

## Fugitive status

Zhu and his wife were arrested in 2023, at which point authorities realized he was already a fugitive in Canada, wanted for his connection to companies in the Chinese dairy industry.[1] He remains in federal custody in California.[1] Wang was indicted for his role in the scheme but fled to China and remains a fugitive from the United States.[1]
