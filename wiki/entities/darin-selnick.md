# Darin Selnick

Darin Selnick served as deputy chief of staff of the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] during the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Suspension in the 2025 Pentagon leak probe

On April 15, 2025, Selnick was suspended and escorted out of the Pentagon building as part of an investigation into potential leaks of sensitive information, according to a defense official cited by [[politico|Politico]].[1] He was one of two top political appointees placed on administrative leave that day, alongside [[dan-caldwell|Dan Caldwell]], a senior adviser to Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]].[1]

The material under investigation reportedly included operational plans for the [[panama-canal|Panama Canal]], a second carrier headed to the Red Sea, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s visit to the Pentagon, and a pause in intelligence collection provided to [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the removals to the argument in [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s *[[government-gangsters|Government Gangsters]]* that officials disloyal to the Constitution must be purged from key positions, writing that leaking classified intelligence to attack the president requires sympathetic personnel inside agencies such as the Department of Defense.[1] She argued the two men should also face prosecution.[1]
