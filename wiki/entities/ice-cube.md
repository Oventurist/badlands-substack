# Ice Cube

Ice Cube is an American rapper, actor and filmmaker who has become a recurring figure in commentary about independent thought and speech restrictions in American public life.[1]

## Tucker Carlson interview

In July 2023, Ice Cube appeared in the tenth episode of [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s Twitter-based program, titled "Stay in your lane," in which the two drove through South Central Los Angeles and discussed what it means to be a free thinker under contemporary cultural conditions.[1] The Badlands News Brief of July 26, 2023 described the roughly thirteen-minute-and-forty-eight-second segment as authentic in tone, with Carlson pressing on subjects the brief characterized as "third rail" — race, politics, corporate America, and the question of heroes.[1]

Commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] highlighted an exchange in which Ice Cube asked, "If it's true, why can't I say it?", to which Carlson replied, laughing, "Well, you can't say it because it's true," prompting Ice Cube to respond, "And that's the problem with the world today."[1] The brief noted that the episode appeared more heavily produced than Carlson's usual boathouse format, reading that as a signal that his new media venture was investing in growth — a development the brief treated as favorable for independent media generally, though it judged the production value secondary to the value of the ideas being aired before a mass audience.[1]
