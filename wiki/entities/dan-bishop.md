# Dan Bishop

Dan Bishop is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from North Carolina and one of the twenty-one conservatives who withheld support from [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] during the January 2023 speakership contest.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speakership fight

Bishop was among the holdouts aligned with the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]] whose votes forced the [[january-2023-mccarthy-concessions|January 2023 McCarthy concessions]].[1] After the deal, he continued to serve on both the [[house-judiciary-committee|Judiciary]] and [[house-homeland-security-committee|Homeland Security]] committees, and McCarthy additionally named him to Judiciary's new [[select-subcommittee-on-the-weaponization-of-the-federal-government|subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]].[1] Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson counts Bishop among the America First members whose assignments show that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] effectively controls the committee structure of the House.[1]
