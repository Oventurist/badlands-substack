# ICS-CERT

The Industrial Control Systems Cyber Emergency Response Team (ICS-CERT) was the U.S. Department of Homeland Security body responsible for responding to cyber incidents affecting industrial control systems and other [[operational-technology|operational technology]] used in [[critical-infrastructure|critical infrastructure]].[1] Its functions were subsequently folded into the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]], the successor organization that, in the framing of the Badlands account, is now "in charge of all of it" — water, energy, banking, communications, and elections.[1]

[[marty-edwards|Marty Edwards]], later Deputy CTO for OT and IoT at [[tenable|Tenable]], was described as "the last and the longest-serving Director" of ICS-CERT before its absorption into CISA.[1] His testimony at the February 6, 2024 [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Homeland Security Committee]] water sector hearing drew on that industrial control systems background, particularly the cross-sector reuse of identical programmable logic controllers.[1]
