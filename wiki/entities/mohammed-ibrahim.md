# Mohammed Ibrahim

Mohammed Ibrahim is a Palestinian-American teenager and Florida resident who was detained by [[israel|Israel]] for more than nine months before being released in December 2025 following the intervention of [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]].[1]

## Detention

Israel detained Ibrahim in February 2025 on allegations that he threw stones at Israeli troops in the West Bank, a charge his family denies.[1] He was never charged, and was instead held under [[administrative-detention|administrative detention]], a practice permitting indefinite detention without due process.[1] Ibrahim turned 16 in prison; his family said his health deteriorated significantly, as he lost more than 20 pounds and contracted scabies — both described as common consequences for Palestinian prisoners according to Israel's own Public Defender's Office.[1] He was visiting family in the West Bank when he was arrested.[1]

## Release

Ibrahim's family lobbied U.S. lawmakers for his release, and the case eventually reached Kushner, who contacted senior Israeli officials demanding the boy be freed, according to a U.S. official and a second source cited by [[times-of-israel|The Times of Israel]].[1] Washington took particular issue with the fact that a U.S. citizen was being held without charge by an allied country.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] drew two conclusions: that Kushner, often accused of sinister motives by parts of the MAGA coalition, demonstrated both clout with Israeli officials and willingness to use it for good — likely at the request of Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas — and that the underlying case raised the question of why Israel imprisoned a 16-year-old foreign national for nine months without charge or due process.[1] He noted the story was reported by The Times of Israel, and argued the episode illustrated a moral distinction between American and Israeli culture rather than antisemitism.[1]
