# Frank Moss

Frank Moss was a United States Senator identified in Badlands Media commentary as one of the close collaborators of President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] in the drive for large-scale federal water development during the early 1960s.[1]

## Water development advocacy

Kennedy had reversed the Eisenhower administration's "No New Builds" agenda and supported bills accelerating federal funding for water, hydroelectric and irrigation projects across the nation.[1] Moss, together with [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert Kennedy]], was among those pushing this program forward.[1] The article states that Kennedy's death derailed many of these projects as a Malthusian anti-development paradigm became infused into national policy making, leaving proposals such as the [[north-american-water-and-power-alliance|North American Water and Power Alliance]] to drift into obscurity.[1]
