# Robby Mook

Robby Mook is an American Democratic political operative who served as campaign manager for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s 2016 presidential campaign.[1] He is named in public records as one of the senior campaign figures who, in coordination with Clinton herself, participated in an effort to publicly link then-candidate [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to Russian president [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] during the 2016 battle for the White House.[1]

## Role in the 2016 Clinton campaign strategy

According to newly declassified evidence discussed in reporting by Just the News and summarized in the Badlands News Brief, the [[fbi|FBI]] was alerted in 2016 to intelligence indicating that Clinton planned to smear Trump by tying him to Putin.[1] Clinton's own words in a 2016 election post-mortem are described as likely to be central to investigators, and the post-mortem identifies a circle of campaign principals involved in the Trump–Russia messaging effort: campaign general counsel [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], campaign manager Mook, campaign chairman [[john-podesta|John Podesta]], communications director [[jennifer-palmieri|Jennifer Palmieri]], and policy adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]], among others.[1]

## Relevance to the Russiagate investigations

Mook's inclusion in that group places him within the scope of questions now facing the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] strike force established by Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] to investigate the affair — specifically whether the 2016 effort amounted to an ordinary political dirty trick or rose to the level of a criminal conspiracy.[1] The [[russiagate-hoax|Russia collusion]] matter subsequently advanced to a grand jury after Bondi signed an order directing a federal prosecutor to present evidence.[1]
