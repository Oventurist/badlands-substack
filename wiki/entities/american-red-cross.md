# American Red Cross

The American Red Cross appears in Badlands Media coverage of the 1917 Russian revolution as the vehicle through which New York Federal Reserve director [[william-boyce-thompson|William Boyce Thompson]] operated in Petrograd.[1]

Thompson was installed as head of the organization during the revolution and was largely recognized as the true U.S. ambassador to the revolutionary government, reporting directly to President Wilson and bypassing the official U.S. Ambassador Francis.[1] From that position he disbursed personal funds to Russian revolutionary factions, including one million rubles to the Social Revolutionaries and $1,000,000 to the Bolsheviks for propaganda work in Germany and Austria.[1] The account frames the Red Cross mission as a cover for political and financial intervention rather than a purely humanitarian operation.[1]
