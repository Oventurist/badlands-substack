# Morning Consult

Morning Consult is an American survey research and polling firm whose primary-election tracking was cited in coverage of the 2024 Republican presidential nominating contest.[1]

## 2023 Republican primary polling

In a survey released on September 29, 2023, Morning Consult reported that 63 percent of potential Republican primary voters supported [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] for the party's 2024 presidential nomination, up from 58 percent in a survey released earlier that week ahead of the [[second-gop-presidential-debate-2023|second GOP presidential debate]].[1] The survey polled 1,183 potential Republican primary voters and was conducted on September 28, one day after the debate.[1]

The same survey found Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] slipping from 15 to 12 percent while remaining in second place; entrepreneur [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] and former South Carolina Governor [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] each losing two points, to 9 and 5 percent respectively; former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] losing one point to 5 percent; and Senator Tim Scott of South Carolina unchanged at 2 percent.[1]

Morning Consult concluded that the data reinforced its view that Trump was "in the driver's seat of the Republican primary," and that Trump-less debates were having little impact on the other candidates' national support and might in fact be helping the former president.[1]

## July 2024 poll on the Trump assassination attempt

A Morning Consult poll released on Monday, July 22, 2024 found that 34 percent of Democrats believed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had faked the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempt on his life]] the previous Saturday.[2] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] contrasted the result with 2021 polling by the Public Religion Research Institute showing roughly 23 percent of Republicans endorsing the central QAnon claim, presenting the comparison as evidence that left-wing conspiracy theorizing — labeled [[blueanon|BlueAnon]] — had gone mainstream.[2]
