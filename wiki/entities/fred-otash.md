# Fred Otash

Fred Otash was a Hollywood private investigator described as both a contractor for the [[fbi|FBI]] and [[cia|CIA]] and a worker for the Mafia and Jimmy Hoffa.[1] He is the single source for the claim that [[marilyn-monroe|Marilyn Monroe]]'s house was bugged and that recordings exist capturing [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]] threatening her life.[1]

## The invisible wiretaps

According to every version of the "Kennedy killed Marilyn" narrative, Bobby Kennedy visited Monroe's home on the day of her death, and her house had been bugged by a private investigator whose recordings supposedly contain audio of Kennedy demanding that she surrender compromising diaries.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] characterized this as circular reasoning at its finest: one non-existent piece of evidence, the diary, is "proven" to exist because of another non-existent piece of evidence, the wiretap recordings.[1] No one has ever heard the supposed tapes, and there is no evidence that they ever existed.[1]

Otash was known for the boast, "I'll work for anybody but communists. I'll do anything short of murder."[1]

## VeVea's chronology

Monroe biographer April VeVea noted that Otash had never said a word publicly about bugging Monroe's home until [[anthony-summers|Anthony Summers]] published his sensational book tying the Kennedys to her death, twenty-three years later.[1] VeVea wrote: "In 1985 Otash first came out with his claims that he had wired Marilyn's home, had heard her killed, etc. These tapes have never been released…. Only after many people had come out of the woodwork did Otash come out and say that he had bugged her apartment (see Anthony Summer's Goddess, 1985) as well as her home on 12305 5th Helena Drive. There has never been any bugging equipment found."[1]
