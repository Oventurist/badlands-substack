# Scottish National Party

The Scottish National Party (SNP) is the governing party of Scotland's devolved administration, and the party responsible for enacting and commencing the [[hate-crime-and-public-order-scotland-act-2021|Hate Crime and Public Order (Scotland) Act 2021]].[1]

## Hate crime legislation

The SNP's law, which came into force on April 1, 2024, created a new offence of "stirring up hatred" covering age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, transgender identity and intersex status, with penalties of up to seven years' imprisonment and a legal threshold requiring prosecutors to show only that hatred was "likely."[1] An SNP minister admitted that author [[jk-rowling|J.K. Rowling]] could be prosecuted for "misgendering" transgender people under the statute.[1] The party's community safety minister, [[siobhian-brown|Siobhian Brown]], first said misgendering would not be a crime, then acknowledged after commencement that such conduct could be reported and investigated, with criminality left to [[police-scotland|Police Scotland]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] situated the SNP's law within a coordinated international push alongside [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]]'s Online Harms Bill in [[canada|Canada]], describing the pattern as evidence of a globalist assault on the sovereign nation state.[1]
