# Kurt von Schleicher

Kurt von Schleicher was the anti-fascist German general voted into office as Chancellor in 1932, during [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]]'s loss of popular support.[1]

Matthew Ehret uses Schleicher's chancellorship as the marker of the Nazi party's moment of maximum weakness: it was precisely at this point, with the party bankrupt, that [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] — acting as a director of [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]] — provided the loans that kept it afloat.[1]
