# Benzion Gopstein

Benzion Gopstein is an Israeli far-right activist described as the current leader of the movement founded by Rabbi [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]].[1]

## Ideology

Gopstein is presented in Badlands coverage as the successor to Kahane at the head of what the outlet terms the "Messianic Movement" within Israeli politics — a current that seeks to expand Israel's borders under the banner of [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]], to codify the state as a formal theocracy promoting radical Jewish supremacy under the [[smotrich-doctrine|Smotrich Doctrine]], and to hasten the coming of the Jewish messiah through [[red-heifer|red heifer]] sacrifice and the construction of the [[third-temple|Third Temple]] on the site of the Al-Aqsa Mosque, a project associated with [[temple-institute|The Temple Institute]].[1] Both Kahane and Gopstein have said that Christians are their ancient enemy and have condoned the burning of churches.[1]

## US sanctions

Gopstein was sanctioned by the [[joe-biden|Biden]] State Department for "destabilizing" activities in the West Bank.[1] Badlands commentary argued these activities were connected to plans to build illegal Israeli settlements in the [[west-bank-annexation|West Bank]], the same construction that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had blocked during his first term.[1]

## Context

Kahane was arrested by the Shin Bet in the 1980s for plotting to blow up the Al-Aqsa Mosque in Jerusalem in the hope of starting a war with the Arabs, and his political party was banned from the [[knesset|Knesset]] in 1994 for a platform explicitly proclaiming Jewish supremacy over Christians, Muslims, and all others.[1] To solidify the alliance between [[likud|Likud]] and the [[otzma-yehudit|Otzma Yehudit]] ("Jewish Power") party, [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] supported reversing that ban in 2022.[1]

## Incitement conviction

As leader of the [[lehava|Lehava]] movement, Gopstein in 2015 called for the expulsion of Christians from Israel, the burning of churches, and for the Jewish people to recognize Christians as their ancient enemy.[2] The Israeli government responded by charging him with incitement of violence; owing to lawfare and the circumstances of the COVID-19 pandemic, the trial was delayed until January 2024, when Gopstein was found guilty.[2] His defense lawyer was [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], the National Security Minister in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s government.[2]

Ben-Gvir and Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] are described as loyal followers of the teachings of [[meir-kahane|Meir Kahane]], the late mentor whose movement Gopstein took over after Kahane was assassinated in New York City in 1990.[2] Badlands commentary situates this network within a broader Israeli oligarchy that includes [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]], and argues that Kahanist hostility to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] dates to Trump's demand, fifteen days into his first term, that Netanyahu halt illegal settlement construction in the West Bank — a practice sponsored and spearheaded by Kahane's followers.[2]
