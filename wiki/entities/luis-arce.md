# Luis Arce

Luis Arce is the President of Bolivia, who in August 2025 accused Washington of using the war on drugs as cover for seeking political and economic domination of Latin America.[1]

## Criticism of the US drug war

Speaking on a Wednesday via video link at the 13th Extraordinary Summit of the Bolivarian Alliance for the Peoples of Our America, Arce strongly criticized the U.S. military deployment to the southern Caribbean, calling it an attempt to control the region rather than a genuine effort to combat narcotics trafficking.[1] The United States had sent air and naval forces to the southern Caribbean Sea earlier that month in what the [[white-house|White House]] described as part of a broader campaign against drug trafficking groups operating in Latin America, particularly those linked to [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

"We know that behind this failed international war on drugs lies the real objective to geopolitically control Latin America for its natural resources and to dismantle organized peoples, so that we cannot follow our own sovereign path," Arce said.[1] He also called on the U.S. government to address the root causes of drug trafficking at home, urging it to curb domestic demand for narcotics and to dismantle the arms industry and what he called the shallow culture sustaining the drug trade.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] responded to Arce's remarks by arguing that "the Trump–Maduro kayfabe is starting to make more and more sense," reading the Caribbean deployment as part of an operation against the cartels rather than against [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] personally.[1]

## 2024 coup attempt

On Wednesday, June 26, 2024, Arce, then 60, confronted the leader of a short-lived military assault on the Bolivian presidential palace in La Paz.[2] Armoured vehicles circled the Plaza Murillo before troops marched on the palace and a small tank rammed the gates of the neoclassical Palacio Quemado; disgruntled former army chief [[juan-jose-zuniga|Juan José Zúñiga]] then faced off against Arce inside.[2] Flanked by cabinet ministers and clasping a ceremonial baton symbolizing his rank as head of state, Arce ordered Zúñiga to stand down, telling him, "I am your captain … withdraw all of your troops right now, general," and the general left through the same broken door and departed in a bulletproof army vehicle.[2] Arce was subsequently accused of plotting the [[2024-bolivian-coup-attempt|coup]] against himself to boost his popularity, an allegation Badlands Media cited as an example of regimes staging attacks on themselves for sympathy.[2]
