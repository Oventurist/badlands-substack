# Ruslan Kravchenko

**Ruslan Kravchenko** is the Prosecutor General of Ukraine, whose public criticism of the country's anti-corruption apparatus preceded a major development in the [[privatbank|PrivatBank]] embezzlement case.[1]

## Criticism of NABU

In July 2025, Kravchenko publicly criticized the [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine]] (NABU) for the slow pace of progress in cases related to [[ihor-kolomoisky|Ihor Kolomoiskyi]].[1] One day later, on July 23, 2025, NABU announced it had completed its investigation into Kolomoiskyi over the misappropriation of UAH 9.2 billion from PrivatBank.[1] The sequence unfolded amid President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]]'s move to place NABU and the [[specialized-anti-corruption-prosecutors-office|Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office]] under executive oversight.[1]
