# Dana White

Dana White is the president of the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]] (UFC) and a longtime associate of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

In April 2023, Trump attended [[ufc-287|UFC 287]] at the Kaseya Center in Miami, where a photo circulated on social media showing him seated ringside next to White.[1] Coverage noted that Trump and White "share a close relationship" built over many years.[1] Trump's appearance at the event, alongside White and in support of fighter [[jorge-masvidal|Jorge Masvidal]], drew a loud positive reaction from the largely Hispanic Miami crowd.[1]

Commentary from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the ringside appearance as an example of the continued and growing reach of the [[america-first-movement|America First movement]] in ordinary American public settings.[1]

## UFC 295 (November 2023)

On November 11, 2023, White was again part of the group accompanying Trump into Madison Square Garden for [[ufc|UFC]] 295, alongside [[kid-rock|Kid Rock]], [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] and [[donald-trump-jr|Don Jr.]], as the former president took ringside seats to a loud ovation moments before the pay-per-view began.[2] Burning Bright placed the White–Trump relationship in a longer arc, noting that [[john-mccain|John McCain]] had pressured New York State into a campaign against the promotion in the early 2000s and that Trump had offered the UFC a free venue and transportation to save a last-minute event whose failure could have bankrupted the company and the emerging sport.[2]

## Meta board appointment (January 2025)

On January 6, 2025 it was reported that White, a close ally of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], had joined the board of directors of [[meta-platforms|Meta]], weeks after [[mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] "bent the knee" at Mar-a-Lago.[3] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the appointment alongside the [[fbi|FBI]]'s release of Meta smart-glasses footage from the [[new-orleans-bourbon-street-attack|New Orleans attacker]] while asking where the line falls between a "golden age" and a "brave new world" for emerging tech.[3]

## WWE–UFC merger

When [[endeavor|Endeavor Group]] announced in April 2023 that it would merge the [[ufc|UFC]] with [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] into a new publicly traded company, White was to remain president of UFC.[4] The merged company was to be led by chief executive [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]], with [[vince-mcmahon|Vince McMahon]] as executive chairman and [[nick-khan|Nick Khan]] as president of the wrestling business.[4]

## UFC 309 with Trump

White led [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] into Madison Square Garden at [[ufc-309|UFC 309]] in November 2024, where the president-elect received what press accounts described as a hero's welcome from a raucous crowd.[5] Bulwark reporter [[marc-caputo|Marc Caputo]] compared the scene to ancient Rome, calling Trump "a conquering, Republican Caesar" entering the Colosseum with "his political gladiators."[5]

## Brokering the end of the Bud Light boycott

A [[new-york-times|New York Times]] feature published in April 2024 described White's "diplomatic ambitions" to un-cancel [[bud-light|Bud Light]].[6] Having completed a reported nine-figure deal in the autumn of 2023 making Bud Light the official beer sponsor of the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]] — a relationship first established more than fifteen years earlier — White found himself accused of selling out by much of the political right and much of the UFC's own audience, which had spent months boycotting the brand over a promotion featuring a transgender influencer.[6]

Publicly, White defended [[anheuser-busch|Anheuser-Busch]] in interviews with [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]], [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] and [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], arguing that the company's desire to be in business with him proved the backlash had worked.[6] Privately he cast the sponsorship as a matter of principle — "We don't cancel people," he told one confidant — and helped hasten a détente, hosting a "great conversation" between [[kid-rock|Kid Rock]] and Anheuser-Busch's chief executive in his green room during a fight night at Madison Square Garden in November 2023.[6] When [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] remained a holdout still attacking the company in February 2024, White phoned him; days later Trump publicly suggested Anheuser-Busch deserved "a second chance," echoing White's talking points.[6] The Times profile characterised White as the arena-filling, Trump-loving, perpetually smirking public face of a multi-billion-dollar sport.[6]
