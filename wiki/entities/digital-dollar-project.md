# Digital Dollar Project

The **Digital Dollar Project** is a nonprofit organization founded by [[j-christopher-giancarlo|J. Christopher Giancarlo]] to "advance exploration of a United States Central Bank Digital Currency."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) It is discussed in the Badlands Media article "Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?" as institutional evidence that the American [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDC]] effort was driven by figures associated with the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration rather than by the [[cabal|cabal]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

## Purpose and framing

Giancarlo has said the project was launched because a free society "has to speak up" in the contest over what values will be encoded in the future of money — whether that money is non-sovereign, like Diem, or sovereign, in the form of a digital dollar.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) The values he identified as at stake are free enterprise, free economic activity, individual privacy, and freedom from government censorship of lawful activity.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)

CNBC reporting cited in the article notes that Giancarlo was lobbying Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]] on crypto issues before he began spearheading the Digital Dollar Project.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom) [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that the project's existence, combined with [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner's]] private advocacy, shows that a digital dollar is inevitable and that the real question is what constitutional protections it will carry.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom)
