# Digital Dollar Project

The Digital Dollar Project is a nonprofit organization founded by [[j-christopher-giancarlo|J. Christopher Giancarlo]], former chairman of the [[commodity-futures-trading-commission|Commodity Futures Trading Commission]], to "advance exploration of a United States Central Bank Digital Currency." It is described in these terms, quoting Wikipedia, in [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s article [[are-cbdcs-the-path-to-fear-or-freedom|"Are CBDCs The Path To Fear, or Freedom?"]].

The article treats the project as the public vehicle for the American position in what Giancarlo called "the fight for the future of money." In a Forbes interview quoted in the piece, Giancarlo explained that the project was launched because a free society must have a say in the values encoded into digital money, whether non-sovereign money such as Diem or sovereign money in the form of a digital dollar. The values he enumerated — free enterprise, free economic activity, individual privacy, and freedom from government censorship of lawful activity — are presented by Lange as the constitutional protections that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is said to be defending behind the scenes in the design of a [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDC]].

Giancarlo's work through the project is placed in the article alongside [[jared-kushner]]'s private advocacy to Treasury Secretary [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]] as the two prongs of an American digital dollar effort said to predate the wider global rush.
