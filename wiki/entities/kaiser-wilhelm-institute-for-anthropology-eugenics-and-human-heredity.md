# Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Anthropology, Eugenics, and Human Heredity

The **Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Anthropology, Eugenics, and Human Heredity** was the second of the two German institutes funded with [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] money, alongside the [[kaiser-wilhelm-institute-for-psychiatry|Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Psychiatry]].[1]

Both were run for the Rockefellers by [[ernst-rudin|Ernst Rudin]], with [[otmar-verschuer|Otmar Verschuer]] and [[franz-kallmann|Franz J. Kallmann]] as his protégés and assistants.[1] Badlands Media treats the institutes as the point at which American philanthropic funding, the [[eugenics]] movement and the Nazi state converged: Rudin's staff, within [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]]'s "Task Force of Heredity Experts," crafted the German sterilization law implemented in July 1933 and reprinted with [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]]'s signature in the American journal *Eugenical News* that September.[1]
