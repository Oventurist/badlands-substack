# Georgia Senate

The Georgia Senate is the upper chamber of the Georgia General Assembly. In January 2024 the Republican-led chamber acted to open a legislative investigation into the Fulton County prosecutor pursuing the state's election interference case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Resolution to Investigate Fani Willis

On Friday, January 26, 2024, the Senate approved a resolution establishing a committee to investigate Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]], who the previous year had brought an election interference case against Trump and eighteen co-defendants.[1] The measure passed 30-19 along party lines, permitting a special committee to investigate Willis amid misconduct allegations.[1]

The resolution stated that the alleged misconduct "includes the ongoing expenditure of significant public funds for the purpose of hiring a special assistant district attorney with whom District Attorney Willis had, and may yet have, an ongoing romantic relationship."[1] The panel was to consist of no more than nine members, including at least three Democrats.[1]

The item appeared as the designated "bonus item" in the January 29, 2024 Badlands News Brief, presented without accompanying commentary.[1]
