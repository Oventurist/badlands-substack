# Battalion 3-16

Battalion 3-16 was a Honduran army unit responsible for carrying out political assassinations and the torture of suspected political opponents of the government during the 1980s.[1] Its members received training and support from the United States [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]], both inside Honduras and at U.S. military bases, and at least 19 members were graduates of the [[school-of-the-americas|School of the Americas]].[1] The CIA is described as having had a strong role in establishing, training, equipping, and financing the battalion.[1]

The unit operated under General Humberto Ragalado Hernández, who was trained at the SOA at the same time that he was linked to Colombian drug cartels; the highest-ranking officers of the Honduran death squad were SOA graduates as well.[1]

The source article treats Battalion 3-16 as the mechanism by which the CIA obtained effective control over Honduras, which served as a base for [[nicaraguan-contras|Contra]] rebels supplied by the agency and as a springboard for Contra drug trafficking along the cocaine route running from Colombia to the United States.[1] The author argues this arrangement devastated the country, contributing to the chronic corruption later exemplified by the conviction of Honduran President [[juan-orlando-hernandez|Juan Orlando Hernández]]'s brother on cocaine trafficking charges in 2019 and Hernández's own 2022 arrest for extradition to the United States.[1]
