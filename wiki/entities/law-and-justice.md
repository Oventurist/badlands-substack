# Law and Justice

Law and Justice (Prawo i Sprawiedliwość, PiS) is a Polish national-conservative political party. As of January 2025 it sat in opposition and was contesting Poland's presidential election with candidate [[karol-nawrocki|Karol Nawrocki]].[1]

## Accusations against Zelensky

In January 2025, politicians from PiS accused Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] of interfering in Poland's upcoming presidential election.[1] The claim followed remarks Zelensky made during a visit to Poland responding to Nawrocki's statement that Kyiv could have no future in the EU or NATO until Ukraine takes responsibility for the [[volhynia-massacres|Volhynia massacres]].[1] Zelensky suggested that without EU or NATO membership and security guarantees for Ukraine, "Pan Nawrocki should start training, because it may turn out that he will have to take up arms" — comments that sparked outrage in Poland.[1] The episode was carried by Badlands Media as a bonus item under the framing that Zelensky stood accused of election interference in a NATO nation.[1]
