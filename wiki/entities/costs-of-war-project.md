# Costs of War Project

The Costs of War Project is a research initiative housed at the Watson Institute for International and Public Affairs at [[brown-university|Brown University]].[1] It has been described as the preeminent academic institution studying the costs, casualties, and consequences of the post-9/11 wars.[1]

## "Death Outlives War" (2023)

In May 2023 the project published a report authored by co-director [[stephanie-savell|Stephanie Savell]] estimating that the post-9/11 [[war-on-terror|War on Terror]] may have caused at least 4.5 million deaths in around half a dozen countries.[1] Rather than counting only direct combat deaths, the report focused on indirect deaths — those caused by the destruction of health systems, infrastructure, food supply, and economies — in Afghanistan, Iraq, Libya, Pakistan, Somalia, Syria, and Yemen.[1] The report emphasized that wars often kill far more people indirectly than in direct combat, particularly young children.[1]
