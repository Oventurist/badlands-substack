# Kurt Cobain

Kurt Cobain was the frontman of the American band Nirvana. His 1994 death, officially ruled a suicide, is disputed in Badlands Media commentary as part of a pattern of suspicious deaths among musicians.[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that by 1994 Cobain had finally resolved his chronic stomach problems and was pain-free, drug-free, devoted to his young daughter, and "happier than he had ever been in his life" — and then died by shotgun.[1] Carlson noted that Cobain also had "enough heroin in his body to kill an elephant," and reframed the usual question: rather than asking why he would shoot himself, the better question is how he could have operated a shotgun in that condition.[1]

## Context

The Cobain case is presented alongside the deaths of [[jeff-buckley|Jeff Buckley]], [[chris-cornell|Chris Cornell]], [[chester-bennington|Chester Bennington]], and [[avicii|Avicii]], and compared to deaths among associates of Bill Clinton — including one said to have hanged himself and another said to have shot himself in the head twice.[1] The essay uses these examples to support its claim that the [[cabal|Cabal]] kills entertainers who defect, because unlike politicians they cannot simply be destroyed by character assassination.[1] Badlands Media notes that its articles represent the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
