# Janisse Quiñones

**Janisse Quiñones** is an American utility executive who served as chief executive of the [[los-angeles-department-of-water-and-power|Los Angeles Department of Water and Power]] (LADWP), the municipal utility responsible for the city's water and electric service.[1]

## Appointment

Quiñones was hired in May 2024 at a salary of $750,000 — almost double that of her predecessor — after Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] ordered the city to max out its budget in order to "attract private-sector talent."[1] The compensation package made her one of the highest-paid municipal officials in the United States and drew scrutiny once questions arose about the utility's readiness for wildfire season.[1]

## Palisades Fire controversy

Following the January 2025 [[palisades-fire|Palisades Fire]], Los Angeles Fire Department insiders told DailyMail.com that Quiñones was responsible for a raft of failures that contributed to the disaster, alleging she had left a nearby reservoir disconnected and fire hydrants broken for months, which led to firefighters running out of water while battling the blaze.[1]

## Prior career

Before joining LADWP, Quiñones served as a senior vice president at [[pacific-gas-and-electric|Pacific Gas & Electric]] from 2021 to 2023.[1] That utility's power lines sparked the Dixie Fire in 2021, the second-largest wildfire in California history, and its involvement in the 2018 Camp Fire resulted in a $13.5 billion legal settlement.[1]
