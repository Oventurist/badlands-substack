# Foro Penal

**Foro Penal** is a Venezuelan human rights organization described by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a grantee of the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]].[1]

During the waves of "nonviolent" street protests that swept [[venezuela|Venezuela]] in 2014, 2017, and 2019, NED grantees such as Foro Penal documented "systematic repression" and trained local journalists to livestream the resulting confrontations.[1] DeLarme situates this within a familiar choreography of barricades in middle-class neighborhoods, clashes with National Guard units, and viral footage of tear-gas canisters and Molotov cocktails, with death tolls of 43 in 2014 and more than 120 in 2017.[1] He compares the pattern to NED and [[usaid|USAID]] activity in Ukraine, Serbia, Georgia, and Lebanon.[1]
