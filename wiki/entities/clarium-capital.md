# Clarium Capital

Clarium Capital is the hedge fund founded by [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]].[1] The firm is one of several Thiel-affiliated financial vehicles through which figures later prominent in the U.S. federal health bureaucracy passed.[1]

[[jim-oneill|Jim O'Neill]] — subsequently Deputy Secretary of the [[department-of-health-and-human-services|Department of Health and Human Services]] and Acting Director of the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] — served as a Managing Director of Clarium Capital from 2008 to 2012, immediately after leaving his first tour at HHS, and concurrently ran the Thiel Foundation and co-founded the [[thiel-fellowship|Thiel Fellowship]].[1] Badlands commentary treated the Clarium period as the pivot at which O'Neill moved from career bureaucrat into the intersection of health industries, emerging technology, and high finance.[1]
