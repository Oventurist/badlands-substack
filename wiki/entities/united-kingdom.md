# United Kingdom

The United Kingdom is a European state whose residual overseas holdings and imperial legacy feature recurrently in the Badlands corpus.

In Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, the United Kingdom appears principally as the controlling power over the [[chagos-islands|Chagos Archipelago]], which it separated from [[mauritius|Mauritius]] in 1965 — an act the [[international-court-of-justice|International Court of Justice]] found unlawful in a 2019 advisory opinion. In May 2025 London approved a plan to hand the islands to Mauritius while retaining access to the joint U.K.–U.S. base at [[diego-garcia|Diego Garcia]] under a 99-year lease. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] criticized that agreement as "an act of great stupidity," and the Brief reports discussions of a direct American purchase from Mauritius that would, in the words of [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], leave the British "sidelined and at the mercy of the United States." He characterizes this as part of a broader pattern under Trump of "dismantling the remnants of the old British Empire."

The British firm [[de-la-rue|De La Rue PLC]] also appears in the same edition as the former owner of [[sequoia-voting-systems|Sequoia Voting Systems]], which it sold to [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] in 2005.
