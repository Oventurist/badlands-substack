# Hans Kerrl

**Hans Kerrl** served as Nazi Germany's Minister of Church Affairs, the official responsible for subordinating the German churches to the National Socialist state.[1]

## Statements on Christianity

Kerrl is quoted in Matthew Ehret's "Real Patriots Don't Think Like Hitler - Part 3" rejecting orthodox Christian doctrine outright. Responding to Dr. Zoellner and the Catholic Bishop of Münster, Count Galen, who had tried to make clear to him that Christianity consists in faith in Christ as the son of God, Kerrl replied: "That makes me laugh... No, Christianity is not dependent upon the [Apostles'] Creed... True Christianity is represented by the party, and the German people are now called by the party and especially the Fuehrer to a real Christianity... the Fuehrer is the herald of a new revelation."[1]

He added that the regime were "no theologians, no representatives of the teaching profession in this sense" and put forth no theology, but claimed to "place the great fundamental idea of Christianity in the center of our ideology [Ideenwelt] – the hero and sufferer Christ himself stands in the center."[1] The quotation is sourced in the article to *Fascism, Totalitarianism and Political Religion*, edited by Roger Griffin (Routledge, 2006).[1]

## Role in the church program

Kerrl's position exemplified the Nazi program of turning German churches into extensions of the state, loyal to the surrogate god of the state itself — the institutional counterpart to the doctrinal rewriting carried out through the [[reichs-bible|Reichs Bible]] and the [[german-christians|German Christians]] movement under [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]].[1]
