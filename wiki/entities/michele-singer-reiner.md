# Michele Singer Reiner

Michele Singer Reiner was an American photographer and producer and the wife of filmmaker [[rob-reiner|Rob Reiner]]. She was found stabbed to death alongside her husband at the couple's Brentwood home in Los Angeles on Sunday, December 14, 2025; she was 68 years old and her husband was 78.[1]

## Death

The Los Angeles Police Department confirmed on Monday, December 15, 2025 that both Reiners had been found fatally stabbed the previous afternoon. The bodies were reportedly discovered by the couple's 28-year-old daughter, Romy. LAPD Chief Jim McDonnell described the incident as tragic at a press conference, saying investigators from the department's Robbery-Homicide Division had worked overnight to take a suspect into custody.[1]

The couple's 32-year-old son, [[nick-reiner|Nick Reiner]], was arrested late Sunday and booked on suspicion of murder, a felony charge, early Monday morning. The LAPD told CNN that he was not being held on bail, despite widely circulated reports of a $4 million bond.[1]

## Aftermath and commentary

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] responded to the killings with a social media post attributing the deaths to Rob Reiner's political opposition to him, a claim police investigators had not substantiated while the homicide investigation was ongoing.[1] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted in commentary that the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] had eulogized the Reiners, framing the sequence of events as "seeding" within a broader narrative contest.[1]
