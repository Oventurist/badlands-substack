# Michael Tsarion

**Michael Tsarion** is an author on occult and alternative-history subjects cited in [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series.[1]

The series recommends Tsarion's two-volume work *The Irish Origin of Civilization* to readers who wish to pursue the claim that the surname "Kennedy" harkens back to Scotland and suggests a connection to ancient Druidic bloodlines, which the author says were indeed considered royalty at one point.[1] Tsarion's work is invoked as supporting background for the series' broader argument that the [[kennedy-family|Kennedy]] and [[donald-trump|Trump]] families descend from a Celtic royal lineage rather than from the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s own elite.[1]
