# Advance Youth Athletic Development

Advance Youth Athletic Development was a Federal Child Nutrition Program site in Minnesota operated by [[guhaad-hashi-said|Guhaad Hashi Said]], a former campaign "enforcer" for Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]], between December 2020 and January 2022.[1]

## Fraud scheme

Through the site, Said submitted fraudulent claims for having served more than one million meals to children, though only a small portion were actually provided.[1] Fabricated attendance rosters, meal counts and invoices supported the false claims, yielding more than $2.9 million in federal reimbursements.[1] Said pleaded guilty in August 2025 to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and conspiracy to commit money laundering in connection with the scheme, which the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Minnesota described as part of a staggering wider pattern of pandemic-era food fraud centered on the state's Somali community.[1]
