# Dwight D. Eisenhower

Dwight D. Eisenhower was the 34th president of the United States, remembered in Badlands Media commentary chiefly for his farewell warning about the growth of the American arms establishment.[1]

## Warning about the military-industrial complex

Joe Lange's "The Master Builder" invokes Eisenhower's warning about the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] as an early recognition of the danger the article attributes to [[nato|NATO]], which it characterizes as a corrupt money-laundering operation serving a global cabal.[1] In the article's framing, Eisenhower's warning went unheeded and the alliance became an instrument of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] rather than a purely defensive arrangement.[1]
