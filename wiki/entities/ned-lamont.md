# Ned Lamont

Ned Lamont is the Governor of Connecticut. In late June 2025 he signed into law legislation barring any Connecticut state government entity from acquiring, holding, or investing in digital assets, including Bitcoin.[1]

## Digital asset prohibition

The bill Lamont enacted received unanimous approval in the state legislature and was framed as a measure to address the risks associated with the volatile nature of digital assets and to safeguard the stability of the state's financial reserves.[1] By preventing state entities from participating in digital asset transactions, Connecticut sought to protect public funds from what lawmakers characterized as inherent regulatory and security risks in cryptocurrency markets.[1]

The action placed Connecticut in deliberate contrast with states pursuing the opposite course. Texas had recently approved a $10 million Bitcoin purchase for a state reserve, signaling a willingness to explore potential advantages of cryptocurrencies, while Connecticut emphasized regulatory clarity and conservative management of public money.[1] Commentators in the Badlands News Brief summarized the decision bluntly as Connecticut officially banning the state from creating a Bitcoin reserve or investing in any digital assets.[1]
