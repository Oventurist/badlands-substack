# Mohamed Bazoum

Mohamed Bazoum was the democratically elected president of [[niger|Niger]] who was ousted by a military junta in July 2023 and remained detained by the coup leaders a month later.[1]

## France's position

[[france|France]] consistently acknowledged only Bazoum's authority as the legitimate government of Niger after the coup.[1] French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] framed French policy in Niger as depending on "the courage of President Mohamed Bazoum, the commitment of our diplomats, of our ambassador on the ground who is remaining despite pressure."[1] The junta that deposed Bazoum came under sanctions from Western and regional African powers, exploited popular grievances against former colonial ruler France, and turned to the Russian mercenary group [[wagner-group|Wagner]] for support.[1]

## Overthrow and aftermath

Bazoum was overthrown by his presidential guard in late July 2023 in what became the [[2023-niger-coup|2023 Niger coup]], the seventh military takeover in less than three years in West and Central Africa.[2] The regional bloc [[ecowas|ECOWAS]] threatened force to reinstate him and demanded that the coup plotters "free the democratically elected president of the republic immediately and without any condition," while [[mali|Mali]] and [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] pledged to defend Niger against any intervention.[2] During the coup Bazoum was denounced by opponents as "a French puppet."[2] US National Security Advisor [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] said Washington "strongly condemn[s] any effort to detain or subvert the functioning of Niger's democratically elected government, led by President Bazoum," calling Niger "a critical partner for the United States."[2]

## Aftermath of his ouster

Nearly two weeks after Bazoum was removed from office, [[niger|Niger]] remained under the rule of a military junta, and US Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] publicly assessed that Russia's [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] was attempting to take advantage of the resulting instability, while stating he did not believe Russia or Wagner had instigated the coup.[3] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] relayed the view of a self-described Nigerien citizen who rejected the framing of Bazoum as a hostage of the junta, insisting instead that "he is a puppet" and calling for an end to puppet leadership across Africa.[3] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that Bazoum's deposed government was backed by the [[european-union|European Union]], the [[united-nations|United Nations]] and the [[state-department|State Department]], whereas the coup was backed by Russia and Wagner.[3]

## French recognition after the coup

Even as it wound down its military commitment, France continued to refuse recognition of Niger's military authorities and insisted that Bazoum, toppled in the July 2023 coup, remained the country's only legitimate authority.[4] French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] announced on September 24, 2023 that France would end its military presence in [[niger|Niger]] by year's end, saying Paris was "not there to deal with internal politics and be hostages of putschists."[4]
