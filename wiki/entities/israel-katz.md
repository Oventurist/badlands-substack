# Israel Katz

Israel Katz is the Israeli Defense Minister whose public statements in April 2026 signalled that [[israel|Israel]] was actively preparing to renew its war with [[iran|Iran]] and was awaiting a United States green light before resuming strikes.[1]

## Posture toward Iran (April 2026)

Katz declared that the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] were ready for both defense and offense, with new targets already marked.[1] He stated that Israel sought to eliminate the Khamenei dynasty, destroy Iran's major energy and power facilities, and deliver further "deadly blows" intended to collapse the foundations of the Iranian regime.[1] Israeli leadership anticipated a possible resumption of hostilities by the end of that week while intensifying intelligence efforts and restocking missile inventories.[1]

## Context

Katz's declarations came as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly resisted pressure to set a timeline on his negotiations with Iran, and as Israeli media reported — contrary to Trump's own statements — that the administration had communicated a Sunday deadline to Tel Aviv.[1] Badlands commentary framed the gap between Katz's offensive posture and Trump's insistence that "Israel will have to defend itself" as evidence that Israel might be pushed to fight Iran without direct American assistance.[1]

## Occupation of southern Lebanon (March 2026)

On March 24, 2026 — a day after Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] said Israel's border "must be the Litani" — Katz stated that Israeli forces would occupy territory up to the [[litani-river|Litani River]] and control bridges and a broader "security zone," formalizing an intent to hold a significant portion of southern [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[2] The announcement followed months of [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] presence in Lebanon and the collapse of the ceasefire with [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], which called any occupation south of the Litani an "existential threat."[2] Lebanese authorities reported more than 1,070 deaths and over one million displaced amid the escalation that began after Hezbollah's March 2 missile launches.[2]

## April 2026 demolitions in Lebanon

As Israeli Defense Minister, Katz said in April 2026 that homes allegedly used by [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in [[lebanon|Lebanon]] would be leveled, with Arab News reporting that demolitions were underway that Sunday, citing Israeli state media.[3] The statement accompanied Israel's order for troops to use "full force" against threats in Lebanon despite the temporary truce, and the publication of a new deployment line creating an Israeli-controlled [[forward-defense-zone|Forward Defense Zone]] in the south.[3]

## March 2026: "no time limit" statement

Quoted by The Times of Israel on March 11, 2026, Katz said the campaign against [[iran|Iran]] would proceed "without any time limit, as long as required, until we accomplish all objectives and achieve victory in the campaign."[4] The remark was reported in direct conflict with [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s statement the same day that the war would end "soon" and that there was "practically nothing left" to target, and Badlands writers treated Katz's language as the clearest evidence that [[israel|Israel]] had diverged publicly from the American narrative.[4]

## Threats Against Erdoğan

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], recounting the June 2026 escalation between [[turkey|Turkey]] and [[israel|Israel]], recalled that Katz — then foreign minister and later defense minister — had warned Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] at the United Nations that he could become the next Saddam Hussein, a reference to the 2003 invasion of Iraq and the execution of its leader by US-led forces.[5] The remark was cited as an early marker in the pattern of escalating public threats between Ankara and Jerusalem dating from the October 7 attacks.[5]

## Statement on Omer Neutra (December 2024)

As Israeli foreign minister, Katz said in December 2024 that [[omer-neutra|Omer Maxim Neutra]] — the 21-year-old American-Israeli tank platoon commander long believed to be held hostage in Gaza — "fell in a heroic battle near [[nir-oz|Nir Oz]] on October 7."[6] The remark accompanied the Israeli military's announcement that Neutra had been killed during the [[hamas|Hamas]]-led attack of October 7, 2023, while his body remained held in Gaza.[6]


## Barring of Antonio Guterres (October 2024)

As foreign minister, Katz announced on October 2, 2024 that UN Secretary-General [[antonio-guterres|António Guterres]] was barred from [[israel|Israel]], declaring him persona non grata after Guterres failed to directly condemn Tehran for the 181 ballistic missiles [[iran|Iran]] launched at Israel the previous night, which forced millions of Israelis into shelters.[7] Katz accused the UN chief of kowtowing to "[[hamas|Hamas]], [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], the Houthis, and now Iran — the mothership of global terror," of failing to denounce the October 7 attacks, and of neglecting to designate Hamas a terrorist organization.[7] Guterres had instead written on X condemning "the broadening of the Middle East conflict with escalation after escalation," saying, "This must stop. We absolutely need a ceasefire."[7] Germany, which summoned Iran's envoy over the attack, called Katz's declaration unhelpful, and the move was expected to deepen the rift between Israel and the [[united-nations|United Nations]].[7] See [[october-2024-iranian-missile-attack-on-israel|October 2024 Iranian missile attack on Israel]].[7]

## Gaza "humanitarian city" proposal

In July 2025 Katz announced that Israel was preparing to establish a so-called "humanitarian city" on the ruins of Rafah in the southern [[gaza|Gaza Strip]], into which the enclave's entire population was to be moved — a plan critics promptly branded an internment camp and warned would produce widespread human rights abuse.[8] Katz told reporters the site would initially accommodate some 600,000 Palestinians, primarily displaced persons living in the coastal Mawasi area northwest of Rafah, with all of the estimated 2.2 million Gazans eventually placed inside.[8] He said the zone would be secured by the Israeli military from a distance and run by unspecified international organizations, that Palestinians would be screened before entry to ensure no [[hamas|Hamas]] operatives slipped in, and that those admitted would not be allowed to return to other parts of Gaza.[8] Katz further admitted that the scheme was ultimately designed to displace the entire Gaza population and encourage it to "voluntarily emigrate" from the enclave.[8] See [[humanitarian-city-plan|Humanitarian City Plan]] for fuller treatment.[8]

## Call to expel Turkey from NATO

In late July 2024, Israeli Foreign Minister Katz demanded that [[nato|NATO]] expel Turkey from the alliance after Turkish President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] threatened to invade Israel.[9] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read Erdoğan's threats — issued repeatedly since October 7 while Turkey continued supplying Israel with fuel and resources — as political posturing, while noting that Russia and China had drawn Erdoğan toward BRICS and Shanghai Cooperation Organization meetings.[9]

The same commentary counted Katz among the radical ministers, alongside [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] and [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]], who hold three of six seats in [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s security cabinet and can dissolve the government by resigning — leverage they have used to threaten Netanyahu against ending the war.[9]

## Channel 12 interview on the Iran war (June 2025)

### Announcement of the June 2025 strikes on Iran

Katz was the first Israeli official to announce the strikes launched against [[iran|Iran]] in the early hours of June 13, 2025, saying in a statement that he had initiated "a special state of emergency" in Israel following a "preemptive strike against Iran."[13] He warned of potential drone and missile attacks against Israel and its civilians in retaliation.[13] The strikes killed [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] commander [[hossein-salami|Hossein Salami]] and other senior Iranian figures; see [[june-2025-israeli-strikes-on-iran|the June 2025 Israeli strikes on Iran]].[13]

In an interview with Israel's Channel 12 reported on June 26, 2025, Katz said Israel had launched its war against [[iran|Iran]] without knowing whether U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would participate in the offensive, though Israel was confident the United States would assist in defending the country.[10] "In defense, we knew they [the US] were with us — and they did an amazing job," he said.[10] Katz also admitted Israel did not know the location of all of Iran's enriched uranium while claiming its strikes had destroyed Tehran's enrichment capabilities, adding that "the uranium itself, the material, was not a target for attack."[10] He reiterated that Israel would have assassinated Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] "if he had been in our sights."[10]

Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued Katz was "saying the quiet part out loud," and that rather than shielding Netanyahu and Trump he was confirming that the timing of the attack was political and that Israel had acted alone rather than as part of the coordinated effort Netanyahu described.[10] Contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] summarized the remarks with the observation that "Katz is off the reservation."[10]

## Gaza annexation threat (March 2025)

In March 2025, as Israel's ground offensive in Gaza entered a fourth day with forces expanding operations in the north and south of the Strip, Katz as Defence Minister threatened to seize land in the coastal enclave.[11] He said Israeli forces would "intensify" the campaign against [[hamas|Hamas]] and use "all military and civilian pressure, including evacuation of the Gaza population south and implementing United States President [Donald] Trump's voluntary migration plan for Gaza residents."[11] Katz instructed the army to "seize additional areas in Gaza, evacuate the population and expand security zones around Gaza to protect Israeli communities and [Israeli army] soldiers," and warned that "the more Hamas persists in its refusal to release the hostages, the more territory it will lose, which will be annexed to Israel."[11]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] placed the annexation threat against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s approval of Egypt's plan to rebuild Gaza — which would allow Palestinians to remain in the Strip under an Arab-backed Palestinian committee — and argued that the timing made it increasingly obvious that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and Trump were not working in tandem.[11]

## June 2024 response to Iran's "obliterating war" threat

In late June 2024, as Foreign Minister, Katz responded on X to [[iran|Iran]]'s threat of an "obliterating war" should [[israel|Israel]] launch a full-scale offensive against [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[12] He wrote: "My response to Iran is clear: 1. If Hezbollah does not cease its fire and withdraw from southern Lebanon, we will act against it with full force until security is restored and residents can return to their homes. 2. A regime that threatens destruction deserves to be destroyed."[12] The exchange came amid daily cross-border fire and Israeli strikes on Hezbollah targets in southern Lebanon.[12]

## Forced screening of October 7 footage for detained activists

In June 2025, Katz ordered the military to screen a video showing atrocities committed on October 7, 2023, to detained activists who had attempted to challenge Israel's blockade on Gaza, according to a statement from his office.[14] The 43-minute video, produced by the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] spokesperson's office, contained uncensored footage of people being massacred and bodies mutilated during the onslaught, much of it taken from terrorists' bodycams.[14] "It's appropriate that Greta the antisemite and her Hamas-supporting friends should see exactly who is the terror group [[hamas|Hamas]] that they support and act on behalf of, what atrocious acts they carried out on women, the elderly and kids, and who Israel is fighting for its defense against," Katz said in the statement, referring to Swedish climate activist [[greta-thunberg|Greta Thunberg]], the most famous of the 12 activists aboard the boat.[14] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] quipped that a "countdown" had begun until Thunberg went "full Zionist."[14]

## "Sterile zone" in southern Syria (December 2024)

In December 2024, as Israeli forces advanced following the collapse of the [[bashar-al-assad|Assad]] government, Defense Minister Katz said he intended to establish a "sterile zone" in southern Syria in order to prevent any "terrorist threat."[15] The announcement accompanied Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s statement that he had ordered the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] to secure territory in the [[golan-heights|Golan Heights]] and that the occupation would be permanent.[15] Badlands commentary ridiculed the stated rationale by asking whether the terrorists in question were the Al Qaeda-linked fighters used to overthrow Assad, and predicted that the "sterile zone" would ultimately extend all the way to the Euphrates River.[15]

## Threat to strike Iran amid US nuclear talks (April 2025)

On Monday, 28 April 2025, with reporting indicating that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was moving ever closer to a new nuclear deal with [[iran|Iran]], Defense Minister Katz re-issued a threat that [[israel|Israel]] remained ready to attack Tehran's nuclear program.[16] "The central mission is to prevent Iran from getting a nuclear weapon," Katz told the IDF high command. "Israel will not allow Iran to get a nuclear weapon, and we [are] prepared — of course, in a deep dialogue with the US."[16] He added that in Israel's broader wars and in its fight against Iran, "We know we have someone to count on when, in the end, we will be required to undertake decisions. We know that there is someone to execute them: and this is true in any place, including with Iran. If there is a need to act [attack], there is someone who will do it."[16]

Despite Katz's threat, top Israeli officials told The Jerusalem Post on an ongoing basis for weeks that there was significant concern Trump would block Israel from acting so that he could cut what they regarded as a mediocre nuclear deal with the Islamic Republic.[16] A separate Israeli official told the Post that Israel estimated the US–Iran negotiations were likely to conclude in an agreement, while noting Israel did not yet know whether it would align with its demand for the complete dismantling of uranium enrichment facilities or resemble the earlier deal Israel considers a "bad agreement."[16]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] contrasted Katz's threat with the apparently productive talks between [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and the Iranians, and recalled that Katz — then foreign minister — had openly threatened Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan at the UN the previous year by promising to do to him what was done to Saddam Hussein.[16] He described "a growing restlessness among the more radical sects of the Zionist movement," who he said had convinced themselves that war with Iran was a Trump campaign promise, and reported seeing pro-Israel influencers express contempt toward Trump for making peace with Iran.[16] He predicted the [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] regime would attempt to subvert Trump's Iran nuclear deal, which he argued would set the stage for a forthcoming Trump–[[vladimir-putin|Putin]] meeting in Riyadh.[16]


## Termination of the UNRWA agreement (November 2024)

As foreign minister, Katz announced on Monday, November 4, 2024 that Israel had formally notified the [[united-nations|United Nations]] it was terminating the 1967 agreement under which [[unrwa|UNRWA]] operated in Gaza and the West Bank.[17] He said in a statement that "UNRWA – the organization whose employees participated in the October 7 massacre and many of whose employees are Hamas operatives – is part of the problem in the Gaza Strip and not part of the solution."[17] Katz added that the UN had been presented with "endless evidence about Hamas operatives working at UNRWA and about the use of UNRWA facilities for the terror purposes and nothing was done about it."[17] The announcement followed Knesset legislation passed the previous week banning the agency's work in Israel.[17]

## February 2024 warning to Lebanon

As Israel expanded its air campaign into Lebanon in February 2024, Katz — then serving as Israeli Foreign Minister — repeated a warning to the Lebanese government that Lebanon would "pay a heavy price" if [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] were not removed from the vicinity of the border.[18] Analysts noted that Lebanon's army was widely regarded as lacking the capability to expel the Iran-backed Shia paramilitary group, and that any attempt to do so would likely trigger another civil war.[18] The warning coincided with [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] strikes near Sidon, some 45 to 60 kilometers inside Lebanon, confirmed by spokesman [[daniel-hagari|Daniel Hagari]].[18]

## Appointment as Defense Minister (November 2024)

On November 5, 2024, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] fired Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] and named Katz, then foreign minister, as his replacement; minister without portfolio [[gideon-saar|Gideon Sa'ar]] took over the foreign ministry.[19] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the choice as striking because Netanyahu elevated one of the religious hardliners already serving in his Security Cabinet — and specifically the one who had already threatened foreign leaders with implied assassination.[19]

The commentary argued that Sa'ar had been the expected pick and would have been more acceptable to [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] generals, since he was thought more willing to enforce the Israeli Supreme Court's nullification of the [[haredi-judaism|Haredi]] draft exemption; Katz was judged far less likely to compel Haredi service even though the generals said they were desperate for soldiers, raising questions about whether the general staff would follow him.[19]

Katz's record of aggressive posturing toward a range of countries, including Syria — which Israel was reportedly bombing, with reports of Israeli special forces operating inside the country — was read as consistent with his rhetoric from January of that year, when he said Israel was already fighting World War III.[19] Given simultaneous conflicts with Hezbollah, Iran and Syria, all aligned with Moscow, the Badlands commentary suggested a direct Israel–Russia kinetic confrontation was not difficult to imagine.[19]
