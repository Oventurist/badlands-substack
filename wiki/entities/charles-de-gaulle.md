# Charles de Gaulle

**Charles de Gaulle** was the French president who, according to commentary published in the Badlands News Brief, discovered in 1958 the illegal partnership under which France assisted [[israel|Israel]] in developing the [[negev-nuclear-research-center|Negev Nuclear Research Center]], and sought to stop it.[1]

As discussed by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] and Matt Ehret on the Badlands program [[breaking-history|Breaking History]], de Gaulle is particularly famous for evading more than 30 assassination attempts on his life, most of them during the 1960s and most allegedly carried out by the [[cia|CIA]] and the [[mossad|Mossad]].[1] His effort to halt the Franco-Israeli nuclear collaboration is presented as a parallel to President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]]'s 1963 attempt to force transparency on [[david-ben-gurion|David Ben-Gurion]] regarding Israel's nuclear ambitions.[1]
