# Patreon

**Patreon** is a membership and fundraising platform named by Badlands Media among the services that have frozen the funds of reader-supported news outlets under pressure from groups such as the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]].[1]

The article groups Patreon with [[gofundme|GoFundMe]] and other fundraising platforms as instruments of a financial-pressure strategy that extends beyond advertiser boycotts and character assassination, citing GoFundMe's August 2023 freeze on [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]]'s fundraiser as the most recent example at the time of writing.[1] The pattern is presented as an extension of the same logic behind the ADL's 2021 partnership with [[paypal|PayPal]] to investigate financial flows associated with "extremist" movements.[1]
