# Mahamadou Sana

Mahamadou Sana is the Security Minister of [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]].[1] In October 2025 he publicly announced that the government had dismantled what it described as an espionage network operating under the cover of a foreign non-governmental organization.[1]

## October 2025 espionage announcement

At a press briefing on Tuesday, October 7, 2025, Sana told reporters that the activities of the Netherlands-based [[international-ngo-safety-organisation|International NGO Safety Organisation]] had been monitored for some time.[1] He said that thanks to the "contribution of citizens," authorities were able to determine that the NGO "constituted an espionage network in Burkina Faso."[1]

Sana added that despite the organization's suspension on July 31, 2025 for allegedly collecting sensitive data without authorization, "some officials continued to carry out clandestine or disguised activities such as gathering information and holding meetings in person or online."[1] Eight workers were arrested, including two French citizens, one Czech national, one Malian national and four Burkinabè citizens.[1]
