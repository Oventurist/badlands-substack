# Stephen Douglas

**Stephen Douglas** was a pro-slavery United States senator and the principal political rival of [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]] in Illinois, described by Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as a representative of the slavocracy and as Lincoln's nemesis.[1]

Douglas, referred to in the period as "Judge Douglas," debated Lincoln in 1858. In one of those debates Lincoln framed the contest in universal terms, stating that the issue would continue in the country long after "these poor tongues of Judge Douglas and myself shall be silent," because it was "the eternal struggle between these two principles – right and wrong – throughout the world," in which "the one is the common right of humanity and the other the divine right of kings."[1]

Ehret uses the exchange to argue that Lincoln understood systems of slavery and hereditary power structures internationally as inextricably connected, and that the means to break both systems of empire and slavery were located in the [[american-system-of-political-economy|American System]] of political economy.[1]
