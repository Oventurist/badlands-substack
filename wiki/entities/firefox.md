# Firefox

Firefox is the open-source web browser developed by [[mozilla|Mozilla]]. It has long been lauded as a leading alternative to Google's Chrome and Apple's Safari, and retains a devoted following among open-source enthusiasts.[1]

## AI features controversy (December 2025)

On December 16, 2025, Mozilla's newly appointed chief executive [[anthony-enzor-demeo|Anthony Enzor-DeMeo]] announced in a blog post that Firefox would become a "modern AI browser and support a portfolio of new and trusted software additions."[1]

The announcement provoked a swift backlash from users who valued Firefox precisely as a refuge from AI integration. "I switched back to Firefox late last year BECAUSE it was the last AI-free browser," one user wrote; another said they had "never seen a company so astoundingly out of touch with the people who want to use its software."[1] The reaction was part of a broader revolt against AI being pushed into every corner of the computing experience.[1]

Mozilla responded on Mastodon by clarifying that "Firefox will have an option to completely disable all AI features," a concession widely characterized as a "kill switch" for the browser's AI functionality.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described Firefox as "a cult classic among open source maxis" and argued the episode illustrated the accountability function performed by the open-source community's watchdogs.[1]
