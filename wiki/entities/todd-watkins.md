# Todd Watkins

Todd Watkins is a US military veteran, a retired United States Border Patrol Chief, and a grassroots freedom activist in Colorado.[1] He serves as vice chairman of the El Paso County GOP, described as the largest county party organization in Colorado and the only county in the nation home to five military commands.[1] The Badlands Media account of his activism also notes, in passing, that he was an altar boy.[1]

## August 2023 letter on January 6 detainees

On August 21, 2023, Watkins sent a letter to the [[colorado-republican-party|Colorado Republican Party]] urging "assistance in contacting legislators … as regards the treatment and handling of Americans who have been arrested and/or charged with crimes related to the January 6, 2021 incident at the US Capitol."[1] More than 200 Coloradans, many of them Republican officials, signed on in support of the letter.[1]

The letter asserted that January 6 defendants had been jailed pending charges and detained without bond on misdemeanor allegations, had suffered physical abuse and injury and been singled out for their political beliefs, had been denied medical treatment for pre-existing conditions and for injuries sustained in custody, had been denied contact with counsel, and had suffered violations of their rights under the Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, Eighth and Fourteenth Amendments to the [[united-states-constitution|US Constitution]].[1]

## Response from Ken Buck

On September 1, 2023, Congressman [[ken-buck|Ken Buck]] (R-CO-04) replied with a letter claiming that Watkins "makes a number of factually incorrect claims, and then proceeds to overstate the threat to our Republic based on these false claims," and citing AP News, The Guardian, The New York Times, CBS News, Just Security and WUSA9 as authoritative sourcing.[1] Buck concluded by calling it "sad that Republican leaders are misdirecting the energy and resources of Republican activists" while the country faced crises stemming from the Biden administration's policies.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], herself a Buck constituent, published a point-by-point rebuttal of Buck's five central assertions with reporting from J6 investigative journalist [[mel-hawley|Mel Hawley]], concluding that Buck's response added insult to injury for the Coloradans who had signed Watkins' letter.[1]
