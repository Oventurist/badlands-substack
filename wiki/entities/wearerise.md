# WeAreRise

WeAreRise is a host in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective.[1]

## Badlands Media role

WeAreRise co-hosts [[the-bunker|The Bunker]] with [[trureporting|TruReporting]], a Saturday 7:30pm ET program added to the Badlands lineup in the January 16–22, 2023 schedule.[1] The show focuses on the geopolitical issues of the day.[1]
