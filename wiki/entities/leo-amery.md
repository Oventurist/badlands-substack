# Leo Amery

Leopold Charles Maurice Stennett "Leo" Amery was a British imperial statesman and co-author, with [[arthur-balfour]], [[alfred-milner|Lord Milner]] and Walter Rothschild, of the 1917 Balfour Declaration.[1] See [[balfour-declaration]].

## Palestine and Jabotinsky

Amery was a close collaborator of the pro-fascist Zionist leader [[zeev-jabotinsky|Vladimir Ze'ev Jabotinsky]] during Amery's management of British Mandate Palestine from 1925 to 1929, and was a co-founder of the Jewish Legion, which Jabotinsky went on to control.[1]

## "A Church of the British Empire"

More than a Zionist, Amery is described as a believer in [[cecil-rhodes|Cecil Rhodes']] vision for "a Church of the British Empire."[1] Of this "peculiar religion" Amery said: "The Empire is not external to any of the British nation. It is something like the Kingdom of Heaven within ourselves."[1] The article notes that "Kingdom of Heaven" was the name of the Templar Kingdom of Jerusalem.[1]

## Family

The article states that while Leo Amery was not openly antisemitic, his son John was a devoted supporter of British Nazism and Adolf Hitler, and his other son [[julian-amery]] worked closely with unreconstructed Nazis after World War Two as part of [[operation-gladio]].[1]
