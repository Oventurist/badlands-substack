# St. Thomas

St. Thomas is the principal island of the [[us-virgin-islands|United States Virgin Islands]] nearest to [[little-st-james|Little St. James]], the private island bought by [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] in 1998.[1]

In Badlands Media commentary by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the island's geography is central to the argument that Epstein's operation relied on discreet transport. Little St. James lies about a mile and a half from St. Thomas; the St. Thomas airport is roughly 10 miles from Epstein's island; and St. Thomas also hosts a submarine base approximately eight miles from it, sheltered by [[water-island|Water Island]].[1]

Carlson argues that [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] obtained a helicopter pilot's license — paid for by Epstein — in order to fly people discreetly from St. Thomas to Little St. James after Epstein's 2008 guilty plea made operating openly in Florida and New York untenable.[1] He acknowledges that a billionaire living on a remote island 10 miles from the nearest airport would plausibly need a helicopter, and finds nothing inherently nefarious in that, while treating Maxwell's separate submarine license as far harder to explain.[1]
