# Lee Gelernt

Lee Gelernt is an [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]] lawyer who litigated against the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s use of the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]] for deportations in March 2025.[1]

## March 2025 Alien Enemies Act hearing

The ACLU sued the administration on Saturday, March 15, 2025, on behalf of migrants at risk of deportation under the wartime statute.[1] At that day's hearing before U.S. District Judge [[james-boasberg|James Boasberg]], Gelernt told the court that two aircraft believed to be carrying Venezuelan deportees had taken off from U.S. territory.[1] Boasberg said Justice Department lawyers had not disputed that claim and ordered that any plane carrying such individuals, whether about to take off or already airborne, be returned to the United States — an order he said needed to be complied with immediately.[1] The administration announced the following day that it had nonetheless deported hundreds of alleged [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] members.[1]
