# Nick Clegg

Nick Clegg is an executive at [[meta-platforms|Meta]], the parent company of [[facebook|Facebook]] and [[instagram|Instagram]], described in Badlands Media coverage as the company official who announced the end of the platform ban on former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Announcement of Trump's reinstatement

In January 2023, Clegg announced on Meta's corporate blog that the company would restore Trump's Facebook and Instagram accounts, which had been suspended following the January 6, 2021 riot at the Capitol.[1] "We will be reinstating Mr. Trump's Facebook and Instagram accounts in the coming weeks," Clegg wrote, adding that the restoration would come "with new guardrails in place to deter repeat offenses."[1] Clegg stated that Trump would return under heightened scrutiny and would face "heightened penalties for repeat offenses" while otherwise being subject to the same rules as other users.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] characterized the reinstatement as "a symbolic admission of wrongful suspension," arguing that it reflected Meta's ability to "read the room" rather than any genuine contrition, and said plainly that he did not believe Clegg "feels one bit of remorse."[1] Getz further predicted that the restrictions and suspension threats attached to the reinstatement would be used "to the fullest and in their typical vindictive manner," concluding that Meta "reeks of desperation."[1] The commentary tied the decision to disclosures from the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] about government involvement in platform moderation, suggesting Meta was attempting to get ahead of similar revelations about its own conduct.[1]
