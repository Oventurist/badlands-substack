# National Association of State Election Directors

The National Association of State Election Directors (NASED) is a membership association of the non-elected state officials who administer elections day to day. Badlands Media's election-integrity commentary groups it with the [[national-association-of-secretaries-of-state|National Association of Secretaries of State]] and county clerk associations as bodies that, in its critics' view, coordinate and constrain election officials from outside the formal structures of government.[1]

## Badlands criticism

In her March 23, 2023 article "Rooting Out Election Corruption," [[holly-at-altitude|Holly at Altitude]] described a range of associations that "gather election officials in order to manage and control elected and non-elected election officials," citing county clerk associations, NASS, and NASED.[1] She wrote that tax dollars are often used to pay membership fees to these "quasi-governmental NGOs" and called on citizens to stop local and state election officials from spending public money on such memberships.[1]

The recommendation appeared under the article's proposal to hold election officials accountable, which also urged legal challenges to dereliction of duty and maladministration and noted that local officials are caught between questionable election laws they cannot control and the duties mandated by their oath of office.[1]
