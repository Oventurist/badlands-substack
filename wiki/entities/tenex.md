# Tenex

Tenex is the commercial arm of the Russian state nuclear corporation [[rosatom|Rosatom]] responsible for uranium sales, and the subsidiary headed by [[vadim-mikerin|Vadim Mikerin]] at the time of the FBI's investigation into the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]].[1]

In 2009 the FBI found enough evidence to conclude that Mikerin, who ran Tenex, was corrupt and that high-level officials at Rosatom were aware of his bribery scheme.[1] The investigation, opened while [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]] was FBI director, documented bribes, extortion and money laundering by Russian entities seeking U.S. government approval to acquire [[uranium-one|Uranium One]], which at the time reportedly controlled some twenty percent of American uranium assets.[1] In 2010 Mikerin was sent to the United States on a work visa approved by the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration to open Rosatom's new American arm, Tenam.[1]

Badlands Media presents the continuation of the Uranium One approval despite the ongoing criminal probe of Tenex management as evidence that the bribes were "going to all the right people."[1]
