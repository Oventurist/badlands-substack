# Jacky Rosen

Jacky Rosen is a Democratic U.S. Senator from Nevada and co-chair, with Sen. [[james-lankford|James Lankford]] (R-Okla.), of the Senate's bipartisan antisemitism task force.[1]

## Antisemitism legislation

Rosen and Lankford introduced a bipartisan antisemitism bill in May 2026 that would require the [[department-of-education|Department of Education]] to appoint an antisemitism coordinator, impose new regulations on colleges receiving federal funding — including mandatory non-discrimination policies and grievance processes — boost nonprofit security grants for Jewish communities to $1 billion, create a dedicated grant program for places of worship, and require online platforms to disclose how they moderate and respond to antisemitic content.[1] Rosen described the effort as a coordinated federal response to protect Jewish communities.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the bill instead as an attack on the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] and the Bill of Rights.[1]

## Response to the Randy Fine tweet (February 2026)

In February 2026 Rosen was among the Democratic lawmakers who publicly denounced Republican Congressman [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]] of Florida after he tweeted that if forced to choose between dogs and Muslims the decision would be easy.[2] Alongside Jewish organizations, Rep. [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]] and other public figures, she condemned the remark as vile, Islamophobic and dehumanizing, amid calls for Fine's censure or resignation; Fine stood by the statement.[2]

## 2024 Senate race and the draft registration controversy

During the 2024 cycle Rosen was among the Democratic Party's most vulnerable Senate incumbents.[3] In June 2024, Senate Democrats added language to the annual defense authorization bill requiring women to register for the [[military-draft|military draft]], prompting a backlash from Republicans and social conservatives.[3] Conservatives led by Senator [[roger-wicker|Roger Wicker]] (R-Miss.) were expected to move to strip the provision, a vote described as politically difficult for Rosen and for fellow at-risk Democrat [[jon-tester|Jon Tester]] of Montana.[3]

Her Republican challenger [[sam-brown|Sam Brown]], an Army veteran severely burned by an improvised explosive device, made the issue central to his campaign, posting a video on X in which he pointed to the scarring on his face and wrote: "Look at my face. This is the high cost of war ... forcing America's daughters to register for the draft is UNACCEPTABLE. Shame on Jacky Rosen."[3]
