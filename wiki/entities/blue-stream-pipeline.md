# Blue Stream Pipeline

The Blue Stream pipeline is a natural gas pipeline that transports Russian gas to [[turkey|Türkiye]] across the Black Sea, and is described as one of [[russia|Russia]]'s key gas export routes to the country.[1] It is served by the Krasnodarskaya compressor station, operated by [[gazprom|Gazprom]].[1]

## July 2026 drone attack

In July 2026, Gazprom said Ukrainian drones targeted the Krasnodarskaya compressor station serving Blue Stream.[1] The company reported minor damage to a building at the facility, with gas deliveries uninterrupted thanks to prompt repairs and backup measures.[1] [[ukraine|Ukraine]] had not publicly commented on the strike at the time of reporting, while Kremlin spokesman [[dmitry-peskov|Dmitry Peskov]] condemned it as a threat to critical international energy infrastructure and said Russia expected affected countries, including Türkiye, to respond.[1]

## Relationship to TurkStream

Blue Stream is distinct from the [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] pipeline, which also carries Russian gas through Türkiye and onward to several European countries.[1] Badlands commentary urged readers to watch both the TurkStream route across the Black Sea and the island of [[cyprus|Cyprus]] as potential flashpoints in a widening confrontation involving Turkey.[1]
