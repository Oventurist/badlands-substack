# Gene Hamilton

Gene Hamilton is the Executive Director of [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] (AFL), the conservative legal organization run by former Trump senior adviser [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]].[1]

## Disney shareholder campaign

In March 2024, Hamilton was the public voice of AFL's letter accusing [[disney|The Walt Disney Company]] of misleading shareholders by prioritizing a "woke political and social agenda" over profits and thereby violating its fiduciary duty.[1] "Disney is an iconic American brand–the product of decades of family-focused content infused with American pride that hundreds of millions of Americans have enjoyed for decades," Hamilton said, arguing that Disney's leadership "appears to have abandoned its roots–and most notably, its shareholders–in hopes of placating an insatiable activist movement that aims to radically reshape the Disney brand into something that is completely inconsistent with its history."[1]

Hamilton drew a distinction between private and public ownership: "If Disney were a privately held corporation, it could make whatever foolish decisions it desired if those decisions complied with the law. But it's not."[1] He concluded that Disney's leadership was "gambling with–and losing–shareholder money and appears to be violating federal law in the process."[1]

## Progressive insurance lawsuit (2023)

In August 2023, Hamilton served as counsel for [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] in a suit against the [[progressive-corporation|Progressive Corporation]] over $25,000 grants offered exclusively to black-owned businesses for vehicle purchases, brought on behalf of the white Ohio business owner [[nathan-roberts|Nathan Roberts]].[2] Hamilton said the case formed part of a broader assault against big corporations that inject "racial considerations into every aspect of their business operations, employment practices, and so much more."[2]
