# Eric Johnson

Eric Johnson is the mayor of Dallas, Texas, who announced in September 2023 that he was changing his party affiliation from Democrat to Republican.[1]

## The party switch

Johnson announced the change in a *Wall Street Journal* op-ed in which he argued that while Dallas had thrived, Democratic policies elsewhere had exacerbated crime and homelessness.[1] He wrote that "the future of America's great urban centers depends on the willingness of the nation's mayors to champion law and order and practice fiscal conservatism," and that cities "desperately need the genuine commitment to these principles (as opposed to the inconsistent, poll-driven commitment of many Democrats) that has long been a defining characteristic of the GOP."[1] He summarized his thesis as: "American cities need Republicans — and Republicans need American cities."[1] Johnson indicated he would leave office in 2027 as a Republican.[1]

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], which carried the story, noted its prior reporting that cities governed by Democrat mayors had seen the largest increases in homicide rates over the previous year and registered the highest per-capita homicide rate in the first quarter among 45 cities surveyed.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] observed that Johnson was not the first Democrat to switch parties recently, citing Elliott Pruitt of West Virginia, Tricia Cotham of North Carolina and Jeremy Lacombe of Louisiana as earlier defectors, but called Johnson "the most notable, and most powerful politician to switch sides in recent memory."[1] Fleuret argued that a sitting mayor of Dallas leaving the Democratic Party signaled that members of the party itself now recognize it has "become infected with far left ideologies and wokeism," adding that "a large portion of the population just doesn't want to embrace communism."[1]
