# DC Draino

**DC Draino** is the online handle of a widely followed American conservative social-media commentator active on Twitter, Telegram, and other platforms.[1]

## Trump declassification letter

On the morning of June 7, 2023, DC Draino published a signed letter from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] dated January 19, 2021 — the day before Trump left office — which was reported as declassifying all of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] documents.[1] Coverage by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] described those documents as material demonstrating that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], [[james-comey|James Comey]], [[john-brennan|John Brennan]], [[james-clapper|James Clapper]], the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[cia|CIA]], the [[state-department|State Department]] and what it termed the Deep State had spied on Trump and knew the Trump–Russia collusion allegations were unfounded.[1]

Commenting on the release, DC Draino wrote on Twitter: "Now you know why they raided Mar a Lago. To steal back evidence of their crimes."[1] The remark was quoted approvingly in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], which argued that the letter undercut the narrative surrounding the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago raid]].[1]
