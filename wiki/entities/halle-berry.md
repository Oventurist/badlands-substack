# Halle Berry

**Halle Berry** is an American actress and the founder of the menopausal care company Respin, through which she has campaigned on women's midlife health issues.[1]

## Criticism of Gavin Newsom

Speaking at The New York Times' DealBook Summit in early December 2025, Berry directly called out California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] for vetoing the [[menopause-care-equity-act|Menopause Care Equity Act]] (AB 432) for the second year in a row, a bill she personally backed and whose veto she called an insult to women's health.[1] "At this stage in my life, I have zero f---s left to give," she told the crowd, adding that Newsom "has vetoed our menopause bill, not one, but two years in a row," that he had overlooked women by devaluing "half the population," and that "he probably should not be our next president either."[1] Her remarks reportedly drew gasps from the audience, particularly because Newsom was scheduled to appear at the same summit later that afternoon.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] joked about the composition of the conference and whether Berry might play Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] on screen.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] took the opposite side of the policy dispute, siding with Newsom's veto and arguing that Berry's grievance was not about the availability of menopause treatment but about who pays for it — namely insurance companies — and that the campaign served to prop up "institutional medicine" and a drive toward single-payer health care rather than making treatments affordable or educating women directly.[1]
