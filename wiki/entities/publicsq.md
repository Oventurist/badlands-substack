# PublicSq

**PublicSq** (PublicSquare) is an online marketplace that connects consumers with small businesses said to share their values, described on its own website as "America's Marketplace: Connecting freedom-loving Americans with local community and the businesses that share their values."[1]

## Role in the parallel-economy argument

The platform is cited by Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] in her December 2022 essay "We Are 'The Plan'" as one of several concrete actions readers can take to contribute to what she calls the [[flywheel-of-freedom|Flywheel of Freedom]].[1] Gillespie recommends that small business owners list their businesses on PublicSq, and that readers patronize businesses aligned with their values whether or not those businesses appear on the platform.[1] She frames the company as "doing something about connecting Americans with American-owned businesses," presenting economic realignment as a practical alternative to passive political commentary.[1]
