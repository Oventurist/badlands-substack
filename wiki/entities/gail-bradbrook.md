# Gail Bradbrook

**Gail Bradbrook** (rendered "Gail Bradbrooke" in the source article) is a co-founder of [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]].[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] notes that Bradbrook formerly worked for [[king-charles-iii|King Charles]]' Business in the Community / Responsible Business Network, an organization that has since become an integrated part of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1] Ehret presents this background as evidence that Extinction Rebellion, far from being a grassroots insurgency against establishment power, grew out of what he calls the cynical cesspool of disenfranchised youth energy and billionaire patronage in England.[1]
