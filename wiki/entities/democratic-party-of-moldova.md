# Democratic Party of Moldova

The Democratic Party of Moldova was the pro-EU political vehicle that rose to dominance in [[moldova|Moldova]] following the 2009 [[2009-moldovan-twitter-revolution|"Twitter Revolution"]] and the displacement of the pro-Russian Communist Party.[1] With the support of [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]], the oligarch [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]] took charge of the party from December 2016, moving to consolidate full control immediately after a Washington visit hosted by the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]].[1]

As public awareness of the [[moldovan-billion-dollar-bank-fraud|"theft of the century"]] spread, the Democratic Party machine fell from favour and the pro-Russian Socialist Party returned to power under [[igor-dodon|Igor Dodon]].[1] On June 15, 2019, the party officially announced that its leader had left the country to visit family "for a couple of days" — the beginning of Plahotniuc's six years as a fugitive.[1]
