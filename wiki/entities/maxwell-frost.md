# Maxwell Frost

Maxwell Frost is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Florida who sits on the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]].[1]

## IRS whistleblower hearing (July 2023)

During the committee's July 19, 2023 hearing with [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] whistleblowers [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] and [[joseph-ziegler|Joseph Ziegler]], Frost invoked George Floyd while responding to Republican claims of a two-tiered justice system favoring the Biden family.[1] Critics said that Frost and other Democrats on the panel were unable to offer effective counterarguments to the whistleblowers' testimony and instead delivered emotional diatribes targeting former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
