# Matt Flynn (Defense Official)

Matt Flynn is a former deputy assistant secretary of defense for counternarcotics and global threats.[1]

## Role in the Afghanistan withdrawal review

According to reporting in November 2024, Flynn was helping lead a [[donald-trump|Trump]] transition effort to compile a list of current and former senior U.S. military officers directly involved in the 2021 withdrawal from Afghanistan and to explore whether they could be court-martialed.[1] The effort was framed internally as a review of how the United States first entered the war in Afghanistan and how it ultimately withdrew, and contemplated creating a commission to investigate the withdrawal's decision-making and execution, potentially reaching charges as serious as treason.[1] A person with knowledge of the plan said the transition was "taking it very seriously."[1]
