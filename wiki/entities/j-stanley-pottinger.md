# J. Stanley Pottinger

J. Stanley Pottinger is a former high-ranking U.S. Justice Department official who served as Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights from 1973 to 1977.[1]

## Association with Jeffrey Epstein

After [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] left [[bear-stearns|Bear Stearns]] in 1981, he partnered with Pottinger and the two shared a penthouse office in the early 1980s.[1]

## Arms-embargo advisory role

Pottinger was cited in indictments for advising Iranian arms exporters — including the Hashemi brothers, among them [[cyrus-hashemi|Cyrus Hashemi]] — on evading U.S. embargoes during the same period in which CIA-backed pipelines supplied Tehran.[1] FBI surveillance tapes from 1980 captured discussions of circumventing embargoes using overseas fronts, and federal investigations and contemporaneous reporting indicate Pottinger assisted in circumvention using shell companies and phony invoices, methods aligned with the covert pipelines that supplied Iran to fund the Nicaraguan Contras.[1] Pottinger was never charged, but his involvement in [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]]-era activity underscores the overlap between Epstein's circle and covert arms financing.[1]

## Media treatment

The [[new-york-times|New York Times]]' December 2025 investigation mentioned Pottinger only briefly as Epstein's "early office-sharing partner" and a former Justice Department official, omitting his 1980–1981 advisory role to Iranian arms exporters — a gap criticized by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]].[1]
