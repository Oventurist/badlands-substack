# SITREP

SITREP is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Thursdays at 9pm ET as of January 2023, hosted by [[canncon|CannCon]] and [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]].[1] The program is devoted to the stories behind the stories of American veterans — why they took the oath, their accounts of service, and the sacrifices made on behalf of the nation.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed SITREP on Thursdays at 9pm ET, with CannCon and Alpha Warrior telling the stories behind veterans' service, why they took the oath, and the sacrifices they made.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed SITREP in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]
