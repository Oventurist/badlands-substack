# Mike Lee

Mike Lee is a Republican United States Senator from Utah.[1] In March 2026 he was the sponsor of the SAVE Act, the election-integrity bill that [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] described as "one of the most IMPORTANT & CONSEQUENTIAL pieces of legislation in the history of Congress, and America itself."[1]

## Defense of the SAVE Act

During Senate debate Lee rebutted Democratic objections to the measure.[1] He directly addressed claims by Senator Dick Durbin that the bill would require expensive passports costing $186 and weeks of delay, or would create burdens for married women needing extra documentation.[1]

Lee explained that the SAVE Act includes built-in accommodations, such as free sworn attestations for voters who lack traditional paperwork — for example if documents were lost in a fire, or "their dog ate it" — along with state verification using existing records and reciprocity agreements between states.[1] He stressed that the process "will not cost them a dime" and that "no one will be excluded."[1]

## Ukraine aid opposition (March 2025)

In March 2025 Lee called for the United States to halt financial aid to Kiev in a post on X, prompting [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] to reply that sanctioning Ukraine's top ten oligarchs — "especially the ones with mansions in Monaco" — would stop the conflict immediately and was "the key to the puzzle."[2]

## Opposition to the 2024 border bill

In February 2024 Lee emerged as one of the sharpest Republican critics of the [[bipartisan-border-bill-2024|bipartisan Senate border bill]], calling for Senate GOP leadership to be replaced.[3] He said on Sunday, February 4, that "secrecy, lies, and broken promises by Senate Republican leadership" had produced a historical pro-migration border surrender that was irredeemable, and urged that Minority Leader [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] be replaced.[3]

Writing on X, Lee described the leadership playbook he said McConnell had used for years: "Even while refusing to let us see the bill they claimed to be negotiating on our behalf—for MONTHS—they were never in doubt, insisting we'd be dumb and even unpatriotic NOT to support it."[3] He concluded, "This is a disqualifying betrayal."[3] Without naming McConnell directly in that passage, Lee appeared to question the leader's loyalty, suggesting he was not working on behalf of his own Republican conference.[3]

## April 2025 call to abolish the IRS and the Federal Reserve

In April 2025, amid controversy over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s push for the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] to revoke [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s tax-exempt status, Lee publicly called for abolishing both the IRS and the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[4] Badlands commentator GMONEY highlighted the statement while quipping that "magaloids love to pay taxes to pedophiles."[4]
