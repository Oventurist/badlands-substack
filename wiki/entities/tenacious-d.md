# Tenacious D

Tenacious D is an American comedy rock band composed of actor-musician [[jack-black|Jack Black]] and musician [[kyle-gass|Kyle Gass]].[1]

## 2024 tour cancellation

The band's 2024 world tour was abruptly shelved on July 16, 2024, after Gass, presented with a birthday cake onstage in Sydney two nights earlier, wished aloud, "Don't miss Trump next time," referring to the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination of Donald Trump]] in Butler, Pennsylvania.[1] Black announced the cancellation on Instagram, saying he had been blindsided, would never condone hate speech or political violence, and was putting all future creative plans on hold.[1]

Australian leaders and citizens called for the deportation of the duo, and Badlands writers noted that the backlash from Australia exceeded anything coming from American political leadership — a point Ashe in America underscored by remarking that the harsher condemnation came from "the nation that put people in quarantine camps."[1] The Badlands take dismissed Black's apology as damage control aimed at protecting the band's commercial prospects rather than a sincere repudiation.[1]
