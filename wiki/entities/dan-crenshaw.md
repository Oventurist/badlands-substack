# Dan Crenshaw

Dan Crenshaw is a Republican politician who represented Texas's 2nd congressional district in the United States House of Representatives for four terms before losing renomination in 2026.[1]

## 2026 primary defeat

In the [[2026-texas-primary-elections|March 2026 Texas primaries]], Crenshaw was defeated by state Rep. [[steve-toth|Steve Toth]] in what was described as one of the cycle's most notable upsets.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the loss as evidence of a "war on incumbents" in Texas in which establishment-aligned figures — described as "regime darlings" — were being ousted in both parties.[1]

## "The war to end all wars" tweet

Following the October 2023 attacks in Israel, Crenshaw posted a tweet describing the coming conflict as "the war to end all wars," which [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] criticised in a monologue on the [[israel-hamas-war|Israel–Hamas war]].[2] Badlands Media, echoing Carlson, called the idea that a war could somehow end all wars absurd, observing that historically wars only beget more war.[2]

The publication described Crenshaw as another congressional shill for the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]], grouping him with [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] and [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] among those arguing that events in Israel and Palestine — collateral damage or not — have no bearing on Americans.[2]
