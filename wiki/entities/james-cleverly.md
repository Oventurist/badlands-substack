# James Cleverly

**James Cleverly** is a British Conservative politician who served as Home Secretary in the government of [[rishi-sunak|Rishi Sunak]].[1]

## Response to Farage's Ukraine remarks

In June 2024, after [[nigel-farage|Nigel Farage]] of [[reform-uk|Reform UK]] blamed [[nato|NATO]] and European Union enlargement for provoking Russia's invasion of Ukraine, Cleverly said Farage was "echoing Putin's vile justification for the brutal invasion of Ukraine."[1] His remarks formed part of a cross-party pushback that also included Sunak, Labour leader [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]], and shadow defense secretary John Healey, delivered against the backdrop of a UK general election scheduled for July 4, 2024 in which Reform UK's rise was splitting the right-wing vote.[1]
