# School of the Americas

The School of the Americas (SOA) was the US military training institution whose Latin American graduates included [[manuel-noriega]], the Panamanian general who became an installed CIA dictator and a paid asset of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]].[1]

In Joe Lange's account the school functions as one node of a wider apparatus that produced compliant strongmen across Central and South America: Noriega, an SOA graduate, went on to run Panama's army and police, to facilitate cocaine trafficking from Colombia into the United States on behalf of the [[medellin-cartel]], and to provide [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]] with the use of Panama's airports, banking system and security officers for laundering drug proceeds.[1] The school is treated in the series as part of the institutional infrastructure of what Lange calls the [[shadow-government]].[1]
