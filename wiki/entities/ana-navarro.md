# Ana Navarro

Ana Navarro is a political commentator and co-host of the daytime talk show [[the-view|The View]].[1]

In May 2023, during a panel discussion of the mass shooting at a Dallas-area mall, Navarro argued that Hispanic or Black identity does not make a person "immune from being racist, from being radicalized, from being a White supremacist, from being evil, from being homicidal."[1] She cited [[proud-boys|Proud Boys]] chairman [[enrique-tarrio|Enrique Tarrio]] as an example of a Hispanic figure leading what she called a white-nationalist group, adding that "there are people who, they don't see themselves as what they are."[1] Her co-hosts reacted with surprise to the suggestion.[1]

The remarks came as anonymous law-enforcement sources claimed the suspected shooter, 33-year-old Mauricio Garcia, may have been motivated by white-supremacist ideology.[1] Badlands Media commentator [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] criticized the exchange as absurd narrative-spinning that exploited a mass-casualty event.[1]
