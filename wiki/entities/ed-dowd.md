# Ed Dowd

Ed Dowd is a former [[blackrock|BlackRock]] portfolio manager who became one of the most widely cited independent analysts of pandemic-era mortality, disability and vaccine-injury data.[1] After leaving institutional asset management, Dowd devoted several years to deep-dive research and analysis of pandemic-related data sets, applying the quantitative and actuarial habits of a career investor to public health statistics.[1]

## Publications and research

Dowd is the author of a book on the subject of excess deaths and sudden deaths in the vaccinated population, which was promoted within alternative-media circles as a landmark compilation of official data pointing to unexplained harm.[1] He works alongside partners Carlos Alegria and Yuri Nunes, with whom he launched [[phinance-technologies|Phinance Technologies]], a research firm that combines conventional macroeconomic analysis with a series of "Humanity Projects" reports on pandemic-related disabilities and excess deaths compiled from official government data.[1]

An analysis by Dowd's group reported that the rate of Serious Adverse Events recorded in the mRNA COVID-19 vaccine clinical trials closely tracked the spike in disabilities reported after the vaccine rollout — a correlation the researchers argued should have caused the trials to be halted.[1] The findings were amplified by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], which framed them within a broader argument that skeptics of mRNA vaccines had spent eighteen months subject to ostracism, deplatforming and flawed fact-checking before the underlying data began to speak for itself.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Coverage of Dowd's work in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated the analysis as part of an expected reckoning for [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]], described by contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] as "one of the key cogs in the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s System of Systems."[1] Burning Bright cautioned that he did not personally claim certainty about where the truth lay on vaccine adverse effects, while predicting that public data mapping onto long-standing community claims would produce "a slow groundswell of simmering anger in the American public."[1]
