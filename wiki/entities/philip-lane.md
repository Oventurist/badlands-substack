# Philip Lane

Philip Lane is the Chief Economist of the [[european-central-bank|European Central Bank]].[1]

## Advocacy for a digital euro (March 2025)

Speaking at a conference in Cork, Ireland, in March 2025, Lane reiterated the ECB's case for a [[digital-euro|digital euro]], arguing that a central bank digital currency was essential to preserving Europe's monetary and financial autonomy amid increasing geopolitical fragmentation.[1] He framed the project explicitly as a defensive measure against dollar-denominated [[stablecoin|stablecoins]] and against European reliance on U.S. payment firms, saying a digital euro would "limit the likelihood of foreign-currency stablecoins gaining a foothold as a medium of exchange in the euro area."[1]

Lane also argued that a digital euro could address Europe's fragmentation in retail payments and serve as a unifying force for collaboration among banks and payment service providers.[1] Badlands commentary read the ECB's urgency as confirmation of the strategic significance of the stablecoin push by [[donald-trump|Trump]], Scott Bessent and Howard Lutnick, arguing that treasury-backed stablecoins — which cannot be issued at will and must be collateralized — are functionally "more real" than central bank notes and therefore threaten the central banking model itself.[1]
