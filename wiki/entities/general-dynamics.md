# General Dynamics

General Dynamics is a major American defense contractor and the manufacturer of the U.S. Army's principal armored fighting vehicles, including the [[m1-abrams|M1 Abrams]] main battle tank.[1]

## New American tank development

In June 2022 the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] announced that it had commissioned General Dynamics to develop the first new American tank in more than forty years — a light tank intended for infantry forces.[1] The resulting vehicle became known as the [[m10-booker|M10 Booker]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that this contract predated the January 2023 transfer of 31 M1A1 Abrams tanks to Ukraine, arguing that the United States was in effect "emptying our surplus" while its next-generation armor program was already under way.[1] He further observed that in September 2023 the Pentagon scrapped its plan to upgrade the aging Abrams fleet, opting instead to develop an entirely new Abrams prototype.[1]
