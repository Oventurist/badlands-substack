# District of Columbia National Guard

The District of Columbia National Guard is the National Guard component of the US capital. Unlike the Guard of any state, it reports directly to the President of the United States rather than to a governor — a structural feature emphasized in the March 13, 2026 [[badlands-brief-559|Badlands Brief]].

## Activation of the 260th Special Purpose Brigade

The Brief's bonus item reports that the DC National Guard activated the [[260th-special-purpose-brigade|260th Special Purpose Brigade]], the Army's only brigade dedicated to coordinating military support to civil authorities and protecting critical infrastructure in the capital. The unit was formed following [[executive-order-14339|Executive Order 14339]] and a directive from the [[department-of-war|Department of War]] calling for a trained and equipped National Guard unit to support public safety and integrate with federal and local security partners. Brig. Gen. [[craig-maceri|Craig Maceri]] described the brigade as providing a command structure for a wide range of missions with rapid coordination during major events, emergencies, and security operations. Among its primary partners is the [[us-marshals-service|US Marshals Service]], which regularly works alongside the Guard in Washington. The brigade inherits the lineage of the 260th Military Police Command, a DC National Guard brigade inactivated in 2011.
