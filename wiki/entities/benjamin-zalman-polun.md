# Benjamin Zalman-Polun

Benjamin Zalman-Polun is an American, described as a 36-year-old former cannabis dealer from Maryland, who was seized by Congolese authorities after the failed coup attempt of May 2024 in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

## Arrest in the 2024 coup attempt

Zalman-Polun was one of two American co-conspirators reported captured alongside [[marcel-malanga|Marcel Malanga]] after Congolese forces thwarted the plot led by [[christian-malanga|Christian Malanga]] in the early hours of Sunday, May 19, 2024 in the Gombe area of northern Kinshasa.[1] The Congolese government said dozens of attackers were arrested and four killed, including Christian Malanga, who was "definitively neutralized" by security forces.[1]
