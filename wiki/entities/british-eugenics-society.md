# British Eugenics Society

The British Eugenics Society was the organization presided over by [[julian-huxley|Sir Julian Huxley]], the founder of [[transhumanism|transhumanism]] and close friend of the Jesuit [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cites Huxley's presidency of the society as evidence that he was not merely an ivory tower commentator but an extremely active grand strategist, whose other institutional creations included the [[international-union-for-conservation-of-nature|International Union for the Conservation of Nature]], the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] and [[unesco|UNESCO]].[1] The society's agenda of [[eugenics|eugenics]] converged with Chardin's own calls for a "nobly human form of eugenics" applied first to individuals and then to society.[1]
