# William Sessions

William Sessions was the Director of the [[fbi|FBI]] during the administration of George H. W. Bush.[1] In the Badlands corpus he is chiefly noted for allegations about his later legal work.[1]

## Alleged representation of Semion Mogilevich

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote in the September 10, 2024 Badlands News Brief that Sessions served as the personal attorney of [[semion-mogilevich|Semion Mogilevich]] — described by the FBI as the "boss of all bosses" of the Israeli-Russian mafia — and continued in that role until Sessions' death in June 2020.[1] The claim is attributed to author Craig Unger's book on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the Russian mafia.[1]

The Badlands framing of the arrangement was blunt: "in 2014, the [[cia|CIA]] overthrew the Ukrainian government and installed a member of the Israeli Mafia as interim president. And the crime boss of the Israeli Mafia had the Bush Family's FBI Director on retainer as his personal attorney."[1] The commentary summarized the whole picture in a single word: "Swampy."[1]
