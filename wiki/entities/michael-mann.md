# Michael Mann

Michael Mann is an American climatologist best known for the "hockey stick" temperature reconstruction, described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] in his June 5, 2025 Badlands Media essay as one of the "proven fraudsters" of climate science.[1] Ehret writes that Mann attempted to erase the [[medieval-warm-period|medieval warming period]] from existence with the hockey stick model, crafted with the help of East Anglia's [[phil-jones|Phil Jones]].[1]

Against the hockey stick, the article maintains that from roughly 1000 to 1350 A.D. global mean temperatures were significantly warmer than anything experienced today, noting that the Vikings in Greenland had no coal plants or SUVs yet lived through warmer mean temperatures "by a long shot."[1] Ehret links to reporting on Mann's defamation lawsuit against Tim Ball, which was decided against Mann, as support for the fraud characterization.[1] In the essay's argument, discrediting the hockey stick removes a central pillar of the claim that [[carbon-dioxide|carbon dioxide]] drives temperature.[1]
