# Ruby Calvert

Ruby Calvert served as Chair of the board of the [[corporation-for-public-broadcasting|Corporation for Public Broadcasting]] (CPB) at the time of its dissolution.[1]

Calvert led the board that voted to dissolve the 58-year-old organization after [[congress|Congress]] fully rescinded its federal funding, describing the outcome as "devastating."[1] She and the board assured that remaining funds would support public media transitions, preserve historical archives in partnership with the University of Maryland and maintain the American Archive of Public Broadcasting, emphasizing that public media entities such as [[npr|NPR]] and [[pbs|PBS]] would continue independently.[1]
