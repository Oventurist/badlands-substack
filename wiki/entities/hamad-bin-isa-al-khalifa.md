# Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa

King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa is the monarch of [[bahrain|Bahrain]], one of the original signatory states of the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]].

## 2026 Middle East settlement talks

As reported in the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," King Hamad was among the regional leaders with whom [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] held discussions on a comprehensive settlement with [[iran|Iran]]. The other participants named in Trump's [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement included [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], [[mohamed-bin-zayed-al-nahyan|Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan]] of the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], Emir [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani]] of [[qatar|Qatar]], Field Marshal [[asim-munir|Asim Munir]] of [[pakistan|Pakistan]], President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] of [[turkey|Türkiye]], President [[abdel-fattah-el-sisi|Abdel Fattah El-Sisi]] of [[egypt|Egypt]], and [[king-abdullah-ii|King Abdullah II]] of [[jordan|Jordan]].
