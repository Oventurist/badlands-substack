# Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa

**Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa** is the King of [[bahrain|Bahrain]] and head of the House of Al Khalifa, the dynasty that has ruled the island state since 1784.[1] In 2024 he also held the rotating chairmanship of the [[arab-league|Arab League]].[1]

## Moscow visit, May 2024

King Hamad arrived in Moscow on Thursday, May 23, 2024, for a two-day working visit. Following a two-hour conversation with Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], the two leaders signed documents covering cooperation in the pharmaceutical and medical industries, transport and transit, and cultural exchange and environmental memoranda, according to a Kremlin readout.[1]

Putin told Hamad that trade between Russia and Bahrain remained "at an early stage" though the trends were positive, while noting that investment ties were stronger, with roughly 30 large projects underway worth around $500 million in total.[1] Hamad praised Putin for his "wise policy" and acknowledged "Russia's role in the fair resolution of Arab issues."[1] He also said Bahrain intended to host an international conference aimed at resolving the Israel-Hamas conflict.[1]

During the visit Hamad announced Bahrain's intention to restore ties with [[iran|Iran]], and Putin invited Bahrain to attend the forthcoming [[brics|BRICS]] summit; Bahrain had applied for BRICS membership in 2022 but was not among the states invited to join in August 2023.[1]

## Arab Summit and peace diplomacy

Shortly before the Moscow trip, Hamad hosted the 33rd Arab Summit in Bahrain, at which a collective pact was issued calling for Israel's total withdrawal from Palestine.[1] He announced an intention to convene an international peace conference in the near future, seeking to enlist the international community in fulfilling the promise of the 1993 [[oslo-accords|Oslo Accords]] and finalizing a two-state solution between Israel and Palestine.[1] In Moscow, Hamad indicated that he regarded Putin as the most important leader from outside the region to attend that meeting — in the Badlands reading, because of Putin's public defiance of Western hegemony.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented the meeting as further evidence of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], with Putin acting as peacemaker and Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] "looming off-screen."[1] He recalled that during the June 2023 Wagner mutiny in Russia, MBS hosted a call with Putin that also included the UAE and Bahrain, and read Bahrain's inclusion as a signal that Sunni and Shia powers were making amends.[1] The commentary noted that Bahrain is a small island state of about 1.5 million people in the Persian Gulf off the Saudi coast near Qatar, with a slight Shia majority, and that after the 2011 Arab Spring protests — during which the government was criticized for its crackdown — the Al Khalifa turned to Saudi Arabia and the UAE for protection.[1]
