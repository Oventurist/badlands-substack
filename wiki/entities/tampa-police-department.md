# Tampa Police Department

The Tampa Police Department is the municipal law enforcement agency of Tampa, Florida, which in November 2023 announced the arrest of [[jimmie-gardner|Jimmie Gardner]], the brother-in-law of [[stacey-abrams|Stacey Abrams]], on human trafficking allegations.[1]

## Gardner arrest

Tampa police said they arrested the 57-year-old Gardner on a Friday for allegedly attempting to engage in sexual acts with a 16-year-old girl.[1] The department's public release laid out a detailed timeline: Gardner met the minor at 1:43 a.m., invited her to his room at the Renaissance Hotel at International Plaza, and there allegedly offered her money for sex before a dispute escalated into a physical altercation in which he placed his hands around her neck and impeded her breathing.[1] The release stated that Gardner left the room afterward and the victim called 911; responding officers found the victim at the scene but Gardner had already departed the hotel.[1]
