# Bill Owens

Bill Owens is an American television news producer who served for many years as executive producer of the CBS newsmagazine [[60-minutes|60 Minutes]].[1]

## Departure from 60 Minutes

Owens stepped down from the program in 2025 after alleging that 60 Minutes had lost its journalistic independence.[1] In his farewell memo he cited unsustainable internal pressures from [[paramount-global|Paramount]] executives as the reason for his exit.[1]

His resignation was followed weeks later by that of [[wendy-mcmahon|Wendy McMahon]], president and chief executive of [[cbs-news|CBS News]], who left citing disagreement with the company over its path forward.[1] The two departures came amid litigation over the network's edited [[60-minutes|60 Minutes]] interview with Kamala Harris.[1]

## Coverage

The May 21, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] carried the National Pulse account of the CBS leadership turmoil as a bonus item, treating the Owens and McMahon exits together as a sign of institutional breakdown inside a flagship legacy-news organization.[1]
