# The Majority Report

"The Majority Report" is an online political program distributed on YouTube, described by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as "a transcendentally cringe and unabashedly pro-DNC YouTube show."[1]

DeLarme referenced the program in "The Candidate Who Isn't There," published August 24, 2024, in connection with co-host [[emma-vigeland|Emma Vigeland]], who posted a claim on X suggesting that [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] was considering an arms embargo against [[israel|Israel]].[1] DeLarme called the claim entirely invented and used it to illustrate what he described as the contortions of liberal influencers attempting to inhabit both the pro-Palestine world and the [[democratic-party|Democrat]] establishment world simultaneously.[1]

The show's role in the article is as a representative case of media figures whose commentary, in DeLarme's telling, supplied ideological cover for a candidacy he considered devoid of any stated policy platform.[1]
