# National School Boards Association

The National School Boards Association (NSBA) is the U.S. organization of local school board members whose September 2021 memo to the Department of Justice became the trigger for Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]]'s October 4, 2021 directive on threats against school officials.[1] Documents released in July 2025 by [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] placed the NSBA memo at the start of the chain of events that produced the Garland memo.[1]

## The 2021 memo

According to the documents, the NSBA memo called on the DOJ to use the Patriot Act to prevent parents from objecting to school mask mandates and to Critical Race Theory in curricula.[1] The Garland memo that followed labeled concerned parents as "domestic terrorists," a characterization America First Legal argued was a political operation of weaponized government rather than lawful enforcement of federal law.[1] Career attorneys in the Department's Civil Rights Division dissented, warning that there was no federal authority or legal basis to target the parents because their speech was protected by the First Amendment.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentary argued that the NSBA's call for the government to deploy the Patriot Act against parents objecting to indoctrination and to what the commentary called pseudoscientific health guidance should itself have been the headline at the time, but that the media instead amplified the association's demands.[1] The commentary concluded that a "golden age must have no Patriot Act" and probably no NSBA either.[1]
