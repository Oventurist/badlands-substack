# Denys Yaroslavskyi

Denys Yaroslavskyi is a Ukrainian military commander who leads the Ukrainian Special Reconnaissance Unit operating in the Kharkiv region.[1]

## Statements on the missing first line of defense

During the [[2024-kharkiv-offensive|2024 Kharkiv offensive]], Yaroslavskyi told the BBC that Ukraine's northern defensive line simply did not exist. "There was no first line of defence. We saw it. The Russians just walked in. They just walked in, without any mined fields," he said.[1] He stated that government officials had claimed to have built mined defenses at huge cost, and concluded: "Either it was an act of negligence, or corruption. It wasn't a failure. It was a betrayal."[1]

Recalling the earlier fighting for the same territory, he added: "When we were fighting back for this territory in 2022, we lost thousands of people. We risked our lives. And now because someone didn't build fortifications, we're losing people again."[1]

His account corroborated the report by anti-corruption investigator [[martyna-bohuslavets|Martyna Bohuslavets]] that fortification funds routed through the Kharkiv Regional Military Administration had gone to front companies.[1]
