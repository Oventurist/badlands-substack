# Executive Committee of the National Security Council

The Executive Committee of the [[national-security-council|National Security Council]], commonly abbreviated ExComm, was an unofficial advisory council formed in the 1960s by [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] and [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]].[1] Badlands Media describes its purpose as keeping peacemaking efforts siloed from the [[joint-chiefs-of-staff|Joint Chiefs of Staff]] and the intelligence agencies during a period of acute superpower tension.[1]

## Composition and the "Irish Mafia"

Unaccustomed to being kept in the dark, officials excluded from the body colloquially referred to the cadre of Kennedys and their childhood friends from the Boston area — among them [[kenneth-odonnell|Kenneth O'Donnell]] — as the "Irish Mafia."[1]

## Backchannel diplomacy

By going around the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]] and the news media, the members of this circle established backchannels to Soviet leader [[nikita-khrushchev|Nikita Khrushchev]] in an effort to avoid the war that, in the Badlands framing, an "invisible enemy" in both nations was eager to see.[1] As tensions rose, Robert Kennedy is said to have snapped "can the f***ing chiefs" amid flaring tempers over what the account calls the war for peace.[1]

## Later invocation

Badlands Media invoked ExComm in November 2024 as a template for understanding [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second-transition appointments and his team's decision to bypass [[fbi|FBI]] background checks, arguing that a "new American Mafia" was forming outside the established national security apparatus.[1]
