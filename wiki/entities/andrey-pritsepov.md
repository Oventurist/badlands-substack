# Andrey A. Pritsepov

Andrey A. Pritsepov is the Ambassador of [[russia|Russia]] to [[guyana|Guyana]].[1]

## Presentation of letters of credence

On August 6, 2025, Pritsepov presented his letters of credence to Dr. [[mohamed-irfaan-ali|Mohamed Irfaan Ali]], President of the Co-operative Republic of Guyana, in a meeting described by the Russian Embassy as a warm welcoming talk covering a wide range of practical bilateral issues.[1]

The ambassador emphasized Russia's readiness to build trusting relations based on equality, to expand areas of cooperation, and to continue close interaction in spheres including higher education, agriculture, energy and air services.[1] He also underlined the importance of strengthening ties within the United Nations system, noting that Guyana is currently a non-permanent member of the UN Security Council.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] read the meeting geopolitically, noting that Russia — an ally of Venezuela — was engaging Guyana shortly before a Russian meeting with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], even as commentators anticipated Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro eventually annexing Guyana.[1]
