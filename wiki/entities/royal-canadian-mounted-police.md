# Royal Canadian Mounted Police

The **Royal Canadian Mounted Police** (RCMP) is Canada's national police force. It appears in Badlands Media coverage of the [[canadian-residential-schools|Canadian Residential Schooling system]] through an allegation reported by author [[kevin-annett|Kevin Annett]].[1]

## Allegation of transfer of victims

In his book *Murder by Decree*, Annett recounts an anonymous phone call from a man claiming to be a retired member of the Canadian civil service, who alleged that the torture of indigenous people was tied up in Department of National Defense projects and that the RCMP delivered the tortured victims into mind control programs.[1] The caller further stated that the program had German origins and that its direction came from the [[pentagon|Pentagon]].[1]

## Context

The allegation is used to support the hypothesis that the 2022 mass grave narrative functioned as a [[limited-hangout|limited hangout]] concealing [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] activity involving indigenous children.[1]
