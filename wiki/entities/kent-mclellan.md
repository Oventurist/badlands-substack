# Kent "Boneface" McLellan

Kent McLellan, known by the nickname "Boneface," is a heavily tattooed American activist in Florida's white-nationalist scene who became the focus of online identification efforts after a masked neo-Nazi demonstration in Florida in early September 2023.[1] Most participants in the rally wore matching new red shirts and face coverings, but McLellan was among the small number who appeared unmasked, allowing observers to attempt to identify him.[1]

## Background and allegations

According to journalist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]], whose account was reproduced by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], McLellan was arrested by the [[fbi|FBI]] in Florida in May 2012 on domestic terrorism grounds, with the bureau stating that he and others were "preparing a terrorist act against national minorities in Florida."[1] Loomer further alleged that he subsequently travelled to Ukraine in 2014 to join [[right-sector|Right Sector]], which she characterized as "a creation of the [[cia|CIA]]," returned to Ukraine in 2022, and was reportedly deported before resurfacing in Florida's local neo-Nazi scene.[1]

Loomer argued that this sequence of events was implausible absent official protection, writing that "there is no way you get away with all of that after being arrested by the FBI for domestic terrorism, and there's no way you get away with traveling to Ukraine to fight in a foreign war as an American Citizen defected mercenary unless you are some type of FBI or CIA informant."[1] She also stated that the marchers she encountered on Interstate 4 claimed to support [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] while waving swastika flags and shouting antisemitic slurs at her.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] treated the episode as evidence of federal infiltration of far-right street groups, arguing that the demonstrators resembled the "middle aged fedbois with the signature ski masks" popularized by [[patriot-front|Patriot Front]] and describing [[blood-tribe|Blood Tribe]] as a likely front for a three-letter agency running COINTELPRO-style operations.[1] DeLarme added that most genuine neo-Nazi combatants, in his view, were in Ukraine, where he alleged CIA-created proxy formations trained and armed them with American tax dollars.[1]
