# John Salvi

John Salvi is described in the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI" as an abortion doctor killer, and is named among the individuals suspected by many of having taken part in the [[cia|CIA]]'s [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]] mind-control program.[1]

## Function ascribed by the article

The essay assigns each of its alleged patsies a purpose. Salvi, in this scheme, "was used to make pro-life supporters seem like violent extremists."[1] The logic mirrors the article's treatment of [[david-koresh|David Koresh]] and [[timothy-mcveigh|Timothy McVeigh]], who are said to have been used to make anti-government groups look violent and crazy, and of [[jacob-chansley|the QAnon Shaman]], whose appearance the article claims was used to make [[donald-trump|Trump]] supporters appear unbalanced.[1] In each case the alleged aim is to discredit a political constituency by association with a single spectacular actor.[1]

## Context

MK ULTRA is described in the article as the mind-control system the CIA developed, using LSD and other drugs together with physical and psychological torture to break people's minds and reprogram them as assets.[1] The article's answer to its own title question is that most alleged patsies not only thought they were working with the CIA or [[fbi|FBI]] but also had alleged ties to CIA mind control.[1] No documentation specific to Salvi is offered.[1]
