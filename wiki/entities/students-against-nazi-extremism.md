# Students Against Nazi Extremism

Students Against Nazi Extremism, abbreviated "SANE," is the name used by a group that claimed responsibility for a campaign of threatening notes left on Tesla vehicles in northern California in February 2025.[1]

## The Arcata incidents

Law enforcement officials in Arcata, California received multiple reports from Tesla owners who found threatening notes on their vehicles, allegedly left by the group.[1] At least one note was found wrapped around a brick, which observers read as an implication that the group was preparing for violence.[1] Four Tesla owners in Arcata reported the notes to the Arcata Police Department over the course of a week, with the first report arriving on Wednesday, February 5, 2025 and three more submitted over the following two days.[1]

The Lost Coast Outpost, a local news outlet, received an envelope containing a note taking credit for the threats. The note, signed "Students Against Nazi Extremism" or "SANE," claimed that ten students in Arcata and Seaside, California had delivered written warnings to thirteen Tesla owners.[1] The envelope also contained a manifesto describing Tesla chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] as "an overt Nazi with a history of fascist, racist, misogynist and criminal behavior."[1]

## Context

The California threats coincided with repeated vandalism and arson attacks on a [[tesla|Tesla]] dealership in Colorado.[1] Badlands commentary argued that years of heated rhetoric from American institutional actors had contributed materially to anti-Musk, anti-Trump and anti-MAGA crime, and criticized Representative [[jamie-raskin|Jamie Raskin]] for remarks the hosts characterized as dog-whistling to supporters inclined toward such acts.[1]
