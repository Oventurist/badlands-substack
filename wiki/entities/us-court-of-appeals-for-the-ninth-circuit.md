# US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit

The United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit is the federal appellate court with jurisdiction over the western United States. It is the largest of the federal circuit courts and hears appeals from district courts across its region, frequently including high-profile challenges to executive branch action.

## Executive Order 14251 collective bargaining case

The February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that a three-judge panel of the Ninth Circuit vacated a lower court's preliminary injunction that had blocked implementation of [[executive-order-14251|Executive Order 14251]]. That order excludes certain federal agencies from collective bargaining requirements, directing that agencies whose primary functions relate to intelligence, counterintelligence, investigative or national security work may be exempted from obligations under the Federal Service Labor-Management Relations Statute.

The panel held that the federal employee unions challenging the order were unlikely to succeed on the merits of their claim that the order amounted to retaliation in violation of the First Amendment, and concluded that the injunction had therefore been wrongly imposed. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] summarized the outcome as meaning that "collective bargaining in national security agencies is dead," counting it among the week's setbacks for opponents of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s agenda.
