# Tata Group

The Tata Group is an Indian conglomerate — characterised in reporting as a "salt-to-software" enterprise — that has become a significant contract manufacturer of Apple iPhones in India.[1]

## Entry into iPhone assembly

In 2023 Tata took over the Wistron Corporation plant in southern Karnataka state, which produced the balance of India-made iPhones not assembled by [[foxconn|Foxconn Technology Group]] (nearly 67 percent) or Pegatron Corporation (about 17 percent) in the fiscal year ended March 2024.[1] Tata subsequently announced plans to build one of India's largest iPhone assembly plants.[1]

## Significance

Tata's expansion is a marker of the [[friendshoring|friendshoring]] trend, in which [[apple|Apple]] and other Western firms shift production away from China toward politically friendlier states.[1] Indian iPhone output exceeded $14 billion in the period, roughly 14 percent of Apple's global iPhone production, even as China retained the largest share.[1] Badlands analysis linked the rise of Indian manufacturing under [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] to a broader multipolar realignment rather than a simple Western victory over China.[1]
