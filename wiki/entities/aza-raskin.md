# Aza Raskin

Aza Raskin is an American technologist and co-founder of the [[center-for-humane-technology|Center for Humane Technology]], the organization behind [[the-ai-dilemma|The AI Dilemma]]. He appears in the Badlands Media corpus as the co-presenter, with [[tristan-harris|Tristan Harris]], of that March 2023 briefing on the societal risks of large-language-model artificial intelligence.

The July 2023 article "Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason" describes Raskin and Harris as arguing that existing AI capabilities already pose catastrophic risks to a functional society, that AI companies are caught in a competitive race to deploy as quickly as possible without adequate safety measures, and that institutions must be upgraded for a post-AI world. The presentation was given at a private San Francisco gathering on March 9, 2023, ahead of the GPT-4 launch, and its viewers were encouraged to contact political representatives to advocate for hearings on AI risk and for regulatory guardrails.

Raskin is cited, with Harris, for the forecast that AI systems will shortly be able to perform dream mapping and dream interpretation using functional MRI data. The Badlands article follows that thread to the film *Minority Report* as an illustration of where such capability might lead.

Raskin is listed by the article among the signatories of the [[pause-giant-ai-experiments-open-letter|March 22, 2023 open letter]] urging a six-month moratorium on training AI systems more powerful than GPT-4 — a proposal the article argues is unenforceable, since it would bind only commercial developers and public-facing products while leaving government, defense, and research programs untouched.
