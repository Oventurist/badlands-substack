# Akhmat Special Forces

The Akhmat Special Forces, better known as the "Kadyrovites," are the Chechen special operations formation of Russia's Chechen Republic, commanded by Major General [[apty-alaudinov|Apty Alaudinov]].[1] Formally designated the 141st Special Motorized Regiment, the unit was created in 1999 at the onset of the [[second-chechen-war|Second Chechen War]] by [[akhmad-kadyrov|Akhmad Kadyrov]], father of the republic's later leader [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]].[1]

## Disbandment and reconstitution

Because it was the Kadyrovites who were guarding Akhmad Kadyrov when he was assassinated in 2004, Ramzan Kadyrov disbanded the unit, merging the small number of men he still trusted — Alaudinov among them — into Russian law enforcement under the Ministry of Internal Affairs.[1] The team of fifty Chechen police officers that Alaudinov led against [[movladi-baisarov|Movladi Baisarov]] in Moscow in November 2006 became the basis for the unit's legal reformation.[1] With Vladimir Putin's help, Ramzan Kadyrov outfitted the refurbished force with mechanized armor and other heavy equipment it had not previously possessed, producing the modern Chechen special forces.[1]

## Deployments

Operating out of a facility in Dagestan, the unit has taken part in several of the most notorious conflicts of the past decade.[1] It deployed to Aleppo, Syria, in 2017 in an effort described as winning the "hearts and minds" of the local population.[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry wrote that the Kadyrovites were on the ground in Ukraine as early as 2014, fighting what he characterized as the [[cia|CIA]]'s campaign after Langley overthrew the elected government and installed [[oleksandr-turchynov|Oleksandr Turchynov]] as interim president.[1] The unit was also sent to Rostov in 2023 to subdue the so-called "Wagner Rebellion" led by [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]].[1]

By October 2024 the Akhmat forces were engaged in the [[kursk-incursion|Kursk]] theatre against Ukrainian troops, a deployment Badlands framed as "crushing the NATO blitzkrieg," and which followed Putin's visit to Dagestan to bless the formation.[1]
