# Jimmy Kimmel

Jimmy Kimmel is an American late-night talk show host. In December 2023 he interviewed comedian [[bill-burr|Bill Burr]], who told him that liberals had been "fucking stupid" in how they handled [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and had made him a martyr.[1]

During the segment Kimmel—characterized by Modernity News as having had "one of the most severe cases of Trump derangement syndrome for years"—asked whether Trump would "go away" if leftists simply ignored him.[1] Burr answered that the indictments had reversed Trump's fade into irrelevance.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] criticized both men, calling their takes unoriginal and their narcissism diagnoses hypocritical, and placed the exchange within a wider pattern of mainstream figures normalizing talk of Trump's death.[1]

## Biden dementia monologue (February 2024)

In a monologue broadcast on 1 February 2024 about what commentators dubbed the [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]] psyop, Kimmel suggested that it was a "crazy conspiracy theory" to believe that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] might be suffering from dementia.[2] He weighed in on assertions by Trump supporters that the NFL might be involved in promoting Swift as part of a voter recruitment strategy benefiting the Biden campaign, opening the segment by calling [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] a "clown who ran for president" who then "added his nut voice to the chorus of cuckoos."[2] Modernity News noted that the media had played up the NFL angle to dismiss the entire issue and attack conservatives as unhinged cranks, despite *New York Times* reporting that the Biden campaign did have a Taylor Swift strategy potentially involving a presidential appearance at one of her concerts before the election.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] compared the segment to the "horse paste" framing once used against ivermectin, arguing that mainstream entertainment media is the first stop when a legitimate discussion is to be recast as a crazy conspiracy theory.[2] She judged the timing notable given a video released by [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]] the previous day in which a [[state-department|State Department]] staffer said that everyone knows Biden is "slowing down," and concluded that Kimmel was "following orders" and had proved himself "nothing more than a regime mouthpiece."[2]

## Suspension of Jimmy Kimmel Live! (September 2025)

In September 2025, [[abc-news|ABC]] announced it would indefinitely halt production of Kimmel's show following remarks he made about the death of conservative activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], and Kimmel was photographed looking downcast leaving his Hollywood studio on the Wednesday evening after the announcement.[3] During Monday's monologue Kimmel had implied that Kirk's alleged murderer, 22-year-old [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]], could have been a pro-Trump MAGA Republican, saying the "MAGA Gang" was "desperately trying to characterize this kid who murdered Charlie Kirk as anything other than one of them."[3] The network's decision followed announcements by affiliate stations controlled by [[nexstar-media-group|Nexstar Media Group]] and [[sinclair-broadcast-group|Sinclair Broadcast Group]] that they would indefinitely cancel broadcasts of the program.[3] Badlands contributor Chris Paul remarked that the sequence looked "all scripted or something."[3] See [[jimmy-kimmel-live-suspension|Jimmy Kimmel Live! suspension]].

## 2025 suspension and reinstatement

In September 2025, Disney-owned ABC took Kimmel's long-running late-night talk show *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* off the air under pressure from the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], station owners, and conservative critics over comments he made after the killing of right-wing activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[4] In his September 15 monologue Kimmel said "the MAGA gang" was trying to paint the accused killer as "anything other than one of them," and suggested President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was feigning grief over the killing to "score political points."[4]

Nearly a week later, [[disney|The Walt Disney Co.]] announced Kimmel would return to television on Tuesday, September 23, 2025, stating: "Last Wednesday, we made the decision to suspend production on the show to avoid further inflaming a tense situation at an emotional moment for our country. It is a decision we made because we felt some of the comments were ill-timed and thus insensitive. We have spent the last days having thoughtful conversations with Jimmy, and after those conversations, we reached the decision to return the show on Tuesday."[4]

Badlands contributor Jon Herold predicted Kimmel would now claim victimhood from the "consequence culture" operation pushed by "Con, Inc.," which would in turn try to "consequence him harder," culminating in calls for government intervention — a dynamic Herold framed as controlled opposition.[4]
