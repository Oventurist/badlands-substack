# Marion Koopmans

**Marion Koopmans** is a Dutch virologist associated with the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]], including its work on outbreak origins and preparedness.[1]

Koopmans is cited in alternative media for having claimed that the WHO was preparing for "10 years of viruses," with 2020 counted as year one.[1] Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of April 7, 2023, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] invoked this claim as context for [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]]'s statement that there would "absolutely be an outbreak of another pandemic," arguing that such predictions arrive "right on cue" from figures who regularly war-game pandemic scenarios and have invested in what he termed the pandemic industrial complex.[1]

## Accusation against the WHO (November 2021)

A May 2023 Badlands Media article, "Behold, a Trojan Horse," reported that Koopmans — a Dutch virologist with the [[world-health-organization|WHO]] — had accused the organization of "undermining" her research into the origins of COVID-19, and that she claimed the WHO had been planning for "ten years of infectious diseases" with 2020 as only year one.[2] The article linked this claim to Fauci's subsequent statement that another outbreak could occur as soon as the following year, arguing both supported the narrative that global health authorities were preparing the public for recurring pandemics to justify the [[pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] and [[international-health-regulations|IHR amendments]].[2] It also noted that WHO Director-General [[tedros-ghebreyesus|Tedros Ghebreyesus]] had "undermined the investigation of Marion Koopmans" at the outset of COVID-19, which the author suggested revealed ulterior motives behind the WHO's push for expanded pandemic powers.[2]
