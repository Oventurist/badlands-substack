# Yaakov M. Roth

Yaakov M. Roth is the acting assistant attorney general for the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s civil division who, on Monday, March 3, 2025, filed a court brief known as a statement of interest in the case of former Mesa County, Colorado clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]].[1]

## Statement of interest in the Peters case

In the filing, Roth declared that "reasonable concerns have been raised about various aspects of Ms. Peters's case."[1] The brief noted that the Department is reviewing cases across the nation for abuses of the criminal justice process, citing the Attorney General Memorandum "Restoring the Integrity and Credibility of The Department of Justice" of February 5, 2025, and stated that the review would include an evaluation of the State of Colorado's prosecution of Peters and, in particular, whether the case was "oriented more toward inflicting political pain than toward pursuing actual justice or legitimate governmental objectives," language drawn from Executive Order 14147, "Ending the Weaponization of The Federal Government," of January 20, 2025.[1]

The New York Times characterized the move as a surprise and as the latest example of the Justice Department under [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s control seeking to use its powers to support those who have acted on his behalf.[1] Badlands contributor Ashe in America described the statement of interest as "absolute fire."[1]
