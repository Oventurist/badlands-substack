# Chip Roy

Chip Roy is a Republican member of the United States [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] from Texas, known within conservative politics for his work on immigration, fiscal restraint, and election integrity legislation.

## SAVE America Act

In the coverage of the February 10, 2026 Badlands Brief edition "Criminal Invader Crashouts & Honeypot Hoaxes," Roy is identified as the sponsor of the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]] (H.R. 7296), legislation that would amend the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act of 1993]] to require documentary proof of United States citizenship in order to register to vote in federal elections. The bill enumerates acceptable documentation, including a US passport, a REAL ID–compliant identification indicating citizenship, or other official proof of citizenship.

Roy introduced the measure on January 30, 2026, after which it was referred to the House Committee on House Administration. Although no final House vote had occurred as of the Brief's publication, House leadership had taken steps toward floor consideration, and Roy indicated in a press release from his office that the House would vote on the bill that week. Under normal procedure, passage in the House would send the measure to the [[senate|Senate]] before it could reach the President's desk.

The bill drew immediate opposition from House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]], who characterized it and broader voter identification efforts as voter suppression. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] defended the measure, citing polling indicating broad public support for photo identification requirements and arguing that Democratic opposition, rather than the policy itself, is the controversial position.
