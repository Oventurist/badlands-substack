# Eddy Cue

Eddy Cue is senior vice president of services at [[apple|Apple]].[1] In May 2025 he testified in court that Apple was considering adding artificial intelligence search engines to its Safari web browser, telling the court that search traffic on Safari had declined for the first time in the prior month because of AI.[1]

Cue named OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity as examples of AI search providers Apple would likely add, saying "[w]e will add them to the list — they probably won't be the default," according to Bloomberg's account of the testimony.[1] The remarks had an immediate market effect: [[google|Google]]'s share price fell roughly 9 percent on the day Cue took the stand, recovering by nearly 2 percent the following day.[1]
