# Ben Scallan

Ben Scallan is an Irish journalist whose reporting on hate-speech policy in Ireland and Scotland drew the attention of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] in August 2023.[1]

## Reporting on NGO-driven censorship

Scallan authored an article arguing that non-governmental organizations backed by the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], the grant-making network founded by [[george-soros|George Soros]], were advancing a "censorship agenda" in Ireland and Scotland that extended to police searches of homes and personal electronic devices.[1] He pushed back against claims by Irish leaders that hate-based crime was rising in the country, arguing instead that police and other authorities had actively encouraged citizens to report such misconduct, producing an increase in recorded statistics without a corresponding increase in actual offences.[1] Scallan further contended that these policies triggered a cultural shift in what the public perceived as hateful, giving authorities a pretext to press for stricter laws restricting free expression.[1]

Musk responded to the piece on X with the words "Exactly. X will be filing legal action to stop this. Can't wait for discovery to start!"[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], quoted Scallan's finding that Soros-funded NGOs "have been falsely claiming that 'hate incidents' are on the rise in order to push restrictions of free expression," treating it as a case study in the manipulation of statistics to shape public sentiment.[1]
