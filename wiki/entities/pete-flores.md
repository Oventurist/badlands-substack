# Pete Flores

Pete Flores is a Republican member of the [[texas-senate|Texas Senate]] representing Pleasanton.[1]

## Senate Bill 4 (2023)

Flores sponsored Senate Bill 4 during the third special legislative session of 2023, legislation that would raise the minimum sentence for smuggling immigrants or operating a stash house from two years to ten years.[1] The bill passed the Texas Senate with bipartisan support by a vote of 29-2 on Thursday evening, 12 October 2023, alongside Senate Bill 11, sponsored by [[brian-birdwell|Brian Birdwell]], which would authorize state police to arrest migrants crossing the southern border.[1]

The two immigration measures advanced after Governor [[greg-abbott|Greg Abbott]] convened the special session to press lawmakers on immigration enforcement and school vouchers.[1]
