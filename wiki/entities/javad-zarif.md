# Javad Zarif

Javad Zarif is an Iranian diplomat and politician who served as Iran's foreign minister. The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief, in a commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], credits Zarif — referred to in the Brief as "Iranian Prime Minister" — as the prime contact and principal negotiator for the Iranian side of the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran nuclear deal]] concluded under President [[hassan-rouhani|Hassan Rouhani]] with the Obama administration, while Rouhani, then president, authorized the meetings.
