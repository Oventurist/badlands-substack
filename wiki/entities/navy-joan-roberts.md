# Navy Joan Roberts

Navy Joan Roberts is the daughter of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[lunden-roberts|Lunden Roberts]], an Arkansas woman described in press accounts as an ex-stripper.[1]

## Reference in the Biden pardons commentary

In a January 2025 appearance on Fox News Channel's "Jesse Watters Primetime," retired professional wrestler [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]] quipped that in the flurry of clemency grants issued by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] shortly before leaving office — which included family members involved in the Biden family business, such as brothers [[james-biden|James]] and [[frank-biden|Frank Biden]] — the only person Biden had not pardoned was "Hunter's little baby with a stripper in case he does anything wrong."[1] The remark drew attention to Navy Joan's exclusion from a set of pardons that Hogan characterized as sweeping up the president's entire family.[1]
