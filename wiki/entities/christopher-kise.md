# Christopher Kise

Christopher Kise is an attorney who served as defense counsel to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the New York civil fraud case brought by the New York attorney general's office.[1]

## Role in the New York civil fraud trial

During Trump's November 2023 testimony in the [[new-york-civil-fraud-trial|New York civil fraud trial]], Kise was the defense lawyer to whom Judge [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]] directed the repeated demand, "Can you control your client?", after Trump answered questions with extended narrative responses rather than the direct answers the bench had requested.[1] Kise pushed back on the state's questioning, objecting that the prosecution's attorney was asking "questions that call for narratives," an objection that framed the courtroom clash as a dispute over the form of the questions rather than merely the witness's conduct.[1]

The exchange occurred in a proceeding from which cameras were barred, meaning Kise's objections and the judge's rebukes reached the public only through reporters' accounts and the parties' own characterizations of the day.[1] Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the resulting headlines as evidence that the trial functioned as a media event in its own right, generating coverage comparable to Trump's rallies despite the absence of cameras.[1]
