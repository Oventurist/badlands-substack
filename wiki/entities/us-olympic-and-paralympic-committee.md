# U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Committee

The **U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Committee (USOPC)** is the national governing body responsible for the United States' participation in the Olympic and Paralympic Games and for oversight of the country's Olympic sport national governing bodies.[1]

## Transgender eligibility policy change (2025)

In July 2025 the USOPC updated its policies in a way that suggested it would comply with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s "Keeping Men Out of Women's Sports" executive order.[1] The committee's transgender eligibility policy page was replaced with a note reading, "As of July 21, 2025, please refer to the USOPC athlete safety policy."[1] The athlete safety policy sets out no explicit guidelines on transgender inclusion in women's sports but includes a paragraph invoking [[executive-order-14201|Executive Order 14201]], pledging that the USOPC "will continue to collaborate with various stakeholders with oversight responsibilities… to ensure that women have a fair and safe competition environment."[1]

The change had immediate downstream effects on national governing bodies, with USA Fencing altering its transgender policy and citing new guidance from the USOPC.[1] The development was reported by [[fox-news|Fox News]] and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
