# Canadian International Development Agency

The **Canadian International Development Agency** (CIDA) was founded by [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]] during his tenure running Canada's Ministry of Foreign Affairs from 1965 to 1967.[1]

According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], CIDA transformed the philosophy of "international aid" from assisting poor nations to build full-spectrum economies into predatory lending tied to conditionalities.[1] The agency is presented as an early institutional expression of the outlook Strong later carried into the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], the UN population forums and the [[rio-earth-summit|Rio Summit]] of 1992.[1]
