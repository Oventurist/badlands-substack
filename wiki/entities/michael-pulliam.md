# Michael Pulliam

Michael Pulliam is a commander of the Washington, D.C. [[metropolitan-police-department|Metropolitan Police Department]] (MPD) who was placed on administrative leave in August 2025 amid allegations that he manipulated crime statistics.[1] The allegations were first reported by NBC Washington and became a focal point of a federal inquiry into whether police officials in the nation's capital falsified crime data.[1]

Pulliam has denied wrongdoing.[1] The MPD declined to comment on the matter, stating that it does not discuss "active internal investigations and personnel matters," and referred inquiries to a statement by MPD Chief [[pamela-smith|Pamela A. Smith]] affirming that the department "is committed to upholding the trust and the confidence of the public."[1]

## Federal investigation

Federal prosecutors, led by U.S. Attorney for the District of Columbia [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]], opened an investigation into whether D.C. police officials manipulated crime statistics.[1] The probe emerged amid an escalating political showdown between the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and the District over control of the police department, and it was not immediately clear what federal laws might have been violated by the alleged manipulation of crime data.[1] The New York Times first broke the news of the investigation, which was later confirmed by the Associated Press.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the episode as part of a wider pattern of institutional data manipulation, describing the falsification of crime statistics as "the most viscous form of gaslighting – denying the reality of public safety," and linking it to alleged distortions of economic, population, health, climate, and election data used to manufacture consent for social change.[1]
