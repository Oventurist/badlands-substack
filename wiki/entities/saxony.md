# Saxony

**Saxony** is a historic German region and the homeland of the Saxon peoples. In part five of [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series, Saxony is advanced as the possible ancestral home of the builders of the ancient Mesoamerican cities, on the basis of two claimed Aztec–German name correspondences.[1]

## The Sacatlan and Ani Sazi correspondences

The essay states that the Aztec word *Sacatlan*, meaning "ancient Mexico," translates into ancient German as *Saschenland*, which it glosses as "country" and, more specifically, as Saxony.[1] It further states that the Aztec phrase *Ani Sazi*, said to mean "ancient ancestors who built the cities," translates into ancient German as *Ahnen Sachen*, meaning "ancestors from Saxony."[1] These two pairs form the climax of the article's [[aztec-german-word-correspondences|Aztec–German word list]] and are treated as more consequential than the ordinary vocabulary matches, because they name a specific place of origin rather than merely a shared root.[1]

## Saxons, the Koryos, and the two-elites hypothesis

The author immediately follows the Saxony correspondences with the observation that [[montezuma|Montezuma]] is portrayed as having red hair, as are the [[koryos|Koryos]] werewolf cult, and asks openly whether the Saxons are an ancient counterbalance to the Koryos, whether the two are related, or whether both propositions hold in part.[1] This question sits inside the series' larger "tale of two elites" framing, in which a malevolent group originating in the region of present-day Ukraine is opposed by a second, benevolent globally influential society to which the author believes the Trump and [[john-f-kennedy|Kennedy]] families belong.[1]

Saxony's significance in that scheme derives from the [[ancient-german-language-thesis|ancient German language thesis]]: if ancient German was once spoken worldwide, then Germanic place-names embedded in Aztec vocabulary would mark the reach of that earlier civilisation.[1] The author separately notes that the Illuminati are reported to have begun in Bavaria, another part of Germany, and treats the recurrence of German origins among elite groupings as itself worth explaining.[1]
