# Soreq Nuclear Research Centre

The Soreq Nuclear Research Centre is an Israeli nuclear research facility that became the subject of international attention in November 2024 when the Iran-linked hacking group [[handala|Handala]] published roughly thirty photographs it claimed had been taken inside the site.[1]

## 2024 leak controversy

Alongside the photographs, Handala released screenshots said to reveal the names of nuclear scientists involved in the centre's particle accelerator project.[1] The office of the Prime Minister of Israel, responding on behalf of the Israel Atomic Energy Commission, denied the authenticity of the material, saying that "following a thorough examination, the images and blueprint do not belong to any of its facilities."[1]

## Significance in Badlands commentary

Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the alleged imagery as more newsworthy than the accompanying celebrity-style leaks of politicians' private photographs, arguing that documented evidence of Israeli nuclear infrastructure bears on Israel's refusal to sign the [[nuclear-non-proliferation-treaty|Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty]].[1] He recalled that France assisted Israel in beginning construction of its first nuclear facility in 1958, and that in January 1961 President Dwight D. Eisenhower warned [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] that the growing Israeli nuclear program would be his number one problem to solve.[1] The definitive public confirmation of Israeli nuclear facilities came in 1985 through the disclosures of whistleblower [[mordechai-vanunu|Mordechai Vanunu]].[1]
