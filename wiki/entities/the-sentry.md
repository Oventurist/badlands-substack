# The Sentry

The Sentry is a Washington-based non-profit founded in 2016 by George Clooney and other Hollywood figures, focused on corruption and illicit finance in Africa.[1]

## 2025 congressional testimony on the DRC

At a March 2025 subcommittee hearing convened by the [[house-foreign-affairs-committee|House Foreign Affairs Committee]], Sentry policy adviser Sasha Lezhnev urged Washington to counter what he described as China's exploitation of corruption in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1] "The biggest obstacle to eliminating mining abuses is the system of kleptocracy in the DRC," Lezhnev said, adding that "the system of state capture allows ruling elites and their commercial partners to personally profit while leaving the population to suffer."[1] He called on the United States to take steps to stop China's "illicit exploitation" in the country.[1]

The testimony came as the Trump administration was considering a minerals deal with the DRC intended to counter Chinese dominance of critical minerals supply chains.[1]
