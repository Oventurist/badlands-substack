# David Barnea

David Barnea is the former Director of [[mossad|Mossad]], Israel's foreign intelligence service, whose five-year term ended in June 2026.[1]

## Badlands analysis

Barnea has long been treated as a person of interest by the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who wrote that he had identified Barnea in 2023 as the actor in [[israel|Israel]] most likely to instigate a war with [[iran|Iran]], and assessed that Barnea achieved that objective before leaving office.[1]

In an earlier appearance on Devolution Power Hour, the same commentator argued that Mossad under Barnea had deeply infiltrated the Iranian government and was likely preparing to launch a coup to take over the country.[1] Related commentary held that the threat of an Iranian nuclear strike was a red herring and that Iran's hypersonic missiles were the true threat to Israel.[1]

## Soleimani theory

The Brief also restates a long-standing speculation that Barnea — who before becoming Director had been assigned to "track" General [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] — was in fact Soleimani's handler, and that the Iranian general was a Mossad mole.[1] Under this theory, that is why President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ordered Soleimani killed, and why Barnea reportedly telephoned Trump the night before the operation to back out and effectively try to prevent it.[1]

## Iran remarks (April 2026)

Speaking at a Holocaust Remembrance Day ceremony in April 2026, Mossad chief Barnea declared that Israel's mission "isn't over until the regime falls," stating that the radical Iranian regime "must disappear from the world."[2] He revealed that Mossad had operated inside Tehran to supply precise targeting intelligence during the campaign, and said the agency's responsibility would continue until regime change is achieved, even after the fighting ends.[2] The remarks came as President Donald Trump publicly declared the war "very close to being over," underscoring both continued Israeli-U.S. alignment on weakening Iran and a divergence over war aims beyond the shaky two-week ceasefire.[2]

## December 2023 hostage negotiations

On December 18, 2023, Barnea met in Poland with [[cia|CIA]] Director [[william-burns|William Burns]] and Qatari Prime Minister [[mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani|Mohammad bin Abdulrahman al Thani]] in the latest effort to secure the release of dozens of hostages held captive in Gaza.[3] The meeting followed bilateral talks between Barnea and al Thani the previous Friday, marking the first attempt by both sides to restart hostage negotiations after the week-long ceasefire between Israel and [[hamas|Hamas]] collapsed on December 1; Burns and Egyptian intelligence leaders were briefed on those talks.[3]

## Doha negotiations (January 2025)

As Mossad chief and head of Israel's negotiating team, Barnea was dispatched to Doha on the night of Saturday, January 11, 2025, and remained in the Qatari capital as of the afternoon of Thursday, January 16, according to an official familiar with the talks, while Israel held off on formally declaring that a ceasefire-hostage release deal had been reached with Hamas.[4]
