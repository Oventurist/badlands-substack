# David Barnea

David Barnea is an Israeli intelligence officer who served as director of [[mossad|Mossad]], ending his five-year term in mid-2026.

## Role in the Iran conflict per Badlands commentary

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief, in commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], identifies Barnea as the figure he had long considered "the most likely actor in Israel to instigate a war with [[iran|Iran]]," claiming that Barnea achieved that objective before ending his term. The commentator further alleges that Barnea — who before becoming director was assigned to "track" General [[qassem-soleimani|Qassem Soleimani]] — was actually Soleimani's handler and that the general was really a Mossad mole; this is offered as the reason President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] killed Soleimani and why, in the commentator's account, Barnea called Trump the night before the operation to try to prevent it. The same commentary argues that Mossad under Barnea had likely deeply infiltrated the Iranian government and was preparing a coup, an account it claims the reported house arrest of former President [[mahmoud-ahmadinejad|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad]] validates.
