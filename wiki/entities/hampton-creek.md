# Hampton Creek

Hampton Creek is the American food technology company that brought artificial plant-based eggs into the mainstream market.[1] In 2013, [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]] invested in the company, roughly a decade before a severe United States egg shortage pushed consumers toward plant-based egg substitutes.[1]

## Role in the 2023 egg shortage commentary

Egg prices spiked 66 percent year over year according to the Consumer Price Index, and consumers worldwide paid more at stores after more than 41 million egg-laying hens died in connection with avian flu; in response, some shoppers turned to artificial plant-based eggs.[1] The Gateway Pundit highlighted the sequence — Gates's decade-old investment in Hampton Creek preceding the shortage — as noteworthy.[1] Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] questioned the coincidence of an egg shortage occurring while Gates, who he said had invested great effort in altering American diets, was backing a plant-based egg company, offering readers the alternatives that it was coincidence, that Gates had an uncanny predictive knack, or that he was acting on globalist and eugenic motives.[1] DeLarme added that the article omitted his contention that the mass death of chickens was not caused by avian flu itself but by preemptive slaughter undertaken because of the potential threat of avian flu, arguing that fear of a virus can substitute for an effective one.[1]
