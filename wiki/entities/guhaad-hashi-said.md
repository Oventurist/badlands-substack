# Guhaad Hashi Said

Guhaad Hashi Said is a former associate and campaign "enforcer" for Democratic Minnesota Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] who pleaded guilty in August 2025 to conspiracy charges arising from a pandemic-era food fraud scheme centered on the Somali community in Minnesota.[1]

## Guilty plea

The U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Minnesota announced that Said had exploited a federally funded child nutrition program during the COVID-19 pandemic, admitting to one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and one count of conspiracy to commit money laundering.[1]

According to court documents, between December 2020 and January 2022 Said operated a Federal Child Nutrition Program site under the name [[advance-youth-athletic-development|Advance Youth Athletic Development]].[1] During that period he submitted fraudulent claims for having served more than one million meals to children, though only a small portion were actually provided, using fabricated attendance rosters, meal counts and invoices that produced more than $2.9 million in federal reimbursements.[1]

Acting U.S. Attorney Joseph H. Thompson emphasized the broader scope of the Minnesota fraud, saying, "The scale of the fraud in Minnesota is staggering, and every rock we turn over reveals more. We must be honest and clear-eyed about the scope of this problem, because ending it will take an unyielding, all-hands-on-deck effort from all of us."[1]
