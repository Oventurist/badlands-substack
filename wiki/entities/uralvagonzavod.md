# Uralvagonzavod

**Research and Production Corporation "Uralvagonzavod"** is one of the largest manufacturers of military equipment in Russia, based in Nizhny Tagil in the Ural region, producing tanks as well as tank support combat vehicles and armored engineering vehicles.[1]

## Captured Leopard 2A6 analysis (2024)

In October 2024, Sputnik reported that a German Leopard 2A6 tank destroyed during the Russian special military operation in Ukraine had been delivered to Uralvagonzavod in Nizhny Tagil for study.[1] "After conducting detailed analysis, an expert assessment will be given of the military-technical capabilities of various systems and the destroyed tank as a whole," the enterprise's press service said.[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the transfer illustrated a structural failure of the Western war effort: Russia had captured a [[nato|NATO]] tank and planned to study it in order to destroy such vehicles more efficiently, meaning the alliance was "not only wasting money, resources, and lives on this doomed war effort" but also giving its adversary every opportunity to research and develop the best techniques to kill NATO soldiers.[1] He placed the episode in a broader claim that Russia was using the war to innovate the next generation of combined-arms doctrine for maneuver warfare, including tactics pairing infantry with drones to eliminate armor — tanks, artillery and armored personnel carriers — as efficiently as possible.[1]
