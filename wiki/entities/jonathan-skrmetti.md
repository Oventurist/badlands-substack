# Jonathan Skrmetti

Jonathan Skrmetti is the Attorney General of the U.S. state of [[tennessee|Tennessee]], and one of the most active Republican state law-enforcement officials in the legal campaign against environmental, social and corporate governance investing.[1]

## BlackRock lawsuit

In December 2023, Skrmetti's office sued [[blackrock|BlackRock]], the world's largest asset manager, alleging that the firm breached Tennessee consumer protection laws by making "misleading" statements about its [[esg|ESG]] investment strategies.[1] According to the court filing, Tennessee alleged that BlackRock downplayed the extent to which ESG considerations drive the firm's investment decisions, as well as the effect of those considerations on portfolio companies' financial performance and outlook.[1] BlackRock rejected the claims, stating that it "fully and accurately discloses our investment practices and our approach to proxy voting."[1]

## Broader ESG investigation

The suit followed a wider inquiry. Earlier in 2023, Skrmetti demanded that ten major asset managers provide information about how they seek to address climate change, as part of an investigation into potential breaches of consumer law.[1] In March of that year, Skrmetti and twenty other Republican state attorneys general wrote to asset managers suggesting that they were breaching their fiduciary duties in their handling of environmental and social issues.[1] The actions formed part of a growing Republican challenge to ESG, drawn heavily from energy-producing states, and stood in contrast to Democratic support for the practice — including President Joe Biden's use of the first veto of his presidency to defend a federal rule on ESG investing.[1]
