# Colorado Education Association

The Colorado Education Association (CEA) is the statewide teachers' union in Colorado.[1]

## 2023 resolution condemning capitalism

In May 2023 the CEA passed a resolution affirming its belief that "capitalism inherently exploits children, public schools, land, labor, and resources."[1] The adopted text stated that "CEA believes that capitalism requires exploitation of children, public schools, land, labor, and/or resources," and that "capitalism is in opposition to fully addressing systemic racism (the school to prison pipeline), climate change, patriarchy, (gender and LGBTQ disparities), education inequality, and income inequality."[1]

A screenshot of an earlier draft of the resolution showed that the union had originally called for capitalism to be replaced with a "new equitable economic system"; that language was removed from the final statement.[1]

## Reaction

Commentary in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief invoked the phrase "you will own nothing, and you will be happy" and argued that the resolution exemplified the indoctrination of children toward dependence on government.[1] The writer contended that under what he termed the "supreme woke" framework success is recast as privilege, that "the nail that stands up gets hammered down," and that such ideology explained declining American educational leadership.[1] The commentary closed by arguing that children should be taught to reach for the stars rather than be limited by virtue-signaling talking points injected into schools.[1]
