# Rabobank

**Rabobank** is a Dutch multinational banking and financial services cooperative whose research arm publishes widely circulated macroeconomic and geopolitical commentary.[1]

## Role in Badlands coverage

Rabobank strategist [[michael-every|Michael Every]] authored the analysis of the [[2023-macron-state-visit-to-china|2023 Macron state visit to China]] that was republished by ZeroHedge under the headline "France Has Already Surrendered To The Coming Conflict" and excerpted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief.[1] The piece argued that the [[european-union|European Union]] was split between states seeking deeper Chinese ties — France, Germany, and Spain — and those looking to the United States, and that this divide could become a schism as national-security concerns overtake economics.[1] It cited Rabobank's own research note "Balance of payments — and power — crises" in arguing that Europe already occupied a structurally weak, emerging-market-like position in a raw geopolitical world.[1]
