# Equality State Policy Center

The **Equality State Policy Center** is a Wyoming advocacy organization that served as plaintiff in a federal lawsuit challenging the state's voter proof-of-citizenship requirement.[1]

## Litigation against Wyoming House Bill 156

The group sued Wyoming Secretary of State [[chuck-gray|Chuck Gray]] over [[wyoming-house-bill-156|Wyoming House Bill 156]], the law requiring voters to demonstrate U.S. citizenship and state residency in order to register.[1] It was represented by Democratic election attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]].[1] On July 22, 2025, U.S. District Court Judge Scott Skavdahl dismissed the case, a result Gray characterized as a defeat for "Marc Elias and the radical left."[1]
