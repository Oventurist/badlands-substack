# John Bel Edwards

John Bel Edwards is a Democratic politician who served as governor of [[louisiana|Louisiana]] and was, at the time of the October 2023 gubernatorial election, the only sitting Democratic governor in the Deep South.[1]

## Term limit and succession

Edwards reached his term limit in 2023 and was succeeded by Republican [[jeff-landry|Jeff Landry]], the state attorney general, who won the gubernatorial race outright in the October 14 jungle primary by taking more than 50 percent of the vote.[1] Landry's victory handed Republicans control of Louisiana's top executive office for the first time since 2016.[1]

Commentary from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described the preceding eight years under Edwards as a period of divided government in which a Republican legislature contended with a veto-heavy Democratic governor, and framed the 2023 flip as clearing the way for Republicans to advance their agenda for the state.[1]
