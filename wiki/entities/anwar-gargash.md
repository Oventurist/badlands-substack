# Anwar Gargash

Anwar Gargash is a presidential adviser of the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]].[1]

## Response to the Barakah drone strike

After a drone struck an electrical generator near the [[barakah-nuclear-power-plant|Barakah nuclear power plant]] on 17 May 2026, Gargash called the incident a "dangerous escalation" and said it violated international law and norms.[1] The UAE attributed the attack to [[iran|Iran]] or allied proxy groups, while Emirati officials said investigations into the source of the drones were continuing.[1]
