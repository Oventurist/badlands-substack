# Ron Rowe

Ron Rowe is a career United States Secret Service official who served as deputy director of the agency and was appointed acting director following the July 2024 resignation of Director [[kimberly-cheatle|Kimberly Cheatle]].[1]

## Appointment as acting director

Cheatle resigned on the morning of Tuesday, July 23, 2024, after intense scrutiny over the [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]]'s failure to prevent the July 13, 2024 assassination attempt on former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at a rally in Pennsylvania.[1] Rowe, then the agency's deputy director, was named acting director in the immediate aftermath.[1]

In a message to Secret Service employees obtained by [[abc-news|ABC News]], Rowe wrote: "We will restore the faith and confidence of the American public and the people we are entrusted to protect."[1]

## Background

Rowe is described as a 24-year veteran of the U.S. Secret Service, making him a long-tenured insider elevated at a moment when the agency faced its most severe credibility crisis in decades.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributors framed the leadership change as part of a broader accountability fight rather than a resolution, arguing that Cheatle's resignation followed her perceived contempt and obstruction before Congress and that the underlying questions about the assassination attempt remained unanswered.[1]
