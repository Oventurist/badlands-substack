# Dustin Stockton

Dustin Stockton is an American political activist and organizer, best known for his involvement in the "We Build the Wall" private border wall campaign.[1] With his wife [[jennifer-lynn-lawrence|Jennifer Lynn Lawrence]] he runs [[america-mission|America Mission]], an organizing project framed as resistance to the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] and the [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1]

A February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay describes Stockton and Lawrence as heavy users of [[twitter-spaces|Twitter Spaces]] for activism, community building, and localized organizing, running sessions that range from structured meetings with agendas and action items to conspiracy deep dives, late-night social conversation, and educational programming such as a cryptocurrency and NFT primer.[1]
