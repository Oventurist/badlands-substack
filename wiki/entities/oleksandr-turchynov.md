# Oleksandr Turchynov

Oleksandr Turchynov is a Ukrainian politician who served as interim president of Ukraine following the removal of [[viktor-yanukovych|Viktor Yanukovych]] in 2014.[1]

## Badlands characterisation

In commentary on President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s statement that Russia was "forced" into its military operation, Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] asserted that the Western Hegemony began the war by overthrowing the Ukrainian government in 2014 and installing Turchynov — whom he described as a member of the "Israeli Mafia" — as interim president until a new election could be organised.[1] This characterisation is a Badlands editorial claim rather than a sourced news report, and is presented in the brief without documentary substantiation.[1]

Turchynov's tenure followed the [[euromaidan|Euromaidan]] events in Kiev in early 2014, in which initially peaceful protests escalated into riots and clashes with police, culminating in Yanukovych's ouster.[1]

## Alleged organized-crime ties

Badlands commentary in the September 10, 2024 News Brief went further than the standard account, alleging that in February 2014, after the [[cia|CIA]] "overthrew the government of Ukraine in the Maidan Coup," it installed Turchynov — described as "a high-ranking member of the Israeli Mafia" — to serve as interim president until [[petro-poroshenko|Petro Poroshenko]] could be elected.[2] The piece asserted that Turchynov was a henchman for crime lord [[semion-mogilevich|Semion Mogilevich]], and that while working for Ukrainian intelligence Turchynov was subpoenaed to appear in Ukrainian court in 2010, accused of destroying files pertaining to Mogilevich.[2]
