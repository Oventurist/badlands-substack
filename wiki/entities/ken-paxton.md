# Ken Paxton

Ken Paxton is the Attorney General of Texas.[1]

In November 2023 Paxton opened an investigation into [[media-matters|Media Matters]] for potential fraudulent activity, following the organization's publication of a study alleging that X had placed major brands' advertisements alongside pro-Nazi content.[1] The investigation, together with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s "thermonuclear lawsuit" and the statement issued by Rumble founder [[chris-pavlovski|Chris Pavlovski]], is presented as a sign that the boomerang is coming back around to [[david-brock|David Brock]] and Media Matters.[1]
