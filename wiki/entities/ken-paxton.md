# Ken Paxton

**Ken Paxton** is identified in the Badlands Media corpus as the Texas Attorney General who opened an investigation into [[Media Matters]] for potential fraudulent activity in the wake of Media Matters' November 2023 study about advertising on X (formerly Twitter). The source frames the investigation as part of a broader "boomerang" effect in which institutions that participated in the censorship regime are beginning to face legal consequences.

## Investigation Into Media Matters

The article reports that Paxton's investigation was opened in response to Media Matters' study that purported to prove major advertisers were regularly associated with neo-Nazi or antisemitic content on X. The source alleges that the study was methodologically flawed, with Media Matters staff allegedly refreshing feeds on obscure posts until ads appeared beside them, then documenting the juxtaposition. Paxton's investigation is framed as examining whether this methodology constituted fraudulent activity.

## Broader Legal Context

The source connects Paxton's investigation to the "thermonuclear lawsuit" filed by [[Elon Musk]] against Media Matters and the statement by [[Rumble]] founder [[Chris Pavlovski]] that Rumble would "go thermonuclear as well." The article expresses hope that other institutions — including the Poynter Institute, the [[Anti-Defamation League]], the Southern Poverty Law Center, and the Center for Countering Digital Hate — will "all receive their own day of legal judgment."
