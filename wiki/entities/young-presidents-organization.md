# Young Presidents' Organization

The Young Presidents' Organization (YPO) is an international network of young chief executives. It became relevant to the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]] story because of a YPO conference held in Moscow in October 2016 titled "Inside The Kremlin."[1]

[[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles "Chuck" Dolan Jr.]] was asked to get involved in the YPO effort by Steve Kupka, a Washington, D.C.-based lawyer and partner at King & Spalding, who wanted to organize the Moscow conference and turned to Dolan for his extensive Russian experience and contacts — specifically his connections with the Russian government.[1] During the spring of 2016 Dolan communicated with [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] about the YPO and the conference, telling the organizer that "Mr. Danchenko could be useful to us on this."[1]

Both Dolan and Danchenko attended the October 2016 Moscow conference, as did Russian government officials.[1] After the conference both men returned to the United States, where they also "had occasion to meet," according to trial testimony that the article's author treated skeptically.[1] Assistant Special Counsel [[michael-keilty|Michael Keilty]] used the conference to establish the continuing and substantive nature of the Dolan–Danchenko relationship during the period the dossier was being compiled.[1]
