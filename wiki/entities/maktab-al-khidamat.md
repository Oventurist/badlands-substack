# Maktab al-Khidamat

Maktab al-Khidamat (MAK) was the precursor organization run by [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] before the formation of [[al-qaeda]] in 1988.[1]

In his book *Prelude to Terror*, investigative reporter Joseph Trento wrote, citing a former CIA officer who filed reports on the matter, that "CIA money was actually funneled to MAK, since it was recruiting young Muslim men to come to join the jihad in Afghanistan."[1] NBC's Michael Moran similarly stated that "the MAK was nurtured by Pakistan's state security services, the Inter-Services Intelligence agency, or ISI, the CIA's primary conduit for conducting the covert war against Moscow's occupation."[1]

MAK is therefore central to Badlands Media's argument that the recruitment and radicalization pipeline feeding the [[mujahideen]] during [[operation-cyclone|Operation Cyclone]] ran through the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and Pakistan's [[inter-services-intelligence|ISI]], with the latter acting as a middleman so that Western intelligence could deny involvement.[1]
