# Cartel de los Soles

Cartel de los Soles ("Cartel of the Suns") is the network the United States government alleges operates as a drug-trafficking organization embedded within the Venezuelan state, including President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolas Maduro]] and other high-ranking officials.[1]

## Terrorist designation

On Monday, November 24, 2025, the United States formally designated Cartel de los Soles as a foreign terrorist organization (FTO), layering additional terrorism-related sanctions on the group.[1] Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] had announced earlier that month that the designation was coming, citing the network's alleged role in importing illegal drugs into the United States.[1]

Venezuela's government rejected what it called a "ridiculous" US plan to designate a group it described as "non-existent."[1]

## Military escalation concerns

The designation arrived as Maduro faced escalating pressure from President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s US military buildup in the Caribbean, raising concerns that Washington might use the FTO label to justify military action.[1] Sanctions experts countered that the statute underlying the designation does not authorize such a move.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the Deep State was "licking its lips in anticipation of another violent coup," while social media influencers marched to the beat of the war drums.[1] He highlighted Maduro's claim in an interview that 85% of the cartels' cash is laundered through major US banks, and that the cartels succeed only because they work with people inside the US government.[1] [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] echoed the banking claim — "The entire fiat banking system is a fraud" — and noted the "amazing timing" with the markets, the crypto story, and "the fake war the Regime is begging for."[1]

## Invoked as basis for potential strikes (November 2025)

In November 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] cited the [[state-department|State Department]]'s designation of Cartel de los Soles as a foreign terrorist organization as legal grounding for potential U.S. strikes on Venezuelan soil, saying, "It allows us to do that, but we haven't said we're going to do that."[2] He made the remark while also indicating openness to negotiations with Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], whom he has accused of operating "narcoterrorist" cartels — an allegation Maduro denies.[2]

## Terrorist designation and Venezuela strikes

Following the collapse of the reported Trump–[[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]] phone negotiation in late November 2025, the Trump administration officially designated the Cartel de los Soles — which it accuses Maduro and his top officials of leading — as a terrorist organization, a move described as paving the way for strikes inside Venezuela.[3] The designation coincided with Trump's declaration that Venezuela's airspace should be considered "closed."[3]

## Foreign terrorist organization designation (November 2025)

On Monday, November 24, 2025, the U.S. Treasury formally designated the Cartel of the Suns — described as a purported criminal network alleged to operate within [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s security services — as a foreign terrorist organization, placing it in the same category as Al-Qaeda and Islamic State.[4] In announcing the step, Treasury reiterated long-standing allegations that [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolas Maduro]], whose legitimacy Washington disputes, heads the group.[4] Despite the designation, Axios reported the following day that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] planned to speak directly with Maduro, with an official saying the near-term plan was to "blow up boats shipping drugs" rather than to strike Maduro personally.[4]

## Named in the 2025 Maduro reward announcement

Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]], announcing a $50 million reward for information leading to the arrest of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] on August 7, 2025, named the Cartel of the Suns together with [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] and the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] as the foreign terrorist organizations Maduro allegedly used to flood the United States with narcotics.[5] She said the [[drug-enforcement-administration|DEA]] had seized thirty tons of cocaine tied to Maduro and his network, nearly seven tons of it directly linked to him.[5]
