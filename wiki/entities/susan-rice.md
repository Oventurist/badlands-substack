# Susan Rice

Susan Rice is a veteran Democratic official who served in senior national security and domestic policy roles under [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], and who figures prominently in Badlands commentary as a potential vehicle for continued Obama-aligned influence over the executive branch.[1]

## 2020 vice-presidential vetting and tension with Harris

Reporting by [[axios|Axios]] in February 2024 on the Biden administration's handling of the southern border noted that tension between Rice and Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] originated in the summer of 2020, when both women were being vetted as Biden's running mate.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the anonymously sourced Axios piece as a narrative deployment rather than ordinary journalism, asking why a previously unreported January 2023 meeting was being surfaced at that particular moment.[1] The scenario she sketched was a ticket swap: Harris would be given a face-saving exit, Rice would join the ticket promising a "strong approach" on the border, and the campaign would present her addition as a restoration of the Obama years.[1] Should such a ticket win, Ashe argued, the vice president and a cabinet majority could then invoke the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]] against Biden, elevating Rice to the presidency — in her framing, "a realistic and achievable plan for the next phase of Obama's shadow presidency."[1] She acknowledged this was a great deal to extrapolate from a single Axios article.[1]
