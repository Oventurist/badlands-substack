# David Becker

David Becker is the election-administration figure associated with the [[electronic-registration-information-center|Electronic Registration Information Center]] (ERIC) and, separately, with the Center for Election Innovation and Research (CEIR).[1] In Badlands election-integrity coverage he is treated as the connective figure between the multi-state voter-roll data consortium and the nonprofit sector.[1]

## Deposition in Wisconsin litigation

Following the denial of the [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]]'s motion to dismiss in Waukesha County Case Number 2024CV001544, *Janel Brandtjen v. Wisconsin Elections Commission, Meagan Wolfe*, researcher [[peter-bernegger|Peter Bernegger]] indicated that the plaintiffs planned to depose Becker as part of full discovery.[1] Bernegger alleged that the Wisconsin commission and ERIC were "unlawfully giving away voters['] personally identifiable information to CEIR (Becker's other company)," characterizing the transfer of Social Security numbers, birthdates and driver's license numbers without voter notification as criminal conduct affecting residents of every ERIC state.[1]

## Calls for prosecution

Writing in September 2024 after a federal court ordered Colorado to disclose [[electronic-registration-information-center|ERIC]] reports, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that if the saga of ERIC "is to end justly, it must end with the criminal indictment of David Becker and his inter-state collaborators," describing the arrangement as "a house of cards" with the wind blowing.[2]
