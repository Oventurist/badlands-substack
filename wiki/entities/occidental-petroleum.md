# Occidental Petroleum

Occidental Petroleum is the American oil company associated with [[armand-hammer|Armand Hammer]] (1898–1990), described in Badlands Media coverage as the most powerful of the Western magnates granted full access to buy up Russian assets under the [[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy]].[1]

Through Hammer, the company's name is tied to the 1920s wave of Soviet–Western business dealings in which Western corporate powers acquired former national utilities for pennies on the dollar.[1] Hammer's access to Russia ended with the ouster of [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] and resumed only after [[joseph-stalin|Joseph Stalin]]'s death in 1953.[1]
