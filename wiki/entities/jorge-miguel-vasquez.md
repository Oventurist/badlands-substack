# Jorge Miguel Vasquez

**Jorge Miguel Vasquez** is a [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] executive named by United States federal prosecutors as a participant in an alleged scheme to bribe Philippine election officials in exchange for voting technology contracts.[1]

## Allegations

A superseding indictment filed in Miami federal court in October 2025 alleges that Vasquez, together with Smartmatic co-founder [[roger-pinate|Roger Alejandro Piñate Martinez]], orchestrated over $1 million in bribes to [[juan-andres-donato-bautista|Juan Andres Donato Bautista]], then Chairman of the Philippines' [[comelec|Commission on Elections]], between 2015 and 2018.[1] The payments were allegedly designed to secure and maintain contracts for SGO Corporation Limited connected to the administration of the 2016 Philippine presidential election, including favorable VAT reimbursements and other government payments.[1]

Prosecutors say the money for the bribes was generated by inflating the cost of voting machines supplied for the 2016 elections, and that the participants disguised the transactions through coded language, fictitious contracts and loan agreements, and bank accounts in Asia, Europe, and the Southern District of Florida.[1]
