# Game Theory (show)

Game Theory is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Thursdays at 12pm ET in the January 2023 schedule.[1]

## Format and hosts

The show is co-hosted by [[sean-morgan|Sean Morgan]] and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who take a macro view of national and international events through the lens of game theory and [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]], attempting to find the truth beneath the surface of reported events.[1] The analytical frame overlaps with Burning Bright's Sunday-night program [[defected|Defected]], which applies narrative warfare and game theory to current events.[1]

## Distribution

Live streams and replays air on the main Badlands Media [[rumble|Rumble]] channel.[1]
