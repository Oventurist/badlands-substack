# Luis Delgado

Luis Delgado is a Florida circuit judge who in mid-2024 ordered the public release of a roughly 150-page transcript from the 2006 Palm Beach County grand jury investigation into [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

The release was made possible by a 2024 Florida statute that legalized publication of grand jury transcripts relating to Epstein.[1] In his accompanying order, Delgado wrote that "details in the record will be outrageous to decent people," and observed that "the story of how Jeffrey Epstein victimized some of Palm Beach County's most vulnerable has been the subject of much anger and has at times diminished the public's perception of the criminal justice system."[1]

The transcript established that prosecutors were aware Epstein had sexually abused underage girls two years before the lenient 2008 plea agreement that resulted in a single count of solicitation of prostitution from a minor, despite evidence of a broader pattern of statutory rape.[1] Commentary published by ZeroHedge argued that the resulting slap on the wrist enabled Epstein's continued trafficking operations until his death in custody in 2019.[1]
