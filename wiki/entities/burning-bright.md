# Burning Bright

Burning Bright is the pen name of a writer and contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] whose work applies fifth-generation warfare framing to political and technological developments.[1]

## The AI Shadow War

On the Devolution Power Hour in late July 2026, Burning Bright argued at length about "the topic that almost nobody in the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] is paying attention to right now," which he believes "has more to do with alternatively starting or stopping the American Golden Age than almost anything else we spend our time arguing about."[1] He claimed the concerted effort by [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] of [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]] of [[anthropic|Anthropic]] to seek government regulation was an attempt to slow and control the AI push, putting it "in collectivist, oligarchic hands," making the two men "retardants" rather than "accelerants" to the AI agenda.[1] That distinction, he argued, explains how audiences can be broadly correct in their anxieties about AI's "serpentine" core personalities while missing that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is pinning much of his futurism agenda on AI's acceleration.[1]

He cited three weekend developments as making his takes "age rapidly, and quite well": former White House AI and Crypto Czar [[david-sacks|David Sacks]] echoing his framing almost beat for beat; Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] reportedly saying the administration aims to "protect open source AI"; and [[china|China]] timing strategic releases of open-weight models that accelerate the industry while blunting Western attempts to slow it, granting Trump narrative shielding.[1] His conclusion was that China is "actually HELPING protect the future of an open-source, open-weight, FREE AI world," and that it is a "good thing Trump and [[xi-jinping|Xi]] are Sovereign Bros."[1]
