# Burning Bright

Burning Bright is the pen name of a writer and contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] who also publishes an independent Substack newsletter of the same name. According to the author's own description, the Burning Bright newsletter focuses on "Narrative Deployments and Macro Concepts in Fifth-Generation Warfare," and its content is offered free to readers.

## Work in the corpus

Burning Bright is the author of the November 16, 2022 Badlands Media essay [[all-assets-deployed|All Assets Deployed]], subtitled "Separating the MAGA Wheat from the Chaff," written in the immediate aftermath of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2024 campaign announcement at [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]]. That essay references an earlier Burning Bright piece on the "Narrative Mastery of one Donald J. Trump," published before the Mar-a-Lago speech, in which the author anticipated the "turn" of [[con-inc|Con Inc.]] and portions of the alternative right against the [[america-first|America First]] movement.

## Perspective

The author's writing is characterized by a [[5th-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] framework in which political events are read as moves in an [[information-war|information war]] waged both against and for the American public. He identifies himself as an adherent of the [[devolution|Devolution]] thesis advanced by [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], and as a proponent of "precipice" theories holding that public conditions must worsen before a critical mass of citizens awakens. His essays frequently counsel patience against both "Doomer" despair and unrealistic expectations, and close with the recurring sign-off "stay Positive, stay Based and most importantly … stay Bright."

## Editorial role

The July 2023 feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]] by [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] closes with a joking reference to Burning Bright in an editorial capacity: the author notes that had he made the piece any longer, "Burning Bright would fire me, or at the very least suggest an untimely and happenstance meeting with his wolf." An appended editor's note replies: "this is correct. PiP has been warned."

## Blitz hosting

Burning Bright co-hosted [[badlands-blitz-episode-11|Badlands Blitz Episode 11]] on March 21, 2026 with [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], using the Iran War narrative to map an anticipated "strategic pincer" by the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] against the [[invisible-enemy|Invisible Enemy]], and warning against "playing white hat-black hat games" with the figures around [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on the [[5th-generation-warfare|fifth-generation]] game board.

A week later he returned to the program with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] for [[badlands-blitz-episode-12|Badlands Blitz Episode 12]], "The Fake and Gay, but Also Real and Serious War," published March 28, 2026. That episode used the contradictory accounts circulating about [[ali-khamenei|Ayatollah Ali Khamenei]] as a point of departure for a discussion of [[narrative-warfare|Narrative Warfare]], [[sovereign-disentanglement|Sovereign Disentanglement]] and [[first-principles|First Principles]], arguing that both the wider world and the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] had forgotten them.
