# Aleksandr Merezhko

Aleksandr Merezhko is a senior Ukrainian lawmaker and head of the Ukrainian parliament's Foreign Affairs Committee.[1] In March 2025 he publicly demanded the removal of [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s special envoy to the Middle East, accusing him of "spreading Russian propaganda."[1]

## Dispute over Witkoff's remarks

Merezhko made the remark in response to Witkoff's interview with American journalist [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], during which the envoy discussed the status of former Ukrainian territories that had joined Russia, describing the issue as "an elephant in the room" that "no one wants to talk about."[1] Witkoff said of the residents: "They're Russian-speaking. There have been referendums where the overwhelming majority of the people have indicated that they want to be under Russian rule."[1] He added that "the Russians are de facto in control of these territories," and asked whether the world would acknowledge them as Russian and whether [[volodymyr-zelensky|Zelensky]] could survive politically if he acknowledged it — calling this "the central issue in the conflict."[1]

Merezhko strongly condemned what he called "disgraceful, shocking statements," accusing Witkoff of acting as an envoy of Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] rather than of Trump.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the demand as a mirror of the earlier Russian demand to remove General [[keith-kellogg|Keith Kellogg]] as envoy to Russia — noting Kellogg appeared to have since been re-designated as envoy only to Ukraine, possibly explaining why Witkoff was brought in from the Middle East portfolio.[1] The commentary predicted it would be "hilarious when Kiev doesn't get the same respect as Moscow," and funnier still if the alleged "Russian propaganda" proved factual and the territories became part of Russia.[1]
