# Arturo Galansino

Arturo Galansino is an Italian art curator who curated the [[ai-weiwei|Ai Weiwei]] exhibition at Palazzo Fava in Bologna.[1]

## Statement on the Porcelain Cube destruction

After a man shattered Ai's "Porcelain Cube" sculpture at the exhibition's September 2024 opening, Galansino said the perpetrator was well known within the art world: "Unfortunately, I know the author of this inconsiderate gesture from a series of disturbing and damaging episodes over the years involving various exhibitions and institutions in Florence."[1] Bologna police subsequently arrested a 57-year-old Czech man.[1] Badlands Media noted that Galansino's claim of the man's notoriety was, in the absence of any published identity or motive, effectively the only characterization available to the public.[1]
