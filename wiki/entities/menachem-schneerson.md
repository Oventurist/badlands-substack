# Menachem Schneerson

Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, known as the Lubavitcher Rebbe, was the leader of the Chabad-Lubavitch Hasidic movement.[1] He appears in Badlands Media commentary chiefly for a filmed encounter with [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] that is treated as a key to understanding the Israeli prime minister's sense of personal destiny.[1]

## Meeting with Netanyahu

Roughly 34 years before November 2024 — at a time when Netanyahu was serving as Israel's Foreign Minister — Schneerson met him on camera and said that "he, Benjamin Netanyahu, will be Israel's prime minister, who will pass the scepter to the Messiah."[1]

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited the remark alongside Netanyahu's pedigree as the son of [[zeev-jabotinsky|Ze'ev Jabotinsky]]'s personal secretary and his self-presentation as heir to the 18th-century Vilna Gaon, arguing that this accumulated messianic framing explains both Netanyahu's power projection and the writer's fear that he is prepared to bring about great cataclysm to fulfil "the destiny that has been prophesied in his ear throughout his life."[1]
