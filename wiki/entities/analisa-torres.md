# Analisa Torres

Analisa Torres is a United States District Judge for the Southern District of New York in Manhattan.[1]

## Ruling in the Ashley Biden diary case

On Thursday, December 28, 2023, Torres ruled in favor of a special master's recommendation that the investigative journalism outfit [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]] must turn over all documents in its possession detailing how it came into possession of the alleged diary of [[ashley-biden|Ashley Biden]], daughter of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], in the fall of 2020.[1] She rejected Project Veritas's claim that journalistic non-disclosure privileges under the First Amendment shielded those records from disclosure.[1]

As a result of the ruling, federal prosecutors stood to obtain more than 900 documents describing the organization's acquisition of the diary.[1] Torres ordered a government evidentiary filter team to separate out any materials protected by attorney-client privilege and to turn the remainder over to government investigators by January 5.[1]

The dispute originated in the fall of 2021, when federal agents executed search warrants at the homes of several Project Veritas employees, including founder and then-CEO [[james-okeefe|James O'Keefe]].[1] Project Veritas argued that the seizure violated its First Amendment rights as a press organization and that investigators should be compelled to return the seized records.[1]
