# Vote from Abroad

Vote from Abroad is the progressive non-governmental organization through which voter registration for American expatriate voters is contracted, and which became a focus of election-integrity criticism during the 2024 United States election cycle.[1]

## Role in the 2024 expatriate voting dispute

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], reporting alongside [[brian-lupo|Brian Lupo]] on the program [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]], argued that overseas voter registration handled through Vote from Abroad appears to occur without eligibility checks, such that in principle anyone could register to vote in a U.S. election through the interface without their eligibility being verified.[1]

This criticism is embedded in a wider argument about the composition of the overseas ballot pool. Military voters, in this framing, are a highly controlled and easily verifiable population, whereas the population of Americans living abroad is ambiguous — raising the question of why the two populations are combined from an election-integrity standpoint.[1] Badlands contends that Democrats had been seeding a narrative since August 2024 that an increase in expatriate votes would decide the election, and that a large announced push to expand expat voting amounts to an effort to dilute the military vote.[1] The site predicted that any resulting outrage over turnout spikes among overseas voters would be reframed by institutions as voter suppression.[1]
