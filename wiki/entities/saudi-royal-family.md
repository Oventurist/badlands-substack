# Saudi Royal Family

The **Saudi Royal Family** — the ruling House of Saud of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] — is treated in the Badlands Media "Q for Dummies" series as one of the principal power structures targeted in the November 2017 Q drops, with its "takedown" presented as imminent at the point in the drops covered by Chapter 8.[1]

## Framing in the Q drops

Chapter 8 opens by telling readers that the drops are about to reach the takedown of the Saudi Royal Family, and returns to that theme repeatedly across drops #61–67.[1] The series reads the phrase "Alice & Wonderland" in Drop #62 as a reference to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and Saudi Arabia.[1]

## Alleged conduct

The article characterizes Saudi Arabia as known for egregious human rights violations, for bribing politicians worldwide, and as the location of major Holy Sites of Islam, while allowing that the country also has "lovely people" and a "lovely culture."[1] The author's claim is that Saudi Arabia has long been used as a hotbed and a port for the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1] Saudi donations to the Democratic National Committee are described as astronomical, with a further connection asserted to the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] that the series defers to later chapters.[1]

## 9/11 connections

Discussing Drop #67, the series argues that the Saudi Royal Family was deeply complicit in harming the United States, pointing to Judicial Watch FOIA material indicating that a Saudi national was permitted to leave the United States on 9/11 despite the nationwide grounding of flights, and that dozens of members of the Bin Laden family were flown out in the days afterward — approvals the author says must have come from the highest level.[1] See [[2001-saudi-evacuation-flights|the 2001 Saudi evacuation flights]].[1]

## Kushner and the smear timing

Drop #67 asks anons to discover why [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] traveled to Saudi Arabia; the series notes Kushner went on to play a crucial role in Middle East relations during the Trump administration.[1] An article smearing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Kushner is read as a [[operation-mockingbird|Mockingbird]] media pre-emption, released because "all hell" was about to break loose in Saudi Arabia.[1]
