# National Labor Relations Board

The National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) is an independent federal labor board whose membership became a test case for presidential removal power during the second Trump administration.[1]

## 2025 removal litigation

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] fired NLRB member [[gwynne-wilcox|Gwynne Wilcox]], and a federal district court voided the removal and restored her to the board.[1] The [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] asked the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] on April 9, 2025 to block that decision along with a parallel ruling reinstating [[cathy-harris|Cathy Harris]] at the [[merit-systems-protection-board|Merit Systems Protection Board]], and Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] issued an administrative stay allowing both removals to go back into effect while the Court weighed the request.[1] Solicitor General [[d-john-sauer|D. John Sauer]] framed the reinstatements as an injury to the president and to the separation of powers.[1]
