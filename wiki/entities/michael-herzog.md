# Michael Herzog

Michael Herzog served as Israel's ambassador to the United States during the Israel–Hamas war and the associated escalation with [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] on Israel's northern border.[1]

## Role in Israel–Lebanon ceasefire diplomacy

In November 2024, according to Israel's Channel 13, U.S. envoy [[amos-hochstein|Amos Hochstein]] informed Herzog that Washington would withdraw from mediation of the Israel–Lebanon ceasefire process if Israel failed to respond positively to the recent American ceasefire proposal.[1] The warning was reportedly delivered during Hochstein's visit the previous week, following a two-day trip to Beirut.[1]

The exchange occurred against a backdrop in which Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had demanded freedom to conduct military operations in southern Lebanon as a condition of any ceasefire — a condition rejected by Lebanese Parliament Speaker [[nabih-berri|Nabih Berri]] and challenged by Hezbollah Secretary-General [[naim-qassem|Naim Qassem]], who said the group was negotiating on the conditions of a complete cessation of Israeli aggression and preservation of Lebanese sovereignty.[1]
