# Transnistria

**Transnistria** is a predominantly Russian-speaking breakaway territory that declared its independence from [[moldova|Moldova]] during the collapse of the Soviet Union.[1] Roughly 60 percent of its ethnic population is composed of Russians and Ukrainians, and more than 220,000 [[russia|Russian]] citizens reside there.[1]

## Appeal to Russia (February 2024)

In late February 2024 Transnistrian lawmakers formally asked Russia for assistance in the face of mounting economic pressure from Moldova.[1] The legislators addressed their request to both the Federation Council and the State Duma of Russia, urging them to implement measures to safeguard Transnistria in light of what they described as Moldova's growing pressure, and citing the presence of the large resident Russian citizenry as grounds for intervention.[1]

The authors of the bill pointed to what they called the unique and positive experience of Russia's peacekeeping work in the region and emphasized that Moscow had served as a mediator in negotiations.[1] Their declaration stated that "the critical situation requires urgent and maximum active international intervention in order to prevent an escalation of tensions and not allow the situation to develop into a crisis."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that the plea Transnistria made to the Russian Federation — consistent with referenda the region has held in favour of such a move dating back to 2006 — was a near-identical scenario to the Donbas region of eastern Ukraine circa 2014.[1] He held that US warnings to the region about Russian interventionism were on their face escalatory, and that President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had already responded with warnings in kind, telling the western hegemon that intervention in Russian affairs, and in those Russia deems its own, would be met in kind.[1]

Burning Bright found it most notable that globalist institutions appeared to anticipate the sequence, having warned EU citizens of Russia's inevitable "invasion" of Moldova as far back as 2022.[1] He also observed that the Council of Europe formally "upgraded the status" of Transnistria in 2023 from territory "under the effective control of the Russian Federation" to "Russian-occupied territory," and summarized the affair with the Hegelian formula "Problem > Reaction ..."[1]
