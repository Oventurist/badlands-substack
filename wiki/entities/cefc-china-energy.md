# CEFC China Energy

CEFC China Energy was one of [[china]]'s largest energy firms. The Badlands Media essay [[and-iran-iran-so-far-away|"And Iran, Iran So Far Away"]] cites it as the source of millions of dollars in payments to members of the Biden family, referencing a House Oversight Committee release on Biden family members receiving payments from a Chinese energy company.

The article uses this history to argue that [[joe-biden]]'s decision to permit China's $400 billion agreement with [[iran]] within two months of taking office — an outcome the author calls a "geostrategic nightmare" — was not mere incompetence: "when you consider that the Biden family has a now very public history of making money off of China, it starts to seem intentional."
