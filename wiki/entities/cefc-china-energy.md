# CEFC China Energy

CEFC China Energy was a Shanghai-based energy conglomerate with extensive ties to Chinese state and party structures, and the counterparty in a series of business dealings involving members of the Biden family that have been the subject of sustained scrutiny in independent media.[1]

## Ireland meeting, September 2017

Commentary by [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] published in the Badlands News Brief documents that [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[jim-biden|Jim Biden]] met with members of "the CEFC group" during a September 2017 trip to Ireland that overlapped with a visit by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] The meeting, dated 13 September 2017, was held "off site" because of the "sensitivity of the discussion and participants," a detail Herold treats as significant in itself.[1]

Herold notes that a follow-up email sent two days later by Sam Morison of an associated "Trade Group" identified two options for the parties, one of which was "the Rosneft transaction," implicating the Russian state oil firm [[rosneft|Rosneft]] in the discussions.[1]

## "Office mates" email

Four days after the Biden party returned from Ireland on 17 September 2017, Hunter Biden circulated the email since known as the "office mates" message, in which Joe Biden was described as sharing office space with CEFC-linked figures.[1] Herold presents the tight sequence — joint travel, sensitive meeting, and then the office-mates arrangement — as evidence that the Ireland encounter was a pivotal point in the CEFC relationship, and asks whether Joe Biden personally attended.[1]

## Role in the Luft allegations

In July 2023, Israeli whistleblower [[gal-luft|Gal Luft]] alleged that the Biden family's benefactors connected to CEFC — described as a China-controlled energy company — were served by an FBI mole who shared classified information with them, and that the family received payments from individuals linked to Chinese military intelligence.[2] Luft said his arrest in Cyprus was intended to prevent him from presenting this account to the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]].[2]

## Mervyn Yan testimony

[[mervyn-yan|Mervyn Yan]], who worked with [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] through CEFC and the associated venture Hudson West III, was subpoenaed by the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] in 2023 for financial records related to those dealings and was scheduled to appear for closed-door transcribed interviews before the Oversight and [[house-judiciary-committee|Judiciary]] committees in late January 2024 as part of the House impeachment inquiry into President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[3]
