# Robert Menendez

Robert Menendez (referred to in the source as "Robert Mendez") is an American politician who served as a Democratic U.S. Senator from New Jersey from 2006 until his resignation in 2024. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," he is named among the supporters of the [[antisemitism-awareness-act|Antisemitism Awareness Act]] whom the source associates with the "deep state."

## Role in the article

The article describes Menendez as "a regular fixture in Brussels and at the annual Military-Industrial Complex functionary gathering, the Munich Security Conference," and lists him among the bill's "biggest supporters" who "were born and bred from the interagency blob." The description supports the source's argument that the interagency blob operates within both parties and "cares not which cause brings sufficient public support for its machinations."
