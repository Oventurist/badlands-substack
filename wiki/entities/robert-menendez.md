# Robert Menendez

Robert Menendez is a Democratic U.S. senator from New Jersey who rose to political prominence in Hudson County, a longtime Democratic bastion in the state.[1] Born to Cuban refugees and raised in Union City, he became New Jersey's senior senator before facing federal corruption charges for the second time in a decade.[1]

## 2023 bribery indictment

In September 2023 Menendez was indicted on federal corruption charges that threatened both his career and his liberty.[1] On Monday, September 25, 2023, he returned to Hudson County to address the allegations publicly, standing alone at a lectern at a community college not far from where he grew up as heavy rain beat against the windows.[1]

Menendez was defiant. He indicated he had no intention of bowing to the chorus of voices calling for his resignation, said the allegations had been framed by prosecutors to "be as salacious as possible," and acknowledged "I recognize that this will be the biggest fight yet."[1] He predicted that once the judicial process concluded, "not only will I be exonerated, I will still be New Jersey's senior senator."[1] The remarks were reported by the [[new-york-times|New York Times]].[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked the senator's defense with the rhetorical question of who does not keep a half million dollars stuffed in random spots all over the house, and lampooned the framing of the case as a politically targeted witch hunt.[1] She argued that the presumption of innocence is applied asymmetrically in American politics: for [[donald-trump|President Trump]], Justice Thomas, and others with anti-communist views, allegations are treated as convictions, while for Menendez, the Clinton campaign, and allied figures, innocence is assumed and the allegations, in her view, never reach a courtroom.[1] She noted that Trump had explicitly called out the double standard on Truth Social and that the post was circulating widely — "it's raining red pills."[1]
