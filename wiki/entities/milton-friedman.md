# Milton Friedman

Milton Friedman was the American free-market economist who, according to Matthew Ehret's Badlands account, made several tours of China during the 1980s in order to train a new generation of technocratic managerial cadre.[1]

Ehret places Friedman's China tours within the broader program overseen by [[zhao-ziyang|Zhao Ziyang]], under which transhumanist futurists such as [[alvin-toffler|Alvin Toffler]] were brought into the country and large numbers of young Chinese economists were sent abroad on Western scholarships to be indoctrinated in free-market economics at Harvard, Yale and Oxford.[1] In this reading, Friedman's instruction complemented the efforts of [[george-soros|George Soros]], who established two Open Society-affiliated think tanks in China managed by Zhao's aides [[chen-yizi|Chen Yizi]] and [[bao-tung|Bao Tung]].[1] The article frames this whole effort as an attempt to keep China locked into the cheap-labor, export-dependent role assigned to it by [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]] and the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]], rather than allowing it to pursue full-spectrum development.[1]
