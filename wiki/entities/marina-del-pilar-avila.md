# Marina del Pilar Ávila

Marina del Pilar Ávila is the governor of the Mexican state of Baja California and a member of the ruling Morena party.[1]

## US visa withdrawal

In May 2025, Ávila said that the United States had withdrawn tourist visas from both her and her husband.[1] She posted on social media on Sunday, May 11, 2025, that the two had been notified of the consular measure, without specifying the reason for the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s decision.[1]

"I fully trust that the situation will be satisfactorily clarified for both of us," Ávila wrote on X.[1] The Baja California press office confirmed to CNN that her visa had been withdrawn.[1] A spokesperson for the US Embassy told the Associated Press that visa records are confidential and that the details of individual cases could not be discussed.[1]

The episode was carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief for the weekend of May 10–11, 2025.[1]
