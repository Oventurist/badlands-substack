# Alison Sosebee

Alison Sosebee is a Georgia prosecutor and jurist who served as district attorney for the Appalachian Judicial Circuit before being appointed a Superior Court judge by Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]].[1]

## Role in 2020 election fraud allegations

Georgia resident [[bob-coovert|Bob Coovert]], who filed open records requests concerning the disputed post-election audit by [[pro-v-and-v|Pro V&V]], said he brought his findings to Sosebee while she was district attorney in the Appalachian Circuit. According to Coovert, Sosebee stopped communicating with him after he presented the evidence.[1]

Badlands Media noted that both Sosebee and [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] — the other official Coovert approached — were later appointed Superior Court judges by Kemp, a pattern the outlet also applied to former [[georgia-bureau-of-investigation|GBI]] Director [[vic-reynolds|Vic Reynolds]].[1]
