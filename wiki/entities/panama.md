# Panama

Panama is a Central American republic whose territory contains the Panama Canal, the 51-mile waterway connecting the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea.[1] In early 2025 the country became a focal point of United States hemispheric policy after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] complained that Panama had ceded effective control over the canal to Beijing.[1]

## 2025 realignment away from China

Following a meeting with Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] — Rubio's first international trip after confirmation — Panamanian president [[jose-raul-mulino|José Raúl Mulino]] pledged on February 2, 2025 to end a key development arrangement with China.[1] Mulino said Panama's sovereignty over the canal would remain unchanged, but that he would not renew the 2017 memorandum of understanding under which Panama joined China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]], and that his government would instead seek closer cooperation with the United States.[1] "I think this visit opens the door to build new relations … and try to increase as much as possible U.S. investments in Panama," Mulino told reporters.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] read the Panama episode skeptically, grouping it with the simultaneous negotiations involving Colombia, Venezuela, Ukraine, [[mexico|Mexico]], [[canada|Canada]] and Greenland as "amazing nonsense" — theatrical storytelling rather than genuine diplomacy — while noting the striking fact that Panama had apparently expelled the United States' principal adversary from the most strategically valuable location in the hemisphere without any naval confrontation.[1]

## 2023 mining protests

In October and November 2023 Panama experienced sustained nationwide protests against a long-term copper mining concession granted to a local subsidiary of the Canadian company [[first-quantum-minerals|First Quantum Minerals]], an episode covered in Badlands Media commentary as a case study in media framing.[2] Associated Press reporting quoted in that commentary described demonstrations extending into a second week, drawing a broad cross-section of society, with critics warning the concession endangered the country's environment and water supply.[2] President [[laurentino-cortizo|Laurentino Cortizo]]'s administration proposed a December referendum on scrapping the deal, though the top electoral authority said a vote could not be held before the following May's presidential election; a referendum date of December 17 was later reported.[2] On November 8, 2023, [[kenneth-darlington|Kenneth Darlington]], a 77-year-old dual US–Panamanian citizen, shot and killed two protesters blockading a highway.[2] For a fuller account see [[2023-panama-mining-protests|the 2023 Panama mining protests]].[2]
