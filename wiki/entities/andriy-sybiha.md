# Andriy Sybiha

Andriy Sybiha is a Ukrainian diplomat and official who served as deputy head of the office of President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1]

## Crimea negotiation remarks (April 2023)

In early April 2023 Sybiha gave remarks to the *Financial Times* signalling that Kyiv was willing to discuss the future of [[crimea|Crimea]] with Moscow if Ukrainian forces reached the border of the Russian-occupied peninsula.[1] The *Financial Times* described this as the most explicit statement of Ukraine's interest in negotiations since it broke off peace talks with the Kremlin in April 2022.[1] Sybiha framed the position as coming from a place of strength, consistent with the general tone of Ukrainian official messaging at the time.[1]

The statement came against the backdrop of the difficult fight for Bakhmut, heavy Ukrainian casualties in the east, and anticipation of a spring counteroffensive, and represented a public reversal from Zelensky's earlier insistence on recovering every inch of Ukrainian territory — including his October 2022 declaration that "We will definitely liberate Crimea."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the remarks as Zelensky ceding narrative ground to Russia, coinciding with a dialling back of U.S. and NATO rhetoric about Ukrainian victory and, in his framing, an eventual abandonment of the Ukraine project by the globalist establishment.[1]
