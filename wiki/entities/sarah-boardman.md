# Sarah A. Boardman

Sarah A. Boardman is a British-born, Colorado-based portrait artist best known for painting the official portraits of Presidents [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and Barack Obama displayed in the [[colorado|Colorado]] State Capitol.[1]

## Trump portrait controversy

Boardman's portrait of Trump had hung in the Colorado Capitol since 2019, during Trump's first term.[1] In March 2025, Trump publicly demanded its immediate removal, calling the painting "truly the worst" and saying it had been "purposefully distorted to a level that" he had "never seen before."[1] In a post on [[truth-social|Truth Social]], Trump said Colorado Governor [[jared-polis|Jared Polis]] should "be ashamed of himself" for displaying it, adding, "I would much prefer not having a picture than having this one," and claiming that many state residents had complained about the artwork.[1] By contrast, Trump described Boardman's Obama portrait for the same building as "wonderful."[1] Both portraits were based on photographs rather than live sittings.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] offered an extended critique, allowing that the artist demonstrated genuine technique in rendering a subject with plausible texture and color blending, but arguing that the work's lack of contrast between lights and darks left it flat and lacking depth — "like a skilled high schooler painted it."[1] He judged the likeness unflattering and short of the near-photorealistic standard expected of a civic portrait of a sitting president, speculating that the governor had recruited an insufficiently qualified artist operating on "old government metrics."[1] While conceding the painting was probably not technically "bad," he concluded that no one would pay for it or accept it as an official future representation of Trump.[1]
