# Islamic Society of Greater Houston

The Islamic Society of Greater Houston (ISGH) is an umbrella organization of mosques and Islamic institutions serving the Houston metropolitan area. It entered national news coverage in January 2025 as one of two bodies to which a Houston-area mosque directed congregants seeking to handle federal inquiries following the New Orleans New Year's Day attack.[1]

## New Orleans attack referral

Masjid Bilal, a mosque near the Houston home of [[shamsud-din-jabbar|Shamsud-Din Jabbar]], posted an Instagram story after the attacker's identity was announced condemning the "terrible acts" and instructing members not to respond to media inquiries.[1] The post added that "if approached by the FBI and a response is necessary, please refer to CAIR and ISGH," naming the [[council-on-american-islamic-relations|Council on American-Islamic Relations]] and the Islamic Society of Greater Houston as the intended intermediaries with the [[fbi|FBI]].[1] The referral guidance was reported by Newsweek and amplified by Breitbart, which framed the instruction as evidence of institutional resistance to federal counterterrorism investigation.[1]
