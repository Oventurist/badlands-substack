# Apple Inc.

Apple Inc. is an American consumer technology company and, as of 2025, the largest American company by market capitalization. In the Badlands corpus it appears as one of five firms said to dominate the [[sp-500|S&P 500]] and as one of the technology companies alleged to have grown out of state-developed capability.

## Market position

Erik Carlson's [[america-has-a-cia-run-economy|America Has a CIA-Run Economy]] lists Apple as the top American company by market capitalization in 2025 and in 2020, and as the second largest in 2010. The essay uses Apple to illustrate the extremity of market concentration: in 2025 Apple was roughly ten times larger than Home Depot, the twentieth-ranked company, whereas in 2000 the largest company was only three times the size of the twentieth.

## Alleged intelligence connections

The article declines to "let Apple off the hook," asserting that [[darpa]] "also probably created SIRI," Apple's voice assistant. It further states that iPhones "listen to everything that is said around them, and track everywhere one goes," placing Apple within the essay's general thesis that consumer technology serves as a surveillance channel for the [[cia]].

Apple is also named among the big technology companies that donated one million dollars to [[donald-trump]]'s inaugural fund in January 2025, alongside Google, [[microsoft]], [[meta-platforms|Meta]], [[amazon]], and [[openai]] — a group Carlson characterizes as massive, big-tech, and heavily involved in [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] development. He describes these donations as "the real pardoning that is happening in America." The essay concludes that it is "hard to imagine an America without Amazon, Apple, FaceBook, Microsoft or Google," and that Trump therefore treats such firms as necessary components of the national economy.
