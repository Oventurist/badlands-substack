# Amy Falls

**Amy Falls** is an institutional investment executive named in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" among the figures associated with the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board.[1]

The article identifies her as the former chief investment officer of [[rockefeller-university|Rockefeller University]], mentored by Wall Street figure [[barton-biggs|Barton Biggs]] and connected to [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]].[1] She appears in the piece as one link in an alleged chain binding Harvard's endowment to Rockefeller-affiliated institutions and legacy Wall Street networks — a roster the author characterizes as "not a list of academics" but a financial cartel operating at the center of the [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
