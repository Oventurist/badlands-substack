# Black Panther Party

The Black Panther Party was a black revolutionary organization founded in the 1960s. In Badlands Media commentary it appears chiefly as an example of a movement alleged to have been infiltrated and financed by the same interests that funded its supposed enemies.[1]

## Alleged infiltration and dual funding

According to Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], [[malcolm-x|Malcolm X]] discovered that both the Black Panthers and the [[ku-klux-klan|Ku Klux Klan]] had been infiltrated by the [[fbi|FBI]] and were being funded by the same people.[1] Carlson named Texas oil tycoon [[h-l-hunt|H.L. Hunt]] among those said to be bankrolling both sides in order to generate chaos, unrest and hate during the 1960s, and framed Malcolm X's murder as a consequence of that discovery.[1]

The claim supports the article's broader thesis that racial and social movements are engineered from above to divide rather than to unite, a pattern Carlson traces forward to [[affirmative-action|affirmative action]] and [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]].[1]
