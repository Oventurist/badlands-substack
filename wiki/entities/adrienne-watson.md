# Adrienne Watson

Adrienne Watson is the chief spokesperson of the National Security Council press office in the [[white-house|White House]] under President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Role alongside Kirby's expanded portfolio

When [[john-kirby|John Kirby]] was promoted in February 2024 to the newly created role of White House national security communications adviser — elevated from deputy assistant to assistant to the president — he was given charge of communications coordination for national security across a variety of agencies.[1] Kirby was to preside over a team separate from the National Security Council's press office, which Watson heads and which continued its daily press operations as usual.[1]
