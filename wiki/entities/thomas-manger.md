# Thomas Manger

Thomas Manger served as Chief of the [[united-states-capitol-police|United States Capitol Police]], the agency with oversight responsibility over the Capitol's security-camera archive.[1]

## Reaction to the McCarthy–Carlson footage arrangement

In February 2023, Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]]'s apparent deal granting [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] access to thousands of hours of Capitol security footage from January 6, 2021 came as a surprise to Manger, according to reporting by [[politico|Politico]].[1] A person familiar with the matter said Manger told associates he did not learn of the arrangement until it began publicly circulating on Monday, February 20, 2023, after Axios broke the news.[1]

Capitol Police had been extremely reluctant to share large portions of their security footage, citing potential risks to lawmakers, aides and the officers tasked with protecting the building.[1] House Sergeant at Arms William McFarland likewise told associates he learned of the arrangement around the same time.[1]

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] observed that Carlson appeared to possess access to, rather than custody of, the footage — an important distinction because airing any of it "may require approval from the Capitol Police Board" — and remarked that someone probably should have informed the Chief and the Sergeant at Arms ahead of the news breaking.[1]
