# Seymour Hersh

Seymour Hersh is an American investigative journalist whose February 2023 reporting on the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream pipeline sabotage]] became a central reference point in alternative-media coverage of the attack.[1]

## The Nord Stream report

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that "the Hersh report nine months ago" documented in detail how the Biden administration — or elements within it — circumvented the U.S. chain of command in order to place proxies on the ground to sabotage German energy infrastructure as a false flag operation against Russia.[1] In this framing, the circumvention of the chain of command was accomplished through intelligence assets, and the later Washington Post reporting naming Ukrainian colonel [[roman-chervinsky|Roman Chervinsky]] was read as an attempt to redirect blame from U.S. planners onto Ukrainian proxies.[1]

Hersh's account is treated within the Badlands corpus as the baseline against which subsequent mainstream reporting on Nord Stream is measured, with later admissions viewed as incremental movement of the Overton window rather than as independent discoveries.[1]

## Career and standing

Badlands contributors describe Hersh as a legendary investigative journalist whose word and work command respect and attention wherever they are offered.[2] His body of work includes uncovering the My Lai massacre, reporting on Watergate and Abu Ghraib, accusing the Obama administration of lying about the killing of Osama bin Laden, rejecting the Russia collusion narrative, and obtaining information on the Seth Rich murder from a source in the [[fbi|FBI]] that no other reporter could access.[2] [[just-human|Just Human]] wrote that when Hersh drops a report it makes an impact, and judged the Nord Stream piece possibly his most explosive yet.[2]

## "How America Took Out The Nord Stream Pipeline" (February 2023)

Hersh's February 2023 dispatch reported that in December 2021, two months before the first Russian tanks rolled into Ukraine, National Security Advisor [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] convened a newly formed task force of officials from the [[joint-chiefs-of-staff|Joint Chiefs of Staff]], the [[cia|CIA]], and the State and Treasury Departments to recommend responses to [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]'s impending invasion.[2] The meetings were held in a secure room on a top floor of the Old Executive Office Building adjacent to the [[white-house|White House]], also home to the President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board, and turned on whether the recommendation forwarded to the President should be reversible — such as further sanctions and currency restrictions — or irreversible kinetic action.[2] According to Hersh's source with direct knowledge of the process, it became clear that Sullivan intended the group to produce a plan for the destruction of the two Nord Stream pipelines, and that he was delivering on the desires of the President.[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that in an age dominated by fraudulent products such as the Steele Dossier and other "wrap-up smears" used by the media industrial complex against enemies of the [[deep-state|Deep State]], the Hersh Report stood as "a beacon of hope for the future of free and independent journalism," emanating from one of the original trailblazers of investigative reporting.[2] He argued the report suggested that the Biden administration, at the behest of globalist allies and using U.S. intelligence agencies while circumventing the military chain of command, had attempted to trigger World War III through the most brazen false flag attack in some time — and had been caught.[2] [[just-human|Just Human]] characterized the conduct as a war crime against Russia and Europe and a crime against the U.S. Constitution, calling for impeachment and removal.[2]

## "Leaving Las Vegas" and the Biden withdrawal (July 2024)

In late July 2024, Hersh published a Substack dispatch reporting that former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] was the impetus behind [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s exit from the 2024 presidential race, based on sources in Washington and the [[white-house|White House]].[3] Hersh wrote that he had gone over reports "with a senior official in Washington who helped me fashion an account of a White House in complete disarray," and that Obama was "deeply involved" in what the [[conservative-treehouse|Conservative Treehouse]] characterized as a coup.[3] According to Hersh's source, Obama called Biden after breakfast on July 20 — following Biden's "incident" in Las Vegas, which outward appearances suggested was a serious slip and fall with a significant blow to the head — and said, "Here's the deal. We have Kamala's approval to invoke the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]]."[3] [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] and [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] were reportedly directly involved.[3]

Hersh's account held that Obama did not plan to endorse [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] immediately, though it was clear she would "get the nod," and quoted the official saying Obama "had an agenda and he wanted to seek it through to the end, and he wanted to have control over who would be elected."[3] Conservative Treehouse argued the account tracked with every known datapoint about the run-up to Biden's announcement and with the broader pattern of the Obama team operating inside the Biden administration.[3]

## "Summer of the Hawks" (August 2023)

In August 2023 Hersh published "Summer of the Hawks," a dispatch examining what he called the Biden administration's foreign policy cluster — Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Tony Blinken]], national security adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] and [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]], whom he described as a trio of war hawks.[4] He reported on Sullivan's American delegation to the [[2023-jeddah-peace-summit|second international peace summit at Jeddah]], led by Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] and attended by Ukrainian president [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], noting that Russia was not invited, that fewer than fifty nations sent delegates, and that the two-day conference drew little international attention.[4] Hersh observed that MBS had months earlier announced a merger of his state-backed golf tour with the PGA and had four years prior been accused of ordering the assassination and dismemberment of journalist [[jamal-khashoggi|Jamal Khashoggi]] in Istanbul.[4]

Hersh concluded that the [[white-house|White House]]'s "wishful approach" to the war would continue apace but that "the end is nearing," dismissing Biden's public assessments as being "out of a comic strip."[4] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] placed the piece alongside a NATO defense official floating renewed negotiations and mainstream admissions that the [[2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive|counteroffensive]] had failed, arguing that Hersh was marking the end of a chapter rather than of the story and connecting the analysis to Hersh's earlier reporting on the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream]] and Crimean Bridge bombings.[4]

## "Leaving Las Vegas" reporting on Obama and Biden

In late July 2024 Hersh published a Substack article, "Leaving Las Vegas," reporting that sources in Washington and the White House had confirmed to him that former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] was the impetus behind pushing [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] out of the 2024 presidential race.[5] Hersh described Obama as "deeply involved" in what the piece characterized as a coup, and reported that Obama telephoned Biden after the president's "incident" in Las Vegas — an episode that, by outward appearances, resembled a serious slip and fall with a significant blow to the head.[5]

"I went over [reports] this week with a senior official in Washington who helped me fashion an account of a White House in complete disarray," Hersh wrote.[5] According to a senior Washington official quoted by Hersh, "Obama called Biden after breakfast [on July 20] and said, 'Here's the deal. We have Kamala's approval to invoke the 25th Amendment.'"[5] [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]], and [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] were reported to have been directly involved.[5] Hersh's sources said Obama did not plan to endorse [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] immediately, though it was clear she would "get the nod," and that Obama "had an agenda and he wanted to seek it through to the end, and he wanted to have control over who would be elected."[5]

The account was amplified by [[conservative-treehouse|Conservative Treehouse]] under the headline "It All Tracks," which argued that Hersh's reporting aligned with every known datapoint preceding Biden's withdrawal announcement and with the broader pattern of the Obama team operating inside the Biden administration.[5]

## Citation in mainstream coverage, March 2023

When the [[new-york-times|New York Times]] reported on March 7, 2023 that new intelligence pointed toward a pro-Ukrainian group as responsible for the [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream sabotage]], its article referenced Hersh's Substack piece "How America Took Out the Nord Stream Pipeline."[6] Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] singled out that reference as notable, arguing that overlaying the Hersh account with the Times report, the parallel [[zeit-online|Zeit Online]] investigation and open-source intelligence would form a "constellation of facts, information and possibilities" bringing readers closer to the truth of the attack.[6]

## "Opera Buffa in Ukraine" and the Crimean Bridge attacks (July 2023)

In a Substack investigative article published in late July 2023, Hersh alleged that U.S. intelligence had helped Ukraine blow up the Kerch Strait (Crimean) Bridge earlier that month, an event that coincided with President [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]'s refusal to renew the [[black-sea-grain-initiative|Black Sea Grain Initiative]].[7] His sources further described U.S. assistance in the larger initial [[crimean-bridge-attack|Kerch Bridge explosion]] of October 2022 that temporarily disabled the span, with Hersh writing that "the Biden administration's role in both attacks was vital."[7]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] situated the report in a sequence running from the alleged [[nord-stream-sabotage|Nord Stream 2 sabotage]] — which he described as a [[cia|CIA]] operation blamed on Russian operators and capable of pushing Europe toward kinetic war with Russia — through subsequent suspected false-flag events including the [[kakhovka-dam-destruction|Kakhovka Dam]] destruction and the bridge strikes.[7] He wrote that he had taken to calling the Biden [[state-department|State Department]] the "(Deep) State Department," observing that the Kremlin rarely addresses [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] directly but often addresses Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]], and argued that the clandestine kinetic arm of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] is the CIA.[7]

## Reporting on Ukrainian embezzlement of US aid

In April 2023, Hersh reported that Ukrainians had embezzled at least $400 million of the money given to them by the United States.[8] According to his reporting, [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] was confronted with evidence of the embezzlement by CIA Director [[william-burns|William Burns]] and responded by dismissing a handful of officials while taking no serious action.[8] The account was amplified by [[and-magazine|AND Magazine]], which paired it with [[transparency-international|Transparency International]] survey data on Ukrainian bribery and with a [[security-service-of-ukraine|Security Service of Ukraine]] disclosure of a military food-procurement embezzlement scheme.[8]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that prominent left-leaning writers — "even a Pulitzer Prize winner" — advancing Ukraine corruption headlines were shifting the [[overton-window|Overton Window]] back toward objective analysis of the funding American and European taxpayers had sent to what he called the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s latest forever-war quagmire.[8]
