# Deep Green Resistance

Deep Green Resistance is an eco-anarchist organization named by Badlands Media as part of an interconnected mesh of agencies which, together with [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]], provide what the article calls disposable misanthropic eco-terrorist cannon fodder while "above ground" operatives penetrate the official corridors of power within politics, academia, military intelligence and polite society.[1]

The group is described as billionaire-funded and is placed on one side of a [[double-bind-theory|false dichotomy]]: having accepted the premise that technology is the intrinsic enemy of humanity and nature, its activists choose to go to war against the industrial system from below, while figures such as [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]] accept the same premise and instead adapt to it from above.[1] Both paths, in the article's argument, converge on a techno-feudal dystopia.[1] Derrick Jensen, associated with this current of thought, is listed among the eco-terrorist theorists whose fundamental assumptions about humanity, technology and history the article says are identical with those of [[prince-philip|Prince Philip]] and [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]].[1]
