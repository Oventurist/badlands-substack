# Wadea Al-Fayoume

Wadea Al-Fayoume was a six-year-old Palestinian American Muslim boy who was stabbed to death on October 14, 2023 at a home outside Chicago, in an attack that also seriously injured his mother.[1]

## Killing and aftermath

The killing was investigated by the Department of Justice as a hate crime.[1] The suspect, [[joseph-czuba|Joseph Czuba]], pleaded not guilty on Monday, October 30, 2023 to charges of murder, attempted murder and hate crimes.[1]

White House press secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] cited the "recent barbaric killing" of Al-Fayoume in announcing that the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would develop a first-ever [[national-strategy-to-counter-islamophobia|National Strategy to Counter Islamophobia]], unveiled by Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] on November 1, 2023.[1]
