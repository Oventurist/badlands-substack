# J.P. Morgan

John Pierpont Morgan was an American financier whose name recurs in Badlands Media's treatment of the origins of American central banking.[1] He appears in a July 11, 2024 essay on the exploitation of trust in connection with the founding of the [[knickerbocker-trust-company|Knickerbocker Trust Company]], which was chartered in 1884 by Frederick G. Eldridge, described as a friend and classmate of Morgan.[1]

## Role in the trust network

The essay's interest in Morgan is structural rather than biographical: it uses the Knickerbocker episode to show how the fiduciary trust company — an institution whose entire business is holding property on behalf of beneficiaries — sat at the centre of the financial establishment of the era, and how the personal networks of financiers such as Morgan ran through those institutions.[1] When Knickerbocker's funds were committed to a scheme to corner the copper market and the firm suspended operations, the resulting run helped produce the [[panic-of-1907|Panic of 1907]], which in turn is seen as the final event leading Congress to form the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve System]] in 1913.[1]
