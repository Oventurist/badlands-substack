# Allison Burroughs

Allison Burroughs is a United States district judge serving on the U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts.[1] In September 2025 she issued the ruling that voided the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's freeze of $2.2 billion in federal grant funding for [[harvard-university|Harvard University]], holding that the freeze was illegal and describing it as "a targeted, ideologically-motivated assault on this country's premier universities."[1]

## Harvard funding ruling

Burroughs agreed with Harvard's argument that the administration had imposed the funding freeze in retaliation for the university's refusal to capitulate to demands for reforms that, in Harvard's view, violated First Amendment protections under the Constitution.[1] The administration had justified the freeze by citing concerns about antisemitism on campus and other issues.[1]

Her ruling vacated the freezing orders affecting Harvard and barred anyone in the Trump administration from enforcing them.[1] The [[white-house|White House]] said it would appeal the decision.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] criticized the outcome, framing it as a judge ordering that taxpayer dollars be delivered as grants to an Ivy League institution already holding a roughly $55 billion endowment, and dismissing the invocation of "checks and balances" as a system in which "Americans pay the overdraft fees."[1]
