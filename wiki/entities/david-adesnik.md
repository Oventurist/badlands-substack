# David Adesnik

David Adesnik is Senior Fellow and Director of Research at the [[foundation-for-defense-of-democracies|Foundation for Defense of Democracies]], a Washington think tank focused on foreign policy and national security.[1]

## Analysis of the UN Gaza casualty revision

In May 2024 Adesnik responded publicly to the [[united-nations|United Nations]]' sudden halving of its published estimates of women and children killed in Gaza. UN data released on 6 May 2024 listed 34,735 killed including over 9,500 women and over 14,500 children, while data released on 8 May listed 34,844 killed including only 4,959 women and 7,797 children.[1]

Adesnik wrote that the change "may signal that the UN has finally recognized the lack of evidence behind Hamas's original claims that more than 14,000 children and 9,000 women have been killed in Gaza," and argued that if so, the UN "should state clearly that it has lost confidence in sources whose credibility it has affirmed for months." He added that while the revision might reflect the conclusion of only one UN office among the many operating in Gaza, it was nonetheless "a clear step forward."[1]

His analysis was cited by [[breitbart|Breitbart]] alongside reporting from the [[jerusalem-post|Jerusalem Post]] and the [[jewish-news-syndicate|Jewish News Syndicate]], the latter of which reported that the UN blamed the "fog of war" for the overcount.[1]
