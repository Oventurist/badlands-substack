# Avi Silverberg

**Avi Silverberg** is a Canadian powerlifting coach who served as the head coach for Team Canada Powerlifting for more than ten years.[1] He became the subject of international news coverage in March 2023 after entering a women's powerlifting competition as a protest against policies permitting transgender women to compete in female categories.[1]

## Heroes Classic protest lift

Silverberg entered the Heroes Classic tournament held in Lethbridge, Alberta, on Saturday, March 25, 2023, after declaring that he identified as a female.[1] Video of the entry, shared by the athlete activist group [[independent-council-on-womens-sports|Independent Council on Women's Sports]] (ICONS), showed him approaching the platform still fully bearded and wearing a standard men's singlet.[1]

He then bench-pressed nearly 370 pounds, beating the standing Alberta women's bench press record by almost 100 pounds.[1] The previous record of 275 pounds had been set by the transgender athlete [[anne-andres|Anne Andres]], who was volunteering at the event and was seen watching Silverberg's lift.[1]

## Reception

The episode was widely interpreted as a deliberate demonstration rather than a competitive attempt, and it was cited by critics of transgender inclusion policies as evidence of the physiological disparities at issue in [[transgender-athletes-in-womens-sports|the debate over transgender athletes in women's sports]].[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[brad-getz|Brad Getz]] wrote approvingly of the protest, arguing that Silverberg was "a regular guy with a beard" whose entry was "purely to send a message," and that such demonstrations "should be happening in every place this woke nightmare" was enabled.[1]
