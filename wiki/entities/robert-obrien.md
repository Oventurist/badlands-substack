# Robert O'Brien

Robert O'Brien served as National Security Advisor during the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Special counsel subpoenas (2023)

In February 2023, Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] issued subpoenas for interviews and documents to former Vice President [[mike-pence|Michael Pence]] and to O'Brien.[1] O'Brien received two subpoenas seeking testimony and documents relating to the two separate inquiries into the former president, according to a person familiar with the matter who spoke on condition of anonymity because the information was non-public.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] situated the subpoenas alongside reporting that former Acting DHS Secretary [[chad-wolf|Chad Wolf]] had spent four hours being interviewed under oath weeks earlier as part of Smith's investigation into the events of January 6, 2021 and attempts to interfere with the transfer of power following the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## Commentary on a second Trump term (December 2023)

O'Brien, described as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s fourth and final national security adviser, was quoted in December 2023 Reuters reporting on how a second Trump administration would staff the national security apparatus.[2] "President Trump came to realize that personnel is policy," he said, adding that "at the outset of his administration, there were a lot of people that were interested in implementing their own policies, not the president's policies."[2] The reporting held that installing loyalists at the [[pentagon|Pentagon]], [[state-department|State Department]] and [[cia|CIA]] would let Trump advance his foreign policy priorities faster and more efficiently than in his first term.[2]

## Voting machine seizure discussions (April 2023)

In a closed-door interview with federal prosecutors in early 2023, O'Brien recounted conversations about seizing voting machines after the 2020 election, including during a heated Oval Office meeting in which Trump participated.[3] The previously unreported interview, disclosed by CNN in April 2023, accompanied grand jury testimony from [[chad-wolf|Chad Wolf]] and [[ken-cuccinelli|Ken Cuccinelli]] that the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] lacked authority to seize the machines.[3] The material illustrated how special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]] was examining the various ways Trump sought to overturn his electoral loss despite contrary advice from senior officials.[3]
