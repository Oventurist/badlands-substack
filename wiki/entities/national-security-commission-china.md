# National Security Commission of China

The National Security Commission is one of the bodies personally chaired by [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] as part of his reorganisation of the Chinese party-state.[1]

## Role in Xi's consolidation

The Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" describes the Commission as one of eight "small groups" that Xi chairs himself, a set that also includes the restructured [[politburo-standing-committee|Politburo Standing Committee]] arrangement through which formerly independent heads of state report to him on economic issues.[1] The article argues that this concentration of chairmanships reverses decades of institutionalised rule and gives the appearance of autocratic government.[1]

The author offers an alternative reading, suggesting that Xi's assembly of a council of military advisers and leaders is consistent with a National Security Council and combatant-commander structure of the kind familiar in the West, and that the arrangement may reflect a law-and-order purge of legacy corruption from the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] rather than simple personal dictatorship.[1]
