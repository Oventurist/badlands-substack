# Allard Lowenstein

Allard "Al" Lowenstein was an American political organiser who served as chief of staff to presidential candidate [[edward-kennedy|Edward "Ted" Kennedy]] in the lead-up to the 1980 elections.[1] He is a central figure in the opening of [[valediction-three-nights-of-desmond|Valediction: Three Nights of Desmond]], where journalist [[paul-fitzgerald|Paul Fitzgerald]] recounts a chance encounter in which Lowenstein described his and Kennedy's intention to shed light on the [[cia|CIA]]'s involvement in the murder of the two Kennedy brothers.[1]

## Death

Two weeks after that exchange, Lowenstein was shot dead in his office by a former colleague.[1] For Fitzgerald and [[elizabeth-gould|Elizabeth Gould]], the killing marked the moment they began to realise they were pressing on something much larger than themselves.[1]

## National Students Association

The couple's subsequent research established that Lowenstein had been the founder and president of the National Students Association, launched in 1951, which operated as a CIA front group designed to recruit both talented young Americans and foreign students who would later be placed in various foreign governments during the Cold War.[1]

By the account in Valediction, Lowenstein had grown sick of playing a part in this machine, and spent his last years organising for [[robert-f-kennedy|Bobby Kennedy]] and [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]]; when both fell in battle, he made the surviving Kennedy brother's presidential campaign his governing passion.[1]
