# Rise Attire

Rise Attire is an American apparel company referenced in the Badlands Brief as the vendor that printed a border wall design created by Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] and sent to the [[white-house|White House]] in 2017. The reference appears in an editor's note appended to [[canncon|CannCon]]'s one-line commentary on the Israeli crocodile-prison story — "Proof Ghost is Mossad" — clarifying that the joke alluded to the earlier wall design rather than to the Israeli proposal itself. A postscript notes that the designer produced a follow-up concept in 2017 "just for fun," which he judged, "like most sequels," inferior to the original.
