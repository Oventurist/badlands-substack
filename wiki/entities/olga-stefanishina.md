# Olga Stefanishina

Olga Stefanishina is a Ukrainian politician and diplomat who was appointed ambassador of Ukraine to the United States by [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]] in August 2025, despite ongoing corruption allegations linked to her earlier government service.[1]

## Career

Stefanishina served as deputy prime minister for European and Euro-Atlantic integration from 2020, making her Ukraine's senior official for European integration.[1] In 2024 she became minister of justice.[1] In July 2025 she was named a temporary special envoy to the United States amid renewed graft allegations, and the following month was elevated to ambassador in Washington.[1]

## Corruption allegations

Ukraine's [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau]] (NABU) confirmed in July 2025 that it had opened a probe into possible abuses involving the [[asset-recovery-and-management-agency|Asset Recovery and Management Agency]] (ARMA), following a media investigation that tied Stefanishina's ex-husband to questionable asset transfers.[1] No formal charges had been filed, but the existence of the pre-trial inquiry was confirmed only days before Zelensky moved to strip Ukraine's anti-graft agencies of their independence.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry connected the appointment to reporting that Zelensky's top aide [[andriy-yermak|Yermak]] had encouraged the raids on NABU and the [[specialized-anti-corruption-prosecutors-office|Special Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office]] in order to provoke Western condemnation that could be leveraged into a coup against Zelensky.[1] The commentary noted the irony that Stefanishina, under investigation by agencies described as controlled by Ukraine's Western backers, would be reporting to those same backers as ambassador, and raised the possibility of her arrest in the United States.[1] The episode was characterized as [[donald-trump|Trump]] "playing rope-a-dope" with the Deep State.[1]
