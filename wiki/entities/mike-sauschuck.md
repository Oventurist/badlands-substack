# Mike Sauschuck

Mike Sauschuck is the commissioner of the Maine Department of Public Safety and served as the principal official spokesman during the search for the gunman in the October 2023 [[lewiston-maine-mass-shooting|Lewiston, Maine mass shooting]].[1]

## Role in the Lewiston manhunt

At a Friday night news conference, Sauschuck announced that [[robert-card|Robert Card]] had died of a self-inflicted gunshot wound.[1] He said the body was located at about 7:45 p.m. local time near the Androscoggin River in Lisbon, a town about eight miles southwest of Lewiston.[1]

The following day Sauschuck provided further detail, stating that the body was found in the overflow parking lot at the Maine Recycling Corporation, in the back of a tractor-trailer.[1] He acknowledged that authorities had twice searched the business — where the gunman had worked at some point — but had not searched the overflow lot; the property owner had contacted police asking them to check.[1] Sauschuck also disclosed that the first three people to contact authorities identifying the man in the released photographs of the gunman were members of Card's family.[1]

## Night of the attack

On the night of the shootings, Sauschuck held a press conference at about 11:30 p.m. at Lewiston City Hall, where a police staging area had been set up, refusing to confirm the number of dead and injured and saying only that Card was a person of interest.[2] He stated that "literally hundreds of police officers working around the state of Maine" were investigating the case and searching for the suspect.[2]
