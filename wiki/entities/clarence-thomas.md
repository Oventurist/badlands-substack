# Clarence Thomas

**Clarence Thomas** is an Associate Justice of the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]], the Court's longest-serving member and its most consistently originalist voice. In the Badlands corpus he appears principally as a justice willing to go further than his colleagues in dismantling what the corpus treats as the legal scaffolding of race-conscious governance.

## Concurrence in Louisiana v. Callais (2026)

In [[louisiana-v-callais|Louisiana v. Callais]], decided in April 2026, Thomas joined the 6–3 majority striking down Louisiana's second majority-Black congressional district as an unconstitutional racial gerrymander, but issued a separate opinion arguing for a far broader holding. He contended that the [[voting-rights-act-of-1965|Voting Rights Act of 1965]] should not be used to challenge how states draw congressional districts at all: the statute, he wrote, is meant to protect access to the ballot, not to regulate the design of district maps. Redistricting decisions, in his view, fall outside the statute's scope, and no claims under that section should succeed in future districting cases.

Where Justice [[samuel-alito|Samuel Alito]]'s majority opinion preserved Section 2 while limiting its reach, Thomas's position would remove redistricting from its ambit entirely — a distinction highlighted in Badlands commentary as the difference between narrowing a tool and retiring it.
