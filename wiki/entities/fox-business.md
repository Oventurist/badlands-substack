# Fox Business

Fox Business is the American business news television network, cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as the venue for market and economic commentary carried onward by other outlets.[1]

In December 2023, economist [[harry-dent|Harry Dent]] appeared on Fox Business to warn that 2024 would bring "the biggest single crash year we'll see in our lifetimes," describing the post-2009 economy as sustained by $27 trillion of "100 percent artificial" money printing and deficits and predicting a decline on the scale of 1929–1932.[1] [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] reported the interview, and the item was reproduced as a bonus entry in the December 22, 2023 Brief.[1]
