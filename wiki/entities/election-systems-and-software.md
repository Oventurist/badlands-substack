# Election Systems & Software

Election Systems & Software, commonly abbreviated ES&S, is described in the Badlands corpus as a United States voting equipment powerhouse and one of the dominant vendors of American election hardware.

In the account given by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, the Obama-era [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]] compelled ES&S in 2010 to divest [[premier-election-solutions|Premier Election Solutions]], the former Diebold subsidiary that ES&S had purchased only six months earlier. The department required ES&S to divest "all of the intellectual property associated with all versions — past, present, and in development — of the Premier voting equipment systems to another company," to "grant a fully paid-up, irrevocable, perpetual license to use the AutoMARK, ES&S's ballot marking device," and to ensure the "right to modify and improve both Premier products and the AutoMARK." The DOJ further stipulated that "ES&S must sell the divestiture assets to a buyer approved by the department" and that ES&S grant the divestiture buyer an opportunity to compete to provide services to Premier customers then under contract with ES&S.

The approved buyer was [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]], a transaction the Badlands account treats as the pivotal moment establishing Dominion's market share and, through Dominion's subsequent purchase of [[sequoia-voting-systems|Sequoia Voting Systems]], its exposure to [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] intellectual property.
