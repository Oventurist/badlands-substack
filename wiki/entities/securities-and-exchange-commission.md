# Securities and Exchange Commission

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is the federal regulator of securities markets, corporate disclosure and merger transactions. In the Badlands Media corpus it appears as an instrument of administrative pressure applied against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s business interests.

## The DWAC–Trump Media merger

In [[a-virus-or-a-metaphor|A Virus, or a Metaphor?]], [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites the SEC as evidence that Trump derives no benefit from the [[deep-state|Deep State]] he is accused of belonging to. The article asks why "the SEC [is] taking the unprecedented step of holding up the merger between DWAC and [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media]], keeping him from growing his media, including injecting money into [[truth-social|Truth Social]]."

The regulatory delay is presented alongside other indicators — the decline in Trump's net worth after taking office and the uniform hostility of the mainstream media — in support of the essay's argument that "if Trump is corrupt and a part of the Deep State, he doesn't appear to be benefiting from his membership in this elite group in any way whatsoever." The article characterizes the SEC's handling of the transaction as unprecedented, though it does not detail the agency's stated legal grounds.
