# Kristi Burton Brown

Kristi Burton Brown is a Colorado Republican official who served as chairwoman of the [[colorado-republican-party|Colorado Republican Party]] during the 2022 election cycle.[1] She was elected to the position in 2021 only because of last-minute support from grassroots election-integrity activists.[1]

## Role at the 2022 assembly

Brown presided over the [[2022-colorado-republican-state-assembly|2022 Colorado Republican State Assembly and Convention]], where the party used discontinued [[edivote100-clickers|Infowhyse EdiVote100 electronic clickers]] to record delegate votes.[1] When voting members moved to prohibit the clickers and use hand-marked paper ballots instead, Brown and the parliamentarian refused to allow debate on the motion.[1] Neither disclosed to the delegates that the watermarked paper ballots, clear ballot boxes, and other provisions named in the motion were already onsite and ready for use; Brown instead framed the request as an impossibility.[1]

One delegate later recounted, "I didn't understand what they were asking me to vote on! Then Kristi said we'd be there until midnight, and we wouldn't know the results."[1] The motion for paper ballots failed two separate votes and the clickers were used for the day's balloting, which was marked by widespread reports of device failures, shortages, and an absence of security controls.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] presented Brown's handling of the motion as a central example of the Republican establishment's local resistance to the [[america-first-movement|America First]] grassroots that had installed her.[1]
