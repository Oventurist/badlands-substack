# Deb Dana

**Deb Dana** is a trauma specialist described as the developer of polyvagal-informed therapy in the April 19, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Remembering: Fractured Selves and the Path Beyond Ego" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The article quotes her that "the most powerful healing comes when all parts of us feel welcomed and safe. Regulation is the new safety, and safety is the beginning of connection."[1] The quotation appears in the essay's section on compassion, supporting its argument that parts of the self transform when they are seen rather than corrected, and that integration is a lived relationship with every corner of the self rather than "a checkbox on a healing journey."[1] Her work links the essay's account of [[parts-work|parts work]] to [[polyvagal-theory|polyvagal theory]] and to [[psychological-integration|psychological integration]].[1]
