# Stefan Halper

Stefan Halper is an academic and longtime intelligence-linked figure whose contacts with Trump campaign associates during 2016 made him a recurring subject of inquiry into the origins of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] investigation.[1]

## Durham records litigation

The Freedom of Information Act lawsuit filed by [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] against the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] in January 2026 seeks unredacted records from former Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s probe, including communications involving Halper, [[british-intelligence|British Intelligence]] head Sir [[richard-dearlove|Richard Dearlove]], U.S. intelligence officials and [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]].[1] The suit also targets [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]]'s 2016 White House briefing notes, the DOJ Inspector General report on the [[fbi|FBI]]'s handling of Crossfire Hurricane, and the 14 missing pages of the Durham Report appendix.[1]

## Palmer National Bank

Long before Russiagate, Halper served as policy director for [[george-hw-bush|George Bush]]'s 1980 presidential bid, and in 1983 he and [[harvey-mclean-jr|Harvey McLean Jr.]] — the campaign's southern finance chairman and a former Dallas real estate developer — founded the Washington-based [[palmer-national-bank|Palmer National Bank]] as the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan scandal]] was heating up.[2] The $2.8 million in seed money came from [[herman-k-beebe|Herman K. Beebe]], the Louisiana banker with alleged Mafia connections responsible for roughly $10 billion in thrift fraud.[2] One of Palmer's established accounts belonged to the [[national-endowment-for-the-preservation-of-liberty|National Endowment for the Preservation of Liberty]], the front organisation for [[oliver-north|Oliver North]], through which money was channelled to North's Swiss account and a $650,000 "loan" reached [[carl-channell|Carl Channell]] to buy weapons for the Nicaraguan Contras.[2] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] notes that the man who built this bank is the same Stefan Halper who later tried to frame Trump with Russian collusion accusations, and asks whether that is a coincidence.[2]
