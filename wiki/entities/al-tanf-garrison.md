# Al-Tanf Garrison

Al-Tanf is a United States military base in southern Syria, near the Syria–Iraq–Jordan border area, garrisoned by American forces under [[us-central-command|U.S. Central Command]].[1]

## October 2023 drone attacks

On October 18, 2023, at least two one-way attack drones targeted the al-Tanf base, and twenty American personnel sustained minor injuries.[1] One of the drones was shot down.[1] CENTCOM said all of the wounded personnel were returned to duty and that no military installations were damaged.[1] The al-Tanf strike was part of a wider series of drone attacks on American bases in Iraq and Syria that wounded two dozen U.S. military personnel in total, a casualty figure CENTCOM disclosed to NBC News on October 24, 2023, after [[pentagon|the Pentagon]] had confirmed the attacks the previous week without giving numbers.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that the injuries were reported just hours after Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] warned of "decisive action" should U.S. troops be further threatened by Iranian proxies in the region — a sequence he read as either the [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian dialectic]] playing out in public or an administration improvising contradictory narratives on a near-hourly basis.[1]
