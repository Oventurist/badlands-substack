# Wesley Hunt

Wesley Hunt is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Texas who emerged in April 2025 as a prospective candidate in the state's Republican U.S. Senate primary.[1]

## 2026 Texas Senate primary maneuvering

Reporting by CNN in April 2025 described Hunt as making moves to enter the Republican primary for the seat held by Senator [[john-cornyn|John Cornyn]].[1] Hunt privately argued to White House political advisers that he was the only candidate capable of winning both the primary and the general election, according to a person involved in the discussions.[1] His entry would complicate an already contentious three-way field, with Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] signaling his own intention to run and Senate Republican leaders pressing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to endorse Cornyn to head off an expensive intraparty fight.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] questioned Hunt's appeal relative to Paxton, and challenged the premise of electability arguments by asserting that Texas elections were as compromised as California's.[1]
