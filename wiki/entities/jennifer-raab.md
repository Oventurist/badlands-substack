# Jennifer Raab

Jennifer Raab is the president of [[hunter-college|Hunter College]] and the head of a stem cell foundation.[1]

## Nomination to BlackRock fund board

Raab was the second of three nominees [[boaz-weinstein|Boaz Weinstein]] of [[saba-capital|Saba Capital]] proposed for the board of BlackRock Capital Allocation Trust during Saba's 2024 campaign against ten [[blackrock|BlackRock]] closed-end funds.[1] Badlands Media flagged her stem cell foundation role as a "red flag," associating stem cell research with aborted fetal tissue, and asserted that she — like fellow nominee [[shavar-jeffries|Shavar Jeffries]] — has numerous connections to [[deep-state|Deep State]] figures and institutions.[1]
