# Scott Wilk

Scott Wilk is a Republican member of the California State Senate representing Santa Clarita. At the time of the June 2023 coverage he had served in the California Legislature for eleven years and was one of only two Republicans on the California Senate Judiciary Committee.[1]

## AB 957 hearing

During a June 13, 2023 hearing of the Senate Judiciary Committee on [[ab-957|AB 957]], Wilk told a gathered crowd of parents to flee the state, warning that the bill would place parents who do not affirm a child's gender transition in danger of child abuse charges.[1] He was described as the lone voice warning against language amended into the bill on June 5 by a Democratic senator, which would have rewritten the California Family Code to list "gender affirmation" alongside a child's need for "health, safety, and welfare."[1] Wilk directly challenged the assurances of Democratic committee members who claimed the measure would merely "give the judge more information."[1]

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] framed the episode as a deliberate test of public tolerance in what he called trans-sanctuary states, intended to polarize activist groups and generate media coverage marginalizing opponents.[1]
