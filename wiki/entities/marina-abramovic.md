# Marina Abramovic

Marina Abramovic is a Serbian performance artist whose work — particularly the ritual-styled pieces associated with [[spirit-cooking|spirit cooking]] — has made her a recurring figure of suspicion within the Truth Community.[1] In September 2023 she returned to prominence in that community after reports that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky had asked her to serve as an ambassador for Ukraine, with a portfolio focused on the country's displaced children.[1]

## The Ukraine ambassadorship reports

The claim originated in a report by *The Telegraph* based on statements attributed to Abramovic; the outlet removed the article over the following weekend, after which *[[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]]* carried the story under its own headline.[1] Badlands Media flagged the retraction directly in an editor's note accompanying its coverage.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described reacting to the appointment news by "swinging between disbelief and disgust," arguing that Abramovic "puts a face to the elusive cult of Lucifarian pedovores" that she associates with child sex and organ trafficking.[1] The commentary noted that such claims are routinely dismissed as "a dangerous QAnon conspiracy theory," except, in the author's framing, when the elite "put it on display and call it art."[1] Abramovic, the piece continued, is frequently photographed alongside powerful figures at art exhibits where participants appear to simulate consuming human flesh and blood and to celebrate human suffering and dehumanization — behavior defended as art beyond the comprehension of the non-"cultured elite."[1]

The prospect of Abramovic overseeing protections for displaced Ukrainian children was presented as an inversion of child safeguarding, closing with the appeal "Save the children."[1] The item connects to broader Badlands coverage of [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]].[1]
