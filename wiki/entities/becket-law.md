# Becket Law

Becket Law, also known as the Becket Fund for Religious Liberty, is a public-interest law firm that litigates religious-liberty cases in the United States.[1]

## Burke v. Walsh

In August 2023 Becket represented Mike and Kitty Burke, a Catholic couple from [[massachusetts|Massachusetts]], in [[burke-v-walsh|Burke v. Walsh]], a lawsuit filed against the state on August 8 after the Commonwealth denied the couple the ability to foster children on account of their conservative Catholic beliefs about marriage and gender.[1] In its press release announcing the case, Becket described the Burkes' background as an Iraq war veteran and a former paraprofessional for special-needs children who run a small business and perform music for Mass, and emphasized that they had completed training in 2022 along with extensive interviews and a home study.[1] The firm framed the denial against the backdrop of a severe Massachusetts foster-care shortage in which more than 1,500 children lacked foster families and the state had resorted to housing children in hospitals for weeks at a time.[1]
