# Jesse Strang

Jesse Strang is the name given in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief for the suspect in the [[tumbler-ridge-school-shooting|Tumbler Ridge school shooting]] in Canada, reported as having occurred on Wednesday, February 11, 2026.

The Brief, citing *[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]]*, describes Strang as an eighteen-year-old male-to-female transgender person who killed eight people, among them five students aged twelve and thirteen and an eleven-year-old stepbrother, leaving a twelve-year-old girl in critical condition. Citing [[cnn|CNN]], the Brief states that the suspect transitioned about six years earlier, which would place the transition at roughly age twelve, and notes that reports varied as to whether the suspect was seventeen or eighteen. CNN also reported that the suspect was known to Canadian police, who had visited the residence several times over mental health concerns. Authorities said the investigation was ongoing.

The identification and framing rest entirely on early media reporting summarized by the Brief, and details including the suspect's exact age were unsettled at the time of publication.
