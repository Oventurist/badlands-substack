# Samuel García Sepúlveda

Samuel García Sepúlveda is a Mexican politician who, as of early 2023, served as Governor of the northern Mexican state of [[nuevo-leon|Nuevo León]].[1] At age 35 he was described as one of the youngest rising political stars in Mexico.[1]

## World Economic Forum ties

García Sepúlveda was invited to participate in the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]'s 2023 annual gathering in Davos, Switzerland.[1] Although other Mexican citizens attended representing banks and oil companies such as PEMEX, García Sepúlveda was the only Mexican politician present at Davos that year.[1] Commentators noted that the invitation was unsurprising given that Nuevo León lies near the U.S.–Mexico border and contains Monterrey, the second largest city in Mexico.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that "Klaus is itching to penetrate another cabinet," referring to WEF founder [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]].[1] DeLarme argued that while attendance at Davos does not automatically mark a politician as a villain — noting that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] attended in 2018 and 2020 and used his speech to warn against the "perennial prophets of doom" shortly before [[greta-thunberg|Greta Thunberg]] took the stage — García Sepúlveda's WEF connections merited a red flag.[1] He contrasted the governor with the sitting Mexican president, [[andres-manuel-lopez-obrador|Andrés Manuel López Obrador]], who had been outspoken against masks and lockdowns, and speculated that WEF-aligned interests might seek to install García Sepúlveda in the Palacio Nacional as a Mexican counterpart to Justin Trudeau.[1]
