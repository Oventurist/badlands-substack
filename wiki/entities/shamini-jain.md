# Shamini Jain

**Dr. Shamini Jain** is quoted in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay attributes to her the statement: "When we live in coherence, we aren't just experiencing wellness, we are transmitting healing to our environment."[1] The author builds on the quotation to argue that the coherent person "become[s] a field of support for others," likened to the Bodhisattva who returns to the world to serve rather than transcending it, so that the nervous system functions as "an instrument for peace."[1]

The citation appears in the "Intelligence of Embodiment" section, immediately after [[mae-wan-ho|Mae-Wan Ho]]'s account of quantum coherence in living systems, and forms part of the essay's transition from individual to collective [[coherence|coherence]].[1]

## "The Liminal" (April 2025)

Jain is described as an "energy medicine expert" in the earlier April 5, 2025 Badlands Media feature "The Liminal."[2] That essay attributes to her the explanation that moments of inner stillness allow for recalibration, with the body — "no longer held together by outdated frequencies" — beginning to attune itself to a new coherence.[2] The author uses the point to reassure readers that the disorientation of the [[liminality|liminal]] state is vital rather than pathological: "You are not breaking down. You are being re-coded."[2] Her citation follows [[andrew-newberg|Andrew Newberg]]'s neurological framing and introduces the essay's account of the behavioural and emotional shifts that accompany recalibration.[2]
