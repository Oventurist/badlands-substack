# Digital Forensic Research Lab

The Digital Forensic Research Lab (DFRLab) is a research program of the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]] that presents itself as an open-source investigative body tracking disinformation.[1] It became a focus of censorship criticism after documents released as part of the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] described its role in compiling account blacklists.[1]

## Role in government-funded censorship

According to revelations from the Twitter Files — a trove of internal records about censorship decisions made within the social media company — the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]] funded groups such as the Atlantic Council's Digital Forensic Research Lab, which in turn compiled blacklists of Twitter accounts supposedly tied to foreign disinformation campaigns.[1] The Digital Forensic Research Lab sent those blacklists to Twitter so that the company could deactivate the accounts listed.[1]

This funding relationship placed DFRLab inside the broader network scrutinized when the [[state-department|State Department]] indicated it was substantially likely to shut down the $100 million Global Engagement Center before President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took office.[1]
