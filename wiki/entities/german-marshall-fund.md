# German Marshall Fund

The **German Marshall Fund of the United States** is a transatlantic policy nonprofit named by Badlands Media in its catalogue of [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]] alleged to form part of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] apparatus.[1]

The article lists it among former and current nonprofits it says can be traced to members and initiatives of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and allied bodies, describing the resulting structure as "a pervasive web of influence stretching across all corners of society."[1] The article directs readers to InfluenceWatch and WikiSpooks for further documentation on the organization.[1]
