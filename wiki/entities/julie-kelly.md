# Julie Kelly

Julie Kelly is an American independent journalist and commentator who writes on federal prosecutions, judicial conduct and what she and allied writers describe as the politicization of the United States legal system.[1]

In April 2025 the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] cited Kelly's reporting, posted to X, in support of the claim that the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]] had repeatedly violated procedural rules and abused the legal process while litigating against the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's use of the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]] to remove Venezuelan migrants.[1] Badlands framed her findings within a broader argument that non-governmental civil-rights litigants operate under lighter ethical scrutiny than defendants such as [[jeff-clark|Jeff Clark]] and John Eastman.[1]

## Role in the Ben'Ary removal (October 2025)

Kelly publicly flagged the prior role of federal prosecutor [[michael-benary|Michael Ben'Ary]] as senior counsel to former Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]], and Ben'Ary was reportedly fired from the [[eastern-district-of-virginia-us-attorneys-office|U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Virginia]] hours later.[2] Kelly told the *Washington Examiner* that her post directly contributed to the removal, saying President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "ran on de-weaponizing and cleaning up the systemically corrupt DOJ, but you can't do that if you have in powerful positions people who were top advisers to the very architects of the lawfare against the president."[2] She also suggested Ben'Ary may have been part of an "internal resistance" to the prosecution of [[james-comey|James Comey]], an assertion disputed by a person familiar with the matter who told the *Associated Press* he had no involvement in the case.[2]
