# Fatou Bensouda

Fatou Bensouda is the former chief prosecutor of the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]], whose tenure produced several of the investigations that continue to shape the court's confrontation with Israel and with Western intelligence services.[1]

## ICC investigation into the Palestinian territories

Bensouda opened a formal investigation in 2021 into alleged war crimes and crimes against humanity in the occupied Palestinian territories.[1] That investigation culminated under her successor, [[karim-khan|Karim Khan]], who in May 2024 announced that he was seeking an arrest warrant for Israeli prime minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and defence minister Yoav Gallant, alongside three [[hamas|Hamas]] leaders — an outcome Israel's military and political establishment had long feared.[1]

## Alleged pressure campaign by Yossi Cohen

According to reporting revealed in May 2024, [[yossi-cohen|Yossi Cohen]], the former head of the [[mossad|Mossad]], allegedly threatened Bensouda in a series of secret meetings in the years leading up to her decision to open the Palestine investigation, attempting to pressure her into abandoning the war crimes inquiry.[1] Badlands commentary noted that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration separately sanctioned Bensouda in 2019, with Secretary of State Mike Pompeo — described as a close friend of Cohen — driving that policy.[1]

## Congo and Afghanistan inquiries

Badlands analysis highlighted that Bensouda had previously investigated Cohen's activities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, conducted on behalf of Israeli mining magnate [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]].[1] Gertler was the third name listed on [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]] over child slave-mining camps and human trafficking in the Congo, and the commentary observed that Bensouda and Trump's team were independently investigating Gertler in 2017 and reached the same conclusion.[1] Bensouda was also investigating alleged CIA crimes in Afghanistan, which Badlands writers characterise as an opium trafficking operation tied to the opioid epidemic.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] maintained that the ICC itself is an illegitimate institution and that justice for such crimes should be adjudicated in American courts, while conceding that the threads of truth surfaced by Bensouda's inquiries "deserve attention."[1]
