# Sue Mi Terry

Sue Mi Terry is a former [[cia|CIA]] analyst and foreign-policy scholar who, at age 54 and living in Manhattan as a native of Seoul, was indicted in July 2024 on allegations that she acted as an unregistered agent of the South Korean government.[1] At the time of the indictment she was a senior fellow at the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], which placed her on administrative leave.[1] She is married to columnist [[max-boot|Max Boot]].[1]

## Allegations

The indictment alleged that Terry used her position as a prominent foreign-policy expert to trade access to senior U.S. officials in exchange for luxury goods and "high-end sushi dinners."[1] Prosecutors stated that she began working on behalf of South Korean intelligence in October 2013, five years after departing the CIA.[1] Over roughly a decade she was accused of having "disclosed sensitive US government information to South Korean intelligence and used her position to influence US policy in favor of South Korea" in return for "money and luxury gifts," according to a statement by FBI Acting Assistant Director in Charge Christie M. Curtis.[1]

Badlands coverage highlighted the timeline: Terry's alleged espionage began three years before her husband began characterizing [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as a Russian asset in the pages of the *Washington Post*.[1]
