# Mustafa Abdul Jalil

Mustafa Abdul Jalil was the post-Gaddafi Libyan leader identified in the article as having agreed to send Libyan weapons to Syria.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The Times quoted an arms dealer known as Abu Ahmad explaining the arrangement: "Libya has too many weapons and the international community wants to stabilise it. [Libyan leader Mustafa] Abdul Jalil wants to weaken the Libyan militias so there is an agreement to send these weapons to Syria."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) Ships carrying surface-to-air missiles from Libya anchored off the Lebanese coast, where the weapons were ferried ashore under cover of darkness and passed to Syrian rebel fighters.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

The article stresses that Abdul Jalil is the same leader whom [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] had supplied with weapons in order to overthrow Muammar Gaddafi, making the subsequent transfer of those arms to [[isis|ISIS]] in Syria a continuation of the same pipeline.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
