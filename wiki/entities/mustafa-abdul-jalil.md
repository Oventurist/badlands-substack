# Mustafa Abdul Jalil

Mustafa Abdul Jalil is a Libyan jurist and politician who served as Chairman of the National Transitional Council during the 2011 Libyan Civil War and later as interim President of Libya. In the Badlands Media corpus, he is identified as a central figure in the post-Gaddafi government and as a participant in the covert weapons-smuggling operation that diverted Libyan arms to terrorist organizations in Syria.

## Role in the Libyan Transition

Following the NATO intervention and the overthrow of [[muammar-gaddafi|Muammar Gaddafi]] in 2011, Abdul Jalil emerged as a leading figure in the transitional government. The source cites an arms dealer known as Abu Ahmad, who claimed that Abdul Jalil "wants to weaken the Libyan militias so there is an agreement to send these weapons to Syria." According to this account, Abdul Jalil made an agreement to ship weapons from Libya to Syrian rebel fighters, with vessels originating in [[benghazi|Benghazi]] and docking in Alexandria, Egypt before proceeding to Lebanon or Turkey.

## Connection to Benghazi and Weapons Smuggling

The article asserts that Abdul Jalil was the same Libyan leader that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] were providing weapons to in order to help overthrow Gaddafi. After Gaddafi's fall, the source claims he continued facilitating weapons transfers to [[isis|ISIS]] and other Islamist groups in Syria. The article notes that ships carrying shoulder fired, heat seeking, surface to air missiles from Libya were intercepted beginning in April 2012, with at least seven other vessels carrying weapons from Libya subsequently unloaded in northern Lebanon.
