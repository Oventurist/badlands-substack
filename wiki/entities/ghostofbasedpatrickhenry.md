# GhostofBasedPatrickHenry

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry is a writer and Badlands Brief contributor whose commentary focuses on [[israel|Israel]], the Middle East, and the narcotics war in the Western Hemisphere.[1]

## Israel and Turkey

In the July 28, 2026 Brief he identified "another pattern emerging": the last time [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] "tried to crash the White House," in September 2025 during the UN General Assembly, [[donald-trump|Trump]] reacted by inviting [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Erdogan]] to the White House the day before Netanyahu was to address the assembly — and with Netanyahu again inviting himself, Trump was again using Erdogan to signal his real intent.[1] He argued Trump's plan is not kinetic war against "America's greatest foreign adversary" but the destruction of Israel's public reputation by allowing it "the space to show the world what it truly is," with the war against Iran costing Israel fifteen years of accumulated goodwill.[1] He described Israel as an empire modeled on the ancient Sanhedrin, seeking "only the glory for itself," and said he would be watching Trump's engagement with Netanyahu, what he offers, what moves follow against [[turkey|Turkey]], and when Russia gets involved.[1]

## Cartels and Mexico

He recounted Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]]'s February 2025 claim that Colombian jungle militias were proxies of the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa cartel]], noted the Trump administration's indictment of ten Mexican officials for cartel ties, and criticized [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]]'s reluctance to help fight the cocaine cartels.[1] He asked why the press seems more concerned with whether the US military will invade [[mexico|Mexico]] than with Americans dying on behalf of a foreign country, argued that the cartels, press and corrupt politicians are all part of "a larger cartel that seeks to rule the world," and held that the only war America should fight is "the war to secure our border, secure our elections, and save our country" — with fully understanding "the treason and fraud that has occurred in and around our government" among its most important objectives.[1]
