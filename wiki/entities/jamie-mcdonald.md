# Jamie McDonald

Jamie McDonald is the individual President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] identified in June 2026 as his preferred replacement for [[jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]] as United States Attorney for the Southern District of New York.[1]

## Role in the DNI nomination standoff

McDonald's pending confirmation became a pivot point in the dispute over the directorship of national intelligence. Trump announced in June 2026 that he was canceling Clayton's Senate confirmation hearing to become director of national intelligence, saying he did not want to remove Clayton from his current post as US attorney until McDonald was confirmed as his successor.[1] Trump stated that the administration "will not be going forward until Jamie McDonald is approved to be US Attorney."[1]

The practical effect of the delay was to leave [[bill-pulte|Bill Pulte]] on track to assume the role of acting director of national intelligence, following the departure of [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]].[1] The standoff was entangled with negotiations over reauthorization of [[section-702|Section 702]] of the [[foreign-intelligence-surveillance-act|Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act]], which had lapsed the previous week.[1]
