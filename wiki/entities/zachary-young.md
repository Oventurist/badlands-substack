# Zachary Young

Zachary Young is a US Navy veteran and military contractor who sued [[cnn|CNN]] for defamation in June 2022 over a November 2021 network segment about private contractors hired to evacuate Afghans following the United States' withdrawal from Afghanistan.[1] Young claimed that the segment, broadcast on *The Lead with [[jake-tapper|Jake Tapper]]*, defamed him and his Afghan transportation business.[1]

## Trial

The case went to trial in January 2025, with reporters and CNN executives expected to be called to give evidence and network executives forced to take the stand.[1] Opening statements began on January 6, 2025, and the proceedings were livestreamed.[1] Because CNN is an institutional media defendant, the applicable defamation standard is actual malice, requiring the plaintiff to prove intent — a bar Badlands commentators described as notoriously difficult but not impossible.[1]

## Context

Media organizations watched the case closely to assess the limits of First Amendment free-speech protections, amid a broader rise in defamation litigation against US news networks.[1] In April 2024, [[fox-news|Fox News]] settled a suit for $787 million over false claims that voting machines were rigged in the 2020 election, and in December 2024 [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] settled his suit against [[abc-news|ABC News]] for $15 million over a broadcast claim that he had raped, rather than sexually assaulted, [[e-jean-carroll|E. Jean Carroll]].[1]
