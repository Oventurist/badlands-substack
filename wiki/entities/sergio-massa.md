# Sergio Massa

Sergio Massa is the Argentine politician who was the establishment candidate defeated by [[javier-milei|Javier Milei]] in Argentina's 2023 presidential run-off.[1] As the sitting economy minister and standard-bearer of the governing coalition, Massa personified the political status quo that Milei campaigned to "break up with," a contrast that Milei made the central theme of his outsider candidacy.[1]

## 2023 run-off and concession

Massa conceded the run-off vote on Sunday evening, 19 November 2023, in a brief speech delivered even before official results had been announced.[1] "Milei is the president elected for the next 4 years," Massa said, adding that he had already telephoned his opponent to congratulate him.[1] Provisional tallies at the time showed Milei with more than 55 percent of the vote — 13,781,154 ballots — with over 94 percent of votes counted, according to data from Argentina's National Electoral Chamber, which had not yet declared an official winner.[1] The contest unfolded against the backdrop of one of the world's highest inflation rates, a condition for which Massa, as economy minister, bore direct political responsibility in the eyes of voters.[1] See [[2023-argentine-general-election|2023 Argentine general election]].[1]
