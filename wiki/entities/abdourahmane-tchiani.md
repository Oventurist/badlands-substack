# Abdourahmane Tchiani

General Abdourahmane Tchiani is the military leader of [[niger|Niger]], having come to power in one of a series of successive coups in bordering West African nations.[1]

## Confederation treaty (July 2024)

In July 2024 Tchiani hosted the first joint summit of the three Sahelian military leaders — himself, Burkina Faso's Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]], and Mali's Colonel [[assimi-goita|Assimi Goïta]] — in the Nigerien capital, Niamey. The three signed a new confederation treaty celebrated as a step "towards greater integration" between their countries, strengthening the mutual defense pact established the previous year as the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]] (AES).[1]

The summit came months after the three states withdrew from the [[ecowas|Economic Community of West African States]] in January, and Tchiani described ECOWAS as "a threat" to their states after the bloc suspended the three countries and imposed sanctions on them. The treaty was read as extinguishing ECOWAS leaders' hopes for the trio's eventual return.[1]

## Context of Western withdrawal

The signing coincided with the completion of the withdrawal of United States military personnel from Niger's Air Base 101, and with the American departure from a separate $100 million drone base near Agadez in central Niger — developments presented as symbolizing the loss of Western colonial political and military influence in the Sahel.[1]

Badlands commentary framed these events approvingly as "Africa rising," noting that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] had deployed the [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] to Niger and that Nigeriens had welcomed the group with large celebrations on Russia's national holiday.[1] The same commentary asserted that the [[state-department|State Department]] had for years implied Africa was incapable of self-governance, and counted the AES as a friend and partner of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[1]

## June 2024 meeting with the Russian defense delegation

In early June 2024, Niger announced that Tchiani, its transitional leader, had received representatives of the [[russian-defense-ministry|Russian Defense Ministry]] in Niamey, where they discussed military, security, and economic cooperation.[2] The visit was part of a Sahel tour by a high-ranking delegation headed by Deputy Defense Minister [[yunus-bek-yevkurov|Yunus-Bek Yevkurov]] that also took in [[mali|Mali]], where interim leader [[assimi-goita|Assimi Goita]], Defense Minister [[sadio-camara|Sadio Camara]], and air force chief [[alou-boi-diarra|Alou Boi Diarra]] met the Russians.[2] Both [[niger|Niger]] and Mali, former French colonies, have severed military ties with France, accusing Paris of meddling.[2]
