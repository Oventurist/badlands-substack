# Correct the Record

**Correct the Record** is identified in the Badlands Media corpus as a superPAC created by [[David Brock]] with the sole purpose of supporting [[Hillary Clinton]]'s 2016 presidential campaign by monitoring and confronting social media users who posted unflattering comments about her. The source frames the organization as an example of the Clinton establishment's willingness to deploy digital trolling and economic pressure to control political discourse.

## Origins and Mission

According to the source, Brock spent millions of dollars to create Correct the Record with the explicit mission of scouring the internet and confronting anyone who posted unflattering comments about Hillary Clinton. The *Los Angeles Times* is cited as having reported in May 2016 that Correct the Record was spending approximately $1 million "to find and confront social media users who post unflattering messages about the Democratic front-runner."

## Tactics and Criticism

The source quotes the *Los Angeles Times* description of Correct the Record's activities: "In effect, the effort aims to spend a large sum of money to increase the amount of trolling that already exists online." The article frames this as part of a broader pattern of using financial resources to manufacture consent and punish dissent, rather than engaging in spirited debate.

## Relationship to Media Matters

The source presents Correct the Record as operating in parallel with [[Media Matters]], Brock's broader media pressure group. Together, the two organizations are described as having "fought tooth and nail" to ensure Hillary Clinton's victory in the 2016 election, attacking anyone who was even slightly critical of her. The article argues that this represents the weaponization of media infrastructure for partisan political ends.
