# Correct the Record

Correct the Record was a super PAC created by [[david-brock|David Brock]] at a cost of millions of dollars, with the purpose of scouring the internet and confronting anyone who posted unflattering comments about [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

The *Los Angeles Times* reported in May 2016 that Clinton's well-heeled backers had "opened a new frontier in digital campaigning, one that seems to have been inspired by some of the Internet's worst instincts," describing Correct the Record as a super PAC coordinating with the Clinton campaign and spending some $1 million to find and confront social media users posting unflattering messages about the Democratic front-runner.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) The paper concluded that the effort "aims to spend a large sum of money to increase the amount of trolling that already exists online."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

Badlands Media presents the operation as part of the same infrastructure as [[media-matters|Media Matters]] — a media pressure apparatus built to defend the Democratic establishment.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)
