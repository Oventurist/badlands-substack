# Roland Harriman

E. Roland Harriman (Yale class of 1917) was an American banker, the younger brother of [[averell-harriman|Averell Harriman]], and a very active member of [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]].[1]

Four of Roland's fellow Bonesmen from the class of 1917 were directors of [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]], among them [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]], father of [[george-hw-bush|George H. W. Bush]].[1] "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" describes the Harriman/Bush grouping as one of the most consequential clusters of Bonesmen, with Averell serving as an "Elder Statesman" of the Democratic Party.[1] In 1932 the [[union-banking-corporation|Union Banking Corporation]] of New York City enlisted four directors from the class of 1917 cell together with two Nazi bankers associated with [[fritz-thyssen|Fritz Thyssen]], who had been financing [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] since 1924.[1]
