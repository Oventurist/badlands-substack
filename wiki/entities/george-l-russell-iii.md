# George L. Russell III

George L. Russell III is the Chief Judge of the United States District Court for the District of Maryland.[1]

## Immigration habeas standing order

In May 2025 Russell issued a standing order for the Maryland federal bench requiring that every apparent illegal immigration case be granted a temporary injunction upon filing of a habeas corpus petition, with the injunction remaining in force until 4 p.m. on the second business day after filing unless extended by the presiding judge.[1] The order prompted the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] to file a complaint against all 15 judges of the district, calling the order an "egregious example of judicial overreach" — the dispute documented at [[doj-lawsuit-against-the-maryland-federal-bench]].[1]
