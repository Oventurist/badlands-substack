# Felix Maurizio

Felix Maurizio is a retired United States Navy Chief Petty Officer and World War II veteran who was singled out by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during his speech at the [[us-navy|US Navy]]'s 250th anniversary ceremony in Norfolk, Virginia, in October 2025.[1]

## Service

Maurizio, who turned 100 in 2025, served as a driver for a landing craft during the Second World War.[1] He was among the few Americans to serve in both the European and Pacific theaters, and landed at two of the war's most famous battles — Omaha Beach at Normandy and Iwo Jima.[1] He was also aboard the USS Talladega in Tokyo Bay in 1945 when the Japanese surrendered, ending the war.[1]

## Recognition

Badlands host [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted Trump's recognition of Maurizio in commentary on the Norfolk speech, closing with "Cheers to you, Felix Maurizio."[1]
