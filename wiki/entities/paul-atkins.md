# Paul Atkins

Paul Atkins is the Chair of the U.S. [[securities-and-exchange-commission|Securities and Exchange Commission]].[1]

## Corporate governance and proxy adviser rulemaking

Speaking to Fox Business on November 14, 2025, Atkins said the SEC would be issuing proposed rules and clarifications for proxy advisers.[1] He recalled that about a month earlier, in Delaware, he had outlined steps the commission would take "with respect to corporate governance and shareholder proposals and really the abuse of the corporate governance system and the weaponization of shareholder proposals by politicized shareholder activists."[1] Atkins added that "these particular advisory companies play a role," saying that "the charges of conflicts of interest are really legion" and that the agency had to address the issue overall.[1]

His remarks were flagged by Bloomberg, which reported that proxy advisers — firms providing voting guidance to shareholders — had come under greater scrutiny.[1] That scrutiny included a reported [[federal-trade-commission|Federal Trade Commission]] investigation into whether two proxy advisers violated federal antitrust laws by advising shareholders how to vote on politically charged topics for their own ideological reasons.[1]
