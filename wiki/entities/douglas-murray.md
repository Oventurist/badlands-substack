# Douglas Murray

Douglas Murray is a British conservative political commentator and author who became the subject of significant online argument in April 2025 after a contentious appearance on the *Joe Rogan Experience*.[1]

## Dispute on the Joe Rogan Experience

Appearing on the podcast alongside comedian [[dave-smith|Dave Smith]], Murray criticized host [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]] for granting a large platform to guests he characterized as spreading conspiracy theories or misinformation.[1] "Look, I just feel we should get it out straight away, I feel you've opened the door to quite a lot of people who now have got a big platform who have been throwing out counter-historical stuff of a very dangerous kind," Murray said on the episode, which aired on a Thursday in April 2025.[1]

Murray named several prior guests, focusing in particular on podcaster [[darryl-cooper|Darryl Cooper]], who had drawn attention for describing Winston Churchill as the "chief villain" of the Second World War and for revising the conventional narrative of the Holocaust.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writers read the exchange as an example of gatekeeper anxiety, arguing that Murray's public standing depends on a level of institutional trust that no longer exists and that an awakening public is therefore an existential threat to commentators in his position.[1] The commentary defended the questioning of established historical narratives, contending that such narratives are "spun" and depend on public trust to stick, that readers should be sparing with trust given how comprehensively it has been violated, and that the sheer volume of available information guarantees disagreement — "but truth does not run from disagreement."[1] On this reading, labeling alternative inquiry "dangerous" is only coherent if one is committed to something other than truth.[1]
