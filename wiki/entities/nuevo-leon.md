# Nuevo León

Nuevo León is a northern Mexican state situated near the U.S.–Mexico border and home to Monterrey, the second largest city in Mexico.[1] Its strategic border position and economic weight give its governorship outsized national significance.[1]

As of early 2023 the state was governed by [[samuel-garcia-sepulveda|Samuel García Sepúlveda]], then 35 years old and the only Mexican politician invited to attend the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]]'s 2023 Davos gathering.[1] Badlands Media commentary held that Nuevo León's location on the border made the state a natural target of globalist political interest.[1]
