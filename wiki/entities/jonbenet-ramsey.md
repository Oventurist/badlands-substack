# JonBenét Ramsey

JonBenét Ramsey was a six-year-old girl whose 1996 murder in Colorado shocked the state and drew sustained national attention.[1] Nearly three decades later the case remained unsolved.[1]

## Renewed investigation

In December 2025, the [[boulder-police-department|Boulder Police Department]] announced that new evidence had been collected in the long-running investigation into her death.[1] Her father, [[john-ramsey|John Ramsey]], told the Daily Mail in response that he was "optimistic — more than I've been in 29 years" about the possibility of finding her killer.[1]
