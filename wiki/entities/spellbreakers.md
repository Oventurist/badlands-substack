# Spellbreakers

Spellbreakers is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Wednesdays at 7:30pm ET as of January 2023, hosted by [[patrick-gunnels|Patrick Gunnels]] and [[matt-trump|Matt Trump]].[1] Its stated purpose is to expose what the hosts call flimflam and pseudoscience across the media and academia.[1]
