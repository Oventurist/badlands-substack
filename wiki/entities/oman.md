# Oman

Oman is the Gulf sultanate that has served as mediator for the indirect nuclear negotiations between the United States and [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## Mediation role in the 2026 talks

The second round of high-stakes indirect negotiations between Washington and Tehran, which concluded in Geneva in February 2026, was Oman-mediated.[1] Because the two governments do not negotiate face to face, Omani intermediaries carried positions between the U.S. delegation — which included special envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] — and the Iranian side led by Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]].[1] Iranian officials reported that the round produced an understanding on core principles aimed at resolving the long-running nuclear dispute, with detailed proposals to follow within two weeks.[1]

## US Embassy cable on Arab public opinion (November 2023)

In November 2023, a diplomatic cable sent from the US Embassy in Oman and obtained by CNN warned that Washington's strong support for [[israel|Israel]]'s military campaign in [[gaza|Gaza]] "is losing us Arab publics for a generation."[2] Citing conversations with "a wide range of trusted and sober-minded contacts," the cable stated that "we are losing badly on the messaging battlespace" and that US support for Israel's actions was being perceived "as material and moral culpability in what they consider to be possible war crimes."[2] The cable was among stark warnings American diplomats across the Arab world sent to the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] after Israel launched operations against [[hamas|Hamas]] in response to the October 7 attacks.[2]
