# Ksenia Sobchak

Ksenia Sobchak is a prominent Russian journalist, television personality and former presidential candidate.[1]

## Alleged 2023 assassination plot

In July 2023, Russia's Federal Security Service ([[fsb|FSB]]) announced that it had uncovered and thwarted a Ukraine-linked plot targeting [[margarita-simonyan|Margarita Simonyan]], editor-in-chief of RT, and that the same group was also preparing to target Sobchak.[1] According to RIA Novosti, members of a neo-Nazi group calling itself [[paragraph-88|Paragraph 88]] were arrested in connection with the alleged plot, which authorities said was disrupted before any attempt was made.[1] The suspects were alleged to have "carried out intelligence at the addresses of work and residence of Simonyan and Sobchak" — effectively casing their homes and workplaces.[1] A report in TASS cited six people detained in connection with the plot, expected to appear before a Moscow court.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cautioned that all Russian wartime media should be consumed with the understanding that propaganda is a given, while arguing that the underlying claim was plausible: prior deaths such as that of [[darya-dugina|Darya Dugina]] and war blogger Vladlen Tatarsky established that assassination campaigns against high-profile Russian media figures were real, and that journalists who threaten a wartime narrative are treated as enemy combatants.[1]
