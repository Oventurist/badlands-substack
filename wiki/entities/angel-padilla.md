# Angel Padilla

**Angel Padilla** is the policy director of the [[indivisible|Indivisible Project]], the left-of-center 501(c)(4) organization created in late 2016 in response to the election of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Background

Before joining Indivisible, Padilla worked as an analyst with the [[national-immigration-law-center|National Immigration Law Center]], an organization funded by grants from George Soros's [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], and as an immigration policy consultant at [[unidosus|UnidosUS]], formerly the National Council of La Raza.[1]

## Significance in Badlands coverage

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited Padilla's résumé as an example of the personnel overlap between Indivisible and the wider Soros-funded nonprofit network, part of his argument that Indivisible's claim to independence from the Democratic Party establishment is undercut by its funding and staffing ties.[1]
