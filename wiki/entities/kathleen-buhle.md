# Kathleen Buhle

Kathleen Buhle is the former wife of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and a close friend of former First Lady [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]].[1] Her 2017 divorce from Hunter Biden, and what Michelle Obama's associates described as the Biden family's subsequent exiling of Buhle, became the focal point of reporting in June 2024 on private tension between the two political families.[1]

According to accounts relayed in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief, Michelle Obama privately expressed frustration over the treatment of Buhle, and believed her friend had been wronged.[1] That frustration, together with the former First Lady's stated disdain for partisan politics, was cited as part of the reason she had not campaigned for [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s re-election even as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] appeared at fundraisers and in campaign videos alone.[1]

The Brief also placed the Buhle divorce in a longer chronology of Biden family turmoil: relations between the families were said to have changed in 2015, the year then-Vice President Biden weighed a presidential run without Obama's encouragement and the year his son Beau died of cancer.[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] dismissed the coverage as "a (too) long gossip piece," arguing the substantive story lay instead with Michelle Obama's [[when-we-all-vote|When We All Vote]] organization.[1]
