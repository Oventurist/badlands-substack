# Anthony Summers

Anthony Summers is an Oxford-trained BBC producer, described as the youngest BBC producer in history, who has played a leading role in constructing the narrative that the Kennedy brothers were responsible for the death of [[marilyn-monroe|Marilyn Monroe]].[1] His 1985 book *Goddess: The Secret Lives of Marilyn Monroe* became a New York Times bestseller and remains the foundational text of that narrative.[1]

## The 650 interviews

Summers collected roughly 650 interviews that were used in the [[netflix|Netflix]] film *The Mystery of Marilyn Monroe: The Unheard Tapes*.[1] Commenting on that body of material, professor Sarah Churchwell wrote that "the interviews often repeated second or third hand accounts of what someone else said, and neither Summers nor his sources is particularly dispassionate."[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argued that Summers has a great deal to lose should the edifice of Bobby Kennedy's supposed murder of Monroe collapse, given his central authorship of it since 1985.[1] It was only after the publication of Summers's book that private investigator [[fred-otash|Fred Otash]] first publicly claimed to have wired Monroe's home — twenty-three years after her death.[1]
