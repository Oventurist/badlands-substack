# Ofcom

**Ofcom** (the Office of Communications) is the United Kingdom's communications regulator, described by Badlands Media as sitting "at the heart" of the censorship apparatus created by the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]].[1]

## Role under the Online Safety Bill

The bill's long-form title explicitly frames the legislation as providing "for and in connection with the regulation by OFCOM of certain internet services."[1] Under the resulting regime, Ofcom is empowered to enforce a ruleset against technology platforms, with non-compliance exposing them to enormous fines and, ultimately, a decision on whether the platform should be banned in the UK.[1]

## Leadership

Ofcom's then-recently appointed Chief Executive, [[melanie-dawes|Dame Melanie Dawes]], publicly welcomed the bill's passage, stating that "Everyone at Ofcom feels privileged to be entrusted with this important role, and we're ready to start implementing these new laws."[1] Badlands characterized her tone as "genuinely gleeful."[1]
