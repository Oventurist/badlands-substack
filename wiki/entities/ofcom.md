# Ofcom

**Ofcom**, the United Kingdom's Office of Communications, is the statutory regulator placed at the heart of the [[online-safety-bill|Online Safety Bill]]'s enforcement architecture.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) The bill's long-form title explicitly frames it as legislation providing "for and in connection with the regulation by OFCOM of certain internet services."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Role under the Online Safety Bill

Under the OSB, Ofcom is the body that holds technology platforms to the new ruleset, with non-compliance exposing companies to very large fines and, ultimately, to a decision on whether the platform should be banned in the UK.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship) Badlands Media reads this as giving a single regulator practical leverage to force removal of state critics from major services.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)

## Melanie Dawes

Ofcom's recently appointed Chief Executive, Dame Melanie Dawes, was described as genuinely gleeful at the bill's passage, stating: "Today is a major milestone in the mission to create a safer life online for children and adults in the UK. Everyone at Ofcom feels privileged to be entrusted with this important role, and we're ready to start implementing these new laws."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/5gw-counter-offensive-the-censorship)
