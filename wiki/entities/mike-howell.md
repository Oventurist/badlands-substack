# Mike Howell

Mike Howell is a conservative oversight advocate associated with the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]]'s transparency litigation efforts. He was named as one of the parties to whom the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] agreed to release redacted audio and transcripts from Special Counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s investigation into [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of classified documents.[1]

Biden intervened in the resulting litigation seeking to block release of the [[mark-zwonitzer|Zwonitzer]] recordings, but a lower court denied a preliminary injunction and the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-dc-circuit|D.C. Circuit]] rejected his emergency appeal in July 2026.[1]
