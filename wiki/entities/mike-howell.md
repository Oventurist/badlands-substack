# Mike Howell

Mike Howell is a transparency litigator associated with the [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]] and named individually as a recipient of records from Special Counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s investigation into [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s handling of classified documents.

According to the July 22, 2026 Badlands Brief, the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] initially withheld the bulk of the Hur materials — including audio and transcripts of Biden's 2016–2017 conversations with ghostwriter [[mark-zwonitzer|Mark Zwonitzer]] — under FOIA exemptions, but later agreed to release redacted versions to Howell, the Heritage Foundation, and the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]].

Biden intervened in the resulting litigation to block release. After a district court denied his motion for a preliminary injunction, he sought emergency relief from the [[dc-circuit-court-of-appeals|D.C. Circuit]], which denied it and extended only a short administrative stay to August 3, 2026. Howell's requests therefore stand, in the Badlands account, as the procedural mechanism by which the Hur audio was pried loose from the executive branch.
