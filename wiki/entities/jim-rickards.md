# Jim Rickards

Jim Rickards is a macroeconomist and CIA advisor cited in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] for his warnings about the future of the U.S. dollar.[1]

## Views on dollar replacement

Commentary by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] stated that, if Rickards is to be believed, the [[federal-reserve-bank-of-new-york|New York Federal Reserve]]-sponsored [[project-cedar|Project Cedar]] pilot — together with [[executive-order-14067|Executive Order 14067]] — could be one of several avenues leading to the creation of a new currency, and ultimately a trackable [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currency]].[1] A video presentation by Rickards on the subject was linked from the underlying Breitbart report.[1]
