# Adil Abdul-Mahdi

Adil Abdul-Mahdi was the Prime Minister of [[iraq|Iraq]] at the end of 2019 and the beginning of 2020, during the confrontation between the United States and Iran-backed militias in his country.[1]

## The Baghdad embassy crisis

After protesters drawn largely from the [[popular-mobilization-forces|Popular Mobilization Units]] attacked the United States embassy in Baghdad on December 31, 2019, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] emphasised in a call with the Iraqi prime minister that Iraq had a responsibility to protect the embassy and American personnel.[1] Badlands Media notes that Baghdad had warned that its relations with Washington were at risk following the earlier American strikes on Kataib Hezbollah facilities, with Iraqi officials accusing the United States of acting without sufficient evidence, violating Iraqi sovereignty and threatening the country's security.[1] Questions were raised as to whether Iraqi forces had allowed the demonstrators to reach the heavily fortified embassy compound at all.[1]

## Connection to the Soleimani strike

On 3 January 2020, Iranian major general [[qasem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]] was killed by a United States drone strike near Baghdad International Airport while on his way to meet Abdul-Mahdi.[1] Badlands Media stresses the sequencing: the man Trump had personally warned about protecting American personnel was the very official Soleimani was travelling to see when he was killed.[1] The author frames the episode as part of a broader message from Trump to every leader in the Middle East that they were either on the American side against Islamic extremism or on the enemy's side, and highlights the chain of connections between Iraq, [[iran|Iran]] and [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1]
