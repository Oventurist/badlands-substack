# Carlton Reeves

Carlton Reeves is a United States District Judge in Mississippi, appointed by President Barack Obama.[1]

## Criticism of qualified immunity

In May 2024, Reeves criticized the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]] over its qualified immunity doctrine.[1] Ruling in a false accusation case in Mississippi, he wrote that the doctrine — established by the Supreme Court, and which shields state and local officials from individual liability — was "an unconstitutional error."[1]

## Context

Newsweek placed Reeves among at least three judges who had recently rebuked the Supreme Court, alongside retired Judge [[david-tatel|David S. Tatel]] and Hawaii Supreme Court Justice [[todd-eddins|Todd Eddins]], describing a growing willingness among judges on both sides of the ideological spectrum to warn publicly about the future of American democracy.[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed in the Badlands News Brief that all three critics were Democratic appointees and cautioned readers against absorbing the resulting fear, uncertainty and doubt about the Court.[1]
