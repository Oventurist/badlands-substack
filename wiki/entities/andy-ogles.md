# Andy Ogles

Andy Ogles is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Tennessee.[1]

## Articles of impeachment against Kamala Harris

On Tuesday, July 23, 2024, Ogles introduced articles of impeachment against Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], as first reported by Politico's Olivia Beavers.[1] The articles accused Harris of "high crimes and misdemeanors," asserting that she "has willfully and systemically refused to uphold Federal immigration laws" and that, in her conduct of the office of Vice President, she "has willfully prevented, obstructed, and impeded the administration of justice."[1] They further alleged that Harris had "demonstrated extraordinary incompetence in the execution of her duties."[1]

The filing came days after President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] announced on July 21, 2024 that he would not seek reelection, after which Harris emerged as the likely Democratic nominee.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] endorsed the move as a useful political play, expressing hope that the articles would address the alleged cover-up of an incapacitated president, which she characterized as a genuine high crime.[1] She argued that impeachment proceedings in the House would function as a reminder of the 117th Congress under Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], and cautioned Republicans to pursue fact- and evidence-based articles rather than campaign theatrics — invoking [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]]'s doctored-tweet episode as a negative example.[1]

## Impeachment articles against Biden and Harris (June 2023)

On June 12, 2023, Ogles introduced articles of impeachment against President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[2] The articles accused Biden of having "weaponized" the presidency, in both his presidential and vice-presidential tenures, to "shield the business and influence peddling schemes of his family from congressional oversight and public accountability."[2] They further charged that his handling of the southern border crisis amounted to acting in a "manner contrary to the public trust and subversive of constitutional government, to the great prejudice of the cause of law and justice and to the manifest injury of the people of the United States."[2]

Ogles told Fox News that "Joe Biden has repeatedly abused his position of power, both as vice president and president, to cover up his illicit family business dealings and exploitation of taxpayer resources."[2] Reporting at the time noted the effort faced an uphill climb in the House, where only a small minority of Republicans had pushed for impeachment.[2]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] welcomed the move, observing that Ogles's decision to target both Biden and Harris was unprecedented, comparing the articles' "manifest injury" language to the Nixon (1974), Clinton (1998) and [[donald-trump|Trump]] (2021) impeachment articles, and arguing that unlike those cases the Biden allegations rested on documented evidence from the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Biden laptop]].[2]
