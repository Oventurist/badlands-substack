# Steve Friend

**Steve Friend** is a former [[fbi|FBI]] special agent who became a whistleblower on the bureau's internal priorities.[1]

Friend was cited during the [[2023-wray-house-judiciary-testimony|July 2023 House Judiciary Committee hearing]] as one of multiple whistleblowers disputing [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]]'s claim that the FBI did not reallocate resources away from sex-crimes investigations in order to surveil parents at school board meetings.[1] According to that account, Friend and others had been working sex-crime cases before being reassigned to tracking the license plates of parents outside school board meetings.[1]
