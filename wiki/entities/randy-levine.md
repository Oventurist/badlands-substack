# Randy Levine

Randy Levine is the president of the [[new-york-yankees|New York Yankees]] baseball franchise.[1]

On September 11, 2025, Levine hosted President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a private box at Yankee Stadium during the club's game on the anniversary of the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]].[1] The crowd greeted Trump's appearance on the stadium screens during the national anthem with a mix of loud cheers and boos.[1]
