# Angela Paxton

Angela Paxton is a Republican member of the Texas Senate and the wife of Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]].[1]

During her husband's 2023 impeachment trial in the [[texas-senate|Texas Senate]], Angela Paxton was seated as a member of the court of impeachment but was prohibited from voting on the sixteen articles brought against him, a restriction adopted to address the obvious conflict of interest.[1] Her husband was acquitted on all counts on Saturday, September 16, 2023, with four additional articles held in abeyance and later dismissed.[1]
