# Christine Lagarde

Christine Lagarde is the president of the European Central Bank and a former managing director of the International Monetary Fund.[1]

At the January 2024 [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meeting in Davos, [[blackrock|BlackRock]] vice chairman [[philipp-hildebrand|Philipp Hildebrand]] invoked Lagarde's assessment when warning that a [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] election victory could produce a transatlantic "rupture" that would challenge Europe fundamentally.[1] Hildebrand said such a rupture was "what President Lagarde has in mind as a risk."[1]
