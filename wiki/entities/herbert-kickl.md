# Herbert Kickl

Herbert Kickl is an Austrian politician and the leader of the Freedom Party of Austria (FPÖ).[1]

## 2024 Austrian parliamentary election

Following preliminary results on Sunday, September 29, 2024, Kickl hailed a "new era" after the FPÖ became the first far-right party to win an Austrian parliamentary election since the Second World War.[1] The result reflected a broader European trend toward anti-immigration and populist parties, but the FPÖ was considered unlikely to form the coalition government required under Austrian law, because every other party had ruled out governing with it.[1]

The sole partial exception was Chancellor [[karl-nehammer|Karl Nehammer's]] outgoing conservative People's Party (ÖVP), but Nehammer refused to work with Kickl personally, citing the FPÖ leader's belief in conspiracy theories and stating, "You can't run a state sensibly and responsibly with him."[1]
