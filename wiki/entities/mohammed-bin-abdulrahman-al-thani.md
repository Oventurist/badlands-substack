# Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani

Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani is the Prime Minister of [[qatar|Qatar]] and a central figure in the Gulf state's mediation diplomacy. On September 23, 2024, he accompanied Qatari Emir [[tamim-bin-hamad-al-thani|Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani]] to a meeting with former U.S. president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at the Mar-a-Lago resort in Florida.[1]

## Role in Qatari mediation

Qatar's leadership, including its prime minister, has served as the principal intermediary between Israel and [[hamas|Hamas]] during the Gaza war, an effort for which the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] was repeatedly complimentary.[1] Doha has argued that the request to open a Hamas political bureau in the Qatari capital originally came from the United States, and that successive Democratic and Republican administrations supported the bureau's continued operation.[1]

## Mar-a-Lago meeting

Trump publicized the Mar-a-Lago meeting in a Truth Social post praising the Qatari leadership, calling the emir "a great and powerful leader of his country, advancing on all levels at record speed" and someone who "strongly wants peace in the Middle East and all over the world."[1] The meeting occurred while Qatar was under sustained criticism from Republican lawmakers over its hosting of Hamas and its funding of the [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]] news network.[1]

## December 2023 hostage mediation

On December 18, 2023, the Qatari prime minister met [[cia|CIA]] Director [[william-burns|William Burns]] and [[mossad|Mossad]] director [[david-barnea|David Barnea]] in Poland in a renewed push to free dozens of hostages held in Gaza.[2] The same group had met twice the prior month in Doha with Egyptian intelligence officials, and al Thani had held bilateral talks with Barnea the preceding Friday — the first effort by either side to restart negotiations after the Israel–[[hamas|Hamas]] ceasefire collapsed on December 1.[2]
