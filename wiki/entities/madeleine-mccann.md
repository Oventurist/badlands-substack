# Madeleine McCann

Madeleine McCann is a British child who disappeared in 2007, at the age of three, while on a family holiday in Portugal, and whose case remains one of the most prominent unsolved missing-child investigations in Europe.[1]

## 2023 DNA claim

In March 2023 a Polish woman, [[julia-wendell|Julia Faustyna]], who also goes by Julia Wendell, submitted samples for forensic testing to determine whether she is the missing British girl.[1] Then 21 years old, she had been claiming on Instagram and TikTok — posting under the handle @iammadeleinemccann — that she might be McCann on the basis of similarities in age and appearance, and had amassed tens of thousands of followers on both platforms as a result.[1]

Faustyna submitted samples for three separate forensic examinations intended to outline her DNA sequence, alongside a 23andMe-style genetic test to establish her ancestry, according to private investigator Dr. Fia Johansson.[1] Johansson said that if results came back indicating British or regional ancestry, "we are going to continue our investigation into Madeleine McCann and communicate with the detectives in Portugal."[1] Johansson had relocated Wendell from Poland to a safe house in the United States following a series of death threats.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor AbsoluteTruth1776 wrote that the development "could end up being very, very interesting" should the woman prove to be McCann, and recalled that artist's sketches of suspects circulated in connection with the disappearance had been noted by some observers for an eerie resemblance to [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] and his brother Tony Podesta.[1] The contributor concluded that if the identification were confirmed, what followed would be "anyone's guess."[1]
