# Lewis Lichtenstein Strauss

Lewis Lichtenstein Strauss was a Virginia-born businessman, philanthropist and naval officer who served two terms on the US [[atomic-energy-commission|Atomic Energy Commission]], the second as its chairman.[1] He is portrayed by Robert Downey, Jr. in Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer*.[1]

## Role in the film

Strauss is the face of the political and military establishment that Nolan contrasts against the socially conscious, pragmatic [[communism|communist]] scientists, and he is painted as a villain in both the film and the historical record.[1] The Badlands review reads the film's depiction of the stripping of [[j-robert-oppenheimer|J. Robert Oppenheimer]]'s security clearance as a premeditated public humiliation ritual — the pivot on which the Strauss antagonism turns.[1]
