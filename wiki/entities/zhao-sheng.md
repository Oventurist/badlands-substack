# Zhao Sheng

Zhao Sheng is a Chinese diplomat who arrived in Kabul in September 2023 as China's new ambassador to Afghanistan.[1]

On Wednesday, September 13, 2023, the [[taliban|Taliban]] hailed Zhao's arrival with unusual fanfare, saying it was a sign for other nations to come forward and establish relations with the administration.[1] His car swept through the tree-lined driveway of the Presidential Palace escorted by a police convoy, and he was greeted by uniformed troops before meeting top-ranking Taliban officials, including Mohammad Hassan Akhund, who heads the administration, and Foreign Affairs Minister [[amir-khan-muttaqi|Amir Khan Muttaqi]].[1] It was the first time since the Taliban takeover that an ambassador to Kabul had been afforded such lavish protocol.[1]

China, the world's second-largest economy, is among only a handful of nations maintaining working diplomatic missions in Afghanistan, and both sides have been open about their desire for closer ties, especially commercial ones.[1] Taliban leaders remain under sanctions and no country formally recognizes them as Afghanistan's legitimate rulers; Afghanistan's seat at the United Nations is still held by the former Western-backed government led by Ashraf Ghani.[1]
