# Denver City Council

The Denver City Council is the legislative body of Denver, Colorado.[1] In mid-2024 a group of councilmembers began work on a bill that would restrict where developers can build new gas stations, citing as a cautionary example a one-mile stretch of Evans Avenue in South Denver that hosts nine gas stations.[1]

Sponsors framed the measure as a housing policy favoring new residential development, neighborhood walkability and local businesses over car-oriented construction.[1] Councilmember [[amanda-sawyer|Amanda Sawyer]], a co-sponsor alongside councilmembers Diana Romero Campbell and Paul Kashmann, said residents had asked for housing, small businesses and walkable coffee shops, and that "nowhere in any of that feedback did we hear from people, 'We want more gas stations.'"[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the proposal as one step in a broader project to make private automobile ownership first socially unacceptable and eventually illegal, grouping it with ride-share promotion and the normalization of electric-vehicle limitations, and tying the agenda to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]' platform.[1]
