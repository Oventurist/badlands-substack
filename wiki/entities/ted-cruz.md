# Ted Cruz

**Ted Cruz** is a Republican United States Senator from Texas. In Badlands coverage he appears as a defender of congressional contacts with foreign governments and as a figure associated with the pro-Israel, Iran-hawk bloc in the Senate.[1]

## Fox News exchange over the Graham–Netanyahu meetings

The March 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] reported that Fox News host [[laura-ingraham|Laura Ingraham]] questioned Cruz about what she described as "secret meetings" between Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] and Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] Cruz responded that members of Congress routinely meet with foreign officials as part of ordinary diplomatic engagement.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the exchange as evidence of a broader problem, writing that Cruz "can't even explain their behavior to a Fox News host," and framing the episode as part of a pattern in which members of Congress openly boast of coordination with foreign leaders while pushing for confrontation with [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## Context

The exchange took place as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] pursued negotiations aimed at limiting Iran's nuclear program while simultaneously authorizing US military strikes as part of joint operations, a dual track that split Republican legislators into pro-escalation and anti-war camps.[1]

## Opposition to the US–Iran deal (June 2026)

In June 2026, Cruz was singled out by Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, who wrote that the senator was "making an absolute ass out of himself" in his public reaction to the [[us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding|US–Iran memorandum of understanding]].[2] The commentary placed Cruz among the Republicans it argued had proven useless and might attempt to torpedo an earnest peace between Iran and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and grouped him with the "Zionists in both Israel and the GOP" it said were walking into a trap.[2]

## The Verdict and Coca-Cola's BLM language

On his podcast *The Verdict with Ted Cruz*, in an episode aired October 18, 2023, Cruz called out [[coca-cola|Coca-Cola]] for its support of [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] after the organization's Chicago chapter posted an image of a paraglider captioned "I stand with Palestine" — a reference to the [[hamas|Hamas]] attackers who paraglided into an Israeli music festival on October 7 and killed hundreds of attendees.[3] The following morning Coca-Cola quietly deleted the corresponding language from its website.[3]

## 2028 positioning and feud with Tucker Carlson

In November 2025, reporting described Cruz as laying the groundwork for a 2028 presidential bid by leaning into his feud with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] and staking out territory as a traditional, pro-interventionist Republican.[4] By attacking Carlson's isolationist foreign policy views and accusing him of antisemitism, Cruz placed himself on a collision course with Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], a Carlson ally widely seen as the 2028 GOP frontrunner, and waded into the ideological divisions over Israel then straining President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s MAGA movement.[4]

"We have a responsibility to speak out even when it's uncomfortable," Cruz said in a statement. "When voices in our own movement push dangerous and misguided ideas, we can't look the other way. I won't hesitate to call out those who peddle destructive, vile rhetoric and threaten our principles and our future. Silence in the face of recklessness is not an option."[4] Carlson dismissed the gambit as "hilarious," adding only, "Good luck. That's my comment and heartfelt view."[4]

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized the feud as kayfabe, arguing that Cruz, Carlson, and Vance were merely pretending to compete to become the next set of rulers after Trump, and that public officials who lie constantly while serving interests other than those of their constitutional constituents are abusing positions of public trust.[4]

## "The Verdict" interview with James Comer (July 2023)

On his podcast "The Verdict," Cruz interviewed [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight]] chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] in July 2023, during which Comer disclosed that his committee had received additional bank records and was preparing a third memorandum that he said would show transactions from Ukraine and Russia linked to the Biden family.[5]

## Smurfing allegations

In June 2025 Badlands commentary reported that, according to Wisconsin-based investigator Peter Bernegger — a primary investigator of donation smurfing and a frequent guest on Why We Vote — Cruz is the number one recipient of smurfed donations in the nation.[6] The claim was raised alongside calls to extend the ActBlue fraud investigation to the Republican platform WinRed.[6] Commentators recalled Cruz's dismissal of the suggestion that he was funded by AIPAC on the grounds that his money came from small independent donors, and questioned whether those nominal individuals were in fact conduits for illegal contributions washed through identities without their knowledge or consent.[6] The accompanying take insisted that party affiliation is no excuse for laundering foreign money and that every violator should be prosecuted.[6]
