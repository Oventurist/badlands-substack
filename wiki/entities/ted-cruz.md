# Ted Cruz

Ted Cruz is a Republican United States Senator from Texas. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears principally as a congressional voice on Middle East policy and as a figure associated with hawkish positions toward [[iran|Iran]] and close alignment with [[israel|Israel]].

## Questioning over Graham's Netanyahu meetings

The March 10, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Cruz was pressed during a [[fox-news|Fox News]] segment by host [[laura-ingraham|Laura Ingraham]] about what she described as "secret meetings" between Sen. [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] (R-SC) and Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]. Cruz responded that members of [[congress|Congress]] routinely meet with foreign officials as part of normal diplomatic engagement.

The Brief's commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the exchange as evidence of a broader collapse of the pro-war position within the Republican Party, writing that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had wielded the narrative so effectively that "Ted Cruz can't even explain their behavior to a Fox News host—that's how bad this is." The same commentary situates Cruz within a group of legislators the author characterizes as "Christian Dispensationalists in Congress," a reference to the [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionist]] current the corpus associates with support for military action against Iran.
