# Sadiq Khan

Sadiq Khan is the Mayor of London and, before entering politics, a practising lawyer. He is identified in Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series as the "Mayor of London" referenced by Q in drop #100, dated November 5th, 2017.[1]

## Q drop #100 reference

Drop #100 groups the Mayor of London together with the British monarchy, migration policy and occult symbolism, a cluster of questions the series reads as pointing to compromised leadership in the United Kingdom.[1] The Badlands treatment answers Q's prompt by naming Khan directly.[1]

## Legal career claims

The series states that Khan served as the lawyer for [[zacarias-moussaoui|Zacarias Moussaoui]], described in the article as a hijacker connected to the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]], and that he defended other accused terrorists during his time in legal practice.[1] The article frames this professional history as evidence of ideological sympathy rather than as ordinary criminal-defence work, and further asserts in polemical terms that Khan's mayoralty has transformed London for the worse.[1] These characterisations are the author's opinion and are presented without supporting documentation in the source.
