# Tony Shaffer

Lt. Col. Tony Shaffer is a retired senior intelligence operations officer who served more than thirty years with the U.S. Army and who has written public commentary on American defense policy.[1]

## Commentary on NATO burden-sharing

Writing in The Daily Caller on 18 July 2018 — the day after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] met [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] in Helsinki — Shaffer argued that [[nato|NATO]] allies had been "ripping off America in trade while also relying on American taxpayers to pay their defense bills for decades," and that Trump's pressure on them to pay their fair share embodied his America First agenda.[1] Shaffer quoted Trump's public complaints that the United States was spending far more on NATO than any other country and that the [[european-union|European Union]] maintained a large trade surplus with the United States alongside significant barriers to American goods.[1]

Shaffer singled out [[germany|Germany]] for claiming it needed protection from Putin while purchasing large quantities of gas from Russia, thereby helping to fund the very adversary it named — a posture he called, "in a word, insane."[1] He noted that in the preceding year the United States accounted for 51.1 percent of the combined GDP of all NATO countries and 71.7 percent of alliance defense spending, contributing more than Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Canada and the United Kingdom combined.[1]

## Use in Badlands Media commentary

Joe Lange's "The Master Builder" cites Shaffer's article as documentation that Trump deliberately "prepped the battlefield" by forcing European members of NATO to shoulder their own defense costs, a step the piece frames as part of a larger strategy to weaken the European central banks and the [[deep-state|Deep State]] financially.[1]
