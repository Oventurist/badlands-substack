# Habsburg Dynasty

The **Habsburg dynasty**, otherwise known as the House of Austria, was one of the most prominent royal houses of Europe and among the families represented at the 1815 Congress (Treaty) of Vienna.[1]

## Historical sketch

After taking over the Holy Roman Empire and marrying into nearly every royal court in Europe, the Habsburgs sought to preserve the "greatness" of their bloodline by marrying within their own family.[1] The resulting consanguinity produced the pronounced facial deformity popularly known as the "Habsburg jawline," and Badlands commentary described the family as the most notorious victim of aristocratic inbreeding, with descendants who were eventually too impaired to function.[1]

## Use as a political metaphor

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked the dynasty in October 2024 as an analogy for the leadership of the [[european-union|European Union]].[1] He characterized the bloc as "a menagerie of wannabe aristocrats, desperately trying to LARP the 1815 Treaty of Vienna" — the settlement at which, after the defeat of Napoleon and the end of the French Revolution, Europe's disgraced aristocrats convened to restore the continent's monarchies and devised systems to persecute free-thinkers, including blacklists distributed to every university and newspaper in Europe.[1] He noted the irony that those blacklisted became unemployable and emigrated to America, "which only made America that much more based."[1] He concluded that the European Union had "proven itself to be an even more egregious disaster than the Habsburgs' genetic sequencing."[1]
