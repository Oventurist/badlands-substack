# Fairfax County Public Schools

Fairfax County Public Schools is Virginia's largest school system, and in March 2023 it became the subject of national coverage after removing a test question that equated political ideology with specific racial and gender demographics.[1]

## AP Government test question controversy

The question appeared on a test given to students in a college-level Advanced Placement Government class at the district's Online Campus, and the school system moved to remove it after it was publicized.[1] The multiple-choice item asked students, "Which of the following is an accurate comparison of liberals versus conservatives?"[1]

The potential answers listed under "liberals" included "Young, white males," "Middle aged, urban lesbian," "College-educated black male professional" and "White, upper-middle class suburban male."[1] Those listed under "conservatives" included "East Coast, Ivy League educated scientists," "Southern male migrant laborer," "Catholic, midwestern middle-aged male" and "West coast, Hispanic teacher."[1]

Rory Cooper, a Fairfax County parent and critic of the school system who posted the question on Twitter, said it was offensive.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] argued that material of this kind was holding the United States back in global education rankings, and that [[critical-race-theory|CRT]], equity and inclusion programming achieved the opposite of their stated aims by dividing children rather than unifying them or teaching the life skills needed to succeed.[1] He asked what the nation's future would look like if academic performance were not turned around immediately, and concluded that the episode demonstrated why parents, rather than the school system, should be the arbiters of their children's education.[1]
