# Ukrenergo

Ukrenergo is Ukraine's national power grid operator. In early September 2024 its supervisory board removed the company's chief executive, [[volodymyr-kudrytskyi|Volodymyr Kudrytskyi]].[1]

The decision came days after Russia's August 26 drone and missile attack, described as the most massive since the beginning of the full-scale invasion, which primarily targeted Ukraine's energy infrastructure and caused significant electricity supply disruptions across the country.[1] The resulting energy crisis was addressed at a meeting of the Supreme Commander-in-Chief's Staff, where President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] instructed Kudrytskyi to submit his resignation.[1]

The board's decision was prompted by incomplete protective structures around Ukrenergo power facilities, which allegedly failed to prevent blackouts following the Russian attacks; four of the six supervisory board members voted in favor of the dismissal.[1]
