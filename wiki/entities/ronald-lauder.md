# Ronald Lauder

Ronald Lauder is the president of the [[world-jewish-congress|World Jewish Congress]] (WJC), who in May 2025 publicly criticized Israeli Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] over his rhetoric on the Gaza war.[1]

## Jerusalem conference remarks

Speaking at a WJC conference in Jerusalem on Tuesday, May 20, 2025, Lauder accused the Israeli government of failing to understand the damage Smotrich's inflammatory statements were doing to the country's reputation.[1] He directed his comments to Foreign Minister [[gideon-saar|Gideon Sa'ar]], who was attending the meeting: "Let me tell you, all the good things that Israel is doing are being destroyed by Smotrich. Because his statements about starving the people and destroying [Gaza] were played all over the world, and the prime minister has the chance to stop him from saying these things, and will not do it. The question is, why not?"[1]

The remarks drew applause from much of the audience, which included Jewish delegations from around the world.[1] Sa'ar responded only that he "took note" of what Lauder had said.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] answered Lauder's rhetorical question by pointing to coalition arithmetic: [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] is described as beholden to Smotrich, whose [[religious-zionism-party|Religious Zionist Party]] controls 14 seats in the [[knesset|Knesset]]; were that faction to withdraw, Netanyahu's 68-seat majority would fall below the 60-seat threshold required to remain Prime Minister.[1] By contrast, [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]]'s Jewish Power Party controls only six seats, so his January resignation — since reversed — still left Netanyahu with a 62-seat majority.[1]
