# PNC Financial Services Group

PNC is a large U.S. regional banking group that emerged in April 2023 as one of the bidders for the assets of the failing [[first-republic-bank|First Republic Bank]].[1]

## Role in the First Republic resolution

Reporting by the Wall Street Journal, relayed by ZeroHedge, identified PNC alongside [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan]] among the big banks set to buy First Republic in a transaction structured to follow a government seizure rather than a private, market-arranged sale.[1] The structure meant acquiring banks would take the desirable assets while losses on the remainder fell to the [[federal-deposit-insurance-corporation|FDIC]] and, indirectly, U.S. taxpayers.[1]

Commentary in the Badlands brief treated the episode as part of a pattern in which government-brokered rescues concentrate assets in a small number of very large institutions, comparable to the [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]]–[[ubs|UBS]] arrangement in Switzerland.[1]
