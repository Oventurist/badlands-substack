# Qana Gas Field

**Qana Gas Field** is an offshore natural gas field in the eastern Mediterranean, located in waters off the southern Lebanese coast near the city of Tyre. In the Badlands corpus it is identified as an economic prize that [[israel|Israel]] has long sought to annex "through lawfare" — a goal the 2026 ground campaign is now positioned to accomplish by force.

## Annexation through the Forward Defense Zone

The Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026 argues that by moving the Israeli border north toward Tyre through the creation of a [[forward-defense-zone|"Forward Defense Zone"]] in southern Lebanon, Israel can justify shifting the maritime boundary and annexing the Qana Gas Field. The analysis presents the field as the economic payoff of the border changes the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] is effecting on the ground: the new deployment line places dozens of villages inside an Israeli-controlled zone extending roughly 5 to 10 kilometers from the border, and the adjusted boundary line would extend Israeli claims into the gas-bearing waters.

## Context

The field's name echoes the Qana massacre of 1996, and the corpus treats the gas field as one of the concrete territorial objectives underlying the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel project]], alongside the capture of [[mount-hermon|Mount Hermon]]. The Brief presents the combination of mountain and gas field as evidence that the conquest of southern Lebanon is proceeding according to a deliberate plan that no ceasefire has interrupted.
