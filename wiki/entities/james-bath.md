# James Bath

**James Bath** is a Texas businessman who served as the North American representative for two wealthy Saudis and relatives of Osama bin Laden: billionaire [[salem-bin-laden|Salem bin Laden]] and banker and [[bank-of-credit-and-commerce-international|BCCI]] insider [[khalid-bin-mahfouz|Khalid bin Mahfouz]].[1]

According to the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] material quoted in Badlands Media commentary, [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] made his first Middle East connection in the late 1970s through Bath.[1] Bath put $50,000 into Bush's 1979 [[arbusto-energy|Arbusto]] oil partnership, probably using Bin Laden–Bin Mahfouz funds.[1]

The article flags Bath, Salem bin Laden and Khalid bin Mahfouz as three names to remember, describing them as major players whom most readers have never heard of and promising further treatment in a subsequent article.[1] It concludes that George W. Bush had major ties to the Bin Laden family, who funneled money to him through the Arbusto partnership.[1]
