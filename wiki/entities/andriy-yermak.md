# Andriy Yermak

Andriy Yermak is a Ukrainian political figure who served as chief of staff to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] and who, as of May 2026, had been named the sole suspect in a major Ukrainian money-laundering investigation.[1]

## Career and role

Yermak was a longtime trusted confidant of Zelenskyy and acted as Ukraine's lead negotiator in talks with the United States, making him one of the most influential figures in the Ukrainian government during the war with [[russia|Russia]].[1]

## Money-laundering investigation

Ukrainian anti-corruption authorities named Yermak as the sole suspect in an investigation into roughly 460 million hryvnia — about $10.5 million — allegedly laundered through elite construction projects near Kyiv.[1] He resigned in November amid the scandal, which was described as the biggest threat to Zelenskyy's government since Russia's full-scale invasion, and his home was searched that same month.[1] Investigators from the [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau]] and the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office had not yet filed formal charges, a step that could take months.[1]

Yermak's lawyer called the suspicion groundless and politically motivated, while Zelenskyy's office said it was too early to draw conclusions.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Yermak was unlikely to be the only Ukrainian official involved in money laundering, framing [[ukraine|Ukraine]] as "a giant crime scene" and suggesting that members of the U.S. [[congress|Congress]] may have been aware of the schemes given their eagerness to send aid.[1]

## Mystic adviser testimony (2026)

Testimony presented in a Ukrainian court case reportedly claimed that a former senior adviser and chief of staff figure connected to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] relied on guidance from a mystic and feng shui consultant when making major political and personnel decisions.[2] The allegations emerged during proceedings tied to corruption and influence investigations, adding a further layer of controversy to internal disputes within Ukraine's political establishment.[2]

The claims fueled renewed scrutiny of the opaque power dynamics surrounding Zelensky's inner circle as Ukraine navigated wartime governance, Western dependency, and intensifying political factionalism. Critics argued the testimony reflected broader concerns about informal influence networks operating alongside official state institutions, though the allegations remain disputed and politically charged.[2]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] examined images of the consultant and her family and concluded that they appeared unremarkable rather than occult, noting that her father had reportedly been a citizen of Russia since moving to Crimea in 2015, possibly to avoid conscription.[2] He suggested the more plausible reading was not mysticism but an affair — "yet another grift to get his girlfriend paid" — characterizing the episode as typical of the Zelensky administration's patronage practices.[2]

The story surfaced while Yermak was on trial for large-scale corruption and as another figure associated with the government, [[timur-mindich|Timur Mindich]], was accused of embezzling hundreds of millions of dollars before fleeing to Israel to escape prosecution.[2]

## Resignation over the nuclear energy scandal

Yermak, chief of staff to President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]], resigned following the November 2025 discovery by Ukraine's [[national-anti-corruption-bureau-of-ukraine|National Anti-Corruption Bureau]] and the Special Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office of a $100 million corruption scheme involving the state-owned nuclear energy operator.[3] Justice Minister [[german-galushchenko|German Galushchenko]] and Energy Minister [[svetlana-grinchuk|Svetlana Grinchuk]] resigned over the same scandal.[3]

## Alleged role in a plot against Zelensky (2025)

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reported allegations of a plot to overthrow [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] involving US officials, Yermak as top Zelensky aide, and Ukrainian military intelligence chief [[kyrylo-budanov|Kirill Budanov]], with claims that [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] may have had knowledge of the meetings or met directly with [[valerii-zaluzhnyi|Valery Zaluzhny]] in London.[4] According to reports attributed to Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR), Zelensky's raid on Ukraine's anti-corruption agencies was Yermak's idea and was actually a ruse designed to turn Western leaders against Zelensky so they would support a coup against him.[4] The same reports held that Yermak and Budanov had negotiated with Zaluzhny to secure their places in his prospective regime.[4]

## Replacement as lead negotiator (November 2025)

Yermak, [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s lead negotiator in peace talks, resigned after prosecutors searched his home in connection with a corruption investigation tied to alleged embezzlement of tens of millions of dollars from the energy sector.[5] Ukraine subsequently sent a new team to continue negotiations with U.S. and Russian representatives on a peace deal, with talks held in Florida over the weekend of 29–30 November 2025 involving Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], special envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and adviser [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]].[5]

Badlands commentary asked who was now leading the Ukrainian side, describing Yermak as having "run away with the hundreds of millions of dollars he helped embezzle and steal," and used his downfall to press broader questions about why American money had been sent to Ukraine and why [[congress|Congress]] had allowed it.[5]
