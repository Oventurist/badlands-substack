# Somalia

Somalia is a Horn of Africa state that has become a southern anchor of [[turkey|Turkey]]'s expanding overseas military footprint.[1]

## Turkish F-16 deployment

According to unnamed Somali officials and satellite imagery analyzed by Middle East Eye, Turkey is deploying additional F-16 fighter jets abroad, extending its defense presence from [[azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]] to Somalia.[1] Badlands commentary framed the two deployments as covering the northern and southern access points to the Middle East, and as sitting astride two critical trade routes connecting Asia to Europe.[1] In this reading, the basing is described as "absolute power projection, but conducted in a benevolent act of brotherhood—not a sinister scheme to start a conflict," and as part of the broader architecture of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] alongside the Turkey–Egypt and Turkey–[[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]]–[[pakistan|Pakistan]] defense pacts.[1]

## Mogadishu elections (December 2025)

Somalia reportedly held its first genuine elections since 1969 on December 25, 2025.[2] Dr Hussein Sheikh Mohamud, Senior Advisor to the President on Constitutional Affairs and Deputy Secretary General of the Justice and Solidarity Party, wrote that after "56 years of appointed councils, clan negotiations, and backroom deals, over 233,000 Mogadishu residents actually cast votes for their local representatives," describing "Real voters. Real ballots. Real counting."[2] Several opposition groups boycotted the vote, citing a lack of inclusivity, a questionable legal framework and a rushed timeline; the presidential advisor countered that "they boycotted because they knew they would lose."[2]

## Dispute with the Trump administration

In early January 2026 President [[hassan-sheikh-mohamud|Hassan Sheikh Mohamud]] issued a public statement condemning remarks attributed to US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had reportedly said in December that the United States would "go the wrong way, if we keep taking in garbage into our country," and called Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] and her friends "garbage."[2] Mohamud responded that millions of Somalis contribute positively to societies around the world, while adding on immigration that "we respect the sovereignty of other nations when it comes to immigration enforcement."[2] The exchange coincided with continued US prosecutions in the [[feeding-our-future|Feeding Our Future]] fraud scheme, in which Somali-specific indictments were filed as recently as November 2025.[2]

## Somaliland recognition dispute (January 2026)

In early January 2026, Somalia's president said that [[somaliland|Somaliland]] had agreed to resettle Palestinians from Gaza and to host an [[israel|Israeli]] military base in exchange for international recognition, according to reports carried by the BBC; Somaliland authorities publicly denied that any such agreement existed.[3] Several governments in the Middle East and North Africa — including Jordan, Qatar, and Algeria — denounced any Israeli move to recognize Somaliland as a violation of Somalia's territorial integrity.[3]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] placed the dispute within "Conflict C: The War for Logistics" in the Horn of Africa, arguing that the underlying contest concerns control of the Red Sea and Suez Canal, through which roughly 30% of global shipping container traffic passes.[3] In his account, the Somalian president's accusation concerned Israeli bases in the Gulf of Aden intended for operations against the Houthis in Yemen, while Sudan's civil conflict is tied to planned Russian naval construction on the Sudanese coast.[3]
