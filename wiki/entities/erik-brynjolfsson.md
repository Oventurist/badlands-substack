# Erik Brynjolfsson

Erik Brynjolfsson is an American economist at [[stanford-university|Stanford University]] known for research on the economic effects of digital technology and, more recently, of artificial intelligence on employment.[1]

## Research on AI and young workers

Research led by Brynjolfsson, cited in March 2026 reporting on Washington's response to AI, found that employment among workers aged 22 to 25 in the most AI-exposed occupations had fallen 16 percent relative to their peers since AI tools became widely available in late 2022.[1] The finding was presented alongside a study referenced by [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] estimating that widespread AI adoption could replace up to 7 percent of U.S. jobs.[1]

Those results fed a broader debate in which public concern was rising — a February 2026 YouGov/Economist survey found about 63 percent of Americans expected AI to reduce jobs — while [[congress|Congress]] had held hearings and introduced bills but passed no legislation on the question.[1]
