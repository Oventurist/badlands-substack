# Bill Gates (Maricopa County)

Bill Gates — not to be confused with the Microsoft co-founder — is the Republican chairman of the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]] who served as the county's public face during the disputed [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] described Gates as "folksy, singsongy, friendly" and derided his November 2022 public-explainer campaign as a comedy show she titled "Arizona Elections for Dummies with Mr. Bill," writing that she could not watch the videos because they made her stomach turn.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) Over the weekend of November 19, 2022, Gates posted a series of videos to the @MaricopaCounty Twitter account answering voters' questions about how the county runs elections; one addressed the transport trucks that run between the county's elections department and its outside vendor, an operation connected to [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

## PTSD claims (May 2023)

In May 2023, Gates said publicly that he was suffering from post-traumatic stress disorder and "crying" as a result of criticism over "election denialism" following the 2020 and 2022 elections in [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]].[2] Reporting by The Gateway Pundit characterized him as a "so-called Republican" and a "pathological liar," questioning why the claim should be believed.[2] Coverage noted that PTSD is a condition commonly associated with war veterans who witnessed atrocities in service.[2]

Quoting Washington Post reporting by Yvonne Wingett Sanchez, the article recalled that when the board met in late November 2020 to accept the election results, Gates compared the supervisors' constitutional duty to uphold those results to the experience of his grandfather, who "went to Europe to fight for democracy" during World War II.[2]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] denounced the comparison, writing that Gates was equating "being a Deep State patsy for personal gain to going overseas, fighting and dying for your country."[2] DeLarme linked Gates to the disputed Arizona gubernatorial midterm and to county official Steven Ritcher, and speculated that Gates was either willingly corrupt, brainwashed, or buckled under pressure from interests with a stake in the outcome.[2] He argued Arizona's midterm was the most critical for those interests to win, citing the state's emergence as the nation's largest semiconductor producer, its role as a corridor for illegal immigration and the flow of guns, drugs and human trafficking allegedly funding black operations, and fear of an "America first" governor in [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]].[2]
