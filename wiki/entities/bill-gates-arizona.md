# Bill Gates (Arizona politician)

Bill Gates is an Arizona attorney and Republican local official who served as chairman of the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]] during the 2022 general election. He is unrelated to the Microsoft co-founder of the same name (see [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]]).

## Public communications after the 2022 election

In the weeks following the November 8, 2022 election, Gates became the county's principal public explainer of its election processes. [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]], in [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]], describes him as "the folksy, singsongy, friendly Chairman" and mocks the effort as a comedy show she dubs "Arizona Elections for Dummies with Mr. Bill," noting that over one weekend he "posted video after video on the @MaricopaCounty Twitter feed, answering voters' questions." A November 19, 2022 post from the county account promoted a video in which Gates addressed the transport trucks running between the county recorder's office and its vendor, [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]]. Gillespie writes that she "just can't watch them all" and that the ones she has seen "make my stomach turn."

Gates's chairmanship coincided with the ballot-on-demand printer failures at "at least 60 voting locations," the [[door-3-ballots|Door 3]] ballot handling problems, and the November 19 letter from Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] questioning whether [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] could lawfully certify its results before the November 28 canvass deadline.
