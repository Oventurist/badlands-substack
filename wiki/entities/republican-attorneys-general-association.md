# Republican Attorneys General Association

The Republican Attorneys General Association (RAGA) is a national organization of Republican state attorneys general.

In October 2025, Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] released FBI files showing that RAGA was among the entities that received records requests in the [[arctic-frost|Arctic Frost]] probe run by special counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]].[1] Grassley made public 197 subpoenas issued to 34 individuals and 163 businesses, including financial institutions, seeking records and communications related to more than 430 individuals and organizations, all of which appeared to be aimed at Republicans.[1] Alongside RAGA, the late conservative figure [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]]'s [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] was named as a recipient of such requests.[1] Grassley characterized the probe as a "fishing expedition" that became "the vehicle by which FBI agents and DOJ prosecutors could improperly investigate the entire Republican political apparatus."[1]
