# Kary Mullis

**Kary Mullis** was the American biochemist credited as the inventor of the polymerase chain reaction (PCR) test.[1]

## Death

Mullis, described as otherwise healthy, "died suddenly" of pneumonia in August 2019 — a timing Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] flagged as notable because it fell in the same window in which the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] abruptly reversed course and began expanding the [[federal-reserve-balance-sheet|Federal Reserve balance sheet]] again, roughly six months before the [[covid-19-lockdowns|COVID lockdowns]].[1] Carlson noted that a couple of months after Mullis's death, COVID was rumored to have been unleashed at the Military World Games in Wuhan, China, after which citizens of Wuhan were reported to be dropping dead in the streets from the so-called "bat virus."[1]

## Criticism of Fauci and the PCR test

Mullis was extremely critical of [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], saying that Fauci fundamentally did not understand science and would say or do anything to make money or gain power.[1] Mullis also stated that the PCR test he himself invented could not be used to test for viruses.[1] Carlson emphasized that these criticisms were made at least six months before most of the public had any idea who Fauci was.[1]
