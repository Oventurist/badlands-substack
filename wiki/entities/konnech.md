# Konnech

Konnech is a Michigan-based election software company that became a focus of election-integrity researchers in 2022 after allegations that data on United States election workers had been stored on servers in China.[1] In Badlands Media's coverage, Konnech is presented as one of two vendor stories — the other being [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck]] — whose simultaneous exposure in late 2022 was taken as evidence of a coordinated timeline for revealing nationwide election fraud rather than a coincidence.[1]

## Data-storage allegations and the Yu prosecution

The company tracks the behavior and scheduling of election workers. According to reporting in the New York Post, the founder and chief executive of Konnech, [[eugene-yu|Eugene Yu]], was arrested in October 2022 on suspicion of stealing personal information belonging to United States election workers and illegally storing it on Chinese computer servers.[1] Under the terms of Konnech's contracts, the company was required to keep its data inside the United States and to provide access only to citizens and permanent residents; the Los Angeles County District Attorney's office alleged that the information was instead held on servers in the People's Republic of China.[1]

The [[los-angeles-county-district-attorney|Los Angeles County District Attorney's office]] subsequently moved to dismiss the case without prejudice, citing concerns about the pace of the investigation and potential bias in the presentation of evidence.[1] Because the dismissal was without prejudice, nothing legally bars a renewed indictment of Yu by the Los Angeles District Attorney or by prosecutors in any other jurisdiction where Konnech held contracts.[1]

## Role in the Badlands narrative

Konnech's exposure has been credited in Badlands coverage to [[gregg-phillips|Greg Phillips]] and fellow researchers associated with the Professional Intelligence Team (PIT), who publicized the company's alleged ties to the Chinese Communist Party.[1] Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argued that the Konnech matter implicates national security and predicted further indictments and revelations concerning both Konnech and Runbeck.[1]
