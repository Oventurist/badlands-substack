# Mohamed Bahi

Mohamed Bahi is a former aide to New York City Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] who served as the city's community affairs liaison to the Muslim community.[1]

## Guilty plea (August 2025)

On Tuesday, August 12, 2025, Bahi pleaded guilty in Manhattan federal court to one count of conspiracy related to organizing illegal campaign contributions, resolving one of the outstanding indictments that arose from multiple investigations into the first-term Democratic mayor.[1]

During a four-minute plea allocution, Bahi said he had been instructed by a volunteer with the Eric Adams 2021 election campaign to organize a fundraiser in December 2020 at which he collected straw-donor campaign contributions from employees that both he and the campaign knew would in fact be reimbursed by the employees' company owners.[1] Reading from a written statement, he added, "I understood that the Adams campaign would then seek matching funds for their contributions."[1]
