# Popular Will

**Popular Will** (*Voluntad Popular*) is the Venezuelan opposition party of [[juan-guaido|Juan Guaidó]].[1]

On 1 February 2019, Dr. Jill Stein — linking to a *Gray Zone* article on the making of Guaidó — described the party as "US-funded" and "known as the most violent faction of Venezuela's rightwing opposition, with a history of sabotage, brutal street violence and a nasty habit of burning people alive."[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites the characterization in support of his argument that Guaidó, far from being a spontaneous democratic champion, was the product of a decade-long project overseen by Washington's regime change trainers.[1]
