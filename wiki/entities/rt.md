# RT

RT is a Russian state-controlled international news television network funded by the Russian government.[1]

The outlet was the original source for October 2023 reporting of remarks by Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] criticizing United States policy in the Middle East following the [[october-7-attacks|Hamas attack on southern Israel]].[1] RT carried Putin's statement that the crisis was "a glaring example of the failures of the US Middle East policies," his claim that Washington had tried to monopolize the peace settlement while ignoring compromises acceptable to both parties, and his call to minimize harm to civilians on both sides of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.[1] Those remarks, delivered during a Moscow meeting with Iraqi Prime Minister [[mohammed-shia-al-sudani|Mohammed al-Sudani]], were subsequently recirculated internationally, including by the Hindustan Times.[1]

## Coverage of Kursk and Venezuela (October 2024)

Badlands cited RT in its October 16, 2024 news brief on two fronts. Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry wrote that although the brief used a ZeroHedge link for the Russian breakthrough in [[kursk-incursion|Kursk]], he preferred RT's version of the story, which centered on Major General [[apty-alaudinov|Apty Alaudinov]] and the [[akhmat-special-forces|Akhmat Special Forces]].[2] He added a sidebar calling RT "a fantastic source of accurate news information" and describing the [[state-department|State Department]] and the corporate media in its proximity as "a laughing stock" by comparison.[2]

The brief also excerpted RT's report that Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] had accused [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] of spending at least $1 billion to incite violence in Venezuela after the July 28 presidential election, naming [[maria-corina-machado|Maria Corina Machado]] as the alleged distributor of the funds.[2]

## Reporting on the Il-76 shootdown findings

RT reported in June 2024 that the [[russian-investigative-committee|Russian Investigative Committee]] had accused senior Kiev military officials of downing a Russian Il-76M carrying Ukrainian prisoners of war with a US-made [[patriot-missile-system|Patriot]] interceptor missile on January 24, killing all 74 people aboard.[3]
