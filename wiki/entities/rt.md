# RT

RT, formerly Russia Today, is a Russian state-funded international broadcaster and news website. Widely restricted or banned across Western jurisdictions, it remains a frequently cited source in the alternative-media ecosystem, including the Badlands corpus, where its reporting is used as a counterweight to Western wire coverage.

The March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief draws on RT twice. It cites an RT report of March 16 that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had threatened media organizations with treason charges over their coverage of the war with [[iran|Iran]], accusing outlets of amplifying AI-fabricated material produced in Tehran. The same RT reporting dates the regional conflict to late February 2026, when United States and [[israel|Israeli]] forces launched coordinated strikes on Iran and Tehran retaliated across the region. The Brief separately cites an RT program aired March 15, 2026 in which former CIA officer [[john-kiriakou|John Kiriakou]] expressed concern for the safety of [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]].
