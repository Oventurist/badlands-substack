# Holden Armenta

Holden Armenta is a Kansas City Chiefs fan who, at the age of nine, was falsely accused of wearing blackface by a [[deadspin|Deadspin]] reporter in late 2023.[1]

Armenta, who is of Native American descent, attended a Chiefs game with his face painted in team colors and wearing a headdress.[1] Deadspin writer [[carron-phillips|Carron Phillips]] wrote that the child had "found a way to hate Black people and the Native Americans at the same time."[1]

His parents, Shannon and Raul Armenta, hired the law firm [[clare-locke|Clare Locke LLP]] to demand a retraction and threatened to sue Deadspin, its publishers G/O Media and Great Hill Partners, and Phillips personally.[1] The firm had previously secured a $787.5 million settlement against Fox News for [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]].[1]
