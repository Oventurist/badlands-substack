# Philermenian Society

The **Philermenian Society** is a Brown University society identified in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the mechanism through which [[brown-university|Brown]] allegedly grooms "identity enforcers."[1]

The article claims the society "fuses performative activism with NGO money to export ideological weaponry," channeling students into [[george-soros|Soros]]-affiliated NGOs and social movement engineering foundations such as [[echoing-green|Echoing Green]].[1] These organizations, the author argues, act as "domestic regime-change agents, destabilizing culture from within via grievance amplification and identity fragmentation," making the Philermenian Society the entry point to Brown's role as the "DEI cathedral" of the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
