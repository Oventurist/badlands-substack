# Mosab Hassan Yousef

**Mosab Hassan Yousef** is a former Israeli intelligence officer and the son of a founder of [[hamas|Hamas]], a biography that has made him a recurring figure in Western commentary on the Israeli–Palestinian conflict.[1]

## June 2026 remarks

Yousef spoke at the Jerusalem News Syndicate's summit in June 2026, at a moment of visible friction between the Trump administration and [[israel|Israel]] over the new US–[[iran|Iran]] oversight mechanism for [[lebanon|Lebanon]] that excluded Israel.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized his remarks as sounding like a threat of an Israeli attack against the United States, and emphasized the irony that the son of a Hamas founder had worked for [[mossad|Mossad]].[1] The commentary framed the outburst as frustration at Washington's refusal to continue deferring to Israeli policy preferences.[1]
