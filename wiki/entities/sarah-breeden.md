# Sarah Breeden

**Sarah Breeden** is a Deputy Governor of the [[bank-of-england|Bank of England]].[1]

In December 2025 Breeden announced the participation of private-market firms in the central bank's newly launched stress test of the private equity and private credit industries, saying that "a range of alternative asset managers that are active in private markets, including major global players, have agreed to participate in this exercise."[1] The system-wide exploratory scenario, due to produce a final report in early 2027, was designed to examine the consequences of a financial shock for the British economy as a whole rather than to publish vulnerabilities of individual firms.[1]
