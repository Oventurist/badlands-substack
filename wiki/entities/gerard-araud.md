# Gérard Araud

Gérard Araud is a former French ambassador to the United States and a frequent commentator on transatlantic relations.[1]

In December 2025 Araud reacted to the release of the [[2025-national-security-strategy|new U.S. National Security Strategy]] with an X post observing that "the stunning section devoted to Europe reads like a far-right pamphlet," and noting that the document "largely confirms" perceptions that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is an "enemy of Europe."[1] His remark was among the most widely quoted European reactions to the strategy, which called for asserting U.S. dominance in the Western Hemisphere and "cultivating resistance" within Europe.[1]
