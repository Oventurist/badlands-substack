# Israel Ganz

Israel Ganz is an Israeli settler leader who in 2024 was named chairman of the [[yesha-council|Yesha Council]], the umbrella organization of West Bank settlements.[1]

## 2024 Finance Ministry meeting

Shortly after his appointment, Ganz attended a meeting convened by finance minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] at the Finance Ministry, alongside the mayors of the largest West Bank settlements such as Beitar Illit, Modiin Illit and Maale Adumim.[1] Smotrich used the meeting to warn settler leaders that international efforts to advance Palestinian statehood constituted a "tangible, developing danger" and to describe his campaign to press Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] to authorize new settlement construction plans.[1]
