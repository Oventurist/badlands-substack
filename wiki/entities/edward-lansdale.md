# Edward Lansdale

Edward Lansdale was a United States psychological warfare specialist who commanded Operation Phoenix in Vietnam.[1]

In 1968 [[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]] was employed in Operation Phoenix directly under Lansdale's command, where, in Matthew Ehret's account, Aquino helped oversee the assassination of more than 40,000 civilians whose existence supposedly aided the Vietcong.[1] The program specialized in psychological warfare, torture, terror and mass murder, and innovated the philosophy of "burning the village to save the village."[1]
