# Riley Gaines

Riley Gaines is a former collegiate swimmer and conservative activist opposed to the participation of transgender athletes in women's sports.[1] In June 2025 she became the focus of a widely covered online exchange with Olympic gymnast [[simone-biles|Simone Biles]] over the issue.[1]

## Dispute with Simone Biles

The exchange began after Gaines criticized a Minnesota softball team that included transgender player Marissa Rothenberger, whose pitching shutout helped bring a state championship to Champlin Park High School.[1] Responding to a photograph of the team celebrating, Gaines wrote, "To be expected when your star player is a boy."[1] Biles publicly rebuked her, urging that she "should be uplifting the trans community" and proposing a separate transgender category in all sports, and adding that "no one in sports is safe with you around."[1]

Gaines replied that "It's not my job or the job of any woman to figure out how to include men in our spaces," and that "Men don't belong in women's sports and I say that with my full chest."[1] In a further reply she invoked Larry Nassar, the convicted former Team USA doctor who abused Biles and other gymnasts, writing that "All the horrific sexual abuse @Simone_Biles witnessed and spoke out against caused by one man, yet [she] believes women should be forced to strip naked in front of men to validate the man's feelings," and adding, "You know how many gold medals you'd have if your 'inclusive' dream came true? Zero."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands correspondent [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the exchange not as an influencer beef but as a "digital, rhetorical homicide," arguing that women bear no obligation to make themselves unsafe or to accept biological disadvantages in elite athletics for the sake of accommodating men, and noting that Biles herself does not compete against transgender athletes and therefore has no direct stake in the question.[1]

## San Francisco State University incident (2023)

In April 2023, Gaines was reportedly assaulted while attempting to speak about her views on transgender issues at [[san-francisco-state-university|San Francisco State University]], and had to be removed by police to a locked room for hours for her own protection.[2] Legal commentator [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] described the episode as the latest example of protesters "deplatforming" speakers with opposing views by shouting them down, and criticized the university's subsequent statement for failing to condemn the mob or promise accountability, instead appearing to express sympathy for the protesters.[2]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that Gaines was "providing a catalyst to expose the malicious and iniquitous character of the trans-activists," and called for a united conservative response grounded in conversation with families and communities rather than open confrontation.[2]
