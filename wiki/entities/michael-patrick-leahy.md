# Michael Patrick Leahy

Michael Patrick Leahy is the CEO and Editor-in-Chief of [[star-news-digital-media|Star News Digital Media]], the network of state-focused conservative news outlets that includes *The Arizona Sun Times*.[1]

Following the December 9, 2022 filing of [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s election contest, Leahy appeared on Steve Bannon's *War Room* to give an overview of the suit and to offer his assessment of the judge assigned to hear it in the [[maricopa-county-superior-court|Maricopa County Superior Court]]. The interview was posted on Rumble.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] cited the segment as her bridge into a broader complaint about the Arizona judiciary, writing that "if there's one thing in America that is more evil than most of the politicians, it's most of the judiciary."[1]
