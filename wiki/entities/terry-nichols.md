# Terry Nichols

Terry Nichols is identified in the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" as [[timothy-mcveigh|Timothy McVeigh]]'s prime accomplice in the April 19, 1995 [[oklahoma-city-bombing|Oklahoma City bombing]].[1]

Within a matter of days of the blast at the [[alfred-p-murrah-federal-building|Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building]], authorities used forensic evidence to link both Nichols and McVeigh to the explosion.[1] The article contrasts the speed of that attribution with the destruction of the building's debris before a thorough examination could be completed, and with ignored eyewitness accounts of more than one bomb, as grounds on which researchers have argued the event was a government-sponsored [[false-flag-operation|false flag]].[1]
