# Temple of Set

The Temple of Set is a theistic Satanist organization founded by Colonel [[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]] as a breakaway from [[anton-lavey|Anton LaVey's]] [[church-of-satan|Church of Satan]], taking 28 of LaVey's members with him as founding priests.[1]

The Temple grew out of Aquino's Summer Solstice "magical working" of 21 June 1975, in which he claimed Satan appeared to him disguised as Set and proclaimed the Aeon of Set — a successor to the Aeon of Horus said to have begun with [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley's]] 1904 Cairo working.[1] Aquino instituted the inverted pentagram as the group's symbol, adopted degrees modelled on the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, and took the title of Ipsissimus, with Lilith Sinclair serving as high priestess.[1]

Aquino was the Temple's founder and high priest, promoting "theistic Satanism" while serving as a US Army psychological warfare specialist.[1] Despite the nominal split, the Temple of Set and the Church of Satan supported each other and both promoted Crowley's Thelemic doctrine, "Do What Thou Wilt Shall be the Whole of the Law."[1] Aquino's rituals at [[wewelsburg-castle|Wewelsburg Castle]] were described in his book *Crystal Tablet of Set* as intended to recreate an order of knighthood for followers of Satan.[1]
