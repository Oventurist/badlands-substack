# Henry Rosovsky

Henry Rosovsky was Dean of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] in the 1970s. "The Harvard Control Grid" reports that Rosovsky "reportedly enlisted CIA Deputy Director [[john-mccone|John McCone]] to advise Harvard on managing its Arab investments during the oil embargo."[1]

The article treats the episode, described as little-known today, as precedent-setting: it demonstrates that "Harvard could, and did, consult with intelligence leaders on economic positioning."[1] The claim is offered as part of a broader case that Harvard's endowment decisions have long intersected with U.S. strategic statecraft.[1]
