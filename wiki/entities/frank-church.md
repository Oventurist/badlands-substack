# Frank Church

Frank Church was a Democratic member of Congress from Idaho who in 1975 headed the congressional committee investigating the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] that came to bear his name, the [[church-committee|Church Committee]].[1]

## The 1975 investigation

The committee was assembled to look into a CIA that many believed had become out of control, wielding far more power than was ever intended when the agency was created in 1947, and using it in ways never contemplated.[1] Under Church's chairmanship, CIA Director [[william-colby|William Colby]] was interviewed under oath multiple times and divulged a great deal of hidden information about the agency and its operations that the CIA did not want exposed.[1]

The sessions brought to public attention [[mk-ultra|MK ULTRA]], a CIA-run mind control operation described as creating unwitting assassins through drugs, torture and other psychological techniques; CIA surveillance of American politicians and civil rights leaders; and assassinations of foreign leaders together with operations to overthrow foreign governments.[1] Perhaps most surprising was [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]], the CIA program to manipulate not only foreign but domestic American media.[1]

## Legacy

In Badlands Media commentary by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the Church Committee's findings are cited as established fact demonstrating heavy CIA involvement in manipulating the press — even as the media continues to frame Operation Mockingbird as conspiracy theory, as when [[abc-news|ABC News]] called [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|RFK Jr.]]'s June 2024 allegations "bonkers" despite the acting CIA director having testified to the same before Church's committee in 1975.[1]
