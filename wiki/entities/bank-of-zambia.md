# Bank of Zambia

The Bank of Zambia is the central bank of [[zambia|Zambia]]. In July 2024 it drafted new currency rules aimed at curbing the use of foreign currencies — particularly the US dollar — in domestic transactions, as reported by the country's national news agency.[1]

## 2024 currency regulations

Under the draft, regulations were to be issued as a statutory instrument by the minister of finance and national planning, requiring the [[zambian-kwacha|Zambian kwacha]] and its subunit, the ngwee, to be used for all domestic public and private transactions.[1] The central bank raised concern that the increasing use of the dollar within the local economy undermined its monetary policy tools and exerted pressure on the exchange rate.[1]

A draft document released by the bank revealed that individuals caught using foreign currency for local transactions could face up to ten years in prison or substantial fines.[1] The penalties were announced by the central bank's deputy governor for operations, [[francis-chipimo|Francis Chipimo]], in a speech at a trade fair in Ndola.[1]

The Badlands News Brief treated the measure as a marker of accelerating de-dollarization, arguing that the perceived flooding of Zambia's economy with a rapidly inflating currency could be read as a tactic to destabilize a country whose currency had recently been the best-performing in Africa against the dollar.[1]
