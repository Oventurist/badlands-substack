# Optimus

Optimus is the humanoid robot developed by [[tesla-inc|Tesla Inc.]], which chief executive [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] has predicted could revolutionize the global economy even if it sharply reduces the need for human labor.[1]

## Development and demonstrations

As of November 2025 the robot was not yet ready for mass production, and technical challenges surrounding manufacturing remained unresolved.[1] Tesla nonetheless demonstrated its capabilities at public events, including handing out candy on Halloween and performing Kung Fu alongside the actor Jared Leto.[1]

## Musk's claims

Speaking at a Tesla shareholder meeting, Musk expressed confidence in the machine's potential to eradicate poverty, stating that "Optimus will actually eliminate poverty."[1] He further suggested that Optimus could transform the criminal justice system by monitoring and preventing crime rather than relying on physical incarceration, and tied the technology to the prospect of a universal income as human labor is displaced.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] responded sardonically, writing that he hoped lawmakers would "fix the laws, the courts, and the definition of cruel and unusual punishment" before crime is stopped by citizens being constantly chaperoned by a personal Robocop.[1]
