# M23 Movement

The M23 Movement is an armed group operating in the eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]], described in the Badlands corpus as a Rwanda-linked militia that has crossed into Congolese territory and seized control of mining towns.

## Role in the eastern Congo conflict

As described in the January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief, M23 is the militia that former president [[joseph-kabila|Joseph Kabila]] was accused in 2025 of aiding and abetting, leading to his treason conviction in absentia. Its political vehicle, the [[alliance-fleuve-congo|Alliance Fleuve Congo]], is led by [[corneille-nangaa|Corneille Nangaa]], who denounced the US–DRC minerals partnership as unconstitutional.

Kabila was reportedly killed on January 25, 2026 in an explosion during a meeting with M23 representatives in Uvira, described in commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as the latest town the group had taken over. The commentary connects the group's backing to [[rwanda|Rwanda]] — "long an ally of [[israel|Israel]] and a faithful purveyor of the Zionist lifestyle" — and notes that [[congress|Congress]] was discussing sanctions against Rwanda.
