# Chad

Chad is a landlocked country in north-central Africa, bordering the Sahel region. It appears in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] as the latest state to announce its withdrawal from the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]].

## Withdrawal from the ICC

The Brief reported that Chad announced on Monday that it is withdrawing from the ICC, accusing the Hague-based court of ineffectiveness and of bias against African countries. The government submitted a formal letter to the [[united-nations|United Nations]] to begin the withdrawal process, which under the [[rome-statute|Rome Statute]] takes at least one year to complete.

Chad's departure was presented as part of a growing wave of exits from the court. [[niger|Niger]], [[mali|Mali]] and [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] all began withdrawing in the previous year, and [[venezuela|Venezuela]] announced its formal withdrawal the preceding Friday, likewise citing "geographical bias" disproportionately targeting Africa and Latin America.

## US response

The [[state-department|State Department]]'s [[bureau-of-african-affairs|Bureau of African Affairs]] welcomed Chad's move on X, urging more of the court's 125 member states to withdraw and "reclaim their sovereignty from this flawed institution." Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] had said earlier in July 2026 that the United States was launching a campaign to dismantle the ICC's threat to national sovereignty, following a February 2025 executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that imposed sanctions on the court over its investigations of [[israel|Israel]].
