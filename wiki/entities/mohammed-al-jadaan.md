# Mohammed Al-Jadaan

Mohammed Al-Jadaan is the finance minister of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[1]

## Remarks on trade settlement currencies (2023)

In a Bloomberg TV interview conducted in Davos in January 2023, Al-Jadaan said the kingdom was open to discussions about trade in currencies other than the U.S. dollar: "There are no issues with discussing how we settle our trade arrangements, whether it is in the US dollar, whether it is the euro, whether it is the Saudi riyal."[1] He added, "I don't think we are waving away or ruling out any discussion that will help improve the trade around the world."[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the remarks as evidence that, with the formation of the [[brics|BRICS]] alliance and Saudi Arabia's continued flirtation with joining its ranks, the era of [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] and Deep State hegemony appeared to be nearing its end, in a community that views the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] and the fiat central banking system as requiring reform or dismantling.[1]
