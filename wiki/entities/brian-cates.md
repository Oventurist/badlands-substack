# Brian Cates

Brian Cates is a political columnist and pundit who contributes to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] and writes the Substack newsletter "Rise of the New Media," described as the personal blog of a columnist formerly writing for The Epoch Times, X22 Report and Uncover DC.[1]

## Writing on Ukraine escalation

In an October 26, 2022 Badlands Media column titled "Dear America: Are You Ready To Do Your Global Duty And Send Your Child To Die For Ukraine?", Cates responded to a [[cbs-news|CBS News]] report — brought to his attention by [[jack-posobiec|Jack Posobiec]] — that the [[white-house|Biden White House]] had positioned the [[101st-airborne-division|101st Airborne Division]] on the border of the territories newly annexed by the [[russia|Russian Federation]] in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] He argued that placing a tip-of-the-spear unit on [[vladimir-putin|Putin]]'s doorstep was a not-so-subtle message that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would commit ground troops to the conflict, and a dramatic escalation between the US/[[nato|NATO]] bloc and Russia.[1]

Cates's central complaint was procedural as much as strategic: he said Biden had never given a major address making the case for Americans dying in a land war over Ukraine, that no polling had established public consent, and that polling firms had inexplicably failed to survey the question.[1] He questioned the competence of an "increasingly frail President" and of military leadership he portrayed as more concerned with virtue-signalling about being "woke" than with readiness for a hot war, invoking the [[afghanistan|Afghanistan]] withdrawal as evidence.[1] He closed by urging Americans — particularly military families — to raise their voices rather than wait for a pollster to call, warning that the situation could "very easily spiral into a nuclear exchange."[1]

The column carried the standard Badlands Media disclaimer that articles and features represent the opinions of contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself.[1] The argument is treated at length at [[us-ground-war-over-ukraine-debate|the debate over a US ground war over Ukraine]].[1]

## Writing on Durham and Spygate

Cates covered the [[durham-special-counsel-investigation|Durham Special Counsel investigation]] closely from June 2019 onward, and in an October 16, 2022 piece written as the [[igor-danchenko|Danchenko]] trial wound down in Alexandria, Virginia, he set out his thesis that [[john-durham|John Durham]] was deliberately prosecuting the [[russiagate-hoax|RussiaGate]] "joint venture" in reverse.[2] He argued that Durham used minor charges against low-level participants as venues to surface evidence held back for five years, because roughly six years of media conditioning — what Cates called [[mass-formation-psychosis|mass formation psychosis]] — had made a fair jury pool impossible.[2] He rejected the prevailing commentary that Durham would bring no further indictments and simply write a final report, and contended that the record from the [[michael-sussmann|Sussmann]] and Danchenko trials would support charges of theft of classified information, espionage and even treason.[2] Cates writes a personal Substack newsletter, "Rise of the New Media," and has previously written for The Epoch Times, X22 Report and UncoverDC.[2]
