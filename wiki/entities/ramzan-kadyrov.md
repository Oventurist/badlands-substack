# Ramzan Kadyrov

Ramzan Kadyrov is the leader of the Russian republic of Chechnya. In January 2026 he became the subject of international attention after Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] suggested that the United States should pursue "some sort of operation" to remove him, explicitly drawing a parallel to the recent US operation that captured Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

## Zelensky's proposal

Zelensky framed the removal of Kadyrov as a means of raising the cost of the war for Moscow and pressuring Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] to reconsider continuing the war in Ukraine.[1] Kadyrov responded by publicly calling Zelensky a coward for asking the United States to do what he would not do himself.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry noted that he had previously discussed the Chechens, their prominence in the combat sports world, and Kadyrov's leadership with Burning Bright on an episode of the program *The Book of Trump*, and treated Zelensky's call for a Maduro-style raid as a sign of the accelerating breakdown of the postwar international order.[1]

## Rise to power and the Akhmat forces

Kadyrov is the son of [[akhmad-kadyrov|Akhmad Kadyrov]], the Chief Mufti of the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria who defected to Vladimir Putin during the [[second-chechen-war|Second Chechen War]], became President of Chechnya in 2003, and was assassinated by a bomb at a memorial parade in March 2004.[2] At the time of the killing Ramzan led the family militia, and he took over afterward, restructuring the Interior Ministry's organized crime unit so that it could operate in a more kinetic capacity.[2] Because the [[akhmat-special-forces|Kadyrovite]] unit had been guarding his father when he was killed, Ramzan disbanded it and merged the few men he still trusted into Russian law enforcement under the Ministry of Internal Affairs.[2]

In November 2006 Kadyrov tasked [[apty-alaudinov|Apty Alaudinov]], with whom he had formed a personal bond, with avenging his father by eliminating [[movladi-baisarov|Movladi Baisarov]], a former member of Akhmad's security force believed to have played a role in the assassination and who had refused to serve under Ramzan.[2] Alaudinov led fifty Chechen police officers into Moscow and killed Baisarov; that group became the legal basis for the reconstituted Akhmat Special Forces.[2] With Putin's help, Kadyrov equipped the refurbished unit with mechanized armor and heavy equipment it had not previously possessed, creating the modern Chechen special forces based out of Dagestan.[2]
