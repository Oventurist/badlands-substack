# Ramzan Kadyrov

Ramzan Kadyrov is the leader of [[chechnya|Chechnya]], a republic of the [[russia|Russian Federation]], and a prominent ally of President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].

## Zelensky's proposal

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] said the United States should intensify pressure on Russia and suggested Washington could pursue "some sort of operation" to remove Kadyrov, drawing an explicit parallel to the US operation that captured [[venezuela|Venezuela]]'s [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]. Zelensky portrayed the idea as a way to raise the cost for Moscow and push Putin to reconsider continuing the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].

## Response

The Brief notes that Kadyrov responded by calling Zelensky a coward for refusing to attempt the capture himself. In a postscript, [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] recalls that [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] had appeared the previous month on The Book of Trump to discuss the Chechens, their role in the combat sports world, and Kadyrov specifically.
