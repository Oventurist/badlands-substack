# Ramzan Kadyrov

Ramzan Kadyrov is the leader of the Russian republic of Chechnya. In January 2026 he became the subject of international attention after Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] suggested that the United States should pursue "some sort of operation" to remove him, explicitly drawing a parallel to the recent US operation that captured Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

## Zelensky's proposal

Zelensky framed the removal of Kadyrov as a means of raising the cost of the war for Moscow and pressuring Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] to reconsider continuing the war in Ukraine.[1] Kadyrov responded by publicly calling Zelensky a coward for asking the United States to do what he would not do himself.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry noted that he had previously discussed the Chechens, their prominence in the combat sports world, and Kadyrov's leadership with Burning Bright on an episode of the program *The Book of Trump*, and treated Zelensky's call for a Maduro-style raid as a sign of the accelerating breakdown of the postwar international order.[1]
