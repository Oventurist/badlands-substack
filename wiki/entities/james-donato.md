# James Donato

James Donato is a United States District Judge based in San Francisco who presided over the remedies phase of the antitrust lawsuit brought by [[epic-games|Epic Games]] against [[google|Google]].[1]

Donato oversaw proceedings after a San Francisco jury found in 2023 that Google had unlawfully stifled competition through its control of Android app distribution and payments, and was weighing proposed reforms to the Google Play store in 2024.[1] In August 2024 the [[federal-trade-commission|Federal Trade Commission]] filed an amicus brief with his court arguing that judges "may order whatever relief is necessary to restore the competition eliminated by the illegal conduct" and urging him to focus on enabling competitor entry into the market.[1] Donato was scheduled to resume hearing evidence from the companies that week.[1]
