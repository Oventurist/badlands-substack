# Thomas Pham LeGro

**Thomas Pham LeGro** (born c. 1977) is an American journalist employed by the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] who was arrested in June 2025 on allegations of possessing child pornography.[1]

## Arrest and allegations

Law enforcement arrested LeGro, 48, at his home in the District of Columbia during the week of June 23, 2025.[1] The U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Columbia said in a release that [[fbi|FBI]] agents searched the residence on Thursday and seized multiple electronic devices.[1] Prosecutors alleged that a folder on a work laptop found in the home contained eleven videos of child sexual abuse material, and that fragments of a hard drive were recovered in a hallway outside the room where the laptop was found.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands host [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that in a just world the case would be devastating for the Washington Post, noting that the paper had hired LeGro, that the material was found on a work laptop, and that the outlet had been dismissive of child trafficking and CSAM coverage; she called for consequences both for the individual and the institution.[1]
