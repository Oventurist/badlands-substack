# Patrick Soon-Shiong

Dr. Patrick Soon-Shiong is a South African–born transplant surgeon and billionaire who owns the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]].[1] Before his business career he worked as an emergency surgeon at Baragwanath Hospital in Soweto during apartheid, an experience his family has invoked in explaining his political positions.[1]

## 2024 presidential non-endorsement

In October 2024 Soon-Shiong decided that the Los Angeles Times would not endorse a candidate for president, overriding an editorial board that had planned to endorse [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1] The decision, announced on October 23, prompted the resignation of editorials editor [[mariel-garza|Mariel Garza]], who told the [[columbia-journalism-review|Columbia Journalism Review]] that she was "not OK with us being silent."[1] The paper offered readers no public explanation for the change.[1]

Coverage of the episode, including by the [[new-york-times|New York Times]], initially framed the non-endorsement as fear of a second [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] presidency.[1] His daughter, [[nika-soon-shiong|Nika Soon-Shiong]], later attributed the choice to opposition to Harris's position on the war in Gaza, writing that "for my family, Apartheid is not a vague concept."[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that Soon-Shiong therefore had a genuine political rationale — one that came from the left rather than from Trump-fear — and that this rationale was systematically omitted by critics because it did not fit the prevailing narrative around the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|2024 newspaper non-endorsements]].[1]
