# Randolph Dilloway

Randolph Dilloway is an informant named in the [[department-of-justice|U.S. Department of Justice]]'s superseding indictment of the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]], filed on June 2, 2026.[1]

## Role in the SPLC case

Prosecutors allege that the SPLC paid Dilloway $6,000 to falsely take responsibility for a 2014 burglary at the West Virginia headquarters of the neo-Nazi [[national-alliance|National Alliance]].[1] The allegation appears alongside the broader claim that the organization directed more than $1.2 million in donor funds, at the direction of former Intelligence Project director [[heidi-beirich|Heidi Beirich]], to a second informant identified only as "F-9" who was embedded in the same group.[1]

Taken together, prosecutors contend these payments show a pattern in which the SPLC financially supported and manipulated actual extremist organizations while publicly denouncing them and designating numerous conservative groups as "hate groups."[1] The charges in the superseding indictment include wire fraud, bank fraud, false statements to a federally insured bank, and conspiracy to commit money laundering.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the Dilloway payment as part of a network she argued should be "rolled up" in full rather than resolved with "ceremonial arrests," calling for every connection to be traced and prosecuted, including media outlets she said assisted the organization.[1]
