# William James

William James was an American philosopher and psychologist, a founding figure of pragmatism and of modern psychology in the United States. In the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay [[a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi|"A Mysterious Masterclass in Multi Sensory Deception"]] he is cited for the aphorism, "There is nothing so absurd that it cannot be believed as truth if repeated often enough."

The quotation is used to explain the role of repetition in the illusion staged at the [[cosmos-mystery-area|Cosmos Mystery Area]], where a guide reiterates claims of "cosmic energy" and altered gravity while visitors' senses are being manipulated. The essay generalizes the point to propaganda in the [[information-war|information war]], arguing that repetition delivered by a perceived authority is one of the core mechanisms by which false narratives are accepted.
