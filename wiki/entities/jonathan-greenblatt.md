# Jonathan Greenblatt

Jonathan Greenblatt is the head of the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]] and is described in Badlands Media coverage as another Democratic operative running a nominally independent watchdog organization.[1]

Under Greenblatt's leadership the ADL spent months harassing and haranguing [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] for failing to crack down sufficiently on speech it deemed "hateful," and led a campaign that drove corporate advertisers away from X by characterizing Musk as an antisemite.[1] When Musk subsequently banned phrases such as "decolonization" and "from the river to the sea," the ADL suddenly and dramatically changed its tune — an illustration, the article argues, of a pressure model in which toxic labels are applied and can only be lifted by public capitulation.[1]
