# Jonathan Greenblatt

**Jonathan Greenblatt** is identified in the Badlands Media corpus as a Democratic Party operative and the head of the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]]. The source frames the ADL under Greenblatt's leadership as a key participant in the coordinated censorship regime, working in close coordination with [[Media Matters]] to police and gatekeep information during the 2023–2024 pre-election period.

## Leadership of the ADL

The article describes Greenblatt as running the ADL, which the source says had been "obsessed with harassing and haranguing Elon Musk for not sufficiently cracking down on speech it deemed 'hateful.'" The source argues that this pressure campaign against [[Elon Musk]] and his platform X (formerly Twitter) was part of a broader pattern in which pressure groups slap "toxic labels" on targets and then require public appeasement through caving in to their demands.

## Campaign Against Elon Musk

The Badlands source asserts that Greenblatt's ADL proclaimed Musk to be an antisemite in order to get corporate advertisers to flee X. The article states that this characterization of Musk as antisemitic is "now demonstrably not true." According to the source, the ADL's campaign succeeded in driving corporate advertisers away from X in large numbers, and when Musk eventually caved on his "free-speech absolutism" and imposed a ban on terms and phrases like "decolonization" and "from the river to the sea," the ADL "suddenly and dramatically changed its tune about Musk."

The source frames this sequence as the standard playbook: pressure groups label targets with toxic epithets, and the only way to resolve the situation is to publicly appease the group by caving in to its demands.

## Relationship to Media Matters

The article describes the ADL and Media Matters as "really interchangeable," working closely together to enforce information orthodoxy. The source argues that both organizations capitalize on the epistemological deficiencies of modern society, where a large portion of the population "will believe anything you write so long as it's preceded by the words 'study finds.'"
