# Aurelio Perez-Lugones

Aurelio Perez-Lugones is a [[pentagon|Pentagon]] contractor identified in court documents as the subject of a federal investigation into the unlawful retention of classified national defense information.[1] He is accused of taking home and printing sensitive intelligence material.[1]

## Investigation

Authorities said that classified information appeared in communications between Perez-Lugones and [[washington-post|Washington Post]] reporter [[hannah-natanson|Hannah Natanson]], and it was on that basis that [[fbi|FBI]] agents executed a search warrant at Natanson's Virginia home on January 14, 2026, seizing electronic devices including a phone, two laptops and a smartwatch.[1]

Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] said the search was carried out at the Pentagon's request and indicated that the individual suspected of the leak was already "behind bars."[1] FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] publicly defended the operation on national security grounds.[1] Press freedom organizations criticized the associated search of a journalist's home as an unusual and aggressive escalation with implications for [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] protections.[1]
