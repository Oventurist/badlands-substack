# Enigma Machine

The **Enigma machine** was a device used by the German military command to encode strategic messages before and during World War II.[1] It appears in the Badlands Brief article "Kekistani Code Talkers" as the fourth of five historical case studies in [[information-warfare|information warfare]].[1]

## Breaking the code

The Enigma code was first broken by the Poles in the early 1930s.[1] In 1939, with a German invasion growing likely, the Poles turned their information over to the British, who established a secret codebreaking group known as Ultra under the mathematician [[alan-turing|Alan M. Turing]].[1]

## Significance

The article treats Enigma and the work surrounding it as a building block without which the transition from the Industrial Age to the Network Age — described by Lt. General [[steven-kwast|Steven Kwast]] on [[rugpull-radio|Rugpull Radio]] — would not have been possible.[1] The episode is presented as evidence that [[cryptography|codes and ciphers]] materially shape the rise and fall of powers.[1]
