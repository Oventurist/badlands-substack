# All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office

The All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) is the U.S. Department of Defense body that succeeded the [[unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force|Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force]] as the government's focal point for investigating [[unidentified-anomalous-phenomena|unidentified anomalous phenomena]].[1]

The reorganization broadened the scope of inquiry beyond aerial sightings to include objects that operate underwater, reflecting the "all-domain" framing of its mandate.[1] The office existed at the time of [[david-grusch|David Grusch]]'s June 2023 whistleblower disclosure regarding alleged non-human-origin craft retrieval programs.[1]
