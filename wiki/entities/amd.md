# AMD

AMD (Advanced Micro Devices) is a U.S. semiconductor company named, alongside [[nvidia|Nvidia]], as one of the foreign AI-chip providers that Beijing's [[china-ai-data-center-buildout|AI data center buildout]] plan explicitly aims to displace.[1]

Under the plan, at least 80 percent of core technology — notably AI chips — is to be sourced from Chinese suppliers such as [[huawei|Huawei]], accelerating the maturation of domestic alternatives to AMD and Nvidia hardware.[1]

## Exclusion from Chinese government procurement (2024)

In March 2024, [[china|China]] issued new government procurement guidelines phasing AMD processors, along with those of [[intel|Intel]], out of state PCs and servers as part of a drive to replace foreign technology with domestic alternatives.[2] The guidance also targeted Microsoft's Windows operating system and foreign database software, and accompanied a localization push inside state-owned enterprises.[2] The measure was described as retaliation for US restrictions on Chinese access to advanced Nvidia AI chips and ASML lithography equipment.[2]
