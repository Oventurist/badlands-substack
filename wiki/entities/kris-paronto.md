# Kris Paronto

Kris Paronto is a former Special Forces officer and [[cia|CIA]] contractor who fought during the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]] and has publicly insisted that a stand down order was given.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

In an interview with Politico, Paronto said: "There is no sensationalism in that: We were told to 'stand down.' Those words were used verbatim — 100 percent. … If the truth of it affects someone's political career? Well, I'm sorry. It happens."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) He recounted the timing of the first call for help, hearing gunfire in the distance, and being told by the base chief to wait while the team stood outside their compound villa readying two military vehicles.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) He described radio calls for help from [[state-department|State Department]] diplomats carrying "an urgency in their voice that I can't portray because you only hear those kinds of things [when] guys feel like they're going to lose their lives."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)

When his colleague John "Tig" Tiegen told the base chief the security team was "losing the initiative; we need to go now," Paronto heard the words "stand down" before the team bucked leadership and departed on its own accord.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty) The article notes that the operators who defied the [[stand-down-orders|stand down order]] were later required by CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] to sign new [[non-disclosure-agreements|non-disclosure agreements]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-dereliction-of-duty)
