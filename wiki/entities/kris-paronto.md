# Kris Paronto

Kris Paronto is a former U.S. Army Special Forces officer who served as a CIA security contractor during the 2012 Benghazi attack. In the Badlands Media corpus, he is cited as a primary witness to the stand-down orders issued during the assault on the U.S. consulate in Benghazi, and as a figure who defied those orders to participate in the rescue effort.

## Role in the Benghazi Attack

According to the source, Paronto was part of a CIA security team stationed at the Benghazi annex on the night of September 11, 2012. The article cites a Politico interview in which Paronto stated: "We were told to 'stand down.'" He recounted hearing the exact words "stand down" before the team "bucked" leadership and left on their own accord to aid U.S. personnel under attack at the consulate.

Paronto described hearing calls for help from State Department diplomats and noted the urgency in their voices. When his colleague John "Tig" Tiegen told the base chief that the security team was "losing the initiative; we need to go now," Paronto heard the words "stand down" before the team departed without authorization. His testimony became part of the public record during congressional investigations into the attack.

## Congressional Testimony

The article notes that Paronto and other CIA officials who fought in Benghazi testified before a subcommittee of the [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence]]. Their testimony helped expose discrepancies between the Obama administration's public statements about the attack and the accounts of those who were on the ground.
