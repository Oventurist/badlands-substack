# National Unity (Israeli political party)

National Unity is the centrist Israeli political party chaired by [[benny-gantz|Benny Gantz]].[1] It joined Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s emergency government days after October 7, 2023, when thousands of [[hamas|Hamas]]-led terrorists stormed into southern Israel, killing some 1,200 people, mostly civilians, and taking 251 hostages into the Gaza Strip, sparking the war in the enclave.[1]

## Withdrawal from the emergency government

On the evening of Sunday, June 9, 2024, Gantz announced the party's long-anticipated withdrawal from the government, accusing Netanyahu of preventing Israel from achieving "true victory" in the war against Hamas and urging fresh elections.[1] The withdrawal came weeks after Gantz had conditioned continued support on the prime minister's acceptance of an agreed-upon vision for the Gaza conflict by June 8.[1]

Gantz framed the party's original entry into the coalition as a wartime necessity rather than an endorsement: "After October 7, just like hundreds of thousands of patriotic Israelis, my colleagues and I mobilized as well" and joined "even though we knew it was a bad government."[1] He continued: "We did it because we knew it was a bad government. The people of Israel, the fighters, the commanders, the families of the murdered, the casualties and the hostages needed unity and support like they needed air to breathe."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted the universal consensus that Gantz — who had run against Netanyahu on a moderate platform in the most recent election — was the moderate voice in the war cabinet, and that National Unity's exit would leave the security cabinet dominated by Zionist-maximalists openly calling for the conquest of Palestine.[1]
