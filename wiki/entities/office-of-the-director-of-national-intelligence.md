# Office of the Director of National Intelligence

The Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) is the United States federal body that coordinates the intelligence community and holds authority over the granting and revocation of security clearances across intelligence agencies.

## Role in the Jack Smith clearance dispute

In the July 27, 2026 [[badlands-brief-245|Badlands Brief]], ODNI appears as the addressee of a watchdog petition. The [[center-to-advance-security-in-america|Center to Advance Security in America]] asked Acting Director of National Intelligence [[william-pulte|William Pulte]] to review, and if warranted revoke, the security clearances of former members of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigative team, based on [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] records released by [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]]. As of the Brief's publication, no action by ODNI in response to the request had been announced.

The Badlands commentary surrounding the item treats the persistence of clearances held by former officials as a structural problem rather than an administrative oversight, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] arguing that post-government clearance retention "seems to invite foreign influence and espionage" by enabling consulting work and speaking fees premised on continued access.
