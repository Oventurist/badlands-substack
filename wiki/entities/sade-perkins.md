# Sade Perkins

Sade Perkins is a former member of the Houston Food Insecurity Board who drew national attention in July 2025 after posting a viral TikTok video about the flood-ravaged Texas summer camp [[camp-mystic|Camp Mystic]].[1]

## Background

Perkins was appointed to the Houston city board by former Mayor Sylvester Turner in 2023, and her term expired in January 2025.[1] According to reporting by Sarah Fields cited in the Badlands News Brief, Perkins is the market manager of Freedmen's Town Farmers Market and a non-resident fellow at Princeton University, and she serves on the board of directors for Houston Food Insecurity in Houston, Texas.[1]

## Camp Mystic video and fallout

In a video posted during the weekend of the [[2025-central-texas-floods|2025 Central Texas floods]], Perkins claimed that Camp Mystic was "White-only, conservative [and] Christian."[1] She opened the video by predicting, "I know I'm probably gonna get canceled for this."[1] The office of Houston Mayor John Whitmire confirmed to Fox News Digital that Perkins had posted the video.[1] In a statement released the following Sunday, the City of Houston confirmed that it would no longer collaborate with Perkins on the city's hunger crisis.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] condemned the remarks in sharp terms, describing Perkins as spewing hate about dead children on the basis of their race.[1]
