# Adriana Kugler

Adriana Kugler is an economist who served as a member of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] Board of Governors until her resignation in August 2025, an unexpected departure that opened a seat on the central bank's board at a politically sensitive moment for monetary policy.[1]

## Resignation and succession

Kugler resigned on Friday, August 1, 2025, leaving a vacancy on the Board of Governors whose unexpired term ran to January 31, 2026.[1] On Thursday, August 7, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that he had selected [[stephen-miran|Stephen Miran]], chair of the [[council-of-economic-advisers|Council of Economic Advisers]], to fill the seat and serve out the remainder of Kugler's term.[1] Trump indicated in a Truth Social post that the search for a permanent replacement would continue, signaling that the nominee for the full fourteen-year term might be someone other than Miran and that Miran could function as a caretaker.[1]

Because the vacancy was short-dated, market coverage treated the Kugler seat as a near-term lever over Fed policy rather than a long-horizon appointment; reporting on the nomination coincided with a rally in [[bitcoin|bitcoin]], which climbed back above $117,000 on the news.[1] One account of the nomination erroneously rendered the departing governor's name as "Andrea Kluger."[1]
