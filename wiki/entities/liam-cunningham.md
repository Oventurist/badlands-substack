# Liam Cunningham

Liam Cunningham is an Irish actor who was aboard the Gaza-bound aid boat Madleen when Israeli naval forces intercepted and detained its crew in June 2025.[1] He sailed alongside climate activist [[greta-thunberg|Greta Thunberg]] and French MEP Rima Hassan on the vessel operated by the [[freedom-flotilla-coalition|Freedom Flotilla Coalition]].[1]

Cunningham is best known for portraying Ser Davos Seaworth on Game of Thrones — a reformed smuggler widely known in the series as the "Onion Knight" for having smuggled a shipload of onions into a besieged city full of starving people.[1] Badlands commentator [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the parallel, and the coincidence of the character's name with Davos, the Swiss host town of the World Economic Forum's annual meeting, as part of the symbolic architecture of the [[madleen-flotilla-interception|Madleen flotilla interception]].[1]
