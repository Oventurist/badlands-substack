# Lesbians United

Lesbians United is an advocacy organization cited by Badlands Media as among the lesbian and gay groups that have released statements opposing the indoctrination of children with [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]] and the homophobia those groups allege it fosters.[1]

## Context

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] named Lesbians United together with the [[lgb-alliance|LGB Alliance]] and [[gays-against-groomers|Gays Against Groomers]] as organizational counterexamples to the assumption that the LGBTQ community speaks with one voice in favor of gender-affirmative care for minors.[1] He argued that the narrative depicting the LGBTQ community as uniformly targeting children functions to radicalize conservatives against progressives while ignoring these dissenting organizations, and that the mirror-image narrative accusing conservatives of "trans genocide" performs the same function in reverse.[1]

Deschamps treated both narratives as instruments of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] serving [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric operations]], in which elevating extremist voices into the illusion of a majority removes any need for the actual majority to hold those views.[1]
