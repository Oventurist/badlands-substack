# Carnegie Endowment for International Peace

The Carnegie Endowment for International Peace is a Washington foreign policy think tank cited in the Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" as an institutional affiliation shared by two architects of the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran Deal]]: [[jake-sullivan]] and [[william-burns]].

The article accuses the Endowment of profiting from [[china]]. Quoting a Daily Caller report, it states that while serving as president of the Carnegie board, Burns welcomed into the board a Chinese businessman named [[zhang-yichen]], described as having links to two CCP entities — the Center for China and Globalization and the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference — and as having donated between $750,000 and $1.5 million to Carnegie. The article adds that Carnegie also received millions of dollars from the china us exchange foundation (CUSEF), which it describes as responsible for United Front work in the United States, money used to build the Carnegie–Tsinghua Center in Beijing.
