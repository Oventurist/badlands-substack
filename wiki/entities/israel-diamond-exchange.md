# Israel Diamond Exchange

The Israel Diamond Exchange is described in Badlands reporting as the largest diamond exchange in the world.[1]

## Schnitzer presidency and the Gertler connection

[[moshe-schnitzer|Moshe Schnitzer]] served as president of the exchange from 1967 to 1993, and when he died in 2007 it was [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] who delivered the eulogy at his funeral.[1] Schnitzer was the grandfather of [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]], the Israeli billionaire whose mining operations in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] became the subject of British, American and Swiss corruption suspicions; Badlands commentary likened the family business to the events depicted in the film Blood Diamond.[1]
