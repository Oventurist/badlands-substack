# New York Post

The *New York Post* is a US newspaper frequently cited in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] coverage as a source for political and national security reporting. In the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]] it supplies two of the edition's reported items.

## Reporting cited in the Brief

The Post is credited with reporting that the Trump administration quietly secured the transfer of roughly 100 cartel leaders from [[mexico|Mexico]] into US custody prior to the Jalisco operation that killed [[el-mencho|El Mencho]], with no additional detail provided about the nature or scope of US coordination. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlights this reporting — noting that the transferred figures included El Mencho's brother — as the basis for his suggestion that the raid may have been coordinated in advance rather than a purely Mexican initiative. The Post is separately cited for reporting that dozens of Democrats planned to skip the 2026 [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union address]] amid a party split over how to protest.
