# Scott Newgent

Scott Newgent is a detransitioned activist described by Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as "a detransitioned culture warrior for the kids," who appears in [[daily-wire|The Daily Wire]] documentary [[what-is-a-woman|"What Is a Woman?"]].[1]

In what the reviewer called a particularly heartbreaking moment of the film, Newgent held up his mutilated forearm with tears in his eyes and said, "We have five children's hospitals in the United States, promoting that" — that is, promoting what she characterized as the mutilation, sterilization and psychological torture of children.[1] Newgent also states in the film that gender surgery costs "$70,000 a pop," and that this covers only the operation itself; post-operative transgender patients face a lifelong regimen of medications, additional surgeries and therapies, which the reviewer described as a booming multi-billion dollar industry actively recruiting patients for life.[1]

In May 2023 Newgent joined Kate Awakening on the program "Raising a Nation," where he recounted his story at greater length in what the reviewer called an eye-opening look at the realities of the medical transition industry.[1]
