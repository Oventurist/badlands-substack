# Andrew Johnson

**Andrew Johnson** served as Vice-President of the United States under [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]] and succeeded to the presidency following Lincoln's assassination in 1865.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] characterizes Johnson as Lincoln's "treacherous" Vice-President, noting that in his 1865 inaugural address Johnson attacked protectionism and declared that "free trade with all the markets of the world is the true theory of government."[1] This position placed Johnson directly against the protective tariff policy and the [[american-system-of-political-economy|American System]] framework that Lincoln had used to finance the [[american-civil-war|Civil War]] through [[greenbacks|greenbacks]] and [[five-twenty-bonds|5:20 bonds]].[1]

In Ehret's account, Johnson's free-trade turn belongs to the broader postwar subversion of Lincoln's program, in which the full reconstruction of the defeated South that Lincoln had planned was abandoned and a new war was waged against the greenbacks by the [[city-of-london|City of London]] and its American agents on Wall Street, culminating in the [[specie-resumption-act|Specie Resumption Act]].[1]
