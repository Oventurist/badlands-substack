# Chris Hipkins

Chris Hipkins is a New Zealand Labour Party politician who served as prime minister for nine months following the abrupt resignation of [[jacinda-ardern|Jacinda Ardern]].[1]

## 2023 general election defeat

Hipkins led Labour into the general election held on Saturday, 14 October 2023, in which voters ousted the party and elected the country's most conservative government in decades.[1] Labour was on track to lose its outright majority in the 121-seat New Zealand parliament for the first time since 2017, a result that analysts said was largely in line with pre-election polling.[1] Late on election night, Hipkins told supporters that he had telephoned [[christopher-luxon|Christopher Luxon]] of the centre-right National Party to concede the race.[1]

Commentary aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] linked Labour's defeat to the pandemic-era policies of the Ardern and Hipkins governments, including vaccine mandates, official designation of state news as the sole source of truth, and the arrest of peaceful anti-mandate protesters.[1]
