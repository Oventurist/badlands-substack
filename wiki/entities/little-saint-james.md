# Little Saint James

Little Saint James is a privately held island in the United States Virgin Islands owned by financier [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], colloquially known in press coverage and online commentary as "pedophile island."[1] Prosecutors have accused Epstein of using the property to groom, assault, and traffic large numbers of women and girls, some reportedly as young as 14.[1] The former attorney general of the US Virgin Islands alleged in litigation that girls as young as 12 were trafficked to Epstein by members of his elite social circle.[1]

## Data-broker exposure of visitors

In April 2024 the technology magazine [[wired|Wired]] reported that location data harvested by the broker [[near-intelligence|Near Intelligence]] documented nearly 200 mobile devices belonging to people who visited Little Saint James in the years before Epstein's death.[1] Because commercial location feeds resolve to individual dwellings and workplaces, the mapped visitation trails pointed back with high precision to the residences and offices of wealthy and influential guests — visitors who appear to have been undeterred by Epstein's prior status as a convicted sex offender.[1]

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary accompanying the item argued that the significance of the story lay less in its content, long familiar to the "truth community," than in its provenance: an establishment technology outlet presenting Epstein material to a mainstream-adjacent audience using a data-driven method, and doing so roughly a week after a wave of coverage concerning Sean "Diddy" Combs.[1] Writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the release through the "War of Stories" and the [[shark-fin-template|Shark Fin Template]], in which established awakening vectors resurface at opportune moments, concluding simply: "Planned."[1]
