# Sam Brown

Sam Brown is a Republican politician and United States Army veteran who ran for the Senate in Nevada in 2024 against Democratic incumbent [[jacky-rosen|Jacky Rosen]].[1] Brown was severely burned by an improvised explosive device explosion during his military service, and the visible scarring from those injuries became a central element of his public identity as a candidate.[1]

## Campaign against female draft registration

Brown made Senate Democrats' 2024 proposal to require women to register for the military draft an issue in the Nevada Senate race, slamming Rosen in a video posted to the social platform X for voting in favor of the requirement.[1]

"Look at my face. This is the high cost of war," he wrote.[1] "Amy and I volunteered to serve, and we honor all who serve," he added, referring to his wife, "but forcing America's daughters to register for the draft is UNACCEPTABLE. Shame on Jacky Rosen."[1] In the accompanying video, Brown pointed to the scarring on his face as evidence of the dangers women would face in combat theaters.[1]

The provision, added to the annual defense authorization bill, prompted a broader backlash from Republicans and social conservatives, with Senator [[roger-wicker|Roger Wicker]] leading efforts to strip it out.[1]
