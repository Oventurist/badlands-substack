# Henry Thompson

Henry Thompson is a Georgia Superior Court judge who presided over the Cobb County divorce proceedings of special prosecutor [[nathan-wade]] and his estranged wife [[joycelyn-wade]], a case that became entangled with the Fulton County election-interference prosecution of [[donald-trump]].[1]

## Unsealing of the Wade divorce records

On Monday, January 22, 2024, Thompson unsealed the divorce records of Nathan Wade, a top prosecutor in Fulton County's election-interference probe, allowing the public to examine personal information that could implicate his boss and alleged romantic partner, District Attorney [[fani-willis]].[1]

## Stay of the Willis subpoena

Thompson also placed a temporary stay on a subpoena issued by Joycelyn Wade, who was seeking to depose Willis that week.[1] The judge ruled that Nathan Wade should be deposed first, after which he would determine whether Willis possessed unique personal information requiring her to be questioned under oath by attorneys for Joycelyn Wade.[1] An evidentiary hearing in the divorce case was set for January 31, and the determination came at the end of a closely watched 30-minute hearing.[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief]], described the ruling as "a brief reprieve" for Willis from testifying in her boyfriend's divorce proceedings, and argued that the stay order raised the profile of Nathan Wade's own testimony now that his divorce had become a matter of public interest.[1] She noted that with the bank records already public, protecting Willis might require Wade to perjure himself, and that his liability in the divorce was the least of his problems given the parallel inquiry into the relationship in the Fulton County RICO case before Judge [[scott-mcafee]], with a hearing scheduled for February 15.[1] The reporting originated with The Atlanta Journal-Constitution.[1]
