# Gabe Kaminsky

Gabe Kaminsky is an investigative reporter for the [[washington-examiner|Washington Examiner]] whose 2023 series "Disinformation, Inc." documented United States government funding of foreign censorship infrastructure.[1]

## "Disinformation, Inc."

The series described how the [[state-department|State Department]] was backing a UK-based agency, the [[global-disinformation-index|Global Disinformation Index]], that creates digital blacklists of disfavored media outlets — including the Dynamic Exclusion List, which flagged at least 2,000 websites as unsuitable for advertising.[1]

A year after publication, the State Department's [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]] continued to resist disclosure, threatening to provide a congressional committee with records only *in camera* until it understood how the committee would use the information — a reaction Racket News tied directly to the appearance of program details in the Examiner's reporting.[1]
