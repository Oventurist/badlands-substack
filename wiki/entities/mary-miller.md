# Mary Miller

Mary Miller is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Illinois and one of the twenty-one holdouts in the January 2023 speakership election.[1]

## Role in the 2023 speakership fight

Miller was part of the [[house-freedom-caucus|House Freedom Caucus]]-aligned bloc that forced the [[january-2023-mccarthy-concessions|January 2023 McCarthy concessions]] from [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]], and in the resulting assignments she remained on the House Agriculture Committee.[1] Badlands Media contributor Erik Carlson treats agriculture as one of the era's critical fronts, citing farmland purchases by China and [[bill-gates|Bill Gates]], fires at food manufacturing plants, and alterations to animal DNA.[1]
