# Haytham Ali Tabatabai

Haytham Ali Tabatabai, also known by his nom de guerre Abu Ali Tabatabai, was a senior commander in the Lebanese militant movement [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], killed in an Israeli airstrike on Beirut on Sunday, November 23, 2025.[1]

## Career

Tabatabai joined Hezbollah as a teenager after the group's formation during Israel's 1982 invasion of Lebanon.[1] He was one of the few long-serving commanders to survive Israel's targeted-killing campaign of the previous year.[1]

## Killing

The strike hit the Haret Hreik district, a Hezbollah stronghold in the Lebanese capital.[1] Both Hezbollah and the Israel Defense Forces confirmed his death.[1] According to Lebanon's Health Ministry, the attack killed at least five people and injured 28 others.[1] Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] praised the strike, calling it part of Israel's ongoing effort to destroy the group.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited US Special Envoy [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]], who has said the American public has been grossly misinformed about Hezbollah's nature — that it began as a political movement to expel Israel from southern Lebanon before mutating into a militia — and that Hezbollah had not once violated the ceasefire signed with Israel the previous year, not even after the pager attack.[1] He asked why Israel was bombing the capital of a foreign country and targeting the leadership of entities with which Netanyahu has signed peace agreements, and what signal that sends to the rest of the world.[1]
