# Xu Caihou

Xu Caihou is a former general of the [[peoples-liberation-army|People's Liberation Army]] named in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" as one of six party leaders removed early in [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s rule for alleged coup attempts.[1]

## Removal

Xu is listed with fellow generals [[guo-boxiong|Guo Boxiong]] and [[sun-zhengcai|Sun Zhengcai]] and with civilian figures [[zhou-yongkang|Zhou Yongkang]], [[bo-xilai|Bo Xilai]] and [[ling-jihua|Ling Jihua]].[1] The article situates these military removals within Xi's broader campaign against corruption in the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], which it says formally punished 1.4 million party members between 2013 and 2017, including more than 100 generals and admirals.[1]
