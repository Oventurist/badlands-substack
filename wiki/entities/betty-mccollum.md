# Betty McCollum

Betty McCollum is a member of the United States House of Representatives from Minnesota whose St. Paul district office became a target of anti-war demonstrations during the opening weeks of the Israel–Hamas war.[1]

## October 2023 protest

Following a pro-Palestinian rally in [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]] on Sunday, October 22, 2023 — during which witnesses reported a vehicle driving through the crowd and one person reported hearing a gunshot — the [[minnesota-anti-war-committee|Minnesota Anti-War Committee]], which helped organize that demonstration, announced a further protest for the following Wednesday afternoon outside McCollum's office in St. Paul.[1] No injuries or arrests were reported in connection with the Sunday rally.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the wave of demonstrations as evidence of a rupture between activist constituencies mobilized in 2020 and the administration those constituencies had helped elect, given the administration's support for Israel following the October 7 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack.[1]
