# Bashar al-Assad

Bashar al-Assad has been President of Syria since 2000, succeeding his father Hafez al-Assad. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," Assad is framed as a target of a CIA-backed regime change operation designed to overthrow a Putin ally and replace him with a government controlled by terrorist organizations.

According to the article, [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and [[mouza-moustafa|Mouza Moustafa]] worked together to overthrow Assad, with McCain traveling to Syria in 2013 to meet with ISIS leaders. The source claims that the [[syrian-emergency-task-force|Syrian Emergency Task Force]] was set up to raise U.S. taxpayer money for Syrian rebels, but that in reality it was "actually a lobbying group for ISIS." It asserts that the U.S. was funding and arming ISIS through this channel with the approval of [[barack-obama|President Obama]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].

The article notes that the CIA trained ISIS fighters in [[benghazi|Benghazi]] and then flew them to Syria through Turkey, using the "J.V. team" narrative to distract the American people while the group grew in strength. It claims that Assad, like [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]] before him, was a target of the same playbook: paint him as a brutal dictator, support terrorist rebels to overthrow him, and steal taxpayer money in the process.

According to the source, Assad's survival represents a failure of the cabal's plan, and his government's continued existence is evidence that the "outsider" forces represented by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] have disrupted the CIA's long-running operation to control the Middle East through perpetual war.
