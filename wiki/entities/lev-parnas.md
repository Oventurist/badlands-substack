# Lev Parnas

Lev Parnas is an American businessman known for his association with [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]] and for his role in Giuliani's efforts concerning Ukraine. Parnas was sentenced to 20 months in prison for violations connected to campaign finance, wire fraud, and making false statements.[1]

## House Oversight testimony (March 2024)

Democrats on the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] invited Parnas as their witness at the committee's March 20, 2024 hearing into the Biden family's alleged influence peddling, where he testified about his work with Giuliani on Ukraine.[1] His appearance served as a counterweight to the Republican witnesses, former [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] associates [[tony-bobulinski|Tony Bobulinski]] and [[jason-galanis|Jason Galanis]].[1]

Badlands Media commentary framed the Democrats' witness selection and questioning as part of a broader pattern of turning the impeachment-inquiry hearing into a forum about former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] rather than about President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]
