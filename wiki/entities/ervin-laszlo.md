# Ervin Laszlo

**Ervin Laszlo** is described as "a pioneer in quantum consciousness" in the March 22, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Shift: Navigating Awakening, Sovereignty and Conscious Creation" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

## Role in the article

Laszlo is quoted for the statement that "The universe is not a collection of things, but a symphony of vibrations."[1] The essay uses this line as the theoretical support for its central metaphor that reality operates on multiple simultaneous frequencies, "like overlapping radio stations broadcasting different songs," some tuned to fear, scarcity and control, others to coherence, connection and possibility.[1]

From the quotation the article draws a practical conclusion: the more attuned a person becomes to these vibrations, the clearer it becomes where distortion exists and where coherence thrives.[1] Laszlo's vibrational framing is placed alongside [[david-bohm|David Bohm]]'s account of reality as an "unbroken wholeness" and [[ken-wilber|Ken Wilber]]'s AQAL framework as part of a cluster of authorities the essay invokes to argue that [[discernment|discernment]] is the navigational compass of awakening.[1]
