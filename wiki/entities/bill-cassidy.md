# Bill Cassidy

Bill Cassidy is a Republican United States Senator from Louisiana and a physician by training.

## Friction over the pace of the 2026 agenda

The June 19, 2026 Badlands Brief, citing Politico, groups Cassidy with Senators Thom Tillis and John Cornyn among Republicans who pushed back on the expectation that the [[senate|Senate]] simply approve President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s proposals without changes or extended debate. Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] emphasized working through the legislative process, pointing to Senate procedure and narrow majorities as real constraints. The Brief notes that despite these disagreements the senators cited generally support most of Trump's policy goals; Trump has nonetheless publicly criticized lawmakers he believes are moving too slowly, and has demanded termination of the [[filibuster|filibuster]] to clear the way for the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]].
