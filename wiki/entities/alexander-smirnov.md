# Alexander Smirnov

Alexander Smirnov is a former FBI confidential human source who was charged in February 2024 with providing false information to federal agents about President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and his son [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] during the 2020 presidential campaign.[1]

## Charges and trial schedule

Smirnov, 43, was charged by federal prosecutors with making false statements and creating a false and fictitious record, and pleaded not guilty.[1] A judge set his jury trial for April 23, 2024 before U.S. District Judge Otis Wright in Los Angeles, and at a hearing on Monday, February 26, 2024 ordered that Smirnov be held in custody until trial after he had been re-arrested.[1]

## Allegations

The indictment alleged that Smirnov provided "false derogatory information to the FBI" about the Bidens, including promoting during a September 2023 interview with FBI agents "a new false narrative after he said he met with Russian officials."[1] In a filing the week before the trial date was set, prosecutors wrote that some of the information Smirnov had shared came from "officials associated with Russian intelligence," and added that he was "actively peddling new lies that could impact U.S. elections after meeting with Russian intelligence officials in November."[1]

Among the false claims prosecutors attributed to Smirnov was the assertion that executives associated with the Ukrainian energy company [[burisma|Burisma]] — where Hunter Biden had served on the board — had paid the Bidens $5 million each, and that a Burisma official had said it would take investigators ten years to locate "illicit payments" to President Biden.[1]
