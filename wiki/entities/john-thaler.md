# John Thaler

John Thaler is an attorney and forensic investigator known in alternative-media circles for claims that the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] used bribery and money laundering to influence elected officials in Arizona.[1]

## Maricopa County allegations

In February 2023, Thaler appeared on Lindell TV with broadcaster [[brannon-howse|Brannon Howse]] and former Arizona State Representative [[mark-finchem|Mark Finchem]] to describe an alleged phony mortgage and real-estate transaction scheme said to have been used by the Sinaloa Cartel to bribe elected officials and exert control over the state of Arizona, including [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] elections.[1]

The appearance followed a joint special meeting of the Arizona Senate and House Elections Committees, at which a presenter identified as Breger delivered what [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] described as an explosive presentation alleging corruption and collusion among American politicians, appointed government officials and the cartels.[1] Coverage tied the allegations to the broader fentanyl trade: Tempe police were reported to have seized over 30 million lethal doses of fentanyl, and in 2022 the [[drug-enforcement-administration|Drug Enforcement Administration]] seized more than 50.6 million deadly fentanyl-laced counterfeit prescription pills and over 10,000 pounds of fentanyl powder nationwide, roughly half of it originating in Arizona.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that Thaler appeared to have uncovered "a massive and wide-spread collusion scheme between the cartels and Deep State politics and government here in the US," with sweeping implications.[1] Deschamps noted the absence of official involvement by the [[justice-department|Justice Department]], the [[fbi|FBI]] or state police, treating that absence as evidence of the negligent condition of American justice institutions, and argued that the inner workings of such a bribery and laundering scheme offered "a powerful window into how the engines of corruption work" for actors he associates with the [[deep-state|Deep State]].[1]
