# ADNOC

ADNOC (the Abu Dhabi National Oil Company) is the state oil company of the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]].[1]

## Bypassing the Strait of Hormuz

Amid the 2026 [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] crisis, ADNOC has expanded pipeline and export infrastructure outside Hormuz as major oil consumers and producers invest in routes that bypass the strait entirely.[1] [[japan|Japan]] has increased purchases of UAE crude routed through Fujairah, a port on the Gulf of Oman side of the chokepoint.[1] Reporting on the shift frames it as a long-term geopolitical realignment of energy routes rather than a temporary wartime adjustment.[1]

## First rupee-settled oil sale to India (2023)

On August 14, 2023, ADNOC sold one million barrels of crude to [[indian-oil-corporation|Indian Oil Corporation]] in a transaction settled without converting local currencies into US dollars — the first such dollar-free oil sale between the United Arab Emirates and India.[2] The trade followed a July 2023 Memorandum of Understanding between the two countries establishing a Local Currency Settlement system facilitated by the [[reserve-bank-of-india|Reserve Bank of India]] and the Central Bank of the United Arab Emirates, permitting bilateral trade in rupees and dirhams.[2]

Commentators described the sale as another blow to the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] and to dollar dominance in global energy markets; the settlement system's first test had been the sale of 25 kilograms of gold from a UAE exporter to an Indian buyer for about 128.4 million rupees ($1.54 million).[2]
