# United States Navy SEALs

The United States Navy SEALs are the US Navy's special operations forces. They appear in the February 24, 2026 Badlands Brief in connection with US–Mexican security cooperation against the cartels.

## Proposed training mission in Mexico

The Brief reports that roughly two weeks before the Jalisco raid that killed [[el-mencho|El Mencho]], a Mexican Senate commission approved a proposal for SEAL Team 2 to deploy to [[mexico|Mexico]] to train Mexican marine infantry and special forces to fight the cartels. The following week, President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] formally invited nearly twenty SEALs, though the Brief stresses that the invitation was in a training capacity only.

[[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treats this as a complicating fact for the narrative of Mexican resistance to US involvement: President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had said he offered to send US troops to fight the cartels and was turned down by Sheinbaum, yet a limited SEAL training presence was simultaneously being arranged.
