# The American Spectator

The American Spectator is an American conservative magazine and commentary outlet.[1]

## 2023 coverage of Trump's polling

In June 2023 The American Spectator published "Trump's Poll Numbers Are a Cry for Justice," arguing that the harder Democrats hit [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], the higher his poll numbers climbed.[1] The piece noted that the [[department-of-justice|U.S. Department of Justice]] had filed a federal indictment against Trump on June 8, 2023 over the alleged mishandling of classified records, and that rather than sandbagging him, Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s 37 charges "pumped helium into the reelection odds of America's 45th president."[1] It cited [[abc-news|ABC News]] anchor [[jonathan-karl|Jonathan Karl]]'s on-air surprise at post-indictment [[quinnipiac-university|Quinnipiac]] polling showing a statistical tie, and observed that Trump's showing was even more impressive when compared with that of his rivals for the GOP nomination.[1]
