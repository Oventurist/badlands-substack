# Adolf Hitler

Adolf Hitler was the leader of Nazi Germany, whose regime orchestrated the [[holocaust|Holocaust]] and the deaths of approximately six million Jewish people. He appears in the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026 as the head of the Nazi apparatus to which 890 previously undisclosed [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] accounts have been tied by the independent investigation conducted by [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]].

The accounts in question were held by entities within that apparatus, including the [[german-foreign-office|German Foreign Office]], the [[schutzstaffel|SS]] paramilitary organization and a German arms-manufacturing company. The same probe found that the bank expropriated funds belonging to Jewish account holders and transferred them to Nazi clients, and that it was involved in schemes assisting Nazis in fleeing to [[argentina|Argentina]] after the war.
