# Mesa County

Mesa County is a county in western [[colorado|Colorado]], United States, best known within the Badlands corpus as the jurisdiction whose election equipment became the subject of the prosecution of former county clerk [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]].

## Role in the Tina Peters case

According to the May 18, 2026 Badlands Brief, Peters was convicted in 2024 for allowing unauthorized access to county voting systems tied to efforts to challenge the 2020 presidential election results. Prosecutors said she enabled unauthorized access to Mesa County election equipment and data that later circulated among election-denial activists. The case became a national rallying point among supporters of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who repeatedly called for Peters' release and claimed in 2025 that he had pardoned her, despite state officials saying presidential pardons do not apply to state convictions. Governor [[jared-polis|Jared Polis]] commuted Peters' sentence in May 2026.
