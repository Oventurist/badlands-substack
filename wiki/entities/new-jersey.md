# New Jersey

**New Jersey** is a Mid-Atlantic US state. In the Badlands corpus it appears chiefly through the enforcement activity of its Office of the Attorney General, led by [[matthew-platkin|Matthew Platkin]], against pro-life pregnancy centers.

## First Choice litigation

The state's subpoena to [[first-choice-womens-resource-centers|First Choice Women's Resource Centers]], seeking donor and operational records, produced a 2026 [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] decision permitting the center to challenge the investigation in federal court on [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] associational grounds. The ruling did not resolve the subpoena's validity but limited the state's ability to confine such disputes to its own courts.

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterizes New Jersey's conduct as a sustained campaign of harassment against reproductive centers that decline to perform or refer for abortion, and reads the ruling as evidence that the weaponization of state government is being dismantled.
