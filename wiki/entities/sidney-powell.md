# Sidney Powell

Sidney Powell is an American attorney known for litigating challenges to the 2020 presidential election results, including a set of federal fraud cases she has described as having been dismissed on bogus grounds.[1]

## Comment on Kemp and Harrison Deal

In February 2026, following the unsealing of [[david-perdue|David Perdue]]'s grand jury testimony implicating Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]] in shutting down Georgia's election investigation, Powell posted on X: "Remember what happened on a highway in GA to a wonderful young man who was connected to Kemp, Loeffler and Perdue after all three called for a signature audit?? Run the timeline on Kemp's change of heart—and add in the bogus dismissal of our 4 federal fraud cases."[1] The young man referenced was [[harrison-deal|Harrison Deal]].[1] Badlands cited the post as capturing what "we were all thinking" about the sequence of events in Georgia.[1]

## Georgia plea

Powell was among the former [[donald-trump|Trump]] attorneys who resolved charges in the Fulton County racketeering case brought by [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]], pleading to relatively minor counts involving unauthorized access to voting machines and restricted areas.[2] Commentators including [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] noted that such charges are comparatively easy to prove but that it was unclear whether her plea would tie Trump to a conspiracy or racketeering scheme — in contrast with the subsequent plea by [[jenna-ellis|Jenna Ellis]] to false statements, which was seen as carrying greater risk for the former president.[2]

## Fox News private assessments (2020)

Court filings unsealed in February 2023 in the [[dominion-v-fox-news-defamation-lawsuit|Dominion Voting Systems defamation suit against Fox News]] showed that the network's hosts privately dismissed Powell's fraud claims even as they aired.[3] [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] told a producer on November 16, 2020 that "Sidney Powell is lying" about having evidence, and described her in texts as an "unguided missile" and "dangerous as hell."[3] [[laura-ingraham|Laura Ingraham]] called her "a complete nut," adding "No one will work with her. Ditto with Rudy," a reference to [[rudy-giuliani|Rudy Giuliani]].[3] [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]] testified in a deposition that "that whole narrative that Sidney was pushing, I did not believe it for one second."[3]
