# Nekima Levy Armstrong

Nekima Levy Armstrong is a Minnesota activist arrested by federal authorities in January 2026 in connection with a coordinated attack on [[cities-church|Cities Church]] in St. Paul, Minnesota.[1]

## Arrest

US Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] announced the arrests of Armstrong and [[chauntyll-allen|Chauntyll Louisa Allen]], stating they had been taken into custody at her direction.[1] Bondi said [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] and [[fbi|FBI]] agents executed the arrests in Minnesota, and that Armstrong allegedly played a key role in organizing the attack on the church.[1]

The arrests followed a protest at the church the preceding weekend that drew national attention after activists disrupted services to target a pastor they said was linked to US Immigration and Customs Enforcement.[1] Federal officials are investigating potential civil rights and other charges related to the incident.[1] Badlands noted that, despite his involvement in the attack, [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] was not arrested.[1]
