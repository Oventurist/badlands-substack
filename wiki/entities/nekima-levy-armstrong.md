# Nekima Levy Armstrong

Nekima Levy Armstrong is a Minnesota civil rights attorney, former president of the Minneapolis NAACP, and activist who was arrested by federal agents in January 2026 in connection with the coordinated attack on [[cities-church|Cities Church]] in St. Paul, Minnesota.

## Arrest in the Cities Church case

Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] announced on January 22, 2026 that federal law enforcement agents had arrested two individuals in connection with the attack on Cities Church, identifying Nekima Levy Armstrong and Chauntyll Louisa Allen as having been taken into custody "at her direction." According to the January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief, Bondi said [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]] and [[fbi|FBI]] agents executed the arrests in Minnesota, and that Armstrong allegedly played a key role in organizing the attack on the church.

The arrests followed a protest at the church the previous weekend that drew national attention after activists disrupted services to target a pastor said to be linked to [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|US Immigration and Customs Enforcement]]. Federal officials said they were investigating potential civil rights and other charges related to the incident. The [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] had previously opened an investigation into the church disruption under the [[ku-klux-klan-act-of-1871|Ku Klux Klan Act]] and the FACE Act, and former CNN host [[don-lemon|Don Lemon]] — despite his alleged involvement — was not arrested.
