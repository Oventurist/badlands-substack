# American Muslim Alliance

The American Muslim Alliance is a United States advocacy organization that participated in the pro-Palestinian mobilization in Washington, D.C. in November 2023.[1]

## Role in the November 2023 march on Washington

At the "National March on Washington: Free Palestine" held on Saturday, November 4, 2023, the group's national director, [[mahdi-bray|Mahdi Bray]], summarized the demonstrators' demand in a single line: "What we want and what we demand is a ceasefire now."[1] The march, organized in coalition with the [[answer-coalition|ANSWER Coalition]], drew thousands of protesters who traveled to the capital by organized bus from across the country.[1]

The demonstration was among the largest pro-Palestinian gatherings in United States history and among the biggest for any cause in Washington in recent years, gathering at Freedom Plaza near the White House before opening with a moment of silence for Palestinians killed since the start of Israel's retaliation for the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]].[1] The rally's central purpose was to oppose President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s policy toward the war, as the United States government continued to reject calls to endorse a blanket ceasefire.[1]
