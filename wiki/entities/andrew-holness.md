# Andrew Holness

Andrew Holness is the Prime Minister of Jamaica. In the aftermath of [[hurricane-melissa|Hurricane Melissa]], which made landfall on the island on October 28, 2025, as a Category 5 storm with 185 mph winds, Holness declared Jamaica a disaster area.[1] St. Elizabeth Parish was reported to be "underwater," and the island's education minister, [[dana-morris-dixon|Dana Morris Dixon]], said 77 percent of Jamaica remained without power the following day.[1]
