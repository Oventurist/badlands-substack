# Heather Podesta

Heather Podesta is a Washington, D.C. lobbyist described as one of the most successful in the capital and as the former wife of [[tony-podesta|Tony Podesta]].[1]

## Role in the Resistance

Podesta was identified as a co-founder of [[the-comedy-resistance|The Comedy Resistance]] (TCR) alongside the filmmaker Stephen Kessler.[1] TCR recruited comedians including Bob Odenkirk, David Cross, Sarah Silverman, Mike Birbiglia, Patton Oswalt and Reggie Watts for organizing tasks and photo opportunities such as voter registration at comedy shows and mask distribution during COVID.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] said he observed the organization's creation firsthand, having lived with one of its video editors in Hollywood.[1]

Her involvement is cited as illustrative of the argument that [[the-resistance|the Resistance]] was not a spontaneous grassroots reaction but an organized product of the professional NGO and lobbying world.[1]
