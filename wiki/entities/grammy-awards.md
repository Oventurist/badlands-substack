# Grammy Awards

The Grammy Awards are the American recording industry's annual awards ceremony, broadcast in 2023 by [[cbs|CBS]].[1] In Badlands Media commentary the ceremony is treated less as an entertainment event than as a vehicle for cultural and occult messaging directed at the public.[1]

## 2023 ceremony controversy

The ceremony held on Sunday, February 5, 2023 became a major Truth Community story after [[sam-smith|Sam Smith]] and [[kim-petras|Kim Petras]] performed "Unholy," the song for which they won best pop duo.[1] The staging featured performers in red, Petras in a cage, and Smith in devil horns encircled by robed dancers, which critics described as an overt satanic ritual.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] wrote that the awards had become a venue for openly venerating Baphomet and argued that "the Godlessness is rewarded" in a degenerating society, calling on the public to reject and rebuke such programming.[1] Contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] responded to the surrounding CBS promotional tweet by noting that the episode "certainly doesn't help to dissuade people who claim that Hollywood is infested with Satanists," and adding that he had not been aware the Grammys had taken place until seeing coverage of the controversy at [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]].[1]
