# Trump Organization

The Trump Organization is the umbrella holding company for the business interests of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], encompassing his real estate, hospitality, and licensing ventures. In the Badlands corpus it appears as a plaintiff-side victim of alleged political debanking by [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]].

## The JPMorgan account closures

According to the January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief, Trump's $5 billion lawsuit against JPMorgan Chase and CEO [[jamie-dimon|Jamie Dimon]] alleges that the bank abruptly terminated multiple accounts belonging to Trump "and his hospitality companies" in early 2021 — a set of accounts the complaint says were in good standing and had processed hundreds of millions of dollars in transactions over the years. The suit alleges the bank provided no warning, appeal process, or alternative banking arrangements.

The complaint further alleges that JPMorgan and Dimon caused Trump, the Trump Organization, affiliated entities, and members of the Trump family to be placed on an internal banking blacklist "shared across federally regulated institutions," a move the complaint characterizes as false, malicious, and designed to prevent other banks from doing business with them. Trump's legal team, led by [[alejandro-brito|Alejandro Brito]], argues the closures were driven by political and social considerations rather than legitimate financial or regulatory concerns, asserting the bank sought to distance itself from Trump and his conservative views when doing so was politically advantageous.
