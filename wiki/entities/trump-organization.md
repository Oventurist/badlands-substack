# Trump Organization

The Trump Organization is the family business conglomerate of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and the corporate defendant in the [[new-york-civil-fraud-trial|New York civil fraud trial]] that opened in early October 2023.[1]

After an appeals court paused Judge [[arthur-engoron|Engoron]]'s order dissolving the Trump businesses, the organization issued a statement calling the order to cancel the Trumps' business certificates "overzealous."[1] It argued that the order "erroneously sought to adjudicate the rights of non-party business entities that employ nearly 1,000 hard-working New Yorkers, have never been accused of any wrongdoing and, were never given their day in court – in clear violation of their fundamental Constitutional rights and Due Process," and pledged to "continue to vigorously defend our company and our incredible employees from this politically-motivated persecution."[1]

The item appeared as a bonus entry in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of October 9, 2023.[1]

## Appellate reversal of the civil fraud penalty (August 2025)

On Thursday, August 21, 2025, a New York appellate court struck down the half-billion-dollar civil fraud penalty imposed on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], his two sons, other executives and the company, ruling the fine "excessive" and in violation of the Eighth Amendment's ban on excessive fines.[2] The penalty stemmed from Judge [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]]'s February 2024 ruling ordering the defendants to pay nearly $364 million in "ill-gotten gains" plus years of interest, bringing the total to roughly $527 million, of which Trump personally accounted for nearly 98%.[2]

## Tel Aviv hotel talks (2025)

In spring 2025 the Trump Organization held preliminary talks with an Israeli real estate developer about branding and managing a new hotel under construction in Tel Aviv, according to a New York Times report relayed by the [[times-of-israel|Times of Israel]].[3] [[eric-trump|Eric Trump]] met with the heads of Nitsba Holdings about investing in the Sarona Hotel, expected to be Israel's tallest hotel on completion, a deal that would place the Trump name atop the Tel Aviv skyline.[3] The talks preceded Israel's preemptive strike on Iran's nuclear program by several weeks.[3] The report noted that since [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took office in January, the organization had already struck property branding deals in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], the [[united-arab-emirates|UAE]], and [[qatar|Qatar]], and that a Trump property in Israel could affect American diplomatic decisions while serving as a target for anti-American terror groups.[3]
