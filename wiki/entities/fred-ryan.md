# Fred Ryan

Fred Ryan is an American media executive who served as publisher of [[washington-post|The Washington Post]] and as chairman of the Ronald Reagan Presidential Foundation and Institute, which operates the Reagan Library.[1]

## Trump's objection to the Reagan Library debate

In an April 25, 2023 [[truth-social|Truth Social]] post objecting to the Republican primary debate schedule, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] singled out Ryan's dual role, writing that the second debate was being held at the Reagan Library, "the Chairman of which is, amazingly, Fred Ryan, Publisher of The Washington Post. NO!"[1] The complaint formed part of Trump's broader argument that the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] debate process was stacked with hostile institutions and networks.[1]
