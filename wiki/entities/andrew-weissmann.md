# Andrew Weissmann

Andrew Weissmann is a former U.S. Department of Justice prosecutor best known for his role as a senior member of the team assembled by Special Counsel [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]] during the Russia investigation. In Badlands Media's coverage he is cited as a recurring figure in what the outlet's contributors describe as the politicized use of federal prosecutorial power.[1]

## Coverage in the Badlands News Brief

In the June 16, 2023 edition of the Badlands News Brief, contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] urged readers to "familiarize yourself with Just Security, and do not forget the old name Andrew Weissmann," presenting Weissmann as a thread connecting the Russiagate era to the 2023 prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] under the [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]].[1] The commentary noted Weissmann's association with the legal commentary platform [[just-security|Just Security]], which the contributor described as partially funded by the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundation]], and framed that overlap as evidence of ideological continuity between the Mueller probe and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s later posture toward Trump.[1]

## Role in the expanded Mueller scope

Badlands Media's "Do You Believe in Coincidences?" associates Weissmann with the portions of the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller investigation]] that reached beyond the original Russia mandate — plans to comb through [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s financial records and to target his children.[2] The article states that when Acting Attorney General [[matthew-whitaker|Matthew Whitaker]] stripped out the "special articles" that [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] had inserted to expand the scope, forcing the inquiry back to its original narrow mandate, it effectively ended "a whole bunch of the Andrew Weissmann parts of the investigation."[2]
