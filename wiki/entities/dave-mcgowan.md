# Dave McGowan

**Dave McGowan** was an American author cited in the Badlands account as making claims parallel to those of former MI6 officer [[john-coleman|John Coleman]] about the manufactured nature of the 1960s counterculture.[1]

In his book *Weird Scenes Inside the Canyon*, McGowan argues — in the source's characterisation, "as clear as day" — that the CIA and military intelligence were connected to many of the rock bands that emerged from the Laurel Canyon area of California in the 1960s.[1] This work is used to corroborate the broader claim that the [[1960s-american-counterculture-movement|1960s American counterculture]] was an engineered phenomenon rather than an organic youth movement.[1]
