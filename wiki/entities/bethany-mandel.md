# Bethany Mandel

**Bethany Mandel** is a Jewish columnist and political commentator cited by Badlands Media among the critics of the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]]'s partisan turn.[1]

Writing for The Federalist, Mandel noted that the ADL under [[jonathan-greenblatt|Jonathan Greenblatt]] had removed the words "anti-Semitism" from its mission statement.[1] Badlands Media presents her observation as documentation of an observable leftward mission shift during the Greenblatt era, one that expanded the ADL's scope to include attacking anything the political establishment deemed "hateful."[1]
