# Peter Nygard

**Peter Nygard** is a Canadian fashion executive whose arrest is cited in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary among the trafficking-related prosecutions that followed the [[q-operation|Q]] drops.[1]

## Citation in Chapter 4

Chapter 4 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series reads drop #29 of November 1, 2017 as reporting that a number of trafficking operations had been stopped while others remained ongoing, framing the effort as an ongoing war that will not end until all bad actors are in custody.[1] The author asks readers to consider the names arrested after that drop and lists [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]], [[harvey-weinstein|Weinstein]], [[ghislaine-maxwell|Maxwell]], Jean-Luc Brunel and Nygard.[1] He argues that this record shows [[donald-trump|Trump]] went after such figures harder than every president before him, and expresses incomprehension that some within the movement fail to recognise it.[1]
