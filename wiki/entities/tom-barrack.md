# Tom Barrack

Tom Barrack is a longtime associate of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] who serves as United States ambassador to Turkey and Syria. The [[badlands-brief-0fb|April 24, 2026 Badlands Brief]] describes him as "President Trump's best friend and ambassador to Turkey/Syria."

## Assessment of Hezbollah

In a Fox News interview cited by the Brief, Barrack said that [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] cannot be annihilated through military force, given the organization's deep integration into [[lebanon|Lebanese]] society over the past several decades. The remark is presented in the source as a counterweight to Israeli declarations of intent to disarm and dismantle Hezbollah, and as evidence that the Trump administration's approach to Lebanon rests on negotiation rather than the military eradication favored by [[israel|Israel]]'s government.
