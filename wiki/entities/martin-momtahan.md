# Martin Momtahan

**Martin Momtahan** is a Republican member of the Georgia House of Representatives from Dallas, Georgia.[1]

## ERIC withdrawal bill

In February 2025 Momtahan introduced legislation that would withdraw Georgia from the [[electronic-registration-information-center|Electronic Registration Information Center]] (ERIC), the bipartisan multistate group that lets officials share data to keep voter rolls accurate.[1] The bill was advanced by House Republicans in a Georgia committee on Tuesday, February 18, 2025, and would make Georgia the tenth state to leave; nine had already departed, though none since October 2023.[1]

Momtahan argued that the departure of other states, including many bordering Georgia, had rendered ERIC's data and network "totally ineffective."[1] Georgia's Republican Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]] has by contrast credited the system with helping him maintain accurate voter data, which officials say is more robust than what states can gather on their own.[1] Twenty-four states and Washington, D.C. were ERIC members at the time.[1]
