# Tom Liddy

Tom Liddy is the civil division chief of the [[maricopa-county-attorneys-office|Maricopa County Attorney's Office]] and the county's principal legal representative during the disputes over the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) He is a son of G. Gordon Liddy of Watergate notoriety, and previously worked as a radio talk show host — a period during which Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] says she worked with him, offering only "No comment."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

Liddy drew attention in November 2022 for a reply sent to a [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] campaign volunteer who had sought answers from the county; the Kari Lake War Room account published the exchange on November 18, 2022, where it circulated widely.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) The following day, Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] addressed her Election Integrity Unit demand letter concerning [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]]'s conduct of the election directly to Liddy.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
