# Tom Liddy

Tom Liddy is an American attorney who served as Civil Division Chief of the [[maricopa-county-attorneys-office|Maricopa County Attorney's Office]], in which capacity he was the county's chief civil lawyer during the disputed 2022 general election. He is the son of [[g-gordon-liddy|G. Gordon Liddy]], the Watergate conspirator and later radio host, and was himself a radio talk show host before entering county service — [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] notes in [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]] that she "worked with Tom back in the day when he was a radio talk show host," adding only, "No comment."

## The 2022 election

Liddy was the addressee of the November 19, 2022 letter from Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]], which set out three categories of alleged non-compliance by [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]] — ballot-on-demand printer configuration failures at at least 60 voting locations, deficient election-day check-out procedures, and the mishandling of [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3"]] ballots — and requested a response before the county submitted its official canvass to the [[arizona-secretary-of-state|Secretary of State]].

Days earlier, on November 18, the Kari Lake War Room account had publicized Liddy's reply to a volunteer for [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] under the caption, "We've asked Maricopa County for answers. This is what we get in return." The post drew over 13,000 likes and became the subject of national reporting on whether Lake's Republican National Committee lawyer had threatened Liddy.
