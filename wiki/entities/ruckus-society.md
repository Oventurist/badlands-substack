# Ruckus Society

The Ruckus Society is an American direct-action training organization described in a September 2025 [[capital-research-center|Capital Research Center]] report as the militant partner of the [[center-for-third-world-organizing|Center for Third World Organizing]].[1]

## Allegations

According to the report, the Ruckus Society trained activists in property destruction and sabotage during the 2020 riots.[1] It was named as part of a set of U.S.-based organizations that the report said engage in "direct actions" meeting the FBI's definition of domestic terrorism, and which had collectively received more than $80 million from the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] since 2016.[1]
