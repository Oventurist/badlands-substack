# RuPaul's Drag Race

*RuPaul's Drag Race* is an American reality competition television franchise centered on drag performance, credited with bringing drag culture into the entertainment mainstream.[1]

## Shangela

The show launched the career of [[shangela|Shangela]], the persona of Darius Jeremy ("DJ") Pierce, who introduced herself on air as "the debutantess of the Deep South."[1] After an early elimination in Season Two, Shangela returned for two further installments of the franchise and became one of its most beloved contestants, later building a public profile as a queer activist.[1] In March 2024, a *[[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]]* investigation reported multiple allegations of sexual assault and coercion against Pierce, raising questions within the franchise's fan community about accountability for its breakout stars.[1]
