# Brian Becker

Brian Becker is a longtime US activist whose organizing network was linked in 2026 reporting to Breakthrough BT Media Inc., a pro-communist outlet.[1]

## Singham funding coverage

Breakthrough BT Media Inc. was listed among six key nonprofits receiving money in the alleged [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]] funding structure, with $1,098,000 attributed to it and the outlet described as linked to Becker's network.[1] That structure fed a broader ecosystem including the [[answer-coalition|ANSWER Coalition]] and the [[party-for-socialism-and-liberation|Party for Socialism and Liberation]].[1]
