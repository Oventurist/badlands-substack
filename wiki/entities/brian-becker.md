# Brian Becker

Brian Becker is a longtime American left-wing activist associated with the [[answer-coalition|ANSWER Coalition]] and the [[party-for-socialism-and-liberation|Party for Socialism and Liberation]].

## Media network and the Singham funding probe

[[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]] reports that Breakthrough BT Media Inc., described as a pro-communist outlet linked to Becker's network, received $1,098,000 within the funding architecture attributed to [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]]. The Brief further states that the six recipient nonprofits in that structure funneled at least $223 million into a broader global network encompassing the ANSWER Coalition, the Party for Socialism and Liberation, the People's Welfare Association and other unidentified groups — all of which fall within the ambit of the federal grand jury inquiry initiated by U.S. Attorney [[jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]].
