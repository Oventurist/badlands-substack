# Jeremy Johnson

Justice Jeremy Johnson is a British High Court judge who was assigned as one of the two judges to rule on [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]]'s bid to block his extradition from the United Kingdom to the United States.[1] Reporting by *Declassified UK*, republished by [[consortium-news|Consortium News]], found that before his elevation to the bench Johnson had acted as counsel for the UK's Secret Intelligence Service ([[mi6|MI6]]) and for the [[uk-ministry-of-defence|Ministry of Defence]].[1]

## Security clearance and government work

Johnson served as a specially vetted barrister, cleared by UK authorities to handle top-secret material.[1] The precise total of his government earnings is not public, but records show he was paid twice by the Government Legal Department for his services during 2018, for sums exceeding £55,000 combined.[1]

## Role in the Assange extradition case

Johnson was to sit alongside his senior judge, Dame Victoria Sharp, in deciding the fate of the [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] co-founder, who faced a maximum sentence of 175 years if extradited.[1] Critics noted that Assange's prosecution had been pursued at the behest of Washington's intelligence and security services, with which British agencies maintain deep relations, and that Assange's journalism had been built on exposing the secrets of exactly those national-security establishments.[1] As with earlier judges who handled the case, this history raised concerns about institutional conflicts of interest.[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] argued that a judge with a long history of representing intelligence agencies and handling classified material is precisely the type of official Assange's work would have been likely to expose, making the assignment especially dangerous for the defendant.[1] Esler tied the case to the broader power struggle between [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the [[deep-state|Deep State]], noting Assange's role in undermining the claim that Russians were the source of the DNC hack that damaged [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[1]
