# Movladi Baisarov

Movladi Baisarov was a Chechen commander and former member of [[akhmad-kadyrov|Akhmad Kadyrov]]'s security force who was killed in Moscow in November 2006.[1]

Baisarov was believed to have played a role in Akhmad Kadyrov's 2004 assassination and refused to serve as a subordinate to Kadyrov's son, [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]].[1] He was known as a "prominent Wahabi" — a designation Badlands treated as a telltale sign of a [[cia|CIA]] connection — and had been accused of running a torture chamber in the prison under his control as well as an extensive kidnapping and assassination operation, features the outlet characterized as hallmarks of [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]].[1]

In November 2006, Ramzan Kadyrov tasked [[apty-alaudinov|Apty Alaudinov]] with eliminating Baisarov.[1] Alaudinov led fifty Chechen police officers into the streets of Moscow, where they confronted and killed him.[1] Moscow authorities opened a criminal investigation, objecting that the operation had used "methods of the Chechen republic," but could not narrow down a specific killer because Baisarov had been struck by so many bullets.[1] The unit that carried out the killing became the nucleus of the reconstituted [[akhmat-special-forces|Akhmat Special Forces]].[1]
