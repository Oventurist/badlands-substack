# Jonathan Karl

Jonathan Karl is an anchor for [[abc-news|ABC News]].[1]

## Reaction to post-indictment Trump polling

On the June 18, 2023 edition of ABC's *This Week*, Karl expressed astonishment that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was rising in public opinion after becoming the first former president to face federal prosecution.[1] Citing a [[quinnipiac-university|Quinnipiac]] poll on a possible Biden–Trump matchup that put [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] at 48 percent and Trump at 44 percent — a survey taken largely after the federal indictment — Karl noted the result was within the margin of error and therefore a statistical tie, asking what it said about Biden that he was barely beating Trump and losing to him in some polls.[1]
