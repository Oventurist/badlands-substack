# Norman Podhoretz

**Norman Podhoretz** is an American neoconservative intellectual identified by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as one of the lesser-known Jewish members of the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] (PNAC).[1]

## Role in PNAC

DeLarme's November 2025 essay [[maga-schism|"The MAGA Schism"]] pairs Podhoretz with [[elliott-abrams|Elliott Abrams]] as figures who tied U.S. power to [[israel|Israel]]'s stability.[1] The essay treats PNAC, active from 1997 to 2006, as the neoconservative hotbed of the Clinton and Bush Jr. administrations from which the foreign policy of that era poured forth — producing the invasion of Iraq, the war in Afghanistan, the broader War on Terror doctrine, diplomatic cover for Israeli military operations, and calls for war with Syria and Iran that DeLarme says are still being pushed by the foreign policy establishment under [[donald-trump|Trump]] 2.0.[1]

## Context

Podhoretz appears in the essay as part of the case that [[neoconservatives|neoconservatism]] and [[zionism|Zionism]] are deeply intertwined, a claim DeLarme advances without taking a position on whether that relationship is good or bad, noting only that it appears fundamentally at odds with Trump's stated commitment to ending forever wars.[1]
