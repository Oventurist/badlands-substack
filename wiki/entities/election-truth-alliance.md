# Election Truth Alliance

The Election Truth Alliance is an organization described in the June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief as a left-wing counterpart to the American election-integrity movement. Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterizes it as "the left's" new election-denial movement — a group contesting the integrity of U.S. elections from the opposite end of the political spectrum.

## Outreach from Why We Vote

In the same Brief, Ashe in America states that he and fellow Badlands host [[canncon|Brian]] intend to invite the Election Truth Alliance onto [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]], the Badlands Media election-integrity program the two have co-hosted weekly for three years. He argues that "no one has covered election integrity in greater depth or with more integrity than Badlands Media across shows," and asks why an organization with "truth" in its name would refuse to appear — while speculating, in a joking aside, that its members might "agree and then bail while the intro is playing."

The commentary frames the outreach around President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s June 7, 2026 walkout on [[kristen-welker|Kristen Welker]] of [[meet-the-press|Meet the Press]] over elections being fake, and around the broader claim, advanced in the Brief, that the United States lacks real elections. The 2026 midterm elections are the immediate political context for the exchange.
