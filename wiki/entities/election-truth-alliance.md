# Election Truth Alliance

The Election Truth Alliance is an organization described in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] as the vehicle of a left-wing election denier movement that emerged by mid-2026.[1]

## Badlands' open invitation

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that she and [[canncon|CannCon]] believed the group's members should appear on the Why We Vote program, asking rhetorically "why would they say no when 'truth' is in the name of the entity?"[1] She noted that Badlands "talk[s] to everyone" and that its only allegiance is to the truth, while acknowledging the pushback the outlet receives "for not trusting an obviously rigged system enough."[1]

She closed by speculating that the group might agree to appear and then bail while the intro was playing, calling that pattern "classic earmarks."[1]
