# Nancy Cano

Nancy Cano is the wife of former Doña Ana County Magistrate Judge [[joel-cano|Joel Cano]] and was arrested with him in April 2025 on charges of evidence tampering.[1]

## Arrest

Federal agents raided the Canos' home in Las Cruces, New Mexico, on Thursday, April 24, 2025, and took the couple into custody.[1] The charges arose from allegations that they harbored Cristhian Ortega-Lopez, described as an illegal alien and a believed member of Venezuela's [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang.[1]

The arrest followed a February incident in which Ortega-Lopez was apprehended at the Cano residence.[1] Court documents indicated he had initially been hired by Nancy Cano for home repairs and was later offered accommodation in the couple's guesthouse.[1]
