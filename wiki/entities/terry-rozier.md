# Terry Rozier

Terry Rozier is a Miami Heat player of the [[national-basketball-association|NBA]] who was among the most notable individuals arrested in October 2025 in a large federal indictment concerning illegal sports gambling and the rigging of poker games.[1]

## The federal gambling indictment

Rozier was arrested along with Portland Trail Blazers head coach [[chauncey-billups|Chauncey Billups]].[1] Beyond the two basketball figures, several mafia groups were implicated in the same federal case, indicating that the alleged betting scheme intersected with established organized crime networks.[1]

[[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] said the fraud uncovered was "mind-boggling" and that the arrests concluded a yearslong illegal gambling investigation that "envelops both the NBA and La Casa Nostra."[1] Patel said investigators had not only cracked the fraud committed "on the grand stage of the NBA" but had also acted against [[la-cosa-nostra|La Cosa Nostra]], naming the Bonanno, Gambino, Genovese and Luchese crime families.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the case as an illustration of institutional convergence — professional sports, federal law enforcement and organized crime intersecting — and read the [[fbi|FBI]]'s promotion of the story as deliberate.[1] The segment closed with the assertion that "sports are fake," tied to the broader [[system-of-systems|System of Systems]] framing used across Badlands commentary.[1]
