# Soldiers for the Hostages

Soldiers for the Hostages is an Israeli anti-war group that organizes serving soldiers, reservists and veterans opposed to the continuation of the war in Gaza, with a particular emphasis on prioritizing the return of Israeli hostages held by [[hamas|Hamas]].[1]

## June 2025 refusal letter

In June 2025 the group organized a letter, shared online late on Tuesday, June 10, in which 41 Israeli military intelligence officers and reservists announced that they would no longer participate in combat operations in Gaza. The letter was addressed to Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], Defence Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]], and the head of the military.[1]

The signatories said the government was waging an "unnecessary, eternal war" in Gaza and that they would refuse to take part in a "war designed to preserve the rule of Netanyahu" and to appease "anti-democratic and messianic elements" in his government. They argued that "when a government acts for ulterior motives, harms civilians and leads to the killing of innocent people, the orders it issues are clearly illegal, and we must not obey them."[1]

The group, understood to include members of the elite military surveillance division [[unit-8200|Unit 8200]], said Netanyahu's government had effectively handed a "death sentence" to the hostages when it "chose to collapse" the ceasefire deal in March, claimed that "many hostages have already been killed by IDF bombings," and accused the government of continuing to "abandon their lives."[1]

One signatory told The Guardian that "the hope is that as many people as possible will challenge the legitimacy of this operation and of this government." Another intelligence officer who had recently refused to serve described the campaign as "immoral and insanely excessive," saying the military "didn't think twice" about harming civilians and that if there was a goal, "it's to try to get rid of Gaza's population by any means."[1]

## Significance

The letter emerged amid growing dissent inside parts of the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli military]] and an apparent increase in the number of soldiers refusing to fight.[1] Badlands commentary framed it as part of a pattern of IDF intelligence breaking ranks with [[mossad|Mossad]] and Netanyahu over how — and how long — the war has been waged, noting that Unit 8200 alumni include figures as varied as Israeli journalist [[barak-ravid|Barak Ravid]]. [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] characterized the episode as "an earnest mutiny" in which the dissidents held the moral high ground, and speculated it might be part of a [[donald-trump|Trump]] strategy to subvert the start of another forever war in the Middle East.[1]
