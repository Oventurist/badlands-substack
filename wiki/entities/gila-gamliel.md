# Gila Gamliel

Gila Gamliel is an Israeli politician and member of the [[likud|Likud]] party who served as Israel's science minister in the government of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]

## Call to fire Bezalel Smotrich

On Wednesday, July 17, 2024, Gamliel publicly called on Netanyahu to "immediately" fire Finance Minister [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] after Smotrich threatened to leave the governing coalition if a hostage deal were reached with [[hamas|Hamas]].[1] Speaking in an interview on Channel 13 News, she said, "There is no reason why this deal should not go through," and argued that "Smotrich's threats — for that, he should have been fired immediately. The very threat should have caused him to go home."[1]

Gamliel extended her criticism to Smotrich's far-right coalition allies, principally National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]], describing their rhetoric as an "unbearable discourse" and noting that in a previous hostage deal in November, Smotrich had publicly claimed he would oppose the arrangement only to vote in favor once he saw the details.[1] "'We will resign,' so resign! In any case, they are a burden on this government. It wouldn't hurt if they resigned," she said.[1]

## Significance

Gamliel's intervention illustrated the internal strain within Likud produced by Netanyahu's reliance on the far-right factions of his coalition, a dynamic that Badlands commentators framed as evidence that Netanyahu had exhausted his moderate political allies and become dependent on radical partners who would not permit any ceasefire with Hamas or [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1]
