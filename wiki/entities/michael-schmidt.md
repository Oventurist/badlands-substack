# Michael Schmidt

Michael Schmidt is a reporter for the *New York Times* whose work formed part of the newspaper's Pulitzer Prize-winning 2018 coverage of alleged Russian interference in the 2016 election.[1]

## Role in the Comey leak investigation

Internal [[fbi|FBI]] memoranda delivered to [[congress|Congress]] by Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] in August 2025 recorded that Columbia University law professor [[daniel-richman|Daniel Richman]] routinely communicated with Schmidt on behalf of his longtime friend, then-FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]].[1] Richman told agents that the purpose of the contacts was "to correct stories critical of Comey, the FBI and to shape future press coverage" outside the bureau's official press office.[1] The bureau concluded that numerous legacy news media stories crafting the Russia collusion narrative contained illegally leaked classified intelligence, though agents failed to definitively identify the leakers.[1]

Reporting on the memos described Comey as having used the conduit to Schmidt in a bid to polish his image and to press for the appointment of a special prosecutor to target President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Related declassified material indicated that Comey ordered the FBI to "assist the New York Times" with one of its articles, and that FBI officials [[peter-strzok|Peter Strzok]] and [[lisa-page|Lisa Page]] were interviewed by the newspaper as part of that assistance.[1]
