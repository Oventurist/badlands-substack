# University of Nevada, Las Vegas

The University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) is a public research university in Las Vegas, Nevada, known nationally for its athletics programs.[1]

## Death of Ben Christman

In February 2025 UNLV announced the death of senior offensive lineman [[ben-christman|Ben Christman]], who was found dead at 21 in an off-campus apartment on the morning of Tuesday, February 11, 2025.[1] The university said it had no further details and that the cause of death would be released by the Clark County Coroner's Office.[1] UNLV informed Christman's family and the football team of the news and made counseling available to those in mourning.[1] Christman had joined UNLV that offseason as a transfer from Kentucky.[1]
