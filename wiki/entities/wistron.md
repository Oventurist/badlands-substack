# Wistron

Wistron (Taiwan Stock Exchange: 3231) is a Taiwanese contract electronics manufacturer that produces servers, computing hardware, and other electronic systems for global technology brands.[1]

## Role in Nvidia's US AI buildout

In April 2025, Wistron was named alongside [[foxconn|Foxconn]] as one of the contract manufacturing partners for [[nvidia|Nvidia]]'s announced buildout of artificial intelligence infrastructure inside the United States.[1] Nvidia said on Monday, April 14, 2025 that it would produce up to $500 billion of AI infrastructure in the US over the following four years, and that two new supercomputer manufacturing plants would be built in Texas in partnership with Foxconn and Wistron.[1] Nvidia stated it expected to mass-produce supercomputers at those sites within 12 to 15 months, while its Blackwell AI chips were already in production at [[tsmc|TSMC]]'s plant in Phoenix, Arizona.[1]

The announcement was framed against the backdrop of [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s trade policy and the broader push to onshore heavy industry and reduce reliance on foreign-made technology.[1] Commentary in the Badlands News Brief characterized dependence on foreign-manufactured technology as one of the most serious US supply-chain vulnerabilities from a national security standpoint, extending the argument to electronic voting equipment.[1]
