# Anton LaVey

Anton LaVey was the founder and leader of the [[church-of-satan|Church of Satan]] and, according to the testimony of [[mark-passio|Mark Passio]] recounted in the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult," the man who personally elevated Passio within the organization.[1]

## Appointment of Mark Passio

Passio states that after being recruited as a teenager circa 1995, LaVey himself appointed him to the position of high priest and granted him authority over a Grotto in the Philadelphia area.[1] In that role Passio was tasked with attending and overseeing midnight meetings with members of the satanic group, an experience that exposed him to a membership drawn from politicians, lawyers, teachers, policemen and priests rather than the countercultural youth he had anticipated.[1] It was this exposure that ultimately led Passio to break with the organization and dedicate his life to exposing what he calls the dark occult agenda.[1]
