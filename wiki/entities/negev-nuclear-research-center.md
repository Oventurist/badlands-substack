# Negev Nuclear Research Center

The **Negev Nuclear Research Center** is the Israeli nuclear facility near Dimona that lies at the center of long-running claims that [[israel|Israel]] developed an undeclared nuclear weapons arsenal.[1]

According to commentary published in the Badlands News Brief, [[david-ben-gurion|David Ben-Gurion]], Israel's first prime minister, was obsessed with obtaining nuclear weapons from the time he assumed office in 1948, justifying the pursuit as a means of preventing another Holocaust.[1] Ben-Gurion found an ally in the French, who helped him develop the Negev Nuclear Research Center; construction began in 1958 and the facility became active sometime between 1962 and 1964.[1] By 1967, Israel is said to have developed its first nuclear weapon.[1]

Public knowledge of the facility's weapons-related work stems largely from [[mordechai-vanunu|Mordechai Vanunu]], an Israeli scientist employed there who defected in 1986 and went public, corroborating his claims with detailed photographs of the facility and the nuclear material housed within it.[1] Vanunu was subsequently drugged and captured by the [[mossad|Mossad]] in Italy, returned to Israel, and convicted of treason in a closed trial.[1]

French President [[charles-de-gaulle|Charles de Gaulle]] discovered the Franco-Israeli nuclear partnership in 1958 and sought to stop it, and in 1963 President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] similarly pressed Ben-Gurion over the program.[1] Kennedy succeeded in arranging inspections of alleged Israeli nuclear sites, but these were not carried out until after his assassination at the end of 1963 and ultimately led nowhere.[1]
