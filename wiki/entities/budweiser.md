# Budweiser

Budweiser is the American beer brand cited in Badlands Media commentary as one of the clearest examples of a consumer boycott inflicting measurable financial damage on a company perceived as having gone "woke."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] pairs Budweiser with [[target|Target]] as "an obvious example" supporting his contention that boycotting works: if consumers stop buying products or services from woke companies, those companies lose money.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

Budweiser also appears on Carlson's personal list of the brands that most represent American culture, alongside Coca-Cola, [[disney|Disney]], Levi's, Hershey's and [[mcdonalds|McDonald's]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america) That dual status is what makes it central to the article's argument: as an [[americana-brands|Americana brand]] that has, in Carlson's view, gone out of its way to upset its customer base through wokeness, it fits the pattern [[andy-frisella|Andy Frisella]] identifies of companies being pushed into positions that invite their own destruction.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

Carlson explicitly declines to tell readers to stop boycotting Budweiser, [[target|Target]], [[disney|Disney]] or any other company pushing wokeness, Satanism or the grooming of children — asking only that they weigh the possibility they are serving the [[deep-state|Deep State]]'s ends.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-communist-plot-to-take-over-america)

## Todd Allen's transfer to Bud Light (2023)

[[todd-allen|Todd Allen]], recently the global marketing vice president for Budweiser, was named in April 2023 to take over marketing for sister brand [[bud-light|Bud Light]] after vice president [[alissa-heinerscheid|Alissa Heinerscheid]] went on leave amid the [[dylan-mulvaney|Dylan Mulvaney]] partnership backlash at [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch InBev]].[2]
