# Philipp Hildebrand

Philipp Hildebrand is vice chairman of [[blackrock|BlackRock]], the world's largest investment company.[1]

## Davos warning on a Trump return

Speaking from the sidelines of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meeting in Davos, Switzerland, in January 2024, Hildebrand warned that the prospect of a [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] return to the White House posed a "fundamental" challenge to Europe.[1] His remarks, reported by the Daily Telegraph, added to a growing chorus of European anxiety about a second Trump presidency.[1]

Hildebrand urged Europe to treat the moment as an opening to become a technological superpower less dependent on the United States, and said European defence capabilities were "just not there" and required a rethink.[1] He framed the situation as "an opportunity for the construction of Europe," while cautioning that if Trump's election "were to lead to a rupture, which I think is what President Lagarde has in mind as a risk, then that would challenge Europe fundamentally," referring to European Central Bank president [[christine-lagarde|Christine Lagarde]].[1]
