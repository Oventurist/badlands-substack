# Paul Stanley

Paul Stanley is an American rock musician, a founding member and front man of the band Kiss.[1]

## Statements on youth gender transition

In spring 2023 Stanley publicly rebuked the practice of allowing children to undergo gender transitions.[1] His remarks were subsequently endorsed by Twisted Sister singer [[dee-snider|Dee Snider]], whose support led San Francisco Pride to cancel Snider's scheduled appearance at its June 2023 celebration.[1]

Commentary in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief noted that Stanley, like Snider, had not previously aligned with conservative causes and had strongly promoted masking and vaccination, but that his large platform gave weight to his opposition to drag shows for children and to medical transition of minors.[1]
