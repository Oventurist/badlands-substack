# NXXT Golf

NXXT Golf is a major professional women's golf tour that operates the NXXT Women's Pro Tour.[1]

## 2024 eligibility policy change

On Friday, March 8, 2024, NXXT Golf announced that individuals born male would not be permitted to compete in its events even after gender transition, effectively banning the participation of transgender golfer Hailey Davidson.[1] In a post published that day, the organization stated that "effective immediately, competitors must be a biological female at birth to participate" in the NXXT Women's Pro Tour, adding that the decision "underscores the organization's commitment to maintaining the integrity of women's professional golf and ensuring fair competition."[1]

The policy followed Davidson's victory in the NXXT Women's Classic earlier in January 2024. Davidson, who was born male and transitioned in 2021, had raised the possibility of becoming the first biologically male golfer to qualify for the Ladies Professional Golf Association (LPGA) tour.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] endorsed "recognizing the biological reality of men and women" while warning against narratives that require litigating the effects of hormone treatments, arguing that transhumanist science funded by figures such as transgender billionaire Jennifer Pritzker could eventually make purely biological arguments harder to sustain.[1] He concluded that the cultural and archetypal notions of male and female must be defended alongside biology.[1]
