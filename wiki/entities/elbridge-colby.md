# Elbridge Colby

Elbridge Colby is the U.S. Under Secretary of Defense for Policy in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Suspension of the PJBD

In May 2026, Colby announced via social media that the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] was pausing its participation in the [[permanent-joint-board-on-defense|Permanent Joint Board on Defense]], the U.S.–Canada military consultation body founded in 1940, "to reassess how this forum benefits shared North American defense."[1] He cited Canada's failure to make "credible progress on its defense commitments" as a key factor, against a backdrop of disputes over Canadian defense spending and Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]'s declaration at the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] that the U.S.-led global order was over.[1]

Commentators noted it was unclear why Colby made the announcement when he did, given that Carney's Davos speech had been months earlier.[1]

## Named in the DIA espionage assessment

In June 2026, NBC News and [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] reported that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] had elevated its assessment of Israeli espionage to a "critical" level, naming Colby — with [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and [[michael-dimino|Michael DiMino]] — among officials allegedly targeted by Israeli intelligence collection focused on U.S. deliberations over [[iran|Iran]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[2] The Pentagon declined comment, the [[white-house|White House]] called the report false, and the Israeli Embassy in Washington dismissed the allegations as politically motivated.[2]
