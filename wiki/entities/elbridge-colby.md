# Elbridge Colby

Elbridge Colby is the United States Under Secretary of Defense for Policy in the second Trump administration. He appears in the Badlands corpus as the official who announced the suspension of American participation in the long-standing U.S.–Canada defense consultation body.

## Suspension of the Permanent Joint Board on Defense

According to the May 20, 2026 Badlands Brief, Colby announced via social media that the [[department-of-defense|Department of Defense]] — referred to in some reports as the Department of War — was pausing its involvement in the [[permanent-joint-board-on-defense|Permanent Joint Board on Defense]] "to reassess how this forum benefits shared North American defense." He cited [[canada|Canada]]'s failure to make "credible progress on its defense commitments" as a key factor, and framed the posture in terms quoted approvingly by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]: "Only by investing in our own defense capabilities will Americans and Canadians be safe, secure, and prosperous."

Commentators noted the timing was unexplained, since Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]]'s [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] speech declaring the U.S.-led global order over had occurred months earlier. Analysts described the pause as largely symbolic, with [[norad|NORAD]] modernization, intelligence sharing, and border cooperation expected to continue.
