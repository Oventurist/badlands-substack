# Harris Faulkner

Harris Faulkner is an American television host at [[fox-news|Fox News]], where she anchors the program "The Faulkner Focus."[1]

## October 2024 Trump town hall

In October 2024, Faulkner hosted a Fox News town hall with former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before a friendly audience of women voters in Georgia.[1] The event was recorded on Tuesday, October 15, 2024, and aired the following day on "The Faulkner Focus."[1]

During the town hall, Faulkner played a clip of Trump describing his Democratic opponents as the "enemy within" and suggesting that the military could be used to quell election-related unrest.[1] Trump used the exchange to reaffirm rather than retreat from the characterization, telling Faulkner that his critics were "a party of sound bites" and that "it is the enemy from within, and they're very dangerous."[1] He went on to single out Democratic California Representatives [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] and former Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]], while insisting, "I'm not threatening anybody. They're the ones doing the threatening."[1]

Badlands Media cited the town hall as evidence that the "enemy from within" framing had become a central axis of the closing weeks of the 2024 campaign, with the outlet's contributors arguing that the oath of office itself contemplates domestic as well as foreign enemies.[1]
