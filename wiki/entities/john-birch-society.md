# John Birch Society

The John Birch Society is an American anti-communist organization that appears in Badlands Media's account of the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]] as an institutional base for early criticism of elite policy forums.[1]

Congressman [[larry-mcdonald|Larry McDonald]] served as its national chairman and was, in that capacity, a vocal critic of what the article calls these secret societies.[1] McDonald introduced in the House of Representatives the American Legion's 1980 Resolution 773 and a comparable Veterans of Foreign Wars resolution, both of which sought a congressional investigation of the Trilateral Commission and the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]]; neither advanced.[1] McDonald died in the downing of Korean Air Lines Flight 007 on September 1, 1983.[1]
