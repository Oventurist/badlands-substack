# MI6

MI6 is the British foreign intelligence service. In [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay it is placed above both the American [[cia|CIA]] and the Israeli [[mossad|Mossad]] in the alleged hierarchy of global control.[1]

## Position in the alleged hierarchy

Carlson writes that when people blame Jews or Israel for everything bad in the world, they have missed that above the CIA and Mossad is the British MI6, and above Tel Aviv and Washington, D.C. is the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[1] He argues that criminals do run the United States and Israel, but that they are merely middle management taking orders from their puppet masters in the City.[1] MI6 thus functions in his framework as the intelligence arm of the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]].[1]
