# Loren AliKhan

Loren AliKhan is a judge of the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia, appointed by President Joe Biden.[1]

## Blocking the 2025 spending freeze

On Tuesday, January 28, 2025, minutes before a directive from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s budget office was to take effect, AliKhan halted the administration's freeze on federal aid programs, ruling that the courts needed more time to consider its potentially far-reaching ramifications.[1] Her order barred the administration from suspending disbursement of any congressionally appropriated funds and was set to expire February 3 at 5 p.m.[1] She characterized the ruling as a "brief administrative stay" intended to maintain the status quo while litigation proceeded, saying, "I think there is the specter of irreparable harm."[1] The decision was a win for the nonprofit and public health groups that had filed suit earlier that day challenging the freeze ordered overnight by the [[office-of-management-and-budget|Office of Management and Budget]].[1]
