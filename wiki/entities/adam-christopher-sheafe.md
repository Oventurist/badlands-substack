# Adam Christopher Sheafe

Adam Christopher Sheafe is an American man, aged 51 at the time of reporting, accused of the religiously motivated murder of an Arizona pastor.[1]

## Confession and alleged hit list

In a jailhouse interview with True Crime Arizona correspondent Briana Whitney, reported in late June 2025, Sheafe said he had crucified 76-year-old pastor [[william-schonemann|William Schonemann]] — known locally as "Pastor Bill" — inside the clergyman's New River, Arizona home in April 2025.[1] Sheafe described the killing as part of what he claimed was a divine mission to punish religious leaders who, in his view, were misleading their followers, and revealed that he had a broader hit list of religious figures he intended to kill during the rampage.[1]

"I drove from there (Phoenix) to Bill's house, like two in the morning on a Sunday night, and I executed him," Sheafe told the correspondent.[1] Schonemann was found dead in his bed, covered in blood, on April 28, 2025 during a welfare check.[1]

The case was carried as a bonus item in the Badlands News Brief covering the news of Tuesday, June 24, 2025.[1]
