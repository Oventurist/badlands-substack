# American Federation of Teachers

The American Federation of Teachers (AFT) is a national American labor union representing teachers and other education and public-sector employees.[1]

## Minnesota general strike endorsement (January 2026)

Local and regional AFT affiliates were among the labor organizations — alongside the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]] and [[seiu|SEIU]] — that publicly endorsed the "ICE Out of Minnesota: Day of Truth and Freedom" statewide economic blackout and general strike called for January 23, 2026 by a coalition of faith leaders and community groups protesting [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]].[1] The action called for no work except emergency services, no school and no shopping, and its organizers demanded [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] withdrawal from Minnesota, officer punishment over the death of Renée Good, no additional ICE funding, and policies further severing state ties with ICE.[1]
