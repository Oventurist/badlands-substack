# The Cradle

**The Cradle** is a news outlet covering West Asian geopolitics, cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as a source for reporting on the Israel–Gaza war.[1]

In November 2023 the outlet reported on an Israeli police investigation, originally published by [[haaretz|Haaretz]], finding that an Israeli attack helicopter had killed some of the attendees at the Nova music festival during the [[october-7-attacks|October 7 attacks]] — the [[nova-music-festival-friendly-fire-admission|first acknowledgement]] that Israeli forces killed some of their own at the site.[1] The item was carried as a bonus entry in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
