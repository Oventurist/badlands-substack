# Genghis Khan

Genghis Khan (c. 1162–1227) was the founder of the Mongol Empire and, in Badlands Media's telling, the pivotal figure linking the histories of China, Russia and [[mongolia|Mongolia]].[1]

## Conquests and dynastic legacy

Genghis Khan conquered China and established the Yuan Dynasty.[1] He sent his eldest son, Jochi, to conquer the Selenga River Valley, the borderlands between modern-day Russia and Mongolia.[1] After Genghis Khan's death, Jochi and his son Batu moved west on what Badlands described as "the most epic conquest in human history."[1] Batu created the state that became known as the Golden Horde, and it was the Golden Horde that ended the Kievan Rus with the Siege of Kiev in 1240.[1]

## Relevance to Russian statehood

The Mongol conquest reshaped the Russian lands. Kiev took centuries to rebuild after the siege, while Moscow fared far better because the Muscovite princes curried favour with the Mongols and served as intermediaries between the Mongol rulers and the Rus nobility; the Golden Horde thereby helped establish Moscow as the centre of influence in the emerging Russian state.[1]

## Contemporary invocation

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] invoked Genghis Khan when commenting on [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s September 2024 visit to Mongolia, writing that "the spirit of Genghis Khan lives" and admiring the pageantry of the welcome extended to the Russian president in the Mongolian capital.[1] In the same brief the figure was worked into the [[trumpamania|Trumpamania]] motif, with the author joking that "Genghis Khan is coming to Trumpamania 2024."[1]
