# Los Lobos

Los Lobos is a drug-trafficking criminal organization designated by the United States as a Foreign Terrorist Organization in 2025.[1]

## Terrorist designation

[[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] said in September 2025 that with Los Lobos and [[los-choneros|Los Choneros]] now designated as Foreign Terrorist Organizations, the groups' "drug money pipelines are drying up."[1] Patel declared that the FBI was "not playing defense" and was instead "eliminating drug and cartel trafficking at the source," concluding that "America will lead, and the cartels will fall."[1]

The designations accompanied an escalating enforcement and military posture toward trafficking networks in the Western Hemisphere, including strikes on alleged drug vessels departing [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1] See also [[foreign-terrorist-organization|Foreign Terrorist Organization]].
