# Allan dos Santos

Allan dos Santos is a Brazilian conservative journalist and video commentator whose channel on the American platform [[rumble|Rumble]] became the focal point of a transnational censorship dispute between Brazilian Supreme Court Justice [[alexandre-de-moraes|Alexandre de Moraes]] and U.S. technology companies.[1]

## Charges in Brazil and flight to the United States

Brazilian authorities alleged that dos Santos committed several offenses in connection with purported disinformation he published about the Supremo Tribunal Federal (STF) and the [[2022-brazilian-general-election|2022 Brazilian general election]].[1] Dos Santos had already left the country, having fled to the United States in 2020, where he remains a legal resident with a pending application for political asylum.[1]

Brazil sought his extradition, but the Biden administration rejected the request in what [[rumble|Rumble]] chief executive [[chris-pavlovski|Chris Pavlovski]] dated to March 2024, on the grounds that the conduct alleged did not constitute an offense in the United States because it is protected by the right to free expression.[1] Pavlovski said U.S. officials characterized the charges as nothing more than "crimes of opinion."[1] The refusal followed the general principle, codified in extradition treaties, that states decline to extradite when the acts underlying the request are not crimes in the requested country.[1]

## The worldwide ban order

Moraes subsequently ordered [[rumble|Rumble]] to close dos Santos's account entirely and to bar him from opening any new ones.[1] Unlike the judge's earlier directives, which required only that the platform block his content for viewers inside [[brazil|Brazil]], this order demanded that Rumble prohibit dos Santos from using or monetizing the platform anywhere in the world, including outside Brazilian jurisdiction, and — as with previous orders — allowed only two hours for compliance.[1]

Pavlovski argued that the American rejection of the extradition request should have ended Moraes's effort to restrict dos Santos's speech in the United States, and accused the judge of attempting to sidestep the U.S. legal system by using secret censorship orders to pressure American companies into banning a political dissident worldwide.[1] The order became the central grievance in [[rumble-and-trump-media-v-moraes|Rumble and Trump Media v. Moraes]], the joint federal lawsuit filed by Rumble and [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media and Technology Group]].[1] Counsel [[e-martin-de-luca|E. Martin De Luca]] asserted that dos Santos, as a legal U.S. resident, is entitled to compensation.[1]

Dos Santos is one of a number of Brazilian dissenting voices who have left the country under judicial pressure, a pattern described in reporting as a growing trend of judicial exiles.[1]
