# House of Windsor

The House of Windsor — formerly Saxe-Coburg-Gotha — is the reigning British royal house, which Matthew Ehret argues carries extensive and unresolved Nazi connections beyond the well-documented case of [[edward-viii|Edward VIII]].[1]

## Nazi connections

Several of these connections implicate the late Prince Consort [[prince-philip|Philip Mountbatten]], Duke of Edinburgh, directly.[1] All three of the Duke of Edinburgh's sisters were married to Nazi princes, and the husband of one of them, Sophie, became a Waffen SS officer with the rank of Oberführer.[1] That husband, Prince Christopher of Hesse-Cassel, was chief of the Forschungsamt (Directorate of Scientific Research), a special intelligence operation run by Hermann Göring, and was also Standartenführer of the SS on [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]]'s personal staff.[1] Philip's four brothers-in-law, with whom he lived, all became high-ranking Nazi Party officials.[1]

Ehret also notes the claim, drawn from the Channel 4 documentary on Edward VIII, that the abdicated king taught his young niece [[elizabeth-ii|Elizabeth II]] to perform a "sieg heil."[1]
