# International Commission of Jurists

The **International Commission of Jurists (ICJ)** is a non-governmental organization composed of judges and lawyers from a range of legal traditions, which describes its mission as building a world based on human rights standards and the rule of law.[1] It should not be confused with the International Court of Justice, which shares the same abbreviation.

## 2025 appeal on global development aid

In February 2025 the ICJ, together with more than 60 civil society organizations, joined an urgent appeal calling on European Union leaders to take immediate action on a global development aid crisis triggered by decisions of the US administration.[1] The appeal cited the executive order signed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on 20 January 2025 imposing a 90-day freeze on all US foreign aid, which the signatories said had already caused clinic closures, the suspension of life-saving disease treatment programmes, the disruption of human rights and rule-of-law initiatives, and a funding crisis for NGOs worldwide.[1] The appeal also objected to the reinstatement of the [[global-gag-rule|Global Gag Rule]].[1]

The signatories urged the EU, as the world's largest foreign aid provider, to step up its leadership and ensure continued support for democracy, human rights, global health, and humanitarian assistance, to prioritize funding for sectors most affected — including reproductive rights, gender equality, and LGBTIQ rights — and to press the US administration to reverse course.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary characterized the ICJ statement as evidence that the funding freeze was achieving its purpose by threatening the organization's own existence, and framed the request as Europe being asked to underwrite programmes American voters had rejected.[1]
