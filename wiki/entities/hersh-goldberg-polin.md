# Hersh Goldberg-Polin

Hersh Goldberg-Polin was an Israeli-American citizen taken hostage during the October 2023 attacks and held in Gaza.[1]

## Death and aftermath

Over the weekend preceding 2 September 2024, the bodies of six hostages, including Goldberg-Polin, were discovered in a tunnel beneath the southern Gaza city of Rafah.[1] The deaths provoked widespread outrage inside Israel, triggering enormous protests and a nationwide general strike on Monday, 2 September, with demonstrators demanding that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] set aside political ambitions and conclude an agreement securing the release of the remaining hostages.[1]

The discovery lent new urgency to US-brokered efforts to pair a hostage release with a pause in the fighting, and preceded President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s public statement that Netanyahu was not doing enough to secure the hostages' release.[1] See [[gaza-ceasefire]].
