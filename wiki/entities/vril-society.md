# Vril Society

The Vril Society was a German occult group of the early 1920s whose name was taken literally from "Vril-ya," the mysterious etheric fluid invented by [[edward-bulwer-lytton|Edward Bulwer-Lytton]] in his novel *The Coming Race*.[1]

Belief in a mystical fluid named "Vril" was among the practices of the [[thule-society|Thule Society]], and both currents drew on Bulwer-Lytton's fiction of an ancient race of hollow-earth superbeings preparing to subjugate surface humanity — a fantasy treated as a living gospel by Nazi occultists and the earlier followers of [[helena-blavatsky|Blavatsky]].[1]
