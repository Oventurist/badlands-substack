# Bill Kramer

**Bill Kramer** is the chief executive of the [[academy-of-motion-picture-arts-and-sciences|Academy of Motion Picture Arts & Sciences]].[1]

In a May 2024 interview, Kramer disclosed that the Academy had launched a $500 million global fundraising drive and had already raised roughly $100 million, with individual donors including [[leonard-blavatnik|Leonard Blavatnik]] and sponsorship deals signed with luxury brands including the Dorchester Collection.[1] He described the Academy's existing broadcast deal with ABC as "very healthy" and the relationship with Walt Disney as "an amazing partnership," while explaining that streaming's disruption of the film and television business had pushed the Academy toward a "revenue diversification campaign."[1] Kramer argued that "No healthy company or organisation should rely on one source of support to a degree that could cause concern if that support decreases."[1]
