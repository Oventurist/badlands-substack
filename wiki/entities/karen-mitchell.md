# Karen Mitchell

Karen Mitchell is a researcher whose work on predatory personality types is cited in Badlands Media commentary on the psychology of the alleged global [[cabal]].[1] According to the Badlands article "Q is Our Best Fren", Mitchell's research provides strong evidence that sociopaths, narcissists, sadists and other predator types are not truly distinct diagnostic categories but variations of a single underlying disorder.[1]

## Persistent Predatory Personality

Mitchell is credited with coining the term [[persistent-predatory-personality|Persistent Predatory Personality]] to describe this unified predator type.[1] The Badlands author endorses her assessment, arguing that "the essence of parasitism doesn't really change from one parasite to the next—they all have their unique traits, but the essence is the same."[1] Readers seeking deeper study are directed to Mitchell's Ph.D. thesis on predatory personalities, hosted at kalmor.com.au.[1]

## Role in Badlands analysis

Mitchell's framework functions in the article as the clinical underpinning for a broader political thesis: that the cabal is best understood as an organism of [[psychopathy-and-parasitism|psychopathic parasites]] rather than a conventional political faction, and that accurate profiling of such personalities is a prerequisite for [[digital-soldiers|Digital Soldiers]] attempting to distinguish genuine allies from infiltrators.[1]
