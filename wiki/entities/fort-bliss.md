# Fort Bliss

Fort Bliss is a major U.S. Army installation in West Texas, near the southern border.[1]

## Role in border deployment

The base has been instrumental in the deployment of troops and equipment to the U.S. southern border and has served as a hub for military deportation flights.[1]

## April 2025 vehicle accident

In mid-April 2025, two service members died and another was seriously injured in a vehicle accident near Santa Teresa, New Mexico, just west of Fort Bliss.[1] The military had not released further details about the accident or the identities of the service members involved at the time of reporting by [[the-independent|The Independent]].[1]
