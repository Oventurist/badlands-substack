# Cynthia Chung

Cynthia Chung is a writer and historical researcher cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] in his November 15, 2025 Badlands Media essay "Don't Be Symbol-Minded."[1]

Ehret referred readers to Chung's essay "Huxley's Ultimate Revolution: The Battle for Your Mind and the Relativity of Madness" for critical analysis of the origins of [[neuro-linguistic-programming|Neuro-Linguistic Programming]] as a substitute for human thinking, developed — in the phrasing of his citation — in the bowels of the Mithraic hub known as the [[esalen-institute|Esalen Institute]] in Big Sur, California.[1] The reference was offered against [[peter-duke|Peter Duke]]'s claim that NLP techniques can replace substantive study of the topics contested in the information war.[1]

## The Empire Where the Black Sun Never Sets

Colonel [[roxane-watkins|Roxane Watkins]] cites Chung's book *The Empire Where the Black Sun Never Sets* as brilliantly articulating the basis for the modern use of [[stay-behind-units|stay-behind units]] in the immediate aftermath of the Second World War.[2] Watkins notes that although the tactic itself is very old, Chung's treatment establishes the postwar application, and that the account involves many members of the [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]].[2] The book is presented as recommended background reading for understanding [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]].[2]
