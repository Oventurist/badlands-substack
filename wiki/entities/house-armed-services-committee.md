# House Armed Services Committee

The House Armed Services Committee is the standing committee of the U.S. House of Representatives responsible for drafting the annual [[national-defense-authorization-act|National Defense Authorization Act]] and overseeing the Department of Defense.[1]

## FY2027 US–Israel defense technology provision

In 2026 the committee's draft Fiscal Year 2027 NDAA contained Section 224, a provision that would create a "[[united-states-israel-defense-technology-cooperation-initiative|United States-Israel Defense Technology Cooperation Initiative]]" expanding defense cooperation between the United States and [[israel|Israel]].[1] The provision would authorize broader collaboration in defense research and development, weapons co-production, joint ventures, licensing agreements, and emerging technology fields including artificial intelligence, quantum technologies, autonomous systems, directed energy, cyber capabilities, and biotechnology, and it references "network integration" and "data fusion" between the two countries.[1]

The measure drew accusations that [[congress|Congress]] was quietly moving to integrate the U.S. and Israeli militaries.[1] Badlands writer [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] assessed the accusation as overblown, a judgment [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] said he trusted while still mocking the political timing of introducing such a bill amid public argument over Israeli influence in Washington.[1]
