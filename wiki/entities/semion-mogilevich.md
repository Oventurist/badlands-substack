# Semion Mogilevich

Semion Mogilevich is described in Badlands coverage as the crime boss of the Ukrainian Mafia, a central figure in post-Soviet organized crime and transnational money laundering.[1]

## Relocation to Israel and the Maxwell connection

According to the late investigative author Gordon Thomas in his book *Robert Maxwell: Israel's Super Spy*, when the demise of the Soviet Union became undeniable in the 1980s Mogilevich briefly moved to [[israel|Israel]] along with his operation.[1] As a longtime business partner of the publisher [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]], Mogilevich is said to have learned money laundering from the man who pioneered the craft in the modern monetary system, with Israel offering the most favorable and low-risk jurisdiction given the nature of its banking system.[1] Thomas's account holds that the Ukrainian and Russian mafias laundered billions of dollars through Israel, and that it was [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]]'s father who taught them how.[1]

## FBI Wanted List

Mogilevich was placed on the FBI Wanted List in 2009 but was removed in 2016 due to the suspicion that "he is believed to be living in a country where the United States doesn't have an extradition treaty."[1]

## Invoked in the 2025 Polish organ case

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry raised Mogilevich while discussing the March 2025 arrest in [[poland|Poland]] of a Ukrainian woman convicted of trafficking 56 kidneys, while acknowledging no established link between Mogilevich and that specific network.[1] The reference was offered to support a broader theory of a Black Sea organ smuggling ring spanning Turkey, Ukraine and [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]], reliant on permissive banking and extradition-averse jurisdictions.[1] See [[2025-poland-organ-trafficking-arrest|the arrest]].[1]

## Alleged Ukrainian and FBI connections

In the September 10, 2024 Badlands News Brief, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described Mogilevich as the crime lord of the Israeli mafia, characterized by the [[fbi|FBI]] as the "boss of all bosses" and nicknamed "The Brainy Don."[2] He alleged that [[oleksandr-turchynov|Oleksandr Turchynov]] — installed as Ukraine's interim president after the February 2014 Maidan events — was a henchman of Mogilevich, and that Turchynov, while working for Ukrainian intelligence, was subpoenaed to appear in Ukrainian court in 2010 accused of destroying files pertaining to Mogilevich.[2]

The same commentary asserted that [[william-sessions|William Sessions]], FBI Director during the administration of George H. W. Bush, served as Mogilevich's personal attorney until Sessions' death in June 2020 — a claim attributed to Craig Unger's book on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the Russian mafia.[2]
