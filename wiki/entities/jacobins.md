# Jacobins

The Jacobins were the political faction that executed the Terror of the [[french-revolution|French Revolution]].[1] In the Badlands Media account by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], there is no argument about the relationship between the [[bavarian-illuminati|Bavarian Illuminati]] and the Jacobins, whereas the Illuminati's relationship to the later Communist Party remains disputed among political scientists; some also believe the Order had a hand in manipulating events during the [[american-revolution|American Revolution]].[1]

[[john-robison|John Robison]]'s *Proofs of a Conspiracy* details the Illuminati's connection with the Jacobins and with the Terror.[1] After reading it, [[george-washington|George Washington]] wrote that he did not doubt "that the doctrines of the Illuminati and principles of Jacobinism had not spread in the United States."[1] Among the Jacobin-era figures Robison identified as an Illuminatus was [[count-mirabeau|Count Mirabeau]], who carried the Order code name Cornelius Scipio.[1]

Judaica scholarship, as summarized in the article, places the Jacobins within a continuum running from the followers of the false Messiah [[sabbatai-zevi|Shabbetai Tzvi]] through the [[frankism|Frankists]] to the Reform movement.[1] DeLarme notes that the Jacobins were to be the subject of a future article.[1]
