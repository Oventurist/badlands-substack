# Austin Private Wealth LLC

Austin Private Wealth LLC is an American investment firm with roughly $1 billion in assets under management that drew scrutiny in July 2024 after a regulatory filing appeared to show it had taken an enormous bearish position in [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]] stock the day before the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempted assassination of Donald Trump]].[1]

## The disputed filing

Writer and researcher Josh Walkos highlighted a filing indicating the firm had "shorted 12,000,000 shares of $DJT via a put option," with a filing date of 12 July 2024 — the day before the Butler, Pennsylvania shooting.[1] A put option is in effect a wager that the underlying asset's price will fall, an outcome that would have been near-certain had [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] been killed.[1] Walkos noted that the position was "by far the largest put placed" by a firm of that size.[1]

A Bloomberg terminal screenshot circulated showing that the contract subsequently disappeared from the record, and the company amended its filing records on 16 July 2024 with the put option removed.[1] Austin Private Wealth denied that it had placed the position, asserting that the original filing was a "clerical error."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the episode to illustrate his framing of the [[deep-state|Deep State]] as a "System of Systems," arguing that the awakening process involves more than exposing a criminal cabal — it extends to exposing "the core tenets of centralization, collectivism and globalism itself."[1] He suggested that a single inflection point such as the assassination attempt could function as a "Shatterpoint of multi-directional awakening" within the [[narrative-warfare|War of Stories]].[1]
