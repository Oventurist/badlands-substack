# Brandon Fellows

Brandon Fellows is a January 6, 2021 defendant cited as an example of prolonged pre-trial detention on misdemeanor charges.[1]

Badlands Media reported that Fellows, along with Eric Christie and Darrell Neely, was indefinitely detained with only misdemeanor charges pending, though the charges evolved over the years as superseding indictments were added — Christie was eventually convicted of a felony.[1] Their cases were offered as a direct rebuttal to Congressman [[ken-buck|Ken Buck]]'s September 2023 claim that the suggestion January 6 defendants had been detained without bond for misdemeanor offenses was "pure fiction."[1]

Fellows went to trial after having access to discovery for only nine hours, and represented himself pro se.[1] His case was presented alongside the practice of [[diesel-therapy|diesel therapy]] as evidence that January 6 defendants had in practice been denied meaningful access to counsel and to the evidence against them.[1]
