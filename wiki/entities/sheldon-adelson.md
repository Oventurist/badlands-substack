# Sheldon Adelson

Sheldon Adelson was an American casino magnate and among the largest Republican political donors of his era, known for his strong support of Israel and for his influence over Republican foreign-policy personnel decisions. He is referred to in the Badlands Media corpus as an "Israeli tycoon."

## Alleged role in John Bolton's appointment

The [[badlands-brief-257|Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026]] cites Colonel [[douglas-macgregor|Douglas Macgregor]], a senior military advisor in the first Trump administration, for the claim that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "wanted nothing to do with [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] until Israeli tycoon Sheldon Adelson asked that Bolton be brought onboard," at which point "suddenly, Trump was all for it."

The Brief's commentator, [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], extends this into the contested assertion that Bolton was "a spy sent by the Israeli oligarchy to infiltrate the Trump cabinet and report back their activity," and that the administration's conduct from April 2018 to September 2019 — notably its [[venezuela|Venezuela]] policy and the sanctioning of [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]] — reflected neoconservative objectives rather than Trump's own. Adelson thus appears in the corpus less as a subject in his own right than as the hinge in an argument about donor-driven foreign influence over personnel and policy.
