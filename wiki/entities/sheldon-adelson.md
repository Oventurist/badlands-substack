# Sheldon Adelson

Sheldon Adelson was an Israeli-American casino magnate and major Republican donor. In Badlands Media commentary he appears as the figure credited with placing [[john-bolton|John Bolton]] inside the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Role in the Bolton appointment (Badlands account)

According to [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], citing Colonel [[douglas-macgregor|Douglas Macgregor]] — who served as a senior military advisor during the first administration — President Trump wanted nothing to do with John Bolton until "Israeli tycoon Sheldon Adelson" asked that Bolton be brought onboard, after which Trump reversed course.[1]

The Brief's author extends this into the claim that Bolton "was a spy sent by the Israeli oligarchy to infiltrate the Trump cabinet and report back their activity," and that the administration's behavior between April 2018 and September 2019 reflected NeoCon foreign-policy objectives in Venezuela pursued, possibly unwittingly, by Trump.[1] These are commentary assertions rather than documented findings.[1]

## Restatement around the Bolton indictment

The claim resurfaced in October 2025, days before the [[indictment-of-john-bolton|indictment of John Bolton]], when Colonel [[douglas-macgregor|Douglas Macgregor]] told [[andrew-napolitano|Judge Napolitano]] that it was Adelson who convinced President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to bring Bolton into the administration.[2] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the interview as proof of "drafting," the idea that Trump used the inertia of the NeoCon and Zionist agenda to advance his own plan.[2]
