# Chen Yizi

Chen Yizi was head of China's Institute for Economic Reform and one of [[zhao-ziyang|Zhao Ziyang]]'s right-hand men, described in Matthew Ehret's Badlands account as a manager of [[george-soros|George Soros]]-affiliated Open Society think tanks inside China.[1]

In his 1990 book *Underwriting Democracy*, Soros wrote that he discussed the concept of a foundation with Chen Yizi and subsequently travelled to China with Liang Heng, who became his personal representative, setting up a foundation on the Hungarian model with Chen Yizi's institute as his partner.[1] Chen went on to co-run the Institute for Economic and Structural Reform (IESR), established after the 1986 "Fund for the Reform and Opening Up of China," which Zhao sponsored with a million-dollar grant from Soros.[1] The IESR interfaced closely with the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]], which set up two offices in China in 1988.[1]

Following the collapse of the 1989 regime-change effort, Zhao was arrested along with hundreds of Soros assets and Soros himself was banned from China.[1]
