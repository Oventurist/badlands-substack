# Mark Esper

Mark Esper is a former United States Secretary of Defense who served under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Account of proposed strikes on cartel drug labs

Esper has been cited as a primary source for reporting on Trump's thinking about military action against [[mexican-drug-cartels|Mexican drug cartels]].[1] According to Esper, while in office Trump considered it possible to bomb the cartels' drug labs and then potentially pin the strikes on another country.[1] The account was reprised in [[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]]'s May 2024 report claiming Trump intended to deploy covert American assassination squads into Mexico in a second term.[1]

## Memoir account of Mexico strike proposals

Reuters reporting in December 2023 cited former Defense Secretary Esper's memoir, in which he wrote that he twice raised objections to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s suggestion of missile strikes on drug cartels in Mexico, the United States' biggest trade partner.[2] The account was offered as an example of the pattern in which senior officials slow-walked, shelved or talked Trump out of proposals during his 2017–2021 term — the dynamic that reporting said a second administration would seek to eliminate through loyalist appointments.[2] Trump did not comment on the memoir claim.[2]
