# Mars, Incorporated

Mars is the candy maker that produces the M&M's brand.[1] In January 2023 the company became the focus of a culture-war controversy over its long-running "spokescandies" mascots.[1]

## Spokescandies controversy

Mars had redesigned the M&M's mascots as part of an effort to make the brand more inclusive, a campaign widely characterised in conservative media as "woke."[1] After sustained pushback from longtime fans over the politicization of the candy brand, the company announced on January 23, 2023, that it would replace the decades-old spokescandies team with the actress [[maya-rudolph|Maya Rudolph]].[1] The story was reported by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and carried by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the reversal as evidence that the immediacy and near-universality of the backlash showed that "the Marxists have pushed too far, and too fast," predicting further cultural boomerangs against such campaigns.[1]
