# Justin Gaethje

**Justin Gaethje** is an American mixed martial artist competing in the lightweight division of the [[ultimate-fighting-championship|Ultimate Fighting Championship]]. He is known for a high-volume, high-damage striking style that has made him one of the promotion's most popular action fighters.

## UFC Freedom 250

At [[freedom-250|UFC Freedom 250]], held on the South Lawn of the [[white-house|White House]] on June 14, 2026 to mark the nation's 250th anniversary and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 80th birthday, Gaethje defeated reigning lightweight champion [[ilia-topuria|Ilia Topuria]] to seize the title. Badlands commentary framed the bout as an American underdog toppling the world champion in "an amazing match that will live in infamy," and noted that after the fight Gaethje paid his respects to the President and the First Lady.

Writing as GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, the Badlands Brief described the night as "a memetic gold mine," treating Gaethje's victory before a sitting president on the White House lawn as an emblem of the resurgent national mood the Brief associated with the [[america-first|America First]] moment and the wider 250th-anniversary celebrations.
