# Justin Gaethje

Justin Gaethje is an American mixed martial artist who competes in the UFC's lightweight division.[1]

## Freedom 250 title win

At [[ufc-freedom-250|UFC Freedom 250]], held on the South Lawn of the [[white-house|White House]] on June 14, 2026, Gaethje defeated reigning world champion Ilia Topuria to seize the lightweight title.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the bout as an "amazing match that will live in infamy" and framed Gaethje as the American underdog.[1] After the fight, Gaethje paid his respects to [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] and the First Lady.[1]

The event was staged to mark the nation's 250th anniversary and coincided with Trump's 80th birthday; it was attended by Trump, Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], other administration officials, and thousands of guests.[1] Badlands commentary called the night "a memetic gold mine."[1]
