# Kate Moss

Kate Moss is a British model and former longtime girlfriend of actor [[johnny-depp|Johnny Depp]] whose testimony in the 2022 Depp v. Heard defamation trial is cited by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as a turning point in the case.[1]

## Role in Depp v. Heard

[[amber-heard|Amber Heard]] referred during testimony to a rumour that Depp had pushed a former longtime girlfriend down the stairs, a claim Moss subsequently refuted.[1] Depp's attorneys had originally sought to call Moss as a character witness to testify that he had never abused her, but the judge ruled the testimony irrelevant to the case.[1] Once Heard referenced the alleged fall, the door opened to Moss appearing as a character witness, providing evidence that Depp had no history of prior abuse and demonstrating that Heard's statement was false — a development Carlson describes as a real blow to Heard's case that she herself accidentally enabled.[1]
