# Sergei Lavrov

Sergei Lavrov is the Foreign Minister of the Russian Federation and the country's principal public voice on international affairs.[1]

In December 2024, following the collapse of the Syrian government and the departure of Bashar al-Assad to Moscow, Western outlets reported that Russia was abandoning its naval base at [[tartus|Tartus]] and the Hmeimim airbase in [[latakia|Latakia]].[1] Lavrov publicly clarified that Russia was not abandoning either installation, a statement Badlands commentary treated as an important corrective to Western reporting on the Syrian transition.[1] The bases remained the focus of intense activity in the following days, with Russian ships reportedly dispatched to Tartus to remove weapons from Cold War–era strategic caches, and Israeli airstrikes hitting the same facilities on December 15–16.[1]

## Statement on Israel as the top geopolitical issue (September 2024)

Amid warnings from [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] that long-range Western strikes inside Russia would amount to NATO going to war with Moscow, Russian Foreign Minister Lavrov said that it was Israel — not Ukraine — that constituted the number one geopolitical issue in the world at that moment.[2] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] said he agreed with the assessment and read it as reinforcing his contention that Ukraine and Israel are connected, both spiritually and legally.[2]
