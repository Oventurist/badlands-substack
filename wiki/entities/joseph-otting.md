# Joseph Otting

Joseph Otting is an American banking executive and former federal regulator who served as Comptroller of the Currency during the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Prior to his tenure at the [[office-of-the-comptroller-of-the-currency|Office of the Comptroller of the Currency]], Otting was chief executive officer and a board member of OneWest Bank from 2010 through 2015, when the institution was sold to CIT Group.[1] OneWest had been co-founded by [[steven-mnuchin|Steven Mnuchin]], who later served as Trump's Secretary of the Treasury.[1]

## New York Community Bancorp

In March 2024, Otting was named chief executive officer of [[new-york-community-bancorp|New York Community Bancorp]] as part of a rescue package in which the embattled regional lender raised $1 billion from a group of investors that included Mnuchin's [[liberty-strategic-capital|Liberty Strategic Capital]].[1] The appointment reunited Otting with his former OneWest colleague, with Mnuchin joining NYCB's expanded board while Otting took operational control of the bank.[1] Analysts framed the combination of new capital and a management and board overhaul as a credibility-restoring measure for investors worried about systemic risk in the regional banking sector.[1]

## Commentary in the Badlands corpus

Badlands Media writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the Mnuchin–Otting intervention at NYCB as significant signal rather than routine finance, arguing that former Trump administration officials moving to stabilize a distressed regional bank fit a broader pattern of alignment in the U.S. financial sector.[1] That commentary situated the NYCB rescue alongside the 2023 failures of [[silicon-valley-bank|Silicon Valley Bank]] and [[first-republic-bank|First Republic Bank]] as parts of a single unfolding sequence.[1]
