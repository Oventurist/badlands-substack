# Betsy Arakawa

Betsy Arakawa was a classical pianist and the wife of actor [[gene-hackman|Gene Hackman]].[1] She was found dead alongside her husband at the couple's $3.3 million Santa Fe, New Mexico mansion in February 2025.[1]

## Death

Arakawa, reported variously as 64 and 65 years old, was found decomposed with bloating on her face and mummification of her hands and feet, according to a search warrant obtained by DailyMail.com.[1] Pills were strewn in the couple's bathroom, and a prescription pill bottle and scattered pills were found in the room where Arakawa and one of the couple's dogs were discovered.[1] Sheriffs said Hackman showed the same signs of decomposition.[1]

The bodies were found by maintenance workers Roland Lowe Begay and Jesse Kesler, who told investigators they had not seen the couple in two weeks.[1] Hackman's daughter Elizabeth Jean Hackman told TMZ the family suspected carbon monoxide poisoning had killed her father, Arakawa, and one of their dogs, but authorities said they found no evidence of a toxic leak and described the deaths as suspicious enough to warrant a thorough investigation, in part because a door to the home was found "unsecured and opened."[1]
