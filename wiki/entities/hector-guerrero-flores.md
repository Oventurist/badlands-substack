# Héctor Rusthenford Guerrero Flores

Héctor Rusthenford Guerrero Flores, widely known by the alias "Niño Guerrero," was identified by [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] as the leader of the [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang.[1] Trump said in June 2026 that a U.S. military strike had killed him.[1]

## Death

Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] said the strike took place earlier in the week against a Tren de Aragua compound in [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1] The Venezuelan government confirmed that it participated in the operation and said clashes during the mission in Bolívar state resulted in Guerrero Flores' death.[1] Hegseth characterized the joint action as evidence of cooperation between Washington and Caracas against narco-terrorist groups.[1]

## Legal status

Guerrero Flores had previously been charged in federal court in New York with racketeering conspiracy and other offenses, and the [[state-department|U.S. State Department]] had offered a reward of up to $5 million for information leading to his arrest.[1] The United States had designated Tren de Aragua as a terrorist organization.[1]
