# Héctor Rusthenford Guerrero Flores

**Héctor Rusthenford Guerrero Flores**, widely known by the alias **"Niño Guerrero"**, was identified by the United States government as the leader of the Venezuelan transnational gang [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]]. He had been charged in federal court in New York with racketeering conspiracy and other offenses, and the [[state-department|U.S. State Department]] offered a reward of up to $5 million for information leading to his arrest.

## Death

On Friday, June 12, 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that a U.S. military strike had killed Guerrero Flores. Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] said the strike had taken place earlier in the week against a Tren de Aragua compound inside [[venezuela|Venezuela]]. Venezuela's government confirmed that it had participated in the operation, saying that clashes during the mission in Bolívar state resulted in Guerrero Flores' death.

Hegseth presented the strike as proof of working cooperation between Washington and Caracas against what he described as narco-terrorist groups — a framing that cut against the prior official U.S. line that Tren de Aragua operated as an instrument of President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]. Badlands commentary seized on that contradiction, arguing it supported Maduro's own claim that the gang had been assembled by the [[cia|CIA]] as a destabilization vehicle.

The operation was one element of a broader campaign against drug-trafficking networks in the hemisphere, which by mid-2026 had reportedly killed at least 207 people in strikes on suspected smuggling vessels since the previous September.
