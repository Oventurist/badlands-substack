# Letitia James

Letitia James is the Attorney General of New York State and a longtime legal adversary of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## Subpoenas quashed

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that US District Judge [[lorna-schofield|Lorna Schofield]] invalidated subpoenas issued to James by the office of [[john-sarcone|John Sarcone III]], whom she simultaneously disqualified as US Attorney for the Northern District of New York on the ground that he had been serving unlawfully. Schofield's opinion criticized the Executive branch for skirting congressional restraints and using that power "to subject political adversaries to criminal investigations."

## Virginia indictments

The Brief also notes that in Virginia, the dismissal of US Attorney [[lindsey-halligan|Lindsey Halligan]] led to the invalidation of indictments against both James and former FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]], and that subsequent attempts to re-indict James in Virginia have failed.

## FHFA referral over insurance representations (March 2026)

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that [[federal-housing-finance-agency|FHFA]] Director [[william-pulte|William Pulte]] again referred James to the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] for potential criminal prosecution, citing allegations that she may have provided false information on a homeowner's insurance application. Pulte sent letters to US Attorneys in Florida and Illinois requesting they "authenticate and investigate" the claims. According to the letters, an application submitted to a Florida-based insurer indicated James' Norfolk, Virginia property would remain unoccupied for five months each year — contradicted, per testimony referenced in the referral, by her niece Nakia Thompson having lived in the home for several years — while a separate filing cited in the Illinois referral shows James allegedly indicated the home would be occupied by a single adult with no children, despite public reporting that her niece and three children lived there. The referral cites analysis from attorney [[mike-davis|Mike Davis]].
