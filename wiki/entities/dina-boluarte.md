# Dina Boluarte

Dina Boluarte is the President of [[peru|Peru]] whose administration presided over the May 2024 health ministry decree classifying transgender and intersex people as "mentally ill."[1]

## Jewellery investigation

Boluarte was under investigation over her unexplained acquisition of expensive jewellery, including a diamond-encrusted Cartier bracelet valued at £40,000.[1] The inquiry dominated Peruvian politics to such a degree that health minister [[cesar-vasquez|César Vásquez]] devoted his public remarks to defending the president rather than addressing the backlash over the transgender classification decree.[1]
