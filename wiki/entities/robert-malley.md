# Robert Malley

Robert Malley is an American diplomat who served as the Biden administration's special envoy to [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## Commentary on a Trump–Iran agreement

In May 2026, amid reports of an emerging U.S.–Iran framework, Malley's remarks were highlighted in the Badlands Brief in connection with the [[2026-iran-deal-negotiations|2026 Iran deal negotiations]].[1] According to the summary of his comments, Malley argued that even if [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] successfully negotiated an agreement with Iran intended to bring peace to the wider region, [[israel|Israel]] would likely not comply with it.[1] He was described as predicting that Israel would continue to wage war on Lebanon and other Middle Eastern countries, justifying such action by claiming to have discovered new intelligence indicating planned aggression against Israel.[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

The Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited Malley approvingly, arguing that a figure's service in the Biden administration does not automatically render his analysis wrong, and observing that a number of career officials on both sides of the aisle had recently begun telling the truth for a variety of motives.[1] Malley's assessment was contrasted with the criticism of the prospective deal voiced by [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]], [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]] and [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]].[1]

## Classified documents investigation (2023–2024)

Malley was quietly placed on unpaid leave in June 2023, two months after his security clearance was suspended, amid a probe into his handling of classified material while leading the Biden administration's diplomatic relations with Tehran.[2] A congressional investigation subsequently uncovered evidence that the [[state-department|State Department]] official may have shared classified material with people outside the U.S. government, with Republican lawmakers indicating he disseminated sensitive documents to allies in order to "advance his diplomatic efforts" related to Iran.[2] GOP lawmakers learned that Malley transferred about a dozen documents, including some marked "sensitive" and "classified," to his personal devices.[2]

A congressional letter quoted in the reporting stated that "Mr. Malley's security clearance was suspended because he allegedly transferred classified documents to his personal email account and downloaded these documents to his personal cell phone," and added that "it is unclear to whom he intended to provide these documents, but it is believed that a hostile cyber actor was able to gain access to his email and/or phone and obtain the downloaded information."[2] According to the State Department, Malley remained on leave while the department cooperated with Congress in its investigation.[2]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] contrasted Malley's fate with that of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], who had been protected from prosecution when special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]] concluded that, despite unlawfully retaining classified documents from his time as vice president and senator, storing them insecurely and apparently accessing them frequently, he was unfit to stand trial.[2] She observed that the Malley scandal was becoming more visible at the same time that the [[cia|CIA]] faced increased scrutiny over the ethics of its clandestine activities abroad and at home.[2]
