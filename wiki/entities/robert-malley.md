# Robert Malley

Robert Malley is an American diplomat who served as the Biden administration's special envoy to [[iran|Iran]].[1]

## Commentary on a Trump–Iran agreement

In May 2026, amid reports of an emerging U.S.–Iran framework, Malley's remarks were highlighted in the Badlands Brief in connection with the [[2026-iran-deal-negotiations|2026 Iran deal negotiations]].[1] According to the summary of his comments, Malley argued that even if [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] successfully negotiated an agreement with Iran intended to bring peace to the wider region, [[israel|Israel]] would likely not comply with it.[1] He was described as predicting that Israel would continue to wage war on Lebanon and other Middle Eastern countries, justifying such action by claiming to have discovered new intelligence indicating planned aggression against Israel.[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

The Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited Malley approvingly, arguing that a figure's service in the Biden administration does not automatically render his analysis wrong, and observing that a number of career officials on both sides of the aisle had recently begun telling the truth for a variety of motives.[1] Malley's assessment was contrasted with the criticism of the prospective deal voiced by [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]], [[ted-cruz|Ted Cruz]] and [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]].[1]
