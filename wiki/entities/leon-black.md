# Leon Black

Leon Black is an American financier described by Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] as another billionaire connected to both [[les-wexner|Les Wexner]] and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Apollo Global Management

Black founded the investment firm [[apollo-global-management|Apollo Global Management]] in 1990.[1] Carlson treats that founding date as part of a recurring pattern, noting a "theme" of investment firms being launched in the early 1990s — the period in which the Soviet Union fell and China began its rise to economic superpower status.[1] In his framing, that timing reflects the British Cabal transitioning from control of the Soviet Union to control of the Chinese Communist Party.[1]

## Purchase of the Babylonian Talmud

In 2015 Black purchased the first printed complete edition of the [[babylonian-talmud|Babylonian Talmud]] for $9.3 million.[1] Carlson describes the Talmud as having been written some 500 years after Christ, says he has not read it himself, and reports having seen multiple critical accounts of its contents, including a claim that it permits a priest to marry a girl as young as three years old — a subject he encourages readers to research independently.[1]

## Framing within the "British Satanic Cabal" thesis

Anticipating the objection that the figures he names share Jewish ancestry, Carlson insists that the alleged British Satanic Cabal "is comprised of all people from all walks of life, races and religions," and that allegiance to the society, not race or religion, is the organising principle.[1] Within that thesis Black is presented as one of several financiers whose careers, wealth and social ties orbit the same network as [[george-soros|George Soros]] and [[jacob-rothschild|Jacob Rothschild]].[1]
