# Chloe Cole

Chloe Cole is an American detransitioner and activist who has become a prominent critic of pediatric gender medicine.[1]

## Litigation and social media claims

Cole has sued Kaiser Permanente for medical malpractice over the treatment she received as a minor.[1] She and other detransitioners have spoken publicly about the role platforms such as TikTok and Instagram played in steering them toward medical transition.[1]

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] invoked Cole's case as a counterpoint to the Buffalo social media lawsuit over [[payton-gendron|Payton Gendron]]'s radicalization, arguing that plaintiffs' lawyers and progressive institutions apply platform-liability theories selectively and are not similarly exercised about social media's documented influence on adolescent medical transition.[1] For Esler, the contrast illustrated a legal system in which [[lawfare]] bends to ideological will rather than to consistent principle.[1]

## Gabbard interview cited by Badlands

Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] cited [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s departure from the DNC and her subsequent interview with Chloe Cole as an apolitical event in the fight over [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]], alongside the work of [[gays-against-groomers|Gays Against Groomers]] and the activist [[billboard-chris|Billboard Chris]].[2] Esler placed Cole within the broader category of [[detransitioners|detransitioners]] whom he described as an exception to [[yuri-bezmenov|Yuri Bezmenov]]'s account of irreversible ideological subversion, having been awakened to authentic information by the trauma of their own medical transitions.[2]
