# Vladimir Putin

Vladimir Putin is the President of the Russian Federation. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears as one of the principal figures of the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar alliance]].

## Role in the Badlands corpus

The article [[a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation|A Gateway to a New World of Cooperation]] describes Putin standing together with [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] and [[narendra-modi|Narendra Modi]] at the [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit in Tianjin on August 31 and September 1, 2025, as representatives of the three founding nations behind the SCO. [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents the three leaders as jointly sponsoring the principles of the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]].

The article further argues that Russia under Putin has fielded next-generation hypersonic missile technologies, including underwater drones, which in the author's assessment demonstrate that a first-strike monopoly by [[nato|NATO]] is unattainable and that no attack on Russia could occur without devastating retaliation. Ehret characterises Putin, along with other multipolar leaders, as preferring cooperation with the trans-Atlantic community over war, while having been forced by events to prepare for the latter, and as unwilling to surrender Russian sovereignty to a Great Reset administered by technocratic institutions.

## Alaska summit (2025)

Badlands coverage catalogued in [[a-look-at-recent-features|"A Look At Recent Features"]] (August 15, 2025) previews an "extremely important" meeting between Putin and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] held in Alaska on August 15, 2025. [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s feature "Trump, Putin, Alaska and the Future of Multipolarity" frames the summit as consequential for the [[multipolar-alliance|multipolar order]] while stressing that its agenda and any prior arrangements were "shrouded in mystery."
