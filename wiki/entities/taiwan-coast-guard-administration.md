# Taiwan Coast Guard Administration

The Coast Guard Administration (CGA) is Taiwan's maritime law-enforcement agency, responsible for patrolling the island's waters and responding to incidents affecting offshore infrastructure.[1]

## Shunxin-39 cable incident

After [[chunghwa-telecom|Chunghwa Telecom]] reported on January 3, 2025 that a subsea communications cable near Keelung had been severed, the CGA deployed a patrol vessel to intercept the Hong Kong-owned freighter Shunxin-39 (also known as Xing Shun 39, IMO 8358427), which was near Yehliu at the time.[1]

Coast guard officials managed to establish radio contact with the ship's captain and conduct an external inspection, but were unable to board the vessel because of rough seas. International law further prohibited seizure of the ship for investigation, as too much time had elapsed since the incident. Tracking data from the vessel's automatic identification system (AIS) and from satellite sources indicated that the Shunxin-39 had dragged its anchor in the location where the cable was damaged.[1]

The CGA's suspicion of a Chinese-owned freighter was described as mirroring security challenges faced by Baltic nations over the preceding year, highlighting vulnerabilities in global underwater infrastructure.[1]
