# Robert De Niro

Robert De Niro is an American film actor who has become one of the entertainment industry's most vocal critics of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a stance that has repeatedly drawn direct responses from Trump himself.

## 2026 State of the Union remarks

De Niro is named in a [[truth-social|Truth Social]] post by Trump quoted in full in the February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief. Writing after his 2026 [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union]] address, Trump described "Trump Deranged Robert De Niro" as "another sick and demented person with, I believe, an extremely Low IQ, who has absolutely no idea what he is doing or saying — some of which is seriously CRIMINAL!" Trump said that watching De Niro "break down in tears" the previous night led him to conclude that the actor "may be even sicker than Crazy [[rosie-odonnell|Rosie O'Donnell]]." The remarks came in the same post in which Trump attacked Representatives [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] and [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]].
