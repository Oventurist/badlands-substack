# Nautilus Defense

Nautilus Defense is a United States defense technology firm identified by Badlands Media as one of the two primary contractors developing computerized clothing for the intelligence community's [[smart-epants|SMART ePANTS]] program.[1]

## SMART ePANTS contract

[[iarpa|IARPA]], in partnership with the Naval Information Warfare Center, Pacific, awarded research contracts to develop and manufacture the computerized clothing totaling over $22 million to Nautilus Defense and [[leidos|Leidos, Inc.]], according to an August 9, 2023 [[pentagon|Pentagon]] announcement.[1]

Reporting in [[childrens-health-defense|Children's Health Defense]]'s publication *The Defender* stated that [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]] and [[blackrock|BlackRock]] — investment giants that benefited from COVID-19 vaccine sales and hold ownership stakes in technology companies developing vaccine passports and digital wallets — are listed among Nautilus Defense's top investors.[1]
