# National Endowment for the Preservation of Liberty

The National Endowment for the Preservation of Liberty (NEPL) was eventually found to be the front organisation for [[oliver-north|Oliver North]], the man who helped run the [[iran-contra-affair|Iran-Contra]] arms funding deal under the nose of President [[ronald-reagan|Reagan]] and [[congress|Congress]].[1]

NEPL held one of the established accounts at [[palmer-national-bank|Palmer National Bank]], the Washington institution founded in 1983 by [[stefan-halper|Stefan Halper]] and [[harvey-mclean-jr|Harvey McLean Jr.]] with seed money from [[herman-k-beebe|Herman K. Beebe]].[1] It was through Palmer that money was channelled to North's Swiss bank account, and through Palmer that [[carl-channell|Carl Channell]], one of the few private citizens convicted in the scandal, obtained a $650,000 "loan" to purchase weapons for the Nicaraguan Contras.[1]

NEPL raised over $10 million to assist the Contras.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cites the arrangement in support of the claim, made by many writers, that the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] used the thrift [[deregulation|deregulation]] process to help pay for covert operations Congress would not or could not support.[1]
