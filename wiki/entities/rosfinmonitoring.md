# Rosfinmonitoring

Rosfinmonitoring is Russia's financial watchdog agency, responsible for maintaining and publishing the country's official list of "terrorists and extremists."[1]

## Role

In November 2025, Rosfinmonitoring published a notice adding former Prime Minister [[mikhail-kasyanov|Mikhail Kasyanov]] and economist [[sergey-guriev|Sergey Guriev]] to that list; the entry offered no explanation for the designation.[1] Inclusion on the list follows earlier Russian administrative measures against government critics, such as the "foreign agent" designation applied to Kasyanov in 2023.[1]
