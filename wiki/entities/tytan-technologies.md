# Tytan Technologies

Tytan Technologies is a German defense startup developing counter-drone systems, selected by the German army to help build a drone defense capability.[1]

## German army partnership

As reported by the Frankfurter Allgemeine Sonntagszeitung in October 2025, the German army agreed to collaborate with Tytan on a drone defense system intended to protect military sites and critical infrastructure from NATO Class II drones, including those used by Russia.[1] The company's technology had already been tested in [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] The project was described as strengthening the partnership with the Bundeswehr's innovation lab in Erding.[1]

## European context

The initiative came as the [[european-union|European Union]] lacked an independent air defense system, prompting discussion of a "drone wall" and a broader air-defense shield after a series of aerial incursions.[1] Drone sightings had recently disrupted operations at Munich Airport, halting flights, in incidents similar to those at Copenhagen and Oslo.[1]
