# Giovanni Vicente Mosquera Serrano

Giovanni Vicente Mosquera Serrano is a Venezuelan national identified by the United States government as the alleged leader of the transnational gang [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] (TdA).[1]

## Sanctions and indictment

On Tuesday, June 24, 2025, the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|United States Treasury Department]] announced that it had sanctioned Mosquera Serrano through its [[office-of-foreign-assets-control|Office of Foreign Assets Control]].[1] The same statement disclosed that he had also been indicted by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]; according to unsealed court documents, he faces charges related to drug trafficking and terrorism.[1] He was added to the [[fbi|FBI]]'s Ten Most Wanted list, with a $3 million reward offered for information leading to his arrest or conviction.[1]

Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] accused Tren de Aragua, under Mosquera Serrano's leadership, of "terrorizing our communities and facilitating the flow of illicit narcotics into our country."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that it would "be interesting to see if this goes anywhere," noting that authorities did not yet have Mosquera Serrano in custody and that, judging by history, apprehending such a figure could take years, if it happened at all.[1] She nonetheless described the action as encouraging evidence that the FBI and the federal government broadly were treating organized criminal networks and their presence inside the United States seriously — a change she attributed to a break with the prior administration, which she accused of admitting such actors and protecting their rackets.[1] She also framed the case as significant for residents of sanctuary jurisdictions, where she argued authorities were still giving "aid and comfort to foreign organized criminals."[1]
