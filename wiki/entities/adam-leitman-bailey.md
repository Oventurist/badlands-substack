# Adam Leitman Bailey

Adam Leitman Bailey is a high-profile New York real estate attorney whose law license was at one point suspended.[1] He became a figure of national interest in May 2024 after stating publicly that he had approached the judge presiding over [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[new-york-civil-fraud-trial|New York civil fraud case]] to offer unsolicited advice about a law at issue in the proceeding.[1]

## Contact with Judge Engoron

In an interview with NBC New York, Bailey said he spoke with Justice [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]] roughly three weeks before Engoron's February 2024 decision fining the former president $454 million for falsely inflating the value of his assets.[1] Engoron, through a court spokesman, denied any impropriety and said he was "wholly uninfluenced" by Bailey.[1] According to sources familiar with the matter, New York's judicial oversight body subsequently opened an investigation into the alleged interaction.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the episode as an example of a broader pattern in which investigations, probes and inquiries arising from the Trump prosecutions are increasingly directed not at Trump himself but at those presuming to judge him, arguing that the "Witch Hunt" was instead exposing corruption within the justice system.[1]
