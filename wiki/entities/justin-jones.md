# Justin Jones

Justin Jones is a Democratic member of the Tennessee House of Representatives who became nationally prominent in April 2023 after being expelled from and then returned to the chamber.[1]

On Monday, April 17, 2023 — a week after being voted back into office — Jones carried a child-size coffin into the Tennessee State Capitol as a symbolic action.[1] He carried the prop casket through the Capitol during a protest against gun violence led by the social activist [[william-barber-ii|Bishop William Barber II]], and attempted to bring it inside the legislature.[1] Video circulating online showed security stopping Barber and Jones; the sergeant-at-arms would not allow the casket onto the floor, telling them "House rules. No props," while Barber asked whether that was "a constitutional rule, or state rule."[1] Jones said he wanted to bring the casket to his desk.[1]

Earlier that month, Jones and fellow Democratic state Representatives [[justin-pearson|Justin Pearson]] and [[gloria-johnson|Gloria Johnson]] joined protesters in the state House chamber and chanted for gun-control legislation following the [[nashville-school-shooting|mass shooting in Nashville]] in which three children and three adults were killed.[1] The action prompted a GOP-led move to expel the three Democrats from the House.[1]

## Badlands commentary

In the *[[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]]*, [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that "everything about this guy screams 'asset' to me, whether knowingly or not," invoking [[cointelpro|COINTELPRO]] as a reference point and urging readers unfamiliar with the term to research it.[1] DeLarme asked whether anyone would care about Jones had the Tennessee GOP simply ignored him, and questioned whether Jones recognized the irony of a Democrat bringing a child-size coffin into the House chamber, imagining the reaction to "a truckload of fetus-sized coffins."[1] He further argued that Jones is silent about killings from gang violence and other criminal behavior in inner cities, and predicted the lawmaker would "skyrocket to the top of the Democratic Party."[1]
