# French Office for Immigration and Integration

The French Office for Immigration and Integration (OFII) is a public agency under the French Interior Ministry that oversees state-run support for legal migrants, asylum seekers and voluntary returns.[1]

In May 2025 the agency was proposed as the new provider of legal assistance inside France's migrant detention centres, under a Senate bill tabled by [[les-republicains|Les Républicains]].[1] The plan would transfer that responsibility from NGOs such as [[cimade|Cimade]] and [[france-terre-dasile|France terre d'asile]] to OFII, or potentially to a neutral judicial figure such as a magistrate.[1] Interior minister [[bruno-retailleau|Bruno Retailleau]] argued that placing the function within a public agency would ensure greater impartiality in a system he criticized as allowing activist organizations to act as "judge and party."[1]
