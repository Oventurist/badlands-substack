# Orin Snyder

Orin Snyder is a partner at the law firm [[gibson-dunn|Gibson Dunn]] who in October 2025 led the creation of a network of more than 40 large U.S. law firms offering free legal assistance to Jewish people facing antisemitism following the October 7, 2023, attack on Israel and the ensuing war in Gaza.[1]

Describing the initiative, Snyder said, "We are building an army of lawyers who will be a virtual law firm dealing with these problems."[1] The network included firms such as [[sullivan-and-cromwell|Sullivan & Cromwell]], [[cooley|Cooley]], and [[covington-and-burling|Covington & Burling]], alongside the [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]]; individuals can report incidents through an app, with reports reviewed by a steering committee able to connect complainants with thousands of top lawyers.[1]
