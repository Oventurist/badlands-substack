# Galina Karelova

**Galina Karelova** is a Russian politician who served as deputy speaker of Russia's Federal Council.[1]

## Statements on child trafficking through Ukraine

In June 2023, Karelova publicly stated that Russia had shut down [[child-trafficking|child-trafficking]] rings running through Kherson and Mariupol, claiming that Kiev was using Ukrainian orphanages to traffic children to Europe.[1] The claim, reported by [[tass|TASS]], was cited by Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] alongside allegations of [[organ-trafficking|organ trafficking]] made by Russia's UN ambassador [[vasily-nebenzya|Vasily Nebenzya]] and remarks by [[steven-seagal|Steven Seagal]] at a Kremlin award ceremony, as part of an argument that the war in Ukraine functions in part as a cover-up implicating officials in Washington given the influence of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] and the [[state-department|State Department]] over Kiev.[1]
