# Sim Hyon Sop

Sim Hyon Sop is a North Korean Foreign Trade Bank representative based in the United Arab Emirates who was indicted by United States authorities in April 2023 in connection with a cryptocurrency money-laundering scheme.[1]

## Allegations

The indictment alleged that Sim conspired with [[north-korea|North Korean]] IT workers who obtained illegal employment abroad and amassed millions of dollars in cryptocurrency for the benefit of the North Korean government, with Sim helping to launder the proceeds.[1] Funds worth millions of dollars were frozen in connection with that case.[1]

## 2025 forfeiture action

On June 5, 2025 the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] filed a civil forfeiture complaint seeking to confiscate $7.7 million worth of cryptocurrency seized in connection with the scheme.[1] Civil forfeiture is a form of civil lawsuit through which the government attempts to seize property it asserts is linked to criminal activity, and the DOJ presented the filing as part of its enforcement effort against North Korean revenue-generation networks operating through remote IT work.[1]
