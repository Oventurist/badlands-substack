# Mali

Mali is a Sahelian state named in the Badlands Brief as one of [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]]'s partners in a bloc of governments that have distanced themselves from Western security arrangements. The Brief reports that Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]]'s allegations against Western intelligence services were made against a backdrop of strained relations with Western countries following the expulsion of French troops and Burkina Faso's closer security cooperation with Russia, Mali and [[niger]]. The three Sahelian states are treated in the corpus as a coordinated grouping realigning away from [[france|France]] and the [[european-union|European Union]] amid ongoing conflict with [[isis|ISIS]]-aligned armed groups.
