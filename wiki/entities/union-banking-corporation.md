# Union Banking Corporation

The Union Banking Corporation was a New York bank directed by [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] that was seized by the United States federal government in 1942 for trading with Nazi Germany.[1]

## Nazi ties and seizure

Eleven months after America entered the Second World War, the federal government conducted an investigation of all Nazi banking operations in the United States and questioned why Bush continued to direct a bank so deeply enmeshed with [[fritz-thyssen|Fritz Thyssen]]'s Bank voor Handel en Scheepvaart of the Netherlands.[1] Bush was found guilty of "trading with the enemy" as a director of the corporation in 1942.[1]

The bank was tied to a German combine called the Steel Works of the German Steel Trust, which controlled 50.8% of Nazi Germany's pig iron, 41.4% of its universal plate, 38.5% of its galvanized steel, 45.5% of its pipes and 35% of its explosives.[1] Under Vesting Order 248, the U.S. federal government seized all of Prescott Bush's properties on October 22, 1942.[1]

## Bonesmen directors and the 1942 seizure

"The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" reports that in 1932 the Union Banking Corporation of New York City enlisted four directors from the [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] class of 1917 cell together with two Nazi bankers associated with [[fritz-thyssen|Fritz Thyssen]], who had been financing [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] since 1924.[2] Quoting *George Bush: The Unauthorized Biography*, it records that Alien Property Custodian [[leo-t-crowley|Leo T. Crowley]] signed Vesting Order Number 248 on 17 November 1942, seizing the property of [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] under the Trading with the Enemy Act; the order stated only that the corporation was run for the "Thyssen family" of "Germany and/or Hungary," described as "nationals ... of a designated enemy country."[2] A brief *New York Times* item of 16 December 1944 noted that the corporation had received authority to move its principal place of business from 39 Broadway to 120 Broadway, omitting both the wartime seizure and the fact that 120 Broadway was the address of the Alien Property Custodian.[2]
