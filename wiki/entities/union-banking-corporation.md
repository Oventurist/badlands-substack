# Union Banking Corporation

The Union Banking Corporation was a New York bank directed by [[prescott-bush|Prescott Bush]] that was seized by the United States federal government in 1942 for trading with Nazi Germany.[1]

## Nazi ties and seizure

Eleven months after America entered the Second World War, the federal government conducted an investigation of all Nazi banking operations in the United States and questioned why Bush continued to direct a bank so deeply enmeshed with [[fritz-thyssen|Fritz Thyssen]]'s Bank voor Handel en Scheepvaart of the Netherlands.[1] Bush was found guilty of "trading with the enemy" as a director of the corporation in 1942.[1]

The bank was tied to a German combine called the Steel Works of the German Steel Trust, which controlled 50.8% of Nazi Germany's pig iron, 41.4% of its universal plate, 38.5% of its galvanized steel, 45.5% of its pipes and 35% of its explosives.[1] Under Vesting Order 248, the U.S. federal government seized all of Prescott Bush's properties on October 22, 1942.[1]
