# Sophie D'Enbeau

Sophie D'Enbeau is a therapist and academic who appeared opposite [[matt-walsh|Matt Walsh]] on an episode of "Dr. Phil" devoted to gender identity, an exchange later replayed in promotional material for the documentary [[what-is-a-woman|"What Is a Woman?"]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] recounted the exchange in which D'Enbeau, confronted with the question "What is a woman?", replied condescendingly, "Part of me wants to ask why you care so much, because it's not really that big of a deal."[1] Walsh answered, "I care about the truth. So, basic truth matters," to which D'Enbeau flippantly replied, "Okay, fine" — a response the reviewer said carried an audible eye-roll.[1] The reviewer used the moment as an illustration of the dismissiveness with which gender theory advocates treat demands for definitional clarity.[1]
