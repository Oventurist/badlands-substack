# Austin Tucker Martin

Austin Tucker Martin was a 21-year-old man from North Carolina who, according to the February 24, 2026 [[badlands-brief-285|Badlands Brief]], breached the secure perimeter of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] resort in Palm Beach, Florida while armed, and was shot and killed by law enforcement.

## The incident

The breach occurred on Sunday, February 22, 2026. Martin was shot and killed by [[united-states-secret-service|US Secret Service]] agents and a Palm Beach County sheriff's deputy at approximately 1:38 AM ET near the property's north gate. He had been reported missing by his family days before. Reports cited by the Brief say he was carrying a shotgun and a fuel canister when confronted; ordered to drop the items, he dropped the fuel can but raised the shotgun into a shooting position, at which point officers fired.

The President and First Lady [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] were at the White House in Washington, DC at the time and were not at Mar-a-Lago.

## Reported motive and investigation

Martin had reportedly fixated on [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] stories, according to text messages obtained and reported by news outlets. Federal and local authorities, including the [[fbi|FBI]], were actively investigating the incident, and motive remained undetermined. The Brief notes that conservative outlets accused corporate media of downplaying the episode as an apparent assassination attempt, and quotes Trump's subsequent public remark: "I don't know how long I'll be around, got a lot of people gunning for me, don't I, hon?"
