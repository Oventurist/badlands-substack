# Austin Tucker Martin

Austin Tucker Martin was a 21-year-old man from North Carolina who was shot and killed by law enforcement after breaching the secure perimeter of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] resort in Palm Beach, Florida, in the early morning hours of Sunday, February 22, 2026.[1]

## The Mar-a-Lago incident

According to reporting summarized in the Badlands Brief, Martin was shot at approximately 1:38 AM ET near the property's north gate by agents of the [[us-secret-service|United States Secret Service]] and a Palm Beach County sheriff's deputy.[1] He had reportedly been reported missing by his family in the days preceding the incident.[1]

Martin was said to be carrying a shotgun and a fuel canister when confronted by officers. Law enforcement reportedly ordered him to drop both items; he dropped the fuel can but raised the shotgun into a shooting position, at which point officers fired and killed him.[1] President Trump and First Lady [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]] were at the White House in Washington, DC at the time and were not present at the resort.[1]

## Reported motive

Text messages obtained and reported by news outlets indicated that Martin had fixated on stories concerning [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1] Federal and local authorities, including the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]], opened an investigation into his motivation and the circumstances of the breach; as of the Brief's publication, motives remained under investigation.[1]

## Commentary and aftermath

Badlands contributors treated the incident as another apparent assassination attempt against Trump, noting that corporate media coverage was comparatively muted.[1] Trump himself referenced ongoing threats the following Monday, remarking, "I don't know how long I'll be around, got a lot of people gunning for me, don't I, hon?" — a comment addressed to the First Lady.[1] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] connected the remark to the security anxieties portrayed in the film *Melania*, including the decision to hold the 2025 inaugural parade indoors.[1]
