# Andre Dickens

**Andre Dickens** is the Mayor of Atlanta, Georgia, who appears in Badlands coverage as the on-stage interlocutor during a February 2026 book-tour appearance by California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] that generated national controversy.[1]

## The Atlanta book-tour appearance (February 2026)

At a book-tour stop in Atlanta, Newsom addressed Dickens and a reportedly majority-black audience with the line, "I'm like you … I'm no better than you. I'm a 960 SAT guy," referencing his low SAT score and his lifelong struggles with dyslexia, and adding that "you'll never see me read a speech, because I can't read a speech."[1] The clip circulated widely on social media, with critics arguing the framing implied a racial equivalence between low test scores and the assembled black audience.[1]

Newsom and his office rejected the criticism as manufactured outrage, noting he had discussed his dyslexia and test scores publicly for years, and the governor issued a profanity-filled reply to [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]] on X.[1] Republican pundits seized on the episode to highlight what they characterized as Democratic double standards on matters of race.[1]
