# Logical Brad

Logical Brad is a contributor within the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] collective of citizen journalists.[1]

## 2023 election guide

In November 2023, the Badlands News Brief promoted an Election Day guide produced by Logical Brad and hosted at logicalbrad.tv, describing it as an "Election Day Guide from One of Badlands' Own."[1] The item appeared alongside the Brief's coverage of election-integrity stories then circulating, including the federal corruption investigation into the campaign of New York City Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]] and disputes over results in Bridgeport, Connecticut and Springfield, Massachusetts.[1] The guide reflects the Badlands model of pairing news commentary with practical resources produced by members of its own contributor network.[1]
