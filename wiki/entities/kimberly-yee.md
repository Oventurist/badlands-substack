# Kimberly Yee

Kimberly Yee is the Republican who won the Arizona State Treasurer contest in the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]] with 55.7 percent of the vote, a result Badlands Media contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] used as an analytical baseline for her claims about the rest of the ballot.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

Yee's race recorded 1,389,215 votes statewide as of November 21, 2022 — more than any of the four top-of-ticket contests, exceeding the U.S. Senate race by 67,780 votes, the governor's race by 101,893, the Secretary of State race by 69,156, and the Attorney General race by 135,119.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) Gillespie argued that the treasurer's office was not a high-profile race — she reported never having seen an advertisement or a road sign for it — and asked what it would mean if manipulation simply had not extended that far down the ballot: "the proof is in the puddin'."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) That Yee's margin "wasn't even close" was, in her reading, evidence that the untouched race behaved differently from the contested ones above it.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
