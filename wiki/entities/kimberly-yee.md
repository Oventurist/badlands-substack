# Kimberly Yee

Kimberly Yee is an American Republican politician who serves as Treasurer of the State of Arizona. Her decisive re-election in November 2022 forms the statistical pivot of [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]]'s argument in [[an-sos-from-ground-zero|An SOS From Ground Zero]].

## The 2022 State Treasurer result

According to the unofficial statewide returns cited by Gillespie on November 21, 2022, the State Treasurer contest drew 1,389,215 votes — more than the U.S. Senate race (1,321,435), the gubernatorial race (1,287,322), the Secretary of State race (1,320,059), and the Attorney General race (1,254,096). Yee won with 55.7 percent of the vote in what Gillespie describes as a result that "wasn't even close."

Gillespie regards this pattern as anomalous, arguing that a low-profile down-ballot office for which she "never even saw an AD or a ROAD SIGN" should not have outdrawn the four top statewide contests by margins ranging from 67,780 to 135,119 votes. Her proposed explanation is that alleged manipulation of the top of the ticket did not extend as far down the ballot as Treasurer, leaving Yee's margin as an unaltered baseline against which the other races appear suppressed. The claim is interpretive and rests on undervote patterns rather than on direct evidence of tampering.
