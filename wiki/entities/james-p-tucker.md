# James P. Tucker

James P. Tucker was an American investigative reporter known for a decades-long effort to cover the annual meetings of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]], documented in *Jim Tucker's Bilderberg Diary: Reporter's 25-Year Battle to Shine the Light on the World Shadow Government*.[1]

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] quotes Tucker's assessment that "The Bilderberg agenda is much the same as that of its brother group, the Trilateral Commission. . . . The two groups have an interlocking leadership and a common vision of the world. David Rockefeller founded the Trilateral but shares power in the older Bilderberg group with Rothschilds of Britain and Europe."[1] The observation supports DeLarme's larger argument that the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]], the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the Bilderberg Group share overlapping membership and objectives, with the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]] and [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]] at the centre.[1]

Tucker's reporting appeared in the Washington tabloid The Spotlight, which DeLarme also cites for the claim that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] attended the 1997 Bilderberg meeting as the first American first lady to do so.[1]
