# Jon Luke Evans

**Jon Luke Evans** is a Jamaican national who was arrested in July 2025 after an attempt to unlawfully purchase a firearm was flagged by the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives.[1] At the time of the attempted purchase, Evans was employed as an armed police officer with the Old Orchard Beach Police Department in Old Orchard Beach, Maine.[1]

## Immigration status

According to [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], Evans entered the United States through Miami, Florida, on September 24, 2023, and failed to board his return flight to Jamaica on October 1, 2023, thereby overstaying his visa.[1] Despite no longer holding legal status, he was subsequently hired as a police officer; the circumstances under which the Maine department hired him remained unclear.[1] The case was reported by The National Pulse and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025.[1]
