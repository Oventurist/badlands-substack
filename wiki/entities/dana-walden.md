# Dana Walden

Dana Walden is a Co-Chairman of Disney Entertainment at [[disney|The Walt Disney Company]], serving alongside [[alan-bergman|Alan Bergman]].[1]

## 2023 Disney layoffs

In April 2023, Walden co-signed the staff memo announcing the second and largest wave of Disney's restructuring layoffs, under which the company expected to reach 4,000 of a targeted 7,000 job cuts within a single week, with a final round scheduled before the start of summer.[1] The memo characterized the cuts as "hard decisions" made "with considerable thought" and pledged that the process would be conducted "with respect and compassion".[1] Disney staff had for weeks referred to the coming round as "the big one" or a "bloodbath", following an initial wave that began March 27.[1]

Badlands coverage placed the layoffs within its broader argument that Hollywood and the entertainment industry function as an extension of the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]], and that the system's control over the American zeitgeist was beginning to fracture.[1]
