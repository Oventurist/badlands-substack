# Yasin Akgul

Yasin Akgul is a Turkish photojournalist with the AFP news agency who was arrested in Istanbul in March 2025 after covering mass anti-government protests.[1]

## Career and arrest

Akgul, 35 at the time, had more than a decade of experience as a photojournalist, with assignments in war-torn Syria and Islamic State–controlled Iraq.[1] Working in Turkey, he says he has been beaten by police on several occasions — including on World Peace Day — and detained many times.[1]

Police arrived at his home in Istanbul in the early morning of 23 March 2025, hours after he returned from covering the protests, while his children were still in bed.[1] Officers told him they had an arrest order but gave no details; his son was awake and Akgul said he could not explain what was happening because he did not understand it himself.[1] Being arrested at home was a first for him.[1]

Akgul was one of seven journalists detained in coordinated dawn raids.[1] All had been covering demonstrations sparked by the arrest of Istanbul's opposition mayor, [[ekrem-imamoglu|Ekrem Imamoglu]], the chief political rival of President [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]].[1] The arrests were reported by the BBC under the framing "Don't Speak, Don't Film," and fuelled fears for the state of democracy in Turkey.[1]
