# Lara Trump

Lara Trump is an American political figure and the daughter-in-law of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], married to his second son [[eric-trump|Eric Trump]].[1]

## Election as RNC co-chair

In March 2024, Lara Trump was elected co-chair of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] at the committee's spring training session in Houston, Texas.[1] The same event saw [[michael-whatley|Michael Whatley]], another Trump-endorsed candidate, elected RNC chair, in what Newsweek described as a further tightening of the former president's grip over the party.[1]

Following her election, she said: "Everything I do at this committee will have one focus: re-electing Donald Trump, flipping the Senate, and expanding the House this November."[1]

## Backlash

Her elevation sparked fury among a subset of the GOP activist base on social media, with a number of Republican activists vowing to suspend their support for the party.[1] The leadership change came shortly after Trump's string of Super Tuesday primary wins and the withdrawal of [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]], his last serious remaining rival.[1] Newsweek reported that it had contacted the RNC for comment without a response as of publication.[1]

## 2024 election integrity operation

As co-chair of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]], Lara Trump announced in April 2024 that the party was "hiring hundreds of election integrity staff across the map — more than ever before," and would recruit thousands more observers to protect the vote in 2024.[2] She said state campaign officials were tasked with recruiting, training, and shifting poll watchers and poll workers "day in and day out."[2] The announcement accompanied a joint campaign–RNC pledge to deploy 100,000 attorneys and volunteers to battleground states.[2]
