# Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society

The Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society is a research center at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] focused on the governance of digital technology.[1]

## Role in Badlands Media's account

*The Ivy Web* names the Berkman Klein Center, together with the [[harvard-kennedy-school|Kennedy School of Government]] and the [[harvard-th-chan-school-of-public-health|School of Public Health]], as one of the Harvard units whose laboratories generated much of the infrastructure used during COVID-19 for lockdown modeling, behavioral nudging, and contact tracing.[1] The article frames these centers as elements of a "staging ground for soft surveillance, biometric tracking, and the normalization of population control mechanisms" whose origins predate the pandemic in preparedness simulations and strategic research contracts.[1]
