# Nathan Roberts

Nathan Roberts is an Ohio small business owner and the founder of Freedom Truck Dispatch. In August 2023 he became the named plaintiff in a lawsuit brought by [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]] and others against the [[progressive-corporation|Progressive Corporation]], challenging a grant program that offered $25,000 to black-owned businesses to purchase vehicles.[1] Roberts, who is white, was barred from applying under the program's eligibility rules, and the suit characterized the exclusion as "patently unlawful" racial discrimination.[1] AFL attorney [[gene-hamilton|Gene Hamilton]] described the case as part of a wider effort to force large corporations to stop injecting racial considerations into their business and employment practices.[1]
