# Renee Nicole Good

Renee Nicole Good was a 37-year-old woman fatally shot on January 7, 2026 in south Minneapolis during a federal immigration enforcement operation involving [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|US Immigration and Customs Enforcement]].

## The shooting and its aftermath

Her death triggered protests that escalated across Minneapolis on January 8, with demonstrators gathering near the Bishop Henry Whipple Federal Building, which houses ICE offices. Authorities deployed crowd-control measures including pepper spray and closed highways in part; no arrests were announced by local officials. Solidarity demonstrations were reported in other US cities. The [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] assumed control of the shooting investigation and, according to [[minnesota|Minnesota]] investigators, blocked state access to information.

Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]] named her explicitly in [[executive-order-26-01|Executive Order 26-01]], which recites that "federal immigration agents shot and killed Renee Nicole Good in south Minneapolis" following "the federal government's surge of masked immigration officials in Minnesota."

## Treatment in the corpus

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] frames the case as an attempted "[[george-floyd|George Floyd]] 2.0 moment." [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] describes the emerging public portrait — "the new George Floyd is a lesbian poet" — and argues the story, while compelling, functions as a distraction from the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali fraud]] hearings. [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] similarly calls the episode "distraction theater" erupting in the same battlespace as the fraud revelations.
