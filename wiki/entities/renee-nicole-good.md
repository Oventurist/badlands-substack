# Renee Nicole Good

Renee Nicole Good was a Minnesota woman fatally shot during a confrontation with an [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] agent in Minneapolis in January 2026. Her death became the proximate trigger for a nationwide wave of anti-ICE demonstrations.[1]

## Incident

According to the account reported in the Badlands Brief, Good struck the ICE agent with her car before she was shot.[1] ICE officials said the agent acted in self-defense, and video of the incident was described as confirming that sequence of events.[1]

## Aftermath

Within days, anti-ICE protests took place across the United States under the banner "ICE Out For Good," organized by [[indivisible|Indivisible]] and related groups, with organizers claiming more than 1,000 events nationwide.[1] Thousands rallied in Minneapolis, where Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] participated, and large demonstrations were held in Washington, D.C. and Philadelphia.[1] Arrests followed protests in Boca Raton, Los Angeles, Austin, and Houston.[1]

Badlands commentary contested the martyr framing, likening the episode to the death of George Floyd — presented by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] as a death occurring during the commission of a crime and subsequently converted into a national protest narrative.[1]


## The shooting

An [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] agent fatally shot Good, a 37-year-old Minneapolis woman, on January 7 during a federal enforcement operation in south Minneapolis, igniting widespread protest and debate over the use of deadly force by federal agents.[2] Federal officials, including [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]], defended the agent's actions as self-defense, while local leaders pointed to video they said contradicted claims that Good posed a threat.[2] Hennepin County Attorney [[mary-moriarty|Mary Moriarty]] asked the public to share recordings and evidence with her office.[2] The case is treated at length as the [[minneapolis-ice-shooting-of-renee-nicole-good|Minneapolis ICE shooting of Renee Nicole Good]].[2]

## Identification and context

The [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]] City Council identified the victim of the January 7, 2026 shooting as Renee Nicole Good, describing her in a statement as "a resident of our city who was out caring for her neighbors this morning" whose "life was taken today at the hands of the federal government."[3] The shooting took place on a residential street in south Minneapolis, less than a mile from the site where [[george-floyd|George Floyd]] was killed by police in 2020, while [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] agents were conducting targeted immigration enforcement operations.[3] The [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] said the agent acted in self-defense after Good allegedly attempted to strike officers with a vehicle, and the incident was placed under investigation with circulating video footage reviewed by investigators.[3]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described Good as "a reportedly lesbian poet from Colorado" who was "experiencing Minneapolis," and argued that saturation coverage of her death conveniently displaced reporting on the [[minnesota-somali-fraud-hearings|House Oversight Committee hearing]] on Minnesota pandemic fraud held the same day.[3] She characterised the resulting coverage as "outrage porn" and said the episode "feels very George Floyd all over again," adding that the Colorado connection reflected that state's role as a "national test kitchen" for tactics that then spread elsewhere.[3]
