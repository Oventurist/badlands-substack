# Sheila Jackson Lee

Sheila Jackson Lee was a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas who died on July 19, 2024, six days after the assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at Butler, Pennsylvania.

## In the Badlands corpus

Jackson Lee appears in the closing "teaser and a timeline" of the Badlands Media essay [[a-week-to-remember|A Week to Remember]], where [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] arranges four events in sequence: the July 13 attempt on Trump's life; Jackson Lee's death six days later; [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s withdrawal from the presidential race two days after that; and, the next day, the testimony of Secret Service director [[kimberly-cheatle|Kimberly Cheatle]] before the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|Committee on Oversight and Accountability]].

The article emphasises that during that hearing Cheatle mentioned Jackson Lee's passing and noted that the congresswoman "was engaged in oversight of the [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]]." Cheatle resigned the following day.

On the basis of this sequence the author poses three questions to readers: "Was Sheila Jackson Lee involved in the planning of the assassination attempt on Trump? If so, is she now dead because of the attempt, or because the attempt was unsuccessful? Did Biden step down fearing he would be next?" The essay adds a fourth, rhetorical question about whether Jackson Lee would "be buried wearing her infamous pedophile ring." These are presented as speculation rather than as documented claims, and the article offers no evidence connecting Jackson Lee to the shooting.
