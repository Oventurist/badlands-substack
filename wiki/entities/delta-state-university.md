# Delta State University

Delta State University is a university in Cleveland, Mississippi, which in September 2025 became the site of a widely publicized student death.[1]

## Death of Trey Reed (September 2025)

A Black student, later identified as [[trey-reed|Trey Reed]], was found hanging from a tree on the Delta State campus early on Monday, 15 September 2025.[1] The 21-year-old's death immediately ignited strong emotions in a Southern state with a history of racist violence.[1]

On Thursday, 18 September 2025, the police department in Cleveland announced that an autopsy by the office of Mississippi's state medical examiner had concluded the student died by suicide.[1] The findings were consistent with a preliminary examination by the Bolivar County coroner, which found no evidence of foul play.[1] Before the results were released, the family and their lawyers had called for an independent autopsy and asked police to share security camera recordings and other video gathered as evidence, with civil rights attorney [[ben-crump|Ben Crump]] stating he would lead an independent investigation.[1]

Badlands contributor Ashe in America noted that Reed and Cory Zukatis, a white homeless man, were found hanging in different Mississippi locations on the same Monday, that early online claims of a double lynching were inaccurate, and that authorities had reported no signs of foul play in either case.[1]
