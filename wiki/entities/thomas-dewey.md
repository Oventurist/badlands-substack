# Thomas Dewey

Thomas Dewey was Governor of New York who commuted the sentence of [[lucky-luciano]] on the condition that Luciano be deported to Italy.[1] The Badlands Media account states Dewey acted under pressure from Secretary of the Navy [[james-forrestal]], [[allen-dulles]] and officials of the [[office-of-naval-intelligence]], and notes that Luciano was a US citizen who was not legally subject to deportation — yet the deportation happened anyway.[1]
