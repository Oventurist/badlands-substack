# Vienna Pharaon

**Vienna Pharaon** is identified as a therapist in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay quotes her writing that "when you don't betray yourself, your relationships either rise to meet you or fall away with grace."[1] The author endorses the claim in the section "Resonance, Leadership & the Living Grid," arguing that what no longer fits departs "not in rupture but in reverence," and that the coherent person need not push anyone out of their life because "your frequency does the sorting."[1]

The citation sits alongside the essay's appeal to [[sue-johnson|Sue Johnson]] on secure attachment as biology rather than luxury, and forms part of its account of the social consequences of [[coherence|coherence]].[1]
