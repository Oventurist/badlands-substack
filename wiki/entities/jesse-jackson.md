# Jesse Jackson

The Rev. Jesse Jackson was an American civil rights leader who died on Tuesday, February 17, 2026, at the age of 84.[1] He had been battling Progressive Supranuclear Palsy, a degenerative neurological disorder, for more than a decade before his death.[1]

## Trump tribute

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] offered warm public praise for Jackson following the announcement of his death, publishing a tribute on [[truth-social|Truth Social]] that described the civil rights icon as "a good man" possessed of "lots of personality, grit, and street smarts."[1] Trump characterized Jackson as gregarious, said he "truly loved people," and called him "a force of nature like few others before him."[1]

Trump said he had known Jackson well long before entering electoral politics and had assisted him along the way.[1] Among the specific forms of help Trump cited were the provision of free office space for Jackson's Rainbow Coalition in the Trump Building at 40 Wall Street, cooperation on criminal justice reform, the securing of long-term funding for Historically Black Colleges and Universities, and support for Opportunity Zones.[1] Trump also noted Jackson's uncredited role in helping to elect Barack Obama, despite the personal differences between Jackson and Obama, and extended his deepest condolences to the Jackson family.[1]

The tribute was carried as a bonus item in the Badlands Brief of February 18, 2026, presented as an illustration of a relationship between Trump and a leading Black political figure that predated and cut across conventional partisan lines.[1]
