# Cato Institute

The Cato Institute is an American libertarian think tank whose monetary-policy writing features in Badlands Media's account of the movement to restore a [[gold-standard|gold standard]] in the United States.[1]

## Leadership

[[john-allison|John Allison]], previously head of the North Carolina-based BB&T bank for 19 years, served as head of the Cato Institute from 2012 to 2015.[1] Allison later met with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the 2016 presidential transition and was mentioned as a possible Secretary of the Treasury.[1]

## Cato Journal and monetary policy

The institute publishes the *Cato Journal*, in which Allison argued in 2014 for abolishing the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], contending that Fed-driven changes to the money supply, distorted interest rates and financial overregulation are the primary source of economic volatility.[1] The same essay called for "a private, free-banking system based on a market standard such as gold," criticizing the government money monopoly established in 1913.[1]

## Significance

For Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], Cato's association with Allison places the think tank within a broader network of sound-money advocates whose ideas he presents as the intellectual foundation of a coming return to gold-backed currency.[1]
