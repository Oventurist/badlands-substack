# Colin Jost

Colin Jost is an American comedian and television writer best known as a "Weekend Update" anchor on NBC's sketch comedy program [[saturday-night-live|Saturday Night Live]].[1]

## Epstein island joke (December 2025)

In a December 2025 broadcast, Jost delivered a monologue segment reacting to newly released photographs of [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s private island, which had been made public by House Democrats earlier that week.[1] Jost told the audience that "the craziest detail of these photos was that there was one room with just a dentist's chair in it," adding that the chair "seems very weird, but keep in mind: a lot of [Epstein's] girlfriends still had braces."[1]

The joke drew audible groans from the studio audience, a reaction Jost leaned into by asking, "You think the victims should have crooked teeth?"[1] He also joked that the photographs made the island look "not as nice as I remember."[1] The bit was reported by The Daily Beast and circulated widely as an example of the Epstein saga breaking fully into mainstream late-night comedy.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the mainstreaming of Epstein material — including Jost's joke — as evidence that the establishment was "stampeding under their own momentum straight into the exposure they spent their careers trying to prevent," arguing that in every plausible reading of the Epstein operation, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "has nothing to fear."[1]
