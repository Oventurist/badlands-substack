# Abu Shabab Clan

The Abu Shabab clan is an armed gang or militia operating in the Gaza Strip that positions itself in opposition to [[hamas|Hamas]]'s rule of the enclave.[1] In June 2025 it became the focus of international reporting after Israeli defense sources confirmed that Israel had been supplying the group with weapons as part of an effort to build up rival power centers inside Gaza.[1]

## Israeli arms transfers

The arrangement was first made public by former defense minister [[avigdor-liberman|Avigdor Liberman]], who said Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had unilaterally approved the transfer of arms to the clan.[1] Netanyahu subsequently confirmed the report, defending the decision on the grounds that it had saved the lives of Israeli soldiers.[1]

## Aid diversion allegations

Israeli journalist Barak Ravid reported that the "crime families" receiving Israeli weapons were the same groups that had been stealing humanitarian aid supplies intended for Gaza's civilian population — thefts that had publicly been attributed to Hamas and used as justification for escalating the war.[1] Opposition figure [[yair-lapid|Yair Lapid]] further raised the possibility of connections between the armed clans and ISIS.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the episode as confirmation of a longstanding thesis that Israel has funded and armed Islamic militant actors for political purposes.[1]
