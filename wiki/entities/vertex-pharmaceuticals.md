# Vertex Pharmaceuticals

**Vertex Pharmaceuticals** is an American biotechnology company whose non-opioid pain drug [[journavx|Journavx]] was approved by the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] on 30 January 2025 for short-term pain following surgery or injury.[1] The approval was described as the first new pharmaceutical approach to treating pain in more than twenty years, providing an alternative to opioid medications such as Vicodin and OxyContin as well as to over-the-counter drugs like ibuprofen and acetaminophen.[1] Coverage of the decision noted that the drug's modest effectiveness and the length of its development process illustrated the difficulty of developing new pain therapies.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the Vertex approval, alongside the FDA's phase-out of [[red-no-3|Red No. 3 dye]], as a sign of an abrupt change in the agency's posture toward opioids and food additives.[1]
