# Rick Slocum

**Rick Slocum** is described in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the current chief investment officer of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]].[1]

The article states that Slocum also steers capital for the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] and holds membership at [[chatham-house|Chatham House]], which it calls the United Kingdom's counterpart to the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]].[1] In the section on intelligence integration he is grouped with Eric Doppstadt of the [[ford-foundation|Ford Foundation]] as personnel through whom [[harvard-university|Harvard]]'s "deeper ties" allegedly run, managing funds the author links to intelligence-adjacent activities within the [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
