# Aidar Battalion

The Aidar Battalion is a Ukrainian volunteer paramilitary formation described in reporting by [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]] as a neo-fascist outfit, and one that has been accused of war crimes by Amnesty International.[1]

## August 2023 controversy

The unit re-entered Western commentary in August 2023 when Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] alluded to a meeting with the Aidar Battalion in a Twitter post published days after his filmed "open conversation" with the 3rd Separate Assault Brigade — the latest rebrand of the [[azov-battalion|Azov Battalion]] — on the outskirts of Bakhmut.[1] In the same post Zelensky praised the 3rd Separate Assault Brigade as "excellent fighters" who had stopped the enemy advancing towards Kostiantynivka and pushed the occupiers back up to eight kilometres.[1]

The pairing of the two units in a single presidential message was cited as evidence that Ukraine's political leadership was engaging openly with formations of explicitly far-right provenance, notwithstanding Western media's tendency to dismiss such evidence by pointing to Zelensky's Jewish heritage.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that in 2022 it had been considered poor form to accuse Ukrainian war fighters of neo-Nazism, and questioned only whether the "neo" qualifier belonged at all.[1] He argued that the reappearance of Nazi symbolism among forces backed by the establishment and by [[nato|NATO]] to the tune of billions in taxpayer funding constituted one of the largest available red pills of the ongoing [[information-war|information war]].[1]
