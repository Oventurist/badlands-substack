# World Central Kitchen

World Central Kitchen is a humanitarian aid organization whose personnel were
killed in an Israeli strike in Gaza in early April 2024, an incident that
became a diplomatic inflection point in [[israel|Israel]]'s relations with both
Gulf states and its supporters in the United States Congress.[1]

## The Gaza strike and its fallout

Seven World Central Kitchen humanitarian workers were killed in Gaza in a
strike attributed to the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli Defense Force]].[1] In
the wake of the deaths, the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]]
announced a suspension of diplomatic coordination with Israel and a halt to all
coordination on humanitarian aid, bringing ties between the two states to what
observers described as near breaking point only a few years after
normalization under the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]].[1]

In Washington, the killings prompted progressive members of
[[congress|Congress]] to call for a halt to US weapons transfers to Israel, a
letter that former House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] signed.[1] The
lawmakers wrote to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and Secretary of State
[[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] that "in light of the recent strike against
aid workers and the ever-worsening humanitarian crisis, we believe it is
unjustifiable to approve these weapons transfers."[1] The letter, released
after the IDF announced the initial findings of its own investigation into the
attack, also called for an independent probe.[1]
