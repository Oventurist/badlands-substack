# Just Security

Just Security is an online forum for analysis and commentary on national security law, democracy, foreign policy, and human rights. It publishes work by lawyers, academics, and former government officials, and is frequently cited in mainstream coverage of federal prosecutions and intelligence matters.[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

In the June 16, 2023 Badlands News Brief, contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] singled out Just Security in his analysis of the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s use of the [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]] against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], writing that the platform "champions itself as a forum based on security, democracy, foreign policy, and rights" while being partially funded by the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundation]].[1] He connected the outlet to [[andrew-weissmann|Andrew Weissmann]] and, through him, to Special Counsel [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]]'s Russia investigation, arguing that a "familiar overtone of Russiagate" ran through the commentary shaping public understanding of the Trump documents case.[1]
