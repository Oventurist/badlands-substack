# Paul Volcker

Paul Volcker was head of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] when [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] came into office in 1981.[1]

Reagan inherited an economy struggling with high inflation, and Volcker was battling it by raising interest rates — a situation Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], writing in 2023, compares to conditions in his own time.[1] One industry in particular struggled badly under those high rates: the savings and loan sector, which was then restricted to residential lending funded by depositors.[1] That squeeze became the stated justification for the banking [[deregulation|deregulation]] pursued by the [[task-force-on-regulatory-relief|Task Force on Regulatory Relief]], allowing thrifts to make riskier commercial investments — the change Lange identifies as the catalyst of the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan crisis]].[1]
