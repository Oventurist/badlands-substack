# Endeavor

Endeavor is the Hollywood talent and entertainment conglomerate led by [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]], and the parent of TKO Group, whose On Location subsidiary sells corporate hospitality packages for major sporting events.[1]

## 2028 Los Angeles Olympics commentary

In March 2025, Endeavor and TKO executive Mark Shapiro predicted at a Morgan Stanley media conference that the 2028 Los Angeles Summer Olympics would be "out-of-control good" despite the setback of that year's wildfires, and that credit for their success would be taken by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Shapiro said the "L.A. and Back" narrative would drive strong interest in On Location's hospitality packages, and told moderator Ben Swinburne that organizers had "had time to rebuild, and it's going to be a coming-out party."[1] He added that "President Trump is going to walk out of office with the trophy that is the L.A. Olympics," saying he was "a true believer that this will be his Olympics as much as anything else."[1]

## Badlands commentary on the Emanuel family

Badlands contributor Chris Paul noted Endeavor's ownership by Ari Emanuel, whose brother [[rahm-emanuel|Rahm Emanuel]] served as Barack Obama's White House Chief of Staff and later as Ambassador to Japan under what the commentary called the fake and illegitimate Biden administration.[1] A third brother, Ezekiel Emanuel, is a physician who has argued that people should die at 75 for the good of everyone, and the commentary noted that the brothers' father belonged to the Irgun.[1]

## Acquisition of WWE

In April 2023, Endeavor Group agreed to combine its [[ufc|UFC]] property with [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]] in the [[wwe-ufc-merger|WWE–UFC merger]], creating a new publicly traded combat sports and entertainment company in which Endeavor held a 51 percent stake against 49 percent for WWE shareholders.[2] The deal valued WWE at $9.3 billion and UFC at $12.1 billion, and was expected to close in the second half of 2023.[2] Endeavor chief executive [[ari-emanuel|Ari Emanuel]] was to lead both Endeavor and the new company, calling it "UFC 2.0," while Endeavor president and chief operating officer [[mark-shapiro|Mark Shapiro]] took the same roles at the new entity.[2] Endeavor shares fell more than 5 percent on the day of the announcement.[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary characterized Endeavor as a Hollywood acquirer whose earlier purchase of UFC had produced stagnation and homogenization of the product.[2]
