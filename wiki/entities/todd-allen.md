# Todd Allen

Todd Allen is a marketing executive at [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch InBev]] who was appointed to lead marketing for [[bud-light|Bud Light]] in April 2023.[1]

## Appointment to Bud Light

Allen had recently served as global marketing vice president for [[budweiser|Budweiser]] when Ad Age reported on April 21, 2023 that Bud Light's vice president of marketing, [[alissa-heinerscheid|Alissa Heinerscheid]], had taken a leave of absence and that the company confirmed Allen would replace her.[1] The change came three weeks after the brand's promotional partnership with transgender TikTok influencer [[dylan-mulvaney|Dylan Mulvaney]] triggered a sustained consumer backlash and boycott.[1]

Alongside the appointment, a company spokesperson said top-level executives would closely monitor Bud Light's marketing activities to avoid a repeat, framing the aim as maintaining "focus on the things we do best: brewing great beer for all consumers, while always making a positive impact in our communities and on our country."[1] The reshuffle was reported by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and excerpted in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of April 25, 2023.[1]
