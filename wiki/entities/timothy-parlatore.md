# Timothy Parlatore

Timothy Parlatore is an American defense attorney who has represented figures in the legal orbit of former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. In August 2023 he appeared as counsel for former White House Chief of Staff [[mark-meadows|Mark Meadows]] in connection with the Georgia racketeering case brought against Trump and seventeen co-defendants.[1]

## Meadows removal filing

In a Tuesday filing reported on August 16, 2023, Parlatore argued that Meadows was entitled to remove the Georgia state prosecution to federal court. His stated rationale was that "the charges against him plausibly give rise to a federal defense based on his role at all relevant times as the White House Chief of Staff to the President of the United States."[1] The filing accompanied a broader argument by Meadows's legal team that "the conduct giving rise to the charges in the indictment all occurred during his tenure and as part of his service as Chief of Staff."[1]

Badlands Media commentary by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the removal effort as a potentially significant signal about the ultimate trajectory of the Georgia RICO case, noting that legal analysts widely expected the removal bid to succeed.[1]

## Defense of Pete Hegseth

In November 2024, Parlatore represented [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]], President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nominee for secretary of defense, against a [[hegseth-sexual-assault-allegation|sexual assault allegation]] dating to 2017.[2] In an exclusive interview with Breitbart News, he said the matter had been "completely investigated" at the time, that Hegseth is "completely and totally innocent," and that the accuser "was the aggressor."[2] Parlatore recounted that after Hegseth spoke at a California Federation of Republican Women conference in Monterey he drank heavily at a bar with organizers and the accuser, whom Parlatore described as sober, escorted him to his room; he said police reviewing video considered charging her instead.[2] He added that the case went nowhere because Hegseth was not going to publicly claim he had been raped.[2]
