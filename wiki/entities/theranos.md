# Theranos

Theranos was a blood-testing startup founded by [[elizabeth-holmes|Elizabeth Holmes]] that was once heralded as a breakthrough in laboratory science before its core technology proved faulty.[1] The company's collapse led to Holmes' criminal conviction for misleading investors and an 11-year federal prison sentence.[1]

## Legacy

The Theranos episode remained a reference point in coverage of new medical-testing ventures. In May 2025, Holmes' partner [[billy-evans|Billy Evans]] was reported to have raised millions of dollars for [[haemanthus|Haemanthus]], an artificial-intelligence startup aimed at medical testing and other settings, with Holmes advising from prison.[1]
