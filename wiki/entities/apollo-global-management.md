# Apollo Global Management

**Apollo Global Management** is an American alternative asset management firm whose chief economist, [[torsten-slok|Torsten Sløk]], is a widely cited commentator on U.S. trade and macroeconomic policy.[1]

## Role in the 2025 tariff debate

In late June 2025 Sløk used Apollo's research platform to publish "Has Trump Outsmarted Everyone On Tariffs?", a post arguing that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] might be executing a deliberate strategy of holding tariffs at moderate levels — roughly 30% on China and 10% elsewhere — while giving trading partners twelve months to lower nontariff barriers.[1] The analysis, which projected some $400 billion in annual tariff revenue, was picked up widely as an admission from a prominent tariff skeptic that the administration's approach might be working.[1]

## Founding

The firm was started in 1990 by financier [[leon-black|Leon Black]].[2] Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats that date as part of a wider pattern of investment firms launched in the early 1990s, coinciding with the fall of the Soviet Union and China's emergence as an economic superpower — a period in which, he argues, the British Cabal transitioned from controlling the Soviet Union to controlling the Chinese Communist Party.[2] Black is described in the same essay as a billionaire connected to both [[les-wexner|Les Wexner]] and [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[2]
