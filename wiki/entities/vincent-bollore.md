# Vincent Bolloré

**Vincent Bolloré** is a French industrialist named in a report on illicit Congolese mineral supply chains published by the law firm [[amsterdam-and-partners|Amsterdam & Partners]] on behalf of the government of the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]].[1]

Bolloré was arrested in 2018 and charged with fraud related to African mining operations.[1] Badlands commentary describes him as a protégé of the French branch of the Rothschild banking dynasty.[1]

## Context

The Amsterdam & Partners report accused major technology companies — including [[apple|Apple]], Intel, Sony, and Lockheed Martin — of smuggling rare earth minerals out of the Congo into neighbouring countries, principally [[rwanda|Rwanda]], so they could be laundered into the global supply chain as "clean."[1] It further accused those firms of using UN-connected organizations such as the [[international-tin-supply-chain-initiative|ITSCI]] and the [[responsible-minerals-initiative|Responsible Minerals Initiative]] to legitimize the trade with fraudulent certifications.[1]
