# Donald Tusk

Donald Tusk is the Prime Minister of Poland and a former President of the European Council.

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] cites Tusk in its survey of deteriorating transatlantic relations. Among the incidents listed as evidence that European officials increasingly view the United States as a rival, the Brief reports that the U.S. ambassador to Poland criticised a Polish lawmaker over President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s Nobel nomination, prompting a public rebuke from Tusk. The episode is grouped with the removal of flags honouring fallen Danish soldiers by the U.S. embassy in [[denmark|Denmark]], the booing of Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] over U.S. agents providing Olympics security in Italy, and Trump's dismissal of [[nato|NATO]] allies' sacrifices in Afghanistan.
