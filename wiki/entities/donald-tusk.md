# Donald Tusk

Donald Tusk is the Prime Minister of Poland.[1]

## Friction with the U.S. embassy

In the run-up to February 2026, Tusk issued a rebuke after the U.S. ambassador to Poland criticized a Polish lawmaker over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Nobel nomination.[1] The incident was cited alongside other episodes — the U.S. embassy in Denmark removing flags honoring fallen Danish soldiers, U.S. agents providing Olympics security in Italy where Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] was booed, and Trump's dismissal of [[nato|NATO]] allies' Afghanistan sacrifices — as evidence of a deepening transatlantic rift in which European officials increasingly view the United States as a rival.[1]
