# Aspen Digital

Aspen Digital is the technology and cyber policy arm of the [[aspen-institute|Aspen Institute]].[1] According to the Washington Free Beacon reporting cited by Badlands Media, Aspen Digital convened a working group in September 2020 composed of social media executives, journalists, and academics to develop a coverage strategy for a hypothetical "hack and dump" of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s emails.[1] That tabletop exercise is treated at length in the page on the [[aspen-institute-hack-and-dump-exercise|Aspen Institute hack-and-dump exercise]].[1]

[[garrett-graff|Garrett Graff]], Aspen Digital's Director of Cyber Initiatives, published an opinion piece in [[wired|Wired]] one week before the [[new-york-post|New York Post]]'s Hunter Biden laptop story, laying out how the press should handle an "October surprise."[1]
