# Shannon Bream

Shannon Bream is a Fox News host who anchors "Fox News Sunday."[1]

On Sunday, February 18, 2024, Bream interviewed George Washington University law professor and Fox legal analyst [[jonathan-turley|Jonathan Turley]] about the evidentiary hearing involving Fulton County District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] and special prosecutor [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]].[1] She pressed Turley on the conflicting statements the two had given in testimony and asked whether he believed the judge had "seen enough" to disqualify Willis from the Georgia election case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Turley responded that the judge had "heard enough," and raised the possibility of a bar referral in addition to removal.[1]
