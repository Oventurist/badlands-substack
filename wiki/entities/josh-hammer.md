# Josh Hammer

**Josh Hammer** is a conservative pundit who became a subject of speculation in October 2025 in connection with the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]].[1]

## Role in the Kirk controversy

Hammer was described as having been part of an "intervention team" that met with Kirk in the Hamptons to address Kirk's public alignment with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] and [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] and his shifting position on Israel.[1] Circulating on the internet in the wake of Kirk's death was an image showing Hammer quote-posting a thirteen-year-old post by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] about the need for public executions in order to send a message; the following day, in the framing of the Badlands commentary, "Charlie Kirk was publicly executed."[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] explicitly declined to accuse Hammer of anything, but argued that because Hammer had personally met with Kirk a month earlier in an allegedly hostile setting to address Kirk's position on Israel — and because Kirk sent messages to [[turning-point-usa|Turning Point USA]] donors on September 8 saying he was leaving the pro-Israel cause — there were "very real concerns and questions that need to be asked by law enforcement to anybody and everybody who expressed hostility toward Charlie Kirk over his evolving position on Israel."[1]
