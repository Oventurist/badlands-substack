# Public Opinion Strategies

Public Opinion Strategies is an American Republican polling firm that conducts the GOP side of the bipartisan [[nbc-news|NBC News]] national survey.[1]

## Role in the November 2023 NBC News poll

Republican pollster [[bill-mcinturff|Bill McInturff]] of Public Opinion Strategies conducted the [[november-2023-nbc-news-poll|November 2023 NBC News poll]] together with Democratic pollster [[jeff-horwitt|Jeff Horwitt]] of [[hart-research-associates|Hart Research Associates]].[1] The survey recorded President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s approval at 40% and showed him trailing former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a hypothetical general-election matchup for the first time, though within the margin of error.[1]

Commenting on the results, McInturff said he could not recall another time when foreign affairs not involving U.S. troops had transformed the American political landscape.[1]
