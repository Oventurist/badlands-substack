# Anduril

Anduril is an American military contractor co-founded by [[palmer-luckey|Palmer Luckey]].[1] The company is part of a cluster of defence-oriented technology firms — alongside [[palantir|Palantir]] — associated with Silicon Valley investors including [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] and [[joe-lonsdale|Joe Lonsdale]].[1]

In July 2025, Anduril's founder was reported to be leading the launch of [[erebor|Erebor]], a proposed United States bank for start-ups and cryptocurrency businesses backed in part by Thiel's [[founders-fund|Founders Fund]].[1]

## Munitions acceleration participation

Anduril Industries was among the startups drawn to the June 2025 [[pentagon|Pentagon]] roundtable of missile makers led by Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] and General [[dan-caine|Dan Caine]], which launched the production drive later reported as the [[munitions-acceleration-council|Munitions Acceleration Council]].[2] The effort presses contractors to double or quadruple output of twelve weapons the Pentagon wants on hand for a potential conflict with [[china|China]].[2]
