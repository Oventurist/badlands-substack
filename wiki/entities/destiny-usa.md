# Destiny USA

Destiny USA is a shopping and entertainment complex in Syracuse, New York, and the largest mall in New York State.[1]

## 2025 umbrella panic

On a Thursday in late August 2025, shoppers at Destiny USA were thrown into panic after a man carrying an umbrella was mistaken for an active shooter.[1] The scare began around 3:20 p.m., when 911 calls flooded in reporting a man carrying what appeared to be an AR-15-style rifle inside the complex; a second caller said the suspect had slung the "weapon" over his shoulder and appeared to cock it.[1]

Officers swarmed the area within minutes, backed by drones and a sheriff's helicopter on standby.[1] By 4:04 p.m., a detective monitoring security cameras spotted the man on an escalator inside the mall and followed him into the food court near the 110 Grill.[1]

Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked the episode, asking why no one simply walked over to inspect the object and joking that the panic was a "bad look" for New Yorkers, who she suggested were supposed to be tougher than residents of Denver or San Francisco.[1] The incident was framed in the brief as an illustration of a broader "culture of panic and dread."[1]
