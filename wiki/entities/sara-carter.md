# Sara Carter

Sara Carter is an American investigative journalist known for reporting on border security, immigration, and national security matters.[1]

## Reporting on Chinese migration at the southern border

In May 2023, Carter reported a 393 percent increase in Chinese "migrants" crossing the United States southern border, raising in both her article and her headline the risk of increased Chinese espionage activity.[1] Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the story "barely received any attention" and was subsequently "memory-holed," even as the administration continued to advance a humanitarian-crisis framing intended to relax longstanding immigration laws.[1]

A month later, testimony before the [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Committee on Homeland Security]] indicated that [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] had intercepted several high-value targets from China as well as nationals of Russia and other countries hostile to the United States — developments that Badlands commentary treated as vindication of Carter's earlier warning.[1]
