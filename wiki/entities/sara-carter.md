# Sara Carter

Sara Carter is an American investigative journalist known for reporting on border security, immigration, and national security matters.[1]

## Reporting on Chinese migration at the southern border

In May 2023, Carter reported a 393 percent increase in Chinese "migrants" crossing the United States southern border, raising in both her article and her headline the risk of increased Chinese espionage activity.[1] Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the story "barely received any attention" and was subsequently "memory-holed," even as the administration continued to advance a humanitarian-crisis framing intended to relax longstanding immigration laws.[1]

A month later, testimony before the [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Committee on Homeland Security]] indicated that [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] had intercepted several high-value targets from China as well as nationals of Russia and other countries hostile to the United States — developments that Badlands commentary treated as vindication of Carter's earlier warning.[1]

## "There are a lot of sealed indictments"

[[qanon|Q]] drop #2626 of December 19, 2018 quoted Carter saying "There are a lot of sealed indictments," alongside [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]]'s statement that "It's all going to come out, U1, Dossier, CF, etc.," and asked why she, Hannity and [[john-solomon|John Solomon]] were "on stage."[2] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] reads the drop as identifying three journalists with genuine sources who were used to deliver accurate information about the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] and the [[sealed-indictments|sealed indictments]] arising from it, while the rest of the media dismissed the scandal as a conspiracy theory.[2]

## Reporting on the declassified texts

"[[the-biggest-boomerang-in-history|The Biggest Boomerang In History]]" credits Carter with breaking the story on the declassified text messages between [[peter-strzok|Peter Strzok]] and [[lisa-page|Lisa Page]], and describes her as one of three select journalists repeatedly fed information in order to help change the narrative.[3] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] links this to Q drop 1282 of April 27, 2018, which asked what redacted texts had been released the previous day and what the purpose of a laser pointer is, and to the [[declas|DECLAS]] cadence of "slow drip > flood."[3] In his account the texts shifted media coverage from Trump–Russia collusion to bias within the [[fbi|FBI]].[3] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
