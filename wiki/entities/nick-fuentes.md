# Nick Fuentes

Nick Fuentes is an American online political commentator and the figurehead of the movement known as the Groypers. He appears in the Badlands Media corpus principally in connection with the intra-right dispute over [[israel|Israel]] and Zionism.[1]

## The Huckabee appointment and the Tucker Carlson interview

The Badlands Brief recounts that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s early appointment of [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] as US Ambassador to Israel "triggered Nick Fuentes and the Groypers into the stratosphere," given Huckabee's lifelong position that Palestine does not exist.[1] When [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] subsequently chose to interview Fuentes, he received significant public backlash, including calls for his censorship — a controversy Trump addressed by defending the principle that people should have access to all information and make up their own minds.[1]

## Interpretation in the corpus

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argues that Trump deliberately constructed this dialectic — appointing Huckabee, then defending Carlson's right to platform Fuentes — in order to take control of a national conversation about Israel and Zionism that establishment actors would have preferred to suppress.[1] In this reading Fuentes functions less as an independent actor than as one pole of a managed argument.[1]
