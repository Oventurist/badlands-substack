# Michael Isikoff

Michael Isikoff is an American investigative journalist best known in the Badlands corpus for his role in the earliest publication of material derived from the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]].[1]

## Role in the 2016 timeline

Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] placed Isikoff in a sequence of events following meetings between Obama White House Counsel [[w-neil-eggleston|W. Neil Eggleston]] and [[fbi|FBI]] officials in the autumn of 2016.[1] On September 21, 2016 — one day after the first of those meetings — [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]], at the direction of [[fusion-gps|Fusion GPS]], briefed the New York Times, Washington Post, New Yorker, Yahoo! News and CNN.[1] Two days later, Isikoff, then at Yahoo! News, published the first known article based on Steele's dossier.[1]

That article is treated in the Badlands narrative as an early instance of the "circular reporting" pattern central to the [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] critique, in which dossier material seeded to the press was later cited as independent corroboration.[1]
