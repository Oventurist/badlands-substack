# Mohibul Hasan Chowdhury

Mohibul Hasan Chowdhury is a former Bangladeshi cabinet minister who served under Prime Minister [[sheikh-hasina|Sheikh Hasina]] and acted as her government's chief negotiator during the 2024 crisis that ended her rule.[1]

## Allegations of a foreign-backed overthrow

In an exclusive interview with RT's Runjun Sharma, broadcast in November 2025, Chowdhury said the 2024 riots that led to Hasina's ouster in the [[2024-bangladesh-government-overthrow|Bangladesh government overthrow]] were backed by [[usaid|USAID]] and the family of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[1]

He said that "certain actions of some NGOs, especially from the United States — naming a few, I mean USAID, for example, or the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]]" involved campaigns run against his government since 2018.[1] Chowdhury characterized the unrest as a "carefully planned" operation bankrolled by Western interests rather than a spontaneous youth revolt, alleged a long-standing "nexus between the Clinton family, and the interim [[muhammad-yunus|Yunus]] regime," and said that funding of clandestine NGOs had gone on quietly by parties "hell-bent on changing the government in Bangladesh."[1] He also pressed the question of where millions of dollars in USAID funds had gone.[1]
