# Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem

The Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem is the Roman Catholic ecclesiastical jurisdiction for the Holy Land, led as of 2026 by Cardinal [[pierbattista-pizzaballa|Pierbattista Pizzaballa]].[1] It holds custodial and liturgical responsibilities at Christian holy sites in Jerusalem, most prominently [[church-of-the-holy-sepulchre|the Church of the Holy Sepulchre]].[1]

## Palm Sunday 2026 dispute

After [[israeli-police|Israeli police]] barred senior Catholic leaders from the Holy Sepulchre on March 29, 2026, the Patriarchate publicly criticized the decision, calling it "a manifestly unreasonable and grossly disproportionate measure" and saying it impeded freedom of worship.[1] Catholic authorities said the episode marked the first time in centuries that top church leaders had been unable to conduct [[palm-sunday|Palm Sunday]] services at the site.[1] The dispute drew criticism from U.S. and European officials on religious-freedom grounds, and Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] subsequently instructed authorities to permit access.[1]

Badlands commentary situated the incident within a longer pattern of Israeli state pressure on Church institutions, including a first-ever property tax on Church properties and the freezing of Church bank accounts when the Church refused to pay.[1]

## Statement on the attack in Taybeh (July 2025)

In July 2025 the Patriarchs and Heads of Churches in Jerusalem, issuing their statement through the Patriarchate, expressed profound concern and unwavering condemnation over a violent assault on the West Bank Christian town of Taybeh, in which vehicles were set ablaze and hateful graffiti sprayed.[2] They described the incident as part of an alarming pattern of settler violence against West Bank communities, noting that days earlier settlers had forcibly entered Taybeh, herded livestock into the town center, and roamed the streets masked, some armed and others on horseback, while fire reached the walls of the town's ancient church.[2] The statement warned of a prevailing climate of impunity that undermines the rule of law and jeopardizes peaceful coexistence, and demanded that the Israeli government hold those responsible accountable without delay, ensure consistent protection for vulnerable communities, and uphold its obligations under international law.[2] See [[taybeh-settler-attacks|Taybeh settler attacks]].[2]
