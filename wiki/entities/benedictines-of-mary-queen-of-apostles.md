# Benedictines of Mary, Queen of Apostles

The Benedictines of Mary, Queen of Apostles are a Catholic religious community of women based in Gower, Missouri, about 45 minutes north of Kansas City.[1]

## The Lancaster exhumation

The community drew international attention in May 2023 when the body of [[wilhelmina-lancaster|Sister Wilhelmina Lancaster]], who died on May 29, 2019, at age 95, was exhumed in April of that year and found to have barely decomposed.[1] Nuns close to the matter said the exhumation had been prompted by construction, and that Lancaster had not been embalmed before burial in a wooden coffin set in very moist clay.[1] The remains were scheduled to be placed in a glass shrine inside the church, and the local diocese opened an inquiry into a possible case of [[incorruptibility|incorruptibility]].[1]

Thousands of visitors flocked to the monastery over a single weekend, with reports of 10,000–15,000 people per day arriving from around the world; visitors were reminded not to touch the remains while paying their respects.[1]
