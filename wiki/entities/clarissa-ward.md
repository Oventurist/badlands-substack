# Clarissa Ward

Clarissa Ward is a [[cnn|CNN]] correspondent whose December 2024 report from Syria — purporting to show a CNN crew rescuing a prisoner of the [[bashar-al-assad|Assad]] regime from a Damascus dungeon — was subsequently retracted in substance by the network.[1]

## The Damascus prison segment

Ward's melodramatic on-camera report showed a man who identified himself as Adel Ghurbai of Homs being freed from a hidden cell.[1] After days of mounting skepticism online and at ZeroHedge, CNN acknowledged that the "rescued" man was in fact a regime intelligence officer, with reports that he had himself committed crimes against civilians.[1] The Syrian fact-checking site Verify-Sy identified him as Salama Mohammad Salama, known as "Abu Hamza," a first lieutenant in Syrian Air Force Intelligence described as an infamous and cruel figure who coerced Syrians into becoming informants.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued the segment was a failed psyop rather than a mere case of a reporter being "duped," pointing to the subject's manicured nails, trimmed beard, clean clothing and general hygiene as inconsistent with months of imprisonment and torture.[1] The commentary questioned how Ward obtained access while embedded with fighters in Syria, suggested the now-empty prison used as the setting may have been one of the country's emptied ISIS detention facilities, and placed the episode within a wider effort to destroy Assad's credibility so that an emerging ISIS/Al Qaeda-linked government could be presented as legitimate.[1] It linked the segment to parallel coverage alleging Assad had airlifted $250 million out of Syria during his evacuation.[1]
