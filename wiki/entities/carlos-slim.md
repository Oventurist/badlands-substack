# Carlos Slim

Carlos Slim is the Mexican billionaire who was, at the time of the 2008 financial crisis, the world's richest man.[1] He was a client of [[lehman-brothers|Lehman Brothers]] and had already lost a great deal of money as the bank's stock price collapsed.[1]

[[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]]'s portion of the Lehman rescue effort, "Project Verde," consisted of persuading Slim to make a potentially life-saving investment in the firm. It failed; on 5 July 2008 Bush reported that Slim "did not express interest in jv (joint venture) or stock purchase."[1] Slim declined to comment beyond a spokesman's statement that he and Bush had been "friends for 20 years, but they have never done business together." The article notes that both men were close to the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]].[1]
