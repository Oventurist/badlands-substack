# Carlos Slim

Carlos Slim is a Mexican billionaire who in 2008 was the world's richest man.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) He was a Lehman client who had already lost heavily as the bank's stock price collapsed.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

During [[lehman-brothers|Lehman Brothers]]' 2008 crisis, [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]]'s assignment under "[[project-verde|Project Verde]]" was to persuade Slim to make a potentially life-saving investment in the firm; on July 5, 2008 Bush reported the meeting had been unsuccessful because Slim "did not express interest in jv (joint venture) or stock purchase."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Slim declined to comment beyond a spokesman's statement that the two had been "friends for 20 years, but they have never done business together."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The Badlands Media account counters that even without direct business, both men were good friends of the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]], described as huge drug traffickers in Mexico.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
