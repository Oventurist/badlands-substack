# Carlos Slim

Carlos Slim Helú (born 1940) is a Mexican business magnate, investor, and philanthropist who was the richest person in the world from 2010 to 2013 according to Forbes. In the Badlands Media article [[a-family-affair]], he is identified as a longtime friend of [[jeb-bush]] and as a potential savior of [[lehman-brothers]] who declined to inject capital into the failing bank in 2008.

## Connection to Jeb Bush

According to [[fox-business]], Jeb Bush and Carlos Slim had been friends for approximately 20 years as of 2008, though they had never done business together directly. In 2008, as Lehman Brothers teetered on the brink of bankruptcy, Bush led "Project Verde," an effort to convince Slim to make a potentially life-saving investment in the firm. On July 5, 2008, Bush reported that the meeting had been unsuccessful because Slim "did not express interest in joint venture or stock purchase."

## Connection to the Salinas family

The article notes that Carlos Slim and the salinas family were close associates, and that Slim had been a client of Lehman Brothers who had already lost money as the bank's stock collapsed. The source frames the Slim-Salinas-Bush connection as evidence of a transnational elite network linking Mexican business, Mexican political corruption, and American financial power.

## Business empire

Carlos Slim built his fortune primarily through telecommunications, with his holding company Grupo Carso controlling América Móvil, the largest mobile-phone operator in Latin America. He also holds significant stakes in various Mexican industrial and financial companies. As of 2024, he remains one of the wealthiest individuals in the world.
