# Joel Greenberg

Joel Greenberg is a former Florida tax collector whose criminal prosecution became the origin point of the sexual-misconduct allegations against Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]].[1]

## Criminal conduct

According to the account compiled in the Badlands News Brief, Greenberg was engaged in "just about every crime imaginable," including charging more than $70,000 in payments to prostitutes on his government credit card.[1] One of the women involved was a 17-year-old minor who had represented herself as an adult in her advertisements; after Greenberg learned she was a minor, he used his access to government machines to manufacture a fake driver's license for her — as well as several false identification documents for himself — and continued to see her.[1] Greenberg later claimed that the girl had described other encounters to him, among them an alleged encounter with Matt Gaetz.[1]

## The Beute letters and arrest

In 2019, rumors circulated that a federal investigation was underway against Greenberg over a cryptocurrency scam he was running, which prompted local music teacher Brian Beute to challenge him in the Republican primary.[1] Feeling threatened, Greenberg sought not merely to defeat Beute but to destroy him, and among his actions he wrote several letters to the school where Beute worked alleging that Beute was molesting students.[1] The letters were turned over to the sheriff's office, which promptly matched Greenberg's fingerprints and DNA to them.[1]

Greenberg was arrested in June 2020 by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and charged with stalking.[1] His phone was confiscated, and federal investigators concluded that the tax collector was a hardcore criminal implicated in embezzlement, drugs, prostitution, pedophilia, identity theft, wire fraud and cryptocurrency market manipulation, among other offenses.[1] In August 2020 the DOJ charged Greenberg with sex trafficking of a minor, identity theft and production of false identification documents, among other counts.[1]

## Cooperation and the Gaetz investigation

Sometime during the summer of 2020, Greenberg's lawyer approached the DOJ under Attorney General [[bill-barr|Bill Barr]] and offered evidence that a sitting congressman — Gaetz — had slept with a minor, in exchange for clemency; a secret investigation was allegedly opened as a result.[1] The Badlands account stresses that the allegations against Gaetz originate with a hardcore criminal who did appear to know Gaetz, with photographs of the two together posted on social media, but that it remains unclear what corroborating evidence he possessed.[1]
