# Natural News

Natural News is an alternative health and news website whose reporting is periodically aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news briefs.[1]

## Coverage of lab-grown meat

In April 2023, Natural News reported on the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]]'s approval of cultivated chicken meat under the headline "Lab-made chicken meat grown from CANCER CELLS receives FDA approval – are you ready to eat TUMOR nuggets?"[1] The article described the FDA as a "corrupt federal regulatory agency," noted that two products — from [[upside-foods|Upside Foods]] and [[good-meat|GOOD Meat]] — had been green-lit, and explained that Upside Foods harvests cells from live animals and chicken tissue to grow meat in stainless-steel bioreactors.[1] It cited the agency's statement that it had "no further questions" about safety and Commissioner [[robert-califf|Robert M. Califf]]'s declaration that the product was safe to eat.[1]

## Editorial posture

The outlet's framing is characteristically adversarial toward federal food and health regulators, treating regulatory clearance as evidence of capture rather than of safety.[1] That framing aligned with the accompanying Badlands commentary from [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] on the state of the food supply.[1]
