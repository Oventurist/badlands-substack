# Juan Tobay Robles

Juan Tobay Robles is an immigration detainee whose case triggered contempt proceedings against the leadership of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] in the District of [[minnesota|Minnesota]] in January 2026.

According to the January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief, Robles was taken into custody by federal immigration agents in early January 2026. On January 14, 2026, Chief U.S. District Judge [[patrick-schiltz|Patrick Schiltz]] issued a directive requiring that Robles receive a bond hearing or be released within seven days. ICE did not comply. Schiltz responded by ordering acting ICE Director [[todd-lyons|Todd Lyons]] to appear personally in [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]] federal court on January 30, 2026 to show cause why he should not be held in contempt, describing the Robles order as one of "dozens of court orders" the agency had disregarded in recent weeks. The court indicated the hearing would be canceled if Robles were released beforehand.
