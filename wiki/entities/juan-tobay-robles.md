# Juan Tobay Robles

Juan Tobay Robles is an immigration detainee whose case became the trigger for contempt proceedings against the leadership of [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] in the federal district court in Minnesota in January 2026.[1]

## Detention and court order

Robles was taken into custody by federal immigration agents in early January 2026.[1] On January 14, 2026, the court issued a directive requiring that he be given a bond hearing or released within seven days.[1] ICE did not comply.[1]

The agency's failure led Chief Judge [[patrick-schiltz|Patrick Schiltz]] to order acting ICE Director [[todd-lyons|Todd Lyons]] to appear in person in Minneapolis federal court on January 30, 2026 to show cause why he should not be held in contempt.[1] Schiltz indicated the hearing could be canceled if Robles were released before that date, making Robles's custody status the direct lever in the dispute.[1]

## Significance

The Robles matter was cited by the court as illustrative rather than isolated: Schiltz described it as one of "dozens of court orders" ignored by ICE in recent weeks, framing the case as evidence of a systemic pattern of non-compliance during the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]]'s enforcement surge.[1]
