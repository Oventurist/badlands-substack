# Neal Katyal

Neal Katyal is a lawyer and commentator who served as Solicitor General in the Obama administration and who has been a frequent op-ed contributor to the [[new-york-times|New York Times]].[1]

## Op-eds on the Durham probe

In commentary published in the February 16, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] noted that the Times was amplifying op-eds by Katyal calling on Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] to "discipline and fire" Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]], and urging Garland to refuse to make the Durham report public.[1] Just Human characterized this as part of a broader effort by the paper to damage Durham's legitimacy and credibility and to get ahead of whatever move he might make next.[1]
