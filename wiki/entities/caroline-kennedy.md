# Caroline Kennedy

Caroline Kennedy is the U.S. Ambassador to Australia who in August 2023 publicly signalled that Washington might accept a plea deal resolving the prosecution of WikiLeaks founder [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]].[1]

## Signalling a plea deal

Kennedy told the *Sydney Morning Herald* that there could be a "resolution" to Assange's then-four-year detention in Britain, saying "There is a way to resolve it" while adding that any deal would be "up to the Justice Department."[1] Such an arrangement would permit Assange, an Australian citizen, to return to Australia rather than face extradition on 18 U.S. criminal charges, 17 of them alleging violations of the [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]].[1]

Her remarks came only weeks after Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] rebuffed Australian calls to end the prosecution, telling reporters after a July meeting with Foreign Minister [[penny-wong|Penny Wong]] in Brisbane that Assange was "charged with very serious criminal conduct."[1] Assange's brother Gabriel Shipton read Kennedy's comments as evidence that "the administration appears to be searching for an off-ramp" ahead of the Australian prime minister's October state visit to Washington.[1] The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] declined to comment and the [[state-department|State Department]] did not respond to press inquiries.[1]
