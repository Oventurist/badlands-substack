# Taylor Lorenz

Taylor Lorenz is an American journalist and technology correspondent known for covering internet culture and social media. In the Badlands Media article "A Glaring Double Standard," she is mentioned as the journalist who doxxed [[chaya-raichik|Chaya Raichik]], the operator of the Libs of TikTok account.

## Role in the article

The article recounts that Raichik "was doxxed by Taylor Lorenz and then went on a media tour warning of the dangers of doxxing, saying, 'Every time I have to give my name for something, my heart skips a beat. It's unnerving.'" The source uses this detail to highlight what it calls Raichik's hypocrisy — condemning doxxing after being doxxed herself while the [[stopantisemitism|StopAntisemitism]] group, which she amplified, engaged in doxxing of Israel critics.
