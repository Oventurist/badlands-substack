# UrAsia Energy

UrAsia Energy Ltd. was the small Canadian mining company through which the financier [[frank-giustra|Frank Giustra]] entered the uranium business, and — in the Badlands Media account — the vehicle that began the chain of transactions culminating in the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Kazakh acquisition, 2005

Within days of Giustra's September 2005 visit to Almaty with [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], during which the two dined with Kazakh president [[nursultan-nazarbayev|Nursultan Nazarbayev]], UrAsia signed a preliminary deal giving it stakes in three uranium mines controlled by the state-run agency [[kazatomprom|Kazatomprom]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article describes the terms as a sweetheart arrangement obtained in exchange for a kickback, which turned Giustra's "fledgling company" into a major player in the global uranium industry.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## 2007 merger with Uranium One

In 2007, UrAsia merged with [[uranium-one|Uranium One]], described in the source as a South African company with assets in Africa and Australia, in a transaction valued at approximately $3.5 billion.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The combined entity retained the Uranium One name but was controlled by UrAsia investors, among them the Canadian [[ian-telfer|Ian Telfer]], who became chairman.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Giustra's personal stake was estimated at about $45 million, and through a spokeswoman he stated that he sold his stake in 2007.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The *New York Times* reporting quoted in the article identifies UrAsia as "the company that originally acquired Uranium One's most valuable asset: the Kazakh mines," and notes that between $1.3 million and $5.6 million in reported contributions to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] came from a constellation of people tied to Uranium One or UrAsia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The Russian investor [[sergei-kurzin|Sergei Kurzin]], a former associate of [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]], was among those involved in the UrAsia uranium venture in [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
