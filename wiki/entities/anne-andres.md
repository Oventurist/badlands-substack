# Anne Andres

**Anne Andres** is a Canadian transgender powerlifter who competes in the women's category in Alberta and who held multiple provincial women's records as of March 2023.[1]

## Competitive record

Andres held the Alberta women's bench press record at 275 pounds and the Alberta women's deadlift record at 544 pounds, which together with her squat gave her the local record for the combined total of all three lifts at 1,245 pounds.[1] According to the [[independent-council-on-womens-sports|Independent Council on Women's Sports]] (ICONS), Andres won eight of the nine competitions she entered in the women's category over the preceding four years.[1]

## March 2023 record protest

In March 2023 Andres's bench press record was broken at the Heroes Classic tournament in Lethbridge, Alberta, by [[avi-silverberg|Avi Silverberg]], the longtime head coach of Team Canada Powerlifting, who entered the women's event after identifying as female and lifted nearly 370 pounds while fully bearded and in a men's singlet.[1] Andres was volunteering at the event and was seen watching the lift.[1]

The episode was reported by the [[new-york-post|New York Post]] and circulated widely as a critique of policies allowing transgender women to compete against female athletes, alongside comparisons to the case of swimmer [[lia-thomas|Lia Thomas]].[1]
