# Porcellian Club

The **Porcellian Club** is a final club at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] identified in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as one of Harvard's principal elite grooming institutions within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]

Alongside the Fly Club and the A.D. Club, the Porcellian is described as a grooming ground for hedge fund managers, private equity titans and monetary policy engineers, with the Rothschild and Rockefeller dynasties said to be interwoven through these societies.[1] The article traces a three-step pathway in which Porcellian members graduate into Rothschild-affiliated financial houses or think tanks such as the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], which then cycle alumni into global policy posts at the [[international-monetary-fund|IMF]], the [[united-nations|United Nations]] or central banks.[1]

The partnership of [[timothy-barakett|Timothy Barakett]] with [[nathaniel-rothschild|Nathaniel Rothschild]] at [[atticus-capital|Atticus Capital]], and Barakett's later chairmanship of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]], is presented by the article as proof of "intergenerational elite continuity" flowing through this channel.[1] More broadly, the piece argues such clubs "aren't just about connection" but "about loyalty, leverage, and legacy" — networks that "don't just network, they enforce."[1]
