# Alberto Fittarelli

Alberto Fittarelli is a senior researcher at [[citizen-lab|Citizen Lab]], the digital watchdog housed at Canada's University of Toronto.[1]

## Paperwall investigation

Fittarelli authored the Citizen Lab report published February 7, 2024, that documented at least 123 Chinese-run websites posing as local news outlets in 30 countries.[1] He named the network [[paperwall|Paperwall]], defining it as "a large, and fast growing, network of anonymous websites posing as local news outlets," and wrote that "the campaign is an example of a sprawling influence operation serving both financial and political interests, and in alignment with Beijing's political agenda."[1]

The research was picked up by The Epoch Times and subsequently discussed in the Badlands News Brief, where [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] used the findings as a starting point for a broader argument about Chinese fifth-generation warfare doctrine.[1]
