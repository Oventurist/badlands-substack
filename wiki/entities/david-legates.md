# David Legates

David Legates is a climatologist and former official of the [[national-oceanic-and-atmospheric-administration|National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration]] who has publicly questioned aspects of mainstream climate modeling.

## White House climate office

As reported in the July 10, 2026 Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles", the Trump administration established a new [[white-house|White House]] climate office and placed Legates at its head — a choice the source describes as putting a "climate policy critic" in charge. Administration officials said the office would review federal climate science and policy to ensure analyses are objective and free from political bias. Critics contended the appointment could reshape how climate research informs federal policymaking and weaken existing climate initiatives.

The appointment was reported alongside a broader administration proposal to revise the federal research grant process so that agencies place greater weight on national priorities, transparency and practical outcomes — a package that researchers and university groups argued could reduce funding for basic research and increase political influence over grant decisions.
