# David Legates

David Legates is a climate policy critic and former National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration official who has questioned aspects of mainstream climate modeling.[1]

## White House climate office (2026)

In July 2026 the Trump administration established a new [[white-house|White House]] climate office headed by Legates.[1] Administration officials said the office would review federal climate science and policy to ensure analyses are objective and free from political bias.[1] Critics contended that the move could reshape how climate research informs federal policymaking and weaken existing climate initiatives.[1]

The appointment was announced alongside a broader overhaul of the [[federal-research-grant-overhaul|federal research grant process]], both of which drew objections from researchers and university groups.[1]
