# Cristhian Ortega-Lopez

Cristhian Ortega-Lopez is a Venezuelan national described in federal filings as an illegal alien and a believed member of the [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] gang, whose sheltering in New Mexico led to the April 2025 arrest of a former judge and his wife.[1]

## Case

Ortega-Lopez was apprehended at the Las Cruces residence of former Doña Ana County Magistrate Judge [[joel-cano|Joel Cano]] and his wife [[nancy-cano|Nancy Cano]] during an incident in February 2025.[1] Court documents indicated he had first been hired by Nancy Cano to perform home repairs and was subsequently offered accommodation in the couple's guesthouse.[1]

On April 24, 2025, federal agents raided the Cano home and arrested the couple on evidence tampering charges tied to allegations that they had harbored him.[1]
