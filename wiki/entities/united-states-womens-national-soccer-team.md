# United States Women's National Soccer Team

The United States Women's National Soccer Team is cited by Badlands Media as an example of a sports organization used as a vehicle for political messaging.[1]

In "Sports in Society," the team is described as having "gone full woke," which the article characterizes as an attempt to indoctrinate young women across the country into supporting Communist values and agendas.[1] The team is grouped with [[colin-kaepernick|Colin Kaepernick]], whom the article describes as having been used as a tool to create division by leading the "take a knee" movement.[1] These examples are offered as an acknowledgment that sports are "clearly not immune to corruption" and have been used as a tool of division and an outlet for propaganda — a concession the author makes before arguing that sports nonetheless serve a greater social good.[1]
