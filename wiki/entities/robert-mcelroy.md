# Robert McElroy

Cardinal Robert McElroy is a Roman Catholic prelate who was appointed by [[pope-francis|Pope Francis]] in January 2025 as the next archbishop of Washington, D.C.[1]

## Profile and appointment

McElroy is described as a vocal critic of President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s immigration policies and an advocate for the "radical inclusion" of LGBTQ church members. His elevation to the D.C. archdiocese came as the Catholic Church was portrayed as splintering between liberal and conservative factions.[1]

## Opposition to mass deportation

McElroy has largely criticized Trump over his views on immigration, including the promise to conduct mass deportations. He was one of twelve Catholic bishops from California who co-authored a statement voicing support for "our migrant brothers and sisters," acknowledging that calls for mass deportations and raids on undocumented individuals had created fear in migrant communities.[1]

After Trump's first election victory in 2016, McElroy called it "unthinkable" that Catholics would "stand by while more than ten percent of our flock is ripped from our midst and deported." He described Trump's mass deportation plan as an "act of injustice which would stain our national honor" and compared it to Japanese internment and Native American dispossession.[1]

## Contrast with Vatican ambassador pick

The appointment contrasted sharply with Trump's own pick for ambassador to the Vatican, [[brian-burch|Brian Burch]], a Francis critic who leads [[catholicvote|CatholicVote]].[1]
