# Michel Doukeris

Michel Doukeris is the chief executive officer of [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch InBev]], the brewing conglomerate that owns [[bud-light|Bud Light]].[1]

In May 2023 Doukeris publicly attributed the nationwide Bud Light boycott — triggered by the brand's promotional partnership with transgender TikTok influencer [[dylan-mulvaney|Dylan Mulvaney]] — to "misinformation" circulating on social media.[1] Speaking to the Financial Times, he said that on social platforms "you have one fact and every person puts an opinion behind the fact," so that after ten or twenty comments "the reality is no longer what the fact is, but is more about what the comments were."[1]

Doukeris also cited "misinformation and confusion" over reports that the Mulvaney-branded can was a general-release product, stating the can was never intended for production and public sale.[1] Critics observed that this defense simultaneously alienated progressive consumers while failing to satisfy the boycotting customer base.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described the CEO's remarks as gaslighting and argued that an accusation of lying requires demonstrated evidence to be meaningful.[1]
