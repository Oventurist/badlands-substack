# Audrey Hale

**Audrey Hale** was the 28-year-old perpetrator of the mass shooting at a Christian school in Nashville, Tennessee on Monday, March 27, 2023, in which three children and three adults were killed.[1] Hale was described in contemporaneous reporting as a female-to-male transgender person and had reportedly attended the school as a child.[1]

## The attack

The [[metro-nashville-police-department|Metro Nashville Police Department]] released video from the scene showing Hale shooting a way into the building and then methodically stalking through its interior.[1] At a press conference, police said Hale had left behind a document they described as a "manifesto," along with maps that noted the locations of security cameras, doorways and other details of the building.[1] Police Chief [[john-drake|John Drake]] said investigators had "some writings that we're going over that pertain to this date, the actual incident," as well as "a map drawn out of how all this was gonna take place."[1]

Asked about motive, Drake said a theory existed that police might be able to discuss later but that it was not confirmed, while appearing to hint that Hale's gender identity was a factor.[1] Asked directly whether Hale's identification bore on the targeting of the school, police replied that there was "some theory to that," and confirmed the attack was targeted.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated the shooting as the product of a long-running, compartmentalized cultural operation rather than an isolated act, describing it as an instance of what he called a "mass form of Manchurian candidate ops" pushed onto children.[1] He argued that the decades-long installation of secular culture, the mainstreaming and eventual corporate and governmental endorsement of LGBTQ+ identity, the fomenting of antagonism with conservative culture, the collapse of debate into acceptance-or-rejection binaries, and media encouragement of activism and violence had jointly created the conditions for the [[nashville-school-shooting|Nashville attack]].[1] He predicted copy-cat events and argued that suppression of such stories would entrench a "left-wing transgender martyrdom ideology."[1]
