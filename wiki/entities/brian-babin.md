# Brian Babin

Brian Babin is a Republican member of the [[us-house-of-representatives|U.S. House of Representatives]] who appeared in November 2023 reporting on the House GOP's search for a stopgap government funding solution.[1]

## November 2023 shutdown deliberations

On Tuesday, November 7, 2023, House Republicans — holding a slim 221–212 majority — met behind closed doors to debate how to structure a [[continuing-resolution|continuing resolution]] that would temporarily fund the federal government after existing funding expired on November 17.[1] Emerging from the meeting, Babin told reporters that "Everybody's got a little bit different position, different opinions," adding: "Right now, we're cogitating about it."[1] His remarks were widely read as evidence that the conference had not coalesced around a plan with only ten days remaining before the deadline.[1]

Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] characterized the same closed-door debate as "positive" and said a plan would be revealed "in short order," while members said they expected a House vote early the following week.[1] Congress must pass twelve appropriations bills to fund the government through its fiscal year.[1]
