# Standard & Poor's

Standard & Poor's (S&P) is a major credit ratings agency. In 2011 it downgraded the United States sovereign credit rating to AA-plus, and the rating stayed at that level thereafter rather than being restored to the top grade.[1]

When [[fitch-ratings|Fitch]] cut the United States from AAA to AA+ on August 1, 2023, it joined S&P at the lower rating, a development that Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described as a further indication that those managing the US economy were failing the American people.[1]

## Aftermath of the 2011 downgrade

Commentary on the May 2025 [[moodys|Moody's]] downgrade recalled that S&P's August 2011 U.S. sovereign downgrade prompted the worst single-day fall in U.S. stock prices since the global financial crisis, though the market recovered quickly.[2] It also noted that the Justice Department launched an investigation into S&P shortly afterward; CEO Deven Sharma left the company within a few weeks, and when the matter became a $5 billion federal lawsuit alleging that S&P had misled banks about the credibility of its ratings before the 2008 crisis, the firm called the action direct retaliation for its downgrade.[2]
