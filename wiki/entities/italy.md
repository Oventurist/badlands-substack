# Italy

Italy is a southern European state and NATO member whose right-wing government under Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] had been one of [[israel|Israel]]'s closest European allies since taking office in 2022.[1]

## Suspension of defense cooperation with Israel (2026)

On April 13, 2026, following consultations with senior ministers including Foreign Minister [[antonio-tajani|Antonio Tajani]] and Defence Minister [[guido-crosetto|Guido Crosetto]], Meloni announced that Italy was suspending the automatic renewal of its long-standing defence cooperation memorandum of understanding with Israel.[1] The 2003 MoU, which entered into force in 2006 under Silvio Berlusconi and renewed every five years unless one side opted out, covers military procurement, training, the exchange of defence equipment and technology, and related activities such as arms transit.[1]

The decision came amid Italy's sharp criticism of Israeli military operations in [[lebanon|Lebanon]], which had caused hundreds of deaths and thousands of injuries, and followed an incident in which Israeli forces fired warning shots at Italian troops serving with the U.N. peacekeeping mission [[unifil|UNIFIL]] in southern Lebanon, damaging a vehicle.[1] Italy responded by summoning Israel's ambassador.[1] Meloni framed the move simply: "When there are things we don't agree with, we act accordingly."[1] Israel's foreign ministry downplayed the suspension, describing the 2003 agreement as an old memorandum with "no substantive content" that had never significantly affected bilateral security cooperation.[1]

## Domestic politics and Badlands reading

Italy's governing coalition includes [[matteo-salvini|Matteo Salvini]]'s [[lega-nord|Lega]], which in 2017 adopted branding modeled on the 2016 [[donald-trump|Trump]] campaign, and Meloni's Brothers of Italy party, which aligned with Lega and others in 2022 to bring her to power.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued Meloni is functioning as an agent provocateur instigating narrative conflict with Israel, which he predicted would fracture the European Union and could see Italy and others seek to leave the bloc if their break with Israel is challenged by continental authorities.[1]

## Antitrust action against Apple (December 2025)

Italy's antitrust authority fined [[apple|Apple]] 98.6 million euros ($116 million) on December 22, 2025 after determining that the company's [[app-tracking-transparency|App Tracking Transparency]] privacy feature restricted App Store competition and constituted an abuse of its dominant position.[2] The authority's objection was not to the privacy policy itself but to the requirement that third-party app makers ask users for consent twice in order to comply with European privacy rules, a "double consent requirement" it called harmful to developers, advertisers and advertising intermediation platforms.[2] Apple announced it would appeal.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that in penalizing a privacy measure on competition grounds, Italy was prioritizing competition over privacy in a way that undermines the European Union's entire stated position on privacy.[2]

## Rejection of Russian drone threat claims, September 2025

Italy publicly distanced itself from escalatory claims about Russia in late September 2025 when Foreign Minister [[antonio-tajani|Antonio Tajani]] said at a Forza Italia event in Telese Terme that he did not believe President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] wanted to start World War III or to send drones to swarm the country, having consulted Defense Minister [[guido-crosetto|Guido Crossetto]] that morning.[3] The statement responded to a claim by Ukraine's [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]] that Russia could target Italy or other European nations with drones.[3] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] treated the episode as another Italian [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] signal that Russia is not an enemy of the West, noting Tajani's appointment as Foreign Minister and Deputy Prime Minister by [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] in 2022 alongside [[matteo-salvini|Matteo Salvini]].[3]
