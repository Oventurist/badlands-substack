# Italy

Italy is a founding member of [[nato|NATO]] and of the [[european-union|European Union]], a member of the [[group-of-seven|Group of Seven]], and host to a substantial United States military presence.

## Energy policy divisions

In coverage of the [[european-energy-crisis|European energy crisis]] summarized in the [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]], officials aligned with Italian leadership were among those calling for a reconsideration of restrictions on [[russia|Russian]] energy imports in order to stabilize supply, with the Italian deputy prime minister publicly urging a return to Russian energy purchases.

## Opposition to United States troop drawdowns

The same edition reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has signaled he is considering reducing or repositioning United States forces stationed in parts of Europe, including Italy and [[spain|Spain]], amid disputes over defense spending and alliance burden-sharing. Prime Minister [[giorgia-meloni|Giorgia Meloni]] said she would oppose any unilateral United States troop withdrawal from Italy, underscoring the country's reliance on the American security presence, a position echoed by other European officials warning that a drawdown could weaken deterrence.
