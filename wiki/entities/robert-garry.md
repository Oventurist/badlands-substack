# Robert Garry

Robert Garry is a virologist who, together with [[kristian-andersen]], was among the small group of scientists consulted by [[anthony-fauci]] during the earliest weeks of the COVID-19 pandemic and who later reversed a privately expressed view that SARS-CoV-2 might not have a natural origin.[1]

## Role in the origins controversy

Garry was a co-author of the January 31, 2020 email to Fauci in which the scientists reported that anomalies in the viral genome suggested a non-natural origin, including features that "(potentially) look engineered."[1] Three days later both scientists had publicly shifted to opposing the [[lab-leak-theory|lab-leak theory]].[1]

At the first hearing of the House select subcommittee investigating COVID-19 origins in March 2023, [[jim-jordan]] pressed on this reversal, noting that the only intervening event was a conference call with Fauci and Francis Collins, and citing roughly $9 million in later grant funding awarded to the scientists.[1] Former [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] Director [[robert-redfield]] testified at the same hearing that he had been sidelined from origins debates.[1] The [[badlands-news-brief]] presented the Garry–Andersen reversal as a case study in how research funding can shape ostensibly independent scientific consensus.[1]
