# Supremo Tribunal Federal

The Supremo Tribunal Federal (STF), Brazil's Federal Supreme Court, is the highest judicial body in [[brazil|Brazil]] and the institution from which the country's most aggressive online censorship orders have issued.[1] Its most prominent member in this context is Justice [[alexandre-de-moraes|Alexandre de Moraes]], described in Badlands coverage as enjoying near-absolute power to determine what speech is permitted in Brazil.[1]

Criticism of the STF itself has been treated by Brazilian authorities as actionable: the offenses alleged against the exiled journalist [[allan-dos-santos|Allan dos Santos]] concerned purported disinformation he published about the court and the [[2022-brazilian-general-election|2022 Brazilian general election]].[1] Orders traced to the court's authority forced [[rumble|Rumble]] to withdraw from Brazil in December 2023, blocked [[x-corp|X]] nationwide, and froze the Brazilian bank accounts of [[starlink|Starlink]] in August 2024 over refusals to remove posts and ban users.[1]

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] conducted a sustained rhetorical campaign against the court during X's conflict with it, calling Moraes "a tyrannical dictator masquerading as a judge" and a "criminal."[1] The STF's reach beyond Brazilian borders — culminating in an order that [[rumble|Rumble]] ban dos Santos worldwide — prompted [[rumble-and-trump-media-v-moraes|Rumble and Trump Media v. Moraes]], in which a U.S. federal court held that the court's directives had no legal force in the United States because they were never served through the Hague Convention, the U.S.–Brazil Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty, or any other valid mechanism.[1]
