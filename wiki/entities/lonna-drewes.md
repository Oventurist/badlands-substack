# Lonna Drewes

Lonna Drewes is a former model and fashion technology entrepreneur who in April 2026 became the fifth publicly identified woman to accuse Democratic Representative [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]] of sexual misconduct.[1]

## Allegations

Drewes alleges that in July 2018, after meeting Swalwell three times that year while he offered political connections for her business, he drugged her with a spiked glass of wine in a West Hollywood hotel room at the Montrose Hotel.[1] She alleges he then raped and choked her unconscious, and said she believed she was going to die.[1] She held a press conference on April 14, 2026 describing the incident, which was also reported as occurring in a Beverly Hills hotel room, and filed a fresh rape claim with the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department.[1]

## Corroboration

Reported corroborating details include Drewes's contemporaneous journal entries, preserved texts and photographs, accounts she shared with friends at the time, and a formal complaint filed the day the story broke.[1] Federal Election Commission campaign finance records show that "Swalwell for Congress" spent $361 at that exact hotel on July 18, 2018, and the Sheriff's Department confirmed the location of the alleged incident.[1] Swalwell has denied all accusations, and the allegations remain unproven in court.[1]
