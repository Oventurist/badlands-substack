# American Greatness

American Greatness is an American conservative online magazine publishing political commentary and analysis, generally aligned with the nationalist and [[america-first-movement|America First]] wing of the political right.[1]

## Coverage of the August 2023 Trump–Carlson interview

In August 2023, American Greatness published an essay — excerpted and republished by ZeroHedge under the headline "200 Million & Counting... Trump Triumphant, Ramaswamy Runner-Up, DeSantis Dud" — assessing the aftermath of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], which aired opposite the [[first-gop-presidential-debate-2023|first Republican presidential primary debate]].[1] The piece reported that the interview had drawn more than 150 million views within hours of posting, making it, by the outlet's reckoning, the most-watched television interview in history and breaking the record previously set by Carlson's interview with [[andrew-tate|Andrew Tate]] earlier that summer.[1]

American Greatness argued that the viewership figure demonstrated Trump's grip on the country's cultural trajectory in a way his rivals could not replicate, noting that despite his latest arraignment his polling margins over his nearest opponents stood at fifty percentage points or higher.[1] The outlet connected this to Trump's own earlier claim that he would need "one more indictment" to win the 2024 election, and observed that legacy outlets including [[cnn|CNN]] and [[time-magazine|Time]] had begun preparing readers for the possibility of a second Trump administration.[1]

## Editorial framing

The essay cast Trump as "the protagonist of this evil chapter of American history," describing his rival candidates as figures who affect disdain for the "drama" of history while pursuing an office destiny had closed to them.[1] It framed Trump's conflict with the [[deep-state|Deep State]] as the "main event" of American politics, inextricably woven with the fate of the nation, and predicted that if Trump's mugshot were taken, "hardly anyone will remember the sideshow in Milwaukee" — a reference to the debate venue.[1] A closing update raised the interview's tally to "over 200 million views and still going."[1]

The piece was reprinted and amplified through [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and then aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] extended the analysis with her own viewership comparisons.[1]
