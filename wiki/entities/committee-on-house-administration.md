# Committee on House Administration

The Committee on House Administration is the standing committee of the United States House of Representatives with jurisdiction over federal election administration, campaign finance oversight, and the internal operations of the House.[1] As of 2026 it is chaired by [[bryan-steil|Bryan Steil]].[1]

## ActBlue investigation

The committee is one of three House panels — with the [[house-judiciary-committee|Judiciary Committee]] and the [[united-states-house-oversight-committee|Oversight Committee]] — investigating [[actblue|ActBlue]] over alleged misconduct, whistleblower retaliation, and possible foreign donations.[1] In June 2026 its chairman joined a letter warning ActBlue CEO [[regina-wallace-jones|Regina Wallace-Jones]] that the organization could face [[contempt-of-congress|contempt of Congress]] for failure to comply with subpoenas issued on July 22, 2025.[1]

Earlier that month, Wallace-Jones testified before the committee and declined to answer questions about allegations that ActBlue facilitated foreign campaign donations to Democratic candidates in federal elections, invoking the Fifth Amendment.[1]

## ActBlue subpoenas, June 2025

The committee's Republican chairman was among the three GOP chairmen — alongside House Oversight and House Judiciary — who issued subpoena letters to ActBlue officials on June 25, 2025 regarding potential widespread fraud on the platform.[2] The action came amid a parallel Department of Justice probe into the unlawful use of online fundraising platforms for straw or dummy contributions.[2]
