# James Madison

James Madison was a United States president and is widely regarded as the Father of the Constitution.[1] His Virginia estate, [[montpelier|Montpelier]], is operated as a historic site.[1]

## 2023 Montpelier exhibit controversy

In May 2023, Madison's legacy became the focus of a congressional oversight dispute when Reps. [[bob-good|Bob Good]] (R-VA) and [[eli-crane|Eli Crane]] (R-AZ) demanded answers about "woke" influences on exhibits at Montpelier and other historical sites.[1] The lawmakers complained that the site lacked exhibits concentrated on Madison's achievements and on the importance of the Constitution, and urged the [[national-trust-for-historic-preservation|National Trust for Historic Preservation]] "to focus on the true life and history of James Madison, rather than succumbing to dishonest, 'woke,' and radical anti-American prejudices."[1]

Badlands Media commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the episode as part of a broader rewrite of American history, noting that Madison is considered the Father of the Constitution while the site's sole constitutional exhibit reportedly presented the supreme law of the land as a "pro-slavery document" — a treatment she described as "[[critical-race-theory|CRT]] on steroids."[1] She linked the controversy to a separate disclosure that the "Chief Innovation Officer" of the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]] had been exploring the use of artificial intelligence to eliminate "inherent bias" in US history.[1]
