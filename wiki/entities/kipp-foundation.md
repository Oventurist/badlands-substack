# KIPP Foundation

The KIPP Foundation is the national nonprofit behind the KIPP charter school network, led as of 2024 by chief executive [[shavar-jeffries|Shavar Jeffries]].[1]

## Badlands Media commentary

In coverage of [[saba-capital|Saba Capital]]'s 2024 proxy campaign against [[blackrock|BlackRock]] closed-end funds, Badlands Media used Jeffries' KIPP role to raise doubts about his suitability for a fund board.[1] The article characterized charter schools broadly as "a good way of getting government funding without having to be transparent about anything involving how the money is spent or how the students are performing."[1]
