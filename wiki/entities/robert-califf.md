# Robert Califf

Robert M. Califf served as Commissioner of the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] during the period in which the agency cleared cultivated chicken meat for human consumption.[1]

## Lab-grown meat approvals

The FDA under Califf announced it had "no further questions" regarding the safety of [[upside-foods|Upside Foods]]' cultivated chicken, and Califf personally declared the product safe for human consumption.[1] A second lab-grown chicken product, from [[good-meat|GOOD Meat]], was cleared as well.[1]

Critics writing in [[natural-news|Natural News]] characterized the agency Califf led as a "corrupt federal regulatory agency" for green-lighting meat grown in stainless-steel bioreactors from harvested animal cells.[1]

## Bird flu preparedness testimony

On May 8, 2024, Califf told senators during a hearing in Washington that the FDA was preparing for a scenario in which highly pathogenic avian influenza begins spreading among humans.[2] "This virus, like all viruses, is mutating. We need to continue to prepare for the possibility that it might jump to humans," he said.[2] Speaking to the [[senate-appropriations-committee|Senate Appropriations Committee]]'s Agriculture Appropriations Subcommittee, he added, "We've been busy getting prepared for if the virus does mutate in a way that jumps into humans on a larger level."[2]

Califf identified the principal danger as the virus reaching the lungs: "The real worry is that it will jump to the human lungs, where, when that has happened in other parts of the world for brief outbreaks, the mortality rates have been 25 percent."[2] He noted that the concern was based in part on how viruses typically mutate, as in the case of COVID-19.[2] At the time, [[h5n1-avian-influenza|H5N1]] had begun spreading among cattle and other species, with one confirmed human case in Texas whose primary symptom was inflamed eyes.[2]
