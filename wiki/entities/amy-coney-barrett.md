# Amy Coney Barrett

Amy Coney Barrett is an Associate Justice of the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court of the United States]]. In the Badlands Media corpus she appears principally as the author of the Court's June 2026 majority opinion upholding Mississippi's late-arriving mail ballot law.

## Mississippi mail ballot ruling (2026)

As reported in "Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes", Barrett wrote for a 5–4 majority rejecting a challenge brought by the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] to Mississippi's practice of counting absentee ballots postmarked by Election Day but received afterward. Her opinion concluded that federal law establishes Election Day but does not require every valid absentee ballot to be physically received by that date. Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] joined Barrett along with the Court's three liberal justices, leaving in place Mississippi's five-business-day receipt window.

The ruling drew a dissent from Justice [[samuel-alito|Samuel Alito]], who argued it "opens Pandora's box" and leaves open opportunities for voter fraud. Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the majority's restraint more favorably, arguing that a Court refusing to legislate from the bench serves the larger goal of returning government to its constitutional boxes and shifting the burden of election reform back to [[congress|Congress]] and the state legislatures.
