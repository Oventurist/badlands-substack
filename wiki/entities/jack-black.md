# Jack Black

Jack Black is an American actor, comedian, and musician, and one half of the comedy rock duo [[tenacious-d|Tenacious D]] alongside [[kyle-gass|Kyle Gass]].[1]

## Cancellation of the 2024 Tenacious D tour

On Tuesday, July 16, 2024, Black abruptly shelved Tenacious D's world tour after Gass joked onstage about the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|assassination attempt on Donald Trump]].[1] In an Instagram post, Black wrote that he "was blindsided by what was said at the show on Sunday" and that he "would never condone hate speech or encourage political violence in any form," adding that after "much reflection" he no longer felt it appropriate to continue the tour and that all future creative plans were on hold.[1]

The remark came at a Tenacious D show in Sydney, where Gass was presented with a birthday cake and, asked to make a wish, blew out the candles and said, "Don't miss Trump next time."[1] The Sydney crowd responded with a mix of boos and cheers, and critics outside the venue did not hold back.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Ashe in America mocked the episode as an unforeseeable turn of the 2024 news cycle — "Who had Jack Black being (nearly) deported from Australia on their 2024 bingo card?" — and observed that condemnation of the joke had been stronger from Australian leaders and citizens, who called for the duo's deportation, than from American leaders.[1] The Badlands take held that Black's cleanup effort was driven by concern for his bottom line rather than genuine offense at Gass's comment.[1]
