# Gabriel Noronha

Gabriel Noronha is a former special adviser on Iran at the [[state-department|US State Department]] who became a prominent critic of the Biden administration's Iran policy.[1]

## Commentary on the Iran portfolio

In the aftermath of the October 2023 [[october-7-attacks|Hamas assault on Israel]], Noronha was quoted saying that "[[rob-malley|Malley]] and others created an incredibly permissive environment for Hamas, for Iran, to do all these things."[1] The remark was cited alongside criticism from Representative [[darrell-issa|Darrell Issa]] in reporting on the suspended Iran envoy's handling of classified material and on the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]]'s reported role in planning the attack.[1]

Noronha's position illustrates a broader current of dissent from inside the diplomatic establishment, in which former officials argued that sanctions relief and diplomatic outreach toward Tehran had material downstream consequences for regional security.[1]
