# Jeremy Brown

Jeremy Brown is a member of the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]] whose Florida weapons-possession conviction became a focal point of disputes over the scope of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 6 pardons.[1] Brown was sentenced to more than seven years in prison in the Florida case.[1]

## DOJ acknowledgment of the January 6 connection

In court filings reported in early February 2025, the [[department-of-justice|US Department of Justice]] acknowledged that Brown's Florida weapons-possession case was directly related to January 6.[1] The admission left his supporters bewildered as to why he remained behind bars even after Trump's January 6 presidential pardon.[1]

Brown's attorney, [[carolyn-stewart|Carolyn Stewart]], demanded his release, saying both of his criminal cases were covered by the pardon.[1] She argued that the filings were ample evidence that the DOJ, Brown's defense team, and a US district judge all agreed the Florida case was a January 6 case, telling Blaze News: "Done. Fully baked… Yet the DOJ has prevented Jeremy's release under President Trump's pardon."[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[canncon|CannCon]] highlighted reporting that Brown's Florida conviction was in fact related to January 6 and noted that Brown had not been permitted to raise January 6 during his trial, concluding that "Jeremy Brown MUST be released!"[1]
