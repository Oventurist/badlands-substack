# Shaiel Ben-Ephraim

**Shaiel Ben-Ephraim** is an Israeli activist and journalist — a former [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldier who served as a self-described proud Zionist until witnessing the atrocities of the post-October 7 campaign firsthand. The Badlands Brief of April 20, 2026 cites his investigative work as a central source for allegations of systematic abuse of Palestinian detainees.

## The evidence trove

According to the Brief, Ben-Ephraim has gathered a trove of evidence — including interviews with IDF prison guards — that he claims can be independently corroborated by third-party organizations, documenting the torture and abuse of Palestinians, including Christians, held in Israeli custody. The most horrifying of the reports is the allegation that prisoners have been raped not only by IDF soldiers but also by dogs trained to perform the act; the Brief notes that Ben-Ephraim provides specific names of prisoner-victims, the prisons where the incidents occurred, and eyewitness accounts from both prisoners and guards.

## Context and corroboration

The Brief argues the claims cannot be dismissed as wartime propaganda, citing a 2024 video of IDF soldiers gang-raping a Palestinian male prisoner that was leaked to the public by the top two lawyers of the IDF — who were subsequently "hunted down and prosecuted" by the Israeli government. The reports also feed the Brief's account of the psychological collapse of IDF reservists who witnessed abuses in [[gaza|Gaza]] and the [[west-bank|West Bank]].
