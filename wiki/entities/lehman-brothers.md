# Lehman Brothers

Lehman Brothers was the investment bank whose 2008 collapse — the largest bankruptcy in U.S. history — triggered the broader global financial meltdown.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

[[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] earned more than $14 million over roughly seven years as an adviser to Lehman and, after the bankruptcy, to Barclays, which took over the firm; the Badlands Media account notes he apparently prefers people not know it.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) In 2008, as Lehman fell into insolvency because of its bad bet on real estate, Bush took part in a firm-wide rescue effort known internally as "Project Green," the process of raising new capital and disposing of bad balance-sheet assets. His own portion was termed "[[project-verde|Project Verde]]," an attempt to convince Mexican billionaire [[carlos-slim|Carlos Slim]] to make a potentially life-saving investment.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Examiner Anton Valukas recorded that on July 5, 2008 Bush reported the meeting had been unsuccessful because Slim "did not express interest in jv (joint venture) or stock purchase."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
