# Lehman Brothers

Lehman Brothers was a global financial services firm founded in 1850 that filed for bankruptcy on September 15, 2008, triggering the broader global financial crisis. In the Badlands Media article [[a-family-affair]], the firm is identified as the investment bank where [[jeb-bush]] earned more than $14 million over approximately seven years as an adviser, and where he participated in a failed effort to secure a bailout from Mexican billionaire [[carlos-slim]].

## Jeb Bush's role

According to [[fox-business]], Jeb Bush was involved in "Project Green," a firm-wide effort at Lehman to raise new capital and dispose of bad assets as the bank slid into insolvency. Bush's specific piece of the effort, "Project Verde," involved convincing Carlos Slim to make a potentially life-saving investment in Lehman. On July 5, 2008, Bush reported that the meeting had been unsuccessful because Slim "did not express interest in joint venture or stock purchase." The article notes that Slim and the salinas family were close associates.

## Collapse and aftermath

Lehman Brothers filed for the largest bankruptcy in U.S. history on September 15, 2008, after failing to find a buyer or secure sufficient capital. The collapse triggered a global financial meltdown, with stock markets plunging worldwide and governments launching massive bailout programs. The article uses Bush's connection to Lehman as evidence of the Bush family's entanglement in the global financial system and their proximity to financial collapse.

## Post-bankruptcy

After Lehman's 2008 bankruptcy, Jeb Bush continued as an adviser to [[barclays]], which acquired Lehman's North American operations. The article notes that Bush earned more than $14 million in total from his advisory roles at Lehman and Barclays.
