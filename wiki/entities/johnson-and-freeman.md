# Johnson & Freeman, LLC

**Johnson & Freeman, LLC** is a law firm that serves as the designated "Administrative Partner" to [[fulton-county|Fulton County, Georgia]] in connection with the county's election administration.[1]

The firm's role became publicly visible during the November 2023 municipal election, when firm attorney [[patrise-perkins-hooker|Patrise Perkins-Hooker]] notified Fulton County Commissioners by email that a discrepancy had been found between the advance voting ballots initially counted and the number reported during the recount, that a box of advance voting ballots was suspected of not having been scanned, and that the Board of Registration and Elections consequently could not certify the results at the scheduled hour.[1] The firm's explanation of the discrepancy was contested by observers who reported that successive counts reduced rather than increased the totals.[1]

See [[2023-georgia-municipal-election-discrepancies]].[1]
