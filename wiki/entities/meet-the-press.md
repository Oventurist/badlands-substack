# Meet the Press

Meet the Press is NBC's long-running Sunday political interview program, hosted by [[kristen-welker|Kristen Welker]].[1]

## Post-election Trump interview

Following his 2024 election victory, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] gave an hour-long sit-down interview to Welker on the program, one of two lengthy interviews he granted in that period alongside a printed interview with [[time-magazine|TIME]].[1]

During the appearance Trump was asked whether he would permit [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|RFK Jr.]] to ban pediatric vaccines or otherwise formalize the claim that vaccines cause autism.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized the answer as reasonable and nondogmatic, neither endorsing nor dismissing Kennedy's assessment, and described the approach as rational and scientific rather than provocative.[1]

Trump was also pressed on mass deportations and responded that he would like to make a deal with Democrats regarding the Dreamers, a shift DeLarme read as evidence of a willingness to transcend partisan bickering — and one that angered some of Trump's supporters.[1]

DeLarme suggested that both the Meet the Press and TIME interviews may have been structured to elicit inflammatory statements usable by [[the-opposition|the Opposition]], pitfalls he judged Trump to have sidestepped.[1]
