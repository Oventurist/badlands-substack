# Meet the Press

Meet the Press is the Sunday-morning public-affairs program of the American broadcast network [[nbc-news|NBC News]]. It is one of the longest-running programs in the history of American television, with a continuous run dating to 1947, and is currently moderated by [[kristen-welker|Kristen Welker]].

## Trump's June 2026 walkout

The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] walked out on Kristen Welker during a Meet the Press interview on Sunday, June 7, 2026, following a heated exchange about elections being fake. The moment went viral, and the Brief's commentary uses it to advance the claim, central to Badlands Media's election-integrity coverage, that the United States does not have real elections — arguing that Trump's remarks to Welker were "true and justified" and that pretending elections are real harms the America First agenda.

The episode is connected in the commentary to the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]] and to the [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] program's three years of election coverage.
