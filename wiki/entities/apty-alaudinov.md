# Apty Alaudinov

Apty Alaudinov is a Russian major general and the commander of the [[akhmat-special-forces|Akhmat Special Forces]], the Chechen formation drawn from Russia's Chechen Republic.[1] He was featured in Badlands Media's October 16, 2024 news brief in connection with the Russian counteroffensive that reversed Ukraine's incursion into the Kursk region, where Badlands described him as "our old friend" and noted the outlet had previously written about his role fighting the Azov Battalion.[1]

## Early life and family

Alaudinov did not personally participate in the [[first-chechen-war|First Chechen War]] of 1994–1996, but the conflict devastated his family: he lost twenty close relatives who fought in it, including his uncle, his brother, and his father, a former officer in the Soviet Army.[1]

## Legal career and the Kadyrov connection

When the [[second-chechen-war|Second Chechen War]] began in 1999, Alaudinov was in law school. After graduating in 2001 he took a post as a prosecutor in the Interior Ministry's organized crime unit.[1] Following the 2004 assassination of Chechen president [[akhmad-kadyrov|Akhmad Kadyrov]], the slain leader's son [[ramzan-kadyrov|Ramzan Kadyrov]] restructured that organized crime unit so that it could operate in a more kinetic, paramilitary capacity.[1]

Ramzan Kadyrov took a personal liking to Alaudinov, and in November 2006 assigned him a mission of great personal significance: to avenge Akhmad Kadyrov by eliminating [[movladi-baisarov|Movladi Baisarov]], the figure held responsible for the assassination.[1] Alaudinov led a team of fifty Chechen police officers out of Chechnya and into the streets of Moscow, where they confronted and killed Baisarov.[1] Moscow authorities objected that the operation had been carried out using "methods of the Chechen republic" by Chechen police rather than by the Moscow police, and opened a criminal investigation; it was ultimately unable to identify a specific killer because Baisarov had been struck by so many bullets.[1]

That group of fifty officers became the nucleus around which the modern [[akhmat-special-forces|Akhmat]] unit was legally reconstituted, and Alaudinov and Ramzan Kadyrov have maintained a close relationship ever since.[1]

## Kursk

By October 2024 Alaudinov's forces were engaged in the Kursk theatre, where Russian troops had broken through the western flank of Ukrainian positions and threatened to encircle Ukrainian units.[1] Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry framed the Chechen deployment as the decisive element "crushing the NATO blitzkrieg" in Kursk, and connected it to Vladimir Putin's recent visit to Dagestan, where he gave his blessing to the Kadyrovite formation.[1]
