# Charlie Sheen

Charlie Sheen is an American actor, best known at the time in question as the star of the sitcom *Two and a Half Men*, who appears in Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s account as the most prominent named client of the "Hollywood Madam" [[heidi-fleiss|Heidi Fleiss]].[1]

## Role in the article

Quoting the *New York Daily News*, the article notes that Fleiss "counted actor Charlie Sheen among her celebrity clients" and that her address book, containing names of celebrities, politicians, sports figures and musicians, was offered for sale on eBay in 2015.[1] Lange observes that at the time of the Fleiss scandal Sheen was "one of the biggest Hollywood stars," using this to argue that the compromise network reached the very top of the industry and gave the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] control over [[hollywood|Hollywood]].[1] This framing reflects Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
