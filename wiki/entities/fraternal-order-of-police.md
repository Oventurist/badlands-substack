# Fraternal Order of Police

The Fraternal Order of Police (FOP) is a national organization representing rank-and-file law enforcement officers in the United States, headed as of 2024 by National President [[patrick-yoes|Patrick Yoes]].[1]

## Involvement in the 2024 assassination-attempt hearings

The FOP was drawn into the congressional inquiry into the July 13, 2024 assassination attempt on former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] when Yoes testified before the [[house-homeland-security-committee|House Homeland Security Committee]] on July 23, 2024.[1] Because the FBI and the Department of Homeland Security declined to appear, Yoes and Colonel [[christopher-l-paris|Christopher L. Paris]] of the [[pennsylvania-state-police|Pennsylvania State Police]] absorbed the bulk of the committee's questioning; their written testimony was submitted to the committee record.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the hearing as another instance of institutional stonewalling in the aftermath of the attack, occurring the same day that [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] Director [[kimberly-cheatle|Kimberly Cheatle]] resigned.[1]
