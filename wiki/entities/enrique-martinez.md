# Enrique Martinez

Enrique Martinez is the chief executive of Fnac Darty, the French retail group that sells consumer electronics and household appliances, including cooling equipment.

In the July 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2b2|Badlands Brief]], Martinez is quoted from BFM Business describing the surge in demand for air conditioners during the French heatwave of summer 2026. He said customers had been waiting outside stores from as early as 4 a.m. and that some incidents involved physical confrontations among shoppers. His account is presented alongside reporting on the scenes at [[lidl|Lidl]] stores in Nanterre, Hazebrouck, Trélazé and near Angers, corroborating that the disorder was not confined to a single discount promotion but reflected broader scarcity in the French cooling market.
