# Luke Rudkowski

Luke Rudkowski is an American independent journalist and the founder of the alternative media outlet [[we-are-change|We Are Change]].[1]

## Commentary on the Vance selection

Rudkowski is cited in Badlands Media's July 2024 assessment of Donald Trump's choice of [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] as his running mate.[1] The article reproduced a post by Rudkowski as further illustration of its central claim: that the discomfort of Bush- and Cheney-era neoconservatives with the Vance pick was itself a favorable indicator for opponents of foreign military intervention.[1] Badlands positioned Rudkowski within the wider independent-media ecosystem whose long-standing criticism of endless war had, in the author's view, begun to find expression inside a major party ticket.[1]
