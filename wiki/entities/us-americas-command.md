# US Americas Command

US Americas Command, referred to in reporting as "Americom," is a proposed combatant command that would consolidate American military responsibility for the Western Hemisphere under the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] reorganisation drafted in late 2025.[1] Currently [[us-southern-command|US Southern Command]] and [[united-states-northern-command|US Northern Command]] are responsible for military operations across the Western Hemisphere; under the plan these would be placed under the US Americas Command.[1]

## Context

The reorganisation is driven by Secretary of War [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]]'s stated intention to break the status quo and reduce the number of four-star generals, and would also downgrade several major headquarters by placing [[us-central-command|Central Command]], [[united-states-european-command|European Command]] and [[us-africa-command|Africa Command]] under a new [[us-international-command|US International Command]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the creation of a single domestic-hemisphere command reinforces the expectation that the US military will be used at home to defend the homeland and defeat the cartels that have long preyed on the American people — adding, "God willing, that will also include the political cartel in DC."[1]
