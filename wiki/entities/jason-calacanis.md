# Jason Calacanis

Jason Calacanis is an American technology investor and podcast host, and one of the hosts of the [[all-in-podcast|All-In podcast]].[1] In May 2023 he interviewed Democratic presidential candidate [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] alongside other members of the show and asked him directly whether he believed the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] was involved in the murder of his uncle, President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]].[1] Kennedy responded without hesitation that the agency was "definitely involved in the murder and the 60-year cover-up," adding that it was still withholding papers it is legally required to release.[1]
