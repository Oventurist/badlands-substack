# Margaret Love

Margaret Love is a former United States pardon attorney who served in that Justice Department role from 1990 to 1997, a position devoted to assisting the president on clemency matters.[1]

## Commentary on the Hunter Biden pardon

Love was quoted in coverage aggregated by the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] assessing the "full and unconditional pardon" that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] granted his son [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] in December 2024.[1] She said she had "never seen language like this in a pardon document that purports to pardon offenses that have not apparently even been charged, with the exception of the Nixon pardon," referring to the 1974 clemency [[gerald-ford|Gerald Ford]] granted [[richard-nixon|Richard Nixon]].[1] She added that "even the broadest Trump pardons were specific as to what was being pardoned," underscoring the unusual vagueness of the Biden document.[1]
