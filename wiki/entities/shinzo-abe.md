# Shinzo Abe

Shinzo Abe was a former Prime Minister of [[japan|Japan]], remembered in Badlands commentary for his close personal rapport with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during Trump's first term.[1]

## Legacy in Badlands commentary

Covering the March 2026 White House meeting between Trump and Prime Minister [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that Takaichi's warm greeting — skipping the handshake for a hug — brought back "Shinzo Abe feels," and closed his account of the expanded U.S.-Japan energy and investment package by remarking that "Shinzo Abe would be proud."[1] Abe thus functions in this coverage as the benchmark for a Japanese leader aligned with the America First posture and with the U.S.-Japan alliance's deepening economic dimension.[1]

## Cited in Badlands assassination commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited Abe's assassination in September 2024 commentary as the one successful killing in a list of attempts against sovereign-aligned leaders, alongside failed attempts on [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]], [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]], and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2] The list was offered as evidence that few world leaders would be outraged if Putin retaliated against Western "black hats."[2]
