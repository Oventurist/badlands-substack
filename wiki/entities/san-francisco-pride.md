# San Francisco Pride

San Francisco Pride is the organization behind what has been described as the world's premiere LGBTQ pride event, held annually in June in San Francisco.[1]

## 2023 cancellation of Dee Snider

In May 2023 the group announced that Twisted Sister front man [[dee-snider|Dee Snider]]'s scheduled performance at its June celebration had been cancelled because of his recent political stance — specifically his support for [[paul-stanley|Paul Stanley]]'s criticism of gender transitions for children.[1] The organization said it had originally intended Twisted Sister's 1980s hard rock anthem "We're Not Gonna Take It" to be the "unofficial rallying cry" of the 2023 event, but pulled the plug over Snider's opposition to sex reassignment for minors.[1]

The episode was cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators as an example of cancel culture directed at figures who question youth medical transition, even when those figures had otherwise aligned with progressive positions on issues such as masking and vaccination.[1]
