# Michael Noble

Michael Noble is a St. Louis Circuit Judge who presided over contempt proceedings involving St. Louis Circuit Attorney [[kim-gardner|Kim Gardner]].[1]

## Contempt findings against Kim Gardner

In late April 2023 Noble issued a ruling sharply critical of Gardner's office, describing it as "a rudderless ship of chaos."[1] He held that there was sufficient evidence to find that Gardner and Assistant Circuit Attorney Chris Desilets had acted with "intentional disregard for the judicial process," and announced that he would appoint a special prosecutor to pursue a case of indirect criminal contempt against them both.[1]

The ruling added pressure to a removal effort already underway through a quo warranto action filed by the Missouri Attorney General's office in February 2023, and preceded Gardner's resignation on May 4, 2023.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief cited Noble's findings and the appointment of a special counsel as decisive factors leaving the [[george-soros|Soros]]-funded prosecutor with no option but to step down.[1]
