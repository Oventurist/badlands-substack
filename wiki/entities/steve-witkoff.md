# Steve Witkoff

Steve Witkoff is a real estate investor who serves as a Special Envoy in the administration of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], handling sensitive Middle East negotiations.

## The Doha talks of June 2026

[[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]] reports that the [[white-house|White House]] announced Witkoff and [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] would travel to Doha, [[qatar|Qatar]], for discussions aimed at advancing implementation of the United States–[[iran|Iran]] memorandum of understanding. Trump said Iran had requested the meeting; Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman [[esmaeil-baqaei|Esmaeil Baqaei]] contradicted him, stating that no negotiations were scheduled "at any level" in the coming days while confirming that an Iranian delegation would travel to Doha for technical discussions on implementing the existing agreement.

Badlands commentator [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reads the contradictory statements as a game of "Chicken" in which each side denies attendance before appearing at the last minute, noting that the theory would soon be tested since Witkoff and Kushner were ostensibly already en route. The trip took place against the backdrop of renewed military exchanges in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] that had strained the recently established ceasefire.
