# Philip Agee

Philip Agee was a former officer of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] who became a whistleblower and critic of U.S. covert operations.[1]

Agee described the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] as an overt substitute for the agency's earlier clandestine political manipulation, saying: "Nowadays, instead of having the CIA going around behind the scenes and trying to manipulate the process by inserting money here and giving instructions secretly and so forth, they have now a sidekick, which is this National Endowment for Democracy, NED."[1] His testimony is cited alongside that of journalists and researchers as evidence that the endowment functions as a CIA cut-out.[1]
