# Richard Branson

Richard Branson is a British business magnate, the founder of the Virgin group of companies, and one of the high-profile figures named in the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] court documents unsealed in early January 2024.[1]

## Naming in the unsealed Epstein documents

In a batch of documents made public on Monday, January 8, 2024, Epstein accuser [[sarah-ransome|Sarah Ransome]] was shown to have written in 2016 emails to then-[[new-york-post|New York Post]] columnist [[maureen-callahan|Maureen Callahan]] that she possessed copies of tapes Epstein had made of several of his prominent associates — former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], former President [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]] and Branson — allegedly having sex with an unnamed woman.[1] Ransome retracted those allegations in an October 23, 2016 email, telling Callahan she wished "to retract everything I have said to you and walk away from this."[1]

The claim regarding Branson was therefore reported as part of a set of allegations that the accuser herself withdrew, a caveat that [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentators argued was frequently buried in coverage of the unsealing.[1]
