# Robert Unanue

Robert Unanue is the chief executive of [[goya-foods|Goya Foods]]. In July 2020 he praised President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], a statement that prompted immediate calls for a boycott of Goya products from Trump's political opponents.[1]

The episode is cited in the [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief as the first clear demonstration of conservative consumer market power: despite boycott-supporting social media posts outnumbering supportive ones by 75 percent, Goya's net sales rose 22 percent in the two weeks after Unanue's remarks according to the market research firm Numerator.[1] Commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] used the Unanue case as the positive counterpart to the punitive boycotts of [[bud-light|Bud Light]] and [[target-corporation|Target]] in 2023.[1]
