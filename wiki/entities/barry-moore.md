# Barry Moore

Barry Moore is a conservative Republican member of the United States House of Representatives representing a district in Alabama.[1]

## Deportation bill for DUI convictions

In June 2025 Moore introduced legislation directing the deportation of illegal immigrants convicted of driving while under the influence (DUI).[1] The bill passed the House on Thursday, June 26, 2025, by a vote of 246 to 160, with no Republican voting against it and 160 Democrats opposed.[1] Thirty-seven House Democrats crossed party lines to support the measure, including [[jim-himes|Jim Himes]] of Connecticut — the ranking Democrat on the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence — as well as moderates Tom Suozzi of New York, Jared Golden of Maine, Laura Gillen of New York, Don Davis of North Carolina, and Marie Gluesenkamp Perez of Washington.[1]

Badlands Media commentary on the vote treated the bipartisan margin as evidence of shifting political ground on immigration enforcement, with host Jon Herold remarking that illegal aliens should be subject to deportation regardless of whether they had also been convicted of drunk driving.[1]
