# Nathan Hochman

Nathan Hochman is the District Attorney of Los Angeles County, California. He appeared in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief of July 2, 2025 in connection with the prosecution of participants in the anti-ICE riots that swept Los Angeles in June 2025.[1]

## Prosecutions arising from the 2025 Los Angeles riots

By July 1, 2025, the number of arrests stemming from the previous month's anti-ICE unrest in Los Angeles had surpassed forty. Among those charged were a teenager accused of attempted murder and a man arrested for striking a police officer with a flagpole.[1]

Hochman signalled that the prosecutorial effort would outlast the demonstrations themselves. "The large-scale protests may have ended for now, but our work has not," he said in a statement issued on Tuesday, July 1. "We are continuing to review evidence, identify suspects and file charges where the law has been broken."[1]

## Context

The riots had erupted in protest at nationwide raids by federal immigration agents enforcing [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s order to detain and deport illegal immigrants. A report published the previous week estimated that the unrest had cost the City of Los Angeles more than $32 million in damages and police overtime, a figure that excluded the cost of the National Guard troops deployed by Trump.[1]

The prosecutions ran in parallel with a federal lawsuit brought by the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] against the City of Los Angeles and Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] over the city's sanctuary policy.[1]
