# Jesse Binnall

Jesse Binnall is an American attorney cited in April 2026 disputing claims made by [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]] about [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]].[1]

An opinion article published by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] under the "Tyler Durden" pseudonym, credited to Matt Margolis via PJMedia, criticized The Atlantic's report and argued the magazine could face legal consequences, citing Binnall's statements alongside social-media responses attributed to Patel and other officials.[1] The Atlantic report alleged that Patel had a serious drinking problem that left him unreachable at times and intoxicated on the job.[1]
