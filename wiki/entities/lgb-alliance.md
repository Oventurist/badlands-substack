# LGB Alliance

The LGB Alliance is an advocacy organization representing lesbian, gay, and bisexual people that has publicly opposed the indoctrination of children with [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]].[1]

## Citation in Badlands analysis

In his essay on the hybridization of asymmetric and narrative warfare, Badlands Media contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] listed the LGB Alliance alongside [[lesbians-united|Lesbians United]] and [[gays-against-groomers|Gays Against Groomers]] as organizations that have released statements opposing the teaching of gender ideology to children and the homophobia they allege it fosters.[1]

Deschamps invoked these groups to rebut the premise that opposition to gender ideology maps neatly onto a left–right divide, arguing that many on the right remain unaware that large numbers on the left — and an increasing proportion of gay and lesbian voters — share that opposition.[1] In his framework, the erasure of these organizations from public debate is characteristic of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] used to sustain [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric operations]] within [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]].[1]
