# Tim Ballard

Tim Ballard is an American anti-trafficking activist and the founder of [[operation-underground-railroad|Operation Underground Railroad]], an organisation dedicated to rescuing children from sex trafficking operations.[1] His life and work are the basis of the 2023 film [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]], in which he is portrayed by [[jim-caviezel|Jim Caviezel]].[1]

The film's commercial success — $100 million at the box office within weeks of release, on a reported $14 million budget, achieved despite hostile coverage from outlets including Rolling Stone and Bloomberg News — brought Ballard's work to a mass audience and made child trafficking a subject of unusually wide public discussion in 2023.[1]
