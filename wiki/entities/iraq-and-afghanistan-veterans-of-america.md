# Iraq and Afghanistan Veterans of America

Iraq and Afghanistan Veterans of America (IAVA) is an American veterans advocacy organization founded by [[paul-rieckhoff|Paul Rieckhoff]], who also founded the media company Righteous Media.[1]

## Association with the 2023 censorship debate

The organization drew attention in July 2023 when its founder, appearing as a commentator on [[msnbc|MSNBC]], called for a post-9/11 style government crackdown to counter Americans he framed as at risk of becoming overly patriotic and drifting toward groups such as Patriot Front and the [[oath-keepers|Oath Keepers]].[1]

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the founder of a veterans organization ought to understand the importance of the Bill of Rights, and treated the episode as evidence that establishment actors were pursuing a whole-of-government effort to restrict opposing political speech.[1]
