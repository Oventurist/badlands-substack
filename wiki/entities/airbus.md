# Airbus

Airbus is the European aerospace consortium that competes with [[boeing|Boeing]] for dominance of the global commercial aircraft market. Its principal operations and headquarters are based in France.

## In the Badlands corpus

Airbus is invoked in the Badlands Media essay [[a-week-to-remember|A Week to Remember]] as a foil to Boeing. Having noted that Boeing's share price fell from $323 to $95 within eight weeks of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] taking office, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] asks: "Have you heard any bad news come out about Airbus during the past few years? Where is Airbus located?" The answer given is "France."

The comparison supports the article's forecast that "the [[deep-state|Deep State]] in America will continue to shift to Europe, more outside of [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s control," and its accompanying prediction of a struggle among what the author calls Cabal families in England, France, and Germany for a larger share of that power. Airbus itself is not accused of any conduct in the source; it functions as a geographic and reputational contrast.
