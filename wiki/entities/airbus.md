# Airbus

Airbus is the European aerospace manufacturer, based in France, that Badlands Media commentary contrasts with [[boeing|Boeing]] to illustrate an alleged relocation of [[deep-state|Deep State]] influence to Europe.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## The Boeing contrast

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] observes that Boeing's share price fell from $323 on the day [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] took office to $95 within eight weeks, while asking rhetorically whether any negative news about Airbus has emerged over the past several years.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) The answer he draws attention to is Airbus's location in France.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

Carlson uses the comparison to support his expectation that Deep State power will continue migrating from America to Europe, beyond [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s reach, amid a struggle among [[cabal|Cabal]] families in England, France and Germany.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)
