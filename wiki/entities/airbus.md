# Airbus

Airbus is the European aerospace manufacturer, based in France, that Badlands Media commentary contrasts with [[boeing|Boeing]] to illustrate an alleged relocation of [[deep-state|Deep State]] influence to Europe.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## The Boeing contrast

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] observes that Boeing's share price fell from $323 on the day [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] took office to $95 within eight weeks, while asking rhetorically whether any negative news about Airbus has emerged over the past several years.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember) The answer he draws attention to is Airbus's location in France.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

Carlson uses the comparison to support his expectation that Deep State power will continue migrating from America to Europe, beyond [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s reach, amid a struggle among [[cabal|Cabal]] families in England, France and Germany.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-week-to-remember)

## New China plant (April 2023)

During the [[2023-macron-state-visit-to-china|2023 Macron state visit to China]] in April 2023, French President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] agreed to a new Airbus plant in China as part of a package of investment deals signed on the trip, a move critics cited as evidence that France was deepening rather than reducing its dependency on Beijing.[2]


## Role in the wokeism-as-economic-warfare thesis

In the February 5, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Control Through Language," [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identified Airbus as [[boeing|Boeing]]'s biggest competitor and contrasted their production bases: Boeing builds its airplanes in the United States, while most Airbus planes are produced in Europe.[3] He used this contrast to argue that [[wokeism|wokeism]] — which he attributes to European origin, especially think tanks run by the [[city-of-london|City of London]] — functions as economic warfare, since the same foreign countries that spread it through America stand to pick up the business lost by damaged American firms.[3]
