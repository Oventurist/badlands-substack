# Mervyn Yan

Mervyn Yan is a business associate of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] who worked with him through the Chinese energy conglomerate [[cefc-china-energy|CEFC]] and the joint venture Hudson West III.[1]

## Subpoena and testimony

Yan was subpoenaed by the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] in 2023 for financial records relating to his business dealings with Hunter Biden.[1] He was scheduled to appear before both the Oversight and [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary]] committees on Thursday of the week beginning January 22, 2024 for closed-door transcribed interviews conducted as part of the House impeachment inquiry into President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] Yan was the first of the Hunter Biden associates to testify that week, followed by [[rob-walker|Rob Walker]].[1]
