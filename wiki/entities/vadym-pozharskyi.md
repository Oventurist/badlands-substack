# Vadym Pozharskyi

Vadym Pozharskyi is a Ukrainian businessman who served as a senior executive of the energy company [[burisma|Burisma]] during the period in which [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] sat on its board.[1]

Pozharskyi features in testimony expected from [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] concerning a December 2015 episode in Dubai.[1] After a Burisma board dinner at the Burj Al Arab Hotel on the evening of Friday, December 4, 2015, Archer and Hunter Biden travelled six miles north to the Four Seasons Resort Dubai at Jumeirah Beach for a drink with one of Hunter's friends.[1] While they were sitting outside at the bar, Pozharskyi telephoned to ask where they were, because Burisma's owner, [[mykola-zlochevsky|Mykola Zlochevsky]], urgently needed to speak with Hunter.[1]
