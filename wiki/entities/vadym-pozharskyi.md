# Vadym Pozharskyi

Vadym Pozharskyi is a Ukrainian businessman who served as a senior executive of the energy company [[burisma|Burisma]] during the period in which [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] sat on its board.[1]

Pozharskyi features in testimony expected from [[devon-archer|Devon Archer]] before the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] concerning a December 2015 episode in Dubai.[1] After a Burisma board dinner at the Burj Al Arab Hotel on the evening of Friday, December 4, 2015, Archer and Hunter Biden travelled six miles north to the Four Seasons Resort Dubai at Jumeirah Beach for a drink with one of Hunter's friends.[1] While they were sitting outside at the bar, Pozharskyi telephoned to ask where they were, because Burisma's owner, [[mykola-zlochevsky|Mykola Zlochevsky]], urgently needed to speak with Hunter.[1]

## Role in the Blue Star Strategies contract

Pozharskyi was the Burisma executive who negotiated the company's autumn 2015 engagement of the Washington consultancy [[blue-star-strategies|Blue Star Strategies]], a process documented in emails from [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden's abandoned laptop]].[2] A 23 October 2015 scheduling email showed a meeting on hold between Pozharskyi, Hunter Biden, Archer and [[sally-painter|Sally Painter]]; a week later Blue Star circulated a "revised proposal, contract and initial invoice for Burisma Holdings."[2] A October 2020 New York Post article described Pozharskyi as "a 'fixer' for shady oligarchs."[2]

In his written response Pozharskyi complained that the proposal offered no "tangible results" and no "names of top US officials in Ukraine," conceded the omission might have been made to be "on the safe and cautious side," and insisted all parties share the same "final goals."[2] He asked for deliverables producing statements of support for "Nikolay/Burisma" from high-ranking US officials to Ukraine's president, chief of staff and prosecutor general, and stated that the "ultimate purpose" of the contract was to bring "widely recognized and influential current and/or former US policy-makers" to Ukraine "with the ultimate purpose to close down for any cases/pursuits against Nikolay in Ukraine."[2] After [[eric-schwerin|Eric Schwerin]] and Hunter Biden assured him that everyone understood the unwritten scope, Pozharskyi agreed the parties "should disregard the wording of the scope," and Hunter told him to "go ahead and sign."[2] The contract was signed in mid-November 2015.[2]
