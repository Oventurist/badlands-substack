# Eagle Pass, Texas

Eagle Pass is a city on the U.S. southern border in Texas that became one of the principal flashpoints of the migration surge along the U.S.–Mexico frontier.[1]

## December 2023 surge

On Monday, December 18, 2023, the U.S. southern border recorded a record number of migrant encounters in a single day, as thousands of migrants flooded into Eagle Pass amid a broader surge that left authorities overwhelmed.[1] [[customs-and-border-protection|Customs and Border Protection]] sources reported more than 12,600 migrant encounters that day — a figure that included over 11,000 apprehensions of illegal immigrants and more than 1,600 people encountered at ports of entry — and which excluded the thousands still awaiting processing in the Del Rio Sector that evening.[1]

By the following morning, agents were still processing and transporting more than 4,500 migrants at the crossing point, with more than 5,300 already in custody and holding facilities running at 260% over capacity.[1] The episode was reported as part of the escalating strain on border enforcement infrastructure heading into the 2024 election year.[1]
