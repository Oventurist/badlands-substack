# Aristide Tapsoba

Aristide Tapsoba is the Burkinabe Ambassador to Russia, who in August 2025 disclosed negotiations to open direct air links between Moscow and the capitals of the Sahel states.[1]

## Direct flights initiative

Speaking to TASS on the sidelines of Technoprom-2025, an international forum on technological development held in Novosibirsk, Tapsoba said a Russian carrier wanted to launch direct flights between Ouagadougou and Moscow, as well as between the capitals of Mali and Niger and Moscow, and that the plans were under discussion.[1] He framed the initiative as a way to boost tourism between [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] and Russia.[1]

Tapsoba highlighted broader cooperation in tourism and culture, noting that a Burkinabe delegation of more than 500 people was due to travel to St. Petersburg in October 2025 to work in tourism and the economy.[1] He also said more than 500 Russians had visited Burkina Faso since the start of 2025, some attending the Festival of Cinema and Television of African Countries in Ouagadougou, where a Russian symphony orchestra performed.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identified the key takeaway as Russia's stepped-up cooperation with African nations across aviation, road, rail and maritime transport, described as a declared foreign policy priority.[1] He argued that Burkina Faso — where roughly 80% of the population lacks electricity despite the country being one of Africa's top gold exporters — was acquiring both a nuclear power plant and an aviation industry, and would become "the case study for the Golden Age" showing how quickly prosperity can be achieved under earnest leadership.[1]
