# Spencer Cox

Spencer Cox is the Republican Governor of Utah. He is identified in the February 10, 2026 edition of the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], "[[badlands-brief-4ec|Criminal Invader Crashouts & Honeypot Hoaxes]]," as one of several prominent Republicans staking out a regulatory position on [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] distinct from that of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## Artificial intelligence position

According to the Politico reporting summarized in the Brief, Cox has pushed for AI regulations focused on children and on social harms, while simultaneously supporting AI education and workforce training. His approach is presented as more targeted than the broad worker-protection framing advanced by Senator [[josh-hawley|Josh Hawley]] or the state-regulation and energy-infrastructure emphasis of Florida Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]], but as belonging to the same emerging Republican current favoring some degree of oversight. Politico's account placed all three in contrast to Trump's deregulatory stance and his administration's December executive order seeking to block states from adopting their own AI rules, and concluded that the disagreement could become a defining issue for the party heading into 2028.
