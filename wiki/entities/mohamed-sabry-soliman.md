# Mohamed Sabry Soliman

Mohamed Sabry Soliman is an Egyptian national identified as the suspect in the [[boulder-terror-attack|Boulder terror attack]] of June 1, 2025, in which six people were injured after a man began "setting people on fire" at an outdoor pedestrian mall in Boulder, Colorado.[1]

## The attack

Soliman, 45, whose visa had expired in 2023, shouted "free Palestine" as he threw Molotov cocktails and used a makeshift flamethrower against demonstrators at a weekly peaceful event held in support of Israeli hostages on Pearl Street.[1] He was taken into custody after the attack, which the [[fbi|FBI]] described as a "suspected act of terrorism."[1]

## Reaction

Colorado Governor [[jared-polis|Jared Polis]] called the assault an act of terror, and figures across the state's political leadership denounced it in the same terms.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who attended school in Boulder, found the universality of the condemnation surprising given what she described as years of Colorado progressives courting such violence, and wrote that the authorities' expressed shock was "quite unbelievable."[1] She noted that U.S. News and World Report had ranked Colorado the second most dangerous state in the nation the previous week.[1]
