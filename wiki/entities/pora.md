# Pora

Pora was the Ukrainian youth movement central to the protests of the [[orange-revolution|Orange Revolution]] of 2004–05.[1]

Badlands Media reports that Pora received direct foreign funding, including an initial seed of $30,000 from the Canadian embassy, as part of a broader flow of money from the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|NED]], [[usaid|USAID]] and allied "democracy promotion" outfits into Ukrainian civil society for training in protest tactics, media manipulation and election monitoring.[1]

A number of Pora's leaders were trained in Serbia at the Center for Nonviolent Resistance, the consulting organization established by [[otpor|Otpor!]] activists to instruct youth leaders worldwide in organizing a movement, motivating voters and developing mass actions.[1] Writing in Dissent, [[mark-r-beissinger|Mark R. Beissinger]] recorded that after the Rose and Orange Revolutions, Georgian and Ukrainian youth movements began to challenge Otpor's consulting monopoly, and that Pora activists joked about creating a new Comintern for democratic revolution.[1]
