# Mohammed al-Bukhaiti

Mohammed al-Bukhaiti is a senior official of the [[houthis|Houthi]] movement in Yemen and one of its principal public spokesmen on the Red Sea shipping campaign.[1]

## Safe-passage declaration for Russian and Chinese shipping

In an interview with the Russian outlet Izvestia in January 2024, al-Bukhaiti said that Russian and Chinese vessels transiting the Red Sea would be granted safe passage. He insisted that the waters around Yemen — which several shipping firms were avoiding — were safe so long as vessels were not linked to certain countries, particularly [[israel|Israel]].[1]

"As for all other countries, including [[russia|Russia]] and [[china|China]], their shipping in the region is not threatened," he said, adding: "Moreover, we are ready to ensure the safe passage of their ships in the Red Sea, because free navigation plays a significant role for our country." He also stated that attacks on vessels "in any way connected with Israel" would continue.[1]

Reporting at the time noted that, despite such assurances, Houthi attacks had struck ships with no apparent connection to Israel.[1]

Badlands commentary read the safe-passage offer through both an "Actual" and a "Narrative" lens: as part of an escalating kinetic blockade of a critical east–west trade route disproportionately harming the West, and simultaneously as an Iranian-proxy attempt to muddy already-tense great-power relations by casting Moscow and Beijing as beneficiaries of the disruption.[1]
