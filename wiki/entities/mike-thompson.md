# Mike Thompson

Mike Thompson is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from California.[1]

## Position on the overtime tax deduction

Thompson was identified as one of the Democrats supporting extension or enhancement of the [[overtime-pay-tax-deduction|overtime pay tax deduction]], a provision of the Republican 2025 tax package that proved broadly popular with filers in the 2026 filing season.[1] Alongside Rep. [[brendan-boyle|Brendan Boyle]], he framed the break as relief for wage workers, while other Democrats such as Sen. [[chris-van-hollen|Chris Van Hollen]] and Rep. [[lloyd-doggett|Lloyd Doggett]] favored alternative approaches or additional scrutiny.[1]
