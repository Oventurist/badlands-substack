# Center to Advance Security in America

The Center to Advance Security in America (CASA) is a government watchdog organization that in July 2026 asked Acting Director of National Intelligence [[william-pulte|William Pulte]] to review the security clearances of former members of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigative team.[1]

CASA cited recently released Justice Department records that it says raise questions about the handling of classified information, and requested that clearances be revoked if an official review determines the allegations warrant such action.[1] Its request relies on internal Justice Department communications released by Senate Judiciary Committee Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]]; according to the released materials as described in reporting, the records allege that members of Smith's office granted access to classified information without the required "need-to-know" authorization, and that concerns were raised internally about security procedures involving a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility.[1] No response from the Office of the Director of National Intelligence had been announced as of publication.[1]
