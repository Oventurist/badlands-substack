# Center to Advance Security in America

The Center to Advance Security in America (CASA) is a government watchdog organization described in the Badlands corpus as pursuing accountability actions against federal officials through records requests and formal petitions.

## Petition on the Jack Smith team's clearances

The July 27, 2026 [[badlands-brief-245|Badlands Brief]] reports that CASA asked Acting Director of National Intelligence [[william-pulte|William Pulte]] to review the security clearances held by former members of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s investigative team, and to revoke those clearances if an official review determined the allegations warranted it.

CASA's request rested on internal [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] communications released by [[senate-judiciary-committee|Senate Judiciary Committee]] Chairman [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]]. As characterized in the reporting, the records allege that members of Smith's office granted access to classified information without the required "need-to-know" authorization, and that concerns were raised internally about security procedures involving a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility. The Brief presents the episode as ironic, given that Smith's office had prosecuted President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the alleged mishandling of classified information. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]'s accompanying commentary treats the request as overdue, arguing that a security clearance "should be granted for a specific purpose, and no one should ever be entitled to it."
