# Henry Starnes

Henry Starnes was president of the Ontario Bank and later Mayor of Montreal.[1] Matthew Ehret writes that when Union officers examined the possessions of [[john-wilkes-booth|John Wilkes Booth]], [[abraham-lincoln|Lincoln]]'s assassin, they discovered a $500 cheque signed directly by Starnes — evidence, in Ehret's reading, that Booth had been deployed from Montreal with local financial backing.[1] The claim is part of the article's broader argument that Montreal and Toronto served as Confederate bases of operations under British protection during the American [[american-civil-war|Civil War]].[1]
