# Nancy Pelosi

Nancy Pelosi is a long-serving Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from California and former Speaker of the House. In April 2026 she played a decisive role in ending Rep. [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]]'s campaign for governor of California.[1]

Amid multiple allegations of sexual misconduct against Swalwell, Pelosi publicly called for him to leave the race, telling NBC that "the young woman who has made serious allegations against Congressman Swalwell must be respected and heard," that the "extremely sensitive matter must be appropriately investigated with full transparency and accountability," and that she had discussed with Swalwell that this was "best done outside of a gubernatorial campaign."[1] Swalwell suspended his campaign on April 12, 2026.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the sequence bluntly — "Pelosi told him to drop out, so he did" — presenting it as an illustration of how party leadership rather than voters determines outcomes, and sarcastically concluding "Hooray for Democracy!"[1]


## Role in the first Trump impeachment

According to documents released by [[tulsi-gabbard|DNI Tulsi Gabbard]] in April 2026, then-Speaker Pelosi and [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|HPSCI]] Chairman [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] used a false, second-hand whistleblower narrative to create media intrigue and to spark the basis for the [[first-impeachment-of-donald-trump|impeachment]] of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in December 2019.[2] The Office of the Director of National Intelligence said the underlying complaint had been advanced by former IC Inspector General [[michael-atkinson|Michael Atkinson]] without firsthand evidence and in violation of standard procedures.[2] Gabbard characterised the sequence as "deep state actors within the Intelligence Community" concocting a narrative "used by Congress to usurp the will of the American people."[2]

## Vote on military aid to Israel (July 2026)

On July 15, 2026, former Speaker Pelosi voted in favor of an amendment introduced by Rep. [[thomas-massie|Thomas Massie]] (R-Ky.) that would have eliminated approximately $3.3 billion in annual US military assistance to [[israel|Israel]].[3] The amendment failed by a vote of 314-104, with 103 Democrats supporting it, 98 opposed and 10 voting "present."[3] Pelosi's vote, alongside that of House Minority Whip [[katherine-clark|Katherine Clark]], put her at odds with House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]], underscoring divisions within Democratic leadership over US policy toward Israel amid the ongoing war in Gaza.[3] See [[july-2026-house-vote-on-military-aid-to-israel|the vote]] for fuller coverage.[3]

## Named in Trump's "enemy from within" remarks

During an October 2024 [[fox-news|Fox News]] town hall in Georgia, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] singled out Pelosi and Representative [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] as he reaffirmed his characterization of his political rivals as "the enemy from within," calling them "so sick" and "so evil" and accusing them of pursuing "phony investigations."[4]

## Comments on the 2023 Trump indictments

In remarks to New York magazine reported on August 8, 2023, Pelosi called the latest indictment against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "beautiful," saying "The indictments against the president are exquisite" and adding that "they're beautiful and intricate, and they probably have a better chance of conviction than anything that I would come up with."[5] Asked about the possibility of another Trump presidency, she commanded the reporter "Don't even think of that," continuing "Don't think of the world being on fire," and asserting "It cannot happen, or we will not be the United States of America."[5] She further stated that "if he were to be president, it would be a criminal enterprise in the White House."[5] The comments followed Trump's description of Pelosi as a "wicked witch" and a "demented psycho" who would reside in hell when she dies.[5]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the remarks through the maxim "when someone shows you who they are, believe them," arguing that when establishment figures invoke phrases such as "threat to our Democracy" they are not lying but disclosing that Trump and his supporters threaten *their* democracy, *their* government and *their* way of life.[5] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] agreed with Pelosi's premise in inverted form, writing that "America will cease to exist if Trump takes office in 2024" as we know it, and framing her statement as messaging for a "club of sadistic parasites" who had hijacked civilization.[5]

## Appointment of Liz Cheney to the January 6 Committee

Preliminary findings released in March 2024 by the [[house-administration-subcommittee-on-oversight|House Administration Subcommittee on Oversight]] noted that [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]] was not the minority ranking member of the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]] but served as Vice Chair, a position reserved for the majority under House rules, having been named by Speaker Pelosi as one of her eight majority appointments.[6] The subcommittee concluded that the minority party was therefore not meaningfully represented on the committee.[6] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued accountability should start with Pelosi, [[bennie-thompson|Bennie Thompson]], and Yogananda Pittman, as well as Cheney and [[adam-kinzinger|Adam Kinzinger]].[6]

## Break with Israel over weapons transfers (2024)

In April 2024 Pelosi signed onto a call by progressive members of
[[congress|Congress]] for the United States to stop transferring weapons to
[[israel|Israel]] over the strike that killed seven
[[world-central-kitchen|World Central Kitchen]] aid workers in Gaza.[7] The
move was described as a significant break with Israel by a long-standing
supporter, underscoring growing fissures between Democrats and the government
of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[7] The letter, led
by Representatives Mark Pocan (D-Wisc.), Jim McGovern (D-Mass.) and Jan
Schakowsky (D-Ill.), was released on Friday with 37 signatures from other
Democrats, including Pelosi, and told President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and
Secretary of State [[antony-blinken|Antony Blinken]] that "in light of the
recent strike against aid workers and the ever-worsening humanitarian crisis,
we believe it is unjustifiable to approve these weapons transfers."[7] Released
after the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] announced initial findings of its own
investigation, the letter also called for an independent probe.[7]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the episode as
evidence that the establishment — "from the
[[state-department|State Department]] to [[nato|NATO]]" — was prepping for a
second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration as an inevitability, and that
fractures were forming within the once-unified Uniparty as the old guard
realized Biden was not truly their president.[7]

## Stock trading and the PELOSI Act

Pelosi's name became attached to the debate over congressional stock trading after her husband, [[paul-pelosi|Paul Pelosi]], purchased up to $5 million in semiconductor stock in 2022 just as the Senate was passing legislation to massively subsidize the semiconductor industry.[8] She was also among a bipartisan group of lawmakers reported to have beaten the market in 2021 across hundreds of millions of dollars in trades, alongside Representatives Austin Scott, Brian Mast, French Hill, John Curtis, and Dan Crenshaw.[8] In January 2023, Senator [[josh-hawley|Josh Hawley]] introduced the [[pelosi-act|PELOSI Act]], legislation named in reference to her that would bar members of [[congress|Congress]] and their families from holding or trading stocks.[8]

## Biden's resentment after the 2024 withdrawal

In December 2024 reporting described Pelosi as a particular target of the outgoing First Couple's resentment, having led the effort to push [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] out of the 2024 election race by personally calling him and demanding he quit in the hours before he withdrew.[9] The pair had been friends for some 50 years, but a wounded Biden had since given her the silent treatment, and any repair in relations was thought unlikely.[9] Democratic mega-donor [[john-morgan|John Morgan]] speculated that Biden had deliberately forced [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] onto the ticket to spite Pelosi and [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], noting that "Pelosi had told her delegation that there would be a convention and a nominating process."[9]

## "The Art of Power" and the wrap-up smear (August 2024)

In August 2024 The New Yorker profiled Pelosi under the heading "Nancy Pelosi's Art of Power," noting her own formulation that she went from "housewife to House member to House Speaker" and describing her as the most persuasive and dominant figure on Capitol Hill since Lyndon Johnson was, in Robert Caro's phrase, "the master of the Senate."[10] The piece contrasted Johnson's domineering and often cruel style with Pelosi's less beastly but comparably Machiavellian arm-twisting, and tied her influence directly to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s decision to stand aside in the 2024 election and cede the nomination to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[10] It recalled her July 10 interview on MSNBC's "Morning Joe," ostensibly booked to promote the cause of freedom in Belarus alongside dissident politician Sviatlana Tsikhanouskaya.[10]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the profile to revisit Pelosi's 2017 public explanation of the "wrap-up smear," a political dialectic in which claims or rumors are first seeded into the media apparatus and then cited as reporting to justify public comment on them, even by the person who planted them.[10] The practice shields both parties: the politician gets to cite "reporting" while the outlet gets an "unnamed source close to the situation."[10] He argued the same dialectic was being applied to an intra-party civil war over the Democratic ticket, with the party's Old Guard all but admitting it had staged a political coup against Biden, and that patriots including [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] recognized this — hence their signaling of the #OurBoyBlue theme back into the central narrative.[10]

## Home exorcism claim (January 2023)

In January 2023, Pelosi's daughter [[alexandra-pelosi|Alexandra Pelosi]] told The New York Times that the [[paul-pelosi-attack|attack on Paul Pelosi]] had weighed heavily on her mother, who over Thanksgiving "had priests coming, trying to have an exorcism of the house and having prayer services" at the family's San Francisco home.[11] The [[archdiocese-of-san-francisco|Archdiocese of San Francisco]] subsequently told [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] that it was "unaware of the exorcism question you raised," undercutting the account.[11] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] dismissed the story as a publicity stunt or falsehood, while speculating it might have been coded messaging that the house "was now clean."[11]
