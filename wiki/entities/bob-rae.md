# Bob Rae

Bob Rae is a Canadian politician and [[rhodes-scholarship|Rhodes Scholar]] who served as the NDP Premier of Ontario from 1990 to 1995 and later as interim controller of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party of Canada]] during several of the Harper years.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) He was a major player in the [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]] think tank.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], Rae managed [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]]'s admission into politics, abdicating his seat to her and thereby ensuring her entry into [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]]'s inner circle, where she became Trudeau's official handler.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Ontario Hydro and the green prototype

As Premier of Ontario, Rae brought in [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]] as President of Ontario Hydro from 1992 to 1994, a period during which Canada's nuclear sector was nearly shut down and a prototype for a "Green New Deal" was applied.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
