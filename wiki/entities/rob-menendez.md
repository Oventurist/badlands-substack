# Rob Menendez

Rob Menendez is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from New Jersey and a member of the [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Committee on Homeland Security]]'s Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Protection Subcommittee.[1]

At the subcommittee's February 6, 2024 hearing on securing [[operational-technology|operational technology]] in the water sector, Menendez asked the witness panel about the [[biden-administration|Biden administration]]'s sector-specific cybersecurity "sprints," describing them as reflecting "the administration's desire to make OT cybersecurity a priority, and better defend critical infrastructure from our adversaries," and asking what results the witnesses had seen.[1]

The Badlands account characterized the question as an attempt "to get some good sound bites for the Biden Administration" that "did not go well": the witnesses reportedly exchanged glances and laughed before [[kevin-morley|Kevin Morley]] said the sprint had focused on technology solutions beyond the maturity of many utilities, and [[robert-m-lee|Robert M. Lee]] said utilities "were pushed very strongly to a government specific answer that didn't actually meet a lot of what they're trying to accomplish, with no resourcing behind it."[1]
