# Lee Saunders

Lee Saunders is an American labor leader who, alongside [[randi-weingarten|Randi Weingarten]], left the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] in June 2025 after being stripped of a key committee assignment by new party chair [[ken-martin|Ken Martin]].[1]

## Departure from the DNC

*The New York Times* reported that Martin had offered to retain Saunders and Weingarten as DNC members but declined to renew their "plum assignments" on the Rules and Bylaws Committee, which governs how the party nominates its presidential candidates.[1] The two leaders, who represent a combined 3.2 million workers, then departed the committee, both questioning the party's direction under Martin.[1] Their exit came shortly after a bruising public fight between Martin and DNC vice chair [[david-hogg|David Hogg]], and amid reporting that the DNC's cash reserves and major-donor fundraising had collapsed.[1]
