# Ernest Jones

Ernest Jones was a neurologist and associate of [[sigmund-freud|Sigmund Freud]] who helped establish organized [[psychoanalysis]] in the United States.[1]

In 1911, working from Freud's ideas, Jones joined [[james-jackson-putnam|James Jackson Putnam]] in organizing the founding of the [[american-psychoanalytic-association|American Psychoanalytic Association]]. Putnam was elected president of the new body and Jones its secretary.[1]

Badlands Media commentary situates this founding within the 1910s, described as the decade in which Americans began to be altered, affected and controlled on an unconscious level.[1]
