# Ashe in America

Ashe in America is a writer and contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] who publishes commentary on the Badlands Brief, focusing heavily on election integrity and Democratic Party politics.[1]

## Commentary in the July 28, 2026 Brief

On the appeals court ruling against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s election administration order, she wrote that "Saturday's decision does not settle the underlying dispute over presidential power but keeps [[indira-talwani|Talwani]]'s injunction intact while the case winds through the courts, a delay that could push final resolution dangerously close to the midterms."[1]

On [[axios|Axios]]'s 2028 Democratic power rankings, she responded "Yes, please," calling it "really quite something that there is no new talent on the Democrat side" and "hilarious that we're talking about [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|AOC]] being anywhere near executive power."[1] She argued she would be concerned only if Democrats were elevating "someone new, someone that seems normal and Kennedy-esque" who tacked back to the middle and restored sanity, whereas [[kamala-harris|Harris]], [[gavin-newsom|Newsom]] and AOC are "punchlines" proving the party is not interested in competent contenders — because "all these people know elections are fake."[1] She closed by asking how far "this DSA Rising op" will go and when the [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] comeback arrives.[1]
