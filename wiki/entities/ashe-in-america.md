# Ashe in America

Ashe in America is the pen name and newsletter title of a writer who contributes to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]. She is credited as the author of the August 30, 2024 essay [[a-mysterious-masterclass-in-multi|"A Mysterious Masterclass in Multi Sensory Deception"]], a first-person account of the fifth [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] stop in Deadwood, South Dakota.

Her work as presented in that article blends personal narrative with commentary on media manipulation and what Badlands writers call the [[information-war|information war]]. In the Deadwood piece she recounts travelling to Mount Rushmore and to the [[cosmos-mystery-area|Cosmos Mystery Area]] with fellow attendees including [[steven-kwast|General Steven Kwast]], and draws from the experience an argument that effective propaganda pairs a kernel of verifiable science with authoritative repetition and involuntary sensory response.

Badlands Media notes at the foot of her articles that contributors' views do not necessarily represent those of the outlet, and directs readers to her independent Substack, Ashe in America, where her work is available free of charge.
