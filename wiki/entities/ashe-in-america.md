# Ashe in America

**Ashe in America** is a writer and commentator associated with the Badlands Substack ecosystem.[1]

## Appearances

Ashe in America appeared as the guest on the sixth episode of [[badlands-blitz|The Blitz]], published February 14, 2026 under the title "The Info War Ouroboros".[1] The host credited the pair with derailing their own planned conversation at the outset, spending most of the episode on the extended metaphors of "Sand Bunkers, Squared Circles and Ouroborosian Rugs" as applied to topics from the Epstein saga to the [[election-reform-campaign|Election Reform Campaign]].[1] The host judged the exchange a good discussion despite — or because of — its unplanned direction.[1]

Ashe in America maintains a presence on Substack, where she is linked from Badlands posts by user mention.[1]

Ashe in America had earlier joined the fourth episode of The Blitz, "Color Revolutions Come Home to Roost," published January 31, 2026.[2] That episode covered what the host described as a week full of "System Sway," ranging from the expansion and acceleration of the Minnesota [[color-revolution|Color Revolution]] to the Fulton County Election Raid and the ongoing pattern of Canadian Capitulation, all treated as converging illustrations of a single Macro System and its inevitable dissolution.[2] As with other episodes, the pair also read from the comments section to survey what subscribers had been saying during the week.[2]

On 25 April 2026 Ashe in America co-hosted the fourteenth episode of The Blitz, "The Dawning Age of American Accountability," alongside [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]].[3] The pair dove into the biggest news of the week, which they characterised as "all white pills" from the Badlands perspective, beginning with the massive indictment against the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] and dovetailing into the unraveling of the [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate Hoax]].[3] They also spent time on what the "[[taco-tuesday|TACO Tuesday]]" crowd gets wrong about [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], and on why there might be much more to Trump's overtures toward [[spirit-airlines|Spirit Airlines]] than meets the eye.[3] Burning Bright noted in the post that Ashe "loves when I pick the goofiest thumbnails."[3]

On 28 February 2026 Ashe in America co-hosted the eighth episode of The Blitz, "On the Reclaiming of American Pride & the Combating of Anti-MAGA FUD," together with [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[4] The two "held down the fort" in the absence of [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who was described as attempting to get his home back in order after a historic blizzard.[4] The resulting instalment was the shortest episode of The Blitz to that point, which the post framed as the most faithful to the format's original intent.[4]

The following week, in the ninth episode of The Blitz, published 7 March 2026 under the title "The New World Order is Dying … The New World is Beginning," Burning Bright announced his return "at the turn of the tide," framing it jokingly as a rescue of the audience "mostly from Ashe in America" after her stint standing in during his absence.[5] Ashe was not a listed participant in that episode, which paired Burning Bright with [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]].[5]

## Commentary in the July 2026 Brief

Writing in the July 10, 2026 Badlands Brief, Ashe in America called election news in an election year "always a heavy lift" but described 2026 as "downright transformational," with the topline being President [[donald-trump|Trump]] finishing off the [[election-assistance-commission|Election Assistance Commission]].[6] She characterized the EAC as the designated expert body that assures the public that code errors, glitches, and database mismatches are no big deal, arguing that such bureaucracies speak about elections with the primary goal of preserving trust "even if it means lying to the public."[6] From that premise she concluded that US elections are run in "unauditable black boxes with fake voters, fake ballots, fake counts, and a whole lot of obstruction," that firing the commissioners was not enough, and that those involved should be prosecuted — closing with "Accelerate."[6]

In the same issue she welcomed the [[department-of-labor|Department of Labor]] Inspector General's [[h-1b-visa-program|H-1B]] fraud and human trafficking investigation, reading Inspector General [[anthony-desposito|Anthony P. D'Esposito]] as front-running a "this is just paperwork" narrative from critics.[6] She tied it back to the December 2024 H-1B controversy that "nearly tore us apart and ultimately cost us [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek]]," quoting her own earlier line that "you can't have 'America First Globalism'" and arguing the 2024 outrage educated the public about corruption in US labor markets.[6]

On the Antifa convictions she wrote that although "domestic terrorism" is usually an overreach label, bombing something for political purposes genuinely qualifies — while insisting on due process for the accused and warning against extending the [[domestic-terrorism|domestic terror]] label to speech, assembly or association, as the truth community would never accept in a case like [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]].[8] "Principles are greater than politics," she concluded.[8]
