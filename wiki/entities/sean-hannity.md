# Sean Hannity

Sean Hannity is an American conservative broadcaster and longtime primetime host at [[fox-news|Fox News]]. In the Badlands corpus he stands with [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] as a representative of the establishment-aligned conservative media whose framing the Brief's commentators argue has quietly colonized parts of the [[truth-community|truth community]].

The March 16, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] mentions Hannity in a "cameo" alongside a laudatory Levin segment addressed to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cites the pairing while asking "Who is the audience of this?!" and uses it to introduce her broader argument that much of the truth community now takes its cues from Fox News, discouraging inconvenient truths during an election year and a war.
