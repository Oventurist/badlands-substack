# Sean Hannity

**Sean Hannity** is an American conservative broadcaster and commentator who appears in Badlands coverage as an antagonist of Democratic politicians and as a foil in the February 2026 dispute over remarks by California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]].[1]

## Clash with Gavin Newsom (February 2026)

After a clip circulated of Newsom telling Atlanta Mayor Andre Dickens and a reportedly majority-black audience "I'm like you … I'm no better than you. I'm a 960 SAT guy," critics — Hannity among the most prominent — accused the governor of racially condescending pandering.[1] Newsom responded on Monday with a profanity-laced post on X directed at Hannity: "You didn't give a shit about the President of the United States of America posting an ape video of President Obama or calling African nations shitholes — but you're going to call me racist for talking about my lifelong struggle with dyslexia?"[1] The governor closed with, "Spare me your fake fucking outrage, Sean."[1]

The Brief noted that Republican pundits, Hannity included, used the episode to spotlight what they characterized as Democratic double standards on matters of race, while Newsom's office dismissed the criticism as manufactured outrage and pointed out that he had discussed his dyslexia and test scores publicly for years.[1]

## Comer and Jordan interview, July 2023

On Monday, July 31, 2023, Hannity hosted House Oversight chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] and House Judiciary chairman [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]] on his Fox News Channel program and pressed them on whether the inquiry had become "officially the Joe Biden bribery allegation" and whether they could prove it.[2] Both indicated they believed they could, and Jordan agreed with Hannity's suggestion that the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] had been helping to cover it up, an exchange that framed subsequent coverage of the [[joe-biden-bribery-allegation|Joe Biden bribery allegation]].[2]
