# Cassidy Hutchinson

Cassidy Hutchinson is a former White House aide whose testimony before the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]] became one of the most widely publicized moments of the committee's prime-time hearings.[1]

## Disputed testimony

Preliminary findings released in March 2024 by the [[house-administration-subcommittee-on-oversight|House Administration Subcommittee on Oversight]] concluded that the committee promoted Hutchinson's sensational testimony despite the fact that it was directly contradicted by the [[united-states-secret-service|Secret Service]] agent driving the presidential SUV.[1] The subcommittee treated this as one instance of a broader pattern in which the committee, led by Chairman [[bennie-thompson|Bennie Thompson]] and Vice Chair [[liz-cheney|Liz Cheney]], elevated favorable accounts while suppressing transcribed interviews that contradicted its narrative.[1]
