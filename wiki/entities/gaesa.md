# GAESA

GAESA (Grupo de Administración Empresarial S.A.) is [[cuba|Cuba]]'s military-industrial conglomerate, the holding company through which the Cuban armed forces control a large share of the island's commercial economy, including tourism, retail, remittances, and port and shipping operations.

## Role in the 2026 sanctions campaign

According to the [[badlands-brief-257|Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026]], GAESA is the central target of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's economic pressure campaign against Cuba. On May 1, 2026, Trump signed an executive order imposing secondary sanctions on companies doing business with the conglomerate — that is, penalizing third-country firms for their commercial relationships with it rather than only prohibiting U.S. persons from dealing with it.

The measure produced rapid effects. The Canadian miner [[sherritt-international|Sherritt International]] and the European shipping lines [[cma-cgm|CMA CGM]] and [[hapag-lloyd|Hapag-Lloyd]] suspended operations in Cuba, with further withdrawals expected from banks and hotel operators. The administration additionally terminated the U.S. green card of a GAESA executive. These steps form part of a broader strategy described internally as "[[accelerationism|accelerationism]]," aimed at methodically constricting the Cuban economy to create conditions for a peaceful transition away from the current government.
