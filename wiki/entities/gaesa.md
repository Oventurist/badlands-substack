# GAESA

GAESA is [[cuba|Cuba]]'s military-industrial conglomerate and the central target of the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's 2026 economic pressure campaign against the Cuban regime.[1]

## Secondary sanctions

On May 1, 2026, President Trump signed an executive order imposing secondary sanctions on companies doing business with GAESA.[1] The measure prompted several foreign firms — including Canadian miner Sherritt International and European shipping companies CMA CGM and Hapag-Lloyd — to suspend operations in Cuba, with additional pullouts expected from banks and hotel operators.[1]

Related measures included the cutoff of Venezuelan oil shipments to Cuba, a [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] indictment of [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]] over the 1996 Brothers to the Rescue shootdowns, and the termination of a GAESA executive's U.S. green card.[1] The administration also stepped up enforcement of existing sanctions while conducting multiagency planning for potential unrest on the island.[1]

Because GAESA controls a large share of Cuba's hard-currency economy, squeezing it is the operational core of the strategy senior officials described as [[accelerationism|"accelerationism"]] — constricting the economy in stages to create conditions for a peaceful transition rather than immediate regime elimination.[1]
