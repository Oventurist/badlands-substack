# Calley Means

Calley Means is a health care entrepreneur who advised [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] on chronic disease policy and who played the role of intermediary in the formation of Kennedy's 2024 alliance with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Role in the Trump–Kennedy alliance

According to reporting by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]], about three hours after Trump was nearly assassinated on a Saturday evening in mid-July 2024, Means telephoned Kennedy to ask whether he would consider joining forces with Trump, and whether he would serve as Trump's running mate.[1] Means suggested that the moment called for unity, noting that Trump had just narrowly escaped the same fate that had befallen Kennedy's father and uncle.[1] Kennedy, then running an independent campaign for president, said he was not interested in the vice presidency and the call ended; a short while later he called back and agreed to speak with Trump.[1]

The overture set off what the Times described as a frenzy of calculations and soul-searching inside the Kennedy camp over whether an alliance with Trump would give Kennedy more power to address chronic disease, censorship, corporate reach into government agencies and the war in Ukraine — or whether it would tear apart his coalition and his family.[1] Trump was not at that point seriously considering adding Kennedy to the ticket, but Means's efforts presented an opening to bring Kennedy into the fold and remove him as a potential drain on Trump's votes.[1]
