# Fortress Investment Group

Fortress Investment Group is an American investment management firm identified in May 2023 reporting as the largest debt holder of the digital media company [[vice-media|Vice Media]].[1]

## Role in the Vice Media collapse

When the New York Times reported that Vice was preparing to file for bankruptcy, it noted that in the event of such a filing Fortress Investment Group could end up controlling the company.[1] The firm's position as principal creditor placed it ahead of Vice's equity investors, which included Walt Disney Co. and Fox Corp., whose stakes were reported as potentially worthless.[1] The situation illustrated a familiar pattern in distressed media: a company once valued at $5.7 billion passing, through the mechanics of debt, into the hands of its lenders.[1]

The [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief that carried the report framed the episode as part of a broader "media death spiral," with contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] asking whether the money faucet sustaining outlets like Vice had simply run dry.[1]

## Attempted sale of Refinery29 (January 2024)

Having taken over [[vice-media|Vice Media]] in bankruptcy in 2023, Fortress was by January 2024 seeking to sell the company's [[refinery29|Refinery29]] women's lifestyle site, according to Wall Street Journal reporting.[2] The move followed a failed attempt to find a buyer for Vice in its entirety, a package that included the namesake news brand, the production studio and the creative agency.[2] Fortress was in discussions with prospective bidders for Refinery29, whose revenue had declined to roughly $30 million from about $50 million the previous year.[2]
