# Hilton Phoenix Chandler

The **Hilton Phoenix Chandler** is a hotel and conference venue in [[chandler-arizona|Chandler, Arizona]], operated under the Hilton brand of [[hilton-worldwide|Hilton Worldwide]].[1] It appears in the Badlands Media corpus as the site of the inaugural stop of the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]].[1]

## GART AZ (April 20–23, 2023)

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] held its first in-person, ticketed conference at the Hilton Phoenix Chandler from April 20 to 23, 2023, using the hotel's event facilities for a weekend of live in-person versions of Badlands shows, panel Q&A sessions and host meet-and-greets.[1] The programme included a Friday night Welcome Reception together with breakfast and lunch across the weekend for in-person ticket holders, and a separately ticketed VIP Dinner for a limited number of guests.[1] Badlands emphasised that General Admission guests would still have ample opportunity to meet and spend time with the network's show hosts, writers and personalities, the VIP dinner being simply an additional event.[1]

Registration for the event was handled through a TicketSpice page, with a hotel booking link supplied via Hilton's "attend my event" system, and a virtual live stream was sold separately for remote viewers.[1]
