# PJM Interconnection

PJM Interconnection is the operator of the largest electric grid in the United States, serving more than 67 million people across the mid-Atlantic and parts of the Midwest.

The January 19, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and a bipartisan coalition of governors announced efforts to confront rising electricity costs attributed in part to the rapid expansion of energy-intensive artificial-intelligence data centres. Officials pressed PJM to increase power supplies and slow rising consumer prices by requiring technology companies to help fund new power generation.

The coalition further urged PJM to hold an emergency energy auction that would require data centre operators to bid on long-term contracts financing up to $15 billion in new power plants, shifting more of the financial burden onto technology firms rather than ordinary ratepayers. The item appears in the Brief alongside [[openai|OpenAI]]'s advertising announcement and [[melania-trump|Melania Trump]]'s comments on AI as part of a section on the technology's expanding legal and economic footprint.
