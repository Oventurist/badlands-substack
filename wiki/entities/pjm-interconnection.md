# PJM Interconnection

PJM Interconnection is the operator of the largest electric grid in the United States, serving more than 67 million people.[1]

In January 2026 the Trump administration and a bipartisan coalition of governors announced efforts to confront rising electricity costs partly attributed to the rapid expansion of energy-intensive [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] data centers, pressuring PJM to increase power supplies and slow rising consumer prices by making technology companies help fund new power generation.[1] The coalition also urged PJM to hold an emergency energy auction that would require data center operators to bid on long-term contracts financing up to $15 billion in new power plants, shifting more of the financial burden onto tech firms rather than everyday ratepayers.[1]

## Interconnection queue and the Three Mile Island restart (2024)

PJM Interconnection operates the electrical grid used by residents of Pennsylvania and several other states, and its interconnection queue became a bottleneck for projects seeking to come online.[2] In September 2024, after [[microsoft|Microsoft]] agreed to buy power from the shuttered [[three-mile-island-nuclear-power-plant|Three Mile Island]] plant, Pennsylvania Governor [[josh-shapiro|Josh Shapiro]] wrote to PJM's leaders asking that the reactor be allowed to skip the line rather than wait as if it were an entirely new development.[2] PJM said in response that it was working on a "fast track" system that would give priority to some electricity projects — a change that could allow reactors to jump ahead at a time when slow grid connections were holding back many electricity projects for years.[2]
