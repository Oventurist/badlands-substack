# Bush family

The Bush family is an American political family centered on [[george-hw-bush]] (41st U.S. President, 1989–1993) and [[george-w-bush]] (43rd U.S. President, 2001–2009), as well as [[jeb-bush]], governor of Florida (1999–2007). In the Badlands Media corpus, the family is characterized as a multigenerational network of political operatives with deep, sustained ties to the [[cia]], international drug trafficking, money laundering, and covert regime influence in Mexico and Venezuela.

## George H.W. Bush and the CIA

According to the source a family affair, [[george-hw-bush]] was a leading CIA figure from the early 1950s onward, long before his official appointment as CIA director in 1976. The article links him to the [[bay-of-pigs-invasion]] through his ownership of [[zapata-offshore]], a company whose ships were loaned to the CIA for the 1961 operation and whose name was adopted as the invasion's code name, [[operation-zapata]]. The source notes that Bush consistently denied CIA membership prior to 1976.

## Texas National Bank and Texas Commerce Bank

The family's Houston base is presented as the financial infrastructure for these operations. [[texas-national-bank]], created in 1953, financed the early development of [[zapata-offshore]] and maintained "mutually profitable working ties" with the Bush oil company in the late 1950s. After merging into [[texas-commerce-bank]] in 1964, the institution is described as a CIA bank staffed by political figures such as [[james-baker]]'s family and board members including Lady Bird Johnson and Gerald Ford.

## Jeb Bush and covert operations

[[jeb-bush]], the son of George H.W. Bush, is portrayed as a CIA "non-official cover" officer placed at Texas Commerce Bank. At age 24, he was dispatched to Caracas, Venezuela, as branch manager and vice president of the bank's Venezuelan operation. The article claims he served as the CIA's main financial liaison to the Venezuelan oil industry and Colombian drug cartels, helping to establish money-laundering links between the CIA and the [[medellin-cartel]] and [[cali-cartel]]. His work in Venezuela allegedly laid the groundwork for the Reagan-Bush administration's covert war against Nicaragua and leftist guerrillas in El Salvador.

## Political influence and foreign connections

The Bush family is linked in the source to corrupt foreign leaders including [[carlos-salinas]] (Mexico) and [[carlos-andres-perez]] (Venezuela), both described as CIA-installed or CIA-supported figures whose regimes facilitated drug trafficking and embezzlement. The family's Miami network, built through Cuban exile connections, is tied to Medicare fraud schemes such as that run by [[miguel-recarey]], to whom [[jeb-bush]] allegedly provided political cover.

## Financial crisis connections

The article notes that [[jeb-bush]] earned more than $14 million as an adviser to [[lehman-brothers]] before and after its 2008 bankruptcy, which triggered the global financial crisis. Bush's role in "Project Verde"—an unsuccessful effort to secure a bailout from [[carlos-slim]]—is cited as evidence of the family's entanglement in high-stakes financial collapse.
