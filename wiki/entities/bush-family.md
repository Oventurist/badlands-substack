# Bush Family

The Bush family is the subject of "A Family Affair," which applies the Q maxim "Follow the family" alongside "follow the money" as an investigative method: family connections are said to reveal who is loyal to the country and who are traitors, and to expose corruption hidden beneath the mainstream media narrative, citing Q drop #3381 as an example.[1]

The article's central contrast is with the Biden family, whose corruption the media has been forced to acknowledge and whose crimes appear rooted in greed. Bush family corruption, by comparison, is described as "far more evil": involving far more money, indifference to human life, long-standing immunity from the law, and treason.[1] Hunter Biden's reputed ten percent cut is dismissed as amateurish next to [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]]'s forty percent stake in [[armando-codina|Armando Codina]]'s firm, obtained without contributing a dime.[1]

The family's institutional footprint in the article runs through Houston: [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]]'s CIA front company [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Offshore]], the [[texas-national-bank|Texas National Bank]] created the same year, and its successor [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]], founded by the family of [[james-baker|James Baker]] and used as CIA cover and as a depository for [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] money.[1] Its foreign entanglements run through Mexico's [[salinas-family|Salinas family]] and Venezuela's [[carlos-andres-perez|Carlos Andrés Pérez]], and its domestic patronage through Cuban exile businessmen in Miami.[1] The author promises a following article on another financial crisis centered on the family, and on what he believes is Donald Trump's plan to return the power of the banking system to the people.[1]
