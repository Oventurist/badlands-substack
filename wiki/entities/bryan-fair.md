# Bryan Fair

**Bryan Fair** is the chief executive officer of the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] (SPLC), the Montgomery, Alabama-based nonprofit best known for its hate-group designations and civil rights litigation.

Fair publicly confirmed the federal criminal probe and the 11-count indictment returned against the organization in 2026, which charged wire fraud, bank fraud, and conspiracy to commit money laundering in connection with more than $3 million allegedly funneled between 2014 and 2023 to paid informants and leaders inside the [[ku-klux-klan|Ku Klux Klan]], neo-Nazi formations, and other groups the SPLC publicly claimed to oppose. He characterized the case as centering on the organization's past use of confidential informants to gather intelligence on violent extremist groups, said the program was kept confidential to protect sources and prevent violence, noted that findings were shared with federal and local law enforcement, and vowed that the SPLC would not be "intimidated."

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read Fair's confirmation as an effective admission of the underlying conduct, leaving only its lawfulness open for judicial review.
