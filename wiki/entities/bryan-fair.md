# Bryan Fair

Bryan Fair is the chief executive officer of the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] (SPLC).[1]

In April 2026 Fair confirmed the federal probe and 11-count indictment against the organization, saying it centered on the SPLC's past use of confidential informants to gather intelligence on violent extremist groups.[1] He vowed that the SPLC would not be "intimidated."[1] Commentators noted that in confirming the informant program, the organization had effectively admitted the underlying conduct charged in the indictment, leaving its lawfulness for judicial review.[1]

## Response to the April 2026 indictment

Described as the SPLC's interim president and chief executive, Fair denied wrongdoing after the Justice Department announced its 11-count indictment on April 21, 2026, calling the allegations false and politically motivated.[2] He said the organization's informants had been used to gather intelligence on violent threats and to help protect staff, and stated that the practice had ended.[2] Prosecutors allege the SPLC secretly routed more than $3 million between 2014 and 2023 to paid informants inside extremist groups including the Ku Klux Klan and neo-Nazi organizations while misleading donors about how contributions were used.[2] The indictment drew scrutiny from legal observers, including a former DOJ Fraud Section chief who, in an analysis published by Just Security, questioned whether the allegations satisfy customary federal fraud standards.[2]
