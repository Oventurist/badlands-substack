# Al-Qaeda in the Islamic Maghreb

Al-Qaeda in the Islamic Maghreb (AQIM) is the North African regional affiliate of [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]], active across Algeria, Mali, Niger and neighbouring states, and identified in parts of the Badlands Media corpus as a participant in the September 11, 2012 attack in [[benghazi|Benghazi]].

## Role in the Badlands corpus

[[a-dereliction-of-duty|A Dereliction of Duty]] cites Investors Business Daily for the claim that "using heavy arms seized from the Libyan army that Obama helped topple, al-Qaida in the Islamic Maghreb, or AQIM, hit the American consulate in Benghazi and assassinated the U.S. ambassador and three of his aides" — the first time in thirty-three years that an American ambassador had died in the line of duty.

The article uses AQIM to make two connected arguments. First, that the arsenal of the fallen [[muammar-gaddafi|Gaddafi]] government, including shoulder-fired surface-to-air missiles, passed directly to Al Qaeda affiliates as a foreseeable consequence of the 2011 intervention — a danger, the article notes, that former CIA officers repeatedly tried to bring to the attention of the [[white-house|White House]] without effect. Second, that the group responsible for killing Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] belonged to the same network that the [[state-department|State Department]] under [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was allegedly arming, which the article presents as the reason no rescue was mounted and no candid account was given afterwards.
