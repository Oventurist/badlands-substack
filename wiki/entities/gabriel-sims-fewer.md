# Gabriel Sims-Fewer

Gabriel Sims-Fewer is the owner of [[the-anarchist-cafe|The Anarchist]], a self-described "anti-capitalist, anti-colonial" cafe and "radical community space" in Toronto that announced its permanent closure in May 2023 after roughly a year of operation.[1]

Sims-Fewer had introduced a "pay what you can" coffee offering, which he conceded cost the business money and was subsidised by higher-priced menu items.[1] Announcing the shutdown on the cafe's website, he blamed a shortage of outside capital rather than the business model, writing that "the lack of generational wealth/seed capital from ethically bankrupt sources left me unable to weather the quiet winter season, or to grow in the ways needed to be sustainable longer-term."[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the statement as an illustration of the reasoning of "thoroughly indoctrinated socialist types" who cannot process the failure of their own model.[1]
