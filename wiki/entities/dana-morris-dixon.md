# Dana Morris Dixon

Dana Morris Dixon is Jamaica's education minister. Following the landfall of [[hurricane-melissa|Hurricane Melissa]] in Jamaica on October 28, 2025, Morris Dixon said that 77 percent of the island was still without power the next day.[1] Prime Minister [[andrew-holness|Andrew Holness]] declared the country a disaster area in response to the storm.[1]
