# Bilderberg

Bilderberg, the annual private conference of North American and European political, financial and industrial leaders, is cited in the Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" as one of the "prestigious deep political think tanks" at which the foreign policy establishment convenes.

The article's specific claim is that [[tom-donilon]] — Obama's National Security Advisor, a key policy figure behind the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran Deal]], and later chairman of the BlackRock Investment Institute — has been a regular fixture at Bilderberg and sat on its steering committee. It groups Bilderberg with the [[council-on-foreign-relations]], the [[trilateral-commission]] and the [[world-economic-forum]] as the venues where "the heads of industry, finance, military, intelligence and politics convene."
