# 8VC

8VC is a venture capital firm founded by [[joe-lonsdale|Joe Lonsdale]], a co-founder of [[palantir|Palantir]].[1] The firm's founder was identified in July 2025 reporting as one of the high-profile technology investors backing [[erebor|Erebor]], a proposed bank for start-ups and cryptocurrency businesses organized by [[palmer-luckey|Palmer Luckey]].[1]

## X shareholder disclosure (2024)

8VC, the venture capital firm co-founded by [[joe-lonsdale|Joe Lonsdale]], was named among the stakeholders in X Holdings Corp. when a federal court ordered [[x-corp|X]] to unseal its investor list in August 2024.[2] Coverage highlighted Lonsdale's role as a co-founder of the intelligence contractor and data analysis platform [[palantir|Palantir]].[2]
