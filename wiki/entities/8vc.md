# 8VC

8VC is a venture capital firm founded by [[joe-lonsdale|Joe Lonsdale]], a co-founder of [[palantir|Palantir]].[1] The firm's founder was identified in July 2025 reporting as one of the high-profile technology investors backing [[erebor|Erebor]], a proposed bank for start-ups and cryptocurrency businesses organized by [[palmer-luckey|Palmer Luckey]].[1]
