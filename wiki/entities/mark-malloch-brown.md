# Mark Malloch Brown

Lord Mark Malloch Brown is a British diplomat, former United Nations official and, since 2021, president of the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]].[1] In Badlands commentary by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] he is presented as the most damning single link between [[george-soros|George Soros]] and the machinery of elections, migration and international institutions.[1]

## Early life and education

Malloch Brown was born in the West End of London in 1953.[1] He graduated from Cambridge before taking a master's degree in political science at the University of Michigan.[1] Carlson finds it odd that someone born and raised in England would move from Cambridge to Michigan, suggesting it fits a pattern of early commissioning into "the team designated to destroy America."[1]

## Career

Fresh out of college, Malloch Brown worked as a political correspondent before taking a post at the [[united-nations|United Nations]] as high commissioner for refugees.[1] Carlson notes the irony that Soros — barred from several countries whose governments accuse him of deliberately attempting to destroy them through mass migration — would later be closely associated with a man who had held the UN refugee portfolio.[1]

He then became the lead international partner at the US-based Sawyer-Miller Group Communication, a firm that applied American-style election campaign methods to foreign governments, companies and public policy debates.[1] Carlson argues this lines up precisely with Soros and [[color-revolution|color revolutions]], adding that Malloch Brown, like Soros, focused on Eastern Bloc countries and Russia.[1]

Malloch Brown subsequently worked at the [[world-bank|World Bank]] before returning to the United Nations as Deputy Secretary-General.[1] Carlson describes him as connected to every arm of the Cabal — "a Cabal Swiss Army Knife, going wherever the Cabal needs him."[1] In 2010 he became a consultant to oil companies and was later sued, settling an allegation that he had passed Soros confidential information subsequently used in a smear campaign against his client — despite his not having officially worked with Soros in any capacity at that point.[1]

## Smartmatic and Open Society

In 2014 Malloch Brown became chairman of the board of directors of SGO Corporation Limited, a holding company whose primary asset is the election technology and voting machine manufacturer [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]].[1] He has also served as chair of the Royal African Society and as a member of the Executive Committee of the International Crisis Group, becoming co-chair of that group's Board of Trustees in July 2014.[1]

In December 2020 he was announced as the successor to Patrick Gaspard as president of the Open Society Foundations, effective 1 January 2021.[1] Carlson asks why Soros would remove Gaspard — a man with ties to the Obama administration and the DNC — and replace him with the effective chairman of an election-counting software company immediately after the 2020 election, speculating that the move was intended either to distance Barack Obama from the Biden presidency or to place Malloch Brown where he could more easily censor election fraud information and debate.[1]
