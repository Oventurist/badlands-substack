# Bahrain

Bahrain is a Gulf kingdom and one of the original signatories of the [[abraham-accords|Abraham Accords]], the normalisation agreements with [[israel|Israel]] brokered under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## Role in the 2026 Middle East negotiations

According to the Badlands Brief edition "[[badlands-brief-3d4|Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns]]," Trump held discussions with King [[hamad-bin-isa-al-khalifa|Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa]] of Bahrain as part of a round of talks with regional leaders on a comprehensive settlement with [[iran|Iran]]. In his subsequent [[truth-social|Truth Social]] statement Trump listed Bahrain among the countries that should sign onto the Abraham Accords simultaneously, while noting parenthetically that it is "already a Member." He cited Bahrain alongside the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], [[morocco|Morocco]], [[sudan|Sudan]] and [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]] as evidence that membership had delivered "a Financial, Economic, and Social BOOM" without any member suggesting withdrawal.
