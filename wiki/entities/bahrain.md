# Bahrain

Bahrain is a Gulf state that became a target of Iranian action during the June 2026 escalation surrounding the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]

The Guardian, cited in the Badlands Brief, reported that a residential building in Muharraq, Bahrain, was struck by an Iranian drone, according to Bahrain's Interior Ministry.[1] The strike came amid several days of renewed hostilities that followed an earlier memorandum of understanding intended to end the U.S.–[[iran|Iran]] conflict, with differing interpretations of the agreement over the Strait of Hormuz cited as a contributing factor.[1]

Bahrain had also been among the states represented at a meeting in the [[united-arab-emirates|UAE]] convened by Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], alongside officials from the UAE and [[kuwait|Kuwait]].[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observed that Iran subsequently struck targets specifically in Kuwait and Bahrain — the two countries he characterized as having colluded to subvert [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s peace framework — and argued that Iran appeared to be acting as an enforcer on Trump's behalf against opponents of his peace plan.[1]
