# Andris Spruds

Andris Spruds is the Defense Minister of [[latvia|Latvia]] and a member of the Progressives party.[1]

In September 2025 he told Delfi TV that his party would submit a proposal the following year requiring both men and women to serve in the military from 2028, extending compulsory military service beyond the male-only conscription Riga reintroduced in 2023.[1] He noted that female-specific equipment was already being introduced into the National Armed Forces in preparation for the change.[1] The move aligns Latvia with other [[nato|NATO]] members expanding their forces in response to what they describe as a Russian threat, and would follow [[denmark|Denmark]]'s decision to begin drafting women from 2026.[1]
