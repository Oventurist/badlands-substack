# Jamie Dimon

Jamie Dimon is the chairman and chief executive officer of [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]], the largest United States bank by assets, and one of the most prominent figures in American finance. He is a named defendant in President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s $5 billion lawsuit over alleged political debanking.

## The $5 billion lawsuit

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Trump filed suit against JPMorgan Chase and Dimon on Thursday, January 22, 2026, in Florida state court in Miami, accusing the bank of cutting off his accounts for political reasons "after decades-long business ties." The complaint alleges JPMorgan abruptly terminated multiple accounts belonging to Trump and his hospitality companies in early 2021 despite their being in good standing, and alleges that Dimon and the bank caused Trump, the [[trump-organization|Trump Organization]], affiliated entities, and Trump family members to be placed on an internal banking blacklist shared across federally regulated institutions.

Trump's legal team, led by attorney [[alejandro-brito|Alejandro Brito]], argues the closures were driven by political and social considerations rather than legitimate financial concerns, at a moment when distancing the bank from Trump was politically advantageous. In a statement attributed to JPMorgan Chase — which Trump's lawyers identify with Dimon's leadership — the bank said: "While we regret President Trump has sued us, we believe the suit has no merit. We respect the president's right to sue us and our right to defend ourselves — that's what courts are for."
