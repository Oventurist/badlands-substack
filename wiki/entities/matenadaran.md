# Matenadaran

The **Matenadaran** is a purpose-built storehouse in modern [[armenia|Armenia]] that holds the country's historic manuscripts.[1] Thousands of the books and documents it preserves were carried out of Ottoman territory by ethnic Armenians fleeing the mass killings that began in 1915; some survivors hid manuscripts inside their clothing, and for many the easily concealed documents were the only remnant of their nation they were able to smuggle to safety.[1]

## Digitization project

As reported by Radio Free Europe and carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, experts at the Matenadaran are involved in a groundbreaking project to place all accessible Armenian manuscripts — ranging from ancient bibles to medieval cake recipes — into a free, searchable online database.[1] Many relevant manuscripts remain scattered in museums and private collections around the world.[1]
