# Paul Clement

**Paul Clement** is an American appellate attorney with the Washington firm of Clement and Murphy who frequently argues before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]].[1] He served as U.S. solicitor general under President George W. Bush from July 2004 to June 2008.[1]

## Heritage Foundation panel on the administrative state (2023)

Clement participated in a September 20, 2023 event hosted by [[heritage-foundation|The Heritage Foundation]] at which lawyers who regularly appear before the Court predicted that the justices might take steps to roll back the [[administrative-state|administrative state]] in the term beginning the following month.[1] He appeared alongside [[lisa-blatt|Lisa Blatt]], a partner at Williams and Connolly.[1] A central case in that discussion was [[sec-v-jarkesy|Securities and Exchange Commission v. Jarkesy]], in which a divided Fifth Circuit had held that removal protections for administrative law judges unconstitutionally shielded them from presidential oversight and that in-house [[securities-and-exchange-commission|SEC]] proceedings violated the Seventh Amendment right to a jury trial.[1]
