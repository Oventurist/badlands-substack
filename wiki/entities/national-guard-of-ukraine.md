# National Guard of Ukraine

The National Guard of Ukraine is a Ukrainian military force that, under a February 2024 decree, was opened to foreign nationals amid a severe manpower crisis in the war with Russia.[1]

## Opening to foreigners (February 2024)

President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] signed a decree on Wednesday, February 21, 2024 opening Ukraine's military forces to "foreigners and stateless persons," allowing them to volunteer for service in the National Guard.[1] Recruits could sign contracts at the private, sergeant or officer levels depending on their qualifications, with an initial three-year term and commitments of up to five years for higher ranks.[1] Ukrainian media reported that to join, foreign citizens needed to apply to the territorial center for recruitment and social support at their place of residence, or directly to the unit in which they wished to serve.[1] Reports added that "foreign women can also be recruited for military service if there are vacant female military positions."[1]

## Context

The measure was presented as an alternative to a domestic mass mobilization and conscription drive, which Ukraine's leadership had long weighed but which faced immense pushback from a war-weary population.[1] It came as Ukrainian troop losses were described as long past crisis levels and forces were retreating in areas outside [[avdiivka|Avdiivka]] in Donetsk after Russia captured the city.[1] The report was published by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and carried as a bonus item in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of February 23, 2024.[1]
