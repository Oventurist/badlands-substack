# Betsy DeVos

**Betsy DeVos** served as United States Secretary of Education in the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Relationship to Erik Prince

Badlands Media's [[purple-revolution|Purple Revolution]] analysis notes DeVos primarily as the sister of [[erik-prince|Erik Prince]], founder of the private military company [[blackwater|Blackwater]].[1] The article treats her appointment as part of a pattern in which Trump drew on the Prince family and the private military sphere rather than on institutions the author regards as compromised, observing that Trump "went on to hire Erik Prince's sister Betsy DeVos to be the US Secretary of Education."[1] The same passage records that Trump pardoned four former Blackwater employees convicted over a 2007 shooting in Iraq in which 17 people were killed.[1]
