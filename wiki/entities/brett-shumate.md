# Brett Shumate

Brett Shumate is an Assistant Attorney General at the [[department-of-justice|United States Department of Justice]]. He authored a sharply worded letter demanding that the [[national-trust-for-historic-preservation|National Trust for Historic Preservation]] withdraw its lawsuit seeking to halt the privately funded [[white-house-ballroom|White House ballroom project]].[1]

In the letter, Shumate said the lawsuit places "the lives of the president, his family, and his staff at grave risk," called on the group to immediately dismiss the "frivolous lawsuit," and cited heightened security concerns following the recent assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|President Trump]].[1] He argued that recent security incidents reinforce the administration's position that the ballroom is necessary to reduce risks associated with presidential travel to external venues.[1] The Justice Department warned that if the case were not withdrawn by Monday morning it would move to dissolve the injunction blocking construction and seek dismissal in court.[1]
