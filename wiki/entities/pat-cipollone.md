# Pat Cipollone

Pat Cipollone is an American attorney who served as White House counsel during the final two years of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Move to the Torridon Group

In January 2024 Cipollone was announced as one of several Trump-administration veterans joining the [[torridon-group|Torridon Group]], the law and consulting firm founded by former Attorney General [[bill-barr|William Barr]] and [[ted-ullyot|Ted Ullyot]].[1] He joined alongside former deputy White House counsels [[pat-philbin|Pat Philbin]] and [[kate-todd|Kate Todd]] as the firm expanded into Washington with a full litigation, regulatory, investigations and crisis-management practice.[1]
