# World Health Organization

The World Health Organization (WHO) is the United Nations' specialised agency for international public health. In the Badlands Media corpus it is treated as one of the principal supranational institutions through which globalist governance is said to be advanced, particularly in connection with pandemic policy and health mandates.

In AI Can Help Us Expose the NWO, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] adds the WHO — together with the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and [[united-nations|the United Nations]] — to the fictional scenario he constructed with [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]], on the grounds that [[toronto-protocols|the Toronto Protocols]] explicitly describe an intention to work through existing globalist institutions. In the AI's synthesised output, the WHO is one of the NGOs and initiatives that the fictional Global Harmony Consortium employs at the strategic, operational and tactical levels, with COVID mandates cited as measures "presented as necessary for the greater good" that subordinate individual rights to collective safety.

## The US withdrawal (January 2026)

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the United States officially withdrew from the WHO effective Thursday, January 22, 2026, following the one-year notice period required by US law. The notice period began on President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first day in office, when he issued an executive order notifying the [[united-nations|United Nations]] of the US intent to exit. The [[white-house|White House]] posted on X: "EFFECTIVE TODAY: The United States has exited the World Health Organization. This fulfills President Trump's commitment under an executive order signed one year ago, following the WHO's mishandling of COVID-19 and its ongoing lack of reform, accountability, & transparency."

A joint statement by Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] said the WHO "abandoned its core mission and acted repeatedly against the interests of the United States," describing the organization as having pursued "a politicized, bureaucratic agenda driven by nations hostile to American interests," obstructing the timely sharing of critical information during the pandemic, and — on the way out — refusing to hand over the American flag that hung in front of its headquarters, arguing it had not approved the withdrawal and claiming the US owed it compensation. The statement said future US engagement with the WHO would be "limited strictly to effectuate our withdrawal and to safeguard the health and safety of the American people."

The Brief notes the exit has triggered a financial crisis at the WHO, of which the United States has historically been the largest donor. Contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] celebrated the departure, calling the WHO "a vehicle for the driving the global change management for the UN Sustainable Development Goals," and catalogued what she called the organization's "greatest hits" during COVID: claiming no evidence of human-to-human transmission, flip-flopping on masking guidance, redefining herd immunity to push vaccination, praising China's transparency, promoting the "infodemic" concept to limit scientific debate, and renaming monkeypox to "mpox."
