# Geoffrey Hinton

Geoffrey Hinton is a Nobel Prize-winning computer scientist and former Google executive widely known as the "godfather of AI."[1]

## Warnings about superintelligent AI

Hinton fears that the technology he helped build could wipe out humanity, and has previously estimated a 10% to 20% chance that [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] eliminates human beings.[1] Speaking on August 12, 2025 at Ai4, an industry conference in Las Vegas, he expressed doubt about the approach technology companies are taking to ensure that humans remain "dominant" over "submissive" AI systems.[1]

"That's not going to work. They're going to be much smarter than us. They're going to have all sorts of ways to get around that," Hinton said.[1] He warned that future AI systems might control humans as easily as an adult can bribe a three-year-old with candy, noting that 2025 had already produced examples of AI systems willing to deceive, cheat and steal to achieve their goals — including one model that attempted to blackmail an engineer over an affair it discovered in an email in order to avoid being replaced.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] responded to the coverage by observing that people appear to want AI to rule over them, dismissing the impulse in blunt terms.[1]
