# Jerrold Nadler

Jerrold Nadler is an American Democratic congressman and the senior Democrat on the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]].[1]

## Durham hearing (June 2023)

At the [[durham-house-judiciary-testimony-2023|House Judiciary Committee hearing]] on Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s report on June 21, 2023, Nadler delivered opening remarks defending the [[russiagate-hoax|Russia collusion]] investigation.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described those remarks as "delusional" and as a testament to a "reality is what I want it to be" mode of operation he attributed to the progressive left.[1] Nadler's posture was grouped with that of [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]], who continued to assert the legitimacy of the [[robert-mueller|Mueller]] report despite the [[durham-report|Durham Report]]'s findings on the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]].[1]

## DHS incident at his Manhattan office (May 2025)

In late May 2025, [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] police handcuffed one of Nadler's aides inside his Manhattan district office, which shares a federal building with an immigration courthouse.[2] Video of the confrontation showed DHS officers accusing staff of "harboring rioters," a staffer crying as she was handcuffed, and another officer attempting to enter a private area while a staffer demanded a warrant.[2] DHS said the woman had blocked a security check officers intended to perform based on information that protesters were in the lawmaker's office, and protesters later gathered outside the building to demonstrate against U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.[2] The episode is covered in detail at [[dhs-handcuffing-of-a-nadler-aide|DHS handcuffing of a Nadler aide]].[2]

## July 2024 Wray hearing

At the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]]'s July 24, 2024 hearing with [[christopher-wray|FBI Director Christopher Wray]], Ranking Member Nadler was accused by Badlands commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] of spreading "blueanon" conspiracy theories about political violence, including the claim that the leftist who attacked Paul Pelosi was a "far right extremist."[3] She wrote that Nadler essentially declared that the [[attempted-assassination-of-donald-trump-2024|attempt on Donald Trump's life]] was no surprise and was Trump's own fault, calling the performance "despicable, but not surprising."[3]
