# Max Blumenthal

Max Blumenthal is an American journalist and the editor of [[the-grayzone|The Grayzone]], an independent outlet known for anti-interventionist reporting.[1]

## Alleged targeting by USAID-funded smear operations

In February 2025, as [[usaid|USAID]] came under intense scrutiny during the opening month of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term, the agency was linked to funding groups that engaged in smear campaigns and censorship efforts directed at American journalists and politicians.[1] These operations were said to target individuals holding anti-establishment views on both sides of the political aisle, with accusations of "disinformation" levelled against figures including Blumenthal and [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]].[1]

The allegation forms part of a wider critique of the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship industrial complex]], in which government aid money is said to have subsidized private intermediaries that police domestic political speech.[1]
