# Maye Musk

Maye Musk (née Haldeman) is the mother of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the daughter of the Canadian Technocracy organizer [[joshua-haldeman|Joshua Haldeman]].[1] Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats her family lineage as the key to understanding the ideological environment in which her son was raised.[1]

## Background

Maye Haldeman was born into a wealthy Canadian family in Regina, Saskatchewan in 1947, one of twin sisters (Maye and Kaye).[1] Her father was an aviation enthusiast, chiropractor and social reformer who led the Saskatchewan branch of the Canadian Fabian movement before joining [[technocracy-inc|Technocracy Inc.]] in 1936.[1] In 1949 the family relocated to apartheid South Africa, where they settled in the wealthiest neighbourhood of Pretoria.[1]

## In Ehret's argument

Ehret writes that Joshua Haldeman's adopted South Africa "awarded him with riches, his daughter with national beauty queen status, his son-in-law with an emerald mine," pointing to Maye's career as a model and beauty queen and to her marriage to [[errol-musk|Errol Musk]].[1] He observes that no member of the family — from Joshua Haldeman to Maye Musk to Errol Musk to Elon Musk, who lived in South Africa until the age of seventeen — has ever offered a critical word about the [[apartheid|apartheid]] regime in any of their writings or interviews.[1]

Ehret also cites what he calls his mother's "proclivities to Satanic symbology" as one of the reasons some observers have come to regard Elon Musk as an anti-Christ figure, a reading Ehret himself rejects while arguing that the family's technocratic roots are the more substantive concern.[1]
