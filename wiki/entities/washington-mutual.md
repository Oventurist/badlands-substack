# Washington Mutual

Washington Mutual was the largest savings and loan association in the United States and was heavily involved in risky mortgage lending in the period preceding the [[2008-financial-crisis|2008 financial crisis]].[1] In 2008 the institution was seized by the federal government and then sold to [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]] for around $1.9 billion.[1]

A May 2023 Badlands essay by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the Washington Mutual sale, together with JPMorgan's roughly $1.2 billion purchase of [[bear-stearns|Bear Stearns]] that same year, as the template for the bank's behaviour during financial crises — an inorganic, opportunistic growth strategy that the essay said helped carry the firm to $3.7 trillion in assets under management and was repeated in the [[2023-banking-crisis|2023 banking crisis]] with the acquisition of [[first-republic-bank|First Republic Bank]].[1]
