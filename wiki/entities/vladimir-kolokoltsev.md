# Vladimir Kolokoltsev

Vladimir Kolokoltsev is the Russian interior minister, described in the July 2024 Badlands News Brief as a trusted envoy of President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1]

According to a Daily Mail report relayed in the Brief, Putin sent Kolokoltsev to the United States carrying new proposals to end the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], with sources in both Moscow and Kyiv describing an "astonishing" new peace plan under which Russia would share sovereignty of [[crimea|Crimea]] with Ukraine.[1] Despite being under sanctions, Kolokoltsev was allowed into the US ostensibly to attend a United Nations meeting of police chiefs.[1] The report claims this was a ruse permitting him to travel to Washington aboard a VIP plane from Putin's presidential fleet in order to transmit the Russian leader's real demands for a peace deal to the US government.[1]

Ukrainian television journalist [[dmitry-gordon|Dmitry Gordon]] said he had received details of the package from intelligence sources, while the Russian Telegram channel Gosdumskaya separately reported a similar set of demands.[1]
