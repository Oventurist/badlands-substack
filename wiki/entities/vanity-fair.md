# Vanity Fair

Vanity Fair is an American magazine whose December 2025 profile of White House Chief of Staff [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]] became a political controversy.[1]

## Wiles interview

Over the course of nearly a dozen interviews with Vanity Fair, Wiles discussed a wide range of issues including the handling of Epstein files, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s legal actions against political rivals, and the personalities around the president.[1] The resulting article painted an unflattering picture of the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] and many of its top officials: Wiles was quoted describing Trump as having an "alcoholic's personality" and Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] as having been a "conspiracy theorist" for a decade.[1]

Wiles subsequently disputed portions of the article in a post on X, saying that Vanity Fair had disregarded "significant context" to create "an overwhelmingly chaotic and negative narrative" about the administration.[1] In a [[new-york-post|New York Post]] interview, Trump defended Wiles as "fantastic" and suggested she had been "deceived" by the writer, adding that he thought her comments about his personality mirrored remarks he had previously made himself.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] said he had read both parts of the Wiles interview and found it "boring and irrelevant," concluding that "there is no scandal there whatsoever."[1]
