# Mattias Desmet

**Mattias Desmet** is a scholar cited in Badlands Media coverage as the leading theorist of [[mass-formation-psychosis|Mass Formation Psychosis]] (MFP).[1] A February 11, 2025 essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]] identifies Desmet as the source of the framework it uses to describe how mass psychological manipulation operates.[1]

As summarized in that article, Desmet's account holds that mass formation thrives on isolation, anxiety, and a fixation on singular narratives.[1] The process is said to work by overwhelming individuals and creating a sense of disconnection from community and identity, which in turn weakens critical thinking and heightens emotional vulnerability.[1] The essay builds on this base to argue that where fear and division are sustained long enough, the resulting conditioned state hardens into a generational cycle in which fear-based compliance is inherited rather than merely imposed.[1]
