# Zagorsk Optical-Mechanical Plant

The Zagorsk optical-mechanical plant is a Russian defense-industrial facility located in the city of Sergiev Posad, northeast of Moscow. The plant develops and manufactures optical and optoelectronic devices for the Russian military and law enforcement, as well as for civilian sectors including healthcare, and reportedly produces night-vision goggles and weapon scopes for Russia's armed forces.[1]

## August 2023 explosion

On Wednesday, 9 August 2023, the plant erupted in a massive explosion of unknown cause, injuring at least 45 people — some in serious condition — and damaging surrounding buildings.[1] The blast occurred against a backdrop of overnight reports that another Ukrainian drone attack on the capital had been thwarted, though TASS and other Russian state outlets attributed the incident to an accident.[1] Russia's federal investigative agency opened a criminal probe into a "violation of industrial safety requirements for hazardous production facilities" while acknowledging that the cause was still being assessed.[1]
