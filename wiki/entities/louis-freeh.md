# Louis Freeh

Louis Freeh is a former Director of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] whose name surfaced in the congressional investigation into the business dealings of the family of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1]

## Connection to the Biden investigation

Israeli whistleblower [[gal-luft|Gal Luft]] told the Justice Department that [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] "was associated with a very senior retired FBI official who had a distinct physical characteristic—he had one eye."[1] That description is widely believed to point to Freeh, who in 2016 gave $100,000 to a trust for two of then-Vice President Joe Biden's grandchildren shortly before writing to Hunter Biden, "I would be delighted to do future work with you."[1] Luft's broader allegation was that the Biden family's benefactors, linked to the China-controlled energy company [[cefc-china-energy|CEFC]], had an FBI mole who shared classified information with them.[1]

The episode was recounted in the July 12, 2023 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], which presented it as part of a pattern of unexamined conflicts of interest surrounding the Biden family and federal law enforcement.[1]
