# Luis Galán

Luis Galán was Colombia's leading presidential candidate in 1989 and had promised to extradite drug traffickers to the United States.[1] He was assassinated in August 1989 in what the article calls one of the most notorious political killings in Colombian history, a killing orchestrated in part by [[pablo-escobar|Pablo Escobar]] and reportedly urged by Galán's own political rivals.[1]

The murder is identified as one of the events that ended any chance of a successful negotiation between the [[medellin-cartel|Medellín cartel]] and the Colombian government — and, in the article's reading, the moment that made Escobar expendable.[1]
