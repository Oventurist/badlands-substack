# Nolanda Sue Hill

Nolanda Sue Hill was a Dallas, Texas entrepreneur and business partner of Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ronald H. Brown]] whose financial dealings with Brown became the seed of a federal criminal investigation into the Clinton administration's Commerce Department.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Indictment

In March 1998, a federal grand jury returned a nine-count indictment against Hill, then 53, and her financial aide Kenneth C. White, 49, of Cumberland, Maryland, accusing them of conspiring to defraud the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] and the [[federal-deposit-insurance-corporation|Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation]] out of hundreds of thousands of dollars between 1989 and 1995.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Although the indictment never named him, Brown had been the original target of the underlying investigation: Attorney General [[janet-reno|Janet Reno]] said in the spring of 1995 that Brown had apparently failed to properly report large payments he received from Hill.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The New York Times described the case as "a vivid example of how an investigation can outlive its target."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

## Testimony about withheld documents

Hill later testified under oath that Brown had shown her a collection of documents — kept in an ostrich-skin portfolio — relating to the sale of seats on [[department-of-commerce|Commerce Department]] trade missions.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Those documents were withheld from [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] in violation of a subpoena and a Freedom of Information Act request.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Her testimony is central to the claim that seats on Brown's trade missions were sold to raise funds for the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] and the 1996 Clinton/Gore campaign.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
