# Mark Zwonitzer

Mark Zwonitzer is the ghostwriter who worked with [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] on his memoir and whose recorded 2016 and 2017 conversations with Biden became evidence in special counsel [[robert-hur|Robert Hur]]'s classified documents investigation.[1]

## The tapes

More than seventy hours of Zwonitzer's recorded interviews were published by the [[oversight-project|Oversight Project]] in July 2026 after Biden's attorneys abandoned a months-long effort to block their release.[1] On the tapes Biden warns Zwonitzer, "Some of this may be classified … So, be careful. I'm not sure … It's not marked classified but —," and elsewhere states, "The next thing I have here is, um, this is classified …"[1] The recordings also capture Biden pausing frequently, speaking haltingly and struggling to recall events.[1]
