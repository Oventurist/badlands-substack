# H.G. Wells

H.G. Wells was a British author identified by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] in the February 5, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Control Through Language" as a member of the same early twentieth-century social circle as [[george-orwell|George Orwell]], [[aldous-huxley|Aldous Huxley]], [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]] and [[margaret-sanger|Margaret Sanger]].[1] Carlson writes that Sanger, the early public "face" of abortion and [[planned-parenthood|Planned Parenthood]], is said to have been a former lover of Wells.[1]

Carlson states that at least some, and possibly all, of the writers in this circle supported [[eugenics|eugenics]] — the belief in controlling who reproduced and how often — and asserts that Adolf Hitler drew many of his ideas from eugenics supporters in early twentieth-century America and England.[1] His broader claim is that the [[cabal|Cabal]]'s plan was shared with these authors, which is why their fiction was able to anticipate the future so accurately.[1]
