# Esalen Institute

The Esalen Institute is a retreat and human-potential center in Big Sur, California. In [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s November 15, 2025 essay "Don't Be Symbol-Minded," it is characterized as a "Mithraic hub" in whose bowels [[neuro-linguistic-programming|Neuro-Linguistic Programming]] was developed as a substitute for human thinking.[1]

Ehret made the claim in passing, directing readers to [[cynthia-chung|Cynthia Chung]]'s essay "Huxley's Ultimate Revolution: The Battle for Your Mind and the Relativity of Madness" for the underlying critical analysis of Esalen's role in the origins of NLP.[1] The reference served his broader argument that techniques marketed as shortcuts to discernment — NLP, and by extension AI summarization — are inferior substitutes for the earned knowledge produced by reading source texts and reasoning from [[universal-principles|universal principles]].[1]
