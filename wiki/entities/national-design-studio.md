# National Design Studio

The **National Design Studio** is a federal body created through an executive order signed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in August 2025, tasked with improving the appearance and usability of United States government websites.[1] It is headed by [[joe-gebbia|Joe Gebbia]], a co-founder of Airbnb, whom Trump's order directed to "beautify government websites."[1]

## Relationship to DOGE

The Studio drew renewed attention in November 2025, when Reuters reported that the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] (DOGE) had effectively disbanded with eight months remaining on its charter.[1] At least two prominent DOGE employees were reported to be involved with the National Design Studio at the time of that reporting, making the body one of several destinations for personnel dispersed from the wind-down of the centralized DOGE effort.[1] Other DOGE functions were absorbed by the [[office-of-personnel-management|Office of Personnel Management]], according to Office of Personnel Management Director [[scott-kupor|Scott Kupor]] and documents reviewed by [[reuters|Reuters]].[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentators treated the Studio's absorption of DOGE staff as evidence for the interpretation that the DOGE project had always been designed to embed its people and tools inside individual agencies rather than persist as a standalone central office.[1] Under that reading — advanced by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] — the migration of personnel into new bodies such as the National Design Studio reflected a planned transition to a "steady state" rather than a failure of the underlying reform program.[1]
