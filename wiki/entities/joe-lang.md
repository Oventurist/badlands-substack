# Joe Lang

Joe Lang is an author who writes for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Work on the currency reset

Lang published an article outlining his interpretation of the "currency reset" situation, asking whether [[central-bank-digital-currency|CBDCs]] are the path to fear or to freedom.[1] In it he suggests that the reason [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has not openly bashed CBDCs is that they are an inevitable medium, and that regardless of whether the world experiences a [[great-reset|Great Reset]] or a [[great-awakening|Great Awakening]], CBDCs in some form will likely become the global currency.[1]

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites Lang's argument in support of the view that central banks themselves may not be the problem so much as how they have been used — that the medium of exchange is not what causes suffering, but rather the rules of the game.[1]
