# ISIS

**ISIS** (the Islamic State of Iraq and Syria) is described in Badlands Media's "A Dereliction of Duty" not as an organic jihadist uprising but as a proxy terrorist group created and armed by the United States to overthrow [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] in Syria.[1]

## Origins and sponsorship

Following the fall of [[muammar-gaddafi|Muammar Gaddafi]], Libya became the source of weapons funneled to the new Syrian proxy force, with Ambassador [[christopher-stevens|Christopher Stevens]] coordinating transfers as liaison between arms dealers and [[al-qaeda|Al Qaeda]] in Libya.[1] The article states flatly that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] and [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] "are responsible for ISIS," having run the arrangement as a secret operation of the [[state-department|State Department]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] under [[david-petraeus|David Petraeus]].[1]

## Expansion under the Obama administration

Obama is said to have protected and nurtured ISIS in Syria and Iraq, deliberately permitting its expansion and enriching it with heavy weapons including [[surface-to-air-missiles|surface-to-air missiles]].[1] Q drop 2640 juxtaposes the years Clinton served as Secretary of State with the years ISIS expanded; the article's answer is that she was supplying the group with weapons while authorizing private U.S. arms sales and directing money to the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]].[1] Obama's requirement that the military obtain [[white-house|White House]] approval for every battlefield action — a [[stand-down-order|stand down order]] in the author's reading — micromanaged the wars so as to protect the weapons sales and money laundering while ISIS grew.[1]

## Collapse under Trump

Q drop 1887 frames the contrast: [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] removed the White House from the battlefield chain of command, and with the handcuffs off the military destroyed ISIS in less than a year.[1]

## Supply routes

Ships originating in [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]] docked in Alexandria, Egypt, where manifests were changed, before continuing to Lebanon or [[turkey|Turkey]].[1] In April 2012 Lebanese authorities intercepted the container ship *Lutfallah II* carrying Libyan rocket-propelled grenades and heavy-calibre ammunition, and at least seven further weapons ships were unloaded in northern Lebanon, some carrying shoulder-launched heat-seeking missiles.[1] By June 2012 press reporting had exposed [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operatives in Turkey directing a smuggling operation through intermediaries including the [[muslim-brotherhood|Syrian Muslim Brotherhood]], paid for by [[qatar|Qatar]], [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and Turkey.[1]
