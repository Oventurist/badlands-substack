# BP

BP, referred to in the source as British Petroleum, is a British multinational energy company identified in the Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" as one of the major Western energy firms that fund the [[atlantic-council]].

The article places BP alongside [[chevron]], [[exxonmobil|Exxon]] and [[shell|Royal Dutch Shell]] as donors with "a vested interest in the Iran deal," arguing that their financial support of the Council translates into influence over the [[state-department]], the [[cia]] and the [[pentagon]] and the ability to lobby for policies that would open [[iran]]. It further notes that [[blackrock]] holds considerable — if not majority — equity stakes in BP and the other majors, all of which stood to gain tremendously from the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|JCPOA]].
