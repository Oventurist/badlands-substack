# Phil Gordon

Phil Gordon served as national security adviser to Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] during the 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

In "The Candidate Who Isn't There," published August 24, 2024, Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] wrote that Gordon issued the statement that "doused with the cold water of reality" left-leaning supporters who had been told Harris was open to an arms embargo against [[israel|Israel]].[1] The claim had originated in a post on X by [[emma-vigeland|Emma Vigeland]] of "The Majority Report," which DeLarme called a fabrication.[1]

DeLarme treated Gordon's rebuttal as notable because it constituted the first official statement he could identify from anyone speaking on behalf of the Harris campaign — a campaign he otherwise characterized as refusing interviews, avoiding unscripted remarks, and publishing no policy platform.[1] He argued the denial confirmed that a Harris administration's posture toward funding and arming Israel would be indistinguishable from that of the [[biden-administration|Biden administration]].[1]
