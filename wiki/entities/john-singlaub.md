# John Singlaub

John Singlaub was a United States Army officer and a founding member of the Central Intelligence Agency.[1]

In Badlands Media commentary, Singlaub is named alongside the [[western-goals-foundation|Western Goals Foundation]] as an architect of what the writer describes as intense psychological brainwashing operations directed at the American public throughout the latter half of the 20th century.[1] That apparatus is credited with elevating figures such as the televangelist [[pat-robertson|Pat Robertson]], and with helping to entrench Christian Zionist sentiment that the writer argues left American believers spiritually vulnerable in their response to Israeli government conduct.[1]
