# Defender of the Republic

Defender of the Republic is a pseudonymous host affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]. Together with [[the-kate-awakening|The Kate Awakening]] she co-hosts **Raising a Nation**, airing Saturdays at 9pm ET in the network's December 2022 schedule.[1]

The program frames Great Awakening themes through the experience of parents attempting to raise what the hosts call the next generation of sovereign citizens in an uncertain world, making it the network's principal family-oriented offering.[1]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[2]
