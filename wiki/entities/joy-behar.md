# Joy Behar

Joy Behar is an American television personality and co-host of the ABC daytime talk program [[the-view|The View]], where she is one of the show's most outspoken liberal commentators on national politics.[1]

## 2024 polling remarks

On Wednesday, October 16, 2024, Behar said on air that she was "starting to worry" about polling that appeared to show favorable outcomes for Republican nominee [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] At the time, national surveys showed Trump and Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] at a deadlock tie, with Trump posting strong numbers in battleground states and improving support among Black and Latino men.[1]

"I'm starting to worry about the polls because I see a lot of the media saying that he's leading, and everybody's getting nervous and that," Behar said, before suggesting the coverage was deliberate: "But they're doing that on purpose because basically it's so disheartening for Democrats and people who are leaning toward Kamala to say 'what are you doing, don't buy into that.' Don't buy into that."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Behar's comments preceded Harris's poorly received [[fox-news|Fox News]] interview with [[bret-baier|Bret Baier]], quipping that Behar was likely "having a bad week."[1] The same commentary catalogued the underlying poll movement Behar was reacting to — Harris's five-point lead collapsing to a 48–48 tie, historically low support among Black men aged 18 to 44, majority support for Trump among young Latino men in Arizona, and the erosion of Harris's advantage with white women — and argued that in a free and fair election Trump would win both the Electoral College and the popular vote.[1]
