# Jordan Seidhom

**Jordan Seidhom** is a South Carolina helicopter pilot and former head of the Chesterfield County Sheriff's Office narcotics unit who flew volunteer supply and rescue missions into flood-cut areas of western North Carolina after [[hurricane-helene|Hurricane Helene]] in late September 2024.[1]

## Banner Elk rescue

On the Saturday morning following the storm, Seidhom saw a Facebook post — being commented on and shared by tens of thousands of people — about a family stranded on a mountain in Banner Elk, North Carolina.[1] The family had run out of water a day earlier and had less than two days of food remaining.[1] Drawing on his law-enforcement background in locating people, Seidhom researched the mountain chain where the family was located and identified a landing site using his mapping software.[1] He loaded bottled water and food into his helicopter and headed toward Banner Elk.[1] "I thought, I have a helicopter, maybe I can help," Seidhom told Queen City News chief investigator Jody Barr.[1]

## Arrest threat at Lake Lure

Seidhom was subsequently ordered out of Lake Lure, North Carolina under threat of arrest while conducting volunteer supply and rescue flights, an episode reported by Queen City News.[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited the case — noting Seidhom's background as a former police officer and volunteer firefighter — as evidence of "subversive individuals in the government, seemingly at all levels," obstructing private citizens who were risking their lives to save others while the government itself did little.[1] He grouped the incident with a similar account from Special Forces veteran [[tim-kennedy|Tim Kennedy]].[1]
