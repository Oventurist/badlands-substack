# Duke University

Duke University is a private research university in Durham, North Carolina, whose academic medical arm, Duke Medicine, is one of the state's leading medical institutions.[1]

## Duke Gender Clinic

In 2015, Duke Medicine launched the Gender Clinic to provide a comprehensive range of treatments for children as young as two years old for gender dysphoria.[1] The clinic's director, transgender activist [[deanna-adkins|Dr. Deanna Adkins]], described her transgender pediatric patients in a 2016 interview with the Charlotte Observer, saying that such distress "is not a choice in any of my patients."[1]

## Controversy

Reporting by investigative journalist [[sloan-rachmuth|Sloan Rachmuth]], amplified by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], grouped Duke with UNC and ECU as North Carolina institutions offering transgender-related treatment to toddlers.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] responded that the practice constitutes a "multibillion-dollar industry" with population-control implications and called for the licenses of participating practitioners to be revoked.[1]

## 2025 civil rights probe and funding freeze

In late July 2025 the Trump administration froze $108 million in federal funding for Duke University, days after opening a civil rights investigation into the school.[2] The investigation, announced Monday, July 28, centers on allegations that Duke's law school made hiring and acceptance decisions on the basis of race and color, and accuses the university of using race preferences in hiring, admissions and scholarship decisions.[2]

The [[department-of-education|Department of Education]] argued that the practices, if substantiated, would violate Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 as well as the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court's]] ruling banning affirmative action, which had previously permitted colleges to weigh race as one factor among many in admissions decisions.[2] The [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]] is responsible for freezing the funds, which will affect the Duke University School of Medicine; a senior administration official said the administration has also accused the medical school of accepting applicants on the basis of race.[2]
