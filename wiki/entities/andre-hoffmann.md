# Andre Hoffmann

**Andre Hoffmann** is the Interim Co-Chair of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]], serving in that role during the transition following the departure of forum founder Professor [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] from its leadership.[1]

## Role at Davos 2026

Ahead of the [[davos-2026|Davos 2026]] Annual Meeting, scheduled for January 19–24, 2026, Hoffmann said the forum's key discussions would focus on growth, geopolitics, innovation and climate, and he emphasized public-private cooperation as the means of tackling interconnected global challenges.[1] Speaking to the meeting's stated theme of "A spirit of dialogue," he described the forum's aim as fostering honest discussion and identifying pragmatic pathways toward sustainable prosperity without harming the planet.[1] He also situated artificial intelligence and its governance, alongside climate priorities, among the meeting's central subjects.[1]
