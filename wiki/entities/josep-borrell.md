# Josep Borrell

**Josep Borrell** is the European Union's former top diplomat, its High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy.[1]

## Response to the 2025 U.S. National Security Strategy

In December 2025, Borrell characterized the new U.S. National Security Strategy issued under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as "a declaration of political war."[1] His remark was cited in reporting on the collapse of morale among European diplomats posted to Washington, who described a season of Trumpian disdain for America's transatlantic allies.[1] One envoy of a midsized European nation, granted anonymity, told reporters that "the Western alliance is over. The relationship will never be the same again."[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] responded that Trump was not treating Europe as an ally because it was not one, asking whether Europe could even be broken up given that "their power projection has been us the whole time."[1]

## Statements on EU complicity in Gaza

In August 2025 Borrell said the [[european-union|European Union]] could be considered complicit in Israeli war crimes in Gaza because it fails to act "in the face of a clear case of massive human rights violations."[2] He argued that Brussels is reluctant to punish [[israel|Israel]] for transgressions that, in his account, surpass other conduct the bloc has condemned elsewhere.[2] Borrell also claimed that a third of Israeli bombs falling on [[gaza|Gaza]] "are made in Europe."[2]

His remarks came against the backdrop of Israel's military campaign in the enclave, launched in response to the 7 October 2023 attack in which [[hamas|Hamas]] militants killed 1,200 people and took more than 200 hostages, and in which more than 60,000 people, mostly civilians, have since been killed according to the Gaza Health Ministry.[2] Israel has additionally been accused of obstructing humanitarian aid in the territory.[2]

## ICC arrest warrants for Israeli officials (2024)

On Tuesday, May 21, 2024, Borrell commented on the [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] Prosecutor's application for arrest warrants against top Israeli officials over alleged war crimes in Gaza, stressing that all European Union member countries would be legally required to comply.[3] He explained that if Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] or Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]] travelled to a European country, they would face arrest, and said the same applied to the Hamas leaders listed alongside them.[3]

"I take note of the decision of the ICC Prosecutor to apply for warrants of arrest before Pre-Trial Chamber I of the International Criminal Court (ICC) against [[yahya-sinwar|Yahya Sinwar]], [[mohammed-deif|Mohammed Deif]], [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]], Benjamin Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant," Borrell stated.[3] Writing on X, he added that "the mandate of the ICC, as an independent international institution, is to prosecute the most serious crimes under international law," and emphasized that "all States that have ratified the ICC statutes are bound to execute the Court's decisions," noting that the EU had taken note of the world court's action.[3] With the exception of Ukraine and Turkey, all of Europe is a signatory to the [[rome-statute|Rome Statute]], requiring those states to apprehend individuals wanted by the ICC.[3]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read Borrell's insistence on compliance as proof that globalist institutions were being weaponized against their own architects, forcing oligarchs to argue for the delegitimization of bodies such as the ICC.[3]

## Rafah ultimatum (May 2024)

Following the [[2024-rafah-tent-camp-strike|Israeli strike on a Rafah tent camp]] and the [[international-court-of-justice|International Court of Justice]] order demanding Israel halt its Rafah offensive, Borrell — in his capacity as the EU's High Representative and head of the Foreign Affairs Council — framed the crisis as a binary choice, saying, "We will have to choose between our support to the international institutions and the rule of law, or our support to Israel."[4] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] said the comment "resonates," but argued that what it really meant was a choice between international institutions and support for the United States, since Israel had become an extension of American power.[4] The commentary noted that Borrell and the EU were scheduled to meet Saudi Foreign Minister [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] and his "Team Peace" group of Arab foreign ministers and diplomats.[4]


## "Rule of law or Israel" remarks (May 2024)

Speaking at a European University Institute event in Florence on May 24, 2024, minutes after the [[international-court-of-justice|International Court of Justice]] ordered Israel to halt its offensive in [[rafah|Rafah]], EU foreign affairs chief Josep Borrell said the bloc faced a "difficult" choice: "We will have to choose between our support to the international institutions and the rule of law, or our support to Israel, and both things is going to be quite difficult to make compatible."[5] He added that disregarding the order "would be against the rules based world order we support and promote everywhere."[5]

Borrell also conceded that the EU's response to the Gaza war had been too sluggish because of deep rifts between member-state capitals, and called for changing the bloc's foreign-policy decision-making, which requires unanimity among all 27 leaders.[5] He announced that the EU Foreign Affairs Council would meet that week with a group of Arab foreign ministers led by Saudi foreign minister [[faisal-bin-farhan|Faisal bin Farhan]] to discuss the situation in Gaza extensively.[5] Badlands commentary described Borrell — High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy and a Vice-President of the [[european-commission|European Commission]] — as expressing grave concern over the future of the "international rules-based order," a term the brief traced to the George H. W. Bush era and read as a euphemism for a [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[5]
