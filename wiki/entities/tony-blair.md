# Tony Blair

Tony Blair is the former British Prime Minister who appears in the second installment of the America for Sale series as one of three former world leaders used by the consulting firm [[teneo-holdings|Teneo]] to attract and entertain clients.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Citing The Washington Times, the article reports that Blair spoke alongside [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] at a reception held on September 20, 2012 at the Essex House in New York City, just days after the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi]] tragedy.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The event was staged with the help of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]], who was paid a $15,000-a-month consulting fee by Teneo while simultaneously serving as a "[[special-government-employee|special government employee]]" advising Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] at the [[state-department|State Department]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) It was not publicised in advance; its purpose, in the article's account, was to wow potential Teneo clients with access to three former world leaders on a single stage.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

The author treats the line-up as significant rather than coincidental, reading it against Q drop 299 of December 7, 2017 — "SA Controls (assigned) US / UK POLITICIANS / Tech Co's (primary)" — and concluding that Clinton, Bush and Blair "were all puppets" of [[prince-alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed]], who is said to have controlled many important U.S. and U.K. politicians for several decades.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Iraq War advocacy and later financial roles

In "The Deep Politics of the '12-Day War'," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cites footage of Blair, described as the former British Prime Minister and a multimillionaire, "lying in the British Houses of Parliament" about weapons of mass destruction — placing him alongside [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and [[colin-powell|Colin Powell]] in a pattern of invoking nuclear and WMD threats to justify war.[2]

DeLarme adds that Blair is a Fabian Socialist who sits on the board of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and became an "advisor" to both Zurich Financial Services, now Zurich Insurance Group, and [[jpmorgan-chase|JP Morgan Chase]], receiving £500,000 and £2.5 million per year respectively for his services.[2] The JP Morgan arrangement is characterised as particularly controversial because the bank stood to profit tremendously from the [[iraq-war|war in Iraq]] that Blair had helped start.[2]
