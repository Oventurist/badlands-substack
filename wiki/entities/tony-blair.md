# Tony Blair

Tony Blair is a British politician who served as Prime Minister of the United Kingdom from 1997 to 2007. He appears in the Badlands Media essay America for Sale — Part 2 as one of three former world leaders who headlined a private [[teneo|Teneo]] client reception in New York City.

According to *The Washington Times*, [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] — then simultaneously a "special government employee" at the [[state-department|State Department]] and a paid Teneo consultant — helped stage a September 20, 2012 event at the Essex House at which [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and Blair appeared as speakers. The paper described the reception as entertaining potential Teneo clients by "wowing them with access to three former world leaders on a single stage," and noted that it took place just days after the [[benghazi|Benghazi]] attack.

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treats the lineup as illustrative of Q drop 299's claim that Saudi Arabia "controls (assigned) US / UK POLITICIANS," describing Clinton, Bush and Blair as "puppets" and asserting that [[prince-alwaleed|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] had controlled leading American and British politicians for decades. This characterization is the author's interpretation rather than a reported fact.

## Board of Peace participant (January 2026)

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Blair was a named participant in the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] when President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] ratified its charter at the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] meeting in Davos, alongside [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], and [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]]. The charter establishes the Board as an "official international organization" chaired by Trump, with permanent members to pay $1 billion each; Blair's inclusion placed the former prime minister at the center of Trump's alternative to the [[united-nations|United Nations]] peace architecture for [[gaza|Gaza]].
