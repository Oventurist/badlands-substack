# Michael Bennet

Michael Bennet is a Colorado Democratic politician cited in Badlands Media commentary as a leading proponent of taxing disbursements from the Justice Department's [[weaponization-fund|weaponization fund]] at 100 percent.[1] According to [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], the purpose of such a levy would be to ensure that recipients — she names [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]] — could not actually retain restitution paid out under the program, an approach she summarized as ensuring "that their weaponization cannot be weaponized."[1]

Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] was described as pursuing a similar strategy with respect to the fund.[1] Ashe in America, herself a Coloradan, argued that moves of this kind would provoke President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to escalate rather than retreat, and offered a tongue-in-cheek apology to the country for Bennet on behalf of "the communist country of Colorado."[1]

## Clash with CIA Director Ratcliffe (March 2026)

At the [[senate-intelligence-committee|Senate Intelligence Committee]]'s annual hearing on March 18, 2026, Bennet clashed with [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]] and asked the chairman to make the Director stop talking.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], a Colorado constituent, described the exchange as her favorite part of the hearing, called Bennet a "whiney beta," and noted that he was running for governor of Colorado seeking to succeed Jared Polis.[2]
