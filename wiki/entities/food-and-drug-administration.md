# Food and Drug Administration

The **Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** is the United States federal agency responsible for regulating food, drugs, biological products and medical devices.

In the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026 the FDA appears in the account of the federal prosecution of [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]], who was convicted of making a false statement to the agency alongside charges of conspiracy to commit wire fraud, wire fraud, and distributing adulterated and misbranded medical devices through [[universal-meditech|Universal Meditech Inc.]]. The devices at issue were more than one million faulty COVID-19 tests imported from China and sold for nearly $4 million between 2020 and 2023. The agency's device-regulation and truthful-reporting requirements thus supplied part of the legal basis for the conviction that connects the California case to the [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas biolab]].
