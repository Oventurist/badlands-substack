# Hassan Rouhani

Hassan Rouhani is a former President of [[iran|Iran]] who took office in 2013, succeeding [[mahmoud-ahmadinejad|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad]].[1]

## Pivot toward Russia

Badlands commentary holds that the defining feature of Rouhani's presidency was his pivot toward [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[russia|Russia]], a power that had for centuries been a rival of Iran.[1] Many analysts have continued to resist characterizing Iran as a genuine ally of Russia, describing the relationship instead as "strategic transactionalism," but Rouhani is credited with making earnest steps to forge economic bonds with Moscow, including the construction of railroads and pipelines and Iran's bid to join the Putin-led [[brics|BRICS]] financial system.[1]

## Nuclear deal and banker arrests

Rouhani was president when the Iran nuclear deal was signed with the Obama administration and he authorized the associated meetings, though Iranian official Javad Zarif is credited as the prime contact and principal negotiator on the Iranian side.[1] In 2018, Rouhani had a number of Iranian central bankers arrested — the same figures later identified by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in his sanctions package — for laundering US dollars through banks in [[iraq|Iraq]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]]; Badlands commentary speculates that these were the same dollars sent to Iran by the Obama administration under the terms of the nuclear deal.[1]

## Disqualification and the succession question (2024)

Following the death of President [[ebrahim-raisi|Ebrahim Raisi]] in a helicopter crash in May 2024, Badlands commentary revisited Rouhani's standing in the Iranian succession picture.[2] Raisi had been expected to become the next Supreme Leader, with the sitting [[ali-khamenei|Ayatollah]] described as effectively in hospice care — but Raisi had not always been the odds-on favorite.[2] Rouhani, his predecessor as president, was once the leading candidate to be selected Supreme Leader by the [[assembly-of-experts|Assembly of Experts]], until he was officially disqualified from consideration by Iranian courts in January 2024.[2]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted the existence of "some really interesting posts about Rouhani by that one infamous anon," which he suggested became more interesting through the lens of Raisi's death.[2]
