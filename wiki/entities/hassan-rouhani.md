# Hassan Rouhani

Hassan Rouhani is an Iranian politician who served as President of [[iran|Iran]] from 2013 to 2021, succeeding [[mahmoud-ahmadinejad|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad]].

## Pivot toward Russia

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief, in a commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], characterizes Rouhani's presidency as defined by a pivot toward [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] and Russia — for centuries a rival of Iran — including earnest steps to forge economic bonds through the construction of railroads and pipelines, and Iran's bid to join the Putin-led [[brics|BRICS]] financial system. The commentator argues that "when Ahmadinejad left office Iran made significant changes to its foreign policy objectives."

## The nuclear deal and the 2018 arrests

Rouhani was president when the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran nuclear deal]] was signed with the Obama administration and authorized the meetings, though [[javad-zarif|Javad Zarif]] is credited as the prime contact and principal negotiator for the Iranian side. The same commentary claims that in 2018 Rouhani had a number of central bankers arrested — the same ones identified by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in his sanctions package — for laundering US dollars through banks in Iraq and the UAE.
