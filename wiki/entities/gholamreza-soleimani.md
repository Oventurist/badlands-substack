# Gholamreza Soleimani

Gholamreza Soleimani was the longtime commander of the [[basij|Basij]], the Iranian volunteer paramilitary militia subordinate to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.

According to the March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], [[israel|Israel]] announced his elimination in the same wave of U.S.-Israeli airstrikes that reportedly killed [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]], Secretary of the [[supreme-national-security-council|Supreme National Security Council]]. The Brief presents the two deaths together as "another major blow to Iranian leadership," delivered during the escalating war between [[iran|Iran]] and the United States and Israel.

Iran's response to the strikes, as described in the source, was to enter what Tehran called a "new phase of oil war," involving drone swarms and attacks on regional energy infrastructure, while continuing to assert control over the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].
