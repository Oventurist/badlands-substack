# Gholamreza Soleimani

Gholamreza Soleimani was the longtime commander of the Basij militia, the volunteer paramilitary force of [[iran|Iran]]'s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.[1]

## Reported death

In March 2026, [[israel|Israel]] announced the elimination of Soleimani in the same wave of U.S.-Israeli airstrikes that reportedly killed [[ali-larijani|Ali Larijani]], Secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, along with Larijani's son, a deputy and bodyguards.[1] Badlands described the losses as another major blow to Iranian leadership.[1]

In retaliation for the strikes, Iran escalated into what it called a "new phase of oil war," launching drone swarms and attacks on energy infrastructure including the Shah oil and gas field in the UAE, Iraq's Majnoon field, and targets in Saudi Arabia, while continuing to assert control over the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]].[1]
