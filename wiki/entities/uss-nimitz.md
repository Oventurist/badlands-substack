# USS Nimitz

The USS Nimitz is a United States Navy aircraft carrier, commissioned on May 3, 1975, and the oldest active aircraft carrier in the US Navy fleet.[1] It serves as the lead ship of the carrier class bearing its name and is scheduled to be decommissioned in 2026, making its mid-2025 deployment possibly its final sea voyage.[1]

## June 2025 Middle East deployment

On Monday, June 16, 2025, a US official told Fox News that the Nimitz carrier strike group was sailing from the South China Sea toward the Middle East ahead of schedule.[1] The strike group had previously been scheduled to relieve the [[uss-carl-vinson|USS Carl Vinson]] carrier strike group, which had already been deployed for several months; because of the accelerated timetable, both carriers were set to be in the Middle East region at the same time.[1] As of the preceding Friday, the Carl Vinson had been the only US aircraft carrier in the region, according to US defense officials.[1]

Reuters, citing data from the ship-tracking website Marine Traffic, reported that the Nimitz departed the South China Sea on Monday morning heading west.[1] Two sources, including one diplomat, told Reuters that the carrier had been scheduled to attend a formal reception in Danang City, central Vietnam, on June 20; that reception was subsequently canceled.[1] The redeployment occurred against the backdrop of the escalating exchange of strikes between [[israel]] and [[iran]].[1]

## Historical significance

Badlands coverage emphasized the symbolic weight of the deployment. The Nimitz was deployed in 1980, when its helicopters participated in [[operation-eagle-claw|Operation Eagle Claw]], the failed US effort to rescue the American hostages held at the US Embassy in Tehran.[1] Commentators framed the United States as having been in a shadow war against Iran ever since that operation.[1]
