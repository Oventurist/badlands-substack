# Willie Nelson

Willie Nelson is an American country musician who co-founded the concert charity [[farm-aid|Farm Aid]] in 1985 to raise awareness and funding for American family farming.[1]

## Farm Aid and Badlands criticism

Badlands Media invoked Nelson's founding role while arguing that Farm Aid has expanded even as family farms have continued to fail, and that most of the artists headlining the charity's 2024 event backed [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and the political status quo.[1] The commentary concluded that although the writer liked much of the musicians' work, "their results indicate the American people should refuse to give them another dime."[1]
