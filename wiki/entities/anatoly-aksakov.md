# Anatoly Aksakov

Anatoly Aksakov is a Russian legislator described as the head of the Duma, the lower house of [[russia|Russia's]] parliament, in reporting on the country's 2024 cryptocurrency trade legislation.[1]

## Cryptocurrency trade law

On Tuesday, July 30, 2024, Russian lawmakers passed a bill allowing businesses to use cryptocurrencies in international trade as part of efforts to skirt Western sanctions imposed after Russia's invasion of Ukraine.[1] Announcing the vote to lawmakers, Aksakov said, "We are taking a historic decision in the financial sphere."[1]

The law, expected to take effect in September 2024, directed the Russian central bank — whose governor [[elvira-nabiullina|Elvira Nabiullina]] backed the measure — to create an "experimental" infrastructure for cryptocurrency payments.[1] It was part of a broader package regulating crypto mining and digital-asset circulation, and it did not lift Russia's domestic ban on cryptocurrency payments.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the law as the "reaction" stage of a "Decentralization Dialectic," positioned between reports of collapsing Russia–[[china|China]] payment channels and [[el-salvador|El Salvador's]] subsequent proposal to settle [[brics|BRICS]] trade in crypto.[1]
