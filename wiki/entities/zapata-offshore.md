# Zapata Offshore

Zapata Off-Shore Company was an offshore oil drilling firm, an 80 percent-owned subsidiary of Zapata Petroleum Corporation, which [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and J. Hugh Liedtke — later head of Pennzoil — founded in Houston in 1953.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The Badlands Media account describes it plainly as a CIA front company and Bush's base of operations from the 1950s onward.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Its early development was financed by [[texas-national-bank|Texas National Bank]], created the same year, through loans for drilling rigs and the vessels that tended them; Zapata became one of that bank's most significant oil-related accounts of the late 1950s.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) TNB later became [[texas-commerce-bank|Texas Commerce Bank]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Zapata was linked to the [[cia|CIA]]'s abortive [[bay-of-pigs|Bay of Pigs]] invasion of Cuba in 1961: three company ships financed by TNB — the *Houston*, the *Barbara J* and the *Zapata* — were loaned by Bush's firm to the agency for the operation, which the CIA code-named OPERATION ZAPATA.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The article presents the naming as evidence that Bush may have been running the operation, and offers Zapata as the model for how oil companies serve as vehicles for laundering covert and narcotics revenue — the same technique [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] later applied in Venezuela.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
