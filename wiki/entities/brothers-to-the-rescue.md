# Brothers to the Rescue

Brothers to the Rescue is a Cuban exile organization whose civilian aircraft were the target of a 1996 shootdown by Cuban military forces, an incident that killed four people and became one of the most controversial episodes in modern US–Cuba relations.[1]

## The 1996 shootdown

On the date in question, Cuban military aircraft downed two civilian planes operated by the group. International investigators later concluded that the aircraft were flying outside Cuban airspace at the time they were shot down, while [[cuba|Cuba]] has continued to deny wrongdoing and maintains that the planes violated its airspace.[1]

## Legal aftermath

In May 2026 the United States unsealed a federal indictment in Miami charging former Cuban president [[raul-castro|Raúl Castro]] with murder and conspiracy in connection with the shootdown. Prosecutors allege that Castro authorized Cuban military forces to take "decisive and deadly action" against the Brothers to the Rescue planes.[1] The indictment was framed in Badlands commentary as part of an intensifying pressure campaign by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration against Havana, and as an overture to the Cuban-American political constituency for whom the shootdown remains a defining grievance.[1]
