# The Post Millennial

The Post Millennial is a conservative online news outlet whose reporting is periodically aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news briefs.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands Media

In May 2023 the outlet reported that the [[food-and-drug-administration|US Food and Drug Administration]] had approved the sale of genetically modified pork for supermarket shelves, drawing on Daily Mail coverage of the research.[1] The Post Millennial's account described how scientists at [[washington-state-university|Washington State University]] had developed the altered pork using the gene-editing tool [[crispr|CRISPR]], and quoted professor Jon Oatley on the project's stated goal of improving meat quality and livestock resilience in harsh environments.[1] The outlet's earlier reporting on genetically modified mosquitoes released in the Florida Keys was linked within the same article.[1]
