# Heterodox Academy

Heterodox Academy is a network of academics, post-secondary professors, other faculty members and students who work to counteract what they see as a lack of viewpoint diversity on campuses across North America.[1]

## Role in the campus debate

The organization's Toronto chapter hosted a screening of the documentary *The Coddling of the American Mind*, attended by a Badlands writer who reported on the event in April 2024 amid the wave of pro-Palestinian campus encampments.[1] The film follows separate stories of college students who were, in the writer's framing, infected by ideological conformity and subsequently recovered.[1]

More significant than the film, according to the account, were the university professors present at the screening, who described in detail watching students at their institutions descend into what they viewed as a form of ideological madness.[1] A recurring example cited by every professor in attendance was a sudden spike in students presenting "accommodation letters" demanding additional time to complete assignments relative to their peers.[1] The professors connected this to what the filmmakers call the Three Great Untruths: that what doesn't kill you makes you weaker; that one should always trust one's feelings; and that life is a battle between good people and evil people.[1]

## Significance

The Badlands commentary argued that the existence and growth of Heterodox Academy reflects academics across North America putting skin in the game to fight for free thought, and predicted that such academically-minded organizations would receive an influx of new members as the scale of student ideological capture became apparent.[1] One professor was reported to have written directly to say he felt "something bigger is going on."[1] The framing situated the campus unrest as a product of sophisticated fifth-generation psychological operations targeting youth, with the awakening of faculty as the counter-movement.[1]
