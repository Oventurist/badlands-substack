# Owasco PC

Owasco PC is a corporate entity established by [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] that became a focus of the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]]'s investigation into the Biden family's finances.[1]

## Payments to Joe Biden

In December 2023, Oversight Committee Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] released subpoenaed bank records showing that Owasco PC made direct monthly payments to [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], apparently beginning in September 2018.[1] Comer emphasized that the transfers did not come from Hunter Biden's personal account but from a corporate account that had received payments from China "and other shady corners of the world."[1] He argued that the payments formed "part of a pattern revealing Joe Biden knew about, participated in and benefited from his family's influence peddling schemes."[1] The committee published the redacted bank records alongside a video released on Twitter.[1]
