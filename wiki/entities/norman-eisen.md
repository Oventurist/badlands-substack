# Norman Eisen

Norman ("Norm") Eisen is an American lawyer, election-law specialist, and former Obama administration official. In the Badlands corpus he is identified as the central organising figure behind what its writers call the "shadow cabal" — the network of lawyers, officials, activists, and private organisations described in [[time-magazine|Time]] magazine's February 4, 2021 account of how the 2020 election was "fortified."

That article, quoted at length in the June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief, cites Eisen as "a prominent lawyer and former Obama Administration official who recruited Republicans and Democrats to the board of the Voter Protection Program," and quotes him saying that "the untold story of the election is the thousands of people of both parties who accomplished the triumph of American democracy at its very foundation."

In June 2026 Eisen was reported by Politico to be among the experts convened by Senator [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] — alongside former Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], the attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], and the heads of Democracy Forward and Protect Democracy — to help Senate Democrats prepare for possible election-disruption scenarios in the 2026 midterms. The Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues that "the current shadow cabal and 2020 shadow cabal are both the brain-children of Norm Eisen and his minions," treating the 2026 planning as a sequel to the 2020 effort.
