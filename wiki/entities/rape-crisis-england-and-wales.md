# Rape Crisis England & Wales

Rape Crisis England & Wales is a British charity and advocacy organisation representing victims and survivors of rape and sexual abuse.[1]

## Role in the 2024 Olympics controversy

Ahead of the opening of the 2024 Paris Summer Olympics, the organisation was among the groups that publicly objected to the inclusion of Dutch beach volleyball player [[steven-van-de-velde|Steven van de Velde]], who had been convicted in 2016 of raping a British 12-year-old girl.[1] Its chief executive, Ciara Bergman, described the inclusion as "irresponsible" and said it created an "enormous sense of impunity," observing that all Olympic athletes sign a declaration promising to be role models.[1]

Bergman argued that the decision would have a "serious impact," stating that "there is always an impact on the individual victim survivor, but every act of violence against women and girls is a crime against society," with a "collateral and collective impact on all other women and girls."[1] She called on the [[international-olympic-committee|International Olympic Committee]] to open an investigation into how van de Velde had been permitted to compete, asking how the situation had reached a point where "raping a child is seen as less important than the medal someone might win at the Olympics" and urging that the moment prompt "real thinking and real change."[1]
