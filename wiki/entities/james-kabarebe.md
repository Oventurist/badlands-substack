# James Kabarebe

**Gen. James Kabarebe** is a senior Rwandan military figure and deputy minister of foreign affairs who was sanctioned by the United States in early 2025 over Rwanda's role in the violence in eastern [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Congo]].[1]

## US sanctions and Rwandan response

The sanctions were imposed as Rwanda-backed rebels captured two major cities in eastern Congo in an armed conflict that came to threaten the Congolese government itself.[1] On the Friday following the designation, the government of [[rwanda|Rwanda]] publicly asserted its solidarity with Kabarebe, describing the measures against him as "unjustified and unfounded."[1]

In its statement, Kigali argued that such action makes "no contribution toward long-term security, peace and stability for all the countries of the Great Lakes region," and maintained that securing the border with Congo is "a matter of national security, and that is our only driver."[1] Reporting on the designation characterised the sanctions as a genuine blow to Rwanda, a state that had for years evaded international punishment over its aggression in neighbouring Congo.[1]

## Context

The Kabarebe designation came amid the wider crisis in the eastern DRC involving [[m23|M23]] rebels, Rwandan President [[paul-kagame|Paul Kagame]], and competition over Congolese mineral wealth.[1] Badlands commentary situated the sanctions within a longer arc beginning with the December 2017 asset freeze on Israeli mining oligarch [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]], arguing that subsequent Rwandan operations in the Congo were an attempt to regain control over lost mineral rights.[1]
