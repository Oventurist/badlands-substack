# Yitzchak Ginsburgh

Rabbi Yitzchak Ginsburgh is a kabbalist associated with the [[chabad|Chabad]] movement.[1]

In a letter published in September 2024, Ginsburgh called for the conquest and annexation of southern Lebanon by the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]], joining a chorus of vocal kabbalist leaders who hold that conquering [[lebanon|Lebanon]] will help bring about the anticipated Jewish messiah — an event those figures expect before the end of the Jewish calendar in 2027.[1] Ginsburgh argued that Lebanon was part of the Land of Israel and had been given to the Jewish people by God, citing verses describing the land as stretching to the Euphrates River: "In our generation, God gives us the ability to receive the gift [of Israel] again, to conquer and settle the land."[1] He continued: "Today it is clear that the time has come to conquer the Lebanese territories as well, only in this way can the threat be removed and true peace brought to the entire country. After the conquest and expulsion of the hostile population, a Jewish settlement must be established, thus completing the victory."[1]

Other rabbis echoed the call, with Rabbi Nir Ben Artzi Shalita saying that ending the war with Lebanon would open "a new way for the Messiah and redemption for the people of Israel."[1] Badlands commentary identified this program as [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]] and characterized it as a religious conquest driven by fanatics occupying the most powerful positions in the Israeli government.[1]
