# Reduxx

Reduxx is an independent investigative outlet covering gender ideology, women's rights and child safeguarding, cited by Badlands Media as the source of original reporting on the transgender medical establishment.[1]

## March 2024 WPATH investigation

Reduxx published an exclusive report in March 2024 revealing that [[laura-a-jacobs|Laura A. Jacobs]], a [[wpath|WPATH]]-certified psychotherapist, had served on the committee that established the organization's most recent [[wpath-standards-of-care|Standards of Care]] and had influenced guidance on adolescent gender dysphoria, despite having authored an anthology chapter promoting BDSM practices including "age play" and "genital torture."[1] The reporting was picked up in the Badlands News Brief and paired with commentary from [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]].[1]
