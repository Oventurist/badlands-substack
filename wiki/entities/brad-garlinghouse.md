# Brad Garlinghouse

**Brad Garlinghouse** is the chief executive officer of [[ripple|Ripple]], a cryptocurrency and payments company.[1]

## Position on regulation

Appearing on [[60-minutes|60 Minutes]] in December 2024, Garlinghouse rejected the common framing of the digital asset industry as seeking freedom from oversight, saying: "We haven't been asking to be deregulated. We've been asking to be regulated."[1] Badlands carried the quotation as a bonus item in its news brief for that weekend.[1]
