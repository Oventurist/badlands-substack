# Chris Wallace

Chris Wallace is an American broadcast journalist and former [[cnn|CNN]] anchor whose November 2024 departure from the network was cited as evidence of the decline of corporate cable news.[1]

## Exit from CNN

Writing at [[breitbart-news|Breitbart News]], [[john-nolte|John Nolte]] pointed to Wallace's exit from CNN as one of several signs that the corporate media had "turned the corner into irrelevance," noting that Wallace had been earning a seven-figure salary and arguing that, with the end of pay-TV carriage fees, CNN could no longer afford him.[1] Nolte reported that Wallace appeared set to move into podcasting, and — while predicting he would fail — conceded that the shift indicated where audience attention had migrated.[1] Wallace's departure was listed alongside the firing of the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] editorial board and [[comcast|Comcast]]'s move to sell off [[msnbc|MSNBC]].[1]
