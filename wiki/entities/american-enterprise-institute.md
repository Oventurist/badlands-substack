# American Enterprise Institute

The American Enterprise Institute (AEI) is a Washington think tank characterized in the Badlands corpus as the "storied neocon hotbed" from which much of the modern American interventionist establishment emerged.

## In the corpus

[[assad-day-in-damascus]] identifies AEI as the institution with which the [[project-for-a-new-american-century|Project for a New American Century]] maintained strong ties following PNAC's creation in 1997 by [[william-kristol]] and [[robert-kagan]]. Through that connection AEI is placed within what commentator [[mike-benz]] calls "the Blob" — the Western foreign policy and national security apparatus whose personnel circulate between think tanks, government appointments and the press.

The article's broader argument is that this institutional network produced both the doctrine of [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] and the specific target lists — including [[syria]] — that shaped American policy from the 1990s onward.
