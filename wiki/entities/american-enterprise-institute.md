# American Enterprise Institute

The American Enterprise Institute (AEI) is a Washington think tank described by Badlands Media as a "storied neocon hotbed" with strong ties to the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]].[1]

In the article's account of the intellectual lineage behind U.S. regime-change policy, AEI supplied the institutional milieu from which PNAC emerged in 1997 under [[william-kristol|William Kristol]] and [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]].[1] That network — including [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]], [[paul-wolfowitz|Paul Wolfowitz]], [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], and [[donald-rumsfeld|Donald Rumsfeld]] — advanced the [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] doctrine that the article identifies as the pretext for interventions in Libya, Iraq, Iran, and Syria.[1]
