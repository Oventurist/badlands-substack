# Andrea Mitchell

Andrea Mitchell is an American broadcast journalist and anchor at [[msnbc|MSNBC]].[1]

## March 2024 State of the Union preview

Previewing [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s State of the Union address on March 7, 2024, Mitchell hosted presidential historian [[michael-beschloss|Michael Beschloss]] and asked him to "take a big picture, zoom out for us on what's at stake for [Biden] tonight," noting his comparison to [[franklin-d-roosevelt|FDR]]'s 1941 Four Freedoms speech: "obviously war time, a bigger challenge, but let's, you know, talk about the challenges tonight."[1] Beschloss responded by declaring that the United States "could be a dictatorship next year" if [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] were elected.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] cited the exchange as evidence of escalating media hysteria and projection ahead of the November election.[1]
