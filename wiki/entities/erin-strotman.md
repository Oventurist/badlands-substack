# Erin Strotman

Erin Strotman is a neonatal intensive care unit nurse at [[henrico-doctors-hospital|Henrico Doctors' Hospital]] in Henrico County, Virginia, who was arrested in early January 2025 on felony charges of malicious wounding and child abuse.[1]

## Arrest and charges

Henrico police took Strotman into custody on Thursday, January 2, 2025, and booked her at Henrico Jail West early the following morning.[1] Court records showed the crimes she is accused of both took place on November 10, 2024.[1] The arrest came one week after the hospital announced it was temporarily halting admissions to its NICU following the discovery that three babies had unexplained fractures in late November and December.[1] According to a statement from HCA, which runs the hospital, the circumstances of the recent fractures were similar to incidents involving four different babies at the facility in the summer of 2023.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that the story hit close to home on two counts: the reporting journalist, Melissa Hipolit of CBS 6 Richmond, had previously helped his family expose a corrupt Virginia Commonwealth's Attorney, and his own son had spent a week in the same NICU two years earlier.[1] He singled out as unforgivable that one of the affected families had Child Protective Services remove their baby on the mistaken belief the parents had caused the injuries.[1] The commentary broadened into an argument that American society has lost the instinct to protect children and punish those who harm them, outsourcing its morality to politicians and entertainers, and that reversing course would require a radical rethinking anchored in First Principles, the Bill of Rights, and the original constitutional framework.[1]
