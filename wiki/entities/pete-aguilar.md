# Pete Aguilar

Pete Aguilar is a Democratic U.S. Representative from California and a member of House Democratic leadership.[1]

## Impeachment infighting, December 2025

In December 2025 Aguilar joined House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] and Representative [[katherine-clark|Katharine Clark]] of Massachusetts in a joint statement denouncing efforts by other Democrats to impeach Trump administration Cabinet officials.[1] Articles of impeachment had been filed against Health Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] and Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]], splitting the caucus over whether the procedure was a worthwhile use of members' time.[1]

The leadership statement argued that the impeachment process would require an immense amount of work to succeed, while Jeffries said separately that his focus was on preventing Americans from having their health care "ripped away."[1] Badlands commentary treated the episode with derision, with [[canncon|CannCon]] joking about "aliens and lizard-people" in reference to the dispute.[1]

## Opposition to the weaponization subcommittee

Aguilar, a California Democrat, was quoted in January 2023 objecting to the newly created [[select-subcommittee-on-the-weaponization-of-the-federal-government|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]], saying: "It's in our best interest to make sure we are representing the will of the caucus and the American public, and that Republicans don't have an opportunity behind closed doors to shape, and to add to, these conspiracy theories."[2] Badlands Media cited the remark as an example of establishment Democrats preparing to "slander and impede" anything arising from the [[church-committee|Church Committee]]-style investigations, replying that "the only conspiracy theories allowed in Congress are the ones peddled by the intelligence community via compromised Democrats and the mainstream media."[2]
