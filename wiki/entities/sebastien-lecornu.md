# Sebastien Lecornu

Sebastien Lecornu is the French defence minister who negotiated France's initiative to send military instructors to Ukraine.[1]

## Instructors for Ukraine

In late May 2024, Ukraine's top commander [[oleksandr-syrskyi|Oleksandr Syrskyi]] said he had signed paperwork allowing French military instructors to visit Ukrainian training centres, announcing the step on Telegram after talks by video link with Lecornu.[1] Syrskyi said he was "pleased to welcome France's initiative to send instructors to Ukraine to train Ukrainian servicemen" and that the documents would enable the first French instructors to inspect training centre infrastructure and personnel shortly.[1] He expressed a belief that France's determination would encourage other partners to join the "ambitious project."[1]

Ukraine's Defence Ministry subsequently issued a "clarification" on X stating that Kyiv had been expressing interest in receiving foreign instructors since February and that discussions with France and other countries remained ongoing.[1]
