# David Sheen

David Sheen is a journalist who reports on Israeli politics and the Israeli far right. A lecture he delivered in May 2022 was highlighted in Badlands Media commentary as a framework for understanding the factional structure of the Israeli political system.[1]

## Four-movement framework

According to Sheen's analysis, Israel's dozens of individual political parties can be reduced to four broad movements: the Theocrats, the Nationalists, the Liberals, and the Socialists. The Nationalist movement — the Zionists — has historically been the largest and most dominant, holding roughly 70 percent of the seats in the [[knesset|Knesset]], and is itself divided between left-wing Labor Zionists and right-wing [[revisionist-zionism|Revisionist Zionists]]. The Theocrats account for roughly 20 percent of the Knesset, with the Liberals and Socialists comprising the remainder.[1]

Sheen described the Zionist current as seeking domination of the Palestinians within a single apartheid state, but as secularists grounding their discourse in rational argument, under which Palestinians would remain in place as second-class citizens. The Theocrats likewise favour a single state, but one free of all gentiles; being religious and, in Sheen's account, inherently pacifist, they do not advance operational plans to bring that about, leaving its realization to God.[1]

## The "Monarchists"

Sheen's central contribution, as relayed by Badlands, is the identification of a fifth movement that emerged in the 1980s, which he terms the [[monarchism-israeli-political-movement|Monarchists]] — a fusion of what he considers the worst ideas of the Nationalists and the Theocrats, producing a doctrine of eliminationism that necessarily entails genocide, coupled with expansionist ambitions toward the [[greater-israel-project|Greater Israel project]].[1] Sheen argued that this faction is most dangerous precisely when Israel is on the verge of signing a peace agreement with the Palestinians — an observation Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] emphasized as predating the October 7 attack by more than a year.[1]

## Reception

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cited the lecture in July 2024 to explain [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s reliance, after his 2020 corruption charges, on radical Theocrats and Monarchists — including [[bezalel-smotrich|Bezalel Smotrich]] and [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]] — following the loss of centrist Nationalist support.[1]
