# Georgy Mazurashu

Georgy Mazurashu is a Ukrainian member of parliament who publicly condemned Kiev's mobilization campaign in July 2025.[1]

## Criticism of mobilization

Speaking to the Superpozitsiya YouTube channel on Tuesday, July 29, 2025, Mazurashu said the actions of enlistment officers "under the guise of so-called mobilization work" had been "horrifying," and that the country's soldiers are treated as "slaves."[1] He described the drive as a "shameful hunt for citizens" and argued that it was failing in its primary purpose of replenishing the ranks of a military experiencing persistent shortages of frontline "trench and assault" personnel.[1]

"This has an extremely negative impact on both the spirit of the citizens and the economy... The consequences are extremely negative and large-scale for defense capabilities," he said.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] compared the described conscription regime to the slave army depicted in the film 300, arguing that the only reason to resort to such measures is that the masters see the conflict as existential.[1] He asked what the American public would do upon learning the horrors committed in Ukraine, and what the world would do when it became clear that the [[state-department|US State Department]] and NGO partners were behind them.[1]
