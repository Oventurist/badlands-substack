# Boston Consulting Group

Boston Consulting Group (BCG) is a large American management consulting firm that came under scrutiny in June 2025 for its role in designing and operating a controversial US- and Israeli-backed aid program in Gaza.[1]

## Role in the Gaza Humanitarian Foundation

BCG was hired in the autumn of 2024 to help design the program that became the [[gaza-humanitarian-foundation|Gaza Humanitarian Foundation]] (GHF) and to run its business operations.[1] According to the firm's own statement, it agreed in October 2024 to provide pro bono support to help establish an aid organisation intended to operate alongside multilateral efforts in Gaza.[1] In addition to developing the initiative in close coordination with Israel, BCG set the prices for paying and equipping the contractors who built four distribution hubs in southern Gaza.[1]

On the Friday before the story broke, BCG withdrew the team it had operating on the ground in Tel Aviv, terminated its contract with GHF, and placed one of the senior partners leading the project on administrative leave pending an internal review.[1] The firm said that "unapproved follow-on work relating to Gaza lacked buy-in from multilateral stakeholders and was stopped on May 30," and that it had begun a formal review.[1] People connected to both organisations, speaking anonymously, said it would be difficult for the foundation to continue functioning without the consultants who created it.[1]

## Reputational context

Reporting noted that US consulting firms had become acutely aware of the reputational risks of their international work following a series of controversies.[1] BCG chair Rich Lesser was called before [[congress|Congress]] in the prior year alongside [[mckinsey-and-company|McKinsey]] chief Bob Sternfels to explain work performed for the Saudi government, while McKinsey's work in China had prompted calls from some Republican lawmakers to strip it of federal contracts.[1]

## Badlands commentary and historical links

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] reported that BCG had already surfaced as a focus in a roundtable live-dig he joined with Colonel Towner-Watkins and Brady Fuchs, for reasons unrelated to the Gaza story.[1] He noted that [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] worked at BCG between 1976 and 1978 as an economic analyst alongside [[mitt-romney|Mitt Romney]], and that the two are said to have formed a lifelong bond from the shared experience.[1] He further observed that BCG assists Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] in planning and executing the [[vision-2030|Vision 2030]] agenda, while cautioning that he was "just sharing relevant datapoints" rather than asserting a connection.[1]

He argued that the contract termination and the partner's suspension implied potential legal or even criminal liability, and highlighted a contradiction between BCG's claim that the work was pro bono and another source's claim that the firm had sent million-dollar invoices.[1]
