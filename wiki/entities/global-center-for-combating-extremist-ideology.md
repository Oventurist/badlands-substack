# Global Center for Combating Extremist Ideology

The Global Center for Combating Extremist Ideology — referred to in Badlands contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]]'s account simply as the "Center to Combat Islamic Extremism" — was inaugurated in [[riyadh|Riyadh]] during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s May 2017 visit to [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], alongside the [[arab-islamic-american-summit|Arab Islamic American Summit]].[1]

## The Orb ceremony

The Wall Street Journal, quoted in the article, reported that Trump produced one of the most memorable photographs of his presidency when he placed his hands on a glowing glass Orb at the center's opening.[1] Saudi officials had created the Orb weeks before the summit as a dramatic prop, envisioning the mini-globe as a tangible symbol of a fight against extremism that must unite all nations, and choreographed a plan for the American president to walk into the operations room with [[salman-bin-abdulaziz|King Salman]] and "activate" the center by touching it.[1]

## Interpretation

Lange presents the dedication as a public declaration that the Kingdom was joining Trump's fight against Islamic extremism, contrasting it with what he characterizes as [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s tendency to make excuses for such extremism.[1] He argues that Saudi Arabia had promoted Islamic extremism for many years and asserts — promising to make the case in a subsequent article — that [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Alwaleed bin Talal]] was the man behind it, with the Saudi leadership now making a clear break from that past because Trump demanded it.[1]

A third leader also placed a hand on the Orb: Egyptian president [[abdel-fattah-el-sisi|Abdel Fattah el-Sisi]].[1] Lange treats his presence as purposeful — a signal to the world that three leaders were going to war against Islamic extremists, with Sisi having already fought and defeated the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] inside [[egypt|Egypt]].[1]
