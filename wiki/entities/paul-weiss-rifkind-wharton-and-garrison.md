# Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP

Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP is a major American law firm that became the subject of a presidential executive order in March 2025.[1]

## Agreement with the Trump administration

[[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] issued an executive order concerning the firm on March 14, 2025.[1] On March 20, 2025, Trump announced via Truth Social that he had agreed to withdraw the order after Paul, Weiss entered into an agreement with him.[1]

Under the terms announced by the president, the firm agreed that "the bedrock principle of American Justice is that it must be fair and nonpartisan for all," and that the justice system is betrayed when it is misused to achieve political ends.[1] The agreement stated that law firms should not favor any political party in choosing their clients, and should not make hiring decisions based on political affiliation, since to do otherwise denies some Americans an equal opportunity for legal services while favoring others.[1] It further asserted that lawyers abandon the profession's highest ideals when they engage in partisan decision-making and betray the ethical obligation to represent those who are unpopular or disfavored.[1] Badlands framed the episode as a capitulation by the firm to the administration.[1]
