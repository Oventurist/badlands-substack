# Russian Orthodox Church

The Russian Orthodox Church is the dominant Christian body in Russia and, under [[patriarch-kirill|Patriarch Kirill]], has positioned itself as a self-described spiritual counterweight to what its leadership characterizes as Western moral decline.[1]

## January 2025 remarks on gender transition

In a televised interview aired on Orthodox Christian Christmas in January 2025, Patriarch Kirill said that Western acceptance of gender reassignment surgery "smells of apocalypse," describing the trend as a marker of moral decay and reaffirming Russia's commitment to preserving its own spiritual traditions.[1] He said such practices were "against God's law" and would never be accepted in Russia so long as its people remained faithful to their millennia-old cultural and spiritual heritage.[1]

Kirill also cited church construction as evidence of Russia's religious vitality, saying observers "are astonished that we are building churches – 400 in Moscow alone," and contrasting this with Western churches being repurposed "in the best case into mosques, and in the worst case into entertainment venues."[1] The remarks followed Russia's November 2024 ban on the adoption of children by countries that permit gender-reassignment procedures.[1]
