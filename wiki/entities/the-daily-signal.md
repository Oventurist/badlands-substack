# The Daily Signal

The Daily Signal is a conservative news outlet that reports on federal and state policy from a right-of-center perspective. It is among the sources aggregated in the Badlands News Brief series published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands Media

The June 16, 2023 Badlands News Brief excerpted The Daily Signal's reporting on a June 13 hearing of the California Senate Judiciary Committee concerning [[ab-957|AB 957]], the bill that would have written "gender affirmation" into the California Family Code.[1] The outlet reported that state senator [[scott-wilk|Scott Wilk]] told parents attending the hearing to flee the state, and had previously reported that the bill would expose non-affirming parents to child abuse charges.[1]
