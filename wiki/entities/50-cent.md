# 50 Cent

50 Cent is an American rapper cited in Badlands Media commentary as a source for claims about the breakup of [[ben-affleck|Ben Affleck]] and [[jennifer-lopez|Jennifer Lopez]] amid the [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]] scandal.[1]

## Claims attributed to 50 Cent

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that, according to 50 Cent, Affleck left Lopez after learning of her involvement in Combs's parties — conduct the essay characterized as something "even a celebrity in the Hollywood cesspool can't come back from."[1] The essay paired this with a timeline running from the March 25, 2024 [[fbi|FBI]] raid on Combs's home to the couple's August 20, 2024 divorce filing.[1]

## Context

50 Cent's remarks function in the essay as celebrity-sourced corroboration for its argument that entertainers connected to Combs were panicking and separating themselves from him.[1] Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the opinions of contributing authors.[1]
