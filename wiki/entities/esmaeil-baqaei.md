# Esmaeil Baqaei

Esmaeil Baqaei is a spokesman for [[iran|Iran]]'s Foreign Ministry.[1]

## Contradiction of the Doha announcement

After President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced in June 2026 that Iran had requested talks and that a meeting would occur in Doha, [[qatar|Qatar]], Baqaei said publicly that no negotiations with the United States were scheduled "at any level" in the coming days.[1] He nonetheless confirmed that an Iranian delegation would travel to Doha for technical discussions related to implementing the existing US-Iran memorandum of understanding.[1] The conflicting statements followed renewed military exchanges in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] that had strained the recently established ceasefire.[1]
