# Esmaeil Baqaei

Esmaeil Baqaei is the spokesman for the Foreign Ministry of the Islamic Republic of [[iran|Iran]].

## Contradiction of the Doha announcement (June 2026)

"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes" reports that after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] announced that Iran had requested talks and that a meeting would be held Tuesday in Doha, [[qatar|Qatar]], Baqaei publicly denied the characterization. He stated that no negotiations with the United States were scheduled "at any level" in the coming days, while confirming that an Iranian delegation would travel to Doha for technical discussions related to implementing the existing U.S.–Iran memorandum of understanding.

The Brief presents Baqaei's statement as an instance of deliberate diplomatic ambiguity: the two governments described the same trip in incompatible terms even as [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] and [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]] departed for Qatar, and even as renewed exchanges in the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] tested the ceasefire.
