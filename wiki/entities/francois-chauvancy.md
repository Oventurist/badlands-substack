# Francois Chauvancy

Francois Chauvancy is a retired French brigadier general and former NATO officer who has become a frequent commentator on European defense matters.[1]

## Criticism of the 2025 Ukraine aid scheme

In remarks to *Le Figaro* published over the weekend before July 14, 2025, Chauvancy warned that Washington's plans to funnel additional military aid to Ukraine through [[nato|NATO]] would run up against a lack of both funds and hardware.[1] "I don't see how NATO can purchase weapons for a third country," he said. "It doesn't have the financial means and can't empty its stockpiles in the areas it controls, as they are few in number. Finally, it can't deliver weapons overnight without approval from NATO structures."[1]

Chauvancy further argued that U.S. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had been "primarily aiming for publicity," and that Trump's actions on the Ukraine conflict were principally directed at the American public: "As always, domestic politics govern his foreign policy."[1]

The scheme Chauvancy criticized was unveiled by Trump on July 14 while hosting NATO Secretary General [[mark-rutte|Mark Rutte]] at the [[white-house|White House]]; under it, European NATO members would bankroll the aid and Washington's envoy to NATO, [[matthew-whitaker|Matthew Whitaker]], would coordinate it.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cited Chauvancy's remarks as evidence that Trump is deliberately "bankrupting NATO," pushing the regime "face-first into the table saw" by taking European demands for war with Russia at face value while removing the expectation that the U.S. taxpayer would foot the bill.[1]
