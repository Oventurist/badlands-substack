# George Mason University

George Mason University is a public university in Virginia, located outside the nation's capital, whose Schar School of Policy and Government announced in September 2025 that it would host a former U.S. vice president as an instructor.[1]

## Mike Pence appointment

Former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] was announced as an incoming instructor in politics at the Schar School of Policy and Government for the spring term.[1] He was slated to teach students studying political science, law, public administration, and related fields.[1] According to the university, Pence would teach seminars that "explore the intersection of politics, leadership, and national governance."[1] Pence had served as vice president to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from 2017 to 2021.[1]
