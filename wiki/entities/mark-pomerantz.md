# Mark Pomerantz

Mark Pomerantz is a former Manhattan prosecutor who investigated [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his businesses, and who became the target of a threatened defamation action by Trump in January 2023.[1]

## Defamation dispute

Trump retained trial attorney [[joe-tacopina|Joe Tacopina]] to pursue Pomerantz for allegedly defaming him.[1] In his first action for the former president, Tacopina sent a letter to Pomerantz on Monday, January 23, 2023, claiming Pomerantz had falsely stated that Trump was "guilty of numerous felony violations" and that it was a "grave failure of justice not to hold [Trump] accountable by way of criminal prosecution."[1] The letter warned: "I strongly admonish you to take these next words seriously: If you publish such a book and continue making defamatory statements against my client, my office will aggressively pursue all legal remedies against you and your book publisher, Simon & Schuster."[1]

The dispute centred on Pomerantz's forthcoming book, *The People vs. Donald Trump*.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] characterised Pomerantz as having "frivolously pursued Trump and his businesses for two years" without success, and as planning to "grift off of his failures to 'get Trump'" through the book.[1]
