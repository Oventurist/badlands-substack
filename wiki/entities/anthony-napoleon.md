# Anthony Napoleon

Anthony Napoleon is described in the Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" as a psychoanalyst, lawyer and researcher of the occult, and is the author of *Shadow Men: An Encyclopedia of Mind Control*.[1]

## Thesis

In *Shadow Men*, Napoleon argues that a consistent and reliable modus operandi has been used throughout history by a class of covert rulers he calls the [[shadow-men|shadow men]], who tend to be intelligent psychopaths.[1] Their central innovation was learning to track the motion of the heavens and to assign socio-religious significance to celestial movements, eventually predicting such events with staggering accuracy.[1] Combined with carefully seeded narratives casting those predictions as proof of communion with the gods, this allowed them to win the trust of a superstitious citizenry.[1]

The article treats Napoleon's argument as convergent with anthropological evidence that peoples in antiquity worshipped and revered heavenly bodies, together establishing a link between astrology and occult groups — the phenomenon it labels [[astrotheology|astrotheology]].[1]
