# Thomas J. Devine

Thomas J. Devine was a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] staff officer and business partner of [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] whose career Badlands Media presents as evidence that Bush's oil ventures functioned as agency cover.[1]

## Zapata and commercial cover

Citing Wikipedia's article on HRG Group, Badlands noted that Zapata Petroleum Corporation was founded in 1953 by Bush along with John Overbey, Hugh Liedtke, Bill Liedtke and Thomas J. Devine.[1] A CIA internal memo dated November 29, 1975 stated that Zapata Petroleum began in 1953 through Bush's joint efforts with Devine, a CIA staffer who had resigned his agency position that same year to enter private business but who continued to work for the CIA under commercial cover.[1]

## Vietnam, 1967

Devine later accompanied Bush to Vietnam in late 1967 as a "cleared and witting commercial asset" of the agency, acted as his informal foreign affairs advisor, and maintained a close relationship with him through 1975.[1] Badlands writer [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treated the 1967 trip as "a key data point," pointing out that it coincided with the [[secret-war-in-laos|secret CIA war in Laos]] to protect the opium trade and with the [[phoenix-program|Phoenix Program]], which he characterized as a CIA death squad program targeting civilians, and argued the timing was not coincidental.[1]

In this reading, Devine's presence establishes that Bush's claim to have had no involvement with the CIA before 1975 was false, and that [[zapata-offshore|Zapata Offshore]] was a front company from the beginning.[1]
