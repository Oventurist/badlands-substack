# Roger Hallam

**Roger Hallam** is a co-founder of the British climate protest group [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]].[1]

Although the organization professes non-violence on the surface, [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] argues that deeper ideological motives are visible in Hallam's own words.[1] Speaking at an [[amnesty-international|Amnesty International]] meeting on 4 February 2019, Hallam said: "We are going to force the governments to act. And if they don't, we will bring them down and create a democracy fit for purpose. And yes, some may die in the process."[1]

When members questioned how Extinction Rebellion's revenue from billionaires squared with its opposition to the capitalist system, Hallam replied that the oligarchs "cry at night, just as we do. We don't want them to commit suicide. No! We want them to ring us up and give us that million quid."[1]
