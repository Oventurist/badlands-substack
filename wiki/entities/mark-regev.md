# Mark Regev

Mark Regev is an Israeli government spokesman and adviser to Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], frequently deployed to explain Israeli military and diplomatic positions to international broadcasters.[1]

## Response to the Beirut strike

Following the January 2, 2024 drone strike in Beirut that killed Hamas deputy chief [[saleh-al-arouri|Saleh al-Arouri]], Regev told MSNBC that Israel had not taken responsibility for the attack.[1] He nonetheless argued that "whoever did it, it must be clear: That this was not an attack on the Lebanese state," and characterized the operation as "a surgical strike against the Hamas leadership."[1] The formulation — neither claiming nor denying the strike while framing its limited scope — was consistent with Israel's practice of not commenting on foreign media reports of its operations, a position the [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli military]] reiterated to Reuters.[1]
