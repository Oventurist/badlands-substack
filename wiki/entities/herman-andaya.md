# Herman Andaya

Herman Andaya is the former director of the Maui County Emergency Management Agency, the official most publicly associated with the failed emergency response to the [[2023-maui-wildfires|2023 Maui wildfires]] that destroyed the town of Lahaina.[1]

## Role in the Lahaina response controversy

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that reporting on the whereabouts of Hawaii's emergency leadership provided convenient narrative cover for the devastating response: the top officials were not on Maui when the fires began, framed as "rotten luck" rather than fault, producing plausible deniability "along with an effective patsy in former Maui County Emergency Management Agency director Herman Andaya."[1] She predicted that government accountability over Lahaina was coming to an end.[1]

The context was an August 23, 2023, report that the heads of the Maui and Hawaii emergency management agencies had been at an annual disaster-response conference on Oahu on August 8, the day fires began leveling Lahaina, a fact confirmed to HawaiiNewsNow by the [[fema|Federal Emergency Management Agency]].[1]
