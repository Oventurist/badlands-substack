# Jared Isaacman

Jared Isaacman is a billionaire private astronaut and former SpaceX customer who was sworn in as the fifteenth administrator of [[nasa|NASA]] in December 2025.[1]

## NASA administrator

Isaacman took the oath of office as NASA's 15th administrator on Thursday, December 18, 2025.[1] Hours after his swearing-in, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] issued a sweeping executive order, [[ensuring-american-space-superiority|"Ensuring American Space Superiority"]], that enshrined the United States goal of returning astronauts to the Moon by 2028 and directed the defense of American assets in space against weapon threats.[1] The sequencing of the two events tied Isaacman's tenure directly to the administration's first major space policy action of its second term.[1]

Isaacman came to the post from the commercial spaceflight world rather than the traditional government or aerospace-contractor career track, having flown as a private astronaut on missions purchased from SpaceX.[1] His appointment coincided with an executive order that urged greater efficiency among private space contractors, reflecting the administration's reliance on commercial providers for its lunar timeline.[1]
