# Homes for the Homeless

Homes for the Homeless is a New York City nonprofit organization contracted by the city to operate shelter facilities, including a migrant shelter at the site of the shuttered Island Shores Senior Residences on Father Capodanno Boulevard in the Midland Beach section of Staten Island.[1]

## Voter registration controversy

In December 2023, Representative [[nicole-malliotakis|Nicole Malliotakis]] and four other Staten Island Republicans held a press conference near the former Island Shores facility to allege that the city's contract with Homes for the Homeless contained a "smoking gun" proving an intent to register non-citizens to vote.[1] According to Malliotakis, page 50 of the contract — obtained through a state Freedom of Information Law request — contains an entire section dedicated to voter registration, requiring that the nonprofit "shall provide and distribute voter registration forms to all persons," with forms made available in Spanish and Chinese.[1] The stipulation reflects a requirement of the New York City charter, but the Republican officials characterized its application to asylum seekers housed at the shelter as both unlawful and underhanded, since asylum seekers are by definition not citizens eligible to vote.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] argued that the contract language amounted to evidence that the city intended to pad the voter rolls, noting that a 2021 New York City measure allowing non-citizen residents to vote in municipal elections had been struck down by a judge under a permanent injunction.[1] The Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] framed the episode as confirmation that "padding the rolls is critical path for the fraud machine to function."[1]
