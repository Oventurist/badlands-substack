# Floyd Mayweather

Floyd Mayweather is an American boxer described in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as one of the best boxers of the modern era and simultaneously one of the most reviled by the general public.[1]

## Use as a narrative analogy

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used Mayweather as an analogy for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s relationship to the media.[1] Mayweather, he argued, was famous less in the sporting sense than infamous, because he prioritized selling himself over selling his fights; as a result his fights sold anyway, with audiences buying either to watch him win or in the desperate hope of seeing him lose.[1] By making himself the story, Mayweather provoked opponents into playing into the narrative he had concocted, producing a fighting style that was among the least aesthetically pleasing of any of boxing's greats — and he was also a counter striker.[1]

Burning Bright drew the parallel that from the moment Trump first descended the golden escalator, he too made himself the story, caring far less what the story being bought or sold was so long as it was about him.[1] The conclusion he drew was that the [[deep-state|Deep State]] has no story to sell that does not involve Trump, which he did not regard as a winning proposition for its side.[1]
