# Jim Cramer

Jim Cramer is a CNBC host and financial commentator, best known for the network's stock-picking programming.[1]

## Reaction to the Public Square listing (2023)

When the conservative marketplace [[public-square|Public Square]] rang the opening bell at the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] on July 20, 2023 and the floor broke into chants of "USA! USA!", Cramer was reported to be visibly unamused, nodding and shaking his head in disapproval, while colleague [[david-faber|David Faber]] covered his ears.[1] Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme, describing Cramer as "the world's worst financial analyst," treated the reaction as evidence that overt patriotism was anathema to establishment financial media.[1]
