# Larry Sinclair

Larry Sinclair is an American man who has claimed for roughly 25 years that he and former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] engaged in drug-fueled sexual encounters, once in the back of a limousine and again in a hotel room in Gurnee, Illinois.[1] He first made the allegations publicly in 2008, while Obama was campaigning for the presidency, and they were widely dismissed at the time.[1]

## Credibility disputes

Sinclair has a lengthy criminal record, including convictions on forgery, fraud and larceny charges, and has served prison time in Arizona, Colorado and Florida.[1] That history has been widely cited by critics to discredit his account.[1]

## 2023 Tucker Carlson interview

In September 2023, Sinclair recounted his allegations in an interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] released on X.[1] Sinclair described being introduced to Obama by a limousine driver after asking for help finding someone to "party" with, saying, "There's this guy that's introduced to me as Barack Obama."[1] He alleged he gave Obama $250 to pay for cocaine, and that while he prepared a line to snort, Obama produced "a little pipe" and smoked.[1] Asked by Carlson how he could not have recognized his companion after two sexual encounters and shared drug use, Sinclair answered, "I had no idea who he was," adding, "It definitely wasn't Barack's first time and I would almost be one to bet it wasn't his last."[1]

The interview circulated alongside earlier reporting by Obama biographer David Garrow, who wrote that Obama had told an ex-girlfriend he "repeatedly fantasizes about making love to men."[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] covered the interview, noting that the resulting online discussion also revived speculation about [[michelle-obama|Michelle Obama]].[1]

## Commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] said he generally avoids sensational coverage of elite "trysts" because such debauchery is secondary to — or a side effect of — what he characterizes as the deeper ideological evils of the ruling class, but conceded that this particular story was entertaining, and speculated that establishment outlets would eventually claim they had always known about Obama's orientation.[1]
