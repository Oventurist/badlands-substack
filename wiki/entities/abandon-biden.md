# Abandon Biden

Abandon Biden is a nationwide campaign promoted by Muslim American community leaders urging voters to withhold support from President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in the 2024 presidential election over his handling of the Israel–Gaza war.[1]

## Origins and grievances

The campaign emerged among constituencies angered by Biden's lopsided support for Israel and his failure to restrain an Israel Defense Forces campaign that had caused tens of thousands of civilian casualties, including a high proportion of children.[1] Michigan, which has one of the largest Muslim populations in America, became the campaign's central battleground.[1]

## Relationship to Listen to Michigan

An allied effort operating under the moniker [[listen-to-michigan|Listen to Michigan]], championed by Representative [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]], directed the same energy into a concrete ballot tactic during the [[2024-michigan-democratic-primary|2024 Michigan Democratic primary]], where more than 100,000 Democrats voted "uncommitted."[1] Together the two efforts constitute the most visible expression of the [[uncommitted-movement|uncommitted movement]].[1]
