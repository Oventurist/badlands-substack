# Tru Reporting

Tru Reporting is a pseudonymous host affiliated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]. He co-hosts the **NCSWIC Podcast** with [[absolute-1776|Absolute 1776]], airing Sundays at 7:30pm ET in the network's December 2022 schedule.[1]

The program takes a weekly deep dive into new [[information-war|Info War]] topics, framed around [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] and drawing on the hindsight and accumulated context that the hosts argue emerges over time.[1] Its title derives from the movement slogan "Nothing Can Stop What Is Coming."[1]
