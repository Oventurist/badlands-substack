# Badlands Story Hour

Badlands Story Hour is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program listed among the new additions to the network's lineup in the January 16–22, 2023 schedule.[1] It airs Tuesdays at 9pm ET.[1]

## Format and hosts

The show is a discussion of entertainment, movies, and storytelling, framed through the contrasting professional backgrounds of its two hosts: [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]], who comes from Hollywood, and [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], who comes from the publishing industry.[1] That pairing gives the program a craft-oriented perspective on narrative distinct from the network's news and geopolitics programming.[1]

## Distribution

Live streams and replays were carried on the main Badlands Media [[rumble|Rumble]] channel following the network's January 2023 consolidation of its streaming.[1]
