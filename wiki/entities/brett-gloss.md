# Brett Gloss

Brett Gloss is an [[fbi|FBI]] agent named in congressional oversight material as one of three bureau employees suspended in connection with the January 6, 2021 Capitol incursion.[1]

## Suspension and whistleblower testimony

An interim staff report released on May 18, 2023 by the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] and its [[select-subcommittee-on-the-weaponization-of-the-federal-government|Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]] revealed that at least three FBI agents had been suspended or had their security clearances revoked over January 6, identifying Gloss along with [[marcus-allen|Marcus Allen]] and [[stephen-friend|Stephen Friend]] as suspended while their cases were reviewed.[1] The report stated that the bureau "weaponized the security clearance adjudication process to silence employees who fight against the politicized 'rot' within the FBI leadership," and incorporated new whistleblower testimony describing "egregious abuse, misallocation of law enforcement resources and misconduct with the leadership ranks of the FBI."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] read the suspensions as confirmation that the bureau was targeting agents who would not toe its leadership's political line, noting that opponents attacked the whistleblowers personally rather than disputing the status assigned to them by [[jim-jordan|Jim Jordan]].[1]
