# Cyber Ninjas

Cyber Ninjas is a cybersecurity contractor that served as the lead firm in the [[maricopa-county-forensic-audit|Maricopa County forensic audit]] of the 2020 general election.[1]

More than a month after the Republican-led [[arizona-state-senate|Arizona Senate]] won a legal battle over access to tabulation machines and roughly 2.1 million ballots from [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]]'s 2020 general election, the Senate announced that it had hired four out-of-state companies — Wake Technology Services, CyFIR, Digital Discovery and Cyber Ninjas — to conduct a forensic audit and full hand recount, with a report expected within about 60 days.[1]

The resulting review produced a hand recount that slightly increased [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s certified total, a result widely publicized as vindication of the official count, alongside a ballot-level analysis that identified 49,718 questionable votes of critical, high or medium severity.[1]
