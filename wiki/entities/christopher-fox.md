# Christopher Fox

Christopher Fox is identified as the Intelligence Community Inspector General to whom material generated by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]]'s review of the 2020 election cycle was referred for further review and possible declassification.[1]

## Role in the Gabbard referral

According to unclassified memos reviewed by [[just-the-news|Just the News]], whistleblower allegations that intelligence agencies concealed evidence of foreign election threats — including claims involving [[china|China]] and [[venezuela|Venezuela]] — were surfaced during a broader review conducted by Gabbard's now-disbanded [[directors-initiative-group|Director's Initiative Group]]. That material was recently referred to Fox in his capacity as [[intelligence-community-inspector-general|Intelligence Community Inspector General]] for further review and possible declassification.[1]

The referral places Fox at the procedural center of the allegations, which include claims that officials downplayed or omitted information about Chinese election interference from final assessments, congressional briefings, and the President's Daily Brief.[1]
