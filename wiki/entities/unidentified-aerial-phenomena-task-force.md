# Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force

The Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force (UAPTF) was a United States government body established to investigate UFOs, now officially termed [[unidentified-anomalous-phenomena|unidentified anomalous phenomena]] (UAP).[1] It was launched by the Navy under the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security.[1]

Member agencies detailed representatives to the task force: [[david-grusch|David Grusch]] served as the [[national-reconnaissance-office|National Reconnaissance Office]]'s representative from 2019 to 2021, and later as the [[national-geospatial-intelligence-agency|National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]]'s representative and co-lead for UAP analysis from late 2021 to July 2022.[1] The task force was subsequently reorganized into the [[all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office|All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office]], whose remit includes objects that operate underwater.[1]
