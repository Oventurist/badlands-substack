# Taylor Swift

Taylor Swift is a global music superstar who became a focus of American political discourse during the 2024 presidential campaign.[1] According to reporting by the [[new-york-times|New York Times]], aides to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] drafting wish lists of potential campaign surrogates — including elected officials and social media influencers — regarded a Swift endorsement as "the endorsement of their wildest dreams."[1]

## Role in the 2024 narrative war

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], described the "Taylor Swift deployment" as an "odd, Ouroborosian white pill," observing that more ordinary Americans had raised it as a psychological operation — even without using that terminology — than any other operation the Truth Community had chronicled over the preceding eight years.[1]

He argued that sports remain the major fulcrum of American entertainment culture and are more important today for the dissemination of sociopolitical propaganda than a decade ago, particularly given the self-induced collapse of Hollywood and much of the entertainment industrial complex.[1] By shamelessly and transparently merging the narrative projection capacity of the biggest sports league with the biggest national music icon, the commentary held, the Establishment was unwittingly baring its fangs in the open, signaling its fear of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s return and red-pilling masses of casual sports fans as to the coordination and falsity of the system.[1]

## "Is There a Taylor Swift Psyop?" (January 2024)

In January 2024 [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] published a piece asking whether Swift's media saturation amounted to a psychological operation, noting that Bloomberg data showed corporate media headlines featuring her name ramping up in the first half of 2023 and hitting record highs later that year.[2] The report was skeptical of legacy media's framing of her relationship with Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce as a "perfect match," writing that the pairing "doesn't pass the sniff test" given her prior romantic history.[2]

Central to the piece was [[michael-benz|Michael Benz]], a former Trump administration [[state-department|State Department]] official whose work has been cited in congressional hearings, who posted a 2019 video on X featuring the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s psychological operations research arm floating the idea of turning Swift into an asset to combat "disinformation."[2] While it remained unproven whether Swift had become a [[nato|NATO]] asset, the article noted Democrats appeared eager to convert "Swifties" into blue voters ahead of the presidential election.[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that ridiculing ordinary Americans for discussing Swift psyops while ignoring years of prior Truth Community claims is self-defeating, likening the process to teaching a child to ride a bike with training wheels before sending them down a mountain.[2] He held that the Swift–Kelce–Chiefs–Super Bowl phenomenon was merging segments of the mainstream "Normie hivemind" that never otherwise interact and forcing them to confront government-sponsored influence operations directly, in the middle of the most contentious election cycle in American history and at an all-time high in the magnitude and velocity of information dissemination.[2] He characterized this as a positive development and urged readers to "watch the Establishment devour itself" rather than lecture newcomers about the finer nuances of conspiracy theories.[2]

## 2025 Trump comparison

In August 2025, while praising [[sydney-sweeney|Sydney Sweeney]]'s [[american-eagle-sydney-sweeney-ad-controversy|American Eagle advertisement]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] compared Sweeney favorably against "Woke singer Taylor Swift."[3] Trump had previously said he "hates" Swift after she endorsed his election opponent, Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], in October, calling the singer "NO LONGER HOT."[3]

## Ticketmaster fiasco and the DOJ suit

Swift was at the center of the 2022 [[ticketmaster|Ticketmaster]] presale failure, when the platform crashed as fans competed with bots for a limited number of tickets to her Eras Tour.[4] More than a year later, on May 23, 2024, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced an antitrust lawsuit against Ticketmaster's parent company, [[live-nation-entertainment|Live Nation Entertainment]], accusing it of unlawfully dominating the market, with Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] declaring, "It is time to break it up."[4] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that "Taylor Swift is truly privileged," noting that ordinary concertgoers had endured Ticketmaster problems for years without federal action and asking why it took Swift's anger for the DOJ to intervene.[4]
