# Miles Guo

**Miles Guo** is an exiled Chinese businessman and dissident who was the target of a foreign influence operation prosecuted by the [[us-department-of-justice|United States Department of Justice]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] wrote that the entertainer [[pras-michel|Pras Michel]] "took part in an influence operation against the Trump Administration and DOJ to get Miles Guo extradicted to China."[1] The underlying Justice Department case described an unregistered back-channel campaign, conducted at the direction of [[jho-low|Jho Low]] and of the Vice Minister of Public Security for the People's Republic of China, whose objectives included having a Chinese national sent back to China alongside the shelving of the [[1mdb-scandal|1MDB]] embezzlement investigation.[1]
