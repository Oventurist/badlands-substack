# Fabian Society

The Fabian Society is a British socialist organization founded in 1884 that advocated gradualist rather than revolutionary social transformation. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats the Society as a long-running partner of the [[rhodes-scholarship|Rhodes Scholarship]] network in the service of a re-organized British Empire.

## Role in the source's argument

The article states that the Rhodes Scholarship program "had worked alongside the Fabian Society for over a century," jointly producing a stratum of trained personnel distributed across media, education, government, military and corporate institutions.

## The Canadian branch

A footnote in the article describes a self-professed "Fabian Society of Canada" established at the height of the Depression by five Rhodes Scholars "in order to create a Canadian fascist regime in 1932." This body, identified as the [[league-of-social-reconstruction|League for Social Reconstruction]], founded the Cooperative Commonwealth Federation, which was renamed the [[new-democratic-party|New Democratic Party]] in 1961. Ehret adds the caveat that "good people have found themselves members of the NDP and Liberals over the years," while maintaining that "this rotten core tied to the highest echelons of the British oligarchy is real."
