# Kevin Wallace

Kevin Wallace is a lawyer in the New York attorney general's office who conducted the state's direct examination of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the former president's November 2023 testimony in the state's civil fraud case.[1]

## Examination of Donald Trump

Wallace opened the questioning by asking Trump to confirm that he had headed the Trump Organization between May 1, 1981 and January 19, 2017.[1] Rather than answer narrowly, Trump used the question to characterize Wallace as one of "every Democratic district attorney, attorney general and US Attorney who were coming after me from fifteen sides, all different cases, all Trump haters, all cases that are not good," concluding with the single word "Weaponization."[1]

The examination proceeded to the heart of the state's theory — that Trump had inflated the stated value of his real-estate holdings to obtain more favorable commercial loans and insurance policies.[1] Trump responded that banks had not treated his statements of financial condition as an important element of their decision-making, and asserted that his brand added millions of dollars of value to his properties, prompting Judge [[arthur-engoron|Arthur Engoron]] to instruct him to "just answer the questions, no speeches."[1] Defense counsel [[christopher-kise|Christopher Kise]] objected that Wallace's questions "call for narratives."[1]
