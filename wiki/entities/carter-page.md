# Carter Page

Carter Page is a former foreign policy adviser to the 2016 presidential campaign of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] who became one of the principal surveillance targets of the [[fbi|FBI]]'s [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] counterintelligence investigation. In the Badlands Media corpus he functions as the human evidence of FISA abuse — the citizen whose communications were monitored on the strength of an application the corpus regards as knowingly defective.

## The Supreme Court's refusal to revive his lawsuit (June 2026)

The Badlands Brief of June 16, 2026, "The G7 Laments the G2," reports that the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|United States Supreme Court]] declined to revive Page's lawsuit against the FBI. Page had sought damages arising from the surveillance conducted against him during Crossfire Hurricane, arguing that the government relied on flawed information in obtaining its Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act warrants. By refusing the petition, the Court left the lower-court rulings against Page in place, ending — at least in that posture — his effort to obtain a civil remedy for the surveillance.

## Reading in the Badlands corpus

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] groups the Page decision with the [[james-comey|James Comey]] prosecution, the [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] investigation, and the newly released documents on the 2021 school board memorandum as four simultaneous illustrations of a single theme: "Everyone of these cases involves weaponized government." In her framing, Page "was denied a chance to argue again that the government was weaponized against him during Comey's reign," a denial she treats as significant precisely because it returns FISA abuse to public discussion at a moment when [[congress|Congress]] "struggles to pass a FISA extension."
