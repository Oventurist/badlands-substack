# Henry P. Davison

**Henry P. Davison** was a Senior Partner of the J.P. Morgan Company and one of the seven men who attended the secret 1910 gathering at the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island Club]].[1]

Alongside [[nelson-w-aldrich|Nelson W. Aldrich]], A.P. Andrews, [[paul-warburg|Paul Warburg]], [[frank-a-vanderlip|Frank A. Vanderlip]], Charles D. Norton and [[benjamin-strong|Benjamin Strong]], he formed a group said to represent about one quarter of the world's wealth, travelling in secrecy under first names or code names.[1]
