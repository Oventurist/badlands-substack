# Heartland Institute

The Heartland Institute is an American free-market think tank that, in February 2024, published a study concluding that fraudulent mail-in ballots "significantly" affected the 2020 presidential election and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would "almost certainly" have been re-elected absent the pandemic-era expansion of voting by mail.[1]

## The 2024 mail-in ballot study

The study attempted to gauge the probable impact of fraudulent mail-in ballots cast for both then-candidate [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and President Trump on the overall 2020 result.[1] It was built on data from a December Heartland/[[rasmussen-reports|Rasmussen]] survey that found roughly one in five mail-in voters admitted to potentially fraudulent actions in the presidential election.[1]

After additional analysis, the researchers concluded that absent the huge expansion of [[mail-in-voting|mail-in balloting]] during the pandemic — often implemented without legislative approval — Trump would most likely have won: "Had the 2020 election been conducted like every national election has been over the past two centuries, wherein the vast majority of voters cast ballots in-person rather than by mail, Donald Trump would have almost certainly been re-elected."[1]

## Badlands critique

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who discussed the study with Brian and [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] on Badlands Daily's [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]], argued the study does not go far enough and reads like "the political study version of the movie 'Rigged.'"[1] She contended that framing individual voter fraud rather than systemic election fraud as the cause of the 2020 outcome is "a distracting lie that ultimately serves the regime."[1]

Her critique held that the study omits algorithms, non-competitive technology contracts, certification gaps, the role of NGOs, intentional voter roll inflation, and the purging of people who had voted from the rolls immediately after elections — because those issues cannot be explained without criminal intent.[1] By blaming anonymous individual voters, "ephemeral boogiemen who can never be held accountable," the framing ensures no accountability even while suggesting fraud was outcome-determinative.[1] She also faulted the study's recommendations for still favoring too much centralization in state hands rather than local counties and precincts, while allowing that its "normie-shocking" conclusions may aid the awakening if readers move quickly past them into the realities of the 2024 cycle.[1]
