# League of United Latin American Citizens

The League of United Latin American Citizens (LULAC) is a Hispanic civil rights organization in the United States that participates in voting rights litigation.[1]

## 2025 elections executive order lawsuit

LULAC was among the plaintiffs that filed suit against [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]]'s March 2025 executive order overhauling the administration of United States elections, joining the [[league-of-women-voters-education-fund|League of Women Voters Education Fund]], the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] and others in calling the order unconstitutional.[1]

On April 24, 2025, U.S. District Judge [[colleen-kollar-kotelly|Colleen Kollar-Kotelly]] sided with the voting rights groups and Democrats, blocking immediate implementation of a documentary proof-of-citizenship requirement on the federal voter registration form while allowing other provisions — including tightened mail ballot deadlines — to proceed.[1] The court reasoned that the Constitution vests authority over federal election regulation in the states and Congress rather than the presidency.[1]
