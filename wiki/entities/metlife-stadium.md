# MetLife Stadium

MetLife Stadium is the American sports venue that hosted the final of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]], in which [[spain|Spain]] defeated [[argentina|Argentina]] 1–0 after extra time on a Sunday in July 2026. [[ferran-torres|Ferran Torres]] scored the match's only goal there in the 106th minute. The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] also reported that photographs from President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s box at the stadium circulated online, prompting readers to ask the identity of one guest, whom contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identified as Saudi defense minister [[khalid-bin-salman|Khalid bin Salman]].
