# Chrystia Freeland

Chrystia Freeland is a Canadian politician and former journalist who served as Canada's Finance Minister and Deputy Prime Minister before being removed from the centre of Canadian political life in 2025.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) On September 16, 2025, an announcement released from the Prime Minister's Office in Ottawa thanked Freeland for her service while making clear that her political sun was setting; Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] wrote, "I have asked Chrystia to serve as Canada's new Representative for the Reconstruction of Ukraine."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

Analyst [[john-helmer|John Helmer]], writing on Freeland's ouster, observed that it "was so rushed, there was no time for her to explain what the hurry was in her departure, nor for Carney to prepare what Freeland would be doing as his special envoy to the Ukraine without any staff or diplomatic rank."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) In the reading advanced by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the role Freeland had been groomed to play on the world stage — a Rhodes Scholar regime-change apostle devoted to unipolarism and eventual war with Russia and China — had come to an end as the world settled into a new alignment of power relations.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Removal and the China pivot

Canada's anti-Russian posture did not end with Freeland's departure: Canadian taxpayers had already given $22 billion to the proxy war since the Special Military Operation began in February 2022.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) What changed was a new agenda geared toward befriending China under Carney, within which Freeland's anti-China hawkishness had no place.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Her belligerent anti-China economic position had already cost Canadian farmers billions through China's retaliatory tariffs on Canadian canola products, imposed in response to Freeland's 100% tariff on Chinese electric vehicles.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Ehret cautions that China should not therefore trust the "new Canada" of Carney, since Canada is treated as a chess piece within a much larger London-directed Great Game whose intention is to weaken the Russian–Chinese alliance.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Formation and career

Freeland was positioned as a counterpart to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], who "was supposed" to win the 2016 election; in Ehret's account, Freeland and her ilk demonstrated outdated thinking in everything they set out to achieve after the 2014 coup in Ukraine.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Her assigned function was the one every [[rhodes-scholarship|Rhodes Scholar]] is conditioned to perform on completing indoctrination at Oxford: to facilitate the transition of the "pre-collapse" world economy into a new "green post-collapse" operating system.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) That task required telling a new pirate class of billionaires that they would have to accept losing much of their wealth and operate under a strict new global regulatory system designed to contract society.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

After leaving Oxford in 1993, Freeland learned the ropes of "perception management" working for the London *Economist*, the *Washington Post*, the *Financial Times*, the *Globe and Mail* and *Reuters*.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Following a stint as editor-at-large of *Reuters*, she was cast in the role of a Valerie Jarrett figure to the "Barack Obama of Canada" then being prepped for the prime ministership, [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) As an asset of the global propaganda system she had made high-level contacts among Ukrainian, Russian and Western oligarchs, including [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]] and [[mikhail-khodorkovsky|Mikhail Khodorkovsky]]; Larry Summers, [[george-soros|George Soros]] and Al Gore were among the Western players she counted as close friends and was happy to bring into Canada during the reorganization of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party]] between 2011 and 2014 under the banner of the [[canada-2020|Canada 2020]] think tank.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

Freeland was bred from a zealous family of Ukrainian nationalists under the patriarchy of her grandfather [[michael-chomiak|Michael Chomiak]], described as a Nazi propagandist; this network was brought to Canada after the Second World War by Anglo-American intelligence and cultivated as a force with ties to pro-Nazi Ukrainian counterparts ever since.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Her admission into politics was managed by another Rhodes Scholar, [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]], who served as interim controller of the Liberal Party during several of the Harper years and was a major player in Canada 2020, abdicating his seat to Freeland and thereby ensuring her entry into Trudeau's inner circle as his official handler.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Writings on plutocracy

Freeland has made clear that she understands the difference in cultural identity between the "new rich" and the older oligarchic families she serves.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) In her 2011 essay *The Rise of the New Global Elite* she wrote that "to grasp the difference between today's plutocrats and the hereditary elite, who 'grow rich in their sleep,' one need merely glance at the events that now fill high-end social calendars."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) She divided the new plutocrats into the technocratically friendly ones ideologically compatible with a New World Order of depopulation — Bill Gates, Warren Buffett, George Soros and others — and the "bad" plutocrats who do not conform to the British Empire's program of global governance and depopulation under the green agenda.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) In this schema [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is a terrible plutocrat, and [[viktor-yanukovych|Viktor Yanukovych]] was a good one until he refused to sacrifice Ukraine on the altar of the collapsing European Union and instead threw Ukraine's destiny into the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]] in October 2013.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

In the same essay Freeland warned that the real threat facing the super-elite was "not modestly higher taxes, but rather the possibility that inchoate public rage could cohere into a more concrete populist agenda — that, for instance, middle-class Americans could conclude that the world economy isn't working for them" and turn to protectionism, quoting Pimco CEO Mohamed El-Erian that "one of the big surprises of 2010 is that the protectionist dog didn't bark."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) She closed with the observation that "the lesson of history is that, in the long run, super-elites have two ways to survive: by suppressing dissent or by sharing their wealth."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

In *Plutocrats vs. Populists* (November 2013) Freeland let her pro-plutocratic worldview out of the bag, praising a form of plutocratic political power that offers "the tantalizing possibility of policy practiced at the highest professional level with none of the messiness and deal making and venality of traditional politics … a technocratic, data-based, objective search for solutions to our problems."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Because a technocratic managerial class committed to a common ideology must be solidified for such a system to work, she made the case for recruiting young people into the imperial civil service: "Smart, publicly minded technocrats go to work for plutocrats whose values they share … They can be pretty well paid to boot."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Appointment as adviser to Zelenskyy (January 2026)

On January 5, 2026, Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] announced the appointment of Freeland — former Canadian Deputy Prime Minister, of Ukrainian heritage — as an unpaid Advisor on Economic Development, tasked with helping the country attract investment and strengthen internal resilience.[2] Zelenskyy said she was "highly skilled in these matters" with extensive experience in attracting investment and implementing economic transformations.[2] Freeland planned to resign her Canadian parliamentary seat in the following weeks to address conflict-of-interest concerns.[2] The appointment followed a $2.5 billion Canadian aid package announced by Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] on December 27, 2025.[2]

### Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized Freeland as a former "[[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] handler," "an avowed globalist and anti-Russia warmonger," and reviewed her record of statements against [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[2] These included a May 2017 statement that Canada was "very troubled" by Maduro's constituent assembly plan; a January 2019 declaration that "having seized power through fraudulent and anti-democratic elections held on May 20, 2018, the Maduro regime is now fully entrenched as a dictatorship"; and an April 2019 statement calling the Venezuelan crisis "an ongoing threat to regional peace and security" following a Lima Group meeting in Santiago.[2]

Burning Bright also recalled [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s December 2024 Truth Social post mocking Trudeau as "governor" and saying of Freeland that "her behavior was totally toxic … She will not be missed!!!", and Trump's February 2025 remarks to The Spectator calling her "terrible" and "a whack" while claiming credit for her resignation as finance minister.[2] Freeland replied on X: "There's a reason Trump called me a 'whack.' … There's a reason Putin kicked me out of Russia, too. I don't back down."[2] Her December 2024 cabinet resignation ultimately preceded Trudeau's announcement that he would step down as Liberal leader; Trump had first met her during negotiations over the [[usmca|Canada-US-Mexico Agreement]] replacing [[nafta|NAFTA]].[2]

From this record Burning Bright argued that Freeland's alignment against Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Maduro alike made her statements useful as "reverse indicators," and that Trump's refusal to endorse — and active condemnation of — the NGO-funded opposition leader [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] indicated that Trump "didn't just execute a regime change … He stopped it."[2]

## 2025 Liberal leadership defeat

Freeland, a former finance minister and friend of [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]], lost the March 2025 Liberal leadership race to him, taking a small share of the vote against his 85.9% among more than 150,000 party members.[3] The contest determined who would replace [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] as prime minister and confront President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the escalating trade dispute.[3]

## Resignation from cabinet (December 2024)

On Monday, December 16, 2024, Freeland resigned from [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]]'s cabinet, citing differences over how to confront President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s "America First" economic nationalism, threatened tariffs and a possible trade war.[4] She had served the previous four years as both finance minister and deputy prime minister, and her abrupt departure was described as the latest blow to an embattled Trudeau whose popularity had nosedived over the prior year, with polls projecting a wipeout for the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party]] if elections were held then.[4]

Freeland posted her resignation letter on her X account on Monday morning, shortly before she was scheduled to deliver a fall fiscal and economic update to Parliament.[4] In it she said Trudeau had told her he no longer wanted her as finance minister and had offered her another cabinet role: "Upon reflection, I have concluded that the only honest and viable path is for me to resign from the Cabinet. To be effective, a Minister must speak on behalf of the Prime Minister and with his full confidence. In making your decision, you made clear that I no longer credibly enjoy that confidence and possess the authority that comes with it."[4]

Badlands framed the episode as part of the "Trump-Canada B plot," arguing that Trump's tariff threats were breaking through generations of cognitive dissonance by exposing that the United States does not require trade with Canada or Mexico, and thereby revealing those nations' lack of leverage.[4] With Freeland ousted by a capitulating Trudeau, the Brief speculated that the "Proxy Prince of Canada" was entering a Zelensky-style arc, turning on his globalist masters to make good with his southern neighbor; it characterized Freeland as one of the stalwarts of globalist COVID hysteria and predicted Brussels would not like the move.[4]

## Scorched-earth resignation letter

Freeland's Monday, December 16, 2024 resignation letter was characterized as "scorched earth," leaving Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] in an even more precarious position as his administration teetered on the verge of collapse.[7] She had publicly opposed Trudeau's plan for short-term spending on voter-pleasing measures such as tax breaks that would worsen the country's budget deficit.[7] "Our country today faces a grave challenge," Freeland, then 56, wrote, referring to President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s threat of tariffs; "that means keeping our fiscal powder dry today, so we have the reserves we may need for a coming tariff war. That means eschewing costly political gimmicks, which we can ill afford and which make Canadians doubt that we recognize the gravity of the moment."[7] Badlands writer Burning Bright described her as a "globalist stalwart" whose very public resignation fed rumours that Trudeau himself was set to resign as prime minister.[7]
