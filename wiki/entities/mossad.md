# Mossad

Mossad is the Israeli national intelligence service, referenced in [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay as one of the two services subordinate to British [[mi6|MI6]].[1]

## Position in the alleged hierarchy

Carlson writes that above the [[cia|CIA]] and Mossad is MI6, and above Tel Aviv and Washington is the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[1] The point of the formulation, in his argument, is that Israel is not the apex of global power but a layer of middle management — and, more broadly, a scapegoat deployed by the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] so that its actual controllers can operate unobserved.[1]
