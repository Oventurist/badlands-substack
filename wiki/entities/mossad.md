# Mossad

Mossad, Israel's intelligence service, appears in Erik Carlson's essay as one of the agencies subordinate to British [[mi6|MI6]] in his proposed hierarchy of power.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Role in the article

Carlson states that above the [[cia|CIA]] and Mossad is MI6, and above Tel Aviv and Washington, DC, is the [[city-of-london|City of London]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The placement supports his broader contention that Israel — like the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]] before it — serves as a visible front while the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]] that actually controls Israel operates in the shadows.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Marc Rich's protection

"America for Sale — Part 3" names Mossad, alongside the [[cia|CIA]], as one of the two intelligence services whose deep connections made the commodities trader [[marc-rich|Marc Rich]] an "untouchable" despite his fugitive status.[2] Rich, the article says, traded oil and other commodities to rogue nations controlled by the CIA, with [[sanctions|sanctions]] providing the secrecy that made the trade possible, and built a large network of front companies, shipping operations and financial institutions to support it.[2] The article attributes the repeated failure of U.S. marshals to arrest Rich in Switzerland to tip-offs from within the [[state-department|State Department]], framing his intelligence ties as the reason he was never brought in.[2]

## Mandated US intelligence sharing (2026)

In June 2026, Senator [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]] introduced a provision to the 2027 Intelligence Authorization Act that would require the President to expand intelligence sharing with Mossad and prohibit the President from suspending or limiting intelligence sharing with [[israel|Israel]].[3] Badlands cited the measure as evidence for its contention that Israeli influence over American institutions now extends to highly classified intelligence, while noting that raising the point is routinely condemned as antisemitic.[3]

The same edition of the Brief referred to a cluster of American commentators hostile to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s negotiated framework with [[iran|Iran]] as "Mossad Media," attributing to them a coordinated attack line portraying Trump as a chickenhawk once he cancelled planned strikes.[3] It also revisited the 1983 Beirut barracks bombing, citing former Mossad officer Victor Ostrovsky for the claim that Israel had advance knowledge of the attack on the US Marine barracks and withheld it so that America would be drawn into the Middle East.[3]

## "Mossad Media" and the 2026 Trump assassination-plot report

In July 2026, US officials were reportedly informed by [[israel|Israeli]] intelligence of a new [[iran|Iranian]] plot to assassinate President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], intelligence that had not previously been identified by US agencies and was still being evaluated.[4] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Trump himself had seeded the narrative during his [[nato|NATO]] press conference, apparently to bait "Mossad Media" and the NeoCon wing of the [[uniparty|Uniparty]] into running with it.[4]

He contended that Israel, rather than Iran, would be the more logical suspect in any attempt on Trump's life, since a successful assassination would let Israel blame Iran, obtain sweeping surveillance powers over the American people, and gain a free pass to wage war on Iran and suspected collaborators.[4] As with [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], he wrote, killing Trump while his public relationship with Israel remained positive would "freeze" that posture permanently, preventing any later narrative turn against Israel's leadership.[4] He further suggested the IDF and Mossad were displeased with Trump's warm public relationships with Muslim leaders in the region, including [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Erdoğan]] and Syria's [[ahmed-al-sharaa|al-Sharaa]].[4] The same contributor characterized "Mossad Media assets" as among those condemning Trump's praise of [[turkey|Turkey]] at the NATO summit.[4]

## Alleged target of a US information operation (2026)

Discussing the April 2026 satellite imagery blackout over [[iran|Iran]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] speculated that the [[4th-psychological-operations-group|4th Psyop Group]] could be running an information operation against Mossad and the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] themselves, such that Israeli services believe the U.S. military is fighting an earnest war against Iran.[5] Such an operation, he suggested, would require manipulated intelligence, manipulated satellite imagery and Iranian officials affirming U.S. claims.[5] In this reading, Mossad and the IDF continue supplying "critical" intelligence to Washington while believing they are the ones controlling and manipulating [[donald-trump|Trump]].[5]

## Alleged infiltration of Iran (2026)

In July 2026, multiple media reports alleged that Iranian authorities had placed former President [[mahmoud-ahmadinejad|Mahmoud Ahmadinejad]] under house arrest and were questioning him over alleged contacts with individuals accused of links to Mossad, as part of an investigation into suspected Israeli intelligence infiltration following Israel's confrontation with [[iran|Iran]].[6] Badlands commentary treated the reports as validating a longer-running theory that Mossad, under then-Director [[david-barnea|David Barnea]], had deeply infiltrated the Iranian government and had been preparing a coup, and that Mossad and the [[cia|CIA]] had penetrated the highest levels of the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|IRGC]], prompting the [[iranian-devolution|Iranian Devolution]] Continuity of Government protocol.[6]

## June 2026 DIA "critical" espionage assessment

In June 2026, NBC News and [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] reported that the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] had elevated its assessment of the threat posed by Israeli espionage to a "critical" level, in a seven-page document with an accompanying chart evaluating Israeli intelligence-gathering capabilities.[7] The assessment allegedly flagged Israeli efforts to collect on U.S. administration deliberations over [[iran|Iran]] and [[lebanon|Lebanon]], naming [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]], [[elbridge-colby|Elbridge Colby]] and [[michael-dimino|Michael DiMino]] among the officials targeted.[7] The Pentagon declined comment, a [[white-house|White House]] official called "the entire story" false, and the Israeli Embassy in Washington said Israeli intelligence activities are directed at adversaries rather than allies.[7]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that DiMino's inclusion made the account plausible, since DiMino holds that [[israel|Israel]] is a security liability the United States should abandon: "So it makes perfect sense that Mossad would want to spy on this guy."[7]

## Poisons capability and the Graham death (July 2026)

Following the July 11, 2026 death of Senator [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that Zionist commentators immediately blamed Putin and Russia for an alleged assassination carried out in concert with Iran, and countered that it is Mossad that is notorious for using undetectable poisons.[8] Citing whistleblowers, he wrote that Mossad maintains scientific laboratories specifically dedicated to developing such poisons, and that Mossad assassins are ordered to attend or observe autopsies so they understand how a dead body is evaluated for signs of foul play.[8]


## PROMIS software and Credit Suisse

Citing research by author Gordon Thomas, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote in April 2026 that Mossad dispatched [[robert-maxwell|Robert Maxwell]] to sell the [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to entities across the world, including [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].[9] The software, equipped with a secret backdoor, is said to have allowed Mossad to monitor the activities of all governments while tracking money laundering by organized-crime syndicates through the banking system and arms deals through weapons manufacturers.[9] Through that surveillance, the account holds, Mossad learned that the [[cia|CIA]] had been using Credit Suisse to launder revenue from off-the-books endeavors.[9]

## Iran campaign disclosures (2026)

Mossad chief David Barnea revealed in April 2026 that the agency had operated inside Tehran to supply precise targeting intelligence during the Israeli campaign against Iran, and said its responsibility would continue until regime change is achieved even after the fighting ends.[10] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry separately cited an interview with Efraim Halevy, the ninth Mossad director, arguing that Halevy's contrasting descriptions of Al Qaeda — which he notes has never attacked Israel — and Hezbollah show the two are regarded very differently by Israeli intelligence.[10]

## Abduction of Mordechai Vanunu (1986)

After Mordechai Vanunu defected to London in 1985 with evidence of Israel's secret nuclear weapons program, the Mossad lured him to Italy, where he was kidnapped and taken back to Israel to face a closed-door trial.[11] He was convicted and sentenced to 18 years in prison, much of it in solitary confinement.[11]

## Turkish arrest of the alleged financial network chief (2024)

On 3 September 2024, Turkish security officials announced the arrest of [[liridon-rexhepi|Liridon Rexhepi]], a Kosovan national identified by the [[turkish-national-intelligence-organization|Turkish National Intelligence Organization (MIT)]] as the head of Mossad's financial network in Türkiye.[12] Turkish media reported that Rexhepi was transferring money to Mossad field agents in the country who filmed Mossad targets with drones, led psychological operations against Palestinian politicians, and gathered intelligence about the Syrian field.[12] MIT said it identified him after detecting irregularities in his financial accounts showing "countless" transfers to field agents via Western Union, and he reportedly admitted that funds from Kosovo were moved to sources in Syria using cryptocurrencies.[12] Since the start of 2024 Turkish authorities had detained and charged dozens of suspected Mossad agents, most of them Turkish nationals, with seven detained in January, six charged in March, and two arrested in April.[12]

## Blamed in Charlie Kirk assassination speculation (2025)

In the hours after the [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination of Charlie Kirk]] at [[utah-valley-university|Utah Valley University]], online commentators accused Mossad of responsibility despite no shooter having been identified.[13] Podcast host [[ryan-matta|Ryan Matta]], who has more than 200,000 followers, asked "At this point does anyone not thi[nk] Charlie Kirk was assassinated by Mossad?", while other accounts asserted more explicitly antisemitic claims.[13] The Times of Israel reported that the speculation was fueled by past allegations of antisemitism against Kirk, by Kirk's own recent comments tying Mossad to the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] case, and by the rapid response of Israeli politicians to his death.[13] A widely shared August 13 post by [[infowars|Infowars]] broadcaster [[harrison-smith|Harrison Smith]] claiming Kirk feared Israel would kill him if he turned against them added to the cycle.[13]


## December 2023 hostage negotiations

Mossad director [[david-barnea|David Barnea]] met in Poland on December 18, 2023, with [[cia|CIA]] Director [[william-burns|William Burns]] and Qatari Prime Minister [[mohammed-bin-abdulrahman-al-thani|Mohammad bin Abdulrahman al Thani]] in a renewed effort to free dozens of hostages held in Gaza.[14] The meeting followed bilateral talks between Barnea and al Thani the preceding Friday — the first attempt by either side to restart negotiations after the week-long Israel–[[hamas|Hamas]] ceasefire collapsed on December 1 — and came after two earlier rounds in Doha involving Egyptian intelligence officials.[14] Talks had stalled over the remaining female hostages, whose release Israel demanded before considering other categories; Hamas claimed the women it still held were IDF soldiers, which Israel and the United States denied.[14]
