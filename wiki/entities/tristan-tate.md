# Tristan Tate

Tristan Tate is a social media influencer and dual US–British citizen, the brother of [[andrew-tate|Andrew Tate]], with whom he shares a large online following of mostly young men attracted to the pair's luxurious lifestyle, hyper-masculine image and misogynist rhetoric.[1] The brothers have been banned from numerous social platforms for hate speech.[1]

## Legal exposure

Tristan Tate faces trial in Romania alongside his brother over allegations that they operated a criminal ring that lured women to the country for sexual exploitation.[1] After Romanian authorities lifted a travel ban, the brothers arrived in Fort Lauderdale, Florida on February 27, 2025.[1] On March 4, 2025, Florida Attorney General [[james-uthmeier|James Uthmeier]] announced a preliminary inquiry and then an active criminal investigation into the brothers, directing the Office of Statewide Prosecution to execute search warrants and issue subpoenas.[1]
