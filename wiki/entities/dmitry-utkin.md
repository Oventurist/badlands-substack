# Dmitry Utkin

Dmitry Utkin was the second-in-command of the Russian mercenary outfit [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]], serving as deputy to founder [[yevgeny-prigozhin|Yevgeny Prigozhin]].[1]

## Death in the August 2023 plane crash

Utkin was reported to be aboard the private Embraer jet that was downed northwest of Moscow on August 23, 2023, in a crash that appeared to have killed the entire top command of the Wagner Group.[1] Russian media moved quickly to report that the bodies of Prigozhin and Utkin had been identified, and the Russian state news agency [[tass|TASS]] made the passenger manifest official, reporting that the Federal Air Transport Agency had confirmed both men were on board the crashed Embraer.[1]

Wagner itself confirmed Prigozhin's death, and the Wagner-affiliated Telegram channel *Grey Zone* characterized the downing as a "murder," warning that "the murder/assassination of Prigozhin will have catastrophic consequences" and that those who gave the order did not understand the mood or morale of the army.[1] Reports at the time indicated an anti-air missile had struck the plane.[1]

## Badlands framing

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] placed the crash at the end of a sequence of Russia–Ukraine narrative events and cautioned that "where it concerns Prigozhin, nothing is usually as it seems," advising readers to focus on the story being told and who was telling it rather than accepting the reported deaths at face value.[1]
