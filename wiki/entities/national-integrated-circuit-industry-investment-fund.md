# National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund

The National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund, known as the "Big Fund," is a Chinese state-backed investment vehicle created to accelerate the development of the country's semiconductor industry.[1]

## Third fund, 2024

In March 2024, Bloomberg reported that the fund was in the process of raising more than $27 billion for its largest chip fund to date, amassing capital from local governments and state enterprises for a third vehicle expected to exceed the 200 billion yuan of its second fund.[1] The third fund is directly overseen by China's tech ministry, a structure Bloomberg described as signaling a resurgent effort at central stewardship of the world's largest semiconductor market after years of mixed success.[1]

The expansion came as the United States prepared to sharply escalate technology curbs designed to curtail Chinese chip and artificial intelligence progress.[1] [[huawei|Huawei Technologies Co.]] and its partner Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation still had to rely on US-origin technology to build an advanced processor in 2023.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] situated the fund within his "Switch" and "Good Reset" theories, arguing that the continuing US–China trade war is a feature rather than a bug: ratcheting tensions between sovereign nations renew public mandates for on-shoring domestic production and nationalism in the United States, Russia and China alike.[1] He argued that semiconductors, not oil, gold, rare earths or wheat, are now the most-traded commodity in the world, and that the [[chips-and-science-act|CHIPS Act]] started a cascade producing a multipolar world "just like the BRICS nations drew it up."[1]
