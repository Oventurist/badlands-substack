# Saleh al-Arouri

Saleh al-Arouri was the deputy chief of [[hamas|Hamas]] and the most senior figure of the Palestinian movement to be killed outside the Palestinian Territories following the group's October 7 assault on Israeli territory.[1]

## Killing in Beirut

Al-Arouri was killed on the night of Tuesday, January 2, 2024, in an Israeli drone strike on Dahiyeh, the southern suburbs of Beirut and a stronghold of the allied Lebanese militant group [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]].[1] Lebanon's national news agency reported that the drone struck a Hamas office, and two security sources said the strike had targeted a meeting between Hamas officials and Lebanon's Sunni Islamist Jama'a Islamiya faction, leaving four Palestinians and three Lebanese dead in total.[1]

The [[israel-defense-forces|Israeli military]] declined to respond to questions from Reuters about the strike, saying it does not respond to reports in the foreign media.[1] [[mark-regev|Mark Regev]], an adviser to Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]], told MSNBC that Israel had not taken responsibility for the attack but insisted that "whoever did it, it must be clear: That this was not an attack on the Lebanese state," describing the operation as "a surgical strike against the Hamas leadership."[1]

## Significance

The strike marked the first targeted assassination of a Hamas official outside the Palestinian Territories since October 7, and was widely read as a signal that the Hamas–Israel conflict could expand to engulf more of the region.[1] Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] framed the killing as an "undeniable mark of escalation" on Israel's part that could pull Iran into kinetic action in the Middle East beyond the proxies it arms and funds, and as part of a broader media transition away from the exhausted Ukraine–Russia storyline toward a new series of crises.[1]
