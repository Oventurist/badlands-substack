# Kevin Gest

Kevin Gest is a contemporary Freemason and the author of *The Secrets of Solomon's Temple*, cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] for his account of the origins of Solomon's name as a metaphor for the cult of the Sun and Moon, or light and darkness.[1]

Gest writes: "Because of its special features, the temple became known as the 'Temple of Sol and Amon'. Sol meaning Sun, Amon meaning Moon. After his death Jedidiah was referred to as the man who built the Sol and Amon Temple, which became corrupted over time to Sol-amon and then to Solomon."[1] Ehret uses this passage to support the argument that occultists regard [[solomons-temple|Solomon's Temple]] as a pagan solar-lunar sanctuary rather than the monotheistic house of worship described in scripture.[1]
