# Kamala Harris

## 2028 Democratic power rankings

In July 2026, [[axios|Axios]]'s Democratic "power rankings" placed former Vice President Kamala Harris atop the party's early 2028 presidential field alongside California Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] and Representative [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]].[1] The rankings were based on interviews with Democratic strategists, donors, elected officials and party insiders, weighing political influence, fundraising strength and national profile, and reflected early positioning rather than declared candidacies in a field Axios described as fluid.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded that it is "really quite something that there is no new talent on the Democrat side," argued that none of the named figures are remotely threatening — "they're punchlines" — and said she would be more concerned if the party were elevating someone new, normal and "Kennedy-esque" who tacked back toward the middle.[1]
