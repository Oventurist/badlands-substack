# Tim Gill

**Tim Gill** is an American philanthropist and chair of the [[gill-foundation|Gill Foundation]], an LGBT-focused grantmaking organization.[1]

In June 2023, [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] publicly thanked Gill and his husband [[scott-miller|Scott Miller]] for making the [[white-house|White House]] Pride Month celebration possible, though neither attended the event.[1] Badlands commentary highlighted the couple's proximity to the administration — Miller serves as ambassador to Switzerland — while criticizing the event's flag arrangement as a breach of the [[us-flag-code|United States Flag Code]].[1]
