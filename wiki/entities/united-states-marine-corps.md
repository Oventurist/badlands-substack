# United States Marine Corps

The United States Marine Corps is the naval expeditionary service branch of the U.S. armed forces.[1] Its domestic employment is politically sensitive, and a June 2025 episode in California brought that sensitivity to the fore.[1]

## Prepared-to-deploy status in Los Angeles, June 2025

About 500 Marines from the Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center at Twentynine Palms were placed in a "prepared to deploy status" in June 2025, ready to assist authorities in Los Angeles as protests continued around immigration enforcement raids.[1] According to [[united-states-northern-command|U.S. Northern Command]], the Marines were held in reserve to support the Department of Defense's mission of protecting federal property and personnel, alongside roughly 2,000 federalized [[california-army-national-guard|California Army National Guard]] soldiers.[1]

Governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] characterized President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s threat to deploy Marines into Los Angeles as an escalation of the situation.[1]

## 2023 F-35B mishap in South Carolina

In September 2023 the Marine Corps asked for the public's help locating an [[f-35|F-35B Lightning II]] worth upwards of $80 million that went missing over South Carolina after its pilot ejected during a "mishap."[2] A Corps spokesperson said search-and-recovery efforts were ongoing and the mishap was under investigation, and the service began a recovery process after a debris field was located in Williamsburg County near [[joint-base-charleston|Joint Base Charleston]].[2] The episode is covered at [[2023-f-35b-disappearance-in-south-carolina|2023 F-35B disappearance in South Carolina]].[2]
