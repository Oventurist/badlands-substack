# Baring Brothers Bank

Baring Brothers Bank was the British merchant bank that, according to "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8," controlled the [[british-east-india-company|East India Company]] during the period in which the Far East opium trade underwrote the founding of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society.[1]

Toward the closing decades of the era described, the British [[rothschild-family|House of Rothschild]] supplanted the Baring Brothers as the controlling financial interest in the China opium trade.[1] Through the sponsorship of the Barings and the Rothschilds, a number of leading New England families — several of which had sided with Great Britain during the American Revolution — were brought into the opium trade as junior partners.[1] These merchant houses, among them the Cabot, Coolidge, Forbes, Higginson, Sturgis, Lodge, Lowell, Perkins and Russell families, ran fleets of ships, became extremely wealthy, and went on to found the [[united-fruit-company|United Fruit Company]] and the Bank of Boston.[1] Over several generations they intermarried heavily and became, in effect, one extended power grouping, with the Russell and Perkins lines furnishing the founding families of Skull and Bones.[1]
