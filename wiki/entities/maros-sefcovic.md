# Maros Sefcovic

Maros Sefcovic is a European Union Commissioner responsible for trade matters.[1] On 7 May 2025 he held a press conference at the EU Council building at which the [[european-union|European Union]] announced its intention to challenge the United States' reciprocal tariff policy at the [[world-trade-organization|World Trade Organization]].[1]

Alongside the WTO challenge, the [[european-commission|European Commission]] opened a public consultation on potential countermeasures targeting U.S. imports valued at $107.4 billion — reported as a roughly 95 billion euro retaliation package — covering a wide range of agricultural and industrial goods including bourbon and tequila.[1] The Commission argued that the U.S. tariffs contravened fundamental WTO rules, stating that its objective was "to reaffirm that internationally agreed rules matter and these cannot be unilaterally disregarded by any WTO member, including the US."[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had previously threatened a 200 percent tariff on EU alcohol imports, sharpening the dispute.[1]
