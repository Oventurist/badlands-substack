# Cathy Harris

Cathy Harris is a member of the [[merit-systems-protection-board|Merit Systems Protection Board]] whose removal by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] became the subject of emergency litigation before the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] in April 2025.[1]

## Litigation over her removal

A federal district court voided Trump's firing of Harris and restored her to her role at the independent board, in a decision paired with a similar ruling reinstating [[gwynne-wilcox|Gwynne Wilcox]] to the [[national-labor-relations-board|National Labor Relations Board]].[1] On April 9, 2025 the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] asked the Supreme Court to block both district court decisions, and Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] issued an administrative stay that temporarily halted the lower court orders, allowing the removals to take effect again while the Court considered the request.[1] Roberts gave lawyers for Harris and Wilcox until 5 p.m. on April 15 to file a response.[1] Solicitor General [[d-john-sauer|D. John Sauer]] argued in the emergency application that the reinstatement orders harmed the president and the separation of powers.[1]
