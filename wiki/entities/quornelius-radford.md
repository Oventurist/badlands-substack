# Quornelius Radford

Quornelius Radford is a US Army active-duty automated logistics sergeant, aged 28 and originally from Jacksonville, Florida, who was identified as the suspect in a shooting at Fort Stewart, Georgia, on Wednesday, August 6, 2025.[1]

## Fort Stewart shooting

At least five soldiers were wounded when Radford, who had recently been arrested, allegedly opened fire at the installation to which he was assigned.[1] Army officials said he used a personal handgun and was tackled by other soldiers before being taken into custody.[1] Officials also noted that he had not deployed to combat.[1] The incident placed the Georgia base under lockdown and was reported by Fox News.[1]
