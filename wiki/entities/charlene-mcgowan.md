# Charlene McGowan

**Charlene McGowan** is the general counsel to Georgia Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]].[1]

## Opposition to State Election Board oversight legislation

In January 2024, McGowan wrote to the sponsor of a Georgia bill that would remove Raffensperger from his seat on the [[georgia-state-election-board|State Election Board]] and allow the board to investigate him. She argued that there was no precedent for removing the secretary of state from the board or for establishing oversight over the office, warning that "giving a board of unelected bureaucrats unchecked power over the state's executive branch is a dangerous policy proposal."[1]

The bill passed the [[georgia-senate|Georgia Senate]] Ethics Committee on Tuesday, January 23, 2024, over her objections; its sponsor, Republican State Sen. [[max-burns|Max Burns]], said supporters were "trying to provide reasonable oversight of a process that affects every citizen of Georgia."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that McGowan's phrasing was self-defeating: by dismissing the State Election Board — the body meant to bestow trust and confidence in Georgia's elections — as "unelected bureaucrats," she had, speaking on Raffensperger's behalf, undermined public trust in elections and "said the quiet part out loud."[1]
