# Adnan al-Jumaili

Adnan al-Jumaili is a former Iraqi Deputy Oil Minister whose case formed the basis of the wide-ranging corruption investigation that triggered the lockdown of Baghdad's [[green-zone|Green Zone]] in late June 2026.[1]

Iraq's state-run Iraqi News Agency, cited by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], reported that Iraqi authorities carried out a series of arrests as part of the corruption probe linked to al-Jumaili, with several political figures detained in connection with the investigation.[1] A security report obtained by the Associated Press and summarized in the Brief said seven people were arrested, including five members of parliament whose immunity had been revoked, with some of those detained linked to the political bloc of former Prime Minister [[mohammed-shia-al-sudani|Mohammed Shia al-Sudani]].[1]

Badlands analysis situated the al-Jumaili case within a broader pattern of Iranian-linked oil and money laundering through [[iraq|Iraq]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], as described in the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|U.S. Treasury]] designation of [[salim-ahmed-said|Salim Ahmed Said]].[1]
