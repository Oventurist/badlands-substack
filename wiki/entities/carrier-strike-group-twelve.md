# Carrier Strike Group Twelve

**Carrier Strike Group Twelve (CSG-12)** is a United States Navy strike group formation built around the aircraft carrier [[uss-gerald-r-ford]]. As reported in May 2026 it comprised the carrier plus at least three escorting destroyers.

In the Badlands reading of the 2026 Iran campaign, CSG-12's movements supply the timeline that undercuts the official narrative. The group escorted the Ford out of the eastern Mediterranean and through the Strait of Gibraltar into the Atlantic, a transit of roughly four to five days at a cruising speed of 20–25 knots. That arithmetic places the group's departure from the area of operations on the Friday or Saturday immediately before [[project-freedom]] was announced on a Sunday and implemented on the Monday — evidence, in the analysis of GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, that the kinetic phase was already over and that the announced initiative was narrative rather than operational.
