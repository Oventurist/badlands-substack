# Carrier Strike Group Twelve

Carrier Strike Group Twelve (CSG 12) is the U.S. Navy carrier strike group built around the aircraft carrier [[uss-gerald-r-ford|USS Gerald R. Ford]].[1]

## 2026 escort composition and the destroyer question

Badlands analyst [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that CSG Twelve was operating with only three destroyer escorts, fewer than the group normally maintains during deployments.[1] When President [[donald-trump|Trump]] posted about three destroyers amid the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] crisis, the coincidence in numbers drew attention, but reporting indicated the destroyers in question were not from CSG Twelve, which was then in the Atlantic and on its way home.[1]

The same analysis argued that claims [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] had "shut down" [[project-freedom|Project Freedom]] amounted to a narrative operation rather than an operational reality, a conclusion drawn in part from examination of the Ford's deployment posture.[1]
