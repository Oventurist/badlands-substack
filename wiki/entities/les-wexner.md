# Les Wexner

Leslie "Les" Wexner is an American billionaire retailer, the former head of Victoria's Secret parent L Brands, and a longtime associate of the late sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## US Virgin Islands subpoena

In February 2023 a New York judge granted the [[us-virgin-islands|US Virgin Islands]] permission to serve a subpoena on Wexner by mail, after a security guard allegedly prevented process servers from reaching the billionaire at his home.[1] The order, issued on Tuesday, February 21, 2023, followed the territory's complaint that it had been frustrated in seeking documents from Wexner as part of its civil suit against [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]].[1]

The US Virgin Islands said it had attempted personal service at Wexner's Ohio home on February 8 and February 11, and also at his charitable foundation — of which Epstein was once a trustee — but was blocked on all occasions.[1] The matter was reported by the Financial Times.[1]

## Context in the Epstein litigation

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] placed the subpoena within a wider revival of Epstein-related legal activity after a period of apparent quiet. Noting that "for a short time, it seemed like the Epstein scandal had gone away" and that black-pilled observers had grown discouraged, the commentator listed the unsealing of names and documents, newly surfaced emails, lawsuits against JPMorgan Chase and Deutsche Bank, an appeal filed by [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], and the Wexner subpoena as evidence of renewed momentum.[1]

## Connection to the Jeffries case

In October 2024, following the arrest of former Abercrombie & Fitch chief executive [[mike-jeffries|Mike Jeffries]] on federal sex trafficking and interstate prostitution charges, Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] highlighted Wexner as a connecting node in the case.[2] Wexner — identified as the co-founder of Bath & Body Works and owner of Victoria's Secret — was described as "a very deep rabbit hole" who is directly connected to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[2] The commentary presented the Jeffries indictment as another strand in a widening network, arguing that "the nodes are becoming a web, and the web will trap all the cockroaches."[2]


## Purchase of Blue Heron Farm (2025)

In July 2025 Wexner bought Blue Heron Farm, the Martha's Vineyard estate that former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and his family rented for three summers beginning in 2009, for $37 million.[3] The sale was brokered by Brian Dougherty of Corcoran and Maggie Gold Seelig of MSG Group Real Estate, and reported by the Cape Cod Times.[3] Coverage identified Wexner as the founder of L Brands, the retail empire that included Victoria's Secret and Bath & Body Works.[3] Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] used the sale to revisit the property's political history, noting that Obama vacationed on Martha's Vineyard every summer as president and that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] visited the island for two days in 2016 while Obama was there, immediately before a flurry of activity tied to the Russia collusion narrative.[3]

## Purchase of Blue Heron Farm (2025)

In August 2025 a trust tied to Wexner bought [[blue-heron-farm|Blue Heron Farm]], the 28.5-acre Chilmark estate on Martha's Vineyard once rented by the Obamas as a summer retreat, for $37 million — $2 million below its May asking price.[4] Wexner was identified by the Real Deal as the longtime head of L Brands and founder of Victoria's Secret.[4] The property had been off the market for more than a decade after an extensive transformation by its previous owners, British architect Norman Foster and his wife, Elena Ochoa Foster.[4] Badlands contributor CannCon highlighted the timing given Wexner's association with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], remarking, "What are the odds after all this Epstein talk?"[4]
