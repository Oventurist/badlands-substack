# Torsten Slok

**Torsten Sløk** is the chief economist at [[apollo-global-management|Apollo Global Management]] and a prominent skeptic of the tariff policies of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## "Has Trump Outsmarted Everyone On Tariffs?"

On Saturday, June 28, 2025, Sløk published a blog post titled "Has Trump Outsmarted Everyone On Tariffs?" in which he sketched a scenario where Trump keeps tariffs below his highest threatened rates just long enough to ease uncertainty and avoid the economic damage that maximal tariffs would cause.[1] "Maybe the strategy is to maintain 30% tariffs on China and 10% tariffs on all other countries and then give all countries 12 months to lower nontariff barriers and open up their economies to trade," he wrote.[1]

The post appeared shortly before the expiry in early July of the 90-day pause on Trump's "reciprocal tariffs," the announcement of which had triggered a large stock selloff in April.[1] Sløk theorised that extending the deadline by another year would give other countries and U.S. businesses more time to adjust to a world of permanently higher tariffs while easing immediate market uncertainty.[1] He concluded that such an outcome "would seem like a victory for the world and yet would produce $400 billion of annual revenue for U.S. taxpayers," adding that "maybe the administration has outsmarted all of us."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the post as evidence that June 2025 had been "the most beautifully chaotic, and yet controlled" month yet from Trump, whom he calls the Master of Narrative Warfare.[1]
