# R. Craig Lawrence

**R. Craig Lawrence** was a senior federal government litigator in Washington, D.C., described in Badlands Media's "[[irregular-warfare|Irregular Warfare]]" as the supervisor of attorney [[lisa-barsoomian|Lisa Barsoomian]].[1]

According to the article, Barsoomian worked a large number of cases with Lawrence, and the pair's client list included [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]], [[james-comey|James Comey]], [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], and [[bill-clinton|Bill]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]], along with the [[fbi|FBI]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]].[1] Much of that work is characterized as defending the government against [[freedom-of-information-act|Freedom of Information Act]] lawsuits.[1] Joe Lange presents this pattern of representation as evidence of an institutional apparatus dedicated to keeping official records out of public view rather than as routine government litigation practice.[1]
