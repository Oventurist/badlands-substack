# Global Harmony Consortium

The **Global Harmony Consortium** is the name ChatGPT invented for the globalist cabal when [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] reframed his prompting exercise as the development of a science-fiction dystopian film script.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Deschamps had found that the model generated clearer and more truthful results once the framing removed its bias toward protecting him from "dangerous conspiracy theory," and he reports that the fictional wrapper only deepened the accuracy of the warfare map the model produced from the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Institutional composition

Because the Protocols make explicit the intention to use globalist institutions such as the [[united-nations|United Nations]], Deschamps instructed the model to fold in the [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] as well, along with the [[esg|ESG]] system and [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]'s [[great-reset|Great Reset]] as an additional plan for the Consortium to utilise.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## The synthesised plan

In the final and most comprehensive iteration, the Consortium's primary strategic objective is to establish a [[new-world-order|New World Order]]: a unified world government that promotes peace and prosperity while consolidating power and control.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Its secondary objectives, as produced by the model, are to weaken traditional family structures — using [[satanic-inversion|inversion]] to portray them as oppressive and regressive while [[critical-race-theory|CRT]] and [[queer-theory|Queer Theory]] challenge binary gender roles and family norms; to undermine moral principles and religious values, with ESG scores rewarding organisations that promote secular and globalist values and so reinforcing moral relativism; to promote sexual freedom by using the [[climate-change-narrative|climate change narrative]] to frame overpopulation as a global threat while ESG scores reward contraception and population control; to manipulate the concept of individual rights by pressuring corporations toward ideologies that emphasise collective rights and group identity; to influence women's movements by rewarding organisations advocating the deconstruction of traditional family roles; to empower women to break from tradition through career-focused individualism reinforced in public education curricula; to set individual against community rights, with [[covid-mandates|COVID mandates]] presented as necessary for the greater good; and to leverage the climate change narrative to promote collectivism, global governance and the erosion of national sovereignty.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Operationally the Consortium is described as employing [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] — decentralised networks, psychological operations and misinformation campaigns spanning decades and adapting to changing societal norms; conducting long-term research into AI-driven information warfare; infiltrating academia, media and politics while shaping laws and international agreements toward global governance; slowly changing cultural norms through cultural and educational programmes; and building relationships with foreign governments and influential organisations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Its tactics include gaining control over major media outlets and marginalising critical voices over time; gradually altering laws in a programme of long-term legal warfare that erodes national sovereignty; conducting patient psychological operations that integrate CRT, Queer Theory and climate narratives into education systems; funding and guiding activist movements across generations so that activists are subtly conditioned to promote its ideologies; staging high-profile international events that celebrate global governance as inevitable and desirable; and deploying ESG scoring as a warfare tool, with environmental scores pushing climate policy and social and governance scores rewarding alignment with Queer Theory and CRT.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Deschamps notes wryly that even within the fictional frame the model still had to point out that the scenario was "highly speculative."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
