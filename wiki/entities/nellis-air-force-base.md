# Nellis Air Force Base

Nellis Air Force Base is a United States Air Force installation in Nevada.[1] In late May 2023 it became the focus of a national controversy when the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] ordered the base to cancel a "family friendly" drag show scheduled to be held on base to mark the start of Pride month.[1]

## 2023 drag show cancellation

The show, which would have been the third held annually at the installation, was set for Thursday, June 1, 2023.[1] Secretary of Defense [[lloyd-austin|Lloyd Austin]] and Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff Gen. [[mark-milley|Mark Milley]] were reported to have ordered that the event either be cancelled or relocated off base.[1]

The intervention followed public pressure from Rep. [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] of Florida, who had been told by Austin and Milley at a congressional hearing two months earlier that they did not approve of drag queen story hours on military bases, and who called both men out when a "cry for help" about the Nellis event was posted.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that "story hour" was a euphemism for adult performances, and traced the term's entry into public discourse through library events for children.[1] She noted that although Austin and Milley disapproved of the event in 2023, they had raised no objection during the prior two years it was held, describing both officials as "Social Justice champions" who had "actively worked to undermine the mission readiness and overall integrity of the US Armed Forces."[1] She characterized the cancellation as "a win for the kids."[1]
