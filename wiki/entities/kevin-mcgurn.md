# Kevin McGurn

Kevin McGurn is a veteran media executive named interim leader of [[trump-media-and-technology-group|Trump Media & Technology Group]] following the departure of chief executive and chairman [[devin-nunes|Devin Nunes]] in April 2026.[1] The company confirmed the leadership transition on a Tuesday, as Nunes moved to focus full-time on his position with the President's Intelligence Advisory Board.[1]
