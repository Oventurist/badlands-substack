# Petr Fiala

Petr Fiala is the Prime Minister of the [[czech-republic|Czech Republic]].[1]

## Opposition to the Trump arms scheme for Ukraine

In July 2025, Fiala announced that the Czech Republic would not participate in the initiative proposed by US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] under which European [[nato|NATO]] members would fund purchases of American weapons for [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] "The Czech Republic is focusing on other projects and ways to help Ukraine," Fiala told the news outlet Publico. "Therefore, at this moment we are not considering joining this project."[1]

The Czech refusal was one of several European rejections of the scheme reported the same week, alongside Italy's determination that its budget did not permit participation, and criticism from the German opposition party [[die-linke|Die Linke]].[1] Moscow likened the effort to the United States preparing a deadly meal for Ukraine and forcing others to pay for it.[1]

Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] read the cascade of opt-outs as evidence that the Trump–[[mark-rutte|Rutte]] announcement functioned as a ploy to bankrupt and expose NATO's European members rather than as a genuine armament program.[1]

## 2025 election defeat

Fiala, the incumbent Czech prime minister, led the three-party SPOLU alliance to a distant second place with 23% of the vote in the [[2025-czech-parliamentary-election|2025 Czech parliamentary election]], behind the 35% won by [[andrej-babis|Andrej Babiš]]'s ANO.[2] During the campaign the two men traded petty insults over everything from foreign policy to the price of butter, with Babiš calling Fiala a "fool" whose brain was clouded by "anti-Babisism."[2] SPOLU ruled out joining a coalition with Babiš.[2]
