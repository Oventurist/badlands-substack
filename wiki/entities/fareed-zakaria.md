# Fareed Zakaria

Fareed Zakaria is an American journalist and author, best known in the Q research context as the writer of *The Post-American World*, the book that [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] was photographed carrying.[1]

## The Post-American World and Q Drop #117

Q Drop #117 asked readers what book Obama had been "caught reading." The answer given in Badlands Media's "Q for Dummies" series is Zakaria's [[post-american-world|The Post-American World]], described there as a work about what many readers consider to be the fall of America.[1] The commentary treats the image of a sitting American president openly displaying such a book as significant in itself, posing the rhetorical question of why a president would be reading — and be seen reading — a book premised on American decline.[1]

## Media affiliations

The series notes that Zakaria has aligned himself over the course of his career with what it calls the usual mainstream-media outlets, including CNN, The Washington Post (rendered in the article as "The Washington Compost"), and MSNBC.[1] Those affiliations are cited as consistent with the broader argument in Drop #113 that a handful of consolidated media companies control the flow of information, an arrangement the series links to [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]].[1]
