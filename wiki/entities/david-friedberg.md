# David Friedberg

David Friedberg is an American entrepreneur and investor. He founded the weather-insurance company Climate Corporation, later acquired by Monsanto, and subsequently led the venture studio Ohalo and The Production Board, focusing on agriculture, food, and biotechnology. He is also widely known as a co-host of the All-In podcast, a forum in which Silicon Valley investors discuss technology and politics.

Friedberg was named in March 2026 among the appointees to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], as reported in the March 26 [[badlands-brief-143|Badlands Brief]], where [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] listed him among appointees identified with the [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]] network.
