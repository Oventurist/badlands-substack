# Accountable.US

Accountable.US is a left-leaning American watchdog group that files complaints and research targeting political figures.[1]

## Voter fraud complaint against RFK Jr.

On the morning of Wednesday, January 8, 2025 — less than two weeks before [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s inauguration — Accountable.US filed a complaint, shared exclusively with Politico, alleging that [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] committed felony election fraud when he voted in the 2024 election.[1] Kennedy was at the time Trump's pick to lead the Department of Health and Human Services.[1] The complaint was lodged with the [[new-york-state-board-of-elections|New York State Board of Elections]] and called on the agency's Division of Election Law Enforcement to investigate Kennedy for "registering for and voting" from "a New York residence at which he does not legally reside."[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] mocked the sudden appearance of leftist watchdog groups professing concern for voter integrity, asking whether Accountable.US was also examining non-citizens on the voter rolls or simply harassing political opponents through lawfare.[1] She allowed that Kennedy should be held accountable if he did commit voter fraud, while noting that complaints about voter fraud are typically governed by what she called "The Doctrine of Ignore, Deny, Dismiss."[1] She further argued that political nonprofits which secure tax-free status by claiming to be non-political should themselves be held accountable — especially one named "Accountable."[1]
