# Sydney Sweeney

Sydney Sweeney is an American actress who in mid-2025 became a focal point of American cultural politics after starring in a controversial denim advertisement for [[american-eagle-outfitters|American Eagle]].[1]

## 2025 American Eagle campaign

The commercial, debuted in late July 2025, drew immediate and polarised reaction. On 4 August 2025 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] praised the spot on Truth Social, saying the jeans were "flying off the shelves" and encouraging Sweeney directly.[1] American Eagle's stock jumped nearly 23% the same day.[1]

Trump also drew attention to multiple weekend reports indicating that Sweeney is registered as a Republican voter in Florida, and contrasted her with singer [[taylor-swift|Taylor Swift]], whom he criticised as "woke" and "NO LONGER HOT" following Swift's endorsement of Vice President Kamala Harris.[1]

Badlands hosts cited the episode as an example of a broader cultural realignment in motion, arguing that entertainment and advertising are the primary terrain of political contest rather than a downstream effect of it.[1]

## Campaign results

By early September 2025 Sweeney's "Sydney Sweeney Has Great Jeans" campaign, launched in July, had been credited with a nearly 30% rise in [[american-eagle-outfitters|American Eagle]]'s stock and a substantial increase in sales.[2] Chief marketing officer Craig Brommers told the company's second-quarter earnings call that "Sweeney is a winner, and in just six weeks, the campaign has generated unprecedented new customer acquisition."[2] Products from the collaboration, including custom jeans, sold out within a week — some within a day — and her "Syd's Picks" shop was restocked at least four times, with the brand gaining new customers in every U.S. county.[2]
