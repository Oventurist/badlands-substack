# Stormy Daniels

Stormy Daniels is an adult film actress at the center of the Manhattan "hush-money" criminal case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Prosecutors alleged that Trump oversaw a $130,000 non-disclosure payment to Daniels roughly two weeks before the 2016 presidential election, in order to silence her regarding an alleged affair.[1] Trump has denied both the affair and any wrongdoing.[1] On April 4, 2023, he was arraigned on 34 counts of falsifying business records connected to the alleged cover-up of that payment.[1]

In May 2023, presiding judge [[juan-merchan|Juan Merchan]] issued a social-media gag order barring Trump from posting evidence or publicly discussing witnesses in the case.[1]

## The 2018 Cohen FEC letter

In March 2023 a purported 2018 letter from a lawyer for [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] to the [[federal-election-commission|Federal Election Commission]] resurfaced, stating that Cohen had used his own personal funds to pay Daniels and that the Trump campaign was not a party to the transaction and did not reimburse him — reporting described the document as "totally exculpatory" and as a threat to the [[manhattan-district-attorneys-office|Manhattan DA]]'s case.[2] Badlands commentary noted that the underlying allegations had already fared badly in court, pointing out that Daniels' former lawyer Michael Avenatti went to prison and that Daniels herself was ordered to pay roughly $300,000 in legal fees.[2]

## Trump's March 2023 remarks

In late March 2023, after the [[2023-waco-rally|Waco rally]], [[donald-trump|Trump]] publicly dismissed the Manhattan case as "a fake case" and said "I think they've already dropped the case … they have absolutely nothing," while disparaging Daniels — legally Stephanie Clifford — with the nickname "Horse Face" and denying interest in her.[3] Reporting on the episode reiterated that the 2018 letter from [[michael-cohen|Cohen]]'s lawyer to the [[federal-election-commission|Federal Election Commission]] stated Cohen used his own funds for the $130,000 hush payment to Clifford and that Trump did not reimburse him.[3]

## Willingness to testify (April 2023)

In an interview with [[piers-morgan|Piers Morgan]] of TalkTV that aired on April 6, 2023 — her first comments after Trump pleaded not guilty to charges related to the hush-money payment scheme — Daniels said she was "absolutely" willing to testify at his New York criminal trial.[4] "Having them call me in and put me on the stand legitimizes my story and who I am," she told Morgan, adding that if prosecutors did not call her "it almost feels like they're hiding me" and that people would automatically assume she must not be a credible witness.[4] Asked directly whether she would testify if asked, she replied, "Oh, absolutely."[4]

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], the analyst [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that Daniels had been making "Redemption" and "Movie" posts on social media while teasing the mainstream press, and argued that her positioning could map onto either side of the conflict.[4] He noted that convictions arising in Daniels's orbit had fallen on figures aligned against Trump, pointing readers to [[michael-avenatti|Michael Avenatti]]'s interviews before the previous attempt to use Daniels against Trump and inviting the same exercise with respect to [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]].[4] Citing [[just-human|Just Human]], he raised the question of what, precisely, Daniels had been paid for by Trump-owned LLCs, and asked whose assets these figures ultimately were.[4]

## Trial testimony (May 2024)

Daniels took the witness stand in the Manhattan hush-money trial in May 2024 and described her alleged encounter with [[donald-trump|Trump]] in explicit detail, testifying that she went to the bathroom in a hotel room and emerged to find him waiting in his underwear.[5] "He was just up on the bed like this," she said, pulling her leg up to her waist on the witness stand, describing the experience as feeling "like a fun house" and recalling that things seemed to move in slow motion despite her being neither drunk nor on drugs.[5]

"I had my clothes and my shoes off. I believe my bra was still on. We were in missionary position," she testified, prompting objections from Trump's attorneys that Judge [[juan-merchan|Merchan]] sustained; Politico reported that one female juror appeared uncomfortable, looking away while holding her forehead.[5] Asked how she came to have sex with Trump, Daniels said she was baffled, testifying, "I felt to myself, 'great, I put myself in this bad situation,'" and adding, "I just think I blacked out."[5] The testimony was covered by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]].[5]

## 2024 trial testimony and perjury allegations

Following the close of the defense case in [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s New York hush money trial in May 2024, Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that Daniels and [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] should be charged with perjury, and that the attorneys who called them as witnesses should be disbarred.[6]

## Reaction to her trial testimony

Comedian [[bill-maher|Bill Maher]] was unimpressed by Daniels' testimony in the New York criminal trial of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], telling a "Real Time" panel that "She's a bad witness!"[7] Noting that the Georgia and classified documents prosecutions had stalled, Maher quipped, "So it's Stormy or bust."[7]
