# Alice Bailey

Alice Bailey was an esoteric writer of the Theosophical tradition, named by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] among the occultists who promoted narratives of "Jewish bankers' conspiracies undermining the noble honest royal families of Europe."[1]

She appears in a list with [[nesta-webster|Nesta Webster]], [[gerard-encausse|Papus]], [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]], [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], and [[george-sylvester-viereck|George Sylvester Viereck]], whose writings Ehret treats as expressions of the deep hostility that occultists have felt toward Jews — a hostility he attributes to the Old Testament's repeated interventions against occultism and human sacrifice.[1] Bailey's tradition, [[theosophy|Theosophy]], is the same milieu through which [[protocols-of-the-elders-of-zion|*The Protocols of the Learned Elders of Zion*]] entered circulation in Russia.[1]
