# Harry Dent

Harry Dent is an economist focused on consumer spending and the owner of HS Dent Investment Management.[1] In December 2023 he issued a widely circulated warning that 2024 would bring the "biggest crash of our lifetime" in the United States.[1]

## 2024 crash forecast

Speaking to Fox Business, Dent said that "since 2009, this has been 100 percent artificial, unprecedented money printing and deficits: $27 trillion over 15 years, to be exact," calling the situation "off the charts" and a dangerous state, and predicting that 2024 would be "the biggest single crash year we'll see in our lifetimes."[1] He argued that markets needed to "get back down to normal" and to "send a message to central banks," saying he did not expect to see a comparable bubble again in anyone's lifetime.[1]

Dent dated the current bubble to late 2021, amid the COVID-19 pandemic.[1] He warned that conditions would not return to normal within a few years and that the coming decline would not be a correction but something "more in the '29 to '32 level," invoking the 1929 crash that led into the Great Depression, and remarking that "anybody who sat through that would have shot their stockbroker."[1] He forecast that most of the crash would occur in 2024 and become more obvious by May, advising investors that stepping aside for six to twelve months risked only modest foregone gains if he were wrong, while avoiding massive losses and enabling reinvestment at very low prices if he were right.[1]

The forecast was carried by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]] and republished as a bonus item in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
