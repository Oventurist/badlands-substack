# Bruce Skaug

Bruce Skaug is a Republican member of the Idaho House of Representatives from Nampa and an attorney, and a co-sponsor of [[idaho-house-bill-515|House Bill 515]], legislation authorizing the death penalty for certain sex crimes against children.[1]

## House Bill 515

Skaug told House lawmakers during the February 2024 debate that capital punishment under the bill would be reserved for heinous cases, such as repeat offenders.[1] "There is a deep, dark, dark side in our culture. And it's our job to protect the children. There are times when things are so wicked that retribution is appropriate," he said.[1]

Responding to objections that the measure conflicted with the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]]'s 2008 decision in [[kennedy-v-louisiana|Kennedy v. Louisiana]], which barred the death penalty for child rape, Skaug — himself a lawyer — argued that the current Court would likely reach a different conclusion, telling colleagues: "Well there's constitutional and there's constitutional. Depends on the court of the day."[1]
