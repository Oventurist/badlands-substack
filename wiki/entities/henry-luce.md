# Henry Luce

Henry Luce was the American publisher behind *Time*, *Fortune* and *Life* magazines, and, in Matthew Ehret's account, one of the most influential American promoters of Italian fascism.[1]

Luce "unapologetically gushed" over Il Duce, putting [[benito-mussolini|Benito Mussolini]] on the cover of [[time-magazine|Time]] eight times between 1923 and 1943 while relentlessly promoting fascism as the "economic miracle solution for America," a line he also pushed in *Fortune* and *Life*.[1] The campaign found an audience among Americans traumatized by the depression that began in 1929, many of whom had come to embrace the idea that an American fascism would put food on the table and help them find work.[1]
