# William Jennings Bryan

**William Jennings Bryan** led the Democratic Party during the period in which the campaign for a federal income tax gathered force.[1]

According to Badlands Media, the [[populist-party|Populist Party]] demanded a graduated income tax in its 1892 platform, mirroring the Democratic Party under Bryan, who advocated for the income tax law passed in 1894 and proposed an income tax in the party's 1908 platform.[1] These efforts culminated in the ratification of the [[sixteenth-amendment|Sixteenth Amendment]] in 1913.[1]
