# Kaja Kallas

Kaja Kallas is the foreign policy chief of the [[european-union|European Union]].

## Greenland and the US

The January 9, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Kallas said the bloc has discussed how it might respond if the United States advances efforts to acquire [[greenland|Greenland]], calling US messaging on the subject "extremely concerning." Her remarks are cited alongside German President [[frank-walter-steinmeier|Frank-Walter Steinmeier]]'s warning that US policy under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is eroding the global order.

The Badlands commentary reads Kallas's statement as a sign that EU officials are "preparing to confront President Trump and the US as an adversary on the issue of Greenland," a development the author suggests could lead to "a diplomatic divorce between the two continents" and the end of the ambitions set out in [[agenda-2030|Agenda 2030]].
