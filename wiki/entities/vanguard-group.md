# Vanguard Group

The Vanguard Group is one of the largest asset managers in the world, best known for its index funds.

## Role in the media ownership argument

A Swan Song for the MSM names Vanguard, alongside [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[state-street|State Street]] and [[berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]], as one of the institutional investors that hold overlapping stakes in all six of the conglomerates that dominate [[mainstream-media|centralized media]]. The article uses this common ownership to argue that competition among the Big Six is superficial and that their news divisions ultimately answer to the same financial interests, whose representatives it describes as fixtures of the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].


## Suspension of SPLC contributions (May 2026)

The May 1, 2026 Badlands Brief reported that Vanguard, alongside [[fidelity-investments|Fidelity Investments]], had paused charitable contributions to the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] after a federal indictment involving an individual allegedly connected to the organization. Although the SPLC was not itself charged, the association prompted a reassessment by donors concerned about legal and public-relations fallout — an episode the Brief cited as evidence of growing pressure on large asset managers to justify both their political neutrality and their charitable affiliations.
