# Dean Pregerson

**Dean Pregerson** is a United States District Judge who sits on the Central District of California.[1]

## Palisades fire litigation

Pregerson was one of two federal judges who lost their Pacific Palisades homes in the January 2025 firestorm and who joined hundreds of their neighbors in suing the [[los-angeles-department-of-water-and-power|Los Angeles Department of Water and Power]] over the utility's preparation for and response to the blaze.[1] He filed the lawsuit last week of April 2025 along with his family and with [[vijay-gandhi|Vijay "Jay" Gandhi]], a former magistrate judge in the same court.[1] Filed in Los Angeles Superior Court, the suit alleges that the [[palisades-fire|Palisades conflagration]] "was caused by both LADWP's water and power assets, specifically empty reservoirs and energized powerlines."[1]

The complaint cites reporting by the Los Angeles Times finding that LADWP's Santa Ynez Reservoir, located in the Palisades, sat empty during the firefight after having been closed months earlier for repairs.[1] It further alleges that despite dire warnings from the National Weather Service of a "Particularly Dangerous Condition-Red Flag Warning" and of "critical fire weather" with the potential for rapid fire spread and extreme fire behavior, LADWP was unprepared for the fire.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that there is "a not insignificant chance that suing the utility leads to the government on this one," while expressing skepticism that the case would reach discovery, partly because it was brought by judges whose filings carry a baked-in legal review.[1] She predicted the case was more likely to settle, with the secrets of public-private-partnership utility governance sealed in private action.[1]
