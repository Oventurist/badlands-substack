# Thomas Speciale

Thomas Speciale is a conservative national security consultant who gained wide attention in late October 2025 after publicly confronting former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] over Brennan's decision to sign the 2020 letter asserting that the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]] story bore "all the classic earmarks of a Russian information operation."[1]

## The Brennan confrontation

Speciale pressed Brennan during a conference event hosted by the Michael V. Hayden Center for Intelligence, Policy, and International Security at George Mason University in Fairfax, Virginia.[1] Video of the exchange circulated widely: Brennan moved into Speciale's face and shouted, "You misrepresented that! We never said it was disinformation; we said it was Russian influence operations, which is what they do. There's a big difference."[1]

Speciale countered that former FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]] "knew" the laptop story was not a Russian influence operation. Brennan replied, "No, you don't know that!" and then, "I'm not going to waste my time with you," before waving Speciale off and walking away.[1]

## Coverage by Badlands Media

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] highlighted the viral clip in its November 3, 2025 news brief, and host Jon Herold announced that Speciale — described as "the man behind that meltdown" — would appear as a guest on that Saturday's episode of the Devolution Power Hour.[1]
