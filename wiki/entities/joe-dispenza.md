# Joe Dispenza

**Dr. Joe Dispenza** is an author and speaker on consciousness and epigenetics quoted repeatedly in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]].[1]

On inherited fear, the essay quotes Dispenza's statement that "genes do not create disease on their own; rather, it is the environment that signals the gene to reprogram the cell," using it to argue for a dynamic interplay between external stressors and genetic responses in the transmission of [[intergenerational-trauma|intergenerational trauma]].[1] On emotional dysregulation, he is quoted as observing that "where attention goes, energy flows," which the author reads as evidence that sustained focus on fear and anger perpetuates a reality defined by scarcity and struggle, while shifting focus toward gratitude, empathy, and resilience opens pathways to empowerment.[1]

Dispenza is also cited within the essay's account of [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]]. His line that "knowledge is power, but knowledge about yourself is self-empowerment" supports the argument for awareness as a weapon, and his claim that "the moment you begin to connect to the energy of your future, you are moving out of the familiar past into a new reality" is used to frame the engagement of the mind-body-soul complex as a shift from reactive survival patterns to empowered intentionality.[1]
