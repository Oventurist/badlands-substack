# Michael Cassidy

Michael Cassidy is a Christian military veteran and former military officer who, in December 2023, destroyed a Baphomet display erected by the [[satanic-temple|Satanic Temple]] in the Iowa State Capitol building.[1] Reporting described him as a former military officer, with Badlands commentary additionally identifying him as an Iowa state representative figure at the time of the incident.[1]

## The Iowa Capitol incident

The pentagram-adorned Baphomet "Satanic Christmas display" installed at Iowa's State Capitol was, according to the Republic Sentinel and Modernity News, "beheaded" by Cassidy, who tore down the caped goat figure and threw the skull in a bin.[1] Cassidy said he took the action "to awaken Christians to the anti-Christian acts promoted by our government."[1]

He added: "The world may tell Christians to submissively accept the legitimization of Satan, but none of the founders would have considered government sanction of Satanic altars inside Capitol buildings as protected by the First Amendment."[1]

## Reaction

The Satanic Temple of Iowa acknowledged that the display had been "destroyed beyond repair," but said it was "proud to continue our holiday display for the next few days that we have been allotted," asking that visitors travel together for safety and use the group's Seven Tenets "as a reminder for empathy, in the knowledge that justice is being pursued the correct way, through legal means." The statement closed with "Hail Satan!"[1]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[paul-fleuret|Paul Fleuret]] praised Cassidy for doing "what so many of us wished we could have done," arguing that the same institutions that host Satanic displays would have mobilized immediately against a Christian shrine in the same building, and thanking Cassidy for "showing the faith, conviction, and backbone that so many of your colleagues lack."[1]
