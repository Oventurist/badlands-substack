# Culture of Change

Culture of Change is a program on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] hosted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], broadcast Sundays at 6PM ET.[1]

Episode 18, "Humanity as a Service; Consciousness as Code," is cited as extended treatment of the emerging [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] threat, in which Ashe in America and CannCon dig into and debate the questions raised in her July 12, 2023 written essay on AI — the [[technological-singularity|singularity]], [[reality-collapse|reality collapse]], and the [[ai-pause-open-letter|proposed pause]] on advanced model training.[1] The episode is hosted on Rumble.[1]

As with other Badlands Media contributions, the program's positions represent the opinions of the contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself.[1]

## Episode 138 (February 2026)

Episode 138 of Culture of Change, streamed on Rumble and aired Monday, February 9, 2026, featured [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] and [[blue-eyes|Blue Eyes]] discussing artificial intelligence's disruption of every industry.[2] Ashe described it as "a fun show" and drew on the conversation in her Badlands Brief commentary supporting the premise behind the [[ai-data-center-compact|AI data center compact]], arguing that the AI transformation will reward companies that harness the technology and marginalize those that chase shiny objects — with AI firms profiting from both and requiring extensive infrastructure to do so.[2]

## August 2023 episode on the Sund interview

On a Sunday in August 2023, host [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] was joined on the program by independent investigative journalist [[mel-hawley|Mel Hawley]], who exclusively covers January 6 truth, to break down [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]]'s [[tucker-on-twitter|Tucker on Twitter]] interview with former Capitol Police Chief [[steven-sund|Steven Sund]].[3] During the discussion Hawley asked whether the inconsistencies between the official January 6 narrative and the official record were the reason the [[january-6-committee|January 6 Committee]] destroyed all of its records — a question Ashe said House Republicans needed to investigate.[3] The episode aired on Rumble as part of the show's regular Sunday 6:00 p.m. ET slot on [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[3]
