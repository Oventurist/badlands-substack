# Britney Spears

**Britney Spears** is an American pop singer presented in Badlands Media commentary as an emblematic victim of the entertainment industry's treatment of child performers.[1]

## Badlands account

Contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that the industry destroyed Spears's life beginning in her childhood on [[disney|Disney]]'s *Mickey Mouse Club*, then turned her into a sexual object as a teenager while abusing her, leaving her mentally broken.[1] He argued that the cycle was completed with mockery, citing the poster for *Ricky Stanicky* in which [[john-cena|John Cena]] appears dressed as Spears from the "Baby One More Time" video.[1] Carlson summarised the pattern with the phrase, "These people are sick."[1]

The Spears example sits within his broader claim that success in the music industry can depend on rituals of self-defilement rather than talent, a dynamic he also associated with [[sean-combs|P. Diddy]].[1]
