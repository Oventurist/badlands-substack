# Minnesota Wild

The Minnesota Wild are a professional ice hockey team competing in the National Hockey League. In March 2023 the franchise became part of a wider league-wide controversy over themed warm-up jerseys.[1]

## 2023 Pride Night jersey refusal

Ahead of the team's second annual Pride Night, held against the Calgary Flames on Tuesday, March 7, 2023, Wild players declined to wear rainbow-themed warm-up jerseys despite the team having previously announced that they would.[1] The event had been designed to highlight the LGBTQ community with numerous in-game elements incorporating the Pride theme.[1]

A special Pride Night ticket package was offered to fans, including a team Pride Night t-shirt, a pregame on-ice photo and a pregame gathering, with a portion of proceeds benefiting Twin Cities Pride, a local nonprofit describing its mission as empowering every LGBTQ+ person to live as their true self.[1]

## Commentary

The [[badlands-news-brief]] characterized the refusal as part of a growing trend across the NHL, with an increasing number of players and teams declining to wear Pride jerseys.[1] The accompanying commentary suggested the campaign had been poorly matched to the league's demographics and posed a hypothetical about whether a mandatory "Christian Pride Night" would be tolerated, arguing that American public patience with the LGBTQ agenda was nearing a limit.[1]
