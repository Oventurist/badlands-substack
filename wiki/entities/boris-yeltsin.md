# Boris Yeltsin

Boris Yeltsin (1931–2007) was the first President of the Russian Federation, serving from 1991 to 1999. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-re-alignment-of-world-systems|A Re-Alignment of World Systems]], his presidency is presented as the political container within which Russia's sovereign economic powers were dismantled.

## Role in the source

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that the accelerated liberalization of the former Soviet bloc was carried out "under the direction of such monetarist 'reformers' such as [[anatoly-chubais|Anatoly Chubais]] and [[yegor-gaidar|Yegor Gaidar]] under Yeltsin," in parallel with Western participants such as the IMF's [[jeffrey-sachs|Jeffrey Sachs]], [[al-gore|Al Gore]], [[strobe-talbott|Strobe Talbott]] and [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]].

The article contrasts this era with the later period in which [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] "began exiling many of these unrepentant oligarchs," who then "quickly made their way to London where many became disposable playthings of the British Empire."
