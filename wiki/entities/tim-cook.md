# Tim Cook

Tim Cook is the chief executive officer of [[apple|Apple]], the American consumer technology company. In May 2025 he became the subject of public comment by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who said he had personally raised objections with Cook over Apple's plans to expand iPhone manufacturing in [[india|India]].[1]

## Trump's objection to Indian manufacturing

Speaking on May 15, 2025 during a visit to [[qatar|Qatar]], Trump said he had spoken with Cook the previous day to discuss what he described as "a little problem."[1] According to Trump's account, he told the Apple chief executive: "Tim, you're my friend. I've treated you very good. You're coming in with $500 billion but now I hear you're building all over India."[1]

Trump continued that he did not want Apple building in India except to serve the Indian market itself: "You can build in India if you want to take care of India," adding, "we put up with all the plants that you built in China for years. Now you've got to build us. We're not interested in you building in India. India can take care of themselves."[1]

## The $500 billion pledge

The figure Trump cited refers to Apple's February 2025 announcement that it would spend $500 billion in the United States on work with thousands of suppliers across all 50 states, direct employment, Apple Intelligence infrastructure and datacenters, corporate facilities, and Apple TV+ productions in 20 states.[1] The company also committed to working with partners on a factory that would build servers to run its Apple Intelligence artificial intelligence service.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the exchange as evidence that decades of offshoring to India — first in IT outsourcing, then business process outsourcing, then creative and consulting work — had been sold to American firms as a "competitive imperative" while hollowing out domestic industry.[1] In that reading, Trump's message to Cook was a signal to Apple to "claim your share of the coming American Greatness."[1]


## American Manufacturing Program announcement (August 2025)

In August 2025 Cook announced that [[apple|Apple]] was raising its US investment commitment to $600 billion over four years and launching the [[american-manufacturing-program|American Manufacturing Program]].[2] "Today, we're proud to increase our investments across the United States to $600 billion over four years and launch our new American Manufacturing Program," he said, adding that the effort included new and expanded work with ten companies across America whose components are used in Apple products sold globally.[2] He also expressed gratitude to the President for his support of the initiative.[2]
