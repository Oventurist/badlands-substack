# Tim Cook

Tim Cook is the chief executive officer of [[apple-inc|Apple Inc.]], the American technology company behind the iPhone and Mac product lines. He has served as CEO since 2011, succeeding company co-founder Steve Jobs.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Cook was among the high-profile business executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] that week. The administration said the delegation would push for new economic agreements with Beijing as Trump prepared to meet Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] at the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Beijing summit]], with the trip expected to focus heavily on trade and investment and to secure business deals and purchase agreements.

The inclusion of Cook placed Apple among a roster of American industrial and financial leaders — including [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] of Tesla, [[larry-fink|Larry Fink]] of [[blackrock|BlackRock]], and [[kelly-ortberg|Kelly Ortberg]] of [[boeing|Boeing]] — traveling with the president to lock in investment flows and commercial realignments with Beijing.
