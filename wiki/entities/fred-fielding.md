# Fred Fielding

Fred Fielding is a veteran Washington attorney and former White House counsel.[1]

In January 2024 he was announced as joining the [[torridon-group|Torridon Group]] of counsel, part of a wave of hires that also included former acting Drug Enforcement Administration head [[tim-shea|Tim Shea]] and several [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration White House lawyers.[1]
