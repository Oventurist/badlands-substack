# Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez

## 2028 Democratic power rankings

Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez was named in July 2026 among the top three figures in [[axios|Axios]]'s early Democratic presidential power rankings for 2028, alongside [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]].[1] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called it "hilarious that we're talking about AOC being anywhere near executive power," argued the ranking reflects a party with no new talent, and framed the episode as part of a "DSA Rising op," asking when a [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] comeback would arrive since "he's the most electable loser in the lot."[1]
