# Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, frequently referred to by the initials AOC, is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from New York and a leading figure of the party's progressive left.

The March 4, 2026 Badlands Brief identifies her, alongside [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]], as a principal backer of insurgent challengers in the Democratic House primaries preceding the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]]. Many of these candidates have declined to commit to supporting House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]], a refusal the Brief presents as evidence of an intra-party "civil war" driven by generational change and anti-establishment frustration.
