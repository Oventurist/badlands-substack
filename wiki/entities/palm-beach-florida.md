# Palm Beach, Florida

**Palm Beach** is a town on Florida's Atlantic coast, best known in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] coverage as the location of [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]]'s [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] estate.[1]

## The August 2022 search

On August 8, 2022, what [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]] called "the serene embrace of Palm Beach, Florida" was disrupted when the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] descended on the Mar-a-Lago estate to execute a search warrant in the [[mar-a-lago-raid|classified documents investigation]] of the former president.[1] The warrant had been authorised by Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] and approved by Magistrate Judge [[bruce-reinhart|Bruce Reinhart]].[1]

The town thereafter became the focal point of the records dispute: the [[2023-trump-classified-documents-indictment|37-count indictment]] returned in 2023 centred on allegations of mishandling records at the Palm Beach property, and the superseding indictment of July 27, 2023 named estate staff including [[walt-nauta|Walt Nauta]] and [[carlos-de-oliveira|Carlos De Oliveira]] alongside Trump.[1]
