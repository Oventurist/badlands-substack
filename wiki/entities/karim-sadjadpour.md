# Karim Sadjadpour

Karim Sadjadpour is an Iran-focused fellow at the [[carnegie-endowment-for-international-peace|Carnegie Endowment for International Peace]] who appears frequently as a television commentator on Iran policy during the 2026 [[iran-war|Iran war]].[1]

## "Suicide dolphins" appearance

In early May 2026, Sadjadpour appeared with anchor [[kaitlan-collins|Kaitlan Collins]] on [[cnn|CNN]] and told viewers that the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] had reported that [[iran|Iran]] was contemplating deploying "suicide dolphins" — dolphins equipped with mines — against U.S. ships.[1] He framed the reported plan as evidence of Tehran's desperation, adding that "that's not a measure which projects real strength."[1] The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] noted that at no point in the segment did the anchor challenge the assertion, and treated the exchange as an example of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]] in which credentialed think-tank experts launder implausible war claims through legacy outlets.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] used the segment to argue that "Uniparty Right and Uniparty Left" media were in lockstep pushing the same war-fomenting material, since [[fox-news|Fox News]] hosts advanced the identical storyline.[1]
