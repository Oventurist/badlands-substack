# CelticCapri Corp

CelticCapri Corp is a passthrough S-corporation entity associated with [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] that became a focus of Republican congressional scrutiny in June 2023.[1]

## 2017 tax returns and the $10 million question

Republican lawmakers demanded transparency from Biden regarding his 2017 tax returns, in which CelticCapri Corp listed nearly $10 million without specifying revenue line items — leaving unanswered who paid the entity and for what.[1] The demand came amid allegations of a $5 million Ukrainian "bribery" scheme linked to Biden.[1]

Before the 2020 election, USA Today published a fact-check article supporting claims that the Bidens earned roughly $15.6 million from speaking fees and book deals between 2017 and 2019, of which more than $10 million was attributed to profits from Biden's memoir *Promise Me, Dad* and $3 million to profits from Jill Biden's book.[1] [[the-federalist|The Federalist]] criticized that accounting, noting that following the source link for the $10 million figure led only to Biden's campaign website with financial disclosure links to individual returns and no S-corporation tax returns: "So, in reality, readers were left with a smokescreen."[1]

That perceived smokescreen is what prompted Republican lawmakers to press for disclosure of the origin of the nearly $10 million routed through the passthrough entity.[1]
