# Alberto Gonzales

Alberto Gonzales is a former Attorney General of the United States who served during the George W. Bush administration. He appears in Badlands Media coverage as one of the former senior Justice Department officials swept into the congressional inquiry surrounding the Jeffrey Epstein case.[1]

## Epstein-related subpoena

On Tuesday, August 5, 2025, the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]], under Chairman [[james-comer|James Comer]] (R-Ky.), issued deposition subpoenas compelling testimony from eleven individuals connected to the handling of the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] matter. Gonzales was named among a string of current and former U.S. Attorneys General on that list, alongside [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]], [[loretta-lynch|Loretta Lynch]], [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]], [[bill-barr|William Barr]] and [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]].[1]

The subpoenas followed a bipartisan action taken on July 23 by the Oversight Committee's Federal Law Enforcement Subcommittee, which approved by voice vote a motion from Rep. [[scott-perry|Scott Perry]] (R-Pa.) authorizing subpoenas for the named officials.[1] The committee separately subpoenaed the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] for documents tied to Epstein and his network.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributors framed the subpoena round as a test of whether former officials would be held to the same standard as Trump-aligned figures such as Steve Bannon and Peter Navarro, who were imprisoned for contempt of Congress after defying subpoenas.[1]
