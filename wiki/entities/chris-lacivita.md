# Chris LaCivita

Chris LaCivita is a Republican political operative who served as a co-campaign manager of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Role in the 2024 debate negotiations

In April 2024, LaCivita and fellow co-campaign manager [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]] sent a letter to the co-chairs of the [[commission-on-presidential-debates|Commission on Presidential Debates]], [[frank-fahrenkopf|Frank Fahrenkopf Jr.]] and [[antonia-hernandez|Antonia Hernandez]], calling for the 2024 general-election presidential debates with President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to be held "much earlier" than the commission had proposed and for additional debates to be added to the schedule.[1] The letter, obtained exclusively by Fox News Digital, stated that the campaign was writing "in agreement with the pending letter" from television networks advocating that presidential debates occur in 2024.[1] The managers acknowledged the commission had already announced three presidential debates and one vice-presidential debate for later in the year but wrote that the campaign was "in favor of these debates beginning much earlier."[1]

Trump himself told Fox News Digital that he was "totally committed" to debating Biden "anytime, anywhere, anyplace."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the debate challenge as an example of Trump's skill at what he called narrative warfare, arguing that Trump's earlier refusal to participate in the Republican primary debates had rendered those events "moot" while his public baiting of Biden was designed to expose the incumbent during what the writer described as a cascading crisis for the Biden campaign.[1]

## Appointment as RNC chief of staff (March 2024)

LaCivita, a senior campaign adviser to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], was named chief of staff of the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] in March 2024 as Trump's operation took over the party's machinery.[2] The takeover followed the election of [[michael-whatley|Michael Whatley]] as chair and [[lara-trump|Lara Trump]] as co-chair, and coincided with a purge of more than 60 RNC staffers overseen by chief operating officer [[sean-cairncross|Sean Cairncross]].[2]
