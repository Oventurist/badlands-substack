# Larry Nassar

Larry Nassar is a convicted sexual predator and former Team USA gymnastics doctor.[1] He is serving a sentence of 40 to 175 years in prison for sexually abusing hundreds of gymnasts, including Olympians [[simone-biles|Simone Biles]], Aly Raisman and Gabby Douglas.[1]

Nassar's crimes re-entered public debate in June 2025 when activist [[riley-gaines|Riley Gaines]] invoked them in an online exchange with Biles over transgender participation in women's sports, contrasting the abuse Biles had witnessed and denounced with what Gaines characterized as Biles's willingness to see women "forced to strip naked in front of men."[1]

## DOJ settlement for FBI failures (April 2024)

In April 2024 the Biden administration agreed to pay over $138 million to victims of Nassar while acknowledging the [[fbi|FBI]]'s failures to properly investigate warnings that he was exploiting his position as a sports physician to molest young girls under the guise of treatment.[2] The [[us-department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] said in an April 23 statement that it had settled 139 civil claims arising from allegations of sexual abuse by Nassar, who had earlier been found guilty of abusing hundreds of victims under the pretext of performing medical treatments.[2]

The settlements totaled $138.7 million and resolved administrative claims against the DOJ alleging inadequate FBI investigation of Nassar's actions.[2] A DOJ watchdog had found in July 2021 that parts of the Bureau's response to the allegations were inadequate, and Acting Associate Attorney General Benjamin Mizer stated that the FBI failed to conduct an adequate investigation of Nassar's conduct.[2] Badlands commentary observed that nearly $1 billion in claims had been awarded across the wider Nassar litigation, and criticized the absence of systemic reform despite an apology from Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]].[2]
