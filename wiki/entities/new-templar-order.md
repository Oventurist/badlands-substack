# New Templar Order

The New Templar Order (Ordo Novi Templi) was an occult chivalric order officially created in 1907 by the Theosophist [[lanz-von-liebenfels|Lanz von Liebenfels]], and later adopted as a model by [[heinrich-himmler|Heinrich Himmler]], described in Matthew Ehret's series as "the chief of the New Templar Order."[1]

## Origins

Liebenfels, a former Cistercian monk, founded the order on Cistercian principles, much as the original Knights Templar had been constituted by the Cistercian grand strategist Bernard de Clairvaux in the early twelfth century.[1] Its first headquarters were established at Werfenstein Castle, decorated in honour of Hugues de Payens, the first Grand Master of the Templars.[1] Liebenfels also created the occult journal Ostara, to which a young [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] was an enthusiastic subscriber.[1]

## Occult connections

German New Templars were closely associated with [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]] and with Dr. Franz Hartmann, head of the German Theosophical Society, both regular attendees at Monte Verità in Switzerland.[1] It was at Monte Verità that Hartmann established an official Theosophical Lodge which interfaced closely with his fellow New Templar initiate Theodor Reuss, co-founder of the [[ordo-templi-orientis|Ordo Templi Orientis]]; by 1915 the OTO had become a conduit of Crowley, who after Reuss's death took control of the worldwide order with deep penetrations into the United States, Canada, Mexico and Germany.[1]

## Under Himmler

Himmler made [[wewelsburg-castle|Wewelsburg Castle]] the headquarters of his New Templar high initiates from 1934, modelling an inner SS priesthood on the twelve knights of King Arthur's Round Table and devoting Ahnenerbe resources to Templar-style quests for the Holy Grail and the Lost Ark.[1] Ehret argues that a later "New Templar takeover" of the US military — driven by [[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]] and a Mithraic revival of neo-Templars — helped usher in a new vanguard of psychological warfare "Jedi warriors" and modern Grail knights.[1]
