# Deamonte Kendrick

Deamonte Kendrick, also known as Yak Gotti, was a defendant in a murder trial in Georgia connected to the YSL prosecution.[1]

The case drew coverage from Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who reported that the prosecution regularly violated Kendrick's rights, contributing at least in part to his acquittal.[1] She has cited the trial as an example of her practice of monitoring criminal proceedings not out of sympathy for defendants but to watch and rebuke the government, writing that she had never heard of YSL and that murder is obviously bad, but that mobs deciding judicial outcomes is worse for all of us and posterity.[1]

In July 2026 she referenced the Kendrick trial alongside the [[derek-chauvin|Derek Chauvin]] case as precedent for her critique of influencer conduct around the [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] prosecution.[1]
