# Goznak

Goznak is a Russian state-owned currency production enterprise responsible for printing banknotes and minting coins, and the firm contracted in 2025 to produce Syria's redesigned currency.[1]

## Structure

Badlands commentary describes Goznak as a Russian central bank enterprise consisting of eight branches: two printing factories, two mints, two paper mills, a printing house, and a research and development laboratory.[1]

## History

The "Goznak" moniker was created by the Bolsheviks in 1919, when the Department of State Currency Production was renamed.[1] It existed as a state enterprise until [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] signed a decree in 2014 transforming it into a joint-stock company that is 100% owned by the state.[1] In 2008, Putin had already included Goznak on a list of strategic organizations in Russia alongside nearly 200 other entities.[1]

## Syrian banknote contract

Reuters reported in August 2025 that [[syria|Syria]] would print the banknotes for its redesigned and revalued currency in Russia, with Damascus reaching a deal with Goznak according to two bankers and another source with alleged knowledge of the plans.[1] The agreement was reportedly finalized during Syrian Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaiban's visit to Moscow in late July 2025.[1] Goznak had printed the Syrian currency during the rule of former President [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar Assad]], a close Moscow ally who was ousted in 2024 in an offensive led by the Islamist group [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham|Hayat Tahrir al-Sham]] and replaced by HTS leader [[ahmed-al-sharaa|Ahmed al-Sharaa]].[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that it is Putin who is effectively printing this money through Goznak, and read the arrangement as running parallel to [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s Azerbaijan–Armenia peace deal and reported Turkish gas transit plans, with the combined effect of subverting Israeli efforts to balkanize Syria in pursuit of [[greater-israel|Greater Israel]].[1]
