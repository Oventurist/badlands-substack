# Elie Honig

Elie Honig is a former federal prosecutor who works as a senior legal analyst for [[cnn|CNN]], where he provides commentary on high-profile criminal prosecutions.[1]

## Commentary on the Fulton County case

On March 13, 2024, after Fulton County Superior Court Judge [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]] quashed six counts of the Georgia 2020 election interference indictment, Honig characterized the ruling as an "embarrassing screwup" and setback for District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]].[1] Badlands Media's summary of the split reaction contrasted Honig's assessment with that of [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]], who read the same ruling as a slight indication that McAfee was leaning toward keeping Willis on the case, while conceding the question remained essentially a toss-up.[1] Honig placed the dismissal in what he described as a long line of embarrassing setbacks stretching back to the case's origins and its special purpose grand jury.[1]
