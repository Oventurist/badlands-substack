# Bari Weiss

Bari Weiss is an American journalist who emerged in the years before the [[october-7-attacks|October 7th attacks]] as one of the self-proclaimed free-speech champions of the American right and center, alongside [[dave-rubin|Dave Rubin]] and outlets such as the [[daily-wire|Daily Wire]] and the Babylon Bee.[1]

## "We Got Here Because of Cowardice"

In 2021 Weiss published an op-ed in Commentary Magazine titled "We Got Here Because of Cowardice. We Get Out With Courage," warning of a climate in which certain views could destroy a person's job and reputation.[1] She wrote that "persuasion—the purpose of argument—is replaced with public shaming," that moral complexity is replaced with moral certainty, facts with feelings, ideas with identity, forgiveness with punishment, debate with de-platforming, and inclusion with exclusion.[1] She closed by quoting Douglas Murray: "The problem is not that the sacrificial victim is selected. The problem is that the people who destroy his reputation are permitted to do so by the complicity, silence and slinking away of everybody else."[1]

## Criticism

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] endorses the substance of that argument — that failing to defend the rights of those you disagree with invites forceful conformity to reach you eventually — while noting he did not abandon the principle after October 7, 2023.[1] He names Weiss with [[ben-shapiro|Ben Shapiro]] and Rubin among conservative figures who once decried [[safe-spaces|safe spaces]] and [[cancel-culture|cancel culture]] but now cheer or tacitly support the silencing of Israel critics, revealing a conditional commitment to the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]].[1]
