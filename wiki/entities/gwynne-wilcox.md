# Gwynne Wilcox

Gwynne Wilcox is a member of the [[national-labor-relations-board|National Labor Relations Board]] (NLRB) whose firing by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was litigated to the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] in April 2025.[1]

## Removal and emergency stay

A district court voided Wilcox's removal and restored her to the NLRB, alongside a companion ruling reinstating [[cathy-harris|Cathy Harris]] to the [[merit-systems-protection-board|Merit Systems Protection Board]].[1] On April 9, 2025 the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] sought emergency relief from the Supreme Court, and Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] temporarily halted the lower court decisions, putting the removals back into effect for the time being and setting an April 15 deadline for responses from lawyers for Wilcox and Harris.[1] Solicitor General [[d-john-sauer|D. John Sauer]] wrote that "the president should not be forced to delegate his executive power to agency heads who are demonstrably at odds with the administration's policy objectives for a single day — much less for the months that it would likely take for the courts to resolve this litigation."[1]
