# Sean Duffy

Sean Duffy is the United States Secretary of Transportation in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration. He appears in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief as the administration official who publicly confirmed and characterized a reported cartel drone incursion into US airspace near El Paso, Texas.

## Statement on the El Paso drone incursion

Duffy posted on X that "the FAA and DOW acted swiftly to address a cartel drone incursion. The threat has been neutralized, and there is no danger to commercial travel in the region. The restrictions have been lifted and normal flights are resuming." The statement followed a surprise notice from the [[federal-aviation-administration|Federal Aviation Administration]], which falls under his department, closing airspace above El Paso and halting all flights up to 18,000 feet within a ten-mile radius for a stated period of ten days on "special security reasons" grounds. The restrictions were lifted on the Wednesday night, considerably earlier than the original notice indicated.

His framing placed the Department of War alongside the FAA as a joint actor in neutralizing the drones, and the Brief notes that the department declined to specify publicly which counter-drone technology was used. Duffy's confirmation stood in contrast to Mexican President [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]]'s statement that there was "no information about the use of drones on the border."
