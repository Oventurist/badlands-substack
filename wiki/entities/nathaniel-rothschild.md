# Nathaniel Rothschild

**Nathaniel Rothschild** is a financier and member of the Rothschild banking family cited in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as co-founder, with [[timothy-barakett|Timothy Barakett]], of the hedge fund [[atticus-capital|Atticus Capital]].[1]

The article uses that partnership to place Barakett — later chair of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] — "at the epicenter of dynastic finance," presenting the sequence as proof of intergenerational elite continuity.[1] More broadly, the piece asserts that the Rothschild and Rockefeller dynasties are "interwoven" through [[harvard-university|Harvard]]'s elite societies including the [[porcellian-club|Porcellian Club]], from which members are said to graduate into Rothschild-affiliated financial houses and think tanks such as the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
