# Saad Hariri

Saad Hariri is a Lebanese politician who served as Prime Minister of [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and is the son of former Lebanese leader Rafic Hariri.[1]

## Detention in Riyadh

In November 2017, during the [[saudi-purge|Saudi purge]] launched by Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], Hariri was among those apprehended after [[king-salman|King Salman]] summoned the Islamic world to Riyadh and effectively transferred authority to his son, who announced a corruption panel to purge the Saudi government of traitors.[1]

Hariri was reported missing for many days after appearing on Saudi television to announce his intent to resign from office, an episode that sent shockwaves across the West and culminated in the French president flying to Riyadh in a fit of panic to "rescue" him amid Western media claims that he had been kidnapped by MBS.[1] Hariri denied those claims and ultimately served out his term.[1] It is now speculated that his apparent resignation was part of a larger plot by Mohammed bin Salman, who was seeking to usurp control of Lebanon from [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] and Iran, both of which backed Hariri.[1]

## Legacy in the 2025 succession

Badlands commentary situates the Hariri affair within the chain of events culminating in the [[2025-lebanese-presidential-election|2025 election]] of [[joseph-aoun|Joseph Aoun]] as Lebanon's president with backing from Hezbollah, Iran, the United States and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] simultaneously.[1]
