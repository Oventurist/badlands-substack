# Russian Defense Ministry

The **Russian Ministry of Defense** (MoD) is the government body responsible for the administration of Russia's armed forces and the principal official source of Russian battlefield claims during the war in Ukraine.[1]

## Claims regarding the 2023 Ukrainian counteroffensive

In early June 2023, the Russian Defense Ministry announced that Ukrainian forces had launched a "large-scale offensive" across five sectors of the front in the South Donetsk direction, and stated that Russian forces had thwarted the attack.[1] According to the ministry's statement, "On the morning of June 4, the enemy launched a large-scale offensive in five sectors of the front in the South Donetsk direction," adding that "the enemy has failed to reach its goals and was unsuccessful."[1]

The ministry claimed that roughly 250 Ukrainian troops were killed, that Ukraine had committed six mechanized and two tank battalions against Russian-controlled southern Donetsk, and that the attacking force lost six tanks, three infantry vehicles, and 21 armored vehicles — losses that would mark a significant defeat if confirmed.[1] The statement characterized the Ukrainian objective as breaching Russian defenses at what Kyiv assumed was the most vulnerable section of the frontline.[1] The claims were relayed to Western audiences through outlets including RT and [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]], and formed the basis of Badlands Media commentary arguing that Western reporting on the [[2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive|2023 Ukrainian counteroffensive]] had been dramatically skewed.[1]

## Announcement of the 2024 tactical nuclear drills

The Russian Defense Ministry announced on 6 May 2024 that drills involving non-strategic nuclear weapons would be held in response to 'provocative statements and threats of certain Western officials regarding the Russian Federation.'[2] When the exercises began on 21 May, the ministry said the first stage envisioned 'practical training in the preparation and use of non-strategic nuclear weapons,' including nuclear-capable Kinzhal and Iskander missiles, conducted in the Southern Military District.[2]
