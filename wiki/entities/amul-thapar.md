# Amul Thapar

Amul Thapar is a federal appellate judge who was [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s first court of appeals appointee, named in 2017.[1] [[usha-vance|Usha Vance]] clerked for him, in addition to her clerkships with Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] and Justice [[brett-kavanaugh|Brett Kavanaugh]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cites the Thapar clerkship as one of a chain of judicial connections tying the Vance family to Trump appointments well before [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] entered electoral politics.[1] (The article renders the judge's surname as "Thaper.")[1]
