# Senate Homeland Security Committee

The Senate Homeland Security Committee is the U.S. Senate panel with oversight jurisdiction over domestic security matters, including threat briefings from federal law enforcement leadership.[1]

## Wray's 2023 terror threat testimony

In late October 2023, [[fbi|FBI]] Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] told lawmakers on the Senate Homeland Security Committee that the threat of a terror attack against Americans had been raised to "a whole other level" because of the ongoing conflict in the Middle East.[1] "The reality is that the terrorism threat has been elevated throughout 2023, but the ongoing war in the Middle East has raised the threat of an attack against Americans in the United States to a whole other level," Wray testified.[1]

The testimony followed the October 7 attack launched by [[hamas|Hamas]] against [[israel|Israel]] and the ensuing military response, which renewed concern within the United States about copycat attacks inspired by Hamas and other terrorist groups, including by domestic and homegrown terrorists.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Wray's remarks served to ratchet tension within the American [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]] and, in the eyes of much of the Anon and Truth community, carried "the shadow of a threat" given growing bipartisan distrust of U.S. intelligence agencies and federal law enforcement following their perceived weaponization against Trump.[1]
