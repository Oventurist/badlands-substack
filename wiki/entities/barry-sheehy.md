# Barry Sheehy

Barry Sheehy is the author of *Montreal: City of Secrets*, a study of Confederate operations in Montreal during the American [[american-civil-war|Civil War]].[1] Matthew Ehret draws on Sheehy for the claim that "the largest Confederate Secret Service base outside Richmond was located in Montreal," under the direct control of Confederate Secretary of State [[judah-benjamin|Judah Benjamin]].[1] Ehret also cites Sheehy's book for the assertion that [[john-wilkes-booth|John Wilkes Booth]] was in Canada for more than five weeks before the assassination of [[abraham-lincoln|Lincoln]], receiving payment, training and directions from British-sponsored Confederates.[1]
