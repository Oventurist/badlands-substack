# Angel Studios

Angel Studios is an American independent film studio and distributor, known within the Truth Community as the company that brought the anti-child-trafficking film [[sound-of-freedom|Sound of Freedom]] to theatres in 2023 after years in distribution limbo.[1]

## Sound of Freedom distribution

According to commentary by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], Sound of Freedom was originally financed by a subsidiary of [[20th-century-fox|20th Century Fox]].[1] After [[walt-disney-company|Disney]] acquired 20th Century Fox, the film was reportedly shelved indefinitely, prompting a years-long battle by Angel Studios to reacquire the distribution rights.[1] Once released, the film supplanted recent Disney-produced tentpoles at the box office, including the latest Indiana Jones instalment.[1]

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described the Angel Studios release as acting like "a perfect storm" to expose the media and child sex trafficking, noting that the picture had grossed over $2 million in early ticket sales and was being regarded as an unlikely hit of the summer.[1] He argued that the film's long delay had been serendipitous: had it appeared in 2018, before the Epstein scandal, the border crisis and the trans agenda had reached the world stage, audiences would not have been prepared to accept its premise.[1]

## Significance

Because major Hollywood studios would not touch the project, the film's success through an independent distributor was treated by Badlands writers as evidence that audience demand, rather than industry promotion, was driving public interest in the subject of child trafficking — and as a demonstration that the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] no longer controlled which stories reached mass audiences.[1]
