# Stéphane Séjourné

**Stéphane Séjourné** is the French Minister for Europe and Foreign Affairs who, together with UK Foreign Secretary [[david-cameron|David Cameron]], co-wrote an op-ed in the *Daily Telegraph* published around April 8, 2024 to mark the 120th anniversary of the [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]].[1]

The op-ed framed the twenty-first-century entente as integral to defeating Russia, declaring: "It is not for France and Britain alone to solve these challenges. But, together, we can rally others to join us in overcoming them… We must do even more to ensure we defeat Russia. The world is watching – and will judge us if we fail."[1] Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] dismisses the pair as a "clown duo" and argues that the pact they celebrate is the same architecture that produced the world wars.[1]

The anniversary was also marked by President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] and by unprecedented ceremonial exchanges, with French troops joining the changing of the guard at Buckingham Palace and British troops marching at the Élysée Palace.[1]
