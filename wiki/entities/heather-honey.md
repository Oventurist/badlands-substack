# Heather Honey

Heather Honey is an election-integrity official at the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration. The Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026 describes her as a former aide and protégé of attorney [[cleta-mitchell|Cleta Mitchell]], who advised Trump during post-2020 election legal efforts.

Honey is named as a participant in [[white-house|White House]] interagency discussions on implementing an expansion of federal involvement in voter verification and mail-in ballot rules, work that spans DHS, the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] and the [[united-states-postal-service|US Postal Service]]. Under the March 2026 executive order [[ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections|"Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections"]], DHS is directed to develop a "state citizenship list," a task that falls within her portfolio; the source reports that DHS and [[united-states-citizenship-and-immigration-services|USCIS]] officials described internal deliberations as ongoing.
