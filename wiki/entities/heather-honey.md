# Heather Honey

Heather Honey is a [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] official responsible for election integrity matters in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1] A former aide, she has been described as a protege of attorney [[cleta-mitchell|Cleta Mitchell]], who advised Trump during post-2020 election legal efforts.[1]

## Role in the federal election integrity effort

In May 2026 Honey was reported to be among the officials participating in interagency [[white-house|White House]] discussions on expanding federal involvement in voter verification and mail-in ballot rules.[1] Those talks brought together the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]], DHS and the [[united-states-postal-service|US Postal Service]], and included [[harmeet-dhillon|Harmeet Dhillon]], head of the DOJ Civil Rights Division, and USPS chief executive [[david-steiner|David Steiner]].[1]

The effort implements Trump's March executive order [[ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections|"Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections"]], which directs DHS — the agency where Honey works on election matters — to develop a "state citizenship list."[1] DHS and [[uscis|USCIS]] officials said internal deliberations on the order remained ongoing at the time of reporting.[1]
