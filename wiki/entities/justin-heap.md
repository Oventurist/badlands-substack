# Justin Heap

Justin Heap is the County Recorder of [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona, the office responsible for voter registration and significant portions of election administration in the state's largest county.[1]

## Dispute with the Board of Supervisors

As of June 2026, Heap was engaged in an ongoing power struggle with the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]] over control of key election functions, including ballot drop-box locations and chain-of-custody procedures.[1] The Atlantic and local outlets reported that officials warned the unresolved disputes could complicate preparations for upcoming elections.[1] The conflict unfolded against the backdrop of a reported [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration plan to condition [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] grants on state adoption of election-related measures.[1]
