# Mexico

Mexico is the United States' southern neighbor and, in the Badlands corpus, a central theater in the debate over cartel power, border security, and US military action in the Western Hemisphere.

## Cartel strike rhetoric

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said the United States may begin conducting land strikes against drug cartels operating in Mexico, expanding on earlier statements about using US military force against transnational criminal organizations. Trump argued that cartels running drug operations across the US–Mexico border pose a direct national security threat and claimed the cartels are running the Mexican government. Mexican officials warned against any violation of national sovereignty. No formal military orders were announced and no timeline was released.

## Political leadership

The Brief traces Mexican policy through two presidencies. Former president [[andres-manuel-lopez-obrador|Andrés Manuel López Obrador]] is described as ideologically leftist but cooperative with Trump, credited with deploying 29,000 Mexican troops to the border. His successor [[claudia-sheinbaum|Claudia Sheinbaum]] is presented as less cooperative: a bilateral trade deal remained unfinished, and in May Trump accused her of being afraid of the cartels after she rejected an offer of US troops.

## Interpretive framing

The Badlands commentary calls Mexico "one of those enigmas that is hard to fully grasp, as far as earnest analysis goes," and advances the contested claim that "Mexico is the real Deep State hub of Latin America." Unlike the corpus's treatment of the [[venezuela|Venezuela]] and [[colombia|Colombia]] situations, where the author disputes US allegations, he states that on Mexico "I actually think President Trump is speaking the truth here."

## Regional links

The [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] is described in the same edition as the principal beneficiary of the smuggling corridors secured through Central America by the Colombian [[national-liberation-army|National Liberation Army]].
