# Ulrich Thoden

Ulrich Thoden is the parliamentary leader of the German party [[die-linke|Die Linke]] (The Left).[1]

In July 2025 Thoden publicly criticized the US plan to funnel [[patriot-missile-system|Patriot]] anti-aircraft systems to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] through European [[nato|NATO]] members, saying it may create "strong profit prospects" for the American [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] but does nothing to pave the way to ending the conflict between Moscow and Kiev.[1] He argued that the United States should supply Kiev with the systems directly rather than force European NATO members to pay for them or to further deplete their own dwindling arsenals.[1]
