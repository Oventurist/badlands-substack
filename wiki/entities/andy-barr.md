# Andy Barr

Andy Barr is a Republican U.S. Representative from Kentucky who won his party's nomination for an open U.S. Senate seat during Kentucky's May 2026 primary.[1]

## 2026 Senate primary

Barr secured the GOP Senate nomination as a candidate endorsed by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], on the same primary day that Trump-backed challenger [[ed-gallrein|Ed Gallrein]] unseated [[thomas-massie|Rep. Thomas Massie]] in the state's 4th Congressional District.[1] Coverage treated the pair of results as evidence that Trump's influence in Kentucky extended beyond the House race and reflected his consolidating hold over the Republican Party.[1]
