# Omond Solandt

Omond Solandt was a Canadian scientist, [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]] member and collaborator with [[maurice-lamontagne|Maurice Lamontagne]] on Lamontagne's Senate Report on Canadian science policy.[1]

## Career

According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], Solandt made his career as science advisor to Lord Mountbatten — whom Ehret calls [[prince-philip|Prince Philip]]'s pedophiliac mentor — during the Second World War, and headed the Defence Research Board until 1957, where he collaborated on [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] alongside the infamous Ewan Cameron at McGill University.[1]

## Position on science

Solandt is quoted as saying, sophistically in Ehret's judgement: "There is no longer any need to advance science. The need is rather to understand, guide and use science effectively for the welfare of mankind."[1] Ehret comments that what "the welfare of mankind" would mean in the mind of an MK Ultra proponent should give one chills.[1] The remark is presented as the companion doctrine to Lamontagne's proposal to redirect research funding away from curiosity and discovery, and as part of the broader zero-growth paradigm advanced by the Club of Rome.[1]
