# Ashley Biden

Ashley Biden is the daughter of President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and the owner
of the personal diary at the centre of a federal theft prosecution in the
Southern District of New York.[1]

## The diary theft case

[[aimee-harris|Aimee Harris]] pleaded guilty in August 2022 to conspiring to
commit interstate transportation of stolen property in connection with the theft
of Ashley Biden's diary.[1] In April 2024 the Justice Department asked a New
York district judge to sentence Harris to four to ten months in prison, a
harsher penalty than the six months of home confinement prosecutors had
originally sought, on the grounds that Harris had sought twelve postponements
of her sentencing hearing.[1]

## Loss of Secret Service protection (March 2025)

On Monday, March 17, 2025, President Donald Trump revoked the Secret Service security details of both Ashley Biden and her brother [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], effective immediately.[2] Trump announced the decision on Truth Social, complaining that Hunter's detail comprised as many as 18 people, all paid for by the American taxpayer, while he vacationed in [[south-africa|South Africa]].[2]

Badlands commentary characterized the withdrawal of protection as an appropriate end to a taxpayer subsidy, arguing that the Biden children could pay for their own security and would likely need it if they remained in the United States.[2]
