# Kim Keck

Kim Keck is the chief executive of [[blue-cross-blue-shield|Blue Cross Blue Shield]].[1] She entered the December 2024 news cycle indirectly, after former *Washington Post* reporter [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] posted an image of Keck on both Bluesky and X alongside an article about Blue Cross Blue Shield ending coverage of anesthesia for the full length of some surgeries.[1] The posts came in the hours after the killing of UnitedHealthcare chief executive [[brian-thompson|Brian Thompson]] in Midtown Manhattan and were reported as appearing to celebrate the murder while suggesting other executives should also be targeted.[1]
