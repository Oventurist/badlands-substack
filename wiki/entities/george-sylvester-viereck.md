# George Sylvester Viereck

George Sylvester Viereck was a German-American writer and propagandist named by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] among the figures who promoted narratives of "Jewish bankers' conspiracies undermining the noble honest royal families of Europe," alongside [[nesta-webster|Nesta Webster]], [[gerard-encausse|Papus]], [[winston-churchill|Winston Churchill]], [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], and [[alice-bailey|Alice Bailey]].[1]

Ehret announces that the next installment of "Real Patriots Don't Think Like Hitler" will introduce a nest of pro-Nazi occultists in the United States operating under the influence of Viereck and Crowley — placing Viereck at the center of the American branch of the network the series examines.[1]
