# Cornelius Vanderbilt

Cornelius Vanderbilt is named in Badlands Media's [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] Chapter 14 as the ancestor of [[gloria-vanderbilt|Gloria Vanderbilt]] and therefore of CNN anchor [[anderson-cooper|Anderson Cooper]].[1]

## Allegations in the series

The article asserts that Vanderbilt helped to fund Adolf Hitler and the Nazi effort during the Second World War.[1] It also states that, according to the documentary *JFK to 9/11: Everything Is A Rich Man's Trick*, Vanderbilt and Averell Harriman were involved in the assassination of John F. Kennedy.[1] Both claims are advanced without primary documentation and rest on the cited film.[1]

The purpose of the claims within the chapter is to argue that Cooper's Vanderbilt descent is materially significant — the author writes that this ancestry makes Cooper's role in mainstream media "even more compelling" as evidence of [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]].[1]

## Biltmore

The Biltmore House in Asheville, North Carolina is described in the article as the Vanderbilt Estate, still owned by the [[vanderbilt-family|Vanderbilt family]], and its pool is the subject of the chapter's most speculative imagery-based claims.[1]
