# Corruption Investigation Office (South Korea)

South Korea's Corruption Investigation Office is the investigative body charged with pursuing criminal cases against high-ranking officials, including the president.[1] In January 2025 it became the lead agency attempting to detain suspended President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]] over his failed attempt to impose martial law the previous month.[1]

## Attempts to Arrest Yoon Suk Yeol

Investigators launched a fresh arrest operation shortly after 4am local time on January 15, 2025, seeking to detain Yoon for questioning on insurrection and abuse of office charges.[1] It was the office's second attempt that month: an initial move to arrest Yoon at his presidential compound had been foiled by his protection officers after a tense, hours-long standoff.[1] Television images broadcast during the second operation appeared to show police tussling with pro-Yoon demonstrators outside the compound, where the suspended president had been holed up for several weeks.[1]

The episode drew commentary on the limits of legal authority when confronted with an armed protective detail: Badlands writers noted the oddity of a government unable to arrest a president accused of treason because his own security force refused to permit it.[1]
