# John Podesta

John Podesta is a longtime Democratic operative and Clinton associate. In "America for Sale — Part 2" he appears in the photograph of the secret 2009 [[north-korea|North Korea]] hostage mission posted by [[qanon|Q]] as drop 227, standing behind [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] during the meeting with [[kim-jong-il|Kim Jong Il]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

His presence, alongside that of [[doug-band|Doug Band]], is offered by the author as evidence that the Pyongyang trip involved the Clintons' inner political and business circle rather than official diplomatic personnel, and therefore warrants scrutiny beyond its stated humanitarian purpose.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## The hacked email and the Pyongyang memo

"America for Sale — Part 3" reports, citing Buzzfeed, that the memo recording the friendly exchanges between Bill Clinton and Kim Jong Il was discovered attached to an email sent to Podesta, who accompanied Clinton on the mission.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) Podesta is described as a former White House chief of staff under Clinton and then head of the Center for American Progress, later serving as campaign chair for [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s presidential campaign; [[wikileaks|WikiLeaks]] published emails from his account, which researchers believed had been hacked by Russian hackers.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The article notes that Podesta had worked in the Clinton administration and was serving as an aide to [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] during the 2009 negotiations, which it says were conducted under the radar at the time.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) It treats the leaked memo as proof that the encounter was not an adversarial hostage negotiation but a warm meeting between parties with an existing relationship.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Alternate elector planning (2020)

Reporting by [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] of the [[foundation-for-freedom-online|Foundation for Freedom Online]], publicised in July 2023, asserted that the Biden campaign's contingency plans to send its own slates of alternate electors if it lost the 2020 election were written and overseen by Podesta.[3] Benz situated the plans within the broader [[transition-integrity-project|Transition Integrity Project]], which Podesta co-led with the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]].[3]

## 2016 Clinton campaign post-mortem

Podesta, as chairman of the [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] campaign, is identified in public records as one of the principals who coordinated with Clinton in an effort to link [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] during the 2016 campaign, together with general counsel [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], campaign manager [[robby-mook|Robby Mook]], communications director [[jennifer-palmieri|Jennifer Palmieri]], and policy adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]].[4] Clinton's own words in her 2016 election post-mortem are expected to be central evidence for investigators examining whether the effort rose to the level of a criminal conspiracy.[4]

## The Podesta Group and Mueller

Podesta served as the political connection behind the [[podesta-group|Podesta Group]], the lobbying firm founded by his brother [[tony-podesta|Tony Podesta]], having worked with the Clintons and [[barack-obama|Obama]]; NBC News reported that he was no longer affiliated with the firm and was not part of [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]]'s investigation, which targeted his brother.[5] [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] nonetheless treats both brothers as targets, citing Q drop 62 (November 2, 2017), which said "PODESTA's attorney was just notified," and Q drop 15, which listed "11.3 - PODESTA indicted."[5]

Q drop 67, posted the following day, asked "Where is John PODESTA? Where is Tony PODESTA? Did one or both escape the country and was let out?" and stated that "PODESTA's plane has military escort (i.e. tag) and is being diverted (forced down)."[5] The same drop linked the episode to post-9/11 aircraft groundings and the single plane authorized to depart, which Lange presents as a thread connecting the Podestas, [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] and the [[september-11-attacks|September 11 attacks]] to be developed in a later article.[5] He characterizes the brothers as puppets who will lead to a much bigger target.[5] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
