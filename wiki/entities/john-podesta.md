# John Podesta

John Podesta is a longtime Democratic operative who served as White House chief of staff under [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] and as chairman of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s 2016 presidential campaign.

## In the Badlands corpus

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]] quotes Podesta's *Vanity Fair* praise of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]: "Huma is a terrific leader. She's multifaceted, has a great strategic sense, and she's a wonderful colleague. She's an integral part of the team, and her competence is only exceeded by her humility." The author seizes on the phrase, asking pointedly, "As Podesta said, Huma was 'an integral part of the team.' But what team was he talking about?" — framing the endorsement as an unwitting confirmation of the article's thesis that Abedin belonged to a network distinct from the Clinton campaign proper.

Podesta also appears in the article's closing lines, which cite the Q markers "11.3 - PODESTA indicted" and "11.6 - HUMA indicted" as anticipated developments to be explored in Part 2.
