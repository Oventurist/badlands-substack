# Cornell University

**Cornell University** is an [[ivy-league|Ivy League]] institution described in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the biotechnical frontier of the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]] control network.[1]

## Alleged role

The article states that Cornell channels its resources toward ag-tech and bioengineering agendas aligned with the [[gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]] and [[monsanto|Monsanto]], calling it "the biotech spearhead."[1] It is said to deploy the future of food systems, CRISPR experiments and synthetic agriculture, with Monsanto links described as remaining strong.[1]

## Quill and Dagger pipeline

Cornell's alleged pipeline runs through the [[quill-and-dagger|Quill and Dagger]] society, which the article says feeds biotech innovation and "agritech surveillance systems," with Gates-funded research and [[darpa|DARPA]]-adjacent trials emerging from the campus.[1] Extending the chain, the article links Quill and Dagger to Gates Foundation partnerships and, by extension, to [[world-health-organization|World Health Organization]] and [[united-nations|United Nations]] bio-surveillance systems — connections it says became particularly visible during the COVID-19 era, when academic research, public health policy and digital identity initiatives allegedly stemmed from the same axis.[1]
