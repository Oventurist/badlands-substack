# Julian's Rum

Julian's Rum is an anonymous researcher and social-media figure described by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] as a "friend of Badlands" and as "one of the best Anons still in the game."[1]

In August 2024, Julian's Rum resurfaced a [[qanon|Q]] drop from 2020 concerning a last-minute change to the Democratic convention — a change ostensibly made under the guise of COVID-19 measures at the time — arguing that the drop had returned to relevance amid the revived monkeypox narrative and the [[2024-mpox-global-health-emergency|2024 mpox global health emergency]].[1] He was himself responding to a viral Wall Street Silver post pointing to what it characterized as an obvious propaganda push around monkeypox in the preceding weeks, at a moment when some officials were calling for the [[2024-democratic-national-convention|2024 Democratic National Convention]] to be delayed or cancelled on public-health grounds.[1]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] praised the find as "a fantastic example of dot-connecting," while saying he personally would not expect the DNC to actually be delayed or cancelled, and read the narrative push as evidence of "a true panic pattern developing for the [[deep-state|Deep State]]" ahead of what should have been its coronation of the next candidate.[1]
