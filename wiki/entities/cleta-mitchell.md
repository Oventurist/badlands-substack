# Cleta Mitchell

Cleta Mitchell is an American attorney long associated with conservative election-law work. The [[badlands-brief-323|Badlands Brief of May 15, 2026]] identifies her as having advised [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the legal efforts that followed the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]], and as the mentor of [[heather-honey|Heather Honey]], the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] election-integrity official described as her former aide and protégé.

Her indirect presence in the 2026 interagency election-integrity initiative — through Honey's role in implementing the executive order [[ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections|"Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections"]] — is treated in the source as evidence of continuity between the post-2020 legal effort and the current administrative push involving the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]], DHS and the [[united-states-postal-service|Postal Service]].
