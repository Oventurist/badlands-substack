# Cleta Mitchell

Cleta Mitchell is an American attorney known for her work on election law and election integrity litigation.[1] She advised President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] during the legal efforts that followed the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## Influence

Mitchell's influence on the second Trump administration's election policy has been described through her professional network. [[heather-honey|Heather Honey]], the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] official handling election integrity and a participant in the 2026 interagency talks on federal voter verification and mail-in ballot rules, has been characterized as a protege of Mitchell.[1]
