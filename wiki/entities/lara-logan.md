# Lara Logan

Lara Logan is an independent journalist whose reporting has circulated widely in alternative and "Truth Community" media, frequently drawing on unnamed intelligence sources and offering interpretations of major events that diverge sharply from mainstream coverage.[1]

## Reporting on the Key Bridge collapse

Following the March 26, 2024 destruction of Baltimore's Francis Scott Key Bridge by the container ship that struck one of its support columns, Logan published a statement on X asserting that her sources rejected the "accident" framing entirely.[1] According to Logan, "multiple intel sources" characterized the collapse as an "absolutely brilliant strategic attack" on United States critical infrastructure, "most likely cyber," and stated that American intelligence agencies were aware of this.[1] She framed the incident in information-warfare terms, arguing that the perpetrators had "divided the US along the Mason-Dixon line, exactly like the Civil War."[1]

Logan's statement emphasized the logistical significance of the span, describing it as carrying the "second busiest strategic roadway in the nation for hazardous material," a route she said would be down for four to five years.[1] She noted that the bridge had been built specifically to move hazardous cargo — fuel, diesel, propane gas, nitrogen, highly flammable materials, chemicals, and oversized loads that cannot pass through the region's tunnels — and concluded that this supply chain was "now crippled."[1] She closed by insisting the event was "an extraordinary attack in terms of planning, timing & execution."[1]

## Reception in alternative media

Logan's account was amplified by outlets including [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], which contrasted her reporting with mainstream accounts of the collapse.[1] Badlands Media writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] treated Logan's "insider" material as offering "some interesting clarity surrounding the nature of this attack," reading the described markers as consistent with [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth generation warfare]] and cautioning that such claims should be "watched carefully and held lightly."[1] Esler placed the episode within a broader framework in which attacks on centralized infrastructure serve a "Primary Strategic Objective" of collapsing nation states and installing a [[new-world-order|New World Order]].[1]
