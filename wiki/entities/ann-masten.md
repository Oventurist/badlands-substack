# Ann Masten

**Dr. Ann Masten** is a resilience researcher cited in a February 11, 2025 Badlands Media essay on [[generational-dependence-syndrome|Generational Dependence Syndrome]].[1]

The essay draws on her concept of "ordinary magic" — the inherent strength within systems and communities to adapt and thrive under pressure — in arguing that [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]] builds resilience through decentralization.[1] Independent media platforms, grassroots movements, and decentralized truth networks are described as forming an adaptive, interconnected web of resistance that bypasses traditional censorship mechanisms and cannot easily be silenced.[1]
