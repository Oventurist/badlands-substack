# Juan García Abrego

Juan García Abrego led the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] and is described as the innovator who turned Mexican trafficking operations from smuggling into supply.[1] By renegotiating deals with the [[cali-cartel|Cali Cartel]], he secured 50 percent of every shipment out of Colombia as payment for delivery, replacing the roughly US$1,500 per kilo previously received; in exchange the cartel had to guarantee any shipment from Colombia to its destination.[1]

His organization was the one connected to the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]], whose member [[raul-salinas|Raúl Salinas]] arranged protected passage for its drugs and whose associates funneled cartel money to [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]].[1]
