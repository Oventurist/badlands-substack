# Juan García Abrego

Juan García Abrego was the leader of Mexico's [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] and, in the Badlands Media account, the man who changed the economics of Mexican narcotics trafficking.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

He is widely known for innovating Mexican trafficking operations, turning them from smugglers into suppliers. By renegotiating deals with the [[cali-cartel|Cali Cartel]], García Abrego secured 50 percent of every shipment out of Colombia as payment for delivery, instead of the US $1,500 per kilo the organization had previously received. The renegotiation carried a price: the cartel had to guarantee any shipment from Colombia to its destination.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

That guarantee obligation is the article's explanation for the strategic value of [[nafta|NAFTA]] to the cartel and to the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]] with which it was connected — an open southern border made delivery far easier to assure.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
