# Gregg Hartley

Gregg Hartley is a Washington, D.C. lobbyist who for more than a decade served as Vice Chairman and Chief Operating Officer of the lobbying firm Cassidy & Associates — the same firm where [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles "Chuck" Dolan Jr.]] was formerly employed.[1] He later worked with HB Strategies.[1]

Hartley surfaced in the third day of the trial of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] when Assistant Special Counsel [[michael-keilty|Michael Keilty]] introduced Exhibit 703, an April 29, 2016 email from Danchenko to Dolan on which Hartley was copied.[1] Attached to that email was a document prepared by Danchenko for [[orbis-business-intelligence|Orbis Business Intelligence]], which Dolan described as an "intelligence briefing note on Kompromat Nadzor in the Russian banking sector."[1] Dolan testified that he read the email and the attachment but was not entirely sure what it was about.[1]

Coverage of the trial treated the Hartley connection as illustrative of the overlapping Washington lobbying and public-relations networks that linked Dolan, Danchenko and Orbis, characterizing the milieu as the "Swamp."[1]
