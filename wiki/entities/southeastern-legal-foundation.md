# Southeastern Legal Foundation

The Southeastern Legal Foundation (SLF) is a public-interest legal organization that pursues government-transparency litigation.[1]

In June 2022 the SLF filed a Freedom of Information Act request with the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]] seeking any documents referencing three pseudonymous email accounts allegedly used by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] while he was vice president: robinware456@gmail.com, JRBWare@gmail.com, and Robert.L.Peters@pci.gov.[1] In a responsive letter, NARA said it had identified approximately 5,138 email messages, 25 electronic files, and 200 pages of potentially responsive records — some 5,400 records in total — that would have to be processed to answer the request.[1]
