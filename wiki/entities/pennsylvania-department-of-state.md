# Pennsylvania Department of State

The Pennsylvania Department of State is the Commonwealth agency that administers elections in Pennsylvania and is headed by the Secretary of the Commonwealth.[1]

## 2024 Senate recount

In November 2024 the Department issued the press release announcing that the margin in the U.S. Senate race between [[dave-mccormick|Dave McCormick]] and [[bob-casey|Bob Casey]] had triggered a legally required statewide recount, a determination made public by Secretary of the Commonwealth Al Schmidt on November 13, 2024.[1] The Department's release set the operative deadlines: the recount was to commence no later than November 20, 2024, counties were to complete it by noon on November 26, 2024, and to report results to the Secretary by noon the following day.[1] The Department also published the unofficial returns of 3,380,310 votes (48.93 percent) for McCormick and 3,350,972 votes (48.50 percent) for Casey.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the Department's recount as an opportunity to gather data on election administration rather than as a likely change to the result, arguing that recounts historically "raise more questions than they answer" while still contributing to the broader effort to restore election integrity ahead of the 2026 midterms.[1]
