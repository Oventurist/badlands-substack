# Executive Intelligence Review

The Executive Intelligence Review (EIR) is a weekly newsmagazine founded in 1974 by the American political figure [[lyndon-larouche|Lyndon LaRouche]].[1] Its editors were the collective authors of the 1978 book [[dope-inc-book|*Dope, Inc.: Britain's Opium War Against the World*]], which LaRouche commissioned.[1]

## Editorial orientation

EIR's output reflected LaRouche's long-running critique of the British Empire, the Royal Family, and Anglo-American financial elites, arguing that international narcotics trafficking functioned as an instrument of imperial policy and that the proceeds were laundered through the world banking system.[1] Through *Dope, Inc.*, EIR advanced the argument that illegal alcohol and illegal narcotics were "two different product lines of the same multinational firm," supplied and financed by British commercial and banking interests.[1]
