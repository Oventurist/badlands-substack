# Jordan Peterson

Jordan Peterson is a Canadian clinical psychologist, author and public commentator best known internationally for his critiques of ideological conformity and institutional overreach.[1]

## World Economic Forum alternative

In late January 2023, Peterson announced that he had assembled an international consortium intended to serve as an alternative to the "apocalyptic narrative" he associated with the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] and its annual Davos gathering.[1] Speaking to podcaster [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]] in an interview aired that weekend, Peterson said roughly 2,000 people would be invited to a consortium meeting in London running from late October into early November, presenting an "alternative to that kind of apocalyptic narrative that's being put forward at least implicitly by organizations like the WEF."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] welcomed the initiative as an overdue attempt to contest ground that populist and constitutionalist movements had ceded to globalist institutions, arguing that a constitutional-style alliance of free nations would eventually be needed as a counterweight to what he termed the New World Order.[1] Deschamps acknowledged that many readers find any global-alliance proposal unpalatable precisely because globalism has been dominated by those actors, but grounded his argument in the premise of inalienable rights and what he described as the brotherhood of man under the fatherhood of God.[1]
