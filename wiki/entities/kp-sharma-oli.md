# K.P. Sharma Oli

K.P. Sharma Oli was the Prime Minister of [[nepal|Nepal]] who resigned in September 2025 during the country's most severe civil unrest in years.[1]

## Resignation

Oli's government banned social media platforms including WhatsApp, a measure that inflamed Nepalese citizens already angry over official corruption and helped trigger the [[2025-nepal-protests|2025 Nepal protests]].[1] After security forces killed at least nineteen demonstrators and protesters set fire to Parliament, the Supreme Court, and lawmakers' homes, Oli resigned along with other top officials and the government retreated from the social media ban.[1] The violence nonetheless continued, and following his departure it was unclear who, if anyone, was in charge of the country until the army moved to restore order.[1]
