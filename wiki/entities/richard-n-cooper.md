# Richard N. Cooper

Richard N. Cooper was a Yale University economist who headed the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]]'s task force on monetary policy.[1]

Badlands Media cites Cooper as a leading illustration of the pattern by which Trilateralists moved into government offices with authority over the very policies they had drafted for the Commission.[1] In his task force role he recommended selling off official gold reserves to private markets; he subsequently became undersecretary of state for economic affairs and "just happened to be presiding" as the [[imf|International Monetary Fund]] sold off a portion of its gold.[1] Cooper later served as Chair of the National Intelligence Council under President Bill Clinton.[1] He is also listed among the Trilateral Commission members who staffed [[jimmy-carter|Jimmy Carter]]'s administration, serving as undersecretary of state for economic affairs.[1]
