# Cargill

Cargill is a major American agribusiness and one of the "Big Four" US beef packers.[1]

## 2026 antitrust investigation

Cargill was named with [[jbs|JBS]], [[tyson-foods|Tyson Foods]], and [[national-beef|National Beef]] in the intensified [[beef-packer-antitrust-investigation-2026|antitrust investigation]] announced in May 2026 by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] and [[department-of-agriculture|Department of Agriculture]], which cited price manipulation, foreign ownership risks, and supply chain issues.[1] Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] said the review covered more than three million documents and hundreds of rancher interviews.[1]
