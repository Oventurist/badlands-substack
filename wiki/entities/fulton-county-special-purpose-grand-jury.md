# Fulton County Special Purpose Grand Jury

The Fulton County special purpose grand jury was a Georgia investigative body convened to examine [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s conduct surrounding the 2020 presidential election in the state.[1] Portions of its report were publicly released in February 2023.[1]

## Partial release of findings

The released excerpts contained comparatively little substance, the most notable element being the grand jurors' opinion that some witnesses may have committed perjury during their testimony.[1] Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] asked pointedly which witnesses were being referred to.[1]

In a press release and posts on Truth Social, Trump reiterated that he had committed no wrongdoing in his Georgia phone calls and praised the special grand jury for its work, with Badlands characterizing the outcome as Trump being "totally exonerated. Again."[1]

## Scope and election fraud evidence

Just Human emphasized a methodological point about the panel's conclusion on fraud: the grand jury heard about election fraud but was not presented with evidence of it.[1] Because the body was not empaneled for the purpose of determining whether election fraud occurred, its statement that it saw no evidence of widespread voter fraud reflected the absence of any such presentation rather than an affirmative finding — in Badlands' framing, "they literally didn't see it because it was never presented to them."[1] The episode fed the wider Badlands treatment of [[2020-election-fraud-claims|2020 election fraud claims]].[1]
