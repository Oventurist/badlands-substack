# Woodrow Wilson

**Woodrow Wilson** was the President of the United States who signed into law the two 1913 measures that Badlands Media identifies as the foundation of modern American financial servitude.[1]

## The Revenue Act of 1913

Following ratification of the [[sixteenth-amendment|Sixteenth Amendment]], Democratic leaders agreed to seek passage of the [[revenue-act-of-1913|Revenue Act of 1913]], which dramatically lowered tariffs and implemented an income tax. Representative [[oscar-underwood|Oscar Underwood]] moved the bill through the [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]] and it won approval in the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]] after extensive lobbying by the Wilson administration. Wilson signed the Act into law on October 3, 1913, allowing for the establishment of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]].[1]

## The Federal Reserve Act

Wilson signed the [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act]] on December 23, 1913, creating the [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve System]].[1] The article concludes that with these moves Wilson "successfully enslaved every American to the fiat monetary system," obliging every citizen to pay income tax while watching the continued devaluation of their money.[1]
