# Jefferson Morley

**Jefferson Morley** is a journalist and researcher widely regarded as an expert on the assassination of President [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]], and serves as vice president of the [[mary-ferrell-foundation|Mary Ferrell Foundation]], described as the nation's largest source of online records concerning Kennedy's killing.[1] He has sued the U.S. government seeking the release of additional assassination records.[1]

## Reaction to the 2025 FBI records discovery

In February 2025, after the [[fbi|FBI]] disclosed that it had located roughly 2,400 previously unreviewed records tied to the Kennedy assassination — contained within some 14,000 pages found during a review triggered by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 23 executive order — Morley called the development significant.[1] "This is huge. It shows the FBI is taking this seriously," he said.[1]
