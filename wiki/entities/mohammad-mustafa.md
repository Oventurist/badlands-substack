# Mohammad Mustafa

Mohammad Mustafa is the Palestinian economist and official appointed prime minister of the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] by President [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] in early 2024, heading the revised Cabinet lineup that Washington welcomed as a step toward Palestinian political reform.[1]

## Appointment and factional fallout

Mustafa's appointment drew the ire of [[hamas]], which complained that it had not been consulted by the Palestinian Authority on the choice.[1] Abbas replied that the PA had likewise not been consulted by Hamas before it decided to conduct what he termed "the October 7th Adventure," an exchange that [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary read as a significant fracturing in the public optics of the Palestinian government and a clear break between [[fatah]] and Hamas, the rival factions contesting Palestinian governance.[1]

The [[state-department|US State Department]] signalled acceptance of the new Cabinet, with spokesperson [[matthew-miller|Matthew Miller]] saying the United States looked forward to working with the new ministers "to deliver on credible reforms" and describing a revitalized PA as essential to conditions for regional stability.[1] Commentary in the brief argued that Washington's embrace of the Mustafa cabinet left Hamas "on the outside looking in."[1]
