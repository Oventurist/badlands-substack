# Rob Bonta

Rob Bonta is the Attorney General of California and a Democrat.[1]

## NFL workplace discrimination investigation

On May 4, 2023, Bonta and New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] jointly announced an investigation into allegations of workplace discrimination at the [[national-football-league|National Football League]], citing lawsuits filed by employees describing sex, racial and age bias, sexual harassment and a hostile work environment.[1] The two attorneys general said they had issued subpoenas to NFL executives as part of an examination of workplace culture at the league's corporate offices in both states.[1]

Both officials said they were exercising their legal authority to seek information from the league regarding gender pay disparities, harassment, and gender and racial discrimination.[1] The inquiry was directed at the league's corporate offices rather than at specific teams or players.[1]

## Response to the 2025 Apple tariff threat

Bonta has emerged as a leader of California's resistance to the second Trump administration, launching or joining numerous lawsuits against White House policies, including the first state challenge to the president's trade measures.[2] On May 23, 2025, following a roundtable with local business leaders on tariff harms, he said at a San Francisco press conference that he was prepared to sue to defend state companies such as [[apple|Apple]] after [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] threatened tariffs on the iPhone maker unless it moved production fully to the United States.[2] "We definitely will assess our potential role," Bonta said, adding that his office would monitor the president's actions and take appropriate action if the law was broken and the state had standing.[2]
