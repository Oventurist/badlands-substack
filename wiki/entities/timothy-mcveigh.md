# Timothy McVeigh

Timothy McVeigh was the prime suspect in the April 19, 1995 [[oklahoma-city-bombing|Oklahoma City bombing]], apprehended in less than ninety minutes after a Ryder rental truck detonated outside the [[alfred-p-murrah-federal-building|Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building]] at 9:02 a.m.[1]

## Arrest and case

McVeigh was stopped while driving without a license plate and with illegal weapons in the car.[1] Despite the chaos of the event, authorities used forensic evidence within a matter of days to link McVeigh and his prime accomplice [[terry-nichols|Terry Nichols]] to the explosion.[1]

## Contested narrative

The Badlands Media essay "False Flags and the Occult" argues that both the media and the authorities ignored eyewitness accounts of more than one bomb found in the debris, and notes that — as with the September 11th attacks six years later — the debris from the Murrah building was carted off and destroyed before a thorough examination could be completed, allowing an official narrative running counter to on-the-ground reports to become the account of record.[1] The article states that many researchers regard the event as a government-sponsored PSYOP, a [[false-flag-operation|false flag]] falling within the forty-day [[season-of-sacrifice|season of sacrifice]].[1]
