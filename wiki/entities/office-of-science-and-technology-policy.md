# Office of Science and Technology Policy

The Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP) is the [[white-house|White House]] office responsible for advising the president on scientific and technological matters and coordinating federal research priorities across agencies.[1]

## Quantum-enabled intelligence initiative (2026)

In June 2026, OSTP announced a new national effort aimed at developing a quantum computer capable of ushering in what it described as the "era of quantum-enabled intelligence."[1] The initiative was presented as a means of accelerating United States leadership in [[quantum-computing|quantum computing]] and related technologies, which the administration identified as strategic priorities alongside artificial intelligence and biotechnology.[1]

The announcement was made under OSTP Director [[michael-kratsios|Michael Kratsios]] and framed within the administration's "Golden Age of Innovation" messaging, with officials describing quantum technologies as central to economic competitiveness and national security.[1] The rollout coincided with a quantum executive order signing at which President [[donald-trump|Trump]] invoked his uncle, the MIT engineer [[john-g-trump|John G. Trump]].[1]
