# John J. Durham

John J. Durham is a United States Department of Justice prosecutor who serves as director of [[joint-task-force-vulcan|Joint Task Force Vulcan]], the federal task force created in August 2019 to prosecute leadership of the transnational gang [[ms-13|MS-13]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[just-human|Just Human]] noted that Durham is a son of Special Counsel [[john-durham|John H. Durham]], drawing a family connection between the MS-13 task force and the special counsel investigation that occupied much of the Truth Community's attention in the same period.[1]

Under his direction, Joint Task Force Vulcan was associated with the unsealing of a four-count indictment in Central Islip charging thirteen of MS-13's highest-ranking leaders with racketeering, material support to terrorists, and narco-terrorism conspiracies.[1]
