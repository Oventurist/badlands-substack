# Michael Flynn

**Michael Flynn** is a retired United States Army lieutenant general, referred to in Badlands Media coverage simply as General Flynn.[1]

## Warnings on central bank digital currencies

In "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?", Flynn is cited as being concerned about and highlighting data on [[central-bank-digital-currency|central bank digital currencies]], and as having gone so far as to refer to CBDCs as the [[mark-of-the-beast|Mark of the Beast]].[1] The article invokes his position while arguing that actions are underway to implement a system of total control and enslavement through CBDCs paired with mandatory global digital identification.[1]

## Role in the August 2024 "Biden returns" narrative

In August 2024, Badlands commentary identified Flynn as one of the principal figures seeding the narrative that President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] might attempt to reclaim the Democratic nomination he had ceded to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[2] Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the fact the narrative was being advanced by both Flynn and [[dan-scavino|Dan Scavino]] — alongside [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s own Truth Social post on the subject — was "not insignificant."[2]

On this reading, if Biden actually did turn against his former allies it would constitute perhaps the most definitive available evidence for [[devolution|Devolution]] short of explicit disclosure by Trump.[2] Even if that never occurred, the coordinated narrative deployment by Flynn, Trump, and Scavino was said to be enough to rattle the Deep State and force hesitation that might disrupt its plans around the election.[2]

## Unmasking and the Patel nomination (December 2024)

In coverage of the December 2024 fight over [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]]'s nomination to lead the [[fbi|FBI]], Breitbart recounted that Patel had helped expose in 2017 the fact that members of the outgoing Obama administration abused their power to "unmask" the names of Americans caught on foreign wiretaps — a practice thought to have led to Flynn's unjust firing and prosecution over false allegations of collusion with Russia.[3] The disclosure made Patel a target of [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]], who in December 2024 demanded that the Senate reject his nomination.[3]


## AI as a strategic threat (2024)

At the Patriotic Podcasters conference in Tulsa in March 2024, one of Flynn's major messages revolved around [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] technology — its dangers, but also the importance of leveraging it.[4] Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] noted that the government-commissioned [[gladstone-ai|Gladstone AI]] report warning of an "extinction-level" AI threat appeared on the heels of Flynn publishing an article describing AI as a nuclear-level threat and foregrounding the topic at the conference.[4]

Flynn argued that the Patriot movement can and should leverage AI to make a greater impact, while emphasizing something Esler said the report omitted: that the rapid, head-spinning changes in society are the result of real-time, AI-based psychological warfare, and that foreign enemies are successfully running such operations now.[4] Esler suggested [[tiktok|TikTok]]'s use to modify youth behaviour toward gender ideology and radical leftism was a likely example, and considered it unlikely Flynn would make such statements without intelligence on the matter.[4]

## Appointment to the West Point Board of Visitors (March 2025)

On Monday, March 17, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] named Flynn to the Board of Visitors of the U.S. Military Academy at [[west-point|West Point]].[5] Trump announced the appointment on Truth Social, at the same time naming Maureen Bannon, daughter of his former adviser [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]], to the board.[5] Although the roles are largely ceremonial, board membership allows participants to discuss curriculum, discipline, and academic methods at the academy.[5]

Wire coverage described Flynn as a retired three-star Army general who challenged the 2020 election results, twice pleaded guilty to lying to the [[fbi|FBI]] before recanting and receiving a pardon from Trump, and who has remained a loyal Trump supporter and high-profile speaker on right-wing podcasts and at conservative events.[5] It also recounted that Flynn served as Trump's first national security adviser but was fired after 24 days for misleading Vice President Mike Pence amid controversy over his contacts with then-Russian ambassador Sergei Kislyak.[5] Badlands commentary rejected the framing that Trump was merely "keeping General Flynn close," asserting that Flynn is a patriot and trusted ally and that claims to the contrary come from bad actors.[5]

## Call to investigate Biden's Ukraine trips (February 2023)

In an interview with Jim and Joe Hoft of [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] broadcast on Wednesday, February 1, 2023, Flynn discussed the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]] and asserted that [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] had visited Ukraine "like 12 or 13 times" during his final year as vice president under [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]].[6] Flynn called the figure "an incredible amount of times," noting there are only twelve months in a year, and said "every single one of those trips needs to be investigated" — including how many involved [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] or Joe Biden's brother — arguing such questions should be asked in public rather than "in back rooms."[6]

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] amplified the remarks, asking whether Biden had been "setting up and facilitating his families dirty dealings" and disputing that arming Ukraine served American national security.[6]

## Fifth-generation warfare manual

In commentary published in January 2023, [[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited Flynn's then-new [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] manual as succinctly laying out the adversary playbook: curate the various subgroups of the general population, pit neighbour against neighbour, and then label the rhetoric of any group that threatens the social order as "hate speech."[7]

## 2024 election warning (2023)

In a May 2023 exclusive with The Western Journal, Flynn argued that the Marxist left had so corrupted America and its election system that no Republican candidate could win in 2024, and that the following two years would be far worse than the preceding two.[8] He described the election system in practically every state as broken and as fostering little trust among voters, and characterized machines, mail-in ballots, ballot harvesting and other voting irregularities as symptoms of a deeper problem: the entrenched corruption of the federal political class filtering down to every county.[8] He contended that this class had practically shredded the Constitution and destroyed what freedom remained.[8] Flynn framed the state governors, rather than the presidential contest, as the level at which the situation could still be changed.[8] Commentary by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] brief endorsed the warning, urging a stoic, matter-of-fact alarmism about election integrity grounded in inarguable facts rather than an assumption that outcomes were guaranteed.[8]


## Statement on Tren de Aragua (2025)

Amid the April 2025 federal indictments of [[tren-de-aragua|Tren de Aragua]] associates, Badlands quoted Flynn's assessment that "Tren de Aragua is a Foreign Terrorist organization directly backed by elements inside of the Venezuelan (VE) government."[9]

## Predictions and commentary (November 2025)

Two weeks before the [[2025-washington-dc-national-guard-shooting|November 2025 shooting of two National Guard members in Washington, D.C.]], Flynn predicted on [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]]'s show that elements of the [[cia|CIA]] would activate Islamic sleeper cells inside the United States in the coming months.[10] After the attack — whose suspect, [[rahmanullah-lakanwal|Rahmanullah Lakanwal]], had worked with the CIA in Kandahar — Paul cited the forecast as having been borne out, describing the disclosures as "spooks admitting their psyops."[10]

Flynn also weighed in on European resistance to the Trump administration's Ukraine peace framework, writing: "I wish these SOBs would fight for peace as much as they desire war. Enough is enough with these warmongers in Europe and here in the United States."[10]

## Comments on European burden-sharing (March 2025)

In an early March 2025 appearance on Real America's Voice with Eric Bolling, Flynn said the world was in "a dangerous moment in history" and argued that Europeans had taken advantage of the United States for far too long, declaring, "They've been ripping us off!"[11] The segment framed the remarks as an explanation of how President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] is ending what was described as the gravy train, and Badlands contributor Paul Fleuret characterized Flynn as laying down the gauntlet by telling European allies that the free ride courtesy of the USA was no longer open for business.[11]

## Invocation of the Higgins memo (November 2025)

Responding on X to the [[seditious-six-video|"Seditious Six" video]] in November 2025, Flynn resurfaced the May 2017 [[higgins-memo|Higgins memo]], noting that its author Rich Higgins had warned that the administration was "suffering under withering information campaigns designed to first undermine, then delegitimize and ultimately remove" the president, and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — who read the memo at the time — "needs to read it again."[12] Flynn's conclusion was that "very little has changed and this is still not 'politics as usual,'" describing the environment as "a form of 'political warfare'" and asserting that the country is "clearly in the midst of a color revolution."[12]
