# Fusion GPS

Fusion GPS is the Washington opposition-research firm at the center of the [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]]/[[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] investigation, whose contacts with the press, the [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] campaign and the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] have been a persistent target of transparency litigation.[1]

## Judicial Watch FOIA suit (January 2026)

In January 2026, [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] filed a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit against the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] seeking fully unredacted records from former Special Counsel [[john-durham|John Durham]]'s probe into the origins of the Russiagate investigation.[1] Among the categories of records sought were emails between Fusion GPS and major media outlets, the Clinton campaign and the DNC, alongside communications involving [[british-intelligence|British Intelligence]] head Sir [[richard-dearlove|Richard Dearlove]], U.S. intelligence officials, [[stefan-halper|Stefan Halper]] and [[christopher-steele|Christopher Steele]].[1] The firm's role connects the dossier author Steele to the political clients and press contacts that circulated the allegations.[1]
