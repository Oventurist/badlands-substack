# Congressional Research Service

The Congressional Research Service (CRS) is the nonpartisan research arm of Congress.[1]

## Injunction analysis cited by Badlands

A CRS product cited by contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] reported that federal judges issued 86 nationwide injunctions against the first Trump administration compared with 28 against the Biden administration over a comparable period — a difference Badlands rendered as 207% more injunctions against Trump.[1] The same CRS analysis found that challengers increasingly seek, and courts increasingly grant, "rule vacatur" under [[administrative-procedure-act|APA §706]], a trend Badlands described as filling the gap left when the Supreme Court curtailed nationwide injunctions.[1]

### Assessment of hypersonic threat detection
In a report cited in February 2025 coverage of President Donald Trump's proposed American "Iron Dome" missile shield, the Congressional Research Service found that "most terrestrial-based radars cannot detect hypersonic weapons until late in the weapon's flight due to line-of-sight limitations of radar detection."[2] The finding was invoked by experts arguing that the homeland missile-defense plan defied physics and practicality, even though fear of adversary hypersonic missiles was the very driver of the push for the dome.[2]
