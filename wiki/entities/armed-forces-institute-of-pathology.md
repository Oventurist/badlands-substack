# Armed Forces Institute of Pathology

The Armed Forces Institute of Pathology (AFIP) was the U.S. military pathology organization that processed the body of Commerce Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] after the April 3, 1996 plane crash near Dubrovnik, Croatia.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Its handling of the case is central to the Badlands claim that Brown was shot rather than killed by the crash.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

As Brown's body was being processed, a perfectly round .45-inch inwardly beveling hole was discovered in the top of his head.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) Lt. Col. Steve Cogswell, an AFIP deputy medical examiner, questioned the official finding of death by multiple blunt-force trauma on the basis of x-rays and photographs, telling reporter Christopher Ruddy of the *Pittsburgh Tribune-Review* in a story published November 24 that the original x-rays showed possible metal fragments in the brain consistent with a high-velocity gunshot wound.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) The two head x-rays were missing from Brown's file, and Cogswell suspected they had never been placed there.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)

On December 5, 1997, AFIP imposed a gag order on Cogswell, requiring him to refer all press inquiries about the Brown case to the institute's public affairs office.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) On December 9, 1997, Lt. Col. David Hause — one of AFIP's leading experts on gunshot wounds and present in the examination room when Chief Petty Officer Kathleen Janoski exclaimed, "Wow, look at the hole in Ron Brown's head" — corroborated the account, verifying that the wound penetrated the skull and exposing brain matter and describing it as looking "like a punched-out .45-caliber entrance hole."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1) After Hause spoke to Ruddy, the gag order was broadened to include all AFIP personnel, which the article calls the completion of the cover-up.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-1)
