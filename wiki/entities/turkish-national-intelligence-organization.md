# Turkish National Intelligence Organization (MIT)

The Turkish National Intelligence Organization, known as MIT, is Türkiye's principal intelligence service. In 2024 it conducted a sustained counterintelligence campaign against alleged [[mossad|Mossad]] networks operating inside the country.[1]

On 3 September 2024, Turkish security officials announced the arrest of [[liridon-rexhepi|Liridon Rexhepi]], a Kosovan national whom MIT identified as the head of Mossad's financial network in Türkiye.[1] MIT reportedly discovered Rexhepi after detecting irregularities in his financial accounts, showing "countless" transfers to Mossad field agents via Western Union, and placed him under surveillance upon his entry into the country on 25 August before detaining him in Istanbul on 30 August in a joint operation with the Istanbul police.[1]

Since the start of 2024, Turkish authorities had detained and charged dozens of suspected Mossad agents, most of them Turkish nationals: seven suspects were detained in January, six people charged in March, and two others arrested in April.[1]
