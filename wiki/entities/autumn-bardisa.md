# Autumn Bardisa

Autumn Bardisa is a Florida woman who, at age 29, was arrested in August 2025 for treating thousands of hospital patients while falsely claiming to be a registered nurse.[1]

## Impersonation and arrest

According to the [[flagler-county-sheriffs-office|Flagler County Sheriff's Office]], Bardisa treated 4,486 individuals at [[adventhealth|AdventHealth]] Palm Coast Parkway beginning in July 2023, working roughly two years without a medical license.[1] She was arrested on Tuesday, August 5, 2025, after the scheme was discovered — not through any complaint about her clinical work, but through paperwork generated when she was put up for a promotion.[1]

Sheriff [[rick-staly|Rick Staly]] called it "one of the most disturbing cases of medical fraud we've ever investigated," adding that "this woman potentially put thousands of lives at risk by pretending to be someone she was not."[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the hospital came out of the story looking worse than the impostor, noting that staff noticed the fraud only from administrative records while Bardisa's actual work product had earned her a promotion.[1] Ashe framed the case as a rebuke of the "trust the experts" class, observing that Bardisa appeared not to need real credentials so much as the story that she had them.[1]
