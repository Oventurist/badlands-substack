# Robert Natelson

**Robert Natelson** is a legal scholar known for his work on the [[united-states-constitution|United States Constitution]] and, in particular, on the [[article-v|Article V]] amendment process.[1]

## Defense of originalism

In pieces for the journal Law & Liberty, Natelson — alongside historian Paul Moreno — defended [[originalism]] against the critique advanced by Harvard historian [[jill-lepore|Jill Lepore]] in her Atlantic essay "How Originalism Has Killed the Constitution."[1] Law & Liberty characterized Lepore's essay as a broadside distinct from the more rigorous scholarly criticism of originalism, arguing that it distorts quotations out of context, misstates facts, and confuses concepts.[1] The counter-argument holds that, properly understood, originalism preserves the Constitution and its Article V amendment process — the lawful mechanism for change that a constitutional republic requires — by distinguishing between judicial and political processes and protecting democratic legitimacy, popular sovereignty, and the formal amendment machinery at the heart of the American Founding.[1]
