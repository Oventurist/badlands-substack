# Barron Trump

Barron Trump is the youngest son of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. In January 2025, at age 18, he was reported to be preparing to launch a luxury real estate venture with two young business partners, following in his father's footsteps.[1]

## Trump, Fulcher & Roxburgh Capital

Barron Trump briefly incorporated the business, Trump, Fulcher & Roxburgh Capital Inc., in Wyoming in July 2024, but the company was dissolved four months later following his father's election win, according to a Newsweek report.[1] Records obtained by the New York Post showed the venture had listed Mar-a-Lago in Palm Beach as its principal address.[1] Co-founder Cameron Roxburgh told Newsweek that the company had been paused to avoid election-related media attention, and said it was set to relaunch in spring 2025 with a focus on high-end real estate projects including golf courses and properties in Utah, Arizona and Idaho.[1]
