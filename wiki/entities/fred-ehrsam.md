# Fred Ehrsam

Fred Ehrsam is an American entrepreneur and investor who co-founded the cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase and later co-founded the neurotechnology company Nudge, in addition to work as a venture investor in digital assets. He is a recognizable figure in the intersection of cryptocurrency finance and frontier biotechnology.

Ehrsam was named in March 2026 as an appointee to the [[presidents-council-of-advisors-on-science-and-technology|President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology]], per the March 26 Badlands Brief. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] grouped Ehrsam among the appointees associated with [[peter-thiel|Peter Thiel]]'s network.
