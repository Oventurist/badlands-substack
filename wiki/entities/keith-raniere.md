# Keith Raniere

Keith Raniere is the founder and leader of [[nxivm|NXIVM]], convicted in federal court on charges related to sex trafficking and sentenced in 2020 to 120 years in prison.[1]

## Prosecution

Prosecutors alleged that NXIVM recruited and groomed women to be used as sexual partners for Raniere.[1] Former *Smallville* actress [[allison-mack|Allison Mack]] pleaded guilty in 2019 to racketeering and racketeering conspiracy charges relating to her role in that scheme and received a comparatively short 36-month sentence in exchange for assisting prosecutors in the case against Raniere.[1]
