# David Asher

David Asher is a former senior investigator for the [[state-department|State Department]] known for his work on illicit finance, sanctions evasion, and transnational criminal networks. He resurfaced in coverage of the 2024 bank money-laundering investigations as one of the principal voices arguing that the [[toronto-dominion-bank|Toronto-Dominion Bank]] case was only the beginning of a much broader enforcement wave against financial institutions servicing narcotics traffickers.[1]

## The Bureau interview and the "big four" prediction

In early September 2024, Asher appeared in a conversation alongside investigative journalist Sam Cooper, author of the Substack publication *The Bureau*, discussing People's Republic of China–linked narcotics money laundering networks operating through North American banks.[1] Asher said, "And most of what we're seeing is coming from this TD Bank case, and there's a lot more. We'll see which one of the big four US banks gets named next."[1]

That prediction acquired new significance weeks later when [[bank-of-america|Bank of America]] disclosed that it was in discussions with federal regulators over deficiencies in its [[bank-secrecy-act|Bank Secrecy Act]], anti-money-laundering, and sanctions compliance programs, and warned that a public enforcement order was possible.[1] Badlands Media commentary treated Asher's remark as an early indicator that the fentanyl-finance investigation would move from Canadian to American institutions.[1]
