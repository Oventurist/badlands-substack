# Sara Netanyahu

**Sara Netanyahu** is the wife of Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] In late November 2024 she dined with U.S. President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at the Trump International Golf Course in West Palm Beach, Florida, a meeting that Badlands Media treated as carrying significant political signal.[1]

## Florida dinner with Trump (November 2024)

According to the Times of Israel, Netanyahu dined with Trump at his Florida golf resort, and a photo of the pair was posted on X by Trump's deputy director of communications.[1] She was in Miami for several weeks to visit her son [[yair-netanyahu|Yair Netanyahu]], a trip that kept her out of Israel during the beginning of the prime minister's scheduled testimony in his criminal corruption trial.[1] Trump has built a rapport with her, and when the Netanyahus last met him in July 2024 the then-candidate quipped that Sara was his "secret weapon," adding, "As long as I have Sara, that's all that matters."[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the July meeting had the staging of a negotiation rather than a social visit — Sara seated at Trump's side, Bibi opposite, and Melania Trump absent.[1] He described the scene as resembling a mob film in which Sara functioned as a bargaining chip or incentive, explicitly declining to characterize her as a hostage, and speculated that Trump might have offered her asylum during the criminal case or clemency for any crimes in which her proximity to her husband made her complicit.[1] Her return to Florida alone to dine with Trump was, in his reading, not a coincidence, and coincided with reporting that Diaspora Minister [[amichai-chikli|Amichai Chikli]] of the [[likud|Likud]] party had told hostage families that Netanyahu should be removed from power over his failures related to the October 7 attack.[1]

Badlands also recounted reports that when the Netanyahus stayed as guests at the Trump [[white-house|White House]], they would go weeks without doing their laundry and bring it with them for the Trump staff to handle, cited as an example of humiliation rituals attributed to the couple.[1]

## Criminal investigation for witness harassment (2024–2025)

Israeli police opened a criminal investigation into Sara Netanyahu over allegations that she sought to intimidate a witness and interfere in the corruption case against her husband, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[2] The State Attorney's Office disclosed the probe in response to a request for information from Knesset member [[naama-lazimi|Naama Lazimi]], confirming that the investigation had been opened on December 26.[2]

The investigation is being conducted by the Israel Police together with the cybercrimes department of the State Attorney's Office, and follows a report by Channel 12's "Uvda" program alleging that Sara Netanyahu ordered Hanni Bleiweiss, her husband's late aide, to orchestrate protests and an online campaign against [[hadas-klein|Hadas Klein]], a key witness in one of the cases in which the prime minister is standing trial.[2] Attorney General [[gali-baharav-miara|Gali Baharav-Miara]] and State Attorney [[amit-aisman|Amit Aisman]] announced on December 26 that they had ordered an investigation on suspicion of witness harassment and obstruction of justice.[2]

Badlands commentary noted that Sara Netanyahu is technically a convicted felon, having been found guilty in 2019 of misusing state funds to cater more than $50,000 in meals despite having access to a full-time chef at the prime minister's residence, and speculated that the new exposure increased [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s leverage over her husband ahead of their February 2025 White House meeting.[2]
