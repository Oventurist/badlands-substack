# The Economic Collapse Blog

The Economic Collapse Blog is an alternative-media outlet focused on economic decline, inflation and financial-system risk in the United States.[1]

In late January 2023 the blog published an analysis headlined around an economic sign that had not occurred since the peak of the Great Depression in 1932, arguing that conditions were much worse than the public was being told.[1] It reported that prices had been rising much faster than incomes, that living standards were declining rapidly, and that more than a third of U.S. adults relied on their parents to pay at least some of their bills.[1] Citing [[fox-business|Fox Business]] coverage of the most recent GDP report, the blog highlighted the [[2022-real-disposable-income-decline|2022 real disposable income decline]] as the largest measured since 1932.[1]

The piece was carried in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] with commentary from [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]].[1]
