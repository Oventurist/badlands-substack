# Adam Curtis

Adam Curtis is a British documentary filmmaker cited by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] in his June 5, 2025 Badlands Media essay on climate science.[1] Ehret credits Curtis's 2011 film All Watched Over By Machines of Loving Grace with demonstrating how the belief that the biosphere is a closed, self-balancing system moved from the scientific fringe into mainstream thinking — despite, in Ehret's judgment, being simply wrong.[1]

That belief, as described in the article, held that a fixed atmospheric average of roughly 400 parts per million of [[carbon-dioxide|carbon dioxide]] was the "natural and ideal amount," and that ecosystems obey rules of entropy characteristic of closed systems.[1] Ehret uses Curtis's account of the machine-modelled ecology paradigm as historical evidence for how climate modelling assumptions became cultural orthodoxy.[1]
