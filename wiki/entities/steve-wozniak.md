# Steve Wozniak

Steve Wozniak is an American engineer and technologist best known as a co-founder of Apple Inc., where he designed the company's earliest personal computers. In later decades he has been associated with technology education initiatives.

Wozniak appears in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] as the creator of WozEd, a technology curriculum used by the [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]] in New York. The Brief quoted reporting that the district found [[realbotix|Realbotix]] "a natural fit" as a vendor precisely because the company's robots are already programmed with WozEd. The curriculum's presence in the district was therefore part of the justification offered for the nearly $60,000 purchase of the humanoid robot "Sally," a proposal subsequently paused after objections over data privacy and the vendor's corporate affiliations.
