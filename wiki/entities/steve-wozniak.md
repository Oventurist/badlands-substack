# Steve Wozniak

Steve Wozniak is a co-founder of [[apple|Apple]] and the creator of WozEd, an educational technology curriculum.[1]

## WozEd and the Salamanca robot pilot

WozEd surfaced in 2026 reporting on the [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]]'s attempt to place an AI humanoid robot in classrooms. The district reportedly regarded the Las Vegas–based vendor [[realbotix|Realbotix]] as "a natural fit because its robots are already programmed with WozEd, a curriculum from Apple co-founder Steve Wozniak that the district uses."[1]

The pilot was paused after parents, teachers, and state officials raised objections over student data privacy and the vendor's ties to a sex-doll manufacturer.[1]
