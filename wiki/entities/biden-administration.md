The Biden administration refers to the executive branch of the U.S. government under President Joe Biden (2021–2025).[1] A May 2023 Badlands Media article cited the administration as having "recently affirming its commitment" to finalizing the proposed pandemic treaty under the [[world-health-organization|WHO]].[1]

The article framed the administration's support for the treaty as part of a broader effort, allegedly backed by globalist actors, to establish an entity "above the US Government" that would force America to cede pandemic authority to the WHO.[1] This U.S. governmental stance was contrasted with warnings from legal scholars such as [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]] and [[reggie-littlejohn|Reggie Littlejohn]] that the treaty and [[international-health-regulations|IHR]] amendments would end national sovereignty.[1]

## Israel policy in the 2023 conflict

"War Pigs" argues that the Biden administration deployed much of the same stale rhetoric used to justify support for Ukraine in order to justify support for Israel, with multibillion-dollar support packages and access to America's most important military assets expected to follow.[2] Badlands Media contended that excessive foreign aid spending was not the only problem: the administration's handling of the situation was fuelling a hatred of the West that could put American citizens abroad in harm's way.[2]

The article highlighted the United States' lone veto of a [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] resolution calling for humanitarian pauses in Gaza, and argued that the safety and welfare of the American people did not appear to be the primary factor in the administration's foreign policy decisions.[2]
