The Biden administration refers to the executive branch of the U.S. government under President Joe Biden (2021–2025).[1] A May 2023 Badlands Media article cited the administration as having "recently affirming its commitment" to finalizing the proposed [[who-pandemic-treaty|pandemic treaty]] under the [[world-health-organization|WHO]].[1]

The article framed the administration's support for the treaty as part of a broader effort, allegedly backed by globalist actors, to establish an entity "above the US Government" that would force America to cede pandemic authority to the WHO.[1] This U.S. governmental stance was contrasted with warnings from legal scholars such as [[francis-boyle|Francis Boyle]] and [[reggie-littlejohn|Reggie Littlejohn]] that the treaty and [[international-health-regulations|IHR]] amendments would end national sovereignty.[1]
