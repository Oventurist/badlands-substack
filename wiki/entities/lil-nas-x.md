# Lil Nas X

**Lil Nas X** is an American musician cited by Badlands Media as an example of how modern culture may be steered toward normalizing occult themes among children.[1]

## Cultural trajectory

The article asks how its hypothesis — that elites consider child sacrifice normal and seek to normalize it for others — frames cultural moments such as Lil Nas X being positioned as an influencer of children with his song *Old Town Road*, only to then flip his image toward worshiping Satan.[1] The example is grouped with the [[sexualization-of-children|sexualization of children]] in public schools through warped sex education and [[queer-theory|queer theory]] as evidence of a broader cultural program.[1]

## Framing

Within the article's [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] framework, war has evolved away from the destruction of human life toward dominance over human cognition, so a modernized version of cultural normalization would manifest as sophisticated mind control operations designed to engineer a culture blind to childhood innocence.[1] Lil Nas X's arc is offered as one visible datapoint in that pattern rather than as an allegation of criminal conduct.[1]
