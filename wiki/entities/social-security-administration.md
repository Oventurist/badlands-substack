# Social Security Administration

The Social Security Administration is the federal agency administering U.S. retirement and disability benefits, and became an early focus of [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] scrutiny in 2025.[1]

## DOGE-era data claims

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] posted in February 2025 that there were 400 million Americans eligible for Social Security, a figure Badlands writer [[canncon|CannCon]] flagged as impossible against a national population of roughly 340 million and compared to bloated voter rolls.[1] The claim sat alongside testimony from LexisNexis CEO [[haywood-talcove|Haywood Talcove]] that verifying recipient identities could save $1 trillion annually.[1]

CannCon argued that such disclosures form a gradual revelation strategy: by first exposing the inefficiency and bloat of government, [[donald-trump|Trump]] and Musk make the plausibility of election fraud legible even to moderates who would have rebuked an immediate assault on the 2020 election result.[1]

## Hiring of Edward Coristine (2025)

In June 2025 the agency confirmed that [[edward-coristine|Edward Coristine]], the 19-year-old known as "Big Balls" who had worked at the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]], had joined as a special government employee after leaving the White House.[2] Spokesperson Stephen McGraw said Coristine's work would focus on "improving the functionality of the Social Security website and advancing our mission of delivering more efficient service to the American people."[2]

## Dual-hatted leadership, 2025

In October 2025, SSA head [[frank-bisignano|Frank Bisignano]] was appointed by Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] to serve concurrently as the first chief executive officer of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]], announcing the move in an email to SSA employees and citing shared customer-service and technology goals across the two agencies.[3]
