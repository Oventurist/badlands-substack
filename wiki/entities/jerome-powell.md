# Jerome Powell

Jerome Powell became chairman of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] in 2018, appointed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to succeed [[janet-yellen|Janet Yellen]].

## In the Badlands corpus

The Badlands Media essay [[a-week-to-remember|A Week to Remember]] treats Powell's tenure as a phase in the forced normalisation of an economy the author considers artificially sustained. According to the source, "Trump-elected-Jerome Powell took over as the Fed Chairman, and started raising interest rates aggressively to the displeasure of Trump." The essay links this tightening to a 20% stock market decline in the autumn of 2018, followed by rate cuts in 2019 that restored the market.

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] frames the episode as vindicating Trump's position rather than Powell's: "Trump knew rates needed to come up, but it had to be gradual. He was right, as he always seems to be." Powell also features implicitly in the article's account of the COVID-19 period, when the author says Trump "forced the Fed to print money at a rate never seen before" in order to keep the economy alive — described as fighting the [[cabal|Cabal]] "with their own weapon, the Federal Reserve."
