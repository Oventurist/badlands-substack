# We Build the Wall

We Build the Wall was a privately funded campaign to construct sections of barrier along the United States–Mexico border, and is the effort for which the activist couple [[dustin-stockton|Dustin Stockton]] and [[jennifer-lynn-lawrence|Jennifer Lynn Lawrence]] are best known.[1]

A February 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay referenced the campaign in identifying the pair, who subsequently founded the organizing project [[america-mission|America Mission]] and became prominent hosts on [[twitter-spaces|Twitter Spaces]].[1]
