# Bill Hemmer

Bill Hemmer is an American television news anchor for [[fox-news|Fox News]].[1] In May 2023 he interviewed former Vice President [[mike-pence|Mike Pence]] on a Thursday morning broadcast, during which Pence warned of an incoming influx of illegal immigrants crossing the southern border and told Hemmer that "a storm is coming one week from today at the Southern Border."[1] The warning came as thousands of migrants waited outside the U.S.–Mexico border for [[title-42|Title 42]] to end.[1] The exchange drew attention in the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] because of the resonance of the word "storm" with [[the-storm|the Q concept of the same name]].[1]
