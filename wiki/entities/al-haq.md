# Al-Haq

Al-Haq is a nongovernmental organization based in the West Bank that has long been accused of ties to the [[popular-front-for-the-liberation-of-palestine|Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine]] (PFLP), an entity designated as a foreign terrorist organization by both the European Union and the United States.[1]

## Open Society funding

A September 2025 report by the [[capital-research-center|Capital Research Center]] stated that the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]] had funneled more than $2.3 million into Al-Haq.[1] The report presented this grantmaking as evidence that the foundation's exposure to groups tied to terrorism or extremist violence was not confined to organizations operating inside the United States.[1]
