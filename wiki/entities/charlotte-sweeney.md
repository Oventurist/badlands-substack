# Charlotte Sweeney

Charlotte Sweeney is a United States District Judge in the [[us-district-court-of-colorado|United States District Court for the District of Colorado]] who presided over [[naacp-v-useip|*NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.*]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

Sweeney terminated the planned five-day trial after three days, entering a judgement based on partial findings under Rule 52(c) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure in favor of the defendants and awarding them costs.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back) In her ruling she stated: "In sum, plaintiffs have failed to introduce any evidence that can remotely be perceived as intimidating or threatening on behalf of the three defendants."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

She also addressed the credibility of the plaintiffs' sole intimidation witness, finding: "It was upon suggestion apparently from plaintiffs' counsel that USEIP was the group canvassing in her county that she got — at which point she adopted that position and included it in her affidavit."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back) Defendants described this finding as a damning rebuke of the plaintiff organizations and their attorneys.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/always-fight-back)

## Alien Enemies Act ruling, April 2025

On 22 April 2025 Sweeney ruled that the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration must give Venezuelan migrants detained in Colorado 21 days' notice before any deportation carried out under the [[alien-enemies-act|Alien Enemies Act]] of 1798, and must inform them of their right to challenge their removal.[2] Her order, issued the same day that Judge [[alvin-hellerstein|Alvin Hellerstein]] weighed a 10-day notice requirement in Manhattan, was read as a signal that the wartime statute's use for expedited removals might not survive judicial review.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Sweeney was the same judge before whom she had argued at trial in July of the previous year.[2]
