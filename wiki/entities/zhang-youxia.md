# Zhang Youxia

Zhang Youxia is a senior general of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and, at the time of the reporting described below, a vice chairman of the Central Military Commission, the body through which the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] exercises control over China's armed forces. He is widely identified as a member of the so-called "red families" — the lineages descended from the founding generation of the CCP — a status that in the accounts summarized here is treated as the key to understanding his political position relative to [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].

## Reported arrest, January 2026

The January 26, 2026 edition of the Badlands Brief ("ICE Getting Boiled, Chinese Coup Chaos, & Carney Bends The Knee") devotes a section to reports that circulated on social media on the preceding Friday alleging a military coup in China. Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry quotes an X account cited by General [[michael-flynn|Michael Flynn]] which stated: "Latest news confirms: Zhang Youxia, Liu Zhenli, and Zhong Shaojun have been arrested simultaneously, and all of Zhang Youxia's family members have also been taken into custody."

The same account listed circumstantial indicators offered as evidence: Zhang's absence from the opening ceremony of a seminar for provincial and ministerial-level leading cadres on implementing the spirit of the Fourth Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee; his absence from a scheduled annual event at the National Defense University; and a report that, following the death of former Deputy Chief of the General Staff Wei Fulin, the funeral wreath Zhang had sent was ordered temporarily removed. A follow-up post cautioned that the source could not be verified and suggested waiting to see "whether Zhang Youxia shows up at the Spring Festival group meeting or not," while adding that if the arrest were real "that would mean Xi Jinping and the red families have completely broken off and reached the point of no return."

The Brief's commentary states that confirmation subsequently emerged of seventeen officials arrested in total, including Zhang and fellow officers [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]] and [[zhong-shaojun|Zhong Shaojun]], with the operation attributed to a special service bureau under Wang Xiaohong. It further reports that the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] has accused Zhang of leaking classified intelligence on China's nuclear weapons technology to the United States.

## Interpretation in the source

GhostofBasedPatrickHenry situates the reported arrests within a "Good Twin and Evil Twin" framework, credited to Chris Paul, in which Xi Jinping and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] represent the reformist twin in their respective countries while the arrested Chinese military leaders, like the American "Uniparty/Deep State," represent the opposing faction. Framed this way, Zhang's removal is read not as instability but as Xi consolidating power against an entrenched military-aristocratic bloc. The commentary notes reporting attributed to the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] and the Journal that more than fifty senior military officers and defense-industry executives have been investigated or removed over the preceding two and a half years, and cites the [[financial-times|Financial Times]] for the conclusion that the purge consolidates Xi's authority.

These claims originate in unverified social-media reporting relayed by a partisan commentary outlet and should be treated as contested.

## Formal investigation, January 2026

The January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief reports the official Chinese announcement of investigations into Zhang, then 75, and General [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]] for "serious violations of discipline and law," a formulation commonly indicating corruption. The probes were revealed over the weekend of January 25–26, 2026, with the *People's Liberation Army Daily* accusing the pair of "seriously betraying" the Communist Party's trust, "trampling on and undermining" the [[central-military-commission|Central Military Commission]], and threatening the authority of its chairman, [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].

The purges reduced the CMC to Xi and one other member, leaving senior [[peoples-liberation-army|PLA]] ranks in disarray. Unverified reports cited by the [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] alleged Zhang leaked core nuclear weapons data to the United States and accepted bribes; other explanations advanced included factional infighting and Zhang's own accumulation of power. Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] reads the removal as the culmination of Xi's [[tigers-and-flies|Tigers and Flies]] campaign and as consolidation rather than instability.
