# Polestar

**Polestar** is an electric vehicle manufacturer named in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]], where it appears among the first five EV companies he listed to test the market's response to climate policy.[1]

From [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s January 20, 2021 inauguration through May 2023, Carlson reported Polestar's stock down 65 percent.[1] He placed this alongside declines at [[tesla|Tesla]], [[nikola|Nikola]], [[rivian|Rivian]], and [[nio|Nio]], arguing that a sector supposedly favored by federal policy should have appreciated rather than collapsed, and that the uniformity of the losses undercut explanations based on individual corporate missteps.[1]
