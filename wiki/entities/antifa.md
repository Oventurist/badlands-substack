# Antifa

Antifa is the loose label attached to decentralized militant left-wing anti-fascist networks in the United States. The Badlands corpus treats it not as a spontaneous movement but, in [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]]'s characterization, as "ostensibly an IC op that recruits vulnerable people, hires them to destabilize the nation," a claim she supports with first-hand observation of Denver-area adherents rather than documentary evidence.

## The North Texas cell prosecution

The March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that a federal jury in Fort Worth, Texas convicted nine defendants over a July 4, 2025 attack outside the [[prairieland-detention-center|Prairieland Immigration and Customs Enforcement detention center]] in Alvarado. The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] described the group as members of a "North Texas Antifa cell" engaged in rioting, explosives offenses and providing material support to terrorists. [[homeland-security-investigations|HSI]] Dallas Special Agent in Charge Travis Pickard called the case "a historic moment as it represents the nation's initial federal indictment targeting a coordinated group of Antifa cell members engaged in violent criminal activity."

[[benjamin-song|Benjamin Song]] was convicted of attempted murder and firearms offenses in connection with the shooting of Alvarado Police Lieutenant Thomas Gross. Eight defendants were convicted of rioting, material support, conspiracy to use explosives and use of explosives, with fireworks cited as the explosive devices. Daniel Sanchez-Estrada was convicted of corruptly concealing documents, and he and Maricela Rueda were convicted of conspiracy to conceal documents tied to the investigation.

## Civil-liberties framing

While endorsing the prosecutions — "People planting bombs deserve prison or worse — full stop. That actually is domestic terrorism" — Ashe in America warns against expansion of the domestic-terror label to cover speech, assembly and association absent underlying criminal conduct, invoking the case of [[tina-peters|Tina Peters]] as the mirror image and concluding that "principles are greater than politics" and "equal justice under the law is nonnegotiable."
