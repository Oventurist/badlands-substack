# Blaise Compaoré

Blaise Compaoré is the former president of [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] who came to power in the 1987 coup during which Pan-Africanist icon [[thomas-sankara|Thomas Sankara]] was assassinated.[1]

## Rule and ouster

Compaoré became the longest-serving Burkinabè president, remaining in office for 27 years until he was ousted in the 2014 Burkina Faso uprising, which was followed by the installation of a transitional government.[1] Badlands commentary, while acknowledging it has no concrete evidence and cannot know Compaoré's true motives, argues it is likely he was installed by the globalist cabal, and speculates that the timing of the 2014 uprising — in geographic and chronological proximity to the 2011 uprising against Muammar Gaddafi in Libya — suggests possible [[nato|NATO]] involvement.[1]

## Aftermath

In September 2015, elements of the former presidential guard attempted to topple the transitional government that replaced Compaoré. Compaoré's former chief of staff, Gilbert Diendéré, and his head of diplomacy, Djibril Bassolé, were sentenced in 2019 to 20 and ten years in prison respectively for allegedly masterminding the failed coup; both were excluded from the March 2025 amnesty issued by interim president [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]].[1]
