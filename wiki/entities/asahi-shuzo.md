# Asahi Shuzo

Asahi Shuzo is the Japanese sake maker behind the premium brand Dassai. In December 2024 it announced plans to brew sake in space aboard the International Space Station.[1]

## Space sake project

The company intends to blast ingredients to the ISS to make a special brew; if the plan succeeds, a single 100ml bottle would sell on Earth for $653,000 — against a standard sake serving of 80ml.[1] Sake is traditionally made from Japanese rice, water, yeast and koji, a type of mold, and normally takes about two months through a process of steaming, stirring and fermenting.[1]

Souya Uetsuki, the brewer in charge of the project, cautioned that "there is no guarantee of 100% success for the fermentation tests," since changes in gravity affect how heat transfers through fluid and therefore alter fermentation.[1] Asahi Shuzo paid the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency for access to the Japanese-developed Kibo experiment module on the ISS in order to conduct tests in a special microgravity environment.[1]

The firm is known for expensive premium products that collectors pay thousands of dollars for, but says the venture is more than a rarity play: Uetsuki stated the company hopes to understand how fermentation works in space with the eventual goal of producing sake on the moon.[1]

## Badlands commentary

The Brief used the story to needle [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s repeated claim that humanity must go to space to preserve the future of human consciousness, arguing that "moon sake" at over $600,000 a bottle reinforces the view that the current space race amounts to "the fever dreams of bored rich people."[1]
