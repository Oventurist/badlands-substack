# Meagan Wolfe

Meagan Wolfe is a Wisconsin election official named as a defendant, alongside the [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]], in Waukesha County Case Number 2024CV001544, *Janel Brandtjen v. Wisconsin Elections Commission, Meagan Wolfe*.[1]

## Brandtjen litigation

The commission's motion to dismiss the case was denied in early January 2026, a development election researcher [[peter-bernegger|Peter Bernegger]] described as a "huge win in court."[1] With the suit advancing to full discovery, Bernegger said the plaintiffs planned to depose [[david-becker|David Becker]] of the [[electronic-registration-information-center|ERIC]] states, alleging that the commission and ERIC unlawfully provided voters' personally identifiable information — including Social Security numbers, birthdates and driver's license numbers — to Becker's Center for Election Innovation and Research without informing the voters.[1]
