# Rachel Crandall

**Rachel Crandall** is an American transgender advocate and the head of [[transgender-michigan|Transgender Michigan]], a Michigan-based transgender advocacy organization.[1] She established the [[transgender-day-of-visibility|Transgender Day of Visibility]] in 2009, holding the first observance on March 31, 2009; the date has been used every year since.[1]

Crandall's role in founding the observance became relevant to political commentary in 2024, when the fixed March 31 date coincided with Easter Sunday and prompted a national dispute over a presidential proclamation issued by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]].[1] Commentators in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] cited the 2009 origin of the date to argue that the overlap was structural rather than newly engineered, while still contending that the proclamation itself was intended to inflame the [[culture-war|culture war]].[1]
