# Matt Lauer

Matt Lauer is a former NBC News anchor who moderated the Commander-In-Chief Forum on September 7, 2016, during the closing stretch of the 2016 presidential campaign.[1]

## The Commander-In-Chief Forum

In the Badlands Media feature "Enjoy the Show," author [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that Lauer's decision to question [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] about her use of a private, unauthorized email server while she was Secretary of State amounted to going "off script."[1] By that point, [[james-comey|James Comey]] had publicly exonerated Clinton on July 5, 2016, and — in Lange's telling — the "controlled media" were expected to treat the server story as closed and to promote Clinton's candidacy without revisiting her legal exposure.[1] Lauer's question is therefore presented as a breach of an otherwise tightly micromanaged campaign environment in which no unscripted questions were supposed to occur in any setting.[1]

## Alleged aftermath and Clinton outburst

Citing reporting by Bill Still of *The Still Report*, relayed through Investment Watch Blog, the article recounts an unnamed NBC associate producer's account that Clinton flew into a backstage rage after the forum: throwing a full glass of water at an assistant's face, screaming at staff, directing racist insults at [[donna-brazile|Donna Brazile]], and vowing to have Lauer fired.[1] In the same alleged tirade Clinton is quoted as saying of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], "If that fucking bastard wins, we all hang from nooses!"[1] Lange notes that most mainstream outlets and fact-checkers deny the incident occurred, but treats Bill Still's credentials as a former newspaper editor and published author as circumstantial support for the account.[1]

Lauer was dismissed by NBC over sexual harassment allegations not long after Clinton lost the 2016 election. Lange frames the timing as suspicious rather than coincidental, arguing that Clinton "made good on her threat" against him.[1]
