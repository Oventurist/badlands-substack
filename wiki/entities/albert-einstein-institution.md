# Albert Einstein Institution

The Albert Einstein Institution (AEI) is the organization founded by [[gene-sharp|Gene Sharp]] to promote and operationalize [[nonviolent-action|nonviolent action]].[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] notes that the institution has virtually no connection to Albert Einstein — a known pacifist — apart from Sharp himself, with whom Einstein corresponded very briefly in the final years of his life.[1] DeLarme draws attention to the timing of its launch: Sharp founded AEI in 1984, the same year the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] was created to carry out operations formerly associated with the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]].[1]

## Role in youth movements

According to the article, AEI has played a key role in training and deploying youth movements around the world in order to prepare the conditions for coups — fostering the impression that targeted governments are deeply unpopular and destabilizing those governments through demonstrations and similar tactics.[1]

After the Cold War formally ended, both [[nato|NATO]] and the Albert Einstein Institution found new purpose in the breakup of the multi-ethnic socialist state of [[yugoslavia|Yugoslavia]].[1] For more than a decade before 2000, AEI had been actively reaching out to "Slovenian democrats" and "Albanian students," a campaign that culminated in a March 2000 workshop on nonviolent action funded by the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] and feeding directly into the Serbian movement [[otpor|Otpor!]].[1]
