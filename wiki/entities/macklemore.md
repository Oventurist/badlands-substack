# Macklemore

Macklemore is an American rapper who, in commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], was named as a public figure who addressed the crowd at the pro-Palestinian demonstration in Washington, D.C. on November 4, 2023.[1]

## November 2023 Washington rally commentary

Writing in the Badlands News Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the day's events as an "insurrection," describing protesters vandalizing the northwest gate of the White House with paint, shaking and climbing the gate, and defacing monuments across the District of Columbia.[1] The commentary reported that Macklemore was "one of the key figures to incite the violence after speaking to the mob earlier in the day," and drew a satirical comparison to the language used by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before January 6, 2021, noting that it was unclear whether the rapper had used the words "peacefully" and "patriotically."[1]

This characterization is Badlands commentary rather than a finding of fact by any investigative body, and no charge or official determination against the performer is reported in the source.[1] The piece situated the episode within an expectation of renewed street mobilization, which the author framed as a "reboot of the Summer of Love" and as a regime-aligned "narrative shift."[1]
