# Walter Mischer

Walter Mischer was a Houston banker and the longtime political fund-raiser for [[george-hw-bush|George Bush]].[1]

His significance in the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan crisis]], as reported by the Village Voice and relayed by Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], lies in his friendship with [[john-riddle|John Riddle]], whose $15 million in defaulted loans was a major factor in the failure of [[sunrise-savings-and-loan|Sunrise Savings]] of Boynton Beach, Florida — a collapse that cost taxpayers $680 million after Bush personally intervened to halt a regulator's investigation.[1] Riddle was one of the five largest defaulters on thrift loans in the nation.[1] Lange offers the chain as an illustration of his maxim that "connections mean everything."[1]
