# Sheffield Steelers

The **Sheffield Steelers** are an English professional ice hockey club competing in the Elite Ice Hockey League and based in Sheffield.[1]

## 2023 death of Adam Johnson

On October 28, 2023, during an Elite Ice Hockey League game at the Steelers' home arena, [[nottingham-panthers|Nottingham Panthers]] player [[adam-johnson|Adam Johnson]] was fatally injured when a skate blade cut his neck.[1] The Steelers player involved was [[matt-petgrave|Matt Petgrave]], who collided with another Panthers player and whose left skate kicked up as he began to fall.[1] [[south-yorkshire-police|South Yorkshire Police]] arrested a man on suspicion of manslaughter on November 14, 2023.[1]
