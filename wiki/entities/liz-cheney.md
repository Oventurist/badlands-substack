# Liz Cheney

**Liz Cheney** is a former Republican member of the [[house-of-representatives]] from Wyoming and the daughter of former Vice President [[dick-cheney]]. Once a member of House Republican leadership, she became the party's most prominent internal critic of [[donald-trump]], served on the January 6 select committee, and was defeated in her 2022 primary by a wide margin.

## As political shorthand

In Trump-aligned discourse, Cheney's name functions less as a reference to a sitting politician than as a category: the Republican who breaks with Trump, is embraced by the national press, and is subsequently destroyed at the ballot box by his own primary electorate. Trump invoked precisely this usage in a February 2026 Truth Social post attacking Representative [[thomas-massie]], writing that Massie's collapsing job-approval numbers in Kentucky — where challenger [[ed-gallrein]] was said to be "crushing him in the Polls" — had placed him in "'Wacky' Liz Cheney territory."

The comparison is a threat framed as a diagnosis: association with anti-Trump positioning, in this case Massie's pursuit of the [[epstein-narrative|Epstein files]] against Attorney General [[pam-bondi]], is presented as electorally terminal within the Republican Party.
