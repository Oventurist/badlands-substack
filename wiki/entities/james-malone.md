# James Malone

James Malone is a Pennsylvania Democrat who won a March 2025 special election for a seat in the Pennsylvania state Senate, flipping a district that had previously been held by a Republican and that was widely regarded as safely red.[1]

## 2025 Pennsylvania state Senate special election

The Malone victory was one of two notable Pennsylvania election results reported in late March 2025. In the Pennsylvania state House, Democrats won a special election that handed them control of that legislative chamber — an outcome treated as unsurprising because the district leaned Democratic. The state Senate contest was different: [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had carried the district by roughly 15 points in November 2024, yet Malone still managed to flip it.[1]

Coverage aggregated in the Badlands News Brief framed the two results as part of a Democratic winning streak that was worrying Republicans ahead of a set of key contests the following week, including the Wisconsin state Supreme Court race and two Florida special elections — among them the race to fill the congressional seat vacated by national security adviser [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]]. In Waltz's former district, Republicans were reportedly concerned after the Democratic candidate out-raised the GOP candidate by more than ten to one.[1]
