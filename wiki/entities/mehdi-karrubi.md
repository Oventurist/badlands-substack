# Mehdi Karrubi

Hojatolislam Mehdi Karrubi is an Iranian cleric and politician who, at the time of the 1991 reporting on the October Surprise allegations, was Speaker of the Iranian Parliament.[1]

## Alleged 1980 meetings

In 1980 Karrubi was described as a representative of Ayatollah [[ruhollah-khomeini|Ruhollah Khomeini]], the founder of Iran's revolutionary government.[1] [[gary-sick|Gary Sick]] alleged that two meetings took place between Karrubi and [[william-casey|William Casey]], [[ronald-reagan|Reagan's]] campaign chairman and later Director of Central Intelligence, at the Ritz Hotel in Madrid in late July 1980.[1] Sick's principal source for the Madrid meetings was Jamshid Hashemi, an Iranian arms dealer who said he and his brother Cyrus helped arrange them, with Casey, the Hashemi brothers and an unnamed American intelligence officer in attendance.[1] The allegation was reported for the first time by Sick.[1]

These contacts form part of the alleged [[october-surprise|October Surprise]] arrangement in which a future Reagan administration would ship arms to Tehran through Israel in exchange for the hostages being held until after the election.[1]
