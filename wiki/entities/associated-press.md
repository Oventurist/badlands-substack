# Associated Press

The Associated Press (AP) is an American not-for-profit news cooperative and wire service whose reporting is redistributed by thousands of member outlets worldwide. In the Badlands corpus it functions primarily as a benchmark of mainstream consensus reporting, cited when the Brief wants to establish what the legacy press has said about an event before offering a dissenting reading.

In the July 6, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2b2|Badlands Brief]], the AP is cited twice. It reported that experts considered President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s broad claims linking Democrats to [[communism|communism]] inaccurate, a framing set against Trump's Mount Rushmore "communist menace" speech of July 3 and against the warning attributed to [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]] fellow [[mike-gonzalez|Mike Gonzalez]] on [[fox-news|Fox News]]. Separately, Arab News drew on AP and AFP reporting for its account of the funeral procession of Iranian Supreme Leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]], including the movement of his coffin and those of family members killed in a February 28 airstrike through Tehran toward Mehrabad International Airport.
