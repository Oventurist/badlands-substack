# Committee to Protect Journalists

The Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) is a press-freedom advocacy organization that monitors and publicizes government actions against journalists and news outlets. As of November 2024 its chief executive was Jodie Ginsberg.[1]

## Response to the Israeli boycott of Haaretz

After the Israeli Cabinet voted unanimously in November 2024 to sanction [[haaretz|Haaretz]] over the paper's critical coverage of the Israel–Hamas war, CPJ was among the press-freedom advocates that condemned the decision.[1] Ginsberg said: "We deplore the Israeli government's attempt to silence a respected Israeli outlet like Haaretz by hurting their advertising and subscription revenue."[1] She added that "Israel's increasing deployment of restrictions on critical media is further disturbing evidence of its efforts to prevent coverage of its actions in Gaza."[1]

The measure at issue, proposed by Communications Minister [[shlomo-karhi|Shlomo Karhi]], barred government and state-owned company staff from communicating with Haaretz and ended official advertising and subscription spending with the newspaper.[1]
