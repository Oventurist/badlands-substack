# Dante Fascell

Dante Fascell was a senior Democratic member of the House Foreign Affairs Committee and a chief sponsor of the 1983 legislation creating the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]].[1]

## The stricken CIA prohibition

When the endowment was established, some members of Congress considered it essential to dissociate it from the [[cia|CIA]], and inserted language barring the involvement of any current or former agency officials.[1] According to a congressional aide who helped write the bill and later spoke to journalist Robert Parry, late one night in the 1983 session — as the bill was about to reach the House floor — the CIA's congressional liaison came "pounding at the door" of Fascell's office with a single message from Director [[william-casey|William Casey]]: the language barring CIA personnel had to be struck.[1] The aide recalled that Fascell consented to the demand without fully recognizing its significance, leaving the endowment without the firewall its drafters had intended.[1]
