# Awan Group

The **Awan Group** is described in Chapter 9 of the Badlands Media series [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] as an information-technology company with offices in Bahrain, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], Pakistan, the United Arab Emirates, Malaysia, China and the United States.[1]

The company is introduced immediately after the chapter's discussion of the [[awan-brothers|Awan brothers]], the congressional IT administrators whose lack of vetting and alleged transmission of sensitive material the author treats as an example of "[[infiltration|infiltration, not invasion]]."[1] The chapter does not document a corporate relationship between the firm and the congressional staffers; it presents the Awan Group's multinational footprint as contextual evidence for the reach of the networks it is describing, particularly their presence in the Gulf states at the centre of the Saudi-money narrative running through drops #68–71.[1]

## Assessment

The Awan Group entry rests on a single assertion in the source article and should be treated as low-confidence. No corroborating detail — ownership, incorporation, contracts, or the nature of any link to U.S. government work — is provided in the chapter.[1]
