# Bodega and Small Business Group

The Bodega and Small Business Group (BSBG) is a New York City trade organization representing thousands of bodegas — the small neighborhood convenience stores that anchor much of the city's retail commerce.[1]

## April 2024 endorsement of Donald Trump

In April 2024 the BSBG issued a statement backing President [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]], criticizing Manhattan District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]], and condemning local politicians the group said had ignored widespread criminality in the city.[1] The group announced it was "honored to host President Donald J. Trump at the Blue Moon bodega in Manhattan where [[jose-alba|Jose Alba]] was arrested for simply defending himself and his business against a convicted felon."[1]

The statement credited a public campaign by store owners and their supporters with forcing Bragg to back off the Alba prosecution, and said the group "appreciates President Trump's support on behalf of tougher enforcement for retail thieves that are making our stores more dangerous and less profitable at the same time."[1]

## Retail theft legislation

The BSBG tied its endorsement to the failure of New York state lawmakers to strengthen penalties for repeat retail theft offenders, noting that legislative Democrats had formally rejected Governor [[kathy-hochul|Kathy Hochul]]'s proposal to make assaulting a retail worker a felony — a rejection the group described as a reflection of the party's internal split over criminal justice and public safety.[1]

## Commentary

Commentary published alongside the item in [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] treated the bodega owners' statement as a signal that ordinary Americans across racial and ethnic lines were rejecting the political class, arguing that "if these bodega owners are any indication, the People are finding their courage again."[1]
