# Steve Somma

Steve Somma is an [[fbi|FBI]] agent who served as case agent on the [[carter-page|Carter Page]] matter during [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] and who took part in the January 2017 interviews of [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]].[1]

## Role established at the Danchenko trial

Supervisory Intelligence Analyst [[brian-auten|Brian Auten]] testified that Danchenko was interviewed over three days in January 2017 by Auten and Somma, with a National Security Division representative — [[david-laufman|David Laufman]] on the first day and Richard Scott on the second — and Danchenko's counsel present.[1] Somma was described as the case agent handling the Carter Page case at that time.[1]

After Danchenko was signed up as a Confidential Human Source of the Washington Field Office during what Auten termed "Crossfire Hurricane 2.0," Somma was his original handling agent; the source was later handed to Agent [[kevin-helson|Kevin Helson]], whose specialty was Russian counterintelligence, after Somma was moved to New York.[1]
