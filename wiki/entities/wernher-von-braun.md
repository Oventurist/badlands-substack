# Wernher von Braun

Wernher von Braun was a German-born rocket engineer brought to the United States under [[operation-paperclip|Operation Paperclip]], where he became a central figure in American rocketry.[1] Badlands commentary characterizes him as "one of the more prolific Operation Paperclip Nazi's."[1]

## The alleged staged threat sequence

Von Braun is cited as having claimed that a global false flag operation involving projections of extraterrestrial craft in the sky — together with projections of gods, mystics and other spiritual figures — was planned as far back as the 1960s or earlier.[1] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] invoked this claim in assessing the June 2023 [[david-grusch|David Grusch]] disclosure, arguing that eight decades of propaganda are consistent with preparation for such a "cosmic false flag."[1]
