# Public Square

Public Square (trading as PublicSq., ticker $PSQH) is an online directory and marketplace of pro-freedom, self-described "non-woke" American businesses, positioned as part of a parallel economy for consumers seeking to avoid companies they regard as politically hostile.[1]

## Public listing

Public Square went public on the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] on Thursday, July 20, 2023.[1] At the time of the listing, Fox Business reported that the platform had more than 1.1 million active consumer members and over 55,000 businesses, 90 percent of which were small businesses, with accounts free for both buyers and sellers.[1] Chief executive [[michael-seifert|Michael Seifert]] marked the occasion by declaring that "the largest network of non-woke businesses in America is officially public $PSQH."[1]

The opening-bell ceremony drew chants of "USA! USA!" from the exchange floor as [[donald-trump-jr|Donald Trump Jr.]], [[kim-guilfoyle|Kim Guilfoyle]] and Seifert stood at the podium.[1] Coverage of the moment focused on the reaction of CNBC personnel: [[jim-cramer|Jim Cramer]] was reported to be shaking his head in disapproval while "Squawk on the Street" host [[david-faber|David Faber]] covered his ears, the network instead steering discussion toward home prices.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme argued that the reaction illustrated both an establishment media hostility to overt patriotism and an unwillingness to report an obvious story, and that Public Square's premise -- directing spending toward companies that share customers' values -- was precisely what its critics feared.[1]
