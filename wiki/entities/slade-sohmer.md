# Slade Sohmer

Slade Sohmer is an American media figure, formerly the managing editor of [[cnn|CNN]]'s now-defunct BEME video sharing app and, until a month before his arrest, editor-in-chief of the left-leaning video-driven news site [[the-recount|The Recount]], who was charged in Massachusetts in November 2023 with child pornography offenses.[1]

## Charges

Sohmer, 44, was freed on $100,000 bail on a Monday after being charged in a Massachusetts court with possessing and disseminating "hundreds of child pornography images and videos."[1] He pled not guilty to two counts of possession of child pornography and two counts of dissemination of child pornography.[1]

## Context

Reporting on the case placed it in a sequence of similar prosecutions, noting that in the preceding twelve months alone two senior producers — one for [[abc-news|ABC]] and one for CNN — had been sentenced to prison for child pornography offenses, prompting the observation that "we're starting to sense a trend."[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the case to revisit early Info War-era debates around [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]], writing that where he once answered the question "You don't think it's ALL of them, do you?" with an elaborate treatise on systems, leverage, blackmail, Satanic belief and narrative projection, he now answered directly: "Yeah, actually. I do think it's pretty much ALL of them."[1] He concluded that "awakening happens very slowly ... and then all at once."[1]
