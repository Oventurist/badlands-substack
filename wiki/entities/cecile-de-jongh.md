# Cecile de Jongh

Cecile de Jongh is a former First Lady of the [[us-virgin-islands|United States Virgin Islands]] who has been named in connection with the territory's litigation over [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s operations there.[1]

## Allegations

In coverage of the USVI lawsuit against [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] noted that de Jongh was accused of covering for Epstein in a quid pro quo scheme, listing the accusation alongside the territorial attorney general's direct claim that the bank facilitated sex-trafficking operations.[1] Epstein maintained a private retreat in the USVI and brought many of his victims there before his death in 2019.[1]
