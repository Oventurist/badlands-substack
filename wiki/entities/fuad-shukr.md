# Fuad Shukr

Fuad Shukr was a senior military commander of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], described as the group's top military chief, who was killed in an Israeli strike in Beirut in late July 2024.[1]

## Killing and regional consequences

Shukr's death came hours before the killing of [[ismail-haniyeh|Ismail Haniyeh]], the political leader of [[hamas|Hamas]], in Tehran.[1] The back-to-back assassinations put the Middle East on alert for a wave of retaliatory attacks by [[iran|Iran]] and its allied forces.[1] Tehran publicly blamed [[israel|Israel]] for Haniyeh's death and vowed that Jerusalem would be made to pay a price, while Israel did not comment publicly on the Tehran killing.[1]

The pairing of the two operations became the central reference point for the diplomatic scramble that followed, in which the [[white-house|White House]] and allied governments pressed both Tehran and Jerusalem to avoid escalation into a regional war.[1] Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] reportedly conveyed a request for restraint to Iran's supreme leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] through envoy [[sergei-shoigu|Sergei Shoigu]] in the same period.[1]
