# Buddy Dyer

Buddy Dyer is the Democratic mayor of Orlando, Florida. In April 2025 he became the subject of a removal threat from state officials over the city's approach to federal immigration enforcement.[1]

## 2025 sanctuary policy dispute

Dyer reportedly told a meeting with the Orlando Police Department that law enforcement should resist immigration enforcement and should adhere to the city's [[orlando-trust-act|Trust Act]], a 2018 measure barring city employees and police from inquiring about an individual's immigration status.[1]

Florida Attorney General [[james-uthmeier|James Uthmeier]] responded with a letter, posted on X, warning that Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] would reserve the right to remove Dyer from office if he pursued that course.[1] The letter stated that "sanctuary policies are not tolerated or lawful in Florida" and that failure to abide by state law could result in penalties "including but not limited to being held in contempt, declaratory or injunctive relief, and removal from office by the Governor pursuant to section 908.107, Florida Statutes and the Florida Constitution."[1]

Badlands commentary used the episode to argue that if governors possess removal authority for legal violations and rarely use it, the non-enforcement itself implies systemic acquiescence to those violations.[1] The dispute is one instance of the broader conflict over [[sanctuary-cities|sanctuary city]] policies.[1]
