# C. Fred Bergsten

C. Fred Bergsten is an American economist and policy advisor who was present at the founding meetings of the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]].[1]

He attended the private organizing session held at [[kykuit|Kykuit]], the Rockefeller estate at Pocantico Hills, on July 23–24, 1972, as one of the founders hand-selected by [[david-rockefeller|David Rockefeller]] and [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]].[1] Bergsten later aided in the preparation of a commission report titled *Interdependence and the Reform of International Institutions*, and then went on to become assistant secretary of the Treasury for international affairs — another instance, in the Badlands account, of a Trilateralist obtaining the office needed to enact his own commission recommendations.[1]
