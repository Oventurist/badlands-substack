# Steve Descano

**Steve Descano** is the Commonwealth's Attorney for Fairfax County, Virginia, one of the progressive prosecutors elected in the wave of reform-minded district attorney campaigns of the late 2010s.

In May 2026 the [[department-of-justice]] announced an investigation into Descano's conduct, focused specifically on his handling of plea deals and sentencing. The department accused him of offering "preferential treatment" to migrant defendants, permitting a number of violent offenders to serve minimal jail time or none at all.

Badlands commentary raised the possibility that such a pattern reflects influence by a cartel or foreign entity seeking to subvert American civic order, and used the case to illustrate a structural peculiarity of Virginia law: the [[commonwealths-attorney]] is designated a Constitutional Officer, outside the executive branch and effectively unaccountable between quadrennial elections. Unlike most states, Virginia's governor cannot remove a prosecutor from a case even where a conflict of interest is manifest — a point the Badlands writer knew firsthand from a 2019 family lawsuit challenging that constitutional provision.

The Descano probe was reported alongside the [[fbi]] searches targeting State Senate President pro tempore [[louise-lucas]] as part of a widening federal focus on Virginia Democratic officeholders.
