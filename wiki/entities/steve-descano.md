# Steve Descano

Steve Descano is the Commonwealth's Attorney for Fairfax County, Virginia — the elected chief prosecutor for the state's most populous jurisdiction.[1]

## DOJ investigation

In May 2026 the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] announced an investigation into Descano's conduct, specifically his handling of plea deals and sentencing.[1] The DOJ accused him of offering "preferential treatment" to migrants, pointing to cases in which violent offenders received minimal jail time or none at all.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the obvious concern raised by such a pattern is the possibility that a prosecutor has been bought off by a cartel or foreign entity in order to subvert American society from within.[1] He situated the case within the broader structural problem of Virginia's [[virginia-commonwealths-attorney|Commonwealth's Attorney]] office, whose constitutional status leaves prosecutors effectively unaccountable between elections.[1] The Descano probe was reported in the same period as the FBI's searches targeting state Senate President pro tempore [[louise-lucas|L. Louise Lucas]].[1]
