# Agustin Carstens

Agustin Carstens is the head of the [[bank-for-international-settlements|Bank for International Settlements]] (BIS). In February 2023 he told Bloomberg TV that the battle between cryptocurrency and fiat currencies had ended in victory for fiat money: "That battle has been won … A technology doesn't make for trusted money."[1]

Carstens argued that "only the legal, historical infrastructure behind central banks can give great credibility" to money, and said he expected a "strong statement" from the G20 nations in favor of stronger regulation of digital assets, noting that crypto as a financial activity can only exist "under certain conditions."[1]

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] treated the remarks as an exercise of declaratory power by an international banking cartel rather than a neutral market observation, framing them within a longer history of campaigns against citizen-issued money such as [[colonial-scrip|Colonial Scrip]].[1]
