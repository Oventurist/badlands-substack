# Sherwin B. Nuland

Sherwin B. Nuland was an American surgeon and writer who taught bioethics, the history of medicine, and medicine at the Yale School of Medicine.[1] He is identified in Badlands Media's "Deep State Spotlight" profile of his daughter, [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]], as the source of her lifelong proximity to prestigious Ivy League circles.[1]

The profile treats his academic standing at [[yale-university|Yale]] as one element of a broader pattern in which the future diplomat was, in the author's phrasing, surrounded from birth by powerful individuals and evocative communicators — a milieu that also included her husband, neoconservative scholar [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]], himself a Yale graduate.[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] used this family and educational background to argue that Victoria Nuland's rise through the [[state-department|State Department]] followed the "hidden paths to power" characteristic of the American foreign policy establishment rather than any ordinary meritocratic ascent.[1]
