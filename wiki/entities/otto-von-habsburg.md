# Otto von Habsburg

Archduke Otto von Habsburg, described as the successor to the throne of the Holy Roman Emperor at the time of its dissolution, is presented in "Shadows, Sovereigns and Civil Wars – Part 2" as the aristocratic figure who carried Count [[richard-coudenhove-kalergi|Coudenhove-Kalergi]]'s project into the postwar era.[1]

Otto von Habsburg ran the [[pan-european-union|Pan-European Union]] for over thirty years.[1] He also created a sister organization, the [[dignitatis-humanae-institute|Dignitatis Humanae Institute]], whose stated purpose was to "unite the right of the world" under what the article characterizes as a gnostic Catholic veneer with a Clash of Civilizations rebranding aimed at the alt-right.[1] The institute is housed in an 800-year-old monastery at Trisulti in Italy and found a frontman in [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]].[1]

In a 2019 study, researcher Stan Ezrol listed Habsburg among Bannon's co-patrons of the Catholic group, alongside His Royal Highness Charles of Bourbon Two Sicilies and Duke of Castro, Field Marshal the Lord Guthrie, and Father Matthew Festing.[1] He belonged to the [[habsburg-dynasty|Habsburg dynasty]], the imperial house whose Austro-Hungarian state was dismantled after the First World War — the same collapse that left Coudenhove-Kalergi without a role and prompted his turn to pan-European organizing.[1]
