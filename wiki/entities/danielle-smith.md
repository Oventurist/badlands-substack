# Danielle Smith

Danielle Smith is the Premier of [[alberta|Alberta]], [[canada|Canada]], and the political figure most closely associated with the province's contemporary sovereignty movement.

## October 2026 referendum

As reported in the Badlands Brief edition "Canadian Separatists & Guerilla Campaigns," Smith announced that Alberta will hold an October referendum asking whether the province should begin the legal process toward a future binding independence vote — the [[alberta-independence-referendum|Alberta independence referendum]]. The announcement prompted a direct warning from Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]], who compared the separatist argument to what he called the "dangerous bluff" of [[brexit|Brexit]] and cautioned Alberta voters against treating separation as a mere negotiating tactic with Ottawa.

Alberta conservatives aligned with Smith responded that federal elites were attempting to frighten voters away from asserting provincial leverage and sovereignty, echoing the charge of "Project Fear" levelled against Carney by pro-Brexit campaigners in 2016. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] expressed support for the Alberta separatists and for what she termed "Canexit."
