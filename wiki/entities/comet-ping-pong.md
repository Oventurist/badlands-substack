# Comet Ping Pong

**Comet Ping Pong** is a Washington, D.C. pizzeria that sits at the center of the [[pizzagate|Pizzagate]] controversy, which grew out of the release of the [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] emails shortly before the 2016 election.[1] Badlands Media commentary describes the restaurant as the focal point of alleged connections between a food business and politicians and other people of power and influence.[1]

## The Pizzagate episode

According to Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]], the Podesta emails contained strange phrasing — terms such as "hot dogs," "cheese pizza," and "walnut sauce" — that online researchers, or Anons, came to interpret as coded references to pedophilia.[1] As those researchers began publicising a possible [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]] connection to Comet Ping Pong, an armed man arrived at the restaurant and fired a single round.[1]

Carlson emphasised that the single shot destroyed the restaurant's computer server, calling it a "magic bullet" and drawing attention to the improbability of that outcome.[1] After the shooting, mainstream outlets debunked the trafficking claims principally on the grounds that Comet Ping Pong has no basement, an argument Carlson characterised as a form of mind control that still persuades people today.[1]

Carlson also observed that CNN told audiences it was illegal for ordinary citizens to read the leaked Podesta emails and that only the mainstream media could lawfully examine them.[1]

## Related venues

Carlson contrasted the fame of Comet Ping Pong with the comparatively obscure [[standard-hotel|Standard Hotel]] in Los Angeles, which also operated a ping pong bar, prompting his rhetorical question about the recurring association between ping pong and pedophiles.[1]
