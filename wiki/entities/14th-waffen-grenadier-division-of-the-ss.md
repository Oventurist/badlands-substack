# 14th Waffen Grenadier Division of the SS

The 14th Waffen Grenadier Division of the SS was a Ukrainian volunteer formation of Nazi Germany's Waffen-SS during the Second World War.[1] The Friends of [[simon-wiesenthal-center|Simon Wiesenthal Center]] has described it as "a Nazi military unit whose crimes against humanity during the Holocaust are well-documented."[1]

## Role in the 2023 Canadian Parliament controversy

The division returned to public attention in September 2023 after the Canadian [[house-of-commons-of-canada|House of Commons]] gave two standing ovations to 98-year-old [[yaroslav-hunka|Yaroslav Hunka]], a guest present for the visit of Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]].[1] Hunka was introduced as a "war hero" who had fought against Soviet invasion; he had done so as a member of the 14th Waffen Grenadier Division of the SS.[1] Speaker [[anthony-rota|Anthony Rota]] and Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]] were both in attendance.[1]

Following the resulting outcry, members of Canada's Parliament sought to erase the incident from the official record, including video records and the original text of Rota's remarks — a move blocked by House conservatives.[1] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] characterised the attempt as a Stalinist "memory-holing" of inconvenient historical information and argued the underlying issue was Nazi influence within Ukraine and the purpose of the proxy war.[1]
