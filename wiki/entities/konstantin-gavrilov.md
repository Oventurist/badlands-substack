# Konstantin Gavrilov

Konstantin Gavrilov is a Russian diplomat who serves as [[russia|Russia]]'s chief delegate to the Vienna talks on military security and arms control.[1]

## Statements on Ukraine, June 2024

In the aftermath of the [[2024-swiss-peace-summit-on-ukraine|Swiss peace summit on Ukraine]] held at Bürgenstock, Gavrilov stated in an interview on the Rossiya-24 television channel that the next formal proposal Russia would put on the table would be a document concerning the Kyiv government's surrender.[1] "There are points of no return. The next thing that Russia will have to offer will be the document about [the Kiev government's] surrender," he said.[1] He described [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s proposals of the preceding week as "realistic" and argued that they could end the war if they were embraced.[1]

Gavrilov's remarks accompanied a broader Kremlin messaging campaign against the summit, in which foreign ministry spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] argued that the Alpine gathering had produced zero results despite the participation of almost 100 countries, and that the attempt to draw in the Global South had failed.[1]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the Kremlin's post-summit posture, including Gavrilov's surrender-document remark, as evidence that the Bürgenstock event was the first major geopolitical defeat for what he termed the Globalist Empire in the War of Sovereignty, and as a demonstration of the growing coherence of the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]].[1]
