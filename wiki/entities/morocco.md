# Morocco

Morocco is a North African kingdom whose claim to [[western-sahara|Western Sahara]] received renewed United States backing in April 2025.[1]

## Western Sahara claim

Morocco de facto controls roughly 80% of Western Sahara, which it annexed shortly after Spain relinquished control of the desert territory in 1975.[1] The claim has been a source of regional tension and a decades-long conflict between Rabat and the Algerian-backed [[polisario-front|Polisario Front]], a separatist group that supports a self-determination referendum originally proposed by the United Nations in 1991 as part of a ceasefire agreement but stalled ever since.[1]

## US and international recognition

Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] declared that Washington recognizes Moroccan sovereignty over Western Sahara and supports the kingdom's "serious, credible, and realistic" autonomy plan as the only basis for a solution, making the remarks in a meeting with Moroccan Foreign Minister [[nasser-bourita|Nasser Bourita]] according to a [[state-department|State Department]] press release.[1] The United States had previously affirmed Moroccan sovereignty over the non-self-governing territory, and its ally [[israel|Israel]] did the same in 2023.[1] France reversed its longstanding neutrality in 2024 and endorsed the autonomy plan after years of strained relations with Rabat, with President Emmanuel Macron calling the proposal the only viable route to resolving the dispute.[1]
