# David Lebryk

David Lebryk was a career civil servant and senior official at the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|US Department of the Treasury]] who resisted allowing representatives of the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]] into the department's federal payment system in late January 2025.[1]

## Departure

Following a standoff over access to the payment system — which disburses money on behalf of the entire federal government — Lebryk was put on leave and then abruptly retired on Friday, January 31, 2025, according to people familiar with his exit.[1] Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] subsequently granted DOGE representatives access to the system late that same day, handing [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the team he was leading a powerful tool to monitor and potentially limit government spending.[1]

Reporting noted that the system could give the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration another mechanism to attempt to unilaterally restrict disbursement of money appropriated by Congress for specific purposes, a push that had already met legal roadblocks.[1]
