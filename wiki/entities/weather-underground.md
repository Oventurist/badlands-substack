# Weather Underground

The Weather Underground was a bomb-building militant organisation of the American "New Left" that emerged in the years after 1968.[1] Badlands Media presents it as the archetype of what replaced [[martin-luther-king-jr|Martin Luther King Jr.]]'s civil rights movement once covert intelligence operations had displaced that movement's disciplined practice of peaceful civil disobedience.[1]

In this account, the transplanting of King's movement with armed and violence-prone operatives running organisations like the Weather Underground — described as having "littered bombs (and STDs) across America" — was not a spontaneous radicalisation but the intended product of programmes such as [[cointelpro|COINTELPRO]], under which FBI-funded radicals took over from the nonviolent leadership that had been assassinated.[1]

## Ideological legacy

Badlands argues that the rise of the drug-loving anarchists of the New Left, many of whom later became leading figures of the contemporary establishment, produced a durable new ethic that equated the whole of Western civilisation — the space programme, atomic technology, the American constitution and Western values generally — with the evils of the [[vietnam-war|Vietnam war]], corporate greed and the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]].[1] That conflation, in this reading, disarmed a generation of any positive programme and left only nihilism where the Kennedy–King agenda of peace and development had stood.[1]
