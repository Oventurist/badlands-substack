# Dmitry Polyansky

Dmitry Polyansky is the Russian deputy representative to the United Nations, who in August 2025 accused [[ukraine|Ukraine]] of subversive activity in Africa and called for an international investigation.[1]

## Allegations of Ukrainian subversion in the Sahel

Polyansky made the allegations on a Wednesday during a [[united-nations-security-council|UN Security Council]] briefing on threats to international peace and security, according to an official statement.[1] He said Ukraine was providing weapons, drones and training for militants to stage coordinated attacks on governments in Africa's Sahel region.[1] "There are concrete facts that clearly demonstrate that the Ukrainian special services, including the Defense Intelligence of Ukraine, have been involved in subversive activities in the Sahel countries and other regions of Africa," he stated, adding that "what the Kiev regime is doing on the African continent is something that deserves our particular attention" and calling for an investigation into Ukrainian actions.[1]

Polyansky commended the work of the UN Office of Counter-Terrorism and the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive Directorate, but criticized the UN Secretary-General's latest report on the threat posed by [[isis|Islamic State]] in Africa.[1]
