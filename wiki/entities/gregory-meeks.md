# Gregory Meeks

Gregory W. Meeks is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from New York and a leading Democratic voice on foreign affairs.[1]

## Proposed shadow secretary of state

In November 2024, Representative [[wiley-nickel|Wiley Nickel]] of North Carolina floated a plan for congressional Democrats to constitute a British-style shadow cabinet mirroring the Cabinet of the incoming second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1] Reporting on the proposal named Meeks as an example of a member who might serve as shadow secretary of state, in which role he would "loudly oppose" any action by the incoming administration that decreased U.S. support for Ukraine in its war with Russia.[1] The pairing reflected Meeks's standing as the senior House Democrat on foreign policy and the centrality of Ukraine aid to Democratic objections to the incoming administration.[1]

Badlands Media's commentary rejected the shadow cabinet concept outright, framing it as an attempt to prevent a duly elected government from functioning rather than as a legitimate accountability mechanism.[1]
