# Lachlan Murdoch

Lachlan Murdoch is the son of media magnate [[rupert-murdoch|Rupert Murdoch]] and his successor at the head of the family's media holdings, including [[fox-news|Fox News]].[1]

## The September 2023 succession

The announcement that Rupert Murdoch was stepping down and handing power to his son came in the same news cycle as the report that [[fox-business|Fox Business]] had aired a misleading graphic about a Trump–Biden poll.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the coincidence and characterized the incoming heir as an "even-more-communist son," rendering his name as "Lochland" in the commentary.[1]

The succession was treated in Badlands coverage as confirmation that Fox's drift away from the audience [[roger-ailes|Roger Ailes]] built the network to serve would continue rather than reverse, with the network's "prime directive" described as manufacturing consent.[1]
