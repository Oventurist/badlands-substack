# Raúl Salinas

Raúl Salinas de Gortari, brother of Mexican president [[carlos-salinas|Carlos Salinas]], went to prison for his role in a murder.[1] Federal Judge Ricardo Ojeda Bohórquez wrote in explaining his verdict that evidence "logically and judicially interwoven" proved Salinas was "the intellectual author" of the September 1994 killing of [[jose-francisco-ruiz-massieu|José Francisco Ruiz Massieu]], secretary general and second-ranking official of the [[institutional-revolutionary-party|Institutional Revolutionary Party]], who had once been married to Adriana Salinas, sister of Raúl and Carlos.[1]

The article states that Raúl was the cartel-connected member of the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]] who granted "green light protection" to [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]] drug shipments moving through Mexico.[1] Cartel money then paid [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]] — the victim's own brother — to alter witness statements deleting references to Raúl's role in the killing.[1] Raúl is also noted as having had a private banker at Citibank.[1]
