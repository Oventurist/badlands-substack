# Raúl Salinas

Raúl Salinas is the brother of Mexican President Carlos Salinas de Gortari and a central figure in the corruption narrative of the [[salinas-family|Salinas family]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Federal Judge Ricardo Ojeda Bohórquez ruled that evidence "logically and judicially interwoven" proved Salinas was "the intellectual author" of the September 1994 killing of [[jose-francisco-ruiz-massieu|José Francisco Ruiz Massieu]], secretary general and second-ranking official of the ruling [[pri|Institutional Revolutionary Party]] and once the husband of Raúl's sister Adriana.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) He was imprisoned for the murder.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

Mexican investigators subsequently found that [[mario-ruiz-massieu|Mario Ruiz Massieu]], the victim's brother and the special prosecutor appointed by President Carlos Salinas, had altered witnesses' statements to delete numerous references to Raúl's role in the slaying — an act the article attributes to payments from the [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) Raúl is described as the official who gave the Gulf Cartel's drugs "green light protection" through Mexico, and as a holder of a private Citibank banking relationship.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]] is described as one of several drug traffickers' friends including Salinas.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
