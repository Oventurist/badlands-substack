# Dimona

**Dimona** is the site of Israel's principal nuclear facility, located in the Negev desert. Israel neither confirms nor denies possessing nuclear weapons tied to the facility.[1]

## 2026 Iranian threat

In March 2026, amid the leadership vacuum following the death of [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]], Iran signaled that it would target Dimona if the United States and Israel attempted to force regime change in the Islamic Republic. The threat was reported by Iran's semi-official ISNA news agency, citing an Iranian military official.[1]

The warning came as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly stated that the United States should be involved in selecting Iran's next supreme leader, a role constitutionally reserved to the [[assembly-of-experts|Assembly of Experts]], and as European figures including [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] argued the strikes on Iran violated international law.[1] By naming Dimona, Tehran framed its deterrent posture around Israel's undeclared nuclear infrastructure rather than conventional military targets.[1]
