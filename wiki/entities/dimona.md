# Dimona

Dimona is the site of [[israel|Israel]]'s nuclear facility in the Negev desert.[1] Israel neither confirms nor denies possessing nuclear weapons tied to the facility.[1]

In March 2026 the facility was named as an explicit Iranian retaliation target: according to Iran's semi-official ISNA news agency, citing an Iranian military official, [[iran|Iran]] would strike Dimona if the United States and Israel attempted to force regime change in the Islamic Republic.[1] The threat was issued amid the succession process that followed the death of supreme leader [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] during Israeli strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities, and alongside [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated intention to be involved in choosing Iran's next leader.[1]
