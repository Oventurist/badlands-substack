# Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR)

The Foreign Intelligence Service, commonly abbreviated SVR, is Russia's civilian foreign-intelligence agency. It is headed by [[sergey-naryshkin|Sergey Naryshkin]], whose public statements serve as the principal channel for Russian intelligence claims about Western covert activity in the Ukraine war.[1]

## Kursk sabotage claims

The SVR alleged in 2024 that intelligence in its possession showed a planned "terrorist attack" against the [[kursk-nuclear-power-plant|Kursk Nuclear Power Plant]] that would have involved seizing and mining the facility.[1] The agency attributed preparation of the Ukrainian sabotage and reconnaissance groups involved to Western services, naming British [[mi6|MI6]] as the primary actor, and said a related operation targeted the transmission lines linking Russian nuclear plants to the national grid.[1] Naryshkin disclosed the allegations at a gathering of post-Soviet intelligence and security chiefs in Astana.[1]

## Significance in Badlands coverage

Badlands commentary used the SVR's disclosure to argue that state-sponsored terrorism is generated by the very agencies that claim to police it, framing US and UK civilian intelligence as manufacturers of the non-state violence that justifies their existence.[1]
