# Badlands Marketplace

**Badlands Marketplace** is an online business directory operated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] at a subdomain of [[badlandsmedia-tv|BadlandsMedia.tv]], introduced alongside the main site in the March 28, 2023 announcement post.[1]

## Concept

The Marketplace is pitched to readers "looking to shop with America First businesses who share your values."[1] Badlands Media states that it believes small businesses are "the heartbeat of America" and that "America's new economy is growing by the day," presenting the Marketplace as a place where Patriot-owned businesses "can proudly hang their shingle and connect with like-minded customers."[1]

## Rationale

The accompanying appeal is explicitly one of consumer realignment: readers are urged to "stop financially supporting companies that don't share our values" and instead to connect with businesses "big and small, local and online" through the directory, in order to help what the organization calls the [[america-first-movement|America First]] economy grow.[1] The initiative fits the wider pattern of parallel-economy building within the movement, in which political affinity is treated as a basis for commercial networking.[1]
