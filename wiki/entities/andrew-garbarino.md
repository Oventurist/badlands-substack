# Andrew Garbarino

Andrew Garbarino is a Republican member of the US House of Representatives from New York who serves as chairman of the House Homeland Security Committee.[1]

In February 2026 Garbarino commented publicly on personnel turmoil at the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]], saying it was "good" that a reported attempt to move chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]] out of his role had failed.[1] Costello nevertheless announced his departure from the agency the following month, after receiving transfer orders for possible reassignment.[1]

## Chairmanship of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Protection Subcommittee

Garbarino (R-NY) chaired the [[house-committee-on-homeland-security|House Committee on Homeland Security]]'s Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Protection Subcommittee, with Representative [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]] (D-CA) as ranking member, four additional Republicans and three additional Democrats.[2] On February 6, 2024 he presided over the hearing "Securing Operational Technology: A Deep Dive into the Water Sector."[2]

In his opening remarks Garbarino said that during his tenure the committee had "made great strides" on securing [[operational-technology|operational technology]], but that "given recent incidents, we must revisit this topic to consider how Congress may further refine and strike the support to critical infrastructure owners and operators."[2] He cited late-2023 cyber activity against OT devices in multiple sectors, including water and wastewater systems, by Iranian-affiliated cyber actors — a reference to the November 2023 intrusion at Pennsylvania's [[aliquippa-municipal-authority|Aliquippa Municipal Authority]].[2]
