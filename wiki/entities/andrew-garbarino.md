# Andrew Garbarino

**Andrew Garbarino** (R-N.Y.) is chairman of the US House Homeland Security Committee, which exercises oversight of the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] and its component agencies, including the [[cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]].[1]

## CISA oversight

In February 2026 Garbarino publicly commented on internal personnel disputes at CISA, saying it was "good" that a reported attempt to move chief information officer [[robert-costello|Robert Costello]] out of his role had failed.[1] Costello nonetheless announced his departure from the agency the following month, after receiving transfer orders for possible reassignment.[1] Garbarino's intervention illustrated congressional attention to leadership instability at CISA at a time when the agency had an acting director, [[nick-andersen|Nick Andersen]], and a stalled nomination for permanent leadership in [[sean-plankey|Sean Plankey]].[1]
