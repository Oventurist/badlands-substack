# Edward VII

**Edward VII** (King of the United Kingdom, 1901–1910) is presented in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the personal architect of the diplomatic encirclement of Germany that culminated in the First World War.[1]

## Geopolitical role

A 1996 EIR report quoted in the article states that the entente diplomacy "was masterminded and carried out by King Edward VII personally, with the various British ministers, cabinets, round tables, and other apparatus merely following in his wake," and that Edward held "a geopolitical vision in the Venetian tradition" of "brutal simplicity: the encirclement of Germany with a hostile coalition, followed by a war of annihilation" in which allies such as France and Russia would also be crippled.[1]

The threat Edward is said to have been responding to was the global spread of the [[american-system|American System]] of political economy — the German Zollverein, Meiji Japan's protective tariffs and national banking, [[sergei-witte|Sergei Witte]]'s [[trans-siberian-railway|Trans-Siberian Railway]], and the Franco-Russian industrial diplomacy of Sadi Carnot and Gabriel Hanotaux — which was eroding the monopoly power of the [[british-empire|British Empire]].[1]

Under his direction, according to the article, came the [[entente-cordiale|Entente Cordiale]] of April 8, 1904 and by 1907 the [[triple-entente|Triple Entente]].[1] A *Daily Mail* editorial of December 14, 1909 stated that "the king and his councillors have strained every nerve to establish Ententes with Russia and with Italy; and have formed an Entente with France, and as well with Japan. Why? To isolate Germany."[1] Kaiser [[wilhelm-ii|Wilhelm II]] lamented in August 1914 that the encirclement had been "slowly and cleverly set going by Edward VII," adding: "Edward VII is stronger after his death than am I who am still alive!"[1]

## Middle East networks

The article also credits Edward's Palestine Expedition Fund, established in 1865, and the [[quatuor-coronati-lodge|Quatuor Coronati]] freemasonic "research lodge" founded in 1886, with organizing the networks of British agents posing as Arabists who infiltrated Bedouin tribes, mapped terrain and prepared the ground for the [[sykes-picot-agreement|Sykes-Picot]] carving up of the [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman Empire]].[1] Part of the lodge's mandate is described as locating Solomon's Temple in order to set the stage for an End Times Zionist cult centered on building a third temple.[1]
