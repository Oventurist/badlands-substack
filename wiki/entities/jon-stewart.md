# Jon Stewart

Jon Stewart is an American comedian and television commentator whose remarks on the aftermath of the June 27, 2024 presidential debate were singled out by Badlands Media as an example of legacy-media complicity in concealing President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s condition.[1]

## Commentary on the Biden debate fallout

In July 2024, Stewart delivered what Badlands characterized as a "fake takedown" of the Biden campaign and prominent Democrats, arguing that "an arthritic status quo, unable or unwilling to respond, in any way, to the concerns of voters who just received new and urgent information about their candidate, also erodes confidence and faith in the system of government."[1]

## Badlands criticism

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded that if Biden's decline was genuinely "new and urgent" information for Stewart's audience, that was itself evidence that Stewart had been complicit in the cover-up and had intentionally failed to keep viewers informed about an obvious condition for political purposes.[1] The commentary placed Stewart within a broader pattern in which media figures were said to be scrambling to distance themselves from a deception that their own silence had made possible, by pretending that Biden's physical feebleness and apparent dementia constituted brand-new information.[1]

## New York property overvaluation controversy

In March 2024 Stewart criticized [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s New York civil real-estate case, rejecting the framing that overvaluing properties was a victimless offense.[2] On his Monday-night "Daily Show" broadcast he replayed CNN's Laura Coates interviewing "Shark Tank" star [[kevin-oleary|Kevin O'Leary]], who argued that the ruling had not gone over well with a real estate industry fearing it would be targeted next and that asset inflation "is done by every real estate developer everywhere on Earth in every city" without prosecution.[2] Stewart responded, "How is he not this mad about overvaluations in the real world? Because they are not victimless crimes," adding that "money isn't infinite. A loan that goes to the liar doesn't go to someone who's giving a more honest evaluation. So the system becomes incentivized for corruption."[2]

Internet sleuths then examined Stewart's own property history, and the New York Post confirmed records showing that his New York City penthouse sold for 829% more than its assessed value — using, per the reporting, the same citation method and metric that New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]] applied to Trump's properties.[2] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that hypocrisy from famous regime narrative-peddlers was unsurprising but that the Stewart disclosure was an escalation of a point Trump, developers and bankers had made since the case was filed, arguing that by Stewart's own standard he should be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law and asking how many honest loan applicants his inflated valuation had disenfranchised.[2]

## Interview with Mikie Sherrill (November 2025)

Speaking to Stewart in November 2025 — the same day she gave up her House seat — New Jersey Governor-elect [[mikie-sherrill|Mikie Sherrill]] said she thinks "all the time" about withholding federal tax dollars from the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and called it a "great idea."[3] "If they're not gonna run the programs, then what are we paying them for?" she told Stewart. "It's like, you know, you're paying us for a service and they're not delivering. So let's stop paying for it."[3]
