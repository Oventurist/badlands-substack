# Jon Stewart

Jon Stewart is an American comedian and television commentator whose remarks on the aftermath of the June 27, 2024 presidential debate were singled out by Badlands Media as an example of legacy-media complicity in concealing President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s condition.[1]

## Commentary on the Biden debate fallout

In July 2024, Stewart delivered what Badlands characterized as a "fake takedown" of the Biden campaign and prominent Democrats, arguing that "an arthritic status quo, unable or unwilling to respond, in any way, to the concerns of voters who just received new and urgent information about their candidate, also erodes confidence and faith in the system of government."[1]

## Badlands criticism

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded that if Biden's decline was genuinely "new and urgent" information for Stewart's audience, that was itself evidence that Stewart had been complicit in the cover-up and had intentionally failed to keep viewers informed about an obvious condition for political purposes.[1] The commentary placed Stewart within a broader pattern in which media figures were said to be scrambling to distance themselves from a deception that their own silence had made possible, by pretending that Biden's physical feebleness and apparent dementia constituted brand-new information.[1]
