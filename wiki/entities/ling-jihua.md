# Ling Jihua

Ling Jihua is described in the Badlands Media feature "Empire's Enigma" as a top aide to former Chinese president [[hu-jintao|Hu Jintao]] and one of six party leaders removed early in [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]]'s rule for alleged coup attempts.[1]

## Removal

His removal is listed together with those of [[zhou-yongkang|Zhou Yongkang]], [[bo-xilai|Bo Xilai]], and generals [[xu-caihou|Xu Caihou]], [[guo-boxiong|Guo Boxiong]] and [[sun-zhengcai|Sun Zhengcai]].[1] Because Ling was closely identified with the outgoing Hu administration, the article treats his fall as part of Xi's demolition of the patronage networks that the Journal of Democracy described Hu as having allowed senior [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] figures to build.[1]
