# Francis Chipimo

Francis Chipimo is the deputy governor for operations of the [[bank-of-zambia|Bank of Zambia]].[1] In July 2024 he announced, in a speech at a trade fair in the city of Ndola, that individuals caught using foreign currency for local transactions under the central bank's draft currency regulations could face up to ten years in prison or substantial fines.[1]

The rules he described would require the [[zambian-kwacha|kwacha]] and its subunit, the ngwee, to be used for all domestic public and private transactions in [[zambia|Zambia]], and were framed by the central bank as a response to dollarization undermining its monetary policy tools and pressuring the exchange rate.[1]
