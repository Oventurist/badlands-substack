# Center for Economic and Policy Research

The **Center for Economic and Policy Research (CEPR)** is a Washington-based research institute cited by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] for its assessment of the human cost of United States sanctions on [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

CEPR estimates 40,000 excess deaths between 2017 and 2019 attributable to the sanctions regime.[1] DeLarme notes that the [[state-department|State Department]] disputes these figures but has never independently refuted them.[1] The estimate appears in his account alongside the collapse of [[pdvsa|PDVSA]] output from 3.5 million barrels per day under [[hugo-chavez|Hugo Chávez]] to 300,000 in 2020.[1]
