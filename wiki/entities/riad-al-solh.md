# Riad Al Solh

Riad Al Solh was the first Prime Minister of Lebanon and, through Alwaleed's Lebanese mother, a grandfather of [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]].[1]

## Significance in the Badlands account

Badlands Media presents Alwaleed's descent from two founding figures — Al Solh in Lebanon and [[abdulaziz-bin-abdul-rahman-al-saud|King Abdulaziz]], the first king of Saudi Arabia — as the source of an unusually broad set of elite contacts that helped make him a global power broker.[1] The article argues Alwaleed would become far more powerful than either grandfather, drawing a distinction between kings and prime ministers, whose power is bounded by their countries, and the small group of "puppet masters" said to control kings, prime ministers and presidents alike.[1] Alwaleed's mixed Lebanese and Armenian lineage is also cited as the reason he could never ascend to the Saudi throne, since princes are expected to possess an entirely Arab family tree.[1]
