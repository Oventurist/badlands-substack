# Jared Harrison

Jared Harrison was the defendant in the Oklahoma federal case in which the statutory ban on firearms possession by marijuana users was ruled unconstitutional in February 2023.[1]

U.S. District Judge [[patrick-wyrick|Patrick Wyrick]] ruled in Harrison's favor in a 54-page opinion handed down on Friday, February 3, 2023, holding that the government could not argue that Harrison's "mere status as a user of marijuana justifies stripping him of his fundamental right to possess a firearm."[1] The decision was framed by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] as part of a widening line of rulings against gun restrictions following the Supreme Court's decision in [[new-york-state-rifle-and-pistol-association-v-bruen|New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen]].[1]
