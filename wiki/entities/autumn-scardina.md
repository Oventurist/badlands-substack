# Autumn Scardina

**Autumn Scardina** is a Colorado transgender attorney and the plaintiff in the long-running discrimination case against Christian baker [[jack-phillips|Jack Phillips]] and his Lakewood bakery, [[masterpiece-cakeshop|Masterpiece Cakeshop]].[1] Scardina requested a pink-and-blue cake to celebrate her gender transition, which Phillips declined to make on the ground that doing so would violate his Christian faith.[1]

On October 8, 2024, the [[colorado-supreme-court|Colorado Supreme Court]] declined in a 4–3 decision to reach the merits of the dispute and instead dismissed the case on procedural grounds.[1] Scardina's attorney, John McHugh, expressed disappointment and said he was evaluating whether any legal options remained, stating that the court "decided to avoid the merits of this issue by inventing an argument no party raised."[1]
