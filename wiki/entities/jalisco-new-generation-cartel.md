# Jalisco New Generation Cartel

The Jalisco New Generation Cartel (Cártel de Jalisco Nueva Generación, CJNG) is a Mexican drug trafficking organization based in Jalisco state, long led by [[el-mencho|Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes]], alias "El Mencho."[1]

## The February 2026 Jalisco raid

Mexican military forces killed El Mencho during an operation in Jalisco state in late February 2026, an action in which a reported 25 members of the Mexican national guard were also killed.[1] In the aftermath, violence erupted across parts of western [[mexico|Mexico]], including vehicle burnings and road blockades attributed to cartel retaliation, and security forces remained deployed in Jalisco and surrounding areas amid concerns of further attacks.[1]

Separate reporting indicated that roughly 100 cartel leaders — including El Mencho's brother — had been quietly transferred from Mexican to US custody by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration before the raid.[1]

## Context in Badlands commentary

Badlands analysis situated the CJNG operation within a broader hemispheric campaign against cartel networks, crediting pressure from Colombian President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], Venezuelan President [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]], and El Salvador's Bukele with degrading cartel infrastructure, while noting Petro's call for President Trump to target the bankers and financiers behind the cartels.[1] Related organizations covered elsewhere in the corpus include the [[sinaloa-cartel|Sinaloa Cartel]] and [[gulf-cartel|Gulf Cartel]].[1]

## Reprisals after the Tapalpa operation

Subsequent Badlands coverage placed the operation that killed El Mencho in Tapalpa, Jalisco, and reported that several cartel members were killed and Mexican troops injured, with authorities seizing heavy weapons and armored vehicles.[2] CJNG factions launched violent reprisals across multiple states, including road blockades, vehicle burnings and attacks on public infrastructure, with at least 14 people reported killed in the unrest.[2]

The [[state-department|U.S. Department of State]] issued shelter-in-place warnings for American citizens in Jalisco and other affected states, while local officials activated "code red" alerts, canceled public events and suspended classes.[2] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that the forces seizing control of urban areas — erecting barricades of burned cars and buses and attacking sites including a hospital — were CJNG rather than the Mexican military, and that images circulating online showed the cartel better equipped than most of the world's militaries.[2]

He speculated that the cartel might escalate across the U.S. border or attempt to trigger a civil war in Mexico as a way to destabilize the United States and draw in the U.S. military, and asked which figures in Washington, on Wall Street and in the banking sector are connected to the cartels.[2] He also recalled that the Mexican Senate had already voted to permit SEAL Team 2 to train Mexican special forces to fight the cartels.[2]
