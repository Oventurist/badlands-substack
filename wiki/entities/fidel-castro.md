# Fidel Castro

Fidel Castro was the Cuban revolutionary who took power in 1959 and led [[cuba|Cuba]] as prime minister and later president for nearly half a century, founding the political dynasty that his brother [[raul-castro|Raul Castro]] continued.

In the Badlands corpus he functions chiefly as a chronological anchor. The March 16, 2026 Badlands Brief notes that [[miguel-diaz-canel|Miguel Diaz-Canel]] is the first non-Castro family member to lead Cuba since Fidel Castro became its leader in 1959 — a fact the Brief uses to underline the significance of reports that a Castro descendant, [[raulito-castro|Raulito Castro]], may be restored to the leadership through negotiations with the Trump administration.
