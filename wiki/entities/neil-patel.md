# Neil Patel

Neil Patel is an American media executive and former White House adviser who, in July 2023, was reported by The Wall Street Journal to be partnering with former Fox News host [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] to launch a new media company.[1]

## The 2023 media venture

According to people familiar with the matter cited by the Journal, Carlson and Patel were seeking to raise hundreds of millions of dollars for a venture that would potentially use [[twitter|Twitter]] as its backbone.[1] The company was to be anchored by longer versions of the free videos Carlson had been posting regularly on Twitter following his departure from [[fox-news|Fox News]], with revenue ultimately driven by subscriptions.[1] Under the reported model, users of Twitter and other platforms would continue to see free, shorter versions of Carlson's show, interviews and documentaries, but would need to subscribe to watch them in their entirety; additional hosts were expected to be added over time.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] framed the venture within a longer history of media consolidation by wealthy interests, arguing that figures such as William Randolph Hearst and Andrew Carnegie built information networks that shaped worldviews rather than merely reporting events, and that a Carlson–Patel subscription platform built on a post-acquisition Twitter represented a direct challenge to that structure.[1]
