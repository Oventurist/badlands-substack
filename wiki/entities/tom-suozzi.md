# Tom Suozzi

Tom Suozzi is a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from New York and a member of the Committee on Ways and Means' Subcommittee on Tax.[1]

## Questioning on the 2025 reconciliation bill

Before the House passed [[donald-trump|President Trump]]'s "big, beautiful bill," Suozzi questioned [[thomas-barthold|Thomas Barthold]], chief of staff of the [[joint-committee-on-taxation|Joint Committee on Taxation]], about the reconciliation package's provisions.[1] Suozzi used the exchange to highlight gaps between Trump's campaign promises and the bill's text, quoting the president's pledge that "your tips will be 100 percent yours" and asking whether the legislation continued the payroll tax on tips.[1] Barthold answered, "Uh, yes, yes it does."[1]

The exchange became a focal point of criticism that the bill fell short of Trump's promises on [[tax-free-tips|tips]], [[no-tax-on-overtime|overtime]] and Social Security taxes.[1] The bill passed the House by a single vote and moved to the [[us-senate|Senate]].[1]
