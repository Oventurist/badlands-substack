# Hashemites

The Hashemites — the Banu ("clan") Hashim — are the descendants of [[hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim]] and of the Prophet [[muhammad|Muhammad]], and one of the most consequential dynasties in Arabian history.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Lineage and standing

The Banu Hashim were part of the [[quraysh|Quraysh]] tribe, which controlled the town of Makkah (Mecca) in the era before the Prophet.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Sunni Muslims have expanded the meaning of "Ahl al-Bayt" (People of the House of the Prophet) to include all members of the Banu Hashim, whereas Shia Muslims limit the group to Muhammad's cousin, daughter, son-in-law, two grandsons, and eleven wives.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Historically most caliphs of Islam were either sharifs (descendants of Muhammad) or members of the Banu Hashim — but the modern "throne" of the Muslim world, held by the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]], is held by neither.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Territory and the modern era

[[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], Jordan, and Syria together comprise the geographic area the Hashemites considered the true Arabia, for which they sought sovereignty from both the [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman Empire]] and the British Crown during the First World War.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Today the Hashemites control only Jordan.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The 1916 Arab Revolt leaders — King Hussein and his son Prince Faisal — supplied the definition of "Arabia" the author uses in his own mapping of the region.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

The [[emirate-of-diriyah|First State of Saud]] collapsed in 1814 precisely in an attempt to seize the Hashemite kingdom of Hejaz from Ottoman control.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The article's promised sequel treats the Hashemite struggle for independence, what it calls the British Cabal's betrayal of the Hashemites, and the usurpation of Hashemite stewardship over the Islamic holy lands by [[british-intelligence|British Intelligence]] via Abdulaziz bin Abdul Rahman Al Saud.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
