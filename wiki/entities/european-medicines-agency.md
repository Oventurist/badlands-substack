# European Medicines Agency

The European Medicines Agency (EMA) is the European Union's medicines regulator, responsible for the scientific evaluation and supervision of medicinal products, including the COVID-19 vaccines authorized during the pandemic.[1]

## Major objection over truncated mRNA

Reporting by [[the-epoch-times|The Epoch Times]], summarized in the Badlands News Brief of February 22, 2023, stated that [[pfizer|Pfizer]]'s COVID-19 vaccine contains truncated mRNA and that the EMA flagged this as the basis for a formal "major objection" — a designation indicating a preclusion of approval.[1] According to the summary, Pfizer had not investigated the detrimental outcomes of truncated mRNA in its vaccines.[1]

The reporting further alleged that Pfizer submitted Western blot figures to both the EMA and the [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] that were digitally generated rather than derived from actual experiments, and that health authorities had shown an alarming lack of action on the issue.[1] Truncated mRNA was said to potentially contribute to multiple vaccine-related injuries, including misfolded spike protein-induced fibrous blood clots, autoimmune disorders and cancer, and to explain drastic batch-to-batch product quality variation.[1]
