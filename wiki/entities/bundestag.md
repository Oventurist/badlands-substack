# Bundestag

The Bundestag is the federal parliament of Germany.[1] It appears in the Badlands corpus principally through the activity of its committees and members on questions of online speech regulation.[1]

## European policy committee and social media

In September 2024, the chairman of the Bundestag's European policy committee, Green Party MP [[anton-hofreiter|Anton Hofreiter]], called for tighter control over social media, "up to the outright blocking of certain platforms," in remarks to the Funke Media Group.[1] Hofreiter argued that "one of the biggest problems of extremism is online radicalization" and that the dissemination of "anti-constitutional content on the Internet" must be stopped.[1] The proposals were widely read as targeting [[x-corp|X]] and [[telegram|Telegram]].[1]

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] characterized the intervention as part of a broader European move against non-compliant platforms, following the arrest of Telegram CEO [[pavel-durov|Pavel Durov]] in France.[1]

## Motion to exclude the AfD (2024)

In November 2024, 112 Bundestag members from across the political spectrum pushed an [[afd-ban-effort|effort to exclude]] the [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]] from the February 23 snap election, submitting a motion citing the party's allegedly "anti-democratic" nature to the Bundestag president with the potential to escalate to Germany's Constitutional Court.[2] Proponents, including figures from the ruling SPD, argued the step was needed to protect German democracy, while the party's supporters called it an undemocratic attempt to silence a rising political force.[2]
