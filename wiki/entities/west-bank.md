# West Bank

The **West Bank** is the Palestinian territory east of the Green Line that has been under Israeli military control since the 1967 [[six-day-war|Six Day War]].[1] It featured prominently in Badlands Media's August 2024 coverage of Israeli operations and displacement policy.[1]

## 2024 anti-terror raid

In late August 2024 the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] launched a major anti-terror raid in the northern West Bank in the wake of an attempted suicide bombing in Tel Aviv.[1] Israeli forces operated in Jenin, Tulkarem and other areas, with the military saying they were working to dismantle an Iranian-backed terror network.[1] At least 11 Palestinians were reported killed in clashes and IDF drone strikes, and several wanted Palestinians were detained.[1]

Foreign Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]] called on Israel to consider temporarily relocating Palestinian civilians as part of the operation, saying the threat should be dealt with "exactly as we deal with terror infrastructure in Gaza," and calling the operation "a war in every sense."[1] Katz alleged that Iran was working to establish a terror front against Israel in the West Bank on the model it used in Lebanon and Gaza, by funding and arming terrorists and smuggling advanced weapons from Jordan.[1]

## Historical displacement

Badlands frames the 2024 evacuation proposal as a repetition of a long-standing pattern.[1] During the Six Day War, the IDF warned West Bank villages near the Israeli border to empty out ahead of bombing campaigns that never occurred; fences were erected and the villages declared part of Israel, displacing over 300,000 Palestinians in the episode known as the [[naksa|Naksa]].[1] Saudi demands that borders under the [[two-state-solution|Two-State Solution]] revert to pre-1967 conditions refer to this displacement.[1]

## Annexation deliberations (September 2025)

In September 2025 Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] was reported to be convening top ministers and aides to explore applying Israeli sovereignty to parts of the West Bank, a step tantamount to annexation.[2] Hebrew media reports said the meeting would focus on the implications of international recognition of a Palestinian state and Israel's possible responses, ahead of a [[united-nations-general-assembly|United Nations General Assembly]] session at which a wave of Western nations intended to recognize Palestinian statehood against Israeli and American wishes.[2] The [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]], which governs day-to-day affairs in parts of the territory, was simultaneously lobbying Washington to lift visa restrictions barring President [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] and some 80 other officials from the UN meeting.[2] Badlands commentary questioned the moral argument for annexing the West Bank, which it noted had nothing to do with the October 7 attacks originally cited to justify the Gaza campaign.[2]
