# Steven Kwast

Steven Kwast is a retired lieutenant general of the United States Air Force and a former Top Gun pilot.[1]

## Badlands appearance

Kwast appeared as a guest on episode 112 of Badlands *Story Hour*, in a discussion built around the film *Top Gun: Maverick*.[1] The Badlands host [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the conversation as using an archetypal story to dissect the evolution not only of modern warfare but of psychological warfare — a theme the host tied to what he called the "Shadow War" character of the second Trump administration and to the market turbulence surrounding [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s tariff policy.[1]

Burning Bright argued in the same segment that the tariff episode functioned as a form of "Trump quicksand," in his view exposing financial commentators, globalist analysts and establishment conservative media as poorly positioned readers of the administration's intentions.[1]
