# Marshall McLuhan

Marshall McLuhan (rendered "Marshall McCluhan" in the source) is listed in Badlands Media reporting among the theorists whose ideas the "new priests of the [[fourth-industrial-revolution|Fourth Industrial Revolution]]" followed in preaching their new gospel to humanity.[1]

He is grouped with [[julian-huxley|Sir Julian Huxley]], cybernetics founder [[norbert-wiener|Norbert Wiener]], Jesuit transhumanist [[pierre-teilhard-de-chardin|Pierre Teilhard de Chardin]] and Chardin's intellectual heir [[ray-kurzweil|Ray Kurzweil]] as sources of the doctrine that technology grows with a life of its own, acting as a synthetic and deterministic *élan vital* independent of human thought or free will.[1] [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents this cluster of thinkers as the intellectual pedigree behind [[yuval-noah-harari|Yuval Noah Harari]]'s [[transhumanism|transhumanist]] pronouncements.[1]
