# Voluntad Popular

Voluntad Popular ("Popular Will") is a Venezuelan opposition political party active in the campaign against the government of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].[1]

In the aftermath of Venezuela's disputed July 2024 presidential election, the party announced on X on Tuesday, July 30 that its national coordinator, [[freddy-superlano|Freddy Superlano]], had been detained by Maduro's security services.[1] The detention was widely read as retaliation for the opposition's refusal to accept the official count, with candidate [[edmundo-gonzalez-urrutia|Edmundo González Urrutia]] continuing to declare victory and to assert that the election had been stolen.[1] Voluntad Popular's activity formed part of a broader opposition effort — led publicly by González and [[maria-corina-machado|María Corina Machado]] — that claimed to have obtained 73.2% of the polling-station tally sheets in order to prove its victory.[1]
