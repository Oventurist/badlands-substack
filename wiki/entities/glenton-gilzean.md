# Glenton Gilzean

Glenton Gilzean is the District Administrator of the [[central-florida-tourism-oversight-district|Central Florida Tourism Oversight District]], the body created by Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] in February 2023 to govern the Florida territory containing Disney World.[1]

On August 1, 2023, Gilzean announced the abolition of all diversity, equity, and inclusion programs at the district, including its DEI board.[1] The district's public statement said the step followed an internal investigation into its policies and responded to hiring and contracting programs implemented under the predecessor [[reedy-creek-improvement-district|Reedy Creek Improvement District]] that it described as discriminating against Americans on the basis of gender and race at a cost of millions of dollars to taxpayers.[1]

Commentators noted that Gilzean's announcement bound the district rather than [[disney]] itself, leaving open how far the prohibition would reach into the company's own employment practices.[1]
