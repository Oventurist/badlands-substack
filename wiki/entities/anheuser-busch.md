# Anheuser-Busch

Anheuser-Busch is the brewing company that owns [[bud-light|Bud Light]], the brand at the center of a prolonged consumer boycott by the American political right beginning in 2023.[1]

## The Bud Light boycott and its unwinding

Bud Light was "pulverized" for months by much of the political right — and by much of the [[ufc|Ultimate Fighting Championship]]'s audience — over a promotion featuring a transgender influencer.[1] In the autumn of 2023 UFC chief executive [[dana-white|Dana White]] completed a reported nine-figure deal making Bud Light the UFC's official beer sponsor, reviving an affiliation that dated back more than fifteen years; the brand plainly hoped the renewed tie-up would help its cause.[1]

White publicly defended Anheuser-Busch in interviews with [[sean-hannity|Sean Hannity]], [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] and [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], arguing that the fact "Anheuser-Busch wants to be in business with me" itself demonstrated the success of the backlash.[1] Privately he framed the sponsorship as a matter of principle — "We don't cancel people," he told one confidant — and helped broker a détente.[1] During a fight night at Madison Square Garden in November 2023, [[kid-rock|Kid Rock]], who had been savaging the brand, had "a great conversation" with Anheuser-Busch's chief executive in White's green room, and the boycott was soon off.[1]

When [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] continued hammering the company in February 2024 as a final holdout, White spoke to him by phone; within days Trump suggested on social media that Anheuser-Busch deserved "a second chance," using talking points that closely echoed White's.[1]
