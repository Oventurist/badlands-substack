# People's Liberation Army

The People's Liberation Army (PLA) is the armed forces of the People's Republic of China, subordinate to the Chinese Communist Party rather than the state and commanded through the [[central-military-commission|Central Military Commission]] (CMC), whose chairman is President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].[1]

## January 2026 purges

In late January 2026, Chinese authorities announced investigations into CMC Vice Chairman Gen. [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]], aged 75, and Gen. [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]] for "serious violations of discipline and law," a formulation that conventionally signals corruption allegations.[1] The *People's Liberation Army Daily*, the force's official newspaper, accused the pair of "seriously betraying" the Communist Party's trust, "trampling on and undermining" the Central Military Commission, and threatening the authority of its chairman.[1]

The removals reduced the CMC to Xi and a single other member, leaving senior PLA ranks in disarray and creating a leadership void after years of anti-corruption campaigns that had ousted dozens of high-ranking officers since 2023.[1] Analysts described the moves as strengthening Xi's absolute control over the military while raising concerns about operational readiness, morale, and a climate of suspicion that might deter promotions and encourage overly cautious decision-making.[1] Despite the turmoil, the PLA remained under Xi's centralized command with no reported change to strategic ambitions such as Taiwan.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the purge not as instability but as consolidation, arguing that Western coverage amounted to orchestrated hysterics and that the removals represented the methodical expulsion of what he termed Globalist Deep State influence embedded in China's institutions over generations.[1] He situated the action within Xi's decade-long [[tigers-and-flies-campaign|Tigers and Flies campaign]], describing it as the deployment of leverage patiently accumulated over years and mistaken by outside observers for stagnation.[1]

## Taiwan Strait interception, September 2024

On September 17, 2024 a U.S. P-8A Poseidon anti-submarine patrol aircraft transited the [[taiwan|Taiwan]] Strait, and the PLA's Eastern Theater Command said it had responded by organizing fighter jets to tail and stand guard against the American flight, "dealing with it in accordance with the law."[2] Senior Captain Li Xi, spokesperson for the Eastern Theater Command, said "theater troops are on constant high alert to resolutely defend national sovereignty and security and regional peace and stability."[2] Beijing views self-ruled Taiwan as a renegade province and claims jurisdiction over the strait separating the island from the mainland; the U.S. Navy's Seventh Fleet confirmed the transit occurred in international airspace but made no mention of being tailed.[2] Badlands writers folded the incident into their "Scare Event" thesis, listing Chinese aircraft intercepts alongside Russia–NATO nuclear rhetoric, a widening Middle East war, and a Fed-engineered financial crack-up as converging crisis vectors.[2]

## Taiwan reunification statement (August 2024)

In August 2024, Central Military Commission vice chairman [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]] told visiting US national security adviser [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] in Beijing that reunification of [[taiwan|Taiwan]] with the mainland is "the mission and responsibility" of the PLA and that the military would not be deterred from pursuing it.[3] The statement accompanied Chinese defense ministry demands that Washington halt arms sales and military cooperation with Taipei.[3]
