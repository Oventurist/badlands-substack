# People's Liberation Army

The People's Liberation Army (PLA) is the armed forces of the People's Republic of China. It is not a state army in the Western sense but the military arm of the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]], commanded through the [[central-military-commission|Central Military Commission]] whose chairman is [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]].

## Leadership turmoil, January 2026

The January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief describes a major shake-up in the PLA's senior ranks. Investigations were announced into CMC Vice Chairman General [[zhang-youxia|Zhang Youxia]] and General [[liu-zhenli|Liu Zhenli]] for "serious violations of discipline and law." The *People's Liberation Army Daily*, the force's official newspaper, accused the two of "seriously betraying" the Party's trust and of "trampling on and undermining" the Central Military Commission.

Zhang, a longtime Xi ally with family ties reaching back to China's revolutionary era and with rare actual combat experience, had previously been regarded as untouchable within the PLA. His removal, together with Liu's, left senior PLA ranks in disarray and followed years of anti-corruption campaigns that had already ousted dozens of high-ranking officers since 2023. Observers cited in the source warned of effects on operational readiness, morale, and promotion dynamics, while noting that the PLA remained under Xi's centralized command with no reported change to strategic objectives such as Taiwan.

## In the Badlands framing

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treats the PLA purge as evidence of the [[tigers-and-flies|Tigers and Flies]] campaign reaching its most senior targets, and as the removal of what he characterizes as Globalist [[deep-state|Deep State]] penetration of China's military institutions — a parallel to shadow campaigns he attributes to sovereign-aligned governments elsewhere.
