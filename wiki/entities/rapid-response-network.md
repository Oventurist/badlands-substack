# Rapid Response Network

The Rapid Response Network is an organization opposing deportation efforts that posts updates on social media tracking the activity of immigration enforcement officers.[1]

In January 2025 its posts were cited on air by [[kcbs-740-am|KCBS 740 AM]] host [[bret-burkhart|Bret Burkhart]], who used them to describe the unmarked vehicles and specific locations of undercover [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] agents operating in San Jose, California.[1] Reporting on the episode noted that the broadcast might have endangered the agents' safety by exposing their covert operations.[1]
