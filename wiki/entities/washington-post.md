# The Washington Post

*The Washington Post* is a major American daily newspaper, owned by Jeff Bezos.

## In the Badlands critique

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] lists the Post among "mainstream, historically spook-friendly outlets" — with [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] and [[the-atlantic|The Atlantic]] — that recruit journalists predisposed to accept establishment narratives. The article's central case study, [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]], was employed by the Post at the time of writing.

## Layoffs

In the TikTok address quoted at length in the article, Lorenz observes that "even mainstream national media outlets owned by billionaires like The Washington Post, where I work, and The Atlantic, where I used to work, have done layoffs." The Badlands author treats this as confirmation that billionaire patronage cannot arrest the decline of [[mainstream-media|centralized media]].
