# Brookfield

Brookfield is a commercial real estate (CRE) giant whose asset management funds hold large office portfolios in major U.S. markets.[1]

In April 2023, a fund belonging to Brookfield defaulted on a $161.4 million mortgage covering twelve office buildings in Washington, D.C.[1] The loan was transferred to a special servicer that was working with "the borrower to execute a pre-negotiation agreement and to determine the path forward."[1] The default became a leading headline in the developing [[commercial-real-estate-crisis|commercial real estate crisis]], arriving amid stress in the regional banking sector and a $400 billion wall of CRE debt maturities due that year.[1]
