# Doug Emhoff

Doug Emhoff is an American attorney and the husband of former Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], serving as the first "second gentleman" of the United States during the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration.[1]

## Post-election blame narrative

In the immediate aftermath of Harris's defeat in the 2024 presidential election, she was publicly affectionate toward her "beloved" second gentleman.[1] After [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was sworn into office in January 2025, however, tabloid reporting described a shift: the Daily Mail reported that Harris had turned to a blame game in which Emhoff — characterized in the piece as "dead weight" — was faulted for scandals said to have contributed to her loss.[1]

A source told DailyMail.com exclusively that "there's plenty of blame to go around as far as Kamala is concerned and Doug has his share."[1] The report tied the reassessment to Harris's weighing of her political future, including a possible 2026 run for Governor of California or another presidential bid two years after that, and to the question of whether Emhoff was an asset or a liability to those ambitions.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media commentator [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] read the coverage as "a total rewriting of history, brought to you by fake elections," noting sardonically that the same press had once promoted Emhoff as a sex symbol, and concluding that "Fake News is Future History."[1]
