# Emily Gabriella Sommer

**Emily Gabriella Sommer** is a Washington, D.C. woman arrested in May 2025 and accused of spitting in the face of [[ed-martin|Ed Martin]], a U.S. Attorney nominee and formerly [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s top prosecutor in the District of Columbia, during a live television interview.[1]

## Arrest and allegations

Prosecutors say Sommer boasted about the attack in taunting social media posts, which is how investigators tracked her down. Authorities focused on an X account using the name "lefttits" that replied to Martin's posts with the admission, "That was me that spit in your face today." In further comments the account wrote, "Hi, hello. A pleasure to hawk a dehydrated, pithy white foam spit into your face," adding, "I only apologize that this city left me so dehydrated and unable to get water that I couldn't produce more to stain and drip down your face." Each reply reportedly ended with a smiling poop emoji.[1]

The [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] charged her with one count of assaulting, resisting, or impeding a government official.[1]

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized spitting as "vile and animalistic," uncivilized and a presentation of "humans at their worst," and described Sommer as a privileged white woman who bragged about assaulting Martin on social media.
