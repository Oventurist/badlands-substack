# Skydance

Skydance is the entertainment company that owns [[paramount-global|Paramount]] following its acquisition of the studio.[1]

## 2026 consolidation talks

In February 2026, [[warner-bros-discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]] reopened takeover talks with Skydance-owned Paramount after receiving a waiver from [[netflix|Netflix]], while continuing to back its merger agreement with Netflix.[1] Because Paramount holds broadcast assets — including [[cbs|CBS]] — and their associated licenses, any resulting transaction would require regulatory approval.[1]

Commentary in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] situated the renewed talks within a broader pattern of legacy studios centralizing in response to the democratization of content production and distribution, including disputes over AI-generated content and intellectual property.[1]
