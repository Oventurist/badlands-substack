# Josh Gottheimer

**Josh Gottheimer** is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from New Jersey.[1]

## IGO Anti-Boycott Act

Gottheimer co-introduced the [[igo-anti-boycott-act|IGO Anti-Boycott Act]] (House Resolution 867) alongside New York Republican [[mike-lawler|Mike Lawler]], making the measure a bipartisan effort.[1] The bill would extend existing federal anti-boycott law to boycotts of Israel promoted by international governmental organizations.[1]

After House leadership cancelled a scheduled vote on the bill in early May 2025 in response to opposition from figures aligned with the "Make America Great Again" movement, a spokesperson for Gottheimer told *Newsweek* that it was "beyond outrageous and offensive that House leadership bowed to extreme-right forces and pulled this commonsense, bipartisan bill that makes antisemitic and hate-driven boycotts illegal."[1]
