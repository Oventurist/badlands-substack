# St. Clair Solar

**St. Clair Solar** is described in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]] as a small corporation that owned two solar farms in Canada.[1] Its significance in the essay lies entirely in its corporate relationship to [[first-solar|First Solar]].[1]

## Role in the subsidy allegation

Carlson writes that First Solar received $455.7 million in public money to subsidize the sale of solar panels to the two Canadian solar farms owned by St. Clair Solar.[1] He then notes that St. Clair Solar was a wholly owned subsidiary of First Solar, so the effect of the arrangement was that the [[barack-obama|Obama]] administration "used our tax money to pay First Solar to sell their solar panels to themselves."[1] The transaction is presented as the clearest documented instance of the favoritism Carlson attributes to the relationship between the administration, [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] donors, and the solar industry.[1] He connects it further to the later appointment of Goldman Sachs executive [[bruce-heyman|Bruce Heyman]] — placed by Obama on his finance committee — as Ambassador to Canada.[1]
