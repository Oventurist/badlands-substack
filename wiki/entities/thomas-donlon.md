# Thomas Donlon

Thomas Donlon is a former federal law enforcement official who was appointed interim Commissioner of the [[new-york-city-police-department|New York City Police Department]] in September 2024, following the abrupt resignation of his predecessor [[edward-caban|Edward Caban]].[1]

## Federal search of his residence

On Friday, September 20, 2024, federal authorities raided Donlon's home — just over a week after Caban resigned in the wake of an FBI raid at his own residence.[1] Donlon acknowledged the search in a brief statement released by the department late on the Saturday night that followed, stating that authorities "took materials that came into my possession 20 years ago and are unrelated to my work with the New York City Police Department."[1] He added that the department would not comment further because the matter was not an NYPD matter.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media's [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Donlon had held the interim post for only about ten days when the raid occurred, having been elevated when Caban resigned amid what were ostensibly public corruption allegations involving Caban's brother and others connected to Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]]' office.[1] She treated the claim that the Donlon search was unrelated to the NYPD with open skepticism, and noted press speculation that the raid was a message from federal authorities to Adams — while observing how readily federal agencies now invoke "classified documents" as grounds for searches.[1] The commentary framed the episode as an early stage of a broader public-disclosure phase in an [[southern-district-of-new-york|SDNY]] saga, and raised the question of how a sprawling RICO-style prosecution would actually be conducted.[1]
