# Andrey Yusov

Andrey Yusov is a spokesman for Ukraine's [[main-directorate-of-intelligence|GUR military intelligence agency]].[1] In late July 2024 he said in an interview that GUR agents had helped plan an insurgent ambush on a convoy of [[wagner-group|Wagner Group]] contractors and Malian troops in northern [[mali|Mali]], describing the operation as being conducted "against Russian war criminals" and promising that "there will be more."[1]

The statement drew condemnation from [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]]'s Foreign Ministry, which accused Ukraine of praising terrorism and of subversive actions threatening the stability of the African continent.[1] Ukraine's embassy in [[senegal|Senegal]] amplified the interview on Facebook before deleting it.[1]
