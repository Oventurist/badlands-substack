# William Kristol

William Kristol is a neoconservative writer and political operative identified by Badlands Media as a co-founder of the [[project-for-the-new-american-century|Project for the New American Century]] (PNAC).[1]

Kristol created PNAC in 1997 together with [[robert-kagan|Robert Kagan]], a partnership the article describes as a "gruesome duo."[1] The think tank drew on the neoconservative network of the [[american-enterprise-institute|American Enterprise Institute]] and assembled figures including [[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]], [[paul-wolfowitz|Paul Wolfowitz]], [[john-bolton|John Bolton]], and [[donald-rumsfeld|Donald Rumsfeld]].[1] Through PNAC, Kristol is associated with the doctrine of [[pax-americana|Pax Americana]] and with a policy vision that contemplated the takeover of Libya, Iraq, Iran, and Syria — the same framework the article applies to the December 2024 fall of [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

## Calls to impeach RFK Jr. (2025)

In September 2025 Kristol joined escalating calls for the impeachment of Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] following a contentious Senate hearing, saying "I've seen enough."[2] His intervention was cited as a marker of a "big-name conservative" turning against Kennedy amid criticism of the secretary's firing of [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|CDC]] Director [[susan-monarez|Susan Monarez]] and of the full federal vaccine advisory panel.[2]
