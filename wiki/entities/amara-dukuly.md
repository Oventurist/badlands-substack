# Amara Dukuly

Amara Dukuly is a 42-year-old resident of Brookhaven, Delaware County, Pennsylvania, and a naturalized U.S. citizen originally from Liberia who worked for [[uscis|U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services]].[1]

## Bribery charges

In June 2025 Dukuly was charged with soliciting bribes from immigrants and making false promises about steps he would — or even could — take to help them with their immigration cases.[1] According to a criminal complaint unsealed in Philadelphia federal court on June 26, 2025, he most recently accepted $6,000 in cash from a confidential informant posing as a Tajikistani immigrant.[1]

The complaint described an April meeting in a [[walmart|Walmart]] parking lot in Eddystone, Pennsylvania, where the informant — who was being secretly audio- and videotaped — handed over the money, and Dukuly said he would use it to pay his connections in the government to make the man's record "clean."[1] He told the informant this could improve the chances of his immigration paperwork being approved, saying: "Me, fight hard. Fix inside. Clean. With these people, clean."[1] The complaint stated that Dukuly then took no action on the man's case.[1] The case was reported by [[philadelphia-inquirer|The Philadelphia Inquirer]].[1]
