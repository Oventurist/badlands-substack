# Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) is the federal agency that administers [[medicaid|Medicaid]], Medicare, and related health coverage programs, and is the vehicle through which federal payments to state Medicaid programs flow.[1]

## Role in the War on Fraud

CMS Administrator [[mehmet-oz|Mehmet Oz]] appeared alongside Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] in February 2026 when the administration announced the suspension of Medicaid payments to [[minnesota|Minnesota]] as the opening action of the [[war-on-fraud|War on Fraud]].[1] Approximately $259 million in Medicaid funding was withheld pending review, with Minnesota given 60 days to address federal concerns and implement corrective measures before the funds could potentially be restored.[1] The administration described the action as part of a broader effort to combat fraud in federal benefit programs and prevent misuse of taxpayer funds, while Minnesota Democratic leaders called the suspension politically motivated.[1]
