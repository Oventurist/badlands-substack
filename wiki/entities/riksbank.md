# Riksbank

The Riksbank is Sweden's central bank.[1] In January 2023 it became a focal point of commentary on stress within the global central banking system after disclosing very large losses for the prior year.[1]

## 2022 loss

On January 11, 2023, the Riksbank said it expected to report a loss of 81 billion Swedish crowns — roughly $7.72 billion — for 2022, attributing the result primarily to higher market interest rates.[1] In its statement the bank explained that "the unrealised loss is mainly due to globally rising market interest rates, which has reduced the market value of the Riksbank's assets."[1]

## Interpretation in Badlands commentary

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] treated the Riksbank loss as the first of two "huge stories" showing that the [[global-debt-system|global central bank debt system]] was beginning to crack.[1] He argued the loss was a direct consequence of the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]] aggressively raising interest rates and driving up the value of the dollar, which he said was crushing the market value of assets held by foreign central banks.[1] Lange paired the Riksbank result with the record loss reported by the [[swiss-national-bank|Swiss National Bank]] as evidence that central banks outside the United States could now only react to Fed policy rather than manipulate the dollar themselves.[1]
