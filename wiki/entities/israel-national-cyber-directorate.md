# Israel National Cyber Directorate

The Israel National Cyber Directorate is the Israeli government body responsible for defending national civilian cyberspace and coordinating cyber-defense with allied governments.[1]

## Joint advisory on Iranian hacking, December 2023

In December 2023 the directorate joined the [[fbi|FBI]], the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] and the [[cisa|Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency]] in an advisory reporting that Iran-affiliated hackers had compromised numerous organizations in the United States by targeting an Israeli-made industrial control device.[1] The advisory stated that "the victims span multiple U.S. states" but did not specify how many organizations had been affected.[1] Among the confirmed victims was the [[municipal-water-authority-of-aliquippa|Municipal Water Authority of Aliquippa]] in western Pennsylvania, along with four other utilities and an aquarium.[1]

The advisory came amid expectations among cybersecurity experts that pro-Palestinian hacktivists and Iranian-backed hackers would escalate operations against [[israel|Israel]] and its allies following the October 7 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack, even though no evidence tied Iran directly to that attack.[1]

## Arrest of an executive in Nevada (August 2025)

In August 2025 [[tom-artiom-alexandrovich|Tom Artiom Alexandrovich]], a 38-year-old Israeli cybersecurity official described online as executive director of the directorate, was arrested in Nevada during a two-week undercover sting by the [[las-vegas-metropolitan-police-department|Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department]] targeting adults seeking to sexually prey on children.[2] He was charged with felony luring of a child with a computer for a sex act, released on $10,000 bail after an initial court appearance and returned to Israel.[2] Reporting on the case noted that the directorate is an Israeli government agency operating under the purview of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s office.[2]
