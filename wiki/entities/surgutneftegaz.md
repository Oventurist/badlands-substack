# Surgutneftegaz

Surgutneftegaz is one of Russia's largest independent petroleum producers. In January 2025 it was named, alongside [[gazprom-neft|Gazprom Neft]], as a principal target of United States sanctions against the Russian energy sector announced in the final week of the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration.[1]

## January 2025 sanctions

The sanctions covered both producers and their subsidiaries — including Naftna industrija Srbije (NIS), which supplies Russian oil to Serbia and neighboring European states — as well as related insurance providers, more than 30 oilfield service companies, and over 180 vessels engaged in delivering Russian oil.[1]

Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]] said at a press conference that the measures were designed to hobble incoming President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], who had signaled willingness to reopen diplomatic channels with Moscow on Ukraine, and that they struck at Serbia and Russia simultaneously.[1] Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] read the timing as deliberate sabotage of the incoming administration in the five days remaining before the transfer of power.[1]
