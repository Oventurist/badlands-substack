# Aleksandr Bortnikov

**Aleksandr Bortnikov** is the director of Russia's [[fsb|Federal Security Service (FSB)]].[1]

After the March 2024 [[crocus-city-hall-attack|Crocus City Hall attack]] outside Moscow, which killed more than 140 people, Bortnikov characterized the advance warning passed privately to Russia by the United States as having been "of a general nature."[1] His comment became a point of contention in reporting by [[new-york-times|The New York Times]] indicating that Washington had withheld part of what it knew about the plot because of the adversarial relationship between the two governments and concern for protecting intelligence sources and methods.[1]
