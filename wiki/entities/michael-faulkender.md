# Michael Faulkender

Michael Faulkender is a Deputy Treasury Secretary in the second [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1] In April 2025 he became the focus of a dispute over the leadership of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]]: Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] pressed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to install Faulkender as acting IRS commissioner after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had gone around Bessent to place [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] in the role, despite the IRS reporting to the Treasury Department.[1] Trump agreed, capping a chaotic stretch in which the IRS was overseen by three different acting commissioners in a single week, and the confrontation is said to have culminated in the [[musk-bessent-white-house-altercation|Musk–Bessent White House altercation]].[1]
