# The Independent

The Independent is a British online newspaper whose reporting is periodically aggregated in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Epstein coverage

In May 2023 The Independent reported on an extensive [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] investigation showing that the circle of global power players who continued to meet [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] after his first sex conviction was far wider than previously revealed, naming figures including the sitting director of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]], [[william-burns|William Burns]], a former White House lawyer, a college president and a member of an international banking dynasty.[1] The outlet noted that none of these names appeared in Epstein's public "black book" or in the flight logs of his private jet, and that the underlying documents — thousands of pages of emails and schedules from 2013 to 2017 — had not previously been reported.[1]
