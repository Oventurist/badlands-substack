# Shigeru Ishiba

Shigeru Ishiba is a Japanese politician who served as Prime Minister of Japan for roughly one year before being succeeded by [[sanae-takaichi|Sanae Takaichi]].[1]

## Tenure and US relations

Badlands commentary on Ishiba's premiership frames it as a short and transactional interlude in the US–Japan relationship. In the Badlands account, Ishiba was working to advance the acquisition of [[us-steel|US Steel]] on behalf of [[nippon-steel|Nippon Steel]] — a transaction blocked in January by Joe Biden and subsequently restructured by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in a negotiation that, in the commentary's telling, effectively inverted the arrangement so that Nippon Steel appears to be controlled by US Steel and the American government.[1]

The same commentary points to Ishiba's early-February visit to the [[white-house|White House]] — making him the first foreign leader hosted after Benjamin Netanyahu — as evidence that he had been dispatched on behalf of Nippon Steel and allied globalist interests rather than on a purely bilateral diplomatic errand.[1]

## Succession

Ishiba's replacement by Takaichi, a protégé of [[shinzo-abe|Shinzo Abe]], is presented in Badlands commentary as a favorable turn for the US–Japan relationship, coinciding with the signing of a critical minerals agreement and expanded defense discussion during Trump's Tokyo visit.[1]

## Trump tariff letter (July 2025)

On Monday, July 7, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] set a 25 percent tax on goods imported from [[japan|Japan]], notifying Ishiba as Japanese Prime Minister by posting a letter on Truth Social addressed to him.[2] The letter warned against retaliation: "If for any reason you decide to raise your Tariffs, then, whatever the number you choose to raise them by, will be added onto the 25 percent that we charge."[2] A parallel letter went to South Korean President [[lee-jae-myung|Lee Jae-myung]], with the new rates set to take effect on August 1.[2]
