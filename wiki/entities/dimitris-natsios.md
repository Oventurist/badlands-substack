# Dimitris Natsios

Dimitris Natsios is the president of [[niki-party|Niki]], a right-wing Greek political party.[1]

## Objection to the Netflix Alexander series

In February 2024 Natsios denounced the [[netflix|Netflix]] series "The Making of a God," which portrayed [[alexander-the-great|Alexander the Great]] as homosexual, calling it "deplorable, unacceptable and unhistorical."[1] He argued the production aimed to subliminally convey the idea, without any basis, that homosexuality was acceptable in ancient times.[1] Natsios put the matter to Culture Minister [[lina-mendoni|Lina Mendoni]] and asked whether the Greek government would take action against Netflix; Mendoni answered that such a move would be unconstitutional under Greece's protections for artistic freedom.[1]

The exchange was reported by [[remix-news|ReMix News]] and carried in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief of February 23, 2024.[1]
