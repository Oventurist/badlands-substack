Badlands Media is an alternative media collective and content network that produces a slate of live shows for what it describes as the "truth community," covering current events, politics, and conspiratorial and ideological topics from a perspective opposed to the mainstream media (MSM).[1] The organization states that its mission is to offer "a wide range of perspectives by and for the truth community" and to help "take back the narrative from the MSM," framing itself with the slogan "We are the news now."[1]

## Distribution and Channels

All Badlands Media shows air live on the Patel Patriot channel, while replays are made available in library format on the Badlands Media Network, both hosted on the Rumble video platform.[1] The live channel is Patel Patriot's Live Channel (rumble.com/c/PatelPatriot) and the library channel is the Badlands Media Library Channel (rumble.com/c/BadlandsMedia).[1]

## Show Slate

As of the December 12–18, 2022 weekly schedule, Badlands Media produced more than two dozen regularly scheduled shows across the week.[1] The flagship and founding program is the Devolution Power Hour, described as "the show that started it all on the Badlands Media Network," hosted by founder [[jon-herold]] and analyzing events through the lens of [[devolution]] and Continuity of Government theories.[1] Other weekday and weekend programs include:

- Badlands Daily (Monday–Friday, 10am ET) — [[jon-herold]] and [[canncon]] present a traditional news broadcast style "without the Mainstream Media spin and corporate BS."[1]
- Sports Talk (Monday & Friday, 12pm ET) — JB White and Absolute1776 cover sports "by cutting through the BS" and focusing on "regaining America's Pastime."[1]
- Vigilant News (Monday & Friday, 3pm ET) — Justin Deschamps and Ryan Delarme cover current events, politics, and "all things that affect our ability to live free and prosperous lives."[1]
- Sauce and Effect (Monday, 5pm ET) — Woke Societies and Johnny Q analyze the week's news with an emphasis on sourcing "that the Mainstream Media tends to leave out."[1]
- Baseless Conspiracies (Monday, 10:30pm ET) — [[zak-paine]] takes [[jon-herold]] "to conspiracy theory school," exploring a new topic each week.[1]
- Trash Talk (Tuesday & Thursday, 8am ET) — Patrick Gunnels and J.B. White discuss "the Information War" alongside pop culture and entertainment.[1]
- Why We Vote (Tuesday, 5pm ET) — [[canncon]] interviews voting experts on election integrity and "repeated attempts at fraud and election theft."[1]
- What We Missed with Chris Paul (Tuesday, 9pm ET) — former Hollywood insider Chris Paul critiques the mainstream media paradigm.[1]
- Eye of the Storm (Tuesday, 10:30pm ET) — Stormy Patriot Joe and Absolute1776 analyze events through the lens of the [[q-operation]] ("the biggest Military Intelligence Information Dissemination Program ever launched").[1]
- The Earth Chronicles (Wednesday, 3pm ET) — David Whitehead and Josh Reid discuss sociopolitical events from a global perspective.[1]
- Big Dig Synergy (Wednesday, 7:30pm ET) — Patrick Gunnels and L examine the "decentralized citizen intelligence apparatus."[1]
- Altered State (Wednesday, 9pm ET) — [[zak-paine]] and Brad Getz examine "the slow, engineered degradation of American Society" via historical psyops.[1]
- Devolution Power Hour (Wednesday & Saturday, 10:30pm ET) — [[jon-herold]], with co-host [[burning-bright]] on Wednesdays and Patrick Gunnels on Saturdays.[1]
- Game Theory (Thursday, 12pm ET) — Sean Morgan and [[burning-bright]] apply [[fifth-generation-warfare]] and game theory to national and international events.[1]
- Knowledge Based (Thursday, 7:30pm ET) — Jordan Sather and Justin Deschamps explore "esoteric knowledge, hidden technology, and psychological operations."[1]
- SITREP (Thursday, 9pm ET) — [[canncon]] and Alpha Warrior cover the stories of veterans and their oaths.[1]
- Rugpull Radio (Thursday, 10:30pm ET) — GMoney and Patriots in Progress discuss Bitcoin/[[bitcoin]]/blockchain and "the corrupt Central Banking system."[1]

- Raising a Nation (Saturday, 9pm ET) — The Kate Awakening and Defender of the Republic discuss the [[great-awakening]] from a parenting perspective.[1]
- NCSWIC Podcast (Sunday, 7:30pm ET) — Tru Reporting and Absolute1776 analyze [[fifth-generation-warfare]] and Info War topics.[1]
- Defected (Sunday, 9pm ET) — [[burning-bright]] and Just Human examine current events through "Narrative Warfare, Game Theory and Fifth-Generation Strategy."[1]

## Hosts and Contributors

The network draws on a recurring roster of independent commentators and "citizen journalists," including founder [[jon-herold]], [[canncon]], [[zak-paine]], and [[burning-bright]], as well as Absolute1776, Justin Deschamps, Patrick Gunnels, Brad Getz, The Kate Awakening, and many others.[1] Badlands Media describes its contributors collectively as "our collective of citizen journalists."[1]

## Business Model

Badlands Media states that it will "always put out our content for free," while inviting readers to support the network by becoming paid subscribers to its Substack newsletter.[1]

## Sponsorship and funding model

In September 2024 Badlands Media introduced [[pecos-wellness|Pecos Wellness]] as a new sponsor of the Badlands Substack, offering readers 10% off its Pecos Gold Resin supplement with the promo code "Badlands."[2] Editions of the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] from this period closed with the standing note that Badlands Media "will always put out our content for free," inviting readers to support the collective of citizen journalists through paid subscriptions under the banner "We are the news now."[2]

## NPR mention (November 2025)

In November 2025 Badlands Media received what host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] believed was its first-ever mention by [[npr|NPR]], in an article headlined "Right-Wing Media Shrugs Off Latest Epstein Document Release."[3] The piece quoted Herold's Badlands Media Rumble livestream calling the newly released [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]] documents "nothingburgers" and also cited Badlands personality [[brian-lupo|Brian Lupo]], who read the emails as showing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] informing on Epstein and [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]].[3] Herold responded that the network was "the only one not taking the bait" and that critics were angry Badlands "didn't go hook, line, and sinker like literally everybody else on the internet."[3]

## Great American Restoration Tour launch (April 2023)

In April 2023, Badlands Media staged the first live gathering of its [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] in Chandler, Arizona, bringing show hosts and Substack writers together for three days of group panels, Q&A sessions and meet-and-greets.[19] VIP tickets sold out in advance, a limited number of in-person tickets remained from a final release, and every panel was streamed live to virtual ticket holders with replays available for six months.[19]

## Editorial model of the News Brief

The outlet describes the News Brief as a hand-picked digest in which the team selects news items of interest from the preceding days to give readers an overview of developments relevant to what it calls the Truth Community.[19] Items frequently carry original commentary from named contributors such as [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]], each linked to their own independent publications.[19] Badlands Media states that its content will always be free, with paid subscriptions offered as voluntary support for its collective of citizen journalists.[19]

## 2024 election special coverage

Badlands Media produced a live special covering the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 election]], streaming for more than 15 hours through election night and making the replay available to viewers throughout the following week.[20] On the broadcast, [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]] framed the difference between 2024 and 2020 as the product of four years of "defenses in depth" and called for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to reform US election systems rather than rely on such layers; contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that Trump might be declared the winner but that "his and our work is only just beginning."[20]

The accompanying News Brief carried takes from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] on Trump's mandate and from [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] on Israeli reactions to a second Trump term and [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s dismissal of Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]].[20] The edition also promoted longtime sponsor EasyRX, which was offering 25 percent off orders of $100 or more with a Black Friday promo code.[20]
