# Badlands Media

**Badlands Media** is an independent media organization that publishes the Badlands Media Newsletter, a Substack-based publication. The organization describes itself as a collective of citizen journalists.[1]

## Self-description and editorial stance

In its inaugural newsletter announcement, published on October 9, 2022, Badlands Media introduced itself as "a collective of citizen journalists providing the best content possible while staying grounded in honesty and integrity."[1] The statement positions the outlet within the citizen-journalism tradition, in which contributors operate outside of legacy newsroom structures, and frames honesty and integrity as the organization's stated editorial commitments.[1]

## Founding announcement

The "Coming soon" post served as the launch placeholder for the newsletter, inviting readers to subscribe ahead of regular publication.[1] Such announcement posts are the conventional first entry of a Substack publication, and the item recorded 132 likes.[1]

## Editorial model and network (2022)

The November 23, 2022 essay "Death Blossom" by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] illustrates several standing features of Badlands Media's operation.[2] The outlet publishes its content free while inviting paid subscriptions, describing itself as "a collective of citizen journalists" working to "take back the narrative from the MSM" under the slogan "We are the news now."[2] Each article carries a standing disclaimer that Badlands Media articles and features represent the opinions of the contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself.[2]

The piece also references the Badlands Media Network, on which contributors appear to discuss ongoing developments, and reflects the outlet's practice of allowing writers to cross-reference their own independent Substacks — in this case Burning Bright's [[burning-bright-publishing|Burning Bright]] publication, devoted to Narrative Deployments and Macro Concepts in [[fifth-generation-warfare|Fifth-Generation Warfare]].[2]

## Live events

Badlands Media stages a recurring live event series, the [[great-american-restoration-tour|Great American Restoration Tour]] (GART), which gathers the network's hosts, writers and audience — collectively styled "Badlanders" — for multi-day weekends of panels and social programming.[3] The organization describes the events as an opportunity to meet and network with fellow patriots and as creating "an awakening pressure cooker where a natural sifting toward sovereignty, nationalism and community-building takes place."[3]

Its sixth stop was announced on October 18, 2024 for Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, running November 14–17, 2024, with weekend VIP and General Admission tickets already sold out and only single-day and two-day combo passes remaining.[3] Badlands sold tickets through TicketSpice and offered a virtual ticket for remote viewers that streamed all Friday, Saturday and Sunday panels, included long-term DVR replay access, and admitted buyers to an exclusive GART [[telegram|Telegram]] chat with hosts and other subscribers.[3]
