# Badlands Media

Badlands Media is an independent media collective that publishes commentary and analysis from contributing citizen journalists via Substack.[1]

## Editorial posture

Badlands Media publishes a standing disclaimer that its articles and features represent the opinions of the contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the outlet itself.[1] The outlet states that its content is always released free of charge while inviting readers to support it through paid subscriptions, describing its mission as a collective effort by citizen journalists to "take back the narrative from the MSM" under the slogan "We are the news now."[1]

## Contributors

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] is among the contributors published by Badlands Media, with his April 2025 essay "A Journey of Ever-changing Truths" appearing on the outlet's newsletter alongside a pointer to his independent Substack.[1]

## The Badlands Brief

Badlands Media publishes the Badlands Brief, described as "your daily drop of Badlands' takes on the narratives dominating the info war," inviting readers to join the conversation in the comments section.[2] The July 28, 2026 edition, titled "Entertaining Obstruction & Artificial Shadow Wars," carried commentary from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] and [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] on the Trump election order litigation, the [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source AI]] fight, [[benjamin-netanyahu|Netanyahu]] and American opinion of [[israel|Israel]], the 2028 Democratic field, and the cartel campaign in [[mexico|Mexico]].[2] The Brief carries a standing disclaimer that opinions expressed are those of the tagged authors and do not necessarily reflect the attitudes or positions of Badlands Media, and directs readers to the outlet's shows streaming on Rumble; the Brief is free, supported by optional paid subscriptions.[2]
