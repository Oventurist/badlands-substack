# Badlands Media

**Badlands Media** is an independent media collective and Substack newsletter that publishes articles, shows, and commentary from what it describes as a collective of citizen journalists. Its stated mission, repeated in the subscription appeal attached to its posts, is to "take back the narrative from the MSM," summarized by the movement slogan "We are the news now." Badlands states that it will always put out its content for free, while inviting readers to support the operation through paid subscriptions.

The outlet is the publisher of the corpus catalogued in this wiki. Its editorial model relies on outside contributors — including the pseudonymous hypnotist [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]], author of [[a-hypnotists-take-on-israel-palestine|"A Hypnotist's Take on Israel, Palestine, and Q"]] — and each contributed piece carries a standard disclaimer that "Badlands Media articles and features represent the opinions of the contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself."

Editorially, Badlands sits within the media-skeptical and [[q-anon|Q]]-adjacent milieu: its writers routinely treat mainstream outlets as instruments of [[operation-mockingbird|Mockingbird]] propaganda and analyze current events through the frame of [[5th-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]]. Contributors frequently note that Badlands readers have "been seeing for many years" what they expect the wider public to discover as trust in legacy media collapses.

## August 2025 roundup edition

The August 15, 2025 edition of the Badlands News Brief, [[a-look-at-recent-features|"A Look At Recent Features"]], was published while the team attended GART and consisted of a curated look back at seven contributor features from July and August 2025. It carried a sponsored placement for All Good, a tallow-based product line by Jessica Storm, and promoted GART virtual tickets. The edition also names the outlet's "Founders & Foundations" article series, then in its third entry.
