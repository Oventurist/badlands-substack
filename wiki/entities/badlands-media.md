# Badlands Media

**Badlands Media** is an independent media organization that publishes the Badlands Media Newsletter, a Substack-based publication. The organization describes itself as a collective of citizen journalists.[1]

## Self-description and editorial stance

In its inaugural newsletter announcement, published on October 9, 2022, Badlands Media introduced itself as "a collective of citizen journalists providing the best content possible while staying grounded in honesty and integrity."[1] The statement positions the outlet within the citizen-journalism tradition, in which contributors operate outside of legacy newsroom structures, and frames honesty and integrity as the organization's stated editorial commitments.[1]

## Founding announcement

The "Coming soon" post served as the launch placeholder for the newsletter, inviting readers to subscribe ahead of regular publication.[1] Such announcement posts are the conventional first entry of a Substack publication, and the item recorded 132 likes.[1]

## "Get Woke, Go Broke" (April 17, 2023)

Badlands Media published [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s essay "Get Woke, Go Broke" on April 17, 2023, an opinion piece arguing that [[esg|ESG]] scoring is used by a deep state "club" to control corporate behavior at the expense of shareholders and customers alike.[4] As with all of the outlet's contributor pieces, it carried the standard disclaimer that Badlands Media articles and features represent the opinions of the contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself, alongside the outlet's statement that its content will always be free and that its collective of citizen journalists aims to take back the narrative from the mainstream media.[4]


## Relationship to the Substack newsletter

Badlands Media's video programming is distributed through its Rumble channel, and the outlet's Substack posts routinely direct readers there, telling subscribers that if they like the Substack they "should check out our shows — streaming now on Badlands Media."[5] The newsletter carries a standing disclaimer that opinions expressed in the Badlands News Brief are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the attitudes or positions of Badlands Media.[5] In January 2026 the organisation extended its Substack presence with [[badlands-blitz|Badlands Blitz]], a week-in-review show published exclusively on the platform rather than on Rumble.[5] Its contributors describe themselves collectively as a "collective of citizen journalists," and posts close with the slogan "We are the news now."[5]

## Great American Restoration Tour events

Badlands Media stages the [[gart|Great American Restoration Tour]] (GART), a travelling series of multi-day events at which its hosts appear on panels before a live audience.[9] In July 2025 the organisation announced [[gart-9-deadwood|GART 9]] in [[deadwood-south-dakota|Deadwood, South Dakota]], its ninth stop and second visit to the town, selling in-person tickets through a TicketSpice page and virtual tickets that provided a livestream of all three days of panels, long-term DVR replay access, and entry to an exclusive GART [[telegram]] chat.[9] The company presented the events as a venue for patriots to meet and network in person, and said that on behalf of the whole Badlands Media team it hoped to see both familiar and new faces, as had happened at every GART to that point.[9]
