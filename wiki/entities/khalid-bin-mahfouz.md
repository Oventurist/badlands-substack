# Khalid bin Mahfouz

**Khalid bin Mahfouz** was a Saudi banker and an insider of the [[bank-of-credit-and-commerce-international|Bank of Credit and Commerce International]] (BCCI), and a relative of [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] by association with the wider Saudi financial elite described in the article.[1]

## Bush connections

According to the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]] material quoted in Badlands Media commentary, bin Mahfouz was one of two wealthy Saudis — alongside billionaire [[salem-bin-laden|Salem bin Laden]] — for whom Texas businessman [[james-bath|James Bath]] served as North American representative.[1] Bath's $50,000 investment in [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]]'s 1979 [[arbusto-energy|Arbusto]] oil partnership was probably drawn from Bin Laden–Bin Mahfouz funds.[1]

## Harken and Bahrain

Time magazine reporters Jonathan Beaty and S.C. Gwynne, writing in *The Outlaw Bank*, concluded that bin Mahfouz, or other BCCI players, must have had a hand in steering the 1990 Bahrain offshore drilling contract to [[harken-energy|Harken Energy]] and thereby to the president's son.[1]

## Assessment

These claims are reported from quoted journalism and secondary books; the article treats them as part of a wider "web" of corruption around the [[bush-family|Bush family]]. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
