# Ivory Coast

Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire) is a West African state that Badlands analysts identify as one of two centers of power within [[ecowas|ECOWAS]], alongside [[nigeria|Nigeria]].[1]

In the framing advanced by [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], while Nigeria functions as the political and economic center of ECOWAS, the Ivory Coast appears to operate as a paramilitary proxy hub used to launch insurgency operations against the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]].[1]

In early January 2026, the government of [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]] reported that it had foiled a coup attempt against President [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] that was allegedly run out of the Ivory Coast, with the intent of installing former leader Lt. Colonel [[paul-henri-sandaogo-damiba|Paul-Henri Sandaogo Damiba]], who has repeatedly been accused of plotting against Traoré while allegedly operating from Ivorian territory.[1]
