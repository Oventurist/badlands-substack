# WikiLeaks

WikiLeaks is the publishing organization credited in "A Bright Red Line" with making the 2008 Moscow cable of [[william-burns|William Burns]] publicly available.[1]

The article states that the cable was never intended for public consumption, but that thanks to the efforts of [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]] and WikiLeaks the text is accessible, spelling out in very clear terms how Ukraine's admission into [[nato|NATO]] would be viewed by Russia — by the [[vladimir-putin|Putin]] government and by the entire Russian population alike.[1] Because such internal documents were never meant for public scrutiny, the article argues, they are void of typical propaganda and deal largely in geopolitical fact.[1]
