# Robert Breedlove

Robert Breedlove is a Bitcoin commentator and podcaster known for arguments about the moral character of monetary debasement. He is quoted in a July 11, 2024 Badlands Media essay on the exploitation of trust for his frequently repeated formulation that "inflation is theft."[1]

The essay deploys the line as shorthand for its central monetary claim: that [[fiat-currency|fiat currencies]] exploit trust, that the present system constitutes one massive, intentional, continuous exploitation of trust, and that the resulting purchasing power accrues to the "Cantillionaires" who sit closest to the source of debasement — see the [[cantillon-effect|Cantillon effect]].[1] Breedlove's aphorism thereby links the essay's definitional analysis of trust to its proposed remedy in trust-less money, [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]].[1]
