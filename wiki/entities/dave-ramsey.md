# Dave Ramsey

Dave Ramsey is an American personal-finance commentator quoted in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]]'s analysis of the 2023 strike wave for his quip that "there are two types of 'millennial': Awesome and Sucks."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-time-to-strike)

## Use in Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked the Ramsey line as a hinge for a wider argument about generational expectations at the bargaining table, allowing that many millennials are "awesome" while contending that the cohort brought social justice politics into the workplace and into corporate brands, and had become the first digital-native generation to reach the union negotiating table.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-time-to-strike) The essay went on to argue that millennial expectations — reflected in [[united-auto-workers|United Auto Workers]] demands for a four-day week and a 46 percent pay increase — risked destroying entire industries, while insisting the fault lay with the media programming and academic education that shaped that worldview rather than with the generation itself.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-time-to-strike)
