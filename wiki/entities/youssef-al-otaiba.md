# Youssef Al Otaiba

**Youssef Al Otaiba** is the Emirati diplomat serving as the United Arab Emirates' Ambassador to the United States, and a central figure in the introduction of Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] to American political and business networks.[1] Working with [[mohamed-bin-zayed-al-nahyan|Mohammed bin Zayed]], Al Otaiba in 2015 connected MBS to the Lebanese-American businessman [[tom-barrack|Tom Barrack]], who in turn connected MBS to then-Republican candidate [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## "A Vision for a Moderate, Modern Muslim World"

In December 2015 Al Otaiba published an article in *Foreign Policy* magazine entitled "A Vision for a Moderate, Modern Muslim World," in which he described a Middle East at political peace under a moderate form of Islam: countries in conflict finding common ground, economic prosperity achieved, high technology and even a space program cultivated, and Orthodox Christian churches constructed and holding mass alongside Muslim mosques.[1] Badlands commentary treats this essay as a blueprint that MBS has since painstakingly delivered, citing the rapprochement of long-hostile Muslim nations, partnerships with [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and Chinese counterparts that produced the private space company Neo Space Group, and the Saudi stock exchange's full opening to foreign investors.[1]

## Role in the MBS reputation dispute

In 2017 Al Otaiba and MBZ promoted MBS around Washington as the figure who would unite the Middle East and liberate it from radical Islam.[1] When the [[cia|CIA]] subsequently blamed MBS and the Saudi kingdom for the murder of [[jamal-khashoggi|Jamal Khashoggi]], Al Otaiba, MBZ and the UAE bore the brunt of the resulting ridicule, with detractors accusing the Emirates of overselling MBS and his transformation agenda.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] cites this shared reputational exposure as evidence against [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s emailed speculation that the Emirates set MBS up.[1]

## Influence in Washington and alleged targeting

Badlands commentary in April 2024 described Al Otaiba as central to the
[[united-arab-emirates|UAE]]'s long-standing influence in Washington, noting he
had been called "the most powerful and charming ambassador in DC."[2]
Contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that he
believed Al Otaiba was the intended target of
[[operation-endgame|Operation Endgame]], an alleged
[[cia|CIA]]/[[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]/[[qatar|Qatari]]
partnership whose documents leaked to the press in January 2024, and connected
it to the psychological operation surrounding the murder of
[[jamal-khashoggi|Jamal Khashoggi]].[2]
