# Trenton Abston

Trenton Abston is a 25-year-old man accused of stalking and attempting to kidnap the mayor of Memphis, Tennessee in June 2025.[1]

## Arrest and charges

Abston was found with a Taser, rope and duct tape after being seen in the mayor's neighborhood, and was charged by Memphis police with attempted kidnapping, stalking and aggravated criminal trespassing.[1] Police said they believed he was targeting Mayor [[paul-young|Paul Young]].[1] "At the time of his arrest, officers recovered a taser, gloves, rope, and duct tape from his vehicle," the police department said in a statement.[1] Abston was held in the Shelby County Jail with no bail or bond listed and a court appearance scheduled for Friday, according to jail records.[1]
