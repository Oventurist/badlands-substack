# Accuracy in Media

Accuracy in Media is a conservative media watchdog organization that, by early 2023, had reoriented much of its work toward undercover video investigations of American public school districts.[1] At the time of the January 2023 reporting, the group was led by Adam Guillette, an alumnus of the undercover journalism nonprofit [[project-veritas|Project Veritas]], and its methods closely mirrored the hidden-camera sting format that Project Veritas popularized.[1]

## Ohio school district sting

In January 2023, Accuracy in Media released a video investigation of school districts surrounding Columbus, Ohio, in which district employees were recorded discussing how they mislead parents who object to [[critical-race-theory|critical race theory]] and related concepts into believing that schools are not exposing their children to those ideas.[1] The release followed earlier Accuracy in Media investigations of districts in Idaho, Iowa and Tennessee, and Ohio administrators were reported to be struggling to rebut the footage.[1]

Narrating the video, Guillette argued that the new batch of recordings confirmed that "parents are playing an endless game of Whac-A-Mole," because schools continue to instill the tenets of critical race theory under different names or conceal disfavored content inside benign-sounding programs.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] cited the footage in arguing that the modern school system functions primarily as an instrument of indoctrination, tracing the arrangement to the Prussian model of education and the forty-hour work week that, in his account, produced the absentee parent.[1] He framed parents as a perceived threat to a future engineered by what he called the Davos globalists, and described the undercover footage as helping to dispel misplaced trust in the education system.[1]
