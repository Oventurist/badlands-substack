# Premier Election Solutions

Premier Election Solutions was a United States voting equipment company and a former subsidiary of Diebold Inc. It figures in the Badlands corpus as the asset whose forced sale reshaped the American election technology market.

As recounted by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] in Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, [[election-systems-and-software|Election Systems & Software]] purchased Premier and, six months later in 2010, was compelled by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]] to divest it. The divestiture order covered all intellectual property associated with every version of the Premier voting equipment systems, past, present and in development, together with licensing and modification rights to ES&S's AutoMARK ballot marking device, and required that the buyer be approved by the department.

The approved buyer was [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]], which shortly afterwards also acquired [[sequoia-voting-systems|Sequoia Voting Systems]].
