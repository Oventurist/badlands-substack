# Premier Election Solutions

Premier Election Solutions was a voting equipment company, formerly a subsidiary of Diebold Inc., whose court-ordered sale reshaped the American election technology market as recounted in the June 9, 2026 *Badlands Brief*.[1]

[[election-systems-software|Election Systems & Software]] purchased Premier and was compelled to divest it roughly six months later, in 2010, by the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under Attorney General [[eric-holder|Eric Holder]].[1] The divestiture order covered "all of the intellectual property associated with all versions — past, present, and in development — of the Premier voting equipment systems," and obliged ES&S to sell only to a buyer approved by the department.[1]

The approved buyer was [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]], which acquired Premier and, a few months later, also purchased [[sequoia-voting-systems|Sequoia Voting Systems]] — the company that had been owned by [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] and that, per a 2008 Delaware Court filing, was still using Smartmatic intellectual property.[1] In the Badlands account, the Premier divestiture is the moment at which Dominion acquired "a formidable share of the market."[1]
