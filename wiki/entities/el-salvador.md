# El Salvador

**El Salvador** is a Central American republic led by President [[nayib-bukele|Nayib Bukele]], known internationally for its mass-incarceration campaign against gang violence and for its cooperation with United States immigration enforcement.[1]

## 2025 migratory agreement with the United States

On Tuesday, February 4, 2025, U.S. Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] left El Salvador with an agreement from Bukele to accept deportees from the United States of any nationality, including violent American criminals then imprisoned in the United States.[1] Rubio, who had met Bukele at his lakeside country house outside San Salvador for several hours late the previous Monday, called it "the most unprecedented, extraordinary, extraordinary migratory agreement anywhere in the world."[1]

Describing the scope of the arrangement, Rubio said of migrants of all nationalities detained in the United States, "We can send them, and he will put them in his jails," adding that Bukele had "also offered to do the same for dangerous criminals currently in custody and serving their sentences in the United States even though they're U.S. citizens or legal residents."[1] Rubio's visit was part of a regional trip pressing friendly governments to do more to meet President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s demands for a major crackdown on immigration.[1]

## Bukele's June 2024 case for national renewal

In a June 2024 interview with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], President [[nayib-bukele|Nayib Bukele]] presented El Salvador's turnaround as "a miracle" and said the first point of the three-part economic plan in his second-term inauguration speech was to "seek God's wisdom."[2] He described the gangs his government dismantled as "satanic," recounting a case in which a baby was murdered as a sacrifice because "the beast" demanded it — a claim treated as conspiracy theory in the United States but, in Badlands' framing, an undisputed fact in El Salvador.[2] Bukele also warned of "a huge erosion of western civilization," saying "if you don't give maintenance to the system, it will fall," and said democracy "works — until it doesn't."[2]
