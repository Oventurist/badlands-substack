# Humberto Acosta

Humberto Acosta is a presiding magistrate judge in El Paso, Texas.[1]

## Border riot release ruling (March 2024)

On Easter Sunday, March 31, 2024, Acosta ordered the release of migrants accused in a "border riot" in which a stampede overwhelmed [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops along the Rio Grande.[1] He made the ruling during an online teleconference bond hearing, accusing the El Paso District Attorney's Office of not being ready to proceed with detention hearings for each defendant.[1] "It is the ruling of the court that all the rioting participation cases will be released on their own recognizance," Acosta ordered.[1] Court officials noted that undocumented migrants would stay jailed where a federal immigration hold blocked release, and another hearing for additional defendants was expected the next day.[1] See [[el-paso-border-riot-case]].[1]
