# A Practical Guide to Free Energy Devices

***A Practical Guide to Free Energy Devices*** is a compilation on alternative and unconventional energy technologies, hosted on the Internet Archive, which the [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] author cited as the direct inspiration for his January 24, 2024 Badlands Media essay "Who Says Free Energy Isn't Real?"[1]

The author described it as an obscure book, stated that he was reading it himself and looked forward to experimenting with the ideas it presents, and offered it to readers as one of many available resources for pursuing alternative energy sources.[1] Reflecting on the text, he concluded that the main reason people do not investigate such material is that they do not believe it is possible, or else have mistakenly come to believe that they lack the capacity to make sense or good use of the information.[1] The book therefore functioned in the essay as the practical counterpart to its abstract cosmological argument for [[free-energy|free energy]].[1]
