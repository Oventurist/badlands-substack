# Alfredo Luna

Alfredo Luna, who goes by the handle AlphaWarrior, is described in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writing as a decorated Marine and deep undercover law enforcement veteran.[1] In "Horseshoe Theory - Part 1," [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|Ghost of Patrick Henry]] cited Luna's account of a midnight raid conducted on his home by what the author called the Biden regime, in which agents pointed weapons at his wife and son and left his infant daughter alone in her crib for hours, screaming for help.[1] Luna gave the account on a Rumble interview episode referenced in the article.[1]

The author used the testimony as an illustration of his argument about the Bleeding Heart: the lump in the throat, swelling tears, clenched jaw and furrowed brow experienced by a listener are, he wrote, not datapoints that can be processed or duplicated by an artificial intelligence, and the resulting righteous indignation is evidence that human political reasoning is not purely logical.[1] Without that emotional faculty, he argued, a purely logical observer might empathize with Luna's plight but conclude it was beyond his concern — "that's what an AI would do."[1] The example forms part of the case underpinning [[horseshoe-theory|Horseshoe Theory]].[1]
