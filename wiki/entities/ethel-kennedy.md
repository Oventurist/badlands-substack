# Ethel Kennedy

Ethel Kennedy was the widow of Senator Robert F. Kennedy and a matriarch of the Kennedy political family. Her memorial service, held in Washington on Wednesday, October 16, 2024, drew the country's two most recent Democratic presidents and produced an unexpected campaign story.[1]

## The 2024 memorial service exchange

During the service, President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and former President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] were captured on video in an off-mic conversation later deciphered for the New York Post by Jeremy Freeman, a London-based forensic lip reader who was born deaf and has served for sixteen years as a University College London-certified expert witness for litigants, police, and journalists.[1]

According to the translation, Biden, then 81, said of Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], "She's not as strong as me." Obama replied, "I know … that's true," and added, "We have time." Biden answered, "Yeah, we'll get it in time."[1] Moments earlier Obama had said, "it's important that we have some time together," in what was read as a possible reference to campaigning alongside Harris.[1] Biden had been forced by fellow Democrats to relinquish the party's nomination in favor of Harris on July 21, 2024, in a move Obama was believed to have supported.[1]

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the exchange as evidence of an internal fracture in the ruling apparatus — "the ghost of Joe Biden angling for one last shot," with staffers leaking his resentment over the perceived betrayal, while [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] was simultaneously threaded back into the news cycle as a more "experienced" alternative.[1]
