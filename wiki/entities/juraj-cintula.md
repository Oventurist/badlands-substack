# Juraj Cintula

Juraj Cintula is the man identified as the detained suspect in the [[2024-slovak-assassination-attempt|May 2024 shooting]] of Slovak Prime Minister [[robert-fico|Robert Fico]].[1] A video of Cintula's interrogation circulated widely after the attack, in which he confessed to saying that he "disagreed" with his government's policies.[1]

Slovak officials treated the shooting as political from the outset. Deputy Prime Minister Robert Kalinak said there was "no doubt" the attack was a politically motivated assassination attempt and attributed it to an inability among Fico's opponents to accept the will of part of the public.[1] Badlands commentary placed the incident in the context of European "stay-behind" operations associated with [[operation-gladio|Operation Gladio]], noting Fico's record of opposition to NATO's Ukraine policy and to EU migration and pandemic governance measures.[1]
