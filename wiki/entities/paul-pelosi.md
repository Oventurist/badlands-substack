# Paul Pelosi

Paul Pelosi is an American businessman and investor, and the husband of former House Speaker [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] (D-CA). His securities trading became a focal point of the debate over congressional stock ownership and the appearance of insider advantage among lawmakers and their families.[1]

## Semiconductor stock purchase

Pelosi was called out in 2022 after purchasing up to $5 million in stock in a semiconductor company at the same time the Senate was moving legislation to massively subsidize the semiconductor industry.[1] The timing of the trade became a standard reference point for critics of congressional trading, and it was cited directly in support of Senator [[josh-hawley|Josh Hawley]]'s [[pelosi-act|PELOSI Act]], the bill that would prohibit members of [[congress|Congress]] and their family members from holding or trading stocks.[1] Nancy Pelosi was also among a bipartisan group of lawmakers reported to have beaten the market in 2021 across hundreds of millions of dollars in trades.[1]

## Body camera footage release

In late January 2023 the San Francisco Superior Court announced that police body camera video of the October 2022 [[paul-pelosi-attack|attack on Pelosi]] at his San Francisco home would be released to accredited media members on Friday morning at 9 a.m., under an order by Judge Stephen M. Murphy in the case of The People v. [[david-depape|David DePape]].[2] Journalists could obtain CDs of the footage and audio from clerks at the Hall of Justice on presentation of identification, a completed criminal records request form and a $25 payment.[2] Badlands contributor Ryan DeLarme, who had long called for the footage's release, urged caution about whether the recording would prove authentic or doctored.[2]
