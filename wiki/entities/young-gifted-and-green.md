# Young, Gifted & Green

Young, Gifted & Green is a nonprofit organization that received a $20 million federal environmental grant during the Biden administration's 2024 distribution of green energy funds.[1]

## EPA criminal referrals

The award was cited in criminal referrals made in 2026 by [[environmental-protection-agency|EPA]] Administrator [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]] to the EPA inspector general and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].[1] According to the referrals, the organization's chief executive applied for the grant while simultaneously serving on the White House Environmental Justice Advisory Council, a body advising the administration on the very grant priorities at issue.[1]

The case was presented alongside the [[greenhouse-gas-reduction-fund|Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund]] disbursements and the $2 billion [[power-forward-communities|Power Forward Communities]] award as evidence of what Zeldin described as self-dealing within a rushed 2024 grant cycle.[1]
