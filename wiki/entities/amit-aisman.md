# Amit Aisman

Amit Aisman is the State Attorney of Israel.[1]

On December 26, together with Attorney General [[gali-baharav-miara|Gali Baharav-Miara]], Aisman announced that they had ordered an investigation of [[sara-netanyahu|Sara Netanyahu]] on suspicion of witness harassment and obstruction of justice.[1] The order followed a Channel 12 "Uvda" report alleging that she had directed an aide to orchestrate protests and an online campaign against [[hadas-klein|Hadas Klein]], a key witness in the corruption trial of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1] The investigation is being conducted by the Israel Police and the cybercrimes department of the State Attorney's Office.[1]
