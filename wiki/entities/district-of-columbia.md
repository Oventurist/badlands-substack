# District of Columbia

The **District of Columbia** is the federal seat of the United States government. In the April 3, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] essay "Is Jerome Powell a Patriot?", [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] presents D.C. not as an ordinary capital district but as one of three quasi-sovereign corporate city-states that together, in his account, direct world affairs.[1]

## One of three sovereign cities

Carlson writes that there are three cities on earth that come under no national authority: they have separate laws, pay no taxes, maintain their own police forces and possess their own flags of independence, and are in fact corporations.[1] These are the [[city-of-london|City of London]], the District of Columbia and [[vatican|Vatican City]].[1] Together, he argues, they control politicians, the courts, educational institutions, the food supply, natural resources, foreign policies, economies, the media and the money flow of most nations.[1]

Within this division of labor, the City of London is said to control the world's financial system, the Vatican world religion, and D.C. the world's military, with some overlap among the three.[1] Carlson illustrates the hierarchy with the *Mad Max: Beyond Thunderdome* character Master Blaster — a small figure riding the shoulders of a hulking, mentally impaired man — casting London and the Vatican as the riders and Washington as the brute.[1]

## Claimed corporate status and the Act of 1871

Carlson states that Washington, D.C. is "not part of the USA," describing it as ten square miles of land with its own flag and its own independent constitution.[1] He notes the claim, held by some, that the [[act-of-1871|Act of 1871]] passed by Congress created a separate corporation known as THE UNITED STATES together with a corporate government for the District of Columbia, so that D.C. operates as a corporation through that Act.[1] He also relays one of several theories about the three red stars on the District's flag: that they denote D.C., Vatican City and the City of London.[1]

## Relation to foreign financial control

The District's alleged corporate character forms part of Carlson's larger thesis that the American financial system has been controlled by foreign powers since the founding of the nation, and that the shift from [[libor|LIBOR]] to [[sofr|SOFR]] under [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s appointees [[jerome-powell|Jerome Powell]] and [[john-c-williams|John C. Williams]] represents an attempt to reclaim that control for the United States.[1]
