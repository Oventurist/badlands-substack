# Anthony Albanese

Anthony Albanese is the Prime Minister of Australia. In December 2025 he led his government's response to the [[bondi-beach-mass-shooting|Bondi Beach mass shooting]], in which 15 people were killed at a Hanukkah celebration in Sydney.[1]

## Response to the Bondi Beach attack

Albanese said officials' conclusion that the attack was inspired by Islamic State was based on evidence obtained, including "the presence of Islamic State flags in the vehicle that has been seized."[1] He appeared alongside federal police commissioner Krissy Barrett at the Tuesday news conference that first publicly confirmed the suspects' assessed ideology.[1]

## Firearms reform

Albanese announced a tightening of Australia's firearms laws, including a national firearms registry and A$100 million in funding for the states, with new limits on gun ownership and a requirement that buyers be Australian citizens. He stated that "strong, decisive and focused action was needed on gun law reform as an immediate action," and emphasized national unity against antisemitism; critics highlighted government failures in handling rising antisemitic incidents.[1]

Badlands commentators treated the rapid pivot from massacre to disarmament legislation with suspicion, with [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] arguing that a global regime seeking to ban guns everywhere would be willing to kill to obtain such legislation.[1]
