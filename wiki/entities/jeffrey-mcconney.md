# Jeffrey McConney

Jeffrey McConney is the retired comptroller of the [[trump-organization|Trump Organization]] and a witness in the New York criminal prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over hush-money payments.[1]

## Testimony in the hush-money trial

On Monday, May 6, 2024, McConney took the stand in Manhattan and testified about the business records and accounting practices that lie at the heart of the case — the documentation through which reimbursements were recorded and which prosecutors allege was falsified.[1] His testimony followed a morning ruling by Justice [[juan-merchan|Juan Merchan]] holding Trump in contempt for a further violation of the gag order in the case.[1] Merchan sent the jury home for the day shortly after 4 p.m.[1]

McConney's appearance situated him among the long-serving Trump Organization finance staff whose records-keeping became central evidence in the [[trump-hush-money-case|hush-money prosecution]].[1]
