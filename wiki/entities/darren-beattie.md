# Darren Beattie

Darren Beattie is a former [[donald-trump|Trump]] speechwriter who was appointed acting Under Secretary for Public Diplomacy and Public Affairs at the [[state-department|State Department]] in early February 2025.[1] He was already serving in a senior role within the department and was expected to assume the new position on a Monday.[1]

## Background

Beattie previously worked as a speechwriter for Trump but was fired in 2018.[1] In 2020, Trump appointed him to the Commission for the Preservation of American Heritage Abroad.[1] He has been an outspoken critic of US foreign policy, which made his elevation to a senior public diplomacy post a notable move.[1]

## The position

As described by the State Department, the Under Secretary for Public Diplomacy and Public Affairs "leads America's public diplomacy outreach, which includes messaging to counter terrorism and violent extremism."[1] The post also oversees the bureaus of Educational and Cultural Affairs and Global Public Affairs and plays a role in foreign policy development.[1]

## Commentary

In the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] praised the appointment, describing Beattie as a reformed Democrat long outspoken against US foreign policy, and expressed hope that he would revitalize US diplomatic relations abroad.[1] The commentary argued that the State Department had for too long treated other nations as feudal vassals, using USAID, the threat of sanctions, and even the implied threat of the CIA and US military to coerce foreign governments into compliance — behavior it characterized as textbook tyranny and the antithesis of America's founding principles.[1]
