# Gina Haspel

**Gina Haspel** is a former career Central Intelligence Agency officer who served as Director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] during the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Career path under Trump

Trump initially appointed [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]] as CIA Director with Haspel serving directly beneath him.[1] When Trump fired Secretary of State [[rex-tillerson|Rex Tillerson]] and moved Pompeo to the State Department, Haspel was promoted to lead the agency.[1] Badlands Media's [[purple-revolution|Purple Revolution]] analysis argues that from this point Trump effectively stopped relying on the CIA for intelligence and shifted to the Department of Justice under [[william-barr|Bill Barr]], leaving Haspel and the agency out of the loop.[1]

## Post-election rumors and later employment

After the 2020 election, rumors circulated that Haspel had died in a shootout in Ukraine while attempting to access election servers; the article states she is in fact alive and questions who propagated the story and why.[1] Notably, she was not among the officials Trump dismissed after the election, alongside [[mark-milley|Mark Milley]] and [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] — a fact the author cautions does not by itself indicate loyalty to Trump.[1]

Haspel served as CIA station chief in London from 2008 to 2011 and again from 2014 to 2017.[1] The article links these postings to its claim that the Cabal is headquartered in the City of London and that the "Russia, Russia, Russia" narrative originated there in 2016.[1] Her subsequent employment by the British defense contractor [[bae-systems|BAE Systems]] is presented as evidence that senior American officials serve foreign interests.[1]
