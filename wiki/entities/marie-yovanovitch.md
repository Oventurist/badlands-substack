# Marie Yovanovitch

Marie Yovanovitch is a former United States Ambassador to Ukraine whose testimony was central to the first impeachment of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and who, in Badlands' account, was warned contemporaneously that [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s business dealings were corrupting American anti-corruption policy in Kyiv.[1]

## The Kyiv embassy warnings

Government memos obtained by [[just-the-news|Just the News]] through Freedom of Information Act litigation, congressional inquiries, and agency sources indicate that U.S. officials in the Kyiv embassy told then-Ambassador Yovanovitch at the end of the Obama administration that Hunter Biden had in fact impacted the U.S. anti-corruption agenda in Ukraine.[1] Badlands commentary read that record as evidence that [[burisma|Burisma]] was using Hunter Biden to pressure then-Vice President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] into securing the removal of Prosecutor General [[viktor-shokin|Viktor Shokin]], and that senior officials knew it and escalated the alarm up the chain to Yovanovitch.[1]

## Impeachment testimony

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] concluded from the memos that Yovanovitch lied under oath during the first House impeachment inquiry, alongside a parallel claim about State Department official George Kent, and that exculpatory evidence was withheld from Trump's defense team during that inquiry and the subsequent Senate trial.[1] The broader charge advanced in the commentary is that the [[state-department|State Department]] and the wider federal establishment covered the matter up for years.[1]
