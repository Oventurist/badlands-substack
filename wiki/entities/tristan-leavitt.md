# Tristan Leavitt

Tristan Leavitt is the president of [[empower-oversight|Empower Oversight]], the whistleblower-advocacy organization that represents [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]] whistleblower [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]].[1]

## Dispute with the Washington Post

In July 2023 Leavitt argued publicly that if the [[washington-post|Washington Post]] did not "come clean" and state that Shapley had never spoken with the paper, then the Post was effectively acting as an accessory to [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s legal defense.[1] The dispute arose because Hunter Biden's attorneys — led by [[abbe-lowell|Abbe Lowell]] — had accused Shapley of illegally leaking to the Post for its October 2022 story headlined "Federal agents see chargeable tax, gun-purchase case against Hunter Biden."[1]

Lowell cited that story in a letter in late June 2023 attacking Shapley and other IRS whistleblowers over alleged "illegal leaks," writing that the whistleblowers "may be claiming that title in an attempt to evade their own misconduct" as part of "an obvious ploy to feed the misinformation campaign to harm our client, Hunter Biden, as a vehicle to attack his father."[1] Shapley has consistently maintained that he was not a source for the Post in that story or in any other story.[1] Leavitt's demand was that the paper state clearly and on the record that Shapley was not its source.[1]
