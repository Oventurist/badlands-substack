# WNEP

WNEP is an ABC affiliate television station serving northeastern Pennsylvania. In late October 2024 the station became a national story after it briefly broadcast fabricated presidential election "results" for Pennsylvania during live programming, days before the actual vote.[1]

## Premature election results broadcast

On October 30, 2024, WNEP flashed Pennsylvania presidential results on screen during its broadcast of the Formula 1 race in Mexico City, leaving them visible for several minutes.[1] The displayed figures showed [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] defeating [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by 52 percent to 47 percent, with 3,293,712 votes for Harris against 2,997,793 for Trump.[1]

After viewers expressed confusion at what they were seeing, the station published an explanation describing the display as part of an internal test that was never intended to reach air. "Those numbers should not have appeared on the screen, and it was an error by WNEP that they did," the station said.[1]

The incident was folded into a wider run of late-October 2024 election-integrity stories covered in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], alongside the [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion]] voter assist terminal programming issue in Michigan and the Colorado voting system password leak.[1]
