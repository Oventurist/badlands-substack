# Kazan

**Kazan** is a city in southwestern Russia situated on the banks of the Volga and Kazanka rivers, historically described as the capital of ancient Tartaria and today counted among the most important cities in the Russian Federation.[1] Badlands Media commentary noted that Russia traditionally recognizes "three capitals" — Moscow, St Petersburg, and Kiev — though the last is no longer part of the country, and that in 2009 [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] officially designated Kazan as "The Third Capital" of Russia.[1]

## Symbolic and geopolitical significance

In the June 13, 2024 Badlands News Brief, contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that the name Kazan carried layered significance beyond its geography, suggesting the term "telegraphs 'Peace'" and that the city clearly held particular importance for Putin.[1] The same commentary asserted an ancestral connection between Kazan and neighboring Kazakhstan, which the author placed within a speculative "dark triangle" of occult mysticism alongside Ukraine and Israel — described explicitly as a theory under development rather than an established claim.[1]

Kazan hosted the BRICS Games — framed in the brief as an Olympics-style competition — launched by Putin in June 2024, and was named as the site of the [[brics|BRICS]] summit Russia hosted in October 2024.[1] The brief connected these events to a wider diplomatic calendar including a July 4 [[shanghai-cooperation-organization|Shanghai Cooperation Organization]] summit in the Kazakh capital Astana, a planned Putin meeting with Turkish President Erdogan in Astana, and use of the Astana Platform to mediate the Syrian civil war.[1]

## Naval namesake

The Russian nuclear-powered cruise missile submarine *Kazan*, of the Yasen class, shares the city's name and became the focus of Badlands commentary after it arrived off the coast of Cuba in June 2024 ahead of joint air and maritime exercises in the Caribbean.[1] The vessel carries the [[zircon-hypersonic-missile|Zircon hypersonic missile]] and has been a longstanding source of concern to the United States and [[nato|NATO]] because of its stealth and strike capabilities.[1]

## December 2024 drone attack

On 21 December 2024, Kazan was struck by a major Ukrainian drone attack launched from some 1,000 kilometres (620 miles) from the frontline, damaging residential buildings and briefly closing the city's international airport.[2] One drone hit a high-rise apartment block and another damaged a skyscraper in the city of more than 1.3 million; Russian foreign ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova said two drones struck a 37-storey apartment block and that no casualties were reported.[2] Three further drones were shot down and three suppressed by air defences, residents were evacuated, and all major public events in the area were cancelled.[2] The city, about 800 kilometres east of Moscow, had hosted the [[2024-brics-kazan-summit|BRICS summit]] two months earlier, a point Badlands commentators emphasised in framing the strike as a deliberate provocation.[2] Details are collected at [[2024-kazan-drone-attack|the 2024 Kazan drone attack]].[2]
