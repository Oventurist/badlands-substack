# Richard Whitney

Richard "Dick" Whitney was chairman of the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] during the period of the Senate banking investigations of the early 1930s.[1] In Badlands Media's account of the [[pecora-commission|Pecora Commission]] hearings, Whitney's fall followed within days of the forced resignation of [[charles-e-mitchell|Charles Mitchell]] of [[national-city-bank|National City Bank]]; Whitney "left the court in handcuffs."[1]

His downfall was part of the highly publicized crackdown that spotlighted the criminal schemes used to gamble savings and commercial bank deposits on securities and futures markets — schemes that led to the orchestrated collapse of the bubble economy in 1929.[1]
