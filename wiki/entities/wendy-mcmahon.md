# Wendy McMahon

Wendy McMahon is an American broadcast executive who served as president and chief executive officer of [[cbs-news|CBS News]], assuming leadership of the division in 2023.[1] She announced her resignation on Monday, May 19, 2025, citing disagreements with the company over its future direction.[1]

## Resignation

In a memo to staff, McMahon called her tenure "a privilege and joy" while acknowledging recent difficulties, writing: "It's become clear that the company and I do not agree on the path forward. It's time for me to move on and for this organization to move forward with new leadership."[1] George Cheeks, president and chief executive of [[cbs|CBS]], responded in a separate memo praising her contributions and stating that under her leadership "the competitive position and culture at our television stations have improved dramatically, and we've expanded local news significantly."[1]

Her departure marked the second major leadership change at CBS News in a short period, following the exit of longtime [[60-minutes|60 Minutes]] executive producer [[bill-owens|Bill Owens]], who stepped down after alleging that the program had lost its journalistic independence and citing unsustainable internal pressures from [[paramount-global|Paramount]] executives in his farewell memo.[1]

## Context: the Harris interview lawsuit

The resignation occurred against the backdrop of litigation over the network's edited [[60-minutes|60 Minutes]] interview with Kamala Harris, a dispute that had become a central grievance of the American right regarding legacy-media editorial practice.[1] The May 21, 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] carried the National Pulse report of the resignation as a bonus item, presenting the successive departures of Owens and McMahon as evidence of institutional turmoil inside CBS News.[1]
