# Sean Curran

Sean Curran is the Director of the [[united-states-secret-service|United States Secret Service]] during the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## UFC Freedom 250 plot response

Curran was one of the two federal officials publicly credited with the disruption of an alleged domestic terror plot against [[ufc-freedom-250|UFC Freedom 250]], the mixed martial arts event held on the [[white-house|White House]] South Lawn on June 14, 2026 as part of Trump's 80th birthday weekend.[1] The [[fbi|FBI]] learned of the threat around June 10 and worked with law enforcement partners including the Secret Service on a multi-state operation that produced arrests and secured the event without incident.[1] While FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]] credited the rapid, coordinated response for stopping the alleged plans, Curran highlighted the agencies' around-the-clock efforts in protecting the gathering.[1]

## DJ Daniel honorary agent moment

At President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[2025-trump-address-to-joint-session-of-congress|address to a joint session of Congress]] on March 4, 2025, 13-year-old cancer survivor Devarjaye "DJ" Daniel was named an honorary United States Secret Service agent and embraced Curran, the Secret Service Director, in a moment Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described as one that "we could feel through the screen."[2]
