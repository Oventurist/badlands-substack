# Beto O'Rourke

Beto O'Rourke is a Texas Democratic politician and organizer whose political network became a target of state investigation in August 2025 amid the fight over mid-decade congressional redistricting in Texas.[1]

## 2025 Texas quorum-break investigations

O'Rourke founded the political action committee [[powered-by-people|Powered by People]], into which Texas Attorney General [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] launched an investigation in early August 2025.[1] A day later, Paxton announced a second probe into the [[texas-majority-project|Texas Majority Project]], a PAC founded by former O'Rourke staffers and reportedly funded by [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1]

Paxton alleged that the two groups may have violated state law through unlawful financial coordination, potential bribery of Democratic legislators who fled Texas to break quorum, and illegal fundraising activities.[1] The lawmakers' walkout was intended to block a Republican redistricting bill that would have created five additional GOP congressional seats.[1]
