# Patrick Gaspard

Patrick Gaspard is the former president of the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], the charitable network of [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1]

## Replacement at Open Society

In December 2020 it was announced that [[mark-malloch-brown|Mark Malloch Brown]] would succeed Gaspard as president of the Open Society Foundations effective 1 January 2021.[1] Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] describes Gaspard as a man with connections to the Obama administration and the DNC, and asks why Soros would remove him immediately after the 2020 election in favour of a figure who had been chairman of the board of the holding company behind the voting machine maker [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]].[1] Carlson floats two possible motives: distancing Barack Obama from the Biden presidency, or placing Malloch Brown where he could more easily censor election fraud information and debate.[1]
