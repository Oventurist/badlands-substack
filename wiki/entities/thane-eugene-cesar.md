# Thane Eugene Cesar

Thane Eugene Cesar was a security guard present at the 1968 killing of Senator [[robert-f-kennedy|Robert F. Kennedy]], and the man whom Robert F. Kennedy Jr. believes actually shot his father.[1]

## Alleged role in the RFK assassination

According to the official narrative described in the Badlands Media essay "Patsies of the CIA and FBI," Kennedy was killed by a lone gunman, [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]], portrayed as an angry supporter of the Palestinians upset by Kennedy's support of Israel.[1] The article calls this ironic, noting that the Kennedys in fact fought to keep Israel from obtaining nuclear weapons — one of the several reasons cited for why [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] was killed.[1]

Against that account, the article reports that Robert F. Kennedy Jr. believes his father was killed by Cesar, an employee of [[lockheed-martin|Lockheed]]'s Burbank facility, which manufactured the CIA-produced U-2 spy planes.[1] Cesar is said to have worked in an "off limits" area under the control of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]].[1]

## Context

The Cesar allegation is paired in the article with a second claim: that in 2010 Sirhan's lawyers accused the CIA of hypnotizing their client and making him "an involuntary participant," and that they believed the agency feared Kennedy because he opposed it.[1] Taken together, the two claims form the article's account of the RFK case — a controlled or hypnotised public suspect in front, and an intelligence-linked shooter behind — which parallels its treatment of [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]] and [[james-earl-ray|James Earl Ray]].[1]
