# Belfer Center for Science and International Affairs

The **Belfer Center for Science and International Affairs** is a research center at [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] described in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the mechanism through which Harvard conducts its military and intelligence integration.[1]

Once led by former Secretary of Defense [[ash-carter|Ash Carter]], the Center is characterized by the article as "a sandbox for military-academic policy coordination."[1] It is said to receive grants and access from defense contractors and Department of Defense budgets, and to channel students into [[pentagon|Pentagon]], [[darpa|DARPA]] and [[state-department|State Department]] fellowships.[1] Where [[yale-university|Yale]]'s [[skull-and-bones|Skull & Bones]] pipeline is described as overt, the Belfer Center represents what the author calls Harvard's more discreet mode of operation within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
