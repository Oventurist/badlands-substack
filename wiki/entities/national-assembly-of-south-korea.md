# National Assembly of South Korea

The National Assembly is the unicameral legislature of [[south-korea|South Korea]] and the institution that reversed President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol's]] imposition of martial law on the night of December 3, 2024.[1]

## Role in the 2024 martial law crisis

During the [[2024-south-korea-martial-law-declaration|2024 South Korea martial law declaration]], troops surrounded parliament while lawmakers voted to reject military rule.[1] Yoon said his government withdrew military personnel following the bipartisan parliamentary vote, and martial law was formally lifted at approximately 4:30 a.m. during a Cabinet meeting after roughly six hours in effect.[1]

Yoon's decree, reportedly a response to continued deadlock in parliament, prompted lawmakers to race to the legislature to reverse the order.[1] Opposition leader [[lee-jae-myung|Lee Jae-myung]] livestreamed himself scaling the walls of the National Assembly, and similar videos showed other opposition members of parliament climbing the walls of the legislature to gain access while decrying the declaration as a coup.[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary noted that the assembly's rejection of the declaration was unanimous, and that there was no apparent evidence of civil unrest that would have justified military rule.[1]
