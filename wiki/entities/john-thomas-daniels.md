# John Thomas Daniels

John Thomas Daniels was an American agro-industrialist and the founder of Archer Daniels Midland.[1] He is named in "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 8" among the notable public-facing members of Yale's [[skull-and-bones|Skull and Bones]] society, cited as evidence that the Order's placements extended beyond government and banking into the commanding heights of American agribusiness.[1]
