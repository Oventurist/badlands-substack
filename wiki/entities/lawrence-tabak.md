# Lawrence Tabak

Dr. Lawrence Tabak served as director of the [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]].[1]

## 2023 transgender youth study letter

In May 2023, more than a dozen Republican lawmakers — including [[lauren-boebert|Lauren Boebert]], [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] and [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] — penned a letter to Tabak demanding answers about an NIH-funded two-year study that prescribed cross-sex hormones to hundreds of young people.[1] The study, titled "Psychosocial Functioning in Transgender Youth after 2 Years of Hormones," examined 315 individuals between the ages of 12 and 20 who identify as transgender and were given cross-sex hormones; the portion involving 240 children was funded by NIH, which acknowledged that "two young people tragically died by suicide."[1] Despite the deaths, the NIH carried the study through to its conclusion.[1]
