# Sam Cooper

Sam Cooper is a Canadian investigative journalist who writes the Substack publication *The Bureau*, which focuses on transnational organized crime, Chinese state-linked influence operations, and the movement of narcotics money through North American financial institutions.[1]

## Reporting on bank money laundering

Cooper's reporting placed him at the center of the 2024 story linking fentanyl profits to major banks. In early September 2024 he took part in a conversation, cited by financial outlet ZeroHedge, with former [[state-department|State Department]] senior investigator [[david-asher|David Asher]] about People's Republic of China–linked narcotics networks operating out of Toronto.[1] That discussion preceded the [[toronto-dominion-bank|Toronto-Dominion Bank]] guilty plea and the subsequent disclosure by [[bank-of-america|Bank of America]] that it faced possible federal enforcement action over its [[bank-secrecy-act|Bank Secrecy Act]] and anti-money-laundering compliance programs.[1]

Within the Badlands Media framing, Cooper's work formed part of a chain of evidence connecting cartel finance, banking compliance failures, and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s proposals to seize cartel assets.[1]
