# Tom Pritzker

Tom Pritzker is an American businessman and heir to the Hyatt hotel fortune, long associated with the Pritzker family's corporate and philanthropic holdings.

## Resignation amid the Epstein disclosures

The February 17, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0a2|Badlands Brief]], citing reporting by [[axios|Axios]], lists Pritzker among the figures facing career consequences from the latest round of Epstein file disclosures. According to that reporting, Pritzker stepped down as executive chair of Hyatt as part of a wider wave of resignations and investigations across business, politics, and law attributed to the release of documents under the [[epstein-files-transparency-act|Epstein Files Transparency Act]].

He is named alongside Paul Weiss chair [[brad-karp|Brad Karp]], who resigned, and Commerce Secretary [[howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]], described as facing investigations or pressure to resign. The Badlands commentary accompanying the item, by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], cautions that appearance in the files is not itself evidence of wrongdoing and questions whether guilt can be attributed uniformly to all figures who associated with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].
