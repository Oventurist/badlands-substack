# Black Lives Matter OKC

Black Lives Matter OKC (BLMOKC) is an Oklahoma City activist organization affiliated with the broader [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] movement. In December 2025 it became the subject of a federal criminal case brought against its longtime executive director.[1]

## Structure and funding

BLMOKC was not a registered tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. It instead accepted charitable donations through its affiliation with the Arizona-based [[alliance-for-global-justice|Alliance for Global Justice]], which served as its fiscal sponsor.[1] That arrangement obliged BLMOKC to spend funds only as 501(c)(3) rules permitted, to account fully for disbursements upon request, and to obtain consent before purchasing real estate.[1]

The organization maintained bank, PayPal, and Cash App accounts to which its executive director had access.[1]

## Federal indictment

A federal grand jury indictment, unsealed and announced by U.S. Attorney Robert J. Troester, charged [[tashella-dickerson|Tashella Sheri Amore Dickerson]], 52, of Oklahoma City, with wire fraud and money laundering.[1] Dickerson had served as executive director since at least 2016.[1] The indictment alleges that beginning in June 2020 and continuing through at least October 2025 she embezzled funds from the organization's accounts for her personal benefit.[1]
