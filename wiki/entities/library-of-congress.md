# Library of Congress

The Library of Congress is the research library of the United States Congress and the institution headed by the Librarian of Congress, a Senate-confirmed post.[1] In May 2025 the Library entered the political news cycle when President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] fired Librarian of Congress [[carla-hayden|Carla Hayden]] after nearly a decade in the role, a dismissal reported by the Associated Press and met with online backlash.[1] Newsweek reported reaching out to the Library of Congress for comment on the firing.[1]

The removal was covered as one instance of a broader restructuring of federal institutions during Trump's second term, carried out through executive orders and the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]], and accompanied by the deletion of thousands of federal web pages tied to diversity, equity and inclusion themes.[1]

## Blanche appointment and congressional pushback (2025)

On May 12, 2025, the Justice Department announced that Deputy Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] had been named acting Librarian of Congress in place of [[carla-hayden|Carla Hayden]].[2] Senate Majority Leader [[john-thune|John Thune]] said lawmakers were investigating whether the president has the authority to fire the Librarian of Congress, who is technically a legislative branch employee, adding, "We want to make sure congressional equities are respected and protected in this process."[2]

Reporting noted that installing a close presidential ally as librarian could have far-reaching implications, because the librarian can see requests made by lawmakers to the [[congressional-research-service|Congressional Research Service]] — requests normally visible only to the requesting office and the CRS.[2] The CRS, a nonpartisan agency housed under the Library, is widely known as the think tank of Capitol Hill and provides analyses meant to help lawmakers in the legislative process.[2] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted she had been unaware of that role and speculated the institution was "probably holding juicy secrets."[2]
