# Library of Congress

The Library of Congress is the research library of the United States Congress and the institution headed by the Librarian of Congress, a Senate-confirmed post.[1] In May 2025 the Library entered the political news cycle when President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] fired Librarian of Congress [[carla-hayden|Carla Hayden]] after nearly a decade in the role, a dismissal reported by the Associated Press and met with online backlash.[1] Newsweek reported reaching out to the Library of Congress for comment on the firing.[1]

The removal was covered as one instance of a broader restructuring of federal institutions during Trump's second term, carried out through executive orders and the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]], and accompanied by the deletion of thousands of federal web pages tied to diversity, equity and inclusion themes.[1]
