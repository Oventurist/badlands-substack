# Saucon Valley School District

Saucon Valley School District is a public school district in Pennsylvania that became the defendant in litigation over whether it must permit the [[after-school-satan-club|After School Satan Club]] to use its facilities.[1]

## Federal court ruling

The [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]] filed suit on behalf of [[satanic-temple|The Satanic Temple]], arguing that a district hosting Christian clubs cannot exclude other religions.[1] In May 2023 a federal judge ruled that the district must allow the club to meet in district facilities, an outcome the ACLU announced on Monday as "a victory for free speech and religious freedom."[1]

The decision was covered by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and framed within the Badlands News Brief as evidence of an ongoing spiritual conflict playing out in American institutions.[1]
