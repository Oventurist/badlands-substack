# Robert J. Conrad

**Robert J. Conrad** is the director of the [[administrative-office-of-the-us-courts|Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts]], the administrative arm of the federal judiciary.[1]

## America First Legal lawsuit

In May 2025 Conrad was named as a defendant, alongside Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]], in a lawsuit brought by the [[america-first-legal|America First Legal Foundation]], the pro-Trump legal group founded by White House aide [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]].[1] The complaint targets Conrad in his capacity as director of the Administrative Office and accuses that office and the [[united-states-judicial-conference|U.S. Judicial Conference]] of performing regulatory actions beyond the judiciary's core functions, arguing their records should be subject to Freedom of Information Act requests.[1]
