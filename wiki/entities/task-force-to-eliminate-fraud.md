# Task Force to Eliminate Fraud

The Task Force to Eliminate Fraud is a U.S. federal interagency effort led by Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] to combat fraud against federal programs and taxpayer funds.[1]

When Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] announced the [[national-fraud-enforcement-division|National Fraud Enforcement Division]] on April 7, 2026, he stated that the new [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] division would coordinate closely with the task force, framing the arrangement as a "supercharging" of enforcement against those exploiting federal programs and taxpayer generosity.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] linked the effort to the disclosures produced by [[doge|DOGE]], arguing that those revelations "require justice not just air time" and that hiring full-time prosecutors implies justice is part of the plan.[1]
