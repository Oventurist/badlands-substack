# Willi Munzenberg

Willi Munzenberg was a radical Bolshevik and, with [[georg-lukacs|Georg Lukacs]], a founder in 1923 of the Institute for Social Research in Frankfurt, Germany — the organization better known as the [[frankfurt-school|Frankfurt School]].[1]

Munzenberg is described as an associate of [[leon-trotsky|Leon Trotsky]] who played an important role in the effort to bring Western society to a state of nihilistic despair sufficient for a one-world technocratic government to be demanded by the masses.[1] The school he helped found produced the intellectual framework for a global aesthetic and scientific revolution premised, in Matthew Ehret's characterization, on the worship of decay, ugliness and death within a Weberian-Freudian-Marxist synthesis.[1]
