# Harri Hursti

Harri Hursti is an election security researcher named in Badlands commentary as part of what [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described as the "acceptable opposition" to official election security authorities.[1]

## Role in the "blockers" theory

In analysis published during the [[coomer-v-lindell|Lindell trial]] coverage, Hursti was grouped with [[j-alex-halderman|J. Alex Halderman]] as a researcher who documents election system vulnerabilities but nonetheless concludes that American elections cannot be hacked, rigged, stolen or subverted where paper ballots and risk-limiting audits exist.[1] That posture, the commentary argued, complements official authorities such as [[chris-krebs|Chris Krebs]] and [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]] in forming a coordinated set of "blockers" preventing the truth about elections from becoming known.[1]
