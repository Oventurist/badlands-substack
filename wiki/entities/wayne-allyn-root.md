# Wayne Allyn Root

**Wayne Allyn Root** (rendered "Wayne Allen Root" in the source article) is an American conservative broadcaster who interviewed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on Christmas Eve 2022.[1]

## Christmas Eve 2022 interview

Two elements of the interview are treated as significant in Badlands Media's coverage.[1] First, Trump used it to explain why he had endorsed [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] for Speaker of the House in the [[118th-congress|118th Congress]], an endorsement that had provoked strong objections within the "truther" community on the grounds that McCarthy was a RINO.[1] Second, Trump escalated his standing claim about [[operation-warp-speed|Operation Warp Speed]], moving from his customary assertion that the program had saved "tens of millions" of lives to a claim that it had saved 100 million.[1]

Badlands contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] treats the shift from "tens of millions" to "100 million" as a deliberate signal warranting investigation, and builds much of her essay "Why Trump Pushed the Vax" around attempting to reconcile that figure with global [[johnson-and-johnson|Johnson & Johnson]] dose data.[1]
