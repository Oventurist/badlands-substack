# Peanut the Squirrel

Peanut was a rescued eastern gray squirrel kept for roughly seven years by Mark Luongo at P'nuts Freedom Farm in New York, where the animal became a widely followed internet sensation before being seized and euthanized by state authorities in late October 2024.[1]

## Seizure and euthanasia

Acting on an anonymous complaint — reportedly lodged by a woman said to be in Texas — the [[new-york-state-department-of-environmental-conservation|New York State Department of Environmental Conservation]] staged a five-hour raid on Luongo's home on a Wednesday in late October 2024.[1] Officers took Peanut into custody along with a raccoon named Fred, and the state subsequently euthanized both animals, stating that the killings were carried out "in order to test for rabies."[1] No evidence of rabies was found.[1]

Luongo announced the death in a widely shared post: "RIP MY BEST FRIEND. Thank you for the best 7 years of my life. Thank you for bringing so much joy to us and the world. I'm sorry I failed you but thank you for everything."[1]

## Political reaction and narrative impact

The episode ignited immediate national outrage in the final days before the 2024 U.S. presidential election, with [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and other outlets reporting that "the internet is ablaze with rage" over the seizure.[1] Commentators at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] framed the killing as a compact parable of state power, arguing that "a government big enough to fulfill all your wishes is powerful enough to raid your home and kill your pets."[1] Badlands writer Ashe in America noted that the story escaped conventional political media entirely, reaching audiences through platforms such as NewsBreak and Xbox Live chat rather than legacy outlets.[1]

Commentator [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] captured the perceived electoral significance by asking on X, "Are we gonna win this election because of this f'ing squirrel?" — a question Badlands answered affirmatively in narrative terms, holding that "in US elections, the narrative is all there is."[1] Badlands writers also observed that public attention fixated on Peanut while Fred the Raccoon, euthanized in the same operation, was largely forgotten.[1]
