# Brooklyn Nets

The Brooklyn Nets are a National Basketball Association franchise based in Brooklyn, New York. In Badlands Media coverage the team figures principally through its handling of guard [[kyrie-irving|Kyrie Irving]] during the 2022–23 season.[1]

After Irving shared a documentary described as antisemitic on social media and repeatedly declined to apologize, the Nets suspended him. Irving then posted an apology on Instagram, served his suspension, and rejoined the team.[1]

The relationship ended the following season-half: after contract extension talks broke down, Irving requested a trade and the Nets dealt him to the [[dallas-mavericks|Dallas Mavericks]] in early February 2023. Shortly afterward, reporters noticed that Irving's apology post had been removed from Instagram, prompting speculation — voiced by Badlands contributor [[absolutetruth1776]] — that the apology had been extracted by the club and the [[national-basketball-association|NBA]] as a precondition for his return to play.[1]
