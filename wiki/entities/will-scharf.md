# Will Scharf

Will Scharf is chair of the [[national-capital-planning-commission|National Capital Planning Commission]], the federal body that reviews construction and planning projects in Washington, D.C.[1]

## Role in the White House ballroom approval

Scharf presided over the commission's 8-1 vote approving President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s plan to build a new [[white-house-ballroom|White House ballroom]].[1] Addressing the volume of public comment the panel received, Scharf said many submissions raised matters outside the commission's authority — including funding, demolition, and political criticism — and summarized the point by saying, "We are not some sort of free-ranging ballroom justice commission."[1] The statement served to delimit the panel's remit to planning and design questions.[1]

After the vote, Trump publicly thanked the commission on [[truth-social|Truth Social]], highlighting the 8-1 margin and noting that Sen. [[rand-paul|Rand Paul]] had voted in favor.[1]
