# Hulk Hogan

Hulk Hogan is the professional wrestler whose name surfaced in November 2024 as a suggested appointee to the Florida U.S. Senate seat being vacated by [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] upon his nomination as Secretary of State.[1]

Badlands commentary noted with amusement that CNN was "openly advocating" for Hogan's appointment to the seat, and framed the prospect in wrestling idiom: if it happened, "TrumpaMania is heading to DC, and will be running wild."[1] The item supplied the title of the brief, "TrumpaMania Reigns ... with a Russian Twist."[1]

## Death

Hogan died on Thursday, July 24, 2025, at the age of 71.[2] First responders were called to his home in Clearwater Beach, Florida, at around 9:50 a.m. after a call reporting a cardiac arrest, and he was taken to Morton Plant Hospital, where he was pronounced dead.[2] The Clearwater Police Department stated that there were "no signs of foul play or suspicious activity," according to Maj. Nate Burnside.[2] His legal name was Terry Bollea.[2]

Both the police and World Wrestling Entertainment confirmed the death.[2] Hogan was described as perhaps the biggest star in WWE's history: he was the main draw for the first WrestleMania in 1985 and remained a fixture for years, facing opponents ranging from Andre the Giant and Randy Savage to The Rock and company chairman Vince McMahon.[2]

[[badlands-media|Badlands]] contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called the news sad and wrote that he was "forever grateful that he gave us Trump-a-Mania," closing with "Rest in peace, brother."[2] See [[trumpamania|TrumpaMania]].[2]

## Kamala Harris body-slam remarks (September 2024)

At a promotional event for his new beer in early September 2024, Hogan joked about body slamming Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] and questioned her racial identity.[3] In video obtained by TMZ, the 71-year-old asked a jeering crowd, "Do you want me to body slam Kamala Harris?!" and "Do you want me to drop the leg on Kamala?!", referring to one of his signature wrestling moves.[3] The comments, which included a remark about Harris's Indian heritage, came amid calls to stop violent political rhetoric following the assassination attempt on [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] nearly six weeks earlier, and were reported by [[bbc|the BBC]].[3]

The same brief noted that [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], guest-hosting Steve Bannon's War Room, wore a "Trumpamania" shirt that Tuesday specifically in honour of Hogan's speech at the Republican National Convention, which Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] took as confirmation of his [[trumpamania|Trumpamania 2024]] thesis.[3]

## 2024 Republican National Convention appearance

Hogan spoke on the final night of the [[2024-republican-national-convention|2024 Republican National Convention]], roughly an hour before a video tribute recalling [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2013 [[world-wrestling-entertainment|WWE]] Hall of Fame induction was invoked in Badlands commentary.[4] On stage he demonstrated [[kayfabe|kayfabe]] by moving in and out of his wrestling persona: flexing and ripping off his shirt to reveal a "Trump/Vance 2024" tank top one moment, then reintroducing himself to the audience by his legal name, Terry Bollea, and calmly explaining his longtime friendship with Trump the next.[4] He then returned to character, coining "[[trumpamania|Trumpmania]]" for the aura surrounding Trump and "Trumpmaniacs" for the MAGA faithful.[4]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that scheduling Hogan and [[linda-mcmahon|Linda McMahon]] within hours of one another was a deliberate communications choice rather than a coincidence, and noted that Hogan had headlined WrestleMania IV in 1988 against Andre the Giant at Trump Plaza — the event that began Trump's relationship with the McMahon family.[4]

## Speculation About a Florida Senate Seat (2024)

Following the November 2024 selection of [[linda-mcmahon|Linda McMahon]] as secretary of education, Badlands commentary speculated that Terry Bollea, better known as Hulk Hogan, might be appointed to Marco Rubio's vacated Florida Senate seat, framing such an appointment as a redemption arc for [[world-wrestling-entertainment|WWE]] figures after McMahon's own 2010 Senate loss in Connecticut to [[richard-blumenthal|Richard Blumenthal]].[5]

## Death and "Trumpamania" commentary (July 2025)

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of July 29, 2025, contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] referred to "the passing of the legendary Hulk Hogan" as a moment when "Trumpa-a-Mania" appeared to be winding down — before President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s new armored golf escort, [[golf-force-one|"Golf Force One"]], supplied fresh political lore.[6]

## "Jesse Watters Primetime" remarks on the Biden pardons (January 2025)

Appearing on Fox News Channel's "Jesse Watters Primetime" on Monday, January 20, 2025, Hogan quipped about the flurry of pardons issued by [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] in the closing moments of his presidency, which included clemency for family members involved in the Biden family business such as brothers [[james-biden|James]] and [[frank-biden|Frank Biden]].[7] Asked by host [[jesse-watters|Jesse Watters]] how he was feeling, Hogan replied, "Brother, I am so, so stressed out. Brother, you know, riddle me this, riddle me that nobody's afraid of the old man who don't know where he's at, brother. What a day."[7] He went on: "Pardon me this. Pardon me that. Pardon my brother, my uncle, my sister, Cheney. Everybody in the world. Fauci, he pardons. The only person he didn't pardon was Hunter's little baby with a stripper in case he does anything wrong" — a reference to [[navy-joan-roberts|Navy Joan Roberts]], the daughter of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and [[lunden-roberts|Lunden Roberts]].[7]

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the clip was time-stamped 8:41 PM Eastern, placing it in the time slot of the Badlands program The Book of Trump, which had been cancelled at the last minute that night so the hosts could remain on the inauguration livestream — an hour during which [[donald-trump|Trump]] was signing executive orders and fielding press questions.[7] He counted the appearance as putting Hogan at three-for-three for going viral during The Book of Trump's 8:00 PM hour since the show premiered on January 6, and noted that during that same Monday night's WWE live show, wrestler CM Punk called out Hogan and demanded a match, promising to "kill" Hulkamania.[7] He read the challenge as both a reason for Hogan to keep the streak alive and an example of "pageantry" removed from politics — the same contrived fiction, he argued, practiced in Washington, DC.[7]

## Suggested role in the second Trump administration

In an interview with Fox News's Brian Kilmeade broadcast in late November 2024, Hogan suggested that President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] might give him a role in the incoming administration.[8] He recounted a conversation with Trump at the October rally at Madison Square Garden in which, as Hogan told it, Trump said: "You know something, you'd be great to run the President's Council on Physical Fitness."[8] The body in question, the [[presidents-council-on-sports-fitness-and-nutrition|President's Council on Sports, Fitness and Nutrition]], is a federal advisory panel promoting healthy eating and physical activity that President Biden renewed through September 30, 2025.[8]

Hogan said the suggestion arose backstage after the rally from a discussion of Robert Kennedy and nutrition, in which he observed that many foreign countries will not let their people eat the food sold in America, calling it "so bad" and saying it had "poisoned a generation of kids."[8] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] joked that the Badlands News Brief had "willed this into existence," declaring that "the TrumpaManiacs are running wild, brother."[8]
