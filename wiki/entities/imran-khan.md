# Imran Khan

Imran Khan is the former Prime Minister of [[pakistan]] whose 2022 removal from office is presented in the Badlands Media essay "And Iran, Iran So Far Away" as one of the foreign policy moves that accompanied the Biden administration's realignment toward [[iran]].

## Background

The article describes Khan as "yet another notch in the belt of the regime change machine at the US [[state-department]]": a populist figure, former cricket star, and founder of the pakistan tehreek e insaf (PTI) party. He was at first perceived as having ties to the military establishment, including the [[inter-services-intelligence]] (ISI), which the article calls Pakistan's CIA and characterises as being kept on a tight leash by the United States.

## Break with the establishment

Although Khan may initially have benefited from ISI support, his relationship with the agency became strained during his tenure. The article draws an explicit parallel with [[donald-trump]]: Khan "seemed to have had his own plans about how to run the country, plans that went against his country's own rogue intelligence and the foreign policy establishment."

A primary reason a target was placed on his back, according to the source, was his "aggressively neutral" stance on [[russia]] and his threats to block Pakistani arms shipments to Ukraine — an independent foreign policy aiming to maintain friendly relations with both Washington and Moscow. The article concludes that "the establishment dislikes leaders making their own foreign policy decisions."

## Ouster

Khan was removed by a parliamentary no-confidence vote in April 2022, which he attributed to military and ISI interference. He openly accused the military — including then-ISI chief nadeem anjum and former army chief General qamar javed bajwa — of orchestrating his removal, after which his relationship with the agency deteriorated significantly. The article states that with Khan gone, CIA- and deep-state-friendly assets once again run Pakistan and the ISI, and connects this to Pakistan's subsequent threats to nuke [[israel]]. It further asserts that Biden, having overthrown Khan's democratically elected government, then attempted regime change against [[narendra-modi]] in [[india]].
