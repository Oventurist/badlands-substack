# James Clapper

James Clapper is a retired United States Air Force lieutenant general who served as Director of National Intelligence during the administration of President [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]], making him the nominal head of the United States intelligence community.

## Role in the 2017 Intelligence Community Assessment

The Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026 cites reporting that the [[2017-intelligence-community-assessment|January 2017 Intelligence Community Assessment]] on Russian interference in the 2016 election "was written at the direction of then-President Obama and largely overseen by then-FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]], former CIA Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] and former Director of National Intelligence James Clapper." That assessment concluded that [[russia|Russia]] had interfered in the election through cyber operations and influence campaigns intended to undermine confidence in the electoral process and to harm Democratic candidate [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].

In the framing adopted by Badlands contributors, Clapper is one of the senior officials whose direction of the assessment forms part of the accountability inquiry now examining [[crossfire-hurricane|Crossfire Hurricane]] and the broader [[russiagate|Russiagate]] episode, with [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] posing the question of who was giving orders to assessment co-author [[gavin-wilde|Gavin Wilde]] between 2017 and 2019.
