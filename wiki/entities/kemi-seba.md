# Kemi Seba

Kemi Seba (rendered "Kema Seba" in the source) is a French-born Beninese politician who has served as an advisor to Abdourahamane Tchiani, leader of the military junta in Niger — one of the three states comprising the [[alliance-of-sahel-states|Alliance of Sahel States]].[1] In January of the year preceding the December 2025 brief, Seba announced his intention to run for president of [[benin|Benin]] the following year.[1]

During the December 2025 coup attempt in Benin, Seba released a French-language video in which he claimed that Nigerian and French special forces had been deployed to Benin to thwart the coup, and that Rwandan troops had also been involved — a claim Badlands commentary said would track with its understanding of [[rwanda|Rwanda]]'s role in the broader African conflict.[1]
