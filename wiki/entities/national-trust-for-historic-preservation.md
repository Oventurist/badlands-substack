# National Trust for Historic Preservation

The National Trust for Historic Preservation is the organization that filed suit seeking to block the White House East Wing Modernization and State Ballroom Project.[1]

## Litigation over the East Wing project

On February 2, 2026, the [[department-of-justice|U.S. Department of Justice]] asked a federal judge to stay in advance any injunction that might be issued against the project, filing pre-emptively "to avoid an emergency situation or at least to facilitate expedited proceedings on appeal."[1] The government argued that "halting construction would imperil the President and others who live and work in the [[white-house|White House]]," that "the current open construction site is, in and of itself, a hazard and complicates Secret Service operations," and that additional reasons set out in a supplemental classified declaration meant that halting construction "will endanger national security and therefore impair the public interest."[1] The filing concluded that "completion of the Project in a timely fashion is imperative for reasons of national security."[1]

## Montpelier exhibit controversy (2023)

In May 2023, Reps. [[bob-good|Bob Good]] (R-VA) and [[eli-crane|Eli Crane]] (R-AZ) sought answers from the head of the National Trust about whether federal funds were used for what they called "woke" exhibits at President [[james-madison|James Madison]]'s [[montpelier|Montpelier]] and other historical sites.[2] Their letter, addressed to Jay Clemens, the trust's interim president, and obtained by the [[washington-examiner|Washington Examiner]], urged the organization "to focus on the true life and history of James Madison, rather than succumbing to dishonest, 'woke,' and radical anti-American prejudices."[2] The lawmakers underscored that the trust was established to "preserve and protect our nation's history, as well as perpetuate the American story," and questioned exhibits presenting the Constitution as a "pro-slavery document" as well as the use of "restricted funds" for operating expenses.[2]
