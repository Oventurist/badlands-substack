# Adeeb Nasir

Adeeb Nasir is a 58-year-old Utah man arrested in September 2025 on terrorism and weapons-of-mass-destruction charges after federal authorities said he and a co-suspect planted an incendiary device beneath a news van in Salt Lake City.[1] He was taken into custody alongside a co-suspect identified only as "Adil" following a multiagency investigation led by the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] and the Salt Lake City Police Department, according to reports from local stations KUTV and ABC4.[1]

## Charges

Nasir faces eight felony counts, including attempted aggravated arson and violations of Utah's weapons-of-mass-destruction statutes.[1] The alleged targeting of a news outlet with an explosive device was characterized as a serious domestic terrorism threat with potentially far-reaching implications for press freedom and public safety in an increasingly polarized environment.[1]

## Context

The incident occurred days after the [[assassination-of-charlie-kirk|assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk]] at [[utah-valley-university|Utah Valley University]] on September 10, 2025, and was described as a dangerous escalation in threats against news media.[1] Kirk's killing heightened security concerns across Utah and on college campuses nationwide, with experts warning of a continued attack on free speech and a subsequent epidemic of [[swatting]] calls targeting universities — at least eight historically Black colleges and universities received threats in the same week, forcing lockdowns and class cancellations in what authorities described as hoax calls designed to terrorize educational institutions.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands host [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] reacted to coverage of the arrests by remarking that commentators were "figuring out a way to make it about Islam again," dismissing that framing as risible.[1]
