# Francisco Ribera

Francisco Ribera (1537–1591) of Salamanca was a Jesuit theologian whom the article calls the "grand strategist and true father of Christian Zionism."[1]

## The futurist argument

Ribera's primary task was to construct an intellectual counter to the Protestant claim that the end times were then underway and that the Whore of Babylon described in the Book of Revelation was the Catholic Church.[1] His solution was to argue that the events of Revelation were neither present nor past — most Christians of the era read the Whore of Babylon as Nero's Rome — but would take place at some distant moment in the future.[1]

In his 500-page treatise *In Sacrum Beati Ioannis Apostoli, & Evangelistiae Apocalypsin Commentari*, Ribera further explained that the signs of the end times would occur only when the Temple of Solomon, destroyed in 70 CE during the first Roman-Jewish War, was rebuilt — additionally implying the restoration of the Jews to their homeland.[1]

## Legacy

Ribera's writings became known as the Futurist School of Pre-Millennial [[dispensationalism]], from which the article says arose modern [[christian-zionism]], rapture theology, and the End Times cults of both Christian and Jewish varieties.[1] His work fell into obscurity by the early 17th century and was rediscovered by S.R. Maitland in the Vatican archives, feeding into the [[albury-conferences]].[1] Ribera also played a major role in the Council of Trent of 1545, tied by the article to the perpetuation of Catholic–Protestant warfare in Europe.[1]
