# Legal Defense Fund

The Legal Defense Fund is an American civil rights legal organization that joined the first wave of litigation against the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration in January 2025.[1]

## Birthright citizenship lawsuit

Late on Monday, January 20, 2025, the Legal Defense Fund joined the [[american-civil-liberties-union|American Civil Liberties Union]] and other rights groups in filing a lawsuit describing Trump's day-one executive action overhauling [[birthright-citizenship|birthright citizenship]] as unconstitutional — a case widely expected to test the limits of presidential executive power.[1] Initial reporting placed the number of states joining parallel challenges at seventeen; Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the figure had risen to twenty-two, and predicted a long and drawn-out conflict.[1]
