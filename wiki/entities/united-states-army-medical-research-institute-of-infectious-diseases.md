# United States Army Medical Research Institute of Infectious Diseases

The United States Army Medical Research Institute of Infectious Diseases (USAMRIID) is the US Army's principal biodefense research laboratory. In August 2023 it was named by Russian military leadership as the central institution in what Moscow described as the [[pentagon|Pentagon's]] military biological program.[1]

## Russian accusations, August 2023

Lieutenant General [[igor-kirillov|Igor Kirillov]], Chief of Protection from Radiation, Chemical and Biological Weapons of the Russian Armed Forces, told a press briefing that the United States had begun preparing for a new pandemic by searching for virus mutations.[1] He said the leading role in the Pentagon's military biological program would be played by USAMRIID.[1]

Kirillov further alleged that documents uncovered during Russia's special military operation in Ukraine confirmed the institute's involvement in accumulating dangerous pathogens in various regions of the world.[1] He tied the claims to the Biden administration's creation, announced in July 2023, of the Office for Pandemic Preparedness and Response Policy, whose priority areas he said included the creation of vaccines and drugs targeting viruses and their genetically modified variants.[1] The accusations were reported by Sputnik and relayed by The Gateway Pundit, which framed them in the context of a possible new pandemic being used to affect the following year's presidential election.[1]
