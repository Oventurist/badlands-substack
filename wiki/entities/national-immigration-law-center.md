# National Immigration Law Center

The **National Immigration Law Center (NILC)** is an American immigration advocacy nonprofit funded in part by grants from George Soros's [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]].[1]

## Role in the Indivisible network

NILC appears in Badlands Media's mapping of the progressive nonprofit network through the career of [[angel-padilla|Angel Padilla]], policy director of the [[indivisible|Indivisible Project]], who previously worked as an analyst at NILC before moving into anti-Trump organizing.[1] The connection was offered as evidence that groups presenting themselves as spontaneous grassroots formations are staffed from an established, Soros-linked professional advocacy layer.[1]

## Broader framing

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] situates NILC within what he calls "NGO world," the web of tax-exempt organizations he describes as the operational limbs of the deep political establishment, directed by a "think tank class" including the [[atlantic-council|Atlantic Council]], the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]] and the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]].[1]
