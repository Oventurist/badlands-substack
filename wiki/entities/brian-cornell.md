# Brian Cornell

Brian Cornell is the chief executive officer of [[target|Target Corporation]], the American mass-market retailer.[1]

## "Woke" capitalism remarks and the 2023 Pride backlash

In mid-May 2023, Cornell publicly stated that "woke" capitalism was "great" for the Target brand and "the right thing for society."[1] Within roughly a week of those remarks, Target began pulling LGBTQ Pride merchandise from selected stores as consumer boycott calls mounted ahead of June Pride Month.[1]

According to a Target insider quoted by Fox News, the retailer convened an emergency meeting and directed stores in the South and rural America to remove controversial Pride-themed products — including "tuck-friendly" swimsuits and gender-fluid mugs — specifically "to avoid the kind of backlash Bud Light has received in recent weeks."[1] The reversal placed Cornell at the center of a broader debate over whether activist-driven merchandising decisions were damaging shareholder value; Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] argued that leadership had panicked in response to a small group of activist employees while undermining the profitability of a business employing thousands.[1] See also the [[target-pride-collection-boycott]] and the [[2023-bud-light-boycott]].
