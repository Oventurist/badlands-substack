# Carrie Coyner

Carrie Coyner is a Republican member of the Virginia House of Delegates and the recipient of the 2022 text messages that became a central controversy in Virginia's 2025 attorney general race.[1]

## Role in the Jay Jones texting scandal

[[jay-jones|Jay Jones]], then a Democratic delegate, sent Coyner messages about then-House Speaker [[todd-gilbert|Todd Gilbert]] in 2022 reading "Three people, two bullets" and "Gilbert, hitler, and pol pot," with Jones writing that "Gilbert gets two bullets to the head."[1] The texts resurfaced in early October 2025, after early voting had begun, and dominated the closing month of Jones' successful campaign against incumbent Attorney General [[jason-miyares|Jason Miyares]].[1]
