# Scott Wiener

**Scott Wiener** is a Democratic member of the California State Senate representing San Francisco and a member of the state legislature's LGBTQ caucus.[1]

## 2023 call to corporate America

In late May 2023, after [[target-corporation|Target]] removed products and relocated Pride displays in some stores in response to consumer protest, Wiener led a call by LGBTQ activists and legislators for corporations to hold firm against public pressure.[1] He said a strategy was needed "on how to deal with corporations that are experiencing enormous pressure to throw LGBTQ people under the bus," and that the message to corporate America should be: "if you're our ally — if you are truly our ally — you need to be our ally, not just when it's easy but also when it's hard."[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the pledge as an admission of weakness, arguing that a movement capable of winning in the free market would not need to demand corporate loyalty during periods of financial loss, and framing the appeal as a veiled threat aimed at securing an ideological response to what were fundamentally business decisions.[1] The episode is covered further at [[target-pride-collection-boycott|Target Pride Collection Boycott]].[1]

## 2023 Pride Month honor for Sister Roma

In June 2023, Wiener invited the drag performer and adult film director [[sister-roma|Sister Roma]], whose legal name is Michael Williams, to the California Legislature's Pride Month celebration, saying he was "proud of Sister Roma and her work in the community."[2] According to the Sacramento Bee, Democratic lawmakers "clapped, whooped, and cheered, giving Roma a prolonged standing ovation."[2] Badlands contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] criticized the honor, questioning what an adult film director contributes to the community and arguing that California politicians were advancing an agenda that residents were increasingly resisting.[2]
