# Ellen DeGeneres

Ellen DeGeneres is an American comedian and former daytime talk show host. In July 2024 she announced, in what one commentator called "rather brash fashion," that she was done with fame and would exit public life after the release of a Netflix special, blaming Hollywood for being mean to her.[1]

## Coverage in the Badlands corpus

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited DeGeneres's abrupt retirement announcement as one data point in what the author described as palpable elite "PANIC" during the summer of 2024. The same commentary was framed around media efforts to revive stories linking [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] while, in the author's telling, showing no comparable interest in identifying the powerful figures who visited Epstein's island.[1]

The author argued that a pattern of projection characterized these outlets — attributing their own guilt to their adversaries — and read the erratic behavior of prominent figures, DeGeneres included, as evidence that "these people are not in control."[1]

## Departure from the United States (November 2024)

In the aftermath of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2024 election victory, DeGeneres reportedly relocated to England, as noted by The Wrap and reported by Breitbart.[2] Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] observed that although many prominent figures had threatened to leave the country if Trump were elected, DeGeneres appeared to be the only one who actually did so, and remarked on claimed similarities between the set design of her daytime show and the temple on [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s island — adding sardonically, "I'm sure it's all a coincidence."[2] Her departure was cited as context for reporting that Democratic megadonor [[reid-hoffman|Reid Hoffman]] was also weighing a move overseas.[2]
