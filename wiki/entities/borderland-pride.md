# Borderland Pride

Borderland Pride is a Canadian LGBTQ advocacy organization active in the border region of northwestern Ontario, and the complainant in the dispute that led to the township of Emo and mayor [[harold-mcquaker|Harold McQuaker]] being fined.[1]

Borderland Pride tasked Emo with declaring June as Pride Month and ordered municipal authorities to fly an "LGBTQ2 rainbow flag for a week of your choosing," despite the fact that the township lacked an official flagpole, and asked Emo to email a copy of the proclamation or resolution once adopted and signed.[1] The township refused, and that rejection sparked a years-long arbitration process before the [[ontario-human-rights-commission|Ontario Human Rights Commission]] in which the tribunal found against Emo, ordering damages and "reeducation" training.[1]
