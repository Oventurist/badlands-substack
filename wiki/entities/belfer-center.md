# Belfer Center

The Belfer Center is [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s center for science and international affairs, focused on international security. "The Harvard Control Grid" notes that former Defense Secretary [[ash-carter|Ash Carter]] returned to Harvard to serve as the center's director.[1]

The article uses the appointment as evidence of Harvard's institutional continuity with the U.S. defense and intelligence establishment — a pattern it also traces through [[robert-gates|Robert Gates]]'s service on the Board of Overseers and [[james-woolsey|James Woolsey]]'s participation in Harvard-linked symposiums.[1]
