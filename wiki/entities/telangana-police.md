# Telangana Police

The Telangana Police is the state police force of the southern Indian state of Telangana. In January 2025 it drew international attention for pioneering the use of trained eagles to intercept rogue drones.[1]

## Intelligence Security Wing and the Garuda squad

The force's Intelligence Security Wing (ISW) is a specialized unit overseeing the security of Very Very Important Persons.[1] Within the ISW, officials trained five eagles over more than three years to identify and down drones posing a threat to VVIP visits or large public gatherings, creating the [[garuda-squad|Garuda squad]] — India's first and only bird squad and the world's second after the Netherlands.[1] After the training proved successful, the state police's top leadership fielded inquiries from the Indian Army, the Coast Guard and the paramilitary Border Security Force, each of which sought to induct similar bird squads.[1]
