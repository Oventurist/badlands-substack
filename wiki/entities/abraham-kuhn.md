# Abraham Kuhn

**Abraham Kuhn** was a co-founder of the New York investment banking firm [[kuhn-loeb-and-co|Kuhn, Loeb & Company]].[1]

Ryan DeLarme's "The Men Behind the Curtain - Part 6" recounts that [[jacob-schiff|Jacob Schiff]], a grandson of the Schiff family that had shared quarters with the [[rothschild-family|Rothschilds]] in Frankfurt in 1785, emigrated to America in 1865 after meeting Kuhn, who invited him to join the New York firm.[1]

Kuhn also figures in the article's account of dynastic intermarriage: quoting history professor Howard M. Sachar, DeLarme notes that "[[solomon-loeb|Solomon Loeb]] and Abraham Kuhn, it is recalled, married each other's sisters," a practice presented as characteristic of families associated with Cabal activity.[1]
