# Fred Malek

Frederic Vincent Malek (December 22, 1936 – March 24, 2019) was an American business executive, political adviser, fraudster and philanthropist, and an adviser to the [[carlyle-group|Carlyle Group]].[1]

## Career

Malek was president of Marriott Hotels and of Northwest Airlines, and served as an assistant to Presidents [[richard-nixon|Richard Nixon]] — in whose purge of Jewish government employees he took an active role — and [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]].[1] Active in politics for more than fifty years, he served as National Finance Committee Chair of Senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]]'s 2008 presidential campaign and as finance chair of the Republican Governors Association, and played roles in the campaign of every Republican nominee for president over four decades with the exception of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Badlands Media interpretation

Badlands Media casts Malek's finance role as the mechanism by which Bush Sr. retained control over the Republican Party's money — an arrangement it says ended when Trump "stepped into the ring."[1] The account notes he was a Green Beret in Vietnam, speculates he had known George H.W. Bush since that period, and observes that he was caught by the SEC bribing a political figure but was only forced to pay a fine.[1] Malek was one of the three Carlyle figures — with [[david-rubenstein|David Rubenstein]] and [[frank-carlucci|Frank Carlucci]] — who in 1991 encouraged [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] to take control of Citicorp.[1]
