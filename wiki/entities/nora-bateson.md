# Nora Bateson

**Nora Bateson** is the systems theorist quoted in the April 5, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] feature "The Liminal" by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites her statement: "When a system transforms, some connections dissolve to allow new patterns to emerge."[1] The quotation appears in the article's section on relationships during the [[liminality|liminal]] passage, supporting its argument that friendships which fade or break during a period of personal transformation represent alignment rather than failure.[1]

Bateson's systems framing allows the author to counsel readers to "let them… allow them," on the grounds that people who cannot meet a person where they now are need not be carried across the threshold.[1]
