# Steve Feinberg

Steve Feinberg is the United States Deputy Secretary of Defense in the second [[trump-administration|Trump administration]].[1]

## Role in munitions acceleration

Feinberg is described as playing an "unusually" hands-on role in the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] effort known as the [[munitions-acceleration-council|Munitions Acceleration Council]], a drive to expand US missile production in preparation for a potential conflict with [[china|China]].[1] According to the *Wall Street Journal*, the deputy secretary personally telephones some defense-industry executives on a weekly basis to track their progress on output targets.[1] The initiative sits under Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] and was launched in June 2025 with a Pentagon roundtable of top missile makers.[1]
