# Eli Crane

Eli Crane is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Arizona (R-AZ).[1]

## Montpelier oversight letter

In May 2023, Crane joined Rep. [[bob-good|Bob Good]] (R-VA) in demanding answers about "woke" influences at President [[james-madison|James Madison]]'s [[montpelier|Montpelier]] and other historical sites, focusing on how exhibits treat slavery and the nation's founding documents.[1] The lawmakers sought answers from the head of the [[national-trust-for-historic-preservation|National Trust for Historic Preservation]] about whether federal funds were used for the exhibits.[1]

Their letter to Jay Clemens, the trust's interim president, urged the organization "to focus on the true life and history of James Madison, rather than succumbing to dishonest, 'woke,' and radical anti-American prejudices."[1] Crane and Good objected in particular to the characterization of the Constitution as a "pro-slavery document" in what they said was the site's sole constitutional exhibit, and to a children's exhibit that included "imagination exercises" for whites and blacks encouraging children to imagine themselves as the aggressor.[1] They also asked about the absence of exhibits on Madison's achievements and about "restricted funds" being used for operating expenses.[1]
