# Howard Stern

Howard Stern is an American radio broadcaster who appears in Badlands Media commentary as an example of a media figure alleged to have ties to [[sean-combs|Sean "P Diddy" Combs]] and to be working against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s return to office.[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] described Stern as a "radio DJ and all-around creep" who asked Trump supporters to stop listening to his radio shows.[1] Carlson emphasized the timing, writing that Stern took this stance only after the Combs arrest and that he "just happens to be photographed at one of Diddy's 'all white' parties," adding sarcastically that this was surely a coincidence.[1]

## Role in the article's argument

Stern is grouped in the essay with celebrities funding and endorsing [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]], presented as part of an effort by compromised public figures to prevent Trump's election and thereby forestall mass arrests.[1] The claims are presented as opinion and inference; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the views of contributing authors.[1]
