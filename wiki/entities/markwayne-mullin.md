# Markwayne Mullin

Markwayne Mullin is identified in the Badlands corpus as Secretary of Homeland Security in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, having previously served as a Republican politician from Oklahoma.

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that at a Thursday press conference Mullin said the administration had located approximately 146,000 migrant children who had previously lost contact with federal authorities after entering the United States as unaccompanied minors. He stated that nearly 300,000 additional children remained unaccounted for and that investigators were pursuing reports of severe abuse involving some of those minors, including allegations from recovered children who said they had been raped "600 to 700 times."

Mullin's announcement accompanied the news that the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] was fully funded following passage of a major immigration and border-security package. The Brief situates his figures against earlier DHS Inspector General findings that hundreds of thousands of unaccompanied minors released from federal custody were not consistently tracked through immigration proceedings.
