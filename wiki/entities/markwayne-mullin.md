# Markwayne Mullin

**Markwayne Mullin** is the Secretary of Homeland Security in the second [[trump-administration|Trump administration]], heading the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]].[1]

## June 2026 trafficking announcement

At a Thursday press conference in June 2026, Mullin said the administration had located approximately 146,000 migrant children who had lost contact with federal authorities after entering the United States as unaccompanied minors.[1] He stated that nearly 300,000 additional children remained unaccounted for and that investigators were pursuing reports of severe abuse involving some of those minors.[1]

Mullin also said investigators were reviewing allegations from some recovered children who claimed they had been raped "600 to 700 times."[1] His remarks came in the same week the administration announced that DHS was fully funded under a major immigration and border-security package, and were presented alongside DHS and [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] efforts to locate missing children and dismantle [[child-trafficking|trafficking networks]].[1]

## Appointment as Secretary of Homeland Security (March 2026)

Trump announced Mullin's appointment on March 6, 2026, saying the "Highly Respected United States Senator from the Great State of Oklahoma" would become Secretary of Homeland Security effective March 31, 2026, succeeding [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]], who moved to become Special Envoy for [[shield-of-the-americas|The Shield of the Americas]].[2] Trump noted Mullin's ten years in the House of Representatives and three in the Senate, called him "a MAGA Warrior" and former undefeated professional MMA fighter, and highlighted that as the only Native American in the Senate he was an advocate for tribal communities.[2] Trump said Mullin would work to keep the border secure, stop migrant crime, and end the scourge of illegal drugs.[2]

Badlands writer [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] raised the question of whether pulling a Republican senator out of the chamber amounted to a rugpull of the [[save-america-act|Save America Act]], observing the irony if the bill were brought to a floor vote timed to March 31.[2]

## Confrontation with Sean O'Brien (November 2023)

While serving as a Republican senator from Oklahoma, Mullin challenged [[teamsters|Teamsters]] president [[sean-obrien|Sean O'Brien]] to a physical fight during a November 14, 2023 meeting of the Senate [[health-education-labor-and-pensions-committee|Health, Education, Labor and Pensions Committee]].[3] Reading aloud a June 21 tweet in which O'Brien had called him "a clown & a fraud" and told him to "quit the tough guy act in these senate hearings," Mullin responded: "This is a time, this is a place to run your mouth. We can be two consenting adults, we can finish it here."[3]

O'Brien replied "OK that's fine, perfect," and when Mullin — a former mixed martial arts fighter — asked "You wanna do it now?", O'Brien said he would "love to do it right now."[3] Mullin then told him to "stand your butt up," O'Brien echoed the challenge back, and both men rose before chairman [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] intervened to end the exchange.[3] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the irony of Sanders acting as peacemaker and cited the episode, alongside allegations that [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] had elbowed Representative [[tim-burchett|Tim Burchett]] the same day, as evidence that "the Congress is a clown show."[3]
