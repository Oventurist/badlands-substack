# Pedro Sánchez

Pedro Sánchez is the Prime Minister of Spain. He appears in the January 19, 2026 [[badlands-brief-02a|Badlands Brief]] in the bonus item covering the [[adamuz-train-collision|Adamuz high-speed train collision]] of January 18, 2026, in which at least 39 people were killed and more than 120 injured near Córdoba.

The Brief reports that national leaders including Sánchez expressed condolences to the victims' families as Spanish authorities suspended high-speed rail services between Madrid and Andalusian cities pending an investigation into the derailment.
