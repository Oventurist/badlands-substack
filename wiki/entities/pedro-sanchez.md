# Pedro Sánchez

**Pedro Sánchez** is the Prime Minister of Spain.[1] He appears in the Badlands corpus in connection with the [[adamuz-train-collision|Adamuz train collision]] of January 18, 2026, in which two high-speed trains derailed and collided in the province of Córdoba, killing at least 39 people and injuring more than 120.[1] Sánchez was among the national leaders who expressed condolences to the victims' families as authorities suspended high-speed services between Madrid and Andalusian cities pending investigation.[1]

## Arms embargo measures against Israel (September 2025)

In September 2025, Sánchez's government scrapped nearly €1 billion ($1.18 billion) in defense contracts with Israeli firms, the strongest step yet under a package of measures he had announced against [[israel|Israel]] over its offensive in [[gaza|Gaza]].[2] The action followed his pledge the previous week to enshrine in law a ban on arms sales to and purchases from Israel.[2] The cancelled agreements included a €700 million deal for twelve SILAM rocket launchers, based on [[elbit-systems|Elbit]]'s PULS platform, and a €287.5 million contract for 168 Spike L.R. anti-tank missiles, with Madrid reportedly preparing to disengage militarily and technologically from Israel while seeking alternative suppliers.[2] See [[spain-israel-arms-deal-cancellations-of-2025|Spain–Israel Arms Deal Cancellations of 2025]].[2]
