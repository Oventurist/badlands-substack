# Robin Niblett

Sir Robin Niblett is a British foreign-policy analyst and Distinguished Fellow of [[chatham-house|Chatham House]], the Royal Institute of International Affairs.[1]

## Davos 2024 panel

Niblett hosted the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] annual meeting session titled "What to Expect from a Possible Republican Administration" in January 2024. During the panel, [[heritage-foundation|Heritage Foundation]] president [[kevin-roberts|Kevin Roberts]] rejected the framing offered from the moderator's chair, telling Niblett that "it's laughable that you or anyone would describe Davos as protecting liberal democracy" and that it was "equally laughable to use the word 'dictatorship' at Davos and aim that at President Trump."[1]

The exchange was widely circulated as an instance of a sitting Davos panel being challenged on its own premises, and Badlands commentary noted the oddity of the Davos set hosting so scathing a public rebuke of its worldview.[1]
