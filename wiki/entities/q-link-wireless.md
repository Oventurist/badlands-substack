# Q Link Wireless

Q Link Wireless LLC is a telecommunications company based in Dania Beach, Florida, and a vendor in the federal [[lifeline-program|Lifeline program]] — the discounted phone service scheme popularly called the "Obama phone" program.[1] Its chief executive was [[issa-asad|Issa Asad]].[1]

## Federal fraud case (July 2025)

Q Link and Asad were sentenced in July 2025 for conspiring to defraud and commit offenses against the United States in connection with a years-long scheme to steal over $100 million from the Lifeline program.[1] The company was sentenced on Count 1 of an Information charging conspiracy to commit wire fraud, to steal government money, and to defraud the United States.[1]

Q Link agreed to a forfeiture money judgment of over $109 million and, jointly with Asad, to pay $109,637,057 in restitution to the [[federal-communications-commission|Federal Communications Commission]].[1] Together with Asad's separate payments to the [[small-business-administration|Small Business Administration]] and a personal forfeiture judgment, the paid financial penalties and restitution totaled over $128 million.[1]

Badlands contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] reported that, according to the Federal Register, Q Link Wireless earned $618,736,494 from the Lifeline program between 2013 and 2019, and that the company generated fake records in an attempt to cover up the fraudulent billing once the investigation became apparent.[1] At least $50 million was reportedly transferred to bank accounts controlled by Asad's family in Jordan.[1]
