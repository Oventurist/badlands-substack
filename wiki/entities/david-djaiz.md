# David Djaïz

David Djaïz is a French left-wing public intellectual and a former advisor to
President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]], serving as the government's
rapporteur for the National Council for Refoundation (CNR).[1]

## Warning of a social revolt

In an April 2024 interview with the Paris-based conservative news magazine
*Le Point*, Djaïz warned that if France continued down its current path with the
green agenda, the country would likely face a "social revolt" far greater than
the [[yellow-vest-movement|Yellow Vest movement]] or the recent bout of farmer
protests.[1] "It's going to end very badly," he said, accusing the government
and political elites of being "content to manage the decline."[1]

Djaïz, an ardent believer in the need for a green transition of the economy in
France and Europe as a whole, argued that there is a "deficit of strategic
thinking" in the halls of power, saying: "I saw from the inside how
intellectually and operationally the state was exhausted."[1] He predicted that
France "could experience a huge public finance crisis, or a social revolt
compared to which the Yellow Vests and the farmers are appetizers."[1]

## Commentary in the Truth Community

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]
treated Djaïz's remarks as evidence of the momentum of the populist movement,
noting that "a tree-hugging green activist" was now openly advocating that
France build up its industrial base to compete with China — an agenda item
typically associated with right-wing nationalism.[1] He framed the convergence
of rural farmers and city-dwellers against the
[[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]] agenda as an instance of
[[horseshoe-theory|Horseshoe Theory]], and the wider French situation as an
example of [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]]'s "Crisis Cascade" and of
[[accelerationism|Accelerationism]].[1]
