# Brooke Rollins

Brooke Rollins is the United States Secretary of Agriculture in the second administration of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], heading the [[united-states-department-of-agriculture|Department of Agriculture]].

## The beef packer antitrust probe

In the announcement described in the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, Rollins joined Acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] in intensifying an antitrust investigation into [[jbs|JBS]], [[cargill|Cargill]], [[tyson-foods|Tyson Foods]], and [[national-beef|National Beef]]. She framed foreign ownership of packers such as JBS and National Beef as a national security matter, referring to reports of slave labor ties, and said on Monday: "Half of these meatpacking giants, including the largest meat packer in the world, are either foreign-owned or have significant foreign ownership and control, making them a threat not just to our cattle producers, but a threat to America itself."

Her remarks accompanied disclosure that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] had reviewed more than three million documents and conducted hundreds of rancher interviews, and came against the backdrop of United States beef herds at their lowest levels since the 1950s.
