# Second Bank of the United States

The Second Bank of the United States was a central banking institution whose establishment is discussed in the article as a precursor to the Federal Reserve. According to the source, when the Second Bank was established, [[Andrew Jackson]] recognized that bankers were overreaching and acting as "a fourth branch of government run by the elite." The article claims Jackson understood that if bankers succeeded in their ultimate plan, they would "enslave every citizen in the country," prompting him to seek to root them out.

The article presents Jackson's opposition to the Second Bank as the defining struggle of his presidency and identifies it as the historical antecedent to the cabal's eventual success in 1913 with the creation of the Federal Reserve. It notes that Jackson favored "hard-money" over the bankers' "paper-money" and remained the only US President in history to completely pay off the US National Debt. The source bitterly observes that the cabal later achieved what Jackson opposed through the Federal Reserve Act and, as an additional insult, placed Andrew Jackson on the $20 bill.

The Second Bank is framed in the article as the first major battleground in the war between hard money and paper money that the source claims has defined American economic history from the founding through 1913 and beyond.
