# Second Bank of the United States

The **Second Bank of the United States** was the national bank whose establishment prompted [[andrew-jackson|Andrew Jackson]]'s campaign against the American banking elite.[1]

Jackson concluded that the bankers behind the institution were overreaching, acting as a fourth branch of government run by the elite, and that if they succeeded in their ultimate plan they would enslave every citizen in the country. He therefore sought to root them out.[1] Jackson further held that the bankers would extend loans to prominent people over common citizens and use that power to enable more corruption.[1]

Badlands Media presents the Second Bank as an earlier iteration of the ambition finally realized in the [[federal-reserve-system|Federal Reserve]].[1]
