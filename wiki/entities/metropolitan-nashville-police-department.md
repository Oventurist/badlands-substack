# Metropolitan Nashville Police Department

The Metropolitan Nashville Police Department (MNPD) is the municipal police agency for Nashville and Davidson County, Tennessee.[1] It led the response to and investigation of the March 2023 shooting at the Christian Covenant School.[1]

## Covenant School shooting and manifesto controversy

The MNPD investigated the attack in which [[audrey-hale|Audrey Hale]] killed six people inside the Covenant School with a firearm; an officer killed Hale as she continued firing on the second floor of the building.[1] Authorities subsequently confirmed the discovery of a manifesto written by Hale in which the attack had allegedly been planned over several weeks, and the document became the subject of an ongoing review by the FBI's Behavioral Analysis Unit.[1]

In November 2023, after commentator [[steven-crowder|Steven Crowder]] published photographs of three pages said to come from the manifesto, the department issued a statement declaring that "the photographs are not MNPD crime scene images" and announcing that an investigation begun that morning was examining the dissemination of the writings during an online discussion about the school.[1] The department said it was working with the Metropolitan Department of Law, and separately told the Daily Caller that it did not know whether the pages were authentic.[1] The episode placed the agency in the position of both disclaiming the leak's provenance and investigating its source, without resolving the question of the document's authenticity.[1]
