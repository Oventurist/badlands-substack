# Paramount

Paramount is one of the major American film studios and a legacy holder of large character and franchise libraries. In the Badlands corpus it appears as a co-plaintiff in the intellectual-property confrontation with the Chinese technology company [[bytedance|ByteDance]] over its generative video model Seedance 2.0.

## Cease-and-desist action against ByteDance

The February 17, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Paramount joined the [[walt-disney-company|Walt Disney Company]] in sending cease-and-desist letters to ByteDance in February 2026, alleging that Seedance 2.0 reproduces copyrighted material and characters without authorization. The letters were not publicly distributed but were first reviewed and reported by [[axios|Axios]].

The Badlands commentary on the dispute, written by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], treats Paramount and Disney as representative of a studio system attempting to enforce traditional governing rights and preserve monopolistic positions in an industry already being transformed. She argues that the studios' most valuable assets are their franchises, that audiences have grown indifferent to studios and even actors while remaining attached to characters, and that litigation will not arrive in time to prevent the democratization of those franchises. She likens the moment to the music industry's failed campaign against Napster.
