# Paul Joseph Watson

Paul Joseph Watson is a British alternative-media commentator and video producer known for his coverage of European immigration and civil-unrest stories. He appears in the Badlands corpus as a cited voice on the [[2023-french-riots|2023 French riots]].[1]

## Commentary on the 2023 French riots

As unrest sparked by the police killing of a teenager of North African descent entered its fifth day in early July 2023, Watson offered an explanation for the rapid nationwide spread of the violence that diverged sharply from that of French officials, who attributed it to social media. Watson said: "This is what happens when you absorb millions of migrants into a European county at an accelerated rate pace -- with zero plan -- with zero prospects for ever integrating them."[1]

His remarks were reproduced in coverage aggregated by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] and carried in the July 3, 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, where they were presented alongside earlier warnings about France attributed to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]
