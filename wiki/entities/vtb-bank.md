# VTB Bank

VTB is a Kremlin-run Russian lender that became a central actor in the sanctions-era legal conflict between Russian institutions and Western banks.[1]

## Litigation against JPMorgan Chase

In April 2024, VTB launched legal action against [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]], the largest United States bank, seeking to recoup money that had become stuck under Washington's sanctions regime.[1] The dispute centred on approximately $439 million in funds that VTB held in a JPMorgan account in the United States; when Washington imposed sanctions on the Kremlin-run bank, JPMorgan was obliged to move the funds into a separate escrow account, with the result that under the terms of the United States sanctions regime neither VTB nor JPMorgan could access the money.[1]

A week after VTB filed suit, a Russian arbitration court in St Petersburg ordered the seizure of JPMorgan Chase funds totalling $439.5 million, targeting funds in JPMorgan's accounts as well as shares in its Russian subsidiaries; the assets in question had already been frozen by Russian authorities in the wake of the Western sanctions.[1] The Financial Times, which reported the seizure order from the Russian court register, presented the case as evidence of the fallout Western companies face from punitive measures against Moscow and of the difficulty Western lenders have encountered in following through on pledges to close their Russia operations since the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022.[1]

## Russian court seizure of JPMorgan funds (April 2024)

In April 2024 a Russian court ordered the seizure of funds in [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]] bank accounts in Russia in a lawsuit filed by the state-owned VTB as it sought to regain funds blocked abroad, according to court filings reported on Wednesday, April 24.[2] The ruling came from the Arbitration Court of St Petersburg and the Leningrad Region and was dated April 22.[2]

The action followed JPMorgan Chase's suit against VTB filed in New York the previous week, which sought to halt VTB's efforts to recover $439.5 million from an account blocked after Russia sent its army into Ukraine in 2022 and VTB was placed under sanctions.[2] JPMorgan described VTB's attempt to recover the money through the Russian courts as a "blatant breach" of the parties' agreement to resolve disputes in New York.[2] The Russian court ordered seizure of all funds in JPMorgan accounts in Russia, including correspondent accounts and those held in a subsidiary's name, while leaving securities, property held by JPMorgan funds, and the jpmorgan.ru domain untouched.[2] VTB did not immediately comment and had previously declined to discuss its legal disputes with JPMorgan.[2]
