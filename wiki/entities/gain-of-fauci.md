# Gain of Fauci

"Gain of Fauci" is the pseudonym of an independent researcher active on X who is credited in Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* with resurfacing the biological dimension of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s foreign entanglements.[1]

## Contribution

The researcher is described as having reminded the public that Harvard's betrayal "wasn't just financial or administrative, it was biological," directing attention to the case of [[charles-lieber|Charles Lieber]] and to the flow of federal grant money into research linked with Wuhan laboratories.[1] The article's acknowledgments give "special thanks to independent investigators like [[melissa-chen|Melissa Chen]], and Gain of Fauci, whose reporting helped uncover critical layers of the *Ivy Web*."[1]
