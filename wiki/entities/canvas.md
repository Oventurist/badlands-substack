# CANVAS

The Center for Applied Nonviolent Action and Strategies (CANVAS) is a Serbian organization co-founded by [[otpor|Otpor!]] leaders Srdja Popović and Slobodan Djinović.[1]

Badlands Media places CANVAS within the "revolution school" through which lessons from one Western-backed uprising fed into the next, alongside the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]] and the earlier Center for Nonviolent Resistance — the consulting body set up by Otpor activists to instruct youth leaders from around the world in how to organize a movement, motivate voters and develop mass actions.[1] Ukrainian [[pora|Pora]] organizers active in the [[orange-revolution|Orange Revolution]] were trained through this Serbian pipeline.[1]

The broader pattern, as described by [[mark-r-beissinger|Mark R. Beissinger]], is that overthrowing governments has become an international business, with numerous consulting operations arising, many led by former revolutionaries themselves.[1]
