# Adam Johnson (ice hockey)

**Adam Johnson** was an American ice hockey player who died on October 28, 2023, after his neck was cut by a skate blade during a game in England.[1] He was 29 years old.[1]

## Fatal incident

Johnson was playing for the [[nottingham-panthers|Nottingham Panthers]] against the [[sheffield-steelers|Sheffield Steelers]] in an Elite Ice Hockey League game at Sheffield's home arena when he was struck by an opponent's skate blade.[1] Video of the incident showed Johnson skating with the puck toward the Steelers net; [[matt-petgrave|Matt Petgrave]] skated toward him and collided with another Panthers player, and as Petgrave began to fall his left skate kicked up and the blade struck Johnson in the neck.[1]

The death reverberated around the hockey community and led to moments of silence in the NHL.[1]

## Arrest

On Tuesday, November 14, 2023, police in England arrested a man on suspicion of manslaughter in connection with Johnson's death.[1] [[south-yorkshire-police|South Yorkshire Police]] did not name the suspect or provide his age, saying only that he was in police custody.[1] The item was carried in the Badlands News Brief as a bonus story sourced to AP News.[1]
