# Museum of Tolerance

The Museum of Tolerance is a Los Angeles institution that in November 2023 hosted a screening of "Bearing Witness to the October 7th Massacre," a compilation of footage from the [[hamas|Hamas]] attack of October 7, 2023, presented by the Israeli actress [[gal-gadot|Gal Gadot]].[1]

A November 9, 2023 *[[new-york-post|New York Post]]* report described police already deployed in force around the "ironically named" museum ahead of the screening.[1] About 200 attendees, including Israeli officials and Hollywood executives, were inside, while roughly 50 protesters gathered outside.[1] Street fighting broke out between people waving Israeli flags and pro-Palestinian demonstrators; officers formed a skirmish line and used pepper spray, and several people were detained.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] cited the episode as an example of an event whose confrontational outcome he judged to be contrived rather than spontaneous, given the advance notice and the heavy police and [[fbi|FBI]] presence.[1]
