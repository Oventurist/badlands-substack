# Anthony Scaramucci

Anthony Scaramucci is a financier who served briefly as White House Communications Director in July 2017 during the first term of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. In the Badlands Media article "Setting the Stage," Joe Lange argues that Scaramucci's famously short tenure was not a blunder but a designed operation — a "temporary hire" whose single job was to purge embedded leakers from the West Wing.[1]

## The leak crackdown

In his first week on the job, Scaramucci publicly decried leaks coming from within the White House and vowed to fire staffers who continued talking to reporters.[1] The Hill reported at the same moment that Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] was preparing to announce several criminal leak investigations centered on news stories containing sensitive intelligence material — an announcement that came as Trump was publicly criticizing Sessions for recusing himself from the Russia investigation.[1]

Lange treats the timing of Sessions's leak-investigation announcement and Scaramucci's hiring as deliberately coordinated: Sessions opened the criminal cases, and Scaramucci was brought in as the "hatchet man" to fire the leakers.[1] The leakers themselves, in this account, were then folded into the [[racketeer-influenced-and-corrupt-organizations-act|RICO]] investigations running in the background, while Trump's public attacks on Sessions served as media cover.[1]

## The "Scaramucci model"

The frame draws on Q drop 2957 (March 4, 2019), which describes an "acting temporary role" designed to "cause destruction then move on," calls it the "SCARAMUCCI model," and describes the position as a "'TEMP' hire by design" and a "Shadow to Sessions by design," concluding: "Public will become aware > doc dump."[1] Lange reads this as confirmation that Scaramucci operated as a deliberately disposable instrument attached to Sessions's leak prosecutions rather than as a conventional communications official.[1]

NBC News later reported that criminal referrals for leak prosecutions surged under the Trump administration, with the number of leaks reported as potential crimes by federal agencies reaching record highs, according to Justice Department data compiled by the Federation of American Scientists.[1] Sessions himself said the administration had tripled the number of leak probes.[1]

## Significance

For Lange, the episode demonstrates that Sessions's recusal covered only the Russia collusion inquiry and did not bar him from criminally investigating administration officials who leaked classified information to push the collusion narrative — with figures such as [[james-comey|James Comey]] and [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] named as major leakers.[1] Scaramucci was the visible edge of that quiet campaign, meaning Trump was trapping not only Obama holdovers but their allies in the press.[1]
