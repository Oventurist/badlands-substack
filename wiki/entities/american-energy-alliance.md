# American Energy Alliance

The American Energy Alliance is an energy policy advocacy organization quoted in November 2024 on the mechanics of rolling back federal energy regulation.[1]

Dan Kish, the group's senior vice president of policy, told The Epoch Times that undoing the [[joe-biden|Biden]]-era rules would proceed "on a regulation-by-regulation basis," and that there were "actually three categories: executive orders, action from Congress, and those things that have to be done through regulation."[1] Kish added that a process had been set up for changing regulations and that all of those avenues were available to [[donald-trump|Trump]] depending on the final congressional outcome.[1]
