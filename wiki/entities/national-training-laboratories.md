# National Training Laboratories

The **National Training Laboratories (NTL)**, also known as the International Institute for Applied Behavioral Sciences, is described by Badlands Media as "definitely a brainwashing center" operating on Kurt Lewin principles.[1]

Its methods include so-called **T-Groups** (training groups) and artificial stress training, in which participants suddenly find themselves immersed in defending themselves against vicious accusations.[1] The article notes that NTL takes in the [[national-education-association|National Education Association]], the largest teacher group in the United States, thereby extending its behavioral methods into American schooling.[1]

NTL appears in the article as one of the case studies illustrating the reach of [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]] into American institutional life.[1]
