# Paul Vallely

Colonel Paul Vallely was a United States Army officer who co-authored, with [[michael-aquino|Michael Aquino]], the 1980 essay [[from-psyop-to-mindwar|From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory]], commissioned for the US military by Major General [[albert-stubblebine|Albert Stubblebine]].[1]

The essay is described as the directing manifesto of a revolution in military affairs that folded [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] trauma-based mind control studies, the SRI Stargate Project, [[esalen-institute|Esalen Institute]] drug experiments and electromagnetic weapons programs into basic military training for asymmetric warfare against civilian minds at home and abroad.[1]
