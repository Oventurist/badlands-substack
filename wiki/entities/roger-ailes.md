# Roger Ailes

Roger Ailes was the founding executive of [[fox-news|Fox News]] and, in Badlands Media's retrospective framing, the figure whose death marked the network's decline.[1]

## Legacy in Badlands coverage

Writing in September 2023 about a Fox Business polling-graphic scandal, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that although "the network is dead," it is important to remember "that it died with Roger Ailes."[1] The commentary credited Ailes with business and communications brilliance: he identified a market opportunity to cater news to the silent majority, gave conservatives a voice, and grew Fox into "the envy of cable news."[1]

Ailes died in 2017, and the same commentary held that Fox has been in decline ever since, concluding that with stories such as the [[stuart-varney|Varney & Co.]] graphic controversy "it's clear that Fox hasn't yet found its bottom."[1] The critique situates Ailes as the counterexample to what the writer describes as the network's present role in manufacturing consent.[1]
