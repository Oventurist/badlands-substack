# Ashley Moody

Ashley Moody is a Florida politician who served as the state's Attorney General and who, in January 2025, was widely reported to be the leading candidate to fill the US Senate seat vacated by [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]].[1]

## Senate appointment speculation

Time was running short for Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] to make his first major decision of 2025 — picking Rubio's replacement in the Senate — and when asked on Tuesday whether he had made up his mind, the governor said he had not yet decided.[1] All signs nevertheless pointed to him leaning toward Moody, with Rubio bound for the State Department under President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

Moody had been a staunch DeSantis supporter while also being vocally supportive of Trump, particularly on his positions on legal matters.[1] She recently filed state charges against Ryan Routh, the man charged with attempting to assassinate Trump at his Florida golf course.[1] DeSantis declined to reveal his thinking during a gaggle with reporters and sidestepped a question about Moody, saying only, "We want someone who is really able to deliver."[1]
