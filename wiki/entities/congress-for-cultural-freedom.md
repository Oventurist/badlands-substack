# Congress for Cultural Freedom

The Congress for Cultural Freedom was a CIA–MI6 funded operation launched in 1949 to carry the cultural project of the [[frankfurt-school|Frankfurt School]] into the West in full force.[1]

Its leading organizers included Lord [[bertrand-russell|Bertrand Russell]] and three former Trotskyists: Sidney Hook, Irving Kristol and [[james-burnham|James Burnham]].[1] The Congress belongs to the same current that justified the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]]'s funding of abstract art, post-modernist literature and atonal music throughout the Cold War.[1] Matthew Ehret states that the group's role in steering mass education and culture over the ensuing century will be the subject of a future report.[1]
