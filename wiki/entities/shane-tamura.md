# Shane Tamura

Shane Tamura was a 27-year-old Las Vegas casino worker identified by investigators as the gunman who killed four people in a Manhattan office building on Monday, July 28, 2025, before killing himself.[1]

## The shooting

Officials said Tamura was attempting to reach the headquarters of the [[national-football-league|National Football League]] inside the building at 345 Park Avenue, but took the wrong elevator bank after shooting several people in the lobby, according to New York City Mayor [[eric-adams|Eric Adams]].[1] Among the four people killed was off-duty New York City police officer Didarul Islam.[1]

## Background and motive

Tamura played high school football in California roughly a decade before the attack but never played in the NFL, and police said he had a history of mental illness.[1] A three-page note found in his wallet indicated a grievance against the NFL over a claim that he suffered from chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE), a degenerative brain disease linked to concussions and repeated head trauma that can only be diagnosed posthumously.[1] In the note he repeatedly apologized and asked that his brain be studied for CTE.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who described having worked in New York City about four blocks from the shooting site, expressed skepticism about the official account, detailing the multiple layers of physical security typical of buildings housing high-profile financial and professional services tenants — badge checks at the door, guards at the escalator, a staffed reception desk, guarded turnstiles, and floor-restricted elevator access.[1] She argued that for a gunman to pass the lobby and reach an elevator, multiple layers of security designed to protect wealthy and powerful occupants would all have had to fail, and called for release of the lobby security footage.[1]

## Media narrative

Early coverage of the [[345-park-avenue-shooting|345 Park Avenue shooting]] included multiple television anchors reporting that the shooter was "possibly white," a description contradicted by later-published images of Tamura.[2] Badlands commentary characterized this as "a fabricated narrative distraction" that diverted attention from the more substantive questions of Tamura's motive, planning timeline and target list, with the ensuing media fight over CNN's characterization serving as the "intended effect."[2] The building at 345 Park Avenue also houses the headquarters of [[blackstone|Blackstone]] and [[kpmg|KPMG]], and Tamura was found dead on its 33rd floor, which contains offices of the building owner, Rudin.[2]
