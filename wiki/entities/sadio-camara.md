# Sadio Camara

Sadio Camara is the Defense Minister of [[mali|Mali]], serving under the country's military government led by interim leader [[assimi-goita|Assimi Goita]].[1]

## Talks with the Russian defense delegation

In early June 2024, Camara and Malian air force chief [[alou-boi-diarra|Alou Boi Diarra]] held talks in Bamako with a high-ranking delegation from the [[russian-defense-ministry|Russian Defense Ministry]] led by Deputy Minister [[yunus-bek-yevkurov|Yunus-Bek Yevkurov]], according to a press release published by the Sahel state's armed forces.[1] The meetings preceded the delegation's reception by Goita at the presidential palace, where the parties discussed Mali-Russia defense and security cooperation and the situation in the Sahel region, which has been plagued by jihadist violence for decades.[1]

Mali, a former French colony, has severed military ties with France, accusing Paris of meddling.[1]
