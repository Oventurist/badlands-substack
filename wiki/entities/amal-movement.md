# Amal Movement

The Amal Movement is a Lebanese Shia political movement, described as an ally of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] and of Iran within [[lebanon|Lebanon]]'s confessional political system.[1]

## Role in the 2025 presidential election

During the [[2025-lebanese-presidential-election|2025 Lebanese presidential election]], lawmakers from Hezbollah and its Shia ally the Amal Movement backed army commander [[joseph-aoun|Joseph Aoun]] in the second round of voting, carrying him past the required threshold with 99 votes after he had fallen short of the 86 votes needed in the first round.[1] The result was announced by parliament Speaker [[nabih-berri|Nabih Berri]], who leads the movement.[1] Badlands commentary highlighted the unusual convergence of Hezbollah, Iran, the United States and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] behind a single candidate as evidence of a longer-running arrangement.[1]
