# Rothschild & Co

Rothschild & Co is the flagship investment bank of the [[rothschild-family|Rothschild family]]. Its predecessor institutions helped finance the Duke of Wellington's victory over Napoleon at Waterloo in 1815, and the firm in one form or another has been publicly listed since 1838.[1]

## 2023 take-private offer

In February 2023 the bank announced that its main shareholder — the family — planned a tender offer to take the firm private, valuing it at roughly €3.7 billion, or about $4 billion. The move ran counter to the direction of many peer institutions, which were seeking public capital at the time, and would end nearly two centuries of public ownership.[1]

As Bloomberg noted, the buyout would mark the latest step in the family's efforts to cement control, following a 2012 reorganization that brought the French and British businesses under one roof and simplified the group's structure.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] described the transaction as "the fathers of the modern financial system" withdrawing one of their flagship banks from public markets, and connected it to the same institution's Napoleon-era dealings. He argued that the family had quietly built the "super-organism" of the global financial system, that its methods underpin the asset-management structures used by firms such as [[blackrock|BlackRock]] and [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]], and that it was unlikely to be a passive spectator while [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] globalists advanced the [[great-reset|Great Reset]]. He conceded there was little to go on beyond the firm's own warning that "2023 was likely to be a more challenging year given the macroeconomic and geopolitical environment."[1]
