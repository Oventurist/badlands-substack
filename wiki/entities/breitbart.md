# Breitbart

Breitbart is an American conservative news and commentary outlet.[1]

## Coverage of the 2023 Trump CNN town hall

Breitbart columnist [[john-nolte|John Nolte]] argued that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s May 10, 2023 town hall on [[cnn|CNN]] might have delivered the "final killshot" to the network, following earlier Breitbart reporting that Trump had "hijacked" the broadcast and "steamrolled" moderator [[kaitlan-collins|Kaitlan Collins]] in New Hampshire, and that CNN had averaged a humiliating 587,000 primetime viewers in April.[1] Breitbart clips coverage also highlighted [[jake-tapper|Jake Tapper]]'s post-town-hall commentary.[1]

Breitbart's town hall coverage was excerpted and analyzed in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated Nolte's argument as a serious read on a potential turn in the public narrative surrounding Trump.[1]

## Trump farmland interview (September 2024)

In a nearly hour-long exclusive phone interview with Breitbart News on Sunday, September 22, 2024, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he intended to use a Pennsylvania appearance the following day to "light up" Chinese Communist Party efforts to buy American farmland.[2] "We have to have control of our land," Trump told the outlet, adding that the country was being "sold out from under us" and asking whether China would permit Americans to buy Chinese land in return.[2] Breitbart's report noted that CCP-affiliated entities had been buying farmland from coast to coast, including near military bases, that the issue surged into prominence with the Chinese spy balloon first spotted over Montana, and that senators including [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] and [[tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]] had warned about the practice for years.[2]

## Manhattan crime hearing coverage (April 2023)

In April 2023, Breitbart reported that New York Democrats raged over Republicans on the [[house-judiciary-committee|House Judiciary Committee]] scheduling the [[2023-house-judiciary-manhattan-crime-hearing|2023 House Judiciary Manhattan crime hearing]], under a headline noting the Democratic claim that New York City is the "safest big city in America."[3] The report detailed the hearing notice's targeting of District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]] and quoted a Bragg spokesperson calling the event a "political stunt."[3]
