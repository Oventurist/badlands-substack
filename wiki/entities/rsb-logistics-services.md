# RSB Logistics Services

RSB Logistics Services Inc. is a Paducah, Kentucky-based trucking firm that the article identifies as the vehicle through which Russian-owned American uranium was legally exported without a direct export license being issued to [[rosatom|Rosatom]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Role in the Uranium One exports

Citing memos obtained by The Hill, the article reports that rather than grant Rosatom a direct export license — a step that would have raised red flags in a Congress already suspicious of the transaction — the [[nuclear-regulatory-commission|Nuclear Regulatory Commission]] in 2012 authorized an amendment to an export license RSB Logistics already held.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The amendment simply added [[uranium-one|Uranium One]] to the list of clients whose uranium the firm could move to Canada.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

The license, dated March 16, 2012, also increased the quantity of uranium ore concentrate RSB Logistics could ship to the Cameco Corp. plant in Ontario, raising the ceiling from 7,500,000 kilograms to 12,000,000 kilograms, and listed Uranium One among the "other parties to Export."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The article states that the move escaped notice in Congress.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)

## Significance

NRC officials told The Hill that Uranium One exports flowed from Wyoming to Canada and on to Europe between 2012 and 2014, and that the approval involved a process spanning multiple agencies.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3) The author uses the RSB Logistics amendment as the mechanical answer to how the Obama administration and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s [[state-department|State Department]] could publicly assure Americans that no uranium would leave the country while uranium was in fact being exported.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-3)
