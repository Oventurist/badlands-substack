# Fran Drescher

Fran Drescher is an American actress and the president of [[sag-aftra|SAG-AFTRA]], the union representing film and television performers.[1]

## 2023 strike announcement

Drescher announced the union's July 2023 strike in what the [[associated-press|Associated Press]] described as a fiery press conference.[1] "You cannot change the business model as much as it has changed and not expect the contract to change, too," she said, adding that the union was "not going to keep doing incremental changes on a contract that no longer honors what is happening right now with this business model that was foisted upon us."[1] She characterized the alternative as "moving around furniture on the Titanic."[1]

Her remarks framed the [[2023-hollywood-labor-strikes|2023 Hollywood labor strikes]] as a structural fight over streaming economics rather than a routine contract negotiation.[1]
