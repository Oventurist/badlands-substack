# Eric Halperin

Eric Halperin was the Director of Enforcement at the [[consumer-financial-protection-bureau|Consumer Financial Protection Bureau]], having been appointed to the position more than three years before his 2025 resignation.[1]

## Resignation from the CFPB

Halperin resigned on the morning of 11 February 2025, the same day as supervision director [[lorelei-salas|Lorelei Salas]]. Both officials cited the stop-work orders issued by acting Director [[russ-vought|Russell Vought]] as the reason for their departure, and both announced the news to their teams by email.[1] Prior to resigning, Halperin and Salas had been placed on leave by Mark Paoletta, general counsel at the White House [[office-of-management-and-budget|Office of Management and Budget]]; the rationale for that action was not publicly determined.[1]

Salas's resignation message argued that halting all supervisory activities and examinations was neither appropriate nor lawful, and Halperin's email conveyed a similar view.[1] The two resignations came as the bureau operated at a virtual standstill under Vought's order, while the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]] examined its internal finance and procurement records.[1]
