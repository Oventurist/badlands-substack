# Lawrence O'Donnell

Lawrence O'Donnell is an American television host associated with [[msnbc|MSNBC]], where his commentary on the 2024 presidential campaign drew attention in Badlands Media coverage.[1]

## Debate accommodation proposal (July 2024)

In the days following [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s widely criticized debate performance against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], O'Donnell publicly called for the rules of the next debate to be changed so that Biden could be accompanied on stage by his aides. He proposed that campaigns be allowed to "have as many staff as they want, join them on the stage throughout the debate," with microphones for all of them.[1]

O'Donnell argued that candidates "should be allowed to turn to their staff and confer with them about anything at any time in the debate," that audiences "should be able to hear everything they say" in order to judge whether a candidate has "competent or incompetent staff," and that viewers could then "watch the candidates actually think and process information," including information supplied to them by an aide mid-answer. He went further, saying "a candidate should be allowed to let staff members actually answer questions for them."[1]

## Reception in the Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the proposal as inadvertently confirming a long-standing claim within the "truth community" — that an unelected group of advisers, rather than the president himself, effectively governs. She characterized the suggestion as "hilarious and, in my humble opinion, dangerous," arguing that the regime was now openly admitting the existence of a shadow apparatus, presenting it as a virtue, and proposing that it debate in the candidate's place.[1] She also read the fact that MSNBC was discussing September debate accommodations at all as evidence that Biden would remain on the ticket despite the [[pressure-campaign-for-bidens-withdrawal|pressure campaign for his withdrawal]].[1]
