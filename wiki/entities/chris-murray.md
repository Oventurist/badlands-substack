# Chris Murray

Chris Murray is the [[colorado-republican-party|Colorado Republican Party]] attorney who filed the party's December 2023 petition to the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|U.S. Supreme Court]] in the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado ballot disqualification case]].[1]

## Role in the Colorado GOP petition

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands News Brief]] of January 8, 2024, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] said the Colorado GOP petition was "so well written, I can't believe Chris Murray wrote it," and expressed surprise that he was fighting the case at all.[1] She judged that his argument seeking Supreme Court review was "crushing" Scott Gessler's two prior failures in the litigation.[1] The petition warned that "the Colorado court has unleashed harms which will creep beyond Colorado's borders."[1]

## Firm affiliation and conflicts

Murray works for the law firm [[brownstein-hyatt-farber-schreck|Brownstein, Hyatt, Farber and Schreck]] (BHFS), which is the registered lobbyist for [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]].[1] Ashe in America cited this overlap as evidence that "things are weirdly incestuous in our elections industry," arguing it would be preferable to strip special interests and profit motivations from elections and return to locally controlled precinct-level voting.[1]
