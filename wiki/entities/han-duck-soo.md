# Han Duck-soo

Han Duck-soo served as acting President of [[south-korea|South Korea]] during the political transition following the country's 2024–2025 leadership crisis.[1]

## Trade diplomacy with the Trump administration

Under Han's acting presidency, South Korea engaged actively with senior Trump administration officials with the goal of lowering the 25% reciprocal tariff that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had announced for the country and subsequently paused along with other high tariffs imposed on a string of nations.[1] As part of that effort, Finance Minister [[choi-sang-mok|Choi Sang-mok]] accepted an invitation from U.S. Treasury Secretary [[scott-bessent|Scott Bessent]] to hold trade talks in Washington in late April 2025.[1]
