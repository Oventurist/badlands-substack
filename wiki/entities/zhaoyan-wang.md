# Zhaoyan Wang

**Zhaoyan Wang** was the business partner of [[jia-bei-zhu|Jia Bei Zhu]] in the [[universal-meditech|Universal Meditech]] / Prestige Biotech operation in Fresno and Reedley, California.

According to the Badlands Brief of May 27, 2026, Wang was indicted for his role in the scheme to fraudulently sell more than a million faulty COVID-19 tests imported from China, but fled to China and remains a fugitive from the United States. Trial evidence cited in the article showed that Zhu and Wang deliberately hired inexperienced employees — cellphone salespeople, supermarket workers and childcare workers — who "would not ask any questions."

Wang's flight is invoked in the source's commentary as an aggravating circumstance that should have weighed against the pretrial release of [[ori-solomon|Ori Solomon]] in the related [[las-vegas-biolab|Las Vegas biolab]] case.
