# The Free Press

The Free Press is an American digital news outlet whose reporting in April 2025 disclosed the administration's plans for the [[2025-state-department-reorganization|reorganization of the State Department]].[1]

## 2025 State Department coverage

Free Press journalists Gabe Kaminsky and Madeleine Rowley obtained the administration's actual reorganization plans after weeks of rumors about a [[state-department|State Department]] overhaul; officials described the plan as the department's biggest shake-up "in decades," involving the closure of more than 130 offices, including those handling human rights, counter-extremism and the prevention of war crimes.[1] The outlet subsequently published an interview with Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], who was overseeing the effort and who addressed the reorganization, soft power, and Iran policy.[1]

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] characterized the outlet dismissively as "the commies at The Free Press," while allowing that Rubio's argument was pitched in terms its audience could follow.[1]
