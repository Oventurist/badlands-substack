# United States Department of the Treasury

The United States Department of the Treasury is the federal department responsible for the government's finances, the issuance of currency and debt, and — through the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) — the administration of economic sanctions and the designation of individuals and entities involved in terrorist financing.

## Designation of the Libyan Islamic Fighting Group

In the Badlands Media article [[a-conflict-of-interest|A Conflict of Interest]], the Treasury Department is cited as a primary documentary source. The article quotes a department press release dated October 30, 2008, announcing the designation of three members of the [[libyan-islamic-fighting-group|Libyan Islamic Fighting Group]] (LIFG) under Executive Order 13224, the order targeting terrorists and their financial, technological and material support networks. The release quoted Adam Szubin, then director of the Office of Foreign Assets Control, as saying that "LIFG, along with other al Qaida affiliates, seeks to exploit our globalized world, raising funds in Europe for transfer to terror cells operating in North Africa."

The same Treasury material, as reproduced in the article, records that the LIFG announced its formation in 1995 among Libyans who had fought Soviet forces in Afghanistan and the Gaddafi regime at home; that the group is believed to have participated in planning the May 2003 Casablanca suicide bombings and has been linked to the 2004 Madrid attacks; that many members fled Libya from the late 1990s, particularly to the United Kingdom; and that in early November 2007 [[ayman-al-zawahiri|Ayman al-Zawahiri]] announced the LIFG had become an official affiliate of [[al-qaeda|al Qaida]]. The LIFG had been named a Specially Designated Global Terrorist in the annex to E.O. 13224 on September 23, 2001 and added to the UN 1267 Committee Consolidated List on October 6, 2001.

The article uses these findings to argue that the U.S. government's own financial-enforcement arm had formally identified as Al-Qaeda the organisation whose leaders [[john-mccain|John McCain]] later met in [[benghazi|Benghazi]] and urged Washington to arm and fund.

## Related institutions

The Treasury is distinct from the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], which the article separately describes as the origin point of the $40 billion in cash flown to Iraq between 2003 and 2008.
