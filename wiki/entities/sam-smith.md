# Sam Smith

Sam Smith is a British pop singer, described in Badlands Media coverage as a "non-binary pop star," whose performance at the 2023 Grammy Awards became a flashpoint in conservative and Truth Community commentary about occult symbolism in mainstream entertainment.[1]

## 2023 Grammy Awards performance

At the Grammy Awards ceremony held on Sunday, February 5, 2023, Smith staged what critics characterized as a satanic performance of the song "Unholy," a track for which Smith and the trans-identified singer [[kim-petras|Kim Petras]] won the award for best pop duo/group performance.[1] Smith and Petras performed in red, as did their backing dancers; Petras danced inside a cage while Smith wore devil horns and dancers in long red robes with long straight hair moved around him in what was described as a ritual circle.[1] The account of the performance circulated in the Truth Community was drawn from reporting by [[the-post-millennial|The Post Millennial]].[1]

## Reaction

Badlands Media contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] framed the performance as evidence of cultural decline, writing "Welcome to Babylon A.D." and arguing that the entertainment industry had moved from normalizing grooming and hyper-sexualization to openly venerating Baphomet in what he called a satanic ritual performance at the [[grammy-awards|Grammy Awards]].[1] He argued that freedom of speech and expression was distinct from what he saw as a road to destruction in which "up is down, black is white," and characterized pop culture as "black magic mind control and predictive programming."[1]

The controversy was amplified by a promotional tweet from [[cbs|CBS]], the ceremony's broadcaster, which was subsequently deleted amid backlash.[1] Actress [[gillian-anderson|Gillian Anderson]] was quoted in the surrounding commentary as saying, "You don't have to say the entertainment industry is satanic, they're doing it for you."[1]
