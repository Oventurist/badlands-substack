# Prince Alwaleed bin Talal

Prince Alwaleed bin Talal is a Saudi Arabian businessman and member of the Saudi royal family. In the Badlands Media article "A Conflict of Interest," Alwaleed is portrayed as a key financial and political node in what the source describes as a global cabal that controls U.S. taxpayer money through its influence over politicians and intelligence agencies.

According to the article, Alwaleed was born in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, and is of Lebanese descent through his mother, Mona El Solh, who was the daughter of Riad El Solh, the first Prime Minister of Lebanon. The source emphasizes that "a lot of roads lead to Jeddah," and that Alwaleed's background gave him "deep and wealthy connections in both Saudi Arabia and Lebanon" from the very beginning of his life.

The article claims that Alwaleed had "strong ties to the Bush family and the CIA," and that he was "one of [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]]'s biggest puppets." It specifically links Alwaleed to [[prince-alwaleed|Basel]], the naturalized American citizen of Lebanese descent who handled the $40 billion in Iraqi cash shipments, noting that both were born in Saudi Arabia and of Lebanese descent. The source speculates that Basel was affiliated with Alwaleed, and that the absence of documentation after the cash reached Baghdad was designed to allow Alwaleed and his network to steal the money.

According to the article, Alwaleed's value to the cabal was his ability to provide financial support to politicians such as [[john-mccain|John McCain]], whom the source claims was "pumping money into the McCain Institute." The piece asserts that Alwaleed's influence over Bush and McCain allowed him to shape U.S. foreign policy in ways that benefited his financial interests and the broader cabal's goal of controlling global money flows.

The article notes that Alwaleed's father, Talal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud, was a member of the Saudi Royal family, and that Alwaleed's maternal grandfather was the first Prime Minister of Lebanon, giving him unique connections across the Middle East that the source claims were exploited for corrupt purposes.

## "America for Sale" and the sixteen year plan

[[america-for-sale-part-1|America for Sale - Part 1]] (December 2024) makes Alwaleed the ultimate principal behind the network it describes. The article notes that Q drop #111 was posted on November 5, 2017 — the day Alwaleed was arrested in Saudi Arabia and his funds frozen — and reads the drop's questions about who funded a former president "pre-political days" as pointing to Alwaleed's alleged payment for [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s Harvard education, connected in turn to the [[harvard-university-muslim-alumni|Harvard University Muslim Alumni]] association.

Answering Q drop #4750's question "Who is Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal?", the author writes that he "was the puppet master who controlled the democrat party under both Obama and Hillary," exercising "primary control" over the U.S. government: he "installed Obama as president, and tried to install Hillary next." This is characterized as a "sixteen year plan to destroy America and the constitution, while forcing [[sharia-law|Sharia law]] on the people," with control of the White House delivering control of the U.S. military and of trillions of dollars spent overseas without oversight. The article further notes that Alwaleed was born in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia — the city to which [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]'s family moved and the base of the Bin Laden family — and identifies both Abedin and [[valerie-jarrett|Valerie Jarrett]] as connected to him.

## Control of Western politicians (America for Sale - Part 2)

[[america-for-sale-part-2|America for Sale — Part 2]] returns to Alwaleed as the figure standing behind the Clinton network. It notes that Q drop 71 — "FOLLOW HUMA" — was posted on November 4, 2017, the day before Alwaleed's arrest in Saudi Arabia, and reads [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] as his chosen liaison to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]. Because her family lived in Jeddah, Alwaleed's home city, and because she was born an American citizen in Michigan, the article calls her "the perfect choice" for the role, one that gave Alwaleed access to the [[state-department|State Department]], the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] and [[teneo|Teneo]].

Citing Q drop 299 of December 7, 2017 — "SA Controls (assigned) US / UK POLITICIANS / Tech Co's (primary)" — [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that Alwaleed had controlled leading American and British politicians for decades, describing [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]], [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and [[tony-blair|Tony Blair]] — the three speakers at a 2012 Teneo client reception — as "puppets." The article also names Alwaleed as one of the Clinton Foundation's biggest donors during Clinton's tenure as Secretary of State, and restates the claim that he installed [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] for eight years as the first half of a "sixteen year plan" whose second half was to be a Clinton presidency. His arrest on November 5, 2017 is presented as the trigger for subsequent events, including [[john-huber|John Huber]]'s appointment. These claims are contested.
