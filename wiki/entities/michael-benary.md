# Michael Ben'Ary

**Michael Ben'Ary** is a former senior federal prosecutor who led the national security section of the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Virginia and who was reportedly fired in October 2025 amid upheaval in the office that had recently brought criminal charges against former FBI Director [[james-comey|James Comey]].[1]

## Removal

Ben'Ary was reported to have been fired on a Wednesday, hours after conservative journalist [[julie-kelly|Julie Kelly]] publicly flagged his prior role as senior counsel to former Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]] during the Biden administration, according to multiple reports citing people familiar with the matter.[1] His departure was one of two by senior prosecutors from the office in the same period.[1]

Kelly told the *Washington Examiner* that her post directly contributed to Ben'Ary's removal, arguing that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "ran on de-weaponizing and cleaning up the systemically corrupt DOJ, but you can't do that if you have in powerful positions people who were top advisers to the very architects of the lawfare against the president."[1]

## Disputed role in the Comey case

While Kelly suggested that Ben'Ary may have been part of an "internal resistance" to the prosecution of Comey, one person familiar with the matter told the *Associated Press* that he had no involvement in that case.[1]
