# Amitai Etzioni

Amitai Etzioni was an Israeli-American sociologist cited in Badlands Media's history of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] for his reaction to [[herman-kahn|Herman Kahn]]'s *On Thermonuclear War*.[1] Commenting on the book's frank treatment of nuclear war planning, Etzioni said that "Kahn does for nuclear arms what free-love advocates did for sex: he speaks candidly of acts about which others whisper behind closed doors."[1]
