# International Boxing Association

The International Boxing Association (IBA) is the international governing body for amateur boxing.[1] It became central to a 2024 Olympic controversy after it emerged that Algerian boxer [[imane-khelif|Imane Khelif]] had been banned from the 2023 world championships by the IBA for failing tests intended to establish gender qualification.[1]

Despite that disqualification, the [[international-olympic-committee|International Olympic Committee]] permitted Khelif to compete at the 2024 Paris Games over the objections of female athletes, setting up the 46-second bout in which Italy's [[angela-carini|Angela Carini]] abandoned the match shouting "This is unjust!"[1] The divergence between the IBA's ruling and the IOC's admission decision framed much of the ensuing public dispute over eligibility standards in women's boxing.[1]
