# Nikolai Yevmenov

Admiral Nikolai Yevmenov, 61, served as commander-in-chief of the Russian navy from May 2019 until his removal in March 2024.[1]

## Dismissal

Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] ousted Yevmenov after a series of humiliating Ukrainian attacks decimated Moscow's Black Sea Fleet, according to multiple Russian outlets including the St. Petersburg news service Fontanka and the pro-Putin newspaper Izvestia.[1] He was replaced by Northern Fleet Commander [[alexander-moiseyev|Alexander Moiseyev]].[1] Fontanka reported that Yevmenov was not expected to return to his office at the Admiralty, the navy's headquarters in St. Petersburg.[1]
