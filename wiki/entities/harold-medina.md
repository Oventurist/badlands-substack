# Harold Medina

Harold Medina is the chief of police of Albuquerque, New Mexico.[1]

## Refusal to enforce the 2023 concealed carry suspension

In September 2023, after Governor [[michelle-lujan-grisham|Michelle Lujan Grisham]] issued an emergency order suspending the right to carry firearms in public across Albuquerque and surrounding Bernalillo County for at least thirty days, Medina publicly stated that he would not enforce the order.[1] The governor had said state police would be responsible for enforcing what amounted to civil violations, a framing that left municipal enforcement discretionary.[1] Medina's refusal, alongside similar reservations from Bernalillo County Sheriff John Allen, undercut the practical reach of the order and fed into calls by state lawmakers for the governor's impeachment over [[second-amendment|Second Amendment]] overreach.[1]
