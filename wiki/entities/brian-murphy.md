# Brian Murphy

Brian Murphy is a United States District Judge sitting in Boston who in March 2026 halted parts of Health Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]]'s effort to revise federal vaccine guidance.[1]

## Ruling on federal vaccine policy

In a ruling issued on a Monday in March 2026, Murphy sided with the [[american-academy-of-pediatrics|American Academy of Pediatrics]] and other medical organizations that had challenged the administration's vaccine policy changes, agreeing that federal health officials had advanced a broad restructuring of national immunization policy outside of established legal procedures.[1]

The order blocked a plan to reduce recommended childhood immunizations and stayed Kennedy's replacement of all seventeen members of the [[advisory-committee-on-immunization-practices|Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices]], including the appointments of the new experts and any votes on further policy changes; the panel was nonetheless permitted to proceed with a meeting already scheduled for that week.[1] The underlying dispute centered on steps taken by the [[centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention|Centers for Disease Control and Prevention]] on January 5 that trimmed the routine childhood vaccine schedule to eleven entries.[1]
