# Matt Quinn

Matt Quinn is the Deputy Director of the [[united-states-secret-service|United States Secret Service]], the agency responsible for the protection of current and former presidents and major presidential candidates.[1] He became a public face of the agency's internal reckoning over the July 13, 2024 attempt on the life of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] at a campaign rally in Butler, Pennsylvania.[1]

## Response to the Butler assassination attempt

In July 2025, nearly a year after the shooting, Quinn publicly acknowledged that the attempted assassination had been an "operational failure" for which the Secret Service was "totally accountable."[1] The admission accompanied the agency's disclosure that six staffers had been suspended without pay or benefits over their roles in the security lapses that allowed a gunman to fire on the former president from a nearby rooftop.[1] Quinn also stated that the agency was working to address the root causes of the failure rather than treating the suspensions as the end of the matter.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated Quinn's admission as a welcome but minimal first step, writing that it was "good to know they're finally getting around to doing something about, ostensibly, the systemic failures that occurred that day" and urging the agency to keep going rather than stopping at personnel discipline.[1] The commentary framed the anniversary of the Butler shooting as a date that Trump supporters "won't forget," closing with the "Fight, fight, fight" phrase Trump mouthed as agents removed him from the stage.[1]
