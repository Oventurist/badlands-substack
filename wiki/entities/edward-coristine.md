# Edward Coristine

**Edward Coristine**, known by the online handle "Big Balls," is a former staffer of the Department of Government Efficiency ([[doge|DOGE]]) who was 19 years old at the time he was attacked in Washington, D.C.[1]

## August 2025 attack

Coristine was assaulted in the early hours of August 3, 2025, by members of a group allegedly causing chaos along U Street, about one mile from the White House.[1] Two 15-year-olds were charged in connection with the incident.[1]

## Sentencing and reaction

Washington, D.C. Superior Court judge [[kendra-biggs|Kendra Biggs]], a Joe Biden appointee, sentenced the two teenagers to probation with no jail time, stating that her role is to "rehabilitate," not punish.[1] The male defendant pleaded guilty to attempted robbery and simple assault, while the female defendant admitted to one count of simple assault for attacking another person at a gas station with pepper spray; prosecutors dropped other charges related to the attack on Coristine as part of a plea agreement.[1]

President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] blasted the lenient decision, saying, "That's terrible. The judge should be ashamed."[1] U.S. Attorney for D.C. [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]] called the sentencing "shocking."[1]

## Move to the Social Security Administration

Coristine, the 19-year-old nicknamed "Big Balls" who worked at the [[doge|Department of Government Efficiency]], joined the [[social-security-administration|Social Security Administration]] in June 2025 after departing the White House.[2] He had originally been hired by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] for the DOGE team.[2] An SSA spokesperson, Stephen McGraw, confirmed to WIRED that "Edward Coristine joined the Social Security Administration this week as a special government employee," and said "his work will be focused on improving the functionality of the Social Security website and advancing our mission of delivering more efficient service to the American people."[2]
