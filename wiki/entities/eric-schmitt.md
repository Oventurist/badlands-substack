# Eric Schmitt

Eric Schmitt is a Republican United States Senator from Missouri and a former attorney general of that state.

According to the Badlands Brief of January 20, 2026, Schmitt introduced the [[white-house|White House]]-backed [[scam-act|Stop Citizenship Abuse and Misrepresentation (SCAM) Act]] in direct response to the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal]]. The bill would expand denaturalization authority by creating a ten-year post-naturalization window — potentially reducible to five years — in which citizenship could be revoked for conduct such as defrauding the government of $10,000 or more, aggravated felonies, espionage, or terrorist affiliations, treating such acts as retroactively disqualifying the "good moral character" requirement for naturalization. Deputy Chief of Staff [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]] publicly championed the measure.
