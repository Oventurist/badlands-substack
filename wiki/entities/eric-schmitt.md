# Eric Schmitt

Eric Schmitt is a Republican United States Senator from Missouri.[1]

## SCAM Act

In January 2026 Schmitt introduced the White House-backed Stop Citizenship Abuse and Misrepresentation Act, known as the [[scam-act|SCAM Act]], in direct response to the Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal.[1] The bill would expand denaturalization powers by creating a 10-year post-naturalization window — potentially reducible to five — to revoke citizenship for acts such as defrauding the government of $10,000 or more, aggravated felonies, espionage, or terrorist affiliations, retroactively disqualifying an applicant's "good moral character."[1] Deputy Chief of Staff [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]] praised the bill and vowed immediate denaturalization and deportation for immigrants involved in the fraud.[1]
