# John Crabb

John Crabb is a federal prosecutor with the U.S. Department of Justice who argued the government's position at the June 2024 hearing on whether [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] should begin serving his [[contempt-of-congress|contempt of Congress]] sentence while pursuing further appeals.[1]

## Role in the Bannon incarceration hearing

After the D.C. Circuit Court of Appeals rejected Bannon's appeal in May 2024, the Justice Department moved to have him immediately incarcerated, arguing that his case no longer presented a "substantial question of law that is likely to result in a reversal or an order for a new trial."[1] Responding to Bannon's contention that his appeal remained likely to succeed, Crabb told the court, "We believe it is the exact opposite. It is unlikely."[1]

U.S. District Judge [[carl-nichols|Carl Nichols]] sided with the prosecution, granting the government's motion and ordering Bannon to self-surrender by July 1, 2024.[1]
