# The Verge

The Verge is an American technology news publication.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

In April 2025 The Verge reported, citing multiple sources familiar with the matter, that [[openai|OpenAI]] was working on its own X-like social network, with an internal prototype focused on ChatGPT's image generation and a social feed.[1] The outlet reported that CEO [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]] had been privately soliciting feedback about the project from outsiders, and that it was unclear whether OpenAI would release the network as a separate app or integrate it into ChatGPT, which had become the most downloaded app globally the previous month.[1]
