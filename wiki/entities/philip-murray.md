# Philip Murray

Philip Murray was president of the Congress of Industrial Organizations (CIO) during and after World War II.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) Described as a pragmatic strategist, he shared with AFL leader [[matthew-woll|Matthew Woll]] the view that organized labor constituted a frontline in the ideological battle unfolding as Allied forces liberated Western Europe, and that labor's collective strength could be used to counter Soviet influence.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

The CIO he led was the more progressive of the two federations that merged in December 1955 to form the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]], joining with the AFL's anti-communist hardliners.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
