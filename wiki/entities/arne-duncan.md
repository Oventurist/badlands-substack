# Arne Duncan

Arne Duncan is a former United States Secretary of Education under President Barack Obama and a member of the advisory board of [[newsguard|NewsGuard]].[1]

## NewsGuard advisory role

Badlands Media's "The Information Imperium" cites Duncan — whom it nicknames the "safe schools czar" — as one of the establishment figures lending institutional weight to NewsGuard's news-rating operation.[1] The article groups him with former NSA and CIA director [[michael-hayden|Michael Hayden]], former Homeland Security Secretary [[tom-ridge|Tom Ridge]] and former State Department official [[richard-stengel|Richard Stengel]], arguing that an advisory board composed of former senior national-security and cabinet officials amounts to "a recipe for a surveillance-state wet dream masquerading as a public service."[1]

Duncan's presence is significant to the article's argument chiefly because NewsGuard's extension is distributed through more than 700 libraries and schools across the United States and Europe, giving the company's credibility ratings direct reach into educational settings.[1]
