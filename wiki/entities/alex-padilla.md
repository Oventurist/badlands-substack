# Alex Padilla

Alex Padilla is a Democratic United States Senator from California. He became the focus of national attention on June 12, 2025, when he was shoved, forced to the ground and handcuffed by federal security agents after attempting to ask a question at a Los Angeles press conference held by Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] on the subject of immigration raids.[1]

## The Noem press conference incident

Noem was in Los Angeles discussing protests in the city over President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s immigration crackdown when Padilla interjected, announcing, "I am Senator Alex Padilla. I have questions for the secretary."[1] Padilla, then 52, told the security agents "Hands off" before he was ushered out of the room.[1] A video subsequently shared by Padilla showed three agents pushing him to the ground and handcuffing his hands behind his back; Reuters reported that it was not immediately able to confirm the authenticity of the video.[1]

## Reaction

The incident was reported by Reuters and circulated widely as evidence of the confrontational atmosphere surrounding federal immigration enforcement in Los Angeles.[1] Commentary in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] took the opposite view from most mainstream coverage: [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that "no one is above the law," characterizing Padilla as being "on camera interfering with ICE agents and resisting arrest," and joked that the only failure was that federal agents had arrested a single senator rather than more of the Senate.[1] The episode occurred against the backdrop of the same week's fight over federalized [[national-guard|National Guard]] troops in Los Angeles.[1]
