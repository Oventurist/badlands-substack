# Spencer Evans

Spencer Evans is a former senior official of the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] dismissed in August 2025 and a plaintiff in the [[fbi-retribution-lawsuit|FBI retribution lawsuit]] filed the following month.[1]

## Litigation

Evans, together with [[brian-driscoll|Brian Driscoll]] and [[steve-jensen|Steve Jensen]], filed suit in federal court on September 10, 2025 alleging that his dismissal formed part of a political purge ordered under pressure from Attorney General [[pam-bondi|Pam Bondi]] and FBI Director [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]].[1] The complaint accuses both officials of knowingly violating the law to satisfy political demands to remove agents connected to earlier investigations of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Evans denies that his prior investigative work was politically motivated.[1] The plaintiffs' attorney [[abbe-lowell|Abbe Lowell]] is seeking back pay and a formal declaration that the firings were unlawful, with the FBI, the [[justice-department|Justice Department]], and the Executive Office of the President named as defendants.[1]
