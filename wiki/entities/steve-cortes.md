# Steve Cortes

Steve Cortes is an American political commentator and campaign surrogate who served as spokesman for the pro-[[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] political action committee during the run-up to the 2024 Republican presidential primary.[1]

## Defection from Trump world

Cortes, previously identified with the pro-[[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] media ecosystem, defected to the DeSantis operation in early May 2023.[1] The move triggered a public war of words with MAGA-aligned figures, most prominently [[steve-bannon]], who accused Cortes of having taken "thirty pieces of silver" — an allusion to Judas Iscariot's betrayal of Jesus.[1]

## The Young Republicans endorsement

The optics of Cortes's switch were complicated on May 24, 2023, when the [[washington-dc-young-republicans|Washington, D.C. Young Republicans]] unanimously endorsed Trump for president on the same day DeSantis launched his campaign. Cortes's daughter, Kingsley Cortes, sat on the board that issued the endorsement.[1] Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] characterized the episode as "pretty indicative of how a Trump vs DeSantis primary will go."[1]

## December 2022 NFT criticism

On the night of December 15, 2022, and the following morning, Cortes and [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] — both normally strongly supportive of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — publicly criticized Trump and his team for releasing the [[trump-digital-trading-cards|Trump Digital Trading Cards]] NFT collection on the same day as the [[digital-bill-of-rights|Digital Bill of Rights]] free-speech rollout, calling it a "grievous error" and saying "heads should roll."[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] argued that Cortes and Bannon were in fact knowing participants in a planned misdirection, their assigned role being to disparage the NFT launch so that attention stayed on the "sizzle" rather than the policy announcement.[2]
