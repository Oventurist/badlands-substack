# Kristen Welker

Kristen Welker is an American journalist for [[nbc-news|NBC News]] and the moderator of the network's Sunday public-affairs program [[meet-the-press|Meet the Press]], a role she has held since 2023.

## Trump's June 2026 walkout

The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that, in a viral moment on Sunday, June 7, 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] walked out on Welker following a heated exchange about elections being fake during an interview on Meet the Press. The Brief's commentary, authored by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], defends the walkout, arguing that "continuing to pretend elections are real harms the President's — OUR — agenda," and claims that "everything the President said to Welker is true and justified" because executing the America First agenda inside the system requires real elections.

The exchange is placed in the context of the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]] and of Badlands Media's ongoing election-integrity coverage, including the [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] program.
