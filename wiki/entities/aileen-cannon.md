# Aileen Cannon

Aileen Cannon is a United States District Judge for the Southern District of Florida, best known in the Badlands corpus for presiding over the classified documents probe involving President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## Role in the Brennan case venue

The April 21, 2026 Badlands Brief notes that the criminal investigation into former [[cia|CIA]] director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]] — newly headed by [[joseph-digenova|Joseph DiGenova]] in an advisory role to acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]] — is being overseen out of the Southern District of Florida, which the Brief identifies specifically as Judge Cannon's district. The Brief further reports that Chris DeLorenz, a prosecutor who served as a former law clerk to Cannon during the classified documents probe, has joined the case, a detail presented as reinforcing the significance of the chosen venue.
