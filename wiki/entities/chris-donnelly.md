# Chris Donnelly

Chris Donnelly is a British defence figure identified as the director of the [[integrity-initiative|Integrity Initiative]], the British-run information-warfare project whose internal documents were leaked in January 2019.[1]

## Role in the Integrity Initiative

The leaked memos furnished what Badlands Media described as a startling account of the techniques deployed by the anti-Russian British operation to infiltrate American intelligence institutions, think tanks, and media outlets.[1] Donnelly appears in that material as the operation's principal networker: the documents recorded high-level meetings he held with figures including [[sebastian-gorka|Sebastian Gorka]], a former adviser to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]; McCain Foundation director [[kurt-volker|Kurt Volker]]; and Pentagon public-relations specialist John Rendon, among many others.[1]

## Significance

Donnelly's meeting log is used in this account as concrete documentary evidence for a broader thesis — that the foreign intelligence hand behind America's deep state has a distinctly British pedigree, and that British-directed clusters operate inside the American policy establishment rather than merely alongside it.[1] The Integrity Initiative disclosures of January 2019 are presented as the opening item in a sequence that continued with the [[kim-darroch|Kim Darroch]] cable leaks and the exposure of the [[steele-dossier|Steele Dossier]]'s origins later that year.[1]
