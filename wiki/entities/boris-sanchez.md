# Boris Sanchez

Boris Sanchez is an anchor for [[cnn|CNN]], co-hosting the network's program "CNN News Central."[1]

## October 2025 exchange with Stephen Miller

On October 6, 2025, Sanchez interviewed [[stephen-miller|Stephen Miller]], the [[white-house|White House]] deputy chief of staff for policy, and repeatedly pressed him on Democratic Illinois Governor JB Pritzker's accusation that [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] was targeting individuals based on their "brown or black" skin color.[1] Sanchez asked whether, "as Pritzker frames it, you are profiling brown people," and whether the immigration crackdown was designed to go after people of color.[1]

Miller laughed and called the question "dumb," responding that illegal aliens present in the United States were taking jobs and health benefits away from black, white, and Latino Americans alike.[1]

The [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] praised Miller's handling of the exchange but questioned whether administration officials should appear on CNN at all, arguing that White House access is "almost single handedly keeping many of these networks alive."[1]
