# Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso is a landlocked Sahelian state governed by a military administration under Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]]. The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] covers the country as a focal point of confrontation between Sahelian governments and Western powers.

## Allegations against Western intelligence

In a speech in the Yaadga region in July 2026, Traoré said operatives from an unnamed Western country's intelligence service had once offered his government four chemical products that could be mixed into food and consumer goods destined for territories controlled by armed groups. He said the offer was rejected and that the visitors claimed the substances had been used previously elsewhere in Africa and Asia. He did not identify the country, provide evidence, or date the alleged meeting. In the same address he urged citizens to buy locally produced goods, alleged that foreign actors exploit ethnic and religious divisions to destabilize African states, and said he received a 2023 letter from a French national he associated with [[france|France]]'s external intelligence agency.

## Relations with the West

The source situates these remarks against strained relations with Western countries following the expulsion of French troops and Burkina Faso's closer security cooperation with Russia, [[mali]] and [[niger]]. The Brief adds that Burkina Faso declared two [[european-union|European Union]] diplomats persona non grata, ordering them to leave within three days. Contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Western intelligence agencies had funded the same [[isis|ISIS]]-aligned groups in the Sahel that they were now offering to poison, describing the arrangement as a [[hegelian-dialectic|Hegelian Dialectic]] whose real effect would be to degrade the neurological and physical capacities of African civilians, since no dissemination method could confine the chemicals to combatants.
