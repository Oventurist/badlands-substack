# John Pelletier

John Pelletier is an American law enforcement officer who served as police chief of Maui County, Hawaii, and who previously worked in the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department. Reporting circulated in August 2023 noted that Pelletier had been the incident commander during the [[las-vegas-shooting|2017 Las Vegas massacre]], the deadliest mass shooting in American history, before being sworn in as a chief deputy and later chief in Maui.[1]

## Role of an incident commander

An incident commander is the top decision maker during a major incident, responsible for delegating tasks, taking input from subject matter experts, and bringing the incident to resolution.[1] The overlap of that role across two of the most contested mass-casualty events in recent American memory drove much of the commentary surrounding Pelletier.[1]

## Maui wildfires

Pelletier held the office of Maui police chief during the [[2023-maui-wildfires|August 2023 Maui wildfires]], which killed nearly 100 people with roughly 1,000 still unaccounted for at the time of reporting, and which destroyed the historic town of Lahaina.[1] Conservative commentators cited by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] argued that his presence at the head of the response reduced the likelihood that the true cause of the fires would ever be established, anticipating a cover-up instead.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that the Route 91 shooting was itself "a cover up of epic proportions," pointing to 911 recordings describing shootings across the Las Vegas strip, a helicopter hijacking, and suppressed evidence, as well as investigators who died or fell silent afterward.[1] Concluding that the same official now led the Maui response, she described Pelletier as "the chief of government coverups" and judged it unlikely that the truth about the fires would be revealed.[1]
