# Gloria Johnson

Gloria Johnson is a Democratic member of the Tennessee House of Representatives. In April 2023 she joined fellow Democratic state Representatives [[justin-jones|Justin Jones]] and [[justin-pearson|Justin Pearson]] in joining protesters in the state House chamber and chanting for gun-control legislation following the [[nashville-school-shooting|mass shooting in Nashville]] that killed three children and three adults.[1] The protest prompted a GOP-led effort to expel the three Democrats from the chamber.[1]
