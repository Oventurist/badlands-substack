# Los Angeles Times

The *Los Angeles Times* is a major American daily newspaper, owned by Patrick Soon-Shiong. In the Badlands Media corpus it is the single most cited example of the contraction of the [[mainstream-media|mainstream press]].

## 2024 layoffs

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] reports that in January 2024 the paper laid off 25 percent of what the article calls an "already-decimated newsroom" in a single day, only months after cutting 13 percent of its workforce. The figure of 115 employees is given, drawn from the paper's own coverage and echoed by [[taylor-lorenz|Taylor Lorenz]] in the TikTok address quoted at length in the article.

According to Lorenz's account as reproduced in the source, the cuts "wiped out their entire DC bureau in an election year," eliminated nearly all of the sports staff, killed the entire technology and business section, and removed breaking news writers and social media editors. The article treats the destruction of a Washington bureau during a presidential election year as emblematic of an industry no longer capable of performing basic civic functions.
