# Erika McEntarfer

Erika McEntarfer is an American statistician and civil servant who served as Commissioner of the [[bureau-of-labor-statistics|Bureau of Labor Statistics]] until her dismissal by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in August 2025.[1]

## Dismissal by President Trump

Trump removed McEntarfer from her post following the release of a disappointing monthly jobs report, publicly accusing her of manipulating labor figures for political reasons.[1] In explaining the firing, he cited both the weak monthly employment numbers and the 2024 annual benchmark revision, which had cut 818,000 jobs from prior hiring estimates, and said he believed the agency's statistics were "phony" and politically skewed to favor Democrats ahead of the election.[1]

## Aftermath and the 2025 benchmark revision

Weeks after McEntarfer's dismissal, the BLS announced on September 9, 2025 that employers had added 911,000 fewer jobs over the 12 months ending in March than initially reported — apparently the largest downward revision ever recorded.[1] White House Press Secretary [[karoline-leavitt|Karoline Leavitt]] framed the revision as vindication of Trump's criticism, saying it proved both that "Biden's economy was a disaster" and that "the BLS is broken," and argued new leadership was needed to restore confidence in the agency's data.[1] Badlands Media commentary treated the sequence as another instance of a federal statistical agency losing public legitimacy.[1]
