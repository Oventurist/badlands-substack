# Antonio Brown

Antonio Brown, commonly known by his initials "AB," is a retired American football wide receiver who emerged in October 2024 as a vocal supporter of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s presidential campaign.[1] Badlands Media covered his political turn in its October 21, 2024 news brief, framing it as one more element in what the outlet described as an increasingly surreal news cycle.[1]

## NFL career

Brown was drafted in 2010 and went on to compile one of the most prolific receiving careers in NFL history.[1] The peak of his career ran from 2014 through 2018 with the [[pittsburgh-steelers|Pittsburgh Steelers]], a stretch in which he broke, in Badlands' description, "basically every record on the books."[1] His run with the franchise was cut short by what appeared to be a mental health situation.[1]

Within a matter of weeks during the 2019 offseason, Brown went from being the highest-paid and most highly coveted wide receiver in the league — under a contract worth tens of millions of dollars — to unemployed and considered unlikely to play at all in the 2019 season, after a trade to the Oakland Raiders and a series of incidents with that team's management resulted in his termination.[1] He later completed a comeback, winning a Super Bowl alongside [[tom-brady|Tom Brady]] and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in 2020.[1]

## Political activity in 2024

Brown told DailyMail.com that he was "all in" and ready to do whatever it took to get Trump to the White House and to make his presidency a success, including taking a job in the Trump administration or running for elected office on Capitol Hill if Trump won the November 2024 election.[1] He appeared at a Trump rally on Saturday, October 19, 2024, and the following day led a voter registration drive at a tailgate party before the Steelers' Sunday evening game against the New York Jets, handing out Trump–Vance rally towels in Steelers yellow while speaking to reporters.[1]

## Donda Sports

Brown is the current president of [[donda-sports|Donda Sports]], the sports and fashion brand founded by [[kanye-west|Kanye West]], meaning that Brown works for West.[1] Badlands' commentator described this detail as the element that made the storyline genuinely surreal, suggesting that the "Yevolution" narrative was "not only back on the menu, but merging with the Trumpamania storyline."[1]
