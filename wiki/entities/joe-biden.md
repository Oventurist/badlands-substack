# Joe Biden

Joe Biden, later the 46th President of the United States, appears in the Badlands Media essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]] in his earlier capacity as a United States Senator and co-author of federal crime legislation.

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] writes that "politicians like President [[bill-clinton|Clinton]] and Senator Joe Biden came up with the Crime Bill of 1994, which increased sentences for non-violent criminals … aka drug dealers." The article situates that legislation within a claimed scheme to fill newly privatized prisons at a time when crime was falling, and states that "kickbacks came to the politicians who helped pass the new crime bills." Biden is thus presented in the source not as an incidental legislator but as one of the named political enablers of prison privatization.

## The UAW picket line, 2023

The essay [[a-time-to-strike|A Time to Strike]] (October 11, 2023) records that Biden made U.S. labor history in late September 2023 when he became the first sitting president to join a picket line, appearing with striking [[united-auto-workers|United Auto Workers]] members in Wayne County, Michigan on the same day the [[writers-guild-of-america|Writers Guild of America]] reached its deal with the studios. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the appearance as "astonishing" and "very telling," arguing that Biden's economic policy and unprecedented spending had themselves destroyed U.S. manufacturing, and referring derisively to the resulting conditions as "#Bidenomics" — an environment in which UAW demands for a 46-percent pay raise and a four-day week were, in her view, as realistic as asking for "unicorns and world peace."
