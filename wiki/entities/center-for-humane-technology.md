# Center for Humane Technology

The Center for Humane Technology is an American technology-ethics advocacy organization associated with [[tristan-harris|Tristan Harris]] and [[aza-raskin|Aza Raskin]]. In the Badlands Media corpus it is known chiefly as the publisher of [[the-ai-dilemma|The AI Dilemma]], released in March 2023.

According to the July 2023 article "[[artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence: Hysteria vs. Reason]]," the organization's presentation argued that existing artificial intelligence capabilities already pose catastrophic risks to a functional society, that AI companies are locked in a race to deploy as quickly as possible without adequate safety measures, and that existing institutions must be upgraded for a post-AI world. The video's description explicitly encouraged viewers to call their political representatives to advocate for congressional hearings on AI risk and for the creation of "adequate guardrails."

The Badlands article treats the Center's framing as technically credible but politically incomplete. It accepts that AI presents "very real threats … that require a reasoned societal response," while contending that the organization's central prescription — a voluntary slowdown by researchers — depends on a change in human nature that the article regards as unattainable, citing the Covid-19 pandemic as evidence that "given the opportunity, powerful people will fabricate and exploit fear among the population to further centralize their power."
