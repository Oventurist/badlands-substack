# Illuminati

The **Illuminati** is the secret society founded in Bavaria in the eighteenth century by [[adam-weishaupt|Adam Weishaupt]], and in conspiracist literature a byword for hidden elite control. Part five of [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series brings the Illuminati into its argument at the point where the series' linguistic thesis meets [[qanon|Q]]'s symbolic directives.[1]

## Bavarian origin and the German connection

The essay observes that the Illuminati reportedly began in Bavaria, a part of Germany, and treats that fact as newly meaningful once an important connection between the German language and [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has been established.[1] Under the [[ancient-german-language-thesis|ancient German language thesis]], German is not merely one European tongue among others but the language once spoken by all the peoples of the world, so a German origin for an elite society is read as a marker of descent from that older global culture.[1]

## The owl symbol

The article identifies the owl as one of the Illuminati's symbols and uses it to interpret Q's post 189, which instructs readers to follow "Y/Owl" symbolism across the world.[1] The proposed decoding is that the ancient German term for "Holy Owl" is *Wai Ulu*, credited to [[frederick-dodson|Frederick Dodson]], with the *wai* offered as a possible referent for the unexplained "Y" — though the author allows that the Y may instead depict two horns.[1] This reading is developed at greater length in the article's treatment of [[y-owl-symbolism|Y/Owl symbolism]].[1]

## Relation to the Druids and the two-elites hypothesis

The essay notes that the previous installment discussed a Celtic/Druid royal connection between Trump and the [[john-f-kennedy|Kennedys]], and adds that many researchers equate the Druids with the Illuminati — a claim that complicates the series' division of the world into benevolent and malevolent elite lineages.[1] The author responds by suggesting that the "good guys" and "bad guys" may be more closely related than one would assume, while insisting this implies no overlap in aim or practice, and by stating that he is still researching the matter and will not draw firm conclusions yet.[1]
