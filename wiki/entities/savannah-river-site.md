# Savannah River Site

The Savannah River Site (SRS) is a decommissioned nuclear weapons production facility located near Aiken, South Carolina, and overseen by the [[department-of-energy|U.S. Department of Energy]].[1]

## Radioactive wasp nest incident

On July 3, 2025, employees dispatched to the decommissioned plant to check radiation levels discovered a wasp nest registering more than ten times the level permitted under federal regulations.[1] The wasps had built the nest around tanks used for the storage of liquid nuclear waste.[1] According to the Department of Energy report, the nest was treated with insecticide spray and then disposed of as radioactive waste, and the waste tanks themselves showed no leaks.[1]

Badlands commentary treated the episode as a narrative curiosity rather than a safety event, with contributor Ashe in America joking that radioactive wasps were being cast in the role previously played by murder hornets, while noting the unexplained question of how the insects became radioactive absent any known contact or leak.[1]
