# Darren Prince

Darren Prince is an American talent and sports agent who appears in Badlands Media commentary as a recurring connective figure between celebrity representation and informal diplomacy.[1]

## Clients and back-channel role

Prince has represented [[mike-tyson|Mike Tyson]] and, previously, Muhammad Ali.[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] identified Prince as the agent responsible for putting Dennis Rodman on The Celebrity Apprentice and then immediately sending him to North Korea to meet [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] — a chain of events the author connects to the later summit between Kim and President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] Prince is also described as having negotiated the Trump and Melania NFT launches.[1]

## Significance in the October 2025 Congo story

When Tyson met Congolese President [[felix-tshisekedi|Felix Tshisekedi]] in Kinshasa during the 50th-anniversary commemorations of the [[rumble-in-the-jungle|Rumble in the Jungle]], the Badlands writer flagged the Prince connection as the analytically important detail, noting he still needed to confirm who arranged the meeting but that the pattern echoed the Rodman precedent.[1] Readers familiar with The Book of Trump, the author argued, would understand the significance of the link.[1]

## Badlands coverage of Prince's network (February 2025)

In a February 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands]] News Brief item on [[linda-mcmahon|Linda McMahon]]'s advancement out of committee, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] traced a chain of connections running through Prince, a talent agent and longtime advocate for recovering addicts.[2] The commentary noted that [[dennis-rodman|Dennis Rodman]] appeared in a Baywatch episode alongside Hulk Hogan's entourage likely because the two shared Prince as an agent.[2] It credited Prince with facilitating a $6 billion grant from the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration to fight the opioid crisis — an event attended by both Kanye West and Rodman — and with brokering the NFT trading card deal Trump launched in 2022.[2] The item further stated that Prince was responsible for "unwittingly" sending Rodman to North Korea in early 2013, weeks after Rodman's dismissal from Trump's show The Celebrity Apprentice, the trip on which Rodman gave [[kim-jong-un|Kim Jong Un]] a copy of The Art of The Deal and which the author linked to the negotiated 2018 peace between North Korea and the United States.[2]
