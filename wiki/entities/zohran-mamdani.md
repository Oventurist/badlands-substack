# Zohran Mamdani

Zohran Mamdani is a New York politician associated with the [[democratic-socialists-of-america|Democratic Socialists of America]] and treated in the Badlands corpus as the emblem of the DSA's urban project.

In the March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]], the commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] writes that the DSA "failed to make every city Mamdani's NY," using his New York as the model the organization sought and largely failed to replicate. She asks what the Democratic Party's shift toward the center before the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]] means "for Mamdani in NY or Johnston in Denver or the revolutionaries still clinging to the burn-it-down phase," suggesting that the party's establishment now regards such figures as liabilities to be handled.


## The Netanyahu arrest narrative

The July 27, 2026 [[badlands-brief-245|Badlands Brief]] reports that Mamdani, as mayor of New York City, released a video declaring Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] a war criminal and stating his intent to arrest him should he ever step foot in the state. Mamdani said his administration had reviewed what authority the city possesses, but later clarified that New York City cannot itself execute an [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] arrest warrant. Reuters is cited for the point that U.S. law provides no domestic mechanism requiring state or local officials to enforce ICC warrants, because the United States is not a party to the [[rome-statute|Rome Statute]].

Netanyahu criticized Mamdani over the comments, accusing him of fomenting hatred and, in a Fox News appearance, of antisemitism. President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] publicly defended Netanyahu. [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] situates Mamdani's move within a timeline that begins with Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]]'s July 13 announcement of a campaign to dismantle the ICC, and closes the segment by asking whether Mamdani is "working with President Trump to generate these narratives developments" or whether Trump is "merely playing the Game Theory board like a master."
