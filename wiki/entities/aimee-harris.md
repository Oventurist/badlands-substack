# Aimee Harris

Aimee Harris is the woman who stole the diary of first daughter Ashley Biden and
who, in April 2024, faced a Justice Department demand for prison time.[1]

## Prosecution and sentencing

Harris pleaded guilty in August 2022 to conspiring to commit interstate
transportation of stolen property in connection with the diary theft.[1]
Prosecutors for the US Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York
had initially sought a sentence of six months of home confinement followed by
three years of supervised release.[1] On Tuesday, 2 April 2024, the Justice
Department instead asked a New York district judge to sentence her to four to
ten months in prison.[1]

Prosecutors argued for the stiffer sentence on the grounds that Harris had
requested that her sentencing hearing date be moved twelve times, characterizing
her excuses as an effort to "improperly delay" her punishment.[1]
