# Sally Yates

Sally Yates is a former United States Deputy Attorney General who served in the final years of the Obama administration and who appears in Badlands Media's reconstruction of the 2016 origins of the Trump–Russia investigation.[1]

## Role in the 2016 timeline

According to a timeline discussed in the Badlands News Brief of July 31, 2025, Yates attended meetings with FBI officials convened by Obama White House Counsel W. Neil Eggleston during the "Midyear Exam" investigation, alongside then-FBI Deputy Director [[andrew-mccabe|Andrew McCabe]]; the meetings, held on September 20 and October 20, 2016, were said to have concerned the contents of thumb drives associated with that investigation.[1]

The brief emphasizes what followed each meeting. One day after the October 20 meeting, on October 21, 2016, the [[fbi|FBI]] submitted its initial Carter Page FISA application to the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court, which granted it the same day — an application that was "approved by Deputy Attorney General Sally Yates."[1] Badlands host [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] treated the proximity of the White House Counsel meetings to the FISA application and to Christopher Steele's press briefings as evidence that [[barack-obama|Obama's]] White House Counsel may have been coordinating with the Bureau, comparing the arrangement to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton's]] use of [[perkins-coie|Perkins Coie]] as a legal cutout.[1]

## Commentary and context

Badlands commentators situate Yates's signature on the Page FISA application within the broader [[russiagate-hoax|Russiagate]] narrative, in which the sequencing of official acts — rather than any single act in isolation — is presented as the evidentiary spine of a prospective racketeering case.[1] Commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that in "the grandest RICO case of them all, causation needs to be stitched into the sequence."[1]
