# Turkey

## Trump's "tremendous ally" remark (2026)

Amid renewed strains between Washington and Jerusalem in July 2026, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] praised Turkey as a "tremendous ally" while hosting Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and publicly criticizing Israeli military operations.[1] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the praise as deliberate signaling, recalling that in September 2025 Trump invited [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdogan]] to the White House the day before Netanyahu addressed the UN General Assembly, and asked what moves would be made against Turkey after Netanyahu's visit — and when Russia would finally get involved in "solving this riddle."[1]
