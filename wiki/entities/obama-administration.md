# Obama Administration

The Obama administration governed the United States from 2009 to 2017 under President Barack Obama.[1] In Badlands commentary its most consequential domestic legacy is treated as the deliberate cultivation of the American technology sector into a small set of unassailable monopolies.[1]

## Cultivation of Big Tech

In an August 2024 essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that the Obama administration grew the [[faang-stocks|FAANG]] stocks — Facebook, Amazon, Apple, Netflix and Google — into mega tech companies.[1] During that period, he argued, these firms received preferential treatment over their competitors, an advantage that ensured they would monopolize their respective markets and become, in his words, invaluable and irreplaceable.[1]

Carlson presented this as the middle stage of a longer arc: initial creation and funding through [[darpa|DARPA]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], regulatory favoritism under Obama, and continued market dominance under the [[biden-administration|Biden administration]], during which the [[magnificent-seven-stocks|Magnificent Seven]] soared.[1] The cumulative effect, in his telling, is a [[big-tech|Big Tech]] sector that owes its existence to the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] and is only now, in 2024, beginning to break with it.[1]

## Immigration and deportation record

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote in October 2023 that the Obama administration deported more people than any other presidency — roughly 3 million undocumented people — at an annual pace exceeding [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s.[2] The administration justified the removals as targeting "criminals," but Carlson cited 2014–2015 statistics showing only 9% of deportees had a violent criminal record, and noted that 42% had children who were U.S. citizens.[2] He argued the removals were designed to "make room" for legal asylum seekers whom he characterized as less willing to assimilate and more hostile to America, comparing an open border to panning for gold in which unvetted mass entry provides cover for terrorists while ordinary economic migrants are sifted out and deported.[2]

Carlson also recalled the administration's public assurance that it was admitting only "moderate" Muslims, and challenged that framing with a [[cygnal|Cygnal]] poll finding that 58% of Muslim Americans considered the October 2023 [[hamas|Hamas]] attack on [[israel|Israel]] justified.[2]

## Airstrikes and asylum intake

Citing [[cnn|CNN]], Carlson reported that in 2016 — [[barack-obama|Obama]]'s last year in office — the administration dropped 26,172 bombs in airstrikes on seven acknowledged countries: Libya, Syria, Iraq, Afghanistan, Pakistan, Yemen and Somalia.[2] He set this against [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] data showing that the leading sources of asylum seekers admitted that same year were the Democratic Republic of Congo, Iraq, Burma, Syria and Somalia, most of which the administration had officially bombed in 2016.[2] The resulting charge is that the administration created refugees abroad and then resettled them domestically as part of a [[deep-state|Deep State]] design, an application of the broader [[mass-immigration-as-a-weapon|mass immigration as a weapon]] model tested in [[sweden|Sweden]].[2] Carlson further contended that the administration's coalition-building deliberately placed mutually antagonistic constituencies on the same political team in order to manufacture conflict rather than diversity.[2]
