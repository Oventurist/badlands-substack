# Obama Administration

The Obama administration governed the United States from 2009 to 2017 under President Barack Obama.[1] In Badlands commentary its most consequential domestic legacy is treated as the deliberate cultivation of the American technology sector into a small set of unassailable monopolies.[1]

## Cultivation of Big Tech

In an August 2024 essay, contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that the Obama administration grew the [[faang-stocks|FAANG]] stocks — Facebook, Amazon, Apple, Netflix and Google — into mega tech companies.[1] During that period, he argued, these firms received preferential treatment over their competitors, an advantage that ensured they would monopolize their respective markets and become, in his words, invaluable and irreplaceable.[1]

Carlson presented this as the middle stage of a longer arc: initial creation and funding through [[darpa|DARPA]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]], regulatory favoritism under Obama, and continued market dominance under the [[biden-administration|Biden administration]], during which the [[magnificent-seven-stocks|Magnificent Seven]] soared.[1] The cumulative effect, in his telling, is a [[big-tech|Big Tech]] sector that owes its existence to the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] and is only now, in 2024, beginning to break with it.[1]
