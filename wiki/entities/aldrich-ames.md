# Aldrich Ames

Aldrich Ames was an officer of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] who was exposed as a double agent working for the Russian KGB.[1] His case is cited in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentary as one of the rare instances in which the [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]] was applied to genuine spying on behalf of a foreign government.[1]

## Cited as a true espionage case

In his June 2023 essay "Smoke and Mirrors," [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the Espionage Act rarely pertains to actual espionage.[1] While 18 U.S.C. § 794 does address spying for foreign governments and carries a maximum penalty of life imprisonment, DeLarme wrote that the provision is almost never why the law is invoked; he offered Ames and [[jonathan-pollard|Jonathan Pollard]], who leaked classified information to Israel in 1987, as the exceptional examples of the statute's core purpose being served.[1]

By contrast, DeLarme argued, the ordinary use of the Act — and the use made of it against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the [[2023-trump-classified-documents-indictment|classified documents case]] — concerns the unauthorized gathering, possessing, or transmitting of sensitive government information rather than espionage in the Ames sense.[1]
