# David Zaslav

David Zaslav is the chief executive officer of [[warner-bros-discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]].[1]

## 2023 Paramount merger talks

In December 2023, Zaslav met with [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]] CEO [[bob-bakish|Bob Bakish]] at a lunch meeting in New York, where the two discussed a possible merger of the two media companies.[1] Zaslav also spoke with Shari Redstone, whose National Amusements Inc. owns a controlling stake in Paramount Global, about a potential combination.[1] Representatives for both companies declined to comment, and the talks were described as very preliminary, with both sides having enlisted bankers.[1]

Under Zaslav, Warner Bros. Discovery carried a debt load of $43.5 billion as of the end of the third quarter, against Paramount's $15.6 billion, though WBD's market capitalization of $28.4 billion as of the close of trading on December 20 made it the larger company by market value compared with Paramount's $10.3 billion.[1] A combination would pool assets spanning TV, film, sports and streaming, with particular interest in merging the Max and Paramount+ premium streaming services to compete with [[netflix|Netflix]] and [[disney|Disney]]'s Disney+ and Hulu.[1]
