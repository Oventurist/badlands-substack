# Ben Horowitz

Ben Horowitz is an American venture capitalist and co-founder, with [[marc-andreessen|Marc Andreessen]], of the Silicon Valley firm [[andreessen-horowitz|Andreessen Horowitz]], established in 2009.[1]

## 2024 support for Donald Trump

In July 2024, Axios reported that Horowitz and Andreessen would each donate to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s re-election effort, a shift described as part of a broader realignment in which a significant portion of the technology industry's money and influence lined up behind Trump.[1] Neither man was believed to have previously donated to a presidential campaign.[1] The pair's move followed [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s announcement that he would endorse Trump and form a political action committee to aid the campaign.[1]

The donations were consistent with a policy Andreessen Horowitz announced in late 2023, under which its partners would begin making contributions to political candidates they judged to be "advancing technology."[1] Axios characterized both men as "techno-optimists," and noted that Silicon Valley, once solidly Democratic with only a handful of Republican outliers, now had a growing and increasingly assertive red camp.[1]
