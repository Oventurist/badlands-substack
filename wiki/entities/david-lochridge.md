# David Lochridge

David Lochridge is the whistleblower who was dismissed by [[oceangate|OceanGate]] in 2018 after warning the company about safety problems with its deep-sea submersible program.[1]

## Warnings and the Titan implosion

When the Titan submersible went missing during a dive to the wreck of the Titanic in 2023, Lochridge said he hoped the five people aboard — including his former boss — could be rescued.[1] He told the BBC: "I always hoped that what happened wouldn't happen. But I just knew if they kept carrying on the way they were going and with that deficient equipment, then there would be an incident."[1]

In June 2023 the submersible imploded, killing all five people on board, among them OceanGate chief executive Stockton Rush.[1] A report from the [[us-coast-guard|US Coast Guard]] published in August 2025 found that OceanGate's failures over safety, testing and maintenance were the main cause of the disaster, corroborating the concerns that had cost Lochridge his job.[1] Summarizing the episode, Lochridge said that "people were sold a lie."[1]
