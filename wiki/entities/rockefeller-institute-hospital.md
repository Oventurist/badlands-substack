# Rockefeller Institute Hospital

The Rockefeller Institute Hospital opened its doors in 1910 and is described in Badlands Media commentary as the first center for clinical research and the birthplace of virology — in other words, the birthplace of [[big-pharma|Big Pharma]].[1]

The hospital was part of [[john-d-rockefeller|John D. Rockefeller]]'s pivot from oil into medicine, which followed the mass-market arrival of the automobile and included his purchase of shares in the German pharmaceutical firm [[ig-farben|I.G. Farben]], the funding of allopathic-only medical schools with [[andrew-carnegie|Andrew Carnegie]], and the rise of the [[american-medical-association|American Medical Association]] as the gatekeeper of recognized practice.[1] After its founding, the article argues, physicians who did not follow the guidelines of the new allopathic medical industry could no longer practice medicine in America — leaving the country, in the author's phrase, a slave to Big Pharma.[1]
