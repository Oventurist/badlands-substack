# Gonzalo Lira

Gonzalo Lira, referred to in "A Bright Red Line" as "the late Gonzalo Lira," is listed among the independent commentators whose individual efforts allowed Western audiences to see a perspective on the Ukraine war that had been conveniently omitted from Western media.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) He appears alongside [[julian-assange|Julian Assange]], Tucker Carlson, Oliver Stone, Douglas Macgregor and Robert F. Kennedy Jr.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line) The article links his work in connection with [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-bright-red-line)
