# Francesca Hong

Francesca Hong is a Wisconsin state representative and progressive candidate in the state's 2026 Democratic gubernatorial primary.[1]

Hong's momentum in the primary prompted some Wisconsin Democratic leaders to consolidate support behind Lt. Gov. [[sara-rodriguez|Sara Rodriguez]].[1] Her candidacy was cited as one instance of a broader progressive surge that alarmed moderate Democrats following left-wing primary victories in New York City and elsewhere.[1]
