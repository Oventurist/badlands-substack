# Hakeem Jeffries

Hakeem Jeffries is the House Minority Leader and the leader of the Democratic caucus in the United States House of Representatives.

The March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]] reports that Jeffries's hold on the caucus is uncertain heading into the [[2026-united-states-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]]. Many insurgent and progressive primary candidates, including those endorsed by [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]] and [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]], have refused to commit to supporting Jeffries as leader, raising fears among party officials that he could inherit a rebellious freshman class should Democrats retake the House majority. The Brief situates this within a broader intra-party "civil war" in which more than $64 million had already been spent on House primaries against thirty incumbents.
