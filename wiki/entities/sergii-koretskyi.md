# Sergii Koretskyi

Sergii Koretskyi is a Ukrainian energy executive and, as of July 2026, Prime Minister of [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[1] Ukraine's parliament voted 289–22 on July 16, 2026 to confirm Koretskyi — formerly chief executive of the state energy company [[naftogaz|Naftogaz]] — replacing [[yulia-svyrydenko|Yulia Svyrydenko]] as President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] reshaped his wartime government.[1]

Zelensky said Koretskyi's experience in the energy sector made him the best choice as Ukraine prepared for another winter under continued Russian attacks.[1] The wider reshuffle also included planned changes at the Defense Ministry and other senior posts; Zelensky described the overhaul as a leadership "renewal," while opposition lawmakers questioned the purpose of replacing officials without broader structural reforms.[1]

Russian outlet RT and some Ukrainian critics highlighted reports linking businessman [[timur-mindich|Timur Mindich]] to members of the new government, allegations that were not substantiated by evidence presented by the Ukrainian government or by Reuters' reporting on the cabinet changes.[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that it was parliament, rather than Zelensky, that installed the new prime minister.[1]
