# Sergii Koretskyi

Sergii Koretskyi is a Ukrainian energy executive and the former chief executive of [[naftogaz]], the state oil and gas company, who was confirmed as Prime Minister of Ukraine in July 2026.

## Confirmation

As reported in the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, the [[verkhovna-rada|Ukrainian parliament]] voted 289–22 to confirm Koretskyi as prime minister, replacing [[yulia-svyrydenko]]. President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] said Koretskyi's experience in the energy sector made him the best choice as Ukraine prepared for another winter of continued Russian strikes on its power grid. Zelenskyy described the broader cabinet overhaul — which also contemplated changes at the Defense Ministry and other senior posts — as a leadership "renewal." Opposition lawmakers questioned the value of replacing officials without broader structural reform.

## Controversy

Russian outlet [[rt|RT]] and some Ukrainian critics highlighted reports linking businessman [[timur-mindich]], described by critics as close to Zelenskyy, to members of the new government. Those allegations circulated in political commentary but were not substantiated by evidence presented either by the Ukrainian government or in Reuters' reporting on the cabinet changes.

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry]] treated the appointment as evidence that patronage networks, rather than the presidency alone, drive Ukrainian personnel decisions, noting pointedly that "it was the Parliament that appointed this guy as PM, not Zelensky."
