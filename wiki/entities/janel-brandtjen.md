# Janel Brandtjen

Janel Brandtjen is the named plaintiff in Waukesha County Case Number 2024CV001544, *Janel Brandtjen v. Wisconsin Elections Commission, Meagan Wolfe*, a Wisconsin lawsuit challenging the handling of voter data by the state's election authorities.[1]

## Litigation

In early January 2026, election researcher [[peter-bernegger|Peter Bernegger]] reported a "huge win in court" after the [[wisconsin-elections-commission|Wisconsin Elections Commission]]'s motion to dismiss the Brandtjen case was denied, allowing the matter to proceed to full discovery.[1] Bernegger said the plaintiffs intended to depose [[david-becker|David Becker]] of the [[electronic-registration-information-center|ERIC]] states, alleging that the commission and ERIC were unlawfully transferring voters' personally identifiable information to Becker's Center for Election Innovation and Research without notice to the voters affected.[1] The case became a focal point of Badlands' argument that election legitimacy would dominate the run-up to the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].[1]
