# Katherine Ku

Katherine Ku is a technology-transfer administrator who spent 27 years as executive director of Stanford University's Office of Technology Licensing.[1] She was one of the women who publicly accused Ninth Circuit judge [[alex-kozinski|Alex Kozinski]] of sexual harassment, allegations that preceded his 2017 retirement.[1]

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] draws attention to her institutional position rather than the allegations themselves, linking Stanford's licensing office to the Stanford Research Institute, which he describes as a conduit for CIA-funded technology development and mind-control research of the [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] lineage.[1] In his reading, technology developed with intelligence and DARPA money is licensed out to private-sector figureheads — he names Google, Facebook, Apple and Amazon as examples — making Ku, in his interpretation, a gatekeeper of that transfer.[1] Carlson presents this as an inference for the reader to weigh rather than a documented finding.[1]
