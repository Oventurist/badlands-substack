# Sumner Redstone

Sumner Redstone was the American media magnate who controlled the corporate predecessors of [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]]. According to the Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]], he handed control of the company to his daughter [[shari-redstone|Sheri Redstone]] in 2016 and died in 2020. The article characterizes him, like his daughter, as a [[donald-trump|Trump]] ally, using the family's political alignment to support its argument that Paramount's recent programming reflects a deliberate turn away from what the author calls woke conditioning.
