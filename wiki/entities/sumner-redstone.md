# Sumner Redstone

Sumner Redstone was the media executive who controlled [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]] before handing the company to his daughter [[sheri-redstone|Sheri Redstone]] in 2016.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood) "A New Hollywood" describes him as a [[donald-trump|Trump]] ally and records that he died in 2020.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)

His significance in the article is as the origin of the Redstone family's control over [[cbs|CBS]], [[showtime|Showtime]] and Paramount+, a lineage that [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treats as one strand of Trump-aligned influence within [[hollywood|Hollywood]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-hollywood)
