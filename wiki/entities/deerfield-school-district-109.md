# Deerfield School District 109

Deerfield School District 109 is a school district in Illinois that became the subject of a federal civil rights complaint in March 2025 over the treatment of middle school girls in sex-segregated facilities.[1]

## Locker room controversy

A middle school in the district reportedly required a class of 13-year-old girls to change in front of a trans-identified male student in the school locker rooms after a Physical Education class, according to the mother of one of the girls involved.[1]

Speaking at a district school board meeting, Nicole Georgas said that on February 5 her daughter came home "frightened" and "upset" after finding a male student in the girls' bathroom at school.[1] When Georgas contacted the administration, she was told that, under direction from the district's legal counsel, a student who identifies as female is free to use any of the sex-segregated facilities.[1]

Days later, according to Georgas, the male student was present in the girls' locker room during PE class, and the girls collectively refused to undress.[1] The following day, Assistant Principal Cathy Van Treese brought the girls into her office and questioned them before escorting them to the locker room and requiring them to change into their uniforms with the boy present.[1] Van Treese was accompanied by Assistant Superintendent Joanna Ford and Director for Student Services Ginger Logemann, and the monitoring to ensure compliance allegedly continued "all week."[1] Georgas told the district that her daughter refused to participate, ran out, and called her for help.[1]

## Reaction

A civil rights complaint was filed following the reported incidents.[1] Badlands commentary condemned the district's conduct in strong terms, arguing that girls historically were taught to voice discomfort and cultivate situational awareness but are now taught to suppress that intuition lest their concerns be labeled bigotry, and called for the prosecution of the school officials involved.[1]
