# Maurice Strong

Maurice Strong was a Canadian businessman and environmental figure who served as President of Ontario Hydro from 1992 to 1994, having been brought into that post by [[bob-rae|Bob Rae]] during Rae's premiership of Ontario.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) According to [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], Canada's nuclear sector was nearly shut down during Strong's tenure and a prototype for a "Green New Deal" was applied there.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

Strong is remembered in this account for describing a work of fiction he said he wished to write someday: "What if a small group of world leaders were to conclude that the principal risk to the Earth comes from the actions of the rich countries? … So, in order to save the planet, the group decides: Isn't the only hope for the planet that the industrialized civilizations collapse? Isn't it our responsibility to bring that about?"1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Ehret cites the passage as evidence of the depopulationist logic underpinning the [[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]] framework.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)

## Named among the "Four Horsemen of the Climate Cult"

In a February 2025 [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] entry on the [[us-department-of-the-treasury|Treasury Department]]'s withdrawal from the [[network-of-central-banks-and-supervisors-for-greening-the-financial-system|NGFS]], commentator [[warhamster|WarHamster]] named Strong — alongside [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] and [[zbigniew-brzezinski|Zbigniew Brzezinski]] — as one of the "Four Horsemen of the Climate Cult."[2] The commentary held that since the 1970s these men and their acolytes had waged psychological warfare rooted in the artificial scarcity thesis derived from [[thomas-malthus|Thomas Malthus]].[2]

## Nuclear moratorium and the Rio process

A later account describes Strong as an "arch-Malthusian, Rockefeller agent and god-father of the Great Reset" who endorsed a nuclear moratorium in Canada while simultaneously serving as head of Petro Canada from 1976 to 1978.[3] In this telling he is the Canadian kindred spirit of [[james-schlesinger|James Schlesinger]], the American official who cancelled nearly all US nuclear projects planned for the 1980s.[3]

Strong's 1990 interview with West Magazine, in which he outlined a novel about elites conspiring to collapse industrial civilization to save nature, is quoted at greater length in this account, including the question "Isn't the only hope for the planet that the industrialized civilizations collapse? Isn't it our responsibility to bring that about?"[3]

Strong oversaw the 1992 [[rio-earth-summit|Rio Earth Summit]], which the source says established a new era in the consolidation of NGOs and corporations under the "green" agenda.[3] That doctrine was formalized in Agenda 21 — later renamed [[agenda-2030|Agenda 2030]] — and the [[earth-charter|Earth Charter]], co-authored by Strong and former Soviet Premier [[mikhail-gorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]] between 1996 and 2000, with the international drafting committee chaired by [[steven-rockefeller|Steven Rockefeller]].[3]

After Rio, Strong took over [[ontario-hydro|Ontario Hydro]] from 1992 to 1995, where the source says he "went to war with the entire Canadian nuclear industry," cancelling refurbishments, new reactor designs and all research and development funding in favour of "sustainable" energy pathways.[3] Although some of the damage was reversed after his departure, Canada's nuclear industry is described as having decayed consistently since, with its fusion energy effort destroyed entirely.[3]

## Career summary in Ehret's 2024 account

From 1963 to 1970 Strong oversaw a vast re-organization of the [[liberal-party-of-canada|Liberal Party]] of Canada, bragging to his biographer Elaine Dewar in 1994 that he sat on the selection committee that brought [[fabian-society|Fabian Society]] agent [[pierre-trudeau|Pierre Trudeau]] into politics in 1963.[4] From 1965 to 1967 he ran Canada's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and founded the [[canadian-international-development-agency|Canadian International Development Agency]], which [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] says transformed the philosophy of international aid from assisting poor nations to build full-spectrum economies into predatory lending tied to conditionalities.[4]

Strong also mentored a young [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]], co-founded the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] in 1971, chaired the first UN Forum on Population in 1972, served on the [[bilderberg|Bilderberg]] Steering Committee, and crafted the worldview that became known as the [[green-new-deal|Green New Deal]] and [[agenda-21|Agenda 21]], launched at the Rio Summit he chaired in 1992.[4] Ehret describes him as a [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Nature Trust]] founder and vice-president of the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] under [[prince-philip|Prince Philip]], and identifies [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] as his spiritual heir.[4]

## Gaia religion and the UN framework

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that Strong was himself a devoted follower of the [[gaia-cult|Gaia religion]] during his lifetime, and that he called for the creation of a "World Gaia Day" as an international holiday celebrating the inauguration of the UN's Conference on Sustainable Development in 1992 — a conference over which he also presided.[5] Together with [[steven-rockefeller|Steven Rockefeller]] and [[mikhail-gorbachev|Mikhail Gorbachev]] he oversaw the UN's Millennial Development Goals, [[agenda-21|Agenda 21]] (later renamed [[agenda-2030|Agenda 2030]]) and the [[earth-charter|Earth Charter]], which Ehret says did much to legitimize fringe pseudo-scientific Gaia concepts as international legal doctrine.[5]

## 1001 Club and WWF

Strong was among the prominent members of the [[1001-nature-trust|1001 Club]] who were also members of the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]], and later became Vice President of the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] under [[prince-philip|Prince Philip]]'s presidency.[5] In that post he replaced [[louis-mortimer-bloomfield|Louis Mortimer Bloomfield]], another 1001 Club member whom New Orleans District Attorney Jim Garrison identified as central to the Montreal-based assassination of [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] in 1963.[5] Strong also belonged to the network of Rhodes Scholars and Privy Councillors around [[alexander-king|Alexander King]], [[maurice-lamontagne|Maurice Lamontagne]], Michael Pitfield and Governor General Roland Michener that presided over the 1971 Montebello meeting launching the [[limits-to-growth|*Limits to Growth*]] project.[5]

## Collapse of industrial civilization musings

In a 1990 discussion of a novel he said he would like to write, Strong — then vice president of the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]] under [[prince-philip|Prince Philip]] and a co-founder of the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] — mused: "What if a small group of world leaders were to conclude that the principal risk to the Earth comes from the actions of the rich countries?… So, in order to save the planet, the group decides: Isn't the only hope for the planet that the industrialized civilizations collapse? Isn't it our responsibility to bring that about?"[6]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] presents this alongside Prince Philip's 1981 remarks on population control and [[dennis-meadows|Dennis Meadows]]' 2022 comments as evidence that the Davos milieu shares the anti-industrial premises of [[eco-terrorism|eco-terrorists]] such as [[ted-kaczynski|Ted Kaczynski]].[6] Strong also created the [[united-nations-environment-programme|United Nations Environment Programme]], which in 1984 joined with the WWF-UK to create Television for the Environment, and founded the Earth Council Alliance in 1992 — bodies connected to [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]] patron [[anthony-st-john|Lord Anthony St John]].[6] He co-sponsored the [[club-of-rome|Club of Rome]]'s 1972 *Limits to Growth* report.[6]
