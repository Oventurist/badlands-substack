# Sevastopol Shipyard

The Sevastopol Shipyard is a ship repair and construction facility in the Russian-controlled Crimean port city of Sevastopol, which serves as the key Black Sea naval port for the Russian Navy.[1] The yard sits in the Kilen-Balka area of the city.[1]

## September 2023 missile attack

In the overnight and morning hours of Wednesday, September 13, 2023, a major attack struck the port, with multiple social media videos showing massive blazes at the shipyard.[1] At least 24 people were reported injured, at least four of them in "serious condition," and possible deaths were reported.[1] Initial reporting by Reuters and CNN pointed strongly to a missile attack, based on statements by Sevastopol governor Mikhail Razvozhaev, who said "Our enemies attacked Sevastopol" and that "The air defense was at work."[1]

Reuters reported that a Ukrainian air attack early on Wednesday sparked the fire at the yard, per the Russia-installed governor's statement on the Telegram messaging app.[1] Initial and unconfirmed social media photographs appeared to show a direct hit on one or more military vessels docked at the naval port, and imagery captured from multiple angles appeared to confirm significant damage to ships and port infrastructure.[1]
