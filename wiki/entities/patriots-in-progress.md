# Patriots in Progress

**Patriots in Progress** (PiP) is a Substack publication and the pen name of the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor who wrote "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"[1]

## Approach

The author describes himself as being deep down a variety of rabbit holes — the Bible, Q, [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], Pepe, and [[donald-trump|Trump]] — and as working a Systems Engineering approach to analyse how everything fits together and paint the big picture, an approach he characterises as rare.[1] He also identifies himself professionally as a safety professional, a background he invokes when discussing COVID-19 vaccine death and injury data.[1]

## The Millennial Reign essay

The essay originated in a season of seeking God and studying scripture in an attempt to reconcile the scale of evil in the world with church teachings such as the pre-Tribulation [[the-rapture|rapture]].[1] The author is careful to frame the piece as a thought exercise rather than a prophecy, states that he is not claiming to be right and that nothing in the piece is a requirement for salvation, and repeatedly stresses that the matters discussed are inconsequential to salvation, which he defines as a personal relationship with Jesus Christ per Romans 10:9.[1] He also states that he neither believes Bitcoin is God nor worships Bitcoin, though he holds it deserves proper analysis now that it has been discovered.[1]

The essay closes with a joke that Badlands editor Burning Bright would fire him if the piece ran any longer, to which an editor's note replies that PiP has been warned, and directs readers to more of his work for free at Patriots in Progress.[1] A standing disclaimer notes that Badlands Media articles represent the opinions of contributing authors and do not necessarily represent the views of Badlands Media itself.[1]

## Badlands Media programming (2023)

The January 2023 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] schedule listed Patriots in Progress as co-host of [[rugpull-radio|Rugpull Radio]] with [[gmoney|GMoney]], airing Thursdays at 10:30pm ET and covering the benefits and dangers of [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and blockchain technology alongside criticism of the central banking system.[2]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[3]
