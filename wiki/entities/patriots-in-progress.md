# Patriots in Progress

**Patriots in Progress** ("PiP") is the pen name of a contributing author to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] who also publishes independently at the Substack newsletter Patriots in Progress. The byline is attached to the July 24, 2023 feature "Are We Entering the Millennial Reign? — Eschaton or Coincidence?", which received 179 likes.

## Style and self-description

The author opens with the greeting "Shadilay, Pepes!" and writes in a register that mixes theological exposition with Q-adjacent internet idiom, punctuating arguments with "Kek." He describes himself as being "deep down a variety of rabbit holes (Bible, [[q|Q]], [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]], Pepe, [[donald-trump|Trump]], etc.)" and as applying a Systems Engineering approach to analyze how everything fits together and paint the big picture, which he calls rare. He also identifies himself as "a safety professional," a background he cites when discussing [[covid-19-vaccines|Covid vaccine]] death and injury data.

## Intellectual posture

PiP frames his eschatological hypothesis with unusually heavy disclaimers: he is not claiming to be right, not issuing prophecy, and not making any of it a requirement for salvation, which he identifies with a personal relationship with Jesus Christ per Romans 10:9. He states that on the details of Revelation "WE ARE ALL WRONG," and argues that it is unwise to approach the topic "from a position of all-knowing close-mindedness." He also states plainly that he neither believes Bitcoin is God nor worships Bitcoin.

The article ends with a joke that had he written more, [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] would fire him "or at the very least suggest an untimely and happenstance meeting with his wolf," followed by an editor's note: "this is correct. PiP has been warned." His closing exhortation is "Let's bring Christ and Bitcoin to the world! Hodl the line, relinquish nothing, advance when possible! God Bless, Patriots!"
