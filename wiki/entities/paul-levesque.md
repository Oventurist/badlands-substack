# Paul Levesque

Paul Levesque, known worldwide by his ring name Triple H, is a former professional wrestler who serves as Chief Content Officer of [[world-wrestling-entertainment|World Wrestling Entertainment]].[1] In that role he functions as the principal creative executive for WWE's programming and its marquee live events.[1]

## WrestleMania 43 announcement

In September 2025, Levesque appeared alongside Saudi official [[turki-alalshikh|Turki Alalshikh]] at the Fontainebleau Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas to announce that [[wrestlemania-43|WrestleMania 43]] would take place in Riyadh in 2027 — the first staging of the event outside North America.[1] Several of professional wrestling's biggest stars joined the two executives for the announcement.[1] Alalshikh described the deal as a significant moment in the Kingdom's partnership with WWE, positioning it as the delivery of a 2018 pledge to pursue large joint projects in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[1]

The move slotted into a broader pattern of Gulf sovereign investment in American sports and entertainment properties that Badlands commentary read as part of a wider realignment of the cultural and media landscape.[1]

## White House appearance (July 2025)

On July 31, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] introduced Levesque — described as his "friend for a long time" — at a [[white-house|White House]] executive order signing tied to the presidential fitness test.[2] Commentators noted that WWE and Levesque have become increasingly difficult to separate from politics: Levesque sat behind [[linda-mcmahon|Linda McMahon]] during her Senate confirmation hearing in February 2025 as she was questioned about the Ring Boys sex abuse scandal, and months later was photographed with Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]][2]

Badlands commentary added biographical context: Levesque married Stephanie McMahon, daughter of WWE founder Vince McMahon, and now serves as WWE's Chief Content Officer.[2] He joined Trump at Mar-a-Lago in December when Linda McMahon was announced as the pick for Secretary of Education, and he co-founded the stable "D-Generation X" with [[hulk-hogan|Hulk Hogan]], often cited as the most popular group of heel characters in WWE history.[2] In April, Levesque publicly called Trump a "genius" and argued that Trump plays a character to antagonize his enemies, a technique Levesque suggested Trump learned from his time in WWE; both men are WWE Hall of Fame inductees.[2]
