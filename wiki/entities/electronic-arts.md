# Electronic Arts

Electronic Arts is the American video game publisher acquired in 2025 through a $55 billion transaction led by Saudi Arabia's [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]].[1] The deal is cited as one of the largest steps in Riyadh's multi-billion-dollar entertainment push under Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]]'s [[vision-2030|Vision 2030]] diversification plan, alongside full control of MBC Group, a $1 billion studio partnership with Arena SNK, and funding for domestic film production and festivals.[1] Badlands commentary situates the EA acquisition within a wider pattern of Gulf sovereign capital anchoring positions across gaming, sports, and film — the same capital scaffolding behind the [[paramount-warner-bros-discovery-merger|Paramount–Warner Bros. Discovery merger]].[1]

## $55 billion buyout, September 2025

On Monday 29 September 2025 Electronic Arts, the video game company behind hits including Madden NFL and The Sims, entered into an agreement to be bought for $55 billion in what was reported as the largest private equity-funded buyout in history.[2] The three buyers were the private equity firm [[silver-lake-partners|Silver Lake Partners]], which is also set to co-own TikTok's new US business; Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, the [[public-investment-fund|Public Investment Fund]]; and [[affinity-partners|Affinity Partners]], the investment firm founded and led by [[jared-kushner|Jared Kushner]], son-in-law of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[2]

Current stockholders were to receive $210 per share in cash, 25 percent above the stock's value the preceding Thursday, with the share price having already begun rising on the Friday as acquisition rumours surfaced.[2] The transaction remained subject to regulatory approval because of Saudi Arabian involvement, though commentary noted the closeness of the Saudi-Trump family relationship, attributed in no small part to the Saudi-backed LIV Golf tour hosting events at Trump's clubs.[2]
