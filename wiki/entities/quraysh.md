# Quraysh

The Quraysh were the Meccan tribe into which [[prophet-muhammad|Muhammad]] was born. According to the Badlands Media essay [[arabian-nights]], the tribe was formed when [[qusayy-ibn-kilab]] brought together a number of [[bedouin]] nomadic clans and led them in retaking the [[kaaba]] from a syndicate of godless criminals who had taken control of Mecca. The success of the mission forged a bond among the participating clans, who swore an oath to one another and thereafter identified themselves collectively as Quraysh.

A map of Arabian tribes in 600 AD reproduced in the article shows the Quraysh occupying and controlling *Makkah* — Mecca — with the [[banu-hashim]] as one of its constituent clans. The same map shows no settlement of significance at the site of modern Riyadh, the seat of the [[house-of-saud]], which lay roughly where *al-Yamamah* is marked.
