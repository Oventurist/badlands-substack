# Quraysh

The Quraysh were the Arabian tribe that controlled the town of Makkah (Mecca) and to which both [[hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim]] and the Prophet [[muhammad|Muhammad]] belonged.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Formation

The tribe was formed when Qusayy ibn Kilab — Hashim's grandfather — brought together many [[bedouin|Bedouin]] nomadic clans and led them to retake the Ka'aba, the sacred stone structure built by [[abraham|Abraham]] and his son Ishmael, from a syndicate of godless criminals who had seized control of Mecca.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) The success of that mission forged a bond among the clans, who swore an oath to one another and thenceforth identified as the tribe of Quraysh.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)

## Composition

The Banu Hashim — the [[hashemites|Hashemites]] — were a clan within the Quraysh.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights) Maps of Arabian tribes circa 600 AD, before the Prophet Muhammad, show the Quraysh occupying and controlling Makkah, while the future Saudi capital of [[riyadh|Riyadh]] does not appear at all, its area roughly corresponding to al-Yamamah, then home to the [[banu-bakr|Banu Bakr]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/arabian-nights)
