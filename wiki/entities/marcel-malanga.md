# Marcel Malanga

Marcel Malanga is an American, aged 21 at the time, who was arrested in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] following the failed coup attempt of May 2024 led by his father, [[christian-malanga|Christian Malanga]].[1]

## Role in the 2024 coup attempt

Marcel accompanied his father — a Congolese former refugee who had become a naturalized American — from their home in Utah to the central African country.[1] The plot, carried out in the early hours of Sunday, May 19, 2024 in the Gombe area of northern Kinshasa near the Palais de la Nation, was thwarted by Congolese security forces, who shot Christian Malanga dead and arrested dozens of the roughly 40-strong militia of "foreigners and Congolese" nationals.[1] Marcel was among those taken into custody, as were two American co-conspirators including [[benjamin-zalman-polun|Benjamin Zalman-Polun]], a 36-year-old former cannabis dealer from Maryland.[1]
