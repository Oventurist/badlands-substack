# Qing Dynasty

The Qing dynasty, also referred to as the Manchu dynasty, was the last imperial dynasty of China and the hereditary system that [[sun-yat-sen|Sun Yat-sen]]'s revolutionary movement set out to dismantle.[1]

The [[wuchang-uprising|Wuchang uprising]] of 1911 eventually brought the dynasty down while Sun was on a lecture and fundraising tour of the United States and Canada.[1] Sun cut the tour short and departed for his homeland to oversee the dismantling of the dynastic system and the establishment of a Chinese republican government for the first time in history.[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] frames the dynasty as one half of a twofold crisis facing China — outdated dynastic traditions from within, and hostile manipulation by empires from without — that the young Sun Yat-sen recognized during his American studies from 1879 to 1883.[1] [[henry-wallace|Henry A. Wallace]] later wrote that the 1911 overthrow of the Manchu dynasty marked the first time in Asia's history that a people turned its back on the whole principle of monarchy and hereditary rule.[1]
