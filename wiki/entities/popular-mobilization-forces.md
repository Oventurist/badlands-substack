# Popular Mobilization Forces

The Popular Mobilization Forces, also referred to as the Popular Mobilization Units (PMU), are a coalition of predominantly Shiite militias in [[iraq|Iraq]].[1] The coalition was formed in 2014 to fight ISIS and was recognised under a 2016 Iraqi law as an independent military force answering directly to the Iraqi prime minister.[1]

## Iranian links

Despite their formal legal status inside the Iraqi state, most observers regard many constituent groups — including [[kataib-hizballah|Kataib Hezbollah]] — as maintaining strong links to [[iran|Iran]] and its security forces, from which they receive funding, other support, and a degree of direction.[1] Badlands Media presents this dual status as evidence that the formal chain of command in Baghdad concealed an effective chain of command running to Tehran, and specifically to [[qasem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]] and the [[quds-force|Quds Force]].[1]

## December 2019 embassy attack

Pro-Iranian demonstrators drawn mostly from the Popular Mobilization Units attacked the United States embassy in Baghdad on December 31, 2019, scaling the walls and forcing the gates of the compound in protest against American airstrikes on Iran-backed militia facilities in Iraq and Syria.[1] Three leaders of powerful militia groups were seen at the protest, including Kataib Hezbollah leader [[abu-mahdi-al-muhandis|Abu Mahdi al-Muhandis]].[1] Questions were raised as to whether Iraqi forces had allowed the protesters to reach the embassy, a highly fortified building in an area that is usually restricted.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] warned Iraqi Prime Minister [[adil-abdul-mahdi|Adil Abdul-Mahdi]] in a telephone call that Iraq bore responsibility for protecting the embassy and its personnel.[1]

According to reporting cited in the article, Soleimani had delegated a campaign of rocket attacks against American forces in Iraq to al-Muhandis in his capacity as a head of the Popular Mobilization Forces.[1]
