# George Carlin

George Carlin was an American comedian and social critic whose remark about elite power is repeatedly invoked in Badlands Media commentary as shorthand for the existence of a governing cabal.[1]

## "It's a big club, and you ain't in it"

[[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] opened his April 2023 essay "Get Woke, Go Broke" with Carlin's line, "it's a big club, and you ain't in it," adding pointedly that Carlin did not live much longer after making the statement.[1]

Carlson set out two possible readings of the quote. The first treats it as a comment on luck: some people are born with advantages and others are not, and the obstacles in a person's path are random misfortune rather than anyone's design.[1] The second holds that a select group — a cabal or [[deep-state|deep state]] — runs the world, and that great power and monetary wealth are unattainable for anyone who has not been initiated into it.[1]

Carlson stated that he sides with the second interpretation: narratives are pushed and agendas implemented by the club, politicians, celebrities and CEOs are chosen from within it, and upward mobility for outsiders is severely restricted.[1] He added that attempting to expose the club results, at best, in a life made extremely uncomfortable and, at worst, in not living long — an implication he tied back to Carlin's own fate.[1] The "club fee" metaphor derived from Carlin's line became the organizing device of the essay's argument about [[esg|ESG]] compliance.[1]

## Invoked in "The War of Stories" (December 2022)

In "The War of Stories" (December 30, 2022), [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] quoted Carlin's remark that "it's a big club, and you ain't in it" while surveying the succession of American presidencies from Kennedy through Johnson, Reagan, the Bushes, the Clintons and Obama.[2] He argued that although the truism had long resonated with the Badlands community and likely with a majority of even unawakened Americans, Carlin was not referring to those named public figures but to "those pulling their strings" — the shadowed names, organizations and collectives being documented for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]].[2]
