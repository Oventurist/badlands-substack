# Time Warner

Time Warner was an American media conglomerate whose holdings included [[cnn|CNN]], HBO and Warner Bros.; it was acquired by AT&T in 2018 and later spun off into Warner Bros. Discovery.

## Role in the Big Six

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] lists Time Warner first among the six conglomerates that controlled nearly all mainstream media as of 2017, alongside [[comcast|Comcast]], [[walt-disney-company|Disney]], [[sony|Sony]], National Amusements (now [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]]) and [[news-corp|NewsCorp]]. The article cites institutional holder data for the AT&T ticker (T) in the course of arguing that these ostensibly competing firms share the same dominant shareholders — [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]], [[state-street|State Street]] and [[berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] — and therefore serve a common establishment interest.
