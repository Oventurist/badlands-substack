# The Hollywood Reporter

The Hollywood Reporter is an American trade publication covering the entertainment industry.[1]

## 2024 non-endorsement reporting

In October 2024 the Hollywood Reporter obtained and reported the explanation behind the [[los-angeles-times|Los Angeles Times]]'s refusal to endorse a presidential candidate, publishing [[nika-soon-shiong|Nika Soon-Shiong]]'s social media thread attributing her father [[patrick-soon-shiong|Patrick Soon-Shiong]]'s decision to opposition to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s handling of the war in Gaza.[1] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] remarked that the trade paper had displayed "more traditional journalistic acumen" than the Los Angeles Times itself, which never explained the decision to its readers, and credited it with surfacing the one substantive fact absent from mainstream accounts of the [[2024-newspaper-non-endorsements|2024 newspaper non-endorsements]].[1]
