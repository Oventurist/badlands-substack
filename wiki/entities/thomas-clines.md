# Thomas Clines

Thomas Clines was a CIA officer who served as [[ted-shackley]]'s deputy during the [[secret-war-in-laos]].[1]

He was one of five men named in a 1983 report by the Australian Joint Task Force on Drug Trafficking to Parliament, which stated that Shackley, [[richard-secord]], Clines, [[rafael-quintero]] and [[edwin-wilson]] had backgrounds "relevant to a proper understanding of the activities of the [[nugan-hand-bank|Nugan Hand]] group and the people associated with that group."[1] Joe Lange observes that none of these names surfaced in media reporting on the bank's collapse, and classes them among the network's protected "untouchables."[1]
