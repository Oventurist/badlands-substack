# Bob Grenier

Robert "Bob" Grenier is a former [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] officer who served as the agency's station chief in Islamabad, Pakistan, and later as director of the CIA Counterterrorism Center.

## Role in the Badlands corpus

Grenier is cited in A Dereliction of Duty as the source for the article's claim that the CIA was legally barred from killing [[osama-bin-laden|Osama Bin Laden]] before the September 11, 2001 attacks. In the Showtime documentary *The Longest War*, quoted by the article via the Daily Mail, Grenier explains: "The CIA had a so-called 'lethal finding' [bill] that had been signed by President Clinton that said that we could engage in 'lethal activity' against bin Laden, but the purpose of our attack against bin Laden couldn't be to kill him."

The article treats this testimony as proof that the [[memorandum-of-notification|Memorandum of Notification]] signed by [[bill-clinton|Bill Clinton]] in August 1998 amounted to a [[stand-down-order|stand down order]] protecting Bin Laden, and connects it to its wider argument that successive administrations shielded a CIA asset in order to sustain what it calls the "never-ending war on terror."
