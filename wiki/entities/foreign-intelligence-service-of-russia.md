# Foreign Intelligence Service of Russia (SVR)

The Foreign Intelligence Service, known by its Russian acronym SVR, is Russia's civilian foreign intelligence agency. It features prominently in declassified American assessments of Russian activity surrounding the 2016 United States presidential election.[1]

## 2016 election material on Hillary Clinton

According to a September 18, 2020 House Intelligence Committee report declassified in July 2025 by Director of National Intelligence [[tulsi-gabbard|Tulsi Gabbard]], the SVR "possessed DNC communications that [[hillary-clinton|Clinton]] was suffering from 'intensified psycho-emotional problems, including uncontrolled fits of anger, aggression, and cheerfulness.'"[1] The report stated that Russian intelligence had obtained damaging information about Clinton's health during her 2016 presidential campaign, including evidence that her condition was being treated with severe sedatives.[1]

The report, produced by the [[house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence|House Intelligence Committee]], was based on 20 interviews with intelligence officers and FBI agents as well as a review of source material for the 2017 Obama-ordered assessment of Russian election meddling.[1] It concluded that [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] chose not to release the material before the election because he believed Clinton would win.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentator Burning Bright argued that the disclosure illustrated a pattern in which "the enemy sometimes tells you the truth, while allowing false context to invert the meaning," and speculated about whether Putin shared his "arsenal of material on Clinton" with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] — framing the possibility that "Russia Collusion was real, and it was a good thing."[1] See [[russiagate-hoax]].
