# Ukrainian Insurgent Army

The Ukrainian Insurgent Army (UPA) was the armed wing of the Ukrainian nationalist movement during and after the Second World War, operating in close association with the [[organization-of-ukrainian-nationalists|Organization of Ukrainian Nationalists]] (OUN).[1]

## Wartime record

Militants of the UPA, together with the OUN, slaughtered up to 100,000 Poles between 1943 and 1945 in the regions of Volhynia and Eastern Galicia — the killings collectively known as the [[volyn-massacre|Volyn massacre]].[1] Both organizations collaborated with Nazi Germany during the war.[1]

## Legacy

The UPA's record remains a live diplomatic issue between Warsaw and Kyiv. Polish President [[andrzej-duda|Andrzej Duda]] has said that Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Vladimir Zelensky]] told him he had never heard of the killings of Poles in western Ukraine and had not been taught about them in school, which Duda took as evidence that Ukrainians are broadly unaware of their nation's troubled past.[1] Ultranationalist leaders associated with the movement, notably OUN head [[stepan-bandera|Stepan Bandera]], are widely revered in Ukraine today.[1]
