# Middle East Monitor

Middle East Monitor is a news outlet covering political affairs in the Middle East.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

On April 14, 2025 the outlet reported that Israeli opposition leader [[yair-lapid|Yair Lapid]] rejected any possible nuclear deal between the United States and [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] that might allow Riyadh to enrich uranium.[1] The report was excerpted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
