# Alexander the Great

Alexander the Great was the Macedonian king and conqueror, described in February 2024 Greek commentary as one of the most prominent figures in ancient history.[1] In 2024 he became the focus of a cultural dispute between the Greek government and [[netflix|Netflix]] over the depiction of his sexuality.[1]

## The 2024 Netflix controversy

The Netflix docudrama "The Making of a God" portrayed Alexander as homosexual, along with his main companion in the show.[1] Greek Culture Minister [[lina-mendoni|Lina Mendoni]] said the series contained numerous historical inaccuracies and that "there is no mention in the sources that this friendship goes beyond the boundaries of friendship as defined by Aristotle."[1] An opinion piece in the Greek daily Eleftheros Typos had earlier called the portrayal a "distortion of the truth" and blamed [[oliver-stone|Oliver Stone]]'s 2004 film Alexander for "launching a propaganda campaign about Alexander the Great's homosexuality."[1]

[[dimitris-natsios|Dimitris Natsios]] of the [[niki-party|Niki]] party called the series "deplorable, unacceptable and unhistorical," arguing that it aimed to subliminally convey the idea, without basis, that homosexuality was acceptable in ancient times.[1] Mendoni said state action against Netflix would be unconstitutional given Greece's longstanding protection of artistic freedom.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief, [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] presented the series as a tactical operation within a [[neo-marxism|Neo-Marxist]] strategic objective to "queer history," in which a single cultural figure is targeted to establish a new historical narrative — comparable, he said, to earlier claims that Joan of Arc was transgender.[1]
