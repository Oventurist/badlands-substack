# The Washington Times

**The Washington Times** is an American conservative-leaning newspaper cited repeatedly in Badlands Media's coverage of the transition following the 2024 presidential election.[1]

## Role in post-2024 coverage

Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] opened his post-election analysis by flagging a Washington Times headline he said he had trouble believing — one implying that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s landslide victory had caught the political establishment off guard.[1] DeLarme disputed the premise, arguing that only corporate media hacks were genuinely surprised and that intelligence and NGO operatives — what [[mike-benz|Mike Benz]] calls "the Blob" — must have known what was coming and had yet to show their hand.[1]

The paper also published an opinion piece by [[peter-navarro|Dr. Peter Navarro]] arguing that [[us-senate|Senate]] Republicans should elect their new leader in open session and that any candidate must pledge to hold at least a 30-day recess in the first month of the new administration.[1] Navarro warned that if [[mitch-mcconnell|Mitch McConnell]] got his way and either [[john-cornyn|John Cornyn]] of Texas or [[john-thune|John Thune]] of South Dakota took the post, the new majority leader would "strangle Mr. Trump's Cabinet appointees in their cribs" and set the Trump agenda back by months.[1] The piece noted that both Trump and [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] had already called for an open-session leadership vote.[1]
