# Tasnim News Agency

Tasnim News Agency is an Iranian news outlet whose reporting served as the primary sourcing for accounts of [[iran|Iran]]'s June 2026 suspension of indirect talks with the United States.[1]

## June 2026 reporting on suspended US talks

Tasnim, citing Iranian officials, reported that Tehran was halting discussions with Washington that had been conducted through mediators, linking the decision to ongoing Israeli military operations in Gaza and [[lebanon|Lebanon]].[1] The agency reported that Iran considered Lebanon to have been a key condition of a previous ceasefire arrangement and regarded that ceasefire as having been violated.[1] Iranian negotiators, per the report, conditioned any resumption of talks on an immediate end to Israeli operations in Gaza and Lebanon and a complete Israeli withdrawal from areas of southern Lebanon.[1]

Tasnim additionally reported that Iran and allied resistance groups were prepared to respond to Israeli actions, with options under consideration including efforts to close the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] and to activate pressure points along other strategic waterways, including the [[bab-el-mandeb-strait|Bab el-Mandeb Strait]].[1] The agency said Iran's armed forces and allied resistance groups remained committed to retaliatory measures unless their demands regarding Gaza and Lebanon were met.[1]
