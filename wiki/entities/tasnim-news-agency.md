# Tasnim News Agency

Tasnim News Agency is an Iranian news organization widely regarded as aligned with the [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] and serving as a semi-official channel for Iranian state messaging.

In the January 28, 2026 Badlands Brief, Tasnim is the cited source for the report that Iranian President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] telephoned Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] on January 27, 2026, telling him that "Muslim Unity" was the key to addressing the region's destabilizing events. The Brief pairs this Tasnim report with the subsequent [[saudi-arabia|Saudi]] announcement that its airspace would not be used by any party for operations against [[iran|Iran]], treating the sequence as evidence of Riyadh positioning itself between Washington, Israel, and Tehran.
