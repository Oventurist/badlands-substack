# Main Directorate of Intelligence of Ukraine (GUR)

The Main Directorate of Intelligence, commonly abbreviated GUR, is Ukraine's military intelligence service.[1]

## Alleged role in Syria

In December 2024, Russia formally accused Ukraine of supporting the al-Qaeda-linked Syrian insurgent group [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham]]. Russian Ambassador to the United Nations [[vassily-nebenzia]] told the [[united-nations-security-council|UN]] that "Ukrainian military instructors from the GUR are present… training HTS fighters for combat operations," accusing Kyiv of stoking the armed insurgency threatening the government of [[bashar-al-assad]].[1] Nebenzia asserted that HTS had "not only not concealed the fact that they are supported by Ukraine, but they are also openly flaunting this," and reports and images of Ukrainian equipment and drones in jihadist hands circulated during the capture of Aleppo.[1] A December 1 report in the strongly pro-Ukraine Kyiv Post suggested the activity formed part of a broader Ukrainian intelligence effort to strike Russian targets outside the immediate theatre of the war.[1]

Badlands Media commentary read the episode as evidence of a wider [[nato]] operation staged out of Turkey to overthrow Assad, expel Russia from the Middle East, and blunt [[vladimir-putin]]'s power projection.[1]
