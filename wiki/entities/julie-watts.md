# Julie Watts

Julie Watts is a correspondent for [[cbs-news|CBS News]] who covers California politics.[1] She drew national attention in October 2025 for an interview with California gubernatorial candidate [[katie-porter|Katie Porter]] that Porter attempted to cut short on camera.[1]

## Porter interview

In the interview, which aired Tuesday, October 7, 2025, Watts asked Porter, "What do you say to the 40 percent of California voters, who you'll need in order to win, who voted for Trump?"[1] Porter challenged the premise of the question, and Watts followed up by asking whether Porter expected to win the remaining 60 percent of voters, and what would happen in a general election against a fellow Democrat.[1]

When Porter objected that the interview had become "unnecessarily argumentative," Watts replied that CBS News had put the same question to the other candidates in the race.[1] Porter then ended the interview.[1] Commentators at the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] described Watts's questioning as fair and standard, arguing that the resulting viral clip exposed Porter rather than the interviewer.[1]
