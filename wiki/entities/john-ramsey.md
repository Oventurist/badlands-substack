# John Ramsey

John Ramsey is the father of [[jonbenet-ramsey|JonBenét Ramsey]], the six-year-old whose 1996 murder in Colorado became one of the most publicized unsolved cases in the United States.[1]

## 2025 remarks

Following the [[boulder-police-department|Boulder Police Department]]'s December 2025 announcement that new evidence had been collected in the case, Ramsey told the Daily Mail that he was "optimistic — more than I've been in 29 years" about the prospect of identifying his daughter's killer.[1]
