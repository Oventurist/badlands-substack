# Aleksandr Khinshtein

Aleksandr Khinshtein is the governor of Russia's Kursk Region.[1]

He met with President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] during the president's May 2025 visit to the region — Putin's first since the territory was fully cleared of Ukrainian forces in April — along with heads of local municipalities and volunteers assisting those affected by the repulsed Ukrainian incursion, according to the Kremlin.[1] The visit coincided with what Russian air defense commander Yury Dashkin described as an unprecedented Ukrainian drone assault on the region.[1]
