# Maricopa County Superior Court

The Maricopa County Superior Court is the Arizona trial court of general jurisdiction for [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], and the venue in which the election contests arising from the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]] were heard.[1]

[[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s 70-page contest, filed December 9, 2022, was assigned to a judge of that court; [[michael-patrick-leahy|Michael Patrick Leahy]] devoted part of a *War Room* interview to discussing the judge who would hear it.[1] An earlier suit brought by [[abe-hamadeh|Abe Hamadeh]] and the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] immediately after the November election was dismissed by the court as prematurely filed, on the ground that under Arizona law results cannot be contested until they are certified — which occurred on December 5, 2022, opening a five-day window for contests.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] treated the Arizona judiciary with open hostility, writing that the judiciary is more evil than most politicians and that Arizona's is "definitely not exempt." She predicted that the courts would strike Lake's case down at every turn until it reached the Supreme Court of the United States, where in her view a precedent might finally be set to overturn both the 2020 and 2022 results up and down the ballot.[1]
