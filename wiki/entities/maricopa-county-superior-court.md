# Maricopa County Superior Court

The Maricopa County Superior Court is the trial court of general jurisdiction for [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona, and the venue in which Arizona statewide election contests arising from the county are heard.

## The 2022 election contests

The court received [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]]'s 70-page election contest, filed on December 9, 2022 against [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], [[stephen-richer|Stephen Richer]], [[scott-jarrett|Scott Jarrett]], and the [[maricopa-county-board-of-supervisors|Maricopa County Board of Supervisors]] and its individual members. It had earlier dismissed as premature a suit brought by [[abe-hamadeh|Abe Hamadeh]] and the Republican National Committee, on the ground that Arizona law does not permit results to be contested before certification — which occurred on December 5, 2022, starting the five-day clock in which the December 9 filings were made.

## Treatment in the corpus

The Badlands Media article "Arizona Earthquake!" treats the court, and the assigned judge in particular, as the decisive variable in the case. [[michael-patrick-leahy|Michael Patrick Leahy]] discussed the judge on Steve Bannon's *War Room*, and the article's author writes that "if there's one thing in America that is more evil than most of the politicians, it's most of the judiciary," adding that Arizona's judiciary "is definitely not exempt." She predicts that the courts will strike the case down at every level until it reaches the [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]].
