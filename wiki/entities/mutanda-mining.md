# Mutanda Mining

Mutanda Mining ("Mumi") is a copper and cobalt mining operation in the [[democratic-republic-of-the-congo|Democratic Republic of the Congo]] held within the [[glencore|Glencore]] group.[1]

## Ownership history and 2026 transaction

[[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]] brokered the deals that brought Mutanda under Glencore's control, retaining an ownership stake himself.[1] After [[executive-order-13818|Executive Order 13818]] named Gertler on its sanctions list in December 2017, Mutanda was drawn into asset freezes and foreign litigation, and mineral rights were stripped from the associated companies.[1]

In February 2026, Glencore and the [[orion-critical-mineral-consortium|Orion Critical Mineral Consortium]] signed a non-binding Memorandum of Understanding for Orion to acquire a 40% stake in Glencore's interests in Mutanda and [[kamoto-copper-company|Kamoto Copper Company]], implying a combined enterprise value of roughly $9 billion; Mutanda would continue to be managed as part of the Glencore Group.[1]
