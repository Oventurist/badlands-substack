# Asif Raza Merchant

**Asif Raza Merchant** is a Pakistani national, aged 46 at the time of his arrest, charged in connection with an alleged plot to assassinate high-ranking U.S. government officials, reportedly including former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

## Arrest and charges

Merchant was apprehended on July 12, 2024 as he attempted to leave the United States, and was subsequently held in federal custody in New York.[1] His alleged plot was foiled when he conspired with undercover agents posing as assassins to arrange the murder of high-ranking U.S. citizens.[1] Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] characterized the scheme as part of a broader Iranian effort to retaliate for the killing of General [[qassem-soleimani|Qasem Soleimani]], according to an August 6 [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] statement.[1] Soleimani was killed in 2020 during the Trump administration after, in Trump's account, seeking to blow up the U.S. Embassy in Iraq.[1]

## Immigration parole controversy

Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] (R-Iowa) questioned how Merchant had been granted immigration parole despite being flagged on a federal watchlist.[1] In an August 9 letter to [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] Secretary [[alejandro-mayorkas|Alejandro Mayorkas]], Grassley sought clarity on Merchant's parole status, citing media reports that Merchant had been interviewed by the Joint Terrorism Task Force upon arriving in the United States on April 13 because he was flagged in a federal database as a "Lookout Qualified Person of Interest."[1]
