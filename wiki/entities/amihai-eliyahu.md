# Amihai Eliyahu

Amihai Eliyahu is an Israeli politician who served as Minister of Heritage during the Israel–Hamas war that began in October 2023.[1] He drew international attention in early November 2023 for suggesting that dropping a nuclear bomb on the Gaza Strip was "a possibility."[1]

## "Nuke Gaza" remark

Asked in an interview with Radio Kol Berama whether an atomic bomb should be dropped on Gaza, Eliyahu answered: "This is one of the possibilities."[1] The comment was reported by The Cradle and generated immediate diplomatic reaction abroad.[1]

Russian foreign ministry spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] said on Tuesday, November 7, 2023 that the remarks "raised a great number of questions," asking first whether they constituted an official statement acknowledging Israel's possession of nuclear weapons, and second where the international organizations — including the [[international-atomic-energy-agency|International Atomic Energy Agency]] — and their inspectors were.[1]

## Context: Israel's nuclear ambiguity

Estimates of Israel's nuclear stockpile range between 80 and 400 warheads, deliverable by aircraft, submarine-launched cruise missiles, and the Jericho series of intermediate- to intercontinental-range ballistic missiles.[1] Israel's first deliverable nuclear weapon is thought to have been completed in late 1966 or early 1967, making it the sixth country in the world to develop them.[1] Israel has never openly tested its nuclear weapons nor signed the [[nuclear-non-proliferation-treaty|Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty]], making it the world's only unacknowledged atomic power, and has never been subjected to an inspection by the U.N. nuclear watchdog.[1]
