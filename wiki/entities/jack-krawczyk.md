# Jack Krawczyk

Jack Krawczyk is a technology executive who served as Senior Director of Product Management for Gemini Experiences at [[google|Google]], the team responsible for the company's [[google-gemini|Gemini]] generative AI products.[1]

## Role in the Gemini image bias controversy

In February 2024, after Gemini's image generation feature was "busted on some very clear race bias" — producing historically inaccurate depictions and reportedly omitting white people from image results — Krawczyk became the public face of Google's response.[1] In a statement to Fox News Digital he said, "We're working to improve these kinds of depictions immediately," adding that "Gemini's AI image generation does generate a wide range of people. And that's generally a good thing because people around the world use it. But it's missing the mark here."[1]

Critics noted that the statement stopped short of a straightforward apology, characterizing it as a qualified acknowledgement issued only after the bias had been publicly documented.[1]

## Musk's attack (February 2024)

As Google paused [[google-gemini|Gemini]]'s image generation of people in late February 2024, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] singled out Krawczyk on X, writing "What a racist douchenozzle!" in reference to what ZeroHedge described as Krawczyk's radical postings on the platform.[2] The remark came as Google acknowledged that Gemini was "offering inaccuracies in some historical image generation depictions" and said it would re-release an improved version of the feature.[2]
