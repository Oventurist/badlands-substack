# Czech Republic

The Czech Republic is a Central European state and member of [[nato|NATO]].[1]

In July 2025 the country became the first NATO member publicly to opt out of US President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s newly announced plan to supply American-made weapons to [[ukraine|Ukraine]] with funding provided by European allies.[1] Prime Minister [[petr-fiala|Petr Fiala]] said on Tuesday that Prague was "focusing on other projects and ways to help Ukraine" and was "not considering joining this project" at that moment.[1]

The Czech decision preceded similar reluctance from [[italy|Italy]], whose budget was reported not to allow participation, and criticism of the plan from the German party [[die-linke|Die Linke]].[1]

## 2025 general election

The Czech Republic held a general election on October 3 and 4, 2025, capping an unusually bitter campaign in which populist billionaire [[andrej-babis|Andrej Babiš]] was struck on the head with a crutch by a pensioner and traded insults with incumbent prime minister [[petr-fiala|Petr Fiala]].[2] Babiš's ANO party finished first with 35% and Fiala's SPOLU alliance second with 23%, with turnout near 70% and no clear coalition immediately apparent in the [[2025-czech-parliamentary-election|2025 Czech parliamentary election]].[2]
