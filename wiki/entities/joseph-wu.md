# Joseph Wu

Joseph Wu is a Taiwanese diplomat who served as Foreign Minister of [[taiwan|Taiwan]], heading the island's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.[1]

## Dispute with Elon Musk

In September 2023, Wu publicly rebuked [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] after the businessman told the All-In Summit in Los Angeles that, from Beijing's standpoint, Taiwan might be "analogous to like Hawaii or something like that, like an integral part of China that is arbitrarily not part of China" largely because the U.S. Pacific Fleet had prevented reunification by force.[1] Musk prefaced the remark by claiming a "pretty good understanding, at least as an outsider, of China."[1]

Writing on the Ministry of Foreign Affairs account on X on Thursday, September 14, Wu responded: "Listen up, Taiwan is not part of the [People's Republic of China] & certainly not for sale!"[1] He added that he hoped Musk would also ask the Chinese Communist Party to permit its own population to use X, the platform Musk purchased in October 2022.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] characterized Musk as one of the most powerful "Mind-Movers" in the information war, arguing that he had directed collective attention toward two of the most important proxy states in the Western security architecture — Ukraine and Taiwan — and that Taiwan's exposure had "yet to begin in earnest."[1]
