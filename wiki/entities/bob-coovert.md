# Bob Coovert

Bob Coovert is a Georgia resident and citizen researcher who facilitated open records requests to county election offices seeking documentation of the post-2020 election "audit" that Secretary of State [[brad-raffensperger|Brad Raffensperger]] said had been performed by the testing laboratory [[pro-v-and-v|Pro V&V]].[1]

## Open records work

Coovert's requests to counties named in Raffensperger's November 2020 announcement produced responses indicating that contracts, invoices and payments for the claimed audit could not be located. He said Raffensperger nevertheless included the "results" of that audit in a ten-page letter to the U.S. Congress, following a December 22, 2020 promise to investigate "250 credible cases of fraud" with "the whole of government."[1]

## Referrals to Georgia officials

Coovert said he brought evidence of 28 instances of alleged fraud to Governor [[brian-kemp|Brian Kemp]] and worked with Evan Meyers, Kemp's executive deputy counsel, who referred the matter to then-Inspector General [[scott-mcafee|Scott McAfee]]. According to Coovert, McAfee said he could pursue only one allegation — the claim that the audit never took place — and then ceased communication after Coovert supplied evidence that audits had not occurred in the six named counties.[1] Coovert said he also presented the material to Appalachian Circuit District Attorney [[alison-sosebee|Alison Sosebee]], who likewise stopped speaking with him.[1]
