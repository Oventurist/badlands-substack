# Stephen Miller

Stephen Miller is an American political advisor serving as Deputy Chief of Staff in the second administration of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], where he is the leading architect of immigration and deportation policy.

In the [[badlands-brief-144|Badlands Brief]] of January 20, 2026, Miller is reported to have hailed Senator [[eric-schmitt|Eric Schmitt]]'s [[scam-act|SCAM Act]], which would broaden federal denaturalization powers. Miller called the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal]] "one of the greatest financial scandals in American history" and vowed immediate denaturalization and deportation of immigrants involved in it. His statements accompanied a broader administration posture that, according to the Brief, included freezing federal funds to high-risk programs and expanding fraud probes into California, New York and Illinois.

## Criticism at the March 2026 hearing

The March 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-165|Badlands Brief]] reports that Senator [[thom-tillis|Thom Tillis]] repeatedly directed criticism at Trump allies including Miller and [[corey-lewandowski|Corey Lewandowski]] during his confrontation with Homeland Security Secretary [[kristi-noem|Kristi Noem]] at the Senate Judiciary Committee hearing of March 3, 2026.
