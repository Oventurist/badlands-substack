# Stephen Miller

Stephen Miller is Deputy Chief of Staff in the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] and a leading architect of its immigration policy.[1]

## Response to the Minnesota Somali fraud scandal

In January 2026 Miller hailed the [[scam-act|SCAM Act]] introduced by Senator [[eric-schmitt|Eric Schmitt]], which would expand federal denaturalization powers.[1] He described the Minnesota Somali welfare fraud scandal as "one of the greatest financial scandals in American history" and vowed immediate denaturalization and deportation for the immigrants involved.[1]

## "Action phase" against political terrorism (July 2026)
As White House Deputy Chief of Staff, Miller said in July 2026 that the administration was entering an "action phase" that would pair diplomatic, law enforcement and financial tools to target organizations using violence or intimidation to advance political objectives, speaking at the Washington summit of more than 65 countries convened by [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]].[2] The initiative is documented as the [[global-crackdown-on-far-left-political-terrorism|global crackdown on far-left political terrorism]]; administration officials said it targets violent transnational organizations rather than lawful political activity, while critics argued it risks conflating extremist violence with broader political movements.[2]

## Minnesota enforcement remarks (January 2026)

Appearing on Fox News' Will Cain Show on the evening of January 13, 2026, White House deputy chief of staff for policy Stephen Miller addressed federal officers deployed under [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]] in [[minnesota|Minnesota]], saying the administration had begun the work of identifying, disrupting and dismantling insurgent networks in the state.[3] He told the officers: "You have federal immunity in the conduct of your duties. Anybody who lays a hand on you or tries to stop you or tries to obstruct you is committing a felony," adding that no city official, state official, illegal alien, leftist agitator or domestic insurrectionist could prevent them from fulfilling their legal duties, and that the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] would pursue officials who crossed into obstruction or criminal conspiracy against the United States or against [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] officers.[3]

## Ned's Club birthday party (August 2025)

Miller's 40th birthday party, held at Ned's Club in Washington, D.C. on August 23, 2025, was cited in Wall Street Journal reporting as an illustration of congressional Republicans' deference to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]: House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] attended and paid tribute to the deputy White House chief of staff.[4] The same reporting recorded [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] comparing the Republican-controlled [[congress|Congress]] to Russia's Duma and senior administration advisers joking about ruling Capitol Hill with an "iron fist."[4]

## Campaign against left-wing networks (September 2025)

Following the assassination of [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]], Miller appeared as a guest on *The Charlie Kirk Show* on Monday, 15 September 2025, when the program was guest-hosted from the White House by Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]].[5] Miller said Kirk's final message to him had called for a coordinated effort against unnamed left-wing groups accused of promoting violence.[5]

He pledged that the federal government would use every available resource against those groups, declaring: "With God as my witness, we are going to use every resource we have at the Department of Justice, Homeland Security and throughout this government to identify, disrupt, dismantle and destroy these networks and make America safe again for the American people. It will happen, and we will do it in Charlie's name."[5] The remarks were part of a broader push by Trump administration officials, including President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s stated willingness to designate [[antifa|Antifa]] and other left-wing groups as domestic terrorists.[5]


## America First Legal suit against Chief Justice Roberts

The [[america-first-legal|America First Legal Foundation]], the pro-Trump legal group Miller founded, sued Supreme Court Chief Justice [[john-roberts|John Roberts]] in May 2025 in his capacity as head of the [[united-states-judicial-conference|U.S. Judicial Conference]], along with [[robert-conrad|Robert J. Conrad]] of the [[administrative-office-of-the-us-courts|Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts]].[6] The complaint argues those bodies perform regulatory actions beyond the judiciary's core functions and that their records should be subject to Freedom of Information Act requests.[6] The suit was characterized as a long-shot move as Trump allies fought court rulings blocking key actions from the Oval Office.[6]


## Disney letter

In March 2024, as head of [[america-first-legal|America First Legal]], Miller's organization sent a letter to [[disney|The Walt Disney Company]] accusing it of unlawful discrimination and of forcing political messages that damaged the company's brand and reputation through what AFL called "management's manufactured misalignment between its woke political and social agenda and the vast majority of the Company's customers."[7] Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] highlighted Miller's role as a former Trump senior adviser in framing the action as part of a coordinated pressure campaign against the company.[7]

## October 2025 exchange with CNN over racial profiling

On Monday, October 6, 2025, White House deputy chief of staff for policy Stephen Miller pushed back on [[cnn|CNN]] anchor [[boris-sanchez|Boris Sanchez]], who repeatedly pressed him on "CNN News Central" about Illinois Governor [[jb-pritzker|JB Pritzker]]'s accusation that [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] was targeting people based on their "brown or black" skin color.[8] Asked whether the immigration crackdown was designed to go after people of color, Miller laughed and replied, "Oh, what a dumb question," arguing that "the illegal aliens who are here are taking jobs away from blacks. They're taking jobs away from whites. They're taking jobs away from Latinos. They're taking their health benefits away… You know it and I know it."[8]

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] praised Miller's handling of the press but questioned the strategy of appearing on legacy networks at all, arguing that [[white-house|White House]] access is "almost single handedly keeping many of these networks alive."[8]

## Claims about the concealment of Biden's condition (July 2024)

Speaking on July 8, 2024 at an event in Washington, DC hosted by the National Conservatism Conference, Miller argued that Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] had violated her oath of office and endangered the nation by helping hide President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s "brain dead" status.[9] He said Harris knew Biden "was cognitively impaired to the point of being nonfunctional — brain dead in common parlance — for three and a half years" until "his true vegetative state was revealed as a result of President Trump's K-O" in the [[2024-presidential-debate|presidential debate]].[9] Miller added that "everybody who was anybody in the Democrat Party knew it, participated in it, covered it up, and allowed the country to be run by secret unnamed Democrat Party interests and staffers for three and a half years as we inched closer to World War Three."[9]

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] called this "the winning message at this critical moment," arguing that Harris, the cabinet, congressional leaders, bureaucrats, the intelligence community and the news media had conspired to hide an incapacitated president — a conspiracy she framed against the origins of the [[twenty-fifth-amendment|25th Amendment]] in Woodrow Wilson's hidden decline.[9]

## Appointment as deputy chief of policy (November 2024)

On Monday, November 11, 2024, President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] named Miller, a longtime adviser, as deputy chief of policy for the incoming administration, announced alongside the selection of former Representative [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]] to lead the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]].[10] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] described "'silent-running' Stephen Miller" as one of the supposed "lesser" names carrying the real significance of the second Trump administration — alongside [[kash-patel|Kash Patel]], [[tom-homan|Tom Homan]], [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]] and [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]] — arguing that such names meant nothing to normies but a great deal to the Truth Community and even more to the Deep State.[10]
