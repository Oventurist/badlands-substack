# Tarek William Saab

Tarek William Saab is a Venezuelan politician and lawyer who served as Attorney General of [[venezuela|Venezuela]] for nearly nine years before resigning in February 2026.[1]

## Resignation and appointment as Ombudsman

Saab tendered his resignation as Attorney General and was immediately appointed acting Ombudsman by the ruling party-dominated [[national-assembly-of-venezuela|National Assembly]], following the resignation of the previous ombudsman.[1] His successor as acting Attorney General is Larry Devoe, head of the National Council of Human Rights.[1] The legislature formed a 13-member parliamentary commission, with a 30-day deadline, to evaluate candidates and define the permanent heads of the Attorney General's Office and the Ombudsman's Office.[1]

## Criticism

Human rights organizations condemned Saab's interim appointment as Ombudsman as a setback for justice reform, questioning the independence of Venezuela's prosecutorial and human rights institutions even as other reforms proceeded.[1] The move came amid broader legal and political shifts in Venezuela's transitional government, including approval of a limited [[venezuela-amnesty-law|amnesty law]] shepherded through a second debate by [[jorge-rodriguez|Jorge Rodríguez]], brother of President [[delcy-rodriguez|Delcy Rodríguez]].[1]
