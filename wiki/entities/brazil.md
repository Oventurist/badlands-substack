# Brazil

Brazil is the largest economy and most populous state in South America, a founding member of [[brics]], and a recurring subject in Badlands coverage of trade policy, multipolar realignment, and election-integrity disputes in the Western Hemisphere.

## 2026 Section 301 tariff dispute

In July 2026 the [[donald-trump]] administration imposed a 25% tariff on certain Brazilian imports at the conclusion of a yearlong [[office-of-the-united-states-trade-representative|USTR]] investigation under [[section-301]] of the [[trade-act-of-1974]]. As reported in the Badlands Brief of July 17, 2026, US Trade Representative [[jamieson-greer]] said the action was taken at the President's direction after officials determined that several Brazilian policies were harming American businesses, workers, and farmers.

The investigation examined Brazilian policies in seven areas: digital trade, electronic payment services, preferential tariffs, anti-corruption enforcement, intellectual property protections, ethanol market access, and illegal deforestation. In June 2026 USTR determined that certain Brazilian practices were unreasonable and restricted US commerce; the agency then reviewed more than 360 public comments and held a July hearing with 77 witnesses before finalizing the tariffs.

Greer said the measures reflected [[america-first]] trade policy and that Brazilian practices had restricted American access to a market of more than 210 million consumers. Washington said it had negotiated for months, including formal consultations in April, without resolution.
