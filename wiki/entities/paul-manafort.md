# Paul Manafort

Paul Manafort is an American political consultant and lobbyist who served as chairman of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2016 presidential campaign and became an early target of the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller special counsel investigation]].[1]

## Indictment and Ukrainian connections

An indictment charging Manafort and his deputy Rick Gates with breaking foreign lobbying law was unsealed in late October 2017.[1] It accused Manafort of hiring the [[podesta-group|Podesta Group]] — identified as "Company B" — to lobby on behalf of an ostensibly independent nonprofit that was "under the ultimate direction" of the Ukrainian president, his party, and the Ukrainian government.[1] Special Counsel [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]]'s inquiry into Manafort's finances subsequently gave rise to the federal investigation of [[tony-podesta|Tony Podesta]] and his firm.[1]

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] emphasizes that the entire media narrative — that Manafort had Russian connections and was being indicted over Russian collusion, thereby tying Trump to Russia — was false: Manafort was not charged with anything relating to Russian collusion, and his connections, like the Podestas', were Ukrainian rather than Russian.[1] He characterizes this as a case study in [[disinformation]] and deception.[1]

## Q claims

Q drop 15 (October 31, 2017) asserted that "Manafort was placed into Trump's camp (as well as others)," framing him as a plant rather than a genuine Trump ally, and stated that the coming corruption disclosures were so serious that deals would have to be cut, since otherwise "70% of elected politicians would be in jail."[1] Lange uses the Podesta Group's abrupt closure following Manafort's indictment as evidence that Mueller's investigation had been redirected away from Trump and toward Democratic-aligned targets.[1] These claims reflect Badlands Media commentary and are not established fact.
