# Shawn Ryan

Shawn Ryan is an American podcaster and independent-media host, best known as the host of the Shawn Ryan Show, a long-form interview program distributed through YouTube and major podcast platforms.[1] Ryan's program has become a recurring reference point in alternative-media coverage of national-security and intelligence topics, and his guest announcements are frequently amplified across the independent-media ecosystem.[1]

## Coverage in the Badlands News Brief

In its May 21, 2025 edition, the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] led with Ryan's announcement — posted on X — that his next guest would be retired U.S. Air Force Lieutenant General [[steve-kwast|Steven L. Kwast]].[1] Ryan described the post as an "official preview" with his forthcoming guest.[1] The Brief's editors noted that the headline had been updated to reflect that the episode was expected to air on a Thursday rather than a Wednesday, and pointed readers to the Shawn Ryan Show YouTube channel as well as to standard podcast distribution.[1]

[[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] supplied the accompanying commentary, writing that he had been looking forward to the Kwast appearance "for a while," and noting that Kwast and his wife attend every Great American Restoration Tour conference hosted by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1] Herold characterized Kwast as "absolutely brilliant and a huge asset to our community."[1]

## Significance

Ryan's platform functions in the Badlands framing as one of the larger independent venues capable of bringing figures such as senior retired military officers to audiences outside legacy broadcast media.[1] The Brief's decision to open its daily roundup with a guest announcement rather than a hard news item illustrates the weight the outlet places on the independent long-form interview circuit as a channel for information it regards as excluded from mainstream coverage.[1]

## Hegseth interview cited in Badlands analysis (November 2024)

An interview Ryan conducted with [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] featured in the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] News Brief's assessment of Hegseth's nomination as Secretary of Defense in November 2024.[2] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who wrote that she did not watch Fox News or cover military affairs and was unfamiliar with Hegseth, said a clip from the Ryan interview clarified the logic of the pick for her, illuminating Hegseth's background, philosophy and reputation for exposing bureaucratic waste in the context of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s eighteen-month plan to streamline the federal bureaucracy.[2]

## Kwast interview release (May 2025)

The [[shawn-ryan-show|Shawn Ryan Show]] released its interview with [[steve-kwast|Lt. General Steve Kwast]] on May 23, 2025, and [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] published a same-day notice urging its readers to watch it, calling it "worth the investment of your time."[3] Badlands described the episode as a sweeping conversation about unleashing innovation, triangulating truth, and the art of the possible for humanity in the Golden Age, and asked readers to like and share the episode and to tell Ryan's audience in the comments that Badlands Media had sent them.[3]
