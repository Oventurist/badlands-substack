# Patriarchs and Heads of the Churches in Jerusalem

The Patriarchs and Heads of the Churches in Jerusalem is the collective body of the traditional apostolic church leaders of the Holy Land, which issues joint statements on matters affecting the Christian communities of Jerusalem and the wider region.[1]

## January 2026 statement on Christian Zionism

On January 17, 2026, the group issued a public statement on the unity and representation of the Christian communities in the Holy Land, describing [[christian-zionism|Christian Zionism]] as a "damaging" ideology.[1] The statement affirmed "before the faithful and before the world that the flock of Christ in this land is entrusted to the Apostolic Churches which have borne their sacred ministry across centuries with steadfast devotion."[1]

It went on to say that "recent activities undertaken by local individuals who advance damaging ideologies, such as Christian Zionism, mislead the public, sow confusion, and harm the unity of our flock," and that such undertakings "have found favor among certain political actors in Israel and beyond who seek to push a political agenda which may harm the Christian presence in the Holy Land and the wider Middle East."[1]

## Reaction

US Ambassador to Israel [[mike-huckabee|Mike Huckabee]] responded on X, respectfully disputing the patriarchs' standing to speak for all Christians in the Holy Land or worldwide and defending Zionism as a straightforward acceptance of the Jewish people's right to live in their biblical homeland.[1] Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] published a copy of the letter and read the group as condemning political leaders such as Huckabee who use their offices to promote war and undermine peace.[1]
