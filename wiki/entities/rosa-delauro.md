# Rosa DeLauro

Rosa DeLauro is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from Connecticut who serves as the ranking member of the House Appropriations Committee.[1]

## Role in the 2023 earmark dispute

In the run-up to the vote on the 2023 debt ceiling legislation, DeLauro warned her Democratic colleagues that they would receive significantly reduced funding for projects in their districts during that appropriations cycle, a warning first reported by Politico.[1] That warning formed the backdrop to subsequent reporting by Axios alleging that Speaker [[kevin-mccarthy|Kevin McCarthy]] had privately promised Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] additional "community project funding" for Democratic districts in exchange for Democratic votes on the debt ceiling bill.[1] According to one lawmaker cited in that reporting, the arrangement was intended to bring Democratic earmarks "closer to parity" with the funds Republicans were receiving in the GOP-controlled House.[1]

Because DeLauro's committee position controls the flow of earmarked appropriations to member districts, her earlier statement about diminished Democratic funding was treated by commentators as evidence that earmark parity was a live bargaining chip in the debt ceiling negotiations rather than an incidental detail.[1]
