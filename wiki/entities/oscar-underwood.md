# Oscar Underwood

**Oscar Underwood** was the United States Representative who quickly moved the [[revenue-act-of-1913|Revenue Act of 1913]] through the [[house-of-representatives|House of Representatives]].[1]

The bill, which dramatically lowered tariffs and implemented an income tax following ratification of the [[sixteenth-amendment|Sixteenth Amendment]], subsequently won approval in the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]] after extensive lobbying by the Wilson administration, and was signed into law by [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] on October 3, 1913.[1]
