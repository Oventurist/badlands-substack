# Oscar Underwood

Oscar Underwood was the U.S. Representative who quickly moved the [[revenue-act-of-1913|Revenue Act of 1913]] through the House of Representatives.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

Following the ratification of the [[sixteenth-amendment|Sixteenth Amendment]] in 1913, Democratic leaders agreed to seek passage of the Revenue Act, which would dramatically lower tariffs and implement an income tax.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913) Underwood's bill won approval in the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]] after extensive lobbying by the Wilson administration, and President [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] signed it into law on October 3, allowing for the establishment of the [[internal-revenue-service|Internal Revenue Service]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
