# Brad Cologero Getz

Brad Cologero Getz is a contributor to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] who writes commentary for the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]

## Commentary

In the January 9, 2025 Brief, Getz commented on the delays surrounding Senate confirmation of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nominees, noting that Senator [[john-fetterman|John Fetterman]] had been meeting with Trump appointees and intended to vote for many of them.[1] He observed that Fetterman's stance had drawn substantial pushback from within the Democratic Party, which in his reading proved once again that "the democrats are about division, not progress."[1]


## Commentary in the April 2025 brief

In the Badlands News Brief of April 4, 2025, Brad Cologero Getz argued that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was "decimating a zillion dollar control apparatus that has enslaved Americans to a lifetime of wage slavery," and warned that every effort would be made to turn people against him — including the use of "right-wing" and "redpilled" influencers who would either turn on him outright or plant seeds of doubt disguised as concern or as "keeping him honest."[2] He identified the tactic as "concern trolling," called it one of the oldest tricks in the book, and advised readers to be wary of those employing it.[2] Commenting separately on reporting that the tariffs' timing was poor for Hollywood, he observed that Snow White had not cracked $70 million domestic in two weeks at the box office, calling the result "brutal."[2]
