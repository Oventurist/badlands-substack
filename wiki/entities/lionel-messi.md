# Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is an Argentine footballer described in the Badlands Brief as "arguably the world's best player," and the principal reason pundits and public figures picked [[argentina|Argentina]] to win the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]].[1] Argentina had won the previous World Cup in Qatar in 2022 and was widely expected to go back-to-back.[1]

The Brief relayed the allegation, circulating among soccer fans, that [[fifa|FIFA]] and its referees have rigged matches in Argentina's favor in order to inflate Messi's scoring and win records for marketing and advertising reasons.[1] Argentina ultimately lost the 2026 final 1-0 to [[spain|Spain]] after extra time, with the Brief characterizing the team's statistical performance as abysmal.[1]
