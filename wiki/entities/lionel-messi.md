# Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is an Argentine footballer widely regarded as the best player in the world and the central figure of the [[argentina|Argentina]] national team's bid to win consecutive World Cups after its 2022 victory in Qatar.

## Coverage in the Badlands Brief

The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] identifies Messi as the principal reason pundits, celebrities and political leaders picked Argentina to win the final of the [[2026-fifa-world-cup|2026 FIFA World Cup]]. The Brief's contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describes him as "arguably" the world's best player and reports a widely held fan allegation that [[fifa|FIFA]] has rigged matches in Argentina's favor specifically "in order to boost Lionel Messi's scoring and win record for reasons related to marketing and advertising." The Brief presents this as an allegation circulating among supporters rather than an established finding, while noting corroborating context such as the [[2015-fifa-corruption-case|2015 FIFA corruption case]] and a new [[fbi|FBI]] money-laundering investigation into the Argentine football association. Argentina lost the final 1–0 to [[spain|Spain]].
