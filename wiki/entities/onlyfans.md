# OnlyFans

OnlyFans is a subscription content platform widely used for the sale of pornographic material by individual performers.[1] In a March 13, 2025 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] article, the pseudonymous contributor [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] discussed the platform in the context of human trafficking, describing OnlyFans models as "online prostitutes" and questioning the assumption that the women involved freely enjoy the work.[1]

## Criticism in Badlands commentary

The article centred on two English OnlyFans performers, [[lily-phillips|Lily Phillips]] and [[bonnie-blue|Bonnie Blue]], both of whom became famous for filming encounters with very large numbers of men and both of whom are publicly described as being supported by their families in what they do.[1] The author argued that such family involvement is better understood as pimping, and that the performers exhibit signs of dissociation and trauma consistent with grooming victims.[1]

The article further argued that displays of wealth by OnlyFans performers directed at young girls online constitute a form of grooming in their own right, a topic the author said merited a separate treatment.[1] It cited a former trafficking victim operating a YouTube channel called "Seizure Girl," who said that her customers never knew a pimp was managing the girls they purchased, as evidence that great lengths are taken to make women in pornography and "sex work" appear willing.[1] On this basis the author called for the entire sex industry to be criminally investigated, and connected the analysis to the broad definition of coercion in the [[trafficking-victims-protection-act|Trafficking Victims Protection Act]].[1]
