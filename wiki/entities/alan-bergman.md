# Alan Bergman

Alan Bergman is a Co-Chairman of Disney Entertainment, the content division of [[disney|The Walt Disney Company]].[1] Alongside co-chairman [[dana-walden|Dana Walden]], he authored the April 2023 staff memo announcing the second and largest round of Disney's planned layoffs.[1]

## 2023 layoff memo

The memo informed employees that affected workers would be notified over the course of that week, as Disney moved to reach 4,000 of a projected 7,000 total staff cuts by Thursday, with a final round to follow before the start of summer.[1] Bergman and Walden wrote that "these are hard decisions and not ones we take lightly – but every decision has been made with considerable thought, and we are doing everything we can to make sure this process is conducted with respect and compassion."[1] Employees had anticipated the round for weeks, referring to it as "the big one" or a "bloodbath"; the initial wave had begun on March 27.[1]

Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the layoffs as a sign that the entertainment arm of the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]] was fraying, invoking [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s remark that "everything woke turns to shit".[1]
