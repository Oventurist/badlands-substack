# Bruce Reinhart

**Bruce Reinhart** is the United States magistrate judge who approved the search warrant used by the [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] in its August 8, 2022 search of [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]]'s [[mar-a-lago|Mar-a-Lago]] estate in [[palm-beach-florida|Palm Beach, Florida]].[1]

## Role in the Mar-a-Lago search

According to [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]], the warrant executed at the estate was authorised by Attorney General [[merrick-garland|Merrick Garland]] and "stamped with the approval" of Magistrate Judge Reinhart, following a referral from the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives and Records Administration]].[1] The warrant Reinhart signed identified potential violations of the [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]] and the unauthorised retention of national defense information, alongside suspected efforts to destroy or conceal records in a manner that would impede, obstruct or influence the federal government, and the illegal removal or destruction of federal government records.[1]

Reinhart's court subsequently handled the release of a redacted version of the supporting affidavit, which — in Alpha Warrior's account — revealed the [[national-archives-and-records-administration|National Archives]]' 2021 attempts to recover materials, the movement of boxes in and out of Mar-a-Lago, and the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s close observation of the property.[1]

## Reception in Badlands commentary

Badlands commentary treats Reinhart's approval of the warrant as one link in a chain of official acts that, taken together, it characterises as a politically motivated assault on an opposing political leader rather than an ordinary records enforcement action.[1] In that reading, the judicial sign-off lent procedural legitimacy to an operation whose precedent could, the article warned, push the United States toward the kind of political turmoil associated with third-world countries.[1]
