# Bayer AG

Bayer AG is a German-based pharmaceutical and biotechnology giant that acquired the American biochemical company [[monsanto|Monsanto]] in 2018, folding it into its crop science division and officially dissolving Monsanto as a corporate entity.[1] In doing so, Bayer also assumed the American company's legal liabilities.[1]

## Roundup and glyphosate litigation

Monsanto has faced ongoing lawsuits since 2015 alleging that the primary active ingredient in its popular herbicide Roundup, glyphosate, is a dangerous carcinogen, and that the company failed to adequately warn consumers.[1] Missouri has become a particularly concerning litigation hotspot for Bayer, with an estimated 25,000 lawsuits pending in the state's Cole County alone.[1]

## State-level political spending

Reporting in March 2025 described Bayer ramping up its political spending at the state level in the United States — especially in Missouri — as it continues to fight the thousands of suits inherited from Monsanto.[1] The evidentiary discovery process in one of the Cole County lawsuits revealed an aggressive media campaign run by ostensible agricultural industry advocacy groups pushing for changes in state law more legally favorable to the German company; those groups were described as essentially political fronts funded by Bayer AG.[1] The effort was characterized as a means of sidestepping the Make America Healthy Again (MAHA) movement's pressure at the federal level.[1]
