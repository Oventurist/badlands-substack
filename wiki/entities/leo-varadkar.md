# Leo Varadkar

Leo Varadkar is an Irish politician who served as Taoiseach (prime minister) of Ireland, and who in late November 2023 became a focal point of public anger over his government's handling of both the release of an Irish-Israeli child hostage held by Hamas and a violent stabbing attack on children in Dublin.[1]

## The Emily Hand controversy

Varadkar posted a message on X about [[emily-hand|Emily Hand]], an eight-year-old dual Irish-Israeli citizen who was returned to her family during the November 2023 hostage exchange, describing the child as having been "lost" and stating that she "has now been found and returned."[1] The phrasing drew immediate criticism for eliding the fact that the child had been abducted and held captive rather than misplaced.[1]

UFC champion [[conor-mcgregor|Conor McGregor]] responded by branding Varadkar "a disgrace," noting that the girl had been "abducted by an evil terrorist organisation" and asking, "What is with you and your government and your paid for media affiliates constantly down playing / attempting to repress horrific acts that happen to children."[1] McGregor tied the remark to the Irish press coverage of the [[parnell-square-stabbing|Dublin stabbing of children]], writing that "The day after a stabbing of children in Ireland, NOT ONE PAPER HAD IT ON THEIR FRONT COVER. We will not forget."[1]

## Broader political context

The exchange came amid what commentators described as a wider crisis in Ireland over immigration, assimilation and press behaviour following the stabbing and the ensuing [[2023-dublin-riots|Dublin riots]].[1] Discussing the situation with [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]], [[steve-bannon|Steve Bannon]] characterised Ireland as "a powder keg" and warned that the same dynamic was "coming here, at scale" in the United States.[1] Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] framed the episode as evidence that the perpetrator of the stabbing — a migrant resident in Ireland for 23 years — had never assimilated, and used it to question whether assimilation under conditions of the [[great-migration|great migration]] remains achievable at all.[1]
