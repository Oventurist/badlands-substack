# Miranda Devine

Miranda Devine is a [[new-york-post|New York Post]] columnist whose October 2020 reporting first published material from the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]].[1] Badlands Media described her article as "explosive, and heavily censored," noting that it appeared roughly one week after [[garrett-graff|Garrett Graff]] of [[aspen-digital|Aspen Digital]] published a [[wired|Wired]] piece advising the press on how to cover a pre-election hack-and-leak.[1] The timing is central to the argument that the [[aspen-institute-hack-and-dump-exercise|Aspen Institute hack-and-dump exercise]] anticipated and shaped the suppression of her story.[1]
