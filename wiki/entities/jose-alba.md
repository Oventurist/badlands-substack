# Jose Alba

Jose Alba is a New York City bodega worker who was arrested at the Blue Moon bodega in Manhattan after defending himself and his business against a convicted felon inside the store.[1] His case became a rallying point for the city's small business community and a symbol of what store owners described as prosecutors punishing victims rather than criminals.[1]

## The case and public campaign

According to the [[bodega-and-small-business-group|Bodega and Small Business Group]], Manhattan District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]] "only backed off after an angry public campaign from store owners and their supporters united behind Mr. Alba and his Constitutional rights."[1] The Blue Moon bodega where the incident occurred was subsequently chosen as the venue for a visit by [[donald-trump|Donald J. Trump]] in April 2024, hosted by the BSBG as part of its statement of support for tougher enforcement against retail theft.[1]

## Significance

Alba's arrest was cited repeatedly in the coverage aggregated by [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] as evidence of a growing divide between New York's ruling class and its working residents, particularly small business owners contending with repeat retail theft offenders.[1]
