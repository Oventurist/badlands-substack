# Alberta Prosperity Project

The Alberta Prosperity Project (APP) is a Canadian group advocating independence for the province of Alberta.[1] US officials from the [[state-department|State Department]] met with APP members three times in Washington since April 2025, according to Financial Times reporting; Jeffrey Rath, the APP's co-founder and legal counsel, reportedly attended the meetings.[1]

"The US is extremely enthusiastic about a free and independent Alberta," Rath told the Financial Times.[1] The APP planned to send a fourth delegation to Washington the following month.[1] The group is seeking a $500 billion line of credit from the US government to support Alberta in the event of a successful independence referendum; no referendum had been called as of the reporting.[1]

A State Department spokesperson responded that "the department regularly meets with civil society types. As is typical in routine meetings such as these, no commitments were made."[1] Canadian Prime Minister [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] said he expects the US administration to respect Canadian sovereignty, noting that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has never raised Alberta separatism with him.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that it was unsurprising "that the most productive part of Canada wants to leave the globalist proxy state," arguing that Canadian liberation is a worthy cause both morally and as a US national security imperative, given the presence of "the global regime on the US northern border."[1] She noted that Alberta provides 80% of Canada's oil and 60% of its natural gas and accounts for most of Canada's energy exports, making greater Canada the sole loser in such a deal.[1] She suggested other productive provinces might follow, and that Trump "should call King Charles and make a deal."[1]
