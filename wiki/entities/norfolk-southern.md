# Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern is a major American Class I railroad, and in the Badlands corpus the operator of the freight train whose derailment devastated [[east-palestine|East Palestine]], Ohio in February 2023.

## The East Palestine derailment

On February 3, 2023, a Norfolk Southern freight train derailed in East Palestine near the Ohio–Pennsylvania border — 38 cars, with 20 of them carrying hazardous materials including vinyl chloride, ethylene glycol, ethylhexyl acrylate, butyl acrylate, and isobutylene. The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief, citing Congressional records, reports that emergency responders intentionally vented and burned vinyl chloride from some tank cars to reduce the risk of a catastrophic explosion, and that residents within about a one-mile radius were ordered to evacuate.

The [[national-transportation-safety-board|National Transportation Safety Board]] and [[federal-railroad-administration|Federal Railroad Administration]] determined that the derailment was caused by a failed/overheated wheel bearing on a railcar.

In February 2025, new lawsuits were filed against the company alleging that at least seven people — including a one-week-old infant — died as a result of exposure to toxic chemicals from the derailment and its aftermath. The Brief notes these deaths are allegations in civil suits and have not impacted official casualty figures.
