# Modernity News

**Modernity News** is an alternative online news outlet cited by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news briefs as a source on establishment narratives and COVID-19 coverage.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands

In August 2024, Modernity News reported that Dr. [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] had revealed he was infected with COVID-19 for a third time despite having been "vaccinated and boosted a total of six times."[1] The outlet contrasted that admission with Fauci's 2021 statements that "if you get vaccinated, you are protected" and that vaccinated people "can feel safe that they are not gonna get infected."[1] Badlands republished the item as evidence that the official COVID-19 narrative was collapsing.[1]
