# Metropolitan Police Department of the District of Columbia

The Metropolitan Police Department (MPD) is the municipal police force of Washington, D.C.[1]

## Federalization in August 2025

In August 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] issued an executive action declaring a crime emergency in the District — framed publicly as "[[liberation-day|Liberation Day]]" for the capital — that imposed federal control over the MPD and deployed the [[national-guard|National Guard]] to patrol city streets.[1] Mayor [[muriel-bowser|Muriel Bowser]] responded by leading a renewed campaign for [[dc-statehood|statehood]], while protests emerged over fears that [[congress|Congress]] might rescind the [[dc-home-rule-act|Home Rule Act]].[1]

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] defended the federalization, arguing that the police of the federal city should be accountable to the federal government rather than to local elected officials.[1]
