# Boulder Police Department

The Boulder Police Department is the municipal law enforcement agency for Boulder, Colorado, and the lead investigating agency in the 1996 murder of [[jonbenet-ramsey|JonBenét Ramsey]].[1]

## December 2025 announcement

In early December 2025, the department announced that new evidence had been collected in the long-running investigation into the Ramsey killing.[1] The announcement prompted the victim's father, [[john-ramsey|John Ramsey]], to say publicly that he was more optimistic about solving the case than at any point in the preceding 29 years.[1]
