# Carbyne

Carbyne is an Israeli intelligence-linked technology firm in which [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] invested.[1]

## Epstein's investment

Badlands Media cites Epstein's funding of Carbyne as part of the pattern by which he and the Maxwell siblings courted the U.S. technology scene in the years after his first arrest.[1] The investment is placed alongside Epstein's claims of holding troves of blackmail material on prominent technology company CEOs during the same period, and alongside the involvement of [[christine-maxwell|Christine]] and [[isabel-maxwell|Isabel Maxwell]] in ventures succeeding the [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] scandal.[1] The article treats these overlapping activities as evidence that the Maxwell family's intelligence-linked business model carried forward from bugged mainframe software into the internet and consumer technology era.[1]
