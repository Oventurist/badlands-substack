# Ghazala Hashmi

Ghazala Hashmi is a Democratic politician projected on November 4, 2025 to be elected Lieutenant Governor of Virginia, making her the nation's first Muslim woman elected to statewide office.[1]

## 2025 election

Hashmi's projected victory came amid a Democratic sweep of the first major elections of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term, which also saw [[abigail-spanberger|Abigail Spanberger]] elected as Virginia's first female governor, [[mikie-sherrill|Mikie Sherrill]] elected governor of New Jersey, [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]] projected to win the New York City mayoralty, and [[jay-jones|Jay Jones]] projected to win Virginia's attorney general race.[1] Trump attributed Republican losses to the government shutdown and to his own absence from the ballot.[1]
