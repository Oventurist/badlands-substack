# Kurt Volker

Kurt Volker is an American diplomat and think-tank figure identified in Badlands Media coverage as a director of the McCain Foundation.[1] He appears in that coverage chiefly through the leaked internal documents of the British-run [[integrity-initiative|Integrity Initiative]].[1]

## Contact with the Integrity Initiative

Internal Integrity Initiative memos leaked in January 2019 recorded high-level meetings between the organisation's director, Chris Donnelly, and a set of American figures, Volker among them.[1] Others named in the same documents included [[sebastian-gorka|Sebastian Gorka]], then a former Trump adviser, and Pentagon public-relations specialist John Rendon.[1] The pattern of contacts is presented as illustrating the techniques by which an anti-Russian British operation sought to infiltrate American intelligence institutions, think tanks, and media organisations.[1]

## Interpretation

In [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s account, the presence of figures such as Volker in the Integrity Initiative's contact network is significant because it shows the operation reaching into the mainstream of American foreign-policy expertise rather than confining itself to fringe or purely British institutions.[1] The 2019 leaks are treated as the first of several disclosures that year which, taken together, pointed to a British hand behind American deep-state activity.[1]
