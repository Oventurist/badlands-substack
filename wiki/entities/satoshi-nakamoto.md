# Satoshi Nakamoto

**Satoshi Nakamoto** is the pseudonym used by the anonymous person or group who created [[bitcoin]]. The Badlands Media feature [[are-we-entering-the-millennial-reign|"Are We Entering the Millennial Reign?"]] treats Satoshi's origin story as carrying theological resonance.

## The record as summarized

According to the article, the Bitcoin White Paper was released on October 31, 2008 — an event referred to in Bitcoin culture as the "Immaculate Conception." On January 3, 2009, Satoshi began the timechain by mining the first block, known as the Genesis block, and continued mining blocks for six days, creating the foundation, before releasing the software to the rest of the world. Satoshi helped the network for a few years, then disappeared and has not been heard from since.

## Interpretation in the corpus

[[patriots-in-progress|Patriots in Progress]] writes that, whether intended or not, this six-day founding period "can be viewed as a metaphor to the 6 days of creation in the Bible," and asks whether there was more to the project than creating "magic internet money." Satoshi's permanent anonymity is later folded into the article's central reading of Revelation: since the returning Christ bears "a name written that no one knows except Himself," the author asks, "Christ is an Anon? Anon like Satoshi? Anon like [[q|Q]]? Anon like Pepe?" The article also notes that the technologies Satoshi assembled — TCP/IP, TOR, SHA-256 and GPS — were all built by the military for nuclear-resistant, wartime application, a fact it uses to raise the possibility, following [[jason-lowery|Jason Lowery]]'s [[softwar|Softwar]], that Bitcoin was released as a military weapon and digital counterinsurgency operation.
