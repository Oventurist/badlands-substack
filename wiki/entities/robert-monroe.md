# Robert Monroe

Robert Monroe was an American researcher and author known for his accounts of out-of-body exploration. His book *Far Journeys* detailed encounters with extradimensional entities said to cultivate human emotions as a form of energetic sustenance, a concept known as [[loosh]].[1]

Monroe's framing described such entities as tending human emotional output "much like farmers tending crops for yield," perpetuating cycles of trauma to maximize the flow of the substance that sustains their hierarchies.[1] The idea was invoked in October 2025 Badlands commentary by [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] to interpret mass political protest and media-driven outrage cycles as engineered emotional harvests.[1]
