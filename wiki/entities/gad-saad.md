# Gad Saad

Gad Saad is a prominent and popular commentator on the American right, described by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a fanatical supporter of Israel.[1]

## Response to the January 2025 attacks

In "Hysteria and Error," DeLarme cited a post by Saad published in the days after the New Year's 2025 attacks in New Orleans and Las Vegas as an example of what he considered indiscriminate anti-Muslim rhetoric circulating within the broader conservative movement.[1] DeLarme's central objection was that Saad was not merely referring to extremists and radicals but to Islam in general, a framing DeLarme mocked by asking whether everyone from Louis Farrakhan to the Wu-Tang Clan should therefore be declared an existential threat to the West.[1]

DeLarme placed Saad within a group of commentators — alongside [[tomi-lahren|Tomi Lahren]] and [[randy-fine|Randy Fine]] — whose reactions he read as evidence that the post-9/11 propaganda framework still shaped conservative discourse.[1] He argued that the real objective of "the consensus builders and foreign policy wizards" was to convince Americans to hate Muslims without differentiating between sects and nationalities, in part to sustain continuous U.S. financing of Israel and in part to build support for escalation with Iran.[1]
