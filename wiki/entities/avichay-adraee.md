# Avichay Adraee

Lt. Col. Avichay Adraee is the Arabic-language spokesman for the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]], the officer through whom Israel issues evacuation warnings and messaging aimed at Arabic-speaking civilian populations.[1]

In October 2024, ahead of a night of Israeli airstrikes on Beirut's southern suburbs, Adraee instructed residents of several specific buildings in the area to evacuate, warning that they were "located near facilities and interests belonging to [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]]."[1] The strikes that followed formed part of the [[2024-israeli-strikes-on-dahiyeh|Dahiyeh strike campaign]], which Israel framed as targeting Hezbollah's financial infrastructure.[1]
