# Whitney Tilson

Whitney Tilson is a portfolio manager at Kase Capital Management and a Democratic donor who publicly urged President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to exit the 2024 presidential race in July 2024.[1]

Speaking to ABC News after Biden's debate and his subsequent interview with [[george-stephanopoulos|George Stephanopoulos]], Tilson said Biden was "not in [a] condition to handle the rigors of the presidency for another four years," adding that "all of us are standing by to see what happens here. Even the wealthiest people have limitations to money."[1] More bluntly, he wrote on X: "Biden's campaign is a ghost. It's over."[1]

Tilson was grouped with [[reed-hastings|Reed Hastings]] and [[bill-harris|Bill Harris]] in reporting on a mega-donor revolt that combined withheld contributions with preparations to fund an alternative nominee through political action committees.[1] Badlands Media treated the financiers' defection as a symptom of a broader elite panic, noting that the Biden campaign's substantial 2024 fundraising haul could only be transferred legally to [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]].[1]
