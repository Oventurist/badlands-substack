# Michael Shellenberger

Michael Shellenberger is an American author and journalist who, with [[matt-taibbi|Matt Taibbi]], reported on the [[twitter-files|Twitter Files]] and testified as a witness before the [[house-weaponization-committee|House Select Subcommittee on the Weaponization of the Federal Government]] on March 9, 2023.[1]

## Weaponization subcommittee testimony

At the hearing, Democratic Ranking Member [[stacey-plaskett|Stacey E. Plaskett]] referred to Shellenberger and Taibbi as "so-called journalists" and described them as dangerous and a "threat" to former Twitter employees, arguing that Republicans had brought in "two of Elon Musk's 'public scribes'" to publish cherry-picked and out-of-context material advancing [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s preferred narrative.[1] The clip of the remark, posted by commentator Greg Price, drew heavy engagement on social media.[1]

The hearing's stated subject was the weaponization of censorship against the American people, and the testimony from Shellenberger and Taibbi centered on evidence that federal agencies had participated in shaping social media content moderation — what Taibbi called a "state-sponsored thought-policing system."[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the hearing as part of a broader disclosure campaign aimed at exposing the [[censorship-industrial-complex|censorship-industrial complex]] to a wider audience, rather than as a routine congressional exercise.[1]

## Testimony on NewsGuard and GDI (2023)

At the 2023 Weaponization of the Federal Government hearings, Rep. [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] asked Shellenberger "Who is NewsGuard?"; he replied that "both the Global Disinformation Index and NewsGuard are U.S. government-funded entities who are working to drive advertisers' revenue away from disfavored publications and towards the ones they favor."[2] Badlands Media's "The Information Imperium" cites the exchange as a concise statement of how [[newsguard|NewsGuard]] and the [[global-disinformation-index|Global Disinformation Index]] convert ratings into financial pressure on dissenting outlets.[2]
