# Claudio Manuel Neves Valente

Claudio Manuel Neves Valente was a 48-year-old Portuguese national identified by US authorities as the suspect in the December 2025 [[brown-university-shooting|Brown University shooting]] and in the killing of Massachusetts Institute of Technology physics professor [[nuno-loureiro|Nuno F. G. Loureiro]].[1]

## Identification and death

Authorities announced on Thursday, December 18, 2025 that the suspect in the shooting at Brown University — which killed two students and injured nine — had been found dead in a storage unit in Salem, New Hampshire.[1] Valente's body was discovered Thursday evening with firearms next to him, and law enforcement officials said he had taken his own life.[1] Leah Foley, the US Attorney for the District of Massachusetts, said the same man was believed to have fatally shot Loureiro at his home in Brookline, Massachusetts, on Monday of that week.[1]

Providence Police Chief Col. Oscar Perez said at a news conference that Valente was a former Brown student and a Portuguese national who was last known to live in Miami.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] questioned earlier viral claims about the shooter, including reports that the gunman had shouted "Allahu Akbar" and the internet's amateur "gait analysis," arguing that the absence of a genuine antisemitism angle was evident from the outset precisely because a coordinated messaging campaign had asserted one.[1]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] was skeptical that a single suspect found dead in a storage unit conveniently closed both the Brown case and the MIT killing, suggesting the resolution foreclosed further inquiry into the murder of "that fusion guy" and pointedly asking who benefited most from the professor's death and what his research involved.[1]
