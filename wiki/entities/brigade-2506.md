# Brigade 2506

Brigade 2506 was a covert CIA team of Cuban exiles created and trained by the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] for the [[bay-of-pigs|Bay of Pigs]] invasion of 1961.[1] According to Frontline, after the brigade's defeat the agency continued to maintain and use this skilled force of covert operators wherever they were needed; their numbers grew into the thousands, and they possessed their own navy as well as other assets provided by the CIA — including businesses and banks.[1]

Joe Lange singles out the detail that the brigade held its own banks, arguing that it was connected to a money laundering operation designed to fund a [[shadow-government]]'s covert activities.[1] The brigade was commanded by [[manuel-artime]], who recruited the accountant [[ramon-milian-rodriguez]] into the CIA in 1972, and counted [[felix-rodriguez]] among its members.[1]

The brigade's Miami base of operations sat under the CIA's [[jmwave]] station, run by [[ted-shackley]].[1]
