# Florida Children and Youth Cabinet

The Florida Children and Youth Cabinet is a state body charged with coordinating policy and oversight relating to children and youth services in Florida.[1] Appointments to the cabinet are made by the Governor.

## 2019 appointment of Forough Hosseini

In 2019, Governor [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] appointed [[forough-hosseini|Forough Hosseini]] to serve on the cabinet.[1] The appointment became a point of criticism in July 2023 when reporting by The National Pulse disclosed that Hosseini and her husband [[morteza-hosseini|Morteza "Mori" Hosseini]] — major DeSantis benefactors — had also donated to a political action committee that funded Democratic Party figures including [[nancy-pelosi|Nancy Pelosi]] and [[jamie-raskin|Jamie Raskin]].[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the appointment undercut any claim of plausible deniability by DeSantis regarding the Hosseinis, asking "why would a Republican Governor appoint a well known Democrat donor to a key position within his administration?" and questioning how a governor campaigning on "fighting for the children" could assign oversight of Florida children and youth to a donor to "Friends of Hillary Clinton" and "Friends of Anthony Weiner."[1] Hosseini is also founder and chair of [[food-brings-hope|Food Brings Hope]], a charity serving homeless and economically disadvantaged students.[1]
