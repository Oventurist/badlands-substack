# Smackover Lithium

Smackover Lithium is a joint venture of a Norwegian oil major and a Canadian mining company developing lithium resources in the Smackover Formation, a deep brine deposit underlying parts of East Texas and Arkansas.[1]

In late September 2025 the venture announced the discovery of the most lithium-rich fluids ever reported in North America, measured deep beneath its Texas claims in the Smackover Formation.[1] The announcement came amid a broader entry by oil and gas firms into critical minerals: [[chevron|Chevron]] and [[halliburton|Halliburton]] announced East Texas projects in the summer of 2025, and [[exxonmobil|Exxon]] holds acreage in Arkansas.[1]

Lithium mining is one of several mineral industries emerging in Texas as part of federal efforts to urgently establish American production of materials required for advanced manufacturing, from batteries and solar cells to wind turbines, microchips and cruise missiles, with competition against China looming over the effort.[1]
