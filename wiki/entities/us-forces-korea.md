# US Forces Korea

US Forces Korea (USFK) is the American military command responsible for the roughly 28,000 U.S. troops and advanced air defense systems stationed in [[south-korea|South Korea]] under a decades-long alliance aimed at countering nuclear-armed [[north-korea|North Korea]].[1]

## 2026 redeployment of air defenses to the Gulf

In March 2026, during the twelfth day of the [[iran-war|US-Israeli military campaign against Iran]], USFK began transferring Patriot interceptors and other air defense assets from Osan Air Base to bases in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the [[united-arab-emirates|United Arab Emirates]], with heavy U.S. military transport aircraft activity reported at Osan over the preceding week.[1] The redeployment was intended to replenish depleted interceptor stocks in the Gulf region.[1]

South Korean President [[lee-jae-myung|Lee Jae-myung]] said Seoul had expressed opposition to USFK transferring the weapons "according to its own military needs," but acknowledged that "it is also the reality that we cannot fully enforce our position," adding that the transfer would not cause a "serious setback" to South Korea's deterrence against North Korea.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] read the move as the [[military-industrial-complex|Military Industrial Complex]] cannibalizing itself, stripping resources from the Pacific theater where they might be needed in a potential showdown with [[communist-china|China]] over [[taiwan|Taiwan]], and as an indicator that [[israel|Israel]] was in serious need of missile interceptors.[1]
