# Henry Wallace

**Henry Wallace** is named in Badlands Media commentary alongside [[franklin-d-roosevelt|Franklin Roosevelt]] as a figure whose conception of the [[united-nations|United Nations]] differed sharply from what the organisation later became.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## The UN as Wallace envisioned it

Contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that the UN as envisioned by Roosevelt or Wallace was never meant to be a world government, but rather a platform for dialogue and for the harmonisation of security and economic interests among civilizations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) This framing supports the essay's wider claim that the drive toward supranational technocratic government associated with the [[great-reset|Great Reset]] represents a departure from, not a fulfilment of, the post-war design.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Context

Wallace is placed within the same anti-colonial post-war vision that Ehret associates with the original [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods]] arrangements — fixed exchange rates to block speculation and long-term credit for large-scale development intended to internationalise the New Deal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Postwar targeting

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that after 1945 the [[truman-doctrine|Truman Doctrine]] and the Anglo-American "special relationship" replaced [[franklin-d-roosevelt|FDR]]'s anti-colonial vision, and an anti-communist witch hunt turned America into a fascist police state under FBI surveillance in which everyone friendly to Russia was targeted for destruction.[2] Wallace and [[harry-dexter-white|Harry Dexter White]] were the first of FDR's close allies to feel that targeting; White's death in 1948 came while he was campaigning for Wallace's presidential bid, and it ended the era of anti-colonialists running the [[international-monetary-fund|IMF]].[2]
