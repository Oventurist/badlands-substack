# Jonathan Mellis

Jonathan Gennaro Mellis is a January 6, 2021 defendant prosecuted by the US Attorney's Office for the District of Columbia.[1]

Badlands Media cited Mellis as a case that contradicts Congressman [[ken-buck|Ken Buck]]'s September 2023 assertion that all January 6 detention decisions were made by the book, reporting that Mellis had never received a bond hearing.[1] The same rebuttal cited Ryan Samsel, who was detained but not indicted for several months, and Frankie Guistino, who was arrested on a complaint but not charged for over a year, though he was not detained.[1] Buck had challenged critics to provide the names of January 6 defendants detained without bond for misdemeanor offenses or pending federal charges; journalist [[mel-hawley|Mel Hawley]] supplied such names in response.[1]
