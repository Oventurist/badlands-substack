# Jim Anderson

Jim Anderson is the chief executive officer of Coherent, the American photonics, laser and semiconductor materials company formerly known as II-VI Incorporated.

## Role in the 2026 China business delegation

The May 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, citing a [[white-house|White House]] official, that Anderson was among the executives expected to accompany President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on his trip to [[china|China]] as the administration pushed for new economic agreements with Beijing. He was listed in a delegation of American technology, industrial and financial leadership traveling ahead of the [[2026-trump-xi-summit|Trump–Xi summit]].
