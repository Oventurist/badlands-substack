# Henry H. Arnold

**General Henry H. "Hap" Arnold** was a United States Army Air Forces commander described as one of the apostles of strategic air power, following the lead of General William "[[billy-mitchell|Billy]]" Mitchell.[1]

He was the commander of General [[bernard-a-schriever|Bernard A. Schriever]], who in 1954 established the Western Development Division, the first dedicated space organization within the US armed forces.[1]

In "[[trump-doctrine-peace-through-space|Trump Doctrine: Peace Through Space]]" Arnold serves as the middle link between Mitchell's court-martialed advocacy of air power and the institutional birth of military space programs — an illustration of the article's thesis that new warfighting domains are pioneered by officers whose institutions initially resist them.[1]
