# Gavin O'Blennis

Gavin O'Blennis is identified in reporting as a contracts officer for the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] based in San Diego who was recorded on hidden camera by an undercover journalist making claims about the agency's covert targeting of American political figures.[1] The recording became public in April 2024 and prompted Infowars founder [[alex-jones|Alex Jones]] to announce that he intended to sue the CIA.[1]

## The undercover recording

In the sting footage, O'Blennis told the undercover journalist that "You can kind of put anyone in jail if you know what to do," elaborating: "You set 'em up. You create the situation to where they have no choice but to act on their impulse. And once they act on that impulse, some would call that entrapment."[1] He also remarked that there is "nothing like putting out a fake social media thing to like really get people mad."[1]

Asked who the agency had "messed with," O'Blennis named Alex Jones, implying that the intelligence community had been involved in the litigation against him and that it had encouraged Sandy Hook families to sue.[1] He described the approach as telling potential plaintiffs, "We just say 'there's no federal statute being broken but you have the option for a civil case and it's a pretty good case in our opinion.'"[1] The alleged CIA officer said the intelligence community had "took his money away" in order to "chop his legs off."[1]

## Significance

For the Badlands audience the O'Blennis tape functioned as apparent confirmation of long-standing claims that federal intelligence agencies engineer legal and financial destruction of dissident media figures rather than prosecuting them directly.[1] The item was carried as a "bonus item" in the April 11, 2024 [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] news brief.[1]
