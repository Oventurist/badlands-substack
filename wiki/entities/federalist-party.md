# Federalist Party

The Federalist Party was the early American political faction that championed a strong centralized federal government, and which collapsed in the years following the War of 1812.[1]

## Decline and dissolution

Having lost four consecutive presidential elections — two to Thomas Jefferson and two to James Madison — Federalists came to detest the very government they had fought tooth-and-nail to establish. Their bitterness culminated in the [[hartford-convention|Hartford Convention]] of 1814, where delegates discussed gathering what states they could and seceding from the Union, potentially allying with Britain while that country was at war with the United States.[1]

The convention is cited as one of the reasons the Federalist Party was abolished when the war ended in 1815, and probably one of the reasons that Anti-Federalists so often accused their rivals of being traitors in incendiary rhetoric.[1]

## Modern invocation

Badlands Media contributors have used the Federalists' late history as a template for understanding contemporary establishment behavior, arguing that there are some men who care only about power and "would rather see the world burn than see somebody else wield it."[1]
