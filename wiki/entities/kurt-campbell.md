# Kurt Campbell

**Kurt Campbell** is an American diplomat who was nominated by President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] to serve as Deputy Secretary of State, the number two position at the [[state-department|U.S. State Department]].[1] Campbell took office in February 2024.[1]

His selection resolved an internal administration personnel contest in which Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]] had also been a candidate to succeed the retiring Wendy Sherman. Nuland had been serving as acting deputy secretary during the seven months following Sherman's retirement, and lost the appointment to Campbell.[1] Nuland announced her own retirement from the State Department in March 2024, weeks after Campbell was sworn in.[1]

## China-Russia accusations (September 2024)

On Tuesday, September 10, 2024, speaking to journalists including [[politico|Politico]] after meetings with European Union and NATO counterparts in Brussels, Campbell said that [[china|Beijing]] is giving [[russia|Moscow]] "very substantial" help to beef up its war machine, and that in return Russia is handing over closely guarded military technology on submarines and missiles.[2] The remarks upped the ante on Beijing: Washington had previously focused on China's supply of so-called dual-use technologies, which can be applied for military or civilian purposes, but Campbell was now unambiguously saying that China is aiding the Russian military, which needs technology to sustain production under international sanctions in its war against [[ukraine|Ukraine]].[2] "These are not dual-use capabilities," Campbell said of the latest materials. "These are basically being applied directly to the Russian war machine."[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] read the statement as narrative preparation rather than intelligence disclosure, arguing that the Media Industrial Complex would attempt to cast an emergent Russia-China-Iran military triumvirate as a "New Axis" posing an existential threat to the Globalist West, and that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] would allow this framing for narrative reasons before codifying peace and trade deals already settled through public narrative.[2]
