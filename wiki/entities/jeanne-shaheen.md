# Jeanne Shaheen

Jeanne Shaheen is a Democratic United States Senator from New Hampshire and a senior member of the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, where she plays a significant role in the confirmation of ambassadorial nominees.[1]

## Waltz nomination deal

In July 2025, Shaheen agreed to support former Representative [[mike-waltz|Mike Waltz]]'s nomination to be United States ambassador to the United Nations after securing an agreement with the [[state-department|State Department]] to release $75 million in foreign aid for Haiti and Nigeria. With her support, Waltz was expected to be confirmed by the full Senate.[1]

Explaining her decision, Shaheen said: "In a Situation Room filled with people like Vice President Vance and Under Secretary Colby, who want to retreat from the world, and like Secretary Hegseth, I think we're better off having someone like Mike Waltz present."[1] She added that this was "particularly true when you consider the alternatives to Mr. Waltz as a nominee," and welcomed "the Administration's commitment to distribute $75 million of lifesaving assistance."[1]

Shaheen voted on Thursday, July 24, 2025, to report Waltz out of committee with a favorable recommendation; he advanced on a 12–10 vote.[1]
