# David Ames Wells

David Ames Wells was a nineteenth-century economist who wrote of the technological advancements of the period from 1870 to 1890 and, in the article's reading, explained how deflation gave more power to the people by increasing productivity.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

The article quotes his 1891 work *Recent Economic Changes and Their Effect on Production and Distribution of Wealth and Well-Being of Society*: "The economic changes that have occurred during the last quarter of a century—or during the present generation of living men—have unquestionably been more important and more varied than during any period of the world's history."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)

Wells is cited alongside [[murray-rothbard|Murray Rothbard]] to support the argument that the [[long-depression|Long Depression]] was a period of genuine prosperity for ordinary citizens under the [[gold-standard|gold standard]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/1913)
