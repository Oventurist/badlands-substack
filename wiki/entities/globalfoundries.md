# GlobalFoundries

GlobalFoundries is a contract semiconductor manufacturer named in the [[white-house|White House]]'s August 2022 promotion of the [[chips-and-science-act|CHIPS and Science Act]] and later cited by critics of that law.[1]

## CHIPS Act partnership

The White House fact sheet of August 9, 2022 announced a new partnership between [[qualcomm|Qualcomm]] and GlobalFoundries that included $4.2 billion to manufacture chips through an expansion of GlobalFoundries' upstate New York facility.[1]

## Singapore expansion

A year after the CHIPS and Science Act was signed, GlobalFoundries opened a $4 billion expanded fabrication plant in Singapore.[1] The 23,000-square-metre site was expected to create 1,000 jobs in Singapore, including new roles for equipment technicians, process technicians and engineers.[1] Badlands commentary used the Singapore opening, together with [[micron-technology|Micron]]'s delayed Syracuse project, to argue that American taxpayers had funded corporate expansion abroad rather than domestic capacity.[1]
