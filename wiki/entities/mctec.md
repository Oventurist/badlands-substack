# Maricopa County Tabulation and Election Center (MCTEC)

The Maricopa County Tabulation and Election Center, commonly abbreviated MCTEC, is the central facility where [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], Arizona, processes and tabulates ballots that are not counted at the polling place.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

MCTEC became central to disputes over the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 general election]] because widespread tabulator failures on November 8 forced large numbers of voters to deposit their ballots in a secure receptacle for later central-count processing — the container that became known as [[door-3-ballots|"Door 3"]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero) [[kitty-gillespie|Kitty Gillespie]] recounted that she and her husband were directed to place their ballots in a container labeled "BOX 3" because their polling place had no working tabulator, and that poll workers reassured them the ballots would be transported to MCTEC accompanied by both a Republican and a Democrat — a safeguard she dismissed sarcastically on the grounds that party affiliation guarantees nothing.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)

Assistant Attorney General [[jennifer-wright|Jennifer Wright]] subsequently wrote that the county appeared to have failed to adhere to statutory guidelines in segregating, counting, tabulating, tallying, and transporting the "Door 3" ballots, citing the county's own admission that non-tabulated ballots had been commingled with tabulated ballots at some voting locations, and a sworn complaint that more than 1,700 non-tabulated ballots from a single location had been placed in black duffle bags intended for tabulated ballots.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/an-sos-from-ground-zero)
