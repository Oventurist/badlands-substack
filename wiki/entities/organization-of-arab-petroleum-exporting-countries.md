# Organization of Arab Petroleum Exporting Countries

The **Organization of Arab Petroleum Exporting Countries (OAPEC)** is the Arab oil producers' bloc that imposed the [[1973-oil-embargo|1973 oil embargo]] in response to Western support for [[israel|Israel]] during the [[yom-kippur-war|Yom Kippur War]].[1]

In Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]]'s account, the embargo had profound effects on the [[liberal-world-order|Liberal World Order]] and on ordinary people, producing miles-long gasoline lines and an emerging economic crisis in the United States, until Israel's military success in the war was credited with stabilizing the regional oil situation.[1]
