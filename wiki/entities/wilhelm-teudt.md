# Wilhelm Teudt

Wilhelm Teudt was a Volkish archaeologist and a leading figure in [[heinrich-himmler|Himmler's]] [[ahnenerbe|Ahnenerbe]] who explicitly identified the Frankish king [[charlemagne|Charlemagne]] as the cause of Germany's loss of its pure pagan spirit.[1]

In his 1929 work *Ancient German Sanctuaries*, Teudt wrote: "We have to unlearn our belief that the Roman Church under the Frankish Emperor Charles brought civilization to the barbaric tribes of Germany," replacing the image of primitive, ragged ancestors with one of well-dressed people in elegant linen, wool and hemp, sumptuous furs, and rich jewelry of gold, silver, amber and bronze, singing melodiously to technically accomplished instruments.[1] Ehret comments that Teudt's portrait nearly makes one forget the raping, pillaging and mass human sacrifices prevalent among Wotan-worshipping eighth-century Vikings.[1]
