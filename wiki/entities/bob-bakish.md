# Bob Bakish

Bob Bakish is the chief executive officer of [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]].[1]

## 2023 merger talks with Warner Bros. Discovery

In December 2023, Bakish met with [[warner-bros-discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]] CEO [[david-zaslav|David Zaslav]] at a lunch meeting in New York to discuss a potential merger of the two media companies, a conversation first reported by Axios and confirmed by *Variety*.[1] Zaslav separately spoke with Shari Redstone, whose National Amusements Inc. holds a controlling stake in Paramount Global.[1] Terms could not be learned, both companies enlisted bankers, and the status of the discussions was described as very preliminary; representatives of both firms declined to comment.[1]

As of the end of the third quarter, Paramount Global reported long-term debt of $15.6 billion, considerably less than WBD's $43.5 billion, though Paramount's market capitalization of $10.3 billion was well below WBD's $28.4 billion.[1] A merger would have combined assets across TV, film, sports and streaming, particularly the Paramount+ and Max services, to better compete with [[netflix|Netflix]], Disney+ and Hulu.[1]
