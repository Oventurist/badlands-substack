# Bain Capital

Bain Capital is a private investment firm named in Badlands Media's *The Ivy Web* among the managers of private equity vehicles in which the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] held stakes prior to its 2025 liquidation.[1]

## Harvard endowment liquidation

According to public filings and insider leaks described in the article, HMC offloaded more than $1 billion in illiquid private equity holdings managed by [[blackstone|Blackstone]], [[kkr|KKR]], and Bain Capital, in what Badlands Media argued was distress selling rather than a strategy shift.[1] Business Insider reporting from January 2024 is cited as showing that the liquidation was mirrored by major institutional partners including [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] and [[blackrock|BlackRock]], which the article reads as a coordinated exit signalling a structural shift beneath the Ivy League.[1]
