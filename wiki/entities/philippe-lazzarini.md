# Philippe Lazzarini

Philippe Lazzarini is the Commissioner-General of [[unrwa|UNRWA]], the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees, and the senior official responsible for the agency during the period in which Israel moved to outlaw its operations.[1]

## Response to the October 7 allegations

After Israel accused UNRWA staff of participation in terrorist activity, an investigation by the U.N. Office of Internal Oversight Services closed in August 2024, debunking some of the allegations and finding that others lacked sufficient supporting evidence.[1] The agency nonetheless acknowledged that nine employees may have been involved in the October 7 attack against Israel if the evidence against them were "authenticated and corroborated," and Lazzarini stated that the contracts of those employees would be terminated.[1]

## Context of the Israeli ban

Lazzarini's handling of the matter did not forestall legislative action: in late October 2024 the [[knesset|Knesset]] voted to ban UNRWA's operations, with [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] reiterating that UNRWA personnel "must be held accountable."[1] Netanyahu's office said that during the ninety days before the legislation took effect — and afterward — Israel stood ready to work with international partners so that humanitarian assistance to civilians in [[gaza|Gaza]] could continue in a manner that did not threaten Israeli security.[1]
