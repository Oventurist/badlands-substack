# Amanda Timpson

**Amanda Timpson** is a former employee of the Fulton County, Georgia district attorney's office who alleged that she was punished and ultimately terminated after warning [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]] about the misuse of federal grant money.[1] Her role in the office involved providing nonviolent juvenile offenders with alternatives to the juvenile court system.[1]

## Allegations

Less than a year into Willis's tenure as Fulton County district attorney, in 2021, Timpson met with Willis and told her she had been demoted after attempting to stop a top Willis campaign aide from misusing federal grant funds intended for a youth gang prevention initiative.[1] A recording of the conversation was reviewed by the Washington Free Beacon.[1] According to Timpson, the aide — Michael Cuffee — planned to spend part of a $488,000 federal grant earmarked for the creation of a Center of Youth Empowerment and Gang Prevention on "swag," computers, and travel.[1]

"He wanted to do things with grants that were impossible, and I kept telling him, like, 'We can't do that,'" Timpson told Willis in the November 19, 2021 meeting, recounting that Cuffee had told colleagues they would get MacBooks, swag, and travel out of what she described as a very specific grant.[1] Willis replied, "I respect that is your assessment. And I'm not saying that your assessment is wrong," and later in the conversation apologized to Timpson and said Cuffee had "failed" her administration.[1]

## Termination and whistleblower complaint

Less than two months after the meeting, Willis abruptly terminated Timpson and, by Timpson's account, had her escorted out of the office by seven armed investigators.[1] When Timpson filed a whistleblower complaint the following year alleging wrongful termination, Willis's office issued a statement describing her as a "holdover from the prior administration" who was terminated for her "failure to meet the standards of the new administration."[1] Willis did not deny the substance of Timpson's allegations.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] read the office's statement as an inadvertent admission, arguing that the standards of the new administration appeared to involve bending and breaking the law and that Timpson was fired because she was unwilling to participate in corruption.[1]
