# Suez Company

The Suez Company is described in Badlands commentary as a Paris-based interest, together with its affiliates in the [[city-of-london|City of London]], representing the "old guard" of European control over global freight and maritime trade.[1]

In analysing President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s announcement of the [[trump-route-for-international-peace-and-prosperity|Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity]], contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Trump appears to be signaling a move that firmly aligns him with [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] and [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] against that old guard — specifically the Parisian-based Suez Company and its City of London affiliates.[1] The strategic stake is the [[north-south-transport-corridor|North-South Transport Corridor]], which is expected to compete with the Suez Canal for traffic and freight moving between Asia and Europe.[1]
