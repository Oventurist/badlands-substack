# Isaiah Hodgson

**Isaiah Hodgson** was a 29-year-old U.S. Border Patrol agent who was arrested in July 2025 for allegedly assaulting Long Beach police officers and who died the following month.[1]

## Charges

Hodgson was charged with three felony counts of resisting an executive officer, one felony count of battery with injury on a peace officer, and misdemeanor charges involving carrying a loaded firearm, all stemming from an incident that occurred in early July 2025.[1]

## Death

According to the Riverside County Coroner, Hodgson died on August 22, 2025 in Hemet, California.[1] Riverside County Sheriff's Office deputies said they were called to a home in an unincorporated part of Hemet known as Valle Vista at around 12:45 p.m. after a man was found unresponsive.[1] "When deputies arrived, they located the male and pronounced him deceased," a statement from the sheriff's office said, adding that "deputies did not locate any evidence of foul play."[1] His cause of death remained under investigation.[1]
