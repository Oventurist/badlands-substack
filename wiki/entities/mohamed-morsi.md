# Mohamed Morsi

Mohamed Morsi was the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]] figure elected president of Egypt following the overthrow of [[hosni-mubarak|Hosni Mubarak]] during the [[arab-spring|Arab Spring]].[1] Badlands Media reports that the election which brought him to power was promoted by the same American government-financed NGOs — including the [[international-republican-institute|International Republican Institute]] and the [[national-democratic-institute|National Democratic Institute]] — that had helped foment the uprising against Mubarak.[1]

## Presidency and removal

Once in office, Morsi immediately placed Muslim Brotherhood allies into powerful positions within the government and began implementing [[sharia-law|Sharia law]], including an attempt to change the Egyptian constitution.[1] The Egyptian people rose up in protest by the millions, unwilling to surrender their freedoms, and the Morsi government was exposed as thoroughly corrupt.[1] The military then stepped in and removed his government in an action backed by the public; the Muslim Brotherhood was once again outlawed and its leaders arrested.[1]

Badlands Media argues that had the military not removed the Brotherhood from power, Egypt would have fallen into a bloody civil war.[1]

## Dispute over the June 30 Revolution

Senators [[john-mccain|John McCain]] and [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] — acting, according to Raymond Ibrahim writing at PJMedia, with the "blessing" of President [[barack-obama|Obama]] — insisted on characterizing the June 30 Revolution as a "military coup," a framing Egyptians found deeply offensive and helpful to the Brotherhood's cause.[1] In reality the revolution consisted of perhaps thirty million Egyptians taking to the streets to oust the Brotherhood.[1] [[al-jazeera|Al Jazeera]] broadcast images of the millions of anti-Morsi protesters and identified them as pro-Morsi demonstrators, disinformation quickly adopted by Western media; several Al Jazeera correspondents resigned over the network acting as the Brotherhood's international mouthpiece.[1]
