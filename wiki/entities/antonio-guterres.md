# António Guterres

António Guterres is the Secretary-General of the [[united-nations|United Nations]].[1] In October 2024 he was declared persona non grata and barred from entering [[israel|Israel]] by Foreign Minister [[israel-katz|Israel Katz]], after failing to directly condemn Tehran for the 181 ballistic missiles [[iran|Iran]] launched at Israel, an attack that forced millions of Israelis to seek shelter.[1]

## The 2024 barring

Katz accused Guterres of kowtowing to "[[hamas|Hamas]], [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]], the Houthis, and now Iran — the mothership of global terror," of failing to denounce the October 7 attacks, and of neglecting to brand Hamas a terrorist organization.[1] The move was expected to deepen the already wide rift between Israel and the UN; Germany, which summoned Iran's envoy to condemn the missile attack, called Katz's declaration unhelpful.[1]

Writing on X amid the barrage, Guterres condemned "the broadening of the Middle East conflict with escalation after escalation," adding: "This must stop. We absolutely need a ceasefire."[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] observed that while it was not technically accurate to say Israel had banned the UN from its country, that would become the narrative attached to the headline.[1] The Brief noted that some readers would approve of the move because they see the UN as the vehicle of a globalist one-world-government project, but argued the barring nonetheless reinforced a perception that the Israeli government was descending into madness and becoming an international pariah.[1]

## Attendance at the 2024 Kazan BRICS summit
Guterres attended the October 2024 BRICS summit hosted by Vladimir Putin in Kazan, as he had the previous year's summit.[2] The decision infuriated many in the West, including the Ukrainian foreign ministry, because the International Criminal Court had issued warrants for Putin's arrest in March 2023 over the abduction of children.[2] The United Nations said Guterres would use the occasion to repeat his view that the invasion of Ukraine is in breach of the UN Charter.[2] Badlands read his presence as a major signal that the geopolitical tides had shifted toward a multipolar world order.[2]

## 33rd Arab Summit (2024)

Guterres attended the [[arab-summit-2024-bahrain|33rd Arab Summit]] in Bahrain in May 2024 as a special guest of honor, at a gathering hosted with Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] that issued a joint statement demanding Israel permanently withdraw the [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] from Palestine and calling on the international community to act.[3]

## Congressional funding threat (October 2024)

In late October 2024, more than one hundred bipartisan members of [[congress|Congress]] addressed a letter to Guterres warning that any downgrade in Israel's status or standing at the UN General Assembly would result in a corresponding downgrade of U.S. financial, material and political support for the [[united-nations|United Nations]].[4] The letter was spearheaded by Representatives [[mike-lawler|Mike Lawler]] and [[jared-moskowitz|Jared Moskowitz]], and stated that Congress had taken note of numerous UN actions "aimed to delegitimize Israel's right to self-defense."[4]

Addressing Guterres directly, the signatories wrote: "We have concluded that the UN is not a neutral party but one that has definitively taken sides against Israel. We remind you that the US is the largest donor to the UN," noting that American contributions account for one-third of the body's collective budget and declaring, "We will not accept the UN's ongoing hostility to our ally Israel."[4] Badlands commentary welcomed the prospect of defunding the UN while warning that a Congress beholden to a foreign lobby might eventually go further, and framed the episode as part of the unravelling of the UN-centered order.[4]
