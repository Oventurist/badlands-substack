# Paul Rouleau

Paul Rouleau is the Canadian jurist who led the public commission examining the [[justin-trudeau|Trudeau]] government's invocation of the [[emergencies-act|Emergencies Act]] against the [[freedom-convoy|Freedom Convoy]], concluding that the invocation was justified.[1]

Badlands writer [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] described Rouleau as a Liberal justice who used "a highly politicized commission" to declare that Trudeau was "entirely justified in his draconian invocation," and argued that the finding had convinced many Canadians that citizen litigation against the government was hopeless.[1] Federal Court Justice [[richard-mosley|Richard Mosley]]'s January 2024 ruling that the invocation was unreasonable and unjustified reopened those pathways.[1]
