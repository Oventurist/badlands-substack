# Joseph Aoun

Joseph Aoun is the President of [[lebanon|Lebanon]] as described in the Badlands Media corpus. He appears in the April 17, 2026 Badlands Brief as one of the two heads of government with whom President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] held what Trump called "excellent conversations" preceding the announcement of a ten-day ceasefire between Lebanon and [[israel|Israel]].

In Trump's Truth Social statement, Aoun is referred to as "the Highly Respected President Joseph Aoun, of Lebanon," and is credited alongside Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] with agreeing that a formal ten-day ceasefire beginning at 5 p.m. EST Tuesday was necessary to achieve peace between the two countries. The ceasefire followed a meeting in Washington, D.C. earlier that week at which Lebanese and Israeli officials met for the first time in 34 years, hosted alongside Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]].

Trump indicated that Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]], Rubio, and Chairman of the [[joint-chiefs-of-staff|Joint Chiefs of Staff]] [[dan-caine|Dan Caine]] would work with both Lebanon and Israel toward a lasting peace agreement. The corpus does not describe Aoun's response to Netanyahu's subsequent recorded demand that [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] disarm and surrender.
