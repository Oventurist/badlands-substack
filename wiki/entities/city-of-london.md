# City of London

The City of London — the one square mile of London, England — is identified by Erik Carlson as the true apex of the global organized-crime syndicate and the seat of the [[deep-state-cabal|Cabal]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Claimed position

Peeling back the layers of his own research, Carlson concluded that the City of London sits atop the organized crime syndicate and controls the United States, Israel, Iran and China alike, sitting at the top of the Deep State.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) Above the [[cia|CIA]] and [[mossad|Mossad]], he writes, is British [[mi6|MI6]]; above Tel Aviv and Washington, DC, is the City of London.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The criminals who run the US and Israel are real, but are merely middle management taking orders from their puppet masters.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Russia and the Bolshevik Revolution

Carlson explains Russia's perennial status as enemy by its centuries of standing up to the City of London.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) During the American Revolutionary War, Russia rebuffed England's demand to ally against the Americans; during the American Civil War, when England tried to help the South, Russian ships blocked American ports and kept Britain out.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) For this reason, he argues, the City of London unleashed communism on Russia to bring the rebellious nation under control.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) In the Second World War, 450,000 English lives were lost against 24 million Soviet — fifty times more — because Russian lives were worthless to the Cabal, which sacrificed millions to achieve its goal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) The [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik Revolution]] gave the City of London control of this centuries-old thorn in its side until the early 1990s.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Pivot to China

When the Soviet Union fell in the early 1990s, Japan was then regarded as America's largest economic competitor; instead Japan entered a thirty-year economic funk and China became an economic juggernaut. Carlson reads this as the City of London pivoting from controlling the Soviet Union to controlling [[communist-china|Communist China]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## Trump's campaign against it

Since early in his first presidency, Carlson writes, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] has fought a silent war against the City of London: replacing the [[libor|LIBOR]] system with [[sofr|SOFR]], transferring control of the American banking system from the City of London to the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) In his second term Trump is onshoring manufacturing out of Communist China and back to America.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths) Tariffs, in this reading, are not merely about fair trade but about removing the globalist power stemming from the City of London.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths)

## The Great Game and Canada

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] treats Canada as a chess piece within a much larger London-directed Great Game, whose intention is to weaken the Russian–Chinese alliance and to exploit resource-rich Canada's role within that game.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) In his framing the depression-era fascist formula could only succeed in the 21st century as long as America was brought firmly under the control of the City of London and their Wall Street lackeys.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) The oligarchs exiled by [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] made their way to London — the upmarket districts nicknamed "Moscow on Thames" — where many became disposable playthings of the British Empire.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems) Ehret closes by asking whether [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]] will be able to salvage the decaying British Empire and the dream of [[cecil-rhodes|Cecil Rhodes]] to recapture the USA.[[2]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-re-alignment-of-world-systems)
