# City of London

The City of London is the one-square-mile financial district at the historic core of London, England. In the Badlands Media essay [[a-journey-of-ever-changing-truths|A Journey of Ever-changing Truths]], [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] identifies it as the seat of the [[cabal|Cabal]] and the true summit of global organized crime and political power — the terminal conclusion of the essay's layered "peeling the onion" method.

## Claimed position in the hierarchy

According to the source, the City of London "controls the US, they control Israel, they control Iran and China as well" and "sit at the top of the Deep State." The article arranges intelligence services in a corresponding order: above the [[cia|CIA]] and [[mossad|Mossad]] stands British [[mi6|MI6]], and above Tel Aviv and Washington stands the City of London. Criminals running the United States and Israel are, in this framing, "merely middle management who take orders from their puppet masters."

The Cabal centered there is described as comprising Jews and non-Jews alike — including "Jews who masquerade as Jews, but who are really Satanists," fake Christians, fake Muslims, and atheists — with Satan worship rather than ethnicity or nationality as the binding element. The article explicitly frames Jews and Israel as scapegoats deployed to keep attention away from this higher layer, applying the same logic by which it says the Italian Mafia was propped up to conceal the Jewish Mafia.

## Russia and the historical case

The essay argues that Russia has been cast as the perpetual enemy of the West precisely because it "stood up to the City of London for hundreds of years." It cites two episodes: Russia's refusal of England's demand for an alliance against the Americans during the American Revolutionary War, and the blocking of American ports by Russian ships during the American Civil War, which the source says kept Britain from intervening on behalf of the South. Communism, the article claims, was "unleashed" on Russia by the City of London to subdue it, with the Bolshevik Revolution delivering control that lasted until the early 1990s. The contrast between 450,000 English dead and 24 million Soviet dead in the Second World War is offered as evidence that Russian lives were treated as expendable by the Cabal.

## Pivot to China and the conflict with Trump

The source reads the fall of the Soviet Union in the early 1990s together with Japan's ensuing thirty-year economic stagnation and China's simultaneous rise as evidence that the City of London "pivoted from controlling the Soviet Union to Communist China."

The article's account of current events casts the real conflict of the present era as one between President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and the City of London — and between Trump and Europe — rather than between the United States and Russia, China, or Iran. It cites the replacement of LIBOR with SOFR, which it says moved control of the American banking system from the City of London to the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], and the onshoring of manufacturing out of China in Trump's second term. Tariffs, in this reading, "aren't just about creating fair trade; they're about removing the Globalist power that stems from the City of London."
