# City of London

The City of London is the historic one-square-mile financial district of London, England. In [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s April 2025 essay it is identified as the seat of the [[deep-state-cabal|Deep State Cabal]] and the apex of the global organized-crime hierarchy.[1]

## Alleged position in the hierarchy

Carlson writes that after concluding that neither the [[italian-mafia|Italian Mafia]], the [[jewish-mafia|Jewish Mafia]], nor Israel occupied the top of the pyramid, his research led him to the City of London as the entity that "sits atop the organized crime syndicate."[1] In his account the City controls the United States, Israel, Iran, and China, and stands above the [[cia|CIA]] and [[mossad|Mossad]] by way of the British [[mi6|MI6]].[1] He describes Washington and Tel Aviv as subordinate capitals whose criminal leadership is merely middle management taking orders from their puppet masters.[1]

## Alleged conflict with Russia

Carlson argues that Russia has been cast perpetually as the enemy because it resisted the City of London for centuries.[1] He cites Russia's refusal of England's demand for an alliance against the Americans during the Revolutionary War, and Russian ships blockading American ports during the Civil War to deter British intervention on behalf of the South.[1] He contends the City therefore "unleashed communism on Russia" to subdue it, and that during the Second World War 450,000 English lives were lost against 24 million Soviet lives — fifty times more — because Russian lives were worthless to the Cabal.[1] Control established through the [[bolshevik-revolution|Bolshevik Revolution]] persisted, in his telling, until the early 1990s, after which the City pivoted to Communist China as Japan sank into a thirty-year economic funk.[1]

## Alleged conflict with Trump

Carlson frames [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] as engaged in a silent war against the City of London since his first presidency.[1] He cites the replacement of the [[libor-to-sofr-transition|LIBOR system with SOFR]] as taking control of the American banking system away from the City and giving it to the [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], and describes second-term onshoring of manufacturing out of China as a further removal of the City's leverage.[1] Tariffs, in this reading, are not merely about fair trade but about dismantling globalist power emanating from the City.[1] Carlson likens Israel, Iran, and China to the middle manager in the film Office Space — the recognizable face who is not making the decisions — and [[doge|DOGE]] to the consultants brought in to expose redundancy.[1]
