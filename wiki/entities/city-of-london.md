## Origins and 337 years of control

Carlson dates the Cabal's residence in the City of London to the late 17th century, meaning roughly 300 to 350 years, and describes the City as a one-square-mile district of banks operating independently from the rest of London and from England, from which decisions that shape and control the world are made.[3] Before that, he argues, the lineage runs through the [[venetian-bankers|Venetian Bankers]] of the 12th century, who devised a [[perpetual-debt-system|perpetual debt system]] before relocating to Holland and founding the [[dutch-east-india-company|Dutch East India Company]] in 1602.[3]

In 1688, according to the article, the Dutch East India Company created the City of London by striking a deal with the British royal family to pay off its debt in exchange for the Crown abdicating its power to the newly formed City; King James II ceded power to his daughter Mary II, who ruled jointly with William of Orange of the [[house-of-orange|House of Orange]], and the British parliamentary system was born while real authority passed to the City.[3] Carlson writes that for the 337 years since, every war, recession, depression and political or societal shift — the French Revolution, both World Wars, the Great Depression, the Russian Revolution, communism, the US [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]], and on to DEI and transgenderism — was created and controlled by the City of London banks and their think tanks.[3]
