# Ben Sabti

Ben Sabti is a defense researcher affiliated with an Israeli defense institute.[1]

## June 2026 remarks

Sabti drew attention in June 2026 for suggesting that the United States needed to experience another Pearl Harbor or another September 11 attack in order to "remember" who its enemies are.[1] Badlands Media reproduced an image of the statement and condemned it as "totally normal behavior, calling for the death of innocent Americans so that more people will like [[israel|Israel]]," adding that the remark might amount to a form of confession.[1]

The comment was cited by Badlands alongside statements by [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and [[mark-levin|Mark Levin]] as part of what the outlet described as an escalating campaign by Zionist commentators against the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration and against Americans who question the U.S.–Israel relationship.[1]
