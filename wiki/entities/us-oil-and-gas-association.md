# U.S. Oil & Gas Association

The U.S. Oil & Gas Association (OGA) is an American industry trade association representing oil and gas producers, whose public commentary in January 2026 framed the terms on which U.S. producers would return to [[venezuela|Venezuela]].[1]

## Conditions for re-entry

The association stated that there were two "must haves" for producers to go back into Venezuela: assurance that their crews would not be kidnapped, and assurance that investment and capital expenditures would be safe from seizure by a foreign government.[1] It argued that the overwhelming show of force by the U.S. military solved the first problem, adding a pointed warning to would-be kidnappers of its executives.[1] The association also praised the approach as "the Art of the Deal," saying there was "no one better than the guy who wrote the book."[1]

## Commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] characterized the association's statement as relying on both maximized power projection and maximized fear to set conditions for exerting power, and noted a cynical reading in which the oil re-entry angle was the real purpose of the entire Venezuela saga.[1] She also observed that the association's social media account had been trolling California.[1] While Venezuelans nominally had agency to make a deal, she argued that under the narrative of energy weapons and super soldiers the arrangement was arguably coercion — and that the deals were reportedly already done.[1]
