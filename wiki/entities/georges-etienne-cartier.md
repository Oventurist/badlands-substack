# Georges-Etienne Cartier

Sir Georges-Etienne Cartier was a Father of Canadian Confederation who led the operation by which the [[hudsons-bay-company|Hudson's Bay]] territories, known as [[ruperts-land|Rupert's Land]], were sold to Ottawa on 18 July 1868.[1]

Cartier stated that "in this country, we must have a distinct form of government in which the monarchical spirit will be found," and openly attacked the "democratic system which prevails in the United States."[1] Matthew Ehret treats this monarchical spirit as the founding principle of the new northern confederacy formalized by the [[british-north-america-act|British North America Act]] — a confederacy prepared at the 1864 [[charlottetown-conference|Charlottetown conference]] even as the slave-based Southern confederacy was being shut down.[1] The same disposition was reflected in fellow founder [[john-a-macdonald|Sir John A. Macdonald]].[1]
