# Clare Locke LLP

Clare Locke LLP is an American law firm specializing in defamation litigation against media organizations.[1]

The firm is best known for representing [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]] in its defamation suit against Fox News, which produced a $787.5 million settlement and coincided with the ouster of Tucker Carlson from the network.[1]

In December 2023 the firm was retained by Shannon and Raul Armenta, the parents of nine-year-old Kansas City Chiefs fan [[holden-armenta|Holden Armenta]], who had been falsely accused of wearing blackface by [[deadspin|Deadspin]] writer [[carron-phillips|Carron Phillips]].[1] Clare Locke demanded a retraction and threatened legal action against Deadspin, publishers G/O Media and Great Hill Partners, and Phillips.[1]
