# Malcolm X

Malcolm X was an American civil rights leader whose 1965 assassination is presented in Badlands Media commentary as the consequence of what he had discovered about intelligence-agency manipulation of 1960s racial politics.[1]

## Alleged reason for his murder

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that Malcolm X was murdered because he learned that both the [[black-panther-party|Black Panthers]] and the [[ku-klux-klan|Ku Klux Klan]] had been infiltrated by the [[fbi|FBI]], and that the two ostensibly opposed organizations were being funded by the same people.[1] Specifically, Carlson said Malcolm X became aware that figures such as Texas oil tycoon [[h-l-hunt|H.L. Hunt]] were financing both sides in order to manufacture chaos, unrest and hate during the already turbulent decade of the 1960s.[1]

The episode is offered as an early template for the argument running through the article: that movements presented as advancing racial justice can be funded by elites whose actual objective is division, and that the [[fbi|FBI]] and [[cia|CIA]] have "their fingerprints all over the Civil Rights movement."[1]
