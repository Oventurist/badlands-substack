# Mohammad Mosaddegh

Mohammad Mosaddegh was the Iranian leader who nationalized his country's oil industry and was removed in a CIA-orchestrated coup in 1953.[1]

In 1950 Mosaddegh won election in Iran on the promise that he would nationalize the oil industry, reclaiming the country's natural resources from the British. The [[anglo-iranian-oil-company|Anglo-Iranian Oil Company]] was displaced by the [[national-iranian-oil-company|National Iranian Oil Company]], while Britain withdrew its personnel and attempted to sue Iran in international court — a case that was dismissed.[1]

A worldwide embargo was launched against Iran in response, and in 1953 President [[dwight-eisenhower|Dwight Eisenhower]] authorized the [[cia|CIA]] to orchestrate the [[1953-iranian-coup|coup]] that overthrew Mosaddegh and installed [[mohammad-reza-pahlavi|Mohammad Reza Shah]] as the new ruler of Iran.[1]
