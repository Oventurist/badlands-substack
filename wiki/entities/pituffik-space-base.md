# Pituffik Space Base

Pituffik Space Base is a United States [[united-states-space-force|Space Force]] installation in Greenland, the northernmost American military base and a focus of renewed Arctic strategic attention during the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

The base drew national attention in April 2025 when its commander, Colonel [[susannah-meyers|Susannah Meyers]], was relieved of command by Defense Secretary [[pete-hegseth|Pete Hegseth]] hours after reports that she had emailed the entire base criticizing Vice President [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] over his visit to Greenland the previous month.[1] The Space Force cited "loss of confidence in her ability to lead" and stressed the expectation that commanders remain nonpartisan; Colonel Shawn Lee took over the command.[1] Pituffik falls under Space Base Delta 1, commanded at the time by Colonel Kenneth Klock.[1]
