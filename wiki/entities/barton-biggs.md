# Barton Biggs

**Barton Biggs** was a Wall Street investment strategist referenced in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" as the mentor of [[amy-falls|Amy Falls]], the former chief investment officer of [[rockefeller-university|Rockefeller University]] associated with the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]].[1]

The article calls him a "Wall Street titan" and cites the mentorship, together with Falls's [[morgan-stanley|Morgan Stanley]] connection, as part of the lineage binding Harvard endowment personnel to legacy Wall Street and Rockefeller-affiliated institutions within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
