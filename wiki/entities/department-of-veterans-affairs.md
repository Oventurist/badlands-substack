# Department of Veterans Affairs

The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is the U.S. federal department responsible for services and benefits to military veterans.[1]

## 2023 appropriations treatment

In the [[september-2023-government-shutdown-standoff|September 2023 government shutdown standoff]], the House Republican stopgap proposal would have kept the VA — along with the Department of Defense — funded at current levels while cutting all other discretionary spending by 8 percent through October 31.[1] The carve-out placed the department among the small set of agencies insulated from the across-the-board reductions sought by House conservatives.[1]

## 2025 House vote on VA cannabis recommendations

On June 25, 2025 the U.S. House of Representatives approved amendments to the Military Construction, Veterans Affairs and Related Agencies (MilConVA) appropriations bill that would authorize VA doctors to issue medical marijuana recommendations to veterans and support psychedelics research and access.[2] One accepted proposal, from Reps. Brian Mast (R-FL) and Dave Joyce (R-OH), co-chairs of the Congressional Cannabis Caucus, would increase veterans' access to state medical marijuana programs and eliminate the standing VA directive barring department doctors from issuing cannabis recommendations.[2] The House Rules Committee had made the cannabis and psychedelics amendments in order for floor consideration two days earlier.[2] Badlands host Jon Herold noted he had raised the subject directly with the Secretary of Veterans Affairs in a recent interview.[2]
