# Muon Space

Muon Space is a commercial satellite operator specializing in Earth-observation and weather-sensing spacecraft. In October 2025 the [[united-states-space-force|United States Space Force]] announced a contract with the company for three prototype satellites intended to supply data such as cloud forecasting in support of terrestrial and maritime military operations.[1]

## Space Force weather prototype contract

The award was structured as a $44.6 million Phase III Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) contract, following on from an earlier December 2024 contract under which Muon Space developed an electro-optical/infrared sensor designed to "provide comprehensive cloud characterization and theater weather imagery."[1] Space Systems Command, the Space Force acquisition organization that issued the release, said the contract "leverages the company's existing commercial mission and builds upon the successful outcomes of its previous FireSat prototype launch."[1]

## FireSat constellation

The military award followed the company's March 2025 launch of the first prototype for a planned 50-satellite constellation known as FireSat, primarily aimed at monitoring wildfires.[1] FireSat is being developed in partnership with the Earth Fire Alliance, a nonprofit coalition backed by Google Research, the Environmental Defense Fund and other philanthropic organizations.[1]

## Significance

The contract is an example of the Space Force's broader practice of buying weather and environmental sensing capability from commercial operators rather than fielding purpose-built government constellations, using SBIR authorities to move a demonstrated commercial sensor into a military prototype program.[1]
