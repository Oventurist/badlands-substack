# Matthew Miller

Matthew Miller served as spokesperson for the [[state-department|US State Department]] during the administration of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]], acting as the principal public voice for American diplomatic positions during the Israel–Hamas war.[1]

## Statements on Palestinian Authority reform

In a statement issued in the spring of 2024, Miller said the United States looked forward to working with the new group of Palestinian ministers appointed under Prime Minister [[mohammad-mustafa|Mohammad Mustafa]] "to deliver on credible reforms," signalling US acceptance of the revised Cabinet lineup.[1] "A revitalized PA is essential to delivering results for the Palestinian people in both the West Bank and Gaza and establishing the conditions for stability in the broader region," Miller said.[1]

The remark tracked the broader Biden administration position that a reformed [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]] — which administers parts of the Israeli-occupied West Bank under President [[mahmoud-abbas|Mahmoud Abbas]] — should also administer the Gaza Strip once the war ended, notwithstanding the authority's limited popular legitimacy among Palestinians.[1]
