# Joseph DiGenova

Joseph DiGenova is an American attorney and former federal prosecutor who represented President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in legal challenges to the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]].

## Appointment to the Brennan investigation

According to reporting summarized in the April 21, 2026 Badlands Brief, the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] selected DiGenova to take over the criminal investigation into former [[cia|CIA]] director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]]. The appointment followed the removal of prosecutor Maria Medetis Long and placed DiGenova in a senior advisory role to acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]]. The case is being run out of the Southern District of Florida, the district of U.S. District Judge [[aileen-cannon|Aileen Cannon]]; prosecutor Chris DeLorenz, a former law clerk to Judge Cannon during the classified documents probe, also joined the case.

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing in the same edition about [[actblue|ActBlue]] employees invoking the Fifth Amendment 146 times and about what she calls the "Grand RICO" encompassing the 2020 election, concludes her analysis with the remark that "Joe DiGenova should be busy."


## April 2026: lead prosecutor on Russia-collusion matters

The Brief of April 20, 2026, citing [[just-the-news|Just the News]], reports that former U.S. Attorney diGenova was scheduled to begin work the following Monday as a lead prosecutor on Russia-collusion matters — part of the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s Fort Pierce, Florida-based investigation into the origins of [[russiagate|Russiagate]].
