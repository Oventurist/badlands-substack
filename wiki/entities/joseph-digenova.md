# Joseph DiGenova

Joseph DiGenova is an American attorney and former federal prosecutor who represented President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in legal challenges to the 2020 presidential election.[1] In April 2026 the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] selected DiGenova to take over the criminal investigation into former [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]].[1]

## Appointment to the Brennan investigation

DiGenova's appointment followed the removal of prosecutor Maria Medetis Long from the case, and placed him in a senior advisory role to acting Attorney General [[todd-blanche|Todd Blanche]].[1] He oversees the matter out of the Southern District of Florida, the district of U.S. District Judge Aileen Cannon.[1] Prosecutor Chris DeLorenz, a former law clerk to Judge Cannon during the classified documents probe, has also joined the case.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], reviewing the parallel [[actblue|ActBlue]] campaign-finance revelations and what she describes as a "Grand RICO" encompassing the 2020 election, concluded that "Joe DiGenova should be busy."[1]

## April 2026 appointment as Russia-collusion prosecutor

Just the News reported in April 2026 that diGenova, a former U.S. Attorney, was scheduled to begin work the following Monday as a lead prosecutor on Russia-collusion matters.[2] The report appeared alongside coverage of the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s unfulfilled request for Senate committee records concerning former [[cia|CIA]] Director [[john-brennan|John Brennan]].[2]
