# Bruce Lipton

Bruce Lipton is a developmental biologist and author known for popular writing on epigenetics and the influence of belief on cellular biology. He is quoted in "Hijacked Consciousness - Field Builders," the sixth article in the [[hijacked-consciousness-series|Hijacked Consciousness]] series by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]].[1]

The article cites him as "Dr. Bruce Lipton" for the line, "The moment you change your perception is the moment you rewrite the chemistry of your body," placing the quotation as a pull-out between its section on the [[resonant-human|resonant human]] and its section on [[microfields|microfields]].[1] The quotation is used to support the article's broader argument that coherence is a bodily state rather than a mental posture, and it sits alongside the piece's appeals to quantum biology, epigenetics, and field theory as sciences said to be catching up with what "mystics and mothers have always known."[1]

## Cited in "The Bridge" (March 2025)

The same quotation — that the moment we change our perception we also rewrite the chemistry of our body — opens the "Expanding Consciousness & Reclaiming Inner Freedom" section of the earlier March 1, 2025 essay "The Bridge: Awakening, Healing, and Ascension," where Lipton is credited as the author of *The Biology of Belief*.[2] There the line introduces the argument that once the mind is rewired and the nervous system healed, the body becomes a clear channel for consciousness, and that higher states of awareness emerge naturally rather than being learned.[2]
