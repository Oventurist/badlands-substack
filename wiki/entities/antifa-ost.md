# Antifa Ost

Antifa Ost, also known as Antifa East or Hammerbande, is a German-based militant antifascist outfit designated by the United States as a terrorist organization in November 2025.[1]

## Activity

Between 2018 and 2023 the group carried out numerous attacks in Germany, and in February 2023 conducted an attack in Budapest.[1] Hungary declared Antifa Ost a terrorist organization on September 26, 2025.[1]

## United States designation

On November 13, 2025 the [[state-department|Department of State]] announced that Antifa Ost would be designated a Specially Designated Global Terrorist (SDGT) and, effective November 20, 2025, added to the list of [[foreign-terrorist-organization|Foreign Terrorist Organizations]], alongside the Informal Anarchist Federation/International Revolutionary Front (FAI/FRI), Armed Proletarian Justice, and Revolutionary Class Self-Defense.[1] With the designation, all property and property interests of the group in the United States or under U.S. persons' control are blocked, U.S. persons are generally prohibited from doing business with it, and knowingly providing material support is a crime carrying potential exposure to secondary sanctions.[1] The action formed part of the broader [[antifa-terrorist-designation|antifa terrorist designation]] campaign pursued by the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]
