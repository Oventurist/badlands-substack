# US Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit

The United States Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit is the federal appellate court based in St. Louis, Missouri, with jurisdiction over federal district courts in Arkansas, Iowa, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, and South Dakota.

## 2026 mandatory detention ruling

The March 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-143|Badlands Brief]], citing Politico, reports that the Eighth Circuit ruled 2-1 in favor of the [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's interpretation of federal immigration law, holding that the statute not only permits but mandates detention in the majority of deportation cases. The decision reversed lower court rulings in Minnesota that had granted release petitions. The brief describes the ruling as the administration's second appellate victory on the question, following a similar 2-1 decision from the [[us-court-of-appeals-for-the-fifth-circuit|US Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit]] the previous month.
