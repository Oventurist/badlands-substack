# Council of Europe

The Council of Europe is a pan-European intergovernmental body whose human rights machinery includes an office of Commissioner for Human Rights.[1]

## Intervention on Slovakia's two-sexes amendment

After [[slovakia|Slovakia]]'s National Council passed a constitutional amendment in September 2025 recognising only two sexes and restricting adoption to married couples, the Council of Europe's Commissioner for Human Rights, Michael O'Flaherty, publicly criticised the measure.[1] He argued that the amendment denies "the realities of trans and intersex people" and could place Slovakia in breach of its international obligations, expressing concern over the potential impact on human rights guarantees such as access to legal gender recognition.[1] His criticism paralleled that of [[amnesty-international|Amnesty International]], which likened the Slovak government's direction to that of Hungary.[1]
