# Joe Tacopina

Joe Tacopina is a New York trial attorney described as one of the country's top trial lawyers, hired by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in January 2023 to pursue a defamation claim against former Manhattan prosecutor [[mark-pomerantz|Mark Pomerantz]].[1]

In his first action as Trump's new counsel, Tacopina sent a letter on Monday, January 23, 2023, to Pomerantz, asserting that Pomerantz had falsely stated Trump was "guilty of numerous felony violations" and that it was a "grave failure of justice not to hold [Trump] accountable by way of criminal prosecution."[1] The letter threatened that if Pomerantz published his book and continued making defamatory statements, Tacopina's office would "aggressively pursue all legal remedies against you and your book publisher, Simon & Schuster."[1] The story was first reported by [[tmz|TMZ]].[1]

## Defense of Trump in the Hush-Money Matter (March 2023)

As [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s attorney in March 2023, Tacopina publicly defended the 2016 payment at the center of Manhattan District Attorney [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]]'s expected indictment, arguing that Trump's payment to [[michael-cohen|Michael Cohen]] was a legitimate legal fee and that Trump never directly paid [[stormy-daniels|Stormy Daniels]] any money.[2] "The payments were made to a lawyer, not to Stormy Daniels," Tacopina said on MSNBC. "The payments were made to Donald Trump's lawyer, which would be considered legal fees. Michael Cohen ... was his lawyer at the time and advised him that this was the proper way to do this to protect himself and his family from embarrassment. It's as simple as that."[2] The MSNBC host responded that the money Trump paid Cohen went to Daniels and was therefore not a legal fee.[2]
