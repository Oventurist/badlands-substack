# Betty Rosa

Betty Rosa is the New York State Education Commissioner, the official responsible for overseeing public education policy and compliance across the state's school districts.

Rosa appears in the July 30, 2026 [[badlands-brief-414|Badlands Brief]] in connection with the [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]]'s suspended plan to place a [[realbotix|Realbotix]] humanoid AI robot in classrooms. The Brief reported that Rosa identified a discrepancy in the district's account: while the district maintained that the robot would not deliver instruction, a board presentation had described the unit as a "tutoring platform." Her office's concerns, alongside those of parents and teachers over student data privacy, contributed to the pilot being placed on hold pending enhanced data-privacy agreements between the district and the state.
