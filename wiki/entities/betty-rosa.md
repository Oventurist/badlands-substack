# Betty Rosa

Betty Rosa is the New York State Education Commissioner.[1]

## Role in the Salamanca AI robot controversy

In July 2026 Rosa intervened in the controversy over the [[salamanca-city-central-school-district|Salamanca City Central School District]]'s plan to place an AI-powered humanoid robot from [[realbotix|Realbotix]] in classrooms. She noted a discrepancy in the district's account: officials said the robot would not deliver instruction, but a board presentation described it as a "tutoring platform."[1]

The pilot was subsequently paused, and remains on hold while the district works out enhanced data-privacy agreements with the state.[1]
