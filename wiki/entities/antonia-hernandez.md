# Antonia Hernandez

Antonia Hernandez is an American civic leader who served as a co-chair of the [[commission-on-presidential-debates|Commission on Presidential Debates]], sharing leadership of the organization with [[frank-fahrenkopf|Frank Fahrenkopf Jr.]][1]

## 2024 debate dispute

Hernandez was one of the two named recipients of an April 2024 letter from [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] campaign co-managers [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]] and [[chris-lacivita|Chris LaCivita]], which urged the commission to schedule the 2024 presidential debates against [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] "much earlier" than planned and to increase their number.[1] The letter aligned the campaign with a separate push by television networks for debates to take place in 2024 and objected to the commission's announced timetable of three presidential debates and one vice-presidential debate later in the year.[1]
