# Confucius Institutes

**Confucius Institutes** are Chinese government–funded language and culture centers hosted at foreign universities, financed through Hanban, described in the Badlands Media essay *Throne of the Ivy Grid* as the propaganda and influence wing of China's Ministry of Education.[1]

The essay states that more than 100 Confucius Institutes were embedded in U.S. universities "not to teach Mandarin, but to police it."[1] In its account, the institutes were strategically placed across campuses to monitor speech, suppress dissent, and relay ideological metrics to Chinese consulates, functioning as "soft-intelligence nodes, cloaked in calligraphy and dragon dances" rather than as cultural centers.[1]

The institutes appear in the essay's broader argument that the [[chinese-communist-party|Chinese Communist Party]] pursues a fifth-column strategy of direct infiltration into American think tanks, state governments, and academic policymaking channels — a strategy the essay also illustrates through the Fang Fang case involving Congressman [[eric-swalwell|Eric Swalwell]], the indictment of [[linda-sun|Linda Sun]], and the operation of unregistered CCP police outposts in U.S. cities.[1] The essay characterizes the resulting arrangement as "not immigration… not diplomacy… not student exchange," but occupation sustained by trade agreements, academic grants, and institutions dependent on tuition and donor income.[1]
