# Simon Flexner

**Simon Flexner** was a member of the board of directors of the [[rockefeller-institute|Rockefeller Institute]] and the man who proposed that his brother [[abraham-flexner|Abraham Flexner]] be hired to survey North American medical schools.[1]

The survey had been begun by the [[american-medical-association|American Medical Association]]'s Council on Medical Education but stalled in 1910 when the AMA ran out of money; the Rockefeller and Carnegie foundations created an education fund to finish it. Simon Flexner's recommendation placed the project in the hands of a brother with no medical background, an arrangement Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] presents as evidence that the resulting [[flexner-report|Flexner Report]] served Rockefeller and Carnegie objectives rather than independent scholarship.[1]
