# United States Capitol Police

The United States Capitol Police (USCP) is the federal law enforcement agency responsible for protecting the Capitol complex, members of [[congress|Congress]], their staff and visitors. It has jurisdiction over the Capitol grounds and controls access to major events held there, including the annual [[state-of-the-union|State of the Union]] address.

## Arrest of an Ilhan Omar guest, 2026

The [[badlands-brief-40d|February 27, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports that Capitol Police arrested a guest invited by Representative [[ilhan-omar|Ilhan Omar]] to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s 2026 State of the Union address, taking the individual into custody following the event. Omar publicly stated that the detention was tied to immigration enforcement concerns and complained that her guest had been mistreated. The incident preceded Trump's [[truth-social|Truth Social]] post attacking Omar and Representative [[rashida-tlaib|Rashida Tlaib]] over their conduct during the address, and Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] referenced it in her commentary on Omar's position amid the [[minnesota-somali-fraud|Minnesota Somali fraud scandal]].
