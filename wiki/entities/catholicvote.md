# CatholicVote

CatholicVote is a right-wing Catholic advocacy group led by [[brian-burch|Brian Burch]].[1]

The organization rallied Catholic support for [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in the 2024 presidential election, and its leader was subsequently chosen by Trump as his nominee for ambassador to the Vatican — a pick reported as contrasting with [[pope-francis|Pope Francis]]'s appointment of Trump critic [[robert-mcelroy|Robert McElroy]] as archbishop of Washington, D.C.[1]
