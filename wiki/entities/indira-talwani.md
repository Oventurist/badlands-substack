# Indira Talwani

Talwani is the federal district judge whose injunction against President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s executive order on election administration was left intact by a federal appeals court in July 2026; the Badlands Brief refers to her only by surname.[1]

## Election order injunction

Her order blocked key portions of the executive order from being enforced in 23 Democrat-led states while litigation continued.[1] The order had directed the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] to compile lists of confirmed citizens eligible to vote, instructed the [[united-states-postal-service|US Postal Service]] to set new standards for mail-in ballots, and directed the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] to prioritize investigations of officials issuing federal ballots to ineligible people.[1] The appeals court denied the DOJ's request to lift the injunction; [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted the decision "does not settle the underlying dispute over presidential power but keeps Talwani's injunction intact while the case winds through the courts, a delay that could push final resolution dangerously close to the midterms."[1] Badlands commentary called the outcome the work of a "very silly judge," arguing that if neither the states nor the President may direct the USPS, no actor would hold its decision rights.[1]
