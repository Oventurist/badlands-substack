# Roman Polanski

Roman Polanski is a film director who, according to Matthew Ehret, worked closely with [[anton-lavey|Anton LaVey]], whose [[church-of-satan|Church of Satan]] overlapped the Manson murders on numerous levels.[1]

During the filming of *Rosemary's Baby*, Polanski hired LaVey to stage-manage the scenes featuring a satanic cult leader in New York who rapes a young woman assigned to host the antichrist.[1] When Polanski's pregnant wife [[sharon-tate|Sharon Tate]] was killed by followers of [[charles-manson|Charles Manson]], it was discovered that Susan Atkins — the woman who carried out the stabbing of Tate — had been employed by LaVey as a topless vampire in his Witches' Sabbath ceremony.[1] Former Church of Satan follower Jay Sebring was among the five victims that evening.[1]
