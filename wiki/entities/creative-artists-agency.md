# Creative Artists Agency

Creative Artists Agency (CAA) is an American entertainment and talent company that represents figures in film, television, publishing and the paid speaking circuit. In early 2025 it drew political attention when it signed both of the outgoing Democratic administration's principals in quick succession, a sequence that became a recurring motif in Badlands Media commentary about American politics as scripted entertainment.[1]

## Representation of Biden and Harris

CAA announced a partnership with former President Joe Biden on February 3, 2025.[1] Later that month, former Vice President [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] reportedly signed with the agency once more, following Biden's lead.[1] Variety reported that Harris's agreement with CAA covered "all areas," with an emphasis on speaking engagements and publishing.[1] Harris had been a CAA client before becoming vice president, and Michelle and Barack Obama also work with the company.[1]

What Harris had in the works with CAA was not specified publicly, though NBC News had reported in January 2025 that she was working on a book that could lay the groundwork for another run at the presidency in 2028.[1] The 60-year-old California native, who campaigned as the first woman of color to capture a major political party's nomination, lost to [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] by 1.5 percent of the popular vote in 2024 and had kept a relatively low profile since her electoral defeat.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributors treated the shared representation as thematically significant rather than merely commercial, noting that Harris had signed with "the same Hollywood talent agency as Biden" and reprising the movement's long-running refrain that "you are watching a movie" — a framing in which prominent political figures are understood as performers in a scripted narrative.[1]
