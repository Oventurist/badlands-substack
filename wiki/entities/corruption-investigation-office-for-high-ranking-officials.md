# Corruption Investigation Office for High-ranking Officials

The **Corruption Investigation Office for High-ranking Officials** (CIO) is the South Korean agency charged with investigating senior public officials, which took a central role in the legal fallout from President [[yoon-suk-yeol|Yoon Suk Yeol]]'s December 2024 martial law declaration.[1]

## Role in the martial law investigation

The office requested the travel ban that South Korea's Justice Ministry imposed on President Yoon on Monday, December 9, 2024 — the first time in [[south-korea|South Korean]] history that such a measure was applied to a sitting president, according to the Dong-A Ilbo daily.[1] The ban was announced by a Justice Ministry official at a parliamentary hearing, according to Reuters.[1]

The head of the Corruption Investigation Office said that a thorough investigation would be carried out into "the leader and key offenders related to treason," as cited by the Washington Post on Monday.[1] Police had earlier launched a separate investigation into Yoon on suspicion of "insurrection."[1] While a sitting South Korean president has immunity from prosecution while in office, that immunity does not extend to allegations of insurrection or treason, for which a president can be arrested or indicted.[1]
