# Bohai Harvest RST

Bohai Harvest RST (BHR) is a China-linked investment vehicle associated in reporting and commentary with [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] and Chris Heinz.[1]

## Henniges transaction

Material circulated in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] cited a 2019 investigation asserting that the Obama administration approved transactions allowing a Chinese government-owned company and an investment firm with Chinese ties to acquire Henniges, described as a U.S. military technology firm, with BHR named as the linked entity.[1] The same account tied these approvals to the broader claim that the administration was aware Hunter Biden's seat on the board of [[burisma|Burisma]] was problematic and interfered with U.S. policy toward Ukraine.[1]

## Context in the pardon debate

The BHR and Henniges material was invoked following [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s blanket pardon of his son, as part of the argument advanced by critics that Hunter Biden served as a conduit for family business dealings abroad rather than an independent investor.[1] Badlands commentary presented these transactions as "barely the tip of the iceberg" of the foreign-dealings record the pardon was said to insulate from federal scrutiny.[1]
