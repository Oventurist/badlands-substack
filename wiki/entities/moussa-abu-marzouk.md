# Moussa Abu Marzouk

Moussa Abu Marzouk is a senior member of [[hamas]], the Palestinian movement designated a terrorist organization by the United States, the European Union, and other governments.[1] He is among the group's leading external political figures and has historically handled its foreign contacts.

## 2023 Moscow visit

In October 2023, Abu Marzouk led the Hamas delegation that traveled to Moscow for talks with Russian officials, a visit confirmed by Foreign Ministry spokeswoman [[maria-zakharova|Maria Zakharova]] on the evening of Thursday, October 26, 2023.[1] Neither Hamas nor [[russia]] had announced the trip in advance.[1] The talks took place while more than 220 hostages remained held in [[gaza]], and Moscow described itself as engaged in crisis diplomacy with both Hamas and Israeli interlocutors.[1] Russian state media reported that during the opening meetings the delegation "commended Putin's position and the efforts of Russia's diplomacy."[1] See [[2023-hamas-delegation-visit-to-moscow]].
