# Thomas Paine

**Thomas Paine** is described by Badlands Media as a revolutionary American thinker whose words are invoked as encouragement for those resisting [[menticide]] and [[totalitarianism]].[1]

## Quotation

The article closes its argument with Paine's observation: "Tyranny, like hell, is not easily conquered. Yet we have this consolation with us, that the harder the conflict, the more glorious the triumph."[1] The quotation is used to underscore the claim that countering mass delusion requires sustained, active effort by as many people as possible rather than passive commentary online.[1]
