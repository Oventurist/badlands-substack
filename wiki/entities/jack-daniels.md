# Jack Daniel's

Jack Daniel's is the Tennessee whiskey brand owned by [[brown-forman|Brown-Forman Corp.]][1] In August 2024 it was named as the next target of commentator and filmmaker [[robby-starbuck|Robby Starbuck]]'s campaign against corporate diversity, equity and inclusion programs.[1]

## 2024 DEI controversy

Starbuck announced the campaign in an overnight Wednesday post on X.[1] Unlike his previous reveals, Jack Daniel's parent company had been tipped off well in advance and cut its woke training before the exposé was published; Starbuck attributed the leak to his team's research through employee LinkedIn pages and posted an internal executive email to employees describing the "evolving" situation around the DEI program.[1]

Coverage by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] placed the episode in a sequence of corporate retreats — Harley-Davidson Inc., Tractor Supply Co. and Deere & Co. had already reversed or eliminated [[diversity-equity-and-inclusion|DEI]] programs under similar pressure.[1]
