# Nottingham Panthers

The **Nottingham Panthers** are an English professional ice hockey club competing in the Elite Ice Hockey League.[1]

## 2023 death of Adam Johnson

On October 28, 2023, Panthers player [[adam-johnson|Adam Johnson]], a 29-year-old American, was fatally injured while playing against the [[sheffield-steelers|Sheffield Steelers]] at Sheffield's home arena when he was struck in the neck by an opponent's skate blade.[1] The other player involved was Steelers defenseman [[matt-petgrave|Matt Petgrave]].[1] The death led to moments of silence in the NHL and, on November 14, 2023, to the arrest of a man by [[south-yorkshire-police|South Yorkshire Police]] on suspicion of manslaughter.[1]
