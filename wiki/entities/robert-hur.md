# Robert Hur

## Biden classified documents probe

Robert Hur is the special counsel whose classified documents investigation obtained more than seventy hours of recordings of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] speaking with ghostwriter [[mark-zwonitzer|Mark Zwonitzer]] in 2016 and 2017.[1] When the [[oversight-project|Oversight Project]] published the tapes in July 2026, reporting noted that Biden's frequent pauses, halting speech and difficulty recalling events aligned with Hur's earlier description of Biden's memory.[1] Oversight Project litigation chief [[jeff-clark|Jeff Clark]] said the recordings should force a full reexamination of the decision to give Biden a pass for keeping and disclosing classified information.[1]
