# Milwaukee Election Commission

The Milwaukee Election Commission is the city agency that administered Milwaukee's elections, including the central count facility at the heart of the [[fbi|FBI]]'s investigation into Wisconsin's 2020 election.

## The 2020 probe

Investigators are examining an incident in which former Commission Executive Director [[claire-woodall|Claire Woodall]] briefly left a flash drive inside a voting machine at the central count facility on Election Night 2020. The May 2026 Badlands Brief reports that the FBI is preparing to interview [[milwaukee-police-department|Milwaukee Police Department]] officers stationed at the central count facility and polling places that night, and that Milwaukee's 2020 ballots still exist despite standard retention timelines, partly due to ongoing litigation brought by a New London man against state and local election officials.
