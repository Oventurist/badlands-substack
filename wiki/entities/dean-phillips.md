# Dean Phillips

Dean Phillips is a Democratic congressman from Minnesota who mounted a long-shot primary challenge to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] for the 2024 Democratic presidential nomination.[1]

## New Hampshire remarks on Trump supporters

While campaigning in New Hampshire in January 2024, Phillips attended a rally held by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and afterward described the attendees in unusually favorable terms for a Democratic candidate, saying he had met roughly fifty Trump supporters waiting in the cold and that "every single one of them" was "thoughtful, hospitable, friendly."[1] He said he had chosen to attend because he wanted "to be a leader who actually invites people, doesn't condemn them," and reported that the people in line were "so frustrated that they feel nobody is listening to them, except Donald Trump."[1]

Phillips then delivered a blunt criticism of his own party, declaring "My party is completely delusional," and adding that "somebody had to wake us up, and if that's my job, so be it."[1] The remarks were circulated approvingly in independent media as evidence that the Democratic Party's characterization of Republican voters had diverged from the reality on the ground.[1]

## Michigan primary result

In the [[2024-michigan-democratic-primary|Michigan Democratic primary]] of February 27, 2024, Phillips — still actively campaigning — took 2.7% of the vote, narrowly trailing [[marianne-williamson|Marianne Williamson]]'s 3.0% despite her having suspended her campaign three weeks earlier.[2]
