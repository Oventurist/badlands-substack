# João Goulart

João Goulart was the president of Brazil deposed in the country's 1964 coup.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The article states that alumni of the [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]] embedded in the metalworkers' union provided intelligence to the [[cia|CIA]] and the [[pentagon|Department of Defense]], which was used to justify military intervention against him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

His overthrow is grouped with the removal of [[cheddi-jagan|Cheddi Jagan]] in Guyana and operations in Guatemala, Honduras, the Dominican Republic, El Salvador and Chile as part of the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]]'s documented role in covert U.S. statecraft.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
