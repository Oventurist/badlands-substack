# Madison Dearborn Partners

**Madison Dearborn Partners** is a Chicago-based private equity firm cited in the Badlands Media essay "The Ivy Dominion" through its co-CEO [[paul-finnegan|Paul Finnegan]], a member of the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] board.[1]

The article characterizes the firm as "a private equity engine linked to Fed governance and infrastructure control," using the connection to argue that Harvard's endowment leadership binds university capital to private equity, [[federal-reserve|Federal Reserve]]-adjacent networks and infrastructure holdings within the alleged [[ivy-dominion|Ivy Dominion]].[1]
