# American Airlines

American Airlines is a major United States air carrier. It appears in the corpus in connection with the January 2026 normalization of relations between the United States and [[venezuela|Venezuela]] following the capture of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]].

## Resumption of Venezuela service

As reported in the January 30, 2026 Badlands Brief, American Airlines announced plans to resume direct flights between the United States and Venezuela for the first time in over six years, pending approval by government authorities and security assessments. The announcement followed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s statement that the United States would reopen commercial airspace over Venezuela.

American's Chief Commercial Officer Nat Pieper said in a statement: "We have a more than 30-year history connecting Venezolanos to the US, and we are ready to renew that incredible relationship. By restarting service to Venezuela, American will offer customers the opportunity to reunite with families and create new business and commerce with the United States."
