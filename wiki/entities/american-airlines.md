# American Airlines

American Airlines is a major US carrier that announced in late January 2026 that it plans to resume direct flights between the United States and [[venezuela|Venezuela]] for the first time in over six years, pending approval by government authorities and security assessments.[1]

The announcement followed President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s statement that the United States would reopen commercial airspace over Venezuela after the US military's capture of [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]] on January 3, 2026.[1]

American's Chief Commercial Officer Nat Pieper said in a statement: "We have a more than 30-year history connecting Venezolanos to the US, and we are ready to renew that incredible relationship. By restarting service to Venezuela, American will offer customers the opportunity to reunite with families and create new business and commerce with the United States."[1]

## January 2025 Washington midair collision

In late January 2025 an American Airlines passenger plane collided with a Black Hawk helicopter over Washington, DC, killing 67 people in what was described as the deadliest US aviation disaster in almost a quarter century.[2] The crash renewed scrutiny of the [[federal-aviation-administration|Federal Aviation Administration]]'s hiring practices, as it was revealed that controller staffing levels were "not normal" at the time and as details resurfaced of a class-action lawsuit alleging the FAA had turned away some 1,000 would-be air traffic controllers on the basis of race.[2]

## 2025 Flight 5342 midair collision

On the evening of January 29, 2025, American Airlines Flight 5342, a Bombardier CRJ700 regional jet operated by [[psa-airlines|PSA Airlines]] with 60 passengers and four crew aboard, collided in midair with a U.S. Army Sikorsky UH-60 helicopter while on approach to Runway 33 at [[ronald-reagan-washington-national-airport|Reagan Washington National Airport]] around 9 p.m. local time.[3] Rescue efforts were concentrated near the Woodrow Wilson Memorial Bridge, and the [[federal-aviation-administration|FAA]] and [[national-transportation-safety-board|NTSB]] opened investigations into the accident, described at [[2025-potomac-midair-collision]].[3]
