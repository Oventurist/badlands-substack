# Kevin Cramer

Kevin Cramer is a Republican United States senator from North Dakota.[1]

## 2024 Attorney General Confirmation Fight

Cramer served as a public conduit for the private misgivings circulating among Senate Republicans over President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s nomination of former Rep. [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]] as attorney general in November 2024.[1] Speaking to reporters on Capitol Hill, Cramer relayed that Trump had been asking Senate Republicans to give his pick "a shot" and had argued that Gaetz was just the man to fix the Justice Department.[1]

His account came as numerous Republican lawmakers were telling Trump, incoming White House chief of staff [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]], and Vice President-elect [[jd-vance|JD Vance]] that they believed Gaetz had little chance of confirmation, and as Trump himself was reported to view the nomination as having less than even odds of succeeding while still wanting a fight.[1]
