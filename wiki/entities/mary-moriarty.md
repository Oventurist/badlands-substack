# Mary Moriarty

**Mary Moriarty** is the Hennepin County Attorney in Minnesota, who in March 2026 opened an investigation into the conduct of federal immigration agents operating in her jurisdiction.[1]

## Investigation of federal immigration agents

Moriarty said her office had created a portal allowing members of the public to submit evidence and would probe the "potentially unlawful behavior" of US Border Patrol agents — including the senior official [[gregory-bovino|Gregory Bovino]] — and pursue charges where appropriate.[1] The probe was tied to federal enforcement actions conducted earlier in 2026.[1]

The [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] rejected the effort, stating that immigration enforcement is a federal responsibility and that state prosecutors lack authority to charge federal officers.[1] The dispute formed part of a wider pattern of legal conflict between state governments and the [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] over immigration enforcement authority.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that "the People of Minnesota should remove that prosecutor," describing the investigation as a wasteful political stunt and comparing the state's posture to that of Colorado, whose attorney general had filed dozens of suits against the administration.[1]


## The Renee Nicole Good investigation

As Hennepin County Attorney, Moriarty called on the public to share recordings and evidence with her office following the fatal [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] shooting of [[renee-nicole-good|Renee Nicole Good]], amid concerns that the federal government was handling the investigation without local participation.[2]
