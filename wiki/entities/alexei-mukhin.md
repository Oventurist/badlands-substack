# Alexei Mukhin

Alexei Mukhin is the Director General of Russia's Center for Political Information, cited for a May 2020 interview quantifying the American biological laboratory presence in the former Soviet Union.[1]

Citing the Russian Ministry of Defense, Mukhin stated that 65 American secret bio-laboratories operate in the post-Soviet space — 15 in Ukraine, 12 in Armenia, 15 in Georgia and 4 in Kazakhstan.[1] He argued that because such activity is prohibited inside the United States, the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] is by its own laws engaged in illegal activity "in spirit, not in letter," with the goal of creating biological weapons directed against the peoples who inhabited the territory of the USSR.[1]

His estimate is used alongside the reporting of [[dilyana-gaytandzhieva|Dilyana Gaytandzhieva]] to contrast the two BSL-4 laboratories China operates within its own borders with the dozens of Pentagon-run facilities scattered across the international landscape — the fourth of the five "facts" [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] offers against the [[lab-leak-theory|Wuhan lab leak hypothesis]].[1]
