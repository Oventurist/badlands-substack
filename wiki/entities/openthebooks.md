# OpenTheBooks

**OpenTheBooks.com** is a transparency watchdog organization that publishes government spending and payment records obtained through public-records litigation.[1]

## NIH royalty disclosures

In August 2023, OpenTheBooks published more than 1,500 pages of unredacted records identifying which companies paid which [[national-institutes-of-health|National Institutes of Health]] scientists for which inventions and when, following a mostly successful Freedom of Information Act battle with the agency.[1] The records covered some 56,000 transactions adding up to more than $325 million, although the individual amounts for each payment and corresponding license were not listed.[1]

The disclosures showed that [[francis-collins|Francis Collins]] and [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]], the longtime directors of NIH and its [[national-institute-of-allergy-and-infectious-diseases|National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases]] respectively, personally received 58 royalty payments from companies licensing inventions developed with taxpayer money.[1] Fauci received 37 payments from three companies between 2010 and 2021: 15 from Santa Cruz Biotechnology, which creates products for medical research including antibodies and made the fifth-most payments in the royalty database; 14 from Ancell Corp., which produces immunology tools; and eight from Chiron Corp., which was acquired by Novartis in 2006.[1] The findings were reported by [[just-the-news|Just the News]].[1]
