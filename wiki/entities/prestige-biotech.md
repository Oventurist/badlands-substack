# Prestige Biotech

Prestige Biotech is the company identified as the owner of a warehouse in Reedley, Fresno County, California, in which local officials discovered an "unlicensed laboratory" in 2023.[1] The facility, described in coverage as a secret Chinese-linked COVID biolab, contained a large stockpile of "infectious agents" including coronavirus, nearly a thousand dead laboratory mice, and vials of unidentified biological fluids.[1]

## Corporate profile and CCP links

Prestige Biotech has ties to multiple Chinese pharmaceutical firms, and its president lives in China and can reportedly only be reached by email.[1] Fresno County code-enforcement officials handled the initial discovery.[1] Barry Zhang is listed as the company's registered agent by the Nevada secretary of state; Zhang was reportedly a leader of the Chinese-American Society of CPAs and of its work with China's United Front espionage and propaganda network.[1] The founder of the law firm representing Prestige Biotech, Michael M. Lin, is reportedly "a regular sponsor of CCP's United Front events in Nevada."[1]

## Federal awareness

The [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] had, in the year before the lab's discovery, issued a recall warning covering nearly 54,000 COVID rapid tests manufactured by the company that owned the lab, indicating that the Biden administration had prior awareness of the firm.[1] Reporting cited in the Badlands News Brief further stated that California governor [[gavin-newsom|Gavin Newsom]] had helped fund the facility.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] argued that the political asymmetry was glaring: "if this was a Russian lab in Florida, DeSantis would be getting skinned alive in the mainstream media."[1] He contrasted aggressive enforcement actions by the ATF and the FDA against ordinary citizens and raw-milk farms with the absence of consequences for Newsom, and predicted the mainstream press would bury the story while federal resources were spent prosecuting [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] framed the episode as evidence that "globalists do as globalists be," invoking the maxim "by their deeds ye shall know them," while speculating that factions exist within China and that "there are white hats everywhere, even in Communist China."[1]
