# Mother Jones

**Mother Jones** is an American progressive news magazine whose political reporting is periodically cited in the Badlands News Brief.[1]

## Coverage of the Greene resignation

In November 2025, Mother Jones reported on Rep. [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Greene]]'s announcement — posted to Twitter/X on a Friday night — that she would resign her House seat on January 5, 2026.[1] The outlet framed the resignation as following her public skirmish with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] over the release of the [[jeffrey-epstein|Epstein]] files, noting that Trump had responded by calling her a "traitor," a "lunatic," and "wacky," refusing her phone calls and floating support for a primary challenger.[1] Mother Jones quoted Greene's statement that "Loyalty should be a two-way street" and her assertion that standing up for American women who were raped at 14, trafficked and used by rich powerful men, should not result in being called a traitor and threatened by the President.[1] The magazine also relayed, in its own editorial register, Greene's declaration that she refused to be "a 'battered wife' hoping it all goes away and gets better."[1]
