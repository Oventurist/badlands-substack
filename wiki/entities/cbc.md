# Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (CBC)

The Canadian Broadcasting Corporation, commonly known as the CBC, is Canada's national public broadcaster. Its news division supplies domestic political coverage and analysis across television, radio, and digital platforms.[1]

## Coverage of Alberta's transgender youth policies

In February 2024, the CBC published an analysis of Alberta Premier [[danielle-smith|Danielle Smith]]'s announcement of what it described as Canada's most restrictive and wide-ranging set of policies governing transgender, gender-fluid, and questioning minors.[1] The piece noted that Smith had spent months insisting she did not wish to politicize the issue, yet appeared alone at the announcement lectern without health, education, or sport ministers, civil servants, or subject-matter experts present.[1] The CBC reported that the package would block minors from obtaining gender-affirming surgery and impose Canada's first ban on hormone treatment and puberty-blocking medication for anyone under sixteen, while quoting Smith's assurance to affected youth that "you are loved and supported as you work through your complex and often changing emotions, feelings and beliefs."[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] used the CBC's account as source material for a broader argument about narrative warfare, contrasting the framing of the parental-rights movement with that of transgender-rights advocacy and noting that in Canada, in the commentator's view, "all mainstream media is state media."[1]
