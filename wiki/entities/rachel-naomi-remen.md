# Rachel Naomi Remen

**Dr. Rachel Naomi Remen** is quoted in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]].[1]

The essay cites her formulation that "healing may not be so much about getting better, as about letting go of everything that isn't you."[1] The author uses the line to close the section on embodiment, answering the implied question of what remains after such letting go: "Not absence, but presence. Not emptiness, but essence."[1] From this the essay draws its claim that the reader is "not a project to perfect" but "a resonance to embody."[1]

Remen's quotation follows the essay's citation of [[bruce-lipton|Bruce Lipton]] on perception rewriting biology and reinforces its framing of healing as "return" rather than repair — return to breath, rhythm and self, and by extension to [[coherence|coherence]].[1]
