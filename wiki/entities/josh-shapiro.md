# Josh Shapiro

Josh Shapiro is a Democratic politician who serves as Governor of Pennsylvania. He was widely reported to be among the finalists on [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]]'s shortlist for the Democratic vice-presidential nomination in 2024, a contest ultimately won by Minnesota Governor [[tim-walz|Tim Walz]].[1]

## 2024 vice-presidential vetting

In an August 7, 2024 interview on Fox News's "Fox and Friends," former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he believed Shapiro would have been a stronger running mate for Harris than Walz.[1] Trump also echoed claims circulating among other Republicans — including remarks attributed to the Speaker of the House — suggesting that Shapiro had been passed over for the role because of his Jewish heritage.[1] The claim was not substantiated by the Harris campaign, but it became a recurring Republican talking point in the days after the Walz announcement.[1]

Shapiro's standing in the discussion reflected Pennsylvania's status as the largest and most closely contested of the 2024 battleground states, and the conventional assumption that a running mate from that state could deliver a decisive electoral advantage — an argument advanced by commentators across the political spectrum after the pick was made.[1]

## Advocacy for the Three Mile Island restart (2024)

After [[microsoft|Microsoft]] agreed in September 2024 to buy power from the shuttered [[three-mile-island-nuclear-power-plant|Three Mile Island]] nuclear plant, Shapiro wrote to the leaders of [[pjm-interconnection|PJM Interconnection]] — the grid operator serving Pennsylvania and several other states — urging regulators to let the reactor bypass the increasingly long interconnection queue.[2] He argued the plant should "be allowed to come online as quickly as possible rather than waiting in the queue as if they were an entirely new development."[2] PJM responded that it was working on a "fast track" system giving priority to some electricity projects.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited Shapiro's intervention as evidence that establishment figures were moving to front-run an American nuclear renaissance they knew was coming.[2]

## 2025 budget proposal and property tax relief

On Tuesday, April 15, 2025, Shapiro unveiled a comprehensive budget proposal addressing several key challenges facing Pennsylvania, including significantly reducing homeownership costs.[3] For the first time, the budget included a $1 billion property tax relief initiative delivered through the homestead and farmstead exclusion.[3] Coverage framed the plan as easing one of the state's most significant financial burdens amid rising living costs and as a precedent for broader fiscal reform in housing affordability.[3] Badlands contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] grouped the proposal with Indiana Governor [[mike-braun|Mike Braun]]'s tax bill as part of a [[property-tax-relief-push-of-2025|2025 property tax relief push]] by "Uniparty governors" getting ahead of the collapse of the property tax system.[3]
