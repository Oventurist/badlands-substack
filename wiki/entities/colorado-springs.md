# Colorado Springs

Colorado Springs is a city in Colorado described in the June 4, 2026 [[badlands-brief-2c0|Badlands Brief]] as "one of the reddest cities in Colorado." It is the setting for the 2023 mayoral election of [[yemi-mobolade|Yemi Mobolade]], which [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argues was decisively shaped by a staged cross burning in front of one of Mobolade's campaign signs. The prosecution in that case, which resulted in convictions of Derrick Bernard and Ashley Blackcloud for making a threat or conveying false information about a threat, held that the incident "affected the city's election."

In Badlands commentary the city functions as a case study in manufactured racial incidents altering local electoral outcomes in conservative jurisdictions, with the question of the mayor's own foreknowledge treated as an avenue that investigators declined to pursue.
