# Covert Action Magazine

Covert Action Magazine is an American publication focused on intelligence-agency exposés.[1] In August 2022 it published an article titled "New Evidence Suggests Marilyn Monroe Was Murdered and LAPD Covered Up Murder," joining the wave of media promoting the thesis that the Kennedy brothers were responsible for the death of [[marilyn-monroe|Marilyn Monroe]].[1]

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] cited the magazine's article as an example of the narrative asking readers to believe that the [[cia|CIA]] — the agency he says orchestrated the assassinations of [[ngo-dinh-diem|Ngo Dinh Diem]] and [[john-f-kennedy|John F. Kennedy]] — was in fact controlled by the Kennedys and used to kill their enemies.[1] He grouped it with [[netflix|Netflix]] documentaries, PBS documentaries and New York Times bestsellers as part of an industry built atop anecdotal testimony and unseen evidence.[1]
