# Louis D. Brandeis

**Louis D. Brandeis** was an American lawyer and later a Justice of the United States Supreme Court.[1] Before his elevation to the Court, he testified on January 23, 1915 before the [[commission-on-industrial-relations|Commission on Industrial Relations]], where he expressed serious concern about the emerging danger of concentrated financial power.[1]

Brandeis told the commission: "When a great financial power has developed... which can successfully summon forces from all parts of the country... to carry out what they deem to be their business principle... [there] develops within the State a state so powerful that the ordinary social and industrial forces existing are insufficient to cope with it."[1] Badlands Media cites the testimony as an early, authoritative warning about the role of the large charitable foundations then being built by the Carnegie and Rockefeller interests — a warning that anticipated later scrutiny of [[tax-exempt-foundations|tax-exempt foundations]] by the [[reece-committee|Reece Committee]].[1]
