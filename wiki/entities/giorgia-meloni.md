# Giorgia Meloni

Giorgia Meloni is the Prime Minister of [[italy|Italy]], leading a right-of-center government that has combined nationalist domestic positioning with continued support for the transatlantic alliance.

## Opposition to a United States troop withdrawal

As reported in the [[badlands-brief-195|Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026]], Meloni said she would not back any unilateral United States troop withdrawal from Italy, underscoring her country's reliance on the American security presence. Her statement came as President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signaled that he was weighing reductions or repositioning of United States forces in parts of Europe, including Italy and [[spain|Spain]], amid disputes within [[nato|NATO]] over defense spending and burden-sharing. The Brief notes that officials allied with Italian leadership have separately urged reconsidering restrictions on [[russia|Russian]] energy imports in response to the [[european-energy-crisis|European energy crisis]].

## Gaza stabilization force invitation

According to the January 26, 2026 [[badlands-brief-577|Badlands Brief]], the United States formally invited Italy that week to become a founding member of the [[international-stabilization-force|International Stabilization Force]] for Gaza, with the invitation delivered to Meloni's office and Italy's Foreign Ministry. The proposal would not obligate Italian combat troops, relying instead on Italy's earlier commitment to help train a future Gaza police force and on its political connections in the region. Italy's government declined to comment and a White House spokeswoman would say only that "announcements on the ISF will come soon." Separately, Meloni asked President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to amend the governance terms of the U.S.-led [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]], citing constitutional problems with its current structure.
