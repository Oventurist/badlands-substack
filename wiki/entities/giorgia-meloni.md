# Giorgia Meloni

Giorgia Meloni is the Prime Minister of Italy.[1]

## Board of Peace

Meloni was invited to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] in January 2026 and expressed readiness to contribute in principle, as did Canada's [[mark-carney|Mark Carney]], without joining the group of unequivocal acceptances that included Hungary, Kazakhstan, Belarus, Argentina, Vietnam and Morocco.[1] The United Kingdom under [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] similarly signaled willingness regarding Gaza's second ceasefire phase, while France declined outright and many leaders avoided public commitments amid fears the board could undermine the [[united-nations|United Nations]].[1]

## Opposition to US troop withdrawal (2026)

In May 2026, Meloni said she would oppose any unilateral US troop withdrawal from Italy, underscoring Italian reliance on the American security presence.[2] Her statement came after President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signaled he was considering reducing or repositioning US forces in parts of Europe, including Spain and Italy, amid disputes over defense spending and [[nato|NATO]] burden-sharing.[2] Separately, allies of Italian leadership called for reconsidering restrictions on [[russia|Russian]] energy imports to stabilize supply during the [[european-fuel-supply-crisis-2026|European fuel supply crisis]].[2]

## Defense of the Holy Sepulchre (March 2026)

After [[israeli-police|Israeli police]] barred Catholic leaders from [[church-of-the-holy-sepulchre|the Church of the Holy Sepulchre]] on [[palm-sunday|Palm Sunday]] 2026, Meloni said the church "is a sacred site of Christianity and, as such, must be preserved and protected for the celebration of sacred rites."[3] She added that preventing the Patriarch of Jerusalem and the Custos of the Holy Land from entering, especially on a solemnity central to the faith, "constitutes an offence not only against believers but against every community that recognises religious freedom."[3] Badlands writers described the Italian response as bolder than the American one and as amplifying the sentiments of Christians worldwide.[3]

## Relationship to Salvini and the Sovereign Alliance

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] presented Meloni as aligned with the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], writing that Italy is part of it and describing her as "#OurGirl."[4] In his account, Deputy Prime Minister [[matteo-salvini|Matteo Salvini]] — the most popular politician in Italy and a face of European right-wing populism — was systematically prevented from becoming Prime Minister by an elite that feared his ascent would align Italy with Russia, and Meloni emerged as the critical new face to carry the torch for Salvini.[4] On that reading, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s public spats with Meloni are intended to help her consolidate power inside and outside Italy: after the direct path to power was stonewalled, the Alliance turned to deception to manipulate Italian elites into supporting a populist government so the populist agenda could be implemented.[4]

## Suspension of Israel defence memorandum (2026)

On April 13, 2026, after consultations with Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani and Defence Minister Guido Crosetto, Meloni announced that Italy was suspending the automatic renewal of its [[italy-israel-defence-memorandum-of-understanding|2003 defence cooperation memorandum of understanding]] with Israel.[5] The memorandum, in force since 2006 under Silvio Berlusconi and renewed every five years absent an opt-out, covered military procurement, training, exchange of defence equipment and technology, and arms transit.[5] The move followed Italian criticism of Israeli operations in Lebanon and an incident in which Israeli forces fired warning shots at Italian UNIFIL troops, prompting Rome to summon Israel's ambassador; Meloni said, "When there are things we don't agree with, we act accordingly."[5] Israel's foreign ministry dismissed the 2003 accord as an old memorandum with "no substantive content."[5] Because her right-wing government had been among Israel's closest European allies since 2022, analysts treated the step as a notable shift linked to domestic positioning and the Lebanon conflict, while Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry cast her as an agent provocateur whose break with Israel could fracture the EU.[5]

## Trump's "restraining order" trolling (July 2026)

On the eve of the [[2026-ankara-nato-summit|NATO summit in Ankara]], President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] escalated his public feud with Meloni by posting a meme on Truth Social captioned "RESTRAINING ORDER NEEDED."[6] The post followed Trump's earlier claim that Meloni had repeatedly sought a photograph with him during the [[2026-g7-summit|G7 summit]], a characterization she publicly rejected.[6] The two leaders had also clashed over [[italy|Italy]]'s decision not to allow US bombers to use Naval Air Station Sigonella during military operations connected to the Iran conflict.[6]

Officials on both sides sought to minimize the dispute: Italian Defense Minister [[guido-crosetto|Guido Crosetto]] said the Italy–United States relationship extends beyond disagreements between individual leaders, while other Italian officials declined to respond directly.[6] Both leaders were expected to attend the Ankara summit, where alliance defense spending, regional security, and the aftermath of the Iran conflict were expected to dominate.[6]

## Badlands reading: martyrdom and consolidation

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] argued that Trump was signal-boosting the trip and prepping the battlespace with memetic intent, leaving European leaders a choice between public capitulation in Ankara or taking the bait and isolating themselves before an emergent multipolar order.[6] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] went further, contending that by "attacking" Meloni, Trump made her a martyr and generated greater political support for her both inside Italy and across Europe.[6] He dated the sequence to June 19, when Meloni cancelled a planned US trip by her top diplomat, after which most of the Italian political spectrum fell in line behind her.[6]

Her government, which took office on October 22, 2022, became the second longest-serving in post-WWII Italian republican history in May 2026 and was projected to become the longest-serving on September 4.[6] Despite that record, her first major failure came in March 2026 when the [[nordio-reforms|Nordio Reforms]] separating judges and prosecutors into distinct career tracks were defeated at referendum; she has continued to push the [[premierato|Premierato]], which would have the prime minister directly elected to a five-year term.[6] GhostofBasedPatrickHenry also cited a Q post stating "Italy is w/ POTUS" regarding Deputy Prime Minister [[matteo-salvini|Matteo Salvini]] as circumstantial support for his view that Meloni is aligned with Trump.[6] He argued Meloni has been steering Europe toward the influence of [[recep-tayyip-erdogan|Recep Tayyip Erdoğan]] — a close ally of both Putin and Trump — and is using her consolidated clout to advance the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] multipolar agenda.[6]

## Ties to Trump world and Divinazione-Expo 24 (2024)

While most European leaders were anxious about a possible return of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to the White House, Rome viewed the scenario as a boon for Meloni, who had forged links with MAGA ideologues at Republican gatherings before becoming prime minister.[7] Her allies believed she would emerge as Trump's go-to European partner if he were re-elected, boosting her relevance with EU peers with whom she had fractious relations, and her [[brothers-of-italy|Brothers of Italy]] party apparatus quietly worked to reinvigorate ties with the Republicans even as she publicly declined to comment on the US vote.[7] Antonio Giordano, a Brothers of Italy MP who attended the 2024 Republican National Convention, said Meloni would be Trump's "natural interlocutor" for understanding how to deal with Europe.[7]

In September 2024 Meloni attended "Divinazione-Expo 24," an agricultural expo in Ortigia, an island off Syracuse, Sicily, telling Italian farmers and small business owners that "Agri-food is a fundamental piece of our name in the world, Made In Italy, and an extraordinary piece of our identity. You are our greatest wealth and strength."[7] The expo focused on defense and enhancement of the agricultural supply chain, fishing and aquaculture, and workplace safety and the fight against "gangmastering" — a form of organized crime in the workplace.[7] [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing from Italy, reported booming business, growing agriculture, overwhelmingly domestic produce in grocery stores, and an apparent baby boom, arguing that if Italy is a pilot for the [[sovereign-alliance|sovereign alliance]]'s aspirations it should be judged a raging success.[7] She also noted escalating media claims that Meloni is "far right," which she attributed to panic over Meloni's ideological alignment with Trump's coalition, including Elon Musk and Robert F. Kennedy.[7]

## Rising profile and the Equalize affair (late 2024–January 2025)

Through late 2024 Meloni's standing in transatlantic politics rose sharply. [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], turning his attention to European politics after helping propel [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to victory, spoke fondly of her, calling her in September a "precious genius" who was "even more beautiful on the inside than she is on the outside."[8] Meloni leads [[brothers-of-italy|Brothers of Italy]], a party with roots in the post-World War II neo-fascist Italian Social Movement; she came to power in 2022 on a platform of lower immigration, tighter border control and traditional values, and was at the time described as Italy's most right-wing leader since Benito Mussolini.[8]

In December 2024 corporate outlets mocked Trump as a "third wheel" to Musk and Meloni after she defended her friendship with Musk in an address to Parliament and a photo of the pair went viral.[8] Trump himself called her "a fantastic leader and person" and vowed to work with her upon returning to office, gushing over her repeatedly through December in interviews and Truth Social posts.[8] Just before Christmas, Meloni publicly stated that "We (Europe) must be friends with Donald Trump."[8] By early January 2025 commentators were describing her as potentially the most powerful leader in Europe — a day before reports that she had succeeded in blocking 200,000 Africans from illegally entering the continent through Italy.[8]

Badlands writer GhostofBasedPatrickHenry linked these developments to the [[equalize|Equalize]] hacking scandal, in which four people were arrested in Milan on November 1, 2024 for attempting to breach Italian government servers; the firm, staffed by former Italian intelligence operatives, allegedly gathered blackmail material on Italian officials and worked for Israeli intelligence and the [[vatican|Vatican]].[8] He concluded that Meloni was "our gal," but was being gamed by [[mossad|Mossad]] for blackmail leverage.[8]

## Rejection of the Franco-British troop plan (March 2025)

In March 2025 Meloni rejected the idea of deploying Italian troops in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], after France and the United Kingdom proposed sending peacekeepers to secure an eventual truce between Kiev and Moscow.[9] She expressed her opposition during an address to the upper house of the Italian parliament on Tuesday, March 18, ahead of a European Council meeting in Brussels expected to discuss the conflict.[9]

France and the UK had been leading efforts to form a "coalition of the willing," with President [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] suggesting that "a few thousand troops per country" would be deployed at key locations in Ukraine.[9] Meloni said the Italian government respected the proposal but was not "convinced" by it, telling the Senate that "sending Italian troops to Ukraine is a topic that has never been on the agenda" and describing the Franco-British plan as "a very complex, risky and ineffective option."[9] She also stated that [[italy|Italy]] supported the peace-making effort launched by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[9]

Badlands commentary read the refusal as the start of a fracturing of [[nato|NATO]], arguing that Keir Starmer and Macron were unwittingly establishing a new offensive mandate for a defensive alliance in a conflict involving a non-member state, and that Meloni's defiance exposed the alliance's inability to compel member nations to supply soldiers.[9]


## Response to Trump's National Security Strategy (December 2025)
Speaking at Atreju, a festival organized by her Brothers of Italy party in Rome on Sunday, December 14, 2025, Meloni cast President Donald Trump's new U.S. national security strategy as a wake-up call for Europe while downplaying tensions within her government and reaffirming Italy's support for Ukraine.[10]

"We want an Italy that is loyal to its partners but is subject to nobody," she said, adding: "It's why we've stood with Ukraine from day one. And it's why we will continue doing so — for a sense of justice but most of all to defend our national interest and security."[10] Meloni argued that the strategy, which had rattled European leaders, underscored Washington's intention to step back from the continent and force Europe to bolster its own defenses.[10]

## Boycott of the UK-France Ukraine meeting (March 2025)

Earlier in March 2025, Italian newspapers La Repubblica and La Stampa reported that Meloni would not take part in an upcoming video conference proposed by the UK and France, in which a so-called [[coalition-of-the-willing|"coalition of the willing"]] intended to increase military support for Ukraine.[11] La Repubblica reported on Tuesday, March 11, that it was "certain" Meloni would boycott the virtual meeting of Western European leaders convened by British Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] for that Saturday.[11] The Italian premier was said to be distancing herself from the "Franco-British axis" over her disagreement with the London-Paris push to send peacekeepers to Kiev, believing that Starmer and [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] were "rushing ahead"; La Stampa noted the move could split the front the two leaders were trying to build.[11]

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed the boycott as continuous with Meloni's alignment with Trump since the Musk-Meloni-Trump "love triangle" narrative spun up by European media in December 2024 — a narrative he said had been preceded by her government's arrest of Mossad agents colluding with former Italian intelligence officers to hack Italian servers for blackmail material on Meloni and [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[11] He argued that the [[european-union|European Union]] was unlikely to survive the conflict and that [[nato|NATO]]'s demise was already a foregone conclusion, citing defections by Slovakia and Hungary and now Italy, Spain and Romania, alongside rising nationalist-populist movements in Germany, Poland, France, Britain and Ireland.[11] He also referenced a [[breaking-history|Breaking History]] episode co-hosted with Matt Ehret featuring ZeroHedge writer Alex Krainer, who theorized that certain Tuscan banking dynasties were loyal to Musk, Trump and the nationalist-populist movement, and closed by wondering how peacemakers like Meloni would be treated by Brussels and Davos.[11]

## Migration model and the Starmer meeting (September 2024)

Meloni's migration policy became a reference point for other European leaders in September 2024, when U.K. Prime Minister [[keir-starmer|Keir Starmer]] traveled to Rome to praise what he called the "remarkable progress" of her nationalist conservative government in reducing the number of migrants reaching Italy's shores by boat.[12] The talks were held at the Villa Doria Pamphilj, a 17th-century mansion in a large park not far from the Vatican, and came after at least eight seaborne migrants died off the French coast attempting to cross the English Channel.[12] Although the Labour prime minister was not a natural ally of Meloni, who heads the far-right Brothers of Italy party, migration had climbed the U.K. political agenda and Starmer hoped Italy's tough approach could help stop crossings in flimsy, overcrowded boats; more than 22,000 migrants had made the crossing from France that year, a slight increase over the same period in 2023.[12] At a joint news conference, Starmer credited Italy with cracking down on smuggling gangs and "working with countries along migration routes as equals."[12]

Meloni had pledged a crackdown on migration after taking office in 2022, aiming to deter would-be refugees from paying smugglers for the dangerous Mediterranean crossing.[12] According to Italy's Interior Ministry, the number of migrants arriving by boat in the first half of 2024 was down 60% from 2023.[12] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], writing from Italy, presented Meloni's results as proof that reversing illegal migration is achievable quickly, describing [[alternative-for-germany|AfD]] as following in her footsteps and the U.K. as exploring its options, with the lesson for Americans being that "it's possible. It won't take long. It simply takes will."[12]

## Solo visit to Trump amid the 2025 tariff war

In April 2025 Meloni's planned trip to the United States unsettled European capitals, as she was set to become the first European leader to meet [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] after he imposed reciprocal tariffs on all countries, including 20 percent on the [[european-union|European Union]].[13] Reporting indicated the visit added to European fears of a disjointed front against Trump on trade, with ministers of a leading EU member publicly airing concerns about its implications.[13]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], who dubbed her "#OurGirl," wrote that Meloni was widely expected to negotiate a bilateral trade agreement between Italy and the United States without the involvement or consent of the EU, and argued that if it happened it would represent the end of the bloc, whose founding premise in 1993 was that collective economic might empowers Europe as a premier world power.[13] He predicted that if Meloni succeeded, all of Europe would follow, and concluded: "All eyes on Meloni."[13]

## April 2025 White House meeting and NATO spending

At a joint press conference in Washington on Thursday, April 17, 2025, Meloni told reporters that "Italy is going to the next summit of NATO, announcing that it will raise [defense spending] to 2%, and that's also progressed."[14] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] interjected, "It will go up," and when asked whether the [[nato|NATO]] 2% target was sufficient replied, "It's never enough."[14] Trump has long insisted that European members boost their own defense, and the burden-sharing debate has further complicated his administration's strained relations with Europe.[14]

[[italy|Italy]]'s defense budget stood at 1.49% of GDP the previous year — one of the lowest in NATO — and Meloni had for some time advocated reclassifying expenditure on border patrols and coast guards as military spending.[14] Badlands commentator GhostofBasedPatrickHenry argued that this reclassification would in theory militarize the borders of European nations and promote the sovereignty of individual states, reversing the [[european-union|European Union]]'s post-1993 status quo of open internal borders with security concentrated at the bloc's external frontier.[14] The same commentary suggested Trump was pressing NATO countries to reallocate funds from social welfare programs toward defense, a shift that would be necessary to sustain support for Ukraine or to raise a continental army.[14]

## ICC warrants and Italian non-enforcement (2025)

In April 2025 Italian media reported that Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] would not be at risk of arrest in Italy under an [[international-criminal-court|International Criminal Court]] warrant, because Italian officials never completed the procedures required to make the warrant valid.[15] Badlands commentary treated this as further evidence that Meloni belongs to what its writers call the [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]], noting that Italy had likewise said in January that it would not enforce ICC warrants against [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and former Israeli Defense Minister [[yoav-gallant|Yoav Gallant]].[15] The commentary read a major European nation's treatment of a UN-associated court as illegitimate as a signal that the clout of the United Nations is waning while a multipolar world order rises.[15]

## Warning to the EU on seizing Russian assets (October 2025)

In an address to the Italian Senate on Wednesday, October 22, 2025, ahead of the [[european-council|European Council]] summit in Brussels, Meloni called on the [[european-union|EU]] to increase pressure on Moscow but cautioned the bloc to respect international law in any plan to use [[frozen-russian-assets|frozen Russian assets]].[16] Her intervention came as the [[european-commission|European Commission]] pushed a [[ukraine-reparations-loan|reparations loan]] for [[ukraine|Ukraine]] backed by proceeds from roughly €210 billion in immobilised funds.[16] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described her as putting "the EU communists on notice" shortly after the Trump administration voiced its own opposition to seizure, while questioning whether EU bureaucrats would listen.[16]
