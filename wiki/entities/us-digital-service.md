# United States Digital Service

The United States Digital Service (USDS) is a federal technology office established under President Barack Obama to streamline government technology programs following the troubled rollout of the "Obamacare Website."[1]

## Renaming under Trump

On his first day of his second term in January 2025, [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] signed an executive order renaming the USDS as the United States DOGE Service and establishing within it a temporary program office — the [[department-of-government-efficiency|Department of Government Efficiency]].[1] The order created a USDS Administrator position in the Executive Office of the President reporting to the White House Chief of Staff, [[susie-wiles|Susie Wiles]], and established modernization teams inside each federal agency.[1]

## Assessment

Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that repurposing the existing USDS rather than building something new was "at first glance disappointing," but on deeper consideration sensible, since the vehicle already existed for exactly the kind of technology-enabled transformation DOGE intends to carry out across the bureaucracy.[1]
