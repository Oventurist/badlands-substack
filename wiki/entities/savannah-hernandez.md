# Savannah Hernandez

**Savannah Hernandez** is an independent American journalist known for on-the-ground video reporting posted to X.[1]

In March 2025 Hernandez published footage in which she said she "caught a [[jackson-hewitt|Jackson Hewitt]] employee coaching illegals on how they could get up to $14,000 in tax refunds whether they're 'working or not.'"[1] She proposed that determining who is receiving tax refunds should be the next target of scrutiny for [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]
