# Patricia Harrison

Patricia Harrison is the president and chief executive officer of the [[corporation-for-public-broadcasting|Corporation for Public Broadcasting]], the private nonprofit founded in 1967 that served as steward of federal funding for American public media.[1]

## 2025 wind-down of the CPB

Harrison announced in August 2025 that the CPB would begin closing its operations after Congress and the Trump administration eliminated its federal appropriation.[1] "Despite the extraordinary efforts of millions of Americans who called, wrote, and petitioned Congress to preserve federal funding for CPB, we now face the difficult reality of closing our operations," she said in a statement.[1] She added that the organization "remains committed to fulfilling its fiduciary responsibilities and supporting our partners through this transition with transparency and care."[1]

Under the plan Harrison outlined, a majority of the roughly 100 staff positions would conclude when the fiscal year ended on September 30, 2025, with a small transition team remaining through January 2026 to complete an orderly closeout.[1] The corporation had distributed funds to some 1,500 local public radio and television stations as well as [[pbs|PBS]] and [[npr|NPR]].[1]
