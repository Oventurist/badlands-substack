# Newt Gingrich

**Newt Gingrich** (R-Ga.) is a former Speaker of the United States House of Representatives who remains an active political commentator and frequent guest on Fox News.[1]

## Comments on a Xi inauguration visit

In December 2024, Gingrich responded to President-elect [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s invitation to Chinese President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] to attend Inauguration Day in January, saying that acceptance would send a global "signal."[1] Speaking to Fox News host Jesse Watters, Gingrich said, "Look, I think Trump believes in constant offense, constant momentum, keeping things going forward."[1] He characterized Trump's daily approach as looking for the next offensive move — "'Let's go to McDonald's or let's go to the garbage truck,' whatever it takes" — and imagined Trump reasoning, "'Yeah, I think my good friend Xi Jinping, we haven't been together in a long time, why don't I?'"[1]

Gingrich called the invitation alone a "tremendous move," and added, "I think there's a 50/50 chance that Xi Jinping is going to show up, which you have to admit, in terms of sending a signal to the planet, would be pretty unbelievable."[1]

## Context

Trump had extended the invitation to Xi shortly after defeating Vice President Kamala Harris in November 2024, though it was not clear whether the Chinese leader had accepted.[1] The transition team also raised the possibility of other world leaders attending, including Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán.[1] Trump had frequently touted his relationship with Xi even while planning hefty tariffs on China — a 10 percent levy on all Chinese goods on top of existing first-term tariffs — and threatening new tariffs on Canada and Mexico on his first day in office.[1] The two leaders had met before, including when Trump hosted Xi at his Mar-a-Lago estate in 2017.[1]
