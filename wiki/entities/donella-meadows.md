# Donella Meadows

**Donella Meadows** is cited in Part 4 of the [[structures-of-control-part-1-series|Structures of Control]] series as one of two systems thinkers whose work the essay recommends for navigating beyond third-dimensional control.[1]

## Role in the argument

The article states that Meadows and [[buckminster-fuller|Buckminster Fuller]] both emphasized the importance of understanding [[feedback-loop|feedback loops]], leverage points, and structural tension.[1] It argues that once one learns to track signal through systems, patterns cease to be overwhelming and become maps, so that "every repressive node reveals the very architecture of its own exit point."[1]

This appeal to systems literacy follows the essay's description of the third dimension as a dynamic, adaptive network in which media conglomerates, central banks, medical institutions, educational policy, and technology infrastructure operate in semi-synchronous feedback.[1] Its examples include the cross-ownership of [[blackrock|BlackRock]], [[vanguard-group|Vanguard]], and [[state-street|State Street]] across thousands of public companies, and the reformation of American schooling attributed to the [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller Foundation]] and scaled worldwide by [[unesco|UNESCO]].[1]
