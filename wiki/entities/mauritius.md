# Mauritius

Mauritius is a small island nation near Madagascar in the Indian Ocean and the prospective sovereign of the [[chagos-islands|Chagos Archipelago]].[1]

In May 2025 the United Kingdom approved a plan to hand the Chagos Islands to Mauritius while signing a 99-year lease retaining access to the joint UK–US base at [[diego-garcia|Diego Garcia]].[1] The sovereignty dispute had been shaped by a 2019 advisory opinion of the [[international-court-of-justice|International Court of Justice]] finding that the UK's 1965 separation of the islands from Mauritius was unlawful.[1]

According to a *Telegraph* report cited by RT, U.S. officials have discussed a proposal to purchase the Chagos Islands from Mauritius once the transfer from Britain is complete.[1] Mauritius has not confirmed receiving any formal offer; RT, citing Reuters, reported that the Mauritian government said it had not received an official proposal and reiterated that its sovereignty over the archipelago is "non-negotiable."[1]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed a direct US–Mauritius transaction as a move that would bypass London entirely and leave the British sidelined.[1]
