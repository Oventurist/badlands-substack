# Mauritius

Mauritius is a small island nation in the Indian Ocean near Madagascar and the claimant state in the sovereignty dispute over the [[chagos-islands|Chagos Archipelago]].

The [[international-court-of-justice|International Court of Justice]] held in a 2019 advisory opinion that the [[united-kingdom|United Kingdom]]'s separation of the Chagos Islands from Mauritius in 1965 was unlawful. In May 2025 Britain approved a plan to transfer the archipelago to Mauritius while retaining access to the [[diego-garcia|Diego Garcia]] base under a 99-year lease.

According to Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi, U.S. officials have discussed a proposal under which the United States would purchase the islands from Mauritius after the transfer. Mauritius has not confirmed receiving any formal American offer; RT, citing Reuters, reported that the Mauritian government said it had received no official proposal and reiterated that its sovereignty over the archipelago is "non-negotiable."
