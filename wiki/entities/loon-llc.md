# Loon LLC

Loon LLC was a company operating under [[sergey-brin|Sergey Brin]]'s big-tech umbrella corporation [[alphabet|Alphabet]] that used high-altitude balloons to deliver wireless internet.[1]

The Badlands Media essay "Glimpses into Hell — Part 1" cites Loon's deployment of balloons to bring wifi to Puerto Rico during Hurricane Maria as precedent for restoring connectivity to [[cuba|Cuba]] after the state cut the internet during the [[2021-cuban-protests|2021 protests]].[1] The essay notes that a number of private companies maintain R&D teams working on long-distance wifi transmission, including [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s [[starlink|Starlink]] and [[facebook|Facebook]]'s satellite internet effort, but observes that such "woke" tech companies would have to choose to act — something the author expects only once it benefits them.[1]
