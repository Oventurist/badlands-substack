# Tariq Habash

Tariq Habash is a Palestinian-American who served as a political appointee at the U.S. Department of Education and became the first [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration appointee to resign in protest over the Gaza war.[1]

## Criticism of Biden's Gaza policy

After the January 2025 [[gaza-ceasefire|Gaza ceasefire]] was reached, Habash told The Independent that it was "a failure of the Biden administration that it took so long for us to get to this point," and said he did not believe the administration "was prioritizing reaching a deal expediently."[1]

He argued that had the administration wished to, "they would have leveraged some real influence, particularly with [Israeli prime minister Benjamin] Netanyahu and the Israeli government," and that the decision not to do so "allowed for the continuation of the violence against Palestinian civilians for over 15 months."[1] Badlands Media presented Habash's remarks — along with those of Josh Paul and Annelle Sheline — as insider confirmation that the ceasefire's timing was driven by the incoming [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's willingness to apply pressure on [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]
