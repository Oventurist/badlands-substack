# John J. McConnell Jr.

John J. McConnell Jr. is the chief judge of the U.S. District Court for the District of Rhode Island.[1] On Monday, November 27, 2023, McConnell summarily dismissed a complaint that sought to disqualify former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the 2024 presidential primary ballot in Rhode Island.[1]

## The Castro ballot challenge

The complaint before McConnell was filed by [[john-anthony-castro|John Anthony Castro]], a lesser-known Republican presidential candidate from Texas who had brought parallel disqualification suits in more than two dozen states.[1] Castro argued that Trump had engaged in insurrection in connection with the January 6, 2021 Capitol breach and was therefore barred from federal office by Section Three of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

McConnell's ruling followed a November 21, 2023 decision by the U.S. Court of Appeals for the First Circuit on the same matter, also brought by Castro.[1] Because the First Circuit exercises appellate jurisdiction over the Rhode Island district court, its ruling was binding on McConnell's court.[1] Commentary published by Badlands Media characterized the dismissal as resting on Castro's failure to demonstrate an "injury in fact," the standing requirement necessary to pursue a Section Three claim.[1]

Rhode Island thereby became the sixth state — after Florida, Colorado, New Hampshire, Minnesota, and Michigan — in which courts declined to remove Trump from the ballot.[1]
