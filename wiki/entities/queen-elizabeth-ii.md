# Queen Elizabeth II

Queen Elizabeth II was the reigning monarch of the United Kingdom who in 1995 conferred an honorary knighthood on [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], the American former Secretary of State and National Security Advisor.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treated the knighthood as a significant tell rather than a diplomatic courtesy, asking why a non-elected American government official would be honoured in that way by the Queen of England, and answering that Kissinger's true employer was what he called the [[british-cabal|British Cabal]] rather than the American public.[1] Carlson placed the honour alongside the existence of a monument in Israel to CIA counterintelligence chief [[james-jesus-angleton|James Jesus Angleton]] as evidence of the same pattern of foreign patronage of American officials.[1]
