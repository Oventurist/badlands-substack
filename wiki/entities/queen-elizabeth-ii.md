# Queen Elizabeth II

Queen Elizabeth II was the reigning monarch of the United Kingdom who in 1995 conferred an honorary knighthood on [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]], the American former Secretary of State and National Security Advisor.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treated the knighthood as a significant tell rather than a diplomatic courtesy, asking why a non-elected American government official would be honoured in that way by the Queen of England, and answering that Kissinger's true employer was what he called the [[british-cabal|British Cabal]] rather than the American public.[1] Carlson placed the honour alongside the existence of a monument in Israel to CIA counterintelligence chief [[james-jesus-angleton|James Jesus Angleton]] as evidence of the same pattern of foreign patronage of American officials.[1]

## Q drop #100 and the monarchy

Chapter 14 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series reads Q drop #100, dated November 5th, 2017, as directed at the British monarchy under Elizabeth II.[2] It notes that she took power in 1952 and held it until her death in 2022, and argues that with such longevity comes corruption.[2] The chapter states flatly that the British royal family is corrupt, citing [[prince-andrew|Prince Andrew]]'s association with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], and — following the documentary *JFK to 9/11: Everything Is A Rich Man's Trick* — claims the royal family are Nazis.[2]

The same section advances the theory that [[diana-princess-of-wales|Diana, Princess of Wales]] was killed because she was about to expose the family, pointing to her letter to her butler warning that [[king-charles-iii|Prince Charles]] planned to stage a car accident.[2]
