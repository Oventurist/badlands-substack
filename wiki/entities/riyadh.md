# Riyadh

Riyadh — "The Gardens" — is the modern capital of [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] and the hometown of the [[house-of-saud|House of Saud]].[1]

The name derives from the string of oases in the area around which communities formed and on which [[bedouin|Bedouin]] nomads depended in order to traverse the desert.[1] [[mohammed-bin-saud|Mohammed bin Saud]] was emir of Diriyah, a small farming community on the outskirts of Riyadh, where the ancient symbiosis between sedentary oasis farmers and warlike nomads gave rise to the politics of government.[1]

Riyadh does not appear on maps of Arabian tribes circa 600 AD because it was not yet a place of significance; its location corresponds roughly to where al-Yamamah is shown.[1] In 2017 the city was the setting for both the [[sword-dance|Sword Dance]] and the [[ritz-carlton-purge|Ritz-Carlton Purge]].[1]
