# Sony

Sony is a Japanese multinational conglomerate whose entertainment arm includes Sony Pictures and Sony Music.

## Role in the Big Six

[[a-swan-song-for-the-msm|A Swan Song for the MSM]] names Sony as one of the six conglomerates that dominated mainstream media across broadcast, print, internet, radio and film as of 2017, alongside [[time-warner|Time Warner]], [[comcast|Comcast]], [[walt-disney-company|Disney]], National Amusements (now [[paramount-global|Paramount Global]]) and [[news-corp|NewsCorp]]. The article's larger argument is that these firms are less rivals than co-owned components of a single [[mainstream-media|centralized media]] system whose ultimate shareholders are the major institutional asset managers.
