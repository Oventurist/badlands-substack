# Charles Stillman

Charles Stillman was a cotton broker and the father of Citibank president [[james-stillman|James Stillman]].[1] He is identified in commemorative material about the bank as a "long-time National City Bank client," one of several figures whose careers Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] describes as tainted.[1]

Stillman made his initial fortune profiteering during the Mexican-American War and expanded it by smuggling contraband cotton out of Brownsville, Texas and across the Rio Grande River during the Civil War.[1] His business partner, and later his son James, served as directors of [[citibank|Citibank]], with James eventually becoming bank president.[1] The family maintained substantial interests in Mexico, which James later leveraged when he brokered a civil war that overthrew the Mexican government during the 1870s.[1]
