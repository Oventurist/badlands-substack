# Timothy Kelly

Timothy Kelly is a United States federal district judge in Washington, D.C., appointed by Donald Trump.[1]

## Hunter Biden IRS lawsuit

Kelly was assigned the civil case [[hunter-biden-v-internal-revenue-service|Hunter Biden v. Internal Revenue Service]], filed in September 2023, in which [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] alleged that [[internal-revenue-service|IRS]] agents had unlawfully disclosed his tax return information and that the agency had failed to safeguard his private records.[1] The suit turned on disclosures by IRS whistleblowers [[gary-shapley|Gary Shapley]] and [[joseph-ziegler|Joseph Ziegler]] and their attorneys.[1] Coverage of the filing noted Kelly's status as a Trump appointee as a salient detail of the case's posture.[1]
