# Cassidy & Associates

Cassidy & Associates is a long-established Washington, D.C. lobbying firm. It appears in the record of the [[igor-danchenko|Igor Danchenko]] trial as a common employer linking two figures in the [[steele-dossier|Steele dossier]] story: [[charles-h-dolan-jr|Charles "Chuck" Dolan Jr.]], who was formerly employed there, and [[gregg-hartley|Gregg Hartley]], who served for over a decade as the firm's Vice Chairman and Chief Operating Officer.[1]

Hartley was copied on an April 29, 2016 email from Danchenko to Dolan that attached an [[orbis-business-intelligence|Orbis Business Intelligence]] briefing note on the Russian banking sector, and commentary on the trial cited the shared Cassidy & Associates background as an example of the tightly interlocked Washington influence networks — "the Swamp" — surrounding the dossier's production.[1]
