# Sergey Glazyev

Sergey Glazyev is a Russian economist associated with the design of a new Eurasian financial architecture.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Role

Badlands Media commentary credits Glazyev with outlining, on numerous occasions, the principles of a fully integrated new financial architecture founded upon real-world values, toward which the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]] is said to be preparing the groundwork in conjunction with China's [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Arctic cities programme

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], describing Glazyev as a former defence minister, cites his programme to construct five advanced cities in Russia's Arctic as an expression of a forward-thinking paradigm that the west lost many decades ago.[2] Ehret places the programme alongside Russia's nuclear-powered domed city of [[umka-arctic-city|Umka]] on the island of Kotelny and Canada's abandoned [[frobisher-bay-domed-city|Frobisher Bay]] plan as models for opening the Arctic as the new frontier of human development.[2]

## Arctic cities program

In his August 2025 essay on the [[2025-trump-putin-alaska-summit|Trump–Putin Alaska summit]], [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] described Glazyev — whom he styles a former defence minister — as the author of a program to construct five advanced cities in Russia's Arctic, an expression of the forward-thinking paradigm Ehret says the west lost decades ago.[3] He grouped the plan with Russia's nuclear-powered domed city of [[umka-arctic-city|Umka]] on the island of Kotelny, 1,000 km from the North Pole.[3]
