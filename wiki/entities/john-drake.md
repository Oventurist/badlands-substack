# John Drake

**John Drake** is the Chief of the [[metro-nashville-police-department|Metro Nashville Police Department]], the agency that investigated the March 27, 2023 mass shooting at a Christian school in Nashville, Tennessee.[1]

At a press conference held shortly after the attack, Drake disclosed that the shooter, [[audrey-hale|Audrey Hale]], had left behind writings that police characterized as a manifesto: "We have a manifesto," he said. "We have some writings that we're going over that pertain to this date, the actual incident. We have a map drawn out of how all this was gonna take place."[1] Police also recovered maps annotating security camera locations, doorways and other details of the building.[1]

On the question of motive, Drake said there was "right now a theory that we may be able to talk about later, but it's not confirmed," while appearing to hint that the shooter's transgender identity factored into the motive.[1] Department representatives told reporters that there was "some theory" that Hale's gender identity related to the choice of target, and confirmed that the attack was targeted.[1]
