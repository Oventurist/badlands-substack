# United States Department of Commerce

The Department of Commerce is the U.S. cabinet department responsible for promoting American trade, economic growth, and export policy, including the licensing of dual-use technology exports.

## In the Badlands corpus

America for Sale   Part 1 treats the Commerce Department under Secretary [[ron-brown|Ron Brown]] as the operational hub of what it calls the Clinton administration's sale of America. Two functions of the department are singled out.

First, its trade missions. According to [[judicial-watch|Judicial Watch]] research quoted in the article, seats aboard Commerce Department trade-mission flights were sold to raise funds for the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] and the 1996 Clinton/Gore campaign, a scheme the source says [[bill-clinton|Bill]] and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] knew of and approved. Judicial Watch sued the department and subpoenaed the underlying documents; the article states these were withheld in violation of both the subpoena and a FOIA request.

Second, technology export approvals. The article quotes the claim that "transfers of American technology, approved and overseen by Ron Brown's Commerce Department, breached national security," specifically the contact arranged on a 1994 China trade mission between [[bernard-schwartz|Bernard Schwartz]] of [[loral-corporation|Loral Corporation]] and a Chinese government official, which led to a missile-technology transfer later examined by [[congress|Congress]]. The department's role is presented as inseparable from waivers signed by President Clinton in 1996 permitting satellite technology exports to China.
