# Treniss Evans

Treniss Evans is a January 6 defense advocate who filed a motion to intervene in the Colorado litigation seeking to disqualify [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from the 2024 ballot under Section 3 of the [[fourteenth-amendment|Fourteenth Amendment]].[1]

Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] after Judge [[sarah-wallace|Sarah Wallace]]'s November 2023 ruling, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] expressed hope that Evans's motion to intervene would be granted if the petitioners appealed, so that "someone can finally, actually argue against the ridiculous insurrection narrative with the entire story in a court of law."[1] She added that she was "not holding my breath, but praying for a chance," noting that because the ruling technically kept Trump on the ballot, no party appeared likely to appeal it.[1] See [[anderson-v-griswold|Anderson v. Griswold]].[1]
