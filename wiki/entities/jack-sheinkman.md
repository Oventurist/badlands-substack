# Jack Sheinkman

Jack Sheinkman was chairman of Amalgamated Bank and a member of the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1]

He is cited by [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a rare on-the-record member willing to describe the practical influence of the group's discussions. In 1996 Sheinkman stated: "In some cases discussions do have an impact and become policy. The idea of a common European currency was discussed several years back before it became policy. We had a discussion about the U.S. establishing formal relations with China before Nixon actually did it."[1]

DeLarme uses the quotation as evidence that the group's recommendations often become official policy despite its claim to hold only informal discussions on world affairs.[1] He speculates, however, that Sheinkman may be among those members who do not understand the true goals of the group's elite leadership, suggesting that much like the Templars within [[freemasonry|Freemasonry]] the Bilderbergs may have an inner circle privy to the real agenda while the wider membership is not.[1]
