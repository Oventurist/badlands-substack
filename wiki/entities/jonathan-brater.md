# Jonathan Brater

**Jonathan Brater** is Michigan's Director of Elections, serving under Secretary of State [[jocelyn-benson|Jocelyn Benson]].[1]

In the December 2025 litigation over absentee ballots with mismatched identification numbers, Brater and Benson argued that decades-long administrative guidance allowed such ballots to be counted as "challenged ballots" rather than being rejected outright.[1] The court rejected that position, holding that election inspectors may tabulate absentee ballots only when the stub and envelope identification numbers match, and that long-standing administrative practice cannot override the Legislature's 2024 strengthening of the matching requirement.[1] The case is covered at [[michigan-absentee-ballot-id-match-ruling|the Michigan absentee ballot ID match ruling]].[1]
