# Linda Yaccarino

Linda Yaccarino is the chief executive of X, the platform formerly known as Twitter, owned by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]].[1]

In its November 2023 report on advertisement placement, [[media-matters|Media Matters]] noted that Yaccarino had attempted to placate companies by claiming that brands were "protected from the risk of being next to" potentially toxic content, and asserted that this was not the case for at least five major brands — Apple, Bravo, Oracle, Xfinity and IBM — whose advertisements it said appeared next to posts touting Hitler and the Nazi Party.[1] Badlands Media argues that these adjacencies were manufactured by researchers refreshing the feeds of fringe accounts with negligible viewership until an ad appeared.[1]
