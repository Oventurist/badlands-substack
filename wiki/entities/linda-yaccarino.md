# Linda Yaccarino

Linda Yaccarino is an American advertising executive who, in May 2023, was reported by the *Wall Street Journal* to be in talks to become the next chief executive of [[twitter|Twitter]].[1] At the time she was [[nbcuniversal|NBCUniversal]]'s influential head of advertising, serving as chairman of global advertising and partnerships.[1]

## Career at NBCUniversal

Yaccarino had been with NBCUniversal for more than a decade, where she was an industry advocate for finding better ways to measure the effectiveness of advertising.[1] As head of NBCU's advertising sales she was key to the launch of the company's ad-supported Peacock streaming service.[1] She also served as Chair of the Advertising Council's Board of Directors and chaired a [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] taskforce.[1]

## Twitter CEO talks

[[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], Twitter's owner, said in a tweet on Thursday, May 11, 2023 that he had hired a new CEO without naming her, adding "She will be starting in ~6 weeks!"[1] Hours later the *Wall Street Journal* revealed Yaccarino's identity.[1] Commentators noted the pick was unsurprising: as arguably NBCU's top advertising executive she was positioned to fill a critical void at the new Twitter, which despite massive traffic had been hemorrhaging ad revenue as brands abandoned the platform following Musk's $44 billion acquisition.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] framed the appointment with the question of whether Twitter's days of encouraging free speech and independent thought were numbered, or whether Musk — days after announcing that [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] was joining the platform — was "playing 4D chess."[1] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]], writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], declined to predict the endgame but argued it was not cause for alarm: Yaccarino's role would likely be geared toward monetization rather than content moderation, though the latter warranted watching.[1] The commentary suggested that having a WEF-associated executive absorb public criticism while Musk owned the platform could be a net positive, adding that Twitter is not the entirety of online speech and invoking [[just-human|Just Human]]'s maxim that "Understanding > Reacting."[1]

## Resignation as CEO of X

On Wednesday, July 9, 2025, Yaccarino announced her resignation as chief executive of [[x-corp|X]], ending a two-year tenure aimed at reviving the platform's advertising business and transforming it into an "everything app."[2] A veteran advertising executive who had joined X from NBCUniversal in June 2023, she confirmed the departure in a post on the platform, thanking [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] for entrusting her with "protecting free speech, turning the company around, and transforming X into the Everything App."[2] Her exit came amid a turbulent period for the company.[2]

The timing coincided with the [[grok-mechahitler-incident|antisemitic Grok posts]] that [[xai|xAI]] was forced to remove from X, and with the unveiling of [[grok-4|Grok 4]] the following night.[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[canncon|CannCon]] speculated that her departure was linked to the Grok controversy and celebrated the exit of what he called the "Censorship WEF Queen," calling for the removal of censorship guardrails and shadow bans.[2]
