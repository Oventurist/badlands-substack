# Kim Guilfoyle

Kim Guilfoyle is an American political figure and media personality associated with the Trump family and conservative political fundraising.[1]

## Public Square opening bell (2023)

Guilfoyle appeared on the floor of the [[new-york-stock-exchange|New York Stock Exchange]] on July 20, 2023 alongside [[donald-trump-jr|Donald Trump Jr.]] and [[public-square|Public Square]] chief executive [[michael-seifert|Michael Seifert]] as the conservative marketplace rang the opening bell for its public listing, an event accompanied by chants of "USA! USA!" from the floor.[1]
