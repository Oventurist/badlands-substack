# Halyna Hutchins

Halyna Hutchins was the cinematographer of the Western film *Rust*, who was killed on set by a live round fired from a prop firearm handled by actor [[alec-baldwin|Alec Baldwin]].[1]

## Legal aftermath

The film's armorer, [[hannah-gutierrez-reed|Hannah Gutierrez-Reed]], was convicted of involuntary manslaughter and sentenced in April 2024 to 18 months in custody for bringing to the set the bullets that killed Hutchins.[1] Baldwin was charged on the same count, but his case was dismissed in the middle of trial on July 12, 2024 after a New Mexico judge found that prosecutors had withheld evidence from the defense.[1] Commentary at [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that the question of who actually brought the live rounds onto the *Rust* set remained unanswered even after the prosecutions concluded.[1]
