# James Percival

**James Percival** is the General Counsel of the United States [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]], the department's chief legal officer during the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

## Guidance on Deporting Noncitizen Voters

In June 2026 Percival was the official who publicly explained new DHS guidance directing [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] to pursue deportation proceedings against noncitizens who vote illegally in American elections or who falsely claim United States citizenship.[1] He said the policy reflects existing provisions of the [[immigration-and-nationality-act|Immigration and Nationality Act]] rather than creating new law, and that it follows the administration's broader election-integrity initiatives.[1]

Under the directive Percival described, ICE attorneys may seek removal based on evidence of illegal voting or a false claim of citizenship even where no criminal conviction has yet been obtained.[1] Departmental officials accompanying the announcement argued that unlawful voting by noncitizens undermines election integrity and independently triggers grounds for removal under federal immigration law, situating the guidance within a wider administration push on voter-eligibility verification and election security.[1]
