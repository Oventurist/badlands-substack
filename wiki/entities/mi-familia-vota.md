# Mi Familia Vota

Mi Familia Vota is a national civic-engagement organization focused on Latino voter registration, turnout, and political participation. It was one of three plaintiff organizations in [[naacp-v-useip|NAACP et al. v. USEIP et al.]], joining the [[naacp|NAACP]]'s Colorado/Montana/Wyoming Area Conference and the [[league-of-women-voters|League of Women Voters of Colorado]] in the March 2022 complaint against the [[useip|United States Election Integrity Plan]].

In "Always Fight Back", defendant [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] groups Mi Familia Vota with the other plaintiffs as organizations that themselves conduct door-to-door canvassing as a matter of business, arguing that their litigation position — that canvassing by election skeptics constitutes intimidation — could not be reconciled with their own practices. The case ended in July 2024 with judgment for the defendants and an award of costs.
