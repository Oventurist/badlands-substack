# Cheryl Mills

Cheryl Mills is an American lawyer who served as chief of staff to Secretary of State [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]. She appears in the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026 in the commentary by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] on the forthcoming congressional depositions of the Clintons.

The commentary asserts that in the wake of the Haiti earthquake, Clinton and Mills were pushing to have [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] election systems installed in Haiti, and quotes Mills as writing, "They just completed a successful election in the Philippines." Ashe in America says she laughed out loud on reading the line, pointing readers to the documentary [[why-we-vote|Why We Vote]] for context on the Philippine election in question. She argues that the [[house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] could and should question the Clintons about this alongside the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]]'s conduct in Haiti, Benghazi, Gaddafi and other matters, before drafting criminal referrals to the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]].
