# Cheryl Mills

Cheryl Mills is a lawyer and longtime aide to [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]] who served as one of Clinton's closest advisers at the [[state-department|State Department]] during Clinton's tenure as Secretary of State.[1]

## Role in the Benghazi document allegation

Whistleblower [[raymond-maxwell|Raymond Maxwell]], then a Deputy Assistant Secretary in the department's Bureau of Near Eastern Affairs, testified that an after-hours weekend session was held in a basement operations-type center at State Department headquarters in Washington, D.C., to "separate" damaging documents before they were turned over to the [[accountability-review-board|Accountability Review Board]] investigating the [[benghazi-attack|Benghazi attack]].[1] Maxwell said he was not invited but showed up on a Sunday afternoon and witnessed the operation, and he identified Mills and [[jake-sullivan|Jake Sullivan]] — two of Clinton's closest aides at the department — as the officials overseeing it.[1]

[[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] presents this episode as evidence that the Accountability Review Board never received the full document production it requested, and therefore that the board's inquiry was part of the cover-up rather than an independent review.[1] The account further argues that the material withheld was the sort of evidence that would have supported charges of treason rather than administrative error.[1] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.
