# John Micklethwait

John Micklethwait is the Editor-in-Chief of [[bloomberg|Bloomberg]].[1] On October 15, 2024, he interviewed former president [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in partnership with the Economic Club of Chicago, an event that opened and closed with a standing ovation for Trump.[1]

At the start of the interview Micklethwait noted that [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] had refused to appear to discuss her economic vision.[1] The exchange turned combative over trade policy, with Trump telling Micklethwait to his face, "It must be hard for you to spend 25 years talking about tariffs as being negative and then have somebody explain to you that you're wrong," a line that drew applause from the crowd.[1] When Micklethwait cited a Wall Street Journal estimate that Trump's economic plan would add more than $7 trillion to the national debt, Trump replied, "What does the Wall Street Journal know? They've been wrong about everything. And so have you, by the way."[1] Micklethwait objected that Trump was "trying to turn this into a debate"; Trump answered, "It's not a debate, but you've been wrong all your life on this stuff."[1]

Badlands contributor Burning Bright characterized the encounter as a rare information-war deployment that sent shockwaves through multiple layers of the "Collective Mind" on both Actual and Narrative levels, describing Trump as having walked "into the den of vampires" to tell the financial press to their faces that he was destroying their system — and doing it to cheers.[1]
