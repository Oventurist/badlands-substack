# Gaza Executive Board

The Gaza Executive Board is the intermediate governance body established under the October 2025 United States-brokered [[gaza|Gaza]] ceasefire framework, responsible for the day-to-day oversight of the postwar Gaza Strip alongside the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]].

## Composition and disputes

According to the January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief, Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] accepted an invitation to join the Board of Peace "despite prior objections to the composition of the Gaza Executive Board," according to Israeli media citing unnamed Israeli officials. The Brief reports, via a Times of Israel account of Arab diplomatic views, that the United Arab Emirates will have a representative on the intermediate Gaza Executive Board, and that Saudi Arabia and the UAE will also have representatives on the Board of Peace itself — "both panels involved in the oversight of the postwar Strip."

The same reporting indicates that Arab Gulf governments' appetite for deeper involvement and financial investment in Gaza had decreased significantly by late January 2026, amid anger over Israel's withholding of over $4 billion in collected tax revenue that the Arab diplomat quoted by the Times of Israel said rightfully belongs to the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]]. As one Arab diplomat put it, "There's a feeling that this isn't heading in the right direction, so the preference for some countries in the Gulf is to mostly remain on the sidelines."

## Relationship to the Board of Peace

The Board of Peace charter ratified at Davos in January 2026 formalized an "official international organization" chaired by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] that oversees the Gaza peace process, with the Gaza Executive Board functioning as its intermediate-level counterpart for the Strip's postwar administration.
