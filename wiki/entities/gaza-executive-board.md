# Gaza Executive Board

The Gaza Executive Board is an intermediate governing panel created as part of the US-brokered postwar arrangements for the Gaza Strip, operating alongside President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] in the oversight of the territory.[1]

## Composition and disputes

Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had previously objected to the composition of the Gaza Executive Board, but nonetheless accepted an invitation to join the Board of Peace, according to Israeli media citing unnamed Israeli officials.[1]

Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates retained representatives on the Board of Peace, and the UAE was also to have a person on the Gaza Executive Board.[1] However, an Arab diplomat speaking to Israeli press said the appetite in Riyadh and Abu Dhabi, and among their regional allies, for deeper involvement and financial investment in Gaza had decreased significantly amid Israel's withholding of more than $4 billion in tax revenues from the [[palestinian-authority|Palestinian Authority]].[1] "There's a feeling that this isn't heading in the right direction, so the preference for some countries in the Gulf is to mostly remain on the sidelines," the diplomat said.[1]
