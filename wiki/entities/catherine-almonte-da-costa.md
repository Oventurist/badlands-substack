# Catherine Almonte Da Costa

Catherine Almonte Da Costa is a New York City political appointee who briefly served as Director of Appointments for Mayor-elect [[zohran-mamdani|Zohran Mamdani]] before resigning after one day on the job in December 2025.[1]

## Resignation

Da Costa tendered her resignation on the afternoon of December 18, 2025, hours after decade-old social media posts invoking antisemitic tropes came to light.[1] The posts included statements such as "Money hungry Jews smh"; "Woo! Promoted to the upstairs office today! Working alongside these rich Jewish peeps."; and "Far Rockaway train is the Jew train," according to the Judge Street Journal, which highlighted them before Da Costa deleted her X account.[1]

## Political context

The episode compounded Mamdani's difficulties with Jewish communities, which had staunchly opposed his candidacy over his past criticism of Israel and his refusal to condemn the phrase "globalize the intifada."[1] The [[anti-defamation-league|Anti-Defamation League]]'s New York and New Jersey chapter, which has a contentious relationship with the incoming administration, wrote that "Tweeting about 'Money hungry Jews' is indefensible."[1]
