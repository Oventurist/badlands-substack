# Patel Patriot Rumble Channel

The Patel Patriot channel on [[rumble|Rumble]] served as the live broadcast home of the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] network as of January 2023, with all Badlands Media shows airing live on that channel.[1] Replays of those broadcasts were made available in library format on the separate Badlands Media channel on Rumble, giving the network a two-channel structure that separated live output from archived episodes.[1]

The arrangement reflected the network's origins in the work of [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]], who published under the Patel Patriot name and whose [[devolution-power-hour|Devolution Power Hour]] was described as "the show that started it all" on the Badlands Media Network.[1]

The December 2022 Badlands Media show schedule confirmed that all Badlands Media programs aired live on the Patel Patriot channel on Rumble, while replays were made available in library format on the separate Badlands Media channel.[2]
