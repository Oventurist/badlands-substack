# Hailey Davidson

Hailey Davidson is a transgender golfer who was born male and transitioned to female in 2021.[1]

## NXXT Women's Classic win and eligibility ban

In January 2024, Davidson won the NXXT Women's Classic, a victory that raised the possibility of becoming the first biologically male golfer to qualify for the Ladies Professional Golf Association (LPGA) tour.[1] On March 8, 2024, [[nxxt-golf|NXXT Golf]] announced that competitors "must be a biological female at birth to participate" in the NXXT Women's Pro Tour, a policy change that effectively barred Davidson from further competition on the tour.[1] The organization framed the decision as protecting the integrity of women's professional golf and ensuring fair competition.[1]

The case was cited by Badlands contributor [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] as an example of the broader conflict over biological reality in women's sports.[1]
