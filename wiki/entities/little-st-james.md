# Little St. James

Little St. James is a private island in the [[us-virgin-islands|U.S. Virgin Islands]] owned by the late sex offender [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]

## Role in the JPMorgan litigation

The island is central to the lawsuit brought by the U.S. Virgin Islands against [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan Chase]], which accuses the bank of missing red flags about Epstein's abuse of women on Little St. James.[1] The territory's case, filed in December 2022, alleges that JPMorgan aided and benefited from Epstein's sex-trafficking ring operating there, that bank executives "developed a close relationship with Epstein," and that Epstein in turn "brought additional high net worth clients" to the institution.[1]

As part of that litigation, U.S. District Judge [[jed-rakoff|Jed Rakoff]] ruled in May 2023 that the territory could serve legal papers on Google co-founder [[larry-page|Larry Page]], described as one such client.[1]

## Purchase, geography and offshore operations

[[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] bought Little St. James in 1998, ten years before pleading guilty in Florida to procuring a child for prostitution and soliciting a prostitute.[2] The island lies about a mile and a half from [[st-thomas|St. Thomas]], whose airport is roughly 10 miles away and which also hosts a submarine base about eight miles from the island, sheltered by [[water-island|Water Island]].[2] The main house on the island was renovated in 2008 while Epstein spent his nights in a low-security Florida jail cell.[2]

Badlands writer [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argues that Epstein's 2008 conviction forced the blackmail operation offshore because it could no longer be run safely inside the continental United States, and that [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]]'s Epstein-funded helicopter license was intended to move people discreetly from St. Thomas to the island.[2] He treats Maxwell's separate submarine license as evidence of covert transport of cargo or passengers beyond the reach of satellite observation.[2] Carlson also notes that Biden family property and vacations on Water Island and St. Croix placed them within 10 to 40 miles of the island during the period the operation was most active — St. Croix being roughly 40 miles away, or a 15 to 20 minute helicopter flight.[2]
