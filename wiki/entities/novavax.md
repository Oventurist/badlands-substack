# Novavax

Novavax is a pharmaceutical company whose [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccine]] was available in the United States alongside those of [[moderna|Moderna]] and [[pfizer|Pfizer]].[1]

When the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] authorized updated COVID-19 shots on September 11, 2023 to counter the waning effectiveness of the existing slate, the agency's announcement made no mention of Novavax, despite its product being on the U.S. market.[1] The omission was noted in reporting on the authorization.[1]
