# Novavax

Novavax is a pharmaceutical company whose [[covid-19-vaccines|COVID-19 vaccine]] was available in the United States alongside those of [[moderna|Moderna]] and [[pfizer|Pfizer]].[1]

When the U.S. [[food-and-drug-administration|Food and Drug Administration]] authorized updated COVID-19 shots on September 11, 2023 to counter the waning effectiveness of the existing slate, the agency's announcement made no mention of Novavax, despite its product being on the U.S. market.[1] The omission was noted in reporting on the authorization.[1]

## FDA restrictions (May 2025)

In May 2025 the [[food-and-drug-administration|FDA]], under Health and Human Services Secretary [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], severely restricted use of the Novavax COVID-19 vaccine, approving it only for older adults and for people over 12 with at least one medical condition creating high risk from Covid.[2] The vaccine will not be given to those who might want it to protect others, and CDC advisers were separately weighing whether to recommend it only for the highest-risk groups.[2]
