# Vijay Prashad

Vijay Prashad is a Marxist activist and writer who founded [[tricontinental|Tricontinental Ltd.]].[1]

## Tricontinental funding

In the 2026 federal grand jury investigation into [[neville-roy-singham|Neville Roy Singham]]'s funding network, Tricontinental — a Massachusetts 501(c)(3) founded by Prashad — was identified as receiving $16,760,000 among the six key nonprofit recipients of money routed from Singham's Shanghai base.[1]
