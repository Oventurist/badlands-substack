# Tim Scott

Tim Scott is a United States Senator who introduced the bipartisan [[road-to-housing-act|ROAD to Housing Act]] in the Senate as S. 2651.[1] The measure — formally the Renewing Opportunity in the American Dream (ROAD) to Housing Act of 2025 — passed out of the Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs unanimously on July 29, 2025.[1]

The bill, described by advocates as the most significant federal housing reform in a decade, was subsequently stripped from the House version of the [[national-defense-authorization-act|National Defense Authorization Act]] (HR 3838) during final negotiations, delaying the reform.[1]
