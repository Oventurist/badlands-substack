# Tim Scott

Tim Scott is a United States Senator who introduced the bipartisan [[road-to-housing-act|ROAD to Housing Act]] in the Senate as S. 2651.[1] The measure — formally the Renewing Opportunity in the American Dream (ROAD) to Housing Act of 2025 — passed out of the Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs unanimously on July 29, 2025.[1]

The bill, described by advocates as the most significant federal housing reform in a decade, was subsequently stripped from the House version of the [[national-defense-authorization-act|National Defense Authorization Act]] (HR 3838) during final negotiations, delaying the reform.[1]

## Characterization as a 2024 primary "prop"

In "Vivek, DeSantis, and Trump's Master Move," published December 20, 2023, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] listed Scott among the candidates he believed had been placed in the 2024 Republican primary field as props rather than genuine contenders.[2] Writing that [[chris-christie|Chris Christie]] "never had a chance," Deschamps grouped Scott with Christie, [[ron-desantis|Ron DeSantis]] and several others as figures positioned to make [[nikki-haley|Nikki Haley]] look "strong" and "presidential," with emphasis on the word look.[2] The claim formed part of his broader theory that the primary was stage-managed to elevate Haley as the [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]] candidate once [[donald-trump|Trump]] had been removed from ballots.[2]
