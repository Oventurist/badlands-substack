# 3rd Separate Assault Brigade

The 3rd Separate Assault Brigade is an elite unit of the Ukrainian military that emerged from the [[azov-battalion|Azov Brigade]].[1] It became known for what a Bloomberg feature described as a "battle-hardened, fearless approach to combat" and for the viral video content it produced as part of a recruitment drive aimed at foreign volunteers.[1]

## Bloomberg coverage and Badlands critique

A piece written by Cameron Manley for [[bloomberg|Bloomberg]] in mid-2024 highlighted the brigade's viral videos and described Azov as "a controversial regiment founded by the right-wing politician Andriy Biletsky in 2014 that played a crucial role in the Russian siege of Mariupol in 2022."[1]

Badlands Media attacked that framing as a cover-up, arguing that the account omitted the role of Israeli-Ukrainian billionaire [[igor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]] in financing the Azov formation, and contending that Bloomberg should be investigated for promoting propaganda that facilitated war crimes.[1] The commentary linked the brigade's recruitment campaign to the flow of foreign mercenaries into units such as [[chosen-company|Chosen Company]], which faced allegations of executing surrendering Russian prisoners.[1]
