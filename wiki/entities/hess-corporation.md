# Hess Corporation

Hess Corporation is an American energy company whose Venezuelan and regional oil rights became the object of a major corporate legal fight in 2025.[1] Hess was acquired by [[citgo|Citgo]], which subsequently began pumping oil off the Venezuelan coast using the acquired assets.[1]

## Exxon litigation

[[exxonmobil|Exxon]] claimed that it had a first-right-of-refusal agreement in place with Hess covering Hess's oil rights in [[venezuela|Venezuela]] should those rights ever go on sale, and sued Citgo in an attempt to have them transferred.[1] Exxon already held an operational presence in the oil belt through licences issued by the neighbouring Guyanese government, and a favourable ruling would effectively have granted it a monopoly over what is described as the largest known oil reserve in the world.[1] Citgo and Hess successfully argued that Exxon's first right of refusal was inapplicable because Citgo was purchasing Hess as a company rather than acquiring its oil rights alone, and the judge denied Exxon's claim in July 2025.[1]

Within days of that judgment, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] issued an exemption to US sanctions on Venezuela permitting Citgo to import Venezuelan crude for refinement, a sequence Badlands commentary treated as unlikely to be coincidental.[1]
