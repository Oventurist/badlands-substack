# Radio Free Europe

Radio Free Europe is a U.S.-funded international broadcaster, described in mainstream reporting as a "pro-democracy media outlet," that became a flashpoint in the fight over impoundment of appropriated funds during [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s second term.[1]

## The 2025 funding fight

In April 2025 the outlet was reported to be at risk of going dark for the first time in 75 years after the Trump administration withheld money [[us-congress|Congress]] had appropriated for it.[1] On Tuesday, April 29, 2025, U.S. District Judge [[royce-lamberth|Royce Lamberth]] ordered the administration to restore $12 million in appropriated funding, and used the ruling to warn that the constitutional system of checks and balances must remain intact.[1]

The dispute placed Radio Free Europe inside a much larger accounting: Democratic appropriations negotiators in the House and Senate published a tracker the same week alleging the administration had targeted at least $430 billion in federal funding across 114 programs during the first 100 days.[1]

## Contested legitimacy

Within the [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] editorial frame, outlets of this type are treated less as neutral journalism than as instruments of the foreign-policy establishment, and the court's intervention on their behalf was rejected outright by contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]].[1] The underlying constitutional claim advanced in the same brief is that a President has plenary authority over the Executive Branch and may defund its operations, making judicial restoration of the money at most a temporary measure.[1]
