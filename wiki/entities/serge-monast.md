# Serge Monast

**Serge Monast** was a Canadian journalist identified as the person who leaked the [[toronto-protocols|Toronto Protocols]], a collection of documents originally circulated in French as "Les Protocoles de Toronto."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

According to one English translation of the material, Monast's leak provides "a document compiled by a clique of senior financiers who met in Canada in 1967 and then in 1985."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) The group described in the documents identified itself as the "6.6.6."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Monast did not present the documents as self-authenticating. Brian Nugent, one of the best-known English translators of the book, records that "Monast himself concluded that it is up to each individual reader to make their own mind up on whether to believe this document, by, he recommends, seeing if it matches the politics, and recent political history, of the last few years."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] writes that the more he has put this test to the documents, the more they have proven their accuracy to him.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)
