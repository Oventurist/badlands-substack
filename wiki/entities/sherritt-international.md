# Sherritt International

Sherritt International is a Canadian mining company historically known as the largest foreign investor in [[cuba|Cuba]], principally through nickel and cobalt operations on the island conducted in partnership with Cuban state entities.

## Withdrawal from Cuba in 2026

The Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026 reports that Sherritt was among the first foreign firms to suspend operations in Cuba following President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s May 1, 2026 executive order imposing secondary sanctions on companies doing business with [[gaesa|GAESA]], the Cuban military's commercial conglomerate. Its suspension, alongside those of the shipping lines [[cma-cgm|CMA CGM]] and [[hapag-lloyd|Hapag-Lloyd]], was cited in the source as early evidence that the secondary-sanctions mechanism was successfully deterring third-country commerce with the Cuban state sector, with additional pullouts anticipated from banks and hotel operators. The company's exit is treated in the corpus as a marker of the practical effectiveness of the strategy described internally as "[[accelerationism|accelerationism]]."
