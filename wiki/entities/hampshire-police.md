# Hampshire Police

Hampshire Police (Hampshire Constabulary) is the territorial police force responsible for Hampshire, England, including the city of Southampton.[1]

## Henry Nowak case

The force drew national criticism for its handling of the fatal stabbing of 18-year-old [[henry-nowak|Henry Nowak]] on December 3, 2025.[1] Officers arriving at the scene arrested and handcuffed Nowak — who repeatedly told them he had been stabbed and could not breathe — after his attacker, [[vickrum-singh-digwa|Vickrum Singh Digwa]], claimed Nowak had launched a racist attack that knocked off his turban.[1] Officers focused on the unverified racism claim rather than treating Nowak's wounds or immediately securing Digwa, despite a visible blood trail.[1] Nowak died about an hour later.[1]

Nearly six months after the killing, and following Digwa's murder conviction, Hampshire Police referred itself for an independent investigation into its handling of the incident.[1] Nowak's family described the treatment of their son as "unbearable," observing that the perpetrator "was believed" while their bleeding son was not.[1]
