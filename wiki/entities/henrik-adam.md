# Henrik Adam

Henrik Adam is the President of [[eurofer|Eurofer]], the European Steel Association, and in that capacity serves as the chief spokesman for European steel producers in trade matters.[1]

## February 2025 tariff warning

On 11 February 2025, Adam publicly warned that United States tariffs on steel imports would damage the European Union steel industry. Responding to the 25% steel tariff order signed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] the previous day, Adam projected that EU steel exports could drop by up to 3.7 million tonnes, observing that the United States was the bloc's second-largest export market and had absorbed 16% of total EU steel exports in 2024.[1] He stated that these losses could not be compensated by shifting exports to other destinations.[1]

Adam situated the tariffs within a broader crisis of global overcapacity, saying cheap steel from Asia, North Africa and the Middle East was already being dumped on what he called a "vulnerable" EU market, eroding producers' capacity to fund the green transition and hastening European de-industrialisation.[1] He pointed to 9 million tonnes of EU capacity closed in 2024 and over 18,000 announced job losses as evidence of the sector's fragility, and said the American executive order would inevitably worsen it.[1]
