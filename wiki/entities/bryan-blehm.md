# Bryan Blehm

Bryan Blehm is an Arizona attorney who has represented [[kari-lake|Kari Lake]] and the election-integrity group [[we-the-people-arizona-alliance|We The People AZ Alliance]] in litigation arising from the [[2022-arizona-general-election|2022 Arizona general election]].[1]

## Runbeck public records action

In May 2023 Blehm filed a Special Action Complaint on behalf of We The People AZ Alliance against [[runbeck-election-services|Runbeck Election Services]] and [[maricopa-county|Maricopa County]], seeking public records relating to chain of custody and video documentation of ballot receipts at the Runbeck facility.[1] Roughly 81% of Maricopa County's 1,311,734 voters in 2022 voted by mail and had their ballots sent to Runbeck for signature verification, a process in which discrepancies in chain-of-custody documentation were alleged.[1]

The records were said to bear on the claim that 35,563 more ballots left Runbeck's warehouse than Maricopa County had sent in — a figure more than three times the margin of victory in the governor's race.[1]

## Sanctions

The [[arizona-supreme-court|Arizona Supreme Court]] issued sanctions against Lake's attorneys over their claims regarding chain of custody, though it denied the defendants' request for attorneys' fees and imposed only a $2,000 sanction payable to the court — an amount [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] characterized as a joke given that the record supported the underlying claims.[1]
