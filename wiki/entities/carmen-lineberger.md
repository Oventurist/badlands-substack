# Carmen Lineberger

Carmen Lineberger is a former federal prosecutor who was indicted in May 2026 for allegedly sending herself a sealed, unreleased portion of [[jack-smith|Special Counsel Jack Smith]]'s report concerning the classified-documents investigation into [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].

## The indictment

According to the indictment, Lineberger allegedly emailed the report from her [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] account to a personal account and disguised the file under innocuous names such as "Bundt_Cake_Recipe.pdf." The case was reported in the May 22, 2026 Badlands Brief alongside the [[american-accountability-foundation|American Accountability Foundation]]'s referral against [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]], as part of the same round of Justice Department accountability news.
