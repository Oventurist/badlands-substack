# Carmen Lineberger

Carmen Lineberger is a former federal prosecutor indicted in 2026 for allegedly sending herself a sealed, unreleased portion of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s report concerning the classified-documents investigation into [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

According to the indictment, Lineberger emailed the report from her [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]] account to a personal account and disguised the file under names such as "Bundt_Cake_Recipe.pdf" — a detail that supplied the title of the Badlands Brief covering the case.[1] The indictment was filed in the Southern District of Florida and became public alongside the separate referral of former Deputy Attorney General [[lisa-monaco|Lisa Monaco]].[1]

## Arraignment and charges (May 2026)

Lineberger pleaded not guilty in federal court in West Palm Beach, Florida, to charges including obstruction of justice, concealing government records, and theft of government property.[2] Prosecutors said Lineberger, who previously served as managing assistant US attorney in Fort Pierce, forwarded the restricted volume of [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]'s report and other internal Justice Department communications from a government email account to personal Hotmail and Gmail accounts.[2] The unreleased volume concerned Smith's investigation into classified documents kept at President [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s Mar-a-Lago residence, and US District Judge [[aileen-cannon|Aileen Cannon]] had previously blocked the report's public release.[2]

According to the indictment, Lineberger allegedly attempted to hide the files by renaming documents "Chocolate_Cake_Recipe.pdf" and "Bundt_Cake_Recipe.pdf" before transferring them, and prosecutors have not disclosed what additional DOJ materials may have been included.[2] If convicted on all counts she faces a maximum sentence of 25 years in prison.[2] The case is being prosecuted by the Northern District of Florida because of potential conflicts involving the Southern District of Florida office where she previously worked.[2]
