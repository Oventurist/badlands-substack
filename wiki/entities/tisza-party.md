# Tisza Party

Tisza is the Hungarian opposition party led by [[peter-magyar|Péter Magyar]] that defeated [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]]'s [[fidesz|Fidesz]] in the 2026 parliamentary election.[1]

At the time of Orbán's concession, Tisza led Fidesz by roughly 52% to 39% of the vote.[1] Magyar campaigned on addressing corruption, improving public services, and restoring [[hungary|Hungary]]'s relations with the [[european-union|European Union]], and the outcome was expected to reshape Budapest's approach to EU policy and its relationship with Brussels.[1]


## 2026 supermajority victory

With roughly 99% of votes counted in Hungary's April 2026 parliamentary election, the Tisza Party led by [[peter-magyar|Péter Magyar]] was projected to win a two-thirds supermajority, taking 138 seats and crossing the 133-seat threshold required for constitutional changes.[2] The outcome, which prompted [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orbán]] to concede a result he called "clear" and "painful," marked one of the most significant political shifts in [[hungary|Hungary]] in over a decade.[2] [[european-union|EU]] Commission President [[ursula-von-der-leyen|Ursula von der Leyen]] wrote that "Hungary has chosen Europe," calling it a country reclaiming its European path.[2]

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the Tisza surge as a "Hungarian Shatterpoint" promising managed decline under Brussels' renewed embrace, and argued globalist media were presenting Magyar's victory as the first domino in a broader Western repudiation of the sovereign nationalist movement that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had projected as the template for a post-unipolar age.[2]
