# Bob Bauer

**Bob Bauer** is a Democratic attorney who served as personal lawyer to President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] and played [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] in Biden's debate rehearsals at Camp David ahead of the June 2024 presidential debate.[1] He is married to senior Biden adviser [[anita-dunn|Anita Dunn]].[1]

## 2024 debate fallout

After Biden's widely criticized debate performance, his family privately blamed his campaign high command at a Camp David meeting and pushed for the ouster or demotion of senior staff, naming Bauer along with Dunn and former chief of staff [[ron-klain|Ron Klain]], who had run point on debate preparation.[1] Reported family grievances included inadequate preparation to go on the attack, excessive focus on defending Biden's record rather than presenting a second-term vision, and the president being over-worked and under-rested.[1] The Biden campaign publicly defended the advisers, and a senior aide denied that frustration had been directed at the three.[1]

## Statements on the Rehoboth Beach search (February 2023)

As President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s personal lawyer, Bauer confirmed the February 1, 2023 [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] search of Biden's vacation home in Rehoboth Beach, Delaware, after CBS News reported it, stating that the president's team had not sought to provide advance notice of the operation.[2] Bauer said federal agents searched the home from 8:30 a.m. to noon and that "no documents with classified markings were found," while acknowledging that, as with a similar search at Biden's other Delaware home, the department took some materials and handwritten notes appearing to relate to his time as vice president for further review.[2] See [[biden-classified-documents-scandal|the Biden classified documents scandal]].[2]
