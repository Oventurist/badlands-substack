# Customs and Border Protection

**US Customs and Border Protection (CBP)** is the border-security component of the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]], encompassing the Border Patrol.[1]

## 2026 funding package

The immigration and border-security legislation signed in June 2026 provides tens of billions of dollars in additional funding for DHS, [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]], and CBP through the remainder of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s term, part of roughly $70 billion in additional immigration-enforcement funding over three years.[1]

## Context

The funding announcement coincided with DHS disclosures about unaccompanied migrant minors who lost contact with federal authorities after crossing the border, a population Badlands contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] put at roughly 450,000 smuggled across between 2021 and 2024.[1] He described the administration as treating Border Patrol and ICE personnel as heroes in the campaign against [[child-trafficking|trafficking networks]].[1]

## Tariff collection role

CBP is also the agency that collects U.S. import duties, a function thrown into flux by the [[supreme-court-ieepa-tariff-ruling-2026|Supreme Court's February 2026 ruling]] against tariffs imposed under the [[international-emergency-economic-powers-act|International Emergency Economic Powers Act]].[2] After the decision, CBP ceased collecting tariffs under the struck-down authority while continuing to collect under the replacement framework of up to 15 percent that the administration issued via the Trade Act of 1974.[2]

## Operation Metro Surge (January 2026)

CBP agents were part of the roughly 2,000 federal personnel deployed to Minneapolis and St. Paul in early January 2026 under [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]], alongside [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|ICE]] and [[homeland-security-investigations|Homeland Security Investigations]].[3] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] then ordered an additional 1,000 CBP agents to the Twin Cities, potentially bringing the total federal presence in [[minnesota|Minnesota]] to about 3,000.[3]

## Record September 2023 encounters

CBP quietly revealed on Saturday, October 21, 2023 that September had produced the highest ever recorded number of encounters with illegal immigrants attempting to cross into the country, and that close to 2.5 million people had crossed the border during that fiscal year.[4] Badlands highlighted the disclosure alongside an Axios report declaring the southern border "more fortified than it's ever been" and dismissing conservative "open border" language as a myth, arguing that the juxtaposition amounted to gaslighting and that if both claims were true the Biden administration faced a major problem.[4]

## End of familial DNA testing (2023)

In May 2023, the Biden administration was reported to be ending [[familial-dna-testing-at-the-border|familial DNA testing]] at the US–Mexico border, a program described as key to preventing the fraudulent entry of illegal immigrants and to mitigating [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]], according to a memo obtained by [[just-the-news|Just the News]].[5] The testing had been implemented in the Trump era and used by CBP following a court order related to the separation of migrant children from their families and evidence that drug cartels were using children to create fake family units.[5] A CBP memo sent to frontline agents stated that "the U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) familial DNA contract with [[bode-technologies|BODE Technologies]] will end on May 31, 2023 and all familial DNA testing will conclude on that date."[5]

## MacArthur Park operation (July 2025)

Customs and Border Protection personnel took part in the federal immigration operation at MacArthur Park in Los Angeles on Monday, July 7, 2025, in which dozens of agents in tactical gear deployed alongside members of the [[california-army-national-guard|California National Guard]].[6] Los Angeles Mayor [[karen-bass|Karen Bass]] said she went to the park and asked "an individual from the custom and border patrol" who was in charge, was put on the phone with that person, and told him the operation needed to end; she said he replied that the agents would leave within minutes and that they did so.[6] The episode is treated in detail at [[macarthur-park-immigration-operation|the MacArthur Park immigration operation]].[6]
