# Indian Oil Corporation

Indian Oil Corporation (IOC) is India's largest oil refiner. In August 2023 it executed what was reported as the first crude oil purchase settled entirely outside the US dollar between India and the United Arab Emirates.[1]

## First rupee-settled oil purchase

On Monday, August 14, 2023, Indian Oil Corp bought one million barrels of oil from the [[adnoc|Abu Dhabi National Oil Company]] in a dollar-free transaction, paying in Indian rupees.[1] The trade was the first executed after the two countries signed a Memorandum of Understanding in July 2023 establishing a Local Currency Settlement system, facilitated by the [[reserve-bank-of-india|Reserve Bank of India]] and the Central Bank of the United Arab Emirates, permitting bilateral trade in rupees and dirhams.[1]

Coverage framed the transaction as another blow to the [[petrodollar|petrodollar]] system and to dollar dominance in global energy trade.[1] The first test of the Local Currency Settlement mechanism had involved the sale of 25 kilograms of gold from a UAE exporter to an Indian buyer for about 128.4 million rupees, roughly $1.54 million.[1]
