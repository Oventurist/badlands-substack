# Cyrus Vance Jr.

Cyrus "Cy" Vance Jr. is a former District Attorney of New York County, the office responsible for the long-running Manhattan investigation into [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s business affairs that his successor [[alvin-bragg|Alvin Bragg]] inherited.[1]

## Role in the Trump investigation

Badlands commentary on the March 2023 indictment watch recalled that roughly a year earlier Vance, then the Manhattan District Attorney, had failed to "get Trump," despite years of grand jury work by the [[manhattan-district-attorneys-office|Manhattan District Attorney's Office]].[1] Two prosecutors working on the matter, Mark Pomerantz and Carey Dunne, resigned over the collapse of the effort, and Vance decided not to run for office again.[1]

## Legacy

[[just-human|Just Human]], writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], used the Vance episode as the template for predicting Bragg's fate: Bragg was attempting the same prosecution and, in this reading, would obtain the same result — with the difference that Trump's [[truth-social|Truth Social]] posts and public statements had focused unprecedented national attention on the Manhattan DA's office itself.[1]
