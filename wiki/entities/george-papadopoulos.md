# George Papadopoulos

George Papadopoulos is an American author and former [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] presidential campaign foreign-policy adviser who became a prominent commentator on intelligence-community conduct and pandemic policy.[1]

## Commentary on renewed COVID restrictions

In August 2023, as several American institutions reinstated mask mandates amid reports of rising COVID-19 infections, Papadopoulos coined a widely circulated formulation for skeptics' suspicion about the timing.[1] In an August 23, 2023 post on X he wrote, "And just like that, the election variant emerges," a phrase that spread rapidly and gave the episode its popular name in dissident media.[1] The remark is credited in [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] coverage with crystallizing the argument that the reported case increase and the mandates accompanying it were being leveraged with the 2024 elections in view.[1] See [[election-variant|Election Variant]].
