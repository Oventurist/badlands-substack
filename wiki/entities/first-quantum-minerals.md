# First Quantum Minerals

First Quantum Minerals is a Canadian mining company whose long-term copper concession in [[panama|Panama]] became the focus of nationwide protests in October and November 2023.[1] The concession was granted through a contract signed between the Panamanian government and a local subsidiary of the company.[1]

## The Panamanian concession

Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] characterised the contract as giving the company rights that amounted to permission to strip mine Panama, including, in his description, the ability to take over privately owned land believed to contain the copper the firm sought to extract.[1]

## Protests and referendum

Associated Press reporting from October 30, 2023, quoted in the article, described protests extending into a second week over the long-term copper mining concession, with a broad cross-section of Panamanian society demonstrating across the country and demanding that the government rescind the contract.[1] Critics argued the concession placed Panama's environment and water supply at risk.[1] The administration of President Laurentino Cortizo proposed sending congress a bill scheduling a referendum in December on whether to scrap the deal, although the country's top electoral authority stated that such a vote could not be held before the presidential election the following May.[1] Carlson noted that the referendum vote was scheduled for December 17.[1]

## Associated violence

On November 8, 2023, [[kenneth-darlington|Kenneth Darlington]] shot and killed two protesters who were blockading a highway as part of the anti-mining demonstrations.[1] Carlson speculated that the killings were intended to intimidate Panamanian voters into leaving the mining contract intact.[1]
