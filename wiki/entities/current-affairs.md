# Current Affairs

Current Affairs is an American left-leaning magazine.[1] Badlands Media identifies it in connection with [[briahna-joy-gray|Briahna Joy Gray]], who serves as one of its contributing editors in addition to her broadcast work on [[the-hill|The Hill]]'s web series [[rising|Rising]].[1]

The magazine appears in Badlands Media's coverage as part of the media ecosystem of the anti-establishment left — the outlets and personalities that the publication argues are increasingly willing to break with Democratic Party orthodoxy on questions of war, empire and support for Israel.[1]
