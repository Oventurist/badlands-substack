# Temple Institute

The Temple Institute is an Israeli non-governmental organization dedicated to preparing for the construction of a new Jewish temple in Jerusalem, and is described in Badlands coverage as the NGO responsible for organizing the sacrificial [[red-heifer|red heifers]] and the construction of the new temple — a project that would require the destruction of the [[al-aqsa-mosque|Al-Aqsa Mosque]].[1]

## Red heifer program

In 2022, five red heifers arrived in Israel from a Texas ranch and were kept in an archaeological park next to Shilo, an Israeli settlement near the Palestinian city of Nablus described in the source reporting as illegal.[1] The Temple Institute imported the heifers for the eventual purpose of using them in a purification ritual, after years of searching for blemish-free cows without a stray white or black hair.[1] Advocates hold that the animals' eventual slaughter on the Mount of Olives would allow Jewish people to be purified so that they could perform rites and worship on the site of Al-Aqsa Mosque.[1]

In August 2024, activists from the Temple Institute were photographed practising the red heifer ritual in front of the Temple Mount.[1] Israeli journalist Yinon Magal posted the images with the comment that "Temple worshipers are now practising the mitzvah [religious duty] of a red cow in front of the Temple Mount, which will enable the return of purity and the observance of all the temple mitzvahs."[1]

## Badlands assessment

Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that he believed the Institute has nefarious origins but that he needed to dig deeper to validate that instinct, noting that the organization claims to be ancient and to be in possession of the Ark of the Covenant.[1] He argued that the people behind the effort are mystics who embrace Practical [[kabbalah|Kabbalah]], and that while they may genuinely believe they are about to summon their messiah, from a Christian standpoint they may in fact be attempting to summon the Antichrist.[1] He connected this reading to the importance of what he calls "Trump's Gambit" and to the counterinsurgency against [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] and his cohorts, whom he characterized as the same people who created [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]].[1]
