# Midjourney

Midjourney is a generative artificial intelligence imaging tool used to produce illustrations from text prompts.[1]

## Role in the 2024 journal retraction

In February 2024 Midjourney was identified as the tool used to create the figures in a fabricated paper published by the journal [[frontiers-in-cell-and-developmental-biology|Frontiers in Cell and Development Biology]], which claimed to depict the signalling pathway of sperm stem cells but instead showed a rat with a penis larger than its own body and four giant testicles.[1] The model labelled the diagram with nonexistent terms including "dissilced," "testtomcels" and "senctolic," and rendered "sterrn cells" being spooned from a Petri dish.[1]

The images passed peer review before scientists reading the published article recognised that nothing in them corresponded to known biology; the journal retracted the paper and apologised.[1] The episode became a widely cited example of generative AI exposing the weakness of academic quality control rather than merely polluting it.[1]

## Use by Badlands Media

Midjourney has also been used as an illustration tool by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributors. An August 2023 retrospective on the [[mar-a-lago-raid|Mar-a-Lago raid]] by [[alpha-warrior|Alpha Warrior]] carried the note that all artwork accompanying the piece was "generated using MidJourney AI Machine Learning."[2]
