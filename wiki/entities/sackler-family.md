# Sackler Family

The Sackler family are the owners and former controlling principals of [[purdue-pharma|Purdue Pharma, Inc.]], the manufacturer of prescription opioid products at the center of the American opioid epidemic.[1]

## $7.4 billion settlement

In late January 2025, Virginia Attorney General [[jason-miyares|Jason Miyares]] announced a $7.4 billion multistate settlement in principle with members of the Sackler family and Purdue Pharma over their leading role in creating and fueling the national opioid crisis.[1] Virginia alone was projected to receive up to approximately $107.4 million over fifteen years.[1] Miyares said the fight to secure the settlement "has been long and hard fought, but my office never gave up," and framed it as holding the family and the company accountable while ensuring that communities receive support to heal from opioid addiction.[1]

Under Sackler leadership, Purdue invented, manufactured and aggressively marketed opioid products for decades, fueling successive waves of addiction and overdose deaths across the United States.[1] The settlement terminates the Sacklers' control of Purdue and their ability to sell opioids in the United States, and directs funding to communities over the following fifteen years for opioid addiction treatment, prevention and recovery programs.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands commentary treated the settlement as a milestone in the fight against Big Pharma but criticized the absence of criminal prosecutions, arguing that no member of the family or the Purdue organization has been charged despite what the commentator characterized as crimes against humanity, and expressing hope that the civil judgment lays groundwork for future criminal charges.[1]
