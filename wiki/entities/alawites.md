# Alawites

The Alawites are a religious minority community in [[syria|Syria]], historically associated with the ruling establishment under [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]].[1]

## Role in the Syria balkanization thesis

In Badlands commentary accompanying the April 2025 report on Israeli–Turkish deconfliction talks, the Alawites were named alongside the [[druze|Druze]] and the [[kurds|Kurds]] as one of the competing ethnic groups whose historic rivalries the [[davids-corridor|David's Corridor]] strategy would exploit in order to balkanize Syria and prevent a unified coalition on the model of [[hezbollah|Hezbollah]] in southern Lebanon.[1]
