# Alawites

The Alawites are a religious community in [[syria]] and the community from which [[bashar-al-assad|Bashar al-Assad]] hails.

## In the corpus

assad day in damascus invokes the Alawites to rebut the Western media portrayal of Syrians as unanimously ecstatic at Assad's fall. While acknowledging that many Syrians welcomed the release of relatives from government prisons, the article insists that there are "other parts of the country, particularly religious strongholds that hold Assad in high regard, particularly Alawites, from whom Assad hails, along with other minorities like Christians who have historically been wary of Sunni Islamist opposition groups, and who have supported Assad due to fears of persecution under different rule."

The article adds that many of these groups are either withdrawing their support or adapting to the new political landscape, and treats their situation as the central unanswered question of the transition given the al-Qaeda lineage attributed to [[hayat-tahrir-al-sham]].
