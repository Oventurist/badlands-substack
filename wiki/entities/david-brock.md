# David Brock

David Brock is an American political operative and the founder of [[media-matters|Media Matters for America]], described by Time as "one of the most influential operatives in the Democratic Party."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) Badlands Media characterizes his career as a story of astounding yet confounding personal transformation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

## Conservative period

In his earlier and less well-known years, Brock wrote op-eds about "campus Marxists," defended Clarence Thomas during the sexual harassment allegations brought by Anita Hill — the subject of his book *The Real Anita Hill* — and instigated the episode known as "Troopergate," while remaining a harsh critic of both Bill and [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

## Reversal

As the Clinton years drew to a close Brock became a steadfast supporter of the Clinton machine.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) The turn came while he was writing *The Seduction of Hillary Rodham*, a book expected to be a hit piece in the manner of his earlier work but which proved largely sympathetic to its subject.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) In *Blinded by the Right* (2002) Brock claimed he had reached "a turning point," saying he had examined the charges against the Clintons, found no evidence of wrongdoing, and did not wish to make further misleading claims.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

Observers across the political spectrum remained skeptical. The conservative-libertarian magazine *National Review* proposed that because "no liberal source in the world would talk to Brock," he could not gather the material required for a proper takedown of Hillary Clinton, and further suggested that during the writing he had been "seduced" by Clinton adviser Sidney Blumenthal.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) Independent journalist [[glenn-greenwald|Glenn Greenwald]] remarked that "overnight he went from one of the most psychotic Hillary haters to someone who treats Hillary Clinton like a deity."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)

## Media Matters and Correct the Record

Independent journalists have theorized that Brock founded Media Matters to funnel money into the Democratic establishment and to create an effective media pressure group.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) He later spent millions establishing the super PAC [[correct-the-record|Correct the Record]], whose purpose was to scour the internet and confront users posting unflattering comments about Hillary Clinton.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism) The lawsuit brought by [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and the Texas investigation led by [[ken-paxton|Ken Paxton]] are described as a boomerang returning to Brock.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-cancer-on-modern-journalism)
