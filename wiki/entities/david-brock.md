# David Brock

**David Brock** is identified in the Badlands Media corpus as a Democratic Party operative and the founder of [[Media Matters]], which the source frames as a liberal activist group that has functioned for decades as one of the most influential organizations in American political media. Time magazine described Brock as "one of the most influential operatives in the Democratic Party."

## Early Career as Conservative Critic

The article recounts that Brock began his public career as a conservative writer, penning op-eds about "campus Marxists," rushing to the defense of Clarence Thomas amid sexual harassment allegations, and instigating "Troopergate" — all while serving as a tough critic of both Bill and [[Hillary Clinton]]. According to the source, Brock was writing a book entitled *The Seduction of Hillary Rodham* that was expected to be a hit piece in the style of his earlier writings on Anita Hill and Bill Clinton.

## Ideological Transformation

The source describes a dramatic shift in Brock's political alignment that occurred during the writing of *The Seduction of Hillary Rodham*. Rather than delivering the expected takedown, the book proved to be largely sympathetic to Mrs. Clinton. In his next book, *Blinded by the Right* (2002), Brock claimed he had reached a "turning point," alleging that he had thoroughly examined charges against the Clintons, found no evidence of wrongdoing, and did not want to continue making misleading claims.

The article notes that many onlookers on both sides of the political aisle remained skeptical of this conversion. The conservative magazine National Review proposed the theory that since "no liberal source in the world would talk to Brock," he could not collect the information necessary to erect a proper takedown of Hillary. National Review also suggested that while writing the book, Brock had been "seduced" by political operative Sidney Blumenthal, a senior advisor and general VIP in the Clinton world.

Journalist Glenn Greenwald is quoted in the source as saying: "I don't know what happened to David Brock, I don't know what was done to him, but overnight he went from one of the most psychotic Hillary haters to someone who treats Hillary Clinton like a deity."

## Founding of Media Matters

The source presents a theory among independent journalists that Brock founded [[Media Matters]] as a way to funnel as much money as possible into the Democratic establishment and to create an effective media pressure group. The organization would prove to be a powerful tool for what the article calls the "Clinton cartel": in 2008, when Hillary ran against [[Barack Obama]], and again in 2016, Media Matters fought "tooth and nail" to ensure her victory at any cost, attacking anyone who was even slightly critical of Clinton.

## Correct the Record and Digital Trolling

The article notes that Brock went so far as to spend millions creating a superPAC, [[Correct the Record]], with the sole purpose of scouring the internet and confronting anyone who posted unflattering comments about Hillary Clinton. According to a May 2016 *Los Angeles Times* report cited in the source, Correct the Record spent some $1 million to find and confront social media users who posted unflattering messages about Clinton, "in effect... spending a large sum of money to increase the amount of trolling that already exists online."
