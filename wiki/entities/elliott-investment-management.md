# Elliott Investment Management

Elliott Investment Management is an activist investment firm that took a controlling influence over the board of [[southwest-airlines|Southwest Airlines]] in 2024, a campaign Badlands commentators identified as the underlying cause of the carrier's abandonment of its signature open-seating and free-checked-bag policies.[1]

## Southwest Airlines campaign

Elliott disclosed in June 2024 that it had taken a $1.9 billion stake in Southwest Airlines, after which it sought to remove chief executive Bob Jordan and executive chairman Gary Kelly and to replace much of the carrier's board.[1] Southwest subsequently appointed six new independent directors effective November 1, five of whom were recommended by Elliott, as part of "cooperation and information sharing agreements" reached with the firm.[1] Kelly, a former Southwest CEO, left the board on that date earlier than previously planned.[1]

## Commentary

A Badlands News Brief contributor described the episode as a "hostile board takeover" and subsequent "activist direction of the company," arguing that Southwest's new leadership had chosen to destroy the most distinctive aspects of the airline's brand at precisely the moment competitors such as [[united-airlines|United Airlines]] were maneuvering against it.[1]
