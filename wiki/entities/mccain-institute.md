# McCain Institute

The McCain Institute is a US academic and policy institution based at Arizona State University, named after the late US senator [[john-mccain|John McCain]] of Arizona.[1]

## Profile

The institute carries the name of a senator described in coverage as a renowned foreign policy hawk who backed the Western-supported change of government that overthrew the Ukrainian government in 2014.[1] Among its programs is the Kissinger Fellowship, named for former US Secretary of State [[henry-kissinger|Henry Kissinger]].[1]

## 2025 Kissinger Fellowship

In January 2025 the McCain Institute announced that former Georgian President [[salome-zourabichvili|Salome Zourabichvili]] had been chosen for the 2025 Kissinger Fellowship, a decision announced in a statement issued on a Monday.[1] The appointment drew immediate criticism from Georgia's parliament speaker Shalva Papuashvili, who said Zourabichvili was going back to "the entity that employed her" and compared the fellowship to the academic position previously extended to former Georgian President Mikheil Saakashvili at Tufts University roughly twelve years earlier.[1] Badlands commentary framed the fellowship as evidence that the institute functions as a landing pad for pro-Western officials removed from power abroad.[1]
