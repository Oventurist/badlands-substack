# Mastercard

Mastercard is one of the two dominant American card-payment networks, alongside [[visa|Visa]], through which a large share of global consumer transactions is cleared.[1]

## Indonesian withdrawal

In March 2023 [[bank-of-indonesia|Bank Indonesia]] prepared to transition away from Mastercard and Visa while building an indigenous domestic payment platform, according to reporting by the Antara news agency.[1] President [[joko-widodo|Joko Widodo]] had urged local agencies and authorities the previous week to abandon international payment systems for cards authorised by Indonesian banks, calling the networks a "problem."[1]

The Indonesian government tied the move to the financial-sector sanctions imposed on [[russia|Russia]] by the United States, the European Union and their allies over the war in Ukraine, presenting domestic payment rails as protection against geopolitical upheaval.[1] Financial analyst [[kirk-elliott|Dr. Kirk Elliott]] treated the exit of a 278-million-person market as another domino in the unwinding of the American financial network effect.[1]
