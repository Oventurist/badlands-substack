# BeiDou Navigation Satellite System

The BeiDou Navigation Satellite System is a satellite-based radio navigation system owned and operated by the China National Space Administration.[1] It serves as [[china|China]]'s alternative to the American Global Positioning System and is a flagship element of Chinese technological export diplomacy.

## Arab participation

At the 10th ministerial meeting of the [[china-arab-states-cooperation-forum|China-Arab States Cooperation Forum]] in Beijing in May 2024, President [[xi-jinping|Xi Jinping]] announced that Arab states would invest in and participate in the development of the BeiDou system.[1] The announcement came alongside proposals for a joint space debris observation centre, the development of space aircraft for scientific and passenger missions, and cooperation in manned space flight with Arab countries.[1] Badlands commentary read the space and navigation agreements as evidence of a consolidating [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] building infrastructure independent of the Western bloc.[1]
