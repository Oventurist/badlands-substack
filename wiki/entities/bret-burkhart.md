# Bret Burkhart

Bret Burkhart is a radio host at [[kcbs-740-am|KCBS 740 AM]], a Bay Area station described as owned by billionaire [[george-soros|George Soros]].[1]

On January 26, 2025, Burkhart disclosed on air details about [[immigration-and-customs-enforcement|US Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] operations in San Jose, California, including the types of unmarked vehicles used by undercover agents and the specific locations where they were present.[1] He cited updates from social media posts by the [[rapid-response-network|Rapid Response Network]], an organization opposing deportation efforts.[1] Reporting noted that the broadcast might have endangered the agents' safety by potentially exposing their covert operations.[1]
