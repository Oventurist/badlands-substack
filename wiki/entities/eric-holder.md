# Eric Holder

Eric Holder served as Attorney General of the United States during the administration of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and subsequently became a leading Democratic figure in redistricting and voting-rights advocacy.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]] reports, quoting Politico, that Holder was among the "slate of experts" convened by Senate Democratic leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] in June 2026 to help senators prepare for possible disruptions around the 2026 midterm elections. The group also included the election attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], the election-law expert [[norman-eisen|Norman Eisen]], and the leaders of Democracy Forward and Protect Democracy; according to the report they helped prepare responses to three possible scenarios and improve coordination with state and local officials who oversee election administration and turnout operations. Badlands commentary frames this convening as a revival of the cross-institutional network described in the 2021 [[time-magazine|Time]] article on the "fortified" 2020 election.
