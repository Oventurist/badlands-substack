# Eric Holder

**Eric Holder** is a former United States Attorney General who served under President Barack Obama and has since been active in Democratic redistricting and election-law efforts.[1]

## 2026 midterm planning

In June 2026 Holder was among the experts convened by Senate Minority Leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] to prepare Democratic senators for a trio of possible election-disruption scenarios ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|midterm elections]].[1] The group also included Democratic election attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], election-law expert [[norm-eisen|Norm Eisen]], and the heads of [[democracy-forward|Democracy Forward]] and [[protect-democracy|Protect Democracy]], and worked on litigation, communications, and coordination with state and local election officials.[1] Badlands commentary treated the gathering as a revival of the 2020 [[shadow-cabal|shadow cabal]].[1]


## Democratic election integrity effort (2026)

In May 2026 President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said that Senate Democratic leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] was hiring Holder as part of a Democrat-led "Election Integrity Group" ahead of the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterms]], describing him as "famous for handing guns to Mexican cartels under the Barack Hussein Obama administration" and alleging the group would try to suppress Republican voters and interfere in the elections.[2] Trump announced an expanded Republican [[election-integrity-army|Election Integrity Army]] as a counter-effort.[2]

## Court-packing remarks and Trump's filibuster demand (December 2025)

On December 1, 2025, President Trump attacked Holder in a Truth Social post, calling him an "Obama sycophant" and citing a speech in which Holder said Democrats would pack the Supreme Court if given the chance.[3] Trump claimed the reported target had risen from fifteen to twenty-one "Radical Left Activist Judges" and used the claim to demand that Senate Republicans terminate the [[filibuster|filibuster]], arguing that doing so would produce midterm and 2028 presidential victories and prevent a January 30 government shutdown.[3]
