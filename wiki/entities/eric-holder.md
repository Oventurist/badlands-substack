# Eric Holder

Eric Holder served as Attorney General of the United States during the administration of [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]] and subsequently became a leading Democratic figure in redistricting and voting-rights advocacy.

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports, quoting Politico, that Holder was among the "slate of experts" convened by Senate Democratic leader [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] in June 2026 to help senators prepare for possible disruptions around the 2026 midterm elections. The group also included the election attorney [[marc-elias|Marc Elias]], the election-law expert [[norman-eisen|Norman Eisen]], and the leaders of Democracy Forward and Protect Democracy; according to the report they helped prepare responses to three possible scenarios and improve coordination with state and local officials who oversee election administration and turnout operations. Badlands commentary frames this convening as a revival of the cross-institutional network described in the 2021 [[time-magazine|Time]] article on the "fortified" 2020 election.


## The 2010 ES&S divestiture order

Badlands Brief: Elections are Fake; as is Trump's Friendship with Bibi recounts that the Obama-era [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] under Attorney General Holder forced [[election-systems-and-software|Election Systems & Software]] in 2010 to divest [[premier-election-solutions|Premier Election Solutions]], the former Diebold subsidiary ES&S had acquired six months earlier. The order required divestiture of all intellectual property associated with every version of the Premier systems, a perpetual license to the AutoMARK ballot marking device, and sale to a buyer approved by the department. That buyer was [[dominion-voting-systems|Dominion Voting Systems]]. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treats the order as the decisive act that consolidated Dominion's market position.
