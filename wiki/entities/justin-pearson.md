# Justin Pearson

Justin Pearson is a Democratic member of the Tennessee House of Representatives. In April 2023 he joined fellow Democratic state Representatives [[justin-jones|Justin Jones]] and [[gloria-johnson|Gloria Johnson]] in the state House chamber alongside protesters, chanting for gun-control legislation after the [[nashville-school-shooting|Nashville school shooting]] in which three children and three adults were killed.[1] The demonstration prompted a GOP-led move to expel the three Democrats from the House.[1]

Pearson and Jones were subsequently returned to office, with Jones voted back in roughly a week before his April 17, 2023 attempt to carry a child-size coffin into the Tennessee State Capitol during a gun-violence protest.[1]
