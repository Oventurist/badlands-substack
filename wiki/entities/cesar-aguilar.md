# Cesar Aguilar

Cesar Aguilar is a Democratic member of the Arizona House of Representatives who drew national attention in September 2023 after describing free speech as "very dangerous" during a state legislative hearing convened to examine free speech violations.[1]

## Remarks at the Arizona free speech hearing

Aguilar made the comment at a hearing held Tuesday, September 5, 2023, by the newly created [[arizona-house-ad-hoc-committee-on-oversight-accountability-and-big-tech|Arizona House Ad Hoc Committee on Oversight, Accountability, and Big Tech]], while confronting an expert witness over why the [[first-amendment|First Amendment]] does not permit censorship.[1] The remark was reported by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]], which characterized it as revealing the "true colors" of lawmakers who favor state regulation of speech.[1]

The committee itself had been formed to investigate censorship by big tech companies and government officials, including former Arizona Secretary of State [[katie-hobbs|Katie Hobbs]], who was accused of working with social media platforms to suppress critics and claims of fraud in the 2020 election.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands Media writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded that under the American conception of inalienable rights, government not only should not censor speech it deems false but constitutionally cannot, observing that "speech crimes were largely unthinkable just a few years ago, but it's a very different world now."[1]
