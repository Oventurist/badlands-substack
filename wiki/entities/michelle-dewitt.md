# Michelle Dewitt

Michelle Dewitt is the mother of [[decarlos-brown-jr|DeCarlos Brown Jr.]], the man charged with the August 22, 2025 stabbing death of Ukrainian refugee [[iryna-zarutska|Iryna Zarutska]] aboard a train in North Carolina.[1] Aged 51 at the time, Dewitt spoke publicly about her son and the killing in September 2025, telling the press that local authorities had failed Brown by turning him away when the family sought help for what she described as his schizophrenic fantasies.[1]

## Account of the mental health system

Dewitt said her son should never have been left to walk the streets as a free man given how rapidly his mental health was deteriorating, and that "He shouldn't have been released," referring to his January arrest after which he was freed on a written promise to appear in court.[1] She described the intake screening she encountered: "When you bring a person to mental health, and [they] tell you 'We don't have enough space' or 'Is he trying to kill himself or someone else?' and I say no, then there is nothing we can do, you need to get a court order."[1] She said Brown had become convinced that someone was controlling him with a "chip" in his brain.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] wrote that Dewitt's description of life with a schizophrenic loved one was accurate and mirrored her own family's experience with a brother-in-law who was turned away from an outpatient clinic after answering "no" to the same screening question and who died by suicide the same day.[1] Ashe argued that the bureaucratic approach to mental health care in the United States is inadequate, immoral, and a public safety threat, while nonetheless calling Brown a monster deserving of the death penalty and noting that even Dewitt said he should not have been walking free.[1]
