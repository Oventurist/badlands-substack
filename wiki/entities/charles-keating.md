# Charles Keating

Charles Keating was among the worst perpetrators of the [[savings-and-loan-crisis|savings and loan scandal]], remembered in the standard telling for defrauding little old ladies and for the campaign contributions that produced the [[keating-five|Keating Five]] affair.[1]

Financial writer [[martin-mayer|Martin Mayer]], whose book *The Greatest-Ever Bank Robbery* is cited approvingly by Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]], retold the standard S&L horror stories of Keating's frauds, crooked Texas daisy chains and self-dealing loans, but concentrated instead on the systemic failure in Washington.[1]

Lange treats the Keating story as the deliberate focus of the [[shadow-government|shadow government]]-controlled media's coverage, a distraction from the underlying money laundering operation: "Instead of wasting time on Charles Keating, I want to talk about someone nobody talks about" — meaning [[herman-k-beebe|Herman K. Beebe]].[1]
