# Cesar Sayoc

Cesar Sayoc is an American man who mailed functional pipe bombs to critics of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], including [[cnn|CNN]], and was sentenced in 2019 to 20 years in prison.[1]

## Invocation in the 2023 town hall debate

Sayoc's case was cited by [[the-daily-beast|The Daily Beast]] in its May 2023 criticism of CNN's decision to host a Republican presidential town hall with Trump.[1] The outlet argued that Trump's repeated characterization of CNN as "fake news" and of critical media as the "enemy of the people" had produced real-world consequences, pointing to the 2018 evacuation of CNN's New York headquarters over a bomb threat and to Sayoc's sentencing the following year.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] rejected that framing, describing the Daily Beast piece as indicative of "the most extreme of the subversive rot that nests at the heart of the [[media-industrial-complex|Media Industrial Complex]]."[1]
