# AFL-CIO

The American Federation of Labor and Congress of Industrial Organizations (AFL-CIO) is the largest labor federation in the United States. In the Badlands Media article A More Perfect (Labor) Union, the AFL-CIO is described as functioning as a covert agent of the U.S. foreign policy establishment through its international arm, the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]], which the article claims uses taxpayer funds to promote political and geopolitical objectives.

## Origins

The article notes that the AFL-CIO is the product of a 1955 merger between [[american-federation-of-labor|the American Federation of Labor (AFL)]] and [[congress-of-industrial-organizations|the Congress of Industrial Organizations (CIO)]], but traces the institution's role as a tool of covert American statecraft further back. As Allied forces liberated Western Europe, AFL leader [[matthew-woll|Matthew Woll]] and CIO president [[philip-murray|Philip Murray]] both viewed organized labor as a frontline in the ideological battle against Soviet influence — Woll as a staunch anti-communist, Murray as a pragmatic strategist.

## "AFL-CIA" partnership

Recognizing unions' credibility among workers, the [[cia|CIA]] forged a secret partnership with the AFL-CIO, earning it the nickname "AFL-CIA." The article quotes *[[jacobin|Jacobin]]* explaining the rationale: interventionists recognized the work the AFL had already done in Europe, and realized that if the CIA wanted to influence foreign labor movements, going through the AFL would be more successful because "workers in other countries would be more likely to trust fellow union members."

The article documents the AFL-CIO's role in covert statecraft across Guatemala, Honduras, the Dominican Republic, El Salvador, and Chile, involving propaganda dissemination, splitting unions, training anticommunist leaders, and supporting regime change. During the [[lane-kirkland|Lane Kirkland]] era (1979–1995), unions backed Reagan's counterinsurgency in Central America and trained Salvadoran death-squad affiliates via the [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|AIFLD]].
