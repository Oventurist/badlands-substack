# George Cabot

George Cabot was a New England merchant and Federalist politician described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as an opium-pushing leader of the [[essex-junto|Essex Junto]].[1] Ehret groups him with [[timothy-pickering|Timothy Pickering]], [[aaron-burr|Aaron Burr]], Col. [[james-wilkinson|James Wilkinson]] and Albert Gallatin as aristocratically minded traitors loyal to the British Empire who advanced a plot to break the republic into two separate confederacies under the guise that slave states and free states could not co-exist.[1]

Pickering addressed to Cabot one of the key surviving letters documenting the scheme, proposing that the British provinces in Canada and Nova Scotia might, with the assent of Great Britain, become members of a Northern League, and that a liberal treaty of amity and commerce would form a bond of union between Great Britain and the Northern confederacy "highly useful to both."[1]
