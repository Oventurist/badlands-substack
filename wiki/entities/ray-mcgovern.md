# Ray McGovern

Ray McGovern is a former [[cia|CIA]] analyst and frequent commentator on United States foreign policy and intelligence matters.[1]

## Commentary on Victoria Nuland's retirement

In March 2024, McGovern gave an interview to the Russian outlet Sputnik in which he speculated about the reasons behind [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]]'s departure from her post as Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs, the number three official at the [[state-department|State Department]].[1] While rumors suggested declining health, McGovern argued instead that the notoriously hawkish Nuland had become a liability at a moment when NATO and Russia were inching closer to direct nuclear-armed confrontation.[1] He said his "best guess here is that the CIA and the Defense Department and the NSA got this message around saying, 'look, Victoria's got her own agenda here.'"[1]

His remarks were highlighted by ZeroHedge, which framed the key question as why the retirement was announced when the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration was attempting to stay the course on Ukraine and was still seeking tens of billions of dollars in defense aid from Republicans in the House.[1]
