# United States European Command

US European Command (EUCOM) is the US combatant command responsible for the European theater, commanded in 2026 by General [[alexus-grynkewich|Alexus Grynkewich]].[1]

## Role in US–Russia dialogue

Under agreements reached at the second trilateral US–Ukraine–Russia meeting in Abu Dhabi on February 4–5, 2026, the United States and [[russia|Russia]] agreed to establish a military-to-military dialogue led by EUCOM's commander.[1] The communication channel had been suspended before the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] began and was characterized as crucial to achieving and maintaining peace.[1]
