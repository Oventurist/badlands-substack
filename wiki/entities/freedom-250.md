# Freedom 250

Freedom 250 is a national, self-described non-partisan organization created to lead the United States' 250th anniversary celebration, launched by President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] on December 18, 2025.[1] The group presents itself as a movement uniting Americans and "freedom-loving people around the world" to celebrate what it calls the Triumph of the American Spirit and to launch a "golden age."[1]

## Washington Monument illumination

On New Year's Eve 2025, Freedom 250 staged what it billed as the "opening signature moment of a year-long series of marquee national events celebrating the triumph of the American spirit," transforming the Washington Monument into what organizers described as the world's tallest birthday candle and using the monument as a projection surface to tell the nation's story "in a new and unexpected way."[1] Repeat performances were scheduled hourly on the hour from 7:00 PM to 11:30 PM on January 2, 3, 4 and 5, 2026, and a five-hour video of the ceremony was posted to the Freedom 250 website.[1] Fireworks at the Washington Monument rang in 2026 and kicked off the year's celebration of America's 250th birthday.[1]

## Mission and structure

Freedom 250 aims to collaborate with state governments, private companies, and civic organizations to spark renewed appreciation for the innovative and entrepreneurial spirit that has defined the nation, while helping lay the groundwork for the next quarter-millennium of independence and opportunity.[1] The effort is designed to coordinate celebrations in every state and internationally by engaging local communities and partners nationwide, working alongside the White House Task Force 250, federal agencies, and the America250 Commission to plan and execute commemorative events.[1] An event calendar published by the organization lists activities in all 50 states, including the July 1 opening of the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library in Medora, North Dakota.[1]

## Commentary

Writing in the Badlands Brief, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that Trump had described the Freedom 250 vision the previous year and called the New Year's celebration in Washington, D.C., a first, encouraging encounter with the organization's work, describing it as the "best transformation program ever."[1]

## December 2025 unveiling

On Thursday, December 18, 2025, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] officially unveiled the Freedom 250 slate of events on social media, outlining the festivities the [[white-house|White House]] is planning for the nation's 250th birthday.[2] Several activities are planned from June 25 to July 10, 2026, including a "Spirit of America" parade to honor fallen soldiers, a "Birthday Fireworks Celebration" on the National Mall promised to be the largest such display in the world, and a "Great American State Fair" featuring pavilions from all 50 states.[2] Trump had repeatedly said on the 2024 campaign trail that he would roll out a grand celebration comparable to past world's fairs.[2]

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that the first day of Freedom 250 coincides with the first day of GART in Deadwood, followed a week later by the Fourth of July, with the party continuing for six more days.[2] Responding to Democrats' comparison of the celebration to the Hunger Games, she argued that in that story both the good-guy and bad-guy authoritarians are eliminated in the final games, leaving liberty in the land — and that if the analogy holds, Trump is not Snow but Katniss.[2]
