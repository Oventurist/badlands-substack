# Daniel Noboa

Daniel Noboa is the president of [[ecuador|Ecuador]].[1] His administration has made a military-backed campaign against organized crime its central focus.[1]

In March 2026, the United States opened its first [[fbi|Federal Bureau of Investigation]] office in Ecuador, intended to strengthen cooperation with Ecuadorian authorities on drug and weapons trafficking, money laundering, and terrorism financing.[1] The office opened against the backdrop of Noboa's security campaign and followed a joint operation between Ecuadorian authorities and US officials targeting drug trafficking networks operating inside the country.[1] Ecuador's interior minister, [[john-reimberg|John Reimberg]], said the key change was the permanent presence of FBI agents working directly with a newly formed unit inside Ecuador's national police.[1]

## Embassy raid and the Glas affair (2024)

In April 2024 Noboa's government sent Ecuadorian special forces into the
Mexican embassy in Quito to seize former Vice-President
[[jorge-glas|Jorge Glas]], whom Mexico had granted political asylum, blowing up
diplomatic relations with [[mexico|Mexico]].[2] Ecuador's Presidency stated
that "Ecuador is a sovereign nation and we are not going to allow any criminal
to stay free," while Mexican President
[[andres-manuel-lopez-obrador|Andrés Manuel López Obrador]] condemned the raid
as a flagrant violation of international law.[2]

## Biography and US ties

Noboa was elected in October 2023, a week after the Hamas attack on
[[israel|Israel]] dominated international headlines.[2] He was born in Miami,
is the son of a billionaire, and studied at NYU's Stern (BA), Northwestern's
Kellogg (MBA) and George Washington (Masters in Political Communication and
Strategic Governance), as well as at Harvard.[2] His wife is described as an
Italian-Ecuadorian socialite and influencer who modeled for Herbal Essences and
owns a fitness center in Tampa, Florida.[2]

The [[white-house|White House]] announced in January 2024 that the United
States was facilitating delivery of over 20,000 bulletproof vests and more than
$1 million worth of security and emergency response equipment to Ecuador,
increasing FBI personnel in-country to support the Ecuadorian National Police
and Attorney General's Office, and deploying
[[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] personnel
to train police and prosecutors.[2] In March 2024 Noboa visited New Jersey,
spoke with Ecuadorian immigrants at New Jersey City University and received a
key to Jersey City from a city councilwoman; the university tied the event to a
memorandum of understanding with GLACO, an alliance of 17 Latin American
consulates in New Jersey.[2] Badlands contributor
[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] noted that although the White House had
sent delegations to Noboa's November 2023 inauguration and to Ecuador in
February 2024 to discuss the illicit drug trade, it did not acknowledge the
March 2024 visit at all, concluding that Noboa "is a bit glowy and appears to
be a Biden ally."[2]

## Presidential delegation to the 2025 inauguration

[[donald-trump|President Donald J. Trump]] announced on May 21, 2025 the designation of a Presidential Delegation to Quito, [[ecuador|Ecuador]] to attend Noboa's presidential inauguration on May 24, 2025.[3] The delegation was led by [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], the U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services.[3]
