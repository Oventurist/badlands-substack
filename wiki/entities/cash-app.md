# Cash App

Cash App is the peer-to-peer payments application developed by [[square-inc|Square, Inc.]] and carried into its successor company [[block-inc|Block]].[1] It allows users to send and receive money for free through a mobile application, and, in Block's later product line, to invest, spend, send or store their money.[1]

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] cites Cash App as a concrete example of the [[peer-to-peer-payments|peer-to-peer]] services that let people transfer money directly through their phones without the permission of third-party intermediaries, a capability he identifies with the [[decentralization|decentralization]] of banking and the return of financial power to ordinary people.[1] Cash App is grouped with [[jack-dorsey|Jack Dorsey]]'s wider bets on [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]] and open-source development as part of an ecosystem intended to connect and enable people worldwide.[1]
