# U.S. Chamber of Commerce

The **U.S. Chamber of Commerce** is characterised by Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] as a [[cia|CIA]]-controlled body representing the corporate interests aligned with the Atlanticist foreign policy establishment.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)

Because an incredible amount of money was on the line in the [[joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action|Iran Deal]] — with [[blackrock|BlackRock]] holding large equity stakes in [[chevron|Chevron]], Exxon, BP and Shell, all of which stood to gain from sanctions relief — DeLarme argues that when [[israel|Israel]] backed [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], it "naturally caused the CIA-controlled Chamber of Commerce to turn on Israel."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away) He lists the Chamber of Commerce companies alongside the world's largest energy firms, Qatar, [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]], Russia, [[communist-china|China]], [[pakistan|Pakistan]] and India as parties with a vested interest in how the Iran question plays out.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/and-iran-iran-so-far-away)
