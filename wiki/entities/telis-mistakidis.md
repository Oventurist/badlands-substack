# Telis Mistakidis

Telis Mistakidis is a former senior executive of the commodities trader and miner [[glencore|Glencore]] who retired in December 2018.[1] His exit is treated in Badlands commentary as part of a cluster of departures that also included [[ivan-glasenberg|Ivan Glasenberg]] in July 2021 and oil executive [[alex-beard|Alex Beard]] in 2019, all set against Glencore's international bribery and corruption investigations.[1]

## Board roles

Mistakidis served as a board member for [[katanga-mining|Katanga Mining]], the company associated with Israeli billionaire [[dan-gertler|Dan Gertler]], and also for [[mopani-mining|Mopani Copper Mines Limited]], which was lawfully acquired by [[international-resources-holding|International Resources Holding]] in March 2024.[1]

Citing a 2018 Bloomberg feature, Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] argued that Gertler, Mistakidis, and Glasenberg all worked closely together to exploit the Congolese people in their mining operations, and treated the interlocking directorships as evidence of how tightly connected the network of commodity trading figures had become.[1]
