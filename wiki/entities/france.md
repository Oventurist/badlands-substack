# France

France is a European state whose historical and intelligence presence in the Sahel is a recurring theme in Badlands Brief coverage of West Africa.

## Role in the Sahel

The Brief reports that [[burkina-faso|Burkina Faso]]'s Captain [[ibrahim-traore|Ibrahim Traoré]] alleged receiving a 2023 letter from a French national whom he associated with France's external intelligence agency, and that Traoré's broader accusations against Western intelligence services were delivered against a backdrop of strained relations following the expulsion of French troops from the country. Burkina Faso's subsequent security cooperation with Russia, [[mali]] and [[niger]] is presented as part of the same rupture, as is the expulsion of two [[european-union|European Union]] diplomats declared persona non grata. Traoré provided no evidence for the allegations involving the French national, and the source notes this.
