# Asian-American Free Labor Institute

The Asian-American Free Labor Institute (AAFLI) was an arm of the [[afl-cio|AFL-CIO]] operating in Asia during the Cold War.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) The article states it received [[pentagon|Department of Defense]] contracts to counter communist unions in the Philippines and Indonesia, and that it even collaborated with death squads against the left-leaning KMU federation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)

AAFLI's work formed part of a broader DoD use of unions in Asia and Africa for "hearts and minds" activity during Vietnam-era operations.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union) In 1997 it was merged, along with the [[american-institute-for-free-labor-development|American Institute for Free Labor Development]], the African-American Labor Institute and the Free Trade Union Institute, into the [[solidarity-center|Solidarity Center]].[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-more-perfect-labor-union)
