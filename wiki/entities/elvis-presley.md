# Elvis Presley

Elvis Presley was the American singer who became the defining figure of early rock and roll. In Badlands Media commentary he serves as a benchmark of authentic, non-manufactured popular culture.[1]

## Contrast with manufactured acts

In "The Media is the Enemy of the People" (September 17, 2024), [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that "God created Elvis Presley," setting him against [[the-beatles|the Beatles]] and [[the-rolling-stones|the Rolling Stones]], which he claimed were created by [[mi6|MI6]] and the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]].[1] Carlson's rationale was that only God can create while the Devil can only imitate, and that the Beatles were an imitation of Presley — a point he supported by quoting John Lennon's statement, "without Elvis, there would be no Beatles."[1]

Carlson also drew a religious contrast, writing that Presley spent his evenings singing Gospel music into the late hours, whereas he could not recall a Beatle ever mentioning the Judeo-Christian God worshipped by most Americans.[1]
