# Douglas Wise

Douglas Wise is a former deputy director of the [[defense-intelligence-agency|Defense Intelligence Agency]] and one of the 51 former intelligence officials who signed the October 19, 2020 public letter asserting that the [[new-york-post|New York Post]]'s reporting on the [[hunter-biden-laptop|Hunter Biden laptop]] bore the hallmarks of a Russian disinformation operation.[1]

## Admission on the laptop letter

In reporting published on January 16, 2023, the New York Post revealed that Wise had acknowledged he knew at the time that a "significant portion" of the recovered laptop files "had to be real," yet signed the letter anyway and did not regret dismissing the exposé.[1] "All of us figured that a significant portion of that content had to be real to make any Russian disinformation credible," Wise said.[1] The letter had been issued five days after the Post began its series on [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s overseas business dealings.[1]

Wise did not respond when the Post sought an explanation in March 2022, but spoke about the matter to the Australian newspaper The Australian in January 2023.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the admission as a case study in what he calls [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]], noting that the Hunter Biden laptop was probably one of the least damaging of the several "laptops" the Truth Community had tracked but the one most ready for public consumption in terms of the corruption it allegedly revealed about the would-be first family.[1] He drew attention to the timing of the admission relative to the concurrent [[fbi|FBI]] and Biden documents stories.[1]
