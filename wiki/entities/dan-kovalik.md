# Dan Kovalik

Dan Kovalik is an American lawyer and self-described human rights advocate who drew national attention in October 2023 after being forcibly removed from a campaign event held by Senator [[john-fetterman|John Fetterman]].[1]

## Fetterman campaign event removal

On Sunday, October 29, 2023, Kovalik approached Fetterman at a campaign event and asked why the senator opposed a ceasefire in the Gaza Strip; he was shoved out of the event and posted video of the altercation to his profile on X shortly afterward.[1]

## Background and affiliations

According to his own website, Kovalik is a "lawyer and human rights advocate" who "currently" teaches courses on international human rights law at the University of Pittsburgh's law school.[1] The university told Insider that his contract had expired in June, was not renewed, and that he was no longer affiliated with the institution.[1]

Kovalik also writes occasional op-eds for [[russia-today|Russia Today]], the Russian state-funded media group that the [[state-department|US Department of State]] had recently described as a critical element of Russia's "disinformation and propaganda ecosystem."[1] In that outlet he raged against "Ukraine's atrocities," called Western liberals "woke and war-crazed" in an essay, and vilified transgender people.[1]

## Commentary

Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] used the episode to argue that, amid intense tension within the [[truth-community|Truth Community]] over the Israel conflict, the worst thing individuals or the decentralized collective could do was shame or discourage the earnest asking of questions of any party on the game board.[1] He observed that when Establishment reactions mirror the sentiments of supposed America First journalists in what Badlands calls Con Inc., [[just-human|Just Human]]'s maxim that "Understanding > Reacting" was made for such times.[1]
