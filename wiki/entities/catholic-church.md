# Catholic Church

The Catholic Church is the worldwide Christian communion headed by the Pope and administered through the Holy See, with a parish and charitable infrastructure present in nearly every country.

## Role as a humanitarian channel in Cuba

In the Badlands Media corpus the Church appears as a non-state distribution channel in the second [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration's Cuba policy. According to the Badlands Brief of May 29, 2026, while the administration methodically constricts [[cuba|Cuba]]'s economy through secondary sanctions on [[gaesa|GAESA]] and related measures, senior officials say limited humanitarian assistance is being routed through non-government channels such as the Catholic Church and charities. The stated purpose is to sustain the population while demonstrating alternatives to the existing system, so that a peaceful transition becomes possible without an abrupt collapse of basic services. This positions the Church as one of the few institutions in Cuba with sufficient independent reach to substitute for state provision, and gives it a substantive role within the strategy described internally as "[[accelerationism|accelerationism]]."
