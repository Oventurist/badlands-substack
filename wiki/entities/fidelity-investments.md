# Fidelity Investments

Fidelity Investments is one of the largest asset managers and brokerage firms in the United States, and through its donor-advised fund arm it is also among the largest conduits of American charitable giving. Its scale places it, alongside [[vanguard-group|Vanguard Group]] and [[blackrock|BlackRock]], at the center of Badlands commentary about concentrated financial power and the political direction of corporate philanthropy.

## Suspension of SPLC donations

The May 1, 2026 Badlands Brief, citing ZeroHedge, reported that Fidelity and Vanguard had paused charitable contributions to the [[southern-poverty-law-center|Southern Poverty Law Center]] following a federal indictment involving an individual allegedly connected to the organization. The Brief framed the decision as evidence of reputational-risk concern among major financial institutions at a moment of increasing scrutiny over where corporate philanthropy is directed.

The SPLC itself had not been charged as an organization, but the association was sufficient to prompt a reassessment by donors wary of legal and public-relations fallout. The Brief suggested the development could have broader implications as large asset managers face mounting pressure to justify both their claims of political neutrality and their charitable affiliations.
