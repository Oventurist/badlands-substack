# Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center

The Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center (MS-ISAC) is a body that has historically provided cybersecurity monitoring and related services to state and local government jurisdictions in the United States, including election administrators.[1]

## Loss of federal funding (2025)

By September 2025, MS-ISAC was no longer able to monitor every local jurisdiction's cybersecurity at no cost, and had begun asking jurisdictions to pay membership fees instead.[1] Badlands contributor Ashe in America cited the change as evidence that, beyond the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]]'s voter-roll lawsuits brought under [[executive-order-14248|Executive Order 14248]], a broader defunding of the election bureaucracy was under way.[1] She characterized the organization's appeal for fees as "laughable to us" but "existential for them."[1]
