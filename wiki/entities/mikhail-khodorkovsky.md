# Mikhail Khodorkovsky

Mikhail Khodorkovsky is a Russian businessman, formerly head of the Yukos oil company, who was imprisoned in Russia and subsequently lived in exile in London. In the Badlands Media essay A Re Alignment of World Systems, he appears among the oligarchs produced by post-Soviet privatization and among the contacts of [[chrystia-freeland|Chrystia Freeland]].

## Role in the source

The article lists Khodorkovsky in its catalogue of oligarchs created by the reforms of the 1990s, and states that Freeland "had made high level contacts with those Ukrainian, Russian and Western oligarchs mentioned above, including [[viktor-pinchuk|Viktor Pinchuk]] and Mikhail Khodorkovsky."

A footnote in the essay observes that when [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]] "began exiling many of these unrepentant oligarchs, they quickly made their way to London where many became disposable playthings of the British Empire," a milieu the article associates with the [[city-of-london|City of London]] and the district nicknamed "Moscow on Thames."
