# Kalyn Free

Kalyn Free is an Oklahoma member of the [[democratic-national-committee|Democratic National Committee]] who triggered the 2025 re-run of the DNC vice chair elections.[1]

## DNC vice chair challenge

Free ran for a DNC vice chair position and lost. In late February 2025 she filed a formal challenge to the way the vice chair election had been conducted, alleging in her letter that the process unfairly gave the male candidates an advantage over the female vice chair candidates.[1]

The DNC Credentials Committee decided in May 2025 to move forward with the challenge and put the question to the full committee, which voted 294–99 to redo the elections for the seats held by [[david-hogg|David Hogg]] and [[malcolm-kenyatta|Malcolm Kenyatta]].[1] The remedy adopted was two separate virtual ballots — one from June 12 to 14 restricted to a male vice chair, and one from June 15 to 17 open to candidates of any gender.[1]

## Reception

Badlands commentary was scathing. [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that claiming an election loss due to "internalized misogyny in the system is very silly," and that caving to such a claim by holding a new election "to appease the loser's feelings is just plain sad," urging that arguments about corrupted elections be grounded in stronger evidence.[1]
