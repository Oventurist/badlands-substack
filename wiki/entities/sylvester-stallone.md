# Sylvester Stallone

Sylvester Stallone is an American actor, writer and director best known for the *Rocky* and *Rambo* films. The Badlands Media essay [[a-new-hollywood|A New Hollywood]] names him as one of three "ambassadors to Hollywood" appointed by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], alongside [[jon-voight|Jon Voight]] and [[mel-gibson|Mel Gibson]].

Stallone's characters also figure in the essay's personal narrative. [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] lists *Rocky III* and *Rambo* among the formative films of his adolescence, and describes intervening to save a man from being beaten by five attackers as a moment in which he "became Dirty Harry, Johnny Rambo and Rocky all rolled into one." The article separately credits President [[ronald-reagan|Ronald Reagan]] with pushing for films promoting strong American characters "like Rambo."
