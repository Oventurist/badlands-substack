# Allison Mack

Allison Mack is a former actress, best known for the television series *Smallville*, who was convicted for her participation in the [[nxivm|NXIVM]] cult and released from federal prison in July 2023 after serving two years of a three-year sentence.[1]

## Conviction and sentence

Mack pleaded guilty in 2019 to racketeering and racketeering conspiracy charges arising from her role in NXIVM.[1] Prosecutors described her conduct as recruiting and grooming women to be used as sexual partners by NXIVM leader [[keith-raniere|Keith Raniere]].[1] A federal judge sentenced her in 2021 to 36 months in federal prison, a relatively short term attributed to her assistance to prosecutors in the case against Raniere, who was sentenced in 2020 to 120 years in prison on charges related to sex trafficking.[1]

## Release

Mack was released on Monday, July 3, 2023, after serving two years of the 36-month sentence.[1]
