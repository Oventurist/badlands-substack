# Kaitlan Collins

Kaitlan Collins is a [[cnn|CNN]] anchor whose program has served as a venue for Iran war commentary during the 2026 [[iran-war|Iran conflict]].[1]

## "Suicide dolphins" segment

In early May 2026, Collins hosted Carnegie fellow [[karim-sadjadpour|Karim Sadjadpour]], who relayed a [[wall-street-journal|Wall Street Journal]] report that [[iran|Iran]] was contemplating using "suicide dolphins" — dolphins fitted with mines — against U.S. warships.[1] The [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]] observed that Collins did not challenge the assertion at any point in the discussion, presenting the omission as characteristic of legacy-media deference to credentialed sources on war narratives.[1]

Badlands writers treated the segment as part of what they call the memetic dimension of the Iran war, in which both [[cnn|CNN]] and [[fox-news|Fox News]] amplified the same unverified claim.[1]

## 2023 Trump town hall

On May 10, 2023, Collins, then anchor of "CNN This Morning," moderated a 70-minute [[cnn|CNN]] town hall with former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] before New Hampshire GOP primary voters at [[saint-anselm-college|Saint Anselm College]].[2] She pressed Trump on whether he wanted Ukraine to successfully deter Russia; he declined to say who should prevail, responding that he wanted "everybody to stop dying" and that he would have the war settled in 24 hours.[2]

[[breitbart|Breitbart]] columnist [[john-nolte|John Nolte]] wrote that Trump had "exposed" Collins — whom he nicknamed "Nurse Ratched" — as a "mental lightweight," and framed the broadcast as a catastrophe for CNN rather than a win.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] argued that despite the "bitter pomp and circumstance" Collins tried to bring to the proceedings, the decisive outcome was that Trump came across as fun, funny and likable.[2] Burning Bright further contended that the segment exposed Collins, and the media by extension, as escalatory and inflammatory warmongers on the Ukraine question.[2]

## 2023 Trump town hall

Collins was named moderator of CNN's [[2023-cnn-trump-new-hampshire-town-hall|New Hampshire town hall]] with [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], announced in early May 2023 and scheduled for May 10 at St. Anselm's College in Goffstown, New Hampshire, at 9 p.m. Eastern.[3] In the format she was to oversee, Trump took questions from Republicans and undeclared voters planning to participate in the 2024 GOP primary — his first town-hall-style event of that campaign.[3]
