# Greece

Greece is a southeastern European state and [[nato|NATO]] member whose long-running rivalry with [[turkey|Türkiye]] over the Aegean Sea and the eastern Mediterranean gives it a role in the Badlands Media corpus.

## In the July 2026 Türkiye escalation thesis

The July 10, 2026 [[badlands-brief-0bd|Badlands Brief "Mossad Bait & Labor Tackles"]] reports contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]]'s account that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] had earlier that week identified Greece as one of Türkiye's intended targets. The author treats this as a datapoint completing a theory he had been developing over the preceding month: that following a Ukrainian drone strike on the Krasnodarskaya compressor station serving the [[blue-stream-pipeline|Blue Stream]] pipeline, a conflict between [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and Türkiye is now more likely than ever, "with Greece potentially coming to the aid of Ukraine."

In this reading Greece functions as the European state most readily drawn into hostilities with Türkiye, consistent with the author's assertion that [[israel|Israel]] "would love nothing more than to see European countries at war with Turkey." The commentary flags the island of [[cyprus|Cyprus]], the [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] pipeline across the Black Sea, and Türkiye's Aegean west coast as places to watch. These claims are the author's interpretation rather than reported events.
