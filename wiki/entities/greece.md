# Greece

Greece is a southeastern European and [[nato|NATO]] member state whose long-running rivalry with [[turkey|Türkiye]] in the Aegean and eastern Mediterranean features in Badlands analysis of a possible 2026 regional conflict.[1]

## 2026 escalation scenario

In July 2026, Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] identified Greece as one of Turkey's intended targets, a claim Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described as completing "a constellation of datapoints" for a theory he had been developing over the preceding month.[1] Under that theory, [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and Turkey could enter into some form of conflict, with Greece potentially coming to Ukraine's aid.[1]

Related watchpoints named in the same commentary include the island of [[cyprus|Cyprus]] in the eastern Mediterranean, the [[turkstream-pipeline|TurkStream]] pipeline across the Black Sea, and Turkey's west coast in the Aegean Sea as a potential site of a NATO/[[israel|Israeli]] operation.[1] The contributor concluded that Israel "would love nothing more than to see European countries at war with Turkey."[1]

## Security Agreement with Israel and the Turkish Feud (2026)

By June 2026 Greece had signed a security agreement with [[israel|Israel]] that Badlands writers described as forged over the two states' shared contempt for [[turkey|Turkey]].[2] Commentary in the Badlands Brief speculated that Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] might leverage the pact by having the Greek navy harass and provoke Turkish forces in the Aegean Sea and possibly around [[cyprus|Cyprus]], where Turkey maintains a territorial claim.[2] The strategic logic offered was that both Greece and Turkey belong to [[nato|NATO]], so a Greek–Turkish conflict would not invoke [[article-5|Article 5]], whereas a direct Israeli attack on Turkey would place Israel at war with the entire alliance.[2] The analysis traced the Greek–Turkish antagonism to the fall of Byzantine Constantinople to the Ottomans and floated a hypothetical three-way alignment of Israel, Greece and [[ukraine|Ukraine]] intended to encircle Turkey and divert its attention from [[lebanon|Lebanon]] and [[syria|Syria]].[2]
