# Boris Pistorius

Boris Pistorius is the Defense Minister of [[germany|Germany]].[1]

## Refusal to join the Strait of Hormuz coalition

In March 2026, when [[donald-trump|President Donald Trump]] called for an international coalition to secure the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] during the U.S. war with [[iran|Iran]], Pistorius questioned the point of sending European frigates when the powerful U.S. Navy was already involved.[1] His stance formed part of a broader refusal by [[nato|NATO]] allies — including France, Spain, Italy and [[greece|Greece]] — to participate in an operation multiple European governments described as "not our war."[1]

Trump criticized the reluctance as a "one-way street" but insisted the United States no longer needed or desired NATO assistance, claiming recent military successes had set Iran back by up to a decade.[1]

## "War in Europe" remarks (October 2023)

In late October 2023, German Defense Minister Pistorius said in a national broadcast interview that German residents must start getting used to the idea of the specter of war in Europe.[2] "We have to get used to the idea that there may be a threat of war in Europe," he said. "[[germany|Germany]] must be able to defend itself. We must be prepared for war."[2] He was responding to questions about Germany's slow rearmament in the wake of the Russian war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]], and about the prospect of the Gaza–[[israel|Israel]] conflict spilling over into a broader Middle East regional war.[2]

His comments echoed a narrative advanced from early September 2023 by top Ukrainian officials, notably [[aleksey-danilov|Aleksey Danilov]] of the [[national-security-and-defense-council-of-ukraine|National Security and Defense Council]], who declared that World War III had already entered an active phase.[2] [[badlands-media|Badlands]] commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] read the remarks as part of a series of lead-ups to potential "Scare Events" percolating in the [[collective-mind|Collective Mind]], noting that while the Israel conflict did not itself fit the bill, it could lead to the sort of crisis cascade that would.[2]
