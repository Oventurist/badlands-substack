# Boris Pistorius

Boris Pistorius is the Defense Minister of Germany.

In the March 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], Pistorius is one of the European officials who declined President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s call for an international coalition to secure the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] during the U.S. war with [[iran|Iran]]. According to the source, he questioned the point of sending European frigates to the waterway when the far more powerful U.S. Navy was already operating there.

His position placed Germany alongside France — whose president [[emmanuel-macron|Emmanuel Macron]] said his country was "not party to the conflict" — as well as Spain, Italy and Greece, all of which also declined. Trump characterized the collective reluctance of [[nato|NATO]] allies as a "one-way street" while insisting the United States no longer needed their assistance.
