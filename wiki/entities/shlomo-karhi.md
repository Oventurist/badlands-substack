# Shlomo Karhi

Shlomo Karhi is an Israeli politician who served as Communications Minister in the government of [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]].[1]

## Haaretz boycott proposal

In November 2024, Karhi authored the proposal that the Israeli Cabinet unanimously approved on a Sunday to sanction [[haaretz|Haaretz]], the country's oldest newspaper, over its critical coverage of the Israel–Hamas war.[1] The measure directed all government staffers and staffers of state-owned companies to end communications with Haaretz and to halt advertising and subscription purchases with the paper.[1]

Press-freedom advocates condemned the decision, framing it as part of a broader campaign by the Israeli government to silence outlets critical of its conduct in Gaza.[1] Jodie Ginsberg, chief executive of the [[committee-to-protect-journalists|Committee to Protect Journalists]], said her organization deplored the attempt to silence a respected Israeli outlet by hurting its advertising and subscription revenue.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry characterized the move as consistent with long-standing Israeli practice of regulating media reporting on government and military activity, and warned American readers against importing similar censorship norms at the expense of the Bill of Rights.[1]
