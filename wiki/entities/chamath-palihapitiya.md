# Chamath Palihapitiya

Chamath Palihapitiya is an American venture capitalist and technology investor associated with the Trump-aligned segment of Silicon Valley finance.[1]

## Open-source AI warning

In July 2026 Palihapitiya was among the venture capitalists and technology executives who criticized proposals that could limit [[open-source-artificial-intelligence|open-source artificial intelligence]] models, warning that a government ban on open-source AI would tank the market and crater both [[anthropic|Anthropic]] and [[openai|OpenAI]] — a claim reported by ZeroHedge and echoed in [[axios|Axios]] coverage.[1] His intervention was cited alongside members of the administration's Innovation Council in arguments that open-source development accelerates innovation, broadens American technological leadership, and prevents a small number of companies from controlling advanced AI capabilities.[1]
