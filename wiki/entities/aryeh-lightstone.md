# Aryeh Lightstone

Aryeh Lightstone is a figure associated with the [[board-of-peace|Gaza Peace Board]] framework who was named in Badlands commentary as a participant connected to talks held between representatives of [[hamas|Hamas]] and members of the board in March 2026.[1]

## Role in the Gaza talks

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted with evident irony that Hamas was reportedly represented at the meeting by "a guy named Aryeh Lightstone," joking that he was "like the Flash Gordon of Gaza."[1] The remark underscored the commentator's broader point that the transitional governance talks for Gaza were no longer being run by [[israel|Israel]] but by a coalition of nations seeking a deal with Hamas, with Israel only one participant among several.[1]

Details of the meeting's participants and outcomes were not fully disclosed in the underlying reporting, and Lightstone's precise function in the negotiations was not specified.[1]
