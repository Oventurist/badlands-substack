# Thames Valley Police

Thames Valley Police is the territorial police force responsible for Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire in England. In February 2026 the force became internationally prominent when it arrested [[prince-andrew|Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor]], formerly Prince Andrew, on suspicion of misconduct in public office.[1]

## Arrest of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor

The arrest took place on a Thursday morning that was also Andrew's 66th birthday, with officers searching properties in Berkshire and Norfolk.[1] Thames Valley Police said the arrest followed a "thorough assessment" and confirmed that an investigation had been opened.[1] Andrew was "released under investigation" that evening.[1]

The offence of [[misconduct-in-public-office|misconduct in public office]] applies to abuse or neglect of power by individuals in government and public service roles and carries a maximum sentence of life imprisonment, according to the [[crown-prosecution-service|Crown Prosecution Service]].[1]

The family of [[virginia-roberts-giuffre|Virginia Roberts Giuffre]] publicly thanked the force "for their investigation and arrest of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor."[1]
