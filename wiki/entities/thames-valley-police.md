# Thames Valley Police

Thames Valley Police is the territorial police force for the Thames Valley region of south-east England, covering the counties of Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire. In the Badlands corpus it appears as the force that arrested the former Prince Andrew in February 2026 in connection with the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] web.

## Arrest of the former Prince Andrew (February 2026)

The February 20, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that former Prince Andrew was arrested by Thames Valley Police on suspicion of misconduct in public office on Thursday, February 19, 2026 — his 66th birthday — as officers searched his properties in Berkshire and Norfolk. He was released under investigation the same evening. Thames Valley Police said the arrest followed a "thorough assessment" and confirmed that an investigation had been opened.

The force was subsequently thanked by name in a statement from the family of accuser [[virginia-giuffre|Virginia Roberts Giuffre]]: "On behalf of our sister, Virginia Roberts Giuffre, we extend our gratitude to the UK's Thames Valley Police for their investigation and arrest of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. He was never a prince."
