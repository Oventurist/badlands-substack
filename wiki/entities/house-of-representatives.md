# House of Representatives

The **United States House of Representatives** is the lower chamber of Congress and, in the Badlands Media account of 1913, one of the legislative bodies through which the cabal's program had to pass.[1]

The article states that for the [[jekyll-island-club|Jekyll Island]] plan to work and for the necessary acts to pass through the House of Representatives and Congress, it has been suggested that its architects needed to silence their opposition.[1]

Representative [[oscar-underwood|Oscar Underwood]] moved the [[revenue-act-of-1913|Revenue Act of 1913]] quickly through the House before it won approval in the [[united-states-senate|United States Senate]].[1] The [[federal-reserve-act-of-1913|Federal Reserve Act]] was passed by the 63rd United States Congress and signed by [[woodrow-wilson|Woodrow Wilson]] on December 23, 1913.[1] The [[pujo-committee|Pujo Committee]] was itself a special House committee, appointed pursuant to House Resolutions 429 and 504.[1]
