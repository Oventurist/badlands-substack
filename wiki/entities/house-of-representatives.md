# United States House of Representatives

The United States House of Representatives is the lower chamber of [[congress|Congress]] and, in 2026, a focal point of internal Democratic Party conflict following a series of primary upsets.[1]

## 2026 Democratic primary turmoil

House Democrats began reassessing their political strategy after Rep. [[diana-degette|Diana DeGette]] of Colorado lost her primary to [[justice-democrats|Justice Democrats]]-backed challenger [[melat-kiros|Melat Kiros]].[1] Members of the caucus described the upset as a warning sign that even established progressive incumbents are vulnerable to challenges from the left, while progressive organizations pledged to keep targeting additional Democratic incumbents.[1] Concurrently, reports indicated that [[kamala-harris|Kamala Harris]] had renewed outreach to Democratic donors and allies as she weighed her next political moves amid the party's shifting leadership landscape.[1]

## Ukraine funding controversy

Badlands commentary identified DeGette as the member who distributed the Ukraine flags waved on the House floor when Congress passed Ukraine assistance, characterizing the appropriation as "money laundering."[1] The episode is cited by critics as emblematic of the chamber's foreign-funding priorities heading into the [[2026-midterm-elections|2026 midterm elections]].[1]
