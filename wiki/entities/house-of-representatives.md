# United States House of Representatives

The United States House of Representatives is the lower chamber of [[congress|Congress]], composed of members apportioned among the states by population and elected to two-year terms. Legislation originating in the House must pass the [[senate|Senate]] before it can be presented to the President.

## Role in the SAVE America Act

The February 10, 2026 edition of the Badlands Brief, "Criminal Invader Crashouts & Honeypot Hoaxes," describes the House as the immediate arena for the [[save-america-act|SAVE America Act]] (H.R. 7296), the Republican bill that would amend the [[national-voter-registration-act|National Voter Registration Act of 1993]] to require documentary proof of United States citizenship for federal voter registration. The measure was introduced by Rep. [[chip-roy|Chip Roy]] on January 30, 2026, and referred to the House Committee on House Administration. As of the Brief's publication no final floor vote had occurred, though House leadership had taken procedural steps toward floor consideration and Roy stated that a vote would be held that week.

House Minority Leader [[hakeem-jeffries|Hakeem Jeffries]] publicly opposed the bill in a [[cnn|CNN]] interview, characterising it and comparable voter identification measures as voter suppression and accusing Republicans of attempting to rig or steal elections. Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded by citing polling showing majority support for photo identification requirements across partisan and demographic lines, and predicted that Democratic opposition would nonetheless be sufficient to make House Republicans abandon the effort.

## Role in Epstein-related investigations

The same edition reported that [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]] declined to answer questions during a congressional deposition, invoking her [[fifth-amendment|Fifth Amendment]] right against self-incrimination and stating that she would testify only if granted clemency or immunity. The deposition formed part of the ongoing congressional inquiry into matters connected to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], which the Brief described as stalled by witness silence.
