# Kelli Ward

Kelli Ward is an Arizona Republican who challenged Sen. [[john-mccain|John McCain]] in his Senate race and criticized him from the right on immigration.[1]

## Challenge to McCain

Ward attacked McCain's support for "comprehensive immigration reform," which she described as a code word for "amnesty."[1] Her challenge came in a state party environment already hostile to the incumbent: in 2014 the Republican Party of Arizona censured McCain for being too liberal on issues such as immigration.[1]

The Badlands account cites Ward's criticism as corroboration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s July 2015 charge, delivered at a Phoenix rally, that McCain was "very weak on immigration," and of Trump's prediction that "if the right person runs against John McCain, he will lose."[1]
