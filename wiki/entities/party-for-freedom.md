# Party for Freedom (PVV)

The Party for Freedom (Partij voor de Vrijheid, PVV) is a Dutch populist political party led by [[geert-wilders|Geert Wilders]], routinely described in international media as "far-right."[1]

## Platform

The PVV has promised to ban mosques in the Netherlands and campaigns for a far more restrictive immigration policy, positions that account for much of the hostile coverage it receives.[1]

## 2023 breakthrough

In the [[2023-dutch-general-election|2023 Dutch parliamentary elections]] the PVV came out on top, and was expected to win between 35 and 37 of the 150 seats in the Dutch House of Representatives, a sharp increase from the 16 seats it previously held.[1] Commentary carried by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] placed the win within a broader pattern of gains by parties on the so-called far right across Europe, and argued that the "far-right" label had been stretched to cover anyone dissenting from mainstream positions on war, taxation, climate policy and gender ideology.[1]
