# Regina Wallace-Jones

Regina Wallace-Jones is the chief executive officer of [[actblue|ActBlue]], the online fundraising platform used by Democratic candidates and progressive organisations.

The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that during a House Republican hearing that week into allegations of fraud, foreign contributions, and donor-verification failures at online fundraising platforms, Wallace-Jones repeatedly invoked her Fifth Amendment right against self-incrimination when questioned by lawmakers. The Badlands writer [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] interprets her invocation of the Fifth as confirmation that ActBlue is under active prosecution, writing that the House Republicans' earlier investigative claims "were telling the truth" and that "the matter isn't going away." Mainstream reporting cited in the same item noted that the hearing produced no new evidence of criminal wrongdoing.
