# Scott Shepard

Scott Shepard is a fellow at the [[national-center-for-public-policy-research|National Center for Public Policy Research]] (NCPPR) and director of the organization's [[free-enterprise-project|Free Enterprise Project]], a conservative shareholder activist group.[1]

## Campaign against ESG

Shepard told The Epoch Times in June 2023 that the tide was turning against environmental, social, and governance investing, arguing that the shift was broader than previous conservative efforts: "We're seeing something very different this time. Because it's not just the conservatives, who are always interested in this sort of thing, it's the whole country."[1]

He argued that [[esg|ESG]], which began as guidelines, had hardened into heavy-handed mandates rooted in controversial "social justice" ideologies.[1] In his framing, that shift created legal exposure: a potential breach of fiduciary responsibility to shareholders opens companies to litigation of the kind his organization had begun to pursue.[1]

Shepard's strategy depended on consumer pressure. Boycotts against corporations such as [[target|Target]] and [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch]] were described as the key to reversing race, gender, and environmental activism in corporate America, because customers abandoning companies pushing left-wing policies gave conservative groups the traction needed to fight those companies legally.[1]
