# Tennessee Highway Patrol

The Tennessee Highway Patrol (THP) is the state police agency of Tennessee. It entered national political debate in April 2025 through reporting by The Tennessee Star about a December 2022 traffic stop involving [[kilmar-abrego-garcia|Kilmar Abrego García]].[1]

## December 2022 traffic stop

According to sources cited by the Star, a THP officer stopped Abrego García on 6 December 2022 and immediately found that he was transporting seven passengers, with eight people in the vehicle in total.[1] During a nearly two-hour stop the officer determined that Abrego García was driving without a valid driver's license and suspected the group was engaged in human trafficking.[1]

Accounts conflicted over the watchlist question: one source said THP discovered Abrego García on a terrorist watch list but could not find him on a deportation list, while another said it was not Abrego García but one of the seven passengers who may have been listed.[1] Both accounts agreed that someone in the vehicle appeared on a terror watch list.[1]

## FBI involvement

THP contacted the [[fbi|FBI]] — then led by Director [[christopher-wray|Christopher Wray]] under the Biden administration — for guidance. The FBI instructed the troopers to photograph all eight occupants and document the vehicle's contents, and within roughly two hours requested that all eight be released. THP complied.[1] Badlands commentary presented the episode as evidence that Abrego García had been "protected by the system for years" prior to his 2025 deportation.[1]
