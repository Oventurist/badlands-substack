# Mark Hamill

Mark Hamill is an American actor best known for playing Luke Skywalker in the *Star Wars* films. He appears in Badlands Media commentary as an example of a performer whose casting is attributed to industry patronage rather than merit.[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] argued that "Mark Hamill didn't get the lead in the biggest movie franchise ever because he was a great actor," and invited readers to notice how intensely Hamill dislikes [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] "for no real reason."[1] The essay advances the general principle that "those who scream the loudest have the most to hide," treating loud celebrity opposition to Trump as a signal of complicity with the [[cabal|Cabal]].[1]

## Context

Hamill is used early in the essay to establish its premise that the entertainment industry has never been meritocratic, and that visible hostility to Trump among A-list entertainers helped the author identify who was inside the system.[1] The claims are opinion; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the views of contributing authors.[1]
