# Valerie Sinason

**Dr. Valerie Sinason** is a British psychotherapist and director of the Clinic for Dissociative Studies in London, known for treating patients who report [[satanic-ritual-abuse|Satanic Ritual Abuse]] and for challenging the clinical standing of [[false-memory-syndrome|"False Memory Syndrome"]].[1]

## Career and controversy

Badlands Media describes Sinason as having been caught up in psychological operations surrounding Satanic Ritual Abuse throughout her entire career, simply because she has continuously received patients who were SRA victims.[1] One of her clients alleges having been sexually abused by [[jimmy-savile|Jimmy Savile]] and a group of hooded occultists in a Satanic ritual conducted in the basement of a hospital where Savile volunteered and even had quarters.[1]

## Observations on language and Satanism

In a talk titled "Valerie Sinason 30 Years of Ritual Abuse Mind Control and Dissociation," Sinason points to the manipulation of language and the special treatment Satanism receives when it comes to child abuse.[1] The article credits her as one of the only professionals raising the question of why the public has been trained to accept ritual abuse from every religion except Satanism.[1]

## Memory in Dispute

Sinason confronts the concept of False Memory Syndrome in her book *Memory in Dispute*, arguing that the term and the organizations built around the alleged diagnosis have no foundation and serve only to protect abusers while supplying mainstream media with ammunition to discredit victims of ritual abuse and trauma-based mind control associated with [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]].[1]
