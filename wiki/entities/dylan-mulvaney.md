# Dylan Mulvaney

Dylan Mulvaney is a transgender TikTok influencer whose 2023 promotional partnership with [[bud-light|Bud Light]] set off a nationwide boycott of the brand and its parent company, [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch InBev]].[1]

The campaign featured a commemorative Bud Light can bearing Mulvaney's likeness, posted to social media, which the brewer later said had never been intended as a production can for general sale.[1] Boycott calls intensified rapidly, and Anheuser-Busch chief executive [[michel-doukeris|Michel Doukeris]] subsequently blamed social-media "misinformation" for the backlash rather than the marketing decision.[1]

Badlands Media commentator [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] wrote that the boycott followed directly from placing Mulvaney on Bud Light packaging in disregard of a customer base that rejects the transgender agenda, and criticized the company's attempt to reframe the reaction as a misinformation problem.[1]

## Continued fallout in on-premise sales

The marketing partnership between Mulvaney and [[bud-light|Bud Light]] on TikTok remained the identified cause of the brand's collapse in bar and restaurant sales through mid-2023, with industry point-of-sale data showing a 34 percent year-over-year drop in on-premise sales share since April 1, 2023.[2]

## Fallout from the Bud Light partnership (April 2023)

The transgender TikTok influencer's promotional partnership with [[bud-light|Bud Light]] continued to generate national coverage into late April 2023, and was directly cited as the trigger for [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch InBev]]'s abrupt change of the brand's marketing leadership roughly three weeks after the campaign launched.[3] Bud Light marketing vice president [[alissa-heinerscheid|Alissa Heinerscheid]], who had championed the repositioning, took a leave of absence and was replaced by [[todd-allen|Todd Allen]].[3] [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] characterized the campaign as an attempt to make Bud Light the "queen" of woke beers, and [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] noted that even the legacy financial press had begun describing it as a potentially catastrophic marketing blunder.[3]

## The Bud Light campaign and its cost (April 2023)

The partnership between [[bud-light|Bud Light]] and Mulvaney, then a 26-year-old transgender influencer, was announced on April 2, 2023 and included custom cans featuring Mulvaney face along with pro-LGBTQ language commemorating a year of Mulvaney publicly identifying as a woman.[4] The campaign launched with a video of Mulvaney drinking Bud Light in a bathtub.[4] Within six days parent company [[anheuser-busch-inbev|Anheuser-Busch InBev]] had lost more than $6 billion in market capitalisation, falling from $132.8 billion to as low as $125.7 billion as bar owners and distributors reported sharply lower Bud Light sales.[4] Anheuser-Busch publicly defended the partnership as one of hundreds of influencer arrangements used to connect with audiences across demographics, but reporting relayed by [[zerohedge|ZeroHedge]] indicated top executives had never approved the campaign.[4] See [[2023-bud-light-boycott|2023 Bud Light boycott]].[4]
