# Wiesław Kukuła

General Wiesław Kukuła is the chief of the general staff of the Polish Armed Forces. In July 2024, during the week of the [[2024-nato-washington-summit|NATO Washington Summit]], he became a focal point of escalation coverage when he publicly called on [[poland|Poland]] to prepare its soldiers for all-out war rather than the lower-intensity contingencies the force had been oriented toward.[1]

## July 2024 "full-scale conflict" remarks

At a press conference on Wednesday, July 10, 2024, Kukuła said: "Today, we need to prepare our forces for full-scale conflict, not an asymmetric-type conflict." He added that the shift "forces us to find a good balance between the border mission and maintaining the intensity of training in the army," referring to the competing demands of Poland's frontier security operations and combat readiness training.[1]

## Context

Kukuła's statement was reported alongside a cluster of announcements that observers characterized as a collective escalation with Russia at the [[nato|NATO]] summit: the United States unveiling dozens of additional anti-air defense systems for Ukraine, and a joint statement from Washington, The Hague and Copenhagen confirming that an initial batch of US-made F-16 fighter jets was en route to Ukraine — aircraft Moscow had already vowed to target.[1]

Commentary published by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] treated the Polish army chief's remarks as evidence that "the stage is set," pairing them with [[dmitry-medvedev|Dmitry Medvedev]]'s call for the demise of Ukraine or NATO "or — even better — both," and framing Poland's preparation for conflict with what the author called "their historic nemesis" as confirmation of the intentionality behind [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]]'s lengthy history lesson to Tucker Carlson.[1]
