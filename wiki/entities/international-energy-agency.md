# International Energy Agency

The International Energy Agency (IEA) is an intergovernmental organization that monitors global energy markets, coordinates emergency oil stock policy among member states, and publishes forecasts of supply and demand.

## Role in the 2026 European fuel warnings

In the context of the [[european-energy-crisis|European energy crisis]] described in the Badlands Brief of May 5, 2026, IEA executive director [[fatih-birol|Fatih Birol]] was cited by contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] as having said roughly two weeks before publication that [[european-union|Europe]] had about six weeks of jet fuel supply remaining before shortages would begin to appear. That estimate was used in the Brief to underline the severity of the continent's refined-fuel position amid restrictions on [[russia|Russian]] energy imports and constrained domestic production.
