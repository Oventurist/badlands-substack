# International Energy Agency

The International Energy Agency (IEA) is an intergovernmental energy body whose assessments are widely cited in debates over European fuel security.[1]

## Warnings on European jet fuel

In April 2026, IEA executive director Fatih Birol said Europe had about six weeks of jet fuel supply before shortages would begin, a statement highlighted by Badlands during the [[european-fuel-supply-crisis-2026|European fuel supply crisis]].[1] The warning framed commentary arguing that Europe's refusal to develop domestic oil and gas or to buy from [[russia|Russia]] had left the continent exposed.[1]
