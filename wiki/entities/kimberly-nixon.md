# Kimberly Nixon

Kimberly Nixon is a transgender person in [[canada|Canada]] whose request to serve as a peer rape counsellor is described in Badlands Media coverage as the origin point of the Canadian conflict between gender identity claims and women-only spaces.[1]

## The Vancouver Rape Relief case

According to a February 2024 essay by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]], the movement began in February 2007 when Nixon asked to be allowed to work as a peer-rape counsellor at the [[vancouver-rape-relief|Vancouver Rape Relief & Women's Shelter Society]], and accused the shelter of discrimination when refused.[1] The shelter was ultimately found not guilty of discrimination on the grounds of freedom of association, which permitted it to operate as a women-only venue regardless of gender identification.[1]

The verdict was nevertheless rendered moot in practice: the City of Vancouver withdrew municipal funding because the shelter did not satisfy its trans equity and inclusion requirements, and the shelter subsequently closed.[1] The essay presents the episode as an early demonstration that [[gender-ideology|gender ideology]] could override legal outcomes through administrative and funding pressure — a form of [[lawfare|lawfare]].[1]
