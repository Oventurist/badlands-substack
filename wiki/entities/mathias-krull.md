# Mathias Krull

Mathias Krull is a banker arrested in 2018 by [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Department of Justice for laundering billions of dollars embezzled from Venezuela's state oil company, [[pdvsa|PDVSA]].[1]

Krull was also laundering money for drug cartels. He pled guilty to all charges and agreed to cooperate fully in exchange for clemency on his ten-year prison sentence, ultimately receiving a 65% reduction.[1] According to Badlands writer [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], Krull's testimony led to massive asset forfeitures at a number of banks and financial institutions — notably [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]], which faced insolvency in 2023 and was acquired by [[ubs|UBS]] in a deal ostensibly forced by the Swiss government — with the money owed to the government of Venezuela.[1] The arrest is presented as the foundation of a broader Trump–[[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]] operation aimed at exposing high-level money laundering by the world's biggest banks.[1]

## Operation Money Flight

Krull's July 2018 arrest came under the DOJ's [[operation-money-flight|Operation Money Flight]], after investigators determined he had laundered $1.2 billion embezzled from PDVSA.[2] Badlands notes that while PDVSA investigations had been underway for years, the evidence trove supplied by [[nicolas-maduro|Nicolás Maduro]]'s March 2018 RICO lawsuit against the oil industry created a map leading to multiple raids in the United States and Europe, including Krull's.[2]

Krull also appears in a 2022 Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project investigation into Venezuelan elites hiding stolen oil money at Credit Suisse, where he provided insight on former PDVSA official [[nervis-villalobos|Nervis Villalobos]].[2] He became a cooperating witness for the DOJ in exchange for clemency on his ten-year sentence.[2]

## The keystone of the Venezuela case

Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] describes Krull as "the keystone" of the Venezuela corruption architecture, alongside [[executive-order-13850|Executive Order 13850]].[3] His arrest came four months after Swiss authorities raided [[helsinge|Helsinge]] in Geneva, when [[donald-trump|Trump]]'s [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] raided the firm's Miami office and charged Krull with laundering over $1.2 billion for oil companies, banks, and the cartel.[3] The sequence followed the March 2018 RICO-styled filings by [[nicolas-maduro|Maduro]]'s lawyer [[david-boies|David Boies]] against 42 entities.[3]
