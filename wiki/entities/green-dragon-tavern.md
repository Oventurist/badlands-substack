# Green Dragon Tavern

The **Green Dragon Tavern** is invoked in the Badlands Media essay "On Nations and Natural Law" (5 October 2025) as the emblematic setting of Colonial-era political conversation.[1] The essay asks the reader to imagine huddling in a darkened corner of the tavern with a group of common patriots, arguing that if the conversation turned political the odds are high that one or all of them would appeal to [[natural-law|Natural Law]] to decry tyranny and encourage resistance.[1]

The image is used to make the point that Natural Law was "baked into the common vernacular" of the period, to the extent that many of the [[founding-fathers|Founding Fathers]] made impassioned appeals to its principles in public debate and writing, and that its refrains were preserved in the founding documents — "the Laws of Nature and of Nature's God" and "certain inalienable rights endowed by our Creator."[1] The essay returns to the tavern in its conclusion, contending that those men, holding Natural Law at the tip of their minds during an epoch of tremendous social change, demonstrated what is possible when a people approach the creation of a government with the right mindset.[1]
