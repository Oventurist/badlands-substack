# Arseniy Yatsenyuk

**Arseniy Yatsenyuk** is the Ukrainian politician who became prime minister following the collapse of the [[viktor-yanukovych|Yanukovych]] government in February 2014. Badlands Media cites his installation as direct evidence of U.S. involvement in the [[euromaidan]] coup: [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]], then Assistant Secretary of State, was famously captured on tape discussing plans to install Yatsenyuk as prime minister.[1]

## Role in the 2014 transition

Yanukovych fled Kyiv on 22 February 2014 as his government collapsed, an event the article describes as the culmination of the coup rather than a spontaneous democratic transition.[1] The prior positioning of Yatsenyuk in the leaked Nuland conversation is treated as showing that the composition of the successor government had been decided in Washington rather than in Kyiv, exposing the [[military-industrial-complex|Military-Industrial Complex]]'s method of shaping post-revolutionary governments.[1] The Euromaidan sequence in which Yatsenyuk rose to power had been fuelled by years of funding from organizations such as the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|National Endowment for Democracy]] and the [[open-society-foundations|Open Society Foundations]], which financed activist training, opposition media, and protest organization.[1]
