# Simone Biles

Simone Biles is a gold medal-winning American gymnast and one of the most decorated Olympians in the sport's history.[1] She was among the hundreds of gymnasts sexually abused by former Team USA doctor Larry Nassar, and spoke out publicly against that abuse.[1]

## June 2025 dispute over transgender athletes

In June 2025 Biles entered a public dispute on X with conservative activist [[riley-gaines|Riley Gaines]] after Gaines criticized a Minnesota high school softball team that included transgender player Marissa Rothenberger.[1] Biles told Gaines she "should be uplifting the trans community and perhaps finding a way to make sports inclusive," suggested the creation of "a transgender category IN ALL sports," and accused Gaines of bullying, concluding that "no one in sports is safe with you around."[1]

Gaines responded by invoking Nassar's abuse of Biles and arguing that no woman bears responsibility for including men in women's spaces.[1] Badlands correspondent [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] criticized Biles's position as asking women to accommodate men at the expense of their own sense of safety, noted that Biles does not herself compete against transgender athletes and so "has no skin in the game," and predicted a future Democratic political career for her.[1]
