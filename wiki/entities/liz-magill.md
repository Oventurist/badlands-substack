# Liz Magill

Liz Magill was president of the [[university-of-pennsylvania|University of Pennsylvania]] until her resignation in December 2023 in the aftermath of congressional testimony on campus antisemitism.[1]

Magill was one of three university leaders—alongside [[claudine-gay|Claudine Gay]] of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]] and Sally Kornbluth of MIT—called before the House Committee on Education and the Workforce to testify about their institutions' responses to alleged incidents of antisemitism following the Israel–Hamas war.[1] All three gave widely criticized testimony in which they failed to state that calls for the genocide of Jews explicitly violated campus harassment and bullying codes.[1]

Magill stepped down first. Her departure shifted the spotlight to Gay and Kornbluth; Rep. [[elise-stefanik|Elise Stefanik]] marked the resignation on X with the post "One down. Two to go."[1] A bipartisan group of lawmakers subsequently sent a letter to the governing boards of Harvard, Penn, and MIT urging removal of the university leaders.[1]
