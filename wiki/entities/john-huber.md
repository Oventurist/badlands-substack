# John Huber

John Huber is the U.S. Attorney for Utah whom Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] appointed in November 2017 to conduct criminal investigations outside the normal Washington chain of command.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Mandate

Huber's first investigation was focused on the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]], which Badlands Media describes as the center of massive corruption leading to many crimes and treason.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Sessions informed Congress that the matters recommended for investigation by [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]], [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] and [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]] were "fully within the scope of [Huber's] existing mandate."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

Huber worked alongside Inspector General [[michael-horowitz|Michael Horowitz]], whose staff of 470 investigators gave him investigative resources far exceeding those of any special counsel.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) As a U.S. attorney, Huber possessed full authority to empanel a grand jury and file criminal charges — and because a grand jury can be empaneled anywhere, indictments could be handed down by citizens of deep-red Utah rather than by a District of Columbia jury pool.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Strategic significance

A [[qanon|Q]] drop of June 16, 2018 framed the structure as "IG = FBI, Huber = DOJ (no DC)," indicating that the Inspector General's office substituted for a compromised [[fbi|FBI]] in the investigative role while Huber substituted for the [[department-of-justice|DOJ]] in the prosecutorial role.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) Legal scholar Jonathan Turley called the arrangement "brilliant."1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2) The author argues this allowed genuine criminal investigations to proceed in the background throughout eight years of public narrative warfare over DOJ and FBI corruption, producing what he describes as hundreds of thousands of sealed indictments awaiting a future prosecution phase.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-for-sale-part-2)

## Role in the Mueller-era narrative

The Badlands article "Do You Believe in Coincidences?" reiterates that Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] appointed Huber as a special prosecutor in November 2017 without public announcement, pairing him with Inspector General [[michael-horowitz|Michael Horowitz]] to examine the [[carter-page-fisa-warrants|Carter Page FISA]] applications, Uranium One, the Clinton Foundation and Hillary Clinton's private server.[2] Crucially, Huber reported directly to Sessions rather than through Deputy Attorney General [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]], leaving the department's number two outside the loop.[2] The article says [[matthew-whitaker|Matthew Whitaker]], as Sessions's chief of staff, was already aware of the Huber inquiry and helped oversee it before becoming Acting Attorney General.[2] Q material quoted in the piece claims Huber was also using FISA warrants against the alleged conspirators — "FISA goes both ways" — and that the FISA court stayed silent about the discovered fraud because the investigations were shielded under a state-secrets designation.[2]

## Hand-off of the investigation

In "Disinformation is Necessary," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] argues that Huber's inquiry — described in Q material as the biggest criminal investigation in history, framed in RICO terms — was already complete by the time most observers were still asking about it.[3] Q drop 1318 (May 8, 2018) asked "Who is HUBER? Define Inspector General… Why did SESSIONS secretly engage HUBER? Why did SESSIONS reveal HUBER?" and immediately noted that the Department of Justice does not discuss ongoing investigations, a juxtaposition Lange treats as the clue that the inquiry had concluded.[3] Huber had already been investigating for many months before [[jeff-sessions|Sessions]] disclosed the assignment publicly.[3]

Lange contends Huber handed his findings off to other prosecutors: part to [[john-durham|John Durham]], appointed by [[william-barr|William Barr]] to investigate the conspiracy to frame [[donald-trump|Trump]], and part to a second prosecutor outside Washington whose existence is unknown to the public and who was given the same mandate and authority as Huber, conducting an even larger investigation with no leaks.[3] One of Huber's principal tasks, per a Q drop linked to a congressional hearing, was investigating corruption at the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]].[3] These claims reflect Badlands Media interpretation rather than established fact.

## The Uranium One assignment

In "The Biggest Cover-up in American History," [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] identifies Huber as the prosecutor to whom Attorney General [[jeff-sessions|Jeff Sessions]] quietly assigned the [[uranium-one-deal|Uranium One deal]] and related Clinton matters on November 13, 2017, five months before the assignment was disclosed to Congress.[4] Sessions informed [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]], [[bob-goodlatte|Bob Goodlatte]] and [[trey-gowdy|Trey Gowdy]] that all the matters for which they sought a special counsel were "fully within the scope of his existing mandate."[4] As a U.S. attorney Huber held full authority to empanel grand juries and file criminal charges anywhere in the country, which Lange argues allowed him to seat jurors in Utah rather than the District of Columbia, where [[john-durham|John Durham]] later struggled to obtain convictions.[4] Paired with Inspector General [[michael-horowitz|Michael Horowitz]] and his staff of 470 investigators, the arrangement gave the [[huber-investigation|Huber investigation]] more firepower than any special counsel would have had.[4] [[qanon|Q]] drop #1711 asked to whom Huber directly reported and why [[rod-rosenstein|Rod Rosenstein]] was side-stepped in his chain of command; Lange answers that Huber reported to Sessions, whose recusal covered only the Russia inquiry.[4] He further contends that Durham reserved the conspiracy charges for Huber and that the resulting [[racketeer-influenced-and-corrupt-organizations-act|RICO]] case produced the [[sealed-indictments|sealed indictments]] still awaiting unsealing.[4]
