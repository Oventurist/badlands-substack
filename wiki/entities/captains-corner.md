# Captain K's Corner

**Captain K's Corner** is the Substack journal published by election analyst [[seth-keshel|Seth Keshel]].[1]

Keshel describes it as a geopolitical journal that is now one of the most widely read in its genre on the entire Substack platform, landing in the inboxes of "many people at the very top of the food chain when it comes to running the United States of America."[1] He characterizes the publication as a massive archive of elections information, solutions, commentary and assorted content that a reader could spend weeks or months perusing to piece together the full picture.[1]

The journal hosts his state-by-state analyses of election administration — including separate anatomies of blue states and red states measured against his [[eight-cardinal-sins|Eight Cardinal Sins]] — as well as his proposals for executive action on elections and his argument that no [[election-reform-committee|Election Reform Committee]] had yet been announced as of February 2025.[1] When Keshel wrote a February 13, 2025 guest post for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], he framed it as a truncated distillation of that larger archive.[1]
