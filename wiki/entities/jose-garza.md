# Jose Garza

Jose Garza is the Travis County, Texas District Attorney who prosecuted former U.S. Army Sgt. [[daniel-perry|Daniel Perry]] for the shooting of an armed [[black-lives-matter|Black Lives Matter]] protester.[1]

When Governor [[greg-abbott|Greg Abbott]] pardoned Perry on May 16, 2024, the accompanying proclamation took aim at Garza, alleging that he had directed the lead investigator to withhold exculpatory evidence and had demonstrated "unethical and biased misuse" of his office in the prosecution.[1] Abbott separately described Texas as having one of the strongest "Stand Your Ground" self-defense laws, which "cannot be nullified by a jury or a progressive District Attorney."[1]

Garza's office responded by telling The Epoch Times that the [[texas-board-of-pardons-and-paroles|Board of Pardons and Paroles]] decision and the governor's pardon amounted to a "mockery of our legal system," adding that "their actions are contrary to the law and demonstrate that there are two classes of people in this state where some lives matter and some lives do not."[1]
