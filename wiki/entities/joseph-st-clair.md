# Joseph St. Clair

**Joseph "Joe" St. Clair** is a decorated U.S. Air Force veteran and language specialist who served four tours in Afghanistan and was detained in Venezuela for nearly a year before his release in May 2025.[1]

## Release

St. Clair was handed over to [[richard-grenell|Richard Grenell]], a former [[trump-administration|Trump administration]] envoy, during a diplomatic exchange on Tuesday, May 20, 2025, according to his family and U.S. officials. "Joe St. Clair is back in America," Grenell confirmed in a post on X. Grenell said he had met Venezuelan officials in a neutral country "to negotiate an America First strategy," adding that the outcome was "only possible because [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] puts Americans first."[1]
