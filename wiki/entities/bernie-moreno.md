# Bernie Moreno

Bernie Moreno is a Republican United States Senator who, as reported in the [[badlands-brief|Badlands Brief]], announced plans to introduce legislation imposing sanctions on [[canada|Canada]] and certain Canadian government officials in response to wildfire smoke that degraded air quality across portions of the United States.

## Proposed Canada sanctions

Speaking in a [[fox-news|Fox News]] interview, Moreno said the proposal would include sanctions, asset freezes, visa restrictions and tariffs. The measure was announced amid unhealthy air quality across parts of the Great Lakes, Ohio Valley, Mid-Atlantic and Northeast attributed to Canadian wildfire smoke, and alongside a letter from four Republican House members criticizing Canadian forest management policy. The Brief's contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] endorsed the proposal, writing that she "fully support[s] sanctions against Canada" and framing the dispute as an opportunity for President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] to confront what she calls the climate cult over its opposition to active forest management. Related reporting cited by the Brief noted operational changes at amusement parks including [[six-flags|Six Flags Great America]] and [[cedar-point|Cedar Point]].
