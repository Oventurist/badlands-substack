# Gabe Banks

Gabe Banks is an Atlanta-area attorney named in testimony concerning alleged witness intimidation in the Fulton County election-interference prosecution led by District Attorney [[fani-willis|Fani Willis]].[1]

## Alleged contact with Terrence Bradley

Defense attorney [[ashleigh-merchant|Ashleigh Merchant]] testified before the Georgia Senate Committee on Special Investigations that, after Willis herself telephoned [[terrence-bradley|Terrence Bradley]] in September 2023, Banks also called Bradley.[1] Merchant described Banks and Bradley as friends and stated that Banks' wife reportedly works for Willis.[1] She further testified that it was possible Banks himself held a contract with the District Attorney's office, though she said she was not certain of this.[1]

Merchant recounted that Bradley telephoned her after the Banks call and told her he believed Banks was attempting to silence him, describing the contact as a "first shot over the bow."[1] Merchant said Bradley was afraid and that she left a family gathering in order to speak with him.[1] A second episode Merchant characterized as intimidation involved [[nathan-wade|Nathan Wade]] contacting a lawyer friend of Bradley's and asking that Bradley be told to "remember his privilege."[1] The chairman of the Senate committee responded to this sequence by remarking, "Sounds like tampering with witnesses."[1]
