# Vladlen Tatarsky

Vladlen Tatarsky was a prominent Russian war blogger whose killing was cited, alongside the death of [[darya-dugina|Darya Dugina]], as evidence that assassination campaigns targeting high-profile Russian media figures during the war in [[ukraine|Ukraine]] are real rather than hypothetical.[1]

## Significance in the information war

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] invoked Tatarsky's death in July 2023 while assessing the [[fsb|FSB]]'s claim to have thwarted a plot against RT editor-in-chief [[margarita-simonyan|Margarita Simonyan]] and journalist [[ksenia-sobchak|Ksenia Sobchak]], arguing that in a conflict where narrative control is a primary objective, journalists whose reporting supports the opposing side are treated as enemy combatants.[1] The commentary noted that Dugina's murder had been covered and then "memory-holed," and framed such killings as a structural feature of modern [[information-war|information warfare]] rather than isolated incidents.[1]
