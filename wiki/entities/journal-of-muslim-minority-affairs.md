# Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs

The *Journal of Muslim Minority Affairs* (JMMA) is an academic journal devoted to the study of Muslim communities living in non-Muslim-majority countries.

## In the Badlands corpus

America for Sale   Part 1 characterizes the JMMA as "an international propaganda journal for the [[muslim-brotherhood|Muslim Brotherhood]]," founded in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia in the late 1970s with the help of [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]]'s father, Syed Abedin. The journal is published by the [[institute-of-muslim-minority-affairs|Institute of Muslim Minority Affairs]], which the article says was established by Al-Qaeda financier [[abdullah-omar-naseef|Abdullah Omar Naseef]], who recruited the Abedin family to run it.

Citing *Newsweek* and PJMedia, the article reports that Huma Abedin "was assistant editor of the journal for a dozen years — from the time she started interning at the Clinton White House in 1996 until right before she joined the [[state-department|State Department]] in 2009," and that during seven of those years she overlapped with Naseef, who was then on the journal's advisory board. When Syed Abedin died, Huma's mother Saleha took over its operations. The article argues that articles from the journal have been used in universities worldwide "to teach the promotion of sharia law and the supremacy of the Muslim religion," making the publication a core instrument of the Brotherhood's "civilization/stealth jihad" strategy of "infiltration, not invasion."
