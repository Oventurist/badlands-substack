# The Elections Group

The Elections Group is a non-governmental organization providing services and consulting to election administrators in the United States.[1] It appears in Badlands coverage as the organization with which consultant [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]] was affiliated at the time of an alleged Georgia recount irregularity, in which totals that reportedly did not match were sent to Macias and subsequently matched.[1]

Badlands treats the group as part of a broader NGO layer that intermediates between private actors and public election offices, a pattern illustrated by Macias later pitching Colorado clerks to fund a separate new NGO of his own while the Georgia allegation was outstanding.[1]

## Wisconsin connection (2026 commentary)

In May 2026, amid reports of an [[fbi|FBI]] inquiry into the administration of the 2020 election in Wisconsin, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] named [[ryan-chew|Ryan Chew]] of The Elections Group alongside former [[milwaukee-election-commission|Milwaukee Election Commission]] director [[claire-woodall-vogg|Claire Woodall-Vogg]] as figures who "need to be interrogated" over events at Milwaukee's [[milwaukee-central-count-facility|Central Count facility]].[2] She drew an explicit parallel to [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]], also of The Elections Group, who had been discussed weeks earlier in connection with Georgia's 2020 recount, and concluded that the recurrence of the organization's personnel indicated the "same sources and methods and actors across states."[2]
