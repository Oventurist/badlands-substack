# The Elections Group

The Elections Group is a non-governmental organization that provides consulting and technical assistance to election officials. It is referenced in the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026 as the organization with which consultant [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]] was affiliated at the time of an alleged Georgia recount irregularity described by [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].

In that account, recount totals reportedly did not match until they were sent to Macias, after which they did — an episode the commentator characterizes as willful election fraud. The Brief situates the organization within a broader critique of privately funded NGOs operating inside public election administration, noting that Macias was simultaneously soliciting Colorado clerks to fund a new NGO of his own.

## The December 2020 Georgia email

The January 23, 2026 Badlands Brief returns to the organization in the context of the [[georgia-state-election-board|Georgia State Election Board]] hearing of January 21, 2026. Investigator [[joe-rossi|Joe Rossi]] testified that a report run at 12:06 p.m. on Thursday, December 3, 2020 was emailed at 12:13 p.m. to [[ryan-macias|Ryan Macias]], whom Rossi said "maybe... worked for The Election Group," speculating that the email asked for help because the count stood at only 511. Combined with [[richard-barron|Richard Barron]]'s certification-week testimony that the county "knew we were short," the Brief concludes the counts were made to match on December 3, 2020 — a claim it presents as evidence of willful manipulation inside Georgia's election administration during the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 election]].
