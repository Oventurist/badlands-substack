# David M. Hoffman

David M. Hoffman is the American media producer credited with founding [[internews|Internews]], the media-development nonprofit that grew from a 1982 satellite-television project into a global organisation operating in more than 100 countries.[1]

## Founding of Internews

According to the origin story recounted in Badlands Media's "The Information Imperium," Hoffman launched the project in 1982, during the late Cold War, to link American and Soviet citizens through satellite television — the stated aim being to "bridge the Iron Curtain with dialogue."[1] By 1986 the effort had been incorporated as a full nonprofit advancing "access to information" as a human right.[1]

## Critical framing

The article treats Hoffman's humble-origins narrative skeptically, noting that the organisation he founded went on to become a global player funded overwhelmingly by [[usaid|USAID]] — $472.6 million over the decades — alongside grants from the [[national-endowment-for-democracy|NED]] and foundations including Ford, [[open-society-foundations|Open Society]] and [[rockefeller-foundation|Rockefeller]].[1] In that reading, the citizen-diplomacy framing of Internews' founding served as durable cover for an organisation whose journalist-training and "disinformation-fighting" programmes aligned closely with U.S. foreign-policy objectives.[1] Leadership passed to [[jeanne-bourgault|Jeanne Bourgault]] in 2011, who steered the organisation further into "information integrity" work.[1]
