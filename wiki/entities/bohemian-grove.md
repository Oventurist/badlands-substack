# Bohemian Grove

Bohemian Grove is a private Northern California campground long associated in alternative-media discourse with gatherings of political, business, and cultural elites, and a recurring subject of allegations concerning ritual behavior and abuse.[1]

## Ryan Garcia allegations (2024)

In March 2024, professional boxer [[ryan-garcia|Ryan Garcia]] made a widely circulated accusation that unnamed elites had tied him down and forced him to watch them rape children at Bohemian Grove, describing the claim during a Twitter Spaces appearance with [[andrew-tate|Andrew Tate]].[1] Garcia said, "They held me down, they made me watch little kids get raped," and, pressed by Tate on where this occurred, replied that "they fucking took me to the fucking woods," insisting he had proof and asserting that "Bohemian Grove is real."[1] The claims were reported by The Daily Caller.[1]

## Commentary in the Badlands corpus

[[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] wrote that Garcia — described as perhaps the most sensational young boxer in the world — had been speaking publicly all week about the ritualized abuse of children by "Elites," and that the accounts were gaining substantial traction.[1] Garcia claimed the displays were part of an attempt to bring him into "The Club" as his celebrity rose through viral fights and a large social media following.[1] Burning Bright characterized this subject as "the Darkest Path" that drew much of the Truth Community into the movement, while urging that Garcia's specific account be taken "with a metric ton of salt," judging it more likely to be obfuscation or fame-seeking than a genuine disclosure given its association with what he called "the Tate machismo."[1] He nonetheless argued that the mainstreaming of such narratives had measurable "Net Effects" on collective perception.[1]
