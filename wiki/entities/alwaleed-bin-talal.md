# Alwaleed bin Talal

Prince Alwaleed bin Talal is the Saudi royal and financier treated across the Badlands Media series as a central node of the network the author calls "the enemy."[1]

## Origins

Alwaleed was born in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia — a data point the article stresses, remarking that "a lot of roads lead to Jeddah."[1] His father, Talal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud, was a member of the Saudi royal family; his mother was Mona El Solh, daughter of Riad El Solh, the first Prime Minister of Lebanon.[1] The article emphasizes that from the outset Alwaleed possessed deep and wealthy connections in both Saudi Arabia and Lebanon.[1]

## Connection to "Basel"

The article draws a parallel between Alwaleed's background and that of [[basel-cpa-official|Basel]], the Coalition Provisional Authority official of Lebanese descent born in Saudi Arabia who received every cash flight in Baghdad, arguing the odds are high that Basel was affiliated with Alwaleed given Alwaleed's strong ties to the Bush family and the [[cia|CIA]].[1]

## Political influence

The article asserts that George W. Bush was "one of Alwaleed's biggest puppets," and that Alwaleed pumped money into the McCain Institute because [[john-mccain|John McCain]]'s value lay in conferring legitimacy on the militant groups the network wished to support.[1]
