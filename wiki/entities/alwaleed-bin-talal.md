# Alwaleed bin Talal

Alwaleed bin Talal is the Saudi billionaire investor and member of the royal family detained during the November 2017 consolidation carried out by [[mohammed-bin-salman]]. He appears in the Badlands Media essay [[arabian-nights]] as a contrast case in the article's reading of Western press coverage.

The source asks readers to contrast [[donald-trump]]'s relationship with the crown prince against his relationship with Alwaleed, and argues that the [[new-york-times]] "attempts to distinguish and segregate" Alwaleed from the Saudi royal family — treating him as a separate, cosmopolitan financial figure rather than as a royal. The article cites a November 4, 2017 New York Times piece on Alwaleed as evidence that even the mainstream press acknowledged that something unusual was occurring between Trump and MBS.

Alwaleed's detention formed part of the round-up of roughly forty relatives and officials at the Riyadh Ritz-Carlton on charges of corruption and treason.
