# Alwaleed bin Talal

Prince Alwaleed bin Talal is a Saudi royal and investor, founder and chairman of [[kingdom-holding|Kingdom Holding Company]], and one of the most visible Saudi figures in Western technology and media investment.[1] [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] famously nicknamed him "Prince Dopey," a label Badlands commentators adopted when discussing his dealings.[1]

## Investment in X and xAI

Kingdom Holding and Alwaleed maintained a $1.89 billion stake in the social media platform X after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s 2022 takeover, making them jointly the second-largest investors in the platform.[1] In 2024 they were named among the top investors in Musk's artificial intelligence startup [[xai|xAI]] as part of its $6 billion Series B round, alongside [[andreessen-horowitz|Andreessen Horowitz]], Valor Equity Partners, Vy Capital, Sequoia Capital and Fidelity Management & Research Company.[1]

## The 2017 purge and loss of control

Badlands analysis stressed a detail omitted from mainstream coverage of the xAI round: Kingdom Holding is no longer effectively controlled by Alwaleed.[1] When Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]] detained scores of wealthy Saudi royals and businessmen on November 4, 2017 — the episode known as the [[saudi-purge|Saudi Royal Purge]] — he also assumed control over their corporate holdings, including the Bin Laden family construction firm.[1] Alwaleed was released in January 2018 under an undisclosed deal and remained chairman of Kingdom Holding, but the company's $12.5 billion in global investment decisions became subject to approval by the Crown Prince's court.[1]

The Badlands reading of this arrangement is that MBS is effectively deploying Alwaleed's money into Musk's ventures, a framing that inverts the assumption that Saudi capital in xAI represents hostile influence.[1]

## X shareholder disclosure (2024)

The Saudi prince was listed among the investors in X Holdings Corp. when a federal judge ordered [[x-corp|X]] to unseal its shareholder list in August 2024.[2] [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] observed that Alwaleed has not been in control of his own assets since 2017, when they were seized by [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], whom Badlands identifies as a [[sovereign-alliance|Sovereign Alliance]] player.[2]

NK -> ARMENIA. ARMENIA -> Iran."[3]
