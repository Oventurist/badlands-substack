# Oded Lifshitz

Oded Lifshitz was an Israeli hostage taken during the October 7, 2023 attacks and later confirmed killed in captivity in the Gaza Strip.[1]

## Identification of remains

On Thursday, February 20, 2025, [[hamas|Hamas]] returned four sets of remains to Israel. Specialists at the Abu Kabir Forensic Institute identified Lifshitz along with the brothers Ariel and Kfir of the [[bibas-family|Bibas family]], while the [[israel-defense-forces|Israel Defense Forces]] announced early the next morning that the fourth body was not that of the boys' mother Shiri Bibas and could not be identified.[1] The handover, conducted in what Israeli media described as a propaganda-filled ceremony, prompted mourning and anger across Israel and renewed demands from hostage families for the return of all captives, alive and dead.[1]
