# Michael Tracey

Michael Tracey is an independent American journalist and commentator. Badlands Media contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] described him as one of the few remaining real journalists, despite their differences of opinion.[1]

## Commentary on Trump and Israel

DeLarme cited Tracey as representative of the argument that [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s apparently unrequited affection for [[israel|Israel]] is proof that a second Trump term would see U.S. bombs continuing to be dropped by Israel across the Middle East.[1] DeLarme called this a valid concern but countered it by pointing to a Trump interview with [[israel-hayom|Israel Hayom]] in which Trump urged Israel to "finish the job" rather than draw out a prolonged offensive, and described how his administration had rendered [[iran|Iran]] too broke to fund militants.[1]
