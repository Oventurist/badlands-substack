# German Foreign Office

The German Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt) is the foreign ministry of Germany. In its wartime incarnation under [[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]] it is named in [[badlands-brief-314|the Badlands Brief of February 4, 2026]] as one of the entities holding accounts at [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]] among the 890 previously undisclosed Nazi-linked accounts uncovered by the independent investigation of [[neil-barofsky|Neil Barofsky]] and announced by Senator [[chuck-grassley|Chuck Grassley]].

The other named account holders were the [[schutzstaffel|SS]] paramilitary organization — including its economic arm — and a German arms-manufacturing company. No specifics were released on the amounts held in these accounts or their current status.
