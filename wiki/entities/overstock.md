# Overstock

Overstock.com is an American online retailer whose former chief executive is [[patrick-byrne|Patrick Byrne]].

The July 14, 2026 Badlands Brief reports the conclusion of Byrne's defamation case against [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]], in which a federal judge ruled that Byrne — identified in the Brief as the former Overstock CEO — defamed Biden by promoting claims that Biden participated in an $800 million Iranian bribery scheme, awarding Biden $1.7 million in punitive damages.
