# Aziz Nasirzadeh

General Aziz Nasirzadeh is the Iranian Defense Minister. In the period leading up to Israel's June 2025 strikes on Tehran, Nasirzadeh warned that [[iran|Iran]] could attack United States bases in the region if nuclear talks with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s administration broke down or if hostilities continued to mount.[1]

That warning was cited as central context when Israel launched its strikes on Iranian nuclear and military sites, killing [[islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps|Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps]] commander [[hossein-salami|Hossein Salami]].[1] U.S. Secretary of State [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]] responded to the strikes by stressing that protecting American forces in the region was the top U.S. priority and warning that "Iran should not target U.S. interests or personnel."[1]
