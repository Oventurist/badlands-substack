# Robert Maxwell

Robert Maxwell was a British publishing magnate and the father of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]]. In Badlands commentary he appears as the operational link between Israeli intelligence, the PROMIS surveillance scandal, and the Swiss banking system.[1]

## PROMIS distribution

Citing research by the author Gordon Thomas, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote in April 2026 that Maxwell was dispatched by [[mossad|Mossad]] to sell the [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to entities across the world, including [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].[1] Because the software carried a secret backdoor, its distribution allegedly allowed Mossad to monitor the activities of all governments that adopted it, while also tracking the money-laundering practices of organized-crime syndicates through the banking system and arms deals through weapons manufacturers.[1]

## Money laundering

The same account holds that it was through this surveillance that Mossad learned the [[cia|CIA]] had been using Credit Suisse to launder revenue from various off-the-book endeavors.[1] By carefully studying and observing those transactions, Maxwell is said to have become the world's most prolific money launderer of the late 1980s.[1] The described technique was moving money between banks in the West and banks in the Soviet Union — a privilege said to require clout and contacts in both hemispheres that nearly no one besides Maxwell possessed.[1]

## Role in the Vanunu affair

Badlands commentary describes Maxwell as the owner of The Daily Mail who, on learning that Israeli nuclear technician Mordechai Vanunu was offering London outlets evidence of a secret Israeli nuclear weapons program in 1985, had the paper publish several hit pieces designed to discredit and impugn Vanunu's character.[2] The stories were false but damaging enough that many dismissed Vanunu's claims as the acts of a disgruntled scientist.[2]


## Ukrainian oligarch connections

Discussing [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s March 2025 proposal to sanction Ukraine's top ten oligarchs, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that he had been reading a book about Maxwell published more than twenty years earlier and found that many of the Ukrainian-Israeli mafia names on his own expanding mind map were doing business with Maxwell in the 1980s and 1990s.[3] He wondered aloud how many of the oligarchs named in the reporting — including [[igor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]], whom he called a notorious gangster — were tied to that same network.[3]
