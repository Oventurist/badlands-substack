# Robert Maxwell

Robert Maxwell was a British publishing magnate and the father of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]]. In Badlands commentary he appears as the operational link between Israeli intelligence, the PROMIS surveillance scandal, and the Swiss banking system.[1]

## PROMIS distribution

Citing research by the author Gordon Thomas, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote in April 2026 that Maxwell was dispatched by [[mossad|Mossad]] to sell the [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] to entities across the world, including [[credit-suisse|Credit Suisse]].[1] Because the software carried a secret backdoor, its distribution allegedly allowed Mossad to monitor the activities of all governments that adopted it, while also tracking the money-laundering practices of organized-crime syndicates through the banking system and arms deals through weapons manufacturers.[1]

## Money laundering

The same account holds that it was through this surveillance that Mossad learned the [[cia|CIA]] had been using Credit Suisse to launder revenue from various off-the-book endeavors.[1] By carefully studying and observing those transactions, Maxwell is said to have become the world's most prolific money launderer of the late 1980s.[1] The described technique was moving money between banks in the West and banks in the Soviet Union — a privilege said to require clout and contacts in both hemispheres that nearly no one besides Maxwell possessed.[1]

## Role in the Vanunu affair

Badlands commentary describes Maxwell as the owner of The Daily Mail who, on learning that Israeli nuclear technician Mordechai Vanunu was offering London outlets evidence of a secret Israeli nuclear weapons program in 1985, had the paper publish several hit pieces designed to discredit and impugn Vanunu's character.[2] The stories were false but damaging enough that many dismissed Vanunu's claims as the acts of a disgruntled scientist.[2]


## Ukrainian oligarch connections

Discussing [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]]'s March 2025 proposal to sanction Ukraine's top ten oligarchs, [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote that he had been reading a book about Maxwell published more than twenty years earlier and found that many of the Ukrainian-Israeli mafia names on his own expanding mind map were doing business with Maxwell in the 1980s and 1990s.[3] He wondered aloud how many of the oligarchs named in the reporting — including [[igor-kolomoisky|Igor Kolomoisky]], whom he called a notorious gangster — were tied to that same network.[3]

## Origins and British career

Badlands contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recounts that Maxwell, father of [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine Maxwell]], was born in what is now Ukraine and fled his homeland when the Nazis invaded, joining the British Army and becoming a British citizen in 1946.[4] Carlson notes it has been said Maxwell was invaluable in the creation of the State of Israel in 1948 and that he was rumoured to have been a Mossad spy, and that he later became a Member of the British Parliament.[4] Maxwell was murdered in 1991, which Carlson cites as evidence that he too was a "string" used by the puppet masters rather than a figure at the top of the pyramid.[4]

## Publishing empire and death at sea

Ian Robert Maxwell, a Czechoslovakia-born Jew, was most popularly known as a publishing mogul, having bought the British Printing Corporation, Mirror Group Newspapers and Macmillan Publishers, among other publishing companies.[5] He is said to have led a "flamboyant lifestyle," was embroiled in a host of controversies, and was alleged to have been working with multiple intelligence agencies in some capacity.[5] In 1991 his body was found floating in the Atlantic Ocean, having apparently fallen from his yacht, the *Lady Ghislaine*.[5] He is widely believed to have been recruited in the 1960s, with the agencies in question variously named as [[mossad|Mossad]], [[mi6|MI6]] and even the KGB; his standing with Israeli intelligence strengthened considerably in the early 1980s when he purchased a web of Israeli companies, many of them official "service providers" for Mossad.[5]

## The Maxwell PROMIS operation

After [[rafi-eitan|Rafi Eitan]]'s first attempt to market the bugged [[promis-software|PROMIS software]] through [[earl-brian|Earl Brian]] and [[edwin-meese-iii|Edwin Meese III]] collapsed into the bankruptcy of [[inslaw|Inslaw]], Eitan approached Maxwell as the man he believed could most effectively promote the software.[5] Maxwell marketed PROMIS through the computer firm [[degem-systems|Degem Systems]] — long used to hide Mossad killers operating in Africa and Latin America — and through the litany of Maxwell-owned companies based in Israel and abroad.[5] The effort was so successful that Israeli intelligence soon had access to the innermost workings of countless governments, corporations, banks and intelligence services around the world, including the military dictatorships of Chile and Argentina, where the software was used to facilitate the mass murder of dissidents.[5] Eitan specifically tasked Maxwell with selling PROMIS to the U.S. laboratories of the Los Alamos complex, including [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia National Laboratory]], in 1984 — concurrent with Eitan's handling of the spy [[jonathan-pollard|Jonathan Pollard]].[5]

## Assassination allegation and succession

In the biography *ROBERT MAXWELL, ISRAEL'S SUPERSPY: The Life and Murder of a Media Mogul*, authors Martin Dillon and [[gordon-thomas|Gordon Thomas]] suggest that Maxwell died at the hands of the spy agency he worked for, Mossad, portraying him as a vain, obese man of many excesses who helped Mossad steal intelligence-gathering software from the United States before selling it around the world.[5] His close ties to Israeli intelligence have also been documented by [[seymour-hersh|Seymour Hersh]], among others.[5] Following his death — which numerous biographers attribute to an assassination carried out by Israeli intelligence — five of his seven surviving children assumed distinct responsibilities within his extensive portfolio: Kevin and Ian Maxwell largely acquired his enigmatic web of interconnected foundations, trusts and corporations along with the accompanying debt; [[ghislaine-maxwell|Ghislaine]], established in New York at her father's urging, went on with [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] to run what the article describes as a sexual extortion operation on behalf of Israeli intelligence; and [[christine-maxwell|Christine]] and [[isabel-maxwell|Isabel]] carried the family's espionage business into the Internet era.[5]
