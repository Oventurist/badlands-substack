# Pam Bondi

Pam Bondi is the Attorney General of the United States in the administration of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], heading the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]].

## The Sarcone appointment

The January 9, 2026 [[badlands-brief-094|Badlands Brief]] reports that when local judges declined to reappoint [[john-sarcone|John Sarcone III]] as US Attorney for the Northern District of New York beyond his 120-day term, Bondi named him a "special attorney" with an indefinite term. Judge [[lorna-schofield|Lorna Schofield]] subsequently found that Sarcone had been serving unlawfully, disqualified him, and invalidated subpoenas his office had issued to New York Attorney General [[letitia-james|Letitia James]], writing that the Executive branch had acted "without lawful authority" in skirting restraints put in place by [[congress|Congress]].

## March 2026 Oversight Committee subpoena

The Badlands Brief of March 5, 2026 reported that the [[house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability|US House Oversight Committee]] issued subpoenas that week in the [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] matter, one of them directed at Bondi. Lawmakers sought documents and communications relating to Epstein investigations conducted in Florida during Bondi's tenure as that state's attorney general. According to the source, the subpoenas followed allegations that key records in the investigation had not been fully released.
