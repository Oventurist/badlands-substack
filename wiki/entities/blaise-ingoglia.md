# Blaise Ingoglia

Blaise Ingoglia is a Republican member of the Florida state Senate representing Spring Hill, known nationally for filing legislation targeting the [[democratic-party|Democratic Party]]'s historical association with slavery.[1]

## The Ultimate Cancel Act (2023)

On Tuesday, February 28, 2023, Ingoglia filed [[ultimate-cancel-act|"The Ultimate Cancel Act" (SB 1248)]], a bill that would direct the Florida Secretary of State to cancel the filings of — and therefore de-certify — any political party that had supported slavery during the Civil War.[1] The filing followed heavy losses for Florida Democrats in the previous election cycle.[1]

"The Democrat party adopted pro-slavery stances in their party platforms and this bill says that if you have done that in the past, then the Secretary of State shall de-certify and get rid of the party," Ingoglia said in explaining the measure.[1] Under the bill, voters registered with any canceled party would automatically become non-party-affiliated voters.[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[chris-paul|Chris Paul]] praised the maneuver as "how to play the game," arguing that regardless of whether the bill passed or took effect it was forcing a national awareness that the Democrats were "the party of slavery" and would put Democrats in the unenviable position of having to explain how they had "changed."[1]
