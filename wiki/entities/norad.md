# NORAD

The **North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD)** is the binational United States–Canada command responsible for aerospace warning and control over North America.[1]

## February 2023 shootdowns

During the [[2023-north-american-aerial-object-shootdowns|February 2023 aerial object shootdowns]], F-22 Raptors operating under NORAD control destroyed two objects in North American airspace within a single day and three within a week, according to reporting by The Drive's War Zone.[1] The engagements included a Chinese high-altitude balloon, an object over Alaska, and an object over Canada's Yukon Territory announced by Prime Minister [[justin-trudeau|Justin Trudeau]].[1] A further object over Lake Huron was downed by U.S. Air Force and Air National Guard pilots.[1] The command's role placed it at the center of a transparency dispute involving the [[pentagon|Pentagon]] and the [[national-security-council|National Security Council]].[1]

## 2024 Utah balloon intercept

On February 23, 2024, U.S. fighter aircraft intercepted a high-altitude balloon over Utah, and NORAD said the following day that the object was a "likely hobby balloon" that had since left United States airspace.[2] The command described it as a "small balloon" encountered at an altitude of 43,000 to 45,000 feet and said it was permitted to keep flying because it was determined not to pose a national security threat.[2] In a Saturday statement NORAD said that after the fighter intercepts, and in coordination with the Federal Aviation Administration, it monitored the balloon via ground radars until it departed U.S. airspace overnight; it offered no additional information.[2] Badlands commentary questioned the decision not to physically inspect the object, noting that chip technology has advanced to the point where functional machines can be smaller than a grain of rice.[2] See [[2024-utah-high-altitude-balloon-intercept|the 2024 Utah high-altitude balloon intercept]].[2]
