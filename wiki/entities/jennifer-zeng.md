# Jennifer Zeng

Jennifer Zeng is a Chinese-born human rights activist, author and journalist who is described as a member of the International Press Association.[1] She is known for interviews and reporting directed at the internal affairs of the People's Republic of China, particularly material sourced from Chinese nationals willing to speak against official narratives.[1]

## Reported COVID-19 origins interview

Between March and April 2020, Zeng recorded an interview with a researcher identified as [[chao-shao|Chao Shao]], who was described as being associated with the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology|Wuhan Institute of Virology]].[1] In the interview, later circulated in an ANI report, Chao Shao claimed that China had engineered the coronavirus responsible for COVID-19 as a "bioweapon," and recounted that a colleague at the institute, Shan Chao, said their superior had supplied four strains of the virus so that the team could determine which spread most effectively across various species, including humans.[1]

The interview resurfaced in reporting in June 2023 and was picked up by outlets including CNBC-TV18, where it was folded into the broader public argument over whether SARS-CoV-2 emerged naturally or through laboratory work.[1]

## Reception in the Truth Community

Coverage of Zeng's interview by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributors treated the material cautiously rather than as settled proof. Commentator [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] wrote that he took a "Bicameral—or Tricameral—perspective" toward COVID-adjacent narratives, saying he did not know whether COVID-19 was an engineered bioweapon or "a bad flu masquerading as the black plague," and guessed it was "a bit of both."[1] He argued that the resurfacing origins narrative indicated the Overton window was being shifted on a mass psychological scale regardless of the underlying truth.[1]
