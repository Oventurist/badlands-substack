# Gaza

Gaza, or the Gaza Strip, is the Palestinian coastal territory governed by [[hamas|Hamas]] and the theatre of the war that followed the [[october-7-attacks|October 7, 2023 attacks]] on southern [[israel|Israel]].

The February 11, 2026 [[badlands-brief-05f|Badlands Brief]] describes Gaza as a live point of friction ahead of Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s Washington visit. Phase 2 of the October 2025 U.S.-brokered ceasefire — requiring Hamas disarmament and Israeli troop withdrawal — was reported to be stalled because of Hamas's refusal to give up its weapons, with Israel arguing that a renewed military operation is required to enforce demilitarisation in line with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s Gaza vision. Incidents continued alongside IDF strikes on suspected ceasefire violators.

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] adds that Netanyahu intended to tell Trump that the [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]]'s Phase Two plan to take control of Gaza will not happen, and that the United States should instead move immediately toward war with [[iran|Iran]]. The same commentary alleges that funds solicited from [[qatar|Qatar]] on humanitarian grounds were walked into Gaza and distributed to families with known ties to Hamas.
