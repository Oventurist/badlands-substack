# James Litinsky

James Litinsky is the chief executive officer of [[mp-materials|MP Materials]], the operator of the only working rare earth mine in the United States.[1]

## Pentagon partnership

In July 2025 Litinsky described the [[pentagon|Pentagon]]'s $400 million preferred-stock purchase in MP Materials as a public-private partnership that would speed the buildout of an end-to-end rare earth magnet supply chain inside the United States.[1] He detailed the structure publicly: the Defense Department agreed to buy 100 percent of the magnets produced at the company's new facility, called 10X, for ten years after the plant is built, serving both defense needs and the commercial market, while [[jpmorgan-chase|JPMorgan]] and [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] provided $1 billion to finance the manufacturing facility.[1]

Litinsky also explained the price-support mechanism: the Pentagon guaranteed a minimum price of $110 per kilogram over ten years for neodymium-praseodymium oxide stockpiled or sold by the company, with the government paying the difference in quarterly cash payments if the market price fell below that level, and receiving 30 percent of the upside above $110 per kilogram once a second magnet facility became operational.[1] "The taxpayers are going to make a lot of money," he said of the arrangement.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] was skeptical of that prediction but concluded that, regardless of the eventual return to the Treasury, MP Materials had "won the lottery."[1]
