# Elijah Schaffer

Elijah Schaffer is a commentator whose social-media polling featured in Badlands Media's analysis of hybridized asymmetric and narrative warfare.[1]

## The July 2 Twitter poll

On July 2, Schaffer posted a Twitter poll asking respondents, "Would you rather have your son be in the KKK or be in the LGBTQ?"[1] The poll drew engagement from more than 65,000 users; 74.8% selected the [[ku-klux-klan|KKK]] and 25.2% selected LGBTQ.[1]

The result was widely circulated and prompted a critical Instagram response from commentator [[christina-buttons|Christina Buttons]], who called the outcome disheartening and described it as an extreme position taken by conservatives.[1] Badlands contributor [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] used the exchange as a case study, arguing that the poll's framing pitted acute contemporary anxiety about the [[sexualization-of-children|sexualization of children]] against an organization he characterized as largely impotent on the modern battlefield, and that the results were therefore better explained by [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]] dynamics than by a resurgence of racism or homophobia.[1] In Deschamps's account, such framings are the raw material of [[narrative-warfare|narrative warfare]], which uses cognitive entrenchment into political camps as an instrument of control in support of [[asymmetric-warfare|asymmetric operations]].[1]
