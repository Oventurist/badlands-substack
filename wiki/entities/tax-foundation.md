# Tax Foundation

The Tax Foundation is a nonpartisan American tax-policy research organization whose analyses of federal and state tax legislation are widely cited in political and economic reporting.[1]

## Analysis of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act

In August 2025 the Tax Foundation released an analysis estimating that American taxpayers would receive an average tax cut of $3,752 in 2026 as a result of the Republicans' [[one-big-beautiful-bill-act|One Big Beautiful Bill Act]].[1] The report described the legislation as making the most significant legislative changes to federal tax policy since the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, and estimated average taxpayer savings state by state.[1] Under the Foundation's estimates, filers in Wyoming and Washington would see the largest average reductions at nearly $5,400, while filers in Mississippi and West Virginia would see the smallest, at roughly $2,400 and $2,500 respectively.[1]

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] cited the analysis as evidence that the groundwork for what he calls a coming American "Golden Age" had already been laid, framing the tax law as a visible marker of a broader economic and political shift.[1]
