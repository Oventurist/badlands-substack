# Raising a Nation

Raising a Nation is a weekly [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] program airing Saturdays at 9pm ET as of January 2023, hosted by [[the-kate-awakening|The Kate Awakening]] and [[defender-of-the-republic|Defender of the Republic]].[1] The two discuss what the network calls all things Great Awakening from the standpoint of parents attempting to raise the next generation of sovereign citizens in an uncertain world.[1]

The December 19–25, 2022 schedule listed Raising a Nation on Saturdays at 9pm ET, with The Kate Awakening and Defender of the Republic discussing the Great Awakening from the perspective of parents raising sovereign-minded children.[2]

The week of December 5–11, 2022 — the “Badlands Media Weekly Show Schedule” post — reaffirmed Raising a Nation in its existing slot as part of the network's expanded truth-community slate, all airing live on the Patel Patriot channel with library replays on the Badlands Media Network.[3]

A second Badlands Media schedule post, published January 3, 2023 to open the 2023 broadcast year, carried the same listing and description, and reiterated that all network shows air live on the Patel Patriot Rumble channel with replays archived on the Badlands Media library channel.[4]

## Referenced in Ashe's *Oppenheimer* review

In her July 2023 review of Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer*, [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] described preparing for an episode of *Raising a Nation* and finding *The Saturday Evening Post*'s explainer on artificial intelligence, a normie-targeted account of the coming AI apocalypse that she forwarded to co-host Kate as a potential discussion topic.[5] The exchange became the starting point for her argument that the [[nuclear-arms-race|nuclear arms race]] is the governing metaphor in the [[artificial-intelligence|AI]] debate.[5]
