# Umkhonto we Sizwe Party (MKP)

The Umkhonto we Sizwe party, abbreviated MKP, is a South African political party founded by former president [[jacob-zuma|Jacob Zuma]] after he evaded prison by claiming terminal illness.[1] Zuma was 82 years old at the time of the party's rise around the May 29, 2024 general election.[1]

The MKP's emergence was central to the collapse of the [[african-national-congress|African National Congress]] vote share to under 40 percent, a result Badlands commentary framed as a successful insurgency by Zuma against ANC leader [[cyril-ramaphosa|Cyril Ramaphosa]].[1] The party appeared to have allied with the Economic Freedom Fighters led by [[julius-malema|Julius Malema]].[1] Both parties positioned themselves rhetorically with the multipolar bloc around Beijing and Moscow while advocating seizure of white-owned land and nationalization of mines and banks — a combination [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] interpreted as a deliberate subversion of the "Sovereign Alliance" effort to legally reclaim mining rights, and thus as "signal, not noise."[1]
