# Intergovernmental Negotiating Body

The **Intergovernmental Negotiating Body** (**INB**) is the body created by the [[world-health-assembly|World Health Assembly]] to draft and negotiate the instrument popularly known as the Pandemic Treaty.[1]

## Establishment

In December 2021, a special session of the World Health Assembly decided to establish the INB, representing all regions of the world, to draft and negotiate "a WHO convention, agreement, or other international instrument on pandemic prevention, preparedness, and response."[1] Soon after, the term "Pandemic Treaty" began circulating in public awareness, but was never clearly defined by the press or by the institutions advocating for it.[1]

## The Bureau's Text

On 22 May 2023, the "Drafting Group of the Intergovernmental Negotiating Body to Draft and Negotiate a WHO Convention, Agreement, or Other International Instrument on Pandemic Prevention, Preparedness, and Response" released an unedited draft titled "The Bureau's Text of the WHO CA+."[1] Badlands Media argued the text revealed goals of the WHO and its funders so "unabashedly dystopian in scope that most people would assume they were satyrical," including the creation of a [[conference-of-the-parties|Conference of the Parties]] under Article 20 and a protocol-adoption power under Article 34.[1] The resulting instrument is formally the [[who-pandemic-accord|WHO CA+]].[1]
