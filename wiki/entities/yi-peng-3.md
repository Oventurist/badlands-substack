# Yi Peng 3

**Yi Peng 3** is a Chinese-flagged cargo carrier implicated in the November 2024 severing of two fiber-optic telecommunication cables in the Baltic Sea.[1] Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], Ryan DeLarme characterized the incident as a Chinese "maritime oopsie," noting that Western officials described the episode as part of a pattern of "hybrid attacks on [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s Western allies."[1] Lithuania stepped up maritime surveillance following the damage to the undersea cable.[1]

## Context

The incident was cited as one of several indicators that [[china|China]] was inserting itself more assertively into the confrontation between [[nato|NATO]] and the Russia-aligned bloc, alongside [[peoples-liberation-army|People's Liberation Army]] combat readiness patrols near Huangyan Dao (Scarborough Shoal) and satellite imagery of a new Chinese carrier in the Bohai Sea.[1] Because the cables were severed in waters adjacent to NATO members while [[russia|Russia]] was being pulled up the escalation ladder over Ukraine, the event was read in this account as evidence of a coordinated Sino-Russian pressure campaign rather than an accident.[1]
