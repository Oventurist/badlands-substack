# United States International Development Finance Corporation

The United States International Development Finance Corporation, generally called the Development Finance Corporation or DFC, is the federal government's development finance institution, empowered to issue political risk insurance, loans and guarantees in support of American commercial interests abroad.

The March 4, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] designated the DFC as the vehicle for an immediate program of political risk insurance and financial guarantees, offered at "very reasonable" prices, covering all maritime trade — especially energy shipments — passing through the Persian Gulf during the war with Iran. Trump paired the announcement with a pledge that the U.S. Navy would escort tankers through the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] if necessary. The measure responded to Iranian attacks and threats to close the strait, which had triggered war-risk policies that froze tanker traffic and sent supertanker rates to record highs, and it caused oil prices to fall sharply.
