# Sheri Davis

Sheri Davis is the County Clerk of Douglas County, Colorado, responsible for local election administration.

## Co-plaintiff in the 2026 overseas voting suit

[[badlands-brief-169|"Condemnation, Calibration, & Constitutional Boxes"]] reports that Davis joined the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]] and Representative Jeff Crank as a co-plaintiff in the Denver District Court challenge to Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]]'s guidance under the [[uniformed-and-overseas-citizens-absentee-voting-act|Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act]].

## Criticism from Ashe in America

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], who identifies Davis as her own county clerk, describes her as "a fraud denier." She recounts contacting the clerk's office about the Konnech scandal and being told that everything was fine because the vendor had said there were no Chinese servers in the county's elections. Ashe argues the response missed the actual allegation, which concerned Chinese access rather than the physical location of servers, and which she maintains the evidence supported. The episode is offered in the Brief as evidence that the Colorado lawsuit's plaintiffs are establishment figures whose election-integrity credentials are, in her view, unearned.
