# Sheri Davis

Sheri Davis is the Clerk and Recorder of Douglas County, [[colorado|Colorado]], and a co-plaintiff in the [[republican-national-committee|Republican National Committee]]'s June 2026 lawsuit against Secretary of State [[jena-griswold|Jena Griswold]] over overseas voter registration.[1]

## Criticism from Badlands

[[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]], a Douglas County resident, described Davis as her county clerk and characterized her as a "fraud denier."[1] She recounted contacting the clerk's office about the Konnech scandal and being told that everything was fine because the vendor reported no Chinese servers in county elections — an answer she said missed the allegation entirely, since the claim concerned Chinese access rather than server location, and was supported by evidence.[1] On that basis she expressed skepticism about the overseas voting lawsuit despite supporting its nominal target.[1]
