# Abbas Araghchi

Abbas Araghchi is the Foreign Minister of [[iran|Iran]] and a veteran negotiator in the country's nuclear diplomacy.

[[badlands-brief-029|The June 12, 2026 Badlands Brief]], summarising Axios reporting, states that Araghchi took part in the talks that produced a tentative United States–Iran memorandum of understanding, negotiated alongside the Qatari mediator Ali Al-Thawadi and reached on a Wednesday night in June 2026. The reported framework — referred to as the [[islamabad-agreement|Islamabad agreement]] and mediated jointly by [[qatar|Qatar]] and [[pakistan|Pakistan]] — would reopen the [[strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]] without tolls, provide sanctions relief tied to Iranian compliance, and extend a ceasefire for 60 days. While President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he expected a signing ceremony over the weekend, an Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson stated that Tehran had not yet made a final decision on the proposal and that issues including sanctions relief remained unresolved.


## Response to the Caspian Sea strike

The July 27, 2026 [[badlands-brief-245|Badlands Brief]] reports that after [[iran|Iran]] accused [[ukraine|Ukraine]] of attacking an Iranian commercial vessel in the [[caspian-sea|Caspian Sea]] — killing one sailor and wounding another — Araghchi raised the matter with European Union foreign policy chief [[kaja-kallas|Kaja Kallas]] and Russian Foreign Minister [[sergey-lavrov|Sergey Lavrov]]. He described the strike as a violation of international law and warned that it "cannot go unanswered," according to Iranian statements reported by AFP. Iran summoned Ukraine's chargé d'affaires in Tehran to protest and said it would pursue the matter through diplomatic channels.

Araghchi is also quoted in the Brief as saying that "Ukraine attacked Iran at the behest of [[israel|Israel]] in order to draw Europe deeper into their war" with the Middle East.

## Solidarity with the Muslim world during the Twelve Day War

The May 18, 2026 [[badlands-brief-5a2|Badlands Brief]] reports that, during the June 2025 [[twelve-day-war|Twelve Day War]] between [[israel|Israel]] and [[iran|Iran]], Araghchi and President [[masoud-pezeshkian|Masoud Pezeshkian]] expressed solidarity with the Muslim world, with Pezeshkian calling longtime rival [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]] "our brothers."
