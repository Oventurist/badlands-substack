# Karl Nehammer

Karl Nehammer is an Austrian politician who served as Chancellor of Austria and leader of the conservative Austrian People's Party (ÖVP).[1]

## Refusal to govern with the FPÖ

After the [[freedom-party-of-austria|FPÖ]] won Austria's September 2024 parliamentary election, Nehammer's outgoing ÖVP was the only party that had not categorically ruled out a coalition with it.[1] Nehammer nonetheless refused to work with FPÖ leader [[herbert-kickl]] personally, citing Kickl's belief in conspiracy theories: "You can't run a state sensibly and responsibly with him. And I still stand by that."[1] His position left the largest party in the new parliament without a viable path to forming a government.[1]
