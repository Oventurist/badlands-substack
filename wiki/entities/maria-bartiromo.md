# Maria Bartiromo

Maria Bartiromo is an American business journalist and the host of Fox News's *Sunday Morning Futures*.[1]

## Role in the "enemy from within" interview

In an interview aired on Sunday, October 13, 2024, Bartiromo asked [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] whether he expected "chaos" on Election Day.[1] Trump answered, "Not from the side that votes for Trump."[1] When Bartiromo pressed further by raising "outside agitators," including individuals on the terrorist watch list, Trump pivoted to his domestic opponents and delivered the remark that became known as the [[enemy-from-within|"enemy from within"]] comment, in which he suggested that the [[national-guard|National Guard]] or the military could be used to handle such actors.[1] Later in the same interview he named Representative [[adam-schiff|Adam Schiff]] as an example.[1]

Bartiromo's program has repeatedly served as the venue for Trump's most widely amplified campaign-season statements, and the October 2024 exchange was reported by outlets including *The Wall Street Journal*.[1]
