# Edward Alden

Edward Alden is a senior fellow at the Center for Foreign Relations, quoted in October 2025 reporting on the escalation of the U.S.-China trade war over rare earth minerals.[1]

## Rare earths commentary

Alden characterized the state of U.S.-China relations bluntly amid new Chinese export restrictions on rare earths: "We're just playing with fire here."[1] The remark appeared in coverage of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s race to respond to a major escalation of the trade war, with the American supply of critical technology hanging in the balance.[1]

Trump administration officials announced plans that week to take a more active role in ensuring access to rare earths — minerals that are key components in several important technologies — in response to the new Chinese rules.[1] Those rules gave Beijing significant leverage as both nations raced to dominate the future of artificial intelligence and the semiconductor chips essential to powering it, and experts said China's actions reflected an unprecedented willingness and ability to test the boundaries of the relationship at a dangerous time.[1]
