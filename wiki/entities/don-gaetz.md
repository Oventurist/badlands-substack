# Don Gaetz

Don Gaetz is a Florida political figure and the father of Representative [[matt-gaetz|Matt Gaetz]]. He is best known in this context as the target of an attempted $25 million extortion by former federal prosecutor [[david-mcgee|David McGee]].[1]

## The extortion attempt

Matt Gaetz publicly described the scheme during a March 2021 appearance on [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] Tonight, saying his father had been approached for $25 million and had worn a wire during his meetings with McGee as part of a sting operation.[1] McGee reportedly told Don Gaetz that if he paid the $25 million to finance the rescue of captured former FBI agent Bob Levinson, President [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] would pardon Matt Gaetz of any crimes tied to the federal investigations then open against him — investigations Don Gaetz had no knowledge of at the time.[1]

A federal grand jury indicted McGee in September 2021 over the attempted extortion, and McGee was later convicted and sentenced to five years in prison.[1]
