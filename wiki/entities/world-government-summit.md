# World Government Summit

The World Government Summit is an annual gathering of government officials held in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.[1] Its 2023 edition drew attention in alternative media after [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], appearing virtually, warned the assembled officials that any attempt to create a world government would lead to the "collapse" of human civilization.[1]

## Musk's 2023 remarks

Musk cautioned that "too much cooperation between governments" creates a civilizational risk because it stifles diversity.[1] He argued that throughout history major civilizations were separated by distance, so that when one declined — such as the Roman Empire — others, such as Islam, were able to rise; in a globalized world this is impossible and humanity becomes permanently dependent on its weakest link.[1] The remarks were reported by [[summit-news|Summit News]] and amplified on social media.[1]

## Badlands commentary

[[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] called the warning valid in many people's eyes but "rather low resolution" in its basis, noting Musk's own formulation that "we want to have some amount of civilizational diversity, such that if something goes wrong with one civilization, that the whole thing doesn't collapse."[1] In substance, Deschamps wrote, this is an argument against world government of the same kind as an argument against placing all one's retirement investments in a single stock — logically sound but simplistic.[1]

Deschamps then raised the possibility that, if Musk has been read in on the [[deep-state|deep state]] and is working with white hats to some degree, the speech may have carried a coded message.[1] He stated he remained unconvinced either way about Musk — "he could be a white hat, a black hat or neither" — but judged that Musk appeared to know more than he was saying while dancing around that knowledge before what Deschamps called a "nest of vipers."[1]

## 2023 summit and Musk's warning

The World Government Summit 2023 was held in Dubai under the theme "Shaping Future Governments."[2] Speaking via video on Wednesday, February 15, 2023, [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] — then chief executive of Twitter, SpaceX and Tesla — used his appearance to argue against the concept of a [[one-world-government|one-world government]], saying attendees should be "a little bit concerned about actually becoming too much of a single world government" and that excessive cooperation between governments creates civilizational risk.[2] He called for "some amount of civilizational diversity" and warned that too much of a single civilization could cause the whole thing to collapse.[2] The exchange was widely reported by [[the-gateway-pundit|The Gateway Pundit]] and highlighted in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] joked that the story amounted to a lecture to globalists on the dangers of globalism.[2]
