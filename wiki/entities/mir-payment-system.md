# MIR Payment System

MIR is Russia's domestic payment card system, operated by the National Payment Card System (NPCS).[1]

## Origins

The NPCS, the operator of MIR cards, was created in 2014 after Crimea voted in a referendum to join Russia, as a contingency against Russian cards being disconnected from international payment systems.[1] The word "mir" means "world" or "peace" in English.[1]

## Rollout

Even at the outset, budget organizations began gradually abandoning payments to Visa and Mastercard cards, and pensions, social benefits and salaries for employees of budgetary organizations began to be transferred through the MIR payment system.[1]

## Strategic role

Badlands Media contributor [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]] describes MIR, alongside the [[spfs|SPFS]] messaging system, as one half of the Russian infrastructure that allows direct settlement with [[brics|BRICS]] partners and other trading states outside Western [[sanctions]] channels such as [[swift|SWIFT]], Visa and Mastercard.[1]
