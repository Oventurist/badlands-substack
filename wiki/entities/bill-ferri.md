# Bill Ferri

Bill Ferri is Global Head of Cantor Fitzgerald Asset Management at [[cantor-fitzgerald|Cantor Fitzgerald]].[1]

## Gold Protected Bitcoin Fund

Ferri was the public voice for the Cantor Fitzgerald Gold Protected Bitcoin Fund, launched in September 2025 after being announced in May at the Bitcoin 2025 conference in Las Vegas.[1] He described it as a five-year strategy that "tackles both risks head-on: it captures Bitcoin's upward trajectory while gold provides a safety net that historically performs well when markets decline."[1] He added that "with risk assets at or near all-time highs, timing and protection matter."[1] The fund materials said the structure "minimizes the risk of short-term volatility and reduces the impact of correlation spikes while continuing to benefit from the long-term upside trend of [[bitcoin|Bitcoin]]."[1]
