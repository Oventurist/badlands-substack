# Yuri Bezmenov

**Yuri Bezmenov** is described as a communist defector whose account of ideological subversion supplies the multigenerational time-horizon in [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]]'s mapping of globalist warfare.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

Bezmenov's contribution, as invoked in the article, is the observation that [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]] would occur over numerous generations rather than in a single political moment.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo) Deschamps instructed ChatGPT to expand the scope of the [[global-harmony-consortium|Global Harmony Consortium]]'s plan to include this multigenerational concept of communist warfare, asking it to map psychological warfare occurring subtly and gradually across generations so that the Consortium could pursue the strategic objective of installing a [[new-world-order|New World Order]] slowly over time.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

The resulting synthesis reflected that instruction throughout: psychological operations conducted with patience, influencing the values and beliefs of entire generations; activist movements funded and guided over multiple generations; laws altered gradually as long-term legal warfare; and institutions infiltrated over years rather than months.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/ai-can-help-us-expose-the-nwo)

## Cited in "Trump Knows"

Bezmenov, described as a Soviet journalist and former KGB agent who defected to Canada, is cited by [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] as having explained this style of cultural warfare in his famous interview with [[g-edward-griffin|G. Edward Griffin]].[5] The reference appears in a passage on [[fifth-generation-warfare|fifth-generation warfare]], where the battlefield is said to be ideals and values in the hearts of men and women rather than soil, and where thorough demoralization leaves a people incapable of governing themselves and unthinkingly demanding safety while confusing tyranny with freedom.[5] Deschamps marked that end state with the note "[You are here]" and concluded that [[donald-trump|Trump]] appears to know all about this agenda of propaganda control.[5]

## The 1984 interview and the limits of subversion

[[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] has repeatedly invoked Bezmenov's 1984 interview on [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]], in which Bezmenov claimed that people conditioned by multi-generational influence would become unable to see that "black is black and white is white."[2] Esler treats this as a description of the cognitive state of students captured by what he calls the [[woke-mind-virus|Woke Mind Virus]], including protesters at the [[2024-pro-palestinian-campus-protests|2024 pro-Palestinian campus protests]] who could not explain their own reasons for attending.[2]

Esler nonetheless argues that Bezmenov's claim admits an exception. [[detransitioners|Detransitioners]], he contends, were completely ideologically captured to the point of sterilising and maiming themselves and yet escaped, demonstrating that the condition is not cognitively inescapable.[2] The same recovery, at lower cost, is visible in the students profiled in [[the-coddling-of-the-american-mind|The Coddling of the American Mind]].[2] On this reading, Bezmenov correctly described the mechanism of subversion — slow generational conditioning amounting to a subtle form of mind control distinct from trauma-based programmes such as [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] — while underestimating the possibility of exit.[2]

## Bezmenov in the Schedule F argument

In his March 2023 column on [[schedule-f|Schedule F]], Badlands contributor [[j-b-white|J.B. White]] described Bezmenov as "a Soviet-era communist defector" and urged readers to research him alongside Schedule F itself.[3] White argued that studying Bezmenov would allow a reader to see how "a singular and individually sovereign American organized a dedicated Irregular Warfare team" to counter a decades-long communist infiltration plan designed to demoralize the United States and destabilize its foundations, producing a level of debilitating chaos that would ultimately normalize a communist-led [[new-world-order|New World Order]] takeover of America.[3] On that reading, the purpose of the covert campaign White called the true [[operation-warp-speed|Operation Warp Speed]] was to invert Bezmenov's sequence, delivering demoralization, destabilization and chaos to America's internal and external enemies rather than receiving it.[3]

## Cited on gender ideology and detransitioners

In a March 2023 Badlands feature, [[simon-esler|Simon Esler]] quoted Bezmenov's 1984 interview definition of [[ideological-subversion|ideological subversion]] as changing "the perception of reality … to such an extent that despite the abundance of information no one is able to come to sensible conclusions in the interest of defending themselves, their families, their community, and their country."[4] He also quoted Bezmenov's description of demoralised individuals who "are programmed to think and react to certain stimuli in a certain pattern" and whose minds cannot be changed even when exposed to authentic information.[4]

Esler argued that [[detransitioners|detransitioners]] constitute an exception to what Bezmenov described: although fully contaminated during their years as "trans youth," the trauma of their mistakes altered their basic perception and awakened them to authentic information.[4]
