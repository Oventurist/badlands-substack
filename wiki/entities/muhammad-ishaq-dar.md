# Muhammad Ishaq Dar

Muhammad Ishaq Dar is a Pakistani politician serving as Deputy Prime Minister and foreign minister, and was the Pakistani counterpart to Indian External Affairs Minister [[s-jaishankar|S. Jaishankar]] at the [[2024-sco-summit-in-islamabad|2024 SCO heads of government meeting in Islamabad]].[1]

Dar and Jaishankar spoke twice in less than 24 hours during the October 2024 summit, an unusually direct exchange given that no formal bilateral discussions had been scheduled on the sidelines.[1] Reporting indicated that the conversations explored resuming some form of cricketing ties between India and Pakistan, with sources cautioning that the talks were preliminary and suggesting that a first step could be the Champions Trophy that Pakistan was set to host the following February.[1]
