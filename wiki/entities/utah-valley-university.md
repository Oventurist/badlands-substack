# Utah Valley University

Utah Valley University is the public university in Orem, Utah, where conservative activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] was killed in September 2025.[1]

## Role in the Kirk case

Prosecutors in the case against [[tyler-robinson|Tyler Robinson]] allege that Robinson shot Kirk during an event at the campus, and the university setting supplies much of the evidentiary record in the prosecution — including surveillance footage and witness statements slated for presentation at Robinson's July 6–10, 2026 preliminary hearing before Judge [[tony-graf|Tony Graf]].[1] Authorities have also cited forensic analyses, autopsy findings, alleged admissions by Robinson, and DNA evidence said to link him to the rifle used in the campus shooting.[1]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] has expressed persistent skepticism about the official account of what happened at the university, writing that the story "never made sense" and that she doubted any presentation of evidence would convince her Robinson killed Kirk.[1] See [[robinson-preliminary-hearing-rulings-june-2026|the June 2026 Robinson preliminary hearing rulings]].[1]

## Site of the Charlie Kirk assassination (2025)

On Wednesday, September 10, 2025, conservative activist [[charlie-kirk|Charlie Kirk]] was fatally shot while on stage at Utah Valley University, an event that triggered a multi-day manhunt.[2] A high-powered rifle was recovered in a wooded area where the suspected shooter fled the campus, and the [[fbi|FBI]] and [[utah-department-of-public-safety|Utah Department of Public Safety]] released images of a person of interest believed to be of college age.[2] The shooting on campus became the focal point of national controversy over online reaction, censorship proposals, and conspiracy speculation.[2]
