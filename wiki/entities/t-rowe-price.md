# T. Rowe Price

T. Rowe Price is an American asset management firm named in Badlands Media's May 2025 essay "Wilted Ivy" as one of the financial institutions whose board membership overlaps with the stewardship of [[harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s endowment.[1]

## Role in the Harvard endowment network

The article argues that the individuals entrusted with managing Harvard's finances through the [[harvard-management-company|Harvard Management Company]] simultaneously sit on the boards of [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]], [[blackstone|Blackstone]] and T. Rowe Price, and maintain affiliations with the [[council-on-foreign-relations|Council on Foreign Relations]], the [[trilateral-commission|Trilateral Commission]], the [[world-economic-forum|Davos]] circuit and, in some cases, the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]].[1] The essay uses this overlap to argue that Harvard's endowment governance is "not an isolated academic exercise" but "a key node in a global matrix of control," creating what it calls a "single point of failure" for the wider system.[1]
