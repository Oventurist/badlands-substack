# iVoteIsrael

iVoteIsrael is an organization operating in central Israel that promotes absentee voting in United States elections by American citizens living in Israel. It advertises itself as a group of American olim — immigrants to Israel — "who share concern for the future of America," presenting participation in US elections as both a civic duty and an exercise of rights.[1]

## Jerusalem Post investigation

Ahead of the November 5, 2024 US presidential election, an investigation by the [[jerusalem-post|Jerusalem Post]] reported that iVoteIsrael had been engaging in [[ballot-harvesting]] — the collection of completed absentee ballots by a third party for delivery to a polling office. The Post characterized the operation as "potentially illegal."[1]

While the group states that it merely encourages voters to submit their ballots, the investigation raised concerns that mass collection of absentee ballots constitutes a prohibited service under the laws of several US states, including Pennsylvania and Alabama. Ballot harvesting is strictly prohibited in at least six states, with many others imposing partial limits on third-party ballot submissions.[1]

The Post had previously reported complaints from US voters in Israel that iVoteIsrael mishandled absentee ballot requests.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media commentary paired the iVoteIsrael story with a same-day Jerusalem Post opinion piece warning of "horrendous consequences" from a Trump victory, arguing that a foreign-based harvesting operation aimed at a US election would provoke broad alarm if the country involved were any other. Writing for [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] noted that the effort proved inconsequential to the outcome — Trump delivered a victory speech at Mar-a-Lago as the commentary was written — "but that doesn't excuse the attempt."[1]
