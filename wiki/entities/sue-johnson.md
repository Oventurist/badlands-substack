# Sue Johnson

**Dr. Sue Johnson** is cited in the April 26, 2025 Badlands Media feature [[the-emergence|"The Emergence"]] by [[observing-consciousness|Observing Consciousness]] on the subject of secure attachment.[1]

The essay credits her with the reminder that secure attachment is "not luxury, but biology": the body "doesn't crave drama," it "craves resonance."[1] It extends the point by arguing that when a person stops outsourcing safety, those who meet them in their own regulated state become "sacred mirrors, not to complete you, but to amplify you."[1]

Johnson's contribution appears in the essay's section on resonance and leadership, adjacent to the therapist [[vienna-pharaon|Vienna Pharaon]] on self-betrayal and to [[joe-dispenza|Joe Dispenza]] on the "quantum signature," within the article's account of how [[coherence|coherence]] reorganises relationships.[1]
