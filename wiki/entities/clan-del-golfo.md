# Clan del Golfo

The Clan del Golfo ("Gulf Clan") is the largest cocaine cartel operating in [[colombia|Colombia]].[1] It has been the principal target of the military campaign waged by President [[gustavo-petro|Gustavo Petro]], conducted with United States assistance since 2022.[1]

## Doha negotiations

In September 2025, representatives of the Gulf Clan met with Petro's diplomats in Doha, Qatar, to negotiate an eventual surrender.[1]

## Alleged role in the 2026 election dispute

Following the contested 2026 Colombian presidential runoff, Petro accused Israeli Prime Minister [[benjamin-netanyahu|Benjamin Netanyahu]] — and, by extension, bad actors within the US government — of aiding and abetting the Gulf Clan's attempt to return to power in Colombia through the election of [[abelardo-de-la-espriella|Abelardo de la Espriella]].[1] Petro has separately alleged that de la Espriella has old ties to drug trafficking, naming an associate known by the alias "Boliche."[1] Badlands contributor [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] framed these claims as potentially the most explosive narrative of the year, arguing they could bear on election-integrity disputes in the United States.[1]
