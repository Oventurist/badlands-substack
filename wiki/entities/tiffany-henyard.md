# Tiffany Henyard

Tiffany Henyard is an Illinois local official who served as supervisor of Thornton Township, a south suburban Chicago township, where she became an embattled figure amid ongoing governance disputes.[1]

## 2025 Thornton Township board brawl

On Tuesday, January 28, 2025, a Thornton Township Board meeting — the first held in months — ended abruptly in a physical brawl, with punches thrown and expletives shouted, forcing leaders to clear the hall and again halt township business.[1] Henyard made a rare appearance at the meeting and ended up involved in the fray herself.[1] The fight began after the public comment portion, when the last speaker, Jedidiah Brown, delivered a lengthy harangue directed at Henyard that ended with him calling her an obscenity; before Brown could return to his seat, Henyard's allies, including her boyfriend Kamal Woods, confronted him and punches were exchanged.[1]
