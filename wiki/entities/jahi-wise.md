# Jahi Wise

Jahi Wise is the former director of the [[greenhouse-gas-reduction-fund|Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund]], the $20 billion climate finance program administered by the [[environmental-protection-agency|Environmental Protection Agency]] under the [[inflation-reduction-act|Inflation Reduction Act]].[1]

## Conflict-of-interest allegation

Criminal referrals issued in 2026 by EPA Administrator [[lee-zeldin|Lee Zeldin]] to the EPA inspector general and the [[department-of-justice|Department of Justice]] identified Wise as an example of alleged self-dealing, stating that he personally oversaw a $5 billion grant awarded to his prior employer.[1] The referral was part of a broader set of allegations that Biden-era green energy funds were routed to pass-through entities connected to former Obama–Biden officials, Democratic donors, and politically connected individuals.[1]
