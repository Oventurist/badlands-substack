# Ordo Templi Orientis

The Ordo Templi Orientis (Order of the Templars of the East, OTO) was co-founded by Theodor Reuss, a [[new-templar-order|New Templar]] initiate associated with Dr. Franz Hartmann's Theosophical Lodge at Monte Verità in Switzerland.[1]

By 1915 the OTO had become a conduit of the English grand master [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]], and after Reuss's death Crowley took control of the worldwide order.[1] Under Crowley the OTO featured deep penetrations into the United States via New York and Los Angeles, as well as into Canada, Mexico and Germany itself.[1]

## American expansion

Part 5 of the series records that Crowley was made outer head of the order by [[theodor-reuss|Theodor Reuss]] in 1912, the same Reuss who had worked with Memphis-Misraim founder John Yarker, Carl Kellner, the Anthroposophist [[rudolf-steiner|Rudolf Steiner]] and Crowley within the Ancient Rite of Memphis and Misraim.[2] While stationed in the United States by British intelligence from 1914 to 1919, Crowley seeded occult cells across the country, including the OTO bodies in New York and California, the Californian lodges later being led by Jack Parsons and L. Ron Hubbard.[2] A branch of the order was also established in Vancouver by Crowley's disciple Charles Stansfeld Jones, whom he visited on his West Coast tour in October 1915.[2] A 1917 United States Office of Naval Intelligence report described the OTO as Rosicrucian and identified Reuss as Crowley's fellow leader within it.[2]
