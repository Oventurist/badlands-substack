# Carolyn Stewart

Carolyn Stewart is a defense attorney who represents [[oath-keepers|Oath Keeper]] [[jeremy-brown|Jeremy Brown]] in his effort to obtain release from prison under President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s January 6 pardon.[1]

## Advocacy for Brown's release

Stewart demanded Brown's release from prison on the grounds that both of his criminal cases were covered by the pardon.[1] She pointed to filings in which the [[department-of-justice|US Department of Justice]] acknowledged that Brown's Florida weapons-possession case — which resulted in a sentence of more than seven years — was directly related to January 6.[1]

Stewart said the filings were ample evidence to prove that the DOJ, Brown's defense team, and a US district judge all agreed the Florida case was a January 6 case.[1] "Done. Fully baked," she told Blaze News. "Yet the DOJ has prevented Jeremy's release under President Trump's pardon."[1]
