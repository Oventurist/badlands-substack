# Bruce Heyman

**Bruce Heyman** is a former [[goldman-sachs|Goldman Sachs]] executive who served as United States Ambassador to Canada, and who appears in Badlands Media contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]]'s May 2023 essay on [[energy-consolidation|energy consolidation]] as a link between Wall Street campaign finance and green-energy subsidies.[1]

## Role in the First Solar allegations

Carlson writes that Goldman Sachs was heavily invested in [[first-solar|First Solar]] before the 2008 election while also serving as a major contributor to [[barack-obama|Barack Obama]]'s campaign.[1] After taking office, Obama appointed two Goldman Sachs executives to his finance committee: Heyman and [[david-heller|David Heller]].[1]

Heyman's later appointment as Ambassador to Canada is presented by Carlson as more than incidental, because the $455.7 million subsidy at the center of his argument financed First Solar's sale of panels to two Canadian solar farms owned by its own wholly owned subsidiary, [[st-clair-solar|St. Clair Solar]].[1] Carlson closes the passage by asking whether the sequence — Goldman investment, campaign donations, a finance committee seat, federal money flowing to First Solar's Canadian operations, and then an ambassadorship to Canada — was a coincidence.[1]
