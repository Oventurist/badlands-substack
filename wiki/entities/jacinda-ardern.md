# Jacinda Ardern

Jacinda Ardern is a New Zealand politician who formerly led the country's Labour Party and served as prime minister until her abrupt resignation, which occurred roughly nine months before the October 2023 general election.[1] She was succeeded in office by [[chris-hipkins|Chris Hipkins]].[1]

## Legacy and the 2023 election

Ardern's tenure is chiefly remembered in the Badlands Media framing for its pandemic governance. Coverage of the October 2023 New Zealand election attributed the defeat of her former party to the government's record of compelling citizens to take COVID-19 vaccines, decreeing state news to be the only legitimate "truth," and jailing peaceful protesters who opposed pandemic authoritarianism.[1] Voters ousted the party once led by Ardern on Saturday, 14 October 2023, electing instead what was described as the most conservative government New Zealand had seen in decades under [[christopher-luxon|Christopher Luxon]].[1]

The result meant the incumbent Labour Party was on track to lose its outright majority in the 121-seat parliament for the first time since 2017.[1]
