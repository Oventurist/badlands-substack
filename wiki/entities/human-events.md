# Human Events

**Human Events** is an American conservative news and opinion publication whose reporting has been aggregated by [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] in its news briefs.[1]

## Coverage cited by Badlands Media

In June 2023, Human Events reported that [[united-states-capitol-police|Capitol Police]] had stopped the [[rushingbrook-childrens-choir|Rushingbrook Children's Choir]] from performing a capella renditions of patriotic songs, including the national anthem, inside the Capitol Building in Washington, D.C., and that former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had responded by inviting the choir to perform at his upcoming South Carolina rally.[1] The outlet characterized the shutdown by Capitol Police and the congressional staffer involved as "a stain on our national character and the people's House," and reported that Trump had called the prohibition "a shame."[1]
