# Kenneth Chesebro

Kenneth Chesebro is an American attorney who advised the 2020 [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] campaign and is widely identified as an architect of the alternate-elector strategy pursued in several contested states after the 2020 presidential election.[1]

## Role in the Michigan case

In dismissing the [[michigan-fake-electors-case|Michigan fake electors case]] on September 9, 2025, Judge [[kristen-simmons|Kristen Simmons]] drew an explicit distinction between the intent of those who "orchestrated the scheme," naming Chesebro and other Trump attorneys, and the intent of the individuals who actually signed the elector documents.[1] Because the prosecution brought by Attorney General [[dana-nessel|Dana Nessel]] targeted the signatories rather than the organizers, Simmons found the state had not proven the intent required for fraud charges.[1]
