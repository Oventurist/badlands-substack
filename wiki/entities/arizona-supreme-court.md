# Arizona Supreme Court

The Arizona Supreme Court is the highest court in the state of Arizona and the final state-level arbiter of appeals arising from Arizona's trial and appellate courts. It appears in the Badlands corpus principally through its role in litigation surrounding the [[2020-united-states-presidential-election|2020 presidential election]] and the prosecution of Arizona's alternate Republican electors.

## The 2020 electors case

According to the June 5, 2026 [[badlands-brief-58d|Badlands Brief]], the Arizona Supreme Court rejected an appeal in the 2020 electors case involving allies of President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. The ruling left standing a lower court's dismissal of the indictment, which a judge had ordered after finding that prosecutors failed to present the grand jury with the precise statutory language tied to the alleged conduct. Rather than ending the matter, the decision prompted Attorney General [[kris-mayes|Kris Mayes]] to announce that her office would return the case to a grand jury and seek a new indictment.

The Brief's commentary treats the court's rejection of the appeal as a procedural setback that Mayes chose to escalate rather than accept, and reads her decision as evidence of what Badlands writers call weaponized government persisting even as public awareness of it grows.
