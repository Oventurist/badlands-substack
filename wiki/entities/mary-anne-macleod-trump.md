# Mary Anne MacLeod Trump

**Mary Anne MacLeod Trump** was the mother of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]. She was born on the Isle of Lewis in the Scottish Hebrides, in the general vicinity of the Isle of Skye.[1]

Her birthplace and surname are central to a genealogical argument in [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]]'s [[the-werewolf-game|Werewolf Game]] series.[1] The series notes that in the 15th century a man named Ulric Kennedy is said to have set up a home base on the Isle of Skye, and that the Macleod clan's castle, Dunvegan Castle, is also located on Skye.[1] The author observes that Macleod is a Celtic/Gaelic name, treats the proximity of the two family seats as further evidence of a long-standing closeness between the Trump and [[kennedy-family|Kennedy]] families, and asks rhetorically whether castles are not usually owned by or named after royalty.[1]
