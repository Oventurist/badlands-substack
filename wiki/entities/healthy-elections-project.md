# Healthy Elections Project

The Healthy Elections Project is a joint Stanford–MIT initiative that maintains a public case tracker documenting litigation surrounding the 2020 United States elections.[1] Its tracker provides individual case detail pages summarizing the parties, claims, procedural posture, and holdings of election-related suits filed during the 2020 cycle.[1]

The project's summaries were used extensively by attorney and independent journalist [[rachel-alexander|Rachel Alexander]] in her index of 2020 election cases, alongside the report assembled by [[election-integrity-info|ElectionIntegrity.Info]].[1] [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] cited the tracker's individual case entries throughout its review of 2020 outcomes, including Pennsylvania poll-observer and ballot-address disputes, the Chatham County absentee-ballot petition in Georgia, injunctions obtained against the Iowa and Minnesota secretaries of state, the Nevada polling-hours ruling, the Wisconsin "indefinitely confined" restraining order, and the Clark County signature-record request.[1]

Because the tracker records the grounds on which cases were disposed of — standing, jurisdiction, timeliness, laches, or public-health considerations — it also supplied the evidence for the claim that 62 of 92 cases were dismissed without any court hearing the merits.[1]
