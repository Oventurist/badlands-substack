# Summit News

Summit News is an alternative media outlet frequently cited in Badlands Media's news roundups.[1]

In August 2023 Summit News reported on an Associated Press/NORC poll finding that 77 percent of Americans — including 89 percent of Republicans and 69 percent of Democrats — considered [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]] too old to serve a second term, while fewer than half of Americans said the same of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1] The report was aggregated in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] with commentary from [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]].[1]
