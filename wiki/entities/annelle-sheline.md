# Annelle Sheline

Annelle Sheline is a former United States [[state-department|State Department]] official who resigned in February 2024 in protest of the [[joe-biden|Biden]] administration's refusal to pressure the Israeli government to limit bloodshed in Gaza.[1]

## Assessment of the January 2025 ceasefire

Speaking to The Independent after the January 2025 [[gaza-ceasefire|Gaza ceasefire]] was announced, Sheline argued that the agreement "demonstrates clearly that Biden could have achieved a ceasefire all along if his people were really serious about it."[1] She added that the substance of the deal had not changed materially: "It's not as if suddenly the terms have shifted significantly, it's just that now you had an incoming US president that was willing to actually use some pressure."[1]

Badlands Media used her testimony, together with statements from Josh Paul and Tariq Habash, to support its argument that the outgoing administration possessed decisive leverage over Israel — funding, arms, and diplomatic protection — and declined to use it, while [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and his envoy [[steve-witkoff|Steve Witkoff]] were willing to apply it.[1]
