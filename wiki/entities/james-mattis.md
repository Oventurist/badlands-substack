# James Mattis

**James Mattis** is a retired U.S. Marine Corps general who served as Secretary of Defense in the first [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration.[1]

In Badlands Media commentary on the killing of [[jamal-khashoggi|Jamal Khashoggi]], Mattis appears among administration officials who publicly cast doubt on the involvement of Saudi Crown Prince [[mohammed-bin-salman|Mohammed bin Salman]], contradicting the assessment of the U.S. intelligence community.[1] Citing [[rolling-stone|Rolling Stone]], the article records that Secretary of State [[mike-pompeo|Mike Pompeo]] said there was no "direct reporting" of the crown prince's guilt, while Mattis maintained there was no "smoking gun."[1]

The article treats these statements as part of a deliberate refusal by Trump and his cabinet to accept a [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] assessment that it characterizes as an instrument for driving a wedge between Washington and Riyadh.[1]

## Assessment

The quoted statements come from cited reporting; the interpretation of motive is the author's. Badlands Media notes that contributor articles represent the opinions of their authors.[1]
