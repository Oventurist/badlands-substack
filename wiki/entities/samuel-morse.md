# Samuel Morse

Samuel F. B. Morse was an American painter and inventor trained by [[benjamin-west|Benjamin West]] at the [[royal-academy-of-fine-arts|English Royal Academy of Fine Arts]], and is described in Badlands Media commentary by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as one of the students who most masterfully carried forward West's method of embedding Platonic ironies and anomalies in art.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## Science and communications

The discoveries of [[benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]] in electricity are said to have given rise directly to Morse's advances in electrical communication, achieved with the help of a variety of scientists around the Humboldt brothers including Carl F. Gauss, Wilhelm Weber and Ampère.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance) The article groups Morse with Franklin, Ampère, Weber and Joseph Henry as representing a school of thought in the sciences that it argues must be revived to overcome the paradoxes of twentieth-century standard model cosmology and quantum mechanics.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)

## Foreign Conspiracy Against the Liberties of the United States

Morse later exposed what the article characterises as a Jesuitical operation in the Americas in his 1841 book *Foreign Conspiracy Against the Liberties of the United States*, describing Prince Metternich's Holy Alliance and its deployment of [[jesuits|Jesuits]] throughout the Americas to undo the American Revolution, writing that they came "in the shape of hundreds of Jesuits and priests; a class of men notorious for their intrigue and political arts, and who have a complete military organization through the United States."[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-new-american-renaissance)
