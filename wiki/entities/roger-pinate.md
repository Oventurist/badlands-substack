# Roger Alejandro Piñate Martinez

**Roger Alejandro Piñate Martinez** is a co-founder and executive of the voting technology company [[smartmatic|Smartmatic]] who was charged by United States federal prosecutors in connection with an alleged bribery and money laundering scheme targeting election officials in the Philippines.[1]

## Allegations

According to a superseding indictment filed in October 2025 in Miami federal court, Piñate and Jorge Miguel Vasquez allegedly orchestrated more than $1 million in bribes to [[juan-andres-donato-bautista|Juan Andres Donato Bautista]], the former Chairman of the Philippines' [[comelec|Commission on Elections]].[1] Prosecutors allege the payments, made between 2015 and 2018, were intended to secure and maintain contracts for SGO Corporation Limited — including favorable VAT reimbursements and other government payments — as Smartmatic sought a deal to help administer the country's 2016 presidential election and to ensure prompt payment for its services.[1]

The indictment states that the scheme was funded by inflating the cost of voting machines used in the 2016 Philippine elections, and that the conspirators concealed the bribes using coded language, fake contracts and loan agreements, and by routing funds through bank accounts in Asia, Europe, and the Southern District of Florida.[1]

## Procedural history

Piñate was among three former Smartmatic executives charged in 2024 in connection with the same scheme; the company itself was not named as a defendant until the 2025 superseding indictment.[1] The case unfolded while Smartmatic continued to pursue a $2.7 billion defamation lawsuit against Fox News over broadcasts alleging the firm helped manipulate the outcome of the 2020 U.S. presidential election.[1]
