# Bill Gates

**Bill Gates** is the co-founder of [[microsoft|Microsoft]] and founder of the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## Reputation shift

In *America Has a CIA-Run Economy*, [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] recalls that by the late 1990s Gates was one of the most hated men in the world. In 2000 he launched the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation and was rapidly recast as one of the world's greatest philanthropists — yet while the public believed he was giving his money away, his net worth doubled within a year.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## Origins of Microsoft software

Carlson relays the rumor that Gates's genius is overstated, and that his mother hired engineers from IBM to develop the early Microsoft software.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## Energy and AI

Gates is reported to be planning to fund nuclear power plants to power Microsoft's artificial intelligence infrastructure.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/america-has-a-cia-run-economy)

## On the 2023 AI pause debate

Gates, described as the former Microsoft chief executive, told ABC News in May 2023 that a moratorium on advanced [[artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] development would be self-defeating: "If you just pause the good guys and you don't pause everyone else, you're probably hurting yourself."[2] Badlands Media's July 2023 essay on AI cites this against the [[ai-pause-open-letter|open letter of March 22, 2023]], with the author remarking that she did not expect to find herself agreeing with Gates but concluding that he is right and that one only hurts oneself by complying.[2]

## Congressional Testimony on Jeffrey Epstein (2026)

In June 2026 Gates testified behind closed doors before the [[united-states-house-oversight-committee|House Oversight Committee]] about his past ties to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], calling the association a "grave error in judgment."[3] He told lawmakers that Epstein later attempted to blackmail him over extramarital affairs, denied knowing about or participating in Epstein's crimes, and said their meetings had concerned philanthropic fundraising discussions.[3] His appearance came as the committee widened its investigation into Epstein's network and the [[department-of-justice|Justice Department]]'s handling of the case.[3]

## AI and "digital misinformation" (February 2023)

In an interview with the German outlet *Handelsblatt* published on February 9, 2023, Gates said artificial intelligence should be considered as a tool to combat "digital misinformation" and "political polarization."[4] Asked about the spread of misinformation via AI, he referred to the events of January 6, 2021, as an "attack on the Capitol," saying: "I'm certainly concerned about political polarization, and I know the U.S. situation best of all. I wouldn't have expected people to attack the Capitol, or people to deny the validity of election results. I'm in a state of shock about that."[4] He added that AI could not be blamed, but that the episode "may have been magnified by digital channels that allow various conspiracy theories like [[qanon|QAnon]] or whatever to be blasted out by people who wanted to believe those things," so that "the digital tools may have played an auxiliary role."[4]

Writing in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] asked who would program such an AI — "the same folks who programmed [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to be biased against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] and [[marjorie-taylor-greene|Marjorie Taylor Green]]?"[4] DeLarme argued that a censorship-oriented AI would "search for, find, and block competing ideas," serving elites who "want to control the narrative" and keep people inside a curated "bubble of awareness."[4] He called Gates "the clown prince of disinformation" and predicted the idea would never come to fruition because "the people are waking up."[4]

## Artificial eggs and the 2023 egg shortage

In 2013 Gates invested in [[hampton-creek|Hampton Creek]], the company that brought artificial plant-based eggs to the mainstream — roughly a decade before a severe egg shortage in the United States.[5] Egg prices rose 66 percent year over year per the Consumer Price Index after more than 41 million egg-laying hens died in connection with avian flu, and some consumers turned to plant-based substitutes.[5] Badlands contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] questioned the timing, noting Gates's sustained effort to alter American diets, and argued that the chickens had largely been preemptively slaughtered over the potential threat of avian flu rather than killed by the virus itself.[5]

## Embalmed-infant autopsy study (August 2025)

In August 2025 Modernity News reported that a [[bill-and-melinda-gates-foundation|Gates Foundation]]–funded autopsy project, published in *Forensic Science, Medicine and Pathology*, described newborn corpses being chemically embalmed for weeks so their organs could be harvested, catalogued, and standardized into databases tied to artificial intelligence, forensic investigations, and global mortality surveillance programs.[6] The report noted that the study followed the Gates-financed "Baby Open Brains" project, in which researchers at the Masonic Institute for the Developing Brain released MRI scans of infants as young as one month old into global AI databases to build the backbone for a nationwide government brain-tracking program.[6] It also placed the embalming project after a Gates-funded tuberculosis vaccine trial in South Africa said to have left 260 previously healthy children infected with TB following injection with live *Mycobacterium bovis* bacteria, and alongside Gates money backing U.S. gain-of-function experiments that engineered tuberculosis to grow unchecked.[6]

Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] responded by invoking the "Baby Hitler Dilemma" — the thought experiment asking whether one would travel back in time to kill Adolf Hitler as an infant — and asked why the debate concerns Hitler in hindsight rather than Bill Gates in the present.[6] She characterized Gates as "a eugenicist that experiments on children in developing nations" who is "trying to block out the sun while also building a brain tracking program using newborn corpses to train the AI," predicting that a saeculum from now descendants would debate the ethics of using time machines on "Baby Gates" and judge their ancestors for inaction.[6]

## 2023 Australian interview on Epstein

During a visit to Australia in late January 2023, Gates was questioned about his connection to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]] on the Australian Broadcasting Corporation's 7.30 program.[7] Host Sarah Ferguson told him "one of the issues that has dogged you is your relationship to Epstein" and asked whether he regretted maintaining that relationship against the advice and wishes of his former wife, Melinda.[7] Gates, described as taken aback, replied: "You're going way back... but I will say for the over a hundredth time that, yeah, I shouldn't have had dinners with him."[7] Gates was in the country to discuss global issues including climate change and artificial intelligence, and was at the time the world's sixth-richest man.[7]

Badlands Media contributor [[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] wrote that Gates appeared "incredibly terrified" to answer questions about Epstein and defaulted to standard talking points, adding that the reach of the Epstein network and the continuing Epstein–Maxwell story meant the saga was far from over.[7] In separate commentary in the same brief, [[justin-deschamps|Justin Deschamps]] cited Gates and [[event-201|Event 201]] as indications that further pandemics were anticipated after the [[covid-19-emergency-declarations-termination|end of the COVID-19 emergency declarations]].[7]

## January 2023 Reddit AMA

In a Reddit "Ask Me Anything" session reported on January 17, 2023, Gates was asked about progress on molten-salt thorium reactors and replied that TerraPower's two reactor designs both use liquid sodium as coolant and uranium as fuel, that progress was "excellent" although the war in Ukraine had delayed the uranium fuel supply, and that the first reactor was being built in Wyoming and should be running by 2030.[8] He said the design would make "a huge contribution to climate challenges since it will be low cost and super safe," and mentioned a recent visit to West Virginia to learn about its energy economy, including a committed FORM battery factory.[8]

[[jordan-sather|Jordan Sather]] wrote that Gates had used the AMA to promote plant-based fake meat and nuclear technology while avoiding any question related to [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]], attributing the advocacy to his large investments in both "green" initiatives.[8] Sather argued that nuclear power remains a centralized energy source requiring a meter and a grid — "exactly what the Powers that Be would want" — and that its promoters never champion decentralized zero-point energy technologies, while suggesting plant-based meat substitutes were likely genetically engineered.[8]


## Mandalay Bay ownership and the Las Vegas shooting

Chapter 12 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series names Gates, alongside [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], as an owner of the top floors of the Mandalay Bay hotel from which the October 2017 [[las-vegas-shooting|Las Vegas shooting]] was carried out, and states that Gates bought out the prince's stake after the attack.[9] The author treats ownership of those floors as ownership of the high ground required for an attack of that scale and describes both men as possible [[deep-state|Deep State]] assets.[9]

## In "Soul Governance"

The Badlands Media essay [[soul-governance|Soul Governance]] names Gates alongside [[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]] and [[anthony-fauci|Anthony Fauci]] as figures it declines to treat as prime movers, describing them instead as "echoes, masks, handlers of a system that only worked because we, the sovereigns, agreed to believe it."[10] The essay's stated point is that focusing on such individuals mistakes the surface of the problem for its root, which it locates in the reader's own forgotten sovereignty.[10]
