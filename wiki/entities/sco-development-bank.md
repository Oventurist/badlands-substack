# SCO Development Bank

The **SCO Development Bank** is a proposed multilateral lending institution announced as one of the five core commitments of the [[tianjin-declaration|Tianjin Declaration]], the concluding document of the 2025 [[shanghai-cooperation-organisation|Shanghai Cooperation Organisation]] summit held in Tianjin, China on 31 August – 1 September 2025.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Purpose

According to Badlands Media contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]], the bank is to provide productive credit for all SCO members.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Its creation sits alongside the declaration's other principles — respect for the sovereign equality of nations, rejection of unilateral coercive measures, resistance to what the declaration calls the rehabilitation of Nazism in the West, and insistence that a just settlement of the Palestinian issue is the only route to peace in the Middle East.1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Significance

Ehret treats the bank as the financial component of an emerging alternative security architecture, one he links to the roughly $3 trillion in large-scale infrastructure investment already mobilised through the [[belt-and-road-initiative|Belt and Road Initiative]] and to the integrated financial architecture being prepared by the [[eurasian-economic-union|Eurasian Economic Union]] along lines outlined by [[sergey-glazyev|Sergey Glazyev]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) In his framing, this productive-credit model recalls the original [[bretton-woods-system|Bretton Woods]] design of long-term credit for large-scale development rather than the speculative practices he associates with the later [[imf|IMF]] and [[world-bank|World Bank]].1(https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)
