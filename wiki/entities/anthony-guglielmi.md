# Anthony Guglielmi

Anthony Guglielmi is a spokesman for the [[us-secret-service|United States Secret Service]].[1]

## 2023 White House cocaine probe

In July 2023 Guglielmi served as the agency's public voice on the [[white-house-cocaine-incident|White House cocaine incident]], telling Breitbart News on July 10 that the investigation was "still open and active at this time" and that no suspect had been identified.[1] Asked about a suspect, he said, "Something like that would come after an investigation is completed."[1] His statements were criticized in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]], where [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the pervasive surveillance of the White House made a week-long failure to identify a suspect implausible.[1]

Earlier, on Wednesday, July 5, 2023, Guglielmi had confirmed that the powdery substance found in the White House over the preceding weekend was cocaine, and specified that it was discovered in an area of the West Wing lobby where individuals can store their phones.[2] The confirmation followed leaked radio intercepts describing a "cocaine like" mystery substance that had triggered a brief evacuation over hazmat fears.[2] White House Press Secretary [[karine-jean-pierre|Karine Jean-Pierre]] said the same day that the lobby was "a heavily traveled area" regularly accessed by both visitors and staff.[2]
