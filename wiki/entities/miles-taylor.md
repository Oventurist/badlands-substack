# Miles Taylor

Miles Taylor is a former official of the first [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] administration who subsequently became a prominent public critic of the former president.[1]

## Commentary on a possible second Trump term

In early May 2024, Taylor was quoted in a *New York Times* report on the mood in Washington, D.C., as the prospect of a Trump return to the White House became a recurring topic at dinner parties and receptions in the capital. Taylor said that "in the past two weeks that conversation for whatever reason has just surged," adding that "people are feeling that it's very obvious if a second Trump term happens, it's going to be slash and burn."[1]

The article in which Taylor was quoted described figures in official Washington — including a former member of Congress, a former agency director, a Biden administration official, a liberal columnist, and a former investigator — joking about relocating to Portugal, Australia, Canada, France, or Poland in the event of a Trump victory, a form of gallows humor that the paper treated as an indicator of the capital's grim mood.[1]

## Badlands commentary

Badlands Media writers treated the *New York Times* piece, and the anxieties voiced in it by figures such as Taylor, as an exercise in projection. The commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] argued that the article's warnings about a dictator, the prosecution of political adversaries, and the dehumanization of large parts of the population described conduct the writers themselves had normalized, and contended that the underlying fear was not of Trump's accountability but of the voters'.[1] The writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] framed the coverage as "Narrative Acceleration along the Trump 2.0 plot rail," arguing that elites feared a Trump return chiefly because it would validate claims that the 2020 outcome had been anticipated and permitted.[1]
