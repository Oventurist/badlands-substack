# SkyFall

**SkyFall** is a [[ukraine|Ukrainian]] drone manufacturer producing FPV (first-person view) attack drones for use in the war against [[russia|Russia]].[1]

## Lindsey Graham gift

According to activist [[laura-loomer|Laura Loomer]], an FPV drone was presented to the late Sen. [[lindsey-graham|Lindsey Graham]] as a birthday gift by SkyFall during his final visit to Kyiv, and the drone would be deployed on the front lines "to kill Russians."[1] Graham, a prominent advocate of U.S. military aid to Ukraine, died after returning from that trip, and [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] attended his funeral in Washington.[1]

## Loomer factory visit and promotion

SkyFall released an online advertisement showing Loomer holding one of the company's drones inside its factory, presenting it as a gift for Russian forces and reminding viewers that Graham had visited that same facility days before his death.[1] Loomer stated that SkyFall and comparable Ukrainian firms were poised to become major players in the U.S. [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]].[1]

## Badlands framing

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] described the advertisement as morbid and situated it within a broader argument that Ukrainian defense interests are expanding into the American arms establishment while Ukrainian-linked operatives are alleged to be active in the Middle East.[1]
