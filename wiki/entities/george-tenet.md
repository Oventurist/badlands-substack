# George Tenet

George Tenet served as Director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|Central Intelligence Agency]] in the period surrounding the [[september-11-attacks|September 11, 2001 attacks]].[1] He is named in a sworn declaration by Department of Defense investigator [[donald-canestraro|Donald Canestraro]] as one of the top CIA officials who allegedly withheld vital intelligence from the [[fbi|FBI]] that might have prevented the attacks.[1]

## Allegations in the Canestraro declaration

The declaration, dated July 20, 2021 and reported by [[florida-bulldog|Florida Bulldog]] in April 2023, compiles interviews with eleven former FBI agents and two former CIA officers supporting long-public allegations that Tenet and other senior CIA figures kept from the Bureau the fact that known al Qaeda operatives and future hijackers Nawaf al Hazmi and Khalid al Mihdhar had entered the United States at Los Angeles shortly after attending an al Qaeda summit in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia in early January 2000.[1]

The accounts also support a theory never fully explored by either the 9/11 Commission or Congress: that the Agency stayed silent because it was working hand in glove with its Saudi Arabian counterpart to recruit Hazmi and Mihdhar as informants, an arrangement the declaration describes as using the Saudis as a go-between because the CIA is forbidden by law from conducting intelligence operations inside the United States.[1]

## Attribution of the 9/11 attacks

At 9:30 PM on September 11, 2001, Tenet — then Director of the [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] — told President [[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] and other senior officials that the agency's Counterterrorism Center had determined [[osama-bin-laden|Osama bin Laden]] and [[al-qaeda]] were responsible for the [[september-11-attacks|attacks]].2 Badlands Media contrasts this timing with Israeli official [[ehud-barak|Ehud Barak]]'s public attribution of the attacks to "a kind of bin Laden Organization" within hours of the strikes, arguing the discrepancy is suggestive of foreknowledge given how intertwined Israeli intelligence is with the CIA.2
