# Celeste Malloy

Celeste Malloy is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Utah.[1]

## Small Business Administration voter-registration hearing

Malloy featured in Badlands Media's June 2024 coverage of a congressional hearing on the weaponization of federal resources, which examined the [[small-business-administration|Small Business Administration]]'s voter-registration agreement with the state of Michigan.[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] singled out Malloy's question-and-answer exchange during the hearing as "excellent," recommending it to readers as the clearest exposition of the case that the arrangement violated the [[hatch-act|Hatch Act]].[1]
