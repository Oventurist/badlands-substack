# Benito Mussolini

Benito Mussolini was the Italian politician who founded the Fascist Party and ruled Italy as its dictator from the 1920s until 1945. In the Badlands Media corpus he appears not as a subject of historical study but as the source of a definition invoked to characterize the contemporary global order.

## Invocation in Badlands commentary

In the March 24, 2026 Badlands Brief, [[badlands-brief-50d|Alternative Annexations & New(ish) Regime Change]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] opens her commentary on Germany's [[alternative-for-germany|Alternative for Germany]] party by noting that the previous day was the anniversary of Mussolini's founding of the Fascist Party in Italy. She quotes his remark that "Fascism should more properly be called corporatism, since it is the marriage of government and corporate power," and then asks how that differs from "the global world order we have now," answering her own question: "It's not."

The quotation functions rhetorically to reverse the direction of the "far-right" accusation levelled at nationalist parties: rather than treating the AfD as the fascist element, the commentary applies Mussolini's own definition to the state–corporate fusion she attributes to the globalist establishment that opposes such parties. The attribution of this specific quotation to Mussolini is historically disputed among scholars, though the article presents it without qualification.
