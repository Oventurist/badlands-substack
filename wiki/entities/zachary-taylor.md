# Zachary Taylor

Zachary Taylor (1784–1850) was the twelfth President of the United States, elected in 1848 as the second [[whig-party|Whig Party]] president and dying in office.

## Death in office

[[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]]'s [[avoiding-wwiii|Avoiding WWIII]] presents Taylor's election as the second of two important nationalist victories won by the Whigs, following that of [[william-henry-harrison|William Henry Harrison]] in 1840. The article asserts that both Whig presidents were poisoned, and notes with evident scepticism that the official cause given for Taylor's death was "too many cherries and cold milk."

Taylor is one of five presidents the article identifies as having died in office after attempting to revive America's nationalist system in the six decades following the destruction of the [[second-bank-of-the-united-states|Second Bank of the United States]] in 1836, the others being Harrison, [[abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]], James Garfield and William McKinley.
