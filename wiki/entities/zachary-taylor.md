# Zachary Taylor

**Zachary Taylor** was the twelfth president of the United States, whose 1848 election [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] describes as the second important victory of the nationalist grouping that had rallied for decades around the Whig Party of [[john-quincy-adams|John Quincy Adams]].[1]

## Presidency and death

Taylor followed [[william-henry-harrison|William Harrison]], and Ehret asserts that both Whig presidents were poisoned.[1] He notes with evident scepticism that the official cause of Taylor's death was "too many cherries and cold milk."[1] Taylor is counted among the five presidents who attempted to revive America's nationalist system in the six decades after the Second National Bank was killed in 1836 and who died in office.[1]

## Context

The killing of the 2nd National Bank in 1836 followed a mass propaganda campaign that convinced a duped mob it was an instrument of tyranny in America; over the following six decades the only five presidents to make serious efforts toward reviving the nationalist system all died in office.[1]
