# Territorial Center for Recruitment

The Territorial Center for Recruitment (TCR) is the Ukrainian state body that oversees Kiev's military conscription drive.[1] By November 2025 the recruitment campaign it administers had grown increasingly brutal as the Ukrainian military faced battlefield setbacks and manpower shortages.[1]

## Forced mobilization and resistance

Hundreds of incidents have been documented online in which TCR officers assault potential conscripts, chase them through the streets, and threaten bystanders who attempt to intervene.[1] In November 2025 a Ukrainian man in the city of Dnepr opened fire on TCR staff, wounding two draft officers after they attempted to conscript him forcibly, according to video circulating on social media.[1]

## Commentary

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] wrote in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] that Ukraine was reaching its breaking point and that the population was turning against the regime, resorting to kinetic resistance in the face of forced conscription.[1] He connected the Dnepr shooting to the reported conscription of actress Angelina Jolie's Ukrainian driver, who was dragged from their vehicle a week earlier, and to Ukrainian commander Sergey Filimonov's lament that more than 290,000 soldiers had deserted the front lines, while Russian forces closed in on the encircled Donbass city of Mirnograd.[1]
