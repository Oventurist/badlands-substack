# Thomas A. Drake

Thomas A. Drake is a former [[national-security-agency|National Security Agency]] official who became one of the most prominent targets of an [[espionage-act|Espionage Act]] prosecution brought against an alleged leaker rather than a spy. His home was raided in 2007 over classified documents, and he was indicted in a case that came to trial in 2011 before the government executed what The Federalist described as an "embarrassing retreat."[1]

## Role in the Jack Smith record

The Drake case is central to Badlands Media's treatment of Special Counsel [[jack-smith|Jack Smith]]. As the Department of Justice's public integrity section chief overseeing the matter, Smith signed off on the government's collapse of the prosecution, a decision the source article presents as an early indicator of the department's willingness to stretch the Espionage Act beyond its intended reach.[1] Badlands contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]] cited Drake's 2011 case, stemming from the 2007 raid over classified documents, as a direct precedent for the 2023 prosecution of [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]].[1]

The Federalist argued that even defendants who "beat the rap" do not escape the process itself, noting that Drake "certainly has not" recovered from the ordeal — a framing the article suggested may be the point of such prosecutions.[1]
