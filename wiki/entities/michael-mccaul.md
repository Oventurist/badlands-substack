# Michael McCaul

Michael McCaul is a Republican member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Texas and a leading congressional voice on foreign policy and Ukraine assistance.[1]

## REPO for Ukrainians Act

McCaul sponsored the REPO for Ukrainians Act, legislation that would permit the confiscation of Russian sovereign assets held under U.S. jurisdiction, with the proceeds liquidated and deposited into a fund supporting Ukraine.[1] In March 2024, House Speaker [[mike-johnson|Mike Johnson]] told Republican senators that a forthcoming House aid package could incorporate something similar to McCaul's bill, alongside structuring aid as a loan or lend-lease arrangement — an approach [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] had floated the previous month.[1] Senators briefed on the discussion said Johnson made no mention of pairing the package with U.S. border reforms such as "Remain in Mexico" provisions.[1]

## April 2024 foreign aid vote

As House Foreign Affairs Chairman, McCaul, a Texas Republican, defended the passage of the [[april-2024-foreign-aid-package|April 2024 foreign aid package]], saying: "We cannot be afraid of our shadows. We must be strong. We have to do what's right."[2]
