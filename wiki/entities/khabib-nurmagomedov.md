# Khabib Nurmagomedov

Khabib Nurmagomedov is a retired Russian mixed martial artist and former [[ufc|UFC]] champion, described in Badlands coverage as a loyalist of Russian President [[vladimir-putin|Vladimir Putin]].[1]

## Encounter with Donald Trump at UFC 302

In early June 2024, following his hero's welcome at UFC 302 in Newark, New Jersey, former President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] was filmed in what Badlands described as an "impromptu" face-to-face meeting with Nurmagomedov at cage side.[1] Badlands writer [[burning-bright|Burning Bright]] treated the encounter as part of a cluster of "Russia-Martial Arts-MAGA" signals in the Info War that weekend, alongside Putin's award of the Order of Friendship to actor [[steven-seagal|Steven Seagal]].[1]

As a related addendum, UFC President [[dana-white|Dana White]] said at the post-fight press conference that if and when the UFC stages an event in Moscow, he wants Putin to be in attendance.[1] Burning Bright argued that the sequence illustrated a deliberate shift of the [[overton-window|Overton Window]], noting that the rising popularity of populist leaders had not previously been reflected in pop culture because "The System has largely leveraged control over most popular entertainment apparatus" — a situation he said was changing rapidly as "the counter culture becomes the culture."[1]
