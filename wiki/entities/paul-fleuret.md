# Paul Fleuret

Paul Fleuret is a citizen journalist and commentator associated with [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]], writing at the Absolute 1776 Substack.[1]

Fleuret contributed commentary to the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]] of May 9, 2023, addressing the inability of the [[us-virgin-islands|US Virgin Islands]] to locate [[larry-page|Larry Page]] for service of a subpoena in litigation over [[jeffrey-epstein|Jeffrey Epstein]]'s trafficking network.[1] He wrote that Page had "conveniently" gone missing and had "probably retreated to some off-the-wall country to avoid being subpoenaed," and argued that the Epstein story "is not going away" but "is only getting bigger and they won't be able to hide from it."[1]

## Commentary in the September 26, 2023 brief

Fleuret contributed two items to the September 26, 2023 Badlands News Brief. On the CDC's refusal to release updated post-vaccination myocarditis data he wrote that the withholding was unsurprising, asking why the agency would release evidence that, in his framing, showed the vaccine to be "a ticking time bomb for the heart," and noting that many people had been cancelled or ostracized for raising myocarditis risk while the agency exercised "all available mental gymnastics and obfuscation."[2] On [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s warning of Russian interference in 2024, he called the episode déjà vu, observing that the Clinton camp had begun warning of interference more than a year before the election, and predicted the rhetoric would intensify as [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s polling strengthened toward November 2024.[2]

## December 2023 commentary on athlete collapses

Writing in the Badlands News Brief for December 18, 2023, Fleuret addressed the on-field cardiac arrest of [[premier-league|Premier League]] player [[tom-lockyer|Tom Lockyer]], the [[luton-town|Luton Town]] club captain's second such incident within seven months.[3] He argued that the acceptance of young athletes collapsing mid-play is "irregular and detached from normal," stressing that soccer players are routinely the most well-conditioned athletes in the world, and noted that the English Premier League had a vaccine mandate in effect for its players during the COVID-19 period.[3]

Fleuret asked why comparable clusters of collapses had not appeared in the United States, speculating that owners or leagues might have gone along with vaccine policy on condition that their athletes receive a placebo to protect their investments.[3] While acknowledging the speculative nature of the question, he characterized the collapses as side effects of what he called a deadly poison foisted upon society and rejected the framing of the incidents as coincidence.[3]


## Q for Dummies

Fleuret is the author of [[q-for-dummies|"Q for Dummies"]], a serialized introductory primer on the [[q-operation|Q]] drops published on Substack.[4] In July 2024 the [[american-hypnotist|American Hypnotist]] author recommended the series to readers of his [[in-defense-of-q-series|In Defense of Q]] essay as the place to "gain a solid footing with regards to the drops themselves," positioning his own series as a supplement to Fleuret's primer.[4]

Fleuret launched the series on December 5, 2023 with Chapter 1, "Intro & Drops #1-5."[5] In it he introduces himself as Absolute1776, the name by which he says he is better known in the movement, and as a participant in several [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] shows including [[eye-of-the-storm|Eye Of The Storm]], co-hosted with [[stormy-patriot-joe|Stormy Patriot Joe]] on Tuesday and Friday evenings.[5] He describes that show as a high-level breakdown of the Q operation and its comms and decodes, and credits [[patriots-in-progress|Patriots In Progress]] with covering the high-level written analysis, positioning Q for Dummies as the deliberately low-level counterpart aimed at newcomers.[5]

Fleuret writes that his opinions derive from years of following and researching Q and decades "down the rabbit hole," but insists he is not the ultimate source and that readers should draw their own conclusions.[5] He states that truth is power and how the movement wins, and that reducing complexity is the best way to overcome newcomers' apprehension.[5] He directly rejects the claim that Q is a psyop telling people to stay home, asserting that the drops never say the military is coming to save anyone and never discourage peaceful action, and that those who say otherwise have not read the drops, do not understand them, or are misrepresenting them deliberately.[5]

Across Chapter 1 he offers several personal readings: that [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]] is "not a black hat"; that [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s near-collapse on September 11, 2016 may have been the day she was detained; that [[george-soros|George Soros]]'s transfers to his non-profits — Soros being referred to derisively as "Mr. Ballsack Eyes" — may reflect asset seizure or evasion of seizure; and that [[operation-mockingbird|Operation Mockingbird]] and the [[4-am-talking-points|4 AM talking points]] remain the mechanism of narrative control.[5] He announced a bi-weekly cadence covering seven to ten drops per installment, thanked readers for supporting Badlands Media, and signed off with the movement slogan "where we go one, we go all."[5] Readers are directed to more of his work at his own Substack, Beyond the Veil.[5]

## Chapter 21 and the 2024 election

Fleuret published Chapter 21 of [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] on December 8, 2024, after a lengthy hiatus he attributed to the demands of the election run-up.[6] The chapter opens with an extended commentary on the [[2024-us-general-election|2024 general election]], noting Republican control of the House, Senate, a majority of statehouses and governorships alongside a 6–3 Supreme Court, and the fact that [[donald-trump|Trump]] became the first Republican to win the popular vote since 1988.[6] He praises the incoming cabinet — naming [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|RFK Jr.]], [[tulsi-gabbard|Gabbard]], [[tom-homan|Homan]], [[pete-hegseth|Hegseth]], [[pam-bondi|Bondi]] and [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]] at the CIA — as a "deep state wrecking crew," and notes [[doge|DOGE]] under [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and [[vivek-ramaswamy|Vivek Ramaswamy]] with its July 4, 2026 target date, which he does not believe is coincidental.[6]

In the same chapter Fleuret sets out his [[the-kick|"kick"]] theory of mass awakening, drawn from the film *Inception*, and connects it to the [[missile-comms|missile comms]] tracked on [[eye-of-the-storm|Eye of the Storm]].[6] He also announces a change of layout for presenting the drops, in response to readers who found his earlier chalkboard graphics hard to read on phones, and a forthcoming Q for Dummies podcast on his own Rumble channel.[6] The chapter closes on the anniversary of Pearl Harbor with the series' customary sign-off that patriots are in control and God wins.[6]
