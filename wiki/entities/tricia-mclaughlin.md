# Tricia McLaughlin

Tricia McLaughlin is a spokesperson for the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] who in January 2026 publicly rebutted reporting that senior Border Patrol commander [[gregory-bovino|Greg Bovino]] had been relieved of his duties in Minneapolis.[1]

## Denial of the Bovino removal report

On January 26, 2026, The Atlantic reported that Bovino had been relieved of his duties in [[minneapolis|Minneapolis]], where federal immigration enforcement was being conducted under [[operation-metro-surge|Operation Metro Surge]].[1] McLaughlin disputed the report on social media, writing: "Chief Gregory Bovino has NOT been relieved of his duties. As @PressSec stated from the White House podium, @CMDROpAtLargeCA is a key part of the President's team and a great American."[1]

Her denial, together with a parallel denial from the [[white-house|White House]], was cited by Badlands as an example of a viral outrage cycle — amplified by conservative influencers — collapsing once officials responded, a pattern the Brief characterized as recurrent.[1] Badlands contributor [[jon-herold|Jon Herold]] separately pointed out that the "Commander at Large" title at the center of the controversy did not exist before the previous year.[1]
