# Kyriakos Mitsotakis

Kyriakos Mitsotakis is the Prime Minister of Greece.[1]

## Odessa missile strike (March 2024)

On Wednesday, March 6, 2024, Mitsotakis was meeting with Ukrainian President [[volodymyr-zelensky|Volodymyr Zelensky]] in the Black Sea port city of Odessa when a Russian missile strike landed near Zelensky's motorcade, in what appeared to be an attempt to target the Ukrainian leader.[1] Greek officials told the Protothema news outlet that Zelensky and members of the Greek delegation were unharmed despite the missile landing roughly 500 feet away.[1] The Ukrainian presidential office did not immediately respond to a request for comment.[1]

Foreign leaders had made frequent visits to Ukraine during the two years of war with Russia, and strikes coinciding with such visits were not unprecedented, but the March 6 incident stood out for the proximity of the impact to both Zelensky and Mitsotakis.[1] European Council President [[charles-michel|Charles Michel]] wrote on X that the attack was "another sign of Russia's cowardly tactics in its war of aggression against Ukraine," adding that it was "reprehensible and below even the Kremlin's playbook."[1]
