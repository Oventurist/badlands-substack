# Amaryllis Fox Kennedy

Amaryllis Fox Kennedy is a former Central Intelligence Agency officer and the daughter-in-law of [[robert-f-kennedy-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]], married to his son Bobby Kennedy III. She served as campaign manager for RFK Jr.'s 2024 presidential campaign.[1]

## Proposed nomination as deputy CIA director

In December 2024, Axios reported that RFK Jr. was pushing for Fox Kennedy to be named deputy director of the [[cia|Central Intelligence Agency]] in the incoming [[donald-trump|Trump]] administration, and that part of his motivation was his belief that the CIA had a role in the assassination of his uncle, President John F. Kennedy.[1] Two Republican sources told Axios that RFK Jr. had been telling people Fox Kennedy would help get to the bottom of the JFK assassination.[1]

If appointed as deputy to [[john-ratcliffe|John Ratcliffe]], Trump's pick for CIA director, Fox Kennedy would be positioned to examine what the agency holds regarding the assassination and could potentially urge the release of documents — an outcome long agitated for by podcaster Joe Rogan and others.[1] Axios reported real behind-the-scenes drama over whether Fox Kennedy was the right choice for the agency's number-two job, while noting that Trump felt indebted to RFK Jr. for his help in the election and was eager to please him.[1]
