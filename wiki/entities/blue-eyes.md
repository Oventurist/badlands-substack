# Blue Eyes

Blue Eyes is a [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor and co-host who appears in the February 12, 2026 Badlands Brief in connection with the program [[culture-of-change|Culture of Change]].

In her commentary on the proposed [[ai-data-center-compact|AI data center compact]], [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] notes that she and Blue Eyes discussed artificial intelligence's disruption of every industry on the Monday edition of Culture of Change, episode 138, describing it as "a fun show." The discussion framed the present moment as a transformation in which companies that harness AI intelligently will leapfrog their competitors into the Golden Age, while those that chase shiny objects will waste money and lose relevance — a framing Ashe then used to argue that the infrastructure demands of that transformation make planned growth an imperative.
