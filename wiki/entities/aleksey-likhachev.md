# Aleksey Likhachev

Aleksey Likhachev is the chief executive of [[rosatom|Rosatom]], the Russian state nuclear corporation.[1]

## Statements on the Zaporozhye strike

Following a fiber-optic-guided drone strike on the machine hall of the sixth power unit at the [[zaporozhye-nuclear-power-plant|Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant]] in May 2026, Likhachev warned of the consequences of an explosion or fire at the facility, stating that resulting radiation would primarily endanger [[ukraine|Ukraine]] and neighbouring European Union countries.[1] Rosatom, which has operated the plant under Russian control since 2022, characterized the strike as Kiev's first deliberate attack on the plant's main equipment.[1]
