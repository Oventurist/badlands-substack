# The Squad

"The Squad" is the informal name for a group of self-described progressive Democratic members of the United States House of Representatives, most prominently [[alexandria-ocasio-cortez|Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]].[1]

## Badlands framing

In his February 14, 2023 essay "Balance, Necessary for Control," [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] treated the Squad's arrival as a deliberate counterweight rather than an organic political development.[1] On his account, once [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] took office the Democratic Party's moderate liberals were no longer sufficient to offset Trump and the rise of Trumpism, so Democratic politicians "had to become extreme progressives."[1] The Squad, and especially Ocasio-Cortez, were in this reading the [[cabal|Cabal]]'s instrument for restoring the balance it requires; extreme progressives were voted in during the 2018 election, and existing moderate liberals "suddenly became more extreme overnight, MORE left."[1]

Carlson argued that the reverse move followed Trump's departure. With the perceived weight over-corrected toward the left, the Squad "needed to take a backseat" and exited the stage, appearing to disappear once Trump was out of office.[1] He placed other far-left figures — [[elizabeth-warren|Elizabeth Warren]], [[bernie-sanders|Bernie Sanders]], [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]], [[dianne-feinstein|Dianne Feinstein]], [[cory-booker|Cory Booker]], [[ted-lieu|Ted Lieu]], [[maxine-waters|Maxine Waters]] and Jerry Nadler — "backstage" alongside them, describing all of them as virtually AWOL during the first two years of [[joe-biden|Joe Biden]]'s presidency.[1] Their silence, he wrote, did not mean far-left policies were absent; keeping the loudest advocates quiet while those policies were rolled out was itself what preserved the perceived balance.[1]

Writing at the start of Biden's third year, Carlson noted that some of these previously quiet politicians were beginning to reappear and make noise again, which he read alongside the classified-documents story as a sign of a plan to jettison Biden from the stage.[1]
