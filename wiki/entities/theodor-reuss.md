# Theodor Reuss

Theodor Reuss was a German occultist and Masonic organizer who in 1912 made [[aleister-crowley|Aleister Crowley]] outer head of the [[ordo-templi-orientis|Ordo Templi Orientis]].[1]

Reuss had previously worked with the Memphis-Misraim Rite founder John Yarker, Carl Kellner, the Anthroposophist [[rudolf-steiner|Rudolf Steiner]] and Crowley within the Ancient Rite of Memphis and Misraim, the order whose Ancient and Primitive Rite Crowley came to head in 1913.[1] A United States Office of Naval Intelligence report dated 10 July 1917 zeroed in on Reuss as Crowley's fellow leader of the Rosicrucian Ordo Templi Orientis, stating that Crowley "maintained some mysterious means of communication with their chief in Berlin, Rudolf Steiner."[1]
