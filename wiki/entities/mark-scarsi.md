# Mark Scarsi

Mark Scarsi is the federal judge who presided over the tax prosecution of [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]] brought by special counsel [[david-weiss|David Weiss]].[1]

## Hunter Biden plea proceedings

On September 5, 2024, Scarsi presided as Biden's attorney [[abbe-lowell|Abbe Lowell]] announced that his client would switch his plea and plead guilty to all nine federal tax charges, a reversal that surprised prosecutors including Leo Wise.[1] The judge had Biden agree under oath that he would no longer remain silent and advised him that pleading guilty meant giving up all of his rights to a trial.[1] Before the court took a 30-minute break, Scarsi observed that "the court doesn't need the government's agreement to accept an Alford plea."[1] Biden remained free on bond until sentencing, scheduled for December 16, 2024.[1]

## August 2024 dismissal ruling

On Monday, August 19, 2024, Scarsi rejected [[hunter-biden|Hunter Biden]]'s latest attempt to dismiss several tax-related charges in Los Angeles, all but ensuring the case would proceed to trial as scheduled early the following month.[2] Biden's attorneys had filed motions in July 2024 seeking dismissal of both his California and Delaware cases, citing a Florida federal judge's decision to dismiss the classified documents case against [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]; Scarsi concluded in a seven-page ruling that those arguments failed on both procedural and factual grounds.[2]
