# United Nations Environment Programme

The **United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)** is the environmental arm of the United Nations, described by [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] as the creation of [[maurice-strong|Maurice Strong]], the Davos co-founder who also served as vice president of the [[world-wildlife-fund|World Wildlife Fund]].[1]

In 1984 UNEP joined with the World Wildlife Fund-UK to create Television for the Environment, a media project whose leading patrons include the hereditary peer [[anthony-st-john|Lord Anthony St John]].[1] Ehret uses UNEP's origins and its media ventures to argue that the institutional environmental movement and the more militant eco-activist groups such as [[extinction-rebellion|Extinction Rebellion]] share a common patronage network running through Strong, the WWF and [[king-charles-iii|King Charles]].[1]
