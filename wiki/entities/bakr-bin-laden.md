# Bakr bin Laden

**Bakr bin Laden** was the head of the [[saudi-binladin-group|Saudi Bin Laden Group]], a major construction conglomerate in [[saudi-arabia|Saudi Arabia]].[1] He was among those swept up in the November 2017 Saudi anti-corruption purge alongside [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]], economy and planning minister Adel Fakeih, former finance minister Ibrahim Assaf, former Saudi Arabian General Investment Authority head Amr Dabbagh, and MBC chief Waleed Ibrahim.[1]

Badlands writer Joe Lange treats the arrest of a senior member of the [[bin-laden-family|Bin Laden family]] as a pivotal detail, connecting it to Q drop 118 (November 6, 2017), which asked what family was permitted to leave the United States immediately after 9/11, who authorized the departure, and whether any of those arrested in Saudi Arabia were connected to the [[podesta-group|Podesta Group]].[1] The article presents that linkage as the setup for a subsequent installment tying the Podestas, [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]] and 9/11 together; the connection is asserted rather than demonstrated.[1]
