# Mark Guzzetta

Mark Guzzetta was a key Republican fundraiser for President [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and later finance co-chairman of [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]]'s campaign for governor of Florida; Jeb Bush served as best man at his wedding.[1]

When IBM Corp. hired the Codina Bush firm to sell its Boca Raton office park — a maze of buildings totaling two million square feet on roughly 565 acres, assessed at $100 million and the city's biggest tract of developable land — Bush made numerous pitches to IBM executives in New York.[1] In early 1997 the property sold for $46.1 million, less than half its assessed value, to a group called Blue Lake Ltd. whose partners included Guzzetta, leaving him with more than fifty million dollars in instant equity.[1] Officials at Codina Bush declined to disclose their commission; associate Hank Klein called it "a trophy property... one of the largest we've ever done."[1]
