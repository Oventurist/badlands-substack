# Mark Guzzetta

Mark Guzzetta is a Republican fundraiser, a key fund-raiser for former President [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]] and later finance co-chairman of [[jeb-bush|Jeb Bush]]'s campaign for governor of Florida. Jeb Bush was best man at his wedding.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)

In one of the biggest real estate deals in South Florida, IBM Corp. hired Bush's company, [[armando-codina|Codina]] Bush, to sell its massive Boca Raton office park — a maze of buildings with 2 million square feet of space on about 565 acres, assessed at $100 million and the town's biggest tract of developable land. In early 1997 the property sold for $46.1 million, less than half its assessed value, to a group called Blue Lake Ltd. among whose partners was Guzzetta.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair) The article's conclusion is that Jeb's friend acquired more than fifty million dollars of instant equity while Jeb took an undisclosed commission.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-family-affair)
