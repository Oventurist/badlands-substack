# Kendra Biggs

**Kendra Biggs** is a Washington, D.C. Superior Court judge and a Joe Biden appointee.[1]

## Coristine assault sentencing

In October 2025, Biggs sentenced two 15-year-olds to probation for their roles in an attack on [[edward-coristine|Edward Coristine]], a 19-year-old former [[doge|DOGE]] staffer known as "Big Balls."[1] The male defendant pleaded guilty to attempted robbery and simple assault; the female defendant admitted to one count of simple assault for attacking another person at a gas station with pepper spray.[1] Prosecutors dropped other charges relating to the attack on Coristine as part of a plea agreement.[1]

Biggs stated that her role is to "rehabilitate," not punish, a position that sparked backlash over perceived leniency.[1] President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said of the decision, "That's terrible. The judge should be ashamed," while U.S. Attorney for D.C. [[jeanine-pirro|Jeanine Pirro]] called the sentencing "shocking."[1]
