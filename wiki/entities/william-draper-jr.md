# William Draper Jr

William Draper Jr was a [[jp-morgan|JP Morgan]] banker, head of General Motors and a leading figure of Dillon Read and Co.[1]

In 1932 he co-sponsored the Third Eugenics Conference in New York alongside the Harriman family, including [[averell-harriman|Averell Harriman]].[1] The conference brought together leading eugenicists from around the world to study America's application of [[eugenics|eugenics]] laws, which had begun in 1907 under the patronage of [[theodore-roosevelt|Theodore Roosevelt]], and to discuss a coming age of "directed evolution of man" under a global scientific dictatorship.[1]
