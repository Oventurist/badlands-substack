# Kris Boyd

Kris Boyd is a cornerback for the New York Jets of the National Football League.[1]

## November 2025 shooting

Boyd was wounded in a shooting in New York City early on Sunday, November 16, 2025, according to multiple reports.[1] The New York City Police Department said shots were fired around 2 a.m. along West 38th Street, and responding officers found a 29-year-old male suffering from a gunshot wound to the abdomen.[1] The victim was taken to Bellevue Hospital and listed in critical condition.[1] The New York Post first reported that Boyd was the victim; authorities did not immediately name him.[1] The item was carried as a bonus story in the [[badlands-news-brief|Badlands News Brief]].[1]
