# Wes Streeting

Wes Streeting is the British Health Secretary who, in June 2025, announced a ten-year plan for the National Health Service under which every baby born in the United Kingdom would receive DNA testing.[1] Speaking to the Telegraph, Streeting framed whole-genome sequencing of newborns as the centrepiece of a shift in the [[nhs|National Health Service]] away from diagnosis and treatment and toward prediction and prevention of illness.[1]

## Newborn genomics plan

Streeting said the screening would cover hundreds of diseases and allow patients to "leapfrog" killer illnesses, with £650 million pledged by the government for genomics research.[1] In his own words, "the revolution in medical science means that we can transform the NHS over the coming decade, from a service which diagnoses and treats ill health to one that predicts and prevents it," adding that "genomics presents us with the opportunity to leapfrog disease, so we're in front of it rather than reacting to it."[1] The proposal is described in more detail at [[uk-newborn-whole-genome-sequencing-programme|the UK newborn whole-genome sequencing programme]].[1]

## Commentary

[[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] commentator [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] treated the announcement as an exercise in fear-based data collection, paraphrasing the pitch as "disease is scary, so you should give us unrestricted access to your genetic information, and that of your children."[1] She called the plan "madness," urged readers to "imagine the power of the data set," and argued that "tyranny is always sold with utopian promises that abandoning your rights is the only way to keep you safe."[1]
