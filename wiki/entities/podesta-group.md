# Podesta Group

The Podesta Group was a Washington, D.C. lobbying firm founded roughly thirty years before its 2017 collapse and described as among the largest and most powerful in the capital.[1] It was founded and chaired by [[tony-podesta|Tony Podesta]], whose brother [[john-podesta|John Podesta]] — chairman of [[hillary-clinton|Hillary Clinton]]'s 2016 campaign and a veteran of the Clinton and [[barack-obama|Obama]] orbits — supplied the firm's political connections, according to [[joe-lange|Joe Lange]].[1]

## Federal investigation

NBC News reported that the firm and Tony Podesta were subjects of a federal investigation led by Special Counsel [[robert-mueller|Robert Mueller]], arising out of the inquiry into the finances of [[paul-manafort|Paul Manafort]].[1] The indictment of Manafort and his deputy Rick Gates identified the Podesta Group as "Company B," alleging Manafort hired it to lobby for an ostensibly independent nonprofit that was "under the ultimate direction" of the Ukrainian president, his party, and the Ukrainian government.[1] Politico reported that neither Podesta nor the group had been charged "publicly" with any crimes.[1]

## Closure

Within days of the Manafort indictment being unsealed, Tony Podesta stepped down as chairman and named longtime chief executive Kimberley Fritts as his successor; Fritts abruptly resigned a week later.[1] Workers began removing dozens of pieces of Podesta's photography and art collection from the firm's walls, and the firm prepared to shut its doors, with staffers unsure whether they would be paid beyond the following week.[1]

## Interpretation in the Q framework

Q drop 1286 (April 27, 2018) asked, "Can the investigation officially/publicly drop POTUS from review & continue? Why did the PODESTA Group close shop?"[1] Lange treats the juxtaposition as the answer to who the [[mueller-special-counsel-investigation|Mueller special counsel investigation]] had turned toward once [[donald-trump|Trump]] ceased to be a target: the Podesta Group, whose sudden dissolution he attributes to sealed indictments rather than reputational fallout.[1] The public narrative that Manafort was being pursued for Russian collusion is described as a lie, since his connections — and the Podestas' — were Ukrainian.[1] These are Badlands Media interpretations rather than established findings.

## Saudi business ties

Chapter 18 of the [[q-for-dummies|Q for Dummies]] series draws on a November 2017 Gateway Pundit report to answer Q drop #118's question about the backgrounds of those detained in the [[saudi-purge|Saudi purge]], noting that several of the top Saudi officials arrested that weekend were linked to the Podesta Group.[2] The chapter observes that the Saudis had extensive business dealings with the firm, which it treats as significant given the material Q had already published concerning [[john-podesta|John Podesta]], and highlights the firm's subsequent closure — reported by Politico in November 2017 — as more interesting still.[2]

## Origins and business model

The firm opened in 1988 as Podesta Associates, co-founded by brothers John and Tony Podesta, both former aides to Democratic lawmakers and presidential candidates who reasoned that their knowledge of the legislative process and their connections to top Democratic policymakers would be valuable to clients and enriching to themselves.[3] Over three decades the name changed from Podesta Associates to Podesta Mattoon to the Podesta Group, and John left and returned more than once.[3] John boosted the firm's clout by spinning through the revolving door — joining the Clinton White House, rising to its highest levels, then cashing out again — while lobbying for insurer subsidies among other work.[3] Tony built the business by becoming a major Democratic donor and bundler, personally contributing more than $1.2 million to the campaigns of politicians his firm lobbied and raising many millions more.[3] Some of Tony's 2016 fundraisers for Hillary Clinton were headlined by campaign chairman John Podesta, and leaked emails showed Tony pulling strings on a Puerto Rico client's behalf with John inside the Obama White House.[3] There was also repeated overlap between the agenda and donors of the [[center-for-american-progress|Center for American Progress]], which John ran, and the Podesta Group's lobbying clients.[3]

## Moldova contract

In July 2016, near the end of the Clinton–Trump campaign, Moldova's [[democratic-party-of-moldova|Democratic Party]] — of which oligarch [[vlad-plahotniuc|Vlad Plahotniuc]] was vice president — signed a $600,000 contract with the Podesta Group for lobbying services in Washington.[3] The deal followed Plahotniuc's May 2016 visit to the United States, made on a tourist visa, during which he met assistant secretary of state [[victoria-nuland|Victoria Nuland]].[3] Badlands writer Joe Lange argues the retainer bought protection for a fugitive at the center of the [[moldovan-billion-dollar-bank-fraud|billion-dollar Moldovan bank fraud]], noting that [[interpol|INTERPOL]] refused for years to list him and only accepted the Moldovan request on February 7, 2025, weeks after Trump's second inauguration and long after the firm had closed.[3]

## Saudi contracts

In August 2017 the firm registered under the [[foreign-agents-registration-act|Foreign Agents Registration Act]] as an agent for the [[saudi-american-public-relation-affairs-committee|Saudi American Public Relation Affairs Committee]], a Delaware-registered, Washington-based group running advertisements attacking [[qatar|Qatar]], for $50,000 per month.[3] Separately, a March 2016 FARA filing recorded Tony Podesta as an active foreign agent of the Saudi government working with the Center for Studies and Media Affairs at the Saudi Royal Court, then headed by [[khaled-al-tuwaijri|Khaled Al Tuwaijri]], with the firm reportedly receiving $140,000 per month.[3] Tuwaijri was arrested in the November 2017 [[saudi-purge|Saudi purge]] alongside [[alwaleed-bin-talal|Prince Alwaleed bin Talal]] and [[bakr-bin-laden|Bakr bin Laden]].[3]

## "The Bridge"

Q drop 1515 (June 16, 2018) labelled the Podesta Group "THE BRIDGE," a bridge "between media, FBI/DOJ, HRC+," and repeatedly asked why the firm closed absent public charges, pointing to [[john-huber|Huber]] and sealed November indictments of John Podesta and [[huma-abedin|Huma Abedin]].[3] Lange extends the metaphor: the firm was a bridge to the $1 billion stolen from [[moldova|Moldova]], to the gold that vanished from [[libya|Libya]], to Ukrainian money flowing into the [[clinton-foundation|Clinton Foundation]] — Q drop 3611 called Ukraine the foundation's single largest donor — to the propaganda media, and to the FBI/DOJ effort to frame Trump.[3] He describes the pattern as a team operation in which the firm signed a client, lobbied the [[state-department|State Department]], and the client kicked money back to the Clinton Foundation.[3] While Manafort was indicted for a FARA violation over the same Ukrainian work, the Podesta Group was permitted to file its FARA paperwork retroactively and was never charged.[3] Six days after Q drop 67 described a Podesta plane being escorted and diverted on November 3, 2017, Tony Podesta ceased operations with almost no warning to employees; Lange presents the sequence as evidence the brothers were "leveraged" and had been fleeing to Saudi Arabia.[3]
