# Sacramento County Sheriff's Office

The Sacramento County Sheriff's Office is the county-level law enforcement agency for Sacramento County, California. It appeared in Badlands Media coverage in January 2025 as the agency behind the arrest of Pulitzer Prize-winning [[washington-post|Washington Post]] cartoonist [[darrin-bell|Darrin Bell]] on child pornography charges.[1]

## Role in the Darrin Bell case

The investigation into Bell was conducted by Sacramento Valley Internet Crimes Against Children (ICAC) detectives operating under the Sheriff's Office umbrella.[1] According to a statement by the Sheriff's Office cited by the outlet BNO, the case began after a tip about 18 files containing child sexual abuse material uploaded to an online service, and investigators ultimately linked a total of 134 videos to the same account.[1] Deputies executed a search warrant at Bell's residence, arresting him and booking him into the Sacramento County Main Jail on January 15, 2025, on a felony charge of "possession/control of obscene matter depicting a person under 18."[1] Bell's inmate record was publicly listed on the Sacramento County Jail's website.[1]
