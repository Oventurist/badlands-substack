# Avicii

Avicii was a Swedish DJ and electronic music producer whose 2018 death is grouped in Badlands Media commentary with those of [[chris-cornell|Chris Cornell]] and [[chester-bennington|Chester Bennington]] as suspicious deaths of musicians allegedly working to expose [[child-trafficking|child trafficking]].[1]

## Claims in Badlands commentary

An October 2024 essay by [[erik-carlson|Erik Carlson]] wrote that Cornell, Bennington, and "Swedish DJ Avicii were said to be producing a documentary exposing child trafficking at the time," and that all three were said to have killed themselves.[1] The essay presents the pattern as retaliation by the [[cabal|Cabal]] against those attempting to leave or expose it.[1]

## Context

Carlson added that [[brad-pitt|Brad Pitt]] and producer [[rick-rubin|Rick Rubin]] are believed to have been part of the same group, and that after seeing their friends die they "got the point and stepped back in line."[1] The claims are presented as opinion and rumor; Badlands Media notes that its articles reflect the views of contributing authors.[1]
