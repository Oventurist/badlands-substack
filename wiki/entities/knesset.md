# Knesset

The Knesset is Israel's national legislature, described in the Badlands corpus as the "Israeli Congress." In the March 13, 2026 edition of the Badlands Brief, "Narrative Warfare For Your Safety", the Knesset appears as a direct participant in the controversy over the alleged sexual assault of a Palestinian detainee at the [[sde-teiman|Sde Teiman]] facility.

## Role in the Sde Teiman case

According to commentary by [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]], members of the Knesset helped lead Israeli settlers who stormed the military base where four accused [[israel-defense-forces|IDF]] soldiers were being held, attempting to free them by force alongside National Security Minister [[itamar-ben-gvir|Itamar Ben-Gvir]]. IDF personnel reportedly had to physically repel citizens who tore down barriers.

The body subsequently held a publicly broadcast debate on whether it should be lawful for men to sodomize male prisoners with objects. During that debate, MK [[hanoch-milwidsky|Hanoch Milwidsky]] of the [[likud|Likud]] party is quoted as saying, "Shut up! If he is a Nukbha, then everything is legitimate to do!" The Badlands commentary treats the debate as evidence that the underlying acts were not in dispute inside Israeli politics — only their permissibility.
