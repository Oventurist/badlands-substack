# Mojtaba Khamenei

Mojtaba Khamenei is identified in the Badlands corpus as the new supreme leader of [[iran|Iran]], having succeeded [[ali-khamenei|Ali Khamenei]] after the latter was killed in the coordinated strikes that opened the 2026 war.

The July 22, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that Foreign Minister [[abbas-araghchi|Abbas Araghchi]], in a lengthy interview, said he has never met the new supreme leader and described Mojtaba Khamenei's appointment as a signal of policy continuity rather than of any change in direction. That characterization is notable within the corpus because it is offered by the Islamic Republic's chief diplomat, who acknowledges having had no personal contact with the head of state on whose behalf he negotiates.

Araghchi's broader account of the wartime period — including his statement that the [[supreme-national-security-council|Supreme National Security Council]] never convened in full because the simultaneous loss of its members could collapse the system, and that meetings occurred in underground tunnels — situates the succession within a leadership structure operating under acute security constraint and, in Araghchi's own telling, deep penetration by foreign intelligence services.

## Trump's June 2026 overtures

The June 5, 2026 Badlands Brief records a marked change in President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s rhetoric toward the new supreme leader. Asked by Fox News correspondent [[peter-doocy|Peter Doocy]] whether he would meet Khamenei, Trump said he is not currently seeking a meeting but would consider one if a deal is reached and would approach it respectfully — beginning with "I don't *want* to meet" before saying he would be "honored" to meet the Supreme Leader, and adding that "some people have suggested it." Trump also described Khamenei as "probably a professional" who "in some circles… has a very good reputation."

Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called this "a rebranding exercise at Warp Speed," arguing that Trump was reframing the Ayatollah as a reasonable actor and signalling that Washington would not attempt to overthrow [[iran|Iran]]'s existing system of government. The shift accompanied Trump's statement that the United States does not need a formal agreement to obtain Iran's enriched uranium, which he described as effectively "entombed" at damaged nuclear sites.

## Trump's CIA claim (March 2026)

The March 27, 2026 Badlands Brief reports that President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said in an interview on [[fox-news|Fox News]]' "The Five" that the [[cia|CIA]] had told him Khamenei is gay, referencing recent intelligence claims. Trump acknowledged the agency relayed the information but indicated uncertainty, noting that others have made similar claims, and reminisced about his support from gay voters in the 2024 election. The reporting connects the claim to broader geopolitical narratives and internal Iranian power dynamics. Badlands contributor [[ghost-of-based-patrick-henry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] observes that Trump made the remarks after weeks of speculation over whether Khamenei was even alive or real, since no footage of him appearing in public had surfaced, concluding that "it feels like we are getting trolled by Trump."

## June 8, 2026 Brief

The June 8, 2026 Badlands Brief repeats Trump's conciliatory posture toward Khamenei in the context of the Iranian missile attack on [[israel|Israel]]: while not currently seeking a meeting with the Supreme Leader, President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] said he would consider one if a deal is reached and would approach it respectfully, and reiterated that [[iran|Iran]] has already agreed not to obtain nuclear weapons, describing that commitment as the central issue in the ongoing discussions. The Brief presents Trump's remarks as evidence that Iran's strikes "did not kick at all" and would not deter his pursuit of a deal.
