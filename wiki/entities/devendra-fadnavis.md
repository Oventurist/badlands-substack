# Devendra Fadnavis

**Devendra Fadnavis** is the chief minister of the Indian state of [[maharashtra|Maharashtra]].[1]

## Response to the Pahalgam attack expulsions (April 2025)

On Monday, 28 April 2025, Fadnavis said there were no Pakistanis unaccounted for on the list of short-term visa holders who had been ordered to leave [[india|India]] in the wake of the [[pahalgam-terror-attack|Pahalgam terror attack]].[1] His statement came a day after deputy chief minister [[eknath-shinde|Eknath Shinde]] said 107 were unaccounted for.[1]

Fadnavis's home department sought to clear up the confusion, reporting that all but 14 of the 55 [[pakistan|Pakistani]] nationals in the state on short-term visas had exited in accordance with the central government directive, and that the state had tracked down the remaining 14, some of whom were on medical visas required to leave by the Tuesday.[1] Departmental data also showed 5,023 Pakistanis living in parts of the state excluding Mumbai, including 2,740 on long-term visas, 2,088 on extensions, and 34 who had overstayed and were living in the state illegally.[1]
