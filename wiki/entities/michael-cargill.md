# Michael Cargill

**Michael Cargill** is a Texas gun dealer and the named respondent in the Supreme Court case [[garland-v-cargill|Garland v. Cargill]], which resulted in the invalidation of the federal bump stock rule on June 14, 2024.[1]

## Litigation

Cargill owned several bump stocks before the [[bureau-of-alcohol-tobacco-firearms-and-explosives|ATF]] rule banning them took effect and later handed them over to the government, after which he filed suit challenging the agency's authority to reclassify the accessories as "machineguns."[1] The [[supreme-court-of-the-united-states|Supreme Court]] ruled in his favor 6–3, holding that ATF exceeded its statutory authority.[1]

## Reaction

Cargill posted on X on the Friday of the decision a message reading simply: "I won."[1] In video remarks noted by Badlands Media, Cargill acknowledged that he frequently hears that "nobody cares about bump stocks," but explained that he had pursued the case for five years because of the constitutional principle that the right to keep and bear arms "shall not be infringed."[1] Badlands contributor [[ashe-in-america|Ashe in America]] closed the segment with congratulations to Cargill.[1]
