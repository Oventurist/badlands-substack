# American Bridge 21st Century

**American Bridge 21st Century** (AB PAC) is a Democratic-aligned opposition research super PAC founded within the network of political strategist [[david-brock|David Brock]].[1]

## Place in the Brock network

In his January 1, 2024 essay, [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[ryan-delarme|Ryan DeLarme]] cited the research site [[influencewatch|InfluenceWatch]] to place American Bridge 21st Century alongside the media criticism organization [[media-matters-for-america|Media Matters for America]] and the legal advocacy group [[citizens-for-responsibility-and-ethics-in-washington|Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington]] as components of a single Brock-centered network of organizations.[1]

DeLarme invoked that network to argue that CREW's litigation to remove [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]] from state ballots in the [[colorado-ballot-disqualification-case|Colorado case]] and in more than a dozen other states was not the work of a neutral watchdog but of an interlocking set of partisan institutions — including the broader [[soros-shadow-network|George Soros shadow network]] of NGOs, foundations and advocacy groups — pursuing election interference under the banner of "[[danger-to-our-democracy|protecting our democracy]]."[1]
