# Washington State University

Washington State University is an American public research university whose animal-science researchers developed the gene-edited pork approved for supermarket sale by the [[food-and-drug-administration|US Food and Drug Administration]] in May 2023.[1]

## Gene-edited livestock research

Scientists at the university produced the altered pork using the gene-editing tool [[crispr|CRISPR]], which takes DNA seen as more "desirable" and artificially injects it into other livestock.[1] The resulting product was to be sold as German-style sausages and was reported to provide adequate nutrition for human consumption.[1]

Professor Jon Oatley, who led the research, said the project's critical goal was to improve both the quality of meat and the ability of livestock to thrive in harsh environments — an outcome he argued would help increase protein sources in underdeveloped countries.[1]

## Reception

The approval drew criticism from [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] contributor [[johnny-q|Johnny Q]], who warned that DNA modifications to livestock could not have been tested for long-term results and side effects, and urged consumers to support local farmers and exercise caution about what they purchase and consume.[1]
