# National Security and Defense Council of Ukraine

The National Security and Defense Council is [[ukraine|Ukraine]]'s top coordinating body for defense and security policy, chaired by the president and headed operationally by a secretary.[1]

## Wartime messaging

In early September 2023, council head [[aleksey-danilov|Aleksey Danilov]] told the Kiev Security Forum that World War III had already begun, having moved from a hybrid period into an "active phase."[1] The statement was part of a broader alarmist narrative pushed by top Ukrainian officials after it became clear that Ukraine's military was losing ground against [[russia|Russia]], and as Kyiv sought new ways to draw more direct support from key European powers.[1] More than a month later, some European leaders — notably German Defense Minister [[boris-pistorius|Boris Pistorius]] — began echoing the same warning about the prospect of war in Europe.[1]

A senior Ukrainian military officer separately told *[[time-magazine|Time]]* that the armed forces had been forced to second-guess orders issued by Kyiv's political leadership, including an order to capture the Donetsk city of Horlivka, asking: "Where are the weapons? Where is the artillery? Where are the new recruits?"[1]
