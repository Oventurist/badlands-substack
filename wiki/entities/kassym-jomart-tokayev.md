# Kassym-Jomart Tokayev

Kassym-Jomart Tokayev is the President of Kazakhstan.[1]

## Board of Peace

Tokayev was among the confirmed acceptances to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] in January 2026, alongside Hungary under [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orban]], Belarus under [[alexander-lukashenko|Alexander Lukashenko]], and Argentina, Vietnam and Morocco.[1] Kazakhstan was one of roughly 60 countries invited to the signing ceremony scheduled during the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] annual meeting in Davos.[1]
