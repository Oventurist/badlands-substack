# Kassym-Jomart Tokayev

Kassym-Jomart Tokayev is the President of Kazakhstan.[1]

## US relations and the 2025 locomotive deal

On September 22, 2025, Tokayev held a phone call with President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]], after which Trump announced that [[kazakhstan|Kazakhstan]] had signed a [[kazakhstan-united-states-locomotive-deal|$4 billion deal]] to purchase American locomotives and rail equipment, which he described as the largest railroad equipment purchase in history.[2] Trump congratulated Tokayev on the agreement in a Truth Social post.[2]

Earlier that day Tokayev met in New York with US Presidential Special Envoy for South and Central Asia [[sergio-gore|Sergio Gore]], congratulating him on his appointment and pledging support for his mission, and also met a [[meta-platforms|Meta]] vice president.[2] He reaffirmed Kazakhstan's commitment to advancing the Kazakh-American strategic partnership and emphasized the country's role as Washington's main economic partner in Central Asia.[2]

## Board of Peace

Tokayev was among the confirmed acceptances to President [[donald-trump|Donald Trump]]'s [[board-of-peace|Board of Peace]] in January 2026, alongside Hungary under [[viktor-orban|Viktor Orban]], Belarus under [[alexander-lukashenko|Alexander Lukashenko]], and Argentina, Vietnam and Morocco.[1] Kazakhstan was one of roughly 60 countries invited to the signing ceremony scheduled during the [[world-economic-forum|World Economic Forum]] annual meeting in Davos.[1]
