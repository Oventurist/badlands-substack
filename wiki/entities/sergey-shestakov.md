# Sergey Shestakov

Sergey Shestakov is a former Russian diplomat who became a United States citizen and worked as an interpreter for federal courts and federal prosecutors.[1] He was charged alongside former [[fbi|FBI]] counterintelligence official [[charles-mcgonigal|Charles McGonigal]] in a scheme prosecutors said involved working for sanctioned Russian aluminum magnate [[oleg-deripaska|Oleg Deripaska]].[1]

Federal prosecutors alleged that the pair worked on Deripaska's behalf in 2021 to investigate an unnamed rival Russian oligarch in violation of U.S. sanctions, and took steps to conceal the activity.[1] Shestakov faced an additional charge of lying to FBI investigators.[1]
