# Global Disinformation Index

The Global Disinformation Index (GDI) is a United Kingdom–based organization that produces digital blacklists rating media outlets for "disinformation risk," and which received support from the United States [[state-department|State Department]].[1]

## Dynamic Exclusion List

Among GDI's products is what Racket News described as "an Orwellian horror" called the Dynamic Exclusion List — a register on which at least 2,000 websites were flagged as unsuitable for advertising, in the group's own words "thus disrupting the ad-funded disinformation business model."[1] Because advertising revenue underwrites most independent outlets, inclusion on the list functions as a financial penalty imposed without adjudication.[1]

## "Disinformation, Inc." and the funding controversy

GDI's US government funding was exposed in "Disinformation, Inc.," a series by investigative reporter Gabe Kaminsky published by the [[washington-examiner|Washington Examiner]] in early 2023, which detailed how the State Department — via the [[global-engagement-center|Global Engagement Center]] — backed the blacklisting agency with taxpayer money.[1]

A year after the disclosures, the State Department responded to continued reporting by threatening to show a congressional committee its records only *in camera* until it obtained a "better understanding of how the Committee will utilize this sensitive information," a posture Racket News characterized as Secretary [[antony-blinken|Tony Blinken]] threatening to take his "transparency ball" home unless coverage of the programs stopped.[1]
