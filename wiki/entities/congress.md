# Congress

The United States Congress appears in "A Bright Red Line" as one of the three centers — alongside the security state and the [[state-department|State Department]] — where Western neoconservatives are said to understand, and to desire, that declaring Ukrainian [[nato|NATO]] membership inevitable will end any possibility of negotiation with Russia.[1]

Congress is implicated more broadly by the article's framing of U.S. war funding: from the outset of the war the United States made clear it would take primary responsibility for funding and arming the Ukrainians, and the article contends this funding adversely affects Americans economically while it remains unclear how the war serves the American people's interest.[1]
