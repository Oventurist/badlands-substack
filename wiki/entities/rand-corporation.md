# RAND Corporation

The **RAND Corporation** appears in Badlands Media commentary as a source of think-tank analysts who, in the author's characterisation, treat a new world war as an exciting and even winnable prospect.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Characterisation in the article

Contributor [[matthew-ehret|Matthew Ehret]] writes that some RAND think-tankers and representatives of the [[military-industrial-complex|military industrial complex]] believe a third world war is winnable according to their computer-modelled scenarios, and that the reality is far different.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) He argues that such a war could never be won: the next-generation hypersonic missile technologies unveiled by both Russia and China, including underwater drones, have demonstrated that a first-strike monopoly by [[nato|NATO]] is a pipe dream, and no attack on Russia or China could be carried out without a vicious retaliation that would take out every major city across what he calls the Rules Based International free world.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Related

The same passage cites the chief executive of [[palantir|Palantir]] telling Americans to prepare for a three-front war with Russia, China and Iran.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation) Ehret's counter-argument is that the existence of atomic weapons has made the errors of the pre-nuclear era intolerable in the twenty-first century, and that a summit must occur before, not after, another catastrophic war.[[1]](https://badlands.substack.com/p/a-gateway-to-a-new-world-of-cooperation)

## Commentary on Western weapons in Ukraine (2024)

In October 2024 RAND air warfare expert [[michael-bohnert|Michael Bohnert]] told Business Insider that if Ukraine used donated F-16s and Abrams tanks to their full potential, few should be expected to survive the war: "If any of the F-16s make it to the end of the war, they probably weren't used hard enough."[2]

[[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] of [[badlands-media|Badlands Media]] quoted RAND's own description of itself as an American nonprofit global policy think tank, research institute and public sector consulting firm whose research has informed U.S. policy since the 1950s on the space race, the Vietnam War, the U.S.-Soviet nuclear confrontation, the Great Society programs and national health care.[2] He characterized the organization as an apologist for the [[military-industrial-complex|military-industrial complex]] that produces the white papers used by bodies such as the Trilateral Commission and the World Economic Forum to justify expanded institutional control, and dismissed Bohnert's framing as sociopathic.[2]

## Badlands criticism (September 2024)

In September 2024 Badlands described the RAND Corporation as "one of the great villains of the modern era" while reviewing the institutional affiliations listed by former CIA analyst [[soo-kim|Soo Kim]], who appeared among the accounts followed by [[ryan-wesley-routh|Ryan Wesley Routh]].[3] The brief examined RAND's posts on X regarding Ukraine's Kursk incursion and US politics, and grouped the organization with [[lmi|LMI]] and the Philip Merrill Center for Strategic Studies at the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies as institutions whose interest lies in preserving the status quo rather than improving the world.[3] [[ghostofbasedpatrickhenry|GhostofBasedPatrickHenry]] called such bodies "merchants of death; a cartel built on the blood and bones of millions," and argued that Americans have a moral obligation to peacefully eliminate them, invoking Patrick Henry's warnings during the Constitutional Conventions of the 1780s and Thomas Paine's line, "I prefer peace. But if trouble must come, let it come in my time, so that my children can live in peace."[3]
