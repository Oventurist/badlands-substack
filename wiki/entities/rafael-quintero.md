# Rafael Quintero

Rafael Quintero was a Cuban exile involved in many covert [[central-intelligence-agency|CIA]] operations.[1]

He was among the five figures named in the 1983 report of the Australian Joint Task Force on Drug Trafficking to Parliament, which held that [[ted-shackley]], [[richard-secord]], [[thomas-clines]], Quintero and [[edwin-wilson]] had backgrounds "relevant to a proper understanding of the activities of the [[nugan-hand-bank|Nugan Hand]] group and the people associated with that group."[1] Joe Lange notes that these names went unmentioned in press coverage of the bank's collapse because, in his framing, they were untouchables.[1] Quintero belonged to the same milieu of CIA-employed Cuban exiles that produced [[brigade-2506]] and operatives such as [[felix-rodriguez]].[1]
