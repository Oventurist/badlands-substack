# Burton Wheeler

Burton Wheeler was a United States Senator and an ally of [[ferdinand-pecora|Ferdinand Pecora]] during the Senate banking investigations of the early 1930s.[1] Commenting on the revelations of fraud extracted from Wall Street's leading financiers, Wheeler said that "the best way to restore confidence in our banks is to take these crooked presidents out of the banks and treat them the same as we treated Al Capone."[1]

Although not every banker went to prison as Wheeler or Pecora would have liked, examples were made of dozens who did, and many more saw their careers shamefully ended.[1]
