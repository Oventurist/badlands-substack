# Voices of Israel

Voices of Israel is the current name of an Israeli government public-influence program originally established as Kela Shlomo and later known as "Concert," designed to conduct what Israel has termed "mass consciousness activities" aimed largely at the United States and Europe.[1]

## Background and relaunch

The program was relaunched as part of a broader Israeli public relations campaign following the outbreak of the Gaza war in October 2023.[1] In a November 2023 [[knesset|Knesset]] hearing, Diaspora Affairs Minister [[amichai-chikli|Amichai Chikli]] told lawmakers that new budget money was available for a pushback campaign separate from traditional public relations and paid advertising, and that 80 programs were already under way to be conducted "in the 'Concert' way."[1]

A June 2024 investigation by [[the-guardian|The Guardian]] reported that Israel had relaunched the controversial entity as part of an effort to target US college campuses and to redefine antisemitism in US law.[1] In its earlier incarnation, Concert had worked with groups spearheading the campaign to pass so-called "anti-BDS" state laws penalizing Americans for engaging in boycotts or other non-violent protests of Israel.[1] The Guardian characterized the latest version as part of a hardline and sometimes covert operation by the Israeli government to strike back at student protests, human rights organizations, and other voices of dissent.[1]

## Badlands interpretation

Badlands contributor GhostofBasedPatrickHenry, who said he had been reporting on the program since September 2023, welcomed the story being "blown wide open" and argued the documentary paper trail supported the macro claims even where individual details required discernment.[1] He framed the revelations as evidence of a transnational effort in which an Israeli deep state is a major component, and argued that free-speech restrictions in the United States were being advanced through the [[republican-party|Republican Party]] rather than only through the political left.[1]
